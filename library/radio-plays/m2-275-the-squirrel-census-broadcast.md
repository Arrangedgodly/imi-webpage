# The Squirrel Census Broadcast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: field-count radio reports, sponsor-driven audience metrics, and live callers inflating a wildlife census*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST NARA, a lemur
FIELD COUNTER KITO, a macaque
CALLER LELA, a gibbon
SPONSOR REP VOSS, a baboon
TECH PAVI, a capuchin

[SFX: OUTDOOR MICROPHONE, LEAVES RUSTLE.]

NARA: Welcome to Count the Canopy, the live program tracking squirrels in the town square. Kito is counting from the east bench, and sponsor representative Voss has brought a branded counter.

KITO: I count one squirrel on the fountain rim.

VOSS: Our counter has a button for each squirrel and a second button for each successful brand impression.

NARA: How do you count an impression?

VOSS: When the squirrel sees the logo.

KITO: The squirrel is looking at a nut.

VOSS: The logo is printed on the nut bag.

[SFX: BAG CRINKLES.]

PAVI: Please do not open food near the counting station.

VOSS: This is an educational demonstration.

KITO: Two squirrels.

NARA: The number has doubled.

KITO: One left the fountain and another arrived.

VOSS: Our dashboard says six.

KITO: Your dashboard has counted the logo.

VOSS: The logo is a participant.

LELA (CALL-IN): I live by the square. There are three squirrels under the bench.

KITO: I see two.

LELA: The third is inside the hollow post.

[SFX: WOOD TAP FROM INSIDE POST.]

NARA: We have a sound from the post.

VOSS: The post is engaging with the brand.

PAVI: It is hollow. Something is moving in it.

Kito walks over and peers into the post. A squirrel pops out, carrying a strip of survey tape.

KITO: That is our third squirrel.

VOSS: Add a point for the tape.

NARA: The tape is not a squirrel.

VOSS: It is a campaign asset.

Kito resets the tally and explains that each animal is counted once, whether seen, heard, or briefly hidden. Voss argues that a squirrel passing the logo should count twice: once as an animal and once as an audience.

KITO: Then the audience number will be higher than the squirrel number.

VOSS: That is how audience measurement works.

[SFX: COUNTER BEEPS RAPIDLY.]

PAVI: Your counter is stuck.

VOSS: It is registering excitement.

NARA: It is in your pocket against the button.

Voss removes the counter. It stops. A squirrel takes the corner of his logo card and runs up the tree.

VOSS: The campaign has gone airborne.

KITO: That is still one squirrel.

LELA: Another one just crossed the path.

NARA: Do we count it?

KITO: Yes. It crossed the boundary once and left.

VOSS: Our sponsor wants a memorable final total.

KITO: Four squirrels observed, one squirrel heard inside a post, one unconfirmed report.

NARA: Why separate heard from observed?

KITO: So listeners know what the count means.

VOSS: Could we call it six active impressions?

KITO: You can call it that. It is not the census.

A listener phones from the west path and claims to have seen seven squirrels. Kito asks whether they were separate animals or one squirrel circling a tree.

CALLER LELA (OFF): It circled three times.

KITO: Then I recorded one squirrel and three circuits.

VOSS: Could the circuits count as engagements?

NARA: Not in the census.

The host asks Kito to announce the method before giving the final number. Kito explains that listeners can submit sightings, but the verified count comes from the fixed boundary and observation window. Voss tries to add the sponsor bag as a landmark. A squirrel drags it under the bench.

[SFX: PAPER BAG SCRAPES.]

VOSS: It has taken our sign.

KITO: It has moved the bag.

PAVI: Do not chase it. The window is closed.

Voss reaches after the bag, then stops at the survey line.

VOSS: The brand is beyond the boundary.

NARA: The count is not.

Nara reads the official total on air. Voss writes the sponsor figure on one card and the actual count on another.

[SFX: TAPE FALLS, LEAVES RUSTLE.]

The squirrel drops survey tape beside Kito's clipboard. Voss asks to count the tape as a returning object. Pavi says it is not an animal, and Nara closes the log with the accurate count.

NARA: Four seen, one heard, one caller report pending.

KITO: No squirrel signed the sponsor release.

[SFX: MICROPHONE FADES, SQUIRREL CHATTER.]
