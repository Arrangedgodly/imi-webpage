# The Case of the Echoing Footstep

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio noir detective convention, where a night watchman reports a second set of footsteps that is actually his own on a hollow bridge*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Detective MIRI, macaque; Sergeant ODO, baboon; witness PIRI, capuchin; bridge keeper TAVI, gibbon.

[SFX: RAIN ON WOOD. A LONE FOOTSTEP, THEN ANOTHER.] 

MIRI: At midnight, the bridge had two visitors. One was Piri. The other kept following him.

PIRI: I heard the steps behind me. I stopped, they stopped. I ran, they ran.

ODO: Did you look back?

PIRI: I looked. No one there. The rain had erased every footprint except mine.

MIRI: Convenient. A culprit with excellent manners and no shoes.

[SFX: THUNDER, THEN A SLOW CREAK.] 

TAVI: Detective, you called for the keeper of this bridge.

MIRI: I called for the owner of the second set of steps.

TAVI: I own one bridge. It came with several noises.

ODO: The suspect is already claiming property rights.

Tavi taps a boot against the first plank. One hollow thump answers with a lower thud.

TAVI: The bridge is old. The boards have different chambers underneath.

PIRI: That is what the follower wants us to think.

MIRI: What did you do when you heard it?

PIRI: I walked faster.

MIRI: And the steps followed faster?

PIRI: Yes.

ODO: A disciplined criminal.

TAVI: Or a very obedient board.

Tavi demonstrates. He steps once; the plank answers. On the sixth plank comes a double knock.

[SFX: ONE STEP, DOUBLE KNOCK.] 

PIRI: That one has two feet.

MIRI: Or one foot with a complicated past.

Odo walks over the plank. It answers in the same rhythm. He freezes halfway, one foot in the air.

ODO: I have been challenged by architecture.

MIRI: Put the foot down, Sergeant. We need evidence, not a statue.

Odo reaches for the railing. It groans in a low note.

ODO: The bridge has a accomplice.

TAVI: That is the handrail. It complains whenever someone grabs it.

MIRI: Did it complain when Piri crossed?

PIRI: I was holding it. I thought it was whispering my name.

ODO: It said "old wood."

PIRI: It sounded like "Piri."

MIRI: Noir makes every creak personal.

He steps. Double knock.

ODO: It knows my shoe.

TAVI: It knows the hollow space under that board. Your boot is irrelevant.

Miri asks Tavi to dry the sixth plank with a cloth. The rain keeps tapping on the rail while he wipes the board.

[SFX: CLOTH WIPES WOOD. ONE THUMP, THEN A QUIET TAP.] 

MIRI: The echo has lost its voice.

PIRI: The suspect is weakening.

A gust catches Piri's coat. Its loose sleeve slaps the railing twice.

[SFX: TWO FABRIC TAPS.] 

PIRI: Someone is behind me!

ODO: It is your coat, which has been following you all evening.

PIRI: I was under the impression it was loyal.

Tavi clips the sleeve to Piri's satchel. Miri directs Piri to cross slowly, stopping after each board. The bridge answers each step. At the sixth, the double knock comes, always delayed by the same fraction.

MIRI: Now cross the grass beside it.

[SFX: RAIN SOFTENS. GRASS FOOTSTEPS.] 

PIRI: No follower.

MIRI: You stopped making the sound. It stopped following.

PIRI: I thought it was chasing me.

ODO: The bridge has been waiting here for years. It has nowhere to be.

Piri asks whether the sign should warn visitors. Tavi says a warning should tell them what to do, not frighten them into sprinting. Odo proposes: "Beware the second suspect." Miri rejects it and paints a stripe beside the plank.

TAVI: Walk, do not run. Sixth board answers twice.

PIRI: That sign gives away the whole mystery.

MIRI: The mystery was six inches of air beneath a board.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALK AWAY, ONE SET. A DOUBLE KNOCK FROM THE BRIDGE.] 

ODO: The suspect has resumed work.

MIRI: Let it. It is the only one of us still on duty.
