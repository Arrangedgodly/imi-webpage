# The Orchard Night Watch

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: suspense radio dramas that build a conspiracy around harmless nighttime noises*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Watchkeeper Fenn, a macaque; investigator Nara, a gibbon; sound editor Odo, a baboon; orchard resident Miri, a capuchin.

[SFX: NIGHT WIND, THREE SOFT TAPS.]

FENN: At midnight, the orchard gate tapped three times.

NARA: The latch is loose.

ODO: Or someone is sending a signal.

MIRI: It was the wind.

ACT ONE

Odo asks for a second take of the tapping. Fenn knocks on the gate with one knuckle. The sound is louder than before. Nara checks the latch and finds it still closed.

NARA: The gate is secure.

ODO: The sound is not secure.

Miri says the wind lifted the gate against its frame. Odo records the wind, but the microphone catches Miri chewing a plum. He calls the chewing a second signal. Miri stops chewing.

MIRI: It was dinner.

ACT TWO

Nara asks Fenn to hold the gate while she tightens the latch. Odo narrates each movement as if they are defusing a device. Fenn asks him to move the microphone so Nara can hear the screw.

[SFX: METAL TURN, LATCH CLICKS.]

The gate now opens and closes quietly. Odo says the signal has stopped. Fenn says the wind is still blowing. Odo asks whether the silence means the message was received.

NARA: It means the latch is fixed.

Miri walks past the gate and brushes it with her shoulder. It does not tap. She walks back and tries again, harder. The gate remains quiet.

MIRI: The orchard is less dramatic now.

Odo asks her to record a final line about the mystery. Miri says, "The wind pushed the gate. We tightened the latch." Odo says it lacks danger. Fenn suggests adding a shadow. Miri points to Odo's microphone stand, which casts one across the path.

Odo asks the residents to reconstruct the night from sound alone. Fenn makes the gate tap once, then repeats the wind against the frame. Nara identifies the difference: the first tap rang from the latch, while the wind made a hollow flap. Miri says she heard both while eating. Odo labels the audio clips "unknown visitor" and "possible plum signal." Nara takes the labels away and writes "loose latch" and "chewing." Odo protests that plain labels leave no mystery. Fenn asks him to narrate the repair instead. Odo describes Nara's screwdriver turning, the latch settling, and the gate closing without a sound. It is quieter than the opening, but the audience can hear exactly what changed.

Miri notices that Odo's mystery labels are still on the sound board. She replaces them with "gate" and "plum." Odo tries to put "unknown night visitor" back on the first clip. Nara plays it once more. The latch makes a bright double click; the wind clip makes a low flutter. Even the listeners can tell which is which. Odo sighs, then admits that the ordinary explanation is more satisfying because they can fix it. Fenn closes the gate gently and leaves the orchard for the night.

ACT THREE

Odo records the story with the actual sequence: wind, loose latch, a sound, then a repair. Nara says no one was hiding in the orchard. Fenn confirms the gate stayed closed. Miri walks to the plum tree and picks one ripe fruit.

[SFX: PLUM TWISTS FREE.]

Odo asks if the twist can be the episode's final sound. Nara says the fruit is not a clue. Miri eats it before he can ask another question.

TAG

[SFX: THREE TAPS.]

Everyone freezes. Fenn checks the gate. It is still quiet. The taps come from Odo drumming his fingers on the microphone.

NARA: The suspect is in the studio.

ODO: I confess to creating suspense.

MIRI: Then you can clean up the plum pit.

[SFX: BROOM, END THEME.]
