# The Unclaimed Voice at Platform Nine

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: automated station announcements that keep addressing absent passengers and mistake every sound for a boarding cue*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Station keeper Omi, a macaque; announcer Sella, a gibbon; conductor Piri, a capuchin; traveler Daro, a lemur.

[SFX: EMPTY STATION. A WIND-UP CLOCK TICKS. A PA SYSTEM CLICKS ON WITH A BURST OF STATIC.]

SELLA: Attention, passengers for the nine o'clock train. Please proceed to platform nine.

OMI: The train is not due until ten.

SELLA: The recording says nine.

PIRI: The clock says eight fifty-seven.

DARO: I am the only passenger here, and I am waiting for the bus.

SELLA: The announcement is for the train.

DARO: There is no platform nine. The station has four platforms.

OMI: We renumbered them last year. The speaker labels were not changed.

PIRI: The old tape has a second problem. The platform number is spliced over a different announcement.

[SFX: TAPE DECK CLICKS. A VOICE SAYS, "PLATFORM NINE," THEN ANOTHER SAYS, "PLATFORM TWO." THE WORDS OVERLAP.]

SELLA: That is why the board and the speaker disagree.

OMI: Can you cut the old phrase out?

PIRI: I can, but the tape will be too short to trigger the message timer.

DARO: Could we record a new announcement on the same tape?

PIRI: Yes. I will record it live, then test it once from the waiting room.

[SFX: PIRI WINDS THE CASSETTE. BUTTON PRESSES. TAPE WHIRRS.]

SELLA: The nine o'clock train is delayed. It will board on platform two. Bus six leaves from the front entrance.

OMI: That is clear.

PIRI: The board is showing the same information now.

[SFX: ELECTRONIC BOARD BEEPS; LETTERS CHANGE.]

DARO: Does the announcement need a chime?

SELLA: One soft chime before the words, none after.

[SFX: A SINGLE SOFT CHIME, THEN THE NEW MESSAGE PLAYS CLEANLY.]

OMI: The platform sign matches. The front entrance is clear.

DARO: I know where to wait now.

PIRI: And nobody is being sent to a platform that does not exist.

DARO: Which way do I go for the bus?

OMI: The bus stop is outside.

SELLA: Please remain on platform nine until further notice.

DARO: I am not on platform nine.

[SFX: A BUS HORN SOUNDS OUTSIDE. THE ANNOUNCEMENT SYSTEM PLAYS A CHIME.]

SELLA: The train is arriving.

PIRI: That was a bus.

OMI: The microphone's noise sensor triggers the train chime when it hears a horn.

DARO: Can someone turn it off?

SELLA: The system is announcing a departure.

OMI: It is announcing itself.

[SFX: PA CHIME, THEN A RECORDING: "MIND THE GAP." A DOOR SLAMS OUTSIDE.]

PIRI: No train is here.

SELLA: The announcement has opened the doors.

OMI: It cannot open doors. That was the bus.

DARO: I need to catch that bus.

SELLA: Please wait for your train.

DARO: I am leaving now.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS MOVE TOWARD THE EXIT. THE PA CHIMES AGAIN.]

SELLA: Passenger departing from platform nine, please return to the waiting area.

DARO: I am not a passenger for the train!

OMI: Sella, switch to the live microphone. We will make one accurate announcement.

SELLA: The live microphone is in the signal booth.

PIRI: I'll go with you.

[SFX: TWO SETS OF FOOTSTEPS CLIMB A SHORT STAIR. A BOOTH DOOR OPENS; SWITCHES CLICK.]

SELLA: I can see the bus stop from here.

OMI: Give the route and time only.

SELLA: The number six bus leaves from the front entrance in two minutes. The nine o'clock train is delayed and will board on platform two.

[SFX: A SINGLE SOFT CHIME. OUTSIDE, A BUS ENGINE IDLES.]

DARO: Thank you. I am at the front entrance.

PIRI: The platform two sign is lit.

OMI: And platform nine?

SELLA: There is no platform nine.

OMI: Correct.

[SFX: THE CASSETTE STOPS. ONE BUTTON CLICKS, THEN THE CLOCK TICKS TO NINE.]

SELLA: The train is now boarding on platform two.

PIRI: Passengers are moving.

DARO: The bus is pulling away.

OMI: Did you catch it?

DARO: No. The announcement was accurate, but a minute late.

SELLA: Should I announce the next one?

DARO: Yes. The next bus is in twenty minutes, and I am still here.

[SFX: A CHAIR SCRAPES INTO THE WAITING AREA. THE PA STAYS QUIET; THE CLOCK KEEPS TICKING.]
