# The Applause Meter Is Overexcited

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: studio game shows that score audience reactions as if they were contestants*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; producer Mara, a macaque; sound operator Odo, a gibbon; contestant Pip, a capuchin; audience volunteer Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Studio set. Applause sign buzzes once.

VEX: Welcome to The Big Reaction! Our applause meter has already scored the audience before the show began.

MARA: The meter is a light bar for the warm-up. It does not award points.

ODO: The sensor is reading the audience mic. A chair scrape can trigger it if the gain is too high.

PIP: I came to answer the quiz questions. Am I competing against the crowd now?

VEX: Everyone competes. Even the room.

NARI: I was asked to clap when the contestant gets an answer right. I didn't know I was being scored.

MARA: We won't score audience members. Odo, keep the audience sensor separate from the contestant score.

PIP: What are the rules for my round?

MARA: Three questions. One point for each correct answer. No audience reaction points.

VEX: The crowd's enthusiasm remains an invisible bonus.

NARI: I'd rather not be a bonus.

VEX: The bar has lit again.

ODO: A chair scrape has triggered the audience sensor. It is not attached to the contestant score.

SFX: Chair leg drags; meter chirps.

MARA: Disconnect that sensor from the bar. Keep the broadcast mic on its taped mark and the score display on its own channel.

ODO: Sensor is disconnected from the bar. Testing normal speech: one, two. The score display stays clear.

VEX: I can clap to see whether the meter is asleep.

MARA: We are testing the audio path, not the audience's reaction. Please don't clap into the microphone.

PIP: Ready for the first question.

VEX: Name the largest ocean.

PIP: Pacific.

ODO: Correct. One point.

NARI: I can clap now?

MARA: If you want, after the answer. It won't change Pip's score.

SFX: Gentle applause; no meter buzz.

VEX: The room responded, but our machine remained neutral.

PIP: That seems like a better quiz show.

MARA: It is. Next question.

VEX: What do bees gather from flowers?

PIP: Nectar and pollen.

ODO: Correct. Two points.

VEX: The audience is holding its breath.

NARI: We are just listening.

VEX: Suppose I give them a special applause round. A big cheer could earn the studio a bonus point.

MARA: There is no studio score. The audience sensor is disconnected, and the contestant gets points only for correct answers.

VEX: Then I will clap myself.

SFX: Vex claps twice; no meter buzz.

ODO: The applause bar stayed dark. The contestant display still reads two.

PIP: I want the last question read from the card, without a hint from the meter.

MARA: Agreed. No one will turn a reaction into a score. The final answer will settle the quiz.

SFX: Page turn; soft studio tone.

NARI: A caller asks whether audience applause changes Pip's score.

ODO: It doesn't. The audience mic is disconnected from the reaction bar, and the contestant display only receives correct answers.

MARA: Pip has two points so far. We will ask the third question as printed.

VEX: The audience may react, but their response is not a score.

PIP: I can hear the rule, and I know how many points I have.

PIP: Ask the last question. I will answer based on its wording, not the flashing wall.

VEX: What gas do plants absorb from the air?
PIP: Carbon dioxide.
ODO: Correct. Three points from three questions.

MARA: The applause light remains off the contestant score channel. The panel can hear the audience without counting its reaction.

TAG

ODO: Three questions, three points.

VEX: The audience scored zero.

NARI: We weren't playing.
