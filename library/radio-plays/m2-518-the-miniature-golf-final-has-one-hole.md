# The Miniature Golf Final Has One Hole

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports commentary that inflates a friendly game into a championship*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Commentator Odo, a gibbon; player Pip, a capuchin; rival Vex, an orangutan; course keeper Mara, a macaque; marshal Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Miniature golf course. Ball taps plastic rail.

ODO: The final round is tied on the last hole.

MARA: The last hole is the only hole. It is a practice lane.

VEX: The trophy is made of polished wood.

PIP: It is a wooden cup from the water station.

SELI: Keep both players behind the line until the lane is clear.

VEX: Pip has a straight shot.

PIP: The ramp curves left.

ODO: The commentators can see the cup.

MARA: The players can see the ramp. The camera is behind the plant.

SFX: Leaves rustle as Mara moves a potted fern.

VEX: Now the course looks less dramatic.

PIP: Now I can see where to aim.

MARA: The rule card says one shot per player, one point for a sink. If the lane obstructs a ball, both players restart from the marked line.

VEX: The scorecard has room for sudden death.

SELI: It is one lane, not a tournament.

ODO: There are two start marks because both players need an equal turn.

VEX: I want Pip to use the farther mark.

MARA: The marks are the same distance from the ramp. The fern made one look farther.

PIP: I can stand at the white line. The camera can move back.

SFX: Tripod feet scrape. Camera shifts behind the score board.

SELI: Both start marks are visible now.

VEX: We need dramatic stakes.

MARA: The only stake is whether the ball reaches the cup.

ODO: The players are set. Pip goes first.

SFX: Pip taps the ball. It rolls up the ramp and catches on a loose seam.

PIP: It stopped at the same crack.

MARA: Pause the round. The plastic panel is lifting.

SELI: No one else shoots until the lane is flat.

SFX: Tool clicks; panel presses level and settles.

ODO: The obstruction is fixed. The ball returns to the start line, and both players keep one shot.

VEX: I would like the replay in slow motion.

MARA: It was a practice shot. We'll play it at normal speed.

SFX: Ball resets. Pip taps; it rolls up the ramp and drops into the cup.

ODO: Pip scores one.

VEX: My shot will decide the championship.

MARA: Same start line, same ramp.

SFX: Vex taps. Ball rolls, reaches the cup, circles the rim, and settles beside it.

SELI: Vex misses. Pip wins the lane.

VEX: The cup is crooked.

MARA: It is level. The ball passed the lip; it just needed more pace.

PIP: You can practice after the broadcast.

ODO: The score is final: Pip one, Vex zero.

VEX: What is the trophy?

MARA: The water cup. We can fill it for the next players.

SFX: Cup fills at a nearby tap.

PIP: I can drink first, then put it back on the course shelf.

VEX: The champion gives up the trophy?

PIP: It's a cup. Everyone gets a turn.

VEX: Could the winner keep it until the next round?

MARA: Then the water station loses its cup.

SFX: A player offstage reaches for water; cup is lifted from the shelf.

ODO: A second player is waiting to use it.

PIP: I can set it back after I drink. The trophy belongs to the course.

VEX: The competition has a practical prize.

SELI: The lane remains available, and the water cup is back where it belongs.

SELI: The lane is open again, fern moved clear, cup returned.

ODO: The miniature golf final is over.

VEX: I will challenge the next player.

MARA: After you finish your water.
