# The Interview That Would Not End

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: long-form interview radio convention, where the host keeps asking guests to summarize after they have already answered*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host PAVA, gibbon; guest LUMA, macaque; producer ODO, baboon; caller MIRI, capuchin.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC, THEN FADE.]

PAVA: Luma, you have built a small bridge between the orchard and the stream. Tell us how you did it.

LUMA: I found three fallen branches-

PAVA: Before the branches, what inspired you?

LUMA: The stream.

PAVA: After the stream, what happened?

LUMA: I found the branches.

ODO: We have time for one question before the break.

PAVA: This is the question before the break. Luma, when you found the branches, what did they represent?

LUMA: They represented branches.

PAVA: And the bridge?

LUMA: A way to cross.

PAVA: A way to cross what?

LUMA: The stream.

Odo points to a clock. Pava nods, then asks Luma to summarize the bridge in one sentence.

LUMA: It stays above the water.

PAVA: Could you make that more personal?

LUMA: It is my bridge.

PAVA: We will take a short break.

Odo holds up a card reading "LIGHTNING ROUND." Pava turns it over and asks Luma whether the bridge was built quickly or slowly.

LUMA: It took two afternoons.

PAVA: Quickly or slowly?

LUMA: Two afternoons.

PAVA: The audience needs a comparison.

LUMA: It was faster than waiting for this question to end.

Odo reaches for the card. Pava pulls it back and reads the next prompt: "How does the bridge feel underfoot?"

LUMA: It feels like wood.

PAVA: And emotionally?

LUMA: Emotionally it is a bridge.

Miri's call crackles through the speaker. She says the current is rising and asks whether the bridge can hold a heavier basket. Luma immediately asks her to put it down until the center support is checked.

PAVA: Is this concern part of your creative process?

LUMA: It is part of the stream.

ODO: The call is over the break.

PAVA: We have not begun the break.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC, THEN CUT OFF.]

ODO: That was the break.

PAVA: We did not play the sponsor message.

ODO: The sponsor message is a recording of you saying "we will take a short break."

PAVA: Then we should play it.

Luma's bridge begins to creak. She listens carefully.

LUMA: The center branch is shifting. I need to move the stone under it.

PAVA: Can you tell us what the shifting symbolizes?

LUMA: It symbolizes that the bridge is shifting.

She leaves the microphone. Odo starts the sponsor message, but the sound of scraping stone comes through the open studio door.

PAVA: We have lost our guest to a practical matter.

ODO: The bridge is part of the interview.

Miri calls in.

MIRI: I tried the bridge. It is sturdy, but one branch sticks out where a tail could catch.

PAVA: What did you feel when your tail caught?

MIRI: I felt the branch.

PAVA: We will return to that feeling after a word from our sponsor.

Luma comes back, carrying the loose branch. She places it on the floor and tests the bridge. The creak stops.

LUMA: Now it is stable.

PAVA: How did the repair change your understanding of the bridge?

LUMA: It changed the bridge.

ODO: We are out of time.

PAVA: Luma, final question. If you could tell listeners one thing about building a bridge, what would it be?

LUMA: Put the stone under the part that moves.

PAVA: And how did that make you feel?

LUMA: Like I should have done it before the interview.

Pava starts the theme music too early. She tries to speak over it, but the music grows louder.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC.]

PAVA: Thank you for joining us.

LUMA: I am still here.

ODO: The interview is over.

LUMA: The bridge is not.

The theme fades. Luma tests the bridge once more. It holds.

[SFX: STEADY FOOTFALLS ACROSS WATER.]

PAVA: Luma, could you summarize that crossing?

LUMA: No.

[SFX: ONE LAST STEP, THEME OUT.]
