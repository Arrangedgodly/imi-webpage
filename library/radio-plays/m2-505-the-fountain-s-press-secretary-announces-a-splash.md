# The Fountain's Press Secretary Announces a Splash

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public relations broadcasts that spin routine maintenance as a major achievement*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Spokesperson Vex, an orangutan; groundskeeper Mara, a macaque; reporter Odo, a gibbon; resident Pip, a capuchin; producer Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Town square fountain. Water trickles unevenly.

VEX: Today the city is proud to announce a bold new water initiative.

MARA: We are cleaning the fountain filter.

ODO: The water is low because the intake screen is clogged.

PIP: The basin is full of leaves.

VEX: The public needs a statement about renewal.

SELI: They need to know the fountain will be off for an hour.

MARA: I can post the sign.

VEX: Say the fountain is entering a restorative pause.

ODO: It is being shut off so we can clear the leaves.

SFX: Valve turns. Water stops.

PIP: I can help move the leaf basket.

MARA: Use the handle, not the screen.

SFX: Basket lifts. Wet leaves slosh.

VEX: The city has mobilized resources.

ODO: It is one basket.

VEX: One basket of civic commitment.

MARA: The screen is clear. Turn the valve slowly.

SFX: Valve turns. Water sputters, then flows evenly.

PIP: The fountain is working.

SELI: The sign says "Closed for maintenance until noon." It is ten now.

VEX: Can we say we completed the work ahead of schedule?

MARA: We have to rinse the screen and check the basin first.

ODO: There's a twig under the nozzle.

SFX: Twig lifts out. Water stream smooths.

PIP: Now it is clear.

SELI: The sign is damp. We need to dry it before putting it away.

MARA: I'll hang it in the shed.

PIP: A child is coming to fill a cup. The stream is clean now.

ODO: We should let the water run briefly after clearing the intake.

SFX: Water pours into a tin cup.

VEX: The city should announce the first successful drink.

PIP: It's just water.

MARA: It's what the fountain is for.

SFX: Cup sets down. Another passerby fills a bottle.

SELI: The square is using the fountain again.

VEX: Can I record a quote from the resident?

PIP: Say the water works, and please don't put the microphone over the spout.

ODO: I can check the filter each week and clear leaves before they build up.

MARA: That's a maintenance schedule, not a new initiative.

VEX: The city can announce it as one.

SELI: The sign is dry now. It says the fountain is open.

ODO: The filter is clean, but the leaves will collect again after the next windy day.

MARA: I'll empty the basket on Friday and rinse the screen.

PIP: I can help carry the wet leaves to the compost.

SELI: Put the maintenance day on the community board so people know when the fountain is off.

VEX: A calendar notice is less exciting than a launch.

MARA: It also tells someone where to get water while the fountain is shut.

ODO: The drinking tap at the library remains open.

PIP: I'll write that on the sign in large letters.

SFX: Marker writes. Paper clips to the board.

VEX: The press release can mention both the clean fountain and the open tap.

SELI: The public has the useful information now.

SFX: Paper folded, basket carried away.

VEX: The initiative has delivered.

MARA: The fountain is open. Put the sign away.

ODO: I'll return the basket to the shed.

PIP: The fountain sign is readable from the path.

MARA: Then the next visitor can find water without asking the press desk.

ODO: The basket is back in the shed. The sign shows when we will check the filter again.

TAG

SFX: Fountain splashes.

VEX: A successful press event.

PIP: It is a fountain.
