# The Mystery of the Missing Echo

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: detective radio serials that treat ordinary acoustics as a criminal mastermind*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Detective Odo, a baboon; partner Nara, a macaque; caretaker Piri, a gibbon; technician Tavi, a capuchin.

[SFX: EMPTY HALL, FOOTSTEPS, ONE ECHO.]

ODO: Someone repeated my footsteps.

NARA: The hall has an echo.

PIRI: We call it the west wall.

ACT ONE

Odo says the echo has been following him since morning. Nara asks him to clap. He claps once; the sound returns from the far wall. Tavi checks the room and finds no speaker or hidden caller.

ODO: The culprit is invisible.

NARA: Or the sound bounced.

PIRI: We painted the wall yesterday. It is smoother now.

Odo calls that a motive. He asks Piri who had access to the paint. Piri says the maintenance crew. Tavi says the crew left a ladder by the wall.

ACT TWO

They test the hall. Nara walks to the center and claps. The echo returns faintly. Odo walks near the wall and claps. The sound comes back quickly.

TAVI: The distance changed.

ODO: The suspect moved.

Piri rolls the ladder away from the wall. The echo becomes less sharp. Odo says the ladder was an accomplice. Nara asks whether the wall is still painted. Piri confirms it is.

Tavi hangs a soft cloth panel on the wall. The next clap barely echoes. Odo asks if the criminal has been captured. Nara says the room has absorbed some sound.

PIRI: We can take the cloth down after the meeting.

ODO: That gives the echo time to return.

Tavi asks Odo to record one clap from each place in the hall. At the center, the echo is soft; by the painted wall, it returns clearly. Odo says the evidence suggests the wall knew the sound was coming. Nara points out that the wall cannot predict anything. Piri explains that the ladder had been reflecting some of the clap toward the microphone. Tavi moves it back and forth to demonstrate. The sound changes every time the ladder shifts. Odo asks whether it was an accomplice or merely an unreliable witness. Piri says it is a ladder. Odo writes "ladder, present at the scene" in his notebook. Nara asks him to erase that before the maintenance crew reads it. He says the file can stay open until the cloth panel comes down tomorrow. Tavi notes that the case will then have a scheduled follow-up, which Odo calls a sequel.

Nara makes Odo clap once more, then waits. The echo arrives a beat later. Odo asks if the room is answering. Nara says the room is returning the sound. Odo nods and closes the notebook. Piri asks if the case is closed. Odo says the notebook is closed; the wall remains available for questions.

Tavi saves both recordings, labeled "center of hall" and "by wall." Odo asks if the file names can be more mysterious. Nara refuses; the technician needs to find them tomorrow. The sound board keeps one button for the echo and one for the dry clap, so Odo can compare them without inventing a culprit.

ACT THREE

Odo records his final report. "The missing echo was not stolen. It was reduced by a cloth panel and a ladder moved from the wall." Nara says that is accurate. He insists the case needs a suspect, so Piri names the smooth paint as an accomplice.

TAVI: Paint cannot be charged.

ODO: Then it walks free.

They remove the cloth panel. The echo returns when Odo claps. He looks startled.

NARA: It came back.

ODO: The suspect has returned to the scene.

TAG

Piri rolls the ladder past the microphone. Its wheels make a small squeak.

ODO: A new clue.

NARA: A new maintenance request.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADE.]
