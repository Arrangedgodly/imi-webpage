# The Birdbath's Water Level Is a Breaking Story

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: nature news reports that turn routine observation into crisis coverage*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Reporter Nari, a lemur; host Vex, an orangutan; gardener Mara, a macaque; recorder Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Garden. Water trickles into a stone birdbath.

VEX: We interrupt our program. The birdbath is nearly empty.

NARI: It is half full. The sun has been out all morning.

MARA: The basin is shallow by design. I refill it each day.

ODO: A leaf is blocking the drain.

PIP: I saw a sparrow drink from it.

VEX: Is the sparrow in danger?

MARA: No. It left after drinking.

SFX: Wings flutter away.

NARI: The water level is lower than yesterday.

MARA: Evaporation and visiting birds both use water.

VEX: We should call it a drought alert.

ODO: The garden hose is coiled by the shed.

MARA: I'll refill the basin and clear the leaf.

VEX: The audience needs a dramatic countdown.

PIP: I can hold the hose, but don't fill it to the rim. Birds need a shallow edge.

SFX: Hose valve turns. Water trickles into stone.

MARA: Stop there. The edge is wet enough to drink and shallow enough to stand.

ODO: I'll rinse the drain.

SFX: Leaf lifts; a drop falls through.

NARI: The basin is clear.

NARI: The recorder can capture the garden for five minutes, but we should first check that the cable is outside the bird's flight path.

MARA: I'll place it beside the stone post, not on the rim.

SFX: Small recorder clicks on; birds flutter nearby.

PIP: A sparrow is waiting on the fence.

VEX: Tell it the water level is normal.

MARA: Birds do not need our announcement. They need a clean basin and a way to leave.

ODO: The drain is clear. I'll pour out the dusty water, then refill to the marked line.

SFX: Water drains; hose trickles.

NARI: The basin is at the mark.

VEX: What if no birds return during the broadcast?

PIP: Then the garden continues without an audience.

SFX: Wings land on the rim. A soft splash.

MARA: Don't walk closer. Let it drink.

NARI: The water is at the same line as yesterday after the refill.

ODO: I'll mark the level on the garden log, not on the stone.

VEX: A graph would make the segment look serious.

MARA: A dated log can show whether the basin needs more frequent attention.

PIP: The sun hits this side first. The shallow edge dries faster.

ODO: I can put the basin in partial shade, but not under the low branch.

MARA: Leave the site where birds can see the approach and the exit.

SFX: Small stone basin lifts and slides beneath a higher branch; water sloshes.

PIP: The path is still clear.

NARI: The shade reaches half the bowl.

VEX: Our urgent bulletin has become a gardening decision.

MARA: The birds still have water, and the caretaker knows what to check at noon.

VEX: The breaking story has a visitor.

ODO: The birdbath is ready for the rest of the day.

VEX: Can we leave a camera running to capture the crisis?

MARA: We can leave a small recorder, but not an unattended hose.

NARI: The recorder picked up the bird drinking, not the water disappearing.

PIP: That is what the garden sounds like when the basin is doing its job.

MARA: We'll turn off the recorder now and clean the rim after the bird leaves.

SFX: Recorder clicks off; wings lift away.

VEX: No breaking news?

NARI: Only the usual water level check at noon.

ODO: I will note the refill time beside today's weather.

MARA: Check again at noon, then close the log until tomorrow.
