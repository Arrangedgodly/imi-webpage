# The Radio Contest Has an Unanswerable Question

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: quiz broadcasts that mistake ambiguity for suspense*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Quizmaster Vex, an orangutan; judge Mara, a macaque; contestant Pip, a capuchin; finalist Rell, a squirrel monkey; scorekeeper Odo, a gibbon; caller Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Studio quiz table. Bell, card shuffle. Two contestants sit at separate microphones.

VEX: We are tied at seven points apiece. One final question decides the contest. Pip and Rell, are your buzzers ready?

PIP: Ready.

RELL: Ready, though the buzzer is louder than the questions.

VEX: Final question: which is the fastest animal?

PIP: On land, in the air, or in water?

VEX: The fastest. One answer. Don't waste our dramatic pause.

MARA: The question needs a category. Different animals hold different speed records in different environments.

ODO: The answer sheet says "cheetah," but the card does not say "on land." The card and key do not match.

VEX: Ambiguity creates suspense. We have a clock, a bell, and a close-up microphone. Use the suspense.

NARI, on phone: I heard it from home. A peregrine can dive faster than a cheetah runs. A sailfish would be a different answer in water.

MARA: Nari has pointed out the flaw; she has not answered for either player. Nobody loses a point on a question with several valid answers.

VEX: The rules say no help from callers.

MARA: The rules also say the judge may void a defective card. Odo, read the tied score before this question.

ODO: Pip, seven. Rell, seven. Neither has buzzed in.

RELL: I was going to say the falcon. I don't want a point awarded for the host changing the category afterward.

PIP: I can answer a clear question. I can't guess which habitat the answer sheet forgot to print.

VEX: You are both refusing the cliffhanger.

MARA: They are asking for the same rule. We will discard the card for both players, then use the reserve card that the rules prescribe for a defective final question.

SFX: Card tears from a clip; reserve envelope opens.

ODO: Reserve card: "Which land animal is commonly cited as the fastest sprinter?" The key says cheetah.

VEX: One question, one answer, and a proper drumroll.

MARA: Read it once to both microphones. No extra clue.

ODO: Which land animal is commonly cited as the fastest sprinter?

PIP: Cheetah.

RELL: Cheetah.

ODO: Both answers match the key. Each earns the single reserve point. New score: eight to eight.

VEX: A tie! We can keep the broadcast going with a sudden-death lightning round.

MARA: The written rules say a tie after the reserve makes co-champions. The rules do not permit another question.

VEX: But the prize ribbon has one loop.

RELL: Tie it in a bow. Both names can fit on the card.

PIP: Or Vex can hold the ribbon between us while Odo reads the result.

SFX: Paper ribbon unfurls; one end lands on each desk.

ODO: Final score, eight to eight. The ambiguous question is void for both contestants; the shared reserve point leaves them tied. The rules declare Pip and Rell co-champions.

NARI: I heard a fair answer and a clear result. The audience did not need to guess what the card meant.

VEX: Can I announce the winners again with more suspense?

MARA: Once. Say both names, then we end the contest.

VEX: Pip and Rell share the title. The ribbon has been divided without cutting it.

PIP: We can hold opposite ends for the photograph.

RELL: Keep the ribbon attached. It is the only thing in this studio that came with a clear instruction.

SFX: Camera click. Bell rings once.

ODO: Score sheet signed. Disputed card clipped to the review copy, not the answer stack.

MARA: We are not scoring bells.
