# The Community Calendar Has Two Saturdays

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local announcement programs that treat a scheduling error as a town-wide event*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Announcer Sela, a gibbon; hall clerk Pip, a capuchin; organizer Mara, a macaque; caller Nari, a lemur; caller Vex, an orangutan; attendee Tavi, a gibbon.

SFX: Studio clock ticks. Paper calendar pages shuffle.

SELA: This is the Community Calendar. We have two announcements for Saturday.

PIP: They are both in the hall at ten.

MARA: My seed-swap is Saturday at ten.

NARI, on phone: My dance class is Saturday at ten. I booked the room in spring.

VEX, on phone: I booked the room in summer for my repair workshop.

MARA: The seed swap was written on the wall calendar.

PIP: The hall calendar says repair workshop.

SELA: What date is written on each?

MARA: Mine says the 14th.

VEX: Mine says the 14th too.

NARI: The 14th is Sunday.

PIP: We have two Saturdays.

SELA: Do we?

PIP: The calendar has a printed Saturday the 13th and a second Saturday handwritten over Sunday the 14th.

MARA: I left the handwritten label there when I flipped back from last month.

VEX: I read both labels and booked the room twice.

SELA: Did anyone check the date?

PIP: The hall computer confirms Saturday the 13th.

NARI: My printed flyer says the 14th.

VEX: My workshop invite says Saturday the 14th too.

MARA: We have a seed swap, a dance class, and a repair workshop.

SELA: All in one room?

PIP: One room, three groups, two dates, zero confirmed Saturday.

SFX: The telephone rings. Pip answers.

PIP: Community Hall. Which event are you calling about?

TAVI, through phone: I'm standing outside. The sign says Saturday.

SELA: What day is it today?

PIP: Friday the 12th.

MARA: So the event is tomorrow.

NARI: Which one?

VEX: The first one to unlock the door.

PIP: I have one key.

SFX: The hall door opens. Two groups begin setting down supplies at once.

MARA: Seed trays on the left.

NARI: The dance mat goes on the right.

VEX: My repair bench cannot roll through either one.

PIP: The calendar has brought three events at the same time.

MARA: I can set up the seed trays in the courtyard.

NARI: The dance class needs the floor.

VEX: The repair bench needs power.

SELA: If all three start outside, where does the hall go?

PIP: We can use the schedule board and draw blocks by hour.

MARA: My swap can run from nine to ten.

NARI: Dance class at ten-thirty.

VEX: Repairs at noon. I can bring the bench after the class.

SELA: Then the room is booked in order.

PIP: It still says Saturday the 14th.

NARI: Tomorrow is the 13th.

MARA: The date is wrong but the day is right.

VEX: We should not print a new date. It will confuse people.

SELA: We can announce the correct date and keep the Saturday label.

PIP: The board can say "Saturday, the 13th. Seed swap at nine, dancing at ten-thirty, repairs at noon."

MARA: And the courtyard stays open for anyone who arrives early.

NARI: We should tell the caller.

PIP: The caller is standing at the door.

SFX: Door latch. Paper sign peels, then a fresh page is taped up.

SELA: Please read the new sign.

TAVI, through phone: "Saturday the 13th." Good. It is Friday now, so I can come tomorrow.

VEX: We have resolved the calendar.

PIP: The calendar still has two Saturdays.

MARA: Only one of them is tomorrow.

SFX: A pencil scratches out the second Saturday. One page turns.

SELA: The Community Calendar will be back next week, unless next week arrives on two Wednesdays.

TAG

SFX: The studio clock strikes once.

PIP: Is that the hour?

SELA: No. It's the calendar falling off the wall.
