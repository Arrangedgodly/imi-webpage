# The Mountain Tram Interviews Its Last Passenger

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live transit reporting, cliffhanger departures, and broadcasters who turn a routine stop into a final-journey special*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Sori, a macaque; tram operator Eno, a baboon; dispatcher Yumi, an orangutan; passenger Fenn, a gibbon; passenger Miri, Fenn's sister, a gibbon; mechanic Bex, a capuchin.

SFX: Mountain station. Tram bell; doors slide closed, then reopen.

SORI: The summit tram is preparing to make its final run of the evening. We have one passenger still on the platform. Is this a rescue?

FENN: No. I am waiting for my sister. She is at the ticket window.

ENO: I reopened the doors because the boarding chime sounded before the platform was clear.

YUMI: The final departure is at six-thirty. It is six-twenty-eight.

SORI: Our listeners hear a countdown.

BEX: They hear the door sensor. It beeps whenever someone steps over the yellow line.

SFX: Beep. Beep. A paper map flaps.

FENN: My sister is right there. She is paying for two tickets.

SORI: Two passengers, one final departure.

YUMI: The second car is already at the summit. We can wait for the passenger to board.

ENO: I cannot leave the doors open while the tram moves. The interlock prevents it.

SORI: Is the tram trapped?

BEX: No. The doors are open at the platform. We close them only after everyone boards.

FENN: Here she comes.

SFX: Footsteps, ticket printer.

MIRI, off mic: I have the tickets.

SORI: We have a second voice from the platform.

FENN: That's my sister. Her name is Miri. She is carrying the tickets.

YUMI: We have two valid tickets. Operator, please check the passenger count.

ENO: I count two adults, one backpack, and the radio crew.

SORI: The radio crew is not boarding.

BEX: Then I count two adults and one backpack.

SORI: The tram's final run is still waiting.

YUMI: The schedule allows a two-minute boarding hold. We are at six-twenty-nine.

SFX: Tram chime rings, then stops.

ENO: The doors closed.

FENN: My sister is outside.

ENO: The sensor showed the platform clear, but I heard a ticket fall. I am reopening before we depart.

BEX: Keep clear of the door track. I will retrieve it from the platform edge after the car is secured.

SFX: Door opens. Ticket flutters against the rail.

MIRI, off mic: I dropped the second ticket.

FENN: It is on the inside of the yellow line.

BEX: I can reach it with the long hook. No one needs to step onto the track.

SORI: A technical retrieval underway.

BEX: Ticket is back. Both fares are valid.

YUMI: Thank you. We have one minute left in the boarding hold.

SORI: The last passenger is not a mystery. It is the person holding the other ticket.

MIRI: I am boarding now.

SFX: Footsteps cross threshold; door sensor chimes once.

ENO: Count is two passengers, one backpack, no radio crew. Doors closing.

SFX: Door closes; interlock clicks.

YUMI: Platform clear. The final run may depart.

SORI: We will stay on the platform and follow by radio.

FENN: You said the tram was making its final run.

SORI: It is. Our station is closed.

BEX: The crew still needs to walk to the lower depot.

SORI: That is not a broadcast event.

YUMI: It is if the host stays on the microphone.

SFX: Tram bell rings; motor climbs steadily.

ENO: We are moving. The passengers are seated, the doors are locked, and the path is clear.

MIRI: I can see the station lights below us.

FENN: You can see them because we are above the station.

SORI: That concludes our final-run coverage. The tram is on schedule, and nobody has been stranded.

BEX: The radio crew may still need a ride down.

SORI: We have a stairway.

YUMI: The station stairs are closed for maintenance.

SORI: Then we will wait for the morning tram.

SFX: Platform clock ticks; a distant bell fades.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The final tram carried two passengers. The radio team remains safely at the station with a very long night ahead.
