# Midnight Tale with Interruptions

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night ghost-story call-in programs, ominous pacing, and listeners who interrupt the tale to identify ordinary noises*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST SORI, a capuchin
STORYTELLER JEM, a gibbon
CALLER TALA, a macaque
ENGINEER MUN, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: LOW WIND, DISTANT CREAK.]

SORI: Welcome to Midnight Tales. Jem will tell us a story from the old lantern house.

JEM: At the top of the stairs stood a shadow that had no owner. It lifted one pale hand...

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

SORI: We have a caller. Tala, are you calling from the lantern house?

TALA: No. I want to know whether the shadow had five fingers.

JEM: I had not decided.

TALA: A shadow's shape depends on the light.

SORI: The story's mystery has deepened.

JEM: The hand turned slowly toward the door...

[SFX: DOOR CREAKS.]

TALA: That is the studio door.

MUN: It sticks in damp weather.

SORI: The old lantern house is known for damp weather.

JEM: We are not in the lantern house.

SORI: Please continue.

JEM: The hand reached for the latch. A cold breath passed across the room.

[SFX: AIR VENT HUMS.]

TALA: Is that a ghost?

MUN: The vent is on.

SORI: It could be a ghost using the ventilation system.

JEM: The figure whispered, "Leave this place before the moon..."

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLES.]

TALA: Someone is turning a page.

MUN: That is Jem's script.

JEM: I need the next line.

SORI: The page has answered the ghost.

JEM: The figure whispered, "Leave before the moon disappears behind the hill."

TALA: How long until that happens?

SORI: In the story, or here?

TALA: In the story. I need to know how much time the character has.

JEM: The character has until dawn.

SORI: A classic deadline.

JEM: She raced down the stairs, but heard footsteps behind her.

[SFX: THREE FOOTSTEPS.]

TALA: There were only three steps.

JEM: She had three steps left.

SORI: A very short staircase.

JEM: It was a steep stair.

MUN: I can make it longer with the wooden block.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS REPEAT, SLIGHTLY FASTER.]

TALA: Now it sounds like someone is following her in circles.

JEM: The figure was following her in circles. It was trapped in the landing mirror.

SORI: A mirror ghost.

TALA: Mirrors do not trap people. They reflect them.

JEM: The character realizes the shadow is her own. She turns toward the lantern and sees the lamp behind her.

SORI: Then who whispered?

JEM: She whispered to herself.

TALA: That is a very practical haunting.

SORI: Our callers are improving the story.

JEM: The character reaches the door, opens it, and steps into the dawn.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. BIRDS.]

SORI: The haunting is over.

TALA: The door creaked before it opened. Maybe the house was trying to warn her.

MUN: That was our door.

JEM: In the story, the house can have a voice. It never had a face.

SORI: Does it have five fingers?

JEM: Houses do not have hands.

TALA: Shadows can.

JEM: We can make that the title of next week's story.

SORI: I will reserve a whole hour for the shadow's reply.

SORI: The caller has left us with one final mystery. Jem, what happens to the lantern?

JEM: The character takes it home and uses it during a power cut.

SORI: The ghost becomes a household light.

JEM: It is a lantern.

[SFX: LIGHT SWITCH CLICKS. ROOM GOES DARK.]

MUN: The power just went out.

TALA: Do we have the lantern?

JEM: I have it right here.

[SFX: LANTERN STRIKES, GLOWS.]

SORI: Our story has crossed into the studio.

MUN: Or the studio has lost power.

SORI: Either way, Midnight Tales is signing off before the next ordinary sound becomes a caller.

[SFX: WIND OUT. LANTERN WICK FLICKERS.]
