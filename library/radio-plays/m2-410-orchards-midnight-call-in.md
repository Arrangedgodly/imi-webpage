# The Orchard's Midnight Call-In

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night call-in radio that stretches a simple caller problem into a personality quiz and suspenseful cliffhanger*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Daro, a baboon; caller Mina, a macaque; producer Seli, a gibbon; night keeper Tuck, a capuchin.

[SFX: RADIO THEME FADES. CRICKETS OUTSIDE THE BOOTH.] 

DARO: Midnight Garden Line. Tell us your worry in one sentence, and we will stay with you through the night.

MINA: The orchard gate is making a squeak.

SELI: A squeak? We should ask what the sound means to her emotionally.

DARO: Mina, when did the gate first become part of your life?

MINA: I came to check the latch ten minutes ago.

TUCK: I am the night keeper. The hinge is dry, but the gate is shut and secure.

SELI: The show card says we need a question, a reveal, then a break.

DARO: Mina, if that squeak were a color, what color would it be?

MINA: It would be loud. I want to stop waking the owls.

[SFX: GATE SQUEAKS ONCE, THEN SETTLES.] 

DARO: We have heard the sound. Stay with us after this quiet message.

[SFX: SHORT THEME STING, TOO LOUD; CRICKETS FALL SILENT.] 

MINA: The sting woke the owls more than the hinge.

SELI: That sting is our sponsor's nighttime bell.

TUCK: The owls are looking at the booth.

DARO: We can keep the conversation gentle. What would you like to happen next?

MINA: Tuck can oil the hinge. I can hold the gate.

SELI: That would end the call before we get to the mystery.

DARO: There is no mystery, producer. The hinge squeaks.

SELI: We could test the audience with a sound quiz. Was that a gate, a fox, or a secret visitor?

[SFX: THREE RECORDED SOUNDS PLAY: GATE SQUEAK, FOX BARK, GATE SQUEAK.] 

MINA: The first and third were the gate. The middle was your fox sound effect.

TUCK: That is right. The fox is asleep by the tool shed.

DARO: Our listener solved the quiz. The hinge still needs oil.

SELI: We cannot just hand her a can. The show has a suspense format.

SELI: Wait. We have a listener poll asking whether you are prepared because you are anxious or because you are naturally organized.

MINA: I am prepared because the gate has squeaked every night this week.

DARO: That is useful information. Tuck, why has no one oiled it?

TUCK: We had rain. Oil washes off, so I waited for a dry night. Tonight is dry.

SELI: That sounds like the mystery is solved before the repair.

DARO: The mystery was a hinge with a reason to squeak. We can tell listeners that without adding a thunderclap.

[SFX: THUNDERCLAP EFFECT STARTS; SELI CUTS IT OFF MID-ROLL.]

SELI: I have removed the dramatic reveal from the board. The hinge gets the next thirty seconds.

MINA: I have the can in my pocket. I came prepared.

[SFX: SMALL CAN RATTLES; CAP TWISTS.] 

TUCK: Hold the gate while I put a drop on the hinge pin.

[SFX: CAN SQUIRTS. GATE SWINGS QUIETLY.] 

MINA: It is quiet.

DARO: And no one had to confess a secret.

SELI: We can ask how the silence feels.

MINA: It feels like the owls went back to sleep.

[SFX: OWL HOOTS ONCE, DISTANTLY.] 

TUCK: That one is the owl. Not the gate.

DARO: We have one final question before our caller goes.

MINA: Is it another personality quiz?

DARO: No. Did the repair work?

MINA: Yes. The gate opens without a squeak and closes on the latch.

[SFX: GATE OPENS, THEN SHUTS WITH A SOFT CLICK.] 

SELI: The audience stayed through the break for that?

DARO: They heard a problem, a repair, and a sleeping owl. That's a complete night.

MINA: I am going home before the gate develops a backstory.

TUCK: I will check the hinge at dawn, not on the air.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADE; CRICKETS RETURN.] 

DARO: Midnight Garden Line is over. Our sponsor's bell has been lowered to a whisper.

[SFX: A TINY BELL TINKS. AN OWL HOOTS FROM THE BOOTH.]
