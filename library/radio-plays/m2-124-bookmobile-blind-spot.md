# The Bookmobile Has a Blind Spot

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: mobile-library radio remotes, quiet-zone etiquette, and a bookmobile driver who cannot read the stop signs hidden behind the van*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Ibis, a gibbon radio host; Len, a macaque librarian-driver; Saff, a capuchin reader; and Tavi, an orangutan map clerk.*

[SFX: Van engine idles, page stamp thumps, turn signal ticks.]

IBIS: The Bookmobile is live at its weekly stop, somewhere between the old fig tree and the painted rock. Len, where are we?

LEN: I am parked beside the fig tree.

TAVI: The route map says the stop is by the painted rock.

LEN: I cannot see a painted rock.

SAFF: It is behind the bookmobile.

LEN: That would explain why I cannot see it.

IBIS: Our listeners can still borrow books. Saff, what are you recommending?

SAFF: A mystery about a missing bookmark.

IBIS: Can you summarize it without spoilers?

SAFF: The bookmark is missing.

TAVI: That's the title.

SAFF: The bookmobile has a quiet zone. Please do not reveal the ending.

[SFX: Bell rings as a reader approaches.]

LEN: A reader has arrived. Would you like a book?

READER: I would, but I can't find the van.

LEN: You are standing beside it.

READER: I see a large blank wall.

SAFF: That's the side of the van.

IBIS: The reader is facing the bookmobile's painted side, where the windows are on the other end.

TAVI: The map arrow points the wrong way.

LEN: I turned the map around to match the road.

TAVI: Now north points south.

LEN: The van is still here.

IBIS: The next stop is across the bridge. Len, can we make that on time?

LEN: The bridge is closed to vehicles.

TAVI: The route says "bookmobile crossing."

LEN: That's a footbridge. The van is not a foot.

SAFF: We can carry a book to the far side and keep a small shelf there.

IBIS: A traveling shelf?

SAFF: A bookmobile without wheels.

LEN: That is a pile of books.

[SFX: A stack shifts, then settles.]

TAVI: We can announce that the far-side shelf is temporary and the van will remain on this side.

IBIS: How will readers return books?

SAFF: Leave them with me. I can carry a stack across.

LEN: The crossing is narrow.

SAFF: I can carry one at a time.

IBIS: The bookmobile becomes an inter-branch book relay.

TAVI: The van can't see the far bank, but the map has a second stop marker there.

LEN: I will park in view of the bridge. Readers can borrow from the van or the shelf.

[SFX: Van horn gives two friendly beeps.]

READER: I found the painted rock. It is on the map, on the van, and behind the van.

IBIS: The bookmobile has three painted rocks now.

TAVI: Only one is the stop marker.

SAFF: The mystery book's bookmark was inside the route map.

LEN: Is that a spoiler?

SAFF: It is a solution.

IBIS: The library has found the correct stop, moved the map arrow, and opened a footbridge shelf.

LEN: We are parked beside the fig tree.

TAVI: And the painted rock?

LEN: Still behind us.

[SFX: Page stamp; engine turns off.]

IBIS: The Bookmobile will remain here until every book is returned or the map is facing north again.

SAFF: It is facing south.

TAVI: We have a long afternoon.

SAFF: I found a picture book about a bookmobile that gets lost.

IBIS: Is it about this van?

SAFF: The story says the driver follows a map backward and parks behind a painted rock.

LEN: That is a very specific story.

TAVI: I will add it to the route shelf so the next driver can recognize the blind spot.

LEN: Keep it on the front shelf. The map is still facing south.

[SFX: Readers browse softly as the van door opens.]
