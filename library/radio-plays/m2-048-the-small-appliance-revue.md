# The Small Appliance Revue

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio talent competitions and novelty sound contests*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Fizz, a tamarin; Judge Olo, a macaque mechanic; Judge May, a gibbon musician; contestant Bap, a baboon baker; and contestant Twee, a capuchin who repairs fans.

[SFX: THEME FANFARE, CUT SHORT BY A GRINDING MOTOR.] 

FIZZ: Welcome to The Small Appliance Revue. Contestants present a household machine with an interesting sound. Judges score timing, usefulness, and whether the machine should be plugged in. Olo, first act?

OLO: Bap brought a toaster.

BAP: It pops twice when toast is ready.

MAY: That sounds like a toaster.

BAP: The second pop is dramatic.

[SFX: LEVER CLICKS. TWO CROOKED POPS.] 

FIZZ: A strong entrance.

OLO: The spring is loose, and the cord is cracked. It is unsafe to plug in.

BAP: I can perform unplugged.

[SFX: LEVER CLICKED BY HAND. ONE SMALL POP.] 

MAY: Better timing. No heat.

FIZZ: Twee has a fan.

TWEE: Three speeds. The slow setting hums in B-flat.

[SFX: FAN HUMS, LOW AND STEADY.] 

MAY: I can play along.

[SFX: A SHORT FLUTE PHRASE.] 

FIZZ: The fan is vibrating.

TWEE: The base is loose.

OLO: Turn it off.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. HUM STOPS.] 

TWEE: That was my best note.

OLO: It was the casing.

FIZZ: The audience says the fan wins.

OLO: It cannot run until it is repaired. A nice sound does not make it safe.

MAY: Could it return with a hand crank?

OLO: If the moving parts are guarded.

TWEE: I brought a small crank with a wooden handle.

[SFX: HAND CRANK TURNS, MAKING A SOFT WHIR.] 

MAY: That gives me a steady rhythm.

FIZZ: Can the toaster join?

OLO: As a prop, unplugged.

BAP: It can pop on cue.

MAY: I'll leave a beat after it.

[SFX: HAND CRANK WHIRS. TOASTER LEVER CLICKS. A MANUAL POP, THEN FLUTE ANSWERS.] 

FIZZ: A duet. The crank holds the rhythm, the toaster gives one pop, and the flute takes the melody. No machine is plugged in.

OLO: The toaster still needs a new cord before anyone uses it.

BAP: I'll take it to the repair shop.

TWEE: I'll tighten the fan base and bring it back after Olo checks it.

FIZZ: Judges, final scores?

MAY: Eight for rhythm.

OLO: Ten for stopping when something was unsafe.

FIZZ: That is not how the score board works.

OLO: Then improve the score board.

[SFX: APPLAUSE.] 

FIZZ: The fan receives the sound ribbon, pending repair. The toaster gets a repair ticket. The duet gets an encore. No one takes home a machine that is unsafe to plug in.

BAP: My toaster is a star.

OLO: It is a toaster with a loose spring.

MAY: Stars need maintenance too.

FIZZ: Next week, a kettle that whistles on pitch and a mixer that knows when to stop. Contestants, bring the manual if you still have it.

TWEE: What if the manual is missing?

OLO: Then bring the model number.

BAP: What if the number is missing?

FIZZ: Then bring the machine unplugged.

[SFX: THE HAND CRANK WHIRS IN TIME WITH THE THEME.] 


[SFX: A DOOR OPENS. A REPAIR SHOP BELL RINGS.] 

OLO: The toaster has a replacement cord and a guarded spring. It can be tested at the shop, not on this stage.
BAP: Does it still pop twice?
OLO: Once. The second pop was the loose spring.
MAY: That's a better rhythm.
TWEE: The fan base is tight now. The slow speed is quiet.
FIZZ: Can we bring it back for a sound check?
OLO: Only after the shop clears it.
FIZZ: The judges have changed the rules.
OLO: The rules should include working equipment.
BAP: I brought the toaster manual.
MAY: It has a song on the back.
BAP: It is a warranty card.
FIZZ: Next week, the warranty card performs.
TWEE: Does it need a microphone?
OLO: It needs to stay dry.
[SFX: PAPERS RUSTLE, HAND CRANK TURNS SOFTLY.] 
FIZZ: The Revue accepts one safe sound at a time. Tonight the fan can hum, the toaster can wait, and the warranty card has missed its audition.
[SFX: HAND CRANK TURNS, ONE PAPER PAGE FLIPS.]
