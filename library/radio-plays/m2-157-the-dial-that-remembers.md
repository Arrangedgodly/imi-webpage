# The Dial That Remembers

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: archival history broadcasts, authoritative dates, scratchy recordings, and the temptation to make every fragment sound momentous*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
PROFESSOR MOSS, a gibbon historian
IVA, a capuchin archivist
BECKET, a tamarin station technician

[ARCHIVE THEME. RECORD STATIC.]

PROFESSOR MOSS: Welcome to The Dial That Remembers, where we open a dusty box and find the past speaking directly to us. Today's recording is labeled "Town Hall, 1912, probably."

IVA: The catalog card says 1918. The "probably" is handwritten by you.

PROFESSOR MOSS: A historian must leave room for mystery.

BECKET: The tape is from last Tuesday. It's the building inspection.

PROFESSOR MOSS: An inspection of the town hall in 1912?

BECKET: An inspection of this station's hall. The recording system stores everything on one reel.

[REEL SPINS. A DOOR CREAKS.]

VOICE ON TAPE: The west stair rail is loose. Please repair it before opening the room.

PROFESSOR MOSS: A warning from a forgotten age. The west stair rail, a symbol of civic fragility.

IVA: It's still loose. The inspector wrote "urgent" on the card.

PROFESSOR MOSS: The past has reached across a century to ask for maintenance.

IVA: It reached across six days. The rail is right outside this studio.

[FOOTSTEPS. RAIL RATTLES.]

BECKET: We should stop leaning on it.

PROFESSOR MOSS: Becket, please prepare the next recording. Perhaps we'll hear a founding speech.

[REEL CLICKS. A SMALL BELL RINGS.]

VOICE ON TAPE: I rang the bell at noon. The fruit cart is open. Keep the walkway clear.

PROFESSOR MOSS: A proclamation! The first fruit cart, perhaps, at the dawn of our market culture.

IVA: It's the bell in the station courtyard. We archive the whole building.

PROFESSOR MOSS: Then we can date the cart by the bell's timbre.

IVA: Or by the date on the recording log: Monday.

PROFESSOR MOSS: Monday could be an ancient festival.

IVA: It was two days ago.

[ANOTHER TAPE CLACKS ON. A MONKEY HUMS A TUNE.]

BECKET: This one says "Unknown voice, historic."

IVA: I recognize that humming. It's me sorting boxes.

PROFESSOR MOSS: The archivist sings while preserving the record. How moving.

IVA: I hum because the boxes are heavy.

PROFESSOR MOSS: A work song, then. We have discovered an unrecorded labor tradition.

IVA: You can call it that if you help carry one.

[BOXES SHIFT.]

PROFESSOR MOSS: I will help. But first, one final fragment.

[REEL WHIRS. A VOICE SPEAKS CLEARLY.]

VOICE ON TAPE: If you can hear this, the transmitter is on. Becket, stop recording and come help with the boxes.

BECKET: That's me from this morning. The tape deck was recording again.

PROFESSOR MOSS: The archive has captured a live instruction from the present. History has become urgent.

IVA: The boxes are in the west hall. The rail is loose, so take them through the east door.

PROFESSOR MOSS: We have our resolution: one repaired rail, one clear walkway, and a fruit cart that is not ancient.

BECKET: I've called facilities. They're fixing the rail now.

[DRILL WHIRS, THEN STOPS.]

IVA: It's stable.

PROFESSOR MOSS: We'll close today's broadcast with a true archival item. The station's first theme recording.

[TAPE PLAYS A SCRATCHY VERSION OF THE SAME THEME.]

PROFESSOR MOSS: It sounds different after a century.

BECKET: I recorded it yesterday to test the tape machine.

PROFESSOR MOSS: Then yesterday has aged beautifully.

IVA: Take a box, Professor.

[BOXES SHIFT. A TAPE RECORDER CLICKS ON.]

BECKET: This reel is recording the move.

PROFESSOR MOSS: We are making an archive of the present.

IVA: Label it "Tuesday, oranges, three carriers."

PROFESSOR MOSS: I would prefer "The Great Migration of the Citrus."

BECKET: The recorder is still running.

IVA: It's catching you negotiating a title while carrying fruit.

PROFESSOR MOSS: Then the next generation will know that scholarship is collaborative, and that oranges are heavier than they appear.

PROFESSOR MOSS: I shall carry this document to the archives.

IVA: That is a box of oranges.

PROFESSOR MOSS: History is heavy, and sometimes vitamin-rich.

[FOOTSTEPS LEAVE. THE THEME PLAYS CLEANLY, THEN WITH OLD STATIC.]
