# Twelve Minutes of Quiet

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio silence specials, solemn pauses, and hosts who narrate the absence of sound until it is no longer silent*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
HOST IVY, a capuchin
MEL, a lemur sound producer
GUEST BO, a macaque meditation teacher

[LOW BELL. ROOM TONE.]

IVY: Tonight, Kettle Public Radio presents Twelve Minutes of Quiet. We invite you to sit comfortably and listen without interruption.

MEL: The station clock is set. Twelve minutes begin now.

[PAUSE. A CLOCK TICKS.]

IVY: That was the first sound. A clock, marking the passage of time.

MEL: You've interrupted the quiet.

IVY: I am documenting it.

BO: Listeners can simply hear the room. They don't need a guide to each tick.

[PAUSE. TICKING. A SOFT CHAIR CREAK.]

IVY: A chair has spoken.

MEL: It creaked when you shifted.

IVY: The room is responding to our presence.

BO: Let's try again. Everyone, breathe in. Breathe out. Don't force the silence; leave room for the sounds that are already here.

[LONG PAUSE. A BIRD CHIRPS.]

IVY: A bird.

MEL: Let it be a bird.

[PAUSE. DISTANT TRAFFIC.]

IVY: Vehicles on the road.

BO: Ivy, the point is to listen, not list.

IVY: I'm making the broadcast accessible.

MEL: The quiet special has a sponsor message at minute six.

BO: Does the sponsor need to speak?

MEL: The contract says a message must run.

IVY: Perhaps they sponsor silence.

MEL: The sponsor is Brass Bell Hardware.

IVY: They would like listeners to enjoy a soft, undisturbed evening. Brass Bell Hardware: tools that fit your hand.

[PAUSE. A HAMMER CLANGS FROM OUTSIDE.]

IVY: The sponsor is demonstrating its product.

MEL: That's a neighbor hanging a shelf.

BO: We can include the sound without judging it.

[HAMMER CLANGS TWICE MORE. A DOG BARKS.]

IVY: Three notes from the hammer, followed by a bark in the lower register.

MEL: Ivy.

IVY: I am still quiet. I'm using a whisper.

BO: Silence is not a volume setting. Let's leave the microphone open and stop narrating.

[PAUSE. CLOCK TICKS. HAMMER. BIRD. DISTANT BUS BRAKES.]

IVY: …

MEL: That was a sound.

IVY: I only breathed.

BO: Breathing is allowed. A silent broadcast can carry life without an announcement.

[PAUSE. A PHONE BUZZES.]

MEL: My phone is vibrating. I'll turn it off.

[BUTTON CLICK. PAUSE.]

IVY: The phone has stopped. We have restored the quiet.

BO: You just announced it.

IVY: Then I will not announce it again.

[PAUSE. THE CLOCK TICKS. THE HAMMER STOPS. BIRD CALLS ONCE.]

MEL: We are entering the final minute. The clock is still ticking.

BO: Let us hear it without measuring the minute.

[PAUSE. CLOCK TICKS. A BIRD CALLS ONCE.]

[A BUS PASSES IN THE DISTANCE. A SECOND BIRD ANSWERS. A LEAF BRUSHES THE WINDOW. ROOM TONE HOLDS.]

MEL: The microphone is picking up the outside street, too.

BO: Let it be there.

[PAUSE. THE CLOCK TICKS WITHOUT MUSIC.]

IVY: I almost described that bird.

MEL: You didn't.

IVY: I was listening.

BO: That is enough.

[PAUSE. CLOCK TICKS. THEN STOPS.]

MEL: The twelve minutes are complete.

IVY: The quiet did not stop with the clock.

BO: No. It continues after the broadcast.

MEL: The station signal is still on. We can let the room tone lead into the closing bell.

IVY: Let's do that.

[LOW BELL. ROOM TONE HOLDS FOR TEN SECONDS. THEN MUSIC FADES IN.]

IVY: Thank you for listening to the sounds around you. We heard a clock, a chair, a bird, traffic, a hammer, and one phone that learned to stop.

BO: That was not a list.

IVY: It was the closing credits.

MEL: Our next program is an hour of uninterrupted brass band music.

[A SINGLE TRUMPET BLAST.]

IVY: This has been Twelve Minutes of Quiet. Good night.

[THEME MUSIC.]
