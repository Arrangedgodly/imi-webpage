# The Snowplow Hotline Has a Celebrity Driver

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: winter traffic radio, celebrity field reports, and a live host who mistakes a cleared road for a parade route*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Dispatcher Mara, a macaque; host Olan, a gibbon; driver Tavi, a baboon; road caller Neri, an orangutan; reporter Pip, a capuchin; crew chief Sola, a lemur.

SFX: Radio studio. Wind rattles a window. A snowplow beeps in the distance.

OLAN: The tracking map shows a green line to the north pass, and the town's most famous snowplow driver is joining us live. Tavi, how does it feel to be a local hero?

TAVI: Cold. I am clearing the shoulder before another vehicle gets through.

MARA: The green line tracks the plow, not road status. The pass remains closed pending inspection; when it opens, only emergency vehicles and buses with chains may use it.

PIP: The plow is approaching the ridge now. I can see a line of cars following it.

MARA: Those cars are not part of the convoy. The plow does not make the road safe behind it until the pass is inspected.

OLAN: They are following the celebrity.

TAVI: They are following my flashing beacon. I will pull into the turnout and let them pass only after the road crew checks for ice.

SFX: Plow slows; indicator clicks.

Neri, caller: I am at the lower gate. The sign says CLOSED, but the app says OPEN.

MARA: Which app?

NERI: The Snow Day app. It shows the plow's location and a green road line.

PIP: The green line follows the plow, not the road status.

OLAN: The app thinks the pass is open because the hero is there.

TAVI: I am at the turnout. The road is not open. I have not passed the inspection marker.

MARA: I can update the official route board. The app uses our data feed, but the green line is a vehicle-tracking layer. The CLOSED sign stays in place until the road crew reports conditions.

SFX: Keypad taps, then a confirmation tone.

MARA: The status board now reads CLOSED: INSPECTION IN PROGRESS.

NERI: The phone app still says open.

MARA: That app caches the vehicle track for ten minutes. I will send a direct alert to the lower gate.

OLAN: The celebrity driver's followers are arriving.

TAVI: I am not taking requests. I am checking the plow blade and waiting for the crew.

PIP: The road crew's truck has reached the marker. They are checking the bridge surface.

SFX: Two-way radio clicks.

SOLA, radio: There is black ice over the bridge seam. We are spreading grit now. Keep the gate closed for five minutes.

MARA: Copy. I will update the route board with the hazard.

OLAN: Could we call it a dramatic pause?

MARA: Call it black ice. That is what drivers need to know.

SFX: Grit scatters under tires. A shovel scrapes once.

PIP: The crew has finished spreading grit. The bridge is being tested by the maintenance truck.

TAVI: I am following behind at a safe distance. The plow is not leading civilian traffic yet.

MARA: The bridge test is complete. Road crew reports stable traction. The pass can open to vehicles with chains; buses must wait for the second lane.

OLAN: Is Tavi allowed through?

MARA: Tavi is still working. He will clear the second lane before the gate opens.

NERI: The direct alert just arrived. It says closed for inspection, then updated to open with chains.

PIP: The app is refreshing. The green line is now labeled PLOW TRACK, not ROAD STATUS.

OLAN: We have separated the hero from the hazard report.

TAVI: The second lane is clear. The shoulder is still drifted over, so I am marking it with cones.

MARA: The pass is open under the posted conditions. The gate attendant is lifting the barrier.

SFX: Barrier motor lifts; plow beeps once.

OLAN: The cars are moving.

TAVI: They are moving slowly, in a line, and leaving room for the maintenance truck.

PIP: The celebrity driver has not waved. Both hands are on the wheel.

OLAN: That is good driving. Our star is the road crew.

MARA: Our report is the conditions. The pass is open with chains, the bridge is treated, and the shoulder remains closed.

TAVI: I will finish the shoulder after traffic clears.

OLAN: The interview is over, but the work continues.

SFX: Plow engine rises; radio fades into weather bulletin.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The north pass is open with chains. Follow the road board, not a green line behind a famous plow.
