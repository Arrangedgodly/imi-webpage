# Ferry Radio, Wrong Shore

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio ferry-route broadcasts, breezy captain updates, and a passenger queue that keeps lining up on the departure side*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Jessa, a macaque ferry announcer; Ulu, a gibbon captain; Orlo, a baboon deckhand; and Rimi, a capuchin passenger.*

[SFX: Harbor water, gulls, engine idling, a bell.]

JESSA: This is the Canopy Ferry service. The eastbound boat will depart from the west shore and arrive at the east shore.

RIMI: I am on the east shore. Should I board the eastbound boat?

ORLO: Yes, when it reaches you.

RIMI: The sign says "Eastbound line starts here."

JESSA: The sign is facing east.

ULU: We are currently at the west shore. The boat has not departed.

JESSA: Thank you, Captain. We will update the announcement.

[SFX: Microphone click.]

JESSA: The eastbound boat is at the west shore. Please board here if you are traveling east.

RIMI: I am on the east shore.

ORLO: You need the boat coming west.

RIMI: The boat is going east.

ORLO: It will come west after turning around.

JESSA: The schedule uses the direction the boat travels after leaving each shore.

RIMI: That is clear after the boat leaves.

ULU: We are casting off. Orlo, count the passengers.

ORLO: Six on board, four on the dock, one on the ramp.

JESSA: The ramp is part of the dock.

ORLO: The passenger is holding both railings.

RIMI: I am checking which shore I am on.

ULU: The boat is not moving until the ramp is clear.

[SFX: One quiet step, then the ramp lifts.]

JESSA: Ferry is underway. Passengers may enjoy the view of both shores.

RIMI: I can see the east shore from here. Is that where we are going?

ULU: Yes.

RIMI: It looks like the west shore from this angle.

ORLO: That is because you are looking back.

JESSA: The ferry will arrive in three minutes. Passengers transferring to the westbound boat should wait at the westbound sign.

RIMI: I see two signs. One says westbound; one says turn around.

ORLO: The turn-around sign is for the ferry.

JESSA: We need to move the signs apart.

[SFX: Rope creaks, bell rings.]

ULU: We have reached the east shore. Keep the ramp clear while passengers disembark.

RIMI: I have arrived at the east shore.

JESSA: Correct.

RIMI: The sign says I am at the westbound line.

ORLO: You are. The westbound line leaves from the east shore.

RIMI: So the line is eastbound or westbound depending on the boat?

JESSA: The line is named for the boat's direction, not the shore.

RIMI: I will wait at the sign that says westbound.

ULU: Good. The return ferry departs after all passengers have crossed.

ORLO: We have five waiting on the east dock, including one passenger who has been here since the first announcement.

RIMI: That is me. I have crossed twice.

JESSA: The service has successfully moved you from one shore to the other and back.

RIMI: I meant to visit the market.

ORLO: The market is on the east shore.

RIMI: I am on the east shore.

ULU: You have arrived.

[SFX: Bell, gangway lowers.]

JESSA: Ferry service is complete. The market is on this side. Please leave the westbound queue clear.

RIMI: I am going to the market now.

ORLO: It is beside the dock.

RIMI: I see it.

[SFX: Footsteps move away from the ramp.]

JESSA: This is Canopy Ferry. The boat will keep crossing until the signs agree.

RIMI: I will put a note on the market: "East shore. Walk straight from the ferry."

JESSA: That is clearer than the sign.

ORLO: We can paint an arrow from the ramp to the market gate.

ULU: And point the westbound queue toward the return ferry.

RIMI: The market has fruit, so I will not board another boat.

JESSA: Excellent. We have delivered one passenger to the correct destination.

[SFX: Engine idles, then water laps against the hull.]
