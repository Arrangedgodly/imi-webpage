# The Station That Played Itself

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: automated radio programming, scheduled music blocks, and an overnight engineer trapped between pre-recorded announcements*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Evi, a macaque overnight engineer; Modo, an orangutan station manager; Click, the recorded announcer; and Lell, a capuchin caller.*

[SFX: Station clock ticks; a relay clicks.]

EVI: The station is on automatic programming. Music plays on the hour, announcements at the half hour, and I only touch the board if something fails.

MODO: Nothing will fail. The system was designed to run unattended.

EVI: You called me to attend it.

MODO: For reassurance.

CLICK: Good evening, listeners. This is your automatic announcer, Click.

EVI: It is 10 p.m. The schedule says a lullaby.

[SFX: Soft music begins; a drum solo interrupts.]

EVI: That is the noon parade recording.

MODO: The playlist must have shifted.

EVI: It is playing tomorrow's noon slot.

CLICK: Good morning, listeners. This is your automatic announcer, Click.

MODO: It thinks it is morning.

EVI: The clock says ten at night.

MODO: Maybe the clock is early.

[SFX: Phone rings.]

LELL: The station just announced that the parade is starting. I am in my pajamas.

EVI: It is not starting. That recording aired by mistake.

LELL: Should I put on my parade sash?

MODO: No. Keep your pajamas.

CLICK: Please remain in your parade position until further notice.

EVI: That line is not in the script.

MODO: It is a pre-recorded emergency backup.

EVI: Why does the emergency backup mention a parade?

MODO: The old station had many parades.

LELL: I have found the schedule sheet. The parade recording is assigned to the 10 p.m. slot.

EVI: Why?

LELL: The sheet is upside down.

MODO: Turn it around.

EVI: Then the lullaby is at noon.

MODO: Correct the schedule and restart the sequence.

[SFX: Keys click, a tape rewinds.]

EVI: The lullaby is loaded. The announcement is next.

CLICK: Good night, listeners. The parade has ended.

LELL: I haven't left my house.

EVI: This is a different tape.

MODO: The system combines the last announcement in one program with the next song in the next.

EVI: It has combined every announcement since midnight.

[SFX: Several recorded voices overlap: "Good morning"; "parade position"; "good night."]

LELL: It sounds like the station is arguing with itself.

EVI: I will switch to live mode.

MODO: The system was designed to run unattended.

EVI: It needs one awake engineer.

MODO: We can update the playlist after the hour.

EVI: The scheduled show is a weather bulletin.

CLICK: Good night, listeners. Tomorrow's weather will be mostly pajamas.

LELL: I am going to bed.

EVI: Good idea. The actual forecast is clear skies.

MODO: And the parade?

EVI: Next week at noon.

MODO: I'll update the schedule.

EVI: Turn the page right side up first.

[SFX: Paper flips; the station clock chimes.]

CLICK: Good night, listeners. This has been your automatic announcer, Click, with a corrected schedule.

LELL: The station is finally quiet.

[SFX: Music begins softly; one relay clicks off.]

EVI: The lullaby is playing. The clock agrees. I am going home.

MODO: The system is unattended now.

EVI: No, you are watching it.

MODO: I am staying at the console. How do I switch back to the schedule view?

EVI: Press the square marked LIST, then confirm the time before you load the tape.

MODO: The square marked LIST says LOOP.

EVI: That is the other square.

[SFX: Button clicks; playlist scrolls.]

MODO: I see the 10 p.m. lullaby, the midnight weather bulletin, and the noon parade.

EVI: Good. Read each start time aloud before loading it.

MODO: 10 p.m., lullaby. Midnight, weather. Noon, parade.

EVI: Correct. Save the schedule.

[SFX: Confirmation tone.]

MODO: The system is ready to run unattended.

EVI: You will remain here until the morning check.

MODO: I am the station manager. I can watch a clock.

EVI: That is why we hired an engineer.

[SFX: Lullaby resolves; station hum continues.]
