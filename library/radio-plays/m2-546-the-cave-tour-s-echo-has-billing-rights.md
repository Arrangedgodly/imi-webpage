# The Cave Tour's Echo Has Billing Rights

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: legal talk shows that treat an echo as an uncredited performer*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; cave guide Mara, a macaque; acoustics keeper Odo, a gibbon; ranger Pip, a capuchin; caller Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Cave entrance. Gravel underfoot; a telephone rings in the booth.

VEX: We have a labor dispute at the cavern. The echo repeats every word without receiving credit.

MARA: The echo is a sound reflection. It is not a staff member.

ODO: The tour route ends at the low chamber and returns along the same marked path. The roped passage beyond it is closed to visitors.

PIP: Please keep voices soft. Loud calls can disturb the bats deeper in the cave.

NARI, on phone from the entrance: I am waiting with the next tour group. Does the ranger have permission to use the echo?

VEX: We could put a microphone at the wall and pay it by the repeat.

MARA: We are not recording a performance. We are giving a geology tour. Nari, you can hang up and join us before we enter.

SFX: Phone closes; gate swings open; gravel footsteps approach.

NARI: I'm with the group now. The sign says the chamber returns sound.

ODO: The low chamber returns about half a second of speech. The shape of the rock makes it carry. I can explain that on the tour card.

VEX: Can I ask the echo for its contract terms?

MARA: No shouting and no repeated calls. We'll do one quiet demonstration from the marked line, then continue along the route.

PIP: The group is behind the rope. The bat passage stays closed.

VEX: I will conduct the demonstration.

MARA: Let Nari try. She asked the question and knows where to stand.

SFX: The group reaches the low chamber along the marked route. Nari takes one step onto the marked stone and says, quietly, "hello." A gentle echo returns, then fades.

NARI: I heard my word come back. No one needs to call again.

VEX: The cavern has repeated the caller's greeting.

MARA: It returned a sound. The terms are not negotiated.

ODO: That is the whole acoustic effect. We can now turn back toward the entrance on the marked path.

VEX: But the echo's testimony...

PIP: We are not proceeding deeper to find another one. Keep to the lit route.

SFX: Group walks back along gravel. One backpack buckle taps the wall.

PIP: Please move the bag away from the rock face. Scraping the wall can break off small pieces.

VEX: The cave has interrupted our legal proceedings.

MARA: The bag did. Wait until the group is still before we continue.

NARI: Should I ask a second question?

MARA: No. We heard the one demonstration and are returning. No need to call into the cave again.

ODO: The visitors are clear of the narrow ledge. Keep the single file line until the entrance widens.

SFX: Footsteps slow; then widen across gravel.

VEX: The audience has been denied a callback.

MARA: The cave is not a studio. The tour card will explain the reflection; it will not claim there is a hidden speaker.

NARI: I understand the difference now. The sound came back because of the rock shape, not because someone answered.

VEX: The tour card could list the echo as a co-host.

ODO: The card lists the chamber's geology. There is no hidden speaker or prerecorded track here; the sound comes from the cave walls.

MARA: We have explained what the group heard. The card will not invent a character to make the sound more dramatic.

PIP: The rope is still in place. No one crossed into the bat passage.

NARI: I will tell the next visitors that we heard one return, then came back to the entrance.

PIP: The group is at the entrance. The bat area remains undisturbed.

ODO: Tour complete. I'll record that the route was clear and the group used one quiet demonstration.

VEX: No contract was signed?

MARA: There was no worker to contract with.

SFX: Gate closes gently; cave ambience fades.

TAG

VEX: I waive the echo's fee.

MARA: There was no fee to waive.
