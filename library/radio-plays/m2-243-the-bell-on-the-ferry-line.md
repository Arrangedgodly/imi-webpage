# The Bell on the Ferry Line

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: serialized detective broadcasts, ominous clue recaps, suspicious witness interviews, and a stolen bell that never left the pier*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
DETECTIVE UMA, a macaque
REPORTER PELL, a capuchin
FERRY KEEPER DON, a gibbon
NIGHT WATCHER SERA, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: LOW RADIO THEME. FOGHORN. DISTANT BELL RINGS TWICE.]

PELL: Tonight on The Bell on the Ferry Line: a harbor bell has vanished, four witnesses disagree on what they heard, and Detective Uma has returned to the pier with the same notebook.

UMA: The notebook is dry this time.

PELL: A new development.

[SFX: PIER BOARDS CREAK.]

DON: The bell was right here at sunset. I heard it ring once.

SERA: It rang twice.

DON: One ring and an echo.

SERA: An echo does not have a second clapper.

UMA: Was the bell attached to its post?

DON: It was attached to the rope.

UMA: That is not the same thing.

[SFX: ROPE CREAKS. BELL RINGS ONCE.]

PELL: The missing bell has spoken.

UMA: That is the spare bell.

PELL: The spare bell has a voice?

SERA: All bells have a voice. Some are less expensive.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC.]

PELL: In the last broadcast, we learned that Don saw a shadow cross the pier, Sera heard a splash, and Detective Uma found a trail of wet footprints leading toward the snack cart.

UMA: The snack cart was being washed.

PELL: A convenient alibi.

UMA: For a cart?

PELL: We follow every lead.

[SFX: CART WHEEL SQUEAKS.]

DON: The ferry rope is wet. The tide pulled the bell under the pier.

UMA: The bell is not missing. It is below us.

PELL: A submerged suspect.

SERA: I tied it to the post so the boats could hear the line in fog. The rope has slipped off the hook.

UMA: Then why did you report it stolen?

SERA: I did not. I reported it missing. The headline said stolen.

PELL: Headlines are shorter.

UMA: And the rope has a knot?

DON: It has three.

[SFX: ROPE TUGS. BELL CLANGS UNDER PIER.]

PELL: We have a sound from below. Could the thief be under the dock?

UMA: It's the bell.

PELL: We cannot rule out a bell accomplice.

SERA: Detective, I can reach the rope from the ladder.

UMA: Wait for the tide to ease. We need the hook before pulling.

[SFX: LADDER PLACED. WATER LAPS. METAL HOOK SCRAPES.]

SERA: I have the rope.

DON: Pull slowly.

[SFX: ROPE TIGHTENS. BELL RINGS TWICE. A SPLASH.]

PELL: The bell is surfacing!

UMA: That's the fender.

PELL: It has the same tone.

UMA: It is a buoy.

SERA: The bell is still under the boards.

[SFX: ROPE SHIFTS. BELL CLANGS LOUDLY.]

DON: There.

[SFX: A METAL BELL IS LIFTED ONTO PIER.]

PELL: Detective, you solved it.

UMA: Sera tied the knot. Don heard the echo. The tide pulled the rope. The bell stayed here the whole time.

PELL: A case without a criminal.

UMA: There was a loose hook.

SERA: And a headline that made it sound like a crime.

SERA: The ferry crew has moved the hook above the waterline.

DON: I have tied the spare bell to the lower cleat.

UMA: Now either bell can warn the boats.

PELL: The investigation concludes with a second bell.

UMA: The investigation concludes with a knot.

PELL: The next broadcast will investigate the hook.

UMA: The hook is old.

PELL: It has a past.

[SFX: BELL RINGS. FERRY HORN ANSWERS.]

DON: We will tie it to the upper cleat and let the ferry use the spare.

UMA: Two bells. Two ropes. No mystery.

PELL: We can still call it a mystery.

UMA: Call it "maintenance."

PELL: That title will not fit the theme.

[SFX: RADIO THEME RETURNS.]

PELL: The bell on the ferry line is back. The tide remains under investigation.

UMA: It has an alibi.

[SFX: BELL RINGS ONCE. FOGHORN FADES.]
