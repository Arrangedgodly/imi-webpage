# Vines Between Us

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Serialized radio soap operas and melodramatic family secrets*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Neri, a macaque daughter; Sela, her mandrill grandmother; Suri, her tree-shrew mother; narrator, an overly invested capuchin.

[THEME: SWELLING VIOLINS, A DOOR OPENS, A SINGLE COCONUT DROPS.]

NARRATOR: In the village of Palmgate, the harvest bell rang before sunrise. Grandmother Sela stood at the bridge with a basket of guavas and a letter she had hidden for twenty years.

SELA: If I give this to Neri, everything changes.

NERI: Grandmother, the bridge changes every time the rain comes. What is in the letter?

SELA: Your mother's handwriting.

[MUSIC STING.] 

NARRATOR: Neri, a young macaque, took the letter. Her mother had left Palmgate to follow the river north, and the family had told Neri she never wrote. The paper smelled of cedar and damp stone.

NERI: "I will come back when the lower bridge is safe." She was waiting for the bridge?

SELA: She was waiting for me to ask her to stay.

NERI: You could have sent this.

SELA: I was afraid she would come back and leave again.

[SFX: BRIDGE ROPE CREAKS. A TROPICAL RAIN SHOWER APPROACHES.] 

NARRATOR: At that moment, the bridge rope snapped. The guava basket swung over the water. Neri sprang, caught the handle with her tail, and landed on the bank. Grandmother Sela grabbed the letter before it flew away.

NERI: That bridge needs new rope before the market opens.

SELA: The council won't replace it until the river falls.

NERI: Then we will close it and build the footpath around the ridge.

SELA: Your mother said the ridge path was too steep.

NERI: She was a tree shrew. I'm a macaque. I climb differently.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON STONE, THEN A KNOCK AT A WOODEN DOOR.] 

NARRATOR: The path led to a cottage by the upper spring. A traveler opened the door. It was Neri's mother, Suri, older and broader, carrying a pack of bridge rope. She had returned the previous evening, but no one had told Neri.

SURI: I heard the bell. I thought it was for me.

SELA: I never should have kept the letter.

SURI: You kept it because you were scared. I was scared too. I didn't know if I had a home here.

NERI: You have one. The lower bridge is out, though.

SURI: I brought rope.

NARRATOR: They did not embrace. Not yet. Suri laid the rope on a bench. Sela unfolded the letter and read the last line aloud: "Tell Neri the guava tree is hers if she wants it."

NERI: I want the tree. I don't want its fruit sent in a basket instead of a person.

SURI: Fair.

SELA: The first guava is yours. The next one we eat together.

[SFX: RAIN STARTS. THE BRIDGE SWAYS IN THE WIND.] 

NARRATOR: The family moved to the ridge path. Neri climbed ahead and cleared wet leaves from each foothold. Suri tied the first new rope around the old bridge post. Sela watched her hands and offered the knot she had taught Suri as a child.

SURI: I remember.

SELA: Then tie it your way.

NARRATOR: Together they closed the lower bridge, posted a sign, and carried the guavas up the safe trail. Neri invited her mother to the harvest supper. Suri said yes, then asked whether she could bring the rope.

NERI: Bring the rope. We may need it again.

NARRATOR: The river ran high. The letter was no longer a secret. At supper, Sela placed the first guava in the middle of the table. Three hands reached for it. The fruit fell, split, and rolled into a cooking pot.

SELA: That is one way to share.

[MUSIC SWELLS. A LADLE CLINKS THE POT.] 

NARRATOR: Next time on Vines Between Us, Suri meets the council, Neri chooses which branch to prune, and the guava soup may be sweeter than anyone planned. Tonight, the bridge is closed, the path is open, and the family is sitting together.

NERI: Mother, you can sit beside me.

SURI: I was hoping you would say that.

SELA: I saved the warm place by the fire.

SURI: I thought the warm place was for the soup.

SELA: The soup can sit on a stool.

NERI: The stool is missing a leg.

SELA: Then the soup can hold it steady.

[THEME RESOLVES, NOT A CLIFFHANGER. END.]
