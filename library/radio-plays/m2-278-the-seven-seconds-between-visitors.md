# The Seven Seconds Between Visitors

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio-tour conventions, dramatic narration, and curators who must label an exhibit that has no sound at all*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
GUIDE NARA, a gibbon
CURATOR LUMA, a lemur
TECH OMI, an orangutan
VISITOR KITO, a capuchin

[SFX: GALLERY ROOM TONE, HEADPHONES BEEP.]

NARA: Welcome to the Museum of Unfinished Sounds. Stop three is the silent shell: a small wooden box that has never made a sound.

KITO: Then why is it in a sound museum?

LUMA: We are preserving the idea of a sound that was never recorded.

OMI: It is an empty box.

NARA: The audio tour asks visitors to imagine what might be inside.

KITO: I imagine a smaller empty box.

LUMA: That is a valid response.

Nara presses the headphone button. It plays seven seconds of silence, followed by a soft click.

[SFX: SILENCE, CLICK.]

KITO: The box made the click.

OMI: The tour button made the click.

NARA: We have a new exhibit.

LUMA: No. We have a technical fault.

Omi checks the button. The audio file contains silence, but the headphone jack has a loose connection. Nara asks whether the click can be added to the label as a historical sound.

LUMA: The click was not made by the box.

KITO: It was made while I was listening to the box.

NARA: The visitor experience includes the click.
 
Luma asks Nara to stop the tour and record a clean version. Nara reads the revised description while Omi monitors the levels.

NARA: The empty box is sealed. The track contains seven seconds of silence. This room contains a fan, visitors, and the guide.

KITO: You forgot the headphones.

NARA: And headphones.

Omi plays it back. The file is quiet, but a visitor coughs in the gallery during the recording.

[SFX: SINGLE COUGH ON HEADPHONES.]

KITO: The cough is in the silence now.

OMI: We can record it again after closing.

LUMA: Or label it as a visitor recording.

NARA: We cannot identify the visitor.

KITO: Then it is an anonymous cough.

Luma writes a note for the archive and asks the visitor whether they consent to the recording. They do not. Omi deletes the take, and the clean track remains untouched.


OMI: The equipment is not the artifact.

Kito moves to the next station: a wax cylinder labeled "Rain on a Roof, Recording Stopped Before Rain." The tour narrator describes a storm that never arrives.

NARA: Imagine the roof trembling. Imagine the drops gathering.

[SFX: HEADPHONE STATIC.]

KITO: The static sounds like rain.

OMI: It is the headphone jack again.

LUMA: The museum is giving us an accidental sound collection.

Omi disconnects the defective headphones. The click vanishes. Nara tries to read a new script with no imagined details.

NARA: This box is silent. It contains no object. The recording is seven seconds long.

KITO: That is less dramatic.

LUMA: It is accurate.

NARA: The silence needs context.

LUMA: The context is that we are listening.

Kito asks to hear the silence again. Omi routes the file to a working speaker. They wait. A ventilation fan hums in the gallery.

[SFX: SEVEN SECONDS OF ROOM TONE.]

KITO: I heard the fan.

OMI: It is part of this room, not the recording.

NARA: Can the tour say, "The museum is quiet, but not silent"?

LUMA: Yes.

KITO: Can it say the box is waiting to be opened?

LUMA: It is sealed for preservation.

NARA: I will not say that.

The gallery opens for visitors. A child asks if there is a sound inside. Kito begins to answer, but Nara plays the clean seven-second track. The room goes still. When the silence ends, the child whispers "nothing."

KITO: That is what I heard.

LUMA: It is what the exhibit holds.

NARA: The label is ready: "A recording of silence, surrounded by a room."

Omi places the headphones on a hook and marks the faulty jack for repair. The click returns when the cord brushes the stand.

[SFX: CLICK.]

NARA: That one is the cable.

KITO: The museum has two sounds now.

LUMA: One is the exhibit. One is maintenance.

NARA: We will put both on the map, with different labels.

[SFX: TOUR BEEP, QUIET ROOM TONE.]
