# The Night Swap Shop

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night swap-shop broadcasts, callers describing mystery objects, and a host who overvalues every item with a funny sound*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST BERA, a gibbon
CALLER DOV, a macaque
CALLER TALI, a capuchin
SOUND TECH RIN, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: RADIO THEME. DESK BELL.]

BERA: Welcome to the Night Swap Shop. Describe an object you want to trade, but do not reveal what it is until our sound technician guesses.

RIN: Why am I guessing?

BERA: You have good headphones.

DOV: I have a small metal object that makes a deep sound.

[SFX: DOV TAPS OBJECT. LOW CLUNK.]

RIN: A pot lid.

DOV: It is not a pot lid.

BERA: We will open the line for offers.

TALI: I can trade a wooden object that makes a high sound.

[SFX: TALI TAPS OBJECT. HIGH TICK.]

RIN: A spoon.

TALI: It is not a spoon.

BERA: The callers have agreed to swap.

DOV: I have not agreed.

BERA: You are on a swap show.

DOV: I am on a radio show. The object is still in my hand.

BERA: We have a listener poll to settle disputes. Press one to trade, two to keep your object, three to hear the chair.

[SFX: THREE KEYS BEEP. THE CHAIR SQUEAKS LOUDER.]

RIN: Someone pressed three.

TALI: It is the best sound on the program.

BERA: The chair is now our most requested item.

DOV: I would trade the bell for that chair.

BERA: The chair is not for sale.

RIN: I can trade the wheel on the chair.

BERA: A chair wheel is not a chair.

RIN: It is one fifth of a chair.

BERA: Four fifths remain at the station.

TALI: Could I trade my fan for the wheel?

RIN: Then the chair will not roll.

BERA: The show is producing consequences.

RIN: The wheel is already loose. If we remove it, the chair will sit still.

[SFX: WRENCH TURNS. WHEEL ROLLS ACROSS FLOOR.]

DOV: It rolled right into my booth.

BERA: That is a delivery. The shop has completed its first transaction.

[SFX: PHONE LINE BEEPS.]

RIN: I can hear a squeak behind the callers.

BERA: That is my chair.

RIN: It sounds like a wheel.

BERA: The chair has a wheel.

DOV: Could I trade for the chair?

BERA: The chair is not for sale.

[SFX: CHAIR SQUEAKS.]

BERA: Our next caller has an object with a soft rustle.

TALI: It is small, blue, and folds in half.

RIN: A napkin.

TALI: It is not a napkin.

BERA: We have three mystery objects and no confirmed object.

DOV: Mine is a bell.

[SFX: SMALL BELL RINGS.]

RIN: That is not metal clunk. That is a bell.

DOV: It has a clapper, so it clunks too.

TALI: Mine is a folding fan.

[SFX: FAN OPENS WITH A SNAP.]

RIN: That rustle was the fan.

BERA: We have now identified both items.

DOV: We have not swapped them.

BERA: The purpose of the show is to connect people.

TALI: I can trade the fan for the bell.

DOV: I need the bell for my door.

TALI: I need the fan for my window.

BERA: A perfect swap.

RIN: The window already has a fan.

TALI: That is a paper fan. Mine folds and opens.

BERA: We have uncovered a secondary object.

[SFX: PAPER FAN FLUTTERS.]

DOV: I will trade the bell for the folding fan, but I want the paper fan too.

TALI: That is not fair.

BERA: The program has become a negotiation.

RIN: I have a chair with a wheel.

BERA: The chair is not for sale.

RIN: It squeaks.

BERA: We are not swapping the chair.

[SFX: CHAIR SQUEAKS. PAUSE.]

BERA: The callers have made a trade: bell for folding fan. The paper fan stays with Tali. The chair remains at the desk.

DOV: That is an acceptable outcome.

TALI: I can open the fan without making a sound.

[SFX: FAN OPENS QUIETLY.]

RIN: The chair made a sound anyway.

BERA: The swap shop is closed. Tune in next week to identify the chair.

[SFX: RADIO THEME. CHAIR SQUEAKS.]
