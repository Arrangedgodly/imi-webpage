# Why the Beetle Blinks

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Science explainer podcasts with experiments and expert interviews*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Kavi, a bonobo reporter; Dr. Umbo, a proboscis monkey entomologist; Pip, a tarsier assistant who mistakes batteries for seeds; beetles, the unbothered subjects.

[SFX: NIGHT INSECTS. A SMALL CAMERA CLICKS.]

HOST KAVI: Welcome to Field Notes at Night. I'm Kavi, a bonobo science reporter. Tonight's question came from a listener in the lower canopy: why do fireflies blink in pairs after rain?

DR. UMBO: We don't know that they always do. We know one species in this valley flashes twice when the air is damp. The second flash may help a mate identify the species through the leaves.

KAVI: How can we tell whether the pair is a signal or just a glitch in the light?

UMBO: We need to compare groups with and without leaves between them. We also need to avoid shining bright lamps at the insects. Light can change their behavior.

KAVI: The night survey begins at the fern bridge. I have a red-filter lamp, two sound meters, and one assistant who has already tried to eat the batteries.

ASSISTANT PIP: I thought they were dried seeds.

KAVI: They were labeled "AA."

PIP: Seeds can have names.

[SFX: LEAVES SHIFT. A DROP OF WATER FALLS INTO A CUP.] 

KAVI: We have set one listening station under an open branch and another behind a curtain of leaves. Umbo marks each flash with a soft clicker. Pip writes the time on a slate. I hold still. As a bonobo, I want to reach for things. Tonight the things are tiny, bright, and best left alone.

UMBO: First flash. Pause. Second flash. There again.

KAVI: The open station sees the same pattern. The shaded station sees it too, but the second blink arrives later.

PIP: Maybe the leaves are slowing the light.

UMBO: Leaves don't slow light enough for us to hear a pause. The beetle may have moved behind a leaf before flashing again.

KAVI: We are recording the direction as well as the timing. This is the part the diagram leaves out. A flashing beetle is an animal with wings, not a lamp attached to a stick.

[SFX: WINGS HUM NEAR THE MICROPHONE. PIP GASPS.] 

PIP: Something landed on the meter.

UMBO: Don't swat it. That's our beetle.

KAVI: Its first flash points down the branch. The second points toward the stream. It has turned mid-flight.

UMBO: That suggests the paired pattern might help another beetle track movement, not just read species. We need more nights to test it.

KAVI: What would prove the idea wrong?

UMBO: If the second flash points in no consistent direction, or if beetles flash twice when no other beetles can see them. We should not start with the answer we like.

PIP: I like "secret conversation."

UMBO: It is a nice phrase. It is not evidence.

KAVI: The rain has stopped. We are moving the shaded station to the stream edge, where there is an open line of sight. Pip has put the batteries back in their case. He has also labeled them "not seeds."

PIP: I learn from mistakes.

[SFX: A FROG CALLS. THE METER BEEPS.] 

KAVI: New observation. A second firefly answered from the opposite bank, one flash, then two. The first beetle changed course. It flew toward the ferns, not the stream.

UMBO: That's useful. It suggests the pair may carry direction or identity. It doesn't tell us which yet.

KAVI: We will repeat the survey tomorrow with the cameras pointed away from the insects and a second team recording from the upper bank. Tonight we learned that the two blinks travel with the beetle, and that a quiet experiment can still surprise you.

PIP: Also that batteries are not seeds.

KAVI: That finding is ready for publication.

UMBO: In the equipment log.

PIP: I have labeled the recorder "not a seed."

KAVI: That is a responsible correction.

UMBO: The recorder is not food either.

PIP: I was not going to eat it.

KAVI: You are holding it like a snack.

PIP: It is small enough to be a snack.

UMBO: It costs more than our field tent.

PIP: I will put it down.

KAVI: Please put it on the table, not in the seed basket.

KAVI: Field Notes at Night will return after one more round of careful observing. The beetles are still blinking. We are leaving them to it.

[INSECTS HUM. TWO SOFT FLASH CLICKS. MUSIC OUT.]
