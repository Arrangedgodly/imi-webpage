# The Neighborhood Auction Calls Lot One Wrong

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live auction radio, escalating bids, and auctioneers who confuse two nearly identical folding chairs*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Auctioneer Pali, a macaque; host Vex, an orangutan; volunteer Juno, a gibbon; bidder Toma, a baboon; caller Riri, a capuchin.

SFX: Community hall. A wooden gavel taps; chairs scrape.

VEX: Welcome to the neighborhood auction. The proceeds will repair the community hall roof. Lot One is a folding chair with a blue stripe, starting at five tokens.

PALI: Five tokens, do I hear six?

TOMA: Six.

JUNO: Wait. The blue-striped chair is the one we use for the registration desk. It is not for sale.

PALI: Then what is Lot One?

JUNO: The folding chair beside it, with the blue stripe painted on the back. The desk chair has a blue cushion.

VEX: We have two chairs and one blue detail. Our auction is already a puzzle.

PALI: I have an inventory card. It says CHAIR, BLUE, LOT ONE.

JUNO: That card is for the desk chair. I made a second card that says LOT TWO.

SFX: Card flutters to floor.

TOMA: I bid six for whichever one is not the desk chair.

PALI: We cannot sell an unidentified chair.

VEX: The bidding is paused. This is a live broadcast, so we will describe the object before we take another offer.

RIRI, by phone: I am at home listening. Are either chairs broken?

JUNO: No. Both fold. One has a blue cushion; the other has a blue stripe painted on its back. The striped one is donated.

RIRI: I can hear the stripe chair squeak when you move it.

SFX: Juno folds one chair. It squeaks. A second chair unfolds with no sound.

JUNO: The squeak is the desk chair's hinge. The donation chair is quiet.

PALI: Then the blue stripe chair is quiet, and the blue cushion chair stays here.

VEX: Let's verify with the donor's photograph.

SFX: Paper is unfolded.

JUNO: The photo shows a chair with a stripe down the back, next to a window. The hall window is on the east wall. The chair by the east wall is the quiet one.

TOMA: I am six tokens for the quiet chair.

PALI: The sale is not open yet. We need to place the correct tag.

JUNO: I will tag the chair's frame, not its cushion. The tag says LOT ONE. The registration chair gets a red KEEP tag.

SFX: String is tied; paper tag taps metal.

VEX: We are resuming. Lot One: the donated folding chair with a blue painted stripe across its back, quiet hinge, tagged with a paper label. It is not the blue-cushion chair.

PALI: Six tokens. Do I hear seven?

RIRI: Seven.

TOMA: Eight.

RIRI: Nine.

PALI: Nine tokens. Going once, going twice--

JUNO: The tag is on the right chair. I can see it from the desk.

VEX: Sold to Riri by phone for nine tokens.

SFX: Gavel taps once. Applause.

RIRI: I will collect it after the broadcast. Please don't send the chair home with the tag.

JUNO: We will hold it beside the door and check your receipt.

TOMA: Can I bid on the other chair?

PALI: The blue-cushion chair belongs to the registration desk.

TOMA: Could we auction the cushion?

JUNO: No. It is sewn onto the chair.

VEX: The auction is for donated property. We do not remove useful parts to create more lots.

PALI: Then Lot Two is a box of unmatched teaspoons.

JUNO: There are matching pairs in that box.

PALI: I will let the inventory card finish before I announce it.

VEX: An excellent procedural reform.

SFX: Card is read; spoons clink softly.

PALI: Lot Two, a set of eight teaspoons, all matching, minimum three tokens.

TOMA: I bid three.

VEX: The next lot is correctly identified before bidding begins. The roof fund has gained twelve tokens, and nobody has bought the registration desk.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The quiet blue-stripe chair goes to Riri, and the blue cushion chair keeps the sign-in sheet steady.
