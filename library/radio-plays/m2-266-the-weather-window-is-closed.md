# The Weather Window Is Closed

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio weather bulletins with indoor demonstrations, studio window reporters, and forecasters who treat every sound as evidence*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST TERA, a capuchin
FORECASTER MOK, a macaque
ENGINEER LON, a gibbon
CALLER FAI, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: WEATHER THEME. WINDOW HANDLE TURNS, DOES NOT OPEN.]

TERA: Welcome to Weather Window. Tonight we will forecast the wind by opening the studio window.

MOK: It is locked.

LON: The handle is painted shut.

TERA: Then describe the weather from memory.

MOK: The forecast calls for light rain, a crosswind, and temperatures dropping after sunset.

TERA: Can we hear the rain?

[SFX: RAIN ON GLASS.]

LON: That is the roof microphone.

TERA: It is raining on the roof microphone?

LON: I am tapping a wet cloth against the glass to demonstrate rain.

MOK: That is not a measurement.

TERA: It is a sound effect.

MOK: Sound effects can be informative.

TERA: Can we hear the wind?

[SFX: LON BLOWS ACROSS A BOTTLE.]

MOK: That is a bottle.

LON: I can make a stronger gust.

[SFX: BOTTLE HOWLS. PAPER FORECAST FLUTTERS.]

TERA: The forecast has blown off the desk.

MOK: The wind is moving indoors.

LON: The vent is on. The paper was near it.

TERA: We have a local indoor gust advisory.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

TERA: Caller Fai, what is the weather outside?

FAI: It is clear, but windy. My hat just blew onto the porch.

MOK: Secure loose objects before the wind picks up.

TERA: Is the wind picking up now?

FAI: My hat is under the bench. It is not moving.

LON: We can recreate the gust with the vent.

MOK: Do not turn the vent up. It will blow our sound panels down.

TERA: We need an indoor demonstration.

MOK: Put a ribbon near the door and watch its direction.

[SFX: RIBBON TIES TO HANDLE. IT STREAMS TOWARD THE STUDIO.]

LON: The ribbon points inward.

MOK: That means air is being pulled into the room.

TERA: What is pulling it?

LON: The exhaust fan.

TERA: We have a wind from the ceiling.

MOK: We have airflow from the fan. Outside wind is still moving west.

TERA: The listener deserves both forecasts.

FAI: I can bring my hat inside, but I have to open the door.

MOK: Hold it by the brim.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. WIND RUSHES IN. PAPERS SCATTER.]

TERA: We have our first direct measurement.

MOK: The papers are moving toward the door. The outdoor wind is pushing in.

LON: The ribbon points outside now.

TERA: The window is still closed, but the door has provided a weather window.

FAI: I caught the hat. It is safe.

MOK: Close the door before the papers leave too.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES. PAPERS GATHERED.]

LON: The weather chart has blown under the desk.

TERA: We should retrieve it before issuing the next update.

MOK: I can see the chart. It says west wind, steady rain, and a temperature drop.

TERA: The chart is under the desk. Is that a colder microclimate?

MOK: It is the floor.

FAI: The chart has caught my hat brim.

[SFX: PAPER SLIDES, HAT LIFTS.]

LON: We have recovered both the chart and the hat.

TERA: The hat is now our most mobile instrument.

MOK: It is not an instrument. It is a hat.

TERA: We will keep it away from the open door.

TERA: Our forecast: windy outside, ventilated inside, window closed, door briefly open.

MOK: And rain on the roof, confirmed by the roof microphone.

LON: The cloth is dry now.

TERA: Then our rain demonstration has ended.

FAI: My hat is staying indoors tonight.

MOK: That is a wise response to the wind.

TERA: Weather Window will return when the window can open. Good night.

[SFX: WEATHER THEME OUT. WINDOW HANDLE TURNS; STAYS SHUT.]
