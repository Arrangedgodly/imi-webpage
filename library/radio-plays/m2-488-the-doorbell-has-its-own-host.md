# The Doorbell Has Its Own Host

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: home makeover shows that make an entrance more important than the people inside*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Vex, an orangutan; resident Mara, a macaque; carpenter Odo, a gibbon; neighbor Pip, a capuchin; producer Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Small cottage porch. Doorbell chimes twice.

VEX: Welcome to Doorway Reveal, where the entrance tells your story.

MARA: I just want the bell fixed. It rings twice when someone presses it once.

ODO: The button sticks against the frame.

VEX: The reveal needs a new bell, a painted arch, and a welcome speech.

PIP: I am outside. Can I come in?

VEX: Not until the host announces you.

SELI: The door is open.

MARA: Pip, come in.

SFX: Door opens. Bell chimes twice again.

PIP: It rang while nobody touched it.

ODO: The wire is pinched where the frame swells.

VEX: This is a dramatic doorbell with a mind of its own.

MARA: It's a damp wire.

ODO: I can isolate the circuit and replace the button.

VEX: Will it have a signature sound?

ODO: It will chime once.

VEX: That lacks personality.

PIP: I like one chime. It tells me the door opened.

SELI: The makeover team brought a brass bell with a long rope.

MARA: The rope would hang across the path.

ODO: It would also need someone to pull it.

VEX: A ceremonial pull could mark the reveal.

MARA: My hands are often full of groceries.

SFX: Basket sets down. A jar clinks.

PIP: The old button is at my shoulder height.

ODO: The new one can fit beside it, with a weather cover.

VEX: Could we paint the cover gold?

MARA: Paint it the color of the door, so it doesn't catch rain.

SFX: Screwdriver turns. Wire is freed from frame.

ODO: Circuit is isolated. I'll replace the switch now.

SFX: Old button clicks free. New button clicks into place.

ODO: Testing once.

SFX: Doorbell chimes once.

PIP: I can hear it from the garden.

MARA: And it stops after one ring.

SELI: Before the reveal, we need a test from outside.

PIP: I'll step onto the path and press the button once.

MARA: Wait until Odo confirms the circuit is isolated from the old stuck switch.

ODO: It is disconnected. The new button is wired to the chime only.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps move down the porch.

PIP: I'm at the path. Can you hear me?

MARA: Yes.

PIP: I can see the button. I'm pressing it once.

SFX: One chime.

VEX: The camera missed the button press.

SELI: The microphone caught it.

VEX: We need a second take.

MARA: No. The test already worked. We are not making Pip walk back to the path just to get a prettier shot.

ODO: He can come in now.

SFX: Footsteps approach; door opens.

PIP: One chime, no sticking, and the basket fits through the doorway.

MARA: That's the whole job.

VEX: We still have gold paint in the van.

MARA: Use it on the show's title card. The cottage door stays the color it is.

VEX: The reveal is ready. Seli, cue the music.

SELI: The bell already made the sound.

VEX: Then cue the host.

MARA: The host can hold the grocery basket while I open the door.

ODO: I'll check the frame for the swelling that pinched the old wire.

SFX: Odo presses lightly at the door edge. Wood creaks.

ODO: It needs a small plane along this corner, not a larger arch.

VEX: A plane is a less exciting tool than a brass bell.

MARA: A plane lets the door close. Do that, then I can carry the basket without catching it.

SFX: Hand plane rasps twice. Wood shaving falls.

PIP: The door moves freely now.

SELI: No extra arch, no rope across the path, and one clear chime.

SFX: Basket lifts. Door opens. One clear chime.

PIP: That's a welcome.
