# Sleepcast at the Sawmill

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: soothing sleep podcasts, slow breath cues, and whispered ambient descriptions undermined by a nearby noisy workplace*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
SOLA, a lemur sleepcast host
TUMI, a capuchin sound editor
RAFF, a gibbon sawmill foreman

[SOFT WIND CHIMES. DISTANT SAW WHINES.]

SOLA: Welcome to Drift Down, the sleepcast that helps you settle into calm. Take a slow breath in. Imagine a quiet forest. Let your shoulders soften.

[SAW ROARS.]

TUMI: That's the sawmill next door. They started the night shift early.

SOLA: Imagine a mighty tree becoming a gentle plank.

TUMI: The microphone is clipping.

SOLA: We'll use the sound as a low, steady drone. Inhale for four counts. Exhale for six.

[SAW ROARS AGAIN, THEN STOPS. A BOARD THUMPS.]

SOLA: That was a branch settling.

TUMI: That was a plank landing.

SOLA: The forest floor welcomes it.

[PHONE LINE CLICKS.]

SOLA: We have a listener joining our rest. Raff, are you comfortable?

RAFF: I'm at the sawmill. The crew can hear the broadcast on the break-room radio. They say the breathing exercise is helping them slow down.

SOLA: Wonderful. Let your body become as still as a leaf.

RAFF: We're moving a stack of boards. The forklift is making a backup beep.

[BEEP. BEEP. BEEP.]

SOLA: A small night bird calls from the distance.

TUMI: It is a forklift.

SOLA: Birds have many voices.

RAFF: The forklift is backing into the marked bay. Everyone is clear.

SOLA: Excellent. Safety is peaceful. Now picture a stream moving over smooth stones.

[WATER POURS.]

TUMI: That is the crew washing glue off a bucket.

SOLA: Every stream begins somewhere.

RAFF: We're cleaning up. The night shift has to leave the aisle open.

SOLA: Imagine a soft blanket of moss.

[A BOARD SLIDES AND SCRAPES.]

TUMI: That board has shifted.

RAFF: It's secure. We're adjusting the stack before we close.

SOLA: We will pause the sleepcast until the work area is quiet.

TUMI: If we stop the show, the crew loses the calming sound.

SOLA: We can keep the microphone here and turn off the ambient effects. Raff, can the sawmill finish its run?

RAFF: The last cut is done. I'm switching off the machines now.

[SWITCHES CLICK. THE SAW FADES. SILENCE, THEN A BROOM SWEEPS.] 

SOLA: There. Listen to the quiet that arrives after a job is done. Breathe in gently. Breathe out slowly.

RAFF: The crew is sweeping the floor. The broom sounds like soft rain.

TUMI: It sounds like a broom.

SOLA: It can be both. Tonight's calm is made from real work ending safely.

[SWEEPING STOPS. A DOOR CLOSES.]

RAFF: We're heading home. The boards are stacked, the aisle is clear, and the machines are off.

SOLA: Before you leave, take one quiet breath and notice your feet on the ground.

RAFF: I'm sitting in the truck. My feet are on the floor mat.

SOLA: That is a steady place to rest.

TUMI: The truck radio is picking up our broadcast. The crew can hear the closing cue.

RAFF: I'll turn it low so the driver can still hear the road.

SOLA: Good. Rest begins when the shift ends, and everyone travels home alert.

[ENGINE STARTS QUIETLY.]

TUMI: That's not a sleep sound.

SOLA: It is a departure sound. We'll let it fade naturally.

SOLA: Rest well. Imagine a forest where every tree has a place to stand, and no one asks the saw to become a bird.

TUMI: That last part was for me.

SOLA: Let the chimes take us out.

TUMI: I've lowered the studio volume so the crew can hear the road.

SOLA: A gentle ending is one that knows when to be quiet.

[WIND CHIMES. A SINGLE BROOM BRISTLE SNAPS.]

RAFF: I'll fix that in the morning.

SOLA: And the morning can wait until you wake. This has been Drift Down.

[THEME FADES TO QUIET.]
