# Dedications at Dawn

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sentimental request shows, cryptic dedications, and a host who turns every ordinary message into a fateful reunion*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
LIO, a ring-tailed lemur radio host
BEA, a capuchin caller
NIM, a macaque caller and bakery runner

[SOFT PIANO THEME. A PHONE LINE CLICKS OPEN.]

LIO: You're listening to Heart-to-Heart at Sunrise, where every song request carries a story, and every story gets a soft piano underneath. Who's calling?

BEA: Bea. I want to dedicate "The Little Teapot Waltz" to someone who knows what they did.

LIO: A cryptic message. A kitchen object. We have all the ingredients for a reunion.

BEA: It's to Nim. He borrowed my teapot for the bakery and returned it with a raisin inside.

LIO: A raisin in the teapot. Listeners, we may be hearing a symbol of a love gone dry.

BEA: No. I just want the teapot back before I have guests.

LIO: Bea, when you say "guests," do you mean the people who gather around a table, or the ghosts of old affection?

BEA: People. They drink tea.

[LIO PLAYS TWO NOTES OF A LOVE SONG, THEN STOPS.]

LIO: We'll keep that thought warm. Nim, you're on the line.

NIM: I'm in the bakery. The teapot is here. I didn't know the raisin was inside.

BEA: You could have called me.

NIM: I was rushing bread to the morning market. The teapot was packed with the cinnamon buns so it wouldn't break.

LIO: Cinnamon buns and a hidden raisin. That sounds less like logistics and more like a message written in pastry.

NIM: It was a snack. I ate the bun on the way here.

BEA: Could you bring the teapot to the station?

NIM: I'm already near the station. I can bring it up after I finish delivering these buns.

LIO: A journey toward the studio! Perhaps the song should wait until Nim reaches the doorway. We'll create a little suspense.

[PIANO THEME SWELLS. A DOOR CHIME SOUNDS, THEN FOOTSTEPS.] 

NIM: I'm outside the studio. The teapot's in the basket.

BEA: Great. Come in.

LIO: Wait. Before the door opens, tell Bea what the raisin meant to you.

NIM: It meant I ate the bun and missed it.

LIO: Such honesty. The piano finds its way through.

[DOOR OPENS. A BASKET RUSTLES.]

BEA: That's my teapot. Thank you. It's still warm.

NIM: The buns were warm. The pot was wrapped in the towel you left in the bakery.

LIO: A towel from her kitchen, wrapped around the object of their shared morning. I'm hearing a duet.

BEA: The towel is also mine. I'd like that back.

LIO: We may have found the true melody: property returning home.

[PIANO STOPS. LIO CLEARS THROAT.]

LIO: We have a request from Nim now.

NIM: "The Little Teapot Waltz." I want to dedicate it to Bea. I'll bring her a fresh cinnamon bun to replace the one I ate.

BEA: No raisins, please.

NIM: I'll check every fold.

LIO: A boundary, a promise, and a pastry. At last, the song knows what it's about.

[THE WALTZ PLAYS. THE DOOR CHIME SOUNDS AGAIN.]

BEA: That's my guest. I need the teapot and towel.

NIM: I'll carry them.

LIO: Listeners, the reunion is complete. Our callers are leaving together, carrying household linens and one accountable pastry.

NIM: Wait, the bakery bell is ringing. I have one more tray in the car.

BEA: I can carry the towel. You take the teapot.

LIO: They have negotiated the duet's instrumentation: ceramic and terrycloth.

BEA: That is not what a duet means.

LIO: Here, it means two people leaving in the same direction for precisely six steps.

[FOOTSTEPS. DOOR CLOSES.]

LIO: They have actually gone in opposite directions. Bea took the teapot home; Nim has deliveries. Our theme returns as a solo.

[PIANO THEME. LIO WHISPERS OFF-MIC.]

LIO: I may have to dedicate a song to the piano. It's been supporting me all morning.

[THE PIANO PLAYS ONE DRY CHORD.]
