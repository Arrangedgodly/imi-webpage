# The Lighthouse Keeper's Logbook

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio lighthouse keeper logs, precise nautical observations, and a keeper whose strongest warning signal is a kettle whistle*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Rell, an orangutan lighthouse keeper; Nima, a macaque radio operator; Voss, a gibbon harbor pilot; and a distant ferry captain.*

[SFX: Wind outside a lighthouse, radio static, a slow lamp motor.]

RELL: Lighthouse log, 18:00. Visibility clear. Sea calm. Lamp turning correctly.

NIMA: The radio is open. Harbor pilot Voss, do you copy?

VOSS: Copy. The north buoy is drifting toward the channel.

RELL: The buoy is anchored.

VOSS: It is anchored to a rope that is attached to a floating log.

NIMA: That is not an anchor.

RELL: It is an arrangement.

[SFX: Kettle begins to whistle.]

NIMA: Is that the fog signal?

RELL: No. That is the kettle.

VOSS: The ferry heard it and changed course.

RELL: The ferry is two miles away.

NIMA: The radio is carrying the whistle.

RELL: Turn off the microphone beside the stove.

[SFX: A switch clicks; whistle fades.]

VOSS: The ferry has returned to the channel.

RELL: Update the log: kettle mistaken for warning signal.

NIMA: The next ferry is requesting a light check.

RELL: The lamp is turning. Sweep interval twelve seconds.

NIMA: I have the beam on the breakwater.

VOSS: I see it. The light is steady.

RELL: It is meant to sweep.

NIMA: It is sweeping. The breakwater is moving at the same speed.

VOSS: The breakwater is fixed.

NIMA: Then the lamp is moving.

RELL: The gear may have slipped.

[SFX: Lamp motor slows, then clicks.]

RELL: The rotation stopped. I will reset the gear.

VOSS: I can guide the incoming ferry while you work.

NIMA: Use the radio, not the kettle.

RELL: Gear is seated. Lamp turning again.

[SFX: Motor resumes, steady sweep.]

VOSS: Confirmed. Light is sweeping north to south.

RELL: The log now shows clear visibility, drifting buoy, false kettle signal, and repaired lamp.

NIMA: The harbor needs a warning about the buoy.

RELL: We can send a radio notice.

VOSS: Keep to the south channel until it is secured.

NIMA: Notice sent. The ferry is adjusting its route.

[SFX: Radio static; distant engine changes pitch.]

RELL: The logbook requires an exact time.

NIMA: It is 18:24.

VOSS: The buoy is tied to the pier now.

RELL: That is a proper anchor.

NIMA: The ferry has passed safely.

RELL: Add that to the log. No warning signal sounded.

[SFX: Kettle clicks off.]

VOSS: The kettle has cooled. I can hear the harbor clearly.

RELL: Then we have one more entry: tea is ready.

NIMA: That is not a nautical observation.

RELL: It affects the keeper's ability to observe.

VOSS: Record it under supplies.

NIMA: Supplies: one kettle, two cups, and no floating logs attached to buoys.

RELL: The lighthouse beam is steady. The channel is clear. The ferry is home.

NIMA: The harbor pilot has located the loose log. It is a spruce trunk from the upstream bank.

VOSS: We have tied the buoy to the pier and marked the log for the river crew.

RELL: The north channel is clear again.

NIMA: I will log the time, the light sweep, and the corrected buoy position.

RELL: Add the kettle after the navigation notes.

VOSS: The ferry can return on the normal route.

[SFX: Distant ferry horn, then quiet water.]

NIMA: Confirmed. The vessel is safely in harbor.

VOSS: I am signing off. The harbor is quiet, and I have heard no kettle.

RELL: The next watch begins at midnight. The lamp will keep turning, and the log will stay dry.

NIMA: Radio standby is set. The kettle is in the galley, with its microphone unplugged.

[SFX: Lamp sweeps; radio clicks to standby.]

RELL: Log closed at 18:30. The sea remains calm, and the kettle is no longer in command.

[SFX: Wind, lamp motor, quiet harbor.]
