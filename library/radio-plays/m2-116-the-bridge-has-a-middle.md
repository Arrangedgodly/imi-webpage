# The Bridge Has a Middle

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio public-works progress reports, civic ribbon ceremonies, and a bridge inspector who refuses to call a plank finished while it is still floating*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Nera, a baboon infrastructure reporter; Soot, a gibbon bridge inspector; Lali, a macaque mayor; and Bim, a capuchin builder.*

[SFX: River current, hammer taps, radio theme.]

NERA: We are live at the new footbridge, scheduled to open today. Mayor Lali, what makes this crossing important?

LALI: It connects the east grove to the west grove. Until now, residents crossed by a series of stepping stones and strong opinions.

SOOT: The bridge has a strong left side.

NERA: And the right?

SOOT: It is still being assembled.

BIM: The right side is mostly there.

SOOT: A plank is floating downstream.

BIM: It is part of the water-resistant design.

LALI: The ribbon is ready. We need only a final inspection.

SOOT: I will test the first plank.

[SFX: One footstep; board creaks.]

SOOT: It holds.

NERA: The inspector advances to the next plank.

[SFX: Another creak; water splashes.]

SOOT: The plank holds the inspector. It does not hold the river.

BIM: The river is not on the bridge.

SOOT: It is under the bridge.

BIM: Then it is not a load-bearing concern.

NERA: A second test: two residents cross together.

LALI: The mayor and I will go first.

SOOT: Please wait until I secure the handrail.

LALI: The handrail is present.

SOOT: It is leaning against the bridge.

BIM: That is how we store it between uses.

LALI: The ribbon-cutting ceremony begins in five minutes.

SOOT: The bridge cannot be opened by cutting ribbon. It requires a continuous deck.

NERA: The builder is fitting the missing plank now.

[SFX: Hammer, then a wooden clack.]

BIM: The gap is closed.

SOOT: That plank is shorter than the others.

BIM: It is a compact plank.

SOOT: There is still a gap at both ends.

LALI: Could we put a small ramp over it?

SOOT: Then we would have a plank with two ramps and no bridge.

NERA: A flock of birds lands on the handrail.

[SFX: Birds chirp; handrail groans.]

SOOT: The rail supports birds. That's promising.

BIM: It supports a mayor too.

LALI: I have not leaned on it.

SOOT: You are currently leaning on it.

LALI: Only emotionally.

NERA: The inspector has found a solution. Bim replaces the short plank with the floating plank.

BIM: It is wet.

SOOT: The bridge is over a river. We expected moisture.

BIM: It needs to dry.

NERA: The sun is out. We will pause for three minutes.

[SFX: Birds, river, three gentle hammer taps.]

SOOT: The plank is in place. The deck is continuous. The handrail is secured. I approve the crossing.

LALI: Then we can cut the ribbon.

SOOT: After the test.

NERA: Soot and Lali are crossing together. One step, two, three. The bridge holds. They reach the west bank.

LALI: We are on the other side.

BIM: I will join you.

[SFX: Bim runs onto bridge; one board squeaks.]

SOOT: Walk, don't run.

BIM: I am celebrating.

SOOT: Celebrate on the bank.

NERA: The ribbon is cut. The bridge is open to foot traffic, one at a time until the town confirms the final weight limit.

LALI: We have reconnected the groves.

SOOT: And the floating plank has found a permanent home.

BIM: The river can keep the old one.

[SFX: Ribbon snips. Residents cross carefully; birds chirp.]

NERA: This is Nera at the bridge. The east and west groves are now connected by a route that remains above the river.

SOOT: For the whole length?

NERA: For the whole length.

NERA: Residents from both sides are crossing with baskets of fruit.

SOOT: One at a time, please.

LALI: The baskets are light.

SOOT: The bridge is new. Let it learn the town slowly.

BIM: I have added a sign: "Walk across. Do not store planks here."

SOOT: Good. The river has enough lumber.

[SFX: Steady footsteps cross, followed by the calm river.]
