# The Very Small Parade

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: community radio coverage of an overplanned parade*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: REPORTER TAVI, a lemur; PARADE MARSHAL PON, a macaque; DRUMMER RILL, a capuchin; and the residents of Fern Hollow.

[SFX: A DRUM ROLL BEGINS, STOPS, THEN A SINGLE TINY TAP.] 

TAVI: We are live at Fern Hollow's annual parade, where the committee has promised a magnificent procession down the main path. I see one banner, two drummers, and a wheelbarrow full of spare streamers.

PON: We planned a long route, but the route passed through a patch of ripe fruit. The parade is waiting until everyone is finished eating.

TAVI: A sensible delay. How many performers do you have?

PON: We have eighteen, plus the flag.

RILL: The flag is not a performer.

PON: It has a solo.

RILL: It catches wind. That is not a solo.

[SFX: WIND LIFTS A CORNER OF THE BANNER. THE FLAG FLAPS LIKE A DRUM.] 

TAVI: We may have heard the flag's rehearsal.

PON: The band is ready. Please take your place behind the ceremonial wheelbarrow.

RILL: Why is it in front?

PON: It carries the route map.

RILL: The map says the route begins at the tree.

PON: It does.

RILL: We're at the tree.

PON: This is the staging tree. The route tree is across the clearing.

TAVI: The procession has a pre-procession.

[FOOTSTEPS. THE BAND STARTS WALKING. THE WHEELBARROW WHEEL SQUEAKS ON EVERY THIRD STEP.] 

TAVI: The first block is underway. The crowd is moving at a stately pace.

PON: That's because the wheelbarrow is squeaking and everyone waits for the squeak before taking another step.

RILL: I can make a beat from it.

[SFX: DRUM AND WHEEL SQUEAK FALL INTO A RHYTHM.] 

TAVI: The parade has found its music. The banner is following the beat, and so is the wheelbarrow.

PON: The map is sliding.

RILL: It says to turn left at the moss.

PON: There are three patches of moss.

TAVI: A navigational challenge.

PON: The one with the pebble.

RILL: That's behind us.

[THE BAND STOPS. THE CROWD STOPS. THE BANNER CONTINUES FORWARD, CARRIED BY THE WIND.] 

TAVI: The parade has divided into a stationary section and a flag.

PON: We can shorten the route. Circle this tree and finish at the starting point.

RILL: Then the parade will have gone somewhere.

PON: Around is somewhere.

[THE BAND PLAYS A QUICK TURN. FEET CIRCLE THE TREE. THE WHEELBARROW SQUEAKS IN TIME.] 

TAVI: The residents have completed the loop. The banner has returned, now tied to the wheelbarrow. The flag is officially in the parade.

RILL: I told you it had a solo.

PON: It has a chorus now.

TAVI: The parade is over. What happens to the streamers?

PON: We roll them up for next year.

RILL: And the fruit?

PON: That was the opening ceremony.


PON: We need to decide whether the banner leads the band or follows it.

RILL: The banner is tied to the wheelbarrow. It follows the wheelbarrow.

PON: The wheelbarrow is carrying the route map. It leads the band.

TAVI: The banner has entered parade governance.

RILL: Can it be untied?

PON: If we untie it, the wind may carry it away.

RILL: It has already carried it away once.

PON: Then we'll use a knot that can be undone.

[ROPE CREAKS, THEN A KNOT SLIPS LOOSE. THE BANNER DRIFTS ACROSS THE CLEARING.] 

TAVI: A second procession has begun, this one without committee approval.

PON: Catch the trailing edge, not the pole.

[FOOTSTEPS HURRY. A SMALL GROUP CATCHES THE CLOTH.] 

RILL: We have the banner. It is now bigger than the parade.

TAVI: The crowd is holding it overhead like a roof.

PON: That wasn't in the program.

RILL: Neither was waiting for lunch, but the parade survived.

PON: We can make the route shorter and let the banner shade the line.

TAVI: So the procession is serving refreshments and shelter.

PON: It is a community event.

[THE BAND PLAYS SLOWLY. PEOPLE WALK UNDER THE BANNER, THEN FORM A CIRCLE AROUND THE TREE.] 

TAVI: The crowd has found a comfortable pace. Nobody is racing toward the finish.

RILL: The wheelbarrow squeak sets the tempo.

PON: I should have started with the wheelbarrow.

RILL: You started with a map that sends us through fruit.

PON: We needed to make sure the route was clear.

TAVI: It is clear now, except for the parade.

[THE CIRCLE STOPS. THE BANNER IS FOLDED OVER THE WHEELBARROW.] 

[SFX: ONE FRUIT DROPS INTO THE WHEELBARROW. THE WHEEL SQUEAKS.]

TAVI: We have reached the end. The crowd is back at the tree, and nobody has been left behind.

RILL: Did the parade get a review?

PON: One fruit. Better than a star; you can eat it.
