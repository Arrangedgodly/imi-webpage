# The Unexpected Encore

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live classical concert broadcasts, hushed continuity announcements, and a conductor who tries to keep an orchestra solemn through a leaking roof*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST VALE, a gibbon
MAESTRO ORA, a capuchin
LENA, a macaque percussionist
STAGE MANAGER KITO, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: SOFT AUDIENCE COUGH. TUNING NOTES. ONE DRIP FALLS INTO A METAL CUP.]

VALE: Good evening from the Rainwood Hall. The orchestra is about to perform The Quiet River, a composition in four movements. The hall asks that you silence your devices.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS. DRIP INTO CUP.]

KITO: The roof is dripping on the timpani.

ORA: Is it in the score?

LENA: It is in measure four. It may be in measure five if the leak continues.

VALE: We will now begin.

[SFX: CONDUCTOR RAISES BATON. ORCHESTRA PLAYS A SOFT, SLOW CHORD.]

ORA: [WHISPERING] Hold that note.

LENA: The cup is filling.

ORA: Hold the note.

[SFX: DRIP, DRIP, DRIP. A SMALL SPLASH.]

VALE: The first movement is called "Still Water."

KITO: The water is not still.

ORA: The orchestra is.

[SFX: A DROP HITS THE TUNED EDGE OF A METAL CUP. PING.]

LENA: We have a new instrument.

ORA: Do not encourage the roof.

[SFX: ORCHESTRA PLAYS AGAIN. THE CUP PINGS IN TIME.]

VALE: The audience may hear an unexpected percussive element.

KITO: The audience is moving their chairs away from the leak.

[SFX: CHAIRS SCRAPE SOFTLY. SHUSHING.]

ORA: We will proceed to the second movement, "The Current Turns."

[SFX: BRISK STRING RUN. A LARGE DRIP FALLS, CUP SLIDES ACROSS FLOOR.]

LENA: The cup is moving toward the bass section.

VALE: It has entered the orchestra.

ORA: It is not a soloist.

[SFX: CUP ROLLS, STRIKES A STAND. MUSIC STOPS.]

KITO: The leak has changed locations.

ORA: The cup found the low note.

VALE: For listeners at home, the orchestra is pausing to make a small adjustment.

LENA: The cup is now under the cello.

ORA: Then the cello has a guest.

[SFX: CELLO PLAYS A LOW NOTE. CUP RATTLES UNDER IT.]

VALE: We now present the third movement, "Rain on Glass," though we are indoors.

[SFX: WINDOW WIND. A DOOR OPENS. GUST BLOWS SHEET MUSIC.]

KITO: The score is leaving the stage.

ORA: Catch it, please.

[SFX: PAGES FLUTTER. AUDIENCE MURMUR.]

LENA: I have page six.

VALE: That is page four.

LENA: They are both wet.

ORA: We will perform from memory.

VALE: The orchestra has played this piece for seven years.

KITO: The ending changes every year.

ORA: Only the ending.

[SFX: ORCHESTRA PLAYS A LIVELY, IMPROVISED PASSAGE. THE CUP RINGS IN THE DISTANCE.]

VALE: The audience has begun to applaud.

ORA: We have not finished.

KITO: They are applauding the roof crew. The leak stopped.

[SFX: APPLAUSE. A LADDER CLANKS.]

VALE: The final movement is "Dry Ground." Maestro?

ORA: We will repeat the first chord and let the hall settle.

[SFX: ORCHESTRA PLAYS A WARM CHORD. NO DRIP.]

LENA: The cup is empty.

ORA: Then the composition is complete.

VALE: That was The Quiet River, with an unannounced cup solo and a roof repair. Maestro Ora, will there be an encore?

ORA: There will be a second bucket.

[SFX: LAUGHTER. APPLAUSE. ONE FINAL DROP LANDS IN THE EMPTY CUP.]

VALE: The crew has stopped the drip above percussion and moved us beneath the remaining leak.

KITO: That one is still active. The roof crew will repair it tomorrow.

ORA: Then tonight's final movement is a dry chord, performed well away from the cup.

[SFX: ORCHESTRA AND CUP MOVE A FEW STEPS. ONE WARM CHORD. NO DRIP.]

LENA: The chord is dry.

VALE: The roof is not repaired; the concert is simply over.

ORA: Good night from Rainwood Hall. Please take your chairs with you, especially the ones under the leak.

[SFX: CHAIRS LIFT, THEME OUT.][SFX: THEME OUT.]
