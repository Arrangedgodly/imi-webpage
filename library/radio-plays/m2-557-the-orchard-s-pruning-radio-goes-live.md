# The Orchard's Pruning Radio Goes Live

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live advice radio that tries to diagnose a tree by sound alone*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; arborist Mara, a macaque; caller Pip, a capuchin; orchard keeper Odo, a gibbon; neighbor Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Radio booth beside an orchard. Leaves stir; a light crackle comes through the phone.

VEX: We are live with the Orchard Doctor. A caller reports a cracking branch. At the end of the hour, one lucky tree gets a diagnosis.

MARA: Trees don't enter contests, and I can't diagnose a branch from sound alone. Pip, can you see the tree from where you are?

PIP, on phone: I'm at the marked path. A dry twig rubs the wire support when the wind moves. The main limb looks whole.

VEX: Aha! A distressed tree, a mysterious wire, and a caller who has spotted the suspect.

MARA: We have an observation, not a suspect. Is anything split, hanging over a path, or touching the ground?

PIP: No. The support wire holds the main limb away from the walkway. The noise is a smaller twig tapping it.

VEX: Listeners, vote now: should we remove the wire, cut the limb, or call the fire brigade?

MARA: Don't invite guesses that could put someone under a limb. Nobody should touch the wire or climb the tree. Odo can inspect from the ground.

ODO: I'm at the orchard gate. I'll close the visitor path and check the marked tree from the ground.

NARI: I'm outside the row with the detour sign. I can keep visitors on the gravel loop.

SFX: Gate latch closes; footsteps on gravel.

ODO: I'm at the tree. The main limb is firm. The dry twig brushes the support wire when the leaves move. I see no split or hanging section.

PIP: Does the wire need to come off?

ODO: No. It is not cutting into the limb. I'll remove the dry twig with the pole tool from the ground, but only when the wind eases.

VEX: Could you make it snap dramatically for the broadcast?

MARA: No. We don't repeat a cut or provoke movement for a sound effect.

SFX: Leaves rustle, then settle.

NARI: The wind has dropped. The path remains closed; no one is below the branch.

ODO: Pole is steady. One small twig, clear of the wire.

SFX: Pole extends; snips close once; twig lands on leaves.

ODO: Twig is down. The main limb and support wire are unchanged.

PIP: So the tree isn't in danger?

MARA: We checked the one branch you described. We did not inspect the whole tree. If you later see a split or hanging limb, keep clear and call a qualified arborist.

VEX: We promised a diagnosis. The show has a red button marked "VERDICT."

MARA: Then use it to announce what we actually learned: a dry twig rubbed a support wire, and the visible main limb looked sound from the ground.

VEX: The red button says "TREE GUILTY."

MARA: Turn the card over.

SFX: Cue card flips.

VEX: It says "TREE NOT ON TRIAL." The printer has no dramatic font for that.

ODO: The twig is in the green-waste bin. Pole is lowered and latched before I leave the row.

NARI: Odo is back through the gate. I'll move the detour sign only now that he is outside and the path is clear.

SFX: Gate latch opens; sign shifts onto gravel.

PIP: I'll write down the observation, not the poll. If the branch changes, I will keep clear and ask an arborist.

MARA: Good. No one climbs or pours anything into the soil to test a sound.

VEX: The Orchard Doctor has refused to prescribe a chainsaw, a fire engine, and a guilty verdict.

MARA: Because none was supported by what we saw. That is the end of this call.

SFX: Phone line clicks closed; leaves continue in the wind.
