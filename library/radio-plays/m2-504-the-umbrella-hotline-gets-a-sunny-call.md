# The Umbrella Hotline Gets a Sunny Call

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in weather shows that keep their format after the weather changes*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; forecaster Vex, an orangutan; caller Nari, a lemur; technician Odo, a gibbon; field voice Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Radio studio. Phone rings. Sunlight warms the window.

MARA: You're listening to the Umbrella Hotline.

NARI: I called because I left my umbrella on the bus.

VEX: Our weather protocol begins with rain probability.

ODO: The sky is clear.

PIP: The bus is still at the stop. I can check the seat.

NARI: I am already outside.

VEX: We have a caller with no umbrella and no rain.

MARA: We can help her find it.

VEX: The show needs a forecast.

ODO: The cloud chart shows no rain today.

SFX: Paper chart unrolls.

VEX: We can generate a light shower for the demonstration.

MARA: No. People use this line to decide whether to bring cover.

PIP: I'm at the bus. The umbrella is under the rear seat.

NARI: It has a yellow handle.

PIP: I see it.

VEX: Please describe the precipitation conditions.

PIP: Sunny, dry, and one forgotten umbrella.

MARA: Nari, the bus driver can bring it to the stop at the end of this route.

NARI: I'll wait under the awning.

VEX: The show is now a lost-item service.

ODO: We can update the hotline greeting: check the sky, then call about umbrellas.

MARA: Don't make people wait through rain advice when they need an answer.

MARA: Nari, does the umbrella belong to you?

NARI: Yes. The yellow handle has a scratch near the hinge.

PIP: I can see it. The strap is wrapped around the handle.

SFX: Umbrella opens halfway, then catches.

ODO: The hinge is bent. It was pressed under the seat.

VEX: The umbrella is damaged. That is a new weather crisis.

MARA: We can close it and carry it home. Don't force the hinge in the station.

NARI: I can repair it with a replacement rib, but I don't need it in today's sun.

PIP: The driver found it before the next route started.

VEX: Can we ask the bus company for a statement?

MARA: The driver already brought it to the stop. Thank them and let the bus leave on time.

ODO: The next bus is due in twelve minutes.

NARI: I'll wait here with the umbrella. The awning is wide enough for two people.

PIP: I can walk you to the repair stall instead.

MARA: The hotline can give directions without pretending rain is coming.

VEX: The sponsor sells weatherproof covers.

NARI: I already have one.

SFX: Umbrella strap buckles.

ODO: The sun is still clear. No rain is expected today.

MARA: The bus driver can leave on time. The route is not delayed by our broadcast.

PIP: I'll walk with Nari, and Odo can close the hotline.

VEX: We have resolved the lost item before the weather segment.

MARA: That's what the call was for.

SFX: Bus engine idles; doors close.

ODO: The route is moving again.

NARI: I'll take the umbrella to the repair stall tomorrow.

MARA: The forecast stays clear. The lost umbrella has a plan.

SFX: Bus doors open. Footsteps approach.

PIP: Here it is. The handle is yellow.

NARI: Thank you. I don't need it right now, but I'll take it home.

VEX: Can we close with a cloud warning?

MARA: We can close with today's forecast: sunny, umbrella returned.

PIP: The forecast line is closed until tomorrow.

ODO: I'll put the number beside the bus schedule.

NARI: I can get there on foot. I will keep the umbrella closed until I reach the repair stall.
