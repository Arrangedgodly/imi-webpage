# The Last Call from the Paper Airship

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: aviation broadcasts that dress a fragile prototype's flight as a historic expedition*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Pilot Seli, a capuchin; announcer Odo, a gibbon; engineer Nara, a lemur; ground caller Vex, a macaque.

[SFX: PAPER CRINKLES. A SMALL FAN SPINS UP. A LIGHT AIRSHIP ROPE CREAKS AGAINST A WOODEN POST.]

ODO: This is the first official broadcast of the Paper Airship, a vessel of uncommon scale.

NARA: It is the size of a laundry basket.

SELI: It is a test model. Please keep the fan low.

VEX: I am ground control. Give me a dramatic call sign.

SELI: Seli.

VEX: Captain Seli of the Cloud Fleet.

SELI: Fine. The Cloud Fleet is tethered to a chair.

[SFX: FAN RISES. THE AIRSHIP LIFTS SLIGHTLY; THE ROPE UNWINDS TWO INCHES.]

ODO: We are airborne.

NARA: We are above the table.

VEX: Captain, report your heading.

SELI: Toward the open window, if the fan keeps pulling.

ODO: A bold course toward the horizon.

NARA: The horizon is the windowsill. We need the rope to stay clear of the propeller.

[SFX: ROPE SLIPS OVER A CHAIR BACK. THE PAPER AIRSHIP TURNS SIDEWAYS AND BRUSHES A CURTAIN.]

SELI: I have lost the forward sail.

VEX: Is that a casualty?

NARA: It is a curtain tie. It snagged the paper.

ODO: An obstacle at the edge of the known world.

SELI: Ground crew, please hold the rope while I guide it away.

[SFX: VEX GRABS THE ROPE; IT TUGS HIS CHAIR FORWARD. THE CHAIR SCRAPES.]

VEX: The ground is moving.

NARA: Your chair is on wheels.

VEX: I am being pulled into the mission.

SELI: Do not wrap the rope around your hand. Hold the wooden handle.

[SFX: ROPE TIGHTENS AROUND A HANDLE. THE AIRSHIP STOPS TURNING.]

ODO: We have reached a stable orbit.

NARA: It is still tied to a chair.

SELI: We should bring it down before the paper tears.

VEX: Captain, request permission to land.

ODO: Permission denied. The audience expects a crossing.

NARA: The airship has a split seam.

[SFX: PAPER CREAKS. THE FAN WHINES.]

SELI: We can lower it and patch the seam. That is the test.

ODO: Can the landing sound like a triumph?

NARA: It can sound like a fan switching off.

[SFX: NARA TURNS OFF THE FAN. THE AIRSHIP SINKS GENTLY ONTO A PILLOW. THE ROPE GOES SLACK.]

SELI: Landed. No tear in the sail.

VEX: I have stopped moving.

ODO: The historic crossing is complete.

NARA: It crossed the table.

SELI: We learned the tie point needs to sit behind the sail. We can test again after the patch dries.

VEX: What should I announce?

SELI: Test suspended until repairs are finished.

ODO: The Paper Airship returns to its hangar, carrying the hopes of the crew.

NARA: It is going in a drawer.

[SFX: AIRSHIP IS FOLDED CAREFULLY. A DRAWER OPENS, PAPER IS PLACED INSIDE, AND THE DRAWER CLOSES.]

VEX: Ground control signing off.

ODO: Captain Seli, any final words?

SELI: Keep the fan low next time.

NARA: Before we call the flight finished, I want to inspect the sail seam.

[SFX: PAPER IS UNFOLDED. A FINGER RUNS ALONG THE SEAM; ONE EDGE RUSTLES LOOSELY.]

NARA: The tape lifted where it brushed the curtain. It is not torn, but it will catch again.

SELI: I can fold the leading edge inward and add a small paper patch.

VEX: How long will that take?

NARA: Longer than the broadcast.

ODO: We can fill the time with a heroic account of the landing.

SELI: Or we can let the repair be quiet.

[SFX: TAPE IS CUT. PAPER PATCH IS PRESSED DOWN. A THUMB SMOOTHES IT FROM CENTER TO EDGE.]

NARA: Try the fan at the lower setting.

[SFX: FAN STARTS ON LOW. THE PAPER AIRSHIP LIFTS GENTLY, STAYS LEVEL, AND SETTLES WHEN THE FAN STOPS.]

SELI: The seam held. The tie point stayed behind the sail.

VEX: That was a better flight.

ODO: It crossed the table without a crisis.

NARA: That is what a successful test sounds like.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES ON A TEST CARD. A SMALL CLIP FASTENS THE CARD TO THE AIRSHIP'S STRING.]

VEX: What is the call sign now?

SELI: Paper Airship, test two. No Cloud Fleet.

[SFX: THE FAN CLICKS OFF. THE ROOM SETTLES INTO QUIET.]
