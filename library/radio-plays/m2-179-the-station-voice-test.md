# The Station Voice Test

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio station identity contests, announcer auditions, and endless debates over a new jingle's pronunciation*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
HOST ORA, a lemur
DAX, a gibbon station manager
FIFI, a capuchin voice actor

[THREE SHORT JINGLE NOTES.]

ORA: Kettle City Radio is choosing a new station voice. We asked listeners for a phrase that can be spoken clearly in the morning. Dax, what is the shortlist?

DAX: "Good morning, Kettle City," "You're tuned to Kettle," and "We're here, mostly."

ORA: The third phrase has strong local support.

DAX: It was submitted by the transmitter technician.

FIFI: I'm ready for my audition.

ORA: Please read the line naturally.

FIFI: Good morning, Kettle City.

ORA: Warm. Clear. Perhaps too clear. Can you make it sound more like radio?

FIFI: Good morning, Kettle City.

DAX: Add a little sparkle at the end.

FIFI: Good morning, Kettle City!

ORA: That sounds like the city has surprised you.

DAX: Try a more relaxed version.

FIFI: Good morning, Kettle City.

ORA: Now it sounds like you're disappointed in the city.

FIFI: I'm saying the same words.

DAX: The tone changes the message.

ORA: We can ask the listeners. Caller, which version do you prefer?

CALLER: The one where she just says it.

ORA: Very good. We'll have the audience vote on three options. The first is spoken by Fifi.

FIFI: Good morning, Kettle City.

ORA: The second is sung.

FIFI: [SINGS] Good morning, Kettle City.

DAX: And the third is whispered.

FIFI: [WHISPERS] Good morning, Kettle City.

ORA: Listeners, call now. We'll take the first vote.

[PHONE BUTTON BEEPS.]

DAX: Someone voted for the jingle.

ORA: That wasn't one of the options.

DAX: They pressed the music line. We can play the new station motif.

[THREE NOTES. ONE NOTE IS FLAT.]

FIFI: The middle note is flat.

ORA: The motif is memorable.

DAX: It's the tuning tone from the transmitter.

ORA: We can include it as a signature sound.

FIFI: Then the new phrase should be "Good morning, Kettle City, and please tune the transmitter."

DAX: That is accurate, but too long.

ORA: We have another caller.

CALLER TWO: I like "We're here, mostly." It sounds honest.

FIFI: That line is for a broken transmitter.

DAX: The technician says the transmitter has been stable for a year.

ORA: Then it's a celebration. We'll make "We're here, mostly" our new identity.

FIFI: How should I say it?

ORA: Like you mean it.

FIFI: We're here, mostly.

[TRANSMITTER CLICKS. STATIC CUTS IN.]

DAX: The station signal dropped.

ORA: The phrase has become unexpectedly relevant.

DAX: It's back.

[STATIC CLEARS.]

FIFI: We're here, mostly.

DAX: We have received twelve votes for the honest phrase, nine for the straight greeting, and three for the jingle.

FIFI: What were the three jingle votes?

DAX: The same listener called three times because they could not hear the first call.

ORA: We will count each listener once. That leaves one vote for the jingle.

CALLER: I was the one who voted for it. I like the three notes, but I wanted the voice to sound plain.

FIFI: Then your vote makes sense. The motif says "station," and the voice says "good morning."

ORA: A useful distinction. Our new sound will be three bright notes, then Fifi's calm greeting.

[THREE NOTES.]

FIFI: We're here, mostly.

[TRANSMITTER HUM STAYS STEADY.]

DAX: The signal holds, the phrase is clear, and there is no accidental static.

ORA: The winning read is settled. The station voice contest is complete, the jingle remains three notes, and the transmitter gets its own credit.

DAX: Kettle City Radio: we're here, mostly.

ORA: The phrase will air at the top of every hour, immediately after the three notes.

FIFI: And no flat note?

DAX: Only when the transmitter truly needs tuning.

[THEME MUSIC. ONE NOTE WOBBLES, THEN RESOLVES.]
