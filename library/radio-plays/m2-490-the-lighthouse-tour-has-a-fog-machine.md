# The Lighthouse Tour Has a Fog Machine

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel programs that add spectacle to places whose job is clarity*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Guide Mara, a macaque; host Vex, an orangutan; keeper Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin; technician Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Lighthouse stairwell. Wind hums through a narrow window.

VEX: Welcome to the coast's most dramatic lighthouse tour.

PIP: I came to see the lamp.

ODO: The lamp is at the top. It is operating.

MARA: We should keep the window clear so visitors can look out.

VEX: We have installed a fog machine for atmosphere.

SELI: It's blowing toward the viewing glass.

PIP: I can't see the shore.

VEX: The fog creates mystery.

MARA: The lighthouse exists to help boats see the coast.

ODO: The lamp is rotating. The fog machine is blocking the beam from inside.

VEX: We can call it an immersive experience.

MARA: Turn it off.

SFX: Switch clicks. Fan slows; mist fades.

PIP: I can see the harbor now.

SELI: The wind outside is bringing real fog in from the water.

VEX: That is excellent timing.

ODO: Real fog means we should check the lamp and signal bell.

MARA: I'll test the bell. Odo, check the turning mechanism.

SFX: Bell rope pulls. Deep bell sounds once.

PIP: That is louder than the fog machine.

VEX: Can we make the bell sound more welcoming?

MARA: It is a warning bell.

ODO: The rotation is steady. The light is clear.

SELI: The recorded tour says the keeper once signaled a passing ship with a hand lantern.

ODO: That was before the lamp was repaired. The main beacon is working now.

VEX: The lantern story adds human drama.

MARA: Keep the story, but don't dim the beacon to demonstrate it.

PIP: The harbor light is still turning.

SFX: A smaller hand lantern is lifted and set beside the lamp housing.

ODO: We can show the lantern without lighting it.

VEX: A dark lantern is not dramatic.

SELI: It is an artifact with a clear label: "Used before electric rotation."

MARA: The visitor can understand the history and still see the active lamp.

PIP: I can hear the bell echo off the water.

SFX: Bell resonates; wind whistles softly through the open window.

ODO: That's enough atmosphere.

VEX: No music?

MARA: Let the working light and bell be the music.

SELI: I'll record that as the tour's final sound.

SFX: Lamp mechanism turns with a slow mechanical whirr.

SELI: The beam crosses the water.

PIP: A boat just changed course.

MARA: It saw the light.

VEX: We should narrate the dramatic response.

ODO: We can say the lighthouse is doing its job.

VEX: That's not a travel slogan.

PIP: It's why I came here.

MARA: The tour can explain how the lamp works, then let visitors look without extra effects.

SELI: I'll put the fog machine in storage.

VEX: At least leave it on the stairs for the finale.

ODO: It will block the exit.

VEX: Then it goes in storage.

SFX: Metal door closes around fog machine.

PIP: Can I see the lamp one more time?

MARA: Yes. Keep to the rail, and wait for the beam to pass.

PIP: The tour stairs are narrow. Can we let the next group pass before we start down?

MARA: Yes. Stand behind the rail and leave the landing clear.

SFX: Footsteps descend; door closes softly at the landing.

ODO: The signal log shows one bell, one clear beam, and no interior fog.

VEX: It needs a more exciting ending.

SELI: We can end when the visitors see the harbor light sweep across the water.

MARA: That is exactly what the lighthouse does.

SFX: Beam mechanism turns. Bell rope sways in the wind.
