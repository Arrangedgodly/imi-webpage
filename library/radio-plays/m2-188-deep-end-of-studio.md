# From the Deep End of the Studio

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: nature radio documentaries that invent dramatic motives for routine animal behavior*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
NARRATOR ORI, a mandrill
SOUND ENGINEER SIV, a capuchin
AQUARIUM KEEPER MAI, a macaque
HOST RU, a gorilla

[SFX: UNDERWATER BUBBLES. A SOFT MOTOR HUM.]

ORI: In the blue stillness, a turtle glides past the glass, searching for a clue to the meaning of the sea.

MAI: The turtle is swimming to the feeding station.

RU: It eats here every day.

SIV: I can remove the pump hum.

[SIV SWITCHES IT OFF. THE WATER FALLS SILENT; THE AQUARIUM PUMP WARNING BEEPS.]

MAI: Turn it back on. The filter needs to run.

### Scene One: The Journey

[SFX: PUMP HUM RETURNS. WATER TRICKLES.]

ORI: The turtle has reached the station. Its journey is complete.

RU: It is still swimming.

[THE TURTLE TURNS AROUND AND SWIMS BACK TO A WARM ROCK.]

ORI: It has rejected the expected path.

MAI: It is basking.

SIV: Should I add a heartbeat under the splash?

RU: No. The turtle's heart is not on this microphone.

[THE CREW PLACES A CONTACT MIC ON THE GLASS. IT PICKS UP A STAFF CART ROLLING THROUGH THE HALL.] 

ORI: A distant rumble approaches.

MAI: That is the lunch cart.

[RU ASKS SIV TO TURN THE CART RECORDING INTO A ROARING STORM. SIV PLAYS IT BACK; THE CART SOUNDS LIKE A LOW HUM AND A RATTLE.]

RU: The storm is approaching the habitat.

MAI: The cart is stopping at the staff door.

[A DOOR LATCH CLICKS. A KEEPER CALLS THAT LUNCH IS READY. THE TURTLE CONTINUES ITS ROUTINE.]

ORI: The landscape remains unmoved.

SIV: The microphone is still on the cart channel.

### Scene Two: The Feeding

[MAI SETS A SMALL PORTION OF FOOD AT THE STATION. THE TURTLE APPROACHES, PAUSES, THEN TAKES A BITE.]

ORI: A tense negotiation begins.

RU: It is eating.

SIV: I have a sound of cracking ice.

MAI: There is no ice.

[SIV PLAYS THE EFFECT. THE TURTLE STOPS EATING AND TURNS TOWARD THE SPEAKER.] 

MAI: Turn that off. It is distracting the animal.

[SIV STOPS THE SOUND. THE TURTLE RETURNS TO ITS FOOD.]

ORI: The intruder has withdrawn.

RU: The sound effect has withdrawn.

[MAI CHECKS THE HABITAT LOG AND SAYS THE TURTLE EATS, RESTS, AND RETURNS TO THE WARM ROCK.]

[RU ASKS IF THE LOG HAS A SURPRISE. MAI TURNS A PAGE; IT LISTS THE SAME FEEDING TIME FOR THE WEEK.]

MAI: This is a routine.

ORI: The episode is about a routine.

ORI: Can we keep the narration simple?

MAI: You can describe what you see.

### Scene Three: The Recording

[RU PLAYS BACK THE TAKE. THE VOICE-OVER DESCRIBES A MYSTERIOUS CROSSING; THE MICROPHONE CAPTURES THE SOUND OF SIV'S CHAIR SQUEAKING.] 

SIV: The chair has entered the habitat.

MAI: It is in the control room.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS AGAIN. ORI STOPS THE PLAYBACK.]

ORI: We can record another take after the cart passes.

RU: Or we can leave it out.

[SIV MOVES THE CHAIR. IT SQUEAKS LOUDER. HE PUTS A TOWEL UNDER ITS FOOT.] 

SIV: The chair has been quieted.

ORI: The animal is still visible.

[THEY RECORD THE TURTLE RESTING ON THE ROCK. THE NARRATION SAYS IT IS RESTING. NO MUSIC PLAYS.]

RU: We have a clean take.

ORI: We have the turtle resting.

SIV: We also have the pump, which must stay on.

MAI: Leave that sound in.

[THE PUMP HUM AND A SINGLE WATER RIPPLE CONTINUE UNDER THE FINAL TAKE.]

RU: No music needed.

### Tag

[SFX: PUMP HUM, A SOFT WATER RIPPLE.]

ORI: A turtle rests beneath the warm lamp.

MAI: That is correct.

[SIV'S CHAIR MAKES ONE FAINT SQUEAK.]

RU: The control room remains mysterious, and it has a squeaky chair.
