# Weather Balloon at the Window

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio weather-balloon reports, increasingly technical altitude checks, and a station crew trying to retrieve its own forecast*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Arlo, a mandrill meteorologist; Vika, a macaque field reporter; Bo, a gibbon technician; and Oona, an orangutan balloon handler.*

[SFX: Radio static, wind, balloon fabric flapping.]

ARLO: We are live at the weather station as Oona prepares the morning balloon. Today's forecast calls for light rain, moderate gusts, and a chance of the forecast itself drifting away.

OONA: The balloon carries a sensor that measures wind, temperature, and humidity. It does not carry the forecast.

VIKA: The forecast is printed on a card. Bo is holding it.

BO: I am holding it with both hands. The wind has taken one corner.

ARLO: What is the launch procedure?

OONA: We fill the balloon, attach the sensor, check the line, and release when the wind is steady.

VIKA: The wind is steady.

BO: It is moving the tent steadily toward the orchard.

[SFX: Tent canvas snaps.]

ARLO: The instrument reads a gust from the northwest.

OONA: The instrument is pointing at the tent.

BO: The tent is now pointing at the orchard.

VIKA: The balloon is ready. Release on my count. Three, two-

[SFX: Balloon launches. The line unspools; a pulley squeals.]

OONA: The line is snagged on the weather vane.

ARLO: We have a balloon ascending vertically while remaining attached to the roof.

BO: The vane now indicates several directions at once.

VIKA: We can still measure wind.

ARLO: It says the wind is variable.

OONA: The vane is spinning.

ARLO: Variable, with enthusiasm.

[SFX: Balloon tugs. A weather vane clanks.]

BO: The balloon is pulling the vane off the roof.

OONA: Reel it in.

BO: Which spool?

OONA: The one marked BALLOON.

BO: Both are marked balloon. One is a spool of string. The other is a picnic basket.

VIKA: Why is a picnic basket marked balloon?

BO: It was the first spool. We have not updated the label.

ARLO: The balloon has cleared the roof. The sensor is transmitting a temperature of 18 degrees.

OONA: That's right.

ARLO: Humidity is 60 percent.

OONA: Also right.

ARLO: Wind speed, four kilometers per hour.

VIKA: The card is flying out of my hand.

BO: It is heading toward the balloon.

[SFX: Paper flaps upward.]

ARLO: The forecast is now at the same altitude as the sensor.

OONA: We need it back before the rain.

VIKA: The balloon line is wrapped around the weather vane. The card is caught in the sensor basket.

BO: If we pull the line, we retrieve both.

OONA: Carefully. The sensor costs more than the basket.

[SFX: A slow creak; line winds onto a spool.]

ARLO: The balloon descends. The card is visible. It reads: "Rain by noon."

VIKA: It is raining now.

[SFX: First raindrops on the microphone.]

ARLO: We have confirmed the forecast at ground level.

OONA: The sensor is safe. The balloon is deflating. The card is damp but legible.

BO: The picnic basket spool is empty.

VIKA: Did we send the balloon up with a picnic basket?

BO: No, but it has been holding the spare line.

ARLO: The station's official forecast remains light rain and moderate gusts. The instrument reports that the tent is in the orchard and the weather vane points toward lunch.

OONA: We should pack up.

VIKA: Can I give the closing report?

ARLO: Go ahead.

VIKA: The balloon launched, took a scenic route around the roof, collected its own forecast, and returned before the picnic basket. I am reporting from under the tent.

[SFX: Tent fabric flaps, rain softens.]

BO: The tent is moving again.

OONA: Tie it to the weather vane.

ARLO: The vane is pointing toward the orchard.

VIKA: Then that is where we will broadcast tomorrow.

[SFX: A rope tightens; rain and radio static blend.]

ARLO: This is Arlo at the station. The forecast has been recovered. The weather remains outside.

[SFX: Balloon rubber deflates with one long squeak; transmission ends.]
