# The Rooftop Sound Archive

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: field-recording purists who refuse to label sounds until the perfect ambience has been captured*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Recorder Seli, a lemur; producer Odo, a baboon; archivist Tavi, a capuchin; neighbor Mara, a gibbon.

[SFX: WIND ON A ROOFTOP. A METAL DOOR OPENS; A RECORDER BUTTON CLICKS.]

SELI: Test file one. Rooftop, dawn. Wind from the east, distant traffic, one loose sign.

ODO: You have not recorded the sign yet.

SELI: I can hear it.

TAVI: Hearing it is not archiving it.

[SFX: THE SIGN CLANGS THREE TIMES INTO THE RUNNING RECORDER.]

SELI: There. Sign, metal, three impacts.

ODO: Was it wind, or the door?

SELI: It sounded like wind.

TAVI: We need a clean source.

[SFX: THE SIGN CLANGS AGAIN WHILE THE RECORDER RUNS.]

SELI: It has repeated itself.

ODO: That is a second event.

TAVI: Or the tail of the first.

MARA: You are blocking the laundry line.

SELI: Could you wait until we finish the sound?

MARA: I need the line for these sheets. They keep flapping into the street.

ODO: Sheets are a separate category.

MARA: The sheets are making the sound.

[SFX: CLOTH SNAPS IN THE WIND. THE LOOSE SIGN CLANGS AGAIN.]

SELI: We have cloth, sign and wind. I need to isolate them.

ODO: Put the microphone closer to the sign.

TAVI: Then the wind becomes a microphone sound.

SELI: I will record the sign alone.

[SFX: SELI CLIMBS A LADDER. THE LADDER RATTLES. A HAND TIGHTENS THE SIGN.]

MARA: I am holding the bottom. Tighten the upper bolt.

SELI: The ladder is in the recording.

ODO: It is a setup sound. Keep it.

TAVI: The archive needs the sign, not the ladder.

MARA: The sign will not come loose if you tighten it.

SELI: But then the sound will be gone.

MARA: The sound is why it is loose.

[SFX: WRENCH TURNS; THE SIGN'S RATTLE STOPS. THE WIND CONTINUES.]

SELI: We have lost the source.

ODO: No. We have fixed it.

TAVI: Can you record the quiet now?

SELI: Quiet has no label.

MARA: Label it 'sign secured.'

SELI: That is a description, not a sound.

ODO: It may still be the most useful recording today.

[SFX: THE CLOTHESLINE CREAKS. MARA PULLS A SHEET FROM THE LINE; IT SNAPS ONCE, THEN GOES STILL.]

MARA: This one needs a new clip. It will make a single snap until I replace it.

SELI: One clean snap, repeatable on request.

TAVI: That is an archive sound.

MARA: It is laundry.

ODO: We can list it as laundry.

SELI: Fine. Rooftop, dawn: one clothesline clip, east wind, source secured.

MARA: If the sign is fixed, I can hang the sheets without holding them.

SELI: Can we capture the new sound first?

MARA: The clip snaps only when I close it.

ODO: That gives us a controlled recording.

TAVI: It gives us laundry being hung.

MARA: It also gives me dry sheets.

[SFX: MARA OPENS A CLOTHESPIN, CLIPS A SHEET TO THE LINE. ONE SHARP SNAP. THE SHEET SETTLES INTO THE WIND.]

SELI: That is one clean transient with a cloth tail.

MARA: It is a clothespin.

SELI: I will file the sound under clothespin, rooftop, east wind.

ODO: Can we get a second take?

MARA: The clip is holding. I am not taking it off to make a noise for your catalog.

TAVI: Good. It is a one-time event.

SELI: I will mark the take as nonrepeatable.

[SFX: THE SHEET FLAPS ONCE; THE CLIP HOLDS. THE SIGN STAYS QUIET. MARA'S FOOTSTEPS CROSS THE ROOF TOWARD THE DOOR.]

MARA: You can keep the recording if you carry the ladder downstairs.

[SFX: SELI FOLDS THE LADDER AND LIFTS IT.]

SELI: I am holding it.

ODO: The recorder is still on.

[SFX: SELI LAUGHS ONCE, EMBARRASSED. THE BUTTON CLICKS OFF. THE RECORDER STOPS.]

TAVI: That one is called 'Recorder turned off late.'

SELI: Label it before it repeats.
