# The Night Bus Interviews Seat Four

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel interview shows that treat a bus seat as a returning celebrity*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Nari, a lemur; driver Mara, a macaque; interviewer Vex, an orangutan; dispatcher Odo, a gibbon; rider Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Bus idling at terminal. Door hiss, low radio chatter.

NARI: Tonight on Routeside, we interview Seat Four, the night bus's most reliable passenger.

MARA: Seat Four is a seat. Pip is the passenger using it.

PIP: I rode this route last week. The seat has a loose cover, but it is still attached.

VEX: Tell us, Seat Four, what do you remember about the midnight route?

ODO, over radio: Dispatcher confirms the route is running on schedule. Please keep the driver channel clear.

MARA: Vex, use the interview mic. The bus radio is for service calls.

VEX: Seat Four, do you prefer the window or aisle?

PIP: I prefer the window. The cover is loose near the front edge.

MARA: Thank you. I'll mark it for inspection at the terminal. It is safe for this trip, but no one should pull the cover.

SFX: Passenger boards, card reader beeps.

ODO: Fare accepted. The aisle is clear.

NARI: Our guest seems reluctant to speak.

VEX: A quiet celebrity. Let us ask about the people who have sat here before.

MARA: We don't keep a list of riders by seat. That would expose passenger information.

PIP: I can tell you the seat is close to the stop cord and the window latch. That's all I need.

VEX: The window latch has a story?

PIP: It sticks in cold weather. The driver checked it before the bus left.

MARA: Window remains shut until we stop. Please don't try to force it.

SFX: Doors close; bus pulls away.

NARI: Route begins. Seat Four is in the second row on the right, if listeners want to sit there tomorrow.

MARA: Don't direct strangers to a particular seat. It may be occupied, and the bus is not a studio tour.

VEX: Can we interview the stop cord?

MARA: It is an emergency signal. Don't tug it for the broadcast.

SFX: Cord sways lightly with bus movement, no bell.

ODO: The dispatcher confirms Harbor Street is the only requested stop before the station.

VEX: Can we ask the driver to stop at the lighthouse for one more interview?

MARA: No. The posted route has one requested pickup; I will keep the service radio free until we reach the station.

SFX: Bus brakes; engine idles at the curb.

MARA: Bus is stopped at Harbor Street. Ramp may deploy.

ODO: The waiting rider has a ramp request. I have counted one boarding passenger.

PIP: I will stay in Seat Four until the aisle is clear. The rider can use the marked front space.

VEX: The interview has become a live arrival.

MARA: It remains a transit service. Keep the microphone away from the ramp.

NARI: I am turning the mic toward the cabin wall. The rider has not agreed to be recorded.

PIP: Ramp is level with the curb. There is room to board without moving my seat.



VEX: Seat Four, would you like to say anything to the arriving passenger?

MARA: The rider is a person, not an audience cue. We do not ask for an interview while they are boarding.

PIP: The rider has reached the marked space. The aisle is clear, and no one has been asked to speak on air.

SFX: Ramp folds and latches; door closes.

ODO: Passenger count updated. Harbor Street pickup is complete.

MARA: We can continue to the station now. The bus is moving only after the ramp is secured and the driver gets the all-clear.

VEX: Seat Four has witnessed a new chapter.

PIP: I am still in Seat Four. The rider is by the front.

MARA: The driver does.
