# A Tale with Two Endings

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: listener-voted radio story hours*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Storyteller Omi, an orangutan; host Vale, a macaque; sound operator Rill, a gibbon; and callers Marn and Tika.

[SFX: PAPER PAGE TURNS. FIREPLACE CRACKLES, THEN A FAN CLICKS ON.]

VALE: Welcome to The Open Ending, the only radio story that lets listeners choose what happens next, after we have already printed both endings. Omi's story has reached a fork. Meli carries medicine to a tortoise across the river. She can wait for the bridge keeper to inspect the old bridge, or walk to the ferry and arrive later. Rill, are the two options equally dramatic?

RILL: The first has creaking planks. The second has a ferry bell.

VALE: That's a difficult choice.

OMI: It's a practical choice. The river is rising, and the bridge has not been checked since the storm.

VALE: We will take calls. Marn, which ending do you want?

MARN, PHONE: The bridge. It has more suspense.

VALE: The bridge ending gets the suspense vote.

OMI: Meli does not hear this broadcast. She sees the bridge move when the wind hits it.

VALE: Our listeners know this is fiction. They can still vote.

OMI: Meli knows the river is real.

RILL: I have made the bridge sound louder.

OMI: Turn it down. The planks already told her what she needed.

[SFX: WOOD CREAKS, RIVER RISES.]

MELI, IN STORY: I will not cross on a plank that bends before I step on it.

VALE: That is not one of the choices.

OMI: It is the choice she made.

VALE: We promised the audience a vote.

RILL: We promised to ask. We did not promise the character would ignore what she can see.

VALE: Tika, what do you choose?

TIKA, PHONE: The ferry. It is slower, but it is open.

VALE: One call for each ending. I will settle this with a live poll.

RILL: The bridge tin has one token. The ferry tin has one token and a receipt.

VALE: Why a receipt?

RILL: The ferry is a working service. It charges one seed per crossing.

VALE: Does the bridge charge anything?

RILL: Only attention.

[SFX: TWO WOODEN TOKENS DROP, THEN A THIRD TOKEN ROLLS ACROSS THE TABLE.]

RILL: The third token fell into the ferry tin.

VALE: The ferry wins by one accidental vote.

OMI: Meli follows the marked path to the landing. The ferry operator ties up, checks the rope, and takes the jar across. A pair of geese is already aboard. They refuse to move from the middle.

[SFX: GEESE HONK, FERRY ROPE CREAKS.]

FERRY OPERATOR, IN STORY: I can wait for the geese to make room, or we can all stand here and become part of the dock.

MELI: I can carry the jar. They can keep the bench.

OMI: The geese shuffle aside. The ferry crosses. Meli arrives later than she hoped, but the tortoise receives the medicine before dark.

[SFX: FERRY BELL, ROPE TIGHTENS, FOOTSTEPS ON THE DOCK.]

MELI: The bridge keeper says the middle plank is split. We can close it before anyone tries to cross.

VALE: And the bridge ending?

OMI: The keeper closes the bridge. That is the ending. Nobody falls in so a caller can hear a splash.

MARN: Could we vote to reopen it?

RILL: Not until the carpenter checks the span.

VALE: The poll is closed. Meli chose the ferry because the bridge was unsafe, and the bridge stays closed until it is repaired.

TIKA: Can I have the token that rolled?

RILL: It is under the microphone. The vote is not binding on the story, but it is binding on the token. Someone has to fetch it.

[SFX: RILL SLIDES THE MICROPHONE, REACHES UNDER THE TABLE.]

RILL: I found three dust bunnies, one old receipt, and the red token.

VALE: Did it vote?

RILL: It was under the table.

[SFX: RILL MOVES THE MIC. A TOKEN TAPS THE TABLE.]

VALE: That sound will be next week's cliffhanger.

OMI: It is a token.

VALE: To be continued.

[SFX: ONE SMALL TOKEN ROLL, THEN THE THEME ENDS.]
