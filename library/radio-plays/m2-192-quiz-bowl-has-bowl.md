# The Quiz Bowl Has a Bowl

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: quiz radio programs that mistake buzzer timing and sound effects for knowledge*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST VERA, a macaque
CONTESTANT PON, a gibbon
CONTESTANT KALI, a langur
SCOREKEEPER ETTI, a gorilla
PRODUCER MOSS, a capuchin

[SFX: STUDIO BELL, THEN A BOWL CLINKING ON A TABLE.]

VERA: Welcome to the Canopy Quiz Bowl. The bowl is a bowl. The quiz begins now.

MOSS: We can make that more exciting.

ETTI: The rules say wait until the question is finished.

PON: I am ready.

KALI: I can hear the timer already.

### Scene One: First Question

VERA: Which instrument measures air pressure?

[PON BUZZES BEFORE THE QUESTION ENDS.] 

PON: Barometer.

ETTI: Correct, but the buzzer was early. No point.

MOSS: Can we replay the buzzer at half speed?

VERA: No. Next question.

[VERA READS THE WHOLE QUESTION. KALI WAITS, THEN BUZZES.]

KALI: Barometer.

ETTI: Correct. One point.

MOSS: The replay showed Pon's wing reached the button first.

ETTI: The answer came before the complete clue.

### Scene Two: The Bowl

[MOSS SETS A CERAMIC BOWL BETWEEN THE CONTESTANTS.] 

MOSS: A surprise prop round.

ETTI: That is not in the rules.

VERA: It is a bowl. We can use it to hold the cards.

[MOSS PUTS A BUZZER INSIDE THE BOWL. THE BUTTON ECHOES WHEN PRESSED.]

[MOSS PUTS A SECOND BOWL UPSIDE DOWN OVER THE BUZZER. THE SOUND IS NOW DULL AND RUMBLY.]

PON: Is that a different answer?

ETTI: The answer is still not a sound.

MOSS: Can I award the bowl a point?

VERA: The bowl is not a contestant.

[THE BOWL ROLLS OFF THE TABLE. PON CATCHES IT BEFORE IT HITS THE FLOOR, BUT THE BUZZER FALLS OUT AND SOUNDS.]

ETTI: The buzzer went off. No one answered.

KALI: We should put the bowl away.

PON: Is the sound part of the answer?

KALI: The sound is in the bowl.

[THEY TRY A QUESTION. KALI BUZZES; THE BOWL RINGS LIKE A SMALL BELL. MOSS PLAYS A CLAPPING TRACK.] 

ETTI: The scoreboard says one point, not applause.

MOSS: The audience responds to noise.

VERA: The audience can hear the question.

[THEY TAKE THE BUZZER OUT OF THE BOWL. THE CLANGING STOPS.]

ETTI: The score is still one point to Kali.

MOSS: Can we have a bonus for recognizing the sound?

VERA: What sound?

MOSS: The bowl.

PON: I recognized it when it clanged.

KALI: That was not a question.

[ETTI REMOVES THE BOWL FROM THE SET. MOSS TRIES TO ROLL IT BACK IN, BUT IT MAKES A LOW RUMBLE ACROSS THE FLOOR.]

VERA: The bowl has exited the round.

### Scene Three: Final Round

VERA: Final question. This bird builds a hanging nest from woven grass. Name the bird after I finish the clue.

[THE TIMER STARTS. PON'S WING HOVERS OVER THE BUTTON. KALI LOOKS AT THE BOWL.]

VERA: It is known for nesting in colonies near open grassland.

[KALI BUZZES AFTER THE LAST WORD.]

KALI: Weaverbird.

ETTI: Correct. That is the final point.

MOSS: We have a tie if we count the early buzzer.

ETTI: We do not.

[PON NODS. THE BOWL IS EMPTY. VERA PLACES THE SCORECARD INSIDE IT.]

VERA: The winner is Kali, with two points.

KALI: Thank you.

PON: I knew the first answer. I should have waited.

[MOSS STARTS THE ROUND-END FANFARE. ETTI SIGNALS HIM TO WAIT UNTIL THE FINAL SCORE IS POSTED.]

MOSS: We have the scores.

ETTI: The scores are on the card.

[ETTI READS THE TWO TOTALS. KALI HAS TWO; PON HAS ONE. THE FANFARE PLAYS ONCE, WITHOUT A REPLAY.] 

VERA: The game is complete.

ETTI: The record shows the timing and the score.

### Tag

[SFX: BOWL CLINKS.]

MOSS: What is this bowl for now?

VERA: The scorecards.

ETTI: Not the buzzer.
