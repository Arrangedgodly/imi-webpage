# The Seed Exchange Has a Waiting List

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: community call-in programs whose hosts turn a simple exchange into a prestige event*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; seed keeper Odo, a gibbon; guest co-host Vex, an orangutan; gardener Pip, a capuchin; volunteer Seli, a lemur; visiting gardener Nari, a macaque.

SFX: Community radio booth. Paper packets rustle.

MARA: This is the Seed Exchange. Bring labeled seeds, take what you can use, and leave room for the next gardener.

VEX: We have a waiting list. I propose a velvet rope and a priority tier.

ODO: The list is for people waiting to collect packets. There are four names and enough packets for everyone.

PIP: I brought beans from the south bed. They are labeled with the year and the drying date.

SELI: I have the table map. Beans go in the blue tray, flowers in the green tray, and the labels face up.

VEX: The most rare-looking packet should go first.

MARA: We don't know what's rare from the paper color. Read the label.

SFX: Packet set down; chair scrapes.

ODO: This one is marigold. It can go in the green tray.

VEX: It has a gold edge. Surely it is premium.

PIP: That's dried leaf stuck to the envelope. The seeds are ordinary marigold.

VEX: Then we need a premium leaf category.

SELI: Please don't add categories while people are waiting. The table is getting crowded.

MARA: Vex, help Seli sort the packets by their labels. Odo will call the list in order.

ODO: First is Nari. She brought peas and asked for bean seed.

VEX: Why shouldn't I choose the first packet? I'm hosting.

MARA: Because you haven't joined the list, and hosting doesn't make you first.

SFX: Paper slides; a packet drops into the blue tray.

PIP: The bean packet needs a note that it came from the shaded row. The plants stayed shorter there.

SELI: I'll add that note to the shared card, not cover the label.

VEX: What if the person at the front takes the last packet?

ODO: The list shows how many people asked for each type. We can divide the bean packets before opening the table.

MARA: And if something runs out, we write it down for next season instead of making a dramatic announcement.

SFX: Door opens; footsteps approach.

ODO: Nari is here. The blue and green trays are clear, and each packet has its label facing up.

PIP: Take the beans you planned for. There are two packets left for the next person.

VEX: The waiting list has reached its first name.

MARA: The exchange is open. Keep the aisle clear.


ODO: Before we call the next name, I want to check the moisture cards. A seed packet that feels damp stays in the closed box, not on the exchange table.

PIP: My beans are dry. I stored them in paper, away from the greenhouse sink.

SFX: Odo sets a damp flower packet in a separate tray.

SELI: I'll set the damp packet aside and write its variety on the waiting card so nobody takes it by mistake.

VEX: A waiting card for a packet that cannot be collected sounds like another waiting list.

MARA: It tells the gardener when to come back. That's useful information, not a status tier.

SFX: Pencil writes on card. A box lid closes.

ODO: Nari has selected beans and left peas for the next person. The counts match the requests.

PIP: I'll take one envelope of calendula. The bees used that row all summer.

SELI: Mark the packet taken. The table still has room for another gardener.


MARA: The radio list is closed for tonight. We will post a fresh count before the next exchange.

ODO: Every packet remaining is labeled, dry, and back in its box.

TAG

VEX: I have reserved a special seed packet for myself.

SELI: It's the leaf you peeled off the envelope.

VEX: I'll plant it in a premium category.
