# A Recipe for Echoes

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: intimate cooking podcasts, overproduced food close-ups translated into sound, and hosts who describe every bite as a revelation*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A cooking podcaster records a crisp apple tart, but the studio's echo turns each kitchen sound into a second, increasingly judgmental chef.

Cast: Host Vela, a macaque; sound mixer Jori, a gibbon; chef Nima, an orangutan; assistant Piri, a capuchin; listener-call guest Roko, a baboon.

[SFX: KNIFE CHOPS APPLE. THE CHOP ECHOES TWICE.]

VELA: Welcome to Soft Crumb, the podcast where we make a recipe sound as comforting as it tastes. That was a crisp apple.

JORI: That was three crisp apples, by echo.

NIMA: It was one apple and a room with hard walls.

PIRI: The microphone has picked up the bowl, the counter, and my tail tapping the chair.

VELA: We can edit the tail.

PIRI: Please don't cut off the rhythm. It's the only part of this recording with a steady tempo.

ACT ONE

Nima weighs flour and asks for cold butter. Piri sets a bowl by the mic. The spoon echoes. Jori lowers the gain, but Vela's whispered ingredients still reach the microphone.

VELA: Two hundred grams of flour.

NIMA: The listener needs to know grams, not whisper it to the sleeve.

VELA: It is an intimate format.

JORI: It is currently a sleeve format.

Nima cuts the butter into the flour. Every tap returns from the soundproofing panels. Vela describes the texture as a sandy beach. Nima says it should resemble coarse crumbs. Vela asks if the listener can hear the difference. Jori plays both descriptions back over the knife sound; the echo repeats "beach" after Nima says "crumbs."

PIRI: The second chef prefers the beach.

NIMA: The second chef is a wall.

Roko asks about ripe pears. Nima says soft ones will collapse in the tart; Vela suggests a dramatic puree. Nima says he can make a different dessert.

ACT TWO

Roko mashes one pear in a bowl for a test. The sound reaches the studio a second later through his call. Jori has added a delay so they can all hear it. The bowl scrape repeats. Vela treats the echo as a second listener response.

VELA: We have applause from the kitchen.

ROKO: That is me scraping the bowl.

PIRI: The echo sounds enthusiastic.

Nima asks Roko to slice a firmer pear. The call drops, but the studio hears the scrape twice. Vela says the fruit is resisting the recipe. Jori realizes the delay is still running.

JORI: The caller is gone. The bowl is not.

Vela tries recording a close-up sound of sugar sprinkling over the apples. Piri pours too much; the sugar lands in a metal tray and rings like hail. Nima scoops it back into the jar. The echo repeats the pour just as Vela says the apples are lightly sweetened.

NIMA: They are not lightly sweetened. We need to weigh what went into the tray.

VELA: The sound was beautiful.

NIMA: It was the sugar alarm.

ACT TWO-B

Jori switches the studio from the large room microphone to a small one clipped to the recipe table. The echo stops. Nima rebuilds the tart: flour, salt, butter, cold water. Piri measures each ingredient. Vela gives the amounts and waits for Nima to name the texture. The cooking sounds are close, not exaggerated.

NIMA: The dough is ready when it holds together without sticking to your fingers.

PIRI: It stuck to one finger.

NIMA: Then dust that finger, not the whole bowl.

Piri taps flour from her fingertip. A tiny puff hits the mic and makes a soft, dry sound. Jori keeps it. Vela asks Nima to repeat it for the episode. Nima refuses; it happened once and should be described, not staged as a sound effect.

Roko reconnects. He has found two firm pears and asks if the soft ones can go in a compote. Nima says yes, cooked separately with a spoon of water. Roko wants to know if he can put the compote under the tart. Nima says he can serve it beside the slice, where it will not make the pastry soggy. Vela calls that a twist. Nima calls it a plate.

ACT THREE

The tart bakes. Jori removes the echo filter and leaves the table microphone on. The timer rings. Piri's tail taps once against the chair; she stills it. Vela asks about the finished tart. Nima says the crust is crisp, pears tender, center set. Vela takes a bite. One crunch, no imaginary applause.

VELA: I was expecting the room to praise it.

JORI: The room is soundproof now.

NIMA: The pastry did the work.

Roko made compote from the soft pears and sliced the firm ones. His voice arrives once. Vela asks whether the recipe was easy. Nima says weighing takes care but the steps are simple. Piri reads the oven temperature. Jori records it without a dramatic chime.

TAG

[SFX: ONE CLEAN SPOON TAPS A BOWL. NO ECHO.]

PIRI: That was the spoon.

VELA: I know. I was here.

FINAL IMAGE: One clean slice rests on a plate beside the measuring scale, while the silent wall waits for another recipe.
