# The Harbor Bell That Joined the Debate

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio call-in panels that turn an ordinary harbor signal into a heated expert roundtable*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Sela, a langur; harbor keeper Nori, a macaque; caller Bex, a young orangutan; producer Tavi, a capuchin.

[SFX: HARBOR BELL RINGS THREE TIMES. STUDIO PHONE LIGHTS FLASH.]

SELA: You are listening to Harbor Hour. That bell means the west gate is closing for the evening tide.

BEX: It means the ferry is late. My aunt has been waiting at the landing for twenty minutes.

NORI: The bell rang three times. That is the gate signal. The ferry uses two.

TAVI: The audience poll says seventy percent heard a ferry. We should stay with the controversy.

SELA: Nori, defend the bell.

NORI: It is a bronze bell on a tower. It cannot defend itself, and it should not need to.

[SFX: BELL RINGS TWICE, FARTHER AWAY. PHONE LIGHT FLASHES.]

BEX: There, two rings. That's the ferry.

SELA: Our open line is buzzing. Caller, what do you hear?

BEX: Still me. I am at the landing. The ferry horn is blowing because the boat is approaching.

TAVI: We can put a microphone outside and ask the bell directly.

NORI: The bell has no microphone.

SELA: The harbor keeper has arrived in studio. Nori, tell us why the bell's message matters.

NORI: It does not send a message in words. Three rings close the gate. Two call the ferry. The crew uses those patterns because engines and gulls are loud.

TAVI: But bell versus boat is the segment title on our board.

SELA: We could have both experts disagree.

NORI: I am not disagreeing. The signal chart is printed beside the dock.

[SFX: PAPER UNFOLDS; A PENCIL TAPS THE TABLE.]

BEX: I can see the chart now. The ferry signal is two rings. The gate is three. The ferry is almost here, and the gate stays open for five minutes.

TAVI: We can ask listeners to vote again. I have a bell, a boat and a picture of a wave on separate cards.

[SFX: THREE CARDS SHUFFLE. PHONE BUTTONS BEEP RAPIDLY.]

SELA: The wave card is winning.

NORI: The wave card has no signal pattern.

BEX: I pressed it because your question said, "Which sound would you rather hear?"

TAVI: The poll is not about the sound. It's about which side is more convincing.

BEX: Then you should have asked the people at the dock. They heard both bells and watched the ferry arrive.

SELA: We have a live correspondent at the landing.

BEX: I am standing there. I can hold the phone out.

[SFX: PHONE MOVES; DISTANT GULL, TWO BELL RINGS, FERRY ENGINE.]

SELA: The audience has heard the evidence.

TAVI: The poll now says boat.

NORI: The boat was always coming. The bell was telling us when the gate moved.

SELA: Ask the harbor what it thinks of the chart.

NORI: The harbor is water. It also has no microphone.

[SFX: PHONE BUZZES. TAVI SLIDES A CARD ACROSS THE DESK.]

TAVI: Our sponsor asks us to announce a special bell-shaped cake giveaway during the argument.

BEX: That cake is blocking the chart.

SELA: Move it before the tide schedule disappears.

[SFX: CAKE BOX SCRAPES; PAPER CHART RUSTLES.]

NORI: The chart says the gate closes in four minutes. The ferry is docking now.

BEX: I can hear the ropes being thrown.

[SFX: ROPE THROWS, WOODEN THUNK, PASSENGERS STEP ABOARD.]

SELA: No winner in the bell debate, then?

TAVI: The audience voted for the cake.

NORI: The cake has a picture of a bell. It is not a signal either.

SELA: We will correct our title. This was a harbor update, not an argument.

[SFX: STUDIO MIC CLICKS. A BELL RINGS THREE TIMES OUTSIDE.]

BEX: Gate closing. I made the ferry.

TAVI: And the cake is still unopened.

SELA: Please do not ring it for dessert.

[SFX: BELL RINGS ONCE, MUTED BY A LID. STUDIO LAUGHTER.]
