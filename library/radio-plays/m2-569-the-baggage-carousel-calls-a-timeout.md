# The Baggage Carousel Calls a Timeout

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio that treats a slow luggage carousel as a close game*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; airport worker Mara, a macaque; traveler Pip, a capuchin; operations clerk Odo, a gibbon; traveler Nari, a lemur; brown-bag owner Rell, a langur (silent).

SFX: Airport baggage hall. Carousel motor turns; wheels rumble.

VEX: The carousel is trailing by three bags in the final minute.

MARA: It's a luggage belt. The bags come off in sequence when they reach the opening.

PIP: My red case hasn't arrived. The airline told me it was checked through.

ODO: The tag number is 214. I am checking the arrival screen, not making a score.

NARI: My blue suitcase has gone around twice. Should I pull it off now?

MARA: Wait until it reaches the clear opening. Don't reach over the moving belt.

VEX: Nari goes for the interception!

NARI: I am waiting for the handle to come around.

SFX: Belt slows, then stops.

PIP: The carousel stopped. Is that normal?

ODO: The operator has paused it because a brown bag is caught near the return lip. Its tag is 882. No one should pull on it.

VEX: The game is delayed.

MARA: The worker is fixing a jam. We can stand behind the yellow line.

SFX: Radio click; released bag lowers onto a cart, which rolls to the nearby counter.

ODO: The bag is clear. Since no one has claimed it, I have brought it to the staffed counter with its tag visible. We will not open it.

VEX: A substitute player has left the field.

MARA: It is property waiting for its owner. Start the belt when the opening is clear.

SFX: Motor starts; belt turns evenly.

VEX: The match resumes.

PIP: I see my tag. Red case, number 214.

MARA: Let it reach the opening. Check the tag before you lift it.

PIP: Tag matches my claim ticket. I'm lifting it from the opening, away from the belt.

NARI: My blue case is here too. I can take it by the handle now.

VEX: A double victory.

ODO: The screen shows one claim still open. The brown bag is at the staffed counter, not on the belt.

VEX: Can we name it captain while it waits?

MARA: No. It has an owner, and we do not put a stranger's property on air as a mascot.

PIP: The claim screen has no jersey number. It has the tag, the carousel, and one open case.

VEX: Then the scoreboard is missing its star player.

MARA: The board is showing baggage status. Please stop trying to interview the luggage.

PIP: My case is closed, zipper intact. I can move to the public area.

VEX: The final score is two bags to one.

MARA: There is no score. Two travelers have their bags, one bag is still being identified, and the carousel is moving.

NARI: I can hear the motor running evenly now.

ODO: Operations are normal. We will stop the belt if another item catches.

NARI: Rell, the owner with ticket 882, is at the desk. They say there is a yellow ribbon on the handle.

MARA: The bag is already at the staffed counter. Match the ticket and the private detail there; don't announce the contents.

ODO: Ticket matches, and the yellow ribbon is on the handle. I am returning the closed bag to its owner.

SFX: Claim ticket slides across the counter; bag handle clicks into its extended position.

VEX: The audience wants to know what is inside.

MARA: The audience does not own the bag. Keep its contents private.

NARI: The bag is closed and back with its owner. No reveal for the broadcast.

PIP: My case is still in my hand, and the carousel keeps moving. That's the entire match report.

MARA: We can return to the terminal announcement. The baggage hall is open, and the claim is closed.

VEX: The carousel has avoided elimination.

MARA: It has resumed baggage service. Let's sign off and clear the claim area.

TAG

PIP: I am taking my case to the exit.

VEX: No replay?
