# The Moon Has a Leaky Roof

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: science fiction radio drama convention, where a lunar colony treats a ceiling drip as an alien signal before tracing it to condensation*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Mission Controller VELA, macaque; technician ODO, baboon; botanist MIRI, gibbon; radio operator PAVI, capuchin.

[SFX: LOW AIR SYSTEM HUM. DISTANT METAL TICK.] 

VELA: Lunar station, log entry four hundred. We have detected a rhythmic signal above the seed bay.

PAVI: It repeats every nine seconds.

[SFX: TICK. PAUSE. TICK.] 

ODO: Could be an external transmitter.

MIRI: Could be water.

Vela asks for a visual check. Odo moves a ladder. Its feet squeak across the metal floor.

ODO: I am approaching the ceiling panel.

PAVI: The signal is faster now.

MIRI: That is the ladder.

Odo reaches the panel. A droplet falls into an empty cup.

[SFX: PLINK.] 

VELA: The signal has entered the room.

MIRI: It is condensate from the cooler.

The station shakes as the ventilation fan changes pitch. The tapping stops. Pavi asks if the source has gone quiet. Miri checks a small leaf tray and finds it damp at one end. She moves the seedlings away from the drip.

ODO: We should isolate the bay.

MIRI: We should place a catch cup and clear the drain.

Pavi listens to the radio. A repeating tone returns, but now it is faint and fast. Odo says the external signal may be adapting. Miri opens the service hatch. A bead of water runs down the inside seam.

MIRI: The cold pipe is sweating.

VELA: In space?

MIRI: The air inside is warm. The pipe is cold. Water forms here and travels along the panel.

Odo checks the insulation. It has slipped away from one joint. He tightens it while Miri holds the panel. The metal rings once.

[SFX: METAL CLANG.] 

PAVI: A new signal.

ODO: That was my wrench.

Pavi records the drip intervals while Miri checks the seedlings. The taps come closer together when the fan runs at full speed. Odo turns the fan down. The tapping slows. Vela compares the rhythm with the radio signal and hears no pattern in it.

VELA: Not a message. A leak.

PAVI: The timing follows the fan.

Miri traces the pipe with a cloth and finds condensation along the cold joint. She asks Odo for insulation tape. He opens a kit and finds three rolls: repair tape, labeling tape and a roll of old mission stickers. He chooses the repair tape.

[SFX: TAPE UNROLLS, CUT WITH SCISSORS.] 

The joint is wrapped. The droplet catches at the edge, swells, then falls into the tray. Odo asks if the repair is complete. Miri says they need to watch it through one fan cycle. Vela announces a hold in the broadcast. Pavi listens as the fan hum rises and lowers. No new drops form.

PAVI: The signal has stopped.

MIRI: The condensation has stopped reaching the panel.

Vela asks if the crew should keep the old audio for the station archive. Pavi says they can label it "condensation on panel," not "unknown transmission." Odo turns the radio off and writes down the fan setting and repair date.

The crew places a shallow tray under the seam and wipes the surrounding surface dry. Miri checks each seedling. One leaf has a drop on it; she shakes the water off gently. Vela asks if the lunar station is safe. Odo says the leak is contained, the pipe is secured and the drain needs cleaning at next maintenance.

PAVI: The rhythm is gone.

MIRI: The roof is dry.

[SFX: HUM CONTINUES. ONE QUIET PLINK.] 

Everyone pauses.

ODO: That is the catch tray.

VELA: Confirmed. One drop, no mystery.

Pavi records the maintenance note. The crew returns to the control room. Miri turns the seedlings toward the lamp. Odo closes the hatch and taps it once to ensure it is latched.

[SFX: SINGLE TAP.] 

PAVI: We have another signal.

MIRI: We have a closed panel.

The crew laughs quietly. The station hum fades into the radio theme.
