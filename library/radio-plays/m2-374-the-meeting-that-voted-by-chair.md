# The Meeting That Voted by Chair

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: civic radio coverage that counts chair noises as votes and misreports a community decision*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Chair Jovi, a macaque; clerk Mina, a capuchin; host Peko, a lemur; resident Sol, a gibbon.

[SFX: COMMUNITY HALL. CHAIRS SCRAPE. MICROPHONE HUMS.]

PEKO: We are live at the meeting where residents will vote on keeping the footpath open after dark.

JOVI: The clerk will call each name. Raise your hand for yes.

MINA: We will count raised hands and record them in the minutes.

[SFX: THREE CHAIRS SQUEAK. A COUNTER BEEPS.]

PEKO: That sounds like three yes votes.

MINA: The counter is attached to the microphone. It counts chair squeaks.

SOL: I have not voted yet.

SOL: I can hear the clerk's paper, but not see which motion you are on.

MINA: I will read the motion aloud before each vote. The radio feed should carry the same wording as the room.

PEKO: I can repeat the title, but not invent the result.

JOVI: Good. We vote on one question at a time.

JOVI: The first motion is to keep the path open until nine. All in favor, raise your hand.

[SFX: HANDS RAISE. ONE CHAIR CREAKS.]

PEKO: The counter has recorded a seven-to-two result already. Shall I announce it before the clerk finishes?

MINA: No. The counter has no ballot. I have only counted the raised hands in this motion.

JOVI: We will wait for the no vote and then close the tally.

SOL: The chair noise is still appearing on the broadcast meter.

PEKO: I will turn the meter display away from the live feed.

[SFX: A SCREEN SWITCH CLICKS. THE COUNTER BEEP DISAPPEARS.]

MINA: I count six hands.

PEKO: The sound counter says seven.

MINA: The chair is not a voter.

JOVI: The motion passes six to two.

PEKO: The live tally says seven to two.

MINA: I haven't counted the no votes yet. That tally is wrong.

[SFX: COUNTER BEEPS AGAIN AS A CHAIR SCRAPES.]

JOVI: Please hold the chairs still while we vote.

SOL: People cannot sit without moving.

PEKO: We could use the counter to capture audience response.

MINA: It has no way to distinguish a chair from a clap or a footstep.

JOVI: We need an accurate record. Turn the counter off.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. COUNTER STOPS.]

PEKO: Listeners will lose the live number.

MINA: I'll announce it after each count.

JOVI: All opposed to keeping the path open until nine, raise your hand.

[SFX: HANDS LIFT. PAPER SHIFTS.]

MINA: Two opposed. Motion passes six to two.

PEKO: I will correct the broadcast. Six yes, two no.

SOL: I have a question. Does the path light stay on until nine, or do we need a separate motion?

JOVI: The posted motion includes the lights. Mina, check the wording.

MINA: It says "path access and lamps until nine." Both are included.

PEKO: Then the tally is final.

JOVI: We also have a proposal to leave one chair at the entrance for late arrivals.

[SFX: ONE CHAIR SCRAPES ACROSS FLOOR.]

PEKO: The counter is off, but that chair made a loud sound.

MINA: A chair is not a vote even when it moves on purpose.

SOL: I moved it so my neighbor could reach the aisle. She has a cane.

JOVI: Then we should leave the seat where it helps, not turn it into a motion.

PEKO: The radio report says six to two, path and lamps open until nine, with one chair moved for access.

MINA: That is accurate.

JOVI: Next item on the agenda.

PEKO: The meeting has not ended.

JOVI: Correct. The path vote has.

[SFX: MINUTES PAGE TURNS. MICROPHONE HUMS SOFTLY.]

SOL: I will use the lit path home.

MINA: The lamps turn on at dusk and stay on until nine.

PEKO: The chairs can keep their own opinions.

[SFX: A CHAIR CREAKS ONCE. NO COUNTER BEEPS. MIC CLOSES.]
