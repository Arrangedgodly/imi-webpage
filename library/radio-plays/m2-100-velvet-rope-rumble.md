# The Velvet Rope Rumble

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio wrestling play-by-play, exaggerated sports jargon, and a referee who must call an accidental dance-off*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Nib, a langur play-by-play host; Tavi, a capuchin color commentator; Bramble, an orangutan wrestler; Sprocket, a macaque referee; and the arena crowd.*

[SFX: A small bell rings. A vast crowd roars as if a mountain has just entered wearing boots.]

NIB: Welcome to the Canopy Colosseum, where Bramble the Boulder meets The Elegant Marmoset under the ancient rules of wrestling and one new rule: nobody may touch the ceremonial fruit table.

TAVI: That table is surrounded by velvet rope, Nib. Velvet rope means importance. It also means the fruit has better seating than I do.

NIB: The Elegant Marmoset has not appeared. The announcer says he is delayed by a dramatic entrance.

[SFX: Three cymbal crashes. A fourth cymbal falls over.]

SPROCKET: I am referee Sprocket. I have the rulebook, the whistle, and a banana tucked behind my ear for emergencies.

NIB: What kind of emergency?

SPROCKET: Any situation where I forget why I entered the ring.

BRAMBLE: I came to wrestle, not wait for a man to arrive through fog he ordered by the hour.

[SFX: A fog machine coughs, then produces one dense puff that drifts directly into the audience.] 

TAVI: The fog has taken a seat. It paid for ringside.

NIB: The Elegant Marmoset has emerged at last. He is walking slowly, one paw extended, as though the ring has personally disappointed him.

SPROCKET: Contestants, touch hands. No scratching, no biting, no tossing the fruit table. That's in bold now.

BRAMBLE: I challenge you to a test of strength.

ELEGANT MARMOSET: I challenge you to a test of posture.

NIB: The wrestlers clasp hands. Bramble lowers his shoulder. The Marmoset lifts one eyebrow. Neither moves.

TAVI: The crowd is counting. They are counting in two different numbers because one side of the arena has a drum.

[SFX: Crowd chants "One! Two!" The drum answers with a slow "Seven!"]

NIB: Ten seconds pass. The contestants remain joined at the hands. The referee starts circling them, then keeps circling after the match stops moving.

ELEGANT MARMOSET: Bramble, you are strong, but your thumb is trembling.

BRAMBLE: It is holding up your entire performance.

NIB: They break apart. Bramble charges. The Marmoset sidesteps, and Bramble's momentum carries him into the ring corner. He bounces once, then lands in a seated position.

NIB: Bramble rises and reaches for the Marmoset. The Marmoset dives beneath his arm, rolls across the mat, and stops precisely in front of the fruit table.

[SFX: Velvet rope twangs.]

SPROCKET: I have to inspect the table. Both of you remain still.

NIB: The referee turns his back to check the rope knot. The Marmoset whispers something to Bramble.

BRAMBLE: Fine. We do it your way.

NIB: They both take one careful step away from the table. The crowd cheers. Then they take another. Bramble raises both hands to show they are empty. The Marmoset salutes the fruit.

TAVI: This is the most respectful retreat since the mountain learned it was standing on a picnic.

SPROCKET: The table is safe. Resume the match.

NIB: Bramble and the Marmoset meet in the middle. They circle. Bramble hooks the Marmoset's arm; the Marmoset pivots and catches Bramble's sleeve. They spin faster and faster, not throwing punches, each trying to turn the other away from the table.

[SFX: Shoes squeak in a rapid circle. The crowd's chant accelerates.] 

TAVI: They have invented the protective waltz. It has no official name, but I can hear a sponsorship opportunity.

SPROCKET: Stop spinning! The ropes are getting dizzy.

NIB: The two wrestlers release at once. The ropes sway, gently bouncing them back toward one another. They bump foreheads and fall side by side, each with one arm draped over the other.

[SFX: Bell rings.]

SPROCKET: Pin! Bramble's shoulders are down. The Marmoset's shoulders are down. I cannot count both as winners without using my other hand.

TAVI: Use the rulebook.

SPROCKET: The rulebook says a double pin earns a dance-off.

NIB: Both wrestlers look toward the fruit table. Their eyes meet. They begin to dance, still maintaining a respectful distance from the velvet rope. Bramble's heavy steps shake loose the rope post; it swings like a metronome. The Marmoset follows its rhythm with precise little hops.

TAVI: Bramble has the power. The Marmoset has the footwork. The rope post has the tempo.

NIB: The audience stomps. The table trembles; a banana rolls to the edge, and Sprocket catches it.

SPROCKET: Emergency banana received.

NIB: The dancers stop. The crowd gives them a standing ovation, except the spectators in the upper row, who are still seated because their chairs are stuck.

BRAMBLE: No winner, then.

ELEGANT MARMOSET: We both protected the table.

SPROCKET: The official result is a draw, with a bonus dance and no fruit casualties.

TAVI: A historic night. The velvet rope remains undefeated.

[SFX: The band plays a victory fanfare. The final cymbal crashes.]

NIB: This is Nib at the Colosseum, where the next match begins after the fog signs its waiver. Good night.

[SFX: Bell rings once; the crowd chants "Rope! Rope!"]
