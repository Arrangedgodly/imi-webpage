# A Lecture on Echoes

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: educational lecture broadcast convention, where the lecturer's echo keeps answering questions before the audience can*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Lecturer ODO, baboon; technician MIRI, macaque; student PAVA, gibbon; host KES, capuchin.

[SFX: LECTURE HALL HUM, MICROPHONE TAP.] 

KES: Welcome to our public lecture on echoes. Professor Odo will explain how a sound returns after reflecting off a surface.

ODO: Thank you. An echo is-

ECHO: Is-

ODO: A repetition-

ECHO: Repetition-

PAVA: Is the echo coming from the back wall?

MIRI: The microphone is feeding into the speaker.

ODO: That is not an echo. It is feedback.

KES: We promised an echo lecture.

MIRI: We can turn the speaker away from the microphone.

She rotates it. Odo claps once. The hall returns a soft clap.

[SFX: CLAP, NATURAL ECHO.] 

ODO: There. The sound traveled to the back wall.

ECHO: Wall.

PAVA: Can I ask a question?

ODO: Yes.

ECHO: Yes.

PAVA: Why did it repeat?

ODO: Because the sound reflected.

ECHO: Reflected.

PAVA: It sounds like you agree with everything I say.

ODO: I do not.

ECHO: Do not.

Kes asks Odo to demonstrate with a sentence. He says, "The hill is far away." The echo returns only "away."

ODO: It repeats the last part.

MIRI: Depending on the distance and the surface.

PAVA: What if I say something short?

ODO: Try it.

PAVA: Acorn.

[SFX: ACORN DROPS. NO ECHO.] 

PAVA: No answer.

ODO: The soft carpet absorbed the sound.

PAVA: The floor ate my question.

Kes moves everyone to the stairwell, where the walls are bare. Pava asks if the echo can repeat a whole sentence. Odo claps; the sound returns clearly.

[SFX: CLAP, LONGER ECHO.] 

PAVA: It answered before I asked.

ODO: It answered your clap.

PAVA: I was about to ask about clapping.

Miri tests the microphone with a low tone. The speaker repeats it immediately, creating a howl. Everyone covers their ears.

[SFX: FEEDBACK WHINE, CUT.] 

MIRI: That's feedback, not an echo. We need to keep the microphone away from the speaker.

KES: The lecture has demonstrated what an echo is not.

ODO: That is still educational.

PAVA: Can we try the hillside?

Before they leave, Kes asks the audience to vote on whether the stairwell is better than the hall. The raised hands make no sound, so Odo cannot use them as evidence.

ODO: Please clap for the stairwell.

[SFX: CLAPS, TWO ECHOES.]

PAVA: The audience clapped twice.

MIRI: They clapped once. The walls repeated them.

KES: We will count that as one vote and one reflection.

Odo carries the microphone into the stairwell. Pava speaks from the bottom step while Miri listens at the top. Her words arrive late and softer.

MIRI: The distance changed the echo.

PAVA: It sounds like a second speaker.

ODO: Only because the sound took time to return.

KES: Could the echo interrupt the next question?

ODO: Not unless the question is long enough to meet itself.

Pava tries: "A long question is a sentence that-"

[SFX: QUESTION ECHO OVERLAPS.]

PAVA: I have interrupted myself.

Odo leads them outside. He calls, "Is anyone there?" The hill returns, "Anyone there?"

[SFX: CALL, ECHO.] 

KES: The audience has answered.

PAVA: The audience is the hill.

ODO: We should not ask it for questions.

Kes takes a question from the floor. A child asks if an echo can learn new words. Odo says it repeats what it hears. Pava tests this by shouting "class dismissed." The echo sends it back across the valley.

[SFX: CLASS DISMISSED, ECHO.] 

KES: The lecture is over.

ODO: I did not say that.

ECHO: Over.

MIRI: Even the hill has left.

KES: Thank you for attending. Please take your questions with you.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS, FAINT ECHO.]
