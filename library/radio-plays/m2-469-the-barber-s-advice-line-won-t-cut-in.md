# The Barber's Advice Line Won't Cut In

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: makeover call-in shows that prescribe a style before hearing what the caller wants*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; barber Pip, a capuchin; caller Nari, a lemur; grooming elder Odo, a gibbon; sponsor voice Vex, an orangutan; caller Runi, a baboon.

SFX: Studio phone rings. Scissors click twice, then stop.

MARA: This is Trim Talk, where callers describe a look and Pip tells them what to do.

PIP: I ask questions first.

VEX: The show card says "before and after."

NARI, on phone: I have a burr caught in my shoulder fur. My troop keeps trying to groom it out, but it snags.

PIP: Is it a thorn or a seed burr?

NARI: Flat and round. It came from the low grass.

MARA: Can you see it?

NARI: I can feel it when I turn.

VEX: We could give her a short cut.

NARI: I did not ask to lose my coat.

ODO: Good. We should not cut fur because a product card needs a reveal.

PIP: The burr has hooked tips. Don't pull it through the fur. Can someone hold the hair around it while you loosen it?

NARI: My friend is here.

SFX: Soft grooming clicks. Nari's fur brushes against cloth.

NARI: She is holding the coat away from my skin.

PIP: Now use your fingers to open the burr from the center, one hook at a time.

VEX: That is not a makeover. That is a careful removal.

MARA: It is the caller's question.

SFX: A burr clicks open. A light rustle.

NARI: It's loose. I can lift it out.

ODO: Does the fur feel pulled?

NARI: No. The hairs are flat again.

PIP: Comb the patch in the direction it grows.

SFX: Small comb strokes.

NARI: The snag is gone.

VEX: We have a before and after.

NARI: Before, I had a burr. After, I don't.

MARA: That's a useful transformation.

VEX: Our sponsor sells a wide-tooth comb.

PIP: The tool is useful if the burr is out. It won't cut through the hooks.

VEX: Can we still say it changed her look?

NARI: It changed how my shoulder feels.

ODO: That is a better answer.

MARA: We have time for another caller.

SFX: Phone line clicks.

VEX: Our sponsor also sells mirror polish.

PIP: We are not polishing the caller.

ODO: You can say the comb helped loosen fur after the burr came out.

MARA: Only if it did.

NARI: I used my fingers first. Then Pip asked me to comb the area.

VEX: The product placement has become a sequence.

MARA: The sequence is the advice.

SFX: Another phone rings.

MARA: Caller Runi, what is your concern?

RUNI, on phone: A seed pod is caught in my forearm fur. My grooming partner is beside me, but last time we pulled at the same time and tightened it.

NARI: Have your partner hold the hair away from your skin. You loosen one hook at a time, from the center.

SFX: Gentle grooming clicks. A seed pod opens.

RUNI: It came free. My partner can brush out the loose fibers now.

PIP: Another before and after.

RUNI: Before, I had a seed. After, I have a seed in my palm.

VEX: The sponsor has a palm-sized comb.

MARA: It can wait until the caller's done.

VEX: This is turning into an advice line.

MARA: It always was.

ODO: Can the show card say that?

VEX: It can, but we lose "before and after."

NARI: Keep both. Put the before in the question and the after in the answer.

MARA: That is our closing tip.

TAG

SFX: Scissors snip a paper strip, not fur.

PIP: The sponsor's card needed a trim.

VEX: You cut the phrase "instant makeover."
