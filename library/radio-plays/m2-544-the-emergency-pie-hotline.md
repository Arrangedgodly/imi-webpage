# The Emergency Pie Hotline

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in crisis shows that inflate a missing dessert into a national emergency*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Nari, a lemur; caller Vex, an orangutan; baker Mara, a macaque; switchboard operator Odo, a gibbon; neighbor Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Small radio booth. Phone rings twice; wall clock ticks.

NARI: This is the Community Help Hour. What is the emergency?

VEX, on phone: The pie is gone. The whole table will collapse without it.

ODO: Caller, what kind of pie and where was it last seen?

VEX: Apple pie, on the kitchen counter at two. At three, only the cooling rack remained.

MARA: I baked the pie, then moved it to the pantry shelf so the table could be cleaned. It is covered and cooling.

VEX: We have a disappearance and a suspect.

PIP: I carried the empty cooling rack to the sink. I didn't touch the pie.

NARI: We have a location, a baker, and a rack. No need to accuse anyone. Mara, can you check that the pie is still covered before we keep talking?

SFX: Door opens, soft footsteps, pantry latch.

MARA: It is here, intact, on the pantry shelf. The cover is on, and it has cooled enough to move.

VEX: The pie has entered witness protection.

ODO: The pantry shelf is inside the kitchen. It is not a secret location.

NARI: Why did you call it an emergency?

VEX: The guests arrive at four. I promised a dramatic unveiling, and I couldn't find it.

PIP: The guests have not arrived yet. We can put the pie on the table when the room is clean.

MARA: We need one person to carry the pie with both hands and set it down on the trivet. The table is dry now.

VEX: I volunteer as presenter.

MARA: You can carry the serving plate. I will carry the pie. Please don't lift the cover until it's on the trivet.

SFX: Plate slides; careful footsteps.

PIP: Table is clear. Trivet centered.

MARA: Pie is on the trivet. I am lifting the cover now.

VEX: The public may approach.

NARI: The guests are in the next room. We'll tell them dessert is ready after it stops steaming.

ODO: Call log should say "dessert located in pantry," not "pie crisis resolved."

VEX: But "crisis resolved" sounds important.

MARA: The pie was safe the whole time. The only problem was that the table was being cleaned.

PIP: And the rack went to the sink.

NARI: Once the pie is on the table, we can close the hotline. If someone calls about an actual emergency, Odo will connect them to the proper service; a missing pie is a kitchen question.

VEX: I can announce dessert from the doorway.

MARA: Announce that dessert is ready. Don't call it a rescue.

SFX: A small bell from the next room; guests murmur.

ODO: Hotline line is clear. Caller Vex has no further concern.

VEX: I have one. Can I have the first slice?

MARA: Once it has cooled enough to cut cleanly. That was true before the call too.

SFX: Kitchen clock advances; soft plate set down.

PIP: The table is set. The pie is still on the trivet, uncovered now that the crust is cool.

NARI: How many guests are expected?

VEX: Six, including the radio audience.

MARA: The radio audience is not in the kitchen. Six guests are in the next room, and we have six plates.

ODO: I can add to the call log that the pie was found before anyone cut it. The location question is closed.

VEX: But what if someone asks for a second dessert?

NARI: Then we will offer fruit. We are not calling the bakery as a crisis service.

PIP: The pie knife is clean and on the board. Mara will cut it; I'll carry the slices to the table.

MARA: One slice per plate. We can save any extra for later.

VEX: The hotline has turned into dinner planning.

NARI: That is what the problem was.

VEX: The emergency is delicious.
