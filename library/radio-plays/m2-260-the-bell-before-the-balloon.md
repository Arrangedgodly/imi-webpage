# The Bell Before the Balloon

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live community-event radio coverage, countdowns, and correspondents who narrate a balloon launch before the knot is untied*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST MALO, a capuchin
REPORTER SERA, a macaque
EVENT ORGANIZER VIM, a gibbon
CHILD CALLER TULI, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: COMMUNITY PARK, CROWD MURMUR, A BELL TESTS TWICE.]

MALO: We are live from the Canopy Day balloon launch. The launch bell will ring when the balloon is ready.

SERA: The balloon is enormous, bright orange, and still tied to the ground.

VIM: The audience is gathered. We only need to untie the anchor rope.

MALO: The ceremony begins with our countdown. Ten!

SERA: The knot is not loose yet.

MALO: Nine!

VIM: The rope is tied to the park bench.

MALO: Eight!

SERA: The bench is now moving.

[SFX: BENCH SCRAPES. CROWD GASPS.]

MALO: The balloon is pulling the bench toward the sky.

VIM: It is pulling the bench across the grass. I will untie it.

MALO: Seven!

SERA: The organizer is approaching the knot.

VIM: I am already holding the knot.

MALO: The radio audience cannot see you. Please describe your approach.

VIM: I am standing beside the bench and loosening the rope.

[SFX: KNOT SLIDES, ROPE TIGHTENS.]

SERA: The rope is caught around the bell post.

MALO: The bell is part of the launch.

VIM: The bell is part of the park. The balloon rope has wrapped it.

[SFX: BELL RINGS, CROWD CLAPS.]

MALO: The launch bell has rung! The balloon is ready!

SERA: The balloon has not moved.

TULI: I am calling from the crowd. Are we supposed to clap now?

MALO: The bell has announced the moment.

VIM: It announced that the rope tugged the bell. Please wait.

TULI: My hands are tired from the first clap.

SERA: The crew is unwrapping the rope from the post.

MALO: Is the countdown continuing?

VIM: No. Stop counting.

MALO: Six!

SERA: Malo, you are still counting.

MALO: The countdown has momentum.

[SFX: ROPE SLACKENS. BENCH THUMPS BACK INTO PLACE.]

VIM: The bench is secure. I have the knot free. We can lift the balloon now.

SERA: The crew is raising it clear of the crowd. The anchor rope is still attached to the launch ring.

MALO: Should we ring the bell?

VIM: Not until the ring is unfastened.

TULI: I can hold the launch ring while you remove the rope.

VIM: Thank you. Hold it steady; the balloon is tugging.

[SFX: FABRIC RUSTLES, ROPE SLIDES THROUGH RING.]

SERA: The ring is clear. The balloon is ready.

MALO: We are ready for the final cue.

VIM: No numbers. Bell once, then release.

MALO: Understood. No countdown.

[SFX: CLEAR BELL RING. ROPE RELEASES. CROWD CHEERS.]

SERA: The balloon is rising. The bench remains on the ground. Tuli is waving.

TULI: I am waving with one hand. The other is resting.

MALO: The balloon is now above the trees. This launch is officially complete.

VIM: The crew is packing the rope and returning the bell to the park keeper.

MALO: The bell rang before the balloon, then rang at the right time. Our event has had two ceremonies.

SERA: One accidental and one successful.

TULI: I liked the second one better. It had a balloon.

MALO: From Canopy Day, this is live coverage. The balloon is away, the bench is home, and we will not begin a third countdown.

SERA: The crew is collecting the launch signs. One still reads "Stand here until the balloon is untied."

VIM: I will take it down before anyone obeys it.

MALO: The final instruction is to go home without waiting for another bell.

TULI: I am taking the long path so I can wave at it once more.

[SFX: CROWD FADES, BELL RINGS ONCE FROM DISTANCE.]
