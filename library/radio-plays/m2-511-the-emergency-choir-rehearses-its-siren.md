# The Emergency Choir Rehearses Its Siren

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live event broadcasts that mistake a rehearsal cue for an emergency*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Conductor Seli, a lemur; singer Mara, a macaque; tenor Pip, a capuchin; announcer Vex, an orangutan; stagehand Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Community hall. Choir members shuffle sheet music.

VEX: We interrupt this broadcast for a citywide alert.

SELI: That's the rehearsal chord.

PIP: I was warming up.

MARA: The bell did not ring. The conductor gave the cue.

ODO: The emergency light is off.

VEX: The audience heard a rising note. They need context.

SELI: It's a siren passage for the winter concert.

VEX: We can still call it an alert.

MARA: Don't send people running because we sang a scale.

SFX: Paper rustles. A chair scoots back.

PIP: I can sing the passage more softly.

VEX: Then it won't sound urgent.

SELI: It's meant to imitate the wind, not warn the town.

ODO: We should put the program title on the board.

MARA: "Songs for Storm Season." That tells people what they are hearing.

VEX: The board is behind the choir.

PIP: I can carry it to the entrance.

SFX: Board lifted, door opens.

SELI: Careful, the corner catches the latch.

ODO: I'll hold the door.

VEX: Can we perform the cue once more for the broadcast?

MARA: Only after the notice is visible and the hall doors stay open.

SFX: Board slides into place by the entrance. Door is propped open.

PIP: From the walkway, I can read "Songs for Storm Season."

ODO: The sheet music marks the rising passage at measure twelve. I'll add that to the slate so the sound has context.

VEX: We should save the title for after the chord.

MARA: No. You just told our listeners there was a citywide alert.

SELI: I'll announce the program title before we play.

SFX: Microphone clicks on.

SELI: "Songs for Storm Season, rehearsal excerpt. This is music, not an alert."

VEX: That removes the surprise.

MARA: It prevents a false alarm. The emergency light stays off, and the hall is open.

SELI: Before the full passage, let's test one note at rehearsal volume.

SFX: Pip sings a single rising note. The sound carries to the entrance.

VEX: That still sounds like a warning.

MARA: It stops in midair because it is only one note. The next note answers it.

ODO: The score marks the response below the staff. It was hidden under the microphone stand.

SFX: Paper lifts from the stand.

SELI: I'll raise the second part slightly so the chord resolves instead of hanging.

PIP: I can sing that harmony without getting louder.

VEX: If the answer comes too soon, we lose the suspense.

MARA: The concert is music, not a siren drill. Keep the response together.

SFX: Seli taps the stand once, then stops before the choir sings.

ODO: The hall doors are still open, and the sign is visible.

PIP: Then I can sing the phrase without worrying that someone will run outside.

SFX: Choir members shuffle into rows. A music stand scrapes; Pip steadies it.

SELI: Ready from measure twelve.

SFX: A single rising chord swells, then the choir settles into a warm, low hum.

MARA: That's the shape. The storm passes.

VEX: It is more musical when people know what they're hearing.

ODO: The audience is clapping, not running for the exit.

PIP: I'll put the score back in its folder. The notice can stay by the door for the concert.

ODO: I will leave the emergency light switch alone and put the program slate beside the microphone.
