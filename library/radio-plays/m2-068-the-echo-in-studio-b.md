# The Echo in Studio B

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: audio fiction production meeting and sound-effects overreach*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: DIRECTOR VENN, a gibbon; ACTOR KALI, a macaque; SOUND ARTIST MOSS, a capuchin; and INTERN PIP, a tamarin.

[SFX: A DOOR CLOSES, THEN THE SAME DOOR CLOSES AGAIN.] 

VENN: That is not an echo. That is the intern closing the door twice.

PIP: I thought you wanted a double close.

MOSS: We need an echo for the cavern scene. The hero calls out, and the mountain answers.

KALI: What does the mountain say?

VENN: Nothing. It is a mountain.

MOSS: I can make it say nothing with a long tail.

[SFX: KALI SPEAKS. HER WORD RETURNS, THEN RETURNS AGAIN WITH A THIRD, TINIER ECHO.] 

KALI: Is that a mountain?

MOSS: It is three mountains.

VENN: The hero is only in one cave.

PIP: I can hold a sheet of metal farther away.

VENN: Why do you have a sheet of metal?

PIP: We are recording a storm tomorrow.

MOSS: The echo can come from the sheet.

[SFX: METAL SHEET SHAKES. A THUNDERCLAP.] 

KALI: The mountain has become weather.

VENN: The script says the hero whispers.

KALI: I can whisper.

[WHISPER. THE ECHO RETURNS AT FULL VOLUME.] 

KALI: The cavern is shouting back.

MOSS: I can lower the return.

VENN: Make the reply smaller than the original.

MOSS: That seems emotionally honest.

PIP: What if we record the actual tunnel by the water dish?

VENN: Does it echo?

PIP: It echoes if you call from the entrance.

KALI: What should I call?

MOSS: Something brief. We need room tone.

KALI: Hello?

[SFX: A SINGLE ECHO: HELLO.] 

VENN: There. One reply. No thunder, no second mountain.

MOSS: The cave has excellent restraint.

PIP: We should record the walk back. The floor is gravel.

[SFX: FOUR PAIRS OF FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL. THE STEPS GROW QUIETER, THEN ONE SET RETURNS.] 

KALI: Why is someone coming back?

MOSS: That's your footsteps bouncing from the tunnel.

VENN: An echo with a clear motive.

PIP: We can use it when the hero changes her mind.

KALI: The hero does not change her mind. She discovers that she left her lunch at the entrance.

PIP: I have placed a marker beside it.

VENN: Is the marker part of the scene?

PIP: It is a stone.

MOSS: The archive of sound has become a collection of props.

VENN: That is a strong motive.

MOSS: I can add the sound of a lunch tin opening.

KALI: No. The scene ends with a voice and its answer.

PIP: The tunnel has a second doorway. If we record from there, the echo may arrive later.

MOSS: We could use the delay as the mountain thinking.

VENN: The mountain does not think.

KALI: The hero might. She asks a question and waits.

VENN: We should hear the question before the answer.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS TO THE FAR DOOR. A SMALL STONE DROPS. IT BOUNCES TWICE.]

MOSS: That is a clean delay.

PIP: The first bounce is the question.

KALI: And the second?

MOSS: The mountain remembering.

VENN: No. It is just a stone.

KALI: The scene can be about a stone.

VENN: Our script is about finding a cave exit.

PIP: The stone can show the floor slopes toward the door.

MOSS: Then the sound tells the actor where to walk.

KALI: I can follow the two bounces, then stop at the exit.

VENN: That is a useful effect. Keep it small.

[SFX: ONE STONE BOUNCES TWICE, THEN STOPS BY A DOOR.]

MOSS: The mountain has offered directions.

VENN: The stone has.

VENN: Let's record the final take.

[SFX: TUNNEL ROOM TONE.] 

KALI: Hello?

[SFX: HELLO? RETURNS ONCE, FAINTLY.] 

KALI: I heard you.

VENN: Cut. The mountain has spoken exactly once.

PIP: And the lunch is still at the entrance.

MOSS: We can record that for another story.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS WALK OUT, ONE SET, NO ECHO.]
