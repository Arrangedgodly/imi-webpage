# The Crossing Signal's Friendly Voice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: accessible traffic broadcasts that prioritize a cheerful branded voice over clear crossing instructions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Presenter Asha, a lemur; engineer Boro, a macaque; crossing guard Tavi, a gibbon; pedestrian Nemi, a capuchin.

[SFX: STREET CORNER. TRAFFIC PASSES. A CROSSING SIGNAL PLAYS A CHEERFUL JINGLE.]

ASHA: We're live at the new friendly crossing signal. It says, "Have a lovely day!"

NEMI: It never told me when to cross.

TAVI: The walk light is on now. The voice is a separate sound.

BORO: The jingle is from the sponsor. It plays over the spoken instruction.

[SFX: SIGNAL BEEPS. CAR ENGINE APPROACHES.]

ASHA: Can we test it again?

TAVI: Wait until the next cycle. Cars are turning.

NEMI: The old cycle said "Have a lovely day" while cars were turning.

TAVI: That line sounded as if crossing were allowed.

BORO: I will remove it from the wait phase entirely. The walk message will finish before any sponsor sound begins.

NEMI: Then I can hear the crossing before I hear the greeting.

NEMI: I heard the jingle, but not the crossing cue.

BORO: The speaker output is too quiet, and the sponsor track is too loud.

ASHA: Could you raise the whole speaker?

BORO: That would make the jingle louder too. I need separate levels.

[SFX: BUTTONS CLICK. SIGNAL CYCLE ENDS. TRAFFIC EASES.]

TAVI: The walk symbol is dark. We wait.

ASHA: Nemi, can you hear the street?

NEMI: Yes. I can hear the turning car and the signal beeps. The voice just says "lovely day."

BORO: I'll mute the sponsor track and test the crossing instruction.

[SFX: JINGLE CLICKS OFF. A CLEAR VOICE SAYS, "WAIT."]

NEMI: That is clear.

TAVI: The walk light is still off. Good.

ASHA: The sponsor asked for the greeting before every cycle.

BORO: The greeting can play after the crossing, not during the instruction.

BORO: The signal cycles too quickly. The greeting begins before the walk cue ends.

TAVI: That leaves no clear quiet interval for the next "wait."

ASHA: The sponsor wants the jingle after every button press.

NEMI: Then the words and the jingle collide again.

BORO: I can move the jingle to the end of the full crossing cycle, after the walk symbol turns off.

[SFX: CONTROL BUTTONS CLICK. A TEST CYCLE STARTS: "WAIT." SILENCE. TRAFFIC. "CROSS NOW." THE WALK CUE SOUNDS. THE SIGNAL GOES DARK; JINGLE PLAYS.]

NEMI: I hear the words first, then the crossing sound, then the greeting after it is over.

TAVI: That's the order we can use.

NEMI: I need to know when to wait and when to go. The greeting can come later.

[SFX: SIGNAL CYCLE STARTS. VOICE: "WAIT." TRAFFIC PASSES.]

TAVI: Still wait.

[SFX: TRAFFIC STOPS. WALK SIGNAL CHIMES.]

BORO: Now it says, "Cross now."

NEMI: I hear it. I will cross.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS AND CANE TIP MOVE ACROSS THE MARKED CROSSWALK. TRAFFIC REMAINS STOPPED.]

TAVI: Nemi has reached the curb.

ASHA: The cycle is complete. Could the jingle return?

BORO: It can play after the crossing ends and before the next wait message.

[SFX: SHORT JINGLE PLAYS, THEN STOPS. SIGNAL VOICE: "WAIT."]

NEMI: The order makes sense now. Wait, cross now, then the greeting.

ASHA: We will report that the sponsor sound moved after the instruction.

TAVI: The guard can hear the signal and watch the traffic, but the walk symbol stays the authority.

BORO: I'll log the separate levels so nobody turns the whole speaker up later.

[SFX: PEN WRITES ON SERVICE CARD. SIGNAL BEEPS AT LOW VOLUME.]

ASHA: The friendly voice is still friendly. It is just no longer speaking over the crossing.

NEMI: That's the first lovely day I've heard from it.

[SFX: TRAFFIC PASSES. MIC CLOSES; SIGNAL PLAYS "WAIT."]
