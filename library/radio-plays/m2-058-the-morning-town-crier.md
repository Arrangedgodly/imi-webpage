# The Morning Town Crier

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: old-fashioned town-crier announcements adapted to modern radio*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Crier Joss, a baboon; announcer Tavi, a macaque; clerk Nessa, a gibbon; and resident Pru, a capuchin who has written several announcements.

[SFX: HAND BELL RINGS. A MICROPHONE IS ADJUSTED.] 

TAVI: Good morning. We are reviving the town crier's announcements as a radio segment. Joss will read notices from the square. The bell is only for opening the program, not for summoning everyone outdoors.

JOSS: Hear ye, hear ye. The water main on Cedar Street will be shut off for repairs between nine and eleven.

TAVI: That is useful. Please give the block numbers.

JOSS: Between the bakery and the clinic.

NESSA: That's not a block number.

JOSS: It is how people give directions.

TAVI: Add the printed map location.

JOSS: The map is at the library and on the town website.

TAVI: Better.

[SFX: PAPER UNFOLDS.] 

JOSS: Next notice. The council will hold a meeting about the new square benches at six.

PRU, PHONE: The benches are already built. What will the meeting decide?

NESSA: Placement and access. There are three designs, and one blocks the curb ramp.

PRU: Can residents comment online?

TAVI: Yes, or in person. We should announce both.

JOSS: Hear ye, residents may speak in person or submit a written comment by Thursday.

TAVI: Add how long the comments may be.

NESSA: Two minutes each.

JOSS: Two minutes, not two hours.

PRU: I have an announcement about the lost tortoise.

TAVI: Is it still missing?

PRU: No. It is in the garden shed.

JOSS: Then why announce it?

PRU: The original notice went out yesterday. We should update it.

TAVI: Correct. A notice needs a correction when the facts change.

JOSS: Hear ye, the tortoise has been found. It was not stolen or traveling to the harbor. It entered the shed through an open door.

PRU: Its name is Pebble.

JOSS: Hear ye, Pebble is home.

[SFX: HAND BELL RINGS.] 

TAVI: That's the opening bell again.

JOSS: I thought we had another notice.

NESSA: We do. The bell at the square is being repaired. It will not ring at noon today.

JOSS: Should we ring this bell instead?

TAVI: No. People would think the repair was finished.

JOSS: Hear ye, the noon bell will remain silent while its clapper is replaced.

PRU: That sounds like an announcement about an announcement.

TAVI: It is. The bell usually marks the market close.

NESSA: The market will close at the usual time. Vendors have signs.

JOSS: Then we should say that too.

TAVI: Yes. Say what changes and what does not.

JOSS: Hear ye, the bell is silent, but the market closes at five.

PRU: I can tell my neighbors.

TAVI: That's how the old crier worked. One person heard the notice and passed it along.

JOSS: The radio reaches farther than my voice.

TAVI: And can be corrected later.

NESSA: We should keep a copy of each notice with the date.

JOSS: Hear ye, the town crier has entered the archive.

PRU: Did we approve that?

NESSA: I'll add it to the agenda.

[SFX: HAND BELL PUT DOWN, STATION CLOCK TICKS.] 

TAVI: That's today's list. Check the notice board for exact details, and call the office if a schedule changes. We will not ask you to gather in the square just to hear a bell.

JOSS: Unless it's the opening bell.

TAVI: Even then, you can listen from home.

[SFX: PAPER FLIPS, PENCIL SCRATCHES.]

TAVI: One correction to the water notice. The repair crew has confirmed Cedar Street from the bakery to the clinic, nine to eleven. The block numbers are on the posted map.

JOSS: Hear ye, if the tap runs dry after eleven, call the office. Do not gather at the square and ring this bell at the pipes.

PRU: Someone was planning to do that.

NESSA: We had two calls asking whether the bell could fix the water.

JOSS: I can announce a repair. I cannot perform one.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC WITH A SINGLE BELL NOTE.]
