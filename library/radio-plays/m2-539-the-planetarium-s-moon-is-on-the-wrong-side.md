# The Planetarium's Moon Is on the Wrong Side

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night astronomy radio that treats a projector alignment issue as a celestial scandal*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Astronomer Mara, a macaque; host Vex, an orangutan; operator Odo, a gibbon; student Pip, a capuchin; caretaker Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Planetarium dome. Projector whirs; audience murmurs.

VEX: The moon has appeared on the wrong side of the sky. Are our maps obsolete?

MARA: The dome is rotated for the evening show. Odo is aligning the projector with the north marker.

ODO: The star field is paused. The projector is on its service setting, so the image appears sideways from the audience seats.

PIP: I thought the moon was rising in the west.

MARA: Not in tonight's program. We haven't begun the show yet.

VEX: Could we tell the audience they have discovered a new moon?

SELI: No. The image is a projection, and the service panel is open. Everyone stay behind the rope.

SFX: Projector fan lowers as Odo powers it down.

ODO: Power off. I can turn the alignment ring without the lamp shining.

VEX: The moon has vanished.

MARA: The projector is off. That is the expected result.

PIP: The north marker is above the center aisle, not over the exit.

ODO: You're right. I was lining up with the service door. The dome was rotated after yesterday's maintenance.

MARA: Use the printed alignment card. It shows the north marker and the first star cluster.

SFX: Card taps against panel; ring turns one notch.

ODO: Alignment set. Panel closed, power stays off until we clear the floor.

SELI: Rope line is secure. No tools left under the seats.

MARA: Start the projector at low brightness. We'll verify the north star before opening the doors.

SFX: Projector whirs. A single star appears overhead.

PIP: North marker matches the star. The moon is still hidden.

ODO: Now the program's moon cue.

SFX: Soft click; moon appears in the eastern part of the dome.

MARA: That's the correct position for the show. The audience can enter.

VEX: I would like to announce that the moon has returned.

MARA: Announce that the projector is aligned and the program is ready. There was no missing moon.

PIP: Can I tell my class I helped find north?

MARA: You spotted the right marker. That's a useful observation.

SELI: Doors opening. Please take the center aisle and leave the rope clear.

VEX: Our celestial scandal has been resolved by a card and one notch.

ODO: And by switching off before adjusting the lamp.


ODO: The alignment card is back in its labeled sleeve. I will write down that the dome was rotated after service and the north marker was checked before opening.

SELI: The rope remains across the service area until the tools are back in the cabinet.

PIP: I can put away the ring spanner. The audience is still outside.

MARA: Carry it by the handle and keep the projector switched off until the panel is latched.

SFX: Tool placed in cabinet; panel latch clicks.

ODO: Panel closed. Now the projector can stay on for the show.

VEX: I will tell the audience the moon was never missing, merely awaiting correct orientation.

MARA: Say the dome was rotated for maintenance. The moon doesn't wait for our announcements.

PIP: North is marked, the moon is east, and the doors are ready.


SELI: The doors can open now. The aisle is clear, and the service rope is stored.

VEX: The audience will see the moon in its proper place.

MARA: They will see the program we planned. Begin the show.


ODO: The show cue list is loaded. No service setting remains on screen.

PIP: The first constellation is ready.

MARA: Open the doors.

TAG

VEX: Tonight's moon is in the east.

MARA: In the projection, yes.
