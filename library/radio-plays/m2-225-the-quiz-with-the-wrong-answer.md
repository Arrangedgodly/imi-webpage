# The Quiz with the Wrong Answer

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio quiz show convention, where a contestant wins by giving answers the host did not expect but that remain technically correct*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host PIRI, capuchin; contestant MIRI, macaque; contestant ODO, baboon; scorekeeper TAVI, gibbon.

[SFX: QUIZ THEME, BELL.] 

PIRI: Welcome to Quick Answer. The first question: What has four legs and a seat?

ODO: A chair.

MIRI: A small table with a cushion.

TAVI: Both answers fit.

Piri asks for the official answer. Tavi says the card only lists chair. Odo receives a point. Miri asks if the quiz is testing knowledge or card reading. Piri says both.

The next question: Name a fruit that is red.

MIRI: A fig.

ODO: An apple.

PIRI: The card says apple.

TAVI: The fig can be red too.

Tavi gives both points. Piri says the scoring system is becoming unpredictable. Miri answers the next prompt with a question: "Where does a river go?" Odo says downhill. Piri says the official answer is to the sea. Tavi asks what happens if the river ends in a lake.

PIRI: The question is too broad.

MIRI: The answer was too narrow.

Odo presses the buzzer. It sticks down, producing a long tone. Piri asks for a repair. Tavi turns it over and finds a pebble beneath the button. Odo says he did not put it there. Miri asks if it counts as a buzzer answer.

TAVI: It is a mechanical response.

Piri begins a lightning round. He asks, "What is the fastest way to cross a room?" Odo says run. Miri says walk, if you are carrying a full cup. Tavi asks which room. Piri looks at the card; no room is specified.

Miri wins a point for identifying the missing detail. Odo wins a point for answering. Piri awards himself a point for asking. Tavi checks the rules. Hosts may not compete. Piri loses his point.

The final question: "What comes after the last question?"

ODO: The answer.

MIRI: The closing theme.

TAVI: Both are acceptable.

Piri asks the studio audience to choose. They ring the bell. He thinks the bell is the answer. Tavi says it is a signal that time is up. Miri and Odo tie.

Tavi asks Piri to read the rules aloud. The rules say contestants may challenge a question if it has more than one defensible answer. Miri challenges the fruit question. Odo asks whether "red" means ripe, painted or colored inside. Piri says the card does not specify.

Tavi adds both points to the score. Odo questions the chair answer; a tree stump with a cushion also has four legs and a seat. Piri says the answer key only lists chair. Tavi checks the rulebook again and accepts the stump. Miri receives a point for finding a second correct example.

Piri tries a new question: "What is the right answer?" Odo says the one that fits the question. Miri says the one the scorekeeper accepts. Tavi says the one in the rules. Piri awards no points because the question is about the rules. Tavi asks why the show keeps using questions with missing details.

PIRI: The cards were written before the rules.

The host asks contestants to write their own final question. Odo writes, "Where is the book's last page?" Miri asks, "Who checks the answer key?" Tavi says both questions are good, but the show has only one minute left. Piri has to choose one. He picks the question about the missing page.

Miri finds the page stuck to the back cover. It contains no answer key, only a thank-you note to the print shop. Odo asks if that counts as the last answer. Tavi says it counts as a closing credit.



Piri announces the prize: a book of quiz questions. The book is missing its last page. Miri says the answer key is missing too. Odo asks for the right answer. Tavi says the contest has no official ending.

[SFX: QUIZ THEME RETURNS.] 

PIRI: That is correct. We are off the air.
