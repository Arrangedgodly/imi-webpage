# The Underground Tunnel's Official Echo

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: cave-tour radio shows that sell a natural echo as a scheduled attraction*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Guide Piri, a macaque; host Sava, a gibbon; geologist Odo, a capuchin; visitor Ren, a lemur; recorded group visitor, a capuchin.

[SFX: CAVE DRIPS. FOOTSTEPS ENTER A CHAMBER. RADIO MIC CLICKS.]

SAVA: Welcome to the Echo Chamber, where every visitor receives one official echo.

REN: The sign says "echo demonstration at three." Is someone coming?

PIRI: No one is coming. The chamber returns sound from the far wall.

ODO: The tour operator installed a speaker last year to make the echo louder.

SAVA: It is part of the visitor experience.

REN: I hear a second voice saying "welcome" before Piri says it.

PIRI: That's the speaker playing a recording.

[SFX: A RECORDED "WELCOME" PLAYS, THEN BOUNCES BACK FROM THE CAVE WALL.]

SAVA: Two welcomes. A double echo.

ODO: One recording and one reflection.

REN: Can we hear the natural echo?

SAVA: The schedule says the speaker demonstration is at three. We can do the natural one after.

PIRI: The speaker is set to play every time it hears a clap. We cannot choose after. It will trigger.

[SFX: A DROP HITS A POOL. SPEAKER CLICKS ON: "WELCOME." THE WORD RETURNS FROM THE CAVE.]

REN: The drop started it.

ODO: The microphone is too sensitive. It hears the drip as a clap.

SAVA: Could we tell visitors the cave welcomed us?

PIRI: No. The speaker welcomed us.

ODO: If we switch the speaker off, we can test one word from the marked spot. The echo will return once.

[SFX: CONTROL SWITCH CLICKS. SPEAKER HUM STOPS.]

SAVA: The tour guide says not to turn off the attraction.

PIRI: The guide says not to cross the rope. We are not crossing the rope. I am turning off a speaker.

REN: What should I say?

ODO: Say one word at a normal voice from the white line.

REN: Stone.

[SFX: "STONE" ECHOES ONCE, SOFTER.]

REN: It came back.

SAVA: That was the cave.

ODO: That was the sound reflecting from the back wall. We can do it once more with the speaker disconnected.

[SFX: PLUG PULLS FREE. CABLE DROPS INTO A BOX.]

SAVA: The next tour group is approaching. Should we leave the speaker off?

PIRI: We should tell them the speaker is disconnected and offer the natural echo.

SAVA: That is not on the schedule.

REN: Put it on the schedule.

ODO: The chamber is safe. The white line marks the best listening spot. Keep voices low so the echo is clear.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACH; A SMALL GROUP STOPS AT THE ROPE.]

PIRI: Welcome. The speaker is off today. If you'd like, say one word from the white line and listen for the cave to return it.

REN: Hello.

[SFX: "HELLO" RETURNS ONCE, THEN FADES.]

SAVA: That's less loud than the recorded effect.

ODO: It is the actual chamber.

SAVA: The schedule can say "natural echo." No timed speaker.



SAVA: The brochure promised a "one hundred percent louder echo." That is why the speaker was installed.

ODO: The brochure compares the natural return with the recorded one. It does not explain that the recording plays twice.

REN: The natural echo reached me once, and I heard it clearly.

PIRI: I'll change the tour card before the next group arrives. It will say the chamber returns a visitor's voice once from the far wall.

[SFX: CARD REMOVED FROM CLIP. A PEN CROSSES OUT THE OLD PROMISE.]

SAVA: No percentage?

ODO: Not unless we measure it.


PIRI: And no automatic cue triggered by drops.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES ON A CLIPBOARD. WATER DRIPS INTO THE POOL.]

REN: The water will not start the welcome anymore.

ODO: Correct. The speaker cable is in the box.

SAVA: The cave did not say hello.

PIRI: It returned a visitor's hello.

REN: That is still a welcome.

SAVA: We can say that without claiming the wall has a voice.



[SFX: GROUP STEPS BACK FROM THE ROPE. ONE LAST "GOODBYE" ECHOES, THEN CAVE DRIPS ONLY.]
