# The Puppet Who Would Not Enter

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: children's radio theater convention, where an announcer tries to narrate a puppet's stage directions while the puppet refuses its cue*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Narrator MIRI, macaque; puppet voice TAVI, gibbon; director PIRI, capuchin; sound effects ODO, baboon.

[SFX: TINY THEATER CURTAIN. WOODEN FOOTSTEPS APPROACH, STOP.] 

MIRI: In a bright green forest, the brave puppet approached a mysterious doorway.

PIRI: The puppet is behind the curtain.

TAVI: I am waiting for the door to invite me.

PIRI: It is a puppet door. It cannot invite you.

TAVI: Then the story has a vacancy in its manners.

Odo opens the little prop door. It squeaks twice.

ODO: Door sound.

TAVI: That sounded like a cupboard complaining about rent.

MIRI: The brave puppet heard a creak.

TAVI: I heard Odo's hand on the hinge.

PIRI: We are recording a story for children, not a hinge inspection.

TAVI: The hinge has a speaking role.

Piri asks Odo for forest sounds. Odo rustles leaves, gives a bird call, then taps a tin of pebbles.

[SFX: LEAVES, BIRD, PEBBLE TIN.] 

MIRI: The forest warned the puppet of danger.

TAVI: The tin warned me that Odo is standing beside it.

ODO: I can take the tin farther away.

PIRI: No. The puppet must cross the forest to reach the door. Narrator, give him courage.

MIRI: The puppet gathered all his courage and took one step.

[SFX: ONE STEP, THEN CURTAIN RING.] 

TAVI: That was my foot caught in the curtain.

PIRI: Try again, without the curtain.

TAVI: I cannot. I am made of cloth and the curtain is winning.

Odo lifts the hem. Tavi walks two steps, then stops.

MIRI: The hero had reached the forest's edge.

TAVI: I have reached the microphone cable. If I go farther, the hero takes the studio with him.

PIRI: We can hide the cable under a rug.

ODO: We don't have a rug.

TAVI: Then the forest has a visible root.

Miri changes the blocking so Odo can pull a paper leaf across the floor as the puppet walks. The leaf catches on the cable. Odo pulls; the cable slides; the microphone rolls toward the door.

[SFX: MIC STAND ROLLING.] 

PIRI: Stop the forest!

ODO: The forest is moving the microphone.

TAVI: I told you the roots were visible.

Miri picks up the microphone and brings it to the puppet's side of the curtain. Now Tavi's footsteps sound close, and Odo's door seems distant.

MIRI: The brave puppet approached the doorway.

TAVI: I can hear it now. It is over there, not under my foot.

Odo knocks twice with a soft knuckle.

[SFX: TWO GENTLE KNOCKS.] 

TAVI: That is an invitation.

PIRI: Enter.

TAVI: I am entering because the cue makes sense, not because the narrator used the word brave.

He crosses. The small door closes behind him with a click.

MIRI: Inside the forest stood a cart with a squeaky wheel.

TAVI: That is not in the script.

ODO: It's the cart outside. I moved it away and the wheel followed us in its own way.

PIRI: We can make the cart the villain. Puppet, confront it.

Tavi pushes the cart. The wheel squeals; he stops it by tightening a loose bolt with Odo's spoon.

[SFX: WHEEL SQUEAL, SPOON TURN, QUIET WHEEL.] 

MIRI: The brave puppet saved the forest from the squeaky cart.

Tavi tests the wheel again. It moves silently, but the cart's handle rattles. He wraps the handle with a strip of curtain cloth.

[SFX: RATTLE STOPS.]

TAVI: Now it has no line and no sound effect.

ODO: It can be an extra.

TAVI: The forest is a theater, and the villain is a prop with maintenance needs.

PIRI: Keep that line. That's our ending.

Miri announces the play is over. Tavi exits through the little door. One soft click.

[SFX: CURTAIN. SMALL BELL.] 

MIRI: The brave puppet has gone home.

TAVI (OFF): I am still in the hallway, and the cart is following me.
