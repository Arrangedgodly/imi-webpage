# The Audition Is Audible

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio talent contest with judges who overvalue stage presence*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: HOST RANA, a macaque; JUDGE VERO, a gibbon; JUDGE PIP, a tamarin; and auditioners LARK, a capuchin, and BO, a squirrel monkey.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC, APPLAUSE FROM A SMALL RECORDED CROWD.] 

RANA: Welcome to The Sound of Fern Hollow, the radio contest where local performers compete for a chance to perform on a stage no one can see. Our first auditioner is Lark, presenting the sound of a branch bending in winter.

LARK: I need a branch.

VERO: We have one in the prop room.

PIP: This is radio. The audience can imagine a branch.

LARK: They can hear this one.

[SFX: A FLEXIBLE BRANCH CREAKS, THEN SPRINGS BACK.] 

RANA: A strong opening. Vero?

VERO: The sound has a beginning, middle, and a return.

PIP: I heard a branch. I was expecting a performance.

LARK: The branch is performing.

PIP: It is doing what branches do.

RANA: Next is Bo, with a piece titled Three Ways to Peel a Fig.

BO: There is one way to peel a fig.

RANA: The title promises range.

BO: The first way is with your fingers.

[SFX: A FIG PEELS.] 

BO: The second way is to open it and eat it.

[SFX: A BITE.] 

BO: The third way is to let it ripen and peel itself.

VERO: That will take time.

BO: I can wait.

PIP: Can you wait with more energy?

BO: I can wait while chewing.

RANA: We also have a listener vote. The phone lines are open.

[SFX: PHONE BUTTONS, ONE BELL.] 

RANA: The first caller says the branch has a lovely low note.

VERO: That is a vote for Lark.

PIP: The second caller says the fig was delicious.

VERO: That is a vote for Bo.

PIP: The third caller says they did not hear the performance because the recorded applause covered it.

RANA: We should turn off the applause track.

[SFX: THE TRACK CUTS OUT. A SMALL ROOM TONE REMAINS.] 

VERO: Now I can hear the studio.

LARK: The branch is still settling back into shape.

BO: The seeds are still in the bowl.

PIP: Both performances continue after the judges stop looking at them.

VERO: Are we judging the performers or the objects?

RANA: We invited local talent. The talent includes knowing when to stop.

BO: I can stop eating.

[SFX: THE BOWL SLIDES AWAY FROM BO.] 

BO: I can stop now.

PIP: That was your strongest moment.

VERO: The branch gave us one clear sound and returned to the tree. The fig gave us three sounds and returned to the bowl.

RANA: Then we can award them both a broadcast, but not a trophy.

PIP: We do not have a trophy.

RANA: The audience can imagine one.

VERO: I can imagine a tiny one.

RANA: The judges are deliberating. Vero, who advances?

VERO: Lark's branch filled the studio with atmosphere.

PIP: Bo's fig had structure.

VERO: We need one winner.

PIP: We could judge on sound, which is the purpose of radio.

RANA: That's a radical rule change.

VERO: Fine. Replay both.

[SFX: BRANCH CREAK. FIG PEEL. BITE.] 

RANA: Lark's branch is holding. Bo's fig is disappearing.

BO: That is part of the performance.

PIP: The audience heard the fig leave.

VERO: Then the winner is the branch.

LARK: I don't want to snap it. It should go back to the tree.

RANA: Can we record it again in place?

LARK: We can record the tree moving in the wind.

[SFX: OUTDOOR AIR. LEAVES SHIFT, THE BRANCH CREAKS SOFTLY.] 

PIP: That is better. It is not alone now.

VERO: It has an ensemble.

BO: I can bring the fig seeds outside.

RANA: Do you want to enter next year's contest?

BO: If there is a category for eating quietly.

PIP: Radio has that category. It is called the break.

RANA: Lark wins a broadcast from the tree, Bo gets the seeds, and the judges learn to listen before scoring.

VERO: We did not agree on that last part.

PIP: You did when you heard the branch.

[SFX: APPLAUSE TRACK STARTS, THEN STOPS. ACTUAL LEAVES RUSTLE.]
