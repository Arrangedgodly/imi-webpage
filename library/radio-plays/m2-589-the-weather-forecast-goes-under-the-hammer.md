# The Weather Forecast Goes Under the Hammer

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: charity radio auction conventions, forecasts sold by the hour, and a meteorologist refusing to auction certainty*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Auctioneer Tavi, a baboon; host Ema, a macaque; forecaster Odo, an orangutan; bidder Sela, a gibbon; station engineer Kiri, a capuchin.

SFX: Radio hall. Gavel taps; a rain stick shivers lightly.

EMA: Welcome to the station fundraiser. Today we auction unusual experiences. Lot One is a recorded weather forecast for next Tuesday.

TAVI: Opening bid: two tokens for a forecast with a chance of rain.

ODO: It is not a private forecast. It is a public bulletin already available on the station site.

SELA: I bid three tokens for the chance of rain.

TAVI: Three. Do I hear four?

KIRI: The forecast feed just updated. It says chance of showers, not rain all day.

EMA: The auction is live, but the forecast is current data. We should not sell the old wording.

TAVI: Shall I withdraw Lot One?

ODO: Keep the lot, but describe what it is: a custom explanation of the forecast, not ownership of the weather.

SELA: I want the meteorologist to explain whether I should postpone my picnic.

TAVI: Can that be auctioned?

EMA: It can be a scheduled public call-in. Highest bidder chooses the time; the answer is still honest and shared with everyone.

SFX: Gavel knocks once.

TAVI: Lot One revised: a ten-minute public weather question answered at the next open slot. The winner sets the time and may ask related questions. Opening bid four tokens.

SELA: I bid five.

TAVI: Five. Going once--

KIRI: The station's next Tuesday slot is full. We have a school forecast and a road bulletin.

EMA: Then we cannot promise that time. Offer the next open slot.

ODO: Wednesday at noon is available. I can explain the picnic forecast then.

SELA: I bid six for Wednesday noon.

TAVI: Six, going twice, sold to Sela.

SFX: Gavel taps; light applause.

SELA: My picnic is Tuesday.

ODO: I can still answer the question Tuesday during the public call-in, but the auctioned segment is Wednesday. The forecast may change overnight.

EMA: The bidder has bought an explanation, not a guarantee.

TAVI: We should add that to the lot card.

KIRI: The printer is out of blue ink. The card says GUARANTEED SUN.

ODO: That is not the current forecast.

TAVI: I will cross out guaranteed sun and write weather explanation.

SFX: Marker squeaks on card.

EMA: What does Tuesday show now?

ODO: A forty-percent chance of brief showers, with a dry afternoon likely. Bring a cover and check before leaving.

SELA: I will move the picnic under the pavilion, but keep the cake covered until serving.

TAVI: Can we auction the dry afternoon?

ODO: No. We cannot assign it to a bidder.

KIRI: The public forecast will update at six tomorrow morning. I can print the link on the lot card.

EMA: That helps the winner and listeners.

SELA: I have already won the ten-minute weather Q&A. May I ask whether wind will flatten my paper decorations too?

ODO: That is related to the picnic forecast. I will include it in Wednesday's segment.

TAVI: I was about to open a second lot. I can close the book.

EMA: The first lot explicitly includes related questions. We will not charge the same listener twice to make the schedule look busy.

KIRI: The county wind advisory is already public. It is light gusts, not a warning. I will add a link to the lot card.

SELA: One winning bid, two related questions, and a public forecast.

SFX: The receipt printer produces one receipt for six tokens.

EMA: The station gets six tokens, and listeners get the forecast for free.

ODO: That is what the broadcast is for.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The auction sells a time slot and an explanation; the weather remains public property.
