# And the Canopy Goes To...

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio awards-night broadcasts, envelope suspense, sponsor interruptions, and acceptance speeches cut off by an orchestra that cannot see the stage*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Ora, a macaque awards host; Peet, a gibbon envelope reader; Kessa, an orangutan stage manager; Vonn, a baboon orchestra leader; and the nominees.*

[SFX: Grand orchestra, audience applause, distant clinking of trophies.]

ORA: Welcome to the Canopy Broadcast Awards. We are live, and our first category is Best Sound Made by a Leaf.

PEET: The nominees are the maple flutter, the fig-leaf crackle, and the emergency palm slap.

KESSA: The envelopes are arranged by category.

PEET: This one says "Best Leaf Sound."

ORA: Open it slowly. Suspense is the heart of radio.

[SFX: Envelope opens; a leaf rustles loudly into the mic.]

PEET: The winner is... the maple flutter!

[SFX: Applause, then a short orchestral flourish.]

ORA: The maple is not here tonight. Its representative is a small branch.

KESSA: The branch is stuck in the backstage curtain.

ORA: We will accept on its behalf.

PEET: Next category: Best Dramatic Pause.

VONN: We need a pause for the nominees.

ORA: The orchestra will hold a chord.

[SFX: Orchestra holds one note, then another.]

KESSA: The curtain is moving.

ORA: That is not a nominee.

KESSA: The branch is pulling it down.

VONN: I can sustain the chord while we fix it.

[SFX: One long chord. Audience waits.]

PEET: The winner is the fig-leaf crackle.

ORA: That is the prior category.

PEET: The envelope says "Fig Leaf."

KESSA: That's the sponsor card. The actual envelope is under the trophy.

[SFX: Trophy lifts; envelope scrapes.]

ORA: Here it is. Best Dramatic Pause goes to the sound of a seed falling into a tin cup.

[SFX: One seed drops into cup. The room is silent.]

VONN: That was a beautiful pause.

ORA: The seed has no pockets, so we will keep the trophy on its seat.

KESSA: The sponsor asks for a mention before the next category.

ORA: Our awards are supported by Branch Rest, the cushion made from yesterday's leaves.

PEET: The product is quiet.

[SFX: Cushion squeaks.]

KESSA: That was our sound nominee.

ORA: We cannot award a cushion during a sponsor read.

VONN: We can play a chord.

ORA: Not yet. Best Entrance goes to the lemur who entered the wrong room and improved the acoustics.

[SFX: A door opens; footsteps reverberate.]

LEMUR: I was looking for the snack table.

ORA: You are nominated. Please stay where you are.

PEET: The envelope says the winner is... the lemur.

KESSA: The lemur is onstage.

LEMUR: I can accept, but I have not heard the other nominees.

ORA: They are all in the snack room. We will announce them next year.

VONN: Orchestra, acceptance music.

[SFX: Orchestra plays; the lemur continues speaking over it.]

LEMUR: I would like to thank the snack table, which provided a strong direction.

ORA: The music is supposed to cue the end of the speech.

LEMUR: I thought it was a second award.

PEET: I have the final envelope: Best Overall Broadcast.

KESSA: That category has no nominees.

PEET: It says the winner is "tonight's program."

ORA: We have won our own award.

VONN: I will play the victory chord.

[SFX: Big chord. The curtain drops fully, muffling the applause.]

KESSA: The branch has won the curtain.

ORA: Our broadcast continues behind fabric. The audience is applauding, the winners are holding their trophies, and the orchestra is playing to a curtain.

PEET: We should end the show.

ORA: The final image for radio is the final sound. Vonn, give us the closing chord.

[SFX: A warm chord. The curtain rises as the branch is freed; applause swells.]

KESSA: The maple branch accepts the curtain, the lemur keeps the entrance trophy, and the seed remains in its cup.

ORA: This has been the Canopy Broadcast Awards, where every category found a winner and the curtain had its own moment.

[SFX: One leaf flutters; orchestra ends cleanly.]
