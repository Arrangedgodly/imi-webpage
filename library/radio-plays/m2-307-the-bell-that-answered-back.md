# The Bell That Answered Back

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live call-in quiz shows that promise an instant answer while burying callers in qualifying rules*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Pavo, a macaw; producer Nera, a capuchin; caller Miri, a macaque; caller Seli, a gibbon; engineer Toma, a gibbon.

[SFX: BRIGHT THEME, SMALL BELL.]

PAVO: Welcome to The Quick Answer, the quiz where the first correct response wins a basket of fruit.

NERA: The basket is empty until the sponsor delivers it.

PAVO: It is a promise of fruit.

TOMA: The line is open.

MIRI: I hear a bell.

PAVO: Excellent. You are our first caller. What has one handle, holds water, and leaks only when used?

MIRI: The cracked cup on your desk.

[SFX: BELL.]

PAVO: Correct! You've won the basket.

NERA: The basket isn't here.

PAVO: You've won the future basket.

ACT ONE

Miri asks when the prize arrives. Nera says the sponsor's cart is on the road. Pavo tells listeners the quiz has one simple rule: first correct answer wins. Toma signals that the call board shows another caller.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CLICK.]

TOMA: A caller has joined. Their name is Seli.

PAVO: Seli, you're on the air. What gets wetter while drying?

SELI: A towel.

[SFX: BELL.]

NERA: We don't have a towel prize either.

PAVO: You have won the same basket. It is a shared prize.

MIRI: I answered first.

PAVO: The bell rang first.

MIRI: The bell rang after my answer.

Toma replays the sound. The bell is delayed by a studio effect he installed to make correct answers feel dramatic.

TOMA: The bell arrives two seconds late.

PAVO: Then our rule is the first answer that survives the echo.

NERA: That wasn't in the rules.

PAVO: It is now a special rule.

ACT TWO

Nera moves the bell closer to the microphone. Pavo announces a lightning round. Seli and Miri both stay on the line. Each caller must answer before the bell rings. The next question asks what can travel around the world while staying in one corner.

MIRI: A stamp.

SELI: A stamp.

[SFX: BELL.]

PAVO: The bell has selected Seli.

NERA: The bell has no voting rights.

Toma turns the effect off. The studio becomes quiet. Pavo asks for another answer. Miri says "a stamp" again, but no bell rings.

PAVO: The answer needs a sound.

NERA: The quiz asked a question, not for an accompaniment.

Seli taps the phone receiver with one finger. The tap sounds like the bell. Pavo declares her the winner, but Nera points out that Seli supplied a sound instead of a new answer.

MIRI: I can ring the bell too.

PAVO: Callers cannot ring studio equipment.

MIRI: Then your rule should say that.

Nera reads the written card aloud. There is no bell, no echo, and no shared prize. It says only "first correct answer wins one basket." The two callers have given different correct answers on earlier questions; the producer cannot award the same nonexistent basket twice.

TOMA: The sponsor cart is here.

[SFX: CART WHEEL SQUEAKS OUTSIDE.]

PAVO: The future has arrived.

The cart is wedged in the doorway. Toma opens the door. A real basket sits on top with four pears. The callers agree to split them, but Pavo says the show's official rules permit only one winner. Nera asks which answer was first. Toma checks the raw recording, before the delayed bell. Miri's answer leads by one second.

ACT THREE

Pavo announces Miri as winner. Miri accepts the basket and immediately gives half the pears to Seli, who helped expose the bell delay. Nera updates the rule card: answers count when spoken, sound effects don't decide, and the prize is delivered before the show promises it.

PAVO: That is less suspenseful.

NERA: It is more accurate.

Pavo asks Toma to play the victory bell. Toma rings it once, live and on time.

[SFX: BELL.]

MIRI: Is that the prize?

PAVO: No, that's just a bell.

TAG

The cart wheel squeaks again as it leaves.

PAVO: We have one more prize question.

NERA: We have no more prizes.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC.]
