# Room for One More Instrument

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: community radio open-mic and music request shows*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Nella, a macaque; bandleader Omi, an orangutan; caller Rafi, a gibbon who plays washboard; Suri, a capuchin accordionist; and stage manager Loon, a practical baboon.

[SFX: TUNING STRINGS, A SPOON TAPS A GLASS.] 

NELLA: This is Open Door Radio, live from the neighborhood hall. Tonight's band can accept one more instrument. We have a guitar, two flutes, a drum, an accordion, and a caller with a washboard. Omi, how many players fit behind the microphone?

OMI: Six.

NELLA: We have seven.

OMI: Then one must stand in front.

LOON: The microphone is on the front edge.

SURI: I can stand by the window.

LOON: The window is open.

Rafi, PHONE: My washboard is quiet. I can stand near the door.

NELLA: We are trying to make music, not make a fire exit unusable. Bring the washboard to the side table.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS, FOOTSTEPS, WASHBOARD SCRAPES.] 

OMI: Let's hear the instruments one at a time. Guitar first.

[SFX: GUITAR PLAYS THREE CHORDS.] 

NELLA: A little faster.

OMI: It is a tuning check.

NELLA: The tuning check has a chorus.

[SFX: FLUTES PLAY A SHORT SCALE, ONE NOTE FLAT.] 

SURI: The second flute is low.

LOON: It is still in the case.

SURI: Then the first flute is low.

OMI: Let's not blame the instrument before we tune it.

[SFX: ACCORDION BREATHES OUT A LOW NOTE.] 

NELLA: The accordion sounds like a door closing.

SURI: It is warming up.

RAFI: The washboard is ready.

[SFX: A GENTLE SCRAPE RHYTHM.] 

OMI: That's useful. Leave space for the bass line.

RAFI: I don't have a bass line.

OMI: The guitar can hold it.

NELLA: What song are we playing?

OMI: The one the callers requested. It's a round. Every person enters when they hear the line before theirs.

LOON: We have one microphone and seven people.

NELLA: We can take turns.

OMI: Or share the room. The microphones pick up sound, not elbows.

[SFX: BAND TRIES THE FIRST VERSE. THEY ENTER TOO EARLY, THEN STOP.] 

SURI: I heard my line.

OMI: You heard the word that comes before it.

SURI: It was close.

RAFI: My washboard followed the accordion.

LOON: It covered the flute.

NELLA: Start again, slower. Rafi, count the beat. Omi, give the entrance with a hand signal.

[SFX: WASHBOARD SCRAPES A STEADY BEAT. GUITAR JOINS, THEN FLUTES, THEN ACCORDION.] 

OMI: That's it. Let the instruments answer each other.

NELLA: We can hear every part now.

RAFI: And nobody had to stand in the doorway.

LOON: I appreciate that.

NELLA: Caller Suri asked for a request. What do you want to hear?

SURI: The song my aunt used to play at the market. She played it too fast, so I never knew where the chorus began.

OMI: We can start at the beginning and leave room for the chorus.

[SFX: BAND PLAYS A SLOW, ORIGINAL FOLK TUNE WITH AN EASY CALL-AND-RESPONSE.] 

NELLA: If you are listening at home, the words are: "Make a little room, and the sound can come through." The callers have joined in. We have found space for one more instrument by moving the chairs and listening.

LOON: And keeping the doorway clear.

NELLA: The doorway has its own solo.

[SFX: BAND ENDS TO APPLAUSE.] 

OMI: Next week, we will try two accordions.

LOON: We will measure the room first.

[SFX: TUNING NOTES FADE.] 


[SFX: BAND PLAYS THE TUNE AGAIN. THE FLUTES ENTER TOGETHER, THEN THE ACCORDION ANSWERS.] 

NELLA: The rhythm has room now.
OMI: Listen for Rafi's scrape before the chorus.
RAFI: I can hear the guitar leave me a space.
SURI: The accordion can wait too.
LOON: Nobody is leaning on the exit.
NELLA: We have a whole verse and a clear doorway.
OMI: That's more room than we started with.
SURI: And the chorus arrives where it should.
NELLA: This has been Open Door Radio. We will keep the mic open for another instrument, as long as we can still hear the one already playing.
