# Seven Innings of Static

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live baseball commentary, over-detailed play-by-play, and an announcer who mistakes every interruption for a historic moment*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
PIP, a squirrel monkey play-by-play announcer
ORR, a gibbon color commentator
SERA, a capuchin groundskeeper

[STADIUM AMBIENCE. A BAT CRACKS.]

PIP: Welcome to the Riverbank Ballpark, where the Capuchins trail the Macaques by one run in the bottom of the seventh. The pitcher winds up. He throws-

[STATIC BURST.]

PIP: -a question mark! The ball has vanished into the broadcast.

ORR: That was the power cable, Pip. Sera is reconnecting it.

SERA: I'm plugging in the scoreboard. The announcer cable is already connected.

PIP: Then we have a two-part event: the cable is safe, and the game remains uncertain. The batter watches the umpire. The umpire watches the scoreboard. The scoreboard shows a picture of a duck.

ORR: That's the mascot graphic.

PIP: But is the duck on base? The crowd is chanting "Quack!" This could be the first waterfowl pinch-runner in local history.

SERA: No. The mascot is behind the fence, waving a sign.

[THE CROWD CHANTS. A DUCK QUACKS.]

PIP: The pitcher delivers. The batter swings! It's a high fly ball toward left field-

[WIND GUST. A PROGRAM FLUTTERS.]

ORR: That's the paper program, caught in the breeze. The ball went foul.

PIP: A paper program has entered play! It glides above the foul line. Sera moves to intercept. She has her cap off-

SERA: I'm picking up litter.

PIP: A clean catch! The paper is retired to the bin.

ORR: Pip, the game is still going. The batter has two strikes.

PIP: The batter resets. The crowd holds its breath. The scoreboard changes to "RAIN DELAY."

SERA: The scoreboard is wet. I'm wiping it down.

PIP: The whole stadium sees the phrase "RAIN DELAY." A hush falls. Every player looks to the sky.

ORR: The sky is clear, and that is a towel in Sera's hand.

PIP: The towel has replaced the weather. An astonishing substitution.

[UMPIRE CALLS "PLAY!" BAT HITS BALL.]

PIP: The pitch comes in. A sharp grounder between first and second. The runner rounds the bag. He is heading home-no, he's heading toward the refreshment stand.

ORR: His route took him to the wrong chalk line. He's coming back.

PIP: He's returning with two lemonade cups. Sportsmanship has gone commercial.

SERA: He gave one to the umpire. The other is for the catcher.

PIP: The catcher accepts the drink and steps back behind the plate. The runner sprints home-safe! The Capuchins tie the game as the crowd erupts.

[CHEERING, BELLS, AND A SECOND QUACK.]

ORR: The duck mascot is celebrating by stealing the umpire's hat.

PIP: A bold base-running play from the bird, but the game is final. The capuchins win when the macaque pitcher laughs and drops the ball. That is not in the rulebook, but the umpire says it counts as an interruption of competitive dignity.

SERA: I'm returning the hat. The duck is under the bench.

PIP: The hat is home.

[THE DUCK QUACKS. THE CROWD COUNTS TO THREE.]

PIP: The duck has emerged with a hot dog. The pitcher has requested a replay, but the umpire says food is outside the review system.

ORR: The duck is heading toward the dugout.

SERA: I opened the gate for it. The grounds crew feeds the mascot there.

PIP: A responsible escort, a stolen lunch, and a walk-off victory. The league will review the duck's eligibility in the offseason. Until then, it remains undefeated against the snack counter.

ORR: The Capuchins are taking a team photo. The duck is in front. The duck is safe. The capuchins take the series.

[THEME MUSIC.]

ORR: Next week we broadcast the scoreboard-cleaning championship.

PIP: A gripping event, Orr. Every wipe could change the standings.

SERA: It's just a cleaning schedule.

PIP: And we will bring you every damp detail.
