# The Wrong Number on the Night Line

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio advice lines, callers who mistake the station for another service, and hosts who keep trying to turn confusion into a segment*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST NARA, a gibbon
PRODUCER OMI, a macaque
CALLER VEX, a lemur
SWITCHBOARD PIP, a capuchin
WEATHER HOST TAVI, a baboon

[SFX: PHONE LINE BEEPS, STUDIO CLOCK.]

NARA: This is the Night Line, where callers ask for local directions. Omi is screening the calls.

OMI: First caller is asking for the ferry schedule.

NARA: We do not cover the ferry.

PIP: They dialed the weather desk extension.

TAVI: The ferry leaves at dawn if the river is clear.

NARA: That is a weather answer.

[SFX: LINE CLICKS.]

VEX (PHONE): Hello? Is this the bakery?

NARA: This is the radio station.

VEX: I need to know whether my order is ready.

OMI: We can connect you to the bakery.

PIP: The bakery number is one digit different.

NARA: Please do not put the caller on air before we transfer.

OMI: The red button is transfer.

PIP: The red button is hold.

[SFX: HOLD MUSIC STARTS.]

VEX (PHONE): I can hear the station's theme.

NARA: We can hear you too.

TAVI: The caller has joined the program.

NARA: We are not taking callers yet.

OMI: The bakery line is busy. I can transfer him when it clears.

VEX: The bakery closes in ten minutes.

PIP: The wrong number has a deadline.

TAVI: That could be a segment.

NARA: It is a person trying to collect bread.

Omi asks Vex for the bakery name. He says it is the little shop beside the covered bridge. Tavi knows the bridge is closed for repairs, so the bakery may have moved its pickup window to the side lane.

TAVI: Use the south entrance.

VEX: I am at the south entrance. The door says "radio."

NARA: That is our south entrance.

PIP: The bakery is next door.

VEX: Your buildings share a wall.

NARA: We can walk the order to the correct door, but we cannot broadcast his name or order.

OMI: The transfer line is open.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICK, PHONE RINGS.]

VEX: I can hear another phone.

PIP: The bakery is ringing.

NARA: Do not leave yet.

The bakery answers. Omi confirms Vex's order privately, then returns to the studio.
 
The switchboard lights up again. Another caller has dialed the station while trying to reach the bridge office.

CALLER (PHONE): Is the bridge open?

TAVI: The bridge is closed for repairs.

PIP: You are on the wrong number again.

NARA: We should not turn this into a regular service.

OMI: I can route the call to the bridge notice line.

[SFX: TRANSFER TONE.]

The caller hears the recorded notice and hangs up. Nara asks Omi to update the paper directory, not the live script, so the next host does not improvise directions.

PIP: The bridge notice is extension five.

TAVI: And the bakery is extension four.

VEX: My order is extension three?

NARA: Your order is already collected.

VEX: I wanted to check.

Omi pins the corrected directory beside the switchboard and reads each number back while Pip tests the lines.


OMI: The order is ready.

VEX: Thank you.

NARA: We have successfully directed a caller to the right room.

TAVI: The night line has a new feature.

NARA: No. We will correct the printed extension list.

PIP: The weather desk extension is one digit away from the bakery.

TAVI: That explains why callers keep asking me about bread.

NARA: It does not explain why you give them ferry schedules.

TAVI: The river affects the ferry.

Omi moves the extension list away from the sponsor board. The two numbers had been covered by a label for the weather segment. Pip tests both lines before the next caller.

[SFX: TWO DISTINCT RINGS.]

PIP: Weather desk.

TAVI: Cloudy, with a chance of bread.

NARA: Please do not make that the forecast.

Vex returns to the phone and says he has collected the order. The studio stays off the air while he speaks.

VEX: This is the bakery line?

PIP: Yes.

VEX: I can hear the radio through the wall.

NARA: We can hear the bakery door close.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES, LINE DISCONNECTS.]

NARA: Night Line signs off. The caller reached his bakery, the extension list has been corrected, and no private order became a segment.

[SFX: STUDIO CLOCK, THEME FADES.]
