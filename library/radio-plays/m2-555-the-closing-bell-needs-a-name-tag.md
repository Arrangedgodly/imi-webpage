# The Closing Bell Needs a Name Tag

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: workplace reality shows that turn a shop closing routine into a farewell special*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; shop manager Mara, a macaque; cashier Pip, a capuchin; closer Odo, a gibbon; customer Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Corner shop. Door chime, register drawer closes.

VEX: Tonight, the closing bell says goodbye to everyone who ever shopped here.

MARA: It's a door chime. We close at eight. Nari is the last customer.

NARI: I bought a bag of oats. I can leave whenever the receipt prints.

PIP: Receipt is printing. I'll hand it to you at the counter.

ODO: The alarm is not set until customers are out and the door is locked.

VEX: The bell deserves a name. I propose "Dingus."

MARA: We are not naming store equipment during closing. Finish the receipt, then check the aisle.

SFX: Receipt tears from printer.

PIP: Here you go, Nari.

NARI: Thank you. I heard the bell when I came in. It has a nice sound.

VEX: It has been with us through every transaction.

MARA: It chimes when the door opens. It doesn't remember the customers.

VEX: We should interview it before we switch off the sign.

ODO: If the sign is off while Nari is still here, the shop looks closed before she leaves.

Mara walks Nari to the door. The bell chimes once as she exits. Pip turns the sign to CLOSED only after the door shuts.

VEX: It said farewell.

NARI, outside: I heard that. It was the door opening.

MARA: Exactly. Now we can begin the closing checklist.

SFX: Key turns; metal shutter lowers halfway, then stops.

ODO: Shutter jammed at the guide rail. Don't pull harder. I'll inspect the track from inside.

PIP: The checklist says no one stands beneath a moving shutter.

VEX: The closing bell has trapped us in the shop.

MARA: The bell has nothing to do with the shutter. Odo, stop it and use the manual release.

SFX: Shutter reverses slightly and stops. Odo checks the track.

ODO: A cardboard price tag is caught in the side guide. I can remove it after the shutter is secured.

MARA: Lower it a few inches, lock the manual stop, then lift the tag free.

SFX: Lever clicks; tag slides loose; shutter lowers smoothly.

PIP: Track is clear. Shutter is down and locked.

VEX: A final obstacle for the closing crew.

MARA: A tag fell off a box. We'll add a check for loose tags to the closing sheet.

PIP: The refrigerated shelf is closed. Temperature light is green, and the milk is within range.

MARA: Log the reading before we arm the alarm. We need the closing temperature and the time.

ODO: Reading entered at 8:12. I will initial it and put the sheet in the night folder.

VEX: Should the fridge get a farewell speech too?

MARA: No. We check the door seal and leave it running overnight.

SFX: Rubber seal presses; latch catches.

PIP: Seal is firm. I didn't leave a carton near the hinge.

NARI, outside: The CLOSED sign is visible through the window. The front lights are still on while the staff finishes.

VEX: It reads CLOSED. The bell has retired.

MARA: The sign means customers should come back tomorrow. The bell has no retirement plan.

ODO: Cooling log is filed, shutter is down, and the shop is empty. Let's all step outside before I arm the alarm.

SFX: Staff footsteps cross the threshold; door shuts. Lock clicks.

PIP: We are outside. Nari has already gone home; no customer is inside.

MARA: Odo, use the exterior keypad and wait for the confirmation tone.

SFX: Keypad beeps; alarm confirmation tone.

ODO: Alarm armed. The shutter is locked, the cooling log is filed, and the door is secure.

VEX: The bell gets no farewell?

PIP: The bell is quiet because the door is still. It is not a person.

MARA: We can go. The only voice still here is the radio recording.

SFX: Staff footsteps recede down the pavement.

TAG

VEX: Good night, Dingus.

MARA: It is not listening.
