# The School Orchestra's Open Channel

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live concert radio feeds that broadcast the rehearsal microphone instead of the performance mix*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Teacher Rina, a gibbon; student conductor Pavo, a capuchin; sound tech Lumo, a macaque; host Tessa, a lemur.

[SFX: SCHOOL AUDITORIUM. CHAIRS SHIFT. A MICROPHONE HUMS.]

TESSA: The student orchestra is ready for its live radio debut.

RINA: They are tuning. The audience is still entering.

PAVO: I have the score open. The first piece begins after the bell.

LUMO: The broadcast is already live.

[SFX: A MICROPHONE PICKS UP A CHAIR SCRAPE, THEN A STUDENT TUNING NOTE.]

TESSA: That was the opening note.

PAVO: No, that was the oboe.

RINA: The performance has not begun.

TESSA: The sponsor's clock says we need music now.

LUMO: The soundboard is using the rehearsal microphone. The concert microphones are muted.

[SFX: MIC FADER MOVES. A LOUD WHISPER: "IS THIS ON?" COMES THROUGH THE RADIO.]

PAVO: That was my stand partner asking me.

TESSA: Listeners heard it.

RINA: The students need their warm-up. Please take them off air.

LUMO: I'll mute the rehearsal channel and open the concert pair when Pavo gives the cue.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. AUDITORIUM ROOM TONE RECEDES.]

TESSA: The sponsor operator is asking for the choir to hum during the pause.

PAVO: We are not a choir. We are tuning to the oboe's A.

RINA: Please keep the microphones off until the students are ready.

LUMO: The sponsor button is on the rehearsal bus. I will disconnect it before the next cue.

[SFX: CABLE LIFTS. THE JINGLE STOPS.]

TESSA: The audience can hear the room settling now, not a sponsored song.

PAVO: We are ready when the oboe finishes.

TESSA: The station is silent.

RINA: The hall is not silent. People are sitting down.

PAVO: Orchestra, A from the oboe. Tuning note only.

[SFX: A OBoe NOTE, STRINGS TUNE. ROOM MURMURS.]

TESSA: Can we broadcast the tuning?

LUMO: We can, if it is labeled as tuning. But the sponsor bought the concert, not the warm-up.

RINA: Let's begin when the audience is settled.

[SFX: CHAIRS STOP MOVING. ONE BELL RINGS.]

PAVO: First piece, ready.

LUMO: Concert microphones open. Rehearsal channel stays muted.

[SFX: TWO FADERS SLIDE. ROOM TONE CLEARS.]

TESSA: The sponsor has asked if the orchestra can play its jingle before each piece.

PAVO: The count-in needs a clear beat. A jingle over it would start the wrong tempo.

LUMO: The jingle is now on its own cue, after the applause. It won't share the rehearsal microphone.

RINA: The students can finish a phrase without the station speaking over them.

[SFX: ONE STUDENT TUNES A NOTE. THE NOTE ENDS; NO JINGLE PLAYS.]

TESSA: That is the last tuning note. The performance begins on Pavo's baton.

TESSA: We are live from the school auditorium. The orchestra has finished tuning and is beginning its first piece.

[SFX: PAVO LIFTS BATON. ORCHESTRA PLAYS A SHORT OPENING.]

RINA: The broadcast is balanced. The strings are clear.

LUMO: The oboe is a little hot. I'll lower its channel between phrases.

TESSA: The sponsor wants its jingle after every song.

PAVO: We have three pieces without breaks. If you play the jingle, the next count-in will be covered.

LUMO: I can schedule the jingle after applause, before the next piece.

RINA: Only if Pavo signals the pause.

[SFX: PIECE ENDS. APPLAUSE FILLS THE HALL.]

PAVO: Next piece in two measures.

[SFX: JINGLE STARTS OVER THE COUNT-IN.]

LUMO: I will cut it.

[SFX: JINGLE FADES. BATON TAPS TWICE. ORCHESTRA BEGINS.]

TESSA: The jingle played too early. The next piece has started.

RINA: Move it after the final applause, not between pieces.

PAVO: I can give the cue once the last chord ends.

[SFX: MUSIC ENDS. APPLAUSE. ONE SHORT JINGLE PLAYS AFTER THE APPLAUSE.]

TESSA: The final cue came after the performance.

LUMO: The concert mix stayed on air. The rehearsal channel stayed off.

RINA: The students can hear themselves now.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES. CASES LIFT; ORCHESTRA ROOM TONE FADES.]
