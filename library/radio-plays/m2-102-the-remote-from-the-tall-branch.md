# The Remote from the Tall Branch

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio remotes from outdoor civic celebrations, uncertain crowd counts, and hosts defeated by local geography*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Olli, a squirrel monkey host; Fern, an orangutan field producer; Zed, a capuchin sound operator; Mayor Kito, a macaque; and listeners at the Sapling Jubilee.*

[SFX: Wind buffets a microphone. Children cheer. A woodpecker knocks on the broadcast van.]

OLLI: Canopy Today is live from the Sapling Jubilee. I am on the tallest branch for a panoramic view of the new town grove.

FERN: You are on a ladder beside the tallest branch.

OLLI: The camera can see the branch. The microphone can suggest it.

ZED: I am pointing the aerial at the stage, but the fairground disappears from range every time I turn.

FERN: Because you are waving it like a flag. Hold it upright.

ZED: It felt festive.

OLLI: Mayor Kito joins us with the planting total.

MAYOR KITO: Forty trees, thirty shrubs, and one decorative log. The log was counted by the committee's optimism.

OLLI: How does it feel to see so many young trees?

MAYOR KITO: Good. They are tall enough for shade and small enough that I can still see the band.

[SFX: Band plays a march. A cymbal answers the woodpecker.]

OLLI: Fern, how many listeners are here?

FERN: Eleven at the rope line, three monkeys in the branches, and one goat in the orchard.

OLLI: We are reaching the whole region.

ZED: The goat is chewing the remote cable.

FERN: Don't announce that. The cable is expensive.

ZED: It has announced itself by going quiet.

[SFX: Broadcast drops; a goat bleats.]

OLLI: That was our live report from the goat.

FERN: It is not trained as a correspondent.

MAYOR KITO: It knows the orchard better than we do.

OLLI: Next, the ceremonial planting. The mayor puts in a seedling as the choir sings.

FERN: We need the microphone at the planting patch.

OLLI: I can descend the ladder.

FERN: It ends at the trunk. The patch is over there.

OLLI: How far?

FERN: Far enough that I brought another microphone.

OLLI: Then why am I up here?

FERN: The view was in the plan.

ZED: I can give you a sound bridge. [SFX: He shakes dried leaves at the mic.] Now it sounds like you're walking.

OLLI: I hear no footsteps.

ZED: Very light leaves.

FERN: Don't shake leaves while the choir sings. It will sound like a forest collapsing.

[SFX: Choir begins a hymn. A gust scatters leaves and a paper program.]

OLLI: The program landed in the planting patch. Mayor, are you ready?

MAYOR KITO: Seedling, spade, and flag saying "First Tree." Ready.

FERN: The flag is upside down.

MAYOR KITO: It reads both ways.

OLLI: Listeners may be surprised the fair is on a slope.

FERN: They are standing on the slope. You are standing in a tree.

OLLI: For a host, all terrain is a studio.

ZED: Signal restored. The goat moved to the program table.

FERN: Has it stopped chewing the cable?

ZED: Yes, but now the cable is tied around its bell.

[SFX: Bell jingles as the goat walks.]

OLLI: We have a mobile microphone. Let's follow it to the ceremony.

FERN: We cannot follow a goat with the broadcast rig.

MAYOR KITO: It has the program. I'll follow with the spade.

OLLI: The mayor jogs after the goat. Fern follows the mayor. Zed follows the bell. I remain at the top of the ladder, describing it all from above.

FERN: I have the seedling. Goat has the flag. Mayor has the spade. We can still plant the tree.

MAYOR KITO: Stop, goat. This way.

[SFX: Bleat, bell jingle, quick footsteps, then a soft thump.]

ZED: The goat dropped the flag beside the hole.

OLLI: The mayor is digging. The choir holds a note while the ceremony catches up.

MAYOR KITO: Seedling in. Soil around the roots. Flag in the ground. First tree planted.

[SFX: Spade taps dirt. The choir resolves warmly.]

FERN: That's the only part of today's plan that went exactly as written.

OLLI: A successful remote, then.

MAYOR KITO: The planting is done. The goat has been appointed to the welcome committee.

FERN: Officially?

MAYOR KITO: It accepted the flag.

ZED: And the cable.

OLLI: Forty trees, thirty shrubs, one decorative log, and one goat with a media credential. I am climbing down to join the celebration.

FERN: Wait. You're on the wrong side of the ladder.

OLLI: I see a ladder.

FERN: That's the tree branch.

[SFX: One careful step. A leaf shakes loose. The choir laughs.]

OLLI: Then Fern will bring the microphone up to me.

FERN: It's already there.

[SFX: Wind brushes the leaves. The goat's bell rings below.]

OLLI: From the branch beside the branch, this is Olli wishing the new grove a long, shady life.

[SFX: Remote fades under choir, birds, and the goat's bell.]
