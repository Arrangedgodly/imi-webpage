# The Horoscope That Changed at Noon

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live astrology call-in broadcasts, vague predictions, and hosts who retrofit every forecast after hearing what happened*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST VERA, a capuchin
ASTROLOGER PEL, a macaque
CALLER NIM, a ring-tailed lemur
PRODUCER ODA, a gibbon

[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIMES, THEN A DESK DRAWER STICKS.]

VERA: Welcome to Stars Over the Canopy. Pel will tell us what the planets have planned for our listeners.

PEL: The planets have not told me anything. I have prepared twelve suggestions.

VERA: Your forecast says, "A door opens when you are ready." That sounds profound.

PEL: It is the studio door. It opens when the latch is lifted.

ODA: The latch is broken. You have to pull twice.

[SFX: TWO PULLS. DOOR OPENS.]

VERA: A prediction fulfilled before we began. Listeners, today's forecast is powerful.

PEL: Today's forecast says a small object may be misplaced.

NIM: I just lost my pencil.

VERA: The stars saw it.

NIM: It is behind my ear.

PEL: The stars have excellent access to your head.

VERA: Nim, your sign is Lemur. What else should you expect?

NIM: I was born under the sign of a very ordinary tree.

PEL: Your chart indicates an unexpected visitor.

[SFX: KNOCKING.]

ODA: That is the delivery of our lunch.

VERA: The prediction is astonishingly specific.

PEL: I wrote it after seeing the delivery schedule.

VERA: We do not expose the process on air.

PEL: Then stop asking me how I know.

[SFX: ENVELOPE SLIDES UNDER DOOR.]

NIM: My lunch bag has a star sticker.

VERA: Your personal forecast includes a meal marked by destiny.

NIM: It is marked by the kitchen.

PEL: The next message says, "Avoid a conflict with a close companion."

ODA: Is that for Vera and me?

VERA: We have never had a conflict.

ODA: You keep calling the chimes mystical. They are two spoons.

VERA: One of them is silver.

[SFX: CHIMES RATTLE. A SPOON DROPS AND ROLLS UNDER A CABINET.]

PEL: A disagreement has caused a small object to be misplaced.

NIM: The pencil was the small object.

PEL: Now there are two. The prediction remains valid.

VERA: Caller, do you have a question for the stars?

NIM: My chart says I should take a bold step. Is the studio stool sturdy?

ODA: We can test it before you climb.

VERA: The stars encourage courage.

PEL: The stars are distant balls of gas. I encourage checking the stool.

[SFX: STOOL WOBBLES.]

NIM: It has one short leg.

VERA: A challenge has appeared on your path.

NIM: It is a stool. I can swap it for the steady one by the wall.

PEL: A sensible solution. The stars have no objection.

[SFX: STOOLS TRADE PLACES.]

VERA: We have an update from the control room. The noon forecast is changing.

ODA: The studio clock is ten minutes slow.

VERA: Pel, revise every sign.

PEL: The signs are unchanged. The time is wrong.

VERA: Then the day itself has shifted.

PEL: No. The clock has shifted.

NIM: I finished my lunch early. Is that in my forecast?

PEL: You were forecast to eat lunch. You did.

NIM: I was hungry before noon.

VERA: A dramatic early fulfillment.

ODA: Actually, the lunch was delivered on time. The clock is wrong, and we ate at eleven fifty.

VERA: That does complicate the horoscope.

PEL: I will add a correction: if your forecast sounds accurate, check whether we learned it from a clock, a schedule, or your own sentence.

VERA: That is less mystical.

PEL: It is more useful.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKING, THEN STOPS.]

ODA: The clock has stopped again.

VERA: The stars have suspended time.

PEL: I pulled the plug to stop the chimes. It shares an outlet.

NIM: So the universe is still running?

PEL: As far as we know.

VERA: Today's signs all receive the same prediction: your afternoon will contain a thing you can check.

NIM: I can check the stool.

ODA: It is steady now.

VERA: And the stars?

PEL: They will remain where they are while we repair the clock.

[SFX: TOOLBOX OPENS. CLOCK STARTS, TICKING TOO FAST.]

PEL: Before we sign off, I found the spoon under the cabinet. It is the one that rolled away during our forecast.

[SFX: SPOON SLIDES INTO DRAWER.]

VERA: That is our noon forecast, delivered at whatever time it is. The stars are not responsible for the clock.

PEL: Nor the stool.

NIM: But perhaps they knew I would ask.

[SFX: MYSTICAL CHIMES. PEL SETS THE RECOVERED SPOON BESIDE ITS TWIN; THE TWO RING IN AN ACCIDENTAL CHORD.]

VERA: A final message from the stars?

PEL: A final message from the drawer. Good afternoon from Stars Over the Canopy.
