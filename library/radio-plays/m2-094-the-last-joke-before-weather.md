# The Last Joke Before Weather

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live stand-up radio hours, canned laughter, and abrupt station break handoffs*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: host Luma, a lemur; comedian Pavo, a gibbon; sound engineer Redd, a baboon; station announcer Vika, a macaque; audience volunteer Toma, a capuchin.

[SFX: STUDIO APPLAUSE. A MICROPHONE THUMPS.]

LUMA: Welcome to Laugh Before the Forecast, where each act gets three minutes and the weather gets the rest.

PAVO: I brought a joke about a banana.

REDD: We have a banana sound effect.

PAVO: Does it laugh?

REDD: It slips.

[SFX: BANANA PEEL SQUEAK.]

PAVO: That's my opening.

LUMA: Toma, you're tonight's audience volunteer.

TOMA: I thought I was bringing tea.

VIKA: The tea is on the laughter table.

TOMA: Why is there a table for laughter?

REDD: We keep the canned laugh there.

[SFX: CANNED LAUGHTER PLAYS.]

PAVO: My joke hasn't started yet.

### Act One

LUMA: Pavo, take the stage.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS TO MIC. AUDIENCE APPLAUSE.]

PAVO: Why did the banana bring a ladder?

[PAUSE.]

PAVO: It wanted to reach the punchline.

[SFX: CANNED LAUGHTER PLAYS.]

PAVO: That was not a ladder joke.

REDD: The laughter cue was early.

LUMA: We can replay the joke.

PAVO: Why did the banana bring a ladder?

[SFX: LAUGHTER PLAYS BEFORE THE PUNCHLINE.]

PAVO: Now it thinks the question is funny.

TOMA: Maybe it is.

LUMA: Toma, how did it land for you?

TOMA: I was waiting for the tea.

VIKA: The forecast begins in six minutes.

LUMA: We have one more comedian.

PAVO: I have another joke.

LUMA: It can go after the weather.

VIKA: The weather cannot be delayed.

PAVO: It can rain later.

VIKA: That isn't how forecasts work.

REDD: I have rain sound ready.

[SFX: RAIN FALLS. A CUP FILLS.]

TOMA: The tea is ready.

### Act Two

LUMA: We will hear Pavo's second joke after a quick weather update.

VIKA: Overnight, clear skies with a chance of light wind.

[SFX: WIND BLOWS INTO THE MICROPHONE.]

VIKA: That was not in the forecast.

REDD: The wind sound effect is on.

VIKA: Turn it off.

[The wind stops. The studio goes silent.]

PAVO: I have a quiet joke.

LUMA: We have time for one.

PAVO: A banana walks into a radio station.

TOMA: Why?

PAVO: It wants to be on the air.

[SFX: CANNED LAUGHTER PLAYS. IT DOES NOT STOP.]

REDD: The laugh machine is stuck.

VIKA: The weather is finished.

LUMA: We cannot hear the next act.

PAVO: The joke has gone on longer than the setup.

TOMA: I laughed at the tea.

REDD: Do you want me to cut the laughter?

LUMA: It is the only audience we have right now.

VIKA: We do have Toma.

TOMA: I am drinking.

[SFX: SIP OF TEA. CANNED LAUGHTER CONTINUES.]

PAVO: My third joke is that the banana was the laugh machine.

REDD: The machine is not a banana.

LUMA: It is shaped like one.

REDD: That is a coincidence.

### Forecast cutoff

VIKA: We have thirty seconds before the station identification.

PAVO: I have a short joke.

LUMA: One line.

PAVO: A banana walks into the forecast.

VIKA: The forecast says clear skies.

REDD: The banana slips.

[SFX: BANANA PEEL SQUEAK.]

TOMA: That's the same sound effect.

PAVO: It is a running joke.

VIKA: The station identification is now.

### Act Three

LUMA: We have reached the end of the comedy hour.

VIKA: The forecast says the laughter will clear by midnight.

REDD: I unplugged the laugh machine.

[SFX: LAUGHTER FADES.]

PAVO: Can I try the first joke again?

LUMA: We have one minute before the station break.

PAVO: Why did the banana bring a ladder?

[PAUSE.]

TOMA: It was going upstairs to make tea.

[SFX: REAL STUDIO LAUGHTER. CUP SETS DOWN.]

REDD: That is the first live laugh tonight.

LUMA: Toma has taken the closing act.

VIKA: The forecast remains clear.

PAVO: At least the tea was good.

### Tag

[SFX: STATION BREAK MUSIC STARTS. A SINGLE LAUGH PLAYS.]

VIKA: You're listening to Laugh Before the Forecast.

TOMA: The tea is over.

[SFX: CUP CLINKS.]
