# The Puppet Theater Takes a Break

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: show-business documentaries that expect performers to work through every pause*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Producer Vex, an orangutan; puppeteer Mara, a macaque; performer Pip, a capuchin; stage manager Odo, a gibbon; caller Nari, a lemur; theater audiences (nonspeaking).

SFX: Small puppet theater backstage. Audience leaves after the matinee; curtain rings settle.

VEX: The matinee has ended, and our puppet star has requested a break.

PIP: My hand is cramping. I need ten minutes before the three o'clock show.

MARA: Take the break. I'll put the puppet on its padded stand and cover the table.

ODO: The matinee audience has left. The next performance begins at three; doors open at quarter to.

NARI, on phone: I missed the matinee because my bus arrived late. I still have the unused ticket. Can I exchange it for the three o'clock show?

MARA: Yes. Ask the ticket desk to exchange it. Don't enter backstage; Pip needs a rest.

VEX: But the audience has come to meet the puppet.

PIP: The puppet is papier-mache with thin control rods. It stays on the stand, away from the window sun.

SFX: Stand set on table; cloth placed over the puppet.

ODO: The puppet is covered. The left rod was rubbing during the last scene. We should inspect it after Pip rests, before the next performance.

VEX: We should interview the puppet about its pain.

MARA: No. Pip is the performer. We can ask him what he needs without turning discomfort into content.

PIP: Ten minutes, water, then I'll check the rod at the table. I don't need anyone to handle it while I rest.

VEX: Our documentary needs a scene of the star suffering backstage.

PIP: The puppet doesn't suffer. My hand is sore; the puppet stays still.

MARA: That's clear. Odo, put the three o'clock sign in the lobby. The doors stay closed until quarter to.

ODO: Sign is up. I will send Nari to the ticket desk for the exchange.

NARI: I can come back for the later show. Thank you for confirming I won't lose the ticket.

VEX: Could we keep the puppet visible in the window while Pip rests?

PIP: No. The sunlight can warp the paper, and I don't want anyone touching the rods.

MARA: The cloth stays on. The stand stays at the back table. Nobody moves it for a photograph.

SFX: Footsteps to water station; cup fills; door closes.

ODO: Ten minutes have passed. Pip, are you ready to check the left rod?

PIP: My hand feels better. I'll uncover the puppet just for the inspection, then cover it again until stage call.

SFX: Cloth lifts briefly; one rod clicks, then cloth settles back.

PIP: The rod is secure now. I have covered the puppet again.

MARA: No extra rehearsal before call. Rest until stage call, and we'll open the doors at quarter to.

VEX: The star has returned.

PIP: I have returned to the backstage table. The puppet is still covered.

SFX: Lobby door opens; quiet family enters.

NARI: I'm back with the exchanged ticket. A family is asking whether the puppet can greet them before the show.

MARA: It cannot. The show begins at three; doors open at quarter to. They can wait in the lobby.

VEX: They paid for the experience.

ODO: Their tickets are for the three o'clock performance. The ticket desk has confirmed their seats.

PIP: I can perform the scheduled show. I won't operate the rods before then.

VEX: Could I hold the puppet for a photograph?

PIP: No. The rods are fragile, and the puppet stays on its stand until I move it for the stage.

MARA: Odo, answer the lobby door and point the family to their seats when the doors open. Keep backstage closed.

ODO: Understood. The audience stays in the lobby until quarter to.

NARI: The schedule is clear. I have the new ticket, and I'll take my seat when doors open.

VEX: The documentary's big reveal is a start time.

MARA: That is what the audience needed to know.

SFX: Wall clock ticks toward quarter to three; lobby door opens; footsteps enter auditorium.

ODO: Doors are open. Pip can uncover and carry the puppet to the stage now.

PIP: I am ready. Hand feels fine, rod is checked, I will carry the puppet to the stage.

SFX: Cloth lifts; Pip carries the puppet across the stage, its rods rattling lightly; curtain rope moves.

TAG

VEX: Will the puppet thank me in its acceptance speech?

PIP: You have heard how much it says when I take a break.
