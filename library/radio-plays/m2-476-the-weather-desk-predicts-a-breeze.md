# The Weather Desk Predicts a Breeze

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live weather reports that turn tiny forecasts into dramatic breaking news*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Anchor Mara, a macaque; forecaster Odo, a gibbon; field reporter Pip, a capuchin; producer Vex, an orangutan; sound operator Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Radio studio. A paper map unrolls. One window shutter taps.

MARA: Good evening. The observatory has issued a special bulletin.

VEX: Use the red lamp.

SFX: Lamp switch clicks. A bell rings once.

ODO: A breeze is expected to pass through the east window at some point before supper.

MARA: How strong?

ODO: It may move a loose leaf.

PIP: I'm at the east window now. There is a leaf on the sill.

VEX: Don't reveal the leaf before the forecast.

PIP: It's already moving.

SFX: Shutter taps. Paper rustles.

NARI: I can mute the field microphone.

PIP: The wind is making this report for me.

MARA: Stay with us as the situation develops.

VEX: We need a map graphic.

ODO: The map has no wind arrows.

VEX: Draw a red arrow from the window to the leaf.

SFX: Marker squeaks across a board.

ODO: Your arrow points through the wall.

VEX: The breeze might take that route.

MARA: Our viewers need to know whether to secure outdoor furniture.

ODO: The only outdoor furniture is a stool.

PIP: It is secured. I'm sitting on it.

VEX: Stand up. We need to see if it moves.

PIP: If I stand, it will move because the floor slopes.

ODO: Then separate the slope from the weather.

MARA: What would a responsible forecast do?

ODO: Compare the stool before and after the breeze, while nobody touches it.

VEX: That takes time.

NARI: We have a live camera on the stool.

SFX: Tiny camera motor turns. The shutter taps. A leaf skitters across the sill.

PIP: The leaf has moved. The stool has not.

MARA: Is this the breaking moment?

ODO: The forecast is accurate so far.

VEX: We can call it a developing story.

PIP: I can give the leaf a name for the graphic.

VEX: No. It is an anonymous leaf.

PIP: The leaf is at the studio door now. It has caught under the mat.

SFX: Door mat scrapes. A dry leaf crackles.

ODO: A forecast is not an instruction to chase debris.

VEX: We need a live shot of the leaf's progress.

MARA: We need to keep the microphone stand from falling.

SFX: Window shutter taps harder. The paper map lifts at one corner.

NARI: The map is moving.

ODO: That gives us a second observation point.

VEX: Mark it as a wind corridor.

PIP: It is just the open window.

MARA: If we close the shutter, we change the conditions. First, record what the breeze does.

SFX: Nari holds the map flat with a mug. A spoon rattles against its rim.

NARI: The mug has not moved.

ODO: The forecast can say light breeze, loose paper, no furniture displacement.

VEX: That will not fill a whole special bulletin.

MARA: We can discuss what the audience should actually do: secure papers, close a window if they need to, and leave the stool where it is.

PIP: And let the leaf go back outside after the report.

MARA: The breeze is getting louder.

SFX: Nari gently opens the studio window. A soft gust lifts the forecast card and drops it over Vex's face.

VEX: I can't see the map.

ODO: The arrow is now pointing at you.

PIP: The leaf made it to the floor.

MARA: And the stool?

PIP: Still secure. The producer is not.

SFX: Vex pulls the card away. The red lamp is switched off.

VEX: We should issue an update.

ODO: The breeze has passed.

MARA: Our special bulletin has ended. Keep your loose leaves indoors.

TAG

SFX: A single leaf slides beneath the studio door.

PIP: The wind has filed a correction.

ODO: Put it on the map.
