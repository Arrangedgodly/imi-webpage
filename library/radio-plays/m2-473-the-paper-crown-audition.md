# The Paper Crown Audition

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: talent competitions that cast a prop as a contestant*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; judge Odo, a gibbon; contestant Pip, a capuchin; costume maker Nari, a lemur; producer Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Studio stage. Applause sign clicks. A paper crown rustles.

MARA: Welcome to Crown Factor, the contest for the next great royal presence.

PIP: I made the crown.

VEX: The crown is the contestant.

ODO: Does it have a talent?

NARI: It is folded from one sheet of paper.

VEX: We are looking for star quality, not a craft lesson.

PIP: The crown can sit on a head.

MARA: That is a useful skill.

ODO: We need an audition sound. Crown, introduce yourself.

SFX: Paper crown set on a table. It makes a soft papery tap.

VEX: Too quiet.

PIP: It is made of paper.

MARA: Let's try the runway walk.

SFX: Footsteps. Pip wears the crown and walks three steps. Paper rustles. The crown slides over his eyes.

PIP: I cannot see the judges.

ODO: The crown has stage fright.

NARI: It is too big for Pip's head.

VEX: We can pin it.

NARI: The rules say no pins.

VEX: Then use tape.

NARI: Tape changes the design.

MARA: The audience wants to know who made it.

PIP: I did, while waiting for the show to start.

VEX: The profile package says the crown was discovered backstage.

PIP: It was on my head.

ODO: The crown's origin story is unclear.

NARI: It was folded at the table in front of us.

VEX: We can still ask how it feels about the competition.

MARA: It cannot answer.

VEX: We can use a narrator.

ODO: I will narrate: "A lonely crown seeks the throne."

PIP: It is not lonely. I can wear it.

SFX: Pip adjusts the paper points. The crown sits more securely.

MARA: Walk again.

SFX: Three steps. Rustle. Pip stops without the crown slipping.

ODO: Better. The contestant has improved.

VEX: What is its special act?

PIP: I can fold it flat again.

NARI: Show us.

SFX: Paper folds. The crown collapses into a square, then opens into a hat.

MARA: A second look.

ODO: The crown can become a hat.

VEX: Now we have range.

PIP: It can also be a bowl if I fold the corners.

NARI: That is not a crown act.

PIP: It can hold acorns.

SFX: Three acorns drop into paper. The folded hat sags but holds.

ODO: The crown has survived the test.

MARA: We need a second round. The audience can choose between royal presence and useful storage.

VEX: Storage is not a talent category.

PIP: It held three acorns without tearing.

NARI: It also sat on his head without tape. Those are two separate tests.

SFX: Acorns roll across the table. Pip catches one with the paper crown.

ODO: The crown has now demonstrated reflexes.

VEX: The contestant is a paper object.

MARA: Then why did you ask it for a special act?

VEX: I was building suspense.

PIP: I can build another one, but I want my name on the card.

NARI: I'll write it before the crown folds again.

SFX: Pencil scratches on card. The applause sign clicks on too early.

ODO: Even the sign thinks the round is over.

VEX: I vote yes.

MARA: We are judging the person who made it.

VEX: Then Pip is the contestant.

PIP: I was here the whole time.

ODO: Does Pip have a profile package?

NARI: He is a capuchin who folds paper to keep his hands busy.

PIP: And I can make a second crown if you need one.

MARA: Do you want to continue in the competition?

PIP: If the prize is a real hat.

VEX: The prize is a title.

PIP: Then I will keep the paper crown.

SFX: Applause rises. The applause sign clicks off.

ODO: The winner is Pip, for making a crown that can also hold acorns.

VEX: That was my idea.

MARA: It was his paper.
