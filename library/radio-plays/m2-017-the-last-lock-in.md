# The Last Lock-In

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Audio escape-room games and timed puzzle narration*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Host Quip, a capuchin game master; Nella, a macaque sound editor; Bo, a gibbon caller; Soot, a chimpanzee caretaker.

[SFX: DOOR SHUTS. A CLOCK STARTS. THREE LANTERN BELLS.] 

QUIP: Welcome, night owls, to The Last Lock-In, the audio escape room with no video, no refunds, and one very real door that should open from the inside. Bo and Nella are in our practice chamber. You have twenty minutes to find the key to the old radio studio. Your first clue is in the room with you.

BO: I hear a clock.

NELLA: I hear a clock with a loose spring.

QUIP: The clock has no hands. It has three bells. What do the bells tell you?

BO: Three rooms?

NELLA: Three notes. High, low, high.

QUIP: The wall has three wooden panels. Knock them in that order.

[SFX: HIGH PANEL, LOW PANEL, HIGH PANEL. A DRAWER SLIDES OPEN.] 

BO: We found a note. It says, "Look where the studio listens."

NELLA: The vent?

BO: The microphone.

QUIP: The microphone is hanging from the ceiling. As a gibbon, Bo can reach it. Please do not break the microphone. It costs more than the room.

BO: I can swing up, but the rope is too short.

NELLA: Use the curtain cord. It is looped around that hook.

[SFX: ROPE CREAKS. BO SWINGS. A TAIL RATTLES AGAINST A METAL GRILLE.] 

BO: There's a key taped under the mic. And a note: "The last lock is not a lock."

NELLA: A knot?

BO: The door has a hasp. No keyhole.

NELLA: We have a key. Maybe it's for the equipment cabinet.

QUIP: You have ten minutes remaining. A helpful clue is available for one banana.

NELLA: That's not a clue. That's a fee.

QUIP: The game master has to eat.

NELLA: We have a banana in the room. But the sign says not to touch props.

QUIP: That sign is a prop.

BO: The key fits the equipment cabinet. Inside are three coils of cable and a reel-to-reel tape deck.

NELLA: The note said the studio listens. Maybe the tape has a sound clue.

[SFX: TAPE REWIND. STATIC. A SOFT VOICE SAYS "KNOT, NOT KEY."] 

BO: The last lock is not a lock. It's a knot.

NELLA: The hasp is tied with that old microphone cord. We untie it, and the door opens.

QUIP: You have seven minutes. Please wait until the clock rings before touching the door.

NELLA: Why?

QUIP: Dramatic timing.

BO: We are not waiting seven minutes.

[SFX: KNOT SLIPS. THE DOOR CREAKS OPEN.] 

SOOT: You're out. Good. I've been in the hall for an hour.

NELLA: Why didn't you open the door?

SOOT: The sign said the room was an escape game. I thought you wanted privacy.

QUIP: The players have escaped in twelve minutes. That is our new record.

NELLA: You gave us clues that were all in the room.

QUIP: That is the principle of an escape room.

NELLA: The banana fee was not.

QUIP: I will remove that clue.

BO: Keep the clock. Lose the fee. And fix the microphone cord before someone ties the next group inside.

SOOT: I can fix it. I tied the knot to test the door.

NELLA: You tied it from the outside?

SOOT: I was confident.

QUIP: The practice chamber is closed until we add an emergency release, make the clue card readable without a flashlight, and return the banana to the snack table.

BO: Is the snack table part of the game?

QUIP: It is now.

NELLA: Then I have solved the snack puzzle. The banana belongs to the person who brought it.

QUIP: That is not in the clue set.

NELLA: It's in the room with us.

SOOT: I brought it. I was waiting in the hall.

BO: You were in the room the whole time?

SOOT: I was listening at the door. The game master said the room was the room.

QUIP: I will revise the welcome recording.

[CLOCK STOPS. BANANA PEELS. THE DOOR CLICKS OPEN AND SHUT ONCE.]
