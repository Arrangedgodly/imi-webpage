# The Harmony Hotline

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio choir rehearsal show where every singer brings a different pitch*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: DIRECTOR MAI, a gibbon; SINGER NIKO, a macaque; SINGER RUE, a capuchin; and ACCOMPANIST PEL, a lemur.

[SFX: PIANO NOTE, CHOIR BREATHES IN.] 

MAI: Welcome to the Harmony Hotline. Singers call in from their nests, and we build one community chorus. Niko, your note is an octave below the piano.

NIKO: It is where my voice sits comfortably.

MAI: Rue, yours is above it.

RUE: I heard a bird and followed it.

MAI: Let's rehearse the start once more. Everyone sing the same note.

[SFX: THREE VOICES ATTEMPT THE SAME PITCH. THEY DRIFT APART.] 

PEL: The piano is steady. The voices are not.

NIKO: My note feels strained when I push it up.

RUE: Mine goes sharp when I try to pull it down.

MAI: We can choose a lower starting note.

PEL: The piano can move down a third.

[SFX: PIANO PLAYS A LOWER CHORD.] 

NIKO: That sits better.

RUE: I can sing above it without chasing the bird.

MAI: Now we have the same starting note. Let's see whether we still need to match all the way through.

[SINGERS HUM. THE NOTES SEPARATE INTO A GENTLE CHORD.] 

PEL: The harmony returned on its own.

MAI: The score says unison.

NIKO: We can sing the opening together and split when it feels natural.

RUE: The split follows the words. One line asks, one answers.

MAI: That is a better arrangement than forcing every voice to the same height.

PEL: I can write the notes down, but I cannot write in the bird.

MAI: Leave a blank measure.

[SFX: PENCIL MARKS A REST. OUTSIDE, A BIRD CALLS ONCE.] 

RUE: The bird is back.

NIKO: It sang before we opened the window this time.

MAI: Then we can keep the rest either way. The chorus should have room for something unexpected.

PEL: And the window stand?

NIKO: I will hold it until the final note.

MAI: You are singing and holding the stand?

NIKO: I have two hands and a tail.

PEL: The piano is playing middle C. We could tune everyone to the same note.

NIKO: We could also choose a song with a lower starting note.

MAI: The arrangement requires everyone to begin together.

RUE: We did begin together. We just did not end on the same note.

MAI: The chord was not in the score.

PEL: It sounded settled.

[SFX: THREE VOICES SING A SOFT, UNPLANNED CHORD.] 

MAI: That is a pleasing sound. Can you repeat it exactly?

NIKO: I can repeat my part.

RUE: I can repeat the bird part if the bird repeats it.

PEL: The piano can hold a note, but the bird is outside.

MAI: I will call the bird into the rehearsal.

[MAI OPENS A WINDOW. A BIRD SINGS ONCE, THEN FLIES OFF.] 

RUE: It has left before the second verse.

NIKO: We should sing the verse as we heard it, not wait for the bird to perform on cue.

MAI: The ensemble is now piano, three voices, and one bird's short entrance.

PEL: We can leave a space where the bird sang.

MAI: A rest is written here, but it is only half a beat.

NIKO: The bird took longer than half a beat.

RUE: We can hold the rest until we hear the next sound.

[THE SINGERS WAIT. OUTSIDE, A SECOND BIRD CALLS.] 

PEL: There is the response.

MAI: Sing the next chord under it, softly.

[SFX: CHOIR ENTERS WITH THE PIANO. THE TWO BIRD CALLS ANSWER.] 

NIKO: We found a harmony without making anyone change their voice.

MAI: The chorus has a lower line, an upper line, and a space for the outside.

RUE: Can we keep the window open?

PEL: Only until the music stand stops shaking.

[THE WINDOW RATTLES. NIKO HOLDS THE STAND.] 

MAI: We have added a percussionist.

NIKO: I would rather sing.

[SFX: PIANO PLAYS THE CHORD AGAIN, THIS TIME WITH THE WINDOW CLOSED.]
