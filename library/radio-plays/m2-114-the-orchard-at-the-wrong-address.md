# The Orchard at the Wrong Address

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio lost-property desk programs, earnest callers, and a municipal map that refuses to recognize the orchard moved*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Sumi, a macaque host; Vell, a gibbon map clerk; Pru, an orangutan caller; and Dex, a capuchin courier.*

[SFX: Switchboard lights blink. Paper map unfolds.]

SUMI: This is Where Did It Go?, the radio desk for objects that have wandered from home. Our first caller, Pru, says her orchard is missing.

PRU: I moved the orchard last season. Now the council map says it is still on the hill.

VELL: The map is correct. The orchard is on the hill.

PRU: The hill was moved.

VELL: Hills do not move.

DEX: This one moved. We rolled the soil downhill into the new park.

VELL: The map still marks the old hill.

SUMI: Can you locate the orchard by sound?

PRU: The bees are humming near the east fence. The stream runs behind the plum trees.

VELL: The old map shows the stream west of the hill.

DEX: We diverted it.

VELL: You moved the hill, the orchard, and the stream?

DEX: The town needed a flatter picnic area.

SUMI: We have a second call. A listener lost a hat at the orchard.

CALLER: I left it on the old hill.

PRU: The old hill is now the picnic lawn.

CALLER: Then my hat is under the new lawn.

VELL: The map has a symbol for lost hats.

SUMI: Why is there a symbol for lost hats?

VELL: It is also the symbol for a pond.

DEX: That explains why the council has been looking for a hat in the pond.

[SFX: Map paper rustles; a small bell rings.]

SUMI: Vell has found the orchard on the new map. It is labeled "temporary fruit area."

PRU: It is not temporary. The trees are rooted.

VELL: The map category is temporary because the town may expand.

PRU: The trees are thirty years old.

VELL: The map is younger.

DEX: I can deliver a marker to the new orchard.

SUMI: Don't move it again.

DEX: I only move things when the route changes.

VELL: The route changed because you moved the stream.

DEX: I followed the water.

PRU: The bees are returning from the old location. They keep going to the empty slope.

SUMI: Can the town post signs?

VELL: The council approved a sign. It says "Orchard, see map."

PRU: The map is wrong.

VELL: We can add a second sign pointing to the map.

SUMI: Let's announce the location plainly. The orchard is beside the east fence, past the stream, behind the picnic lawn.

DEX: The stream now runs in front of the picnic lawn.

VELL: I can update the map.

PRU: Draw the orchard where it is today. Do not wait for the council to decide what to call the grass.

VELL: The orchard is marked with a green circle. The old hill is marked as lawn. The stream now curves behind the plum trees.

SUMI: And the lost hat?

CALLER: I found it on the picnic lawn. It was holding down the new map.

DEX: I can deliver it.

CALLER: Please leave it here. It is doing useful work.

SUMI: The orchard has been located, the map corrected, and a hat promoted to paperweight.

[SFX: Map refolds. Bees buzz gently nearby.]

VELL: The council may still move the label.

PRU: The trees can stay where they are.

SUMI: From Where Did It Go?, this is Sumi. If your orchard is missing, ask the bees before the map.

PRU: The bees have followed the updated marker. They are circling the correct plum tree.

VELL: I have changed the map legend. Green circle now means orchard, not temporary fruit area.

SUMI: The council has learned to let the fruit define the location.

[SFX: Switchboard dims; bees fade into the evening.]
