# A Very Small Emergency Broadcast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: emergency broadcasts that use alarming language for harmless neighborhood inconveniences*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Announcer Nara, a baboon; technician Kito, a macaque; gardener Pava, a gibbon; caller Omi, a capuchin.

[SFX: ALERT TONE, THEN A CLEARING THROAT.]

NARA: This is a small emergency broadcast. A garden gate is squeaking.

KITO: It squeaks when opened.

PAVA: It is the only gate.

NARA: Residents should remain calm near the gate.

ACT ONE

Omi calls to ask whether the squeak means the gate is unsafe. Kito says the hinge is loose but the gate still closes. Pava says she has a small oil can. Nara asks whether there is an official repair team.

PAVA: I am the gardener. I can tighten the hinge.

NARA: Are you authorized to use a screwdriver?

PAVA: It is my screwdriver.

Nara says the alert procedure requires a safety announcement before any tool is used. Kito reads from the manual: "Use the proper tool at the proper gate." Pava says that is what she plans to do.

[SFX: GATE SQUEAKS.]

NARA: The situation has repeated.

ACT TWO

The station asks Pava to describe the sound. She opens the gate slowly. It squeaks once, then stops when she lifts the hinge slightly.

KITO: The gate is telling us what it needs.

NARA: Do not quote the gate as an authority.

Omi asks if the community should avoid the garden. Pava says people can still use the other side of the path. Nara says the alert is active until the gate has been inspected and signed off.

Pava tightens the hinge with her screwdriver. She oils it, then opens and closes the gate twice. It moves quietly. Nara asks her to do it a third time for the recording.

[SFX: GATE OPENS, CLOSES QUIETLY.]

NARA: We have lost the squeak.

KITO: The emergency is over.

NARA: We still need a closing alert.

Pava says the gate is working and the path is safe. Nara asks if she can say "all clear." Kito says yes, but the manual requires a second person to witness the repair. Omi comes to the station and listens at the gate. She opens it herself. No squeak.

OMI: It is quiet.

NARA: Confirmed by a caller.

Kito finds a copy of the manual's call tree. Before the repair, the station sent the alert to three people, who each called the station back to confirm it. Nara listens to the recorded calls: one asks whether to avoid the gate, one asks whether the first caller should avoid the gate, and one says the gate is already open. The repeated calls have made the phone board flash like a control room. Omi volunteers to walk to the gate and test it. Nara insists on reading the safety notice first. Pava waits with the screwdriver. The notice ends, Omi opens the gate quietly, and the alert can be closed with one witness instead of a chorus.

Before closing the alert, Nara asks Pava to state the repair time. Pava checks the station clock; it is six minutes slow. Kito says the gate was quiet at the correct time, even if the bulletin is late. Nara crosses out "immediate response" on her form and writes "hinge tightened before sunset." Omi signs as witness, but her pen squeaks. Everyone looks at the gate. It remains silent.

ACT THREE

The broadcast tone sounds again. Nara announces that the gate is repaired and the garden is open. Pava asks if the station can alert them the next time a hinge needs oil. Nara says only if it becomes a small emergency.

KITO: What qualifies?

NARA: A very small emergency is one that can be fixed during the announcement.

PAVA: Then I will keep the screwdriver nearby.

TAG

The gate closes with a soft click.

NARA: We have received a report of a quiet click.

KITO: Do not issue an alert.

[SFX: TONE BUTTON CLICKS OFF.]
