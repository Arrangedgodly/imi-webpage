# Parade of Three Pumpkins

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live civic parade broadcast convention, where announcers keep inventing grand significance for a village procession of one cart*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Announcer MIRI, capuchin; co-host TAVI, macaque; marshal ODO, baboon; drummer KES, gibbon.

[SFX: DISTANT SINGLE DRUM.] 

MIRI: The annual procession is approaching the town square.

TAVI: It is one handcart, carrying three pumpkins.

ODO: The largest parade in the county.

TAVI: It is the only parade on this street.

KES: I have a drum.

MIRI: The first float celebrates harvest, patience, and wheel maintenance.

[SFX: CART WHEEL SQUEAKS.] 

ODO: The cart is not supposed to squeak.

MIRI: A historic squeak, honoring the first cart maker.

TAVI: It was repaired yesterday.

Odo tries to tighten the wheel bolt while the cart is moving. The cart stops. The pumpkins roll toward the front.

[SFX: PUMPKINS ROLL, CART HALTS.] 

KES: My drum is ready.

MIRI: The parade is holding for a musical interlude.

KES: I only know one beat.

TAVI: That is enough for one cart.

Kes drums once. The cart moves a few feet, then stops again because Odo is still holding the wheel.

ODO: I am the marshal. I must walk beside it.

MIRI: A marshal should not be attached to the float.

ODO: I am preventing a second squeak.

TAVI: You're causing the first stop.

Miri asks Tavi to describe the crowd. There are two families and a goat. The goat eats a ribbon from the cart handle.

MIRI: The front row is deeply involved.

TAVI: The goat is eating the banner.

ODO: The banner says "Harvest." It is edible cloth.

KES: I can drum faster to distract it.

MIRI: Please do not accelerate the parade. The cart cannot exceed walking speed.

Kes plays two beats. The goat follows the drum, pulling the ribbon loose. The cart rolls forward unexpectedly. Odo lunges for the handle; Tavi catches the pumpkins.

[SFX: DRUM, WHEEL, PUMPKINS THUMP INTO BASKET.] 

TAVI: The cargo is safe.

MIRI: The parade has achieved a thrilling formation change.

ODO: We are now traveling in a line.

TAVI: We were already in a line.

The procession reaches the square. Miri announces the ceremonial arrival.

[SFX: CART ROLLS ONTO STONE. WHEEL CLICKS.]

MIRI: The cart enters the square, bearing the season's harvest.

TAVI: One pumpkin has a leaf on it.

MIRI: A tribute to the growers.

ODO: The other two are rolling toward the curb.

Kes plays the drum to slow the pumpkins' motion. The beat is gentle. The pumpkins wobble to a stop against the wheel.

KES: I have discovered a parade tempo.

TAVI: You have discovered friction.

The goat follows the drum to the square and noses the cart. Odo blocks it with the banner pole. The pole is the only thing keeping the banner upright, so the cloth falls across the pumpkins.

MIRI: A canopy has descended over the harvest.

ODO: It is the banner.

TAVI: It says "Harvest" on the inside now.

Miri announces that the parade has arrived at its planned destination. Odo releases the cart, but the wheel begins to roll backward. He wedges the drum beneath it. The procession stops at last. The cart's wheel squeaks one final time, then falls quiet.

[SFX: WHEEL SQUEAK, THEN SILENCE.] 

ODO: The repair held.

MIRI: We have witnessed the closing of an era.

TAVI: The era was one block long.

Kes plays the final beat. The crowd applauds, though the goat bleats louder. Miri asks whether there will be an encore. Odo says only if the cart is loaded again.

MIRI: The parade will return next year, with more wheels.

TAVI: And the same three pumpkins?

ODO: They are still in the basket.

MIRI: Then the tradition is secure.

[SFX: CART ROLLS AWAY, ONE DRUM BEAT.]
