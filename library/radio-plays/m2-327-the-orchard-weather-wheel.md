# The Orchard Weather Wheel

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: game-show mechanics that turn simple forecasts into elaborate contests*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Leni, a gibbon; contestant Tavi, a capuchin; forecaster Nara, a macaque; scorekeeper Odo, a baboon.

[SFX: WHEEL SPINS, STOPS.]

LENI: The wheel says "partly cloudy."

TAVI: Is that good?

NARA: It is a forecast.

ODO: The score is tied.

ACT ONE

Leni says the contestant must predict whether rain will reach the orchard. Tavi chooses "maybe." Odo says only yes or no are accepted. Nara explains that the clouds are moving east and the forecast is uncertain.

LENI: Then the wheel decides.

NARA: The wheel has a weather icon.

ACT TWO

Tavi spins again. The wheel stops between sun and cloud. Odo awards half a point. Nara says the forecast hasn't changed. Leni asks the studio audience to vote. A breeze flips the forecast card from "cloudy" to "sunny."

TAVI: The wind changed the answer.

NARA: It moved the card.

ACT TWO-B

Nara lays out the actual forecast chart. The clouds are thin, the wind is light, and rain is unlikely before evening. Leni says the wheel's options are too broad; partly cloudy could mean sun and shade. Tavi asks if the scorekeeper can use the chart. Odo says contestants must play the game as designed. Tavi spins and lands on "storm." Nara says no storm is expected. Leni awards a dramatic-risk point anyway. A breeze moves the wheel one notch, leaving it between storm and sun. Odo calls for a replay. Nara points to the real sky; the cloud has drifted past the orchard. Tavi asks if the wheel can be adjusted. Leni says only after the round. The studio audience hears a drop on the awning. Everyone looks up. It is a berry falling from the branch. Odo marks it as precipitation until Nara identifies it. Tavi says the forecast should come from the sky, not the wheel. Leni agrees to let the next contestant choose an answer after hearing the forecast, but she keeps the wheel for the bonus round.

Tavi asks the audience to predict whether the orchard will get rain. One listener says yes because a cloud is present; another says no because the sun is out. Nara says both observations can be true. Leni turns the wheel toward the middle. Odo refuses to score a middle answer. Tavi suggests two half-points. The scorekeeper checks the rules and discovers that half-points were never listed.

TAVI: Then I have no score.

ODO: You have half a score.

TAVI: That is not on the chart.

Nara reads the actual forecast: mostly clear, with a small chance of a passing shower. Tavi says "maybe" again. Leni starts to award the round, but Nara asks her to wait for the clouds to pass the orchard. The contestants and host stand by the window; the wheel spins in a draft and points at every answer in turn.

Leni holds it still while Nara tapes the forecast chart beside it. Tavi moves the wheel away from the window so the breeze cannot alter the game. The chart stays pinned to the studio wall. The score will wait for the actual weather.

Leni asks whether the contestants can keep the weather wheel for future broadcasts. Odo says it belongs to the show. Nara says the chart belongs to the orchard. They hang both side by side, with a label distinguishing forecast from game.

ACT THREE

A few drops fall near the station. The chart's small chance of rain has occurred, but the orchard remains mostly dry. Leni awards the rain round to Tavi, who chose "maybe." Odo updates the score once; the wheel remains beside the chart as a game prop.

TAG

The wheel spins by itself as the breeze reaches it.

LENI: The forecast has entered overtime.

[SFX: WHEEL CLICK.]
