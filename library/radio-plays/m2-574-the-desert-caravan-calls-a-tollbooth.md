# The Desert Caravan Calls a Tollbooth

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: remote expedition broadcasts, checkpoint suspense, and hosts who mistake ordinary road signs for threats*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Sal, a macaque; guide Efi, an orangutan; driver Juno, a gibbon; toll clerk Piri, a baboon; field producer Ren, a capuchin.

SFX: Desert wind across a microphone; tires crunch gravel.

SAL: We are live from the Great Dry Crossing, where our caravan has reached a barrier marked TOLL AHEAD. Guide Efi, is this an emergency?

EFI: It is a tollbooth. We knew the road had a toll.

REN: Our map says the checkpoint is unmanned after sunset.

JUNO: It is sunset. There is a person behind the window.

PIRI: Good evening. Please state how many vehicles are crossing.

SAL: A voice from the booth. Our first contact.

EFI: It's the clerk. We are paying the road fee.

PIRI: One bus and one supply truck. The rate is posted on the sign.

REN: The sign is turned around.

PIRI: The wind spun it. The rate is the same as printed on your permit.

SAL: The booth's sign is facing away from us. Could it be a decoy?

JUNO: It is a metal sign on a swivel. I can turn it.

EFI: Stay by the driver's door. There is no reason to walk into the sand.

SFX: Sign creaks, then bangs once against its stop.

REN: The sign says ROAD FEE: TWO TOKENS PER VEHICLE.

SAL: Two tokens. That is the toll. Our expedition may continue.

PIRI: Correct. Do you want a receipt?

SAL: We will need evidence for the broadcast.

EFI: Ask for a receipt, not evidence.

PIRI: I can print two receipts after the full fee is paid. The printer is jammed.

SFX: Printer hums, grinds, and stops.

JUNO: Is the printer in danger?

PIRI: It has wrapped the paper around its roller. I can clear it if the line is empty.

SAL: The line is empty. There is no one behind us.

REN: Wait. The supply truck's trailer is still on the road. We cannot pull forward until the barrier rises.

EFI: The barrier rises after payment. Juno, use the bus lane; Ren, hand Piri two tokens through the slot.

SFX: Tokens clink in metal tray. Barrier motor starts, then stalls.

JUNO: The gate lifted six inches.

PIRI: The wind pushed a tumble of paper receipts under the arm. I will stop the motor before it bends.

SAL: The road itself is resisting us.

EFI: The road is clear. The gate arm is pinning old paper.

PIRI: I have cut power. I can reach the base from this side. Please keep the vehicles still.

REN: The truck brake is set. The bus is in park.

SFX: Paper rustles; clerk pulls paper from barrier. Motor resets with a click.

PIRI: The arm is clear. I am testing it without vehicles underneath.

SFX: Barrier rises fully and lowers again.

JUNO: It works.

SAL: Our checkpoint crew has defeated the gate.

PIRI: We removed paper. Nobody defeated anything.

EFI: The printer is clear, but we have paid only two tokens.

PIRI: I will hold the receipts until the full fee is paid. One bus, one truck, two tokens each.

REN: The permit looked like two tokens total.

PIRI: It says per vehicle. The rate sign is right-side up now. The print is large.

SAL: A clarification at the tollbooth.

EFI: We budgeted four tokens. Ren, pay the second two.

REN: Here. The total is four.

SFX: Two more tokens clink. Barrier raises; printer whirs and produces two slips.

PIRI: One receipt for the bus and one for the truck. Both fees are paid.

SAL: The caravan proceeds.

JUNO: The tires are moving. The bus is rolling at walking speed. The truck is following.

PIRI: Safe crossing. Keep to the marked lane; loose sand begins beyond the post.

EFI: Copy. We are past the booth and staying on the paved road.

SAL: One final question: is the toll road haunted?

PIRI: No. But the receipt printer does make a noise after midnight.

REN: We should record that for tomorrow.

EFI: We should not. We should arrive at camp before the wind picks up.

SFX: Tires crunch away; barrier clicks down behind them.

SAL: Tonight's expedition crossed the tollbooth, paid four tokens, and received two valid receipts.

PIRI: Correct. The first print attempt jammed before it produced anything.

SAL: We will file the blank paper under dramatic tension.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The road is open, the clerk has gone home, and every vehicle has its own receipt.
