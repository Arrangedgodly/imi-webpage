# The Brass Band's Unplanned Rest

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: concert broadcasts, conductor cues, and musicians who interpret every pause as a chance to tune*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST LUMA, a lemur
CONDUCTOR BENA, a gibbon
TRUMPETER ODO, a baboon
DRUMMER PIP, a capuchin
LIBRARIAN SAVA, a macaque

[SFX: BAND TUNES, MUSIC STANDS CLATTER.]

LUMA: We are live at the town brass band's final rehearsal before the river festival. Conductor Bena is preparing a dramatic rest.

BENA: At bar twenty, everyone stops for four beats.

ODO: Is that a rest or a pause to tune?

BENA: A rest.

PIP: Do we count silently?

BENA: Yes.

SAVA: Please remember the library shares this wall. Count quietly.

[SFX: BAND PLAYS, THEN STOPS.]

The musicians stop. Four beats pass. Odo's trumpet makes a tiny squeak.

ODO: My instrument has continued.

BENA: That was not written.

PIP: It was a fifth beat.

SAVA: It was a squeak.

Luma asks Bena to begin again. The band plays the opening. At the rest, every musician holds still. A chair creaks in the audience.

[SFX: CHAIR CREAK.]

BENA: Who made that sound?

SAVA: The chair.

ODO: It has entered on beat three.

PIP: The chair is ahead of the drum.

BENA: Do not assign musical roles to furniture.

The band starts again. During the rest, Pip's drum strap slips and the drum rolls across the floor.

[SFX: DRUM ROLLS, THUMPS.]

PIP: The drum has taken its rest on the floor.

BENA: We need to secure the strap.

SAVA: Please do so quietly.

Pip tightens the strap. The buckle snaps shut with a loud click. The band freezes.

LUMA: The rest has acquired a click.

ODO: It could be a cue.

BENA: It is not a cue.

Sava asks the musicians to rehearse the silence without instruments. They raise their hands and count. The brass players move their fingers, producing tiny valve clicks.

[SFX: MANY TINY CLICKS.]

PIP: The band is still playing.

BENA: We are not touching the keys.

ODO: The valves are touching themselves.

SAVA: I can hear every finger.

Luma suggests moving the rehearsal outside. Bena worries about the wind blowing the score pages. They set the pages under the drum and play in the courtyard. The rest arrives; birds call over the silent band.

[SFX: BIRDS CHIRP.]

BENA: That is not in the arrangement.

SAVA: It is outside the library.

ODO: Can the birds take the rest?

PIP: They have not stopped.

Bena adapts the score. The band holds the silence while the birds continue, then enters together on her raised hand. The phrase sounds fuller than the indoor rehearsal.

LUMA: The audience heard the rest.

BENA: They heard the courtyard.

SAVA: The courtyard is the venue.

At the festival, the band plays the piece.
 
The band returns indoors for one final run. Bena gives the rest cue, and every player stays perfectly still. The library wall gives a soft thump from the other side.

[SFX: WALL THUMP.]

SAVA (OFF): We heard the rest.

BENA: Was it quiet enough?

SAVA (OFF): We heard the band stop.

ODO: That is the purpose of a rest.

SAVA (OFF): We thought the rehearsal had ended.

Bena says the rest lasts four beats, then the music resumes. Sava taps the library desk four times to count. The brass section comes in exactly on the last tap, surprising the librarian and satisfying the conductor.

PIP: The library has joined the score.

SAVA: Only as a metronome.

BENA: We will credit the wall.
 The four-beat rest arrives. The crowd is silent, except for one stroller wheel turning.

[SFX: WHEEL SQUEAKS ONCE.]

BENA: Enter.

The band plays the closing phrase cleanly. Odo's trumpet squeaks again after the final chord.

ODO: That one was not written.

PIP: It is the encore.

BENA: The encore is one more song.

LUMA: The band has scheduled the squeak for tomorrow.

[SFX: SINGLE TRUMPET SQUEAK, APPLAUSE.]
