# The Snow Report Is a Whisper

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local weather radio, soothing winter forecasts, and competitive correspondents who measure snow with increasingly unsuitable objects*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST EDA, a capuchin
WEATHER DESK KEL, a macaque
FIELD REPORTER FENN, a snow macaque
CALLER LUMA, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: GENTLE WINTER THEME. WIND WHISPERS INTO MICROPHONE.]

EDA: Good evening. The Snow Report is a whisper, because the snow is sleeping and we would like it to stay that way.

KEL: We have measured two centimeters at the station.

EDA: What did you use?

KEL: A ruler.

EDA: Sensible. Our field reporter Fenn has climbed the hill to confirm conditions.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC, BOOTS CRUNCHING.]

FENN: I am on the hill. The snow is three centimeters deep.

KEL: That is a centimeter more than here.

FENN: My ruler is tilted.

EDA: The hill is not tilted?

FENN: Everything is tilted from here.

EDA: Please speak softly. The snow may hear you.

FENN: Snow has no ears.

KEL: It has many flakes. Some may be listening.

EDA: A caller is joining us. Luma, how is the weather where you are?

LUMA: My porch has a dusting. I measured it with a spoon.

KEL: A spoon is not a ruler.

LUMA: It has a straight handle and a bowl that catches the depth.

EDA: A careful observer. How many spoonfuls?

LUMA: One, but I can do a second pass.

[SFX: SPOON SCRAPES SNOW.]

LUMA: The snow is shallow enough to stir.

KEL: That is not a measurement.

EDA: It is a useful forecast for soup.

FENN: The wind is picking up on the hill.

[SFX: WIND RISES.]

EDA: What is the gust speed?

FENN: I cannot see my flag.

KEL: Our official windsock is hanging beside the studio window.

EDA: It is not moving.

KEL: The window is closed.

EDA: That may be a local microclimate.

FENN: I have tied my scarf to a branch. It is pointing west.

KEL: The branch points west.

FENN: Then the scarf is confirming it.

EDA: We are receiving reports from all elevations. At the station, two centimeters. On the hill, three tilted centimeters. On Luma's porch, one spoonful.

LUMA: I can offer a second spoonful if listeners need more precision.

KEL: Please do not add snow to make the report more interesting.

LUMA: I am not adding it. I am moving it from one side of the spoon to the other.

[SFX: SNOW SCOOPS, THEN DROPS.]

EDA: The snow has relocated. That can affect a neighborhood forecast.

KEL: It cannot affect the forecast. It was a teaspoon of snow.

FENN: A gust just took my scarf.

EDA: Is it still pointing west?

FENN: It is pointing wherever the scarf is going.

[SFX: SCARF FLAPS, THEN LANDS.]

KEL: It landed on the station sign.

FENN: I am at the station sign.

EDA: You have returned from the hill already?

FENN: The hill was the other side of the sign.

EDA: This is a very compact weather region.

KEL: We should give listeners a useful alert. The path is slippery near the hill, and a scarf is on the station sign.

EDA: We cannot whisper a safety warning. Please use your clear voice.

KEL: The path is slippery. Walk slowly and keep your scarf attached.

[SFX: SCARF FLAPS OFF THE SIGN.]

FENN: It has left again.

EDA: We have a moving scarf advisory.

LUMA: I can catch it with my spoon.

KEL: Do not chase a scarf on a slippery path.

LUMA: I am indoors. The scarf is on the radio feed.

FENN: I caught it on a fence. It is safe.

EDA: And the snow?

FENN: Still three tilted centimeters.

KEL: We can now confirm that no additional snow is expected before morning.

EDA: Unless the spoon measures more?

KEL: The spoon is not our instrument.

LUMA: It is clean, if you need one.

EDA: Thank you. Tomorrow's forecast is flurries, with a chance that our equipment will be described by kitchenware.

KEL: The station ruler remains available.

FENN: My scarf is tied to it now.

[SFX: RULER DROPS. SOFT CRUNCH.]

EDA: The Snow Report is a whisper. The snow is settled, the path warning stands, and the scarf has been recovered.

FENN: I am keeping it in my pocket.

EDA: Good night. Speak softly to the snow, but clearly about the ice.

[SFX: GENTLE WINTER THEME OUT.]
