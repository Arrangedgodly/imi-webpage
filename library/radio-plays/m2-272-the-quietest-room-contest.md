# The Quietest Room Contest

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: silent competition broadcasts, sound-meter scoring, and announcers who mistake equipment noise for contestants*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST PIRI, a macaque
JUDGE LUMA, a gibbon
COMPETITOR NOLL, a capuchin
JANITOR TAVI, a ring-tailed lemur
ANNOUNCER ODO, a baboon

[SFX: STUDIO TONE, A DISTANT PIPE TICKS.]

ODO: Welcome to the regional quiet room championship. The competitors will sit in separate rooms. The room with the least sound wins the silver ear.

PIRI: Our first contestant, Noll, has trained by listening to snow.

NOLL: I live beside a laundry drum.

PIRI: That is harder.

LUMA: The rules say no speaking, humming, or deliberate sound-making.

TAVI: Does cleaning count as deliberate?

LUMA: Please do not clean during the contest.

TAVI: The rooms are dusty.

[SFX: BROOM BRISTLES, THEN STOP.]

ODO: The contestants are seated. The sound meter is ready.

[SFX: TONE, CLICK.]

PIRI: Room A registers one point.

LUMA: That was the pipe.

NOLL: I heard it too.

PIRI: Contestant, you have spoken.

NOLL: I am still outside the room. I am on the monitor.

ODO: The quietest room has a loud announcer.

LUMA: Turn off your microphone.

[SFX: MIC SWITCH CLICKS; SMALL ELECTRICAL HUM.]

The contestants sit in silence. A clock ticks. A chair creaks. Tavi tries to hold the broom still, but a bristle snaps.

[SFX: TICK. CREAK. TWANG.]

PIRI: The janitor has introduced three events.

TAVI: I am not competing.

LUMA: You are on the sound meter.

ODO: The broom registers as a contestant.

TAVI: Then it should be disqualified. It is a tool.

PIRI: Tools are allowed if they are quiet.

TAVI: This one has a loose bristle.

The meter jumps when a radiator knocks. Judge Luma inspects it; the contestants remain still. Noll's room seems quieter, but the microphone is unplugged.

ODO: Noll has achieved perfect silence.

PIRI: His microphone is disconnected.

NOLL: I did not touch it.

LUMA: The plug slipped from the socket.

PIRI: Can we count the silence?

LUMA: Only if you count the room.

[SFX: PLUG INSERTED, SMALL HUM.]

Noll's meter now records the radiator, clock, and a soft breath. The neighboring contestant's room registers a lower level because its thick curtains absorb the ticking.

PIRI: We have a new leader.

TAVI: The curtains are dusty.

LUMA: Tavi.

TAVI: I am not moving.

The janitor's cleaning cart rolls slowly down the hall. Its squeaky wheel grows louder, then fades. Every room records it.

ODO: The cart has won all rooms at once.

TAVI: Its left wheel needs grease.

LUMA: That is not part of the contest.

TAVI: It is part of the building.

Piri announces a tie between the curtained room and Noll. Luma asks the contestants to leave their rooms for a final test. Noll steps out without making a sound; the heavy door slams behind him.

[SFX: DOOR SLAMS, LONG ECHO.]

PIRI: The door has broken the tie.

LUMA: The door was not competing.

ODO: The door is now the loudest finalist.

Noll holds the door gently open while Tavi puts a felt pad on its latch. They close it again; it clicks softly.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES, SOFT CLICK.]

LUMA: The contest is over.
 
The contestants file out, still trying not to make noise. Noll steps on a loose floorboard and freezes. Tavi points out that the winning room's curtain has hidden the squeaky board rather than repaired it.

NOLL: Does the room get to keep the curtain?

TAVI: The museum owns the curtain.

PIRI: The sound meter says the hallway is louder now.

ODO: The hallway has no contestant number.

LUMA: It does now.

She clips a number card to the mop cart. Its wheel squeaks. Tavi pushes it across the hall, and the squeak sounds like a tiny applause.

TAVI: I will fix it after the awards.

PIRI: The room won because it got a felt pad.

NOLL: The felt pad is the champion.

ODO: We have a winner no one can carry.


PIRI: Who wins?

LUMA: The room with the felt pad.

NOLL: Does the room get the silver ear?

ODO: The room cannot wear it.

TAVI: I can put it on the door.

PIRI: That is a sound installation.

[SFX: SILVER EAR HANGS ON HOOK.]

ODO: The quiet room championship ends with a tied score and one newly padded door. No contestant has to whisper on the way home.

LUMA: The radiator still needs work.

TAVI: I heard it.

[SFX: RADIATOR KNOCKS ONCE, THEN ROOM TONE.]
