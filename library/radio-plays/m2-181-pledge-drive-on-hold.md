# The Pledge Drive Is on Hold

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public-radio pledge drives that interrupt the program to gamify listener contributions*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST TESS, a macaque
PRODUCER LIO, a capuchin
CALLER MARN, a gibbon
CALLER NERI, a ring-tailed lemur
ENGINEER ODA, a gorilla

[SFX: CALM THEME MUSIC. A PHONE RINGS OVER THE FINAL NOTE.]

TESS: You are listening to Canopy Radio's hour of creek sounds. We will now pause those sounds to ask you to support the station that plays them.

LIO: We have a special tote bag for the first caller.

ODA: It is covering the mixing board.

TESS: Caller, are you ready to pledge?

MARN: I called to say the creek is louder today. Someone moved a microphone too close to the rocks.

[SFX: CREEK ROARS AT EXTREME VOLUME. TESS WINCES.]

### Scene One: The Goal

LIO: We are three hundred nuts from our pledge goal. If we reach it, I will ring the victory bell.

ODA: The bell is wired into the creek channel.

LIO: That explains why the river sounded like an alarm.

[TESS TURNS THE BELL OFF. THE CREEK RETURNS TO A NORMAL LEVEL.]

TESS: Marn, what do you like about the station?

MARN: I can hear the creek while I work. Today I heard the bell drowning it out.

LIO: Would a thank-you tote help?

MARN: I already have a bag.

[SFX: A SECOND PHONE RINGS. LIO PRESSES THE BUTTON; IT PLAYS A PRE-RECORDED APPLAUSE TRACK.]

ODA: That is not a donor. It is the applause line.

TESS: We have a caller for the pledge drive who did not call about the pledge drive.

[THE PHONE RINGS AGAIN. LIO ANSWERS WITH A BRIGHT VOICE.] 

LIO: You have reached our pledge line. Are you calling to support the station?

NERI: I am calling to ask you to turn down the applause track. It sounds like a crowd inside my kitchen.

ODA: The applause button is patched into the call return.

[ODA UNPLUGS THE TRACK. IT PLAYS ONE LAST FAINT CLAP, THEN FALLS SILENT.]

TESS: Thank you. The kitchen is quiet again.

LIO: Can I offer you a tote?

NERI: No, but I will stay tuned.

### Scene Two: The Matching Gift

LIO: A listener will match every pledge if we hit the goal before the top of the hour.

ODA: They asked us not to say their name or hurry listeners.

LIO: I have a graphic that says "DON'T LET THE MATCH EXPIRE."

TESS: This is radio. We do not have a graphic.

LIO: It is on my tablet.

[MARN CALLS BACK.]

MARN: The creek microphone is still too close. You can hear water, but not birds.

ODA: I will move it behind the bank marker.

[SFX: THROUGH ODA'S FIELD HEADSET, FOOTSTEPS AND A CABLE UNCOILING. THE CREEK AND DISTANT BIRDS BALANCE. ODA RETURNS TO THE STUDIO WHILE LIO READS THE PLEDGE NUMBER.]

TESS: That's the sound we meant to broadcast.

LIO: We are two pledges from the match.

MARN: I can pledge one, but I don't need a tote.

LIO: The tote is the premium.

MARN: Then give it to the caller who wants it.

### Scene Three: The Bell

[SFX: PHONE RINGS. ODA ANSWERS; NO APPLAUSE PLAYS.]

ODA: A pledge has arrived. No bell.

LIO: We are at the goal. I can ring it once.

TESS: The bell is off the creek channel.

[ODA SETS THE BELL ON THE TABLE. LIO RINGS IT. IT MAKES A SMALL, DIGNIFIED TINK.]

MARN: That is better.

TESS: Thank you for keeping this station on the air. We will return to the creek now.

LIO: Can I read the tote winners?

TESS: After the birds.

[SFX: THE CREEK, A BIRD CALL, THEN THE QUIET THEME MUSIC.]

ODA: You can hear both.

TESS: And neither needs a countdown.

[THE VICTORY BELL ROLLS OFF THE TABLE AND LANDS INSIDE THE TOTE BAG.]

LIO: One premium item has found its own carrying case.
