# Traffic and the Missing Left Turn

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: breakfast traffic reports, cheerful rerouting, and a reporter who treats tiny delays like a citywide crisis*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
FEN, a capuchin traffic reporter
LOU, a ring-tailed lemur studio host
MIM, a squirrel monkey courier

[UPBEAT BREAKFAST THEME. CAR HORNS IN DISTANCE.]

LOU: It's 7:15, and time for the traffic update. Fen is live at the intersection of Acorn and Willow, where one driver has been waiting to turn left.

FEN: That's right, Lou. The line is four vehicles deep, the signal is working, and the left-turn arrow has not appeared. The mood is tense, though a pigeon has made a confident move onto the crosswalk.

LOU: Is traffic moving?

FEN: The right lane is flowing. The left lane is considering its options.

[HORN HONKS TWICE.]

FEN: A driver has sounded the horn. That may be a protest, a greeting, or a reminder that the car in front is a parked delivery van.

MIM: I'm delivering breakfast to the station. I'm parked for thirty seconds.

FEN: We have confirmed the source of the delay. The van is occupying the left-turn lane.

MIM: I'm in the loading zone. The turn lane begins after the white line.

FEN: Our map is a napkin drawing, so the line is not to scale.

LOU: Can the driver turn another way?

FEN: There is a right turn, followed by a loop around the block. But the loop passes the bakery, and a second van is parked there.

MIM: That's the bakery's loading space. They're bringing out muffins.

FEN: A breakfast convoy has formed. The city is moving in a delicious direction.

[CAR HORNS. A BICYCLE BELL.]

LOU: Are you safe at the corner, Fen?

FEN: I am on the sidewalk. The drivers are taking turns, very slowly, as if the intersection were a board game. One car rolls forward. The next waits. A third waves the pigeon through.

MIM: That pigeon has right of way. The crossing signal is on.

FEN: The signal has changed. The pigeon crosses. The car line advances one vehicle.

LOU: Is there any actual obstruction?

FEN: The van is in the loading zone. The delay comes from one left-turn arrow timing with a longer cycle than the right lane. The drivers can either wait or take the next street and loop back.

LOU: That sounds manageable.

FEN: It is manageable, but radio thrives on the phrase "major backup." I'll try it now. Major backup at Acorn and Willow!

[MIM HONKS A TINY BICYCLE HORN.]

FEN: Correction: the backup is four cars, and one is now through. The second has turned right. The third is waving the fourth ahead.

MIM: I've delivered the breakfast. I can move the van now.

FEN: The van departs the loading zone. The left-turn lane is clear. The arrow changes green; all remaining cars turn left in a tidy little parade.

LOU: How long was the delay?

FEN: Two minutes and forty seconds. The drivers used the time to learn each other's names.

MIM: I met the baker. We're trading muffins for traffic updates.

FEN: That is an excellent exchange. Lou, I'll be taking one cinnamon muffin back to the booth.

LOU: Is it affecting traffic?

FEN: Only my route to the studio. I'm turning left now.

[A HORN. FOOTSTEPS.]

FEN: I've crossed the street and joined the breakfast line. The traffic is moving again, and the baker has added a second muffin to the trade.

LOU: Great news. No citywide crisis, no closed roads, and a small delay near the bakery.

FEN: We'll return with another update when something actually blocks the route.

LOU: What if nothing happens?

FEN: We'll report that the streets remain surprisingly usable.

[THEME MUSIC. A SOFT BICYCLE BELL.]
