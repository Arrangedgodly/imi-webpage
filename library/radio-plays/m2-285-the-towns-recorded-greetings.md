# The Town's Recorded Greetings

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: automated greeting recordings, local voice contests, and a clerk whose edits erase the details callers need*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST PIRI, a capuchin
CLERK LUMA, a macaque
RECORDER ODO, a baboon
CALLER VEX, a gibbon
MAYOR TAVI, a lemur
RECORDED VOICE, the automated greeting recorded by Luma

[SFX: STUDIO BEEP, TAPE DECK WHIRS.]

PIRI: The town is recording a new telephone greeting. Clerk Luma has written a script for the after-hours line.

LUMA: "Thank you for calling the town office. We are closed. Please leave your name, number, and reason for calling."

ODO: That is twenty seconds.

TAVI: The council wants it under ten.
 
Luma plays the draft greeting for a caller who needs to ask about a lost umbrella. The clip says only that the office is closed.

CALLER (PHONE): Should I call the shelter or come tomorrow?

LUMA: We have removed the hours and the callback instruction.

ODO: The time limit made the greeting shorter.

TAVI: It did not make the caller's problem shorter.

Piri asks Luma to put the office hours before the recording announcement. Odo edits out the mayor's welcome, not the practical details. The result fits the allowed time and gives callers a clear next step.

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS, RECORD BUTTON.]

LUMA: We have room for the opening hours and the tone.

VEX: We have no room for my greeting.

PIRI: Your message can be after the beep.


VEX: I called last night and heard the whole greeting twice.

LUMA: The system repeated it because you did not leave a message.

PIRI: We will audition shorter versions.

[SFX: RECORD BUTTON.]

ODO: Version one. "Town office. Leave a message."

LUMA: It needs the hours.

ODO: Version two. "Closed. Leave a message."

TAVI: People will not know when we open.

ODO: Version three. "Leave a message."

VEX: I do not know where I called.

PIRI: The greeting has been shortened past usefulness.

Luma adds the opening hours. Odo records the message at high speed. The words blur together.

[SFX: FAST PLAYBACK.]

VEX: Is the office open or closed?

ODO: It is efficient.

TAVI: It sounds like a squirrel with a deadline.

The mayor asks them to record one version in each council member's voice. Tavi speaks the full greeting, but Odo trims out the hours to meet the time limit. Luma records the hours separately; the tape starts late and captures only "nine to four."

LUMA: We need the days too.

PIRI: Can the phone system play two clips?

ODO: It can play one greeting.

VEX: Then put the useful part in the one greeting.

They choose a concise version: office closed now, open weekdays nine to four, leave a name and callback number. Odo records it at normal speed. The first take includes the chair squeaking.

[SFX: CHAIR SQUEAK.]

ODO: The chair is on the recording.

LUMA: Keep the take. It proves the room is occupied.

TAVI: It proves the chair is worn.

They try again. This time Vex calls the office line from the studio phone. The greeting plays clearly, then ends.

[SFX: PHONE RING, GREETING PLAYS.]

RECORDED VOICE: Town office is closed. We open weekdays from nine to four. Please leave your name and callback number after the tone.

[SFX: BEEP.]

VEX: I would like to ask when you open.

PIRI: You just heard it.

VEX: I wanted to see if it was recorded.

ODO: It is recorded.

VEX: Then I will leave my message.

He begins speaking before the beep. The system does not record him.

[SFX: MESSAGE CUT OFF.]

LUMA: The tone is the cue.

VEX: The greeting should say to wait for the tone.

TAVI: Add four words.

ODO: That makes it too long.

PIRI: The beep is doing its job.

Luma records the final version with "after the tone." Vex calls again and waits. After the beep, he leaves his name and number.

[SFX: BEEP, MESSAGE RECORDS.]

VEX: This is Vex. The chair squeaks in the greeting.

PIRI: The message is recorded.

TAVI: We will fix the chair before we fix the greeting again.

[SFX: TAPE DECK STOPS, ONE CHAIR SQUEAK.]
