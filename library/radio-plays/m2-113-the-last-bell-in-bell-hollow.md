# The Last Bell in Bell Hollow

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio coverage of a town clock restoration, solemn civic interviews, and a bell that strikes whenever anyone says it is fixed*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Mara, a macaque public-radio reporter; Fenn, a baboon clockmaker; Odo, a gibbon town clerk; and the mayor, a langur.*

[SFX: Wind chimes, distant bell gives one uncertain clang.]

MARA: We are live in Bell Hollow, where residents are restoring the town clock after it stopped at 3:17. Fenn, what caused the failure?

FENN: The clock's pendulum was interrupted by a nut. Not the metal kind. A real nut stored in the case.

ODO: The squirrel says it was temporary storage.

MAYOR: We have invited the squirrel to join the restoration committee.

MARA: How does the committee plan to fix the clock?

FENN: Remove the nut, clean the gears, and reset the hands.

ODO: The official plan also includes a ribbon cutting.

FENN: We need a clock, not ribbon.

MAYOR: The ribbon marks the moment time resumes.

[SFX: Scissors snip. A clock ticks twice.]

MARA: The clock has moved. What time is it?

FENN: Still 3:17. Those were test ticks.

MAYOR: The town cheered.

ODO: They thought the clock struck the hour.

MARA: Bell Hollow is quiet again as Fenn opens the mechanism.

[SFX: Gears turn; a nut rolls.]

FENN: There. The nut was wedged behind the escapement.

ODO: The squirrel says it is an acorn.

FENN: It is a nut. The clock is not responsible for identifying lunch.

MAYOR: We will label it for the archive.

MARA: The mayor is placing a small label beside the clock. What does it say?

MAYOR: "Nut, recovered at 3:17."

[SFX: Bell strikes three times.]

FENN: That is not the clock. The bell rope is tied to the archive shelf.

ODO: The shelf shook when the nut rolled.

MARA: We have a bell at 3:17, although no one knows what hour it intended to announce.

MAYOR: The town appreciates punctuality, even when it is inaccurate.

FENN: I have reset the hands to noon. Now the pendulum moves freely.

[SFX: Clock ticks steadily.]

ODO: We should wait one minute before declaring success.

MAYOR: I can make a statement.

FENN: Please don't say "fixed" until we have watched it.

MAYOR: Residents of Bell Hollow, our clock is almost-

[SFX: Bell rings once.]

FENN: The rope is still touching the shelf.

ODO: It rang when the mayor spoke.

MARA: Could the clock have heard him?

FENN: Clocks do not hear. Shelves do not listen. The rope is loose.

MAYOR: Then I will say nothing.

[SFX: Pause. Bell rings again.]

ODO: It rang anyway.

FENN: The pendulum has reached the bell trigger. It is finally keeping time.

MARA: How do we know?

FENN: Because it struck at the hour. It is noon.

MAYOR: The restored clock is working.

[SFX: Bell rings once more.]

FENN: That second ring is the shelf.

ODO: The shelf is celebrating.

MARA: The town has two bells now: one mechanical and one civic.

MAYOR: We should dedicate the shelf.

FENN: No ribbons.

MAYOR: A plaque, then.

ODO: What will it say?

MAYOR: "Shelf, restored alongside the clock, at an uncertain hour."

MARA: Fenn adds a felt pad beneath the shelf. The test bell no longer rings when the mayor speaks.

MAYOR: Then the restoration is complete.

[SFX: Bell strikes once.]

FENN: That is the clock. It is one o'clock.

ODO: And the shelf is quiet.

MAYOR: We restored the clock and retired the shelf from public service.

MARA: The squirrel has returned for the archived nut.

FENN: It is an acorn.

ODO: The label says nut.

MAYOR: Correct it before the next hour.

[SFX: Pencil scratches over the label; clock ticks.]

[SFX: The clock ticks; the shelf bell gives a small sympathetic clang.]

MARA: Bell Hollow's clock is running, its nut is archived, and its shelf has acquired a civic role. This is Mara, reporting at precisely the time the clock believes it is.

[SFX: Steady ticking fades under one final clear bell.]
