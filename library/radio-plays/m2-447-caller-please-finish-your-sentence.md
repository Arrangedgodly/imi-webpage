# Caller, Please Finish Your Sentence

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live call-in advice radio, tight commercial clocks, and hosts trained to fill every pause with a follow-up question*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A call-in host keeps interrupting a thoughtful caller because the studio treats silence as dead air, until the callers organize a pause.

Cast: Host Neri, a gibbon; caller Roko, a macaque; producer Luma, an orangutan; engineer Tavi, a baboon; cohost Veli, a capuchin.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CONNECTS. A CLOCK TICKS.]

NERI: You are on Evening Answers. What would you like help with?

ROKO: My sister and I have been trying to decide how to use the old workshop, and I - 

NERI: Is it a money issue?

ROKO: I was getting there.

LUMA: We have two minutes before the station break.

ACT ONE

Neri apologizes and asks Roko to continue. He says the workshop belonged to their aunt. His sister wants to teach carving there; he wants to keep it open for repair demonstrations. Neri begins listing compromises before Roko finishes. Veli raises a card reading LISTEN. The producer has another card marked CLOCK.

ROKO: We already tried alternating days.

NERI: That sounds fair.

ROKO: It isn't. The repair class takes three days to set up.

NERI: Then alternate weeks.

ROKO: I have not explained the class.

Tavi says the line has a seven-second delay, so Neri hears the beginning of Roko's sentence after she has already asked another question. Luma asks if they can shorten the delay. Tavi says the delay protects the broadcast from accidental profanity and cannot be removed while live.

ACT TWO

Neri asks Roko to state his main concern in one sentence. He pauses. The station's dead-air light flashes. Luma begins a sponsor read. Veli takes the microphone away from her and asks Roko to finish at his own pace. The dead-air alarm gives a soft beep.

ROKO: My sister wants the workshop to stay open, and I want people to learn repairs. We want the same thing, but we keep fighting over the calendar.

NERI: You both want public access.

ROKO: Yes. We need more storage, not separate days.

Neri stops. Veli looks at the dead-air light; it has gone dark. Luma asks whether they can put shelves in the back room. Roko says they could, if the carving tools were locked in a cabinet and the repair benches remained clear. His sister is listening off-mic from the workshop and calls in on the second line to confirm.

SISTER: I can teach carving at one table. I don't need the whole shop.

ROKO: I can move the parts bins off the benches.

NERI: That sounds like a solution.

ROKO: It is the first one I got to finish explaining.

ACT TWO-B

Luma says they have thirty seconds before the station break. Neri asks Roko and his sister if they can agree to test the layout for one week. They say yes, after measuring the back room. Tavi asks both callers to hold while he switches to the break. The dead-air alarm begins to beep again; Veli covers the talkback mic so it does not interrupt their goodbye.

NERI: I will let you finish.

ROKO: Thank you. We will measure first, then move the bins.

The two siblings agree on a Saturday setup. Neri asks what time, then catches herself. Roko gives the exact time and location without interruption. The sister confirms. Luma writes it on a cue card so the host will not repeat a different schedule later.

Veli says the sponsor message can play after the call. Luma reads the sponsor's name in one sentence. The dead-air timer stays off because Tavi routes the caller line around the fill detector. The studio clock continues to tick, but no one speaks over the pause.

ACT THREE

Neri summarizes only after Roko and his sister finish. The workshop will stay open, carving will use one table, repair demonstrations will use the benches, and storage will move to the back room after they measure it. The station does not promise that the trial will solve every disagreement. The callers say they are willing to test it.

Luma lets them hang up first. Veli lowers the LISTEN card. The clock reaches the break. Neri takes a breath before introducing the next caller. Tavi confirms the audio delay is still active and the dead-air detector is muted on the live line.
