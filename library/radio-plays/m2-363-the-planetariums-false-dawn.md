# The Planetarium's False Dawn

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: educational planetarium programs that trigger a sunrise cue during the night sky show and try to turn the mistake into a lesson*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Presenter Juna, a lemur; projectionist Ovi, a macaque; guest astronomer Bel, a gibbon; student Kito, a capuchin.

[SFX: PLANETARIUM ROOM. PROJECTOR WHIRS. SEATS CREAK.]

JUNA: We are looking at the winter constellations. Please keep the room dark while Bel points out the northern star.

BEL: It sits above the cedar ridge in the model. The real sky is clear tonight.

[SFX: PROJECTOR FAN CHANGES. A BIRD CHIRPS. LIGHT SWELLS OVER THE CEILING.]

KITO: The sun is coming up.

OVI: The dawn cue started. It should only play after the final star map.

JUNA: Perhaps the program is showing how stars fade at morning.

BEL: It is showing the sunrise effect, not the real sky. The clock in the projector room is six minutes ahead.

OVI: The cue sensor saw the scheduled sunrise time. I can pause the program.

[SFX: BUTTON PRESSES. LIGHT HOLDS AT PALE ORANGE.]

JUNA: We could explain that the early light is part of the experience.

KITO: The star map disappeared. I can't see the northern star now.

BEL: Kito came to learn where the star is. The dawn cue is covering it.

JUNA: Ovi, can you return to the winter sky without restarting the whole show?

OVI: If I choose "night hold," it will freeze the light and keep the star layer. The dawn animation will remain queued.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. ORANGE LIGHT FADES. STARS REAPPEAR.]

KITO: There's the northern star again.

BEL: Find it just above the ridge. The little dipper points toward it.

JUNA: That is a good moment. We can let the room stay dark.

OVI: The cue clock still thinks it is dawn. If it reaches the time again, it may start a second transition.

JUNA: Turn the automatic dawn cue off until the show ends.

OVI: It is a separate timer. I'll disable it in the schedule.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. TWO BEEPS.]

OVI: Dawn cue disabled. The projection stays on the night scene.

KITO: The sunrise began before we found the northern star. Is the model clock part of the sky?

BEL: No. The model clock schedules the effect. The real dawn arrives when the sun reaches the horizon.

OVI: The demonstration profile uses a timer for classroom convenience. I can set it after the star sequence, not at the room's wall-clock time.

JUNA: Put that in the control note. The next presenter should know which time is being shown.

[SFX: OVI WRITES A NOTE ON THE SHOW CARD.]

KITO: Then the orange light is a picture of dawn, not the actual morning.

BEL: Exactly. The planetarium can show it when the night sky lesson is finished.

JUNA: Bel, continue with the constellations.

BEL: There is Orion to the south. Three stars form the belt. Kito, can you find the two bright stars above it?

KITO: I hear the pointer click. I see them.

[SFX: POINTER TAPS TWICE AGAINST SCREEN.]

JUNA: We will not add a sunrise explanation over the star map. That would make the fault sound intentional.

OVI: I have the schedule open. The sunrise was set to six-oh-five in the demonstration profile. This show began at six-oh-one.

BEL: That is why the light came early.

KITO: Can we see the dawn after the stars?

JUNA: Yes, after the night lesson. First we finish the sky.

BEL: The moon is just below the horizon in this season. In the model it appears over the ridge if we turn the calendar wheel.

[SFX: CALENDAR WHEEL TURNS. MOONLIGHT WASHES THE LOWER CEILING.]

KITO: The moon is in the right place now.

OVI: No sunrise has started. The timer is disabled.

JUNA: We have seen the winter stars and the moon. Now the show is finished, so we can demonstrate dawn on purpose.

OVI: I will check the clock before enabling the cue.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. PROJECTOR FAN CHANGES; SLOW DAWN LIGHT RISES AFTER THE NIGHT SCENE FADES.]

BEL: There is the sunrise effect. It follows the star lesson this time.

KITO: The stars faded after we found them.

JUNA: That is the order the show promised.

OVI: I have returned the clock to the same time as the control desk and saved the corrected schedule.

[SFX: SEATS FOLD. PROJECTOR SHUTS DOWN.]

JUNA: Please leave by the lit aisle. The stars are gone because we ended the program, not because morning arrived early.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS EXIT; ROOM LIGHTS RISE TO NORMAL.]
