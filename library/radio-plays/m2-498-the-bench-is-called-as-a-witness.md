# The Bench Is Called as a Witness

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: courtroom broadcasts that give trivial neighborhood disputes grand legal weight*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Judge Odo, a gibbon; advocate Vex, an orangutan; neighbor Mara, a macaque; witness Pip, a capuchin; clerk Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Community hall arranged as courtroom. Gavel taps. Wooden bench creaks.

ODO: The case concerns a bench that has been moved six inches.

VEX: The bench was moved without notice.

MARA: It blocked the garden path.

SELI: The complaint says the bench was moved at dawn.

PIP: I saw Mara move it. She lifted one end, then the other.

VEX: Did she ask permission?

PIP: She asked me to hold the gate open.

ODO: The court will hear the bench.

MARA: The bench cannot speak.

VEX: Then I will ask it questions.

SFX: Bench creaks as Vex sits.

VEX: Bench, were you content beside the path?

SFX: Another creak.

VEX: It objects.

SELI: The bench is old. It creaks whenever someone sits.

ODO: The court will examine the path.

MARA: The wheelbarrow couldn't pass. We were carrying soil to the garden.

PIP: I held the gate. The bench scraped the stone.

VEX: Was the bench damaged?

MARA: No.

ODO: Did anyone lose access to the seating?

MARA: I moved it beside the same path, against the low wall.

VEX: The sun reaches that spot differently.

PIP: It is still in the sun after lunch.

SELI: The court could measure the shade.

ODO: Bring the bench outside.

SFX: Bench lifts; feet scrape. Birds chirp.

MARA: Here is where it was.

PIP: The wheelbarrow catches the corner.

ODO: And here is the new place.

ODO: The court will hear the neighbor who uses the bench in the morning.

PIP: I sit there to tie my shoes before taking the wheelbarrow out.

VEX: Did you consent to the move?

PIP: I wasn't there at dawn.

MARA: The path was blocked, and I moved the bench six inches to clear it.

SELI: The bench now sits beside the wall and still faces the garden.

VEX: I would like a written notice before anyone moves it again.

ODO: That is reasonable if the notice doesn't stop urgent access.

MARA: We can mark the bench's outline with chalk. If it needs moving, leave a note and return it after the cart passes.

PIP: I can help carry it. The legs are uneven, so lift together.

SFX: Bench rocks; three hands steady it.

VEX: It is heavier than it looked.

MARA: That's why I asked Pip to hold the gate.

ODO: The court finds that the move solved the obstruction. A note is posted for future moves.

SFX: Paper tacked to wall.

VEX: I will accept the chalk marks if the seat still faces the afternoon sun.

MARA: We can turn the bench without blocking the path.

PIP: I'll sit and check. The wall shades it now.

ODO: Move it one foot toward the tree.

SFX: Bench feet slide across paving.

PIP: This spot gets sun after lunch and leaves room for the wheelbarrow.

VEX: The court has negotiated with the bench.

SELI: No, the neighbors adjusted its position.

ODO: The note is posted, the route is clear, and the bench remains available.

SELI: It says "Bench may move when path is blocked. Return it to the chalk marks."

SFX: Bench lowers. A wheelbarrow rolls past without contact.

VEX: I request that the bench be returned to its original location.

ODO: On what grounds?

VEX: It was its location.

MARA: The garden path has to stay open.

ODO: The bench may remain by the wall. The court is adjourned.

SFX: Gavel taps. Bench creaks.

VEX: It objects again.

SELI: It is just settling.

PIP: I have the wheelbarrow.

MARA: The path is clear.
