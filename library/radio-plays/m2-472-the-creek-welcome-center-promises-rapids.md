# The Creek Welcome Center Promises Rapids

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: tourism broadcasts that market an ordinary local feature as an extreme adventure*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Sela, a gibbon; guide Pip, a capuchin; visitor Mara, a macaque; tourism director Vex, an orangutan; naturalist Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Creek water trickles over stones. A microphone windscreen rustles.

SELA: We are live at Rushing Creek, promoted by the Welcome Center as a white-water thrill.

PIP: The water is ankle-deep.

VEX: The brochure says "rapid descent."

MARA: It says that because the path slopes toward the creek.

NARI: We can describe the walk. There are shallow steps, slippery stones, and a small bridge.

VEX: That sounds calm.

SELA: We need excitement for the travel segment.

PIP: There is a branch in the water.

SFX: A twig clicks softly against a stone.

VEX: A log hazard. Good.

NARI: It's a twig.

MARA: The park removed the larger branch yesterday.

VEX: We can call it a remnant of the storm.

NARI: There was no storm.

SELA: What can a visitor actually do here?

PIP: Follow the gravel path to the bend, listen for the water, and cross the footbridge. The trail is open and marked.

VEX: The bridge is a narrow adventure crossing.

MARA: It has a handrail.

VEX: A safety handrail.

NARI: It is there because the planks get slick.

SFX: One careful footstep on wood. A board gives a small creak.

SELA: That sounded dramatic.

PIP: It is the bridge.

VEX: We can add a danger score.

MARA: There is no danger score.

NARI: The water is shallow, but the rocks are slippery. Keep to the path and use the rail.

VEX: That will send people away.

PIP: People came here to see the creek, not to be told it is a rapids course.

SELA: Vex, what is the best moment of the walk?

VEX: The drop at the bend.

MARA: There is no drop.

VEX: The path drops by the bend.

NARI: It descends one step.

SFX: Footstep down. Gravel shifts.

SELA: I can hear that.

PIP: That's the trail surface.

VEX: We need a stronger sound bed.

NARI: Don't add rushing water that isn't here. Visitors may think the path floods.

MARA: Can we play the welcome recording instead?

SFX: Button click. Recorded voice says "Rushing Creek is a peaceful walk beside clear water. Stay on the marked path."

VEX: That is too accurate.

SELA: It tells people what to expect.

PIP: The bridge is ahead. We can cross one at a time.

SFX: Mara steps across. Boards creak, handrail taps once.

MARA: The view is lovely from here.

NARI: You can hear the whole creek without standing in it.

VEX: That could be the attraction. The quiet view.

SELA: We can sell the walk as a short outing, not a stunt.

MARA: I came for a picnic spot. The bench is just beyond the bridge.

PIP: The trail forks left to the picnic clearing, right back to the road.

VEX: Add "choose your own descent."

NARI: Add a sign at the fork.

SFX: Pencil writes on a map.

SELA: The Welcome Center will update the brochure: gentle creek walk, one low step, one bridge, no rapids.

VEX: Can we retain "Rushing"?

MARA: The creek's name is Rushing Creek.

PIP: It rushes when the rain comes.

NARI: Today it trickles.

VEX: We'll offer a second recording during storms.

SELA: Only if the park closes the bridge when it is unsafe.

SFX: Water burbles. A bird calls.

MARA: The place is enough.

VEX: The brochure needs to say more than that.

PIP: It says where to walk.
