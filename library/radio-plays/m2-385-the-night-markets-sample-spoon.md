# The Night Market's Sample Spoon

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: food radio competitions that make vendors serve tiny samples while announcers mistake the tasting line for a queue to buy*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Pema, a gibbon; vendor Luri, a capuchin; market marshal Odo, a macaque; caller Tavi, a lemur.

[SFX: NIGHT MARKET. CROWD MURMURS. SMALL SPOON TAPS A POT.]

PEMA: The night market's best stew will win the silver ladle. Each stall offers one sample spoon.

LURI: The sample is a taste, not a serving. The full bowl is on the menu.

ODO: The line is supposed to split at the blue rope: tasters left, buyers right.

[SFX: FEET MOVE. A SIGN FALLS FLAT.]

PEMA: The market flyer says "taste every stew" in large letters. It never explains how to order a bowl.

ODO: Add a small arrow to the cashier and keep the sample cups at the tasting table.

LURI: I can keep one serving ticket at the sample station, but payment stays at the register.

TAVI: If I take the ticket to the cashier, do I need to leave the line?

ODO: Yes. The two lines meet only after you have paid.

[SFX: TICKET TEARS FROM A PAD. FOOTSTEPS MOVE TO REGISTER.]

TAVI: I paid. Now I can wait for the bowl without blocking the tasting table.

PEMA: The flyer will say one free taste, bowls sold separately.

LURI: That is what the pot has been saying all night.

TAVI: I thought the sample line was the payment line. I paid for three spoons.

LURI: Those are sample spoons. The cashier is across the lane.

PEMA: The crowd has formed one long queue. It looks like the stew is winning.

ODO: It looks like everyone is waiting for the same tiny cup.

LURI: The pot is ready, but I can't serve full bowls over the sample table.

[SFX: LADLE DIPS. A SMALL SAMPLE CUP FILLS.]

ODO: The route to the exit stays clear while the judge tastes.

LURI: I will keep the pot lid on until the public line reaches the cashier.

PEMA: One spoon for the judge.

ODO: The silver ladle contest is separate from the public line. We need to clear the aisle.

TAVI: I am standing on the rope.

[SFX: ROPE STAND WOBBLES. METAL CLINKS.]

ODO: Step back. I will move the sign, not the rope.

PEMA: Could the host announce the winner while they taste?

LURI: The judge has not tasted the other stalls.

ODO: First we separate the lines. Marshal, put the sample sign at the pot and the menu sign at the cashier.

[SFX: TWO SIGNS LIFT AND SET DOWN.]

TAVI: The sample cup says "try one spoon." The menu says "pay here."

LURI: Correct. If you want a bowl, the cashier can give you a ticket.

PEMA: I have a ticket.

ODO: You are the host.

PEMA: I can buy dinner after the broadcast.

[SFX: CROWD SPLITS; FOOTSTEPS FORM TWO SHORT LINES.]

TAVI: I will buy one bowl, not three samples.

LURI: Here is your ticket. I will ladle it after the tasting table clears.

PEMA: Judge, how is the stew?

[SFX: SPOON TASTES. A BREATH.]

JUDGE VOICE: Smoky, with beans and a little lemon. I choose Luri's pot.

ODO: The judge has voted. The aisle is still open.

PEMA: Luri wins the silver ladle.

LURI: Thank you. The sample is finished, but the pot is open for orders.

TAVI: I can hear the difference now. Sample here, pay there.

ODO: Keep the rope in place. We need a clear lane to the exit.

[SFX: LADLE SETS ON TABLE. CASH REGISTER BEEPS. A FULL BOWL SLIDES ACROSS COUNTER.]

PEMA: The contest ends with one winner and a separate dinner line.

LURI: The tasting spoon is small. The stew isn't.

[SFX: MARKET CROWD RISES, THEN FADES UNDER THE RADIO SIGN-OFF.]
