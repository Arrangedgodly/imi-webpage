# Advice Between the Beeps

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in advice programs that force every ordinary dilemma into a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST PELI, a gibbon
PRODUCER RAK, a macaque
CALLER NOMA, a capuchin
CALLER BIR, a langur
CALLER NERI, a squirrel

[SFX: GENTLE PHONE LINE CHIME.]

PELI: This is "Ask Peli." Our first caller has a decision with two equally good choices.

RAK: We have a suspense bed ready.

PELI: We don't need it.

NOMA: I want to choose a time for our garden club. Mornings suit some members; evenings suit others.

PELI: Could you alternate?

NOMA: We could. I wanted to hear someone say it on the radio.

### Scene One: The Big Decision

[RAK CUES A LOW DRUM ROLL.]

RAK: What is at stake?

NOMA: The meeting schedule.

[RAK PUTS A LARGE QUESTION MARK ON THE STUDIO MONITOR. IT ROTATES SLOWLY.]

PELI: Could we leave the question on the screen?

RAK: It is a radio show.

NOMA: I can hear the monitor humming through the phone.

[RAK TURNS THE SCREEN OFF. THE QUESTION MARK VANISHES, BUT THE DRUM ROLL CONTINUES. PELI PULLS THE AUDIO FADER DOWN.]

PELI: Now we can talk.

PELI: Do you have a room available at both times?

NOMA: Yes.

PELI: Then alternate weeks and post the schedule.

RAK: That's the answer?

NOMA: It is useful.

[SFX: THE DRUM ROLL CONTINUES AFTER THE CALL ENDS. PELI UNPLUGS THE MUSIC PLAYER.]

PELI: We have resolved one question in thirty seconds.

### Scene Two: The Reveal

[SFX: PHONE RINGS TWICE.]

BIR: My neighbor keeps borrowing my watering can and returning it in a different color.

RAK: A betrayal?

BIR: It is still mine. I want to know why it changes.

PELI: Did you label it?

BIR: No. We both have blue cans.

RAK: The mystery deepens.

[PELI ASKS BIR TO CHECK THE HANDLE. BIR READS A NAME SCRATCHED INTO IT.] 

BIR: It says my neighbor's name.

RAK: The can has a history.

BIR: It was his can. I took the wrong one from the shed.

PELI: You can swap them back.

BIR: I will do that tonight.

[RAK CUES A REVEAL STING. IT PLAYS AFTER THE CALLER HAS HUNG UP.] 

PELI: We have discovered a label.

### Scene Three: Producer's Dilemma

[RAK HOLDS UP TWO CUE CARDS: "CONFRONTATION" AND "CALL BACK." ]

RAK: Which should we use for the final segment?

PELI: Neither. We can read the station hours.

RAK: The show needs a twist.

PELI: The twist is both callers had ordinary problems and solved them.

[RAK LOOKS AT THE CALL LOG. ONE NUMBER HAS BEEN DIALED TWICE.] 

RAK: Noma called back.

NOMA: I forgot to mention the room has a whiteboard.

PELI: Write the alternating dates on it.

NOMA: I will.

[RAK CUES THE DRUM ROLL. THE STATION CLOCK BEEPS FOR THE END OF THE HOUR.] 

PELI: That is our actual deadline.

[THE PHONE RINGS AGAIN. RAK LOOKS AT THE CLOCK BEFORE ANSWERING.]

RAK: We have one minute. What is your question?

NERI: My neighbor plays the radio too loudly.

PELI: Have you asked them to turn it down?

NERI: Yes. They did.

RAK: The resolution is in the first sentence.

NERI: I wanted to tell you the station sounds better without the drum roll.

[RAK PLAYS A SHORT CLIP OF THE SHOW WITHOUT THE DRUM ROLL.]

PELI: That is our new sound.

RAK: It is a little quieter.

[RAK UNPLUGS THE PLAYER COMPLETELY.]

### Tag

[SFX: PHONE LINE CLOSES.]

RAK: Should we end on a dramatic question?

PELI: No. End on the station name.

[THE DRUM ROLL CONTINUES UNDER THE STATION ID. PELI REACHES FOR THE SWITCH, BUT RAK HAS LABELED IT "DO NOT STOP." HE UNPLUGS THE PLAYER INSTEAD.]

RAK: The suspense is unplugged.

PELI: That is a useful answer.
