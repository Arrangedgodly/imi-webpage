# The Gallery of Unfinished Things

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio tours, overconfident docent narration, and a sound-sensitive exhibit that keeps changing the route*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Timo, a macaque audio-guide narrator; Sela, a gibbon curator; Nibs, a capuchin technician; and a visiting family of lemurs.*

[SFX: Museum doors open. A soft electronic chime.]

TIMO: Welcome to the Museum of Almost. Please begin in Gallery One, where you will hear about inventions that nearly worked.

SELA: Timo, you are narrating from the control booth. Keep the visitors on the marked path.

NIBS: The path is marked, but the arrows point in both directions.

SELA: One arrow is for entry. The other is for reflection.

TIMO: Exhibit one: the self-stirring porridge bowl. It stirred once, then stirred the spoon into the ceiling.

[SFX: A visitor gasps. A spoon drops somewhere overhead.]

LEMUR CHILD: Is that the spoon?

NIBS: No, that is a different spoon. The original spoon remains in the ceiling.

TIMO: Please do not look up. The next display is the first portable ladder.

SELA: It is not a ladder. It is a ladder-shaped sculpture.

TIMO: It was designed to help monkeys reach high fruit.

SELA: It was designed by someone who had never climbed a ladder.

[SFX: A soft metallic wobble.]

LEMUR PARENT: Why is it moving?

NIBS: Because the exhibit is not bolted down.

SELA: It is bolted down.

NIBS: To the rotating platform.

SELA: That is intentional.

TIMO: Gallery Two features the first umbrella with a built-in fan.

[SFX: Fan starts. The museum map flaps loudly.]

LEMUR CHILD: It is blowing the map away.

TIMO: The umbrella kept rain off the inventor while sending all his papers into the river.

SELA: That is accurate. It was a serious failure.

NIBS: The fan also cools the visitors.

SELA: It is pointed at the exhibit.

NIBS: It is a very broad exhibit.

TIMO: Please proceed to Gallery Three for the whispering statue.

[SFX: A low whisper, then a squeak.]

LEMUR PARENT: What did it say?

NIBS: It said "turn left."

SELA: It said "turn the light off."

TIMO: The statue's sculptor wanted visitors to ask questions. The microphone was installed backwards, so it whispers answers to the wall.

LEMUR CHILD: What is the wall asking?

TIMO: That is outside our collection.

[SFX: Another whisper; a door clicks open.]

SELA: Nibs, why did the service door open?

NIBS: The whisper sensor is connected to the door sensor.

SELA: Disconnect it.

NIBS: I would, but the audio path runs through the exhibit.

TIMO: Visitors, remain on the marked path. The arrows may now point toward the storage room.

LEMUR PARENT: We are already in the storage room.

TIMO: That is Gallery Four, the archive of things we have not decided to display.

SELA: It is a storage room.

NIBS: We found the missing umbrella fan.

[SFX: Fan starts again; loose labels scatter.]

TIMO: The museum's audio map is now circulating among the visitors. Please choose a label and read it aloud.

LEMUR CHILD: "Object: a button. Purpose: unknown. It may have been important."

SELA: That is a button from my coat.

LEMUR PARENT: I have a label that says "The curator's coat, pending return."

SELA: How did that get there?

NIBS: The archive is very thorough.

TIMO: The final exhibit is the unbuilt staircase. It was planned to connect every gallery, but no one agreed where the first step should go.

LEMUR CHILD: I see a staircase.

SELA: That is the ladder sculpture.

NIBS: The rotating platform has lined it up with the stairs.

[SFX: A click, then the platform turns slowly.]

TIMO: The ladder is now a staircase to Gallery One.

SELA: That is not an approved route.

LEMUR PARENT: But it connects the galleries.

SELA: The ladder is moving.

NIBS: So is the path.

TIMO: The exhibit has completed the museum's original plan: every gallery is now connected, though the route takes visitors past the spoon in the ceiling twice.

SELA: We will close the rotating platform and install actual arrows.

NIBS: One arrow or two?

SELA: One arrow that points to the exit.

TIMO: Thank you for visiting the Museum of Almost, where our collection is incomplete but our tour is now circular.

[SFX: Door chime. The fan winds down.]

LEMUR CHILD: I liked the button.

SELA: You may keep the label. The button is going back on my coat.

[SFX: A button clicks into place; soft museum ambience fades.]
