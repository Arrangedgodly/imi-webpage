# The Cliffhanger at the End of the Reel

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: serialized radio mysteries that cut away before obvious solutions and recap the same clue repeatedly*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
NARRATOR VERA, a macaque
DETECTIVE LOK, a gorilla
ASSISTANT ANI, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST RUM, a langur
SOUND TECH KET, a gibbon

[SFX: THUNDER, A CREAKING DOOR, FOOTSTEPS ON WOOD.]

VERA: At last, Detective Lok reaches the locked archive. The key is missing. The clock strikes midnight.

LOK: It is two in the afternoon.

KET: I used the night filter.

ANI: And the door is not locked.

[ANI TURNS THE HANDLE. IT OPENS.]

VERA: Our mystery has begun to unravel.

### Scene One: The Clue

[INSIDE THE ARCHIVE, A METRONOME TICKS.]

LOK: The missing ledger was last seen beside the clock.

ANI: Here it is, on the desk.

KET: We need to save the reveal for the end.

LOK: It is open to the inventory page.

VERA: Could the ledger itself be a decoy?

ANI: The label says "Ledger." The pages list the archive contents.

[SFX: A DRAMATIC STING.]

KET: That is my sting button.

LOK: Please label the buttons.

### Scene Two: The Suspect

[FOOTSTEPS APPROACH.]

VERA: A shadow enters the room.

ANI: That is the archivist.

RUM: I came to lock up. Why is the door open?

LOK: We are looking for the missing brass bookmark.

RUM: It is in the ledger. I checked it out this morning.

[LOK ASKS ANI TO REPLAY THE LAST TEN MINUTES. KET PLAYS FOOTSTEPS, A DOOR CREAK, AND THE SOUND OF THE LEDGER OPENING.]

LOK: Which sound matters?

ANI: The pages. Rum checked the entry this morning.

VERA: A clue hidden in plain hearing.

RUM: It is written on the front of the page.

[KET PLAYS A SHORT OMINOUS STING. IT IS LOUDER THAN THE LEDGER'S PAGE TURN. ANI TURNS THE STING DOWN.]

KET: A confession!

RUM: It is written on the page.

[ANI TURNS THE LEDGER TOWARD THE MICROPHONE. PAPER RUSTLES.]

ANI: The entry says, "Brass bookmark, loaned to reading group, return Thursday."

VERA: The mystery points toward the reading group.

LOK: The entry tells us where it is.

### Scene Three: The Chase

[SFX: FAST FOOTSTEPS, THEN A RECORD SCRATCH.]

VERA: The detective sprints through the corridor.

LOK: I am walking. The reading group is in the next room.

[THE DOOR OPENS. A SMALL GROUP IS FINISHING A BOOK DISCUSSION.]

ANI: Here is the brass bookmark.

RUM: Please return it to the archive after the meeting.

[LOK LIFTS THE LEDGER. A LOOSE METAL CLASP RATTLES.]

LOK: That is the new sound.

KET: Should we use the thunder?

ANI: We should tighten the clasp.

[KET TURNS OFF THE THUNDER BUTTON AND HANDS ANI A SMALL SCREWDRIVER. THE CLASP STOPS RATTLING AFTER A QUARTER TURN.]

VERA: The clue is no longer making a sound.

LOK: We have reached the end of the file.

RUM: And the end of the case.

[KET SLIDES THE STILL-POWERED EFFECTS BOARD TO THE END OF THE DESK. THE METRONOME CONTINUES TICKING IN THE ARCHIVE.] 

KET: We have solved the case too early.

VERA: We could reveal the solution next week.

LOK: The audience can hear it now.

### Scene Four: The New Mystery

[THE GROUP HANDS OVER THE BOOKMARK. A TINY METAL CLINK SOUNDS INSIDE THE LEDGER'S COVER.]

VERA: A second clue?

ANI: That was the bookmark striking the repaired clasp.

LOK: One returned bookmark, one sound.

VERA: Unless the bookmark carries a message.

RUM: It says page thirty-two.

KET: I have thirty-two thunderclaps ready.

ANI: We will manage with a page turn.

[KET PLAYS A THUNDERCLAP. THE ARCHIVIST TURNS OFF THE EFFECTS BOARD.] 

RUM: There is no storm in the archive.

VERA: The case is closed.

[THE DOOR OPENS. THE SOUND IS JUST A DOOR. KET DOES NOT ADD A STING.]

KET: The door is now open.

### Tag

[SFX: DOOR SHUTS. A KEY TURNS.]

KET: Now it is locked.

LOK: We are inside.

ANI: We have the key.

KET: I will open the door.
