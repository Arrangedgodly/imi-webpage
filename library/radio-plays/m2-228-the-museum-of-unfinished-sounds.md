# The Museum of Unfinished Sounds

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio arts documentary conventions, where a producer insists every unfinished noise be interpreted as a masterpiece*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Producer SAVA, lemur; sound artist ODO, baboon; curator MIRI, capuchin; visitor KITO, macaque.

[SFX: GALLERY HUM. A METAL OBJECT DROPS, BOUNCES TWICE.] 

SAVA: You have just heard "Untitled Number Seven," a meditation on gravity and the visitor's interrupted attention.

KITO: I dropped my water cup.

MIRI: It is a museum cup. The sound belongs to the exhibition now.

ODO: I was still working on that piece.

SAVA: The cup gave it a title. "Two Bounces Before Thirst."

ODO: It bounced once.

MIRI: The second sound was the table.

SAVA: A collaboration between metal and furniture. We should record the room tone.

She switches on a recorder. A ventilation fan hums. Odo's tail brushes a hanging paper sheet.

[SFX: LOW HUM, PAPER BRUSH.]

SAVA: That is "Air Learning to Hesitate."

ODO: The fan needs cleaning.

KITO: I thought the art was unfinished. Maybe the fan is still learning.

Miri leads them to Odo's workbench. There are three objects: a hollow gourd, a spoon, and a small drum. Odo says he is testing how each sound changes when moved through the gallery. Sava wants a single interpretation before the listening tour begins.

SAVA: The spoon is a memory of a meal.

ODO: It is a spoon.

MIRI: The label can say both.

SAVA: Visitors need a doorway into the work.

KITO: They need a doorway that doesn't lock behind them.

Kito turns the spoon over. It rolls off the bench, tapping the floor.

[SFX: SPOON ROLLS, THREE LIGHT TAPS.]

SAVA: "A Meal Leaving the Table."

ODO: That sound is too bright. The spoon is supposed to answer the drum.

SAVA: Then hit the drum.

Odo taps it. The drum gives a flat thump. The gourd rolls against it, producing a hollow rattle. Sava listens, then begins to narrate over the sound.

SAVA: In the silence after the meal-

MIRI: You are covering the silence.

SAVA: I can whisper.

She whispers directly into the microphone. The recording clips with breath.

ODO: Your whisper is louder than the drum.

SAVA: The microphone is very sensitive to art.

Miri moves it farther back. Kito carries the gourd to the gallery entrance, where a draft makes it rock on the floor.

[SFX: GOURD ROCKS, THEN SETTLES.]

KITO: It keeps almost rolling.

SAVA: "The Decision Not to Depart."

ODO: That is what I was testing: the gourd is balanced only when the spoon braces it.

Miri sets the spoon beside the gourd, but it rolls away again. Odo adds a small felt pad. Now the gourd is still; the fan hum and paper brush continue.

SAVA: Does the work have a final title?

ODO: It has a final sound, if everyone stops making suggestions.

They all hold still. A visitor's cup drips into the basin. The drop echoes under the gourd. Sava opens her mouth to explain, but Miri puts a finger to her lips.

[SFX: SINGLE WATER DROP. QUIET ROOM.]

KITO: I can hear the room.

ODO: That is the piece.

SAVA: I have to tell the listening tour what it means.

MIRI: Tell them where to stand. Let them decide what it means.

Sava turns on the gallery speaker. Her recorded voice begins: "The piece explores-" She rushes to switch it off, but presses the wrong button. The fan stops. In the new quiet, the gourd gives one last tiny creak.

[SFX: FAN CLICKS OFF. GOURD CREAK.]

SAVA: A final statement.

ODO: No. The gourd is settling.

MIRI: Then let it finish.

Sava lifts the recorder and asks whether the last creak should be labeled as a conclusion or a transition. Odo gently removes his hand from the table; the paper sheet flutters once.

ODO: Neither. That was my sleeve.

SAVA: Can we include the sleeve?

KITO: You can include everything. You cannot explain everything.

Sava puts the recorder down. For once, she leaves the label blank. The visitors stop to listen, waiting for the gallery to tell them what to think. No one speaks.

[SFX: ROOM TONE, THEN FADE.]
