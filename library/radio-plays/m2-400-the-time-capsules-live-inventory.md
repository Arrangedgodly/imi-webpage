# The Time Capsule's Live Inventory

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: anniversary radio broadcasts that announce objects for a time capsule before the archivist has checked what is actually inside*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Sela, a macaque; archivist Tumi, a lemur; builder Noro, a gibbon; caller Vena, a capuchin.

[SFX: COMMUNITY HALL. METAL BOX LID OPENS. PAPER RUSTLES.]

SELA: We are sealing a time capsule for the town's fiftieth anniversary. Tumi has the inventory.

TUMI: The inventory is still being checked. We have a bus ticket, a school photo and a letter.

NORO: The exhibit card also lists a street map.

VENA: I brought a new map this morning. It is not inside the box.

SELA: The card says it is.

TUMI: The card is a draft. I have not signed it.

[SFX: PAPER CARD LIFTS. METAL BOX SHIFTS.]

SELA: Could we announce the map as a surprise?

TUMI: Not until it is placed inside and logged.

NORO: The box has a narrow slot. The photo won't fit through it.

TUMI: The lid is open. We can place items one at a time and record each one.

VENA: The letter is sealed. Should it stay sealed?

TUMI: Yes. The envelope is labeled with the writer and date. We do not need to open it.

[SFX: PAPER ENVELOPE SETS INSIDE METAL BOX.]

SELA: First item, the letter.

TUMI: Logged. Second, the school photo, dated this year.

[SFX: PHOTO SLIDES INTO BOX.]

NORO: The bus ticket is damp.

TUMI: It was in a coat pocket outside. Put it on the drying sheet, not in the capsule.

SELA: The broadcast promised three items.

TUMI: The inventory will have two until the ticket is dry and checked.

VENA: I have a clean ticket from the same route, dated yesterday.

TUMI: It is a different object and would tell a different story. We should not substitute it.

[SFX: DRYING SHEET RUSTLES. TICKET PRESSES FLAT.]

SELA: The capsule is due to be sealed at six.

NORO: It is five fifty. We can wait for the ticket to dry.

TUMI: The ticket has ink bleeding across the route number.

SELA: Can we still include it?

TUMI: I need to log it as damaged and place it in an archival sleeve. If the ink transfers, we leave it out.

[SFX: SLEEVE OPENS. TICKET SLIDES INSIDE.]

VENA: The route number is still readable.

TUMI: Then it can go in. The sleeve keeps it from touching the letter.

[SFX: SLEEVE SETS IN BOX. PENCIL SCRATCHES ON INVENTORY.]

TUMI: The promo copy claims a map is in the box. It is on the exhibit table.

SELA: I will record a correction before the sealing announcement.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.]

SELA: Correction: the time capsule holds a dated letter, a school photo and a sleeved bus ticket. The current street map remains on display in the hall.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES.]

VENA: The map is still available for visitors to read.

NORO: The lid sticker says "MAP INCLUDED." We need to replace it before we bolt the box.

[SFX: OLD STICKER PEELS OFF METAL. PAPER CRINKLES.]

TUMI: Use the inventory label, not the exhibit list.

SELA: The sponsor asked for a simple headline.

TUMI: "Three objects sealed" is simple. "A map in the box" is false.

[SFX: NEW LABEL PRINTS AND PRESSES ONTO THE LID.]

SELA: Three objects are inside: letter, school photo and sleeved bus ticket.

TUMI: Correct. The map stays in the exhibit case, not the capsule.

SELA: The recorded correction is saved. It will replace the promo in the replay schedule.

TUMI: Check the label against the inventory before closing.

[SFX: PAPER LIST HELD BESIDE THE METAL BOX. A FINGER TAPS EACH LINE.]

VENA: Letter, school photo, sleeved ticket.

TUMI: Three entries. No map.

NORO: The lid is clear. Nothing is caught under the edge.

[SFX: METAL LID SETS DOWN. BOLTS TURN ONE AT A TIME.]

SELA: The capsule is sealed after the corrected announcement, not before.

NORO: The box is ready to close.

[SFX: LID CLOSES. TWO BOLTS TURN.]

SELA: The live inventory is complete.

VENA: And the map is still available to read today.

TUMI: The capsule label lists only what is inside.

[SFX: FINAL LABEL PEELS, PRESSES ON METAL. MIC CLOSES.]
