# The Parade Route in the Studio

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio event coverage that recreates a whole outdoor parade in a cramped booth and mistakes layered sound effects for live reporting*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Reko, a macaque; field producer Ena, a gibbon; sound mixer Kito, a langur; marshal Balu, a capuchin.

[SFX: STUDIO BOOTH. DISTANT PARADE DRUMS PLAY THROUGH A SPEAKER.] 

REKO: We are live at the Spring Parade. The lead drum line is approaching the plaza.

ENA: We are not at the plaza. We are in Studio Two, with a recording of last year's drums.

KITO: The weather feed says rain. The parade has moved indoors to the gym, so we are recreating the route.

REKO: The audience was promised a live street broadcast.

BALU: I am the parade marshal. The gym route is four circles around the basketball court.

ENA: We can create the plaza with sound. Drums left, crowd right, floats behind the mic.

[SFX: DRUM TRACK PANS LEFT; CROWD TRACK PANS RIGHT; A BICYCLE BELL DINGS.] 

REKO: The parade is passing us now.

BALU: That bell is my bicycle. I am standing outside the booth.

KITO: The bell track came from the wrong channel. I will mute it.

[SFX: BELL STOPS; DRUMS AND CROWD CONTINUE.] 

REKO: The parade has reached the fountain.

ENA: There is no fountain in the gym.

BALU: There is a water cooler by the bleachers. Keep the floats away from it.

[SFX: PAPER FLOAT WHEELS ROLL; RUBBER WHEELS SQUEAK.] 

KITO: The miniature float is turning around the studio chair.

REKO: Our camera can show the parade on the tabletop.

ENA: The banner reads "STUDIO ROUTE." We should be clear that we are using a model.

REKO: The sponsor wanted the sound of marching feet.

[SFX: KITO SHAKES A BOX OF WOODEN BEADS; RAPID CLACKS.] 

BALU: Those are not feet. The gym floor has rubber mats.

KITO: I can add soft footfalls.

[SFX: THREE HEAVY STOMPS; MIC PEAKS.] 

REKO: The parade is shaking the broadcast.

ENA: That was Kito under the desk.

KITO: I was reaching for the fader.

BALU: In the gym, the marshal walks beside the floats and keeps the wheels clear. No one stomps.

REKO: Listeners, we are recreating the route from inside the studio. The actual parade is in the school gym.

[SFX: LIVE PHONE LINE CLICKS.] 

CALLER: I am at the gym. The first float is crossing the court, and the drums are quiet.

ENA: Can you describe what is happening?

CALLER: The children are walking, not marching. The fish float has lost a paper fin.

KITO: The sponsor logo is printed on the spare fin.

BALU: The float is a fish. Its fin should look like a fin.

REKO: We can turn the logo inward so the children can finish the float.

ENA: The caller says the tail is flapping against the gym door.

BALU: I am there now. Hold the paper steady while I tape it.

BALU: I will bring the spare fin to the gym door.

REKO: We have a live detail from the real route. The fish float needs a paper fin.

KITO: I can stop the old drum track while Balu carries it.

[SFX: TRACK FADES; STUDIO GOES QUIET EXCEPT PAPER RUSTLE.] 

ENA: The studio is silent now. That is the accurate sound while we wait.

REKO: The caller says the fin is attached. The fish float has rejoined the circle.

BALU: The route is complete. The last group is returning to the gym entrance.

[SFX: DISTANT CHILDREN CLAP; ONE PAPER WHEEL ROLLS BY.] 

REKO: Our broadcast was not on the parade route, but the parade has finished. The studio model can go back in its box.

KITO: I will label the drum recording "last year's parade," not "live plaza." 

ENA: And the bicycle bell stays out of the crowd track.

REKO: Thank you for joining the real parade from wherever you were.

[SFX: BOX LID CLOSES; MIC CLICKS OFF.]
