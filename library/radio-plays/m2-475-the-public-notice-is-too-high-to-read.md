# The Public Notice Is Too High to Read

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: official announcement shows that celebrate a notice without checking who can use it*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Nari, a lemur; clerk Odo, a gibbon; resident Pip, a capuchin; chair Mara, a macaque; installer Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Town square. Ladder feet scrape against stone. Paper flaps above.

NARI: This is Your Town in Print. The council has posted its new notice about the spring market.

ODO: It is eight feet above the ground.

PIP: I cannot read it.

VEX: It is visible from far away.

MARA: It is visible to the taller residents.

ODO: The text says "Market opens at nine. Use the east gate."

PIP: I can hear you read it. I need to know whether I can bring my cart through the gate.

VEX: The notice does not mention carts.

NARI: That's a problem.

VEX: The font is large.

MARA: The words are too high.

ODO: I can move the paper lower.

SFX: Ladder shifts. Vex climbs two rungs. The notice flaps in his face.

VEX: The wind is in the way.

PIP: The wind is not the reader.

MARA: Bring it down, Vex.

SFX: Paper peels from board. Staple clicks loose.

VEX: The notice has fallen.

ODO: It landed face down.

NARI: We can read it on the table.

SFX: Paper smooths flat. A pencil rolls.

PIP: The east gate is closed for repair.

MARA: The new notice says to use it.

ODO: The gate repair notice is still in the folder.

VEX: The market notice is the one everyone needs to see.

NARI: They also need the gate information.

PIP: If I take my cart to the east gate, I have to turn around and cross the square.

MARA: We can post both notices at eye level, side by side.

VEX: The board is small.

ODO: We can print a short version. "Market at nine. West gate open. Carts welcome."

PIP: I can read that.

NARI: The market opens tomorrow, Saturday, May 10. This template says May 8, last year's date.

MARA: Put tomorrow's date on the new notice.

ODO: Today is Friday, May 9. The market is not open yet.

PIP: Good. I can bring my cart through the west gate in the morning.

ODO: The printer used last year's template.

PIP: Two shoppers came to the east gate yesterday, following the old May 8 notice.

SFX: Cart wheel rattles in the distance. It stops. A latch clunks.

MARA: I will tell them tomorrow's market uses the west gate.

VEX: We need a second board.

NARI: We need a correct notice at a readable height.

ODO: I can print a new page with today's date and the west gate directions.

SFX: Printer whirs. Paper feeds. A fresh page tears off.

PIP: Put the top line where I can reach it.

MARA: The notice should be reachable by the people who need it.

VEX: We can mount it beside the board, not above it.

ODO: The paper is at shoulder height.

PIP: I can read it without a ladder.

NARI: We have a useful notice at last.

VEX: Should we announce its success?

MARA: The market opens tomorrow. Announce the market.

PIP: I'll roll my empty cart from here to the west gate. You can hear whether the route is clear.

SFX: Cart rolls across square stones, then pauses at a wooden bump.

PIP: There's a board leg sticking into the path.

VEX: I'll turn it sideways.

SFX: Board shifts. Cart rolls smoothly.

NARI: Add "clear path to west gate" to tomorrow's notice.

ODO: I can fit it under "carts welcome."

NARI: This has been Your Town in Print. Tomorrow: Saturday, May 10, nine o'clock, west gate open, carts welcome.

TAG

SFX: The old notice flaps from high on the wall.

PIP: That one still says east gate.

VEX: I can reach it now.

SFX: Paper tears free and lands in the bin.
