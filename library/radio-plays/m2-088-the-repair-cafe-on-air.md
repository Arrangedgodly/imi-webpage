# The Repair Cafe on Air

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in radio program helping listeners fix a squeaky nest platform*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: HOST MIRI, a macaque; FIXER DEX, a capuchin; CALLER TOLL, a gibbon; and a wooden platform.

[SFX: TOOLBOX OPENS, A SMALL SQUEAK.] 

MIRI: Welcome to Fix It by Ear, where listeners describe a problem and Dex helps them decide what to inspect. Toll, what needs repair?

TOLL: My nest platform squeaks when I step on the left corner.

DEX: Does it wobble?

TOLL: No.

DEX: Does the support move?

TOLL: I don't know.

MIRI: Can you step on it again while we listen?

[ONE FOOTSTEP, A HIGH SQUEAK.] 

DEX: That sounds like wood rubbing against wood.

TOLL: Should I add oil?

DEX: Not until we know where the squeak comes from. Is there a loose twig caught under the corner?

TOLL: I can look.

[SFX: PLATFORM LIFTS, A TWIG SLIDES OUT.] 

TOLL: There is a twig under it.

DEX: Is it part of the support?

TOLL: It is a short dry twig, not attached to anything.

DEX: Set it aside, then lower the platform carefully.

[PLATFORM SETS DOWN. ONE FOOTSTEP, NO SQUEAK.] 

TOLL: It's quiet.

MIRI: The repair took less time than the explanation.

DEX: We can still explain why we checked before adding oil.

MIRI: Please do.

DEX: Oil can make wood slippery. If a twig is trapped underneath, removing it solves the problem without changing the platform.

TOLL: I have a second question. One side of my nest is higher than the other.

DEX: Is the platform level?

TOLL: Yes.

DEX: Is the nest comfortable?

TOLL: Yes.

DEX: Then you may not need to fix it.

MIRI: This show is called Fix It by Ear.

DEX: Sometimes you hear that nothing is broken.

MIRI: Dex, should we tighten the platform while Toll is on the line?

DEX: We can ask Toll to inspect the supports from a safe position. Do not climb under a platform that may shift.

TOLL: I am on the ground. The left support is still seated in its socket.

DEX: Is the floor beneath it soft?

TOLL: The soil is damp, but the support is not sinking.

DEX: Then the platform is stable. We have removed the twig that caused the squeak.

MIRI: Our listeners may be waiting for a tool demonstration.

DEX: We can show the difference between a loose joint and a trapped object with two pieces of scrap wood.

[SFX: TWO BOARDS RUB TOGETHER, MAKING A SQUEAK. A SMALL TWIG IS REMOVED; THE RUBBING STOPS.] 

MIRI: That is a clear demonstration.

TOLL: I have found another twig on the platform.

DEX: Is it trapped in the joint?

TOLL: No. It is lying on top.

DEX: You can move it if you want to, but it is not causing the sound.

TOLL: I will leave it. It can go back outside when I sweep tomorrow.

MIRI: We have identified the cause, checked the support, and resisted adding oil.

DEX: That's a repair show.

MIRI: We have one more caller asking how to stop a branch from creaking in the wind.

DEX: First find out whether the branch is healthy and attached. A creak alone does not tell us it is unsafe. If it is cracked, hanging low, or changing suddenly, get help from someone who can inspect it.

TOLL: My platform is quiet now.

DEX: Check it again after a day of use. If it changes, call someone who can inspect the support.

MIRI: And the branch can make whatever sound the wind gives it.

[SFX: A FEATHER BRUSHES THE MICROPHONE.] 

TOLL: Something is brushing the platform now.

DEX: Is it attached?

TOLL: It's a feather from my tail.

MIRI: That one can stay.

DEX: Unless it starts squeaking.

[SFX: TOOLBOX CLOSES, PLATFORM STAYS QUIET.]
