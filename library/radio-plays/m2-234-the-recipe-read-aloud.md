# The Recipe Read Aloud

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: cooking radio convention, where a presenter reads a recipe literally while listeners cook along*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host PIRI, capuchin; cook KES, macaque; caller ODO, baboon; producer LUMA, gibbon.

[SFX: KITCHEN TIMER, THEME MUSIC.] 

PIRI: Today we make a simple nut loaf. Step one: add one cup of ground nuts.

KES: The nuts are whole.

PIRI: It says ground.

KES: Then I have to grind them.

LUMA: The microphone is live.

KES: The mortar is louder.

[SFX: PESTLE CRUSHING NUTS.] 

PIRI: We will continue after that brief percussion break. Step two says "mix until smooth."

KES: The nuts are dry. There is nothing to smooth.

PIRI: Perhaps smooth is a texture goal.

KES: It is a direction.

Luma reads ahead. The recipe says to add a spoonful of oil, but the studio only has a spoon and a jar with no label.

ODO: I can call in with a spoonful of experience.

PIRI: Caller Odo, what oil do you use?

ODO: I don't. I use water.

KES: That changes the loaf.

ODO: It makes a different loaf.

PIRI: Our show follows the printed recipe.

KES: Then print the part where the jar is unlabeled.

Luma checks the jar. It is syrup, not oil. Piri says they can substitute it if the recipe permits. Kes asks whether a radio recipe can smell the difference.

[SFX: JAR LID, STICKY SPOON.]

PIRI: Step three: fold in the syrup.

KES: It says oil.

PIRI: The syrup is already on the spoon.

KES: I can wash it.

PIRI: We are on a schedule.

Kes refuses to pour it. She wipes the spoon clean and measures the oil. The timer rings though nothing is in the oven.

ODO: The timer heard "schedule."

PIRI: Step four: bake for thirty minutes.

KES: We haven't mixed the dry ingredients.

PIRI: We have mixed them on air.

KES: The listeners cannot eat the broadcast.

Luma switches off the timer. Kes adds nuts, flour, water and oil into a bowl. Piri reads each step slowly, but she gets ahead and tells Kes to pour the batter into a pan before Kes has greased it.

[SFX: BATTER STOPS, BOWL SCRAPE.]

KES: The pan is not ready.

PIRI: The recipe says prepare the pan.

KES: You skipped that line.

PIRI: I was reading aloud.

KES: You were reading the next page.

Odo calls again. He says his loaf is in the oven, but he used the jar syrup because he heard Piri say to fold it in.

PIRI: Odo, take the loaf out.

ODO: It's not baked yet.

KES: Then do not take it out.

PIRI: We are losing the audience.

LUMA: The audience is cooking.

Kes greases the pan, pours the batter, and sets the timer.

Piri reads the ingredient list again. It calls for two cups of flour, but the studio measure is marked only with a line.

PIRI: How much is a cup?

KES: It is the cup on the counter.

PIRI: The line is halfway down.

KES: Then someone drank half the flour.

LUMA: Flour is still in the bag.

PIRI: We can estimate.

KES: The recipe does not say estimate.

Odo calls back. His syrup loaf has stuck to the pan. He asks how to save it. Kes tells him to let it cool before loosening the edge. Piri starts to invent a dramatic rescue technique; Luma takes the phone off speaker and repeats Kes's actual instruction.

ODO: It came free.

PIRI: A successful rescue!

KES: A cooling rack.

PIRI: The recipe has many heroes.

Five minutes pass while Piri reads the final recipe notes. The timer rings. Kes tests the loaf with a twig; it comes out wet. Piri asks her to describe the smell. There is no smell yet.

KES: It smells like a bowl.

PIRI: A bowl with promise?

KES: A bowl with batter.

The timer rings. Kes tests the loaf with a twig; it comes out wet. She adds five minutes. Piri reads the recipe's final line: "Serve warm."

KES: After it is baked.

PIRI: Yes. We can tell the listeners to do that part at home.

[SFX: TIMER RINGS AGAIN. WOODEN TAP ON LOAF.]

KES: Now it is done.

PIRI: And how was the recipe?

KES: It was very easy to follow when I did not follow you.

ODO: Mine is still syrup.

PIRI: Tune in next week for a recipe with fewer pages.
