# The Call-In Contest With No Caller

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio competitions that fill dead air with fake callers and accidentally award prizes to studio staff*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Host Mena, a gibbon; producer Taro, a macaque; engineer Suli, a capuchin; caller Bex, a lemur; caller Iru, a langur; caller Yali, a tamarin.

[SFX: STUDIO PHONE RINGS. THEME MUSIC PLAYS. MIC OPENS.]

MENA: Welcome to Guess the Garden Sound. The seventh caller can win a basket of garden tools.

TARO: We have no callers yet, but the clock says contest hour.

SULI: The phone line is not connected. It is ringing inside the producer booth.

TARO: That is our cue. Put it on air.

MENA: Line seven, you're live. What sound did we play?

[SFX: THEME MUSIC. A PHONE RINGS IN THE STUDIO, THEN A DISTANT VOICE SAYS, "HELLO?"]

BEX: I called the number, but I hear the studio through another phone.

MENA: You're our caller?

BEX: I am outside the station. Your phone line rang and then played a recording of someone saying hello.

TARO: The contest system has a demo caller.

SULI: It was meant for sound check. The demo button is connected to the live queue.

MENA: Did Bex hear the garden sound?

BEX: No. I heard the phone ring.

TARO: Can we count the ring as the contest sound?

MENA: No. The prompt was a bird tapping a seed pod.

SULI: The demo file is queued now. Don't touch the caller button again until I mute it.

[SFX: A SHORT PLAYBACK: BIRD PECKING A HARD SEED POD.]

BEX: That sounds like a woodpecker.

MENA: It was a finch at the garden feeder.

TARO: Bex has guessed close enough. Give them the tool basket.

BEX: I didn't hear that before. You just played it.

MENA: You're right. You can't win a contest you didn't enter.

TARO: We need a winner before the sponsor break.

SULI: We need an actual line. I can connect the public phone once the demo path is disabled.

[SFX: BUTTON PRESSES. A LOW TONE. PHONE LINE CLICKS.]

SULI: Public line is open. Bex, can you hear the prompt now?

BEX: I hear a bird sound.

[SFX: BIRD PECKS A SEED POD, THREE RAPID TAPS.]

BEX: Finch tapping a seed pod?

MENA: Correct. You are caller one. The contest asked for caller seven.

TARO: We have six empty positions.

BEX: Then I cannot be number seven.

MENA: The prize goes to the seventh caller, not the seventh ring in the booth.

TARO: Could we call six times from the office phone?

SULI: No. The contest rules require separate callers.

MENA: We can wait for more callers or end the contest.

TARO: The sponsor paid for seven winners.

SULI: The sponsor paid for a contest. It did not buy fake callers.

[SFX: PHONE LINE RINGS. ANOTHER LINE CLICKS OPEN.]

TARO: Caller two.

MENA: You are live. What did you hear?

YALI: A bird tapping something. Is it a nut?

MENA: It is a seed pod. Not quite.

SULI: That is a real caller, and a real guess.

TARO: We still have five calls to go.

MENA: Then we keep the line open and let people call. No studio demo.

[SFX: PHONE LINES RING SPORADICALLY. THE HOST TAKES ONE BREATH, THEN THE MUSIC ENDS.]

BEX: I can hear the callers waiting now.

MENA: Caller two was close. No prize until caller seven.

TARO: The sponsor break is here. We can announce the basket remains unclaimed.

SULI: And the demo button is disconnected.

[SFX: CABLE UNPLUGS. A SMALL BEEP CONFIRMS.]





[SFX: CALLS THREE THROUGH SIX CONNECT IN TURN. EACH HEARS THE PROMPT ONCE; THEY GIVE DISTINCT GUESSES AND DISCONNECT. THE SEVENTH LINE RINGS.]

MENA: Caller seven is on line one. What did you hear?

IRU: A finch knocking a seed pod against the feeder tray.

MENA: Correct. The caller is seven, the line is real, and the basket is yours.

TARO: We can deliver the prize after the show.

SULI: The demo caller stays unplugged.

[SFX: A PRIZE BASKET LIFTS FROM THE SHELF. PAPER WRAPS AROUND ITS HANDLE.]

BEX: That was an actual contest.

MENA: Yes. It took seven callers and one honest sound.

[SFX: THEME STING. MIC CLOSES. PHONE LINES REMAIN OPEN; NO RECORDED CALLER PLAYS.]
