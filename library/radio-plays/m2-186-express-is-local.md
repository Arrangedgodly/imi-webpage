# The Express Is a Local

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: commuter radio updates that turn routine timetable changes into urgent travel drama*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST LOR, a macaque
CONDUCTOR NEM, a gibbon
ENGINEER TAVI, a capuchin
CALLER SADA, a gorilla

[SFX: TRAIN STATION AMBIENCE, TWO BELL TONES.]

LOR: This is the Canopy Line update. The 8:10 express is running as a local service today.

TAVI: The opening sting says "major disruption."

LOR: The service stops at three additional stations.

TAVI: I can add a warning tone.

NEM: I am at platform two. The platform sign reads "8:10 Local Stops."

LOR: Can you read it again for the urgency?

NEM: I can read it at the same volume.

[THE STATION PA CRACKLES. A SECOND VOICE ANNOUNCES A DELAYED EXPRESS ON ANOTHER LINE.]

TAVI: We have a second disruption.

NEM: That train is not ours.

LOR: The update is getting crowded.

### Scene One: Platform Report

[SFX: PA SPEAKER CRACKLE.]

NEM: Passengers for the express should board at platform two. It will make all local stops today.

LOR: Conductor Nem, what caused the change?

NEM: A signal inspection between Oak and Hill. We will travel at the posted limit.

TAVI: Can you make that sound more suspenseful?

NEM: The inspection is finished. We are boarding now.

[SFX: A DOOR BEEPS. A SINGLE PASSENGER RUNS TOWARD THE TRAIN.]

SADA: Is this the 8:10?

NEM: It is. You have time to board.

LOR: The race against the clock has reached the platform.

SADA: I am walking at a normal pace.

### Scene Two: The Map

[TAVI OPENS A PAPER ROUTE MAP.]

TAVI: The graphic shows the train stopping at four stations instead of one.

LOR: There are four intermediate stops.

TAVI: I can call them unexpected detours.

NEM: They are scheduled stops today.

[SFX: TRAIN ROLLING, THEN BRAKES.]

NEM: We have arrived at Oak Street. Doors open on the left.

[THE DOORS BEEP TWICE. A PASSENGER ASKS IF THE TRAIN WILL STOP AT FERN ROAD.]

NEM: Yes. It is making the local stops today.

LOR: A destination question has entered the broadcast.

TAVI: I can play the warning tone.

NEM: The station chime already played.

[TAVI PRESSES THE TONE BUTTON. IT PLAYS THE SAME TWO BELL NOTES AS THE PLATFORM SIGNAL.]

NEM: We are leaving Oak Street now.

SADA: The connection bus is still at Hill?

NEM: Yes. It will wait for this train.

LOR: We can report that without a suspense bed.

TAVI: The tone is ready if the bus leaves.

NEM: It will not. The driver can see the platform.

LOR: One stop completed. Three remain.

SADA: I am getting off at Hill. I have a longer walk from this station.

NEM: The connection bus is waiting across the road.

TAVI: I have a sound cue for "missed connection."

LOR: Do not play it. The bus is waiting.

[SFX: DOORS CLOSE. THE TRAIN ROLLS ON.]

### Scene Three: Arrival

[SFX: TRAIN BELL, THEN A SOFT STOP.]

NEM: Hill Street. Passengers for the connection bus, exit at the front.

SADA: That gave me time to ask about the route.

LOR: The 8:10 arrived four minutes later than its usual express schedule and served the local stations.

TAVI: The listener knows what happened.

LOR: We did not need a warning siren.

[THE CONDUCTOR ANNOUNCES THE NEXT DEPARTURE. TAVI STARTS THE DRAMATIC STING; IT SOUNDS LIKE THE TRAIN DOOR BEEP.]

NEM: That is the boarding signal.

LOR: The riders have reached their stops, and the platform announcement remains clear.

TAVI: The warning tone is back in the sound-effects drawer.

NEM: Good. We need the boarding chime.

### Tag

[SFX: TWO BELL TONES.]

LOR: This has been the Canopy Line update. The 8:10 continues as a local service.

TAVI: And the platform remains open.
