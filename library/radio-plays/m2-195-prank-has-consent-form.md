# The Prank Has a Consent Form

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: morning radio prank calls that treat a recorded reaction as permission to continue the bit*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST KITO, a macaque
PRODUCER VERA, a capuchin
CALLER NALA, a gorilla
ENGINEER SIO, a langur

[SFX: MORNING THEME MUSIC. A PHONE BUTTON BEEPS.]

KITO: It is time for our famous surprise call. The caller has agreed to be on air after the reveal.

SIO: That means they agreed to the reveal, not the whole segment.

VERA: I have a soundboard and a fake delivery script.

KITO: Let's call Nala.

### Scene One: The Call

[SFX: PHONE RINGS TWICE.]

NALA: Hello?

KITO: Your parcel is here, but the label says it contains a live goat.

NALA: I ordered a lamp.

[VERA PLAYS A GOAT BLEAT.]

NALA: That is a recording.

SIO: The caller recognized the soundboard.

KITO: It is a very convincing goat.

NALA: I am at work. Please stop the call.

[VERA REACHES FOR THE NEXT SOUND. SIO DISCONNECTS THE LINE.] 

KITO: We have a reaction.

SIO: We have a request to stop.

### Scene Two: The Consent Form

[VERA HOLDS UP A RELEASE FORM.]

VERA: She signed this last month for a station interview.

SIO: It says interview. It does not mention a prank.

KITO: Can we use the first thirty seconds?

SIO: She asked us to stop. We should not use the call.

[THE AUDIO BOARD HAS A BUTTON MARKED "LAUGH." VERA PRESSES IT; IT PLAYS A SINGLE GOAT BLEAT.]

VERA: The sound effect is still funny.

SIO: The bit is over.

[VERA PLAYS THE CALL BACK FROM THE START. THE RECORDING INCLUDES NALA SAYING SHE IS AT WORK AND ASKING THEM TO STOP.]

KITO: We should not use this audio.

VERA: The reaction was not what I planned.

SIO: She did not consent to the prank.

[VERA DELETES THE AUDIO FILE. THE SCREEN ASKS, "ARE YOU SURE?" SHE PRESSES YES.] 

KITO: That is the clearest decision we have made today.

[VERA REACHES FOR A BUTTON MARKED "SURPRISED SILENCE." IT PLAYS THREE SECONDS OF ROOM TONE, THEN A RECORDED GASP.] 

SIO: That is not silence.

VERA: It is an effect labeled silence.

KITO: Delete that button too.

[VERA PRESSES DELETE. THE BUTTON VANISHES, TAKING THE GOAT BLEAT WITH IT.] 

[KITO CROSSES THE PRANK OFF THE RUN SHEET. HE PICKS UP THE PHONE TO CALL NALA BACK OFF AIR.]

KITO: I will apologize and ask if she wants to do the lamp interview later.

### Scene Three: The Follow-Up

[SFX: PHONE RINGS ONCE.]

NALA: Hello?

KITO: This is Kito from the station. We stopped the prank when you asked. We will not broadcast the call. I am sorry we interrupted your work.

NALA: Thank you. You can interview me about the lamp. It is for the reading corner at our hall.

KITO: Would you like to come on the program tomorrow?

NALA: Yes, for the interview.

SIO: We have written permission for that segment only.

VERA: Can I use the goat sound under the lamp discussion?

NALA: No.

[VERA DELETES THE GOAT BUTTON. IT MAKES ONE LAST BLEAT AS IT DISAPPEARS.]

SIO: The effect has been removed.

KITO: We can still interview Nala tomorrow.

[VERA PRINTS THE NEW RUN SHEET. IT SAYS "LAMP INTERVIEW - ASK BEFORE RECORDING." KITO READS IT ALOUD TO THE MICROPHONE, THEN CROSSES OUT THE PRANK SLOT.] 

SIO: No recorded reaction.

KITO: A real conversation instead.

[SIO TESTS THE PHONE LINE. NALA'S LINE IS NO LONGER PATCHED INTO THE LAUGH TRACK.]

SIO: The interview channel is separate.

VERA: And the prank channel is gone.

KITO: We will record only with consent.

SIO: The line is checked.

### Tag

[SFX: A REAL LAMP SWITCH CLICKS.]

KITO: Tomorrow, the reading-corner lamp interview.

SIO: No goat.

VERA: The soundboard is empty.
