# The Camera That Only Heard

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio film-club discussion, overconfident audio descriptions, and a silent movie scene whose sound effects are supplied by the wrong audience members*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Nemi, a gibbon film-club host; Rallo, a baboon projectionist; Vika, a macaque critic; and Peri, a capuchin audience member.*

[SFX: Projector hum, theater seats creak.]

NEMI: Welcome to Frame by Frame, our radio film-club. Tonight we screen a silent short called The Runaway Hat. Rallo, start the reel.

RALLO: It is turning. The opening card says, "A windy afternoon."

VIKA: I can hear no wind.

PERI: [BLOWS SOFTLY INTO MIC.]

NEMI: Thank you, Peri. The hat is now moving across the screen.

VIKA: The hat has not moved. The actor is looking at it.

RALLO: The actor is a macaque. She has lifted one eyebrow.

NEMI: That is a close-up. The audience should infer alarm.

PERI: [GASPS.]

VIKA: The gasp was not in the film.

NEMI: We are producing a radio version of a silent film. Some sound is necessary.

VIKA: Then tell us which sounds are from the reel and which are from the audience.

RALLO: The reel has a piano track, but the projector belt squeaks over every scene.

[SFX: Projector squeaks.]

NEMI: The hat rolls down the hill.

PERI: [ROLLS A METAL CUP ALONG THE FLOOR.]

VIKA: That is a cup.

PERI: It is the hat's journey.

RALLO: The actor is chasing the hat past a fountain.

NEMI: We hear splashing.

[SFX: A small splash.]

VIKA: The fountain is a bowl of water beside the projector.

RALLO: I use it to cool the machine.

PERI: I supplied that splash.

NEMI: The scene is working. The hat reaches the market.

VIKA: The card says "The market." There are no stalls.

RALLO: The stalls are offscreen.

VIKA: We are on the radio. Everything is offscreen.

NEMI: Our critic has found the central challenge of film-club broadcasting.

PERI: The hat lands on a pumpkin.

[SFX: A soft thump.]

VIKA: That was a cushion.

PERI: It is round like a pumpkin.

RALLO: The actor picks up the hat and places it on her own head.

NEMI: The chase is over.

VIKA: How do we know it was her hat?

RALLO: The final card says, "She had worn it all along."

PERI: Then whose hat was she chasing?

RALLO: The same hat, blown off and returned to her head.

NEMI: A complete circular journey.

VIKA: The plot has resolved, though the hat never left the screen.

[SFX: Projector stutters, then stops.]

RALLO: The reel is finished. We have another short, The Silent Door.

NEMI: Does that one have a plot?

RALLO: It has a door. The card says "A visitor arrives."

PERI: Should I knock?

VIKA: Not until the film does.

[SFX: Three knocks from the screen, followed by one from the theater.]

NEMI: That is a good transition. The visitor has arrived.

VIKA: The visitor is the projectionist. He is bringing the next reel.

RALLO: The door in the film remains closed.

PERI: Then this is a radio film club with two visitors.

NEMI: We will count the real one and describe the other.

VIKA: The real one is carrying a reel. The silent one is still a door.

RALLO: The reel is ready. Do we screen it?

NEMI: Not tonight. We have learned to label our sounds before adding them.

PERI: I will take the cup and water back to the kitchen.

VIKA: Leave the cushion.

[SFX: Cup stops rolling; projector hum fades.]

NEMI: This has been Frame by Frame. The hat has found its head, the door has not opened, and every sound has an owner.

VIKA: The program note says the silent hat was made of felt.

PERI: The felt hat should sound soft.

RALLO: There is no sound for felt.

NEMI: We can leave the audience a moment to imagine it.

[SFX: Long, quiet pause.]

VIKA: That is the most accurate sound in the film.

PERI: I will not add a hat sound.

RALLO: Then the reel is complete.

[SFX: A final card turns; the empty theater settles.]
