# Emergency at the Turntable

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night music request show and overproduced dedications*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: DJ FEN, a gibbon; PRODUCER LALA, a macaque; CALLER NESTOR, a capuchin; and an unseen record collector.

[SFX: MIDNIGHT JINGLE, NEEDLE DROP, THEN A RECORD SPINS TOO FAST.] 

FEN: You're listening to Moonlit Grooves, the only program that takes your dedications and gives them a spotlight, a slow zoom, and a twelve-piece string arrangement.

LALA: We have a request from Nestor, who wants a song for his neighbor's missing cup.

FEN: Missing? Then we need a minor key.

NESTOR: It's on the shelf by the radio.

FEN: Found objects deserve a triumphant return.

LALA: You can simply dedicate a song.

FEN: The audience expects emotional weather. Nestor, describe the cup.

NESTOR: Brown, with a chip on the rim. I use it for water.

FEN: A cup with a wound.

LALA: A chip.

FEN: We can build from that. Cue rain.

[SFX: A SPRINKLER TURNS ON IN THE STUDIO.]

LALA: That's the plant mister.

FEN: The atmosphere is here. Nestor, how long has it been missing?

NESTOR: It isn't missing. I said where it is.

FEN: How long has it been waiting?

NESTOR: Since I put it down.

FEN: The suspense is unbearable.

[HE PLAYS A RECORD. THE NEEDLE SKIPS ON A CRACK.] 

NESTOR: That is not my song.

FEN: It is the cup's memory, looping.

LALA: It's a scratch in the record.

FEN: We will use the scratch as percussion.

[SFX: NEEDLE SKIP, A HAND CLAPS IN TIME.]

NESTOR: I found the cup.

FEN: Don't reveal it before the chorus.

NESTOR: I am holding it.

FEN: We can hear the reunion in your voice.

LALA: He is standing next to the radio with a cup. There's no reunion.

FEN: Nestor, place the cup on the shelf and step back for the final note.

NESTOR: Why?

FEN: For the sound of closure.

[THE CUP IS SET DOWN. IT MAKES A SMALL, SOLID TAP.] 

FEN: Beautiful. Can we amplify the tap?

LALA: It is already the loudest part of this segment.

FEN: Then let's play the requested song without adding a storm.

[RECORD NEEDLE SETTLES. A WARM, SIMPLE INSTRUMENTAL PLAYS.] 

NESTOR: That's nice. I wanted to say thanks for keeping the night shift company.

LALA: That's a dedication.

FEN: No strings attached. No rain. One neighbor, one cup, one song.

[THE SONG PLAYS. THE SPRINKLER CLICKS BACK ON.] 

NESTOR: The plant's getting watered.

LALA: And the cup is on the shelf.

FEN: Tonight's emotional weather: lightly damp, with clear skies over the kitchen.


LALA: Fen, the collector on line three says the record belongs to him.

FEN: The cup record?

LALA: The record with the scratch.

COLLECTOR: It is a rare pressing. I lent it to the station years ago, and now your needle has made the same scratch in the same place.

FEN: A sonic signature.

LALA: A repair bill.

COLLECTOR: I want it returned before the next song.

FEN: We are in the middle of a dedication.

COLLECTOR: The dedication can finish on another record.

NESTOR: He should get his record back. The cup does not need a soundtrack.

FEN: But the scratch has become part of our arrangement.

LALA: Then you can reproduce it with a spoon and a blank disc.

FEN: That would be counterfeit percussion.

[THE COLLECTOR ENTERS. THE STUDIO DOOR CREAKS.] 

COLLECTOR: I can hear the scratch from the hallway.

FEN: Would you like to hear the chorus before you take it?

COLLECTOR: I would like to take it now.

LALA: The request is clear.

[FEN LIFTS THE NEEDLE. THE ROOM GOES QUIET.] 

NESTOR: The cup is still on the shelf.

COLLECTOR: Good. It can keep the place warm.

FEN: That is a lovely image.

COLLECTOR: It's a cup.

LALA: We can play Nestor's song from the station collection. No scratches, no weather, no dramatic return of the object.

FEN: I accept the challenge.

[RECORD SLEEVE SLIDES OPEN. A CLEAN NEEDLE DROP.] 

COLLECTOR: There. My record is safe.

FEN: And the cup?

NESTOR: Still holding water.

LALA: We have managed to end a segment with both objects in their proper places.

FEN: That is the kind of closure listeners can hold in one hand.
[SFX: JINGLE. THE RECORD SPINS TO A STOP. A CUP TAPS THE SHELF ONCE MORE.]
