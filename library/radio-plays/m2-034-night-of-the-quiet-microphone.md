# Night of the Quiet Microphone

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overnight radio request shows*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Ren, an insomniac macaque; Jo, a young capuchin board operator; caller Marn, a tired gibbon baker; caller Vela, a baboon crossing guard; and the station's janitor, Lou, a slow loris.

[SFX: LOW THEME MUSIC, SOFT ELECTRIC HUM.] 

REN: It is two minutes past midnight. This is The Quiet Mic, where you can request a song, tell us what kept you awake, or listen without saying a word. Jo is at the board. Jo, how quiet can we make a radio station?

JO: Quiet enough that I can hear you ask that question.

REN: Then we are close. First caller is Marn, baking bread at a bakery that starts work before dawn.

MARN, PHONE: The ovens are on, the dough is rising, and I can't sleep later than four. I came to request a song for the night shift.

REN: What kind of song?

MARN: One with no bells. My timer rings every twelve minutes.

[SFX: A SMALL TIMER DINGS IN DISTANCE.] 

REN: We will find a song without bells. How many loaves are you making?

MARN: Twenty. One is a test loaf. The crust split in the wrong place.

REN: There is a wrong place?

MARN: If it splits over the seam, it opens neatly. If it splits along the side, it leaks steam and dries out.

JO: My toast splits wherever it wants.

MARN: That's because it is toast.

[SFX: GENTLE LAUGHTER.] 

REN: Caller two, Vela is on the line from the crossing at First and Pine.

VELA, PHONE: I work the morning crossing. I'm waiting for the street crew to finish painting the lines. They promised to be here at midnight. It is now twelve-oh-eight.

REN: Did they call?

VELA: They said the truck is delayed. I am sitting inside the booth, which has a heater and a stack of old newspapers. I asked for a request, so play something for people who wait outside in the cold.

JO: I have a slow guitar track. No bells.

REN: Good. We will play it after we check on you. Do you have warm clothes and a way to call if the truck is delayed again?

VELA: Yes. I have a coat, a thermos, and the city number on my phone.

REN: Then stay inside the booth until the crew arrives. No one needs a fresh line of paint badly enough to stand in traffic.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CLICKS OFF. GUITAR MUSIC, SOFT.] 

REN: You are listening to a song made by three strings and a patient hand. The station janitor has appeared in the doorway. Lou, are you cleaning?

LOU: I'm changing the filter in the ventilation fan. It rattles when the studio gets warm.

JO: I thought that was our new percussion track.

LOU: It was a loose grille. I tightened it.

[SFX: HUM LOWERS, THEN STOPS.] 

REN: That is the quietest repair this station has ever had.

LOU: I have not finished. The vent above the booth still whistles.

REN: We can live with a whistle. We cannot live with you standing on a chair to reach it.

LOU: I brought the step stool.

JO: It has one short leg.

LOU: I brought a different step stool.

[SFX: STOOL SET DOWN, TESTED.] 

REN: It is stable. Please let the building crew inspect the vent tomorrow.

LOU: I will leave a note.

[SFX: PAPER AND PENCIL.] 

REN: We have a message from Marn. The test loaf split neatly. She says the song helped the timer feel slower.

JO: The timer didn't slow down.

REN: The night got more room around it. That counts.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.] 

REN: One more call. A listener says they cannot sleep because the apartment upstairs makes a soft thumping sound.

JO: Could be someone walking.

REN: Or a washing machine out of balance. We cannot know from here. If it sounds unsafe or continues, contact the building manager in daylight. For tonight, try earplugs or move to the room farthest from the noise. We do not want you climbing up to knock on a stranger's ceiling.

CALLER: I was thinking of leaving a note.

REN: A polite note in the morning is safer. We will put on something quiet while you write it.

[SFX: SOFT PIANO THEME.] 

REN: This is The Quiet Mic. If you are awake, you are not the only one. If you are tired, you can turn us down and rest. Jo will keep the levels low.

JO: I can do that.

[SFX: MUSIC FADES TO SOFT ROOM TONE.]
