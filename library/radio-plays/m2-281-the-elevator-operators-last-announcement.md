# The Elevator Operator's Last Announcement

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: elevator announcements, floor-name confusion, and hosts who describe routine stops as a guided tour*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST SERA, a gibbon
OPERATOR VEX, an orangutan
PASSENGER TAVI, a lemur
MECHANIC ODO, a capuchin

[SFX: ELEVATOR MOTOR, DOOR CHIME.]

SERA: We are broadcasting from the old town elevator, where Operator Vex has promised an announcement at every floor.

VEX: Ground floor. Please mind the step.

TAVI: There is no step.

VEX: It is a traditional warning.

ODO: The elevator is level.

[SFX: DOOR CHIME.]

VEX: First floor. Visitors for the reading room may exit to the left.

TAVI: The reading room is on the second floor.

VEX: The sign says first floor.

ODO: The building has two numbering systems.

SERA: We have reached the first conflict.

TAVI: Which system should I follow?

ODO: The floor buttons are numbered from the lobby. The rooms are numbered from the hillside entrance.

VEX: Second floor. Please exit to the left for the reading room.

TAVI: Now it is correct.

VEX: I am making progress.

[SFX: BUTTON PRESSED, MOTOR STARTS.]

The car stops between floors. The light stays on. Vex announces the third floor.

ODO: We are between one and two.

VEX: The announcement keeps people calm.

TAVI: It has made me less calm.

SERA: We are not moving.

VEX: Third floor. Please exit to the left.

ODO: There is no floor here.

VEX: Then please remain inside the elevator.

Tavi presses the alarm button. It rings a small bell in the lobby.

[SFX: BELL DINGS DISTANTLY.]

VEX: That is the fourth floor bell.

ODO: That is the alarm.

SERA: The broadcast has reached a new floor.

Odo checks the panel and finds a service switch left halfway between positions. He asks everyone to stand still while he resets it. Vex begins describing the maintenance process as a scenic interlude.

VEX: Observe the careful hands of our elevator mechanic.

ODO: Please stop narrating while I work.

TAVI: The elevator is still.

VEX: The stillness is part of the journey.

Odo flips the switch. The car descends to the lobby and opens level with the floor.

[SFX: MOTOR, CHIME, DOORS OPEN.]

VEX: Ground floor. Please mind the step.

TAVI: Still no step.

ODO: There is now a slight gap. Watch your feet.

Sera thanks the passengers for waiting. Vex reads the buttons aloud before closing the doors. The car moves one floor, stops correctly, and chimes once.

VEX: Second floor. Reading room to the right.

TAVI: We are still in the lobby.

ODO: The door has not opened.

VEX: Then the reading room will have to wait.

SERA: That is the first accurate announcement.
 
A passenger boards and asks for the reading room. Vex says "second floor," then waits for the door to open before continuing. The car rises smoothly and stops level.

[SFX: MOTOR, CHIME.]

VEX: Second floor. Reading room to the left.

PASSENGER: The sign says right.

TAVI: It is still backward.

ODO: We can turn the sign now that the car is stopped.

Vex asks the passenger to hold the door while Odo rotates the sign. The screw is loose, and the sign spins like a weather vane.

[SFX: SIGN SPINS, SCREW TAPS.]

VEX: The reading room is whichever way the sign points.

TAVI: That is not a direction.

ODO: It points left now.

The passenger exits. Vex records the announcement again, omitting scenic commentary and saying only the floor and room direction.


[SFX: DOOR CHIME, MOTOR HUM.]

Vex switches off the microphone. The elevator reaches the second floor. He turns the sign around; it has been mounted backward.

VEX: Second floor. Reading room to the left.

TAVI: We have arrived.

ODO: The sign is still backward.

SERA: Our tour has ended at the repair panel.

VEX: The elevator will resume service after the sign is turned around.

[SFX: SCREWDRIVER TURNS, DOOR CHIME.]
