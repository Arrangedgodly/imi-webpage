# The Theater Ghostlight Calls the Police

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night theater radio, ghost stories, and stage crews reporting a single safety lamp as a supernatural visitor*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Luma, a macaque; stage manager Odo, an orangutan; actor Rika, a gibbon; electrician Tavi, a baboon; dispatcher Sera, a capuchin.

SFX: Empty theater. One small lamp hums on the stage. A phone rings.

LUMA: We are live from the old Lantern Theater, where an emergency call reported a figure on stage after midnight. Stage manager Odo, do you see anything?

ODO: I see the ghostlight. It is the safety lamp we leave on when the theater is empty.

RIKA: I am in the seats. From here, the lamp makes a tall shadow on the curtain.

LUMA: The caller said the figure moved.

TAVI: The ceiling fan shifted the curtain. The shadow moved with it.

SERA: Dispatch received a call saying someone was trespassing. We are checking the caller's location before sending an officer into the locked building.

ODO: The side door is locked. I have the key. We finished rehearsal at ten and set the ghostlight at center stage.

LUMA: Why is it called a ghostlight?

RIKA: It stays on so nobody walks into the orchestra pit. The name is traditional.

LUMA: The theater may have summoned its own suspect.

TAVI: The lamp is on a floor stand. Its cable runs to the wall. If the curtain crosses it, the lamp's shadow can look like a person.

SFX: Curtain rustles; stand taps lightly.

RIKA: The figure moved again.

TAVI: The stand is stable. The curtain brushed the cable, not the lamp.

SERA: I have the caller. It was a neighbor across the square. They saw a silhouette through the lobby glass and heard a bump.

ODO: The bump was the internal stage-service door latch. That door swings shut after the fan cycles; the exterior side door remains locked.

LUMA: Could we check the window without entering the stage?

ODO: I can unlock the lobby. Tavi will stay by the breaker. Rika, please wait in the seating aisle.

SFX: Key turns; lobby door opens; footsteps echo.

SERA: Dispatcher confirms an officer is nearby but holding outside until you verify there is a person inside.

ODO: I am at the glass. The reflection shows the ghostlight and the empty stage. No figure.

RIKA: I can hear something behind the curtain.

TAVI: That is the stage fan. I will switch it off from the wall panel.

SFX: Fan winds down; curtain settles.

RIKA: The tall figure has gone. Now I can see the shadow belongs to the lamp stand.

LUMA: The stage is now empty.

SERA: I am updating the call: no intruder seen, safety lamp confirmed, curtain movement caused the shadow.

ODO: I will secure the fan switch and check the internal stage-service door latch. It should not knock against the frame.

SFX: A screwdriver turns; latch closes softly.

TAVI: The latch is adjusted. The ghostlight stays on. We do not turn off the safety lamp to improve the photograph.

LUMA: The theater's mystery is solved, but our broadcast needs an ending.

RIKA: I can perform the ghost's line.

ODO: There is no ghost.

RIKA: I can perform the neighbor's line, then. "I saw someone in the window." The ghostlight replied, "You saw me doing my job."

LUMA: That is a very responsible ghost.

SERA: The officer has arrived. They will remain outside while I close the report. No entry is needed.

TAVI: The lobby door is locked again. The theater crew is inside, and the public is out.

ODO: The lamp has a fresh bulb and a safe cable path. It can stay lit overnight.

SERA: Dispatch has logged the caller report and our verified explanation. The case is closed; no officer needs to enter.

LUMA: We will sign off from the empty seats, with the safety lamp visible and the side door locked.

SFX: Phone receiver settles; one lamp hums steadily.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The ghostlight remains on, the curtain is still, and dispatch has closed the call without entering the theater.
