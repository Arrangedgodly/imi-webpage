# The Space Station Simulation Needs a Mop

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: space mission broadcasts that turn ordinary mishaps into cosmic emergencies*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Commander Mara, a macaque; engineer Odo, a gibbon; pilot Pip, a capuchin; director Vex, an orangutan; mission voice Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Training room. Fan hum. Velcro patches peel.

VEX: We are live from the simulated space station. A leak has been detected.

PIP: It's a water bottle with a loose cap.

MARA: The bottle tipped when you unbuckled it.

ODO: The floor mat is damp. The console is dry.

VEX: Our listeners need to feel the danger.

NARI: Mission control, do you copy?

MARA: We copy. The crew is containing a small spill.

VEX: Say, "We have a breach."

PIP: We do not.

ODO: The mop is in the equipment cabinet.

VEX: No one ever hears about the mop in a space drama.

MARA: Then they can hear us use it.

SFX: Cabinet latch clicks. Mop handle taps the wall.

PIP: The cabinet is locked.

ODO: I have the key on my belt.

SFX: Key ring jingles. Lock turns.

VEX: We are losing pressure.

NARI: The cabin pressure is stable.

VEX: Add a warning tone.

SFX: Artificial alarm beeps.

MARA: Turn that off. I need to hear the crew.

ODO: The mop head is dry. I'll dampen it at the sink.

SFX: Water runs briefly. Mop wrings.

PIP: I can hold the bottle upright.

MARA: Keep the cap on. Odo, clean from the edge inward so we don't spread water toward the console.

VEX: Mission control, initiate emergency procedure.

NARI: Which procedure?

VEX: The one where the crew floats away from danger.

ODO: No one is wearing a harness, and this room has gravity.

PIP: I can get the spill kit instead.

SFX: Cabinet opens; plastic bag crackles.

MARA: The kit has absorbent pads. Put one around the damp edge, not on the console.

VEX: We need the crew to report their emotional state.

PIP: I feel annoyed that someone set off an alarm over a bottle cap.

NARI: That is a clear report.

ODO: The pad is wet. The floor mat is contained.

MARA: We should check the console legs and cable run before we move the mat.

SFX: Mara taps the console frame. A switch clicks off.

MARA: Power to the floor strip is isolated. The cable is dry.

VEX: Could we keep the alarm on while you inspect?

MARA: No. It masks the actual equipment.

SFX: Alarm stops.

PIP: I can hear the fan and the radio again.

ODO: The console is safe. I'll mop the remaining water.

VEX: This is a very small crisis.

MARA: Then we have a very small success. The important thing is that nobody makes the spill bigger to improve the broadcast.

VEX: We have a coordinated response.

NARI: Mission control confirms the alarm was a training cue.

MARA: It was a bad cue. No leak, no pressure loss.

VEX: The audience expects a countdown.

PIP: Count down to the floor being dry.

ODO: The mat is clean. I'll hang it over the rack.

SFX: Mop slides across the mat. Water drips into a bucket.

MARA: Bottle is capped and secured. Console is dry.

PIP: The alarm log still says "hull breach."

ODO: I'll correct it to "bottle spill" and keep the original entry for the training record.

VEX: That phrase will not fit the dramatic title.

MARA: The training record is for the next crew, not the poster.

SFX: Pen scratches across a clipboard.

NARI: Mission control confirms the room is ready for its next exercise.

PIP: Can the next exercise be a quiet one?

NARI: Training log: one loose cap, one spill, one successful cleanup.

VEX: The episode could be called "The Great Vacuum."

MARA: The mop did the work. Call it "The Crew Cleans Up."

PIP: The bottle stays in the cup holder this time.

TAG

SFX: Velcro straps close.

VEX: Is the station ready for launch?

ODO: The training room is ready for lunch.
