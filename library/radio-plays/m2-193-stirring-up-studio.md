# Stirring Up a Studio

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio cooking shows that rely on sound effects and leave listeners guessing about the actual recipe*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST LENA, a macaque
COOK MARI, a gorilla
PRODUCER FIP, a capuchin
CALLER ODO, a langur
CALLER KET, a squirrel

[SFX: KITCHEN AMBIENCE. A PAN SIZZLES TOO LOUDLY.] 

LENA: Welcome to "Dinner by Ear." Mari is making a lentil stew, and Fip has brought us a sound-effects library.

FIP: I have a dramatic chop, a sizzling fire, and a thunderclap.

MARI: I have lentils, onions, and a pot.

[THE THUNDERCLAP PLAYS. THE PAN SIZZLES.]

LENA: The stew has encountered a storm.

MARI: The stove is on medium.

### Scene One: The Ingredients

[MARI SETS INGREDIENTS ON THE TABLE.]

MARI: First, rinse the lentils. Then soften the onions in oil.

FIP: Can I add a chopping sound?

MARI: The onions are already chopped.

[FIP PLAYS A LOUD CHOP. A SPOON FALLS OFF THE TABLE.] 

ODO: I am listening from home. I cannot tell what went into the pot.

LENA: Mari, can you describe the ingredients as they go in?

MARI: Onions, lentils, water, and a pinch of salt.

FIP: We need a secret ingredient.

MARI: There is no secret ingredient.

[FIP PLAYS A MYSTERIOUS CREAK. MARI LIFTS THE POT LID; STEAM RISES WITH A SOFT HISS.]

LENA: The pot has revealed the stew.

MARI: It has simmered for ten minutes.

ODO: I can hear mine bubbling.

FIP: I have a bubble sound effect.

MARI: Use the actual pot.

[FIP MOVES HIS MICROPHONE CLOSER TO THE POT, THEN PULLS IT BACK WHEN A DROP OF STEAM CONDENSES ON THE GRILL.]

### Scene Two: The Timer

[SFX: POT LID CLINKS.]

LENA: The lid goes on while the stew simmers.

FIP: I can play the sound of a clock ticking.

MARI: A timer is already running.

[FIP CUES A DRAMATIC TICK. IT SPEEDS UP.]

ODO: Is it supposed to boil faster?

MARI: No. It is a sound effect.

[THE KITCHEN TIMER RINGS.]

LENA: The stew is ready.

FIP: We did not build to a climax.

MARI: The lentils are tender.

[LENA ASKS MARI TO DESCRIBE THE TEXTURE. SHE STIRS THE POT; THE SPOON MAKES A SOFT, THICK SOUND.]

MARI: The lentils are soft, and the broth is still loose.

ODO: Should I add more water?

MARI: If you want a thinner stew. Taste it first.

[ODO TASTES HIS BOWL AT HOME. HE CALLS BACK WITH A SPOON STILL IN HIS HAND.]

ODO: It is good. I added a little lemon.

FIP: A surprise ending.

MARI: A choice after tasting.

[FIP ASKS MARI TO NAME A SECOND RECIPE FOR TOMORROW. SHE LOOKS AT THE INGREDIENT BOARD; IT IS BLANK.] 

MARI: We can ask listeners what they want to cook.

LENA: One caller has a question.

KET: Can I replace the lentils with split peas?

MARI: Yes, but they may need a different cooking time.

[FIP REACHES FOR THE TIMER SOUND. LENA SETS THE TIMER BESIDE THE POT INSTEAD.] 

[ODO CALLS BACK.]

ODO: I made it at home while you spoke. It took the same amount of time.

### Scene Three: Taste Test

[MARI LADLES A SMALL BOWL. FIP PLAYS A CROWD CHEER.]

LENA: How does it taste?

MARI: Warm, savory, and soft.

ODO: Mine is ready too.

FIP: Can we have a reveal?

MARI: We can have dinner.

[LENA TAKES A BITE. THE CHEER TRACK CONTINUES IN THE BACKGROUND. SHE TURNS IT OFF.] 

LENA: That is good stew.

FIP: No one gasped.

ODO: I am eating.

[MARI GIVES THE RECIPE IN CLEAR MEASURES. FIP PUTS AWAY THE THUNDER BUTTON AND TURNS THE POT MIC DOWN.] 

### Tag

[SFX: SOFT SPOON ON BOWL.]

LENA: Dinner by ear has reached the table.

FIP: The spoon is our applause.

MARI: Please eat before it gets cold.
