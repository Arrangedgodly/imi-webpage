# The Forecast Is Mostly Acorns

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in gardening radio conventions, where listeners expect certainty about plants and the host can only hear acorns*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host OMI, macaque; botanist NARA, lemur; caller PAVA, gibbon; producer KES, capuchin.

[SFX: GARDEN BIRDS, STUDIO THEME.] 

OMI: Welcome to "Grow What You Know." Today's forecast is mostly acorns.

NARA: That means the oak is dropping them.

OMI: We have callers asking when the acorns will stop.

NARA: When the tree is finished.

PAVA: My garden is covered in acorns. Is the oak sick?

NARA: It is autumn.

PAVA: Could I rake them into a pile?

NARA: Yes, if you want a pile.

OMI: That is not a very satisfying forecast.

NARA: It is accurate.

Kes slides a tray of acorns toward the microphone. One rolls under the console.

[SFX: ACORN ROLLING, SMALL TAP.] 

OMI: We have lost one acorn to the studio.

NARA: It is not lost. It is under the desk.

OMI: It has left our forecast.

Kes reaches under the console and cannot find it. Omi announces an acorn watch.

OMI: Listeners, remain calm. The acorn is stationary.

NARA: Unless it rolls.

Pava calls back. She asks whether she should plant every acorn. Nara explains that some are eaten, some sprout, and some dry out.

OMI: Can we predict which ones will sprout?

NARA: Not by radio.

OMI: What if they make a sound?

NARA: An acorn does not tell you its plans.

Kes hears a faint tapping under the desk. Omi asks Nara to interpret it.

NARA: That is Kes hitting the console with her knee.

KES: I am trying to reach the acorn.

OMI: The acorn has a knee?

KES: The knee is mine.

Omi invites callers to send in acorn sounds.

Caller Eli says his acorns make a hollow knock when they hit the shed roof. Nara asks if the shed has a roof of its own. Eli says yes, but it is also mostly acorns now.

OMI: Is this a storm?

NARA: No. It is a tree.

OMI: The program's forecast map has one symbol for storms.

KES: Use the acorn symbol.

Omi draws a circle with three dots. The marker squeaks, sounding like a small bird. Nara says that means nothing. Omi adds a caption: "possible bird."

Pava calls again to report that she has raked the path. An acorn rolls back across it, stopping against her boot.

PAVA: I have one more.

NARA: The tree has not finished. Pava taps one with a spoon. The microphone catches a sharp click. Nara says that is a hard acorn. Omi declares the forecast "dry with a chance of tapping."

PAVA: Should I cover my seedlings?

NARA: If they are delicate, yes. But not because the acorns are predicting rain.

OMI: The program has no weather instrument.

KES: We have a window.

They open it. A breeze blows leaves and acorns through the studio. One lands inside the tray; another rolls under the desk.

[SFX: WIND, ACORNS SCATTERING.] 

OMI: The forecast has increased.

NARA: The tree is outside.

PAVA: My garden is outside too.

NARA: Then your garden is part of the forecast.

Omi asks listeners to sweep the path if acorns make it slippery. Nara approves. Kes finally finds the first acorn in her sleeve.

KES: It must have rolled from the microphone stand into my sleeve.

OMI: That is not under the desk.

NARA: It moved.

OMI: We have a developing situation.

[SFX: ONE ACORN DROPS INTO TRAY.]

OMI: The forecast calls for acorns until the tree is done.

PAVA: Should we put a basket beneath the tree?

NARA: If you want to collect them.

OMI: And if the basket fills?

NARA: Use another basket.

OMI: We have a second wave of preparation.

NARA: It is just a second basket.

KES: And then?

OMI: A chance of leaves.
