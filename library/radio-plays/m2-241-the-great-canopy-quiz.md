# The Great Canopy Quiz

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live call-in quiz shows, suspenseful answer reveals, sponsor plugs, and contestants who identify every sound as food*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST NELL, a capuchin
BONGO, a macaque contestant
TAVI, a gibbon contestant
PRODUCER RUE, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: STUDIO THEME. APPLAUSE SIGN CLICKS. TWO AUDIENCE MEMBERS CLAP.]

NELL: Welcome to The Great Canopy Quiz, the show where one correct answer wins a year of free tree shade.

BONGO: Is the shade portable?

NELL: The prize committee will answer after the show.

RUE: We are live in three, two-

NELL: Our first sound is behind the curtain. Contestants, name it. [SFX: THREE QUICK TAPS, THEN A LONG RUMBLE.]

BONGO: A drum rolling down a hill.

TAVI: A sleeping stomach.

NELL: Both answers are dramatic. The correct answer is a rolling trash barrel.

[SFX: BARREL RUMBLES INTO STUDIO. AUDIENCE GASPS.]

RUE: It has crossed the curtain.

NELL: Contestants, do not touch the equipment.

BONGO: I thought we were allowed to touch the answer.

TAVI: I think the answer is still rolling.

[SFX: BARREL STOPS AGAINST HOST DESK.]

NELL: One point to Bongo for a plausible instrument. The actual answer was a barrel. Our sponsor, Barkside Water, invites you to drink from a cup that does not roll away.

RUE: We had a rolling cup in the first rehearsal.

NELL: We don't mention the first rehearsal.

[SFX: THEME STING.]

NELL: Round two. Identify this sound. [SFX: HIGH CHIRP, SCRATCHING, THEN A SOFT THUMP.]

TAVI: A bird landing on a branch.

BONGO: A pear falling into a basket.

NELL: The sound is actually a bird landing on a branch.

BONGO: I said basket because the bird sounded hungry.

TAVI: Birds do not usually land inside baskets.

BONGO: This one might. It's a quiz.

[SFX: WING FLUTTER. SMALL BIRD CHIRPS.]

RUE: The bird is on the prize chair.

NELL: There is no bird in our prize chair.

RUE: There is now.

NELL: We will award a second point to Tavi and ask our feathered guest not to take home the shade.

[SFX: APPLAUSE SIGN CLICKS. AUDIENCE CLAPS.]

NELL: Final round. Contestants must identify a sound and perform it. [SFX: A WOODEN CLACK, A METAL RING, THEN TWO SOFT FOOTSTEPS.]

BONGO: A spoon hitting a pot, followed by two people leaving for lunch.

TAVI: A gate latch, a bell, and someone walking away.

NELL: The answer is our studio door closing. For the performance bonus, please perform the sound.

BONGO: [IMITATING] Clack, ring, footsteps.

TAVI: [IMITATING] Clack, ring, footsteps.

[SFX: DOOR CLACK, BELL RINGS, TWO FOOTSTEPS APPROACH.]

NELL: Someone just came in.

RUE: The sound effect player has returned.

NELL: It is the actual door this time.

BONGO: Is that a bonus point?

NELL: It is a bonus person.

RUE: We need to close the door before the bird leaves with the prize chair.

[SFX: WING FLUTTER. CHAIR SCRAPES.]

NELL: The final score is one point for Bongo's plausible answer, two points for Tavi's correct bird, and zero points for moving the furniture.
BONGO: So Tavi wins the shade?
NELL: Correct. The bird was not entered in the contest.

[SFX: AUDIENCE APPLAUSE. BIRD CHIRPS. FLOOR MANAGER ROLLS A SMALL UMBRELLA INTO THE STUDIO.]

RUE: The prize committee has brought Tavi's year of shade. It is a small umbrella.
TAVI: A year is a long time to hold this.
BONGO: The bird can sit under it while you rest your arm.

[SFX: TAVI OPENS UMBRELLA. BIRD HOPS ONTO PRIZE CHAIR UNDER ITS EDGE. CHAIR SCRAPES.]

NELL: Tavi has won the prize, and the bird has won the chair.
RUE: We do not award chairs.
NELL: We do now. The audience may enter the shade queue in alphabetical order.

BONGO: Does the queue have a sound?

NELL: We will record it next week.

NELL: That's our show. Join us next week for the sound of a quiet room, assuming we can record one.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC. BARREL RUMBLES FAINTLY IN DISTANCE.]
