# One Minute of Nothing But Pebbles

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: ASMR and relaxation podcasts, exaggerated close-microphone textures, and hosts who monetize silence*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A relaxation host promises listeners one uninterrupted minute of pebbles, but the recording studio cannot stop labeling, counting, and enhancing them.

Cast: Host Eri, a macaque; sound artist Piri, a gibbon; producer Toma, an orangutan; listener Neko, a capuchin; sponsor voice Vali, a baboon.

[SFX: A SINGLE PEBBLE ROLLS ACROSS A WOODEN TRAY.]

ERI: Settle in. This episode offers one minute of pebbles, without commentary.

TOMA: That was the test roll. The sponsored minute starts after the ad.

PIRI: You put the microphone two centimeters from the tray. It sounded like a boulder crossing a canyon.

VALI: Pebble Calm is a premium meditation service. For the price of a small stone, receive a large inner landscape.

ERI: Can the sponsor read after the minute?

TOMA: It is the sponsor's minute.

ACT ONE

Piri arranges three smooth pebbles on felt. Eri asks whether to describe their color. Piri says the program promised sound, not a museum tour. Toma asks for a scrape. The microphone catches a rough crunch.

PIRI: They are smooth.

TOMA: The recording sounds sharp.

PIRI: The gain is too high.

Eri lowers the gain. Vali asks them to add a bell at the start so listeners know the minute has begun. Toma rings a service bell. Its echo consumes the first six seconds.

ERI: The stones have not started.

TOMA: The bell was an introduction.

PIRI: The introduction is longer than the pebble.

Neko calls to say the last episode's rain track woke him because an engineer added thunder. Eri promises no storm this time. Vali offers a pebble-shaped sleep timer. Piri asks if they are recording stones or selling a clock.

ACT TWO

Toma begins the recording. Pebble one rolls three inches. Pebble two clicks against it. Pebble three stays still. Eri whispers, "Notice the space between sounds." A cough from the producer's booth cuts across the quiet. Toma asks for another take. The cough happens again, then the sponsor's ad plays from a monitor that was supposed to be muted.

VALI: Let the advertisement breathe.

PIRI: It is breathing through the talkback speaker.

Toma tapes a note over the monitor: SILENT DURING RECORD. Eri asks for sixty quiet seconds. They try again. Piri wraps her tapping tail in a folded cloth. The first pebble rolls; the second clicks against it; the third stays still.

[SFX: SILENCE. A LIGHT, UNEVEN ROLL. ONE CLICK.]

The minute ends. Toma plays it back. The first failed test lasted sixty seconds, but its six-second bell cue left only fifty-four seconds of pebble sound. Eri says the next take will time only the stones. Toma says the recording clock begins at the bell. Piri says the bell was an introduction, not a pebble.

ACT TWO-B

Neko calls again before the final take. He heard the test roll and thought the episode had started. Eri explains that the first attempt included a bell and lost six seconds of pebble sound. Neko says he would rather hear a clear beginning than an extra sound pretending to be calm.

ERI: For the final take, the minute starts with the first stone moving.

PIRI: No bell, no voice, no sponsor, and the caller line is closed.

TOMA: I will start the timer on that first roll and stop it at sixty.

Piri checks the talkback channel, then mutes it. Vali puts the sponsor card facedown. Eri gives a hand signal to the producer but does not speak into the microphone. Toma nods and starts recording.

[SFX: EXACTLY 60 SECONDS OF PEBBLES: ONE ROLLS, ANOTHER CLICKS ONCE, THE THIRD REMAINS STILL. NO VOICES OR OTHER CUES.]

The timer stops at sixty. Piri raises one finger to confirm the file is complete. Toma checks the duration: one full minute of pebble sounds, with no bell or cut-in. Eri opens the caller line only after the recording light is off. Neko says the minute sounded like a small, changing room rather than silence. Eri thanks him and ends the call before the next track is prepared.
ACT THREE

Eri introduces the recorded minute and says it begins with a pebble rolling. The studio plays the full take. Nobody speaks over it. Afterward, Neko calls back and says it did not put him to sleep, but he noticed how quiet his room was. Piri says that is a valid response, not a result they can promise. Vali offers a discount on the sleep timer but waits until after the segment.

Toma asks if they should release the first test roll as an extra. Eri says only if labeled as a test. Piri spaces the stones on the felt so none rolls accidentally. The room is quiet.

TAG

TOMA: That was not a new episode.

ERI: It was one stone.
