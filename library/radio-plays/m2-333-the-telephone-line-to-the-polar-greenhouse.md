# The Telephone Line to the Polar Greenhouse

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: remote customer hotlines that rely on cheerful scripts instead of understanding the caller's conditions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Operator Luma, a macaque; greenhouse keeper Reni, a capuchin; supervisor Vex, a gibbon; field engineer Niko, a lemur.

[SFX: WIND HOWLS. A TELEPHONE RINGS THROUGH STATIC.]

LUMA: Thank you for calling Greenhouse Support. How may I brighten your day?

RENI: The greenhouse is dark. The daylight shutter is stuck.

LUMA: I am sorry to hear that. Have you tried opening the daylight shutter?

RENI: The crank is outside, and the door is frozen shut.

LUMA: Is the greenhouse door open?

RENI: It is not open.

LUMA: Thank you for confirming. Please remain cheerful while I check our guide.

[SFX: HOLD MUSIC, A TINNY UKULELE LOOP. WIND UNDER IT.]

RENI: The seedlings are leaning toward the emergency lamp.

LUMA: Our guide says plants respond to positive language.

RENI: They are plants. They are responding to light.

VEX: Luma, use the approved script.

LUMA: The approved script says to ask whether the caller has tried the handle.

RENI: I tried the handle. It is frozen.

LUMA: Have you tried asking the handle to thaw?

RENI: I would rather not insult it.

[SFX: THE LINE CRACKLES. A SECOND CLICK, THEN NIKO'S VOICE JOINS.]

NIKO: This is Niko in the field truck. I can reach the outside crank if someone opens the service hatch.

RENI: The hatch is inside the greenhouse.

LUMA: Is the hatch open?

NIKO: I am not in the greenhouse.

VEX: We need to classify the request before dispatching.

RENI: Classify it as cold.

LUMA: I have options for cloudy, overcast, and emotionally gray.

RENI: The shutter is jammed. The motor clicks, then stops.

[SFX: THREE CLICKS OVER THE PHONE, THEN SILENCE.]

NIKO: I heard the motor. The crank chain may have slipped. Reni, can you reach the manual latch from the inside?

RENI: I can reach it, but the storage crate is in front of the hatch.

LUMA: Please move the storage crate to a safe location.

RENI: I have one free hand and a phone.

VEX: This is outside our script.

LUMA: Supervisor, could you hold the line? Niko, can you guide her by sound?

NIKO: Reni, tap the hatch twice if you can reach it.

[SFX: TWO KNOCKS, THEN A CRATE SCRAPES ACROSS THE FLOOR.]

NIKO: Good. Now pull the latch down. Do not turn the crank yet.

[SFX: METAL LATCH CLICKS. WIND WHISTLES THROUGH A NARROW GAP.]

RENI: It opened a little.

NIKO: I can turn the crank from here. Tell me when the shutter moves.

[SFX: CRANK TURNS OUTSIDE. A HEAVY SHUTTER SLIDES UP; WIND FADES.]

RENI: Light is coming in. The seedlings are facing the window.

LUMA: I am glad the day has improved.

VEX: We did not follow the script.

LUMA: We did. We asked what happened, listened, and found someone who could reach the crank.

NIKO: The chain was not broken. It was caught on a loose guide.

RENI: I'll secure it when the temperature rises.

VEX: If the shutter is open now, the call is resolved. We should close the case.

RENI: The chain still catches when the wind pulls it. I need the field engineer to show me the loose guide.

LUMA: Niko, can you stay on the line until Reni marks the guide?

NIKO: Yes. I will tap the crank once when it is seated.

[SFX: METAL TOOL SCRAPES, THEN A SMALL TAP. THE SHUTTER RATTLES BUT REMAINS OPEN.]

RENI: I see it. The guide is bent, not broken. I can keep the shutter open until the repair team arrives.

VEX: That requires another service ticket.

LUMA: Create one, and attach it to this call.

VEX: The system only lets me close a call.

LUMA: Then leave it open.

VEX: There is no button for that.

RENI: The light is already in. You can keep the ticket pending.

[SFX: LUMA ENDS THE CALL. THE UKULELE HOLD MUSIC PLAYS FOR ONE EXTRA BEAT, THEN STOPS.]

VEX: Should we revise the script?

LUMA: Yes. Remove the question about asking a handle.

[SFX: A PENCIL CROSSES A LINE OFF A PAPER FORM. WIND STOPS; THE GREENHOUSE FAN STARTS.]
