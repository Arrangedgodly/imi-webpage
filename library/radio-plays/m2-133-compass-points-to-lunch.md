# The Compass Points to Lunch

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio orienteering coverage, overconfident navigation jargon, and a route marker relocated to keep shade over the picnic table*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Deni, a macaque event host; Roro, a gibbon navigator; Sef, an orangutan marshal; and Nika, a capuchin competitor.*

[SFX: Forest birds, map unfolds, small bell.]

DENI: We are live at the Canopy Orienteering Rally. Competitors use a compass and map to find three markers, then return to the start. Roro, what is the first bearing?

RORO: North by northeast, past the stump, toward the blue flag.

SEF: The blue flag has moved. It is now over the picnic table.

DENI: Why?

SEF: The table needed shade.

NIKA: My compass points to the flag.

RORO: Your compass points north.

NIKA: The flag is north.

SEF: The table is north. The flag is tied to the table.

[SFX: Bell rings; competitors start running.]

DENI: Nika takes the trail at speed. Roro calls a steady bearing. The other competitors head toward the stump.

RORO: Stop at the fork and check the map.

NIKA: I am at the fork.

RORO: Which fork?

NIKA: The one with three paths.

RORO: That's a fork in the trail. Don't choose the utensil.

NIKA: I found an actual fork on the ground.

SEF: It marks the lunch area.

DENI: The picnic table has now become a navigation point, a shade shelter, and a lunch marker.

RORO: The first checkpoint is the old cedar. Nika, read the symbol.

NIKA: A triangle.

RORO: That means the tree.

NIKA: There are five triangle-shaped trees.

SEF: Only one has a ribbon.

NIKA: The ribbon is around the table.

SEF: The table is near the tree.

RORO: The map marks tree and table separately.

DENI: We have a competitor circling the picnic area.

NIKA: I found the first marker under the cedar.

[SFX: Marker flag snaps open.]

RORO: Good. Take the bearing to the creek.

NIKA: My compass points toward the table again.

RORO: The table is not magnetic.

NIKA: The fork is.

SEF: That is a metal fork, not a compass.

DENI: The second marker is at the creek, and the competitor is carrying cutlery.

NIKA: I can use the fork as a pointer.

RORO: Put it down. Follow the compass needle.

[SFX: Light footsteps; creek burbles.]

NIKA: I found the creek marker. The final checkpoint is the bell.

SEF: The bell is by the start.

RORO: The route loops around the ridge before returning.

DENI: Nika has the last marker. She is taking the eastern trail, avoiding the table and fork.

NIKA: I can hear the bell.

RORO: That's the lunch bell.

SEF: The checkpoint bell is a smaller one.

NIKA: They sound the same.

DENI: The marshal is ringing both bells to distinguish them.

[SFX: Two bells; one sharp, one hollow.]

NIKA: I have the sharp bell. I am returning to the start.

RORO: Use the path beside the cedar.

NIKA: The table is beside the cedar.

SEF: Everything is beside the table.

DENI: Nika crosses the finish line with all three markers and no picnic equipment.

RORO: The route has been completed correctly.

SEF: We will move the table after lunch and update the map.

NIKA: Please leave the shade.

[SFX: The lunch bell rings; people settle by the table.]

DENI: This has been the Canopy Orienteering Rally. The compass pointed north, the fork pointed nowhere, and lunch stayed in the shade.

RORO: Nika's compass now points east, but the finish bell is north.

NIKA: The compass needle is stuck against the metal marker flag.

SEF: We should not carry the marker. I will move the bell to the finish line.

DENI: The marshal moves the bell; the compass settles. Nika follows the trail and crosses the line without the lunch fork.

NIKA: I brought all three markers and left the table in the shade.

RORO: That is the correct route.

[SFX: Map refolds; birds continue.]
