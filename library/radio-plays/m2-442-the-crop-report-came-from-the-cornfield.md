# The Crop Report Came from the Cornfield

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: rural call-in radio, urgent crop reports, and studio hosts who narrate field work as if it were a championship*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A farm reporter tries to deliver a simple crop update while the studio keeps turning ordinary corn inspection into a dramatic contest.

Cast: Host Omi, a macaque; field reporter Pavo, a baboon; grower Neri, an orangutan; caller Leko, a gibbon; sound operator Kavi, a capuchin.

[SFX: CORN LEAVES RUSTLING. A HAND CRACKS AN EAR OF CORN.]

OMI: The valley crop report. Our field reporter has found a mystery in the cornfield.

PAVO: I found a dry leaf.

OMI: A clue.

NERI: It is a leaf. We are checking whether the ears are ready.

ACT ONE

Pavo reports from Neri's field. The crop is at the stage where growers check the kernels before harvest. Neri opens one ear and presses a kernel; it is still milky. Omi asks for a dramatic countdown. Kavi plays a drum roll. Neri asks him to stop because she needs to hear the irrigation pump.

NERI: The pump is running, but the west row is dry.

PAVO: We can hear water on the east side.

OMI: A race between the rows.

NERI: There is no race. The valve is partly closed.

Neri walks to the pipe and checks the handle. It is turned halfway. She opens it to the marked line; water flows toward the west row. Pavo asks if the crop is saved. Neri says it was never in danger; she noticed the uneven flow during the morning check.

ACT TWO

Omi invites a listener to guess the crop's harvest date. Leko calls from the cooperative shed. He says the field needs another week, depending on weather. Omi asks him to pick a number for a prize. Leko says the prize is not the point; a date depends on the crop and conditions.

LEKO: The kernels are not ready today.

OMI: Can I say "one week" with a cymbal?

LEKO: You can say it without a cymbal.

Kavi tries a soft cymbal. The sound overlaps Neri's radio, making the irrigation pump seem to surge. Neri signals for the sound effect to stop. She checks the valve again and listens for water at the end of the row. It arrives steadily. Pavo asks whether the audience can hear it.

PAVO: We have a quiet trickle.

NERI: The water is reaching the crop. That is what matters.

Omi asks for a yield estimate. Neri says they have not harvested, so she cannot give a final number. She can report the row count and kernel check. Omi asks if the show can announce a record crop anyway. Neri says a record can be announced after weighing, not before.

ACT TWO-B

Pavo walks with Neri to the far end of the west row. The field microphone picks up dry leaves brushing the cable. Kavi asks them to pause while he secures the line along the path. The rustling stops. Neri checks the plants; the leaves are dry at the edges but the ears are developing normally.

OMI: Is that a warning sign?

NERI: It is a reason to keep checking. The soil is moist now.

LEKO: I can send someone to look tomorrow morning.

NERI: I will check at sunrise. No extra visit is needed tonight.

Omi asks listeners to send in their own crop forecasts. Kavi mutes the open line so no one can shout a harvest date over Neri. Pavo asks how she decides when to harvest. Neri says she checks the kernels, weather, and storage space. She demonstrates the milk test on one ear, then places it in a basket rather than throwing it away.

PAVO: No dramatic reveal.

NERI: The kernel is still a kernel.

ACT THREE

Omi gives the report: the west valve was partly closed and has been corrected; the water is now reaching the row; the crop needs more time before harvest. No yield record has been claimed. Leko confirms the cooperative can help with the next inspection if the field conditions change. Neri says she will check again in the morning.

Kavi removes the drum roll and leaves the field sound on. The irrigation runs evenly. Pavo signs off from the path, not the middle of the crop. Omi thanks the growers for the update. She asks the audience to listen for the pump, not a victory sound.

TAG

[SFX: WATER FLOWS STEADILY THROUGH THE PIPE.]

OMI: No winner announced.

NERI: Just a row getting watered.

FINAL IMAGE: The open valve stays at its marked line while the corn leaves rustle in the evening breeze.
