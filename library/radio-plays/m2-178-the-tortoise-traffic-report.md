# The Tortoise Traffic Report

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: commuter traffic updates, helicopter-style urgency, and a breakfast host stretching one tortoise crossing across two sponsored bulletins*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
HOST JIN, a capuchin
REPORTER ODA, a macaque at the crossing
MOSS, a tortoise on his way to the pond
CALLER UNA, a ring-tailed lemur waiting in her car

[BREAKFAST THEME. DISTANT ENGINE IDLES.]

JIN: We interrupt the weather to bring you a developing situation on Fern Avenue. Oda, describe the scale of the incident.

ODA: A tortoise is crossing the road. Three cars are waiting.

JIN: Three vehicles immobilized by a mobile shell. Are you above the scene?

ODA: I am beside the crossing, standing on a curb.

JIN: Our low-altitude correspondent has exclusive access. Oda, how long before this obstruction clears?

MOSS: I have ten feet to go.

JIN: Ten feet! He has twice as many feet as previously reported.

ODA: He means distance. His anatomy remains unchanged.

[STUDIO CHIME.]

JIN: Our anatomy update is brought to you by Zoom Juice. When the road stops, you don't have to.

MOSS: Is that something I can drink at the pond?

JIN: Moss, you are live across the city. Please describe your journey.

MOSS: From the grass to the pond. I brought a leaf.

JIN: Provisions for an expedition. Oda, can you see the leaf?

ODA: Yes. It is in his mouth.

JIN: Has he abandoned radio contact?

MOSS: I am chewing.

[PHONE LINE OPENS.]

UNA: I'm in the first car. He is almost across.

JIN: Thank you, eyewitness. How has the ordeal affected you?

UNA: I changed the station twice. You are reporting it on both frequencies.

JIN: Our partner station carries the regional perspective.

UNA: Their report says a convoy of shells.

ODA: They are counting the pictures on our traffic website.

JIN: The website refreshes every thirty seconds. The situation is evolving.

MOSS: I am the same tortoise in every picture.

JIN: We will confirm that when we have independent identification.

UNA: His leaf has the same bite out of it.

[SOFT BICYCLE BELL.]

ODA: Moss has reached the far curb. The cars are moving.

JIN: Hold the report. We have another sponsored update in forty seconds.

ODA: I cannot hold a crossing for your advertising schedule.

JIN: Could you hold the microphone on the empty road?

ODA: Certainly. It sounds like cars going past.

[THREE CARS PASS. A BICYCLE BELL RECEDES.]

JIN: The evacuation is underway.

UNA: I am driving to the bakery. Please stop calling it an evacuation.

JIN: We have a moving eyewitness. Stay with us, Una.

UNA: Your signal gets poor by the bakery. That is why I go there.

[PHONE LINE CLICKS OFF.]

ODA: The crossing is clear. Moss is eating his leaf at the pond.

JIN: Can we get him back on the line for the recovery phase?

MOSS: I never left. Your microphone cable is beside my lunch.

JIN: How do you feel after reaching your destination?

MOSS: Hungry. You have made one leaf last two bulletins.

JIN: That is an exclusive consumption timeline.

ODA: Jin, the sponsor slot is here. Give the actual traffic result.

[STUDIO CHIME. A FAST SYNTH JINGLE.]

JIN: Fern Avenue is clear. This rapid resolution was brought to you by Zoom Juice.

MOSS: I did not drink any.

JIN: The product works at a distance.

ODA: The sponsor's van has just arrived. Its driver is asking where the traffic jam went.

JIN: Tell him we have photographs.

MOSS: He can photograph my leaf. It is half gone now.

ODA: The driver wants a queue behind the brand banner. There isn't one.

JIN: Could the van wait at the loading bay?

ODA: It is waiting there already. The driver has put his camera on a tripod.

JIN: A stationary vehicle. We have our next developing situation.

MOSS: You will need another leaf. This one has reached its destination too.

[CRUNCH. THE TRAFFIC JINGLE STARTS, THEN ODA UNPLUGS THE FIELD MICROPHONE.]

JIN: Oda? Oda, have we lost our aerial view?

[STUDIO CHIME.]

JIN: Please enjoy a brief silence, sponsored by the coverage gap on Fern Avenue.
