# The Great Boardgame Broadcast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio board-game tournament coverage, over-detailed move narration, and contestants who keep arguing over what a token can see*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Hal, a baboon game-show host; Suri, a gibbon rules commentator; Puck, a macaque contestant; and Nella, a capuchin contestant.*

[SFX: Tournament theme, dice tumble, wooden tokens click.]

HAL: Welcome to The Great Boardgame Broadcast, the only tournament where we describe every move twice: once as played and once as imagined by the players.

SURI: Our finalists are Puck and Nella. Their game is Orchard Quest, where two teams race to harvest four fruit tokens.

PUCK: I move my basket token three spaces along the path.

HAL: A bold advance from the blue basket.

SURI: It is the only legal route.

NELLA: I move my squirrel token beside the bridge.

HAL: The squirrel is eyeing the river.

SURI: It is a painted token. It cannot eye anything.

PUCK: The river tile blocks the direct route. I play the wooden plank card.

NELLA: The rules say the plank card spans one space. That is a two-space gap.

SURI: Correct. The move fails.

PUCK: The basket could throw the plank.

SURI: Baskets cannot throw.

HAL: The crowd is split between those who support the rules and those who support throwing baskets.

[SFX: Audience murmurs.]

NELLA: I draw a card. It says "Unexpected Wind." My squirrel moves one step backward.

HAL: A reversal! The squirrel has reconsidered the bridge.

NELLA: It is being blown backward.

SURI: That is what the card says.

PUCK: I draw "Helpful Crow." Can the crow carry my basket over the river?

SURI: It can carry one fruit token, not a basket.

PUCK: Then it carries the basket token.

SURI: The token is not a basket.

HAL: A fascinating distinction between symbol and object.

SURI: The crow card moves one fruit token to the next tree.

PUCK: I send my pear.

[SFX: A wooden token slides.]

NELLA: I place my squirrel beside the pear and claim the shade bonus.

SURI: You must be under the tree. Your squirrel is beside it.

NELLA: The tree's shadow extends one space.

SURI: The board has no shadow rule.

HAL: The players request a lighting cue.

[SFX: A lamp clicks on; the board is briefly illuminated.]

SURI: We are not adding rules during the final.

PUCK: Then I will take the plum token at the west tree.

NELLA: I play "Rain." The bridge closes for one turn.

PUCK: I am not on the bridge.

NELLA: The card says the whole path is muddy.

SURI: The route slows by one space. Puck's basket stops short of the tree.

HAL: The basket is one step from victory.

PUCK: I roll. Four!

[SFX: Dice tumble and settle.]

SURI: Your token moves two spaces. The other two spaces are blocked by the pond.

PUCK: The basket could float.

SURI: Baskets cannot throw, fly, or float unless a card says so.

NELLA: I have "Dry Day." The route clears. My squirrel moves to the bridge, and I harvest my fourth fruit.

HAL: Nella wins Orchard Quest!

PUCK: I would like to appeal to the basket.

SURI: The basket has no vote.

HAL: Nella receives the golden acorn trophy and a complete copy of the rules.

NELLA: I will read them before the next tournament.

PUCK: Can I keep the crow card?

SURI: Yes, but it cannot carry your trophy.

[SFX: Audience applause, game tokens swept neatly into a box.]

HAL: The Great Boardgame Broadcast ends with one champion, one careful rules reader, and a basket that remains a symbol.

SURI: The judges confirm Nella's fourth fruit. The token is printed on the board, not carried by a crow.

NELLA: I will put the trophy beside the rulebook.

PUCK: I want the rules to say baskets may float.

SURI: You can propose an expansion after the tournament.

HAL: Next season's edition may include a boat-shaped basket, though it still cannot throw.

[SFX: A paper boat is folded; it tips over beside the game board.]

PUCK: The river has taken my prototype.

NELLA: The tournament is over.

[SFX: Dice roll once inside the closed box; theme fades.]
