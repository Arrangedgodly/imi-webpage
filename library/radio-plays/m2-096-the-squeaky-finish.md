# The Squeaky Finish

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio athletics, play-by-play jargon, and commentators overcalling ordinary events*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: commentator Rell, a baboon; analyst Jori, a gibbon; race marshal Tavi, a macaque; runner Nema, a capuchin; listener caller Odo, an orangutan.

[SFX: CROWD MURMUR. WHISTLE SQUEAKS.]

RELL: Welcome to the Canopy Cup. Runners are at the start.

JORI: The course is one loop around the tree.

RELL: Nema looks focused.

JORI: Nema is checking her shoe.

TAVI: The starting whistle is ready.

[SFX: WHISTLE SQUEAKS AGAIN.]

RELL: The race has begun!

TAVI: That was the warm-up whistle.

JORI: Nema has not moved.

RELL: A tactical pause.

### Act One

TAVI: Runners, take your marks.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRASS. A SHOE SQUEAKS.]

RELL: Nema is off!

JORI: She took one step.

RELL: A powerful first step.

TAVI: The start signal has not sounded.

[SFX: WHISTLE BLOWS CLEARLY. CROWD CHEERS.]

RELL: Now the race is underway.

JORI: Nema leads the field.

RELL: The field is Nema and one warm-up runner.

JORI: The warm-up runner is carrying a cup.

RELL: A surprising challenger.

NEMA: I'm just running the route.

TAVI: The warm-up runner is off the course.

RELL: Nema rounds the first tree.

JORI: There is only one tree.

RELL: The first time around it.

[SFX: RUNNING FOOTSTEPS. A SMALL SQUEAK REPEATS.]

RELL: A shoe is squeaking.

JORI: The shoe belongs to Nema.

RELL: It is announcing her position.

### Act Two

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS SLOW. CROWD MURMUR.]

JORI: Nema is slowing near the finish.

RELL: She has a cramp.

NEMA: I dropped my leaf number.

TAVI: The number is pinned to your back.

NEMA: It fell off.

JORI: The number is on the track.

RELL: Nema stops to retrieve it.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOP. PAPER LIFTS.]

TAVI: The race marshal will move the number out of the lane.

NEMA: It is my number.

RELL: A show of sportsmanship.

JORI: She is putting the number back on.

[SFX: PIN CLIPS.]

RELL: The runner resumes.

JORI: She has lost three seconds.

RELL: Three seconds can change everything.

NEMA: There is no other runner.

RELL: The warm-up runner is near the finish.

[SFX: CUP SET DOWN.]

JORI: The warm-up runner is no longer running.

RELL: Nema is closing the gap.

TAVI: There is no gap.

[SFX: SHOE SQUEAKS, FOOTSTEPS ACCELERATE.]

RELL: Nema sprints toward the finish.

JORI: She is walking quickly.

RELL: The finish tape approaches.

TAVI: We have no tape.

RELL: The finish is the chalk line.

NEMA: I can see it.

### Act Three

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CROSS CHALK. WHISTLE.]

RELL: Nema crosses the line!

JORI: The official time is one minute, twelve seconds.

TAVI: The course record is one minute, twelve seconds.

RELL: We have a new record.

NEMA: I was the only runner.

JORI: A record is still a record.

RELL: We have a caller.

ODO: I heard the race on the radio. The shoe squeaked exactly twelve times.

JORI: We counted eleven.

ODO: The first squeak was before the start.

TAVI: It was the warm-up whistle.

RELL: The shoe did not squeak before the start.

ODO: It did when she checked it.

NEMA: I checked the shoe before the start.

JORI: Then the total is twelve.

RELL: The squeaky shoe has matched the course record.

TAVI: Shoes do not have records.

ODO: This one has a record of squeaks.

RELL: We can award it a medal.

NEMA: The medal will weigh down the shoe.

JORI: It is a tiny medal.

TAVI: The race is complete.

RELL: The race is complete, the record is confirmed, and the shoe is still squeaking.

[SFX: SHOE SQUEAKS ONCE.]

[SFX: REPLAY TAPE SPINS. THE SAME SHOE SQUEAK PLAYS THREE TIMES.]

RELL: We review the winning moment.

JORI: That is the shoe before the start.

TAVI: The shoe is being tested.

RELL: We need an official recording of each squeak.

NEMA: The race is over.

ODO: The archive is just beginning.

### Tag

RELL: One more squeak.

JORI: That is after the race.

NEMA: The shoe is celebrating.

[SFX: SHOE SQUEAKS TWICE, THEN FOOTSTEPS FADE.]
