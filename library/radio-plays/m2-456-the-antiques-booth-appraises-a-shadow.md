# The Antiques Booth Appraises a Shadow

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: antiques appraisal broadcasts that attach a grand provenance to whatever the microphone can hear*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; appraiser Seli, a lemur; seller Mara, a macaque; sound recordist Pip, a capuchin; caller Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Small hall. Folding table unfolds. A lamp clicks on.

ODO: Welcome to Heirloom Hour. Today we appraise objects with history. Seli, what is our first item?

SELI: I cannot see one.

MARA: The lamp is on.

PIP: I can hear something moving by the table.

SFX: A faint brush across the floor.

ODO: Could it be a rare carpet?

SELI: It could be a draft.

MARA: I brought a brass spoon. My aunt used it to stir jam.

SFX: Spoon placed on table. It rings once.

SELI: That is a nice spoon. The mark on the handle says "Hotel Service."

MARA: She worked at a hotel.

ODO: A spoon that traveled.

SELI: It is brass, probably mass-made in the last century. It may have stirred a lot of jam.

MARA: That's more history than I expected.

PIP: The sound is moving closer.

SFX: Brush, brush, brush. Pause.

ODO: Our second item has arrived.

SELI: The caller hasn't come in.

VEX, on phone: I am at the table. I brought a shadow.

MARA: Shadows are not antiques.

VEX: This one belongs to an old umbrella.

SELI: An umbrella blocks light. It does not own the shadow.

ODO: How old is the umbrella?

VEX: It belonged to my grandfather.

PIP: Is it here?

VEX: The umbrella is outside. The shadow followed me in.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps cross the room.

MARA: The shadow moved with you.

ODO: A remarkable chain of custody.

SELI: Let's place the umbrella under the lamp. Then we can see what makes the shadow.

SFX: Umbrella opens. A wooden rib clicks.

VEX: Careful. It is fragile.

SELI: The fabric is patched. The handle is plain wood. The shadow is changing shape as we turn it.

PIP: I can record the sound when the rib flexes.

SFX: Umbrella turns. A soft creak.

ODO: A sound from the past.

SELI: A loose hinge.

VEX: The umbrella belonged to my grandfather, and he carried it through three storms.

MARA: Did he tell you that?

VEX: He told me it leaked.

SELI: The repairs matter more than a dramatic pedigree. This patch is hand-sewn.

ODO: Can the shadow be appraised separately?

SELI: It disappears when the light moves.

ODO: So its value is difficult to hold.

PIP: The lamp is sliding.

SFX: Table leg scrapes. Lamp shade rattles.

MARA: The table is uneven. The lamp isn't sliding; the floor is sloped.

SELI: Put the spoon under the short leg.

MARA: Not the spoon. It's our first item.

ODO: We have a spoon, an umbrella, and an unstable table. We need a second opinion.

SFX: Phone line crackles.

VEX: I say the shadow is priceless.

SELI: That is not an appraisal. It's a feeling.

MARA: The spoon can brace the leg. It is a service spoon; it has served again.

SFX: Spoon slides beneath leg. Table steadies. Lamp holds still.

PIP: The shadow has stopped moving.

ODO: Its value has gone up.

SELI: The spoon is worth a modest amount. The umbrella is worth more to someone who remembers its repairs. The shadow is free with the light.

VEX: Can I insure it?

SELI: You can insure the umbrella. The shadow changes owners every time the sun moves.

ODO: Then we have our estimate.

MARA: I will keep the umbrella. I'll lend the spoon to the station until the table is level.

PIP: The station's shadow is still on the floor.

SELI: That's ours. It comes with the table.

TAG

SFX: Lamp clicks off. The shadow vanishes. The spoon rings beneath the table.

ODO: We have lost a valuable item.

SELI: No. The room got dark.
