# The Conveyor's Secret Rattle

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: factory radio tours that narrate a conveyor belt's loose bolt as if it were an intentional musical instrument*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Mena, a macaque; mechanic Oru, a capuchin; guide Seli, a gibbon; worker Pavi, a lemur.

[SFX: FACTORY FLOOR. CONVEYOR RUMBLES. A METAL RATTLE REPEATS.]

MENA: The packing line has a rhythmic sound, like a factory drum.

ORU: It is a loose guard bolt.

SELI: The tour brochure calls it "the conveyor's percussion."

PAVI: It started this morning. The guard vibrates when the belt is full.

MENA: Could we keep it for the radio tour?

ORU: Not until I inspect it. A loose guard can catch a box.

[SFX: BELT STOPS. MOTOR WHINES DOWN.]

SELI: The tour group is waiting.

PAVI: The next batch of boxes is at the loading side.

ORU: Keep the belt stopped while I check the guard. The bolt is missing a washer.

MENA: The rattle was a warning, not a performance.

MENA: The tour guide has a drumbeat in the introduction.

ORU: That beat is the same rattle we heard from the loose guard.

PAVI: The guide says, "listen for the line's signature sound."

SELI: We should revise it before the next tour. The line's signature sound is a motor and moving trays.

MENA: The sponsor promised visitors an industrial symphony.

ORU: The belt is not an instrument. The guard was loose.

[SFX: PAVI PLACES AN EMPTY TRAY ON THE BELT. MOTOR HUMS EVENLY.]

PAVI: When the line is loaded, the tray makes a soft clack at the end.

SELI: That's a normal part of the process.

MENA: Could that be the signature sound?

ORU: Call it a tray arriving. It is not percussion.

[SFX: TRAY REACHES THE END AND STOPS AGAINST A RUBBER PAD.]

PAVI: The pad catches it. No loose metal.

SELI: I'll tell the guide to describe the motion instead of scoring it.

MENA: The tour can still be interesting without giving a belt a band name.

SELI: The brochure is already printed.

PAVI: We can put a sticker over "percussion" and write "maintenance pause."

[SFX: WRENCH TURNS. METAL GUARD SHIFTS.]

ORU: I have the washer. The bolt is still in the frame.

MENA: The sponsor wants us to record the repair as an industrial rhythm.

ORU: The wrench is not a drumstick.

SELI: I can change the tour line. We can tell people what the sound means and why the belt stopped.

[SFX: BOLT TIGHTENS. GUARD RATTLES ONCE WHEN TAPPED.]

ORU: It is secure now. The rattle on the recording was the guard shaking.

PAVI: The motor can start at low speed. I will run an empty belt first.

[SFX: MOTOR STARTS. BELT MOVES SLOWLY. NO RATTLE.]

MENA: I hear the motor and the boxes, not the metal beat.

ORU: Add one empty tray. Check the guard under load.

[SFX: EMPTY TRAY RIDES BELT. MOTOR HUMS. NO RATTLE.]

PAVI: The line is quiet.

SELI: The tour group can come in now.

MENA: What will we call the segment?

PAVI: The safety card says stop the belt if a guard rattles.

MENA: We can record that in the tour note, but no one needs to repeat the rattle.

ORU: The empty tray test is complete. The guard stays in place and the belt moves smoothly.

SELI: I am removing the phrase "industrial percussion" from the next print run.

MENA: The sponsor can keep the name on the tour, not on the sound.

[SFX: PRINTER STARTS. A NEW TOUR CARD FEEDS OUT.]

PAVI: This one says "watch a tray travel safely to the end."

ORU: That is what the line does.

ORU: "The Sound the Mechanic Stopped."

SELI: That is accurate, and the visitors will hear the line running normally.

[SFX: FACTORY FLOOR DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS ENTER.]

PAVI: The line is moving. The guard is quiet.

MENA: The conveyor's percussion has left the tour.

ORU: Good. It was never in the orchestra.

[SFX: RADIO MIC CLICKS OFF. BELT RUNS AT STEADY SPEED.]
