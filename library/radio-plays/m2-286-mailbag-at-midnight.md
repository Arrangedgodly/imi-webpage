# Mailbag at Midnight

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live postal dispatch reports, dramatic misdelivery suspense, and a carrier who reads every label aloud*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST OMI, a macaque
DISPATCHER LUMA, a gibbon
COURIER PIP, a capuchin
CALLER VEX, a baboon

[SFX: NIGHT WIND AGAINST A DEPOT WINDOW. A MAIL SACK SETS DOWN.]

OMI: Welcome to Midnight Mailbag. Tonight, one envelope has arrived without a return address.

PIP: Most of the delivery address is here.

LUMA: Most is not enough for a route.

OMI: A letter without an origin. A destination without a number. A courier with no answers.

PIP: I have a torch.

[SFX: TORCH CLICKS ON. PAPER RUSTLES.]

LUMA: The visible instruction says green door, porch light on. Four houses fit that description.

OMI: Four suspects.

PIP: Four possible recipients.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

VEX: I live in one of those houses. Is the letter for me?

LUMA: We need your name and house number.

VEX: Vex. Fourteen Green Lane. My neighbor has a green door too, with a brass fish.

OMI: A fish enters the case.

LUMA: A fish enters the description. Pip, check beneath that folded corner.

[PAPER CRINKLES. THE CORNER RESISTS.]

PIP: The damp label has folded onto itself.

OMI: A concealed identity.

PIP: A wet corner.

LUMA: Set it beside the desk fan. Do not tear it.

[SFX: SMALL FAN STARTS.]

VEX: Is that the wind outside?

OMI: The storm has followed the envelope indoors.

LUMA: It is our desk fan, on its lowest setting.

VEX: My porch light has a moth around it. That might help.

OMI: A solitary witness circling the scene.

PIP: We cannot ask a moth for a signature.

[SFX: OMI TAPS A DRAMATIC MUSIC KEY. A LOW STRING NOTE SOUNDS.]

LUMA: That key plays through the dispatch channel. The next courier will think the bridge is closed.

[OMI RELEASES IT. THE NOTE STOPS.]

OMI: Our music has encountered an administrative obstacle.

VEX: The bridge is open. I used it when returning my neighbor's keys.

OMI: Keys! Someone has supplied access to the green door.

PIP: We are delivering a letter. We do not need access to the house.

VEX: Then why did you ask about the door?

LUMA: We didn't. Whoever addressed this envelope wrote a landmark instead of a number.

[SFX: DRYING PAPER LIFTS FROM THE DESK.]

PIP: The corner is loose. I can unfold it now.

OMI: Listeners, the next sound could change everything.

[PIP UNFOLDS THE LABEL. ONE SMALL PAPER CRACKLE.]

PIP: Vex. Fourteen Green Lane. The instruction was added below the actual address.

VEX: That is me.

LUMA: Put it in route fourteen's dry pouch. You can leave when the squall passes.

OMI: Before it leaves, can we discover what kind of letter it is?

PIP: The kind that fits this envelope.

[OMI TOUCHES THE FLAP. LUMA SLIDES THE ROUTE FORM UNDER HIS PAW.]

LUMA: You can discover which box to sign on our dispatch sheet.

OMI: The sheet has no suspense.

PIP: It has a delivery deadline.

[SFX: WIND FALLS. RAIN BECOMES A SOFT DRIP. ZIPPER CLOSES.]

LUMA: Conditions have cleared. Pip, route fourteen.

PIP: Leaving the depot.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS, BOOTS ON PAVING. PIP'S FIELD RADIO CLICKS ON.]

PIP: At number fourteen. Green door. Porch light. One moth.

VEX: I am still on the phone. Should I answer the door?

OMI: Our recipient confronts the courier.

LUMA: Yes, Vex. Answer the door.

[SFX: KNOCK, DOOR OPENS. A PEN SCRATCHES A RECEIPT.]

PIP: Delivered. Vex is opening it inside.

VEX (PHONE): It's a thank-you note for returning the keys.

OMI: To the brass-fish neighbor?

VEX: Yes. They put their landmark on my letter so I would know who sent it.

LUMA: Our sender has used the recipient line for their own address.

OMI: At last, a twist.

VEX: Shall I call the neighbor and explain?

LUMA: Tomorrow. The letter has reached you.

[SFX: DEPOT STAMP, FIELD RADIO CLICKS OFF.]

OMI: Midnight Mailbag closes with one delivered envelope. Join us tomorrow for the parcel marked THIS WAY UP on every side.
