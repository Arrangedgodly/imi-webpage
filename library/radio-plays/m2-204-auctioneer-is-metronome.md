# The Auctioneer Is a Metronome

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live auction radio coverage that accelerates bids and treats ordinary lots as priceless revelations*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST MENA, a macaque
AUCTIONEER BO, a gorilla
CLERK PAV, a capuchin
BIDDER RUA, a langur

[SFX: GAVEL TAPS TWICE. PAPER SHUFFLES.]

MENA: We are live at the town hall auction. First lot: a wooden bench, donated by the park committee.

BO: Who will start at ten?

RUA: I will bid five.

BO: Five is not the starting bid.

PAV: The listed minimum is five.

BO: The energy in the room is rising.

### Scene One: The Bench

[SFX: GAVEL TAP.]

BO: Five from Rua. Do I hear six?

RUA: I can pay five.

MENA: It is a bench with one repaired leg.

PAV: The repair note is on the lot card.

BO: The craftsmanship tells a story.

RUA: The story is that the leg was fixed.

[BO TAPS THE GAVEL TOO EARLY.]

PAV: The lot has not closed.

BO: The suspense got ahead of me.

[RUA BIDS FIVE AGAIN. NO OTHER BIDDER RAISES A HAND.] 

BO: Sold for five.

### Scene Two: The Mic Stand

[PAPER CARD TURNS.]

MENA: Next lot: a microphone stand from the old radio booth.

BO: A relic of broadcasting history.

PAV: It wobbles.

RUA: I can use it for a lamp.

BO: A rare collectible.

RUA: It is a stand.

[PAV TIGHTENS THE CLAMP. THE STAND STOPS WOBBLING.]

PAV: It works now.

BO: The value has increased.

MENA: The screw was loose.

[RUA OFFERS THREE. BO STARTS A RAPID COUNT.]

BO: Three, four, five, six...

PAV: Rua bid three. No one else bid.

BO: The numbers were for atmosphere.

### Scene Three: The Final Lot

[SFX: A SMALL WOODEN BOX OPENS.]

[PAV TURNS THE LOT CARD.]

PAV: The box contains two ink pads and a date stamp.

BO: Rare office artifacts.

RUA: I need the date stamp for the library desk.

MENA: The starting price is one.

RUA: I bid one.

BO: One going once, one going twice...

[BO SPEAKS FASTER. PAV RAISES A HAND.]

PAV: The only bid is one.

BO: The bid is still one.

[THE GAVEL TAPS ONCE. THE INK PADS SLIDE INTO THE BOX.]

RUA: I can use both.

MENA: The lot was sold as a set.

MENA: Final lot: the auctioneer's gavel, starting at one.

BO: I will bid two.

PAV: The auctioneer cannot bid on his own lot.

BO: I was demonstrating the value.

RUA: I bid one.

[BO TRIES TO RAISE THE BID. PAV REMOVES THE GAVEL FROM HIS HAND AND SETS IT ON THE CLERK'S TABLE.] 

PAV: One from Rua. Going once, going twice, sold.

MENA: The gavel is sold to the bidder.

RUA: Can I use it to call the next auction?

PAV: After the receipt is signed.

[PAV SETS THE RECEIPT ON THE TABLE. BO REACHES FOR IT WITH THE GAVEL.] 

PAV: The gavel belongs to Rua now.

BO: I was testing the ink.

[RUA SIGNS THE RECEIPT. THE DATE STAMP SLAMS DOWN ON THE PAPER AND LEAVES A MARK ON THE TABLECLOTH.]

MENA: The receipt is now dated.

PAV: And the tablecloth will need washing.

[THE PHONE LINE BUZZES. A REMOTE BIDDER ASKS IF THE BENCH IS STILL AVAILABLE.]

MENA: The bench has sold.

BO: We can open a new lot.

PAV: The auction is over.

[BO SETS THE GAVEL DOWN. IT ROLLS INTO THE WOODEN BOX.]

RUA: The next lot has arrived.

MENA: The lot is the box itself.

PAV: It is empty now.

[BO LIFTS THE BOX. IT HAS A SQUARE OF DUST ON THE TABLE WHERE IT SAT.]

BO: The box has been sold twice.

### Tag

[SFX: A SINGLE GAVEL TAP.]

MENA: The hall auction has ended.

BO: I have lost my gavel.

RUA: You can borrow the bench.
