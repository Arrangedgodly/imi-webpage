# The Night Shift at the Pond

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overnight nature livestreams, breathless live narration, and hosts who narrate every small movement as a major sighting*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST LARI, a gibbon
NATURALIST PON, a macaque
CAMERA OPERATOR SIV, a capuchin
LISTENER KEN, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: NIGHT INSECTS, SOFT WATER, DISTANT FROG.]

LARI: Welcome to Night Shift at the Pond. It is midnight, and the water is still.

PON: A frog has just moved one step along the bank.

LARI: A dramatic repositioning.

SIV: It moved from one lily pad to another.

LARI: A dramatic repositioning across the pond.

PON: The pond is three meters wide.

LARI: Every distance is meaningful at night.

[SFX: FROG CROAKS ONCE.]

LARI: We have a vocalization.

PON: One croak. It may be a call to another frog.

SIV: Or it may be my chair.

[SFX: CHAIR CREAKS.]

LARI: The chair has answered.

PON: It has not. Siv shifted his weight.

LARI: We must remain quiet so wildlife can behave naturally.

SIV: I need to move the camera. The frog is behind the reeds.

LARI: Move silently.

[SFX: CAMERA TRIPOD SCRAPES LOUDLY. FROG SPLASHES.]

PON: The frog has entered the pond.

LARI: It has made a bold aquatic decision.

SIV: I was trying to follow it.

PON: Now we wait. It may surface nearby.

LARI: While we wait, let's hear from our overnight listener. Ken, are you seeing the pond?

KEN: I am listening on the radio. I hear water and an occasional click.

LARI: What do you imagine?

KEN: A quiet pond and a chair that keeps trying to join the wildlife.

SIV: I will stop moving.

[SFX: CHAIR CREAKS AGAIN.]

KEN: The chair has returned.

PON: That was a frog. I heard a splash.

LARI: We have two possible sightings: frog, chair.

KEN: Can the chair be entered into the pond checklist?

PON: No.

LARI: Our field checklist includes frog, water, reeds, and an unidentified sound.

SIV: The unidentified sound is my jacket zipper.

[SFX: ZIPPER.]

LARI: It may be a small mammal.

PON: It is a zipper.

LARI: The certainty of the naturalist is reassuring.

[SFX: FROG CROAKS THREE TIMES, CLOSE BY.]

PON: There. The frog is back on the near bank.

SIV: I have it in frame.

LARI: The frog has returned to the same lily pad.

PON: It is not the same pad. It is a flat stone.

LARI: It has moved from water to land.

PON: It is sitting at the edge. It may be hunting insects.

KEN: I can hear the insects now.

[SFX: INSECTS HUM. FROG SNAPS UP AN INSECT.]

LARI: A capture! The frog has caught its midnight meal.

PON: That is a useful observation. It waited, then snapped at prey.

LARI: How long did we wait?

SIV: Forty minutes.

LARI: The frog's meal lasted half a second.

KEN: It was still a successful evening.

PON: We should leave the pond undisturbed. The camera can stay pointed at the water while we pack up.

SIV: I will lift the tripod carefully.

[SFX: TRIPOD FOLDS. CHAIR CREAKS.]

LARI: The chair has made one final call.

PON: We are done interpreting furniture.

KEN: Is the camera still live?

SIV: The picture is live, but the microphone is off.

LARI: We can end without narrating the dark screen.

PON: The pond remains. The frog has gone to feed somewhere else.

KEN: And the chair?

SIV: I am carrying it back to the studio.

[SFX: CHAIR LIFTED. CREAKING STOPS.]

LARI: A quieter pond, now that its rival has left.

PON: That was not the pond's rival.

LARI: We will let the listener decide.

KEN: Good night to the pond and the chair.

LARI: Night Shift at the Pond will return tomorrow, assuming the frog has not moved the studio.

[SFX: WATER, INSECTS, THEME QUIETLY OUT.]
