# The Footbridge Opening Has a Ribbon Problem

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: civic ribbon-cutting broadcasts where ceremony blocks the thing being celebrated*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; planner Mara, a macaque; mayor Vex, an orangutan; resident Pip, a capuchin; recorder Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Creekside. Water runs under a new footbridge. Ribbon stretches across the entrance.

ODO: We are live at the opening of the new footbridge.

VEX: The ribbon is aligned, the scissors are polished, and the speech is ready.

PIP: Can I cross to the garden?

MARA: Not until the ceremony ends.

VEX: First the ribbon, then the speech, then the group photograph.

PIP: The garden crew needs the watering cans on the other side.

SELI: The cans are beside the microphone stand.

VEX: We can include them in the photograph.

MARA: They are tools, not decorations.

SELI: A group has gathered on the far bank with the watering cans.

PIP: I can see them. The bridge is exactly wide enough for one cart.

MARA: The rail has a loose cap near the center.

ODO: I tightened it this morning, but we should test it before the opening.

VEX: A bridge inspection was not in the schedule.

MARA: It is more useful than a photograph.

SFX: Mara steps onto boards. Planks creak softly.

PIP: Does it feel steady?

MARA: Yes. Odo, check that cap while I keep the ribbon off the rail.

SFX: Wrench turns. The loose cap clicks snug.

ODO: It is secure.

VEX: Can I cross for the speech?

MARA: Walk, don't pose. Keep the microphone cord clear of the rail.

SFX: Footsteps cross. Cord trails and catches briefly on the ribbon.

PIP: The cord is snagged.

VEX: Pull it free.

MARA: Don't pull. That can drag the microphone into the creek.

SFX: Ribbon unties; cord slips free.

ODO: The bridge path is clear now.

VEX: I can begin the speech from this bank.

SELI: The public can hear you from the bridge entrance.

VEX: But the group photo...

PIP: The watering cans are already across. The garden crew is waiting.

SFX: Scissors open and close.

VEX: I will cut on the count of three. One, two...

PIP: The ribbon is tied to the rail.

MARA: It is also tied to the other rail. Cutting the middle won't clear the entrance.

VEX: Then cut both ends.

ODO: We have one pair of scissors.

PIP: I can untie one knot while you cut the other.

MARA: Wait. The ribbon is taut across the walking path. If we cut it, it may snap back.

SELI: The wind is pulling it toward the creek.

VEX: We need the ceremonial moment.

MARA: We can move the ribbon to the side rail, cut it there, and leave the path open.

SFX: Ribbon loosens. Cloth slides along rail.

PIP: Now I can carry a watering can across.

VEX: Not until the speech.

PIP: The garden plants are drying.

ODO: Let him carry one can during the speech. The bridge can open while the mayor speaks.

VEX: That ruins the order.

MARA: It gives the bridge a purpose.

ODO: The ribbon is tied to the side rail now, and the microphone cable is free.

SELI: The far-bank crew can come across in pairs.

PIP: I'll meet them at the entrance and take the empty cans back.

VEX: No applause cue?

MARA: The water cans crossing are the cue.

SFX: Can lifts. Footsteps cross wooden planks.

PIP: It holds. The rail is steady.

SELI: The bridge is open.

VEX: I will deliver the speech from the middle.

MARA: Keep clear of the watering cans.
