# The Puddle Needs a Crossing License

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: municipal service broadcasts that turn a small repair into a permit chain*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Announcer Mara, a macaque; inspector Vex, an orangutan; caller Neri, a capuchin; sound operator Pip, a lemur; clerk Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Remote curb feed connects. Morning traffic hum. A shallow splash; two quick footfalls stop.

MARA: This is City Helpful Hour. Today's question: how do you cross a puddle without wetting your socks?

NERI: I'm calling from Fern Lane, between the bus stop and the bakery. My little brother is waiting beside me on the dry curb.

VEX: I have the curb camera open. I can see both of you and the puddle.

VEX: Is it a public puddle?

NERI: It is in the street.

VEX: Streets are public. Puddles are water. Water is managed by Utilities.

MARA: We have Inspector Vex on the line. Inspector, can you inspect it?

VEX: I have to file a field visit request.

PIP: The microphone is picking up another splash.

NERI: That's my brother. He tried to jump again.

SFX: A small leap. Wet slap. Silence.

NERI: Now he has one wet sock.

VEX: That is an incident, not an inspection.

MARA: Can the city provide stepping stones?

VEX: Stones require placement approval. Approval requires a puddle measurement.

NERI: How do I measure it?

VEX: In the wet zone, using the dry-zone form.

PIP: The form is on paper?

VEX: Naturally.

MARA: Could you tell us the number of forms?

VEX: One for the puddle, one for a crossing, and one if a person has already crossed.

NERI: My brother tried to cross.

VEX: Then we need form C.

PIP: He is still wearing the sock. I hear him squelch.

SFX: Squelch. Squelch. A chair scrapes.

MARA: Clerk Odo, can you bring form C to the studio?

ODO: I did. It's a map of the puddle. I'm sending Neri a photo now.

SFX: A phone notification chimes on the curb feed.

NERI: It has three squares.

ODO: One is for the puddle. One is for the crossing. The last is for the dry place you stand after.

VEX: Who issued this form?

ODO: Your office.

VEX: Then the map is official.

MARA: Can we mark it from here?

ODO: We can hear the splash. Pip, give us the studio's puddle sound.

PIP: This is a studio demonstration only. I have a bowl of water and a spoon.

SFX: In the studio, one small spoon splash.

VEX: That's not deep enough.

PIP: The caller said it was shallow.

NERI: It reaches my ankle.

VEX: In that case, we need an ankle-depth notation.

MARA: Odo?

ODO: Form C has a box. I can check it.

SFX: Pencil tick. Paper stamp.

ODO: Approved for one crossing, by one person, wearing a washable sock.

NERI: My brother has one wet wool sock from his first jump. He will stay on the curb while I test the stones.

VEX: Wool is not washable in a public puddle.

MARA: He can keep the wet sock on and wait safely. The form covers one crossing, not two.

PIP: The bus is braking at the stop. Neri has not moved yet.

MARA: We are going to test the route on air. Neri, place two flat stones on either side. Odo, read the permit. Vex, watch for traffic.

SFX: A bus brakes at the marked stop. Its doors fold open.

VEX: The stones are beside the transit stop. We need Transit to approve any step near the curb.

MARA: The bus is boarding. Can we get the answer before it leaves?

ODO: Transit has a recorded line.

SFX: Phone keypad. Tinny hold music. A recorded voice clicks on.

ODO: Form C covers a pedestrian crossing at a marked stop.

VEX: The stones aren't marked.

NERI: The puddle is.

MARA: Put the stones beside the curb, outside the bus path. Neri, wait for the bus to leave before you cross.

SFX: On the remote curb feed, two stones are set down. Bus doors close; the bus pulls away. Water ripples.

VEX: Traffic is clear. The stones are flat and spaced apart.

MARA: The permit covers one person. Neri, cross alone while your brother stays on the dry curb.

SFX: On the remote phone microphone, three careful steps cross the stones.

NERI: I'm on the bakery side. My feet are dry. My brother is still waiting beside the bus stop.

PIP: Those were Neri's steps through the phone. My spoon splash was only the studio bowl.

ODO: I'll close the request for one crossing, as the permit allows.

MARA: City Helpful Hour is closed. Neri has a dry path, and her brother can wait safely while we request a second permit.

SFX: Stamp. A drop falls into the studio's water bowl.
