# The Park Ranger's Whistle Is Not a Siren

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: emergency broadcasts that inflate a routine trail signal into a rescue operation*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; ranger Mara, a macaque; trail runner Pip, a capuchin; dispatcher Odo, a gibbon; visitor Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Park trailhead junction. Wind through leaves; one short whistle.

VEX: Rescue alert! A whistle has sounded from the woods.

MARA: That was my signal to gather the trail group at the junction. Nobody is missing.

ODO: All six hikers checked in. Pip is at the north marker, leading them.

PIP, over radio: Group together. A fallen branch blocks the narrow path, so we have stopped before it.

VEX: Six souls cut off from civilization.

PIP: We can see the picnic tables.

NARI: I'm beside those tables. Should I call for help?

MARA: We already have their location and a marked alternative. Odo, check the lower loop notice.

SFX: Paper lifts from a clipboard.

ODO: Lower loop open. Pip, bring the group back to the junction; I'll meet you there.

PIP: Returning now. Nobody is stepping over the branch.

VEX: Their retreat begins.

MARA: Their detour begins. This park's signal card explains the whistle; it is not an alarm siren.

VEX: Could you perform the urgent-assistance signal for listeners?

MARA: Not while actual hikers are within earshot. We can read the card without sounding it.

VEX: Radio needs sound.

MARA: You have leaves, boots, and a group who would like to finish its walk.

SFX: Vex presses a button. An enormous recorded siren begins, then cuts off.

NARI: Was that the ranger station?

ODO: That came from your portable broadcast unit, Vex. I muted its outside speaker.

VEX: It is my rescue-program theme. The brass section normally follows.

PIP, over radio: We heard it. Are we supposed to stop again?

MARA: Keep returning to the junction. There is no new incident; our broadcaster played his theme in the park.

VEX: It was supposed to add urgency.

NARI: It added a question.

SFX: Velcro tears as Vex removes a program card from his sound unit.

VEX: Fine. We shall use my peaceful-trails theme.

ODO: Read its label first.

VEX: It says peaceful-trails theme, with optional avalanche.

MARA: Leave the unit muted.

VEX: My show has paid for a great deal of music it cannot use here.

NARI: You could play the quiet parts.

VEX: Those were free.

SFX: The group's boots approach on gravel. Radio switches off nearby.

PIP: Here are our six hikers, back at the junction. We'll take the lower loop.

ODO: I have the group count. Maintenance has the branch location and will attend the closed upper path.

MARA: Nari, can you put this detour card below the map while I point out the turn?

NARI: Yes. Does the lower loop lead past the picnic tables?

PIP: It does. My group has voted to inspect them closely at lunch.

VEX: Survivors return to the very tables they thought they would never see again.

PIP: They saw them four minutes ago.

MARA: Vex, give your listeners the detour, not a rescue that never happened.

VEX: Take the marked lower loop. The upper trail is temporarily closed by a branch. No hikers are missing.

NARI: That fits on the card.

VEX: The broadcast version usually has an exclusive eyewitness interview.

PIP: You can interview me while we walk away. My testimony is that the lower loop adds ten minutes.

VEX: Ten minutes that changed six lives.

PIP: Ten minutes that moved lunch.

SFX: Boots leave down the lower loop. Their chatter fades. A card slides into the trail-board slot.

ODO: Maintenance acknowledges the notice. We'll update the board when their work is complete.

MARA: And that is our report.

VEX: Can I at least end with one triumphant note?

NARI: From the brass section?

VEX: From me.

MARA: At ordinary speaking volume.

VEX, quietly: Ta-da.

NARI: Now that is a signal I understand.
