# Stretch Before the Jingle

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio exercise programs that promise a full workout but interrupt the pacing with sponsor jingles*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
INSTRUCTOR MAI, a macaque
PRODUCER JEN, a capuchin
LISTENER ODO, a gibbon
ENGINEER RAL, a gorilla

[SFX: UPBEAT MUSIC, THEN A SPONSOR JINGLE.]

MAI: Welcome to Five-Minute Stretch. Find a clear space and stand comfortably.

JEN: This program is sponsored by Quick Nut, the snack that keeps you moving.

RAL: The jingle is longer than the stretch.

### Scene One: Warm Up

[THE MUSIC BEGINS AGAIN.]

MAI: Start with your feet comfortably apart. Keep your knees soft.

JEN: We have an ad break in thirty seconds.

RAL: The program is five minutes. The ad is sixty seconds.

MAI: We can finish the movement before the break.

[JEN CUES THE JINGLE EARLY. IT REPLACES THE MUSIC AS MAI BEGINS A SHOULDER ROLL.]

ODO: The tune says snack, not stretch.

MAI: Keep breathing. We will wait until the jingle ends.

[THE JINGLE ENDS. THE STRETCH CONTINUES AT THE SAME PACE.]

RAL: We have thirty seconds left in the segment.

MAI: Roll your shoulders gently. Breathe in, then out.

JEN: Can we speed it up? The station clock is running.

MAI: The stretch is not a race.

[JEN CUES A FAST DRUM BEAT. MAI STOPS MOVING.]

MAI: The pace changed. Please bring the music down.

[RAL LOWERS THE BEAT TO A SLOW RHYTHM.]

ODO: I am listening while I stretch. The music is easier now.

### Scene Two: The Ad Break

[THE SPONSOR JINGLE CUTS IN AGAIN, MID-BREATH.]

JEN: Quick Nut. Keep going.

MAI: Pause. The jingle interrupted the count.

RAL: The music bed is back on the right channel.

ODO: I thought the exercise ended.

[MAI ASKS ODO TO STAY IN THE COMFORTABLE RANGE OF MOTION. JEN CUES THE AD MUSIC AGAIN.]

MAI: We will pause while the jingle plays.

ODO: Should I hold the stretch?

MAI: No. Relax your arms.

[THE MUSIC STOPS. MAI REPEATS THE GENTLE SHOULDER ROLL.]

RAL: The break changed the timing.

JEN: The sponsor slot has ended.

MAI: Then we can continue without rushing.

JEN: We need the sponsor read.

MAI: We can finish the stretch, then say the sponsor name.

JEN: The sponsor bought the first two minutes.

RAL: The stretch has used thirty seconds of those minutes.

MAI: The listener has used the other ninety waiting through the tune.

ODO: My shoulders have been up by my ears since the third jingle.

MAI: Let them rest. No one has to match the music.

[JEN CUES A LITTLE BELL. RAL COVERS THE SPONSOR BUTTON WITH A PAPER CUP.]

RAL: The bell is our quiet cue. It does not need a chorus.

JEN: I can say the product name at speaking volume.

MAI: After the last movement.

[THE MUSIC RETURNS. MAI GUIDES A SIDE REACH AND A GENTLE WRIST SHAKE.]

MAI: Hold for two breaths. Switch sides.

[JEN STARTS A COUNTDOWN FROM TEN. IT ENDS BEFORE MAI FINISHES THE SECOND SIDE.] 

JEN: The show is over.

MAI: The stretch is not.

### Scene Three: Complete the Set

[ODO CALLS BACK.]

ODO: I missed the second side because of the jingle.

RAL: We can repeat that part once.

MAI: No one needs to force a stretch. We will do the other side at the same pace.

[JEN'S HAND HOVERS OVER THE SPONSOR BUTTON. RAL MOVES IT OUT OF REACH.]

[JEN TRIES TO CUE A FINAL JINGLE DURING THE WRIST SHAKE. MAI HOLDS HER POSITION UNTIL THE MUSIC STOPS.]

MAI: We are not done yet.

ODO: I am still following.

RAL: The timer has two minutes remaining.

JEN: I thought the program was five minutes.

RAL: It is. The sponsor break does not count as exercise time.

MAI: Breathe in, then out. Let your shoulders rest.

[THE MUSIC FADES AFTER THE LAST COUNT.]

[ODO SHAKES OUT HIS ARMS. JEN STARTS A JINGLE, THEN STOPS IT BEFORE THE LAST COUNT.]

JEN: The sponsor can wait.

ODO: The jingle was longer than my left side.

MAI: Then let both sides have the same quiet finish.

MAI: We are clear to end.

RAL: The timer has reached five minutes.

JEN: Quick Nut supports Five-Minute Stretch.

ODO: Now the message is after the exercise.

### Tag

[SFX: ONE QUIET CHIME.]

MAI: That was the full set.

RAL: The timer agrees.

JEN: The jingle can wait for the next show.
