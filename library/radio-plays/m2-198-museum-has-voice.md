# The Museum Has a Voice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio guides that turn every object label into a grand, overlong narration*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
GUIDE NERI, a macaque
CURATOR BALA, a gorilla
VISITOR TOMA, a langur
TECH SIV, a capuchin

[SFX: QUIET MUSEUM AMBIENCE. A FOOTSTEP ECHOES.]

NERI: Welcome to the Museum of Everyday Things. To your left, a wooden spoon. This spoon has stirred thousands of meals and witnessed the quiet evolution of soup.

BALA: It stirred two pots. We found it last month.

SIV: The audio guide is three minutes long.

TOMA: The label is six words.

### Scene One: The Spoon

[BALA SETS THE SPOON ON A DISPLAY STAND.]

NERI: This humble instrument belongs to a tradition of cooking that reaches back through generations.

BALA: It came from the staff kitchen.

TOMA: Is it part of the collection?

BALA: It was donated with the pot.

[SIV PLAYS THE GUIDE THROUGH A SMALL SPEAKER. IT HAS A DRAMATIC STRING BED.]

TOMA: I cannot hear the guide over the music.

SIV: The music makes it important.

BALA: The spoon is important enough to label clearly.

[TOMA MOVES TO A DISPLAY OF A CERAMIC CUP.]

NERI: This cup traveled across continents before it reached our hands.

BALA: It was made across town and donated by the maker.

TOMA: Can I hear the maker's name?

SIV: The guide is still talking about the journey.

[BALA HANDS TOMA THE SHORT LABEL. IT READS THE MAKER'S NAME, THE MATERIAL, AND THE YEAR.]

TOMA: That is who made it.

NERI: The long version is still in the headphones.

[TOMA MOVES TO A DISPLAY CASE WITH A PAIR OF BOOTS. SIV PLAYS A FOOTSTEP RECORDING; IT SOUNDS LIKE SOMEONE RUNNING UP THE STAIRS.] 

TOMA: The boots are on a shelf.

BALA: They belonged to the museum's first night guard.

NERI: He patrolled the halls for twenty years.

BALA: He worked here for six months. Then the alarm system was installed.

[SIV STOPS THE FOOTSTEP LOOP. THE ROOM IS QUIET AGAIN.] 

TOMA: The short label tells us more.

[SIV LOWERS THE MUSIC. A DISPLAY LIGHT CLICKS OFF.] 

### Scene Two: The Chair

[FOOTSTEPS MOVE TO A WOODEN CHAIR.]

NERI: This chair carries the weight of every visitor who has ever waited.

BALA: It is a reproduction. The original is in storage.

TOMA: Can I sit on it?

BALA: Yes.

[TOMA SITS. THE CHAIR SQUEAKS. SIV RECORDS THE SOUND AND ADDS IT TO THE GUIDE.] 

NERI: The chair speaks of time.

TOMA: It speaks of a loose hinge.

[BALA TIGHTENS THE HINGE. THE CHAIR STOPS SQUEAKING.] 

SIV: The recorded squeak is now the only squeak.

[THEY WALK TO A DISPLAY OF A BRASS KEY. NERI BEGINS A STORY ABOUT A SECRET PASSAGE. BALA OPENS THE CASE AND READS THE CARD.]

BALA: It opens the back door to the loading dock.

TOMA: Does the guide say where the key is used?

SIV: It is after the story about the passage.

[NERI FAST-FORWARDS THE AUDIO. THE WORD "LOADING DOCK" PLAYS AT DOUBLE SPEED AND SOUNDS LIKE A SECRET CODE.]

NERI: The key opens the loading dock.

[THE HEADPHONES PLAY THE SHORT LABEL WITHOUT MUSIC.]

### Scene Three: The Label

[BALA HANDS NERI A SHORT LABEL: "WOODEN SPOON, STAFF KITCHEN, 2025."]

NERI: I can read this in one sentence.

SIV: The guide needs a dramatic finish.

TOMA: Let me hear the objects without narration.

[THE MUSIC AND GUIDE TURN OFF. A SPOON TAPS A POT. A CHAIR MOVES. THE MUSEUM ROOM TONE HOLDS.] 

NERI: We have the sound of the museum.

BALA: That is enough for this room.

[SIV EXPORTS THE SHORT GUIDE. IT PLAYS THE LABEL, THEN THE SPOON TAP.]

### Tag

[SFX: A SPOON TAPS A POT ONCE.]

TOMA: I heard the exhibit.

NERI: And the sentence.
