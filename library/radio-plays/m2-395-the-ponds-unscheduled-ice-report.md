# The Pond's Unscheduled Ice Report

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: winter radio updates that use a crack in pond ice as an entertainment cue while skaters need a clear closure notice*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Reporter Tavi, a macaque; ranger Poma, a capuchin; host Liri, a lemur; caller Beni, a gibbon.

[SFX: WINTER POND. ICE CREAKS. A RADIO MIC CLICKS.]

LIRI: We are live at the pond, where skaters are waiting for the ice report.

TAVI: A crack just sounded near the north bank.

POMA: No one should skate yet. The ranger checks the marked test holes first.

LIRI: The crack made a wonderful sound for our winter special.

BENI: I am at the gate. Is the pond open?

POMA: Not until I measure the ice at the marked points.

[SFX: MEASURING ROD TAPS ICE. POMA KNEELS.]

TAVI: The crack echoed across the whole pond.

POMA: The sound can travel. It does not tell me how thick the ice is.

LIRI: The festival producer has a soundboard cue for a dramatic crack. They want it under the ice report.

POMA: Do not play a crack while families are deciding whether to step onto the pond.

TAVI: I will take the cue file off the live board.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICKS. A FILE DELETES FROM THE PLAYLIST.]

BENI: The sign is visible from the gate now. People are turning back toward the warming hut.

POMA: Good. I will read each marked test point into the log. The number at the north edge is the reason for the closure, not the sound we heard.

LIRI: We can report the ice creak as something heard, but not as a test result.

POMA: Exactly. The gauge and marked holes tell us what the ice measured.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES THE LAST MEASUREMENT. LOGBOOK CLOSES.]

TAVI: We have the measurements, sign and gate in the same report.

LIRI: The audience wants to hear it again.

POMA: We will not stage another crack. Keep people off the ice.

[SFX: GATE CHAIN RATTLES. A SIGN FLAPS IN WIND.]

BENI: The sign says "winter fun begins today."

TAVI: That is an old festival sign. The closure board is behind it.

POMA: Move the festival sign so the closure board is visible.

[SFX: SIGN LIFTS, SNAPS INTO A DIFFERENT POST. CHAIN CLOSES.]

LIRI: The sign says "closed until ranger check."

BENI: I can read that now.

POMA: First test point is four inches. The second, by the north bank, is three and a half.

TAVI: Is that enough?

POMA: Not at the north bank. I need the whole marked area checked before I can make a decision.

[SFX: ROD TAPS TWO MORE MARKED POINTS. ICE CREAKS ONCE, SOFTLY.]

LIRI: The sound effect team says they can replay the crack under your measurement.

POMA: Don't. We need the live sound for the test, not a recording loop.

TAVI: The water is moving under the edge.

POMA: That means the north bank stays closed. The center is thicker, but the pond is not open as one surface.

BENI: Can skaters use the center?

POMA: No. The access path crosses the thin edge. We keep the whole pond closed until conditions change.

LIRI: That is not the opening the festival ad promised.

POMA: The ad was made before the test.

TAVI: We should record the closure and the reason.

LIRI: The pond is closed today. The north bank measured three and a half inches, and the center cannot be reached safely.

[SFX: PAPER NOTICE SLIDES UNDER GATE CLIP.]

BENI: I can tell the people at the entrance.

POMA: Tell them the warming hut is open, but the ice is not.

LIRI: We can use the crack sound as a cue for the sign change.

POMA: No. The sign changes because the measurement says closed.

[SFX: SIGN CLIP CLICKS. CHAINS RATTLE.]

TAVI: The crew is leaving the ice alone.

LIRI: The winter special can report the test, not turn the crack into a game.

POMA: I will test again tomorrow at the marked holes.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF. WIND MOVES OVER THE CLOSED POND.]
