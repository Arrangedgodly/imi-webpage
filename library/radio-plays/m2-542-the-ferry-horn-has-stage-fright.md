# The Ferry Horn Has Stage Fright

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: backstage documentaries that turn a working ferry signal into a nervous performer*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Mara, a macaque; deckhand Pip, a capuchin; sound director Vex, an orangutan; mechanic Odo, a gibbon; dispatcher Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Ferry terminal booth. Low engine idle, water against dock.

MARA: We are broadcasting the morning ferry departure. The horn has not sounded yet; the boat is still tied to the dock.

VEX: Our lead performer is silent. The ferry horn has stage fright.

PIP: The horn is controlled from the bridge. I have not pressed the signal switch.

ODO: I checked the horn yesterday. The air line and switch worked during the test.

NARI, over radio: Dock is clear. One passenger is waiting at the marked gate.

VEX: Perhaps the horn needs applause.

MARA: It needs a departure signal only when the ferry is ready to leave. The passengers should not be startled by a rehearsal.

Pip looks through the terminal window. A rope still connects the bow to the dock cleat.

PIP: We are not ready. The bow line is attached.

VEX: The actor refuses its cue.

ODO: A line is secured. No signal should be given until the deck is clear and the crew calls ready.

SFX: Radio click.

NARI: Passenger is through the gate. I am at the ramp, checking that the wheelchair lift is folded and latched.

MARA: Thank you. We will wait for your clear call.

VEX: The silence builds tension.

PIP: The silence is a safety check.

Odo asks Pip to repeat the horn test from yesterday. Mara stops him.

MARA: No test while the ferry is boarding. The mechanic can check the pressure gauge at the dock after this trip.

ODO: The gauge reads in range. The scheduled maintenance was already logged; there is no reason to sound it now.

VEX: The performer is healthy but refuses to perform.

NARI: Ramp is clear. Passenger seated, gate latched, and the ferry is ready to depart.

PIP: Bow line off. Stern line off. I have both lines secured aboard.

MARA: Dispatcher, confirm the crossing is clear.

VEX: I found a recording of the horn from last season. Could we play it beneath the departure?

MARA: No. A recorded horn could be mistaken for a live signal.

ODO: The broadcast console is connected to the terminal speakers. Passengers might hear it as a departure cue.

PIP: The actual horn is only for a vessel or hazard. Keep the mic on the crew channel.

VEX: We could label it "dramatic memory."

MARA: It would still sound like a signal. Delete it from the playlist.

SFX: Console button; short confirmation beep.

ODO: Recording removed. The dock channel is clear.

VEX: The red recording light is on. I can at least give the horn a silent close-up.

MARA: Keep the camera on the ramp and the passenger. The light is not permission to add a signal.

NARI: Passenger is seated and has asked whether the horn means we are leaving. I told them the crew will announce departure plainly.

PIP: I will make that announcement once the captain confirms.

VEX: The horn has lost its cue and the host has lost his close-up.

MARA: The passenger gets a clear instruction. That is the point of the broadcast.

NARI: Crossing is clear. No boat in the channel.

SFX: Engine rises softly; ferry eases away.

VEX: And now, the horn!

PIP: We only sound it if the captain needs to signal another vessel. There is no vessel in the channel.

VEX: So it gets no solo?

MARA: It remains silent because the crossing is clear. The departure is the engine and water, no recorded cue.

ODO: The passenger is seated, both lines are aboard, and the engine is underway. I will inspect the air line after docking, with the ferry secured.

SFX: Engine hum and water fade under music; no horn.

VEX: Our backstage special ends without its star solo.

MARA: The crossing gets a clear channel.

PIP: It is a horn, Vex. It has no calendar.
