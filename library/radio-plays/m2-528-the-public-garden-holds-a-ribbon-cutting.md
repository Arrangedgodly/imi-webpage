# The Public Garden Holds a Ribbon Cutting

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local radio ceremony derailed by ceremonial scissors and actual gardening*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Reporter Mara, a macaque; host Vex, an orangutan; groundskeeper Seli, a lemur; volunteer Pip, a capuchin; clerk Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Garden gate. Crowd murmurs; a pair of scissors opens and closes.

VEX: We are live at the grand opening of the east path. The ribbon is stretched across the entrance, and our honored guests are ready.

SELI: The path isn't open yet. The stone at the third step is loose, and we marked it with a cone.

MARA: I can see the cone behind the ribbon. Why is the ribbon tied to the two rails across the path?

ODO: The printed event plan says to make a ceremonial barrier.

PIP: The ribbon is blocking the route the crew needs to reach the loose stone.

VEX: We can cut it dramatically. Then the path can be repaired in front of the audience.

SELI: People should not walk on a loose step while we repair it. Please keep the ribbon in place and move it to the lawn-side posts.

MARA: We can move the ceremony to the flat lawn, then cut the ribbon after the repair inspection.

VEX: That would mean the ribbon cutting happens after the opening.

ODO: The path opens when it is safe to walk on, not when we cut fabric.

SFX: Scissors close with a snip; no ribbon falls.

PIP: Vex cut the loose end, not the ribbon. It is still tied to the rails.

VEX: The scissors are ceremonial. They have never cut anything.

SELI: I'll untie the fabric. Mara, hold the post so it doesn't swing into the path.

SFX: Knot loosens. Ribbon pulls free.

MARA: The lawn-side posts are clear. The cone still marks the step.

SELI: The repair stone is ready. I will test it with the hand weight before anyone walks through.

SFX: Small weight lands on stone; solid tap.

SELI: It is firm now. I'll remove the cone and check the edge once more.

ODO: The route is clear from the lawn to the gate.

VEX: We can finally make the announcement.

MARA: Announce that the repair is finished and invite people to walk slowly. The ribbon can mark the lawn entrance after the route is open.

PIP: I can tie the ribbon to the short posts, leaving the walkway unobstructed.

SFX: Fabric ties around posts; scissors snip the loose ends cleanly.

VEX: The ribbon is cut, the path is open, and nobody has stepped on the loose stone.

SELI: Correct. Please keep to the path; the lawn is still soft from watering.

MARA: Our live report: east path open after repair, ribbon relocated, ceremonial scissors finally useful.

VEX: Can I cut a second ribbon for the repaired step?

SELI: No. The step has already been through enough.

ODO: The event plan is updated. Next time, the repair inspection comes before the ceremony.

MARA: That is a practical note. The joke is that the scissors needed a rehearsal before their first job.

VEX: They did very well under pressure.


ODO: Before we reopen the gate, I want to verify the route from the lawn. No one should have to step around the ribbon or the repair tools.

SELI: Tools are in the cart. The stone is firm under the hand weight, and the cone is back in the shed.

MARA: I'll walk the path once and stop before the wet lawn edge.

SFX: Footsteps on stone. Three even taps, then a pause.

MARA: The repaired step is level with its neighbor. The path reaches the gate without crossing the grass.

PIP: The ribbon posts are outside the walking line. The ends are tied short so they won't flap across the opening.

VEX: Now the ceremony has a clear entrance. Could I invite the crowd forward?

SELI: Ask them to walk, not rush.

VEX: The east path is open. Please enter slowly and stay on the stone.

SFX: A few careful footsteps move past the microphone.
