# The Orchard Lap Final

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live local race commentary, sponsor breaks, and play-by-play announcers who describe a fruit-picking lap like a championship finish*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
COMMENTATOR VEK, a capuchin
CO-COMMENTATOR SULA, a macaque
RACE MARSHAL BRI, a gibbon
RUNNER PEL, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: CROWD, STARTING BELL.]

VEK: Welcome to the Orchard Lap Final. The runners must circle the apple trees, pass the water barrel, and cross the chalk line without carrying fruit.

SULA: A simple course, except for the low branches and the sponsor tent.

BRI: Racers, remember: this is a race, not a harvest.

VEK: The bell rings, and they are off.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRASS.]

SULA: Pel takes the inside path. A bold choice.

VEK: The inside path is shorter.

SULA: Every advantage matters at this level.

BRI: Pel is approaching the low branch. Duck!

[SFX: BRANCH RUSTLES.]

VEK: Pel has ducked under the branch without losing speed.

SULA: The branch has kept one leaf.

VEK: A small victory for the tree.

SULA: Pel is at the water barrel. The rules allow a sip but not a swim.

[SFX: CUP DIPS, WATER SPLASHES.]

VEK: Pel has spilled half the cup.

SULA: Perhaps to lighten the load.

BRI: There is no load. The racers are carrying nothing.

VEK: A second runner has reached the barrel and is inspecting the cup.

BRI: That runner is a volunteer. She is refilling it.

SULA: The competitors may drink, but only at the barrel. The sponsor tent is not a hydration station.

VEK: The rules are being enforced at the exact location where a cup is available.

BRI: And no one may take an apple from the trees, even if it falls.

[SFX: APPLE DROPS INTO GRASS.]

VEK: A fruit has entered the course.

SULA: It is outside the marked path.

BRI: Leave it there until the race ends.

VEK: The sponsor tent is next. Listeners can enjoy Orchard Spark, the drink that is not part of the race.

SULA: Pel is passing the tent.

VEK: A volunteer is offering a sample.

BRI: No refreshments during the lap.

[SFX: PAPER CUP SQUEEZES, SPLASH.]

SULA: Pel has refused the sample and still has a dry sleeve.

VEK: The refusal is fast. The sleeve is faster.

SULA: Second turn now. Pel has taken the outside route.

VEK: Why leave the inside path?

SULA: A branch is blocking it.

BRI: That branch is the finish ribbon.

VEK: The finish ribbon is on the wrong tree.

BRI: We moved the finish line after a tractor crossed the old one.

SULA: The runners are circling again. The course has gained an unexpected extra lap.

VEK: Pel is asking the marshal for directions.

BRI: Follow the white chalk to the shed. I am marking the finish line now.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOP. CHALK SCRAPES.]

SULA: The marshal has drawn a clear line beside the shed.

VEK: We need an immediate course correction. Pel is now walking toward the water barrel.

SULA: A sensible route to the nearest clear path.

BRI: The finish line is beside the shed. The lap ends there.

VEK: Pel has accelerated. The crowd is rising.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS QUICKEN, CROWD CHEERS.]

SULA: Pel crosses the line first.

BRI: One lap complete. The chalk line is visible, and Pel has crossed it.

VEK: The official time is twelve minutes, including the extra turn.

SULA: Was the extra turn part of the race?

BRI: It is now part of the result.

PEL: I would like the prize cup.

VEK: The winner receives a cup filled with Orchard Spark.

PEL: Can I drink it after I stop running?

BRI: Yes. The race is over.

[SFX: CUP SET DOWN. PEL BREATHES.]

SULA: Pel has waited until the finish before taking the sample.

VEK: A careful athlete and a decisive winner.

BRI: We have moved the ribbon to the shed. It will stay there for the next race.

VEK: The Orchard Lap Final is complete. The apples remain on the trees, the cup is being enjoyed, and no one is taking another lap.

[SFX: CROWD FADES, BELL RINGS ONCE.]
