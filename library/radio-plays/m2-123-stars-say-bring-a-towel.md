# The Stars Say Bring a Towel

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio horoscope call-ins, mystical vagueness, and a fortune host whose predictions are explained by ordinary monkey habits*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Vela, a lemur astrologer-host; Tibo, a capuchin producer; Anya, an orangutan caller; and Ro, a macaque caller.*

[SFX: Celestial synth, small chimes.]

VELA: Welcome to Night Sky Advice. The stars offer guidance, not guarantees. Tonight's first sign is the Traveling Fig.

TIBO: There is no Traveling Fig in the official chart.

VELA: It appears when a fig is carried from one place to another.

TIBO: That's every fig.

VELA: It is a very active sign. Anya, what question brings you to the stars?

ANYA: I keep arriving late to the branch meeting.

VELA: The moon suggests a delay near a comfortable seat.

ANYA: The meeting is under my favorite branch.

TIBO: That is also where her hammock is.

VELA: The stars advise you to set a bell before resting.

ANYA: I have a bell. I ring it when I arrive.

VELA: Then ring it before.

TIBO: The chart is precise tonight.

[SFX: Chimes.]

VELA: Ro, what are you hoping to learn?

RO: Whether I should bring a towel to the creek tomorrow.

VELA: The constellation of the Wet Leaf suggests a damp crossing.

RO: It is a creek.

VELA: The stars are aligning with the water.

TIBO: This is a creek forecast, not astrology.

VELA: The stars guide the towel. The creek supplies the water.

RO: I will bring one.

VELA: Excellent. Your stars are now prepared.

TIBO: A caller asks if the moon is favorable for peeling a banana.

VELA: The banana is ruled by the crescent moon.

TIBO: Why?

VELA: It curves.

TIBO: That is geometry.

VELA: The stars use geometry.

ANYA: If I peel it before the meeting, will I be late?

VELA: The chart says peel while walking.

TIBO: That is unsafe on a branch.

VELA: Then sit down first.

ANYA: If I sit down, I will fall asleep.

VELA: The moon suggests setting a bell.

TIBO: We have returned to the first prediction.

VELA: Astrology is a circle.

[SFX: The chimes begin to wobble.]

RO: The stars are making that sound?

TIBO: The chime stand is loose.

VELA: A rare alignment between the heavens and our equipment.

TIBO: I'll tighten it.

VELA: Do not disturb the celestial pattern.

TIBO: I'm disturbing a bolt.

[SFX: Wrench turns; chimes settle into tune.]

VELA: The sky is calm again. Our final caller asks whether an old friend will return.

RO: That's me again. I want to know if my towel will come back from the creek.

VELA: The stars indicate a damp object near a flat rock.

RO: I left it on a flat rock.

TIBO: There is a chance it is still there.

VELA: The stars confirm: bring a second towel next time.

RO: That is useful.

VELA: The night sky supports practical preparation.

ANYA: I have rung my bell. I am on time for the meeting.

TIBO: The meeting began an hour ago.

ANYA: I was resting beside the bell.

VELA: The stars predicted the seat.

[SFX: Soft celestial theme.]

VELA: This has been Night Sky Advice. Bring a towel near water, a bell before a nap, and a chart only if you enjoy circles.

TIBO: Our studio chart has one more star than the published sky.

VELA: Where did it come from?

TIBO: It's the red light on our recording booth.

VELA: It predicts that we will finish this broadcast.

TIBO: It always turns off after we finish.

VELA: Then the prediction is excellent.

ANYA: I made it to the meeting. Everyone is leaving now.

VELA: The stars indicate you can help stack the chairs.

TIBO: That's the cleanup chart.

[SFX: Chairs fold; the chimes are packed away.]

[SFX: Chimes ring once; a hammock creaks.]
