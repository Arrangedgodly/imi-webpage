# The Last Ferry Bell Rehearsal

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public service radio dramas that rehearse emergency procedures so often the actors trigger a real departure sequence*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Director Halla, a gibbon; sound operator Keno, a macaque; ferry keeper Bira, a capuchin; actor Nix, a lemur.

[SFX: FERRY TERMINAL AFTER HOURS. RAIN AGAINST WINDOWS. A BELL SOUNDS TWICE.]

HALLA: Stop rehearsal. That was the emergency departure bell, not our prop bell.

KENO: The sound board is unplugged. The real bell is in the terminal.

BIRA: The harbor master pressed it. The river is rising and the ferry must leave the lower berth.

NIX: Then our rehearsal is now the actual announcement.

HALLA: We need to clear the gangway and get passengers to the upper waiting room. No scripted flourish.

KENO: I can open the public address mic.

BIRA: I have the dock radio. The ferry crew has the line.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS. WATER LAPS HIGHER AGAINST WOOD.]

BIRA: Ferry crew, this is Bira. Confirm the lower berth is clear.

NIX: I can read the notice.

HALLA: Read only what is posted. Don't improvise.

NIX: "The lower landing is closing. Passengers move to the upper waiting room. Do not use the floating steps."

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS HURRY UP WOODEN STAIRS. A DOOR OPENS.]

KENO: The public address mic is live, but the old rehearsal track is queued behind it.

HALLA: Disable the track. Keep the live mic.

[SFX: BUTTONS CLICK. A TAPE MOTOR STARTS BRIEFLY, THEN STOPS.]

KENO: It is off.

NIX: I can hear the old recording start underneath me.

HALLA: Is it still playing?

KENO: It stopped. That was the tape's motor winding down.

BIRA: I hear a scrape at the lower landing. Something is moving.

[SFX: A FLOATING STEP SCRAPES AGAINST A PILING. WATER SPLASHES.]

HALLA: Nobody go down there.

BIRA: The ferry crew is already at the upper gangway. I will ask them to raise it from their side.

[SFX: RADIO CLICK.]

BIRA: Crew, raise the upper gangway and lock it. Keep the lower steps clear.

NIX: The notice says upper room. Should I say the ferry is canceled?

BIRA: No. It is changing berths. The harbor master will announce the next crossing.

HALLA: Tell people where to wait, not what we guess will happen.

[SFX: PA MIC CLICKS ON.]

NIX: Attention, passengers. The lower landing is closed because the river has risen. Please remain in the upper waiting room. The crew is securing the upper gangway. We will announce the next crossing when the harbor master confirms it.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOP. A CROWD SETTLES. RAIN CONTINUES.]

KENO: The rehearsal track is not in the queue anymore.

HALLA: Good. No one needs to hear our practice instructions while they are moving.

BIRA: Crew reports the upper gangway is locked. The ferry is shifting to the high berth.

NIX: Do I announce that?

BIRA: Wait until the harbor master confirms it.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC, THEN A CLEAR VOICE OVER RADIO: HIGH BERTH SECURE. NEXT CROSSING AT SIX.]

BIRA: Confirmed. Next crossing is at six from the high berth.

HALLA: Now you can announce it.

[SFX: PA MIC CLICKS ON.]

NIX: The high berth is secure. The next crossing is at six. Please use the upper gangway and keep the lower landing clear.

KENO: The bell has stopped.

BIRA: The ferry is secured. Nobody is on the lower steps.

HALLA: We can end rehearsal. The real instructions were clear without our dramatic music.

NIX: What about the scripted scene where I shout, "The river has taken the bell"?

BIRA: The river has not taken the bell.

KENO: It is still mounted above the door.

[SFX: BELL SOUNDS ONCE, A SMALL TEST TONE. THE ROOM IS QUIET.]

HALLA: That is the only bell cue we need.



KENO: The old track is saved on the rehearsal cart, not in the live PA. I will unplug that cart so it cannot start again by accident.

[SFX: POWER CABLE LIFTS FROM SOCKET. REEL MOTOR COASTS TO A STOP.]

HALLA: Good. We will not rehearse an alarm over an actual departure.

NIX: The scripted version has a crowd rushing to the door.

BIRA: The real crowd is staying where they were told. Leave that good ending alone.

NIX: I will cross out the fake rush cue.

[SFX: PENCIL SCRATCHES THROUGH A STAGE DIRECTION. THE PAPER IS FOLDED.]

HALLA: The show can wait until the ferry is safe and the water goes down.

BIRA: The crew is clear. The river is still rising, so the lower berth stays closed.

[SFX: MIC CLICKS OFF. RAIN TAPS THE WINDOW; WATER LAPS BELOW THE HIGH BERTH.]
