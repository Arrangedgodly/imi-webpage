# The Fire Lookout's Moving Shadow

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: emergency news simulcasts that dress a routine observation in alarm graphics before verifying the headline*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Lookout Ena, a gibbon; host Riko, a macaque; dispatcher Sema, a lemur; ranger Tavi, a capuchin; studio dog Nib, a beagle.

[SFX: STUDIO. RADIO MIC OPENS. A SMALL DOG SNORES UNDER THE DESK.]

RIKO: Fire Watch Live is on radio and video tonight. Our sponsor supplied a red banner for any breaking forest alert.

SEMA: The banner says "SMOKE ON NORTH RIDGE." No alert has been issued.

ENA: I can see a dark patch moving across the north slope. It follows the cloud edge. There is no smoke column.

RIKO: The headline writer has already loaded the banner.

TAVI: I am at the trail lookout. The ridge is visible between two trees, and I see no flame or plume.

RIKO: Possible smoke on the north ridge!

ENA: Say "cloud shadow under observation." The air is clear here.

[SFX: A KEYBOARD CHIRPS. THE VIDEO LOWER-THIRD PLAYS A CHEERY SPONSOR JINGLE.]

SEMA: The graphic and the dispatch system are separate. The graphic is on the stream. The alert draft is on my terminal.

RIKO: The sponsor wants the red banner to stay up until the weather break.

ENA: Then the stream would show a fire while the lookout says cloud.

RIKO: On radio, we have no banner. We can hear the jingle.

[SFX: NIB WAKES AND BARKS TWICE AT THE JINGLE. A WATER BOWL CLINKS.]

SEMA: Nib thinks the jingle is the doorbell.

TAVI: I can see the cloud edge moving east. The darker patch is lifting from the trees.

RIKO: I have to correct the on-air line. I said "possible smoke" a moment ago. That was wrong. The lookout reports a moving cloud shadow and no visible smoke.

SEMA: The dispatch terminal has the prepared smoke alert. I am canceling that draft now. No crew will be sent.

[SFX: ONE KEY PRESS. THE ALERT TERMINAL GIVES A SINGLE CONFIRMATION BEEP.]

ENA: Thank you. I can still see the ridge. The cloud is moving. Nothing is burning.

RIKO: Producer, replace the red banner with the observation. Keep the sponsor logo under the weather label.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. VIDEO SYSTEM CHIMES.]

SEMA: The stream now reads "Cloud shadow, north ridge. No smoke confirmed." The dispatch draft remains canceled.

SEMA: The weather label is saved. The dispatch draft stays canceled.

[SFX: A SOFT SAVE TONE. NO ALARM SOUNDS.]

RIKO: The sponsor asks us to keep the word "fire" in the headline because it raises clicks.

ENA: There is no fire to report.

RIKO: Then I will say it on air: no fire was seen. The dark patch moved with the cloud and has cleared the north trees.

TAVI: The path is open. I have reached the gate.

SEMA: The dispatch screen stays canceled while the video title updates.

RIKO: The sponsor has sent a new title: "ClearSpring's Fire Cloud."

ENA: That isn't a fire cloud. It is a cloud crossing the ridge.

RIKO: The sponsor would like the name to sound urgent.

TAVI: A cloud does not need a name to move.

[SFX: NIB BARKS ONCE. THE SPONSOR JINGLE STARTS, THEN STOPS WHEN A BUTTON IS PRESSED.]

SEMA: Nib has vetoed the jingle.

RIKO: The station dog has no editorial authority.

SEMA: He has more than the sponsor's banner.

RIKO: Fine. The weather segment gets "A Cloud Passed Over the North Ridge." ClearSpring can sponsor the forecast after the report.

ENA: The ridge is bright again. I can see the tree line.

TAVI: I am walking back along the marked trail. No fire, no dispatch, no reason to close the path.

SEMA: The alert queue is empty. The graphic is saved as a weather observation, not a warning.

RIKO: Fire Watch Live ends with the confirmed report: one cloud shadow, no smoke, and one dog unimpressed by our theme song.

[SFX: NIB SNORES AGAIN. STUDIO MIC CLICKS OFF; THE VIDEO JINGLE STAYS MUTED.]
