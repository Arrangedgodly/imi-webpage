# Wrong Coast, Right Call

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in travel programs, exoticizing narration, and remote correspondents who keep getting patched to the wrong location*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A travel host tries to interview a ferry guide on the western coast, but a switchboard mistake keeps sending her to a monkey at the eastern bus depot.

Cast: Host Pavi, a macaque; ferry guide Reni, an orangutan; caller Mako, a capuchin; switchboard operator Joss, a gibbon; producer Teli, a baboon.

[SFX: RADIO THEME, SEAGULLS, THEN A BUS BRAKE.]

PAVI: From the western coast, we bring you the first ferry across Mirror Bay.

MAKO: I am at the east bus depot.

PAVI: Our ferry correspondent is already on the line.

MAKO: I am waiting for the number six.

JOSS: That is the caller line, not the ferry line.

PAVI: We will keep both journeys moving.

ACT ONE

Pavi introduces Reni, a ferry guide, who is supposed to be standing near the west pier. The switchboard connects Mako, who is actually at the east bus depot with a paper map. Reni's voice can be heard faintly in the background, saying she is at the pier. Joss attempts to patch her through; instead, the bus station's departure board chimes.

PAVI: What is the wind like on the water?

MAKO: There is no water here. There is a bench and a snack cart.

PAVI: How far is the crossing?

MAKO: Three stops, if the bus is on time.

Teli asks Joss to check the labels. Two buttons say WEST: one for the coast, one for the west-side bus shelter. Joss says the storm damaged the old board. Pavi asks Reni for the ferry route.

RENI: The crossing takes twelve minutes, and the north pier is closed today.

MAKO: My bus is marked north pier.

RENI: Then it is going to the neighborhood, not the ferry landing.

ACT TWO

Pavi tries a dramatic travel introduction: "Beyond the blue horizon, a vessel carries visitors into the unknown." Reni says the ferry crosses a sheltered bay between two named piers. Mako says he is standing by a mural of a blue horizon. Pavi asks if that is the water view. Mako says it is a painted whale on the bus station wall.

Teli brings up a map. The East Depot's "West Pier" is a bus route, not the ferry pier. The soundboard plays a bus engine under Reni's description of the tide.

RENI: The tide is low, so the ferry is loading from the lower ramp.

MAKO: My bus has a high step.

PAVI: We have two ramps.

JOSS: We have two locations.

Mako boards the bus, and his microphone picks up the doors closing. Pavi narrates his departure as a voyage. Mako corrects her: the bus moved one block, then stopped for a red light. Reni laughs, and the ferry horn sounds over the call. Joss manages to put her on the correct line, but now Reni hears Mako's bus announcement in her earpiece.

ACT TWO-B

Teli moves the two guests to separate channels. Joss asks Reni to name the pier. She says South Landing, beside the green shed. Mako is at the East Depot, three miles away. Pavi repeats each location in the correct order. The map graphic now shows a bus route in brown and the ferry route in blue.

PAVI: Reni, where should a visitor check ferry status?

RENI: At the harbor board beside the green shed. It shows departures and tide closures.

PAVI: Mako, where should a bus traveler check the route?

MAKO: At the depot sign, which says East Depot, not West Pier. I see it now.

JOSS: The switchboard label is wrong. I have crossed out the duplicate.

Teli gives Pavi the map. She traces the bus line with one finger, then the ferry route with the other. No music plays. Mako finds his correct bus bay and steps onto the bus. Reni confirms the ferry has not departed yet; the crew is still loading bicycles.

MAKO: I am aboard the right bus.

RENI: I am still on the right pier.

PAVI: And we are on the right call.

ACT THREE

The ferry horn sounds once. Reni tells listeners the crossing is calm and the green shed is easy to spot. Mako reports the bus is heading east, away from the coast. Pavi says the program has two correct journeys and one bad switchboard label. Joss peels the old label off and replaces it with "WEST-SIDE BUS."

PAVI: Our scenic sound bed will now be the actual ferry horn.

Teli plays the recording from Reni's line. It is a short, low call. The bus station chime no longer overlays it. Mako reaches his stop and says the mural whale is much larger than expected. Pavi does not describe it as a real whale. She signs off with both guests in their correct places.

PAVI: Separate labels are better.
