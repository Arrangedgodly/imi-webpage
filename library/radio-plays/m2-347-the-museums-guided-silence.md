# The Museum's Guided Silence

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio tours that mistake a visitor's pause for a cue to invent interpretation*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Guide Aro, a gibbon; technician Simi, a macaque; visitor Ketu, a lemur; director Nala, a capuchin; recorded artist Miri Vale, a gibbon.

[SFX: QUIET MUSEUM GALLERY. SOFT SHOES ON STONE. A HANDHELD AUDIO GUIDE BEEPS.]

ARO: Stop at the blue painting. This work is called Rain Ladder, by Miri Vale. Please press one to hear the artist's note.

KETU: I did.

[SFX: BUTTON BEEP. NOTHING. FIVE SECONDS OF QUIET.]

ARO: You are now experiencing the artist's intentional silence.

KETU: Is there supposed to be a recording?

ARO: The guide has detected a reflective pause.

NALA: Excellent. That line is in the new tour script.

SIMI: The audio file did not load. The device is waiting for the gallery server.

NALA: But the silence sounds meaningful.

KETU: I was waiting for the note.

ARO: Some artists invite us to wait.

KETU: Did Miri Vale invite us, or did the battery fail?

SIMI: Battery is full. The access point has dropped the connection.

NALA: Could the guide say, The museum invites you to listen inward?

SIMI: No. The artist's recorded note says, I painted this after watching rain run down a ladder. It is twelve seconds long.

ARO: That is less mysterious.

KETU: It is more specific.

[SFX: DEVICE BEEPS AGAIN. A DISTANT DOOR OPENS AND CLOSES.]

ARO: The guide has detected a door closing. Would you like to hear the sound's relationship to the painting?

KETU: No.

NALA: That prompt was added by the interpretation team.

SIMI: It triggers whenever the microphone hears a loud sound. We can disable it.

NALA: But the visitors like the interactive moments.

KETU: I pressed one for the painting. I don't want a theory about the door.

ARO: I can read the artist's note from my paper copy.

SIMI: The guide is meant to provide access for visitors who cannot stand with a docent.

ARO: Then I should not replace it with my memory. Let's restore the actual recording.

NALA: The server is in the next gallery, behind the sculpture.

[SFX: FOUR SLOW FOOTSTEPS. A SOFT BEEP, THEN A BRIEF BURST OF STATIC.]

SIMI: The receiver is pointing at the wall. The sculpture is blocking the signal.

NALA: The sculpture has been there for twenty years.

SIMI: The access point was moved yesterday for a donor photo. It's behind a metal case now.

KETU: Can we hear the note while you move it?

ARO: I'll read the printed label, which gives the title and date. I won't invent the audio.

NALA: The donors arrive in five minutes. Could we leave the silence as a special preview?

KETU: Then label it connection unavailable. That's honest.

SIMI: I'll move the access point back to its marked shelf. Please keep the doorway clear.

[SFX: CASE DOOR OPENS. A SMALL DEVICE LIFTS. CABLE UNPLUGS, THEN PLUGS INTO ANOTHER SOCKET. TWO BEEPS SOUND.]

SIMI: Signal is back. Testing the file now.

[SFX: GUIDE BEEPS. A RECORDED ARTIST'S VOICE PLAYS, CALM AND BRIEF: I watched rain run down a ladder outside my window. I painted the moment the water turned the rung into a river.]

KETU: That's the note.

ARO: And the quiet before it was a loading delay, not part of the painting.

NALA: Do we still call it an interactive pause?

SIMI: We call it a loading screen and tell the visitor when it happens.

NALA: That sounds like admitting the equipment is equipment.

KETU: It is equipment.

[SFX: DEVICE BEEPS. A NEW PROMPT STARTS: PLEASE WAIT WHILE THE RECORDING LOADS. THEN THE ARTIST'S NOTE PLAYS ONCE.]

ARO: The tour now says what it is doing.

NALA: It has less mystery.

KETU: The painting has enough mystery. It doesn't need the speaker to pretend it is thinking.

SIMI: The connection is stable. I have locked the access point on its shelf.

NALA: Then we will open the gallery.

ARO: Rain Ladder, Miri Vale. The artist watched rain run down a ladder. Please press one to hear her note.

[SFX: KETU PRESSES ONE. THE NOTE PLAYS CLEARLY. FOOTSTEPS MOVE TO THE NEXT WORK; NO PROMPT INTERRUPTS.]
