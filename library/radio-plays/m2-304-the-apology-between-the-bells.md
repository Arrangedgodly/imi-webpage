# The Apology Between the Bells

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: formal public radio apologies, where station staff keep rehearsing an apology for a bell that rang during a silence*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Announcer Nara, macaque; producer Odo, baboon; bell keeper Seli, capuchin; listener Piri, gibbon.

[SFX: STUDIO TONE, BELL RINGS ONCE.]

NARA: We apologize for the bell heard during yesterday's broadcast.

ODO: It was a scheduled bell.

SELI: It was not scheduled for the silence.

PIRI: I liked the bell.

NARA: We have received one complaint.

Odo holds up a form. It has two boxes: "sound" and "silence." Piri says the bell was a sound, and the silence was still quiet afterward.

ODO: We need a formal statement.

NARA: We can say the bell was not meant to ring.

SELI: The bell always rings at noon.

NARA: The broadcast was observing one minute of silence at noon.

PIRI: The bell has its own schedule.

Odo asks whether the station should apologize to the bell. Seli says it has no feelings. Piri says the bell may be disappointed that nobody listened.

[SFX: SMALL BELL TAPS.]

NARA: Please do not ring it during the rehearsal.

SELI: I only touched the stand.

Odo writes a draft: "We regret any disturbance caused by an unplanned sound." Nara says the bell was planned, but not the timing. Seli suggests: "We regret that our silence included a bell."

PIRI: That sounds like the bell was invited.

NARA: It was not invited.

PIRI: It was installed.

Odo asks the listener to describe how the bell sounded. Piri says it was clear, brief, and not disturbing.

ODO: We still need an apology.

PIRI: Apologize to the people who wanted silence.

Nara records a new version: "We planned a quiet minute, but the village bell rang at noon. We should have started the segment at another time."

Seli says the bell cannot be moved. Odo suggests a different broadcast time. Piri says the silence can happen at 12:01.

NARA: Then we do not need an apology.

ODO: We still need to read the apology.

NARA: We can read the schedule change.

She records: "Tomorrow's quiet segment begins at 12:01, after the village bell."

[SFX: RED LIGHT CLICKS OFF.]

PIRI: That is a solution.

ODO: It is not an apology.

SELI: We did not do anything wrong.

NARA: We can still acknowledge the interruption.

Piri calls in again. She says she heard the recording and liked the bell. Odo asks if the station should include that in the statement.

PIRI: You can say some listeners enjoyed the sound.

NARA: We cannot make the apology longer than the bell.

[SFX: BELL RINGS ONCE.]

ODO: That was eight seconds.

NARA: Then the apology is eight seconds.

Seli presses the record button. The red light glows. Odo places the form beside the microphone and points to the two boxes.

ODO: Say that the bell disrupted our silence.

NARA: It interrupted it. The distinction matters.

PIRI: I listened for the silence. I heard the bell, then I heard you all argue about the bell. That part was louder.

SELI: The bell's rope is connected to the clock. I can hold it, but then the clock won't ring tomorrow.

Odo asks her to hold it for one rehearsal. Seli grips the rope. The bell gives a muffled thud inside its padded case.

ODO: Now the silence has a thud.

NARA: We are not apologizing for the clock. We are changing the segment time.

ODO: The audience expects accountability.

PIRI: Then account for the minute. It was quiet for fifty-two seconds and musical for eight.

Nara starts a new take, but Odo signals her to stop. He wants the apology to include the bell keeper. Seli says she kept the bell as scheduled; the station chose the same minute. Odo wants to say "we regret the overlap." Piri says that sounds like two shadows meeting.

NARA: What if we state the choice plainly and let the listener decide?

ODO: That is not an apology.

PIRI: It is a sentence.

Nara takes a breath and records the explanation without grand music or a solemn pause. Seli releases the rope. The bell sits silent in its case.


NARA: The bell rang. We will move tomorrow's silence to 12:01.

PIRI: Thank you.

[SFX: ONE BELL, END THEME.]
