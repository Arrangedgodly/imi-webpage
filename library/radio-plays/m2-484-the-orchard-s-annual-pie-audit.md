# The Orchard's Annual Pie Audit

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: food competitions that count presentation before taste*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Judge Vex, an orangutan; baker Mara, a macaque; clerk Odo, a gibbon; helper Pip, a capuchin; announcer Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Orchard pavilion. Plates clink. A pie tin slides across a table.

SELI: The annual orchard pie audit begins now.

MARA: I brought apple pie.

VEX: First we assess the crust's public image.

ODO: The entry card says "apple."

VEX: The pie needs a narrative.

PIP: The apples came from the tree beside the pavilion.

VEX: A compelling origin story.

MARA: Can we taste it?

ODO: The rules say weigh, measure, photograph, then taste.

SFX: Scale creaks. Tin set down.

ODO: Six pounds.

PIP: That's the tin and pie.

VEX: We will record the combined presentation weight.

MARA: The tin is not part of the dessert.

VEX: It is part of the entry.

SFX: Camera shutter clicks.

SELI: The photograph shows the top crust.

PIP: The pie is tilted. The filling is running toward the edge.

ODO: We must not disturb the display until the photograph is approved.

MARA: The filling is hot. We need to set it down flat.

VEX: The visual story is one of movement.

MARA: It's going to spill.

SFX: Plate scrapes. Pip slides a board under the pie tin.

PIP: I'll steady it.

ODO: The tilt is corrected. Now the photograph is accurate.

VEX: The audit panel gives it a strong line.

MARA: The pie smells good. Can we serve it?

VEX: We have not tested the crust's symmetry.

PIP: It is round.

ODO: One edge is half a finger wider.

VEX: That could be a signature shape.

MARA: The apples are soft. If we wait, the crust will get soggy.

SELI: The crowd is waiting.

VEX: The contest is about standards.

MARA: The standard is whether people want another slice.

SFX: Knife presses through crust. A slice lifts cleanly.

ODO: The crust is crisp.

PIP: The filling stays in place.

VEX: I will taste a sample for audit purposes.

SFX: Fork taps plate. A bite.

VEX: The apple flavor is excellent.

MARA: And the score?

VEX: It has lost points for an uneven edge, gained points for being edible, and received a special citation for feeding the audience.

PIP: The slices are different sizes.

VEX: We should weigh them before serving.

MARA: No. The first slice is for the person who steadied the tin.

PIP: That was me.

ODO: The second goes to the orchard keeper who supplied the apples.

SELI: The judge's plate is still empty.

VEX: I am waiting for the official score sheet.

MARA: It is beneath the pie. Lift the pie and the sheet comes free.

SFX: Pie tin lifts. Paper peels loose, leaving a small apple-syrup mark.

VEX: The score has acquired a glaze.

ODO: We can still read it.

PIP: You gave the taste category full marks.

VEX: I stand by the result.

MARA: Then let's stop auditing and eat before it cools.

SFX: Plates clink as slices are served.

SELI: The winner is Mara's pie.

MARA: We can cut the rest now.

ODO: The scale will be free.

TAG

ODO: The audit form asks how many servings the pie made.

MARA: Eight.

PIP: Nine if the judge counts the taste sample.

VEX: It was a professional portion.

SELI: I will record nine servings and one clean tin.

PIP: The last crumb is on the score sheet.

MARA: Move it before Vex eats the paperwork.

SFX: Paper lifts. A crumb drops onto a plate.

ODO: The tin weighs less now.

VEX: At last, an audit result with no argument.

SFX: Plates pass hand to hand.

VEX: Put my score sheet beside my slice.

PIP: It is underneath the pie.
