# The Jingle That Ate Tuesday

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio advertising auditions, focus-group revisions, and jingles that keep expanding until the product disappears*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Pella, a macaque advertising producer; Quince, a gibbon composer; Doro, a baboon client; Mina, a capuchin caller; and Riff, a langur singer.*

[SFX: Studio clock ticks. Tape clicks into record.]

PELLA: Final audition for Aha! Leaf Wipe. One sheet cleans a leaf. The spot is twelve seconds. We need a memorable jingle approved by everyone who has seen a leaf.

DORO: Never say "wipe." It sounds like cleaning. We want customers to feel a lifestyle shift.

QUINCE: How about "Aha! Leaf Wipe, wipe your leaf"?

DORO: Too literal. Can it say "Aha!" six times and never mention what it does?

RIFF: I can sing that in three notes.

PELLA: Nine seconds. Begin.

RIFF: [SINGING] Aha! Aha! Aha! Feel the leaf, believe the leaf, breathe the leaf!

[SFX: A leaf flutters down onto the microphone.]

PELLA: That's nine seconds. The leaf is stuck to the mic. It might count as a demonstration.

DORO: No demonstrations. Legal says the wipe must not touch a leaf until tested on a leaf.

QUINCE: It's a leaf wipe.

DORO: The name is aspirational.

PELLA: New version. One factual line; keep it mysterious.

RIFF: [SINGING] Aha! Aha! The soft square that meets your tree!

DORO: Better. Replace "square" with "companion."

QUINCE: The companion that meets your tree? The meter fell out of the song.

DORO: Put the meter back with a reassuring bell.

[SFX: Bell dings, then keeps ringing because Quince tied the clapper to his sleeve.]

PELLA: Stop the bell. Focus-group caller Mina is on the line.

MINA: I heard the jingle from my hammock. I thought Aha! was a band warning us about a forest emergency.

DORO: Did you feel inspired to purchase?

MINA: I felt inspired to check the trees. One leaf had a jam stain. I used your wipe; it worked.

DORO: You used it before our results?

MINA: It was sticky. I took initiative.

PELLA: That's our best consumer evidence.

DORO: We cannot mention the result until a study confirms the existence of jam.

QUINCE: I'll write a chorus about a mysterious stain.

DORO: Only if it isn't caused by fruit.

MINA: It was fruit jam.

DORO: Then we cannot establish motive.

PELLA: We have twelve seconds left on the schedule. New take, one clear point.

RIFF: [SINGING] Aha! Aha! Leaf wipe, make the sticky leave!

DORO: "Sticky" is negative. Call it "unexpected texture."

RIFF: [SINGING] Aha! Aha! Leaf wipe, resolve your texture!

QUINCE: That sounds like the leaf joined a counseling program.

DORO: That's a brand extension.

[SFX: Clock ticks faster.]

PELLA: The network needs a sound of the product in use.

DORO: It makes no sound.

QUINCE: A quiet rustle.

DORO: Rustling implies distress.

MINA: My leaf made a tiny squeak.

PELLA: Why?

MINA: The wipe was folded over the microphone.

DORO: That's not approved, but it is memorable.

QUINCE: I hear the jingle: [SINGING] Aha! Aha! The leaf says, "Ah!"

DORO: The leaf must not speak. It might have a warranty claim.

RIFF: I can sing "ah" in a leaf-like voice.

DORO: Does the voice have a representative?

PELLA: Nobody represents the leaf. That's why it needs a wipe.

MINA: A leaf has a stem. The stem can sign.

DORO: We must verify it can hold a pen.

[SFX: Pen rolls off desk.]

PELLA: We will record only what happened. Quince, write it plainly.

QUINCE: A leaf had jam. Mina used a wipe. The leaf became clean. The group added a bell, then removed it.

DORO: That is not a jingle. That is a deposition.

RIFF: I can sing a deposition.

PELLA: Try it. Twelve seconds.

RIFF: [SINGING] Aha! The jam is gone; the leaf can carry on!

[SFX: Tiny trumpet flourish.]

MINA: Catchy. I remember the product and what it does.

DORO: List the flaws.

PELLA: It says jam, leaf, and wipe. It has a result, and one sentence.

QUINCE: It rhymes.

DORO: Too much information. We'll launch a teaser instead.

PELLA: The deadline is now. The network is taking the recording as is.

[SFX: Recording light clicks off; a station monitor opens.]

ANNOUNCER: Aha! Leaf Wipe: the jam is gone, the leaf can carry on. Find it beside the sap cups.

DORO: They used the whole line.

MINA: And the leaf sounds happy.

[SFX: A clean leaf flutters against the mic, then settles.]

PELLA: Approved by actual use. Thirty seconds left.

QUINCE: Shall we write the next one?

DORO: Yes. We sell the feeling of owning a clock.

PELLA: That is a clock.

DORO: Exactly. Roll sound.

[SFX: Tape clicks off as the clock keeps ticking.]

MINA: I am keeping the wipe.

[SFX: The concise jingle plays once.]
