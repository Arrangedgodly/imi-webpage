# The Trial of the Borrowed Spoon

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: courtroom radio drama, where petty household disputes receive elaborate legal procedure*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Judge Leni, a baboon; advocate Kito, a gibbon; witness Omi, a macaque; clerk Tavi, a capuchin.

[SFX: GAVEL, SPOON CLINK.]

LENI: Court is in session. The matter concerns one borrowed spoon.

KITO: The respondent returned it with jam on the handle.

OMI: I wiped the bowl.

TAVI: The record notes jam.

ACT ONE

Kito says he lent Omi the spoon for tea. Omi says she returned it after washing the bowl. The handle still had jam because she held it while wiping. Judge Leni asks whether the spoon was damaged. Tavi examines it. It is clean except for a small sticky spot.

LENI: What remedy is requested?

KITO: A formal apology and return of the spoon.

OMI: I returned the spoon.

TAVI: It is on the evidence table.

ACT TWO

Kito argues that the jam made the spoon unusable. Omi says she would have washed it again if asked. Leni asks whether the spoon was part of a matched set. Kito says it is the only spoon with a blue handle. Omi says the blue handle helped her find it among the dishes.

Tavi brings a cup of water. The spoon is still on the table. Leni orders it cleaned before testimony continues. Kito says the court should preserve the evidence. Omi dips a cloth in water and wipes the handle. The jam comes off.

[SFX: CLOTH WIPES SPOON.]

LENI: The evidence has changed.

TAVI: It is now clean.

ACT TWO-B

Kito asks Omi to show the court how she held the spoon. She holds it by the blue handle and wipes the bowl, not the handle. The sticky spot came from her thumb after she touched a dab of jam. Tavi checks the table and finds the jam jar beside the evidence cloth. Leni asks whether the spoon was ever marked as borrowed. Kito says he handed it over after breakfast. Omi remembers returning it while Kito was washing the cups. The spoon sat on the drying rack for an hour before the hearing. Kito admits he could have washed the handle himself. Leni asks if there is any damage, missing property, or continuing risk. Both answer no. Tavi suggests a settlement: Omi wipes the handle, Kito accepts it, and the court returns to its scheduled tea break. Kito says he still wants the apology recorded. Omi says she will apologize, but not for stealing a spoon she was given.

Kito asks to enter one final statement. He says he did not mean to accuse Omi of keeping the spoon; he wanted her to know the handle felt sticky. Omi says she would have wiped it if he had told her. Leni asks whether they can resolve the matter without assigning blame. Both agree. Tavi records that the spoon was lent, returned, and cleaned after a small complaint. Kito says that sounds less dramatic than "The People versus the Jammed Handle." Leni says the court has no jurisdiction over titles.

Tavi stamps the form. The ink smears because the spoon is still beside it. Omi wipes the mark from the table, then hands Kito the clean cloth. Leni notes that the court has settled both the spoon dispute and the accidental ink stain.

The clerk files the form under "spoon," not "theft."

ACT THREE

Leni asks Kito whether he accepts the spoon. He says he does. Omi apologizes for returning it with jam. Kito apologizes for making the matter a court case. Leni orders both parties to share the tea and not write another complaint.

TAG

Tavi sets down two cups and one spoon.

KITO: Who gets it?

LENI: The court is adjourned.

[SFX: GAVEL.]
