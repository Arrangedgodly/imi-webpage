# The Lost Scarf Has a Court Date

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio legal parody where a missing scarf is tried for being in the wrong drawer*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Judge Mara, a macaque; clerk Odo, a gibbon; advocate Vex, an orangutan; witness Pip, a capuchin; caretaker Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Small community hearing room. Papers tap in order.

MARA: This hearing concerns a blue scarf reported missing from the library coat rack. We will establish where it was found before assigning blame.

VEX: I represent the scarf. It was last seen in public and later discovered in the supply drawer. Clearly, the drawer abducted it.

ODO: The drawer is not a party to this hearing.

PIP: I put the scarf in the drawer when the coat rack was being cleaned. I attached a note, but the note came loose.

NARI: I found the scarf beside the spare hooks. It was dry and folded.

VEX: The scarf may have entered the drawer against its will.

MARA: Scarves do not enter drawers. People place them there. Ask what happened to the note.

SFX: Paper rustles.

NARI: The note was under the drawer liner. It says, "Pip's blue scarf, held here during cleaning." The name is still readable.

ODO: The library log says the cleaning started at noon and ended at one. The scarf was found at twelve-thirty.

VEX: So it was held without a witness for half an hour.

MARA: The cleaner was the witness. Nari, were you present?

NARI: Yes. I moved the scarf from the rack into the drawer, then put the note beside it. I didn't notice the paper slip under the liner.

PIP: I came back for it at one, but the drawer was locked. I assumed someone had taken it.

VEX: The locked drawer is suspicious.

ODO: It is locked whenever the supply room is unattended.

MARA: There is no evidence of theft. The scarf was stored for cleaning and the note was misplaced. What remedy does Pip request?

PIP: I want it back and a label on the drawer that says lost items are taken to the staffed desk.

NARI: I can return the scarf now. I'll also add a temporary hold tray outside the supply room.

SFX: Cloth lifted from a table; soft shake.

NARI: The scarf is clean and dry. Pip, check that it's yours.

PIP: Blue wool, one loose tassel. Yes.

VEX: I object to returning it without a formal verdict.

MARA: The verdict is that the scarf was misplaced during cleaning, not abducted. The remedy is a staffed hold tray and a clear note.

ODO: Entered in the log. No penalty for the drawer.

VEX: The drawer leaves court with its reputation intact.

MARA: It is furniture. Hearing closed.


MARA: Clerk, return the scarf and place the hold tray where visitors can see it from the coat rack.

ODO: I have a shallow basket by the front desk. The sign will say, "Items found during cleaning go here; ask staff for help."

NARI: I'll add the date and my initials to the log. The scarf will be marked returned to Pip, not cleared from the record.

VEX: Does the scarf accept the terms?

PIP: It has tassels. It doesn't sign. I can confirm that it is mine.

SFX: Pen scratches, basket shifts on desk.

MARA: That completes the handoff. No one should leave a found item in the locked supply drawer again.

ODO: The spare hooks remain in that drawer, but the public tray is outside it.

VEX: I withdraw the abduction claim. The drawer is released.

MARA: Hearing closed, and the scarf goes home.


NARI: The hold tray is labeled, visible, and outside the supply room.

ODO: The scarf is no longer in the drawer. The log records exactly where it went.

MARA: We have finished the hearing.

TAG

SFX: Drawer slides shut.

VEX: I appeal.

ODO: You cannot appeal against a drawer.
