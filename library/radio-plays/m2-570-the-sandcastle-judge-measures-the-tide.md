# The Sandcastle Judge Measures the Tide

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: beach competition coverage that treats a rising tide as a rival judge*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; judge Mara, a macaque; builder Pip, a capuchin; beach steward Odo, a gibbon; caller Nari, a lemur and parent beside the beach phone; second builder Seli, a langur (nonspeaking).

SFX: Beach. Small waves, shovel in damp sand; public phone rings beside the lifeguard stand.

VEX: Final round of the sandcastle championship. The tide has entered the competition.

MARA: The tide is not a contestant. The builders have until noon to finish inside the marked area.

ODO: The waterline is behind the blue rope. We don't build below it.

PIP: My tower is stable. I need two more minutes to finish the arch.

NARI, on phone: My child is building near the wet sand. Should they hurry?

MARA: They should stay inside the marked area and follow the steward's instructions. The event ends at noon whether or not every castle is finished.

VEX: The tide is closing in.

ODO: It is moving as expected. The tide chart is posted by the lifeguard stand.

PIP: The arch is sagging. I can shore it with damp sand from the bucket, but I won't dig a trench toward the water.

MARA: That's allowed. Keep the bucket inside the line.

SFX: Bucket tips; sand pats into place.

VEX: A defensive wall against the ocean.

PIP: A support for the arch. It is a small sculpture, not a fortress.

ODO: Two minutes remain. I will call time once and then Mara will score what is there.

VEX: Can the tide count as an artistic effect?

MARA: No. We judge shape, teamwork, and use of material. The tide is a natural condition.

NARI: Seli is back behind the blue rope. Thank you. I will hang up and join your scoring table.

SFX: Phone receiver clicks down; footsteps approach on sand.

NARI, nearby: Here are the scorecards from the lifeguard stand.

VEX: The challenger is also a messenger.

MARA: She is a parent bringing stationery.

NARI: Seli helped Pip hold his bucket steady. Could you mention that instead?

VEX: An alliance has formed against the sea.

PIP: An alliance against spilling our sand. It is going very well.

PIP: Arch is standing. I'm stepping back now. No more sand is going on after time.

SFX: Beach whistle, one short blast.

ODO: Time. Tools down, everyone behind the line.

VEX: The final work is frozen in time.

MARA: It is complete for judging. Pip, you may leave it in place while I inspect it.

PIP: I will carry my shovel to the tool tray. I won't touch the castle again.

ODO: The tide is still behind the rope. The event crew will clear tools before the water reaches the work area.

MARA: Both arches are still standing. Pip's is taller; Seli's base is wider. Nari, please pass the cards before Vex invents a tallest-wave prize.

NARI: Here you are. He has already written OCEAN on the spare card.

VEX: I like to prepare for surprise entries.

MARA: The scorecards allow four points for structure and three for teamwork. Every entry must stay inside the marked line.

VEX: The tide has crossed the line, so it is disqualified.

MARA: The tide is not entered. The builders are the contestants.

PIP: Do we get points for dramatic narration?

MARA: We do not.

VEX: An entire career excluded from the rubric.

ODO: I'll carry the tool tray away now. Vex, your microphone cable runs through its handle.

VEX: It was a convenient place to hang it.

SFX: Cable slides free; tools rattle in a lifted tray.

MARA: Pip receives four structure points and two for teamwork. Seli receives three structure points and three for teamwork. Both totals are six.

VEX: A tie in the final round. Surely we need a sudden-death tower?

MARA: The rules say a tie stands. We don't ask the builders to extend the event into the tide.

PIP: I am happy to share the result. We both made something inside the line.

VEX: The contest ends in a draw.

NARI: I have pinned those totals at the lifeguard stand. The builders can leave the work area now.

SFX: Odo sets the tool tray by the stand; footsteps retreat from the water.

ODO: Area is clear. No tools or visitors remain by the rope.
VEX: The water is approaching the towers. My spare scorecard remains blank.

TAG

SFX: By the lifeguard stand, well above the work area. Waves sound farther away.

PIP: I will let the tide have the castle.

VEX: A gracious champion.
