# The Page-Turn Prize

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: literary radio contests that reward dramatic readings but interrupt readers with arbitrary page-turn scoring*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Mavo, a gibbon; judge Sera, a lemur; librarian Koro, a macaque; reader Timi, a capuchin.

[SFX: SMALL RADIO STUDIO. PAGES TURN. A BUZZER BEEPS.]

MAVO: Welcome to the Page-Turn Prize. Readers earn points for each dramatic pause and page turn.

SERA: The contest is for reading aloud, not turning pages.

MAVO: The scoring screen rewards page turns.

KORO: Timi has a short story with only three pages.

TIMI: It has a lot of sentences. I can read it at a steady pace.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.]

TIMI: "At the river bend, Jo found a glass bead in the mud. It held a blue thread of light."

[SFX: ONE PAGE TURNS. BUZZER BEEPS.]

MAVO: One point for page movement.

SERA: The score is interrupting the reading.

KORO: The page-turn sensor thinks any paper sound is a page turn.

[SFX: A SCOREBOARD BEEPS AT EVERY SMALL PAGE SHIFT.]

TIMI: Can I keep reading?

MAVO: The rules say pause after every point.

TIMI: The story doesn't pause there. Jo is following the thread.

SERA: Let the reader finish the scene. We can count score after the passage.

MAVO: But the live audience needs a contest.

KORO: Then score the reading by the judges, not the paper noise.

MAVO: That would change the rules mid-show.

SERA: The rules printed on the entry form say "judges award points for clarity and expression." Page-turn points came from the producer's screen.

KORO: It has a microphone pointed at the page. Every time Timi breathes near it, the counter moves.

[SFX: TIMI BREATHES. BUZZER BEEPS.]

MAVO: The breath earned another point.

TIMI: The character is out of breath too. The machine is scoring Jo.

SERA: The story is doing the work. Mavo, mute the page microphone.

MAVO: I don't know which slider it is.

KORO: The one labeled paper sound.

[SFX: SLIDER CLICKS DOWN. SCOREBOARD BEEPS STOP.]

MAVO: The meter is quiet. Timi, continue.

TIMI: "The thread led Jo to a willow root. Under it, the river had caught a small wooden boat."

[SFX: PAGE TURNS. NO BUZZER.]

SERA: That is the first point. Clear image, clean pace.

KORO: I heard the boat hit the root. The page noise is gone.

TIMI: "Jo lifted the boat. A name was scratched across the bow, and the river carried the same name away."

MAVO: That line could use a pause.

TIMI: The sentence already has one.

SERA: Exactly. The reader chose it.

[SFX: TWO QUIET HEARTBEATS OF ROOM TONE.]

KORO: The story is complete.

MAVO: I have a score sheet with three boxes: clarity, pace, expression.

SERA: We can use those. Timi gets three marks for a story that held together.

MAVO: The page-turn screen shows twelve points.

KORO: It can show twelve. It no longer decides the winner.

MAVO: The sponsor donated a page-turn bell. It rang whenever a reader reached a new page.

KORO: It rang during the story before we muted the sensor. It rang during Timi's breath, too.

SERA: We should leave the bell off. A reading contest is not a paper contest.

MAVO: The bell can ring for the winner instead.

TIMI: Then it will mark the end, not interrupt the sentence.

[SFX: ONE BELL RINGS AFTER THE STORY, THEN STOPS.]

SERA: That is the only page-turn sound we need tonight.

MAVO: Timi wins the Page-Turn Prize.

TIMI: I read the pages in order and let the story carry me.

SERA: That is the prize. The page turns were just paper.

[SFX: A SMALL TROPHY SETS ON TABLE. APPLAUSE FROM THREE PEOPLE, NO BUZZER.]

MAVO: We will move the page sensor away from the microphone before the next reader.

KORO: And put the score sheet beside the judges, where the reading can be heard.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES. PAGES LIE STILL.]
