# The Lost Note in the Choir Loft

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: musical mystery serials that overcomplicate a misplaced sheet of music*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Director Pava, a gibbon; singer Omi, a macaque; page-turner Kito, a capuchin; caretaker Fenn, a baboon.

[SFX: CHOIR HUM, PAGES TURN.]

PAVA: The third verse is missing.

OMI: I have it in my hand.

KITO: That is the second verse.

FENN: The loft has one music stand.

ACT ONE

Pava says the choir cannot rehearse without the third verse. Omi checks her folder; it is empty. Kito checks the stand; the second verse is on top. Fenn asks if the page fell behind the bench.

ACT TWO

They search the loft. Pava hums the melody. Omi sings the first two lines. Kito turns the page and finds the third verse clipped to the back of the stand. It was hidden by the music folder.

PAVA: The note was concealed.

KITO: It was clipped.

ACT TWO-B

Fenn climbs the short ladder to inspect the stand's clip. Kito reads the printed order of the music pages aloud. The first verse is clipped at the front, the second rests on the stand, and the third is folded behind the folder. Omi says she put it there to keep it from blowing away when the window was open. Pava checks the loft window; it is now closed. The missing page was never lost from the room, only hidden from the singer. Kito asks Omi to hold the folder while he removes the clip. She grips the folder by its spine. The page slides out without tearing. Pava asks whether the melody needs another rehearsal. Omi sings the third verse alone, then with the group. The last note resolves cleanly. Fenn says the sound carries well through the loft. Pava says no mystery remains. Kito points out that the page still needs to be returned to the folder in order. Omi puts it between the second and back cover.

Pava asks Omi to test only the rescued third verse, not restart the entire rehearsal. Omi sings the first line; Kito turns the page at the marked cue. The final note resolves. Fenn stands by the window so a gust cannot lift the folder again.

PAVA: The page is back in order.

KITO: I have marked its number.

Omi asks if she may keep the repaired page with her music. Fenn says it returns to the folder after the performance. Kito attaches a numbered tab to the folder, and Pava puts it on the stand with the spine facing the choir.

FENN: The window is closed. The page is clipped. No search required.

Omi warms up the final note once, softly. Pava calls that enough rehearsal and saves the full song for the broadcast.

The music stand slips on its uneven foot. Kito catches it before the folder slides. Fenn folds a square of cloth beneath the stand, and the legs settle level.

KITO: The page was not the only thing tilted.

PAVA: The choir can read it now.

Omi checks the numbered tab from her place in the row. The third verse follows the second, and no one has to turn back to the loft window.

FENN: Wind is out. Stand is steady.

Pava asks for the starting note. Omi gives it quietly; the other singers join only for the first measure, then stop at her raised hand.

OMI: The order is clear.

KITO: The page will stay clipped until the broadcast.

PAVA: Good. We have one clean performance to give, and no search left to stage.

ACT THREE

The choir rehearses all three verses. Omi sings the ending clearly. Pava thanks Kito for finding the page. Fenn returns the clip to the stand.

TAG

Pava checks the folder again.

PAVA: Is the fourth verse missing?

KITO: There are only three.

[SFX: CHOIR RESOLVES.]
