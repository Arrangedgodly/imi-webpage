# The Ferry Horn Has a Sponsor

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sponsored local radio event coverage, branded live announcements, and reporters forced to work a sponsor message into every update*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST NERI, a capuchin
REPORTER ASA, a macaque
SPONSOR REP BELL, a gibbon
DECKHAND TAVI, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: HARBOR WIND. FERRY HORN.]

NERI: We are live at the harbor for the evening ferry departure, brought to you by Cloudberry Blankets, the blanket for a breezy crossing.

ASA: The ferry is ready to leave. The dock is crowded, and the harbor master is raising a green flag.

BELL: Before we report the departure, say the full sponsor line.

NERI: The ferry is ready to leave in a blanket-approved breeze.

ASA: The flag is still green. Passengers are boarding.

BELL: Mention that the blanket folds into a square.

NERI: Passengers are boarding beside a square-folding blanket.

ASA: One passenger has brought a large blanket and is blocking the ramp.

TAVI: It is the sponsor sample. The wind keeps lifting it over the gangway.

[SFX: FABRIC FLAPS. FOOTSTEPS STOP.]

NERI: A brief delay, supported by Cloudberry Blankets.

BELL: Say "cozy delay."

ASA: It is not cozy. People cannot board.

BELL: Could you say "a temporary pause in comfort"?

TAVI: I can tie the blanket to the railing.

NERI: The deckhand is securing our sponsor.

TAVI: I am securing the blanket.

[SFX: ROPE TIES. FABRIC FLAPS LESS.]

ASA: The ramp is clear. The passengers are moving again.

BELL: Could we have a close-up description of the blanket?

ASA: This is radio. The close-up is my voice saying the blanket has a blue edge.

NERI: It has a blue edge, ideal for identifying it when it sails away.

[SFX: WIND GUST. KNOT SLIPS. FABRIC LIFTS.]

TAVI: The blanket is headed for the boat.

BELL: Can we describe that as an in-water demonstration?

ASA: It is in the air above the water.

NERI: The blanket is now testing its wind resistance.

TAVI: I caught it on the horn rope.

[SFX: FABRIC SNAGS. FERRY HORN BLARES.]

ASA: The horn has sounded. The ferry captain thinks it is time to depart.

NERI: The ferry horn is sponsored by Cloudberry Blankets.

BELL: It is not. The sponsor paid for the report, not the horn.

NERI: Our coverage has expanded to the horn's independence.

TAVI: I need the captain to wait while I release the fabric.

ASA: Captain, hold the ferry. The blanket is caught on the horn rope.

[SFX: HORN ROPE TUGGED, SHORT BLAST.]

BELL: Can that be our jingle?

NERI: A blue-edged blanket over harbor water makes a sound like...

ASA: Do not pull it again.

TAVI: I have the knot. The fabric is free.

[SFX: BLANKET FOLDS. PASSENGERS BOARD.]

ASA: The ramp is clear. All passengers are aboard, and the ferry can depart.

BELL: We need to mention the blanket one more time.

NERI: Cloudberry Blankets folds into a square, and this one is returning to the sponsor's dry case.

TAVI: It is damp at one corner.

BELL: That's still within the guarantee.

ASA: The ferry is leaving now. The horn will sound without sponsorship.

[SFX: FERRY HORN. ENGINE STARTS.]

NERI: The ferry has departed, carrying passengers and no blanket. Our report was sponsored, the horn was not, and the ramp stayed clear.

BELL: Please say the blanket line on the way out.

NERI: Cloudberry Blankets: warm on land, complicated near a ferry.

ASA: We are clear of the dock.

[SFX: WATER, FERRY RECEDES.]

TAVI: I am folding the sample into a square.

NERI: The broadcast is over. The blanket is dry enough to sponsor tomorrow's bus stop.

BELL: I will need a new report.

ASA: The bus stop has a roof.

BELL: Then the report can describe how dry the blanket stayed.

[SFX: WIND, THEME OUT.]
