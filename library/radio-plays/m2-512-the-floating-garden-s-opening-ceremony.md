# The Floating Garden's Opening Ceremony

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: environmental showcases that celebrate a prototype before checking where it will drift*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; builder Odo, a gibbon; sponsor Vex, an orangutan; gardener Pip, a capuchin; scout Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Canal bank. Water laps under a floating garden platform.

VEX: Today we unveil a garden that can grow anywhere.

MARA: It can grow on the canal if the anchor holds.

ODO: The platform is tied to the bank with one rope.

PIP: The current is pulling it sideways.

VEX: The ribbon is ready for the opening.

NARI: The gardener is still on the platform.

MARA: Pip, step to the middle and keep your weight low.

SFX: Rope strains. Pots clink.

ODO: The anchor knot is sliding.

VEX: We can cut the ribbon before it drifts.

MARA: Do not cut anything until the platform is secured.

PIP: I can pass the spare line from the bank.

NARI: The piling has a ring we can tie to.

ODO: I'll brace the line while Mara ties it.

SFX: Rope runs through a ring. Knot tightens.

PIP: The platform stopped moving.

VEX: The opening has been delayed.

MARA: It has been made safe.

ODO: We need a second tie at the downstream corner.

VEX: There is another rope in the supply box.

SFX: Box opens. Rope uncoils.

PIP: The basil pot is close to the downstream corner. It is heavier than the mint.

MARA: Keep the pots where they are until both anchors hold. A moving load can pull the frame sideways.

VEX: The camera has a better angle if we move the basil to the front.

NARI: The camera can move. The pot stays where the weight is balanced.

ODO: I'll check that each planter is tied to the frame and not just resting on the mat.

SFX: A pot rim taps the frame. Rope creaks under the platform.

PIP: This one is secure.

MARA: Good. The garden is part of the load test, not a prop to rearrange.

VEX: The live reveal is losing its symmetry.

MARA: The plants need the platform to stay level more than the picture needs symmetry.



NARI: Tie it to the platform frame, not the planter.

PIP: The frame is steady now.

VEX: Can I cut the ribbon?

MARA: After we test the anchored platform with its planters and no one standing on it.

TAG

ODO: I tied the second line at the downstream corner.

MARA: Check the knot against the frame, not the planter box.

PIP: The planter could shift if the soil gets wet.

NARI: The current is strongest near the far corner.

SFX: Rope tightens; platform rocks once, then settles.

ODO: Both lines are holding.

PIP: I can see the downstream corner lifting a little.

MARA: Don't move the pot. Odo, check whether the frame is level before anyone steps off.

SFX: A small level clicks against the frame.

ODO: The bubble is centered. The corner only rose when the line tightened.

VEX: Is that good enough for the opening?

MARA: We haven't checked it without the gardener yet.

ODO: The two anchor knots are separate. If one loosens, the other still holds the platform.

PIP: The basil and mint are watered. Their pots are stable.

MARA: We need to test with the pots in place, then again with the gardener stepping off.

VEX: Can the opening proceed during the test?

MARA: This is the opening. People should hear how it works.

SFX: Pip steps carefully from platform to bank. Water laps; ropes hold.

PIP: The platform stays level without me.

ODO: The downstream line is taut, but the frame isn't twisting.

MARA: Good. The basil and mint are upright. Now we can cut the ribbon beside the bank.

VEX: The ceremony can happen after the garden is stable.

SFX: Ribbon snips.

VEX: The garden is open.

PIP: The basil is still in its pot.
