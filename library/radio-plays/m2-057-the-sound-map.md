# The Sound Map

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: field-recording travel programs and immersive soundscape tours*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Guide Mina, a macaque; producer Ebb, a tortoise; listener Jori, a capuchin; and ferry operator Tavi, a langur.

[SFX: HEADPHONES CLICK ON. A RIVER FLOWS IN STEREO.] 

MINA: Welcome to The Sound Map. Put on your headphones if you have them. We are walking the old riverside path from the footbridge to the market. Producer Ebb, are we recording?

EBB: Yes. The microphone is pointed toward the water.

MINA: The water is behind us.

EBB: That explains the recording.

[SFX: MICROPHONE TURNS. FLOWING WATER MOVES TO THE LEFT CHANNEL.] 

MINA: Now the river is on our left. We can hear the ferry bell ahead. We are not going to tell you this is a secret path. People use it every day.

TAVI, DISTANT: Last crossing in five minutes.

MINA: The sound map calls that a ferry bell. It is not a bird.

EBB: The guide text says "a mysterious silver note." 

MINA: Change it to "ferry bell." The ferry carries people across.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON PLANKS. A BICYCLE BELL RINGS.] 

MINA: We are at the bridge entrance. Give way to the cyclist.

JORI, PHONE: I hear two bells. How do I know which one is which?

MINA: One is higher and close. That is the bicycle. The ferry bell is lower and farther across the water.

EBB: Can we use a sound cue for the market turn?

MINA: The market is full of voices, baskets, and a cart wheel. That is not one cue. It is a whole place.

[SFX: MARKET AMBIENCE SWELLS, THEN SOFTENS.] 

MINA: If you are walking this path, keep to the right near the loading gate. The curb ramp is on your left. Do not stop in the lane to record. There is a bench by the river where you can listen safely.

JORI: The app says to follow the sound of a bell.

MINA: That's a problem. A bell could be on a bicycle, ferry, or shop door. Use the map and signs, not audio alone.

EBB: The sound map was meant to guide people without looking at a screen.

MINA: Sound can add detail, but it cannot tell you the whole route. The path changes after rain. The ferry schedule changes by season. We should include those limits.

[SFX: A CART RUMBLES. A WHEEL SQUEAKS TWICE.] 

MINA: That is the baker's cart. It makes a squeak on every turn.

EBB: Should we label it?

MINA: No. The baker did not ask to be part of our map.

JORI: I can hear the river getting louder.

MINA: We are near the lower steps. The route is not accessible from this end. Return to the bridge and take the ramp beside the ferry booth.

TAVI: The ramp is open. The crossing departs in two minutes.

MINA: The guide should say that clearly. Ebb, remove the line that says "the path welcomes all travelers."

EBB: It sounds kind.

MINA: It is not true for this stairway.

EBB: I'll replace it with directions to the ramp.

[SFX: TAPE EDITING CLICK, RIVER CONTINUES.] 

MINA: The sound map now marks the bridge, ferry, market, and ramp. It names what each sound is and tells you when sound is not enough. We are not selling the river as an untouched escape. We are helping people find their way along a working path.

JORI: I found the bench. I can hear the water and the market at once.

MINA: That's a good place to pause.

EBB: Should we record your footsteps?

JORI: No, I'm sitting.

[SFX: BENCH CREAK, WATER MOVES IN BOTH CHANNELS.] 

MINA: Before we sign off, a schedule update. This recording was made Tuesday. The ferry boards at the west landing today, but check the posted timetable before traveling.

EBB: The old recording sent listeners to the closed east ramp.

MINA: We have replaced it, dated the new card, and put a spoken warning at the start. The path is still working, and the sound alone cannot tell you which entrance is open.

JORI: I can hear the ferry. I am staying on the bench until the ramp is clear.

MINA: Good. Ebb, record the departure from here.

[SFX: FERRY HORN, DISTANT. BENCH CREAKS.]

MINA: This is The Sound Map. The path is open. The ferry is leaving. Please do not follow our microphone onto the road.

[SFX: FERRY HORN, DISTANT.]
