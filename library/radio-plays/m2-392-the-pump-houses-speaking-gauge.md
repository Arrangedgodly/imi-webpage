# The Pump House's Speaking Gauge

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: utility radio bulletins that turn a water level gauge's warning buzzer into a spoken emergency announcement*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Operator Leka, a macaque; host Piri, a gibbon; engineer Sumi, a lemur; caller Daro, a capuchin.

[SFX: PUMP HOUSE. WATER RUNS THROUGH PIPES. A BUZZER SOUNDS TWICE.]

PIRI: The pump house has issued a flood warning.

LEKA: It has not. The gauge buzzer means the reservoir reached its test mark.

SUMI: The speaker system translated the buzzer as "evacuate."

DARO: I heard it from the footpath. I am at the gate.

PIRI: Is water rising over the path?

LEKA: No. The outlet is flowing normally.

[SFX: WATER RUSHES STEADILY. GAUGE NEEDLE CLICKS AGAINST A MARK.]

SUMI: The level is below the warning line. The buzzer is a monthly test.

LEKA: The warning mark is three inches above the current level. The test buzzer does not mean the water is rising.

SUMI: The controller has an old line labeled "high water." It was connected to the test contact during installation.

PIRI: That sounds like the exact mistake we need to explain.

DARO: I would rather hear one test chime than another evacuation message.

[SFX: SUMI TURNS A KEY. A SWITCH CLICKS INTO PLACE.]

SUMI: Test contact is separate. If the gauge crosses the warning mark, the red light and warning tone will activate.

LEKA: I am watching the needle. It is steady.

[SFX: NEEDLE TAPS ONCE AGAINST ITS GUIDE.]

PIRI: A tap from the gauge is not a warning tone.

SUMI: Correct. The speaker is muted until the new message is tested.

PIRI: The radio card says emergency announcement.

LEKA: The warning mark is three inches above the current level. The test buzzer does not mean the water is rising.

SUMI: The controller has an old line labeled "high water." It was connected to the test contact during installation.

PIRI: That sounds like the exact mistake we need to explain.

DARO: I would rather hear one test chime than another evacuation message.

[SFX: SUMI TURNS A KEY. A SWITCH CLICKS INTO PLACE.]

SUMI: Test contact is separate. If the gauge crosses the warning mark, the red light and warning tone will activate.

LEKA: I am watching the needle. It is steady.

[SFX: NEEDLE TAPS ONCE AGAINST ITS GUIDE.]

PIRI: A tap from the gauge is not a warning tone.

SUMI: Correct. The speaker is muted until the new message is tested.

PIRI: The radio card says emergency announcement.

LEKA: The card is outdated. The new gauge has separate test and warning tones.

DARO: Why did it say evacuate?

SUMI: The controller maps every buzzer to the same spoken phrase.

PIRI: We need to stop the spoken phrase until the control is corrected.

LEKA: I can silence the speaker, but leave the gauge alarm active.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. SPEAKER MUTES. BUZZER SOUNDS ONCE, CLEARLY.]

SUMI: Gauge alarm remains. Public speaker is muted.

DARO: I can hear the water. It is running below the path.

PIRI: We should not tell people there is no danger until the gauge is checked.

LEKA: I am reading it now: below warning mark, steady pressure, no overflow.

SUMI: The warning switch is on the upper contact. The test switch is below it.

[SFX: SCREWDRIVER TURNS. A SWITCH CLICK.]

SUMI: I will separate the contacts and run a test tone.

PIRI: What will the public hear?

SUMI: A short chime and the words "system test."

[SFX: ONE SHORT CHIME. SPEAKER VOICE: "SYSTEM TEST."]

DARO: That is clear.

LEKA: Now I will test the warning contact without opening the speaker.

[SFX: GAUGE TEST BUTTON CLICKS. BUZZER SOUNDS THREE TIMES. SPEAKER SILENT.]

SUMI: Warning contact is separate. It does not trigger the public message during a test.

PIRI: The reservoir is below the line. We can report normal flow and a completed system test.

DARO: Can I walk the path?

LEKA: Yes. The gate is open and the water is below the mark.

[SFX: GATE LATCH OPENS. FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL.]

PIRI: No evacuation message.

SUMI: I have updated the sign at the gate. It says the buzzer is a monthly test unless the warning light is on.

[SFX: PAPER SIGN PRESSES FLAT.]

LEKA: The warning light is dark. Pressure is steady.

PIRI: We will record: test completed, no flood warning, path open.

DARO: That's what the gate should have said the first time.

[SFX: PUMP HUMS. RADIO MIC CLICKS OFF; WATER CONTINUES AT STEADY FLOW.]
