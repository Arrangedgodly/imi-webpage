# Weather Is a Regional Dialect

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: hyperlocal radio weather updates that compete to sound more specific than the forecast supports*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
ANNOUNCER VERA, a macaque
WEATHER HOST TUK, a capuchin
CALLER YANI, a gibbon
SOUND ENGINEER POL, a gorilla

[SFX: NEWS JINGLE. A THUNDERCLAP PLAYS, FOLLOWED BY A DRY RATTLE.]

VERA: This is the neighborhood weather desk. Tuk, what is happening outside?

TUK: Light rain in the east blocks, cloudy by the library, and a suspicious dampness near the studio window.

POL: That last one is our roof.

TUK: The roof is a microclimate.

### Scene One: Block by Block

[SFX: PAPER MAP UNFOLDS.]

VERA: Caller Yani is in the west blocks. What do you see?

YANI: The sky is clear. The schoolyard has a puddle left from yesterday.

TUK: Our map shows blue over the schoolyard.

YANI: That blue is the puddle.

POL: We are using an old satellite image.

TUK: I can add a sun icon over the puddle.

VERA: Would that make it accurate?

YANI: It would describe two different times.

[SFX: COMPUTER BEEPS.]

POL: The update shows a small shower moving east. It should reach the library in twenty minutes.

TUK: Can we call it a storm cell?

VERA: It is a shower.

TUK: A very localized storm cell.

### Scene Two: The Sound Map

[TUK PLACES THREE PAPER MAPS ON THE STUDIO DESK.]

TUK: I have a map for the library, another for the bakery, and a third for the bench between them.

POL: The bench is on the same block as the bakery.

TUK: It has its own forecast.

YANI: I am walking to the library. I can report the weather as I go.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS. A SMALL DRIP FALLS ON THE MICROPHONE.]

YANI: One drop. The shower is arriving.

TUK: The library is now under a dramatic weather alert.

VERA: Is there a warning?

POL: No. The update is a brief shower.

[VERA ASKS TUK TO READ THE ACTUAL FORECAST CARD. HE HOLDS IT SO CLOSE TO THE MICROPHONE THAT THE PAPER CRINKLES OVER HIS VOICE.] 

TUK: Cloudy by noon, light shower east of the library, clearing by two.

POL: That is the whole update.

TUK: It does not mention the bench.

YANI: The bench is not a region.

[TUK DRAWS A TINY CIRCLE AROUND THE BENCH ON THE MAP. IT IS SMALLER THAN THE ERASER ON HIS PENCIL.] 

VERA: The circle is now a weather system.

[TUK CUES A SECOND THUNDERCLAP. A STACK OF MAPS SLIDES OFF THE DESK.]

TUK: The maps have reacted.

POL: They are paper.

### Scene Three: The Correction

[SFX: RAIN ON WINDOW, GENTLE AND SHORT.]

VERA: The shower has passed the library. Tuk, do you want to revise the forecast?

TUK: It passed the library but may still be near the bench.

YANI: I am at the bench. It is dry.

TUK: The bench has cleared.

VERA: The forecast was accurate for the library and inaccurate for the bench.

POL: The bench is thirty steps away.

[TUK REMOVES THE EXTRA MAP. A DROP FROM THE STUDIO ROOF LANDS ON IT.]

TUK: Now the bench has rain.

VERA: In the studio.

POL: Our maintenance crew will check the roof after the broadcast.

[SFX: ONE THUNDERCLAP, THIS TIME FROM OUTSIDE.]

TUK: That one was real.

YANI: It is still sunny where I am.

VERA: Weather varies by location. The roof, apparently, has its own forecast.

[THE PAPER MAP SLIDES UNDER THE DRIP AND SOAKS ONLY THE STUDIO ICON.]

TUK: We have a wet studio and a dry neighborhood.

[POL PUTS A BUCKET UNDER THE STUDIO DRIP. THE WEATHER BOARD'S SMALL BLUE ICON IS DIRECTLY ABOVE IT.]

VERA: We have found rain over the studio.

TUK: It is moving west.

POL: It is coming from the ceiling.
