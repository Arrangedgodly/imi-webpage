# The Bell Tower Debate

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live call-in debate programs, adversarial moderator interruptions, and experts who treat a roosting problem like a constitutional crisis*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST PELL, a gibbon
DR. NOMA, a capuchin ecologist
ALDER VOSS, a macaque council member
CALLER TIKI, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: RADIO THEME, THEN A BELL RINGS THREE TIMES.]

PELL: Good evening. Tonight's question: should the old bell tower remain open to bats after sunset?

VOSS: It is a public tower.

NOMA: It is also a roost.

PELL: Our listeners deserve a clear yes or no.

NOMA: They deserve to know which bats.

VOSS: The ones making noise.

PELL: We have a caller. Tiki, you are on the line.

[SFX: TELEPHONE CLICK.]

TIKI: I live next to the tower. At dusk the bats come out. They do not knock. They do not ask the caretaker for a key. They fly through the opening.

VOSS: Exactly. No access control.

NOMA: They are bats.

PELL: Please answer the question.

TIKI: I was answering it. The tower has an opening, and they use it.

PELL: That sounds like a yes.

VOSS: It sounds like a no. We need to close the opening.

NOMA: The opening is how the bats leave.

[SFX: PAPERS SHUFFLE. BELL RINGS ONCE.]

PELL: We now take a sound from the tower.

[SFX: WING FLUTTERS. BATS CHIRP.]

VOSS: Was that part of the program?

PELL: Our sound technician says it was a bat.

NOMA: Which bat?

PELL: We are live; we cannot ask them all.

VOSS: We can close the tower and let the bats find another place.

NOMA: There is no other roost nearby. The bell rope also hangs through the opening. When the council rung the bell at dusk, it disturbed the colony.

VOSS: We rang it for the program.

PELL: This program is not about the bell.

[SFX: BELL RINGS FOUR TIMES, RAPIDLY.]

PELL: It seems to be now.

TIKI: The rope is moving because a bat is inside the tower.

NOMA: Please do not pull it.

[SFX: ROPE CREAKS. BELL RINGS.]

VOSS: I am not pulling it.

PELL: The caller is not pulling it.

NOMA: The bat has brushed it.

VOSS: Then it has activated the alarm.

PELL: We have no alarm.

[SFX: ONE BAT FLUTTERS, BELL RINGS ONCE.]

TIKI: I can see the opening from my window. They are leaving now.

NOMA: They leave to feed and return before morning. The tower can stay open at night and close during the day for maintenance.

VOSS: I can support that if the bell remains available.

PELL: For what?

VOSS: For municipal use.

TIKI: The bell can ring once in an emergency, and not every time someone wants a dramatic pause.

PELL: That is a clear proposal. The tower stays open after sunset, the bell rope is tied short, and the council checks the stonework in daylight.

VOSS: I will move the maintenance meeting to noon.

NOMA: The bats will be asleep.

VOSS: Then I will be quiet.

[SFX: RADIO THEME RETURNS. BATS CHIRP UNDER IT.]

PELL: The debate is resolved. The tower stays open to its regular occupants. We will return tomorrow with the question of who gets the echo.

TIKI: The tower gets the echo.

PELL: We will debate that.

PELL: One last caller before we close. Tiki, the tower is open tonight.

TIKI: Then I will leave my porch light off so the bats can find the opening.

VOSS: We cannot ask everyone to turn off a light.

NOMA: We can ask everyone not to aim it into the roost.

PELL: That is our practical agreement: open window, tied rope, no spotlight.

[SFX: A BAT CHIRPS. BELL RINGS SOFTLY.]

VOSS: Is that the bell?

NOMA: No. That is a bat.

PELL: For once, we know the difference.

[SFX: BELL RINGS ONCE. MUSIC OUT.]
