# The Seed Bank's Final Bid

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: high-pressure auction broadcasts that make ordinary objects sound priceless*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Auctioneer Vex, an orangutan; keeper Nari, a lemur; bidder Odo, a gibbon; caller Mara, a macaque; runner Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Seed bank hall. A hand bell rings. Paper packets rustle.

VEX: Welcome to the once-a-year auction of rare seeds.

NARI: The first lot is three dry acorns collected from the old oak.

ODO: How old is the oak?

NARI: Old enough to have dropped them last autumn.

VEX: A remarkable vintage. Who will bid?

MARA: I need acorns for the community planting bed.

VEX: The opening bid is one polished pebble.

MARA: I brought a basket of pebbles.

VEX: Then you are already invested.

ODO: I bid one green pebble.

SFX: A pebble lands on the table.

VEX: One green pebble. Do I hear two?

PIP: The packet says three acorns.

NARI: It does.

PIP: One rolled under the table.

SFX: A small object rolls. Pip catches it.

VEX: The lot has increased in value during bidding.

MARA: It has returned to its original quantity.

ODO: I bid a yellow leaf.

VEX: A bold escalation.

NARI: These seeds are meant to be planted, not stored in a trophy case.

VEX: We can mention that after the sale.

MARA: I bid two pebbles and a promise to plant them today.

ODO: I bid three pebbles.

PIP: The yellow leaf is stuck to your sleeve.

ODO: Then my bid is four items.

VEX: The auction recognizes the leaf.

NARI: The rules say bids must be intentional.

ODO: I intended to keep my sleeve.

MARA: The acorns are drying out in the packet. If nobody plants them, they won't be rare next year.

VEX: Scarcity is the heart of an auction.

NARI: The point of a seed bank is that the seeds leave.

ODO: Before the next lot, I want to see the seed register.

VEX: The register is confidential until the auction closes.

NARI: It is a planting schedule, not a secret ledger.

SFX: Pages turn.

MARA: These acorns are listed for the north bed. The west bed already has a packet reserved.

PIP: Then the bid needs a destination.

ODO: I'll write it on the lot card.

VEX: That lowers the mystery.

NARI: It lowers the chance that we plant them in the wrong place.

VEX: We could sell the destination as an upgrade.

MARA: You could say "north bed" into the microphone for free.

VEX: Fine. Lot one: three acorns for the north bed, planted this afternoon.

ODO: I bid one pebble and a promise to water.

MARA: I bid two pebbles and a spot on the planting crew.

PIP: I can carry the tray. The route outside has a loose step, so use the side door.

NARI: I can carry the tray outside after the lot closes. Vex, hold the side door so the path is clear.

SFX: Door opens; wind chime rattles.

VEX: The auction is still in this hall.

SFX: Nari opens a planting tray. Soil shifts.

VEX: We cannot plant the lot before the final bid.

MARA: Then I bid the first planting hole.

ODO: I bid the second hole.

PIP: I can carry the packet. There are three holes.

SFX: Packet opens. Acorns drop into soil one at a time.

VEX: The auctioneer's hammer is still raised.

NARI: Lower it. The acorns are sold to the ground.

ODO: I withdraw my pebble bid.

MARA: I'll return your leaf too.

ODO: Keep it. It has chosen the seed bank.

VEX: Lot one is closed. The winning bid was three holes, water, and immediate planting.

NARI: That is the first bid that fits our purpose.

SFX: Water trickles into tray.

PIP: Will the acorns grow here?

NARI: Not on the table. We will move the tray outside after the broadcast.

VEX: Could we auction the water?

MARA: No.

VEX: Going once, going twice-

NARI: Stop. That's the closing bell.

VEX: Sold to the evening.
