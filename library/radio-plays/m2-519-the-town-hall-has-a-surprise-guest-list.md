# The Town Hall Has a Surprise Guest List

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: event broadcasts that treat a clerical mix-up as a celebrity reveal*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; organizer Vex, an orangutan; clerk Odo, a gibbon; speaker Pip, a capuchin; stage manager Nari, a lemur; guest gardener Rumi, a squirrel.

SFX: Town hall. Folding chairs scrape. A microphone squeals, then settles.

VEX: Welcome to our special evening. We have a surprise guest, whose name is sealed until the perfect moment.

MARA: The guest list has one line. It says, "Special guest: ______." The line is empty.

ODO: The printed copy is the same as the signed form. Whoever filled it in wrote the topic and skipped the name.

NARI: The audience is looking at that blank line on the screen.

VEX: It looks mysterious. Keep the screen up.

PIP: I'm on the program to explain the garden path closure. I have the map and the repair schedule.

VEX: You are our first speaker, not the surprise.

PIP: Good. I brought one map, not a disguise.

MARA: Let's introduce Pip and get the meeting started. We can call the guest by name when we confirm it.

VEX: The announcement needs suspense. What if I say the guest is someone who has worked in the garden?

ODO: That describes the entire maintenance crew.

SFX: Papers shuffle. Odo turns pages; one sheet slides off the clipboard.

ODO: Here is the original invitation. It says, "Please invite the gardener to show the new route." There is no name attached.

MARA: Which gardener did you invite?

VEX: I sent the message to the garden office and asked for the person with the route map.

NARI: That office has three gardeners. The door is open; the audience can hear the hallway.

VEX: Perhaps one will appear when I say, "The moment has arrived."

MARA: First ask the office. Odo, put the form on speaker.

SFX: Phone buttons. A ring, then a click.

RUMI, on phone: Garden office. Rumi speaking. I'm carrying the route map over now. The east path is still closed because the gravel shifted.

MARA: Thank you. We'll introduce you by name and let you explain the map.

VEX: Can you wait outside for a dramatic entrance?

RUMI: No. I'm already at the door, and the map is catching on the handle.

SFX: Door handle rattles. A rolled map knocks twice against wood.

NARI: I'll open it. Keep the microphone stand clear.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps. A map roll bumps gently into a chair.

RUMI: I'm here. The north path is open. This is the east turn that needs another load of gravel.

PIP: Your map marks the closed section with a thick line. My copy has the same turn in pencil.

MARA: Put both maps on the table. We can compare them without blocking the aisle.

SFX: Paper unrolls and is pinned by two cups.

RUMI: The route is clear up to the oak. Past that, please use the north path until tomorrow's repair.

VEX: Our mystery guest has arrived with a map and a closure notice.

RUMI: That's not a mystery. It's the meeting topic.

MARA: It's useful. Odo, correct the screen: Rumi, garden crew, route update.

ODO: Done. The blank line is gone.

NARI: The audience is reading the name now instead of guessing at the door.

VEX: And the suspense?

PIP: It ends at the east path, where the gravel is.

VEX: Then I will announce the surprise: the guest list has been corrected.

MARA: Announce our guest, please.

VEX: Rumi from the garden crew, here to explain the route.

RUMI: Thank you. Please take the north path home. The east path is not a shortcut yet.

SFX: Chairs shift as listeners fold the map's corner back into place.

TAG

VEX: Next month we have another surprise guest.

ODO: The name is already printed.

VEX: Where is the suspense?

MARA: In the meeting agenda. It's twelve pages long.
