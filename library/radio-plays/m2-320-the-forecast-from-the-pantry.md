# The Forecast from the Pantry

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: science bulletins that overstate the significance of mundane household discoveries*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Fenn, a macaque; researcher Leni, a gibbon; pantry keeper Omi, a capuchin; producer Pava, a lemur.

[SFX: SCIENCE THEME, JAR LID POPS.]

FENN: We have breaking news from the pantry. A jar has been opened.

LENI: It contains oats.

OMI: It was opened this morning.

PAVA: We need a closer look.

ACT ONE

Fenn asks Leni to describe the discovery. She says the lid was sealed yesterday and opened today. Omi says she made breakfast. Fenn asks whether the oats were exposed to air.

OMI: Yes. That is how I poured them.

PAVA: The implications are wide.

Leni says the oats are still oats. Fenn asks if the team can test the jar. Omi lets them shake it gently. The oats rustle.

[SFX: OATS SHAKE.]

FENN: The contents are moving.

ACT TWO

Pava places a microphone beside the jar. Leni counts the oats by sound, but the grains make a soft rattle. Omi says the jar is almost empty. Fenn asks how they can know. Omi tips it; a few flakes fall out.

PAVA: A dramatic depletion.

LENI: The jar has been used.

Fenn suggests the pantry keeper may have hidden the oats. Omi opens the cupboard. There are two more jars, one full and one half-full. Pava calls it a supply network.

OMI: I bought oats in a bag and poured them into jars.

Leni checks the labels. One says "breakfast"; another says "baking." Fenn asks whether the labels are coded. Omi says they indicate what she uses them for. Pava asks if anyone has checked the date. The jars are both fresh.

Fenn asks Omi to show how she opens the jar. She grips the lid, twists, and pours oats into a bowl. Pava asks whether the pop means the seal was broken. Omi says yes, that is how a sealed lid works. Leni examines the rim and finds no damage. Fenn asks if the jar should be labeled with the date it was opened. Omi says she can add today's date beside the breakfast label. Pava calls it a data point. Omi writes the date on a small card and tucks it under the jar. Fenn asks whether every pantry item needs a broadcast. Omi says only the ones the station keeps announcing. Leni reads the label aloud, then returns the jar to the shelf. The sound is ordinary glass on wood. Pava asks for a final suspense sting. Fenn refuses; the jar is simply back where it belongs.

Pava asks if the forecast has a tomorrow section. Fenn says tomorrow's breakfast has not happened yet. Leni says the oats will probably still be in the jar. Omi checks the lid and confirms it is closed. Pava asks if that is enough certainty. Fenn says it is enough for tonight.

Omi pours a final spoonful into a bowl, then sets the lid beside the jar. Leni listens for the sound the seal will make when Omi closes it. Fenn asks if the oats are disappearing. Omi says they are still in the jar; she is waiting for the announcer to stop asking about them.

ACT THREE

Leni explains that the only new event is that one jar has been opened and breakfast was made. Fenn asks what listeners should do. Omi says close the lid after pouring so the oats stay dry. Pava tries to make that sound like a discovery.

PAVA: In a development that may reshape morning routines...

OMI: Put the lid on.

Fenn places the lid on the jar. It seals with a pop.

[SFX: LID CLICKS.]

TAG

FENN: Our pantry correspondent will return after breakfast.

OMI: You can return the microphone now.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC.]
