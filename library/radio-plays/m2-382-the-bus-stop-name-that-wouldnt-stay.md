# The Bus Stop Name That Wouldn't Stay

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: transit information radio segments that rename a stop for sponsors and leave drivers using different directions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Tavi, a capuchin; dispatcher Miri, a lemur; driver Jo, a macaque; passenger Lolo, a gibbon.

[SFX: BUS ENGINE IDLES. RADIO MIC OPENS. AUTOMATED VOICE: NEXT STOP, OLD FIG TREE.]

TAVI: We are live from the newly sponsored Old Fig Tree stop, now called Bright Branch Plaza.

MIRI: The route map still says Old Fig Tree.

JO: My driver display says Bright Branch Plaza, but the printed card says Old Fig Tree.

LOLO: I pulled the bell for Old Fig Tree. The bus has not stopped.

JO: The new audio file triggers at the plaza entrance, one block later.

MIRI: That is the sponsor's address. The public stop is still by the fig tree.

TAVI: The advertisement says the stop has moved.

MIRI: The stop has not moved. The advertisement moved its name.

[SFX: BUS BRAKES. DOOR OPENS AT A CURB.]

LOLO: Is this Old Fig Tree?

JO: Yes. The sign has the old name.

MIRI: I have sent the contractor a proof. The route name is in large letters and the sponsor is below it.

JO: Until it arrives, I'll make one announcement at the old curb and one before the plaza.

LOLO: The bus sounds the same at both places. How will I know which one it means?

JO: I will call the stop name when the doors open, not when the bus passes the sponsor's building.

[SFX: BUS SIGNAL CLICKS. BRAKES SET AT THE OLD FIG TREE.]

JO: Old Fig Tree. Doors open.

LOLO: That's the stop.

TAVI: The live show can use the driver's announcement as the test.

MIRI: The route display will keep the map name until the new sign is installed.

TAVI: The new sign is arriving tomorrow.

MIRI: The sign should show the route name and the sponsor beneath it. The bus needs one stop location.

JO: The display just announced Bright Branch Plaza again.

[SFX: AUTOMATED VOICE: NEXT STOP, BRIGHT BRANCH PLAZA.]

LOLO: I don't know whether to get off here or farther down.

MIRI: We need to pause the sponsored announcement. Drivers will use the map and the physical sign.

TAVI: But the campaign needs airtime today.

MIRI: We can say "Old Fig Tree, sponsored by Bright Branch Plaza." The name stays clear.

MIRI: The route office will update the map after the stop name is approved.

TAVI: We can leave the sponsor message in the audio without changing the location.

LOLO: Then I can plan tomorrow's trip from the same map.

JO: I can change the display to the map label at the next safe stop.

[SFX: BUS SIGNAL CLICKS. BUS PULLS TO CURB; BRAKES SET.]

JO: Parked. I am selecting the route name.

[SFX: BUTTONS PRESS. A CHIME SOUNDS.]

JO: Old Fig Tree is back on the display.

LOLO: Now I know where to get off.

TAVI: The sponsor will still be mentioned?

MIRI: Yes. After the stop name, not instead of it.

[SFX: AUTOMATED VOICE: OLD FIG TREE. SPONSORED BY BRIGHT BRANCH PLAZA.]

JO: That's the correct order. The curb is the same, the name matches the map, and passengers hear the sponsor second.

TAVI: We can put the new panel up with both lines, same font size.

MIRI: The route map, driver's display and audio must agree before the campaign starts.

LOLO: I will tell my neighbor. She waits at the tree after work.

TAVI: The bus is leaving. Next stop is Market Lane.

[SFX: DOORS CLOSE. BUS ENGINE RISES; WHEELS MOVE.]

JO: I will tell the next passengers the old name first.

MIRI: Keep it that way until the panel is installed.

TAVI: Bright Branch Plaza has joined the route, but it has not replaced the tree.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF. BUS PASSES UNDER FIG LEAVES.]
