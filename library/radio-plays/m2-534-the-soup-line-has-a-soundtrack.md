# The Soup Line Has a Soundtrack

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio cooking demonstrations where every utensil gets a sponsor cue*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Cook Pip, a capuchin; announcer Vex, an orangutan; producer Mara, a macaque; timer Odo, a gibbon; volunteer Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Community kitchen. Pot simmers; spoon taps rim.

VEX: Welcome to One Pot Radio. Every ingredient gets a musical cue, sponsored by the spoon company.

PIP: The soup is already simmering. Please don't bang the spoon while I taste it.

MARA: We have one pot of vegetable soup for the volunteer table. The recipe card is beside the cutting board.

ODO: Timer set for ten minutes. Burner is low.

VEX: The carrots enter with a fanfare.

NARI: They're already in the pot. They went in before the broth.

VEX: Then we can replay their entrance.

PIP: We do not add the carrots twice. The soup has enough.

SFX: Spoon scrapes gently along pot edge.

MARA: Odo, move the microphone away from the burner. Keep the cable behind the table.

ODO: Cable clipped. The timer is still audible.

VEX: I have a sponsor bell for the salt.

PIP: I have salted the broth. Don't add more until I taste it.

VEX: A dramatic pause before seasoning.

PIP: It needs pepper, not salt. The vegetables are already salted.

NARI: The timer has six minutes left. The soup is steaming but not boiling hard.

VEX: Could we raise the heat for a stronger cooking sound?

PIP: No. The pot is hot enough, and the bottom will catch if we rush it.

SFX: Ladle fills a small cup. Blow, sip.

PIP: Flavor is balanced. I'll turn off the burner and let it sit while the bread is sliced.

ODO: Timer has three minutes. Do you still need it?

PIP: Yes, for the bread in the toaster oven, not the soup. It's on the separate counter.

MARA: Thank you for saying which timer. Keep the soup covered and move the tasting spoon to the wash tray.

VEX: The soup's final sound is a lid closing.

SFX: Lid settles. Toaster dings once.

NARI: Bread is ready. The volunteers can serve from the side table.

PIP: I'll carry the pot with both handles and set it on the heat pad. No one reaches across the cord.

MARA: Heat pad is clear. The serving ladle is beside the bowls.

VEX: Our soundtrack was mostly careful kitchen noises.

PIP: That's what supper sounds like when nobody adds a second handful of salt for the sponsor.


NARI: The volunteers are ready at the side table. We need to move the pot without bringing the spoon microphone with it.

MARA: Odo, mute the kitchen mic while Pip lifts the handles.

SFX: Mic switch clicks off; pot handles creak.

PIP: Pot is on the heat pad. The burner is off. I'll ladle a small taste into the marked cup for the kitchen log.

ODO: The timer belongs to the toaster oven. It has stopped, and the oven is off.

VEX: I was going to play the ding again for the closing music.

PIP: The bread is already out. The ding is not a request for another batch.

MARA: Serve the soup now while it is warm. Keep the topping bowls on the far side of the table.

NARI: The line is moving, bowls are full, and no one is reaching over the pot.

SFX: Ladle dips, bowls set down.


VEX: Before we sign off, should I ask the volunteers for a review?

PIP: Ask if they have soup. Don't ask them to rate the spoon.

SFX: Bowls settle on table; spoon clinks once.

MARA: Kitchen is clear.

TAG

ODO: Timer is off.

VEX: Spoon sponsor, we saved the soup.

MARA: We saved the soup from you.
