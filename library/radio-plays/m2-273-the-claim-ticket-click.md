# The Claim Ticket Click

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio variety orchestras assembled from found objects, where claimed props vanish during the performance*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST SERA, an orangutan
MUSICIAN VEX, a macaque
CUSTODIAN NIMA, a lemur
VISITOR KET, a squirrel monkey
DIRECTOR BENA, a gibbon
SCARF OWNER, a macaque collecting a wool scarf

[SFX: CLOSET DOOR OPENS, OBJECTS RUSTLE.]

SERA: We are live from the town lost-and-found, where director Bena plans to assemble an orchestra from unclaimed objects.

BENA: The concert begins at six. We have two scarves, a saucepan, a cracked umbrella, and one boot.

VEX: That is not an orchestra.

NIMA: It is what we have.

KET: I found the other boot.

BENA: Excellent. We can have a duet.

VEX: Boots do not play.

KET: They squeak when I press the heel.

[SFX: BOOT SQUEAK.]

VEX: That is a percussion instrument.

BENA: Mark it as first chair.

NIMA: Please do not polish it. Someone may claim it.

SERA: The orchestra is rehearsing with property that may leave the building at any moment.

BENA: We need a reliable melody. Vex, test the saucepan.

[SFX: SPOON TAPS SAUCEPAN.]

VEX: It has a bright tone.

NIMA: The saucepan is still damp.

VEX: It has a bright damp tone.

Sera shakes the umbrella. A rib rattles. Bena writes down the pitch. Nima hangs the scarves between two chairs to make a soft curtain, but the fringe tangles around Vex's wrist.

[SFX: FABRIC SNAGS.]

VEX: The curtain has captured the conductor.

BENA: We will use it as a visual cue.

NIMA: There is no audience if we are behind a curtain.

KET: The lost-and-found has one audience member. He is looking for his hat.

The visitor approaches the desk. He recognizes the saucepan as his and takes it. Vex's main instrument is gone.

BENA: We have lost the kettle.

NIMA: That is a saucepan.

BENA: We have lost our lead percussion.

[SFX: EMPTY DESK TAPS.]

SERA: The orchestra has experienced its first departure.

VEX: I can play the boot.

The visitor tries the saucepan handle, then leaves without taking the umbrella. Nima notices a paper tag attached to it: "Found near the fountain; owner unknown."

NIMA: The umbrella is available, but the tag says not to bend it.

VEX: We can play the ribs without bending them.

[SFX: UMBRELLA OPENS, RIBS RATTLE.]

BENA: That is a chord.

KET: It is a warning.

A second visitor claims one scarf. The curtain drops on the remaining chair. Bena tells Nima to hold the edge, but the fabric slides. Vex strikes the boot heel, and the squeak matches the umbrella rattle.

[SFX: BOOT SQUEAK, UMBRELLA RATTLE.]

SERA: The two objects have found a common rhythm.

BENA: Add the second boot.

KET: It belongs to the same owner as the first.

BENA: Then we have a reunited pair.

NIMA: We cannot keep them for a concert.

VEX: We will play them before the owner returns.

The band rehearses a short piece using only found sounds: the umbrellas rattle, boots squeak, scarf fringe brushes the chair, and Vex taps a cardboard claim ticket. The rhythm is uneven but deliberate.

[SFX: FOUND-OBJECT RHYTHM BUILDS.]

SERA: A caller has sent a message: "My boots are outside the office. I hear them playing."

KET: The owner is back.

BENA: We have one minute for the finale.

NIMA: Return the boots first.

The owner collects both boots. Their squeak leaves the rhythm. Vex taps the empty claim counter instead; it makes a hollow wooden beat. The orchestra continues with the objects still available, ending on the soft brush of the scarf.

[SFX: COUNTER TAP, UMBRELLA RATTLE, SCARF BRUSH, STOP.]

SERA: The concert has ended.
 
The audience in the hallway claps. Nima puts out a claim form beside the counter. The scarf owner returns and asks why the fabric is hanging from two chairs.

NIMA: We used it as a curtain.

SCARF OWNER: It is a scarf.

VEX: It also brushed a chair in B flat.

SCARF OWNER: It is wool.

The owner takes the scarf. The sound ensemble loses its soft ending, so Vex taps the claim stamp against the counter once more. The stamp's spring makes a crisp click.

[SFX: STAMP CLICKS.]

BENA: That is a better ending.

NIMA: The stamp belongs to the office.

BENA: We can borrow it for the next show.

NIMA: Only if you sign it out.

Bena fills out the form, then stamps the line marked purpose. The click concludes the performance.
 Every claimed object went home, and the counter remains in the building.

BENA: We have found our permanent instrument.

NIMA: Do not lose the counter.

[SFX: CLAIM TICKET STAMP, ROOM TONE.]
