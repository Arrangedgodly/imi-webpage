# Bulletin from the Breakroom

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Investigative television news and urgent newsroom updates*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[NEWS STING: brass fanfare, a microwave beep, then a paper shuffle.]

ANCHOR ADA: Good evening. I'm Ada Vale. Tonight, the office printer has been producing an extra page after every job. Who is paying for the mysterious sheet, and why does it say "You are doing great" in twelve-point italic?

[REMOTE LINE CRACKLES.]

ADA: We go live to reporter Finch in the copy room. Finch?

FINCH, REPORTER: Ada, I'm a monkey reporter standing three feet from the printer. My tail is holding the mic, because the copy room has no clean surface. Behind me, the paper tray is open, the toner warning is flashing, and a colleague has placed a tiny knitted hat on the machine.

ADA: What does the hat tell us?

FINCH: It tells us someone cares about this printer, Ada. It does not tell us who is printing the extra page.

ADA: Have you asked the printer?

FINCH: It has no comment. It did make a grinding noise when I mentioned the budget.

[SFX: PRINTER SPITS OUT A PAGE.]

FINCH: We have a fresh development. Another message. "You are doing great." Same font, same paper. We checked the supply closet. The paper comes from a box marked "Board Presentation."

ADA: A serious source.

FINCH: The most serious. The box has a lid.

ADA: Who had access?

FINCH: All staff, two visitors, and a courier who says he only prints at home.

ADA: We have obtained a statement from office manager Priya.

PRIYA, RECORDED: I noticed the extra pages last Friday. I was grooming a colleague by the paper tray when I found one stuck to his sleeve. I thought someone was testing alignment. Then my daughter called and told me to drink water. The font looked like her font. But the printer had no reason to know my daughter's font.

ADA: You suspected a printer conspiracy.

PRIYA: I suspected a setting. I work in operations. We suspect settings first.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TYPING, A SINGLE KEY STICKS.]

FINCH: Ada, we're examining the print queue now. The queue shows three jobs: "Quarterly Forecast," "Happy Note," and "Do Not Print Happy Note."

ADA: Who submitted the last job?

FINCH: The account is listed as "Conference Room." That room has no login credentials.

ADA: Can a room print?

FINCH: It can if someone forgets to log out of the shared laptop. We found an open document with a list of affirmations and a note: "For team morale. Anonymous."

ADA: Anonymous. That word again.

FINCH: The document was authored by Trent in finance. Trent says he made it for the wellness committee. The committee says it never requested it.

TRENT: The first page was supposed to be a private test. I didn't know the printer would make a copy every time anybody printed anything.

ADA: Why did you title the setting "Happy Note"?

TRENT: I wanted to find it later.

ADA: And "Do Not Print Happy Note"?

TRENT: That one was supposed to turn it off.

FINCH: We can confirm the printer was set to append the affirmation sheet to every job. A box had been checked. Not by the printer. By a person.

TRENT: I checked it. I thought it said "print notes separately."

ADA: Did you consider the cost?

TRENT: I thought morale was priceless.

ADA: Paper is priced by the ream.

TRENT: I know that now.

[SFX: THE PRINTER RUMBLES.]

FINCH: The extra page has stopped. The knitted hat stays. The team has voted to keep one copy of Trent's message pinned above the machine, but only because it is now a single page.

ADA: And the board presentation?

FINCH: It will be delivered without the words "You are doing great." The board already knows.

ADA: Does it?

FINCH: We have not confirmed that, Ada.

ADA: That's all the time we have. Tonight's investigation found no mastermind, no sabotage, and no printer with feelings. It found a checkbox and a finance analyst with good intentions and poor menu-reading skills.

[SFX: MICROWAVE DINGS.]

ADA: At eleven, our next report asks whether the breakroom soup is soup or a meeting request. I'm Ada Vale. Good night, and check your print settings.

[STING. END.]
