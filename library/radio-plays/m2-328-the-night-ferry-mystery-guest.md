# The Night Ferry Mystery Guest

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: celebrity interview programs that disguise routine guests as dramatic surprises*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Odo, a baboon; guest Piri, a gibbon; producer Seli, a lemur; ferry keeper Nara, a macaque.

[SFX: MYSTERY MUSIC, DOCK ROPE.]

ODO: A special guest is waiting behind the curtain.

NARA: There is no curtain on the dock.

PIRI: I am standing by the boat.

SELI: Keep the reveal.

ACT ONE

Odo asks listeners to guess the guest. Piri says she came to take the ferry home. Odo asks her not to give away the surprise. Nara asks whether Piri has a ticket. She does.

ACT TWO

Seli plays three clues: "has a ticket," "stands beside the dock," and "knows the ferry keeper." Odo asks the audience to guess. Nara says it is Piri. Odo says the answer is locked until the final segment.

PIRI: My boat is leaving.

ACT TWO-B

Seli says the guest must wait for the cue. Nara explains that the ferry leaves on its posted schedule. Odo asks Piri what it is like to be a mystery guest. She says she is cold and wants to board. Odo turns that into a clue: the guest has mentioned the weather.

PIRI: My ticket is valid. The boat is leaving.

SELI: We have not played the reveal music.

NARA: We have played it twice.

Seli starts a drumroll. A bird flaps across the gangway and lands on the rail. Everyone waits until it flies to a post. Piri asks if the bird is the guest. Odo says the answer is still locked.

NARA: Last call for boarding.

Odo tries a five-second countdown. Piri takes one step toward the gangway at three. Nara asks her to stop until the bird clears the rail. She waits, then holds up her ticket. Odo asks what secret she brought. Piri says she has apples for a pie.

ODO: The guest is carrying fruit.

PIRI: I am carrying a bag.

Seli plays a rising chord. Nara checks the bag's knot and confirms it is safe to stow. Piri refuses to describe the crust before she has boarded. Odo says that is a clue about the pastry. Piri says it is a clue about wanting to go home.

The ferry horn sounds. Seli stops the music; the bird is off the rail, but Odo is still holding the microphone across the gangway. Nara points to the boarding line. He steps aside at last. Piri waits by the ticket post while Nara calls the final passenger.

Piri looks at the ticket clock. The scheduled departure is less than a minute away.

PIRI: The ferry is ready. I am ready. The mystery is only happening in this microphone.

ODO: The audience has not guessed.

NARA: The audience can hear you saying her name.

Seli checks the transmission light. It is live.

SELI: We have broadcast every clue, including the answer.

ODO: Then the answer is very well hidden.

NARA: I can untie the gangway now, or I can wait for the program to finish. I cannot do both.

Odo reaches for the final cue card. It is stuck under the microphone clip. Piri removes it with one quick pull. The card says "REVEAL GUEST." She hands it to Odo, who turns it around and reads it aloud.

PIRI: I am not reading that into a live microphone.

ODO: We have a cue card.

NARA: You have a boarding call.

Seli gives Odo a three-second count. This time he does not add a drumroll. The dock stays quiet except for the water and the ferry's idling motor. Piri waits at the line, ticket in hand, until Nara calls her forward.

ACT THREE

Odo reveals Piri as the mystery guest. Nara opens the gate. Piri boards and waves from the deck. Odo asks her about the surprise.

PIRI: I was going home.

TAG

SELI: Next week, a mystery guest arrives by the path.

NARA: It is the mail carrier.
