# The Kite Festival's Emergency Broadcast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: festival radio coverage that inflates routine delays into breaking news and keeps correspondents on air through obvious non-events*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Puck, a macaque; field reporter Nima, a langur; kite maker Boro, a gibbon; producer Leli, a capuchin.

[SFX: FESTIVAL CROWD. WIND BUFFETS MICROPHONE.]

PUCK: We interrupt the kite festival for breaking news. A red kite has stopped moving.

NIMA: It is tied to a bench. The maker is adjusting its tail.

LELI: Stay with it. We have a live camera and the red banner says BREAKING.

BORO: The wind is gusty. I brought the kite down before a knot pulled loose.

PUCK: Is the festival in danger?

BORO: No. I am holding it in my hands.

[SFX: FABRIC FLAPS, THEN GOES STILL.] 

NIMA: The kite is safe. Boro is checking the cross spar.

LELI: Ask what he saw in the sky. We need a development.

BORO: I saw a cloud and a child running toward the snack cart.

PUCK: A child is running toward the snack cart. We are tracking two stories now.

NIMA: The kite has a loose tail knot. Boro is tying it again.

[SFX: STRING PULLS THROUGH A LOOP; KNOT TIGHTENS.] 

BORO: Fixed. I will wait for a steady gust, then launch from the marked field.

LELI: Can you say, "The situation is developing"?

BORO: It is not developing. The knot is finished.

PUCK: The crowd has gathered around the bench. We have a visual of the red kite at rest.

NIMA: They are gathered because the snack cart is parked behind it.

PUCK: The crowd is moving toward the cart, not away from the kite.

LELI: The camera operator followed them. Our live shot is now a row of buns.

BORO: At least the buns are not tied to the bench.

NIMA: One bun has a ribbon. It is attached to the cart handle, and the cart is still parked.

[SFX: CART BELL DINGS. CROWD SHIFTS LEFT.] 

PUCK: The crowd has shifted. Could the kite be moving?

NIMA: The crowd is moving. The kite is still tied to the bench.

LELI: We should say the scene is changing.

BORO: I am untieing the kite now. The field marshal is checking the gust.

[SFX: WIND GUST RISES, THEN FALLS.] 

NIMA: Marshal says wait. The cloth is pulling too hard sideways.

PUCK: We have a hold on the launch. Red kite remains grounded.

BORO: That is the sensible choice.

LELI: We need one more update before the banner times out.

PUCK: The red kite is not in the air. This is not an emergency. It is waiting for a safer breeze.

NIMA: Another kite has launched at the far end. It is yellow and flying normally.

PUCK: We can compare the two situations.

BORO: No. The red one has a repaired knot and is waiting. The yellow one is flying. That's all.

[SFX: FESTIVAL BELL RINGS. WIND EASES.] 

NIMA: The marshal gives the red kite a go. Boro is walking to the launch line.

BORO: Ready. Please step behind the rope.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS STOP. KITE CLOTH RUSTLES; STRING PAYS OUT.] 

PUCK: The red kite has launched. It is rising over the field.

LELI: Change the banner to GOOD NEWS.

PUCK: The red kite is flying. The knot stayed fast, and the breeze is steady.

NIMA: Boro is smiling. The crowd has gone back to flying their own kites.

BORO: That is the point of a festival.

LELI: Our breaking coverage will continue until the kite turns left.

PUCK: It turned left.

NIMA: It followed the wind. I am returning the microphone to the booth.

[SFX: MIC WINDSCREEN REMOVED; FESTIVAL MUSIC SWELLS.] 

PUCK: The only breaking item today was the snack cart's paper napkin. We are back to the program.

[SFX: A NAPKIN FLUTTERS PAST THE MIC; HOST LAUGHS.]
