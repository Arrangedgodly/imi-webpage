# The Songbird Traffic Report

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: commuter reports that describe ordinary foot traffic as a transit crisis*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Reporter Odo, baboon; host Nara, macaque; crossing guard Piri, gibbon; cyclist Meli, capuchin.

[SFX: MORNING BIRDS, TWO BICYCLE BELLS.]

NARA: How is traffic on the garden path?

ODO: A flock of songbirds has stopped near the berry bush.

PIRI: They are eating.

MELI: I have dismounted my bicycle.

ACT ONE

Odo asks Piri how long the delay is. Piri says the birds arrived three minutes ago. Meli says she can wait. Nara asks whether there is a detour. Piri says the path is wide enough for people to walk around the bush.

ODO: We have a developing bottleneck.

PIRI: There are four birds.

Odo asks Meli to ring the bell again for the report. Meli says that could startle them. Nara says the traffic update must not cause more traffic.

ACT TWO

A squirrel crosses the path with an acorn. Odo reports a secondary vehicle. Piri asks him not to compare wildlife to traffic. The squirrel pauses while a bird drops a berry. Meli moves her bicycle to the grass so the squirrel can pass.

[SFX: TIRES ON GRASS.]

NARA: A courteous detour.

ODO: The path has reopened.

PIRI: The birds are still there.

Odo asks if the flow has improved. Piri says the walkers pass one at a time. Meli rings her bell softly after the birds finish eating. They flutter to a nearby branch. The path is clear.

ACT TWO-B

Odo asks Piri to hold the crossing while he interviews the birds. Piri says birds do not give interviews. Meli waits with the bicycle. One bird drops a berry; another hops down to retrieve it. Odo calls this a transfer operation. Piri says it is lunch. Meli takes the bicycle by its handlebars and walks it around the bush, giving the flock room. Odo asks whether the path has reopened. Piri says the path never closed. A young bird flutters into the lane and lands near the rear tire. Meli stops. Odo describes a near miss, but Piri says no one moved until the bird flew to the branch. Nara asks why the report uses the word "incident" when everyone paused safely. Odo says "pause" sounds too ordinary for a bulletin. Piri replies that ordinary is what happened. He reluctantly re-records the update: a cyclist dismounted, a bird moved, and the path remained passable.

Nara asks Odo to listen to the raw recording before he labels it. The playback includes Meli's bell, a bird chirp, and Piri's calm direction. There is no crash or alarm. Odo removes "bottleneck" from the headline and writes "brief courtesy pause." Piri says that describes the path exactly. Meli rings the bell only after the flock has moved away. The birds remain on the branch, still eating, while the cyclist continues at walking speed.

Nara adds that the birds chose the bush because it was full of fruit. Odo asks if they will clear the lane tomorrow. Piri says they may return, and the path will still be there. He changes the traffic map to show a walking route around the bush instead of a warning symbol.

The report ends with Meli pedaling slowly past the berries while Piri keeps the path clear.

ACT THREE

Nara asks Odo to summarize the delay. He says there was a brief pause near the berry bush, followed by a safe pass. Piri thanks Meli for waiting. Meli says she was not in a hurry.

Odo announces that the path is moving again. A second flock lands on the bush.
