# The Pet Adoption Hour Has a Paperwork Parade

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: adoption radio that makes forms interrupt the animal introductions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Nari, a lemur; coordinator Mara, a macaque; announcer Vex, an orangutan; volunteer Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Adoption center lobby. A soft dog bark; clipboard pages turn.

VEX: Welcome to Meet Your Companion. First, the parade of forms.

MARA: First, we meet the animals. Then we answer questions and complete the right paperwork with the coordinator.

PIP: I am interested in the quiet older dog in the corner. I brought my household form.

ODO: The form is missing the emergency contact line.

VEX: We cannot proceed until every box has been checked in a dramatic order.

NARI: The dog is resting behind the low gate. We can introduce her without taking her out of the quiet space.

MARA: Her name is Fern. She likes slow walks and needs a home without stairs. Pip, does that fit your place?

PIP: My apartment has one flight of stairs. I can carry supplies, but Fern would have to climb them every day.

MARA: Then let's not assume she's the right match. We have an older rabbit who uses a low pen and is comfortable indoors.

VEX: A plot twist: the form has changed the cast.

ODO: It hasn't. Pip answered a question about the dog's needs.

SFX: Gate latch, then a soft thump of a rabbit shifting straw.

NARI: This is Moss. He is calm around people and has a low enclosure. He needs daily hay and a secure indoor space.

PIP: My living room can fit the pen. I want to ask about the hay schedule and vet records before deciding.

MARA: That's the right next step. The form can wait while you meet him from outside the pen.

VEX: Can we announce the adoption now?

PIP: No. I haven't decided, and I need to talk with my housemate.

ODO: I'll add the emergency contact later, after Pip knows whether to continue. We shouldn't collect a blank answer just to fill a box.

NARI: Moss has moved to the far side of the pen. We can give him space.

MARA: We'll arrange a second visit tomorrow. Pip can bring the housemate and review the care sheet.

VEX: The paperwork parade has paused before the finish line.

MARA: The animal's comfort comes first. There is no prize for signing quickly.

PIP: I appreciate that. Please keep the care sheet with my name at the desk, not on the public table.

ODO: Done. The visit note is in the private folder.

NARI: Fern is resting; Moss has room; the lobby is quiet.

VEX: We have no adoption to announce.

MARA: We have a visit scheduled. That's the honest ending.


ODO: The care sheet lists hay, water, exercise, and a quiet indoor pen. Pip can take a copy home and discuss it with the housemate before tomorrow's visit.

PIP: I'll read it tonight. If the space works, we'll return together and ask about Moss's medical history.

MARA: We can answer those questions before anyone decides. Fern will remain in her quiet space, and Moss keeps his pen.

VEX: What should I tell listeners who expected an adoption announcement?

NARI: Tell them a careful visit is progress. The animals are not prizes to be matched before the show ends.

SFX: Paper folds; pen is set down.

ODO: Pip has a plain care-sheet copy to take home; the visit note is in the private folder. The emergency contact is still blank because no placement is being made today.

PIP: That's right. I know what I need to find out next.

MARA: Appointment set for tomorrow at three. We'll end the broadcast quietly so the animals can rest.

SFX: The lobby recording light clicks off; rabbit rustles once.
