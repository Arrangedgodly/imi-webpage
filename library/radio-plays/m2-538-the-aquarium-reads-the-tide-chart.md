# The Aquarium Reads the Tide Chart

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: educational radio segment where hosts mistake feeding time for a tidal emergency*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Presenter Nari, a lemur; host Vex, an orangutan; caretaker Mara, a macaque; technician Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Aquarium gallery. Water pump hums, then a feeding bell chimes.

VEX: The tide tank is rising. We may have a flood in progress.

MARA: The water level is at its marked line. The bell means feeding time.

NARI: The chart shows today's simulated tide cycle. It rises by design and drains back into the same tank.

PIP: I heard the bell and thought the animals were being moved.

ODO: The pump is running normally. The sensor is reading the tank, not the ocean outside.

VEX: Could the chart be more dramatic? A red arrow would look urgent.

MARA: It already uses blue for rising and gray for falling. Red would imply a warning we don't have.

SFX: Water trickles through a return pipe.

NARI: The level is reaching the high mark. The tide display will pause there for a minute.

VEX: The tank is at its peak. Should we evacuate the gallery?

MARA: No. The floor is dry, and the rail is clear. Please keep visitors behind the viewing line.

PIP: I'll move my bag back from the line.

ODO: The feeding tray is on the shelf. Mara, do you want to place it now?

MARA: Yes, the bell has sounded. I'll lower the tray using the approved handle.

SFX: Tray lowers into water; small splashes.

NARI: The fish approach the food. The water remains within the marked range.

VEX: A spectacular migration to the tray.

MARA: They are feeding. The tide cycle is separate from the feeding cue.

ODO: The pump has switched to return. Water level is descending at the normal rate.

SFX: Pump pitch changes; water drains.

PIP: The chart's gray line matches the level now.

NARI: At low tide the system stops, then begins the next simulated cycle. We can explain that to visitors without calling it a rescue.

VEX: I will announce: "The tank is safe, the fish are fed, and the water is following its chart."

MARA: Good. Add that this is a demonstration tank, not a real shoreline.

PIP: The sign is behind the column. Could we move it where people enter?

ODO: I can place a duplicate at the entry desk, not beside the tank.

NARI: That will help visitors understand the model before they hear the bell.


ODO: The duplicate sign is printed. I'll place it on the entry desk, where visitors can read it before they reach the tank.

PIP: The old arrow points toward the glass. Someone might think the feeding bell is an alarm.

MARA: Move that arrow to the model label. Keep the emergency exit sign unobstructed.

SFX: Sign lifts; paper clips tap the desk.

NARI: It now reads: "Model tide cycle and feeding bell. Water stays in the tank."

VEX: Could we add a red border to attract attention?

MARA: Use blue. Red is reserved for actual hazard notices in this building.

PIP: The fish are still eating. The tank is at the falling mark.

ODO: The pump has returned to its normal hum. No leak on the floor.



NARI: The feeding is over. The bell is silent, and the water level is descending as expected.

MARA: We'll log the cycle and leave the entry sign where visitors can see it.

VEX: No evacuation, then.


PIP: I'll take the visitor question card to the desk. It asks whether this is a real tide, and the new sign answers it.

NARI: Good. Put it beside the model label.

TAG

VEX: The tide is out.

MARA: The tide is simulated.

VEX: The audience is still here.
