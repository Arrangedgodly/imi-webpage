# The Last Train of the Night Garden

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: suspenseful station announcements that stretch a minor delay into a cliffhanger*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Announcer Seli, a lemur; conductor Pava, a gibbon; passenger Toma, a macaque; station cat Miri, a small monkey.

[SFX: RAIL HUM, DISTANT NIGHT INSECTS.]

SELI: The Night Garden shuttle will depart in one minute.

PAVA: It departs when the gate opens.

TOMA: The gate is open.

PAVA: Then it will depart in one minute.

Miri sits beside the gate, grooming her arm. The shuttle waits with its doors open.

ACT ONE

Toma asks whether the delay is mechanical. Pava says the schedule was printed before the gate was built. Seli reads the announcement card, which says "Stand by for departure." She adds dramatic music.

[SFX: TWO LOW NOTES.]

SELI: We are standing by.

TOMA: So is the shuttle.

Pava checks the gate latch. It is open, but a loose ribbon hangs across the doorway. He lifts it. Miri reaches for the ribbon and pulls it back to her bed.

PAVA: The cat has closed the route.

MIRI: I am not a cat.

SELI: The station calls you a station cat.

MIRI: The station is wrong.

Toma offers Miri a folded cloth to replace the ribbon. She sniffs it and returns to grooming. Pava says the train cannot leave until the path is clear, but the path is clear. Seli changes the announcement to "A small obstacle may affect service."

ACT TWO

Miri walks onto the shuttle ramp and sits directly in front of the boarding line. Pava asks her to move. Miri climbs onto his shoe. He steps carefully around her; she steps with him.

TOMA: The obstruction is mobile.

SELI: Should I announce a moving obstruction?

PAVA: Announce that we are negotiating.

Pava offers a piece of fruit, then remembers the station rules prohibit feeding animals on the platform. Miri watches the fruit but stays on his shoe. Toma unfolds the cloth. Miri grabs one corner and starts dragging it toward her resting spot. Now the cloth stretches across the ramp.

SELI: The obstacle has acquired a tail.

PAVA: That is the cloth.

Toma folds the cloth into a small square and places it beside Miri's bed. She lets go. The ramp is clear. Pava invites the passengers aboard. Seli's microphone clicks, but she has no new card.

SELI: We regret to announce that the route may now be clear.

TOMA: It is clear.

SELI: I need confirmation from the schedule.

Pava finds the schedule card stuck to the back of the microphone. It says the shuttle is already two minutes late. He asks Seli to announce immediate boarding. She worries that "immediate" sounds too abrupt after all the suspense.

PAVA: The passengers are waiting.

SELI: They are listening.

TOMA: I am doing both.

Seli takes a breath and says the shuttle is ready. Toma boards. Pava checks the ramp; Miri has moved to the cloth and is settling down. The doors close gently.

Toma asks if the station can stop calling the shuttle "the last train." Pava says it is the last one tonight, which is technically true. Seli begins to record a clarification, but the doors have already closed. She must send the correction over the passenger speaker. Miri's ears twitch at the announcement; she lifts her head, then settles again when Seli says there are no more departures until morning.

ACT THREE

The shuttle begins to roll. Seli announces the departure time, which is exactly when the gate opened. Pava says the schedule is finally accurate. Toma asks whether the train will arrive on time.

PAVA: It will arrive when it arrives.

SELI: That sounds like another announcement.

PAVA: It is the whole railway.

TAG

The station speaker gives one last chime.

SELI: The Night Garden shuttle has departed.

MIRI: I was ready the whole time.

[SFX: RAIL HUM FADES INTO CRICKETS.]
