# The Quizmaster Lost the Buzz

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio quiz shows, capricious point awards, and hosts who mistake sound effects for contestants*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A radio quiz's broken buzzer keeps answering before contestants, until the scorekeeper separates real answers from sound effects.

Cast: Quizmaster Ralo, a baboon; contestant Nemi, a macaque; contestant Jori, a gibbon; engineer Tavi, an orangutan; caller Peli, a capuchin.

[SFX: THE BUZZER SOUNDS BEFORE THE THEME.]

RALO: The buzzer has answered the first question.

TAVI: There was no question.

NEMI: Then it gets no point.

JORI: It may appeal after the theme.

ACT ONE

Ralo explains the rules. Each round has exactly three clues. A correct identification earns one point; sound effects earn none. Nemi and Jori start at zero. The buzzer panel is already sticking, so Tavi unplugs its physical button and routes the game through a soundboard. The recorded buzz still plays when Ralo says "ready."

RALO: Ready?

[SFX: BUZZER.]

RALO: Jori gets a point.

JORI: I have not answered.

TAVI: The effect is mapped to the word "ready." The scoreboard heard it; the contestants did not.

NEMI: Then the score stays zero.

Ralo crosses out Jori's accidental point on the slate and says no score changed. He reads the first three clues: "It grows in a plant"; "it splits when dry"; "its seeds can rattle inside."

PELI: Is that the round sound from the orchard?

RALO: Yes. Contestants, answer.

NEMI: A dry seed pod.

JORI: A loose stool leg.

TAVI: The sound is a seed pod opening against a branch. Nemi is correct.

Ralo records Nemi one, Jori zero. Peli confirms the recording was made in the orchard and hangs up. The buzzer sounds again while the slate is updated. It does not change the score.

ACT TWO

Ralo reads the second question's three clues: "It makes a repeating clack"; "air comes out at the end"; "the sound stops when a tire is full." The buzzer fires before either answer. Ralo reaches for the scorecard, but Tavi mutes the effect channel.

JORI: A bicycle pump releasing air.

NEMI: A hand fan.

TAVI: The source is a pump at the repair bench. Jori has it.

RALO: Nemi one, Jori one. The effect remains at zero.

Nemi raises one hand to answer next time and tucks the other around her tail so it will not brush the microphone. Jori raises both hands; Ralo asks him to choose one. They agree that the remaining rounds will use spoken names, not the soundboard.

ACT TWO-B

The final question has three clues: "A bird makes the first sound"; "there are three notes, then two"; "a soft tap follows." Ralo plays the recording at normal speed. Jori says it is a bird call. Nemi adds that the tap comes from a finger touching the wooden perch. Tavi checks the source notes: the bird called twice, then the stagehand's finger touched the stand.

TAVI: The complete answer is a bird call and a separate finger tap.

RALO: Nemi gets one point. The score is two to one.

Jori asks whether there can be a tie. Ralo says only if they have equal points after the final round; the score is settled. Nemi wins and receives two tickets to the sound archive. She invites Jori to come as her guest. He accepts and asks if the archive has a quiet room where no buzzer can score him.

ACT THREE

Ralo announces Nemi as the winner. Tavi unplugs the soundboard's ready cue and removes the stuck button cap. The cap springs free with a small click. Ralo does not award a point for it. Nemi and Jori leave with the two tickets; Peli's orchard sound receives a thank-you credit, not a score.

Tavi tests the game panel. The physical buzzer now works only when pressed. Ralo says "ready," and nothing sounds. Jori returns to the booth to press the button once. It buzzes, then stops. Nemi says that is the first correct answer the buzzer has given all night.

TAG

[SFX: ONE QUIET CLICK AS THE BUTTON CAP IS SET ON THE TABLE.]

RALO: No points for the tag.

FINAL IMAGE: The scorecard reads Nemi 2, Jori 1, while the repaired buzzer rests beside the muted effect channel.
