# The Quietest River in the World

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: nature live-broadcast convention, where a crew tries to record a river without narrating over the sounds they came to capture*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Field host SELI, lemur; sound recorder PAVA, gibbon; guide KITO, macaque; producer ODO, baboon.

[SFX: RIVER FLOW, BIRDS.] 

SELI: We are standing beside the quietest river in the world.

KITO: It is flowing.

SELI: Quietly?

KITO: It is flowing at its usual speed.

PAVA: Hold the opening. The river is giving us a clean sound.

SELI: And now, dear listeners, we shall hear the tranquil murmur of-

PAVA: We heard your voice over it.

Odo signals for Seli to whisper. Seli whispers loudly.

SELI: We are whispering in the wilderness.

KITO: The birds have stopped.

[SFX: BIRD CALL STOPS.] 

PAVA: Don't explain the silence. Let it be silent.

Seli holds both hands up. At five seconds a plane passes overhead. Odo lowers the microphone.

ODO: We need another take.

KITO: The plane also lives in this part of the sky.

ODO: It isn't in our nature program.

KITO: The sky did not read your schedule.

Pava checks the headphones. A small macaque arrives with a bucket, fills it at a shallow inlet, and leaves. The water splashes.

SELI: Our river has a visitor.

KITO: That is Mina from the orchard. She is watering a plant.

ODO: Can we ask her to repeat it? The splash happened while Seli was talking.

Mina, already farther down the path, calls back that she is carrying a full bucket and has no free hands for a second take.

PAVA: The splash is on the recording. It is a real part of this place.

ODO: But it sounds like the water has a guest.

KITO: It does. The river is not a studio.

Odo asks for dramatic music to cover the plane. Pava refuses and moves the microphone beside the reeds. Seli tries a whisper from farther away, but the microphone catches every word.

SELI: The reeds have framed the river beautifully.

PAVA: They are reeds, not a frame.

SELI: Can I say they are framing it poetically?

KITO: You can say anything. The river will keep doing this.

The crew listens to a playback: water, two bird calls, the bucket splash, and Odo clearing his throat.

ODO: Remove the throat.

PAVA: I cannot remove a throat from a sound without cutting the bird call under it.

ODO: Then remove the bird and keep my throat.

KITO: The bird was here first.

Odo steps away to cough. Seli begins to announce the next take, but Pava gives her a look. Seli closes her mouth. The river keeps its steady rush. A second bird answers the first.

[SFX: TWO BIRDS, RIVER.] 

PAVA: That is the balance. Water, birds, reeds. No score.

SELI: No opening narration?

PAVA: We can record the narration after we leave.

KITO: If you leave, this place will continue without an announcer.

They test the quieter bend, where the stream spreads over flat stones. Seli listens, then starts to call it the quietest part. Kito points downstream; the channel narrows and grows louder.

ODO: We could move the title to the louder bend. "The Loudest River in the World" has stronger energy.

PAVA: It is one river. The microphone changed position.

SELI: What if the title asks a question? "Is This the Quietest River?"

KITO: Then the answer is no. It is the river by the orchard.

Pava sets the microphone down and records the bend without commentary. Odo's stomach growls.

[SFX: STOMACH GROWL OVER WATER.] 

ODO: Please remove that.

PAVA: It's a real part of this place.

Kito offers Odo a piece of fruit. Odo accepts it quietly. The recording continues: water, a bird, and a small bite.

SELI: That is our ending. No one speaks until the river fades.

The water continues. A bird calls once. Seli waits, then whispers as the microphone turns away.

SELI: It was quiet enough to hear us trying.
