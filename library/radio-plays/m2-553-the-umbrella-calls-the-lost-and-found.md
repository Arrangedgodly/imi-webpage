# The Umbrella Calls the Lost-and-Found

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: consumer call-in shows that give forgotten objects a dramatic voice*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Nari, a lemur; caller Vex, an orangutan; station clerk Mara, a macaque; finder Pip, a capuchin; producer Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Bus station broadcast booth beside the lost-property counter. Phone rings; rain on the window.

NARI: Welcome to Found Things Live. Our caller says an umbrella has been abandoned.

VEX, on phone: It is a green umbrella by the bus shelter. It has endured the storm alone.

MARA: It is in the lost-and-found cabinet at the station. Pip brought it in after the rain.

PIP: The umbrella was closed and dry inside. I turned it in with a note about which shelter I found it at.

ODO: The ticket number is 18. The owner can identify the handle and collect it at the desk.

VEX: Can the umbrella speak for itself?

MARA: We should not pretend an item has feelings. Ask the caller to identify it.

NARI: Vex, what makes you think it's yours?

VEX: It has a green canopy, wooden handle, and a small patch on one rib.

PIP: Those details match the property ticket. The name card is under the strap.

MARA: The name card says Vex. We can arrange pickup at the station desk, after he confirms the ticket number.

VEX: I can come to the station. I am two blocks away.

NARI: We will hold it at the desk. The program can stay on air while Mara checks the cabinet.

SFX: Footsteps; key turns; cabinet opens.

MARA: Umbrella is here, folded, handle intact, patch on the second rib. I'm placing it on the pickup shelf behind the desk.

VEX: The reunion is at hand.

PIP: It is on the shelf. You'll pick it up from a person, not reach into the cabinet.

NARI: What should listeners do if they find something?

MARA: Bring it to the staffed desk. Tell us the location. Keep contact details private; the owner can identify the article to the clerk.

ODO: I'll mark ticket 18 as awaiting pickup, not returned yet.

VEX: The umbrella would like to come home.

MARA: The owner can collect it when he arrives. We don't know what the umbrella wants.

NARI: While Vex walks over, I'll explain how we protect owners' details. The call-in line is recorded for the show, but the pickup record stays at the station.

ODO: I have marked the public note "green umbrella at east shelter." The name and handle description remain on the desk copy.

MARA: Right. We don't broadcast a claimant's phone number or ask callers to reveal a home address.

VEX: Can we make the pickup a live reunion?

MARA: It can be a live handoff if Vex agrees. The phone archive recorder is separate from the studio broadcast; I will stop archiving his call before he comes to the counter.

SFX: Phone archive recorder clicks off; its red light goes dark. The studio broadcast fader stays open. Station door chimes.

VEX: I don't mind appearing on radio, but I would rather not have my address read aloud.

NARI: It won't be. Give Mara your ticket and identify the patch to the clerk.

PIP: Vex is at the public side of the desk. The phone archive is off; the live studio mic remains open, as he agreed.

MARA: Ticket number?

VEX: Eighteen. Green canopy, wooden handle, patch on the second rib.

ODO: All match. Vex, please check the strap card before I close the ticket.

VEX: My name is written there. This is mine.

MARA: Handing it over now. Please open it outside so it doesn't drip on the station floor.

SFX: Umbrella strap snaps loose, but canopy stays closed.

VEX: The umbrella has been reunited with its owner.

NARI: We are closing ticket 18 as returned. Thank you for listening.
