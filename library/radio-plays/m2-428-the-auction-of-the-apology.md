# The Auction of the Apology

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: celebrity charity auctions, breathless bidding commentary, and hosts who turn every donation into a competitive spectacle*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A live charity auction offers a famous actor's apology as its headline lot, but the bidders keep trying to purchase the wrong thing.

Cast: Auctioneer Boro, a baboon; host Luma, a gibbon; donor Kiri, a macaque; caller Savi, a capuchin; sound producer Odo, an orangutan.

[SFX: GAVEL TAP, CROWD MURMUR.]

LUMA: Tonight's auction raises money for the community shade garden. Our headline lot is an apology from award-winning performer Kiri.

KIRI: I am here to apologize for taking the last stool from the rehearsal room.

BORO: A personal apology, delivered live, with no reserve price.

KIRI: The stool was not mine, and I should have asked before I carried it away.

SAVI: I bid twenty seeds for the stool.

LUMA: The lot is the apology.

SAVI: Is the stool included?

ACT ONE

Boro explains that the auction sells experiences, not props. Kiri donated one public apology about sharing rehearsal space. The first bidder offers ten garden tokens. Odo plays a sting.

BORO: We have ten tokens for accountability.

KIRI: They are for the shade garden.

BORO: The accountability is complimentary.

Savi raises the bid to twenty, then asks if the winner receives the stool. Luma repeats that the stool stays at the rehearsal room. Odo switches off the sting because it keeps sounding like a gong every time someone moves a chair.

Kiri rehearses the apology once more. Boro asks her to slow down so the bidders can appreciate each syllable. Kiri refuses to make an apology theatrical. She says the stool was needed for rehearsal and she took it home without checking whether anyone else was waiting. Luma adds that the garden needs funds for shade cloth and two young trees.

ACT TWO

A phone bidder offers thirty tokens for a signed transcript. Kiri can sign one later, but the apology stays spoken. Luma stops Boro from selling the words separately.

BORO: I am not selling the words. I am auctioning the occasion.

SAVI: Does the occasion come with seating?

LUMA: The hall has chairs.

SAVI: Are they borrowed?

The crowd laughs. Odo cuts the sting as Boro raises his gavel. Kiri confirms she returned the stool to the rehearsal room. The stage manager verifies it is beside the music stand.

KIRI: Then we can auction the apology without moving furniture.

BORO: The lot remains one sincere apology and a short conversation.

Savi increases the bid to fifty tokens, but asks whether the winner can choose the wording. Kiri says she will not read a script written by the highest bidder. The bid falls quiet. Luma asks bidders to support the garden rather than direct the apology. A child raises a sign showing five tokens and asks if small bids count.

LUMA: They count toward the shade cloth.

ACT TWO-B

Boro opens the phone line for one final bid. The caller offers to lend a shade tarp instead of tokens. Luma says the garden can use it, but the donation has to be inspected for holes and wind ties. Odo plays the sting softly, like an inspection bell. Kiri volunteers to help put it up after the event.

KIRI: I can carry the tarp, but I will ask before I borrow another stool.

BORO: That is the apology lot?

KIRI: That is what I learned from it.

The child raises five tokens. Savi raises ten. The tarp caller gives the tarp with no bid. Boro starts to announce a bidding war; Luma points to the posted rule that says there will be one winner and no pressure on guests to outbid one another. Savi withdraws her higher bid and contributes ten. The child keeps the five-token pledge. The community fund receives fifteen tokens and one checked tarp.

LUMA: The tarp will be inspected tomorrow, then installed with volunteers.

BORO: And the apology?

KIRI: I am sorry I took the stool without asking. I returned it. Next time I will check who needs it.

Savi says she accepts. Boro closes the lot at fifteen tokens. No one receives the stool, which remains at rehearsal.

ACT THREE

Luma announces the garden will add shade cloth to the sunny bench and plant the two donated saplings. The tarp caller confirms it is folded and ready for inspection. Kiri asks Boro to stop calling the apology a premium experience. He agrees and calls it what it is: an apology made in public, with a plan to do better.

Boro taps the gavel once, then sets it down. The audience applauds without music. Odo lets the applause end before opening the next lot: a guided garden walk with no celebrity narration. Savi bids one token. The child raises two. They agree to attend together and bring gloves.
