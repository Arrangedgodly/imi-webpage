# The Night Bus Announces Every Stop Twice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: transit radio satire about automated announcements competing with the driver*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Driver Mara, a macaque; announcer Vex, an orangutan; dispatcher Odo, a gibbon; passenger Pip, a capuchin; mechanic Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Night bus idles. Interior announcement chimes.

VEX, recording: Next stop, Lantern Square. Next stop, Lantern Square.

MARA: The system is playing the same announcement twice. I have not left the depot.

ODO, over radio: The route display shows Lantern Square as the next stop, but the bus is still at the terminal.

PIP: I got on here. I need the library stop, three stops after the square.

VEX: The announcement is giving passengers extra confidence.

MARA: It is confusing them. Odo, can you switch to the manual route card while Seli checks the speaker?

ODO: Manual card is ready. Do not touch the screen while parked unless the brake is set.

SFX: Brake engages. Engine idles.

SELI: The stop button is stuck. It is sending two signals each time the recorder advances.

VEX: Two announcements for the price of one.

PIP: I don't know whether we're moving or still at the terminal.

MARA: We are parked. I'll announce our location once, then we'll depart on schedule.

MARA: This is the night bus at Central Terminal. Lantern Square is the first stop.

ODO: I see the route card. It lists the correct order: Square, Garden Lane, Library.

SFX: Seli opens the speaker panel after switching off the announcement unit.

SELI: The button is clean but the spring is caught under its edge. I'll release it, then test once while parked.

VEX: Could you leave it caught? It makes my voice sound authoritative.

SELI: No. I'm switching the unit off first.

SFX: Power switch clicks off. Panel opens; spring clicks free. Panel closes.

SELI: Unit back on. One test.

SFX: Chime. "Next stop, Lantern Square." Silence.

PIP: That's one announcement.

MARA: Doors are closed. We can leave the terminal now.

SFX: Doors hiss shut; bus pulls away.

VEX: Next stop, Lantern Square.

MARA: The bus is moving. That announcement is correct.

ODO: The display advanced once, and the speaker played once. Continue the route.

SFX: Turn signal clicks; brakes ease at a stop.

MARA: Lantern Square. Please exit through the front door.

PIP: I'll stay on for Garden Lane and the Library.

VEX: Do you want a preview of the Library announcement?

PIP: No. I can wait until we get there.


ODO: The bus is stopping at Garden Lane. The doors open on the curb side, and the ramp is aligned with the sidewalk.

PIP: I'll remain seated until the passengers ahead have left. The library is the next announced stop.

VEX: Next stop, Library. Next stop, Library. I could say it twice for emphasis.

MARA: The repaired system is set to announce once. Please let the chime finish before you speak.

SFX: One chime; doors open. Footsteps depart, then the doors close.

ODO: The route display advanced correctly. No duplicate signal.

PIP: I can hear the difference. The bus is moving, and I know where to get off.

MARA: Library stop ahead. We will announce it when the bus reaches the stop, not before.

VEX: Understood. I will wait for the actual arrival.


ODO: The display reads Library. Doors stay closed until the bus stops completely.

MARA: We are at the curb. Now the doors may open.

SFX: Brake sets; doors hiss.

PIP: That's my stop.


SELI: The repair log shows the switch spring is free and the speaker test passed once.

MARA: We can keep the route card in the cab until the technician signs the repair.

VEX: Next stop, Garden Lane.

MARA: One time is enough.
