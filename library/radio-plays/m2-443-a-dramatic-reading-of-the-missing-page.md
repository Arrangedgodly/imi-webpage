# A Dramatic Reading of the Missing Page

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: audiobook production, cliffhanger narration, and studio teams who hide a missing page with dramatic pauses*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: An audiobook narrator reaches the final chapter and finds the page missing, while the director tries to turn a printing defect into a cliffhanger.

Cast: Narrator Vala, a baboon; director Nemi, a gibbon; editor Koro, a macaque; publisher Tavi, an orangutan; assistant Piri, a capuchin.

[SFX: PAGE TURN. PAPER RUSTLE.]

VALA: "And at the gate, the traveler discovered..."

NEMI: Hold.

KORO: The next page is missing.

NEMI: That is our cliffhanger.

TAVI: It is a manufacturing problem.

ACT ONE

Vala is recording the last chapter of a mystery novel. The bound proof jumps from page 188 to 190. Piri checks the loose pages and finds 189 tucked into the back cover, upside down. Nemi says they should leave a pause where the missing page belongs. Koro asks if the publisher can send a clean copy.

TAVI: The reading should match the book.

NEMI: The pause has dramatic potential.

VALA: The sentence does not end there.

Piri turns the loose page upright. It contains a paragraph and a half. The traveler meets the gatekeeper, asks about a brass key, then finds the key inside a watering can. Nemi says the reveal is small. Koro says it is the ending. Tavi calls the publisher and requests a correct proof before recording resumes.

ACT TWO

The studio starts again from the last complete paragraph. Vala reads the gatekeeper dialogue. Nemi asks her to pause after "the key was gone" so listeners can wonder what happened. Vala says the next line explains that the key was in the watering can. Nemi says she knows; suspense needs air.

VALA: A short pause is fine. An entire page of silence is not.

KORO: We need to mark the breath, not remove the answer.

Piri compares the proof with the digital file. The digital page is complete. The print proof was bound with one page missing. Tavi says the publisher will fix the next print run, but the audio must be recorded from the approved digital text. Nemi asks if they can keep the loose page as a special insert.

TAVI: It is the actual page from this copy.

NEMI: It could be a collectible.

PIRI: It is upside down.

Nemi asks Vala to read the page from the loose sheet. The text is visible, but the paper keeps crackling near the microphone. Koro places it on a flat board and clips the corners. The sound stops. Vala delivers the line in one take, including the key's location and the traveler's response.

ACT TWO-B

A new issue appears: the digital file has a paragraph not present in the loose page. The traveler tests the key in the gate, then learns the gate was never locked. Nemi wants to keep both endings as alternate audio. Tavi checks the author-approved text and confirms only the gatekeeper conversation belongs in the book; the extra paragraph came from an earlier draft.

KORO: We have one approved ending.

NEMI: Could the draft be a bonus scene?

TAVI: Not under the author's name.

Piri deletes the earlier draft from the recording session and labels the approved page. Vala rerecords the final exchange. The gatekeeper says the key was in the watering can because he set it there while carrying the garden hose. The traveler returns the key. The gate opens with a simple latch.

VALA: The traveler stepped through.

NEMI: No pause?

VALA: A natural breath, then the sentence ends.

Nemi listens. The scene holds tension until the key is found, then resolves without another reveal. Koro marks the final file and exports a short sample for the publisher. Tavi confirms the page order before anyone handles the printed proof again.

ACT THREE

Vala records the chapter's final paragraph and the book's closing line. The gatekeeper says goodbye; the traveler walks through and closes the gate behind her. There is no extra page, hidden key, or alternate ending. Nemi approves the performance and asks for a short silence before the credits, but Koro sets it to two seconds.

Tavi labels the defective print proof for correction. Piri places the loose page in its proper position and clips the proof closed. The audio file follows the approved digital text. Vala thanks the crew and removes her headphones. Nemi says the missing page created a useful pause. Vala says the page created the story.

TAG

KORO: That was the final page.
