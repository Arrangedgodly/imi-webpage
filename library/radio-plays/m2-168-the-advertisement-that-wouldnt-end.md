# The Advertisement That Wouldn't End

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio commercials, escalating product claims, jingles, and sponsor reads that keep interrupting the program they fund*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
HOST DARA, a macaque
JEX, a capuchin sponsor representative
MILO, a ring-tailed lemur listener

[PROGRAM THEME.]

DARA: You're listening to Good Morning, Kettle City. Today's forecast is mild, the roads are moving, and our sponsor is Cloud Pillow, the pillow that supports your dreams.

JEX: Cloud Pillow supports your head, neck, shoulders, ideas, ambitions, and any dream you have ever had while lying down.

DARA: That is a wide range of support.

JEX: Cloud Pillow is also a pillow, so you can rest on it while considering the range.

[CHEERFUL JINGLE.]

JEX: Cloud Pillow: lie down, look up.

DARA: That was our sponsor. Now, local news: the community garden has planted a row of beans.

JEX: With Cloud Pillow, you can imagine sleeping beside beans.

DARA: We are not selling the pillow for gardening.

JEX: The pillow is not recommended for outdoor use, wet conditions, or direct bean contact.

DARA: We'll return to the news after this word from Cloud Pillow.

[JINGLE.]

JEX: If you're tired of waking up on the wrong side of the bed, bring the bed to you with Cloud Pillow.

DARA: It is a pillow. It does not bring the bed.

JEX: Results may vary based on mattress position, gravity, and whether you are currently inside a bed.

[PHONE LINE OPENS.]

DARA: We have a listener. Milo, what do you think of the ad?

MILO: I bought the pillow, but I'm trying to figure out what the phrase "supports your dreams" means. I had a dream about a boat.

JEX: Cloud Pillow is not a boat, does not float, and cannot guarantee nautical dreams.

MILO: Then why does the package show a cloud over water?

JEX: The cloud is a metaphor.

DARA: A metaphor that appears to require a disclaimer.

JEX: Cloud Pillow also supports your imagination, though not in any medically measured way.

MILO: I used it while sleeping. The pillow was comfortable. I dreamed about a boat, but it sank.

JEX: We cannot be held responsible for dream weather.

DARA: Can you answer one simple question: what does the pillow do?

JEX: It cushions your head.

DARA: There. That is useful. Milo, did it cushion your head?

MILO: Yes. My head was comfortable. The boat still sank.

JEX: We can offer you a replacement pillow, but not a replacement boat.

MILO: I don't need a boat. I need a different dream.

DARA: Our station's dream desk will be opening later this year. Until then, perhaps try a nap without an ocean.

[JINGLE STARTS, DARA CUTS IT OFF.]

DARA: We have to stop the advertisement now. The weather is still mild, the beans are growing, and Cloud Pillow is a pillow for your head.

JEX: It is not a bean pillow, boat pillow, cloud, or dream insurance policy.

MILO: Does it come in a smaller size?

JEX: Yes, the travel model folds into a case.

DARA: Does it fold into a case, or does the case fold around it?

JEX: The case folds around the pillow.

DARA: Good. Listeners, the product is now accurately described.

MILO: I'll take one for the boat-free naps.

JEX: We cannot guarantee the boat-free part.

JEX: Yes. The travel model.

DARA: Does it support travel?

JEX: It fits in a bag.

DARA: Perfect. A factual claim. Thank you, Jex.

[SHORT JINGLE, ONE BAR.]

JEX: Cloud Pillow: comfortable enough to sleep on.

DARA: That is the entire advertisement.

MILO: I can finally hear the news.

DARA: The beans are doing well. They do not use the pillow.

[PROGRAM THEME RETURNS.]

JEX: We could sponsor the harvest report.

DARA: We are going to commercial break without you.

[JINGLE FADES UNDER THE NEWS.]
