# Signal from the Seed Shelf

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night community radio science desk debating how to label seed storage*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: HOST LIO, a macaque; BOTANIST NARA, a capuchin; ENGINEER VEM, a squirrel monkey; and CALLER SUL.

[SFX: STATION IDENTIFICATION, A RADIO DIAL TURNS.] 

LIO: This is Night Science. Tonight's question: when is a seed stored, and when is it just sitting on a shelf?

NARA: A seed is stored when it is dry, labeled, and kept where it can be found again.

VEM: Our shelf has a jar marked "maybe acorns."

NARA: Open it.

[JAR LID TWISTS. SEEDS RATTLE.] 

VEM: They are not acorns.

NARA: What are they?

VEM: They are seeds.

LIO: The label is technically correct.

NARA: It should say what kind.

VEM: We can ask the caller. Sul, you sent these in.

SUL: I put them there after the rain. I meant to label them, but the paper got wet.

NARA: Do you know what plant they came from?

SUL: The small red berries by the north path.

NARA: Then they may be from the berry shrub. Keep them separate from the acorns until we check.

VEM: Our storage map has one box labeled "small things."

LIO: How many shelves are in the station?

VEM: Four.

LIO: How many boxes say "small things"?

VEM: Four.

NARA: That is not a map. That is a repeated guess.

NARA: Before we store the jars, let's check whether the shelf can hold them.

VEM: The left support is firm. The right side is not touching the wall.

LIO: Could we place the jars on the floor until the shelf is fixed?

NARA: Yes, but keep them dry and away from the path.

[SFX: JARS LIFTED ONE AT A TIME, SET INTO A WOODEN CRATE.] 

SUL: I have a spare block at home. I can bring it in the morning.

VEM: The station opens at dawn. The seeds will be on the floor overnight.

NARA: In the crate, with labels. That is safer than an overloaded shelf.

LIO: We should not fill a container just because it is empty.

VEM: The crate has enough room for the confirmed acorns and the uncertain berries.

NARA: Keep them in separate jars. A shared crate is fine; mixed seeds are not.

VEM: I will place the berry jar on the left and the acorns on the right.

SUL: The paper label may still smear if the jar sweats.

NARA: Use a tag tied around the neck, and write the source and date.

[SFX: STRING TIES, PENCIL SCRATCHES ON CARD.] 

LIO: The seed shelf is empty now.

VEM: It looks like we have no collection.

NARA: We have a collection in a safer place. Storage is a practice, not a picture of jars on a shelf.

SUL: I will bring the block. Don't put the shelf back in service until it is level.

LIO: That is the first accurate label of the evening.

[SFX: PAPER UNFOLDS, A PENCIL SCRATCHES.] 

VEM: We can label by source: north path, west slope, and garden bed.

SUL: The berries were from the north path, but I found them already fallen.

NARA: Good. That is useful information. Don't claim a seed came from a plant if you only found it nearby.

LIO: The label now says: "Found near north path after rain; plant not confirmed."

VEM: It's a long label.

NARA: It is accurate.

[SFX: LABEL STICKS TO A JAR. THE SHELF CREAKS.] 

VEM: This shelf is bowing.

LIO: Does that mean it is stored or sitting?

NARA: It means the shelf needs support before we add more jars.

SUL: I can bring a block of wood tomorrow.

VEM: We should mark the shelf as temporarily full.

LIO: The seed shelf is closed until the support is in place.

NARA: That is not a scientific conclusion.

LIO: It is a useful one.

[SFX: JAR SET DOWN ON A TABLE, RADIO DIAL RETURNS TO STATION.]
