# The Lunch Bell's Two Meanings

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: school radio announcements that reuse a lunch bell recording for dismissal and emergency drills*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Announcer Kiri, a macaque; principal Oda, a gibbon; technician Favi, a capuchin; student Lilo, a lemur.

[SFX: SCHOOL HALLWAY. BELL RINGS TWICE. STUDENTS CHEER.]

KIRI: Lunch is over. Please return to class.

LILO: That was the fire drill bell.

ODA: It is the lunch bell. The drill uses three short rings.

KIRI: The principal's office has two paper schedules, lunch and drill. The audio system should not merge them.

ODA: The lunchtime bell has one ring. The drill alarm has three. We will keep those patterns distinct.

LILO: The third ring made the students reach for their backpacks.

FAVI: The bell file should not be in the same playlist as the announcement voice.

KIRI: I can speak first: "The fire drill is a test." Then the three short rings.

ODA: Only at the scheduled drill. Do not use it during lunch.

[SFX: TECHNICIAN OPENS A CONTROL PANEL. SWITCHES CLICK.]

FAVI: The lunch announcement uses one chime. The drill uses three bursts and a red light.

LILO: We can hear the difference from this hallway.

ODA: We will also post the schedule by the office so staff know which test is next.

KIRI: The next bell is lunch, not a drill.

[SFX: ONE BELL. STUDENTS CHEER, THEN WALK TOWARD THE CAFETERIA.]

LILO: That one means lunch.

KIRI: Good. The three short bells will wait for practice.

FAVI: The speaker system has one bell file for both announcements.

[SFX: BUTTON BEEPS. THREE SHORT BELLS PLAY.]

LILO: That's the fire drill sound.

KIRI: It played before the announcement.

ODA: The schedule selected the wrong cue.

FAVI: The system has two buttons, but both point to the same file.

KIRI: Can I read the announcement without a bell?

ODA: Yes. Students know lunch is over from the clock and the hallway monitor.

LILO: We came back because the bell sounded like a drill. Some of us went outside.

ODA: Then we need a clear correction before the next class moves.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.]

KIRI: Correction: the earlier bell was a lunch signal, not a fire drill. The fire drill has not started. Please return to class.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS TURN BACK. A DOOR CLOSES.]

FAVI: I found the file names. "Bell A" is lunch and "Bell B" is drill. Both links point to "Bell B."

ODA: Separate them before the afternoon practice.

FAVI: I can record a new lunch chime from the clock bell. It will be one ring, not three.

[SFX: SINGLE BELL RECORDS. BUTTON CLICKS.]

FAVI: Lunch button plays one ring. Drill button plays three short rings.

KIRI: Test lunch.

[SFX: ONE BELL.]

LILO: That's lunch.

ODA: Test drill.

[SFX: THREE SHORT BELLS.]

LILO: That's the drill.

KIRI: No announcement yet. We are testing the files, not calling students outside.

FAVI: The display labels now match the buttons.

ODA: Keep the drill switch covered until the scheduled practice. No one should think a test is an evacuation.

[SFX: COVER SLIDES OVER RED BUTTON.]

LILO: Can the lunch chime come before the menu announcement?

KIRI: Yes. The menu comes after the single ring.

[SFX: ONE BELL. MIC OPENS.]

KIRI: Lunch ends in ten minutes. The afternoon menu is posted by the kitchen.

LILO: I know which bell I heard this time.

[SFX: A CAFETERIA TRAY CLATTERS. STUDENTS PAUSE.]

KIRI: That was a tray, not a fourth bell. No one needs to evacuate the lunch line.

ODA: The hallway monitor is holding up the menu board. Classes can see lunch is still open.

FAVI: I am removing the old shared cue from the backup playlist too.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. A BEEP SOUNDS.]

FAVI: Backup now has the single lunch chime. The drill cue is locked behind its cover.

LILO: The tray sounded more urgent than the bell.

KIRI: The tray is not scheduled to announce anything.

[SFX: RADIO MIC CLOSES. SCHOOL BELL TICKS QUIETLY.]
