# The Ferry Captain Takes Requests

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in ferry radio where passengers dictate the route*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Captain Mara, a macaque; announcer Vex, an orangutan; deckhand Pip, a capuchin; dispatcher Odo, a gibbon; passenger Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Small ferry engine idles. Water taps the hull.

VEX: Welcome aboard. We are taking passenger requests for today's crossing.

MARA: We are taking the scheduled route from the west dock to the island pier. We are not taking requests to steer.

PIP: The passenger list is complete. Everyone is aboard, and the ramp is still down.

ODO, over radio: The island pier is clear. There is a floating branch near the marker, outside the channel.

SELI: Could we go closer so I can see the old bell tower?

MARA: We can point out the tower from the channel. The ferry stays in the marked route.

VEX: The audience has requested a dramatic detour.

MARA: The audience isn't on the deck. Keep the route card in front of you.

SFX: Ramp lifts; chain rattles and locks.

PIP: Ramp secured. Lines clear.

MARA: Cast off the west line. Slow ahead until we're beyond the dock.

SFX: Rope unties. Engine changes pitch.

SELI: The tower is behind that tree.

VEX: We could circle the island once for a better view.

ODO: Wind is shifting north. The posted route has room to turn at the channel marker, not at the island point.

MARA: We'll reach the marker, make the scheduled turn, and keep the pier approach open.

SFX: Water passes in a steady rhythm. A bell sounds twice.

PIP: Marker to port. Branch remains outside the channel.

SELI: If we stop here, could I sketch it?

MARA: We don't stop in the turning lane. You can sketch from the bench after we dock.

VEX: A route request denied. Can I call it a mutiny?

SELI: I asked to see a tower, not take the wheel.

ODO: The island pier has one service boat departing in five minutes. We should keep our arrival slot.

MARA: Helm two points starboard. Maintain slow speed.

SFX: Engine lowers; fender brushes against the pier.

PIP: Pier contact. Lines ready.

MARA: Secure the bow line. Ramp stays up until the engine is neutral.

SFX: Rope catches around cleat. Engine idles, then settles.

PIP: Neutral. Bow line secure.

MARA: Lower ramp.

SFX: Ramp descends with a clank.

SELI: From here I can see the bell tower. The top has a little weather vane shaped like a fish.

VEX: We crossed the whole bay for a fish.

MARA: We crossed the bay on schedule. The fish is a bonus.

ODO: Next departure is in fifteen minutes. The branch is still outside the route, and the channel is clear.

SELI: I'll sketch the tower from the bench, where the ferry is tied up.

VEX: Can we take requests for the return trip?

MARA: Requests for commentary, yes. Requests for navigation, no.


SELI: I have my sketchbook. The bench is on the island side, away from the boarding lane.

PIP: The ramp is still lowered, but the boarding is finished. Should I raise it before we leave?

MARA: Yes. Keep passengers behind the rail while you lift it.

SFX: Ramp rises and locks.

ODO: The departure slot is clear. The next ferry is still fifteen minutes away.

VEX: Could we make a short announcement for the passengers who wanted the tower?

MARA: Say that the weather vane is visible from the pier and the return route follows the same marked channel.

VEX: The fish on the tower is visible from the pier. Please board for the scheduled return.

SELI: That's enough. I have my sketch and the ferry is ready.

SFX: Bowline released; engine turns over.

PIP: Ramp secure, lines clear, passengers aboard.

MARA: Cast off at slow speed. The route stays on the channel markers.

TAG

SFX: Ferry bell rings once from the pier.

VEX: The passengers chose the bell.

MARA: The captain chose the route.
