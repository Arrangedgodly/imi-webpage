# The Bell Tower Keeps the Night Shift

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overnight public-radio mysteries, ominous field recordings, and hosts who turn ordinary noises into breaking discoveries*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A midnight host investigates a bell heard from the station's attached river tower, only to discover a wind-up clock and a metal effects triangle are conducting the mystery.

Cast: Host Mina, a macaque; engineer Jori, a gibbon; caretaker Ess, an orangutan; caller Fenn, a capuchin; producer Kavi, a baboon.

INT. STATION ANNEX, ATTACHED TO THE CLOSED RIVER TOWER - NIGHT

[SFX: NIGHT WIND THROUGH A SMALL WINDOW. A WIND-UP CLOCK TICKS CLOSE TO THE MICROPHONE.]

MINA: This is Night Window. At 12:15, a bell rang from the old river tower. We have a recording.

[SFX: A METALLIC DONG, THEN A SECOND DONG.]

MINA: Two strikes. The tower has been closed for repairs since spring.

JORI: That recording is from our studio clock.

KAVI: It is the tower recording now.

JORI: It was in the effects cabinet before you put it beside the clock.

MINA: Then we have a clue and an argument, both recorded in stereo.

ACT ONE

Jori runs the tape backward; the clock bell becomes a squeak.

MINA: More unsettling.

KAVI: Can we have the tower echo?

Ess is outside the locked service entrance shared by the tower base and the station annex. The tower clapper was removed for repairs. She knocks on the metal door; it sounds hollow, not bell-like.

ESS: That is the door. I have been knocking for six minutes.

MINA: We heard it as a warning.

ESS: It was a request to be let into the studio annex.

Jori checks the wind-up clock: its hammer rings every quarter hour. Kavi says the recording has two chimes three seconds apart. Mina asks Ess to knock at the shared door again. The clock ticks, Ess knocks, and Kavi layers the tracks; their rhythms sound intentional.

FENN: I heard that from my kitchen. There is definitely someone answering the tower.

ACT TWO

Mina asks Fenn to describe the room. He says his kitchen window faces the station, not the tower. A radio ad is playing on his counter, and his kettle is cooling. The ad ends with a pair of bell sounds. Fenn had mistaken the commercial for an answer.

FENN: I am on the east side. The tower is west.

MINA: The sound crossed the whole town.

FENN: The radio crossed the kitchen.

The radio ad plays again. Its bells overlap the clock's quarter-hour chime. Mina hears a third strike. Kavi whispers that the mystery has grown. Ess, still at the shared service door, says she can hear the studio through a vent. She knocks once, twice, then pauses. The station clock answers with a bell. Everyone stops talking.

ESS: I knocked on the duct because the door still won't open.

JORI: The vent carries her knock into this room.

KAVI: So the tower answered from a kitchen ad, a clock, and the ventilation system.

MINA: That is three suspects and one very tired caretaker.

ACT TWO-B

Jori slides the clock's strike-stop lever to silent. Its hands keep ticking, but the hammer cannot ring. He asks Ess to knock once more. Her knock arrives faintly through the vent. Fenn turns off his radio; the commercial vanishes. Mina asks the audience to listen to the remaining sound.

[SFX: ONE SOFT KNOCK THROUGH A VENT. A LONG PAUSE.]

MINA: That is not a bell. That is someone waiting to go home.

Ess says the tower repair crew left the padlock key with the station's night caretaker. Kavi takes the tagged key ring from its hook in the annex. Jori reads the tag aloud. Kavi passes the tower key through the service hatch to Ess; she takes it outside.

KAVI: We have the key.

ESS: I have it. I will open the shared entrance from outside.

KAVI: We will stay on the line while you unlock it.

[SFX: KEY TURNS OUTSIDE. THE SHARED DOOR CREAKS OPEN. FOOTSTEPS ENTER THE TOWER BASE.]

ESS: Empty tower base. No bell. One ladder, two buckets, and a note asking us to stop filming the door.

MINA: The mystery has left a note.

ESS: The repair crew is tired of the camera.

ACT THREE

Jori returns the wind-up clock to the effects shelf. Ess leaves through the shared entrance, locks it, and passes the key back through the hatch, and Kavi returns it to its hook for the repair crew. Fenn turns his radio back on; the ad has ended.

FENN: No third strike.

MINA: The tower is silent.

KAVI: The station is still broadcasting.

MINA: Verified: clock, ad, vent, and a caretaker trying to get indoors. No haunting.

ESS: Send me the raw recording.

Jori plays it without music. The door knock and clock tick are separate. Kavi strikes the metal triangle once on the effects shelf; it rings clearly. He then places it in the padded drawer.

TAG

[SFX: PADDED DRAWER CLOSES.]

MINA: One source identified, one clock silenced.

FINAL IMAGE: The triangle rests in its padded drawer while the wind-up clock's hands continue moving with its strike lever set to silent.
