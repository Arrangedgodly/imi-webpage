# The Museum Audio Guide for One Pebble

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio tours that turn simple objects into grand revelations through solemn narration*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Guide Nara, a gibbon; recorded guide voice Piri, a capuchin; visitor Odo, a baboon; technician Meli, a squirrel monkey.

[SFX: GALLERY ROOM TONE, SOFT FOOTSTEPS.]

NARA: Stop at the blue marker. Before you is a pebble.

ODO: It looks like a pebble.

PIRI: That is the first of its mysteries.

MELI: The audio guide has three minutes left on its battery.

NARA: Please continue to the listening point.

ACT ONE

Piri describes the pebble as a modest oval, gray on one side and tan on the other. Odo asks how it reached the museum. Piri says it was found beside the garden path, where a visitor placed it on the welcome table.

PIRI: It was admired by three people and returned to the path.

ODO: Was it special?

PIRI: It was the only pebble on the table.

Nara presses PLAY. A recorded version of her voice whispers, "In this room, even a small object has a past." Odo looks around for the room.

MELI: The room has been here since Tuesday.

The guide continues. It asks listeners to imagine the pebble's journey through rain, sun, and several careful footsteps. Odo says it may have come from the path directly outside. Piri calls that one interpretation.

ACT TWO

Meli's battery warning beeps. Nara fast-forwards to the interpretive section, but the device jumps to a recording of Piri rehearsing the exhibit description.

RECORDED PIRI: The pebble invites us to consider scale, patience, and what we leave behind.

ODO: It invites me to put it back.

PIRI: Please don't touch the exhibit.

Odo points out that the pebble is sitting in an open wooden tray with no barrier. Piri says the tray is meant to encourage closeness, but the guide says not to touch it. Meli checks the exhibit plan. The plan calls it "interactive." Nara asks if the plan and recording disagree.

MELI: They were made by different committees.

Piri adjusts the guide. The speaker crackles and begins a special bonus track: a dramatic voice describes a massive stone monument. Odo asks where it is. Piri says the track was intended for the next room.

NARA: The pebble has become a monument.

ODO: It has not grown.

The track plays music from an ancient empire. Meli grabs the device before its battery dies. The audio suddenly drops to silence. The room sounds ordinary: footsteps, a cough, and a distant door.

PIRI: The silence was not planned.

NARA: It has context now.

Odo asks if the museum can make the guide shorter. Piri says the long version was designed to feel important. Odo suggests a simpler track that tells visitors where the pebble came from and what they can do with it.

Meli offers Odo the new guide to test without seeing the label. He listens, then asks where to put the pebble back. Piri points to the tray. The guide has answered his question in one sentence. Nara records that as a success. The battery warning chirps again, but this time Meli has brought a spare cell. She replaces it before the guide can deliver an ominous final track.

ACT THREE

Nara records a new guide while Meli holds the microphone near the tray. Piri gives the facts without the orchestral music: "This pebble came from the path beside the museum. Please leave it here so the next visitor can see it." Odo listens and nods.

NARA: You may now continue to the next room.

ODO: Is there a monument?

PIRI: There is a larger pebble.

ODO: Does it have a longer guide?

MELI: Its battery is dead.

TAG

The new guide clicks off. Odo takes one last look at the small pebble.

ODO: I understand its journey.

PIRI: Which part?

ODO: It was on the path. Now it is not.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS TO NEXT ROOM.]
