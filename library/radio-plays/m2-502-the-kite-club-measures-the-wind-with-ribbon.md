# The Kite Club Measures the Wind With Ribbon

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcasts that mistake equipment specs for competition*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Commentator Odo, a gibbon; captain Pip, a capuchin; builder Mara, a macaque; sponsor Vex, an orangutan; marshal Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Open field. Kite string hums. A ribbon snaps in the breeze.

ODO: Welcome to the annual kite club flight trials.

VEX: The winner is the kite with the longest tail.

MARA: The trial is about steady flight, not decoration.

PIP: My kite has a short tail and a strong frame.

SELI: The wind is rising from the west.

VEX: We need a dramatic launch.

MARA: Let each flyer test the wind with a ribbon before sending a kite up.

ODO: Ribbon test begins.

SFX: Three ribbons lift at different angles.

PIP: Mine points sideways.

MARA: That means the wind is crosswise. Wait until the gust passes.

VEX: Waiting is bad for the broadcast.

SFX: Another gust catches Vex's long ribbon and wraps it around a stand.

VEX: My measurement is compromised.

SELI: The stand is in the flight lane.

PIP: I'll unhook it, but don't pull the ribbon.

MARA: I'll hold the stand while Odo frees the knot.

SFX: Ribbon loosens.

ODO: The gust has settled. Launch on my signal.

ODO: The club rule asks each flyer to land before the line crosses the marked path.

MARA: Pip, your kite is holding steady. Keep the string loose enough to respond to gusts.

PIP: I feel a pull to the left.

SELI: Wind shifted again. Ribbons are pointing north now.

VEX: My kite has the longest tail, so it should be the most stable.

MARA: The tail is twisting around the spar.

SFX: Line slackens; kite dips.

PIP: I can bring it down before the tail tangles further.

ODO: Landing call received. The flight is paused.

VEX: Is that a penalty?

SELI: It is the rule for everyone. Clear the lane, check the kite, then relaunch.

MARA: We can shorten the tail and tie it at two points.

VEX: That changes the entry.

PIP: It changes the part that made it unstable.

SFX: Scissors snip ribbon. Knot tightens.

ODO: The gust has passed. Both lines clear.

SFX: Kite line pays out. Wind hum rises, then steadies.

PIP: The kite is climbing evenly now.

ODO: Its frame is level and the line is clear of the marked path.

MARA: Keep the handle angled into the wind. Do not wrap the string around your hand.

VEX: My kite is ready too. The shorter ribbon is no longer twisting.

SFX: Second kite lifts with a soft flap.

SELI: Both kites are airborne, and neither is crossing the other's line.

ODO: The trial score is based on stable flight for one minute.

VEX: My kite held steady for forty seconds.

PIP: Mine reached the minute mark.

VEX: Then Pip wins the flight test.

MARA: The trophy spool is his to keep, but we can share the field.

VEX: I will launch again with a shorter tail.

SFX: Kites lift. One line hums; another flaps and descends.

VEX: My tail is the longest, but the kite is diving.

MARA: The frame is twisted by the ribbon wrap.

PIP: We can land it and straighten the spar.

VEX: The scoring clock is running.

SELI: We pause the trial when a line crosses the lane.

ODO: Pip's kite is steady at medium height.

MARA: That's the safe flight we came to measure.

VEX: The trophy is shaped like a spool.

PIP: I can lend you my short tail.

VEX: The rules don't allow borrowed tails.

MARA: The line is secure. We'll lower both kites before the wind turns again.
