# Voices for a Volcano

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio voiceover auditions, impossible sound descriptions, and one sound artist whose quiet noises keep being mistaken for disaster*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Etta, a squirrel-monkey casting director; Kumo, a gibbon actor; Marn, an orangutan sound designer; and Vee, a capuchin network executive.*

[SFX: Casting room, pencils, microphone test tone.]

ETTA: Welcome to auditions for Volcano Island: The Animated Documentary. We need a narrator, three lava effects, and a convincing pebble. Kumo, you first.

KUMO: I thought I was auditioning for the volcano.

ETTA: The volcano has no lines. It has presence.

KUMO: I can do presence.

VEE: Make it sound expensive.

KUMO: [LOW RUMBLE] I am the mountain, and I have a secret.

MARN: That sounds like a stomach before lunch.

VEE: An expensive stomach. Like one at a resort.

ETTA: Marn, demonstrate an eruption.

MARN: [SFX: He blows across a bottle, then taps a tin tray.]

ETTA: A breeze followed by a small dinner plate.

MARN: A geological event in miniature.

VEE: We need larger. Trailer says, "The earth breaks open."

MARN: The earth isn't in this room.

VEE: We have a budget for replacement sound.

MARN: I can replace it with a coconut rolling down stairs.

[SFX: Coconut rolls down three steps and stops against a door.]

KUMO: A boulder has arrived late.

ETTA: Good. Pebble role. Kumo, small stone landing on sand.

KUMO: [SFX: Tiny click.]

VEE: Smaller.

KUMO: [SFX: Almost inaudible tap.]

VEE: We lost it.

ETTA: That's what a pebble does. It lands and becomes part of the ground.

VEE: The audience must hear it become part of the ground.

MARN: I can record it under a microphone.

[SFX: Whisper of grit.]

ETTA: Perfect. Kumo, narrate the scene where a scientist discovers a warm rock.

KUMO: "At dawn, Dr. Mica found a stone with the heat of the sun."

VEE: More amazed.

KUMO: "At dawn, Dr. Mica found a stone with the heat of the sun!"

MARN: The stone is room temperature. I checked.

VEE: The line says sun. The rock must sound hot.

ETTA: A voice cannot change its temperature.

KUMO: I can pant between words.

KUMO: "At dawn, Dr. Mica found a stone - [PANTING] - with the heat of the sun."

VEE: Now the rock sounds like it ran here.

ETTA: That's the actor. The rock is stationary.

MARN: I could warm the stone and record it.

VEE: Is there a sound for warm?

MARN: Usually no.

VEE: Then make one.

[SFX: Marn rubs two smooth stones. They squeak.]

KUMO: The rock sounds offended.

ETTA: Useful. Vee, add a small crack, a larger crack, then the eruption.

MARN: [SFX: One twig snaps. A second twig snaps.]

VEE: That was two twigs. Where is the volcano?

MARN: We are in a casting room. The volcano is on vacation.

ETTA: Combine tray and coconut.

[SFX: Tray rattles; coconut rolls; bottle gives a low moan.]

VEE: That's it! The volcano is awake.

KUMO: It sounds like the mountain found a tray in its kitchen.

ETTA: Keep it. Kumo, closing narration.

KUMO: "The island was not silent. Beneath the ash, the mountain breathed."

MARN: [SFX: Soft airy exhale.]

VEE: Bigger.

MARN: [SFX: Soft exhale, followed by one careful click.]

VEE: Why the click?

MARN: The microphone stand shifted.

ETTA: Sounds like a pebble leaving the island.

VEE: Pebble gets a callback. Wonderful.

[SFX: Recording light turns on.]

KUMO: "The island was not silent. Beneath the ash, the mountain breathed. One pebble rolled home."

MARN: [SFX: Coconut rolls a short distance, then stops in grit.]

ETTA: That's the ending: volcano breathes, pebble settles, crew stops trying to make the rock shout.

VEE: Approved, if the trailer says "voices performed by actors and rocks."

KUMO: Rocks are not actors.

MARN: Mine hit its mark.

[SFX: Studio door opens. A real pebble rolls in, clicks against the microphone base, and stops.]

ETTA: That's the lead pebble. We can wrap.

[SFX: Recording light clicks off; the room returns to quiet.]
