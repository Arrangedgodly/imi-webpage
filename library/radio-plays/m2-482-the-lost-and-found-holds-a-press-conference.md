# The Lost and Found Holds a Press Conference

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: crisis press briefings that hide simple facts behind polished statements*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Spokesperson Vex, an orangutan; reporter Nari, a lemur; clerk Odo, a gibbon; finder Pip, a capuchin; caller Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Community hall. Microphones click on. A single mitten rests on a table.

VEX: We are here to address the situation involving an unclaimed mitten.

NARI: Is the mitten lost?

ODO: It was found on the south bench at noon.

VEX: We are not using the word "lost" until the review is complete.

PIP: I found it. It was wet and cold.

VEX: We appreciate your statement. Please do not speculate about the mitten's intentions.

NARI: Does it have a name tag?

ODO: No tag, no initials, no matching mitten.

MARA: I'm calling from the bus stop. My left mitten is missing.

VEX: We have not confirmed the mitten's handedness.

PIP: It has a thumb on this side.

NARI: That seems like a left mitten.

VEX: We will not prejudge.

ODO: The lost-and-found book has a picture of it.

SFX: Book opens. Paper turns.

ODO: The picture is upside down.

PIP: It's still the same mitten.

MARA: Mine has a blue stitch inside the cuff.

SFX: Pip turns cuff outward.

PIP: There is a blue stitch.

VEX: We cannot confirm identity based on one stitch.

NARI: What else would confirm it?

VEX: A claimant must provide a detailed account.

MARA: I put it in my pocket when the rain started. Then I took it off to carry my basket. I sat on the south bench.

ODO: That matches the time and place.

VEX: We need a statement about the mitten's emotional condition.

PIP: It was cold.

VEX: That is an allegation.

NARI: It is a wool mitten sitting in a wet tray.

SFX: A drip falls into tray.

ODO: The tray is leaking. The mitten is getting wetter.

MARA: Please give it to the clerk. I'll come right over.

VEX: We cannot release an item during a press conference.

NARI: Why not?

VEX: The briefing has not reached its conclusion.

PIP: I can hand it to Odo while you conclude.

SFX: Mitten lifts from tray. Cloth squeezes softly.

ODO: The return book needs a signature.

MARA: I'm here now. I'll sign it.

SFX: Pen scratches.

VEX: The mitten has been returned to its owner, pending final confirmation.

NARI: She is wearing the pair.

PIP: The other one matches.

VEX: We will release an update after the second mitten's review.

MARA: They have both been on my hands since I came in.

TAG

ODO: The return book needs a place to dry. The shelf is full of umbrellas.

PIP: I can hold the book while Mara hangs the mittens.

MARA: Put the wet pair on the lower rack, not in the sealed box.

VEX: We need a photo of the recovered item.

NARI: Ask Mara first. She came to retrieve it, not pose for the briefing.

MARA: Take a picture of the empty tray. Then people can see where to look next time.

SFX: Camera shutter. A tray slides onto the desk.

ODO: I'll add the south bench and time to the lost-and-found book.

PIP: And if someone finds a mitten?

MARA: Bring it to the clerk. Don't wait for a statement from the mitten.

VEX: The official statement should say the item was located through a collaborative process.

NARI: It was on the south bench, and Mara described the blue stitch.

ODO: I can put that in the book.

PIP: The next finder can bring a mitten straight to the rack.

MARA: And the owner can ask for it without an investigation.

SFX: Cloth hangs on a low hook. The drip tray slides beneath it.

SFX: Microphone switches off.

NARI: The press conference has concluded.

VEX: I need a statement about that.
