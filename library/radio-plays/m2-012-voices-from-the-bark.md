# Voices from the Bark

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Late-night conspiracy call-in broadcasts*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Cricket, a squirrel monkey host who checks claims; Venn, a gibbon field reporter; Moss, a macaque who hears a voice in the hollow tree; Ara, a ring-tailed lemur grove keeper.

[INTRO: A LOW DRONE, TWO KNOCKS ON WOOD, A TUNING DIAL.]

HOST CRICKET: It is midnight. You are listening to Voices from the Bark, where callers bring a strange claim and we ask for something we can check. Tonight, listener Moss says the old trees are whispering troop names after dark. Moss, welcome.

CALLER MOSS: I hear them through my hollow branch. Last night, one said my name. Then I heard, "Move to the west grove."

CRICKET: Did anyone else hear it?

MOSS: My cousin did. She's sleeping in the east grove tonight.

CRICKET: What happened before the message?

MOSS: A storm broke the crown of the tallest tree. The sound carried down the root path. We found splinters near the old burrow.

CRICKET: That is worth investigating. We will send a recording team. No one should move because a radio host says so. Check the tree with the grove keeper.

[SFX: NIGHT WALK. CRICKETS, LEAVES, A BRANCH CREAKS.] 

REPORTER VENN: I'm at the west grove with Moss and keeper Ara. We have placed a recorder at the hollow root where Moss heard the voice. Ara, what do you hear?

ARA: The stream passes through a narrow rock gap below us. When the wind comes from the north, it pushes air up this hollow. It sounds like a breath.

MOSS: It said my name.

VENN: Can we hear the original clip?

[SFX: RECORDED WHISPER, GRAINY: "MOSS... WEST... GROVE..."] 

ARA: That is the wind catching the loose bark. The word "grove" might be two branches scraping.

CRICKET, IN STUDIO: We have the recording. I hear a low moan and a few sharp sounds. I do not hear a clear sentence.

MOSS: I heard my name. I know what I heard.

CRICKET: I believe that you heard something. We should keep those ideas separate until we can check them.

MOSS: A caller on another show says the tree roots are moving troop nests toward the west. He says the grove council knows and is hiding it.

CRICKET: Roots grow. They do not pick up a nest and carry it. Has anyone measured where the nests are?

ARA: The east grove is losing soil near the stream. The nests are being moved because branches are falling. The council posted a route map last week.

MOSS: I didn't see it.

ARA: It was pinned behind the fruit stall. The storm blew it down.

[SFX: PAPER FLAPS IN WIND.] 

VENN: We found the map under the stall. It marks five trees for relocation and shows the path to a new nesting ridge. The message Moss heard matches no council notice. It may be the sound of wind and remembered words meeting in a dark place.

MOSS: So the trees aren't telling me to move?

ARA: No. The grove keeper is asking everyone to move the nests because the bank is crumbling. I should have come to the east grove and told you in person.

MOSS: I thought they were keeping something from us.

ARA: We were late. That is enough to make a rumor grow.

CRICKET: The relocation is real. The whisper is not evidence of a secret plan. Ara will visit each nest tonight, and Moss will help mark safe branches. We will post the map where people actually gather.

MOSS: I'll put one at the water pool too.

CRICKET: Good. As for the caller who said roots are carrying nests, we invited him to speak. He declined after we asked for a location and date.

MOSS: Could he have been trying to help?

CRICKET: Maybe. A claim can be wrong without its speaker being a villain. Still, check before you spread it.

[SFX: WIND SOFTENS. BARK SETTLES AGAINST STONE.] 

MOSS: I can hear the stream now. It sounds like a long exhale.

ARA: That is all it was.

CRICKET: Voices from the Bark is ending. The grove will move its nests before the next rain. Moss has the map, Ara has a lantern, and I have a recording of a branch doing nothing sinister at all.

VENN: The recorder has picked up a second noise.

CRICKET: What is it?

VENN: Ara's lunch tin.

ARA: It is not sinister.

MOSS: It is full of dates.

CRICKET: Then we have confirmed the source.

VENN: The dates or the tin?

[THE DRONE ENDS. A REAL OWL CALLS ONCE. MUSIC OUT.]
