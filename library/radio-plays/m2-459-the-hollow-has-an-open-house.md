# The Hollow Has an Open-House

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: house-hunting broadcasts that judge a home by staged features rather than its residents*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Seli, a lemur; agent Vex, an orangutan; buyer Runi, a baboon; resident Pip, a capuchin; sound engineer Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Forest ambience. A hollow tree gives a low, roomy knock.

SELI: Welcome to Hollow Hunt, where we tour homes without showing a single room. Vex, what is the asking price?

VEX: Three baskets of acorns, plus one polished stone.

PIP: This is my home.

VEX: And a wonderful home it is. Listen to the echo.

SFX: Pip taps the inner wall. The sound returns softly.

RUNI: How many nests fit inside?

VEX: This hollow has excellent acoustic character.

RUNI: I have a growing family. I need to know where everyone sleeps.

PIP: I sleep near the entrance. My sister uses the upper notch. We keep the back chamber clear because the rain runs through a crack.

SELI: Can we hear the crack?

MARA: There is no rain today.

VEX: The house is staged with a decorative drip bowl.

PIP: That bowl catches water. It is not decorative.

SFX: A drop plinks into the bowl.

RUNI: The back chamber is damp.

VEX: It has a water feature.

RUNI: It has a leak.

VEX: A leak with a relaxing tone.

MARA: The exterior branch is sturdy. But the entry is narrow. Runi, try going in with your basket.

SFX: Basket scrapes bark. Runi pauses.

RUNI: It fits if I turn it sideways.

VEX: A clever storage solution.

RUNI: I shouldn't have to turn sideways every time I come home.

PIP: I can move my sleeping mat back. That clears the entrance.

VEX: We could call that an open floor plan.

SELI: The show needs three selling points.

MARA: It has shelter, a high perch, and the entrance faces east.

VEX: The east-facing entrance catches morning light. It also has an exclusive drip bowl.

RUNI: Does the upper notch have room for my children?

PIP: One child can perch there. Two would crowd my sister.

VEX: The family can use the nearby sapling as an extension.

RUNI: Is it included?

SELI: Runi.

RUNI: The listing should say what is included.

MARA: The sapling belongs to the park. It is not for sale.

VEX: The property's neighborhood is expanding.

PIP: The sapling is where the woodpecker eats.

SFX: A hollow knock from outside. A short answering knock from inside.

RUNI: The tree is occupied.

VEX: The woodpecker is a neighbor, not a co-owner.

MARA: It is part of the soundscape.

SELI: What would you change, Runi?

RUNI: I would patch the back crack, widen the entry, and leave the upper notch for Pip's sister.

VEX: Those are renovations.

PIP: I can patch the crack with bark and resin. I cannot widen the trunk.

RUNI: Then it doesn't fit us.

VEX: The show usually asks buyers to compromise.

RUNI: I won't ask my children to sleep in a leak.

VEX: We can describe it as a soothing rain feature.

RUNI: You can describe it as a repair waiting for the park keeper.

SELI: That is the first line of the listing that mentions the actual house.

SFX: Pip wedges a bark patch against the crack. The drip slows, then stops.

PIP: That patch is temporary. The park keeper can repair it properly tomorrow.

MARA: We should note that as the repair plan, not call it a feature.

SELI: Runi, are you making an offer?

RUNI: No. I am staying in my current tree. Pip should keep this hollow until the park repairs it.

VEX: But the echo is wonderful.

RUNI: I heard my answer echo.

SELI: That closes our open house.

VEX: Can we list the decorative drip bowl?

PIP: It belongs to the rain.
