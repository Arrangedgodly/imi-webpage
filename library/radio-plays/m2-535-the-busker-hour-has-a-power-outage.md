# The Busker Hour Has a Power Outage

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: street performance radio that treats unplugged amplification as artistic sabotage*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; busker Pip, a capuchin; technician Odo, a gibbon; critic Vex, an orangutan; passerby Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Plaza. Guitar chord, then amplifier hum cuts out.

VEX: The performance has lost power. We may be witnessing a protest against amplification.

PIP: The battery pack is empty. I can play acoustically while Odo checks the spare.

MARA: Keep the plaza path clear. The crowd can gather on the marked side, not around the cable.

ODO: The spare pack is charging in the equipment case. The amplifier is off, so I'll unplug it before connecting anything.

SFX: Plug pulls free; case opens.

SELI: I can hear the guitar without the amplifier. The birds are louder too.

VEX: A spontaneous outdoor orchestra.

PIP: It's a quiet song. I wrote it for the courtyard, not a stage.

MARA: Play the piece you planned. We don't need to invent a crisis.

SFX: Guitar begins, clear and unamplified. A soft rhythm of footsteps passes.

VEX: I give the unplugged version a seven for intimacy and a ten for technical failure.

PIP: It isn't a failure if it still works. I can finish this song acoustically.

ODO: The spare pack is charged. The cable is intact. I'll connect it while the amplifier remains off.

MARA: Is the battery seated?

ODO: Yes. Switch still off. Plug secure.

PIP: I'll test one chord at low volume.

SFX: Amplifier clicks on; one guitar chord, moderate volume.

SELI: That's clear without overpowering the plaza.

VEX: The outage has ended. Can we replay the opening at full volume for the broadcast?

PIP: No. I already played it. The next verse is different.

MARA: Let the live performance continue from where Pip is, not from where the microphone wants to begin.

SFX: Guitar resumes; a bird answers with two chirps.

VEX: The bird has joined the ensemble.

SELI: The bird was there the whole time.

PIP: I can hold the final chord and let it ring.

SFX: Chord fades; plaza remains audible.

MARA: Performance complete. Odo, turn the amplifier off before packing the cable.

SFX: Switch clicks off; cable coils neatly.

ODO: Battery pack goes back in its case for charging. No cable crosses the walkway.

VEX: Our power outage was a quiet success.

PIP: It was a short change of equipment. The song was the same length.


ODO: The case latch is closed and the battery pack is separated from the amplifier for charging. The spare cable is coiled with its plug protected.

MARA: Pip, do you want to play one more piece, or is the set finished?

PIP: One short encore, still acoustic. The plaza is quiet and the crowd is listening.

VEX: I can introduce it as the triumphant return of electricity.

PIP: Just say the title. It is called "Bench in the Shade."

SFX: Guitar strings tune; the crowd hushes. A short melody plays, then fades.

SELI: That sounded better without the speaker. I could hear the last note and the leaves together.

MARA: The performance is complete. The cable stays coiled, the walkway stays open, and the next busker can use the plaza.

VEX: I will give the encore a ten for ending exactly once.


ODO: Plaza mic is off. I can hear normal foot traffic again.

SELI: The crowd is leaving through the clear walkway, and Pip has the guitar in its case.

MARA: That's the end of the performance.


PIP: The guitar is secured. I'll walk it out after the plaza crew has passed.

MARA: Take the marked path. The cable is already packed, so nobody has to step over it.

TAG

MARA: The plaza is open again.

VEX: The birds may continue their set.
