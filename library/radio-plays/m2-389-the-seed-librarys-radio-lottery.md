# The Seed Library's Radio Lottery

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: community seed exchanges that turn a fair distribution into a radio raffle and confuse seed packets with prizes*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Dema, a lemur; librarian Sori, a macaque; grower Pali, a gibbon; caller Miko, a capuchin.

[SFX: COMMUNITY SEED ROOM. PAPER PACKETS SHUFFLE. RADIO MIC OPENS.]

DEMA: Tonight we will raffle the last packets of moon beans. Callers can win one by guessing how many seeds are inside.

SORI: They are not prizes. They are reserved for growers who asked for them.

PALI: I am here to collect two packets for the school garden.

MIKO: I called to ask whether you have any yellow beans.

DEMA: The raffle basket is on the table.

SORI: The packets have names on the ledger. The school has a reservation.

[SFX: PACKETS SHUFFLE. A DRAWER OPENS.]

SORI: The sign-up sheet lists each grower, variety and packet count. We should check it before the next pickup.

PALI: The school needs two packets now, and one reserve if a class fails to sprout.

SORI: We have four open moon-bean packets. I can set three aside and leave one open to other growers.

MIKO: The yellow packet I requested can go in my name, not in a prize basket.

DEMA: I can announce the reservations without drawing names.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES THREE SCHOOL PACKETS, ONE MIKO PACKET.]

SORI: The seed count is still fair. The sign-up order decides who receives them.

PALI: We can return saved seeds after harvest.

DEMA: The producer has numbered the packets from one to ten.

SORI: Those numbers match the reservation list, not a raffle ticket.

PALI: Our school number is three.

MIKO: Mine is seven. I signed up last week.

DEMA: We can draw the numbers on air.

SORI: Then one grower gets the packet another grower reserved.

[SFX: PAPER SLIPS DROP INTO A BOWL.]

DEMA: The bowl is ready.

SORI: Please stop the draw.

[SFX: BOWL SLIDES AWAY. SLIPS SPILL.]

PALI: My number is on the floor.

SORI: I can check the ledger. Nobody has opened a packet.

DEMA: The raffle sponsor donated a seed scoop.

SORI: The scoop can be used to fill packets after the reservations are sorted.

MIKO: I don't need a prize. I need to know if yellow beans are available.

SORI: We have four yellow packets. Two are reserved. Two are open.

PALI: The school needs two moon bean packets, not yellow.

DEMA: We could make a bonus round for the open packets.

SORI: Or we can announce what is available and let growers request them.

[SFX: LEDGER PAGES TURN. PENCIL WRITES.]

SORI: Moon beans: six packets, two reserved for the school, four open. Yellow beans: four packets, two reserved, two open.

DEMA: That is an inventory report, not a game.

PALI: It tells the gardeners what they can take.

MIKO: I will reserve one yellow packet. I can leave the other for someone else.

SORI: I will write your name beside it.

[SFX: PENCIL SCRATCHES. DRAWER SLIDES SHUT.]

DEMA: The raffle bowl is still full of numbers.

SORI: They go back in the ledger envelope. Each number belongs to a request.

[SFX: SLIPS SORTED INTO AN ENVELOPE. ENVELOPE FLAP PRESSES CLOSED.]

PALI: The school packets are ready?

SORI: Yes. Two moon beans, labeled and held for pickup.

DEMA: We will not draw them. We will read the ledger every week.

MIKO: That makes the seed library sound like a library.

DEMA: It is one.

[SFX: PACKETS DROP INTO A PAPER BAG. BAG FOLDS.]

PALI: I can bring the empty envelopes back after planting.

SORI: That will help. Keep the seeds dry.

DEMA: The seed lottery is canceled. The seed exchange is open.

[SFX: RADIO MIC CLOSES. DRAWER OPENS FOR THE NEXT GROWER.]
