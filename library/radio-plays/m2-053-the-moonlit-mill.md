# The Moonlit Mill

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: serialized radio melodrama and supernatural cliffhangers*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Narrator, an overdramatic macaque; Lina, a practical capuchin miller; Bram, her gibbon cousin; and Sella, a village bat researcher.

[SFX: WIND THROUGH OLD BOARDS. A DOOR CREAKS.]

NARRATOR: Midnight at the old mill. A shadow crosses the window. A knock comes from the upper floor. And from the pantry, a silence where the cheese should be.

LINA: The window is open.

BRAM: I heard three knocks.

LINA: Those are the loose shutters.

NARRATOR: But the cheese?

LINA: In the pantry.

BRAM: You checked?

LINA: I live here.

[SFX: THREE KNOCKS. A BAT FLUTTERS.]

BRAM: Something brushed my ear!

NARRATOR: A creature of the night has chosen Bram.

SELLA: It's a bat. Don't touch it.

NARRATOR: Sella, the village bat researcher, arrives at the exact moment our story requires an expert.

SELLA: I was already downstairs. The narrator keeps turning up the music whenever a bat moves.

LINA: The bat came through the roof gap. It roosts in the rafters.

NARRATOR: Then who made the sound above us?

SELLA: Bram set an empty flour tin on the top stair.

BRAM: I did that before supper.

NARRATOR: The clue was planted hours ago.

LINA: The tin was placed on the stair. Nobody planted it.

[SFX: TIN ROLLS DOWN THE STAIRS.]

BRAM: It's coming back!

LINA: It is rolling downhill.

[SFX: TIN STOPS AGAINST A BOOT.]

NARRATOR: Lina has caught it. But a low thump shakes the wheel room.

BRAM: The wheel is turning.

LINA: The wind has stopped.

SELLA: The bat is moving along the beam. The loose slat taps the wheel when it passes.

NARRATOR: A bat, a tin, and a wheel that moves when a bat moves. Could the mill be haunted by carpentry?

SELLA: The wheel should remain still until the carpenter checks it. A branch is jammed in the water channel.

LINA: We'll clear it in daylight. Nobody is going into the stream tonight.

BRAM: I can get the cheese.

NARRATOR: A brave cousin enters the pantry.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS, CHEESE WRAPPER RUSTLES.]

BRAM: It was on the shelf the whole time.

LINA: I said so.

NARRATOR: The station has promised a storm before the next break.

[SFX: THUNDER SHEET RATTLES.]

LINA: It is not storming.

SELLA: Keep that sheet away from the rafters. The bat is trying to settle.

NARRATOR: Our listeners expect danger.

BRAM: They heard a tin roll down the stairs. That was danger enough for the tin.

[SFX: THE THUNDER SHEET ROLLS BACK UP.]

NARRATOR: We can add a creaking door.

LINA: You already have one.

[SFX: DOOR CREAKS. THE NARRATOR TRIES IT AGAIN, LOUDER.]

SELLA: That's the same door. It didn't become more haunted the second time.

NARRATOR: The station's sponsor sells lantern oil. Perhaps the dark itself can return after the advertisement.

LINA: The dark is here. We turned the lamp down for the bats.

BRAM: The cheese is on the shelf, the bat has a moth, and the door is a door.

NARRATOR: Our listeners have been promised a cliffhanger. The mill is safe, the cheese is found, and the bat is eating a moth.

BRAM: You can still give them one.

[SFX: CUPBOARD DOOR GROANS.]

NARRATOR: The cupboard will not open!

LINA: It's stuck because you put the flour bag against it.

[SFX: BAG MOVES, CUPBOARD OPENS.]

NARRATOR: Next week: the cupboard opens. Or does it?

SELLA: It does.

BRAM: There goes next week's episode.

NARRATOR: We could follow the flour bag.

LINA: It is in the cupboard now.

NARRATOR: I will need a new teaser.

SELLA: Try "the bats sleep through the whole thing."

BRAM: That is what they are doing.

NARRATOR: We will return when something else makes a noise.

[SFX: BAT CHIRPS. ONE SHUTTER KNOCKS. THE THEME MUSIC STOPS.]
