# The Last Stop Is a Tree

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio transit announcement system struggling to describe a moving bus stop*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: ANNOUNCER PEL, a macaque; DRIVER YU, a gibbon; RIDER SAI, a capuchin; and AUTOMATED VOICE.

[SFX: BUS ENGINE IDLES, DOORS OPEN.] 

AUTOMATED VOICE: Welcome aboard the Fern Hollow shuttle. Next stop: East Tree. Please remain seated until the vehicle stops moving.

YU: We have reached East Tree.

PEL: The stop sign is not here.

YU: It was beside this tree yesterday.

SAI: The tree is here. The sign is around the corner.

SAI: The riders are asking why we stopped before the sign.

YU: Tell them the shuttle is clear of the nest platform and the stop marker is down.

PEL: The path is open, but everyone needs to stay together while we walk to it.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Please remain aboard until the vehicle reaches the stop.

YU: The vehicle cannot reach the stop. The stop is behind the platform.

SAI: We could move the stop temporarily to the path entrance.

PEL: We need a marker.

YU: Use the shuttle's small orange cone, then put it away after we lift the sign.

[CONES SET DOWN. RIDERS STEP OUT ONE AT A TIME.] 

SAI: The older passengers are taking the handrail. The young ones are carrying the bags.

PEL: We can leave space between them so no one gets crowded.

AUTOMATED VOICE: East Tree stop is temporarily located at the orange cone.

YU: Better.

[THE GROUP WALKS A FEW PACES. A NESTLING CHIRPS FROM THE PLATFORM.] 

SAI: We should wait here until the parents return.

YU: We can hold the shuttle. The next stop can wait.

PEL: The schedule says two minutes at East Tree.

YU: This is the stop. We are stopped.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Delay at East Tree: two minutes.

SAI: The sign is clear now. I can lift it without touching the platform.

[METAL SIGNPOST SETS UPRIGHT. THE CONE IS PICKED UP.] 

PEL: The temporary marker is gone. The stop is back beside the tree.

YU: The route computer can update at the depot.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Next stop: depot, one tree away.

PEL: That is close enough for now.

YU: And nobody missed their stop.

PEL: The automated route only knows the sign's location.

YU: The tree is a better landmark.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Next stop: East Tree, approximately one tree away.

PEL: That is technically correct and not useful.

SAI: I can see the sign from here. It is behind the new nest platform.

YU: We cannot pull the shuttle up to the nest.

PEL: We can announce the stop, then let riders walk the short path.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Please disembark at the sign, not the tree.

SAI: The sign is on the ground.

YU: Did someone move it?

SAI: It fell over. The platform is resting against it.

[SFX: BUS BRAKES RELEASE. THE VEHICLE ROLLS A FEW FEET.] 

YU: I will park beside the path, away from the nest.

PEL: We need to tell riders why we are stopping short.

AUTOMATED VOICE: We are stopping short because the stop has moved.

PEL: The stop did not move. The sign fell down.

AUTOMATED VOICE: We are stopping short because the sign is on the ground.

SAI: That is clearer.

YU: I'll set the brake. We can raise the sign after everyone gets off.

[DOORS OPEN. RIDERS STEP DOWN.] 

PEL: East Tree stop. Please use the path and keep clear of the nest platform.

SAI: I can help lift the sign.

YU: Wait until the shuttle is parked and the path is clear.

[THE LAST RIDER STEPS AWAY. SIGNPOST LIFTS, THEN SETS IN A STABLE SOCKET.] 

PEL: The sign is upright again.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Next stop: East Tree.

SAI: We are still at East Tree.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Next stop: East Tree, one stop away.

YU: The route computer has not learned the difference between arriving and announcing.

PEL: We will update its map after the run.

[SFX: DOORS CLOSE. BUS PULLS AWAY, ENGINE FADES.]
