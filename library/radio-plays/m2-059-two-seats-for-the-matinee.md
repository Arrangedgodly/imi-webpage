# Two Seats for the Matinee

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: serialized radio romantic comedies*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Announcer Vale, a macaque; Nella, a capuchin who runs the ticket booth; Kett, a gibbon who repairs stage lights; and caller Sumi, a baboon looking for a lost seat partner.

[SFX: THEATER LOBBY MURMUR. A TICKET TEARS.] 

VALE: This is The Matinee Line, broadcasting from the old playhouse. Tonight's serial begins with two tickets, one umbrella, and a question no one should ask a stranger before the show: "Are you my seat partner?"

NELLA: I have one ticket at Row F, Seat 8. Its matching ticket is missing.

KETT: The light booth has one ticket at Row F, Seat 9.

NELLA: That is not matching.

KETT: The person in Seat 8 left a note saying, "Save the seat beside me."

[SFX: PAPER UNFOLDS.] 

NELLA: The note says, "I will be late. The bridge path is closed. Please hold my place."

VALE: A message from the past?

NELLA: It is from twenty minutes ago.

KETT: I can walk to the bridge and check.

NELLA: You have a show to light.

KETT: The first scene is dark.

NELLA: It is supposed to be dark.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.] 

VALE: Sumi is on the line. Do you have the other ticket?

SUMI, PHONE: I have a ticket for Row F, Seat 8. I am at the north entrance. The main bridge is closed, but the lower ramp is open.

NELLA: Are you the person who left the note?

SUMI: I asked the usher to take it. I did not know there was a radio show.

VALE: The audience is now involved.

SUMI: Can the usher walk me to the seat?

NELLA: Yes. The ramp is clear. Take your time.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS, A CANE TAPS TWICE.] 

KETT: The lower path is dry. I can hold the door.

NELLA: Please keep the cable out of the walkway.

KETT: The cable is taped down.

VALE: A tender moment.

NELLA: It is a safety check.

[SFX: TICKET TEARS.] 

SUMI: I am here. Is seat eight this one?

NELLA: Yes. Seat nine is beside it.

SUMI: Who is sitting there?

KETT: Me, unless the light board needs me.

NELLA: You said you were in the booth.

KETT: I moved the work light. The show starts in five minutes.

VALE: A triangle forms.

NELLA: There is no triangle.

KETT: I can sit at intermission.

SUMI: I don't mind if you watch the first act from the booth.

KETT: I have seen the play. I can take a break.

[SFX: AUDIENCE SETTLES. HOUSE LIGHTS DIM.] 

VALE: The two seats are filled. The umbrella is still missing.

NELLA: It is on the ticket desk.

VALE: A second mystery.

SUMI: I brought it. It's mine.

KETT: The show has resolved two mysteries before the curtain.

NELLA: Only because we checked.

VALE: Will Kett and Sumi meet again at the next performance?

SUMI: I come to the matinee every month.

KETT: I repair the lights every month.

NELLA: That is not a promise.

SUMI: It is a schedule.

VALE: And so the romance begins with a schedule.

NELLA: The play is starting. Please let the actors speak.

[SFX: CURTAIN RISES, A LOW STRING CHORD.] 

VALE: The Matinee Line will return after the show. We will find out whether the first act has a happy ending, or whether it needs better lighting.

KETT: It needs the work light.

SUMI: I brought a second ticket for next month. Do you want to come?

KETT: I will be in the light booth.

NELLA: Kett, the booth has two stools. One can be empty.

KETT: Then yes. I can sit beside you after the first act.

VALE: The serial has reached a decision.

NELLA: It has reached a calendar.

SUMI: That's better. We can change our minds if the play is bad.

KETT: The light will still be good.

[SFX: HOUSE LIGHTS FADE.]
