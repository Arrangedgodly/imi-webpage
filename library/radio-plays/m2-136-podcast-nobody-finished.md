# The Podcast Nobody Finished

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: podcast engagement dashboards, episode-completion metrics, and producers who make the ending shorter until listeners skip to the beginning*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Ivo, a macaque podcast host; Bren, an orangutan producer; Lu, a capuchin analyst; and Tavi, a gibbon listener.*

[SFX: Studio microphone, laptop chime.]

IVO: Welcome to the Making It Through podcast, episode 40. Today we discuss how to finish a podcast.

BREN: The dashboard says only one in five listeners reaches the ending.

LU: Most leave after the opening music.

IVO: Then the opening music is too long. We can cut it to one note.

[SFX: One note plays.]

TAVI: I thought the episode had started, so I skipped ahead.

LU: You skipped to the ending.

TAVI: It was also one note.

BREN: The data shows a spike in replays at the start.

IVO: People like the start.

LU: They may be restarting because they missed the first sentence.

IVO: Then we should make the first sentence the whole episode.

TAVI: What is the episode about?

IVO: Finishing things.

BREN: That's too broad. Give us three practical steps.

IVO: Step one: pick a task. Step two: work on it. Step three: finish.

LU: The dashboard reports a drop at step two.

BREN: Add a cliffhanger before the middle.

IVO: "Will our host complete the task? Stay tuned."

TAVI: I stayed tuned. You were still introducing the task.

LU: Listeners leave when we say "stay tuned."

BREN: Then remove that phrase.

IVO: "Will our host complete the task? The answer is yes."

TAVI: That gives away the ending.

LU: The completion score improved.

BREN: We have a sponsor message.

IVO: The sponsor is a pencil company. They asked us to sharpen our point.

TAVI: I thought the sponsor was an eraser.

BREN: That was last week. The pencil company bought the midroll.

LU: The dashboard says listeners skip the midroll, then rewind to see if they missed the episode.

IVO: That's a successful rewind.

TAVI: I rewound to find out what the sponsor sold.

BREN: Pencils.

IVO: The episode is now 30 seconds long. We have introduced the problem, stated the steps, and resolved it.

LU: The completion rate is 100 percent.

TAVI: The dashboard counts me as three listeners because I played it twice.

LU: It counts the plays, not the people.

BREN: Can we celebrate the metric?

IVO: Yes, after we finish recording.

[SFX: Red light clicks on.]

IVO: To finish a task, begin it, continue through the difficult part, and stop when it is done.

TAVI: That's clear.

LU: The dashboard has a new spike at the end.

BREN: Listeners finished!

IVO: We are finished.

TAVI: I have one question. What happened to the ending?

IVO: This is it.

LU: The data shows no one skipped ahead.

BREN: The episode is too short to skip.

TAVI: I can still skip it. [SFX: A button clicks.] I skipped the whole thing.

IVO: And yet you heard the ending.

BREN: The dashboard is celebrating.

LU: It thinks the button click was another play.

[SFX: Dashboard chime repeats.]

TAVI: Please end the episode.

IVO: This has been Making It Through. The task is complete, the ending has ended, and the dashboard may now close.

BREN: The episode has finished, but the dashboard still shows one listener at 99 percent.

IVO: Who is it?

TAVI: I paused just before the last word to see if you would end it.

LU: The system cannot tell a pause from a dropout.

BREN: We can close the episode with a short end tone.

[SFX: A small bell rings after the final sentence.]

TAVI: That tells me the task is done.

IVO: We have finished the episode and the dashboard agrees.

[SFX: Laptop shuts; one note plays, then silence.]
