# The Cart Derby Live

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio coverage, exaggerated race calls, and judges who mistake repair work for a pit stop*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
ANNOUNCER LEO, a capuchin
TEAM LEADER PAVA, a gibbon
MECHANIC KITO, a macaque
JUDGE NOLL, a lemur
RIVAL OMI, an orangutan

[SFX: CROWD, CART WHEELS RATTLE.]

LEO: Welcome to the town cart derby. Each team pushes a handcart one lap around the market square and crosses the line with its cargo intact.

NOLL: The cargo is one stack of empty baskets.

OMI: Our cart has a flag.

PAVA: The flag is not cargo.

LEO: The crowd loves a flag.

[SFX: HORN.]

The teams start. Omi's cart rolls quickly, but its baskets wobble.
 
At the far corner, a chalk line marks the required turn. Omi takes the corner wide to protect the baskets. Pava turns sharply but slows to keep the rope from dragging.

[SFX: CART WHEELS SKID, ROPE SCRAPES.]

LEO: Pava has sacrificed speed for control.

KITO: She has kept the cargo inside the cart.

OMI: We are still ahead.

NOLL: The course includes the turn. Cutting across the fountain is not allowed.

Omi's flag covers the chalk line, and Noll asks the team to stop and show their route. Omi says the flag is part of the cart. Noll says the route line is still visible underneath.

PAVA: We will take the full turn.

LEO: The strategy has slowed.

KITO: The cart is still straight.
 Pava's team takes the corner slowly. Kito watches the wheel.

LEO: Omi is pulling ahead.

KITO: The right wheel is loose.

PAVA: Should we pass?

KITO: The lane is narrow. Let them reach the repair bay.

NOLL: There is no repair bay.

LEO: The judges have marked a pit stop beside the fountain.

NOLL: We have a water station.

[SFX: CART RATTLE GROWS, THEN CLATTER.]

Omi's wheel slips from the axle. The cart stops before the fountain. The baskets remain stacked, but Omi holds the handle at an angle.

OMI: We need a wrench.

LEO: A dramatic breakdown!

PAVA: It is a loose wheel.

KITO: The rule allows repairs, but the clock continues.

NOLL: Correct.

Omi's team repairs the wheel. Pava's team catches up, then stops because a basket has slid sideways.

PAVA: The cargo is shifting.

KITO: We can tie the baskets together.

LEO: A tactical strap!

NOLL: A piece of rope.

The teams move again. Omi's flag catches a low branch and pulls loose. The flag lands in Pava's cart.

OMI: You stole our colors.

PAVA: The branch delivered them.

LEO: A transfer at speed!

NOLL: No contact between carts.

PAVA: The flag is on our cart.

NOLL: The flag is not cargo. Return it after the race.

Pava hands the flag to Kito, who holds it above the cart. The teams reach the final turn. Omi sprints, but the repaired wheel squeaks. Pava stays at a steady push. The baskets remain level.

[SFX: WHEELS SQUEAK, CROWD CHEERS.]

LEO: Omi is surging toward the finish.

KITO: The wheel is warming.

OMI: It is squeaking.

NOLL: The cart is still moving.

Omi reaches the finish first, but one empty basket has fallen and rolled across the line after the cart.

NOLL: The cargo must cross on the cart.

LEO: A basket has made a solo finish.

Pava crosses second with every basket aboard. Noll checks the rule card and awards Pava's team the win.

OMI: We crossed first.

NOLL: Your cargo did not.

PAVA: The baskets stayed.

LEO: A victory of load stability.

Kito returns the flag. Omi asks for a rematch. Noll says the next race is a walking pace, which Omi calls an insult to competition.

PAVA: It is a cart derby.

OMI: It is a race.

LEO: Both are true.

[SFX: CARTS ROLL TO PARKING AREA.]

Noll checks each wheel before the next event. Omi watches him tighten the loose axle.

OMI: You could have checked it before.

NOLL: I did. You waved your flag over the wrench.

LEO: The champion's flag is returned; the carts are parked; the baskets are still aboard.

PAVA: The trophy should be a basket.

NOLL: The trophy is a wheel chock.

[SFX: WOODEN CHOCK SETS DOWN, CROWD FADES.]
