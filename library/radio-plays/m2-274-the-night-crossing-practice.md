# The Night Crossing Practice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live ferry safety broadcasts, ceremonial signal confusion, and hosts who narrate operational delays as pageantry*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST LUMA, a macaque
FERRY CAPTAIN ODO, a baboon
SAFETY OFFICER PIRI, a gibbon
DECKHAND TAVI, a capuchin
PASSENGER, an orangutan waiting on shore

[SFX: WATER LAPS, FERRY ENGINE IDLES.]

LUMA: This is the Harbor Hour, where the crew rehearses the night crossing before carrying passengers. Captain Odo says the new bell system will make every instruction clearer.

ODO: The bell signals departure, the horn signals arrival, and the hand bell signals attention.

TAVI: Which sound means stop?

ODO: The engine stops when I say stop.

PIRI: We need a clear signal for stop before we load the practice cart.

ODO: The bell means attention.

LUMA: We are hearing a disagreement about sound.

[SFX: HAND BELL RINGS.]

TAVI: I have attention.

ODO: The rehearsal has begun.

PIRI: The rehearsal cart is still on the dock.

ODO: Bring it aboard.

Tavi pushes the cart up the ramp. Its wheel catches at the seam.

[SFX: WHEEL CLACKS, CART STOPS.]

PIRI: Stop the push.

ODO: I heard the bell.

LUMA: The hand bell rang before the cart moved.

ODO: That was attention.

TAVI: I paid attention, then pushed.

PIRI: This is why we need an unambiguous stop signal.

Odo taps the departure bell. The crew begins releasing the ramp rope. Piri raises a hand and shouts for them to stop.

[SFX: BELL, ROPE SLIDES.]

LUMA: The departure bell sounds exactly like the attention bell.

ODO: They are different bells.

TAVI: They are the same size.

PIRI: We should label them.

Odo ties labels on the bells: ATTENTION and DEPART. He tests each. Both ring the same note.

[SFX: TWO IDENTICAL RINGS.]

TAVI: The labels are clear.

LUMA: The sound is not.

Piri suggests using a spoken call paired with a single distinct bell for stop. Odo objects that spoken calls are less ceremonial. Tavi says the cart is still stuck.

ODO: Stop the cart.

[SFX: CART WHEEL CREAKS.]

PIRI: That call worked.

ODO: The cart was already stopped.

They lift the wheel over the seam. The cart rolls onto the deck. Odo reaches for the arrival horn, but Piri asks him to test the stop call first.

ODO: Stop!

Everyone freezes. A gull cries.

[SFX: GULL CRIES.]

TAVI: The gull did not stop.

PIRI: It is not crew.

LUMA: We are not training the harbor.

Odo rings the attention bell once and says "stand by." The crew stands by. He says "depart" without ringing; the crew keeps still. Then he calls "stop" and everyone freezes again.

ODO: We have a working pattern.

PIRI: Because the words are different.

TAVI: The bells are decorations.

ODO: They are historic.

LUMA: They can decorate the control board.

The crew secures the ramp and checks the cart brakes. Odo announces the crossing practice complete. A passenger approaches from shore and hears the arrival horn.

[SFX: HORN BLAST.]

PASSENGER (OFF): Is the ferry back?

TAVI: It has not left.

PIRI: The horn means arrival.

ODO: It also means the captain has found the horn.

LUMA: Night Crossing Practice ends with the engine still at the dock and the stop call finally clear.
 
Tavi releases the ramp rope and begins to lower the ramp. Odo rings the departure bell by habit. Piri raises a hand and calls "stop." Everyone freezes before the ramp moves.

[SFX: ROPE STOPS, METAL RAMP SETTLES.]

ODO: The bell was not the stop.

TAVI: The word was.

PIRI: Keep the word. Give the bells jobs the crew can see.

She assigns one bell to ready and the horn to arrival, but the crew must answer each signal aloud. They rehearse: Odo rings once, Tavi says "ready"; Odo calls "depart," and the crew releases the rope. Piri calls "stop," and every hand pauses.

LUMA: The sequence is clear.

ODO: It is less ceremonial.

TAVI: It is more useful.

A gull lands on the dock post. Odo raises the hand bell; the gull flies off.

[SFX: WINGS FLAP.]

PIRI: That signal was not for the crew.

ODO: It was a test.

TAVI: The gull passed.

The crew returns the cart to the dock and secures its brake. Odo announces that no passenger will board until the ramp seam is repaired. The ferry horn sounds only when the empty boat returns to its slip.

PASSENGER (OFF): Is it back now?

LUMA: Now it is.

PIRI: And the horn means arrival.


[SFX: ENGINE SHUTS DOWN, WATER LAPS.]
