# The Count at Six O'Clock

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio election-return coverage, premature projections, and a macaque candidate who insists on counting applause as votes*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Bex, a baboon election host; Orin, a capuchin data editor; Toma, an orangutan candidate; Lila, a gibbon precinct clerk; Caller A, a squirrel-monkey voter; and Caller B, a macaque ballot-box finder.*

[SFX: News theme, paper shuffling, wall clock.]

BEX: This is the Canopy Count, live on election night. Polls closed at six. First result from Fig Street: two votes for Toma, one for the incumbent, and one ballot folded into a paper hat.

ORIN: We cannot count the hat.

BEX: Can we count the voter who wore it?

ORIN: Yes. The hat is not a candidate.

BEX: Our projection desk says Toma is leading the hat two to zero.

TOMA: I am pleased to be leading anyone.

LILA: The hat was cast by a voter who wanted shade.

BEX: Lila joins us from the precinct. How was turnout?

LILA: Excellent. The line moved steadily until a lemur started grooming another voter. They formed a grooming circle, and everyone politely waited.

ORIN: Fourteen ballots from the ridge precinct. Six for Toma, five for the incumbent, three unmarked.

BEX: A tie among the unmarked.

ORIN: No, Bex.

BEX: The west branch is shaded green for Toma.

ORIN: That's the tree-cover layer.

BEX: It looks decisive.

TOMA: I'll accept any area that looks decisive.

[SFX: Phone line clicks open.]

CALLER A: I'm a voter at Fig Street. Does my fruit receipt prove my address?

ORIN: No. It proves you bought fruit.

CALLER A: I bought it across the street from home.

BEX: Strong neighborhood evidence.

ORIN: Not a ballot.

BEX: A dramatic update: the precinct found another envelope.

LILA: It contains three paper hats and a list of who needs hats.

BEX: Hat support is growing.

ORIN: There is no hat candidate.

TOMA: Should I make a statement?

BEX: Yes, but pause until the music swells.

[SFX: News theme rises, then cuts off as Orin unplugs the monitor.]

ORIN: Full ridge count: Toma 29, incumbent 31. Two ballots spoiled; someone used banana stickers instead of marks.

BEX: Two votes for fruit?

ORIN: Spoiled ballots.

TOMA: I will not claim the fruit vote.

BEX: That restraint could influence undecided listeners.

LILA: Fig Street count is final: 42 for Toma, 39 for the incumbent, and one blank ballot.

BEX: Toma wins Fig Street.

ORIN: Town-wide count is incomplete.

BEX: We can project the winner of Fig Street from Fig Street.

ORIN: We just did.

[SFX: A bell rings; the studio map falls off its stand.]

BEX: The map has collapsed. We are moving to audio-only projections.

ORIN: Complete count: Toma wins 214 to 207, with two spoiled ballots and one empty envelope.

TOMA: Thank you to the voters. I will serve every branch, including the ones that did not vote for me.

BEX: Will you serve the hat?

TOMA: The hat can sit in the council gallery.

LILA: The clerk has certified the count. I am handing Toma the result sheet.

[SFX: Paper passed across desk.]

ORIN: Read the sheet, please.

TOMA: It says I am elected town arborist. I thought I was running for mayor.

LILA: The ballot listed both. You won the mayor's seat and ceremonial pruning post.

BEX: A double victory: leader and operator of shears.

TOMA: I accept the mayor's office. The pruning post goes to the hat.

ORIN: The hat is not eligible.

BEX: It has been here all evening.

TOMA: Then it can be honorary shade minister.

[SFX: Crowd cheers; one paper hat rustles.]

BEX: The Canopy Count confirms Toma as mayor, two spoiled fruit ballots, and one popular hat. This is Bex, projecting no further outcomes tonight.

[SFX: Phone rings again.]

ORIN: Do not answer it. We are off air in ten seconds.

BEX: It could be the hat calling with a concession.

TOMA: The hat cannot speak.

LILA: Neither can the empty envelope, and it still got a mention.

BEX: We'll take one last call. Hello?

CALLER B: I have the missing ballot box. The goat rolled it behind the stage.

ORIN: Is it sealed?

CALLER B: Yes. The seal is intact. There are no ballots inside, just four apples.

BEX: The goat is running for orchard treasurer.

TOMA: It can have that position. It has demonstrated excellent inventory control.

[SFX: Theme returns. The phone rings once more and is ignored.]

[SFX: Theme plays cleanly as the clock strikes six once more.]
