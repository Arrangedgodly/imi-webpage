# The Last Leaf Gets a Farewell Broadcast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sentimental seasonal broadcasts that turn ordinary change into a ceremony*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Seli, a lemur; gardener Mara, a macaque; producer Vex, an orangutan; groundskeeper Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Orchard in late autumn. One dry leaf rattles on a branch.

SELI: We are live for the farewell of the last leaf.

VEX: A season is ending. We need a speech.

MARA: The tree is going dormant. The leaf will fall when it's ready.

PIP: It is still attached.

ODO: The forecast says wind after lunch.

VEX: Can we ask the leaf how it feels?

MARA: We can't.

SFX: Branch creaks. Leaf flutters.

VEX: It is trembling with emotion.

PIP: The wind just touched it.

SELI: We can record the sound of the orchard and let listeners make their own meaning.

VEX: That won't fill the program.

ODO: The grounds crew has a rake and a compost bin.

MARA: Fallen leaves feed the soil; this one can be a sample first.

VEX: A renewal arc. Better.

PIP: Can I catch it?

MARA: If it falls, don't pull it down.

SFX: Rake scrapes nearby. Odo pauses.

ODO: The rake might shake the branch.

VEX: Cue the farewell music.

SELI: No music. The branch is moving.

SFX: Leaf loosens and drops onto soft grass.

PIP: There it is.

MARA: It's dry and whole. Odo, bring the paper sleeve; we'll press it for the nature table after the wind settles.

VEX: The ceremony is over?

ODO: The tree needs its space. We can rake the path after the gusts pass.

SELI: The last leaf has landed, and the orchard is quiet.

PIP: Should we gather the other fallen leaves too?

MARA: Not until the wind is done. Some are still shelter for insects.

ODO: The grounds crew can leave a strip beside the tree and rake the path.

VEX: But our listeners expect a clean ending.

MARA: The orchard is not a studio set. It changes at its own pace.

SFX: Leaves rustle in the neighboring trees; the branch that dropped the sample stays still.

PIP: The first branch is empty now.

SELI: We can note where the leaf fell without naming it a farewell.

VEX: Is the leaf pressed?

MARA: Odo has put the leaf in his paper sleeve. We will write today's date on a small label.

MARA: Pip, put it in the press beside the spring bud samples. The children can compare what the tree drops with what it grows.

PIP: I'll carry it inside now.

SFX: Door opens; footsteps cross a wooden floor. Paper sleeve slides into the leaf press. Pip returns outside along the orchard path.

PIP: It is in the press beside the buds.

VEX: A full-circle story.

SELI: A seasonal record. No speech required.

ODO: The gust has passed. The branch is clear, and I can rake the path without disturbing the sleeve inside.

SFX: Rake tines scrape stones; leaves gather in a pile.

PIP: There's a seed under the leaves.

MARA: Leave it where it is. It may take root.

VEX: The broadcast could follow the seed until spring.

SELI: That would require us to visit when it grows, not invent a story now.

PIP: I can put a marker beside it so the grounds crew sees it.

ODO: I'll use a small wooden stake beyond the path.

SFX: Stake taps into soil.

MARA: Now the leaf and seed have different futures. The pressed leaf goes on the nature table; the seed stays in the orchard.

VEX: That is a better ending than a speech.

VEX: The season has changed.

PIP: The tree will grow new leaves in spring.

PIP: I'll tell the children not to pick up the marker stake.

MARA: Good. The seed can stay undisturbed, and the leaf can be labeled with today's date.
