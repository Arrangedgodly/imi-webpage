# The Tiny Town Hall

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live civic forums where process consumes the time reserved for the actual decision*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Chair Piri, a baboon; clerk Tavi, a macaque; resident Leni, a gibbon; resident Odo, a capuchin.

[SFX: GAVEL, ROOM MURMUR.]

PIRI: This meeting will come to order. We have one item: where to place the new water bowl.

TAVI: The bowl is currently on the table.

LENI: It tips when the door opens.

ODO: I move it to the floor.

PIRI: We need a motion before we can discuss the motion.

ACT ONE

Tavi reads the agenda. Item one is "consideration of a bowl location." Piri asks whether the bowl is a permanent fixture. Leni says it is a wooden bowl that monkeys use after gardening. Odo asks to move it away from the door.

PIRI: Is there a second?

LENI: I second.

PIRI: The motion is now eligible for discussion.

Tavi starts a timer for each resident. Odo says the bowl tips because the door catches a draft. Leni says the floor near the wall is stable. Piri asks whether a bowl on the floor is accessible to short monkeys. Tavi measures the proposed height with the tape against the floor. The bowl remains on the table until the test.

ACT TWO

Piri proposes a subcommittee to study floor placement. Odo asks how long it would take. Tavi says the committee must meet before it can recommend a date. Leni places the bowl on the floor. It stays put.

PIRI: We cannot test it before the public comment period.

ODO: It is already being tested.

Tavi records the bowl's position in the minutes. The door opens, and the bowl remains still. Piri says the test is preliminary because the door opened only once. Leni opens and closes it three times. The bowl does not tip.

Piri calls for public comment. Odo raises his hand to say the bowl is stable. The timer begins before he speaks and expires while he takes a breath.

PIRI: Your time has elapsed.

ODO: I have not started.

TAVI: The record shows a pause.

Leni asks if the meeting can vote. Piri says they must decide whether to vote by voice or paw. Tavi says the charter allows either. Piri says the charter's wording is ambiguous.

Tavi suggests a show-of-paws vote. Piri asks everyone to raise a paw for keeping the bowl where it is. Leni raises both. Odo raises one, then uses the other to steady the bowl. Tavi records two votes in favor and one abstention. Piri says that is not how hands are counted. Tavi explains that the charter says "members present may signal." Piri asks if holding a bowl counts as a signal. Odo lets go. The bowl wobbles, then settles on the floor. The chair calls the vote again, and everyone raises a paw. The clerk writes "unanimous" while the residents ask for water.

Piri asks Tavi to read the minutes aloud. Tavi says, "The bowl was moved to the floor, where it did not tip." Odo asks if the minutes can mention that everyone drank. Piri says refreshments are not part of the vote. Leni says the water was the reason for the bowl. Piri pauses, then asks the clerk to add one sentence: "The members may test the approved bowl by using it."

ACT THREE

Tavi reads the approved minutes back to the room: the bowl remains beside the wall, where it held steady through the door test. No new motion is offered. Piri checks that the water is clean; Leni fills the bowl while Odo holds the door open.

PIRI: The location is settled.

ODO: Is drinking part of this agenda?

TAVI: The minutes say members tested the bowl by using it.

Leni drinks first. Odo follows, then Tavi. Piri reaches to call the next meeting to order, but the residents have finished their water and are already leaving.

PIRI: I have not adjourned.

LENI: The bowl is empty.

ODO: That is a separate agenda item.
