# The Puppet Theater Reviews Its Own Show

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: arts review panels that critique a performance while it is still happening*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Critic Vex, an orangutan; puppeteer Nari, a lemur; stagehand Odo, a gibbon; puppet Pip, a capuchin; host Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Small theater. Curtain rings slide. A wooden puppet taps the stage.

MARA: Welcome to Opening Night Review. The play is beginning now.

VEX: My notes are ready.

NARI: I have not started the show.

VEX: The empty stage conveys bold restraint.

ODO: The curtain is stuck.

PIP: I can see the audience.

NARI: We're fixing it.

MARA: We need to let the audience know the curtain will open when it is fixed.

NARI: I'll make a quick announcement.

VEX: Say the delay is an artistic choice.

PIP: They can hear Odo working.

SFX: Ring scrapes against wood. Odo taps the post gently.

ODO: The curtain catches because the post has a splinter.

NARI: I have a strip of cloth to wrap around it.

VEX: A repair should happen out of sight.

MARA: The audience is sitting in a small room. They can see the problem and the fix.

SFX: Cloth wraps around post. Ring slides cleanly.

PIP: The curtain opens now.

NARI: I'll start the show with the acorn rolling.

SFX: Fan hums on low. The puppet begins its roll.

VEX: The wind is too soft.

PIP: It rolls at a believable speed.

MARA: The puppet reaches the soil without falling off the stage.

ODO: Fan stays low. The curtain is clear.

VEX: My notes say the first act needs a dramatic fall.

NARI: The story is about growth, not falling.

PIP: I can take the acorn through the soil flap.

SFX: Paper rustle; puppet slips into soft earth.

VEX: The delay creates anticipation.

MARA: Let's let them finish.

SFX: Odo lifts the curtain ring. It slides open.

NARI: The play is called The Acorn's Journey.

PIP: I am the acorn.

VEX: The central image is underdeveloped.

NARI: The story begins when the acorn rolls from the tree.

SFX: Puppet rolls across stage.

VEX: The movement lacks motivation.

PIP: I was pushed by the wind.

SFX: A fan clicks on. Puppet rolls off the small stage.

ODO: The fan is too strong.

NARI: Stop the fan. Pip is on the floor.

SFX: Fan clicks off. Puppet taps wood.

VEX: The fall is an exciting turning point.

MARA: It was an accident. Let Nari reset the puppet.

NARI: Pip, can you climb back onto the stage?

PIP: The puppet string is caught around the table leg.

ODO: I'll free it.

SFX: String slips loose.

VEX: The rescue adds a second act.

NARI: The acorn finds a safe patch of soil.

SFX: Soft earth pats into a pot.

PIP: I will grow into a tree.

VEX: The ending is too direct.

MARA: The show is for children. They understand the change.

NARI: The curtain is stuck again.

ODO: The ring slid behind the post.

PIP: I can hold the puppet while you pull.

SFX: Ring clicks free. Curtain closes.

VEX: A strong final image: the curtain denying the critic access.

MARA: The audience can decide for themselves.

MARA: The children in front can see the puppet now.

NARI: Pip is holding the acorn above the paper soil.

PIP: It has landed. The tree can grow after the show.

VEX: The ending leaves a question.

MARA: It leaves a seed. That's enough.

ODO: Curtain track is free.

MARA: We can reset for tomorrow's matinee without moving the table.

NARI: I'll keep the fan unplugged unless the stage manager calls for it.

PIP: The acorn is ready for its next performance.

SFX: Applause.

NARI: Thank you for watching.

VEX: I give the performance three leaves out of five.

PIP: I give the review one acorn.

TAG

SFX: Curtain opens a crack.

VEX: Is there another act?

NARI: No. That was the curtain.
