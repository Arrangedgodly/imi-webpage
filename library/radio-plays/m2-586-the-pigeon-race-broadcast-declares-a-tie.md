# The Pigeon Race Broadcast Declares a Tie

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio conventions, homing pigeon timing, and commentators who forget that birds arrive by direction, not lane*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Ruma, an orangutan; race steward Niko, a macaque; timer Bex, a gibbon; loft keeper Seli, a capuchin; pigeon handler Daro, a baboon.

SFX: Country field. Flapping wings, crowd behind a rope, stopwatch clicks.

RUMA: Welcome to the county pigeon race. The birds launched at dawn and return to their home lofts. Our finish line is a timing mat at the main loft.

NIKO: The rules say the first registered bird across its own mat wins. There are two lofts today, east and west, each with its own mat.

RUMA: The timing display shows a tie at 10:42.

BEX: It shows two arrivals at 10:42, but not at the same loft. One bird checked in at the east mat; one at the west.

DARO: My bird, Blue Dot, came to the east loft. It is registered to my loft there.

SELI: My bird, Marble, arrived at the west loft. It belongs there.

RUMA: The display placed them in one column. The crowd thinks they shared a finish line.

NIKO: They did not. The race has two home lofts and one shared broadcast clock.

SFX: A pigeon coos; wing rustles.

BEX: I can read the timing cards separately. East mat: Blue Dot, 10:42:18. West mat: Marble, 10:42:41.

RUMA: The birds arrived twenty-three seconds apart.

DARO: Then Blue Dot wins.

NIKO: Blue Dot wins the east-loft category. Marble is the only return to the west loft so far.

SELI: The radio board still says TIE.

BEX: The board is programmed for one mat. It reads both mats on the same clock line.

RUMA: Can we correct it without moving the birds?

NIKO: Yes. The birds stay in their lofts. Bex, switch the display to separate categories and preserve the raw times.

SFX: Keyboard beeps; two columns appear.

BEX: East: Blue Dot, 10:42:18. West: Marble, 10:42:41. No tie.

RUMA: We have a winner in the east and a finish in the west.

DARO: Will the birds get medals?

NIKO: Their bands are checked first. We do not remove a bird from its loft to award a medal.

SELI: Marble has a green band, matches the entry card, and is resting in the shaded crate.

DARO: Blue Dot has the blue band. He is drinking water now.

RUMA: The handlers are checking their own birds while we report from the field.

BEX: There is another entry at the road, but it has not reached its mat.

RUMA: Is that a bird?

BEX: It is a delivery bicycle.

SFX: Bicycle bell rings twice; wheels roll to a stop.

NIKO: The rider brought the spare timer battery. The mat is still recording correctly.

RUMA: The bicycle cannot enter the race.

DARO: Unless it has a band.

SELI: We do not band bicycles.

BEX: I have rechecked the raw times. Blue Dot is first at east. Marble is first at west. The display no longer combines the lofts.

RUMA: There is still a third bird circling.

NIKO: It has not landed. The rules require the bird to cross the mat at its registered loft. No score is posted until it arrives or the race closes.

DARO: I will wait with my bird in the loft.

SELI: I will keep the crate shaded and the water available.

RUMA: The public board will show each category and the closing time. We will not declare a tie just because one screen had one column.

SFX: A wingbeat approaches from above; brief coo; mat chirps.

BEX: A third arrival at the west loft. The band matches. It clocked 10:43:06.

NIKO: West category now has two finishes. The official times remain separate.

RUMA: The radio display updates, and no one has to carry a bird to a different line.

DARO: Blue Dot still has the east win.

SELI: Marble was first to the west, and the later bird has its own time.

RUMA: That is the result. The birds return to their home lofts; the handlers receive the cards.

SFX: Stopwatch clicks off; crowd applauds.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Blue Dot wins the east category, Marble leads the west, and the scoreboard no longer calls separate lofts a tie.
