# The Shelter's Borrowed Bark

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: animal shelter radio segments that use one recorded bark for every animal profile and misidentify who is calling*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Nori, a macaque; shelter worker Mela, a lemur; volunteer Davi, a gibbon; caller Sumi, a capuchin.

[SFX: ANIMAL SHELTER. A DOG BARKS ON A RECORDED CLIP. RADIO MIC OPENS.]

NORI: Meet Pip, a small terrier who loves quiet walks and soft blankets.

MELA: That bark belongs to Bristle, not Pip.

DAVI: The profile system plays the same sound file for each dog.

NORI: It makes the segment feel lively.

SUMI: I called to ask about Pip. The bark sounded large and sharp.

MELA: Pip does not bark at visitors. Bristle does when the gate opens.

[SFX: GATE LATCH CLICKS. A DOG BARKS LIVE, THEN PAWS SCRAPE.]

NORI: Is that Bristle?

DAVI: Yes. He is in the run by the gate.

MELA: Pip is resting in the quiet room. We should record his profile without a bark.

NORI: The station needs a sound for each animal.

SUMI: A soft blanket or a leash clip could tell me something more useful.

MELA: Pip leans on the door when he wants a walk.

[SFX: NOSE BUMPS DOOR. SOFT SCRATCH.]

DAVI: That is Pip. No bark.

NORI: The clip library only has one bark file.

MELA: Then remove the bark from Pip's profile. We can record his own sound.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. PAWS PAD ON MAT. COLLAR TAG TINKLES.]

SUMI: That sounds calm.

MELA: Pip is walking with Davi. He is not in the studio.

DAVI: He is beside me. He stopped when the tag touched the mat.

NORI: Could we say he loves quiet walks?

MELA: That's in his foster notes. The sound doesn't prove it. Say he prefers a quiet path and soft blankets, and this is his collar.

NORI: Understood. I will correct the profile.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. BARK FILE DELETES FROM PIP'S PAGE.]

DAVI: Bristle's recording stays with Bristle. Pip gets the collar tag and the door bump.

SUMI: I can visit Pip without expecting him to bark.

MELA: Call first. He is resting until the afternoon walk.

SUMI: The adoption website also plays the bark when I click Pip's name.

MELA: Remove it from the page and the audio preview. One file was copied into every profile.

NORI: The shelter's promo says every dog has a "big voice."

MELA: Pip's profile should say what he does, not what the jingle claims.

SUMI: A quiet animal may be easier to meet when I know the room is calm.

DAVI: I will take Pip through the side door so the gate barks stay outside.

[SFX: SIDE DOOR OPENS. SOFT PAWS CROSS A MAT. NO BARK.]

NORI: That is Pip returning to his room after the walk.

MELA: The profile now has his own sound, and the shelter can stop borrowing Bristle's.

DAVI: I will check the kennel label before I record another sound.

[SFX: KEYBOARD CLICKS. A BARK CLIP CUTS OFF.]

NORI: Pip's page now plays the collar tag and the nose bump, not Bristle's voice.

SUMI: That sounds like the quiet walk you described.

MELA: He is ready for visitors after his rest. The recording is not a promise about his mood.

[SFX: SOFT COLLAR TAG TINKLES ONCE, THEN ROOM TONE.]

NORI: The profile page now lists his habits, not a borrowed bark.

[SFX: GATE CLOSES. DOG BARKS ONCE FROM DISTANCE.]

DAVI: That one was Bristle.

MELA: Yes. He is in the yard, and the gate is shut.

NORI: We can label the bark clip with his name and location.

SUMI: The radio guide should tell callers which dog is near the microphone.

NORI: I will add that note.

[SFX: LABEL PRINTER FEEDS "BRISTLE, OUTDOOR RUN." COLLAR TAG TINKLES ONCE.]

MELA: Pip's walk is finished. He is back in the quiet room.

NORI: The shelter broadcast can end without making every animal sound the same.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF. ROOM SETTLES; NO RECORDED BARK PLAYS.]
