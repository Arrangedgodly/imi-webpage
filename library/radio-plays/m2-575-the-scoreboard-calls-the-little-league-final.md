# The Scoreboard Calls the Little League Final

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio play by play, instant replay, and announcers who treat a slow scoreboard as a rival team*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Announcer Varo, a baboon; color commentator Miri, a gibbon; scorekeeper Jax, a macaque; groundskeeper Toma, an orangutan; batter Niko, a capuchin; umpire Sola, a lemur.

SFX: Small baseball field. Bat taps plate; audience of families murmurs.

VARO: Welcome to the junior final. The score is tied, two outs, bottom of the fourth. The scoreboard currently says 4 to 4.

MIRI: The actual scorebook says 3 to 4. The scoreboard has added a run to the home team.

JAX: I entered the visiting run, then the board froze while I pressed HOME.

VARO: We have controversy before the final pitch.

TOMA: The scoreboard is powered, but the remote is in the equipment shed.

MIRI: Is it safe to pause the game?

NIKO: I am at bat. I would like the correct score before the pitch.

VARO: The batter requests a review. The crowd is silent.

SFX: One child coughs; a glove squeaks.

JAX: The official book says home 3, visitors 4. I have the lineup cards and the plate umpire's notes.

VARO: The panel is reviewing the evidence.

MIRI: It is one scorekeeper checking a book.

JAX: First inning, home scored once. Second, visitors scored twice. Third, home scored twice and visitors once. So home 3, visitors 3.

MIRI: Then the top of the fourth added a visitor run. Four to three.

JAX: Correct. I mistakenly pressed HOME when that run came in.

VARO: The board has committed an error.

TOMA: The board cannot commit an error. It has no hands.

VARO: The remote has buttons.

TOMA: The person using the remote has hands.

SFX: Laughter from the stands; a bell rings.

SOLA: Play resumes with the correct score announced. Visitors lead, four to three. Two outs.

VARO: We are back after an official review lasting forty-seven seconds.

MIRI: There was no review panel.

VARO: The radio audience deserves a dramatic label.

NIKO: I am ready. Where is the runner?

JAX: First base. He has not moved.

MIRI: The runner had already reached first before the scoreboard froze. No one is out of position.

SFX: Pitch thrown; Niko swings and misses.

VARO: Strike one. The board still says four-four.

JAX: I am not touching it until the inning ends. The book remains official.

VARO: That is a bold strategic decision.

MIRI: The scorebook is what our game officials are using.

SFX: Second pitch. Bat contacts ball; short grounder; fielder scoops.

VARO: A ground ball toward second. The runner breaks for second. The fielder tags him before he reaches the bag!

SOLA: Out. That is the third out.

NIKO: Did that ground ball count as an at-bat?

JAX: Yes. You put it in play, and the tagged runner ends the inning. The score is still home 3, visitors 4.

VARO: Our scoreboard continues to claim otherwise.

TOMA: The remote battery is dead. I brought the spare.

MIRI: We can correct the board between innings, after the umpire confirms the book.

SOLA: Confirmed. Visitors lead four to three.

SFX: Shed door opens. Remote buttons beep.

JAX: The board is reset. Home 3, visitors 4. The inning display reads five.

VARO: We have a corrected board and a new inning. The visitors lead four to three and come to bat in the top of the fifth. The home team will have its turn after that half-inning.

TOMA: I will write "CHECK THE BOOK" on a reminder card and clip it to the remote case.

MIRI: Keep the card clear of the buttons.

SFX: Paper clip snaps onto the remote case; the buttons remain clear.

VARO: The equipment has a better plan now.

MIRI: The scorekeeper has a better plan. The equipment has a label.

SFX: Crowd cheers as the first pitch of the fifth is delivered.

VARO: The game continues with visitors up by one, and the radio booth will read from the official book until the board earns our trust.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The score is four to three, and the scoreboard is no longer allowed to provide commentary.
