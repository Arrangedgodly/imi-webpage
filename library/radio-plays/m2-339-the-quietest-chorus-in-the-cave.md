# The Quietest Chorus in the Cave

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: cave-choir acoustics tours that demand silence while demonstrating how loudly every sound returns*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Choir leader Neri, a gibbon; sound guide Kato, a macaque; singer Vela, a capuchin; ranger Piri, a lemur.

[SFX: CAVE DRIPS. FOOTSTEPS ENTER ON A GRAVEL PATH. A DISTANT DROP ECHOES TWICE.]

KATO: Welcome to the quietest chorus in the cave. Please whisper.

VELA: Why is the choir here if we are not allowed to sing?

KATO: We will demonstrate the echo with one pure tone.

NERI: Our concert begins when the ranger gives the signal.

PIRI: The signal is my lantern bell. One ring means begin, two means stop.

[SFX: A SMALL BELL RINGS ONCE. VELA SINGS A SINGLE NOTE. THE CAVE RETURNS IT THREE TIMES, EACH ECHO SOFTER.]

KATO: Beautiful. Now we wait for the sound to disappear.

VELA: It has disappeared.

[SFX: THE LAST ECHO RETURNS FROM A DEEPER TUNNEL.]

NERI: There is another chamber.

PIRI: It is not part of the tour.

KATO: The sound has discovered it.

[SFX: THE CHOIR HUMS A SOFT CHORD. THE ECHO GROWS UNTIL THE CHORD SOUNDS LIKE A FULL CHOIR.]

VELA: That is more than one voice.

NERI: It is still only us.

KATO: The cave is adding singers.

PIRI: The cave is adding delay.

KATO: Please do not correct the experience.

[SFX: A DRIP FALLS, THEN A WATER BOTTLE SQUEAKS AS VELA TAKES A SIP. THE ECHO MAKES A LONG, RUBBERY WAIL.]

VELA: I did not sing that.

NERI: It was the bottle.

KATO: The bottle has a remarkable register.

PIRI: Please stop squeezing it. The echo is carrying into the restricted chamber.

KATO: We need a sound sample for the tour.

PIRI: You already have the choir.

KATO: The choir is not the cave.

NERI: We can let the cave answer once, then leave. No more repeated notes.

[SFX: NERI GIVES A LOW, SHORT HUM. THE CAVE RETURNS A SINGLE WARM ECHO. THEN WATER DRIPS IN THE DISTANCE.]

VELA: That is lovely.

KATO: Could we do it again, louder?

PIRI: No. The trail is narrow, and the next group is entering.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACH FROM THE MAIN ENTRANCE. A SECOND TOUR GROUP WHISPERS.]

KATO: We will guide them through the quiet passage.

NERI: They can listen without a demonstration.

KATO: How will they know when the echo ends?

PIRI: It ends when you stop talking.

KATO: The second group is waiting at the entrance. We should move along.

NERI: Vela, give them the short tone, then we leave the cave quiet.

VELA: One note, no repeats.

[SFX: VELA HUMS A LOW NOTE. THE ECHO RETURNS FROM THE MAIN CHAMBER, THEN FROM THE DEEPER TUNNEL.]

KATO: It came back twice. Should I tell the group the cave has two voices?

PIRI: Tell them the first reflection came from the main chamber. The second came from the tunnel wall.

KATO: That is a lot of geology for one note.

NERI: It is also accurate.

[SFX: KATO OPENS HIS GUIDEBOOK. PAGES TURN, LOUD IN THE CAVE. THE ECHO RETURNS THE PAGE SOUND AS A RUSTLE.]

KATO: I cannot even open the program quietly.

PIRI: Hold the spine and turn one page at a time.

[SFX: KATO TURNS A PAGE MORE GENTLY. THE RUSTLE IS SMALLER AND DOES NOT ECHO.]

KATO: There. The cave has not answered.

VELA: It is still listening.

NERI: We should leave the next group room to hear it for themselves.

[SFX: THE NEXT GROUP ENTERS. ONE VISITOR WHISPERS, "IS THIS THE QUIETEST CHORUS?" THE CAVE RETURNS A FAINT ECHO.]

KATO: The cave has answered the question.

PIRI: The visitor answered it by whispering.

KATO: Then I will let the guidebook stay closed.

[SFX: THE GUIDEBOOK CLOSES SOFTLY. FOOTSTEPS CONTINUE TOWARD THE EXIT; THE ECHO FADES NATURALLY.]

[SFX: KATO CLOSES HIS GUIDEBOOK. THE ECHO OF ITS COVER CLAPS ONCE. EVERYONE WAITS. NO SECOND ECHO RETURNS.]

KATO: It is quiet now.

VELA: The cave has the last word.

PIRI: It did not say anything.

NERI: That is why it was quiet.

[SFX: THE GROUP WALKS OUT. A FINAL DROP FALLS IN THE EMPTY CHAMBER, RETURNING AS ONE SOFT NOTE.]
