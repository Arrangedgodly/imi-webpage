# The Recipe Measured in Footsteps

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: culinary radio features that describe cooking through theatrical movement while losing track of actual ingredients*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Ema, a capuchin; chef Rolo, a macaque; floor director Ivi, a lemur; caller Pala, a gibbon.

[SFX: RADIO KITCHEN. FOOTSTEPS PACE LEFT AND RIGHT. A MIXING BOWL SCRAPES.]

EMA: Welcome to The Moving Meal. Chef Rolo will make oat cakes while we follow the recipe by walking.

ROLO: The recipe says two cups of oats, one egg, and a quarter cup of water.

IVI: It also says the host crosses the studio for every ingredient.

EMA: I will count steps as measurements. One, two, three.

ROLO: A step is not a cup.

EMA: It gives the audience something to follow.

[SFX: EMA'S SHOES CROSS THE ROOM. A CUP FALLS, BOUNCING ON TILE.]

ROLO: You kicked the measuring cup.

EMA: I was at step eleven. The counter is at step twelve.

PALA: I am listening from home. How much water did you add?

ROLO: None yet. The cup is on the floor.

IVI: The show's movement track says keep walking until the chorus.

EMA: We have no chorus.

IVI: The producer added one.

[SFX: SHORT JINGLE BEGINS. EMA WALKS IN A CIRCLE.]

ROLO: Stop. The stove is on, and the oats need water.

EMA: I can walk to the sink during the refrain.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS SPEED UP. A TAP RUNS. WATER POURS TOO LONG.]

ROLO: That's far more than a quarter cup.

EMA: The path was longer than expected.

IVI: We can name it a generous measure.

ROLO: We cannot. The batter will be soup.

PALA: Could you add more oats?

ROLO: Yes, but I don't know how much water is in the bowl. We should pour off the excess and measure again.

[SFX: BOWL TILTS INTO A MEASURING JUG. WATER TRICKLES, THEN STOPS.]

ROLO: That's half a cup. We need a quarter. I'll pour half back into the bowl and keep the other half aside.

EMA: Can I walk to the shelf now?

ROLO: Walk after I finish measuring.

IVI: The dance cue is still running.

EMA: I will stop moving.

[SFX: MUSIC CUTS. SHOES STOP.]

ROLO: Oats, measured. Egg, cracked. Water, measured. Stir until the dry flakes disappear.

[SFX: EGG CRACKS. SPOON STIRS.]

PALA: That sound is better than the song. I can tell what is happening.

EMA: We were trying to make the recipe theatrical.

ROLO: Then let's give each step a sound that belongs to it. Pour oats. Crack egg. Stir. Set the bowl down.

[SFX: OATS POUR. EGG CRACK. SPOON STIRS. BOWL SETS ON COUNTER.]

IVI: We can keep the movement between steps, not use it instead of the measurements.

EMA: That's still a show. I can walk from counter to stove while the cakes cook, but the burner stays on the cook's timing.

PALA: Can I count steps while you cook?

ROLO: Count the rounds instead. Three spoonfuls, spaced apart.

EMA: I can walk from the counter to the pan after each one, but I won't call the walk a measure.

IVI: The movement cue is now between the ingredient steps. It is no longer setting the amount.

[SFX: SPOON DROPS THREE PORTIONS. SHOES TAKE THREE STEPS BACK FROM THE STOVE.]

ROLO: The first side is set. I am turning the cakes one at a time.

EMA: The recipe is moving at pan speed now, not studio speed.

ROLO: Pan is warm. I am spooning the batter into three small rounds.

[SFX: BATTER SIZZLES.]

EMA: How long?

ROLO: Until the edges set and bubbles open on top. That's about two minutes, but I will watch the pan.

IVI: The jingle cue says eight steps.

ROLO: The jingle cue can wait.

[SFX: GENTLE SIZZLE. A SPATULA SLIDES UNDER A CAKE.]

ROLO: One cake turns cleanly. The next needs another moment.

EMA: The audience can hear the pan and the spatula. They don't need me to march.

PALA: The batter sounds thick now. I know the water was measured.

ROLO: These are ready. Three oat cakes, no floor cup, no mystery quantity.

[SFX: CAKES LIFT TO A PLATE. THE JINGLE PLAYS FOUR BARS, THEN STOPS.]

EMA: The recipe measured in cups, with one short walk to the plate.

IVI: We have removed "step count" from the ingredient list.

ROLO: Good. It was never an ingredient.

[SFX: PLATE SLIDES ACROSS COUNTER. MIC CLOSES; A SPOON TAPS THE BOWL ONCE.]
