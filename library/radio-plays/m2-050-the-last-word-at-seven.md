# The Last Word at Seven

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live call-in debate and roundtable radio programs*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Moderator Varo, a macaque; Councilor Pella, a gibbon; caller Udo, a baboon; resident Fen, a capuchin; and producer Sima, a lemur who watches the station clock.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC, THREE SECONDS TOO LONG. MICROPHONE SWITCHES.] 

VARO: This is Seven O'Clock Forum. Should the village square close to carts during lunch? Each caller gets one minute, no interruptions, and one final sentence. Pella, you support the closure.

PELLA: The square should stay open for people walking from the clinic to the market. Carts can use the north path. It is wider and avoids the fountain.

VARO: Udo, you oppose it.

UDO, PHONE: The north path is steep. My delivery cart carries flour. I can't push it uphill without help. The bakery is busiest at lunch, so I can't unload earlier.

VARO: Fen, what do you think?

FEN: I walk through the square to reach the bus. Carts are fine. I mind when drivers block the curb ramp.

PELLA: A rule would help.

UDO: A rule about the ramp, not the hour.

SIMA: Thirty seconds left.

VARO: One final sentence each.

PELLA: The square should be safe for people who walk slowly.

UDO: The route should work for people carrying heavy goods.

FEN: The ramp should stay clear all day.

VARO: That's three final sentences.

SIMA: We are out of time.

[SFX: MUSIC STING, CUT OFF.] 

VARO: We will take one caller. Keep it brief.

CALLER: The market committee can mark a loading zone by the bakery door. Carts stop there for fifteen minutes, then move before the clinic traffic peaks.

VARO: That sounds like a proposal.

PELLA: It could work if the ramp stays clear.

UDO: I can unload in fifteen minutes if someone holds the door.

FEN: I'll put the ramp rule on the sign.

VARO: We have a consensus.

PELLA: Is that allowed on a debate show?

SIMA: The clock says two minutes remain.

VARO: We need another disagreement. Caller, can you disagree with the proposal?

CALLER: The sign should be blue.

UDO: It should be white.

PELLA: The street crew only has yellow paint.

FEN: Then the sign will be visible.

VARO: The debate is resolved by the paint supply.

[SFX: PAPER FLIPS.] 

VARO: We will take the loading-zone plan to the market committee. The ramp stays clear, carts get a marked place, and the trial lasts one week. The committee will check whether the bakery can meet the schedule.

SIMA: You used your final sentence.

VARO: I have one more.

SIMA: The microphone is off.

VARO: My last sentence is that the microphone is off.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICK. A SMALL BELL RINGS.] 

FEN: It still sounds on.

SIMA: That's the clock.

UDO: Does the clock get a minute?

VARO: It already has seven.

SIMA: This is Seven O'Clock Forum. The trial plan will be posted tomorrow. If it fails, bring evidence to the committee, not another final sentence to my studio.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC, CLEAN FADE.] 


[SFX: ONE WEEK LATER. PAPER NOTICE UNFOLDS.] 

VARO: The one-week loading-zone trial has ended. Pella, what happened?
PELLA: The ramp stayed clear. Two carts arrived at once.
UDO, PHONE: I waited six minutes. I can manage that. The north path is too steep for flour.
FEN: The sign fell over after the rain.
SIMA: We have a report, not a debate.
VARO: What should change?
PELLA: Use a heavier sign base. Keep the marked zone and the fifteen-minute limit.
UDO: Add a second space during the morning delivery window.
FEN: Keep the ramp clear all day.
VARO: That is a motion for the committee.
SIMA: Thirty seconds each.
VARO: One last caller.
CALLER: The square was easier to cross. The carts moved when the timer rang.
UDO: We moved them.
VARO: The committee will extend the trial and review a second space next month. No one gets the last word tonight.
SIMA: Finally.
[SFX: MICROPHONE SWITCHES OFF. CHAIRS SCRAPE.]
