# The Time Capsule Has a Sponsor Read

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio history specials, sponsor copy, and a ceremonial unearthing delayed by the microphone*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Yara, a macaque; historian Odo, an orangutan; town sponsor Vex, a baboon; groundskeeper Piri, a capuchin; student reporter Lio, a gibbon.

SFX: Town square. Shovel scrapes soil; a crowd shuffles behind rope.

YARA: Welcome to the town centennial. The town was founded one hundred years ago, and this capsule was sealed at its fiftieth anniversary. In a moment we will open it. First, a message from our sponsor.

VEX: This ceremony is brought to you by Vex Hardware, keeping hinges swinging since last Tuesday.

ODO: The capsule is buried under the plaque. We should open it before the rain.

YARA: The sponsor message is only thirty seconds.

PIRI: The rain is already starting.

VEX: Our slogan needs a second line. "When time is sealed, we have the key."

LIO: Is that historically accurate?

VEX: It is sponsor accurate.

YARA: We are live. The capsule is beneath the centennial plaque. Groundskeeper, can you lift the plaque?

PIRI: Not until the sponsor banner is moved. It is tied to the lifting hooks.

VEX: The banner is part of the ceremony.

ODO: The plaque must rise evenly. If the banner snags, the hooks will pull sideways.

YARA: Vex, please loosen the banner.

VEX: I can loosen one end during the sponsor read.

SFX: Rope creaks; banner snaps in wind; microphone pops.

YARA: We lost the sponsor.

VEX: I am still here. The banner is not.

PIRI: I have lowered the rope. The plaque is clear.

ODO: I will check the lifting points. The plaque should rise evenly. We will inspect the box once it is exposed.

LIO: This is the centennial.

YARA: We have confirmed one fact without sponsorship.

VEX: I can sponsor the confirmation.

SFX: Two lifting straps tighten. Stone rises slowly. Rain taps the microphone shield.

PIRI: The plaque is clear. It is resting on the padded blocks.

ODO: Now the metal box is exposed. Its label says OPEN ON THE CENTENNIAL. The wax is cracked, but the clasp is locked. I can open it without moving the plaque.

VEX: Vex Hardware has a hinge oil--

YARA: Do not put oil on the capsule.

VEX: I was going to say our store is two streets away.

LIO: The box is open. What is inside?

ODO: A folded town map, a paper menu, and a letter. The map marks the old ferry landing, where the library stands now.

YARA: Read the letter aloud.

ODO: It says: "To the future: we hope you have kept the river path clear, planted shade by the school, and made room for the children to ask questions." It is signed by the school class of 1976.

LIO: They hoped for questions. I have one. Why is there a hardware coupon tucked into the letter?

VEX: The original sponsor was my grandfather. This coupon offers one free hinge through 1978.

YARA: The coupon expired forty-eight years ago.

VEX: I will honor it as written: one free hinge, donated to the archive. Their display case has a loose one.

SFX: A tool bag opens; a small wrapped item is handed across.

VEX: I brought samples for the promotion. Take this wrapped hinge and check its fit before the repair.

ODO: I will label it as your donation and take it with the documents.

PIRI: Your store opened last Tuesday.

VEX: We honor family traditions, and the case needs a hinge.

ODO: The documents need to stay flat and dry. We will carry the map and letter to the archive in a protective sleeve. The archive display case has a loose hinge, so Vex's donated hinge will be useful. The capsule itself can go on display after it is cleaned.

YARA: What about the paper menu?

ODO: It lists the school picnic: bean soup, apple cake, and no sponsor copy.

VEX: I can sponsor the apple cake.

LIO: The menu is fifty years old. You would need to honor the price.

VEX: I have already honored the coupon with one donated hinge. The apple cake can be its own sponsor read.

YARA: We will record the contents in the town archive before choosing a display. The opening ceremony is complete.

SFX: A short rain shower. The crowd applauds; the plaque remains safely on its padded blocks.

PIRI: The plaque remains on the padded blocks. The banner is secured on its own rope, away from the lifting hooks.

ODO: The map will be scanned today. The original goes in its protective sleeve.

VEX: I have one final message.

YARA: Is it about hardware?

VEX: The archive has a free hinge because my grandfather wrote a clear offer. Once the hinge is fitted, the display case can finally have a grand opening.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The capsule is open, its contents are recorded, and one expired coupon has produced an actual hinge for the archive.
