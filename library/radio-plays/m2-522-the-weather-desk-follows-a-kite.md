# The Weather Desk Follows a Kite

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio weather forecasts that treat one gust as a national event*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Forecaster Nari, a lemur; producer Vex, an orangutan; kite maker Mara, a macaque; runner Pip, a capuchin; caller Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Studio wind chime. Paper flaps against a window.

NARI: The afternoon forecast is calm, with one moderate gust over the west field.

VEX: The kite has lifted. Change the headline to "Wind Takes Control." Keep the map centered on it.

MARA: It is my kite. I tied the tail on with a loop, not a knot, because the cloth needs to turn freely.

PIP: I can see it from the studio window. It is at the height of the low tree.

VEX: Can you run after it and keep it in the live shot?

NARI: Our weather map shows the wind moving southeast. The kite is drifting toward the field gate, away from the road.

ODO, on phone: I am at the gate. It is shut. I can open it so the kite won't catch on the wire.

MARA: Don't open the gate while the kite is still overhead. The string could cross the path.

VEX: We need a dramatic chase.

PIP: We need to reel in the string. The spool is beside the microphone stand.

SFX: Spool rolls across the desk. Pip catches it with his foot.

PIP: The line is still attached to Mara's wrist loop.

MARA: It should be on the handle. Give me the spool and I will wind it in slowly.

NARI: The gust is easing. Wind speed is down, not up.

VEX: Could you phrase that as a lull before a second gust?

NARI: I could, but it would be false. The kite is descending toward the open grass.

SFX: Line winds onto spool; the kite fabric flutters.

MARA: Keep the line clear of the chair legs. It has snagged on the stand.

PIP: I can lift the line over the stand without pulling it tight.

VEX: Our crew is now in the eye of the storm.

NARI: There is no storm. The stand is in the studio.

SFX: Line slips free. A soft tap as the kite touches grass outside.

ODO, on phone: It landed inside the field. The gate stayed shut and no one crossed the line.

MARA: Good. I'll walk out through the side door and collect it, holding the string at the handle.

VEX: Can I narrate the recovery as a final descent?

MARA: You can say the kite landed safely. Please don't call it a rescue.

SFX: Side door opens. Footsteps in grass. Kite paper rustles.

MARA: The tail loop is fine; one paper corner needs a patch. I'll bring it back by the marked path.

NARI: Forecast update: one kite down, one gust passed, no change to the regional outlook.

VEX: This was almost our biggest weather special.

NARI: It was our smallest accurate one.


NARI: We will update the field report after Mara collects the kite. The gate remains shut, so nobody has to cross the road to follow the line.

ODO, on phone: The field staff have the gate key. I'll ask them to meet you at the marked entrance, not the vehicle lane.

MARA: Good. Pip, hold the spool here until I return. Don't wind the line around your hand.

PIP: It's on the handle. I can keep the loose string off the floor.

SFX: Footsteps recede over grass, then stop. Paper kite rustles once.

MARA: I have it by the frame. The tail is tangled in itself, not in the tree. I'll fold it only after the paper dries.

NARI: The gust has dropped below the field flag's first marker. It did not carry the kite beyond the grass.

VEX: Can we keep the dramatic headline?

NARI: We can keep the accurate one: "Kite Lands in Field; Forecast Unchanged."

MARA: Put that on the board. The kite needs a patch before the next flight.

TAG

PIP: The kite is back on its hook.

VEX: And tomorrow's forecast?

NARI: A hook, with a chance of no kite.
