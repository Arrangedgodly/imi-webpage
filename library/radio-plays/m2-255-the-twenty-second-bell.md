# The Twenty-Second Bell

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: official radio time signals, precision announcements, and stations that discover their clock is less exact than their ceremony*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
ANNOUNCER TESSA, a macaque
TIMEKEEPER ROL, a capuchin
ENGINEER NIX, a gibbon
CALLER BEE, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: PRECISE, SINGLE TONE.]

TESSA: This is the Canopy Standard Time Service. At the top of the minute, we will sound the official bell. Please set your clocks by our signal.

ROL: The bell is ready. The studio clock says twelve minutes past.

NIX: The wall clock says eleven minutes past.

TESSA: We will use the official clock.

ROL: Which one is that?

TESSA: The one in the studio.

NIX: The studio clock is plugged into the wall clock.

TESSA: Then they agree electrically.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS, ONE BEAT TOO FAST.]

ROL: They are still one minute apart.

TESSA: We will correct that after the signal. Listeners, the exact moment is approaching.

BEE: I called to ask whether the bell is early or my watch is late.

TESSA: Your watch may be early, late, fast, or slow.

BEE: That is a large range.

ROL: We have ten seconds.

NIX: According to which clock?

ROL: The one with the second hand.

NIX: Both clocks have second hands.

[SFX: TWO CLOCKS TICK OUT OF SYNC.]

TESSA: We will count down together. Ten. Nine. Eight.

ROL: Wait. I have started on the wall clock.

NIX: I started on the studio clock.

TESSA: Seven. Six. Five.

BEE: Are you counting together?

ROL: We are counting in adjacent minutes.

TESSA: Four. Three. Two.

[SFX: BELL RINGS.]

NIX: That was early by three seconds.

ROL: It was late by two.

TESSA: The official time signal has occurred.

BEE: Which time?

TESSA: The time at which it occurred.

ROL: We need to calibrate. Nix, use the radio pulse generator.

[SFX: SWITCH. A TONE PULSES UNEVENLY.]

NIX: The pulse generator is following the studio clock.

ROL: Then it is official.

NIX: It was built from the wall clock.

TESSA: We have a reliable chain of agreement.

BEE: It is a circle.

ROL: We can compare the clocks to the sundial outside.

TESSA: It is cloudy.

NIX: The sundial is also behind the building.

TESSA: We could hear the noon bell from the harbor.

[SFX: DISTANT BELL RINGS.]

ROL: That bell is for the ferry.

NIX: It is not noon. The ferry is leaving.

TESSA: The ferry is leaving exactly when it leaves.

BEE: My watch just stopped.

ROL: We can reset it to the next signal.

TESSA: We have another official tone in one minute. Until then, do not adjust your clocks.

NIX: The station clock has stopped.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKING STOPS.]

ROL: The wall clock is still running.

TESSA: Use that one.

NIX: It is a minute behind the stopped clock.

TESSA: A stopped clock is correct twice a day.

ROL: Unless it stopped just before the correct moment.

TESSA: Then we will use the harbor bell as a reference.

BEE: The ferry is gone.

[SFX: DISTANT FERRY HORN.]

NIX: It is still within hearing range.

TESSA: Bell on the harbor, horn on the water, our timekeeper at the desk. We will sound the signal when Rol is ready.

ROL: I am ready now.

TESSA: That is not an exact time.

ROL: It is exact for me.

TESSA: Listeners, set your clocks to the first tone after this sentence.

[SFX: CLEAN TONE.]

NIX: That tone was generated by the wall clock.

ROL: Which is one minute slow.

TESSA: We have corrected the service. The tone is our reference, and the clocks can follow it.

BEE: My stopped watch is now correct.

TESSA: Temporarily.

ROL: The ferry bell is ringing again.

[SFX: HARBOR BELL, THEN TONE.]

TESSA: The Canopy Standard Time Service is signing off. The signal is exact at the moment it sounds.

NIX: I have written down when that was.

ROL: Which clock did you use?

NIX: A notebook.

[SFX: PENCIL SCRATCHES. TWO CLOCKS RESUME, OUT OF SYNC.]
