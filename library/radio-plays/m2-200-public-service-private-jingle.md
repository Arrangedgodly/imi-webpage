# Public Service, Private Jingle

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio public-service announcements that bury the message under branding and catchy slogans*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
ANNOUNCER RIN, a macaque
PRODUCER TESS, a capuchin
COMMUNITY OFFICER KEL, a gorilla
CALLER ASA, a langur

[SFX: BRIGHT JINGLE.]

RIN: This message is brought to you by Bright Leaf, your neighbor in better sidewalks. When crossing the street, use the marked crossing and wait for the walk signal.

TESS: The sponsor name came before the instruction.

KEL: The instruction still needs to be heard.

[RIN RECORDS A SECOND VERSION. TESS CUES THE JINGLE TOO EARLY, SO THE CROSSING INSTRUCTION IS COVERED AGAIN.]

RIN: We have a new take.

TESS: The sponsor cue is still over the word "wait."

KEL: Move the music after the instruction.

[TESS REWINDS THE TAKE AND MOVES THE CUE MARKER. THE WORDS ARE NOW CLEAR; THE JINGLE PLAYS AFTER THE COMPLETE MESSAGE.]

RIN: Use the marked crossing. Wait for the walk signal.

TESS: Bright Leaf supports Canopy Radio.

### Scene One: The Spot

[TESS PLAYS THE ADVERTISEMENT BACK.]

RIN (RECORDED): Bright Leaf brings you safe steps, bright days, better crossings, and a brighter you.

KEL: Where is the crossing instruction?

TESS: It was under the jingle.

[THE JINGLE PLAYS AGAIN. THE WORDS ARE BARELY AUDIBLE.]

RIN: The slogan has covered the message.

KEL: Can you read the instruction without the music?

RIN: Use the marked crossing. Wait for the walk signal.

TESS: That is shorter.

### Scene Two: The Sponsor

[PHONE RINGS.]

ASA: I heard the announcement. Does the walk signal apply at the crossing by the market?

KEL: Yes. The signal is there for pedestrians. The sponsor does not change the rule.

TESS: Can we say the sponsor installed it?

KEL: The city installed it.

ASA: I am standing at the corner now. The signal is red.

RIN: Please wait for the walk sign.

[SFX: A SIGNAL CLICKS. FOOTSTEPS CROSS AT A STEADY PACE.] 

ASA: I am across.

KEL: Thank you for waiting.

[KEL TAKES A FIELD RECORDER TO THE MARKET CORNER. RIN CHECKS LEVELS FROM THE STUDIO.]

KEL: We are at the crossing. The walk signal is audible, traffic is moving, and the marked lines are visible.

RIN: Can we use that sound under the announcement?

KEL: Use one signal click. Keep the traffic low.

[A CAR HORN SOUNDS. TESS PULLS THE TRAFFIC LEVEL DOWN. THE WALK SIGNAL REMAINS CLEAR.]

TESS: The instruction will not compete with this sound bed.

### Scene Three: The Rewrite

[TESS REMOVES THE BRIGHT MUSIC TRACK.]

[RIN READS THE MESSAGE ONCE AT HER NORMAL ANNOUNCER PACE. THE FIRST TAKE IS CLEAR, BUT TESS'S SPONSOR CUE STARTS OVER THE SIGNAL CLICK.] 

RIN: The sound should come after the instruction.

TESS: The spot is only fifteen seconds.

KEL: The crossing instruction takes five seconds. There is room.

[TESS MOVES THE SPONSOR CUE TO THE END OF THE TIMELINE. SHE PLAYS THE SEQUENCE: VOICE, SIGNAL CLICK, THEN A SHORT JINGLE.] 

RIN: The message is first.

TESS: The sponsor is still included.

KEL: The signal is clear.

[THEY LISTEN ON A SMALL PORTABLE RADIO. THE FIRST PLAYBACK HAS NO MUSIC OVER THE WORDS.]

ASA: I can hear it from the corner.

TESS: Then we have the right mix.

RIN: Public-service announcement: Use the marked crossing and wait for the walk signal. Bright Leaf supports Canopy Radio.

TESS: The sponsor fits after the message.

KEL: That works.

[TESS EXPORTS THE SHORT SPOT. THE OLD VERSION PLAYS BY MISTAKE, WITH THE SLOGAN OVER THE SIGNAL SOUND.]

[KEL STOPS THE OLD FILE BEFORE IT PLAYS AGAIN. TESS RENAMES THE CORRECT TAKE "CROSSING NOTICE FINAL."]

TESS: The signal sound is audible now.

RIN: And the instruction is first.

### Tag

[SFX: ONE CLEAR SIGNAL CLICK, THEN A SHORT JINGLE.]

RIN: The message comes first.

TESS: The sponsor comes after.
