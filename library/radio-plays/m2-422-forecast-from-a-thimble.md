# Forecast from a Thimble

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: elaborate local weather bulletins, sponsor maps, and meteorologists forced to forecast around an uncooperative studio prop*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A weather host uses a thimble as a rain gauge and accidentally gives the whole valley a forecast measured in sewing supplies.

Cast: Forecaster Luli, a gibbon; producer Rasu, a macaque; field reporter Fenn, a capuchin; sponsor voice Homa, an orangutan; caller Bex, a baboon.

[SFX: WEATHER THEME, THEN A TINY DRIP INTO METAL.]

LULI: Good evening. The valley had three millimeters of rain today, according to the official gauge. Our studio thimble collected one drop and a piece of lint.

RASU: The sponsor wants the thimble in the forecast.

LULI: It is not calibrated.

RASU: It has a label.

HOMA: This weather bulletin is brought to you by Stitch Strong Thread. Whatever the sky throws at your roof, keep your hem secure.

LULI: We are not measuring rain by hems.

ACT ONE

Luli spreads a map over the desk. Rasu's magnetic cloud sticks to the metal thimble, curling the map. Fenn calls from the hill station.

FENN: The gauge says three millimeters. The fence says it has rained sideways.

LULI: Is the gauge level?

FENN: It was, until a goat leaned on the post.

RASU: Can the goat be our field graphic?

FENN: It has left the frame.

Homa asks Luli to name rainfall in spools. Luli refuses and reads the official measurement, wind direction, and chance of showers. Rasu moves the sponsor logo over the map; the cloud magnet snaps to it and drags the logo south.

LULI: The cloud is moving south.

RASU: The logo is moving south.

LULI: Those are not the same thing.

Bex calls to ask whether to bring a raincoat. Luli says showers are likely after noon. Rasu suggests an umbrella with a spool attached for scale. Bex says she is taking a basket, not a prop department.

ACT TWO

Fenn reports an east wind. His hand-held gauge is too close to the microphone; each gust sounds like a drum roll. Rasu asks for the wind in thread lengths. Luli says wind speed is measured in kilometers per hour.

HOMA: We have an exclusive: twelve spools per gust.

LULI: No, we do not.

[SFX: GUST, PAPER MAP FLAPS AGAINST MICROPHONE.]

FENN: My notes are flying. The thimble is rolling toward the path.

RASU: Catch it before the sponsor mark goes with it.

Fenn catches the thimble. The sound effect makes it seem like a large metallic object falling down a hill. Luli asks if the thimble is intact. It contains one drop, a thread, and a dent. Bex calls back from the market: a gust turned her umbrella inside out, so she is using it as an apple basket. The graphic shows rain there because the magnet is stuck.

LULI: The forecast says showers later, not now.

BEX: The graphic says my apples are underwater.

ACT TWO-B

Rasu removes the thimble and puts it on a plastic tray. The magnetic cloud stays in place. Luli points to the actual map symbols: blue means expected rain, not current rain. Fenn checks the outdoor gauge against a calibrated jar. They both read three millimeters, though the goat has turned the fence post a little.

FENN: The measurement agrees. The fence is crooked.

LULI: Then we can trust the gauge after leveling the post.

RASU: Can we say the rain is three millimeters tall?

LULI: No. It is accumulated rainfall.

HOMA: Three millimeters of rain, three spools of thread, one sponsor mention.

Luli asks Homa to stop using the unit conversion. Homa does, but starts counting the cloud icon's stitches. Rasu asks Bex to describe the umbrella. She says it is now a fruit basket and still useful.

BEX: I will carry it until the rain begins.

LULI: Please keep your eye on the actual sky, not our map.

Fenn levels the gauge post using a flat stone. A goat bleats. Fenn says it wants its stone back, moves the stone, and keeps the post upright.

ACT THREE

Luli gives the final forecast with no thimble. Showers are expected after noon, clearing by evening; wind shifts east, and the market should remain dry until the late afternoon. Rasu returns the magnet cloud to the correct area. Homa announces the sponsor, this time without measuring the weather in thread.

LULI: The valley forecast uses a tested gauge, a checked map and a real wind direction.

FENN: The gauge is level. The fence is still crooked.

LULI: We have a forecast, not a fence report.

Bex says the apples are safe and her umbrella basket is open side up. The theme begins. Rasu reaches for the thimble; Luli puts it in a drawer marked "NOT A GAUGE." Homa asks the drawer's thread count. Luli switches off the mic.

TAG

[SFX: A SINGLE DROP FALLS INTO THE THIMBLE.]

RASU: It rained in the studio.

LULI: That was the ceiling.

FINAL IMAGE: The thimble sits upside down in the drawer while the official rain gauge stands level on the hill.
