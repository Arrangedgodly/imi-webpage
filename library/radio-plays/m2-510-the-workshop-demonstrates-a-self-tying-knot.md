# The Workshop Demonstrates a Self-Tying Knot

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: invention showcases that announce a breakthrough before testing the prototype*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Vex, an orangutan; maker Pip, a capuchin; tester Mara, a macaque; technician Odo, a gibbon; producer Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Workshop. Rope slides across a table. A small motor clicks.

VEX: Today, a revolutionary self-tying knot.

PIP: It's a spool with a spring clamp. It is still a prototype.

MARA: What should it hold?

PIP: A bundle of garden stakes.

ODO: The clamp is not fastened to the base.

VEX: We can demonstrate anyway. The audience wants to see it work.

NARI: The spring is exposed. Keep hands clear during the test.

MARA: Put the bundle in the groove, then step back. We will stop at the first sign of slipping.

SFX: Stakes clatter into the groove. Pip steps away.

PIP: Ready.

ODO: The battery is connected. The table is clear.

SFX: Motor turns. Spool rolls sideways; rope uncoils across the table.

PIP: It pulled loose.

MARA: Stop the motor.

SFX: Switch clicks off. Motor stops.

ODO: Remove the battery before touching the clamp.

SFX: Battery latch opens; battery comes out and is placed in a tray.

NARI: Power is isolated.

VEX: Could we restart it once more for a better angle?

MARA: No. The clamp isn't secured, and the spool walked off center. The test is over.

PIP: The spring is pulling sideways. It twists the rope instead of drawing it tight.

ODO: I'll write that in the maintenance notes with two corrections: widen the base and cover the spring. We are not building or testing those changes today.

SFX: Pencil scratches in a notebook.

VEX: What would count as a successful retest?

MARA: First the base must stay still without anyone holding it. Then the guard must cover the spring. Only after those checks can the maker try one bundle.

ODO: I'll write those as future test conditions. I will not install the bolts or guard today.

PIP: The rope can still be useful even if the machine is not.

NARI: I'll keep the battery in the tray and put the prototype tag where the camera can read it.

VEX: The episode needs a finished demonstration.

MARA: We can demonstrate the knot people actually use while the prototype stays unplugged.

PIP: The stakes still need to go to the garden.

MARA: Lay them in one bundle. Keep the rope ends long enough to pull.

SFX: Rope loops once around the bundle, crosses, and tightens with a firm hand pull.

PIP: That knot holds.

ODO: The stakes are secure. I can check the bundle without touching the exposed clamp.

VEX: We have a success.

MARA: The hand-tied bundle is successful. The machine failed its first test.

NARI: I'll label the notebook and prototype separately.

SFX: Paper tag is tied to the prototype.

NARI: "Prototype: failed first test. Battery removed. Do not use."

VEX: That's a gloomy reveal.

PIP: It's an accurate one.

MARA: Put the bundle by the door. It can go to the garden now.

SFX: Bundle lifts. Footsteps cross the workshop.

ODO: The prototype stays on the table, unplugged, until a later repair session.

SFX: Odo lifts the tied bundle by its handle and sets it in a wheelbarrow.

PIP: The rope holds the stakes together, and the spring clamp is nowhere near them.

VEX: The hand method has a clear result.

MARA: The garden gets its stakes today. The prototype gets a notebook and a warning tag.

VEX: Tomorrow's episode can show a retest.

MARA: If the base and guard are actually built and it passes. Until then, it's not a product.

TAG

SFX: Vex mimics a plug-in click with his mouth.

NARI: Corrected tag: "Do not plug in."

VEX: I was only making a sound effect.

MARA: Then the sound effect is unplugged too.
