# The Birdwatching Contest Hears an Alarm Clock

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: nature radio contests, field identification, and hosts who mistake mechanical calls for rare birds*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Mira, a macaque; naturalist Eno, an orangutan; contestant Pip, a capuchin; sound recorder Bex, a gibbon; groundskeeper Tavi, a baboon.

SFX: Wetland at dawn. Reeds sway; several birds call, then a two-note alarm chirps.

MIRA: We are live at the annual dawn count. Contestants identify birds by sound. Pip has heard a rare two-note call from the reed bed.

PIP: I think it is a marsh warbler.

ENO: We need to listen again before we identify it. Bex, can you isolate the sound?

BEX: I recorded it on the left channel. The birds are clear; the two-note sound repeats every minute.

MIRA: A very punctual bird.

TAVI: The park's maintenance cart is parked behind the reeds. It has a reversing alarm.

PIP: The sound came from the marsh.

TAVI: Sound carries across water. I am backing the cart out of the boardwalk entrance.

SFX: Alarm chirps twice, then stops; cart motor idles.

ENO: It stopped when the cart stopped.

MIRA: Then the rare warbler has left.

PIP: I will change my answer to cart.

MIRA: The contest accepts bird species, not equipment.

BEX: The recorder has a species guide and a separate equipment channel. I can play the sound again at half speed.

ENO: Slow playback can change a call's pitch. Use the original first.

SFX: Original recording plays: birdsong, then two-note chirp.

PIP: The finch is in the foreground. The chirp is farther away.

ENO: Good distinction. The contest scores only calls confirmed by two listeners. Tavi, can you move the cart away from the boardwalk, then we can make a clean recording?

TAVI: I will park it by the gravel lot and switch the alarm off while stationary.

SFX: Cart drives away; engine stops. Reeds and birds remain.

MIRA: The two-note call is gone. We have a clean marsh recording.

BEX: A high trill from the far bank, then a lower three-note phrase.

PIP: The trill is a reed bunting. The lower phrase sounds like a rail.

ENO: A rail call has a rough edge and repeats in short bursts. Let's listen again at the regular speed.

SFX: Bunting trill. Three-note call follows, slightly raspy.

PIP: Rail. I am confident.

ENO: I agree. The call is from the marsh rail on the far bank.

MIRA: Two listeners, one confirmed species. Pip earns the point.

PIP: Do I get a bonus for the warbler?

MIRA: The cart alarm cannot compete.

TAVI: It was not entered in the contest.

BEX: The sound archive labeled it BIRD-LIKE, which is why it showed up in the morning clips.

ENO: Change the file label to CART ALARM and keep the marsh calls in a separate folder.

MIRA: We have another sound. A clunk followed by a squeak.

TAVI: That is the boardwalk gate. I am opening it for the first visitors.

PIP: I hear a bird call right after it.

ENO: That is the gate hinge. I can lubricate it after the tour.

MIRA: The judges are now competing to identify the equipment.

BEX: The actual birds are still audible under the chatter. I am marking a twenty-second quiet interval.

SFX: Studio talkback closes. Twenty seconds of reeds, water, and distant calls.

MIRA: We are back. No one spoke during the interval.

PIP: I heard two calls from the reed bed and one from the trees.

ENO: The first was a rail; the second a moorhen. The tree call was a finch.

BEX: All three recordings are clean. The alarm file is isolated and the equipment notes are separate.

MIRA: The final score is one point to Pip for the confirmed rail. The cart alarm earns no score, though it did start the morning.

TAVI: The maintenance crew has moved the cart and closed the boardwalk gate quietly.

PIP: I will not count another two-note call until I see a bird.

ENO: Listen for the bird, not the confidence of the sound.

MIRA: That is our result. The wetlands remain open, and the count is complete.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Pip wins with one confirmed rail call; the two-note alarm is returned to the maintenance file.
