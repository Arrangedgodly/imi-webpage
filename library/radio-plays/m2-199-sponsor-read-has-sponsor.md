# The Sponsor Read Has a Sponsor

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio advertising segments that interrupt every feature with nested sponsor acknowledgments*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST JEN, a macaque
PRODUCER MOR, a capuchin
ENGINEER KAI, a gorilla
CALLER TUL, a gibbon

[SFX: STATION ID. A SHORT JINGLE PLAYS.]

JEN: This program is supported by Clear Cup, makers of cups that hold drinks. Now, our interview with the person who designed the station's new microphone stand.

MOR: The interview is supported by the microphone stand sponsor.

KAI: The microphone stand sponsor is the station.

### Scene One: The Read

[JEN OPENS A SCRIPT.]

JEN: Our guest, Tul, has built a stand from reclaimed wood.

TUL: It keeps the microphone at mouth height.

MOR: Mention the sponsor before Tul speaks.

JEN: Tul, this interview is brought to you by Clear Cup.

TUL: I built a microphone stand.

MOR: We need a product connection.

KAI: The cup is beside the stand.

[THE CUP TIPS OVER. WATER RUNS TOWARD THE MIXER. KAI CATCHES IT.] 

KAI: That is not a connection we need.

### Scene Two: The Nested Read

[MOR HANDS JEN A SECOND CARD.]

JEN: The stand is made from a table leg and two brackets. The brackets are provided by Anchor Bolt.

MOR: Anchor Bolt is a subsidiary of Clear Cup.

TUL: It is a hardware store.

JEN: Our microphone is brought to you by the stand holding the microphone.

KAI: We have reached a loop.

[MOR ADDS A THIRD CARD: "THE READ IS PRESENTED BY THIS SCRIPT."]

JEN: This script is sponsored by the script that says this script is sponsored.

TUL: Does the stand work?

[JEN MOUNTS THE MICROPHONE. IT HOLDS.]

KAI: It works.

[MOR HANDS KAI A CARD THAT SAYS "THE STAND IS BROUGHT TO YOU BY THE STAND." KAI TURNS IT OVER; THE BACK SAYS "READ THIS CARD." ]

KAI: The stand needs no sponsor.

MOR: Everything on air needs a sponsor mention.

[JEN MOVES THE MICROPHONE. THE STAND SQUEAKS ON THE AIR. MOR CUES AN ADVERTISEMENT FOR LUBRICANT.] 

TUL: That is not what I used. I tightened a screw.

KAI: The squeak is gone now.

[MOR'S SPONSOR CARD COVERS THE SCREW. KAI MOVES IT SO THE AUDIENCE CAN HEAR THE EXPLANATION.] 

### Scene Three: The Interview

[JEN REMOVES THE CARDS FROM THE DESK.]

[MOR TRIES TO SLIDE A COUPON UNDER THE MICROPHONE BASE. THE STAND TILTS. JEN CATCHES THE MIC BEFORE IT FALLS.] 

JEN: The coupon is now part of the engineering test.

TUL: It is too thick to level the base.

KAI: Put it in the envelope for callers.

[MOR REMOVES THE COUPON. THE STAND SITS LEVEL AGAIN.]

[THE PHONE RINGS. A LISTENER ASKS WHICH COMPANY MADE THE BRACKETS.]

JEN: Anchor Bolt supplied the brackets.

TUL: I bought them at the hardware store.

MOR: Can we read the full sponsor relationship?

KAI: There is no relationship. Anchor Bolt is an independent shop.

[MOR CROSSES OUT THE SUBSIDIARY LINE. JEN READS THE SHOP NAME ONCE, THEN RETURNS TO THE INTERVIEW.] 

TUL: The stand is holding the microphone.

KAI: And it is no longer sponsored by itself.

JEN: Tul, why did you build the stand?

TUL: The old one wobbled. I had a spare table leg and wanted to fix it before the call-in show.

MOR: Is there a brand we can mention?

TUL: The leg came from the station's storage room.

[KAI TIGHTENS A BRACKET. THE STAND STOPS WOBBLING.] 

JEN: Thank you for fixing the station's microphone stand.

TUL: You're welcome.

MOR: We have not mentioned the sponsor in two minutes.

JEN: The interview is over.
