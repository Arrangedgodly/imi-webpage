# The Biography in Beeps

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: archival biography podcasts, cassette restoration, and authoritative timelines*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: host Selli, a macaque; archivist Doro, a gorilla; guest Vem, a gibbon; producer Pex, a baboon; caller Niri, a capuchin.

[SFX: TAPE DECK CLICKS. STATIC. THREE SHORT BEEPS.]

SELLI: Tonight, we tell the life story of Vem, the first keeper of the west grove.

DORO: The archive begins in year twelve.

VEM: I was born in year fourteen.

PEX: The archive begins before the subject.

SELLI: Perhaps it is a prequel.

VEM: Perhaps it is the wrong tape.

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS. STATIC.]

DORO: The label says "Vem, west grove."

VEM: I lived in the east grove.

SELLI: A journey is already underway.

### Act One

PEX: The first recording says, "I found the west tree."

VEM: That was after I moved there.

DORO: The tape date is year nine.

VEM: I was a child.

SELLI: The archive has placed your childhood after your adulthood.

VEM: The archive has mixed up the tapes.

DORO: We have a timeline.

PEX: It has two year twelves.

SELLI: We will hear the earliest recording.

[SFX: TAPE CLICKS. YOUNG VOICE, VEM, SPEAKS THROUGH HISS.]

YOUNG VEM: I want to be a keeper.

VEM: That was year fifteen.

DORO: The tape is labeled year eight.

VEM: I was seven years old.

PEX: The archive has the right age, wrong label.

SELLI: We can correct the label.

DORO: We need a source document.

VEM: Ask my sister.

SELLI: We have a caller.

NIRI: I am Vem's sister.

PEX: Can you confirm the year?

NIRI: I can confirm Vem wanted to be a keeper.

DORO: Do you know when?

NIRI: Before the tape deck was invented.

VEM: That is impossible.

NIRI: We used a shell recorder.

SELLI: The archive says the shell recorder began in year fourteen.

NIRI: The archive was made in year twenty.

PEX: We have found an earlier recording device.

DORO: We need to verify it.

### Act Two

[SFX: SHELL TAPPED. A FAINT RECORDING PLAYS.]

SELLI: We hear a voice.

VEM: That is our mother.

DORO: The date?

NIRI: Year eleven.

PEX: The archive says year eleven was a quiet period.

VEM: It was quiet because we were listening.

SELLI: The documentary now contains a family recording.

DORO: We need permission to use it.

NIRI: You have permission.

PEX: Can we use it in the program?

NIRI: If you do not call it a prophecy.

SELLI: The voice says, "Keep the west tree safe."

VEM: That was advice.

DORO: The archive has listed it as a prophecy.

PEX: It is in the folder marked "Prophecies."

NIRI: Move it.

[SFX: FILE DRAWER OPENS. PAPERS SHUFFLE.]

SELLI: We will move the recording to the family folder.

DORO: There are six family folders.

VEM: One is labeled "Other."

PEX: The archive has too many categories.

DORO: The archive has categories for everything.

SELLI: Where does a childhood ambition go?

DORO: Under "First Plans."

VEM: I have never seen that folder.

[SFX: DRAWER OPENS. IT IS EMPTY.]

### Act Three

SELLI: We can tell the story in the order the family remembers it.

DORO: That is not a verified timeline.

NIRI: It is our timeline.

VEM: I wanted to keep the west tree safe. I moved there when I was old enough. My mother gave me that advice before I left.

SELLI: The archive had the events. It lost the order.

PEX: We can label the recordings by voice instead of year.

DORO: We should note that the dates are uncertain.

VEM: And the facts are true.

DORO: The archive can say both.

NIRI: Can it say the first keeper was not the first one?

SELLI: Who was first?

NIRI: Our mother kept the tree before Vem.

VEM: I was the first official keeper.

DORO: The archive never recorded her.

PEX: We have a new episode.

SELLI: We have a corrected one.

[SFX: TAPE DECK CLICKS. A FOURTH BEEP.]

VEM: What was that?

DORO: The recorder started.

NIRI: It is recording us now.

PEX: Then label the tape.

SELLI: "The day the archive learned to listen."

DORO: That is too poetic.

VEM: It is accurate.

### Tag

[SFX: TAPE STOPS. LABEL PEELS, THEN IS PRESSED DOWN.]

DORO: The label says "OTHER."

NIRI: That's where we all are.
