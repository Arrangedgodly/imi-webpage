# The Operator Knows Your Name

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: nostalgia radio programs that dramatize routine archival recordings as personal messages from the past*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST MIRA, a macaque
ARCHIVIST TOL, a gorilla
OPERATOR SEN, a capuchin
CALLER RAE, a gibbon
RECORDED VOICE, an archival hall announcement

[SFX: OLD TELEPHONE RING, THEN A CLICK.]

MIRA: Tonight, we open the station archive and hear a message from 1986.

TOL: It is a request for the community hall's lost umbrella.

MIRA: It could be a clue to someone's life.

SEN: It is an announcement asking the owner to call the desk.

[TOL CHECKS THE ARCHIVE LOG. THERE ARE TWO UMBRELLAS WITH SIMILAR LABELS, ONE GREEN AND ONE BROWN.]

TOL: The 1986 entry says green. The recent recording says brown.

MIRA: Could they be the same umbrella after the color faded?

SEN: We can ask the caller, not guess from a sound.

[MIRA READS THE LABEL FROM THE SECOND TAPE. IT SAYS "HALL NOTICE, 1986." THE FIRST LABEL IS CLEARLY FROM LAST WEEK.] 

TOL: The tapes were filed in the wrong order.

MIRA: The mystery has become an archive shelf.

[SEN PUTS THE RECENT TAPE IN A BOX MARKED "THIS WEEK." IT DOES NOT FIT, SO TOL TAKES OUT A STACK OF OLD REQUESTS FOR LOST COATS.]

SEN: We have three more umbrella messages.

MIRA: Are they all from 1986?

TOL: One is from 1984. One is today's recording. One is a rehearsal.

[SEN LABELS THE CASES. THE DATE ON EACH IS NOW VISIBLE.] 

### Scene One: The Recording

[SFX: TAPE REEL STARTS. A TINNY VOICE PLAYS.]

RECORDED VOICE: If you left a green umbrella at the hall, it is by the coat rack.

MIRA: A message across forty years.

TOL: This tape is dated 1986. Last week's recording is the other reel.

[SFX: TAPE STOPS.]

SEN: That explains the green umbrella.

TOL: The voice is the caretaker asking for it back.

MIRA: Can we hear the rest?

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS, THEN PLAYS.]

RECORDED VOICE: Please collect it by Friday. We need the rack for the concert.

### Scene Two: The Caller

[SFX: PHONE LINE.]

RAE: I heard the program. My family kept a green umbrella from the hall for years.

MIRA: Is it the same umbrella?

RAE: I don't know. It has a label inside with a name.

TOL: Could you read it?

RAE: "Hall coat rack." It belongs to the hall.

[SEN LAUGHS SOFTLY.]

MIRA: A family heirloom returned.

RAE: I can bring it tomorrow.

TOL: We will leave it at the archive desk, not on the rack.

[THE TAPE REEL CLICKS AS IT REACHES ITS END.]

[TOL REWINDS THE ORIGINAL TAPE. IT CLICKS AT THE SAME POINT EACH TIME.]

MIRA: The recording is not a telephone message.

SEN: The voice was recorded on a cassette, not a telephone line.

RAE: My grandmother kept the umbrella because it belonged to the hall.

MIRA: Do you know who left it?

RAE: No. The note only says to collect it by Friday.

### Scene Three: The Return

[THE NEXT DAY. A DOOR OPENS. AN UMBRELLA SHAFT TAPS AGAINST THE DESK.]

RAE: I brought the umbrella.

TOL: It is the same one from the recording.

MIRA: The past has returned.

SEN: The umbrella has returned. The past is on a tape.

[TOL WRITES A NEW LABEL WITH THE CORRECT DATE AND OWNER. MIRA HOLDS THE MICROPHONE CLOSE TO THE UMBRELLA AS IT OPENS.]

MIRA: That is a full sound.

TOL: It needs to dry before it goes in storage.

[SEN PLACES A TOWEL UNDER THE UMBRELLA. WATER DRIPS ON THE ARCHIVE FORM.] 

SEN: It has left a mark.

TOL: A current mark.

### Tag

[SFX: TAPE REEL CLICKS OFF. UMBRELLA OPENS.]

MIRA: A message from the past, and a drip from today.

RAE: I will bring the towel back too.
