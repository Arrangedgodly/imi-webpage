# The Last Recipe on Air

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: cooking radio that treats imprecise measurements as a dramatic crisis*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Chef Nara, a macaque; host Piri, a capuchin; caller Odo, a baboon; sound operator Leni, a gibbon.

[SFX: PAN SIZZLE.]

PIRI: Tonight we make a simple fruit cake.

NARA: We need one bowl of berries, two scoops of flour, and a pinch of salt.

ODO: How large is the bowl?

NARA: The one on the counter.

PIRI: Our first measurement mystery.

ACT ONE

Odo calls from home. He has a bowl but no scoop. Nara says a scoop can be any small cup, as long as he uses the same one twice. Piri asks if that is precise enough for radio. Nara says it is a cake, not a bridge.

Leni plays a suspenseful heartbeat while Odo looks for a cup. He returns with a shell.

ODO: Is this too big?

NARA: It is a scoop.

PIRI: The shell has entered the recipe.

ACT TWO

Nara pours flour into her bowl. Odo measures with the shell. Piri asks listeners to imagine the texture. Nara says the flour should mound gently, but not like a hill. Odo asks how steep the hill can be. Nara tells him to stop packing the flour down.

[SFX: SHELL SCRAPES BOWL.]

Odo presses the shell into the flour until it is full. Nara says a loose scoop is better. Odo dumps it back, then tries again. Piri calls it a second take. Nara asks Leni to stop the heartbeat.

The recipe needs a pinch of salt. Odo asks if he should use the shell. Nara says no, just a pinch between two fingers. Odo pinches too much and drops it in. Piri says the cake has a bold flavor. Nara asks how he knows; it is not baked.

Odo asks if he can taste the batter before it goes in the oven. Nara says raw flour is not for tasting. Piri turns the warning into a suspenseful teaser: "Will the cake survive the mixing?" Leni cuts the music. Odo's batter is too thick to stir, so Nara asks him to add a spoonful of water. He pours in a whole ladle. The shell scoop floats to the top. Odo says the recipe has gained a boat. Nara drains a little liquid back into the measuring cup and folds the batter until it holds together. The shell is rinsed and set beside the stove. Piri asks if it is now part of the equipment. Nara says it was always equipment. Odo asks if the cake will taste different because he used a shell. Nara says the shell measured flour; the oven will handle the rest. Leni gives the timer a small test ring, then silences it before Piri can add a dramatic countdown.

Before the tag, Nara asks Odo to describe the cake's texture. He says it is light and crumbly. Piri asks if that was because of the shell. Nara says it is because the flour was not packed down. Odo writes that on the recipe card. The shell is placed beside the cup, ready for the next batch.

ACT THREE

Nara sets both bowls side by side. Odo follows along with his shell scoop. His batter is thicker, but still workable. Nara adds a little water and tells him to stir slowly. Piri attempts to name the recipe "The Uncertain Loaf." Nara says it is a berry cake.

ODO: I never set a timer.

NARA: You can still set one now.

Leni winds the kitchen timer. Odo puts both cakes in the oven on separate trays. The timer rings once; Nara checks hers, and it is ready. Odo's center is still soft. He reaches for the dial.

PIRI: That is not the oven bell.

ODO: It is a timer.

NARA: Keep it running.

They wait through one quiet turn of the dial. Leni gives the timer a clear tap for the microphone. Odo tests the cake with a skewer; it comes out clean. The contestants remove both trays together.

TAG

[SFX: OVEN DOOR OPENS, TWO TRAYS SET ON THE RACK.]

PIRI: Two cakes have risen.

NARA: Two centers are baked.

ODO: And the shell is still a scoop.

[SFX: KNIFE CUTS CAKE, THEME.]
