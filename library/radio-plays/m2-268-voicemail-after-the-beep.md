# Voicemail After the Beep

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: listener voicemail radio segments, strict message time limits, and hosts who replay every cut-off as a cliffhanger*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST VANI, a gibbon
PRODUCER KET, a macaque
CALLER RUM, a capuchin
CALLER SAI, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: RADIO THEME, PHONE BEEP.]

VANI: Welcome to Voicemail After the Beep. Listeners leave a message in twenty seconds. We play the message, then we do not call you back.

KET: The first message is from Rum.

[SFX: RECORDED VOICEMAIL BEEP.]

RUM: Hello. I have a question about the notice on the community hall door. It says the door opens at eight, but it does not say whether that means the door is unlocked or the event begins. I have been waiting here since...

[SFX: BEEP.]

VANI: The message has ended at the crucial moment.

KET: The time limit ended it. We know he has been waiting.

VANI: For what?

KET: The door, probably.

VANI: We will replay the ending.

[SFX: MESSAGE REWIND, BEEP.]

RUM: I have been waiting here since...

[SFX: BEEP.]

VANI: An unresolved timestamp.

KET: We can read the hall notice. It says the door opens at eight and the event starts at nine.

VANI: Does Rum know that?

KET: We said we would not call him back.

VANI: Next message.

[SFX: BEEP.]

SAI: I lost a blue umbrella at the hall. If anyone finds it, please leave it inside the front door, unless the door is still locked, in which case please...

[SFX: BEEP.]

VANI: Another cliffhanger.

KET: We can add the found umbrella to the table beside the door.

VANI: Then callers might know where to look.

KET: That would be useful.

VANI: Would it end the mystery?

KET: Yes.

VANI: We will replay the last phrase first.

[SFX: MESSAGE REWIND.]

SAI: Please leave it inside the front door, unless the door is still locked, in which case please...

[SFX: BEEP.]

VANI: The umbrella is trapped between inside and outside.

KET: It is in the studio. I found it beside the phone.

VANI: That is not the same umbrella. This one has a green handle.

KET: The blue umbrella is here too. They are leaning together.

VANI: They have formed a waiting area.

KET: I can take both to the hall.

VANI: Then we would know what happens.

KET: The door is unlocked now. I can put them inside.

VANI: We must not reveal the ending before the message finishes.

KET: The message did finish. The beep stopped it mid-sentence.

VANI: We should revise the format. Let callers leave a second message if the first is cut off.

KET: Then it is a voicemail show with follow-ups.

VANI: A two-part mystery.

[SFX: PHONE BEEPS.]

RUM: Follow-up. I am inside the hall now. The event starts at nine. The door opened at eight.

VANI: His second message resolves the first.

KET: And he did it without us calling him.

VANI: The hall manager sent him the notice.

KET: We should make that clear to listeners.

VANI: We will. Rum waited outside until eight, entered, and discovered the event starts at nine. Sai's umbrella is on the table inside the door.

[SFX: UMBRELLA SETS DOWN.]

SAI: I left another message. The green one is mine too.

KET: Then there are two umbrellas.

VANI: A sequel.

KET: We can label them and return both.

SAI: I left them with the hall caretaker, who brought them to the station after the event.

VANI: That explains how the lost-and-found items reached our phone desk.

VANI: The mystery is solved. Our time limit is twenty seconds, and our new policy is to let callers complete their thought in another message.

KET: We will not replay every beep.

VANI: Unless it sounds ominous.

[SFX: BEEP.]

KET: That was the outro button.

VANI: Good night from Voicemail After the Beep.

[SFX: THEME OUT.]
