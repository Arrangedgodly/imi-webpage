# The Wool Auction Ends When the Sheep Says So

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: fast-paced auction broadcasts that confuse speed with value*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Auctioneer Sela, a lemur; caller Mara, a macaque; appraiser Odo, a gibbon; sound mixer Pip, a capuchin; seller Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Barn hall. Fleece rustles. A hand bell rings.

SELA: Welcome to Wool Call, the live auction for clean fleece, spun thread, and the things people knit when the power goes out.

VEX: This bundle came from my hillside flock. It is soft and very rare.

ODO: It is ordinary gray wool.

VEX: Rare for this particular hill.

MARA: I called to bid. I need enough wool for a nest lining.

SELA: We start at ten acorns. Ten, do I hear twelve?

MARA: Eleven.

SELA: Twelve.

MARA: I said eleven.

SELA: I heard twelve. Sold at twelve.

MARA: I did not bid twelve.

ODO: The mixer is echoing the last number.

PIP: I have the auction mic pointed at the back wall.

SELA: Then the wall made a bid.

VEX: Does the wall want the whole fleece?

MARA: The wall does not have a nest.

ODO: We need a clean line. Pip, turn the mic toward the callers.

SFX: Microphone stand shifts. A soft baa from the barn.

SELA: Thirteen.

MARA: That was the sheep.

VEX: It has excellent timing.

ODO: The sheep cannot bid.

PIP: The mic is picking up the fleece rack rubbing against the stall.

SFX: Fleece scrapes wood in a low shhh.

SELA: Fourteen.

MARA: The rack has bid twice.

VEX: It has a lot of wool on it.

SELA: We cannot sell to a rack. Mara, what is your maximum?

MARA: Twelve. I need the wool, but not enough to owe the rack.

ODO: The appraisal is ten to twelve. There is no rare-hill premium.

VEX: I will add a certificate.

MARA: Is the certificate wool?

VEX: It proves the wool is from the hill.

SELA: That adds three.

MARA: The hill is right behind me.

SFX: An auction bell rings, then a sheep bleats from nearby.

SELA: Fifteen.

MARA: If the sheep is bidding, ask it to show its number.

PIP: It has a paper tag on its collar. I can hold it to the mic.

ODO: No. Read the tag.

PIP: It says "Fleece lot three."

VEX: That is the wool's inventory tag.

SELA: The sheep has not bid. The rack has not bid. The mic has been inventing bidders.

MARA: Then restart at ten, and let people say their own numbers.

SELA: Ten acorns?

MARA: Ten.

VEX: Eleven.

SELA: Seller bid. Twelve?

MARA: Twelve.

SELA: Twelve to Mara. Once.

SFX: A soft fleece bundle slides across the table.

SELA: Twice.

VEX: Sold.

SFX: Hand bell. A sheep bleats in the pause.

SELA: The buyer is Mara at twelve acorns.

MARA: Thank you. I will card it and line the nest by hand.

VEX: I can deliver it.

MARA: Leave it at the low branch. My troop will carry it up.

ODO: The appraisal was correct.

VEX: I still think the hill made it special.

MARA: Before I carry it, is this one fleece or two tied together?

VEX: Two. I combed them together as one lot.

ODO: The edges separate. That explains the extra weight on the sample card.

SELA: We sold one lot, not the whole rack.

MARA: Good. I need one part for a hollow liner and one for a winter bed.

SFX: Mara separates the fleece along the soft seam. Two bundles settle on the table.

ODO: Two useful pieces, one bid.

SELA: The sale stands at twelve acorns.

PIP: The microphone heard no objection from the wall.

VEX: The wall has learned its limit.

PIP: The hill did not place a bid.

SELA: Our next lot is a spinning wheel.

MARA: Does it bid?

PIP: It makes a sound when it turns.

ODO: We will aim the microphone away from it.

TAG

SFX: Wheel turns. A full roll of wool unspools and drapes over the auction bell.

SELA: We have a new lot.

MARA: We have a scarf.
