# The Choir's Emergency Instrument

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live classical broadcasts that turn a missing prop into a crisis special*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Announcer Nari, a lemur; conductor Odo, a baboon; singer Mara, a macaque; stagehand Pip, a capuchin; choir ensemble, offstage; sponsor voice Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Hall ambience. Chairs settle. A tuning note wavers and stops.

NARI: We are live from Bellflower Hall. The choir is ready. The handbell that signals the opening chord is missing.

ODO: It is not missing. It is misplaced.

PIP: I checked the bell table, the music stand, and your coat pocket.

ODO: Then it is missing.

MARA: We can begin without it.

ODO: The broadcast card says "bell, then chorus." If I skip the bell, the listeners will think we skipped the beginning.

NARI: They can hear us speaking.

ODO: That is not the beginning. That is an announcement.

VEX, recorded: This concert is brought to you by BrightTone, the bell that makes every moment official.

SFX: Sponsor chime. A chair squeaks.

MARA: The sponsor has a bell.

VEX, recorded: BrightTone is not for musical use.

PIP: The voice came from the sponsorship reel. I can try the desk chime.

SFX: Small desk bell, weak ting.

ODO: That sounds like a spoon asking permission.

MARA: It is enough to start a song.

ODO: It is too small to start a broadcast.

NARI: We have thirty seconds to air.

ODO: Find me something with a clear attack and a tail that decays.

PIP: A triangle?

ODO: The triangle is in the second act.

MARA: My opening note has an attack.

ODO: Your note has a vowel.

MARA: It has a beginning.

SFX: Mara sings one clean, resonant "Ah." The hall answers with a soft echo.

PIP: That has a tail.

NARI: It also sounds like music.

ODO: The bell is a visual cue.

NARI: Radio has no visual cue.

ODO: The television simulcast has one.

PIP: Then I can make a bell shape with my hands.

ODO: That is silent.

PIP: I can shake them.

SFX: Pip shakes a pair of wooden clappers. They knock unevenly.

MARA: Those sound like a cart crossing the bridge.

ODO: The bridge sound is in the weather report.

NARI: The weather report is tomorrow.

VEX, recorded: BrightTone, the official sound of an official moment.

SFX: The sponsor chime rings again, then cuts off.

PIP: The chime is on the tape. I can hold the button for one beat.

NARI: That might play the whole sponsor message.

PIP: I can lift my finger after the first note.

ODO: Carefully.

SFX: Button click. A bright two-note chime. Finger lifts.

MARA: That's a fine opening.

ODO: It's not a handbell.

NARI: It is the sound we have, and the choir is here.

SFX: Choir breathes together.

ODO: Fine. Mara, give the pitch. Pip, one chime. No sponsor copy.

MARA: The first note is A.

PIP: The tape is cued.

NARI: We are live in five, four, three.

SFX: Chime. Mara sounds A. The choir enters, warm and steady.

NARI: The opening chorus is underway. The missing bell has not stopped the music.

ODO: It has changed the plan.

MARA: The plan can sing.

SFX: Choir resolves. Applause rises, then fades.

PIP: I found the handbell.

ODO: Where?

PIP: It was on the choir's music cart under the clappers. It rolled into the folded curtain.

NARI: Is it damaged?

PIP: No. It was keeping the curtain from closing.

ODO: We will use it for the next piece.

MARA: The next piece begins with a rest.

ODO: Then the bell can wait.

[Pip wedges the handbell beneath the curtain hem to keep the fabric from folding shut. It gives a small note as the curtain settles.]

ODO: It has a job before its solo.

TAG

SFX: A tiny handbell rings from behind the curtain.

NARI: The curtain has announced the intermission.
