# The Spelling Bee's Stolen Silence

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio quiz shows that treat a contestant's thinking pause as a wrong answer and buzz before the word is complete*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Quizmaster Renu, a gibbon; contestant Pila, a lemur; scorekeeper Bako, a macaque; caller Sena, a capuchin.

[SFX: GAME STUDIO. BELL DINGS. TIMER TICKS.]

RENU: The word is "perennial." You have ten seconds.

PILA: P-e-r-e

[SFX: BUZZER SOUNDS.]

BAKO: The machine marked the answer wrong.

PILA: I had not finished spelling.

RENU: The rules say the buzzer ends your turn.

BAKO: The timer sensor is connected to the audience pause meter. It buzzes when the room is quiet for two seconds.

SENA: I am listening at home. I heard the buzzer before the answer.

RENU: We can call it a dramatic miss.

PILA: It wasn't a miss. I was thinking about the second e.

BAKO: The scoreboard now gives the other contestant a point.

RENU: We need to stop the round and correct the score.

[SFX: SCOREBOARD BEEPS. A BUTTON CLICKS.]

RENU: I will also announce the remaining time aloud instead of relying on the buzzer.

PILA: Then I can hear the clock and the word at the same time. I still get the full ten seconds.

BAKO: I have frozen the points. The answer has not been recorded.

RENU: The clock shows six seconds left. Pila, continue.

PILA: N-i-a-l. Perennial.

BAKO: Correct spelling.

SENA: The silence was part of the answer.

BAKO: The scoreboard rules also say a contestant may use the full allotted time.

PILA: I want that printed above the word display.

RENU: I will ask the station to put it on the next card, then read it before the round starts.

SENA: The sponsor jingle can wait until the answer is locked.

BAKO: The jingle is now on a separate button. It does not share the buzzer circuit.

[SFX: TWO SWITCHES CLICK INDEPENDENTLY. TIMER TICKS, JINGLE STAYS QUIET.]

RENU: That gives us a timer, a score and a sponsor cue, each with its own control.

PILA: Now the contestant can think without being counted wrong.

RENU: The game is supposed to reward quick spelling.

PILA: Quick is different from interrupted.

BAKO: I can reroute the pause sensor to the audience meter only. The spelling timer has its own clock.

[SFX: CABLES SHIFT. ONE PLUG CLICKS INTO A DIFFERENT SOCKET.]

BAKO: Testing the spelling timer.

[SFX: TIMER TICKS TEN SECONDS. NO BUZZER.]

RENU: The clock is moving. The pause sensor is separate now.

SENA: Can we hear the next word?

RENU: Pila, your next word is "cicada."

PILA: C-i-c-a-d-a. Cicada.

[SFX: SOFT BELL DINGS.]

BAKO: Correct. The score stays tied.

RENU: The contest can continue. I will wait until the contestant says the word is complete before I call time.

PILA: That rule should have been in the opening.

RENU: It is now. A pause does not count as an answer.

SENA: The show has a sponsor jingle after every correct word.

[SFX: JINGLE STARTS OVER THE NEXT WORD.]

PILA: I can't hear the spelling cue.

RENU: Cut the jingle until the word is finished.

[SFX: JINGLE STOPS.]

BAKO: The microphone is clear.

RENU: Your next word is "whistle."

PILA: W-h-i-s-t-l-e. Whistle.

[SFX: BELL DINGS, THEN A SHORT JINGLE PLAYS.]

SENA: That was clear from the call line.

RENU: The round ends with Pila and the other contestant tied.

BAKO: The corrected score sheet has no premature buzzer point.

PILA: I would like a rematch with the timer actually timing.

RENU: We'll do one after the break.

[SFX: SCORE SHEET TEARS OFF. STUDIO BELL DINGS ONCE.]

SENA: The spelling bee has stopped buzzing at silence.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES. TIMER TICKS FOR THREE SECONDS, THEN STOPS.]
