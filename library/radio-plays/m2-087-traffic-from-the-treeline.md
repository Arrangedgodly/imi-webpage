# Traffic from the Treeline

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio traffic report about animals crossing a single forest path*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: REPORTER SENA, a lemur; DISPATCHER ORO, a macaque; CALLER PIP, a squirrel monkey; and a flock of geese.

[SFX: TRAFFIC HELICOPTER SOUND EFFECT, THEN A RECORDER BEEP.] 

SENA: We are above the main path, where traffic is moving slowly near the old oak. There is no road, but there are many feet.

ORO: Our map shows a congestion point.

SENA: The congestion point is a puddle.

ORO: Several animals are walking around it.

SENA: One goose is walking through it.

ORO: We have a caller. Pip, what is the delay?

PIP: I am carrying a basket of nuts, and the path narrows by the root.

SENA: Can you take the side route?

PIP: The side route is where the geese are coming from.

ORO: We can redirect the geese.

[SFX: WINGS FLAP. GEESE HONK.] 

SENA: The geese have heard dispatch.

ORO: They are not following instructions.

PIP: They are following the water.

SENA: The geese have stopped at the puddle.

ORO: The geese have reached the narrow point by the root. They are moving in a line.

SENA: Is the path blocked?

ORO: It is occupied.

PIP: I can step off the path and let them pass.

SENA: The ground beside the path is soft from rain. Stay on the firmer edge.

PIP: My basket is wider than the gap.

ORO: Can you turn it sideways?

PIP: I can, but the nuts roll toward the handle.

[THE BASKET TILTS. NUTS RATTLE.] 

SENA: A nut has fallen. The caller has paused to retrieve it.

ORO: We can use the wide clearing beyond the oak as a passing point.

SENA: That would mean waiting for the geese to reach it.

PIP: I can wait. The basket is not in a hurry.

ORO: Our map says the clearing is a route option.

SENA: It is a wide place where both sides can stand without blocking the trail.

[GEese HONK, FEET SPLASH THROUGH THE PUDDLE.] 

PIP: The geese are across. I can carry the basket through now.

ORO: Traffic is moving.

SENA: One group at a time, with room for a return trip.

PIP: I am through. The basket is still full except for the nut I gave to a young goose.

ORO: Did the goose need a nut?

PIP: It picked one up from the ground and carried it to the bank.

SENA: The traffic report has documented a delivery.

ORO: And no collision.

SENA: The traffic desk will monitor the path at dusk, when shadows make the puddle harder to see.

ORO: We will mark the edge with stones, not a warning siren.

PIP: The nut is not lost. It is with the goose.

SENA: We will list the route as open, the puddle as passable, and the clearing as a place to wait.

ORO: The path is now clear.

SENA: The path is clear for one basket and seven geese.

PIP: I can wait until they pass.

ORO: We should report the delay.

SENA: There is no delay if no one is in a hurry.

ORO: We are a traffic report. We need a status.

SENA: Status: the path is open at walking pace.

[SFX: BASKET SET DOWN. NUTS RATTLE.] 

PIP: One nut fell out.

SENA: A small hazard on the path.

ORO: Should I dispatch a cleanup crew?

PIP: I can pick it up.

SENA: The caller has removed the hazard before dispatch could arrive.

ORO: Traffic is moving again.

SENA: The geese crossed the puddle, Pip picked up the nut, and the path is still a path.

ORO: We should update the map.

SENA: Draw the puddle, not a red line.

[SFX: PENCIL SCRATCHES, GEESE FADE into the distance.]
