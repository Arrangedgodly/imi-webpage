# A Court of Small Claims

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio courtroom broadcast about a dispute over a shared shade patch*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: JUDGE IVA, a macaque; CLERK BODO, a gibbon; PETITIONER LEM, a capuchin; and RESPONDENT SANA, a lemur.

[SFX: COURTROOM BELL, BENCH CREAKS.] 

BODO: The court is now in session for the case of Lem versus Sana. The claim concerns a patch of shade beneath the fig tree.

IVA: Lem, what are you asking the court to do?

LEM: I want Sana to stop sitting in my shade.

SANA: The tree does not belong to Lem.

LEM: I was there first.

IVA: At what time?

LEM: Before the sun moved.

IVA: The sun moves every day.

LEM: It moved into the shade I was using.

BODO: The court records that shade is not fixed property.

LEM: I placed a leaf there to reserve the spot.

SANA: The leaf blew away. I thought it was litter.

IVA: Did you sit on the leaf?

SANA: No. It was on the ground.

LEM: It was a marker.

IVA: Was it marked?

LEM: No. It was a leaf.

BODO: The court has received no written reservation.

LEM: I cannot write on a leaf.

IVA: You could scratch your name into the soil.

SANA: That would make the shade patch look like a courtroom.

[SFX: A LEAF SKITTERS ACROSS THE FLOOR.] 

BODO: Another leaf has entered evidence.

IVA: Is it the same leaf?

LEM: No. This one is smaller.

SANA: May I move it away from the bench?

IVA: You may. The court is not claiming the leaf.

[SANA MOVES THE LEAF. A LITTLE SHADE SHIFTS OVER THE BENCH.] 

LEM: The shade moved again.

IVA: The tree has no obligation to hold it still.

BODO: The court will now hear a statement from the tree.

IVA: Trees do not testify.

BODO: The branch is casting the shade in question.

IVA: A branch cannot confirm ownership.

SANA: It could confirm where the shade falls.

LEM: The shadow used to reach the rock at noon.

IVA: At what season?

LEM: Last dry season.

SANA: The sun is in a different place now.

BODO: The court could schedule a second hearing when the shadow reaches the rock.

IVA: That would make the court dependent on the sun.

BODO: It is already dependent on the light to read the exhibits.

[SFX: PAPER SHIFTS, A LITTLE GAVEL TAPS.] 

IVA: We will consider the current evidence. Lem placed an unmarked leaf on the ground. Sana moved the leaf because she thought it was litter. Neither monkey owns the shade.

LEM: I still want to know when I can rest there.

SANA: I use the patch for a few minutes while eating. After that I move.

LEM: I rest longer, but I can use the other side of the tree when the sun shifts.

IVA: Then there is no need to divide the shade by claim. You can tell each other when you arrive and make room when the light moves.

BODO: Should I write this as a judgment or a suggestion?

IVA: A suggestion. The tree will not enforce a judgment.

SANA: Neither will I.

LEM: I can agree to that.

BODO: The court has settled a case without assigning the tree a lawyer.

IVA: Close the file before a branch becomes a witness again.

LEM: Could we agree to share the patch in turns?

SANA: I only need it while I peel fruit. I can sit on the sunny side after.

LEM: I rest beneath the tree at midday. You can peel earlier.

SANA: I cannot control when fruit ripens.

IVA: Then the court suggests a flexible arrangement: whoever needs the shade may use it, and both of you may move when the light moves.

LEM: That is not a victory.

SANA: It is a place to sit.

BODO: The court will write the agreement on a board beside the tree.

IVA: Do not use a leaf as a deed.

[SFX: BELL RINGS. A BRANCH CREAKS, THE SHADE CROSSES THE BENCH.]
