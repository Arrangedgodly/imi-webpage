# The Puppet Caravan Recording

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio travel documentaries, immersive field recordings, and a host who narrates a puppet caravan without noticing the puppets are performing*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST RILL, a macaque
TUNER, a capuchin sound engineer
PUPPETEER SOL, a gibbon
PUPPET FOX, a ring-tailed lemur puppet voiced by Sol

[SFX: FIELD RECORDER TURNS ON. WAGON WHEELS, DUSTY ROAD.]

RILL: We travel with a small puppet caravan, crossing the meadow at dawn. The wheels creak as the troupe begins its long journey.

TUNER: That is my chair.

RILL: It is a creaking chair?

TUNER: It creaks when I sit down.

[SFX: CHAIR CREAK.]

RILL: We have captured an authentic road sound.

SOL: We are still in the studio.

RILL: The studio is a stop along the route.

SOL: It is the only stop.

[SFX: WAGON WHEEL ROLLS. A SMALL BELL RINGS.]

RILL: The caravan approaches a village, where a fox asks for directions.

FOX: Which way is the river?

RILL: The puppet fox has asked a very practical question.

TUNER: The fox is pointing at your desk.

FOX: The river is under your desk.

RILL: That is a metaphor.

SOL: That is a cable.

[SFX: RECORDER CABLE SCRAPES ACROSS FLOOR.]

RILL: Our guide says the path is narrow. We follow the wagon through a grove of old trees.

[SFX: PAPER LEAVES RUSTLE. WAGON WHEEL SQUEAKS.]

TUNER: The wagon is stuck on the microphone cable.

RILL: The journey has encountered a small obstacle.

SOL: Lift the wagon.

RILL: No, we should record the struggle.

[SFX: WHEEL TURNS, CABLE TIGHTENS, MICROPHONE STAND TIPS.]

TUNER: You are recording the microphone falling.

RILL: That is a dramatic moment.

[SFX: STAND CLATTERS.]

FOX: We can go around the tree.

RILL: The fox knows a side path.

SOL: The tree is a coat rack.

RILL: It has excellent foliage.

[SFX: COAT HANGERS RATTLE.]

TUNER: We need a true outdoor sound.

RILL: Fine. We will go outside.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. WIND, DISTANT BIRDS, WAGON WHEELS.]

RILL: The caravan has reached the meadow. The river is audible in the distance.

SOL: That is traffic.

RILL: It sounds like water.

TUNER: The bus just honked.

[SFX: BUS HORN, BIRDS FLUTTER.]

RILL: The puppets take shelter beneath the wagon.

FOX: The wagon is still in the studio.

SOL: The wagon is outside now.

RILL: We have crossed a boundary.

[SFX: WIND BLOWS PAPER MAP. MAP FLAPS IN TUNER'S HAND.]

TUNER: The map has flown away.

RILL: The caravan continues without a map.

SOL: We are going back inside.

RILL: We have lost our guide.

FOX: I am the guide.

RILL: The fox speaks.

SOL: The fox always spoke.

[SFX: WAGON WHEELS TURN BACK TOWARD THE DOOR.]

RILL: After a long and winding journey, the caravan returns to the studio. We are tired, but enriched by the road.

TUNER: We went fifteen feet.

RILL: It was a meaningful fifteen feet.

FOX: Can I stay outside?

SOL: You are a puppet.

FOX: I can still look at the river.

TUNER: The recorder also captured the bus braking, the door opening, and Sol asking where the map went.

RILL: We will edit out the bus. It damages the sense of wilderness.

SOL: Keep the bus. It is the only thing that sounded like a journey.

FOX: Keep my river question too. I may get an answer next time.

RILL: This is a documentary. We cannot promise the river will answer.

TUNER: We can promise the bus will pass again in six minutes.

RILL: Then the caravan will wait beside the road and record its return.

SOL: That is a traffic report.

RILL: A traffic report with puppets has a richer emotional landscape.

[SFX: BUS PASSES. WAGON WHEEL RATTLES IN RESPONSE.]

FOX: The wagon says the bus is late.

TUNER: The wagon is not a clock.

RILL: It is now our timekeeper. The wheel rattled twice, so we have been out here two minutes.

SOL: We have been out here four.

RILL: The wheel has a different calendar.

[SFX: BUS HORN, THEN DOOR CLOSES.]

RILL: This has been our travel recording. The chair, the cable, and the fox have all made it back safely.

TUNER: The map did not.

RILL: Then next week we travel to retrieve it.

[SFX: FIELD RECORDER CLICKS OFF.]
