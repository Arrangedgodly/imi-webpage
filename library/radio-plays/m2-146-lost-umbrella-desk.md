# Canopy Radio's Lost Umbrella Desk

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio lost-and-found call-ins, increasingly elaborate identifying clues, and an umbrella whose owner is determined by its weather forecast*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Mina, a baboon host; Jori, a macaque lost-property clerk; Ketu, a gibbon caller; and Aro, an orangutan caller.*

[SFX: Switchboard lights blink, rain patters on a window.]

MINA: This is the Lost Umbrella Desk. If you've found an umbrella or misplaced one, describe it clearly. Jori, what is in the studio?

JORI: One yellow umbrella with a green handle. It has a torn corner and a tag that says "Rain Later."

KETU: That's mine. I lost it at the fruit market.

MINA: How can you identify it?

KETU: It has a green handle and a torn corner.

JORI: There are six yellow umbrellas with green handles and torn corners.

KETU: Mine predicts rain later.

JORI: They all have that tag. It's the umbrella brand.

MINA: Can you describe what is inside the handle?

KETU: A tiny weather chart.

JORI: This handle has a weather chart.

MINA: We have a match.

ARO: I have lost a red umbrella with a blue handle.

JORI: We have a red umbrella with a blue handle.

ARO: It opens only when the sky is gray.

JORI: That's how umbrellas work.

ARO: Mine opens inside too.

MINA: Is there a button that sticks?

ARO: Yes. It opens in my closet every time I hang my coat.

JORI: Then we have found your umbrella and identified a problem.

ARO: Could you test it?

[SFX: Umbrella snaps open indoors.]

MINA: It opened in the studio.

JORI: The spring is too sensitive. We should not carry it open.

ARO: I can bring it to the repair desk.

MINA: The repair desk is across town.

JORI: We can keep it folded in a wide box until then.

KETU: My yellow umbrella is still at the market. I can pick it up after the broadcast.

MINA: The market closes in ten minutes.

KETU: The rain starts in twenty.

JORI: Your umbrella says rain later. It may be correct.

KETU: I will walk quickly.

MINA: We have another item: a green parasol left at the bus stop.

ARO: Is it an umbrella?

JORI: It has no waterproof lining.

MINA: So it is not for rain.

KETU: It could still provide shade.

JORI: The owner left a note saying it belongs to the gardener.

MINA: We know who owns it.

JORI: The gardener says it was borrowed by a capuchin, who left it at the stop.

ARO: The capuchin is waiting at the studio door.

[SFX: Door opens; footsteps enter.]

CAPUCHIN: I brought it back. I thought the sun would stay out.

MINA: It is raining now.

CAPUCHIN: I have a coat.

JORI: Your coat is inside the red umbrella.

ARO: That explains the closet trigger.

MINA: We have reunited two umbrellas and returned a parasol. Ketu is going to the market, and Aro will take the red one to repair.

JORI: The lost umbrella desk now has no umbrellas.

MINA: Please do not lose one before we sign off.

[SFX: Rain stops, sun comes out.]

KETU: I am back. The market had my umbrella. It says "Rain Later," and it was right.

MINA: That is a rare forecast with a confirmed owner.

JORI: We will label the remaining repair ticket, not keep the umbrella.

MINA: This has been the Lost Umbrella Desk. Describe the handle, check the tag, and bring the closet-opening one to the repair shop.

JORI: I have added a simple tag to the red umbrella: "Fold before hanging." That should stop it opening in the closet.

ARO: I will test it at home after the spring is repaired.

MINA: The yellow umbrella stays at the market for Ketu, and the parasol is with its gardener.

JORI: The desk is empty, which means everyone has what belongs to them.

[SFX: Switchboard dims; one umbrella closes with a soft click.]
