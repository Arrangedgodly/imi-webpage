# The Orchard Service

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: automated telephone menu and customer-support escalation*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: AUTOMATED VOICE, a calm lemur; AGENT SIVA, a macaque; CALLER RON, a capuchin; and MANAGER KET, a gibbon.

[SFX: PHONE RING, THEN A THREE-NOTE MENU TONE.] 

VOICE: Thank you for calling Orchard Services. For fruit delivery, press one. For fruit that has arrived but is not where you left it, press two. For general inquiries, press three. To repeat these options, please say "again" or remain silent.

RON: Again.

VOICE: We did not understand. To repeat these options, please say "again" or remain silent.

RON: Again!

VOICE: We did not understand. Your silence has been recorded.

[SFX: HOLD MUSIC: A FLUTE ATTEMPTS A SIMPLE TUNE.] 

SIVA: Orchard Services, this is Siva. How can I help?

RON: I ordered six pears. The basket has six pears, but one is on the roof.

SIVA: Is the basket on the roof?

RON: No, the pear is on the roof.

SIVA: I can open a fruit-location case.

RON: Can you just tell me how to get it down?

SIVA: Is the roof accessible?

RON: From the tree, yes.

SIVA: I cannot advise climbing. I can dispatch a trained fruit retrieval specialist.

RON: How long?

SIVA: Our next available specialist is between now and when the wind changes.

RON: The wind is changing now.

SIVA: I will update the case.

[THE PHONE BEEPS. A SECOND AGENT JOINS.] 

KET: I'm the manager. I understand there is a pear situation.

RON: It is one pear.

KET: We classify by customer impact, not fruit count.

SIVA: The customer is standing under the tree.

KET: Is the pear posing an immediate hazard?

RON: It is resting on a branch.

KET: Is the branch a service branch?

SIVA: It is part of the tree.

KET: Then the tree has authority over the branch.

RON: Can I shake the branch gently?

SIVA: That is a reasonable first step if nothing is underneath it.

VOICE: Please confirm the pear is no longer in its previous location.

RON: It is in the basket now.

VOICE: We did not understand. To confirm fruit relocation, press one.

RON: One.

VOICE: You have reached the relocation desk. Your current estimated wait is one tree.

RON: I am still standing by the same tree.

SIVA: We can merge the calls.

KET: The menu cannot merge calls.

SIVA: It can transfer them.

KET: The transfer desk is closed.

RON: I have another pear in the basket. Can I eat that one while you sort this out?

KET: Is it part of the case?

RON: It is part of the fruit order.

SIVA: The caller may use delivered fruit while waiting for service.

VOICE: For questions about eating, press one.

RON: I am going to eat it.

SFX: ONE BITE.

KET: The caller has taken action without a ticket.

SIVA: The caller has eaten a pear.

KET: I will close the ticket.

SIVA: The ticket has no box for "successfully used the item."

KET: Then mark it resolved.

[RON SHAKES A BRANCH. THE PEAR ROLLS ALONG IT AND DROPS INTO THE BASKET.] 

RON: It fell into the basket.

KET: Can you confirm receipt?

RON: I can confirm the pear is here.

SIVA: Your case is resolved.

VOICE: To close your case, press one. To reopen your case, press two. To report that the menu took longer than the pear, press three.

RON: Three.

VOICE: Your feedback is important. Your estimated wait time is one pear.

KET: Can we say the system helped?

SIVA: The system kept him on the phone while the wind moved the pear.

RON: I was already under the tree.

KET: We can improve the menu.

VOICE: For menu improvements, press one.

[SFX: RON HANGS UP. THE FLUTE MUSIC CONTINUES FOR ONE BAR, THEN STOPS.] 

SIVA: Should we call him back?

KET: The pear has his attention.

[SFX: ONE BITE, THEN DIAL TONE.]
