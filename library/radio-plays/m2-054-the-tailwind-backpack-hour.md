# The Tailwind Backpack Hour

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio home-shopping infomercials*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Bibi, a macaque; product designer Len, a gibbon; caller Toma, a baboon; and producer Fenn, a capuchin who times every demonstration.

[SFX: BRIGHT JINGLE. ZIPPER RUNS, THEN SNAGS.] 

BIBI: Welcome to Tailwind, the backpack designed for monkeys who use their tails. Len, what makes this pack different?

LEN: It has a padded tail opening, two shoulder straps, and a small pouch for snacks. The opening does not hold the tail. It lets the wearer move freely while the bag stays on the back.

BIBI: And our special offer?

FENN: We have not set a price yet.

BIBI: Then the offer is that we are not charging you until we know the price.

LEN: That is not an offer. That's how pricing works.

BIBI: Let's demonstrate. I put it on.

[SFX: STRAPS PULL. ZIPPER SNAGS AGAIN.] 

BIBI: My tail is stuck.

LEN: You put the strap through the tail opening.

BIBI: I thought it was a loop.

LEN: It's an opening. Turn the pack around and try again.

[SFX: BAG TURNS. STRAPS ADJUST.] 

BIBI: Now I can swing my tail.

FENN: The zipper is still caught on the tag.

LEN: Remove the tag before wearing it.

BIBI: That seems important.

TOMA, PHONE: Is it waterproof?

LEN: It resists light rain. It is not waterproof. The seams are not sealed, and you should not use it to carry a phone through a river.

BIBI: Can it hold a water bottle?

LEN: Yes, on the side. The bottle should have a lid.

FENN: The demonstration bottle is empty.

BIBI: We wouldn't want a spill in the studio.

TOMA: Does the pack fit a long-tailed monkey?

LEN: It comes in two sizes. Measure the torso first. A pack that pulls on the shoulders can cause discomfort.

BIBI: Does it include snacks?

LEN: The pouch holds snacks. It does not come with snacks.

FENN: The ad said "snack-ready."

BIBI: I heard "snack included."

LEN: That is why we changed the label.

[SFX: PAPER LABEL PEELS OFF. A NEW LABEL IS TAPED ON.] 

BIBI: The Tailwind pack. Room for a tail, room for a bottle, and room for exactly the items you choose to pack. Len, how much?

LEN: The small is twenty coins, the large is twenty-four. There is no discount for buying two until we know whether two fit in the shipping crate.

FENN: They do not.

BIBI: Then do not buy two.

TOMA: Can I return it if the opening does not fit?

LEN: Yes, within thirty days, clean and unused. Check the size guide before ordering.

BIBI: Call now?

FENN: The number is on the screen.

BIBI: This is radio.

FENN: The number is on the station website.

BIBI: We should say that before the offer.

LEN: We did.

BIBI: Could we include a snack as a bonus?

LEN: We would need to price the snack and list allergens.

BIBI: What about a photograph of a banana?

LEN: It is not edible.

BIBI: It is a picture of an idea.

TOMA: I just want the backpack.

BIBI: Then order one size, check the fit, and carry only what you need.

FENN: Final demonstration. Bibi turns, the tail swings, and the pack stays put.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS, ZIPPER PULL, PACK SLIDES OFF A BENCH.]

FENN: Stop. The zipper snagged on the sample's loose thread, not on the tail loop.

LEN: We can trim the thread and test again. No one should have to tug hard on a strap.

BIBI: Tailwind is water-resistant, not waterproof. The pouch fits a snack, not a picnic. The return tag is thirty days, clean and dry.

TOMA, PHONE: Can it carry a whole bunch of bananas?

LEN: It can carry the labeled load limit. A bunch is not a unit of weight.

FENN: We trimmed the thread. Try it now.

[SFX: ZIPPER RUNS, TAIL SWISHES, ONE BANANA ROLLS.]

BIBI: The pack passed. The banana did not.

BIBI: The pack passed. The banana has left the set.

LEN: Bananas were not in the load test.

FENN: Then our next product is a return label for fruit.

[SFX: BRIGHT JINGLE CUTS OFF AS THE BANANA ROLLS AWAY.]
