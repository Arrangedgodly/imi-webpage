# The Open Mic Interviews a Ventriloquist

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: community radio open-mic conventions, sound checks, and hosts who cannot tell the performer from the puppet*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Leni, a lemur; ventriloquist Toma, an orangutan; puppet Mr. Beak, a capuchin puppet voiced by Toma; sound engineer Fia, a macaque; audience member Odo, a baboon; village chorus, three macaques.

SFX: Small studio. A microphone clicks on; chair legs scrape.

LENI: This is Open Door Radio. We invite local performers to demonstrate their work. Tonight, Toma will perform with Mr. Beak, his puppet.

MR. BEAK: I am not a puppet. I am the guest.

TOMA: He is a puppet.

MR. BEAK: That is what he says when he wants my chair.

FIA: The microphone is picking up both voices clearly. Toma, please speak at the mic. Mr. Beak, stay near it.

MR. BEAK: I am near it.

TOMA: His beak is covering the grille.

FIA: The beak is foam. It is blocking the sound.

LENI: The guest has a technical note.

MR. BEAK: I want a second microphone.

FIA: There is only one open-mic channel. If I add another, the puppet's voice will echo.

MR. BEAK: An echo would prove there are two of us.

TOMA: Or it would prove there are two microphones.

SFX: Second mic lifts from its stand, feeds back. A sharp squeal.

ODO: The audience has heard the proof.

LENI: We will keep one microphone. Toma, can you turn Mr. Beak toward it?

TOMA: I can, but he will complain.

MR. BEAK: I will face the microphone. I will not face the engineer.

FIA: Thank you. The sound check is clean.

LENI: Let's begin the interview. Toma, when did you start performing?

TOMA: At school, with a sock puppet.

MR. BEAK: It was a very good sock. It had excellent timing.

TOMA: The sock never spoke unless I moved it.

MR. BEAK: That is also true of me.

LENI: What is your favorite part of the act?

MR. BEAK: The applause.

TOMA: He is not supposed to speak during the applause.

MR. BEAK: I am working on the pause.

SFX: Audience applauds; Beak speaks over it.

MR. BEAK: Thank you, thank you.

FIA: The applause mic is live. The audience is louder than the guest.

ODO: I can clap softly.

LENI: Please do. We want to hear the performer.

TOMA: Mr. Beak, we have a short bit prepared about a bird visiting a library.

MR. BEAK: I returned the book.

TOMA: You returned it late.

MR. BEAK: It was a long flight.

LENI: How many weeks late?

MR. BEAK: I cannot remember. My calendar is a nest.

TOMA: The librarian charged a late fee.

MR. BEAK: I offered her a worm.

ODO: Was it accepted?

MR. BEAK: It was not a library book.

SFX: Laughter; a chair squeaks.

LENI: That joke had a clear ending. We can hear the mic again.

FIA: The chair squeak came from the audience. The puppet's beak is still blocking the grille when it turns sideways.

TOMA: I can angle him toward the mic stand.

MR. BEAK: I object to being mounted.

TOMA: You are a hand puppet.

MR. BEAK: I am a guest with a seating preference.

FIA: I can place a small table beside the mic. The puppet can rest there when not speaking, then turn toward the mic for lines.

MR. BEAK: I will accept a table. I prefer walnut.

ODO: This is a folding table.

MR. BEAK: I will accept it conditionally.

SFX: Table unfolds. Puppet rests; microphone meter quiets.

LENI: We will take one listener question. Odo?

ODO: Can Mr. Beak answer without Toma moving his mouth?

MR. BEAK: Yes.

FIA: The meter shows the voice is coming from the same direction as Toma.

TOMA: That is how ventriloquism works.

MR. BEAK: I would like to answer the question anyway. I can perform with less visible movement, but my voice still comes from the performer.

LENI: A candid answer from the puppet.

MR. BEAK: A candid answer from Toma.

TOMA: We are both ready for the next show.

SFX: Applause, now balanced; mic stays clear.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Open Door Radio has one performer, one puppet, and one microphone that can distinguish neither.
