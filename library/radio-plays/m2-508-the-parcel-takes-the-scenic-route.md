# The Parcel Takes the Scenic Route

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel shows that romanticize inefficient routes*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Courier Odo, a gibbon; host Vex, an orangutan; sender Mara, a macaque; recipient Pip, a capuchin; route planner Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Post depot. Parcel lands on counter. Map unfolds.

VEX: This parcel will travel the scenic route to the hill station.

PIP: It is addressed to my cabin at the footbridge.

NARI: The direct route is one mile.

VEX: The scenic loop crosses three picturesque valleys.

MARA: Pip needs his replacement boot before tomorrow's hike.

ODO: I can carry it on the direct path.

VEX: The show needs a journey.

PIP: The scenic route is muddy.

NARI: The map shows a dry road beside the creek.

VEX: But it has no overlook.

MARA: The parcel is not a tourist.

SFX: Odo lifts the package; paper rustles.

ODO: The label says "keep dry."

VEX: We can pack it in a waterproof sleeve before the valley.

PIP: Or take the dry road.

VEX: The camera crew is already waiting at the overlook.

NARI: They can film the landscape without carrying the parcel there.

MARA: Odo, take the direct path. I'll call the crew to meet you at the footbridge.

NARI: We have reached the fork. The picturesque trail climbs to the overlook; the dry road follows the creek.

ODO: The parcel is in a paper sleeve. It is not wet, but I won't take it across the muddy path.

VEX: The audience came for the valley view.

PIP: I came for my replacement boot. The left one split at the toe.

MARA: The direct road is wide enough for the cart. The scenic trail narrows at the ravine.

VEX: We can carry the parcel by hand.

ODO: The sign says the ravine track is closed after last week's rain.

SFX: Branch knocks against the sign in the wind.

NARI: The sign is visible from the fork. We can record the landscape from here.

VEX: That is not the same as hiking through it.

MARA: The travel crew can take an empty camera bag up later when the trail opens. Odo should deliver the boot today.

PIP: The forecast says rain tomorrow. I'd rather have it before then.

VEX: We'll film the parcel taking the safe route, then meet it at the bridge.

ODO: I can keep the package dry and arrive before the light fades.

SFX: Footsteps continue along the creek. Water splashes over a stone.

SFX: Footsteps on gravel. Creek runs alongside.

ODO: The road is dry so far.

VEX: Describe the journey.

ODO: I am walking with a parcel.

MARA: I can hear you from the path. Is the package still dry?

ODO: Yes. The sleeve stayed closed, and the label is readable.

PIP: I need the boot in my left paw before I test the trail tomorrow.

VEX: Could we film the fitting?

MARA: Only after Odo finishes the delivery. The crew can stay on the public road.

NARI: The camera operator has the overlook in frame from here.

ODO: The parcel is at the footbridge. I'll hand it to Pip and return the empty sleeve.

SFX: Parcel passes between hands. Paper sleeve rustles.

PIP: This is the correct size. The old boot split at the toe, so I can replace it before morning.

VEX: And the scenic route?

NARI: Still there, for people who choose it when the trail is open.

PIP: I can see you from the cabin.

VEX: We can record the walk back from the footbridge.

ODO: I will take the direct route again. The crew may film the valley from the overlook later, once the trail is dry.

PIP: The boot will be ready before the hike.

MARA: The parcel reached its destination without the scenic detour.

NARI: The landscape did not move. We can still show it from the public road.
