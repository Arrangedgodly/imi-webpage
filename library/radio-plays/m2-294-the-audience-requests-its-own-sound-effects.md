# The Audience Requests Its Own Sound Effects

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio drama conventions, audience sound requests, and a Foley artist whose props change the story mid-scene*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST VEX, a capuchin
ACTOR NARA, a macaque
ACTOR KITO, a gibbon
FOLEY ARTIST SAVA, a lemur
CALLER OMI, an orangutan

[SFX: STUDIO BELL, PAPER SHUFFLE.]

VEX: Tonight's radio adventure is called "The Lost Expedition." Listeners may request one sound effect, but the actors must continue the story around it.

NARA: I am the explorer.

KITO: I am the mapmaker.

SAVA: I am the sound table.

VEX: You are the Foley artist.

SAVA: I have prepared gravel, rain, a door, and one coconut.

[SFX: COCONUT TAP.]

NARA: We reach the mountain pass.

[SFX: GRAVEL FOOTSTEPS.]

KITO: The map says the pass is ahead.

VEX: Caller Omi has requested a sound.

OMI (PHONE): A squeaky shopping cart.

SAVA: I do not have a shopping cart.

VEX: Make one.

Sava rubs a wet finger around a cup. It squeaks. The actors improvise a cart on the mountain.

[SFX: CUP SQUEAK.]

NARA: The expedition has found a supply cart.

KITO: It is coming uphill by itself.

VEX: The sound request has changed the plot.

OMI: I wanted to hear the wheel.

SAVA: The cup is the wheel.

NARA: We need to catch the cart before it reaches the cliff.

[SFX: COCONUT ROLLS.]

KITO: The cart is rolling.

VEX: Caller Luma requests a storm.

SAVA: I have a rain stick.

[SFX: RAIN STICK.]

NARA: The storm is washing away the map.

KITO: The map is made of waterproof cloth.

NARA: Then it is washing away our chalk notes.

Sava shakes the rain stick, but the sound lasts too long and covers dialogue. Vex tells her to stop; she tilts it upright and the noise fades.

VEX: We have a weather break.

KITO: The cart is under a ledge.

NARA: The expedition must climb down.

OMI: Can you make the sound of a ladder?

SAVA: I have no ladder.

VEX: Use the props.

Sava taps two wooden sticks in a climbing rhythm. The actors descend. Nara's boot catches on a rope, and Kito pulls her free. The rope makes a twang.

[SFX: ROPE TWANG.]

KITO: We have reached the cart.

NARA: It is empty.

OMI: I requested the cart, not the supplies.

VEX: The expedition has found the source of the sound.

SAVA: It is a cup.

Nara asks the audience to choose whether the explorers take the cart or leave it. The callers vote to take it, but the cup squeak repeats under every line.

NARA: We cannot move it quietly.
 
Omi requests the sound of a door opening. Sava pulls the wooden door prop across the table, but it scrapes like a wagon. Kito asks why the mountain has a door.

NARA: It is the expedition's cabin.

KITO: We have not found a cabin.

VEX: The audience requested the door sound.

OMI: I requested a door, not a cabin.

Sava changes the scene: the cart is now a supply wagon outside a cabin. Nara says the expedition has found shelter. The door slams, startling the actors and covering the line that tells listeners where they are.

[SFX: DOOR SLAMS.]

SAVA: I will make it softer.

VEX: Keep the latch, lose the slam.

The Foley artist cushions the door with a folded program. This time listeners hear the click without the crash.


KITO: We can grease the wheel.

SAVA: The wheel is a cup.

VEX: The explorers have found a solution.

Sava touches the cup with oil, but the squeak stops entirely. The actors lose their cart sound.

NARA: We have silenced our transportation.

KITO: We will carry the cart.

VEX: That is not how a cart works.

NARA: This is not how a mountain expedition works either.

Sava rubs the cup again, gently, restoring one small squeak. The actors lift the imaginary cart and walk down the mountain.

[SFX: SOFT CUP SQUEAK, GRAVEL FOOTSTEPS.]

VEX: The expedition returns with the cart.

OMI: That was not the ending I expected.

NARA: It was the sound you requested.

[SFX: STUDIO BELL.]

VEX: The audience has influenced the drama. Next time, we will ask what the sound is for.

SAVA: And I will bring a real cart.

[SFX: CUP SET DOWN, MIC FADES.]
