# The Toy Train Museum Replays Its Departure

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio drama, recorded effects, and a miniature railway mistaken for an active transit service*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Vela, an orangutan; curator Niko, a macaque; model operator Seli, a gibbon; visitor Rafi, a baboon; narrator Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Toy train museum. Tiny whistle, click of rails, low motor whir.

VELA: The museum's miniature train is departing from its station, though no visitor has touched the controls. Curator Niko, is the train moving on its own?

NIKO: The model is on a timed loop. It circles the display every fifteen minutes.

RAFI: The loudspeaker just announced, "Now boarding for Harbor Town." Is there a Harbor Town?

NIKO: That is the recorded narration from the original exhibit. Harbor Town is a painted backdrop.

PIP, recorded: Please stand behind the yellow line.

VELA: There is no yellow line in the miniature station.

SELI: There is one in the visitor gallery. The recorded voice is intended for the audience, not the model passengers.

RAFI: The train stopped at the painted platform.

NIKO: It stops there every circuit to trigger the station sound. The model has no passengers.

VELA: The broadcast is making a toy run sound like a service announcement.

SFX: Tiny doors click; whistle. A bell rings twice.

PIP, recorded: The last passenger has boarded.

RAFI: But no one boarded.

NIKO: The sound belongs to the old exhibit narration. It was recorded when the museum had little figurines inside the cars. We removed them for restoration.

VELA: Why does the recording still say passengers are aboard?

SELI: The restored cars have an empty interior so people can see the wheel mechanism. The narration was not updated.

NIKO: I can pause the loop. The train is approaching the maintenance bridge.

SFX: Model slows; motor stops.

SELI: The train is still on the track. I will place the display brake and check the wheel.

RAFI: Did it lose a passenger?

NIKO: It lost a signal. The station magnet is under the track. The narration sensor is set at the wrong distance.

VELA: So the train stopped because of a magnet, not a missing traveler.

SELI: Correct. The stop marker shifted when we replaced the bridge. I can move it back without lifting the train.

SFX: Small tool turns; a metal clip clicks.

SELI: Marker aligned. I will run the train one circuit without narration.

SFX: Train moves; wheels clack steadily; stops at platform and continues.

NIKO: It stayed on schedule, and the motor did not stall.

VELA: The audio still says a passenger boarded.

SELI: We can record a new line: "The model train is beginning another display circuit." No departure announcement.

RAFI: Could the old recording be kept for history?

NIKO: Yes. It is part of the archive, but should not play as current narration.

PIP: I can record the new line from the booth.

VELA: Please say it as a museum narrator, not a conductor.

PIP: "The miniature train begins another display circuit. The cars are empty so visitors can see the restored wheels."

SELI: That is accurate. I will link it to the station sensor after the first turn, not before the platform.

SFX: Recorder starts. The new narration plays at low volume as the train circles.

PIP: The miniature train begins another display circuit. The cars are empty so visitors can see the restored wheels.

RAFI: It sounds less urgent.

VELA: It is less urgent. The train is twelve centimeters long.

NIKO: The old recording can play in the archive booth, labeled as the 1970 narration. The live display gets the new line.

SFX: A small label is placed on the sound console. Click.

SELI: The old loop is disconnected. The new narration is cued once per circuit.

PIP: Should I say "boarding" again?

NIKO: Only if someone adds passengers back to the model.

RAFI: I could add one tiny figure.

NIKO: The restoration team is keeping the cars open for viewing.

VELA: The figure can wait for another exhibit.

SFX: Train makes a clean circuit; bell rings once at the station.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The train has no passengers, its loop runs cleanly, and Harbor Town remains painted on the wall.
