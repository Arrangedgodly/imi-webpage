# The Community Kite Hotline

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio kite-flying advice, callers describing tangled lines, and a host who keeps confusing wind speed with kite enthusiasm*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Lio, a langur radio host; Tana, a macaque kite-builder; Miri, an orangutan caller; and Jex, a capuchin caller.*

[SFX: Wind buffets a microphone; paper kite flutters.]

LIO: Welcome to Updraft Advice. Tonight we help callers build and fly kites safely. Tana, what should a new flyer check first?

TANA: Open space, steady wind, and a clear line. Keep away from trees and power wires.

MIRI: My kite is in a tree.

TANA: How did it get there?

MIRI: I flew it near a tree.

LIO: We have identified the cause.

TANA: Is anyone climbing after it?

MIRI: My cousin is halfway up.

TANA: Tell them to come down carefully. We can retrieve the kite with a pole.

[SFX: Branch rustles; a cousin climbs down.]

MIRI: They are down. The kite is still up.

TANA: Leave it until the wind drops. Don't pull hard; that can break the frame.

LIO: Our next caller has a kite that refuses to rise.

JEX: It stays on the ground. I have a strong tail and a long spool.

TANA: What is the wind doing?

JEX: Blowing away from me.

TANA: Run into the wind, not away from it.

JEX: I ran and the kite ran too.

TANA: The line may be too loose. Keep a little tension.

JEX: It pulled me toward the hill.

TANA: Don't let a kite drag you. Use a shorter line and stand in an open place.

LIO: Is the hill open?

JEX: It has one tree at the top.

TANA: That is a tree, not an open place.

[SFX: Wind gust; kite paper flaps.]

MIRI: My kite has come loose. It's flying over the orchard.

TANA: Is the line attached?

MIRI: Yes, but it is wrapped around the branch.

TANA: Keep people clear. Don't yank the line.

LIO: Could a second kite lift it off?

TANA: No. That would tangle two kites.

JEX: My kite could help.

TANA: Please keep your kite on the ground.

JEX: It is trying to rise.

TANA: Hold the spool firmly and walk away from the tree.

JEX: I am walking, but the kite is pulling in another direction.

LIO: The hotline's wind meter shows a gust coming from the west.

TANA: Let the line out slowly. When the gust passes, reel it in with steady tension.

[SFX: Wind builds, then fades. A kite line reels.]

MIRI: The kite is down. One tail ribbon is missing.

TANA: The frame is intact. Replace the ribbon before flying again.

LIO: And Jex?

JEX: My kite is on the ground. I am standing in the open field.

TANA: Good. Wait for a steady breeze.

JEX: It is rising.

[SFX: Kite flutters upward.]

LIO: A kite is in the sky, one is safely recovered, and no one climbed a tree.

MIRI: I will wait until tomorrow before flying again.

TANA: Check the weather, choose a clear field, and keep the line away from branches.

LIO: Updraft Advice ends with both callers on the ground and the kites where they belong.

JEX: The breeze has changed. My kite is pulling toward the hill again.

TANA: Bring it down slowly. The line is drifting toward the fence.

JEX: I have the spool. I am walking backward into the open field.

LIO: The kite comes down without snagging. The callers have chosen the grass field for tomorrow's practice.

MIRI: I will bring a pole to retrieve mine from the tree, not climb up.

TANA: Good. Keep the tree clear until the wind drops.


LIO: Our hotline will keep the line clear and callers safely on the ground.

[SFX: Wind softens; paper kite flutters in a steady breeze.]
