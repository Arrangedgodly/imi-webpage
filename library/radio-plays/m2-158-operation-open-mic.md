# Operation Open Mic

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: detective radio serials, brooding voice-over, suspicious pauses, and clues that are treated as sinister until examined*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
DETECTIVE RUMBLE, a capuchin investigator
MIRA, a ring-tailed lemur sound technician
MR. PEEP, a macaque club owner

[RAIN. SAXOPHONE.]

RUMBLE: The case opened at midnight, when the club's microphone vanished. There was a singer onstage, a crowd in the dark, and a cable leading nowhere. I knew someone had planned this.

MIRA: The microphone is on the chair beside you.

RUMBLE: A decoy. I won't touch it until I understand why it's facing the wall.

MIRA: It's not. You're holding it upside down.

RUMBLE: The first clue is always the one that looks ordinary.

MIRA: It looks ordinary because it is the microphone.

[FOOTSTEPS. SAXOPHONE STOPS.]

PEEP: Detective, we're ready for sound check. The show starts in ten minutes.

RUMBLE: Nobody leaves this room. The missing microphone has disappeared within these walls.

MIRA: We have three microphones. One is in the hand of the detective.

RUMBLE: And two are unaccounted for.

MIRA: One is on the stand. One is in the equipment case.

RUMBLE: That leaves one, then.

PEEP: We need the microphone on the stand for the singer.

RUMBLE: A suspect assigning a microphone. How convenient.

[PHONE RINGS. MIRA ANSWERS.]

MIRA: Sound desk. Yes? The singer's outside? She can't find the door?

RUMBLE: The door is another witness.

MIRA: No, detective. It's the alley entrance. I'll go let her in.

RUMBLE: Don't cross the alley. It's raining, and footprints can be tampered with.

MIRA: I have a coat. I'll be back in thirty seconds.

[DOOR OPENS. RAIN GETS LOUDER. DOOR CLOSES.]

RUMBLE: The sound technician has left. The room is now full of suspects and equipment.

PEEP: There are two of us. I own the club and you're investigating it.

RUMBLE: Precisely. Motive: the singer wanted a louder set. Means: access to the sound booth. Opportunity: the dark.

PEEP: The lights are on.

RUMBLE: The dark is metaphorical.

[METAL CASE CLICKS OPEN.]

RUMBLE: A glint of silver! The microphone has been found inside this case.

PEEP: That's a spare microphone.

RUMBLE: Why does a microphone case contain a microphone?

PEEP: It's the case for microphones.

[RUMBLE SETS DOWN HIS OWN MIC. A THUMP.]

MIRA: I'm back. The singer is at the door. Why is the case open?

RUMBLE: I have discovered the accomplice. The microphone case was hiding a second microphone.

MIRA: It was hiding nothing. It was storing the spare. You put the missing one on the chair.

RUMBLE: Then the criminal took it from the chair and placed it in my hand.

MIRA: You picked it up when you walked in.

PEEP: The singer is waiting.

RUMBLE: I see. The culprit is distraction itself. It disguised itself as a case, a cable, and a chair.

MIRA: The culprit is the detective's loose grip. You set it down and forgot.

[THE SINGER ENTERS.]

SINGER: Can I have the microphone now?

MIRA: Wait, there's a second cable under the chair.

RUMBLE: A new lead! The chair may have been communicating with the microphone.

MIRA: It's the singer's instrument cable. She dropped it when she came in.

SINGER: I need that to plug in my guitar.

RUMBLE: Then the cable is an accomplice, but one acting in the open. The microphone is on the stand; the guitar is in the case; and the chair is innocent.

MIRA: You solved the room by looking at it.

RUMBLE: Observation is the detective's most dangerous tool.

RUMBLE: The case is closed. The microphone is cleared of all charges.

MIRA: Here. This one works.

[SINGER TESTS IT. A CLEAN NOTE RINGS.]

PEEP: The show is saved.

RUMBLE: I'll return to my office and write the report.

MIRA: You're in the club office.

RUMBLE: Then I'll write it right here, where the clues are safe.

[RAIN FADES. SAXOPHONE RETURNS.]

RUMBLE: Case closed. The singer starts at eight. I'll be listening from the dark.

MIRA: Sit near the microphone stand so you can hear.
