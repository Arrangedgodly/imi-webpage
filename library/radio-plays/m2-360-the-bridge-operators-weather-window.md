# The Bridge Operator's Weather Window

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local radio traffic reports that force a complicated crossing into one dramatic headline*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Bridge operator Nima, a lemur; field reporter Soli, a gibbon; studio host Daro, a macaque; cyclist Fenn, a capuchin.

[SFX: RIVER WATER. WIND RATTLES A BARRIER ARM. A BICYCLE FREEWHEEL CLICKS. A PHONE LINK OPENS.]

DARO: We're live at the river bridge. Soli, give us the word.

SOLI: Which word?

DARO: Open or closed. One word fits the traffic sting.

NIMA: The vehicle barrier is down during the wind hold. The separate footpath is open.

DARO: That's twelve words. The sting is only four seconds.

FENN: I've got a bicycle and both statuses seem to be looking at me.

NIMA: The green path signal applies to you walking it across. The barrier applies to the vehicle lane.

SOLI: Vehicle lane closed, footpath open. I'll read both.

[SFX: FOUR-SECOND RADIO STING. SOLI INHALES; THE STING ENDS BEFORE SHE SPEAKS.]

DARO: The pause was excellent. Could the news be shorter?

SOLI: I haven't said the news yet.

DARO: Then we can still optimize it.

FENN: May I start walking?

NIMA: The path light is green. Yes.

[SFX: FENN'S FOOTSTEPS AND BICYCLE WHEELS MOVE ONTO THE PATH. A VEHICLE HORN SOUNDS FROM THE WAITING LANE.]

DARO: There! Movement. The bridge is open.

SOLI: The cyclist is on the footpath. Cars are waiting at the vehicle barrier.

DARO: Closed with movement?

NIMA: Two crossings, two statuses.

DARO: Our headline board has one line.

SOLI: Use a semicolon.

[SFX: STUDIO KEYS TAP. THE TRAFFIC STING PLAYS AGAIN.]

DARO: I found the semicolon. It looks like a tiny fallen cyclist.

FENN: I'm still upright.

[SFX: WHEELS CROSS A METAL JOINT. FENN'S FOOTSTEPS CONTINUE.]

NIMA: The wind hold will release after ten calm seconds. The sensor is counting now.

DARO: Excellent. We have a countdown. Ten, nine, eight...

[SFX: WIND GUST. A TIMER BEEPS AND RESETS.]

NIMA: The gust reset it. There is no guaranteed opening time.

DARO: I have already reached eight.

SOLI: The sensor has returned to zero.

DARO: Could it wait until I finish?

NIMA: It is measuring the weather. It doesn't listen to radio.

FENN: I do. It has made this a very long short walk.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS REACH THE FAR SIDE. THE BICYCLE BELL RINGS ONCE.]

FENN: Across. The path is still green behind me.

DARO: Congratulations to our live correspondent. Can you cross back for the other headline?

FENN: My lunch is on this side.

SOLI: Let him have lunch. We're reporting the crossing, not sending him through a tunnel of captions.

[SFX: WIND EASES. THE SENSOR TIMER TICKS QUIETLY. STUDIO PAPER TEARS.]

DARO: I've split the headline board. Left half says vehicle closed, right half says path open.

SOLI: Read it.

DARO: Vehicle path closed open.

NIMA: Read one half, then the other.

DARO: Our autocue scrolls across both halves.

SOLI: Put the cards one under the other.

[SFX: TAPE PEELS. PAPER CARDS ARE MOVED. THE TIMER GIVES A COMPLETION BEEP.]

NIMA: Ten calm seconds completed. The vehicle arm is lifting now.

[SFX: BARRIER MOTOR. ARM RISES WITH A CLUNK. FIRST CAR CROSSES.]

SOLI: Vehicle lane open. Footpath open.

DARO: Finally. One word.

FENN: Two cards.

DARO: Both can say open. We have achieved complete editorial harmony.

SOLI: You're taping them together, aren't you?

[SFX: TAPE PEELS AGAIN.]

DARO: It saves a headline.

NIMA: It saves no time if they change independently.

DARO: We'll deal with tomorrow's story tomorrow.

FENN: I'm leaving now. My sandwich has been open longer than the vehicle lane.

DARO: Stay where you are. That is a marvelous food headline.

FENN: It will be closed in three bites.

[SFX: FENN RIDES AWAY. DARO PLAYS THE FOUR-SECOND TRAFFIC STING, THEN TRIES TO SAY VEHICLE LANE OPEN; FOOTPATH OPEN BEFORE IT ENDS. HE REACHES FOOTPATH AFTER THE FINAL NOTE.]

SOLI: We could make the sting longer.

DARO: We would have to cover the new sting before the news.

[SFX: SOLI LAUGHS. THE PHONE LINK CLOSES; RIVER WATER AND PASSING CARS REMAIN.]
