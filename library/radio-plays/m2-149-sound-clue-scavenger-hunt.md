# The Sound Clue Scavenger Hunt

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio scavenger-hunt broadcasts, elaborate audio clues, and listeners who follow the sounds to the station's own missing sign*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Neri, a macaque hunt host; Vale, an orangutan clue writer; Timo, a capuchin contestant; and Ressa, a gibbon caller.*

[SFX: Game-show bell, a bicycle bell rings twice.]

NERI: Welcome to the Sound Clue Hunt. Listeners solve each clue from a sound, then call in with the location. Our first clue: a bell rings beside a path that never moves.

TIMO: A bicycle at a tree.

VALE: Close. A bicycle bell at a signpost.

RESSA: The signpost marks the route to the orchard.

NERI: Correct. Timo wins one point. Clue two: a soft flap, a tap, then a hollow knock.

TIMO: A bird at a wooden door.

VALE: A flag flapping, a twig tapping, and a hollow branch.

RESSA: That is the old meeting tree.

NERI: Correct. The second clue led to a tree, although the sound effect was made with a curtain and a spoon.

VALE: The recording was clean.

NERI: Our next clue is live: a short scrape followed by two clicks.

[SFX: Scrape, click, click.]

TIMO: A cart wheel passing a gate.

VALE: The cart wheel squeaks. This was a latch closing.

RESSA: I am at the station shed. There is a latch here.

NERI: Correct. You have one point.

TIMO: She heard it at the shed. How is that fair?

VALE: The contestants may be anywhere in town.

NERI: Our final clue: three soft taps and the sound of a page turning.

[SFX: Three taps, page turns.]

TIMO: A reader at the library desk.

RESSA: Or a woodpecker tapping on a book.

VALE: The clue is a page turning after the taps.

NERI: Ressa, where are you?

RESSA: At the library. I can see the station's missing sign on the floor.

NERI: That's not one of the listed locations.

TIMO: We were supposed to find the sign?

VALE: The first clue described its post. It fell during last night's wind.

NERI: That was not in the instructions.

VALE: It is the bonus clue.

RESSA: The sign reads "Sound Clue Hunt." It is underneath the return-books table.

NERI: We have found the missing sign. I am walking to the library to pick it up.

TIMO: Does that count as a clue?

VALE: The sound of the host's footsteps may be used in next week's hunt.

[SFX: Footsteps cross studio, then pause.]

NERI: I am at the library. The sign is here. The clue was not sound; it was the sign itself.

RESSA: We can mount it back at the station.

TIMO: I won two points. What does the winner receive?

NERI: The sign, once installed.

TIMO: I will help carry it.

VALE: Then the clue leads to its own reward.

[SFX: Sign lifted, wood taps against door frame.]

NERI: We have hung the sign. Listeners who heard the hunt can find us by following the sound of the station bell.

RESSA: The bell rings by a path that does not move.

VALE: That is exactly the first clue.

TIMO: And the missing sign is back where it belongs.

NERI: This has been the Sound Clue Hunt. Two winners, one recovered sign, and one clue writer who concealed the bonus in the instructions.

[SFX: Bell rings again, but this time no bicycle passes.]

NERI: The sound effect has returned. Is it the bell at the station?

VALE: No. It is a bicycle bell from the street, from another contestant.

TIMO: Does that create a new clue?

VALE: Only if we record it and give it a different location.

RESSA: The sign is back on the wall. The bell is beside the path. The hunt is finished.

NERI: We will take the live bell out of next week's sound list. It belongs to the street.

[SFX: Bicycle bell rings; broadcast ends.]
