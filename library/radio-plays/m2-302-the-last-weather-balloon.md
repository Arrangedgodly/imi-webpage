# The Last Weather Balloon

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: suspense radio expeditions, where a weather balloon's squeaking basket is mistaken for a signal from the storm*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Field host Luma, lemur; pilot Odo, baboon; meteorologist Nara, macaque; engineer Piri, capuchin.

[SFX: WIND, BALLOON ROPE STRAINING.] 

LUMA: We are above the meadow. The balloon is steady.

ODO: The basket has started squeaking.

NARA: The wind is changing direction.

PIRI: The balloon rope is rubbing against the rail.

LUMA: A coded message?

NARA: A rope.

PIRI: We should not let the host name every sound.

LUMA: I have named one sound.

A squeak repeats, followed by a tap. Odo asks if the balloon is losing lift. Nara checks the gauge; the altitude is stable.

ODO: We have a noise and no explanation.

NARA: We have a rope and a rail.

Piri moves the rope away from the rail. The squeak stops. Luma says the signal has gone quiet.

LUMA: It may have heard us.

NARA: It was no longer rubbing.

They continue the weather reading. Piri reports the wind speed, while Nara records cloud cover. Odo says the balloon is drifting east, toward the ridge. Luma asks if they can steer it back.

ODO: We can change the tether angle.

PIRI: We cannot steer a weather balloon like a boat.

LUMA: Then we have lost control.

NARA: We are measuring drift, not navigating.

A gust shakes the basket. The rail taps twice. Luma hears the earlier rhythm again.

LUMA: The message has returned.

PIRI: The rope is clear.

NARA: The rail is loose.

Odo checks the fastener. It has backed out slightly. He tightens it, then the basket creaks when the balloon shifts.

ODO: We have a second sound.

LUMA: Is it also a message?

NARA: It is the basket flexing.

Piri asks Odo to keep the balloon steady while she braces the rail with a cloth wrap. They hear only wind for several seconds.

[SFX: WIND ONLY.]

LUMA: The silence is unusual.

NARA: The wind has eased.

Odo checks the gauge. The balloon is descending slowly as the air cools. They need to return before the tether reaches its limit.

PIRI: The readings are complete.

LUMA: One more pass over the ridge.

NARA: No. We have enough data.

Odo begins the descent. The rope squeaks once more, then falls quiet as the basket drops below the rail.

[SFX: ROPE SQUEAK, WIND FADES.]

LUMA: The balloon is coming down.

PIRI: The balloon is landing.

NARA: The observations are saved.

They reach the meadow safely. Odo sets the basket down; the rail taps one last time.

[SFX: BASKET SETTLES, SINGLE TAP.]

LUMA: A final transmission.

PIRI: A basket on grass.

NARA: File the report under weather.

On the ground, Nara reads the final observations into the recorder. The temperature dropped two degrees, wind moved east, and cloud cover thickened over the ridge.

NARA: The storm is farther north than expected.

ODO: We should have turned sooner.

LUMA: We have a complete report.

PIRI: We have one loose rail to repair on the ground.

Odo asks Piri to check the tether before anyone leaves. She pulls the line through her hands and finds a frayed section near the basket.

PIRI: This needs replacing before another flight.

LUMA: The balloon is safe now.

PIRI: It is safely on the ground. The line is not ready for another trip.

Odo coils the good section separately from the damaged part. Nara tags the rail for repair. The host asks if they can launch again tomorrow.

ODO: Only after the equipment passes inspection.

PIRI: And only if the weather allows it.

NARA: The forecast is clear tomorrow. The line still needs replacing.

LUMA: The balloon does not take requests.

[SFX: WIND IN MEADOW, END THEME.]
