# Debate on the Branch

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Live political radio debates and moderator timekeeping*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Moderator Sella, a bonobo; candidate Brak, a baboon incumbent; candidate Yori, a macaque challenger; audience caller Pru, a gibbon fruit seller.

[INTRO: CAMPAIGN JINGLE PLAYS, THEN CUTS OFF MID-NOTE.] 

MODERATOR SELLA: This is Branchside Debate Night. Our candidates are running to lead the Canopy Council. Each gets one minute for an opening statement. The microphone will dim when time expires. We tested it on the mayor. It works.

BRAK: My neighbors, I have kept this branch safe for every monkey. We have built two new platforms, repaired the east swing, and made the fruit market easier to reach.

YORI: I am running because the platforms are crowded and the east swing is too high for young climbers. We need a lower crossing and a quiet path for elders who do not want to leap over traffic.

BRAK: The lower crossing is already in my plan.

YORI: It is a picture in your plan.

BRAK: Pictures are how plans begin.

SELLA: One minute each. Candidate Brak has used his time to discuss three platforms and a picture. Candidate Yori has used hers to describe a route. First question: how will you make the market accessible?

BRAK: We will build a second high branch.

YORI: That does not help anyone who cannot jump.

BRAK: We can provide jumping lessons.

SELLA: For the audience, the microphone will dim again if you answer with a slogan.

BRAK: I support safe access for all.

[MICROPHONE FADES OUT.] 

YORI: I would ask fruit sellers where customers get stuck. Then we can place a ramp and rope bridge where the ground is firm.

BRAK, FAINTLY: I support that too.

SELLA: The microphone is off, Candidate Brak.

BRAK: I was agreeing quietly.

SELLA: The next question concerns the old nest grove. Brak, you approved a night festival there. Yori says the festival lights disrupt the fruit bats.

BRAK: It brings visitors and food sales. We can dim the lights after the first hour.

YORI: The bats leave at dusk. The first hour is the one that matters.

BRAK: Then we begin later.

YORI: The later hour is still dusk.

SELLA: A listener question. Pru from the market, you are on the air.

PRU: I sell fruit below the grove. Visitors keep leaving paper lanterns on my awning. What will either candidate do about the litter?

BRAK: We will form a working group.

YORI: We can move the festival to the stone terrace, use lanterns that hang from poles, and pay vendors to collect any left behind.

PRU: The terrace is far from my stall.

YORI: We can put the stalls along the path and keep the grove dark.

BRAK: That could work. I was trying to avoid changing the festival site after permits were issued.

SELLA: The permit is for the terrace. The posters show the grove.

BRAK: That was a printing error.

YORI: The posters are the size of a young tree.

BRAK: The printer had a deal.

SELLA: Both candidates now agree to use the terrace, remove the lights from the grove, and place stalls along the path. What remains undecided is who will lead the council. The vote is tomorrow.

BRAK: I will update my plan before morning.

YORI: I'll visit the market with Pru and ask where the path needs widening.

SELLA: This debate has not chosen your representative for you. It has shown two candidates changing a plan when a neighbor points out who pays for it.

[JINGLE RETURNS, THIS TIME PLAYING THE WHOLE NOTE.] 

SELLA: Branchside Debate Night ends here. Remember to vote tomorrow. The microphone will remain on for one minute after the sign-off, so candidates can no longer claim they were misheard.

BRAK: I support the fruit stalls.

YORI: The fruit stalls support themselves.

SELLA: And both candidates are still on the air.

[SFX: BRAK WHISPERS "I SUPPORT THE RAMP." HIS MIC IS STILL ON. FADE OUT.]
