# The Lanterns Have Opinions

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live call-in public radio and manufactured debate*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: HOST MARA, a macaque; ENGINEER PIP, a tamarin; CALLER BRAM, a mandrill; CALLER TESS, a squirrel monkey; and the callers on the line.

[SFX: RADIO THEME, TWO QUICK BELLS, THEN A THIRD BELL THAT WOBBLES.]

MARA: This is Branch Talk, where the community decides what matters. Tonight's question: should the village lanterns face inward or outward? Our board says the issue is divisive.

PIP: The board says that because you wrote DIVISIVE in large letters.

MARA: We do not influence debate. We frame it attractively. Bram is on line one.

BRAM: Lanterns should face outward. Visitors need to see the path.

MARA: Tess is on line two.

TESS: They should face inward. Nestlings need to see their way home.

MARA: Strong disagreement. Bram, what do you say to Tess?

BRAM: I say the path is outside the nest.

MARA: Tess, your response?

TESS: I say the nest is inside the path if you walk in circles.

PIP: That is true of this studio.

MARA: We have already received seven texts saying this is the most important lamp conversation in village history.

PIP: All seven are from the same number.

MARA: One very engaged listener. Let us bring in the lantern keeper.

[SFX: LINE BUTTON, A SMALL CLINK.]

KEEPER (PIP): I am not the keeper. I am holding a spoon near the microphone.

MARA: Can the spoon clarify the stakes?

PIP: It reflects light inward, but there is nothing to see inside it except my worried face.

BRAM: Put it outside the studio.

TESS: Then it will reflect the path.

MARA: The callers appear to agree on the spoon.

BRAM: I do not agree. A spoon is not a lantern.

MARA: Now the disagreement has a new front.

PIP: I have a practical update. The lanterns are mounted on swivels. We can turn them any direction.

MARA: That is a solution, not a debate.

TESS: We could angle each one toward the path at dusk and toward the nests after the last visitor leaves.

BRAM: That takes two trips.

PIP: You are the lantern keeper. Your job is literally to visit the lanterns.

BRAM: I have a very broad job description.

MARA: We have time for one last caller. Please settle the matter without settling it.

[SFX: LINE BUTTON.] 

CALLER: I cannot see the lantern from where I live. There's a fig leaf covering it.

TESS: Is the leaf on the path side?

CALLER: It is on both sides. It is a large leaf.

PIP: The lantern is working. The tree has joined the discussion.

MARA: We are going live to the square. Bram, will you rotate the lanterns?

[SFX: DOOR OPENS, NIGHT CRICKETS, FOOTSTEPS.]

BRAM: This one points outward. Now inward. Now the wind turned it halfway.

TESS: That is the useful setting.

MARA: We have reached a consensus no caller requested. The lanterns will point where the wind leaves them, and Bram will clear the leaf.

BRAM: The leaf is now on the lantern.

PIP: It is glowing.

MARA: A compromise! Branch Talk, where every side gets a little light and the tree gets the microphone.


MARA: The producer says our caller board is blinking again.

PIP: That is because the bell is wired to the caller board.

BRAM: Can a lantern have a caller slot?

MARA: Only if the lantern has an opinion.

TESS: Mine says the square is too dark near the seed table.

BRAM: That's not a direction. That's a location.

TESS: The lantern points where the seed table is.

MARA: We have discovered a third position in this argument: where people actually walk.

PIP: I can take the spoon outside and see which direction catches the light.

MARA: Can you do that without bringing a studio utensil into community planning?

PIP: The spoon is already on the line. It has more airtime than either caller.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. WIND BLOWS THROUGH THE MICROPHONE.]

PIP: The spoon reflects the lantern into the path, then into the windows, then into my eyes.

BRAM: Turn the lantern down a little.

TESS: Now turn the shade toward the nest.

MARA: The callers are coordinating. This is an unexpected development.

PIP: I have set the shade. The spoon is back inside.

MARA: And the lantern?

PIP: It points at the path without shining through the windows. The leaf is still there.

BRAM: I'll clear the leaf after the broadcast.

TESS: Clear the branch too. It is touching the glass.

MARA: We have a follow-up issue already.

PIP: We can schedule it for tomorrow, same time, same lantern.

MARA: Or we can leave it to the person holding the broom.

BRAM: That would be me.

MARA: Then you're our first call-in guest for tomorrow's leaf report.
[SFX: RADIO THEME. THE WOBBLY BELL RINGS ONCE, THEN A LEAF RUSTLES AGAINST IT.]
