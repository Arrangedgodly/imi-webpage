# The Bakers' Guild Holds a Flour-Free Bake-Off

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: baking competitions that replace the recipe with a rule designed for television*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Seli, a lemur; judge Vex, an orangutan; baker Mara, a macaque; baker Pip, a capuchin; guild clerk Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Kitchen bell. Bowls set down. Paper recipe cards rustle.

SELI: Welcome to the Flour-Free Bake-Off. Today's challenge is to make a loaf without flour.

MARA: What should we use?

VEX: The rule says no flour. It doesn't say what replaces it.

PIP: I brought mashed plantain and ground nuts.

ODO: The guild form says bread must contain a grain.

MARA: Then the challenge needs a grain.

VEX: We can add one at the twist.

SELI: The twist is that the clock has already started.

SFX: Timer clicks on. A loud ticking begins.

PIP: We have twelve minutes.

ODO: I can read the permitted grains.

VEX: Not during the challenge. The contestants must show their instincts.

MARA: I don't bake by instinct. I measure.

SFX: Spoon scoops. Bowl clinks.

MARA: Plantain, ground nuts, one egg, and a pinch of salt.

PIP: I'm making a steamed loaf with millet.

VEX: Millet counts as flour if you grind it.

PIP: It is whole.

ODO: The guild chart lists millet as a grain, whole or ground.

VEX: We need a surprise.

SELI: We have a surprise. There is no flour.

VEX: Then we need another.

SFX: Timer tick grows louder.

PIP: My batter is too loose.

MARA: Add the oats you measured. They will soak up some moisture.

VEX: You are helping your rival.

MARA: I am talking over a table.

PIP: The oats were in my bowl.

ODO: The clock has eight minutes.

SELI: Where is the oven?

VEX: It is in the next room. Contestants may not leave the kitchen.

MARA: Then how do we bake?

VEX: Bake-off is a metaphor today.

PIP: My loaf still needs heat.

SFX: Small steamer lid lifts. A soft burst of steam.

PIP: I brought a covered pot. It can steam.

VEX: That is not an oven.

MARA: The rules did not require an oven.

ODO: They required a finished bread item.

SELI: A steamed loaf is a bread item.

VEX: The judges need a golden crust.

PIP: The plantain loaf has no crust.

MARA: We can toast the outside after it sets.

SFX: Timer ticks. Bowl scrapes. Pot settles on a burner.

ODO: Five minutes.

VEX: How are you both calm?

MARA: Because the pot is covered.

PIP: The steam is escaping from the lid.

SFX: Lid rattles. A drip hisses on the burner.

MARA: Turn it down. The water is bubbling too hard.

PIP: My loaf is holding its shape.

ODO: Three minutes.

SELI: Judge Vex, what are you scoring?

VEX: Height, crust, and narrative.

MARA: The challenge never mentioned narrative.

VEX: The loaf must have a journey.

PIP: Mine began as a bowl and is now a pot.

ODO: The timer rings.

SFX: Timer bell. Pot removed. Two wooden boards set down.

MARA: Let it cool before slicing. If we cut now, it will collapse.

VEX: The show cannot wait.

SELI: It can wait a minute. Odo, mark the scorecard.

SFX: A knife taps the loaf. The crust crackles on Mara's toasted plantain loaf. Pip's steamer releases a soft puff.

PIP: Mine is soft, but it stands.

MARA: We followed different methods.

VEX: Which one wins?

SELI: Mara's loaf holds a slice and tastes like roasted plantain. Pip's is tender and the whole millet is cooked. The winner is Mara, for a good loaf made under a bad rule.

MARA: Can Pip have the runner-up ribbon?

VEX: There is only one ribbon.

PIP: Give it to the oven.

ODO: The oven was in the next room.

SELI: Next week, a cake without cake.

MARA: I will bring a bowl.
