# Kettle on the Air

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio cooking demonstrations*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Fara, a macaque; chef Leni, a patient capuchin; caller Jojo, a nervous young baboon; Aunt Palla, a gibbon; and a kettle that whistles at inconvenient moments.

[SFX: KETTLE STARTS TO HEAT. SPOON STIRS A BOWL.] 

FARA: Welcome to Kettle on the Air, the cooking show you can follow without seeing our counter. Chef Leni, what are we making?

LENI: A warm bean and squash stew. It uses one pot, a cutting board, and ingredients you can swap if you have allergies or different vegetables.

FARA: I have a squash, two onions, and beans in a jar. How do I open it?

LENI: With a jar opener, if you have one. If you don't, ask someone to help. Don't tap glass against the counter.

[SFX: A DRAWER OPENS, UTENSILS CLINK.] 

FARA: We have a grippy mat.

LENI: Put it beneath the jar. Turn the lid with a towel. There. Now drain and rinse the beans.

[SFX: TAP RUNS, WATER DRAINS.] 

FARA: Caller Jojo is making this for the first time. Jojo, what is in your kitchen?

JOJO, PHONE: I have lentils, carrots, and a sweet potato. I don't have squash.

LENI: Those will work. Lentils need more liquid and longer cooking than canned beans. Put the lentils in first with the water, then add the sweet potato and carrots. We will talk about the timing.

JOJO: Do I need to peel the carrot?

LENI: If it's clean and the skin is thin, you can scrub it. Cut away any damaged parts. Use a stable board and keep your fingers curled away from the blade.

FARA: I have cut the onion. My eyes are running.

LENI: Step back from the board. It will still be there when you can see.

[SFX: KETTLE WHISTLES, VERY LOUD.] 

FARA: The kettle is ready.

LENI: It is not part of the stew. Move it off the heat. We need water, but not boiling water poured onto a hot pan.

FARA: The kettle has opinions.

LENI: It has steam.

[SFX: KETTLE LIFTED, SET ON A MAT.] 

FARA: We are heating a little oil in the pot. How hot?

LENI: Medium. Add the onion and stir until it softens. If it starts to brown fast, lower the heat. Then add the spices. We have cumin and paprika. You can leave either out.

[SFX: ONION SIZZLES. WOODEN SPOON STIRS.] 

JOJO: I have a spice mix from my aunt, but I don't know what's in it.

LENI: Check the label for ingredients that might bother you. If you can't tell, don't add it. A meal is still a meal without mystery powder.

FARA: The onion is soft. I add squash, beans, and water. Jojo adds lentils, carrots, and sweet potato. How much water?

LENI: Enough to cover the lentils and vegetables, then a little more. Start with less. You can add more as it cooks.

[SFX: LIQUID POURS. POT LID SET DOWN.] 

FARA: How long do we wait?

LENI: Bring it to a gentle simmer, then cook until the vegetables are tender. Check the lentils after twenty minutes. The canned beans only need to warm through.

FARA: Radio cannot tell whether food is tender.

LENI: Your spoon can. Taste carefully after it cools. Do not put your whole face over the pot.

[SFX: TIMER BELL.] 

FARA: Our stew is ready. The squash has softened, the beans are warm, and the broth is thick. Jojo says the lentils need another five minutes.

JOJO: The carrots are soft, but the lentils are still firm.

LENI: Add a little water, cover it, and give them time. This is why we describe the ingredients before promising a quick recipe.

AUNT PALLA, PHONE: I made the stew with pumpkin and split peas. My pot is small, so I used half the recipe.

LENI: Excellent. Keep the simmer low and leave room at the top so it does not boil over.

FARA: We serve with bread, or rice, or nothing at all. Thank you for cooking with us. Please turn off the burner before you leave the kitchen.

[SFX: BURNER CLICKS OFF. KETTLE QUIETS.] 

FARA: The kettle is silent.

LENI: It has finished its segment.
