# The Lost Note in the Sound Library

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio archive programs that misfile sound effects and make the archivist perform increasingly elaborate searches*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Archivist Yuma, a macaque; host Seli, a lemur; technician Fenn, a capuchin; caller Olo, a gibbon.

[SFX: ARCHIVE ROOM. DRAWER SLIDES, CASSETTE DECK CLICKS.]

SELI: Our sound library is missing the recording of a spring rainstorm. Yuma says the catalog lists it under "audience."

YUMA: The catalog lists every file with applause as audience. This rain recording has applause.

FENN: I loaded it. It sounds like rain, a thunderclap, and clapping.

[SFX: CASSETTE PLAYS: RAIN, THUNDER, THEN APPLAUSE.]

OLO: I called because I recorded that storm. The applause is my roof drain overflowing onto a metal bucket.

SELI: That is a remarkable performance by the weather.

YUMA: It's a bucket. We need the original reel, not this edit.

FENN: The shelf label says Weather, shelf four.

YUMA: Shelf four has three reels marked "rain." None has the date.

SELI: We could play each one until we find the right storm.

YUMA: The archive has thousands. We need the accession number.

OLO: I wrote it on the paper sleeve. It should start with R-18.

[SFX: DRAWERS OPEN, PAPER SHUFFLES.]

FENN: I found R-18. It says "rain against tin awning, no voices."

SELI: Let's hear it.

[SFX: REEL SPINS. RAIN PATTERS AGAINST METAL. A DOOR SLAMS. THEN A MONKEY LAUGHS.]

YUMA: That's not no voices.

FENN: The next reel is labeled "rain, final final." It has the right date.

SELI: Play that.

[SFX: REEL SPINS. RAIN, THUNDER, A LOUD APPLAUSE TRACK.]

YUMA: This is the edited version. Someone layered audience applause over the storm for a station ident.

SELI: Why would anyone add applause to rain?

FENN: The note says "make the storm sound responsive to the host."

OLO: I asked you to record the storm, not teach it manners.

YUMA: We can recover the original from the edit track if the source reel is still in the transfer cabinet.

[SFX: CABINET DOOR OPENS. REELS CLINK.]

SELI: There are two source reels, both marked R-18.

YUMA: One says "raw" and one says "copy." Read the splice marks before you play them.

FENN: The raw reel has a blue tab at the beginning and one clean splice. The copy has a red tab and an extra leader.

SELI: The blue one, then.

[SFX: BLUE-TABBED REEL SPINS. RAIN, ONE THUNDERCLAP, WATER DRIPPING INTO A BUCKET. NO VOICES.]

OLO: That's the recording. The bucket makes one hollow note after the thunder.

YUMA: I can hear it. That's the sound we needed.

SELI: Can we put it on air?

YUMA: With its proper label: "Roof rain and drain bucket, recorded by Olo, east shed, spring storm."

FENN: The catalog field only allows forty characters.

YUMA: Then abbreviate the location, not the sound.

SELI: "Rain on east shed; bucket after thunder."

OLO: Add my name.

FENN: Done. The file is now in weather, not audience.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. CATALOG BEEPS ONCE.]

SELI: For the broadcast, should I introduce it as applause from the clouds?

YUMA: No.

SELI: Rain with one bucket note?

YUMA: Yes.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.]

SELI: Here is Olo's recording of rain on the east shed roof, followed by one drop into a metal bucket.

[SFX: RAIN PLAYS. A SINGLE HOLLOW DRIP.]

FENN: The label matches the sound.

YUMA: Put the edited reel back in "station effects" and mark its applause track as added later.

SELI: I was going to call it the storm's encore.

OLO: The storm didn't know it was on air.



SELI: The station manager wants a dramatic title for the catalog display.

YUMA: We can make a title dramatic without changing what was recorded.

FENN: The display says "Storm Applause." That was the edit label.

YUMA: Replace it with "Rain and a Bucket After Thunder." The producer can use the old title only for the edited effect.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. A PRINTER STARTS, THEN FEEDS OUT A LABEL.]

SELI: The label is longer than the sound.

OLO: The sound was three minutes. The label can take its time.

FENN: I have attached it to the correct reel, not the edited copy.

YUMA: Now the next archivist can find the storm without playing the applause first.

[SFX: REEL STOPS. DRAWER CLOSES. A SMALL LABEL IS PRESSED FLAT.]
