# The Village Puts Its Own Parade on the Route

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcasts that treat every neighborhood event as a championship*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Commentator Odo, a gibbon; captain Pip, a capuchin; marshal Nari, a lemur; rival Vex, an orangutan; drummer Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Village lane. Drum taps. A small cart rolls over cobbles.

ODO: The annual parade cart race is underway.

NARI: It is a parade, not a race. The carts carry flower pots from the square to the garden.

VEX: My cart has the largest wheel.

PIP: Mine has a loose handle.

MARA: Tighten it before you start.

VEX: The crowd is ready. We need a dramatic launch.

NARI: The route is marked with chalk. Stay between the white lines.

SFX: Two carts roll forward. A wheel squeaks.

ODO: Vex takes the lead.

PIP: The flower pots are leaning.

MARA: Stop at the first corner. I'll straighten them.

VEX: Stopping loses momentum.

NARI: It keeps the pots from falling into the path.

SFX: Cart stops. A clay pot rattles.

PIP: One is cracked.

MARA: That's the display pot. It has been cracked since last year's parade.

VEX: Could the crack be a dramatic injury?

NARI: It's a pot. We can replace it from the supply table.

ODO: The crowd is chanting "keep rolling."

PIP: They are chanting because Mara is beating the drum.

MARA: I'll change the beat. Slow means slow.

SFX: Drum plays a measured rhythm.

VEX: The crowd is counting each wheel turn.

NARI: They can count the flower pots instead.

PIP: I have three upright.

MARA: Vex has four. One cart has more weight, so speed is not a fair measure.

VEX: I could remove a pot.

NARI: The delivery list says every pot goes to the garden.

ODO: We can judge both carts by whether the load arrives safely.

VEX: Then I will keep all four and take the turn carefully.

SFX: Cart wheels slow. Pot rims clink, but none tip.

ODO: The competitors adjust pace.

VEX: This is not how championships sound.

NARI: The race rule is to deliver every pot upright.

VEX: I can still win if I take the straight route.

PIP: The straight route crosses the chalk line and the flower bed.

VEX: The course should have been designed for speed.

MARA: It was designed to end at the garden.

NARI: The chalk line has shifted where the rain crossed it.

ODO: Stop both carts. We should reset the route before another wheel touches the flower bed.

VEX: We are tied and the finish is close.

PIP: The finish is the garden entrance, not the chalk.

MARA: I can mark a wider turn with a spare rope.

SFX: Rope uncoils and lays on the stones.

NARI: The new route gives both carts room to turn without crossing the planting strip.

VEX: That makes the race longer.

ODO: It makes the route usable by the wide cart and the narrow cart.

VEX: My wheel is wide. This course is biased.

PIP: My handle is loose. I am not asking for a shorter race.

MARA: Tighten your handle before we move again.

SFX: Wrench turns. Handle squeaks, then holds.

ODO: Both carts ready?

PIP: Ready.

VEX: Ready, and still in the lead.

NARI: No one is in the lead. This is a delivery route.

SFX: Drum begins at a steady pace. Carts roll around the rope-marked bend.

SFX: Wheels roll over a gentle ramp. Both carts pass the corner carefully.

ODO: Pip arrives first, but Vex's cart has all its pots.

NARI: They both delivered every pot upright.

VEX: Then we tie?

PIP: We can share the garden hose and water them.

MARA: The parade is over when the flowers are planted.

ODO: The crowd has moved from the finish line to the planting bed.

VEX: Is there a trophy?

NARI: The empty cart is the trophy. Take it back to the square.

TAG

SFX: Drum taps as carts roll away.

ODO: Vex is leading the return leg.

NARI: There is no return race.

VEX: Then I'll win quietly.
