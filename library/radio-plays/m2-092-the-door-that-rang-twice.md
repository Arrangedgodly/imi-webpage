# The Door That Rang Twice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: gothic radio mysteries, ominous narrators, and thunder on cue*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: narrator Odo, an orangutan; caretaker Fenn, a gibbon; visitor Sella, a macaque; locksmith Bim, a baboon; messenger Nix, a capuchin.

[SFX: WIND. A DOOR CREAKS. THUNDER RUMBLES.]

ODO: At midnight, the door to Hollow House rang twice.

FENN: That was the doorbell.

SELLA: It sounded like thunder.

BIM: It is a brass bell on a string.

NIX: I rang it once.

FENN: We heard it twice.

NIX: The string bounced.

ODO: The house kept a secret.

BIM: The door is open.

ODO: The secret is also open.

SELLA: I'm here to return a key.

FENN: Which key?

SELLA: The key to the north room.

BIM: There is no north room.

ODO: In Hollow House, every room faces north.

FENN: That is not true.

ODO: The map says so.

BIM: The map is upside down.

[SFX: PAPER UNFURLS. THUNDER.]

SELLA: Why is the map wet?

FENN: The roof leaks.

ODO: The storm has entered the evidence.

NIX: I brought a note.

FENN: From whom?

NIX: The note says, "Do not open the north room."

SELLA: Who wrote it?

NIX: It was folded inside the key.

BIM: Keys do not hold notes.

NIX: This one has a paper tag.

ODO: The note has been concealed for years.

FENN: I tied it there this morning.

ODO: The caretaker reveals a motive.

FENN: I tied the note so no one would lose the key.

SELLA: I was asked to return it.

BIM: The key doesn't fit the front door.

ODO: It must fit the hidden room.

FENN: It fits the cupboard.

[SFX: KEY TURNS. CUPBOARD OPENS. A BROOM FALLS OUT.]

NIX: The north room has a broom.

ODO: The broom has seen things.

BIM: It has seen dust.

SELLA: There is another key in the cupboard.

FENN: That is the spare.

ODO: The mystery deepens.

BIM: The mystery is a cupboard.

[SFX: THUNDER. A METAL CLANG.]

SELLA: Something is in the wall.

FENN: That is the bell rope.

NIX: The rope goes through the wall?

BIM: It goes through a hole in the door frame.

ODO: The house has a passage.

FENN: It has a bell wire.

SELLA: Why did it ring twice?

NIX: The bell bounced on its string.

ODO: The visitor has returned to the beginning.

BIM: We are not at the beginning. We are at the door.

[SFX: BELL RINGS ONCE. THEN A SECOND SMALLER RING.]

SELLA: There it is again.

FENN: The small bell is the kitchen timer.

NIX: The cake is ready.

ODO: A second signal from within.

BIM: The kitchen is beside the door.

SELLA: What cake?

FENN: The one I baked.

ODO: No one leaves until the cake is identified.

BIM: It is a walnut cake.

NIX: I don't eat walnuts.

FENN: There is a fruit cake too.

[SFX: OVEN DOOR OPENS. PAN CLANGS.]

ODO: The night releases its final secret.

SELLA: The cake is warm.

BIM: The front door is still open.

FENN: Close it before the storm comes in.

[SFX: DOOR SHUTS. WIND FADES.]

ODO: And so Hollow House kept one final mystery.

SELLA: Where does the broom go?

FENN: The cupboard.

[SFX: WIND HOWLS THROUGH A CRACK. THE DOOR HANDLE TURNS.]

ODO: The north room calls again.

SELLA: The door is closed.

BIM: The wind is pushing the handle.

FENN: I can wedge it with the broom.

NIX: The broom belongs in the cupboard.

ODO: The caretaker approaches the door with the only tool that can silence it.

FENN: It is a broom.

[SFX: BROOM HANDLE SLIDES UNDER DOOR. WIND STOPS.]

SELLA: The house is quiet.

ODO: Too quiet.

BIM: The storm has passed.

NIX: The cake timer is still ringing.

[SFX: BROOM SWEEPS THREE TIMES.]
