# The Cave Echo Repeats the Host's Slogan

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: adventure broadcasts that mistake an echo for an audience response*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Vex, an orangutan; guide Mara, a macaque; recorder Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin; ranger Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Cave entrance. Water drips. Footsteps crunch on gravel.

VEX: Welcome to the deepest cave tour in the county.

MARA: The marked route ends at the first chamber.

PIP: I can hear my footsteps come back.

VEX: The cave is applauding.

ODO: That's an echo.

VEX: Say the slogan.

PIP: "Stay on the marked path."

SFX: "Stay on the marked path" echoes from the chamber.

VEX: The cave agrees with us.

NARI: The echo repeats any loud sound.

MARA: We should keep our voices low near the bats.

VEX: Can we get the cave to repeat our sponsor message?

ODO: That would disturb the roost.

PIP: The path turns left at the limestone column.

MARA: Right. The lower passage is closed because water is running through it.

VEX: It could be an uncharted route.

NARI: It is marked closed on the map.

SFX: A drop of water splashes in the lower passage.

PIP: That sounds deep.

MARA: We won't enter it. The safe route is behind us.

VEX: The episode needs a cliffhanger.

ODO: The echo can create one. Say "we are leaving."

PIP: We are leaving.

SFX: "We are leaving" echoes twice, fading.

MARA: Stop here. The orange line marks the safe turn.

ODO: I can set the microphone down to capture the echo without calling toward the roost.

SFX: Microphone placed on a padded case. Drip echoes once.

PIP: That sound traveled farther than my voice.

VEX: The cave is answering our equipment.

NARI: It is reflecting the drip from the far wall.

MARA: This chamber is limestone, and the curved ceiling carries sound. We can explain that without inventing a personality.

ODO: I'll ask everyone to stay behind the line while I record three quiet sounds.

SFX: Water drip. Footstep on gravel. Cloth sleeve brushes a jacket.

PIP: The cloth barely echoes.

ODO: The rock gives the sharper sounds a longer return.

VEX: Can we make the host line echo one more time?

MARA: Only if we stay at the entrance. We're not going deeper for a better effect.

SFX: Footsteps turn back toward daylight.

PIP: I can still hear the water.

NARI: The lower passage remains closed. The sound is coming from the chamber beside the marked path.

VEX: The listener can imagine what is beyond.

MARA: The listener can imagine it from a safe distance.

VEX: The cave wants us to go.

MARA: It is our cue to turn around.

PIP: I can take the last recording slate outside with me.

NARI: Keep it dry. The cave air is damp.

ODO: I'll pack the microphone case and close the visitor gate.

TAG

VEX: We should leave the sponsor sign at the cave mouth.

NARI: It covers the route marker.

MARA: Move it to the visitor board, not the path.

SFX: Sign lifts and settles beside the entrance map.

PIP: Now the orange line is visible.

ODO: We'll leave the chamber quiet, as it was when we entered.

MARA: That is the end of the tour.

SFX: Footsteps exit.

MARA: We have recorded the drip, the footstep, and the cloth. That's enough to show how sound changes in the chamber.

ODO: I'll label each track with the place and distance from the wall.

PIP: The footsteps sounded different near the entrance.

NARI: The stone is rougher there, so the echo dies faster.

VEX: Can I say the cave swallowed the sound?

MARA: You can say it was absorbed by the rough wall.

PIP: That's a better mystery. Now I know what to listen for next time.

VEX: The cave has the last word.

NARI: It repeated yours.
