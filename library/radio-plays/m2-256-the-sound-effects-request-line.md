# The Sound Effects Request Line

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night listener request shows, sound-library demonstrations, and hosts who promise effects they cannot reproduce*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST DARA, a gibbon
SOUND LIBRARIAN POK, a macaque
CALLER ELLI, a capuchin
ENGINEER NARA, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: SOFT RADIO THEME. A DESK BUTTON CLICKS.]

DARA: Welcome to The Sound Effects Request Line. Tonight, listeners request a noise and Pok finds it in our library.

POK: I have thousands of recordings, organized by source, date, and whether the microphone was facing the right way.

DARA: Caller Elli, what sound would you like to hear?

ELLI: A door opening in a castle.

POK: I have castle door, wooden, heavy, distant, and "castle door, but the hinge squeals." 

DARA: Play the first one.

[SFX: MASSIVE DOOR GROANS OPEN.]

ELLI: That sounds like a cupboard falling over.

POK: The cupboard was recorded in a castle.

DARA: Our next request?

ELLI: Rain on a leaf.

POK: I have rain on a roof, rain on a tent, and water poured into a metal bowl.

NARA: I can make rain on a leaf now.

[SFX: NARA SPRINKLES WATER OVER A LEAF.]

DARA: That is lovely.

POK: It is also live, which means we cannot replay it.

NARA: I can sprinkle again.

[SFX: WATER POURS, LEAF BENDS, WATER SPLASHES ON THE CONSOLE.]

DARA: Now the console is making the sound of a small lake.

ELLI: I request thunder.

POK: I have distant thunder, mountain thunder, and a recording labeled "thunder, possibly a bin lid." 

DARA: We should play the uncertain one.

[SFX: LOW RUMBLE, THEN METAL CLANG.]

NARA: That was a bin lid.

POK: The label said possibly.

DARA: The storm has developed a metallic edge.

ELLI: Can you make a horse walk across a wooden bridge?

POK: I have hoofbeats and a bridge. They were recorded separately.

DARA: Can you combine them?

POK: Certainly.

[SFX: HOOVES CLIP-CLOP. WOODEN BOARDS CREAK IN ANOTHER RHYTHM.]

ELLI: The horse is walking beside the bridge.

NARA: I can match the rhythms.

[SFX: HOOVES AND BOARDS ALIGN, THEN BOTH STOP.]

POK: That is now a horse that reached the bridge and changed its mind.

DARA: The request line is open for a final effect.

ELLI: A quiet room after the storm.

POK: I have an empty studio from last Tuesday.

DARA: Play it.

[SFX: SILENCE FOR THREE SECONDS.]

NARA: The console is still wet. It is making a tiny bubbling noise.

POK: That is not in the library.

DARA: We have a new recording. Label it "studio after storm, with bubbling console."

POK: Was there a storm in the studio?

DARA: We played thunder.

POK: That is not the same thing.

ELLI: Could you play the cupboard again? I want to hear whether it sounds more like a castle the second time.

[SFX: MASSIVE DOOR GROANS OPEN.]

NARA: The hinge squeal is missing.

POK: That was a different recording.

DARA: Add the squeal.

[SFX: DOOR GROANS, THEN A CHAIR SQUEAKS.]

ELLI: Now it sounds like the castle is sitting down.

POK: I have a chair recorded in a castle.

DARA: No. We have enough castles for one night.

ELLI: Then I request the sound of a request show ending.

POK: I have a jingle, a microphone switch, and a host saying good night.

DARA: Play them in order.

[SFX: JINGLE STARTS, MICROPHONE SWITCH CLICKS TOO SOON.]

DARA: Good night from The Sound Effects Request Line.

POK: The microphone is still on.

NARA: The console is bubbling again.

DARA: Add it to the library.

[SFX: BUBBLING JOINS THE JINGLE.]

ELLI: I will request it next week.

POK: It is already under "weather, indoor." 

DARA: We will leave the category there until the room dries.

[SFX: THEME OUT, BUBBLE POP.]
