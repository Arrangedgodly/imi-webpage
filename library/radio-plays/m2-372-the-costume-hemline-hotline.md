# The Costume Hemline Hotline

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio makeover segments that change performers' costumes on air without checking the stage marks or movement*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Leka, a gibbon; dresser Pani, a capuchin; dancer Rumi, a lemur; stage manager Koro, a macaque.

[SFX: BACKSTAGE. FABRIC RUSTLES. A TAPE MEASURE SNAPS OPEN.]

LEKA: Our listener wants a longer hem before tonight's dance. Pani says she can fix it live on the hotline.

PANI: I need Rumi to stand on the marked floor and show me the step.

RUMI: The hem catches my heel on the turn.

KORO: The stage mark is two feet farther downstage than this tape line.

LEKA: We can pin it short and let her test a turn.

PANI: Not until I check both sides. This costume has an uneven panel.

[SFX: PINS CLICK INTO A CUSHION. FABRIC DRAGS ACROSS FLOOR.]

RUMI: The left side is already higher.

PANI: Because you are standing on a slope. The backstage ramp meets the level stage.

LEKA: The audience is calling in with suggestions. One says cut a fringe.

RUMI: The dance includes a spin. Loose fringe could wrap around my leg.

KORO: Keep the costume off the stage until we move to the level mark.

[SFX: TWO FOOTSTEPS. RUMI STEPS FROM RAMP TO FLAT FLOOR.]

PANI: Now stand naturally. Turn once, then stop.

[SFX: FABRIC SWISHES. SHOE PIVOTS, THEN STOPS.]

RUMI: No catch on the left. The back edge still brushes the heel.

PANI: I can raise the back seam half an inch. I won't cut the front panel.

LEKA: Can we say it is a dramatic redesign?

RUMI: It is a hem adjustment.

KORO: The show card says the dancer will debut a new costume.

PANI: The costume is the same. The hem is changing.

[SFX: HAND SEWING BEGINS. THREAD PULLS THROUGH CLOTH.]

LEKA: We have twenty minutes before places. Can you finish in time?

PANI: I can make a temporary tack, then Rumi can try the full turn. If it catches, we remove the tack.

RUMI: Do not pin it while I am wearing it.

PANI: I am sewing it on the mannequin. It is safer and faster.

[SFX: NEEDLE PULLS FREE. SCISSORS SNIP THREAD.]

LEKA: The caller who suggested fringe wants to hear whether it moves during the spin.

RUMI: It would move, but it would also catch. We have tested the hem with the dance, not with a loose decoration.

PANI: I can attach a removable strip later for a still photo. It stays off the performance costume.

KORO: The stage mark is visible from the wings now. I have put a second strip on the ramp edge.

[SFX: TAPE PRESSES FLAT. A FOOT TESTS THE RAMP-TO-STAGE SEAM.]

RUMI: The edge is easy to feel underfoot. I know where to stop.

PANI: The seam is held. Rumi, try it again on the mark.

[SFX: COSTUME RUSTLES AS RUMI CHANGES OFF MIC. FOOTSTEPS RETURN.]

RUMI: Ready. Slow turn first.

[SFX: ONE CONTROLLED TURN. FABRIC SWISHES, NO CATCH.]

KORO: Mark is clear. Now the dance step.

[SFX: THREE STEPS, TURN, TWO STEPS. FABRIC SWISHES CLEANLY.]

RUMI: It clears my heel. I can move.

LEKA: The callers are asking whether they chose the new hem.

PANI: They suggested options. Rumi tested it, and the costume fits the choreography.

RUMI: Keep the fringe suggestion for a scarf, not this skirt.

KORO: Places in five minutes. I will tape the stage mark where the floor changes level.

[SFX: TAPE PEELS, THEN PRESSES TO FLOOR.]

LEKA: The hemline hotline has one change: half an inch at the back, tested on the actual step.

PANI: I am saving the cut fabric. We did not need a redesign.

[SFX: SCISSORS CLOSE. COSTUME HANGER CLICKS ON RACK.]

RUMI: Now the costume is ready. The floor mark is ready too.

[SFX: STAGE DOOR OPENS. MUSIC STARTS BRIEFLY; DANCER'S FOOTSTEPS LAND ON THE MARK.]
