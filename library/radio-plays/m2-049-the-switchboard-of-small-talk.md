# The Switchboard of Small Talk

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio matchmaking and compatibility call-in shows*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Della, a macaque; Nori, a gibbon caller; Bram, a baboon caller; and producer Sedge, a capuchin who scores answers with colored cards.

[SFX: TELEPHONE SWITCHBOARD LIGHTS CHIME. A SOFT ORGAN STING.] 

DELLA: This is Two Good Questions, the radio hour that introduces people without promising a perfect match. Callers answer ordinary questions, then decide whether they want to speak again. Sedge, put away the colored cards.

SEDGE: Green means compatible.

DELLA: What does purple mean?

SEDGE: Extremely compatible.

DELLA: No colors.

[SFX: LINE CONNECTS.] 

DELLA: Nori, what do you enjoy on a quiet afternoon?

NORI: Repairing kites. I find the weak point before the whole thing tears.

DELLA: Bram?

BRAM: Baking bread. People think nothing is happening while it rises, but the dough is changing.

SEDGE: Green card.

DELLA: Put it down. Do either of you have a question?

NORI: What do you do when a kite breaks in the air?

BRAM: I land it if I can. I don't keep pulling the string.

NORI: That's sensible.

BRAM: What kites do you repair?

NORI: Small ones made from cloth and reeds. I enjoy building them, but not competitions.

BRAM: I entered a bread contest once and spent more time describing my loaf than tasting anyone else's.

DELLA: That sounds exhausting.

BRAM: It was a very dry afternoon.

[SFX: LOW LAUGHTER.] 

DELLA: Would you like to continue talking after the broadcast?

NORI: Maybe. Do you put raisins in bread?

BRAM: Only if someone asks. I don't hide fruit in a loaf.

NORI: Good. My sister hides raisins in everything.

BRAM: My uncle puts olives in muffins.

DELLA: We have discovered shared family concerns.

SEDGE: That's purple.

DELLA: It is a choice. If both callers agree, I can pass your numbers privately. We do not read them on air.

NORI: I agree.

BRAM: So do I.

[SFX: PAPER PASSED, SWITCHBOARD CLICKS.] 

DELLA: Our next callers are Rill and Tuck. Rill wants someone who likes long walks. Tuck says he hates walking.

RILL, PHONE: I like walking, but not in the rain.

TUCK, PHONE: I like walking to the bakery. I don't like hiking uphill.

DELLA: That is more specific than your form.

RILL: How fast do you walk?

TUCK: Slowly enough to read the signs.

RILL: That might work.

SEDGE: Green card?

DELLA: Ask if they want to talk later.

RILL: I would.

TUCK: I would too.

DELLA: Sedge will pass your details privately. If either changes their mind, no one owes the show an explanation.

SEDGE: Can I still use the cards?

DELLA: Use them for the mail.

[SFX: CARDS SHUFFLED, ENVELOPES SORTED.] 

DELLA: Compatibility is not a color on a card. We make introductions; callers choose what happens next. Nori will call after she finishes a kite. Bram will save her a loaf without raisins.

NORI: I will bring a kite string.

BRAM: I will bring a knife to slice the bread.

DELLA: You can decide about the bread later.

[SFX: ORGAN PLAYS A SINGLE NOTE, THEN FADES.] 


[SFX: SWITCHBOARD LIGHTS CHIME AGAIN.] 

DELLA: We have a note from Nori and Bram. They met at the bakery after the show.
SEDGE: Green card?
DELLA: No card. What happened?
NORI, PHONE: We talked about kite repairs. Bram brought bread, and I brought a small kite that needed a new tail.
BRAM, SECOND LINE: I did not bring raisins.
NORI: He checked the label.
DELLA: And do you want to speak again?
NORI: Yes. We are planning to walk to the riverside on Saturday.
BRAM: The flat path, not the hill.
SEDGE: Purple card?
DELLA: Put it away. A good first meeting is not a guarantee. It is a conversation the callers choose to continue.
NORI: I will bring string.
BRAM: I'll bring a knife to slice the loaf.
DELLA: Keep it wrapped until you need it.
BRAM: Sensible.
DELLA: That answer may be compatible with a picnic.
[SFX: ORGAN BEGINS A ROMANTIC SWELL, THEN STOPS WHEN THE CALLERS LAUGH.]
