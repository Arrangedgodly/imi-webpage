# The Last Voice in the Cave

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio-theatre sound-effects workshops, cavernous voice-over, and producers who keep adding echoes until actors cannot finish a line*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
DIRECTOR RASK, a gibbon
ACTOR MIRI, a capuchin
FOLEY ARTIST TUM, a macaque
ENGINEER OBI, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: STUDIO DOOR CLOSES. THREE LAYERS OF ECHO.]

RASK: Welcome to the final recording of The Last Voice in the Cave. We need one clean line from our hero: "I have found the way out."

MIRI: I have found the way out.

[SFX: ECHO: OUT, OUT, OUT, OUT.]

RASK: Too many outs. Obi, reduce the echo.

OBI: Which one?

RASK: All of them.

OBI: They are all on separate tracks.

TUM: I can cover the microphone with this stone.

MIRI: That is a foam rock.

TUM: It has a convincing weight.

[SFX: FOAM ROCK DROPS WITH A HOLLOW THUMP.]

RASK: Excellent. The cave has a floor.

MIRI: I was supposed to be alone in this scene.

TUM: I am in the foley booth. I am alone in a different room.

RASK: We will record the hero's footsteps. Tum, use gravel.

[SFX: GRAVEL SCRAPES, THEN CRUNCHES.]

MIRI: Those are loud footsteps.

TUM: The hero is carrying a bucket of gravel.

RASK: The hero is not carrying a bucket.

TUM: Then the cave floor is unusually generous.

OBI: We have a sound problem. The echo is still returning the word "out" from take one.

RASK: Use it as a ghost.

MIRI: The script has no ghost.

RASK: It does now. The hero hears an echo and wonders if the cave remembers everyone who got lost.

MIRI: Does the hero still find the exit?

RASK: We can decide after the atmosphere.

[SFX: WIND THROUGH A TUBE. DISTANT DRIP.]

TUM: I made the wind with a cardboard tube.

MIRI: It sounds like someone blowing through a tube.

TUM: That is how the wind was made.

RASK: We will layer a low rumble.

[SFX: RUMBLE GROWS UNTIL THE TABLE VIBRATES.]

OBI: That is the ventilation fan.

RASK: It has excellent bass. Keep it.

MIRI: I cannot hear my cue.

RASK: The cave is swallowing your cue. Speak when the rumble pauses.

[SFX: RUMBLE STOPS.]

MIRI: I have found...

[SFX: RUMBLE RETURNS.]

RASK: The cave interrupted her. That is suspense.

MIRI: The fan interrupted me.

OBI: I can lower it, but then the room gets hot.

TUM: We can put the foam rock in front of it.

[SFX: FAN MUFFLES. A PIECE OF PAPER FLUTTERS AWAY.]

OBI: Now the script is under the desk.

MIRI: I can improvise the ending. "I have found the way out, but it is under the desk."

RASK: Too practical. Add dread.

MIRI: "I have found the way out. It is under the desk, and the desk has been here longer than I have."

TUM: I can make the desk breathe.

[SFX: DESK CREAKS.]

RASK: Terrifying. We have our final line.

OBI: We still have to record the echo. The track is looping the old take.

RASK: Let it answer her.

MIRI: I have found the way out.

[SFX: ECHO: OUT. A SINGLE ECHO RETURNS: "WHERE?"]

MIRI: That is new.

OBI: I accidentally recorded Tum asking where the cable went.

TUM: I found it. It was under the foam rock.

RASK: Keep the question. The cave can challenge the hero.

MIRI: Then she answers: "Through the door marked EXIT."

RASK: Too easy.

MIRI: The door is marked EXIT because we are in a studio. The hero still has to open it.

[SFX: STUDIO DOOR HANDLE TURNS. IT STICKS.]

TUM: The door is jammed.

RASK: Perfect. That is our ending. The hero finds an exit that will not open.

OBI: I can fix the handle.

RASK: Don't. We have a cliffhanger.

MIRI: This was meant to be the final episode.

RASK: Then the audience can imagine the door opening.

MIRI: Or imagine us staying in here forever, recording the same line.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS WITH A CLICK.]

OBI: I lifted the latch.

RASK: That ruins the cliffhanger.

MIRI: It resolves the story.

TUM: The hero is out. The director is still in the cave.

RASK: I am not in a cave.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES. THE ECHO RETURNS: "CAVE."]

OBI: The recording is done. I have muted the echo track.

RASK: Keep one echo.

MIRI: Which one?

RASK: The one that says we are finished.

[SFX: SINGLE ECHO: FINISHED. STUDIO LIGHT CLICKS OFF.]
