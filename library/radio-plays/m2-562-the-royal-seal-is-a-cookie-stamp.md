# The Royal Seal Is a Cookie Stamp

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: historical reenactment broadcasts that confuse a kitchen tool with state authority*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Presenter Vex, an orangutan; baker Mara, a macaque; historian Odo, a gibbon; helper Pip, a capuchin; caller and fair visitor Nari, a lemur; gallery guide (nonspeaking).

SFX: Heritage kitchen. Dough rolls; stamp's wooden handle taps the table.

VEX: Our royal proclamation has been stamped into a cookie.

MARA: This is a reproduction cookie stamp made for the history fair. It is not the royal seal.

ODO: The original seal is in a locked display upstairs. The stamp's pattern copies the crown from a public coin.

PIP: The dough is chilled. We can press one sample, then bake the tray.

VEX: A pastry has claimed the throne.

MARA: The pastry will be eaten after baking. Pip, keep the sample tray level.

NARI, on phone: I heard the cookie is an official royal document. Can I use it to settle a parking dispute?

ODO: No. It is a demonstration recipe. The pattern is decorative, and the cookie has no legal authority.

VEX: Our listeners deserve a dramatic reading of the proclamation.

MARA: We can read the recipe card and explain how seals marked letters.

ODO: Historically, wax seals helped show that a message had been closed. This metal stamp is a modern kitchen tool made for the fair.

PIP: One sample is ready to press. I will use the stamp after dusting it with flour.

SFX: Flour shaker; stamp presses gently.

MARA: The crown pattern is clear. Don't press too hard or the dough will stick inside the small points.

VEX: The royal portrait has appeared.

PIP: It is a crown, not a portrait. The stamp is on the table, not touching any archival object.

NARI: Can I take a cookie home?

MARA: After they're baked and cooled. The demonstration batch is for visitors.

ODO: I will put a label beside the tray: "Replica pattern; not an official seal."

VEX: The crown gives me authority over this fair. Pip, carry the tray upstairs to the royal display. We will present it beside the original.

SFX: Tray lifts; quick footsteps; a rope barrier rattles.

PIP: I am at the gallery rope. The guide is asking whether Vex has authority to bring in the tray.

MARA: Vex has no office here. Turn around and bring the tray back to the kitchen.

VEX: I am invoking the crown.

ODO: The pattern came from a public coin; the gallery guide has the keys.

SFX: Pip carries the tray down the stairs and settles it on the kitchen counter; the gallery door remains closed.

PIP: Tray is back beside the oven. Nothing crossed into the gallery.

SFX: A clean scrap of dough lands on the board.

VEX: For the record, I will stamp my own hand as royal authority.

MARA: The stamp belongs on dough, Presenter. Press this scrap.

SFX: Vex presses the stamp into the scrap; dough sticks. Pip eases it free, leaving a lopsided crown.

PIP: The imprint is crooked, but the dough is still usable.

NARI: Is the crooked crown a royal pardon?

ODO: It is a practice mark. The parking ticket remains with the clerk.

VEX: The court rules in favor of the cookie.

MARA: No court. We can bake the sample now.

MARA: When the tray is baked and cool, volunteers can take a sample at the fair kitchen counter.

SFX: Baking tray slides into oven; door closes. Oven timer starts.

PIP: Twelve minutes on the oven timer. The stamp is boxed.

VEX: The royal court will reconvene at dessert.

MARA: The fair kitchen will be open after the cookies cool.

SFX: Twelve minutes pass; oven timer rings. Mitts lift the tray onto a cooling rack.

MARA: Now leave the tray to cool. Nari, end your call and join us at the fair counter.

NARI: I am in the lobby. See you shortly.

SFX: Phone disconnects. After a cooling interval, footsteps enter the kitchen; Nari picks up a serving plate.

PIP: The cookies have cooled. Nari, there is your crooked crown.

NARI: I will take one sample.

SFX: Cookie breaks with a crisp snap.

NARI: Cinnamon and butter. No royal decree.

VEX: The taste has been officially noted.

MARA: By a hungry visitor, not a court.

ODO: The fair can serve the tray now.

TAG

NARI: Is the cookie's signature valid?

ODO: Only if you're hungry.
