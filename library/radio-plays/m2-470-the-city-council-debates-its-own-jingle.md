# The City Council Debates Its Own Jingle

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public campaigns that make a short slogan pass through a full committee process*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Chair Odo, a gibbon; councilor Mara, a macaque; councilor Vex, an orangutan; singer Pip, a capuchin; clerk Nari, a lemur; caller Tavi, a gibbon.

SFX: Council chamber. A table microphone clicks on.

ODO: The agenda item is the city jingle. The current version is four notes and a word.

PIP: It is "Use the path."

MARA: That is clear.

VEX: It does not mention the council.

NARI: The jingle is for pedestrians.

VEX: The council funds the path.

MARA: The music budget pays for four notes, not a council announcement.

VEX: The purchase order allows nine words.

NARI: The rest of that order pays for the crossing signal.

PIP: Nine words won't fit on the tune.

ODO: We should not confuse the two contracts.

MARA: A jingle is not a plaque.

ODO: We will hear the public version.

SFX: Pip sings four notes and says, "Use the path."

VEX: It needs the word "together."

PIP: That adds three syllables.

MARA: The path is already for everyone.

VEX: The council should be heard.

NARI: The microphone is on.

ODO: We will take amendments.

MARA: I move we keep the four notes and the word.

VEX: I amend it to "Use the path together, from the council."

PIP: It no longer fits the tune.

VEX: The tune can change.

SFX: Pip tries a longer melody. He runs out of breath on "council."

MARA: The singer has demonstrated the problem.

ODO: We should not vote before the public has heard both versions.

NARI: There is one caller waiting.

SFX: Telephone rings. Nari opens the line.

TAVI, through phone: I use the path to get to the orchard. Can the song tell people not to leave peel on it?

MARA: That's a practical request.

VEX: We can add "keep the path clean."

PIP: The line needs a second breath.

ODO: We have a three-note tune and two messages.

NARI: We can sing "Use the path. Take peel away."

MARA: That sounds like a warning label.

PIP: I can make it fit if we repeat the first note.

SFX: Pip sings the revised phrase; the last note catches.

VEX: Could we add "with council care"?

MARA: No.

ODO: The caller has requested a rule, not a sponsor tag.

VEX: I am a councilor.

NARI: You can put your name in the meeting minutes.

MARA: Let's test the message outside. Pip, sing it as the chamber door opens.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps. A peel crinkles and drops.

PIP: Use the path. Take peel away.

SFX: A person picks up the peel and drops it in a bin.

ODO: The listener understood.

VEX: The council was not mentioned.

MARA: The path was.

NARI: The public has already acted. We can close the amendment.

MARA: Test it after the crossing signal, not over it.

SFX: Signal beeps twice. A pause. Pip sings his four notes.

PIP: Use the path. Take peel away.

SFX: A bin lid opens. One peel drops inside.

NARI: The song fits after the warning.

VEX: No one heard the council.

MARA: They heard the instruction. That was the job.

ODO: All in favor of the short jingle?

SFX: Three hands tap the table. One cough.

VEX: I abstain because no one thanked the council.

PIP: I will sing your name after the meeting.

VEX: That would be outside the jingle.

MARA: The jingle can be used at the crossing now.

NARI: The clerk has recorded the words and the vote.

ODO: Then the item is adopted.

VEX: May I have the microphone for a closing statement?

MARA: The meeting is over.

SFX: Microphone clicks off. Vex continues talking, muffled.

PIP: The jingle was four notes.

NARI: The meeting was forty minutes.

TAG

SFX: The crossing signal plays Pip's short tune.

VEX, distant: With council care!

ODO: The signal has amended it.
