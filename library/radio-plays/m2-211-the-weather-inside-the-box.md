# The Weather Inside the Box

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio weather bulletin convention, where a traveling forecast team reports on a sealed packing crate with unreliable vents*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: ANNOUNCER / MEI, macaque; FENN, gibbon meteorologist; TAVI, capuchin sound operator; CRATE, effects made by cast; WORKER, off-mic warehouse voice.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC. A soft wooden knock.] 

MEI: Good evening, listeners. We are broadcasting from inside a shipping crate, where conditions are close and the forecast is uncertain.

FENN: The temperature is warm. The air is still. There is a chance of a loose hinge after midnight.

TAVI: That hinge has already moved.

[SFX: A RATTLE.] 

MEI: The crate is drifting east.

FENN: We are on a cart. The cart is being pushed west.

[SFX: WHEELS SQUEAK, THEN STOP.] 

TAVI: The cart has stopped. The crate has not.

MEI: Listeners, stay tuned for our hourly update. First, Fenn will explain how to read a crate's internal pressure.

FENN: You listen for a steady knock. One knock means the crate is being carried. Two knocks mean someone is checking the latch. Three knocks mean Tavi has found the snack tin.

TAVI: I have not found it.

[SFX: TIN RATTLE FROM UNDER THE FLOOR.] 

MEI: The snack tin is below us.

FENN: That is the floor.

MEI: A floor can still be below us.

Tavi tests the vent with a twig. A paper label tears loose and flutters against the wall.

TAVI: Wind from the north.

FENN: It came from the vent.

MEI: The vent is north of the microphone.

FENN: Our listeners deserve a map.

Mei attempts to unfold a map. It catches on the crate's corner. The paper makes a rushing sound.

TAVI: A gust has entered the forecast.

FENN: The map is blocking the vent. Please fold it.

Mei folds the map. Air begins to move. The crate sounds quieter. Tavi taps the wall once, then twice. A return tap comes from outside.

MEI: We have a reply from the neighboring crate.

FENN: Or the warehouse worker.

Mei asks Fenn to chart the crate sounds as if they were wind readings. He marks one knock as still air, the squeak as a cross-current and the tin rattle as a low-pressure system.

FENN: The chart is mostly sound effects.

MEI: That is how weather begins indoors.

Tavi checks the vent again and notices the paper label is wedged in its hinge. He eases it free with the twig. Air slips through the opening. The crate shifts a little as the cart begins moving. Fenn says the pressure is changing. Mei tells listeners to brace for a gentle turn.

[SFX: WHEELS ROLL, CRATE CREAKS.] 

The cast leans to the left, then right. The crate slows. Tavi asks if they are at the destination. Fenn listens to the wheels and says the cart is still moving along a smooth floor. Mei asks the neighboring crate for an update. Two taps answer, then a pause.

TAVI: It has gone quiet.

FENN: It has been left behind.

MEI: Our forecast has outlasted its audience.

They tap a pattern. Three taps answer, then a long scrape. Mei asks if the other crate has a weather warning. Fenn listens.

FENN: The sound is a pallet jack.

[SFX: PALLET JACK APPROACHES, STOPS.] 

TAVI: We are being moved.

MEI: The forecast has changed to relocation.

The crate is lifted. The cast sways and steadies themselves. Fenn asks everyone to crouch so the balance stays low. Mei announces the descent before the wheels bump across a seam.

[SFX: THUMP.] 

TAVI: The crate has crossed a ridge.

FENN: It crossed a floor seam.

Mei hears a latch open. A bright wash of room tone replaces the close wooden sound. The crate flaps lift.

MEI: Fresh air has arrived from outside the crate.

[SFX: THREE PAIRS OF FEET CLIMB OUT AND LAND ON THE FLOOR.] The warehouse worker calls that the box is now empty and can be folded. Mei asks if the forecast can continue from the open floor. Fenn says there is no weather to report now. Tavi retrieves the snack tin from the underside of the pallet.

TAVI: We have confirmed a dry snack outlook.

MEI: And a clear ending.

[SFX: CRATE FOLDS FLAT. RADIO TONE.]
