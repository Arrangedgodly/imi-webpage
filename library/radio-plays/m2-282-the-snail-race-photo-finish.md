# The Snail Race Photo Finish

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio photo finishes, overexcited lap calls, and announcers who cannot tell a finish from a rest*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
ANNOUNCER PIRI, a macaque
TRACK JUDGE NOLL, a lemur
TRAINER OMI, an orangutan
RACER SLOW, a snail
REPORTER KET, a capuchin

[SFX: SMALL CROWD, GRASS RUSTLE.]

PIRI: Welcome to the annual Snail Sprint. The racers are approaching the starting line.

NOLL: They are already on the line.

PIRI: The race begins at the horn.

OMI: We have one snail in each lane.

KET: One has turned around.

OMI: It is inspecting the track.

PIRI: The favorites are Slow, moving in lane one, and Quick, who has not moved yet.

NOLL: Quick is asleep.

[SFX: HORN BLAST.]

PIRI: They are off!

KET: No, they are exactly where they were.

NOLL: Slow has moved half a shell length.

PIRI: A powerful start.

Omi places a lettuce leaf beyond the finish line. The racers lift their eyestalks toward it. Quick wakes and moves one antenna.

PIRI: Quick is making a move.

OMI: It is smelling the leaf.

KET: Slow has crossed the first painted stripe.

PIRI: Lap one complete.

NOLL: There is only one stripe.

PIRI: The track has changed.

The snails move at different speeds. Slow follows the edge of the grass, while Quick takes a short path across a damp patch. The audience cheers each time either snail changes direction.

[SFX: TINY CHEERS.]

PIRI: Quick is gaining.

NOLL: Quick is resting.

KET: Slow has reached the flowerpot.

OMI: The flowerpot is outside the lane.

NOLL: It is a course marker.

OMI: It is my water pot.

Piri asks the judge whether the racers can be moved back to their lanes. Noll says no hands may touch a racer once the horn has sounded.

PIRI: The rules are strict.

KET: The racers are not in a hurry.

The snails reach the leaf. Slow stops beside it. Quick climbs the leaf. Omi says the finish is five centimeters farther. Piri calls for a photo finish.

[SFX: CAMERA SHUTTER.]

NOLL: The photograph shows Quick's shell ahead.

OMI: The racer is behind the leaf.

PIRI: The shell counts as the racer.

NOLL: The rules say the nose crosses the line.

KET: A snail has two eyestalks and a nose.

NOLL: The front of the foot is the official marker.

Quick begins sliding down the leaf. Slow moves across the finish line first, then pauses. The crowd erupts.

PIRI: We have a winner!

NOLL: Slow crossed the line.

OMI: Quick is still on the leaf.

KET: Slow has reached the lettuce.

PIRI: The winner has received the prize.
 
The track judge checks the official line and finds one flower petal across it. The camera photograph shows the petal before Slow's shell, so Omi asks whether the petal counts as part of the racer.

NOLL: The petal is not attached to the snail.

PIRI: It crossed first.

OMI: It fell from the prize leaf.

KET: The prize has entered the race.

Noll removes the petal and resets the finish marker. Slow continues forward, crossing the line again. The crowd cheers louder for the second crossing than the first.

PIRI: We have a replay.

NOLL: We have a correction.

OMI: The official time begins at the horn and ends when the nose crosses.

KET: Then the clock has been running for a long time.

NOLL: It is still running.

Piri keeps commentating while Noll stops the timer and writes down the winning foot-length.


NOLL: The award is for first place, not dinner.

Omi offers a small leaf to the winner and moves the rest away. Quick follows the scent and crosses afterward. Noll records both times; the difference is too small for the stopwatch.

PIRI: A tie?

NOLL: No. Slow won by one foot-length.

KET: Can we review the photo?

NOLL: The camera captured a leaf.

[SFX: SHUTTER CLICKS, CROWD LAUGHS.]

Piri announces Slow as champion. Omi reminds the crowd that the race will begin again next year after the grass dries. The snails continue past the finish toward shade.

PIRI: They are leaving the course.

NOLL: The race is over.

KET: The broadcast is not.

[SFX: CROWD FADES, GRASS RUSTLE.]
