# A Lunch Box Beneath the Path

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: archaeology documentaries that overstate ordinary discoveries*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Lead excavator Mara, a macaque; narrator Vex, an orangutan; recorder Odo, a gibbon; student Pip, a capuchin; site keeper Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Dig site. Brush sweeps dirt. A metal trowel taps.

VEX: A rare artifact has emerged from the earth.

PIP: It is a tin lunch box.

MARA: Don't open it yet. We need to record where it was found.

ODO: The box is shallow, rectangular, and partly buried.

VEX: Its owner may have vanished.

NARI: The lunch room is across the yard.

PIP: I left my lunch box here yesterday when we came to dig.

VEX: An ancient mystery.

MARA: We should still document the find.

SFX: Pencil writes on a field card.

ODO: Depth: four centimeters. Position: beside the north marker.

VEX: The object is sealed.

PIP: It has a latch.

MARA: Let the site keeper check it. We don't know what is inside.

NARI: The box belongs to Pip. I'll ask before opening it.

PIP: You can open it. There should be an apple and a sandwich.

SFX: Latch clicks. Paper wrapper rustles.

VEX: The contents reveal a ritual meal.

PIP: It's yesterday's sandwich.

MARA: Is it still safe to eat?

NARI: No. It has been in the sun.

PIP: I can throw it in the compost bin.

VEX: We are losing the artifact.

MARA: We can preserve the box and dispose of the food.

ODO: The box has a name scratched underneath.

PIP: That's mine.

NARI: I'll add it to the site record and return it to you.

VEX: The excavation reveals a student named Pip.

PIP: I was here the whole time.

MARA: The lunch box is an object, not a civilization.

ODO: I can close the trench around its outline before we cover it.

NARI: Before we cover the trench, we should record the layers.

MARA: Topsoil here, then packed gravel from the path repair.

ODO: The lunch box was just below the gravel, not in an older layer.

VEX: Can I describe it as a discovery across generations?

PIP: It was one day old.

MARA: A useful find can still teach us how the site is used. The box was left beside the current path, not under the ancient stones.

NARI: We'll mark the spot on the modern site plan and keep the old layers undisturbed.

SFX: Pencil marks a square on paper.

ODO: The trowel struck a stone at the edge. Leave it where it is until we know whether it belongs to the path.

VEX: That could be the real artifact.

PIP: It could also be a loose stone.

MARA: Either way, don't pull it out for the camera.

PIP: I should clean the tin before it goes home.

NARI: Use water at the wash station, not the creek.

SFX: Water runs into a shallow basin.

MARA: The name is scratched underneath, so keep that side visible for the site photo.

ODO: I'll take the picture with the field card beside it.

VEX: The image needs a dramatic shadow.

MARA: The daylight is enough. We need a clear record, not a poster.

SFX: Camera shutter clicks.

NARI: The tin is clean and returned to Pip. The trench is closed for the night.

PIP: Tomorrow I'll bring a lunch that stays in my bag.

SFX: Trowel set down.

NARI: The team is covering the shallow find and logging its location.

VEX: The mystery continues underground.

MARA: The record is complete for today. We can come back with the site map tomorrow.

SFX: Brush sweeps; soil settles.

VEX: The team has uncovered evidence of a forgotten lunch.

TAG

SFX: Compost lid opens.

PIP: The sandwich has found a new site.
