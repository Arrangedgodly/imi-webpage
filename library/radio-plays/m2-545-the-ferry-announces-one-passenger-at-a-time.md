# The Ferry Announces One Passenger at a Time

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overproduced transit announcements that turn boarding order into a ceremonial pageant*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Announcer Vex, an orangutan; dock lead Mara, a macaque; ticket clerk Odo, a gibbon; deckhand Pip, a capuchin; passenger Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Ferry terminal. Gate bell, water against the dock, ticket printer.

VEX: Passengers, prepare to board the Grand Crossing. First, the honored traveler of the western quay!

MARA: We board by ticket group, one person at a time. There is no honored traveler.

ODO: Tickets are numbered. Nari has number one; she is waiting behind the marked line.

NARI: I can wait. My bag is light, and I can use the ramp.

PIP: The ramp is lowered and locked. I checked the hinge; the deck is level with the landing.

VEX: We need a trumpet fanfare for the first passenger.

MARA: We need the boarding bell once the crew calls ready. The trumpet recording could mask an instruction.

SFX: A brass fanfare begins, then cuts off abruptly.

VEX: It was only a brief announcement sting.

ODO: It covered the ticket printer. We could not hear the next number.

MARA: Remove it from the board. We'll call the ticket numbers clearly.

Nari approaches the ramp when Odo calls her number. Vex speaks over the call, describing her as a voyager setting off on destiny's tide.

NARI: I couldn't hear the number. Was that mine?

ODO: Yes, number one. You may board now.

MARA: Vex, give the passenger the ramp. The boarding announcement is not a narration track.

VEX: I will narrate after she is seated.

PIP: Ramp is clear. Nari is aboard and at the marked seat beside the rail.

SFX: Ticket printer. Paper tears.

ODO: Number two, the passenger with the green satchel.

VEX: The satchel enters the vessel.

MARA: Just say "number two." The passenger is walking safely.

PIP: The aisle is clear. No one needs to carry the fanfare speaker onboard.

VEX: Could we announce each step? "One foot, then another."

NARI: Please don't. I can walk without commentary.

MARA: That's the rule. We call the boarding group and let passengers proceed at their own pace. If someone needs help, we ask what kind.

ODO: The manifest lists six passengers. Nari is first; five remain.

VEX: The final travelers approach.

PIP: The next group is ready, but we wait until number two is seated and the ramp stays clear.

SFX: Bench creaks, footsteps; ramp hinge remains quiet.

MARA: Number two is aboard. Number three may approach.

ODO: Numbers three, four, and five have boarded. Five passengers are aboard; one remains at the line.

VEX: The ferry awaits its final cast member.

NARI: It is not a play. I can see the safety sign from my seat.

PIP: The ramp stays down while boarding remains open. The landing is clear, and I can see the next passenger approaching.

ODO: Five tickets are scanned. One passenger is still at the line; the captain has not called the gate closed.

VEX: A late arrival! Hold the boat for a dramatic entrance.

MARA: The schedule allows boarding until the posted time. We will wait if the captain has not called the gate closed.

PIP: The late passenger is walking to the gate. The ramp is still latched and level.

NARI: I can wait in my seat. No need to hurry the last passenger.

SFX: Footsteps cross the lowered ramp and continue onto the deck; a ticket tears once.

ODO: Ticket confirmed. Passenger six is aboard; count updated to six.

PIP: All six are beyond the ramp. Clear to raise it.

SFX: Ramp folds and latches.

VEX: The cast has grown.

MARA: No. The manifest has six passengers. Let Odo confirm the number to the captain.

ODO: Six aboard, ramp clear. Dock confirms the gate can close.

SFX: Gate chain slides into place.

NARI: No one was hurried onto the ramp.
PIP: I have the all-clear from the dock. The captain may depart.
ODO: All six tickets scanned. The manifest matches.
MARA: Now we can leave.
SFX: One departure bell; engine rises gently.
