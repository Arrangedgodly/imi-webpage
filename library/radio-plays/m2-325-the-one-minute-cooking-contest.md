# The One-Minute Cooking Contest

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: timed cooking competitions that create stakes out of ordinary kitchen tasks*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Nara, a gibbon; contestants Piri, a capuchin, and Odo, a macaque; timer Kito, a baboon.

[SFX: TIMER BEEP.]

NARA: Contestants, you have one minute to prepare a fruit plate.

PIRI: Is slicing allowed?

NARA: Yes.

ODO: Is placing allowed?

NARA: That is essential.

ACT ONE

Piri arranges berries in a circle. Odo begins peeling a pear. Kito starts the timer. Nara asks the contestants to describe their strategy while their hands work.

PIRI: I am making a circle.

ODO: I am making slices.

NARA: Both are ambitious.

Odo's peel comes off in one long strip and lands on the table. Kito says the clock has forty seconds left. Piri places the berries closer together to make the circle look full.

ACT TWO

Nara announces a surprise rule: the plates must be served without moving the table. Odo has put his plate near the edge. He slides it inward. Piri asks whether the rule applies to the plate or the table. Nara says the plate may move.

Kito calls ten seconds. Odo has three slices left. Piri's circle has a gap. She places one berry in it. Nara says the plate needs a garnish. Piri uses a mint leaf. Odo puts a slice of pear upright, but it falls onto the peel.

[SFX: PEAR SLICE TAPS PLATE.]

ACT TWO-B

Kito asks the contestants to describe the fruit they chose. Piri used small berries, while Odo chose a pear that requires peeling. Nara reveals the surprise round: each plate must travel from the counter to the serving mat without dropping anything. Odo carries his plate in both hands. The peel ribbon shifts, and one slice slides toward the rim. He stops, sets the plate down, and moves the slice back to the center. Piri walks carefully, keeping her berry circle in place. Kito says the timer will not stop during transport. Nara adds that no one may use the microphone stand as a tray. Odo had been about to balance his plate there. He turns toward the mat instead. Piri reaches the serving spot first, but a berry rolls from the circle. She catches it against her palm and returns it to the plate. Nara asks whether catching counts as plating. Kito says it counts as not dropping food.

Piri and Odo set their plates beside one another. Piri's berries form a neat circle; Odo's pear slices overlap the peel ribbon. Kito says the buzzer has stopped, but the timer's light keeps blinking. Nara checks the control panel. It is set to replay the buzzer whenever the microphone catches a click.

ODO: My plate is being timed again.

PIRI: That is the sound board.

Kito taps the panel. The buzzer chirps. Odo covers his plate with both hands as if the round has restarted. Nara switches the replay off and asks the contestants to hold their dishes still for the final count.

NARA: The timer stopped once. We will judge once.

Piri asks whether the peel ribbon counts as fruit. Nara says it does, but decoration earns no extra points. Odo tries to arrange the peel into a numeral one; it falls flat. Kito slides the serving mat between the plates so neither fruit touches the timer.

KITO: Both plates are safe from the clock.

The contestants wait for the final tasting instead of adding another garnish.

ACT THREE

The timer rings. Piri's plate has a berry circle and mint leaf. Odo's plate has pear slices and one folded peel that resembles a ribbon. Nara tastes one piece from each. Both are fresh.

NARA: The winner is the plate that can be eaten.

KITO: They are both edible.

NARA: Then we have a tie.

TAG

Odo removes the peel ribbon and eats it.

PIRI: That was decoration.

ODO: It was fruit.

[SFX: TIMER BEEP.]
