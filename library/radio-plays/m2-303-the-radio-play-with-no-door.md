# The Radio Play with No Door

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: melodramatic radio theater, where performers keep adding a door sound to a scene that has no door*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Narrator Nara, macaque; director Seli, capuchin; sound artist Odo, baboon; performer Pava, gibbon.

[SFX: STUDIO ROOM TONE.]

NARA: In a quiet room, a traveler waited for the evening train.

PAVA: There is no train station in this scene.

SELI: We can add one.

ODO: I have a door.

NARA: The traveler is in a field.

ODO: I can make the door sound distant.

Pava reads the script. Her character is sitting on a low rock, waiting for a bird to return. Odo tests a wooden door prop. It squeaks.

[SFX: DOOR CREAK.]

PAVA: That is not a bird.

SELI: It is a door to the field.

NARA: There is no door in the field.

SELI: Every scene needs an entrance.

NARA: The traveler has entered from the path.

Pava tries the line again. She says the bird will return at sunset. Odo adds a knock.

[SFX: THREE KNOCKS.]

PAVA: The bird does not knock.

ODO: It could be a very polite bird.

NARA: We need the sound of wings.

Odo flaps a pair of paper fans. The sound is too large.

[SFX: PAPER WINGS, LOUD.]

PAVA: That is a cupboard opening.

SELI: We need a smaller bird.

Odo folds the fans in half. The wings become a quick flutter. Pava speaks over it.

NARA: The traveler heard wings above the grass.

PAVA: That works.

Seli says the scene needs a transition. She asks Odo to close the door and open it again. Nara says the door is still not in the script.

SELI: It marks a change of mood.

NARA: The mood changes when the bird returns.

Odo tries making a door sound with a creaking chair. The chair squeaks twice.

PAVA: That is a chair.

ODO: It is an old door.

NARA: The traveler is sitting on a rock.

SELI: We can put a chair on the rock.

Pava refuses. She says the character should remain in the field. Seli asks her to perform the scene without any sound cue except the wings.

[SFX: SOFT WINGS.]

PAVA: The traveler waited. The grass moved. The bird landed beside her.

Odo makes a soft foot tap for the bird. Pava turns her head.

PAVA: There you are.

The scene ends. Seli says it needs one more beat. Nara suggests silence.

SELI: Silence is not a cue.

NARA: It is what happens after the bird lands.

Odo stops moving. The room tone remains.

[SFX: ROOM TONE.]

Pava lets the pause continue. Seli raises a hand to call cut, then lowers it. The actors wait. The radio studio's clock ticks.

[SFX: CLOCK TICK.]

SELI: The scene is complete.

ODO: I still have the door.

PAVA: Put it away.

Odo folds the door prop flat. It makes a final squeak.

[SFX: DOOR CREAK.]

NARA: The traveler has left the field.

PAVA: No. The door has left the studio.

Seli asks the cast to record a second take without the clock. Pava repeats the scene: she sits on the rock, waits, hears the wings, and greets the bird. This time Odo keeps both hands still.

[SFX: SOFT WINGS.]

PAVA: There you are.

The line lands cleanly. Nara asks whether the pause before the greeting should be longer. Pava says the bird needs time to settle. Seli asks Odo to repeat the wing cue once, quieter.

ODO: The bird is small.

PAVA: It is beside me.

NARA: The microphone is close.

Odo flutters a single paper fan. Pava turns toward the sound at the right moment.

SELI: That is the take.

ODO: The door stays folded.

NARA: The field stays open.

[SFX: SMALL WINGS, END THEME.]
