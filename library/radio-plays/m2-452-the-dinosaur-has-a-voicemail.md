# The Dinosaur Has a Voicemail

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio tours and fan hotlines that answer every question with a dramatic backstory*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Night attendant Runi, a gibbon; paleontologist Sela, a macaque; volunteer Pip, a capuchin; caller Vex, an orangutan; automatic exhibit voice, prerecorded; recorded caller one, a gibbon; recorded caller two, a capuchin.

SFX: Museum closing bell. Heavy door rolls shut. A telephone rings once, then twice.

RUNI: Night desk. The museum is closed.

VEX: I know. I'm calling the dinosaur.

RUNI: The dinosaur is a fossil.

VEX: It has a phone number on the new sign.

SFX: A relay clicks. The exhibit voice booms.

EXHIBIT: Welcome, visitor. Your question will be answered after three ages of silence.

VEX: I asked if it had feathers.

SFX: A dramatic drum roll. A speaker squeals.

EXHIBIT: The answer is hidden in the stone.

Sela enters carrying a file box.

SELA: The answer is on the label. Some relatives had feathers. This specimen does not preserve them.

RUNI: Why does the sign offer a voicemail?

SELA: Marketing wanted the dinosaur to feel available after hours.

VEX: It sounds less available and more trapped in a cave.

PIP: I can fix the line. The phone is plugged into the sound-effects rack.

SFX: A low rumble. Pip follows the cable behind a display.

PIP: It goes to the volcano button.

SELA: That button belongs to the geology room.

RUNI: So the dinosaur's answer is a volcano?

PIP: The wiring says yes.

VEX: I left another question. Does it know what happened to the fern in the case beside it?

SELA: That is a plastic fern.

VEX: Then the mystery deepens.

RUNI: We should play the messages before the morning crowd arrives. Pip, switch to the mailbox.

SFX: Beep. A soft hiss of tape.

CALLER ONE, recorded: Dear dinosaur, did you like the meteor?

EXHIBIT, recorded: Your message has been placed in the ancient queue.

CALLER TWO, recorded: Can you tell my class why your teeth are so large?

EXHIBIT, recorded: Please leave a bone after the tone.

SFX: Beep.

PIP: The answer machine is using the wrong script.

SELA: The visitor questions are real. The answers were written for a haunted castle exhibit.

RUNI: No wonder it keeps talking about silence.

VEX: Play mine again. I asked about feathers.

SFX: Button press. Short drum roll.

EXHIBIT: The answer is hidden in the stone.

SELA: It isn't hidden. The label says "fossilized impressions can be incomplete."

VEX: That is a less satisfying mystery.

SELA: It's a more useful answer.

PIP: I can unplug the volcano and connect the label reader. But the label reader has no speaker.

RUNI: We can read it into the phone.

VEX: Will the dinosaur answer?

SELA: The dinosaur won't. We will.

SFX: Plug pulled. The rumble stops. A phone button clicks.

PIP: Try it now.

VEX: Did this dinosaur have feathers?

SELA: Some related dinosaurs did. The fossil cannot tell us whether this one did.

VEX: So the answer is "we don't know."

SELA: That's what the stone says.

SFX: The recorded mailbox beeps by itself.

EXHIBIT: Your question has been answered. Please remain in the museum until the next geological period.

RUNI: I am not staying another hundred million years.

PIP: That's a timer. The line plays that message whenever we disconnect.

SELA: We can replace it with the closing announcement.

RUNI: Say it now. I'll record it.

SELA: The museum is closed. The dinosaur cannot answer questions, but the paleontologist can.

VEX: Can I leave a question for tomorrow?

RUNI: You can leave it at the front desk.

VEX: Does the front desk have a geological period?

RUNI: It has a pencil.

SFX: Pencil scratches. Message tone.

TAG
