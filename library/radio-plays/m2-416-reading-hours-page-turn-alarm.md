# The Reading Hour's Page-Turn Alarm

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public reading broadcasts that add suspense stings to quiet passages and mistake ordinary page turns for plot twists*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Reader Mavi, a lemur; producer Tolo, a gibbon; sound operator Sari, a macaque; caller Dena, a capuchin.

[SFX: QUIET STUDIO. PAPER PAGE TURNS.] 

MAVI: The old tree stood beside the river. Its branches reached across the water.

[SFX: DRAMATIC ALARM STING.] 

MAVI: A bird landed on the branch.

TOLO: That was the page-turn cue. The audience should wonder what happens next.

SARI: It played because the paper moved. The sensor is too sensitive.

DENA: I am listening from the library. The alarm sounded like a fire drill.

MAVI: The story has no fire. The bird is resting.

TOLO: We need a little suspense before the break.

SARI: I can lower the sensor. It should trigger only when the page is fully turned.

[SFX: DIAL TURNS; PAPER MOVES WITHOUT STING.] 

MAVI: The river carried a leaf downstream. The bird watched it pass.

TOLO: That is a beautiful moment. Add a heartbeat.

SARI: There is no heartbeat in the story.

TOLO: The audience needs a reason to stay through the break.

TOLO: The app asks listeners to tap when they feel suspense. It reports no taps so far.

DENA: I tapped when the alarm went off, because I thought the library was closing.

SARI: That was a false suspense score. The app counted a startled tap as interest.

MAVI: The bird has landed, the leaf has passed, and the tree is still standing.

TOLO: Can we repeat the alarm once for listeners who missed it?

DENA: I heard it. I would prefer the rest of the story.

SARI: I am removing the alarm cue from the reading preset. The page sensor will only trigger the recording light, a green dot that nobody at home can hear.

MAVI: I can pause at the end of a sentence. I will not make the bird afraid of a leaf.

DENA: Please keep reading. I want to hear what the tree does.

[SFX: STUDIO CLOCK TICKS; PAPER SLIDES.] 

MAVI: At dusk, the bird flew home. The tree listened to the quiet water.

[SFX: A SMALL BELL DINGS; NO ALARM.] 

TOLO: The bell is the sponsor tag. It is softer than the alarm.

SARI: The page sensor stayed quiet. It is working now.

MAVI: Then I can turn the next page without a siren.

[SFX: PAGE TURNS; ALARM BLARES AGAIN.] 

DENA: The alarm is back.

SARI: It is the sponsor bell playing through the wrong channel. The sensor is fine.

TOLO: The sound board has the alarm and sponsor cue on one fader.

MAVI: I will hold the page until you separate them.

[SFX: FADER SLIDES; BELL TRACK STOPS. A SWITCH CLICKS.] 

SARI: Sponsor bell is on its own track. Test the page.

[MAVI: The river carried a small leaf beneath the bridge.] 

[SFX: PAGE TURNS; SOFT PAPER ONLY.] 

MAVI: The bird has gone home, and the tree is quiet.

TOLO: That is the end of the chapter. We can still use a sting after the ending.

DENA: Please don't. The story already ended.

TOLO: The reading hour needs a transition.

SARI: Use the library chime, not an alarm. It tells listeners the next segment is starting.

[SFX: GENTLE LIBRARY CHIME.] 

MAVI: This reading has ended. The next program is a conversation about river trees.

DENA: I can hear the chime and stay in the library.

TOLO: The audience did not need to fear the page or wonder whether the library alarm was part of the story.

SARI: The page sensor is disarmed between readings.

[SFX: SENSOR SWITCH CLICKS OFF; PAPER SETTLES.] 

MAVI: And the bird has already gone home.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES; QUIET ROOM TONE.]
