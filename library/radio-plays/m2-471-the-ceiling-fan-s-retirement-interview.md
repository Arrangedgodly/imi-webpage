# The Ceiling Fan's Retirement Interview

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: farewell interviews that turn a routine replacement into a career retrospective*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; studio engineer Nari, a lemur; caretaker Mara, a macaque; producer Vex, an orangutan; caller Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Studio ceiling fan turns with a steady hum. A microphone clicks on.

ODO: Today on Long Service, we honor the ceiling fan that has worked in this studio for twenty years.

NARI: The fan has not worked for twenty years. It was installed last Tuesday.

VEX: It has already become part of the team.

MARA: It rattles when we turn it to high.

ODO: Fan, what do you want listeners to know?

NARI: It cannot answer.

VEX: We can let the blades speak through their movement.

SFX: Fan clicks up one speed. A rattling beat develops.

ODO: That is a strong opening statement.

MARA: That is a loose screw.

PIP, on phone: I have listened every morning. The fan was off last week.

VEX: Listeners remember the feeling of a fan, not its installation date.

NARI: We should tighten the screw before it falls.

ODO: First, we need the anniversary questions. Did the fan keep the studio cool?

MARA: It made the microphone noisy.

VEX: A constant presence, even when no one noticed.

NARI: Everyone noticed. The recordings have a low buzz.

PIP: I thought the station had a bee in the ceiling.

ODO: That can be its nickname.

MARA: It has no nickname.

VEX: The fan's retirement deserves a name.

NARI: I can inspect the bracket. First I'll isolate power and wait until the blades stop.

SFX: Wall switch clicks off. The fan slows to a stop. A tester chirps.

NARI: Power is off. The blades are still. I can open the bracket when we finish the interview.

VEX: We will take a brief pause while the fan prepares to leave.

MARA: That is how repairs begin.

SFX: Toolbox opens. A screwdriver is set beside the bracket.



PIP: The silence is louder.

NARI: The microphones are picking up the clock now.

ODO: We should ask the fan how it feels about the silence.

VEX: It cannot answer.

ODO: Then the producer can answer for it.

VEX: The fan is proud of its service and grateful for the air it moved.

MARA: The air is not moving now.

NARI: I can open the side window, but then street noise comes in.

SFX: Window latch lifts. A bird calls outside. A bus hisses at the curb.

PIP: The fan was quieter than the bus.

MARA: Only on low.

ODO: There is our anniversary lesson. It worked best when we didn't demand a dramatic setting.

VEX: Can we frame the new fan as its successor?

NARI: We don't have a new fan.

MARA: The caretaker has a hand fan.

ODO: Bring it to the microphone.

SFX: Paper fan opens. Gentle air swishes across the mic.

VEX: It has a warmer tone.

PIP: It also doesn't rattle.

NARI: And it can be folded and put away.

MARA: The old fan stays off until the screw is fixed. The hand fan gets us through the interview.

ODO: We have a successor story.

NARI: We have a repair ticket.

VEX: Which is more inspiring?

MARA: The ticket is scheduled for tomorrow.

NARI: Power is still isolated. I'll tighten the bracket now.

SFX: Screwdriver turns twice. The bracket gives one solid knock.

MARA: That's secure. Restore the fan at low speed.

SFX: Wall switch clicks on. The fan starts with a steady, quiet hum.

PIP: I can hear the clock again.

VEX: The fan has returned to work.

ODO: Then the fan is not retiring. It is on leave.

SFX: Studio clock clicks once under the fan's even hum.

PIP: Was that the fan?

NARI: That's the clock.

VEX: The hand fan will receive a plaque.

MARA: The plaque can go on the wall.
