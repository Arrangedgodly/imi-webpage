# The Noon Bell That Never Rang

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: clock-repair mysteries that multiply the diagnosis before anyone checks the clock's simple mechanism*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Clockmaker Daro, a gibbon; apprentice Piri, a macaque; customer Tavi, a capuchin; inspector Fen, a lemur.

[SFX: MANY CLOCKS TICKING AT DIFFERENT TEMPOS. A DOOR BELL RINGS; ALL TICKS STOP FOR A BEAT, THEN RESUME.]

TAVI: This clock passed twelve without ringing. It rang normally at one.

DARO: Which clock?

TAVI: The blue one by the window. It reaches twelve, stays silent, and carries on to one.

PIRI: It has been doing that all week.

FEN: We need a formal diagnosis. Is the clock late, early, or historically inaccurate?

TAVI: It is missing the noon chime.

FEN: That is not on the form.

[SFX: PAPER SHUFFLING. A CLOCK CHIMES ELEVEN.]

DARO: Let me hear it. Wind it, Piri.

[SFX: KEY TURNS. THE CLOCK TICKS; THE HANDS MOVE TO TWELVE. SILENCE WHERE THE CHIME SHOULD SOUND.]

PIRI: The hands reached twelve; the bell stayed quiet.

FEN: The minute hand is exactly at twelve. The hour hand points to noon.

TAVI: Is that how the chime vanished?

DARO: It may be. First listen to the escapement; the cause is still moving inside.

DARO: The hammer is not reaching the bell.

FEN: Could that be the cause?

DARO: We must rule out the calendar.

TAVI: The clock has no calendar.

FEN: Then we need a calendar exemption.

[SFX: DRAWER OPENS. A SMALL METAL CLINK.]

PIRI: I found a loose washer against the twelve-strike rack.

DARO: The clock has no washer listed in its inventory.

TAVI: Could it be the missing chime?

FEN: A chime cannot be a washer.

PIRI: The rack reaches this washer only at its full twelve-strike travel. At one strike, it never gets this far.

DARO: Let's remove it carefully.

[SFX: SMALL SCREWDRIVER TURNS. THE WASHER LIFTS FREE. THE CLOCK TICKS; THE RACK SHIFTS, THEN CATCHES ON A SMALL BENT EDGE.]

TAVI: The clock is ticking.

FEN: What time does it show?

PIRI: Twelve. The hands moved; the chime stayed blocked.

FEN: The noon bell is still missing.

DARO: The washer bent the rack stop. Its edge still catches at full travel.

TAVI: Can you straighten it?

DARO: Yes, but first we must find out why the washer was inside.

PIRI: The clock has waited here with its back open. The drawer above holds spare washers.

FEN: Then the clock has borrowed a part.

TAVI: It did not ask.

FEN: Before repair, I need to record the clock's condition. Is the case cracked?

PIRI: No. The glass is clean. There is a scratch on the back plate.

TAVI: I did not notice it before.

DARO: The loose washer scratched the plate on its way into the rack. It blocked the full noon cycle.

FEN: Should that be a separate finding?

DARO: It is the same finding. The washer was not part of the clock.

PIRI: The drawer where it came from has a loose bottom.

[SFX: PIRI OPENS THE PARTS DRAWER. A SMALL METAL RATTLE IS HEARD. HE LIFTS OUT A TRAY; THE RATTLE STOPS.]

PIRI: Here is the gap in the drawer bottom, directly above the open clock case. The washer slid through.

TAVI: So the clock did not borrow it.

FEN: It received an unauthorized part.

DARO: It received gravity. That is not a repair category.

[SFX: THE BLUE CLOCK TICKS. OTHER CLOCKS CONTINUE AROUND IT.]

DARO: I will straighten the rack stop. Piri, hold the hammer clear.

PIRI: Holding.

DARO: Now let it strike at twelve.

[SFX: THE CLOCK HANDS HOLD AT TWELVE. THE HAMMER STRIKES THE BELL TWELVE TIMES, CLEAR AND EVEN.]

TAVI: There is the noon chime.

FEN: Twelve strikes, all recorded.

DARO: The hands and the bell agree.

FEN: I will amend the form.

TAVI: Can I take it home?

DARO: After it runs through the hour.

[SFX: FAST CLOCK TICKS AS TIME PASSES. A SINGLE CHIME SOUNDS AT ONE.]

PIRI: It kept time.

FEN: The repair is approved. The washer goes in this closed parts tin until Piri fixes the drawer bottom.

TAVI: Label it before another clock borrows it.

[SFX: A TAG IS TIED TO THE WASHER. THE SHOP'S MANY CLOCKS TICK TOGETHER FOR THE FIRST TIME.]

DARO: What time is it?

PIRI: One.

FEN: Good. We have only missed the noon bell once.
