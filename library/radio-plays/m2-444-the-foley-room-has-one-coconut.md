# The Foley Room Has One Coconut

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: foley demonstrations, sound-effects showcases, and behind-the-scenes hosts who claim every prop is a different animal*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A foley artist must create a whole jungle soundscape with one coconut shell, but the host keeps identifying the same hoofbeat as new wildlife.

Cast: Foley artist Piri, a capuchin; host Velo, a gibbon; director Roko, a baboon; actor Nema, an orangutan; sound mixer Kavi, a macaque.

[SFX: TWO COCONUT HALVES TAP ON A MAT.]

VELO: A horse crosses the jungle floor.

PIRI: That is one coconut half.

ROKO: Could it be a tapir?

NEMA: It sounds like two shells.

KAVI: It is two shells. We have one coconut.

ACT ONE

The studio is demonstrating foley for an adventure serial. Piri has one coconut shell pair, a tray of gravel, and a bucket of water. Velo asks her to create a horse, a giant bird, and a landslide. Piri says the shells can suggest hoofbeats if the rhythm is right, but they will not become three animals just because the host names them.

PIRI: Horse: even steps, then a stop.

[SFX: COCONUTS TAP FOUR TIMES, THEN STOP.]

VELO: A tapir enters.

PIRI: I said horse.

Roko asks Nema to play a traveler reacting to each sound. She hears the hooves and ducks. Velo says a dragon has entered. Kavi asks if the dragon should have wings. Piri says the coconut is not a dragon and the script only includes a horse.

ACT TWO

Piri demonstrates gravel under a boot, then water in the bucket. Velo narrates each as a stampede and a waterfall. Roko asks for a landslide. Piri tips a tray of gravel onto a padded mat. The sound is a short rattle. Velo says it could be a mountain collapsing. Nema says it could be a bowl of pebbles falling.

NEMA: I need a sound I can act against.

PIRI: You can hear the gravel hit, then settle.

NEMA: Then I will wait until it stops before moving.

Roko records the scene. The actor hears hooves approach, a branch snap, and gravel shift. She waits, then crosses the path after the sound ends. Velo asks to add a roar. Piri says there is no animal in the scene. Velo says the audience expects one. Nema says the audience can hear the traveler breathe and the shells stop.

Kavi reviews the take. The coconut taps sound clear, but Velo has narrated over the footfalls. He asks for a second take with the host silent. Velo resists; he is supposed to explain the craft. Piri offers him a mic to introduce the cue, then asks him to stop speaking during the demonstration.

ACT TWO-B

The demonstration begins again. Piri taps the coconut shells in an even pattern, then breaks the rhythm as the horse stops. Nema says, "The animal has paused." Gravel shifts. She waits for it to settle. A small splash follows as a branch falls into the bucket. She steps around it.

VELO: Was that a river?

PIRI: It was water in a bucket.

VELO: Could it be a hidden river?

KAVI: It could be labeled "bucket water."

Roko asks Piri to show how the sound changes when the shells are held differently. She turns them open-side down and taps on the mat; the rhythm is duller. Turned open-side up, they ring more sharply. Nema identifies the second as a hollow prop sound, not a hoof. Velo agrees that the shells are carrying the whole scene, but still refuses to call them coconuts on air until the reveal.

ACT THREE

Velo introduces the foley track and then lets it play. The shells suggest a horse crossing and stopping; gravel shifts; water splashes. Nema reacts to each sound in sequence. The traveler waits for the path to clear, then moves on. No dragon, tapir, or waterfall is added. Kavi keeps the narration out of the sound demonstration.

Piri explains that foley creates readable sounds from objects, but the listener should still follow the action. Velo asks if the coconut can get billing. Piri says it can be credited as a coconut shell pair. Roko says the shell is the only cast member with three animal roles. Nema asks whether it gets a chair.

TAG

[SFX: ONE COCONUT HALF ROLLS, THEN STOPS AGAINST A MAT.]

VELO: A small animal retreats.

PIRI: One coconut half stopped.

FINAL IMAGE: The coconut shells sit beside the labeled gravel tray and water bucket, ready for one accurately described scene.
