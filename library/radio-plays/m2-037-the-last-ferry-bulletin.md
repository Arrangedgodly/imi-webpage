# The Last Ferry Bulletin

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local transit radio announcements and commuter call-ins*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Announcer Pell, a calm langur; dispatcher Nima, a quick macaque; captain Doss, a baboon ferry operator; caller Bex, a gibbon commuter; and a flock of gulls that interrupts every update.

[SFX: HARBOR RADIO TONE. GULLS CRY. A FERRY ENGINE IDLES.] 

PELL: This is the Bellwether Transit Hour with an update on the evening ferry. The 6:10 crossing is delayed by a floating branch near the south marker. Our producer has asked whether this is breaking news.
NIMA: It is news. We do not know that it is breaking.
PELL: The branch has declined to comment.
NIMA: The branch is caught beside a buoy. The crew is checking the channel.
PELL: Please do not send us photographs of other branches. We have enough branches for this bulletin. The crew has stopped the boat at the dock and is checking the channel. Please do not board until the captain gives the all-clear.

NIMA: We have two buses waiting on the hill and a queue at the market stop. The shuttle can take passengers to the north pier, where a second ferry is still running.

PELL: Is the north pier accessible from the market stop?

NIMA: Yes. There are six steps at the old entrance, but the ramp is open by the ticket kiosk.

[SFX: PHONE LINE BEEPS.] 

PELL: Caller Bex is waiting with a crate of medicine for the island clinic.

BEX, PHONE: The clinic expects it before seven. The crate is packed in a cooler. Can the shuttle carry it?

NIMA: It can if the cooler fits inside the passenger area. We cannot strap it to the roof. The shuttle driver has confirmed there is room near the back seats.

PELL: Captain Doss, what is the branch doing?

DOSS, RADIO: It is caught beside a buoy. The water is moving it, and we will not pass until it is clear. I have called the harbor crew.

[SFX: GULLS, THEN A HORN FROM FARTHER OFF.] 

PELL: We have a second announcement. The 6:25 return ferry will depart from the north pier instead of the south. Passengers with bicycles should use the rack at the front of the shuttle. Please keep the walkway clear for anyone carrying bags or using a mobility aid.

BEX: I can take the cooler to the north pier. The clinic asked me to check the seals if the route changes.

NIMA: The shuttle is arriving now. The driver will help load it. Please keep the cold pack level.

[SFX: BUS BRAKES, DOORS OPEN.] 

PELL: The branch has moved farther into the channel. Captain Doss is holding position. The harbor crew is approaching by workboat.

DOSS: It is a willow limb, not a whole tree. The branch is longer than it looked from shore. We have a line around it and are towing it toward the floating debris boom.

PELL: How long?

DOSS: I do not know. We will not guess a departure time while it is still moving.

NIMA: The north route remains open. The shuttle can take the 6:40 group and connect with the ferry there. The transit board has been updated at both stops.

[SFX: PAPER PRINTER, BUTTONS CLICK.] 

PELL: The first bus has left for the north pier. Bex is aboard with the cooler. The clinic has received an estimated arrival time of 6:52, with a note that traffic may change it.

BEX: I'm at the pier. The ramp is clear. The captain has made room by the cabin door.

PELL: Thank you. The ferry crew is bringing the medicine across as soon as it is safe.

DOSS, RADIO: Debris boom has the branch. We have checked the channel. The ferry can move at reduced speed.

[SFX: ENGINE RISES, THEN SETTLES.] 

PELL: The south ferry is underway. It is seven minutes behind schedule. The north route remains in service for passengers who need it. We will update if that changes.

NIMA: No one should run for the boat. There will be another crossing in thirty minutes.

PELL: That is the transit update. The branch has been removed, the medicine is on its way, and the ferry is moving safely. We will not call a delay a disaster just because the clock is impatient.

[SFX: GULLS CALL. ENGINE FADES ACROSS WATER.]
