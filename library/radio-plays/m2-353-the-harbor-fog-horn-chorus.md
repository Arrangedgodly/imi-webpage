# The Harbor Fog Horn Chorus

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: coastal radio bulletins that turn fog signals into a musical event and confuse their timing*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Signal keeper Ivo, a capuchin; announcer Mara, a gibbon; pilot Neri, a macaque; dispatcher Tesh, a lemur.

[SFX: DENSE HARBOR FOG. A FOGHORN SOUNDS LONG-LOW. RADIO STATIC.]

MARA: Welcome to the morning harbor chorus. The east pier sounds a long blast every thirty seconds.

IVO: It should sound one long blast every thirty seconds. The west pier is two short blasts.

NERI: I heard a long, then two short, then another long. Which entrance is clear?

TESH: Neither signal is a permission to enter. Wait outside the marked channel until visibility improves.

MARA: The horns are harmonizing beautifully.

IVO: They are overlapping because the west timer is early.

MARA: Can we call it an unplanned duet?

IVO: You can, after we establish which signal came from where.

[SFX: TWO SHORT BLASTS SOUND, FOLLOWED BY A LONG BLAST. RADIO CLICK.]

NERI: The short blasts seem to come from behind me now.

TESH: Wind is carrying them across the water. Keep your position by the outer buoy.

IVO: The west timer is firing every twenty-two seconds. The east timer is on thirty.

MARA: I can count the intervals on air.

IVO: Do not count them as a song. Give Tesh the measured times.

MARA: West: two short blasts at twenty-two seconds. East: long at thirty.

TESH: Copy. I am closing the west pier approach until I can correct its timer.

[SFX: RADIO BUTTONS CLICK. PAPER CHART UNFOLDS.]

NERI: I can see the outer buoy by its bell, but not the pier.

TESH: Stay outside it. I will signal when the lane is clear.

MARA: The morning chorus is paused.

IVO: Good. I am at the west control box. The timer dial has shifted from its bracket.

[SFX: BOOTS ON METAL GRATING. A BOX LATCH OPENS. WIND BUFFETS MIC.]

MARA: Are you in position?

IVO: Yes. I can hear the horn through the housing. The dial is loose, not the horn.

TESH: The old schedule has a seasonal offset. It should be thirty seconds.

IVO: I see the mark. The pointer slipped to twenty-two.

MARA: Could the extra blasts be a call-and-response?

IVO: They are an early timer.

[SFX: SCREW TURNS. METAL DIAL CLUNKS INTO POSITION.]

IVO: The pointer is seated. I will wait for the next cycle.

[SFX: WIND AND WATER. TWENTY-NINE SECONDS. A LONG HORN SOUNDS.]

IVO: East horn.

[SFX: ONE SECOND. TWO SHORT BLASTS.]

IVO: West horn. Thirty seconds after the last west cycle.

TESH: That aligns now. I am reopening the marked channel for slow entry, with visibility still low.

MARA: Neri, did you receive the instruction?

NERI: I heard the west pier twice and the east pier once. I know which signal is which now. I will follow the buoy line slowly.

TESH: Correct. Do not use the horn as a musical cue. Use the chart and keep radio contact.

MARA: The harbor chorus has become an interval report.

IVO: Which is more useful to a pilot.

[SFX: BOAT ENGINE REVS GENTLY. A BELL BUOY TOLLS. FOGHORN LONG BLASTS AT STEADY INTERVAL.]

NERI: I'm past the outer buoy. The channel is clear of traffic.

TESH: Maintain slow speed until the inner marker.

MARA: We will leave the horns to the signal keepers.

IVO: The east is long, the west is two short, and both now keep time.



MARA: A fishing boat has called in. Its crew heard the two short blasts and thought the east pier was signaling.

TESH: Tell them the west pier is two shorts and the east is one long. Both are audible, but entry stays slow.

IVO: The west timer is fixed. I can hear the two short bursts with a full interval between them.

[SFX: TWO SHORT BLASTS SOUND. A PAUSE. TWO MORE SHORT BLASTS AT THE SAME INTERVAL.]

MARA: The second pair follows the first at the corrected interval. No harmony required.

NERI: The buoy bell is clear through the fog. I am staying on the marked course.

TESH: Good. No vessel is using the horn as a starting signal.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF. FOG AND WATER CONTINUE; ONE LONG BLAST SOUNDS.]
