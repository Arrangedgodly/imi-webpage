# Choose Your Own Sound Effect

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: interactive radio dramas that promise audience control while limiting choices to prewritten sound cues*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
NARRATOR RAL, a macaque
HERO VIK, a gorilla
PRODUCER PIP, a capuchin
CALLER SERA, a gibbon
CARETAKER NUI, a langur

[SFX: MYSTERY MUSIC.]

RAL: You, the listener, choose the hero's next move. Press one for the dark corridor, or two for the creaking stair.

PIP: We have already queued the corridor.

VIK: What if I take the lit hallway?

PIP: That is not a choice.

### Scene One: The Choice

[PHONE BUTTONS BEEP.]

SERA: I pressed one. The corridor sounds safer if the lights are on.

RAL: The choices are dark corridor or creaking stair.

SERA: Neither says the lights are on.

[PIP OFFERS A THIRD BUTTON, WHICH PLAYS A WIND SOUND.]

SERA: I choose the window.

PIP: There is no window path.

VIK: We could use the actual lit hallway.

RAL: That is not in the script.

SERA: Then your choices are not choices.

[PIP PLAYS THE DARK CORRIDOR AGAIN. IT IS LOUD ENOUGH TO COVER RAL'S NEXT LINE.]

RAL: The corridor has drowned the narrator.

VIK: I will keep walking until I find the caretaker.

[PIP PLAYS THE DARK CORRIDOR CUE. IT HAS FOOTSTEPS, A DOOR, AND A SQUEAKING CART.] 

VIK: That is a storage hallway.

PIP: It has a mysterious reverb.

VIK: I will follow the cart sound.

[THE CART ROLLS TO A STOP.]

VIK: The caretaker is delivering chairs.

RAL: Your choice has uncovered a supporting character.

### Scene Two: The Branching Path

[PIP OFFERS THE STAIR CUE. IT IS A RECORDING OF A SINGLE CREAK LOOPED THREE TIMES.]

SERA: I choose the stairs, but only if they have a handrail.

PIP: We did not record a handrail.

VIK: I can ask the caretaker.

[FOOTSTEPS. VIK CALLS OUT.]

VIK: Is there a handrail?

NUI: Yes. It was installed last week.

[PIP SEARCHES THE SOUND LIBRARY. HE FINDS A DOOR SLAM, A THUNDERCLAP, AND A SNEEZE.] 

PIP: I have no handrail sound.

RAL: We can describe it without an effect.

VIK: I am holding the rail and walking upstairs.

### Scene Three: The Ending

[PHONE BUTTONS BEEP AGAIN.]

SERA: Can I choose the ending?

RAL: Door one is a dramatic escape. Door two is a cliffhanger.

SERA: I choose the exit door.

[THE EXIT DOOR OPENS. A QUIET ROOM TONE, THEN THE CART WHEELS AWAY.]

VIK: The caretaker has finished delivering chairs.

PIP: We have reached the end of the story.

SERA: I chose a route that exists.

[PIP OFFERS A FINAL BUTTON. IT PLAYS AN EXPLOSION.]

SERA: I choose not to explode.

VIK: The exit is still open.

RAL: The audience has selected the quiet ending.

[PIP PRESSES THE EXIT LIGHT. IT STAYS LIT. THE EXPLOSION CUE PLAYS BY ACCIDENT, THEN STOPS WHEN SERA HANGS UP.] 

SERA: I am leaving the call now.

PIP: The listener has left the story.

[VIK WALKS OUT THROUGH THE LIT HALL. THE CART WHEELS PAST IN THE OTHER DIRECTION.] 

[THE PHONE LINE BEEPS. PIP'S CONSOLE HAS ONLY ONE LIGHT ON: "EXIT." ]

[PIP OPENS THE CALL LOG. THE NEXT LISTENER HAS REQUESTED A LIT HALLWAY.]

PIP: That sound is not in our old library.

RAL: We can record one.

[VIK WALKS THE ROUTE AGAIN, THIS TIME IN THE REAL HALL.]

### Tag

[SFX: A HANDRAIL CREAKS ONCE.]

PIP: We need to record that sound.

VIK: It is the studio chair.

[PIP PLAYS THE STAIR CUE ONE MORE TIME. A SINGLE CREAK REPEATS.]

SERA: That was the same stair.

RAL: It was looped for every choice.

VIK: The real stairs have different steps.

[PIP STOPS THE LOOP. HE RECORDS VIK WALKING UP THE STUDIO STAIRCASE; EACH STEP MAKES A DIFFERENT SOUND.]

PIP: We have a new path.

SERA: Next time, record the hall first.
