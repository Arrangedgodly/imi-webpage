# The Pebble Museum's Audio Tour Has a Beach Sound

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum guides that add theatrical sound instead of context*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Curator Mara, a macaque; host Vex, an orangutan; guide Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin; technician Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Small museum. A pebble rolls in a display tray.

VEX: Welcome to the world's most moving rock collection.

PIP: The label says these pebbles came from the north beach.

MARA: They were smoothed by water over many years.

VEX: Our audio tour begins with surf sounds.

SFX: Recorded surf plays loudly.

ODO: We are indoors. The surf is masking the guide.

SELI: The speaker is too close to the display case.

PIP: I can't hear the label, and the cord crosses the walking path.

VEX: The sound immerses visitors in the beach.

MARA: Visitors need a clear path between cases. Seli, route the cord behind the cabinet.

SFX: Cord lifts, slides behind cabinet, and settles.

SELI: The walkway is clear now.

PIP: I nearly caught my foot.

VEX: That was a near miss, not part of the audio tour.

ODO: I'll stop the surf track and read the label at speaking volume.

SFX: Surf cuts off. Museum becomes quiet.

ODO: "North Beach pebble, gathered after the spring storm. Its rounded edge was shaped by repeated waves."

PIP: I can hear the place and the date.

MARA: The label has the location and date. The recording should add what a visitor cannot get from looking.

VEX: A dramatic tide.

SELI: The speakers are small. A loud recording only blurs the words.

VEX: What if we begin with "Imagine the shore"?

PIP: I can imagine it from the pebbles.

MARA: Let's compare the beach stone to the river pebble in the next case.

SFX: A pebble rolls gently in the tray.

ODO: This river stone is smoother on one side and has a darker stripe.

PIP: The sound is different when it rolls.

VEX: We can use separate water tracks for each display.

MARA: Only if the sounds help explain the stones, not cover them.

SELI: I'll set the surf low for the beach case and leave the river recording off.

MARA: Pip, stand at the entrance and tell us whether you can hear the guide.

SFX: Footsteps move across the room. The surf starts softly, then rises.

PIP: I can hear the waves, but the words disappear near the door.

VEX: The entrance should feel like you are stepping onto the beach.

ODO: A visitor needs to hear where the case is before they turn toward the sound.

MARA: Lower the track and point the speaker toward the listening mark.

SFX: Stand swivels. Dial turns down.

SELI: The cord is clear and the speaker faces the bench now.

PIP: From the doorway, I can hear the guide invite me toward the beach case.

SFX: A control dial turns. Soft surf returns behind a clear voice.

ODO: "The beach stone was rounded by waves. Look for the worn edge and pale salt marks."

PIP: I can hear every word. The surf stays in the background.

VEX: The tide is less dramatic at this volume.

MARA: The visitor can hear the guide and still notice the actual pebble.

SFX: Odo places a smooth stone in a shallow dish. It rolls, then stops.

ODO: That is the real sound in this room, not a soundtrack.

PIP: I understand why the beach stone is rounded.

SELI: I'll record the new levels and Odo's revised guide so every player uses the same setting.

SFX: Tape deck opens. Microphone clicks on; reel turns.

ODO: "North Beach pebble, gathered after the spring storm. Its rounded edge was shaped by repeated waves."

SFX: Tape stops, rewinds, and plays back at low volume.

PIP: The words are clear from the entrance and at the display mark.

MARA: Save that reel. The old loud track can go in the archive, labeled outdated.

VEX: At least keep its title: "The Tide Arrives."

SELI: I'll keep it in storage. It is not on the visitor tour.

VEX: Can we add one wave at the end?

MARA: One quiet wave, after the guide has finished.

SFX: A brief soft wash of surf. Then silence.

ODO: The tour has ended. The aisle is clear, and the labels are audible.

TAG

SFX: Recorded surf fades out.

VEX: The tide has gone.

MARA: The museum is still here.
