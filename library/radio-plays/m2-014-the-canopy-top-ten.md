# The Canopy Top Ten

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Pop countdown radio shows and fan-vote chart reveals*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Zaza, a capuchin DJ; Teo, a squirrel monkey producer; Tavi, a cockatoo drummer; the Night Parrots, a bird band; the Glass Frogs, amphibian musicians.

[JINGLE: SYNTH, HAND DRUM, A MONKEY WHOOP.]

HOST ZAZA: It's Saturday night, and this is the Canopy Top Ten. I'm Zaza, a capuchin DJ with a tail around the fader and both feet on the beat. The chart comes from listener votes, call-ins, and our studio's highly scientific basket of song requests.

ZAZA: At number ten, "River Stones" by The Mudskippers. At nine, "No More Mosquitoes" from Pippa Moss. At eight, the dance track everyone hums while pretending not to know it, "Swing Left at the Fig Tree."

[CLIPS: THREE DISTINCTIVE ORIGINAL MELODIES, EACH TWO BARS.] 

ZAZA: The top two are tied. "Drum on the Hollow Log" by the Night Parrots, and "Quiet Feet" by the Glass Frogs. The vote counter says one hundred and twelve each. Our phone lines say the same. The basket says both songs smell like mango.

PRODUCER TEO: The result feed has a problem. The "Quiet Feet" votes include repeated calls from one number.

ZAZA: How many?

TEO: Ninety-four.

ZAZA: That is not a fan club. That is a very busy frog.

TEO: The calls came from the public payphone outside the swimming hole. The handset is stuck down.

ZAZA: So it has been voting all afternoon?

TEO: Every time the line goes quiet, it dials again.

ZAZA: We cannot count the repeated calls. We'll freeze the chart and check with the acts. Night Parrots, can you stay on the line?

[PHONE RINGS TWICE. A PARROT SQUAWKS INTO MIC.] 

PARROT SINGER: We'd rather play the song than win by accident. Our drummer, Tavi, has a loose wrist. She performs the rhythm with her beak and never gets to sing.

ZAZA: Tavi, do you want to say something?

TAVI: The track has my rhythm, but the recording label lists our producer as the songwriter. I asked about it twice. He said the chart didn't need extra names.

TEO: The session tape has Tavi's count-in and the whole drum pattern. The liner notes left her off.

ZAZA: That's a chart problem and a credit problem. We can fix the credit now. The position will wait until the payphone is repaired and we remove duplicate calls.

PRODUCER REX, PHONE: I thought the beat was part of the session. I didn't know it had a name.

TAVI: It has a name. My name.

REX: You're right. I'll issue the correction before the next pressing.

ZAZA: Good. Now let's hear the clean count. Tavi, give us your rhythm without the backing track.

[TAVI TAPS A SYNCOPATED PATTERN ON A HOLLOW LOG. OTHER MONKEYS JOIN WITH HANDCLAPS.] 

ZAZA: That is the sound. The audience has stopped calling and started clapping. Tavi, do you want the band to keep the arrangement?

TAVI: Keep it. Just leave me a space after the second beat so the sound can breathe.

ZAZA: The chart is back. After duplicate calls are removed, "Drum on the Hollow Log" takes number one by seven votes. The Glass Frogs land at number two, and they say the payphone has excellent taste.

TEO: The phone is fixed. The swimmer who sat on it apologizes.

ZAZA: We have invited both bands to play a shared set next Friday. The Glass Frogs will add a quiet section, the Night Parrots will give Tavi the drum lead, and the label will print corrected credits.

TAVI: Can I pick the opening song?

ZAZA: You're number one. You can pick the whole set.

TAVI: Then start with the quiet song. Let everyone hear the space.

[MUSIC: A TWO-SONG BLEND, QUIET FROG NOTES BENEATH TAVI'S HOLLOW-LOG RHYTHM.] 

ZAZA: That was the Canopy Top Ten. We count listeners, not stuck buttons. Keep the songs coming, and keep the names attached to the hands that made them. Good night.

TEO: The repaired payphone has called the studio.

ZAZA: It has a request?

TEO: It wants its own single.

TAVI: Does it have a rhythm?

TEO: It has a busy signal.

TAVI: We can work with that.

GLASS FROG: We will add a quiet bridge.

ZAZA: The title is "Hold, Please."

TEO: The chart rules say the phone cannot vote for itself.

ZAZA: It can buy a record.

TEO: It has no wallet.

[JINGLE, END.]
