# The Echo Chamber Has an Open Mic

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night comedy radio, audience call-ins, and hosts who mistake the studio's acoustic echo for a second act*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A late-night open-mic host tries to feature a quiet comic, but the room's echo keeps delivering the punchline before the performer does.

Cast: Host Daro, a baboon; comic Reni, a gibbon; engineer Mavi, a macaque; caller Selo, an orangutan; floor helper Tavi, a capuchin.

[SFX: MIC TAP. A SHORT ECHO RETURNS.]

DARO: Welcome to the Late Laugh Line, where the audience is close and the walls are closer.

RENI: I have a joke about my tail.

[SFX: ECHO: TAIL.]

DARO: The wall has the setup.

MAVI: The wall has a slapback delay. It is not a performer.

ACT ONE

Reni is trying out a story about carrying a basket while her tail holds the door open. Daro asks her to repeat the first sentence. The echo repeats the last word just before she says it. Tavi moves a folded blanket behind the microphone; the echo softens, but the blanket falls onto the desk and covers the cue sheet.

TAVI: I have damped the room and lost the set list.

DARO: The room is becoming a critic.

MAVI: It is becoming quieter.

Selo calls from home and says the echo is funny, but it sounds like two comics sharing one microphone. Reni says the wall keeps taking her pauses. Daro wants to leave the echo in as a cohost. Mavi explains that delay can be reduced, but they should record one clean take without the echo first.

ACT TWO

Reni starts the routine. She says she went to the market with one hand free and one tail occupied. The wall repeats "occupied" before the punchline. Daro laughs early. Reni stops and asks if the audience heard the end. Selo says he heard the room tell him when to laugh, not why.

RENI: I had the basket, the door, and my own tail. The door was the easiest thing to carry.

The joke lands after a pause. Mavi turns the monitor down. Daro says he did not know whether to laugh at the line or the silence. Tavi finds the cue sheet under the blanket and restores it to the stand. The next comic is scheduled to perform a sound-only set, but his wind chimes are too close to the microphone.

MAVI: We can move the chimes three feet back.

DARO: Will they still sound like a storm?

MAVI: They will sound like wind chimes.

Reni asks for a second mic angled away from the wall. Mavi adjusts it, then checks the phase so the two channels do not create another echo. Daro says the show has taken longer to set up than to tell the joke. Reni says the door was the easiest thing to carry, and the crew laughs at the right time.

ACT TWO-B

The caller line opens for listeners to tell a short joke. Selo shares one about a banana that applied for a seat belt. Daro asks why. Selo says the bus had a fruit basket. The echo repeats "basket," then drops out. The pause stays. Reni laughs after the punchline instead of before it.

Mavi plays the recording back. The echo is faint but still present at the last word. She asks whether to use the dry mic only. Reni says yes; she wants the audience to hear her, not the wall. Daro asks if the wall can be credited as an audience member. Tavi says the wall has been paid in blankets.

The second take is clean. Reni tells the story with one held pause before the final line. No echo interrupts. Selo laughs through the phone. Daro waits until the line is complete before responding. The crew leaves the folded blanket behind the wall, where it softens the room without falling onto the desk.

ACT THREE

The open-mic segment airs. Reni delivers the basket routine; Selo's call follows. Daro introduces each speaker and lets the pauses belong to the performer. The sound-only comic's chimes play from the far side of the room, clearly separate from the voices. Mavi monitors the delay and keeps it off.

Daro thanks the performers and says next week's show will bring a larger audience, not a larger echo. Reni asks if the walls will be invited. Daro says only if they can wait for the punchline. Tavi checks the cue sheet and returns the blanket to its hook.

TAG

[SFX: ONE SOFT ECHO.]

MAVI: That was the monitor.

DARO: We have learned to identify the source.
