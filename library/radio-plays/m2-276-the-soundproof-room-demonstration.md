# The Soundproof Room Demonstration

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: soundproofing product broadcasts, staged demonstrations, and sales hosts whose microphones expose every promised silence*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST LELA, a macaque
SALESPERSON VEX, a baboon
ENGINEER NIMA, an orangutan
NEIGHBOR PIRI, a gibbon

[SFX: STUDIO DOOR SHUTS; TRAFFIC HUM.]

LELA: We are inside the new QuietShell demonstration booth. Salesperson Vex promises total silence.

VEX: Complete sound exclusion. No street noise, no bird, no neighbor.

PIRI (OFF): Is the demonstration started?

VEX: It is.

[SFX: KNOCK, THREE TIMES.]

NIMA: That is Piri at the service hatch.

VEX: The hatch is sealed.

[SFX: HATCH OPENS; KNOCKS CONTINUE.]

LELA: The sound continues.

VEX: The sound is inside the booth.

NIMA: The microphone cable passes through the hatch.

VEX: The cable is approved.

PIRI: I can hear everything you say.

LELA: So can our listeners.

VEX: The booth is soundproof. The microphone isn't.
 
Vex removes the headset and hands it to Piri. The earcup seal leaves a round mark in her fur. Piri turns toward the open hatch and taps the booth wall.

PIRI: I can hear the street through this side.

NIMA: The wall is quiet. The sound is traveling along the cable.

LELA: Is that the microphone cable or the room?

NIMA: Both. The cable crosses the seal and conducts vibration.

VEX: We will add a wireless microphone to the package.

PIRI: Then add the cost to the chart.

VEX: The chart is for sound reduction.

LELA: It may need a second column.


Vex steps out of the booth into the control alcove and closes the service hatch from outside. The knocks stop, but the hatch pinches the cable carrying Lela's booth microphone to Nima's monitor.

[SFX: MUTE, THEN VOICE THROUGH WALL.]

LELA (IN BOOTH): Vex, we cannot hear the product claim.

VEX (OUTSIDE, MUFFLED THROUGH MONITOR): The room is quiet.

NIMA: We can hear the ventilation fan.

VEX (OUTSIDE, MUFFLED THROUGH MONITOR): The fan is included.

NIMA: That is a sound.

Vex opens the door and points to a chart showing decibel reduction. The chart is printed in such small type that Lela asks him to read it aloud. He does; the microphone catches his voice bouncing in the booth.

LELA: We have a repeated Vex.

VEX: That is the acoustic treatment.

PIRI: That is an echo.

NIMA: The foam panels are on the walls, but not the ceiling.

VEX: We have treated the surfaces people see.

NIMA: Sound uses the other surfaces too.

Piri knocks on the booth ceiling from outside. A loose panel rattles.

[SFX: CEILING PANEL CHATTERS.]

VEX: That is the ceiling's one permitted sound.

LELA: Does the warranty permit it?

VEX: It is a feature.

Nima places a thick test speaker outside the booth and plays a recording of a quiet stream. The sound comes through the uncovered vent. Vex turns up the booth speaker to mask it.

[SFX: STREAM, THEN LOUDER STREAM.]

LELA: The stream is now louder.

VEX: We are demonstrating control.

NIMA: You are adding sound to hide sound.

PIRI: The booth has become a waterfall.

Nima closes the vent for the test. The stream disappears, but the air inside becomes stuffy. She reopens it and explains that the room needs ventilation and sound treatment together. Vex asks whether they can record the quietest three seconds.

LELA: We can try.

[SFX: ONE BREATH, A DISTANT BUS HORN.]

VEX: The bus has interrupted our silence.

PIRI: It is outside the booth.

NIMA: The microphone cable is still through the hatch.

She reroutes the cable through a sealed panel. Vex closes the door, then gives the silence demonstration. The ventilation hum remains faintly audible, but traffic and knocks are reduced.

LELA: That is quieter.

VEX: Complete silence.

NIMA: No. Reduced street noise with working ventilation.

PIRI: I can still hear you selling it.

VEX: That is the voice system.

LELA: The voice system is Vex.

The booth door opens. The sound returns. Vex asks for one final take, this time describing what the booth actually does.

VEX: QuietShell reduces street noise, keeps the air moving, and contains one salesperson.

[SFX: CEILING PANEL TICKS ONCE.]

PIRI: It has a small rattle.

NIMA: We will repair the panel.

LELA: That is our report. Quiet is measurable, silence is not guaranteed, and the salesperson remains audible.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES, VENT HUM, BUS FADES.]
