# After the Last Peanut

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Live sports radio commentary and postgame analysis*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[ARENA ORGAN: three tubas, a coconut drum, one excited hoot. CROWD CHATTER.] 

PLAY-BY-PLAY PIP: Good evening from the Infinite Monkey Industries annual office-chair relay, where eight departments race around the atrium while balancing one peanut on a teaspoon. I'm Pip Banana, joined by former relay champion and current calendar invite, Coach Momo.

MOMO: I accepted the invite by mistake. They said there'd be bananas.

PIP: There are bananas at the finish line.

MOMO: Then I stand by my decision.

PIP: The teams are lined up. Legal has brought a rule binder. Product has brought a prototype spoon with a handle that says "disrupt." Facilities has brought a wrench, which may or may not be legal equipment. The starter raises the clipboard.

[SFX: WHISTLE. CHAIRS ROLL.] 

PIP: They're off! First lap, Finance takes an early lead. That's Yoyo on the chair, cheeks puffed out, using the classic low center of gravity. A bold choice from a monkey who ate the warm-up banana.

MOMO: That's carb loading. Or hiding evidence. We'll learn after the review.

PIP: Design is closing fast. Tikka has one hand on the spoon, one hand drawing a new spoon, and somehow a third hand on the wheel.

MOMO: That's why you never let Design use the prototype during a live event.

[SFX: PEANUT TINKS ON TILE.] 

PIP: The peanut has fallen! The peanut has fallen in the atrium! The crowd inhales. Yoyo brakes, pivots, and retrieves it with a tail-assisted scoop. Spectacular recovery.

MOMO: The rules say no tail.

PIP: The rules say no tail on the spoon. The tail touched the floor, Coach.

MOMO: A distinction worthy of Legal. They are now filing an objection while rolling backward.

PIP: Lap two, Facilities has removed a loose wheel from the chair and replaced it mid-race. That's an unscheduled pit stop.

MOMO: They've got a wrench. That's a performance advantage and a workplace hazard. I respect both.

[SFX: METAL CLINK, CROWD HOOTS.] 

PIP: Product moves into second. Their spoon handle has snapped, but they have attached the peanut to the prototype using a tiny subscription plan. It will renew every lap.

MOMO: I don't think the peanut agreed to recurring billing.

PIP: It appears to have clicked "accept" when they offered a seed discount.

[ANNOUNCER: "LAST LAP!"]

PIP: Finance and Facilities neck and neck. Yoyo's chair wobbles. The peanut is vibrating. The finish line is three chair lengths away. Yoyo reaches for the banana table.

MOMO: No, Pip. Eyes on the peanut. Your prize is at the finish.

PIP: Yoyo has accelerated! Facilities' wheel comes loose again! The wrench is airborne, the peanut is airborne, and the crowd is ducking behind the snack bar. Yoyo catches the peanut in midair. On the spoon. That is extraordinary.

MOMO: It also counts as a catch, so the peanut has to return to the start by the rule they invented at lunch.

PIP: Wait. The peanut has been declared a relay baton. Officials confer. Legal's objection has arrived in triplicate, addressed to the peanut itself.

[SFX: WHISTLE, THEN A LONG, EMBARRASSED SILENCE.] 

PIP: Decision. The race is a tie. Everyone gets a banana and a mandatory chair inspection. Yoyo accepts the trophy, which is a spoon with a peanut glued to it.

MOMO: That's not a trophy. That's their equipment.

PIP: The CEO says the peanut will go into a glass case by reception.

MOMO: The spoon is in the case, but the peanut is gone. The door is open. That intern has suspiciously full cheek pouches.

PIP: In a late-breaking development, an intern has eaten the trophy peanut.

MOMO: Good. The only fair result. This is Momo, reminding you that you can replay the race in the company portal, where the video buffers every time somebody says "peanut."

PIP: I'm Pip Banana. Good night from the atrium. Please return the chairs to their departments, and the wrench to Facilities.

[ORGAN OUT. CHAIR WHEEL SQUEAKS. END.]
