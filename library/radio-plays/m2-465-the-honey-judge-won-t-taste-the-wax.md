# The Honey Judge Won't Taste the Wax

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: food competitions that judge a product by every feature except its flavor*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Nari, a lemur; honey keeper Mara, a macaque; judge Odo, a gibbon; sound mixer Pip, a capuchin; sponsor Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Studio table. Three jars set down. A low recorded bee hum fades in.

NARI: Welcome to Golden Drip, the honey competition broadcast where a panel will judge three local honeys.

ODO: By color, pour speed, and the sound they make when tapped.

MARA: Honey is food. You should taste it.

VEX: Our sponsor makes glass jars that sing when you tap them.

PIP: The hum is from a recording of the bees.

ODO: It is an important part of the judging.

NARI: The listeners cannot see the color.

ODO: We can describe it.

MARA: My jar is pale and runny. It came from the hill flowers.

VEX: Give us the pour.

SFX: Lid opens. Honey glugs slowly into a spoon.

ODO: A confident pour.

PIP: That was a glug.

NARI: Candidate two?

MARA: This one is darker. The bees worked near the clover.

SFX: Spoon scrapes jar. Thick honey stretches, then snaps back.

ODO: Excellent viscosity. The audience can hear the structure.

VEX: Tap the jar for resonance.

SFX: Spoon taps glass. A high note rings.

ODO: It has a bright finish.

MARA: That's the jar.

ODO: We judge what the microphone receives.

NARI: Judge Odo, taste the honey.

ODO: The program card says "visual, acoustic, and texture round."

NARI: It says tasting is the finale.

ODO: We have five minutes of broadcast. The finale is in the last minute.

VEX: We can use the time to play the sponsor hum again.

PIP: The recording is from one hive. The bees are not in the jars.

ODO: I thought the honey was humming.

MARA: Honey does not hum. Bees do.

SFX: Recording starts. A small chorus of bees buzzes around the microphone.

PIP: That's too loud. It is clipping.

VEX: Can we get a cleaner buzz?

PIP: The bees do not take direction.

ODO: Candidate three, please.

MARA: This is buckwheat honey. It is dark, and it has a strong smell.

SFX: Jar lid twists. Odo sniffs and coughs.

ODO: That is a powerful opening.

MARA: It smells like the flower.

VEX: We need to test the jar's ring.

SFX: Spoon taps. The jar gives a dull thud.

ODO: Poor resonance.

MARA: It's a wide jar with thick glass.

NARI: The honey is not the glass.

ODO: If it loses the tap round, it may not reach the tasting.

MARA: Then the contest is over before anyone eats.

PIP: I can take the lids off and set equal spoons in front of the judge.

VEX: The jars are the sponsor's product.

NARI: The sponsor can keep the lids on. We are tasting honey.

SFX: Three spoons laid in a row.

ODO: Which one is the winner?

MARA: Try them in order.

SFX: Spoon tasting. A pause.

ODO: The first is light, the second has a clover finish, and the third has a stronger, almost toasted taste.

NARI: Which would you choose on toast?

ODO: The clover.

MARA: That is from the jar that made the best sound.

ODO: Then the sound round was right.

PIP: You picked it before tasting.

ODO: I picked it again after tasting.

VEX: Can we award the jar a prize?

NARI: The award is for the honey. Mara's clover jar wins.

MARA: I brought more, if the crew wants a spoon.

SFX: Paper napkins unfold. Spoons clink.

ODO: I did not know judging would include snacks.

NARI: That is because you were judging the glass.

TAG

SFX: Empty jar tapped. It rings brightly.

ODO: I award this one for the encore.

MARA: It's empty.
