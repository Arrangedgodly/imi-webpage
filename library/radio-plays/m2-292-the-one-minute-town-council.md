# The One-Minute Town Council

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public meeting broadcasts, timed comment periods, and chairpersons who run out the clock with procedural announcements*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST TAVI, a gibbon
CHAIR SERA, a macaque
CLERK OMI, an orangutan
RESIDENT KITO, a capuchin
RESIDENT VEX, a baboon

[SFX: GAVEL, SMALL CROWD.]

TAVI: The town council is testing a one-minute public comment rule. Resident Kito will speak on the new footpath.

SERA: Your minute begins now.

KITO: The path ends at the creek, where the stepping stones - 

SERA: Point of order. The creek is maintained by Parks.

KITO: I am asking for a rail - 

SERA: You have thirty seconds.

OMI: The timer began when you called the point of order.

SERA: It did not. The chair was speaking.

TAVI: The timer says forty-five seconds have elapsed.

KITO: I have not finished my first sentence.

VEX: I have a comment about the timer.

SERA: One minute.

VEX: The timer is loud.

[SFX: TIMER TICKS.]

TAVI: We have a second issue.

Kito tries to continue, but Sera announces that written comments may be submitted in triplicate. Omi slides a form across the desk. It is two pages long.

KITO: The footpath needs a rail by the creek.

OMI: Please write that on line seven.

KITO: Line seven asks for the creek's official name.

VEX: I know it as the creek.

SERA: We need the precise location.

TAVI: The broadcast map shows the path ending at the blue line.

OMI: That is the old creek course.

KITO: The creek moved after last spring's storm.

SERA: Then the map needs revision before we can approve the rail.

VEX: The rail can go where the path is.

OMI: The path also moved.

TAVI: The minute is nearly over.

Sera tells Omi to stop the timer. Omi does; the timer continues because it is a wind-up model. Sera taps it with the gavel. It speeds up.

[SFX: TIMER TICKS FASTER.]

SERA: The timer has become disorderly.

KITO: It is running normally.

VEX: Can I use my minute to say the clock is wrong?

SERA: Your minute has not begun.

VEX: The timer is still running.

Omi lifts the timer and removes its spring key. It stops. The council realizes it has spent five minutes discussing how long a minute lasts.
 
Sera asks Omi to read the public comment rules aloud. The document has one-minute sections for residents and unlimited sections for chair clarifications.

OMI: The chair may clarify the agenda.

VEX: Can the residents clarify the chair?

SERA: That is not in the rules.

KITO: Could we let residents speak until the timer sounds, then give the chair the same time to respond?

SERA: The chair has three topics.

VEX: The residents have one path.

TAVI: The timer is still on the desk.

Omi proposes a visible light that turns amber at forty-five seconds and red at one minute. The chair may not speak while a resident's light is on.

SERA: That would make the timer fair.

KITO: And let me finish the location.

The clerk writes the proposed rule on the agenda. The timer stays stopped while the council votes on it.


TAVI: The path request is still open.

SERA: Kito, you may finish your comment without interruption.

KITO: Put a rail along the creek-side edge from the last dry marker to the bridge. The stones are slippery after rain.

OMI: I have the location. We can update the map.

VEX: Can we install the rail before the next storm?

SERA: Parks will inspect the path this week and report back.

TAVI: That is a decision.

SERA: It is a request for an inspection.

KITO: The rail is not approved.

OMI: The inspection is.

VEX: We have used the minute.

SERA: We have used several.

[SFX: GAVEL.]

Tavi reports that the council will change its timer: a speaking light will count a resident's minute and freeze during chair comments. Omi places the timer on the table, where it remains stopped.

TAVI: The public comment period is over.

VEX: Can I have a minute to comment on that?

SERA: Next meeting.

KITO: Please update the map first.

[SFX: PAPERS STACK, CROWD MURMURS.]
