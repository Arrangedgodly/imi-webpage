# The Signal under the Floorboards

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio ghost-story convention, where a mysterious tapping beneath the floor is traced to a loose water pipe and an overactive narrator*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host SELI, lemur; caretaker KITO, macaque; visitor MIRI, gibbon; sound engineer ODO, baboon.

[SFX: OLD HOUSE ROOM TONE. THREE KNOCKS UNDER FLOOR.] 

SELI: In this house, a sound has been heard beneath the boards each night at nine.

MIRI: I heard it last night.

KITO: The water pipe runs under this room.

ODO: That is what the ghost would say.

Seli leads the listeners through the room. Miri repeats the sound: three knocks, pause, two knocks. Kito says the pipe expands when the heater starts. Seli says the sound occurs even when the heater is off.

[SFX: TWO KNOCKS, THEN A DRIP.] 

ODO: The spirit has spoken.

MIRI: It sounds like water.

Kito opens the wall panel. A pipe is loose where it passes beneath a joist. The pipe taps when a nearby pump shuts off. Odo asks if the house has a spirit of plumbing. Seli says the episode requires suspense.

Kito tightens the bracket. The tapping stops. Seli asks everyone to wait until nine. Odo dims the lights. Miri listens. The room is quiet. At nine, the pump stops and the pipe gives one small tap.

ODO: It has returned.

KITO: The bracket shifted.

He adds a soft pad between pipe and joist. The next pump cycle ends without a knock. Seli says the ghost has been silenced. Miri says the pipe has been secured. Odo asks if they can still record the old sound for the show. Kito says yes, if they label it accurately.

[SFX: OLD RECORDING OF THREE KNOCKS, PAUSE, TWO KNOCKS.] 

Seli plays the clip. It sounds louder in the darkened room. Miri switches on the lamp. The sound is still a pipe.

SELI: The light has exposed the spirit.

MIRI: The light has exposed the panel.

Before closing it, Kito asks Odo to switch the pump on again. The crew listens from the floor. There is no knock, only the low hum of water. Odo taps the board once with his knuckle. The sound is dry and close, unlike the pipe's hollow knock.

ODO: The floor is not answering.

Seli asks Miri to stand at the other end of the room. Miri hears the pump through the wall, not beneath her feet. The sound direction confirms the repair. Odo removes the felt pad to show the loose bracket, then replaces it. The pipe is held safely away from the joist.

MIRI: We can keep the old recording, but label it as a pipe.

SELI: The mystery is allowed to have a name.

Kito closes the panel and marks the next inspection date. Seli asks the listeners to send in their own unexplained sounds. Odo asks if the show will solve them. Kito says only if there is a loose part.

Miri asks Odo to record the pipe with the room lights on and off. The darkness makes the tapping feel closer, but the microphone levels stay the same. Seli asks if the listeners heard a ghost because the sound changed or because the host told them to expect one.

SELI: I may have frightened the pipe.

KITO: The pipe has no ears.

Odo plays the two recordings back to back. In the first, Seli whispers about a haunting before the knocks. In the second, Kito simply counts the pump cycle. The same tap sounds ordinary.

Miri asks him to keep both clips for the episode. Seli agrees to introduce the ghost story as a story, then let Kito explain the source. The crew tests the wall panel once more. It stays closed, and the pipe does not touch the joist.

[SFX: TWO PUMP CYCLES, NO KNOCK.] 

TAG

[SFX: ONE FINAL KNOCK.] 

Everyone freezes.

KITO: That was the door.

SELI: The door is closed.

Miri opens it. A visitor stands outside holding a box. The box is tapping against their knee.
