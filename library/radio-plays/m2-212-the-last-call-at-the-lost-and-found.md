# The Last Call at the Lost-and-Found

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night call-in radio convention, where every caller describes the same missing hat from a different acoustic angle*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host PIRI, capuchin; caller NARA, gibbon; caller ODO, baboon; caller SELI, lemur; producer KITO, macaque.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC, SOFT STATIC.] 

PIRI: This is The Last Call at the Lost-and-Found. Our line is open for one more object before closing. Nara, what did you lose?

NARA: A green hat. It was on the shelf beside the bell.

PIRI: Was it wide-brimmed?

NARA: It was a leaf hat. The brim was wide when it was dry.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLE.] 

KITO: We have another caller. Odo, what did you find?

ODO: A green leaf on the floor. I thought it was a hat because it had a fold.

PIRI: Is the fold still there?

ODO: No. I stepped on it.

NARA: That is not my hat.

ODO: I did not say it was.

Piri takes a note. Kito whispers that the callers are sharing one telephone in the lost-and-found room. Their voices echo slightly.

PIRI: Seli, can you describe the object you returned?

SELI: I found a green hat on the bell shelf. I hung it on the hook by the door.

NARA: That is my hat.

SELI: It was wet. I left it to dry.

ODO: The shelf is empty. I checked.

KITO: The hook is directly behind Odo.

Odo turns. His chair squeaks. A hook rattles.

ODO: I have found a hat.

NARA: Is it green?

ODO: It is leaf green.

PIRI: We have a possible reunion. Please describe the damage.

NARA: There is a small notch near the brim.

Odo feels the hat carefully. Seli says not to pull the fold. He opens it gently. A soft leaf rustle.

ODO: The notch is here.

NARA: That is it.

PIRI: The hat is recovered. What happened to the bell?

[SFX: A SMALL BELL, MUFFLED.] 

KITO: It is beneath the phone book.

Piri lifts the book. The bell rings clearly. Odo asks if the bell was also lost. Piri says it was only covered. Seli says she put the hat on the hook so nobody would cover the bell again.

NARA: Can I come by for it tomorrow?

SELI: The office closes at six.

PIRI: Our phone line closes at six. The hook remains available.

Odo hands the hat to Kito. Kito places it on the shelf and moves the phone book away from the bell. A page flips and lands over the hat.

ODO: The hat has disappeared again.

Kito asks Nara if she can confirm the hat is dry enough to take home. Nara touches the brim. A soft rustle answers. Seli says the inside is still damp and suggests hanging it overnight. Odo offers to bring a basket so it will not be stepped on. Piri asks if the hat has a claim ticket.

NARA: It has my name inside the fold.

Seli feels along the inner seam and finds a small stitched initial. Odo had not noticed it because he looked only at the outside. The hat has a tiny patch where the brim meets the crown. Nara says she sewed it herself after a branch snagged the edge. Kito writes the identifying detail on the tag, so another green hat will not be mistaken for this one.

PIRI: The lost-and-found has learned to listen.

ODO: It has learned to check the inside.

Kito hangs the hat on the hook by the door. The hook squeaks. Odo tightens it with a small wrench. The hat now hangs clear of the floor and the bell shelf. Seli checks that the exit remains unblocked. Nara says she will return tomorrow to pick it up.

Piri asks whether they should leave the line open. Kito says the office closes at six, but the hook will hold the hat all night.

Seli lifts the page.

SELI: It is still here.

PIRI: And that is our final call. The object has not moved since we found it.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC RETURNS, BELL RINGS ONCE.]
