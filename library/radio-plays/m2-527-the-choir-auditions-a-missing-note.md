# The Choir Auditions a Missing Note

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: talent radio contest where judges rank rests as performances*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Nari, a lemur; judge Vex, an orangutan; singer Pip, a capuchin; accompanist Odo, a gibbon; stage manager Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Small hall. Piano gives three notes, then a long pause.

NARI: Welcome to the Quiet Note auditions. Contestants perform a musical rest. Judges will score timing, control, and how well the silence supports the piece.

VEX: Our first contestant is Pip, who has prepared a rest that lasts exactly four beats.

PIP: I have a score. Odo will play the two bars before my rest and the two bars after it.

ODO: Ready on your cue.

SFX: Piano phrase, four-beat pause, resolving chord.

VEX: I heard nothing. I give it a zero.

NARI: The rest was written into the score. It was not a mistake.

MARA: We should ask Pip to perform it again only if the accompanist is ready. The room noise covered the first beat.

PIP: The air vent clicked during my rest. I can hold my hands still and begin after Odo's pickup.

ODO: I will wait for the final note to decay before the count.

SFX: Piano phrase; final note fades. Four quiet beats. Resolving chord.

VEX: I heard the vent. Does that count as a note?

NARI: It counts as the room making noise. Pip did not make it.

VEX: Then the room gets a point and Pip gets a tie.

MARA: The room isn't entered in the contest.

PIP: I'd like the score to judge the performance, not the vent.

NARI: Fair. The panel will listen again without the vent. Odo, can you close the small louver? Don't block the main airflow.

SFX: Louver slides shut. Vent hum softens.

ODO: The room is quieter. We can do one final audition.

VEX: The silence is becoming increasingly loud.

NARI: Judge, please mark the timing now.

SFX: Piano phrase, four-beat rest, chord.

VEX: It was controlled. The silence had shape. I give Pip eight points.

PIP: Thank you. The rest is part of the song, not an empty space waiting for commentary.

MARA: The same rule applies to the judges. Let the last note finish before you speak.

SFX: Piano chord rings; Vex inhales, holds it, then lowers his score card.

NARI: That was a better ending than a score announcement.

ODO: Should I keep the louver closed for the next contestant?

MARA: Ask them first. The vent isn't broken; it is just part of this room.

VEX: I would like to enter the next round with a seven-beat rest.

PIP: You may need to wait through it.

VEX: I can do that. I have prepared a long, thoughtful silence.

NARI: We will know it is thoughtful if you don't interrupt it.

SFX: One piano note. A quiet pause. Vex's score card slips softly to the floor.


NARI: We have one more performer, Vex, with a seven-beat rest. Odo will play a short phrase before it and a held note after.

VEX: I would like the room to listen, not count.

PIP: The written score has seven beats. We can listen and count at the same time.

ODO: Ready. The held note lasts four counts, then the rest begins.

SFX: Piano plays; held note fades. Seven measured beats. Piano resolves.

NARI: The timing was exact.

VEX: I did not speak once.

MARA: You also let the note finish. That helped.

VEX: I award myself nine points for restraint.

NARI: Judges cannot score themselves. Pip, what score would you give?

PIP: Eight for timing, one for not asking to be interviewed during the rest.

VEX: That's fair.
