# The Karaoke Switchboard

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio karaoke contests, dramatic caller voting, and a host who must sing every jingle when the phone lines jam*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST MICA, a capuchin
DJ LOU, a gibbon
CALLER PIP, a macaque
CONTESTANT TESS, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: UPBEAT THEME. PHONE LINES RINGING.]

MICA: Welcome to The Karaoke Switchboard, where listeners vote by calling in. Tonight's prize is a portable speaker with one working button.

LOU: The volume button?

MICA: It is the button we found.

[SFX: ONE PHONE RINGS. OTHERS JOIN, THEN CUT OUT.]

MICA: Our first contestant is Tess, who will sing the caller's requested song.

TESS: What was the request?

MICA: We lost the line.

LOU: The request is "something with a chorus."

TESS: That is most songs.

MICA: You have thirty seconds.

[SFX: KARAOKE INTRO MUSIC. TESS SINGS A BRIGHT, ORIGINAL TUNE ABOUT A WASHING LINE.]

TESS: My shirts go round, my socks go slow, my laundry has a place to go.

LOU: Strong imagery.

MICA: We now take caller votes. [SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

PIP: I vote for the washing line.

MICA: That is a vote for Tess.

PIP: The line was better than the chorus.

LOU: The switchboard has recorded that as "line."

MICA: The first vote goes to the equipment.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

PIP: I am calling back. I vote for the singer.

MICA: That vote goes to Tess.

LOU: The system says the caller has already voted.

MICA: It has a memory.

PIP: It does not have a redial button.

[SFX: BUTTON BEEPS.]

MICA: We have a technical interruption. Lou, play the sponsor jingle.

LOU: The speaker has one working button.

MICA: Then sing the jingle.

[SFX: MUSIC CUE. LOU SINGS A SHORT, ORIGINAL AD ABOUT A SPEAKER BUTTON THAT ONLY PLAYS THE JINGLE.]

MICA: Our second contestant is the phone line. It has been ringing continuously.

LOU: The phone line cannot sing.

MICA: It can ring in key.

[SFX: PHONE RING, HELD AS A PITCH.]

TESS: That is a B.

PIP: It is a busy signal.

MICA: It is a B with a business plan.

[SFX: SECOND KARAOKE INTRO. TESS SINGS A SLOWER ORIGINAL TUNE ABOUT A DOOR THAT OPENS ONLY WHEN SOMEONE KNOCKS.]

TESS: Knock once for morning, twice for night; the door will open when it's right.

LOU: The voting lines are back.

[SFX: FIVE PHONES RING SIMULTANEOUSLY.]

MICA: The audience is voting. Please stay on the line.

PIP: I am the only caller.

MICA: You have five lines.

PIP: I am holding five phones.

LOU: All five votes are for Tess.

MICA: Is this allowed?

LOU: The rules say callers may use any phone.

TESS: I would like to vote for the phone line.

[SFX: ONE PHONE RINGS, THEN A SINGLE BEEP.]

MICA: Before we announce the winner, each finalist gets a prize demonstration.

LOU: The speaker has one button, and we have demonstrated it.

MICA: We also have a microphone with a mute switch. Tess, show us how it works.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. TESS'S VOICE CUTS OUT.]

TESS: [MUTED, GESTURING.]

PIP: I vote for whatever she just said.

MICA: The phone system has awarded that vote to Lou.

LOU: I did not sing.

MICA: You demonstrated silence. It was a brave choice.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS BACK.]

TESS: I was saying the prize needs a second button.

LOU: I can add one.

[SFX: LOU TAPS THE SPEAKER. A TINY, UNPITCHED CLICK.]

MICA: The audience has heard the new feature.

PIP: I vote for the click.

MICA: The click has already signed an exclusive contract with our jingle.

TESS: Can I sing one more line to close the contest?

MICA: Only if the phones can vote before the line ends.

[SFX: TESS SINGS A HELD NOTE. PHONE BUTTONS BEEP RAPIDLY.]

LOU: The system counted every beep as a separate caller.

MICA: We have twelve thousand listeners and one caller holding five phones.

MICA: The audience has selected Tess and the switchboard as co-winners.

LOU: The speaker has one working button.

MICA: Press it once.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICKS. THE JINGLE PLAYS.]

TESS: That's the same tune you sang.

LOU: The speaker can play one thing very well.

MICA: And that's our show. We will return after the jingle finishes.

[SFX: JINGLE PLAYS AGAIN. PHONES RING IN TIME.]
