# Forecast for the Covered Bridge

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: breathless weather bulletins that turn a tiny local shower into a national emergency*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Forecaster Luma, an owl monkey; field reporter Kito, a macaque; station manager Seli, a lemur; bridge keeper Odo, a baboon.

[SFX: WEATHER THEME, RAIN ON TIN.]

LUMA: Good evening. A cloud has formed above the eastern garden. We are calling it Cloud Eight because seven clouds declined to comment.

SELI: Please report only what we can hear.

KITO: I am live at the covered bridge. A single drop has landed on my clipboard.

ODO: The bridge is covered.

LUMA: We have a confirmed roof.

ACT ONE

Seli asks Luma to read the official rain gauge. It shows one millimeter. Luma says the gauge might be under a leaf. Odo says the leaf is part of the weather station.

[SFX: LEAF DRIPS INTO A METAL CUP.]

LUMA: The situation is changing.

KITO: A drop fell from the leaf into Odo's cup.

ODO: The cup was already outside.

Luma asks Kito to describe the wind. Kito says the fern moved once. Luma warns listeners to secure loose ferns. Seli crosses that instruction off the bulletin.

SELI: This is a garden report, not a storm shelter broadcast.

LUMA: We must respect the possibility of a storm.

ODO: It is still a cloud.

ACT TWO

A second drop hits the clipboard. Luma declares a developing pattern. Kito tries to identify the cloud's direction by holding a ribbon. It hangs straight down.

KITO: There is no breeze.

LUMA: A calm before weather is still weather.

Odo opens the bridge roof hatch to check for leaks. One drop lands on his shoulder. He shuts the hatch.

ODO: The roof has passed inspection.

Luma asks whether the bridge is at risk. Odo says the covered bridge is dry except for the two drops that fell through the inspection hatch. Seli tells him to close it again.

[SFX: HATCH LATCHES.]

Kito hears birds in the trees. Luma asks if the birds are evacuating. Kito says they are eating berries. Luma calls it a possible feeding event.

KITO: They are not carrying bags.

SELI: Stop interviewing the birds.

Luma's broadcast monitor starts flashing. She thinks it is a weather alert, but Toma's voice comes over the control speaker: the warning light means the studio's rain sound effect is still playing. Luma had added rain ambience for drama. The field recording has been dry.

LUMA: The rain was on the broadcast, not the bridge.

SELI: Then tell listeners which sound was real.

Kito taps the clipboard. Odo taps the bridge rail. The drops are so sparse that the taps sound like a slow duet.

Before the final report, Luma asks Kito to hold the clipboard over the gauge. The leaf drips again, but the gauge stays at one millimeter. Seli asks whether that qualifies as breaking news. Luma says it qualifies as a second drop. Odo notes that the bridge has stayed dry, while Kito's clipboard now has a tiny wet corner. Luma practices saying "isolated dampness" without alarming the audience. The phrase takes three attempts.

ACT THREE

The cloud drifts past without rain. Kito reports that his clipboard has dried in the warm air. Luma gives a final forecast: clear skies, one damp leaf, and a bridge that remains covered.

ODO: The roof did its job.

LUMA: Our severe weather watch is canceled.

SELI: You called it a national emergency.

LUMA: I said the word "developing" with concern.

Kito asks if they can keep the weather theme. Seli says yes, but only once. The theme plays at normal volume while the crew listens to the faint last drops.

TAG

[SFX: ONE DROP.]

LUMA: Breaking news: the cloud has made its final appearance.

ODO: It was never on the bridge.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC FADES.]
