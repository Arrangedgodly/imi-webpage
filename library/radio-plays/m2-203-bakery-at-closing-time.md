# The Bakery at Closing Time

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio human-interest profiles that invent a grand personal journey for a routine neighborhood job*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST ORA, a macaque
BAKER KEMI, a gibbon
PRODUCER DOR, a capuchin
CALLER VELA, a gorilla

[SFX: BAKERY OVEN TIMER. A DOOR CHIME.]

ORA: Tonight, we follow baker Kemi through the final hour before closing.

DOR: The promo calls it "the journey that changed bread forever."

KEMI: I turn off the oven, clean the counter, and lock the door.

ORA: Let us begin.

### Scene One: The Final Tray

[SFX: OVEN DOOR OPENS. A METAL TRAY SLIDES OUT.]

KEMI: The last rolls are ready.

DOR: Can we say they have emerged from adversity?

KEMI: They rose for an hour.

ORA: What does this batch mean to you?

KEMI: It means the oven is free to clean.

[DOR CUES SOFT PIANO. A ROLL DROPS ONTO THE FLOOR.]

DOR: A setback.

KEMI: That one is for the compost bin.

### Scene Two: The Profile

[FOOTSTEPS. A BROOM SWEEPS THE FLOOR.]

ORA: Kemi began baking with her grandmother.

KEMI: My grandmother taught me how to measure flour.

DOR: Did she tell you that baking was your destiny?

KEMI: She told me to close the flour tin.

[THE TIN LID CLANGS SHUT.] 

ORA: A message passed between generations.

KEMI: It keeps the flour dry.

[THE DOOR CHIME SOUNDS. VELA ENTERS TO PICK UP A RESERVED LOAF.]

VELA: I am late. Is it still here?

KEMI: Yes. I put your name on the bag.

DOR: Can you describe the reunion?

VELA: It is a loaf.

[KEMI WRAPS THE LOAF. THE PAPER RUSTLES.]

ORA: What will the customer do with it?

VELA: Take it to the meeting. We have a shared table.

DOR: Is there a personal memory?

VELA: It is for lunch.

[THE BAG RUSTLES AS VELA LIFTS IT.]

ORA: We have heard the bread leave the building.

[DOR CUES A GENTLE HARP. VELA SETS THE LOAF IN HER BAG AND PAYS.]

KEMI: The receipt is inside.

[VELA TAKES THE BAG. THE PAPER HANDLE TEARS. KEMI TRANSFERS THE LOAF TO A REUSABLE CLOTH BAG.]

ORA: A moment of unexpected loss.

VELA: The bread is still here.

DOR: Does the bag have a history?

VELA: I bring it to the market.

[DOR CUES A DRUM ROLL. KEMI SETS THE CLOTH BAG ON THE COUNTER.]

KEMI: It is clean and ready.

ORA: The customer has her bread.

### Scene Three: The Close

[SFX: COUNTER WIPED, TRAY STACKED.]

KEMI: The counters are clean. The oven is off. The reserved loaf is picked up.

ORA: The shop closes at eight.

DOR: We have twenty seconds for a final reflection.

KEMI: I will come back tomorrow and bake again.

ORA: The journey continues.

DOR: Can we get a sunrise shot?

KEMI: The bakery opens at six.

[THE LIGHT SWITCH CLICKS OFF. DOR'S PIANO CONTINUES PLAYING IN THE DARK.]

[DOR TRIES TO RECORD A VOICE-OVER AFTER THE LIGHTS ARE OUT. THE PIANO CUE CONTINUES, AND HE CANNOT HEAR HIS OWN READ.]

DOR: The baker's journey is complete.

KEMI: The music is still playing.

DOR: I am turning it off.

[THE PIANO STOPS.]

ORA: We can hear the lock click.

KEMI: That is the real closing sound.

[THE DOOR CHIME RINGS AGAIN. VELA HAS FORGOTTEN HER RECEIPT. KEMI OPENS THE DOOR A CRACK AND PASSES IT THROUGH.] 

ORA: One final return.

VELA: I have the bread and the receipt.

DOR: Can we call this a reunion?

KEMI: It is a receipt.

[THE DOOR CHIME RINGS. DOR STOPS THE PIANO. KEMI LOCKS THE DOOR AND PUTS THE KEY IN HER POCKET.]

ORA: The shop is closed.

KEMI: I will reopen it at six.

### Tag

[SFX: DOOR LOCK TURNS.]

ORA: The bakery is closed.

DOR: The music is off.

KEMI: I can hear the lock from outside.
