# The Cabinet of Borrowed Footsteps

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sound archives that preserve ordinary noises with museum reverence and then make them impossible to retrieve*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Archivist Doma, a macaque; conservator Pell, a gibbon; visitor Rill, a capuchin; curator Fen, a lemur.

[SFX: QUIET ROOM TONE. A KEY TURNS IN A HEAVY CABINET. A DRAWER SLIDES OUT, THEN STOPS WITH A WOODEN THUNK.]

FEN: The archive is open. Please do not touch the recordings.

RILL: I only need the sound of my own footsteps. I recorded them last winter.

DOMA: Do you remember the file number?

RILL: It went tap, tap, squeak.

PELL: That describes at least nine hundred files.

RILL: The squeak was on the third step.

DOMA: Better. Was it a wooden stair, a stone stair or the portable demonstration stair?

RILL: I was going up to the attic.

FEN: We have four attic recordings.

[SFX: CARD DRAWER OPENS. CARDS FLICK QUICKLY, THEN STOP.] 

DOMA: Found one: Rill, west stair, winter. The file has been transferred to cylinder.

RILL: Can I hear it?

PELL: Certainly. First we must identify the cylinder's shelf.

RILL: Is it not in the cabinet?

PELL: The cabinet is arranged by texture, not by date.

FEN: We preserve each sound in its original environment.

[SFX: PELL OPENS A SECOND CABINET. A TINNY FOOTSTEP RECORDING PLAYS, THEN A DOOR CREAKS.] 

RILL: That is the attic door.

DOMA: No, that is the archive door. The microphones were placed too close together.

PELL: I can filter the hinge.

[SFX: FILTER SWITCH. THE CREAK DISAPPEARS, BUT THE FOOTSTEPS BECOME A LOW, DISTANT RUMBLE.] 

RILL: Now it sounds like someone walking through a tunnel.

DOMA: The filter has removed the stair's character.

FEN: We cannot remove the character. It is the point of the collection.

RILL: Then could we record my footsteps again?

DOMA: That would be a reproduction, not the original.

RILL: I am standing right here. I can walk.

[SFX: RILL TAKES THREE STEPS. THE THIRD SQUEAKS.] 

PELL: Excellent. That is the sound we need.

DOMA: It is too clean. The old file contains room hiss.

FEN: We can add the hiss from the attic recording.

[SFX: HISS LAYERED UNDER THE STEPS. A FAINT DOOR CREAK RETURNS.] 

RILL: The door is back.

PELL: It belongs to the environment.

RILL: It belongs to this building. I am trying to remember my steps, not the archive.

DOMA: Then let us remove only the door. Pell, use the short gate, not the broad filter.

[SFX: SMALL SWITCH. THE DOOR CREAK FADES. THE THIRD STEP SQUEAK REMAINS.]

RILL: There. That is my winter stair.

RILL: Can I have a copy for home?

DOMA: We can make one from the new recording. The original cylinder stays in the archive.

RILL: Will the copy have the door?

PELL: It will have the repaired door.

RILL: There is no repaired door in my memory.

FEN: Memories are not cataloged by door condition.

RILL: Mine is. The door creaked before I reached the top. I remember stopping, then hearing it behind me.

DOMA: That is a different sequence. We can record it as an alternate memory, not alter the original.

[SFX: THE ARCHIVIST SLIDES A SECOND CYLINDER FROM A LINED BOX. IT ROLLS A FRACTION OF AN INCH AND STOPS AGAINST A FELT PAD.]

PELL: The cylinder is fragile. We should label the copy before making it.

RILL: Label it 'attic steps with a door behind me.'

FEN: That is a long title.

RILL: It is a long stair.

DOMA: We'll use the full title on the drawer and a short code on the cylinder.

FEN: We should store the new recording beside the original.

RILL: Please label it so I can find it next winter.

DOMA: We will put the label on the drawer.

[SFX: PAPER LABEL PEELED. IT STICKS TO A GLOVE. PELL RUBS IT FREE AND PRESSES IT TO THE CABINET.]

PELL: The label says RILL, WEST STAIR, THIRD STEP.

FEN: And the original?

DOMA: Same shelf, under 'stairs that answer back.'

RILL: Does the cabinet have to answer?

[SFX: THE CABINET DOOR CREAKS. A SINGLE FOOTSTEP SQUEAK PLAYS FROM INSIDE.]

RILL: It does now.

FEN: The archive is closed. Please do not touch the recordings.

[SFX: KEY TURNS. THE CABINET SHUTS. RILL WALKS AWAY; THREE STEPS, THE THIRD SQUEAKS. THE DOOR CREAKS ONCE, THEN STOPS.]
