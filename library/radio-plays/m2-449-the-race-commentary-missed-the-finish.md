# The Race Commentary Missed the Finish

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live sports radio, breathless play-by-play, and commentators who keep calling the race before the finish marker is found*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A commentator declares a hill race finished at the wrong flag, while the actual runners are still circling the course behind the announcer's tent.

Cast: Commentator Beni, a baboon; cohost Lira, a macaque; marshal Roko, an orangutan; runner Fela, a gibbon; sound tech Piri, a capuchin.

[SFX: RUNNERS' FOOTSTEPS ON A GRAVEL PATH. CROWD CLAPS.]

BENI: The leaders have reached the blue flag. We have a winner!

ROKO: That is the water station.

LIRA: The finish flag is red.

BENI: The race has gained another lap.

ACT ONE

Beni is calling a community hill race from a table near the course. The blue flag marks the water station, while the finish line is around the next bend. Roko is a course marshal and confirms the runners are still moving. Piri's field microphone is pointed at the water station, so the commentary hears cups being filled and runners slowing down.

BENI: The leader is taking a drink.

LIRA: The leader is checking the route.

FELA: I am runner four. The finish is behind you, not beside the blue flag.

BENI: We are broadcasting from the scenic position.

LIRA: We are broadcasting from the wrong end.

Roko walks to the red flag and checks that the line is visible. A volunteer's jacket hangs over the marker. He removes it. Beni asks if that was a flag change. Roko says it was a jacket that had been blocking the finish sign.

ACT TWO

Piri moves the microphone toward the bend. The runners' steps fade, then return from the opposite side. Beni says they are beginning a second lap. Roko says the course is a single loop. Lira checks the printed route; the loop crosses the same gravel patch before reaching the finish. The footstep recording repeats because Piri's monitor is playing the delayed feed.

PIRI: I have the current sound and its delay.

BENI: The field has doubled.

LIRA: The monitor has doubled.

Piri mutes the return channel. The footsteps become one group. Fela calls from the path: the leaders are approaching the red flag now. Beni begins a finish call. Roko asks him to wait until the runners cross the marked line. Beni counts down anyway, reaching zero while the leaders are still ten meters away.

LIRA: They have not finished.

BENI: The countdown has.

Roko stands by the red line and signals the volunteers to keep the path clear. Fela crosses first, followed by two runners. The bell at the water station rings in the background. Beni nearly calls that the finish bell; Lira stops him. The official timekeeper reads the results from the line.

ACT TWO-B

The top three times are confirmed. Fela finished first, but only by two seconds. Beni asks whether the blue flag runner was second. Roko says no; that runner stopped for water and continued after the leaders. Lira corrects the order using the timekeeper's list. The crowd cheers at the actual finish, not at the station table.

FELA: I heard the announcer declare me finished before I crossed.

BENI: I was excited.

FELA: I was still running.

Piri repositions the microphone so it captures the line and not the delayed monitor. The finish bell is a small hand bell held by the marshal. Roko rings it once for the last runner, who completes the loop. Beni waits until the runner crosses before announcing that the race is complete.

ACT THREE

Lira reads the final results: Fela first, then Neri, then Odo. Roko confirms all runners are off the course. Beni thanks the marshals and describes the route accurately: one hill loop, one water station, one red finish line. Piri turns off the delayed feed. The sound mix now carries one set of footsteps, then applause from the finish.

Fela receives a cloth ribbon and a cup of water. She says the course was marked clearly once the jacket came off the flag. Roko takes the jacket to the lost-property table. Beni says the blue flag will remain at the water station and the red flag marks the end. No more winner is declared.

TAG

BENI: Now they have finished.

FINAL IMAGE: The red finish flag stands uncovered as the last pair of footsteps fades away.
