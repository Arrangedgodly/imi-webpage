# The Ferry Is on the Other Side

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio travel reports that narrate a short ferry crossing like an expedition*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST VERA, a macaque
REPORTER MOK, a gibbon
FERRY OPERATOR LUZ, a gorilla
CALLER NEM, a capuchin

[SFX: HARBOR WATER, ONE BELL, FERRY ENGINE IDLING.]

VERA: We are live at the inlet, where a ferry is preparing to cross to the east dock.

MOK: The far shore is nearly visible through the mist.

LUZ: It is clear. The dock is right there.

MOK: How long is the crossing?

LUZ: Four minutes.

### Scene One: Boarding

[FOOTSTEPS ON A RAMP. A BELL RINGS.]

VERA: The passengers are beginning their voyage.

NEM: I am carrying a toolbox to repair the east dock's sign.

MOK: How long have you been waiting?

NEM: Three minutes. The ferry was unloading a cart.

VERA: An obstacle before the crossing.

LUZ: The cart was just off the ramp. We have room for passengers now.

[A CHILD'S SCOOTER RATTLES UP THE RAMP. LUZ HOLDS THE GATE WHILE ITS RIDER BOARDS.]

MOK: The expedition has added a scooter.

NEM: The rider is going across to school.

[LUZ CHECKS THE RAMP, THEN SOUNDS THE BOARDING BELL. THE ENGINE STARTS WITHOUT DRAMA.]

MOK: What challenges do you expect?

NEM: I hope the ferry leaves on time.

[LUZ CLOSES THE GATE. THE ENGINE STARTS.]

LUZ: We are crossing now. Please stay behind the rail.

MOK: The water moves beneath us.

VERA: The ferry moves across the water.

[MOK HOLDS THE MICROPHONE TOWARD THE ENGINE. IT IS SO LOUD THAT HE CANNOT BE HEARD.] 

LUZ: Move the mic back. The motor is not the story.

### Scene Two: The Open Water

[SFX: ENGINE LOWERS. WATER LAPS AGAINST THE HULL.]

MOK: We have reached the midpoint.

LUZ: We are passing the buoy.

VERA: Caller Nem, what do you see?

NEM: The other dock. The sign is missing its top arrow.

MOK: A landmark erased.

NEM: The wind took it last night.

[LUZ POINTS TO THE SAFE EXIT. MOK TRIES TO GET A SHOT OF NEM'S TOOLBOX. THE BOX SLIDES A FEW INCHES ON THE DECK.] 

LUZ: Put it against the bench until we land.

NEM: Done.

VERA: The cargo is secured.

MOK: The crossing continues.

[THE FERRY HORN SOUNDS. A SEAGULL CALL ANSWERS.]

MOK: Another vessel has replied.

LUZ: That is the gull on the piling.

[THE WEATHER RADIO IN THE CABIN REPORTS A LIGHT BREEZE. MOK ASKS IF THEY SHOULD CALL THE CROSSING WINDY.]

MOK: The conditions are calm.

VERA: We have completed the crossing.

LUZ: It is calm enough to operate.

VERA: The report confirms a clear crossing.

MOK: Can we still call it an expedition?

NEM: You can call it a ferry ride.

MOK: We will use the local name.

VERA: The crossing is complete.

[THE FERRY BELL RINGS AGAIN, ANNOUNCING THE NEXT DEPARTURE.]

[THE GULL CALLS AGAIN. THE FERRY ENGINE MAKES A LOW THRUM UNDER IT.]

MOK: The bird has repeated its message.

LUZ: It wants the snack someone dropped on the deck.

[NEM PICKS UP A CRACKER AND PUTS IT IN THE BIN. THE GULL FLIES TO THE PILING.]

VERA: The wildlife report has ended.

### Scene Three: Arrival

[SFX: ENGINE SLOWS. GATE CLINKS OPEN.]

LUZ: East dock. Crossing complete.

NEM: I can repair the sign before the next trip.

MOK: The traveler has reached the far shore.

NEM: I live here.

VERA: We have reported the crossing, the missing arrow, and the gull.

[MOK PLAYS A DRAMATIC STING. THE FERRY BELL RINGS AT THE SAME TIME.] 

MOK: The bell has interrupted our ending.

LUZ: It means the next trip is boarding.

### Tag

[SFX: ENGINE STARTS AGAIN.]

VERA: The ferry is returning to the near shore.

MOK: An entirely new expedition.

LUZ: Same route.
