# Commentary on a Very Quiet Match

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio commentary, where announcers try to dramatize a slow-moving match of lawn bowls*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Commentator Pava, gibbon; analyst Odo, baboon; umpire Nara, macaque; player Seli, capuchin.

[SFX: FIELD AMBIENCE, DISTANT BIRD.] 

PAVA: We are live at the lawn bowls final. The green is still.

ODO: The players are still.

NARA: The match has not started.

PAVA: The tension is building.

Seli rolls a wooden bowl down the lane. It moves slowly and stops beside the jack.

[SFX: BOWL ROLLS, STOPS.]

PAVA: A bold approach.

ODO: The bowl has reached the target.

NARA: It is one point away.

Pava asks Odo to explain the rules. Odo says players roll their bowls toward a small target ball called the jack. The nearest bowl scores.

PAVA: Can the jack move?

NARA: Only if a bowl strikes it.

Seli measures the distance. Her bowl is closest. Odo says the next player may challenge.

PAVA: The contest is underway.

NARA: It began when the first bowl was rolled.

Odo predicts the second player's line. The bowl rolls wide, then stops short.

[SFX: BOWL ROLLS, GRASS RUSTLE.]

PAVA: The challenger has missed.

ODO: It is still a legal bowl.

NARA: The point remains with Seli.

Pava asks for a replay. Seli rolls the bowl again, but the umpire calls it practice. Odo says the replay has changed the match.

NARA: The score has not changed.

PAVA: The audience needs to know what might have happened.

ODO: We have two possible outcomes.

NARA: We have one actual result.

The next bowl taps the jack, moving it a few inches. Seli's original bowl is still closest, but the distance has changed.

[SFX: JACK TAPS.]

PAVA: The game has shifted.

ODO: The target moved.

NARA: That is allowed.

Pava asks whether the match is now tied. Nara measures each bowl with a small string. Seli is still closer by half a bowl-length.

ODO: A narrow lead.

PAVA: The smallest margin in the tournament.

Seli takes another turn. She rolls carefully; the bowl stops just short of the jack.

PAVA: She has protected her lead.

ODO: The bowl is not moving. It may be considering its options.

NARA: The bowl has stopped. The score is unchanged.

Pava asks if the players can hear the broadcast. Seli says they can, and that the commentary has made her want to roll too hard. Odo apologizes by whispering. His whisper is picked up by the studio microphone and booms through the field speaker.

[SFX: FEEDBACK HUM, THEN QUIET.]

PAVA: We regret the volume.

NARA: You can turn off the speaker.

PAVA: Then we cannot bring the audience into the moment.

ODO: The audience is already in the moment. It is listening.

Pava asks Seli to describe what she sees, but Seli says she must watch the bowl. The analyst fills the silence by predicting a dramatic rebound. Nothing rebounds. The bowl remains where it stopped.

ODO: The tension is in the measurement.

NARA: The measurement is in my hand.

Nara stretches her string between the jack and each bowl. Odo tries to estimate from the commentary booth, but his view is blocked by the flag. He leans left; the flag follows the breeze and blocks him again.

PAVA: Our analyst has lost sight of the match.

ODO: I can still hear it.

NARA: There is nothing to hear.

PAVA: That's the atmosphere.

Seli asks them to stop talking until her next roll. Pava agrees, then whispers into the microphone that the silence is intentional. Odo tries to say "quietly," but Nara points at the live meter. They both lean away from it and let the field sounds return.


ODO: She has placed a second bowl.

NARA: She has two points.

Pava's voice rises, but the players ask the commentators to be quieter. The bowls require concentration.

PAVA: We will call the final end in a whisper.

Odo whispers the score. Nara confirms it. Seli rolls once more; the bowl touches the jack and moves it toward her first bowl.

[SFX: WOODEN TAP, ROLL.]

NARA: The jack has moved. We measure again.

PAVA: This is the deciding moment.

ODO: Both bowls are close.

Nara measures. Seli's second bowl is now nearest.

NARA: Seli scores one point.

PAVA: She wins.

ODO: By a small distance.

NARA: By the rule.

Seli shakes hands with the other player. Odo asks if there will be a celebration. Pava says the audience may clap.

[SFX: MODEST APPLAUSE.]

PAVA: A thrilling finish.

NARA: A quiet finish.

ODO: The grass remains still.

[SFX: DISTANT BIRD, END THEME.]
