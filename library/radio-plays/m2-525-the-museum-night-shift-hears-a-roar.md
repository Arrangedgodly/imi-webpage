# The Museum Night Shift Hears a Roar

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: audio museum tour that confuses recorded effects with exhibits*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Guide Mara, a macaque; technician Odo, a gibbon; guard Vex, an orangutan; volunteer Pip, a capuchin; curator Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Museum gallery at night. Vent hum, followed by a low recorded roar.

VEX: There is a large animal in the fossil hall.

MARA: That's the audio button for the ancient reptile exhibit. It's supposed to play one roar when a visitor presses it.

ODO: It has been looping for three minutes. The button may be stuck.

PIP: I was testing the tour. I pressed it once, and it kept going.

SELI: Don't pull the speaker grille. The control box is behind the display and has a service latch.

VEX: The sound is coming from the wall. Could the fossil be alive?

MARA: The exhibit is a cast skeleton, and the roar is a recording. Let's keep the night tour calm while we fix it.

SFX: A second roar plays, then a click.

ODO: The playback trigger is spring-loaded. It should release when the visitor lets go.

PIP: My sleeve might be caught on the button. I was reaching for the label.

VEX: The creature has taken a volunteer.

PIP: My sleeve is caught on the display edge, not in the machine.

MARA: Hold still. I'll free the fabric without touching the control.

SFX: Cloth slides free. The roar stops.

ODO: It stopped when Pip's sleeve came off the trigger. I'll still inspect the latch before we reopen the audio.

SELI: Good. The button should be tested once, not held down through the whole story.

VEX: But the repeat gave the room an impressive atmosphere.

MARA: It made the next gallery sound as if a crocodile was following us.

SFX: Footsteps toward the service panel. A key turns.

ODO: The spring is clean. The button returns normally now.

PIP: Can I test it from the front this time?

SELI: One press, then let it return.

SFX: Button presses. One roar. Silence.

PIP: It stopped.

VEX: The beast has gone quiet.

MARA: The recording has completed. That's what it was designed to do.

ODO: I'll add a small mark beside the button to show where a finger should press. The label is still readable.

SELI: No mark on the fossil, please. It is a replica, but we keep the gallery clean.

VEX: Could the guide say, "The animal stirs" before the audio plays?

MARA: The guide can say, "Press once to hear the reconstruction." That tells visitors what they're hearing.

PIP: I'll add the instruction to the recorded tour tomorrow, after the curator approves it.

SFX: Recorder starts, a clean voice says: "Press once to hear the sound reconstruction." Recorder clicks off.

SELI: Approved. The service panel is locked and the button is free.

VEX: Then the night guard can return to his patrol.

MARA: The night guard can return to his post without reporting a living fossil.


MARA: The night tour will start again from the entrance. Vex, read the new label aloud from where a visitor stands.

VEX: "Press once to hear the sound reconstruction." Clear. Then the next panel identifies the skeleton as a cast.

SELI: Keep the path open between the two displays. Visitors sometimes step backward when the roar plays.

PIP: I'll place the floor marker beside the button, not across the walkway.

SFX: Marker unrolls and is pressed flat at the gallery edge.

ODO: The button gives one sound, and the audio recorder carries the instruction. Both controls are back to idle.

VEX: Could we play the roar as our program sign-off?

MARA: Only once, and after I say it is a recording.

SELI: Correct. You can use my voice: the exhibit is a reconstruction, and the sound is recorded.

SFX: Button pressed. One roar. Silence.

VEX: The night tour is ready. I will patrol the hall without narrating the walls.

VEX: Was that the exhibit?

SELI: No. That was the building settling. The exhibit has a label.
