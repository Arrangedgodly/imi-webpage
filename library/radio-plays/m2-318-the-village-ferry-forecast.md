# The Village Ferry Forecast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel broadcasts that promise seamless journeys while every small transfer becomes a production*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Announcer Miri, a gibbon; ferry keeper Sol, a macaque; traveler Pava, a capuchin; producer Odo, a baboon.

[SFX: WATER LAPPING, DOCK BELL.]

MIRI: Welcome to the village ferry report. The crossing takes one minute.

PAVA: The boat is tied to the dock.

SOL: We are waiting for one passenger.

ODO: Our travel segment is underway.

ACT ONE

Pava steps onto the ferry carrying a small basket. Sol asks her to sit in the middle so the boat stays level. Odo asks if she can sit near the microphone instead. Sol says the microphone can move; the passenger cannot.

Miri reports that the boarding process is smooth. Pava's basket catches on the rope. Sol frees it. Odo asks for a retake to capture the "moment of embarkation."

PAVA: I am already aboard.

MIRI: We will call that pre-embarkation.

ACT TWO

The ferry pushes off. Odo describes the water as a broad blue corridor. Sol says it is a narrow channel. The boat drifts a little sideways because Pava leans to look at the shore. Sol asks her to sit back in the center.

MIRI: A gentle course correction.

SOL: A passenger shifted.

Odo tries to interview Pava about the scenic view. She says the other dock is visible. Miri asks if the ferry has a refreshment service. Sol says the trip is too short to serve anything. Odo produces a tiny cup and offers it to Pava. It tips as the boat rocks.

[SFX: CUP TAPS DECK.]

Pava catches the cup. Sol ties the basket handle to the seat so it won't slide. Odo calls it a safety feature. Sol says it is a knot.

The boat rocks again as a small wake passes. Pava's basket slides toward the edge, but the handle is tied to the seat. She reaches for it and shifts her weight. Sol asks her to sit back in the middle. Odo calls it the crossing's most dangerous moment. Sol says it is the only moment that required a correction. Miri asks if the return trip will have the same scenic route. Sol says the ferry follows the same short channel both ways. Pava notes that the water looks different from the other dock. Odo begins a poem about perspective. Miri asks him to keep the microphone away from the rope. The boat reaches the dock, and Sol ties it before Pava steps off. She unties the basket and thanks him. The trip stays within the promised minute, though the travel report has enough material for a full program.

Before the final announcement, Pava asks Sol if the tied basket is part of the safety procedure. Sol says no, but it is a useful way to keep a passenger's things from sliding. Odo tries to call it "shore-to-shore cargo security." Miri asks him to say "knot" instead. He does, and the ferry makes its return crossing quietly.

At the dock, Miri asks Pava to lift the basket so Sol can check that the knot is clear of the rail. Pava lifts it; the loose handle no longer catches. She sets it back in the middle. Sol says the boat is ready, and Odo records the rope as part of the crossing rather than a crisis.

ACT THREE

The ferry reaches the opposite dock. Miri announces an arrival. Pava steps onto the planks and unties her basket. Sol secures the boat before unloading the next passenger.

PAVA: One minute, just as promised.

ODO: We have several scenic details.

SOL: The crossing was one minute.

MIRI: Our report includes the rope, the cup, and the knot.

TAG

The dock bell rings.

MIRI: The ferry is ready for its return trip.

PAVA: I can walk back.

SOL: Then this is the end of your journey.

[SFX: WATER LAPPING.]
