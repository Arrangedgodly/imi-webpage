# The Midnight Repair Line

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in repair radio convention, where listeners ask for quick fixes and a caller's squeaking cart is solved by changing its load*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host SELA, lemur; mechanic ODO, baboon; caller TAVI, macaque; producer PAVA, gibbon.

[SFX: TELEPHONE RING, RADIO THEME.] 

SELA: This is The Midnight Repair Line. Tavi, what is making that sound?

TAVI: My cart squeaks every time I push it.

[SFX: CART SQUEAKS TWICE.] 

ODO: Can you lift the front wheel?

TAVI: I am carrying three baskets.

PAVA: Put the baskets down first.

Tavi unloads them. The cart stops squeaking. Sela asks if the repair is complete. Odo says the load may have been uneven. Tavi says one basket is heavier because it holds wet leaves.

ODO: The wheel may need oil.

PAVA: Or the baskets need to be balanced.

Tavi reloads them evenly. The cart moves quietly. He asks if he should still oil the wheel. Odo says to inspect it first. Tavi turns the cart over. The wheel spins freely; a twig is caught in the frame.

[SFX: TWIG PULLED FREE.] 

SELA: A tiny branch was the mechanic.

TAVI: It was carrying the squeak.

Pava says not to oil the wheel until they know it needs it. Odo agrees and asks Tavi to test the cart with the wet leaves on the bottom and lighter baskets above. The cart rolls quietly.

A second caller, Miri, reports a door that will not close. Odo asks if anything is blocking it. Miri says a basket is nearby. She moves the basket and the door closes. Pava asks if the show can provide a more complicated solution. Sela says the simple one is the right one.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES.] 

Tavi calls back. The cart squeaks once more. Odo asks him to stop moving. The sound is a bird chirping from the basket. Tavi opens the top basket and finds a small bird perched among the leaves. He sets the basket down gently and lets the bird fly out through the open window.

ODO: The cart is quiet.

TAVI: The leaves are lighter now.

Tavi asks Odo to explain how he found the twig. Odo says he listened near the axle rather than guessing at the bearing. Sela repeats the lesson for callers: first unload the cart, then inspect the wheel, then remove only what is caught. Pava writes the steps in large print for the studio board.

Tavi asks if the show should play the squeak again. Odo says no; the cart is fixed. Sela asks the caller to describe the load after rearranging it. Tavi says the wet leaves ride low, the baskets are balanced, and the wheel turns freely.

Pava tests the cart off mic. It rolls over a floor seam without a squeak. She asks if that counts as a second repair. Odo says it is a confirmation test. Sela distinguishes the two on air: the repair removed the obstruction; the test confirms the wheel is quiet. The audience hears the difference between the original squeak and the smooth roll.

The line goes silent for a moment. Pava checks the board, then signals that the next caller is ready. Sela asks listeners not to oil a wheel just because a sound is unfamiliar. First locate the sound; then check the part.



Tavi checks the written steps before signing off. Pava circles the words 'inspect first' so callers can find them in the transcript.

Sela asks if the bird should get a repair credit. Pava says the show has no bird budget. The theme music begins. Tavi pushes the cart once more. No squeak, only a soft wheel roll.

SELA: We will keep the line open for repairs that need a listener.

ODO: And inspect before applying oil.

PAVA: Good night.
