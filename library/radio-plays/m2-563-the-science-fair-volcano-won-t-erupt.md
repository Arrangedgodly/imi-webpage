# The Science Fair Volcano Won't Erupt

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: breaking-news science broadcasts that treat a quiet demonstration as a disaster*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; teacher Mara, a macaque; student Pip, a capuchin; lab aide Odo, a gibbon; judge Nari, a lemur.

SFX: School gym. Folding chairs scrape; a small plastic cup sets down.

VEX: Emergency at the science fair. The volcano has refused to erupt.

MARA: It is a model for a classroom demonstration. The ingredients are on the table, and no one has started the reaction.

ODO: Pip is waiting for the judge to clear the area. Goggles are on the table.

PIP: My project shows how an acid and a base can make bubbles. It isn't a real volcano.

NARI: I'm the judge. The table is clear, and I have my safety glasses.

VEX: The crowd has gathered for the explosion.

MARA: It is a small reaction in a tray. There will be no explosion.

PIP: I need baking soda, vinegar, and a little dish soap. I can add them after Nari gives the signal.

VEX: The volcano's silence is a statement.

NARI: The student is following a procedure. Let him explain the project before he starts.

ODO: The spill cloth is under the tray. I will keep the rinse bottle nearby.

MARA: Pip, read the amounts from your card. Don't add extra for a bigger effect.

PIP: One spoon of baking soda, one spoon of soap, then ten milliliters of vinegar.

VEX: Could we use a bucket?

PIP: No. The amount is small because this is a model and we are measuring the reaction.

NARI: Ready. Pip and Odo have put on their goggles, as have I. The audience is behind the tape.

SFX: Small pour, then fizzing bubbles.

VEX: It is erupting! That needs a bigger sound.

SFX: Vex raises the gym microphone; feedback squeals. His startled elbow knocks the paper mountain across the judging table.

NARI: The score sheet is under the model.

PIP: I will hold the tray steady while you lift the paper mountain.

MARA: Mic down, Vex. The reaction stays in its small tray; nobody adds more vinegar.

SFX: Microphone fades; paper model lifted and set upright.

VEX: The volcano has caused a second incident.

NARI: Your microphone did. I am ready to judge Pip's actual project now.

PIP: Bubbles are forming and carrying the soap upward. The reaction is slowing now.

ODO: Tray stayed on the table. No liquid crossed the edge.

VEX: The mountain has survived.

NARI: Project is complete. My question is why the bubbles formed.

PIP: The vinegar reacts with the baking soda and releases carbon dioxide. The soap traps some gas as foam.

MARA: Thank you. Keep the tray still until the fizz ends, then Odo will dispose of it as the teacher instructed.

ODO: The pouring is finished. Pip, keep the goggles on until the fizz ends and we can move the tray.

VEX: The volcano has gone dormant.

MARA: The reaction is finished. Pip will explain the result to the judge; no second pour for a louder sound.

NARI: My scoring sheet asks whether the materials were measured and the explanation matches what happened.

PIP: My measured amounts are on the card. Vex asked for a bucket, but I used the original small quantities. His sound was larger than my reaction.

VEX: Could a larger reaction earn a higher score?

NARI: No. A safe, clear project scores better than an uncontrolled spill.

ODO: The tray stayed inside the spill cloth. I'll move it to the cleanup table after the fizz ends.

MARA: Pip, tell the audience what would happen if you used too much vinegar.

PIP: It would make more gas, but the foam could overflow. That wouldn't prove anything new.

VEX: The drama lies in restraint.

MARA: The measurement lies in the recipe card. Keep the card with the project until judging is complete.

SFX: Paper card clipped to the display; tray rests on mat.

NARI: I can read the amounts and see the result. No emergency response is needed.
SFX: Fizz fades to a few small pops.

VEX: Our live report has documented a contained event.

MARA: It has documented a planned demonstration. The gym remains open.

TAG

NARI: The project wins for clear measurements.

VEX: No award for dramatic lava?

NARI: No.
