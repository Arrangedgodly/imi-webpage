# The Answer Is Probably

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in radio quiz contests*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Mel, a macaque; caller Boro, a baboon; caller Sif, a gibbon; and rules keeper Tavi, a capuchin who reads fine print aloud.

[SFX: FAST QUIZ THEME, ONE NOTE TOO HIGH.] 

MEL: Welcome to The Answer Is Probably, the quiz where callers have ten seconds to answer and twenty minutes to learn the rules. Tonight's prize is a basket of fruit. Rules keeper Tavi, is the basket complete?

TAVI: It has four apples, two pears, and a note that says the banana is for display only.

MEL: Why?

TAVI: The sponsor's logo is printed on it.

MEL: We have a caller. Boro, ready?

BORO, PHONE: I was ready before the theme started.

MEL: Question one. How many legs does a spider have?

BORO: Eight.

MEL: Correct. You have one point.

TAVI: The rules say points are awarded after the full question.

MEL: I read the full question.

TAVI: The question is, "How many legs does a spider have, if none have been lost?"

BORO: Eight.

MEL: Still correct. Question two. Which animal stores food in cheek pouches?

BORO: A chipmunk.

MEL: Correct. Two points.

TAVI: The prize rules say one point per correct answer.

MEL: Two points is more celebratory.

TAVI: It is also disqualifying.

[SFX: BUZZER.] 

MEL: We are pausing to check the score. Caller Sif, you are next. Your line is clear.

SIF, PHONE: I can hear the basket rustling.

TAVI: That is the fruit display.

MEL: Question one. Why do some monkeys groom one another?

SIF: To remove dirt and parasites, and to maintain social bonds.

MEL: Correct. You have three points.

TAVI: How did she get three?

MEL: There were three parts to the answer.

TAVI: The question had one question mark.

MEL: Questions can be generous.

SIF: I would like the basket.

TAVI: The rules say callers take turns. Boro has two answers, Sif has one, and no caller has won yet.

MEL: We have a tiebreaker. What is the banana for?

BORO: Eating.

SIF: Display.

MEL: Tavi?

TAVI: The banana is a prop. The prize is the apples and pears.

MEL: Correct. The rules keeper wins the basket.

TAVI: I was not a caller.

MEL: The rules do not say staff cannot win.

TAVI: They say "callers only."

MEL: Then the basket goes to the caller who answered correctly most often. Tavi, which caller was that?

TAVI: Sif had one correct answer. Boro had two, if we count the revised wording. Boro wins.

MEL: Congratulations, Boro. You have two points and the basket.

BORO: Can I share it with Sif?

TAVI: The rules allow sharing after the score is final.

SIF: I'll carry the score sheet. Boro can carry the basket.

MEL: That sounds like a tie.

TAVI: It is not. Boro won. Sif is helping him carry the prize.

BORO: The basket has room for both of us.

[SFX: BASKET LIFTS. FRUIT RUSTLES.]

MEL: The display banana stays on its stand.

BORO: Does it get a point?

TAVI: No.

MEL: Does it count as part of the fruit prize?

TAVI: No.

BORO: Does the sponsor get to keep it?

TAVI: The sponsor can take it home after the show.

BORO: Then it's the only contestant who knows the rules.

MEL: Next week, one edible prize, one clear question, and the rules printed before the theme.

TAVI: I will read them before the show.

MEL: That's a good answer.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC STARTS. A BANANA WOBBLES ON ITS PAINTED STAND.]

MEL: We have another question. If the stand moves, does the banana get a point?

TAVI: No. The banana is an object.

BORO: The basket is an object too.

TAVI: The basket is the prize.

SIF: Can I hold the stand still while Boro takes the basket?

TAVI: Yes.

[SFX: STAND SETTLES, BASKET LIFTS.]

TAVI: Don't touch the banana.

BORO: I didn't. The stand answered for me.
