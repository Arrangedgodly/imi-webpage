# The Library's Whispering Hour

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: wellness broadcasts that sell silence as a premium experience*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Seli, a lemur; librarian Mara, a macaque; technician Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin; sponsor Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Library reading room. A clock ticks. A chair creaks.

SELI: Welcome to Quiet Hour, brought to you by Vex's Premium Silence.

VEX: For one small fee, we remove all unnecessary sound.

PIP: Does that include the clock?

ODO: We can unplug it.

MARA: The clock tells the readers when the library closes.

VEX: Then it's necessary. We keep it and charge extra.

SELI: Today's demonstration is a minute of complete quiet.

SFX: A long pause. The clock ticks. Someone turns a page.

VEX: That page turn was not in the package.

PIP: I am reading.

VEX: Read more softly.

MARA: A reader has to turn pages.

VEX: We can provide padded pages.

ODO: They would be difficult to write on.

SELI: Our premium room has thick curtains, felt on the chairs, and a sign asking visitors not to breathe dramatically.

PIP: I can breathe normally.

VEX: That's not the premium way.

MARA: What sound are we removing first?

VEX: The chair.

SFX: Odo wraps the chair legs with felt. Mara sits. The chair no longer creaks.

MARA: Good. Now I can get up without waking the room.

PIP: I need to ask where the atlas is.

VEX: Please submit your question on a silent card.

PIP: I don't know where the cards are.

SELI: Whisper the question.

VEX: Whispering is a higher tier.

MARA: Pip, the atlas is on the low shelf beside the window.

SFX: Pip crosses the room. A book slides from the shelf and lands with a thump.

VEX: That is a premium disturbance.

ODO: The book is large. It has a map of the entire valley.

PIP: I found it. The north path is marked in pencil.

MARA: That's my note for the trail crew. It means the bridge is out.

VEX: Can the library announce that?

SELI: Only if we use the public-address voice.

VEX: The public address is not silent.

MARA: Someone might use that path today. We need to tell them.

VEX: I can sell an emergency whisper.

PIP: The page is heavy. I'll carry it to the front desk.

SFX: Pip carries the atlas. Pages flap; the marked page sticks out.

ODO: If we leave it open, the warning is visible.

MARA: We'll put it beside the exit.

VEX: That makes the room more useful and less exclusive.

SELI: The Quiet Hour demonstration is over. Readers can whisper, turn pages, and ask for directions without buying a tier.

ODO: The felt is not the only sound we can manage. The door squeaks every time someone enters.

MARA: The door is how readers get in.

VEX: Could we ask them to wait until the silence package is ready?

PIP: Someone is coming in now.

SFX: Door squeaks. A small bell rings.

SELI: The visitor is holding a book return.

VEX: We could cushion the slot.

ODO: If we fill the slot, books cannot be returned.

MARA: Oil the hinge, keep the bell, and leave the return slot open. Quiet should not make the library unusable.

PIP: The visitor can hear that.

SFX: Book slides into the return box.

VEX: The box has a satisfying thump.

MARA: Then we will use a padded base, not a padded book.

ODO: I have a folded cloth.

SFX: Cloth placed in box. Another book drops with a softer thump.

SELI: The return is quieter and the book is still intact.

VEX: Can I at least charge for the felt?

MARA: The library owns it.

VEX: That sound is complimentary.

PIP: So is the way out.
