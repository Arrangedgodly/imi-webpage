# The Museum Guide Lost the Room

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio tours, numbered listening stops, and a guide who insists every room is a turning point*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
GUIDE VOICE, a macaque
CURATOR IMANI, a capuchin
VISITOR TOBI, a gibbon
VISITOR KET, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: MUSEUM DOOR OPENS. SMALL AUDIO PLAYER BEEPS.]

GUIDE VOICE: Welcome to the Canopy Museum audio tour. Press the number beside each exhibit, then stand where the floor arrows suggest.

TOBI: The arrows point to the gift shop.

IMANI: The gallery is being repainted. We moved the exhibits.

KET: Did you move the arrows?

IMANI: We moved some of them.

[SFX: PLAYER BEEPS: STOP ONE.]

GUIDE VOICE: Stop one: the ancient ceremonial bowl. Imagine it filled with fruit at a royal feast.

TOBI: The pedestal is empty.

IMANI: The bowl is in the repair room. A visitor leaned on the display and it developed a wobble.

GUIDE VOICE: Consider the bowl's absence. What does emptiness tell us about power?

KET: Someone carried the bowl away.

TOBI: Is the repair room on the tour?

IMANI: No, but the door is open.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS, DOOR CREAKS.]

GUIDE VOICE: Stop two: the ceremonial bowl is not located here. Continue to the carved paddle.

KET: The paddle is on the bench.

IMANI: We brought the bench here because the paddle's stand broke.

TOBI: This museum keeps moving the objects into reach.

GUIDE VOICE: The paddle represents a journey across water, but not necessarily this journey.

KET: The floor arrows now point back to the bowl.

IMANI: Someone reversed them.

TOBI: I thought the arrows were part of the exhibit.

[SFX: PLAYER BEEPS: STOP THREE.]

GUIDE VOICE: Stop three: the portrait of the explorer. Notice the confident posture and the map held upside down.

KET: The portrait is facing the wall.

IMANI: The wall is being painted. It needs to dry.

GUIDE VOICE: The explorer turns away from us, perhaps contemplating a distant horizon.

TOBI: He is facing a wet wall.

IMANI: Please do not touch it.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS IN DISTANCE.]

IMANI: The gallery attendant is calling. The floor arrows lead visitors into the paint area.

KET: We should turn them around.

TOBI: I can stand at the corner and redirect people.

GUIDE VOICE: Stop four: the museum's directional system. It has been revised many times and is an artifact in its own right.

IMANI: That is not an exhibit.

GUIDE VOICE: The tour app has just added it.

[SFX: PHONE BEEPS.]

KET: It says, "Continue through the wet paint for a deeper experience."

IMANI: I will disconnect the app.

TOBI: While you disconnect it, I will move the arrows to the open gallery.

[SFX: PLAYER POWERS DOWN. CLOCK TICKS THROUGH THE REPAIR INTERVAL, FOLLOWED BY TOOLS, FOOTSTEPS, AND IMANI RECORDING AN OFFLINE TOUR ON THE DESK MICROPHONE.]

IMANI: Good. The bowl is back on its repaired pedestal. The paddle can go on its stand, and the portrait can face the room again.

[SFX: BOWL SET DOWN. PADDLE STAND CLICKS. FRAME TURNS.]

GUIDE VOICE: New stop five: the gallery has been restored to its original arrangement.

KET: The arrows point to the exit.

TOBI: That seems useful.

IMANI: I finally replaced them all.

GUIDE VOICE: Thank you for taking the Canopy Museum audio tour. Our gift shop is in the direction of the gift shop.

KET: At last, a location that agrees with the recording.

[SFX: PLAYER BEEPS: STOP THREE. FRAME CREAKS.]

TOBI: It is looping the old stop again.

IMANI: I left the old stop in the offline recording. The portrait is facing the room.

[SFX: IMANI PRESSES RECORD, SPEAKS THE REPLACEMENT STOP, THEN PLAYS IT BACK.]

KET: Then the tour can say exactly that.

GUIDE VOICE: Stop six: the portrait is facing the room. Please do not turn it again.

TOBI: The player has stopped looping.

IMANI: And the exit arrow is still pointing to the exit.

TOBI: I will take the extra arrows down before the next group arrives.

[SFX: TWO SIGNS LIFT FROM WALL. PLAYER BEEPS ONCE. DOOR OPENS. THEME OUT.]
