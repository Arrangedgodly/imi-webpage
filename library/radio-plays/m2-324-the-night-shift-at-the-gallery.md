# The Night Shift at the Gallery

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overnight mystery programs that make routine security rounds sound like a haunting*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Guard Seli, a lemur; curator Odo, a baboon; night technician Piri, a gibbon; announcer Kito, a macaque.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS, KEYS JINGLE.]

KITO: At midnight, a shadow moved through the sculpture gallery.

SELI: It was me.

ODO: The report says "unknown figure."

PIRI: The report was written before the lights came on.

ACT ONE

Seli walks the gallery with a flashlight. Odo asks her to repeat the route for the recording. She passes a bronze bowl; the light casts a large shadow on the wall.

KITO: The figure has returned.

SELI: I am still here.

Odo says the archive needs a dramatic account. Seli says she checks the doors and windows. Piri asks if any are open. They are locked.

ACT TWO

The clock ticks. Piri hears a tap near the display shelf. Seli shines the flashlight; the sculpture's label is loose and taps the glass when the air vent turns on.

[SFX: LABEL TAPS GLASS.]

KITO: A second sound.

ODO: The gallery has answered.

PIRI: It is a label.

Odo tries to record a whispering voice. The vent makes a low hum. Kito asks if the building is speaking. Piri turns off the vent for maintenance; the hum stops, and the room warms.

SELI: Put the vent back on. The exhibit needs climate control.

Piri switches it back. The label taps again. Seli uses a small clip to secure the label.

ACT TWO-B

Piri checks the gallery door while Seli holds the flashlight steady. The lock is secure. Kito narrates the process as a search for hidden intruders. Seli asks him to stop saying "intruder" when the only person present is the guard. Odo finds a second shadow near the tall sculpture. It moves when he walks. The flashlight beam has crossed the bronze bowl and doubled its outline. Seli steps to the side; the shape changes again. Piri explains how the gallery lights cast overlapping silhouettes. Odo wants to preserve the mystery for the recording. Seli turns on the low work light, revealing the empty floor and the sculpture's stand. The shadow vanishes. Kito says the gallery has been cleared. Piri points out that they never suspected a real person; the log simply used a vague word. He replaces "unknown figure" with "guard on round" and secures the paper label with a clip.

Seli checks the remaining doors. Each lock clicks once, then holds. Odo asks whether she can make the clicks sound more ominous. Seli uses the keys normally; the sound remains a small jingle. Piri says the night round is complete. Kito asks if the crew should stay for another unexplained noise. Seli says they should finish the checklist and go home. Odo agrees to label the recording "routine patrol."

Kito asks what will happen if the label loosens again. Piri says the clip is secure, and Seli will inspect it on tomorrow's round. Odo writes "possible sequel" in his script, then crosses it out when Seli hands him the actual checklist.

Seli switches off the flashlight. The gallery is quiet, and the bronze bowl stays on its plinth.

Piri signs the log, and the guard completes the round.

Seli turns the key quietly in the lock.

Tomorrow brings another round.

ACT THREE

Kito records the final report: the shadow came from the flashlight, the tapping from a loose label, and the hum from the vent. Odo asks if the museum is still mysterious. Seli says the locked doors remain locked.

SELI: I am going to the break room.
