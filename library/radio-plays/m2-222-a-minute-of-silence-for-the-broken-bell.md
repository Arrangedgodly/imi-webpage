# A Minute of Silence for the Broken Bell

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio memorial broadcast convention, where a community tries to observe silence for a bell that was removed for repair*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Announcer PAVA, gibbon; bell keeper TAVI, macaque; repairer ODO, baboon; listener MIRI, capuchin.

[SFX: STUDIO HUM, NO THEME.] 

PAVA: The village bell is silent while it is repaired. Our broadcast will observe one minute of silence in its honor.

TAVI: Should I start the clock?

PAVA: No. We will know when a minute has passed.

ODO: The clock is right behind your microphone.

PAVA: Then move the microphone.

Odo shifts it. The stand squeaks.

PAVA: We will begin again after the stand settles.

MIRI: Listener Miri on line one says she can hear us settling.

PAVA: Tell her we are not on the air.

MIRI: The red light is on.

PAVA: We are on the air. Tell her to remain silent with us.

MIRI: She says she called because the bell keeper told her the bell would ring at noon.

TAVI: I said we would test it at noon. The bell is still on this table.

ODO: And its clapper has a burr. If I fix it, we can return it to the tower.

PAVA: The memorial minute comes first. Please put down your file.

Odo lays it down. The file rolls off the table and lands on a felt pad.

[SFX: SOFT METAL TAP.] 

PAVA: That was a sound.

ODO: A very small sound.

PAVA: The silence is not measured in decibels.

TAVI: What is it measured in?

PAVA: Respect.

MIRI: Miri says the village clock ticks loudly enough to be disrespectful.

PAVA: Ask her to stop the clock.

MIRI: She says she is calling from the village square, where she cannot reach it.

TAVI: That is the clock in the tower. We could hear it from here if the bell were installed.

PAVA: We are beginning the minute now. No one speaks, moves paper, repairs bells, or makes respectful suggestions.

MIRI: The caller says the village has brought cushions.

PAVA: Cushions are silent.

MIRI: She says they brought a brass band too.

PAVA: Ask the band to wait.

TAVI: They are already tuning.

[SFX: DISTANT BRASS NOTE, CUT OFF.]

ODO: I can hear the tuba being respectfully restrained.

PAVA: The broadcast is dedicated to the bell. It cannot become a concert about the bell.

[SFX: STILLNESS. CLOCK TICKING. A BIRD CALL OUTSIDE.] 

Pava raises one finger. Odo reaches for his file, then stops. Tavi's sleeve brushes the bell. A tiny rattle sounds.

PAVA: Was that the bell?

ODO: Its clapper, asking to be repaired.

PAVA: We are observing silence for the bell. Please do not let it interrupt.

TAVI: It's difficult to honor a bell by preventing it from speaking.

The station phone rings once. Everyone looks at the red light. Miri holds up the handset, listening.

MIRI: The caller says the village has gathered. They want to know if noon is still noon without the bell.

PAVA: Tell them the clock can answer.

MIRI: The clock is not audible on the broadcast.

ODO: I can repair the clapper in ten seconds.

PAVA: That would end our minute.

ODO: It would end the silence. The minute would continue on the clock.

Tavi checks the wall clock without speaking and holds up five fingers, then four. The minute is nearly done. Pava nods. They wait through the final ticks.

[SFX: ONE CLOCK CHIME, SMALL AND CLEAR.] 

PAVA: The minute is complete. Odo, repair the bell.

ODO: I could have done it in ten seconds.

PAVA: I know. That is why I made it a minute.

Odo files the burr; Tavi carries the bell toward the door. Miri reports the village is still waiting, now listening to the clock.

[SFX: BELL RINGS ONCE FROM OUTSIDE. THE CLOCK TICKS UNDER IT.] 

TAVI: They heard noon.

PAVA: And our minute of silence?

MIRI: The bell says it was just long enough.
