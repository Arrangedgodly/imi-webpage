# The Snail Race Has a Photo Finish

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcasts that stretch a tiny race into a season finale*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Commentator Seli, a lemur; timer Odo, a gibbon; racer Pip, a capuchin; sponsor Vex, an orangutan; marshal Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Garden path. Small crowd gathers. A bell rings.

SELI: The garden snail race begins at the lettuce leaf.

VEX: Our finish camera is ready.

MARA: The snails are in separate lanes. No one should touch them.

PIP: My racer is facing the wrong way.

ODO: The start bell hasn't rung.

VEX: The cameras are rolling. We need a favorite.

MARA: Let the snails move at their own pace.

SFX: Bell rings. Leaves rustle faintly.

SELI: They are underway.

VEX: Pip's snail has the inside lane.

PIP: It hasn't moved.

ODO: It has extended one tentacle.

VEX: A strategic probe.

MARA: The other snail is moving toward the shade.

SELI: The course is sunny at this end.

PIP: We could move the finish line to the cool patch.

VEX: That changes the race.

MARA: The snails need shade. The course is for them, not the broadcast.

SFX: Leaves placed as a shaded tunnel.

ODO: Both snails are moving again.

SELI: One has reached the finish leaf.

VEX: Photo review!

ODO: The camera shutter is stuck.

SFX: Camera button clicks twice.

PIP: They are both at the leaf.

MARA: Then they can share it.

MARA: Clear the lanes now. The next group is bringing a magnifier, not a trophy.

ODO: I'll return the leaf pieces to the garden bed.

SELI: The official result is a tie, with both racers safe in the shade.

VEX: I'll prepare a dramatic scorecard for next year.

PIP: Keep it far from the lettuce.

SELI: The next heat begins after the garden is watered.

MARA: We'll move the shade leaves back to the bed.

ODO: Both snails are safe and off the course.

PIP: The crowd can leave without stepping on the finish line.

VEX: I will announce the tie to the sponsor.

ODO: I will reset the camera on the chalk mark, not closer to the snails.

MARA: The finish leaf stays in place until they move away.

SELI: Spectators, take the path around the garden bed.

PIP: I can carry the shade cloth back to the shed.

VEX: The award ceremony will be very short.

MARA: There is no award ceremony.

VEX: A brief announcement, then.

SELI: The announcement is: tied race, clear path, lettuce intact.

ODO: The camera is packed and the audience can leave by the stepping stones.

PIP: The snails still have the leaf.

TAG

SELI: The two snails are still side by side at the leaf.

ODO: The camera is working again. It shows their shells crossing the chalk finish at the same time.

VEX: We need a tiebreaker.

MARA: The rules say the first to the leaf, and they reached it together.

PIP: We can mark the race a tie.

VEX: The season needs a champion.

ODO: We can award each snail a leaf and keep the larger prize for next year.

SFX: Two leaves placed beside the lane.

SELI: The snails do not appear to be collecting awards.

PIP: They're eating the finish line.

MARA: Move the camera back so it doesn't block the path.

SFX: Tripod legs fold.

VEX: The audience will want a replay.

ODO: The camera missed the actual crossing. We should not fake one.

PIP: I saw both snails reach the lettuce together.

SELI: Then the call is a shared win.

VEX: Can we call it the slowest tie in history?

MARA: We don't know every race in history.

VEX: The slowest tie in this garden.

ODO: That we can confirm.

SFX: Leaves rustle as the snails continue eating.

SFX: Lettuce crunches.

VEX: The winner is dinner.

PIP: No, the snails are.
