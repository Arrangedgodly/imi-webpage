# The Stairwell Is Listening

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Paranormal investigation podcasts with skeptical hosts*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[THEME: eerie wind, a coconut tapped against a radiator, faint elevator bell.] 

HOST LUZ: Welcome to The Unexplained at Work, the show that investigates strange events without leaving the building. I'm Luz. That's our sound engineer and skeptic, Benji. Tonight, the seventh-floor stairwell has been whispering employees' names after 6 p.m.

BENJI: It has a motion sensor and a door that needs oil.

LUZ: Two facts can coexist with a ghost, Benji.

BENJI: That sentence is why we keep the show under budget.

[SFX: STAIRWELL DOOR CREAKS. FOOTSTEPS ASCEND.] 

LUZ: We're at the landing between six and seven. The lights just flickered.

BENJI: They flicker when someone opens the door. The switch is on the other side.

LUZ: And did you touch it?

BENJI: No.

LUZ: The recorders caught a voice saying "Luz."

BENJI: I said, "Luz, your mic is brushing your scarf."

LUZ: We have a second witness. Security guard Otis, what did you hear?

OTIS: At 6:13, someone whispered "Otis." I followed it to this landing. Then the elevator arrived, and the door opened onto a mop bucket.

LUZ: A spectral mop bucket?

OTIS: A regular one. But it had my name on the handle. I wrote it there because people keep borrowing my bucket.

BENJI: Case closed.

LUZ: A case may be closed. A haunting often has excellent stationery.

[LOW RUMBLE, THEN A TINY SQUEAK.] 

LUZ: There. That was a voice.

BENJI: That's the door hinge.

LUZ: It said "Mara."

BENJI: The hinge says "eee-uh." Mara is our producer. Mara, are you on the landing?

MARA, FROM BELOW: I'm on the landing below you. I heard you say my name and came up.

[SFX: THREE SETS OF FOOTSTEPS MEET.] 

LUZ: Otis, did the stairwell predict Mara's arrival?

OTIS: No. You called her name. She answered. The building has great acoustics and mediocre boundaries.

LUZ: We set up an experiment. I will speak a name into one recorder. Benji will stand at the next landing and report whether he hears it.

BENJI: Fine.

LUZ: Ada.

BENJI: Heard it.

LUZ: Bananas.

BENJI: Heard that too. Now my turn. The stairwell is listening.

LUZ: I heard it.

BENJI: Because I spoke loudly. This is not a difficult building.

[SFX: DISTANT DOOR SLAMS. A SINGLE WHISPER: "FIRE DRILL."] 

LUZ: Nobody said fire drill.

OTIS: The overhead speaker did. That's tomorrow's scheduled test. The announcement system is leaking into the stairwell.

BENJI: Building ghosts have a facilities ticket number?

LUZ: We found a ticket on the bulletin board. "Replace hinge, fix speaker bleed, stop saying my name." It is marked unresolved.

OTIS: I filed it. The work order went to a contractor who came here, heard his own echo, and left a note that says "haunted, recommend exorcism." 

LUZ: We have contacted the contractor. He declined to comment, then emailed a quote for three hundred dollars.

BENJI: A very living response.

[THEME SOFTENS. A WRENCH TURNS.] 

LUZ: Otis oils the hinge. The whisper fades. Facilities patches the speaker. The stairwell is silent for the first time in months.

OTIS: I miss it a little. It was the only thing in this building that said my name.

BENJI: I can say your name.

OTIS: You keep calling me Bucket Guy.

BENJI: I can change.

LUZ: The haunting had a mundane cause, a neglected ticket, and one guard whose bucket has achieved a kind of celebrity. The stairwell is quiet. The podcast will remain loud.

BENJI: Next week, the printer that predicts lunch orders.

LUZ: That is the printer doing its job. Good night.

[DOOR CLOSES. HINGE MAKES A SOFT, PERFECTLY ORDINARY SQUEAK. END MUSIC.]
