# The Bedtime Story with Too Many Endings

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: children's bedtime radio, where the narrator keeps adding final endings after the listener is ready to sleep*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Narrator Odo, baboon; child caller Nara, macaque; sound artist Piri, capuchin; producer Seli, gibbon.

[SFX: SOFT MUSIC BOX, NIGHT CRICKETS.]

ODO: Once upon a time, a small fox went to bed.

NARA: Is that the end?

ODO: Almost. The fox tucked in its tail.

PIRI: [SFX: BLANKET RUSTLE.]

ODO: Then the moon came to the window.

NARA: The fox can see the moon from bed.

ODO: The moon waved.

NARA: The moon cannot wave.

Seli asks Odo to keep the story gentle and short. Odo says a bedtime story needs a calm ending. Piri makes a soft wind sound; the studio fan rattles.

[SFX: LOW WIND, FAN RATTLE.]

NARA: Is there a storm?

ODO: No. The fox is sleeping.

Piri turns off the fan. The wind stops. Odo resumes: the fox dreamed of a hill with a silver path. Nara asks if the fox was still in bed.

ODO: The fox can dream of a hill.

NARA: Then it is not going anywhere.

Odo adds a rabbit, a lantern, and a small bridge. Nara asks if the fox needs to cross the bridge.

ODO: Only in the dream.

Piri taps a wooden block to sound like the bridge.

[SFX: ONE SOFT TAP.]

NARA: The bridge is very quiet.

ODO: It is made of soft wood.

Seli says the story should be ending now. Odo says the fox has not returned from the dream yet. Nara says it never left bed.

ODO: Then the dream can end.

Odo narrates the fox waking up, but the moon is still at the window. Nara asks if the fox went to sleep again.

ODO: The fox is not tired.

NARA: It was bedtime.

Piri turns the music box down. The room becomes quiet. Odo says the fox closed its eyes and listened to the crickets.

[SFX: CRICKETS.]

NARA: Is that the end?

ODO: Yes.

Seli signals to stop. Odo continues: the fox opened one eye to check the moon. Nara says the fox had already closed its eyes.

ODO: It opened one eye.

NARA: The story has begun again.

Odo says the fox heard one cricket chirp and went back to sleep. Piri adds a single soft chirp.

[SFX: ONE CRICKET.]

NARA: Is that the end?

ODO: Yes.

PIRI: The music is still playing.

Seli fades it out. Odo takes a breath, then begins, "And in the morning..."

NARA: No.

ODO: The morning comes after the story.

NARA: I am listening now. End it.

Odo closes the book. Seli holds up one finger: the red light is still on. Odo reopens the book with a squeak.

ODO: The fox heard the book open and wondered if the story had returned.

NARA: The fox is in bed. The microphone is on the table. Those are different places.

PIRI: I can add a tiny page sound.

[SFX: ONE PAGE TURN.]

ODO: The fox heard one page turn and knew the wind had brought a visitor.

NARA: No visitors.

ODO: A small visitor.

NARA: It is midnight.

Seli taps the desk twice, signaling a thirty-second station limit. Odo glances at the clock and tries to compress the story. He speaks faster; the soft music box speeds with him as Piri turns its crank.

ODO: The fox closed its eyes, the moon closed its eye, the rabbit folded the map, and the bridge went to sleep-

NARA: Bridges do not sleep.

PIRI: This one can rest. I have stopped the block sound.

The sudden absence of the bridge cue makes Odo pause. He tries to add a moral about listening to quiet things. Nara asks if the fox needs a moral to sleep. Odo says no, but the station's story form has a box marked LESSON.

SELI: Leave it blank.

ODO: The form will be awake all night.

Seli folds the form and sets it outside the microphone's reach. Odo lowers his voice. Nara asks for one ending that does not reopen the tale.


ODO: The fox was asleep.

NARA: Good night.

Seli reaches for the microphone switch. Odo whispers that the fox may dream again.

NARA: Not tonight.

[SFX: CRICKETS FADE, END THEME.]
