# The Jingle Audition Is Over

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio station branding auditions, endless slogan revisions, and panels who judge a three-note jingle as if it were a symphony*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST VELA, a macaque
COMPOSER NOD, a gibbon
JUDGE PIP, a capuchin
ENGINEER ASA, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: THREE-NOTE JINGLE, CUT OFF MID-NOTE.]

VELA: Welcome to our station identity audition. We need a new jingle for Canopy Radio. It must fit before the news, after the weather, and under a sponsor announcement.

PIP: How long may it be?

VELA: Three seconds.

NOD: My first composition is called "A Place for Every Voice."

[SFX: THREE NOTES, THEN A LONG HELD TONE.]

ASA: It is seven seconds.

NOD: The final tone represents inclusion.

PIP: It also covers the news opening.

VELA: We need three seconds exactly.

NOD: I can remove the final tone.

[SFX: TWO NOTES. SILENCE.]

PIP: Now it sounds like we lost power.

VELA: The second entry, please.

NOD: "Branch by Branch." It uses a hand drum and a bell.

[SFX: DRUM, BELL, DRUM, BELL.]

ASA: That lasts four seconds.

PIP: Can the bell ring sooner?

NOD: Then it sounds like an alarm.

VELA: Our news does begin with an alarm.

PIP: We are not a news station.

VELA: We have news.

NOD: I will try a sung version.

[SFX: NOD SINGS: "CANOPY RADIO, WHERE WE GROW." THE WORD GROW SUSTAINS.]

ASA: That is six seconds.

VELA: The word "grow" could fade under the weather.

PIP: Then listeners will hear "Canopy Radio, where we..." and expect a threat.

NOD: The third entry is a whisper.

[SFX: WHISPERED JINGLE.]

ASA: The microphone did not pick it up.

VELA: We can turn up the gain.

[SFX: WHISPER BECOMES A LOUD BREATH.]

PIP: It sounds like the station is sneaking up on me.

VELA: That is memorable.

NOD: The brief says warm, welcoming, and unmistakable.

PIP: The first version was unmistakable because it never stopped.

VELA: We have listener votes. Asa, play the phone poll.

[SFX: PHONE LINE. THREE BEEPS.]

ASA: One vote for the drum, one for the whisper, and one for the bell.

VELA: A tie between three entries.

PIP: That is not a tie. It is three separate votes.

VELA: The audience has selected all finalists.

NOD: We can layer them.

[SFX: DRUM, BELL, WHISPER, SUSTAINED NOTE COLLIDE.]

ASA: The result is seven seconds and a startled microphone.

PIP: What does the station manager want?

VELA: She asked for something we can finish before the news.

NOD: What if the jingle is two notes and one breath? Three seconds.

PIP: Try it.

[SFX: TWO WARM NOTES, SHORT BREATH.]

ASA: Exactly three seconds.

VELA: It sounds friendly, and it fits.

PIP: I approve.

NOD: Should I add the words?

VELA: No. The test is whether the jingle can stand alone.

[SFX: TWO NOTES, BREATH. THEN NEWS THEME STARTS ON TIME.]

ASA: It ended before the bulletin.

PIP: A historic first.

VELA: The winning jingle is approved. We can now audition station voices to say the name after it.

NOD: I thought the audition was over.

VELA: It is. The station voice test is next week.

PIP: Can it be three seconds?

VELA: We will find out after the first revision.

NOD: Before we leave, I want to test the winning jingle over the actual bulletin.

VELA: The bulletin starts now. Asa, cue the news.

[SFX: TWO NOTES, BREATH. NEWS THEME.]

ASA: It fits. No note covers the first headline.

PIP: The second note sounds like the news is about to begin.

VELA: That is the purpose of a station jingle.

NOD: The breath makes it sound like someone is just about to say something.

PIP: That is the purpose of a station voice.

VELA: We have two separate auditions, and no one will combine them tonight.

ASA: I will save the approved version in the station folder.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS.]

ASA: There are eleven folders named FINAL.

NOD: Put it in the one marked APPROVED.

ASA: That folder is empty.

VELA: Then make a new folder called FINAL APPROVED.

PIP: That will be twelve folders named final.

VELA: But only one has the three-second jingle.

[SFX: FILE SAVES. THE THREE-SECOND JINGLE PLAYS ONCE.]

ASA: It is saved, measured, and finished.

PIP: Please do not audition it again.

[SFX: TWO NOTES, BREATH. THEME OUT.]
