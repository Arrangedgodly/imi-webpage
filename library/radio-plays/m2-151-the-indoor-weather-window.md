# The Indoor Weather Window

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local radio weather bulletins, false precision, sponsor interruptions, and dramatic storm language for ordinary conditions*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
MARA, a capuchin forecast host
TAVI, a gibbon studio engineer
DOT, a squirrel monkey with a window-washing cart

[THEME MUSIC: GRAND BRASS. A soft drip.]

MARA: Good morning from Kettle City Radio. At 7:02, skies are mostly optimistic, with a forty percent chance of one drop landing directly on the microphone.

TAVI: That drop was from the ceiling vent.

MARA: The ceiling is part of our local climate. Our sponsor, Dry Again Umbrellas, reminds you: if you can hear the rain, you are already making memories.

[THUNDER SHEET. A tiny squeak.] 

TAVI: That was Dot's cart wheel.

MARA: A sudden squall is approaching Studio B from the north. Residents may wish to seek shelter behind the weather map.

DOT: I'm right here. I'm washing the weather map.

MARA: Dot, your spray bottle has formed a dense fog bank over the eastern counties.

DOT: It's window cleaner. The bottle says "mist."

MARA: We report what the instruments tell us. Tavi, what does the instrument tell us?

TAVI: The instrument is a kitchen thermometer clipped to the microphone stand. It says the room is warm because you put your tea beside it.

MARA: Then we expect a warm front to move into the beverage district by eight.

[PHONE RINGS. TAVI ANSWERS.]

TAVI: Weather line. Hello?

DOT: I'm calling from the window. I can see blue sky.

MARA: A caller has confirmed a mysterious blue opening between the cloud layers. Could be a break in the weather. Could be a reflection from the aquarium shop.

DOT: It's the sky. I'm outside now.

MARA: The forecast desk appreciates your field report. Please do not approach the blue opening without a licensed umbrella.

TAVI: We have no licensed umbrellas.

MARA: That is why the warning is urgent.

[STREET DOOR OPENS. DOT WHEELS THE CART BACK INTO THE STUDIO.]

[WIND MACHINE ROARS. PAPER WEATHER MAP FLAPS LOOSE.]

TAVI: The fan is pointed at the map again.

MARA: The gust has changed direction. We now have a rotating low-pressure system in the studio, moving past the coastline of my notes.

DOT: That's me trying to catch the map.

MARA: Viewers at home-listeners in cars-should expect scattered paperwork.

TAVI: No, just scattered paper. And no one is watching us at home.

MARA: Our severe weather desk is checking the radar.

TAVI: We don't own a radar.

MARA: The radar is the round wall clock. Its sweeping hand indicates that the storm will arrive at the top of the hour.

DOT: The clock is stopped.

MARA: Then the storm has been delayed indefinitely.

[THEME STING. A SECOND DROP LANDS IN MARA'S CUP.]

MARA: Breaking update: isolated precipitation has entered my tea. The cup's surface is choppy, with a strong chance of biscuit.

DOT: I found the leak. The vent pipe above the studio is loose. If I move the cart, I can catch the drips in its bucket.

TAVI: Do it. We can turn the fan off, too.

MARA: We can't simply stop the storm. We have listeners depending on us for their morning certainty.

DOT: I'll leave one drip going. Here.

[ONE DRIP. THEN A SILENCE.]

MARA: The storm is now confined to a small, well-behaved puddle in the cart bucket. Dot has repaired the vent with a towel and a wrench. Tavi has turned off the fan. The skies are clear indoors.

TAVI: The sky was clear outside all along.

MARA: After careful analysis, we can confirm a lovely day, unless you are standing under the vent.

DOT: I'm standing under the vent. It's fixed.

MARA: Then it's a lovely day there, too. Dry Again Umbrellas will be demonstrating its new waterproof model in our studio bucket.

[UMBRELLA POPS OPEN. BUCKET ROLLS AWAY.]

MARA: A fresh gust-

TAVI: Door draft.

MARA: Tomorrow: a high of comfortable and a low chance of sponsor equipment escaping. This is Mara at Kettle City Radio, signing off beneath a ceiling of mixed materials.

[THEME MUSIC. ONE FINAL DRIP LANDS IN THE UMBRELLA.]
