# The Auditorium of Borrowed Voices

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: voice archives and imitation acts that preserve a performer by replaying their voice over whoever is speaking now*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: sound archivist Nella, a lemur; performer Odo, a baboon; technician Piri, a capuchin; director Rusk, a macaque.

[SFX: EMPTY AUDITORIUM. ONE MICROPHONE HUMS. A REEL SPINS UP AND AN OLD APPLAUSE TRACK FADES IN.]

RUSK: We are ready to record the opening address. Odo, use the archive voice for the greeting.

ODO: Which archive voice?

NELLA: The celebrated voice of Master Jori. His recordings are on the blue reel.

PIRI: The reel is labeled "Opening, old hall." The address is for this hall.

RUSK: A greeting is a greeting. Play it under Odo.

[SFX: TAPE STARTS. A WARM, RESONANT VOICE SAYS, "WELCOME, FRIENDS." ODO SPEAKS AT THE SAME TIME. THE TWO VOICES CLASH.]

ODO: Welcome, friends.

RUSK: That sounds like two hosts.

NELLA: It is one host and one recording.

RUSK: Can we make them sound like one?

PIRI: Not without blending their words.

NELLA: Jori's voice is part of the archive. We cannot erase it to make a new performance.

RUSK: Then let it introduce Odo.

ODO: I can answer after the recording.

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS. APPLAUSE SWELLS TOO EARLY, BEFORE THE GREETING.]

PIRI: The applause is from the old hall. It starts before the first line.

RUSK: That's a good reaction.

NELLA: It is from another audience.

RUSK: They are welcoming us too.

ODO: Let me try without the tape.

[SFX: TAPE STOPS. A SHORT SILENCE.]

ODO: Good evening. I am Odo. This auditorium is new, and the sound is ours tonight.

[SFX: HIS VOICE ECHOES NATURALLY THROUGH THE EMPTY ROOM.]

RUSK: It lacks the warmth of the archive.

PIRI: The room is empty. There is no audience to absorb the echo.

NELLA: We could set a few seats and record the room response after the program.

RUSK: Or we could add the applause now.

NELLA: That would be borrowed applause too.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ENTER. NELLA MOVES A MICROPHONE STAND; ITS BASE SCRAPES.]

PIRI: The stand scrape is loud.

ODO: It is also real.

RUSK: We cannot begin with a scrape.

NELLA: We can begin with the sound of preparing to speak. Jori's recording did that too.

RUSK: Did it?

NELLA: Listen.

[SFX: TAPE STARTS. A CHAIR CREAKS, THEN JORI TAKES A BREATH BEFORE SPEAKING. THE OLD APPLAUSE IS MUTED.]

ODO: That breath makes the voice feel present.

PIRI: But it belongs to Jori.

NELLA: We can keep the recording as an archive excerpt, then leave a clean pause for Odo's live greeting.

RUSK: Will the audience know the difference?

NELLA: I will announce the excerpt.

RUSK: The archive is valuable because Jori was famous. Should we not use more of him?

NELLA: We should use exactly what he recorded and say when we use it.

ODO: His voice can open the evening. Mine can welcome the audience now.

PIRI: The reel also contains his cough, chair scrape and closing announcement.

RUSK: We could build a whole host from those pieces.

NELLA: That would make a recording impersonate a living speaker.

RUSK: It would make the introduction seamless.

ODO: It would make me sound like I had already left.

[SFX: THE REEL PAUSES WITH A SOFT MECHANICAL CLICK.]

NELLA: We can preserve the full recording in the archive. For this program, we need one excerpt, one attribution, and a quiet handoff.

PIRI: I'll mark the reel at the greeting and cut playback there.

RUSK: No applause?

NELLA: The audience can provide its own.

[SFX: MICROPHONE SWITCH CLICKS.]

NELLA: Before we begin, you will hear a short fragment from the first performance in this hall's predecessor. The next greeting belongs to tonight's host.

[SFX: TAPE PLAYS JORI'S SINGLE "WELCOME, FRIENDS" WITHOUT APPLAUSE. IT STOPS. A BEAT OF SILENCE.]

ODO: Welcome, friends. This room is new. I am glad we can fill it together.

[SFX: SMALL REAL AUDIENCE APPLAUSE, THEN ONE CHAIR CREAKS.]

RUSK: Keep that. The creak is from here.

PIRI: It is from row two.

RUSK: Then row two has joined the cast.

[SFX: THE REEL IS STORED. ODO STEPS AWAY FROM THE MICROPHONE; THE ROOM ECHOES ONCE AND GOES QUIET.]
