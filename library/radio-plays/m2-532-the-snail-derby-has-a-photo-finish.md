# The Snail Derby Has a Photo Finish

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcasts that manufacture speed statistics for animals*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Commentator Vex, an orangutan; referee Mara, a macaque; timer Odo, a gibbon; contestant Pip, a capuchin; gardener Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Garden track. Small bell; leaves rustle.

VEX: Welcome to the annual Snail Derby. The competitors are at the starting leaf, and our champion is Pip's blue-ribbon snail, Dot.

MARA: The snails choose their own pace. No tapping their shells and no moving the finish leaf closer.

ODO: The course is a damp board, twelve inches long, with a chalk line at either end.

PIP: Dot is at the start. I have not touched her since I set the leaf beside her.

VEX: Can we say she is warming up?

SELI: She is resting. The board is shady and moist, as the care sheet says.

SFX: A stopwatch clicks.

VEX: And they're off! Dot is making a daring advance.

MARA: No one has moved. The first line is behind her, and she is facing sideways.

ODO: The timer starts when the first snail crosses the chalk, not when Vex says "off."

VEX: I was giving the audience a dramatic start.

PIP: If you clap, Dot may retract. Could everyone stay quiet?

SFX: Crowd hush. One soft leaf scrape.

SELI: Her feelers are out. She has turned toward the finish leaf.

VEX: A tactical maneuver. She's reading the track.

MARA: She is moving toward the leaf because it is damp. That's the finish direction, but don't call it strategy.

ODO: Dot's shell crossed the first chalk line. Timer started.

VEX: Record-breaking acceleration!

ODO: One inch in four minutes.

VEX: The scoreboard needs a different unit.

MARA: It needs an accurate unit. The other snail has reached the line too.

PIP: Both are on the board. Dot is following the wet edge.

SFX: Tiny bell rings; a leaf shifts.

ODO: Dot has crossed the finish chalk. Time is seven minutes, twelve seconds.

VEX: A photo finish.

MARA: The shell is fully across. That's a clear finish.

SELI: I'll move both snails back to the garden bed, away from the hot board.

PIP: Dot can rest under the lettuce leaf. No medal on the shell, please.

VEX: We have a champion and a course record.

ODO: No. We have a finisher and a time. It's the first race today.

MARA: The audience can applaud now, after the finish.

SFX: Gentle clapping; leaves rustle.


MARA: Before we start another heat, Seli will check the board temperature. The snails go back to the garden bed, not into a scoreboard cabinet.

SELI: The board is cool enough to touch, but I am rinsing it before storing it. No shell marks or paper numbers go with the snails.

ODO: I'll record the time and the course length on the event card. The second snail crossed two minutes after Dot.

VEX: Was that second place?

MARA: It was a finish, and no one was pushed. We can call it second only if both owners entered the race that way.

PIP: We agreed the first shell fully across wins. Dot met that rule first.

SELI: Both snails are in shade, and the lettuce leaf is damp.

VEX: The crowd is asking for a rematch.

MARA: They can ask next season. The snails are done today.


SELI: I'll rinse the board outside the garden bed, then set the snails beneath the lettuce.

PIP: Dot is settled under the leaf. The other snail is beside her, and neither is on the track now.

MARA: Course closed. The chalk line can wait for next year.


VEX: No sponsor medal for Dot?

PIP: No medal. A leaf to rest under is enough.

TAG

VEX: Next year, a sprint.

SELI: Next year, a shady bed.
