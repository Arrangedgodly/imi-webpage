# The Orchard Hotline

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in advice broadcasts whose host turns every practical question into a personal brand*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Tavi, a macaque; caller Leni, a gibbon; producer Sol, a capuchin; sponsor voice, a parrot.

[SFX: PHONE RING, CHEERFUL THEME.]

TAVI: Welcome to The Orchard Hotline, where every problem can become a growth opportunity.

SOL: The sponsor asked you to say "fruit opportunity."

TAVI: We can grow together.

LENI: I have a branch that leans over the path. I want to trim it before anyone bumps their head.

TAVI: First, what does this branch mean to you?

ACT ONE

Leni says it is a low branch on the plum tree. Tavi asks if it represents a boundary. Sol whispers that listeners want advice, not a metaphor. Tavi says the hotline can provide both.

LENI: I need to know whether to cut it.

TAVI: Have you tried naming the branch?

Leni says she has not. Tavi suggests "Boundary Brenda." Sol turns on a bell whenever Tavi gives a practical answer. It rings three times in one minute.

TAVI: The bell is our sponsor's signal that this call is ripe.

SOL: It means you forgot the sponsor line.

Tavi mentions the orchard's pruning kit. The sponsor voice interrupts to advertise a tiny basket. Leni asks if the basket will catch the branch. The parrot says the basket is for fruit, not branches.

ACT TWO

Tavi asks Leni to describe the angle. She says the branch points across the path and has two green plums. Tavi asks how she feels about losing the plums. Leni says she feels fine; the path needs to be clear.

TAVI: Then your instinct is already fruitful.

SOL: Give her the actual steps.

Tavi tells Leni to ask the orchard keeper to check the branch and mark a safe cut. Leni says she is the orchard keeper. She has the shears, gloves, and a clear morning. She just wanted to know if removing the branch was sensible.

Tavi begins a brand exercise: imagine the branch as a guest leaving a gathering. Leni says she does not want the branch to leave. She wants to shorten it so people can walk underneath.

The sponsor voice offers a coupon for a basket if Leni can fit the branch inside it. Leni laughs and says the branch is longer than the basket. Tavi tries to turn that into a slogan.

TAVI: Big branches need big boundaries.

SOL: Please stop naming slogans.

Sol asks Leni to describe how she will work, so listeners can hear the decision in action. Leni puts on gloves, marks the low branch with a strip of cloth, and tests that no one is standing beneath it. Tavi starts to ask what the cloth symbolizes. Leni says, "It symbolizes the place I will cut." The sponsor voice offers to sell branded markers. Sol mutes the line. Leni removes the short branch in one clean cut, then sets it aside for mulch. The plums stay on the upper limb. Tavi asks if she feels liberated. Leni says she feels able to walk through her own garden. Sol makes Tavi repeat the practical advice without a slogan. It takes three takes because Tavi keeps rhyming "shears" with "careers."

ACT THREE

Leni describes marking the branch, checking the path, and pruning only the low section. She saves the healthy fruit and leaves the main limb intact. Tavi tries to ask what the branch taught her.

LENI: It taught me the path was blocked.

SOL: That's the answer.

Tavi thanks her and says the hotline will now answer the next caller without assigning an inner meaning to their gardening tools. The sponsor voice asks listeners to buy the basket anyway.

TAG

The bell rings once.

TAVI: Is that a ripe call?

SOL: No. I took the fruit out.
