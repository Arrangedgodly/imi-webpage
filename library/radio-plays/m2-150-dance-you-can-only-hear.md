# The Dance You Can Only Hear

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio dance-recital broadcasts, overconfident movement narration, and a choreographer who claims every studio noise as part of the performance*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Sella, a gibbon radio host; Maru, a macaque choreographer; Deen, an orangutan stagehand; and Lio, a capuchin dancer.*

[SFX: Shoes brush the floor. A piano begins.]

SELLA: Welcome to Dance by Ear. Tonight Lio performs "The Unfolding Branch." Maru, describe the opening.

MARU: Lio stands at center stage with arms raised toward morning.

DEEN: We are in a radio studio. Morning is the wall with the clock.

LIO: Which direction should I face?

MARU: Toward possibility.

SELLA: For listeners at home, possibility is beside the microphone.

[SFX: A sleeve brushes the microphone.]

SELLA: A soft rustle. The branch is awakening.

DEEN: That is his sleeve.

MARU: The sleeve is part of the choreography.

LIO: It is caught on the microphone clip.

MARU: The branch resists the limitations of form.

DEEN: Hold still while I release it.

[SFX: Clip opens with a sharp click.]

SELLA: An unexpected percussion entrance.

DEEN: An expected repair.

MARU: Keep it. The click represents a seed splitting.

LIO: Shall I start dancing now?

MARU: You never stopped.

[SFX: Two steps, a pivot, then a chair rolls.]

SELLA: Something crosses the floor with great confidence.

DEEN: My chair. Lio kicked it.

MARU: The chair represents the passing seasons.

DEEN: The passing seasons have my lunch on them.

[SFX: Deen catches the chair. Cutlery rattles.]

SELLA: Autumn arrives with tiny bells.

LIO: I have been rehearsing this for six weeks. There are no tiny bells.

MARU: Live performance must respond to the space.

DEEN: Then respond by giving me back my chair.

SELLA: We have a message from a listener. They ask whether the dance is a furniture delivery.

MARU: Tell them the chair is an image of movement.

DEEN: It has wheels. That is what they are hearing.

LIO: I will perform the quiet kneeling passage. Nobody move anything.

[SFX: Three measured steps. Fabric settles. A cable scrapes.]

SELLA: The branch bends beneath its burden.

LIO: I am picking up the cable so I can kneel.

DEEN: Please don't pull it. It connects to the piano microphone.

[SFX: A microphone stand slides. One low piano chord booms.]

MARU: The forest answers.

SELLA: That sounded expensive.

DEEN: It will be if he pulls another inch.

LIO: I have stopped.

MARU: A powerful stillness.

DEEN: A powerful need for cable tape.

[SFX: Tape tears. Deen presses it down.]

SELLA: A dry leaf breaks beneath the branch.

DEEN: If you call that choreography, put me in the credits.

MARU: Stagehands remain invisible.

DEEN: On radio, everyone remains invisible. You have described my chair more often than your dancer.

LIO: He has a point. My feet have had one sentence.

SELLA: Maru, can we hear the actual movement without narration?

MARU: The audience needs my interpretation.

SELLA: The audience is asking which warehouse dispatches the seasons.

DEEN: Let Lio dance. I'll secure the chair and the cable.

MARU: The chair cannot be removed. It supplies the transition.

DEEN: Then it gets a performer fee.

MARU: We cannot pay a chair.

DEEN: I'll collect on its behalf.

[SFX: Deen rolls the chair one deliberate foot.]

SELLA: The passing seasons are negotiating.

LIO: I will give you a transition for free. Three steps, turn, then the branch folds inward.

MARU: We must preserve the unexpected quality of the first take.

SELLA: It was broadcast live. Six listeners have ordered your chair.

DEEN: Tell them delivery is extra.

MARU: Fine. One clean phrase. I will remain silent.

[SFX: One piano note. Three steps, a pivot, a soft sleeve brush, then stillness.]

SELLA: That was Lio. The entire sound was Lio.

LIO: Thank you.

MARU: The branch has finally found its

DEEN: Silence clause. You agreed.

[SFX: Deen rolls the chair away from the microphone.]

SELLA: This has been Dance by Ear, performed by Lio, with incidental furniture by Deen.

MARU: I want the chair removed from the credits.

DEEN: You'll have to speak to its agent.

[SFX: A telephone rings from the chair seat.]

DEEN: Passing Seasons Management. Yes, it is available for weddings.
