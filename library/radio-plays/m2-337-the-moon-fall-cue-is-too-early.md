# The Moon-Fall Cue Is Too Early

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: theater sound-effects crews who trigger the spectacular cue before the story is ready for it*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Sound designer Loma, a gibbon; director Vex, a macaque; actor Piri, a capuchin; stage manager Sella, a lemur.

[SFX: THEATER WARM-UP. A ROPE CREAKS. A METAL DISC RUMBLES ACROSS A WOODEN TRACK, THEN STOPS.]

VEX: The moon falls in the final scene. We need one clear crash.

LOMA: I have a wooden roll, a metal scrape and a padded landing.

PIRI: Does the moon actually fall?

SELLA: The script says it begins to descend when the hero rings the bell.

VEX: The bell is in act one. We can use the fall there as a teaser.

SELLA: The bell in act one opens the gate. It does not move the moon.

VEX: The audience should anticipate.

[SFX: A SMALL BELL RINGS. THE METAL DISC RUMBLES AND CRASHES INTO A PADDED BOX.]

PIRI: I have not reached the gate yet.

LOMA: That was the sound test.

VEX: It sounded like the moon fell.

SELLA: The cue is marked for act three.

VEX: Move the mark to the first bell.

PIRI: Then what makes the moon fall later?

VEX: A bigger bell.

[SFX: PIRI RINGS A LARGER BELL. THE DISC RUMBLES, SCRAPES, AND CRASHES. A WOODEN PLATFORM CREAKS.]

LOMA: The platform has moved.

SELLA: The moon is still above the stage.

VEX: We need a visual effect.

LOMA: On radio, we need the sound to tell us what happened.

PIRI: The sound says something fell, but not what.

SELLA: The script says the hero pulls a rope, the moon lowers halfway, then the guide catches it.

VEX: That is a long way from a crash.

LOMA: Then we should not crash it.

[SFX: ROPE TIGHTENS. A COUNTERWEIGHT SLIDES. THE METAL DISC DESCENDS, HANGS, THEN SETTLES WITH A LOW WOODEN THUMP.]

PIRI: That sounds like a moon lowering.

VEX: It sounds small.

SELLA: The hero is carrying it down a ladder.

PIRI: I can breathe under it. It is made of cloth.

VEX: Could we add a storm?

LOMA: The storm belongs after the gate opens.

SELLA: And before the hero reaches the ladder.

VEX: You are making the cue list longer.

SELLA: I am making it chronological.

PIRI: The first bell still needs a cue. The hero must be onstage before it rings.

SELLA: I will wait for his boots to stop at the gate, then ring once.

LOMA: I can record a short rope creak after the bell. That gives the audience the opening before the gate sound.

VEX: No crash?

LOMA: No crash. The gate can open without the moon falling.

[SFX: BOOTS CROSS A WOODEN PLATFORM AND STOP. A SINGLE BELL RINGS. A ROPE CREAKS. A GATE LATCH LIFTS AND THE GATE SWINGS OPEN.]

PIRI: That is act one. I am inside the gate, and the moon is still overhead.

SELLA: The cue sheet is in order now.

VEX: Could the rope creak be bigger?

PIRI: The rope is above me. If it sounds huge, the moon has started moving.

LOMA: Keep it small. The audience can hear the difference between a latch and a landing.

VEX: I can hear the difference too.

[SFX: SELLA MARKS THE CUE SHEET. PENCIL SCRATCHES BESIDE ACT ONE: BELL, GATE. ACT THREE: ROPE, LADDER, SOFT LANDING.]

[SFX: PAPER CUE SHEET TURNS. A PENCIL MOVES THE CRASH CUE FROM ACT ONE TO ACT THREE.]

LOMA: We can keep a small bell in act one, the rope in act two, and the soft landing in act three.

PIRI: What does the hero do between the rope and the landing?

SELLA: He climbs the ladder and guides the moon down.

VEX: That is a calm ending.

LOMA: A calm ending has sound too.

[SFX: LADDER CREAKS ONE STEP AT A TIME. ROPE SLIDES THROUGH A PULLEY. THE MOON LANDS ON THE PADDED BOX WITH A SOFT, ROUND THUD.]

PIRI: It is down. The gate is open.

VEX: And no crash?

LOMA: The crash was the wrong cue.

[SFX: THE BELL RINGS ONCE, AT THE CORRECT MOMENT. A GATE OPENS; THE MOON STAYS ON ITS PAD.]

SELLA: That is the first bell. The gate opens.

VEX: I hear it now.

PIRI: May I leave the stage?

SELLA: After the moon is secured.

[SFX: ROPE IS TIED OFF. A KNOT TIGHTENS. PIRI STEPS DOWN THE LADDER AND EXITS.]

LOMA: Cue list complete.

VEX: Put the crash back in the trailer.

SELLA: The trailer ends with the soft landing.

[SFX: THEATER LIGHTS CLICK OFF. THE METAL DISC DOES NOT MOVE.]
