# The Grand Opening of the Small Shed

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: corporate launch events that inflate modest improvements into historic milestones*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Host Miri, a capuchin; builder Kito, a gibbon; organizer Odo, a baboon; neighbor Pava, a macaque.

[SFX: SCISSORS, ONE SMALL CLAP.]

MIRI: We gather to open the new garden shed.

KITO: It has a door and a shelf.

ODO: Please wait for the ribbon.

PAVA: The tools are outside.

ACT ONE

Odo explains that the shed is twice as wide as the old storage box. Miri calls it a major expansion. Kito says it is still small enough to carry a watering can. Pava asks if the tools can be moved in now. Odo says the schedule requires a speech first.

ACT TWO

Miri gives a welcome address. She calls the shelf an innovation in vertical storage. Kito demonstrates by placing a trowel on it. The shelf is at shoulder height, so Pava asks for a lower one. Kito says he can add a peg beneath it.

Odo says the peg must be unveiled separately. Pava reaches for the trowel, but the shelf is too high. Kito lowers the trowel and hands it to her. Miri calls this a successful transfer.

ACT TWO-B

Pava takes the watering can inside, but the doorway is too narrow while the ribbon hangs there. She asks Odo to move it. Odo says the ribbon will be cut during the ceremony. Kito says the ceremony is over; the scissors are still in Miri's hand. Miri cuts the ribbon in one motion and hands both ends to Odo. He tries to hold them up for a photo, but the shed door swings shut. Pava opens it again and sets the can on the floor. Kito tests the shelf with a light trowel. It holds. He adds a second hook below it for the gloves. Miri calls this phase two of the launch. Pava hangs the gloves and asks where the watering can goes. Kito clears a space beside the door. The shed now holds the tools it was built for, though the event schedule had allotted only time for speeches. Odo asks if they should unveil the hook separately. Pava hangs a hand brush on it before he can answer.

The launch team tries to take a group photograph. Odo asks everyone to stand outside the shed and point toward the door. Pava is already inside, putting tools away. Miri asks her to come out for the photo. Pava says then the shed will be empty again. Kito solves it by taking the photograph from the doorway, with Pava visible beside the shelf. The watering can blocks part of the ribbon, which Odo says ruins the symmetry. Miri says the tools are more important than the ribbon. Odo moves the ribbon to the side and captures the picture. Pava checks the shelf one last time, then takes the can back outside to water the garden. The shed's first use lasts longer than its ceremony.

Kito asks if anyone knows where the old storage box went. Odo says it is beside the compost bins. Pava says they can reuse it for seed packets. Miri adds that the launch has created a second project. Odo starts writing a new event schedule, but Pava takes the clipboard and writes "use box for seeds" instead. They bring the packets over and place them on the lower shelf, where Pava can reach them. The shelf now holds both tools and seeds, and the ceremony has finally produced a practical arrangement.

ACT THREE

Miri records one closing announcement. Kito confirms that the shelf and lower peg hold their tools. Pava places the seed packets in the reused box and shuts the shed door without blocking the path.

MIRI: The new shed is open for use.

ODO: A historic expansion.

PAVA: It is a dry place for the watering can.

TAG

Odo asks if he can cut the ribbon again for another photograph.

PAVA: We are using the shed.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES.]
