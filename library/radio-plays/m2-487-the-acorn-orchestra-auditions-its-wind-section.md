# The Acorn Orchestra Auditions Its Wind Section

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: elite music auditions that confuse technical precision with musicality*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Conductor Seli, a lemur; judge Vex, an orangutan; player Pip, a capuchin; tuner Odo, a gibbon; composer Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Community hall. Music stands unfold. An acorn rattles in a wooden cup.

SELI: The acorn orchestra auditions its new wind section.

VEX: We are seeking a player with polished technique and quiet confidence.

PIP: I have a reed whistle.

VEX: Blow one scale.

SFX: Pip blows. A clear note wavers.

ODO: It is slightly flat.

MARA: It is also the only instrument in the room that can play without a bow.

VEX: We are judging intonation.

PIP: I can tune it. The reed is dry.

SFX: Whistle warms in Pip's hands. He blows again, note steadier.

ODO: Better.

VEX: Can you play louder?

PIP: The whistle is small.

SELI: We need a section, not a solo.

MARA: I brought a paper horn, and Odo has a seed shaker.

ODO: The shaker is for keeping time.

VEX: We can audition them separately.

SFX: Seli taps baton. Pip plays; Odo shakes. The rhythms do not match.

VEX: The shaker is late.

ODO: I was following the whistle.

MARA: The whistle is holding one note.

PIP: I can play a tune if everyone stops scoring the first breath.

SELI: Let's do a short phrase together.

SFX: Paper horn gives a low rasp. Whistle rises over it. Acorn shaker adds a dry pulse.

VEX: The horn is uneven.

MARA: It is folded from a page in the old program. Its unevenness makes the note bend.

ODO: The sound blends with the whistle.

VEX: The audition form has separate boxes for pitch, tone, and volume.

SELI: Add "plays together."

VEX: There is no box.

PIP: Then write it in the margin.

SFX: Pencil scratches.

MARA: Start the phrase again. This time, Odo, shake only on my hand signal.

ODO: Ready.

SFX: Baton tap. Whistle, paper horn, and shaker settle into a slow melody.

SELI: That's a section.

VEX: It sounds less impressive than a full symphony.

PIP: The hall is small. The music fits.

ODO: The audience is humming.

SFX: A few audience members hum the final note.

VEX: We can count that as a successful audition.

MARA: Only if the score says they listened.

VEX: The audition is supposed to select one winner.

MARA: The paper horn cannot compete with the whistle. They do different things.

ODO: The form asks for a ranking from one to five.

PIP: I can play the phrase alone and then with everyone. Let the judges hear the difference.

SFX: Pip plays a clear, solitary note.

VEX: Very precise.

SFX: The group repeats the phrase; the note bends warmly over the steady seed rhythm.

SELI: The second version fills the room.

VEX: But the first was cleaner.

MARA: A clean note isn't always the right note. The melody needs room for the other instruments.

ODO: I can mark the whistle as lead and the paper horn as harmony.

PIP: I don't need to win the audition. I want a part in the group.

VEX: The form has no box for "part."

SELI: I'll write it across the top.

SFX: Pen scratches. Stand legs shift as the players make space together.

MARA: One more phrase, with the audience humming the last note.

SFX: The group plays. Audience hum joins; the final chord rests.

VEX: That ending was not on the score sheet.

ODO: It was on the room's breath.

SELI: The wind section is hired.

VEX: Under "ensemble" or "individual"?

SELI: Under "music."

TAG

SFX: Acorn drops into the cup.

ODO: The percussionist has finished.

PIP: That's the conductor's snack.

SELI: Keep it in time.
