# The Radio Tower That Heard Itself

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: broadcast stations that treat feedback as audience participation and bury the actual message under station branding*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Engineer Pavo, a capuchin; host Nella, a gibbon; manager Vex, a macaque; caller Rima, a lemur.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC. A MICROPHONE OPENS. A HIGH FEEDBACK WHINE RISES, THEN CUTS OFF.]

NELLA: Good evening, listeners. You are tuned to the valley's clearest signal.

PAVO: The signal is not clear. That was feedback.

VEX: It sounded bright. Keep it in the opening.

NELLA: We are taking calls about the new tower. Caller, you are live.

RIMA: I can hear myself before I speak.

[SFX: A HALF-SECOND DELAY. RIMA'S WORDS ECHO BACK, THEN A SOFT WHINE RETURNS.]

PAVO: The tower is feeding the studio transmitter back into the studio receiver.

VEX: Can it be a feature? Listeners hear their thought arrive early.

RIMA: It is my voice, and it is late.

NELLA: Rima, please hold while we fix the line.

VEX: Do not put her on hold. That is a new segment.

PAVO: If I mute the return channel, the echo will stop.

VEX: But then the tower won't sound like a tower.

PAVO: It should carry voices out, not bring them back into the microphone.

[SFX: SWITCHES CLICK. THE FEEDBACK WHINE STOPS. A LOW HUM REMAINS.]

NELLA: Rima, can you hear me now?

RIMA: Yes. I hear you once.

VEX: Before we go back on air, I want the listeners to hear the new tower name.

PAVO: The transmitter is clean, but the return monitor should stay muted while we test.

NELLA: I will say the name once, and Rima can tell us whether it arrives once.

RIMA: I am ready.

[SFX: MICROPHONE OPENS. A SOFT SWITCH CLICK.]

NELLA: Valley Radio, from the ridge above the river.

[SFX: THE SIGNAL GOES OUT, THEN RETURNS THROUGH THE STUDIO RECEIVER ONCE. NO WHINE FOLLOWS.]

RIMA: I heard it once, then the hill went quiet.

VEX: Could we make the return slightly warmer?

PAVO: The return is for monitoring, not broadcast. The warm tone comes from the studio speaker.

VEX: I want the station to sound recognizable.

NELLA: The station should sound like the voice coming through, not like the station repeating itself.

RIMA: When it echoed, I stopped talking because I was waiting for myself.

VEX: That is useful feedback.

PAVO: The delay was a cable loop. The cable is now routed away from the antenna and secured to the wall.

[SFX: A CLIP FASTENS THE CABLE. A SMALL TEST TONE PLAYS ONCE, THEN STOPS.]

NELLA: No loop. No delayed greeting. One voice, one response.

VEX: Keep that as the station line.

RIMA: And let callers finish before the sign-off.

VEX: We need a station identification before the call resumes.

PAVO: We need a new cable. The return line is coiled around the outgoing line.

VEX: Can you uncoil it while she is on air?

PAVO: Not without unplugging the tower.

NELLA: How long?

PAVO: Two minutes.

VEX: We cannot have two minutes of silence. Play the tower's recorded welcome.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICKS. A RECORDED ANNOUNCER SAYS, "VALLEY RADIO WELCOMES YOU." THE SAME WORDS RETURN WITH A DELAY, THEN AGAIN, EACH TIME SOFTER.]

VEX: That's an excellent loop.

PAVO: It will loop until the battery dies.

NELLA: The recording has become its own caller.

RIMA: I am still on the line.

NELLA: We hear you. Pavo is going to fix the cable.

RIMA: I can wait without hearing myself.

[SFX: STUDIO POWER SWITCHES OFF. FAN SPINS DOWN. A DOOR OPENS; CABLES SHIFT ON THE FLOOR.]

PAVO: The return cable is wrapped around the outgoing cable twice. Vex, hold this end. Nella, keep Rima connected on the hand line.

[SFX: CABLES UNWIND. TWO PLUGS DISCONNECT, THEN CONNECT TO SEPARATE JACKS.]

PAVO: Power on.

[SFX: FAN SPINS UP. TRANSMITTER HUM, NO FEEDBACK.]

NELLA: Rima, speak once.

RIMA: One clear voice from the hill.

[SFX: HER VOICE TRAVELS THROUGH THE TRANSMITTER. A SMALL RECEIVER IN THE STUDIO PLAYS IT ONCE, CLEANLY.]

PAVO: That's correct.

VEX: Could we add the old echo under the sign-off?

RIMA: Please don't.

NELLA: We'll sign off with the clean signal.

VEX: Valley Radio. One voice at a time.

[SFX: TRANSMITTER CLICKS OFF. THE STUDIO ROOM TONE SETTLES.]

PAVO: Now it is the valley's clearest signal.

RIMA: And I only hear myself when I choose to speak.

[SFX: THE HAND LINE CLICKS OFF. ONE SWITCH CLICKS; SILENCE.]
