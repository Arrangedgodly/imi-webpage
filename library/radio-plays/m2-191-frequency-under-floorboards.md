# The Frequency Under the Floorboards

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: amateur radio mystery programs that turn tuning and signal noise into coded warnings*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST KORI, a macaque
OPERATOR DALI, a capuchin
CALLER TAVI, a gibbon
ENGINEER MENA, a gorilla
TECH LEM, a macaque technician in the next room
RECORDED TECH VOICE, a prerecorded macaque announcement

[SFX: RADIO STATIC. A LOW BEEP, THEN A SECOND LOW BEEP.] 

KORI: We are listening on the neighborhood band. Operator Dali, what did we pick up?

DALI: Two beeps and some static.

KORI: A warning?

MENA: It is our test signal.

TAVI: I am calling from the workshop. I hear the same beeps under the floorboards.

KORI: The mystery has moved off-site.

### Scene One: Tuning In

[DALI TURNS A LARGE DIAL. STATIC SWELLS, THEN A DISTANT VOICE COMES THROUGH.]

RECORDED TECH VOICE: Checking the transmitter. One, two.

KORI: A hidden message.

MENA: A station technician checking a transmitter.

[DALI TURNS THE DIAL TOO FAR. A WEATHER BULLETIN, A MUSIC STATION, AND A QUIET BEEP OVERLAP.]

DALI: We have three messages.

MENA: We have three stations.

KORI: Can you separate them?

MENA: I can tune to one at a time.

[DALI FINDS A CLEAR VOICE.]

RECORDED TECH VOICE: Test, test. If you can hear this, please stop transmitting.

KORI: A warning from an unknown operator.

MENA: That is Lem, our station technician, in the next room.

[MENA OPENS THE CONTROL ROOM DOOR. THE TECHNICIAN WAVES THROUGH THE GLASS.] 

DALI: The message came from inside the station.

KORI: We have been looking outward.

[THE WEATHER BULLETIN FADES OUT. THE BEEP REMAINS.] 

### Scene Two: The Workshop

[SFX: WORKSHOP AMBIENCE, A SMALL RADIO CRACKLE.]

TAVI: The sound comes from the back corner. It repeats every minute.

KORI: Is anyone hiding a transmitter?

TAVI: I found a battery radio on the shelf.

[DALI TRIES TO REMOTE-TUNE THE RADIO. IT CLICKS AND LOSES THE SIGNAL.] 

DALI: The signal disappeared.

TAVI: I turned the radio off.

MENA: Leave it off until we inspect the cord and battery.

KORI: There is a cord?

TAVI: It is plugged into a charger.

[SFX: PLUG PULLED. THE BEEPS STOP.]

MENA: The charger is sending interference to the station receiver.

KORI: The beeps began when the radio was plugged in.

TAVI: I moved it to the same outlet as the console.

MENA: That outlet shares the receiver's power strip.

[TAVI UNPLUGS THE POWER STRIP. THE STUDIO LIGHTS GO OUT; THE EMERGENCY LIGHT COMES ON.]

DALI: We have another signal.

MENA: It is the backup light.

DALI: The mystery was a charger.

KORI: Was it transmitting a clue?

TAVI: It was charging a radio.

[THE RADIO'S BATTERY LIGHT BLINKS. TAVI SETS IT ON A WOODEN TABLE AWAY FROM THE CHARGER.]

TAVI: It can charge here without crossing the receiver cable.

MENA: I will reroute the studio lead along the wall.

[A CABLE CLIP SNAPS INTO PLACE. THE BEEPS DO NOT RETURN.]

DALI: The signal is gone.

KORI: The signal was never a message.

### Scene Three: On Air

[BACK IN THE STUDIO. KORI HOLDS A NOTE THAT SAYS "UNEXPLAINED SIGNAL." MENA CROSSES IT OUT.]

KORI: We have identified the signal as interference from a charger.

DALI: Should we replay the beeps?

MENA: We can demonstrate the cause.

[TAVI PLUGS IN THE CHARGER. TWO BEEPS SOUND. HE UNPLUGS IT; THE RECEIVER GOES QUIET.] 

KORI: The mystery has a plug.

DALI: That is a short episode.

MENA: The fix was short.

[THE STUDIO PHONE RINGS. KORI ANSWERS; IT IS THE TRANSMITTER TECH, CALLING TO SAY THE TEST SIGNAL IS OVER.] 

KORI: We have a final message. It says the test is over.

DALI: No code?

KORI: Just a sign-off.

### Tag

[SFX: RADIO STATIC, THEN QUIET.]

TAVI: The workshop is quiet now.

MENA: The radio is charging on the other table.

KORI: Mystery solved at a safe distance from the dial.
