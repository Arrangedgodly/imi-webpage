# The Town's Treehouse Has a Noise Ordinance

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: neighborhood meetings that regulate every sound except the meeting itself*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Chair Mara, a macaque; resident Pip, a capuchin; neighbor Nari, a lemur; inspector Odo, a gibbon; clerk Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Wooden platform creaks above a small clearing. A paper notice flaps.

MARA: This neighborhood meeting will come to order. We are discussing the treehouse noise ordinance.

PIP: My family uses it to groom and watch the path.

NARI: I live in the trunk next door. Every afternoon, Pip's platform squeaks.

ODO: We can measure noise. Vex, read the standard.

VEX: The ordinance limits sounds to three chirps per hour.

PIP: Gibbons call to each other across the grove.

SFX: A short call from the canopy. Another answers from farther away.

VEX: One chirp here, one there. Two violations.

NARI: That was my family asking if the meeting had started.

ODO: The ordinance would regulate calls between neighbors?

MARA: We came to address one repeating squeak, not the way animals speak.

NARI: And macaques chatter while grooming. The grove isn't quiet.

MARA: What sound is bothering you, Nari?

NARI: The same squeak repeats whenever Pip shifts his weight. At night it carries through the trunk.

PIP: I can stop shifting.

SFX: Platform groans. Pip freezes. A seed drops onto the floor.

NARI: That sound too.

VEX: We need a formal test. Odo, take the meter to the platform.

SFX: Meter turns on. A steady hiss.

ODO: Background level is seven.

VEX: The limit is three.

MARA: Is the stream counting as a violation?

VEX: The form says neighborhood sound. It does not list exceptions.

NARI: I didn't call about the stream. I called about the squeak.

PIP: I can oil the hinge. The platform is built from two branches tied together with vine.

ODO: Oil may damage the bark.

MARA: We can add a soft wrap at the rubbing point.

VEX: The wrap is not in the ordinance.

NARI: It doesn't need to be in the ordinance. It is a repair.

SFX: Pip climbs one rung. Vine tightens; platform squeaks.

ODO: That is the sound.

PIP: I am above the trunk now. If I move the wrap, the platform may tilt.

MARA: Come down first.

SFX: Careful steps descend. A small tail thumps the ladder.

VEX: The tail made a sound.

PIP: It is attached to me.

NARI: I did not complain about the tail.

MARA: The ordinance shouldn't decide whether a sound is allowed before we know what causes it.

ODO: We can pad the contact point on the ground.

PIP: I have soft bark strips from the repair pile.

NARI: Use those. Leave the seed shelf clear.

SFX: Bark strips slide between two braces. Vine is retied. The platform creaks once, then stops.

ODO: Test it.

SFX: Pip steps on the platform. The meter needle clicks but no squeak follows.

PIP: I can shift.

NARI: That's quiet enough for me.

VEX: It still reads four.

MARA: Put the meter by the water.

SFX: Footsteps to the stream. Water burbles. The meter jumps to seven.

ODO: The creek is louder than the platform.

VEX: Then we cannot issue a certificate.

NARI: We can certify the repair by listening where the problem happens, without counting the whole grove.

MARA: We will amend the rule. The sound limit applies to avoidable repeated noise, not calls, stream, or ordinary movement.

VEX: That sentence will not fit on the notice.

PIP: Put it on the back.

SFX: Paper turns. Pencil writes.

ODO: What about meeting noise?

MARA: We will keep the meeting short.

VEX: The clock says we've used our three chirps.

NARI: The birds did.

PIP: They don't attend meetings.

NARI: That one was the wind.

VEX: The wind is not on the list.

MARA: We'll add the grove.
