# The Pledge Drive of One

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio fundraising appeal that treats one listener like a crowd*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: HOST VIKA, a macaque; FUNDRAISER PELL, a gibbon; LISTENER NIM, a capuchin; and a ringing phone.

[SFX: PLEDGE DRIVE JINGLE, PHONE RINGS ONCE.] 

VIKA: It is time for our annual station pledge drive. Our goal is a hundred supporters. We have one phone call.

PELL: One hundred is still possible if Nim pledges one hundred times.

NIM: I am one monkey.

PELL: You can pledge on behalf of your left hand and right hand.

NIM: They do not have fruit.

VIKA: A pledge can be a promise to listen.

PELL: The board prefers a number.

NIM: I can promise to listen tomorrow.

PELL: How about two days?

NIM: I cannot hear two days at once.

VIKA: We should ask what the station needs. The roof leaks near the antenna.

PELL: We need funds for a roof, a new microphone, and a pledge thermometer.

NIM: The roof is more important than the thermometer.

PELL: The thermometer shows progress.

NIM: The roof lets the thermometer stay dry.

[SFX: A DROP OF WATER LANDS IN A METAL CUP.] 

VIKA: We have a live demonstration of our need.

PELL: We should not use the leak as an incentive.

VIKA: We're reporting what happened.

NIM: I can help patch the roof with a broad palm leaf.

PELL: We cannot pay for repairs with a leaf.

NIM: I did not ask for payment.

VIKA: The station is supported by listeners, volunteers, and occasionally a leaf.

PELL: We have a pledge from the station board. They will match the first contribution.

NIM: How much?

PELL: One matching contribution.

VIKA: That is a count, not an amount.

PELL: The board wants the drive to look active.

NIM: Can the board bring a tool to fix the roof?

PELL: The board can bring a ladder.

VIKA: A ladder is useful. We still need to know how much the repair costs.

PELL: I can ask the roofer to inspect it before we name a target.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS TWICE, THEN A LINE OPENS.] 

CALLER: I heard the water drop. The leak is at the west corner. I can patch it with a woven palm panel.

NIM: Is the roof still dripping?

CALLER: Not now. The rain stopped.

VIKA: We can air the repair update tomorrow after someone checks the panel.

PELL: We should not promise a fix on air before we know if it holds.

NIM: I can hold the ladder while the roofer works.

PELL: That is a volunteer offer. I will put it on the board.

VIKA: How many supporters do we have now?

PELL: One listener, one caller with a panel, and a board with a ladder.

NIM: That's not a hundred.

PELL: It is enough to begin.

VIKA: Our goal is to keep the signal dry, not to fill the thermometer.

PELL: I will put the thermometer away until we have a real repair estimate.

[SFX: THERMOMETER SLIDES INTO A DRAWER. THE CUP CATCHES ONE LAST DROP.]

PELL: I can revise the drive. We need one supporter for the repair, and we will ask for more only after we know the cost.

NIM: I will bring a leaf tomorrow and listen today.

VIKA: That is a pledge with a clear purpose.

PELL: Should I mark one supporter?

NIM: Mark one monkey.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES ONE NAME. A SECOND PHONE RING SOUNDS.] 

VIKA: Another call! Hello?

CALLER: I heard about the roof. I have a dry reed mat.

PELL: We have two offers and a smaller goal.

VIKA: The pledge drive may be working.

NIM: Or the station may have told people what it needs.

[SFX: RAIN TAPS THE ROOF, THEN STOPS ABOVE THE CUP.]
