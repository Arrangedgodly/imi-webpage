# The Opera in the Elevator Shaft

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: opera broadcasts, grand recitative, vocal warmups, and a diva who treats an interruption as a tragic aria*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
DIVA PEARL, a capuchin soprano
ORO, a gibbon baritone
MICA, a squirrel monkey stage manager
PROP ATTENDANT, a tamarin stagehand

[ORCHESTRA TUNING. A DISTANT ELEVATOR DING.]

MICA: We're live from the Grand Kettle Opera House. Tonight's performance begins with a warming note from Pearl.

PEARL: Aaaaah!

[THE NOTE ECHOES THROUGH THE SHAFT. AN ELEVATOR DING ANSWERS.]

ORO: The lift answered her.

PEARL: It has an excellent ear. Let us begin the overture.

[ORCHESTRA STARTS. ELEVATOR DING, DING.]

MICA: There's a problem. The freight elevator is stuck between floors, and someone inside is pressing the bell.

PEARL: A captive voice, trapped in iron! It is the perfect entrance for my character.

ORO: The character is a shepherdess.

PEARL: A shepherdess can be trapped in a mountain.

MICA: It's the prop attendant. He's bringing the painted sheep up from storage.

PEARL: The mountain has sheep. Continue.

[RECITATIVE BEGINS.]

PEARL: O stone-faced walls, release my flock!

ELEVATOR BELL: DING.

ORO: The bell is in the key of C.

PEARL: Then the shaft has joined the ensemble. I shall sing to it.

[SHE SINGS. THE BELL DINGS IN TIME.]

MICA: I'm calling the lift operator. Please keep the hall clear.

PEARL: Clear the hall? The chorus needs a crowd.

ORO: There are twelve patrons waiting outside.

PEARL: Let them approach the shaft. They will hear the mountain's lament.

MICA: They'll block the fire exit.

PEARL: A tragic obstacle.

ORO: Pearl, the patrons are trying to reach their seats. The elevator is not part of the performance.

PEARL: Everything is part of the performance once it has echoed.

[PHONE RINGS. MICA ANSWERS.]

MICA: The operator says the lift is moving. Everyone stand back from the doors.

[MECHANICAL HUM. ELEVATOR RISES. BELL DINGS ONCE.]

PROP ATTENDANT: I'm out. All six painted sheep are off the lift. One is behind the curtain, and the cart lost a wheel on the ramp.

PEARL: The shepherd has returned with five sheep and a prophecy of a sixth!

ORO: It is a prop cart. We need the spare wheel.

MICA: The spare is under the scenery shelf. I'll fetch it while the prop attendant brings the last sheep from behind the curtain.

[WHEEL ROLLS. A CART LIFTS. METAL CLINKS.]

PROP ATTENDANT: Spare wheel fitted. The cart rolls evenly now.

[SMALL HOOVES CLACK AS A SIXTH SHEEP JOINS THE OTHERS.]

MICA: Six sheep counted, all backstage, cart repaired. The painted mountain can wait by the wall.

PEARL: Then the mountain shall be moved by human hands-by monkey hands-and my aria will tell of the rescue.

ORO: We are about to begin act one.

[ORCHESTRA SWELLS.]

PEARL: O shaft of iron, you have held a note and released a friend. Let the chorus sing!

ORO: That's not the libretto.

PEARL: It is now.

[CHORUS ENTERS, SINGING.]

PEARL: We have restored the flock, the hallway, and the musical balance.

MICA: We also have five minutes until curtain.

ORO: The audience can wait through one overture.

PEARL: They will wait through two if I sing them beautifully.

PROP ATTENDANT: The cart is fixed and all six sheep are backstage.

MICA: Six accounted for. I'll bring one to the stage for the aria.

[SMALL HOOVES CLACK TOWARD THE STAGE.]

PEARL: The flock is reunited. The solo sheep may enter on the high note.

ORO: That sheep is a painted prop.

PEARL: It has a very convincing profile.

[ONE PROP SHEEP ROLLS ON ITS REPAIRED CART.]

MICA: The spare wheel is fitted; the cart rolls evenly.

PEARL: The flock is reunited. The tenor may now proceed to the mountain.

ORO: I'm the baritone.

PEARL: You can be any range you wish in the mountain.

PEARL: Wait. The audience should hear their footsteps.

[SMALL HOOVES CLACK.]

ORO: That is a surprisingly fine finale.

PEARL: I shall hold the final note until the last sheep leaves.

[LONG OPERATIC NOTE. THE ELEVATOR DINGS ONCE MORE.]

MICA: It's going up empty.

PEARL: An encore from the shaft.

ORO: We're finished. The lift can go home.

[APPLAUSE.]

MICA: You've heard the Grand Kettle Opera: where even the elevator gets a part, but only after the hallway is clear.
