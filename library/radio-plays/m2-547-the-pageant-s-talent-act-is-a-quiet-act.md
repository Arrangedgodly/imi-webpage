# The Pageant's Talent Act Is a Quiet Act

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: talent competitions that insist every act needs a louder finale*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; stage manager Mara, a macaque; performer Pip, a capuchin; judge Odo, a gibbon; backstage runner Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Community hall backstage. Curtain rings slide; audience murmurs.

VEX: Our next contestant will astonish you with a quiet act.

MARA: Pip's act is a shadow puppet story. The lights need to be low and the audience quiet.

ODO: The judging card scores originality, timing, and clarity. It does not score volume.

NARI: The hand lamps are charged. I will dim the house lights when Mara gives the cue.

PIP: I brought a paper bird and a small screen. The story takes one minute.

VEX: Could we add a drumroll before the reveal?

PIP: The reveal is the bird flying across the screen. A drumroll would cover the wings.

MARA: Keep the percussion track off. Vex, introduce the act, then step away from the microphone.

SFX: House lights lower; audience settles.

VEX: Prepare for the most thunderous silence in pageant history.

MARA: That is still a loud introduction. Please keep it short.

VEX: Pip presents "Bird at the Window." No other sound.

Pip begins. Paper rustles softly as the bird shadow crosses the screen. A small silhouette of a tree appears.

SFX: Soft paper movement.

ODO, whispering: Clear shape. Good pacing.

NARI: The audience can see the bird.

VEX: I can barely hear the act.

MARA: The act is visual. The radio audience gets the sound of paper and the artist's narration after the scene.

PIP, quietly: The bird sees its reflection and thinks another bird has arrived.

VEX: We need a surprise cymbal.

PIP: The surprise is that the reflected bird is the same bird.

MARA: Let him finish.

SFX: Paper bird turns; brief pause.

PIP: It flaps once, then leaves the window.

SFX: Paper screen folds.

AUDIENCE: Gentle applause.

PIP: The next part is the reflection. I turn the paper bird sideways, and its shadow becomes the bird in the window.

NARI: I could see the change before I heard the line. The bird was looking at itself.

VEX: Could we add a bright title card that says "TWIST"?

MARA: That would cover the shadow. Leave the screen clear until Pip finishes.

SFX: Paper shifts, then rests.

ODO: A lamp cable has slipped onto the floor behind the screen. I'll lift it onto the taped hook before the next contestant walks through.

MARA: Do that after Pip clears the paper figures. Keep the lamp off until the cable is secured.

PIP: Figures are on the tray. The paper bird stays flat; I won't fold its wing.

ODO: Cable is on the hook. Lamp remains off until the stage manager calls the next setup.

VEX: The act has produced both a twist and a backstage obstacle.

MARA: The obstacle is resolved, and no one needs to add a cymbal.

VEX: The paper bird has left the screen, but the panel has not filed its card.

ODO: I'll score only the published categories after the act.

MARA: Hold the screen until the score is filed. The next performer can prepare beside the curtain.

SFX: Cue sheet rustles; lamp relay clicks.

ODO: The judging card has five categories, and no bonus for an encore. I will score only the act we watched.

VEX: We could add a second round where Pip performs a louder version.

PIP: That would be a different act, and my hand puppets need to stay flat until the next time slot.

MARA: Write the score now. The next contestant can set up after the props are stored.

NARI: One child laughed when the bird saw its reflection. That was before the final flap.

VEX: Should laughter count as a judge?

ODO: No. The panel scores the listed categories. The audience can enjoy the act without changing the rules.

Vex asks to hold the paper bird for a photograph. Pip keeps it on the flat prop tray.

PIP: The beak bends if you hold it by one wing.

VEX: A star needs an image.

MARA: A prop needs safe storage. Take the photo of Pip's paper screen instead.

SFX: Camera shutter; one click.

ODO: Scores are filed: originality four, timing five, clarity five. The act earns fourteen points.
PIP: I'll return the paper figures to their flat tray.
MARA: The screen and lamps are clear for the next performer.

ODO: That is allowed.
