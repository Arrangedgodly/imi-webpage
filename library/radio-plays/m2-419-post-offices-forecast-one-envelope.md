# The Post Office's Forecast for One Envelope

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: service radio updates that turn a routine delivery delay into a weather bulletin and overstate tracking uncertainty*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Announcer Poma, a langur; clerk Tiri, a macaque; courier Besh, a capuchin; listener Om, a gibbon.

[SFX: POST OFFICE COUNTER; RAIN ON ROOF. PRINTER FEEDS PAPER.] 

POMA: Delivery advisory. One envelope is delayed in the eastern district due to rain.

OM: Is it the letter I sent to my sister?

TIRI: We have many envelopes in the east route. What is the tracking number?

OM: I only know it has a drawing of a blue heron inside.

BESH: I am the courier. I have a weather pouch and the east route list.

POMA: The route display says "cloudy, uncertain arrival." That sounds serious.

TIRI: The display uses weather icons when a scan is late. It does not know where the envelope is.

OM: The letter is not valuable. I just promised it would arrive today.

POMA: We will stay with this developing story.

[SFX: PRINTER SPITS A RECEIPT; RAIN GROWS LOUDER.] 

BESH: The east route bus stopped for a flooded crossing. The driver turned back to the post office.

TIRI: The envelopes are dry in the pouch. They will go out when the road opens.

TIRI: The display is set to "all alerts." It turns every late scan into a weather banner.

BESH: It did the same thing to my route card yesterday when a scanner battery died.

POMA: The forecast said lightning over the sorting room.

OM: I heard that bulletin. I thought the building was closed.

TIRI: We can keep the delivery notice and remove the cloud icon. Weather belongs to the route report, not the letter's identity.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS; CLOUD ALERT DISAPPEARS.]

BESH: My pouch is still dry. The only storm was on the screen.

POMA: The graphic shows a storm cloud over the envelope.

TIRI: That is a placeholder. The envelope is in the station bin.

OM: Can I pick up the letter here?

BESH: If you describe the stamp, I can check the route bag. Blue heron, you said?

OM: Yes. A small bird beside a reed.

[SFX: PAPER LIST SHUFFLES; BAG ZIP OPENS.] 

BESH: I have it. The bag is dry. The stamp is on the front, and your address is clear.

POMA: Our live report has located the envelope.

TIRI: It has not been delivered. It is awaiting the reopened route.

POMA: The screen now says "sunny, delivered." That is wrong.

TIRI: The system cleared the weather icon when the courier scanned the bag, not the letter.

BESH: I scanned the route pouch. The envelope is still inside it.

OM: So it is safe, but not at my sister's house.

POMA: We should correct the status before the bulletin says delivered.

[SFX: KEYBOARD CLICKS; ALERT TONE.] 

TIRI: Corrected: route paused for rain; mail secured at station. No delivery estimate until the crossing opens.

POMA: That is less dramatic, but it is accurate.

OM: I can call my sister and tell her it is delayed. She will not wait by the window.

BESH: The rain has eased. The road crew is checking the crossing now.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS; A DISTANT VOICE ANSWERS.] 

OM: I will tell her the blue heron is safe in its pouch.

POMA: The delivery advisory remains in effect. One route is paused; no envelope is lost.

TIRI: The postal weather graphic will show the district, not draw a storm around one letter.

BESH: I can leave as soon as the crossing is cleared.

[SFX: RADIO CRACKLES.] 

BESH: Route update: crossing open. The east bus is heading out now.

OM: I will stop listening to the forecast and let the mail travel.

POMA: No further alert unless the route changes again.

TIRI: The printer has stopped making storm maps.

[SFX: PRINTER WHIRS ONCE; A PLAIN RECEIPT PRINTS.] 

POMA: One envelope, dry, delayed by weather, back on route.

OM: That is enough news for one letter.

[SFX: MAIL BAG ZIPS; MIC CLOSES.]
