# The Ferry That Only Crosses the Pond

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: adventure serials that turn a short trip into a heroic expedition*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Captain Mara, a macaque; navigator Odo, a gibbon; passenger Pip, a capuchin; announcer Seli, a lemur; dock manager Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Small pond dock. Water laps against a wooden ferry.

SELI: The little ferry will cross the pond in three minutes.

VEX: Make that a perilous crossing. We have a sponsor paying for danger.

MARA: It is a flat pond with a clear view of the other shore.

ODO: The water is shallow enough to see the stones.

VEX: Then don't mention the stones.

PIP: I brought a bag of apples. Where should I sit?

MARA: On the bench, away from the rope.

VEX: The passenger should stand at the bow and stare into the distance.

PIP: I can stare from the bench.

SELI: Our voyage begins.

SFX: Rope unties. Ferry creaks away from the dock.

ODO: The wind is pushing us a little east.

MARA: I can correct with the oar.

VEX: Call it a current.

ODO: It is a pond, not an ocean.

VEX: A local current.

PIP: I see the far dock. It is the same color as this one.

SELI: The horizon is full of mystery.

MARA: That's a willow tree behind the dock.

SFX: Oar dips. Water sloshes. A duck quacks.

VEX: A warning cry from the wild.

ODO: It's the duck that lives here.

PIP: It wants the apple peel.

MARA: Keep the peel until we tie up.

VEX: The crew faces a moral test.

PIP: I can put it in my pocket.

MARA: We have two passengers on the dock who need the return crossing.

PIP: I can hand over the apples when we tie up.

VEX: Wait, the serial needs a clue hidden in the cargo.

ODO: The apples are ordinary, and they are in the bag.

VEX: One could be a coded message.

PIP: It is a snack.

SFX: Bag rustles. One apple rolls beneath the bench.

MARA: Secure the bag before the return trip. A loose apple can roll under the pedal.

PIP: There it is.

SFX: Pip reaches under bench, retrieves apple, folds bag closed.

ODO: The cargo is safe. We can depart.

SELI: Our heroes turn toward home, with two waiting passengers and a secured apple bag.

VEX: We need a cliffhanger.

MARA: The rope is caught on the cleat. That's enough trouble for this boat.

SFX: Mara frees rope. The ferry leaves the dock at an easy pace.

SFX: Ferry bumps gently against the far dock.

SELI: We have reached the distant shore.

MARA: The crossing took two minutes.

VEX: The voyage must not end before the sponsor message.

ODO: We could inspect the dock rope and make sure it hasn't snagged.

MARA: Good. Pip, hold the ferry steady while I check the knot.

PIP: I have the rope.

SFX: Rope slips through Pip's hand. The ferry drifts a short distance.

PIP: It is moving away.

MARA: Keep hold. Odo, bring the oar around.

ODO: The oar is under the bench.

VEX: At last, a crisis.

MARA: Pip, wrap the rope around the cleat, not your hand.

SFX: Rope catches the cleat. Oar dips and the ferry turns back toward the dock.

PIP: I have it.

ODO: The knot was loose. It's secured now.

MARA: We'll check the knot before every departure.

VEX: You cannot say that in an adventure serial.

SELI: I can say it in this one. The crew has arrived safely, and the ferry is ready for the return trip.

VEX: Can the return trip be called the sequel?

PIP: Only if it takes apples to the starting shore.

MARA: It lives here.

VEX: The mystery continues.
