# The World's Slowest Auction

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: auction broadcasts, escalating bid increments, and auctioneers who mistake a pause for competitive suspense*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST TAVI, a lemur
AUCTIONEER ODO, a baboon
BIDDER SERA, a macaque
CLERK NOLL, a capuchin
CALLER LUMA, a gibbon
CALLER REX, a macaque on a delayed phone line

[SFX: SMALL HALL, GAVEL TAPS.]

TAVI: We are live at the town auction of a hand-carved bench. The opening bid is one shell.

ODO: Do I hear one shell?

[SILENCE.]

ODO: I hear no bid.

SERA: One shell.

ODO: The bid is one shell. Do I hear two?

[SILENCE.]

TAVI: The audience is holding its breath.

NOLL: The audience is reading the lot card.

LUMA (PHONE): Why does the bench have a wheel?

SERA: It has one wheel.

ODO: It is a rolling bench.

NOLL: It is a bench with a wheel attached.

ODO: I will accept bids by hand signal, voice, or written form.

SERA: Two shells.

ODO: Two shells! Do I hear three?

[SILENCE.]

TAVI: We are entering the dramatic pause.

NOLL: There is no minimum time for a pause.

ODO: A good auction leaves room for desire.

SERA: I desire to know whether the wheel is included.

ODO: The wheel is attached.

NOLL: It can be removed.

ODO: Then it is included until removed.

Sera bids three shells, but Odo waits for a rival bid. Nobody moves. The silence grows long enough for the clerk to turn a page.

[SFX: PAPER TURN.]

ODO: I hear four shells.

NOLL: That was me turning the lot card.

ODO: You may bid if you wish.

NOLL: I do not wish.

TAVI: The auctioneer has bid against the page.

Luma phones to ask whether the bench is suitable for a waiting room. Odo praises its comfort. Noll points out a sign under the seat: "For display only; one leg needs repair."

SERA: The leg is loose?

NOLL: It is noted in the catalog.

ODO: The bench is a restoration opportunity.

TAVI: The broadcast has changed from auction to disclosure.

Odo invites a five-shell bid. Sera asks to test the bench. The room goes quiet as she sits. The wheel rolls; Noll catches the bench before it reaches the microphone stand.

[SFX: WHEEL ROLLS, WOOD THUMPS.]

SERA: It is not stable.

ODO: It is highly mobile.

NOLL: The loose leg is part of the lot.

SERA: I bid one shell, conditional on removing the wheel and fixing the leg.

ODO: That is two conditions.

NOLL: The sale terms allow conditions.

ODO: They do not.

Tavi asks listeners to call in with a bid. A caller offers three shells but only if the bench is delivered. Odo says delivery is not included. Sera offers two shells and will collect it herself after the leg is repaired.

The station receives a second call, but the line delay makes the caller's bid arrive after Odo has moved on.

REX (PHONE): Four shells.

ODO: I hear four shells.

NOLL: That call is delayed. The caller spoke before the repair condition.

TAVI: We cannot attach an old bid to a new lot description.

ODO: The auction has become chronological.

SERA: That is usually how auctions work.

REX (PHONE): I can withdraw my bid. I made it before the repair condition.

ODO: I heard four shells after I called for another bid.

NOLL: The phone delay carried an old offer into a new call.


[SFX: GAVEL LIFTS, PAUSE.]

ODO: Do I hear three?

No answer. Odo waits. Noll sets a small repair block under the bench. It stops rolling. The audience relaxes.

ODO: Three shells?

SERA: Two shells.

ODO: Going once at two.

[SILENCE.]

ODO: Going twice.

LUMA (PHONE): The bench sounds quiet now.

ODO: Sold for two shells to Sera, with the wheel removed and the leg repaired before pickup.

TAVI: We have witnessed the world's slowest auction and the first sale contingent on basic stability.

NOLL: I will remove the wheel.

ODO: I will update the catalog.

SERA: I will wait until the repair is done.

[SFX: GAVEL TAPS, TOOLBOX OPENS.]

TAVI: The bench is sold. The pause is over. Our broadcast ends before the repair becomes another lot.

[SFX: MIC FADES, ONE WRENCH TURN.]
