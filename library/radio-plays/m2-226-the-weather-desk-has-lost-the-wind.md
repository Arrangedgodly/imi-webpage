# The Weather Desk Has Lost the Wind

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: urgent radio weather bulletin conventions, where an overprepared host tries to report a missing gust*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host RUMA, macaque; forecaster ELO, gibbon; engineer KES, capuchin; caller TAVI, baboon.

[SFX: NEWS THEME, WIND MACHINE WHIRRING.]

RUMA: This is the village weather desk with an urgent update. The wind has gone missing.

ELO: The wind is not missing. It is calm.

RUMA: Calm is how missing wind wants us to describe it.

KES: We have a fan in the studio, if you need evidence of moving air.

RUMA: That is a machine. We need the real wind, preferably before the top of the hour.

ELO: The top of the hour is not a weather condition.

Ruma unfolds a map. It flaps loudly.

[SFX: PAPER FLAPPING.]

RUMA: Wind from the northwest.

ELO: That is your map.

RUMA: It says northwest in three places.

KES: One is the printer label.

Tavi phones in from the orchard.

TAVI: The leaves are still. My hat is on my head. I would call this a calm morning.

RUMA: How long has your hat been on your head?

TAVI: Since breakfast.

RUMA: We cannot rule out a wind that has been following you.

ELO: We can rule it out by checking the flags. There are no flags moving.

RUMA: Could the flags be holding their breath?

ELO: Flags do not breathe.

KES: I can test the studio air with a ribbon.

She ties a ribbon to a microphone stand. The fan kicks on and the ribbon whips around.

RUMA: Wind warning! We have a sudden gust indoors.

KES: That is the fan I mentioned.

RUMA: The audience needs a clear distinction between outdoor wind and domestic wind.

ELO: Turn the fan off.

KES: The switch is behind the cabinet.

They move the cabinet. It scrapes across the floor, making a gusty sound.

[SFX: CABINET SCRAPE, THEN A DRAFT.]

TAVI: I felt that in the orchard.

RUMA: The wind has returned. Where is it now?

ELO: Under the door. You created a draft by moving the cabinet.

RUMA: A local wind event.

ELO: A local furniture event.

The station phone rings again. Tavi says his hat has lifted. Everyone freezes.

TAVI: No, I am taking it off. It was hot.

Elo suggests moving the microphone outdoors to the ridge. Ruma insists the bulletin should remain in the studio until there is something urgent to say. Kes opens the back door to test the air. The door swings shut, catching the ribbon under it.

KES: The wind is trapped.

ELO: The ribbon is trapped.

RUMA: Do not open the door. We have a weather event in progress.

Kes frees the ribbon. It hangs straight. The crew carries the microphone to the porch. They hear leaves, a bird, and no breeze.

RUMA: It is calm all the way to the ridge.

ELO: The ridge is two miles away.

RUMA: Could be a very large calm.

ELO: We are not reporting a wind until one arrives.

RUMA: False alarm. Keep the hat in view.

KES: We could report the actual forecast: clear skies, light breeze later.

RUMA: No urgency in that.

ELO: Then make the urgency honest. Tell listeners when they may expect a breeze.

RUMA: At three, the forecast says a light wind may cross the ridge.

ELO: That is accurate.

RUMA: Listeners can prepare their leaves.

ELO: Leaves do not need preparing.

RUMA: We have to give them something to do.

KES: Tell them to secure hats if they are standing near the ridge.

Ruma hesitates, then faces the microphone.

RUMA: At three, a light breeze may cross the ridge. Secure loose hats. Do not move furniture to summon it.

[SFX: NEWS THEME RETURNS. FAN CLICKING OFF.]

TAVI: The wind is still missing here.

ELO: It is just late.

RUMA: Then it can report to the desk when it arrives. We have filed a missing gust notice.
