# Traffic Is Moving at the Microphone

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio traffic reports that manufacture urgency from ordinary delays and repeat the same route warnings*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST SENA, a macaque
REPORTER KAVI, a gibbon
ENGINEER LOTT, a capuchin
CALLER IRO, a gorilla
CALLER RIN, a macaque
ATTENDANT KEN, a langur

[SFX: TRAFFIC BED, HORN, NEWS CHIME.]

SENA: The east bridge is slow because a delivery cart has stopped in the right lane. Reporter Kavi, are you at the scene?

KAVI: I am in the studio. The road camera shows a cart unloading chairs.

LOTT: We could add a helicopter sound.

SENA: We do not have a helicopter.

### Scene One: The Backup

[SFX: A MAP UNFOLDS.]

KAVI: The map shows traffic backed up to Cedar Street.

SENA: How long is the delay?

KAVI: Two light cycles.

LOTT: That is not a major event.

KAVI: It is if you are in a hurry.

[LOTT PLAYS A HORN. IT SOUNDS LIKE A DUCK QUACK.] 

SENA: Is that the traffic?

LOTT: It is our horn sample.

[KAVI CALLS THE BRIDGE ATTENDANT.]

KAVI: How long until the cart is unloaded?

KEN: Three minutes. Then we reopen the lane.

SENA: Could we say "gridlock"?

KAVI: No. Cars are moving.

### Scene Two: The Alternate Route

[PHONE RINGS.]

IRO: I am at Cedar Street. The side road is open, but there is a school crossing guard at the corner.

SENA: Is traffic moving there?

IRO: Yes, one car at a time while students cross.

LOTT: We can call it the secret bypass.

KAVI: It is a public street.

[THE MAP GRAPHIC SHOWS A RED ARROW DOWN THE SIDE ROAD. IT COVERS THE SCHOOL CROSSING SYMBOL.]

SENA: The arrow is blocking the important part.

LOTT: The arrow is moving.

[KAVI REMOVES THE ARROW. THE MAP SHOWS THE CROSSING.]

KAVI: Let the crossing finish before turning.

[SENA TAKES A SECOND CALL.]

[A THIRD PHONE LIGHT FLASHES. LOTT PUTS IT ON AIR BEFORE THE NEWS CHIME.]

RIN: I am at the park entrance. The road is open, but a wagon is parked over the bike lane.

SENA: Can riders get around it safely?

RIN: Yes, if they use the marked path.

KAVI: We can tell them the lane is temporarily blocked.

LOTT: Do we need a horn?

SENA: No. The caller gave us the location and the open route.

[THE PHONE LINE CLICKS OFF. THE STUDIO MAP ADDS ONE SMALL SYMBOL FOR THE WAGON.]

[LATER, THE PHONE RINGS AGAIN. RIN IS NOW APPROACHING THE EAST BRIDGE FROM THE PARK ROAD.]

RIN: I am on the bridge approach. Is the delivery cart still there?

KAVI: The attendant says unloading is complete.

RIN: I can see the cart. It is parked at the curb.

LOTT: We can play the horn cue.

SENA: The lane is open. No cue needed.

[THE CALLER DRIVES PAST THE CART AT A STEADY PACE. THE MICROPHONE PICKS UP ONE TURN SIGNAL CLICK.] 

RIN: The road is moving.

SENA: The report is current.

IRO: I am waiting with the other cars.

[SFX: A CHILD'S BICYCLE BELL, THEN FOOTSTEPS.]

SENA: We can hear the crossing from the caller's microphone.

IRO: The students have crossed. Cars are moving again.

### Scene Three: Clear Lane

[SFX: ROAD AMBIENCE SOFTENS.]

KAVI: The bridge lane has reopened. The cart is parked off the road.

SENA: The delay lasted two light cycles and three minutes of unloading.

LOTT: We have a dramatic outro.

[LOTT PLAYS THE DUCK-HORN SAMPLE. IT MAKES A QUACK.] 

IRO: That is not a car horn.

SENA: It is not even a duck.

KAVI: The bridge is clear now.

[THE MAP UPDATES. LOTT'S RED ARROW REMAINS OVER THE SCHOOL CROSSING. HE MOVES IT TO THE OPEN BRIDGE.]

KAVI: The bridge is clear, the side road is moving, and the crossing guard is still at the school.

LOTT: We can close the traffic bed.

[THE NEWS CHIME PLAYS. THE DUCK-HORN SAMPLE QUACKS ONCE MORE FROM LOTT'S DESK.] 

SENA: The sound effect is the only delay left.

### Tag

[SFX: NEWS CHIME, THEN A SINGLE DUCK QUACK.]

SENA: Traffic is moving. We will not call the crossing a bypass.

LOTT: The duck has left the studio.
