# The Village Weathercock Gives Testimony

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: courtroom broadcasts that make a direction indicator responsible for the weather*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Judge Odo, a gibbon; plaintiff Vex, an orangutan; keeper Mara, a macaque; reporter Pip, a capuchin; clerk Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Town hall. Gavel taps. Wind whistles outside.

ODO: The court will hear the complaint against the village weathercock.

VEX: It pointed east, but the rain came from the west.

MARA: The vane shows wind direction. It does not promise where rain will fall.

NARI: The complaint asks for a refund on a ruined picnic.

PIP: I saw Vex set the picnic on the east lawn.

VEX: The arrow said east.

ODO: The arrow points into the wind.

VEX: I understood it to point toward the weather.

MARA: It has one job: rotate with the breeze. The court can examine the weather report before it hears from the vane.

ODO: Clerk Nari, read the picnic forecast.

NARI: "Clouds increasing by afternoon. Scattered showers possible after two."

VEX: I thought east was the direction the weather would arrive from.

PIP: The picnic began at noon. The report did not promise a dry day.

MARA: The vane isn't a forecast. It shows the source of the current wind at that moment.

ODO: The court will inspect it in the square.

SFX: Weathercock creaks outside.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps cross gravel.

PIP: The arrow is stuck on the finial.

MARA: The screw is rusted. It hasn't turned all week.

NARI: I'll record that it was stuck facing east, not that it predicted the rain.

PIP: That is what I saw from the lawn.

VEX: So it gave the wrong direction.

NARI: It gave no direction at all.

ODO: We need to oil the bearing and test it.

SFX: Oil bottle tips onto the bearing. Metal squeaks, then turns freely.

PIP: I brought the picnic basket back. The blanket is still wet.

VEX: The weathercock promised a dry day.

MARA: It cannot promise weather. It was stuck and has just been repaired.

ODO: We need to test a second breeze from another direction.

SFX: A hand fan moves air from the south. Vane swivels.

PIP: The arrow points south, toward the source.

MARA: That's correct; wind comes from the south.

VEX: I thought an arrow showed where the wind was going.

ODO: This vane uses the arrowhead to face the source. The sign will say that.

NARI: I'll post it at eye level, not up by the roof.

PIP: We can move lunch to the pavilion, away from the wet lawn.

VEX: The court is issuing a picnic relocation.

MARA: The court is explaining the instrument and returning to lunch.

SFX: A second hand fan moves air from the north. The vane swivels.

MARA: Now the breeze comes from the north, and the arrow points north.

VEX: The court has repaired the witness.

PIP: The picnic is still wet.

ODO: The town cannot reimburse the weather. It can post an explanation under the vane.

MARA: I'll write: "Arrow points from the wind."

SFX: Chalk writes on a small board. Nari props it beneath the vane.

NARI: The note is at eye level now.

PIP: I can read it from the picnic lawn.

VEX: It still doesn't say whether rain is coming.

ODO: The forecast board beside it does. It lists the chance of showers and the time it was posted.

MARA: They answer different questions. The vane shows current wind; the forecast estimates future weather.

NARI: I'll keep both signs together and replace the forecast at sunset.

PIP: The pavilion is dry. We can spread the blanket there while it airs.

VEX: And my picnic?

PIP: You can move it under the pavilion.
