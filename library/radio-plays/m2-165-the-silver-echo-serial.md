# The Silver Echo Serial

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: cliffhanger adventure serials, recap-heavy openings, ominous sound effects, and a hero who narrates each obvious move*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-006*

CAST
CAPTAIN CLOVE, a gibbon explorer
NORI, a capuchin mapmaker
THE ECHO, a squirrel monkey in the next room

[DRUMROLL. WIND HOWLS.]

CLOVE: Previously on The Silver Echo: we found a silver bell beneath the old tower, and its sound seemed to come from behind us.

NORI: It came from the stairwell. We were facing the wall.

CLOVE: Our journey now continues into the lower chamber, where every shadow hides a secret and every door opens somewhere else.

[DOOR CREAKS.]

NORI: This is the same chamber. We're standing beside the bell.

CLOVE: The chamber has changed since last week.

NORI: The bell is still on the table. The map is still on the floor.

CLOVE: Then the mystery is remarkably consistent. Nori, read the inscription.

NORI: "Ring once for entry. Ring twice for return."

CLOVE: We ring once.

NORI: We should first decide which door we want to open.

CLOVE: The one ahead.

[ONE BELL RING. A DOOR OPENS.]

NORI: That opened the cupboard.

CLOVE: A hidden room! I shall enter.

NORI: It's where they keep the spare lanterns.

CLOVE: The secret archive of light.

[A LANTERN CLINKS.]

NORI: There are six lanterns and a box of matches.

CLOVE: We may need every one. This chamber is dark.

NORI: The lights are on.

CLOVE: We may need every one for the part of the chamber that is dark in the future.

[FOOTSTEPS. A LOW HUM.]

CLOVE: Do you hear the silver echo?

NORI: I hear someone humming through the wall.

CLOVE: The echo speaks in a language older than the mountain.

THE ECHO: I'm trying to tune the radio in the next room.

NORI: There's a radio room next door. The tower caretaker uses it.

CLOVE: Then we must cross the chamber and confront the mysterious broadcaster.

NORI: We can use the door.

[DOOR OPENS. A SHORT WALK. SECOND DOOR OPENS.]

THE ECHO: Hello. I'm Echo. I was asked to test the bell line.

CLOVE: You are the Echo?

THE ECHO: I'm a squirrel monkey named Echo. My desk is in this room.

NORI: That explains why the sound came from behind us.

CLOVE: But what of the inscription?

THE ECHO: The bell controls the old service door. One ring opens it. Two rings close it. The cupboard isn't connected.

NORI: We rang once, and the service door opened behind the map shelf.

[DOOR CREAKS.]

CLOVE: A passage! We have found the way out.

THE ECHO: That leads to the courtyard. It's the normal exit.

CLOVE: Our adventure has carried us through danger to the outside world.

NORI: We started inside a tower. The courtyard is outside.

CLOVE: A satisfying resolution nonetheless. Echo, will you join our expedition?

THE ECHO: I can join after I finish the broadcast test.

NORI: The radio is live.

[ON-AIR LIGHT CLICKS.]

THE ECHO: Good evening. The bell works. The service door opens. The explorers have found the courtyard.

CLOVE: We are still here.

THE ECHO: The microphone picks you up from the courtyard.

CLOVE: We have been followed by our own story.

[THEME SWELLS.]

NORI: That was the recap. We haven't gone outside yet.

CLOVE: Then next week, we shall venture through the door.

THE ECHO: It's open now.

CLOVE: The suspense is unbearable.

[ONE FINAL BELL RING. THE SERVICE DOOR OPENS WIDER.]

NORI: We are stepping into the courtyard now.

[FOOTSTEPS THROUGH THE DOOR. BIRDS. OPEN AIR.]

CLOVE: We have escaped the tower and reached the outside world. The silver echo was a sound check, the bell opened a door, and the courtyard is real.

THE ECHO: You can borrow the bell for your next episode, but bring it back before evening.

NORI: That is the whole mystery solved.

CLOVE: Except which of us was standing closest to the door.

NORI: I was.

CLOVE: Then next week we investigate why the hero was behind the mapmaker.

ANNOUNCER: The courtyard has been reached. The next cliffhanger concerns a single footprint.

[CLIFFHANGER MUSIC CUTS OFF.]

[CLIFFHANGER MUSIC CUTS OFF.]
