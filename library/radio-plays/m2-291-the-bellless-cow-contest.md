# The Bellless Cow Contest

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: rural radio contests, livestock identification, and commentators who confuse a bell with the animal carrying it*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST LUMA, a lemur
FARMER ODO, a baboon
LISTENER NARA, a gibbon
JUDGE PIP, a capuchin

[SFX: FIELD WIND, COWS LOW.]

LUMA: We are live at the county's bellless cow contest. Farmers identify their own cows by sound, without tags or bells.

ODO: My cow Juniper has a low moo, then a short snort.

PIP: You will have five calls to identify her.

[SFX: COW MOOS, SNORTS.]

ODO: That is Juniper.

NARA (PHONE): The sound came from the left field.

ODO: Juniper is in the right field.

PIP: The wind is carrying the sound.

LUMA: The contest has begun with disputed geography.

A second cow moos. Odo identifies her as Fern. Pip asks him to describe the difference.

ODO: Fern holds the note longer.

[SFX: LONG MOO.]

PIP: That was a gate hinge.

[SFX: HINGE CREAKS.]

ODO: The hinge is new.

NARA: It sounds like Fern.

LUMA: The gate has entered the contest.

Pip places a recording of Juniper's moo through a small speaker. Two cows answer, and Odo points to the wrong one.

ODO: That is Juniper's call.

PIP: The speaker played Juniper.

ODO: Then the cow answering is Fern.

[SFX: TWO MOO ANSWERS.]

NARA: The cows are answering each other.

LUMA: We have a call-and-response round.

Odo says Juniper always snorts after a moo. A cow snorts, but it is the animal standing behind him.

PIP: Juniper is behind you.

ODO: She has moved.

LUMA: The contestant has followed the sound.

PIP: The rules say farmers stay at the microphone.

[SFX: BOOTS STOP IN MUD.]

Odo returns to the microphone. He asks to see the farm map. Pip notes that the microphone is beside a metal water trough, which echoes every call. The judge moves it to a patch of grass. Now each moo sounds softer and closer.

ODO: I can hear the breath at the end.

NARA: Juniper has a little cough.

[SFX: MOO, SOFT COUGH.]

ODO: That is her.

PIP: Correct. The cough is not on the official scoring card.

LUMA: The farmer has identified the cow from a feature not listed in the rules.

PIP: We will allow it. The contest tests recognition, not the card.

Nara calls in with one last guess. She identifies the gate hinge as Fern.

[SFX: HINGE CREAK.]

NARA: The long note is back.

ODO: That is the gate.
 
Pip asks Odo to stand beside Juniper while Luma moves the microphone to the far side of the field. Odo calls her name once. Juniper answers with the low moo and short snort. The gate hinge creaks at the same distance.

[SFX: MOO, SNORT, HINGE.]

NARA: The snort came after the moo.

PIP: The hinge came before it.

ODO: Juniper is closer.

LUMA: The microphone has moved.

PIP: We need a fixed position for the next round.

They mark a safe spot with a white flag, keeping the cable away from the trough and gate. Odo listens from the mark and identifies Fern by the longer note. The gate stays still; the scorekeeper records the cow, not the hinge.

ODO: The rules are finally measuring the animals.

NARA: And the gate has been disqualified twice.


PIP: You have learned the sound.

NARA: I have learned the gate.

Luma asks Odo to make one final call. He closes his eyes and listens. Two cows moo; Juniper answers with the short snort.

ODO: That is Juniper.

[SFX: MOO, SNORT.]

PIP: Correct.

LUMA: The winner is Odo, by one cough.

NARA: And the gate?

PIP: Not eligible.

ODO: I will oil it after the broadcast.

LUMA: The bellless cow contest ends with a cow identified, a gate disqualified, and no animal fitted with a bell.

[SFX: COWS LOW, HINGE SILENT.]
