# The Midnight Map Room

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: expedition serial convention, where a map is treated as destiny even when the route is around the corner*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Captain NARA, macaque; navigator TOVI, gibbon; recorder POM, capuchin; guide ELI, baboon.

[SFX: NIGHT WIND, PAPER UNFOLDING.] 

NARA: At midnight, our expedition reached the edge of the unknown.

ELI: It is the edge of the orchard.

NARA: The map says unknown.

TOVI: The map is folded over the part that says orchard.

POM: Our listeners cannot see a fold.

NARA: They can hear one.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLE.] 

TOVI: There. The route is a straight path around the water barrel.

NARA: A perilous water barrel.

ELI: It is empty.

NARA: Worse. We cannot tell how deep the peril goes.

Eli taps the side. The barrel rolls with a hollow clunk. Pom records the sound as "the drum of ancient ground."

ELI: It was a storage barrel. I moved it this morning.

POM: You moved an ancient ground?

NARA: The map has a dotted line through the barrel.

TOVI: The dotted line marks the cart path.

NARA: Why would a cart go through unknown territory?

ELI: Because it takes apples to the shed.

Nara declares they must establish camp before proceeding. Tovi unfurls a small blanket. It covers only one boot.

TOVI: The camp is ready.

NARA: This is a blanket for a very small explorer.

ELI: It is a mat.

POM: The microphone hears a dispute about equipment.

NARA: The microphone is not part of the expedition.

POM: It is how the expedition exists.

Tovi points out the north star, then the painted arrow on the shed. Nara refuses to trust the arrow because it is not on the map. Eli turns the map around. The path appears to point directly at the barrel.

NARA: We must cross the barrel.

ELI: Walk around it.

NARA: That would be improvisation.

POM: The map is upside down.


Nara orders Tovi to make a rubbing of the route before they move the barrel. Tovi lays paper on the ground and rubs with a charcoal nub. The map's dotted line appears, but so do tire marks from Eli's cart.

[SFX: CHARCOAL SCRAPE.]

NARA: The land is leaving us a message.

ELI: That is a wheel track.

POM: Can we record the message?

ELI: The message says I need my cart back.

NARA: We shall return it after we decipher the path.

Eli lifts the barrel; the cart rolls free. The wheel follows the exact route printed on the map. Tovi compares the track and the dotted line.

TOVI: The map was not predicting the cart. It was drawn after it passed.

NARA: Then the cart is a scout.

ELI: The cart is mine.

[SFX: THREE STEPS, THEN A THUD.] 

Nara bumps the barrel. It rolls a few feet and reveals the path marker underneath.

ELI: That marker says "Shed, 20 paces."

NARA: The barrel concealed the key to the route.

TOVI: It concealed a sign.

NARA: Adventure is often a sign someone forgot to uncover.

They count paces. Pom's microphone cable catches on the barrel; it rolls back, blocking the path again.

POM: The unknown has returned.

ELI: Hold the cable. I'll move the barrel.

NARA: No one moves it until I record the crossing.

Eli rolls it aside. The sound is exactly the hollow drum Pom recorded earlier.

POM: Our ancient ground has migrated.

Tovi reads the map correctly. The group reaches the shed, whose door is already open. Inside is a lantern and the next day's route.

NARA: We have found the end of the unknown.

ELI: It is a shed.

NARA: At midnight, a shed is a destination.

[SFX: MAP FOLDS, BARREL SETTLES.]

POM: Captain, where does tomorrow's dotted line go?

NARA: Around the barrel. We know it is there now.

TOVI: That is called a path.

NARA: The map calls it a sequel.
