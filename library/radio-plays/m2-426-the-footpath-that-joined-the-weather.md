# The Footpath That Joined the Weather

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: immersive nature documentaries, reverent field-recording narration, and producers who insist every habitat has a story arc*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: Two wildlife recordists try to capture a quiet dawn trail, while their producer keeps stitching unrelated sounds into a dramatic migration.

Cast: Narrator Sumi, a gibbon; field recordist Tavi, a macaque; producer Neri, an orangutan; ranger Leko, a capuchin; editor Pavo, a baboon.

[SFX: DAWN FOREST. A SMALL BIRD CALLS TWICE.]

SUMI: Before sunrise, the valley wakes one sound at a time.

PAVO: That is a bird in the north grove.

NERI: It is a studio bird on the effects shelf.

TAVI: The field microphone is still recording.

[SFX: WIND ON GRASS, THEN FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL.]

SUMI: The trail approaches.

LEKO: I am walking to the water pump. The trail does not approach.

ACT ONE

Neri has asked Tavi to record the dawn chorus without narration. Pavo wants a sequence that suggests the animals are gathering. The microphones are placed at the north grove, the pump, and a wooden bridge. Leko walks through the grove, then across the bridge, checking that no cables cross the path.

NERI: We need one hour of clean sound.

PAVO: We need an emotional journey.

TAVI: The birds are in the north grove. The stream is below the bridge. They are not traveling together.

Sumi records a short narration about the first light. Neri asks her not to call it a migration unless an animal actually migrates. Pavo plays a low drum under the wind and adds a distant hoof sound.

LEKO: That is my toolbox rolling down the slope.

PAVO: The sound has urgency.

TAVI: The toolbox is empty now.

ACT TWO

Tavi moves the microphone to the stream. A water strider skims across the surface; a frog calls from the bank. Pavo asks if the strider can be made to sound larger. Tavi turns down the gain to avoid clipping. Neri tells Pavo to stop adding music. The producer agrees, then plays a heartbeat under the water.

NERI: That is not a frog.

PAVO: It is a pulse.

LEKO: It is my boot tapping while I wait for you to move the microphone.

Sumi introduces the stream as a place where small animals cross at their own pace. Pavo cuts to a thunder effect, but a real gust hits the bridge and rattles the loose rail. Tavi stops recording and checks the safety line. Leko tightens the rail bolt. The gust fades. No animal approaches while they work.

TAVI: The weather interrupted the recording.

PAVO: The weather gives us a turning point.

NERI: The rail gave us a repair.

ACT TWO-B

The team reviews the field audio. The north grove recording contains a bird call and a distant pump. The bridge recording has wind, footsteps, and the repaired rail. The stream recording has water, frog, and a faint metallic rattle. Pavo tries to arrange the clips so the bird call answers the frog. Sumi asks to hear the unedited tracks first.

SUMI: The bird calls at dawn. The frog calls after the sun warms the bank.

PAVO: It could be a reunion.

TAVI: It is two recordings at two times.

Neri proposes a sound map with three clearly named locations. Pavo says that is less cinematic. Sumi reads the names slowly: north grove, footbridge, stream bank. Leko walks the path once more. His steps pass each microphone in order and stop at the pump.

LEKO: That is one walk along one path.

PAVO: It does have a beginning and end.

NERI: So does the day. We do not need a chase.

Pavo removes the drum, hoof and heartbeat. The three natural recordings remain distinct. Sumi narrates only what the listeners can hear and leaves pauses for the actual sounds.

ACT THREE

The program airs the sound map. The bird calls from the north grove. The stream answers with water and one frog. The bridge catches the wind, then settles. Sumi gives the location and time for each recording. Pavo lets every clip play at its real length. No animal is described as approaching another.

A listener calls to ask if the footpath is open. Leko says it is open until dusk, but the bridge rail is newly repaired and should not be climbed. Neri thanks him for the practical note. Pavo asks if they can call the sound map a journey. Sumi says listeners can make their own route between the labeled sites.

TAG

[SFX: A TOOLBOX ROLLS ONE INCH AND STOPS.]

PAVO: The final sound of the valley.

LEKO: The toolbox is back on flat ground.

FINAL IMAGE: Three labeled audio tracks play in separate channels while the forest remains outside the editing room.
