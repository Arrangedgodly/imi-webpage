# The Library Lost and Found

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public library community radio hour*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Librarian Hesta, a macaque; junior librarian Pip, a marmoset; caller Nell, a colobus; caller Daro, a capuchin; and the library's lost umbrella, heard rolling around the studio.

[SFX: BOOK CART ROLLS. A SMALL UMBRELLA CLACKS AGAINST A CHAIR.] 

HESTA: Good afternoon. This is Shelf Notes, broadcasting from the Bellwether Library. We have books, a lost-and-found shelf, and an umbrella that has been moved three times and continues to fall over. Pip, what is the oldest item waiting for its owner?

PIP: A green scarf. It has been here since the first frost.

HESTA: What is the newest?

PIP: The umbrella.

[SFX: UMBRELLA CLACKS AGAIN.] 

HESTA: It has a wooden handle and a paper tag that says "not mine." We are taking calls. Nell, you left a book in the reading room?

NELL, PHONE: I left a field guide open to the page about moss. It has a pressed leaf between the pages. I went back, and it was gone.

PIP: We found it in the children's room. A child thought the leaf was a bookmark and moved it with the book.

HESTA: The leaf is still there. We have kept the book face down so it does not slip out.

NELL: Thank you. I want to know the name of that moss. The leaf came from a tree near the reservoir.

HESTA: Bring it to the desk and we can help you find a plant guide.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CLICK. ANOTHER LINE BEEPS.] 

HESTA: Daro says his child has misplaced a blue boot.

DARO, PHONE: It is one boot. The other is on the child's foot. We searched the bus, the path, and the playground.

PIP: We have a blue boot in the lost-and-found.

HESTA: Is it the right size?

DARO: That would be a useful clue.

PIP: Size five. There is a dinosaur sticker on the heel.

DARO: That's it. We will come before closing.

HESTA: We will hold it at the desk. Please bring the other boot so we can make sure they are a pair.

[SFX: A DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS APPROACH.] 

PIP: The umbrella is rolling again.

HESTA: It has wheels?

PIP: No. The floor slopes toward the door. The handle is round.

HESTA: Can we set it in a basket?

PIP: The basket is holding three library cards and a jar of marbles.

HESTA: Why is there a jar of marbles?

PIP: Someone brought it to the children's story hour and forgot it.

[SFX: GLASS JAR LID TWISTS.] 

HESTA: We can put the umbrella against the wall, handle down, and tie the strap around the hook. It should stay upright.

PIP: It is staying.

HESTA: We have solved the umbrella's temporary problem. We have not solved who owns it.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.] 

CALLER: I left a recipe card in the library book about soup. It has my grandmother's handwriting.

PIP: We found a card in the cookbook shelf. It says "add water until it looks right."

CALLER: That's hers. She never used measurements.

HESTA: We will keep the card at the front desk. If you want, we can make a copy before you pick it up.

CALLER: Please do. My sister wants the recipe too.

HESTA: That is a good use for the copier.

[SFX: COPIER STARTS, MAKES A LOW GRINDING SOUND, THEN PRINTS.] 

PIP: The copier worked.

HESTA: And it only asked once.

PIP: The umbrella is leaning.

HESTA: Put the basket beside it.

[SFX: BASKET SET DOWN. UMBRELLA STOPS ROLLING.] 

HESTA: The lost-and-found shelf is open until six. Describe an item clearly. We cannot promise every object will be claimed, but we can keep it safe while you look. If you found something, bring it to the desk rather than taking it home to guess who owns it.

PIP: The blue boot has been collected. The recipe card has a copy. The green scarf is still waiting.

HESTA: And the umbrella?

PIP: It is holding up the basket.

[SFX: BOOK CART ROLLS AWAY. UMBRELLA REMAINS STILL.]
