# The Last Seat on the Night Bus

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night transit radio, where seat allocation and host narration inflate an ordinary ride into a live crisis*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST RUMA, a lemur bus-radio host
DRIVER ODO, a baboon
PASSENGER VELA, a gibbon carrying a folding stool
DISPATCHER PIP, a capuchin at central control
DRUMMER, a ring-tailed lemur traveling with a drum
TIRED PASSENGER, a macaque on the hill route

[SFX: BUS ENGINE IDLES, RAIN TICKS ON ROOF.]

RUMA: This is Night Route Radio, reporting from the last bus before dawn. Driver Odo has one empty seat and three passengers who each have a different claim to it.

ODO: The seat is empty because the route has not reached the hill stop.

VELA: I have a reservation.

PIP (RADIO): The dispatch board shows one seat available.

RUMA: Our listeners can hear the tension in the upholstery.

[SFX: SEAT SPRING SQUEAKS.]

ODO: That was the seat.

VELA: It sounded nervous.

PIP: The reservation list has Vela, two parcels, and a name written as "whoever gets there first."

RUMA: We have three claimants and one seat. A classic overnight transit crisis.

ODO: It is a bus. We can wait for the next stop.

VELA: Market stop is my stop. I must be rested for my speech.

RUMA: What is the speech about?

VELA: The importance of not standing in the aisle.

[SFX: BUS DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS BOARD.]

RUMA: Two more passengers have boarded. One is carrying a drum.

ODO: The drum has its own ticket.

RUMA: The audience has heard the drum's argument.

[SFX: DRUM ROLL.]

DRUMMER: I am not arguing. I am keeping time.

VELA: The seat is assigned to me.

PIP: Your reservation is for a seat, not that seat.

VELA: The other seats are near the heater.

ODO: It is a bus. All seats are near the heater.

RUMA: We are following this dispute as it travels.

[SFX: TURN SIGNAL TICKS.]

ODO: Hill stop. Anyone getting off?

No one moves. Odo opens the door. Cold air enters. Vela's folding stool unfolds with a snap, blocking the aisle.

VELA: I brought my own seat.

PIP: That is a stool and it is blocking the exit.

VELA: It is a portable reservation.

ODO: Fold it before someone trips.

[SFX: STOOL CLACKS, FOLDS.]

RUMA: Our seat dispute has created a second seat, which cannot be used while it is folded.

DRUMMER: I can sit on my drum case.

ODO: The case is not a seat.

DRUMMER: It is a case with a cushion on top.

PIP: The cushion belongs to the drum.

DRUMMER: Then I will stand beside it.

Vela offers the single bus seat to a tired passenger who has been standing quietly. The passenger takes it. Vela unfolds her stool only after Odo confirms it is stable and out of the aisle. She sits facing backward.

RUMA: The claimant has withdrawn her claim.

VELA: I have moved to a more portable jurisdiction.

PIP (RADIO): The dispatch board now shows one available stool.

ODO: There is no stool on the board.

[SFX: BUS ENGINE REVVS, STOOL LEG TAPS FLOOR.]

The dispatcher reports over the radio that the next bus has a lift for passengers who cannot use the folding stool. Vela says she can wait on the curb with the stool folded; her speech argues for keeping aisles clear.

PIP (RADIO): The listeners are asking whether the empty seat can be reserved for the drum.

ODO: The drum is not a passenger.

DRUMMER: It has a ticket.

RUMA: Our studio has received a complaint from the seat spring.

[SFX: SPRING SQUEAKS TWICE.]

VELA: The seat objects to being discussed as furniture.

ODO: It can file a complaint at the depot.

PIP (RADIO): The depot is closed.

ODO: Then the complaint can wait until morning.

The drummer lifts the drum case to make room for the tired passenger. The case rolls once against the rail and settles upright. Vela steps off at her stop only after Odo brings the bus to a full halt. She folds the stool and waits beneath the shelter for the lift-equipped bus.

[SFX: DOORS OPEN, RAIN SOFTENS.]

RUMA: The route has delivered one passenger to a seat and one speaker to the curb.

ODO: Nobody fell over.

VELA: I will deliver my speech from the shelter.

RUMA: Please keep the aisle clear for the words.

[SFX: DOORS CLOSE, BUS ENGINE IDLES.]
 
PIP (RADIO): The next bus is three minutes behind you.

VELA (OFF): I will wait here.

RUMA: This was Night Route Radio. The last seat remains occupied, the portable jurisdiction is folded, and the drum has stopped campaigning.

[SFX: BUS PULLS AWAY, DRUM BRUSHES A QUIET BEAT.]
