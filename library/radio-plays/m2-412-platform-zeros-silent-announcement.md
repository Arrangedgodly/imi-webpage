# Platform Zero's Silent Announcement

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: railway announcement systems that use theatrical suspense and vague platform branding instead of clear service information*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Announcer Tessa, a macaque; station manager Viko, a langur; conductor Rell, a capuchin; passenger Uma, a gibbon.

[SFX: STATION CLOCK TICKS. TRAIN BRAKES SQUEAL FAR AWAY.] 

TESSA: Attention, passengers. Platform Zero invites you to experience the arrival of the evening train.

UMA: Which platform is it arriving at?

VIKO: Platform Zero. The sign was installed for the new audio experience.

RELL: There is no platform zero. The evening train uses Platform Two.

TESSA: The announcement says zero because the sponsor wanted a clean, mysterious brand.

UMA: I would prefer the platform number.

[SFX: SPEAKER CHIMES THREE NOTES; THEN LONG PAUSE.] 

TESSA: A moment of silence for the journey ahead.

UMA: Is the train delayed?

VIKO: It is on time. The pause is part of the announcement.

RELL: The doors open in two minutes. I can see the train.

TESSA: We cannot say that yet. The dramatic reveal comes after the pause.

UMA: I have been standing here through three pauses.

[SFX: SPEAKER PLAYS A LOW DRUM ROLL. TRAIN HORN SOUNDS.] 

RELL: The train is here. Platform Two, track side.

VIKO: Please let the announcement finish before directing anyone.

UMA: The doors are open. People are boarding.

TESSA: And now the reveal: the train has arrived.

UMA: You announced that after it happened.

TESSA: The name Platform Zero tested well with the focus group.

RELL: The signs say Two. The speaker says Zero. The ticket says Two. Which one should passengers follow?

VIKO: The signage is correct. We should update the announcement.

UMA: My ticket says North Branch, car four. Is that on this train?

RELL: Yes. Car four is behind the blue stripe. Platform Two is the track under our feet.

TESSA: The app has pushed a notification: Your mysterious departure begins soon.

UMA: It says no time and no platform.

VIKO: The sponsor wrote that line. The station can override it.

TESSA: I will send a correction now. North Branch, Platform Two, car four behind the blue stripe.

[SFX: PHONE SEND TONE. A DOZEN PASSENGERS MOVE TOWARD THE BLUE STRIPE.]

RELL: They understood it. The blue stripe ends at the correct doors.

UMA: The mystery is solved by paint on the floor. The station map has the same blue stripe, and the car numbers are printed above the doors.

TESSA: The sponsor's prerecorded version has a slow build and a chime.

UMA: Could the chime say Platform Two?

[SFX: KEYBOARD CLICKS; TEST CHIME PLAYS, FOLLOWED BY A LONG, DRAMATIC PAUSE.] 

TESSA: That is still the old sequence. The platform word comes after the pause.

RELL: The train will depart before it finishes.

VIKO: Cut the suspense track. Use the station voice directly.

[SFX: A BUTTON CLICKS; CHIME STOPS.] 

TESSA: Service announcement. The evening train is boarding on Platform Two. It departs in one minute.

UMA: That is clear.

RELL: Passengers for the north branch, please board now.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS HURRY, SUITCASE WHEELS ROLL.] 

VIKO: The sponsor wants the zero logo on the sign.

UMA: Put it on the snack kiosk. There is nothing mysterious about a sandwich.

TESSA: I will record the station announcement without the pause.

VIKO: Include the platform, destination and departure time.

TESSA: North branch, Platform Two, departing now.

[SFX: TRAIN DOORS BEEP. UMA BOARDS.] 

UMA: I made it with four seconds to spare.

RELL: The old version would have announced the number after the doors shut.

[SFX: TRAIN ROLLS OUT; SPEAKER TONE CLICKS.] 

TESSA: The next train uses Platform Three. That is the actual platform, not a title.

VIKO: And the sound system will say it before the train arrives.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS; A SIMPLE PLATFORM TWO CHIME.]
