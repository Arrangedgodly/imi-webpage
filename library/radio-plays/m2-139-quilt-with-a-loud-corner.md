# The Quilt with a Loud Corner

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio quilting-circle programs, patient craft instruction, and one quilt square that announces itself through a loose patch of bells*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Anu, a macaque quilting host; Bea, a baboon stitcher; Nori, a gibbon caller; and Zim, a capuchin fabric cutter.*

[SFX: Sewing machine hum, cloth folds, tiny bells jingle.]

ANU: Welcome to Patchwork Hour. Tonight we join the East Grove quilting circle as they make a welcome quilt for the new library. Bea, what block are we sewing?

BEA: A simple four-patch. Light square, dark square, then repeat.

NORI: My square has bells in it.

ANU: That is a sound square.

ZIM: It is meant to be silent. The bells were inside the lining.

[SFX: Bells jingle.]

BEA: The quilt will hang in a reading room. It should not announce every turn of a page.

NORI: I can remove them.

ZIM: The bells are sewn into the patch.

ANU: We can unpick the seam. First, mark the corner and turn the fabric inside out.

NORI: Which corner?

BEA: The one with three blue stitches.

NORI: All four corners have three blue stitches.

ZIM: I added symmetry.

ANU: Then find the bell by sound. Tap gently.

[SFX: Three taps; a soft jingle from one corner.]

NORI: Found it.

BEA: Cut the thread, not the fabric.

ZIM: I have small scissors.

ANU: Hold the cloth flat. Do not pull the thread too hard.

[SFX: Scissors snip, cloth rustles.]

NORI: The bell is out. It has a little ribbon tied to it.

ZIM: That was supposed to be the library's bookmark pocket.

BEA: The pocket is attached to the bell?

ZIM: The bell is attached to the pocket.

ANU: We have a detachable bookmark chime.

BEA: The library asked for a quiet quilt.

NORI: I can sew the pocket back without the bell.

ZIM: The ribbon is too short now.

ANU: Fold a new strip from the edge fabric. Make the seam wide enough to hold a book marker.

[SFX: Sewing machine runs a short line.]

NORI: The pocket is back. It is quiet.

BEA: Test it with a bookmark.

NORI: The bookmark fits. It does not jingle.

ZIM: The bell can go on the door.

ANU: The library has a bell on its door.

ZIM: Then it can go on the quilt's storage bag.

BEA: The storage bag should also be quiet.

ZIM: I will keep it in my sewing basket.

ANU: The four-patch is complete. Light square, dark square, then a pocket square for the bookmark.

NORI: The quilt is warm and has no sound effects.

BEA: We will stitch the blocks together and finish the border next week.

ANU: The library opening is tomorrow.

BEA: Then we finish the border tonight.

[SFX: Sewing machine starts; bells remain silent.]

ZIM: I will cut the border strips. The chalk line is straight.

NORI: My patch is turned the wrong way.

BEA: It still fits.

ANU: The quilt can be asymmetrical as long as the pocket is accessible.

[SFX: Fabric squares are arranged and stitched.]

NORI: The four-patch holds. The pocket is at the lower corner.

BEA: The bookmark is inside.

ANU: The welcome quilt is complete. The library can hang it without alarming the readers.

ZIM: The bell will have a new job at home.

BEA: What job?

ZIM: It will remind me where I put the scissors.

[SFX: A bell jingles from the sewing basket; everyone laughs.]

ANU: This has been Patchwork Hour. Sew carefully, mark the corners, and keep the bells out of the book quilt.

ANU: The librarian arrives to collect the quilt. She needs the bookmark pocket on the reader's side.

LIBRARIAN: The pocket is on the outside edge. I can reach it from the chair.

NORI: No bells, no loose threads, and the marker fits.

BEA: The quilt is ready for the reading room.

ZIM: I kept the bell for the sewing basket. It rings only when I lift the lid.

[SFX: Basket opens; bell gives one small jingle.]

ANU: That is a suitable sound effect for Patchwork Hour.

[SFX: Sewing machine stops; cloth folds neatly.]
