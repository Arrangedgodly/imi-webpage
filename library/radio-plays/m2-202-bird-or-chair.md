# Is That a Bird or the Chair?

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio sound-identification contests that reward confidence before the recording finishes*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST JALI, a macaque
CONTESTANT TUP, a gibbon
CONTESTANT RAE, a langur
SOUND TECH KEM, a gorilla

[SFX: A SHORT, HIGH SQUEAK.]

JALI: Welcome to "Name That Sound." Contestant Tup, your guess?

TUP: A tree frog.

KEM: It was one second long.

RAE: We were supposed to hear the whole clip.

JALI: The buzzer is already on.

### Scene One: Round One

[KEM PLAYS THE CLIP AGAIN. IT SQUEAKS, THEN A CHAIR LEG SCRAPES.]

JALI: Listen for the full recording, not just the first sound.

[KEM PLAYS A SECOND CLIP: THREE LIGHT TAPS, A PAUSE, THEN A LONGER TAP.]

TUP: Woodpecker.

RAE: Someone is knocking on a hollow door.

KEM: It is a woodpecker on a tree trunk, recorded from inside the cabin.

JALI: Both guesses heard a tap. Only one heard the setting.

[TUP PRESSES THE BUZZER BEFORE THE RECORDING ENDS. IT COVERS THE FINAL TAP.]

RAE: He interrupted the clue again.

RAE: That is a chair moving across the floor.

[KEM PLAYS A THIRD CLIP. IT BEGINS WITH A LOW HUM, THEN A DOOR SLAMS, THEN THE HUM STOPS.] 

TUP: A thunderstorm.

RAE: A fan running until someone closes the door.

KEM: It is the studio fan and the sound booth door.

JALI: The recording contains its own setting.

[TUP WAITS FOR THE END THIS TIME.]

TUP: It starts like a frog.

JALI: The answer is a chair.

TUP: I was partly right.

KEM: There is no partial sound credit.

[JALI PRESSES A BUZZER. IT MAKES THE SAME SQUEAK AS THE CLIP.] 

RAE: Is the buzzer also the chair?

KEM: It needs oil.

### Scene Two: The Bonus Round

JALI: Bonus sound. Listen until the end.

[KEM PLAYS WATER DRIPPING INTO A METAL BUCKET. AFTER SIX DROPS, A DOOR OPENS AND FOOTSTEPS ENTER.] 

TUP: A cave.

RAE: A bucket under a leak. Then someone came in.

KEM: Correct. The recording was made in our hallway.

JALI: How many points?

KEM: One. For identifying the clip.

TUP: I heard water.

JALI: The recording continued after the water.

[KEM PLAYS THE CLIP AGAIN. TUP PRESSES THE BUZZER AFTER THE FIRST DROP.] 

TUP: Drip!

KEM: The clip has not ended.

[THE RECORDING CONTINUES WITH FOOTSTEPS AND A DOOR CREAK. TUP PRESSES THE BUZZER AGAIN.]

TUP: A hallway.

KEM: The complete clip was a bucket under a leak while someone entered the room.

JALI: The answer contains more than the first noise.

[RAE WAITS. THE DOOR OPENS IN THE RECORDING.] 

RAE: Someone came inside.

JALI: Now the whole sound has been heard.

### Scene Three: Final Clip

[KEM SETS A SMALL MICROPHONE UNDER THE STUDIO DESK. A SOFT SQUEAK SOUNDS.]

JALI: Our final sound is live.

[KEM PLAYS A LONGER RECORDING: A SQUEAK, THREE TAPS, THEN A DOOR OPENING AND A CUP SET ON A TABLE.]

TUP: The chair again.

RAE: The door and cup come after the squeak.

JALI: What can you conclude?

RAE: Someone entered and set a cup down. The squeak is probably from the chair.

KEM: That was our studio recording.

[TUP PRESSES THE BUTTON AFTER THE CUP SOUND. THE BUZZER CLICKS QUIETLY.] 

TUP: I waited for the whole clip.

TUP: A bird.

RAE: The chair.

[KEM STANDS. THE CHAIR SQUEAKS AGAIN.]

KEM: The studio chair has been identified.

JALI: Does that win the contest?

KEM: It explains the first sound.

[RAE WINS. JALI HANDS HER A SMALL TROPHY SHAPED LIKE AN EAR.]

[THE TROPHY HAS A BUTTON. TUP PRESSES IT; IT PLAYS THE FIRST SQUEAK CLIP AGAIN.] 

TUP: I recognize that one now.

TUP: Can I try again?

RAE: Listen all the way through.

### Tag

[SFX: CHAIR SQUEAK, THEN BUZZER SQUEAK.]

KEM: Same chair.

JALI: Different recording.
