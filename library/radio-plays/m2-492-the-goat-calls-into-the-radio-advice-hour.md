# The Goat Calls Into the Radio Advice Hour

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: advice programs that answer every problem with a scripted confidence*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; caller Pip, a capuchin; adviser Vex, an orangutan; producer Nari, a lemur; keeper Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Radio booth. Phone rings. A goat bleats faintly outside.

MARA: Welcome to Ask Us Anything. Caller, you're on the air.

PIP: My neighbor's goat has been standing in my garden for an hour.

VEX: Have you tried setting a boundary?

PIP: The fence has a gap.

VEX: Boundaries are important.

NARI: Is the goat eating the lettuce?

PIP: It has eaten half of it.

VEX: We can offer a three-step plan: acknowledge, affirm, and move on.

MARA: The goat is still eating.

ODO: I am the keeper. I'll come collect it.

VEX: We should not interrupt the advice segment.

PIP: Can someone close the gap while Odo comes?

MARA: Yes. Put a board across the opening, but don't trap the goat inside the garden.

SFX: Board scrapes. Goat bleats.

NARI: The goat heard the board.

VEX: It may be expressing a need for space.

ODO: It is looking for the clover patch. The garden gate was left open.

PIP: I can guide it out with a basket of clover.

VEX: Our show recommends calm communication.

MARA: Pip can speak calmly while moving the goat toward the gate.

SFX: Clover rustles. Hooves shuffle across soil.

PIP: This way. The clover is outside.

ODO: It is following you.

VEX: Our listeners expect a solution they can use without getting their hands dirty.

MARA: Some problems need an actual repair. Odo, can you check the fence while Pip watches the gate?

ODO: The board fits, but one nail is loose.

SFX: Hammer taps. Goat chews clover outside.

PIP: It is staying with the clover. The garden gate is shut.

VEX: Could we advise the caller to forgive the goat?

MARA: The goat did not choose a broken fence.

PIP: I can put the lettuce basket on the high shelf while Odo hammers.

NARI: Our advice should name who can fix what.

ODO: I can nail this slat back after the broadcast. Pip can move the lettuce seedlings to the raised bed until then.

PIP: I'll take the basket. The goat can have the clover, not the lettuce.

SFX: Seedlings lifted; soil crumbles.

VEX: That is a three-step plan after all.

MARA: It came from the situation. We didn't make one up before hearing it.

NARI: The listener line is open. A second caller says a neighbor's hen is in the shed.

ODO: I'll go check the shed after I finish the fence.

VEX: The show has become a list of errands.

PIP: They're the errands that solve the callers' problems.

NARI: The lettuce is safe.

VEX: What did you learn from this experience?

PIP: To fix the fence and keep the gate closed.

MARA: That's a practical answer.

VEX: Can we turn it into a three-step plan?

ODO: We can: close gate, mend fence, plant more lettuce.

PIP: I like the last step.

SFX: Goat reaches clover and munches.

NARI: The caller's garden is clear.

ODO: I'll put a temporary mesh across the repaired gap, then return with a proper board tomorrow.

PIP: I'll check the latch once more before I leave.

PIP: I'll water the raised bed and leave the gate latched.

NARI: The caller has a plan for tonight and a repair for morning.

VEX: Can we bill the fence under household wellness?

MARA: Bill it under lumber.

MARA: Our advice is complete.

VEX: Should we schedule a follow-up?

PIP: After the lettuce grows.

TAG

SFX: Phone clicks off. Goat bleats once.

VEX: We have another caller.

ODO: That's the goat.
