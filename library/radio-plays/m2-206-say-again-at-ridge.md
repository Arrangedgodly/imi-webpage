# Say Again at the Ridge

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: wilderness expedition broadcasts that inflate routine radio check-ins into heroic last transmissions*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST DARA, a macaque
RANGER KEL, a gorilla
HIKER SULI, a gibbon
PRODUCER PON, a capuchin

[SFX: WIND ON A RIDGE, RADIO STATIC.]

DARA: We are receiving a field check from the north trail. Hiker Suli, do you copy?

SULI: I copy. I am at the marked overlook.

PON: The signal is breaking up. We need an urgent music bed.

KEL: It is breaking up because you are holding the antenna.

### Scene One: The Check-In

[PON LETS GO OF THE ANTENNA. THE SIGNAL CLEARS.]

KEL: Suli, confirm your battery level.

SULI: Half. I have a spare in my pack.

PON: Could we call it a dwindling power supply?

KEL: Half is enough for this check-in.

[SULI OPENS THE PACK AND FEELS FOR THE SPARE. A ZIPPER SOUNDS OVER THE RADIO.]

DARA: We hear the pack opening.

SULI: I found the battery. It is still sealed.

KEL: No need to replace the one in the radio.

[PON STARTS A LOW BATTERY BEEP. SULI'S RADIO DOES NOT BEEP.]

SULI: That is your effect.

PON: The studio has no wilderness sounds.

KEL: It has the report.

DARA: Suli, report your position.

SULI: At marker six, beside the bench.

DARA: Are you in distress?

SULI: No. I am checking in as scheduled.

PON: Can you say "the trail is disappearing"?

SULI: The trail is right in front of me.

[KEL LOOKS AT THE MAP.]

KEL: The next marker is half a kilometer ahead. The route is open.

DARA: We have a clear transmission.

### Scene Two: The Static

[SFX: STATIC GROWS.]

PON: A storm is blocking the signal.

KEL: That is the studio fan.

[PON TURNS IT OFF. THE STATIC STOPS.]

DARA: Suli, can you hear us?

SULI: I can hear you. The wind is light.

DARA: Any hazards?

SULI: A branch is across the trail by marker seven. I can walk around it safely, but I am reporting it to the ranger.

KEL: I will send the trail crew after this broadcast.

PON: Could we call it a fallen tree?

SULI: It is a branch. I can step around it.

[PON PLAYS A LOW RUMBLE. SULI'S RADIO PICKS IT UP.] 

SULI: That sound is coming from your studio.

KEL: It is the air vent.

### Scene Three: The Return

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL, THEN A CLEAR RADIO BEEP.]

DARA: Suli has checked in from marker eight. Is the route open?

SULI: Yes. The branch is marked with a trail ribbon.

KEL: The crew will remove it after the rain.

PON: We could end with a rescue helicopter sound.

DARA: No one needs rescuing.

[SULI'S RADIO CLICKS.]

SULI: I am leaving the overlook and heading back to the trailhead.

[THE RADIO CLICKS AGAIN. THE RETURN SIGNAL IS STRONGER NOW THAT SULI IS CLOSER TO THE VALLEY.]

KEL: We have a clear signal.

DARA: The hiker is returning by the marked route.

PON: No rescue?

KEL: No rescue.

DARA: The check-in is complete.

KEL: The trail is open.

DARA: We have a normal return transmission.

PON: The audience may feel let down.

KEL: The audience knows where the hiker is.

PON: The program guide promises "an edge-of-the-map encounter."

KEL: The map has a border. The trail does not stop there.

DARA: The guide also says "unconfirmed danger."

SULI: I can confirm the danger is a sign pointing back to the trailhead.

[KEL UNFOLDS THE PAPER MAP. IT CRACKLES LOUDLY OVER THE RADIO.]

PON: That sounds like a collapsing tent.

KEL: It is a map. I am folding it again.

DARA: Please don't fold the hiker into the report.

[THE STUDIO FAN TURNS ON BY ITSELF. STATIC RETURNS FOR A MOMENT.]

PON: The ridge is back.

KEL: The fan is back.

[SULI REACHES THE TRAILHEAD. THE RADIO CLICK IS CLEAR AND SHORT.]

SULI: I am back at the trailhead. Check-in complete.

DARA: We have confirmed arrival.

PON: Can we play the helicopter now?

KEL: No. The hiker is at the trailhead.

### Tag

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF.]

DARA: End of field check.

PON: End of the adventure?

KEL: End of the broadcast.
