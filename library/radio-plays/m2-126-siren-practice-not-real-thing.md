# Siren Practice, Not the Real Thing

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio emergency-alert tests, reassuring disclaimers, and a test siren whose operator keeps finding reasons to test it again*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Odo, a macaque emergency announcer; Ressa, an orangutan safety chief; Nix, a capuchin siren operator; and Fen, a gibbon caller.*

[SFX: Alert tone begins, then stops abruptly.]

ODO: This is a scheduled siren test. This is only a test. There is no emergency.

NIX: The siren stopped before the second tone.

RESSA: That's why we are testing it.

FEN: Should I follow the evacuation route?

RESSA: No. It is a test.

FEN: I am already outside.

ODO: You may remain outside or return to your home.

FEN: I left my lunch inside.

RESSA: This is not a lunch retrieval drill.

NIX: The test panel has three buttons: TEST, RESET, and DO NOT PRESS.

ODO: Which one did you press?

NIX: TEST.

RESSA: Why is the warning light on?

NIX: It is always on during tests.

ODO: Then the warning is working.

[SFX: A low siren begins.]

ODO: Attention, residents. This is a test. The sound you hear is not an emergency.

FEN: It sounds like a real siren.

NIX: That is because it is the real siren being tested.

RESSA: Please do not add details that cause confusion.

ODO: The test will last ten seconds.

[SFX: Siren continues for ten beats, then cuts off.]

NIX: It lasted eleven.

RESSA: One extra second is not an emergency.

FEN: Is that the official message?

ODO: We can add it to the next test.

RESSA: No.

NIX: The reset button is blinking.

RESSA: Press RESET.

[SFX: Button clicks; a short chirp sounds.]

ODO: Residents, the siren has stopped. The test is over. There is no emergency.

FEN: The chirp sounded like another alert.

NIX: It is the reset confirmation.

RESSA: We need to tell residents that the chirp is not a new test.

ODO: Attention, residents. The brief chirp you just heard means the test has ended.

FEN: What if someone missed the first announcement?

RESSA: Then they heard the end message.

ODO: Attention, residents who heard only the second announcement: the first one said this was a test.

NIX: The control panel shows "test complete."

RESSA: Good. Turn the light off.

NIX: The light is labeled DO NOT PRESS.

RESSA: That's the button.

NIX: There is no switch for the light.

FEN: The warning light is still on.

ODO: It may remain on until maintenance.

RESSA: We should not broadcast every panel detail.

[SFX: Phone rings.]

CALLER: My neighbor heard the siren and brought over a casserole.

RESSA: That is kind, but there is no emergency.

CALLER: Should I take it back?

ODO: No. The casserole is not part of the test.

NIX: The siren has passed inspection. We can close the station.

RESSA: One final announcement: the test is complete. Thank you for listening and for not changing your plans.

FEN: I changed mine. I came outside for lunch.

ODO: You may now return inside for lunch.

FEN: I will retrieve it quietly.

[SFX: Door opens; a plate is moved.]

RESSA: The test worked, the tone stopped, and the community received a casserole.

NIX: Should we test it again next week?

RESSA: Only on the scheduled date.

ODO: This has been a test. The message is complete.

NIX: Maintenance has found the problem. The test button is fine; the end-of-test chime was turned too high.

RESSA: Can you lower it?

NIX: Yes. I will set it to one quiet beep.

[SFX: One soft beep.]

ODO: That sounds like a test ending.

FEN: It also sounds like my kitchen timer.

RESSA: We will label the panel clearly and leave it off until next week's scheduled test.

NIX: The warning light is off now.

ODO: The station is officially quiet.

[SFX: A plate clinks in the lunch room.]

[SFX: A tiny confirmation chirp; silence follows.]
