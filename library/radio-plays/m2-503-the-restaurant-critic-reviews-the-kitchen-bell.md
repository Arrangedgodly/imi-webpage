# The Restaurant Critic Reviews the Kitchen Bell

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: food review broadcasts that rate ambiance over the meal*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Critic Vex, an orangutan; cook Mara, a macaque; server Pip, a capuchin; host Nari, a lemur; repairer Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Small diner. Kitchen bell rings. Pan sizzles.

NARI: Tonight we review the new neighborhood diner.

VEX: The entryway lacks a signature scent.

MARA: It smells like soup.

PIP: The bell rings when an order is ready.

SFX: Bell rings twice.

VEX: The bell is repetitive.

ODO: The striker is loose. I can tighten it after the orders go out.

MARA: We have three bowls waiting.

VEX: The table spacing is too ordinary.

PIP: It lets me carry soup without bumping chairs.

NARI: The kitchen is open. We can hear the cooks.

VEX: A successful restaurant should surprise us.

MARA: Today's soup is lentil with carrot. It is on the menu.

VEX: I expected a reveal.

PIP: The bowl is the reveal.

ODO: The kitchen ticket says one bowl without carrot.

MARA: That one is for Nari at the counter. She asked before we served.

VEX: Could the substitution be a surprise?

PIP: A surprise is not useful if someone ordered around an ingredient.

NARI: The menu has the ingredients listed. I can read them before I choose.

MARA: The soup pot is here. I'll ladle the plain lentil bowl separately.

SFX: Ladle dips. Bowl set down.

VEX: The room is getting busier. Our review needs an atmosphere note.

ODO: The bell rings once when an order is ready. It is not a mood effect.

MARA: We can make the room quieter by closing the service window between orders.

SFX: Window slides halfway shut. Pan hiss softens.

PIP: That makes it easier to hear the table.

VEX: I can hear the spoon touch the bowl.

MARA: That's people eating.

VEX: The diner is small.

NARI: It is also warm, the soup is good, and the server brought exactly what I asked for.

ODO: The review card can include the menu, service, and sound.

MARA: We have more orders. Odo, the bell is quiet now.

ODO: I can fix the striker after the lunch rush. For now, I'll watch the pass.

SFX: Kitchen bell rings once.

PIP: Bowl for table two.

NARI: I can carry it.

MARA: Use the tray. The floor is wet by the sink.

SFX: Tray slides beneath bowl. Footsteps cross safely.

VEX: That is good service, but is it memorable?

NARI: I'll remember the soup and that nobody made me wait while a critic discussed the door.

VEX: A fair review.

MARA: Odo, don't tighten the bell until the line is clear.

ODO: I can do it when the last order is out.

VEX: Fine. I will give the bell one point for clarity, no points for mystery.

SFX: Bowl set down.

VEX: The steam fogs my review card.

NARI: Try the soup before you write.

VEX: I review the experience, not just the taste.

MARA: The soup needs salt. The salt is on the table.

PIP: I can bring a spoon.

SFX: Spoon taps bowl. Vex tastes.

VEX: The lentils are tender. The carrot is sweet. This is a very good soup.

NARI: And the bell?

ODO: I tightened it. One ring now.

SFX: Bell rings once.

PIP: That means the next order is ready.

VEX: The bell has a clear purpose.

MARA: So does the soup.

NARI: The lunch rush is ending. The soup pot is empty.

VEX: I will finish my review before dessert.

MARA: We'll leave the menu by the door for evening diners.
