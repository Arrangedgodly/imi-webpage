# The Mail Carrier Delivers a Blank Postcard

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel broadcasts that turn a missing detail into an emotional mystery*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Mail carrier Nari, a lemur; host Vex, an orangutan; recipient Mara, a macaque; sorter Odo, a gibbon; sender Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Post office. Mail slot clacks. Envelope slides across counter.

VEX: A postcard has arrived from the coast, but it has no message.

MARA: It has a picture of a lighthouse.

ODO: The address is complete.

NARI: The stamp is cancelled. It traveled through three towns.

VEX: What does the blank side mean?

PIP: I sent it. I forgot to write anything.

MARA: You mailed me an empty card?

PIP: I wanted to send the lighthouse picture.

VEX: Perhaps the silence holds a deeper story.

NARI: The back is blank because Pip didn't write on it.

ODO: We should still deliver it.

MARA: I like the picture. I want to know whether Pip saw the lighthouse.

PIP: I did. The fog machine was broken, so you could see the whole bay.

VEX: That detail belongs in the travel segment.

NARI: The card doesn't have room for a second message.

PIP: I can call Mara.

SFX: Phone rings.

MARA: I have the card in my hand.

PIP: The lighthouse light made one slow turn while I watched.

MARA: I can see the beam in the picture.

VEX: The postcard has become a conversation.

ODO: The postal rules say the address must stay visible.

NARI: We could use the empty space for a return note.

VEX: Add "Wish you were here."

PIP: I was there. Mara was home.

MARA: Write "I saw the bay at low tide."

PIP: I'll send another card with that.

VEX: The blank one is more poetic.

NARI: It is more likely to be a mistake.

MARA: I'll keep it anyway.

ODO: The sorter can stamp the card with today's date and put it in the keepsake envelope.

VEX: That sounds like a museum treatment.

NARI: It's a way to keep the picture from bending.

MARA: The postmark shows it took four days to reach me.

PIP: I didn't know the blank card would travel through so many towns.

ODO: Each sorting mark is a little record of the route.

VEX: The card is a map now.

NARI: Only of the mail route, not the coast.

MARA: That's still useful. I'll put it beside the photo I took at the harbor last year.

PIP: Then the two pictures can compare the view.

VEX: Should there be a voiceover?

MARA: No. We'll make room for the next card.

MARA: The postmarks run through Ridge, Mill, and Harbor towns.

PIP: The card traveled farther than I walked.

ODO: I'll keep it flat in the envelope.

SFX: Card slides into envelope. Envelope enters drawer. Drawer closes.

VEX: A memento of the coast.

PIP: The post office has blank cards on the rack. I'll write what I saw on this new one instead.

SFX: A fresh postcard slides from the rack.

MARA: Tell me what you want me to know.

PIP: "The lighthouse lamp turns every few seconds. The tide was low. I liked the view."

ODO: That fits on the card.

NARI: The address is clear. I'll add a stamp from the counter roll.

VEX: We can send this one with a message.

MARA: I'll look for it in four days.

SFX: Card drops into the outgoing tray.

ODO: This tray is for letters leaving today. I'll sort it before closing.

MARA: Keep the picture card in the drawer. It can remind me where Pip went.

VEX: And the written one begins the next story.

NARI: With a message this time.

TAG

SFX: Mail slot clacks again.

NARI: Another blank postcard just came through the slot.

PIP: That one is mine too.
