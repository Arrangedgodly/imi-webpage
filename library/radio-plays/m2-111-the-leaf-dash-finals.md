# The Leaf Dash Finals

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live radio coverage of an obstacle race, breathless play-by-play, and a course redesigned mid-competition by the contestants*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Jori, a squirrel-monkey sportscaster; Dell, a macaque color analyst; Pika, a gibbon runner; and Momo, an orangutan course marshal.*

[SFX: A starter whistle, crowd claps, wind through leaves.]

JORI: Welcome to the Leaf Dash Finals. Four competitors race through the orchard, collect one fallen leaf, and return to the finish. Dell, what should listeners watch for?

DELL: Footwork, focus, and whether anyone remembers the leaf basket is at the start.

MOMO: The course is marked with blue ribbons. Follow them, do not climb the trees, and do not eat your leaf.

JORI: A reminder for the athletes and the crowd.

[SFX: Whistle. Feet pound along a path.]

JORI: They're off! Pika takes the inside route. Nolo follows the blue ribbon toward the shed.

DELL: The ribbon is tied to a branch. The course turns before the branch.

JORI: Nolo is climbing for a better line.

MOMO: He is out of bounds.

NOL0: I can see the finish from here.

MOMO: That is not the finish. That is a blue tarp.

DELL: The tarp is now a race landmark.

JORI: Pika has reached the leaf pile. She is selecting a leaf carefully.

PIKA: This one has a hole.

JORI: Is that a scoring advantage?

DELL: It is lighter, but the rules say collect one leaf, not one aerodynamic leaf.

MOMO: Pika, choose and run.

PIKA: I am choosing. The leaf has character.

[SFX: A gust lifts the pile; leaves scatter.]

JORI: The wind has entered the race and appears to be leading.

DELL: The wind has no bib number.

JORI: One leaf lands on Momo's marshal vest.

MOMO: I am not a competitor.

DELL: You're carrying the leaf.

MOMO: I am carrying the course clipboard.

JORI: The clipboard is now a collection device.

PIKA: I have my leaf. The other runners are chasing the pile.

DELL: The finish is behind us. Pika is running in the right direction, which gives her a rare advantage.

JORI: The others have formed a search party. Nolo is sweeping leaves into his hands.

MOMO: One leaf only.

NOL0: I am checking for the best one.

MOMO: You are holding eleven.

DELL: He has multiple finalists.

[SFX: Bell rings at finish.]

JORI: Pika reaches the line. Her leaf is intact, though it has stuck to her shoulder.

PIKA: I finished. Does the leaf count if it is wearing me?

MOMO: It counts if you can place it in the basket.

PIKA: I can.

[SFX: She peels leaf from shoulder and drops it into basket.]

JORI: Pika wins the Leaf Dash by one clean deposit.

DELL: Nolo is still selecting. He has asked the wind for a second opinion.

NOL0: The wind says this one.

MOMO: The wind is not an official judge.

JORI: The crowd is clapping. The basket is filling with unclaimed leaves.

DELL: Could be a record harvest.

MOMO: The event is a race, not a cleanup crew.

PIKA: We can clean the path after the award.

MOMO: Good. Everyone return only the leaves you picked up.

[SFX: Competitors walk back; a gentle rain of leaves.]

JORI: Pika receives the golden leaf medal. It is made of painted wood, so it will survive the weather.

PIKA: I will hang it by the finish line.

DELL: Next year, the course will use chalk arrows.

MOMO: And an actual finish marker.

JORI: This is the Leaf Dash Finals. One runner, one leaf, one very busy gust of wind. The course is now closed to climbing.

JORI: The course marshal has placed a branch across the path. Is that part of the obstacle?

MOMO: It fell naturally. We will leave it there.

DELL: Pika steps over it without dropping her leaf. Nolo tries to collect it.

NOL0: It is technically a leaf-bearing branch.

MOMO: The rule is one leaf. You cannot bring the whole tree.

JORI: The branch returns to the orchard. The course is clear, though the runners are still debating what counts as fallen.

[SFX: Whistle, then leaves rustle into a neat pile.]
