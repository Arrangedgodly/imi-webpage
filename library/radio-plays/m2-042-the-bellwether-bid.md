# The Bellwether Bid

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live charity auction broadcasts*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Auctioneer Vale, a lively macaque; stage caller Bim, a capuchin; donor Mrs. Keel, a langur; and bidder Marn, a cautious baboon who keeps bidding on the wrong lots.

[SFX: AUCTION GAVEL. ROOM MURMUR.] 

VALE: Welcome to the Bellwether Radio Auction. Every lot raises money for the community greenhouse roof. We will describe each item carefully. If you bid by phone, please wait until Bim repeats your number. Lot one is a pair of garden gloves, size medium, left glove green, right glove also green but with a patch.

BIM: Opening bid, five coins.

MARN: Seven.

VALE: Bidder four says seven.

MARN: I am bidder three.

BIM: Then bidder three says seven.

VALE: Sold to bidder three. Lot two, a handwoven basket from Mrs. Keel.

KEEL: It holds apples or seedlings, but not both at once. The bottom is narrow.

VALE: Useful detail. Opening bid, ten.

MARN: Twelve.

BIM: Bidder three says twelve.

MARN: I wanted the gloves.

VALE: The gloves are sold. You have won the basket.

MARN: I can use a basket.

KEEL: It does not hold tools.

MARN: Can it hold gloves?

KEEL: If the gloves are small.

[SFX: ROOM LAUGHS.] 

VALE: Lot three is a greenhouse bench, repaired last month. It seats two adults or one adult with a large watering can.

BIM: Opening bid, twenty.

MARN: Twenty-five.

VALE: Bidder three is attentive now.

BIM: I am the stage caller. That was my voice.

VALE: Bim, are you bidding?

BIM: No. I was reading the number.

VALE: We have a non-bid.

KEEL: I'll bid thirty. The greenhouse needs a place to sit while people wait for the hose.

MARN: Thirty-five.

KEEL: Forty.

MARN: Forty-five.

BIM: Bidder three, are you sure?

MARN: The bench sounds useful.

VALE: At forty-five, the bench goes to bidder three.

MARN: Does it need carrying?

BIM: Yes.

MARN: I thought it was a radio bench.

VALE: We are in a room. The bench is in the room.

MARN: I can carry it with help.

KEEL: I will help. The greenhouse is on my route.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS. Bim answers.] 

BIM: We have a phone bid for lot four, the handcart.

VALE: It has one squeaky wheel and a new handle. Do we hear twenty?

CALLER: Fifteen, if the wheel is included.

VALE: The wheel is attached.

CALLER: Then fifteen.

MARN: Eighteen.

VALE: Bidder three is bidding again.

MARN: I need a cart for the basket.

KEEL: The basket fits in your arms.

MARN: It may be holding gloves.

BIM: The gloves are yours too.

VALE: You bought them at the start.

MARN: Then I need the cart.

VALE: Sold at eighteen. The greenhouse fund has enough for the roof patch, the gutter, and the bench screws. It does not have enough for a new greenhouse. That is next month's problem.

KEEL: I can donate spare glass panels.

BIM: We will list them after measuring.

VALE: No surprise panels. We need the right size.

[SFX: GAVEL. APPLAUSE.] 

VALE: Thank you, Bellwether. Please collect what you won. Bim, take bidder three to the bench.

MARN: I thought I was bidder three.

BIM: You are. I'm stage caller.

MARN: Then who bought the gloves?

BIM: You did.

MARN: Good. I will wear them to carry the bench.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CHIMES.] 

BIM: One final donated lot. A set of labeled roof tiles from the old greenhouse.
VALE: These are not for sale. The committee will use them to match the roof patch.
MARN: Can I bid on one?
BIM: Not unless you plan to put it on the roof.
KEEL: He does have gloves now.
VALE: We will call this a volunteer lot. Anyone who helps carry the tiles gets tea.
MARN: I can carry two.
BIM: The sign says one at a time.
MARN: I have learned to read the signs.
VALE: Then the auction is over. The next lot is a quiet afternoon in a repaired greenhouse.
[SFX: GAVEL. APPLAUSE.]

[SFX: BENCH SCRAPES, ROOM LAUGHS, RADIO THEME.]
