# The Coat Check Count-In

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live event broadcasts, coat-check tickets, and announcers who confuse a numbered hook with a seat in the audience*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST LUMA, a capuchin
COAT CHECKER PAVA, a lemur
USHER VEX, a gibbon
GUEST ODO, a baboon
STAGE MANAGER SERA, a macaque

[SFX: THEATER LOBBY, COATS RUSTLE.]

LUMA: We are live from the winter concert hall. The coat check has reached ticket number fifty, and the doors open in five minutes.

PAVA: I have fifty coats on fifty hooks.

VEX: We have fifty seats in the hall.

PAVA: The coats are not seats.

ODO: My ticket says fifty.

VEX: Your seat is row five, seat zero.

ODO: There is no seat zero.

SERA: The seating chart starts at one.

LUMA: We have found a numbering issue.

PAVA: Coat ticket fifty belongs to the blue coat with a torn cuff.

ODO: I have a brown coat.

VEX: You also have ticket fifty.

ODO: I collected two tickets by mistake.

[SFX: PAPER TICKETS FLUTTER.]

Pava checks the rack. The blue coat is still there; the brown coat is not. Vex says the audience cannot enter until all coats are accounted for. Odo insists he has only one coat.

SERA: The performance begins in five minutes.

PAVA: Then we need to find the brown coat.

LUMA: This is a live broadcast about a coat.

VEX: It is an important coat.

ODO: It is warm.

Pava turns the ticket over. The back reads "fifty - brown." The blue coat's tag says "fifteen." The tickets were handed out in reverse order because the dispenser was loaded backward.

PAVA: The coats are here. The tickets are reversed.

ODO: So my ticket fifty is really fifteen?

PAVA: It identifies coat fifty by rack position, not your coat.

VEX: The audience seats are numbered separately.

ODO: I would like the seat number printed in larger type.

SERA: The seat number is on your program.

[SFX: PROGRAM OPENS.]

ODO: I am row five, seat ten.

LUMA: That is not seat zero.

VEX: You read the fold as a zero.

The doors open. Guests present tickets.
 
A guest returns from the auditorium and hands Pava a coat ticket with no number. Pava recognizes the scarf pinned to the collar and finds the matching hook by color.

GUEST: I lost my ticket.

PAVA: You remembered the scarf.

GUEST: It is my sister's.

VEX: Is your sister in the hall?

GUEST: She is on stage.

SERA: The concert has started.

Luma asks the guest to wait until intermission. Pava marks the coat as claimed by description, not by ticket. The guest leaves the scarf on the counter so the sister can identify it afterward.

[SFX: COAT RUSTLES, AUDIENCE APPLAUSE.]

PAVA: The system needs a lost-ticket procedure.

SERA: Add that to the after-show list.

LUMA: The coat count is still fifty.

VEX: The numbered tickets were not the seat numbers.

ODO: I know that now.

The guest returns at intermission with the correct ticket and collects the coat. Pava crosses the item off the list before the next announcement.
 Pava matches coat descriptions, not ticket numbers, while Vex directs each person to the auditorium. Sera asks the announcer to delay the opening by one minute. Luma narrates the queue as a championship countdown.

LUMA: The blue coat advances. The striped scarf is in the lead. Ticket forty-two is approaching the rack.

PAVA: This is not a race.

VEX: The audience is already inside.

Odo finds his brown coat on a hook labeled "fifty" and realizes his ticket was the coat number. He takes it and heads toward row five. Pava reverses the ticket dispenser, then tests it with a spare tag.

[SFX: DISPENSER CLICKS.]

PAVA: One, two, three. The numbers face forward now.

SERA: The doors are clear. Begin the concert.

LUMA: Coat check has completed its count-in.

VEX: The audience has found its seats.

ODO: And coat fifty has found its owner.

PAVA: Please keep the ticket until after the show.

ODO: I will use it to find my seat.

VEX: That is the other number.

[SFX: ORCHESTRA TUNES, LOBBY FADES.]
