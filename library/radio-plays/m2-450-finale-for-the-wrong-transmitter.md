# Finale for the Wrong Transmitter

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: prerecorded serial finales, overblown cliffhangers, and stations that send an episode preview to the wrong transmitter*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A station's final adventure episode is routed to a mountain relay during an off-air test, and the crew must correct the path before airing the recording once.

Cast: Director Omi, a gibbon; actor Kalo, a macaque; engineer Rava, an orangutan; producer Piri, a capuchin; relay keeper Venn, a baboon; recorded gatekeeper voice Lumo, a lemur.

[SFX: OFF-AIR TEST LINE. STATIC, THEN A RECORDED VOICE: "THE DOOR IS OPEN."]

OMI: That was a preview sent to the mountain relay.

RAVA: We are off air. The public feed is still carrying the previous episode.

KALO: I have been waiting three weeks to open that door.

PIRI: The test selector is on RELAY, not PUBLIC.

ACT ONE

The station is preparing to air the final installment of its adventure serial. The preview was only one recorded line, meant to verify the mountain relay. The transmitter sent it to the relay instead of the control-room monitor. Venn answers from the mountain: he heard the door open, but none of the story that explains what is behind it.

VENN: The relay room has one door. It is still shut.

KALO: I meant the one in the episode.

VENN: I know. I checked.

Omi asks Rava to switch the selector to PUBLIC. Rava says the switch is stuck halfway. Piri tries to move it, but the relay test light goes dark. Venn confirms the mountain receiver is quiet. The public feed remains on the previous episode. No part of the finale has aired.

ACT TWO - OFF-AIR ROUTING CHECK

Rava checks the schedule. The approved finale recording is loaded after an old repeat. The control room can route it manually, but they must disconnect the relay first. Venn unplugs the test cable at the mountain end and confirms the line is quiet. Rava sets the selector to PUBLIC but keeps the program feed muted.

OMI: Public channel only.

RAVA: Selector is set. We are still off air.

PIRI: The approved recording is ready.

Kalo rehearses one line at the microphone, not for broadcast. The monitor plays his voice back two seconds late; he stops and asks Piri to mute it. Piri does. The delay is removed from the rehearsal channel. Omi asks if they need to rerecord the finale. Rava says the approved file is intact; only the route failed.

KALO: The key turns. The door opens.

OMI: What is inside?

KALO: The map that shows the way home.

PIRI: That is the approved ending.

Omi suggests one more mystery after the map. Rava refuses. The story is finished; the rehearsal is only checking the mix. They listen to the file's opening and stop before it reaches the final scene. No audience feed is open.

ACT TWO-B - OFF-AIR TEST

Piri plays a short test tone through the public channel. Rava watches the meter. Venn listens at the relay and reports silence; the mountain test cable is unplugged. The test tone reaches the control-room monitor and no other station. Rava lowers the fader and confirms that the public transmitter can carry the recording. The previous episode remains on air until the scheduled switch.

PIRI: Test tone stopped.

RAVA: Public feed is ready.

OMI: Is the relay isolated?

VENN: The cable is unplugged and tagged. Nothing from the test channel is reaching the mountain.

Kalo listens to the preview in the studio. The key, door and map cues are in order. Piri labels the file FINAL EPISODE - APPROVED and deletes the old repeat from the queue. Rava arms a single playback; she will not rewind it after transmission starts. Omi waits for the scheduled top of the hour.

ACT THREE - ON AIR

[SFX: ON-AIR LIGHT CLICKS ON. APPROVED PRE-RECORDED FINALE PLAYS ONCE.]

[RECORDED SFX: KEY TURNS. WOODEN DOOR OPENS. PAPER MAP UNFOLDS.]

RECORDED KALO: The map is here. The keeper left it so I could find the way home.

RECORDED LUMO: You found the path. Take the key back to the village.

RECORDED KALO: I will return it before the sun sets.

[RECORDED SFX: FOOTSTEPS CROSS THE THRESHOLD. BRIEF TRAVEL MUSIC. VILLAGE VOICES FADE UP.]

RECORDED KALO: Home before sunset. Here is your key.

[RECORDED SFX: KEY SET ON THE VILLAGE TABLE. AN ORDINARY FRONT DOOR CLOSES.]

The recording continues through its credits without a second opening, additional clue, or new cliffhanger. Rava watches the public meter and sees a steady signal. Piri does not touch the transport controls. Omi lets the closing music finish before turning off the program feed.

OMI (LIVE): That was the final episode of the serial. The story is complete, and the broadcast reached the public channel once.

TAG

[SFX: AFTER THE BROADCAST, ONE RELAY SWITCH CLICKS OFF.]

VENN (LIVE, ON PHONE): The mountain relay stayed quiet.

RAVA (LIVE): And the audience got the finale.

FINAL IMAGE: The selector rests on PUBLIC, the tagged test cable hangs unplugged, and the approved recording is stopped after its single broadcast.
