# The Sound of the Last Snowplow

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: municipal winter reports that narrate each pass of a snowplow as if the road were a live sporting event*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Reporter Luma, a gibbon; driver Piri, a capuchin; dispatcher Senn, a macaque; caller Odo, a lemur.

[SFX: WINTER WIND. A SNOWPLOW ENGINE APPROACHES, SCRAPING A ROAD. A RADIO MIC CLICKS ON.]

LUMA: We are live at the north road, where the final snowplow of the season is making its pass.

SENN: It is the final scheduled pass. The road still needs clearing.

LUMA: The plow is entering the bend. The blade is down. The crowd is holding its breath.

PIRI: There is no crowd. I can hear you in my cab.

LUMA: The plow has taken the inside line.

PIRI: I am following the center marker.

[SFX: ENGINE PITCH CHANGES. SCRAPE MOVES LEFT, THEN RIGHT.]

LUMA: A bold maneuver around the snowbank.

PIRI: A driveway is blocked. I am clearing it.

SENN: Please report road conditions, not commentary.

LUMA: The surface is firm, visibility is low, and the plow has moved a mailbox.

PIRI: I did not move the mailbox. The snow pushed it.

[SFX: METAL MAILBOX CREAKS. SNOW SLIDES OFF ITS TOP.]

LUMA: The mailbox has re-entered play.

SENN: Luma.

LUMA: I apologize. We have a caller.

ODO: I am on the south road. The plow passed twice, but the snow drift is still at my gate.

SENN: There is a second pass scheduled for the south road.

LUMA: The plow is turning now. Piri, do you have the south route?

PIRI: I have the route card, but the gate is blocked by a fallen branch.

ODO: I can move it.

SENN: Stay clear of the road. Piri can use the side lane.

[SFX: PLOW ENGINE IDLES. A CAB DOOR OPENS. PIRI STEPS INTO SNOW, THEN SHUTS THE DOOR.]

LUMA: The driver is leaving the vehicle. We may have a substitution.

PIRI: I'm checking the side lane before I enter it. The branch is across the shoulder.

ODO: I'll hold the gate open from inside.

SENN: No one should stand behind the plow.

LUMA: The route is paused. Piri, can the blade clear the drift without catching the branch?

PIRI: If Odo stays inside the gate and I take the left edge, yes.

[SFX: CAB DOOR CLOSES. ENGINE REVS LOW. THE BLADE SCRAPES ALONG THE ROAD; A BRANCH CRACKS AND SLIDES CLEAR.]

ODO: The gate is open. The road is passable.

SENN: Confirm the mailbox is stable.

PIRI: It is leaning, but upright. I'll mark it for repair.

LUMA: The pass is complete. No points have been awarded.

SENN: Correct. The report records a cleared lane, a leaning mailbox and one driveway restored.

ODO: That is what I needed to know.

[SFX: ENGINE TURNS AWAY. TIRES CRUNCH DOWN THE CLEARED ROAD.]

LUMA: Dispatcher, the caller is asking whether the road will stay clear.

SENN: We can report the pass and the conditions, not promise the weather.

LUMA: The report says the north bend is clear, the south driveway is passable, and the mailbox is leaning.

PIRI: The blade is raised. I am at the pullout, out of the traffic lane.

[SFX: A METAL CLANK AS THE BLADE LOCKS. THE ENGINE IDLES.]

ODO: I heard the plow twice and thought another pass was coming.

SENN: The first scrape was the north bend. The second was the south driveway.

ODO: Thank you. I can tell the difference now.

LUMA: We should make separate entries in the route log.

SENN: One report per street. No score, no race, no running tally.

LUMA: Understood. The snow is clearing, and the operator is heading to the depot.

PIRI: I am taking the cleared route back, slowly.

[SFX: ENGINE STARTS. TIRES CRUNCH ON COMPACTED SNOW, THEN FADE TOWARD THE DEPOT.]

ODO: The road is open.

LUMA: That is the report.

SENN: Sign off.

LUMA: The final scheduled plow is leaving the north road.

PIRI: The route card has one more street.

SENN: Then it was not the final pass.

LUMA: We will update the headline.

SENN: Just report the remaining road.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF. THE ENGINE FADES TOWARD THE SOUTH ROAD; WIND FALLS QUIETLY OVER THE CLEARED LANE.]
