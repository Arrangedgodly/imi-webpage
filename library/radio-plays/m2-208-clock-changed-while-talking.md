# The Clock Changed While We Were Talking

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overnight radio specials about daylight-saving time that overdramatize a one-hour clock change*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST KORI, a macaque
ENGINEER VEL, a capuchin
CALLER SANI, a gibbon
TIMEKEEPER OMI, a gorilla

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS. A SINGLE BELL SOUNDS.]

KORI: At two o'clock tonight, the clocks move forward one hour. We are broadcasting live through the change.

VEL: I have two clocks. They are both set to the same time.

[VEL PUTS THE CLOCKS SIDE BY SIDE. ONE HAS A BATTERY COMPARTMENT. THE OTHER HAS A PLUG.]

OMI: The plugged clock updates automatically. The battery clock needs to be adjusted by hand.

KORI: Which one goes on air?

OMI: The official studio clock.

SANI: My microwave is still five minutes fast.

OMI: That remains a separate problem.

OMI: One is the studio clock. One is the backup.

KORI: Will they agree?

OMI: At the moment, yes.

### Scene One: The Countdown

[VEL SETS A LARGE DIGITAL TIMER TO TEN MINUTES.]

KORI: We will stay with the clock as it approaches the change.

SANI: I am listening from home. My kitchen clock is five minutes fast.

OMI: Use the official time signal, not the kitchen clock.

VEL: The sponsor wants a countdown every minute.

KORI: We can announce the change once.

[THE TIMER BEEPS EARLY.]

VEL: The hour has arrived.

OMI: It is ten minutes early. Your timer was set incorrectly.

[VEL LOOKS AT THE DEVICE. IT IS COUNTING DOWN TO A COMMERCIAL BREAK.] 

### Scene Two: Two O'Clock

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS. QUIET ROOM TONE.]

KORI: It is one minute to two.

OMI: At two, the local time becomes three.

SANI: My microwave says it is still one fifty-five.

VEL: Should we call it a lost hour?

OMI: The clock moves forward. The date changes normally.

[THE SECOND HAND PASSES TWELVE. BOTH CLOCKS SHOW TWO. THE PLUGGED CLOCK UPDATES TO THREE. THE BATTERY CLOCK REMAINS AT TWO. VEL TURNS ITS HANDS FORWARD; IT CLICKS AT THREE.]

[OMI READS THE OFFICIAL TIME SIGNAL AGAIN. VEL'S COUNTDOWN TIMER BEEPS, BUT IT IS STILL COUNTING TOWARD THE COMMERCIAL.] 

KORI: The signal, not the timer, tells us the time.

VEL: The timer has reached zero.

OMI: The time is three.

SANI: My microwave still says one fifty-five.

KORI: We are not changing that by radio.

[SANI PRESSES THE MICROWAVE CLOCK BUTTON AT HOME. A BEEP SOUNDS ON THE CALL LINE.]

SANI: It is three now.

OMI: The official time has not changed again.

KORI: We will return to regular programming.

### Scene Three: The Correction

SANI: My kitchen clock changed when I pressed the button.

KORI: Was it automatic?

SANI: No. It is a microwave.

VEL: We have a second clock story.

OMI: We have two people adjusting two clocks.

[KORI READS THE OFFICIAL TIME. VEL'S COUNTDOWN TIMER BEEPS AGAIN.]

VEL: Commercial break.

KORI: The hour has passed. We will return to regular programming.

VEL: The break is scheduled for the top of the hour.

OMI: The top of the hour was just announced.

KORI: We have two announcements and one hour change.

VEL: The sponsor wants the change announced after the break too.

OMI: That would make it sound as if the clocks changed twice.

SANI: My microwave is now correct, but the radio clock says three.

KORI: They agree.

SANI: The microwave is still five minutes fast.

OMI: That part did not change.

[VEL PULLS THE COUNTDOWN TIMER'S PLUG. IT STOPS BEEPING.]

VEL: The timer has been unplugged.

KORI: Has the hour changed?

OMI: The official clock still says three.

SANI: My microwave says three-oh-five.

KORI: We have confirmed the time and the kitchen appliance.

VEL: The break is still due.

OMI: A commercial does not change the time.

[VEL CUES THE BREAK MUSIC. IT LASTS FOUR SECONDS, THEN CUTS OFF.]

VEL: That was the shortest break on record.

KORI: We have returned from it.

[SFX: TWO CLOCKS TICK IN SYNC.]

### Tag

VEL: Which clock is right?

OMI: The one that says three.

KORI: And the one that needs a button.
