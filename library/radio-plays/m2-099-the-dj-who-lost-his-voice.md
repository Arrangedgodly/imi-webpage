# The DJ Who Lost His Voice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live music-radio broadcasts, DJ banter, and emergency filler playlists*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: DJ Riko, a gibbon; producer Mava, a macaque; technician Doro, a baboon; caller Beni, a capuchin; station manager Sella, an orangutan.

[SFX: UPBEAT STATION JINGLE. RECORD CLICKS INTO PLACE.]

RIKO: You're tuned to Branch FM. We have an hour of music and a host with a very good voice.

[SFX: RIKO COUGHS. HIS VOICE SQUEAKS.]

MAVA: Your voice has changed.

RIKO: It sounds like a door hinge.

DORO: The mic is fine.

RIKO: I can't host like this.

SELLA: We are live.

RIKO: Then play a record.

[SFX: MUSIC STARTS. A SCRATCH. MUSIC STOPS.]

DORO: The record is stuck.

MAVA: We need filler.

### Act One

MAVA: Riko, can you speak?

RIKO: [HOARSE] Barely.

SELLA: We have a playlist.

DORO: The playlist is in the locked cabinet.

MAVA: Where is the key?

DORO: Riko has it.

RIKO: [HOARSE] I do not.

SELLA: You said you had a good voice.

RIKO: I had a good voice.

[SFX: DRAWER OPENS. PAPERS SHUFFLE.]

MAVA: We can use the listener requests.

DORO: The requests are handwritten.

SELLA: We can read them.

MAVA: You have a voice.

SELLA: I have a manager's voice.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

BENI: I requested the river song.

MAVA: Which river song?

BENI: The one with water.

DORO: We have three.

SELLA: Pick one.

BENI: The one where they sing about water.

MAVA: That doesn't narrow it down.

RIKO: [HOARSE] Play the quiet one.

MAVA: Which is quiet?

RIKO: The one with fewer drums.

DORO: We have one record with no drums.

[SFX: RECORD STARTS. SOFT FLUTE MUSIC PLAYS.]

SELLA: That is the station identification.

MAVA: It has no words.

RIKO: That's the point.

### Act Two

[SFX: MUSIC FADES. PHONE RINGS.]

BENI: I can identify the song.

DORO: Please hum it.

BENI: I don't remember the tune.

MAVA: What do you remember?

BENI: Water.

SELLA: We need a title.

BENI: River Morning.

DORO: We don't have a song called that.

RIKO: [HOARSE] Make one.

MAVA: We have a musician in the hall.

SELLA: Is the musician ready?

DORO: They are tuning.

[SFX: FLUTE TUNES. A STRING TWANGS. A DRUM ROLLS ONCE.]

MAVA: We have an ensemble.

SELLA: We have a hallway.

[SFX: MUSIC BEGINS, UNEVEN BUT CHEERFUL.]

BENI: That is the river song.

MAVA: You know it now?

BENI: The water is in the flute.

DORO: The drum is a little loud.

RIKO: [HOARSE] It's a current.

SELLA: We need to announce the song.

MAVA: We can announce it after it plays.

RIKO: [HOARSE] Branch FM presents "River Morning."

[SFX: MUSIC BUILDS. THE DRUM ROLLS INTO THE FLUTE.]

### Act Three

[SFX: MUSIC ENDS. STUDIO APPLAUSE.]

SELLA: The station has survived without a record.

DORO: The record cabinet is still locked.

MAVA: We can play live music.

BENI: Can Riko host again?

RIKO: [HOARSE] Not yet.

SELLA: We can let the music speak.

MAVA: We need to fill the rest of the hour.

DORO: The hallway musicians have gone home.

BENI: I can hum the river song.

SELLA: We cannot put a caller on the playlist.

RIKO: [HOARSE] We can.

MAVA: It might be the first caller record.

BENI: I will sing it.

[SFX: BENI HUMS THE TUNE. IT IS OFF KEY.]

DORO: The note is wandering.

SELLA: The station is allowed to wander.

MAVA: I will join.

[SFX: MAVA HUMS. RIKO JOINS, STILL HOARSE.]

RIKO: [HOARSE] The song is better with three voices.

BENI: We have a chorus.

SELLA: The station has a new band.

DORO: The record player is working again.

[SFX: RECORD SPINS.]

MAVA: Do we use it?

RIKO: [HOARSE] Let it rest.

[SFX: RECORD STOPS.]

### Tag

[SFX: STATION JINGLE, SUNG BY THE THREE VOICES. RIKO'S LAST NOTE SQUEAKS.]

SELLA: Branch FM.

DORO: We are still here.

RIKO: [HOARSE] And we are listening.
