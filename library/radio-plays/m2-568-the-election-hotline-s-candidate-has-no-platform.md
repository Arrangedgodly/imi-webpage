# The Election Hotline's Candidate Has No Platform

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: political call-in programs that manufacture a campaign from an empty podium*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; moderator Mara, a macaque; candidate Pip, a capuchin; candidate Rell, a lemur; clerk Odo, a gibbon; caller Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Community hall studio. Empty podium mic; chairs shift.

VEX: Tonight, candidate Pip unveils a bold platform.

PIP: I haven't announced a platform. I am here to answer questions about the garden council election.

MARA: The event rules allow each candidate two minutes for an opening statement and one minute per question.

ODO: The timer is set. The audience cards are collected in the box.

VEX: I have written a slogan: "More bananas for all."

PIP: That isn't my policy. We are voting on water schedule, path repairs, and shared tool storage.

NARI, on phone: I heard the campaign is promising a banana in every shed.

MARA: No such promise was made. Odo has screened the submitted questions. We will take them from callers and the moderator; Pip can answer in their own words.

VEX: The empty podium is a powerful symbol.

MARA: Pip is standing beside it. It is not a candidate.

PIP: My priorities are to publish the watering schedule, fix the west path, and keep the tools in a labeled cabinet.

VEX: Where is the dramatic reform?

PIP: Those are the three things residents asked us to discuss.

ODO: Opening statement ends in ten seconds.

PIP: I will post a weekly watering chart and ask for volunteers to inspect the path before requesting materials. The cabinet needs a lock so tools are returned.

SFX: Timer bell.

VEX: A short agenda, not a revolution.

NARI: How will residents know whether the path repair is complete?

PIP: We can post the work date and reopen the path after the board checks it. If funds are not available, I will say so rather than promise a date.

VEX: Could the host endorse the candidate?

MARA: Save endorsements for another program. Here the audience can decide after hearing both candidates.

ODO: Pip has one final question before Rell's turn. Rell will receive the same opening and question slots.

PIP: Then let us take that question, and I will step aside.

VEX: Nari, ask the candidate whether the garden council will guarantee the west path by Friday.

NARI: The materials have not been approved yet. Pip said he would inspect the path and post a date after the council reviews the cost.

VEX: That sounds like a delay.

PIP: It is an honest condition. I can't promise a repair date before we know what the repair needs.

MARA: The question deserves a direct answer. Will you publish the inspection date?

PIP: Yes. I will post the inspection date by Wednesday. If the path needs materials, I will list what is required and ask the council to vote.

ODO: The timer gives Pip thirty seconds to finish.

VEX: Could we call that a bold commitment?

PIP: Call it a schedule. The repair depends on what the inspection finds.

NARI: That is clearer than saying the path will be fixed by Friday.

MARA: The moderator's job is to let the audience hear the answer, not dress it up.

VEX: I have no slogan for conditional work.

PIP: The work is public. Residents can check the posted date and the inspection note.

SFX: A second microphone opens for the other candidate.

ODO: Rell's two-minute opening begins now.

SFX: Timer resets; chair slides toward the second microphone.

RELL: I'm Rell. I would put the tool cabinet near the west beds and ask gardeners to sign tools out on the chalkboard.

VEX: A cabinet relocation! At last, a platform with altitude.

RELL: It is a shelf. It should stay dry, and both garden crews need a key. I would keep the watering chart beside it, so gardeners can collect tools and check their time in one place. For the path, I would ask residents to mark the muddy sections on a map before the inspection. We need to know whether water pools there before buying more gravel.

VEX: You have introduced mud without promising a mud-free future.

RELL: I am offering a map, not control of the weather.

SFX: Opening timer bell.

ODO: Questions now. Two one-minute slots, as Pip received.

MARA: First question: who would decide whether that cabinet moves?

RELL: The council after both garden crews inspect the site. I would post a drawing first, so we can check its distance from the beds and whether the door clears the path.

NARI: Second question: what if the wall is damp?

RELL: We keep the cabinet where it is and publish the reason. A convenient location is not useful if it damages the tools. We can inspect the wall with Pip on Wednesday and post the result with the path date.

PIP: I will join you. Please leave enough space on the map for the tools we already own.

VEX: A coalition formed around a cupboard.

SFX: Final question timer bell.

ODO: Both candidates have answered. Each had an opening and two question slots. The unused audience cards remain in the box.

VEX: Can I endorse the shelf?

MARA: No. You can introduce our next program without turning furniture into a campaign.

VEX: The empty podium has been filled.

MARA: The audience has heard both candidates. We can close the forum.

VEX: But no one promised bananas.

MARA: They discussed the garden's water chart, west path, and tool cabinet. The audience can decide which plan they trust.

NARI: Now I have heard both plans, I can decide how to vote.

VEX: The empty podium has lost the election.
