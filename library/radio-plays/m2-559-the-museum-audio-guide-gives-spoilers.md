# The Museum Audio Guide Gives Spoilers

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: immersive audio guides that narrate every reveal before visitors see it*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Curator Mara, a macaque; narrator Vex, an orangutan; technician Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin; guide Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Museum gallery. Headphones click on; soft footsteps.

VEX, on audio guide: Before you enter, the final room contains the oldest mask in the collection.

PIP: I haven't reached the final room yet.

MARA: That's the problem. The guide is giving the reveal before visitors get to it.

ODO: I loaded the full route in the order on the map. The player may have jumped to track seven when the headphones started.

NARI: Pip, what number is on the screen?

PIP: Seven. The map says start at one.

VEX, on audio guide: The last room also contains a cracked drum.

MARA: Pause the player. Odo, check the track labels before anyone else starts.

SFX: Button click; audio stops.

ODO: The card is reversed. Track seven is listed first, but it belongs at the end. I can reorder the card without changing the recordings.

VEX: The ending has been discovered.

MARA: It has been played out of sequence. The exhibit itself is still in order.

PIP: Can we start at track one now?

ODO: Yes. I will confirm the first stop is the entrance panel, then test the next track before we resume.

SFX: Two clicks; headphones tone.

VEX, on audio guide: This gallery explores how masks were made. At the first display, look for the carved edge...

PIP: That matches the object in front of me.

NARI: Good. The second track should describe the workbench, not the final room.

ODO: Track two is queued. I have removed the preview of the ending from the welcome screen.

VEX, on audio guide: At the workbench, notice the small stone tools.

PIP: That's what I see. No spoiler.

VEX: The narrative has lost its surprise.

MARA: The visitor has gained the right order.

PIP: Track three covers the tool table. It describes how the stone tools were used.

ODO: That track comes after the mask-making display. The player is following the map now.

NARI: I can test the revised card at the desk while Pip follows the first two stops.

VEX: A second reviewer could hear my narration out of order.

MARA: Only if we put the wrong card back. Odo is checking the sequence before we issue it.

ODO: The card now lists one through seven in order. The welcome screen starts at one; the last-room description stays on seven.

NARI: The player is at volume two. Good. We don't need a dramatic voiceover to cover the room.

MARA: Keep the volume low enough for visitors to hear the guide and one another.

VEX: My final-room reveal will be heard only by visitors who reach the final room.

ODO: Correct. The spoiler has been removed from the welcome screen.

MARA: Give the corrected card to the front desk; we can finish checking the player.
NARI: Should we warn people who heard track seven?

MARA: We can reset their players and offer a new card. No one needs to return the exhibit objects to recreate an ending.

ODO: I will put the corrected card at the desk and recycle the reversed cards after checking that no other device uses them.

PIP: I can restart at one. I still want to see the final room myself.

VEX: The mask will wait behind the door.

MARA: It will remain on its display, whether or not anyone knows it's coming.

SFX: Player resets; footsteps continue in gallery.

NARI: The welcome track starts at one now, and visitors can follow the printed map or explore without the guide.

VEX: Will my voice still narrate?

MARA: Yes, in the right order.

TAG

VEX: In the final room, there is a very nice bench.

PIP: That is not a spoiler.
