# The Museum Tour's Haunted Headphones

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: museum audio guides that mistake equipment bleed and visitor echoes for supernatural evidence*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Guide Omi, a lemur; technician Varo, an orangutan; visitor Fenn, a squirrel monkey; curator Sada, a macaque.

[SFX: MUSEUM GALLERY. HEADPHONES CLICK ON. A FAINT VOICE WHISPERS, "TURN BACK."]

OMI: Welcome to the Hall of Ancient Navigation. Your audio guide will lead you through the exhibits.

FENN: Mine says turn back.

SADA: The guide should say turn left at the compass display.

VARO: I hear the whisper in every headset. It is audio from the next room bleeding into this channel.

OMI: The exhibit is about sailors. A mysterious voice will make the tour exciting.

FENN: It sounds like the recording is coming from behind me.

[SFX: HEADPHONES WHISPER AGAIN. FENN TURNS; SHOES SQUEAK.] 

OMI: That is because the speaker is behind the display case.

SADA: We do not want visitors to think the collection is haunted.

OMI: The brochure says, "hear the past speak." This is technically doing that.

VARO: The cable is crossing two channels. I can separate them if the tour stops for a minute.

SADA: The school group arrives in five. We need a clean track before they put on the headsets.

FENN: My guide just said, "the ship sank in this room."

OMI: That line belongs to the shipwreck exhibit down the hall.

[SFX: HEADPHONE BUTTONS CLICK; TWO RECORDINGS OVERLAP, ONE WHISPERS, ONE PLAYS WAVES.] 

VARO: The audio loop is patched into both transmitters. I will mute the navigation channel and test one headset.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. WHISPER STOPS. WAVES CONTINUE.] 

FENN: Now I hear waves and the words "turn left." That is still odd, but less threatening.

SADA: The wave track is also from the shipwreck room.

VARO: I have found the splitter. It is plugged into the wrong output.

OMI: Can we keep the whisper for the evening ghost tour?

SADA: There is no ghost tour. The evening program is a lecture about mapmaking.

OMI: We could call it a ghost lecture.

FENN: Please do not make the map whisper at me.

[SFX: CABLE UNPLUGS. A JACK CLICKS INTO A DIFFERENT SOCKET.] 

VARO: Navigation channel is isolated. Test again.

OMI: Follow the compass rose. Turn left at the star map.

FENN: I heard the guide. I did not hear a shipwreck or a ghost.

SADA: Good. Now test the second headset.

[SFX: SECOND HEADSET CLICKS ON. A LOUD FEEDBACK SQUEAL.] 

FENN: That one screamed.

OMI: The ghost has returned.

VARO: No. The ear cup is pressing the microphone button while it sits on the table.

SADA: We have six more headsets on the cart. Check them before the students arrive.

[SFX: HEADSETS LIFT AND CLICK. FOUR CLEAN TONES; ONE WHISPER; ONE SQUEAL.] 

VARO: Four work. One still has the cross-channel whisper. One has a stuck button.

OMI: Can we label the working ones with stars? It suits navigation.

SADA: Label the other two OUT OF SERVICE. The stars can stay on the exhibit map.

FENN: I will take the working set. It tells me where to go.

[SFX: GALLERY DOOR OPENS; CHILDREN CHAT IN DISTANCE.] 

OMI: The school group is here. Welcome to navigation. Please turn left at the compass display.

SADA: The exhibit speaks through the objects and the guide. The headphones just carry the sound.

VARO: The repaired track is steady tonight.

FENN: And nothing told me to turn back.

[SFX: A HEADSET ON THE DEFECTIVE CART WHISPERS, "TURN BACK." EVERYONE PAUSES.] 

VARO: That one is unplugged. It is the next room's speaker leaking through the open door.

SADA: Close the door before the ghost gets a school group.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES. WHISPER CUTS OFF. TOUR FOOTSTEPS MOVE LEFT.]
