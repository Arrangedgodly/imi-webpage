# The Phone Tree That Called Itself

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: automated community phone trees that mistake their own recorded announcement for an incoming emergency report*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Operator Lemi, a lemur; host Pavo, a gibbon; technician Risa, a macaque; resident Koro, a capuchin.

[SFX: PHONE TREE HUB. SEVERAL LINES RING IN SEQUENCE.]

PAVO: The phone tree is calling every resident with a water notice.

LEMI: The notice says the west tap is closed for repair.

KORO: My phone rang, and the recording said, "Please call the water desk."

RISA: The system has placed its own callback number in the message.

PAVO: That is the contact number.

LEMI: The campaign screen shows a repeat loop after each completed call.

RISA: I will disable that loop before we resume. The callback number will remain in the recorded message, outside the dialing list.

KORO: That means callers can reach the water desk without the desk calling itself back.

PAVO: The jingle had the water desk's number sung in it.

LEMI: Remove that version. The spoken message has the correct number and no music.

LEMI: The dialer is treating the recorded callback as a new report and starting the phone tree again.

[SFX: LINES RING. RECORDED VOICE: "PLEASE CALL THE WATER DESK." BUTTON BEEPS.]

KORO: It called me twice.

RISA: The return line is in the same queue as the residents. We need to remove it from the call list.

PAVO: Can we stop the calls?

LEMI: The cancel button ends the campaign, but residents still need the notice.

RISA: I can pause the dialer and keep the recorded message available on the hotline.

[SFX: PAUSE BUTTON PRESSES. RINGING STOPS.]

LEMI: The repair crew is at the west tap now. It is closed, and the east tap is open.

KORO: I can still call the hotline to hear that?

RISA: Yes. The hotline is separate from the outbound dialer.

PAVO: The show has already announced that every household will be called.

LEMI: We can send one corrected message after the return number is removed.

RISA: I am editing the campaign list. The water desk line goes in the message, not in the recipient list.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. A SINGLE TEST CALL RINGS AT THE CONTROL DESK.]

RISA: Test call only to the control desk. Listen for the message, then confirm it does not redial.

[SFX: RECORDED VOICE: "WEST TAP CLOSED FOR REPAIR. EAST TAP OPEN. CALL THE WATER DESK FOR UPDATES." THE LINE DISCONNECTS.]

LEMI: It disconnected.

RISA: No new campaign started. The callback number is not in the recipient field.

KORO: I can call the hotline now.

[SFX: KORO DIALS. ONE RING. RECORDED MESSAGE PLAYS. HE HANGS UP.]

KORO: The update is clear, and my phone is quiet.

PAVO: Send the corrected call to residents who have not received it.

PAVO: The sponsor wants a second round so every household hears the jingle again.

RISA: That would restart the same loop unless I disable the callback line first.

LEMI: The notice has already reached twenty-seven homes. We should retry the four failed numbers and stop.

KORO: I can confirm the east tap is open. No new notice is needed.

[SFX: DIALER SELECTS FOUR LINES. FOUR RINGS SOUND, ONE AFTER ANOTHER.]

RISA: Four retries only. Each line disconnects after the message.

PAVO: The sponsor jingle stays off this time.

LEMI: The message is the service. The jingle is not a reason to call again.

LEMI: The system log shows twenty-seven completed calls and four failed numbers.

RISA: It will retry only those four. It will not call the whole list again.

[SFX: FOUR OUTBOUND LINES DIAL. NO OTHER RINGS.]

KORO: The phone tree stopped calling itself.

LEMI: The west tap remains closed. The east tap is open until the crew finishes.

PAVO: That is the notice. No second campaign.

[SFX: DIALER BEEPS ONCE. SCREEN SAVES. MIC CLOSES.]
