# The Airport Parrot Calls the Wrong Runway

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: airport radio procedures, talking birds, and a ground crew separating copied phrases from actual air traffic control*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Controller Vela, an orangutan; host Pip, a capuchin; ground marshal Rona, a macaque; parrot Pogo, a green-cheeked conure; mechanic Tavi, a gibbon.

SFX: Airport visitor center. Radio hiss; distant engine idles. A separate public guest line connects from the tower.

PIP: We are at the airport visitor center, where a parrot has just announced, "Runway two is clear." Controller Vela joins us from the tower on the public guest line. Is that official?

VELA: No. Official runway instructions come from the control tower. The parrot is repeating a phrase it heard during yesterday's tour.

POGO: Runway two is clear. Welcome aboard.

RONA: Runway two is closed for repainting. Ground crew is moving a paint cart across the service lane.

PIP: The bird gave an unsafe instruction.

VELA: The bird made a sound. The visitor center speaker routed it to our broadcast microphone. No aircraft received it.

TAVI: I can disconnect the visitor speaker from the tower monitor. The two systems should never share a channel.

PIP: Could the parrot repeat a safer phrase?

RONA: We should not train it on radio commands. It may repeat them at the wrong time.

POGO: Paint cart crossing. Thank you.

VELA: That is a tour phrase, not a clearance. Let's keep the visitor channel isolated.

SFX: Switch flips; the visitor speaker goes quiet. The control radio remains audible to the tower crew only.

TAVI: Visitor speaker isolated. Control radio is not connected to the studio feed.

PIP: Can we still broadcast from here?

VELA: You can report general information, but do not rebroadcast private control instructions. The public display will show which runways are open.

RONA: The display says runway two closed, runway one open. The paint crew has cones across the service lane.

POGO: Runway two is clear.

RONA: That is the old recording. It is playing from the parrot's training button.

TAVI: I will remove the training button from the visitor perch. Pogo can still talk naturally, but not press a stored phrase during tours.

PIP: He pressed it himself?

TAVI: The button is foot-height. He steps on it when he climbs the perch.

POGO: Paint cart crossing. Thank you.

RONA: He learned that one on today's tour.

VELA: The phrase is about the cart. It is not a runway instruction. The visitor center needs a sign saying bird phrases are not flight information.

PIP: I can read that sign on air.

RONA: First, let the crew finish the closure check. The cart is clear, the cones are still in place, and the runway inspector is walking the painted line.

SFX: Boots on pavement; radio crackle.

VELA, from the control tower: Runway one remains open. Runway two stays closed until inspection is complete.

VELA: That is a tower transmission. It is heard only by the control staff. We will not repeat it as clearance.

PIP: For visitors: the public board lists runway status in plain language, and staff will update it after inspection.

TAVI: I have removed the parrot's phrase button and placed it in a labeled drawer. The visitor speaker stays disconnected from the control monitor.

POGO: Welcome aboard.

RONA: That is acceptable. It is a greeting.

PIP: Are we cleared to leave?

VELA: The visitor center is not on a runway. You may leave through the exit door.

PIP: Copy. I am walking to the exit, not taxiing.

SFX: Door opens; visitor bell rings.

POGO: Runway two is clear.

TAVI: He said it after the button was removed.

VELA: He remembered the phrase. The public sign says CLOSED, and the crew has not changed it.

RONA: The runway inspector confirms the paint is dry, but the safety sweep is not complete. Runway two remains closed for now.

PIP: We can report that the phrase was wrong and the closure remains.

VELA: Exactly. The control tower will announce any change through its official channel.

POGO: Welcome aboard.

RONA: Welcome to the visitor center, Pogo.

SFX: Visitors laugh; public board chimes as staff update an informational notice.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Runway two remains closed until the inspector completes the sweep. The parrot is not air traffic control.
