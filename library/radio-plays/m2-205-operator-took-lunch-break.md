# The Operator Took a Lunch Break

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio phone-in request shows that promise a personal song dedication but route every call through a complicated queue*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST KEL, a macaque
OPERATOR RUMA, a gorilla
PRODUCER NEN, a capuchin
CALLER SAVA, a gibbon

[SFX: PHONE RING, THEN BUSY SIGNAL.]

KEL: Welcome to Dedicate a Tune. Caller, who would you like to hear a song for?

[SILENCE.]

NEN: The call dropped.

RUMA: It is in the queue.

KEL: The queue has answered with a busy signal.

### Scene One: The Request

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

SAVA: I would like to dedicate a song to my sister, who opens the community library every morning.

KEL: Which song?

SAVA: "Open the Door." It is by the local band.

NEN: We need a dramatic backstory before we play it.

SAVA: She opens the library door at eight.

KEL: That is the backstory.

[RUMA PUTS THE CALL ON HOLD. A HOLD TUNE PLAYS THE SAME FOUR NOTES IN A LOOP.]

SAVA: I can still hear the hold music.

KEL: We are trying to find the song.

[NEN ASKS RUMA TO TRANSFER THE CALL TO THE MUSIC LIBRARY. THE HOLD TUNE PLAYS WHILE THE LINE CHANGES.]

SAVA: I can hear the transfer.

RUMA: The music librarian is on another call.

NEN: We can offer a different song.

SAVA: I asked for the local band's song.

[NEN CHECKS THE STATION CATALOG. THE REQUEST IS FILED UNDER "OPEN THE DOOR" AND ALSO UNDER "LIBRARY."]

NEN: We found it.

KEL: The song is ready.

### Scene Two: The Queue

[RUMA CHECKS A BOARD WITH THREE CALLS.]

RUMA: Caller one has a dedication. Caller two wants to request a song. Caller three is an echo of caller two.

NEN: Can we play the echo as a teaser?

KEL: No. Play the song requested by the caller who is waiting.

[RUMA TAKES SAVA OFF HOLD.]

SAVA: I have to leave for work soon.

KEL: We will play it now.

[NEN CUES A LONG INTRO. THE SONG DOES NOT START; THE HOLD TUNE KEEPS LOOPING.]

[RUMA DISCONNECTS THE HOLD MUSIC AND PATCHES IN THE RECORD. THE FOUR-NOTE LOOP ENDS MID-MEASURE.]

SAVA: There it is.

KEL: The request was in the catalog the whole time.

NEN: The queue was the obstacle.

[THE SONG'S FIRST CHORUS PLAYS. NEN REACHES FOR THE FADER TO TALK OVER IT. KEL HOLDS UP A HAND; THE SONG CONTINUES UNINTERRUPTED.]

SAVA: My sister will recognize this part.

RUMA: The line is still open.

KEL: It can stay open until the song ends.

[THE HOLD QUEUE LIGHTS REMAIN STEADY. THE FOUR-NOTE TUNE DOES NOT RETURN.]

NEN: We have a proper dedication.

[THE PHONE BEEPS AGAIN. RUMA CHECKS THE BOARD; CALLER TWO IS A NEW REQUEST, NOT AN ECHO.]

RUMA: The next caller is ready.

KEL: We will take that after the dedication finishes.

SAVA: Thank you for playing the whole song.

RUMA: The line is clear now.

[THE GUITAR INTRO CONTINUES UNDER THE LAST LINE, THEN FADES.] 

RUMA: Wrong channel.

[NEN SWITCHES CHANNELS. THE HOLD TUNE STOPS. A GUITAR INTRO BEGINS.] 

SAVA: That is the song.

### Scene Three: Dedication

[KEL SPEAKS OVER THE MUSIC.]

KEL: This song is for Sava's sister, who opens the library every morning.

NEN: Can we ask for a dramatic response?

SAVA: She is at work. She will hear it later.

[THE SONG PLAYS CLEARLY FOR A FEW BARS. RUMA CLEARS THE HOLD QUEUE. CALLER TWO BEEPS IN, BUT KEL DOES NOT INTERRUPT.] 

KEL: We will take the next request after the song.

NEN: The caller is on the line.

KEL: The dedication is still playing.

### Tag

[SFX: SONG ENDS. PHONE BEEPS ONCE.]

RUMA: The operator is back from lunch.

KEL: We have a queue.

NEN: It is one clear call.

RUMA: The line is ready.
