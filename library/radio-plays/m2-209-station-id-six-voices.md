# A Station ID in Six Voices

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio station identifications that accumulate slogans, departments, and unnecessary character voices*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
ANNOUNCER SORI, a macaque
PRODUCER KEL, a capuchin
ENGINEER RAN, a gorilla
VOICE LEM, a langur
VOICE TAVI, a gibbon

[SFX: SHORT MUSICAL LOGO.]

SORI: You are listening to Canopy Community Radio, station KCR.

KEL: The legal ID needs the city, the frequency, and the call letters.

RAN: It is already seven seconds long.

KEL: We have five voices available.

### Scene One: The First Take

[SORI READS FROM A CARD.]

SORI: Canopy Community Radio, 88.4 FM, KCR, serving the valley.

[KEL PLAYS THE TAKE BACK. THE LOGO MUSIC LASTS THREE SECONDS.]

KEL: We have room for a slogan.

RAN: The call letters have to be clear.

LEM: Can we add a chorus?

TAVI: I can sing the frequency.

SORI: That would make it a song.

[TAVI SINGS "EIGHTY-EIGHT POINT FOUR" ON FOUR NOTES. THE NOTES RING OVER THE CALL LETTERS.]

RAN: The number is clear. The letters are not.

[KEL REWINDS. THE STATION ID PLAYS AGAIN WITH THE SLOGAN IN THE MIDDLE.]

SORI: The station name is now at the end.

KEL: We need to recognize it at the beginning.

[RAN RECORDS A CLEAN TAKE. THE CALL LETTERS ARE CLEAR, BUT KEL ADDS THE SLOGAN AFTER THEM.] 

SORI: The legal ID is complete.

KEL: We can tag on the slogan.

RAN: It is still in the recording.

[THEY PLAY IT BACK. THE SLOGAN OVERLAPS THE LOGO MUSIC.]

TAVI: It sounds like the station is called "The Sound of Every Branch."

LEM: That is not a call sign.

[KEL TAKES THE SLOGAN OFF THE CARD.]

KEL: Use it before the program, not inside the ID.

[SORI READS THE FOUR FACTS AGAIN. RAN PLAYS IT ON A PORTABLE RADIO FROM THE NEXT ROOM.]

RAN: I heard the name, frequency, call letters, and city.

KEL: No extra voices?

SORI: No extra voices.

[KEL REACHES FOR THE MUSIC LOGO. RAN PLAYS IT ONLY AFTER THE ID ENDS.]

TAVI: The logo can follow the call letters.

KEL: We have our station ID.

KEL: Add the slogan: "The sound of every branch."

RAN: Does that need to be in the legal ID?

KEL: It makes it memorable.

[LEM RECORDS THE SLOGAN IN A DEEP VOICE.]

LEM: The sound of every branch.

TAVI: That sounds like a tree falling.

KEL: Can we do it brighter?

[LEM READS IT IN A HIGHER VOICE.]

LEM: The sound of every branch!

SORI: Now it sounds like a bird alarm.

### Scene Two: The Extra Voices

[KEL ADDS THE WEATHER, TRAFFIC, AND CULTURE DEPARTMENTS TO THE ID.]

KEL: Each department says one word: weather, traffic, culture.

RAN: The station name comes first.

[SORI READS THE NAME. LEM SAYS "WEATHER." TAVI SAYS "TRAFFIC." THE TWO VOICES OVERLAP.] 

SORI: We have three departments on top of the call letters.

TAVI: I was saying traffic.

LEM: I was saying weather.

RAN: The signal was clear before they came in.

[KEL REORDERS THE VOICES. THE EDIT PLAYS WEATHER, TRAFFIC, CULTURE, CALL LETTERS, CITY, FREQUENCY, THEN THE STATION NAME.] 

SORI: The name should be first.

### Scene Three: The Short ID

[RAN PLAYS THE SIMPLE TAKE: STATION NAME, FREQUENCY, CALL LETTERS, CITY.]

SORI: That is clear.

KEL: It has no slogan.

TAVI: We can use the slogan in the program intro.

LEM: And the voices in the weather segment.

[KEL LISTENS AGAIN. THE SIMPLE ID FITS BEFORE THE THEME SONG.] 

KEL: We will keep it short.

[SORI RECORDS THE FINAL TAKE. THE LEGAL ID PLAYS ON AIR WITHOUT EXTRA MUSIC.]

### Tag

[SFX: SHORT MUSICAL LOGO.]

SORI: Canopy Community Radio, 88.4 FM, KCR, serving the valley.

RAN: Seven seconds.

KEL: We can add a bird.

LEM: If we add a bird, it will want its own identification.

TAVI: Our bird is a recording of a real valley thrush.

SORI: Does the thrush consent to station membership?

RAN: It lives on the transmitter roof. It has excellent reception.

[A THRUSH CALLS OUTSIDE, THEN THE RECORDED THRUSH ANSWERS FROM THE STUDIO SPEAKER.] 

KEL: They have found each other.

SORI: Keep the station name short enough for both to finish.

LEM: Canopy Community Radio.

TAVI: The thrush has finished.

RAN: Six seconds.

[THEY ALL STAY QUIET.]
