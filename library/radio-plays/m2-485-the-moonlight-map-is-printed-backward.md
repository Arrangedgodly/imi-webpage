# The Moonlight Map Is Printed Backward

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: expedition broadcasts that value dramatic maps over usable directions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Leader Nari, a lemur; mapmaker Vex, an orangutan; guide Mara, a macaque; scout Pip, a capuchin; recorder Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Night trail. Paper unfolds. An owl calls.

NARI: Our expedition will follow the moonlight map to the old lookout.

VEX: This map has a silver border and an embossed compass rose.

MARA: It is upside down.

VEX: The compass rose is decorative.

ODO: North is printed at the bottom.

PIP: Then we are holding it backward.

VEX: It is designed for dramatic orientation.

MARA: The path forks here. Which way?

VEX: The map shows a majestic tree.

PIP: There are three trees.

ODO: The map was drawn from the hill, looking back toward town.

NARI: Then we need to turn it around.

VEX: That will reverse the title.

MARA: Better the title than our route.

SFX: Paper turns. A twig snaps underfoot.

PIP: The left path is muddy.

MARA: The right path has dry leaves and goes uphill.

NARI: The map's contour lines show a ridge on the right.

VEX: The contour lines are part of the decorative border.

ODO: They are the actual terrain marks.

VEX: I intended them to be both.

PIP: I can hear the stream below us. It should be on the map.

SFX: Water trickles faintly.

MARA: The stream runs east. That tells us which side of the hill we're on.

NARI: We can use the sound and the map together.

VEX: But what about the silver compass rose?

ODO: Hold it to the moon. The reflection points to the stream.

PIP: The moon does not point. The map is catching light.

MARA: Still useful. We can follow the stream uphill to the lookout path.

NARI: No. We should stay on the marked dry trail, then cross the stream at the footbridge.

VEX: The map has no footbridge.

ODO: I added it after the last storm.

MARA: You updated the route but not the drawing?

ODO: I updated the map. Vex printed the old decorative version.

VEX: It has a very good border.

ODO: We should test the map from the other direction before we leave it for hikers.

VEX: The silver border has to face outward.

MARA: The route should face the reader.

PIP: Hold the paper at arm's length. I can follow the stream mark to the bridge, then the ridge.

NARI: Start at the trailhead.

PIP: Here. The water sound is left of us, so the footbridge is ahead and slightly right.

ODO: Correct.

VEX: I will add a note that the moonlight is not a compass.

MARA: Add it in small letters. The arrow and path name matter more.

SFX: Pencil writes. The map folds along its creases.

PIP: Now it folds so the lookout title stays outside.

VEX: At last, a dramatic map that opens the right way.

PIP: The path sign says LOOKOUT, with an arrow.

SFX: Wooden sign creaks.

NARI: That is clearer than the map.

VEX: We can place the map beside the sign and let the audience compare.

MARA: We can also mark the footbridge in ink before anyone else walks this trail.

SFX: Pencil scratches. Paper smooths against a tree trunk.

ODO: The arrow now points to the bridge.

VEX: The border still shines.

PIP: And the directions work.

TAG

NARI: The route card is ready for the trailhead.

ODO: I'll pin it below the map, with the bridge note facing up.

MARA: Keep the old version in the box marked "superseded."

VEX: The silver border can stay on the new print.

PIP: As long as the arrow points somewhere real.

SFX: Owl calls again.

VEX: Is that on the map?

MARA: No. That's the owl.
