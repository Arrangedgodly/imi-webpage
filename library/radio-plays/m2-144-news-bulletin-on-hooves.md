# The News Bulletin Arrives on Hooves

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio urgent-news bulletins, live corrections, and a town messenger who outruns the station's obsolete alert system*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Lela, a macaque news host; Dori, a gibbon editor; Venn, an orangutan town messenger; and Suki, a capuchin caller.*

[SFX: News theme cuts sharply; hoofbeats in the distance.]

LELA: We interrupt our scheduled program with a community update. The west path is closed due to a fallen branch.

DORI: That's from ten minutes ago. The branch has been cleared.

LELA: We should issue a correction.

DORI: The town messenger is bringing the update now.

[SFX: Hoofbeats approach, then stop.]

VENN: The west path is open. I ran the notice from the work crew.

LELA: Thank you. We will correct the broadcast.

SUKI: I heard the path was closed, so I took the east trail. I am now on the wrong side of the creek.

DORI: The east trail is still open.

SUKI: Yes, but I wanted the west path.

VENN: I can carry a correction to the east trailhead.

LELA: We can reach more people by radio.

DORI: The radio announcement is delayed in the west valley.

LELA: We need an update system that works on both sides.

VENN: I can run faster than the delay.

SUKI: How far is the east trailhead?

VENN: Around the creek.

SUKI: I can reach it in an hour.

LELA: The branch crew says the west path reopened at 7:10. We announced closure at 7:05.

DORI: The radio delay is five minutes, not ten.

LELA: Then the correction will air at 7:15.

VENN: I can reach the east trailhead by 7:12.

DORI: What if the path closes again?

VENN: Then I run back.

SUKI: Could you carry a flag with the current status?

VENN: I can carry a sign with two sides: open and closed.

LELA: We should not ask people to rely on a running messenger for every update.

DORI: The radio can announce the status. Venn carries the board to the valley while the delayed signal catches up.

SUKI: I will wait at the creek for the board.

VENN: I'll bring it to the east trailhead.

[SFX: Hoofbeats recede.]

LELA: We have a correction. The west path is open. Please use either trail; the east route is passable and the creek crossing is safe.

DORI: The update is now playing in the valley.

SUKI: I heard it on the portable radio. I am staying on the east trail.

LELA: The messenger has also reached you with the status board.

[SFX: Board flips; a hinge clicks.]

SUKI: The open side is showing.

VENN: The crew can flip it if the path changes again.

DORI: The system now has two parts: radio announcement and a physical board at the trail junction.

LELA: That is more reliable than sending Venn at a gallop.

VENN: I enjoyed the run.

SUKI: You can carry the board back when the shift ends.

VENN: I will walk it.

[SFX: Hoofbeats begin at a calm walking pace.]

LELA: The west path is open, the east trail is safe, and the update has reached both sides of the creek.

DORI: Correction received; no further bulletin is needed.

LELA: This is the Canopy News Desk. We will interrupt the schedule only when there is news.

[SFX: Theme returns at low volume, then fades.]SUKI: The status board has arrived at the east junction. I see the open side, so I can take the west trail home now.

VENN: I will leave the board by the crossing. The next messenger can flip it if the work crew changes the status.

DORI: The delayed radio correction will repeat at the next interval.

LELA: It will say the path is open, and the live board agrees.
