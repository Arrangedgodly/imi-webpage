# The Kitchen Timer Orchestra

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: cooking radio programs that arrange appliance beeps into music while food overcooks*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Chef Luma, a capuchin; producer Omi, a macaque; caller Tavi, a gibbon; technician Rook, a lemur.

[SFX: RADIO KITCHEN. OVEN TIMER BEEPS THREE TIMES. A POT BOILS.]

OMI: Hold the music. The oven has entered in A minor.

LUMA: The oven is telling me the tart is ready.

TAVI: It beeped three times in the same pitch. The kettle is making the harmony.

[SFX: KETTLE WHISTLES. POT LID RATTLES.]

ROOK: The kettle is boiling over.

OMI: That's our high note. Keep the microphones open.

LUMA: The recipe says remove the pot when it whistles.

OMI: The audience wants a kitchen symphony.

LUMA: The audience can hear the pot spilling.

[SFX: WATER HITS A HOT BURNER AND SIZZLES.]

TAVI: It sounds like applause.

ROOK: It smells like burnt rosemary.

LUMA: Turn the heat off. Rook, take the kettle from the burner. Omi, mute the soundboard.

OMI: If I mute it, we lose the live performance.

LUMA: If you don't, we'll lose the soup.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. KETTLE LIFTS. THE WHISTLE STOPS.]

TAVI: The high note is gone.

OMI: The soundboard has a loop of a kettle whistle. I can play it without the actual kettle.

LUMA: The loop doesn't tell me when the soup is boiling.

ROOK: The oven timer is still beeping. The tart needs to come out.

[SFX: OVEN DOOR OPENS. PAN SLIDES OUT. A TIMER BEEPS AGAIN.]

LUMA: The crust is dark at the edge. It should have come out when the first beep sounded.

OMI: But then we would only have one instrument.

LUMA: We had food. Now we have a burnt edge and a soundboard.

TAVI: I can scrape the edge while Luma checks the soup.

LUMA: Don't scrape it; the filling will collapse. Set it on the cooling rack.

[SFX: PAN SETS ON METAL RACK. POT SIMMERS.]

ROOK: The soup is safe. The bottom is not stuck.

OMI: We can make a rhythm from the cooling rack and the timer.

LUMA: We can record the timer after the cooking is done. The recipe is a sequence, not a band.

TAVI: I have a wooden spoon. It makes a good low beat.

[SFX: SPOON TAPS POT ONCE.]

LUMA: That's a pan, not a drum. Please stop.

OMI: We're live. The listeners heard it.

LUMA: Then tell them the timer beeped and we removed the tart.

OMI: That is a very short show.

TAVI: The tart has to cool for ten minutes. We can play the cooling rack.

LUMA: No. The rack is not a timer, and the food does not cool faster because we add a score.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS. A MIC SWITCH CLICKS OFF.]

ROOK: I have set the oven timer to silent mode. It still flashes, but it won't interrupt the conversation.

OMI: There is no sound.

LUMA: Exactly. We can describe the next step without making the appliances perform.

TAVI: The soup is back at a simmer.

LUMA: Then add the beans. Now stir slowly.

[SFX: SPOON STIRS. POT BUBBLES GENTLY.]

OMI: That is actually a good sound.

LUMA: It is a cooking sound. Don't loop it.

OMI: I'll leave it live, no effects.

[SFX: BEANS POUR INTO POT. SPOON STIRS AGAIN.]



OMI: The sponsor wants the beeps looped beneath the recipe. They say the sound makes listeners hungry.

LUMA: The beeps made us leave the tart in too long. They are not music, and they are not instructions once the oven is off.

ROOK: I have disconnected the loop and moved the timer speaker away from the microphone.

[SFX: CABLE UNPLUGS. A SINGLE OVEN BEEP SOUNDS IN THE ROOM, NOT OVER THE BROADCAST.]

TAVI: That is quieter.

OMI: The sponsor has sent a note: they will accept a closing sting after the kitchen is clear.

LUMA: Fine. One beep after the oven is off, then silence.

[SFX: THE OVEN DOOR CLOSES. THE ROOM SETTLES.]

ROOK: The timer light is flashing. Ten minutes are up.

LUMA: The tart is cool enough to serve. We made dinner, and the kitchen equipment did not become the orchestra.

OMI: Can we use the three oven beeps as our closing sting?

LUMA: Only after the tray is out and the oven is off.

[SFX: OVEN SWITCH CLICKS OFF. THREE RECORDED BEEPS PLAY ON THE RADIO, THEN STOP.]

TAVI: One repeat, after the cooking.

OMI: Kitchen Timer Orchestra is signing off.



[SFX: MIC CLOSES. SOUP SIMMERS SOFTLY; NO APPLIANCE BEEPS FOLLOW.]
