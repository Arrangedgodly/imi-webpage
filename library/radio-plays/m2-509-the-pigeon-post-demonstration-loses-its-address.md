# The Pigeon Post Demonstration Loses Its Address

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: technology demos that hide the simple step that makes the system work*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Presenter Vex, an orangutan at North Roof; handler Mara, a macaque at North Roof; trainee Pip, a capuchin at North Roof; clerk Odo, a gibbon at North Roof; home-loft handler Seli, a lemur at West Garden.

SFX: North Roof release platform. Wind moves the netting. The pigeon is quiet in its travel basket.

VEX: Today we demonstrate the original message network: pigeon post.

MARA: This bird is trained to return to its home loft at West Garden. It was brought here earlier for one release.

PIP: The message tube is ready. The address card is not.

ODO: The tube has a place for a human recipient and a short message.

VEX: The pigeon already knows where it is going. Why put an address on it?

MARA: The bird follows home. The written address tells the person at the loft who the message is for.

SELI: I'm at West Garden loft. The landing perch is ready, and the area is clear.

VEX: Our camera should show the bird, not paperwork.

PIP: The paperwork is how the message reaches Seli.

MARA: Write her name and the loft location so anyone finding the tube knows where it belongs.

SFX: Pencil scratches on card.

PIP: "For Seli, West Garden loft. Meet me at the orchard gate at noon."

ODO: The message and the address are legible. I'll slide the card into the tube.

SFX: Paper rolls into a small tube. Latch clicks shut.

MARA: Secure the tube to the carrier band. Keep it light and check that it cannot swing into the bird's wing.

SFX: Small clasp clicks. Pip tests the tube with a gentle tug.

PIP: It stays in place.

VEX: The address should face outward so the bird can read it on the way.

MARA: The bird will not read it. Keep the tag clear for the handler, but the bird follows its home loft.

ODO: The message tube must also sit clear of the wing. I'll check its swing while the bird is still settled in the basket.

SFX: Odo gently checks the tube; the clasp does not strike the feathers.

PIP: It is secure and out of the wing's path.

SELI: From the West Garden loft, I can identify the message by my name and location. The bird only needs a safe trip home.

VEX: The demonstration is less magical than I planned.

MARA: It is more accurate.

VEX: Can we announce the release now?

MARA: First we confirm that the bird is calm and the route home is open.

SELI: No workers are on the landing roof. The perch and water dish are set.

ODO: North hatch is latched. We can open it toward the marked release lane.

SFX: Latch slides. Wind rushes across the platform.

PIP: The bird is settled in my hands. No one is touching its wings.

VEX: We are ready.

MARA: Open your hands at the lane. Don't toss it.

SFX: Wings beat strongly, then fade into the distance.

VEX: The message is on its way.

ODO: The bird is heading west, toward its familiar loft.

SELI: I can see it above the garden. It has landed on the West Garden perch.

MARA: Let it settle before you handle the carrier.

SFX: At West Garden, wings fold and claws tap wood.

SELI: It stepped onto the perch. I'm lifting the tube from the carrier band now.

SFX: Clasp unhooks. Tube opens.

SELI: The card says, "For Seli, West Garden loft. Meet me at the orchard gate at noon."

VEX: The pigeon read the address.

MARA: No. It flew home. The address was for the human who received the tube.

SELI: I'll send a photo of the message card to the North Roof crew so the delivery is logged.

SFX: Phone camera clicks.

ODO: Photo received. The message is legible, and the tube is empty.

SELI: The bird is drinking from the loft dish now.

SELI: I'll keep it in the home loft to rest. No more flights today.

VEX: Could it carry a reply?

MARA: We would need a bird whose home loft is North Roof. This one has finished.

SELI: I can send the reply by phone. It has no wings to rest.

TAG

SFX: Loft door closes gently.

SELI: The bird is home. The message got where it needed to go.
