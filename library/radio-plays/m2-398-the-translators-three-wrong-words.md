# The Translator's Three Wrong Words

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: language radio demonstrations that force a machine to interpret animal calls as human sentences and mislead a live guest*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Translator Ena, a gibbon; host Kavi, a capuchin; engineer Selo, a macaque; researcher Piri, a lemur.

[SFX: FIELD RECORDING OF THREE MONKEY CALLS. KEYBOARD BEEPS.]

KAVI: Our translation program says the troop called, "Bring the red basket."

PIRI: We can't translate a call into a full sentence. We can describe pitch, timing and context.

ENA: The interface requires a sentence before it will save the clip.

SELO: The demo template has three likely phrases: food, danger or greeting.

KAVI: The audience wants to know what the troop said.

PIRI: They were calling near the feeding area, but the basket was blue and empty.

[SFX: CLIP PLAYS AGAIN. THREE CALLS, PAUSE, ONE CALL.]

ENA: The program chose "food" for the first three notes and "danger" for the last.

SELO: It is matching patterns to old labels. The classifier was trained on a small set of calls.

KAVI: Could the last call mean danger?

PIRI: A branch fell near the enclosure after the recording. The call came before it fell.

[SFX: BRANCH CREAKS. LEAVES RUSTLE.]

ENA: That is a new sound, not part of the recording.

KAVI: The show needs a translation headline.

PIRI: Call it "three contact calls near the feeding area, followed by one separate call." We do not know the words.

SELO: I can change the file type from "translated sentence" to "observed pattern."

[SFX: MENUS CLICK. A CONFIRMATION TONE.]

SELO: Pattern mode is available. I will mark three calls, a pause, then one call.

ENA: Add time and location.

SELO: Enclosure north, 10:42 a.m.

KAVI: Does the machine still offer a phrase?

SELO: It offers "bring the red basket" as a suggestion.

PIRI: Remove it from the public transcript. It was generated from a template, not heard in the calls.

[SFX: DELETE KEY TAPS.]

SELO: The automatic caption also printed "bring the red basket" on the screen.

PIRI: Remove that caption. It is an untested template phrase, not a translation.

ENA: The researcher notes are open beside the waveform. We can label the calls by timing and location.

KAVI: The sponsor has a sound bite ready: "The monkeys have a message."

PIRI: That sentence claims more than the recording shows.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. PROJECTED CAPTION DISAPPEARS.]

SELO: The public display now reads "three calls, pause, one call."

ENA: That matches the audio, and it does not pretend to know the words.

KAVI: I'll record that exact description for the show.

PIRI: The research log also records the time of day and who else was nearby.

ENA: Add the microphone position. We were near the feeding area.

SELO: The metadata now includes all three observations.

KAVI: Then a future researcher can compare the pattern without inheriting our guess.

KAVI: The audience may be disappointed.

ENA: We can explain how the program made the guess. That is more interesting than pretending it knows monkey grammar.

PIRI: I can compare today's pattern with the group's other recordings, but I need more samples before deciding if the pause has meaning.

KAVI: Let's play the recording once without the text.

[SFX: THREE CALLS, PAUSE, ONE CALL.]

ENA: That's what we heard.

SELO: The template is now disabled for this file. The save button records the pattern, not a sentence.

KAVI: I will say the program marked a pattern near the feeding area. It did not translate the calls.

PIRI: Good. The troop may have been greeting, calling or responding to something outside the microphone.

[SFX: A SECOND FIELD CLIP PLAYS, DIFFERENT RHYTHM.]

KAVI: This one has five quick calls.

PIRI: Log it separately. Do not give it the same phrase.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. TWO FILES SAVE WITH DISTINCT TIMES.]

ENA: The transcript has no invented quotation.

KAVI: The translation segment ends with a pattern we can replay and a sentence we cannot claim.

[SFX: RADIO MIC CLOSES. FIELD CALLS FADE UNDER ROOM TONE.]
