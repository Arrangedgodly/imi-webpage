# The Wind Tunnel's Applause Button

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: science broadcasts that use audience applause as a measurement of successful experiments*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Miri, a gibbon; engineer Tavo, a macaque; test pilot Oso, a capuchin; researcher Neka, a lemur.

[SFX: LARGE FAN SPINS UP. METAL TEST CHAMBER RUMBLES. STUDIO MIC OPENS.]

MIRI: Tonight we test the new glider in the valley wind tunnel. The audience will applaud when it flies.

TAVO: The applause meter sends a cue to my control panel. I armed the demo preset so applause would increase the fan speed after takeoff. I thought I could override it.

OSO: I am in the glider harness. I need the airflow steady before I release the wing.

MIRI: We'll begin with a gentle breeze.

[SFX: FAN RISES. A FEW HANDCLAPS FROM THE STUDIO.]

TAVO: The meter says the wing has earned a stronger gust.

NEKA: The applause meter measures volume. It does not measure lift.

OSO: The nose is pulling left. I'm holding it.

MIRI: Listeners, applaud if you think Oso is ready.

OSO: Do not ask them. I can feel the harness twisting.

TAVO: The control panel has a preset that turns applause into an increase.

[SFX: FAN SPEED RISES TWO NOTCHES. WING FABRIC SNAPS.]

NEKA: Tavo, reduce the fan to idle.

TAVO: The stop switch is behind the soundboard rack.

MIRI: The meter is climbing. The audience loves it.

OSO: The glider is not flying. It's dragging me sideways.

[SFX: METAL CARABINER CREAKS. Oso's boots scrape the floor.]

NEKA: I am muting the soundboard, not the fan. Tavo, reach the red stop switch.

TAVO: I have it.

MIRI: We have a dramatic moment. Can we keep the applause?

NEKA: No. The soundboard is muted, but the fan has its own circuit. Hit the red stop switch.

[SFX: MUTE SWITCH CLICKS. THE FAN KEEPS ROARING. TAVO REACHES BEHIND THE RACK AND SLAMS THE RED STOP SWITCH. FAN WINDS DOWN TO SILENCE.]

OSO: The harness is slack. I'm on the floor.

TAVO: Red switch down. The fan is off.

MIRI: So the test has failed?

NEKA: We didn't test the glider. We tested an audience meter wired to a control preset.

TAVO: The meter should trigger a light only. The light can show the room's noise level without changing the airflow.

MIRI: But the show needs a result.

OSO: We can run the glider once at steady speed. I can tell you when the wing lifts. No applause needed.

NEKA: First, we check the harness anchors while the fan is off.

[SFX: STRAPS PULL TIGHT. CARABINER CLICKS. A CHECKLIST PAGE TURNS.]

OSO: Left anchor holds. Right anchor holds. I'm back in the harness.

MIRI: The audience is quiet. Shall we begin?

MIRI: The soundboard can still raise the applause light if the room claps.

NEKA: That's fine. The light reports room volume. It cannot send a command to the fan.

TAVO: I am removing the applause cable from the speed-control input. The fan will have its own switch and fixed setting.

[SFX: PLUG PULLS FREE. A METER BEEPS AS TAVO TESTS THE LIGHT.]

TAVO: Three claps register on the display. The fan stays off.

OSO: That is the test we should have done before putting me in the harness.

MIRI: The audience has been asked to clap, so the test starts with the room quiet. We can run the safe demonstration after that.

TAVO: I will set the fan at the marked test speed and lock the control panel.

[SFX: TWO SWITCHES CLICK. FAN RISES TO A STEADY WIND.]

OSO: Air is even. The wing is lifting at the center. Hold it there.

NEKA: The airflow stays steady. No change from the applause meter.

MIRI: We have a test result. The glider lifts at the marked speed when the airflow stays constant.

OSO: I can guide it now.

[SFX: WING FABRIC HUMS IN THE WIND. A HAND SIGNAL BEEPS. FAN SLOWS.]

TAVO: Test complete. The meter is showing applause as a light only.

MIRI: No fan increase when we clap?

[SFX: THREE CLAPS. A LIGHT BEEPS ONCE. FAN DOES NOT CHANGE.]

NEKA: That is the correct response.

OSO: Now I can hear the wind tunnel instead of the studio.

MIRI: Our sponsor has sent a replacement slogan: "Applaud our shampoo. Keep the fan out of it."

OSO: After this wind, the shampoo can sponsor my comb.

[SFX: FAN SWITCH CLICKS OFF. WING SETTLES; MIC CLOSES.]
