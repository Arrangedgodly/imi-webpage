# Supper by Ear

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Radio cooking demonstrations and overly confident celebrity chefs*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Host Goma, a bonobo chef; Aunt Rilla, a mandrill caller; Kip, a young macaque helper; the studio parrot, a cockatiel that repeats the last instruction.

[THEME: PAN LID RATTLES, KNIFE CHOPS, A COOKING SHOW JINGLE.] 

HOST GOMA: Welcome to Supper by Ear, where you can cook along without seeing the pan and I can pretend that is a normal way to teach. Tonight we are making roasted squash with warm lentils. The squash is on the tray. The lentils are in the pot. If you hear a parrot, do not feed it the salt.

PARROT: Do not feed it the salt.

GOMA: First, rinse the lentils in a bowl. Kip, hand me the bowl.

KIP: The blue bowl?

GOMA: The bowl with the lentils.

KIP: They are both blue.

GOMA: This is why recipes say "one bowl." We have two bowls and one lentil.

[SFX: WATER RUNS. LENTILS RATTLE IN CERAMIC.] 

GOMA: Drain them. Add fresh water, a pinch of salt, and one crushed clove of garlic. Crush it with the flat side of the knife. You will hear a small thump.

[THUMP. THE STUDIO PARROT SQUAWKS.] 

PARROT: Small thump!

GOMA: Do not crush the parrot. We are cooking for four, not five.

KIP: The parrot has moved to the top shelf.

GOMA: Good. That's where the paprika is.

KIP: The paprika is beside the cinnamon.

GOMA: Take the red tin, not the brown jar. Paprika smells smoky. Cinnamon smells like a bakery is getting ready.

KIP: This smells like both.

GOMA: The listener has just discovered our studio spice drawer. Put it back. We'll use lemon and cumin instead.

[SFX: POT SIMMERS. A TIMER TICKS.] 

GOMA: While the lentils cook, brush the squash with oil. Don't drown it. The pieces should shine, not swim. Roast them until the edges brown. They will sound quiet in the oven, then soften when pressed with a spoon.

RILLA, CALLER: Chef, my squash turned to mush last week. I roasted it for an hour because I thought longer meant sweeter.

GOMA: Was it cut into small pieces?

RILLA: Smaller than a mouse's ear.

GOMA: Then it needed less time. A soft center is good. A pan of collapse is a different dish.

RILLA: Can I save it?

GOMA: Add it to the lentils at the end and call it stew. Food is allowed to change its mind.

RILLA: I like that. My family thought I'd ruined dinner.

GOMA: Tell them the squash has chosen a new career.

[SFX: OVEN DOOR OPENS, A HOT TRAY SLIDES.] 

KIP: The edges are brown. The middle is still firm.

GOMA: That's ready. Add the squash to the lentils with lemon juice and cumin. Stir gently so it holds its shape. Taste before adding more salt.

PARROT: Taste before adding more salt.

KIP: The parrot has taken over the lesson.

GOMA: It has the best audio position. Now add chopped greens at the end. They need one minute, just enough to wilt. If you add them earlier, they lose their color and complain about it.

RILLA: My greens always complain.

GOMA: They are probably overcooked.

[SFX: SPOON SCOOPS INTO A BOWL. SOMEONE TASTES.] 

KIP: It needs more lemon.

GOMA: I agree. Add a little, stir, taste again. There. The lentils are savory, the squash is sweet, and the greens still have a voice.

RILLA: I added my mushy squash to the pot. It's good. The family wants the recipe.

GOMA: Tell them the recipe is a guide. The important sounds are a steady simmer, a timer, and someone saying stop before the vegetables become a story.

GOMA: Supper by Ear is done. Turn off the oven, let the pot sit five minutes, and don't trust the parrot with the leftovers.

PARROT: Don't trust the parrot.

KIP: We have a plate for you.

GOMA: The parrot has a plate. It is the red tin.

[PARROT PECKS THE TIN. A SPOON CLINKS. MUSIC OUT.]
