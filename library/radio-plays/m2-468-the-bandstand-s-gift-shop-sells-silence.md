# The Bandstand's Gift Shop Sells Silence

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: concert broadcasts that add a retail pitch to the one quiet moment in the program*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Announcer Pip, a capuchin; musician Mara, a macaque; promoter Vex, an orangutan; sound technician Nari, a lemur; audience member Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Outdoor bandstand. A tuning string plucks. A sign creaks in the wind.

PIP: We are live at the village bandstand. Mara's small ensemble is ready to play its new piece, "Quiet Before Rain."

VEX: It has three movements and a special pause.

NARI: The pause is in the score.

MARA: It is the part where the instruments stop.

VEX: The sponsor has placed a gift table beside the microphone. The souvenir program includes a coupon for the pause.

ODO: Can I buy a longer one?

VEX: The extended silence is a limited edition.

MARA: Please move the table away from the stage. The wind keeps blowing the coupons onto my music.

SFX: Paper skitters. Mara catches a program against her shoe.

VEX: The table is part of the event.

NARI: The paper is covering the floor microphone.

PIP: Listeners, the first movement begins now.

SFX: Four instruments play a light, quick pattern. A loose coupon flaps against the stand.

MARA: Hold the last note.

SFX: A flute tone sustains. Wind rattles the program table.

VEX: This is the perfect time to mention the coupon.

MARA: The note has not ended.

VEX: The sponsor asked for a spoken read during the pause.

ODO: Is this the pause I bought?

NARI: No one bought the pause.

VEX: The table sold three.

MARA: Who is playing them?

VEX: The audience can request them.

PIP: There is no pause request in the program.

ODO: I would like to request a pause.

MARA: You are sitting in the audience. You can have one after the second movement.

VEX: Could we sell it first?

NARI: The instruments need to tune between movements.

VEX: That is inventory.

MARA: That's maintenance.

SFX: The first movement ends. One tuning peg slips, giving a low plunk.

ODO: Is that the pause?

MARA: That is a string that needs tuning.

VEX: We can sell the tuning note.

NARI: No.

SFX: Nari turns the peg carefully. The note settles.

PIP: The second movement begins.

SFX: A slow tune. The wind moves the sign, making it squeak softly.

ODO: The sign is singing along.

VEX: Is the sign part of the ensemble?

MARA: It is not on the score.

NARI: I can tie it back from the microphone stand.

SFX: Rope pulls tight. Sign stops creaking. The audience settles.

PIP: We have reached the special pause.

SFX: The musicians stop. Wind and distant birds remain.

VEX: For one acorn, you can take home this moment of stillness.

ODO: I can hear the birds.

MARA: You can hear the birds for free.

NARI: The gift table's paper is gone now. The stage is quiet.

VEX: The coupon promised silence, not birds.

PIP: No outdoor concert can promise silence.

ODO: I want my acorn back.

VEX: The coupon is nonrefundable.

MARA: We will play the final movement without a sales read.

SFX: Music rises, then falls to one soft chord. It decays naturally in the open air.

PIP: The piece has ended.

ODO: That was the silence.

MARA: That was the chord fading away.

VEX: We can sell a recording.

NARI: Only if the musicians agree.

MARA: We agree to play it again next week. We do not agree to sell the quiet between notes.

ODO: I can put an acorn in the support jar.

VEX: The jar is part of the gift shop.

NARI: It is at the gate, and the money pays the musicians.

MARA: We can hear one acorn go in. It does not buy a pause.
