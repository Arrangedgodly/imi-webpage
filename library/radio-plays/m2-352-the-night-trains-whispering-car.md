# The Night Train's Whispering Car

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: railway mystery broadcasts that treat ordinary carriage sounds as a supernatural presence*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Conductor Evi, a lemur; host Joss, a macaque; mechanic Danu, a baboon; passenger Kiri, a gibbon.

[SFX: NIGHT TRAIN ROLLING. WHEELS CLACK. A COMPARTMENT DOOR SLIDES OPEN.]

JOSS: Tonight on Midnight Rails, our passenger says the empty car is whispering.

KIRI: It keeps saying "change sides" whenever we pass a bridge.

EVI: That is the track joint. The car rocks and the loose luggage rack taps the wall.

JOSS: Or the carriage is warning us.

DANU: The rack is above seat six. The latch has slipped.

[SFX: TRAIN CLACKS OVER A BRIDGE. METAL RACK TAPS, THEN A LOW AIRY WHISTLE.]

KIRI: There. "Change sides."

EVI: The air vent whistles when the door is half open. The sound is coming from this compartment.

JOSS: Can you keep recording while we investigate? Listeners love evidence.

EVI: I am keeping the door open and asking the passenger to stay seated until the train slows at the next station.

KIRI: I'm seated. The tapping is above me.

DANU: I can secure the rack when the train stops. I won't reach above a moving passenger.

JOSS: The whisper has a mechanical explanation, but perhaps the train is communicating through its own machinery.

EVI: The train is communicating through a loose latch. That's plenty.

[SFX: PA ANNOUNCEMENT: NEXT STOP, ASH CROSSING. BRAKES BEGIN TO HISS.]

KIRI: The tapping stopped.

DANU: The wheels are slowing. I'll check the latch from the aisle.

[SFX: TRAIN STOPS. BRAKES SET. DOOR SLIDES OPEN TO PLATFORM AMBIENCE.]

DANU: The rack bracket is intact. The latch pin slipped out of its catch. There is a scarf caught around it.

KIRI: That's mine. I put it up there when the compartment got cold.

JOSS: The scarf has been haunting the rack.

KIRI: The scarf has been holding the latch open.

EVI: I'll take it down. Danu, can you reset the pin?

[SFX: SCARF PULLED FREE. METAL PIN SLIDES INTO PLACE WITH A CLUNK.]

DANU: It is locked. I will test it with the carriage still.

[SFX: RACK SHAKES ONCE. NO TAP.]

JOSS: We need a sound test while rolling.

EVI: We can test when the train is moving again and the rack is empty.

KIRI: Put the scarf in my bag. It can haunt the luggage compartment.

[SFX: SCARF FOLDS INTO BAG. TRAIN DOORS CLOSE. ENGINE WHINES; TRAIN PULLS AWAY.]

JOSS: We're moving. Danu, what do you hear?

DANU: Wheels, vent, no rattle.

[SFX: TRAIN CROSSES A JOINT. WHEELS CLACK. VENT WHISTLES BRIEFLY. RACK STAYS SILENT.]

KIRI: It only sounds like a whisper when I expect words.

JOSS: That is what mystery programs are built on: a sound, a suggestion, and a host willing to ask a follow-up.

EVI: The useful follow-up is whether the latch is secure.

DANU: It is. The scarf is stowed, the rack is quiet, and the vent is still whistling.

JOSS: We can call this "The Ghost That Was a Scarf."

KIRI: Call it "I Need a Coat Hook."

EVI: The next compartment has proper hooks. I'll show you when we reach the next stop.

JOSS: And Midnight Rails will close before we hear anything else in the walls.

JOSS: I had prepared a ghost story intro with a whistle and a distant voice.

EVI: A whistle will sound like the vent. Don't add one while passengers are listening for a fault.

DANU: The rack is locked. I am removing the spare scarf from the hook so it cannot slide back up there.

[SFX: COAT HOOK CREAKS. SCARF GOES INTO A ZIPPERED BAG.]

KIRI: The next time I need warmth, I will use the proper hook by the heater.

JOSS: Midnight Rails has lost its ghost and gained a coat check.

EVI: A coat check does not delay the train.

[SFX: TRAIN ROLLS INTO DISTANCE. ONE QUIET VENT WHISTLE, THEN WHEELS ONLY.]
