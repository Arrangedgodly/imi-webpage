# Swap Shop Saturday

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in swap shop radio programs*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Host Nibs, a chatty tamarin; Alix, a practical macaque producer; Mrs. Oru, a retired baboon; Kettle, a young capuchin caller; and Bo, an orangutan listener with a collection of tins.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC ON A WOBBLY PIANO. A BELL RINGS.] 

NIBS: Welcome to Swap Shop Saturday, where one neighbor's spare thing becomes another neighbor's useful thing. We accept furniture, garden tools, puzzles with every piece, and mysteries only if the mystery has a phone number. Alix, what is our first offer?

ALIX: Mrs. Oru has two sturdy stools. She wants a step ladder that does not wobble.

ORU, PHONE: The stools are brown. One has a mark from a hot teapot. They are not precious, but they hold a person.

NIBS: We like a clear product description. Anyone need two stools?

[SFX: PHONE LINE LIGHT CHIMES.] 

KETTLE: I have a short step ladder. It wobbles because one rubber foot is missing. I can bring it if someone has the right foot.

ALIX: I have a box of spare chair feet. We can check the size before we swap anything.

NIBS: That is the spirit. No one should climb on a ladder until it is stable. We will not be demonstrating a wobble on air.

[SFX: PAPER TURN, PHONE RING.] 

NIBS: Bo is offering twelve tins. What do the tins contain?

BO, PHONE: Nothing. They are empty. I collect tins with pictures of fruit. I have twelve pears and no place to keep them.

NIBS: Do you want a box?

BO: I want a shelf. The tins keep rolling under the bed.

ALIX: Mrs. Oru has a small shelf. It fits two stools and a ladder, but not twelve tins.

ORU: I have a narrow shelf. I stopped collecting cookbooks. It is pine. One bracket is bent.

BO: I can fix a bracket. I have a tin of screws.

NIBS: We have moved from fruit tins to screw tins. This program is becoming a hardware shop.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS IN STUDIO. FOOTSTEPS, A METAL TIN ROLLS.] 

ALIX: Bo is here. He brought all twelve tins. The fruit pictures are different. One has a plum, one has an orange, and one has an apple with a face.

NIBS: Is the apple smiling?

BO: It may be grimacing.

NIBS: We will not appraise its expression.

[SFX: ANOTHER PHONE LINE.] 

CALLER: I have a box of jigsaw puzzles. They are missing pieces.

NIBS: We cannot list incomplete puzzles as complete.

CALLER: Could I trade them for a complete puzzle?

ALIX: We have a puzzle that is complete but the picture on the box is faded. The pieces are all there. You can take it if you help sort your box and mark which ones are missing.

CALLER: That sounds fair. I can bring them to the library table.

NIBS: We will mark the boxes clearly. Nobody likes discovering a missing corner halfway through a rainy afternoon.

[SFX: STUDIO CLOCK CHIMES.] 

NIBS: We have twenty minutes left. Mrs. Oru's stools are still available. Kettle has brought the ladder. Alix has the spare foot. Let's test it on the studio floor.

[SFX: LADDER SET DOWN. RUBBER FOOT PRESSED INTO PLACE. THREE CAREFUL TAPS.] 

ALIX: Stable. Kettle gets the stools. Mrs. Oru gets the repaired ladder. Bo gets the shelf after he straightens the bracket.

BO: And where do the tins go?

NIBS: On the shelf, with the fruit side facing out.

BO: What if the apple looks upset?

NIBS: Then it can face the wall.

[SFX: SMALL LAUGHTER.] 

ALIX: Before we close, a reminder. Swap items should be safe, clean, and described honestly. We will not trade medicines, pets, or things someone else owns. If a tool needs repair, say so before someone carries it home.

NIBS: That's our Saturday. We traded stools, a ladder, a shelf, and several opinions about a printed apple. Next week, bring an item you can carry without blocking the bus.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC. ONE TIN ROLLS ACROSS TABLE.] 

BO: That one is supposed to roll.

NIBS: A successful swap.
