# The Orchestra Tunes a Doorbell

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: classical radio critics forced to score neighborhood sounds*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Conductor Nari, a lemur; critic Vex, an orangutan; technician Odo, a gibbon; caretaker Mara, a macaque; caller Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Studio. A tuning fork rings; a doorbell answers with two notes.

VEX: The neighborhood sound has arrived. I give the doorbell a seven for restraint and a nine for bourgeois longing.

NARI: It is the rehearsal room's bell. We need to tune the orchestra, not review the entrance.

ODO: The bell's second note is flat. I can adjust the chime plate after we unplug it.

MARA: It belongs to the caretaker's office. Please ask before opening the cover.

PIP: I pressed it to see if the rehearsal room was open. I didn't know it was part of a broadcast.

VEX: Everything becomes part of a broadcast when the microphone is hot.

NARI: First we tune the oboe to A. Odo, give the note.

SFX: Oboe note rises; doorbell rings over it.

NARI: Stop. That bell is masking the reference pitch.

VEX: A bold duet. The bell challenges the woodwind, then withdraws.

MARA: It is someone at the door. I'll answer it.

SFX: Door opens. Hallway air enters; bell's chime fades.

MARA: Nobody there. The bell button stuck down. Pip, did you release it?

PIP: Yes, but the button stayed in. I can lift it gently.

ODO: Don't pull the wire. The bell is still connected. I'll shut off the small circuit at the labeled switch.

SFX: Switch clicks off.

ODO: No power. Now I can remove the plate and clear the button.

VEX: We have a technical pause. Can the orchestra sustain a chord while the repair happens?

NARI: Not until we have a quiet room. Everyone take the instruments away from the doorway.

SFX: Chairs scrape back; a music stand folds.

MARA: The button is released. Odo, test it once after the cover is secure.

ODO: Cover on. Circuit restored.

SFX: Switch clicks on. Doorbell rings two clean notes.

PIP: The second note sounds higher.

ODO: The button no longer sticks, so the full chime is ending instead of being held down.

VEX: I revise my score. The bell has found its resolution.

NARI: Now the A. Oboe, please.

SFX: Oboe gives a clear A. The bell remains quiet.

NARI: That's the pitch. Begin the rehearsal from the top, with the doorway closed.

VEX: The doorbell's review will appear in tomorrow's paper.

MARA: No. You can review the concert after the concert. The bell is a doorbell again.

PIP: Should I knock next time?

MARA: If the button is working, ring it once and wait.


NARI: We have another audition. Mara will ring the bell once, then Odo will match that pitch on the piano. The players can hear the room tone between them.

MARA: Is the bell still connected to the office circuit?

ODO: Yes, but the circuit is safe. It is a low-voltage chime, and the cover is secured.

SFX: Bell rings two notes. Piano repeats the lower note.

VEX: The piano has answered the bell. I award the bell a point for initiating conversation.

NARI: We are not scoring the bell. The note helps us confirm the repair did not change the chime.

PIP: The second note is still clear. The button released properly.

MARA: Now we can start the orchestra without the interruption.

SFX: Oboe gives A; strings tune beneath it.

VEX: I hear the room come into tune.

NARI: That's the rehearsal. The bell has no further part in it.

TAG

SFX: Two doorbell notes; a pause; soft footsteps away.

VEX: A thoughtful exit.

NARI: The caller left before your review.
