# The Lost Umbrella's Press Conference

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: breaking-news press conferences that inflate a minor public-property mix-up into an official investigation*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Reporter Vala, a macaque; caretaker Binu, a gibbon; mayor's aide Soro, a lemur; caller Etti, a capuchin.

[SFX: COMMUNITY HALL. MICROPHONES TEST; UMBRELLA OPENS.] 

VALA: We are live at the hall where one blue umbrella was found beside the coat rack.

SORO: The mayor's office has prepared a statement. We are treating the umbrella's absence from its owner as a serious matter.

BINU: It was left at the coat rack after the rain stopped. I put it in lost property.

VALA: How long was it unaccounted for?

BINU: About twelve minutes.

SORO: The statement says it was missing for an uncertain period.

ETTI: I called because the community bulletin said "umbrella disappearance under review." I thought the hall had closed.

VALA: The hall is open. The umbrella is in a labeled basket.

[SFX: PAPER FLAPS; A SIGN IS TURNED AROUND.] 

BINU: The label says FOUND TODAY, BLUE UMBRELLA, ASK THE DESK.

SORO: The aide's draft says "mysterious blue object." It sounds more official.

VALA: Can the owner describe it?

ETTI: Mine has a yellow patch on the handle and a bent rib. I left it in the hall.

BINU: This one has a yellow patch. One rib is bent.

[SFX: UMBRELLA OPENS; METAL RIB TICKS.] 

VALA: We have a match. The umbrella was located.

VALA: A banner on the podium says "Umbrella Watch: stay tuned."

SORO: The communications office printed it for the statement.

BINU: It is blocking the lost-property hours.

ETTI: I could not see the desk time from the door.

VALA: Move the banner, and the useful sign can stay.

[SFX: BANNER ROLLS UP; CLIP RELEASES.]

SORO: We can reuse the paper for tomorrow's meeting. It does not need an emergency logo.

ETTI: I found the desk as soon as I could read the sign.

SORO: The mayor's statement is still queued for release.

BINU: Please cancel it before the bulletin calls this a citywide emergency.

[SFX: PHONE BUTTON PRESSES; A CANCEL TONE.] 

SORO: Canceled. I will replace it with a lost-property notice.

VALA: Do we need a press conference for a notice?

SORO: The podium was already booked for the afternoon.

ETTI: I can come to the desk and take my umbrella.

BINU: Bring your coat too. The rain is starting again.

[SFX: RAIN BEGINS AGAINST WINDOWS.] 

VALA: The umbrella has been recovered before the owner arrived.

SORO: The draft wants us to say a review is ongoing.

BINU: The review is over. We checked the basket, matched the patch, and called the owner.

VALA: I will report that sequence.

SORO: Could we at least have a closing image of the umbrella beside the podium?

BINU: It belongs with its owner, not as a backdrop.

[SFX: HALL DOOR OPENS; WET FOOTSTEPS APPROACH.] 

ETTI: That's mine. I recognize the bent rib. Thank you for keeping it dry.

BINU: It was under the coat rack, away from the window.

VALA: Here is the owner receiving the umbrella. No mystery remains.

SORO: The mayor's office can post the lost-property hours instead of a statement.

ETTI: That would help. I did not know the desk stayed open after meetings.

BINU: It is open until six. The sign has the time on it.

[SFX: SIGN FLIPS; PAPER CLIP SNAPS.] 

VALA: The sign had been turned backward for the press conference.

SORO: I will stand it upright. The public can read the actual hours.

ETTI: I am going home before the umbrella gets a spokesperson.

VALA: That is the complete update. A blue umbrella was found, matched and returned.

SORO: The statement is canceled; the coat rack is not under investigation.

[SFX: UMBRELLA OPENS OVER RAIN; DOOR CLOSES.] 

BINU: The rain stopped yesterday, not today.

VALA: That will be our next bulletin, if it becomes news.

[SFX: MIC CLICKS OFF; RAIN CONTINUES.]
