# The Night Market Microphone

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live remote radio broadcasts, roving microphones, and hosts who mistake every market sound for a planned segment*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST KORI, a gibbon
REPORTER JALA, a macaque
VENDOR NEF, a capuchin
DRUMMER PAX, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: NIGHT MARKET CROWD, FOOTSTEPS, COOKING SIZZLE.]

KORI: We are live at the evening market, where every stall has a story and every story may be interrupted by a bell.

JALA: I am walking past the fruit sellers. The air smells of roasted nuts and hot bread.

KORI: Describe a color for listeners at home.

JALA: The lanterns are orange, the cloth awnings are green, and one basket is...

[SFX: HAND BELL RINGS.]

JALA: We have been interrupted by a bell.

NEF: That is my bell. The tea is ready.

KORI: The market has announced a beverage segment.

NEF: The tea is for customers. Do you want a cup?

KORI: We are broadcasting.

NEF: Then take one after your report.

[SFX: CUP SET DOWN.]

JALA: I have reached the spice table. Nef's stall is behind us.

KORI: Can you compare the spices by aroma?

JALA: I can. The cinnamon is warm, the ginger is sharp, and the pepper is...

[SFX: DRUMBEAT STARTS NEARBY.]

KORI: The market's percussion ensemble has joined our scent story.

PAX: I am rehearsing. The bandstand is around the corner.

JALA: The drum is louder than the interview.

KORI: We have captured the pulse of commerce.

PAX: It is my drum. I need to hear whether the skin is loose.

[SFX: DRUM TUNED, BEAT SOFTENS.]

JALA: Thank you. I am now at the cloth stall. The vendor has a bright blue scarf.

KORI: Ask what it represents.

NEF: It keeps the night wind off your neck.

KORI: A practical symbol.

NEF: It is a scarf.

JALA: Someone is waving from the other side of the aisle.

[SFX: FABRIC FLUTTERS.]

KORI: The scarf has a message.

NEF: I am trying to keep it from blowing into the tea.

JALA: The market keeper is ringing the closing bell. Vendors are folding their tables.

[SFX: CLOSING BELL, TABLE LEGS FOLD.]

KORI: We need one last impression from the night market.

NEF: The last impression is that you are standing in the aisle while I pack.

JALA: I will move aside. The lanterns are going dark one by one.

[SFX: LANTERN SWITCHES CLICK, CROWD THINS.]

KORI: The soundscape is changing from conversation to footsteps.

PAX: And one drumbeat. I am taking my instrument home.

[SFX: SINGLE DRUMBEAT, CASE LATCHES.]

JALA: The drum is packed. The tea is packed. The scarf is secure.

NEF: The microphone is still in the aisle.

KORI: We will pick it up after signoff.

JALA: The reporter will help hold it.

[SFX: SCARF TUGS.]

KORI: We have a live demonstration of market teamwork.

NEF: You have a live scarf covering the microphone.

[SFX: AUDIO MUFFLES, CROWD LAUGHS.]

JALA: I can still hear you, but the listeners may hear fabric.

KORI: This is an immersive report.

NEF: The scarf is now tied to the basket handle. The tea is safe.

PAX: My drum is tuned. Can I play one market beat before you leave?

KORI: Yes, but only one.

[SFX: SINGLE DRUMBEAT.]

KORI: That was a full performance.

PAX: It was one beat.

JALA: The stalls are closing. Nef has packed the tea, and the scarf has stayed on the basket.

KORI: We have experienced flavor, sound, cloth, and one cup we did not drink.

NEF: It is cold now.

KORI: The market has given us a final weather update.

JALA: We are leaving the aisle before the next object becomes a segment.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS AWAY, BELL RINGS.]

KORI: The bell is Nef's again.

NEF: I am closing my stall.

KORI: This has been our live report. Good night from the market, where even the signoff may be a vendor at work.

[SFX: MARKET FADES, DRUMBEAT OUT.]
