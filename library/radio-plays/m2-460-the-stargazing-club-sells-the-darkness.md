# The Stargazing Club Sells the Darkness

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sponsored outdoor broadcasts that brand the conditions their audience came to experience*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; astronomer Nari, a lemur; sponsor rep Vex, an orangutan; club member Mara, a macaque; student Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Night field. Crickets. A car door closes. Portable lamp clicks on.

ODO: We are live at the hilltop stargazing night, sponsored by BrightTrail Lanterns.

NARI: Please switch off the lamp. It is washing out the sky.

VEX: BrightTrail helps everyone see.

MARA: We came to see what is above us.

VEX: We can put the logo on the telescope.

PIP: The telescope points at the sky.

VEX: Then it will be seen by the stars.

NARI: The stars do not read logos.

ODO: Our first target is the crescent moon.

SFX: Telescope mount creaks.

PIP: I can't see it.

NARI: The lamp is reflecting in the eyepiece. Mara, switch it off.

VEX: The sponsor needs a visible product.

MARA: It has a red setting.

VEX: The red setting still glows.

NARI: We need darkness to see the faint stars. The club can use a small covered light to read the chart, then cover it again.

VEX: Our offer includes a lantern demonstration.

PIP: Demonstrate it pointed at the ground.

SFX: Lamp clicks off. Fabric cover slides over a small red bulb. Night sounds return.

MARA: I see the moon now.

PIP: There is a bright point near it.

NARI: That's Jupiter. Hold the telescope steady. Let your eyes adjust.

VEX: Can we name it BrightTrail Jupiter?

NARI: No.

ODO: The sponsor has a question. Vex, what did you hope to see tonight?

VEX: Our lantern in every shot.

MARA: You can see it when the program ends.

VEX: That is not a sponsorship benefit.

PIP: I can record the lantern's click. It has a good switch sound.

SFX: Covered lamp clicks on, clicks off.

VEX: That could be the sponsor cue.

ODO: We have a sound logo.

NARI: As long as it stays off while people look through the telescope.

VEX: We can put the product name in the announcement.

MARA: Quietly.

VEX: BrightTrail Lanterns: for seeing the path back to your car.

NARI: That is accurate.

ODO: We should check the chart for the meteor shower.

PIP: I see a streak.

NARI: That's an airplane. It has steady lights.

VEX: Could we call it a sponsored meteor?

MARA: No.

SFX: Pip draws a quick breath as another streak crosses the sky.

PIP: That one moved faster.

NARI: Make a note of the time. We can compare its direction with the chart.

SFX: The chart flaps in the breeze and taps the telescope tripod.

PIP: I need the chart to stay still.

VEX: I can aim the lantern at it.

NARI: Use the covered red bulb. Keep the beam on the paper and away from the telescope.

SFX: A clip catches the chart to the tripod. Pencil scratches as Pip marks the meteor's path.

PIP: The line begins above the ridge and ends east of Jupiter.

NARI: Good. The chart records what we saw, not what we wish we'd seen.

ODO: Is it part of the show?

NARI: It was there whether or not we were broadcasting.

VEX: We have an unsponsored celestial event.

MARA: Put it in the report.

PIP: The sponsor lantern is still off.

VEX: But the path is visible in the red light.

NARI: The chart is legible, and the sky is dark again.

ODO: The club has seen Jupiter, one airplane, and a meteor.

VEX: And one lantern.

MARA: The lantern is packed.

SFX: Fabric cover folds. A bag closes.

VEX: May I hold it for the closing photograph?

NARI: No flash.

VEX: I was going to pose beside the telescope.

PIP: It is pointed away now. The lens cap is on.

ODO: This has been Stargazing Live. The sky remains open after sign-off.

TAG

SFX: Club members walk downhill. A covered red lamp clicks on, aimed at the path.

VEX: BrightTrail shows the way home.

MARA: At last, it has the right job.
