# Underwriting the Mural

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public-radio sponsor underwriting, restrictive acknowledgments, and arts coverage*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: host Vale, a macaque; muralist Omi, a gibbon; station producer Nera, a baboon; sponsor rep Lio, a capuchin; caller Tavi, a lemur.

[SFX: STUDIO HUM. A PAINTBRUSH SCRAPES. A SMALL BELL RINGS.]

VALE: You're listening to the Canopy Arts Hour, supported by Barkwell Boards, makers of sturdy boards for artists who prefer to work on boards.

NERA: That is the approved wording.

LIO: It must include "responsibly harvested."

VALE: You're listening to the Canopy Arts Hour, supported by Barkwell Boards, makers of responsibly harvested sturdy boards for artists who prefer to work on boards.

OMI: My mural is on a wall.

LIO: Is the wall responsibly harvested?

OMI: The wall was here before the station.

LIO: We can say the mural is responsibly attached.

NERA: That's not in the agreement.

VALE: We are joined by muralist Omi, whose new work covers the east wall of the library.

OMI: It is a picture of the river.

LIO: Please don't call it a river.

OMI: What should I call it?

LIO: A flowing water feature.

OMI: It is paint.

NERA: The sponsor is paying for the broadcast.

VALE: We can call it a painted flowing water feature.

OMI: I paint rivers.

LIO: That sounds like a claim.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

VALE: We have a caller.

TAVI: I am standing in front of the mural.

VALE: What do you think?

TAVI: It looks like the river.

LIO: Is the caller authorized to identify waterways?

NERA: We can ask them not to.

TAVI: I can hear you.

VALE: Please describe what you see without naming it.

TAVI: Blue paint, green banks, a canoe, and a little monkey waving.

OMI: The monkey is my signature.

LIO: Does the monkey represent Barkwell Boards?

OMI: It represents me.

LIO: Is the monkey on a board?

OMI: It is on the wall.

NERA: We are about to take a break.

VALE: After the break, Omi will tell us how the mural was made.

LIO: Please do not say the wall was painted.

OMI: I used paint.

LIO: Could we call it color application?

VALE: We'll find a word after the break.

[SFX: RECORDED MUSIC STARTS. THE PHONE RINGS OVER IT.]

NERA: We are still on air.

VALE: The break music has begun.

TAVI: My call is still connected.

LIO: Can you hold?

TAVI: I am holding a basket.

OMI: What is in it?

TAVI: Lunch for the crew.

LIO: Is the basket responsibly harvested?

TAVI: It is woven.

LIO: From what?

TAVI: Vines.

OMI: That sounds responsibly harvested.

LIO: We have not approved the basket.

NERA: The mural crew needs lunch.

VALE: This is a station about arts and daily life.

LIO: It is a station about boards.

OMI: Barkwell makes boards. My mural has a wooden frame.

LIO: The frame is in the mural?

OMI: Around it.

LIO: Then we can acknowledge the frame.

VALE: Our program is supported by Barkwell Boards. The mural's frame uses a responsibly harvested board. The river is made of paint.

OMI: Thank you.

[SFX: PAINTBRUSH SCRAPES. A WOODEN FRAME TAPS AGAINST THE STUDIO TABLE.]

TAVI: I can hear the frame.

LIO: That's good.

NERA: We have reached a useful agreement.

VALE: Omi, how long did the mural take?

OMI: Three weeks.

LIO: The frame took four days.

OMI: The frame took one afternoon.

LIO: We are discussing boards.

VALE: The mural was painted over three weeks. The frame was attached in one afternoon. Barkwell Boards supports arts coverage and secure attachments.

OMI: That is accurate.

TAVI: I see the little monkey waving.

OMI: The monkey is waving at the library.

LIO: We need to close with the sponsor acknowledgment.

VALE: The Canopy Arts Hour is supported by Barkwell Boards, responsibly harvested boards for walls that stay still.

[SFX: STUDIO HUM STOPS. A DISTANT DRIP SOUNDS.]

OMI: The wall is leaking.

LIO: Is the water feature painted or real?

VALE: It's real.

[PAUSE.]

NERA: We need to move the microphone.

[SFX: MIC STAND SCRAPES. WATER DRIPS INTO A BOWL.]
