# The Orchard at Midnight

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio mystery serial where the clues are ordinary animal behavior*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: DETECTIVE NELL, a macaque; KEEPER FOSS, a slow loris; WITNESS ARU, a capuchin; and ANNOUNCER.

[SFX: MYSTERY THEME, NIGHT WIND, ONE APPLE ROLLING TO A STOP.] 

ANNOUNCER: Midnight in the orchard. The basket of ripe fruit is missing from its shelf. Detective Nell has arrived, notebook open and magnifying glass pointed at the moon.

NELL: Keeper Foss, when did you last see the basket?

FOSS: At dusk. The basket was on the low shelf. The fruit was inside.

NELL: Aru, you were on the night path. Did you see anyone carry it away?

ARU: No. I heard a scrape and a soft thump. Then I saw an apple beside the north path.

NELL: A clue with two parts.

FOSS: Or one apple and one sound.

NELL: Let us not dismiss either possibility before examination. Where did the sound come from?

ARU: Under the fig tree. I did not approach. It was dark, and I was sleepy.

NELL: Were you asleep?

ARU: I was closing one eye at a time.

NELL: A careful witness. Foss, has anything else moved tonight?

FOSS: The wind shifted the dry leaves. One empty crate rolled from the wall to the path.

NELL: Was it a basket?

FOSS: It is a crate. The basket has a round handle.

NELL: Could a crate sound like a basket in the dark?

ARU: They both sound like wood scraping.

NELL: We will test the sound. No reenactment until we know what we are reenacting.

[SFX: FOSS SETS AN EMPTY CRATE ON THE FLOOR. IT SCRAPES, THEN THUMPS AGAINST A STONE.] 

ARU: That is close. The thump was softer outside.

FOSS: There was no stone under the fig tree. There was an apple.

NELL: An apple could roll against the crate.

ARU: I heard the scrape first, then the thump.

FOSS: The wind could move the crate and the apple. It often pushes loose things down that path.

NELL: But it would not move the fruit basket from the shelf.

FOSS: No.

NELL: So where is the basket?

FOSS: We should check the storage room. I moved the fruit there at dusk because the evening was warm. I left the basket on the shelf.

NELL: You moved the fruit before the basket went missing?

FOSS: Yes. I carried each piece inside, then returned to close the side door.

NELL: The shelf was empty of fruit, but not of basket.

FOSS: Correct.

NELL: When did you last look at the basket?

FOSS: Just before the wind picked up.

ARU: The shelf is beside the vent. The wind blows papers off it.

NELL: Then the basket could have moved without footprints.

[THEY WALK TO THE STORAGE ROOM. A DOOR OPENS. BOWLS CLINK.] 

FOSS: The fruit is here, covered and cool.

NELL: No missing fruit. Now the basket.

ARU: I hear the vent flap.

[SFX: VENT FLUTTERS. A LIGHT BASKET SCRAPES ALONG A SHELF, THEN FALLS INTO A BIN OF LEAVES.] 

FOSS: There it is. It was on the shelf above the vent.

NELL: The basket moved when the gust pushed it. The scrape and thump came from the crate and apple under the fig tree.

ARU: Two sounds, one mystery.

NELL: Two ordinary sounds, one missing basket, and a fruit delivery made before the wind rose.

FOSS: I should have put the basket in the cabinet after I emptied it.

NELL: The cabinet is away from the vent. Put it there now.

[BASKET LIFTS, DOOR CLOSES.] 

ANNOUNCER: Detective Nell closes the case. The fruit is in the cool room, the basket is in the cabinet, and the apple is still beside the path.

ARU: Should we bring in the apple?

NELL: No. It is not missing.

FOSS: What will you call the case?

NELL: The Basket the Wind Borrowed.

ARU: Will you charge it rent?

NELL: Only if it comes back tomorrow.

[SFX: THE THEME RETURNS. WIND RUSTLES THE FIG TREE; THE CABINET STAYS SHUT.]
