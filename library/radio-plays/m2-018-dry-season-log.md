# Dry Season Log

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: First-person survival diaries and wilderness broadcasts*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Field recorder Ludo, a macaque surveyor; Amba, a galago scout; Kiri, a baboon cartographer heard over radio; the desert fox, a wary neighbor.

[SFX: DRY WIND, A METAL CUP TAPS AGAINST A PACK.] 

LUDO, RECORDING: Day one. The spring at Red Shelf has stopped. I have two canteens, a compass, and a map that says the next water is a half-day walk east. The map was drawn before the new dune crossed the valley. I will follow the ridge and keep the radio dry.

[CLICK. WIND RISES.] 

LUDO: Day two. The ridge is not a ridge. It is a long sand lip that slides when I step on it. I came down too far and can no longer see the survey marker. Amba is ahead with the radio. She can move across the sand without sinking because she is light and keeps her feet quick. I am a macaque carrying the equipment, and the equipment is winning.

AMBA: Your pack is pulling you backward. Take the metal cup off the outside strap. It is catching wind.

LUDO: I need it for water.

AMBA: You need it inside the pack until water exists.

[SFX: BUCKLE OPENS. CUP SLIDES INTO BAG.] 

LUDO: That helped. I will admit it later.

[CLICK. NIGHT INSECTS BEGIN.] 

LUDO: Day three, night. We found tracks circling our camp. Fox. Amba says we should leave a strip of dates beside the flat rock, not inside the tent. The fox will eat the dates and leave the water pouch alone. I asked if the dates are our food. She says yes, but tomorrow we can share a little and keep the rest.

AMBA: You can record that the fox took exactly six.

LUDO: I am recording that the fox took exactly six. It did not take the pouch. The method works, which means we still need a method for ourselves.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC.] 

KIRI: Ludo, this is Kiri at the lower camp. The water map is wrong. The east spring dried last month. There may be seepage at the white stone basin, two ridges north.

LUDO: How do you know?

KIRI: The goats have stopped crossing the eastern path. They drink at the basin at dusk. I saw damp hoof marks.

AMBA: We should travel at dawn. Shade will last longer on the north side.

LUDO: I had planned to follow the compass east.

AMBA: The compass can tell you north. It cannot tell you where the water is.

[CLICK. MORNING BIRDS, VERY DISTANT.] 

LUDO: Day four. We reached the basin. It is mostly dry, but there is a damp line beneath the white stone. Amba found a crack where roots have pushed into the rock. We collected enough seep water to refill one cup after filtering it. Not enough for camp. Enough to keep walking safely to the shaded ravine.

AMBA: There are prints. Small antelope. They come here before sunrise.

LUDO: We will not block the basin. We marked the crack with stones and moved on.

[SFX: FILTER DRIPS INTO METAL CUP.] 

LUDO: Day five. We reached the lower camp before noon. Kiri has a new map with the dry spring crossed out and the basin marked as a limited source. The survey team will ask local herders before setting another route. I have returned the fox's dates to our food bag. There are three left.

KIRI: The map has a space for route notes now.

LUDO: How big is the space?

KIRI: One line.

AMBA: Write "the water is where the goats go."

LUDO: The goats go many places.

AMBA: Then write the time too.

KIRI: There is no second line.

LUDO: Fold the map.

KIRI: It is laminated.

LUDO: Then write on the back.

KIRI: The back has a compass rose.

AMBA: Put the route in the margin.

LUDO: The margin is for the scale.

KIRI: I will add a field card.

LUDO: Make it waterproof.

KIRI: The card is made from a cereal box.

AMBA: Then keep it away from the water.

AMBA: Two. You ate one while walking.

LUDO: The recording did not mention that.

AMBA: It does now.

LUDO: This log is complete. We found water, changed the route, and left the basin available for the animals already using it. Tomorrow we will repair the map instead of arguing with the compass.

[SFX: CUP SET ON TABLE. RADIO CLICKS OFF. WIND FADES.]
