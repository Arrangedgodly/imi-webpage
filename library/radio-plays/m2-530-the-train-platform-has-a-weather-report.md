# The Train Platform Has a Weather Report

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: commuter radio report becomes a dispute over an umbrella that is already dry*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; reporter Nari, a lemur; station keeper Odo, a gibbon; traveler Vex, an orangutan; porter Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Station platform. A distant train chime; light rain ticks on the canopy.

NARI: We are on Platform Two, where commuters are deciding whether to open their umbrellas.

VEX: Mine is wet already. I opened it in the station hall.

ODO: The hall is dry. You carried it open through the doors and brought the rain inside on the canopy.

MARA: Please close it and use the drip stand beside the bench.

VEX: If I close it, the broadcast will lose its weather image.

PIP: The weather image can be the rain outside the platform edge. No one needs to hold water over the timetable.

SFX: Umbrella folds; water drips into a tray.

NARI: The rain is light, wind from the east. The train is listed at 11:20.

ODO: Track notice: it is due in three minutes. Please wait behind the yellow line.

VEX: The rain is increasing dramatically.

MARA: It is tapping the canopy at the same rate.

VEX: I can stand nearer the edge for a better sound.

PIP: Stay behind the line. The train hasn't arrived, and the canopy already gives a clear recording.

SFX: A station bell rings. Footsteps move back from the edge.

VEX: I have returned to the safe area. My umbrella is in the drip stand.

NARI: A gust is pushing rain beneath the canopy at the north end. The south bench remains dry.

ODO: That bench is near the station door. Travelers can wait there until boarding.

MARA: We should move the microphone away from the timetable. The droplets are striking the printed arrival time.

SFX: Microphone stand shifts; paper rustles.

PIP: The schedule is still readable. I'll clip a clear cover over it after this report.

VEX: The rain has challenged our information system.

ODO: The timetable is under a roof. It has not been challenged.

SFX: Train approaches, brakes hiss.

NARI: Train arriving on Platform Two. Please stand behind the yellow line until it stops.

MARA: Vex, keep the umbrella closed until you're aboard. The platform is covered.

VEX: What if the rain follows me onto the train?

PIP: Then you close the umbrella inside and use the rack by the door.

SFX: Train doors open. Passengers board in an orderly line.

ODO: The train is stopped. The yellow line is clear.

NARI: Our weather report concludes: light rain, steady canopy, one umbrella properly drained.

VEX: Can I take the umbrella as my broadcast prop?

MARA: Take it closed, and don't open it in the carriage.

VEX: What if there is a cloudburst?

ODO: Then the train will still have a roof.


NARI: Before the next train, the keeper will check the rain gauge at the end of the platform. It is mounted beyond the yellow line, so passengers should not approach it.

ODO: I'll read it from the service path after boarding is complete. The canopy runoff goes into a drain, not onto the track.

PIP: I clipped the schedule under its clear cover. The time is visible, and the paper is dry.

VEX: Does the rain gauge show a dramatic increase?

ODO: It shows two millimeters since the last reading. That's light rain.

MARA: Keep the report in context. No flooding, no delay, and the platform remains covered.

SFX: Another train chime in the distance.

NARI: The next service is on time. Vex's umbrella is drained and stored in the carriage rack.

VEX: I have reached a dry place.

PIP: And the weather is still outside.

MARA: That's a useful forecast.
