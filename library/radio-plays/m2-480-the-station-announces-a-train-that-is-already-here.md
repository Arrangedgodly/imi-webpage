# The Station Announces a Train That Is Already Here

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: transit announcements that trust the schedule more than the platform*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Announcer Nari, a lemur; station master Odo, a gibbon; porter Mara, a macaque; traveler Pip, a capuchin; inspector Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Small station platform. Clock ticks. A train bell rings outside.

NARI: The 10:15 train is expected at 10:20.

PIP: It is standing beside the platform.

ODO: The schedule says expected at 10:20.

MARA: The clock says 10:18. The driver waved at me.

VEX: Do not announce arrival until the board updates.

NARI: I can hear the engine.

VEX: The board has authority.

SFX: Board letters click: EXPECTED 10:20.

PIP: My ticket says 10:15.

ODO: Your ticket is not the schedule board.

MARA: The train doors are open.

VEX: The platform is not officially ready.

MARA: I put the ramp down at 10:16.

VEX: The ramp is ready, but the board is not.

PIP: I need to board before the train leaves.

NARI: We will issue an update as soon as the arrival is confirmed.

SFX: Train idles. A door chime sounds.

ODO: The driver says they have been here for three minutes.

VEX: Drivers are not trained in board updates.

MARA: I am the porter. I can see the doors, the ramp, and the passengers waiting.

VEX: The board is the official source.

PIP: Can I board the official source?

NARI: We should perform a platform check. Mara, is the path clear?

MARA: Clear. The ramp is stable, the door is open, and the marked space is free.

ODO: I have the passenger list. Pip is on it.

VEX: Is the list current?

PIP: I am holding the ticket.

VEX: We need to verify that the ticket belongs to the traveler.

MARA: It has his name.

VEX: He could be carrying someone else's ticket.

PIP: It says Pip, capuchin, one passenger, one small bag.

SFX: Bag rustles.

ODO: Confirmed.

NARI: May I announce boarding?

VEX: Wait for the board.

ODO: We should check why the board lagged.

MARA: The clock mechanism is behind the announcement switch.

SFX: A cabinet opens. A small gear ticks slowly.

ODO: The board updates only when this gear completes a turn.

VEX: Can we make it turn faster?

NARI: Not while the train is here. Someone might read the board and think a second train has arrived.

PIP: The waiting area sign also points toward the closed east stairs.

MARA: We need to cover that arrow and put a temporary card toward the west ramp.

VEX: Two corrections in one broadcast will sound like trouble.

NARI: It is trouble. A passenger could take the wrong route.

SFX: Paper tears. Mara tapes a temporary arrow to the wall.

MARA: West ramp, platform one. Pip, can you see it from your seat?

PIP: Yes. It points where the ramp actually is.

ODO: I'll repair the board gear after the next departure, when the platform is clear.

SFX: Board clicks. EXPECTED 10:20 changes to ARRIVED 10:15.

VEX: The board says arrived.

MARA: The train has been here the whole time.

PIP: I can still board, then?

ODO: Yes. The ramp is down, and the door is open.

SFX: Pip boards. Bag set down. Door chime.

PIP: Thank you. I made it on board.

NARI: The 10:15 service arrived at 10:15, despite the board's 10:20 estimate. Boarding is in progress.

VEX: That announcement contradicts the schedule.

NARI: It describes the platform.

MARA: The train is departing.

SFX: Door closes. Bell rings; engine pulls away.

VEX: The board still needs a note explaining the early arrival.

ODO: We can write, "The train may be physically present before its scheduled update."

MARA: Make the letters large enough for the platform.
