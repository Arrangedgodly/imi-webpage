# The Court of the Cracked Cup

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: courtroom radio dramas, where neighbors argue over who cracked a cup during a shared tea service*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Judge Pava, gibbon; clerk Seli, capuchin; claimant Odo, baboon; respondent Nara, macaque; witness Tavi, lemur.

[SFX: COURTROOM MURMUR, GAVEL.]

PAVA: Court is now in session. We are hearing the matter of one cracked cup.

ODO: The cup was intact when I set it down.

NARA: It was already cracked.

SELI: The cup is in evidence.

TAVI: It is on the table.

PAVA: We will refer to the table as evidence if it becomes relevant.

Seli reads the claim. Odo says Nara borrowed the cup for tea. Nara says she returned it with a hairline crack already in the base. Odo says the crack appeared after she set it down.

PAVA: Did you hear the crack?

ODO: I heard a tap.

NARA: That was the saucer.

TAVI: I heard a spoon.

PAVA: Three sounds, one cup. The court will hear the cup.

Seli lifts the cup. A tiny chip falls from the rim.

[SFX: SMALL CERAMIC CHIP.]

PAVA: The evidence has changed.

NARA: The chip was loose.

ODO: It was not loose when I bought the cup.

PAVA: When did you buy it?

ODO: Last market day.

NARA: That was last week.

ODO: The cup is new.

TAVI: It was on the shared shelf.

Pava asks who owns the shared shelf. Seli says the tea club. Odo argues that the cup was his because he brought it. Nara says the label under the cup says "club."

ODO: The label is on the bottom.

PAVA: The court has found a label.

Seli turns the cup over. It reads "CLUB SET - RETURN AFTER USE." Odo says he did not see the label.

NARA: It was under the cup.

PAVA: A difficult place to read.

Tavi is called as a witness. He says he heard a tap, then a scrape, then a second tap. Odo says that was the cup hitting the saucer. Nara says the saucer was already on the table. Seli demonstrates with a similar cup.

[SFX: CUP TAPS SAUCER, SCRAPE, TAP.]

PAVA: The sound is clear.

ODO: It is not the same cup.

PAVA: This court does not require identical acoustics.

Nara says she put the cup down gently. Odo says he heard it land hard. Pava asks them to demonstrate their versions. Odo sets it down; the cup rings. Nara sets it down; the saucer slides.

SELI: The table is uneven.

PAVA: Is the table now part of the case?

Seli checks; one leg is shorter. She slides a folded napkin under it. The table steadies.

Pava delays the ruling and asks Seli to bring out the second cup from the set. It has a similar ring but no crack. Odo insists the sound proves his cup was damaged during the tea service.

PAVA: Did you hear the crack occur?

ODO: I heard a tap.

NARA: The cup taps every time it touches the saucer.

Tavi places a cloth between the cup and saucer. The tapping stops.

[SFX: CUP ON CLOTH, NO RING.]

PAVA: The sound cannot tell us when the crack happened.

SELI: We have a quiet cup.

PAVA: We have a quieter table.

PAVA: The evidence cannot show when the crack appeared. The cup belongs to the tea club, which will replace it. Neither neighbor owes payment.

ODO: I will read every label.

NARA: I can inspect the saucers.

Seli sets the cup in its evidence tray on the now-level table. It makes a small ring.

[SFX: CUP SETTLES.]

PAVA: Court is adjourned.

ODO: The table was the loudest witness.

NARA: It has been fixed.

TAVI: The next case can be about the napkin under its short leg.

[SFX: GAVEL, END THEME.]
