# The Town Crier's Microphone Test

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public announcement broadcasts, antique ceremonial calls, and performers whose projection overloads the new microphone*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST KITO, a capuchin
TOWN CRIER VELA, a gibbon
SOUND TECH NOLL, a macaque
COUNCIL CLERK PAVA, a lemur

[SFX: STUDIO DOOR, MICROPHONE FEEDBACK.]

KITO: The town crier is testing a microphone before the market opens. Vela has announced the market by voice for twenty years.

VELA: The bell rings, I call the hour, the market opens.

NOLL: The new microphone is supposed to carry your voice across the square.

PAVA: It also has a volume control.

VELA: I speak to the whole town.

[SFX: FEEDBACK SQUEAL.]

KITO: The microphone has spoken first.

NOLL: Step back from the speaker.

VELA: I am standing at the usual spot.

NOLL: The usual spot is directly in front of it.

PAVA: The council wants the test recorded for the archives.

VELA: Then record my voice, not the speaker.

Noll moves the microphone to the bell post. Vela calls the market hour. Her voice echoes off the stone buildings and returns delayed.

[SFX: CALL, ECHO.]

VELA: The market is open.

ECHO: Open.

VELA: Who repeated me?

KITO: The square.

NOLL: The microphone picked up the reflection.

PAVA: We have recorded the town answering.

NOLL: We have recorded an echo.

Vela asks for another test without the bell. She calls softly, then loudly. The soft call is clear; the loud call distorts.

[SFX: SOFT CALL, LOUD DISTORTION.]

NOLL: The microphone is clipping.

VELA: I am not clipping.

KITO: The sound is.

PAVA: Can the town hear the hour at the far corner?

Noll carries the speaker to the fountain. Vela calls from the market gate. The sound reaches the fountain, but a vendor's grinder covers the final word.

[SFX: GRINDER WHIRS.]

VELA: The market is - 

KITO: We lost the hour.

PAVA: The grinder is part of the market.

NOLL: Then the speaker needs to point away from the stall.

Vela rotates the speaker. It now points toward the library. Pava asks them not to direct market announcements through the reading room windows.

VELA: The town has many directions.

KITO: And the microphone has one.

Noll marks a spot on the post, lowers the volume, and angles the speaker toward the square. Vela rings the bell once, then speaks at her normal volume.

[SFX: BELL, CLEAR VOICE.]

VELA: The market opens at sunrise. Please keep the east lane clear for carts.

PAVA: Clear at the fountain. Clear at the gate.

KITO: The announcement can be heard without shaking the windows.

Vela asks to record one final version.
 
Noll tests the microphone at the bell post once more, this time with the speaker aimed across the market square. The sound reaches the far cart lane, but the bell rattles the stand.

[SFX: BELL, STAND RATTLES.]

NOLL: The post is loose.

VELA: The bell is fixed to the post.

PAVA: We can secure the base without muffling the bell.

KITO: The town needs to hear the hour, not the post shaking.

Noll wedges the stand and asks Vela to repeat the call from the marked spot. The echo is gone, the vendor's grinder is behind the speaker, and the final word remains clear.

[SFX: BELL, CLEAR VOICE, NO ECHO.]

PAVA: The market heard the hour.

VELA: And the archive heard me.

NOLL: We have not recorded yet.

PAVA: Please press the button before she announces the whole town again.
 Noll presses the button. She speaks; the recording sounds just like her, but without the stone echo.

VELA: The microphone carries the voice. It does not replace the crier.

PAVA: The archive will label both the call and the bell.

KITO: The test is complete.

NOLL: We should also label the volume knob.

[SFX: KNOB CLICKS DOWN.]

VELA: The market is open.

[SFX: CARTS ROLL, FEEDBACK DOES NOT RETURN.]
