# The Orchestra Pit's Unseen Door

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: theatre radio broadcasts that treat every door sound as a stage entrance even when the orchestra pit door is opening*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Conductor Vela, a gibbon; stage manager Noku, a macaque; announcer Pesa, a capuchin; musician Rumi, a lemur.

[SFX: THEATRE. ORCHESTRA TUNES. A DOOR CREAKS.]

PESA: The leading singer has entered from the left wing.

RUMI: No. That was the pit door.

VELA: We have not begun the overture.

NOKU: The stage entrance is on the opposite side. The pit door is behind the cellos.

PESA: The radio mix has the door creak under the entrance cue.

RUMI: The singer is still in the corridor.

[SFX: DOOR CREAKS AGAIN. MUSICIAN CHUCKLES.]

VELA: The door latch catches. Someone is carrying the timpani mallet case through.

NOKU: I can hold the door until the case is clear.

PESA: The announcer card says "first entrance."

VELA: Change the card to "stage is set." We have not started the opera.

[SFX: MALLET CASE SCRAPES THROUGH DOOR. LATCH CLICKS SHUT.]

NOKU: The door is closed. The singer is at the stage entrance now.

[SFX: TWO KNOCKS ON A DIFFERENT DOOR.]

PESA: That is the stage door.

NOKU: Wait for my cue. The orchestra is still tuning.

RUMI: The radio audience can hear each section tuning.

VELA: We do not need to call it the overture.

RUMI: The singer is on the other side of the pit door. I can hear the costume beading brush the wall.

NOKU: I will escort her through the stage entrance after the orchestra settles. The pit door stays for equipment only.

VELA: Cellos, tune your A. Winds, hold. We are nearly ready.

[SFX: CELLISTS TUNE. A SINGLE A NOTE SOUNDS.]

PESA: The note tells us the orchestra has finished tuning.

RUMI: The pit door is quiet now. The mallet case is in the storage bay.

[SFX: STAGE DOOR KNOCKS TWICE.]

NOKU: I am opening the stage entrance.

[SFX: STAGE DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS CROSS THE FLOOR.]

PESA: That is the singer's entrance, but the scene has not started yet.

VELA: She is taking her place. The overture comes first.

PESA: I'll wait until the conductor's cue.

RUMI: The singer is on the other side of the pit door. I can hear the costume beading brush the wall.

NOKU: I will escort her through the stage entrance after the orchestra settles. The pit door stays for equipment only.

VELA: Cellos, tune your A. Winds, hold. We are nearly ready.

[SFX: CELLISTS TUNE. A SINGLE A NOTE SOUNDS.]

PESA: The note tells us the orchestra has finished tuning.

RUMI: The pit door is quiet now. The mallet case is in the storage bay.

[SFX: STAGE DOOR KNOCKS TWICE.]

NOKU: I am opening the stage entrance.

[SFX: STAGE DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS CROSS THE FLOOR.]

PESA: That is the singer's entrance, but the scene has not started yet.

VELA: She is taking her place. The overture comes first.

PESA: I'll wait until the conductor's cue.

PESA: The producer wants a dramatic build. Can I announce the singer over the tuning?

VELA: No. The audience has not been seated and the orchestra is not ready.

[SFX: CHAIRS SETTLE. A USHER CLOSES A DOOR FAR AWAY.]

NOKU: House is ready. Stage door is open.

[SFX: STAGE DOOR OPENS. FOOTSTEPS CROSS A WOODEN FLOOR.]

PESA: The singer is entering from the left wing.

RUMI: That is the stage entrance. The pit door stays shut.

[SFX: ONE TUNE NOTE FROM OBOE. A CONDUCTOR'S BATON TAPS MUSIC STAND.]

VELA: We can begin now. Announcer, say the opera title once.

PESA: The evening performance begins with "The River Gate."

VELA: Orchestra, tuning complete. Ready.

[SFX: ORCHESTRA PLAYS A BRIEF OVERTURE. MUSIC SWELLS.]

NOKU: The pit door is locked. The stage entrance is clear.

RUMI: The singer has crossed to the center mark.

PESA: That was the entrance.

VELA: The first note will tell you the music has started. A door creak is only a door.

[SFX: OVERTURE ENDS. APPLAUSE.]

PESA: The singer enters in the first scene, not the overture.

NOKU: The mallet case is stored, the pit door is latched, and the stage door is closed.

RUMI: We can hear the orchestra now.

[SFX: MUSIC STAND TAPS. MIC CLOSES; QUIET TUNING RETURNS.]
