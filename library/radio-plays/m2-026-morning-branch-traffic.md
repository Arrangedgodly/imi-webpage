# Morning Branch Traffic

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: Breakfast radio traffic reports and cheerful commuter banter*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO*

Cast: Host Marn, a macaque; traffic reporter Tobi, a gibbon; app developer Ivo, a squirrel monkey; caller Peb, a capuchin fruit seller.

[JINGLE: FAST UKULELE, A HORN, A MONKEY WHOOP.] 

HOST MARN: Good morning, it's six thirty on Morning Branch Traffic. I'm Marn, and the east canopy route is moving at the speed of a sleepy sloth. Tobi is above the junction. Tobi, what can you see?

TOBI: The main swingway is blocked by a fallen fig branch. Traffic is backing up at the three-rope merge. A family of fruit bats is using the lower route as a nursery, so please take the boardwalk.

MARN: How long is the delay?

TOBI: If you are a macaque, five minutes. If you are a tortoise, you already left yesterday.

MARN: We have a caller at the fig market. Peb, how does it look from there?

PEB: People are piling up by my stall. The map says the bridge is open, but the bridge is carrying the fallen branch.

MARN: Can vehicles cross?

PEB: One can. A second tried, and now the bridge is making a sound like a wet drum.

TOBI: That means stay off it. The repair crew is on the way.

MARN: Our station sponsor is the new Canopy Commuter App. It offers a route that knows your species, your tail length, and whether you prefer a branch with moss.

TOBI: The app just sent a langur onto the closed bridge.

MARN: It's in beta.

TOBI: It's in a tree.

[SFX: PHONE ALERT, THEN A BRANCH GROANS.] 

MARN: Use the app's "I can see the road" button to report the closure. Or call us. We have a human-sized microphone and a monkey-sized map.

CALLER DELL: I'm listening from the lower path. A troop of young macaques has started a shortcut through the fig market. They're swinging over the stalls.

PEB: They're using my awning as a landing pad.

MARN: Is everyone safe?

PEB: The figs are safe. My hats are not.

TOBI: The lower boardwalk is clear. I can guide them around the market if somebody ties a rope between the two posts.

PEB: I have a display rope. Take it. It has a "touch the fruit" sign on it, so they may stop and look.

MARN: A traffic barrier and an advertisement in one.

[SFX: ROPE STRETCHES. FOOTSTEPS CHANGE DIRECTION.] 

TOBI: The young troop is on the lower boardwalk. They are taking turns instead of racing. One of them has picked up a fallen hat and returned it to Peb.

PEB: That's mine. I thought the hat was lost in traffic.

MARN: We have an update. The repair crew has sawed the fig branch into smaller pieces and cleared the swingway. The bridge is closed until the support rope is checked. The app has changed its route and now recommends "go around."

TOBI: That is sound navigation.

MARN: The five-minute delay has become twelve. The breakfast cart has brought tea to the waiting commuters. Nobody is moving fast, but everyone has a cup.

PEB: I sold all the figs from the awning. The hat is back. The app is still trying to route me through my own stall.

MARN: We'll send that bug to the developers, by hand.

[JINGLE RETURNS, SLOWER.] 

MARN: Morning Branch Traffic will be back after the news. The east swingway is open. The lower bridge is closed. The market route has figs, tea, and one repaired hat. Please leave the fruit stall as you found it, unless you bought something.

TOBI: The app says arrive safely.

MARN: The app has learned one thing.

MARN: We have the app developer on the line. Can you explain why it routed Peb to Peb's own stall?

DEVELOPER: It matched the word "figs" in his route to the nearest result. The nearest result was Peb.

PEB: I am not a road.

DEVELOPER: We can add a setting for "people who are not roads."

MARN: Add "fruit sellers" too. They should not be sent through their own awnings.

TOBI: The three-rope merge is clear. The commuters have started using the lower boardwalk in pairs, and nobody is trying to pass a gibbon on a narrow branch.

PEB: The app has listed my stall as a destination.

MARN: You are a destination now.

PEB: At least the fruit is fresh.

[HORN, UKULELE OUT.]
