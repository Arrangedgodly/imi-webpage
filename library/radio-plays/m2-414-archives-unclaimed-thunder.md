# The Archive's Unclaimed Thunder

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio archive programs that treat mislabeled effects as rare historical recordings and ignore basic source checks*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Archivist Yara, a macaque; presenter Felo, a langur; technician Miko, a capuchin; donor voice Hedd, a gibbon.

[SFX: OLD TAPE SPINS; A LARGE BOOM RUMBLES.] 

FELO: Tonight, the archive presents a rare storm recorded during the first lantern festival.

YARA: The box says "thunder, unknown source." The tape label says "kitchen, Thursday."

MIKO: The reel was filed with weather because the thunder has a dramatic tail.

FELO: It sounds historical.

YARA: It sounds like a cupboard falling over. We should check the catalog before announcing provenance.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS; A PRINTER STARTS.] 

MIKO: The tape log says recorded at 3:14 p.m. in the sound kitchen.

FELO: The festival began after sunset.

YARA: That does not rule it out. Read the rest of the log.

MIKO: "Cupboard door struck by coconut crate. Repeated for clean take."

FELO: A coconut crate made that thunder?

YARA: The crate was wooden. The cupboard was empty. That can make a low boom.

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS; PLAYBACK: A WOODEN CLACK, THEN A BOOM.] 

FELO: I introduced it as a historic storm.

HEDD: I donated the reel. I was told it came from the old festival stage.

YARA: The stage has a cupboard too. Do you remember recording it?

HEDD: I remember the crate falling, and the sound artist asking for one more take.

MIKO: The second boom has a chair squeak under it.

[SFX: PLAYBACK AGAIN; BOOM, CHAIR SQUEAK.] 

FELO: Perhaps the chair was part of the festival.

YARA: Or the sound kitchen. The log is specific. We should describe it accurately.

MIKO: The catalog also has a waveform drawing. It matches the crate impact, not a rolling storm.

FELO: The sponsor paid to feature the archive's oldest weather sound.

YARA: We can feature the oldest verified sound. This one was made last month.

HEDD: I wrote "thunder" because the crate made me jump. I did not know it would become a storm.

MIKO: The catalog copied the box label and dropped the kitchen log.

YARA: Then we should restore the source note in both places.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS; PAPER LABEL PEELS.]

FELO: The sponsor card can say "a cupboard boom from the foley kitchen."

HEDD: That is exactly what happened, and the crate is still sturdy.

FELO: The program card already says "storm of the century."

MIKO: We can change the card before the next playback.

[SFX: PAPER CARD PEELS OFF GLASS; NEW CARD SLIDES INTO PLACE.] 

FELO: What should I say to listeners who tuned in for the storm?

YARA: Tell them it is a foley effect: a cupboard door and a coconut crate, recorded in the kitchen.

HEDD: I brought the crate in from the orchard. It had three coconuts in it.

MIKO: The tape contains no coconuts falling. Just the empty crate hitting the cupboard.

FELO: That is less grand than the festival storm, but it has a maker and a date.

YARA: And the source is part of the recording's history.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.] 

FELO: Correction to our introduction. This sound was made in the archive kitchen, not at the lantern festival. It records a cupboard and a crate.

HEDD: I am glad the crate is credited.

MIKO: There are two more reels in the box labeled "weather." One says "sneeze in stairwell."

FELO: We are not calling that a gale.

YARA: We will inspect the logs first.

[SFX: TAPE STOPS; LABEL PENCIL SCRATCHES.] 

FELO: The archive has preserved a sound effect, a correction, and a very specific cupboard.

HEDD: I can identify the squeaky hinge too. It still needs oil.

MIKO: The hinge is not on the tape.

YARA: But it is in the room. Let's oil it before someone records it as thunder.

[SFX: HINGE SQUEAKS; OIL CAN SQUIRTS; DOOR CLOSES QUIETLY.] 

FELO: The next archive program will begin with a verified label.

[SFX: ONE CLEAN TAPE CLICK; MIC CLOSES.]
