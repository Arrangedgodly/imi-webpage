# The Night Before the Festival

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live festival preview broadcast convention, where hosts try to announce every detail before the event has been assembled*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host TAVI, macaque; organizer LUMA, gibbon; sound operator ODO, baboon; caller PIRI, capuchin.

[SFX: FESTIVAL THEME, HAMMERING IN DISTANCE.] 

TAVI: Welcome to our preview of tomorrow's festival. The lanterns are glowing, the stage is ready, and the gates are open.

LUMA: The lanterns are in a box. The stage is two planks. The gates are still on the cart.

ODO: I can add those sounds.

[SFX: HAMMER, LANTERN BOX RATTLE.] 

TAVI: Our listeners can imagine the festival.

LUMA: They need to know where to come.

TAVI: The entrance is beside the pear tree.

LUMA: There are three pear trees.

PIRI: Caller Piri here. Which one?

LUMA: The one with the blue ribbon.

ODO: I took the ribbon out of the lantern box.

TAVI: We can announce that the ribbon will be installed shortly.

LUMA: The ribbon is currently holding the microphone cable together.

The cable slips. The microphone falls against a board.

[SFX: THUMP, CABLE RATTLE.] 

TAVI: A preview of tomorrow's drum performance.

ODO: It is not the drum.

LUMA: We need to put the lanterns up before the sun goes down.

TAVI: We are on the air. The audience expects a preview.

LUMA: The audience expects a festival.

Piri asks whether there will be a food stall. Luma says the oven is ready, but the table is still being carried from the storage shed. Odo records footsteps as "festival traffic."

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS APPROACH.] 

TAVI: A lively crowd is arriving.

LUMA: That is Odo moving the table.

TAVI: The festival is beginning early.

The table catches on the gate. Luma asks Tavi to stop talking and hold the other side. He keeps announcing while lifting.

TAVI: The grand table enters the square-

[SFX: WOOD SCRAPE, TABLE SET DOWN.]

LUMA: We need two more hands.

PIRI: I can come now.

TAVI: The festival begins tomorrow.

LUMA: The setup begins now.

Piri arrives and frees the ribbon from the microphone cable. Odo clips the cable securely to the stand. They try the ribbon across the entrance, find it too short, then tie it to the cart handle. The cart blocks the gate.

ODO: The entrance is now in the cart.

TAVI: A mobile entrance. Very modern.

LUMA: Move the cart.

They roll it aside. The ribbon stretches across the path and catches a lantern box. Luma holds the box steady while Odo releases the ribbon from its corner. The lanterns remain unlit, but the route is clear.

PIRI: Which tree is the entrance?

LUMA: Follow the ribbon to the open path, then look for the lanterns.

TAVI: They are still in the box.

LUMA: We will hang them before morning.

Tavi tries to announce the program. The list is damp, and one line is unreadable.

TAVI: At sunrise, music, food, dancing, and-

LUMA: The lantern lighting is at dusk.

TAVI: There are two sunrises?

LUMA: One sunrise. The lanterns are at dusk.

Odo clicks on a work light. The square brightens; the lantern box casts a huge shadow.

[SFX: SWITCH, LOW HUM.] 

TAVI: The lanterns are glowing.

LUMA: That is the work light.

PIRI: It helps us see the lanterns.

TAVI: We can say the festival preview has begun.

LUMA: Say the setup is underway and invite people to help.

Tavi pauses, then reads the true invitation. Piri and Odo begin carrying lanterns to the trees as the broadcast continues. The sound of work replaces the theme.

TAVI: Tomorrow, the festival opens at the pear tree with the blue ribbon.

LUMA: Which is still tied to the cart.

TAVI: Tomorrow, we will tie it to the tree.

[SFX: HAMMERING, LANTERN GLASS TINKLES.]

PIRI: I am moving the blue ribbon to the tree now.

[SFX: RIBBON UNTIES, FOOTSTEPS, A KNOT TIGHTENS.]

ODO: The cable stays on its stand. The entrance finally stays in one place.

TAVI: A historic moment in stationary entrances.

LUMA: Please hold this lantern.
