# The Mangrove Count's Click Track

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: field radio counts that mistake a recording's repeated clicks for separate animals*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Naturalist Reni, a lemur; host Talo, a macaque; technician Vemi, a capuchin; caller Oka, a gibbon.

[SFX: MANGROVE WATER. MUD BUBBLES. A CLICKING SOUND REPEATS.]

TALO: We are counting mud crabs by the clicks they make on the roots.

RENI: We have counted forty in five minutes.

VEMI: The recorder has counted one hundred and twenty.

OKA: I hear the same click every few seconds.

TALO: The sponsor wants the count to pass one hundred before the tide turns.

RENI: The recorder is looping the same ten seconds. We are counting the clicks again each time it repeats.

[SFX: CLICKS PLAY. A SHORT DIGITAL CHIME SOUNDS. THE SAME CLICKS PLAY AGAIN.]

VEMI: The loop marker is set to replay the sample for the broadcast.

TALO: It sounds like the mangroves are full of crabs.

RENI: They are. But this audio doesn't tell us how many.

OKA: Can you make a new recording without the loop?

VEMI: I can stop playback and record the next minute live.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICKS. LOOP STOPS. RECORDING STARTS.]

RENI: We will count one crab at a time if we see one cross the marked root.

TALO: The count will be slower.

RENI: It will be a count, not an effect.

[SFX: WATER DRIPS. MUD POPS. A SMALL CRAB CLICKS ONCE.]

OKA: I heard one click.

RENI: I see one crab at the red root. That is one.

[SFX: LEAVES RUSTLE. ONE CRAB CLICKS ONCE; ANOTHER CLICKS TWICE AS BOTH CROSS.]

RENI: Two more crossings, two crabs. Total three.

VEMI: The broadcast console has a separate replay cache. I will clear it so the old loop cannot resume.

[SFX: TWO BUTTONS CLICK. A CONFIRMATION TONE.]

TALO: The sponsor operator is asking us to restore the saved number.

RENI: Read the field card. Three animals observed, four clicks. The larger looped number was not a count.

OKA: A listener asks whether the clicks all came from the same crab.

RENI: We cannot tell from the first recording. On the live one, I saw three animals cross. The third crab clicked twice during its one crossing.

VEMI: I have split the audio at the reset. The looped section is marked playback, and the live section is marked observation.

TALO: That distinction belongs in the segment notes.

[SFX: FILES SAVE. A SMALL DIGITAL BEEP.]

OKA: The tide is reaching the lower roots.

RENI: Then we stop when this minute ends. We have a fixed time window, not an all-night census.

TALO: The sponsor's screen still says one hundred and twenty.

VEMI: I can clear the playback meter and display the live count.

[SFX: KEYS TAP. DISPLAY BEEPS.]

VEMI: Meter reset. It now reads three.

TALO: The announcer card has "one hundred" in the headline.

RENI: Change it to "Three crabs observed at one root in one minute." That is our method.

OKA: Another crab is carrying a leaf.

[SFX: LEAF DRAGS OVER ROOT. ONE CLICK.]

RENI: Four. It crossed toward the water, and the tide has not turned yet.

TALO: We can leave the microphone running and log the next crossing.

VEMI: The recorder is live. No repeat button is armed.

[SFX: A CRAB CLICKS TWICE. WATER SPLASHES.]

RENI: A fifth crab has crossed and clicked twice. Five animals, seven clicks.

OKA: Two clicks during its first crossing, and it never crossed the root again.

RENI: Then it stays one animal. The sound is two clicks, not two crabs.

TALO: I see the difference now.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES "5 ANIMALS, 7 CLICKS" ON A FIELD CARD.]

VEMI: The display can show both numbers.

TALO: Five crabs observed, seven clicks recorded. The loop is off.

RENI: That is our one-minute count. The sponsor can have one hundred if it sponsors twenty identical documentaries. No counting the replays.

[SFX: TIDE WATER RISES AGAINST ROOTS. RECORDER CLICKS OFF.]

TALO: We will not let the sound track count the animals for us.

OKA: The mangroves can keep clicking after we stop.

[SFX: RADIO CLOSES. MANGROVE CLICKS CONTINUE, UNMIXED.]
