# The Museum's Night Guard Tours the Empty Room

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: prestige museum audio tours that make absence sound exclusive*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Guard Mara, a macaque; curator Vex, an orangutan; visitor Pip, a capuchin; guide Odo, a gibbon who also recorded the tour; tape technician Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Museum gallery at night. Key turns. An empty pedestal stands in a quiet room.

VEX: Welcome to the private unveiling of our newest exhibit: the space where the sculpture used to be.

PIP: Where is the sculpture?

VEX: In conservation.

ODO: The audio tour says it is in this room.

SELI: The tour was recorded before the sculpture went for cleaning. The conservators called; it is coming back tonight, earlier than expected. The gallery opens to the public tomorrow.

MARA: We can update the recording.

VEX: A private tour must feel rare. Tell the visitor the absence is intentional.

PIP: Is the empty pedestal part of the collection?

VEX: It represents the idea of support.

MARA: It represents a place where dust collects.

SFX: Cloth brushes the pedestal. A small bell rings.

ODO: The bell is part of the security system.

PIP: I thought it was an exhibit.

VEX: That is good. Confusion creates engagement.

SELI: The current tour should tell visitors it is expected back tonight and opens to the public tomorrow.

VEX: Don't reveal the date. The mystery is valuable.

MARA: People came to see it. We should say where it is and when it returns.

PIP: I can wait until tomorrow.

VEX: The private tour ends in two minutes.

ODO: We can play the recording.

SFX: Button clicks. A recorded voice echoes in the room.

ODO (RECORDED GUIDE): Notice how the bronze figure draws your eye toward the empty plinth.

PIP: It draws my eye because there is nothing else here.

MARA: That's an honest observation.

VEX: It is also the most expensive ticket.

PIP: I want to know whether the pedestal is part of the tour price.

VEX: Every object in the room contributes to the experience.

MARA: Pip bought a ticket to see the sculpture. He should know it is away before he reaches the gallery.

SELI: I'll record the accurate entrance update and replace the old reel.

SFX: Tape deck opens. Microphone clicks on. Reel turns.

ODO (RECORDED GUIDE): The bronze figure is in conservation and is expected back tonight. Public viewing begins tomorrow.

SFX: Tape stops. Reel clicks free.

SELI: This is the new entrance reel. I'll install it beside the door player.

SFX: Reel seats in deck. Button clicks; the recorded voice plays back.

ODO (RECORDED GUIDE): The bronze figure is in conservation and is expected back tonight. Public viewing begins tomorrow.

ODO: That is plain language.

VEX: Can you add a pause after "tonight" for reflection?

PIP: A short pause. Not the whole evening.

MARA: Put the same update on the door, where visitors decide whether to enter.

SFX: Paper taped to door.

SFX: A cart rolls in the hallway. Wheels squeak.

PIP: If the recording changes, will the old tape be saved?

SELI: Yes. We can label it "outdated" and keep it in the archive.

VEX: That sounds less mysterious than "forbidden."

MARA: It sounds more useful.

ODO: I can set a reminder to replace the sign when the sculpture returns.

PIP: Then the next visitor won't have to guess whether the empty room is the exhibit.

SELI: The cart has arrived from conservation, earlier than expected. It returned tonight; public viewing still begins tomorrow.

VEX: Before it enters, let's conclude the tour.

PIP: I can hear the cart.

ODO: We should clear the doorway.

MARA: I'll move the rope.

SFX: Rope barrier lifts. Cart stops at the room entrance.

VEX: No, don't bring it in yet. The tour ends at the moment before the return.

MARA: It ends when the work is safely back on its pedestal.

SELI: The cart is balanced. We can roll it in.

SFX: Sculpture cart crosses the threshold. Wooden cradle lowers onto the pedestal.

PIP: Now I can see it.

SELI: I'll record the final room update with the work in place.

SFX: Tape deck opens. Microphone clicks on. Reel turns.

ODO (RECORDED GUIDE): The bronze figure has returned from conservation and is on its pedestal. Public viewing begins tomorrow.

SFX: Tape stops. Seli replaces the entrance reel with the room reel and presses play.

ODO (RECORDED GUIDE): The bronze figure has returned from conservation and is on its pedestal. Public viewing begins tomorrow.

VEX: The private unveiling is complete.

MARA: Tonight it is back in place. Tomorrow the public can see it.

PIP: Can the tour say that?

VEX: It now says exactly that.

TAG

SFX: Guard locks the gallery.

MARA: The pedestal has finally been promoted from exhibit to employee.
