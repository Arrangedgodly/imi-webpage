# The Night Shift at the Nest

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio workplace comedy about a late-night nest maintenance crew*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: SHIFT LEAD TOMA, a macaque; NEW HIRE JIN, a gibbon; and NIGHT WATCHER ELO, a slow loris.

[SFX: NIGHT INSECTS, SOFT WOVEN TWIGS, A CLOCK TICKS.] 

TOMA: Welcome to the night shift. Our job is to keep the nursery quiet, the nests stable, and the path clear until sunrise.

JIN: I brought a clipboard.

ELO: Clipboards are louder than they look.

[THE CLIPBOARD CLACKS AGAINST A POST.] 

JIN: I will hold it more carefully.

TOMA: The first inspection is the east nest. Check the rim for loose twigs, then listen for movement.

JIN: I hear a cricket.

ELO: That is outside the nest.

JIN: I hear a tiny squeak inside.

TOMA: That's a sleeping infant. We don't inspect further.

JIN: The checklist says verify occupancy.

ELO: The squeak verified it.

TOMA: Put a mark on the sheet and move on.

[THEY WALK. TWIGS SHIFT UNDERFOOT.] 

JIN: The path is blocked by a branch.

TOMA: That's not a branch. It is our ladder.

JIN: Why is the ladder on the path?

TOMA: We were repairing the roof and had to stop when it started raining.

ELO: It stopped raining at midnight.

TOMA: Then the ladder has been the path hazard for six hours.

JIN: Should we file a report?

TOMA: We should move the ladder.

[JIN LIFTS THE LADDER. A BUNDLE OF TWIGS SLIDES OFF.] 

ELO: Those are the repair supplies.

JIN: The supplies are now on the path.

TOMA: We will move them to the dry shelf.

JIN: Where is the dry shelf?

ELO: Under the east nest.

TOMA: We cannot put the supplies under the occupied nest.

JIN: Then the dry shelf is not available.

ELO: It is a shelf, not an employee.

JIN: We have another inspection note. The west nest has a loose straw sticking out.

TOMA: Is it near the edge?

JIN: It is inside the rim.

ELO: If it is holding the weave together, leave it.

JIN: The checklist says remove loose material.

TOMA: The checklist was written for loose things on the path, not parts of a nest.

JIN: It does not say that.

ELO: Then the checklist needs an edit in daylight.

JIN: How do we know whether the straw is loose?

TOMA: Touch it lightly. If the weave tightens around it, it belongs there.

[JIN TOUCHES THE STRAW. THE NEST CREAKS SOFTLY.] 

JIN: It moved the rim.

ELO: Then stop touching it.

TOMA: This is why we inspect by listening first.

JIN: I will write: "Do not pull a straw just because you can see it."

ELO: Add: "Do not write near the infant nest."

JIN: The clipboard is still loud.

TOMA: Put it on the floor and use the pencil. The floor is padded.

[THE CLIPBOARD SETS DOWN WITH A MUTED THUMP. A PENCIL SCRATCHES.] 

JIN: That is quiet enough.

TOMA: Now we can check the path without waking the nursery.

ELO: And when the path is clear, we go back to the nest and let it be.

[THEY CARRY TWIGS TO A COVERED ALCOVE. A NIGHT BIRD CALLS.] 

TOMA: Pause. Let the bird finish.

JIN: Is that on the checklist?

TOMA: No. The checklist is not awake.

ELO: The bird is warning us about the loose roof panel.

[SFX: A PANEL CREAKS ABOVE.] 

JIN: We can secure it with the twine from the repair bundle.

TOMA: That means climbing back for the twine.

JIN: The bundle is dry now.

ELO: And off the path.

TOMA: You solved the inspection and the obstruction.

JIN: Should I write that down?

TOMA: After sunrise. For now, tie the panel and leave the rest for daylight.

[THE TWINE TIGHTENS. THE ROOF STOPS CREAKING.] 

ELO: The infant is still asleep.

JIN: The checklist is complete.

TOMA: What about the final box?

JIN: "Were all tasks performed quietly?"

ELO: No.

TOMA: But we stopped making noise when we noticed it.

JIN: That sounds like a yes with details.

[SFX: A CRICKET CHIRPS, THE CLOCK TICKS TOWARD DAWN.] 

TOMA: Welcome to the night shift. The quietest work is knowing when to stop.
