# The Library Cart Calls a Play-by-Play

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports commentary that turns a routine book return into a championship*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Announcer Vex, an orangutan; librarian Mara, a macaque; page Pip, a capuchin; clerk Odo, a gibbon; patron Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Public library. Cart wheels roll slowly on carpet.

VEX: We are live at the return desk, where the red cart approaches the shelf.

MARA: It's a book cart. Pip is bringing returns to the sorting table.

PIP: The cart is half full and balanced. I am walking; please don't shout at the patrons.

ODO: The returns are due to be scanned before they go onto the shelving cart.

NARI: I need to return this atlas. It is heavy, so I'll set it on the counter.

VEX: A late contender enters with a massive volume.

MARA: Nari, place it on the counter. Pip can load it with the heavy books at the bottom.

SFX: Book set on counter.

PIP: Atlas is on the counter. I'll scan it, then lower it onto the cart shelf.

VEX: And the scan is good! The barcode has crossed the line.

ODO: The book is checked in. The system says it is reserved for another patron, so it goes on the hold shelf, not the cart.

VEX: An unexpected substitution.

MARA: It is a hold pickup. The next reader is waiting.

PIP: I'll take the atlas by its sides and place it on the hold shelf. The cart remains parked by the sorting table.

SFX: Footsteps to shelf; book placed.

NARI: Does the atlas need to go back on the cart?

MARA: No, it is already checked in and reserved. The hold shelf is the next stop.

VEX: The cart loses a star player.

ODO: The cart has other books to sort. Please do not lean on it.

Vex taps the cart handle like a commentator slapping a bench. Its wheels roll an inch; Pip catches it before it hits a chair.

PIP: Brake is on. It was parked on the slight slope.

NARI: A child is trying to return a picture book. Is the cart safe to approach?

MARA: They can bring the book to the counter, not place it on the moving cart.

PIP: The cart is locked on the level floor now. I'll take the book from the counter after I finish this stack.

VEX: A junior contender joins the match.

ODO: We don't announce a child's name or turn their library visit into a broadcast.

SFX: Picture book set on counter; one barcode beep.

PIP: Book scanned. It is due back in the children's room, so it goes on this side of the cart.

MARA: Keep the picture book on top of the cart so it is easy to sort. The atlas stays on the pickup shelf.

NARI: I can help direct the child to the return receipt, then leave the sorting to staff.

VEX: The cart has gained a new book.

PIP: The cart has gained one return, recorded under its proper category.



MARA: Move the cart to the level floor, then lock the brake. No one moves it while patrons are near.

SFX: Cart rolls three feet, brake clicks.

ODO: Stable. Pip can sort the remaining returns by call number.

VEX: The crowd is silent.

NARI: They are reading.

MARA: This is a library. We keep the play-by-play off the public speaker.

VEX: I'll whisper into the booth mic.

PIP: The mic is still connected to the library speaker.

SFX: Switch clicks off.

VEX: My broadcast has been benched.

MARA: Your broadcast is paused. The cart is parked and the reading room is quiet.

VEX: The last book crosses the finish line.

PIP: It is a field guide. I am setting it on the correct shelf, spine out.

SFX: Book slides into place.

NARI: The aisle is clear, and readers can reach the shelves again.

MARA: We keep the play-by-play on the radio mic, not the library speaker. The desk stays quiet.

VEX: One final call: the returns are in their proper places.

PIP: The cart is not competing. It carried the books.

MARA: The work is done.

TAG

ODO: Return list complete. Cart is parked and ready for the next shift.
