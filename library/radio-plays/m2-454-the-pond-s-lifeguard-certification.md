# The Pond's Lifeguard Certification

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: emergency-training broadcasts that mistake a dramatic demonstration for a safe procedure*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; trainer Vex, an orangutan; trainee Pip, a capuchin; park keeper Nari, a lemur; certification clerk Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Park pond. Ducks chatter. A metal ring clanks against a wooden post.

MARA: Welcome to Ready on the Water, the show that certifies lifeguards before lunch. Trainer Vex, what is our first test?

VEX: A lifeguard must enter the water with confidence.

NARI: The water is waist-deep at the edge and deep in the middle.

PIP: I don't swim.

VEX: Confidence can be learned.

PIP: My tail can be used for balance. Not as a rudder.

MARA: What else can a lifeguard do?

VEX: Call for help.

NARI: We are beside the park office.

ODO: I have the certification card. The first box says "rescue technique."

VEX: Demonstrate a heroic dive.

PIP: Into that pond?

VEX: For the radio audience, describe the dive.

PIP: I lean in, my foot slips, and I stay dry because I grab the rail.

SFX: A shoe squeaks. Tail brushes a rail. A sudden clank.

MARA: That is a safe demonstration.

VEX: It lacked drama.

NARI: A rescue ring is hanging beside you.

VEX: The ring is for emergencies, not an audition.

ODO: It says "throw, don't go" on the tag.

VEX: It also says "training device." We need to make a splash for the show.

MARA: There is a floating practice buoy.

SFX: Buoy knocks the dock. A rubber duck squeaks.

PIP: Is that the buoy?

NARI: That's my lunch duck. The buoy is farther out.

VEX: Can we use the duck?

NARI: No. It is a toy, and it is not a person.

PIP: The buoy has a rope attached. I can reach from shore with the pole.

VEX: The certification requires a rescue.

ODO: We can mark a rescue if the trainee brings the buoy to shore.

MARA: We do not need to turn it into a person.

VEX: I was going to wear the duck as a hat.

NARI: That is also not a rescue.

SFX: A gust pushes the buoy away from the edge.

PIP: It's moving toward the middle.

MARA: Pip, tell us the steps.

PIP: I stay on the bank. I call to the person. I throw the ring or reach with the pole.

NARI: There is no person. Use the buoy.

PIP: Buoy, come toward the bank.

SFX: Pole extends. Ring splashes into the pond. Rope feeds off the reel.

VEX: You missed.

PIP: I aimed in front of it. The wind moved it.

MARA: Pull the rope back and try again.

SFX: Rope reels in. Ring scrapes against the dock.

ODO: The form has a box for a second attempt.

VEX: Good. A rematch.

NARI: It is practice.

PIP: The buoy is turning. It is beside the post again.

VEX: Now throw!

SFX: Rope whips through the air. Ring lands around the buoy with a hollow knock.

PIP: I have it.

MARA: Pull slowly, keep your feet planted.

SFX: Rope draws taut. Buoy bumps the shore.

NARI: The buoy is in reach.

[Pip uses the hooked pole to draw it to the bank, then lays the ring beside it.]

ODO: A successful rescue.

VEX: It did not look heroic.

PIP: It looked like the buoy came back.

MARA: That's the point.

ODO: The last box asks for your approved rescue sound.

VEX: Give us a splash.

PIP: No.

SFX: Pip sets the wet ring on the dock. Water drips twice into a bucket.

ODO: Two drips?

PIP: Two points for keeping my feet dry.

VEX: Certified?

ODO: Certified as a shore-based rescuer. The form has a stamp for that.

SFX: Stamp. The rubber duck squeaks again.

MARA: The duck was not part of the test.

NARI: It has been watching.

TAG

VEX: The duck can apply next week.

PIP: It can swim already.
