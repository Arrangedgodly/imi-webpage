# The Umbrella That Wanted Applause

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio product-launch remotes, forced testimonials, and a self-opening umbrella that mistakes every sound for a cue*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Lelo, a gibbon shopping-show host; Brin, a capuchin inventor; Miri, a macaque demonstrator; and Vax, a baboon publicist.*

[SFX: Bright launch music, light rain on a canopy.]

LELO: Welcome to the live unveiling of the Applausebrella, the umbrella that opens when it hears appreciation.

BRIN: It responds to clapping, cheering, or a single approving whistle.

MIRI: What if someone sneezes?

BRIN: It may interpret the sneeze as applause.

VAX: That is a feature. We are demonstrating it outdoors, under rain.

LELO: Miri, please approach the stage and admire the device.

MIRI: It is compact, colorful, and apparently nervous.

[SFX: Umbrella snaps open.]

BRIN: It heard the compliment.

MIRI: I said it was nervous.

VAX: The tone was positive.

LELO: Let's try a round of applause.

[SFX: Audience claps; umbrella opens and closes twice.]

BRIN: It opens on applause. The closing motion is a courtesy bow.

MIRI: It is closing every time I clap.

BRIN: It expects the applause to stop.

VAX: We can edit the demonstration later.

LELO: This is live, Vax.

VAX: Then we will call it interactive.

[SFX: Rain grows heavier.]

MIRI: We need shelter. Brin, will the umbrella open if you say something nice?

BRIN: You have excellent timing.

[SFX: Umbrella opens.]

MIRI: I do not have excellent timing. The umbrella opened after I walked under the canopy.

BRIN: The canopy rustled like clapping.

LELO: The device responds to the environment.

VAX: That phrase is approved.

MIRI: What happens if we need to close it?

BRIN: We say something mildly disappointing.

MIRI: It is raining on my shoes.

[SFX: Umbrella closes.]

LELO: Successful close. Please continue.

MIRI: The stage is wet.

[SFX: Umbrella snaps open over Brin.]

BRIN: It is protecting the inventor.

VAX: We can market it as loyal.

MIRI: It just covered Brin because I criticized the stage.

LELO: A personal umbrella with emotional intelligence.

BRIN: It uses a sound sensor. It has no emotions.

VAX: Please don't say that into the product microphone.

[SFX: A camera shutter clicks. Umbrella opens.]

MIRI: The camera triggered it.

BRIN: The shutter resembles a clap.

LELO: What about a quiet room?

BRIN: It stays folded.

VAX: We have a quiet room on the soundstage.

MIRI: We are outside in a storm.

VAX: The quiet room is a portable booth.

[SFX: Booth door closes; rain muffles.]

BRIN: In here, the umbrella stays closed.

LELO: I will say a few words about its quality.

BRIN: Keep it calm.

LELO: This umbrella is reliable, practical, and well made.

[SFX: Umbrella opens.]

BRIN: It heard your praise.

MIRI: The booth is small. The canopy just knocked over the microphone.

LELO: That is a generous canopy.

VAX: We have our hero shot.

[SFX: Mic tips; feedback squeals. Umbrella closes.]

BRIN: Feedback sounds like disapproval.

MIRI: It closed to avoid the noise.

LELO: That is a safety feature.

VAX: We need a final testimonial.

MIRI: The umbrella reacts to claps, sneezes, camera shutters, feedback, and praise. It works as long as nobody needs it to stay open while being applauded.

[SFX: Audience laughs, then claps. Umbrella snaps open. Rain patters on top.]

BRIN: That is the product working.

MIRI: It is raining on us again.

BRIN: The umbrella opened over the audience.

VAX: The crowd is dry. The demo is a success.

LELO: The Applausebrella opens for appreciation, closes when the applause ends, and has protected everyone except the demonstrator.

MIRI: I will take the plain one from the van.

[SFX: Ordinary umbrella opens with a calm click.]

BRIN: The Applausebrella has launched.

VAX: And it is receiving a standing ovation.

[SFX: A final clap makes the device open over the soundboard; music fades under rain.]
