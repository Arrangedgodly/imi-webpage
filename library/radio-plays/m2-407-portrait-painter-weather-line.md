# The Portrait Painter on the Weather Line

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio weather desks that interrupt forecasts to sell atmospheric mood and poetic soundscapes*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Forecaster Ada, a bonobo; painter Jori, a macaque; producer Venn, a gibbon; caller Sumi, a squirrel monkey.

[SFX: RAIN ON STUDIO WINDOW. PAPER RUSTLES.]

ADA: This is the morning weather line. Rain will continue until noon, with a west wind at twelve miles an hour.

VENN: Hold. We need a more immersive opening. Jori is painting the window sound.

JORI: I am painting the forecast map. The window sound is a leak above the radiator.

ADA: The roof is dripping onto our weather map?

JORI: The blue paint is spreading over the county line.

[SFX: DRIP, DRIP. WET PAPER SAGS.]

VENN: It looks like a dramatic rain front. The sponsor wants a visual weather moment for the camera feed.

ADA: The map has a hole in it. The rain is coming from inside the studio.

JORI: I can lift it before the ink runs through the harbor.

[SFX: PAPER LIFTS; A DROP HITS A METAL CUP.] 

SUMI: I called to ask whether the ferry leaves at ten. The forecast sounded as if the whole studio were flooding.

ADA: The ferry leaves at ten. Rain, not a flood. The west wind is steady.

VENN: Can we get the painter to describe the clouds? That will keep the segment warm.

JORI: I paint what I can see. That is not the same as forecasting.

ADA: The radar shows a narrow band moving east. It should pass by noon.

VENN: Say it with feeling. Our weather audience wants atmosphere.

ADA: The rain is wet. The wind is moving east. That is my feeling.

[SFX: RAIN TAPS THE CUP IN A REGULAR PATTERN.] 

JORI: The cup is catching the leak. It is not a rain gauge.

VENN: The microphone caught a beautiful drip pattern. Let's call it the studio storm.

ADA: We should call maintenance. The forecast is not leaking.

SUMI: I can hear the difference. The window is far away, the cup is right beside the microphone.

VENN: Our audience thinks the storm has entered the room.

JORI: Then move the microphone away from the cup. The map is still readable if it dries flat.

[SFX: MIC STAND SLIDES. DRIPS BECOME QUIETER.] 

ADA: The forecast graphic shows rain ending around noon. The roof leak is in the northeast corner of this room.

VENN: We cannot put a roof leak in the weather crawl.

ADA: We can put it in the building log. The crawl should tell people whether to carry an umbrella.

JORI: The map dried with a blue ring around the studio marker. It looks like our building needs an umbrella.

SUMI: Is the ferry still leaving at ten?

ADA: Yes. The dock is outside the rain band. Keep your umbrella for the walk from the landing.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CLICKS. A MAINTENANCE CART ROLLS PAST THE DOOR.] 

VENN: Can the cart make one pass under the microphone? That sounds like thunder.

ADA: No. It is a cart, and we know where it is.

JORI: I painted the cup, the map, and the little blue leak. They are separate subjects.

VENN: Our segment is called Weather Portraits. I wanted one image to explain the morning.

ADA: Then paint the actual morning: rain outside, a cup inside, and a forecast that doesn't confuse them.

[SFX: PENCIL SKETCHES. RAIN SOFTENS AT THE WINDOW.] 

SUMI: I can hear the window now. It sounds farther away.

ADA: That's because we stopped interviewing the cup.

VENN: We will keep the painting and remove the false flood alert from the feed.

[SFX: KEYBOARD CLICKS. A SINGLE CLEAN WEATHER TONE.] 

ADA: Rain until noon, west wind twelve miles an hour. Roof repair scheduled after the broadcast.

JORI: The portrait is dry. The cup is full.

VENN: Keep the cup out of the weather graphic.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES. ONE LAST DRIP LANDS IN THE CUP.]
