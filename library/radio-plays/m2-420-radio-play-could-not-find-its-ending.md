# The Radio Play That Could Not Find Its Ending

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio drama productions that keep adding cliffhangers and recap breaks after the story has already resolved*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Director Hala, a macaque; actor Venn, a gibbon; sound operator Piki, a capuchin; writer Doro, an orangutan.

[SFX: STUDIO MIC OPENS. RAIN TRACK PLAYS.] 

VENN: The traveler reached the bridge and found the river rising.

HALA: Cut. The page says the bridge is closed, not the river.

DORO: I added the flood because the first ending felt small.

PIKI: We have a rain track, a river track and three thunder cues ready.

HALA: The traveler can turn back and use the safe road. That is the ending.

VENN: The producer wants a cliffhanger before the sponsor break.

DORO: I can add a mysterious lantern on the hill.

HALA: Does the lantern change the traveler's decision?

DORO: No. It glows.

HALA: Then we do not need it.

[SFX: PAGE TURNS; PENCIL CROSSES OUT A PARAGRAPH.] 

VENN: The traveler turns back. The rain stops. He arrives home.

PIKI: That lands cleanly. I can fade the rain after the door closes.

HALA: Good. Record it.

[SFX: MIC OPENS. RAIN TRACK STARTS.] 

DORO: The note from the station says the sponsor wants a second act at the bridge.

HALA: The traveler has turned away from the bridge. A second act would send him back.

PIKI: I can stop the river track now and keep the rain under the dialogue.

VENN: Then the audience will hear the choice instead of an explosion.

HALA: Exactly. He chooses the road, the sound follows him, and the door closes.

[SFX: RIVER TRACK FADES; RAIN SOFTENS.]

VENN: The traveler turned back and took the safe road. When he reached home, the kettle was warm.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS; FOOTSTEPS; KETTLE LID RATTLES.] 

HALA: That's the ending. Keep the kettle as the last sound.

DORO: What if the kettle whistles like a message?

HALA: Is there a message in the script?

DORO: Not yet.

PIKI: The kettle is already boiling. It will whistle if I do not turn it off.

[SFX: KETTLE WHISTLES; PIKI SWITCHES IT OFF.] 

VENN: The sound is natural. It can be the final note.

DORO: Then maybe the traveler discovers a letter beneath the kettle.

HALA: That restarts the story. The traveler is home, the danger is behind him, and the kettle has finished.

[SFX: PAPER SHUFFLES; A NEW PAGE RIPS OUT.] 

DORO: I am removing the letter. I keep writing another doorway after the last one closes.

HALA: The scene needs one decision, one consequence and one last sound. The traveler chose the safe road and got home.

VENN: I will say the final line again without a teaser.

[SFX: MIC OPENS; RAIN FADES.] 

VENN: He set his wet coat by the door, poured tea, and listened to the rain pass outside.

[SFX: CUP SETS ON SAUCER; RAIN SOFTENS.] 

PIKI: The kettle is quiet. The rain is gone.

HALA: Hold the room tone, then close.

DORO: I want the audience to wonder what happens tomorrow.

HALA: They can wonder without an extra scene. Tomorrow is not in this play.

[SFX: THREE SECONDS OF QUIET ROOM TONE.] 

VENN: The traveler slept through the night.

HALA: That line was cut. We already had the ending.

VENN: Right. Sorry. I read the old page.

DORO: I will mark the final line in ink and shred the discarded cliffhangers.

[SFX: PAPER SHREDS; MACHINE STOPS.] 

PIKI: The recording has one ending now.

HALA: Play the final take, beginning to end.

[SFX: TAPE STARTS; RAIN, FOOTSTEPS, DOOR, KETTLE, QUIET.] 

VENN: He set his wet coat by the door, poured tea, and listened to the rain pass outside.

[SFX: TAPE STOPS.] 

HALA: That is the end.

DORO: I have written nothing after it.

PIKI: The studio clock says we still have time for the sponsor.

HALA: The sponsor has a separate slot. This play is finished.

DORO: Their advertisement ends with "Find out next week which tea bag survives."

HALA: Give them the river track.

[SFX: MIC CLICKS OFF; TEA CUP LIFTS.]
