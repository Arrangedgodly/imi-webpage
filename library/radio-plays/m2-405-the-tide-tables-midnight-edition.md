# The Tide Table's Midnight Edition

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: coastal radio updates that read tomorrow's tide chart as a live report and alarm people at the wrong hour*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Pali, a capuchin; harbor clerk Eno, a macaque; sailor Tera, a gibbon; engineer Luma, a lemur.

[SFX: RADIO STUDIO AT NIGHT. PAPER CHART UNFOLDS. A CLOCK TICKS.]

PALI: Midnight harbor update. The tide is rising fast and will peak at 4:10 a.m.

TERA: It is not rising. The water is falling.

ENO: Pali is reading tomorrow's table. The date tab is still set to Thursday.

LUMA: The display says Friday.

LUMA: The automated feed is set to "next high tide." It reads from the default harbor if the date field is blank.

ENO: The feed card has a blank date.

TERA: That is why it read tomorrow's south inlet at our dock.

PALI: I will remove the old card from the studio desk. It has no harbor name on it.

[SFX: PAPER CARD TEARS OFF A CLIP. ANOTHER CARD SLIDES INTO PLACE.]

LUMA: The replacement shows East Dock, Friday night, low tide at 1:20.

ENO: The paper binder still uses a sliding date tab. It slipped one column over.

[SFX: TAB SLIDES BACK INTO THURSDAY. A CLIP FASTENS IT.]

TERA: The paper chart and the automated display now show the same dock and date.

PALI: The sponsor wanted the dramatic "tide surge" phrase.

ENO: The tide is falling. Don't call it a surge.

[SFX: WATER LAPS SLOWLY. THE GAUGE NEEDLE TICKS DOWN.]

LUMA: We can report what the gauge shows and read the correct time.

PALI: The paper chart has Thursday at the top.

TERA: My boat is tied at the lower dock. I need tonight's tide.

[SFX: RADIO MIC CLICKS. WATER LAPS AT A DOCK.]

ENO: Tonight's table says low tide at 1:20 a.m., rising after that.

PALI: I already told listeners the peak is at 4:10.

LUMA: We should correct the broadcast now. The printed table has the wrong date for tonight.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.]

PALI: Correction: I read tomorrow's tide. Tonight's low tide is expected at 1:20. The water is falling now.

TERA: I can check the gauge at the dock.

ENO: Use the marker on the east post. It shows the current level.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON PIER. WOOD CREAKS. A METAL GAUGE CLINKS.]

TERA: Water is below the blue mark. Falling slowly.

PALI: The chart says it should be higher.

ENO: Check the date tab and station name. This is East Dock, not the south inlet.

TERA: The south inlet has a different range.

LUMA: The automated feed uses the south inlet by default.

PALI: That is why it said rising fast?

ENO: The chart is for the south inlet and tomorrow's date. Two settings were wrong.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. TWO MENUS BEEP.]

LUMA: I have selected East Dock and tonight's date. The gauge and chart now match.

PALI: The new readout says low tide at 1:20, rising after.

TERA: That's what I see at the post.

PALI: The station can read a paper chart, but not if the date and harbor are wrong.

ENO: I will tape the date tab to the chart edge so it cannot slide behind the binder.

[SFX: TAPE TEARS. A PAPER TAB PRESSES FLAT.]

LUMA: The automated feed is saved to East Dock, Friday night.

PALI: We will announce the corrected tide once more at the top of the hour.

TERA: My boat is secure. I can leave the dock now.

[SFX: ROPE KNOT TIGHTENS. FOOTSTEPS RETURN ON PIER.]

ENO: The tide table and gauge now agree.

PALI: Tonight's low tide is at 1:20, and the water is falling slowly. No rapid rise.

TERA: My boat is tied above the marker. The warning can stay in the studio.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES. WATER LAPS BELOW THE PIER; CLOCK TICKS.]
