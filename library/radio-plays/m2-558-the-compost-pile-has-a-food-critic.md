# The Compost Pile Has a Food Critic

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: restaurant review broadcasts that score a working compost pile like a fine dining room*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Critic Vex, an orangutan; gardener Mara, a macaque; mixer Pip, a capuchin; groundskeeper Odo, a gibbon; volunteer Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Community garden. Shovel turns compost; birds call.

VEX: I give this compost pile two stars for ambiance and one for table service. It has failed to bring me a menu.

MARA: It's a garden bin. The guide is on the lid; nobody is serving lunch.

ODO: The mix has dry leaves, vegetable scraps, and soil. It is not ready to spread on the vegetable beds.

NARI: I brought coffee grounds in a paper filter. May I add them?

MARA: A thin layer is fine. We'll balance it with dry leaves, then close the lid.

VEX: No cheese course? No chef's special?

PIP: Cooked food and dairy stay out. They attract pests. I can put the grounds in without dropping the filter's plastic band.

SFX: Lid lifts; paper filter tips into bin.

NARI: The pile is warm in the middle. Does that mean it is ready?

MARA: Warmth is part of decomposition, not a doneness bell. Odo checks moisture with the tool; we don't touch or taste it.

ODO: The center is damp but not dripping. I'll turn one section so air can pass through.

SFX: Fork turns; dry leaves rustle.

VEX: The chef reveals the center. Is that a truffle?

ODO: It is an avocado pit from an earlier batch. It stays in the bin until it breaks down.

PIP: Please don't lift it out for the camera. I have a clean fork for the mix, and the pit is not a garnish.

VEX: The menu is refusing close-ups now.

NARI: A volunteer asks why the pile smells sour near the lid.

MARA: That can happen if one area is too wet or has too much green material. We can look from the side without putting our faces over it.

ODO: There's one wet clump near the lid. The rest is damp, not soaked.

VEX: A crisis at the pass! Shall I call for a second opinion from a chef?

MARA: Call the gardener. Pip, add a handful of dry leaves around that clump. Odo can turn only the top layer while I hold the lid clear.

PIP: Leaves are in. The plastic band stays out. I'm not adding anything else to chase the smell.

SFX: Fork shifts leaves; one clump breaks apart.

ODO: The wet clump is separated and mixed with dry leaves. Air slots remain clear; the bin is closed.

VEX: I need to taste the corrected dish before I revise the stars.

MARA: No one tastes compost. We checked one patch, adjusted it, and left the rest of the pile alone.

VEX: Then tell me when the meal is ready.

MARA: It isn't a meal. The garden will check the mix at its next scheduled turn. Today's material is not going on the beds.

NARI: I can put the empty paper filter in the brown bin and keep the plastic band out of it.

PIP: I'll rinse the fork at the tool tap. The microphone stays at the path, not over the pile.

VEX: The critic has been denied a spoon and a dramatic steam shot.

ODO: The lid is down, the fork is rinsed, and the coffee grounds are mixed with leaves. That's the work completed today.

MARA: We can close the broadcast. The pile has no rating, and no one needs to turn it again for the microphone.

SFX: Shovel rests against shed; lid settles once.

TAG

VEX: One star for patience, four for not asking me to eat it.

MARA: The pile still cannot read reviews.
