# The Empty Bandstand Encore

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: venue programming that schedules an encore after the performers and audience have gone home*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Stage manager Fen, a macaque; caretaker Rumi, a capuchin; pianist Daro, a gibbon; voice on the schedule, a lemur.

[SFX: NIGHT WIND. A PARK BANDSTAND DOOR OPENS. CHAIRS SCRAPE AS THEY ARE STACKED.]

FEN: The concert ended at nine. Why does the schedule say encore at nine-fifteen?

RUMI: It says "encore if requested." No one requested one.

FEN: The program manager preloaded a recording.

[SFX: A SMALL SPEAKER CLICKS. APPLAUSE PLAYS, FOLLOWED BY A PIANO RIFF.]

DARO: That is my piece.

FEN: You are standing here.

DARO: I am also in the recording.

RUMI: The park is empty. The audience left after the final bow.

FEN: The system will play the encore automatically.

DARO: It should only play if the audience calls for one.

[SFX: APPLAUSE RECORDING FADES. THE SPEAKER PLAYS A VOICE: "MORE! MORE!"]

RUMI: That sounds like someone asking.

FEN: It is a recording of last year's crowd.

DARO: Last year's crowd is home.

FEN: The schedule has a second encore, too.

RUMI: The chairs are stacked.

[SFX: A CHAIR SLIPS AND CLATTERS TO THE FLOOR. THE SPEAKER CLICKS ON AGAIN.]

VOICE ON RECORDING: Wonderful response. Let us hear one more!

DARO: That was a chair.

FEN: The sound sensor treats any clatter as applause.

RUMI: Then the empty bandstand can keep calling itself full.

[SFX: RECORDED APPLAUSE SWELLS. THE PIANO RIFF STARTS AGAIN.]

DARO: I can play a quiet ending over it.

FEN: It will trigger the encore.

DARO: Only if the sensor hears clapping.

RUMI: I can cover the microphone.

[SFX: A CLOTH IS PULLED OVER A SMALL MIC. THE RECORDED APPLAUSE CONTINUES THROUGH SPEAKER; THE SENSOR'S RED LIGHT CLICKS OFF.]

FEN: It stopped listening.

DARO: Let the recording play out. We should not interrupt the piece halfway through.

FEN: I am changing the cue. The recording will play only after a live request from the audience.

RUMI: How will the sensor know the difference between clapping and a chair falling?

FEN: It will not. I can mute it and listen for the request myself.

DARO: That makes the stage manager part of the system.

FEN: It makes the system wait for a person.

[SFX: FEN OPENS THE CONTROL BOX. A CABLE IS UNPLUGGED; THE SENSOR LIGHT CLICKS DARK.]

RUMI: What about the prerecorded crowd asking for more?

FEN: I will remove that track from the cue list.

[SFX: BUTTONS BEEP AS FEN DELETES THE APPLAUSE FILE. A SHORT TONE CONFIRMS THE ERASE.]

DARO: The piece is over. If we play another, it should be because somebody here wants to hear it.

RUMI: I do. But I do not want the recording.

FEN: Daro can play a live tag. No stored applause.

DARO: I will play four bars, then stop.

[SFX: PIANO PLAYS FOUR BRIGHT BARS. FEN AND RUMI CLAP ONCE EACH AT THE END; DARO STOPS.]

FEN: That was our request.

RUMI: And our response.

DARO: The encore has ended. We can stack the chairs now.

[SFX: CHAIRS ARE STACKED ONE BY ONE. NO PA ANNOUNCEMENT FOLLOWS.]

RUMI: The chairs are ready to go into storage.

DARO: Give it one minute.

[SFX: THE PIANO RECORDING REACHES ITS FINAL CHORD. IT RINGS INTO THE EMPTY PARK, THEN STOPS. NO APPLAUSE FOLLOWS.]

FEN: The program should end there.

RUMI: I will change the schedule so the encore requires a live audience request.

FEN: Does the form have a box for that?

RUMI: It has a box for "unexpected response." I will use it.

[SFX: PENCIL WRITES. A CLIPBOARD CLOSES.]

DARO: Do you want me to play a real encore?

FEN: We are the audience.

RUMI: There are three of us.

DARO: Three requests are enough.

[SFX: FEN, RUMI AND DARO CLAP THREE TIMES. DARO PLAYS A SHORT, LIVE PIANO TAG. IT ENDS CLEANLY.]

FEN: That was an encore.

RUMI: The schedule can record that.

[SFX: THE SPEAKER STAYS OFF. CHAIRS ARE CARRIED OUT; THE BANDSTAND DOOR CLOSES.]
