# The Slowest Race in the Valley

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live sports radio coverage of a deliberately slow endurance race*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: COMMENTATOR RELL, a macaque; COLOR VOICE SADA, a capuchin; RUNNER OBI, a gibbon; and MARSHAL ETT, a tortoise.

[SFX: STARTING HORN, CROWD CHEERS, THEN A SINGLE FOOTSTEP.] 

RELL: Welcome to the Valley's slowest race. The runners will travel one hundred paces from the fig tree to the stone marker. Speed is discouraged. Falling fruit is not a penalty.

SADA: Our defending champion, Obi, has begun with a measured step.

RELL: He is looking at the ground.

SADA: He is choosing where to put his foot. That is good strategy.

RELL: The crowd is urging him forward.

SADA: The crowd is also standing in his way.

OBI: Please move to either side of the path.

[THE CROWD SHUFFLES AWAY, MAKING A CLEAR LANE.] 

RELL: The race has paused for spectator safety.

SADA: Obi's pace is unchanged.

RELL: The challenger, a young squirrel monkey called Fip, is approaching from the rear.

SADA: Fip has stopped to inspect a leaf.

RELL: Is that allowed?

ETT: The rules say the runner may pause for any reason except to ask the marshal how long the race will last.

FIP: How long will the race last?

ETT: That is prohibited.

FIP: Then I will inspect the leaf.

[LEAF TURNS. A SMALL INSECT CRAWLS ACROSS IT.] 

SADA: Fip has discovered a beetle.

RELL: Does the beetle qualify as a competitor?

ETT: Only if it crosses the finish line under its own power.

SADA: It is moving in the opposite direction.

RELL: The race is underway again. Obi and Fip have both reached the shadow of the marker.

SADA: They are waiting for the shadow to move.

RELL: Is that required?

ETT: No. They can cross when ready.

FIP: The sun is moving faster than I am.

OBI: The marker will still be here.

RELL: A calm exchange between the runners. No one has challenged the clock.

SADA: A runner from the back is carrying a leaf to the finish line.

ETT: The leaf is not an entry.

SADA: It is in the lane.

RELL: The marshal has cleared the leaf. The runners are moving again.

FIP: I am going to take a shorter step.

OBI: Shorter steps are still steps.

SADA: Fip has narrowed the gap without increasing speed.

RELL: The crowd wants a photo finish.

ETT: We don't have a finish camera. We have a chalk line.

RELL: Can a chalk line determine a winner?

ETT: It can determine who arrived first. It cannot determine who had the better afternoon.

SADA: The runners are at the line together.

RELL: And they have stopped just before it to let the beetle pass.

RELL: The field is spread across three paces. Obi remains in front.

[OBI STOPS. A FIG DROPS NEAR HIS FOOT.] 

SADA: The leader has stopped.

RELL: He is picking up the fig.

OBI: The path is slippery here. I will move it off the track.

ETT: Good course care. The race continues.

RELL: Fip is now beside Obi.

FIP: I can carry the fig.

OBI: It is one fig. We can roll it to the grass.

[FIG ROLLS, THEN STOPS IN THE GRASS.] 

SADA: The runners are side by side. The beetle has reached the stone marker.

RELL: We have a surprise leader.

ETT: The beetle was never entered.

SADA: It has crossed the finish line, though.

RELL: The crowd is applauding the smallest competitor.

[APPLAUSE BUILDS. THE BEETLE CRAWLS UNDER THE MARKER.] 

ETT: We cannot award a medal without an entry form.

OBI: Give it the leaf.

FIP: The beetle can take the leaf home.

SADA: Both runners have crossed together. No one has asked who won.

RELL: The race has ended at exactly the speed the valley needed.

ETT: Next year, we will move the finish marker farther away.

RELL: How far?

ETT: One careful step.

[SFX: CROWD CHEERS SOFTLY. THE HORN SOUNDS ONCE, AT WALKING VOLUME.]
