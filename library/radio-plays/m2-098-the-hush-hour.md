# The Hush Hour

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night relaxation broadcasts, whispering hosts, and callers who mistake silence for dead air*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: host Sali, a lemur; technician Boro, a gorilla; caller Nema, a macaque; caller Tulo, a gibbon; producer Kett, a baboon.

[SFX: SOFT WATER. A WIND CHIME SOUNDS ONCE.]

SALI: Welcome to The Hush Hour. We invite you to settle, breathe, and let the quiet arrive.

KETT: The show begins in one minute.

BORO: We are already on air.

SALI: Then the quiet has arrived early.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS LOUDLY.]

NEMA: Is this the call-in show?

SALI: Yes.

NEMA: I can't sleep.

SALI: What is keeping you awake?

NEMA: The phone.

KETT: We can turn down the ring.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS SOFTLY.]

NEMA: That's better.

### Act One

SALI: Tell us what helps you settle.

NEMA: I count the leaves outside my window.

SALI: How many are there?

NEMA: I lose count.

SALI: You don't need to finish.

NEMA: That is a relief.

BORO: We have a second caller.

[SFX: SWITCHBOARD CLICKS.]

TULO: I called to ask whether silence is supposed to have a hum.

BORO: That is the studio equipment.

SALI: A gentle hum can be part of the room.

KETT: The fan is rattling.

BORO: It is not gentle.

[SFX: FAN RATTLES.]

TULO: It sounds like a basket rolling downhill.

SALI: Imagine the basket reaching a soft landing.

[SFX: BASKET FALLS. METAL CLANG.]

KETT: That was the microphone stand.

SALI: The studio is settling too.

### Act Two

[SFX: WIND CHIME. TWO NOTES. A LONG PAUSE.]

SALI: We will now have a short guided listening exercise.

KETT: How short?

SALI: Until the chime.

BORO: The chime is in my hand.

[SFX: CHIME SOUNDS.]

SALI: That was quick.

KETT: We can try again.

[SFX: LONG PAUSE. WATER TRICKLES.]

SALI: Notice the sound of water.

NEMA: Is it raining?

BORO: A pipe is dripping.

SALI: The sound may be water.

KETT: The pipe is leaking near the archive.

SALI: We can listen while we fix it.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS. TOOLBOX OPENS.]

TULO: I can hear you walking away.

SALI: I am still here.

TULO: Your voice is farther away.

SALI: The microphone moved with me.

KETT: The cable is stretching.

BORO: Stop walking.

[SFX: CABLE TUGS. MIC THUMPS.]

SALI: We have found a limit to quiet.

NEMA: I can still hear the water.

BORO: The drip stopped.

KETT: I put a bowl beneath it.

[SFX: DRIP INTO METAL BOWL.]

SALI: A gentle, regular sound.

TULO: It is very loud.

KETT: The bowl is empty.

BORO: The drip is hitting the bottom.

### Listener sound

[SFX: SWITCHBOARD CLICKS. A THIRD CALLER JOINS.]

PALA: I hear a chirp outside my window.

SALI: Is it a bird?

PALA: It happens every minute.

BORO: That is the studio clock.

KETT: The clock is in the hallway.

PALA: I am in the hallway.

SALI: Can you move the clock?

PALA: I can close the door.

[SFX: DOOR SHUTS. THE TICKING FADES.]

PALA: The quiet is better.

KETT: The clock is still there.

SALI: It can keep time quietly.

### Act Three

SALI: We have reached the end of the hush hour.

KETT: We have three calls waiting.

SALI: We can let them wait quietly.

NEMA: I feel sleepy.

TULO: I feel calmer.

BORO: The fan has stopped rattling.

[SFX: FAN STOPS. SILENCE.]

KETT: The room is quiet.

SALI: What do you hear?

NEMA: Nothing.

TULO: I hear nothing.

BORO: I hear the clock.

[SFX: CLOCK TICKS VERY LOUDLY.]

SALI: The clock is new.

KETT: It has always been there.

SALI: We have discovered another sound.

NEMA: It is regular.

TULO: It is a little too regular.

BORO: I can move it farther away.

[SFX: CLOCK LIFTS, TICKS FADE.]

SALI: The hush has returned.

KETT: We are off the air.

SALI: Are you sure?

BORO: The red light is off.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS. EVERYONE GASPS.]

KETT: The line is still open.

SALI: We can answer tomorrow.

### Tag

[SFX: PHONE RING STOPS. WATER DRIPS INTO THE BOWL.]

KETT: The bowl is full.

SALI: We will listen to that in the morning.
