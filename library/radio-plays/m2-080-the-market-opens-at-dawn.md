# The Market Opens at Dawn

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio coverage of a farmers market with overcomplicated auction patter*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: ANNOUNCER PELA, a macaque; SELLER ODO, a capuchin; AUCTIONEER REN, a gibbon; and customer MIM, a squirrel monkey.

[SFX: MARKET AWNING UNFURLS, CRATES SET DOWN, MORNING BIRDS.] 

PELA: The Fern Hollow market opens at dawn. Today the featured lot is a basket of ripe figs, sold by weight, flavor, or whichever measure the buyer can explain clearly.

REN: I will begin the bidding at three leaves.

ODO: The price is two leaves per fig.

REN: We have an opening bid of three leaves for the entire basket.

ODO: That is not enough.

REN: The seller has declined the dramatic opening.

MIM: I need four figs for breakfast.

REN: A focused buyer enters the bidding.

MIM: I am not bidding. I am buying four figs.

REN: Can you offer a counterleaf?

MIM: I can give you two leaves for each fig.

ODO: That is the posted price.

REN: The market has reached a stable price without a single raised hand.

PELA: We are witnessing an efficient transaction.

REN: This is unusual. Let's add suspense. The basket may contain a surprise fig.

ODO: All figs are visible.

REN: A surprise is only hidden until discovered.

MIM: I can see all of them.

REN: Then you have discovered the surprise.

[SFX: A FIG ROLLS ACROSS A TABLE AND LANDS IN AN EMPTY CRATE.] 

ODO: That one is bruised. It is for the jam pile.

REN: A secret second lot.

ODO: No. It is bruised fruit I will not sell at full price.

MIM: Can I take it for jam?

ODO: Yes. One leaf.

REN: We have a second bidder.

MIM: I am still the only customer.

PELA: The auctioneer has transformed a small purchase into a contest with one contestant.

REN: The clock is ticking toward breakfast.

ODO: The figs are ready now.

REN: We can move the basket to the shade while MIM chooses.

PELA: We have another customer approaching the stand. They are pointing at the basket.

REN: The bidding is reopened.

ODO: The figs are not a single lot. They are sold by the piece.

MIM: My cousin wants one fig, not the whole basket.

REN: Can your cousin enter a proxy bid?

MIM: My cousin is carrying water. I am buying the fig.

ODO: The price is two leaves.

REN: The market has returned to its opening figure.

PELA: The auctioneer has completed a full circle.

REN: I would like to add a ceremonial final call.

ODO: There is no need. Mim is ready to pay.

MIM: Here are two leaves.

[LEAVES CHANGE HANDS. ONE FIG SETS DOWN IN A SMALL BASKET.] 

REN: Sold to the customer who asked for it.

ODO: Thank you.

PELA: That is how a market works when the announcer lets it work.

REN: We can still have an auction later for the large squash.

ODO: The squash belongs to the soup pot.

REN: Can the soup pot bid?

MIM: The soup pot does not have leaves.

REN: Then we will accept a recipe.

ODO: We are not selling the squash.

PELA: The market is open, but not every object is for sale.

[THE SQUASH IS SET DOWN. IT MAKES A DULL, HEAVY THUD.] 

REN: A powerful bidder has entered.

ODO: That was the squash.

REN: It has made its offer.

[MIM SELECTS FOUR FIGS. A CRATE SCRAPES INTO THE SHADE.] 

MIM: These are ripe. That one is soft but not spoiled.

ODO: Take it for the jam pile. No charge.

REN: The seller has offered a bonus.

ODO: I am giving away one bruised fig so it does not go to waste.

PELA: The market has made its clearest announcement: buy what you need, and let the rest be used.

REN: Can we call that a successful auction?

MIM: I got breakfast and a jam fig.

ODO: I sold four and kept the rest shaded.

REN: Then the bids are closed.

[SFX: CRATES SHIFT, A BIRD CALLS, MARKET CHATTER BEGINS.]
