# The Rope Bridge Traffic Report

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: morning traffic bulletins, urgent route detours, and a bridge reporter who describes every tail-length as a vehicle*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST MIRA, a capuchin
TRAFFIC REPORTER DEX, a macaque
BRIDGE KEEPER ORO, a gibbon
CALLER FENN, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: MORNING JINGLE. DISTANT CARS. BIRDSONG.]

MIRA: It is seven fifteen. We go now to Dex at the Cedar Rope Bridge.

DEX: Traffic is moving at a thoughtful pace. We have a group of macaques crossing in single file, a gibbon carrying a basket, and one squirrel stopped to consider the view.

MIRA: Is there a delay?

DEX: There is a view.

[SFX: ROPE BRIDGE CREAKS.]

DEX: The bridge is swaying. The keeper asks everyone to hold the side ropes.

ORO: The bridge is designed to move.

DEX: The listeners will want to know how much.

ORO: A little.

DEX: Define little.

ORO: The amount that keeps it attached to both banks.

MIRA: We have a caller.

[SFX: PHONE CLICK.]

FENN: I am at the north end. A line of tails is extending across the bridge.

MIRA: Is that blocking traffic?

FENN: It is how the monkeys stay together.

DEX: The line is now passing the midpoint. We have three baskets, two lunch pails, and one gibbon checking the side rope.

ORO: That is normal.

DEX: The hat is on the bridge.

ORO: Then it is not normal.

[SFX: HAT FLUTTERS IN WIND.]

MIRA: Is a detour available?

DEX: The trail under the bridge is muddy. The upper path is closed for a beehive.

FENN: The beehive has not closed the path.

DEX: The bees are using it.

MIRA: We need a safe route.

ORO: Let the crossing group finish. The bridge has a two-at-a-time limit.

DEX: We are reporting a traffic jam of two.

MIRA: That sounds manageable.

[SFX: BRIDGE CREAKS. BASKET RATTLES.]

DEX: One traveler has stopped at the midpoint to adjust a banana sling.

ORO: They can step to the side platform.

DEX: There is no side platform.

ORO: There is a widened plank.

DEX: I see it. The banana sling is now adjusted.

MIRA: How long is the delay?

DEX: Seven seconds.

MIRA: That is shorter than the report.

DEX: We are still reporting it.

[SFX: A BELL RINGS.]

ORO: The crossing is clear.

DEX: The bridge is open both ways. A line of macaques is now returning with the hat.

FENN: The hat belongs to ORO.

ORO: I am wearing my hat now. The wind blew it off, and I have just picked it up.

DEX: One hat, one owner, and a bridge that is clear.

MIRA: We will take a short break. When we come back, Dex will report on a leaf that is blocking the bike lane.

DEX: It is a large leaf.

ORO: It is a leaf.

[SFX: RETURN FROM BREAK. JINGLE.]

MIRA: Dex is back at the bridge. The leaf remains in the bike lane.

DEX: The leaf has turned over. It is now two leaves.

ORO: A gust split it.

DEX: Traffic is backed up behind a group returning from the viewpoint. They are carrying a basket of windfall pears.

FENN: They can cross one at a time, but the basket is wider than the plank.

ORO: Pass the basket over first. The bridge carries people; the bank can carry pears.

[SFX: BASKET PASSED HAND TO HAND. BRIDGE CREAKS.]

DEX: The basket is across. The crossing group is moving. We have no cars, one leaf, and a clear bridge.

MIRA: That is a quiet traffic report.

DEX: The leaf is still moving. ORO: It is moving off the road.

FENN: Wait. It has caught on the bridge rope.

[SFX: LEAF RUSTLES AGAINST ROPE. BRIDGE CREAKS.]

DEX: The leaf is now an obstruction above the roadway.

ORO: I can reach it from the bank with this long branch.

MIRA: Is the bridge closed?

ORO: Only while I remove the leaf. Everyone can wait on firm ground.

[SFX: BRANCH SCRAPES. LEAF FLUTTERS FREE AND LANDS IN A BASKET.]

FENN: The road is clear again.

DEX: The leaf has been routed to compost. It is the first vehicle today to leave by basket.

MIRA: We are clear to sign off.

[SFX: JINGLE OUT.]
