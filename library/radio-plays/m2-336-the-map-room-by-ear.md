# The Map Room by Ear

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: expert navigation programs that turn a simple route into a performance of jargon and scenic flourishes*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Guide Orla, a macaque; mapmaker Bex, a lemur; hiker Tavi, a gibbon; ranger Neri, a capuchin.

[SFX: WIND THROUGH PINES. A MAP UNFOLDS, PAPER FLUTTERS, THEN IS PINNED UNDER A COMPASS.]

ORLA: Welcome to the map room. Today we will guide hikers by sound alone.

TAVI: I can see the trail from the window.

ORLA: Close your eyes, then.

TAVI: I am trying to leave before the rain.

BEX: The route is easy. Follow the creek until it splits, then take the path with the wooden bridge.

ORLA: That is too direct. We need landmarks.

NERI: The creek makes a steady sound. The bridge has one loose plank that knocks when you step on it.

ORLA: Excellent. A sound signature.

[SFX: THREE DIFFERENT CREEK RECORDINGS PLAY IN TURN: FAST WATER, SLOW WATER, THEN A DRIPPING TAP.]

TAVI: Which creek is ours?

BEX: The middle one. I recorded it at the path fork.

ORLA: It lacks depth. Add the bridge.

[SFX: A WOODEN PLANK KNOCKS ONCE, THEN TWICE.]

TAVI: That sounds like a door.

NERI: It is a bridge plank.

ORLA: The listener must distinguish it by context.

TAVI: I am standing in the rain. I would prefer a sign.

[SFX: RAIN BEGINS ON THE ROOF, SOFT AT FIRST, THEN LOUDER.]

BEX: The sign at the fork has blown down.

ORLA: Then the sound becomes essential. Tavi, play the creek and plank together.

[SFX: CREEK RUSHES. THE PLANK KNOCKS TWICE. A THIRD SOUND, A METAL CLINK, CUTS THROUGH.]

TAVI: That is not the bridge.

NERI: It is my ranger bell. The north path is closed.

ORLA: We can use that. Bell, creek, bridge. Three landmarks.

BEX: The bell means do not take the north fork. It is not a landmark.

TAVI: I hear a bell, water, and knocking. Which direction is safe?

NERI: The bridge is downstream, south of the fork. The north path ends at a washout.

ORLA: We should narrate the direction in the guide.

BEX: That's the map's job.

[SFX: PAPER MAP IS TURNED. A PIN DROPS AND ROLLS ACROSS THE TABLE.]

TAVI: The compass is still pointing north. I can use that.

NERI: Keep the bell to your left and follow the creek noise until it is louder. Then cross at the bridge.

ORLA: Say it again, without the scenic embellishment.

NERI: Bell on your left. Louder creek. Two plank knocks. Cross.

TAVI: That I can follow.

[SFX: A PENCIL TAPS A ROUTE ON THE MAP, THEN SLIDES TO A SECOND FORK.]

BEX: We should test the recording from the door, not from the map table.

ORLA: The sound mix was balanced here.

NERI: The hiker will hear it outside, with wind, footsteps and their own coat hood.

TAVI: I can stand by the door and listen.

[SFX: TAVI WALKS TO THE DOOR. THE RECORDING PLAYS QUIETLY THROUGH A SMALL SPEAKER: BELL, CREEK, TWO KNOCKS. WIND BUFFETS THE MICROPHONE.]

TAVI: The bell is clear. I lose the creek under the wind.

BEX: Then raise the creek slightly, not the bell.

ORLA: That changes the balance of the guide.

NERI: It changes the balance for the person using it.

[SFX: A KNOB TURNS. THE CREEK RISES A LITTLE; THE BELL STAYS THE SAME.]

TAVI: Now I can hear it. I know to go south at the fork.

ORLA: And when you hear two knocks?

TAVI: Cross the bridge.

NERI: Good. The recording gives the clue, and the words give the direction.

ORLA: I will remove the extra rain track.

[SFX: A SWITCH CLICKS. THE RAIN TRACK STOPS; THE LIVE WIND REMAINS.]

[SFX: THE RECORDING PLAYS BACK: BELL, CREEK GROWING LOUDER, TWO WOODEN KNOCKS. TAVI REPEATS THE SEQUENCE WITH A FINGER TAP.]

BEX: The map room is quiet enough now.

ORLA: We should add a separate track for the rain.

NERI: The rain is not part of the route.

ORLA: It is part of the experience.

TAVI: The experience is that I am wet.

[SFX: THE ROOF DRUMS WITH RAIN. NERI OPENS THE DOOR. THE RAIN IS MUCH SOFTER OUTSIDE.]

NERI: The trail is ready. The storm is passing.

ORLA: Our guide ends here, at the door.

TAVI: Good. I can hear where to go.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS DESCEND THE PORCH. BELL LEFT, CREEK AHEAD, TWO PLANK KNOCKS. A FINAL STEP CROSSES THE BRIDGE.]

BEX: He made it across.

ORLA: The guide is approved.

NERI: The sign is still face down.

[SFX: A WOODEN SIGN IS LIFTED AND SET UPRIGHT. ITS POST CLICKS INTO THE GROUND.]
