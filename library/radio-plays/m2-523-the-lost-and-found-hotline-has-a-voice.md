# The Lost-and-Found Hotline Has a Voice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: call-in radio that gives lost objects dramatic biographies*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; clerk Odo, a gibbon; caller Pip, a capuchin; caller Seli, a lemur; archivist Vex, an orangutan.

SFX: Radio studio. Phone line clicks; a small fan hums.

MARA: Welcome to Found Things, where callers describe an object and we help it find its owner. Odo, what came in today?

ODO: A blue mitten, a brass spoon, and one very damp envelope.

VEX: The envelope has a story. We should play its mysterious journey with music.

MARA: First, dry it flat. The return address may still be readable.

SFX: Paper laid between two clean blotters.

PIP, on phone: I lost my lunch tin near the footbridge. It has a painted red door on the lid.

ODO: No tin has been turned in.

VEX: Perhaps this envelope contains a clue to the tin.

MARA: It is an envelope. Don't promise a connection before we know one.

SELI, on second line: I found a metal lunch tin near the footbridge. It has a red door painted on the lid, and a name inside: Pip.

PIP: That's mine. The latch sticks unless you lift the lid at the hinge.

ODO: We have the tin at the studio reception desk. The envelope is still unidentified.

VEX: Can we reveal the tin as the envelope's long-lost companion?

MARA: No. Caller Seli found the tin; that's already enough good radio.

SFX: Odo tests the latch. It clicks, then catches.

ODO: The latch is bent. We can hold the lid shut with a band until Pip collects it.

PIP: Please don't put it in the envelope. I need the tin for tomorrow's lunch.

MARA: We'll keep it at reception under your name. Seli, can you tell us what else was near it?

SELI: A folded napkin and a paper map. No envelope.

VEX: Two discoveries in one hour. The footbridge is a portal.

SELI: It is a footbridge. People drop things while crossing.

SFX: A phone buzzes on the desk.

ODO: The envelope has a legible return line now. It belongs to the library archive. They send us catalog cards each week.

MARA: Then it isn't lost property. It was misdelivered with today's post.

VEX: Could the envelope be the tin's guardian?

PIP: I just want my lunch tin back.

MARA: Fair. Odo, put the archive envelope in the outgoing mail tray. Seli, thanks for checking the area. Pip, reception will hold your tin.

SFX: Envelope slides into a paper tray; the tin latch is secured with a soft band.

ODO: The envelope is routed. The tin has its owner's name. The spoon and mitten remain unclaimed.

VEX: We should interview the spoon.

MARA: We should label it and keep it dry. If someone calls, we'll ask where they last saw it.

PIP: I can pick up my tin after the show. The red door is easy to recognize.

SELI: I'll leave a note at the footbridge that found items go to the staffed desk, not the rail.

VEX: You have removed the mystery from the bridge.

SELI: I have removed a place where someone might trip over a spoon.


SFX: Reception desk phone rings.

ODO: Reception has a caller. Pip, your lunch tin is ready. The repaired latch is not part of this handoff; we have only secured it with the band.

PIP: I'll pick it up after the show and take it to the repair bench tomorrow. I don't want the band pulled off in the hallway.

MARA: Seli, can you write Pip's name on the hold tag and leave the tin on the staffed shelf?

SELI: Done. The tin is beside the desk bell, not out by the public door.

VEX: The reunion is complete. Should we play a fanfare?

PIP: A quiet one. I still need to hear the clerk when I arrive.

ODO: The archive envelope goes into the outgoing tray now. It will return to the library tomorrow.

MARA: That's two ordinary routes working as intended. We can close the line.

SFX: Phone disconnects; paper tray slides shut.

TAG

MARA: One tin reunited, one envelope routed.

VEX: The spoon remains a person of interest.

ODO: It is cutlery, Vex.
