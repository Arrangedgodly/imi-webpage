# The Radio Drama Sound Crew Calls a Pause

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: behind-the-scenes radio drama conventions, sound effects, and an actor who cannot deliver a line until the props stop arguing*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Director Sela, a gibbon; actor Odo, an orangutan; sound artist Kimi, a macaque; producer Vex, a baboon; stage runner Pip, a capuchin.

SFX: Studio booth. A door creaks, then a coconut clops across the floor.

SELA: We are recording the final scene of The Castle at Midnight. Odo, enter through the door, hear the horse, and deliver your warning.

ODO: Which sound was the horse?

KIMI: The coconut clops were the horse. The door was a real door.

VEX: We need the horse to sound farther away, like it is crossing the valley.

PIP: The coconut is under the microphone stand.

KIMI: I can roll it across the gravel tray from left to right.

SELA: Let's take it from the door. Ready.

SFX: Door creaks; coconut rolls; glass shatters.

ODO: The castle is under attack!

SELA: Cut. That was the jar of buttons, not the glass.

PIP: The buttons were on the shelf behind the gravel tray.

VEX: They sounded like a small cavalry charge.

KIMI: I can move the jar to the sound table. It should not be beside the door prop.

SFX: Jar lifted; buttons rattle, then settle.

SELA: We will record the horse alone, then the door, then Odo's line. The mix will put them together.

ODO: That is sensible.

VEX: We need a single live take. The station's audience wants to hear the entire scene performed in real time.

SELA: Then we will perform it live, but cues are still called in order. Nobody throws anything at the microphone.

PIP: I am putting the breakable jar in a padded box.

KIMI: Horse cue on my hand. Door cue on the slate. Glass cue after the line.

VEX: Why after the line?

KIMI: The hero sees the broken window after warning the villagers. We must not break it before he speaks.

SELA: Correct. Let's rehearse the cue order. Door, horse, line, glass.

SFX: Door creaks. Coconut rolls in gravel.

ODO: A rider approaches from the western road!

SFX: Glass break, created by dropping a small sheet of sugar glass into a padded box.

SELA: Good. The sequence is in time. One more with the chorus outside.

VEX: We have a chorus?

PIP: Three actors are in the hall. They think they are the cavalry.

SELA: They are the villagers. Give them a line after the glass, not before.

SFX: Door opens; coconut rolls.

ODO: A rider approaches from the western road!

SFX: Sugar glass breaks.

VILLAGE CHORUS, off mic: We heard you!

SELA: Cut. They were supposed to say, "We are ready!"

VEX: They heard the warning and improvised.

PIP: The hall door is open. They were standing outside the booth.

KIMI: We need to close the hall door before the next take. The booth microphone is picking up the hallway.

SFX: Hall door closes. The actors whisper to each other outside.

SELA: We'll keep the chorus line simple. Pip, hand them the cue card. Kimi, keep the hall mic off until their line.

PIP: Card delivered. It says, "We are ready," in large letters.

VEX: Could we have the horse louder?

KIMI: The signal level is already safe. I can make the gravel tray closer to the mic, not increase the volume.

SELA: Let us do the final take. Door. Horse. Warning. Glass. Villagers.

SFX: Door creaks once. Coconut rolls from left to right.

ODO: A rider approaches from the western road!

SFX: Sugar glass breaks in the padded box.

VILLAGE CHORUS, off mic: We are ready!

SELA: And cut. That is the take.

VEX: The horse sounded exactly the same as the buttons.

KIMI: The buttons are boxed. That was the coconut.

PIP: The jar is still in the padded box. I labeled it BUTTONS, NOT HORSE.

ODO: Does the horse get a credit?

SELA: Sound effects: Kimi. Horse: one coconut and a tray of gravel.

VEX: We can put the coconut on the poster.

KIMI: Only if the poster says it is a sound prop.

SFX: Slate claps; recording stops cleanly.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The scene is complete, the glass is safely broken, and the horse has not asked for a speaking role.
