# The Midnight Power Desk Broadcasts by Candlelight

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: emergency radio bulletins, blackout reporting, and presenters whose dramatic tone risks confusing repair updates*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Tera, a gibbon; utility dispatcher Mako, an orangutan; field electrician Seli, a macaque; caller Odo, a capuchin; producer Bex, a baboon.

SFX: Studio in darkness. Battery radio hum; rain taps the window.

TERA: We are on the emergency power circuit. A storm has cut electricity to three streets. Please tell us where you are calling from before describing what you see.

ODO: I am in the market. The whole district is black.

MAKO: The map shows the east market still has power. Odo, can you see the clock tower?

ODO: I can see one candle in the bakery window.

SELI: That does not tell us whether the street circuit is live. I am at the switch cabinet with a meter.

TERA: Our field electrician is entering the dark.

SELI: I am standing under a streetlamp that is off. The cabinet is dry, the cover is locked, and I have permission to test it.

BEX: The host's microphone is on the battery channel. We can hear the update even if the studio lights stay off.

TERA: I was not worried about the lights.

BEX: You asked if the microphone still works.

TERA: I asked if the broadcast still works.

MAKO: The outage map shows a branch fault between the market and the bridge. The rest of the city has power.

ODO: I can hear a generator near the bakery. Is that the utility crew?

MAKO: No. The bakery has a backup generator for its freezer. It is running behind the building.

TERA: We have a second power source.

SELI: A private generator, not connected to the grid. It is operating safely outside with its exhaust clear.

ODO: The bakery window candle is still there.

TERA: The candle may be a sign that the bakery is open.

ODO: It is a candle. The door is locked.

BEX: Odo, please do not test the door while we are broadcasting.

ODO: I was checking whether the door was closed.

MAKO: We have a repair update. The fault is a branch line brought down by a tree limb. The crew has isolated the line and is removing the limb. Power will remain off in the three streets until the line is inspected.

TERA: How long?

MAKO: We do not have a safe restoration time yet.

TERA: Could we say soon?

SELI: Not until the inspection is finished. I can see the crew at the bridge. The line is clear, but they are testing the insulators.

SFX: Radio crackle; a handheld meter beeps.

SELI: Meter reading is stable. The crew will reconnect after the second check.

MAKO: The map is still red. I will leave the public notice red until dispatch confirms.

ODO: The market has a battery lantern now. The candle is going out.

TERA: The lights are returning?

ODO: No. The bakery is closing. The owner took the candle inside.

BEX: Thank you for clarifying before we announced a recovery.

TERA: I am learning to distinguish a window light from a grid update.

SFX: A relay clicks faintly; studio remains dark.

MAKO: Dispatch confirms the branch is repaired and the circuit test passed. I am updating the map. The three streets will return one block at a time.

SELI: I am at the first cabinet. Voltage is steady. The crew is checking the second block now.

MAKO: First block restored. Second block is clear. Final block needs one more meter reading.

SELI: Final meter is stable. The crew has checked each block.

ODO: I can see the market sign again.

TERA: Is it illuminated?

ODO: No. It is reflecting a lantern.

BEX: Host, wait for dispatch.

MAKO: All three blocks are restored. The outage is closed. The bakery remains closed until morning.

SELI: The line is stable, and I am securing the cabinet.

TERA: The studio emergency channel will return to standby after this report. The district is back on power; do not enter a repair area to check your lights.

ODO: The market clock is moving again.

MAKO: It runs on batteries.

TERA: We have one final signal: the report is over.

SFX: Battery radio clicks off; normal lights return in studio.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Three streets are restored, the bakery is closed, and the candle was never a power indicator.
