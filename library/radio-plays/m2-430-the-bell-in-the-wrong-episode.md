# The Bell in the Wrong Episode

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: serialized audio dramas, recap-heavy franchises, and sound libraries that reuse a signature cue in the wrong story*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-015*

Logline: A radio adventure's signature bell keeps interrupting its courtroom episode, forcing the sound editor to separate franchise mythology from the current case.

Cast: Director Miri, a gibbon; actor Kalo, a macaque; sound editor Venn, a capuchin; writer Rilo, an orangutan; continuity keeper Suma, a baboon.

[SFX: A BELL RINGS THREE TIMES.]

KALO: The lighthouse keeper has returned!

MIRI: This episode is set in a courtroom.

RILO: The bell is our series signature.

VENN: It is the lighthouse bell from last week's episode.

SUMA: This week's case is about a disputed garden boundary. There is no lighthouse.

ACT ONE

Rilo has written a courtroom drama in which two neighbors argue over a line of berry bushes. Kalo plays the judge and both neighbors. Miri asks him to change his voice between characters. Every time he says "objection," Venn's soundboard plays the three-note lighthouse bell.

KALO: Objection.

[SFX: BELL.]

MIRI: That was a sound cue, not a legal ruling.

VENN: The button is labeled OBJECTION / LIGHTHOUSE REVEAL.

SUMA: Why?

VENN: The series began as a lighthouse mystery. We added the legal stories later.

Rilo says the cue has become familiar to listeners. Miri asks whether familiarity means it belongs in every episode. Rilo says the audience expects a connection. Suma points to the script: the berry bushes are on a shared fence line, and the neighbors need a surveyor to identify the boundary.

ACT TWO

The actors perform the dispute. Kalo, as neighbor one, says the bushes shade his tomatoes. As neighbor two, he says the berries belong on her side. He changes seats for each voice, but the studio chair squeaks in both. Venn adds the bell to cover the squeak. Miri stops the take.

MIRI: Now the bell sounds like a verdict.

KALO: It might be.

RILO: Could there be a lighthouse near the garden?

SUMA: The setting is a hill orchard, two valleys inland.

Venn checks the effects library. The bell file is called LIGHTHOUSE_FINAL_3. The soundboard shortcut shares a button with the objection cue. Miri tells him to separate them. He renames the button JUDGE'S GAVEL, then realizes the production owns no gavel sound.

VENN: I can record one.

Suma taps a wooden block. It sounds like a small knock. Rilo says a courtroom needs something grander. Kalo taps the desk with a pencil; it sounds like a tiny bell. Miri asks them to use the ordinary knock and let the dialogue carry the scene.

ACT TWO-B

A new take begins. The surveyor enters, played by Kalo in a lower voice. He measures the distance from the fence post to the orchard stone. The first measurement is wrong because one neighbor is holding the tape against a berry stem instead of the post. The neighbors argue; the surveyor asks them to let go of the plant.

SURVEYOR KALO: I am measuring the fence, not the fruit.

NEIGHBOR KALO: The fruit is on the fence.

SURVEYOR KALO: The fruit is on the branch.

Miri lets the exchange play without music. The surveyor resets the tape and finds the boundary is exactly between the two oldest bushes. Neither neighbor owns the whole row. They agree to pick from opposite sides and trim only their own branches. Rilo says the ending needs a callback. Suma points to the three-note bell cue. Venn keeps his hand away from the button.

KALO: Objection.

MIRI: Sustained.

The wooden block knocks once. The bell does not ring. Rilo laughs and says the scene has a sound of its own. Venn saves the recording under COURTROOM_BOUNDARY, not LIGHTHOUSE.

ACT THREE

Miri plays back the episode's final mix. The courtroom opens with a chair squeak, then the characters speak. The surveyor measures the fence correctly. The neighbors agree on shared access to the bushes. The judge rules that neither side may move the boundary stone. The block knock marks the final decision.

Suma checks the continuity sheet: no lighthouse, no bell, no returning keeper. Venn removes the three-note signature from the recap and leaves only the current episode title. Rilo asks if they can include the lighthouse sound after the credits as a teaser. Miri asks what it would tease. Rilo has no answer. They cut it.

KALO: The bushes remain in the orchard.

SUMA: The boundary remains between them.

VENN: The bell remains in the library.

TAG

[SFX: ONE WOODEN KNOCK.]

MIRI: That is the courtroom episode.

RILO: I can hear the difference.
