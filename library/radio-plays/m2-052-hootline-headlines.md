# Hootline Headlines

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: urgent local radio news bulletins*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-002*

Cast: Anchor Vala, a macaque; field reporter Kemi, a gibbon; editor Noot, a capuchin; and two callers from the town square.

[SFX: NEWS STING, THEN A SMALL HOO OUTSIDE.]

VALA: We interrupt our program with breaking news. A loud call has been heard near the east side of the square. A sandwich is also missing from a bench. We do not yet know whether the two events are connected.

NOOT: The owl calls at dusk. The sandwich is usually there at lunch.

VALA: We are still investigating.

[SFX: PHONE LINE OPENS.]

KEMI, REMOTE: I'm at the square, behind the fence. A great horned owl is on the fig tree. It is calling to another owl over the roof.

VALA: Is it holding anything?

KEMI: A twig. I can see no food.

NOOT: The missing sandwich was wrapped in paper.

VALA: Do not infer a crime from a twig and an empty bench.

KEMI: A crowd has gathered. One person is holding up a wrapper and pointing at the owl.

VALA: We can confirm there is a wrapper. We cannot confirm it came from the bench.

NOOT: Our producer has put "possible lunch connection" on the headline board.

VALA: Please erase "possible." It sounds cautious but still suggests the owl is involved.

[SFX: ERASER RUBS, CHALK SCRATCHES.]

CALLER ONE: I left a sandwich on the bench. It is not missing. I went to get water and the wind moved the napkin.

VALA: Is the sandwich still there?

CALLER ONE: Yes. The owl has not touched it. It is on the ground now.

KEMI: The crowd is backing away from the tree. A volunteer has moved the sandwich and put up a sign asking people not to feed the owl.

NOOT: I have written a correction: "Owl Seen Near Fig Tree; Sandwich Unharmed."

VALA: Add that the caller moved it, not the owl.

CALLER ONE: I moved it into my bag. I am going home.

VALA: Thank you. The bird call was a bird. The sandwich remains with its owner. No lunch crime has occurred.

NOOT: The bulletin board still has room for a second headline.

VALA: We don't need one.

NOOT: It says "Owl Declines Interview."

KEMI: The owl has flown to the far side of the roof. It declined by leaving.

VALA: That is an observation. Keep it.

[SFX: NEWSROOM TYPEWRITER.]

NOOT: Should the correction replace the first headline?

VALA: It should sit beside it. We made the first claim too exciting for the facts. People can hear where we changed it.

NOOT: The original headline says "Owl Suspected in Lunch Incident."

VALA: Then let the correction be larger.

[SFX: PAPER STAMP.]

KEMI: The path is open again. The owl is gone, the wrapper is in the caller's bag, and nobody has fed the bird.

NOOT: I have one more headline for the board. "Residents Stare at Tree."

VALA: That is not news.

NOOT: "Radio Desk Removes Three Adjectives."

VALA: That's a correction to our own headline.

KEMI: The caller is at the square exit. She says the sandwich is safely in her bag now, and wants us to stop broadcasting her lunch's whereabouts.

NOOT: We have already corrected the sandwich report.

VALA: Then stop investigating the bag.

NOOT: What about the owl's press secretary?

KEMI: It is another owl. It has left too.

VALA: Please don't call that a walkout. Birds leave branches.

[SFX: PAPER NOTICE TACKED TO A BOARD.]

NOOT: The correction is posted next to the first bulletin. The headline board is now accurate.

VALA: For how long?

NOOT: Until the next bulletin.

VALA: Hootline Headlines will return at six. If you hear another call, first find out whether it is a bird. Then decide whether it is news.

NOOT: And check the bench before naming a suspect.

[SFX: OWL CALL. NEWS STING CUTS OFF MID-NOTE.]
