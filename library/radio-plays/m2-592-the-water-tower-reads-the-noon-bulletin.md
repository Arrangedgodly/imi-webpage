# The Water Tower Reads the Noon Bulletin

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: civic radio ceremonies, emergency announcements, and a town tower that broadcasts weather from a stuck recording*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Announcer Lio, a gibbon; engineer Sera, a macaque; mayor Daro, a baboon; caller Odo, an orangutan; technician Nessa, a capuchin.

SFX: Municipal studio beside water tower. A loudspeaker pops, then plays a recorded chime.

LIO: The noon bulletin is about to play from the water tower. Today's public message concerns road repairs and a light shower.

SERA: The tower is about to play yesterday's bulletin. The playback unit is stuck on the last recording.

DARO: Does it still work?

SERA: It works very well. That is the problem.

SFX: Tower loudspeaker: "Good morning. Today is Tuesday. Expect strong winds after sunset."

ODO: It is Wednesday, and the wind stopped at dawn.

LIO: We have a time-traveling announcement.

NESSA: The controller panel has two buttons: RECORD and BROADCAST. Someone left it on BROADCAST.

SERA: The unit needs a new card. The card is labeled Tuesday. I will remove it and record today's message.

DARO: Can we pause the tower while she changes it?

NESSA: The mute switch is inside the locked panel. Sera has the key.

SERA: I have the key, but the panel is warm. The amplifier is on. I will disconnect power before opening the cabinet.

LIO: The old message continues while we wait.

SFX: Tower loudspeaker: "Please secure loose items."

ODO: That advice is still useful today.

LIO: The forecast says a light shower, not strong winds.

ODO: I will secure my hat anyway.

SFX: Key turns. Power switch clicks; speaker stops.

SERA: The amplifier is off. I can safely remove the old card.

NESSA: I will read the new bulletin while you record. "Wednesday noon: road repairs at the east bridge. One lane is closed until four. Light rain possible after three."

DARO: Add that the library is open for people waiting for the bus.

LIO: That is not weather or road information.

DARO: It is town information.

SERA: The tower has a ninety-second limit. We can record the bridge notice first and put library hours on the community board.

DARO: All right. Keep the bulletin short.

SFX: Record button clicks; indicator buzzes.

NESSA, recorded: Wednesday noon. Road repairs at the east bridge. One lane is closed until four. Light rain is possible after three. Use the south sidewalk.

SERA: The recording is thirty-two seconds. I will check it before broadcasting.

LIO: Can we listen privately?

SERA: Yes. The monitor is on the tower test channel, not the outdoor speaker.

SFX: Recording plays softly in studio.

ODO: It says south sidewalk. That route is open.

DARO: The road crew just called. They closed the north sidewalk too.

LIO: Then the message is incomplete.

NESSA: I will record a correction: use the temporary path behind the library.

SERA: We should confirm the path is clear before saying so.

ODO: I am at the bridge. The temporary path is open and marked with cones. I can see the crossing.

LIO: Confirmed by a caller at the site.

SERA: I will replace the recording, not append it. One message, one current route.

SFX: Record clicks; previous card slides out; new card clicks into the slot.

NESSA, recorded: Wednesday noon. Road repairs at the east bridge. One lane is closed until four. The north sidewalk is closed; use the marked temporary path behind the library. Light rain is possible after three.

SERA: The card is correct. Amplifier power is back on. I am selecting BROADCAST.

LIO: Ready for the noon bulletin.

SFX: Tower chime; then the current message plays outside, clean and once.

DARO: It did not mention the library is open.

LIO: We can put library hours on the community board.

NESSA: The board has a space for today's message. I will pin a separate notice.

ODO: I will secure my hat after all.

LIO: The town has one current bulletin, one old recording removed, and a dry place for the bus wait.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Wednesday's message plays once from the tower; Tuesday's forecast is filed in the archive.
