# The Tractor Pull Has No Tractor

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio county-fair coverage, escalating announcer speculation, and a competition whose main machine has been parked in the wrong field*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Nell, a macaque fair announcer; Tor, a gibbon mechanic; Bobo, an orangutan farmer; and Zia, a capuchin caller.*

[SFX: Fairground crowd, distant brass band, an engine coughs.]

NELL: We are live at the annual Canopy County tractor pull. The crowd is ready, the track is muddy, and the lead tractor is not here.

TOR: It is in the parking field.

BOBO: That's the combine.

TOR: The tractor is behind the feed tent.

NELL: We have three machines, two feed tents, and one long strip of mud. Which vehicle competes first?

BOBO: The small green tractor.

ZIA: The crowd is cheering for the red tractor.

BOBO: The red one belongs to the band.

NELL: Why does the band have a tractor?

BOBO: It carries their instruments.

[SFX: Brass band tests a note; engine revs from the wrong field.]

TOR: The competition tractor is moving. It appears to be heading away from the track.

NELL: Can you guide it here?

TOR: I can signal with a flag.

BOBO: The driver thinks the flag means "start."

NELL: The flag is pointing toward the track.

ZIA: The tractor is now pulling a tent.

[SFX: Canvas strains; crowd gasps.]

TOR: That is the feed tent, not the track sled.

BOBO: The tent is moving better than the tractor.

NELL: We will pause the event while the tent is returned.

ZIA: The driver is turning. The tent is following.

TOR: Stop the engine. Detach the rope.

BOBO: The rope is tied to a hay bale.

NELL: The hay bale is tied to the tent?

BOBO: It was holding the corner down.

[SFX: Engine cuts; canvas drops gently.]

ZIA: The tent is back. The tractor is on the track.

NELL: We can now begin the pull. The sled is loaded with stones.

TOR: Those are apples.

BOBO: The stone cart was sent to the flower show.

NELL: The tractor will pull apples?

BOBO: They are heavier than they look.

TOR: They are bruised.

NELL: The officials have approved the replacement weight. The green tractor is at the line.

[SFX: Bell. Engine revs; tires churn in mud.]

ZIA: It is moving! The sled advances six meters.

NELL: A strong run.

TOR: The driver has tied the sled to the ground stake.

BOBO: It can't move much farther.

NELL: The tractor is pulling the whole contest marker.

[SFX: Stake creaks, then pops loose.]

ZIA: The marker is rolling backward toward the fairground.

TOR: Everyone clear the path.

BOBO: The apples are spilling.

NELL: The crowd is collecting them. We have a new record in apple retrieval.

ZIA: The tractor stops at the track's end. The driver has completed the pull.

TOR: The marker is back in place. The tent is secure. The tractor is parked where we can find it.

NELL: The fair judge awards the green tractor first place for pulling the sled, the marker, and one apple basket.

BOBO: The apples can be sold at the farmstand.

ZIA: The red band tractor gets second place for staying with the band.

NELL: This has been the Canopy County pull. The contest is complete, the feed tent is anchored, and no tractors are missing.

NELL: The second contestant has arrived with a larger tractor. It is pulling a wagon full of pumpkins.

TOR: The rules allow a tractor, not a wagon.

BOBO: The wagon is only carrying the driver.

ZIA: The driver is standing beside it.

BOBO: Then it is carrying a hat.

NELL: The judge has ruled that the hat stays outside the event. The tractor may proceed.

[SFX: Engine revs, tires turn, bell rings.]

TOR: The second machine moved the sled one meter before the mud stopped it.

NELL: The green tractor wins by five meters. The apples have been swept up, the hat is back on its owner, and the track is clear.

[SFX: Crowd applauds; brass band plays beside the parked tractors.]
