# The Final Lap Is Three Laps Away

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: sports radio play-by-play that treats routine amateur contests as championship finales*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
ANNOUNCER TAVI, a macaque
COMMENTATOR ELI, a gibbon
SCOREKEEPER NOMA, a gorilla
RUNNER PIP, a capuchin
VOLUNTEER RON, a langur

[SFX: FIELD AMBIENCE. A WHISTLE, THEN A SINGLE BELL.]

TAVI: Welcome to the annual park loop. Twenty runners will complete one lap and receive a paper ribbon.

ELI: The pressure is immense.

NOMA: It is a community run.

[PIP TIES A SHOE.]

ELI: One athlete is preparing for the moment.

PIP: My lace came undone.

### Scene One: The Start

[SFX: WHISTLE. FOOTSTEPS START, THEN FADE AROUND THE TRACK.]

TAVI: The pack moves out. Pip is in the middle.

ELI: Could this be a strategic decision?

PIP: I am keeping pace with my friend.

NOMA: The course is marked with orange flags.

ELI: The orange flags indicate a crucial turning point.

NOMA: They indicate the turn.

[THE RUNNERS PASS THE FIRST FLAG. A VOLUNTEER CLAPS TWICE.]

ELI: The crowd has erupted.

TAVI: The crowd is one volunteer.

[RON CLAPS AGAIN, ONCE FOR EACH RUNNER. THE MICROPHONE PICKUP MAKES THE CLAPS SOUND LIKE A SMALL DRUM LINE.] 

ELI: The applause is growing.

RON: I am clapping for my sister.

[PIP PASSES THE FLAG WITH THE FRIEND. A WATER CUP IS SET OUT FOR THEM BOTH.] 

TAVI: They are staying together.

NOMA: That is their plan.

### Scene Two: The Turn

[FOOTSTEPS APPROACH A WATER TABLE.]

TAVI: At the halfway marker, Pip slows to take a cup.

ELI: The leader is losing momentum.

PIP: I am thirsty.

[THE CUP SQUEEZES, SPLASHING WATER ON PIP'S NUMBER.]

ELI: A setback.

NOMA: It is a paper number.

[PIP HANDS THE CUP TO THE VOLUNTEER AND CONTINUES. THE RADIO MIXER ADDS A HEARTBEAT.]

TAVI: The sound bed is louder than the runner.

ELI: Can you turn up the finish line?

NOMA: There is no microphone at the finish line.

[FOOTSTEPS GO QUIET AS THE RUNNERS PASS BEHIND THE BUILDING. THE ANNOUNCERS HEAR A DOOR OPEN.]

ELI: They have disappeared from the race.

PIP: We are behind the gym.

TAVI: We can still hear you.

[THE RADIO CREW'S CABLE PICKUP CLIPS A GATE. THE GATE OPENS WITH A CLANK.]

ELI: A dramatic entrance from the course.

PIP: We are at the back of the gym.

NOMA: The course loops behind it. The finish is ahead.

[THE FOOTSTEPS TURN THE CORNER. THE BELL AT THE FINISH RINGS EARLY WHEN A VOLUNTEER TESTS IT.] 

ELI: The bell has announced the winner.

NOMA: It announced the test.

### Scene Three: The Finish

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS RETURN, SLOW AND STEADY. A BELL RINGS ONCE.]

NOMA: Pip crosses the finish. The timer reads twelve minutes.

ELI: A historic finish.

PIP: I finished with my friend.

TAVI: The paper ribbons are on the table.

[ELI ANNOUNCES A PHOTO FINISH. NOMA PLACES THE TIMER ON THE TABLE; IT HAS NO PHOTO, ONLY A TIME.] 

NOMA: There is no tie. Everyone who completes the loop gets a ribbon.

ELI: Then the podium is unnecessary.

[THE CREW TAKES DOWN THE PODIUM. PIP GIVES HIS PAPER RIBBON TO THE VOLUNTEER WHO CLAPPED AT THE TURN.]

PIP: You cheered for all of us.

VOLUNTEER: It was one lap.

[ELI BEGINS TO ANNOUNCE A POST-RACE INTERVIEW. PIP AND THE FRIEND KEEP WALKING TOWARD THE WATER TABLE.] 

TAVI: The interview is moving out of microphone range.

NOMA: The race is finished.

[THE ANNOUNCER LETS THE FOOTSTEPS FADE, THEN FADES UP THE PARK AMBIENCE.]

ELI: The post-race show will not be extended.

### Tag

[SFX: FIELD AMBIENCE. THE ANNOUNCERS ARE STILL TALKING.]

TAVI: The runners are walking home.

ELI: We will stay with this developing story.

NOMA: The park is closing.

[THE MICROPHONE CLICKS OFF.]
