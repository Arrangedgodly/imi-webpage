# The Broadcast from the Supply Closet

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio space mission convention, where a stuck push-to-talk button makes the crew believe the supply closet is transmitting an alien message*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Captain SELA, lemur; engineer KITO, macaque; radio officer PAVA, gibbon; voice of the closet, recorded by ODO, baboon.

[SFX: SHIP HUM. RADIO STATIC.] 

PAVA: We are receiving a voice from an unlisted compartment.

SELA: Identify the source.

PAVA: It says, "More tape, please."

KITO: That is the supply closet.

The crew hears the message again, stretched by static. Sela asks if anyone is inside. Kito says the closet contains repair supplies and a mop. Pava hears a second message: "Careful with the hinge." The closet door clicks once.

[SFX: RADIO PING. METAL CLICK.] 

They approach the closet. Kito opens it carefully. A roll of tape falls out, followed by a small tool pouch. Pava says the signal intensified. Sela asks why a closet would request tape. Kito notices the radio microphone cable is caught in the door.

KITO: The push-to-talk switch is stuck down.

PAVA: We have been broadcasting inside the closet.

A second voice echoes through the ship speakers. It is Odo, recorded earlier, asking for tape. Sela asks him to report to the bridge. Odo answers from the closet itself; he was searching for a wrench when the door closed.

SELA: How long have you been there?

ODO: Since the first message.

Kito frees the cable. The radio goes quiet. Pava asks if the closet has stopped transmitting. Odo knocks from inside. His voice comes through the wall, not the radio.

PAVA: We have a local signal.

Kito opens the door. Odo is holding the wrench and a roll of tape. He says the hinge needs tightening. Sela checks the door and notices the latch catches only when it is pulled hard. Odo tightens the hinge. The door closes gently and opens easily.

Pava asks the crew to run a second test with the closet door held open. Odo stands inside and speaks toward the microphone. His voice is clear. Kito closes the door halfway; the signal becomes muffled. The hinge clicks, and the radio transmits the click as if it were a reply.

PAVA: The door is answering again.

KITO: The cable is clear now.

Sela moves the microphone cable along the wall and secures it with a clip. Odo closes the door gently. The radio remains quiet. He opens it, speaks, and gets a clean response from the bridge.

SELA: No alien. No trapped engineer. One loose cable and one tight hinge.

Odo asks if they should make a warning label for the closet. Pava prints "RADIO CABLES KEEP CLEAR OF DOORS." Kito attaches it at eye level. The crew checks that the label does not block the handle or latch.

Pava asks Odo to repeat the first message for the archive. Odo says, "More tape, please," from beside the microphone. The recording captures it clearly. Sela files it under "Supply Request," not "Transmission from Space." 



The crew tests the radio. Pava presses the switch and speaks. Her voice returns clearly. Odo presses it, but speaks while turned away from the microphone. The signal becomes muffled. Kito angles the mic toward him.

KITO: The microphone is not the closet.

Odo says he was testing room acoustics. Sela asks them to label the push-to-talk button and keep cables clear of the door. Pava makes a short maintenance note and reads it over the radio. The ship responds with a clean confirmation tone.

[SFX: CONFIRMATION PING.] 

PAVA: The signal is understood.

ODO: Can we still call it alien?

SELA: Only in the maintenance log.

They leave the closet open while Odo puts the tools away. The mop leans against the door, holding it at a safe angle. The radio remains silent as the ship hum continues.
