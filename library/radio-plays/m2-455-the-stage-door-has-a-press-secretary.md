# The Stage Door Has a Press Secretary

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: celebrity interviews that turn a performer's late entrance into a carefully managed event*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Sela, a gibbon; actor Vex, an orangutan; publicist Nari, a lemur; stage manager Pip, a capuchin; usher Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Theater lobby, crowd murmurs. A clock ticks. A door latch rattles.

SELA: We are live in the lobby of the Little Lantern Theatre. The star of tonight's play has not arrived.

NARI: Vex is here. He is behind the stage door.

VEX, muffled: I am preparing to enter.

PIP: The door is stuck.

NARI: He is building anticipation.

MARA: He is pulling the handle the wrong way.

SFX: Door handle jiggles. A soft thump.

SELA: Publicist Nari, what can you tell the audience?

NARI: Mr. Vex values a quiet arrival. He asked us not to reveal his costume.

PIP: He is wearing his coat.

NARI: That is his private coat.

VEX, muffled: I am ready.

MARA: The door opens inward.

SFX: Door creaks inward a few centimeters, then stops.

PIP: The costume rack is behind it.

NARI: The costume rack is part of the dramatic entrance.

SELA: It is blocking the door.

VEX: I hear you.

NARI: He has excellent acoustics.

MARA: We should move the rack before someone gets pinched.

NARI: We cannot show the costume before the curtain speech.

PIP: The rack has wheels. We can roll it to the wall.

NARI: If the rack moves, the costume moves.

SELA: The crowd can hear us negotiating.

NARI: That is a preview of the production's themes.

SFX: A wheel squeals. Fabric swishes. Door opens another inch.

VEX: I can see the lobby.

PIP: We need to move the tall hat too. It is caught on the door closer.

MARA: Take it off the rack first.

NARI: No, the hat must arrive with Vex.

VEX: I cannot wear the hat through a door I cannot pass.

NARI: We have a plan. Pip, slide it under the gap.

SFX: Soft felt scrapes across the floor.

VEX: The hat is at my feet.

PIP: Put it on after you step through.

NARI: The publicist's plan has been improved by the stage manager.

SELA: We have a revised arrival.

SFX: Rack rolls. Door opens with a loud squeak.

VEX: I am entering now.

MARA: That is the coat rack entering.

SFX: Rack wheels cross threshold, one wheel bumps the sill.

PIP: Stop. The rack is in the aisle.

NARI: The star is still behind it.

VEX: The rack was my advance party.

SELA: Mr. Vex, can you step around it?

VEX: The camera will see my left side.

NARI: His left side is the side with the costume tag.

MARA: I can unclip the tag.

VEX: Please do not disclose the role.

SFX: A small clip snaps free.

SELA: The crowd has waited six minutes. Should we begin without the star?

NARI: The press release says Vex enters at eight.

PIP: It is eight now.

NARI: The press release is aspirational.

VEX: The stage door is clear. I can enter.

MARA: Your hat is under the door.

VEX: I will take the hat later.

NARI: A brave departure from the plan.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps come through. The crowd applauds.

SELA: Vex has arrived.

VEX: I have not arrived. My coat has.

PIP: You are standing in the lobby.

VEX: The character is still on the other side.

NARI: That is a thoughtful distinction.

SELA: We are out of interview time. What is the play about?

VEX: A door that will not open.

MARA: Did you rehearse this?

VEX: The door chose the material.

NARI: The actor has entered. His hat has not.

PIP: It can have its own understudy.
