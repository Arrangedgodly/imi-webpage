# Gate Three Is a Tree

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: airport-style radio terminal announcements, rolling gate changes, and a flock of birds that claims priority boarding*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-005*

*Cast: Mara, a macaque terminal announcer; Hesh, a gibbon gate agent; Nori, a baboon pilot; and Pippa, a capuchin passenger.*

[SFX: Terminal chime, rolling luggage, distant propeller.]

MARA: Canopy Air passengers for flight 12 to the Orchard, please proceed to Gate Three.

PIPPA: Gate Three is a tree.

HESH: It was a tree. The terminal built a platform around it.

MARA: The gate is now beside the tree.

HESH: The sign still points up.

PIPPA: That is where the gate used to be.

[SFX: Wings flutter; several birds chirp.]

MARA: A flock has gathered on the platform. Are they passengers?

HESH: They are on the passenger list.

NORI: They booked one flight for the whole flock.

PIPPA: How many seats?

NORI: One branch seat. They said they would perch.

MARA: We need to board by row.

HESH: Birds don't have rows.

NORI: They have a formation.

MARA: Flight 12, formation boarding now.

[SFX: Birds shuffle along a railing.]

PIPPA: My boarding pass says Gate Three, seat 4B.

HESH: 4B is in the tree.

PIPPA: Is that a window seat?

HESH: It's an opening between branches.

NORI: I can move the aircraft closer.

MARA: Please don't taxi into the terminal.

NORI: I was going to align the wing with the platform.

HESH: That is still a terminal.

[SFX: Propeller starts, then idles.]

MARA: Flight 12 will board from the lower platform. Passengers should use the ramp.

PIPPA: The ramp leads to a nest.

HESH: The birds reserved it.

MARA: We can assign passengers another gate.

HESH: Gate Four is a shrub.

MARA: Does it have a ramp?

HESH: It has a low branch.

PIPPA: Is the flight delayed?

NORI: We are waiting for the flock to board.

HESH: The flock is already aboard.

MARA: The manifest lists 18 birds and one passenger.

HESH: The passenger is Pippa.

PIPPA: I am standing here.

NORI: We have a no-show passenger in seat 4B.

PIPPA: That is the tree seat.

MARA: Could the seat be occupied by the branch?

HESH: Branches are not passengers.

[SFX: One bird sings three notes.]

NORI: The flock says they are ready.

MARA: Thank you for confirming.

PIPPA: Can I board now?

HESH: Yes. Use the ramp that is not a nest.

PIPPA: There are two ramps.

HESH: The left one goes to the aircraft. The right one goes to the tree.

MARA: Gate Three has been relocated to the lower platform. The flock has boarded, and Pippa is taking seat 4B.

[SFX: Footsteps up ramp, birds flutter, propeller revs.]

NORI: All aboard. Flight 12 is clear for departure.

HESH: The flock is still in the tree.

NORI: They are part of the flight.

MARA: The aircraft is leaving without them.

NORI: They requested an open-air connection.

PIPPA: I can wave to them from the window.

HESH: That's the branch opening.

MARA: The flight has departed. The birds remain at Gate Three for their connecting route.

[SFX: Propeller recedes; birds chirp from the platform.]

MARA: This concludes today's terminal update. Gates may change when trees grow, and birds may board by branch.

HESH: I have moved the gate sign to the ground-level platform and marked the branch as the flock's waiting area.

NORI: The next flight is a regular passenger route. No open-air connection.

PIPPA: My seat is beside the window.

HESH: The window is closed, and the tree is outside it.

MARA: Flight 12 has arrived at the orchard gate. Passengers can exit by the lower ramp.

[SFX: Door opens; steps reach the platform.]

NORI: Everyone is off. The birds may keep their branch, and the terminal has one clear gate.

[SFX: Chime; a flight board clicks to "departed."]
