# The Bandstand's Last Sound Check

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live music broadcasts that keep testing the setup after the audience is already listening*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Announcer Seli, a lemur; bandleader Vex, an orangutan; drummer Pip, a capuchin; singer Mara, a macaque; sound technician Odo, a gibbon.

SFX: Open-air bandstand. Audience settles. A single drum tap.

SELI: This is the final sound check before the evening concert.

MARA: We are already on the air.

ODO: The program slate says "sound check."

VEX: We need to confirm every instrument.

PIP: I checked the drum.

SFX: One tap, then a light rattle.

ODO: The drum is rattling.

PIP: A seed is rolling inside.

VEX: Take it out.

PIP: The drum head is tied on.

MARA: It is a seed rattle, too. We can use it.

ODO: The chart says snare drum.

VEX: We can change the chart.

SELI: The audience is waiting.

VEX: Sound check first. Mara, give us one note.

MARA: We tuned before the doors opened.

SFX: Mara sings a note. Odo's meter peaks.

ODO: Too loud.

MARA: I sang softly.

ODO: The meter is beside the speaker.

VEX: Move it farther away.

SFX: Stand scrapes. The meter squeals with feedback.

PIP: Now it is hearing itself.

SELI: Can we start the concert?

VEX: Not until the monitor is checked.

MARA: The monitor is the speaker that just squealed.

ODO: It needs to face the band.

VEX: It faces the audience.

ODO: The musicians need to hear themselves.

PIP: I can turn it toward us.

SFX: Speaker stand rotates. Feedback stops.

MARA: I can hear the drum now.

PIP: It sounds like a seed in a bowl.

VEX: It should sound like a drum.

PIP: It is a drum with a seed in it.

SELI: We are still live.

VEX: The sound check is part of the program.

SELI: We have reached the time listed for the first song.

SFX: A gust flips the set list. Paper skitters toward the edge of the bandstand.

PIP: I have the drum. I cannot catch the chart.

MARA: Put your foot on that corner.

VEX: The chart is part of the sound check.

ODO: The wind is not listed in the equipment notes.

SELI: It is moving the equipment notes.

SFX: Paper flaps. Mara pins the set list with her shoe while Pip holds the drum steady.

MARA: We can keep the page or keep the beat. Not both.

PIP: Tap the rhythm and I'll hold the chart with the drum rim.

SFX: Pip lays the drum across one corner. A seed rattles under the page.

VEX: That is our count-in.

ODO: The seed is now an approved percussion instrument.

SELI: The chart says the song starts now. The chart is pinned down, so let's obey it.

ODO: The meter still reads high.

MARA: The crowd is clapping.

SFX: A few audience claps, then more. Meter needle clicks.

ODO: The audience is louder than the singer.

VEX: We should ask them to stop clapping until after the song.

SELI: They are practicing for the concert.

MARA: Let's use the claps as the count-in.

PIP: One, two, three, four.

SFX: Drum taps with the audience's claps. The seed rattles in time.

MARA: That's the rhythm.

VEX: We have accidentally made a new arrangement.

ODO: I need to check the meter.

SELI: After the song.

MARA: Sing it.

SFX: Mara sings a short verse. Pip's drum keeps time. Vex joins on a low note. The crowd claps in rhythm. The final chord lands.

ODO: The levels were balanced.

SELI: The audience has the set list now.

VEX: They only heard the first song.

MARA: They clapped the count-in, held the chart, and kept time. They are the backing band.

PIP: The seed gets a solo.

ODO: I will write that into the equipment notes.

VEX: There is no box for audience percussion.

SELI: Draw one. The program is live, and your pen is still in your hand.

SFX: Pencil scratches on paper as the crowd claps once, together.

VEX: We did not test the second verse.

MARA: It will sound like the first verse.

PIP: The seed will still be in the drum.

VEX: Then we have a complete sound check.

SELI: The concert has begun and ended its first song.

ODO: We can mark the check complete.

SFX: Pencil marks the slate. A soft seed rattle.
