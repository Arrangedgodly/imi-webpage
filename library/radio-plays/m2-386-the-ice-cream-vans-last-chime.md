# The Ice Cream Van's Last Chime

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local radio promotions that use a route chime for an advertisement and send customers to a parked vehicle*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Tera, a capuchin; driver Simo, a macaque; dispatcher Leni, a gibbon; caller Omi, a lemur.

[SFX: RADIO JINGLE. A SMALL ICE CREAM CHIME PLAYS TWICE.]

TERA: The blue ice cream van is making its final neighborhood round. Listen for the chime and wave it down.

SIMO: I am parked at the depot. The route ended an hour ago.

LENI: The advertisement is playing the chime recording, but it sounds like the van.

OMI: I heard it on my street and ran outside. The van never came.

TERA: The show promised the last chance for cones.

SIMO: The freezer is back at the depot. There is no ice cream on the route.

OMI: My neighbor says the jingle played twice after the first correction.

LENI: I removed the overnight copy, but the local station cache still has yesterday's ad.

TERA: We need to replace the cached file too, or it will return at the next scheduled hour.

SIMO: The depot broadcast receiver is on the same station. I can test that the van's route chime stays off while parked.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. THE VAN SPEAKER IS SILENT.]

LENI: Cache cleared. The next scheduled message is the corrected one.

SIMO: I will put the actual route chime back on the van only after the morning load.

TERA: The station will play the correction once before the overnight schedule begins.

OMI: I will tell the neighbors the van is parked, and tomorrow's time is still noon.

TERA: The false call ends here, before anyone sets out again.

LENI: The ad was supposed to say tomorrow's route begins at noon.

[SFX: CHIME RECORDING PLAYS AGAIN, FOLLOWED BY A JINGLE.]

OMI: It sounded like it was outside my house.

TERA: The speaker is on the station roof. The chime carries over the neighborhood.

SIMO: Please stop the ad. People are waiting for a van that isn't moving.

LENI: I can cut the chime out and rerecord the time. The route card says noon at the school yard.

TERA: The sponsor wants the chime as their sound mark.

LENI: It can play at the studio after we say it is a recording. It cannot be used as a live route signal.

[SFX: FADER SLIDES DOWN. CHIME STOPS.]

OMI: I can hear the street now. No van.

TERA: We need to correct the announcement before the next loop.

SIMO: Also, the route chime only plays when I press the outside bell.

LENI: The van's speaker has a switch at the depot. Is it off?

SIMO: I'm checking now.

[SFX: VAN DOOR OPENS. SWITCH CLICKS OFF. A FREEZER MOTOR HUMS.]

SIMO: Off. The chime is not coming from the van.

TERA: The ad created a phantom route.

OMI: My neighbor is still standing at the curb with a bowl.

TERA: Tell them the van is at the depot, and tomorrow's route starts at noon by the school yard.

LENI: I'll record that with the chime only after the message.

[SFX: MIC OPENS.]

TERA: Correction: today's ice cream route has ended. The blue van is parked at the depot. The next neighborhood stop is tomorrow at noon beside the school yard. The chime you heard was a recording.

[SFX: MESSAGE ENDS. ONE CHIME PLAYS, THEN CUTS.]

OMI: Now the sound has a label.

SIMO: I'll load the freezer in the morning. The actual chime will sound when the van reaches each stop.

TERA: The sponsor can have its mark after the facts, not instead of them.

LENI: I removed the looping ad from the overnight schedule.

[SFX: BUTTON CLICKS. STATION DISPLAY BEEPS.]

OMI: My bowl is empty.

TERA: You can save it for noon tomorrow.

[SFX: DEPOT DOOR CLOSES. FREEZER HUMS; THE RADIO STAYS QUIET.]
