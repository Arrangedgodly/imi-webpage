# The Wind-Up Toy Repair Line

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: customer-support shows that follow a script instead of listening to the broken device*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Host Pip, a capuchin; repair specialist Nari, a lemur; caller Odo, a gibbon; script supervisor Vex, an orangutan; assistant Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Studio phone rings. Clockwork toy ticks in uneven bursts.

PIP: This is Fix It Live. Caller, what does your wind-up duck do?

ODO: It walks backward and quacks before I wind it.

NARI: How many times have you turned the key?

ODO: I haven't. I opened the box and it started.

VEX: Our first troubleshooting step is to confirm that the toy has power.

MARA: It has a key.

VEX: The script says ask for power.

PIP: Does the key move?

ODO: It moved away from me.

SFX: Tiny wheels squeak backward across a table.

NARI: Don't wind it. Let it stop.

VEX: The next scripted step is to unplug the device.

MARA: It is a duck.

VEX: We can ask if it has a cable.

ODO: It does not. It has a key, which is now under the table.

PIP: The line says "If unplugging does not help, restart the device."

NARI: That would mean winding it.

ODO: I already said it is moving on its own.

SFX: Duck quacks twice, then ticks.

VEX: We should not skip a required step.

NARI: We should ask what the sound means. Odo, is there anything in the duck's beak?

ODO: A little strip of blue paper.

MARA: A feather?

ODO: It came from the box label.

NARI: Pull it gently if the duck has stopped. Do not reach near the gears while they are moving.

SFX: Ticking slows. A small metal click. Silence.

ODO: It stopped.

PIP: We have restored the customer's device.

NARI: We have not found the key.

VEX: The script says the issue is resolved if the sound stops.

MARA: The toy is still walking backward, just not right now.

ODO: I wound it yesterday and it ran forward. Today I opened the box, and it backed out.

NARI: The key is probably inside the winding slot, pressing the gear. The paper strip may be caught in the escapement.

VEX: Our script says not to open the casing.

NARI: The casing has four simple screws and a repairable mechanism. Odo, do you have a small screwdriver?

ODO: I have one from the toy's box.

VEX: We should first confirm the warranty.

ODO: It was my grandmother's. The warranty has expired.

MARA: By several birthdays.

NARI: Place the toy on its back. Remove the four screws. Keep the key out. We will stay on the line while you open it.

SFX: Four screw turns. Wooden shell lifts.

ODO: The key was lodged against a gear. The blue paper is under the spring.

NARI: Lift the paper away with tweezers. Do not pull the spring.

ODO: Done.

SFX: Paper lifts. The mechanism turns once and stops.

ODO: It quacked.

PIP: In which direction?

ODO: Forward.

VEX: The script says to wind it three turns and check the status light.

NARI: It has no light.

ODO: I can turn the key once.

SFX: Key winds one click. Wheels roll forward. Duck gives a clean quack.

ODO: It works.

PIP: We have fixed the problem.

VEX: The next script step is to ask whether the customer has any other devices.

ODO: I have a wind-up frog.

NARI: Does it move?

ODO: It is in the same box.

MARA: Leave it there until the duck is put away.

SFX: Box lid closes. A quiet frog croak from inside.

VEX: That may be a second device.

NARI: It can call tomorrow.

PIP: That was Fix It Live. We diagnosed a stuck key, removed a label strip, and ignored the warranty script.

VEX: We did not ignore it. We confirmed it had expired.
