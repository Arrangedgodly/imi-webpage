# The Lighthouse's Five-Star Fog

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel radio reviews that give places ratings during severe weather and mistake warnings for customer-service problems*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Cato, a gibbon; keeper Rima, a macaque; caller Olli, a lemur; producer Fara, a langur; harbor operator Nima, a capuchin, heard on radio.

[SFX: WIND HOWLS. LIGHTHOUSE FOGHORN BLASTS.] 

CATO: Welcome to Stay or Sail, where listeners rate their overnight destinations. Olli, how was your night at the lighthouse?

OLLI: I did not stay overnight. I am at the quay. The fog is thick and the horn is loud.

FARA: Our travel card says the lighthouse has a peaceful sea view. Ask him for a star rating.

RIMA: I am the keeper. The horn is a warning to boats, not an amenity.

CATO: Olli, did the horn interrupt your sleep?

OLLI: I am awake because I am outside. It is doing its job.

[SFX: FOGHORN BLASTS AGAIN; A ROPE CREAKS.] 

RIMA: The channel is foggy. Boats should stay at anchor until visibility improves.

FARA: We need a usable review. Was the mattress comfortable?

OLLI: I am standing on a wet dock. There is no mattress.

CATO: The map shows guest rooms in the keeper's tower.

RIMA: That is a storage room. The tower is not a hotel.

FARA: But our sponsor paid for the lighthouse getaway segment.

RIMA: The sponsor can pay for a lamp. It cannot make fog into a vacation.

[SFX: RADIO STATIC. A DISTANT ENGINE REVOLVES, THEN STOPS.] 

OLLI: A small boat is trying to leave the outer buoy.

RIMA: Tell them to hold position. I will signal the harbor office.

FARA: The app is trying to post a review automatically. It marked the horn "too loud" and the fog "poor visibility."

RIMA: The horn is meant to be heard. Visibility is poor because there is fog.

OLLI: The app has reviewed the weather as if the lighthouse chose it.

CATO: We can suspend the rating while the harbor is closed.

[SFX: PHONE BUTTON PRESSES; A TINY ERROR TONE.]

FARA: It asks whether we want to rate the error tone.

RIMA: No. Keep the channel open.

CATO: Can we rate the communication?

RIMA: You can listen to it.

[SFX: RADIO MIC OPENS; TWO SHORT CLICKS.] 

RIMA: Harbor office, this is North Light. Fog has closed the channel. The vessel at outer buoy should remain anchored. Over.

[SFX: RADIO CRACKLE.] 

NIMA (ON RADIO): North Light, received. We are calling the vessel. Over.

FARA: The review could say the staff responded quickly.

OLLI: That is a useful review. Put the warning first.

CATO: We have a live safety update, not a hotel score. The channel is closed until the harbor office clears it.

FARA: The star graphic is still on screen.

RIMA: Change it to a closed channel symbol.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS; HORN SOUNDS ONCE.] 

CATO: The lighthouse is not accepting guests. It is guiding boats away from the rocks.

OLLI: That is five stars for the lamp and zero for my imaginary mattress.

RIMA: There is no mattress.

FARA: The audience has submitted a review: "Very loud, could not sleep, kept us from hitting shore." 

CATO: That sounds like the correct report.

[SFX: WIND SOFTENS. RADIO CRACKLE.] 

NIMA (ON RADIO): North Light, visibility is improving. We will reopen the inner channel in twenty minutes. Outer vessel remains anchored.

RIMA: Copy. The light will keep sweeping until the clearance arrives.

CATO: The travel segment is over. We will not invite anyone to rate a warning as an inconvenience.

FARA: I am changing the sponsor card to "Respect the light. Check the harbor notice." 

OLLI: I can wait here. The quay is dry under the shed.

RIMA: Then you have found the actual guest shelter.

[SFX: SHED DOOR CLOSES AGAINST WIND. LIGHTHOUSE LAMP MOTOR TURNS.] 

CATO: North Light is keeping watch. Our rating is one steady beam, and no stars required.

[SFX: FOGHORN SOUNDS; RADIO MIC CLOSES.]
