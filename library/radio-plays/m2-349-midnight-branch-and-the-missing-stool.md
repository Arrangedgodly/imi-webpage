# Midnight Branch and the Missing Stool

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: late-night call-in radio that rewards increasingly dramatic listener confessions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Host Sori, a capuchin; producer Pell, a macaque; caller Mina, a lemur; caller Oru, a gibbon.

[SFX: NIGHT IN AN ORCHARD. CRICKETS. STUDIO MIC OPENS OVER A SOFT THEME.]

SORI: You're listening to Midnight Branch, where listeners share the secret they have kept from the village.

PELL: Our first caller is Mina, who says she has a confession.

MINA: I took the last stool from the packing shed because my aunt needed a place to sit while she sorted pears.

SORI: A stolen stool, hidden in the family shed. A powerful beginning.

MINA: I did not hide it. It's beside her table.

PELL: Our sponsor asks callers to reveal a twist before the music bed swells.

SORI: Mina, is there anything darker beneath the stool?

MINA: It has a cracked rung. I put a cushion on it.

[SFX: PELL FADES IN A DRAMATIC ORGAN CHORD. A STOOL LEG SCRAPES.]

SORI: The cracked rung was a clue.

MINA: It was a safety problem. I tied a bright ribbon around it so no one would sit there.

PELL: The audience likes an unresolved mystery.

MINA: My aunt knows where the stool is. She asked me to bring it back after the pears were packed.

SORI: But did you tell her why you took it?

MINA: Yes. She said thank you.

PELL: We have to keep the line open for the reveal.

SORI: Then let's ask what Mina has never confessed.

MINA: I don't like being asked to make this sound worse.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC FADES OUT. CRICKETS COME THROUGH THE OPEN WINDOW.]

PELL: The sponsor's guide says each call needs a secret, a betrayal, and a dramatic pause.

SORI: Perhaps we can pause without inventing betrayal.

MINA: I can tell you about the packing shed. We are short one stool, so I borrowed it. I should have written that on the board.

PELL: That is a practical note, not a secret.

SORI: It is the reason she called. We should let her finish.

MINA: The board blew away in the wind. I thought Aunt Lela saw the ribbon and knew it was mine.

[SFX: PAPER FLUTTERS INTO A MIC. PELL CATCHES IT.]

PELL: Here's the board. It says stool borrowed for sorting. The wind pushed it under the door.

SORI: That's our twist. The note was here the whole time.

MINA: It was under your microphone?

PELL: It just arrived.

SORI: Mina, do you want to call your aunt on air and tell her you are bringing the stool back?

MINA: She is listening. Auntie, I'll bring it after the shift.

[SFX: PHONE LINE CLICKS. A SECOND CHANNEL OPENS.]

ORU: I'm calling from the cider press. We also borrowed a stool, but we painted it blue so it wouldn't get mixed up.

PELL: A second stool, a second secret.

ORU: It's labeled CIDER. You can read it from the road.

SORI: We are apparently the village's stool information service.

PELL: The audience has sent in three messages asking where the stools went.

MINA: Did anyone call about the missing weighing scale?

ORU: It's on the truck. We secured it after the wind knocked it down.

SORI: Tonight, the orchard has returned one stool, located another, and found a scale on a truck. Midnight Branch is becoming a storage map.

PELL: We can brand that as a special investigative episode.

SORI: We can brand it as the end of the call. Mina has a cracked rung to replace, Oru has a marked stool, and the scale is secured.

MINA: I'll bring my aunt's stool back with the broken rung facing out so nobody sits on it.

ORU: I'll paint a new label on the cider stool.

PELL: Do we need a final confession?

SORI: Yes. I confess I came here for a dramatic secret and got an accurate inventory.

[SFX: SHORT THEME STING. THE CRICKETS CONTINUE; THE STUDIO MIC CLOSES.]
