# The Parcel Scale's Anniversary

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: public radio segments that celebrate a machine's anniversary with a ceremony that disrupts its routine job*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Host Toma, a macaque; clerk Isha, a capuchin; mechanic Velo, a lemur; caller Nemi, a gibbon.

[SFX: POST ROOM. MECHANICAL SCALE CLUNKS. PAPER RUSTLES. RADIO THEME PLAYS.]

TOMA: The parcel scale turns ten today. We will weigh the ceremonial package and announce its service record.

ISHA: I have six parcels in line. The scale is ready for work.

VELO: The anniversary ribbon is tied around the weighing arm.

NEMI: Does the machine get a cake?

TOMA: We have a cake-shaped parcel for the demonstration.

ISHA: That is a real parcel, addressed to the school. It needs a postage label.

VELO: The ribbon is touching the zero lever.

[SFX: SCALE ARM TICKS, THEN STICKS. A PARCEL DROPS ON THE PLATFORM.]

ISHA: It reads three kilos, but the package weighs one.

TOMA: The machine is exaggerating its history.

VELO: It is not reading the load. The ribbon is pulling the lever.

ISHA: We have four more parcels waiting.

TOMA: The ceremony is scheduled before the mail pickup. Can we finish the speech first?

ISHA: If the scale gives the wrong weight, those labels will be wrong.

[SFX: PRINTER WHIRS. PAPER STOPS MIDWAY.]

VELO: The scale feeds the label printer. It is refusing to print because the reading is outside the parcel range.

NEMI: The machine is protesting its birthday.

ISHA: The machine is stuck. I can remove the ribbon.

[SFX: RIBBON PULLS FREE. SCALE ARM CLICKS BACK TO ZERO.]

VELO: Don't weigh anything until I check zero and the calibration weight.

TOMA: Our opening line is "ten years of perfect service."

ISHA: It needs a new line.

TOMA: "Ten years of service, interrupted by a ribbon."

NEMI: That sounds like a better anniversary.

[SFX: METAL TEST WEIGHT SETS ON SCALE. ARM MOVES, THEN SETTLES.]

VELO: One kilo reads one kilo. Zero returns when I lift it.

TOMA: We'll unveil the ribbon and weigh the anniversary package together.

VELO: The ribbon is still against the arm. I have not signed off the scale.

ISHA: The line is waiting. I will move the parcels to the side until the test is done.

[SFX: PARCELS SLIDE INTO A SORTING BIN. PAPER CRINKLES.]

NEMI: The cake-shaped parcel has a return label. The school sent the wrong box.

ISHA: That means we need its true weight before it goes back.

TOMA: The ceremony can wait until the calibration weight reads correctly.

VELO: Correct. The arm is clear now. I can test zero, then one kilo.

[SFX: TEST WEIGHT SETS DOWN. SCALE ARM MOVES AND SETTLES.]

VELO: One kilo. Zero again. It is safe to weigh mail.

TOMA: We will celebrate after the parcel pickup, once the scale has earned its ribbon.

ISHA: The machine is ready. First parcel is the school package.

[SFX: PARCEL SETS DOWN. SCALE CLUNKS. PRINTER STARTS; LABEL FEEDS.]

TOMA: The scale has weighed its cake-shaped parcel.

ISHA: It is a stack of library books, not a cake.

NEMI: The package smells like paper.

TOMA: I see the tag. The school ordered books for the anniversary display.

VELO: The scale is working. The rest of the line can move.

[SFX: TWO PARCELS SET DOWN IN TURN. PRINTER FEEDS LABELS.]

ISHA: One kilo, two kilos. All within range.

TOMA: We should celebrate without tying anything to the arm.

NEMI: I can put the ribbon on the wall behind it.

VELO: Keep it clear of the lever and the platform.

[SFX: TAPE STRIPS RIP. RIBBON IS FASTENED TO WALL. SCALE CLUNKS ONCE.]

TOMA: Ten years, and still weighing parcels correctly.

ISHA: The celebration can happen after the mail pickup.

NEMI: I brought a small cake for the staff.

TOMA: Where is it?

NEMI: In the parcel line. It has no label.

ISHA: Then it cannot go in the mail. We can eat it here after work.

[SFX: POSTAL DOOR OPENS. A CART ROLLS IN.]

VELO: The scale stays free.

TOMA: The machine gets the first slice, in spirit.

ISHA: Machines do not eat cake.

NEMI: The clerk gets the first slice, then.

[SFX: RADIO THEME RETURNS BRIEFLY, THEN FADES UNDER THE NEXT PARCEL WEIGHING.]
