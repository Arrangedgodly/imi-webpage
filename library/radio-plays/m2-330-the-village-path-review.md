# The Village Path Review

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: consumer review programs that rate ordinary public spaces as if they were luxury resorts*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-011*

Cast: Reviewer Miri, a capuchin; path keeper Odo, a baboon; walker Leni, a gibbon; sound producer Tavi, a macaque.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS ON GRAVEL. TAVI PLAYS A GRAND ARRIVAL CHIME.]

MIRI: Welcome to our review of the village path. First impressions: a firm surface, natural shade and an unusually informal check-in.

ODO: You walk onto it.

MIRI: No reception desk?

LENI: There is a bench halfway along.

TAVI: We could record the bench as reception.

ODO: It does not answer questions.

[SFX: A BIRD CALLS FROM THE TREE.]

MIRI: Complimentary music. Promising.

TAVI: That was real. My recording has not started.

MIRI: Start it. We must compare the two services.

[SFX: RECORDED BIRDSONG OVER THE REAL BIRD. THE REAL BIRD STOPS.]

LENI: You have replaced the entertainment with your own cover band.

TAVI: My bird has three additional notes.

ODO: Our bird does not charge for requests.

[TAVI SWITCHES OFF THE SPEAKER. A LIVE CALL RESUMES.]

MIRI: Original performer restored. We will note the interruption.

[SFX: WALKING CONTINUES, THEN A BENCH CREAK AS MIRI SITS.]

MIRI: Reception is sturdy, but one leaf occupies my seat.

ODO: I can move it.

[LEAF BRUSHES AWAY.]

MIRI: Prompt housekeeping. Do you provide refreshments?

LENI: The orchard is at the end.

MIRI: Delivered to the guest?

ODO: Delivered by the tree. You still have to pick them.

MIRI: Self-service dining. I shall inspect it.

[SFX: MIRI STANDS. FOOTSTEPS MOVE TOWARD THE ORCHARD GATE.]

TAVI: I am counting the steps. We can describe the journey accurately.

MIRI: Luxury distance should be expressed in experiences.

LENI: Thirty steps is one experience, if you don't stop for every adjective.

[SFX: GATE LATCH CLICKS.]

ODO: Orchard. End of path.

MIRI: The accommodation ends at a gate. Is there a private room beyond?

LENI: There is a public orchard beyond.

MIRI: And the return shuttle?

ODO: Your feet.

[TAVI PLAYS THE RECORDED FOOTSTEPS BACKWARD.]

MIRI: A reverse shuttle. I can hear it arriving.

LENI: That is my walk, played backward.

TAVI: It sounds more exclusive this way.

ODO: It is taking you away from the microphone.

[TAVI STOPS THE RECORDING. MIRI CHECKS HER SCORECARD.]

MIRI: Four out of five for access, shade, firm footing and reception.

LENI: You mean the bench.

MIRI: One point withheld for absent room service.

ODO: It is a path.

MIRI: Then it could serve its room more efficiently.

[LENI TAKES A PEAR FROM A LOW BRANCH.]

LENI: I have obtained lunch. Would you like one?

MIRI: I cannot accept inducements during a review.

LENI: I will carry it while you finish.

[TAVI STARTS THE CLOSING MUSIC. ITS GRAND ARRIVAL CHIME PLAYS AGAIN.]

MIRI: We appear to have reached a second property.

ODO: Same gate. Wrong sound.

TAVI: I pressed arrival instead of departure.

MIRI: Can departure be grander? We have already arrived once.

[ODO CLOSES THE GATE GENTLY. A SMALL WOODEN CLICK.]

ODO: That is departure.

MIRI: Modest, but precise. The score remains four.

LENI: Shall we walk back now?

[FOOTSTEPS TURN TOWARD THE VILLAGE.]

MIRI: Does the return route have different amenities?

ODO: The view faces the village. The bench is on your other side.

MIRI: A reversible reception desk. That should have been in the brochure.

TAVI: I can make a brochure.

LENI: Please don't stop walking to make it.

[SFX: BIRDSONG AND FOOTSTEPS. MIRI'S STOMACH RUMBLES.]

LENI: The pear is still available.

MIRI: We have completed the review. Thank you.

[THEY STOP BRIEFLY AS LENI HANDS IT OVER. MIRI BITES THE PEAR.]

MIRI: Fresh, crisp, excellent presentation.

ODO: Are you reviewing lunch now?

MIRI: I need another scorecard.

[TAVI UNFOLDS A FRESH CARD. THE BIRD GIVES ONE SHARP CALL.]

LENI: That is its review of the crew leaving.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CONTINUE. NO MUSIC PLAYS.]
