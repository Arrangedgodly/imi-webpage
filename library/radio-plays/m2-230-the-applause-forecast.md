# The Applause Forecast

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live awards broadcast convention, where a host schedules applause as carefully as the ceremony*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-008*

Cast: Host MIRI, macaque; stage manager PAVA, gibbon; award recipient ODO, baboon; orchestra leader KES, capuchin.

[SFX: CEREMONY THEME, APPLAUSE SIGN CLICKING ON.] 

MIRI: Good evening. Our ceremony begins with exactly twelve seconds of applause.

PAVA: The sign is on.

[SFX: APPLAUSE, THEN CUTS OFF.] 

ODO: I was still walking.

MIRI: You may finish walking during the next applause window.

ODO: I have an award to receive.

MIRI: That is scheduled for after the sponsor mention.

KES: The orchestra is ready to play him off.

ODO: I have not spoken yet.

KES: We can play him off preemptively. It saves time.

Pava clicks the applause sign again. The audience claps. Odo attempts to cross the stage, but Kes starts a triumphant chord. Odo bows; the chord stops.


MIRI: The applause window is closed.

Pava hands Odo a card with his allotted speech. It has space for just three lines. He unfolds it, and a second card drops to the floor.

ODO: There is an appendix.

KES: The orchestra is prepared to play the appendix off.

ODO: The appendix thanks the people who made the award possible.

MIRI: The award itself was made by the people who made it possible.

ODO: Then I will thank the award.

PAVA: You cannot thank an object.

ODO: I thanked an envelope.

Miri clicks the sign. The audience applauds while Odo reads the tiny card silently, moving his mouth. Kes tries a soft chord under the applause. The orchestra's music and clapping become indistinguishable.

KES: We have achieved a continuous ceremony.

PAVA: We have achieved a continuous noise.

MIRI: Please save the noise for the finale.

ODO: I haven't reached the microphone.

MIRI: The microphone is reserved for the winner.

ODO: I am the winner.

PAVA: You are the recipient. The winner is the envelope.

Pava opens an envelope, which is empty. The room goes quiet.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLE, ONE COUGH.] 

MIRI: We have a suspense pause.

KES: Is it an intentional pause?

PAVA: It is now.

Odo holds up his award. The plaque is stuck to the envelope by a dab of glue. He pulls; the paper tears, leaving a strip attached to the trophy.

ODO: I won an envelope.

MIRI: Please thank the envelope.

ODO: I would like to thank the committee, the plaque maker, and whoever glued these together.

Kes begins the play-off music. Odo speaks louder over it.

ODO: And I thank the orchestra for allowing my last sentence to have a melody.

KES: That was not your last sentence.

MIRI: We have a strict thirty-second acceptance limit.

ODO: Then I will make the next sentence shorter. The envelope was empty.

PAVA: The result card must have fallen out.

Odo checks his pocket. He finds the card folded behind a program. Pava reaches for it, but the applause sign turns on. The room claps while nobody knows the winner.

MIRI: Applause for the suspense.

ODO: I am still holding the award.

MIRI: Applause for the award.

Odo walks to the microphone. Kes plays a chord. The sign clicks off mid-clap. Odo takes a breath.

ODO: I thank-

The orchestra begins again. Odo waits, then gestures for them to stop.

ODO: I thank the orchestra for stopping.

KES: That line was not on the card.

PAVA: The card says "recipient thanks all present."

ODO: Then all present are thanked. Including the card.

Miri announces the ceremony has concluded. Kes raises his baton for the exit theme, but the audience applause sign flickers on once more. Everyone claps at the empty stage.

[SFX: APPLAUSE, EMPTY STAGE.] 

MIRI: Please hold your applause until the next available winner.

ODO: Can I take the envelope home?

PAVA: It belongs to the ceremony.

ODO: Then I will thank it here.

[SFX: ENVELOPE FOLDS. THEME OUT.]
