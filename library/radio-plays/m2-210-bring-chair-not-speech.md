# Bring a Chair, Not a Speech

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: local radio event previews that turn simple community gatherings into grand civic ceremonies*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-007*

CAST
HOST MIRA, a macaque
ORGANIZER KEL, a gorilla
REPORTER DUN, a capuchin
CALLER ANI, a langur

[SFX: COMMUNITY HALL ROOM TONE. CHAIRS SCRAPE.]

MIRA: We are previewing Saturday's neighborhood book swap. Organizer Kel, what should people bring?

KEL: A book to trade, and a chair if they need one.

DUN: The promo calls this "a historic exchange of ideas."

KEL: It is the monthly book swap.

### Scene One: The Setup

[DUN MOVES A MICROPHONE STAND TO THE FRONT OF THE HALL.]

DUN: We need a podium for the opening speech.

KEL: There is no speech.

MIRA: How will people know where to put the books?

KEL: The tables have signs.

[DUN PLAYS A GRAND FANFARE. A BOX OF PAPERBACKS ARRIVES AND SETS DOWN WITH A SOFT THUMP.]

DUN: The collection has arrived.

KEL: That is the first box.

[DUN ADDS AN ECHO TO THE HALL MICROPHONE. THE BOX SETS DOWN AGAIN, AND THE ECHO MAKES IT SOUND LIKE A CRASH.] 

MIRA: It is a box of books.

KEL: We can put it on the left table.

[DUN TRIES TO ADD A CEREMONIAL RIBBON. THE BOX FLAPS ARE TAPED SHUT.]

DUN: The ribbon cannot open it.

KEL: We can use scissors.

[THE TAPE PEELS. THE BOOKS SLIDE OUT IN A NEAT STACK.]

[ANI CALLS IN.]

ANI: Can I bring cookbooks? I am looking for a mystery novel.

KEL: Yes. Put the cookbooks on the left table.

DUN: A quest begins.

ANI: I will bring a tote.

### Scene Two: The Preview

[MIRA ASKS KEL TO WALK THROUGH THE EVENT. KEL MOVES A BOOK FROM ONE TABLE TO ANOTHER.] 

MIRA: What happens after the doors open?

KEL: People browse, trade, and sit down to read.

DUN: Do we need a ceremonial bell?

KEL: We have a doorbell.

[DUN RINGS THE DOORBELL. IT SOUNDS TWICE AS A VOLUNTEER ENTERS WITH A FOLDING CHAIR.] 

DUN: The first guest has arrived.

KEL: The event is not until Saturday.

[THE VOLUNTEER SETS THE CHAIR BESIDE THE WALL. THE AISLE REMAINS CLEAR.]

[KEL CHECKS THE TABLE SIGNS: COOKBOOKS LEFT, MYSTERY NOVELS CENTER, LARGE PRINT RIGHT.]

MIRA: People can find a place for each book.

DUN: Could we add a map?

KEL: The signs are visible from the door.

[DUN HOLDS UP A GIANT MAP. IT COVERS THE FIRST TABLE. MIRA LOWERS IT SO THE BOOKS SHOW.]

DUN: The books are the map.

MIRA: The signs are enough.

[DUN MOVES THE MICROPHONE STAND TO THE AISLE. KEL MOVES IT BACK AGAIN.]

KEL: People need to carry books through here.

DUN: The microphone is not part of the swap.

[THE FANFARE STARTS, THEN STOPS WHEN MIRA UNPLUGS THE MUSIC PLAYER.]

MIRA: The setup is nearly done.

KEL: We need one more sign for the large-print books.

DUN: The producer asks whether we can call that a literary pavilion.

KEL: We can call it the table with large-print books.

MIRA: The name will fit on the sign.

[DUN TRIES TO UNFURL A LONG BANNER. IT DROOPS OVER THE TABLE. KEL FOLDS IT ONCE AND THE WORDS "BOOK SWAP" SHOW.] 

DUN: That is a shorter title.

KEL: It is also what is happening.

ANI: I am still on the phone. Your directions are easier to follow than the promo.

[DUN ROLLS THE BANNER INTO A TUBE. IT BECOMES A CONVENIENT HOLDER FOR THE EXTRA SIGN.] 

MIRA: The event has its directions.

[DUN ASKS FOR AN AUDIO CLOSE-UP. MIRA RUSTLES THE SIGN BESIDE HIS MICROPHONE, THEN OPENS AN ORDINARY BOOK.] 

### Scene Three: The Event

[SFX: SATURDAY HALL AMBIENCE. BOOKS RUSTLE, CHAIRS SCRAPE SOFTLY.]

MIRA: The book swap is open. People are browsing.

DUN: The exchange has begun.

ANI: I found a mystery novel. The cookbook went to someone who wanted it.

KEL: That is the point.

[DUN PLUGS THE MUSIC PLAYER BACK IN AND STARTS THE FANFARE. THE MUSIC COVERS A VOLUNTEER ASKING WHERE THE LARGE-PRINT TABLE IS. MIRA TURNS THE MUSIC OFF.] 

MIRA: The sign is by the entrance.

[ANI PLACES HER CHAIR AGAINST THE WALL AND OPENS THE BOOK.]

ANI: I will sit and read for a while.

DUN: The gathering has reached its climax.

KEL: It is just beginning.

### Tag

[SFX: ONE PAGE TURNS.]

MIRA: Bring a chair if you need one.

DUN: No speech?

KEL: No speech.
