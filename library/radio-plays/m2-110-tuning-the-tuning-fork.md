# Tuning the Tuning Fork

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio music-instrument repair advice, remote diagnosis, and a tuning fork that keeps revealing the studio's hidden noises*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

*Cast: Coda, a gibbon host; Prill, a macaque repair expert; Nook, a capuchin caller; and Deni, a langur producer.*

[SFX: A pure tone rings, then wobbles.]

CODA: Welcome to Tune-Up, the program that repairs instruments by sound. Tonight's caller says their tuning fork rings crooked.

NOOK: It makes a note, then a smaller note that follows it around.

PRILL: That might be an echo. Strike it once.

[SFX: Fork rings; another faint tone trails after.]

CODA: There it is.

PRILL: Is the fork near a wall?

NOOK: It is in the studio.

CODA: The caller is here? This is a phone-in program.

NOOK: I am in the booth next door. The phone line was busy.

DENI: We were not expecting a live repair.

PRILL: Put the fork on a cushion and strike it again.

[SFX: Tone rings, followed by a muffled second tone.]

PRILL: The table is vibrating. Place it on felt.

NOOK: I don't have felt. I have a banana peel.

CODA: A banana peel is not felt.

NOOK: It is soft.

PRILL: Do not place the tuning fork on the peel. It will slide.

[SFX: Fork strikes, skates across tabletop, and falls into a mug.]

DENI: We have a note in the mug.

CODA: It sounds warm.

PRILL: The mug is resonating. That is not a repair.

NOOK: Could I play the mug?

PRILL: You could, but tonight we are repairing the fork.

CODA: The second note remains. What else is near it?

NOOK: A desk, a mug, and one studio assistant chewing a leaf.

[SFX: A quiet crunch.]

PRILL: There. The sound follows the fork because the assistant is chewing in time.

DENI: The assistant says he is not.

NOOK: He stopped when I looked over.

CODA: We will test the room. Deni, strike the fork. Everyone else remain silent.

[SFX: Tone rings. The room falls quiet. A chair squeaks.]

PRILL: The chair is adding a second pitch.

DENI: I was sitting still.

CODA: Your tail is on the chair lever.

DENI: I am a langur. My tail is on several things.

PRILL: Move the chair. Strike again.

[SFX: Tone rings cleanly.]

NOOK: It's fixed.

PRILL: The fork was never broken. The studio was playing along.

CODA: Let's prove it. I will ring the fork once; each of us names the extra sound as it happens.

[SFX: Fork rings; chair squeaks; fan hums; leaf crunches; ceiling creaks.]

NOOK: I heard a parade.

DENI: I heard the building practicing.

PRILL: I heard five unrelated noises arrive in the same key. That is almost music.

CODA: Deni, strike the fork once more.

[SFX: Fork rings; everyone holds perfectly still.]

NOOK: Clean.

DENI: My tail is hovering over the chair lever.

PRILL: Keep it there. The fork deserves a quiet workplace.

CODA: Our next caller says a drum has a rattle.

[SFX: A tiny rattling from the ceiling.]

DENI: That's the ceiling.

PRILL: Does it rattle when the drum plays?

DENI: It rattles when the drum doesn't play too.

CODA: We cannot repair the whole building by ear.

NOOK: I can make a list of every sound.

PRILL: Please don't. It will become an orchestra.

NOOK: The list begins: tuning fork, mug, chair, leaf chewing, ceiling.

DENI: The fan is humming in B-flat.

PRILL: There are no more repairs tonight.

CODA: We solved our caller's problem. The tuning fork rings true when placed away from the mug, the chair, and the very cooperative chewing assistant.

NOOK: I am taking it home.

PRILL: Keep it in its case. Do not use the banana peel as padding.

[SFX: Case snaps shut; a banana slides across the floor.]

CODA: Tune-Up will return next week, if the fan agrees to stop holding the note.

DENI: I can turn it off.

[SFX: Fan clicks off. Silence. Then the ceiling gives one soft creak.]

PRILL: We'll call that a separate episode.

[SFX: Tuning fork rings once, clear and alone.]
