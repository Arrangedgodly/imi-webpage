# The Studio Door's Automatic Welcome

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio audience programs that treat a door-triggered recording as a live guest greeting*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-014*

Cast: Host Peli, a gibbon; technician Rani, a macaque; producer Toma, a capuchin; visitor Kesi, a lemur.

[SFX: STUDIO DOOR OPENS. A RECORDED VOICE SAYS, "WELCOME, GUEST."]

PELI: Welcome to the open studio, where every visitor gets a live greeting.

KESI: I heard that from the hall before I came in.

TOMA: The door sensor starts the greeting.

RANI: It also starts the phone line, the guest light and the recording.

PELI: That seems efficient.

KESI: It called me "Guest" three times while I was still outside.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. "WELCOME, GUEST." PLAYS AGAIN.]

TOMA: The sensor retriggers if the door moves before it resets.

RANI: The recording is supposed to play once. The contact switch is stuck.

PELI: Could we keep it on for the audience? It makes the studio feel busy.

KESI: It sounds like the room is greeting the door, not me.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES. RECORDED VOICE SAYS, "WELCOME, GUEST." AGAIN.]

TOMA: It played after the door closed.

TOMA: The sponsor wants the greeting to say its name before the guest comes in.

KESI: I did not come here to be introduced to a sponsor.

PELI: I can turn that line off. The welcome should say who is here, not what paid for the door.

RANI: I found a second fault. The switch spring moves, but its electrical contact stays closed. The playback button also sticks when the door shuts.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. RECORDED GREETING PLAYS. BUTTON CLICKS, THEN STICKS.]

TOMA: It started again after the door closed.

RANI: That is the playback button, not the door contact. I will release it and test the spring.

[SFX: BUTTON RELEASES WITH A CLICK. DOOR CLOSES. NO GREETING.]

PELI: One entry, one greeting. Now I can talk to the person.

RANI: The sensor is still stuck in the open position.

PELI: I can welcome Kesi live.

KESI: Please do. I am standing right here.

PELI: Welcome, Kesi. You are our guest tonight.

KESI: Thank you. That is one greeting.

RANI: I will disconnect the recording and test the switch.

[SFX: CABLE UNPLUGS. DOOR SENSOR CLICKS. NO GREETING PLAYS.]

TOMA: The audience light is still on.

RANI: It uses the same stuck switch. We should test each line before we reconnect anything.

[SFX: LIGHT SWITCH CLICKS OFF.]

PELI: The guest line is open. Kesi, can you hear us?

KESI: Yes. I can hear you and I am in the studio.

TOMA: The recording still has the old message. We can keep it for the next visitor if the sensor is repaired.

TOMA: The show archive saved all three false greetings. I will delete those takes so nobody thinks a guest answered.

KESI: My name is still on the guest card.

PELI: I'll read it once before we record. No sensor, no countdown, no door voice.

[SFX: CARD LIFTS. PAPER RUSTLES.]

PELI: Kesi, visitor and guest on tonight's program.

KESI: That sounds like a person, not a door.

RANI: First I will clean the contact and reset the spring.

[SFX: SCREWDRIVER TURNS. A SPRING CLICKS BACK. RANI RECONNECTS THE PLAYBACK CABLE. DOOR OPENS ONCE.]

RANI: Test one. Door opens, greeting plays once, light comes on.

[SFX: "WELCOME, GUEST." ONE CHIME. DOOR CLOSES.]

RANI: Test two. Door closes, recording stops. It does not start again.

TOMA: We should keep the recording at the door, not on the show mic.

PELI: Then the show can greet people by name after they enter.

KESI: The next visitor can hear one welcome, not an automatic conversation.

TOMA: I will label the archive take "door greeting," not "guest interview."

PELI: Good. The door gets a cue; the guest gets a name.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS. RECORDED GREETING PLAYS ONCE. VISITOR FOOTSTEPS ENTER.]

PELI: Welcome to the studio. The recording has finished.

[SFX: MIC CLOSES. DOOR SENSOR CLICKS QUIETLY.]
