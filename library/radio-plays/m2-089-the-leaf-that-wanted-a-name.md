# The Leaf That Wanted a Name

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio nature story and listener poll about naming a fallen leaf*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: STORYTELLER VERO, a slow loris; REPORTER UMA, a macaque; LISTENER NEO, a capuchin; and a fallen leaf.

[SFX: BREEZE THROUGH BRANCHES, ONE LEAF DRIFTS DOWN.] 

VERO: A leaf fell from the broad fig tree and landed beside the path. The leaf had no name, but the radio station opened a poll to find one.

UMA: We have three suggestions: Captain Green, Little Rustle, and Leaf.

NEO: I voted for Leaf.

VERO: Why?

NEO: It is already a leaf.

UMA: The audience expects imagination.

NEO: I can imagine it without renaming it.

VERO: The leaf has traveled from a high branch to the ground. Does it need a title?

UMA: We could call it The Last Leaf of Summer.

NEO: There are green leaves still on the tree.

UMA: It is the last one we noticed.

VERO: That is a different claim.

[SFX: A TINY FOOTSTEP APPROACHES. LEAF RUSTLES.] 

UMA: A listener has arrived at the station with another leaf.

NEO: Is it the same kind?

UMA: It came from a different tree.

VERO: The leaves are not the same object. We should not place them in a contest.

UMA: The poll closes in one minute.

NEO: Who is voting?

UMA: Listeners called, and several sent notes.

VERO: The notes are all in one basket.

UMA: The basket is full.

NEO: That does not tell us which name won.

VERO: Before the poll closes, let's inspect the second leaf. It has a small tear at one edge.

UMA: Perhaps that is why it fell.

NEO: Or the wind moved it.

VERO: We should not decide its history by looking at the tear alone.

UMA: The listeners have sent more names. There is "Duchess of the Path" and "Green Comet."

NEO: The leaf is brown.

UMA: It was green once.

VERO: It does not need a title to be interesting. It changed color, fell, and is now useful to a bird.

UMA: The bird has returned.

[SFX: WINGS FLUTTER, TWIGS SHIFT.] 

NEO: It brought the first leaf to the nest.

UMA: The second leaf is still here.

VERO: The bird is choosing what it can carry. We can leave the other leaf where it is.

UMA: The poll results are close. Captain Green has four votes. Little Rustle has four. Leaf has five.

NEO: Then the simple name won.

UMA: Should we announce a winner?

VERO: We can announce what happened, but the leaf was not competing.

NEO: It did not ask for a name.

UMA: The listeners did.

VERO: Then tell them the poll says "Leaf" and ask them to notice one on their walk tomorrow.

UMA: That's the entire conclusion?

VERO: It is enough to notice something without owning it.

[SFX: PAPER POLL IS FOLDED. THE SECOND LEAF MOVES A LITTLE IN THE BREEZE.] 

NEO: It is still here.

VERO: For now.

[SFX: PAPERS SHUFFLE, THEN A SINGLE BIRD CALL.] 

UMA: The station has received a new message. A bird says the leaves are good nesting material.

VERO: It did not suggest a name.

NEO: The bird has given them a use.

UMA: Do we give the leaf to the bird?

VERO: Only if it can reach it. Place it near the branch, and let the bird choose.

[LEAF LIFTED. WINGS FLUTTER, THEN A TINY TUG.] 

UMA: The bird took the first leaf and left the second.

NEO: The poll has two results.

VERO: One leaf was gathered. One leaf stayed by the path.

UMA: What shall we call the segment?

NEO: "A Leaf Was Here."

VERO: That is accurate and leaves room for the next one.

[SFX: THE SECOND LEAF RUSTLES IN THE WIND, THEN SETTLES.]
