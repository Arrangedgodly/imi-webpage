# The Mapmaker's Voice Through Stone

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio adventure serials that use staged cave echoes as reliable directions*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Narrator Kes, a macaque; surveyor Leni, a gibbon; guide Ravo, a capuchin; editor Tulu, a lemur.

[SFX: CAVE DRIPS. BOOTS ON STONE. A RADIO MIC OPENS.]

KES: In today's adventure, Leni follows the voice of the cave and discovers the hidden spring.

LENI: I am at the fork. My map marks a spring to the north, but the passage has turned.

RAVO: The cave doesn't speak. Your radio is echoing through the repeater.

TULU: The audience expects a voice from the rock.

KES: The old recording says, "take the left passage."

LENI: That is the same line you recorded at the entrance. It doesn't tell me which way is north.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS. "TAKE THE LEFT PASSAGE" RETURNS TWICE, EACH TIME MORE DISTANT.]

RAVO: Those are reflections off the chambers. The repeater plays one message, then the cave returns it.

TULU: It sounds like the cave is answering.

LENI: It sounds like the same direction twice. I need the survey marks.

[SFX: FINGERS BRUSH STONE. CHALK SCRATCHES.]

LENI: The white triangle is on the left wall. The map key says triangles mark measured turns.

RAVO: The survey team put them at each turn, not at every branch.

KES: Can we keep the "cave voice" line? It's the best moment in the episode.

LENI: Not if I follow it and reach the wrong passage.

TULU: The microphone is on the right channel. The return loop is feeding your voice back into the cave speaker.

RAVO: I can disconnect the speaker here. The ranger line will stay live.

[SFX: SWITCH CLICKS. THE REPEATED VOICE STOPS. DRIPPING CONTINUES.]

LENI: I hear only the drip now.

RAVO: The sound comes from the lower chamber. That could be water, or it could be condensation.

LENI: The map has a second marker. I can compare the distance from the first triangle.

[SFX: MEASURING TAPE UNWINDS. A METAL HOOK CATCHES ON STONE.]

LENI: Twelve meters. The map says the spring is sixteen meters from the fork.

KES: The adventure narrator is prepared to reveal the spring.

LENI: Not yet. The passage turns twice. I need to mark the route as I go.

TULU: The editor has a line that says the cave whispers "north."

LENI: We don't know north from a whisper. We know it from the compass.

KES: The line is already in the script.

TULU: I can cut it. The audience will hear the switch, though.

RAVO: Leave the small edit click in. It tells them we corrected the story.

[SFX: EDITOR CLICKS ONCE. A SHORT PAUSE.]

LENI: The map shows the marked turn. We will follow the dry passage and measure each branch.

RAVO: The left turn is dry. The right turn has a narrow ledge.

LENI: The map shows the narrow ledge on the south side. My compass says south is behind me.

TULU: The compass sound effect is ready.

LENI: It has a needle. It doesn't make a sound.

[SFX: COMPASS CASE OPENS. NEEDLE CLICKS AGAINST THE GLASS.]

RAVO: It is stuck against the case. Tilt it flat.

[SFX: CASE TAPS AGAINST PALM. NEEDLE SWINGS AND SETTLES.]

LENI: North is toward the dry passage. The chalk triangle matches. We'll take that path.

KES: Then the voice was not the direction.

LENI: The map is. The voice was a recording.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FOLLOW A NARROW PASSAGE. DRIPS GROW LOUDER.]

RAVO: The spring is ahead. I can hear water running, not dripping.

LENI: Found it. The map's mark is right where the stream enters the basin.

TULU: Could the cave repeat "found it"?

LENI: No. Record the stream as it sounds.

[SFX: WATER RUNS INTO A STONE BASIN. NO VOICE RETURNS.]

KES: Our adventure ends at the spring, by compass, chalk and measured steps.

LENI: And a radio with its echo disabled.

[SFX: RADIO CLICKS OFF. STREAM CONTINUES.]
