# The Night Museum Broadcast Hears Its Own Bell

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: overnight museum radio, security suspense cues, and staff who mistake a playback test for a visitor*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Daro, a gibbon; guard Miri, an orangutan; sound engineer Tavi, a macaque; curator Oko, a baboon; caller Nessa, a capuchin.

SFX: Museum at night. A bell rings once, followed by a soft echo.

DARO: We are live from the museum after hours. A bell has rung in the closed gallery. Guard Miri, are we dealing with a visitor?

MIRI: I am at the gallery door. It is locked. The motion sensor is green, and the bell sounded once.

OKO: The bronze bell is behind glass. Nobody has touched it since the afternoon tour.

TAVI: The broadcast microphone is picking up the exhibit speaker. The sound may have come through the audio guide system.

DARO: That would be a very small ghost.

MIRI: I am not calling it a ghost until I check the corridor.

SFX: Footsteps on stone. Keys jingle. A second bell rings through the speaker.

MIRI: No one in the corridor. The gallery door is still locked.

TAVI: The second bell came from the sound desk. I pressed TEST when I opened the broadcast board.

DARO: Why does the test sound like it is behind us?

TAVI: The gallery speaker and studio monitor share a return feed. The bell test is being played in the gallery and fed back to us.

OKO: The security log recorded two bell rings.

MIRI: It records the sensor event, not the audio. The sensor did not move.

DARO: Our first clue is no one moved.

TAVI: Our first clue is the speaker. Please stop describing every cable as a suspect.

SFX: Tavi turns a dial. The bell sound ends. A low hum remains.

OKO: The exhibit speaker is off now, but the hum is still in the room.

MIRI: I can hear it near the painted portrait.

DARO: The portrait is not connected to electricity.

TAVI: The humidifier is. Its fan turns on every fifteen minutes to protect the canvas.

OKO: That timer is supposed to be silent.

SFX: Hum grows louder; a paper label flutters.

MIRI: The fan is pushing air through a loose vent cover. The cover is tapping the wall.

DARO: A clue with a beat.

OKO: The portrait is vibrating.

TAVI: It is not. The frame is steady. The label is fluttering in the vent draft.

MIRI: I am not entering the gallery until Oko confirms the fan can be shut off safely.

OKO: The timer has a manual pause. I am turning it off for one cycle, not disabling humidity control.

SFX: Switch clicks; hum stops. The paper label falls flat.

DARO: The room is quiet.

MIRI: Now I can hear a third sound: a phone ringing in the coat room.

OKO: It is my phone. I left it with my coat.

TAVI: Is it connected to the bell system?

OKO: No. It is playing my alarm called MUSEUM BELL.

DARO: Why did you name your alarm that?

OKO: I was on the night shift when I set it. I wanted a sound I would notice.

MIRI: It worked. I noticed it from the gallery door.

SFX: Oko silences the phone. No alarm follows.

DARO: For listeners joining late: no intruder, no haunted painting, and no bronze bell moved. We have one test playback, one vent cover, and one curator who named a phone alarm after an exhibit.

OKO: I will rename it LUNCH.

TAVI: That might be even more alarming.

MIRI: The security log can now be corrected. It shows zero motion events and one scheduled speaker test.

DARO: We have our night report. Curator, will you replace the vent cover before opening?

OKO: Yes. I will secure it, then run the humidifier cycle while Miri listens from the hall.

SFX: A screwdriver turns twice. The vent cover seats firmly. The fan starts for one quiet test; no tapping.

MIRI: Clear. The portrait remains still.

DARO: And the bell?

TAVI: Disconnected from the studio monitor. The gallery announcement channel is separate now.

OKO: The bell is ready for visitors at ten.

DARO: This has been Night at the Museum Radio. We will not test the bell again on air.

SFX: The station plays one archived bell note; the actual bronze bell remains behind glass until opening.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The gallery remains closed overnight; the bell rings once at opening, on schedule.
