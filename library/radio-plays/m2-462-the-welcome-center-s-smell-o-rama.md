# The Welcome Center's Smell-O-Rama

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: travel programs that sell sensory gimmicks instead of telling visitors where to go*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-016*

Cast: Guide Nari, a lemur; host Odo, a gibbon; visitor Pip, a capuchin; center manager Vex, an orangutan; caretaker Mara, a macaque.

SFX: Visitor center door opens. A small fan whirs. Glass jars clink.

ODO: Welcome to the valley visitor center. Today we test the new Smell-O-Rama tour.

VEX: Each jar represents a destination. Pine for the ridge, damp soil for the garden, and ripe fruit for the orchard.

PIP: Where is the map?

VEX: The map is in the experience.

NARI: The map is on the wall behind you.

PIP: I can't smell a direction.

ODO: First, inhale the ridge.

SFX: Lid twists. A small breath.

PIP: It smells like a wooden cabinet.

MARA: The pine jar is empty.

VEX: It held a pine cone this morning.

NARI: The pine cone is at the trailhead.

PIP: Then the trailhead smells like no pine cone.

VEX: That is an authentic scent.

ODO: What is our next destination?

NARI: The garden. It is through the side door, past the sign.

NARI: Could the labels name the places, instead of promising that every jar smells the same?

VEX: A clear label would take the surprise out.

PIP: I would rather know which door to use than smell a surprise.

MARA: We can keep a sample and add a small map card to each lid.

ODO: That is a second product.

NARI: It is the information visitors asked for.

VEX: Please sample the soil.

SFX: Jar opens. Pip sniffs once, then sneezes.

PIP: The soil smells like the garden.

MARA: The jar came from the garden.

VEX: The visitor is responding to the exhibit.

NARI: He is sneezing.

ODO: Can we lower the fan?

VEX: The fan spreads the scent.

SFX: Fan speeds up. Lids rattle. A loose paper map skitters across the floor.

NARI: Turn that off before every jar empties.

SFX: Fan stops. Jar lids settle.

PIP: I can follow the map.

VEX: The tour is scent-led.

PIP: Then open a fruit jar.

SFX: Lid twists. Seeds rattle.

PIP: This one smells like bananas.

MARA: The orchard has apples.

VEX: The fruit jar is from the snack room.

NARI: Visitors need a true guide. We can give them directions first and let them smell samples after.

ODO: But the show promised an immersive route.

PIP: I can walk with my eyes closed.

NARI: Don't. There are chairs between here and the door.

PIP: I smelled the chair just now.

MARA: That was the polished wood.

VEX: Perhaps we can label the chair as the furniture stop.

NARI: It is the waiting room. The tour does not include it.

ODO: Guide, take us to the orchard using only the sign.

NARI: Door to the hall. Turn left at the blue map. Follow the path past the fountain. The orchard gate is the one with apple blossoms painted on it.

PIP: I found the hall door.

SFX: Door opens. Footsteps. A sign creaks.

PIP: Map, fountain, blossom gate. I'm there.

VEX: Did you use the scent?

PIP: I used my eyes.

MARA: That's why we put up signs.

NARI: The smell jars can stay as samples beside the map. We will replace the pine cone and label the apples from the orchard.

VEX: Could we keep the name Smell-O-Rama?

PIP: As long as the map is the main attraction.

ODO: The tour is one minute long.

NARI: Then visitors can spend the rest of their visit where they wanted to go.

SFX: Visitors enter. Map unfolds. Jar lids open one by one.

MARA: The orchard sample is fresh.

PIP: The pine jar is still empty.

VEX: We have a refill station.

NARI: It's outside.

TAG

SFX: Fan starts again for one second, then clicks off.

PIP: The building just sneezed.
