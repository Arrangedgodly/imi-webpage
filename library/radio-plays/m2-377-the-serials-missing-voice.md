# The Serial's Missing Voice

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio drama recordings that replace an actor's line with a sound effect and pretend the character intended it*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Director Vela, a gibbon; actor Dori, a capuchin; editor Ketu, a macaque; announcer Mazi, a lemur.

[SFX: RECORDING STUDIO. A DOOR SLAMS. TAPE REWINDS.]

MAZI: Episode twelve of The River Key begins with Captain Lumo entering the station.

DORI: My line is "The key is in the locker."

VELA: You said "the boat is in the locker."

DORI: The cue card says key.

KETU: The door slam covered the first word. We can record the line again.

VELA: The sound mix has a loud splash after it. Maybe the captain is joking.

DORI: I did not make a joke. I read the card.

[SFX: TAPE PLAYS: DOOR SLAM, "THE BOAT IS IN THE LOCKER," SPLASH.]

MAZI: It sounds as if the station is underwater.

KETU: The splash cue is from the next scene.

VELA: The script jumps from the station to the river. We can crossfade.

DORI: The listener will think the locker is flooded.

KETU: The editor sent the splash on the wrong track. I'll move it after the next line.

[SFX: TRACK FADER SLIDES. SPLASH MOVES LATER.]

VELA: Let's record Dori again. Keep the door slam, then the correct line.

DORI: The key is in the locker.

[SFX: DOOR SLAM. DORI: "THE KEY IS IN THE LOCKER." TAPE STOPS.]

KETU: Clear. The splash belongs after Captain Lumo opens the hatch.

KETU: The sound library has five versions of the station door.

DORI: The current one is a studio door, not the old brass gate in the script.

VELA: We don't need to change the door sound. It is a radio serial, and the audience can follow the dialogue.

MAZI: The transcript also has the wrong word. I'll update "boat locker" to "key locker" before release.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. A PRINTER FEEDS A CORRECTED TRANSCRIPT.]

KETU: Audio and transcript now agree.

DORI: Then the scene can stay a scene, not a puzzle.

VELA: We have another take where the actor says "boat." Use the clean one.

DORI: I want to hear it with the next scene before you lock it.

MAZI: That is not the usual recording process.

DORI: The last episode said the key was a boat. The mix did that too.

KETU: I'll play the full transition.

[SFX: TAPE PLAYS. DOOR SLAM. "THE KEY IS IN THE LOCKER." PAUSE. A HATCH CREAKS. SPLASH.]

VELA: That works. The key is stored in the station locker, then the next scene moves outside.

VELA: The continuity sheet calls the key "a silver boat key."

DORI: It is brass, not silver. The audio says key, and the inventory says brass.

KETU: We will keep both records consistent.

KETU: I'll correct that too. The prop list says brass locker key.

MAZI: The episode recap calls it the "boat locker."

DORI: The recap is wrong too.

KETU: I can replace that word in the recap track. The rest of the episode stays.

[SFX: SMALL RECORDER CLICKS ON.]

MAZI: Last time on The River Key: Captain Lumo found the boat locker.

DORI: Stop. "The key locker."

[SFX: RECORDER STOPS. TAPE REWINDS A FEW SECONDS.]

MAZI: Last time on The River Key: Captain Lumo found the key locker.

VELA: Good. No sound effect over that phrase.

KETU: The recap now matches the scene.

DORI: And I am credited for the new line.

MAZI: The credits already list you.

DORI: The updated take needs a take number.

KETU: Take three, clean dialogue. Saved.

[SFX: KEYBOARD TAPS. FILE BEEPS.]

VELA: Episode twelve can go to mix.

MAZI: The boat has not been in the locker at all.

DORI: Correct.

[SFX: DOOR SLAMS ONCE FOR THE FINAL MIX. NO SPLASH UNTIL THE HATCH CREAKS.]
