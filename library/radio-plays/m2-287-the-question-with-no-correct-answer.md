# The Question With No Correct Answer

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio quiz shows, trick questions, overconfident buzzers, and producers who protect a sponsor's misleading wording*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-010*

CAST
HOST NARA, a lemur
CONTESTANT TAVI, a gibbon
CONTESTANT ODO, a baboon
JUDGE SAVA, a macaque
PRODUCER VEX, an orangutan

[SFX: QUIZ SHOW THEME, BUZZERS.]

NARA: Welcome to the Canopy Quiz. Today's prize is a brass telescope. Our first question: which direction is the river?

ODO: Downhill.

TAVI: East.

NARA: The answer is east.

SAVA: The river bends. At the bridge it runs east, then south.

VEX: The answer card says east.

SAVA: The question asks which direction is the river, not at the bridge.

NARA: We will accept the card.

ODO: I answered downhill.

TAVI: That can also be true.

VEX: Please keep the contest moving.

[SFX: BUZZER.]

NARA: Question two: name a sound that is silent.

TAVI: A written note.

ODO: A sleeping drum.

SAVA: The question is contradictory.

VEX: The answer card says "a whisper."

SAVA: A whisper is sound.

NARA: We have a tie.

ODO: I buzzed first.

TAVI: The buzzer sounded after I raised my hand.

[SFX: BUZZER DOUBLE-TAPS.]

VEX: The sponsor has provided a tie-breaker question. "How many sides does a square have?"

ODO: Four.

TAVI: Four.

VEX: Incorrect. The sponsor's square has five sides.

SAVA: Then it is a pentagon.

VEX: It is a square-shaped product display.

NARA: A display is not a square.

ODO: Is this quiz about geometry or branding?

VEX: It is about the telescope.

NARA: The contestants are tied, and the prize has not moved.
 
Sava asks each contestant to restate the question before answering. Odo summarizes it as "name a silent sound." Tavi points out that the question asks for a sound that is silent, so the wording defeats the answer.

SAVA: The host should withdraw the question.

VEX: That would leave a gap in the show.

NARA: We can fill it with a properly worded question.

ODO: What is something written that can be read aloud?

TAVI: A note.

SAVA: That is fair.

[SFX: BUZZER.]

NARA: Correct. The buzzer has made a sound.

VEX: The sponsor requested a tie-breaker.

SAVA: The tie-breaker must be answerable.

Odo offers the telescope to the library rather than choosing between them. Nara asks the contestants to share it, but the sponsor insists the prize photo include the mascot.


Sava asks to inspect the answer cards. One is printed on the back of a sponsor flyer. The geometry diagram shows a square beside a folded corner, making five visible edges.

TAVI: The corner is folded.

ODO: The shape has four sides when the paper lies flat.

SAVA: We have found the correct answer.

VEX: The card says five.

SAVA: The card is folded.

[SFX: PAPER FLATTENS.]

NARA: The square has four sides.

VEX: The sponsor's display has five.

NARA: That was not the question.

The producer whispers that the telescope can be awarded to whoever answers a final question. Sava insists the wording be checked before it goes on air.

VEX: Which animal has the longest tail?

ODO: The ring-tailed lemur.

TAVI: Some monkeys have longer tails.

SAVA: It depends on how you measure.

NARA: The answer card says "the sponsor's mascot."

ODO: That is not an animal.

VEX: It is an illustrated animal.

TAVI: Then award the prize to the illustration.

The studio laughs. Nara cancels the final question and awards the telescope to the town library, where the contestants can use it together. Vex objects that the sponsor wanted a winner.

SAVA: We have three winners and one telescope.

ODO: We can take turns.

TAVI: That is not a quiz result.

NARA: It is a clear rule.

[SFX: THEME MUSIC RETURNS.]

VEX: The sponsor asks that the library display the mascot.

SAVA: The mascot may be on the flyer, if the paper lies flat.

NARA: This has been the Canopy Quiz. All answers are provisional until the question is clear.

[SFX: BUZZER, THEME FADES.]
