# The Cooking Contest's Oven Has a Timeout

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: competitive cooking broadcasts that treat a cooling oven as a rival contestant*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; baker Mara, a macaque; contestant Pip, a capuchin; judge Odo, a gibbon; runner Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Community hall kitchen. Timer buzzes once; oven door opens carefully.

VEX: The oven has timed out, and the final loaf is trapped.

MARA: The timer ended the bake. Pip is checking the loaf with a thermometer before taking it out.

PIP: The crust is golden. Center temperature is not high enough yet.

ODO: Contest rules allow five more minutes. The timer can be reset once.

VEX: The oven is sabotaging the contestant.

NARI: It is still warm. No one should touch the rack with bare hands.

MARA: Pip, put the loaf back for the extra five minutes. I will restart the timer.

SFX: Rack slides into oven; door closes; timer button clicks to five minutes.

VEX: A dramatic setback.

PIP: A temperature check. I won't guess based on the color.

ODO: The other contestants are finished. The judging table is clear, and no one will serve until all loaves cool.

VEX: But the crowd is hungry.

MARA: The contest is judged on the finished bread, not on who serves first.

NARI: I can label each cooling rack with the contestant's number, so no loaf gets swapped.

PIP: My number is four. The loaf stays on rack four.

VEX: This oven has become an obstacle.

MARA: It's a tool. Keep the camera away from the hot door.

NARI: Another contestant says Pip got extra time because the oven favored his loaf.

ODO: The same rule applies to every contestant whose center reads below range.

PIP: I asked for the extra time after the first check. I didn't restart the bake or change the ingredient list.

MARA: The oven did not choose. The thermometer gave a reading, and the published rule gave Pip five minutes.

VEX: Can the oven testify?

MARA: No. The judge can compare the temperature log and the contestant's card.

ODO: Rack four stayed labeled after its first check. No loaf was moved between racks.

NARI: I will record this extension on the judge's sheet while the timer runs.

PIP: My crust is golden, but I will wait until the cooling period is done before the judge cuts it.

VEX: The audience wants the reveal now.

MARA: The bread needs the same cooling time as the others. The judge can wait.

SFX: Five-minute extension timer ticks; oven fan hums.

NARI: The timer is running. Every contestant can hear it.

PIP: Rack four is marked, and I have not changed the recipe.

VEX: Could we give the loaf a dramatic countdown?

MARA: A countdown is fine if it measures the bake, not the audience's patience.

ODO: I will taste the loaves in number order after this one cools.

VEX: The oven has one final chance to impress us.

PIP: It is heating the bread. The bread will do the impressing.

SFX: Oven fan hums steadily under the last minute of dialogue.

SFX: Five-minute timer rings; oven switch clicks off. Mitts lift rack four onto the marked cooling grid.

ODO: The extension is complete. Pip, make one final temperature check.

PIP: The center is in range. The loaf stays whole while it cools.

MARA: Start the shared cooling clock. All entries will be tasted at the same time.

VEX: Another clock? This kitchen has more clocks than judges.

NARI: One measures baking. One measures cooling. Neither awards points.

SFX: Cooling clock ticks, then rings once. Knife slices each numbered loaf in turn; tasting plates slide across the table.

ODO: Having tasted every entry, I award the bread round to Pip. His center is cooked and his crust is the crispest.

SFX: Vex cues a triumphant fanfare; the crust crackles over it.

ODO: The loaf supplied its own sound effect.

PIP: The single extra five minutes did it.

VEX: I appeal to the oven.

NARI: It is off and has no microphone.

MARA: Record the score and bring out the serving plates.

TAG

PIP: I hope the audience likes the crust.

VEX: The oven has no vote.
