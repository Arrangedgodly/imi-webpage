# The Donor Plaque Has a Witness

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: investigative broadcasts that turn a smudged museum plaque into a conspiracy*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Host Vex, an orangutan; registrar Mara, a macaque; conservator Odo, a gibbon; guide Pip, a capuchin; caller and visitor Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Museum lobby. Low public-room murmur; a cloth brushes stone.

VEX: Our investigation begins with a plaque whose donor name has vanished.

MARA: The name is covered for cleaning. The plaque is not missing a line.

ODO: The conservator applied a removable paper mask before polishing the brass. It will come off when the surface is dry.

PIP: The tour group is waiting by the sculpture. They can hear us from here.

NARI, on phone: I heard the museum erased its benefactor. Is the collection at risk?

MARA: No. The donor record is in the archive. The public plaque is being cleaned.

VEX: The public has a right to know who gave the statue.

ODO: The record says three donors, not one. We will display the approved wording after the polish is done.

VEX: Three suspects.

MARA: Three names in a donor line. There is no case.

SFX: Cloth wipes; soft metallic squeak.

PIP: The paper mask lifted at one corner. I can hold the edge while Odo finishes the approved polish.

ODO: Please do not touch the brass. The mask is there to keep cleaner off the engraved letters.

VEX: Someone may have tampered with the mask.

MARA: The corner lifted because the cloth caught it. It is still attached on the other side.

NARI: If the name is not a secret, why cover it?

ODO: The polish leaves a residue until it dries. The cover protects the lettering during that interval.

VEX: A temporary cover concealing a permanent record.

MARA: Yes. That is exactly what conservation work is.

Odo completes the polish, then waits. Pip asks whether the tour can begin; Mara says the group will enter after the plaque is dry. Vex tries to remove the mask himself.

MARA: Stop. The conservator will lift it when the surface is ready.

VEX: I am protecting the truth.

ODO: You are about to smudge wet polish.

SFX: Paper settles back; no tearing.

PIP: The group has moved to the gallery entrance. We have a few minutes.

NARI: Can you read the donor record on air while we wait?

MARA: We can read the approved public text, not private correspondence. The plaque lists the Greystone family, who donated the sculpture together.

VEX: The mystery has three names and a shared act of generosity.

NARI: I'll end the call and come to the gallery. I want to see the plaque after the cleaning is finished.

SFX: Phone line clicks off; gallery door opens; lobby footsteps join the tour group.

NARI: I'm here at the gallery entrance. I'll wait behind the marked line with the visitors.

ODO: Surface is dry, but the mask remains attached. I will lift it after the group clears the corner.

NARI: What should visitors do while the plaque is covered? They have already walked across the lobby to see it.

MARA: The sculpture is still on display. Pip can lead them through the gallery from the opposite side and explain the artists' work while the mask stays on.

PIP: I'll use the printed tour card. It lists the sculpture's material and the year it was installed.

VEX: Will the audience miss its unveiling?

ODO: The cover is not for dramatic effect. It protects the plaque during cleaning.

SFX: Gallery door opens; a small group enters.

PIP: Welcome. The donor plaque is masked until the conservator removes the paper. The sculpture itself is ready to view.

VISITOR NARI: I can see the names through the gap.

MARA: Please don't lift the paper. Odo will remove it when the group clears this corner.

VEX: The suspense is unbearable.

PIP: The sculpture's story is more interesting than a piece of paper.

MARA: Take the group to the next display. We will reopen this corner when Nari is in position and the visitors have cleared the plaque.

SFX: Group footsteps move to the next display; Pip returns to the plaque as the gallery door swings shut.

ODO: The surface is dry. I am lifting the paper mask from the loose edge.

SFX: Paper peels cleanly.

PIP: The brass letters are visible. No scratch, no missing name.

VEX: My investigation has concluded.

MARA: The cleaning has concluded. Odo, place the used mask in the waste bin and update the conservation log.

SFX: Used paper crumples into the bin; pencil writes in the log.

TAG

VEX: Three donors. One plaque.

MARA: And no witness called to testify.
