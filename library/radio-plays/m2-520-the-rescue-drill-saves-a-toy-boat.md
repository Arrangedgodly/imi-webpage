# The Rescue Drill Saves a Toy Boat

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: emergency broadcasts that make practice sound like a live disaster*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-018*

Cast: Captain Mara, a macaque; announcer Vex, an orangutan; trainee Pip, a capuchin; dispatcher Odo, a gibbon; observer Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Harbor training dock. A buoy bell taps twice. Water laps against the boards.

VEX: This is Harbor Alert. We have a vessel drifting beyond the yellow marker.

MARA: This is a scheduled drill. The vessel is a toy boat, and no person is in the water.

PIP: The toy's string snapped at the knot. It has passed the near marker and is moving toward the reeds.

ODO: Wind from the west. Current is moving east along the dock. The skiff is tied off and our boundary rope is secure.

VEX: Should we sound the full harbor siren?

MARA: No. The siren means a real emergency. Use the practice bell once, and tell listeners it's a drill.

SFX: Hand bell rings once.

SELI: Practice drill in progress. Trainees remain on the dock behind the yellow line. No one enters the water.

VEX: The boat is approaching the reeds. We need a dramatic launch.

MARA: We need a reach pole. Odo, unlock the long pole from the shed. Pip, keep both feet behind the line.

SFX: Lock turns. Metal pole slides free from its hooks.

PIP: The ring is facing away from me.

MARA: Lower the hook into the water beside it. Don't chase the mast; the ring is made for this.

SFX: Pole tip touches the boat. A hollow plastic tap.

VEX: Contact! The rescue is underway.

SELI: The hook missed. The boat is now touching a reed stem. The pole is still in Pip's hands, and Pip is behind the line.

PIP: I can reach the ring if we first pull the boat a little toward open water.

MARA: Correct. Don't yank. Odo, call the movement from the dock edge.

ODO: Current still east. The reed is bending but not broken. Ease the pole left, then let the current bring the stern toward you.

SFX: Pole slides through water. Reed leaves brush the toy hull.

PIP: The ring is turned toward the hook now.

MARA: Hold the pole steady. Catch the ring, then draw it along the boards, slowly.

SFX: Hook catches. Water ripples. The boat glides toward the dock.

VEX: The vessel has changed course under skilled command.

SELI: The toy is beside the dock. Pip has not crossed the yellow line.

PIP: I have the ring. I'm lifting it onto the boards now.

SFX: Plastic boat lifts; water drips onto the dock.

MARA: Recovery complete. No one entered the water, and the skiff remained tied.

VEX: Can we call that a rescue?

MARA: Call it a successful drill. A rescue announcement would make people think someone was in danger.

ODO: The practice log says 10:14, west wind, east current, reach pole used, no entry into water.

PIP: The string knot frayed where it rubbed against the ring. I'll put the toy in the drying tray.

SELI: Check the ring for a bend before the next launch.

SFX: Ring taps the boards. It gives one clean, even click.

PIP: Straight. I'll leave the old string in the repair bin and wait for the replacement at the workbench.

VEX: The audience has heard a full harbor operation, and the toy boat is home.

MARA: It is a drill result, not a disaster. We can reset after the new string is tied.

SFX: Clipboard closes. A bell taps once, softer than before.

TAG

VEX: Harbor Alert: the next practice begins at two.

MARA: And if the toy boat drifts, we use the pole from the dock.

SELI: The actual emergency is that Vex has been calling the drying tray a dry dock.

VEX: It is a very small dry dock.
