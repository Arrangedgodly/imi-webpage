# The Chess Club Final Calls Its Moves Backward

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: radio sports commentary, notation mix-ups, and players who must correct the announcer without revealing a strategy*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Commentator Lio, a gibbon; arbiter Mara, a macaque; player Daro, an orangutan; player Niko, a capuchin; recorder Vex, a baboon.

SFX: Quiet club room. Chess clock clicks; a glass of water sets down.

LIO: We are at move twenty-three of the town chess final. Daro has a knight on f six and Niko is considering the queen's side.

MARA: The board is turned toward the players. The audience is hearing the moves, not seeing them.

VEX: The live notation screen shows Niko moved the bishop to c four.

NIKO: I have not moved yet.

DARO: I did. My bishop is on c four.

LIO: The screen reversed the player names.

MARA: The notation operator selected the wrong clock. Daro made the move, and Niko's timer is running.

VEX: I can correct the screen without changing the clocks.

LIO: Please do. The commentary has just given Niko a move he did not make.

NIKO: I would like to keep it.

DARO: You cannot claim a move because the radio said it.

NIKO: I am asking the arbiter.

MARA: The board position is unchanged. Daro moved bishop to c four. We will correct the record and restart Niko's clock.

SFX: Clock button clicks; screen tone.

VEX: Names corrected. Daro is to the left, Niko to the right. The clocks show Daro at six minutes, Niko at five.

LIO: Now the audience knows who has the advantage.

MARA: Time remaining is not the same as advantage.

DARO: It is if I use mine wisely.

NIKO: I am thinking.

LIO: Niko's hand is hovering over the queen.

MARA: The microphone is live. Please do not narrate a player's unmade move.

LIO: I will describe the clock instead.

SFX: Clock ticks, slow and regular.

VEX: The notation has frozen again.

LIO: What move did the screen show?

VEX: It shows a knight taking a rook. Neither piece moved.

MARA: That is the replay buffer from yesterday's demonstration. It is not live notation.

NIKO: Can you turn it off?

VEX: I can switch to the board camera and clear the replay window.

LIO: The match will continue while we wait.

MARA: We must not interrupt play. The clocks continue; players may stop only if there is a technical issue affecting the match.

DARO: My opponent's move appeared on the screen before he made it.

MARA: That is a technical issue. I am pausing both clocks.

SFX: Two clock buttons click; room quiets.

VEX: Replay is off. The live camera shows the board. I will read back the last two moves from the paper scoresheets.

MARA: Daro: bishop to c four. Niko: pawn to e six. The board matches both sheets.

NIKO: My clock was five minutes.

VEX: It is five minutes, eight seconds. We paused it at the right moment.

MARA: Resume Niko's clock. Daro made the last move.

SFX: Clock resumes; chair creaks.

DARO: I will offer a draw.

NIKO: I decline.

LIO: We have a decision.

MARA: No. A draw offer is not a move. The game continues.

NIKO: I move my rook to d one.

VEX: Recorded: rook to d one. The live board confirms.

LIO: Niko has made a move. Daro is thinking.

NIKO: I offer a draw.

DARO: I accept the draw.

NIKO: I accept.

MARA: Both players have agreed to a draw. The match ends level, and the final position is recorded.

VEX: I am closing the replay system and printing the notation.

LIO: Was the match backward?

MARA: The screen was backward. The moves were not.

DARO: Can the broadcast announce the draw in both directions?

NIKO: Only if the result stays the same.

SFX: Handshake; clock clicks off.

FINAL SIGNOFF: The final is a draw, the notation is corrected, and no one gets a free move from yesterday's replay.
