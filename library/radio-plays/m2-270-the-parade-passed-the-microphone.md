# The Parade Passed the Microphone

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: live parade radio coverage, sponsor floats, and announcers who identify every passing sound as the grand marshal*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-009*

CAST
HOST TAVI, a macaque
ANNOUNCER LEX, a gibbon
MARSHAL BENA, a capuchin
DRUMMER NOL, a ring-tailed lemur

[SFX: CROWD CHEERS, PARADE DRUMS APPROACH.]

TAVI: We are live at the Canopy Day parade. The first float is approaching our broadcast stand.

LEX: It is tall, colorful, and carrying the words "Welcome to Canopy Day."

BENA: That is the welcome float. Please do not block the route.

TAVI: We are at the official radio stand, outside the route.

[SFX: WOODEN WHEELS RUMBLE.]

LEX: The float is passing us now. Its lanterns are bright, its ribbons are green, and its rear wheel is making a squeak.

NOL: That is my drum cart. It is behind the float.

TAVI: The parade has a second rhythm.

LEX: The grand marshal is waving.

BENA: That is a child on the curb.

LEX: The grand marshal has a smaller hat than expected.

TAVI: We have another float, sponsored by the Canopy Seed Co-op.

[SFX: SEEDS RATTLE IN TIN.]

LEX: A sound of abundance. The float is rolling toward us.

BENA: It is not moving.

TAVI: The sound is rolling toward us.

NOL: That is my cart. A seed tin fell off.

LEX: The tin has become a rolling parade unit.

BENA: Stop it before it reaches the street.

[SFX: TIN ROLLS, FOOTSTEPS RUN, TIN CAUGHT.]

TAVI: The tin is safe. The co-op float remains at the corner.

LEX: A delay in the pageant.

BENA: The driver is waiting for the drum line to clear.

NOL: We are next in the order. My cart wheel is squeaking because the axle is loose.

TAVI: Is that safe?

BENA: We are stopping the cart to tighten it.

LEX: The parade has paused for a mechanical solo.

NOL: It is not a solo. I need a wrench.

TAVI: Our engineer has one.

[SFX: WRENCH TURNS. WHEEL TESTS, SQUEAKS ONCE.]

NOL: The wheel is secure.

LEX: The drum line advances. The rhythm approaches the microphone.

[SFX: DRUMS GROW LOUDER, THEN PASS.]

TAVI: The sound is moving away now.

LEX: The grand marshal is waving again.

BENA: That is the seed co-op driver, signaling the line to move.

LEX: The marshal has a steering wheel.

TAVI: We will correct that in our notes.

BENA: The last unit is the banner team. They carry the parade name between them.

[SFX: FABRIC FLAPS.]

LEX: The banner reads Canopy Day, but the wind is turning it around.

BENA: Banner team, rotate it before the finish line.

TAVI: They have turned it. The name is visible again.

LEX: We have seen the front and back of the parade.

BENA: There is no back. The route continues around the park.

TAVI: The crowd is following the music along the path.

LEX: A whistle has sounded. Is that the next unit?

BENA: That is the crossing guard letting a family cross the route.

LEX: The parade has paused for a family crossing.

TAVI: The band has stopped until the family reaches the other side.

[SFX: NOL'S HANDHELD RADIO CLICKS ON FROM DOWN THE ROUTE.]

NOL: My drum cart wheel is quiet now. I can hear the birds above the route.

BENA: The crossing is clear. The parade is moving again.

[SFX: DRUMS RESUME, FOOTSTEPS AND CHEERS FOLLOW.]

LEX: The entire crowd has rejoined the parade.

TAVI: Our broadcast stand is behind the final banner now.

LEX: The parade has passed the microphone.

NOL: I can roll the repaired cart home.

TAVI: The route is clear, the seed tin is recovered, and the banner is facing forward.

LEX: The grand marshal waves one last time.

BENA: That is the child again.

LEX: The child is following the last banner past our stand.

TAVI: From Canopy Day, this is the end of live coverage. The parade continues around the park; our microphone stays here.

[SFX: DISTANT DRUMS, CROWD FADES.]
