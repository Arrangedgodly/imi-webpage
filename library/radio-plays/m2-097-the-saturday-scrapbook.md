# The Saturday Scrapbook

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: family-history call-in broadcasts and sentimental listener dedications*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-004*

Cast: host Vela, a gibbon; caller Jori, a macaque; caller Mett, a capuchin; researcher Pali, a baboon; caller Senn, an orangutan.

[SFX: SOFT PIANO THEME. PAPER PAGES TURN.]

VELA: Welcome to The Saturday Scrapbook, where listeners share the stories behind their family photographs.

PALI: We have one photograph.

JORI: I am holding it.

METT: I have the same photograph.

SENN: So do I.

VELA: A family mystery.

JORI: It is a picture of our grandpa.

PALI: The label says "Uncle."

METT: Grandpa was also my uncle.

VELA: The family tree is already branching.

[SFX: PIANO SWELLS. A PHONE RINGS.]

### Act One

VELA: Jori, tell us what you remember.

JORI: Grandpa sat by the river and carved spoons.

METT: He carved one spoon.

JORI: He carved several.

SENN: I have one in my kitchen.

PALI: The archive lists a spoon maker named Mett's grandfather.

METT: That's the same monkey.

VELA: We have found a connection.

PALI: The dates don't match.

JORI: The dates on the photo are wrong.

VELA: A second mystery.

SENN: The photo was taken in spring.

METT: It was taken in winter.

JORI: Look at the tree.

PALI: The tree has leaves.

JORI: It is an evergreen.

VELA: The season is undecided.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLES. PENCIL SCRATCHES.]

PALI: I can enlarge the photograph.

VELA: Can we enlarge a memory?

PALI: I can enlarge the image.

METT: The image shows a spoon.

SENN: It shows Grandpa holding a bowl.

JORI: The spoon is behind the bowl.

VELA: The family is uncovering details.

### Act Two

VELA: We have another caller.

SENN: I am still here.

VELA: A new caller.

[SFX: PHONE SWITCH CLICKS.]

MIRA, O.S.: My name is Mira. I have the original negative.

PALI: Can you describe it?

MIRA: The photograph was taken at the picnic.

JORI: There was a picnic?

MIRA: There is a basket in the background.

METT: That could be a toolbox.

SENN: It has a handle and a cloth.

PALI: The picture is reversed.

VELA: That changes the story.

MIRA: Grandpa was not holding the bowl. He was handing it to his brother.

JORI: He had a brother?

SENN: He had three.

METT: The family tree has widened.

PALI: The archive has one sibling listed.

MIRA: The other two are in the photo.

VELA: Can we identify them?

MIRA: One is my mother.

JORI: Your mother is Grandpa's sister?

MIRA: My mother was Grandpa's daughter.

PALI: The photo is reversed, not the family.

VELA: We have a correction.

[SFX: PIANO THEME STOPS. A SOFT CHIME.]

### Act Three

VELA: Let's look at the picture again.

[SFX: PHOTO PASSES HAND TO HAND.]

JORI: The one holding the spoon is my great uncle.

METT: The one holding the bowl is Grandpa.

SENN: The one behind them is their sister.

PALI: The photo has three people and four labels.

VELA: Who wrote the labels?

JORI: Grandpa did.

METT: He was the one with the spoon.

SENN: He wrote "Uncle" on the back for the person in the photo.

PALI: The label traveled with the picture.

VELA: The family has solved the photograph.

MIRA: The picnic basket was mine.

JORI: You were there?

MIRA: I was a baby.

METT: That would explain why you don't remember.

SENN: Do you still have the spoon?

MIRA: I have it here.

[SFX: WOODEN SPOON TAPS ON A BOWL.]

VELA: The sound is familiar.

JORI: Grandpa used to tap the bowl before serving.

PALI: The archive has a new recording.

METT: We can label it correctly.

SENN: "Grandpa's spoon at the picnic."

VELA: And the photograph?

MIRA: "The family, passing the bowl."

PALI: That is accurate.

JORI: We should send everyone a copy.

VELA: The family archive is complete.

[SFX: PRINTER WHIRS. PAPER SLIDES OUT.]

SENN: It printed the picture upside down.

PALI: Turn it around.

[SFX: PAPER FLIPS.]

VELA: The family is right-side up.

### Tag

[SFX: PIANO THEME RETURNS. WOODEN SPOON TAPS ON A BOWL.]

VELA: Next week, we learn who owned the bowl.

METT: It was Grandpa.

JORI: We already know.
