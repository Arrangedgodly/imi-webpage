# The Ferry's Last Recorded Departure

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: transport announcements that keep replaying a departure long after the ferry has docked*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-012*

Cast: Dispatcher Edda, a macaque; announcer Varo, a gibbon; deckhand Kiri, a capuchin; passenger Sol, a lemur.

[SFX: HARBOR WATER. FERRY ENGINE IDLES. A PA ANNOUNCEMENT PLAYS: FINAL DEPARTURE IN FIVE MINUTES. THEN A BELL.]

SOL: The ferry is tied up at the dock. Why does it say final departure?

KIRI: It arrived twenty minutes ago. We are unloading the last crate.

VARO: The schedule file is labeled final departure. I thought it was today's closing message.

EDDA: That file is from last night. Today's ferry has another run at dusk.

VARO: The panel says scheduled message. It doesn't show the date.

SOL: I can read the paper timetable from here. It says we docked at the west pier.

KIRI: And the recording says final departure from the east pier. It is giving directions to a boat that is not here.

EDDA: Keep the passengers on the dock side until the live message is clear.

KIRI: It also plays the bell before the words. People are lining up to board again.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS TURN TOWARD THE GANGWAY. A CART WHEEL RATTLES.]

SOL: I was about to get off.

EDDA: Nobody board. The boat is secured and unloading.

VARO: I can make a live announcement.

EDDA: Use the hand mic. The schedule is locked until the dock office confirms the file.

[SFX: PA CLICKS. A LITTLE FEEDBACK SQUEALS.]

VARO: Attention, passengers. The ferry has arrived.

[SFX: RECORDED MESSAGE CUTS IN: FINAL DEPARTURE IN FIVE MINUTES. BELL SOUNDS TWICE.]

KIRI: The automated message is still running over you.

EDDA: The override button is labeled hold, not stop. Press and hold it.

[SFX: BUTTON PRESSED. ANNOUNCEMENT FADES. ENGINE IDLES.]

VARO: Attention, passengers. The ferry is docked. Please leave by the landward gangway. The next departure is at dusk.

SOL: That's clear. I'm going ashore now.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS CROSS THE GANGWAY. A CART WHEEL RATTLES IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION.]

KIRI: The crate is on the dock. The rope is clear. We can close the loading door.

EDDA: Before we resume the panel, let's check why it chose last night's recording.

VARO: The list shows last departure at the top.

EDDA: That's the file label, not the time. The date field is on the second screen.

[SFX: BUTTONS CLICK. DISPLAY BEEPS TWICE.]

VARO: Date: yesterday. Repeat setting: daily.

KIRI: So it replayed the closing notice today because someone left daily repeat on.

EDDA: We need to cancel that repeat and select the arrival message. The dusk departure stays scheduled.

VARO: I can do that with the dock-office key.

EDDA: I have it. The control is inside this panel, not on the PA console.

[SFX: SMALL KEY TURNS. PANEL OPENS. A SWITCH CLICKS OFF.]

KIRI: The old message is disabled.

EDDA: Select arrival, date today, one play only.

VARO: Saving. The console says confirmed.

[SFX: TWO BEEPS. A SHORT ARRIVAL ANNOUNCEMENT PLAYS: FERRY AT DOCK. PLEASE EXIT TO THE LANDWARD GANGWAY.]

KIRI: That is what is happening now.

EDDA: We should not test it again while people are moving.

VARO: Agreed. The next scheduled announcement is at dusk.

[SFX: A DISTANT HORN SOUNDS. ENGINE REVS SLIGHTLY, THEN RETURNS TO IDLE.]

SOL: From the shore: I heard the correct message. I am off the boat, and the walkway is clear.

KIRI: Crate is in the marked bay. Gangway is ready to lift.

EDDA: Raise it after the passengers are clear.

[SFX: ONE LAST SET OF FOOTSTEPS CROSSES. THE GANGWAY WINCH TURNS AND LIFTS; A PIN SLIDES INTO PLACE.]

VARO: The ferry has arrived. No departure is scheduled until dusk.

EDDA: That's the live status. The file can stay quiet now.

[SFX: PA SWITCH CLICKS OFF. WATER LAPS AGAINST THE HULL; THE ENGINE IDLES WITHOUT AN ANNOUNCEMENT.]
