# The Repair Cafe Fixes a Broken Clock

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: makeover and restoration shows that value a reveal over whether an object works*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Vex, an orangutan; clock owner Mara, a macaque; repairer Odo, a gibbon; assistant Pip, a capuchin; producer Seli, a lemur.

SFX: Repair cafe. Clock ticks irregularly, then stops.

VEX: Today we reveal a clock transformed beyond recognition.

MARA: I brought it because it loses ten minutes every hour.

ODO: The pendulum is rubbing the case.

PIP: I can hear it scrape.

MARA: The clock has stopped completely. Can you show me what to watch if it starts losing time again?

ODO: Listen for the pendulum to strike the case. The tick should be even.

PIP: We can make a mark on the paper dial at the same time as the kitchen timer.

SFX: Pencil marks a small line on a scrap card; timer clicks.

VEX: The card is not part of the reveal.

MARA: It's a simple check. If the marks drift, I'll bring the clock back.

ODO: Don't force the pendulum if it stops. Call the repair cafe.

VEX: That sounds like aftercare.

SELI: It is. The show should explain what happens after the camera leaves.

SFX: Pendulum swings. Tick-tock continues.

PIP: The mark is still aligned.

MARA: I'll take the clock home carefully, upright in its box.

ODO: I have packing straw. Keep it from swinging during the walk.

VEX: Could we wrap it in the gold cloth?

MARA: Use plain cloth. I need to see the hands when I get home.

VEX: We should sand the case and paint it gold.

MARA: I want it to keep time.

SFX: Odo opens clock case. A screw drops into a tray.

ODO: The pendulum hook is bent. The case is sound.

VEX: Could we add a larger face?

ODO: That won't fix the hook.

Seli: We have a reveal wall ready.

VEX: Great. Cover the clock while Odo repairs it.

MARA: Don't cover the open case. The screw is in the tray.

SFX: Cloth rustles. Tray slides away from the table edge.

PIP: I caught it.

ODO: Thank you. I need the small wrench.

PIP: It is beside the clock key.

VEX: The key could be part of a dramatic before-and-after shot.

MARA: The key winds the clock. Keep it nearby.

SFX: Wrench turns. Metal gives a small click.

ODO: The hook is straight now. I'll swing the pendulum by hand.

SFX: Pendulum swings. Tick, tick, tick.

PIP: It sounds even.

MARA: Let's compare it with the kitchen timer.

SFX: Timer clicks on. Clock ticks alongside.

VEX: This is not visually dramatic.

ODO: The clock is keeping pace.

VEX: Can we reveal it now?

MARA: It needs to run for a while before we know.

SFX: Chair scrapes. A pause as clock ticks steadily.

SELI: Ten minutes have passed. The timer and clock still match.

ODO: The repair holds.

VEX: The case is still brown.

MARA: It belongs to my grandfather. I want it to look like itself.

PIP: We can dust it and return the key.

SFX: Cloth wipes wood. Key turns; clock begins again.

PIP: The case doesn't rattle when the pendulum swings.

ODO: That's a good sign. The hook clears the wood by a small margin.

MARA: The clock used to stop whenever the door slammed.

SELI: The new hook gives it room to move.

VEX: We could add a soft-close hinge to the house door too.

MARA: Fix the clock we have, then let me decide what the house needs.

VEX: The transformation is complete.

MARA: The clock is fixed. That's enough.
