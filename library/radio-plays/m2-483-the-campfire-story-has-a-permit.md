# The Campfire Story Has a Permit

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: outdoors adventure programs that turn simple activities into compliance spectacles*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-017*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; ranger Odo, a gibbon; camper Pip, a capuchin; producer Vex, an orangutan; night listener Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Forest clearing. Crickets. A small metal fire bowl clinks.

MARA: Tonight we bring you a campfire story from the woods.

ODO: There is no fire yet. We need to check the permit.

VEX: Make the permit sound suspenseful.

PIP: I have the permit. It says one small contained fire.

ODO: The bowl is on bare stone, away from dry leaves.

NARI: I hear a twig snap.

VEX: That could be a creature.

PIP: It's the firewood bundle settling.

MARA: We need three sticks. The permit says keep the flame below the rim.

ODO: We can do that.

SFX: One match strikes. Small flame catches.

VEX: The blaze rises.

PIP: It is below the rim.

VEX: The audience needs peril.

MARA: Tell the story while Odo watches the bowl.

PIP: My story is about a squirrel who hears a noise outside his burrow.

SFX: Soft rustle beyond the clearing.

NARI: That was a real rustle.

ODO: The leaves are moving in the wind. The fire is contained.

VEX: This is perfect. Is the squirrel brave?

PIP: He wants to look, but he knows his burrow is safe.

MARA: What does he do?

PIP: He takes a lantern and asks his neighbor to come with him.

VEX: A buddy system? Good. The suspense builds.

SFX: Lantern handle creaks. A second set of steps approaches.

NARI: Someone is coming through the trees.

ODO: It's the ranger's assistant with the water bucket.

VEX: Don't identify the figure yet.

MARA: The fire needs another stick.

ODO: No. The flame is already at the permitted height.

PIP: Then the squirrel can decide not to go outside.

VEX: But the story hasn't reached the frightening part.

PIP: The frightening part is when he hears his own tail brush the leaves.

SFX: Pip's tail sweeps across dry leaves. He startles.

MARA: That's you, not the squirrel.

PIP: I know. The sound was convincing.

ODO: We should put the lid nearby in case the wind picks up.

SFX: Metal lid set beside bowl.

VEX: The story's hero could use that lid as a shield.

PIP: He could put it over the fire when he's done.

NARI: The breeze is stronger.

SFX: Leaves rustle. Flame flickers.

ODO: Time to close the bowl.

MARA: The story can end with the squirrel choosing a safe place to listen.

PIP: He returns home, puts the lantern by the door, and sleeps while the wind tells the rest.

ODO: The permit asks for a final cold check before we leave.

MARA: The bowl is covered. The ashes are contained.

PIP: I can feel the outside of the bowl. It's cool enough to carry.

VEX: That is not a dramatic ending.

NARI: It is a safe one, and the audience can hear the difference between a story and a real fire.

SFX: Odo lifts lid slightly, checks, then settles it again.

ODO: No flame, no glowing edge. We're clear to leave the clearing.

MARA: Pip, take the lantern. I'll carry the bowl.

PIP: The squirrel's lantern can light the path home.

VEX: Keep the sponsor mention for the next episode.

SFX: Metal lid settles over fire. Flame goes out with a soft hiss.

VEX: The episode is over. The danger has ended.

ODO: The permit is still valid.

NARI: The clearing is dark now. We should take the permit and the water bucket with us.

ODO: I have both.

PIP: And I have the lantern. The squirrel knows the path.

MARA: Then this campfire story can finish with everybody walking home.
