# The Complaint Department

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: municipal radio service desk for tiny neighborhood disputes*
*By: IMI Audio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-003*

Cast: CLERK DOVA, a macaque; SUPERVISOR RIM, an orangutan; CALLER PEA, a squirrel monkey; and CALLER TOLL, a baboon.

[SFX: HOLD MUSIC PLAYED ON A SMALL XYLOPHONE.] 

DOVA: You have reached the Fern Hollow service desk. For path concerns, press one. For branch concerns, press two. For concerns about someone pressing one, please remain on the line.

RIM: We have three pending cases and one listener who has been on hold since breakfast.

DOVA: I can take the calls now.

RIM: Read the disclaimer first.

DOVA: This call may be recorded for training, archived for municipal memory, and replayed to the branch if the branch requests it.

RIM: Branches cannot request things.

DOVA: Then they won't object.

[SFX: LINE OPENS.] 

PEA: The path to the water dish has a twig across it.

DOVA: Is the twig blocking all travel?

PEA: No. You can step over it.

RIM: Then classify as minor obstruction.

PEA: I am small. It is a large twig.

DOVA: We can send a crew to assess its scale.

RIM: A crew requires a form.

DOVA: I can file an urgent form.

RIM: The forms are in the cabinet behind you.

[DRAWER OPENS. PAPERS SLIDE OUT.] 

DOVA: We have Form 8, fallen twig. Form 8B, twig that may be a branch. And Form 8B-1, branch witnessed falling by a twig.

PEA: Could you just move it?

RIM: We must identify the appropriate authority.

DOVA: I am the appropriate authority if it is a twig.

RIM: And if it is a branch?

DOVA: You are the supervisor.

RIM: I am also the one who put it there.

[PAUSE.] 

PEA: Why did you put it there?

RIM: I was marking the route to the water dish.

DOVA: You blocked the route while marking the route.

RIM: This is how the municipality learns.

[SFX: SECOND LINE OPENS.] 

TOLL: Someone has moved the bench one handspan to the left.

DOVA: Is anyone unable to sit?

TOLL: No, but I have been looking in the old place.

RIM: That is a complaint about memory.

DOVA: We do not have a form for memory.

RIM: We could open one.

DOVA: Then we'd need a memory of where the form is.

TOLL: Can you move the bench back?

RIM: The bench was moved because a root grew under one leg.

TOLL: Then leave it. I will look to the left.

DOVA: We have resolution by citizen adjustment.

RIM: Record that phrase. It sounds expensive.

RIM: Before we close the case, the service desk has to notify the path users.

DOVA: There are three people on the path. I can tell them.

RIM: We need the public notice.

DOVA: The notice printer is jammed.

RIM: Can you file a printer ticket?

DOVA: The printer ticket form is stuck in the printer.

PEA: I can hear all this from the path. The twig is moved.

RIM: Please confirm whether the new route is accessible.

PEA: I just walked it to tell you the route is clear.

DOVA: That is field verification.

RIM: We need a signature.

PEA: I am holding a water dish. I cannot sign.

DOVA: A voice confirmation is acceptable for a minor obstruction.

RIM: Since when?

DOVA: Since the form drawer closed.

RIM: That is not a policy.

DOVA: It is a procedure we can perform without a form.

RIM: We will add it to the handbook.

DOVA: The handbook is in the cabinet.

RIM: Leave it there.

[THE CLERK AND SUPERVISOR CARRY THE TWIG TO THE SIDE OF THE PATH.]

PEA: That's much better.

DOVA: Your case is closed.

PEA: Is the twig still a twig?

RIM: It is now a boundary marker.

DOVA: Then we need Form 12.

[THE XYLOPHONE HOLD MUSIC STARTS AGAIN.]

RIM: Don't file it. We can just leave the form drawer closed.

DOVA: I will note that under the category "good enough."

RIM: We don't have that category.

DOVA: We do now.

[SFX: A SINGLE XYLOPHONE NOTE. THE DRAWER CLOSES.]
