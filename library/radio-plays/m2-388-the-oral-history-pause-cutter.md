# The Oral History Pause Cutter

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: oral history broadcasts that cut every silence and accidentally remove the speaker's most meaningful moments*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-013*

Cast: Interviewer Loma, a macaque; elder Sani, a gibbon; editor Vira, a capuchin; host Peka, a lemur.

[SFX: ARCHIVE STUDIO. TAPE SPINS. A QUIET ROOM TONE.]

PEKA: Tonight we hear Sani describe the old footbridge. The archive edit removes long pauses to keep the story moving.

SANI: I crossed that bridge with my sister after the flood. She carried the kettle. I carried the blanket.

[SFX: THREE SECONDS OF ROOM TONE.]

LOMA: The editor marked that silence for removal.

VIRA: It is seven seconds long. The broadcast clock is tight.

SANI: That was the place where the bridge moved under us.

PEKA: The pause comes before you say that.

SANI: I was remembering how it sounded.

VIRA: I can leave one second. The piece needs to fit the slot.

LOMA: The pause is part of the memory. It tells us what Sani had to return to.

PEKA: The station wants a clean, quick story.

SANI: I can shorten the first sentence, but not the part where I remember the bridge.

VIRA: The sound bed covers the pause. I can put river noise under it.

SANI: Then you won't hear that I stopped.

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS. RIVER SOUND FADES IN OVER THE SILENCE.]

LOMA: It sounds as if you were walking the whole time.

SANI: I was not walking then.

VIRA: I will remove the river bed.

[SFX: RIVER SOUND FADES OUT.]

PEKA: Could we keep the pause and shorten the introduction?

LOMA: I can cut the station history and leave Sani's account intact.

VIRA: The current edit is two minutes over. The intro is forty seconds.

PEKA: The broadcast can run a short opening and a complete memory.

SANI: I will tell it once more. I stood on the near bank with the kettle. My sister took the blanket across first.

[SFX: TWO STEPS ON WOOD. A ROPE CREAKS.]

SANI: She called me. The bridge made a low sound.

[SFX: SIX SECONDS OF ROOM TONE. TAPE CONTINUES.]

SANI: I waited until the water dropped. Then I carried the kettle over.

LOMA: That is where the pause belongs.

VIRA: I can keep it exactly as recorded, without an effect or splice.

PEKA: The station will need to trim something else.

LOMA: Trim my question. Sani's memory is the piece.

[SFX: TAPE REWINDS. A SHORT EDIT BEEP. RECORDING PLAYS.]

PEKA: The archive system has a checkbox called "remove silence longer than three seconds."

VIRA: It is on by default for every interview. I can turn it off for this file.

LOMA: The system cut seven seconds of Sani's memory without asking what the pause meant.

SANI: I stopped because I heard the bridge shift. Then I waited.

VIRA: I'll keep the room tone through the full pause and mark the editor's version as a copy.

[SFX: KEYBOARD CLICKS. A FILE SAVES.]

PEKA: The broadcast can still fit if we shorten the introduction, not the memory.

LOMA: The edit is now clear about what it changed.

SANI: I stood on the near bank with the kettle. My sister took the blanket across first. She called me. The bridge made a low sound.

[SFX: SIX SECONDS OF ROOM TONE. TAPE CONTINUES.]

SANI: I waited until the water dropped. Then I carried the kettle over.

PEKA: The pause stays in the archive.

VIRA: The cleaned version is not cleaner. It is shorter and less true.

LOMA: Put the original beside the edit. The archive should say which one is broadcast.

SANI: I would like the pause left in both.

PEKA: We can do that.

[SFX: TWO LABELS PRINT: "ORIGINAL" AND "BROADCAST WITH FULL PAUSE."]

VIRA: The file fits. The silence is in it.

[SFX: TAPE STOPS. ROOM TONE FADES NATURALLY.]
