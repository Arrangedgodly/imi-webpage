# The Rescue Drill's Narrator Won't Stop

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: documentary narration that turns a routine rescue exercise into a perilous expedition*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-019*

CAST: Narrator Vex, an orangutan; drill lead Mara, a macaque; climber Pip, a capuchin; belayer Odo, a gibbon; safety caller Nari, a lemur.

SFX: Indoor climbing wall. Harness buckle, rope through belay device.

MARA: We are beginning the scheduled practice drill. Pip is already on the ground, and Odo is checking the belay.

VEX: At dawn, the climber faces a sheer mountain.

PIP: This is the indoor training wall. The highest hold is marked with yellow tape.

ODO: Harness check complete. Carabiner is locked; rope is threaded correctly.

NARI: The practice area is clear. I will call "stop" if anyone enters the landing zone.

VEX: One wrong move could send Pip into the abyss.

MARA: Please don't narrate danger while we practice. It can distract the climber.

PIP: I can hear Vex over the wall speaker.

VEX: Beneath him, the abyss is waiting.

PIP: I lost count of my starting holds when that came through. I need the quiet cue to climb.

MARA: Hold at the mat. Odo, pause the speaker feed; Vex can record narration after the drill.

SFX: Speaker switch clicks off.

VEX: The expedition is silenced.

ODO: Pip, are you ready to climb?

PIP: Ready. Rope is snug, and I can step down if needed.

MARA: Begin at your own pace. The goal is to practice lowering, not reach the top.

SFX: Shoes grip wall; rope slides slowly.

VEX, faint from control booth: The cliff face resists.

NARI: The wall speaker is off. I still hear him through the open booth door.

MARA: I'll close the door. Continue only if Pip confirms he can focus.

PIP: I can continue. He can wait until I'm back on the mat.

Odo belays without rushing. Pip reaches a yellow hold, checks his footing, and asks to descend.

PIP: Ready to lower.

ODO: Lowering slowly. Keep your feet against the wall.

SFX: Rope feeds; shoes touch mat.

MARA: Pip is down, stable, and uninjured. Drill is complete.

VEX: The climber has survived the mountain.

PIP: I practiced a controlled descent on a training wall.

NARI: The landing zone stayed clear. No one entered while the rope was moving.

ODO: I will inspect the harness and rope after Pip unclips. The equipment check comes after the practice.

PIP: The buckles were checked before I climbed. The mat stayed flat, and I asked to descend from the yellow hold.

VEX: We should replay the descent for a camera angle.

MARA: There is no camera in the landing zone. Pip has completed the exercise; we won't ask for a repeat just for footage.

NARI: The next group is waiting outside. I will tell them the wall opens after the equipment check, not at a dramatic reveal.

SFX: Pip opens the harness buckle and lays the harness on the mat. Odo lifts it for inspection.

ODO: Stitching is intact, the carabiner gate moves freely, and the rope shows no fray.

PIP: My hand is fine. The inspection is complete; Odo can rack the harness and coil the rope.

MARA: Let Odo put the gear away, then we clear the wall for the next climber.

VEX: The documentary has no slow-motion fall.

NARI: Good. There was no fall.

ODO: The harness is on the rack and the rope is coiled.

MARA: Equipment check is complete. Vex can record one accurate narration outside the climbing area.

VEX: The narration must sound dangerous.

MARA: It can sound dramatic without claiming a real emergency. Say "training exercise," then describe the controlled descent.

VEX: "In today's exercise, our climber practiced lowering from the yellow hold."

PIP: That's accurate.

VEX: It lacks a blizzard.

NARI: There was no snow in the gym.

MARA: We are finished here. The next group can enter the practice area.

SFX: Door opens; distant voices enter.
