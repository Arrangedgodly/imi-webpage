# The Clockwork Orchard Calls Its Own Opening Hours

*Format: Radio play parody*
*Parody target: municipal radio announcements and clockwork attractions that interrupt official schedules*
*By: IMI Radio Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-AUDIO-020*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; orchard keeper Odo, a gibbon; engineer Vex, an orangutan; caller Pip, a capuchin; town clerk Nari, a lemur; royal voice Ollie, a capuchin (recorded).

SFX: Radio studio, with Odo and Pip on a live line from the orchard gate. A tiny bell rings three times on that line; gears whir, then stop.

MARA: This is Valley Morning. The clockwork orchard opens at nine, according to the sign, the brochure, and the keeper.

ODO: Correct. I turned the brass key at eight fifty-five. The gates open at nine.

VEX: The orchard clock says eleven.

MARA: It cannot be eleven. My studio clock says eight fifty-six.

NARI: The orchard clock has a guest dial. It displays the time in the orchard's fictional kingdom.

PIP: I am outside the gate. The king's hour is eleven. The public gate is locked.

ODO: The gear train is set to story time. The gate latch is set to civil time.

MARA: Then we will not announce that the orchard is open until the latch opens.

SFX: Gear clicks. A wooden bird chirps. A gate does not move.

VEX: The bird has announced something.

ODO: It announces that the king has woken up.

PIP: The king is a wooden squirrel. He has been awake all week.

NARI: The public schedule says nine. The clock's dial is decorative, not a time authority.

MARA: We are live at eight fifty-seven. Keeper, can you show us the gate mechanism?

ODO: I can, but I need to turn the key back to the marked notch. The key has four settings: STORY, RESET, CLOSED, and OPEN.

VEX: Which one is it on?

ODO: Between STORY and RESET.

MARA: That is not a setting.

ODO: It is a very persuasive click.

SFX: Brass key scrapes; gears clack backward.

PIP: The sign just changed from KINGDOM TIME to OPENING SOON.

NARI: It was a flip card on the gate.

VEX: The orchard's official account is now posting that the king has delayed opening.

MARA: Who gave the king an account?

ODO: The town clerk. He needs to post seasonal poems.

NARI: The king's account is scheduled. It posts every time the clock reaches eleven.

MARA: The clock is still showing eleven.

NARI: I can pause the post. I cannot unring a bell.

SFX: Three bells. OLLIE, recorded: My orchard welcomes you. The studio printer starts.

VEX: The king has spoken, and the printer is answering.

NARI: That printer has no connection to the clock.

MARA: Then why is it printing a proclamation?

NARI: Because I pressed PRINT when the announcement arrived. The proclamation is mine.

PIP: The gate still hasn't opened.

ODO: The latch is on the outside. I can see it through the bars. It is blocked by the welcome mat.

MARA: A mat has stopped the kingdom?

ODO: It folded under the door when the clockwork bird flapped.

SFX: Scraping. A mat is pulled free. The gate latch lifts with a clunk.

PIP: The gate moved. I am still outside.

ODO: That was a test. I will set it back to CLOSED now.

SFX: Latch lowers; gate clicks shut.

PIP: It is shut again. The public gate opens at nine, on civil time.

MARA: Please say that again for anyone listening from the kingdom.

ODO: The fictional king's eleven is a story cue. Public admission is nine to five. We will open the gate when the studio clock reaches nine.

VEX: Can you stop the bird from ringing the bell at eleven?

ODO: Yes. I can disconnect the story bell. I will keep the gate latch on the public timer, and reset the guest clock to nine so the wooden squirrel returns to sleep.

SFX: Odo turns a small switch. The wooden bird chirps once, then goes quiet. He turns the key to RESET. Gears wind backward and the guest dial clicks through its reset. Then Odo turns the key to CLOSED.

PIP: The squirrel has lowered its head onto its pillow. The guest dial shows nine now.

NARI: I have paused the scheduled royal post and corrected the public notice to say nine.

MARA: The studio clock has reached nine. Keeper?

SFX: At nine, Odo turns the key from CLOSED to OPEN. Gate rolls aside.

PIP: I am entering the orchard. The king is still asleep.

VEX: That is a peaceful opening.

ODO: The king is asleep again. That is where the story starts.

MARA: And the visitors get the morning.

SFX: Public gate chime rings once; the story bell stays quiet as birdsong and the ordinary gears resume softly.

FINAL SIGNOFF: Public hours remain nine to five. The royal account and bell stay paused; staff may restart the story cue manually after closing.
