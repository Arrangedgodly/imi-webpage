# The Flight Attendant's Demo Cart Has a Passport

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: airline workplace procedurals, safety demonstrations, and crew who discover a passenger's identity inside the service cart*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: FLIGHT ATTENDANT NARA, a gibbon; PILOT, an orangutan; PURSER, a baboon; PASSENGER LIO, a capuchin; GATE AGENT, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN - DAY

Nara opens the safety-demo cart. A passport slides out.

NARA: This cart has traveled farther than we have.

PURser: The passenger is still at the gate.

The plane door is closed. A knock sounds from outside.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The cabin crew prepares for departure. Lio is seated in 3A, holding a boarding pass for 3B. The purser says the manifest has one missing passenger, but every seat is occupied. Nara finds the passport inside the cart, with a stamp from a country the flight is not visiting.

NARA: Who packed this cart?

PURser: It passed inspection.

PILOT: Did inspection check the passport?

The gate agent calls from the jet bridge and says someone is asking to board. Nara opens the door. A second passenger, also named Lio, waits with a ticket for 3B. The first Lio says he was moved to 3A by the gate system, but the manifest still lists him in 3B.

LIO: We have exchanged seats without meeting.

The purser checks identification. The passport belongs to the second Lio. The first Lio has a library card and a ticket. The agent confirms the second passenger was delayed at security, while the first was upgraded. The passport fell into the demo cart when a cart was loaded at the gate.

ACT TWO

The crew must decide whether to hold the flight while the passenger gets his passport. The pilot says the flight cannot depart without matching identification. The first Lio offers to swap back to 3B. The second Lio asks whether the seat has more legroom.

PURser: Both seats are the same size.

LIO TWO: Then the passport can sit in 3A.

Nara refuses to store identity documents in the cart. She checks the passport against the boarding pass and hands it directly to its owner. The gate agent verifies the name over the intercom. The passenger is cleared to board. The first Lio returns to 3B, but his seatbelt is twisted under the cushion.

NARA: We are not moving until the belt is clear.

The first Lio untangles it. Nara gives the safety demonstration. The cart wheel squeaks every time she lifts the demonstration mask. The passengers start repeating the motions before she does.

PILOT: The cabin is ready.

PURser: The cart is not.

The purser removes the passport from the demo cart, secures the cart latch, and confirms the emergency equipment list. The passenger buckles in. The two Lios exchange boarding stubs, then realize they have identical names but different middle initials.

INT. FORWARD GALLEY - DAY

The purser asks both passengers to verify their middle initials and destination. One Lio is traveling to Port Sable; the other is traveling to Sable Port, a nearby airport with a similar code. The gate agent checks the itinerary over the intercom. The names match, but the flight numbers do not. Nara opens the cart's inventory log. The passport was logged at the gate as a lost item, then mistakenly loaded with the safety cards.

PURser: The cart was checked.

NARA: The cart was checked for equipment.

PILOT: The passenger was checked for identity.

The two Lios compare boarding passes. Their seat changes came from a software update that moved one passenger to an aisle seat, then failed to refresh the printed manifest. The purser prints a new seat list. The cart is locked while the pages come through. The printer jams, and the gate agent reads the corrected seating assignments aloud instead.

LIO ONE: I am 3B.

LIO TWO: I am 3A.

NARA: And the passport is with its owner.

The captain asks the crew to confirm the cabin count. The purser counts the passengers by row while Nara checks that the demo mask, seat belt extension, and safety cards are back in the cart. The passport is not among them. The gate agent confirms both passengers boarded under their own travel documents. The crew can now close the door without a mismatch.



ACT THREE

The pilot receives clearance. Nara completes the safety demonstration and locks the cart in its assigned compartment. The gate agent closes the jet bridge. The passengers take their seats. The purser returns the cart inventory sheet to the correct pocket.

LIO TWO: I have my passport.

LIO ONE: I have my seat.

FINAL IMAGE: The cart rolls into storage with every safety card in place and no travel documents inside.
