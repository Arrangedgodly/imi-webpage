# The Period Drama Needs a Spoon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: historical dramas that prize period detail while delaying a scene over one missing prop*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A costume drama crew prepares a royal breakfast scene, but the prop department's antique spoon is in a different century.

Cast: Director Vela, a macaque; actor Odo, a gibbon; prop master Lali, a capuchin; and historian Runi, a baboon.

Episode: "Breakfast at the Court"

COLD OPEN

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - MORNING

Odo sits at a table in a velvet robe. A bowl of porridge is in front of him. Vela checks the camera.

VELA: The king raises the spoon and declares war.

ODO: Why?

VELA: The scene needs a turning point.

LALI: The spoon is from the wrong century.

[THEY ALL LOOK AT THE SPOON.]

ACT ONE

Runi compares the spoon with a history book. Lali opens a prop box.

RUNI: This spoon was made two hundred years after the scene.

VELA: Can we hide the handle?

RUNI: The bowl is wrong too.

ODO: The porridge is also from today.

VELA: We need breakfast for the shot.

LALI: The prop kitchen has an older wooden spoon.

[THEY TRY IT. THE SPOON IS TOO WIDE FOR THE SMALL BOWL.]

RUNI: That shape is plausible for the period.

ODO: It cannot fit in the bowl.

ACT TWO

INT. PROP ROOM - LATER

Lali finds a narrow spoon with a cracked handle. Vela wants to glue it. Runi checks the book again.

RUNI: The handle is a later repair.

VELA: That means the spoon has a history.

ODO: Can I eat with it?

RUNI: It is a prop. Do not use it for food.

[ODO PUTS THE SPOON DOWN. A TINY PIECE OF THE HANDLE BREAKS OFF.]

LALI: The repair is not holding.

VELA: Can we film the scene without the spoon?

ODO: The script says I raise it.

RUNI: The king could point with his hand.

VELA: That is less dramatic.

ACT TWO-B

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - AFTERNOON

Vela rehearses the declaration. Odo holds his hand above the bowl. Lali replaces the bowl with a wooden cup.

ODO: The king is angry because the spoon is missing?

VELA: He is declaring war over a border dispute.

RUNI: The border is a river. The spoon has nothing to do with it.

LALI: We can use a table knife instead.

RUNI: The knife is also wrong for the period.

[THE CAMERA ROLLS. ODO REACHES FOR THE SPOON, FINDS ONLY THE BOWL, AND LOOKS AT VELA.]

ODO: We have run out of breakfast.

VELA: That is not the line.

ODO: It is what happened.

ACT TWO-C

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - EARLY EVENING

Runi checks the script while Vela resets the camera. Odo folds the robe sleeves away from the porridge.

RUNI: The king would not declare war over a spoon.

VELA: The spoon is our episode hook.

ODO: The scene says the river border moved.

RUNI: The river changed course after the flood. That is the dispute.

LALI: We can show the old river on a map.

VELA: The set map is on the other side of the table.

[LALI TURNS THE MAP AROUND. THE RIVER IS DRAWN THROUGH THE KING'S BREAKFAST BOWL.]

ODO: The map puts the river in the porridge.

RUNI: It is a prop map. The bowl is in front of it.

VELA: The camera makes it look like the border is in the bowl.

LALI: We can move the bowl.

ODO: Then the king has no breakfast.

VELA: He can point at the river instead.

RUNI: That is closer to the historical account.

[ODO POINTS. THE SLEEVE OF THE ROBE BRUSHES THE BOWL. THE PORRIDGE SPILLS ACROSS THE MAP.]

LALI: The river has flooded the border again.

RUNI: That is not history. That is breakfast.

ACT TWO-D

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - NIGHT

Lali replaces the paper map with a clean copy. Vela marks the camera angle. Odo stands by the window.

VELA: We will film the river dispute without the spoon.

ODO: And the breakfast?

LALI: The props have been cleared.

RUNI: The scene can begin with the map and end with the decision.

VELA: No cliffhanger?

RUNI: The audience can see what the king decides.

ACT THREE

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - FINAL TAKE

Runi clears the table. Odo stands by the window and points toward the painted river backdrop.

ODO: The border is the river. We can cross it without a spoon.

VELA: Keep that.

LALI: The prop list says one bowl, no spoon.

[THE CAMERA ROLLS. ODO MAKES THE DECLARATION WITH HIS HAND. THE SCENE PLAYS CLEANLY.]

TAG

Vela places a modern spoon in a museum case. Runi adds a card: "Not used in this scene."

ODO: The war is over.

LALI: Breakfast is still waiting.
