# The Museum's Night Guard Follows His Own Shadow

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nocturnal crime mysteries, ominous security footage, and guards who treat a shadow as a second intruder*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: GUARD ORI, a baboon; DETECTIVE VELA, a gibbon; CURATOR, an orangutan; TECH, a capuchin; CLEANER, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - NIGHT

A shadow crosses a blank wall. Guard Ori turns sharply and shines a flashlight. The shadow grows enormous.

ORI: We have an intruder.

DETECTIVE VELA: Where?

ORI: Behind me.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The curator reports that a small statue has moved overnight. Vela examines the pedestal. It is dusty except for a clean oval where the statue used to stand. Ori says he followed a figure through three rooms but never caught it. The security video shows Ori walking quickly with a flashlight; a large shadow follows him.

VELA: It is your shadow.

ORI: It has been following me all night.

The tech explains that the gallery lights are on timers. Each time Ori crosses a sensor, a different lamp turns on behind him, stretching his shadow toward the statue. The curator says the statue was last seen in the west gallery. Ori insists he saw it in the east gallery.

CURATOR: We have two galleries.

ORI: And one statue.

The cleaner arrives with a cart and says she moved the statue into storage so she could polish the pedestal. The curator says the statue was not listed for cleaning. The cleaner shows a signed work order. It says "clear the plinth." She thought the statue was a plinth decoration.

ACT TWO

INT. SECURITY ROOM - NIGHT

Vela watches the footage at half speed. The statue's outline appears to move across the wall. The tech overlays the lighting map. Ori's flashlight casts his shadow over a second light source, creating a shape with a long neck. The cleaner's cart blocks the camera for four minutes, so no one can see the statue being moved.

ORI: Four minutes is enough time.

TECH: To polish a pedestal.

The curator checks the storage room. The statue is there, wrapped in a cloth, with no damage. The cleaner says she placed it on the cart, wheeled it through the east hall, and stored it on the padded shelf. She did not tell the guard because he was busy chasing his shadow.

ORI: I was conducting an investigation.

VELA: You were following yourself.

The tech recreates the light pattern. Ori walks past the statue's former location; the shadow again appears to lift the statue's shape. Vela moves the floor lamp. The figure disappears. Ori walks back the other way. His shadow now points toward the exit.

ORI: It is leaving.

TECH: You are.

Vela orders the sensor lights adjusted and asks the curator to update the statue's cleaning record. The cleaner replaces the cloth with a labeled storage cover. Ori apologizes for accusing the room of a break-in.

INT. WEST GALLERY - LATE NIGHT

Vela asks Ori to repeat his patrol without the flashlight. The sensor lights come on one at a time, and the guard's shadow changes shape with each lamp. At the second doorway, the cleaner's cart reflects in the glass case, appearing to move ahead of them. Ori reaches for his radio.

ORI: There are two intruders.

TECH: One is you. One is the cart.

The curator watches the footage and sees a third shadow at the west wall. It comes from a small projector used for the museum's evening tour. The projector is set to run a silhouette animation after closing. The image shows a long-necked animal walking across the wall. Ori thought it was the missing statue.

CURATOR: It is a prehistoric bird.

VELA: It is a loop.

The tech switches off the projector. The shadows stop. Vela asks the guard to identify the real statue in storage. Ori names its shape, position, and label correctly. The cleaner returns the wrapped statue to the plinth. The curator checks the inventory and adds a separate note for the projector schedule. The guard tests each light sensor from the center of the room, then records the points where the shadows stretch.

ORI: The room is clear.

CLEANER: The room is clean too.



ACT THREE

INT. WEST GALLERY - NIGHT

The statue is returned to its pedestal. The guard patrols with the flashlight lowered. Vela checks the room from each angle. The shadow stays attached to Ori and shrinks when the wall lamps are repositioned.

ORI: I have closed the case.

CLEANER: The cleaning order is still open.

Ori signs it and returns to his patrol.

FINAL IMAGE: The statue stands on its clean pedestal while the guard's small shadow follows him toward the exit.
