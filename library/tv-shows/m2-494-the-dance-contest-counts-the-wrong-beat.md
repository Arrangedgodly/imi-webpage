# The Dance Contest Counts the Wrong Beat

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised dance competitions that let the score graphics outrun the music and the dancers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Jori, a lemur; judge Meka, an orangutan; contestant Vali, a macaque; contestant Piri, a capuchin; scorekeeper Tanu, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. DANCE STAGE - NIGHT

The band plays a steady four-count. Vali finishes a turn on beat four. The score display flashes 10 before the music reaches beat two.

JORI: A perfect score!

MEKA: The dance is still happening.

ACT ONE

INT. DANCE STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Tanu says the graphic is set to predict scores during the final pose. Meka asks the control room to hold it. Piri begins her routine; the display flashes 8, 9, 10 while she is still tying her shoe. She stops to retie it. The score changes to 6.

PIRI: I have not danced yet.

JORI: The scoreboard is excited.

Meka asks for the judges' numbers only after the routine. Tanu clears the display. The audience claps; the display awards a bonus point for enthusiasm. Tanu disconnects the applause input. The score drops back to zero.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Tanu checks the timing feed. The display receives its cue from the rehearsal track, which is four beats ahead of the live band. Meka asks the band to play the actual tempo. The score graphics begin flashing before the contestants enter. Jori follows the numbers with his eyes and introduces the next dancer too early.

[Vali enters while Jori is saying Piri's name. The two contestants meet center stage and politely trade places. The camera follows the score graphic instead, which continues to show Vali's predicted result.]

Piri asks to perform with the music, not with the screen. Meka tells Tanu to unplug the rehearsal cue. Tanu follows the cable from the scoreboard to a small pedal beneath the judges' table. Meka's foot is resting on it. She lifts her foot; the graphic freezes on 10.

MEKA: That is not my score.

TANU: It is your shoe.

The stage manager moves the pedal. The display goes blank. Jori asks for the scores on paper. Tanu hands him three cards, each marked TEN. Meka says she has not judged anyone yet. The cards are printed for the season opener.

INT. DANCE STAGE - LATER

Piri and Vali rehearse a short duet. They agree on a shared final pose. The band counts them in. Tanu watches the dancers, not the monitor. Jori tries not to announce the score before the last step. He holds his cue card over his mouth.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Tanu tests the score board with a blank screen. It stays blank. Jori rehearses the introduction without mentioning numbers. Meka gives him a hand signal for when the judges are ready. He mistakes it for the start of a dance and performs a stiff two-step. The audience laughs. Jori bows; no score appears. Tanu confirms the prediction cable is disconnected and leaves the real score keys in reach of the judges. Jori reads the next cue from the correct card. Piri and Vali each mark the beat with one foot. The band waits for their nod. When the music begins, the scoreboard stays dark through the first turn, the missed step, and the recovery. Meka gives no score until both dancers finish.

ACT THREE

INT. DANCE STAGE - NIGHT

Piri and Vali perform. They miss a turn, recover, and finish together on the final beat. The scoreboard stays dark. The audience applauds. Meka gives her score after the music stops. Tanu enters each judge's number by hand. The total appears once, after both dancers have bowed.

Jori reads the result. Piri wins by one point. Vali congratulates her. The score display begins another prediction, showing a crown over the host. Tanu traces the wire to the applause meter, which is still connected to the graphic. She switches it off. The crown disappears.

JORI: I will accept the result after I finish hosting.

Meka asks Piri to repeat the final pose for the closing photograph. Piri and Vali refuse to repeat the whole dance, but they stand together and hold the pose. The band plays a brief four-count sting. The display waits until the sting ends, then shows the final score correctly.

TAG

INT. DANCE STAGE - AFTER THE SHOW

Tanu removes the prediction pedal and labels it REHEARSAL ONLY. Jori tries a bow. The scoreboard gives him no points. He bows again, lower. Meka hands him the season opener's printed tens.

FINAL IMAGE: Piri and Vali's final pose fills the screen, while the score graphic remains still until the music ends.
