# The Guesthouse with One Room

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel series convention, where hosts review a tiny guesthouse while the only room is occupied by the production equipment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A travel host arrives to review a cozy guesthouse, but the crew luggage fills the only room and the host keeps pretending there is space.

Cast: Host Nara, a gibbon; guide Kito, a macaque; camera operator Piri, a capuchin; and innkeeper Vela, a lemur.

Episode: "A Room with a View"

COLD OPEN

INT. GUESTHOUSE, DAY

Vela opens the guest room. It contains a bed, a window and three camera cases. Nara smiles at the camera.

NARA: Spacious and thoughtfully equipped.

KITO: The cases are ours.

Piri asks where the tripod should go. Vela points to the only corner.

ACT ONE

Nara tours the room. She praises the bed, the view and the handcrafted shelf. Kito opens the closet. It holds two coats, a mop and the the inn spare pillows. Piri tries to fit the camera bag inside. The closet door will not close.

Vela says the room is usually larger when no crew is present. Nara asks if the equipment can go in the hall. Vela says it blocks the path. Kito suggests placing cases under the bed. They fit halfway, stopping the bed from moving.

ACT TWO

EXT. GUESTHOUSE PORCH, AFTERNOON

The crew moves the equipment outside. Rain begins. Vela offers a covered porch, but the camera cases must stay dry. Piri drapes a cloth over them. The wind lifts it. Kito holds one corner while Nara conducts the review under the eaves.

NARA: The lodging offers an outdoor storage experience.

VELA: That is not a guest service.

The crew asks if they can film the view. The window looks over a garden, but the camera cannot fit between the bed and the cases. Piri removes a lens, then realizes he needs it for the wide shot. Kito opens the window from outside and frames the garden through the glass.

ACT TWO-B

INT. GUESTHOUSE, AFTERNOON

Vela asks the crew to film the room without the cases. Piri carries them to the porch. Kito keeps the door open while Nara describes the window. A gust blows the cloth cover off the equipment. Piri sets down the camera to catch it before it reaches the path.

NARA: The porch needs a stronger hook.

VELA: It is meant for drying towels.

Kito notices that the tripod blocks the porch steps. They carry it inside, but the hallway is too narrow to turn around. Piri folds the legs. The camera remains safe, though the tripod catches on the coat rack. Vela moves the rack and points out a second, unused hook behind it.

The crew hangs the cloth there. The cases fit under the porch bench. Nara asks if the guest room can now be reviewed honestly. Vela says the room is small but clean. Piri confirms the shelf holds a camera battery and the bed is not blocked. Kito tries the closet door. It closes, but the mop handle sticks out.

KITO: The closet is still sharing with the cleaning tools.

Vela moves the mop to a wall hook. The door closes fully. The room now has clear floor space and an unobstructed route to the door. Nara records the review with no equipment in the shot.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. GUESTHOUSE GARDEN, EVENING

Nara reviews the footage with Kito. The first shot shows the room crowded with cases. The later shot shows the clear walkway and the window. Piri asks which one should lead the segment. Nara says they should show the room before and after the crew packs away its gear.

Vela brings a small bench outside for the cases. The bench is not a storage rack, so they place only the dry cloth on it. The cases remain under the porch roof, off the path. Kito checks the room one last time: bed clear, closet shut, shelf usable, window open. The guesthouse has no extra room, but the crew has a better plan for travel equipment.

ACT THREE



INT. GUESTHOUSE, EVENING

Vela asks the crew to choose one room feature to keep. Nara chooses the bed. Kito chooses the window. Piri chooses the shelf for the camera battery. Vela says the crew can use the room in turns: one rests while the others store gear on the porch when the rain stops.

They reorganize the cases vertically and clear a safe walkway. Nara tests the bed. Kito opens the window. The view is good. Piri places one small camera on the shelf and packs the rest away.

TAG

Vela brings a guestbook. Nara writes, "Best room we have visited." Kito asks if she means best view. Nara says the room was fine once the equipment left. Piri puts the camera case in the hallway, blocking the exit. Vela opens the door and points outside.

VELA: The guesthouse has one more feature.
