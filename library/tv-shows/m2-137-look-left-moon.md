# Look Slightly to the Left of the Moon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: astronomy programs that promise spectacular live sky events, then improvise through clouds, camera limits, and exaggerated constellation stories*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Neri promises viewers a rare meteor shower from the hilltop observatory. Astronomer Falo wants to explain what can actually be seen, while producer Puck keeps switching between the cloudy sky, the planetarium projector, and a stock image of a comet.

Cast: Neri, a macaque host; Falo, a gorilla astronomer; Tavi, a lemur camera operator; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "Cloud Cover Is Part of the View"

### Cold Open

[OBSERVATORY ROOF. CLOUDS COVER THE SKY. NERI LOOKS THROUGH A TELESCOPE.]

NERI: The meteor shower should begin any moment.

FALO: We cannot see through the clouds.

PUCK: The graphics team has a meteor.

[PUCK HOLDS UP A PRINTED PICTURE OF A COMET.]

NERI: It is in our hand.

TAVI: The clouds are live.

### Act One

[FALO TURNS THE TELESCOPE TOWARD A CLEAR PATCH. THE CLOUDS MOVE OVER IT.]

NERI: Can you find the moon?

FALO: It is behind that cloud.

PUCK: Should we show the stock image?

FALO: The moon is not the meteor shower.

[THE CAMERA SHOWS THE OBSERVATORY ROOF. NERI POINTS TO A SMALL SIGN: "PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH THE TELESCOPE."]

NERI: We have an interactive segment.

FALO: The telescope is not interactive.

[PUCK SETS A MODEL PLANET ON THE TABLE. THE MODEL SPINS TOO FAST.]

NERI: Is it supposed to move that quickly?

FALO: It is a model of rotation. It is not a prediction.

[PUCK TURNS THE MODEL DOWN. IT STOPS WITH THE DARK SIDE FACING CAMERA.]

TAVI: Now it looks like the planet disappeared.

FALO: It is still there. We are seeing the unlit side.

[NERI ASKS THE CAMERA TO SHOW THE WHOLE MODEL, NOT ONLY THE SHADOW.]

TAVI: That is not the sky right now.

NERI: It is a preview.

FALO: It is a teaching model.

### Act Two

[THE CREW MOVES INSIDE TO THE PLANETARIUM. FALO PROJECTS THE REAL SKY MAP FOR TONIGHT.]

FALO: The shower's radiant point is near the northeast horizon. We may see a few meteors if the clouds clear.

NERI: How many is a few?

FALO: We cannot promise a number.

[PUCK ADDS A COUNTER GRAPHIC TO THE SCREEN. IT STARTS AT 100.]

FALO: Please remove that.

PUCK: It is a target.

NERI: We are looking for the first one.

[THE COUNTER DROPS TO 99. NOTHING IS VISIBLE.]

TAVI: The camera is aimed at the ceiling.

[PUCK ADJUSTS THE LENS. IT POINTS AT THE EXIT SIGN.]

FALO: The northeast is the other direction.

[THEY TURN THE CAMERA. THE CLOUDS FILL THE WINDOW.]

NERI: The show is still live.

FALO: We can show the observation log.

[HE WRITES THE TIME, CLOUD COVER, AND VIEWING DIRECTION. NERI ASKS HIM TO DRAW A STAR.]

NERI: Can we give viewers a constellation while we wait?

FALO: We can show Orion, but the clouds cover those stars too.

[PUCK PUTS A CONSTELLATION CARD OVER THE WINDOW. THE STARS APPEAR ON THE PAPER, NOT IN THE SKY.]

TAVI: It is a map.

NERI: A useful map.

FALO: A map is fine if we label it as one.

FALO: A star would not tell us whether a meteor appeared.

[PUCK ERASES THE COUNTER AND WRITES "WAITING FOR CLEAR SKY."]

### Act Three

[THE CLOUDS THIN. TAVI LOOKS THROUGH THE WINDOW, NOT THE CAMERA.]

PUCK: The counter is ready again.

FALO: We are not counting planes.

[PUCK REMOVES THE COUNTER AND REPLACES IT WITH THE TIME AND CLOUD COVER.]

TAVI: There is a light moving across the northeast.

FALO: It is a plane.

NERI: Can we track it?

FALO: It is not a meteor.

[THE PLANE BLINKS AND PASSES BEHIND A CLOUD. PUCK STARTS A DRAMATIC MUSIC CUE, THEN STOPS IT.]

NERI: We have no confirmed meteor tonight. We do have the sky conditions and a way to check again tomorrow.

FALO: The best viewing is after midnight, away from streetlights.

[NERI TURNS OFF THE PLANETARIUM PROJECTOR. THE GROUP LOOKS OUTSIDE.]

[FALO OPENS THE OBSERVATORY LOG TO THE NEXT PAGE. IT IS BLANK.] 

FALO: We will record what we saw, not what we hoped to see.

NERI: Time, direction, and cloud cover.

TAVI: The plane is gone now.

[PUCK WRITES "NO METEOR CONFIRMED" ON THE SCREEN AND LEAVES THE COUNTER OFF.]

[TAVI CLOSES THE OBSERVATORY ROOF. FALO SETS AN ALARM FOR AFTER MIDNIGHT.]

NERI: Will we know if the clouds clear?

FALO: We can check the sky and the forecast.

[PUCK LABELS THE COMET PICTURE "ILLUSTRATION" AND PUTS IT IN THE FILE.]

### Tag

[PUCK PUTS THE COMET PICTURE BACK IN ITS ENVELOPE.]

PUCK: Should I label it "not live"?

FALO: Label it "illustration."

[NERI POINTS THE TELESCOPE AWAY FROM THE EXIT SIGN.]

NERI: Next time, we will check the clouds first.
