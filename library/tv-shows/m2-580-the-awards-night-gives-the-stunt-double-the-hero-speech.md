# The Awards Night Gives the Stunt Double the Hero Speech

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live award-show reversals, suspense envelopes, and a producer who mistakes the performer for the person being honored*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Host Tavi, a gibbon; nominee Zip, a squirrel monkey stunt performer; lead actor Ralo, a baboon; stage manager Nima, a capuchin; presenter Odo, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. AWARDS THEATER - NIGHT

Odo opens an envelope and stares at the card.

ODO: The winner is... the person who fell out of the window.

RALO: That narrows it down.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The annual Silver Palm Awards are live. Ralo is nominated for Best Hero. Zip performed the falls, swings, and one careful landing into a foam fruit cart. The show has placed the stunt ensemble prize immediately before the hero award, but producer Nima has arranged the envelopes backward on the podium.

TAVI: We celebrate the craft behind the spectacle.

NIMA: And the spectacle after the craft. Please keep walking toward the marked X.

ZIP: My X is under a cable.

NIMA: That is the camera track. Use the other X.

RALO: I have one X and an entrance cue.

Odo takes the first envelope. Tavi whispers that the cue card says "stunt ensemble." The envelope says "hero." Odo does not want to announce a category incorrectly, so he asks Nima to confirm the sequence. Nima has a headset in one ear and an earpiece playing last year's applause in the other.

NIMA: The applause is on time. The awards are not.

TAVI: Can we hold for the correct card?

RALO: I can hold the pose.

Zip steps onto the X and instinctively crouches, checking the floor for a landing pad. The cameras mistake the crouch for a dramatic reaction shot. Tavi moves to block the cable from Zip's heel.

TAVI: That is a safety check, not a gasp.

ZIP: The floor is clear now.

ACT TWO

Odo opens the envelope marked HERO only after Nima checks the printed category. The correct winner is Ralo. The theater applauds. Ralo walks to the stage, then notices Zip still at the lower stairs, holding the foam cart's strap.

RALO: This award is for my performance.

ZIP: I know. I am making sure the cart is not rolling into the aisle.

RALO: The cart is part of the film. You did the fall.

Nima tries to signal for a commercial break, but the live show has no break for another eight minutes. Tavi points out that the next category, stunt ensemble, can be announced when its envelope is ready. Nima asks for the card. It is under a stack of cue sheets and a tiny trophy used as a paperweight.

NIMA: The trophy has been sitting on the result.

ODO: The result has been sitting under the trophy.

ZIP: Should I carry it to the table?

TAVI: Carry only the trophy.

Zip lifts the award, revealing the card. Nima checks the label. It is the stunt ensemble category and lists Zip and the rest of the crew. The camera director asks Ralo to leave the stage so they can reset for the next introduction. Ralo refuses to let the show make it look as though he is being escorted away.

RALO: I can stay and introduce the people who made the falls work.

NIMA: That was not on the cue sheet.

TAVI: We can read the actual names and keep the camera on the stage.

Zip tells Nima the harness was checked before the window scene, but the foam cart strap was still attached after the last take. Nima removes it from Zip's shoulder and hangs it on the cart hook. No one asks for a reenactment of the fall.

ACT THREE

Tavi announces the stunt ensemble award. Zip and two crew members walk up together. Ralo stays beside them and speaks briefly, naming the team instead of repeating his own hero speech.

RALO: I got to run toward danger because they made the landing safe.

ZIP: We also made the window break into soft pieces.

TAVI: And the cart stayed in its lane.

NIMA: The correct card is now in the correct envelope. I checked twice.

ODO: Then we can give the award once.

Zip accepts the ensemble trophy on behalf of the team. The live camera frames the entire group, including Ralo, who steps out of the center mark so the crew can fit. The award show's orchestra starts a flourish, but Nima gives them a small downward hand signal before it becomes a second ending.

ZIP: Is there a stunt for leaving the stage?

NIMA: There is a ramp.

Zip walks down the ramp, looks at the foam cart, and secures its brake. Ralo follows at a normal pace. The host reads the next category from the verified card. No one reopens the hero envelope.

FINAL IMAGE: The two trophies sit on the awards table beside a clipped note: CHECK CATEGORY BEFORE ORCHESTRA.
