# The Fishing Show Catches Its Boom Mic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: outdoor fishing programs, patient nature footage, and hosts who treat every empty hook as a cliffhanger*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Omi, a baboon; guide Vala, an orangutan; camera operator Seli, a macaque; audio tech Rori, a gibbon; ranger Pavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. POND DOCK - MORNING

Omi holds a fishing rod over the water. The boom microphone dips into the frame. A fish splashes near the dock.

OMI: We have a bite.

RORI: We have a microphone in the water.

[The audio tech lifts the boom. A drop falls from it into Omi's hat.] 

VALA: The fish is still there.

[The theme music starts. The host raises the rod, but the line is not in the water.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. POND DOCK - CONTINUOUS

The ranger reviews the fishing rules with the crew. They have a permit for catch-and-release and are allowed one rod at the designated dock. Omi asks whether a fish can be called a guest star. Pavi says the fish is wildlife, not a cast member.

PAVI: The microphone stays above the rail.

RORI: Understood. I can use the short boom from the platform.

SELI: The camera can frame both the line and the water without moving closer.

[The host casts. The line lands in the water. Vala shows Omi how to keep the rod steady without pulling at every ripple.] 

OMI: The line moved.

VALA: A leaf brushed it.

[The camera zooms in on the floating leaf. Omi asks for a dramatic sting. The audio tech plays a tiny bell instead.] 

OMI: Is that the fish?

RORI: That is my cue to check the boom.

[The ranger points out a turtle surfacing near the far bank. The crew watches it from the dock without moving the rod toward it.] 

ACT TWO

EXT. POND DOCK - LATER

The line pulls gently. Omi asks if this is the bite. Vala tells him to wait until there is steady tension. He waits; the line slackens. The host looks at the camera, hoping for a cliffhanger. Seli keeps recording the pond.

OMI: Perhaps the fish has seen the camera.

VALA: The camera is pointed at the water. The fish is free to leave.

[The boom microphone swings when a breeze crosses the dock. Rori catches its handle before it touches the water. The host says the boom has been caught. Vala points out that the equipment is not bait.] 

PAVI: Keep gear inside the rail.

RORI: I will clip the short boom to the post.

[The clip holds. The host recasts at the same marked spot. A fish pulls the line steadily this time. Vala tells Omi to reel slowly. The fish surfaces, then slips free before reaching the dock.] 

OMI: It has escaped our season finale.

VALA: It was released without being caught. That is fine.

[The ranger says no catch needs to be recorded. Omi asks whether they should try again. Vala checks the time and says one more cast is allowed within the permit.] 

[The ranger checks the log sheet and asks whether the crew plans a second cast. Omi says there is one minute before they must pack. Vala points to the sign limiting the dock to one rod. The host examines the boom clip and sees a water drop on its foam wind cover.] 

PAVI: We can dry the microphone away from the shoreline.

RORI: The boom did not enter the water. The drop came from the fishing line.

OMI: The only thing we caught on camera was the microphone almost touching the pond.

[The crew reviews the footage and deletes one shot where the rod crossed the marked boundary. Seli keeps the fish release and the clear dock rules in the final cut.] 

VALA: The segment can end with the fish leaving, not the equipment falling in.

ACT THREE

EXT. POND DOCK - AFTERNOON

Omi makes one final cast. A small fish takes the hook and is brought close to the dock. Vala wets her hands, removes the hook quickly, and returns the fish to the water. The camera stays on the fish swimming away rather than on the host's face.

OMI: We caught one.

VALA: We released one.

PAVI: That is the end of the permitted fishing segment.

[The ranger signs the catch-and-release log. The audio tech checks the boom clip; it stayed dry. Seli turns the camera toward the posted dock rules. Omi tries to create a final suspense sting, but the sound operator leaves the pond's natural audio.] 

OMI: No cliffhanger?

SELI: The line is out of the water.

[The host reels in the empty line, then places the rod in its case. The crew packs all gear and leaves the dock clear.] 

TAG

A splash sounds off camera. Omi turns toward it, but Pavi points at the sign: NO CASTING AFTER THE SEGMENT.

FINAL IMAGE: The rod is packed away as the fish swims past the dock in clear water.
