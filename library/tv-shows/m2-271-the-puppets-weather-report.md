# The Puppet's Weather Report

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's variety television, where a puppet host insists on controlling the weather with classroom props*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A puppet host forecasts sunshine using paper suns and a desk fan, while the real weather keeps arriving through the studio window.

Cast: Puppet host Puck, a gibbon; presenter Nara, a macaque; weather watcher Odo, a baboon; stagehand Seli, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Clouds on Cue"

### Cold Open

[BRIGHT STUDIO SET. PAPER SUNS HANG OVER THE DESK.]

PUCK: We have planned a sunny day.

NARA: The forecast says rain.

ODO: The paper sun is ready.

NARA: The forecast is not.

Seli lowers the map and faces the camera.

SELI: We will report what we see.

[SFX: RAIN TAPS WINDOW.]

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[NARA FACES A HAND-DRAWN WEATHER MAP.]

Nara explains that the forecast calls for showers by noon. Puck says the show has already built a sunny set. Odo has placed a desk fan behind the paper clouds to make them move.

PUCK: We should keep the forecast cheerful.

NARA: We should keep it accurate.

SELI: The blue paper is supposed to be rain.

PUCK: The rain is backstage.

Odo starts the fan. The paper clouds slide off their clips and fall onto the weather map.

ODO: The clouds have arrived.

NARA: The clouds are on the floor.

Puck asks Seli to clip the paper suns back up. She does, but one sun covers the storm symbol.

PUCK: Now the storm is sunny.

NARA: The symbol is hidden.

### Act Two

[THE STUDIO WINDOW OPENS A CRACK.]

A gust blows the paper map off its board. Nara catches it before it reaches the floor. The real rain has started, and droplets tap steadily against the glass.

ODO: The weather is making sound effects.

PUCK: It is taking over the show.

Nara moves the microphone away from the window. Puck asks for a cloud puppet. Seli holds a cotton cloud above the desk. The desk fan blows it into Odo's face.

ODO: We have fog.

NARA: We have cotton.

Puck asks Odo to report from outside. Odo opens the door, and a gust flips his umbrella inside out.

PUCK: The umbrella has turned into a flower.

ODO: It has broken.

NARA: We should close the door.

Puck insists they include an outdoor report. Seli holds the umbrella while Odo resets its spokes. The rain stops for a moment, then begins again.

[THE CREW TESTS A SECOND FORECAST.]

Odo brings out a jar marked "sunshine." It is filled with yellow paper circles. Puck wants to sprinkle them over the set. Nara says the forecast does not call for falling suns.

ODO: They are confetti.

NARA: The show is not a celebration.

Seli opens the jar. A draft from the door sends circles across the floor. The crew chases them while the camera keeps rolling.

PUCK: The sun is escaping.

NARA: It is paper.

Puck catches one circle and places it on the map. It covers the rain symbol again.

PUCK: The storm is gone.

NARA: The storm is under the sun.

Odo moves the circle aside. Nara points to the actual forecast: showers are possible, but the afternoon may clear.

SELI: We can show both.

Puck asks if the set can have a sunny side and a rainy side. Seli rotates the map board. The storm icon is now visible beside the paper sun.

PUCK: The weather has two moods.

NARA: The forecast has two periods.

### Act Three

[THE CREW RESETS THE MAP.]

Nara replaces the paper suns with a clear overlay that shows clouds and rain. Puck asks if the show can keep one sun on the map for the audience.

NARA: The sun may come out after the rain.

PUCK: Then it can arrive at the end.

Seli clips the map to the board. Odo turns off the desk fan. The cotton cloud stays in place.

ODO: The forecast is stable.

NARA: The weather is not.

The rain softens. Nara says showers will continue through noon, then clear. Puck asks if they can demonstrate a rainbow. Odo holds up a prism, but the sun is still behind clouds.

PUCK: The rainbow is late.

NARA: We cannot schedule it.

SELI: The prism is ready.

The sun comes through the window briefly, casting a small band of color on the desk. Everyone looks down.

PUCK: The rainbow has arrived.

NARA: It is a reflection.

PUCK: It is on the show.

### Tag

[THE STUDIO WINDOW IS CLOSED.]

Puck places the paper sun beside the weather map rather than over it.

PUCK: Tomorrow will be sunny.

NARA: Tomorrow has not been forecast yet.

ODO: The paper sun is ready.

[SFX: ONE RAIN DROP, THEME OUT.]
