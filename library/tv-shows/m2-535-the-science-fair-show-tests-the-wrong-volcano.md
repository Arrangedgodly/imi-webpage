# The Science Fair Show Tests the Wrong Volcano

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: science demonstrations that favor a spectacular reaction over the experiment students prepared*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Pavo, a capuchin; student scientist Kiri, a macaque; science teacher Oda, an orangutan; producer Vali, a lemur; camera operator Selo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCIENCE FAIR SET - DAY

Kiri sets a small paper volcano on the table. Vali rolls in a much larger prop mountain.

PAVO: Our biggest eruption yet!

KIRI: Mine uses a spoonful of vinegar.

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Vali says the audience expects a huge blast. Oda asks Kiri to explain her model. She plans to compare two mixtures and record which one foams longer. Pavo holds up the oversized prop. It has a hidden tube connected to a pump.

KIRI: That is not part of my experiment.

Selo frames the pump instead of the model. Oda moves the camera to the labels: mixture A and mixture B. Vali asks for a dramatic countdown. Kiri says the results need to be observed at equal intervals. Pavo counts down anyway. The pump sends a burst of confetti from the prop mountain, covering both sample labels.

ACT TWO

INT. BACKSTAGE - LATER

Oda helps Kiri replace the labels. Vali wants to repeat the confetti shot. Kiri asks him to keep the two cups separate. Pavo tries to put the prop volcano behind the table, but its tube catches on the backdrop. Selo films the crew untangling it; the spectacle is now a mountain being slowly dragged sideways.

Oda checks the samples and explains the planned comparison. Vali asks for a winner. Kiri says the first cup is still bubbling, so she has not recorded the second interval. Pavo asks whether the audience can vote for the taller foam. Kiri points out that the cups have different amounts. Oda resets them to equal marks.

INT. FAIR SET - MOMENTS LATER

Kiri pours measured amounts into two clear cups. Oda starts a stopwatch only after both are ready. The foam rises modestly. Vali signals Selo to zoom in; the camera shows a foam bubble and loses both cups. Selo widens the shot. Pavo leans toward the microphone and whispers that a smaller eruption is still television. Kiri records the heights on her chart.

ACT THREE

INT. SCIENCE FAIR SET - AFTERNOON

Kiri repeats the comparison once with the same measured amounts. Oda calls out the time marks. The first cup keeps foaming longer. Kiri records the result beside the labels. Vali asks Pavo to announce a champion volcano. Kiri says the result applies to these mixtures, not to mountains. Pavo looks at the huge prop, whose tube is still tied to the backdrop.

Oda gives the camera a close view of the chart: equal amounts, timed observations, one clear difference. Selo frames the cups beside the written result. Vali asks for an eruption shot before the segment closes. Kiri places the prop's pump controller on the floor and steps away. Pavo accidentally presses it with his heel. The prop coughs one final burst of confetti, but this time the labeled cups stay clear. Oda turns off the pump and asks Vali to remove it from the display. Vali carries the mountain away, tube first. It turns sideways in the doorway and blocks the exit. Kiri holds up the chart for the camera while the crew squeezes past.

Pavo reads the result accurately and asks Kiri to explain what she would test next. She proposes changing one variable at a time. Vali starts to raise the countdown card, sees the samples, and puts it down. Selo ends on the chart rather than the prop mountain. The audience applauds the clear result; one piece of confetti drifts onto the word "equal."


Vali asks for a winner card to appear over the chart. Kiri says the test compared duration, not size, and the card should not turn one measure into a championship. Pavo tries to place the prop mountain behind her for scale. Its loose tube swings into frame and knocks the blank winner card onto the floor. Oda picks it up and writes the measured observation: cup A foamed longer in this trial. Vali asks whether that line can fit on the screen. Selo widens the camera until both cups and the words are readable. Kiri points out that the second mixture was prepared with the same spoon and measured amount. Oda records the method beside the result. The crew takes down the trophy graphic before Pavo can cue it.

TAG

INT. FAIR SET - AFTER THE SHOW

Oda stores the cups separately. Kiri pins up the chart. Vali leaves the pump controller with the props crew.

FINAL IMAGE: The small volcano model sits beside its measured chart while the oversized mountain waits sideways in the doorway.
