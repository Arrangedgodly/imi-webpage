# The Talent of Staying Still

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: talent competition convention, where a contestant's successful stillness act conflicts with increasingly intrusive stage effects*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A slow loris enters a talent contest with a quiet balancing act, but the host keeps adding lights, music and a spinning platform.

Cast: Host Tavi, a macaque; contestant Luma, a slow loris; judge Piri, a gibbon; stage manager Odo, a baboon; and drummer Miri, a capuchin.

Episode: "Hold That Pose"

COLD OPEN

INT. TALENT STAGE, NIGHT

Tavi announces the next act. Luma stands beside a low branch with a leaf balanced on one finger. The audience waits. The leaf stays in place.

TAVI: Remarkable control.

A spotlight swings onto the stage. the shadow of Luma moves; the leaf does not.

PIRI: The shadow has entered the contest.

ACT ONE

Luma explains that she can balance the leaf without rushing. Tavi asks if she can add a trick. She says the balance is the trick. Odo sets a small platform under her feet. It spins slowly. Luma steps off before it moves.

TAVI: We need a bigger moment.

PIRI: The moment is already happening.

Miri plays a drumroll. The vibration makes the leaf tremble. Luma asks the drummer to stop. The leaf settles. The audience applauds quietly.

ACT TWO

INT. BACKSTAGE, LATER

The producers suggest a wind machine. Luma says wind is part of the challenge, but the machine is too strong. Odo tests it with an empty leaf. The leaf flies into the judging table. Piri catches it. Tavi calls it a rehearsal success.

Luma chooses a broad leaf and tests her balance on the still stage. A camera operator moves for a close shot. The floor vibrates. Luma steps down to protect the leaf. She asks Odo to mark a quiet zone around the platform. Odo places soft pads beneath the camera stand.

The crew lowers the lights and turns off the fan. Miri taps a small rhythm with her fingers instead of the drum. Luma returns to her place. The leaf balances. The audience leans forward. One judge begins to clap, then stops so the vibrations do not disturb the act.

ACT TWO-B

INT. BACKSTAGE, LATER

The producers propose a new round: Luma must balance three leaves while the stage rotates. Luma refuses the spinning platform. Piri tells Tavi the act needs a finale. Tavi asks what would make the balance harder without disturbing Luma. Odo suggests a small fan at a safe distance.

Miri tests the fan with a loose leaf. It shifts gently, then settles. Luma chooses a broad leaf and places it on the branch. The fan starts. The leaf moves slightly, but she adjusts her finger without losing balance. The audience watches quietly. Tavi holds up a sign asking for no applause until the act is finished.

The producers add a countdown clock. Its ticking makes Miri's drum unnecessary. Piri asks if the clock can be turned off. Odo disconnects it. The red numbers disappear, and the stage feels still. Luma balances the leaf for a full minute. When the fan stops, she takes the leaf in her hand.

TAVI: The act is complete.

PIRI: We did not need the platform.

The judges discuss the score. One gives points for control, another for composition. Odo says the stage crew should be credited for keeping the floor stable. Luma says the quiet audience helped most. Tavi asks if the judges can agree on a single winner. They show the same score.

ACT TWO-C

INT. TALENT STAGE, FINAL

The crew rehearses the quiet setup. Odo tapes the fan cord to the floor so no one will trip. Piri removes the spinning platform and rolls it to storage. Tavi checks that the spotlight is fixed and pointed away from Luma's eyes. Miri tests one soft drum tap from the back row; the leaf stays balanced.

Luma chooses where to stand. The stage manager asks if she wants a cue. She says a small hand signal is enough. Tavi explains the act to the audience without introducing a countdown. The crowd leans forward. One baby monkey begins to clap; a parent gently holds its hands. Luma waits until the room is quiet again.

She raises her finger with the broad leaf balanced on it. The fan starts. It moves the leaf once, and Luma adjusts. A gust from the open side door begins to affect it. Odo quietly closes the door. The leaf steadies. Piri does not cue music. The moment lasts long enough that the judges stop looking at their scorecards and watch.

Afterward, the judges ask Luma what she was thinking about. She says she was listening to the air. Tavi asks if the act can be repeated for a close-up. Luma lowers her hand and says the leaf has had enough of the show.

ACT THREE



INT. TALENT STAGE, FINAL

Luma holds the balance for a full minute. The leaf remains still. Tavi announces the score. Piri gives the highest mark. Odo says the stage crew deserves credit for keeping the platform stable. Luma bows, and the leaf falls into her hand.

TAVI: A perfect finish.

LUMA: It fell when I moved.

TAG

Backstage, the wind machine turns on by mistake. The leaf floats down the hall. Everyone follows it at a walk. It lands in the trophy cup. Piri says the leaf has won the competition.
