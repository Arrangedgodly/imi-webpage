# After Midnight with Margo

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk show*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A late-night host interviews guests in a cramped studio, but tonight's planned celebrity has missed the ferry, leaving the band, crew, and a very opinionated audience to fill the hour.

Cast: Margo, a quick-witted macaque host; Ellis, her gibbon bandleader; Venn, a red colobus producer who keeps a strict rundown; Pippa, a young capuchin stagehand; and Mr. Crumb, the theater's elderly tortoise caretaker.

## Episode: "The Guest Who Missed the Boat"

### Cold open

INT. SMALL STUDIO, NIGHT

Margo walks to her desk as the band hits a flourish. She checks the empty guest chair.

MARGO: Our guest is on a ferry. We are told the ferry is moving. We are told our guest is not.

Ellis plays a soft boat horn on his trombone. The audience laughs. A stagehand holds up a sign reading "APPLAUSE FOR TRANSPORTATION."

### Act one

Venn says the guest's ferry is delayed. Their planned conversation and cooking segment are both stuck aboard it.

Pippa suggests Mr. Crumb, the theater caretaker. He enters with a mop and asks them to clear the stage so he can fix a leak.

Margo asks him to sit. He refuses because his shell makes the chair squeak. Ellis adjusts the chair. Mr. Crumb sits. The audience applauds. Margo asks how long he has worked at the theater. He says forty-one years, though he spent one summer away because the roof fell in.

### Act two

The chef is also on the ferry. Margo and Pippa try to make midnight toast, but the outlet is being inspected. Mr. Crumb refuses to move the lobby stove into the studio.

Ellis and the band make music from the squeaky chair, dripping bucket, and light switch. Mr. Crumb identifies the leak above the balcony. The audience asks about ghosts and the theater's first show. He recalls forgotten costumes and a laugh from a line he could not hear backstage.

Venn says they have exceeded the guest segment. The audience rejects a rerun. Pippa says they came to see people, not a clock.


INT. STUDIO, LATER

Margo turns the empty guest chair toward the camera. Ellis plays a short ferry horn. Pippa holds up the revised rundown, which has one page and a wet corner.

MARGO: Our guest is delayed.
VENN: The ferry is in fog.
ELLIS: The horn is early.
MARGO: Is the guest onboard?
VENN: We do not know.
PIPPA: I called the dock.
MARGO: What did they say?
PIPPA: The guest is waiting.
ELLIS: For the ferry?
PIPPA: For another ferry.
MARGO: That is a long interview.
VENN: We have a cooking segment.
PIPPA: The chef is also waiting.
MARGO: How many ferries?
VENN: Three.
ELLIS: Then three guests.
MARGO: We have one chair.
MR. CRUMB: I can bring chairs.
MARGO: You are the guest.
CRUMB: I am carrying a mop.
VENN: That's not on the rundown.
CRUMB: The leak is.
PIPPA: Which leak?
CRUMB: The one above you.
MARGO: Should we move?
CRUMB: The bucket is ready.
ELLIS: Can the bucket sing?
CRUMB: It can catch water.
MARGO: Better than singing.
VENN: We need a segment.
PIPPA: Ask about the theater.
CRUMB: I know the theater.
MARGO: How long?
CRUMB: Forty-one years.
ELLIS: Any ghosts?
CRUMB: Several costumes.
MARGO: Any famous ones?
CRUMB: The wet ones.
PIPPA: The roof leaked then?
CRUMB: It always has.
VENN: That's a callback.
CRUMB: It's a bucket.
MARGO: Tell us the blackout story.
CRUMB: The drummer kept playing.
ELLIS: Was it good?
CRUMB: It was very dark.
PIPPA: The ferry guest is calling.
MARGO: Put them on.
VENN: They are seasick.
MARGO: Let them go home.
CRUMB: I can finish the story.

### Act three

Margo asks the audience whether they would rather watch a rerun or hear the caretaker tell one more story. They call for a story. Mr. Crumb describes a night when a storm knocked out the lights during a dance show. The performers kept dancing by following the music and each other's hands. He held a lantern in the aisle until the power returned.

Ellis asks whether the orchestra kept playing. Mr. Crumb says the drummer did, because he thought the blackout was part of the show. Ellis starts a gentle rhythm on the desk. The band joins him. Pippa and Margo tap the beat against mugs. Mr. Crumb hums the melody he remembers from that night. It becomes a quiet song, rough at the edges and easy to follow.

The ferry guest finally calls in from the harbor. The producer offers to put them on screen. Margo asks if they have something to say. The guest says they are cold, seasick, and sorry. Margo tells them to get home safely. The studio laughs, then applauds.

Venn writes "roof check" on next week's rundown. Margo thanks Mr. Crumb for filling the chair. He says the chair was already there.

### Tag

The show ends. Margo asks the camera operator to hold one extra moment. A drop falls from the ceiling into Mr. Crumb's bucket.

MARGO: We have our first returning guest.

The bucket rings like a bell. Ellis plays the boat horn. Mr. Crumb rolls the bucket away, keeping perfect time. Pippa switches off the studio lights, but one work lamp remains on above the empty guest chair. Margo bows toward it, and the band plays the theme softly as the crew carries the mop and chairs back to storage.
