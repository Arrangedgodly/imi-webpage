# The Hospital Drama of a Wilted Leaf

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: hospital dramas, urgent monitors, rival specialists, and doctors who announce diagnoses before checking the plant's water*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A hospital team rushes a wilted houseplant into the emergency ward, where two specialists compete to identify the crisis while the janitor quietly spots an empty watering can.

Cast: Dr. Vex, a macaque; Dr. Nala, a capuchin; nurse Omi, an orangutan; intern Tavi, a gibbon; janitor Rell, a ring-tailed lemur; and administrator Palla, a mandrill.

## Episode: "Code Green"

### Cold Open

[HOSPITAL CORRIDOR. A PLANT DROOPS.]

VEX: We are losing the leaf.

NALA: Get the monitor.

Rell pours water into the pot.

PALLA: It is a plant, not a patient.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[EMERGENCY WARD.]

Administrator Palla has brought in the lobby fern, which has wilted during a heat wave. Dr. Vex orders an urgent assessment. Dr. Nala examines the stem. Nurse Omi attaches a paper monitor to the pot.

OMI: There is no pulse.

VEX: The monitor is not connected.

Tavi wheels in a cart of instruments. The fern's pot is dry. Rell arrives with a watering can, but Vex tells him to wait until the diagnosis is clear.

RELL: The soil is dry.

NALA: We need a differential.

VEX: It could be root shock, leaf blight, or a rare condition from the south greenhouse.

NALA: It could be heat stress.

Palla asks for an update. Vex gives a dramatic one: "The plant's condition is fragile." Nala asks whether the lobby window was open. Palla says the sun was shining directly on the fern.

### Act Two

[EXAM ROOM.]

The doctors compete to run tests. Vex orders a scan of the root ball. Nala tests soil moisture with a wooden stick. Tavi brings a lamp to inspect the leaf; its heat makes the plant droop further.

OMI: The test is making the patient worse.

TAVI: It is a desk lamp.

VEX: We need a consult.

Nala calls the greenhouse specialist. The specialist asks whether the plant has been watered. Everyone goes quiet. Rell fills the can. Palla says the maintenance schedule shows the fern was watered two days ago.

Vex orders a second soil test. Tavi brings a pH strip and dips it into the watering tray instead of the soil. The strip turns a bright color. Vex announces an acidic emergency. Nala points out that the tray has cleaning solution in it; Rell used it to wipe the table before the fern arrived.

NALA: That sample is not from the plant.

VEX: The chart says abnormal.

OMI: The chart is reading the tray.

Omi removes the strip and labels the tray for cleaning supplies. Nala uses a fresh probe in the soil. The pH is normal. The root ball is dry at the top and damp at the bottom, so Rell loosens the surface without flooding the pot. Palla asks whether the fern needs surgery. Vex says the only procedure is moving it out of the sun.

The hospital crew wheels the fern across the hall. The plant's leaves brush against the doorway, and Tavi holds the door open. The monitor cable gets caught on a chair. Omi removes it before the pot tips. Everyone pauses while Rell steadies the fern.

RELL: We have an intact root ball.

VEX: That is a good outcome.

NALA: The weather has been hot. The soil dried faster.

VEX: That does not explain the brown edge.

NALA: It does if the leaf is scorched by the window.

They move the fern away from direct sun. Rell waters it slowly. Omi checks the drainage tray. The water begins to collect.

### Act Three

[DOCTORS' STATION.]

The team waits for the leaf to recover. It takes time. Vex checks the plant every thirty seconds. Nala suggests leaving it alone. The monitor remains blank. Omi removes it. Tavi creates a "plant chart" with the watering times and sunlight location.

PALLA: Are we charging for this treatment?

VEX: The hospital does not treat plants.

PALLA: Then why is it in the hospital?

NALA: It was in the lobby.

Rell moves the fern to the shaded courtyard, where it can recover. The doctors agree to check the soil before announcing another emergency. Palla updates the building plan to place plants away from direct afternoon sun.

VEX: The leaf is stable.

NALA: It is no longer in the window.

OMI: And it has water.

### Tag

[COURTYARD.]

A new plant is delivered. Vex reaches for the monitor. Nala hands him the watering can. He checks the soil first, then waters only when needed.

PALLA: That is not a dramatic ending.

RELL: It is a healthy one.

The leaf straightens slightly as the afternoon shade covers it.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
