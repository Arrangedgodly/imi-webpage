# Sunshine Until Further Notice

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cheerful weather broadcasts that maintain a sunny tone while ignoring what the forecast actually requires viewers to do*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Weather host Luta wants a bright, upbeat forecast. Producer Pippa has sold the opening segment to ClearDay hats and keeps insisting on a sunny graphic. Field reporter Koro needs viewers to move the outdoor book fair before the storm arrives, but the sponsor block delays the warning until rain reaches the books.

Cast: Luta, a macaque weather host; Koro, a gorilla field reporter; Pippa, a lemur producer; and Nix, a capuchin camera operator.

## Episode: "Bring an Umbrella and a Tarp"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. LUTA STANDS BEFORE A GREEN SCREEN. THE GRAPHIC SHOWS A SUN WITH SUNGLASSES.]

LUTA: Good morning, grove. We have a beautiful day ahead, with a small chance of a quick shower.

[PIPPA ENTERS HOLDING A SOAKED WEATHER MAP.]

PIPPA: Koro says the fairground is already wet.

LUTA: It is a quick shower.

[PIPPA HOLDS UP A CLEARDAY SPONSOR CARD WITH A SUN OVER THE FAIRGROUND.]

PIPPA: Their segment bought the sunny opening. Keep the cloud off the logo until the break.

[PIPPA WRINGS WATER FROM A CORNER OF THE MAP. A SMALL PUDDLE FORMS ON THE DESK.]

NIX: Should I tilt the camera so the puddle looks like a lake?

LUTA: No. We are not exaggerating the weather before the first segment.

### Act One

[THE WEATHER GRAPHIC CHANGES TO A BLUE CLOUD. LUTA SMILES AT CAMERA.]

LUTA: At eleven, a few drops may visit the eastern field.

PIPPA: The book fair is in the eastern field.

LUTA: Then the books may enjoy a refreshing visit.

[PIPPA POINTS TO THE RADAR. A WIDE GREEN BAND COVERS THE FIELD.]

PIPPA: That is not a few drops.

LUTA: The green means light rain.

PIPPA: The legend says heavy rain.

[LUTA TURNS TO THE LEGEND. NIX ZOOMS IN. IT SAYS "HEAVY RAIN: MOVE PAPER UNDER COVER."]

LUTA: The legend is being very direct today.

[KORO APPEARS ON THE STUDIO MONITOR FROM THE FAIRGROUND. HE STANDS BESIDE A TABLE OF BOOKS.]

[PIPPA SLIDES THE SPONSOR SUN OVER THE RADAR. THE HEAVY-RAIN BAND DISAPPEARS.]

KORO: I can no longer see the fairground on your map.

LUTA: Pippa, move the logo.

PIPPA: The sponsor has twelve seconds left.

[KORO LOOKS DOWN AT A BOOK WITH A WET CORNER. HE HOLDS IT UP TO CAMERA.]

KORO: We have twelve tables outside, two tarps, and one gazebo with a leak.

LUTA: How is the atmosphere?

KORO: Damp around the table legs.

LUTA: Any sunshine?

KORO: I can point the camera toward the yellow tent.

[PIPPA SIGNALS FOR A COMMERCIAL BREAK. LUTA KEEPS SMILING.]

LUTA: After the break, we will explore whether the rain is making a temporary or permanent visit.

### Act Two

[FIELD. KORO AND VOLUNTEERS LIFT BOOK BOXES. THE CAMERA FOLLOWS A VOLUNTEER CARRYING A CARTON.]

KORO: We are moving the books into the library hall before the storm.

[STUDIO. LUTA WATCHES.]

LUTA: An exciting change of plans. Our forecast suggests a possible adjustment to the outdoor reading experience.

PIPPA: Say "move indoors."

LUTA: We have not confirmed the hall's capacity.

KORO: It holds forty chairs and a lot of books. We have sixteen volunteers.

PIPPA: Confirmed.

[LUTA TRIES TO POINT AT THE MAP. THE GREEN SCREEN GRAPHIC SLIDES. A CARTOON SUN COVERS THE FAIRGROUND.]

LUTA: The sun is obstructing the warning area.

NIX: I can move it.

[NIX DRAGS THE SUN. IT LANDS ON THE STORM BAND.]

LUTA: Now the storm is sunny.

PIPPA: Fine. Read the radar wearing this ClearDay hat.

[PIPPA PUTS A HAT ON LUTA. ITS BRIM COVERS HER EYES. LUTA TURNS IT BACKWARD WITHOUT BREAKING HER SMILE.]

[LUTA FACES THE CAMERA.]

LUTA: The rain will arrive before eleven. The book fair is moving into the library hall. Please bring boxes through the east entrance and leave the reading room aisle clear.

[PIPPA NODS. KORO HEARS THE UPDATE ON HIS EARPIECE.]

KORO: You heard her. East entrance, aisle clear.

[THE VOLUNTEERS MOVE QUICKLY. ONE CART CANNOT FIT THROUGH THE DOOR.]

KORO: We need to take the books off the top shelf of the cart.

[THEY UNLOAD THE CART. THE RAIN STARTS OUTSIDE. THE LAST BOX MAKES IT UNDER COVER.]

[KORO CHECKS THE TARP OVER THE GAZEBO. WATER DRIPS INTO A BUCKET ALREADY USED TO HOLD DONATED BOOKMARKS.]

KORO: We need a second bucket.

PIPPA: The forecast mentioned localized puddles.

KORO: This one has a zip code.

[A VOLUNTEER CARRIES THE BOOKMARKS INSIDE. THE CAMERA TILTS UP TO SHOW THE GAZEBO ROOF SAGGING.]

### Act Three

[STUDIO. LUTA POINTS TO A NEW MAP WITH THE LIBRARY HALL MARKED.]

LUTA: The storm has reached the field. The books are inside, the volunteers are dry, and the reading room remains open.

PIPPA: ClearDay has one more sponsor read.

LUTA: They can sponsor the tarp report. The forecast stays on screen.

PIPPA: That is the forecast.

LUTA: It is also the report.

NIX: Can I put the sun back on the map?

LUTA: Put it over tomorrow.

[THE GRAPHIC SHOWS A SMALL SUN BESIDE A CLOUD. LUTA LOOKS AT THE LEGEND BEFORE SPEAKING.]

LUTA: Tomorrow is partly sunny. Pippa, you can sell them that half.

[FIELD. KORO CLOSES THE LIBRARY DOOR AS RAIN RUNS OFF THE TARP.]

KORO: The tarp is still outside.

PIPPA: Leave it there. It has done enough.

[THEY CARRY THE LAST BOOK BOX AWAY.]

### Tag

[STUDIO. NIX REMOVES THE SPONSOR SUN GRAPHIC. PIPPA HOLDS ITS MATCHING PAPER CARD UNDER THE LEAK.]

LUTA: ClearDay hats: bright enough for a sunny logo.

[A DROP FALLS ON THE CARD. THE PAPER SUN SLIDES INTO THE PUDDLE.]

NIX: The sponsor is under water.

LUTA: Cut to the tarp.
