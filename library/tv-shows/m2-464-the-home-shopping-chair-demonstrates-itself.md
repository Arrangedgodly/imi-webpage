# The Home Shopping Chair Demonstrates Itself

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live home-shopping television, countdown pressure, and hosts who sell furniture while sitting on it*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Tavi, a gibbon; demonstrator Roko, an orangutan; caller Mimi, a macaque; floor manager Seli, a baboon; camera operator Jori, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - DAY

A folding chair stands on a small platform. A price card reads TODAY ONLY. The host sits on the chair while the demonstrator tries to unfold a second one.

TAVI: This chair has never been more comfortable.

ROKO: It is still folded.

[The host's chair clicks and folds slightly. Tavi stands quickly. The floor manager raises a stop card.] 

SELI: Demonstration pause. Check the hinge lock.

TAVI: We are still live.

SELI: Then we are live while checking it.

ACT ONE

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Roko places the chair on the floor and checks the hinge. A safety latch is not fully engaged. Seli asks the camera operator to keep the close-up on the hinge, not the host. Tavi reads the product copy, which claims the chair opens with one hand.

ROKO: It opens with one hand after the latch is clear.

TAVI: I opened it with one hand.

SELI: You sat before the second click.

[The demonstrator opens the chair fully and presses the latch until it clicks. Jori frames the hinge and the legs. The host asks Roko to repeat the demonstration at double speed. Roko refuses and repeats it slowly.] 

ROKO: One hand opens it. One push secures it. Then it can be sat on.

TAVI: We have a three-step sale.

ROKO: We have a chair.

[The studio phone rings. Seli answers and puts caller Mimi on speaker. Mimi asks whether the chair fits under a standard desk. The host answers yes before checking its height.] 

MIMI: I have the desk measurements here.

TAVI: So do we, somewhere.

ACT TWO

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - LATER

Roko measures the chair height and compares it with the desk card. It fits under the desk when folded, but not while open. The host wants to say it fits either way. Seli shakes her head and shows the actual product dimensions.

SELI: It stores under the desk. It does not sit there open.

MIMI: That is what I needed to know.

TAVI: Our countdown is down to two minutes.

ROKO: The shipping offer lasts another hour.

TAVI: The graphic says two minutes.

SELI: The graphic is for the segment. The offer is on the product page.

[The floor manager changes the screen to show the two dimensions. The camera operator zooms out so both the folded chair and the open chair are visible. Tavi tries to create urgency by leaning against the display chair; Seli moves it away from the host.] 

TAVI: I wanted to show how it looks beside a sofa.

ROKO: You were about to sit on the unlocked one.

[The host looks at the latch and waits for Roko to secure it. Mimi asks how to clean the seat. Roko explains the fabric care label and turns the chair to show the label.] 

[Mimi calls back to ask whether ordering two chairs doubles the countdown discount. Seli checks the product page; the listed shipping terms are the same for one or two chairs.] 

TAVI: We can answer without a second offer.

ROKO: The chair folds separately. You do not need to buy a set.

MIMI: One is enough for my desk.

[The host removes the graphic showing a stack of chairs. Jori frames one folded chair and one open chair side by side.] 

ACT THREE

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - AFTERNOON

The live segment reaches its last minute. Roko demonstrates opening and locking the chair one final time. Tavi reads the price and dimensions accurately. The countdown graphic reaches zero, but the product page remains available. Seli turns off the dramatic chime before it can sound.

TAVI: The special segment is over. The chair remains available at the listed price.

MIMI: Thank you. I can compare it with my desk now.

ROKO: That is the right next step.

TAVI: We do not have to buy before the countdown ends?

SELI: No. The countdown was for the broadcast slot.

[The host stands beside the chair rather than sitting on it. Roko folds the demonstration model after releasing its latch. Jori captures the folding action in one uncut shot.] 

ROKO: Folded, it stores under the desk. Open and locked, it supports a person. Those are different states.

TAVI: We should have said that first.

[The floor manager holds up the STOP card, then flips it to SEGMENT COMPLETE. The host waits until the camera operator gives the cut signal.] 

TAG

Tavi carefully checks the latch on a second chair before sitting. Seli nods, and the host sits without a sound effect.

FINAL IMAGE: The folded chair slides beneath the desk while the open, locked chair stays beside the host.
