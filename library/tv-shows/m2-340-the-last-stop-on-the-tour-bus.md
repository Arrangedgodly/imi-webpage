# The Last Stop on the Tour Bus

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentaries that make routine transit sound like an exotic expedition*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A documentary crew rides the village shuttle one stop to explain the drama of a short commute.

Cast: Host Pava, a gibbon; driver Odo, a baboon; passenger Nara, a macaque; field producer Kito, a capuchin.

Episode: "Across the Garden"

COLD OPEN

EXT. SHUTTLE STOP - DAY

Pava stands beside a painted bench and a small shuttle.

PAVA: We are about to cross the village.

NARA: It is one stop.

ODO: The ride takes two minutes.

Kito raises the camera. The shuttle door opens with a squeak.

PAVA: Our journey begins.

ACT ONE

INT. SHUTTLE - CONTINUOUS

Nara boards carrying a basket of herbs. Odo asks her to sit in the middle so the basket does not block the aisle. Pava interviews her about the journey. Nara says she is going to the other side of the garden to deliver herbs.

Pava asks what she expects to see along the route. Nara points out the pond, the tool shed, and the old fig tree. Pava calls each a landmark. Odo says the shuttle passes them every day.

The vehicle pulls away. Kito films through the window. The camera picks up a reflection of Pava's face over the landscape. She asks if the crew can move to the other side. Odo says they may not stand while the shuttle is moving.

Pava narrates the ride as a passage through distinct ecosystems: pond, shed, fig tree. Nara says they are all in the same garden. The shuttle bumps over a small root. Pava grabs the seat.

PAVA: An unexpected terrain shift.

ODO: The road has one bump.

ACT TWO

EXT. POND STOP - MOMENTS LATER

The shuttle pauses at the pond. Odo asks if anyone is getting off. No one is. Pava wants a scenic interview, so Nara steps outside with the basket and stands beside the stop sign.

Kito asks her to say what the pond means to her. Nara says it gives the frogs water. Pava asks if she feels connected to the frogs. Nara says she is delivering herbs to a neighbor who lives beyond the pond.

Odo warns that the shuttle will leave in thirty seconds. Nara gets back on. The camera crew follows, and Kito's lens cap falls. It rolls under the seat. Nara reaches down and retrieves it before the vehicle moves.

PAVA: A close call with our equipment.

NARA: It was a lens cap.

The shuttle continues. Pava asks Odo how he became a driver. He says he passed the route test. The test required knowing where the brakes are and when to stop. Pava asks if he has a favorite turn. Odo says the turn by the shed is wide enough for the vehicle.

ACT TWO-B

INT. SHUTTLE - LATER

The shuttle approaches the last stop. A sign reads "Garden Gate." Pava asks the crew to prepare a final panorama. Kito opens the side window, but the breeze blows the map into the aisle. Nara pins it under her basket.

Odo slows at an intermediate garden gate. A goat stands beside the curb. It is not blocking the road, but it looks toward the shuttle. Pava whispers that a local guardian has appeared. Odo waits. The goat steps away and begins eating grass. Nara stays aboard; the final stop is still ahead.

NARA: It lives here.

PAVA: A resident encounter.

Odo opens the door at the stop. Nara steps down, carrying the herbs. She is at the neighbor's house. The journey has taken two minutes and twenty seconds because of the goat.

Before the final stop, Odo announces that the shuttle is approaching the village gate. Pava asks him to describe the road conditions. Odo says the road is clear except for the root bump. Nara asks if the crew can keep the camera inside this time. Kito secures the lens cap and folds the map so it will not catch the breeze. The shuttle passes the fig tree without stopping. Pava asks if the tree has a story. Odo says it was planted by the garden committee. Nara says she has delivered herbs to the committee there before. Pava calls it a recurring destination.

ACT THREE

EXT. GARDEN GATE - DAY

Pava asks Nara if the journey changed her perspective. Nara says she saw the goat and delivered the herbs. Odo parks the shuttle in its marked spot. Kito retrieves the map from under the basket.

PAVA: We crossed a landscape of water, wood, fruit, and grass.

ODO: We drove one stop.

Nara hands the herb basket to her neighbor, who thanks her. The camera captures the exchange. Pava says the trip has shown how connected the village is.

TAG

The shuttle returns to the first stop. Pava boards.

PAVA: We must now make the return journey.

ODO: We are going back to the same place.
