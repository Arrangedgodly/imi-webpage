# The Envelope Behind the Curtain

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap operas, overheard confessions, dramatic entrances, and cliffhangers built around ordinary envelopes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Two siblings overhear a sentence about an envelope and spend the afternoon assuming it contains a life-changing secret.

Cast: Yara, a gibbon; Nilo, a baboon; Ebi, a capuchin; Sada, a macaque; Narrator, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. FAMILY HALL - DAY

Yara holds a sealed envelope. Nilo enters through one door; Ebi enters through another. They freeze when they see her.

NARRATOR: One envelope. Two siblings. A hallway with excellent acoustics.

YARA: I have to tell you what is inside.

NILO: What is inside?

Yara turns the envelope over. The camera cuts to her eyes.

YARA: I don't know.

ACT ONE

Yara says Sada gave her the envelope to deliver. Nilo heard Sada say, "It's time to make this official." Ebi heard "The key is inside." Each assumes the envelope contains news about a family dispute over a small storage room.

NILO: Sada has the key.

EBI: I heard her say the key is inside.

YARA: Inside the envelope?

Ebi looks at the envelope. It is too thin to hold a key.

EBI: Perhaps a very small key.

Sada enters. Nilo slips behind a curtain to listen. Ebi joins him. Yara stays in the open, holding the envelope.

SADA: You found it.

YARA: I haven't opened it.

SADA: You don't need to.

Nilo gasps behind the curtain. Ebi covers his mouth. The curtain falls over both of them.

ACT TWO

Sada asks Yara to deliver the envelope to the storage room caretaker. Yara asks what it is. Sada says it is a note. Nilo emerges, tangled in the curtain, and asks why the note needs to be delivered with such care.

SADA: Because it has the return key taped inside.

NILO: I knew it.

EBI: The envelope is too thin.

SADA: It is a paper key tag. The brass key is still on my ring.

Nilo says Sada should have told them. Sada says she did, but they were whispering. The narrator pauses for a dramatic sting. The sting is a single harp note played twice.

YARA: So the envelope is a note and a paper tag.

SADA: Correct.

NILO: What did you mean by "make this official"?

SADA: The storage room is being shared. I wrote down the new schedule.

EBI: I thought the room was being sold.

SADA: Why would I sell a room with a broken window?

ACT TWO-B

Nilo asks to see the schedule. Sada hands him the envelope. He turns it over and finds a small adhesive label on the back. Yara peels it off. It says "RETURN KEY TO CARETAKER." The label tears in half.

NILO: The message has been damaged.

SADA: It is still readable.

EBI: What does the note say?

SADA: Open it and read it.

Yara opens the envelope. A folded page falls to the floor. Nilo reaches for it, but the curtain is still caught around his ankle. He pulls the page toward him and rips one corner.

YARA: Stop pulling the note.

NILO: I'm trying to help.

SADA: Then release the curtain.

Nilo untangles himself. Ebi reads the schedule aloud: Yara gets the room in the morning, Nilo in the afternoon, and Sada on weekends. Sada says the arrangement lasts one month while the window is repaired.

EBI: That is the secret?

SADA: That is the schedule.

NILO: You said "make this official."

SADA: I signed the schedule.

NARRATOR: The mystery has reached the end of its paper.

The harp sting plays again. Sada starts to leave, but the envelope catches on the doorknob. Nilo interprets the stop as a sign and turns back. Yara frees the paper carefully.

NILO: It won't let you go.

SADA: The flap is stuck to the handle.

Ebi peels it loose and smooths the torn corner of the schedule. Nilo reads the names again and notices his afternoon is marked with a small star.

NILO: What does the star mean?

SADA: You said you wanted the window-repair shift.

ACT THREE

The four go to the storage room. Sada unlocks it with her brass key. She shows them a shelf, a small table, and the broken window covered by a board. Yara sticks the return tag inside the door. Nilo asks to trade his afternoon for Sada's weekend. Sada says they can discuss it after the month ends.

NILO: I thought you were selling the room.

SADA: You thought a key was inside an envelope.

NILO: It was a paper key tag.

SADA: That is not the same thing.

Ebi writes the schedule on the wall calendar. The marker runs out after the first name. Sada finds a second marker in the drawer. They finish the list together.

TAG

The narrator begins an ominous voice-over as Yara closes the storage room.

NARRATOR: Next time, a new envelope arrives.

YARA: It is a bill.

NARRATOR: The suspense deepens.

FINAL IMAGE: The family leaves the labeled envelope on the shelf beside the repair schedule, while Nilo checks the brass key is still on Sada's ring.
