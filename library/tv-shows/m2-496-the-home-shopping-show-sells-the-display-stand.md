# The Home Shopping Show Sells the Display Stand

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-shopping broadcasts that create a scarcity countdown around a sample item that cannot be shipped*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Gela, a macaque; demonstrator Tavo, an orangutan; producer Nuri, a capuchin; caller Sena, a lemur; floor manager Davi, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - DAY

Gela holds up a sturdy wooden display stand. A timer reads THREE LEFT.

GELA: Call now before this one is gone.

TAVO: It is the only one, and it belongs to the studio.

ACT ONE

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Nuri whispers that the warehouse shipment is delayed. Gela continues the pitch. Tavo demonstrates the stand's adjustable shelf, moving a potted plant from top to bottom. The stand wobbles. He steadies it with one hand. Nuri asks him to call it a limited display edition.

TAVO: It is a sample.

GELA: A sample of what your room could become.

Davi finds the product card. It describes a metal stand with wheels, not the wooden prop on set. Gela reads the features aloud while Tavo turns the wooden stand around, searching for wheels. The camera frames its feet close enough to hide the difference.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

A caller asks whether the stand arrives assembled. Nuri signals Gela to say yes. Tavo points to the screw kit taped under the shelf. The caller asks if the plant is included. Gela says it is not; the plant is a studio prop. Nuri starts the countdown at ten. The timer reaches zero, but the phone lines remain open.

GELA: We have sold through.

TAVO: We have not sold the stand.

Davi looks at the order screen. There are no orders. The display stand has been blocking the camera operator's path, and the phone button that marks a sale is stuck beneath one foot. Nuri asks the crew to lift it. Tavo raises one side; the phone button releases, and the screen shows zero orders.

[The studio timer starts over at ten. Nuri pulls the plug. The timer goes dark.]

Gela asks the caller to stay on the line. Sena says she wanted to know if the wooden sample had a finish she could touch. Tavo offers to let her inspect it after the show. Nuri signals to cut the call. Davi keeps the phone live, saying it is the first customer request the segment can actually fulfill.

INT. ORDER DESK - MOMENTS LATER

Davi runs a test order through the system. The form rejects the wooden stand because its item number belongs to the metal model. Gela tries to enter the sample as a one-of-a-kind offer. Nuri points out there is no shipping box for it. Tavo measures the sample against the box on set; the legs are too long. He folds the box flap closed and it pops open again. The caller asks if she can buy the plant instead. Gela checks the prop tag and finds the plant is rented.

INT. STUDIO FLOOR - MOMENTS LATER

Tavo demonstrates how the shelf locks. Gela reads from the product card and accidentally describes a metal wheel assembly. She catches herself, stops, and asks Nuri for the correct card. He has only the sales sheet. Davi writes SAMPLE DISPLAY - NOT FOR SALE on a board and holds it beside the stand. The camera finally widens enough to show the plain wooden legs.

ACT THREE

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - AFTERNOON

The shipment arrives in two boxes. Gela opens one on camera. Inside is a metal stand in pieces, with clear assembly instructions. Tavo compares it with the sample. The wheel parts are in a small bag taped inside the second box. Nuri tries to restart the countdown. Davi removes the timer from the table and asks Tavo to assemble one unit before they sell it.

Tavo fits the legs, locks the shelf and rolls the stand across the set. It stays level. Gela places the potted plant on it, then reads the price and shipping terms from the corrected card. Sena is still on the call. She decides to order one. The order screen registers a single sale. The countdown begins at one and reaches zero. Nothing else is claimed.

GELA: We are sold out.

TAVO: We had one order.

Nuri adds the sample stand to the studio inventory sheet. Davi wheels the new stand to the marked demonstration area. The phone button no longer sits beneath a leg. Sena asks if the plant comes with her order. Gela says no, but the finished stand does.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

The sample stand is wheeled back to storage. Gela removes the timer from the table. Nuri labels it COUNTDOWN DEMO. Davi tests the phone button; it rings normally.

FINAL IMAGE: The product card shows the assembled metal stand, while the wooden sample stands behind it under a clear NOT FOR SALE sign.
