# The Match Is a Matchbook

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating reality shows that infer lifelong compatibility from staged first impressions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Matchmaker Sula pairs two monkeys for a televised dinner based on their love of night walks. The producer keeps inventing compatibility tests while the pair, Lemi and Odo, try to order dinner without turning every preference into a destiny graphic.

Cast: Sula, a macaque host; Lemi, a capuchin gardener; Odo, a langur astronomer; and Fenn, a gorilla producer.

## Episode: "Under the Same Lamp"

### Cold Open

[DINNER SET. TWO CANDLES, TWO MENUS, AND A GRAPHIC READING "SOULMATES?" LEMI AND ODO SIT DOWN.] 

SULA: You both enjoy walks after sunset.

LEMI: I like checking the garden when it is cool.

ODO: I look for the moon.

[FENN CUES A ROMANTIC STRING STING. A CANDLE FLICKERS OUT.] 

SULA: A sign.

LEMI: It is a draft.

### Act One

[FENN GIVES THEM EACH A CARD WITH A QUESTION: "WHAT IS YOUR LOVE LANGUAGE?" ]

ODO: I brought a sky chart.

LEMI: I brought the seed list.

SULA: Those are thoughtful gifts.

[FENN DISPLAYS A COMPATIBILITY METER. IT RISES WHEN THE PAIR HOLD THE CARDS SIDE BY SIDE.]

FENN: The meter is at ninety percent.

LEMI: The cards are touching.

ODO: It is measuring paper.

[FENN PUTS A SECOND METER ON THE TABLE TO TRACK "CHEMISTRY." IT RISES WHEN THE CANDLE FLAME LEANS TOWARD THEM. SULA MOVES THE CANDLE AWAY FROM THE VENT.]

SULA: The room was drafty.

LEMI: The chart almost blew over.

ODO: We can hold it down with the menu.

[THE METER DROPS TO ZERO. FENN SHAKES THE CANDLE BASE AND THE NUMBER JUMPS TO 100 AGAIN.] 

FENN: We found a connection.

SULA: You found a button.

[THE METER DIPS WHEN ODO SETS HIS CARD DOWN. FENN PUTS IT BACK UP.] 

FENN: We need a dramatic dip before the connection.

SULA: Ask what they want for dinner.

### Act Two

[THE WAITSTAFF BRINGS TWO DIFFERENT DISHES. LEMI'S HAS A GARDEN SALAD. ODO'S HAS HOT SOUP.]

FENN: The meal choices are opposite.

LEMI: I ordered the salad because I had soup at lunch.

ODO: I chose soup because I was outside all afternoon.

[FENN PULLS A SMALL BELL FROM HIS POCKET. HE RINGS IT WHENEVER THE PAIR AGREE.] 

SULA: Please don't score their conversation.

[LEMI ASKS ODO ABOUT THE MOON CHART. HE SHOWS THE SKY POSITION FROM LAST NIGHT. LEMI POINTS OUT A GARDEN PATH THAT BLOCKED HIS VIEW OF THE SOUTHERN STARS.] 

ODO: I can move the telescope.

LEMI: The path is better with the low lamps.

[THEY SKETCH A ROUTE THAT KEEPS THE LIGHTS LOW AND LEAVES SPACE FOR THE TELESCOPE. FENN'S METER SPIKES TO 100 PERCENT.]

[FENN TRIES TO MOVE THE TABLE INTO A MOONLIT CORNER FOR THE FINAL SHOT. THE LOW LAMP SHINES DIRECTLY INTO ODO'S TELESCOPE. ODO TURNS IT AWAY FROM THE WINDOW.] 

ODO: The camera light is in the way.

LEMI: We can take the walk outside.

[SULA LEADS THEM TO THE GARDEN PATH. FENN CARRIES THE COMPATIBILITY METER; IT RUNS OUT OF BATTERY BEFORE THEY REACH THE GATE.]

FENN: The score is gone.

LEMI: The map still works.

[THE CAMERA CREW SETS UP A MOONLIT WALK SHOT. FENN HOLDS UP A CARD THAT SAYS "FIRST KISS?" LEMI AND ODO IGNORE IT AND POINT TO THE LOW LAMP THAT NEEDS A NEW BULB.] 

LEMI: That one has started flickering.

ODO: I can bring a ladder tomorrow.

FENN: Could you say it like a grand gesture?

ODO: I have a ladder.

[SULA TAKES THE CARD. THE METER, STILL DEAD, TUMBLES OUT OF FENN'S HAND AND LANDS ON THE CARD.] 

FENN: We have reached destiny.

ODO: We have drawn a path.

### Act Three

[SULA ASKS EACH WHETHER THEY WANT ANOTHER DATE. LEMI AND ODO SAY YES, BUT THEY ALSO SAY THEY WILL EACH KEEP THEIR OWN EVENING ROUTINE.] 

FENN: A split ending?

LEMI: We can meet for a walk and still have our own plans.

ODO: That is a good arrangement.

[THE COMPATIBILITY METER FLASHES "PERFECT MATCH." FENN ADDS A HEART GRAPHIC OVER THE ROUTE THEY DREW. IT COVERS THE LOW-LAMP MARKS.]

SULA: The practical detail is the part they agreed on.

[FENN TRIES TO GIVE THEM A MATCHING PAIR OF JACKETS. THE SLEEVES COVER THEIR SEPARATE MAPS. THEY PUT THE JACKETS ON TWO EMPTY CHAIRS INSTEAD.]

LEMI: We can wear our own coats.

[FENN MOVES THE HEART. THE METER CONTINUES TO FLASH. LEMI AND ODO WALK OFF TOGETHER, EACH CARRYING THEIR OWN MAP.] 

### Tag

[THE CAMERA FINDS FENN AT THE TABLE, STILL HOLDING THE COMPATIBILITY BELL. THE WAITER CLEARS THE DISHES.]

FENN: Was that a match?

SULA: It was a dinner.
