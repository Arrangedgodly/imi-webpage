# The Hallway Makeover Must Stay Secret

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation television that hides practical construction behind reveal-day spectacle*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; carpenter Tavi, a macaque; resident Seli, a capuchin; designer Pava, a lemur; camera operator Rell, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. SMALL HOUSE - DAY

[Blue tape marks the hallway. Seli steps over a paint tray. Odo holds a card reading "DO NOT LOOK."]

ODO: We have transformed this hallway without the resident seeing it.

SELI: I live here.

[She turns around. A ladder is blocking the kitchen door.]

ODO: We are still in the transformation.

[Theme sting.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Welcome to Secret Makeover, where a surprise is more important than the people who use the room.

PAVA: The brief is a calm hallway with storage.

SELI: The hallway is already storage. My shoes are there.

TAVI: We will build a bench with cubbies.

SELI: The door needs to open.

[The camera frames a shot of the empty hallway. A pair of shoes rolls in from the side. Seli has nudged them with her foot.]

ODO: Can we clear those for the reveal?

SELI: Those are the shoes I need to leave.

PAVA: We can place them in the new cubbies.

SELI: If there are cubbies.

[The crew measures the wall. A loose section of trim hides a narrow access panel. Tavi taps it and hears a hollow sound.] 

TAVI: There is a cavity here.

ODO: A hidden feature.

SELI: That's where the heater valve is.

ODO: A hidden utility feature.

ACT TWO

[Tavi removes the panel. Pava holds a swatch beside the wood. Odo instructs the camera operator to film Seli facing away from the work.] 

ODO: The audience must not see the storage until reveal day.

SELI: I am standing beside it.

[Rell backs into the kitchen with the camera. His cable catches on a shoe. Seli catches the camera before it tips.] 

SELI: The hallway is not a studio.

RELL: The hallway is a very small studio.

[Tavi builds a narrow bench around the access panel. Pava chooses a bright cushion. Seli presses the cushion down; it blocks the panel.] 

SELI: The valve is covered.

TAVI: We can make a hinged seat.

ODO: A reveal within the reveal.

PAVA: It needs a finger pull.

[The crew adds a pull loop. A tail could fit through the loop, but Seli's hand catches it easily. She lifts the seat and reaches the valve.] 

SELI: This works.

ODO: Could you react as if you have just discovered it?

SELI: I discovered it while you were painting.

[He asks for another take. Seli opens the seat, finds the valve, closes it, then opens it again on his cue.] 

ODO: More wonder.

SELI: The valve is still there.

[Tavi checks the bench measurements. One side is slightly longer than the other, so the hinged seat catches against the wall.]

TAVI: We need to shave a little off this edge.

PAVA: The cushion will cover it.

SELI: The seat must open without lifting the whole cushion.

[She demonstrates. The cushion catches on the wall, then slides off and lands on the paint tray.]

ODO: We have a setback.

SELI: We have a cushion in the paint.

[Tavi trims the seat. Pava wipes the cushion and flips it to its unpainted side. The hinge opens cleanly. Seli reaches the valve without removing the cushion.]

SELI: That's the access I asked for.

ODO: We can call it the hidden utility reveal.

SELI: You can call it the heater valve.

ACT THREE

[The crew removes the blue tape and sets the camera for the big reveal. Odo covers Seli's eyes with a scarf. She hears the bench lid creak as Rell steps on it.] 

SELI: Get off the seat.

RELL: I was checking the shot.

TAVI: The hinge is holding.

[Odo uncovers her eyes. The hallway is freshly painted, the bench has cubbies, and the shoes are inside. The front door swings open and hits the bench, stopping just short of the wall.] 

SELI: The door still opens.

PAVA: The cushion is bright enough to find in the dark.

SELI: The heater valve is reachable. Those are the things I asked for.

ODO: And the surprise?

SELI: You hid the shoes.

[She opens a cubby. A pair of sandals has been placed heel to toe. She slips one on, then notices the other is the wrong size.] 

SELI: These are Tavi's.

TAVI: They fit the shelf.

[She swaps them for her own shoes. Odo asks her to sit for the final shot. Seli opens the bench lid and removes the paint-splattered scarf.] 

SELI: The hallway has a storage cubby for the show's props.

ODO: We don't store props in homes.

SELI: Then take your scarf.

TAG

[Odo reaches for it. The bench lid closes over his sleeve.] 

TAVI: The reveal has a hidden feature.

SELI: It is the hinge.
