# A Date Under the Moon Vine

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality dating competitions, producer-manufactured jealousy, and dramatic elimination ceremonies*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A dating show producer tries to force three adult contestants into a jealousy triangle, but the contestants notice the planted cues and turn the elimination ceremony into a real choice.

Cast: Host Sera, a lemur; adult contestants Luma, a capuchin; Oren, a gibbon; and Miva, a macaque; producer Daro, a baboon; florist Kema, an orangutan.

## Episode: "The Manufactured Moon"

### Cold open

EXT. MOON-VINE GARDEN, NIGHT

[Three stools face a row of individual fruit plates. Sera holds a silver token. Daro watches from behind a camera.]

SERA: Tonight, the moon token chooses who gets a private walk.

LUMA: A walk where?

SERA: Beneath the moon vine.

OREN: We are already beneath it.

MIVA: What makes it private?

DARO: The cameras will follow from a respectful distance.

KEMA: The cameras are in the flowers.

[One flower turns toward the contestants.]

LUMA: That is not a respectful distance.

DARO: The flower is part of the garden.

[The token slips from Sera's hand and lands on the table.]

SERA: The ceremony is already in motion.

### Act One

EXT. MOON-VINE GARDEN

[Sera places three individual fruit plates in front of the contestants.]

SERA: Before the token choice, tell us what you hope to find.

LUMA: Someone who listens without waiting for their turn to speak.

OREN: Someone who can share a quiet evening.

MIVA: Someone who doesn't schedule a surprise.

DARO: That is not a romantic answer.

MIVA: It is an honest one.

KEMA: I have arranged a garden walk for anyone who wants to see the night-blooming flowers.

DARO: That is not the date.

KEMA: It's a walk.

DARO: Our walk has a token.

OREN: The token seems to be causing more work than the walk.

[Sera reads from a cue card. The card has "GASP" written in the margin.]

SERA: One contestant will choose first. The others will watch.

LUMA: Why?

DARO: For suspense.

MIVA: We can all choose a path.

DARO: The format requires one choice.

KEMA: There are three paths.

DARO: Only one has a camera.

[Kema points to three visible paths. A camera lens peeks from every vine.]

### Act Two

INT. CONFESSIONAL HOLLOW

[Daro interviews Luma. A monitor behind him shows Oren waiting by a path.]

DARO: Luma, how would you feel if Oren chose Miva?

LUMA: I would think he liked Miva.

DARO: Would you be jealous?

LUMA: No.

DARO: Could you try?

LUMA: Why?

DARO: The audience expects a reaction.

LUMA: Then show them this one.

[She looks directly into the camera and calmly eats her fruit.]

[Cut to Oren's confessional.]

DARO: How would you feel if Luma left with the host?

OREN: I would ask why the host is in the competition.

[Cut to Miva.]

DARO: Would it bother you if you were eliminated?

MIVA: I have not been competing.

DARO: Every contestant is competing.

MIVA: Against whom?

[Back in the garden, Daro whispers to Sera.]

DARO: The confessionals are not working. We need a surprise.

SERA: The token ceremony is next.

DARO: Make it a rose ceremony.

SERA: We have moon leaves.

DARO: Call them roses.

KEMA: They are not roses.

DARO: The audience can't tell from this angle.

[He puts three moon leaves in a bowl. One falls out.]

SERA: We have two leaves.

DARO: Make one choice count twice.

LUMA: We heard you.

OREN: We can hear the camera too.

### Act Three

EXT. MOON-VINE GARDEN, LATER

[Sera stands beside the bowl. Daro holds a cue card that reads "DRAMATIC PAUSE."]

SERA: The moon token chooses the first walk.

MIVA: I choose to walk with Kema and see the flowers.

KEMA: I can show you the night bloom.

SERA: That is not one of the options.

MIVA: It is the one I want.

OREN: I would like to walk with Luma.

LUMA: I would like that.

DARO: Wait. The token has not been drawn.

OREN: We have drawn a conclusion.

LUMA: We can decide what to do after the walk.

DARO: We need a cliffhanger before the commercial.

SERA: The walk is the ending.

DARO: The audience needs to wonder whether they'll have a second date.

MIVA: We will decide after we meet.

KEMA: I can recommend a quiet path.

[The contestants and Kema leave together in two groups. Sera stays by the empty bowl.]

SERA: The show has produced two walks.

DARO: No one chose the token.

SERA: The token is not walking.

DARO: It can choose next week.

LUMA: [OFFSTAGE] We won't be here next week.

DARO: Can the token have a date?

SERA: You can walk with the camera.

[Daro glances at the flower camera. It pivots toward him.]

### Tag

EXT. MOON-VINE GARDEN, NIGHT

[Sera places the two unused moon leaves back on the vine. Daro follows the camera down the empty path.]

SERA: The garden is private now.

[The camera remains pointed at Daro's retreating back.]
