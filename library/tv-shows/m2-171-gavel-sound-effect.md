# The Gavel Is a Sound Effect

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom procedurals that compress hearings into theatrical confessions and dramatic rulings*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: A television crew films a small claims hearing over a damaged greenhouse window. Host-judge Malu wants a dramatic courtroom confession; clerk Omi needs both neighbors to describe what happened without the show's sound effects interrupting them.

Cast: Malu, a macaque host-judge; Omi, a gorilla clerk; Pera, a capuchin greenhouse owner; and Gadi, a lemur ball player.

## Episode: "The Pane and the Pinecone"

### Cold Open

[COURTROOM SET. A WINDOW PANE SITS ON A TABLE. MALU RAISES A GAVEL.]

MALU: Did you throw the object that broke the greenhouse window?

GADI: I hit a practice ball toward the wall. It bounced off a tree.

[THE SOUND TECH PLAYS A THUNDERCLAP.]

OMI: That was not the sound of the ball.

MALU: It was the sound of the ruling.

PERA: We have not heard the witness yet.

[OMI TAKES THE GAVEL AND SETS IT ON A PAD.]

### Act One

[PERA SHOWS A PHOTO OF THE BROKEN PANE AND A SMALL BALL MARK IN THE DIRT. THE GRAPHIC CALLS IT "THE IMPACT THAT SHOOK THE GARDEN." ]

PERA: I was inside when I heard the crack. The ball was outside the greenhouse.

MALU: Did you recognize the sound?

PERA: I heard glass break.

[THE SOUND TECH PLAYS THE CRACK AT HALF SPEED. GADI LOOKS AT THE SPEAKER.]

GADI: That is a coconut dropping.

OMI: We have not established that.

[MALU ASKS FOR THE COURT'S OFFICIAL RECORD. OMI PLUGS IN A SMALL RECORDER. IT PICKS UP THE AIR CONDITIONER, THE CAMERA FAN, AND BOBO WHISPERING "SUSPENSE." ]

OMI: The record needs to include what was said, not the effects.

BOBO: Can we use the original audio?

OMI: Yes. It contains the voices.

[MALU TRIES A SECOND TAKE OF THE QUESTION. THE GAVEL IS OUT OF REACH, SO SHE TAPS A PENCIL ON THE TABLE. IT MAKES A TINY CLICK.] 

MALU: Did you throw the ball?

GADI: Yes.

PERA: I heard the glass break.

OMI: That is clear enough for the record.

[MALU TURNS TO GADI.]

MALU: Why were you practicing near the greenhouse?

GADI: The field was closed for watering. I was using the wall as a target.

[THE GRAPHIC CHANGES TO "THE WALL THAT CAUSED IT ALL." ]

PERA: The wall is on my property.

MALU: Is this a property dispute?

PERA: It is a broken window.

### Act Two

[OMI PLACES A MAP BETWEEN THEM. THE FIELD AND GREENHOUSE ARE CLEARLY MARKED. MALU HOLDS UP A DOCUMENT WITH THE CAMERA-FRIENDLY HEADING "EXHIBIT A." ]

OMI: The map shows the practice area beyond the wall.

GADI: I thought the wall was the boundary.

PERA: It is, but the ball can go over it.

MALU: Do you admit you threw it?

GADI: Yes. I did not see where it would land.

[THE SOUND TECH CUES A GASP. OMI POINTS TO THE SPEAKER. IT STOPS.] 

MALU: The confession is in.

OMI: The statement is one fact. The agreement is still open.

[PERA SHOWS A REPAIR ESTIMATE. GADI OFFERS TO HELP REPLACE THE PANE. THE CAMERA ZOOMS ON THEIR HANDS.]

MALU: A handshake before the verdict?

PERA: We are discussing the repair.

[THE SOUND TECH PLAYS A HEARTBEAT. PERA AND GADI LOOK AT EACH OTHER, THEN AT THE SPEAKER.]

GADI: The repair costs less than the sound effects.

[BOBO CUTS THE EFFECTS. MALU ASKS PERA WHETHER THE PANE CAN BE REPAIRED BEFORE THE WEEKEND MARKET. PERA CHECKS THE ESTIMATE; THE CREW CAN REPLACE IT FRIDAY.] 

PERA: If the frame is straight, yes.

GADI: I can bring the replacement with the crew.

MALU: That is an agreement worth writing down.

[OMI WRITES THE AGREEMENT ON THE ACTUAL COURT FORM. BOBO TRIES TO HAND HER A SCRIPT PAGE INSTEAD. IT HAS A LARGE FONT AND A THUNDER CUE.] 

OMI: I need the names and the repair date.

[MALU SIGNS THE FORM. THE SOUND TECH PLAYS A SINGLE CLICK WHEN THE PEN LIFTS.]

### Act Three

[MALU SETS THE ESTIMATE BESIDE THE PHOTO. THE CLERK'S SCREEN SHOWS A SIMPLE RULING FORM.]

MALU: Gadi, you will pay for the replacement pane. Pera, you will let the repair crew use the path beside the wall.

OMI: Both parties agree?

PERA: Yes.

GADI: Yes.

[THE SOUND TECH REACHES FOR THE GAVEL CRASH. OMI UNPLUGS THE EFFECTS BOARD. MALU TAPS THE PADDED GAVEL ONCE.] 

MALU: The matter is settled.

[THE CREW REENACTS THE AGREEMENT. GADI CARRIES THE NEW PANE WITH BOTH HANDS. PERA HOLDS THE DOOR. THE CAMERA TRIES TO GET A SLOW-MOTION SHOT, BUT THE PANE REFLECTS THE LIGHTS AND BLINDS THE LENS.] 

OMI: We can finish when the lens cap is on.

### Tag

[THE SOUND TECH FINALLY PLAYS A DRAMATIC STING. IT COMES OUT AS A SOFT DOOR CHIME.] 

MALU: Court is adjourned.

PERA: The greenhouse is open again.
