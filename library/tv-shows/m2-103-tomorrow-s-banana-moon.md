# Tomorrow's Banana Moon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: anthology science-fiction television that turns ordinary problems into grand cosmic stakes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A distant colony discovers its moon is about to disappear. The crew's alarmed theories give way to a practical explanation: the moon is a reflective fruit shield that a curious juvenile has carried away.

Cast: Captain Aro, a mandrill; engineer Tess, a macaque; science officer Lumo, a gibbon; and junior explorer Pip, a young capuchin.

## Episode: "The Orbiting Snack"

### Cold Open

[THE CREW STARES THROUGH A VIEWPORT. THE MOON VANISHES.]

ARO: The moon has disappeared.

LUMO: Our charts say it should be there.

TESS: Something is blocking the reflector.

[THE REFLECTOR SPINS BACK INTO VIEW. A BANANA HALF IS STUCK TO IT.]

PIP: I was saving that.

### Act One

[THE SHIP'S ALERT LIGHTS FLASH. A STAR MAP SHOWS A RED WARNING ZONE.]

ARO: We have lost our night signal. Without it, the colony cannot navigate the cargo ship home.

LUMO: The moon reflector was built to catch light from the beacon.

TESS: It is not a moon. It is a shield in a stable orbit.

ARO: Then why does everyone call it the moon?

LUMO: The public prefers one word.

[THE ALARM SOUNDS AGAIN.]

ARO: We need to restore the reflector's position.

PIP: Can I help?

TESS: Did you touch the shield?

PIP: I touched the banana.

[THE CREW TURNS TO THE BANANA HALF STUCK ON THE REFLECTOR.]

### Act Two

[TESS PLOTS THE SHIELD'S MOVEMENT ON A CONSOLE.]

TESS: Its orbit is stable. Something pulled the hinge.

LUMO: The hinge is not built for fruit.

PIP: I put the peel there so it would not get lost.

ARO: The peel is stuck to a satellite.

PIP: It is a very clean satellite.

[THE PEEL SLIDES. THE REFLECTOR TILTS.]

TESS: We need to turn the handle slowly.

ARO: How slowly?

TESS: Slowly enough that the peel does not move.

[ARO TURNS THE HANDLE. PIP REACHES FOR THE BANANA.]

TESS: Do not pull it.

PIP: I am holding it still.

LUMO: Your hand is pulling it.

[PIP LETS GO. THE REFLECTOR SETTLES.]

ARO: Good. We have a stable angle.

[THE BEACON SIGNAL RETURNS. A SMALL LIGHT APPEARS ON THE NAVIGATION SCREEN.]

LUMO: The cargo ship has our signal.

ARO: Tell them to hold course.

TESS: The shield still needs cleaning.

PIP: I can clean it.

TESS: With a cloth, not your mouth.

[LUMO CHECKS THE SIGNAL HISTORY ON THE CONSOLE.]

LUMO: The reflector moved only when the peel touched the hinge.

TESS: The banana stuck because of the sugar on its surface.

PIP: It was not sticky when I started eating it.

ARO: A little sugar can make a large mess in zero gravity.

[PIP FLOATS THE CLOTH TOWARD THE REFLECTOR. IT DRIFTS PAST.]

TESS: Hold the rail first.

[PIP GRABS THE RAIL, THEN CATCHES THE CLOTH WITH A FOOT.]

PIP: I am holding on and cleaning.

LUMO: You are using your foot.

PIP: It is still attached to me.

[ARO SECURES A STRAP AROUND THE CLEANING TOOL.]

ARO: The repair crew has learned to tether loose objects.

TESS: We should add that to the checklist.

PIP: And label snacks before they orbit.

LUMO: The checklist can have a snack section.

[THE SIGNAL LIGHT REMAINS STEADY WHILE THE CREW PUTS THE PEEL IN A SEALED SAMPLE BAG.]

[THE CARGO PILOT SENDS A THANK-YOU MESSAGE. ARO READS IT ALOUD.]

ARO: "Your reflector made the docking route visible. Please keep the signal on."

LUMO: We should send the crew a fruit basket.

TESS: A basket stays inside the ship.

[PIP TAKES THE BANANA PEEL SAMPLE BAG AND POINTS TO THE LABEL.]

PIP: I will mark it "not a reflector."

ARO: Add "not lunch."

PIP: It already says evidence.

### Act Three

[THE CREW USES A LONG TOOL TO LIFT THE PEEL. THE REFLECTOR TURNS, THEN LOCKS INTO PLACE.]

ARO: The moon is restored.

LUMO: The night signal is steady.

PIP: My banana is still there.

TESS: It is not safe to eat.

PIP: Can we call it space food?

ARO: We can call it evidence.

[THE CARGO SHIP DOCKS. A CREW MEMBER WAVES THROUGH THE VIEWPORT.]

CARGO PILOT: We followed your moon home.

LUMO: The moon is a reflector.

CARGO PILOT: We followed your reflector home.

[THE CREW LAUGHS. PIP USES THE CLEANING CLOTH TO WIPE THE LAST STICKY SPOT.]

PIP: I have finished the repair.

TESS: You also need to wash your hands.

[PIP LOOKS AT THE HAND THAT HELD THE PEEL. THE SHIP'S ALERT LIGHT BLINKS.]

ARO: Is there another emergency?

TESS: The hand-washing station is on the other side of the ship.

[THE CREW SETS A COURSE. PIP FLOATS TOWARD THE SINK WHILE HOLDING THE BANANA PEEL WITH TONGS.]
