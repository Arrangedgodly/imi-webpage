# The Apartment Thermostat Treaty

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: roommate sitcoms, where a shared household disagreement escalates into a ceremonial negotiation*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Three flatmates try to agree on a temperature for their single-room treehouse.

Cast: Roommates Odo, a baboon; Piri, a capuchin; Leni, a gibbon; and landlord Nara, a macaque.

Episode: "Set It and Regret It"

COLD OPEN

INT. TREEHOUSE - MORNING

Odo turns the heater up. Piri opens a window. Leni enters wrapped in a blanket.

LENI: Who changed the temperature?

ODO: The room was cold.

PIRI: The room was stuffy.

The thermostat beeps and displays "ROOMMATE DISAGREEMENT."

ACT ONE

Nara, the landlord, arrives to check the thermostat. It works normally. She asks what temperature each roommate prefers. Odo says warm enough to nap. Piri says cool enough to work. Leni says comfortable enough to stay in the room.

Nara suggests they choose a middle setting. Odo asks who gets the remote. Piri says it stays on the wall. Leni asks if they can use the window for fresh air instead of changing the thermostat.

They try a vote. Odo and Leni vote warm; Piri votes cool. The setting changes twice before Nara leaves.

ACT TWO

Odo writes a schedule: warm in the morning, cool at noon, warm at night. Piri says the sun warms the treehouse at noon already. Leni asks if the schedule includes the window. Odo adds a column. The chart becomes too large for the wall.

Piri creates a second schedule using colored stickers. Red means heater; blue means window. Odo mistakes blue for "cool" and opens the window at night. Leni wakes up cold and closes it.

Odo says they need an official meeting. Piri brings a talking stick. Whoever holds it can set the thermostat. Leni holds it and says they should open the window for ten minutes, then close it. Odo says the rule should be permanent. Piri says no one temperature fits every hour.

Nara returns with a small thermometer. The room is already comfortable in the shade. They had been adjusting the thermostat based on how they felt near the heater or window.

ACT TWO-B

The roommates move the chair away from the heater. Odo tries sitting by the window; it is cooler there. Piri moves her work table into the shade. Leni puts the blanket on a hook instead of wearing it indoors.

They set the thermostat to a middle number. Nara puts a small note beside it: "Wait ten minutes before changing." Odo asks if the note has authority. Nara says the thermostat does not need a roommate vote every minute.

ACT TWO-C

A cold breeze moves through the window. Leni puts the blanket back over her shoulders. Odo reaches for the thermostat. Piri asks him to wait ten minutes before changing the setting, as Nara's note suggests. Odo checks the thermometer; the room is still within the agreed range.

Leni says the draft is coming from the window frame, not the heater. Piri tests it with a strip of tissue. The tissue flutters near one corner. Nara returns with weather stripping and presses it along the gap. The draft stops.

Odo says the roommates have found a fourth solution without a vote. Piri says it was a repair. Leni brings the schedule chart down from the wall and folds it into a small square. The roommates put the talking stick in a drawer and keep the thermometer on the shelf.

ACT TWO-D

INT. TREEHOUSE - EVENING

Piri sets a camera on the table. Odo has arranged the chairs like a negotiation panel and borrowed the blanket from its hook for his shoulders. Leni holds the talking stick; Nara lays the weather-stripping package beside the signed repair note.

ODO: Welcome to the first annual thermostat summit. We have settled the temperature and repaired the window. Any final objections?

PIRI: I object to the word "annual." This is the first meeting.

LENI: I object to the camera. The room is not running for office.

Odo presents a one-page treaty: wait ten minutes before touching the thermostat, and report drafts before changing the setting. Piri reads it aloud. The page is so small that Odo's paw covers the signature line.

NARA: Move your paw.

ODO: I am preserving the document.

LENI: You are vetoing it with your elbow.

Odo shifts. The treaty slides off the table and lands on the blanket. Leni retrieves it; the camera follows the blanket, not the agreement.

PIRI: Even the camera wants a warmer shot.

Nara gives each roommate a turn with the pen. Odo signs first, then Piri, then Leni. Odo adds a clause allowing him to keep the blanket for naps. Leni initials it, then takes the blanket off his shoulders and hangs it on the hook.

ODO: The treaty is binding.

PIRI: The blanket is available.

NARA: The thermostat stays where it is.

ACT THREE

The three roommates work in the same room. Odo naps on the sofa, Piri reads by the window, and Leni uses the table. No one touches the thermostat. The heater cycles once, then stops.

Odo wakes up and says he is warm. Piri says she is fine. Leni takes the blanket down anyway and places it over Odo.

TAG

The thermostat beeps.

ODO: What does it say?

PIRI: It says low battery.

[ODO REACHES FOR THE TALKING STICK IN THE DRAWER. LENI COVERS IT WITH THE SIGNED TREATY.]

LENI: The next summit requires a new agenda.
