# The Tour Guide Missed the View

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentaries that film a destination while the crew stays focused on the itinerary*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A travel host races through a scenic reserve to reach a lookout that is visible from the trail.

Cast: Host Rell, a capuchin; guide Sora, a macaque; camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; sound recordist Nima, a lemur; guest Doro, a baboon.

Episode: "The View at the End"

COLD OPEN

EXT. HILL TRAIL - DAY

A valley spreads behind the group. Rell reads from a card and keeps walking.

RELL: Our first stop is the overlook, twelve minutes ahead.

SORA: The overlook is behind you.

[TAVI TURNS THE CAMERA. THE VALLEY FILLS THE SCREEN.]

RELL: We will film that when we arrive.

ACT ONE

Sora leads the group along the ridge. Rell asks whether the trail has a hidden landmark. Sora points to a large stone arch beside them. Doro says they already walked under it. Nima records a breeze and asks everyone to stop talking so the microphone can capture it.

RELL: We are here to describe the scenery.

SORA: You can look at it while describing it.

Rell keeps reading. Tavi asks him to step toward the light. Rell steps off the marked trail and Sora guides him back. The camera catches the view; the host's card blocks half the frame.

ACT TWO

At a fork, Sora points to the lookout. Rell says the itinerary lists a water stop first. Doro drinks from his bottle. Nima asks whether the bottle has a sound worth recording. Doro tips it; the last drop falls onto the itinerary and blurs the next stop.

RELL: The route has changed.

SORA: The route is the same. Your card is wet.

Rell improvises a route update. He calls a grove of trees a hidden sanctuary. Sora says it is a picnic grove open to everyone. Tavi films a squirrel carrying a leaf. Rell asks whether it is local wildlife or a staged visitor.

SORA: It lives here.

RELL: We have exclusive access.

NIMA: The squirrel has not signed a release.

ACT TWO-B

The crew reaches a small lookout. Rell announces the reveal, but the camera faces the tripod legs. Tavi adjusts it. The view appears, yet Rell stands in front of it to deliver the introduction.

DORO: I can see the valley around your shoulders.

RELL: The host establishes scale.

SORA: You are blocking the view.

[Rell steps aside. Wind lifts his itinerary and sends it down the trail. Tavi follows it with the camera.]

RELL: We have lost the schedule.

SORA: We know where we are.

Nima records the sound of the paper flapping against a bush. Doro fetches it. The card is torn but the route remains legible. The crew asks him to recreate the recovery. He refuses and hands it to Rell.

ACT TWO-C

Rell tries to summarize the destination without reading the card. He names the valley, ridge, and stone arch correctly but says the lookout is ahead. Sora turns him around. The group is standing beside it.

RELL: We arrived while I was explaining that we had not arrived.

TAVI: That is the shot.

NIMA: The wind is clear in the microphone.

SORA: The trail has no more stops. We can stay here for a minute and look.

Rell lowers the card. Doro spots the squirrel on the stone arch again. The crew films without moving to another waypoint.

ACT TWO-D

The crew stays at the lookout while Rell takes one last look at the itinerary. He insists the next stop is a lookout, not the stone arch behind them. Tavi points to the camera footage; the arch has been in the background of every shot.

RELL: We have been traveling with the landmark.

SORA: You have been reading while walking.

DORO: The map is folded so the arrow points behind us.

[Rell unfolds it. A sticky note covers the legend. He peels it back; the note says "LOOK UP." Everyone looks up at the ridge.]

NIMA: The wind has changed. It is coming from the valley now.

TAVI: That sound is better than the music track.

RELL: Can we record the valley without naming it a hidden sanctuary?

SORA: Yes. It is a valley.

[THE CREW HOLDS THE CAMERA STILL. RELL PUTS THE ITINERARY IN HIS POCKET.]

RELL: The guide is allowed to look too.
ACT THREE

The team takes in the view. Rell finally turns to camera and says the overlook was the destination, not a reward for completing the itinerary. Tavi frames the group to one side so the valley stays visible.

TAG

Rell reads the final cue: "Look left." Everyone looks at the camera instead of the view.

SORA: Look left of the camera.

[THEY TURN. THE SQUIRREL CROSSES THE FRAME WITH THE ITINERARY CARD.]

[MUSIC: TRAVEL DOCUMENTARY THEME.]
