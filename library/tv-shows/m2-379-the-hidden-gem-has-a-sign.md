# The Hidden Gem Has a Sign

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel television, secret destinations, scenic detours, and hosts who call every stop undiscovered*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A travel host searches for a hidden waterfall, only to find the town has posted a large sign directing every visitor to it.

Cast: Host Eko, a gibbon; local guide Piri, a macaque; producer Venn, a baboon; camera operator Tavi, a capuchin; park keeper Olo, an orangutan; visitor, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

EXT. TOWN ROAD - MORNING

Eko faces the camera beside a sign reading "WATERFALL VIEWPOINT - 300 METERS."

EKO: We have found a secret destination.

PIRI: The sign is visible from the bus stop.

VENN: Can we film it from the other side?

TAVI: It will still say waterfall.

ACT ONE

Piri leads the crew along the marked trail. Eko asks her to describe how locals keep the waterfall secret. Piri says they do not; visitors follow the public path and stay behind the rail. Venn asks whether there is a less obvious entrance.

PIRI: There is a maintenance gate. It is locked.

VENN: Can we get a shot of you discovering it?

OLO (FROM PATH): You would be discovering a locked gate.

Olo unlocks the public trail gate and asks the crew not to block the path. Eko starts a voice-over about a secluded paradise. Behind him, a family reads the sign and walks toward the viewpoint.

EKO: Beyond the known route lies a hidden gem.

PIRI: The route is known.

ACT TWO

At a fork, Piri points to the left path. Venn asks the crew to take the right path because it has a better camera angle. The right path leads to a picnic area. Eko calls it "an unexpected local gathering place." Olo says it is a picnic area with two empty tables.

TAVI: There is a cooler under this one.

OLO: It belongs to the caretaker.

Venn tells Tavi not to film the cooler. Eko interviews Piri about the scenery. A squirrel runs along the railing. Eko asks if it is a rare local animal. Piri says squirrels live in the park. Venn asks Tavi for a close-up.

PIRI: You can see it without the camera.

EKO: The camera lets us appreciate it in detail.

The squirrel runs behind the camera and out of sight. The crew turns to follow. Tavi nearly steps off the paved path; Olo points to the boundary marker. The camera tilts down to show the marker, then back up to Piri waiting patiently.

ACT TWO-B

The crew returns to the fork. Piri follows the marked trail. Eko describes the climb as a "remote ascent." It is a gentle ramp with a handrail. Tavi pushes the equipment cart up the ramp. Venn asks if they can make it look steeper by filming from ground level.

OLO: The ramp is accessible. Keep the cart on the path.

EKO: A hidden path with a handrail.

PIRI: A public path.

At the viewpoint, the waterfall is visible beyond the rail. Eko asks Piri to stand beside it and point dramatically. Piri points to a plaque with the trail name, hours and safety notices. Venn asks for a shot without the plaque. The plaque is bolted to the rail.

TAVI: I can frame around it.

The camera frames around it and captures the sign at the trail entrance reflected in a puddle. Eko tries a voice-over without the word "secret." He says the town has made a beautiful public place easy to find. Venn asks for another take with more mystery.

PIRI: It is easy to find because people wanted it that way.

ACT THREE

Eko introduces the viewpoint as a local favorite and names the public trail. Olo thanks the crew for keeping the walkway open. Venn says the footage has no reveal. Tavi shows him the shot of the crew arriving at the obvious sign, then walking the trail without leaving it.

VENN: We could make the sign a twist.

EKO: We can tell viewers where they are going.

PIRI: That's why the sign is there.

The host asks Piri what visitors should bring. She says water, appropriate shoes, and time to enjoy the view. Eko starts a dramatic outro. A group of visitors reaches the viewpoint behind him and looks around his camera.

VISITOR: Is this the waterfall?

PIRI: Yes.

EKO: You found it.

TAG

The crew packs up. Venn asks if they can film a "departure from the secret place." Olo points to the same signed trail back to town.

FINAL IMAGE: Eko walks past the large entrance sign, giving it a courteous nod as the camera follows the unmistakable route home.
