# The Island Has a Producer

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival competition shows, staged obstacles, dramatic confessionals, and contestants bargaining for supplies*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Two teams compete to reach a flag on a small island, but the producer keeps moving the finish whenever contestants solve the obstacle.

Cast: Host Toma, a baboon; contestant Reni, a macaque; contestant Pavo, a capuchin; contestant Lala, a gibbon; safety marshal Odo, an orangutan; producer Kemi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

EXT. SMALL ISLAND COURSE - DAY

Toma points to a flag ten steps away. The contestants stand beside a shallow stream.

TOMA: Cross the stream, collect the flag, and win the supply crate.

RENI: Where is the stream?

ODO: There. It is ankle-deep.

KEMI (IN EARPIECE): We need a bridge shot.

A stagehand carries in a plank.

ACT ONE

The contestants study the plank. Reni tests it with one foot. It wobbles. Odo checks it and says the supports are loose. Lala suggests stepping across on the flat stones. Pavo says the show promised an obstacle course.

TOMA: The stream is the obstacle.

PAVO: I can cross it without getting wet.

KEMI: Can you make it look harder?

Pavo tries hopping across the stones, but his tail brushes the water. He lands safely and raises both hands.

PAVO: I have survived the stream.

Toma tells the camera it was a daring crossing. Reni points to a sign that says "NO RUNNING."

ACT TWO

The contestants reach the flag. Kemi moves it farther up the path while the cameras reset. Toma announces a second stage: carry a supply sack to the ridge. Odo opens the crate to check its contents. Inside are water bottles, fruit, and a folded map.

ODO: These are the actual supplies. They stay with the crew until the challenge ends.

LALA: We can read the map first.

KEMI: The map is a twist.

RENI: The map shows the path.

Kemi asks the contestants to draw a route in the sand instead. Lala sketches the ridge and marks the safe path. Pavo adds an arrow toward the camera tower. Reni points out that it leads away from the flag.

PAVO: It had a strong silhouette.

RENI: It goes to the camera tower.

The crew gives each contestant a lightweight sack. Pavo lifts his overhead; a strap slips over his face. Lala adjusts the strap so it sits on her shoulder. Reni carries hers with both hands. They walk up the path, stop at the marked rest point, and take water.

TOMA: The teams are conserving energy.

KEMI: Can we get a close-up of someone struggling?

ODO: They are walking safely.

ACT TWO-B

At the ridge, the flag is gone. Kemi has moved it behind a low rope barrier to create a surprise finish. Odo checks the barrier; it is waist-high and has no safe crossing point.

ODO: Nobody goes over this.

RENI: The instructions said collect the flag.

TOMA: The flag is on the other side.

LALA: Then the instructions changed.

Kemi suggests crawling under the rope. Odo says the boundary marks the course edge and the challenge is over. Pavo looks at the map, then points to the original flag location, where the crew left its empty stand.

PAVO: We finished at the first flag.

RENI: That was the route we were given.

TOMA: We need a winner.

ODO: Both teams reached the finish before it moved.

Kemi tells the contestants there will be a tie. Toma looks at the camera, then announces a shared win. The supply crate is opened. The contestants each take a bottle and piece of fruit. Odo keeps the map and checks the route before they walk back.

ACT THREE

Reni asks whether they can keep the plank as a souvenir. Odo says it belongs to the crew and has a cracked support. Pavo says the stream was the best obstacle. Lala says the map was the most useful item. Kemi tries to film a final confessional beside the flag, but the crew keeps moving it out of the frame.

KEMI: Just say what you learned.

RENI: The flag moved.

PAVO: The water was shallow.

LALA: The map was accurate.

KEMI: That is not emotional enough.

ODO: It is accurate enough.

Toma announces the result once and wraps the episode. The safety marshal returns the crew to shore in a small boat. The contestants carry their shared prize crate without racing.

TAG

On shore, Toma discovers the flag in the supply crate. Kemi says it was a bonus twist. Odo takes it out and plants it beside the course map.

FINAL IMAGE: The flag stands still on the mainland while the boat carries the entire obstacle course away.
