# Good Morning, Grove

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: morning news broadcasts, rapid segment handoffs, and cheerful breaking-news tone*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A morning news team races to keep a live broadcast on schedule after their weather reporter's map sprouts real vines and the studio clock begins running backward.

Cast: Anchor Seli, a macaque; co-anchor Boro, a gorilla; weather host Lita, a lemur; field reporter Pemm, a gibbon; and producer Vika, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Day That Started Twice"

### Cold open

INT. MORNING NEWS STUDIO, DAWN

[A large clock reads 6:00. A vine curls across the weather map.]

SELI: Good morning, Grove. The time is six o'clock.

BORO: It is five fifty-nine.

VIKA: The clock is running backward.

LITA: The weather map is growing.

PEMM: I am live at the eastern bridge.

SELI: We are not live yet.

PEMM: I can see you on the monitor.

VIKA: We are live.

[The clock flips to 5:58.]

BORO: Good morning again.

### Act One

INT. NEWS DESK

[Seli reads a card. Boro checks a second clock. Vika signals through the glass.]

SELI: Today's top story is a fallen branch blocking the east trail.

BORO: Our reporter is at the bridge.

PEMM: The trail is open.

SELI: It was blocked when we wrote the card.

PEMM: The crew moved the branch.

VIKA: We need a new top story.

LITA: The weather map is becoming a tree.

BORO: Is that the weather?

LITA: It is growing through the studio.

SELI: Can you give us the forecast?

LITA: Sunny intervals, with a chance of vines.

[The vine wraps around the temperature number.]

VIKA: We are losing the map.

LITA: The map is gaining leaves.

SELI: We will be right back after a quick update.

BORO: The clock says we already came back.

VIKA: The clock says five fifty-seven.

SELI: That is not the time.

BORO: It was the time a few minutes ago.

### Act Two

INT. STUDIO HALLWAY

[The team walks backward toward the desk. The clock on the wall ticks backward.]

VIKA: We have to keep the segments in order.

PEMM: I have the bridge update.

SELI: Was there a delay?

PEMM: No. The bridge is clear.

BORO: Any traffic?

PEMM: One slow tortoise.

VIKA: That is a traffic report.

SELI: Tell us more.

PEMM: The tortoise has arrived at the bridge.

BORO: And now?

PEMM: It is still there.

SELI: We will return to you after the weather.

[They step into the studio. Lita's map has sprouted around the weather monitor.]

LITA: The wind is light.

VIKA: The wind is moving the leaves.

LITA: There is no wind inside.

BORO: The map is creating a breeze.

SELI: Is there a forecast for the clock?

LITA: It will be earlier in five minutes.

BORO: That sounds nice.

VIKA: The clock is not a time machine. It is broken.

SELI: Can we fix it?

VIKA: Not before the next segment.

PEMM: We can tell the audience the time.

SELI: What time is it?

PEMM: The clock says five fifty-four.

BORO: The sun says morning.

LITA: The birds say breakfast.

SELI: That's a reliable panel.

### Act Three

INT. MORNING NEWS STUDIO

[Vika switches off the clock. The clock stops at 5:50. Lita carefully trims the vine away from the forecast.]

VIKA: We will finish the broadcast without the clock.

SELI: We have one minute.

BORO: According to what?

SELI: According to my card.

PEMM: The bridge is still clear.

LITA: The sun is still up.

VIKA: The map is stable.

[The vine releases the temperature number. It falls to the desk.]

SELI: Today's high is now on the desk.

BORO: The weather has moved indoors.

LITA: The afternoon will be warm.

PEMM: The tortoise has crossed the bridge.

SELI: A major development.

BORO: The trail is open, the weather is clear, and we have no idea what time it is.

VIKA: The broadcast ends now.

SELI: Thank you for starting your day with us.

BORO: Or starting it again.

PEMM: We will see you tomorrow.

LITA: Unless it becomes earlier.

VIKA: It won't.

[The clock clicks once and moves backward a minute.]

SELI: It has.

### Field update

EXT. EASTERN BRIDGE

[Pemm stands beside a sign that reads "BRIDGE CLEAR." The sign is upside down.]

PEMM: The bridge is open to morning traffic.

SELI: Is the sign correct?

PEMM: The sign is upside down.

BORO: Does that mean the bridge is closed?

PEMM: It means the letters are inverted.

VIKA: Can you turn it around?

[The camera turns around instead. The sign is now readable, but Pemm is upside down in frame.]

PEMM: The bridge is clear.

SELI: You are upside down.

PEMM: I am standing normally.

BORO: The camera has found a new angle.

PEMM: The tortoise has crossed the bridge twice.

SELI: That is today's traffic.

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, LATER

[The crew packs up. The vine curls around the stopped clock.]

VIKA: Leave it unplugged.

LITA: It is not plugged in.

[The clock ticks backward once.]
