# The Fruit Cart Is Not a Migration

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nature documentaries that turn an ordinary search for food into a long-distance epic*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Narrator Odo, a gibbon; field researcher Seli, a lemur; camera assistant Pip, a capuchin; field camera operator Tavi, a macaque; caretaker Mara, a macaque; troop elder Runi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. ORCHARD PATH - DAWN

[A red handcart stands beside an apple tree. One large apple rolls out of the cart and stops in a patch of sunlight. Field camera operator Tavi frames it while Odo whispers into a microphone.] 

ODO: At first light, the fruit begins its migration.

MARA: I parked it there.

[The apple rolls downhill. Music swells.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Welcome to Wild Tracks, where every journey begins with a close-up of a paw.

[Tavi zooms in on Pip's hand as he picks up the apple.] 

PIP: This came from the cart.

ODO: The young forager has discovered a rare red fruit.

SELI: The cart is twelve steps away.

[Odo signals Pip to carry the large apple through a longer route between the trees. Pip tries to tuck it into his cheek pouch, cannot fit it, takes it back out, and places it in the nearest harvest basket.]

PIP: My cheek said no.

ODO: A remarkable adaptation.

MARA: He is carrying lunch.

[Tavi walks backward to keep Pip in frame. Pip takes a step, then sits on a low branch. Tavi bumps the cart. A basket tips and several apples roll onto the path.] 

ODO: A sudden abundance.

SELI: A tipped basket.

[The troop approaches. Runi inspects an apple, turns it, and bites the soft side. The others copy the choice.]

RUNI: This one is good.

ODO: A ritual passed through generations.

RUNI: It's ripe.

ACT TWO

[Odo narrates while the troop picks up apples. Mara attempts to return them to the cart. Runi carries one to a branch and eats it there.]

ODO: The elder carries the prize away from the group.

MARA: He likes that branch.

[The camera tracks Runi to a low branch where he can sit balanced. Pip climbs the cart frame to reach the top basket. The wheels shift; Mara blocks it with her foot.] 

SELI: The cart is not a tree.

PIP: It has rungs.

MARA: It has wheels.

[Odo asks for a wide shot of the habitat. The camera reveals the paved orchard lane, the storage shed, and a sign reading "Fruit pickup - 8 a.m." He asks Mara to move the sign.] 

MARA: It tells us when to bring the cart back.

ODO: It interrupts the wilderness.

[The wind blows the sign around, making it point toward a second cart in the shed. Runi looks up, then walks toward it. Pip picks up a small apple from the spilled basket and tucks it into his cheek pouch.] 

SELI: He's following the food smell.

ODO: He has detected a distant migration.

[The troop follows. Pip sets the small apple from his cheek pouch on a stone, then stops to retrieve it. The others keep moving. He hurries after them, carrying the apple in both hands.] 

PIP: The migration is back toward the cart.

[The loaded cart reaches the orchard gate. One wheel catches in a shallow rut. The crew pushes, but it rolls back to its starting spot.]

MARA: We need the wheel on the stone.

PIP: I can lift the handle.

[Runi braces the cart. Seli places a flat twig beneath the wheel, and Pip levers the handle upward. The wheel clears the rut.]

ODO: A coordinated migration.

MARA: A stuck cart.

[Runi removes the twig and drops it into the compost bucket. The cart rolls the last step to the shed.]

RUNI: Now the fruit is home.

ODO: The young forager learns the path.

PIP: I knew the path. I was carrying one apple.

ACT THREE

[At the shed, Mara opens the door. A row of clean baskets waits inside. Runi sits at the threshold, finishing his apple. The troop gathers around him.] 

ODO: We have reached the seasonal feeding grounds.

MARA: We reached the storage shed.

[Runi drops the core into a compost bucket. Pip keeps his uneaten small apple in one hand. The camera zooms on the small pile.] 

ODO: The remains tell the story of a journey.

SELI: They tell us to empty the cart.

[The crew starts loading fruit. Odo continues narrating. Pip lifts his small apple from his hand and puts it back in his cheek pouch.] 

ODO: The young forager stores a second resource for the return trip.

PIP: This small one fits. The big one did not.

MARA: You can use the basket.

[Pip places the apple in the basket. The cart is now evenly loaded. Runi uses a twig to scrape a soft spot from another apple, then passes it to Mara.] 

RUNI: This one is ready.

ODO: A rare act of generosity.

RUNI: It is extra.

[The cart rolls smoothly back toward the orchard. Odo walks beside it, whispering commentary.]

ODO: The fruit has completed its journey.

SELI: The cart is returning to where it started.

ODO: Nature has a circular structure.

TAG

[Mara parks the loaded cart beneath the apple tree. A different apple still attached to a low branch drops into the same patch of sunlight.]

ODO: A new migration begins.

MARA: That's the tree, not the cart.
