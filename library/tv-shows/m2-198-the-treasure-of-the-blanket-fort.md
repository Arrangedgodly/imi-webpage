# The Treasure of the Blanket Fort

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's adventure show convention, where a pillow fort becomes an expedition with rules, maps and a suspiciously small treasure*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: Two young explorers follow a map through a blanket fort to find a treasure, while their older sibling keeps changing the route to protect the cushions.

Cast: Host Miri, a macaque; explorers Tavi and Sela, squirrel monkeys; and fort keeper Odo, a gibbon.

Episode: "The Cushion Kingdom"

COLD OPEN

INT. LIVING ROOM, DAY

Tavi unrolls a map. It points to a blanket fort. Sela puts on a leaf as a hat.

TAVI: We seek the treasure.

Odo enters carrying a cushion.

ODO: No climbing on the fort roof.

The roof sags under the map.

ACT ONE

The explorers crawl through the blanket tunnel. Miri narrates their journey. The map shows a river, but the river is a blue scarf. Tavi steps over it. Sela tries to jump and lands on a cushion. Odo asks them to walk carefully because the couch is being used as the fort's support.

They find a riddle taped to a chair: "I hold you when you rest, but I am not a bed." Sela guesses a pillow. Odo says the cushion is part of the fort wall. They look behind it and find another map.

ACT TWO

The second map leads to a dark corner. Miri gives them a flashlight. The beam reflects off a spoon, making it look like a shining cave. Tavi enters and sees a small box. Before opening it, they check for a clue. Sela finds a note: "Return cushions after adventure."

Odo says that is the fort's safety rule. The explorers agree to return each cushion as they go. They carry one to the living room, but the fort shifts and the tunnel closes. Miri calls this a cave-in. Odo says it is a blanket slipping off the chair.

The team rebuilds the support with a folded blanket. They find a small box beneath the final cushion. It contains three raisins and a note that reads "Treasure shared equally." Tavi and Sela divide the raisins. Odo asks whether the box is part of the fort supplies. Miri says it can be a treasure chest next time.

ACT TWO-B

INT. BLANKET FORT, AFTERNOON

Miri finds an old note tucked into a cushion seam. It says the fort has a second exit, but the arrows were drawn upside down. Tavi and Sela turn the map around. The route now leads beneath the coffee table, where the blanket hangs low. Odo warns them to keep the supports in place.

They crawl through. A cushion shifts and blocks the passage. Sela tries to push it, but the whole fort wall begins to move. Tavi holds the wall while Odo resets the cushion on the chair. The passage reopens. Miri calls it a structural challenge. Odo says it is a chair being used as a chair.

The explorers hear a soft rustle behind the curtain. Tavi shines the flashlight. A small monkey is asleep under a folded blanket. The team keeps its distance and takes the other route. The blanket is the fort's spare roof, so Odo lifts one corner and gently slides it away without waking the sleeper.

A new clue appears on the back of the map: "Treasure is where the fort stays dry." They check the roof seam and find a small gap. Sela places a folded cloth over it. No treasure is beneath the cloth, but the fort is now dry.

ACT TWO-C

INT. LIVING ROOM, LATE AFTERNOON

The children ask Odo to test the fort before the final search. He presses each cushion lightly and checks that the chair legs are stable. Miri asks if the fort can hold a third explorer. Odo says only if no one climbs on top. Tavi rearranges the cushions to widen the entrance. Sela tapes the map to the inside wall, where it will not cover the roof.

They follow the map's last arrow to the cushion chest. The chest is the laundry basket, which is empty. Inside is a note from Odo: "Please return the laundry basket after play." The treasure clue has been used to deliver a household request.

ACT THREE


The explorers return the cushions to the couch, blanket to the chair and scarf to the drawer. The living room is clear again. Odo checks the fort is folded safely. Miri announces that the treasure was teamwork, but Sela says it was raisins.

TAG

Tavi hides one raisin beneath a cushion for the next adventure. Odo finds it while cleaning and puts it in a small box labeled "future treasure."
