# Sugar and Sawdust

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: baking competition show*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: Four bakers race to make a birthday cake in a kitchen where the mixer has broken, the oven door sags, and the judges value a good recovery more than a perfect tower.

Cast: Host Pru, a calm orangutan; Kit, a meticulous tamarin pastry cook; Mella, a bold capuchin improviser; Ox, a baboon baker who works best by hand; and Judge Lott, a retired macaque caterer with a sharp palate.

## Episode: "The Cake That Leaned"

### Cold open

INT. COMPETITION KITCHEN, MORNING

The bakers lift their cakes from the oven. Kit's sponge is level. Mella's has a dramatic dome. Ox's cake is flat and wide. The oven door swings open by itself and nudges the tray. Mella's dome slides sideways.

MELLA: It has chosen a direction.

Judge Lott takes a bite from the tilted edge.

LOTT: It has chosen a flavor too.

### Act one

The challenge is a cake for the village reading club. The mixer breaks. Ox whisks by hand, Mella uses clean paddles, and Kit worries the batter will not rise.

The oven door sags at one hinge. The technician leaves to fix a camera. The bakers take turns checking the door.

Kit's batter looks smooth, but he has used more sugar than the recipe allows. Mella adds mashed pear to keep her cake moist. Ox chooses a cornmeal base because the flour is nearly gone. Judge Lott walks the kitchen and asks each baker what the cake should taste like. Kit says "reliable." Mella says "like a picnic after rain." Ox says "like a table with enough room for another plate."

### Act two

The cakes enter the oven. The handle slips, and the door opens a few inches. Mella's cake sinks in the center. Kit's top browns too quickly. Ox's cake cooks unevenly because the oven has a hot corner.

Pru calls a pause so the crew can secure the hinge with a proper bracket. The clock stops. Judge Lott asks the bakers to explain their next move. Kit wants to cover his cake with foil, but the kitchen has only one sheet. Mella offers it to him because her sunken cake can become a filled tart. Ox rotates his pan to even the heat.

They wait fifteen minutes for a safe repair. Pru refuses to cut the delay from the show.

When the cakes come out, Kit's is dry at the edge but tender in the center. He trims the rim and turns the cake into two layers. Mella bakes her batter in a shallow pan, fills the dent with fruit, and calls it a pear garden. Ox's cornmeal cake is sturdy but plain. He makes a warm apple topping from the leftover fruit.

### Act three

At judging, Kit serves a pear-filled cake. Lott likes the crumb but finds the frosting too sweet. Mella's uneven tart has a different fruit pattern in each slice.

Ox serves warm slices with apple topping. The youngest club members ask for seconds. Lott praises the sturdy texture. Ox says he wanted a cake that could travel without a plate.

Mella wins the challenge. She shares the ribbon with the kitchen crew, who repaired the oven. Kit gives the reading club his extra frosting in a jar. Ox packs the remaining cake for the volunteers. Nobody takes home a tall showpiece, but every baker leaves with a method they can use again: trim, adapt, and rotate a pan when the heat runs uneven.

Pru thanks the club and asks what they will read next. They choose a book about orchards. Mella says she is glad she used pears. Judge Lott says the cake does not need to know the book was about orchards.
