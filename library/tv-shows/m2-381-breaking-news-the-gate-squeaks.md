# Breaking News: The Gate Squeaks

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local television news, urgent banners, repeated live hits, and anchors who interrupt for tiny updates*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A neighborhood playground's squeaky gate becomes breaking news when a local station discovers it can interrupt its own broadcast indefinitely.

Cast: Anchor Sela, a macaque; reporter Omi, a capuchin; producer Daro, a baboon; camera operator Venn, a gibbon; groundskeeper Tavi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. NEWS STUDIO - EVENING

The anchor reads the weather. A red banner wipes across the screen: "BREAKING: GATE MAKES NOISE."

SELA: We are interrupting this forecast.

DARO: The gate squeaked again.

SELA: It has not moved.

ACT ONE

Omi stands outside the playground gate. The camera shows children playing inside. Tavi pushes the gate; it squeaks. Omi turns toward the microphone.

OMI: We are at the site of the sound.

SELA: Is anyone in danger?

OMI: No. The gate is open and the path is clear.

DARO (IN EARPIECE): Can you ask a witness what it felt like?

Omi asks a parent whether the squeak surprised them. The parent says it sounds like the gate always sounds. A child swings the gate gently. It squeaks again.

OMI: The sound has repeated.

SELA: We are tracking developments.

ACT TWO

Daro splits the screen: Sela in studio, Omi beside the gate, and a close-up of the hinge. He enlarges the hinge until the live reporter's face disappears.

SELA: We are hearing reports of a second squeak.

OMI: That is the same squeak. I pushed the gate once.

DARO: We need a new angle.

Venn walks around the hinge with a camera. The gate opens silently from the other side. Omi says it squeaks when pulled toward the playground. Tavi explains that the hinge is dry and needs maintenance. Daro asks whether they can test it once more for viewers.

TAVI: I can grease it after the park closes.

OMI: We shouldn't keep swinging the gate while people are using it.

Sela interrupts to say the station has received a viewer message. It says "My garden gate also squeaks." She reads the message aloud twice. Omi asks whether it is from this neighborhood. It is from a town three hours away.

SELA: The story is spreading.

OMI: The sound is not.

ACT TWO-B

The station's ticker changes to "SQUEAK HEARD AGAIN." Omi asks Tavi to describe the planned repair. Tavi says he will clean the hinge and apply suitable lubricant after the playground closes. Daro asks him to demonstrate. Tavi refuses to do maintenance in the live walkway. Instead, he shows the station's camera a work tag with the park's scheduled repair time.

TAVI: It is on the maintenance list for tomorrow morning.

SELA: We have a time.

DARO: Can we call it a deadline?

OMI: It is a work schedule.

Venn tries a shot from behind the gate. The squeak becomes louder through the microphone, though it is unchanged. Omi asks Venn to lower the audio gain. The squeak returns to normal volume. Daro tells Sela that the station is now following both the gate and its sound level.

A second update interrupts the forecast. Daro plays a recorded clip of the gate from the wrong side; it sounds silent. He asks Omi to repeat the live test. Omi declines while a child walks through the entrance.

OMI: We can demonstrate it when the path is clear.

SELA: Is the path clear now?

OMI: A family is passing through.

The family exits. Tavi closes the gate gently, then opens it without swinging. It squeaks once. Daro puts a red arrow on the screen pointing at the hinge. The arrow covers the maintenance tag.

TAVI: That tag has the repair time.

DARO: I can move the arrow.

He moves it. It now points at Omi's shoe.

OMI: The shoe is not involved.

SELA: Could the gate be responding to the camera?

TAVI: It is responding to being opened.

ACT THREE

Sela returns to the weather forecast and leaves the story banner up. Omi reports that the gate is open, the path is safe, and a routine repair is scheduled. Tavi puts a small "maintenance tomorrow" tag on the hinge. He does not close the gate while children are still using it.

DARO: We can stand by for the repair.

OMI: It is tomorrow morning.

SELA: We may return then.

Omi ends the live hit. Daro removes the red banner but leaves a small note on the studio monitor. Sela reads the forecast without interruption. A gust moves a map on the desk, and the paper rustles into her microphone.

SELA: We have a new sound.

DARO: Is it breaking?

SELA: It is paper.

TAG

The next morning, the studio runs a short update: the hinge has been serviced. The gate opens quietly. Omi nods to camera and leaves.

FINAL IMAGE: Sela looks at the ticker, which now reads "GATE QUIET," while Daro tries to make the words fit inside the tiny banner.
