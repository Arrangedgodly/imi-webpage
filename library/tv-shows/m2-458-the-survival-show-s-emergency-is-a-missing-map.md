# The Survival Show's Emergency Is a Missing Map

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: wilderness survival competitions, artificial countdowns, and hosts who treat a marked trail as a plot twist*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Rell, a baboon; contestant Nima, a macaque; contestant Odo, an orangutan; field guide Sela, a gibbon; camera operator Fenn, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. PARK TRAILHEAD - MORNING

A sign shows a two-mile loop with a shelter at the halfway point. Rell faces the camera beside two contestants holding packs.

RELL: Our survivalists have been left deep in the wilderness.

SELA: We are at the public trailhead, next to the ranger station.

NIMA: We have water and a map.

ODO: The map is folded backward.

[The host points to a countdown clock. It reads 90 MINUTES.] 

RELL: They must find shelter before time runs out.

SELA: The shelter is marked on the map, and the trail is open.

[The contestants unfold the map. The host's countdown starts early, before the safety briefing.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. TRAIL - LATER

Sela explains the marked trail and the meeting points. The contestants carry water and a basic first-aid kit. Rell follows with the camera crew and asks them to pretend not to know where they are.

NIMA: We can follow the blue blazes.

ODO: The route card also says north at the fork.

RELL: The audience loves a fork in the story.

SELA: The fork is an actual trail fork. We stop here and check the map.

[They pause at the junction. Odo holds the map upright. Nima matches the trail marker to the printed route. Fenn keeps the camera on the sign while Rell tries to step beyond the marked path for a dramatic angle.] 

SELA: Stay on trail. The brush is protected.

RELL: I have been redirected by nature.

[The countdown monitor displays 45 minutes remaining. Sela points out that the clock began at the trailhead, not at the survival challenge. Rell asks if it can be reset for suspense. Sela says the original start time is written on the production sheet.] 

NIMA: We have enough time to reach the shelter at a normal pace.

ODO: The host has a faster pace on camera.

ACT TWO

EXT. SHELTER CLEARING - MIDDAY

The group reaches a roofed rest shelter beside the trail. Nima checks the posted rules; the shelter is for public use and cannot be altered. Odo unfolds a compact tarp to demonstrate a temporary shade screen away from the entrance. Sela checks the wind direction.

RELL: Your task is to build shelter from nothing.

NIMA: We brought a tarp, as allowed.

SELA: And this is already a shelter. The challenge is to make a safe rest stop, not cut branches.

[Odo ties the tarp to two existing posts using quick-release loops. Nima checks the knots. Fenn films their hands. Rell tries to add a dramatic branch across the path, but Sela removes it.] 

SELA: No trip hazards. Keep the entrance clear.

ODO: The tarp can shade the bench without blocking the door.

[They test the shade by sitting under it. The tarp flaps once in the breeze. Nima unties one loop, lowers the corner, and reties it. The shade holds.] 

RELL: A near-disaster.

NIMA: It was a loose loop, now tightened.

[The host asks the contestants to sleep outside overnight for a second-day twist. Sela checks the park schedule: the trail closes at sunset. Odo folds the tarp and stows it; the group packs all supplies back into their bags.] 

ACT THREE

EXT. TRAILHEAD - AFTERNOON

The contestants return with their gear. The countdown shows four minutes; Rell asks them to race the last ten steps. Sela stops the clock at the trailhead, where the task started. Nima and Odo finish together, both with water remaining and all equipment packed.

RELL: We have a dramatic tie.

SELA: The goal was a safe round trip. They both completed it before closing.

NIMA: The map did not go missing.

ODO: The map was just folded wrong.

[Fenn turns the camera toward the sign and records the official route, including the distance and shelter marker. Rell stands beside the map with a prop compass.] 

RELL: For viewers at home, the wilderness is two miles long today.

SELA: The trail remains open until five.

[The contestants return the compass prop and thank Sela. The host searches the pack for a surprise clue. Nima shows the map, folded correctly, with the route marked.] 

TAG

Rell holds the map upside down again. Sela silently rotates it. The host turns it back, then follows the label reading NORTH.

FINAL IMAGE: The contestants leave the trailhead together while the survival show crew packs up beside the posted map.
