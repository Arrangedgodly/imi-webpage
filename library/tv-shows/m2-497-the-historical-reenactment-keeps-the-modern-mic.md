# The Historical Reenactment Keeps the Modern Mic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: historical reenactment programs that insist on immersion while modern production gear stays in the scene*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: narrator Yara, a lemur; captain Boro, a macaque; scribe Leni, a capuchin; director Omi, a gibbon; boom operator Tavi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. REENACTMENT SET - DAY

Captain Boro stands beside a cardboard ship wheel. A boom microphone hangs above him.

YARA (V.O.): In the year 1492, the captain addressed his crew.

BORO: Is that a microphone?

[The boom operator lowers it out of frame.]

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Omi tells the cast to stay in character. Tavi raises the boom. Boro begins his speech again. The mic dips into frame. Leni, playing the ship's scribe, reaches up and catches it.

LENI: The heavens have lowered a black fruit.

Omi says the line is not in the script. Yara records a new narration: the scribe encounters a strange object. Tavi tries a smaller boom. It is still visible against the painted sky. Omi asks him to hide behind the mast. The mast is a painted flat and cannot hide anyone.

ACT TWO

INT. SCRIPT TABLE - LATER

Leni studies the historical notes. The scene takes place on a ship, but the script calls the ocean "a lake." Boro asks whether the captain would have known the difference. Yara says the narration will explain it. Omi asks for a take without the microphone in frame. Tavi holds the boom from the floor. His arm appears in the lower corner.

[They reset. Boro raises a spyglass. The prop is a cardboard tube with a camera lens visible through the other end. He looks through it and sees the crew filming him.]

BORO: The horizon is watching us.

Omi breaks character to ask Tavi to lock the lens cap. The cap falls off and rolls across the deck. Leni picks it up and places it on the scribe's table as a mysterious artifact. Yara narrates that the object will alter the course of history. Tavi retrieves it and returns it to the camera.

INT. SET - LATER

Omi asks the cast to perform the scene while the camera remains still. Boro gives the speech. Leni writes on a scroll. The scroll is blank, but she fills it with the episode's call sheet. Omi notices the modern date at the top. Leni folds it before the camera sees. The boom microphone dips again, this time with a small foam windscreen shaped like a lemon.

LENI: The sky has sent a fruit.

Tavi apologizes. Omi says they will keep the line if the boom stays out of frame.

INT. SET - MOMENTS LATER

Omi calls for a quiet reset. Tavi tests the boom from behind the painted mast. It stays out of frame until Boro turns the cardboard wheel. The wheel squeaks loudly. Leni asks if the ship is haunted. Yara begins a voice-over about an omen. Tavi stops the wheel with one hand; the squeak ends. Omi asks for the wheel to turn only when the captain gives the cue, then restarts the take. Tavi marks the wheel's starting position with tape. The painted horizon no longer drifts.

ACT THREE

INT. REENACTMENT SET - AFTERNOON

The crew performs one full take. Tavi hides behind the ship's wheel, keeping the boom above the camera frame. Boro addresses the crew. Leni writes the captain's words on a fresh scroll. The scroll catches on the wheel and winds around it. Boro turns the wheel; the paper wraps around his arms.

BORO: We are turning toward the new world.

LENI: The new world is inside the wheel.

Omi calls cut. The paper is safely unwound. Yara asks for a narration line to cover the stop. She records that the ship's records were preserved. Omi checks the footage. The boom stays out, but the camera lens reflects the modern lighting grid in the spyglass. Boro's final line is clear; the grid is unmistakable.

Omi asks if they should reshoot. Tavi rotates the prop lens away from the lights. They film only the final close-up again, this time reflecting the painted sky. Leni stands by the wheel with the recovered scroll. No modern object enters the frame. The director accepts the take, then immediately steps into shot to tell them to hold.

TAG

INT. SET - AFTER THE SHOOT

Yara records the final narration while Omi reads the call sheet. Tavi hangs the boom on a rack marked MODERN EQUIPMENT. Boro removes his costume hat and puts it on the rack too.

FINAL IMAGE: The painted ship sails across a flat blue backdrop, while the boom operator's shadow remains visible on the floor.
