# The Snow Match Has No Ball

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcasts that preserve the usual rules even when weather makes the equipment impossible to see*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A snow-covered field hosts a friendly ball game, but the commentators spend the match arguing about where the ball went.

Cast: Commentator Venn, a macaque; analyst Omi, a gibbon; referee Ralo, a capuchin; player Kori, a baboon; and camera operator Tavi, a lemur.

Episode: "Whiteout Final"

COLD OPEN

EXT. FIELD - DAY

Snow covers the grass. Venn sits in a booth. Players wait beside a bright orange ball.

VENN: The teams are ready for the final.

OMI: The ball is visible against the snow.

[TAVI ZOOMS IN. THE BALL ROLLS BEHIND A WHITE SNOWBANK.]

VENN: The ball has disappeared.

RALO: It is behind the bank.

ACT ONE

Kori kicks the ball. It rolls across the field. The camera follows, but the ball is lost against the snowy ground.

VENN: Kori sends it downfield.

OMI: I cannot see the ball.

RALO: It is moving toward the goal.

[TAVI PANS TO THE WRONG GOAL. THE BALL STOPS BESIDE A CONE.]

KORI: That's the sideline cone.

VENN: We have a stoppage.

ACT TWO

EXT. FIELD - LATER

Ralo marks the ball's location with a small flag. Snow begins to fall harder. Omi checks the score.

OMI: The teams are tied.

VENN: The clock has three minutes left.

KORI: The ball is under the snow.

RALO: We can pause and retrieve it.

VENN: The broadcast needs continuous play.

[THE PLAYERS SEARCH. ONE FINDS A GLOVE, ANOTHER FINDS A SMALL ORANGE BALL.]

OMI: That is the ball.

KORI: It is a lost practice ball.

RALO: The match ball is larger.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. FIELD - AFTERNOON

The teams line up for a restart. Tavi places a bright marker beside the match ball. Venn narrates.

VENN: The marker will help the viewers follow play.

[TAVI SETS A WHITE FLAG. IT DISAPPEARS IN THE SNOW.]

OMI: We need a different color.

[TAVI SETS A RED FLAG. IT BLOWS OVER.]

KORI: The wind has carried the marker away.

RALO: We can move the game to the indoor court.

VENN: The final was scheduled outdoors.

OMI: The court is visible.

[THE SNOW COVERS THE SCOREBOARD. RALO BRUSHES IT OFF.]

ACT TWO-C

EXT. FIELD - LATE AFTERNOON

The teams search near the snowbank. Venn has a clipboard. Omi checks the time. Kori brushes snow from the sideline cone.

VENN: We cannot continue without the match ball.

OMI: The indoor court is available.

KORI: The ball may be under the snowbank.

RALO: I will check the edge with the rake.

[RALO RAKES A SHALLOW LINE. THE BALL APPEARS UNDER A THIN LAYER OF SNOW.]

VENN: We have found it.

OMI: It is wet but intact.

KORI: Can we finish outside?

RALO: The field markings are covered.

VENN: The teams can agree to move the game indoors.

[KORI AND THE OTHER CAPTAIN NOD. TAVI CARRIES THE BALL TOWARD THE BUILDING.]

VENN: We have a relocation, not a cancellation.

OMI: The new court has visible lines.

KORI: And a roof.

[THE TEAMS WALK INSIDE. VENN FOLDS THE SNOW-COVERED LINE MARKER AND CARRIES IT UNDER HIS ARM.]

ACT TWO-D

INT. INDOOR COURT - LATE AFTERNOON

The referee places the ball at center. The scoreboard is visible. Venn takes his seat in the booth. Tavi frames both goals.

VENN: The teams have resumed with a one-minute warmup.

OMI: The court lines are clear.

KORI: The ball is back in play.

[KORI PASSES TO A TEAMMATE. THE PASS REACHES THE GOAL AREA. RALO BLOWS THE WHISTLE.]

RALO: That play started before the signal.

VENN: The team is surprised by the call.

KORI: We were told to resume.

RALO: I had not blown the whistle.

OMI: The referee is ready now.

[THE BALL RETURNS TO CENTER. BOTH TEAMS WAIT.]

VENN: We have a restart.

KORI: The indoor court has a roof. It does not have a warmer referee.

VENN: The teams are set for the final minute.

OMI: The camera can follow the ball indoors.

[TAVI CHECKS THE LENS AND NODS.]

ACT THREE

INT. INDOOR COURT - FINAL MINUTE

The teams resume play indoors. Venn watches the screen. Omi checks the ball.

VENN: We can see the ball again.

KORI: I can see the basket.

[RALO BLOWS THE WHISTLE. THE GAME ENDS IN A TIE.]

OMI: The score is still tied.

VENN: We will go to a shootout.

RALO: The teams agreed to share the result.

KORI: We are cold.

OMI: The warm court has ended the weather delay.

VENN: The teams leave with a shared score.

TAG

Tavi finds the original ball outside. It is bright orange on top of the snowbank.

VENN: The ball has returned.

OMI: The game is over.
