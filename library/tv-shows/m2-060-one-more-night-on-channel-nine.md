# One More Night on Channel Nine

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television series finales, network cancellation pressure, and season-ending cliffhangers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: On the station's final scheduled broadcast, each host fights to finish their own storyline before the transmitter shuts off, while the producer insists every goodbye must end with a cliffhanger.

Cast: Anchor Lema, a macaque; weather host Poro, a lemur; interview host Venn, a baboon; technician Kiri, a capuchin; and station manager Doro, an orangutan.

## Episode: "The Last Night"

### Cold open

INT. CHANNEL NINE CONTROL ROOM, NIGHT

[A red timer counts down from 30:00. A test pattern waits on the largest monitor.]

DORO: At midnight, the main transmitter shuts down.

KIRI: The backup can reach the lower grove.

DORO: It isn't approved.

LEMA: Then we have thirty minutes to get it approved.

VENN: I have a guest who can help.

DORO: Who?

VENN: You.

DORO: I don't want to be interviewed.

PORO: The forecast is clear.

LEMA: We have a closure, a guest, and a weather report.

KIRI: We also have a countdown.

[The timer jumps from 30:00 to 29:58. Doro reaches for the switch.]

### Act One

INT. NEWS STUDIO

[Lema sits at the desk. Poro holds a weather map. Venn sits in the guest chair across from Doro.]

LEMA: Our top story: Channel Nine will end its broadcast tonight.

VENN: What caused the decision?

DORO: The network wants the frequency for a test pattern.

VENN: Why?

DORO: The test pattern is easier to schedule.

LEMA: That is the official explanation.

PORO: The unofficial forecast is cloudy with a chance of static.

DORO: Please give the weather report.

PORO: A cloud is moving over the west grove.

VENN: Is that the cloud we have covered before?

PORO: It is a new cloud.

LEMA: How can you tell?

PORO: This one has a flat bottom.

KIRI: We have twelve minutes to test the backup transmitter.

DORO: We have twenty-six minutes.

KIRI: The timer is not the transmitter.

LEMA: We need the test pattern to remain off.

VENN: I can ask the manager a final question.

DORO: Don't.

VENN: What will you miss most?

DORO: The quiet after we sign off.

[The studio goes silent. Poro's weather map rustles.]

PORO: The wind is rising.

### Act Two

INT. TRANSMITTER SHED

[Kiri connects a cable. Lema holds a flashlight. Venn carries his interview microphone.]

KIRI: The backup signal is weak.

LEMA: Can you make it stronger?

KIRI: I need the antenna moved above the roofline.

VENN: I can carry it.

DORO: You are supposed to be interviewing me.

VENN: The station is the guest now.

PORO: The cloud is over the shed.

LEMA: It is not raining.

PORO: The cloud is blocking the dish.

[The signal meter dips. Kiri adjusts the antenna.]

KIRI: I need someone to hold this steady.

DORO: I can hold it.

KIRI: Not by the cable.

[Doro grips the cable. The picture on the monitor breaks into colored bars.]

LEMA: The test pattern is coming through.

VENN: The final broadcast is becoming the thing it fears.

DORO: Let go of the cable.

[Doro lets go. The picture returns.]

KIRI: The lower grove can receive the signal.

DORO: The network owns the transmitter.

KIRI: The network owns the tower. The backup belongs to the community station.

DORO: It reaches six trees.

LEMA: Those trees are where our viewers live.

VENN: We have a decision.

DORO: We have a contract.

KIRI: We have a switch.

[The countdown reaches 10:00. Doro looks at the switch.]

DORO: If we use the backup, the main channel ends as planned.

LEMA: Then we say goodbye to the upper grove.

PORO: And hello to the lower one.

### Act Three

INT. CONTROL ROOM, 23:59

[The hosts take their places. Kiri stands by the backup switch. Doro holds the contract.]

LEMA: We are one minute from sign-off.

VENN: I have one final question for the manager.

DORO: Ask it.

VENN: Do you want the station to end?

DORO: I want it to stop pretending it can reach everyone.

KIRI: The backup signal is live.

PORO: The lower grove is clear.

LEMA: We can stay on for the listeners who can see us.

DORO: The network will object.

KIRI: The network can object on the main frequency.

DORO: And the staff?

LEMA: We can keep producing the morning report.

PORO: I can keep tracking clouds.

VENN: I can interview the neighbors.

DORO: Then this is not the end.

LEMA: It is the end of this signal.

DORO: That is a reasonable ending.

[The timer reaches zero. The main monitor turns to a test pattern. The backup monitor remains on the hosts.]

KIRI: We are still broadcasting.

VENN: The upper grove has the test pattern.

PORO: The lower grove has the forecast.

LEMA: We will see you at sunrise.

DORO: On the backup channel.

LEMA: We haven't named it.

DORO: We can decide tomorrow.

### Tag

INT. CONTROL ROOM, MIDNIGHT

[The main monitor displays a test pattern. On the small backup screen, the hosts begin striking the set. Behind them, the countdown timer restarts at 30:00.]

KIRI: The timer has reset.

DORO: That is next week's problem.

[The camera pulls back. The backup signal light stays on.]
