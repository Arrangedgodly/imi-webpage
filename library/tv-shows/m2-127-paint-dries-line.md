# Stay on the Line Until the Paint Dries

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: charity telethons that use countdown clocks, escalating pledges, and celebrity appeals without explaining what each donation pays for*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Roko wants a rousing pledge drive for the community hall's roof. Treasurer Mina needs the crew to fund one specific repair instead of promising a complete transformation by midnight.

Cast: Roko, a macaque host; Mina, a gorilla treasurer; Seli, a lemur stage manager; and Puck, a capuchin caller coordinator.

## Episode: "The Thermometer Is Not the Roof"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A LARGE THERMOMETER GRAPHIC READS "ROOF FUND." THE NEEDLE IS AT ZERO.]

ROKO: Every pledge brings us closer to a dry hall.

MINA: We need one section of flashing repaired.

[SELI STARTS A COUNTDOWN CLOCK. IT READS 10:00.]

ROKO: Ten minutes to save the roof.

MINA: The roofer comes next Tuesday.

[THE PHONE RINGS. PUCK ANSWERS.]

PUCK: The first caller pledges a ladder.

ROKO: We are already climbing.

MINA: We need a repair, not a ladder.

### Act One

[ROKO FACES CAMERA. THE THERMOMETER NEEDLE JUMPS WHEN SELI TAPS THE SCREEN.]

ROKO: We have raised one ladder, two tarps, and a box of nails.

MINA: The box of nails is not a pledge. It is in the supply closet.

[PUCK HOLDS UP A SIGN: "CALL NOW AND DOUBLE YOUR IMPACT."]

MINA: Double what?

PUCK: The impact.

ROKO: Every contribution helps.

MINA: The repair estimate is six hundred. We have two hundred in the fund and a written bid for the remaining work.

[ROKO ASKS SELI TO MAKE THE NEEDLE MOVE SLOWLY.]

SELI: It is a graphic, not a bank balance.

[THE NEEDLE SHOOTS TO THE TOP AND FALLS OFF THE SCREEN.]

ROKO: We have exceeded the roof.

MINA: We have lost the thermometer.

[PUCK TAKES A CALL.]

PUCK: A caller offers to host a benefit concert.

MINA: That could help after we confirm the roofer's date.

ROKO: Can we announce a concert tonight?

MINA: We do not know the performers.

[MINA POINTS TO THE HALL ROOF ON A SIMPLE DIAGRAM. THE LEAK IS OVER THE SMALL STORAGE ROOM, NOT THE MAIN HALL.]

MINA: This is the section the roofer will repair.

ROKO: And the rest?

MINA: It will be inspected next month.

[ROKO MOVES THE CAMERA SO VIEWERS CAN SEE THE MARKED SECTION.]

ROKO: A small repair with a real address.

### Act Two

[THE CREW RETURNS AFTER A BREAK. ROKO STANDS BESIDE A DISPLAY OF GIFT ITEMS: A MUG, A SCARF, AND A MINIATURE HAMMER.]

ROKO: Pledge at the silver level and receive our limited-edition roof mug.

MINA: That mug is from last year's raffle.

PUCK: We put a new sticker on it.

[MINA PEELS BACK THE STICKER. IT READS "WINTER WALK."]

ROKO: The mug has traveled with us.

MINA: It has been in the closet.

[THE PHONE RINGS. A CALLER ASKS WHAT A FIFTY-UNIT PLEDGE BUYS.]

CALLER: Will the hall be dry by the concert?

MINA: The roofer expects the repair to be finished before then.

ROKO: Can we make that a guarantee?

MINA: We can share the schedule and update it if weather changes.

[ROKO CROSSES OUT "MIRACLE BY MIDNIGHT" ON THE TELEPROMPTER.]

ROKO: Hope.

MINA: A portion of the flashing repair. We will publish the invoice and the completion date.

[SELI WRITES THE TOTAL ON A WHITEBOARD. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK BEEPS.]

ROKO: We have three minutes left.

MINA: The deadline is next Tuesday.

ROKO: The show ends in three minutes.

MINA: Then we can use the last three minutes to read the estimate.

[MINA HOLDS UP THE BID. PUCK FINDS A SMALL PRINT LINE THAT SAYS "MATERIALS INCLUDED."]

PUCK: This covers the flashing and the labor.

MINA: Good. We can state exactly what happens.

### Act Three

[ROKO READS THE FUND TOTAL. THE GRAPHIC SHOWS 600, MATCHING THE BID.]

ROKO: We have raised the repair amount.

[THE STUDIO APPLAUDS. MINA CHECKS THE NUMBER AGAIN.]

MINA: The roofer confirmed the date. We will keep the rest of the hall fund separate.

ROKO: Can we say the roof is saved?

MINA: We can say one repair is paid for.

[SELI CHANGES THE THERMOMETER LABEL TO "FLASHING REPAIR." THE NEEDLE STOPS AT 600.]

ROKO: A focused finish.

MINA: A paid invoice.

[THE CALLERS ARE INVITED TO LEAVE THEIR CONTACT INFORMATION FOR THE CONCERT. PUCK REMOVES THE GIFT TABLE.]

SELI: The phone line is still open.

ROKO: What are we asking callers to do?

MINA: Leave a contact number if they want concert details. No pledge is required tonight.

[PUCK REPLACES THE SIGN WITH A CLEAR ONE: "ROOF REPAIR FUNDED. CONCERT INFO AVAILABLE NEXT MONTH."]

### Tag

[ROKO HOLDS THE MINIATURE HAMMER.]

ROKO: This is a symbol of everything we can do together.

MINA: It is a paperweight.

[THE HAMMER HOLDS DOWN THE SIGNED BID WHILE MINA FILES IT.]
