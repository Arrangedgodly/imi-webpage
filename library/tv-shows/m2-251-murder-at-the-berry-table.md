# Murder at the Berry Table

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cozy village mystery television, where a missing berry tart is investigated with an oversized clue board and tea service*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: The village mystery club investigates the disappearance of a berry tart from the community table, though everyone knows who baked it and where the crumbs went.

Cast: Amateur sleuth Pava, gibbon; baker Nara, macaque; librarian Odo, baboon; gardener Seli, capuchin; host Luma, lemur.

## Episode: "The Tart of St. Bramble"

### Cold Open

[COMMUNITY HALL. AN EMPTY CAKE STAND.]

LUMA: At tea, the tart was here. Now there are crumbs.

PAVA: We have a crime.

NARA: It was served.

ODO: That is what a culprit would say.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PAVA STANDS BEFORE A BOARD WITH A DRAWING OF A TART.]

PAVA: The missing dessert was a berry tart, one hand wide, with a lattice crust.

NARA: I baked it.

PAVA: A compelling connection.

NARA: I also cut it into slices.

Odo pins a crumb to the clue board. It falls off.

ODO: The evidence is fragile.

SELI: It is a crumb.

Luma asks everyone where they were at tea. Seli says she was in the garden, bringing flowers. Odo says he was in the library. Nara says she was serving the tart. Pava says she was watching the room for suspicious behavior.

LUMA: Did you see anyone take a slice?

PAVA: I saw everyone take one.

ODO: A coordinated group.

NARA: A tea party.

### Act Two

[THE CLUB SEARCHES THE HALL.]

Pava finds a berry smear on a napkin. She calls it a clue. Nara identifies it as blackberry.

PAVA: Which berry was in the tart.

NARA: And in the jam on the table.

Odo lifts the tablecloth. A crumb falls from the edge. He places it in an envelope.

ODO: We have a second sample.

SELI: It came from the tart.

PAVA: We cannot assume that.

Nara points to the plate stack. Every plate has a small pastry mark. Odo labels the stack "possible evidence." Luma says the plates should be washed.

PAVA: We must preserve the scene.

LUMA: The scene is a hall with dishes.

Seli says she brought a basket of berries from the garden, but it is still outside. Pava asks if any berries are missing. Seli checks; one berry is gone.

PAVA: The tart thief took a berry.

SELI: A bird took a berry.

Odo puts "bird" on the suspect list.

[PAVA INSPECTS THE TEA SERVICE.]

She asks Nara to list every slice. Nara names the six guests and their plates. Odo points to a seventh plate with no tart mark.

ODO: The missing slice could be here.

NARA: That plate was for the teapot.

PAVA: The teapot is not a suspect.

SELI: It has been on the table all afternoon.

Odo checks beneath the plate and finds a berry seed. He announces a breakthrough. Nara says the seed fell from the tart while she served it. Pava asks her to demonstrate. Nara carries a plate from the kitchen; a crumb drops along the same path.

PAVA: The crumb trail leads from the oven to the table.

LUMA: The tart took that route.

ODO: The culprit was the baker.

NARA: The baker served the tart.

PAVA: A delicious alibi.

### Act Three

[THE CLUB RECONSTRUCTS THE TEA SERVICE.]

Nara lays out the slices. There were six. Odo remembers eating one. Seli remembers eating one. Pava says she had half. Luma says she took a slice to the kitchen.

PAVA: The missing tart was distributed.

ODO: Then where is the final slice?

Nara checks the kitchen. The last piece is in a covered dish on the counter. Pava opens it with ceremony.

PAVA: The tart has reappeared.

NARA: I saved it for the cleanup crew.

Luma confirms that she carried the dish from the hall. Odo removes "culprit" from the clue board and replaces it with "leftovers."

PAVA: We must now investigate why the tart was covered.

NARA: So it would stay fresh.

SELI: A sensible motive.

The club makes a final inventory: one tart served, one slice saved, crumbs on the table, berry stain on the napkin.

### Tag

[THE CLUB SITS AROUND THE TABLE.]

PAVA: The case is closed.

ODO: Until the jam goes missing.

LUMA: The jam is in the pantry.

PAVA: For now.

Nara brings the final slice. Everyone reaches for it at once.

SELI: We have a new mystery.

NARA: No. We have five forks.

PAVA: The case was always about the dessert.

ODO: And the dessert is still here.

LUMA: Then pass the plate.

PAVA: The final clue was on the table.

[SFX: FORKS TAP PLATE.]

PAVA: The evidence is compelling.

NARA: It is dessert.
