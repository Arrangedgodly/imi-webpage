# The Science Host's Magnet Sticks

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's science shows that promise a surprising result even when the demonstration fails as designed*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A science host tries to make a magnet stick to a wooden spoon, while the young assistant keeps demonstrating what magnets actually do.

Cast: Host Tavi, a macaque; science assistant Runi, a gibbon; camera operator Vela, a capuchin; and school visitor Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "Magnetic Mystery"

COLD OPEN

INT. CLASSROOM STUDIO - DAY

Tavi holds a magnet near a wooden spoon. Runi watches. Vela counts down from behind a camera.

TAVI: Will the magnet stick to the spoon?

RUNI: No. The spoon is wood.

TAVI: Let's find out.

[THE MAGNET FALLS TO THE TABLE.]

TAVI: A surprising result.

RUNI: It did not stick.

ACT ONE

Tavi sets up a tray of objects: a paper clip, a wooden spoon, a shell, and a steel washer.

TAVI: The magnet may attract any of these.

RUNI: The paper clip and washer are magnetic. The spoon and shell are not.

TAVI: Let's test them one by one.

[THE MAGNET LIFTS THE PAPER CLIP. IT DOES NOT LIFT THE SHELL.]

TAVI: The shell is hiding something.

ODO: It is made of calcium carbonate.

Vela zooms in on the shell. Tavi rotates it as if searching for a hidden piece.

RUNI: The shell is not magnetic.

TAVI: We have not tried the other side.

ACT TWO

INT. CLASSROOM STUDIO - LATER

Tavi asks Odo to hold the wooden spoon. Runi brings a compass.

TAVI: If we wave the magnet, can we move the compass needle?

RUNI: Yes, if it is close enough.

[THE NEEDLE MOVES. ODO'S SPOON STAYS STILL.]

TAVI: The spoon has not moved.

ODO: It is wood.

TAVI: Could the spoon have a secret metal core?

RUNI: We can check it with the magnet.

[TAVI RUNS THE MAGNET ALONG THE SPOON. NOTHING HAPPENS.]

TAVI: No secret core.

RUNI: That is the answer.

TAVI: We can still make the experiment exciting.

[HE PLACES THE MAGNET UNDER THE TABLE. THE COMPASS ON TOP SWINGS.]

ODO: The table is not moving.

TAVI: The compass is responding.

ACT TWO-B

INT. CLASSROOM STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Vela plays a dramatic sting. Tavi puts a paper clip under a folded card. Runi holds a magnet underneath.

TAVI: Can the magnet move the clip through the card?

RUNI: Yes. The card is thin and not magnetic.

[THE CLIP SLIDES ACROSS THE CARD.]

TAVI: It is traveling by itself.

RUNI: It is following the magnet.

ODO: The clip moves. The card stays.

[TAVI LIFTS THE CARD. THE CLIP DROPS INTO A CUP.]

TAVI: It has fallen into the magnetic mystery cup.

RUNI: It is an ordinary cup.

ACT TWO-C

INT. CLASSROOM STUDIO - LATE AFTERNOON

Vela brings in a second tray with a steel spoon, an aluminum lid, and a wooden ruler. Tavi sets the magnet in the center.

TAVI: Our next mystery is which objects will jump.

RUNI: None of them will jump. We can test whether they attract.

ODO: The steel spoon should respond. The aluminum lid should not.

TAVI: Will the ruler stick?

RUNI: No. It is wood.

[TAVI SLIDES THE MAGNET UNDER THE TABLE. THE STEEL SPOON CLICKS TOWARD IT. THE LID AND RULER STAY STILL.]

TAVI: One moved, two did not.

RUNI: We have a result.

TAVI: Can we make the lid move for the surprise?

ODO: It is aluminum.

TAVI: Could the magnet be stronger?

RUNI: We can use a stronger magnet, but the lid is still not attracted to it.

TAVI: Then the surprise is that it does not move.

[VELA STARTS A DRUMROLL. THE LID REMAINS STILL. THE DRUMROLL ENDS.]

VELA: The lid held its position.

ODO: The table also held its position.

RUNI: That is the same result.

ODO: We can label each object with what happened.

TAVI: The steel spoon moved. The wooden ruler did not.

RUNI: And the aluminum lid stayed still.

[TAVI PUTS THE OBJECTS BACK ON THE TRAY. THE SPOON ROLLS AGAINST THE MAGNET.]

TAVI: The spoon is still attracted.

ACT THREE

INT. CLASSROOM STUDIO - FINAL DEMONSTRATION

Tavi faces the camera with the spoon, shell, clip, and washer. Runi points to each object.

RUNI: Magnets attract some metals. They do not attract wood or shells.

TAVI: Our spoon did not stick.

ODO: It did a good job being a spoon.

[TAVI USES THE SPOON TO STIR A CUP OF WATER. THE MAGNET PICKS UP THE STEEL WASHER BESIDE IT.]

TAVI: Two different jobs.

RUNI: Now that is a useful result.

TAG

Tavi holds the magnet near the camera tripod. Nothing happens.

TAVI: The camera is not magnetic.

VELA: The screw on it is.

[THE MAGNET PICKS UP ONE LOOSE SCREW. THE CAMERA TILTS SLIGHTLY.]
