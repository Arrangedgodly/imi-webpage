# The Studio Audience Is One Person

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sitcom pilots that manufacture a laugh track and enthusiastic applause before there is an audience*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Kessi, a macaque; producer Daro, a gibbon; stage manager Luma, an orangutan; sound mixer Pavi, a lemur; audience guest Rono, a capuchin; non-speaking camera and lighting crew.

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO - DAY

A studio set has a kitchen, a sofa, and a door that opens onto a painted brick wall. The audience seats are empty except for Rono, who sits in the front row with a paper cup.

KESSI: Welcome to the pilot taping!

[The applause sign lights. Rono claps alone. The sound mixer plays a huge applause effect. The echo rattles a prop plate off the counter.]

LUMA: We have one audience member and no plates left.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Producer Daro tells Kessi the network wants a lively audience response. Kessi tries a warm-up joke about the wall behind the door. Rono laughs politely. Pavi plays a recorded laugh, but it arrives before the punch line.

KESSI: Our timing is already ahead of the comedy.

Daro asks Luma to fill the front rows with extras. Luma brings in the studio crew. Each person sits in a different section, but they continue holding equipment: a boom pole, a slate, and two cables. Pavi plays applause again. The crew claps while trying not to drop their gear.

[Kessi performs the first scene. She opens the painted door and greets the brick wall. Rono laughs. The sound mixer adds a crowd roar that drowns out the next line.]

KESSI: We need to hear the one person who came.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Pavi pulls the canned-laughter fader down, but it sticks halfway. Daro worries that the set will feel empty. Rono asks if he can move seats. Luma says he is the audience, so the camera will follow him. He sits in the back row. The camera operator zooms to the empty front seats.

Daro signals Kessi to repeat the joke. Kessi does. Rono does not laugh. He is trying to find the restroom. The laugh track plays anyway. Pavi tries the fader again; it is stuck. He mutes the effects channel at the console, but a monitor speaker still plays the laugh under Kessi's line.

PAVI: The effects channel is still routed to the monitor.

LUMA: Then mute that route, not the room.

[Rono reads the restroom sign and leaves the audience area. Kessi continues the scene to three empty rows. The applause sign lights; no one claps. Daro gives a silent thumbs-up to the camera.]

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The network calls the producer and asks for louder laughter. Daro asks Pavi to raise the canned track. Pavi shows him the muted effects channel and leaves it off. Luma asks two crew members to sit in the front row. One holds a light reflector; the other still wears a headset. Daro asks them to clap. The reflector catches the applause sign and throws red light across the set. Pavi dims the sign and keeps the room microphone open for the real audience.

ACT THREE

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Rono returns from the restroom and takes his seat in the front row. Kessi restarts the scene. This time the camera stays on Rono, who laughs at the brick wall because Kessi opens the door and offers it a seat.

KESSI: The wall has been waiting all day.

[Rono laughs. The crew laughs too. Pavi does not add a track. Daro asks for a second take with the applause sign off. It works better; Rono's small laugh lands clearly, and the camera catches the rest of the crew smiling behind him.]

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Pavi routes Rono's microphone through the broadcast delay, then asks Kessi to clap once. The test monitor plays the clap before its delayed camera picture shows it. Pavi adjusts the audio delay until sound and picture land together. Rono tests it by laughing on cue; the clap and laugh now match his image. Kessi repeats the wall joke. Rono laughs at the right moment, and Pavi leaves the canned track muted.

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE TAKE

The test-screening monitor replays the scene with the sound effect muted. Rono laughs at the right moment. Daro asks him to stay for another take. Rono says he has a bus to catch. He steps out of the studio and boards the bus visible through the loading-bay window. Luma folds his empty chair and places it in the front row for the next guest. Pavi saves the audio as a clean room track and deletes the canned version.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE TAKE

Daro announces the pilot has been accepted for a test screening. Luma folds the audience chairs. Pavi labels the sound file REAL LAUGH - RONO. Kessi opens the painted door again. The wall remains.

KESSI: At least the set is consistent.

FINAL IMAGE: The bus pulls away beyond the studio window; Rono's front-row chair is empty beneath the dark applause sign.
