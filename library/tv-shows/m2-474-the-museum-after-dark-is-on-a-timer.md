# The Museum After Dark Is on a Timer

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation television, night-vision suspense, and crews who mistake routine building sounds for ghosts*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Reka, a macaque; historian Olo, an orangutan; investigator Miri, a gibbon; camera operator Pali, a baboon; night guard Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - NIGHT

The gallery is empty except for a bronze clock, a glass case, and a velvet rope. The host holds a flashlight. A light clicks off, then on again.

REKA: Something is moving in the museum.

TAVI: The lights are on a timer.

MIRI: We heard a click in the east gallery.

OLO: The clock ticks every second.

[The crew turns on night vision. The screen shows a green image of the velvet rope.] 

ACT ONE

INT. GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

The investigator marks the time and direction of the click. The historian explains that the museum lights shift to low mode at closing and return to full brightness when a motion sensor detects a guard. Reka asks whether an object has moved. Olo checks the case and confirms the artifacts remain in place.

REKA: Could the bronze clock have moved?

OLO: It is bolted to the wall.

MIRI: I recorded a scrape.

TAVI: That was the guard cart crossing the tile seam.

[The camera operator replays the sound. The scrape repeats as the cart passes the seam. Reka asks the guard to walk again for a second take. Tavi refuses because the cart is already parked.] 

PALI: The original recording is clear.

[The crew tests the light timer from the guard station. Tavi demonstrates the motion sensor; the gallery brightens. The host stares into the night-vision camera and whispers.] 

REKA: The building has sensed us.

OLO: The sensor detected motion.

ACT TWO

INT. EAST GALLERY - LATER

The crew follows the clock's ticking. Miri finds a loose velvet rope hook swaying after the guard cart passed. The host asks whether it could be a spirit. Olo points to a vent above the hook, where the airflow is strong enough to move the rope.

MIRI: The hook is loose.

TAVI: I can secure it before opening.

REKA: Is that safe to do now?

TAVI: The gallery is closed. I will block the area, then tighten it.

[The guard places a cone by the rope. The historian waits outside the marked boundary while Tavi secures the hook with a small wrench. The camera operator keeps the case in frame, not the guard's face.] 

PALI: The rope no longer moves.

REKA: The presence has left.

OLO: The airflow is still running.

[The host asks Miri to revisit the opening click. She checks the time stamp and sees the light timer changed modes at 9:00. The second click was the ventilation relay. Tavi checks the panel display.] 

TAVI: The relay is scheduled. No fault light.

[The crew captures the display and returns the flashlight to the case.] 

[The host finds a soundboard button labeled GHOST MOAN and reaches for it. Pali stops the take and checks the recording file. The sound came from a special effect library, not the gallery.] 

PALI: That cue was never recorded in the museum.

MIRI: Then it cannot support our investigation.

REKA: It could support the trailer.

OLO: It would suggest an event that did not happen.

[Reka deletes the sound cue from the timeline. The remaining track has only the clock, cart, relay, and ventilation. Tavi confirms the gallery will reopen with the rope fixed.] 

TAVI: The maintenance note is complete.

[The investigator signs the recording log and hands it to the historian, who files it with the museum's equipment notes.] 

ACT THREE

INT. MUSEUM CONTROL DESK - NIGHT

The team prepares the episode. Reka wants to title it THE GHOST THAT MOVED THE ROPE. Miri proposes AFTER-HOURS SOUNDS: A CLOCK, A RELAY, AND A LOOSE HOOK. Olo confirms that the artifacts did not move. Tavi records the rope repair in the maintenance log.

REKA: The second title is less frightening.

MIRI: It matches what we found.

PALI: We also have the night-vision footage of the sensor.

[The editor plays the clip. A green rope fills the screen. The host asks if the audience can see a shadow. Tavi points at the timecode; it is the cart shadow from the overhead light.] 

TAVI: That is the cart.

OLO: The clock is still ticking.

[Reka listens to the recording without music. The clock, relay, and cart sound distinct. The host removes the dramatic sting from the edit and writes a plain caption.] 

TAG

[The lights dim on schedule. Reka reaches for the flashlight, then sees the motion sensor brighten the gallery as the crew exits. She puts the flashlight away.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The secured velvet rope hangs still beneath the ordinary museum lights.
