# Class Trip to the Moon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's educational television, where a classroom's moon lesson turns into an imaginary voyage with very literal props*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Teacher Piri leads a class through a pretend moon mission, but the students keep correcting the show's oversimplified space facts with observations from their own backyard.

Cast: Teacher Piri, a capuchin; scientist Tavi, a macaque; student Odo, a baboon; student Seli, a gibbon; and camera operator Nara, a lemur.

## Episode: "One Small Hop"

### Cold Open

[CLASSROOM. A CARDBOARD ROCKET HAS A PAPER MOON ON TOP.]

PIRI: Today we travel to the moon.

ODO: In the cardboard rocket?

PIRI: In our imagination.

TAVI: Then we don't need a rocket.

NARA: We need a camera shot of the rocket.

SELI: The moon is not made of paper.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PIRI STANDS BESIDE A BOARD WITH THREE DRAWN PLANETS.]

PIRI: The moon is far away. We will reach it by counting down from five.

ODO: It takes more than five.

PIRI: It takes imagination.

TAVI: How long does it take in real space?

PIRI: Much longer than five.

Tavi points to the moon diagram. It shows a flat gray circle with a smiling face.

TAVI: The moon does not smile.

PIRI: It is friendly for the lesson.

SELI: The moon has craters.

ODO: We can make craters with a bowl.

Nara films as Seli presses a bowl into the sand tray. The sand makes a crater. Odo tries to jump over it and lands in the tray.

PIRI: A moon landing!

ODO: I have landed in the sand.

### Act Two

[THE CLASSROOM IS NOW A CARDBOARD SPACECRAFT.]

Piri gives each student a paper helmet. Odo's helmet is too small and sits on one ear.

ODO: My helmet has a leak.

TAVI: It is paper.

PIRI: We are pretending it protects us.

TAVI: It does not.

Seli tries to teach how gravity works by dropping two soft fruit into a box. One lands first because it is larger.

SELI: The heavy one fell faster.

TAVI: They were different shapes.

PIRI: We should use two identical objects.

Odo holds two matching seeds and drops them. They land together. Nara asks for a slow-motion replay. The camera operator drops the seeds again, but one rolls away.

NARA: The moon has lost a sample.

ODO: It rolled under the desk.

Piri turns the lesson into a search mission. The students crawl beneath the desk, where they find the missing seed, a pencil and an old snack wrapper.

SELI: We discovered a new moon.

TAVI: It is the underside of the desk.

### Act Three

[THE CLASS PREPARES FOR A PRETEND WALK ON THE MOON.]

Piri reads a cue card: "Take one small step." Odo takes a giant leap and knocks over the cardboard rocket.

PIRI: The rocket has launched.

TAVI: The rocket has fallen.

Nara shows the footage. The leap looks like a launch because the camera was tilted.

NARA: We can call it a moon jump.

ODO: I did not leave the classroom.

SELI: You left the floor.

Before the model, Piri leads a short gravity experiment. Each student places a hand on the desk and lifts it. Odo announces that the desk is floating.

TAVI: The desk is touching the floor.

ODO: The floor is holding it down.

SELI: That is not how gravity works.

PIRI: What would help us tell?

Tavi rolls a marble down a book ramp. It slows on a towel, then stops.

TAVI: The surface changes how it moves.

NARA: Can we film it from the side?

Piri repeats the roll. This time Odo places a pencil in its path; the marble stops sooner.

ODO: I have discovered an obstacle.

TAVI: You placed the obstacle.

Piri asks the class to design a better lunar model. Tavi puts pebbles into a tray for craters, while Seli makes a paper circle. Odo places the circle too close to the earth drawing.

TAVI: The moon is much farther away.

ODO: The string will not reach.

PIRI: The string is not the distance.

TAVI: Then the model needs space.

They push the desks apart to make room. The paper moon hangs from a string over the open floor. Odo takes a small hop beneath it.

PIRI: One small hop.

ODO: One classroom hop.

PIRI: And no space travel required.

TAVI: Yet.

### Tag

[THE CLASSROOM ROCKET IS REBUILT WITH A LARGER BASE.]

SELI: The moon is still far away.

TAVI: But the model is closer.

PIRI: Next week we visit the sun.

ODO: We cannot go to the sun.

PIRI: In our imagination.

TAVI: Then we need sunscreen in our imagination.

[SFX: CLASS LAUGHS, PAPER HELMETS RUSTLE.]
