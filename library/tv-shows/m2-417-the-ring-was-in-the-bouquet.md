# The Ring Was in the Bouquet

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised wedding specials, countdown montages, staged first looks, and producers who schedule the bouquet toss before the ceremony*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A wedding show schedules every romantic beat, but the ring box lands in the flower basket before the couple has even exchanged vows.

Cast: Host Piri, a gibbon; partner Luma, a macaque; partner Tavi, an orangutan; planner Neri, a capuchin; director Odo, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. GARDEN CEREMONY - AFTERNOON

Piri holds a bouquet. Neri looks at the schedule.

NERI: We are doing the bouquet toss before the ceremony.

LUMA: We are not married yet.

The ring box is tucked among the flowers. Tavi reaches for it.

ACT ONE

Odo calls for the couple's first look. Luma and Tavi stand at opposite ends of a path. They already saw each other during setup. Neri asks them to turn around, then walk together again for the cameras. They agree to one take, but do not pretend it is a surprise.

PIRI: Can I ask what you felt?

LUMA: I felt that we were waiting.

TAVI: I felt the flowers were hiding the rings.

Odo zooms in on the bouquet. The ring box slides down between the stems and disappears into the basket. Neri asks Piri to keep holding the flowers still. Piri lowers them carefully and notices the box.

PIRI: The rings are in the bouquet.

ACT TWO

Neri searches through the flower stems. Luma asks her not to pull apart the arrangement. Tavi holds the basket steady. The ring box is stuck behind a ribbon.

TAVI: It must have slipped from the table.

LUMA: It was beside the bouquet for the close-up.

Odo asks for a slow-motion replay. The monitor shows Neri carrying the basket and placing the ring box inside to clear the table. She freezes.

NERI: I moved it for the camera.

PIRI: The camera can move around the box.

Neri removes the box without damaging the flowers. Luma checks both rings; they are present. Odo asks the couple to repeat the bouquet shot. They decline, and Neri places the rings on the ceremony table instead.

ACT TWO-B

The producers set up the bouquet toss again, this time after the ceremony. Tavi asks if they can skip it. Luma says they want a group photo with everyone instead. Piri asks whether the show can film the photo. Luma says yes, if no one tosses anything near the pond.

Odo shifts the camera to frame the ceremony table. The floral arch blocks the view of the officiant, so Neri moves one branch aside. The ring box stays closed on the table. The couple checks the order: welcome, vows, rings, signing, photo. Piri marks each in the schedule.

PIRI: This is the actual order.

NERI: The bouquet toss is not on it.

Odo asks for a dramatic countdown to the vows. Luma looks at Tavi; he nods. They begin. Odo starts music too early, then stops it when the officiant speaks. The crew keeps the camera wide and captures the full ceremony without redoing the first-look scene.

ACT TWO-B - CEREMONY HOLD

Odo calls for a dramatic close-up. Piri pans toward the bouquet, and the producer's cue card tells the couple to reach for the rings. Luma keeps her hands at her sides.

LUMA: The officiant has not asked us yet.

TAVI: We will wait for the actual words.

Neri tries to move the bouquet away from the table. Its ribbon catches on the ring case. She stops, unties the ribbon, and sets the bouquet back in its basket. The case remains closed on the ceremony table.

NERI: I am not moving this again.

PIRI: The camera can find the couple without moving anything.

Odo shifts one step left and discovers the arch blocks the officiant. Neri trims no flowers; she simply turns the arch a few inches. The shot clears. The officiant checks that the couple is ready. Odo lowers his hand instead of giving a countdown. Luma and Tavi begin their vows in their own words. The show crew goes silent. When Tavi's sleeve brushes the bouquet, Piri steadies the basket with one hand and keeps the other off the ring case.

PIRI: Clear frame.

NERI: Clear table.

The officiant asks for the rings. Luma opens the case herself and hands one ring to Tavi. Neri looks at her empty hands, then sits down.

ACT THREE

Luma and Tavi exchange rings and sign the certificate. The officiant pronounces them married. Piri congratulates them. Neri carries the ring box away from the flowers and places it in the couple's keepsake case.

TAVI: We can do the group photo now.

LUMA: Everyone, stay away from the pond.

The guests gather for the photo. Odo counts down. Piri holds the bouquet low, clear of the camera. The couple stands beside their friends and family. No flowers or rings are tossed.

TAG

Neri checks the schedule. The bouquet toss slot remains blank. Odo asks if they need a closing shot. Luma and Tavi wave once, then leave together.

FINAL IMAGE: The bouquet rests in its basket beside the closed ring case while the guests remain in frame for the group photograph.
