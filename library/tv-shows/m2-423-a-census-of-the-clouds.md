# A Census of the Clouds

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's science television that turns observation into a countdown and a contest*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Pavi, a macaque; science teacher Nari, a lemur; child guest Pip, a capuchin; camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; weather reader Odo, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

EXT. SCHOOL ROOFTOP GARDEN - DAY

[Pavi points at the sky. A single thin cloud passes overhead.] 

PAVI: Can you count every cloud before this one leaves?

PIP: There are four.

NARI: There are two cloud patches.

ODO: The weather card says partly cloudy.

PAVI: We have a thrilling tie.

[Theme music, with a game-show buzzer.]

ACT ONE

PAVI: Welcome to Sky Count, the science show that makes looking up feel like a race.

NARI: The goal is to describe what you see, not to win.

TAVI: Camera ready.

PIP: I see a long cloud and a small one.

PAVI: Name them.

PIP: Cloud and little cloud.

PAVI: That is scientifically cautious.

[Odo reads the weather card.] 

ODO: There is a twenty percent chance of rain after lunch.

PAVI: Can you make the number bigger for television?

ODO: No.

NARI: We can explain what twenty percent means.

PAVI: Explain it with a countdown clock.

[Tavi sets a small clock to thirty seconds. Pip watches the clouds.] 

PIP: The small cloud is getting wider.

NARI: The air is moving it across the sky. The clouds aren't growing; we are seeing more of the same patch.

PAVI: That sounds like a twist.

ACT TWO

[The crew lays a transparent grid over the camera monitor. Pavi hands Pip a pointer.]

PAVI: Count the squares the cloud crosses.

NARI: We should not treat the edge of a cloud as a solid boundary.

PIP: It is changing shape.

ODO: Wind can stretch a cloud and break it apart.

PAVI: Can it break apart before the commercial?

[The cloud thins. Pavi turns the clock toward it, as if the countdown will speed it up.] 

TAVI: The lens makes it look smaller.

PIP: The cloud is still up there.

NARI: The roof is also moving in the frame because the camera is on a rolling cart.

[Tavi locks the cart wheel. The image steadies. Pavi asks for the "dramatic cloud close-up." Odo moves a white sheet into the frame as a prop. A breeze lifts it and drapes it over the camera.] 

ODO: The cloud has come indoors.

TAVI: That's the reflector.

[They take the sheet off. Pip points to a faint new cloud. Pavi resets the countdown clock.]

PAVI: New cloud. New round.

NARI: It is the same sky, not a new round.

PIP: There are three patches now.

NARI: Good observation. Say how you decided they were separate.

PIP: There's blue space between them.

[Odo's card flutters out of his hands. He catches it against a watering can, which tips and releases a small stream across the roof drain.]

ODO: The rain chance has arrived early.

NARI: That's the can.

ACT THREE

[The clouds move behind the school roof. The countdown clock reaches zero. Pavi raises the buzzer.]

PAVI: We have run out of sky.

PIP: The sky is still there. The cloud went behind the building.

NARI: Exactly. A hidden cloud is not a vanished cloud.

[Tavi pans the camera to the other side of the roof. The cloud reappears, stretched into a thin band. Pavi tries to declare Pip the winner.] 

PAVI: The contestant who spotted it wins the cloud census.

NARI: A census records what is present. It does not crown a champion.

ODO: The weather card has one more update.

PAVI: Is it the winner?

ODO: The wind is moving northeast. The chance of rain is still twenty percent.

[Pavi lowers the buzzer. Pip hands him the pointer.] 

PIP: You can count the patches.

[Pavi looks up. One cloud patch has split into two smaller wisps.] 

PAVI: I count three.

NARI: I count two connected bands.

PAVI: Can we both be right?

NARI: We can explain our methods.

[They do. Tavi frames both of them pointing in opposite directions.]


[Nari draws two outlines on the clear grid and marks the time. Pip looks at the note rather than the countdown clock.]

PIP: If we check again in five minutes, we can see which way the wind moved it.

NARI: Yes. We compare the same patch over time.

PAVI: And if it disappears?

NARI: It may have thinned until it is hard to see, or moved behind something. We record what we can observe.

[Tavi locks the grid to the monitor. Odo holds the weather card down with a clothespin. The sheet flutters, but this time it does not cover the lens.]

ODO: The card is staying on its own mark.

PAVI: A dramatic recovery.

NARI: A clothespin.

TAG

PAVI: Join us next time, when we count raindrops.

[Odo looks at the weather card.] 

ODO: We need rain first.

[A single drop falls from the tipped watering can onto Pavi's cue card.]

PAVI: The forecast has begun.
