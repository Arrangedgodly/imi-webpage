# The Raincoat Runway Has an Umbrella

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fashion competition shows that reward dramatic presentation even when the clothes must work in real weather*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A rainwear runway competition prizes a dramatic entrance, but the practical coats keep outperforming the showy designs when the sprinklers turn on.

Cast: Host Dela, a macaque; judge Venn, a gibbon; designer Omi, a capuchin; model Suri, a lemur; and stagehand Ralo, a baboon.

Episode: "Walk Through the Weather"

COLD OPEN

INT. SHOWROOM - DAY

Suri walks out wearing a bright yellow raincoat with a hood. Omi follows with a silver cape. Dela claps.

DELA: The theme is rain, but make it unforgettable.

VENN: Does the coat keep water off the wearer?

OMI: The cape catches the stage lights.

[SURI OPENS A SMALL UMBRELLA. IT FITS UNDER THE HOOD.]

VENN: That works.

DELA: We are judging fashion.

ACT ONE

Ralo marks a runway across the floor. Venn checks the garment cards. Omi pins reflective strips to the cape.

OMI: The silver fabric catches every light.

SURI: It also catches on the door handle.

[THE CAPE SNAGS ON THE HANDLE. SURI TURNS, AND THE GARMENT PULLS THE DOOR OPEN.]

DELA: An unexpected exit.

VENN: The coat has to move through a doorway.

OMI: I can shorten the hem.

SURI: The hood keeps rain out of my face.

DELA: The audience needs to see your expression.

[SURI PULLS THE HOOD BACK. A SPRINKLER ABOVE THE SET DRIPS ON HER HEAD.]

SURI: The expression is wet.

ACT TWO

INT. SHOWROOM - LATER

The models line up. Ralo tests the sprinkler controls. Dela holds a card reading "SURPRISE SHOWER." 

VENN: We need a functional test before judging.

DELA: The shower is the dramatic part.

OMI: My cape is water resistant.

SURI: The lining is not.

[THE SPRINKLER TURNS ON. WATER HITS THE CAPE. IT BECOMES HEAVY AND SLIDES OFF ONE SHOULDER.]

OMI: Stop the water.

RALO: The switch is behind the runway curtain.

[HE RUNS TO THE SWITCH. THE CURTAIN GETS SOAKED AND DROOPS OVER THE SWITCH.]

VENN: The yellow coat is still dry underneath.

DELA: It is a very ordinary coat.

SURI: It has pockets.

[She pulls a small towel from one pocket and dries the bench.]

INT. SHOWROOM - FITTING CORNER

Venn asks Suri to try the coat over a thick sweater. Ralo sets a small fan on the floor.

VENN: A raincoat has to fit over the clothes someone is already wearing.

OMI: The cape is intended for a dramatic layer.

SURI: I can zip the coat with my scarf underneath.

[SURI ZIPS THE COAT. SHE RAISES HER ARMS AND REACHES FOR A DOOR HANDLE.]

SURI: I can move.

OMI: My cape needs both hands to hold it open.

DELA: That is a presentation choice.

VENN: It is also a problem when carrying an umbrella.

[THE FAN BLOWS. OMI'S CAPE LIFTS AND WRAPS AROUND THE DISPLAY RACK AGAIN.]

OMI: I need another person to hold the hem.

SURI: I have both hands free.

DELA: We can make that a feature.

ACT TWO-B

INT. SHOWROOM - PRE-JUDGING

Venn asks each model to carry a folded umbrella and walk through a narrow doorway. Omi adjusts the cape. Suri opens and closes her coat zipper.

VENN: A rain garment should fit over layers and let you move.

DELA: The show needs a signature pose.

VENN: Put the umbrella in the coat pocket.

SURI: It fits.

OMI: That looks too practical for the runway.

DELA: The camera can follow the pocket.

VENN: The umbrella stays dry.

OMI: The coat is still comfortable.

OMI: I can spin the cape.

SURI: There is a puddle where you would land.

[RALO PUTS A NONSLIP MAT OVER THE PUDDLE. THE MAT SLIDES.]

RALO: We need a heavier mat.

VENN: Or less water.

DELA: The water is our runway effect.

OMI: My cape is getting heavier.

[OMI TRIES TO SPIN. THE CAPE WRAPS AROUND A DISPLAY RACK.]

SURI: I'll hold the rack.

OMI: That is not a runway pose.

SURI: It is preventing a fall.

ACT THREE

INT. SHOWROOM - FINAL WALK

Suri walks the runway in the yellow coat. Omi carries the silver cape folded over one arm. The sprinkler drips lightly.

DELA: The judges have chosen the look that made the biggest impression.

VENN: The yellow coat passed the water test, the door test, and the pocket test.

OMI: The cape made an impression on the rack.

[THE JUDGES PRESENT A RIBBON TO SURI. She hangs it from the coat's loop.]

SURI: It can dry here.

TAG

Dela steps onto the runway without an umbrella. The sprinkler releases one last drop directly onto her head.

DELA: Is that part of the show?

RALO: It is part of the weather.
