# The Orchestra Has a Substitute Conductor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: classical music specials that turn a last-minute replacement into a televised crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Sela, a gibbon; conductor stand-in and page-turner Pip, a capuchin; concertmaster Nari, a lemur; host stand-in Vex, an orangutan; stage technician Tavi, a macaque; delayed conductor Odo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. CONCERT HALL - EVENING

[Pip stands at the podium. He is much shorter than the conductor's coat hanging behind him. A baton rests on the stand.] 

SELA: Our guest conductor is delayed.

NARI: Pip knows the score.

VEX: He is holding the baton upside down.

[Pip flips it. The orchestra begins to tune.]

ACT ONE

SELA: Welcome to Maestro Tonight. Our conductor is delayed, but the music must begin.

PIP: I can start the overture.

NARI: You have the score?

PIP: I have the first page.

[The page has a large title and no notes. Tavi hands him the full score. The pages cascade onto the floor.] 

PIP: It is a long overture.

VEX: We should introduce the stand-in with a profile package.

SELA: The guest conductor is not here.

[Vex puts on the conductor's coat. It drags along the floor. He steps on the hem and nearly falls.] 

VEX: The coat is a little large.

NARI: So is the podium.

[The camera zooms on Pip's baton. He holds it upright. The orchestra stops tuning and watches him.] 

PIP: Do I raise it?

NARI: You can give the downbeat when you are ready.

[He raises it. One violin enters early. Tavi signals them to wait.] 

ACT TWO

[A phone buzzes over distant train brakes and a station announcement. Odo, the delayed conductor, calls from his seat on the stopped train to say it is still delayed. Sela asks Pip to lead a rehearsal while the crew gets a shot of the empty doorway.] 

PIP: I can do the second piece too.

NARI: It has a page turn.

PIP: I can turn a page.

[He does, but the page sticks to his fingers. The musicians wait. Pip shakes his hand; the sheet flies onto Vex's coat.] 

VEX: The score has chosen me.

TAVI: It's stuck to the sleeve.

[Vex tries to conduct with one hand while holding the paper away from the coat. The orchestra follows his gestures. The tempo changes with each attempt to free the page.] 

SELA: We have a rehearsal.

NARI: We have a coat trying to conduct.

[Pip watches the musicians, then taps the stand twice. The orchestra follows his rhythm. The page drops from Vex's sleeve. The group ends together.] 

PIP: That was the rehearsal ending.

TAVI: That was the tuning chord, not the second piece.

[The musicians applaud. Vex bows, then steps out of the coat.] 

VEX: I have discovered my calling.

NARI: Your calling is to return the coat.

[Vex puts the oversized conductor coat back on for a publicity photo. The sleeves cover his hands, so he cannot hold the baton.]

VEX: I look like a conductor.

NARI: You look like a coat rack.

[Tavi pins the sleeves back with two clothespins. The camera flashes. Vex raises one arm; the coat hem lifts and reveals his shoes. ]

SELA: Can we get the shoes out of the shot?

PIP: They keep the tempo.

[Vex removes the oversized coat, folds it over a chair, and returns to the wings in his own coat.]

[The camera operator checks the frame. Pip points to the hallway, where the delayed conductor's shoes appear beneath the door.]

NARI: He is here.

VEX: We have the arrival shot.

SELA: Hold the frame until he enters.

[The shoes move out of view while the crew resets the lights.]

ACT THREE

[Odo arrives, breathless. He asks whether the orchestra is ready. Sela tells him that Pip claims to have led them through the second piece.]

ODO: Which piece?

PIP: We only got as far as the tuning chord. Sela is promoting it as the second piece.

[Odo places the first page of the score on the podium. Pip gives him the baton.] 

NARI: Are you still conducting?

PIP: I am turning pages.

[The performance begins. Pip stands beside the music stand and leans forward at the correct moment. His tail hooks the next page and turns it too early.] 

PIP: Sorry.

[The musicians pause, then repeat the phrase from the beginning. The conductor adjusts the tempo to match them. Vex watches from the wings, wearing his own coat.] 

SELA: The broadcast is back on schedule.

TAVI: The train missed its cue.

[The final chord lands. Pip turns the last page, revealing a note: "Encore if needed." The audience applauds.] 

PIP: We needed it.

[The crew clips the final score to the music stand. The sheet catches the baton and turns one page back.]

NARI: We have a repeat.

PIP: I can turn it forward.

[He frees the page without tearing it. Odo checks the score and nods.]

TAG

[Vex approaches the podium carrying the oversized conductor coat. Its sleeve catches on the baton.] 

NARI: The coat has been appointed assistant conductor.

PIP: It will lead the hem.
