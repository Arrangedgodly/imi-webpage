# The Laugh Track Got There First

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sitcoms, canned audience reactions, repeated door jokes, and actors waiting for laughter that has not happened*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A sitcom's laugh track fires before each joke, forcing its actors to change a scene about a stuck door in real time.

Cast: Star Pavo, a gibbon; co-star Leni, a macaque; neighbor Roki, a capuchin; director Seli, a baboon; sound editor Nema, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. SITCOM APARTMENT SET - NIGHT

Leni pulls the front door. It sticks. The audience laughs before she says anything.

LENI: I haven't made a joke.

PAVO: The door has excellent timing.

SELI: The laugh track is on the wrong cue.

ACT ONE

Seli calls for a reset. Nema moves the laughter cue to a button. Pavo opens the scene by entering through the front door. The door sticks again. The audience laughs on cue, then the recorded laughter continues after Pavo has crossed the set.

PAVO: I have reached the kitchen.

NEMA: The laugh is still playing.

ROKI: The audience thinks you are still opening the door.

Seli asks the actors to hold for laughter. Leni holds. Pavo holds. Roki continues reading the newspaper. The laughter cue ends just as Roki delivers his line.

ROKI: I brought the groceries.

ACT TWO

Nema plays the laugh track from the beginning. It laughs over the quiet line and stops before the punchline. Seli decides to keep the sound as a "live audience reaction." Leni proposes a new scene: her character is trying to open the door while Pavo narrates the attempts.

LENI: First, I push.

The laugh track plays. She pushes. The door does not move.

PAVO: That was a push.

The laugh track plays again. Roki looks at the door hinge and suggests lifting slightly. Leni lifts the door and it opens. The track is silent.

ROKI: That worked.

SELI: We need a laugh there.

NEMA: I can add one.

PAVO: Please do not add laughter to a working door.

Seli asks the actors to pause at the door. Nema plays a small chuckle when the handle turns. Leni freezes with one hand on the knob. Pavo looks toward the speakers. Roki checks his grocery list.

SELI: We need a reaction from all three.

ROKI: I am buying onions.

NEMA: I can move the cue after the line.

PAVO: The line is not funny yet.

Nema places the laugh cue after the next line. Pavo tries: "The door and I have an understanding." The track bursts in before he finishes. He waits; the laughter runs on for several seconds. Leni opens the door silently while the recording continues. Roki walks through, still holding the grocery list.

LENI: The recording thinks we are all laughing.

ROKI: I am still buying onions.

Seli calls reset. Nema changes the cue to manual. Pavo delivers the line again, then waits. The audience chuckles at the awkward pause, but Nema does not press the button.

PAVO: That was better.

NEMA: That was real.

ACT TWO-B

The crew attempts one more take. Roki enters holding grocery bags. The door opens easily. Seli calls cut because the scene no longer has a problem. Leni tries to close the door; it sticks again. Pavo leans against it to help. The laugh track starts early, pauses, then plays over the actors' silence.

LENI: The sound is telling us when to react.

PAVO: The door is telling us when to push.

ROKI: We could fix the hinge.

SELI: Then there is no episode.

Nema reaches beneath the console and disconnects the laugh cue. Seli asks for a dry run without it. Pavo pushes the door; it sticks. Leni lifts the handle. The door opens. Roki enters with the bags.

ROKI: I brought the groceries.

LENI: We have a door.

PAVO: We have a scene.

The studio audience laughs naturally. Nema checks the console. The track is disconnected. Seli tells the actors to continue.

ACT THREE

They play the scene once with the working door. Leni lifts the handle, Pavo holds it open, and Roki steps inside with the bags. No cue interrupts them. Seli lets the audience response finish before calling cut. Nema marks the laugh track control as "manual only."

SELI: We have the take.

NEMA: Without the track.

PAVO: The audience found the joke.

SELI: The door found the timing.

Roki sets the groceries on the table. One paper bag has a small tear. Leni catches an orange as it rolls toward the door. The audience laughs. Nema does not press anything.

TAG

The crew resets. Pavo opens the door, and it sticks once more. He lifts the handle without looking at camera. The audience laughs before the laugh track can play.

FINAL IMAGE: Seli closes the laugh-track panel while the actors keep the door propped open with the grocery bag.
