# The Midnight Bean Counter

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: prime-time workplace drama convention, where a routine inventory check gets scored like a crime investigation*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: An audit team investigates one missing bean, while a camera crew keeps trying to turn a seed library's inventory discrepancy into a high-stakes scandal.

Cast: Miri, a macaque night manager; Tavi, a squirrel monkey cataloger; Odo, a baboon auditor; Piri, a capuchin camera operator; and the morning worker, a langur.

Episode: "The Missing Bean"

COLD OPEN

INT. SEED LIBRARY, NIGHT

Miri locks a cabinet. Tavi checks the ledger.

TAVI: The count is exact.

A bean slips from a paper scoop, rolls across the desk and disappears beneath it.

MIRI: There goes the exact count.

Piri zooms in on the empty scoop. Odo enters with a clipboard and a desk lamp.

ODO: Seal the room.

TAVI: It is under the desk.

ODO: Seal the room around the desk.

[SFX: OMINOUS THEME, CUT OFF BY PIRI.] 

ACT ONE

INT. SEED LIBRARY, LATER

Odo tapes a line on the floor around the desk. No one may cross it until the inventory record is reconciled. Piri asks Miri to look worried. She points out the staff cannot reach the seed shelves from the other side of the tape.

MIRI: I am worried about the tape.

Tavi reads the ledger aloud. The cabinet holds two hundred and twelve beans. The sample bowl holds six. The fallen bean came from a scoop used to fill the bowl. Odo writes "one bean at large" on a board.

PIRI: Can we say missing?

ODO: It has not left the room.

Piri turns the board toward camera. The tape line makes the desk look like a crime scene. Odo lifts the desk with one hand. Tavi checks beneath it, but the bean has rolled into a felt caster sleeve. He cannot see it.

TAVI: Nothing here.

Odo lowers the desk. The caster squeaks. The bean rolls deeper into the sleeve.

ODO: The suspect is mobile.

ACT TWO

INT. SEED LIBRARY, MIDNIGHT

The audit team reconstructs the bean's route. Tavi repeats the scoop motion with an empty spoon. Piri films in slow motion. Odo asks if the spoon was calibrated. Miri brings a measuring scoop. Its handle is bent, so she marks the line where a full scoop should stop.

The crew replays the footage. The bean left the scoop at the edge of the desk, not the cabinet. Odo says the desk is now part of the inventory system. Miri says the desk is furniture. Piri captions it "PERSON OF INTEREST."

Odo proposes lifting the desk again. Tavi says the bean moved when they did that. Miri suggests sliding a shallow tray beneath the caster, then raising only one side. She places the tray under the desk and wedges a folded cloth beneath the leg. Odo checks that it is stable before they lift.

The caster squeaks. Nothing falls. Tavi gently taps the felt sleeve. A bean rolls down the desk leg and lands in the tray.

TAVI: Recovered.

Piri films the bean from three angles. Odo checks its size and color against the stock. It matches the inventory. Miri reaches for the tape line. Odo stops her until the count is signed. Piri films her hand hovering over the tape.

ACT TWO-B

INT. SEED LIBRARY, LATER

Odo asks Tavi to repeat the scoop motion without beans. The bent handle catches on the cabinet lip. Piri rushes to frame the spoon's shadow across Miri's face.

PIRI: Can you look as though you knew about the handle?

MIRI: I am looking directly at it.

PIRI: That is very incriminating.

Odo moves the recovered bean beside the ledger. Piri moves the desk lamp until the bean casts a shadow larger than the auditor's clipboard. Tavi switches on the ceiling lights. The shadow disappears.

TAVI: One bean. Ordinary size.

PIRI: We were getting somewhere.

ODO: We got under the desk.

Piri opens his editing screen. He has cut the bean's fall into three separate angles. Odo counts the falls, then looks at the tray.

ODO: Your film has three missing beans.

PIRI: It is the same bean.

ODO: Put that in the caption.

Piri writes "THREE ANGLES, ONE SUSPECT." Miri adds "RECOVERED" across the bottom. The new caption covers the ominous close-up completely.

ACT THREE

INT. SEED LIBRARY, PRE-DAWN

Tavi puts the recovered bean in the sample bowl. Odo counts six samples and checks the cabinet's two hundred and twelve. He marks the spill log recovered and signs the inventory once.

Piri lifts the lamp again. Miri pulls away the floor tape before he can light it.

PIRI: That was our crime-scene perimeter.

MIRI: It is the route to the shelves.

Tavi fits a cloth guard around the caster. Piri aims the camera at it as if it were a newly fortified vault. Odo holds the signed count in front of the lens.

ODO: There is no missing bean.

PIRI: Could we end with a question?

MIRI: Why are you still filming?

Piri leaves that question on the screen while the night crew hangs up its coats and departs.

TAG

INT. SEED LIBRARY, MORNING

The morning worker finds Piri asleep beside the camera. Its screen reads "MISSING: A GOOD NIGHT'S SLEEP." She taps his shoulder.

WORKER: Are you part of the stock?

Piri wakes, takes the camera and backs toward the door. The worker begins counting the sample bowl. He raises the lens again.

WORKER: Six.

Piri lowers it. She shuts the door before he can ask for a seventh.
