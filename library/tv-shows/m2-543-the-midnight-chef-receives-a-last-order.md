# The Midnight Chef Receives a Last Order

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: high-pressure cooking competitions that reveal a final order after the kitchen has closed*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: CHEF LUMA, an orangutan; CHEF NIKO, a capuchin; HOST, a gibbon; JUDGE, a baboon; FLOOR MANAGER, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - NIGHT

A countdown clock reads 00:10. Two chefs plate a single dumpling each. The host leans toward the camera.

HOST: Judges, one final order.

JUDGE: A midnight snack.

FLOOR MANAGER: The kitchen closed an hour ago.

The host rings a service bell. Both chefs turn toward the pantry.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - NIGHT

Luma and Niko stand at separate stations. Each has prepared a dish for the contest's "last plate" challenge. The rules said they would cook for the judges only. The judge reveals a handwritten order from the show's sponsor: one bowl of soup, no onions, served in a cup.

LUMA: We prepared plates.

NIKO: I have soup.

JUDGE: In a bowl.

HOST: A cup is just a bowl with ambition.

The floor manager checks the pantry. There are no clean cups. There is a stack of measuring beakers, all marked NOT FOR SERVICE. Niko reaches for one. The manager blocks him.

FLOOR MANAGER: That is laboratory glass.

HOST: Tonight's mystery ingredient is restraint.

Kex, the producer, whispers through the host's earpiece that the sponsor's order must be served before the clock reaches zero. The clock resets to ten minutes. Luma begins ladling soup into a chilled metal bowl. Niko makes a small dumpling and places it on the rim.

JUDGE: You were told no garnish.

NIKO: It is a lid.

The dumpling slides into the soup. Niko tries to retrieve it with chopsticks, but the bowl is too deep. Luma offers a long spoon. The judge says that counts as a tool, not a garnish.

ACT TWO

INT. PANTRY - NIGHT

The floor manager finds one clean ceramic cup on a high shelf, behind a stack of ingredient boxes. Luma climbs a stable step ladder and reaches for it. Niko holds the ladder and reads the sponsor's order aloud: "A cup of soup, no onions, no garnish, no spoon."

LUMA: How will the judge eat it?

JUDGE: I will drink it.

NIKO: Then the cup needs a handle.

The cup has no handle. The host asks the camera to zoom in on the crisis. The cup is cracked, so the manager rejects it. Niko scans the clean equipment rack and finds a heatproof bowl with a pouring lip. Luma checks it for cracks. The floor manager approves it as service ware.

HOST: This is not a cup.

FLOOR MANAGER: It is safe and clean.

JUDGE: I can drink from it.

Luma pours the soup through the lip into the judge's waiting cup-shaped hands. The judge tastes it. It is warm, onion-free, and plain. The clock pauses at 00:01 for a dramatic close-up. Niko's dumpling remains in the original bowl.

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - LATER

The floor manager calls time, but the sponsor sends another note through the control room: the soup must be served without a spoon, straw, lid, or bowl. The judge reads the note twice. Luma asks if a plate is acceptable. The sponsor's reply says "liquid service vessel." Niko points to the measuring beaker again.

NIKO: It is still not for service.

The manager searches the clean rack and finds a small sauce pitcher. The judge tests its lip with water, then approves it. Luma pours a sample portion. The pitcher drips onto the counter. Niko folds a clean towel beneath its spout, but the sponsor's note forbids garnish, not towels. The host asks the camera to hold on the dramatic drip.

HOST: The vessel is leaking.

FLOOR MANAGER: The pitcher is not cracked. The spout is uneven.

Luma turns the pitcher so the smooth side faces the judge. The pour becomes steady. The judge tastes the soup, then checks that it is onion-free. Niko places his dumpling on a separate plate and asks if the sponsor may have it after judging. The floor manager says the dish is not part of the order, so the dumpling can be served to the crew.

JUDGE: One order, one vessel, one correct portion.

NIKO: And one dumpling for the night shift.

The host tries to ring the service bell again. The floor manager takes it away until the competition ends.



ACT THREE

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - NIGHT

The judge awards the challenge to Luma for following the actual order. Niko receives a special mention for the dumpling that survived the soup. The floor manager turns off the burners and locks the pantry.

HOST: Chefs, one last order.

FLOOR MANAGER: No.

The host looks at the service bell and sets it on the counter. It rings as it settles.

FINAL IMAGE: The judges leave with the soup bowl while the show crew eats the dumpling after the kitchen is officially closed.
