# The Police Procedural Chases a Missing Spotlight

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedural evidence boards, urgent interviews, and a detective whose suspect is a lighting instrument*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Detective Suri, a macaque; sergeant Odo, an orangutan; witness Pip, a squirrel monkey; lighting technician Vex, a baboon; producer Nari, a gibbon; camera operator (nonspeaking).

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO PRECINCT SET - NIGHT

A spotlight shines on an empty chair. Detective Suri enters.

SURI: Someone has stolen the spotlight.

The beam follows her.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The procedural show is filming a case about a missing spotlight. Producer Nari wants a mystery that can stretch through three act breaks. The light is not missing; its wheeled stand has been moved by the crew to illuminate a temporary interview chair. Detective Suri has been handed a crime scene diagram that shows the chair in three places.

SURI: Which chair was here when the light moved?

ODO: The chair with the evidence tent.

PIP: I was sitting there.

NARI: Please give us the witness reaction again, but look more mysterious.

PIP: I was eating a pear.

Suri pins a photo of the spotlight to the evidence board. It has a cable trailing behind it, ending just beyond the frame. Odo asks Vex when he last saw the light. Vex says he parked it near the stage door before lunch, then went to move a scenery flat.

VEX: The spotlight had wheels.

SURI: Did you push it or did it roll?

VEX: I pushed it until I heard a bell. Then I stopped.

The show's bell is actually the cue bell from the set. Nari asks Suri to question Vex again after the commercial break. Suri refuses to pretend the clue has changed.

ACT TWO

Odo follows the cable from the spotlight to a power strip. The strip is unplugged, and the light is off. Nobody has stolen it; the crew moved it into the interview area and disconnected it to stop it from shining into the camera lens. Nari wants the detective to accuse the stage manager anyway, because the act break is approaching.

NARI: We need a suspect.

SURI: We have a location and a reason.

PIP: Can the suspect be the chair? It keeps moving between takes.

ODO: The chair is on a rolling platform.

SURI: Then the chair is a witness to the movement.

Nari asks for an evidence close-up. Vex wheels the dark spotlight two feet. Pip follows it with a handheld work lamp; that beam sweeps across the board and illuminates the spotlight photo. The camera zooms into the picture while Vex watches the lamp.

VEX: It looks like the light is following its own case.

SURI: Pip is holding the work lamp. The spotlight is still unplugged.

Suri checks the cable route. The plug is safely stored in a labeled hook on the wall. Vex had put it there after moving the light. He remembers the bell: he heard Nari call for the scene, so he stopped moving the equipment and disconnected the unit. The bell did not signal a theft; it called the next take.

PIP: I heard the bell too. I put my pear down and moved off the chair.

NARI: That gives us a witness with a timeline.

PIP: I can also say the pear was good.

SURI: The pear is not in evidence.

Vex identifies the wheeled base as a borrowed prop from the lighting department. Odo checks the sign-out board. It records the spotlight's move to the interview zone; its return field is still blank. Nari asks if they can keep investigating. Suri asks what remains unknown.

SURI: We know who moved it, where it went, why it was unplugged, and where the cable is.

NARI: We could discover a second missing object.

ODO: The evidence board itself is missing a pin.

ACT THREE

Suri returns the spotlight to the taped equipment bay with Vex's help. Vex checks the wheels, locks them, and hooks the cord safely. Odo confirms the unit is plugged in only after it is positioned. Suri points the beam at the interview chair for a lighting test, then turns it off when the test is complete.

SURI: Case closed. The spotlight was moved by its own department.

NARI: Can we call it a suspect?

SURI: It did not move itself.

PIP: The chair rolled.

ODO: The chair was pushed by the crew.

PIP: It is an accessory to the case.

The show ends with an evidence recap. Nari tells the camera the case was solved by checking the sign-out board. She does not ask Pip to repeat the pear testimony. Vex disconnects the spotlight after its test, signs the spotlight back into the equipment bay and leaves the unplugged cord hooked neatly beside it.

FINAL IMAGE: The spotlight is dark in its marked bay while the empty chair waits under a modest desk lamp.
