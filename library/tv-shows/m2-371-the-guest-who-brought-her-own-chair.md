# The Guest Who Brought Her Own Chair

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows, obligatory anecdotes, sponsor bits, and the house band cueing every reaction*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A late-night host interviews a practical librarian who brought her own folding chair and refuses to pretend it is a luxury product.

Cast: Host Koro, a gibbon; guest Ena, an orangutan; bandleader Vimi, a macaque; announcer Pex, a capuchin; stage manager Lulo, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. LATE-NIGHT SET - NIGHT

Pex announces the guest. The band plays a triumphant fanfare as Ena walks out carrying a folded chair. Koro looks at the chair.

KORO: We provided a seat.

ENA: This one has a handle.

VIMI: Should I play the chair entrance sting?

LULO: You just did.

ACT ONE

Koro introduces Ena as the head librarian of a neighborhood reading room. The audience applauds. Vimi plays the same fanfare again, longer. Ena unfolds her chair and sits beside the guest chair.

KORO: Tell us about your new book program.

ENA: We let people take books home and bring them back.

KORO: That is the whole program?

ENA: It works.

Koro glances toward Lulo, who holds a card reading "BIG ANNOUNCEMENT." Ena explains that the library's reading room is adding a low shelf for children. Koro asks for a more surprising reveal. Ena shows a photo of the shelf. The audience applauds politely; Vimi plays a drum roll that continues after the photo disappears.

ACT TWO

Pex announces a sponsor segment. A rolling display arrives with a velvet cushion and a gold price tag. Koro says it is the "ultimate reading chair." Ena asks whether it folds. The sponsor representative, off camera, says it reclines, vibrates and has four beverage pockets.

ENA: Can I carry it up a flight of stairs?

KORO: It has wheels.

ENA: The stairs do not.

Vimi plays a dramatic sting. The display chair's automatic footrest opens and blocks the path to the bandstand. Lulo tries to push it. The footrest closes on his prop clipboard. He frees the clipboard and rolls the chair backward.

KORO: A bold new guest has joined the show.

ENA: It is in the way.

Koro asks Ena to try it. She sits, presses one button, and the chair vibrates. Her folded chair rattles sympathetically beside it. The audience laughs. Vimi hits a cymbal, startling the sponsor representative, who reaches to unplug the display. The cord is too short, so the chair stops at an angle.

ACT TWO-B

Koro returns to the interview. He asks Ena how she keeps children interested in reading. Ena says she lets them choose a book, find a comfortable place and read at their own pace. Koro asks for a tip that fits in a sound bite.

ENA: Sit down. Open the book.

KORO: Could you say that while holding this chair?

ENA: I brought mine.

She unfolds the chair. The movement is simple, quick and silent. Vimi looks at his cue cards, then plays a quiet plucked chord.

KORO: That might be the first useful product demonstration we've had.

Lulo tries to move the sponsored chair out. It will not fit behind the curtain while the footrest is extended. Ena points to the release latch. Lulo folds it, rolls the chair through the curtain and disappears from sight. The audience applauds. Vimi begins a full outro before Koro has finished speaking.

KORO: We are still on the interview.

VIMI: I thought the chair exit was the end.

KORO: It was a chair leaving. It did not have a contract.

Lulo wheels the sponsor chair back in for a comfort comparison. Its footrest opens across the camera lane. Pex announces the next segment from behind it. Koro waves to the lens, but the tall headrest blocks his face.

KORO: I have been replaced by furniture.

ENA: The furniture has a beverage pocket.

KORO: I have pockets.

ENA: They do not recline.

Vimi plays a short sting. The massage setting starts, shaking a stack of cue cards onto the floor. Lulo scrambles to collect them while Ena watches the cards slide beneath the footrest.

LULO: None of those cards are in order.

KORO: Neither are the chairs.

ACT THREE

Koro asks Ena to invite viewers to the library. She names its address and says visitors can borrow books or sit quietly. The sponsor display is gone. Koro points to her folding chair.

KORO: Would you leave it here?

ENA: No. I came with it.

She folds it, hooks it over one shoulder and walks toward the exit. Koro asks if she can leave a book for the host. Ena hands him a book about birds. Koro opens it, and Vimi plays the cue for a joke.

KORO: Is this the part where I say something funny?

ENA: You can just read it.

Koro looks at the first page. He sits in the empty guest chair and begins reading. Vimi lowers his sticks. The audience quiets.

TAG

Pex announces the next guest while Koro is still reading. The band starts a fanfare. Lulo gently raises a card that says "LET HIM FINISH." Vimi stops playing.

FINAL IMAGE: Koro turns one page in silence as Ena's folding chair swings from her shoulder on the way out.
