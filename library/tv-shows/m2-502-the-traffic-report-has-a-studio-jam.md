# The Traffic Report Has a Studio Jam

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live traffic broadcasts that dramatize ordinary slowdowns while their own map and studio cues fail*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: traffic host Nala, a macaque; reporter Oru, an orangutan; cartographer Pavi, a lemur; producer Seli, a capuchin; driver caller Miko, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. TRAFFIC STUDIO - MORNING

A map shows one orange line on a quiet road. Nala stares at the camera.

NALA: A major delay is developing.

PAVI: That is the line for the parade route.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Seli says the map layers are mislabeled. Pavi tries to clear the parade line. It disappears, then reappears across the studio floor graphic. Nala asks for the live camera. Oru reports from the road beside a parked van. No cars are moving behind him.

ORU: Traffic is light.

NALA: Are you certain?

A bicycle passes, towing a small cart. The camera follows it. The map labels the bicycle as a bus. Seli asks Pavi to correct the icon. Pavi clicks; the bus becomes a construction crane.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Miko calls from the road to say the parade is already over. The map shows a red jam at the studio entrance. Seli asks whether a crew truck is blocking the street. Oru turns around and sees the station's satellite van stopped at the curb, with its hazard lights on. The driver is waiting for the live shot to finish.

NALA: We have found the source of congestion.

ORU: It is our van.

Pavi tries to move the red line off the map. It sticks to the studio icon. He drags the studio toward the highway. The animated station building slides across the map, dragging the red line behind it. Nala continues the report while the studio logo moves past her shoulder.

[The camera widens. A stagehand wheels a model bus across the set to reset the road map. The animation tracks the toy and labels it a commuter service.]

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Oru walks to the van and asks the driver to pull into the loading bay. The driver says the bay is occupied by the crew's camera cart. Seli calls the floor team. Two people move the cart; its wheel is locked by a cable. Pavi traces the cable to the graphics console. The live map cable has been laid across the doorway and caught the wheel.

Nala asks the audience to stay patient. The map shows a 40-minute delay. Miko says the road is clear and he has arrived at work. The map shows his car still on the highway. Seli removes the route marker and announces Miko's location correctly by voice.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Pavi tries a static map on paper. He draws the open road and marks the loading bay with a small X. Nala asks whether the map can show the current route without a moving line. Pavi holds it beside the camera. The view is clear until the stagehand's model bus rolls down the ramp and covers the X. Oru lifts the bus and places it on the correct road. The map remains still while the report continues. The model bus stays on the table. Nala gives the final update without a countdown.

ACT THREE

INT. TRAFFIC STUDIO - LATE MORNING

The camera cart is moved to its mark. The van enters the loading bay. Pavi resets the map using a fresh road layer. The orange parade route disappears. Nala asks Oru for an update. He reports no delay on the road, one short wait at the gate, and a clear route into the city.

The graphic tries to show a green line. It draws a route through the studio wall and ends at Nala's chair. Pavi shuts off the animation. Nala gives the report without it. Miko calls again, now from his desk. He asks whether the broadcast can mention that the bike lane is clear. Oru confirms it. Seli adds the lane to a static card.

The map is now still and accurate. Nala says the commute is moving normally. Pavi waits for the studio icon to stay in place. It does. The camera pulls back enough to show the model bus resting beside the map, its route card reading STATION LOADING BAY.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE REPORT

Seli parks the camera cart behind a tape line. Oru returns from the van. Pavi labels the new map layer CURRENT ROADS. Nala points at the loading bay and calls it clear.

FINAL IMAGE: The studio map shows an empty road, while the real camera cart waits behind the tape instead of blocking the door.
