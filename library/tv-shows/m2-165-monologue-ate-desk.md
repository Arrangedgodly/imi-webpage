# The Monologue That Ate the Desk

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows that expand a throwaway host bit until the guest segment disappears*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Host Kalo rehearses a short joke about a wobbly desk before a guest arrives to demonstrate a community-built telescope. The bandleader and producer keep adding music, props, and audience participation until the desk bit threatens to consume the whole show.

Cast: Kalo, a macaque host; Venn, a capuchin producer; Sita, a mandrill bandleader; and Dr. Ral, a langur astronomer and guest.

## Episode: "One More Desk Joke"

### Cold Open

[LATE-NIGHT SET. KALO LEANS ON THE DESK. IT WOBBLES. HE CATCHES A PEN BEFORE IT ROLLS OFF.] 

KALO: This desk has stage fright.

[SITA PLAYS A THREE-NOTE STING. VENN RUNS OUT WITH A WRENCH.] 

VENN: We can make it a recurring bit.

KALO: Fix the leg first.

[THE CAMERA CUTS TO THE AUDIENCE. A CREW MEMBER HOLDS UP A SIGN: "WOBBLE AGAIN." ]

KALO: I see the sign.

### Act One

[DR. RAL WAITS IN THE GUEST AREA WITH A SMALL TELESCOPE. VENN SETS A LEVEL ON THE DESK.] 

VENN: We have a desk challenge. Kalo tells one joke while the desk wobbles.

SITA: I can underscore the wobble.

DR. RAL: I have a telescope demonstration after the monologue.

KALO: It will be a two-minute interview.

[THE DESK WOBBLES WHEN KALO SITS. SITA ADDS A DRUM ROLL EACH TIME IT MOVES. THE PEN ROLLS OFF AGAIN AND LANDS IN A COFFEE CUP.] 

KALO: The desk is stealing my props.

VENN: The audience likes the pen.

[KALO RETRIEVES THE PEN. THE DESK WOBBLES AGAIN, SO HE USES THE PEN TO MARK WHICH LEG IS SHORT.] 

KALO: It has become a co-host.

[KALO REMOVES THE COFFEE CUP. SITA PLAYS A FINAL CRASH. THE DESK'S LOOSE LEG SLIDES AN INCH.] 

### Act Two

[THE MONOLOGUE RUNS LONG. VENN WHEELS OUT A SECOND DESK, A LEVEL, AND A CARDBOARD CUTOUT OF THE WOBBLY DESK.]

KALO: This is a desk, not a franchise.

SITA: I wrote a theme song.

[SITA SINGS TWO LINES ABOUT THE WOBBLE. THE BAND PLAYS A BRIDGE. VENN HOLDS UP A VOTING CARD: "REPAIR OR REMATCH?" THE AUDIENCE VOTES FOR REMATCH.] 

DR. RAL: Does the rematch include stars?

VENN: We can add a star sticker.

DR. RAL: I mean the telescope.

[DR. RAL SETS THE TELESCOPE ON THE DESK. IT WOBBLES, SO SHE CARRIES IT TO A STABLE SIDE TABLE.]

KALO: That is where the demonstration will happen.

VENN: The desk bit is still in progress.

[KALO REMOVES THE CARDBOARD CUTOUT. ITS FLAT FOOT WEDGES UNDER THE DESK LEG, STABILIZING IT. THE BAND STOPS MID-NOTE.]

[VENN PULLS THE CUTOUT FREE TO SHOW THE CAMERA HOW IT WORKS. THE DESK WOBBLES AGAIN. KALO SETS HIS HAND ON IT WHILE SITA TRIES A DIFFERENT ANGLE WITH THE WRENCH.]

KALO: The joke is now a repair demonstration.

SITA: The repair needs a close-up.

DR. RAL: My telescope needs that side table.

[VENN ROLLS THE SECOND DESK INTO PLACE. ITS LEGS ARE LEVEL, BUT A LARGE SPOTLIGHT MAKES THE TABLETOP LOOK LIKE A MOON.]

KALO: We have made the desk look bigger than the guest.

DR. RAL: The moon does that too.

[SITA PLAYS A DRUM ROLL. THE TABLE DOES NOT MOVE. THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS THE ABSENCE OF A WOBBLE.]

VENN: We can get one more vote.

KALO: We already voted.

SITA: The desk has been solved.

VENN: We have no ending.

DR. RAL: We have a telescope.

### Act Three

[VENN ROLLS THE SECOND DESK AWAY; ITS LARGE TOP HIDES THE TELESCOPE FROM CAMERA. KALO PUTS THE CARDBOARD FOOT BACK UNDER THE ORIGINAL DESK. THE LIVE SHOW RESUMES WITH THAT DESK STEADY. HE INTRODUCES DR. RAL, WHO SHOWS HOW THE TELESCOPE TURNS TO FOLLOW THE MOON.]

DR. RAL: The mount moves smoothly when its base is level.

[KALO LOOKS AT THE CARDBOARD CUTOUT PROPPING THE DESK. THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS.]

KALO: We have a new piece of equipment.

VENN: It is a picture of your desk.

SITA: It has completed its arc.

[DR. RAL LOOKS THROUGH THE TELESCOPE AND INVITES THE AUDIENCE TO SEE A MODEL MOON ON THE STAGE SCREEN. VENN STARTS THE WOBBLE VOTE AGAIN; KALO TAKES THE CARD AND TURNS IT OVER TO REVEAL THE GUEST'S NAME.]

[THE VIEW OF THE MODEL MOON IS SHARP. VENN HOLDS THE VOTE CARD BEHIND THE TELESCOPE, BUT THE CAMERA FOCUSES ON THE MOON INSTEAD OF THE WORD "REMATCH."]

SITA: The telescope has won the close-up.

KALO: It has a better angle.

[THE DIRECTOR CALLS FOR A COMMERCIAL BREAK. THE DESK DOES NOT WOBBLE THROUGH THE ENTIRE SPONSOR READ.]

KALO: We are talking about astronomy now.

### Tag

[AFTER THE SHOW, SITA TRIES TO REST HER MUSIC STAND ON THE DESK. THE CARDBOARD CUTOUT SLIDES OUT. THE DESK WOBBLES ONCE.] 

SITA: Encore?

[KALO HANDS HER THE WRENCH. THE BAND PLAYS A SINGLE TUNING NOTE WHILE THEY FIX IT.]
