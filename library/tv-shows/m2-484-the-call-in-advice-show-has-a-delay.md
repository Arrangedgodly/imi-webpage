# The Call-In Advice Show Has a Delay

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live call-in advice programs whose hosts answer callers before the delayed audio reaches the studio*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Pavi, a lemur; producer Ranu, an orangutan; caller Kessa, a macaque; audio engineer Joro, a gibbon; co-host Nemi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. CALL-IN STUDIO - DAY

Pavi smiles at the camera. A phone light blinks. The delay meter reads eight seconds.

PAVI: Tell us what's troubling you.

[The caller's audio arrives late. Pavi answers before hearing it.]

PAVI: You should not move the chair.

NEMI: She has not asked about a chair.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Kessa's question comes through. She is trying to decide whether to keep a large chair she found on the curb. Pavi says not to move it, assuming it belongs to a neighbor. Nemi listens to the full question and learns Kessa has already called the city to check whether it is discarded.

KESSA (OVER PHONE): I have a receipt from the reuse center.

PAVI: Then the chair is yours.

Joro watches the delay meter. The line is eight seconds behind. Ranu asks the hosts to leave a pause after each question. Pavi attempts to hold a neutral face while Kessa talks. His expression grows increasingly concerned.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Joro reduces the delay by two seconds. Kessa's voice now overlaps Pavi's answers. Pavi tries to wait, but Nemi nods at him too early. He advises Kessa to measure the doorway before carrying the chair inside. The advice is useful, but he says it before the caller mentions the doorway.

PAVI: I guessed the next problem.

NEMI: It is the most common problem with a chair.

[Ranu puts a card beside the camera: LISTEN UNTIL THE BELL. Joro sends a soft bell tone when the caller finishes. The tone arrives through the same delay and rings while Kessa is still speaking.]

KESSA (OVER PHONE): I also want to know if I should paint it.

Pavi waits. The bell rings. He answers about painting, but the audio is still delayed. Kessa hears him answer the earlier question about moving the chair.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Ranu tests the line with a prerecorded question. The playback is still eight seconds late. Joro puts the host headphones on a direct monitor and leaves only the broadcast feed delayed. Pavi asks Kessa to say a short test phrase. He hears it immediately; the viewers hear it later. Nemi repeats the words on the studio monitor, and the two versions overlap like an echo.

JORO: We need to mute the room speaker.

RANU: It is feeding the delay back into the caller line.

Joro lowers the speaker and disconnects the return feed. Kessa asks the same chair question again. This time Pavi waits, listens, and answers once. Ranu signals the camera to stay on the host until the delayed audio has caught up.

ACT THREE

INT. STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Joro routes the caller's audio through a local monitor with no delay. The broadcast retains an eight-second safety delay, but the hosts hear Kessa live in their headphones. Pavi asks her to repeat the last question. She asks whether blue paint will suit the chair. Nemi says to test a sample on the back. Pavi waits until she finishes, then repeats the advice clearly.

KESSA: Thank you. I can try a small patch first.

[The delay monitor shows the exchange eight seconds later. Ranu watches it without interrupting. The camera stays on Pavi and Nemi as they wait for the broadcast to catch up.]

INT. STUDIO - LATER

After the sign-off, the producers replay the call for quality review. They hear Pavi interrupting before the questions finish. Ranu asks him to listen to the whole clip before choosing an answer. The recording reaches the final line, where Kessa says the chair has a loose leg. Pavi turns toward the studio chair beneath him. He stands and checks its leg before sitting again.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Ranu cues the sponsor bumper while Kessa is still on the line. Pavi starts reading the copy, then hears the loose-leg detail in his headphones and stops. The delayed feed plays his sponsor line over Kessa asking whether she should sit on the chair. Joro pulls the delayed return from the hosts' earpieces; the broadcast delay remains intact. Nemi asks Kessa to lay the chair carefully on its side so the loose leg will not scrape her floor. Kessa does. Pavi waits until she finishes describing the wobble. He suggests a repair shop, not a paint color. Ranu tries the bumper again, but Joro holds up one finger until the caller is done. The sponsor jingle finally begins after Kessa says goodbye, and the hosts let it finish without talking over it.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - LATER

Pavi signs off. The last caller's delayed voice says goodbye after the show has ended. Nemi waves to the empty camera. Joro lets the delay finish before closing the line.

FINAL IMAGE: The chair sits beside the studio door with a small blue paint sample on its back and a tag reading TEST FIRST.
