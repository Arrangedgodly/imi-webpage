# The Meet-Cute Has a Floor Plan

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality dating shows, engineered first meetings, producer prompts, and reaction shots after every greeting*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Two contestants on a dating show keep missing their producer-planned meet-cute because they both read the floor plan and use the exits.

Cast: Host Savi, a gibbon; contestant Telo, a macaque; contestant Mina, an orangutan; producer Neri, a baboon; camera operator Vala, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SHOW GARDEN SET - DAY

Savi announces that Telo and Mina will meet beside a fountain. A floor plan is taped to the wall. Mina enters, reads it, and walks to the marked exit. Telo enters and does the same.

SAVI: They have missed each other.

NERI: They are on the same schedule.

ACT ONE

Neri resets the scene. Telo is asked to carry a flower basket; Mina is asked to reach for the same flower. Vala places the basket on a low table. Telo walks around it. Mina picks up the basket and moves it out of the camera lane.

MINA: This was blocking the path.

TELO: Thanks. I was about to move it.

Neri signals Savi to ask what they felt during the moment. Telo says the basket was heavy. Mina says it had a loose handle. They inspect the handle together. The camera zooms on their hands. Mina stops it from falling.

MINA: The handle is twisted.

TELO: It needs a knot.

SAVI: Did you feel a connection?

TELO: To the basket.

ACT TWO

Neri tries a second setup: a sudden rain sound, two umbrellas, one shared bench. The sound effect begins. Telo opens his umbrella and walks toward the set door. Mina picks up a tarp and covers the camera equipment.

MINA: The lens is getting wet.

VALA: It's a sound effect.

MINA: The camera is under a real drip.

Vala moves the camera. Neri asks the pair to sit on the bench. They sit at opposite ends. Savi asks them to share a story about a memorable first date. Mina says she once went on a hike where the guide took them to the wrong trail. Telo says he was the guide on a hike where nobody noticed the wrong trail.

TELO: I had the map upside down.

MINA: Was it the same hike?

TELO: It could have been.

They compare route maps. Mina recognizes Telo's drawing of the same fork she visited. The two laugh. Neri moves a reflector to catch their faces. It blocks the map.

SAVI: Let's hold that look.

MINA: The map is covered.

ACT TWO-B

The production team brings in a dinner table for the final date. A spotlight points at one chair. Telo asks whether the light is hot. Mina checks the path between the table and the exit. Neri tells them to sit facing each other. They rotate their chairs so they can both see the garden.

NERI: Face one another.

MINA: We can talk from here.

TELO: I can see you and the door.

Savi asks each to say one thing they appreciate. Mina says Telo reads maps carefully once they are turned the right way. Telo says Mina notices loose handles before anyone drops anything. Neri asks for a kiss. Both contestants decline, saying they have only met today.

SAVI: Would you like another date?

MINA: I'd like to finish our conversation without a camera cue.

TELO: We can meet at the park trail.

Neri asks whether the network can film it. Mina says they can come if they stay behind the marked boundary and do not move the signs.

The director calls for a final walk-and-talk. Vala follows as the pair stroll past the set gate. Neri slides a bench into their path to create a spontaneous pause. Mina notices the wheels and stops it with her foot before it rolls into the camera cable.

MINA: This bench has wheels.

TELO: It is also in the marked walkway.

Neri tells Vala to frame the pair beside the bench. Telo reads its taped label: "STAGE PROP." Mina finds the locking lever and secures it. Savi asks if the moment felt romantic. The two laugh, then point to the bench as the reason they stopped.

SAVI: You could sit together.

MINA: We could. After the crew moves the cable.

Vala shifts the cable and rolls the bench safely off the route. Telo and Mina continue walking, talking about the public trail. Neri follows at a distance, whispering for a close-up. Vala keeps the lens wide enough to include the boundary marker.

ACT THREE

Savi announces that Telo and Mina have chosen to exchange contact cards and arrange a walk at the public trail. Neri asks Vala for one final reaction shot. Vala points to the floor plan, where the route shows both contestants leaving by the same door.

NERI: Could you go out separately?

MINA: We are going to the same place.

TELO: The exit is wide enough.

They walk out together. Neri checks the empty set and sees the flower basket is still blocking the camera lane. He moves it. The handle loosens again. Savi catches it before it falls.

SAVI: We have a connection.

NERI: To the basket.

TAG

The credits roll over the garden. The fountain sound effect continues after the water is turned off.

FINAL IMAGE: Telo and Mina appear briefly beyond the set gate, walking side by side while Vala keeps the camera inside the marked boundary.
