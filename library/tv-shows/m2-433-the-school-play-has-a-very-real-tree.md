# The School Play Has a Very Real Tree

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: behind-the-scenes drama series that mistakes practical props for emotional breakthroughs*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Director Pava, a lemur; actor Toma, a macaque; actor Seli, a capuchin; stage manager Runi, a gibbon; drama teacher Moss, a slow loris.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM - AFTERNOON

[A real potted tree stands center stage. Toma wears a paper crown. Seli holds a cardboard moon.] 

PAVA: The tree is a symbol of family.

TOMA: The tree is blocking my entrance.

[The pot scrapes the floor. Theme sting.]

ACT ONE

PAVA: Welcome to Backstage Pass, where every rehearsal has a secret and every prop has a close-up.

MOSS: It's a school play about the seasons.

PAVA: The audience needs to feel the stakes.

RUNI: The stakes are in the prop box.

[Seli climbs a low rung to hang the moon. She balances on the ladder with her tail wrapped around a rail. The cardboard moon bends.] 

SELI: It's too heavy for the tape.

TOMA: The moon is cardboard.

SELI: The tape is one inch.

[Pava asks Toma to deliver his line facing the camera. He turns, then cannot see Seli's cue.]

TOMA: I have forgotten the season.

MOSS: It is autumn.

PAVA: Can you say it with more uncertainty?

[Toma tries. The paper crown slips over one eye.]

ACT TWO

[Runi moves the potted tree two feet to clear the entrance. The stage floor is uneven, and the pot begins to tilt. Toma catches it. The tree's leaves brush his crown.] 

TOMA: The tree is attacking the king.

PAVA: This is our conflict.

MOSS: It is a plant in a pot.

[The students rehearse the scene. Seli's moon falls; she catches it and holds it like a shield. Pava calls for a retake.] 

PAVA: Keep the moon in frame.

SELI: I am keeping it from the floor.

[She tucks the moon under one arm and uses the other hand to steady the ladder. Runi secures the pot with a sandbag.] 

RUNI: The tree is stable.

TOMA: My character is still afraid of it.

PAVA: Good. Let's build on that.

[Toma steps around the tree. A root has grown through the drainage hole and caught on the stage rug. He pulls gently; the rug wrinkles.]

TOMA: It has a hold on the set.

MOSS: We should lift the pot, not pull the root.

[The crew and actors raise it together. Seli balances the moon above them, like a second potted plant. Runi slides a wheeled plant dolly under the pot and tucks the root clear of the rug.] 

[Before the house opens, Pava asks the students to run the tree scene once without stopping for camera notes. Moss hands Toma the crown and Seli the moon.]

MOSS: The audience will sit close. Watch each other's cues.

TOMA: I enter after the moon rises.

SELI: I raise it when the wind changes.

[The auditorium vent starts early. Seli sees the leaves move and lifts the moon. Toma enters on cue. The crown stays in place.]

PAVA: That was clean. Can we make it more surprising?

RUNI: It is a play. The audience came to see what happens.

PAVA: They came to feel what happens.

MOSS: The feeling comes from the scene, not the cutaway.

[Toma returns the crown to the costume box and checks that the real tree is steady. Seli places the moon on its hook. The students leave the prop positions marked with tape.]

PAVA: We have our next episode.

TOMA: We have the next class.

ACT THREE

[The performance begins. Toma enters as the king. He pauses beside the real tree, then delivers his line without looking at camera.] 

TOMA: The tree remembers every season we stood together.

[The audience quiets. The tree's leaves shake as the auditorium vent turns on. Seli's cardboard moon flutters. Toma holds the crown in place and continues.] 

TOMA: Even when the wind moves us, we remain rooted.

[Pava whispers to Moss.] 

PAVA: That was not in rehearsal.

MOSS: It was in the script.

[Seli raises the moon. The tape gives way again, but Runi catches it with one hand and places it on the hook. It looks like the moon rising behind the tree.] 

SELI: It's working.

RUNI: The hook was there the whole time.

[The play ends. The audience applauds. Pava steps onto stage to discuss the emotional journey. Toma pushes the tree on its dolly to the wing, giving the next class a clear stage.] 

PAVA: Our cast has found closure.

TOMA: Our cast has found the floor.

[Toma parks the pot on the taped mark, with the root clear of the rug.]

TAG

[Pava announces the next episode: "The Curtain That Wouldn't Fall." Behind her, the curtain slides down on its own.]

MOSS: That is the actual end of rehearsal.

PAVA: Can we get one more take?
