# The Recycling Challenge Sorts the Bin by Color

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: competition shows that turn a practical sorting task into a color-coded race*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Kesa, a macaque; contestant Nori, a gibbon; contestant Vela, an orangutan; judge Tavi, a lemur; producer Dali, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME STAGE - DAY

Three bins stand in a row: blue, green and black. Kesa points to a plastic bottle.

KESA: Which bin takes this?

NORI: I need to read the label.

[The countdown starts before he turns the bottle around.]

ACT ONE

INT. STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Dali says the format is a speed round. Tavi asks contestants to sort by local recycling rules. Kesa says the colors are universal. Vela reads the sign: the blue bin is for paper in this town. The plastic bottle belongs in a different container. Dali asks the graphics team to make the rules fit the clock.

TAVI: The clock cannot change the rules.

Kesa turns the bottle so the label faces camera. It says returnable. Vela points to the deposit crate, which is offstage.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The contestants try again with the rule card visible. Nori sorts a newspaper into blue. Vela places a clean jar in green. The buzzer sounds before they finish. The screen shows Nori winning by three items. Tavi checks the table and sees the jar is still in Vela's hand. Dali asks whether the score can count the label rather than the item.

[The crew moves the deposit crate onstage. The bottle fits. Kesa asks for a dramatic close-up. The camera zooms into the crate and loses the contestants.]

Tavi asks Dali to show the actual rules. The graphics display three colors but omit the special waste box. Nori finds a battery in the sorting tray. Vela stops him from placing it in blue. Tavi opens a separate battery container. The timer continues to count while the judge explains. Kesa asks for a pause; the button is decorative.

INT. STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Tavi removes the timer from the sorting table. The contestants continue at a normal pace. Nori reads each label aloud. Vela checks whether the jar is clean. Kesa asks viewers to guess the final bin. The control room adds a multiple-choice graphic. It lists three options, none of them deposit crate.

INT. STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Tavi puts four sample items on a table: a newspaper, a clean jar, a return bottle and a battery. Dali asks Kesa to start the countdown. Tavi takes the timer off the table and sets it with the prize tote.

TAVI: They get to read the labels first.

Nori and Vela step to their marks. The local guide is taped beside the bins, including the separate battery box and deposit crate. Kesa asks the camera to zoom on the bins; the shot crops off the words. Poro widens it until both words and colors are visible. Dali asks if the faster contestant can win a bonus. Tavi points to the rule card: one point per correct item. The contestants read the card aloud, then wait for the judge's signal. Kesa tries to cue the buzzer early. Tavi keeps one finger on the button until the round begins.

Tavi asks the operator to show the rule card full screen. Dali's countdown overlay covers the bottom line, so Kesa tells him to remove it. Nori points out the line that says containers must be clean. Vela tests the jar with a glance inside; it is clean. The contestants return each sample to the table, keeping the scoring round fresh. Dali moves the tote prize farther from the finish mark so no one mistakes it for a bin.

ACT THREE

INT. GAME STAGE - AFTERNOON

The final round is one item at a time, using the town's posted rules. Nori reads the bottle label and puts it in the return crate. Vela sorts a paper bag into blue. Tavi accepts both. The scoreboard records two correct choices. Dali asks if a speed bonus is available. Tavi says accuracy is the contest.

The screen shows the final result without a countdown. Kesa announces a tie. The prize is a reusable tote bag. There is only one. Nori and Vela open it and find the rules poster inside. They unfold it and hold opposite ends. Kesa asks who gets the tote. Tavi says they can share it to carry the poster back to the sorting station.

Dali wants a final sprint. Nori and Vela walk the items to the proper bins, taking time to check the labels. The camera follows the destination signs. The battery goes in the battery box. The return bottle goes into the crate. No buzzer sounds.

TAG

INT. STAGE - AFTER THE SHOW

Tavi posts the local sorting guide beside the bins. Kesa reads it before introducing the next round. Dali switches off the empty timer.

FINAL IMAGE: The bins are labeled with words as well as colors, while the shared tote carries the unfolded guide.
