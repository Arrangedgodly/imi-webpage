# The Game Show Door Opens to Storage

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television game shows, mystery prizes, and suspenseful doors that reveal production supplies*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Reko, a macaque; contestant Mavi, a gibbon; stage manager Doro, an orangutan; prize announcer Hali, a baboon; camera operator Sumi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW STAGE - NIGHT

Three doors stand across the stage. A sign reads CHOOSE YOUR PRIZE. The host faces contestant Mavi, who holds a small token.

REKO: Behind one door is a grand prize.

MAVI: What is behind the other two?

DORO: We are about to find out.

[The host opens Door One. It reveals a stack of folding chairs and a box of spare bulbs.] 

HALI: A maintenance prize package.

REKO: Close that door. We have a mystery to preserve.

[The camera operator zooms in on the spare bulbs. The audience applauds.] 

ACT ONE

INT. GAME SHOW STAGE - CONTINUOUS

The stage manager closes Door One and checks the label on the back: STORAGE. Reko tells Mavi to choose Door Two or Door Three. The contestant asks whether the game rules allow a change after seeing one door. Hali reads the card; changes are allowed once.

MAVI: I choose Door Three.

REKO: You may switch to Door Two.

MAVI: I will stay with Three.

[Reko reaches for the handle. Doro stops him and checks that the door is latched. A piece of tape reads COSTUMES - KEEP CLOSED.]

DORO: Door Three is storage too.

REKO: We only have one prize door?

DORO: The prize is on Door Two.

[The host asks whether the audience can pretend Door Three was a mystery. The contestant asks whether Door Two can be opened now. The stage manager checks the game card and confirms that Door Two is the correct prize door.] 

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The team must reset for another round. Doro moves the folding chairs from Door One into the backstage rack, then checks the bulb box. Hali asks the host to build suspense around Door Two. The camera operator reframes the three doors so the STORAGE labels are no longer visible to the audience. Doro objects; the labels are needed by the crew.

DORO: We can cover the labels on the front, but keep the back marked.

REKO: Can we put a curtain over every door?

MAVI: Then I will not know which handle I chose.

[The host tries to add a fourth door, but Doro points out there is no fourth prize. Sumi tracks the stage marks and shows how Door Two can be framed without hiding Door One's storage function.] 

SUMI: Camera left makes the set look symmetrical. The doors are still labeled backstage.

[The contestant receives a new token and practices the choice. Door Two opens to a bicycle helmet and a basket. Hali confirms this is the actual prize; Mavi can keep the helmet or trade it for a sealed box.] 

MAVI: I will keep the helmet. It fits.

REKO: The sealed box could contain anything.

DORO: It contains the listed runner-up prize.

[Doro runs a final inventory before the audience returns. Door One contains the folding chairs, Door Two holds the bicycle and basket, Door Three contains costumes. Reko holds the prize sign while Hali checks the game card.] 

DORO: All three doors open to what their labels promise.

REKO: And the audience gets a mystery.

MAVI: The audience gets a choice. I get a helmet.

[The stage manager tests each latch and leaves the storage keys on the control hook. Sumi records the doors opening once, then closes them in the same order. The crew has a reset plan if a contestant chooses to switch.] 

HALI: No one needs to open the chair closet for a prize reveal.

[Reko places the sign above Door Two, not over the whole row.] 

ACT THREE

INT. GAME SHOW STAGE - NIGHT

The round begins. Reko introduces three doors, but Mavi asks whether the stage team has verified the labels. Doro checks all three: two are storage, one is the prize door. Hali reads the rules aloud, including the allowed switch. Mavi chooses Door Two and stays with it.

[The host opens Door Two. The bicycle helmet and basket appear, correctly labeled. The audience applauds. Sumi keeps the camera on Mavi, not the door hinge.] 

REKO: You won the prize you selected.

MAVI: And the chairs stay in storage.

DORO: Correct. We need them for the audience.

[Hali hands over the helmet and basket. The host offers the sealed box anyway; Mavi declines it. Doro closes Door One and Door Three, then locks the storage latches.] 

REKO: Our mystery is solved.

DORO: Our inventory is also accounted for.

[The stage crew clears the prize display for the next contestant. Mavi exits through the marked contestant door and waves to the audience.] 

TAG

Reko knocks on Door One. A chair squeaks inside the storage room. Doro flips the front sign to PLEASE DO NOT OPEN.

FINAL IMAGE: The winner carries the helmet past three closed doors, while the prize sign stays over the correct one.
