# The Last Leaf Standing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised cooking competitions, timed mystery baskets, and judges' critiques*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: Three cooks race to turn identical mystery baskets into a meal before the studio timer runs out, but the only ingredient everyone needs is stuck to the host.

Cast: Host Tavi, a gibbon; chef Uma, a macaque; chef Rillo, a capuchin; chef Kett, a baboon; judge Suma, an orangutan; and floor manager Pip, a squirrel monkey.

## Episode: "The Secret Ingredient"

### Cold open

INT. CANOPY KITCHEN SET, DAY

[Three counters hold baskets covered with cloth. A studio clock counts down from 30:00. A leaf sticks to Tavi's nose.]

TAVI: Welcome to The Last Leaf Standing. Today our cooks will create a dish using a surprise ingredient.

UMA: Is the surprise under the cloth?

RILLO: Or in the clock?

KETT: The surprise is that the clock is already running.

TAVI: Chef Kett has spotted our first challenge.

PIP: We started the clock during the sponsor message.

TAVI: We are live, and the leaf is seasonal.

SUMA: I am ready to judge.

TAVI: Open your baskets!

[The chefs lift the cloths. Each basket contains roots, nuts, three unripe fruits, and an empty cup.]

RILLO: Mine is missing the ingredient.

UMA: Mine has the same ingredients.

KETT: That is how identical baskets work.

### Act One

INT. CANOPY KITCHEN SET

TAVI: Your mystery ingredient is a leaf.

SUMA: Which leaf?

TAVI: The last one standing.

[All look at the leaf stuck to Tavi's nose.]

PIP: We had one leaf on the table, but the fan blew it away.

TAVI: Then the ingredient is here.

RILLO: Can we have it?

TAVI: After I finish introducing the challenge.

UMA: The timer says twenty-eight minutes.

TAVI: You have twenty-seven minutes to prepare a dish that celebrates texture, balance, and surprise.

KETT: Can the leaf be washed?

TAVI: It is being worn.

SUMA: I expect a clear point of view.

RILLO: I will make root stew.

UMA: I will roast the nuts.

KETT: I will make an empty cup of something.

SUMA: That is not a dish.

KETT: It has potential.

[Uma peels a fruit with fast, careful fingers. Rillo crushes nuts with a smooth stone. Kett stares at the empty cup.]

TAVI: The chefs are moving quickly.

PIP: Kett hasn't moved.

KETT: I am letting the ingredients speak.

RILLO: They are saying nothing.

KETT: That's a difficult flavor.

### Act Two

INT. JUDGES' TABLE

[The clock reaches ten minutes. Tavi tries to remove the leaf. It stays on.]

TAVI: Floor manager, the leaf is attached.

PIP: You can lift it from the side.

TAVI: I don't want to disrupt the challenge.

SUMA: The leaf is part of the challenge.

UMA: I need it for my garnish.

RILLO: I need it for the stew.

KETT: I need it for the empty cup.

TAVI: One at a time. We will have a fair leaf lottery.

PIP: The bowl is ready.

[Tavi leans over the bowl. The leaf still won't leave. Pip gently lifts Tavi's nose; the leaf drops into the bowl.]

TAVI: The ingredient has entered the basket.

KETT: The bowl is my station.

UMA: I have a second garnish. It's the top of a root.

RILLO: I can make the leaf into a broth.

KETT: I can now make the empty cup into a full cup.

SUMA: This is the first practical development.

[Tavi takes the bowl toward the cooks. The leaf slides from one side to the other.]

TAVI: It is mobile.

PIP: Keep the bowl level.

TAVI: I am a host, not a shelf.

### Act Three

INT. JUDGES' TABLE, LATER

[The clock hits zero. The chefs bring forward their plates.]

SUMA: Uma, describe your dish.

UMA: Roasted roots with a crisp leaf garnish. The nuts are for crunch.

SUMA: It smells good. The leaf is mostly on one side.

UMA: The plate kept turning.

SUMA: Rillo?

RILLO: A stew of roots, crushed nuts, and leaf broth.

SUMA: It is warm.

RILLO: The leaf gave it a strong beginning.

SUMA: And Kett?

KETT: A cup of clear broth with the last leaf floating on top.

SUMA: The cup is empty.

KETT: The broth evaporated during my presentation.

SUMA: There is no steam.

KETT: It was a quiet evaporation.

TAVI: Judges, your decision.

SUMA: Uma balanced the flavors. Rillo used the leaf most clearly. Kett presented a cup.

KETT: Thank you.

SUMA: The winner is Rillo.

RILLO: I would like to thank the leaf.

UMA: It stuck to the host for most of the show.

TAVI: It was an unusually committed ingredient.

PIP: The sponsor sent a replacement leaf.

[The replacement leaf drops from the ceiling and lands on Tavi's shoulder.]

TAVI: We'll save that for next week.

### Tag

INT. CANOPY KITCHEN SET

[The crew cleans up. Kett picks up the empty cup. A nut falls out of it.]

KETT: The cup has a surprise.

SUMA: That's a nut.

KETT: It is a dessert course.

[The clock starts ticking again.]

[The wheel keeps turning as a seedling leans toward the studio lights.]
