# The Teleshop Needs a Real Problem

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live shopping television, where hosts invent emergencies to sell an ordinary tool*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A shopping channel tries to sell a garden hook, but its host keeps manufacturing disasters that product expert Odo is forced to solve on air.

Cast: Pava, a gibbon host; Odo, a baboon product expert; Nari, a macaque caller; Tavi, a capuchin stage assistant; Seli, a lemur floor manager.

COLD OPEN

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - DAY

Pava holds up a plain iron hook. A cable dangles behind her. A potted fern sits on the floor.

PAVA: Does your watering can need somewhere to hang?

ODO: This hook can hold a watering can after it is mounted in a solid post.

TAVI: The demonstration can is on the floor.

Pava looks down. The fern is leaning into the can. She grabs it just before it tips.

PAVA: We have a situation.

SELI (IN EARPIECE): You have a fern. Do not call it a situation.

ACT ONE

Pava introduces the hook as a limited-time solution. Odo explains that it screws into a solid post and can hold a can, a hat, or a small basket. Seli points out the row of identical hooks behind the camera.

PAVA: Those are not the offer.

SELI: They look exactly like the offer.

PAVA: They're production hooks. This one has a close-up camera.

Odo screws the hook into the solid demonstration post, checks that the screws are snug, and hangs the can. It stays. Pava asks him to add more weight. He adds a second can. The post begins to lean.

ODO: Stop. The hook is strong. The post base is not secured.

PAVA: Viewers, this is why you need the hook.

ODO: You need a sound post.

Seli places a cue card below the lens: SHOW DRAMA. Pava reads it, then lifts the cue card as if it were a technical diagram.

PAVA: Does your garden contain an unstable post?

ODO: Then don't load it.

Tavi runs in carrying a pile of scarves. She pretends to stumble. The scarves land neatly in her arms.

TAVI: Emergency. The scarves have fallen off the chair.

PAVA: A textile avalanche.

ACT TWO

Seli opens the phone line. Nari calls from her shed and says she already owns a hook.

PAVA: What made you watch?

NARI: I want to know if you'll explain how to put it up.

ODO: Check that the post is solid. Mark the screw location, drill a pilot hole, then tighten it. Follow the package directions.

PAVA: We are saving that tip for the premium segment.

NARI: You just said it.

Pava reaches for the second can. Odo blocks her hand. Nari says she has a post and a drill, but the post sounds hollow when she knocks.

ODO: Don't mount it there.

PAVA: Could she attach it to the shed door?

ODO: Only if the door and fasteners are rated for it.

PAVA: Our hook is being extremely selective.

SELI: Our hook is a hook.

The countdown light flashes. A stagehand rolls in a small ladder with a scarf tied to its top. Pava points to it as if rescuers have arrived.

PAVA: A new challenge: can the hook hold a scarf at this height?

ODO: The ladder is unstable. Take that down.

Tavi removes the scarf. The folding ladder wobbles; Odo catches it, folds it flat, and slides it out of the camera lane. Pava reaches for the dangling cable and nearly pulls the demonstration post toward her. Seli disconnects the cable instead.

ACT TWO-B

Nari calls back. Her neighbor has a solid post, but it is too close to a walkway. Odo tells her to choose a safe location with clearance. Pava asks if they can sell a location-finding compass.

NARI: I can look at the walkway.

PAVA: A compass would come with a pouch.

SELI: We do not sell a compass.

Tavi rolls the demonstration post away from the path and locks its wheeled base. She tests the base with a gentle push; it stays put. Nari says that height works for her. She asks whether the hook will hold a damp towel. Odo says the metal can hold the towel's weight if the mounting surface and hook rating allow it; keep it clear of the walkway.

NARI: I'll check the instructions on the hook I already own.

PAVA: That's a very responsible call.

NARI: I called for installation advice. I got it.

Pava points at the clock. Seli holds up two fingers: two minutes remain. Pava offers a second hook at half price. Nari says one is enough. Odo demonstrates the limit using one can, a hat, and a small empty basket. Pava stacks apples into the basket. Seli removes the apples before the post begins to bend.

ODO: The hook is not a shelf.

PAVA: Then the apples are not included.

TAVI: Neither is the post.

ACT THREE

Pava announces the hook's modest price. Odo holds the package instructions beside it. Nari says she will install her own hook after checking her post and the clearance. The phone line clicks off. The studio crew hangs its scarves on the old hooks and clears the set.

PAVA: Order now, or use the hook you have.

SELI: That is the whole offer.

PAVA: Then we have successfully explained a hook.

Seli points to the live shot. The demonstration hook is empty. Pava reaches to hang her headset on it; Odo shows the camera the tiny load-rating sticker on the packaging.

ODO: Check the rating first.

Pava hangs the headset on a production hook instead.

TAG

Tavi starts rolling the demonstration post offstage. Its wheels snag the studio cable. Pava reaches for the hook, then stops. Seli unplugs the cable and rolls the post away.

PAVA: No emergency.

SELI: We're off the air.

The red light is still on. Pava smiles directly at the lens.

FINAL IMAGE: Odo quietly turns the live camera toward the empty hook as the shopping theme plays over the crew coiling the cable by hand.
