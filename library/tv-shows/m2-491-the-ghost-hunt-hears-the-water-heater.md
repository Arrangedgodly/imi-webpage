# The Ghost Hunt Hears the Water Heater

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal reality shows that turn routine building noises into cliffhanger evidence*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Miko, a macaque; investigator Sela, a lemur; sound tech Doro, a gibbon; caretaker Enu, an orangutan; producer Rika, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. OLD THEATER - NIGHT

Miko stands in the dark foyer. A pipe clunks behind the wall. The camera zooms to his face.

MIKO: Something has answered us.

[The water heater clicks on.]

ACT ONE

INT. THEATER FOYER - CONTINUOUS

Rika asks the crew to hold the shot. Doro checks the audio meter. The clunk repeats, followed by a hiss. Sela calls it a pattern. Enu, the caretaker, says the water heater cycles every twenty minutes. Rika asks him to leave so the investigators can test the room alone.

MIKO: We are hearing a response from the unseen.

ENU: You are hearing the heater I just turned on.

Enu exits. The heater stops. Rika signals for a dramatic music sting. The sting arrives before the next sound. Everyone freezes and watches the boiler closet. Nothing happens. Miko asks if the silence is meaningful. Sela says it may be withholding.

ACT TWO

INT. STAGE LEFT - LATER

Doro places a microphone near the pipe. Rika asks him to move it closer for a clean recording. He sets it against the wall. The pipe clunks; the sound overloads the meter. Rika asks for a reaction shot. Miko whispers that the ghost has become louder.

Sela proposes a question-and-answer test. She asks the room to tap once for yes. The heater clicks once. She asks if the ghost knows the theater's name. The heater clicks twice. Enu returns carrying a toolbox and says the thermostat is making the sound. Rika tells him not to explain the evidence on camera.

[Enu opens the closet. The heater clicks as it cools. The cast jumps. Enu points to the pipe, then to the metal casing.] 

ENU: It taps when the hot water moves through the bend.

MIKO: Could it be a message about plumbing?

Rika asks for a close-up of the temperature gauge. It is set high. Enu turns it down. The meter stops registering the clunks. Rika looks disappointed and tells him the crew needed another take before he changed anything. Enu says the water is now safer to use.

INT. BOILER CLOSET - MOMENTS LATER

Enu asks Miko to hold a flashlight while he checks the service sticker. Miko aims it at the water heater's warning label. Rika whispers that the glow looks ominous. Sela reads the sticker aloud: annual inspection due next month. The camera zooms in until the date fills the screen. Doro records the heater's normal cycle from a safe distance, with no music over it. The pipe clicks once as the thermostat settles. Enu shows the crew the loosened bracket that had amplified the knock and tightens it with one tool turn. He adds a separate tag: BRACKET SECURED TODAY. The annual inspection sticker still says due next month.

INT. CONTROL ROOM - LATER

Sela reviews the sound file. Doro has captured the pipe, but the dramatic sting covers the second clunk. Rika asks him to restore the sting over the first clunk instead. Miko records narration about a warning from beyond. Enu says the warning is that the heater needs maintenance. Rika asks him to save that for the credits.

ACT THREE

INT. THEATER FOYER - NIGHT

The crew tries a final isolation test. Enu switches the heater off at the breaker. The room stays quiet. Miko asks a question; nothing answers. Rika signals Doro to play the recorded clunk through a hidden speaker. The sound comes from the wrong side of the room. Sela points at the speaker. Miko pivots and addresses it directly.

MIKO: If you are here, please identify yourself.

DORO: That is the monitor.

Rika tells Doro to lower the playback. The clunk becomes a tiny tap. Miko asks whether the ghost has retreated. Enu restores power to the heater and asks the crew to stand clear while it warms. The pipe knocks once. Miko raises both hands. Sela calmly notes that the temperature is rising. Rika calls for a dramatic reveal. Enu opens the closet door; the annual inspection sticker is visible beside the separate bracket tag dated that evening.

Sela records the label. Miko asks if the ghost could have put it there. Enu says he secured the bracket himself. Rika reluctantly keeps the shot because the bracket tag has the clearest date card in the episode. The heater runs without another clunk. Doro packs the microphone. Miko steps toward the exit; the heater clicks once as it shuts down. He turns back, but Sela holds up a photograph of the BRACKET SECURED TODAY tag.

TAG

INT. THEATER FOYER - AFTER THE SHOOT

Rika records a voice-over: "The theater's mystery remains unresolved." Enu walks behind the camera holding his matching maintenance note. He changes the line to: "The noise had a source." Miko asks whether they can still call the next episode THE WHISPERING STAIRS. Enu points to the elevator.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew leaves through the theater doors while the quiet heater sits beneath its annual inspection label and a separate tag reading BRACKET SECURED TODAY.
