# A Very Special Bridge to Nowhere

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: prestige documentary specials that inflate small civic projects into historic turning points*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: A film crew arrives to chronicle a short footbridge over a rain channel. Narrator Sol wants a sweeping legacy story; engineer Nima needs the crew to keep its lights off the fresh sealant and let residents use the safe crossing.

Cast: Sol, a mandrill narrator; Nima, a macaque engineer; Bex, a capuchin director; Ludo, a langur resident who walks a dog; and Olin, a red colobus resident with a stroller.

## Episode: "Across the Drain"

### Cold Open

[RAIN CHANNEL. A FOUR-METER FOOTBRIDGE CROSSES A SHALLOW DITCH. BEX POINTS A CAMERA AT NIMA, WHO HOLDS A SMALL INSPECTION CLIPBOARD.]

SOL (V.O.): For generations, the valley was divided by a treacherous abyss.

NIMA: It is a storm drain.

[BELOW, LUDO STEPS ACROSS THE DITCH ON A DRY STONE.]

BEX: Can we ask him to use the bridge instead?

LUDO: I will, when the sealant dries.

SOL (V.O.): The crossing waits for its first brave traveler.

NIMA: The sign says keep off until noon.

### Act One

[BEX SETS A LOW FOG MACHINE BESIDE THE DITCH. THE FOG ROLLS UNDER THE BRIDGE AND INTO A STORM GRATE.] 

SOL (V.O.): Beneath this modest structure, the earth exhales.

NIMA: Turn that off. It will obscure the inspection marks.

BEX: We need atmosphere.

[NIMA UNPLUGS THE MACHINE. SHE CHECKS EACH BOLT AND PAINTS A SMALL GREEN MARK ON THE INSPECTION SHEET.]

[BEX ASKS NIMA TO REPEAT THE INSPECTION FOR A CLOSE-UP. NIMA POINTS TO THE FOUR CHECKS ALREADY MARKED.]

NIMA: The camera has the same view I had.

BEX: Could you pause before the last bolt?

NIMA: I am not leaving it loose for suspense.

[SHE TIGHTENS IT. THE CREW LIGHTS A PORTABLE LAMP, WHICH CASTS A HUGE SHADOW OF THE HANDRAIL ACROSS THE DRAIN.]

SOL (V.O.): The bridge's shadow stretches toward an unknown future.

LUDO: It is just sunset.

[BEX LOWERS THE LAMP. THE SHADOW SHRINKS.]

SOL (V.O.): One by one, the signs of an ancient struggle emerge.

NIMA: These are torque checks from Tuesday.

[LUDO RETURNS WITH A BICYCLE. HE READS THE CLOSURE SIGN AND TURNS AROUND. BEX FOLLOWS HIM WITH THE CAMERA.] 

LUDO: I am going the long way to the market.

BEX: Tell us what this bridge means to you.

LUDO: It means I will have a shorter walk after noon.

### Act Two

[THE CREW PLACES A DRAMATIC RED RIBBON ACROSS THE BRIDGE. NIMA REMOVES IT BEFORE IT TOUCHES THE SEALANT.] 

BEX: Could the ribbon mark a historic first crossing?

NIMA: It would mark a trip hazard.

SOL (V.O.): No one knows whether the new path can bear the weight of hope.

NIMA: We have the load test report.

[THEY SET A TEST WEIGHT IN THE CENTER. THE BRIDGE DOES NOT MOVE. BEX PANS SLOWLY ACROSS THE CLIPBOARD.] 

BEX: The report has very little suspense.

NIMA: It is useful.

[LUDO CHECKS HIS WATCH. HE IS CARRYING TWO MARKET BAGS.]

[A RESIDENT PUSHES A STROLLER TO THE CLOSURE SIGN. NIMA SHOWS THE DETOUR ARROW WHILE BEX TRIES TO GET A REACTION SHOT.]

OLIN: Is the bridge open yet?

NIMA: In two minutes, when the sealant is ready.

LUDO: The detour is level. I just came back that way.

BEX: Could you say it more ominously?

LUDO: It is a sidewalk.

LUDO: Is noon here yet?

NIMA: Three minutes.

[SOL WHISPERS FOR A MORE EMOTIONAL ANSWER. LUDO LOOKS AT HIS BAGS.] 

LUDO: The bridge would save me carrying these uphill.

BEX: That is the human-scale detail.

NIMA: It is also the point of the bridge.

### Act Three

[THE CLOSURE SIGN COMES DOWN. NIMA MAKES THE FIRST CROSSING, THEN CHECKS THE RAILING AT THE FAR END. LUDO WALKS OVER WITH HIS BICYCLE.] 

SOL (V.O.): Against the odds, the town reunites.

LUDO: I was already on this side yesterday.

BEX: How did the journey feel?

LUDO: Flat.

[NIMA SHOWS HIM THE OPEN ROUTE AND THE MAINTENANCE CONTACT ON THE SIGNPOST. LUDO PUSHES HIS BIKE ACROSS. THE CREW TRIES TO GET A LOW TRACKING SHOT; ITS DOLLY STOPS AT THE EDGE OF THE DITCH.] 

BEX: The equipment cannot cross.

NIMA: It is a footbridge.

SOL (V.O.): The crew remains divided from the story by a small technical limit.

[LUDO REACHES BACK AND HANDS BEX THE CAMERA. BEX CROSSES ON FOOT, PUSHING THE DOLLY BY HAND.] 

### Tag

[THE CAMERA CAPTURES THE BRIDGE, THE DRAIN, AND THE UNDRAMATIC WAY HOME.]

SOL (V.O.): History has shortened the walk to market by four minutes.

NIMA: That is measurable.

[BEX ROLLS THE FOG MACHINE TOWARD THE VAN. IT BUMPS OVER A CURB AND PUFFS A CLOUD AROUND THE CREW'S OWN FEET.]
