# The Sitcom Has a Quiet Hour

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: apartment sitcoms, overlapping roommate plots, laugh tracks, and a quiet-hours rule that becomes the episode's central crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: Three roommates agree to keep the apartment quiet for one hour, but every character's separate plan depends on making a sound.

Cast: Roommate Neri, a capuchin; roommate Vex, a macaque; roommate Palla, a gibbon; neighbor Omi, an orangutan; and laugh-track operator Tavi, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "The Quiet Hour"

### Cold Open

[APARTMENT LIVING ROOM. A CLOCK READS 7:00.]

NERI: Quiet hour starts now.

VEX: I have a drum lesson.

PALLA: I have a blender.

[CANNED LAUGHTER.]

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE ROOMMATES MEET.]

Omi knocks from the neighboring apartment. He has an online meeting and asks for quiet between seven and eight. The roommates agree. Neri wants to practice silent yoga, Vex wants to learn drums using a practice pad, and Palla wants to make a smoothie with a hand-cranked blender.

Omi returns to his room. Tavi, the laugh-track operator, waits by the soundboard. Neri asks whether laugh tracks count as noise.

TAVI: Only if I press the button.

Neri stretches. Her tail taps a glass. Vex catches it before it falls. Palla turns the blender handle slowly, but the fruit sloshes loudly inside the jar.

### Act Two

[ROOMMATES TRY TO STAY QUIET.]

Vex puts felt pads on the practice pad. The drum becomes nearly silent, but his tail taps the wall in time. Palla wraps the blender jar in a towel. The towel catches on the handle, and the jar begins spinning on the counter.

Neri tries to stop it. She whispers directions. Vex whispers back. Tavi adds a laugh track after every whisper, making the room louder.

TAVI: The audience needs to know when something is funny.

NERI: The audience can see the blender.

Palla opens the jar. The fruit is still whole. She uses the hand crank again. The handle squeaks. Omi knocks on the wall.

OMI: I can hear the handle.

PALLA: I can hear the wall.

VEX: We can move the blender to the hallway.

They carry it out. The hall light has a motion sensor; when Palla waves to keep it on, her sleeve brushes the metal railing with a long squeal. Every apartment door opens a crack. Tavi rolls the laugh-track cart toward them, and its wheel clicks like a tiny applause track.

NERI: Can we unplug the audience?

TAVI: I am on batteries.

Vex replaces his drumsticks with two soft socks. The practice pad is silent, but his elbows thump the sofa as he plays. Palla takes the blender lid off to stop the sloshing. A strawberry makes a careful escape across the counter and drops into Vex's open drum case. He closes it before it can roll out, producing the episode's loudest drum hit.

OMI: That sounded like a door slamming.

VEX: It was a fruit solo.

The laugh track starts, then abruptly cuts off when Tavi's tail lands on the mute switch. In the silence, the building manager calls Neri back. Her phone is set to play a loud ringtone. Neri catches it, but the screen displays a call from the manager and a countdown to voicemail.

NERI: We have ten seconds to be quiet loudly.

She answers, whispers into the phone, and the manager whispers back. Neri points to the sign: it says quiet hour, not no communication. Omi knocks again to ask whether the call is still happening. He can hear every whisper through the wall.

VEX: We can move the blender to the hallway.

Neri points to the quiet-hours sign. It applies to the whole building. The roommates look at each other. They each have one hour of work that requires sound: Vex's drum lesson, Palla's blender, Neri's phone call to the building manager about the sign.

### Act Three

[THE FINAL FIVE MINUTES.]

Neri calls the manager, who answers in a whisper. Vex demonstrates his rhythm by tapping two fingers on his knee. Palla decides to cut the fruit with a knife instead of blending it. The roommates coordinate silently, using hand signs. Tavi adds no laugh track.

The clock reaches eight. Omi finishes his meeting and comes out. He hears the fruit being chopped and Vex's soft rhythm.

OMI: You managed it.

NERI: We found quieter ways.

PALLA: The smoothie is now fruit salad.

VEX: The drum lesson is now finger percussion.

Omi thanks them. Tavi finally presses the laugh-track button. The sound plays after the joke is over.

TAVI: It was delayed.

OMI: That is the quietest laugh track.

Neri asks if they can make noise now. Omi says yes. Vex plays a short drum fill. Palla eats the fruit salad. Neri finishes her phone call.

### Tag

[APARTMENT. NEXT EVENING.]

The roommates plan a second quiet hour. Omi asks them to do it earlier. Tavi sits ready at the soundboard.

PALLA: Will the fruit be quieter?

VEX: We bought softer fruit.

[CANNED LAUGHTER.]

The clock reads 7:00. They all begin at once.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
