# The Surprise Is a Fire Drill

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: prank-reaction television that hides ordinary safety procedures for a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Producer Fenn plans to surprise a workplace with a fake office invasion, but safety coordinator Pala insists that every crew member follow the building's real fire-drill plan. Host Riko keeps mistaking the orderly evacuation for a prank gone wrong.

Cast: Riko, a macaque host; Fenn, a capuchin producer; Pala, a gorilla safety coordinator; and Imi, a lemur office receptionist.

## Episode: "Gotcha, Please Exit"

### Cold Open

[OFFICE BREAK ROOM. FENN CARRIES A RUBBER MASK TOWARD THE DOOR. PALA HOLDS A FLOOR PLAN.]

RIKO: Our crew will leap out and surprise the night team.

PALA: The drill begins at six. The route is posted.

FENN: We can hide beside the route.

PALA: Keep the exits clear.

[RIKO OPENS A CABINET TO HIDE. IT CONTAINS A FIRE EXTINGUISHER.]

RIKO: This cabinet has an emergency in it.

IMI: It is labeled.

### Act One

[THE CLOCK HITS SIX. A DRILL ALARM SOUNDS. THE NIGHT TEAM STANDS, TAKES THE EXIT ROUTE, AND MEETS OUTSIDE. FENN JUMPS FROM BEHIND A COAT RACK IN HIS RUBBER MASK.]

FENN: Surprise!

[THE STAFF WAVES AND CONTINUES TO THE ASSEMBLY POINT.]

IMI: We practiced this last month.

RIKO: Nobody screamed.

PALA: The goal is to leave safely.

[FENN MOVES TO THE PARKING LOT TO RESET. HE BLOCKS THE ACCESSIBLE RAMP WITH A PROP BOX. PALA ROLLS THE BOX AWAY BEFORE ANYONE APPROACHES.] 

PALA: Props stay outside the route.

FENN: It was supposed to be a reveal.

IMI: It was a box.

### Act Two

[RIKO READS A CARD: "PRANK TWO: THE OFFICE IS FULL OF SMOKE." PALA TAKES THE CARD, CROSSES OUT THE LINE, AND POINTS TO THE DRY-ICE MACHINE.] 

PALA: We do not put obscuring effects in an exit corridor.

FENN: We can use the conference room.

IMI: That room has one door.

[THE CREW MOVES TO A CLEAR TRAINING AREA. PALA SETS OUT A SIGNED ROUTE MAP. RIKO TRIES TO START A FAKE ALARM. THE REAL DRILL ALARM SOUNDS AGAIN AT ITS SCHEDULED TEST.] 

RIKO: Was that you?

PALA: The building test.

[FENN CUES A REACTION CAMERA. THE CREW MEMBERS WALK TOWARD THE ASSEMBLY POINT, CHECK THE HEADCOUNT, AND WAIT.]

RIKO: They are calm again.

IMI: We know the route.

[THE RUBBER MASK SLIPS OFF FENN'S HEAD. A STAFF MEMBER PICKS IT UP AND PUTS IT ON THE TABLE, CLEAR OF THE DOOR.]

[RIKO SETS UP A MOCK INTERVIEW AT THE ASSEMBLY POINT. FENN WAVES THE MASK BEHIND HIM TO GET A REACTION. THE STAFF MEMBER TURNS, SEES IT, AND TELLS PALA A PROP IS OUT OF PLACE.]

PALA: The prop table is back by the van.

FENN: Nobody was startled.

IMI: I was checking whether the route was clear.

[FENN PUTS THE MASK ON THE PROP TABLE. RIKO TRIES A MORE SERIOUS QUESTION.]

RIKO: What did the drill change for you?

IMI: I know where to meet my team if we have to leave.

RIKO: That is a good answer.

PALA: And the team knows who is taking attendance.

[THE OFFICE TEAM RETURNS. IMI CHECKS THE BREAK ROOM EXIT SIGN AND NOTICES THE PROP CART PARKED UNDER IT.]

IMI: This has to move before we reset.

FENN: It blocks the prank camera.

PALA: It also blocks the sign.

[FENN ROLLS THE CART BACK. THE RUBBER MASK TIPS OVER AND COVERS THE SIGN-IN SHEET. RIKO LIFTS IT OFF AND READS THE EVACUATION ROUTE ALOUD.]

RIKO: Clear hallway, marked exit, assembly point outside.

PALA: Correct.

[THE STAFF PRACTICES THE ROUTE ONCE MORE. FENN STAYS BESIDE IMI, HOLDING A DOOR OPEN INSTEAD OF WAITING TO JUMP OUT.] 

### Act Three

[RIKO STANDS BY THE ASSEMBLY POINT WITH A MICROPHONE. FENN TRIES TO CUE A SECOND SURPRISE, BUT THE CREW HAS ALREADY RETURNED TO WORK.]

RIKO: We wanted a big reaction.

PALA: The response was correct.

FENN: There is no twist?

IMI: The twist is the drill worked.

[PALA HANDS RIKO A CHECKLIST. HE EXPECTS A PRIZE CARD. IT IS A HEADCOUNT SHEET.]

RIKO: I have been assigned a task.

PALA: Count the production crew.

[RIKO COUNTS THEM. FENN HAS WANDERED BEHIND THE COAT RACK AGAIN. RIKO CALLS HIM OUT AND MARKS THE HEADCOUNT COMPLETE.]

RIKO: All present.

FENN: I was hiding for the shot.

PALA: The assembly point is not a hiding place.

[FENN TAKES THE MASK OFF AND CARRIES IT BACK TO THE PROP TABLE. THE STAFF RETURN INSIDE AFTER THE ALL-CLEAR.] 

### Tag

[RIKO LOOKS INTO THE BREAK ROOM CABINET. THE EXTINGUISHER IS STILL THERE.]

RIKO: It did not jump out.

IMI: It is not a prank.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO FENN, WHO NOW WEARS A BRIGHT VEST AND HOLDS THE HEADCOUNT SHEET.]
