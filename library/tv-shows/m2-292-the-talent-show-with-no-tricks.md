# The Talent Show with No Tricks

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised animal talent competitions, where judges expect tricks but the contestants demonstrate natural skills*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Judges search for a spectacular talent, but the contestants show climbing, listening, and seed sorting instead of stage tricks.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; judge Pava, gibbon; judge Nara, macaque; contestant Seli, capuchin; contestant Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Show Us What You Do"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. TWO CONTESTANTS WAIT BESIDE A LOW BRANCH.]

ODO: What is your special talent?

SELI: I can climb.

TAVI: I can find a seed by sound.

PAVA: Can you juggle?

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE JUDGES SIT BEHIND A TABLE.]

Odo introduces the competition. Pava says every act needs a beginning, a middle, and a finale. Nara says the contestants can choose what to demonstrate.

Seli climbs the branch and reaches a platform. Odo asks her to do it faster. Seli says she wants to check the branch first.

NARA: The branch is stable.

SELI: I am checking the bark.

PAVA: The audience is waiting.

Seli climbs smoothly, pauses to test the platform, then descends. The judges applaud.

ODO: Can you add a spin?

SELI: No.

PAVA: A dramatic pose?

SELI: I can look at the view.

### Act Two

[TAVI STANDS BESIDE A TRAY OF SEEDS.]

Tavi closes his eyes and listens as Nara shakes a small tin. He identifies which seed is largest by the sound it makes when it rolls.

NARA: Which one is the sunflower?

TAVI: The round one with the hollow tap.

Odo asks Tavi to repeat it with more drama. Tavi asks Nara to shake the tin again. The first time, the seeds do not move; the second time, one slips out.

TAVI: The sound changed.

PAVA: Can you find it?

Tavi follows the rolling seed to the edge of the stage and catches it before it falls.

ODO: A thrilling finish.

NARA: That was careful listening.

Pava asks the contestants to compete against one another. Seli can climb quickly; Tavi can hear the seed. The judges ask who wins.

SELI: We are doing different things.

[THE PRODUCER ASKS FOR A REHEARSAL.]

Pava asks Seli to climb again while Tavi shakes the seeds below. Seli reaches the platform, but the noise makes her pause. She says she cannot hear whether the branch is stable.

NARA: We need one performer at a time.

PAVA: The combined act needs both talents.

SELI: I can climb after Tavi finishes.

Tavi demonstrates the seed sound first. He sorts three seeds by size, setting each in a cup. Seli waits until the tray is quiet, then checks the branch and climbs.

ODO: The audience will see both skills.

PAVA: It loses the simultaneous effect.

NARA: It is safer and clearer.

Odo asks the stage crew to move the seed tray farther from the branch. The tray sits on a table that wobbles. Tavi steadies it with a folded cloth.

TAVI: The table has its own performance.

Pava asks for a close-up of the cups. The camera light startles Tavi, who turns toward the sound and drops one seed.

SELI: We need the light lower.

SELI: The seed rolled under the stage.

### Act Three

[THE JUDGES HUDDLE.]

Odo suggests a combined act. Seli climbs to the platform and calls down when she spots a marked seed. Tavi listens for the seed rolling in the tray and identifies its sound.

NARA: They are working together.

PAVA: The competition requires a winner.

Odo checks the rules. The show allows a team act, but it does not say how to score one. Pava gives them a shared ribbon for coordination.

SELI: We did not plan a team act.

TAVI: We were asked to find the seed.

NARA: And the seed was found.

The audience applauds. Odo asks the judges to rate the finale. Pava gives it a high score for timing. Nara praises the safe landing. Odo lifts the blank scoreboard and waits for the judges to agree on a result.

[THE JUDGES TRY TO WRITE ONE SCORE.]

Pava writes a number, then pauses. Seli points to the branch she checked; Tavi points to the seed cup he sorted. The two score cards slide together on the table and cover the single score slot.

PAVA: There is no box for two different talents.

NARA: Then do not force one score.

Odo turns the scoreboard around. Its reverse is blank. He clips the shared ribbon across the front so the contestants can each hold one end.

ODO: The team wins the team round.

SELI: We did not enter as a team.

TAVI: We became one when the seed rolled under the stage.

The audience applauds. Odo hands each contestant a half of the ribbon, but it comes apart at the seam. Seli ties the two ends back together and hangs it from the score board, not the branch.

PAVA: One result, no ranking.

NARA: Now announce it once.

ODO: The team act wins the team round.

TAVI: That is one result.

### Tag

[THE CONTESTANTS LEAVE THE STAGE.]

Pava asks if they will return next week.

SELI: I have a branch to climb.

TAVI: I have seeds to sort.

ODO: We have a talent show.

NARA: Then invite someone who likes stages.

[SFX: AUDIENCE APPLAUSE, SEED TAPS TRAY.]
