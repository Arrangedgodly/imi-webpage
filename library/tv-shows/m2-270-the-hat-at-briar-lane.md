# The Hat at Briar Lane

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cozy village mystery television, where a missing scarecrow hat brings the whole village to the tea table*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Amateur sleuth Nara investigates a missing hat from the village scarecrow, while gossip turns a simple borrowing into a scandal.

Cast: Sleuth Nara, macaque; librarian Pava, gibbon; baker Odo, baboon; gardener Seli, capuchin; constable Tavi, lemur; Pip, a young macaque parade participant.

## Episode: "The Case of the Bare Scarecrow"

### Cold Open

[LANE. A SCARECROW STANDS WITHOUT ITS HAT.]

NARA: The hat is gone.

PAVA: The scarecrow is still here.

ODO: I heard it was carried away.

SELI: Who told you?

ODO: The scarecrow.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[TEA ROOM. NARA SPREADS A MAP OF BRIAR LANE.]

Nara asks each villager when they last saw the hat. Pava says it was there at breakfast. Odo says he saw it by the fence at noon. Seli says she brought a basket through the lane and did not see it.

TAVI: That is three accounts and no hat.

NARA: There is also a rumor.

ODO: I heard the hat was stolen by a rival scarecrow.

SELI: There is no other scarecrow.

ODO: Then the rival is very secretive.

Pava says the hat belonged to the village gardening club. Nara asks who has a key to the shed where it is stored. Seli says everyone on the club has a key.

TAVI: Then the lock is not a clue.

NARA: It may still be a clue.

### Act Two

[THE GROUP SEARCHES THE LANE.]

They find a ribbon on the fence. Pava says it matches the hat band. Seli says it is a ribbon from the market basket. Odo asks if it could have been planted.

NARA: Who would plant a ribbon?

ODO: Someone with a sewing kit.

PAVA: I have a sewing kit.

TAVI: So does the constable.

Pava shows the ribbon to Nara. It is not torn; its loose end still carries the market basket tag. Nara asks who knows how to untie a knot.

SELI: Anyone.

Odo says he saw the baker's assistant carrying a bundle at noon. Tavi asks if he saw a hat in it. Odo says he saw a bundle. The rumor grows: the assistant may have taken the hat, may be opening a millinery shop, or may be planning a parade.

PAVA: We need to speak to the assistant.

NARA: We need to find out whether the hat was borrowed.

[PAVA OPENS A VILLAGE RECORD BOOK.]

The book lists the gardening club's shared supplies. The hat was checked out for last year's harvest parade, then returned with a new ribbon. Pava says the missing item may be a club matter.

PAVA: The book says "hat returned."

NARA: Does it name who returned it?

PAVA: It says "returned by hand."

ODO: Whose hand?

TAVI: Any hand.

Odo remembers seeing the assistant carry a bundle, but now says the bundle was wrapped in a tea towel. Seli says the bakery uses striped towels, not plain ones.

NARA: That is a useful detail.

PAVA: The town is full of towels.

Tavi asks the baker to show them the towel. It is plain, and still damp from covering warm bread. The assistant was carrying a striped scarf, not a hat.

ODO: The rumor has changed shape.

SELI: It is still only a bundle.

The villagers begin offering theories: the hat was used to shade seedlings, hide a loose roof tile, or mark a seat at the tea room. Nara asks each person to distinguish what they saw from what they heard.

NARA: We have three facts: the hat was on the scarecrow at breakfast, Odo saw it by the fence at noon, and now it is missing.

PAVA: We also have a rumor about a rival scarecrow.

TAVI: That is not a fact.

ODO: I heard it twice.

NARA: Repetition is not evidence.

They return to the lane to inspect the knot.

### Act Three

[THE SCARECROW IS EXAMINED CLOSELY.]

Nara checks the wooden arm. A small mark shows where the hat's string was tied. Seli spots straw caught in the knot. She says the wind may have lifted the hat, but the string was loose.

TAVI: The hat may have blown into the orchard.

Odo says the assistant was in the orchard. They walk there and find the hat on a low branch, with a note tucked inside: "Borrowed for the scarecrow parade. Returning tonight."

PAVA: The note explains the absence.

NARA: Who wrote it?

Pip steps forward and says she borrowed the hat for a parade rehearsal, then forgot to return it. Odo says the village has accused an innocent scarecrow rival.

ODO: We should apologize.

TAVI: To the scarecrow?

NARA: To the child.

The child returns the hat. Seli helps tie it securely around the scarecrow's head.

### Tag

[TEA ROOM. THE VILLAGERS GATHER.]

PAVA: The case is closed.

ODO: The rival scarecrow remains at large.

TAVI: There is no rival.

NARA: We will discuss only things we can see.

Pava closes the map. Odo whispers that the scarecrow is wearing the baker's hat now.

NARA: That is the same hat.

ODO: It looks different from this angle.

[NARA REOPENS THE FILE AND SKETCHES THE HAT FROM THREE ANGLES. TAVI QUIETLY TAKES THE MAP OFF THE TEA TRAY.]

[SFX: TEACUPS CLINK, VILLAGE BELLS.]
