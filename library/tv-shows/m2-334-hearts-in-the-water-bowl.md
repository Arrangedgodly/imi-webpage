# Hearts in the Water Bowl

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap operas, where minor household conflicts become romantic betrayal and cliffhangers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Two neighbors believe they are competing for one water bowl, unaware that a third neighbor has moved it for cleaning.

Cast: Tavi, a macaque gardener; Leni, a gibbon baker; Odo, a baboon post carrier; and Piri, a capuchin caretaker.

Episode: "The Bowl Between Us"

COLD OPEN

EXT. COURTYARD - MORNING

Leni sees Tavi carrying the blue water bowl.

LENI: You took it.

TAVI: I moved it.

Leni drops a basket of pears. A pear rolls between them.

ODO: This changes everything.

Piri enters, holding the bowl's cleaning cloth.

PIRI: It was dirty.

ACT ONE

EXT. COURTYARD - MOMENTS LATER

The theme swells as Leni explains she had planned to water the seedlings with the bowl. Tavi says she needed it to rinse her hands after gardening. Odo asks why neither neighbor asked the other.

LENI: I thought the bowl was mine.

TAVI: It belongs to the courtyard.

Piri shows them a mark on the underside. The bowl is shared property and should be rinsed after use. Leni says she was going to bake pear cakes and needed a little water. Tavi says she had already rinsed her hands.

Odo says the bowl has divided the courtyard. Piri says it is one bowl. Odo holds it dramatically at chest height as if it were a letter from a distant lover.

ACT TWO

INT. COURTYARD WASH AREA - LATER

Piri washes the bowl. Leni and Tavi stand on opposite sides of the sink, silent. Odo narrates their glances. Tavi reaches for the towel. Leni reaches too. Their hands touch.

LENI: I thought you were hiding it.

TAVI: I thought you were waiting for me to finish.

Odo gasps. Piri tells him to stop narrating. The bowl slips slightly in the wet sink. Tavi catches it. Leni steadies Tavi's elbow.

Piri dries the bowl and places it on a shelf. Leni asks if it will be free at noon. Tavi says she plans to water the seedlings then. Leni asks if she can use it after. Tavi says yes, if she leaves enough water for the plants.

Odo says the agreement has been forged. Piri says it is a schedule. He writes Tavi at noon and Leni after lunch on a small slate. The bowl is returned to the shelf.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. COURTYARD - AFTERNOON

Tavi waters the seedlings. The water bowl is half full. Leni arrives with a jug. She asks if she can borrow the remaining water. Tavi says the plants need one more row. Leni waits while Tavi finishes. Odo watches from behind a post.

ODO: The silence grows.

PIRI: They are waiting.

Leni carries the bowl to the wash area after the watering is done. She rinses it and returns it to the shelf. Tavi brings her a pear cake. Leni says it is still warm. Tavi says she baked it after they agreed on the schedule.

Odo wonders if the cake is a peace offering or a declaration. Leni offers him a slice. Piri asks whether he will eat it or narrate it.

Odo asks Tavi whether she thinks Leni and Tavi are becoming friends. Tavi says they have shared a bowl and a schedule. Odo calls it a beginning. Piri says friendship does not need a cliffhanger. Just then the bowl rolls off the shelf. Tavi catches it before it hits the floor. Leni reaches out to help, and their hands meet again. Odo gasps. Piri says the shelf is uneven. Nara tests it and finds one corner raised. Tavi fetches a felt pad. The bowl sits level. Leni asks if they should write down that repair too. Piri adds one line to the sign: "If the shelf wobbles, tell the caretaker." Odo insists this is a new secret between them. Tavi says everyone heard it.

Piri checks the bowl again. It is level and clean.

ACT THREE

EXT. COURTYARD - SUNSET

The neighbors sit together. Piri writes a sign: "Shared bowl. Please rinse and return." Tavi adds the watering times. Leni adds the baking schedule. Odo writes his name beneath both, though he has not used the bowl.

PIRI: You do not need to reserve it.

ODO: I am reserving a slice of cake.

Tavi gives him one. The bowl remains on the shelf, clean and available. Leni and Tavi agree that they should ask before assuming the other has taken something.

TAG

Odo picks up the bowl.

PIRI: What are you doing?

ODO: Taking it to the sink.

Piri watches him rinse it too thoroughly.
