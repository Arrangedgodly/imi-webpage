# River School: What Is a Current?

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: educational nature television, where a host tries to teach about water flow while the crew keeps staging dramatic demonstrations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Field host Luma brings viewers to a stream to explain currents, but the crew's props keep interrupting the lesson.

Cast: Host Luma, a lemur; scientist Nara, a macaque; camera operator Odo, a baboon; student Piri, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Follow the Water"

### Cold Open

[STREAMBANK. LUMA HOLDS A LEAF.]

LUMA: A current is water moving in a direction.

ODO: Can we make it more dramatic?

NARA: It is already moving.

PIRI: The leaf is gone.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[LUMA STANDS BESIDE A SHALLOW STREAM.]

LUMA: Watch this leaf. I will place it in the water and see where it goes.

She lets it go. The leaf circles in a small eddy and returns to her feet.

PIRI: It came back.

NARA: The current moves around the rock, then circles behind it.

ODO: We need a stronger shot.

He places a larger leaf upstream. It moves quickly past the camera and gets stuck on Odo's boot.

LUMA: The current carried it downstream.

ODO: It carried it to me.

PIRI: You are standing in the stream.

Nara asks Odo to step to the bank. The leaf floats free again.

### Act Two

[THE CREW SETS UP A CLEAR TUBE FILLED WITH WATER.]

Luma shows how a small paper scrap moves through the tube. Odo adds a spoonful of dye to make the current visible.

NARA: Use one drop.

ODO: I used one spoonful.

The water turns dark blue.

LUMA: We cannot see through it now.

PIRI: The current is hidden.

NARA: It is still flowing.

Odo tips the tube. The blue water spills onto the sand.

[SFX: WATER POUR.]

ODO: We have a flood.

LUMA: We have a spill.

Piri uses a cloth to absorb it. The water moves through the cloth more slowly than through the sand. Nara explains that different materials absorb water at different rates.

PIRI: The cloth is a small riverbank.

NARA: It is a towel.

[LUMA SETS TWO SMALL FLAGS BESIDE THE STREAM.]

Nara asks Piri to predict which flag the leaf will pass first. Piri chooses the one beside the center channel. Odo places a second leaf at the same spot. One leaf drifts toward the flag; the other circles behind a rock.

PIRI: They started together.

NARA: The water does not move at the same speed everywhere.

ODO: Can we make them race?

LUMA: We can observe them. We do not need a winner.

Odo asks for a slow-motion replay. Odo's camera captures the leaf passing behind the rock, then returning into view.

ODO: The leaf disappeared.

NARA: It was behind the rock.

PIRI: The current carried it around.

Luma asks Piri to move one flag to mark the eddy. He places it too close to the water; it falls in and drifts downstream.

PIRI: The marker has joined the current.

NARA: Retrieve it from the bank, not from the water.

Piri walks along the edge and catches it in reeds.

### Act Three

[THE CLASS RETURNS TO THE STREAM.]

Luma asks Piri to find a place where the current is slow. Piri chooses a wide shallow stretch. Nara says the water spreads out there and moves more gently.

ODO: I can add a rock for a dramatic fast section.

NARA: There are rocks already.

Odo tries to place one in the water, but it shifts. Piri stops him and finds a stable stone on the bank.

LUMA: We can observe without changing the stream.

PIRI: The current is fast near the middle and slow beside the reeds.

NARA: The shape of the channel affects the flow.

Odo films the leaf again. This time he stays on the bank. It drifts around a rock and moves downstream.

Before the tag, Odo offers to place a small floating marker in the stream. Nara says they should use a fallen leaf instead. The leaf moves faster when released in the center and slowly near the bank.

LUMA: Both paths reach the same place.

PIRI: The fast leaf got there first.

NARA: We are not timing them.

ODO: The camera is already recording.

PIRI: Then the camera can watch the water.

Nara picks up the fallen marker from the reeds and sets it on the bank.

### Tag

[LUMA FACES CAMERA.]

LUMA: A current is water moving in a direction.

ODO: And sometimes around a boot.

PIRI: If the boot is in the stream.

NARA: The camera is on the bank.

LUMA: The lesson is also on the bank.

PIRI: I will remember the leaf.

ODO: We have more than one take.

[SFX: WATER RUNS. LEAF TAPS A STONE.]
