# The Contestant Who Read the Rules

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised cooking competitions that spring arbitrary twists during a timed round*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: On a live dumpling contest, contestant Ena follows the posted recipe and discovers the judges changed the rules after the clock started. Host Riff wants a frantic twist reveal; floor judge Omu wants the contestants to know what they are actually being scored on.

Cast: Riff, a macaque host; Ena, a red colobus cook; Omu, a gorilla judge; and Zed, a capuchin stage manager.

## Episode: "The Surprise Ingredient"

### Cold Open

[COOKING SET. FOUR STATIONS HOLD FLOUR, CABBAGE, AND STEAMERS. A CLOCK STARTS AT 30:00.]

RIFF: Make a family-style dumpling dish using the ingredients on your table.

ENA: Is there a written rubric?

OMU: It is on the board behind you.

[THE BOARD SAYS "TASTE, SAFE COOKING, AND CLEAR PRESENTATION." ZED UNVEILS A SMALL BOWL OF LICORICE ROOT.]

RIFF: Surprise ingredient!

ENA: Is it part of the required ingredients?

ZED: It is part of the television.

### Act One

[THE CLOCK TICKS. ENA READS THE BOWL LABEL WHILE THE OTHER COOKS STARE AT THE CAMERA.]

[THE CAMERA CUTS TO A SECOND STATION. A CONTESTANT WAVES THE LICORICE BOWL AT THE LENS. OMI CHECKS THE RECIPE CARD.]

OMU: No one has to use it.

RIFF: But it is the twist.

ENA: A twist can be optional.

[ZED SLIDES A NEW SCORECARD UNDER THE CAMERA. IT HAS A BLANK CATEGORY LABELED "SURPRISE." ENA LOOKS AT THE THREE CRITERIA STILL ON THE BOARD.]

ENA: Which card are we being judged by?

OMU: The posted one.

[RIFF TRIES TO HOLD BOTH SCORECARDS UP. THE PAPERS COVER HIS FACE. HE LOWERS THE NEW CARD.]

RIFF: One rubric at a time.

ENA: This is an optional spice. The label says use sparingly.

RIFF: Can you make it the star?

OMU: The challenge did not say that.

[RIFF TURNS TO THE CAMERA AND HOLDS UP THE BOWL. ZED'S PROMPTER SHOWS "EVERYONE PANIC." OMI TURNS IT OFF.] 

RIFF: We need urgency.

ENA: My dough needs to rest.

[ENA COVERS THE DOUGH AND SETS A TIMER. RIFF LOOKS AT THE MAIN CLOCK, THEN MOVES A CAMERA TO FRAME THE RESTING BOWL.] 

RIFF: The dough is resting under pressure.

OMU: It is covered.

### Act Two

[ZED BRINGS AN ENVELOPE TO THE JUDGING TABLE.]

ZED: The producer note says the mystery ingredient must appear in every dish.

OMU: The contestants never received that instruction.

RIFF: Can we announce it now?

ENA: The clock has less than ten minutes.

OMU: We will score the posted challenge. The note can wait for next week.

[RIFF TEARS OPEN THE ENVELOPE. IT CONTAINS A SECOND NOTE THAT SAYS "HOLD FOR DRAMATIC REVEAL." HE LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.] 

RIFF: The twist has a twist.

ENA: My steamer is ready.

[ENA PLACES HER DUMPLINGS IN THE STEAMER. THE LICORICE BOWL IS STILL SEALED. ZED TRIES TO PUT IT NEXT TO HER PLATE. SHE SLIDES IT BACK TO THE PROP TABLE.] 

ENA: I did not choose it, so I will not serve it.

OMU: Correct.

[THE CLOCK HITS ZERO. RIFF SWEEPS INTO THE FRAME WITH A BELL. THE BELL'S HANDLE FALLS OFF.]

[THE BROKEN BELL ROLLS UNDER ENA'S STATION. ZED RETRIEVES IT WHILE SHE SETS HER DISH ON THE MARKED PLATE. THE OTHER CONTESTANTS FINISH THEIR OWN RECIPES, NOT THE OPTIONAL SPICE.]

RIFF: The whole kitchen resisted the twist.

OMU: The kitchen followed the rules.

[ENA FINDS A SMALL PIECE OF DOUGH STUCK TO THE BOTTOM OF HER APRON. SHE ROLLS IT INTO A SAMPLE FOR THE JUDGES. RIFF REACHES FOR THE LICORICE BOWL, BUT OMU SLIDES THE SEALED BOWL INTO THE PROP CART.]

ENA: That is the last dumpling. No surprise filling.

RIFF: The twist has left the kitchen.

### Act Three

[OMU TASTES ENA'S DUMPLING. HE TAKES NOTES ON A CARD WITH THE THREE POSTED CRITERIA.]

OMU: The wrapper is even. The filling is seasoned. You cooked it through.

RIFF: But what about the surprise ingredient?

OMU: It was not on the challenge board.

ZED: Should I put it on next time?

ENA: Put it on the board before the timer starts.

[RIFF TRIES TO HOLD A SUSPENSEFUL PAUSE. OMU PASSES THE SCORECARD TO ENA. SHE HAS WON THE ROUND.] 

RIFF: The results are in.

ENA: We can read them.

[THE OTHER COOKS APPLAUD. ZED TAKES THE BOWL TO THE PANTRY, WHERE A SECOND SEALED BOWL WAITS WITH A LABEL: "NEXT WEEK?" HE CROSSES OUT THE QUESTION MARK AND WRITES "ASK FIRST."]

### Tag

[RIFF OPENS THE ENVELOPE AGAIN, HOPING FOR ANOTHER TWIST. IT IS EMPTY.] 

RIFF: The suspense is over.

OMU: Good. We can wash the steamer.
