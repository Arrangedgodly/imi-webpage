# The Debate Club Has a Turnip

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised student debate, where contestants argue policy positions on whether a turnip should be a school mascot*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: The student debate team is assigned to defend opposing positions on the turnip mascot, but the turnip keeps rolling off the podium.

Cast: Moderator Seli, gibbon; debater Nara, macaque; debater Odo, baboon; timer Piri, capuchin; advisor Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "For the Turnip"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. A TURNIP SITS BETWEEN TWO PODIUMS.]

SELI: Should the school adopt a turnip as its mascot?

ODO: Yes.

NARA: No.

PIRI: The turnip is rolling.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE DEBATERS TAKE THEIR PLACES.]

Seli explains the format: opening statement, rebuttal, and audience question. Odo argues the turnip is sturdy and local. Nara says it is difficult to draw and does not represent the students.

ODO: It grows in the school garden.

NARA: So do onions.

SELI: The debate is about the turnip.

Tavi asks each debater to define "mascot." Odo says it is a symbol. Nara says it is something people can recognize.

PIRI: The timer is ready.

Odo begins his opening, but the turnip rolls toward Nara. She catches it.

NARA: The candidate has crossed the floor.

ODO: It is not a candidate.

### Act Two

[THE AUDIENCE QUESTIONS BEGIN.]

A student asks whether the turnip can appear on a banner. Odo says yes. Nara asks who will draw it. Odo volunteers, then struggles to sketch the shape.

ODO: It is a round vegetable.

NARA: A turnip is not round.

PIRI: Your time is up.

Odo tries to keep speaking. Seli moves to the rebuttal. Nara says the turnip is hard to carry and could bruise. Odo says it can be put in a basket.

Tavi brings a basket. The turnip fits, but hides the school seal beneath it.

SELI: The mascot has covered the school.

ODO: It is protecting the seal.

NARA: It is sitting on it.

[THE MODERATOR CALLS A RECESS.]

Piri stops the clock while the debaters prepare a diagram. Odo draws a large turnip on the board. It resembles a potato.

ODO: The resemblance is close enough.

NARA: The school has a potato already.

SELI: We do?

Tavi says the cafeteria serves potatoes every Friday. Odo argues that the mascot can be different from the menu. Nara says students will confuse the symbols.

PIRI: We can add leaves.

Odo draws leaves. The turnip now resembles a beet.

NARA: We also have beets in the garden.

ODO: The garden is full of possible mascots.

Seli asks the audience to name what makes a mascot distinct. One student says color; another says a story. Nara proposes that the school create a character who represents both the garden and the students.

SELI: That may be beyond today's motion.

TAVI: The debate has become a design session.

### Act Three

[THE DEBATERS PREPARE THEIR CLOSING ARGUMENTS.]

Nara proposes a seedling as mascot. Odo says it is too small to see. Seli asks what makes a mascot useful. The students say it should be easy to draw, carry, and recognize.

Odo admits the turnip is not easy to draw. Nara admits it is easy to recognize when it is on the table.

PIRI: The debate has reached common ground.

The turnip rolls off the basket and into the aisle. Odo catches it.

ODO: We can use a picture.

NARA: A drawing of the turnip?

ODO: Yes.

Nara says a mascot should represent the school, not just the garden. Tavi suggests a sprout growing out of a turnip. The debaters agree to test the design.

[THE AUDIENCE VOTES ON THE MOTION.]

Seli reads the motion aloud: "The school should adopt a turnip as its official mascot." Odo says yes. Nara says no, but proposes a garden symbol instead.

PIRI: The audience will vote on the motion, not the alternative.

TAVI: We need a separate motion for that.

Odo asks if his drawing can count as evidence. Seli says it can be shown but not used as proof that everyone recognizes a turnip.

NARA: The question is whether the school can identify it.

SELI: The drawing looks like a potato.

ODO: It is an early draft.

Piri counts the audience votes. The original motion loses by two hands. Nara's garden-symbol proposal receives more support, but Seli says it must be introduced at the next meeting.

PIRI: We have a result and a new agenda item.

### Tag

[THE BOARD SHOWS A SIMPLE TURNIP WITH A LEAF.]

Seli asks the audience to vote. Most raise their hands for the design.

PIRI: The turnip has won.

NARA: The drawing has.

Odo sets the turnip on the basket. It rolls again.

[SFX: TURNIP TAPS PODIUM. APPLAUSE.]
