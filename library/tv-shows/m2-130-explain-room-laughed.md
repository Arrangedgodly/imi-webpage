# Explain Why the Room Laughed

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised stand-up contests that score comedy with panels, replay buttons, and demands for contestants to explain their jokes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Dako wants a measurable winner for the Grove Laugh-Off. Judges Mela and Riff keep requesting explanations after each joke, while contestant Turi tries to finish a set without annotating it.

Cast: Dako, a macaque host; Turi, a lemur comic; Mela, a gorilla judge; Riff, a capuchin judge; and Sola, a tamarin stage manager.

## Episode: "Hold for Laughter"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. DAKO STANDS BESIDE A BUZZER AND A SCOREBOARD.]

DAKO: One joke, one score. The loudest laugh wins.

[TURI WALKS TO THE MIC.]

TURI: I bought a quiet alarm clock. It wakes only me.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. RIFF PRESSES THE BUZZER.]

RIFF: Explain the word "quiet."

TURI: It is quiet.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS AGAIN. DAKO LOOKS AT THE SCOREBOARD.]

DAKO: That is two laughs, but one joke.

SOLA: We may need another category.

### Act One

[BEFORE THE SHOW CONTINUES, SOLA CHECKS THE MIC CABLE. THE BUZZER SOUNDS WHEN SHE PLUGS IT IN.]

RIFF: That was a strong opening.

SOLA: That was feedback.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. DAKO WRITES "CABLE" ON THE SCOREBOARD, THEN CROSSES IT OUT.]

[THE SHOW CONTINUES. TURI TRIES A SECOND JOKE.]

TURI: My neighbor installed a motion light. Now the porch turns on whenever I go outside to look for the motion light.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. MELA RAISES A CARD.]

MELA: Did the neighbor install it for safety or convenience?

TURI: I do not know. I am just trying to get the mail.

[RIFF REPLAYS THE JOKE ON A MONITOR. THE LAUGHTER IS MUTED.]

RIFF: The laugh starts before the second mention of the light.

DAKO: Can you do the joke again and pause for the score?

TURI: That would change the timing.

SOLA: It would also make the audience wait while the judges discuss.

[THE TIMER ON STAGE STARTS. TURI LOOKS AT IT.]

TURI: How long do I have?

DAKO: Until the red light goes off.

TURI: What happens then?

SOLA: We switch to the next segment.

TURI: Good. I was worried the timer had a bigger role than me.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. THE RED LIGHT FLASHES. DAKO DOES NOT CUT TO COMMERCIAL.]

TURI: Is this part of my act?

DAKO: It is the show clock.

TURI: Then my next joke is about the show clock.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. THE SCOREBOARD ADDS A POINT TO THE TIMER.]

### Act Two

[MELA AND RIFF SIT AT A TABLE WITH SCORECARDS.]

MELA: We need to judge originality, delivery, and audience response.

RIFF: I have a scale from one to ten.

MELA: What is a seven?

RIFF: It is between six and eight.

[TURI WAITS AT THE MIC.]

TURI: I can tell another joke while you decide.

DAKO: We need a clean scoring interval.

TURI: My interval is going to be standing here.

[THE AUDIENCE CHUCKLES. RIFF PRESSES THE BUZZER.]

TURI: Was that a score or a doorbell?

RIFF: I am signaling for clarification.

TURI: The signal needs a clarification.

[MELA TAKES THE BUZZER AND PLACES IT UNDER HER SCORECARD.]

RIFF: We need to know why that is funny.

TURI: Because you are still holding the buzzer.

[MELA LOOKS AT RIFF'S HAND. HE IS PRESSING IT.]

MELA: That is an observation, not a joke.

TURI: It got a laugh.

SOLA: We could measure the room instead of making the performer repeat.

[SOLA HOLDS UP A SIMPLE SOUND METER. THE NEEDLE MOVES WHEN THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS.]

DAKO: The room gave that line a seven.

RIFF: A seven out of what?

SOLA: The audience.

### Act Three

[THE LAST CONTESTANT HAS FINISHED. DAKO READS THE SCORES.]

DAKO: Turi has the most audience laughter. The judges' average is a tie.

TURI: Who won?

MELA: The audience response was highest.

RIFF: The panel did not reach consensus.

DAKO: The audience is still in the room.

[THE CROWD APPLAUDS. RIFF STARTS TO ASK FOR AN EXPLANATION, THEN LOWERS THE BUZZER.]

MELA: The timing made the answer funnier.

RIFF: I was going to give it an eight.

DAKO: Out of what?

RIFF: I am still deciding.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS AT THE SCORECARD. SOLA TURNS IT AROUND SO THE JUDGES CANNOT SEE IT.]

RIFF: I think I understand.

TURI: Do you?

RIFF: No, but I am letting the laugh finish.

[MELA WRITES "TIMING" ON HER SCORECARD. DAKO REMOVES THE REPLAY BUTTON FROM THE TABLE.]

[TURI THANKS THE HOUSE BAND. THEY PLAY A SHORT CHORD. RIFF DOES NOT REACH FOR THE BUZZER.]

TURI: That was the ending.

RIFF: I heard it.

### Tag

[THE CREDITS BEGIN. THE AUDIENCE IS STILL LAUGHING AT THE SOUND METER.]

DAKO: We cannot score that one.

SOLA: Then leave it off the chart.

[RIFF LOOKS AT THE NEEDLE. IT IS STILL MOVING.]
