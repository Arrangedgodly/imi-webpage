# The Branch Caucus

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: political satire programs and televised committees that replace practical work with talking points*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A forest council broadcasts a debate over repairing a footbridge. Each representative arrives with polished slogans, but the bridge's actual users interrupt the process with immediate needs.

Cast: Chair Rill, a mandrill; representative Omi, a macaque; representative Tova, a gibbon; clerk Ness, a capuchin; and young residents Pip and Sela.

## Episode: "Motion to Cross"

### Cold Open

[THE COUNCIL SITS BEHIND A TABLE. A MODEL BRIDGE SITS BETWEEN THEM.]

RILL: We are here to discuss the bridge.

OMI: My platform is progress.

TOVA: My platform is stability.

[THE MODEL BRIDGE COLLAPSES WHEN NESS SETS A CUP ON IT.]

NESS: The bridge has filed an amendment.

### Act One

[THE CLERK PASSES OUT SPEAKING CARDS.]

RILL: Each member has two minutes.

OMI: We must build a bridge that connects tomorrow to opportunity.

TOVA: We must preserve the strength of yesterday.

PIP: The bridge is broken today.

RILL: The residents will have a chance to speak.

SELA: We need to carry water across before the afternoon heat.

NESS: I have recorded the request.

OMI: We can call it the Water Access Initiative.

TOVA: I support access, subject to further review.

PIP: Can you fix the loose plank?

[OMI AND TOVA LOOK AT THE MODEL BRIDGE.]

### Act Two

[THE COUNCIL WALKS TO THE ACTUAL BRIDGE. A PLANK LIFTS AT ONE END.]

RILL: The bridge is closed until inspection.

OMI: We can issue a statement.

TOVA: We should form a task group.

PIP: The water is on the other side.

[THE CHILDREN SET DOWN THEIR BUCKETS.]

OMI: We can make an emergency motion.

NESS: The agenda says motions are only accepted after recess.

TOVA: We can move recess forward.

RILL: That requires a second.

[THE TWO REPRESENTATIVES RAISE THEIR HANDS AT THE SAME TIME.]

RILL: The motion passes.

[THE GROUP TAKES A SAFE PATH TO A TOOL SHED. RILL AND THE CHILDREN CARRY A REPLACEMENT PLANK.]

OMI: I can hold one side.

TOVA: I can secure the other.

PIP: We need a hammer.

NESS: I brought the ceremonial gavel.

RILL: The gavel is not a hammer.

[NESS HANDS OVER A HAMMER FROM THE SHED.]

[NESS BRINGS OUT A STACK OF DRAFT STATEMENTS.]

NESS: I prepared three versions: one for the residents, one for the press, and one for the council archive.

RILL: Read the resident version.

NESS: "The crossing is temporarily unavailable due to plank-related circumstances."

PIP: It is broken.

OMI: We can make that clearer.

TOVA: "The bridge needs repair."

SELA: That is clear.

OMI: We should say the repair is underway.

[OMI HOLDS UP THE REPLACEMENT PLANK.]

RILL: The plank is still on the ground.

OMI: Then the statement is aspirational.

NESS: Should I put "aspirational" in the archive?

RILL: Put the plank on the bridge.

[THEY LIFT THE PLANK. OMI HOLDS ONE END, TOVA THE OTHER, WHILE RILL HAMMERS THE FASTENER.]

PIP: The residents can cross when it is secure.

OMI: That is a good line.

TOVA: It is also what we are doing.

[THE COUNCIL REVIEWS THE REVISED NOTICE.]

NESS: "Bridge closed for repair. Use the east path until the plank is secure."

PIP: That tells us what to do.

OMI: It has no title.

RILL: It does not need one.

TOVA: We should post it at both ends.

[THE RESIDENTS TAKE THE NOTICE TO THE PATH. THE COUNCIL FOLLOWS WITH THE TOOLBOX.]

NESS: Do I record this as public outreach?

RILL: Record it as putting up a sign.

[THEY NAIL THE NOTICE TO A POST. A WIND GUST FLIPS IT BACKWARD.]

PIP: The sign is facing the forest.

OMI: I will turn it around.

[OMI TURNS IT. THE NOTICE NOW FACES THE PATH.]

TOVA: A successful motion.

RILL: It was a nail.

### Act Three

[THEY TEST THE REPLACEMENT PLANK WITH AN EMPTY BASKET. RILL COMPLETES THE INSPECTION AND SIGNS THE TOOL-SHED CHECKLIST.]

RILL: The bridge is usable. Inspection complete.

OMI: We can name it the Access Bridge.

TOVA: Stability Crossing.

SELA: Can it be called the bridge?

[THE COUNCIL LOOKS AT THE NEW PLANK.]

RILL: The residents will decide.

PIP: We call it the bridge because we can cross it.

[THE GROUP CARRIES WATER OVER, ONE BUCKET AT A TIME.]

OMI: I will submit a report on the repair.

TOVA: I will co-sign it.

NESS: The report needs a name.

RILL: "Bridge repaired."

OMI: Could we add a subtitle?

RILL: No.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS ON THE REPORT. IT READS "BRIDGE REPAIRED, 3:14 PM." THE GAVEL SLIPS OFF THE TABLE AND LANDS IN THE TOOLBOX.]

NESS: The gavel has joined the maintenance budget.
