# The Long Way to the Orchard

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentary television that treats minor detours as epic journeys and confuses travel with constant narration*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A travel host journeys to a nearby orchard with a camera crew. Each detour is narrated as a major expedition, while a local guide keeps pointing out the sensible short route.

Cast: Host Jori, a lemur; guide Kavi, a macaque; camera operator Bim, a gibbon; and porter Rua, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Across the Great Hill"

### Cold Open

[THE CREW STANDS BEFORE A LOW RIDGE. THE ORCHARD IS VISIBLE ON THE OTHER SIDE.]

JORI: We begin a journey into the unknown.

KAVI: The orchard is over that hill.

BIM: We can see it.

JORI: The unknown is still ahead.

### Act One

[JORI UNFOLDS A LARGE MAP.]

KAVI: The path is marked in blue.

JORI: We will take the historic route.

KAVI: That route goes around the hill.

JORI: Every great traveler takes the scenic way.

[RUA LIFTS A HEAVY BAG.]

RUA: What is in this?

JORI: Spare maps, an extra scarf, and a decorative compass.

KAVI: We do not need the compass.

JORI: It points north.

KAVI: The orchard is west.

[THE CREW STARTS AROUND THE HILL. JORI NARRATES WHILE WALKING.]

JORI: The terrain tests our resolve.

BIM: The path is flat.

JORI: It tests our patience.

### Act Two

[THEY REACH A FORK. BOTH PATHS LEAD TO THE ORCHARD.]

JORI: The map offers two routes.

KAVI: Take the left path. It avoids the muddy bank.

JORI: We will consult the compass.

[JORI HOLDS THE COMPASS. ITS NEEDLE WOBBLES.]

KAVI: It is stuck to the metal buckle.

[RUA MOVES THE BAG. THE NEEDLE POINTS WEST.]

JORI: The instrument has found its direction.

KAVI: You moved the bag.

[THEY TAKE THE LEFT PATH. BIM SETS UP A TIME-LAPSE CAMERA.]

BIM: We should capture the cloud crossing the sun.

JORI: It marks the passage of time.

KAVI: It marks lunch.

[RUA OPENS THE BAG. A BANANA ROLLS DOWNHILL.]

JORI: Our provisions escape.

[RUA CATCHES THE BANANA WITH A FOOT.]

RUA: The bag was open.

JORI: Our porter has saved the expedition.

RUA: I closed the bag.

[AT THE ORCHARD GATE, THE KEEPER EXPLAINS WHICH TREES ARE READY TO PICK.]

KEEPER: Take only the ripe figs. Leave the rest for the troop.

JORI: The orchard has its own travel etiquette.

KAVI: It has fruit that is not ready.

[RUA HOLDS THE BASKET WHILE JORI REACHES FOR A FIG. THE BRANCH BENDS DOWN.]

KAVI: Do not pull the branch.

JORI: I was reaching.

[RUA STEADIES THE BRANCH. JORI TAKES A RIPE FIG AND LEAVES THE GREEN ONES.]

JORI: A small harvest.

KEEPER: Enough for the walk back.

[BIM FILMS A CLOSE-UP. A MONKEY FROM THE ORCHARD TROOP TAKES ONE FIG FROM THE BASKET.]

JORI: We have been robbed.

KAVI: The orchard troop has a claim.

[RUA DIVIDES THE FIGS BETWEEN THE BASKET AND THE TROOP.]

[KAVI POINTS TO THE RETURN PATH.]

KAVI: We can carry the basket on the shaded side.

JORI: The documentary will be shorter on the way back.

BIM: We have already filmed the hill.

RUA: We can use the time to return the camera case to the cart.

[JORI LOOKS AT THE BAG. THE COMPASS IS STILL IN HER POCKET.]

JORI: I can carry this.

KAVI: The compass?

JORI: The map. It is still purple.

[THE CREW TAKES THE SHORT PATH, FOLLOWING THE SHADE. THE ORCHARD TROOP FOLLOWS AT A DISTANCE.]

JORI: The return trip has no new route, but it has a lighter bag.

[JORI HANDS THE COMPASS TO KAVI BEFORE THEY START WALKING.]

### Act Three

[THEY ARRIVE AT THE ORCHARD. THE ORCHARD KEEPER HANDS THEM A BASKET.]

JORI: We have crossed a great distance.

KAVI: We walked twenty minutes.

JORI: The journey was rich in detail.

[JORI BITES A FIG. JUICE DRIPS ON THE MAP.]

BIM: The blue path is now purple.

JORI: The map records our arrival.

KAVI: It records lunch.

[RUA USES A LEAF TO WIPE THE MAP.]

JORI: What did we learn?

KAVI: Ask a local before choosing a route.

RUA: Close the bag.

BIM: Check whether the compass is stuck.

JORI: Those are practical lessons.

KAVI: The orchard is practical.

[THEY WALK BACK ON THE SHORT PATH. JORI FOLLOWS THE GUIDE WITHOUT NARRATING. AFTER THREE STEPS, SHE TURNS TO THE CAMERA.]

JORI: We now enter the return leg.

KAVI: It is the same path.

JORI: The journey has come full circle.

[THEY ARRIVE AT THE STARTING POINT. RUA OPENS THE BAG. THE COMPASS IS GONE.]

JORI: A new mystery awaits.

KAVI: It is in your pocket.

[JORI PULLS OUT THE COMPASS. THE NEEDLE POINTS AT HER.]
