# The Quiz Show Has a Memory Round

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television quiz shows that turn ordinary recall into dramatic suspense and celebrity trivia*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Vex, a macaque; contestant Nari, a lemur; contestant Pip, a capuchin; scorekeeper Odo, a gibbon; floor assistant Leni, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. QUIZ SHOW STUDIO - NIGHT

[Three empty podiums face a board of covered answers. Vex holds a card.] 

VEX: Contestants, name something you saw five minutes ago.

NARI: The card in your hand.

VEX: That was not one of the approved categories.

[The board lights up: CELEBRITY PETS. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

VEX: Welcome to Recall Royale, where the answers are easy and the lights suggest otherwise.

ODO: We have one point for Nari.

LENI: The round has not begun.

ODO: She remembered the card.

[The contestants take their places. Vex explains the rules: he will show an image for three seconds, then ask a question about it. He displays a photograph of a blue bowl with three seeds.] 

VEX: What color was the bowl?

NARI: Blue.

VEX: What was inside?

PIP: Three seeds.

VEX: What was the name of the bowl's owner?

PIP: It did not have a name.

VEX: Incorrect. It was called Kevin.

NARI: Who named it?

VEX: The producers.

[Odo places a point token on Vex's podium by mistake. Vex covers it with his card.] 

ACT TWO

[The next image is a close-up of a green leaf. Nari squints.] 

VEX: Name the celebrity who owns this leaf.

NARI: It came from a plant.

VEX: The plant is famous.

PIP: Is the leaf a celebrity?

VEX: It was in the background of a cooking show.

[The studio fan blows the leaf off the monitor. Leni catches it.]

LENI: This is a real leaf.

ODO: It has no agent.

VEX: Final answer?

NARI: It is a leaf.

[The board reveals a celebrity chef holding a bowl of greens. Vex celebrates the correct answer. Odo awards half a point for "leaf."]

PIP: Why half?

ODO: It was half visible.

[For the last question, Vex shows a group photo for three seconds. It is the contestants standing in the studio before the game.]

VEX: Which of these people is wearing a yellow scarf?

NARI: Leni.

LENI: I am not in the photo.

[They examine the image. The scarf is on a chair behind the podium. Vex insists that it belongs to a famous guest.] 

VEX: It was worn by the star of our pilot episode.

PIP: The scarf was on the chair before we arrived.

NARI: I remember it because it kept falling off.

[The chair shifts; the scarf slides to the floor. Odo picks it up and drapes it over Vex's shoulder.]

VEX: I am the star now.

[Leni lays out the revealed image cards: the blue bowl, the leaf, the scarf, and the contestants. Odo makes a clean answer sheet.]

ODO: We can score only what appeared on the board.

VEX: The scarf appeared in the room.

NARI: It was not on the board.

[Vex drapes the scarf over the answer sheet. The blue bowl card slides off the podium and lands beside Pip.]

PIP: I remember this one. Three seeds.

NARI: You were right the first time.

[Odo turns over the blue bowl card. It has a handwritten note on the back: "Kevin."] 

ODO: The production named the bowl after the photograph was taken.

VEX: Then Kevin is a celebrity.

LENI: The bowl was still blue before it got a name.

[The contestants place one answer card under each category. Nari puts "blue" under color, Pip puts "three seeds" under contents, and Vex places the scarf under "self portrait."] 

VEX: I am wearing it now.

NARI: That is not a memory.

VEX: I remembered to put it on.

ACT THREE

[The final round asks contestants to recall the answers that have already been given. Vex turns his back to the board and covers his ears.] 

VEX: What color was the bowl?

NARI: Blue.

VEX: Wrong. It was Kevin.

PIP: Kevin was the bowl's name.

ODO: The bowl was blue Kevin.

[The scorekeeper flips the board. It shows "BLUE KEVIN" as one answer. Nari points to the wording.]

NARI: The question asked for a color.

VEX: The answer contains a color.

NARI: Then accept blue.

[Odo checks the rules card. It says "Accept answers that fit the category." He hands it to Vex.] 

VEX: The category is now "things in the answer."

LENI: That is not a category.

[The fan blows the card from Vex's hand and lands it on the scorekeeper's podium. Odo reads the rule aloud.]

ODO: The contestant with the most correct answers wins.

NARI: Then I have three.

PIP: I have two and half a leaf.

[Odo totals the board. Vex has one point for the scarf, which he wore himself.]

TAG

VEX: Join us next week for the history round.

NARI: Will the questions be about history?

VEX: The memory round is still open.
