# The Dog Show Awards the Whistle

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised dog competitions that let whistles and scoreboards reward the wrong performer*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Pavi, a macaque; handler Sela, a lemur; judge Toma, an orangutan; sound operator Riko, a gibbon; dog Miso, a dog; stage manager Nala, a capuchin; non-speaking camera operator.

COLD OPEN

INT. DOG SHOW ARENA - DAY

Miso sits on a mat. Pavi blows a whistle. The scoreboard awards ten points to the whistle.

PAVI: What a response.

SELA: Miso did not move.

ACT ONE

INT. ARENA - CONTINUOUS

Riko says the whistle cue is connected to the scoring bell. Judge Toma checks the rules. The dog earns points for completing the course; the whistle only starts the run. Pavi asks Sela to demonstrate. She waits for the judge's signal. Riko blows the whistle; the scoreboard flashes a trophy.

Sela asks Riko to mute it. The whistle still sounds through the arena speaker. Miso looks at the speaker, then at Sela. Pavi calls it an impressive alert response. Toma points to the scoring sheet, which has no category for speakers.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Riko follows the audio cable. It leads from the whistle mic to a sound effect marked VICTORY. He disconnects it. Pavi tests the whistle; no bell sounds. Miso stays beside Sela. Toma asks them to begin the course. Miso walks to the first cone, sits and looks back for a cue.

[Sela gives the hand signal used in practice. Miso moves through the cones and stops at the mat. The scoreboard remains blank because Riko has disconnected the score feed too.]

Pavi asks the judge for a score. Toma says the run was clear. Riko reconnects the display, but selects the camera score instead. It awards a point to the close-up of Miso's nose. Toma says the dog should receive the points, not the image. Pavi asks the operator to frame both handler and dog.

INT. ARENA - MOMENTS LATER

Miso starts the course again. The whistle sounds once from Sela's hand. Miso waits. She gives the hand signal; Miso trots the course and returns. Riko records the run on the correct camera. Pavi narrates the obstacle order, then asks the audience not to clap during the final mat.

INT. DOG SHOW ARENA - MOMENTS LATER

Riko checks the separate scoring-trigger lead, which remained connected when he isolated the audio effect. Pavi asks him to test the whistle on the second course. Riko stands at the start line and gives one short blast. The scoreboard awards the whistle another ten points. Miso remains beside Sela, watching the judge's hand.

TOMA: The dog has not started.

PAVI: The whistle has a perfect record.

Riko unplugs the scoring bell from the whistle channel. Nala, the stage manager, asks him to keep the bell ready for the winner's cue. He plugs it into the finish sensor instead. The board flashes a trophy when Miso touches the final mat during a quiet practice run. Sela looks at the course; they have not begun the scored event.

SELA: That was a practice mat.

TOMA: The score needs a run, not a rehearsal.

Pavi asks the camera operator to replay the moment. The replay shows Miso entering frame, then a close-up of the empty mat. Pavi calls it a photo finish. Toma points out that the dog is still walking toward the camera. Riko switches the scoring sensor from automatic to judge-confirmed operation, keeping it inactive until the judge raises the green card. Nala asks whether the board can still show a dramatic number. Toma says it can show the number the dog earns.

Miso and Sela take their places. Riko holds the whistle down. Toma raises the green card; Sela gives Miso the familiar hand signal. Miso runs the weave, circles the low hurdle and returns to the mat. Riko presses the finish control only when the judge nods. The board displays MISO - CLEAN RUN. Pavi begins to announce the whistle's points, sees Toma's raised eyebrow and changes his copy to the dog's name.

ACT THREE

INT. DOG SHOW ARENA - AFTERNOON

Riko hands the whistle back to Pavi before the final event, a recall. Sela walks to the far end of the arena. Pavi gives the whistle to the judge. Toma keeps it lowered. Sela calls Miso. The dog trots to her and sits. The scoreboard displays the handler's name instead of the dog's. Riko changes the card to MISO, and Toma records the score.

The speaker plays a victory bell from the earlier recording. Miso turns toward it but stays at Sela's side. Toma awards the ribbon for the completed run. Pavi tries to give the whistle a prize. Toma asks what behavior it performed. Riko places it in the sound booth.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE SHOW

Riko labels the cue START ONLY. Sela clips the ribbon to Miso's collar. Pavi checks that the scoreboard shows the dog's name.

FINAL IMAGE: Miso rests beside the ribbon while the unused whistle sits behind a soundproof panel.
