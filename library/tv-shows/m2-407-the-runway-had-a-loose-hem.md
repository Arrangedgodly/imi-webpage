# The Runway Had a Loose Hem

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fashion-design competitions, timed sewing challenges, dramatic runway reveals, and judges who score garments before anyone can walk*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: Two designers race to make a market-day jacket, but the runway's loose hem keeps catching the camera cable before the judging begins.

Cast: Host Tavi, a baboon; designers Leni, a macaque, and Pavo, a gibbon; judge Reka, an orangutan; judge Nemi, a capuchin; stagehand Seli, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. DESIGN STUDIO - DAY

The runway lights come up. A cable snags the hem of Pavo's jacket and pulls him sideways. Seli catches the cable.

TAVI: We have a look.

PAVO: The look is connected to the camera.

ACT ONE

Tavi explains the challenge: design a jacket for a busy market day, with one secure pocket and room to move. The designers have thirty minutes. Leni chooses durable canvas. Pavo chooses a shiny fabric that catches the lights.

REKA: How will your jacket move?

PAVO: It will reflect the market.

NEMI: Does it have a pocket?

PAVO: It will have a pocket.

The clock begins. Leni measures her sleeve and tests a shoulder turn. Pavo drapes the fabric over a mannequin. Tavi asks for a confessional from each designer before they have begun sewing. Leni says she needs to stitch first. Pavo tells the camera he is creating "a statement about commerce."

ACT TWO

The runway floor has a cable crossing its center. Seli tries to tape it down. Tavi asks for a dramatic walk test before the garments are finished. Leni holds up her jacket and practices turning. Her sleeve clears the cable. Pavo tries a spin; his loose hem catches the edge of the tape.

PAVO: The runway has interfered with my design.

LENI: It is a cable crossing the floor.

REKA: We will judge the garment, not the trip.

Nemi checks the pocket on Leni's jacket. It closes with a small button. She places a market token inside and asks Leni to walk. The token stays. Pavo has sewn a wide pocket with no closure. Nemi places a token inside; it falls out when he turns.

PAVO: It is an open-access pocket.

NEMI: It is an open pocket.

ACT TWO-B

The clock reaches five minutes. Leni stitches a final button. Pavo tries to sew a flap onto the pocket while wearing the jacket. Tavi says the contest does not prohibit last-minute alterations. Reka asks Pavo to remove it before he catches the needle on the lining.

PAVO: The pocket needs a signature.

REKA: It needs a closure.

Seli repositions the camera cable beyond the runway. The crew tests the path with an empty garment rack. It rolls cleanly. Leni checks the hem of her jacket and clips one loose thread. Pavo finishes a flap but uses a large decorative button that does not go through the buttonhole.

PAVO: It is secure from a distance.

NEMI: The token is not.

He replaces the button with a smaller one, just as the clock ends. The host calls time. Pavo lets go of the needle and raises both hands; Leni places her scissors on the table.

Before the runway, Reka asks each designer to test the pocket while wearing the jacket. Leni places a market token inside, bends to lift a crate and stands. The button stays closed. Pavo places his token in the new pocket flap and turns his shoulders. The button holds, but the shiny fabric catches his cuff.

PAVO: It is a close fit.

NEMI: It is a close sleeve.

He frees the cuff without pulling the seam. Reka checks the stitching and finds one loose thread, which Seli snips before the runway. Tavi asks whether that alters the design. Reka says a thread trim is maintenance, not a new garment. The contestants take their places under the lights.

ACT THREE

The runway show begins. Leni walks steadily, turns at the end, and returns. The token remains in her pocket. Pavo walks slowly to show the shine, then turns. His button holds and the token stays. Both garments clear the cable-free runway.

REKA: Leni's construction is clean and functional.

NEMI: Pavo solved the pocket at the last minute, but the original flap caught the lining.

Tavi announces Leni as the winner for durable material, movement and a secure closure. Pavo accepts the result and asks to keep the shiny fabric for another garment. Reka says he can use it after testing the seams. Seli takes the cable tape off the runway and puts it in the equipment bin.

TAG

Tavi tries the winning jacket on over his host suit. The sleeves are too short for him. Leni says it was made to her measurements.

FINAL IMAGE: Leni walks off with the market token still in her pocket as the camera follows without crossing the cleared runway.
