# The Night Shift at the Glasshouse

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: workplace dramas set during overnight crises, tense monitor close-ups, and crews who treat routine maintenance as a season finale*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A night crew at the botanical glasshouse tries to protect rare seedlings from a temperature drop while the day manager keeps calling the event an emergency finale.

Cast: Night supervisor Luma, a gibbon; technician Neri, a macaque; guard Odo, an orangutan; intern Vex, a capuchin; and day manager Palla, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "Three Degrees"

### Cold Open

[GLASSHOUSE. A THERMOMETER CLICKS.]

LUMA: The temperature is dropping.

ODO: It is supposed to drop at night.

VEX: The alarm is on.

PALLA (over phone): No one move. I am coming in.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[CONTROL DESK. MONITORS GLOW.]

Palla arrives wearing a coat over her pajamas and declares the glasshouse in crisis. The thermometer shows 18 degrees. The night setting is 17 degrees. Luma says the seedlings are safe, but the heater has stopped cycling.

NERI: The room is warm. The sensor is reading the door draft.

PALLA: We cannot trust a room that has a draft.

Odo checks the door seal. It is closed. Vex holds a leaf beside the vent to see airflow. The leaf barely moves. Palla says the plant is not responding.

LUMA: It is a plant.

Palla orders the crew to move every seedling to the emergency warm room. Neri objects that the room is full of mature ferns and moving trays could damage roots.

PALLA: We need a montage.

VEX: We need to check the sensor.

### Act Two

[HEATER CONTROL ROOM.]

Neri and Vex open the panel. A small gecko has curled beside the sensor, warming it. The heater believes the room is warmer than it is. Odo gently guides the gecko onto a nearby rock. Palla insists this is sabotage.

PALLA: The gecko has compromised the system.

LUMA: It found a warm place.

Neri moves the sensor away from the rock. The display drops to 16 degrees. The heater starts. Palla watches the numbers rise, then fall, then rise again as the system cycles.

Palla orders Vex to film the gauge in close-up. The numbers change too slowly for an exciting shot. Vex tries a time-lapse, but the heater's indicator light blinks out of sync with the camera. Neri asks him to record the sound of the fan instead.

VEX: It is a steady hum.

PALLA: Can you make it ominous?

VEX: I can stand beside it.

He stands there. His gibbon arms block the control panel. Palla calls for a second angle. Odo turns the camera. The shot now includes a watering can, a tea cup, and the gecko's rock, each labeled with a tape tag.

LUMA: We do not need a close-up of every object.

PALLA: We need viewers to follow the investigation.

NERI: The issue was the sensor position. We have fixed it.

PALLA: It is unstable.

NERI: It is maintaining the set temperature.

Palla asks Vex to deliver a dramatic update to the crew. Vex steps in front of the monitor.

VEX: The system is functioning. We have relocated the sensor and left the plants where they are.

PALLA: Add a pause before "functioning."

VEX: Why?

PALLA: It needs tension.

Odo brings hot tea to the control desk. He sets the cup near the sensor. The reading climbs again.

LUMA: Move the tea.

Odo moves it. The temperature normalizes. Palla asks whether the tea has interfered with the whole house. Neri says the sensor is sensitive to nearby heat, which is why it needs clear space.

### Act Three

[SEEDLING ROOM.]

The crew checks the rare seedlings. The leaves are firm. The roots are moist. One tray has dried because its watering line was pinched under a cart. Vex spots it while returning the tea cup. Neri clears the line, and Odo waters the tray by hand.

PALLA: We have discovered another crisis.

LUMA: We have discovered a dry tray.

The manager asks for a final status. Luma reports that the sensor has been moved, the heater cycles correctly, the gecko has a warm rock, and the tray is watered. Palla requests a dramatic all-clear.

LUMA: The plants are safe. The heater is working. The night shift is ending.

Palla looks disappointed. She asks if anyone has learned something. Neri says not to put tea beside the sensor. Vex says a leaf is not a monitor. Odo says the gecko should not be assigned a shift.

### Tag

[CONTROL ROOM AT DAWN.]

Palla leaves. The heater cycles off. The thermometer settles at 17 degrees. Luma turns off the monitor. The gecko returns to its rock.

VEX: The emergency is over.

NERI: The shift is over.

Odo wheels the cart away from the watering line. The tray remains wet.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
