# The Quiz Night Host Answers for the Audience

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: audience-participation quiz shows whose host interrupts every answer to explain the game*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Lemi, a lemur; contestant Poro, a macaque; contestant Rava, a gibbon; floor producer Nima, an orangutan; scorekeeper Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. QUIZ STUDIO - NIGHT

Poro holds a card with one answer. Lemi leans toward the camera.

LEMI: Before Poro answers, let me explain the rules.

PORO: I was about to answer.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Nima says the host's explanation runs longer than the round. Tavi has set a two-minute timer. Lemi introduces a simple picture question: identify the tool on the screen. Rava presses her buzzer. Lemi interrupts to explain that the fastest correct answer gets the point. Rava waits. The buzzer sounds again when her elbow hits it.

RAVA: It is a rake.

The screen shows a spoon. The camera operator has loaded the wrong image. Poro says spoon. Tavi gives him the point. Lemi begins explaining why the spoon was correct. Nima taps the timer; half the round is gone.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Nima asks Lemi to use a short cue: question, answer, score. Lemi tries. He announces the category, explains how categories work, describes the score system, then asks a question. Tavi's timer expires before the question mark.

[The contestants hold up their answer cards. Lemi continues explaining. Rava lowers hers. Poro turns his card toward the camera.]

Nima asks the director to cut the host microphone between questions. Lemi notices and speaks into the contestant mic, still narrating the rules. Tavi moves the timer to the floor so Lemi cannot see it. Lemi steps over it and announces that the round has no time limit.

RAVA: The card says sixty seconds.

LEMI: That is a guideline.

Tavi displays the written rules. The time limit is printed in large letters. Lemi asks whether the audience knows what a guideline is. The studio audience does not respond. He explains anyway. Poro answers a question while Lemi is speaking. Tavi gives him the point, and the score changes before the host can comment.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

The final round asks the contestants to name a bird from a silhouette. Lemi starts to explain the scoring. Nima raises one finger. Lemi stops. The studio becomes quiet. Rava says her answer: heron. Poro says crane. Tavi reveals the image: a heron. Rava gets the point.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Nima checks the round order. Each question has a one-line answer window and a visible timer. Lemi practices the handoff: he reads the question, then steps away from the contestants' microphones. Rava gives a test answer. Tavi records it without the host speaking over her. The timer shows forty seconds remaining. Lemi asks if he may explain why the timer matters. Nima hands him a card that says SAVE IT FOR AFTER THE ROUND. He puts the card in his pocket and smiles at the camera. The camera operator zooms in on the pocket. Lemi starts to explain the camera cue, catches himself, and simply gestures toward Rava. She answers the next question before the timer reaches ten. Tavi records one point and leaves the score visible for the audience. Rava asks Lemi to repeat the answer instead of explaining it again. He does, and she nods. Tavi writes the score on the card. The next question is ready. Lemi waits while Nima listens; the audience applauds the contestant before the next prompt.

Nima asks the director to keep the timer visible even when it reaches zero. The director wants a slow-motion replay of the host's silent pause. Lemi says he can provide a longer one. Tavi puts the cue card over the replay button. The crew agrees to let the contestants' answers fill the remaining airtime.

ACT THREE

INT. QUIZ STUDIO - NIGHT

Poro and Rava are tied. Lemi asks the audience to vote on a tie-breaker. The rules specify one final question. Nima points to the card. Lemi begins explaining the card, catches himself and reads the question instead: what do you use to open a locked door? Poro answers key. Rava answers key. The judge accepts both. The score stays tied.

Lemi looks at the audience, about to explain the co-winner rule. The contestants read the rule aloud together. Tavi presents two small trophies. Lemi asks for a reaction. Poro and Rava hold the trophies up. The camera catches the moment before Lemi speaks. Nima signals the closing music. The host lets it play.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Lemi reads the next week's rules to himself. Tavi starts the timer. He finishes before it rings. Nima gives him the unused cue card.

FINAL IMAGE: The contestants leave with their trophies as the host holds up a one-line card that reads THANK YOU.
