# The Carpet Is Still Rolling

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: awards-show red carpets, rapid-fire outfit questions, and interviewers who refuse to let anyone reach the door*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A red-carpet reporter tries to keep guests posing until the live show begins, but the carpet itself keeps unrolling toward the street.

Cast: Reporter Vina, a macaque; host Noro, a gibbon; camera operator Pex, a capuchin; award nominee Tali, an orangutan; event manager Seli, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

EXT. AWARDS HALL - NIGHT

Vina holds a microphone as the red carpet rolls past her and down the entrance ramp.

VINA: We are live from the carpet.

SELI: The carpet is moving.

Noro steps onto it. The roll pulls him slowly backward toward the curb.

NORO: I'm arriving in reverse.

ACT ONE

Seli wedges a rubber stop under the roll. Pex reframes the shot so the street disappears. Vina resumes her interview pose. Tali arrives, wearing a modest green shawl and carrying her own award invitation.

VINA: Who are you wearing?

TALI: My coat.

VINA: Who made it?

TALI: A tailor. I brought her name.

Vina tries to read the name card. A gust flips it over. Pex zooms in on the blank back. Noro announces "a mystery designer," and Tali corrects him.

TALI: Her name is on the other side.

Seli flips the card. Vina reads the tailor's name and asks Tali to turn for the camera. Tali turns once. The carpet unrolls another foot. Pex pans to follow it.

ACT TWO

Vina asks Tali how it feels to be nominated. Tali says she is pleased and hungry. Noro asks for a more emotional answer. Tali points toward the hall, where dinner is being served.

TALI: There are small sandwiches inside.

VINA: Do you have a favorite?

TALI: The one I can reach.

The carpet begins rolling again. Seli checks the stop. A decorative velvet rope has been tied to the carpet's loose end. As the roll moves, the rope pulls two gold stanchions across the path. Pex films them like a choreographed entrance.

NORO: The decor is moving into position.

SELI: It is moving out of position.

Vina asks Tali to stay for another question. Tali says she can answer while walking inside. Vina steps onto the carpet; it slides under her feet. She holds her balance by grabbing the microphone stand. Pex follows the action with the camera, then slips a paw onto the curb.

Pex: The shot is still live.

VINA: Then keep the camera on the carpet.

ACT TWO-B

Seli stops the roll by pinning it with a sandbag. The sandbag is branded for the show's sponsor. Noro tries to hide it with a program booklet, which is too small. The carpet stays still, but the booklet starts to slide. Tali catches it with her broad hand.

TALI: Is this part of the outfit question?

VINA: No. It is the program for tonight's awards.

Tali reads the categories. She is nominated for Best Set Design, not acting. Vina tries to ask about her character. Tali explains she designed a set with wide entrances so the crew could carry scenery without turning it sideways.

VINA: What was the biggest challenge?

TALI: Getting the carpet through the loading door.

Everyone looks down. The roll cannot pass the doorway when fully wound. Seli removes the sandbag, and the carpet unrolls again. Noro and Vina step onto its edges to keep it in place. Pex walks backward, shooting the shot, while Tali moves the invitation stand out of the way.

TALI: We can roll it from the far end.

SELI: That end is under the camera cable.

TALI: Lift the cable first.

They lift the cable, unhook the velvet rope and roll the carpet toward the hall. It reaches the entrance without dragging the stanchions. Tali walks along the side on bare stone so the carpet stays flat.

ACT THREE

Vina gives one final interview as Tali reaches the hall. She asks whether Tali is excited about the ceremony. Tali says yes and points to the place cards where the designers are seated. Noro announces the awards begin in two minutes. Seli secures the carpet with two stops. Pex checks that the camera cable is clear.

VINA: Any last message to the viewers?

TALI: Please credit the tailor when you ask about the coat.

VINA: We did.

TALI: And keep the carpet still.

Noro steps off the carpet and stands on the floor. Vina does the same. Seli nods: the stop is holding. Tali enters the hall at her own pace. The camera follows her through the door, leaving the carpet behind.

TAG

The red-carpet set is empty. The loose end begins to roll. Seli catches it, looks at the sponsor camera and holds it in place without saying a word.

FINAL IMAGE: The carpet stops at the doorway as the awards music begins inside, already underway.
