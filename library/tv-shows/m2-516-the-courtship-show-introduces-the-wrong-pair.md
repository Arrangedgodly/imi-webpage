# The Courtship Show Introduces the Wrong Pair

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating programs that let matching graphics and dramatic introductions decide compatibility before the people meet*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Sera, a macaque; guest Nilo, a gibbon; guest Pema, an orangutan; producer Tavi, a lemur; graphics operator Luko, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. DATING STUDIO - NIGHT

Two silhouettes appear behind separate curtains. The screen joins them under a gold heart.

SERA: You are our perfect match.

NILO: I have not met anyone yet.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Tavi says the matching graphic is based on the guests' questionnaire cards. Luko has put Nilo's hobbies under Pema's photo and Pema's under Nilo's. Sera reads that Nilo loves quiet walks and Pema enjoys drums. Nilo says he plays drums. Pema says she takes quiet walks.

SERA: Then the match is even better.

Pema asks if she can meet Nilo before the reveal. Tavi says the format calls for a dramatic entrance. Sera opens the wrong curtain; an assistant carrying the score cards walks out. The audience applauds. Luko's screen pairs the assistant with the host.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The producer asks Luko to swap the profiles. He drags the cards on screen. The hearts remain, but the names change. Sera reads a shared interest: both guests enjoy hiking. Nilo says he avoids trails with drums. Pema says she has never played an instrument.

[Tavi locates the questionnaires. Nilo's card has a check beside "live music"; Pema's card has a check beside "quiet parks." The marks were shifted one line by a stapler hole.]

Sera asks whether they would still like to meet. Nilo says yes, but not under a score. Pema agrees. Tavi asks for the compatibility percentage. Luko removes the number. The screen displays only their names. Sera tries to fill the silence with a voice-over. Nilo asks her to let the introductions happen.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

The curtains open at the same time. Nilo and Pema greet each other. Pema asks about his drum set. Nilo says he plays softly in a rehearsal room. Pema says she likes quiet walks in the park. They discover both attend the same outdoor music event, but at different times: Nilo performs in the afternoon; Pema walks there at dawn.

Sera asks them to choose a first date. Luko displays three options: drum studio, park, and restaurant. Tavi tells the graphics team to wait. Nilo suggests tea at the park after the music ends. Pema says that sounds pleasant. The screen tries to add a heart. Luko turns off the animation before it lands over their faces.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Tavi prints the two questionnaire cards and sets them face down. Sera asks each guest one question without giving a score. Nilo asks what time Pema takes her walk. Pema says sunrise, when the paths are empty. Pema asks when Nilo rehearses. He says late afternoon, after the park clears. They notice that both routines use the same place but do not overlap. Tavi asks whether he should move them to the heart-marked chairs. Nilo and Pema remain where they are, at a comfortable distance. Sera lets the camera keep that framing. Luko asks for a score, but Tavi removes the percentage graphic. Nilo and Pema compare park routes while the set remains live. Tavi starts to move them to the heart-marked chairs, but they stay where they are. Luko fades the heart graphic; it shrinks until it covers only the unused score box. The guests keep talking about the park while Sera lets the silence stay on air.

Luko slides two identical coffee mugs onto the heart-marked table. Pema moves one back to the service tray. Sera asks if the mugs are part of a compatibility test. Nilo says they are just coffee.


ACT THREE

INT. DATING STUDIO - NIGHT

The host asks each guest whether they want to continue off camera. They both say yes. Tavi removes the compatibility board. Sera tries to announce a perfect score. Nilo asks who is being graded. Luko replaces the graphic with two checkboxes: BOTH AGREED. Sera reads it aloud and closes the segment.

Pema and Nilo exchange contact cards. The producer asks them to stand beneath the heart for a final shot. They move to the side, where they can talk without looking at the screen. The audience applauds. Sera looks at Tavi for a cue. He points to the exit. She lets them leave without a posed embrace.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Luko separates the questionnaire cards and fixes the stapler alignment. Tavi stacks them in two labeled folders. Sera walks past the heart graphic, which is now blank.

FINAL IMAGE: The two guests' names appear side by side without a score, while the animated heart waits unused on the studio monitor.
