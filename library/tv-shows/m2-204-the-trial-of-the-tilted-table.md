# The Trial of the Tilted Table

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: small-town legal drama convention, where a courtroom must decide whether a wobbly picnic table is a hazard or a feature*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A village court hears competing claims about a tilted table and must decide whether to repair it or preserve its history.

Cast: Judge Sela, a lemur; claimant Rell, a capuchin; respondent Odo, a baboon; carpenter Miri, a macaque; and clerk Pavi, a gibbon.

Episode: "A Level Question"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY COURT, DAY

Pavi places a cup on a small table. It slowly slides toward the edge.

PAVI: The court is in session.

Sela catches the cup.

SELA: The court is also thirsty.

ACT ONE

Rell says he nearly spilled water at the picnic. Odo says the table has been tilted for years and is a community landmark. Miri checks the legs and finds one foot shorter because it rests on a root. Pavi reads the complaint: "Table leans east." 

Sela asks for evidence. Rell sets the cup down. Odo holds it steady. Miri asks him to let go. The cup slides again. Odo says the table was designed for a slope. Miri asks who designed it. No one knows.

ACT TWO

EXT. PICNIC GROVE, AFTERNOON

The court inspects the site. The table sits beside a tree. One leg rests on a root, and the ground slopes toward a stream. Miri proposes a flat stone beneath the short leg. Odo says that would change the table. Rell says it would stop the cup from sliding.

Sela asks whether the table has historical value. Pavi finds initials carved under the edge. Odo says they belong to the group that built it. Miri sees the table is sturdy, but the root has grown around one leg. Moving it could damage the tree.

A cup is placed on the table again. It stays level after the flat stone is added. Odo asks if the stone counts as part of the table. Sela says the court can record it as a repair, not a redesign.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COMMUNITY COURT, LATE AFTERNOON

Pavi calls two witnesses. A young squirrel monkey says the table wobbles when everyone sits on one side. A tortoise says the table has never moved during a meal. Sela asks whether either witness has tried the stone support. Neither has.

Miri brings a flat stone. She sets it under the short leg. The table rests level. Pavi places a cup in the center. It stays. Rell pushes gently on one corner. The stone shifts. Miri adjusts it and presses the earth down around it.

ODO: We have changed the evidence.

SELA: We have tested a repair.

Odo worries the stone will wash away in rain. Miri says it can be checked during regular maintenance. Pavi records the location and draws a small marker on the site map. Rell asks whether the court can order a permanent fix. Sela says the table is safe now, but the next inspection will decide if the support needs replacing.

The witnesses sit down together. The table holds. A cup slides when Pavi sets it at the edge. Sela moves it toward the center and asks if anyone objects. No one does.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. PICNIC GROVE, LATE AFTERNOON

The court reviews the table's history. Pavi finds a maintenance note from the last rainy season. It says the table was moved away from a puddle and placed beside the root. Odo recognizes his own initials on the note, though he does not remember moving it. Rell says the root has grown since then.

Miri checks the table's underside. The legs are intact, and the top is dry. The problem is the slope, not a broken board. She shows the court how the flat stone shares the weight and keeps the short leg from rocking. Sela asks Rell to repeat the test with a cup at each corner. The center remains steady; the edge cup still needs to be moved inward.

A small monkey arrives carrying a tray of fruit. The monkey sets it down, and the table wobbles once before settling. Odo begins to call this a new incident. Miri shows that the tray was placed on the outer edge. The monkey moves it to the center. It stays level.

SELA: The court recommends a marked serving area.

Rell paints a small circle on the center of the table. Odo worries the paint will look like a target. Pavi adds a tiny cup symbol. The residents agree to use the table as it is, with the support stone and a simple inspection note.

ACT THREE



Sela rules that the table can remain in place with the stone under its leg. Miri marks the support and checks it after everyone sits. Rell sets down a cup. It stays still. Odo tells the story of how the table was built, adding that it was never meant to hold a cup at the edge.

TAG

Pavi places the cup on the very edge. It stays. Sela moves it back to the center.

SELA: The verdict does not repeal common sense.

Odo adds the flat stone to the maintenance log.
