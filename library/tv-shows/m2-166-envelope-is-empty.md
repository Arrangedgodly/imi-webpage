# The Envelope Is Empty

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: awards broadcasts that stretch brief acceptance speeches into suspenseful television*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: At the annual Canopy Service Awards, presenter Nemi is told to make every category feel like a cliffhanger. Stage manager Koro wants the winners to get their plaques before the broadcast ends, but the envelopes keep being used as props for fake surprises.

Cast: Nemi, a macaque presenter; Koro, a gorilla stage manager; Afi, a langur volunteer honoree; Bobo, a capuchin announcer; and Toma, a red colobus repair crew leader.

## Episode: "The Plaque Goes to..."

### Cold Open

[AWARDS STAGE. NEMI HOLDS A SEALED ENVELOPE. A TIMER COUNTS DOWN TO COMMERCIAL.] 

NEMI: The award for volunteer service goes to...

[THE ENVELOPE IS EMPTY. KORO LEANS IN AND WHISPERS.] 

KORO: The card is in your other hand.

[NEMI OPENS HER PALM. THE CARD IS STUCK TO HER SLEEVE.] 

NEMI: Our first suspenseful reveal is stationery.

BOBO: The audience is on the edge of its seats.

[THE AUDIENCE IS SITTING IN FOLDING CHAIRS ON LEVEL FLOOR.]

[KORO CHECKS THE STAGE MARKS. BOBO HAS MOVED THE PODIUM TO COVER THE WINNER'S EXIT.] 

KORO: The honorees need room to leave with their plaques.

BOBO: The camera needs a reveal.

[AFI TRIES TO CARRY THE STACK OF CHAIRS PAST THE PODIUM. IT BLOCKS HER. KORO MOVES IT ONE METER.]

AFI: Now I can pass.

NEMI: That is a better entrance than the drum roll.

### Act One

[BACKSTAGE. KORO HANDS NEMI A RUNNING ORDER. EVERY ACCEPTANCE SPEECH HAS A 20-SECOND SLOT.] 

KORO: Keep the names clear, hand over the plaque, then let the next winner speak.

BOBO: Can we play the drum roll under every name?

KORO: Only for the winner.

[NEMI OPENS THE NEXT ENVELOPE. A CONFETTI PACKET BURSTS, COVERING THE CARD.] 

NEMI: I cannot read this.

BOBO: We have a mystery finalist.

[AFI ENTERS CARRYING A STACK OF LIBRARY CHAIRS. SHE SETS THEM DOWN TO TAKE HER PLAQUE.] 

AFI: Could you say my name before I go back for the next row?

NEMI: Afi, for keeping the library open during repairs.

[BOBO PLAYS THE LONG DRUM ROLL. KORO POINTS AT THE COMMERCIAL CLOCK.]

### Act Two

[THE NEXT CATEGORY IS "BEST COMMUNITY REPAIR." KORO BRINGS OUT THREE PLAQUES. NEMI HAS ONE ENVELOPE.] 

NEMI: There are three finalists.

BOBO: The envelope contains only one card.

KORO: The winner is on the card. The other finalists are on the list.

[BOBO ADDS A SPINNING WHEEL TO THE SET. IT SPINS TOO FAST AND STOPS BETWEEN TWO NAMES.] 

NEMI: The wheel has not chosen.

KORO: It was not part of the judging.

[AFI CARRIES THE PLAQUES TO THE MARKED TABLE. ONE FALLS FACE DOWN. SHE TURNS IT OVER AND READS THE NAME.]

[THE WINNING REPAIR CREW'S FOREMAN IS NOT IN THE ROOM. TOMA, ITS SHIFT LEADER, WAITS BESIDE THE STAGE TO ACCEPT FOR HIM. BOBO ANNOUNCES A THREE-MINUTE VIDEO PACKAGE. KORO CHECKS THE CLOCK: THE BROADCAST HAS FOUR MINUTES LEFT.]

KORO: Can we hand the plaque to the person who came to accept it?

BOBO: We need the absent-winner reveal.

[NEMI HOLDS THE PLAQUE WHILE BOBO PLAYS A VIDEO OF AN EMPTY CHAIR. THE CARD ON THE CHAIR HAS THE WINNER'S NAME.]

NEMI: The winner sent a message. We can show it after the award.

KORO: That would be a useful order.

AFI: The repair crew won.

BOBO: The suspense has been broken by a plaque.

[KORO GIVES TOMA THE CREW'S AWARD. HE STARTS A SPEECH. THE COMMERCIAL CLOCK BEEPS.]

KORO: One sentence, please.

TOMA: We fixed the roof.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. BOBO REACHES FOR THE DRUM ROLL. KORO TAKES THE DRUMSTICK AWAY.]

[THE PLAQUE TABLE IS NOW EMPTY EXCEPT FOR ONE OPEN ENVELOPE. NEMI CLOSES IT BEFORE BOBO CAN REVEAL IT AS ANOTHER FINALIST.] 

NEMI: It is the shipping receipt.

### Act Three

[NEMI RETURNS FOR THE FINAL AWARD. BOBO HIDES THE ENVELOPE UNDER A CLOTH.] 

BOBO: For a true live surprise, we will reveal the card after one last commercial.

NEMI: The audience is watching now.

KORO: We have twenty seconds.

[NEMI LIFTS THE CLOTH. THE ENVELOPE IS AGAIN EMPTY; THE CARD IS UNDER BOBO'S SHOE.] 

BOBO: I have found the twist.

[AFI POINTS TO HIS SHOE. BOBO MOVES IT AND READS THE CARD.]

BOBO: Afi, for service to the library.

AFI: I already got one.

KORO: This one is for the volunteer schedule you made.

[AFI TAKES THE PLAQUE. IT HAS HER NAME SPELLED CORRECTLY. NEMI READS THE CITATION WITHOUT A DRUM ROLL. THE BROADCAST CLOCK REACHES ZERO AS THE APPLAUSE STARTS.] 

### Tag

[BACKSTAGE, BOBO OPENS A FINAL ENVELOPE. IT CONTAINS ONLY A NOTE: "PLEASE RETURN THE ENVELOPES."]

BOBO: And the recipient of all tonight's envelopes is...

[KORO OPENS A BOX LABELED "PROP DEPARTMENT." BOBO HOLDS THE STACK ABOVE IT FOR A FINAL DRUM ROLL. KORO TAKES THE STACK AND SHUTS THE LID.]

BOBO: We have not announced the box.

KORO: It knows who it is.
