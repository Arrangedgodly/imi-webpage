# The Baking Contest Mystery Box Has a Label

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality baking competitions, surprise ingredients, confessionals, and dramatic reveals undermined by plain packaging*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Host Nima, a macaque; baker Odo, an orangutan; baker Sela, a gibbon; judge Bram, a mandrill; pantry runner Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. BAKING TENT - DAY

The host lifts a silver lid. Inside is a paper sack stamped FLOUR.

NIMA: The mystery ingredient is... flour.

SFX: A single disappointed cymbal.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The baking show has built a mystery-box challenge around a sealed ingredient. Host Nima announces that the bakers will create a dessert using whatever is hidden beneath the silver lid. Odo and Sela open their boxes. Both contain plain sacks marked FLOUR, while the production card says "regional surprise."

NIMA: We wanted suspense.

SELA: You supplied the main ingredient.

ODO: I have flour at home.

BRAM: Contestants, please show shock for the camera.

TAVI: The sacks are labeled. No one needs to open them to know.

Nima asks the camera operator to shoot the sacks from an angle that hides the labels. Tavi says the labels are now on the camera monitor. The crew swaps the sacks for opaque jars, but one jar still has a lot number stamped across the lid. Odo recognizes it from the pantry inventory.

ODO: That lot is our regular flour.

NIMA: Is the lot number part of the twist?

SELA: Only if the twist is that the flour is the same flour.

Bram says the rules require one sealed mystery ingredient per baker and a shared pantry of standard supplies. The box contains no ingredient card, so neither baker knows what is allowed. Nima wants them to start the clock while the show calls the pantry runner.

ACT TWO

Tavi reads the challenge rules from the monitor. The mystery ingredient should be a regional fruit, nut, or spice. The production team mistakenly packed pantry flour into both boxes. The clock is stopped before anyone opens another package. Nima asks whether a replacement can arrive quickly. Tavi checks the pantry list and finds a jar of dried mountain plums that has not been used in another episode.

TAVI: The ingredient is real. It is sealed and listed.

BRAM: We need equal portions and the same instructions for both bakers.

ODO: How much?

NIMA: The clue card says one cup.

SELA: It says "one cup total, split equally, not one cup each" in handwriting.

NIMA: That is a very small distinction.

Tavi measures one cup total and divides it into two equal half-cup bowls, then lets both bakers inspect the ingredient. The production crew photographs the actual contents instead of an empty box. Nima asks for a dramatic countdown. The timer is reset only after Bram confirms both bowls are equal, then starts at his signal.

BRAM: Contestants may use the plum in any form. Standard pantry ingredients remain available. You have forty minutes.

NIMA: And now, the mystery begins.

ODO: We know what it is.

NIMA: The recipe is still mysterious.

Odo decides to bake plum hand pies and uses his knuckles to crimp the edges. Sela makes a tart but puts the plums in a small bowl to soften with citrus. The show's confessional camera asks each baker whether the ingredient has changed everything.

ODO: It has changed my filling.

SELA: It has not changed the laws of pastry.

The timer ticks. Nima keeps trying to call the plums "ancient mountain jewels." Tavi points out that the label on the jar says they were dried last week. Nima changes the phrase to "recently dried mountain jewels."

ACT THREE

The clock reaches zero. Both desserts are finished. Odo's pies have crisp edges and a tart filling. Sela's tart has a glossy top and a soft center. Bram tastes both and judges them on texture, balance, and use of the plums. He gives Sela the higher finish mark and Odo the higher crust mark.

BRAM: The winner is Odo, by one point for balance. The filling tastes like plum instead of sugar.

NIMA: Our mystery ingredient has chosen a winner.

TAVI: The judges chose the winner. The plums did not vote.

Sela congratulates Odo and asks to keep the recipe card. Nima tries to place it inside the empty silver mystery box. Tavi gives her the actual card with the ingredient written clearly on it.

NIMA: Next week the box will contain an ingredient we did not already use every day.

ODO: That's a mystery.

BRAM: We will check the label before the camera rolls.

The crew keeps one flour sack on the counter as a joke prop, with its plain label facing the camera. Nima puts the plum jar in the pantry return crate and reads its date aloud.

FINAL IMAGE: The two desserts sit on the judging table beside a silver box now labeled CHECK CONTENTS FIRST.
