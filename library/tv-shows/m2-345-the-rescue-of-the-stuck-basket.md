# The Rescue of the Stuck Basket

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rescue dramas that bring a full command center to a small, solvable problem*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A basket gets wedged in a low tree fork, and the rescue crew must resist making the situation more dramatic than it is.

Cast: Rescue lead Nara, a macaque; dispatcher Odo, a baboon; climber Pava, a gibbon; caller Kito, a capuchin.

Episode: "Operation Basket Lift"

COLD OPEN

EXT. ORCHARD - DAY

Kito points up at a basket wedged in a low branch.

KITO: My basket is stuck.

Odo speaks into a radio.

ODO: We have a basket situation.

Nara looks up. The branch is only a few feet overhead.

NARA: It is reachable.

ACT ONE

The rescue crew arrives with ropes, helmets, and a portable command board. Odo draws a map of the tree. Nara asks Kito how the basket got stuck. Kito says he hung it on the branch while picking fruit, then the handle slipped into a fork.

Pava examines the branch. It is sturdy and low. She can reach it from the ground. Odo asks if they should deploy the ladder. Nara says not yet.

Kito tries to pull the basket down. The branch bends slightly. Nara tells him to stop. Odo marks the bending branch as a secondary hazard.

PAVA: The branch is flexible.

ODO: I have noted the flexibility.

ACT TWO

Pava puts on climbing gloves. Odo reads the safety checklist aloud. Nara checks the ground and moves a stone out of the way. Kito holds the basket's strap from below.

Pava reaches the fork and lifts the handle. The basket does not move. It is caught on a twig inside the loop. Nara asks her to rotate the handle instead of pulling. Pava turns it sideways. The basket comes free.

Odo announces that phase one is complete. The basket is still in Pava's hand. She lowers it carefully. Kito receives it and checks the fruit inside. All the apples are present.

Odo asks whether they need to inspect the branch. Nara checks the bark where the handle caught. There is no damage. Pava says the branch will be fine.

ACT TWO-B

The rescue team meets to review the operation. Odo presents the command board, with a diagram and three arrows. Nara points out that they used only one ladder and never deployed it. Odo says the ladder was a contingency asset.

Kito asks if he can return to fruit picking. Nara says yes, but he should use a lower hook for the basket. Pava shows him a simple loop that will release easily.

Odo asks if he should record an official rescue. Nara says it was a quick assist. The crew removes its rope from the tree and packs the ladder.

ACT TWO-C

Kito explains that the basket has a loose handle, which is why it got caught. Nara asks him to show the crew the lower hook. He places the basket on it and pulls gently; it comes off without snagging. Pava checks that the hook is attached to a solid post, not a young branch.

Odo asks if they should practice another recovery. Nara says there is no reason to put the basket back in the tree. The camera crew asks for a slow-motion replay. Pava reenacts the turn of the handle using an empty basket, but Odo insists on adding a dramatic countdown. Kito moves the basket sideways, exactly as before. It slides free in one motion.

The crew notices a second basket hanging from a higher branch. Kito says it belongs to the neighbor and is not stuck. Odo starts to call in the second team. Nara stops him and asks the neighbor before touching it. The neighbor says it is secure. The crew leaves it alone.

Pava explains that safe rescue work begins by checking the person, the object, and the route. Odo writes the steps on the command board, then erases the unnecessary deployment plan.

Nara asks Kito if the basket can be repaired instead of replaced. He says the handle can be tightened with a strip of woven fiber. Pava demonstrates the knot, leaving a loop that can be released from the lower hook. Kito tests the handle with a few apples. It holds. Odo asks whether the repair should be added to the mission report. Nara says only if it helps prevent the basket from getting stuck again. Kito agrees to keep the basket on the low hook when it is not in use.

ACT THREE

Kito hangs the empty basket on a lower peg and resumes picking. The rescue crew watches from the path. Odo asks if the mission is complete.

NARA: The basket is down, the branch is clear, and Kito is safe.

PAVA: We can close the call.

Odo writes "basket recovered" on the board.

TAG

A small apple drops into the basket.

ODO: New call.

NARA: That is how baskets work.
