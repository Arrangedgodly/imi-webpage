# The Relay Team Has Four Baton Lengths

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports documentary crews that create drama from the official measurement of a handoff*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Coach Nari, a lemur; runner Pip, a capuchin; runner Vex, a macaque; official Mara, a baboon; commentator Odo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. SCHOOL TRACK - AFTERNOON

[A relay team stands at the exchange zone. Odo narrates beside a slow-motion monitor.] 

ODO: The baton must pass within twenty meters.

MARA: Twenty meters is marked in white.

[Vex hands Pip a baton that is twice the regulation length.]

PIP: This is a branch.

[Theme music.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Tonight on The Last Lap, one team fights the clock, the rules, and a very long stick.

NARI: The baton is in the equipment shed. That's a branch from the shade tree.

VEX: It feels aerodynamic.

MARA: It is not a baton.

[Pip rotates the branch between his hands. It catches on the lane divider and pulls a small flag loose. Nari lifts it clear and lays it across an open equipment cart beyond the track. The cart is parked on a slight slope with one wheel unlocked.] 

PIP: It has a wide turning radius.

NARI: We will use the short baton. The race starts in ten minutes.

ODO: Can we tell the story of the branch first?

NARI: It fell yesterday.

ODO: A veteran object.

ACT TWO

[The runners practice exchanges with the regulation baton. Pip runs, then slows as Vex reaches backward. Vex's hand misses. The baton falls and rolls into the next lane.] 

MARA: The exchange must be in the zone.

VEX: I was looking at the camera.

NARI: Look at Pip's hand.

[They reset. Pip gives a clear vocal cue. Vex turns too early and runs backward, still holding out his hand. Odo asks the camera to follow the mistake in slow motion.] 

ODO: The anticipation is powerful.

PIP: The handoff is not.

[Nari places a cone at the mark. Vex uses the cone's shadow to measure his start. The shadow shifts when a cloud passes.] 

VEX: The mark moved.

MARA: The cone did not.

[The coach tells Pip to use his ears. He hears Vex's shoes strike the track and starts on the right beat. The baton passes cleanly. Mara raises a flag.] 

MARA: Legal exchange.

ODO: Can we get another take?

MARA: It was a race.

[The crew reviews the practice exchange on the monitor. Odo freezes the picture as Pip's hand closes around the baton.]

ODO: This is the turning point.

MARA: His foot is inside the zone. It is legal.

NARI: The mark is still visible behind his heel.

[Vex asks to watch the replay at half speed. The picture stretches the branch in the background until it appears longer than the track.]

PIP: That branch was not in the exchange.

ODO: It is the parallel story.

MARA: The race has one baton and two runners.

[She places the baton in Vex's palm and tells him to keep his eyes on the approaching runner. Pip jogs toward him again. Vex catches the baton cleanly without looking at the camera.]

VEX: That felt easier.

NARI: You watched the runner.

ODO: We can use that as the training montage.

PIP: It was one handoff.

ACT THREE

[The official race begins. Pip starts smoothly. Vex waits at the exchange line, eyes fixed on Pip rather than the camera. The baton passes into his hand inside the marked zone. He accelerates, then sees Odo running alongside with a microphone.] 

VEX: The camera is in lane three.

ODO: I am capturing your effort.

[Odo steps behind the cone and blocks the shadow mark. Vex looks down; the actual white line remains visible. He runs through the zone and passes to Pip again. The second handoff is legal, but the long branch from earlier rolls out of the equipment cart and lies across the finish line.] 

PIP: Obstacle!

[Vex leaps over it, lands inside the lane, and carries the baton across. Pip follows and crosses the finish first. Mara checks the clock.] 

MARA: New team record.

ODO: By how much?

MARA: One second.

NARI: That's a record, not a crisis.

[The branch rolls again and knocks the timing tripod sideways. Odo catches the camera. The runners help Mara reset the finish markers.] 

VEX: We have completed the race and the crew's warm-up.

PIP: The branch is still not a baton.

ODO: It is the story of the race.

MARA: It is a fallen branch. Move it off the track.

[They lift it together and carry it to the shade tree. Pip hangs the regulation baton from one end.]

PIP: Now it has a use.

NARI: The baton has a rack.

TAG

[Odo records the final shot. A gust rolls the branch out of frame; the baton stays on its hook.] 

ODO: The baton has survived its season.

MARA: It is still one race old.
