# The Reunion Special Has No Old Footage

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reunion specials that promise nostalgic clips, then stretch a missing archive into a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Rali, a macaque; former contestants Toma, a gibbon, and Feni, an orangutan; archivist Pava, a lemur; producer Seli, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. REUNION STUDIO - NIGHT

A screen reads TEN YEARS OF MEMORIES. Rali turns to the guests.

RALI: Let's watch your first challenge.

[The screen displays a blue error box: TAPE NOT FOUND.]

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Seli whispers that the archive is still being searched. Rali asks Toma and Feni to describe the challenge. Toma remembers a bridge made of paper. Feni remembers a bucket of feathers. Pava checks the catalog. The episode is labeled BRIDGE OF FEATHERS, although neither guest remembers the title.

TOMA: I remember carrying a bucket.

FENI: I remember building a bridge.

Rali asks if they were on the same episode. The producer cues a montage. The screen shows a row of empty shelves. Pava says the footage is stored on a drive whose label has peeled off. Seli asks him to identify it by the episode number. There are three different numbers on the box.

ACT TWO

INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - LATER

Pava connects the drive. The computer asks for a password. Toma guesses the show's name; it fails. Feni guesses the year; it fails. Rali tries the reunion date. The screen opens a folder of rehearsal stills. Seli asks whether those count as old footage. Pava says they are from yesterday's tribute shoot.

[The guests look at a photo of themselves wearing new costumes that imitate their old costumes. The seam tags are still visible.]

TOMA: That is not my old jacket.

FENI: It has the new tag.

Rali returns to the set and asks the guests to recreate their first challenge from memory. Pava finds a clip, but it is a camera test of an empty stage. Seli asks whether the show can use it under voice-over. Rali narrates a dramatic bridge crossing while the empty stage remains on screen. Toma and Feni walk behind the monitor, pretending to cross it. Their feet appear below the picture.

INT. REUNION STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Pava discovers a shoebox of photographs under the archive desk. The first image shows Toma holding a bucket. The second shows Feni balancing a paper bridge. They compare the backgrounds and realize the photos are from different episodes. Seli asks if the title was combined for the reunion graphic. Pava says the graphic team joined two file labels to save space. Rali asks for the corrected titles. The screen now lists both episode names, each on its own line.

INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Pava checks the two photograph envelopes against the labels. One says FEATHER RELAY; the other says PAPER BRIDGE. Toma recognizes the striped backdrop in the first photo. Feni recognizes a painted lighthouse in the second. Seli holds both cards up to the camera, making sure the episode numbers do not overlap. The screen no longer suggests they were one combined challenge.

ACT THREE

INT. REUNION STUDIO - NIGHT

Rali introduces the separate memories. Toma describes carrying feathers across the set. Feni describes testing a paper bridge with a stack of beanbags. Pava finds the correct stills and displays them one at a time. There is no surviving video. The guests laugh at how different their recollections were. Seli asks for the dramatic reveal music. Rali says the guests have already identified the mix-up.

Toma and Feni reconstruct a small version of each challenge using paper, a bucket, and soft blocks. Toma carries the bucket without spilling. Feni places one block on the paper bridge. It bends but holds. The camera stays on the props instead of switching to an imagined archival clip. Rali asks which part they want to remember. Toma says the crew helped rebuild the bridge after it folded. Feni says the feather bucket took longer to sweep up than the challenge.

Pava adds a card to the archive box with both episode numbers and dates. Seli tries to announce a surprise clip. Rali points to the empty screen. Seli lowers the cue card and asks the guests to sign the corrected labels. They do. The crew shelves the photos in separate envelopes.

TAG

INT. REUNION STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Seli changes the opening graphic to MEMORY NIGHT. The screen shows the guests' two signed archive cards. Rali says the reunion is complete. Pava closes the box before the old labels can slide together again.

FINAL IMAGE: Two separate photographs appear side by side on the screen, each with its own date and episode title.
