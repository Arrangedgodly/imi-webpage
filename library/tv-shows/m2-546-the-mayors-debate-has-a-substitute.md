# The Mayor's Debate Has a Substitute

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised political debates, strict response clocks, and candidates sending stand-ins when the schedule gets difficult*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: MODERATOR, a gibbon; CANDIDATE LUMA, an orangutan; SUBSTITUTE NIX, a capuchin; CANDIDATE BRAM, a baboon; TIMEKEEPER, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL TELEVISION STUDIO - NIGHT

The moderator announces that candidate Luma is delayed. A capuchin in a sash labeled LUMA takes the podium.

MODERATOR: Are you the candidate?

SUBSTITUTE NIX: I am the substitute.

BRAM: Is that on the ballot?

The timekeeper starts the clock before the opening statement.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The moderator asks each candidate to explain the plan for fixing the town's broken fountain. Bram proposes a new pump, new pipes, and a public committee. Nix says Luma proposes a blue sign. The moderator asks whether the sign will repair the pump.

NIX: It will tell people where to stand while it is broken.

BRAM: That is not a repair.

NIX: It is a location.

The timekeeper gives each candidate ninety seconds. Bram uses the full time. Nix's answer ends in eight seconds, so the clock continues counting down while everyone waits. The moderator tries to fill the silence with a follow-up question. Nix points to the timer.

NIX: I have used my time.

MODERATOR: You have seventy-eight seconds left.

NIX: That is the clock's time.

ACT TWO

The moderator asks about bus routes. Bram proposes running the buses more often. Nix says Luma would add a stop at the fountain so residents could observe the repair. Bram accuses Nix of turning every issue into a sign. Nix says a sign can be moved.

BRAM: A bus cannot be moved by a sign.

NIX: A bus driver can read.

The moderator brings out a map. The timekeeper holds the map sideways, and the candidates argue about which route goes north. The moderator asks Nix to clarify the candidate's position. Nix takes the map and turns it. Bram admits his route proposal relies on the same detour Luma's team recommended last year. The audience murmurs.

MODERATOR: We now have agreement.

BRAM: We have a shared map.

Nix receives an earpiece message and says Luma is arriving. The moderator announces a surprise closing statement. Bram gets thirty seconds. Nix gets thirty seconds. Nix uses twenty-five seconds to say the candidate is not present, then uses the last five to ask for a new date.

INT. DEBATE STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

The moderator asks the candidates about the town's new bus schedule. Bram reads a prepared chart with eight route changes. Nix raises a hand and says the chart is upside down. Bram turns it over. The route numbers are printed on the reverse, but none match the stops on the map.

MODERATOR: Is this the schedule?

BRAM: It is the proposal.

NIX: The proposal has no pickup point.

Bram says the buses will stop at the fountain during repairs. Nix says that is the same location Luma suggested. The moderator asks whether the candidates now agree. Bram says they agree on the map but not on who drew it. The timekeeper starts the response clock while Bram is still talking. He asks for a reset. Nix says the clock can reset but the bus still has to arrive.

The audience laughs. The moderator asks Nix to describe Luma's position without the substitute's interpretation. Nix reads a note from the candidate: fix the fountain pump first, post a temporary water station near the library, and keep the bus route unchanged. Bram says he supports the water station if it does not block the stop. The moderator asks whether that is a concession. Bram says it is a practical condition.

The producer signals for a confrontation. Nix and Bram are asked to stand at opposite podiums and face each other. Nix's podium is too low. A stagehand raises it on a stack of the debate schedules. The pages slide out.

NIX: We have lost the schedule again.

BRAM: We can read the bus map.



ACT THREE

Luma finally appears at the studio entrance, carrying a broken fountain valve. The moderator asks why she sent a substitute. Luma says Nix is her campaign sign painter and had the only copy of the debate schedule. The schedule listed the broadcast an hour later.

LUMA: I came as soon as I saw the live feed.

NIX: The sign said "Tonight."

BRAM: The sign was not a time.

Luma places the valve on the table and explains her actual plan: fix the pump before replacing the sign. Bram agrees to join the repair committee if the fountain gets a working drain. The timekeeper stops the clock and announces the show ran over by one minute.

MODERATOR: We have time for one audience question.

TIMEKEEPER: We do not.

FINAL IMAGE: The candidates carry the valve out together while Nix carries the sign that says DEBATE RESCHEDULED.
