# The Campout Has a Press Team

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival documentaries that turn simple outdoor tasks into urgent expedition television*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A family tries to pitch a tent while a documentary crew insists that every ordinary decision is a survival crisis.

Cast: Host Vela, a gibbon; camper Runi, a macaque; camper Dax, a capuchin; field producer Omi, a baboon; sound operator Kes, a lemur.

Episode: "The Last Stake"

COLD OPEN

EXT. CLEARING - DAY

A folded tent rests on the grass. Runi opens the bag and lays out poles. Omi crouches behind a camera.

OMI: We are five minutes from nightfall.

RUNI: The sun is high.

VELA: A shadow has crossed the clearing. The camp must be established before darkness.

DAX: That's my tail.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO DAX'S TAIL LYING ACROSS THE TENT BAG.]

ACT ONE

Runi sorts the poles by length. Vela narrates the search for the correct end. Dax unfolds the tent, but the crew asks him to stop and repeat the reveal more slowly. The folded fabric snaps open and covers the lens.

KES: We have lost visual contact with the expedition.

DAX: We are under a tent.

VELA: The canopy has swallowed one camper.

[DAX POPS HIS HEAD OUT OF THE DOOR.]

DAX: I found the entrance.

OMI: Cut. We need you to find it from the outside.

RUNI: I have the corners. Put the stakes through the loops.

Omi rolls out a map and points to a ridge. Runi says the map shows the trailhead, not the campsite. Vela describes the ridge as a possible storm front though the sky is clear. Kes hears a bird call and asks if it is an alarm. Dax identifies it as the neighbor's parrot.

ACT TWO

The crew places a red flag beside the tent as a dramatic marker. Runi removes it because it is tied to a guyline. Vela says the camp has lost its perimeter. Omi asks Runi to explain the danger of the missing flag.

RUNI: The flag was a hazard. Now it isn't.

OMI: Could you say that with more concern?

RUNI: The flag was a hazard. Now it isn't!

DAX: That sounded like the flag was the danger and the solution.

[THE WIND LIFTS ONE CORNER OF THE TENT. THE CAMERA CREW GASPS. RUNI GRABS THE CORNER WHILE DAX HAMMERS A STAKE.]

VELA: A sudden gust threatens the shelter.

RUNI: The stake is loose. Hold the fabric while I reset it.

DAX: I have the mallet.

OMI: Stay in frame. This is the turning point.

[THE STAKE GOES IN. THE TENT STOPS FLAPPING.]

KES: The wind has been defeated.

RUNI: It changed direction.

Omi asks for an equipment check. Dax empties a pocket: a snack, a folded towel, and a tiny whistle. Vela calls the whistle a signal device. Dax says it is for finding his sleeping bag. Kes blows it; a bird answers from nearby. The crew swings the camera toward the bird, but the tent door closes in the background.

ACT TWO-B

The campers crawl inside to test the floor. The crew sets up a second camera at the entrance. Vela whispers that two explorers have entered the cave. Runi asks whether the camera can see the tent floor. Kes says only if the operator also crawls in.

Omi sends Dax back out to narrate the discovery of the water jug. Dax walks six steps, picks it up, and returns.

DAX: The water was six steps away.

VELA: We have secured a vital resource.

RUNI: It was our jug.

Omi asks if they can film the journey again at dusk. Runi points to a line of cloud and says they should pack the camera stands before rain. Vela calls this a weather emergency. Kes checks the sky and agrees that a shower is coming. The crew finally helps fold the stands instead of narrating them.

ACT TWO-C

The first drops fall while the crew coils cables. Runi and Dax pull the tent fly into place. Omi tries to hold a reflector over the camera instead of the open bag of gear. Vela continues her narration until water runs down the microphone.

VELA: The rain has reached the expedition's voice.

KES: The voice is wet. The tent is dry.

DAX: We are ready to go inside.

OMI: One last shot of the storm.

RUNI: You can film it from under the fly.

[OMI TAKES TWO STEPS INTO THE TENT. THE CAMERA FITS; THE PRODUCER DOES NOT.]

OMI: I can hear the rain very clearly.

ACT THREE

The rain eases. The tent stands, the gear is dry, and everyone sits beneath the fly. Vela announces that the camp has survived the nightfall. Runi points at the bright western sky: it is still afternoon.

RUNI: We pitched a tent before it got dark.

DAX: And the camera learned where the door is.

OMI: We have enough for the opening episode.

TAG

Kes plays the tiny whistle. The neighbor's parrot answers from the tent roof.

VELA: A visitor has found our camp.

RUNI: That's our neighbor.

[THE PARROT SAYS "CUT." THE CREW LOOKS AT OMI.]

[MUSIC: EXPEDITION THEME.]
