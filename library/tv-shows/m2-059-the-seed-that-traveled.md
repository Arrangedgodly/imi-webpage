# The Seed That Traveled

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: serialized adventure shows, cliffhanger rescues, and recap-heavy episodes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A young courier carries a rare seed across the grove before sunset, while each obstacle is made more suspenseful by the show's relentless recap narrator.

Cast: Courier Piri, a squirrel monkey; navigator Talo, a macaque; bridge keeper Oma, a baboon; narrator Vale, a gorilla; and rival courier Fen, a gibbon.

## Episode: "The Last Crossing"

### Cold open

EXT. FOREST PATH, DAY

[Piri clutches a small seed in a leaf packet. A wooden bridge sways over a narrow creek.]

VALE: Previously, the seed began its journey.

PIRI: This is still today.

TALO: The bridge is swaying.

OMA: It always sways.

FEN: The bridge is closed.

PIRI: The bridge is not closed.

OMA: It is closed to running.

VALE: Our courier faces an impossible choice.

PIRI: I can walk.

[The bridge steadies. Vale's ominous music continues.]

### Act One

EXT. BRIDGE

[Talo studies a hand-drawn map. The creek ripples below.]

TALO: The map says the bridge is here.

OMA: You are standing on it.

PIRI: It also says the seed should be planted before sunset.

FEN: You will never make it.

PIRI: The grove is on the other side.

FEN: There is another path.

TALO: The path goes through the berry patch.

FEN: It is shorter.

OMA: It has a fence.

FEN: The fence is low.

VALE: The rival reveals a dangerous shortcut.

PIRI: How low?

FEN: Low enough for you.

[Piri looks at the bridge. The bridge creaks.]

TALO: We can cross one at a time.

OMA: That is the safe way.

FEN: It is the slow way.

PIRI: The seed is small. It can wait in my pocket.

VALE: But can the audience wait?

PIRI: The audience is watching a bridge.

### Act Two

EXT. BERRY PATCH

[Piri takes the short path. A low fence separates the patch from the grove. Fen is already on the far side.]

PIRI: You crossed already.

FEN: I am the rival courier.

TALO: The fence has a gap.

FEN: The gap is hidden.

OMA: It is behind you.

FEN: I knew that.

VALE: In the previous episode, the gap was not there.

TALO: This is the same fence.

VALE: The recap has changed.

[Piri slips through the gap. The leaf packet catches on a twig.]

PIRI: The seed is stuck.

TALO: Don't pull.

FEN: Pull gently.

OMA: Both are the same instruction.

[Piri frees the packet. A tiny seed rolls onto the ground.]

PIRI: We need to find it.

VALE: The seed is lost.

TALO: It is beside your foot.

PIRI: I see it.

FEN: That was the entire cliffhanger?

VALE: We will return after the break.

[They wait. Nothing happens.]

OMA: Are we back?

VALE: We have returned.

PIRI: I have the seed.

TALO: We can continue.

FEN: The fence has moved.

[The fence is still where it was. Fen has stepped around it.]

### Act Three

EXT. PLANTING GROVE, SUNSET

[Piri and Talo reach the planting site. Oma follows with a small trowel. Fen catches up.]

VALE: The final crossing is at hand.

PIRI: There is no crossing here.

TALO: The creek loops around the hill.

OMA: The planting site is beside the tree.

FEN: We made the long trip for one seed.

PIRI: One seed can grow a tree.

VALE: The courier must now choose a place.

TALO: This side gets morning light.

OMA: The soil is damp.

FEN: That spot is just right.

PIRI: It is my delivery. I will choose.

[Piri digs a small hole. The seed slips from the leaf and falls in.]

PIRI: It is planted.

VALE: A triumph after many obstacles.

TALO: There were two obstacles.

FEN: And one recap.

OMA: The seed has reached its destination.

PIRI: We can come back tomorrow to water it.

FEN: I will bring a cup.

TALO: The creek is nearby.

FEN: I will bring a cup anyway.

VALE: The journey has ended, but the story continues.

PIRI: The seed is in the ground.

VALE: The ground may be dangerous.

OMA: It is soil.

VALE: We will find out next week.

PIRI: We can find out now.

[They look at the seedling. A small leaf appears.]

TALO: It has started.

FEN: That was fast.

OMA: The soil was ready.

VALE: The next episode begins.

### Recap within the recap

EXT. PLANTING GROVE, MOMENTS LATER

[Vale's voice overlays the scene. Piri holds the seed above the hole.]

VALE: Previously, the seed was in Piri's hand.

PIRI: It is still in my hand.

VALE: The recap is catching up.

TALO: If we wait, the sun will set.

FEN: If we plant it now, the recap will miss the ending.

PIRI: The seed does not need a recap.

[She places it in the soil.]

VALE: The seed has entered the ground.

TALO: The recap has ended.

FEN: We can use that line next week.

PIRI: No. Next week we water it.

### Tag

EXT. PLANTING GROVE, MORNING

[Piri returns with a cup. The new seedling is wet from dew.]

PIRI: I brought water.

TALO: The weather got here first.

VALE: Previously, the seed was planted.

[The camera pulls back to show the grove in morning light.]
