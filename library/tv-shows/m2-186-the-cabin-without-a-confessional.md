# The Cabin Without a Confessional

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating reality show convention, where contestants seek private interviews in a cabin with no walls and the cameras keep wandering in*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A romance show builds a "private" cabin from leaves, then discovers every contestant can hear every confession through the thin walls.

Cast: Host Miri, a macaque; contestants Tavi, a gibbon; Piri, a capuchin; and Koto, a mandrill camera operator.

Episode: "Quiet Hearts"

COLD OPEN

EXT. REALITY SET, DAY

Miri welcomes the contestants to a romantic retreat. A leaf-built hut stands beside a stream. Tavi leans toward Piri.

TAVI: The walls are transparent.

PIRI: They are leaves.

Miri says the cabin offers privacy for heartfelt conversations. A breeze lifts one wall. The camera operator lowers the camera so it appears to be a private moment.

ACT ONE

INT. LEAF CABIN, AFTERNOON

Tavi enters for a private interview. He whispers that he likes Piri's laugh. Piri is sitting outside, clearly hearing him. She whispers that she heard him. Miri announces a connection.

Koto adjusts the camera. The lens points through a gap between leaves. He moves to the other side. The camera now films the cabin's interior from the outside.

MIRI: We need a confession that feels intimate.

TAVI: I can say it louder.

Piri enters the cabin. The leaves rustle loudly. She asks Tavi what he likes about her. He says her laugh. She laughs. The sound carries across the set, where the other contestants applaud.

ACT TWO

EXT. STREAM, LATER

Miri schedules a surprise date. Tavi and Piri are given a picnic basket and told they are alone. Koto films from behind a rock. The rock is too small to hide him; his camera lens sticks out like a third eye.

Piri spots him and waves. Koto waves back. Miri asks him to preserve the illusion. Koto moves to a tree branch, but the branch bends. He lowers himself into the frame behind the couple.

TAVI: We can talk after the crew leaves.

PIRI: How will we know?

Miri steps in to say the date is over. Tavi asks if the show can give them a quiet moment. Miri checks the schedule. There is a two-minute gap before the next challenge.

They sit beside the water. Koto stays behind the rock and turns the camera off. Miri signals the crew to wait. The couple talks without a microphone. The other contestants gather nearby, trying to stay out of frame.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. STREAM, EVENING

Miri asks Tavi and Piri to share a final challenge: find a quiet place where they can talk. They walk beside the stream while Koto films from a distance. Every few steps, Miri appears with a card telling them to "share a meaningful detail." Piri says the cards make the conversation feel like a test.

TAVI: I like how you notice when someone needs help.

PIRI: I like that you say what you mean, even when the cameras are there.

The crew waits for a kiss cue. None comes. Miri looks at the card, then puts it down. The stream burbles louder than the microphones. Koto lowers the boom so it does not enter the shot. A frog jumps onto a leaf beside the couple.

PIRI: It has found the quiet spot.

Miri asks whether the frog is part of the scene. Koto says it is not on the call sheet. The frog jumps into the water. Piri and Tavi laugh, and the sound technician catches it cleanly.

Miri asks the crew to move farther away. Koto does. The camera loses focus, then holds a wide image of the stream, the trees and the pair talking. The producers ask for a closer shot. Miri refuses, saying the couple requested privacy. She gives the crew a different task: dismantle the leaf cabin before wind scatters it.

Tavi and Piri help fold the leaves. They decide they would rather meet again without a planned date. Miri asks how the show will describe their choice. Piri suggests "two people leaving the set." Tavi says the set can keep the cabin.

ACT THREE


EXT. SET CLEARING, EVENING

Miri asks the contestants to choose the most romantic location. Everyone votes for the stream because it is the only place without a wall. Koto sets the leaf cabin aside and uses the leaves to make shade for the equipment.

Tavi and Piri decide to leave the show together, but not as a finale couple. They want to keep talking without a camera. Miri respects the choice and asks for one closing line.

PIRI: The stream was nice.

TAVI: The cameras were not.

Miri asks Koto to turn off every mic. He does. A small frog croaks from the water. The crew turns their heads toward the sound.

TAG

EXT. STREAM, NIGHT

Miri sits alone for her host wrap-up. She whispers to camera that the set has become quiet. Behind her, the leaves rustle as a crew member packs them. Miri turns around.

MIRI: We are still recording, aren't we?

Koto holds up the unplugged camera. The frog croaks again. Miri gives it the closing shot.
