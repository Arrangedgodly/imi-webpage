# Three Minutes to Plate

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: timed cooking game shows, where contestants decorate a simple snack while the clock keeps resetting*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Contestants have three minutes to plate fruit, but the host keeps resetting the timer whenever a garnish falls.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; contestant Piri, capuchin; contestant Seli, gibbon; judge Nara, macaque; assistant Tavi, lemur; executive producer Pava, squirrel monkey.

## Episode: "Fruit in a Hurry"

### Cold Open

[COUNTER. FRUIT AND TWO PLATES.]

ODO: You have three minutes.

PIRI: To eat it?

NARA: To plate it.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE TIMER STARTS.]

Piri arranges apple slices. Seli makes a row of berries. Odo calls out the time every ten seconds.

ODO: Two minutes, fifty seconds.

NARA: Let them work.

Tavi offers a small leaf for garnish. Piri places it on the plate. A fan blows it onto the floor.

PIRI: The garnish has fallen.

ODO: We reset the clock.

NARA: Why?

ODO: The contestants lost time.

NARA: The clock is the challenge.

Seli asks to keep the plate near the wall, away from the fan. Odo says all plates must stay at the counter's edge.

### Act Two

[THE CLOCK RESTARTS.]

Piri sets the fruit in a circle. Seli uses a leaf under the berries. Tavi turns off the fan, but the studio becomes warm.

ODO: We need airflow.

NARA: The fan is moving the garnish.

Seli cuts the apple slices thinner. Piri asks if she can borrow a leaf. Nara says each contestant has the same ingredients.

PIRI: The leaves are from the same plant.

NARA: They are still on her plate.

Odo announces one minute. Seli's berries roll toward the edge. She catches them with a ring of apple slices. The plate is stable.

ODO: Time.

NARA: The timer has thirty seconds.

ODO: I thought it was one minute.

Tavi shows him the timer; it is running backward from three minutes, and Odo has been reading it upside down.

[THE JUDGES INTRODUCE A BONUS INGREDIENT.]

Tavi brings out a small bowl of seeds and says the contestants can add them if they finish before the timer. Odo restarts the clock at three minutes, even though it has not reset.

NARA: We already plated.

ODO: The bonus round begins now.

PIRI: The plates are being eaten.

Seli moves her plate out of reach. Piri puts a few seeds on the empty space. The fan is still off, so the garnish stays put.

NARA: We need equal conditions.

Odo turns on the fan. The seeds scatter.

TAVI: The fan is not part of the challenge.

Odo turns it off again. The camera light warms the counter and the berries soften slightly. Nara asks the crew to move the lamp.

PAVA: The lighting affects the plating.

SELI: We are still using the same ingredients.

Piri makes a small circle with the seeds. Seli arranges the remaining seeds in a line. Nara tastes one from each plate and asks them to explain why they chose the pattern.

PIRI: The circle keeps the berries together.

SELI: The line makes the plate easier to divide.

ODO: We have two solutions.

NARA: And two plates.

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK HITS ZERO.]

The final berry rolls toward the plate's edge. Piri cups both hands around the plate; Seli blocks it with an apple slice. They save the fruit as the timer beeps.

ODO: Time.

NARA: Both plates are complete.

Odo reaches to reset the clock. Nara takes the knob away and turns the timer around so he can see the numbers.

PIRI: It is done.

SELI: The fruit is still here.

The crew leans in for the close-up. Tavi says the camera needs a clean frame; Odo tells everyone not to touch the garnish. One seed rolls from the bonus bowl and lands exactly in the center of Piri's plate. The camera zooms so close that the seed fills the entire screen.

ODO: A surprise final garnish.

NARA: That was not the challenge.

PIRI: But it is on my plate.

Odo asks for a replay of the landing. Nara points to the zeroed timer; the round is over, and the seed has already been counted as garnish.

Nara tastes each plate once and names no winner: one is neat, the other survived the camera crew. The contestants split the fruit with the crew before Odo can demand a second tasting.

SELI: The clock ran once.

NARA: The round is over.

Odo looks for the reset button. Tavi puts the timer in a drawer and closes it.

### Tag

[THE DRAWER IS CLOSED.]

The drawer gives one muffled beep. Odo reaches for the handle, but Tavi has placed the ribbon over it like a seal.

ODO: The timer wants another round.

NARA: The drawer is closed.

PIRI: The show is over.

[SFX: MUFFLED BEEP, THEME OUT.]
