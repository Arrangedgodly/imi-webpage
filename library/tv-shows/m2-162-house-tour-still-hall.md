# The House Tour Is Still in the Hall

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: celebrity home-tour programs that manufacture intimacy from staged glimpses and luxury props*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Celebrity host Zola agrees to a tour of her modest treehouse but her publicist keeps hiding ordinary possessions and placing luxury props in frame. The camera operator keeps finding the practical details that make the home work.

Cast: Zola, a gibbon singer; Perri, her macaque publicist; Tavi, a capuchin camera operator; and Odo, a langur neighbor who built the stairs.

## Episode: "My Favorite Quiet Corner"

### Cold Open

[TREEHOUSE ENTRY. PERRI MOVES A BOOT TRAY OUT OF FRAME. ZOLA MOVES IT BACK UNDER A DRIP FROM THE ROOF.] 

PERRI: The tour begins with your favorite room.

ZOLA: The entry. It keeps the rain off the books.

TAVI: The entry is three steps wide.

PERRI: We can call it a foyer.

[ODO CLIMBS THE STAIRS WITH A TOOL BAG.]

ODO: The handrail is loose again.

PERRI: Can we do that after the close-up?

ZOLA: It is the close-up.

[PERRI SETS A CRYSTAL VASE ON THE ENTRY SHELF. THE FLOOR SLOPES; THE VASE SLIDES TOWARD THE EDGE. ODO CATCHES IT AND SHIMS THE SHELF WITH A THIN WOOD OFFCUT.]

TAVI: The vase has moved more than the camera.

ZOLA: The shelf was built to hold shoes.

PERRI: The vase is the elegant detail.

ODO: The shoes are the detail that fits.

### Act One

[CAMERA ROLLS. A SMALL KITCHEN TABLE HOLDS A MUG, A REPAIR MANUAL, AND A BOWL OF WASHED BERRIES. PERRI REMOVES THE MANUAL.] 

PERRI: The table should look effortless.

ZOLA: I use the book when I fix things.

TAVI: What is this mark on the wall?

ZOLA: The height chart for my nieces.

[PERRI HANGS A FRAMED AWARD OVER THE MARK. TAVI LOWERS THE CAMERA TO SHOW THE CHILDREN'S PENCIL LINES BESIDE IT.] 

ZOLA: Leave the marks. They visit every week.

[ODO TESTS THE HANDRAIL. IT SHIFTS. HE TIGHTENS A BOLT; PERRI TRIES TO SLIDE A DECORATIVE PILLOW OVER THE TOOLBOX.] 

ODO: The pillow cannot fix a bolt.

### Act Two

[IN THE MUSIC ROOM, PERRI SETS A GOLD RECORD ON A NARROW SHELF. IT TILTS.]

PERRI: This is the intimate side of fame.

ZOLA: This room is where I practice.

[TAVI FRAMES A SOUNDPROOFING PANEL MADE FROM RECYCLED BLANKETS. PERRI TRIES TO COVER IT WITH A SILK DRAPE.] 

ZOLA: The blankets keep the neighbors from hearing me rehearse at dawn.

ODO: They also keep the room warm.

[PERRI PULLS THE DRAPE. THE GOLD RECORD SLIDES TOWARD THE EDGE. TAVI CATCHES IT, THEN TURNS THE CAMERA TO THE SHELF BRACKET.] 

TAVI: This needs two screws.

PERRI: We have a stylist.

ODO: A stylist does not have a level.

[ODO INSTALLS A SECOND BRACKET. ZOLA HOLDS THE LEVEL. PERRI HOLDS THE RECORD WITH BOTH HANDS, AFRAID TO LET GO.] 

ZOLA: You can set it down now.

PERRI: It looks more valuable when I hold it.

[TAVI PANS TO A STACK OF REHEARSAL NOTES AND A REPAIRED MUSIC STAND. PERRI REACHES TO HIDE THEM. ZOLA PUTS THE NOTES BACK ON THE TABLE.]

ZOLA: We can leave the work in the room.

TAVI: The show is called a home tour.

PERRI: I thought it was called "inside the star's world."

### Act Three

[THE TOUR REACHES A SMALL BALCONY WITH A CLEAR VIEW OF THE CANOPY. PERRI SETS A BOTTLE OF SPARKLING WATER ON THE RAIL.] 

PERRI: One last elegant moment.

[THE BOTTLE ROLLS. ZOLA CATCHES IT AND PUTS IT IN A CUP HOLDER ODO BUILT INTO THE RAIL.]

ZOLA: This is my favorite feature.

ODO: It was for your tea.

TAVI: It works for both.

[PERRI ASKS ZOLA TO GIVE A CLOSING LINE. ZOLA POINTS OUT THE STAIRS, HANDRAIL, AND REPAIR BOOK. THE CAMERA MOVES THROUGH THE HOME AS THE CREW PUTS EVERY ITEM BACK WHERE IT BELONGS.] 

ZOLA: People see a song on a stage. Here I keep the things I use, and the people who help me keep them safe.

[PERRI PUTS THE DECORATIVE PILLOW BACK ON A CHAIR. THE CHAIR IS WOBBLY. ODO TIGHTENS ITS LEG BEFORE THE CAMERA FINDS IT.] 

[TAVI ASKS PERRI TO RECREATE THE ENTRANCE FOR A PICKUP SHOT. PERRI ENTERS, MOVES THE BOOT TRAY, THEN WATCHES ZOLA SLIDE IT BACK INTO PLACE.]

PERRI: The tray is in every take.

ZOLA: It catches rain in every season.

[ODO CHECKS THE RAIL ONE LAST TIME. IT HOLDS. THE CAMERA RECORDS THE HANDRAIL, THE TOOL, AND THE THREE SET OF BOOTS BY THE DOOR.] 

### Tag

[THE CAMERAS STOP. PERRI FINDS THE REPAIR MANUAL OPEN ON THE TABLE.]

PERRI: You left the book in the shot.

ZOLA: It is my house.

[TAVI PANS TO THE LEVEL ON THE SHELF. THE GOLD RECORD IS STRAIGHT.]
