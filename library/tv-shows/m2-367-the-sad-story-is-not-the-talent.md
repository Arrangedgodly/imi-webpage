# The Sad Story Is Not the Talent

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised talent auditions that demand a personal confession before allowing a performance*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A confident orangutan tap dancer must get the judges to watch her routine while the producers keep asking for a tragic backstory.

Cast: Host Lolo, a gibbon; contestant Una, an orangutan; judge Brin, a macaque; judge Tavi, a capuchin; producer Neri, a baboon; stagehand, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. TALENT SHOW STAGE - NIGHT

Una steps onto a small platform wearing tap shoes. The judges lean forward.

LOLO: Before you perform, tell us what you had to overcome.

UNA: The platform is too small for my routine.

NERI (IN EARPIECE): Ask about childhood.

LOLO: What was your childhood like?

UNA: I had a larger platform.

ACT ONE

Neri signals to the camera crew: FIND TEARS. Una looks at the empty seats, then at the judges.

UNA: Would you like me to perform?

BRIN: Yes. We have a countdown segment after you.

TAVI: Your shoes sound ready.

LOLO: Can we get a close-up of your hands?

UNA: Tap dancing uses my feet.

Lolo asks about her first rejection. Una says a show once refused to let her dance because they thought tap shoes were too loud. Neri brightens and signals for a sad violin cue. The cue plays over Una's next words.

UNA: The floor was carpeted.

BRIN: That's a real obstacle.

UNA: It was difficult to tap.

Neri switches on a fan to ruffle Una's costume for a dramatic close-up. Her fringe blows across the microphone and taps against it. The judges hear an accidental rhythm.

TAVI: There is the beat.

ACT TWO

Lolo introduces a short audition. Una asks for the stage to be cleared. The camera operator leaves a rolling microphone stand directly in the center. Una starts a simple pattern, tap-tap-pause, then circles the stand without touching it. The stand wheels after her as its brake is not locked.

LOLO: The microphone is following the act.

UNA: Please stop the microphone.

Neri whispers to Lolo to let the moment build. Una catches the stand by its base. Brin steps in and locks the wheels. Una resumes, switching rhythm each time the stage light changes. Tavi claps on the wrong beats. Brin quietly corrects her, then finds himself joining the rhythm.

BRIN: That's an eight-count.

TAVI: I know. I started on seven.

Una finishes with a heel-toe pattern. The audience applauds. Neri signals for a close-up on the judges' tears. Tavi is smiling, not crying.

NERI: Ask if she thought she would make it this far.

LOLO: Did you think you would make it this far?

UNA: I walked in from the lobby.

ACT TWO-B

Neri says they need one more take for the audition package. Una asks whether the judges have already scored her. Brin says her performance is complete; the scorecard is waiting. Neri argues that the viewers need a story to follow.

NERI: Give us a setback before the vote.

UNA: The setback was a rolling stand.

Neri rolls the stand back to center stage. Una points at it. The crew locks its wheels and moves it offstage. She gives the cameraman one useful suggestion: frame her feet and leave space around the turns. He changes the shot.

TAVI: I liked the part where we could see the dancing.

NERI: But we did not get the confessional.

UNA: You can ask me why I dance.

Lolo turns back to camera.

LOLO: Una, why do you dance?

UNA: It is satisfying to make a clean rhythm.

BRIN: That's enough for me.

The judges mark their cards. Neri tries a slow-motion replay. The edit catches Una standing still while the microphone stand rolls by itself. Everyone looks at the screen.

ACT THREE

Lolo announces the result: two yes votes and one no from Tavi, who says she likes the act but wants more practice on the narrow platform. Una accepts the critique and asks if she can return with a wider stage. Brin says the next round has a full floor.

TAVI: Then I can vote yes next time.

UNA: I'd rather you vote based on the act.

Tavi changes her vote. The panel advances Una to the next round. Neri asks for an emotional close-up. Una thanks the stage crew for locking the stand and exits on the same rhythm she performed.

TAG

In the green room, the stand's wheels roll a few inches. Una stops it with one tap of her shoe. The stagehand clicks the brake.

STAGEHAND: That is the last one.

UNA: Good. I have no more backstory.

FINAL IMAGE: Una taps her way out of frame while the show plays a solemn violin sting over the now-stationary microphone stand.
