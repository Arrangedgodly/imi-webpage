# The Night Sky Show Points the Telescope Indoors

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live astronomy broadcasts that keep narrating through clouds, lens caps and misaligned telescopes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Pava, a lemur; astronomer Riko, an orangutan; camera operator Suma, a macaque; producer Telo, a capuchin; caller Deni, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. OBSERVATORY DOME - NIGHT

A narrow shutter slit cuts the dome wall. Riko stands at a mounted telescope aimed across the room. Its tube points at a laminated FIRE EXIT sign. A separate broadcast camera on a tripod faces the telescope's capped eyepiece. The monitor shows a black circle.

PAVA: The universe is opening before us.

RIKO: The fire exit is opening before us.

ACT ONE

INT. OBSERVATORY DOME - CONTINUOUS

Telo counts down in Pava's earpiece. Suma checks the camera monitor and frames the eyepiece cap in a dramatic close-up. Riko turns the telescope toward the shutter slit, but its barrel stops against the padded inner dome wall. Telo asks whether the wall can be described as a curtain of space. Riko says it is painted plywood.

Suma pans from the cap to the telescope finder scope, which is aimed at a ceiling lamp. Riko takes the cap off the eyepiece and places it in a yellow tray marked CAPS. The monitor now shows the finder scope's bright lamp. Pava calls it a young star. Riko taps the finder housing and asks Suma to frame the tube, not the finder.

Suma pulls back. The full telescope appears, swung back toward the sign to clear the padded wall while the shutter opens. Telo tells Pava to keep speaking while Riko adjusts it. Pava narrates the number of bolts on the mount. Riko swivels the tube to the slit and lines up the finder with the moon. Suma's tripod camera records the movement; Telo asks for a close-up, but Suma keeps both the instrument and target direction in frame.

ACT TWO

INT. OBSERVATORY DOME - LATER

The dome motor opens the shutter another handspan. Riko looks through the eyepiece, then points to a small lever. Suma's camera zooms in on it. Telo says the lever could be the night's reveal. Riko turns it; the telescope tracks the ceiling lamp because the mount's preset is labeled STUDIO STAR.

RIKO: That is our lighting department.

Pava asks whether they can call it a binary system. Riko resets the mount to MANUAL and points to the coordinates on his paper chart. He does not touch the eyepiece cap in the yellow tray. Suma follows the movement on the tripod camera; the broadcast monitor shows the tube, the slit and the open patch of sky in one shot.

A cloud slides over the moon. Telo gives Pava a card reading UNEXPECTED CELESTIAL DRAMA. Riko says the cloud is expected; it is on the forecast pinned beside the chart. Pava reads the forecast aloud as though it were a rare discovery. Deni calls from a backyard under clear sky.

DENI: I can see the moon from here.

Telo asks him to send a picture. Deni holds up his phone, but Suma says a phone close-up would replace the actual telescope feed. Riko asks Deni to describe the crescent instead. Deni gives its position above a chimney. Riko checks the chart and confirms the direction from the dome slit.

ACT THREE

INT. OBSERVATORY DOME - LATER

The cloud clears. Riko removes the eyepiece and installs the small imaging camera from its padded case at the focuser, connecting its feed to a separate telescope-view monitor. He sets the mount to the checked coordinates and turns the manual tracking knob. Suma holds the camera on the monitor, where the moon enters as a pale crescent. The image wobbles once when Telo bumps the production table. He freezes with his hand raised. Riko steadies the mount and asks for ten seconds without zoom or narration.

Pava looks at the silent host card, flips it face down and waits. The crescent sharpens. Riko names one crater and explains how the grazing light makes its rim visible. Suma keeps the framing wide enough to show the moon against the dark field. Deni confirms the same crescent from his backyard, this time with his phone lowered.

Telo silently holds up a card reading BIGGER. Riko shakes his head. Pava continues without magnification, and the audience sees the small clear image on the monitor. Telo lowers the card. The shot holds while Riko notes the time and Suma records the image. No one moves the camera or telescope.

TAG

INT. CONTROL ROOM - AFTER THE SHOW

Suma stops the recording and checks the opening footage. The first image is the fire exit sign, followed by the black eyepiece cap. Telo labels the clip WRONG DIRECTION and saves it for the outtakes. Riko lifts the cap tray and sets it on the shelf, away from the mount.

FINAL IMAGE: The monitor holds one sharp crescent moon; below it, the yellow tray contains the cap and the fire-exit sign remains outside the camera frame.
