# The Practice Tree

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports competition broadcasts and their obsessive instant replay of small animal athletic feats*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A troop prepares for a branch-to-branch relay. The announcer calls every leap historic, while the competitors keep stopping to check one another's footing.

Cast: Announcer Venn, a lemur; coach Tali, a macaque; runners Boro, a gibbon, and Eko, a capuchin; and judge Sela, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The Safest Leap"

### Cold Open

[A ROPE MARKS A STARTING LINE UNDER A LOW TREE.]

VENN: The great relay begins now.

[THE RUNNERS LOOK UP. A SMALL BRANCH BENDS.]

TALI: We test it first.

VENN: The event has already begun.

SELA: The branch has not passed inspection.

### Act One

[SELA CHECKS EACH BRANCH WITH A POLE.]

BORO: I can take the high route.

EKO: The high route is wet.

TALI: We can use the low branch and pass the marker.

VENN: The team abandons the dramatic ascent.

BORO: We are choosing the sturdy route.

[THE RACE STARTS. BORO MOVES ACROSS THE LOW BRANCHES. EKO WAITS AT THE MARKER.]

VENN: A thrilling handoff.

[EKO REACHES FOR THE MARKER, BUT IT IS ON THE FAR SIDE OF THE TRUNK.]

EKO: I cannot reach it safely.

TALI: Move it to the center.

SELA: The rules say the marker may be moved before the race.

[THEY RESET IT.]

### Act Two

[THE RELAY CONTINUES. A BIRD LANDS ON THE NEXT BRANCH.]

VENN: A surprise challenger has entered the course.

SELA: It is a bird.

TALI: We will wait until it leaves.

[THE RUNNERS PAUSE. THE BIRD FLIES AWAY.]

VENN: The clock is running.

BORO: The branch is clear now.

[EKO TAKES THE PASS. HE LEAPS TO THE NEXT BRANCH AND STOPS TO CHECK THE LANDING.]

VENN: He could have continued.

EKO: The bark is loose.

[SELA INSPECTS IT. A PIECE OF BARK FALLS.]

SELA: The pause prevented a slip.

VENN: The pause is now part of the broadcast.

[THE ANNOUNCER REVIEWS THE SLOW-MOTION FOOTAGE.]

VENN: Here is the moment Eko paused before the loose bark.

EKO: I saw it move.

VENN: Here is Boro checking the branch.

BORO: The branch was sturdy.

SELA: The replay shows the team making safe choices.

VENN: It does not show a dramatic finish.

TALI: The finish is everyone returning to the starting line.

[THE TEAM WALKS THE ROUTE AGAIN WITHOUT THE CAMERA CREW. THEY POINT OUT A LOW BRANCH THAT NEEDS A MARKER.]

SELA: We should place a warning ribbon there.

BORO: I can tie it.

[HE TIES THE RIBBON. IT HANGS TOO LOW.]

EKO: It may catch someone.

[TALI MOVES IT HIGHER. THE BRANCH IS MARKED.]

VENN: A course adjustment after the event.

SELA: A safer course for the next event.

[THE TEAM CARRIES THE WATER BOWL TO THE SHADE. THE TROPHY STAYS ON THE FINISH STUMP.]

TALI: The marker is ready.

VENN: The prize is still here.

BORO: Someone will bring it back after the next race.

[THE SMALL MONKEY PICKS UP THE TROPHY, SETS IT BESIDE THE WATER, AND WALKS AWAY.]

[THE TEAM GATHERS AROUND THE STUMP AFTER THE CAMERA PACKS.]

TALI: The course is safe for the next runners.

EKO: I can show them where the bark was loose.

BORO: We should also test the branch after rain.

SELA: That belongs in the course notes.

[VENN RETURNS WITH A SCRIPT CARD.]

VENN: The network wants a dramatic quote for the ending.

TALI: We were careful.

VENN: Can you say it louder?

TALI: We were careful.

[THE TEAM LOOKS AT HER.]

BORO: That sounded dramatic.

EKO: It sounded true.

[THEY CARRY THE MARKER BACK TO THE START. THE WATER BOWL REMAINS UNDER THE TREE.]

VENN: The relay has ended.

SELA: The course is ready for tomorrow.

[THE SMALL MONKEY TAKES THE SCRIPT CARD AND USES IT TO LEVEL THE TROPHY.]

[THE TEAM TESTS THE MARKED ROUTE ONE MORE TIME. THE BIRD IS GONE, THE BRANCH IS DRY, AND THE STONES ARE FIRM.]

SELA: The course is clear.

[THEY PUT THE ROUTE NOTES IN A WATERPROOF SLEEVE AT THE STARTING LINE.]

TALI: The next team can read them before they race.

### Act Three

[THE FINAL RUNNER REACHES THE FINISH. THEY COME IN SECOND.]

VENN: A historic second place.

TALI: We completed the route.

BORO: No one fell.

EKO: And the bird got the branch.

[SELA HANDS THE TROPHY TO THE WINNERS. IT IS A SMALL GOLDEN MARKER.]

VENN: The prize is the marker from the course.

SELA: It is safe to carry.

[THE TEAM TAKES A PHOTO. THE MARKER ROLLS OFF THE STUMP. BORO CATCHES IT.]

VENN: An incredible save.

BORO: It was a short roll.

[THE TEAM RETURNS THE MARKER TO THE START LINE FOR THE NEXT RACE.]
