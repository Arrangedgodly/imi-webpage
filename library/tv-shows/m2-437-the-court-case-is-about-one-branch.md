# The Court Case Is About One Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom television that expands a minor neighborhood dispute into an elaborate legal drama*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Judge Mara, a macaque; clerk Pip, a capuchin; claimant Nari, a lemur; neighbor Vex, an orangutan; arborist Seli, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SMALL CLAIMS COURT - DAY

[A low branch rests on an evidence table. Mara peers at it through a magnifier.] 

MARA: Who moved the branch?

NARI: It fell.

VEX: It blocked my path.

[The branch rolls off the table. Pip catches it. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

MARA: This court will hear a dispute over one fallen branch.

PIP: Case number twelve. The branch has been returned to the table.

VEX: It fell from the tree in my yard and landed in the shared path.

NARI: The tree is on the boundary.

VEX: Then it is half my branch.

MARA: Trees do not divide at the property line.

[The clerk marks a map. Pip's pencil breaks. He uses the branch as a straight edge to draw the boundary.] 

PIP: The branch is now part of the record.

[The courtroom gasps.]

MARA: It was evidence before. It is not a witness.

NARI: I used it to reach a fruit that had fallen behind the fence.

VEX: She entered my yard.

NARI: My arm entered the yard. My feet stayed on the path.

[The camera cuts to the path. It is narrow and muddy. The branch is wide enough to block it.] 

MARA: We will hear from the arborist.

ACT TWO

[Seli examines the branch. A row of small scratches marks its bark.] 

SELI: It came down in a windstorm. The tree is healthy. This branch was dry.

VEX: Did it fall on my side?

SELI: It fell across the path.

NARI: I moved it to reach a fruit.

MARA: Did you carry it into Vex's yard?

NARI: I used it as a pole from the path.

[Vex demonstrates. He stands in the courtroom aisle and reaches the branch across an imaginary fence. His sleeve catches on the evidence table, pulling it toward him.] 

PIP: The aisle has moved.

VEX: I was showing the reach.

MARA: Show the reach without moving the furniture.

[The court recesses for a camera interview. Nari grooms a leaf from Vex's shoulder. Vex freezes, then begins grooming a speck from hers.] 

PIP: The parties are grooming each other.

MARA: The court is in recess.

[They sit back in their chairs.]

VEX: We have resolved the leaf matter.

NARI: Not the branch.

[The judge returns. She asks whether either neighbor tried to pick it up. Vex says the branch was too heavy. Nari says it was too long to drag. Seli places a small stone beneath one end.] 

SELI: Lift one side, roll it, then carry it together.

MARA: The court will test that method.

[Pip pins the map on the evidence board. The tree sits on the boundary line, while the shared path runs between both yards.]

PIP: Where should the branch go?

NARI: The compost pile is at the end of the path.

VEX: That is on my side.

MARA: Is the compost pile shared?

VEX: We both use it.

NARI: We both put leaves in it.

[The judge asks Seli to mark a route. Seli uses chalk to draw a line from the tree to the compost pile. The line passes through the courthouse hallway and ends at the doorway.]

PIP: The path is shorter than the legal argument.

MARA: The court will note that.

[Vex points to the map and spots a second fallen branch drawn beside the shed.]

VEX: That one is also mine.

NARI: It has been there all winter.

MARA: It is not part of this case.

[Vex folds the map so the shed disappears.]

VEX: Then I will save it for another episode.

ACT THREE

[The parties lift the branch. Vex takes one end; Nari takes the other. They carry it through the courtroom door, but it catches on the frame.] 

VEX: This doorway is still blocked.

NARI: Turn your end toward me.

[They rotate the branch. It fits through the doorway. Pip follows with the map and ruler.] 

MARA: The branch will be placed in the shared compost pile.

VEX: Who pays for moving it?

MARA: You both did.

NARI: And we used the court's evidence table as a lever.

[Pip measures the doorway. The branch is slightly longer than the room is wide.] 

PIP: It could not have fallen indoors.

MARA: That is not the dispute.

VEX: It is now an appeal.

[Mara taps the gavel. The branch rolls gently toward the compost cart, and both neighbors stop it with their feet.] 

TAG

[They leave together carrying the branch. Pip closes the file.] 

PIP: Case closed.

MARA: The branch is still open.
