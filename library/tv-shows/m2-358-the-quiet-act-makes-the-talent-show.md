# The Quiet Act Makes the Talent Show

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised talent contests that judge spectacle before the act has finished*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A contestant performs a silent balance act while the judges keep interrupting to ask for a bigger moment.

Cast: Host Venn, a baboon; contestant Kiri, a gibbon; judges Pava, a macaque, and Olo, a capuchin; stage manager Miri, a lemur.

Episode: "Wait for the Pebble"

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO - EVENING

Kiri places a pebble on a narrow board. The audience hushes.

VENN: Tonight, one performer will change our lives.

KIRI: I balance stones.

OLO: Can the stones be taller?

KIRI: They are the size they are.

[THE PEBBLE ROLLS. KIRI CATCHES IT WITH ONE FINGER.]

ACT ONE

Miri marks a small circle on the floor for Kiri's act. Venn asks whether the spotlight can move faster. Kiri says a moving light makes the stones hard to see. Pava asks if the act has a musical build. Kiri explains that the balance depends on the pebble staying still.

OLO: We need a reveal.

KIRI: The reveal is when I let go.

The judges watch a rehearsal. Kiri stacks three flat stones, pauses, and adds a round pebble. Olo leans forward and asks when the dramatic part begins. The pebble shifts; Kiri steadies the board.

PAVA: That was the dramatic part.

OLO: It looked like a pause.

KIRI: It was.

ACT TWO

Venn suggests a countdown. Kiri says the stones may fall if the audience counts in different rhythms. The audience tries anyway. Some count quickly, some slowly. The calls overlap and Kiri's shoulders tighten.

MIRI: Stop the count. Give her quiet.

VENN: The audience is part of the show.

KIRI: The stones are part of the show too.

Miri dims the applause sign and asks the studio to hold still. Kiri begins again. Olo quietly unwraps a snack; the wrapper crackles. Pava reaches to hush him and knocks her scorecard onto the stage. Kiri catches the card without disturbing the stones.

PAVA: That is a bonus skill.

KIRI: No. I heard it fall.

ACT TWO-B

The producers place a large drum beside the stage to create suspense. Miri removes it. Venn asks for one sound cue at the final moment. Kiri agrees to a single bell after the pebble is balanced.

She stacks the stones again, each one slower than the last. The camera zooms too close and loses the top. Tavi adjusts the framing. Venn whispers that the audience is waiting. Olo stops moving. Pava lowers her scorecard.

[KIRI PLACES THE PEBBLE. IT WOBBLES, THEN SETTLES.]

KIRI: Now.

[MIRI RINGS THE BELL. THE STUDIO APPLAUDS.]

ACT TWO-C

Venn asks the judges to decide immediately. Pava says they should judge the finished act, not the interruptions. Olo says the quiet made the balance visible. Venn asks whether silence counts as entertainment.

KIRI: You all watched without moving.

[THE AUDIENCE NODS. THE PEBBLE SLIPS A FRACTION. EVERYONE FREEZES AGAIN.]

MIRI: The act is still happening.

The stone stack holds. Kiri remains still while the judges prepare their scorecards. Venn asks for a final pose; Kiri leaves the stones in place and steps away slowly, but has not bowed yet.

ACT TWO-D

A producer brings out a golden buzzer and sets it beside Kiri's stones. Venn explains that a judge may press it to send a contestant straight to the finale. Olo reaches toward it, but Pava stops him.

PAVA: The performance is still in progress.

OLO: I thought the quiet meant it was over.

KIRI: I haven't bowed.

[THE TOP PEBBLE WOBBLES. EVERYONE HOLDS THEIR BREATH.]

MIRI: No one touch the buzzer.

Venn moves the buzzer away from the edge. His sleeve brushes the table; the bell rings. The audience gasps as if he has pressed it.

VENN: I did not press it.

OLO: The bell has advanced you to the next round.

PAVA: There is no next round for the bell.

Miri checks the mechanism and finds a loose spring. She places a paper guard around the button. Kiri waits until the stones settle, then steps back and bows. Only after the bow does the audience applaud.
[MIRI TAPES A "QUIET" CARD OVER THE BUZZER. OLO LEANS FORWARD TO READ IT AND HIS CHAIR SQUEAKS.]

OLO: The chair has made a noise.

PAVA: It is not a contestant.

KIRI: It is welcome to stay seated.

Olo shifts carefully. The chair squeaks again, and Miri carries it off the stage. The room settles. Kiri walks past the table without touching the stones; the stack remains upright as the crew resets the lights.

ACT THREE

The stage crew clears the set without touching the stack. Venn narrates the result in a hushed voice. Olo speaks in a whisper. Pava gives Kiri the award.

TAG

After everyone exits, one pebble rolls off the board and lands in the trophy cup.

MIRI: It has chosen a winner.

[MUSIC: TALENT SHOW THEME.]
