# The Choir Class That Would Not Sing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's music education program convention, where the teacher tries to force one rhythm on a group with different natural calls*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A music teacher leads a forest choir, but the students each have a different call and the lesson plan assumes everyone has the same voice.

Cast: Teacher Miri, a gibbon; students Tavi, a macaque, Pava, a squirrel monkey, and Odo, a baboon; and accompanist Seli, a capuchin.

Episode: "Find Your Note"

COLD OPEN

INT. MUSIC ROOM, MORNING

Miri raises a hand. The students sing one note. Odo's voice is deep, Pava's is high, and Tavi claps instead.

MIRI: That was not one note.

SELI: It was several notes together.

A paper music chart slips off the wall.

ACT ONE

Miri asks each student to repeat the exercise. Odo tries to sing higher, but his voice cracks. Pava tries to sing lower, then laughs. Tavi keeps clapping on the beat. Miri asks him to stop. He stops; the room loses the rhythm.

Seli taps a soft pattern on a log. Tavi joins in. Pava adds a call. Odo answers. Miri listens and writes each sound on a separate line. The class now has a short pattern that fits all their voices.

ACT TWO

EXT. MUSIC DECK, AFTERNOON

The students practice outside. Wind carries the sound into the trees. A bird answers. Miri says the bird is not on the roster. Seli asks if they should include it anyway. The bird calls again at a different pitch. Odo tries to copy it; the bird flies to a farther branch.

Tavi notices the rhythm works best when he stands beside the log. Pava moves near the branch so her call can travel. Odo stands back to avoid overpowering them. Miri rearranges the music chart to match the group rather than asking the group to match the chart.

A rehearsal begins. A gust blows the chart away. Tavi catches it, but the paper is blank on the back. Miri turns it around and uses the blank side to write the rhythm in simple marks: clap, call, answer.

ACT TWO-B

INT. MUSIC ROOM, LATE AFTERNOON

Miri listens to the students separately. Odo can sustain a low note, but he loses breath when asked to hold it too long. Pava's high call carries across the room, but the echo makes it difficult to hear Tavi's claps. Seli asks the group to move outside, where the sound can spread.

They test the rhythm on a log. Tavi claps a steady beat. Pava answers with two short calls. Odo gives one low response. Miri tries to lead with a bell, but the bell's ring continues after the other sounds have stopped. Seli asks her to try a hand signal instead.

MIRI: The lesson plan says use a bell.

SELI: The song says stop together.

Miri lowers her hand. The group begins again. This time Odo hears the final clap and ends his note at the same moment. A nearby troop answers from the tree line. Pava worries they have copied the class. Miri says the forest is an audience, not a second choir.

The group decides to invite the troop to the performance, but not to require them to sing. Tavi draws a simple cue card. A circle means start; a leaf means finish. He places it at eye level for the students and hangs another at the edge of the clearing for listeners.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. MUSIC DECK, EVENING

The students rehearse in the clearing. Miri uses the hand signal to begin. Tavi claps, Pava calls, and Odo answers. A gust rattles the leaves and adds a rustling rhythm. Seli asks if they should stop. Miri listens and adjusts the tempo to match the wind.

The nearby troop gathers. One young monkey claps too early. The rhythm breaks. Tavi turns to the audience and demonstrates the start signal again. The young monkey tries once more, this time waiting for the circle cue. Odo's low note carries across the deck, while Pava's high call answers from beside the tree.

Miri checks the students' spacing. Odo stands too close to the log, making his note louder. He steps back. Pava moves nearer the tree so her call can travel without shouting. Seli asks Miri to join the pattern. Miri has been directing, but the students give her the finishing leaf cue. She adds one soft note after Odo's response.

The choir completes the sequence without a bell. The audience does not applaud until the final hand signal. A bird calls from the canopy after the group finishes. The students laugh and bow together.

ACT THREE



The choir performs for families. Each student makes a different sound, but the pattern holds. The bird answers from the trees. Miri does not stop it this time. Seli ends with a soft tap on the log. The audience applauds.

TAG

Miri asks the students to sing the final note together. They do. It is still several notes. The paper chart slips down again, but this time Tavi catches it and hangs it lower.

TAVI: Now everyone can reach it.
