# The Last Library Card

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: family adventure sitcom convention, where a household's expedition to replace one library card becomes a test of every member's habits*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: When the family library card expires, the troop must travel across the grove before closing, with each member sure they know the best shortcut.

Cast: Kiri, a macaque parent; Pako, a gibbon parent; Rell, a capuchin teenager; and Nima, a young squirrel monkey who keeps the books.

Episode: "Return by Sunset"

COLD OPEN

INT. TREEHOUSE, AFTERNOON

Nima holds up a library card. It has expired. Rell searches his pockets for a snack. Kiri checks the clock.

KIRI: The library closes at sunset.

PAKO: We can make it.

Rell pulls out three borrowed books, a leaf and a second expired card.

NIMA: The whole family has been checking out books on one card.

KIRI: That is efficient.

The card tears down the middle.

ACT ONE

EXT. GROVE PATH, AFTERNOON

The family sets out. Kiri chooses the straight path. Pako prefers the ridge trail. Rell says he knows a shortcut through the fig trees. Nima checks the map and points to the marked route.

NIMA: The map says the footbridge is closed.

RELL: That was last week.

They reach the bridge. A sign confirms it is closed. Rell turns the sign around, hoping the reverse side is open. Kiri redirects them to the ridge.

At the top, Pako stops to identify a distant tree. He says it is the library. Nima tells him it is the water tower. Pako insists the library roof used to be blue. The roof is still blue on the map.

ACT TWO

EXT. STREAM CROSSING, LATER

The ridge trail leads to a shallow stream. Rell wants to hop across. Kiri tests the stones first. Pako finds a dry line of rocks. Nima holds the books above the water. They cross one at a time. Rell reaches the bank, then realizes the library card is in his pocket and has become damp.

RELL: It is still readable.

NIMA: The number is running.

Kiri uses a leaf to blot it. Pako says the library can issue a replacement. Rell asks if the card can be renewed without the books. Nima says they still must return the books.

They reach the library just before closing. The librarian checks the books. One is overdue, one is due today and one belongs to the nature desk. Rell says he borrowed it for the walk. Nima says the book has not been read.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. GROVE PATH, LATE AFTERNOON

The family tries the ridge route, but Pako stops at every turn to name the trees. Rell looks up each name in the borrowed nature book. Nima keeps the card dry inside its cover. Kiri checks the clock and asks the family to move along. Pako says recognizing a tree is useful navigation. Rell says the book describes the tree but not the route.

They reach a split trail. Nima recognizes the stream path from the map. Rell spots a marker painted on the trunk, but it has faded. Kiri finds a fresh footprint in soft earth and follows it to the maintained path. Pako points out a fig branch over the trail. A ripe fig drops into Rell's open book and leaves a stain.

RELL: The library will know where we have been.

NIMA: The library will know what you ate.

The family arrives at the stream. The water is low, but the old stepping stones are slick. Kiri uses a sturdy branch as a handrail. Pako crosses first, then holds the branch for Rell. Nima passes the books across in a cloth wrap. Rell asks if the route counts as a reading break. Kiri says it counts as walking.

ACT THREE


INT. LIBRARY, SUNSET

The librarian accepts the books and replaces the card. She gives the family one card each. Kiri asks if that means four cards can be used at once. The librarian says yes, but each member must remember their own.

Pako immediately misplaces his. Rell finds it inside the nature book. Nima writes each name on the cards. Kiri asks if she can keep the old torn one as a bookmark. The librarian says it is no longer valid.

The family leaves with new cards. Outside, Pako admits the water tower did look like the library from the ridge. Rell says the detour was worth it because they got back before sunset.

TAG

EXT. TREEHOUSE, NIGHT

Nima checks out a book using her new card and places it on the shelf. Kiri returns with the old torn card, now cut into four small bookmarks.

NIMA: They are all expired.

KIRI: They are all useful.
