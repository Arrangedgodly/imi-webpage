# The Last Planet Is a Set Piece

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised space operas, emergency bridge scenes, repeating alarms, and cliffhangers timed to the final commercial*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A starship crew prepares to abandon orbit when the planet below appears to flash a distress signal, but the signal repeats every time the ship turns on its landing beacons.

Cast: Captain Rala, a macaque; engineer Noro, an orangutan; navigator Pex, a gibbon; communications officer Kemi, a capuchin; mission producer Vali, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. STARSHIP BRIDGE - NIGHT

A red alarm flashes across the navigation screen. Kemi points to the planet below.

KEMI: It is answering our signal.

RALA: What did it say?

KEMI: It blinked.

Pex raises a star map. The planet flashes again, in perfect time with the ship's beacon.

ACT ONE

Rala asks for a full report. Pex identifies a clear landing zone on the planet's north side. Noro says the signal repeats each time the landing beacon pulses. Rala orders a red-alert briefing anyway. A warning klaxon begins; Kemi stops it after one note.

RALA: Why did you stop the alarm?

KEMI: It was making the screen flash.

Noro points to a magnified surface scan. The bright patches on the planet line up with the ship's beacon, not with any moving object. Rala asks Pex to compare the flashes with the ship clock. Pex puts up a countdown graphic that covers the map.

RALA: Move the countdown.

PEX: It is the only dramatic part of the display.

Noro moves the graphic to the corner. The patches brighten on every pulse. Kemi asks whether the planet has a reflective mineral surface. Pex says the survey data lists pale crystal deposits in the valley.

ACT TWO

Rala asks for a landing plan. Noro wants to turn off the beacon and run a passive scan. Vali says the audience needs to know what the crew is risking before the ad break. Rala summarizes the danger in a low voice. The light flashes again, and the bridge turns red.

VALI: Can we end the act on a question?

KEMI: Is the planet answering us?

Noro shuts off the landing beacon. The flashes stop. Pex runs another scan. A pale band remains, reflecting the system's star. Rala says they have not established whether the planet can support a landing. Noro points out that they have a survey probe.

RALA: Launch it.

Pex sets the probe to descend. Its camera shows a clear valley, low winds, and a wide flat shelf. A mineral ridge reflects the probe's light directly back to the lens. Vali asks whether the reflection means something. Kemi rotates the probe camera. The bright spot slides across the screen.

KEMI: It is reflecting the probe, too.

ACT TWO-B

The crew debates whether to continue. Pex says the valley is safe, but the mineral deposits can make optical readings look like movement. Noro proposes a landing path using radar only. Rala approves it. Vali asks for one more reaction shot. The crew turns to the camera at once.

RALA: We are making a careful landing.

Noro begins the descent. The ship tilts slightly. A mug slides across the console. Kemi catches it with her tail before it hits the floor.

KEMI: The mug is secure.

RALA: Report the ship.

Noro reads the instruments: stable. The landing gear touches down in the valley. The crew waits for the planet to flash. It does not. Pex turns off the navigation display and restores the normal map.

ACT THREE

Rala orders the crew to send a ground team only after checking the air and surface conditions. Noro confirms the probe's readings. Kemi receives no transmission from the planet. Pex opens the hatch camera; the valley is quiet and illuminated by the star.

VALI: We have landed. Can we still end on a question?

RALA: What is beyond the ridge?

Noro shows a survey map. It is another valley, clearly marked. Rala says they will examine it tomorrow. The crew logs the reflective deposits and keeps the landing beacons off.

KEMI: No response signal.

PEX: No alarm.

Noro turns the red warning light to green. Vali closes her notes. The mission continues, without a mystery created by the display.

Kemi marks the beacon switch with a yellow tag so no one activates it by accident. Pex points the ship's instrument lamp at the valley map. The crystals reflect one small spot back onto the bridge ceiling.

RALA: Is that the planet again?

KEMI: No. That is our lamp.

Noro turns the map away from the reflection. The bright spot disappears. Vali crosses out the word "CONTACT" on her notes and writes "REFLECTION."

TAG

The mug remains clipped to the console. Noro switches on the work light. The crystals outside flare brightly through the porthole.

KEMI: The planet blinked.

RALA: The work light did.

FINAL IMAGE: The ship rests in the valley as the display stays calm and the stars move slowly across the porthole.
