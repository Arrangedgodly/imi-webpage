# The Lanterns of Hollow Village

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sentimental holiday television specials that delay a live community pageant for scripted miracles and inspirational speeches*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Storyteller Odo wants the village lantern pageant to deliver a scripted miracle. Organizer Mina needs the walk to begin before the borrowed lights run out. When the false miracle fails, the cast has to finish one live procession with the lanterns they actually have.

Cast: Organizer Mina, a macaque; storyteller Odo, a gibbon; electrician Fenn, a capuchin; and young helpers Lili and Pavo.

## Episode: "One Light at a Time"

### Cold Open

[A VILLAGE PATH IS LINED WITH PAPER LANTERNS. ODO HOLDS A SCRIPT. MINA CHECKS A BATTERY PACK.]

ODO: At the final turn, the last lantern will shine by itself. Then I say, "Even in darkness, hope finds the way."

MINA: It is connected to the battery pack.

FENN: And the pack has twenty minutes left.

ODO: The light should feel like a miracle.

FENN: It will feel like a light if the wire stays connected.

[MINA LOOKS DOWN THE PATH. A CROWD IS WAITING.]

MINA: We start now.

ODO: We need the inspirational speech first.

### Act One

[ODO STEPS IN FRONT OF THE CROWD.]

ODO: Friends, on this special evening, we gather to witness the triumph of...

[THE BATTERY PACK BEEPS.]

MINA: The lights need to be switched off between rehearsals.

ODO: That will spoil the reveal.

FENN: Keeping them on will spoil the battery.

LILI: The lantern at the gate is already dim.

PAVO: I can hold the cord.

[THEY SECURE THE CORD WITH A CLIP. THE LIGHT BRIGHTENS.]

ODO: A sign from the village.

MINA: A clip from the toolbox.

ODO: We can still use the moment in the pageant.

MINA: We are not adding a moment. We are starting the walk.

[ODO TURNS TO THE CROWD, SCRIPT IN HAND.]

ODO: The pageant begins when the first light...

[THE FIRST LANTERN TIPS IN THE WIND. FENN CATCHES IT.]

FENN: The first light needs a stronger hook.

MINA: Lili, help him. Pavo, keep the crowd on the path.

[THE HELPERS REHANG THE LANTERN. ODO WATCHES THE CROWD START TO WANDER.]

ODO: They have moved before the speech.

MINA: They are following the route.

### Act Two

[THE PROCESSION BEGINS. MINA LEADS THE CROWD. FENN CHECKS THE CORDS. ODO WALKS BEHIND THEM, TRYING TO READ HIS SCRIPT.]

ODO: At the midpoint, we pause for the miracle.

MINA: The midpoint is where the path forks.

FENN: The left branch is dark.

ODO: That is where the miracle happens.

FENN: That is where the battery cable is loose.

[THE GROUP STOPS. LILI AND PAVO HOLD THE LANTERN WHILE FENN SECURES THE CONNECTOR.]

ODO: Can we dim the other lanterns so this one seems brighter?

MINA: Then people cannot see the path.

ODO: We need a contrast.

MINA: We need the path.

[THE CONNECTOR CLICKS. THE LANTERN GLOWS. THE CROWD APPLAUDS.]

ODO: You see? The miracle.

LILI: Fenn fixed it.

FENN: The wire was loose.

ODO: The audience does not need the mechanics.

PAVO: I held the lantern so he could fix it.

MINA: The audience saw the mechanics. They helped.

[THEY CONTINUE. ODO STEPS AHEAD TO HIS MARK, BUT THE CROWD KEEPS WALKING.]

ODO: We cannot pass the finale before my speech.

MINA: We are not passing it. We are arriving.

[THE LAST TURN COMES INTO VIEW. THE FINAL LANTERN IS DARK.]

ODO: This is the moment.

FENN: The battery is empty.

ODO: We have one more light.

MINA: The moon is already lighting the path.

### Act Three

[ODO LOOKS AT HIS SCRIPT, THEN AT THE CROWD. MINA HOLDS UP A HAND LANTERN. LILI AND PAVO BRING THE DIM LANTERN CLOSER.]

LILI: We saw how it stayed lit.

ODO: I had planned a miracle.

MINA: We have a path, a crowd, and enough light to finish.

ODO: My speech was longer.

FENN: The battery is not.

[ODO FOLDS THE SCRIPT AND SETS IT INSIDE THE EMPTY BATTERY BOX.]

ODO: Then I will say the short version. We carried the light together.

LILI: We carried the lantern.

PAVO: And Fenn fixed the wire.

MINA: Keep walking. The pageant is not over until everyone reaches the village.

[THE CROWD MOVES THROUGH THE FINAL TURN. THEY LIFT THEIR OWN LANTERNS, AND THE PATH GLOWS WITHOUT THE ELECTRIC STRING.]

ODO: This is the ending.

FENN: Keep the battery box with us. Your script is blocking its handle.

[THE LAST PERSON PASSES THE TURN. FENN TUGS THE BOX HANDLE FREE OF ODO'S SCRIPT. HIS ELBOW JOSTLES THE CONNECTOR; THE FINAL LANTERN FLICKERS ON FOR ONE SECOND, THEN GOES DARK.]

ODO: A miracle.

MINA: That was Fenn's elbow. Put it in the credits.

[THE CROWD LAUGHS. THEY CARRY THE DARK LANTERN BACK TO THE TOOL SHED, ALL IN ONE PROCESSION.]
