# The Town Name Is Under Review

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: political town halls and rebranding campaigns that ask residents to vote on a name before explaining the change*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A committee proposes renaming a village for a sponsor, then struggles to fit the new name on its own signs.

Cast: Chair Omi, a gibbon; councilor Dax, a baboon; resident Piri, a macaque; sign painter Nalo, a capuchin; moderator Seli, a lemur.

Episode: "Welcome to New Welcome"

COLD OPEN

INT. TOWN HALL - EVENING

A banner reads WELCOME TO WELCOME. Dax stands beside a sponsor banner: WELCOME TO ACORN PLUS.

DAX: Our town will now be called Acorn Plus Welcome.

PIRI: Why?

OMI: The sponsor will fund the new welcome sign.

NALO: The current sign is readable.

[THE TWO BANNERS FALL. THE SPONSOR ONE COVERS THE TOWN SEAL.]

ACT ONE

Seli opens the public meeting. Residents may comment for one minute each. Dax says the new name will attract visitors. Piri asks whether the sponsor pays for road maps, mail labels, and the town bell. Omi says the contract covers the sign and one opening ceremony.

PIRI: Then the town gets a name change and a party.

DAX: Also a branded bench.

NALO: The bench is not a sign.

DAX: It has the town name on it.

Nalo unfolds the proposed lettering. It is too long to fit the sign board. Dax suggests smaller letters. The first line says ACORN PLUS; the second line says WELCOME TO. The town's name would be on a third line, but there is no room.

ACT TWO

Omi calls for a vote. Each resident receives a green card for yes and a brown card for no. The sponsor has printed its logo on both sides of the green card. Piri asks if she can vote no without holding the sponsor logo. Seli gives her a blank card.

DAX: A blank card is not an official vote.

SELI: It is an official card. I am moderating.

Dax asks Nalo to test the new lettering on the existing sign. Nalo paints it on a removable board. The phrase is so long that it covers the arrow to the meeting hall.

PIRI: Visitors will not find the hall.

DAX: They will find the brand.

PIRI: That's the problem.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. OMI RINGS THE BELL FOR ORDER. THE BELL IS WRAPPED IN A SPONSOR RIBBON AND MAKES A MUFFLED THUD.]

ACT TWO-B

The committee holds a televised debate. Dax stands at a podium with the sponsor's long name. Piri stands beside the old town map. Seli asks each speaker to explain the practical change.

DAX: We will be more memorable.

PIRI: People already know where they live.

DAX: We will have a new bench.

PIRI: Is the bench more important than the name?

DAX: The bench has a back.

Nalo rolls in the proposed sign. It catches on the door because it is taller than the doorway. The committee tilts it sideways and the sponsor logo faces the camera while the town name faces the wall.

Seli says the public may have one final comment. A child points at the old sign and says she can read it from the door. Dax says the new one needs to be bigger. Nalo tells him the wall is not bigger.

ACT TWO-C

Omi reads the contract. The sponsor funds one sign only if the sponsor name appears in the town name. Piri asks whether the money could instead repair the existing sign. Dax says that would not be a rebrand. Nalo says the old sign needs a new post, not a new name.

[THE OLD SIGN WOBBLES OUTSIDE. EVERYONE LOOKS THROUGH THE WINDOW.]

OMI: We can vote on the repair separately.

DAX: The sponsor won't fund that.

PIRI: Then the sponsor is not funding the town. It is buying space on the sign.

Seli checks the cards. The blank cards and brown cards both count as no; the motion fails. Dax asks whether he may keep the bench. Nalo says it is a display sample and must go back to the supplier.

[NALO TURNS THE SPONSOR BENCH AROUND. THE LOGO IS ON THE BACK, WHERE NO ONE CAN READ IT.]

DAX: We can turn it sideways for the photograph.

PIRI: Then nobody can sit on it.

OMI: The motion has failed. The bench is leaving.

DAX: Could we call it a mobile sign?

NALO: It is a bench in a truck now.

[SHE ROLLS IT OUT. THE WELCOME BANNER FALLS OVER THE PODIUM.]
ACT THREE

The town votes to repair the old sign and keep its name. Nalo paints the post. Dax carries the sponsor bench toward the truck. The camera follows him through the door.

TAG

The sign reads WELCOME TO WELCOME. The sponsor logo is now on the bench, facing the wall.

DAX: It still has a back.

PIRI: The town does too.
