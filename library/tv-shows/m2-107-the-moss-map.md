# The Moss Map

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: animated adventure series with collectible clues, side quests and merchandise-ready catchphrases*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Two young explorers follow a map to a hidden spring, but every clue introduces another collectible object. Their troop leader wants the route completed before sunset, not converted into a franchise.

Cast: Rumi, a young macaque; Tavi, a gibbon; guide Nella, a langur; and elder Bo, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Moss Map"

### Cold Open

[A MOSSY MAP IS UNROLLED ON A ROCK. A RED X MARKS A SMALL SPRING.]

RUMI: The map says the spring is three turns away.

TAVI: It says turn left at the bent root.

[THE ROOT BENDS IN TWO DIRECTIONS.]

NELLA: The map is old.

RUMI: The map is collectible.

### Act One

[BO CHECKS A WATER GOURD.]

BO: We have enough water to reach the spring, if we keep moving.

RUMI: We should gather the three leaf keys.

BO: There are no keys.

[THE MAP HAS DRAWINGS OF THREE DIFFERENT LEAVES.]

TAVI: These look like markers.

RUMI: The legend calls them keys.

NELLA: The legend was added later.

[RUMI FINDS A RED LEAF AND PUTS IT IN A POUCH.]

RUMI: First key collected.

BO: It is a leaf. Leave it on the tree.

[RUMI HANGS IT BACK.]

### Act Two

[THE GROUP REACHES A ROCKY STREAM. THE MAP'S SECOND CLUE SHOWS A BLUE LEAF.]

TAVI: I see a blue leaf across the stream.

NELLA: That is a reflection.

RUMI: A reflection can still be collected.

[HE REACHES TOWARD THE WATER. BO PULLS HIM BACK.]

BO: We cross on the stones.

[THEY CROSS. RUMI'S POUCH CATCHES ON A BRANCH. A PAPER LABEL FALLS OUT: "MOSS MAP COLLECTOR SET."]

NELLA: Who put that in your pack?

RUMI: It came with the map.

TAVI: There is a shop at the trailhead?

RUMI: The map has a shop?

BO: The map has a spring.

[THEY FIND THE BLUE LEAF ON THE FAR BANK. IT HAS NO KEYHOLE, JUST A SMALL HOLE FROM AN INSECT.]

RUMI: Is this the second key?

NELLA: It is a leaf with a hole.

TAVI: A collectible hole.

[BO CONTINUES WALKING. THE OTHERS FOLLOW.]

[THE GROUP FINDS A CARVED ARROW ON A TREE. IT POINTS TOWARD A DIFFERENT SPRING.]

RUMI: The map has a bonus clue.

BO: That is an old trail mark. It points to the dry ridge.

NELLA: The carved arrow is useful, but the paper map is not the trail.

TAVI: We can compare the two.

[THEY CHECK THE SUN AND LOOK FOR THE WATER FLOW. NELLA MARKS A SAFE PATH ON THE MAP.]

RUMI: Should we call this an unlock?

BO: Call it a route we can use.

[THEY WALK THE RIDGE. RUMI SLIPS ON A LOOSE STONE, CATCHES A ROOT, AND PULLS THE MAP FROM HIS PACK.]

TAVI: Hold the root. I will take the map.

[RUMI CLIMBS BACK TO THE PATH. NELLA FOLDS THE MAP INTO A SMALLER SQUARE.]

NELLA: We can carry this without needing a collector's pouch.

RUMI: The pouch came with the set.

BO: The pouch is now carrying the map.

[THE SPRING BUBBLES INTO A SHALLOW POOL. TAVI PLACES THE GOURD ON A FLAT STONE.]

TAVI: It will not tip here.

RUMI: I thought the collectible cup would fit better.

NELLA: A gourd is not a cup.

[BO TESTS THE GOURD. IT LEAKS FROM A SMALL CRACK.]

BO: We will share the water and carry it upright.

RUMI: That is a better item description.

[HE USES A BIT OF MOSS TO SEAL THE CRACK. THE GOURD HOLDS.]

TAVI: The map did not show this spring.

NELLA: The map was drawn before the water changed course.

RUMI: We should add it for the next traveler.

BO: A useful map is one that can be changed.

[RUMI FOLDS THE MAP INTO HIS POCKET. BO CHECKS THAT THE WATER GOURD IS SECURE.]

### Act Three

[THEY ARRIVE AT THE SPRING. BO FILLS THE GOURD.]

RUMI: We reached it without all three keys.

BO: We reached it by reading the land.

TAVI: The map was useful where it matched the trail.

NELLA: We should update it for the next troop.

[RUMI TURNS THE MAP OVER AND DRAWS THE SAFE CROSSING.]

RUMI: I will call this the bonus route.

BO: Call it the route.

[THE GROUP DRINKS. RUMI FINDS A SMALL LEAF IN HIS POUCH.]

RUMI: A green key.

BO: Put it back where it belongs.

[RUMI RETURNS IT TO A BRANCH. A LITTLE MONKEY TRIES TO TAKE IT.]

TAVI: The next adventurer has arrived.

[BO HANDS THE MONKEY THE UPDATED MAP. RUMI CLOSES HIS POUCH, WHICH IS FULL OF UNUSED PAPER LABELS.]
