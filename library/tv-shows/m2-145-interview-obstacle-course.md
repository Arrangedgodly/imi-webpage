# The Interview Has an Obstacle Course

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised job competitions that replace actual work samples with timed challenges, dramatic rankings, and surprise eliminations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Kito wants a high-energy hiring final for one library assistant. Hiring manager Sura needs candidates who can sort returns accurately and help visitors find books; producer Puck keeps adding speed rounds.

Cast: Kito, a macaque host; Sura, a gorilla hiring manager; Jani, a lemur candidate; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "The Job Is at the Desk"

### Cold Open

[LIBRARY SET. THREE CANDIDATES STAND BESIDE A CART OF BOOKS.]

KITO: One job, three candidates, and a race to sort the returns.

SURA: The job is helping people and keeping the books in order.

[PUCK STARTS A COUNTDOWN CLOCK. JANI READS THE RETURN SLIPS.]

JANI: These books go to different floors.

KITO: You can read the labels after the race.

SURA: Read them first.

[THE CLOCK BEEPS. JANI SORTS THREE BOOKS. THE OTHER CANDIDATES RACE THE CART INTO A SHELF.]

KITO: We have a collision in the stacks.

SURA: We have a cart in the wrong aisle.

[JANI MOVES THE CART CLEAR AND CHECKS FOR DAMAGED SPINES.]

PUCK: Can this count as a bonus round?

SURA: It counts as checking the books.

[JANI FINDS ONE BOOK WITH A TORN DUST JACKET AND SETS IT ASIDE FOR REPAIR.]

### Act One

[PUCK SETS UP A SPEED ROUND. BOOKS WITH COLORED STICKERS ARE SCATTERED ON THE TABLE.]

KITO: You have thirty seconds to organize the collection.

SURA: There is no collection category on the label.

JANI: These are sorted by floor and section.

[THE CANDIDATES START. JANI CHECKS EACH SPINE. ONE BOOK IS MISFILED.]

JANI: This one belongs upstairs.

KITO: That will cost time.

SURA: It will save a visitor a trip.

[PUCK ADDS A BUZZER WHEN THE TIMER REACHES TEN SECONDS. JANI STOPS TO ASK AN OLDER VISITOR WHICH FLOOR THEY NEED.]

KITO: The buzzer has interrupted the challenge.

SURA: The visitor has a question.

[JANI WALKS THE VISITOR TO THE EAST DESK. THE TIMER CONTINUES TO COUNT DOWN, BUT NO ONE LOOKS AT IT.]

PUCK: Should I pause the clock?

SURA: The job does not have a clock for each question.

VISITOR: I am looking for the large-print shelf.

JANI: It is next to the east desk. I can show you.

[KITO POINTS TO THE TIMER. SURA TURNS IT OFF.]

### Act Two

[THE FINAL CHALLENGE IS A ROLE-PLAY. PUCK GIVES SURA A CARD THAT SAYS "CONFUSED VISITOR."]

KITO: Jani, help the visitor find a book before the clock runs out.

SURA: The visitor has not said what they need.

JANI: What are you looking for?

SURA: A book about local birds, large print if possible.

[JANI CHECKS THE CATALOG SCREEN. THE WIFI DISCONNECTS.]

KITO: A technical twist.

SURA: It is a network issue.

[JANI ASKS THE VISITOR ABOUT THE TOPIC. SURA SAYS SHE WANTS A GUIDE TO BIRDS AT THE LAKE. JANI FINDS A PRINTED INDEX AND POINTS TO THE SHELF.]

JANI: This one has large type and a map of the shoreline.

SURA: How did you know the visitor needed large print?

JANI: They said so.

[PUCK RESTARTS THE WIFI. THE CATALOG LOADS. THE BOOK IS AVAILABLE.]

KITO: The clock is almost out.

SURA: The visitor has the right book.

[SURA ASKS THE CANDIDATES IF THEY HAVE QUESTIONS. JANI POINTS TO THE DESK GUIDE.]

JANI: Who handles the repair shelf?

SURA: The assistant flags damaged books. The conservator repairs them.

[JANI WRITES THAT DOWN. PUCK ERASES "ELIMINATED" FROM THE OTHER CANDIDATES' CARDS.]

### Act Three

[THE THREE CANDIDATES STAND AT THE DESK. SURA HOLDS THE SCORECARDS.]

KITO: The speed round did not identify our winner.

SURA: It identified that the cart needs better wheels.

[JANI CHECKS THE CART. ONE WHEEL IS JAMMED WITH A PAPER CLIP. SHE REMOVES IT. THE CART ROLLS STRAIGHT.]

KITO: We have a winner.

SURA: I am hiring Jani because she checked labels, asked a clear question, and found the book.

PUCK: She was not the fastest sorter.

SURA: Speed was not the requirement.

[JANI IS OFFERED THE JOB. THE OTHER CANDIDATES HELP RE-SORT THE CART AFTER THE RACE.]

KITO: Any advice for future applicants?

JANI: Read the job description.

[SURA HANDS HER A REAL DESK GUIDE. PUCK REMOVES THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK.]

JANI: When is my first shift?

SURA: Monday morning.

KITO: Could we make it a dramatic start?

SURA: It is a shift at a library.

[JANI OPENS THE GUIDE TO THE PAGE MARKED "FIRST DAY."]

### Tag

[KITO TRIES THE SPEED ROUND ALONE. HE SORTS ONE BOOK INTO THE WRONG BIN.]

SURA: Check the label.

[KITO MOVES IT TO THE RIGHT BIN.]

KITO: I have improved.

[THE CLOCK BEEPS. PUCK SWITCHES IT OFF.]
