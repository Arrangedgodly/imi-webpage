# The Campaign for the Shade Tree

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: political campaign satire, where candidates promise sweeping change for a tiny local decision*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Three candidates campaign to decide which side of the community tree gets a new bench.

Cast: Candidate Kito, a macaque; candidate Odo, a baboon; candidate Piri, a gibbon; moderator Nara, a capuchin; campaign manager Tavi, a lemur.

Episode: "A Seat for Every Branch"

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY TREE - DAY

A bare patch of ground lies beneath the tree. Nara holds a microphone.

NARA: The question is where to place one bench.

KITO: I will put it in the shade.

ODO: I will put it in the shade with a ribbon.

PIRI: I will ask the tree.

The tree drops a leaf onto the ballot box.

ACT ONE

The candidates present their platforms. Kito promises a low bench for small monkeys. Odo promises a ceremonial opening. Piri promises a listening tour around the trunk.

Tavi distributes campaign leaflets. The leaflets show three benches, though the committee budget covers one. Kito asks who approved the extra pictures. Tavi says they were generated by the printer.

At the debate, Nara asks how each candidate will handle the path crossing. Kito says the bench should not block the path. Odo says he will appoint a bench-placement advisor. Piri says the tree's roots are already marking the safe area.

Odo calls Piri's answer anti-bench. Piri says she is pro-sitting.

ACT TWO

Each candidate has one minute for a closing statement. Odo spends half his time introducing a dramatic music cue. Kito describes the actual measurements. Piri invites residents to sit on a temporary log and see where they prefer to rest.

Tavi asks if campaign volunteers can move the log. Nara says it is a test, not a campaign prop. The residents sit on the sunny side, then the shaded side. More prefer the shade, but the path remains clear.

Odo says the test needs an official observer. Kito measures the gap between the log and the path. Piri asks the gardener to confirm the roots. The gardener says the roots are shallow near the west side, so the east side is safer.

The candidates agree on the east side. Odo says he will claim the decision as a bipartisan victory. Kito says the location comes from the measurements. Piri says the tree has been consulted.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. COMMUNITY TREE - LATE AFTERNOON

The bench arrives. It is heavier than expected. The candidates each take one end. They carry it together and set it on the east side. The legs wobble on uneven ground.

Kito wedges a flat stone under one leg. Odo asks if that counts as infrastructure. Piri says it counts as making the bench steady. Nara tests it before inviting anyone to sit.

ACT TWO-C

The campaign teams place their signs around the tree. Kito's sign says "SAFE SHADE." Odo's says "A SEAT WITH CEREMONY." Piri's sign says "LISTEN TO THE ROOTS." Tavi notices that the signs block the path. Nara asks the candidates to move them farther from the walkway.

Odo wants a televised debate about the signs. Kito says they already had a debate about where to put the bench. Piri asks the residents to mark the place where they would sit. Several stones appear on the east side, away from the roots. One stone is placed in the path; Nara moves it.

The candidates measure the area together. Kito uses a branch to mark the bench length. Piri asks whether someone can reach the seat without stepping on the exposed roots. Odo tests the approach and finds a clear route. They agree the location is safest.

Tavi asks if there will be a primary. Nara says the committee has one bench and one suitable site. Odo argues for a ribbon. Kito says a ribbon can be tied after the bench is installed, if it does not block the seat. Piri agrees.

Nara asks each candidate to remove any sign that blocks the path. They do. Kito carries his to the fence; Piri rolls hers beside the tree; Odo props his against the campaign table. The residents can now reach the bench area safely. Piri asks Odo if he still wants a ceremony. He says he does, but it can happen after the bench is installed. Kito offers to help lift it. Piri checks the route once more.

ACT THREE

Residents sit in the shade. The bench does not block the path, and it rests beyond the exposed roots. Nara announces that the committee chose the site based on safety and use. The candidates thank the voters, though there was no vote.

Odo asks for a ribbon-cutting. Kito gives him a piece of string. Piri asks the first resident if the seat is comfortable.

TAG

A bird lands on the back of the bench.

ODO: The first endorsement.

NARA: It did not vote.
