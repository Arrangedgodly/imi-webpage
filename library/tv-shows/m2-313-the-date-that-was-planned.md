# The Date That Was Planned

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating reality television, producer-arranged meet-cutes, whispered confessional interviews, and contestants manipulated into increasingly awkward surprises*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A dating show producer engineers a series of accidental meetings for two primates who already share an honest interest, but each setup makes the contestants more suspicious of the cameras.

Cast: Contestants Mira, a macaque, and Odo, a gibbon; host Vex, a capuchin; producer Palla, a ring-tailed lemur; and camera assistant Nari, an orangutan.

## Episode: "The Accidental Walk"

### Cold Open

[COMMUNITY GARDEN PATH. MIRA AND ODO ARRIVE FROM OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS.]

MIRA: You again.

ODO: We both signed up to water the trees.

PALLA: That is the meet-cute.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PRODUCER TENT.]

Palla tells Vex that Mira and Odo have met twice at the gardening club. The show needs a spontaneous first encounter. She arranges for each to arrive at the same path from opposite sides, carrying watering cans.

VEX: They will be surprised.

NARI: They have already met.

Palla asks them not to mention it on camera. The contestants arrive, greet each other by name, and compare the dry soil around a sapling.

MIRA: The north side needs water.

ODO: I brought a can for that.

Vex interrupts and pretends to introduce them.

VEX: Have you two met?

MIRA: Three times.

Palla asks Nari to record a confessional. She tells Mira to describe Odo as mysterious. Mira says he is practical and wears green boots. Odo's confessional prompt asks if Mira is "the one." Odo says he has only known her for two weeks.

### Act Two

[PRODUCER TENT. PALLA PLANS A SECOND SURPRISE.]

Palla arranges a candlelit picnic beneath the sapling. The candles attract moths. The producers add a violinist. The music frightens the birds away from the tree, and the contestants move the food to a bench.

VEX: The plan is going beautifully.

NARI: The violinist is standing in the sprinkler zone.

The sprinkler turns on. The violinist moves; the camera follows. Mira and Odo are left in the rain without the host. They laugh and cover the sapling with a tarp.

Palla calls for an intimate conversation. Vex whispers questions from behind a bush. The contestants hear him.

ODO: We can hear the prompts.

MIRA: The bush is shaking.

Palla tries a third surprise: a lost note tucked under a watering can. The note reads "Tell them how you feel." It blows into the sprinkler, gets soaked, and sticks to Vex's sleeve.

Palla then arranges a second date with the wrong pair of picnic baskets. Mira opens hers and finds a bouquet, while Odo finds a note saying "meet your secret admirer." The producer expects them to react with jealousy. Instead, they exchange the baskets and ask which one belongs to the gardening club.

MIRA: The flowers are for the sapling.

ODO: The note is for somebody else.

Nari checks the labels. Palla swapped the baskets so the contestants would have a surprise. The labels are now stuck to the wet table. Vex tries to read the note aloud, but the ink has run. Mira identifies one word: "watering."

They discover the note was a reminder for the next garden shift. The supposed love letter is the club's schedule. Palla asks the contestants to pretend to be moved by it. Odo says he is moved because someone remembered to water the trees.

The producer asks them to repeat the exchange for a cleaner take. Mira declines; the sprinkler has stopped, and the sapling needs water now. Odo carries the can while Mira steadies the young trunk. Nari records the ordinary work from a respectful distance, then lowers the camera when they ask.

### Act Three

[THE GARDEN.]

Mira tells Odo she enjoys working with him and would like to get coffee after the program. Odo agrees. They set a time. Palla tries to stage a final rose ceremony, but both contestants ask for a watering can instead.

VEX: The show usually ends with a grand gesture.

MIRA: The tree needs water.

ODO: We can water it together.

Nari asks whether the crew should leave. The contestants say yes. The cameras remain, but Odo notices and politely turns them toward the empty path. The producers finally pack the equipment.

Palla admits the best scene was not planned. Vex asks if they can recreate it. Mira says no; the soil is already wet, and the moment has passed.

### Tag

[COFFEE STAND, NEXT DAY.]

Mira and Odo sit together without cameras. They talk about the garden. A hidden camera peeks from behind a flower pot. Nari reaches in and turns it off.

NARI: They asked us to leave.

Palla sighs and carries the equipment away.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
