# The Science Fair's Big Reveal Is a Bubble

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised science fairs, overbuilt demonstrations, and hosts who demand a bigger result than the experiment measures*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Vela, a gibbon; student Timo, a macaque; judge Orri, a baboon; safety lead Paku, an orangutan; camera operator Senn, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

A student project sits on a table: a shallow dish, a straw, and a soap solution. Timo holds a card reading HOW LONG CAN A BUBBLE LAST? The host leans into frame.

VELA: Tonight, one student will make the biggest bubble in school history.

TIMO: I am timing how long a bubble lasts.

ORRI: The question is duration, not size.

VELA: The graphics team made a giant bubble.

[The screen shows an animated bubble covering the gym. It pops over the school logo.] 

SAFETY LEAD PAKU: No one is blowing toward the lights.

[Senn turns the camera to the small dish. The host's dramatic music fades.] 

ACT ONE

INT. SCHOOL GYM - LATER

Timo explains the test: same solution, same straw, one bubble at a time. A timer records each result. The first bubble pops immediately. Vela leans toward the table as if the result were a cliffhanger.

VELA: A sudden ending.

TIMO: It touched the table edge.

ORRI: That is a useful observation. The table edge can interfere with the test.

[The student moves the dish toward the center. Paku marks a clear area on the table so sleeves and camera cables stay away. Vela asks for another take of the pop. Orri stops the request.] 

ORRI: We record the next trial once. It either lasts or it doesn't.

[The bubble rises from the dish. Senn pans slowly. Vela whispers a countdown. Paku lowers the microphone away from the dish. The bubble floats for eleven seconds, then pops.] 

TIMO: Eleven seconds. The first one was less than one.

VELA: An astonishing increase.

TIMO: I moved it away from the edge. I should repeat it more times.

ACT TWO

INT. SCHOOL GYM - CONTINUOUS

Timo makes three more bubbles. The results are eight, twelve, and ten seconds. The host asks for the best one to be shown as the final result. Orri asks Timo for the average. The student writes the numbers on the board and calculates ten seconds.

VELA: Can we call it the ten-second champion?

ORRI: It is the average of four trials. The twelve-second bubble lasted longer.

TIMO: The next test is to compare two solution recipes. I have only tested one today.

[The host points to a second bottle, labeled RECIPE B. Timo says it was mixed after the first trials and has not been measured yet. Vela wants to pour it into the dish for a head-to-head finale. Paku checks the label and confirms that the bottle is sealed.] 

PAKU: We can do another trial, but it will be a new test. We should not mix the results.

ORRI: Correct. If we compare recipes, each needs the same number of trials.

[Vela starts to introduce an unplanned championship round. Timo sets the second bottle aside and reads the project question from the card. The camera frames the written data instead of the host's face.] 

TIMO: Today's answer is that moving the dish away from the edge gave the bubbles more room, and the average lasted ten seconds.

VELA: The data has a smaller graphic budget than our giant bubble.

[The judge asks Timo to show the timer's start and stop. Timo resets it and demonstrates with an empty soap wand, timing the wand's trip from the dish to the paper towel. Vela tries to call the test a bonus round.] 

TIMO: It is only a demonstration of the timer. It is not a bubble trial.

ORRI: The labels on the chart will keep those results separate.

[The student writes DEMONSTRATION above the new number. The host removes the countdown graphic from the monitor.] 

VELA: We will not accidentally crown the stopwatch.

ACT THREE

INT. SCHOOL GYM - AFTERNOON

The judge awards Timo a ribbon for clear method. Vela holds up the ribbon as if it were a giant trophy. Timo asks to keep the stopwatch and dish for the next test. Paku places them on the table after checking the surface is dry.

ORRI: Your next comparison needs equal trials and one changed variable.

TIMO: Same straw, same table, different solution.

VELA: We can return next week for the rematch.

TIMO: If the school allows cameras in the gym.

[Senn packs the oversized animated bubble graphic. It deflates on the screen without making a sound.] 

VELA: Our bubble has lost its scale.

ORRI: It was never part of the experiment.

[The student pins the trial chart next to the project question. The four times remain visible; the host cannot cover them with the ribbon.] 

TAG

[Vela attempts to blow a bubble in a cup of water. Paku takes away the straw before the host can begin. Vela looks at the camera and nods.] 

FINAL IMAGE: Timo carries the data chart to the school display while the small dish sits centered on the table.
