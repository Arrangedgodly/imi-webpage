# The Weather Balloon Has a Photo Finish

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: science broadcasts that race instruments against a balloon without explaining the wind*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; meteorologist Nari, a lemur; student Pip, a capuchin; technician Sela, a macaque; anchor Vex, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

EXT. SCHOOL FIELD - MORNING

[A weather balloon tugs at its tether. Pip holds the release clip. Odo has a starter flag.] 

ODO: We are racing the balloon.

NARI: We are measuring wind speed.

[The balloon pulls the flag out of Odo's hand. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

VEX: Welcome to Forecast Faceoff. Can a student, a stopwatch, and a balloon predict the next gust?

NARI: The balloon does not predict wind. It moves with the wind.

ODO: We can still call it a race.

[Sela sets up a wind meter beside the tether. Pip reads the display while Nari checks the direction of a ribbon.] 

PIP: The meter says five.

NARI: Five what?

PIP: It does not have units on the screen.

SELA: It is showing battery level.

[Odo lowers the starter flag. The balloon lifts and strains the rope. Pip tightens the knot.] 

ODO: The race has begun.

NARI: We have not started the timer.

ACT TWO

[The team checks the instructions. The wind meter must be held away from buildings and trees. Vex asks them to stand beside the school wall for a clean background.] 

NARI: The wall blocks the wind.

VEX: It frames the balloon.

[They move to the field. Pip holds the meter at shoulder height; the display shows numbers now. A gust catches the balloon and lifts it toward the treeline.] 

PIP: The balloon is moving faster than the meter.

NARI: The meter measures speed, not a race time.

[Odo runs beside the balloon with the flag. He trips over the tether and spins around the stake. Sela catches him.] 

ODO: We have a human wind vane.

SELA: We have a trip hazard.

[Nari marks the gust direction on a paper chart. Pip calls out the meter reading. The balloon shifts, then pauses as the wind drops.] 

PIP: The number is lower.

NARI: The gust has passed.

VEX: Is the balloon losing?

NARI: There is no finish line.

[Odo plants a small flag as a finish marker. The tether curves around it. The balloon drifts across the marker, then returns when the breeze changes.] 

ODO: It has crossed twice.

SELA: It has crossed the flag's path.

[Sela places a second wind meter beside the first. The two numbers differ because one is pointed toward the open field and the other toward the school wall.]

PIP: Which one is right?

NARI: Both measure the wind where they are.

VEX: We need one official number.

NARI: We need to say where we measured.

[She moves both meters to the same mark. The readings match. Odo asks the camera to zoom in; the display is small, so Pip holds a white card behind it.]

ODO: The instrument has a backdrop.

SELA: The card is to make the numbers legible.

[The balloon's tether casts a shadow across the card. Pip shifts to the side, then checks that he is not standing under the balloon.]

PIP: The shadow moved.

NARI: The sun moved.

[They record the time beside the reading. The anchor repeats it for the broadcast and calls it "nine units." Nari corrects him.]

NARI: Nine meters per second at the open-field mark.

VEX: That is more words than "nine."

NARI: It is what nine means.

ACT THREE

[The group plots three readings on the chart: low, high, then lower. The balloon remains tethered in the center of the field. Vex asks for the winner.] 

VEX: We need a result for the broadcast.

NARI: The highest wind reading was nine. The balloon moved east, then back west.

PIP: I recorded the number and direction.

ODO: Who won?

NARI: The measurement worked.

[The starter flag flaps sharply. Pip checks the meter. It shows nine again. The balloon lifts, but the tether knot holds.] 

SELA: That is the second gust.

ODO: We have a rematch.

NARI: The same weather has changed again.

[They wait until the gust falls. Pip and Sela lower the balloon together, keeping the tether away from the camera equipment. Odo tries to wave the flag, but the wind catches it and turns it inside out.] 

VEX: The forecast is undecided.

NARI: The chart shows what happened. It does not promise what happens next.

[Pip writes "gust passed" beside the second nine. The balloon deflates slightly as Sela releases gas through a valve.] 

PIP: The race is over.

ODO: Can we have a finish photo?

[They line up beside the balloon. It slumps between them, touching the ground. Sela keeps one hand on the tether.] 

TAG

VEX: The winner is the weather.

NARI: The weather does not collect prizes.

[Odo hands the starter flag to Pip. The breeze catches the flag, and its loose ribbon snags on the balloon tether.] 

ODO: It has collected the flag.
