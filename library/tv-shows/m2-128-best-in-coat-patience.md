# Best in Coat, Worst in Patience

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: pet grooming competitions that prioritize elaborate looks, dramatic judging, and timed transformations over the animal's comfort*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Miri wants a dazzling finale for a grooming contest. Groomer Oda wants the visiting dogs comfortable, and judge Pavi keeps scoring bows and silhouettes instead of calm handling.

Cast: Miri, a macaque host; Oda, a gorilla groomer; Pavi, a lemur judge; and Niko, a capuchin stage assistant.

## Episode: "The Bow Is Optional"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. THREE GROOMERS STAND BESIDE DOGS. A TIMER SHOWS 20:00.]

MIRI: Twenty minutes to create a show-stopping look.

[ODA CHECKS THE DOG'S PAW AND LOWERS THE CLIPPERS.]

ODA: This dog needs a break.

PAVI: The timer is running.

ODA: The dog is not.

[NIKO HOLDS UP A GLITTERING BOW.]

NIKO: Can I put this on the dog?

[THE DOG SNEEZES. THE BOW FALLS INTO THE WATER BOWL.]

PAVI: An early costume change.

[A SECOND CONTESTANT BRINGS A DOG TO THE WASH STATION. NIKO TESTS THE WATER TEMPERATURE WITH HIS WRIST.]

NIKO: Warm, not hot.

MIRI: Our prep segment is under way.

PAVI: Can we score the temperature?

ODA: You can score whether the dog steps into the tub voluntarily.

[THE DOG SNIFFS THE WATER, THEN PUTS ONE PAW ON THE RAMP. ODA WAITS. THE DOG STEPS IN.]

PAVI: That took forty seconds.

ODA: It took forty seconds.

### Act One

[THE GROOMERS BEGIN. MIRI NARRATES EACH STATION.]

MIRI: At station one, we have trimming. At station two, color. At station three, accessories.

ODA: We are doing a brush-out and nail check.

PAVI: Where is the dramatic reveal?

ODA: After the dog is comfortable.

[THE DOG STANDS UP AND SHAKES. FUR FLIES ACROSS THE JUDGE'S SCORECARD.]

PAVI: That was not on the schedule.

ODA: It is normal.

[MIRI LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.]

MIRI: A spontaneous moment from our contestant.

ODA: A dog drying off.

[NIKO MOVES THE SCORECARD AWAY FROM THE BLOWER. PAVI WRITES "CONFIDENT SHAKE" IN THE STYLE COLUMN.]

ODA: Please score the grooming, not the dog's personality.

### Act Two

[THE TIMER BEEPS. ODA STOPS AND OFFERS THE DOG WATER. THE OTHER CONTESTANT CONTINUES.]

MIRI: Oda is taking a break with the clock still running.

ODA: We are not racing the dogs.

PAVI: The challenge is timed.

ODA: Then my station is finished on the dog's schedule.

[THE DOG DRINKS. NIKO CLEANS WATER FROM THE TABLE. PAVI PINS THE WET BOW TO THE DISPLAY BOARD.]

MIRI: Could you create a matching look with the accessories?

ODA: The dog will wear a collar, not a costume.

PAVI: The judging rubric has a costume category.

ODA: Then remove the category.

[PAVI FLIPS THROUGH THE RUBRIC. THE NEXT LINE READS "MOST MEMORABLE PAW POSE." THE DOG LIES DOWN.]

PAVI: It has refused to pose.

ODA: It is resting.

[THE DOG CLOSES ITS EYES. MIRI WHISPERS AS IF IT WERE A REVEAL.]

PAVI: Is it asleep?

ODA: It is resting after the noise.

MIRI: Can we keep the camera still?

[THE CREW STOPS MOVING. THE DOG SETTLES ON ITS MAT. PAVI LOWERS THE SCORECARD SO IT DOES NOT BLOCK THE SHOT.]

MIRI: A moment of stillness.

ODA: A nap.

[NIKO REMOVES THE TIMER FROM THE TABLE SO IT WILL NOT BEEP BESIDE THE DOG.]

MIRI: The other station has completed a star-shaped trim.

PAVI: Will the dog keep the star?

ODA: It will grow out. The dog can go home clean and comfortable.

[THE DOG SHAKES AGAIN. THE WATER SPLASHES THE SPONSOR LOGO. PAVI BLINKS AT THE WET SCORECARD.]

PAVI: I cannot read the score.

ODA: You can look at the dog.

### Act Three

[THE JUDGES ASSESS THE GROOMERS. PAVI HOLDS THE SCORECARD.]

PAVI: Oda gets full marks for comfort and coat condition, but no points for accessories.

MIRI: Can we give a bonus for the water break?

PAVI: The rubric does not include water.

ODA: It should.

[THE DOG WAKES AND WALKS TO ODA. THE GROOMING IS NEAT, WITH NO BOW.]

PAVI: The coat is even. The nails are checked. The dog is calm.

ODA: That was the goal.

[PAVI CROSSES OUT "PAW POSE" AND WRITES "ANIMAL COMFORT" AT THE TOP OF THE CARD. MIRI ANNOUNCES THE WINNER.]

MIRI: The award goes to the groomer who finished without rushing the dog.

[THE OTHER GROOMERS APPLAUD. NIKO POURS OUT THE WATER BOWL AND REFILLS IT.]

MIRI: We will update the rules before the next contest.

PAVI: The category should reward patience.

ODA: And clean tools.

[NIKO PLACES THE CLIPPERS IN A SANITIZING TRAY. THE TIMER IS TURNED OFF.]

### Tag

[MIRI HOLDS UP THE GLITTERING BOW.]

MIRI: Should we put this on the winner?

ODA: The winner is a person.

[PAVI PINS THE BOW TO THE TROPHY BASE INSTEAD.]
