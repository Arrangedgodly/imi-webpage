# The Live Painting Show Frames the Wrong Canvas

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live art broadcasts that demand a reveal before the artist has chosen a stopping point*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Toma, a gibbon; painter Seli, an orangutan; critic Riko, a macaque; producer Nala, a capuchin; camera operator Pavi, a lemur; non-speaking studio assistant.

COLD OPEN

INT. ART STUDIO - DAY

Seli paints a blue stripe across a landscape canvas. A second, blank canvas stands beside it. Pavi frames the blank one on the broadcast monitor.

TOMA: The finished work is unveiled.

SELI: You are unveiling the spare.

ACT ONE

INT. ART STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Nala tells Pavi the feed labels were switched. Pavi follows the cable tags: A runs to the landscape; B runs to the spare. He selects A, but the camera image is upside down. Riko studies it anyway.

RIKO: The hill appears to be falling into the sky.

SELI: The camera is upside down.

Toma asks whether the blue stripe represents the sea. Seli says it is underpainting. Nala glances at the segment clock and asks for a reveal in three minutes. Seli asks for her clean brush. Pavi brings the brush from the B easel. Its bristles carry red paint from the test palette beside the spare, and it makes a red streak across the blue.

Seli stops the stroke, lays the brush on a tray and takes a clean one. Nala asks if the streak can be called a bold revision. Seli says she will decide after she paints around it. Pavi rotates the camera upright and restores the A label. The monitor shows the whole landscape, including a narrow path below the hill.

ACT TWO

INT. ART STUDIO - LATER

Seli paints the path's edge. Toma asks for a close-up of her brush. Pavi zooms in so far that the broadcast shows only blue paint. Riko asks where the path went. Pavi pulls back; it returns to frame. Nala asks him to mark that shot as DETAIL, not REVEAL.

Seli puts the brush down. The red streak is covered, but the newly painted base layer beneath the hill is still wet. She says she will add one shadow after this layer dries, then sign the lower corner. Nala says the reveal cloth is ready. Seli says it can wait beside the spare. Pavi places the cloth on a stand, not over either canvas.

Toma turns to the blank canvas and starts an introduction. Riko steps into his camera frame and points at the A label. Toma pivots toward Seli's work and asks what title she has chosen. She has not chosen one. Nala hands him a blank title card. The producer asks whether a blank title card counts as minimalist programming. Riko says it counts as blank.

The clock runs down. Nala asks Seli to pause for a teaser shot. Seli agrees to let Pavi film the wet paint from the side, where the shine makes the drying surface visible. The camera's red tally light is on; the viewers see the streak, brush tray and blank title card in the same shot. Toma starts to say "finished," sees Seli still holding the clean brush, and changes his line to "still in progress."

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Pavi checks both feeds. A shows the landscape, upright; B shows the untouched spare. He tapes the labels directly beside the monitors. Nala asks for a blank-canvas cutaway while the base layer dries. Pavi switches to B, and the broadcast displays the spare with its label in view. Riko asks Nala if she wants viewers to think the blank one is the art. Nala tells Pavi to return to A.

ACT THREE

INT. ART STUDIO - AFTERNOON

The base layer is dry. Seli checks the lower edge with a clean cloth, then paints a single curved shadow beneath the hill. She signs the lower corner once. Pavi keeps the camera wide: the path leads toward the hill, the shadow sits beneath it, and the signature is legible.

Toma asks for a dramatic cloth pull. Seli points to the blank spare. Pavi frames both canvases side by side, their A and B cards visible. Nala looks at the countdown clock, then at the signed landscape. She cancels the cloth cue. Riko gives one short critique: the path makes the hill feel walkable. Seli says that was the idea.

Toma starts to announce a second unveiling, then sees the live monitor showing the same finished work they watched her paint. He turns the blank title card over and writes LANDSCAPE, then crosses out the word because Seli has not named it. She takes the card, writes "Untitled," and hands it back. Nala cues the wide shot once. The broadcast holds on the completed canvas while Seli steps beside it. No one covers it or reveals it again.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE SHOW

Pavi files the wide shot under FINAL FRAME. Nala hands the unused cloth to the studio assistant, who folds it and puts it beside the labeled spare canvas. Toma places the signed title card beneath the correct monitor.

FINAL IMAGE: The closing credits roll over Seli's signed landscape, with the blank spare visible at the edge of the frame.
