# The Pitch Needs a Smaller Ladder

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: startup pitch programs that demand explosive growth stories from modest, practical services*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Founder Kesi wants support for a neighborhood ladder library. Investor Mavo expects an app-shaped market opportunity, while maintenance lead Rulo wants the borrowed ladders returned in safe condition.

Cast: Kesi, a macaque founder; Mavo, a gorilla investor; Rulo, a lemur maintenance lead; and Puck, a capuchin host.

## Episode: "One Ladder Per Block"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. KESI STANDS BESIDE A FOLDING LADDER.]

PUCK: What is your company?

KESI: We lend ladders to neighbors who need to reach a shelf or clean a gutter.

MAVO: How many users do you have?

KESI: Twelve households.

MAVO: What is your growth curve?

KESI: Two more blocks asked to join.

[MAVO LOOKS AT THE LADDER.]

MAVO: What is the total addressable market?

KESI: The blocks where people need a ladder for one afternoon.

PUCK: That is a very specific map.

KESI: It is the map we serve.

[RULO UNFOLDS A PAPER MAP WITH THREE MARKED BLOCKS. MAVO LOOKS FOR A METRO AREA GRAPHIC.]

MAVO: Where is the rest of the city?

RULO: We have not inspected the rest of the city.

MAVO: Can it scale?

RULO: It folds.

[PUCK TURNS TO CAMERA.]

PUCK: A product that answers its own pitch.

### Act One

[KESI SHOWS A SIGN-OUT BOARD WITH NAMES, RETURN TIMES, AND SAFETY CHECKS.]

KESI: Each ladder has a weight rating and inspection date. Members reserve one for a day.

MAVO: Can we add a premium level?

RULO: The ladders are the same.

MAVO: Priority access?

KESI: That would make people wait longer.

[PUCK PLACES A GRAPHIC ON SCREEN: "LADDERS PER CUSTOMER."]

RULO: One per block is enough.

MAVO: What is the recurring revenue?

KESI: A small annual fee covers repairs.

MAVO: Could you sell a ladder to every member?

KESI: They borrow one because they do not need to own one.

[RULO OPENS THE LADDER AND CHECKS THE HINGES. HE FINDS A LOOSE RIVET.]

MAVO: How many units does that affect?

RULO: One ladder.

MAVO: Can we present it as a supply-chain risk?

KESI: We can replace the rivet before the next loan.

[PUCK REMOVES THE "CRISIS" GRAPHIC FROM THE SCREEN. RULO ADDS A REPAIR TAG.]

RULO: This one needs repair before it goes out.

MAVO: Is repair a separate division?

KESI: It is part of the library.

### Act Two

[THE INVESTORS ASK FOR A LIVE DEMONSTRATION. PUCK SETS A SHELF HIGH ON A WALL AND PUTS A CAMERA ON A MOTOR TRACK.]

PUCK: We need a fast visual that says scale.

KESI: We can show the borrowing board. It is how the service works.

MAVO: A board is not a product reveal.

[RULO LOOKS AT THE FLOOR. A CABLE CROSSES THE DEMO AREA.]

RULO: The track is in the walking lane. Move the demonstration to the clear platform.

[MAVO PULLS A GROWTH CHART FROM HIS JACKET. IT HAS A LINE THAT GOES STRAIGHT UP.]

MAVO: Add one ladder for every new user. How many by next quarter?

KESI: Some blocks only need one shared ladder.

MAVO: The chart requires a steep curve.

[PUCK ROLLS IN THREE DECORATIVE LADDERS, EACH TALLER THAN THE LAST. THEY ARE SET PIECES, NOT FOR USE. THE TALLEST HIDES THE BORROWING BOARD.]

RULO: That one blocks the exits and the sign-out list.

PUCK: It gives the frame height.

KESI: Our members need to read the return dates.

[MAVO TAKES A STEP STOOL AND POINTS IT AT THE HIGH SHELF. RULO MOVES IT BACK TO THE PLATFORM BEFORE ANYONE CLIMBS.] 

RULO: The surface is uneven. We are not doing that demo here.

MAVO: The episode needs a dramatic finish.

KESI: The book goes onto the shelf after the floor is clear.

[PUCK ROLLS THE CAMERA TRACK TO THE SIDE. THE TRACK CATCHES THE GROWTH CHART AND PULLS IT DOWN. THE LINE THAT WENT STRAIGHT UP UNFURLS ACROSS THE FLOOR, BLOCKING THE DEMO AREA.] 

PUCK: The chart has scaled into the aisle.

[RULO AND KESI ROLL IT UP. MAVO HOLDS THE END; THE PAPER KEEPS UNROLLING, WRAPPING AROUND HIS SHOES.] 

MAVO: What is the cost per user?

KESI: Less than buying a ladder each.

MAVO: That is not a dramatic number.

RULO: It is the number people use.

### Act Three

[MAVO'S TEAM SETS OUT A CONTRACT WITH A LARGE BLANK FOR THE VALUATION. PUCK PUTS THE FOLDING LADDER IN THE CENTER OF THE STAGE AND A SPOTLIGHT ON IT.]

MAVO: I can fund expansion if the company agrees to sell a ladder to each member next year.

KESI: Then it would no longer be a lending library.

MAVO: The chart needs recurring purchases.

RULO: The chart can borrow one.

[RULO WRITES A DUE DATE ON A LOAN CARD AND CLIPS IT TO THE LADDER. PUCK TURNS THE SPOTLIGHT TO THE CARD.]

PUCK: It has a return date.

MAVO: It cannot return itself.

KESI: A neighbor brings it back. Another neighbor borrows it.

[MAVO TURNS THE CONTRACT AROUND. THE BACK HAS A SECTION FOR EQUIPMENT GRANTS. HE WRITES "TWO SIZES, THREE BLOCKS" AND LEAVES THE SALES CLAUSE CROSSED OUT.] 

MAVO: I will fund another size and two more blocks. I want the inspection tags on the application.

KESI: Those tell members which ladders are ready.

MAVO: They also give me a number I can put on a chart.

[THE SIGN-OUT BOARD ADDS TWO BLOCKS. RULO SETS THE DISPLAY LADDER BACK ON ITS FEET AND ATTACHES A BORROWER CARD. THE BOARD HAS SPACE FOR ONLY ONE MORE NAME.] 

PUCK: We need a growth graphic.

[HE TURNS THE CHART UPSIDE DOWN. THE LINE NOW DESCENDS TO THE NUMBER OF LADDERS ACTUALLY NEEDED.] 

MAVO: That looks like a decline.

KESI: It is a smaller estimate.

RULO: And all the names fit.

### Tag

[PUCK HOLDS UP A SMALL STEP STOOL.]

PUCK: Could this be the next product?

KESI: It is already in the community hall.

MAVO: Can it scale?

[RULO FOLDS IT AND HOLDS IT IN FRONT OF THE SIGN-OUT BOARD, HIDING THE LAST UNUSED LINE.]

RULO: It stacks.
