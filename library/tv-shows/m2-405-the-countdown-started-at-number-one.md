# The Countdown Started at Number One

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: music-video countdown shows, chart suspense, rapid clips, and hosts who tease the top spot before the list is ready*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A music countdown host begins at the top of the chart, then learns the control room sorted the video files alphabetically instead of by rank.

Cast: Host Kavi, a macaque; chart editor Nemi, a lemur; guest performer Boro, an orangutan; video operator Pex, a gibbon; stage assistant Rali, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. MUSIC STUDIO - NIGHT

Kavi announces the number ten video. Pex plays a clip. Nemi holds up a card that says "NUMBER ONE."

KAVI: We have reached the top.

NEMI: We have started at the top.

The clip's title begins with A.

ACT ONE

Nemi checks the chart sheet. The positions are correct, but the video playlist sorts by title. Kavi says they can count down in reverse. Pex says the audience has already seen number one. Boro is waiting to perform the number ten song as a guest.

BORO: Am I still number ten?

KAVI: You are still our guest.

Nemi explains that a chart slot is based on viewer requests and plays, not the file name. Kavi introduces number nine. Pex finds a video beginning with B and plays it. The song is ranked number eight.

NEMI: We have advanced one place by accident.

KAVI: We are moving quickly.

ACT TWO

The hosts pause the countdown. Nemi moves the video files into numbered folders. Pex tests the next clip; a behind-the-scenes slate appears instead of the music video. Rali holds up a board reading "RECUT." Pex switches feeds. The correct clip plays.

KAVI: Number nine, finally.

BORO: How many clips have we shown?

NEMI: Three, two, eight, and nine.

BORO: That is a very confident sequence.

The control room puts up a scrolling chart. It begins at number ten and moves upward. The audience votes in a poll about the top video. The results display before the poll closes; the wrong option has been selected because Pex left last week's poll active.

KAVI: The viewers have voted for last week's chart.

NEMI: We can close it and start a new poll.

ACT TWO-B

Nemi manually checks each rank against the playlist. Boro asks to perform his number ten song while they verify the top five. Kavi agrees. Boro steps to the stage, but the playlist starts at number one again. He waits, then taps the mic. Pex cuts the video and brings up the live camera.

BORO: I am the guest performer. I can wait for my own cue.

KAVI: We have found number ten.

NEMI: We have found the guest.

Nemi gives Pex a printed run order with the correct rank and filename beside each clip. Pex reads both aloud before loading. The chart proceeds from number five to number one. Kavi asks each time whether the clip and rank match. Nemi confirms. The countdown gets quieter and more accurate.

The monitor switches to the audience poll. Last week's result remains on screen until Nemi closes it. Pex asks whether the chart positions include the guest performance. Nemi says the ranking is based on the week's viewer requests and completed plays; a live studio set is not ranked. Boro asks if he should wait until the official list is complete.

BORO: I can stand by the microphone.

KAVI: We have a camera for that.

The live camera frames him waiting. A small timer appears in the corner and counts down from thirty seconds. Boro does not sing; he checks that the microphone cable is behind his foot and tests the stand height. Kavi asks if it is ready.

BORO: The equipment is ready. The cue is not.

Nemi confirms the number ten card, filename, and live mic are all set. Pex displays a green check beside each. Only then does Kavi return to the ranking.

ACT THREE

At number one, the correct video plays. The audience applauds. Nemi confirms the rank from the printed chart and closes the poll. Boro performs the number ten song after the countdown. Kavi introduces him without changing his chart position.

KAVI: The list is complete, and the guest is still waiting.

BORO: I am ready.

Pex runs the live camera; Nemi marks the playlist complete. Rali removes the number cards from the stage and stacks them in order. Kavi thanks the audience for staying through the file corrections.

TAG

The credits begin. Pex opens the next folder. It is labeled "NEXT WEEK - DRAFT." Nemi takes the mouse before he clicks.

FINAL IMAGE: The number one card stays on the wall while Boro bows to the camera on his actual number ten cue.
