# The Talent Show Is a Talent Show

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised talent competitions, dramatic judging pauses, golden buzzer moments, and performers whose act is interrupted by the production*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A community talent competition keeps interrupting acts for reaction shots, forcing the contestants and judges to finish the show on their own terms.

Cast: Host Palla, a gibbon; judge Vex, a macaque; judge Omi, an orangutan; performer Neri, a capuchin; performer Tavi, a ring-tailed lemur; producer Rell, a mandrill; and supporting stage manager and camera operator.

## Episode: "The Golden Buzzer"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. NERI STANDS WITH A STACK OF CUPS.]

PALLA: What is your talent?

NERI: I stack cups quietly.

Rell drops a cymbal offstage.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[BACKSTAGE.]

The show begins with a loud intro, flashing lights, and a countdown. Neri's act is a silent cup-stacking routine. Producer Rell says the program needs an emotional backstory. Neri says she learned stacking cups while helping at the cafe.

PALLA: What did you overcome?

NERI: A shortage of cups.

Rell asks for a childhood photograph. Neri hands him a picture of herself stacking cups at the cafe. The editor adds a storm sound and a slow push-in. Neri watches the monitor as the image gets more dramatic.

NERI: It was Tuesday. The cafe was open.

RELL: We can make Tuesday feel like a finale.

He tries an interview question about sacrifice. Neri says she returned the cups at closing. Rell asks her to repeat it with tears. Neri places a cup on his head; he holds still until it begins to slide. Palla calls that an act, and Rell cues a commercial break.

Vex and Omi sit at the judges' table. Vex says the act must build suspense. Omi asks whether the cups are stable. Neri demonstrates a small pyramid. The stage manager's tail catches a cable, and the lights flicker.

Before the performance, Neri requests a stable table and a single camera view. The producer installs a small camera on a moving track so the tower can have a dramatic entrance. Neri asks the crew to lock the track. Rell says the camera needs to travel. It glides past the cups, bumps the table leg, and makes the pyramid lean. Neri catches it with her tail while holding the bottom cup steady.

NERI: This is why I stack on the floor.

RELL: Can you do that again, but discover the floor?

Palla starts the act over. Rell interrupts to ask for a reaction shot from Vex. Then a close-up of Omi's hands. Neri waits, balancing the same three cups, until the camera returns. Vex tries to look surprised, but his eyebrows have been held up so long that they begin to tremble.

OMI: I am impressed by her patience.

VEX: I am impressed by my face.

Neri finishes a small tower. The crew celebrates, then notices only three cups are stacked. Rell asks whether she can make it a grander act. Neri reaches for a second set. One cup is missing; the stage manager is drinking from it. She gets the cup back, rinses it, and sets the performance table farther from the camera track.

### Act Two

[PERFORMANCE.]

Neri begins stacking cups. The camera cuts to Vex's reaction, then Omi's, then the audience. By the time the shot returns, the pyramid is finished. Palla asks Neri to start over so viewers can see it. Neri starts again, but Rell introduces a slow-motion replay from the previous take. The cups appear to fall in reverse.

VEX: The stack is defying gravity.

OMI: The video is reversed.

Rell hits the golden buzzer. Confetti falls onto the cups. Neri catches the top cup before the tower tips. The act continues, now under confetti. Vex gives a standing ovation. Omi asks whether the confetti was part of the performance.

NERI: It was not.

Palla brings on Tavi, who performs a balancing act with a long pole. The camera operator asks for a close-up. The shot cannot show both pole ends. Tavi requests a wide shot. The camera zooms in anyway, then the pole catches the curtain.

### Act Three

[THE JUDGES DELIBERATE.]

Rell asks the judges to choose one winner. Vex votes for Neri's cup stacking. Omi votes for Tavi's balancing. Palla says both acts were interrupted by the production. Rell tries a dramatic pause. The audience waits.

PALLA: We can let them perform again without cuts.

The stage crew clears the confetti. Neri stacks the cups in one uninterrupted shot. Tavi balances the pole in the wide frame, with no close-ups. The acts finish. The audience applauds both.

OMI: The performance was visible from beginning to end.

VEX: That is a strong finale.

Palla awards the prize to the stage crew for keeping the show together. Neri and Tavi share the runner-up trophy. Rell asks for a reaction shot. Both performers look directly at the camera and wave.

### Tag

[EMPTY STAGE.]

The cup tower stands beside the pole. Rell taps the golden buzzer. The unplugged button gives no sound.

PALLA: The show is over.

RELL: One more take?

Neri lifts the unplugged buzzer from its stand and carries it offstage before he can press it again.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
