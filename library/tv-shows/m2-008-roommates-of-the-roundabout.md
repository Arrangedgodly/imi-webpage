# Roommates of the Roundabout

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sitcom about mismatched flatmates*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: Three monkeys sharing a rented flat must get a large wardrobe through a narrow hall before the landlord's inspection, and each is sure the others are making it harder.

Cast: Dex, an orderly macaque who labels shelves; Rue, a relaxed capuchin who collects maps; Fenn, a long-limbed gibbon who practices dance turns indoors; and Mrs. Pogo, their landlord, who measures doorways with a piece of string.

## Episode: "The Wardrobe Problem"

### Cold open

INT. FLAT HALLWAY, MORNING

Dex pushes a wardrobe toward the bedroom. Rue pulls from the other end. Fenn stands in the doorway giving advice while spinning a spoon.

INT. FLAT HALLWAY, CONTINUOUS

The wardrobe is wedged at the corner. Rue braces the door while Fenn checks whether his long arms can reach the handle. Mrs. Pogo measures the gap with string.

DEX: Turn it left.

RUE: My left or your left?

The wardrobe turns in a complete circle and returns to its starting point. Mrs. Pogo appears outside the open front door.

MRS. POGO: Inspection is in one hour.

The wardrobe blocks the door. She steps over it without comment.

### Act one

The flatmates bought the wardrobe at a neighborhood sale. Dex wants to install it before the landlord arrives because his clothes are stacked in the kitchen. Rue says the flat needs a shared coat closet. Fenn says the wardrobe would make a fine dance partner if everyone would stop pushing it.

Dex has assembled it inside out. The doors open against the wall, and the drawers face the hallway. He says the instructions were unclear. Rue discovers he used the diagram upside down because it fit better on the floor. Fenn proposes sliding it through the open window. The flat is on the second floor.

Mrs. Pogo begins the inspection in the kitchen. She checks the stove, then the fruit bowl. Rue has used an old map as a placemat. The map is from a country that no longer exists. Mrs. Pogo asks if it is clean. Rue says it has been to many places, mostly under breakfast.

### Act two

They try to turn the wardrobe at the hall corner. It catches on the rail and hangs at a diagonal.

Mrs. Pogo finds them holding it and reminds them to keep the hall clear. A neighbor opens the door, cannot pass, and hands Rue his groceries.

Mrs. Pogo suggests removing the flat door. Rue maps the hallway and finds the wardrobe will fit if they rotate it inside the bedroom.

The fixture blocks the upright wardrobe. They remove it, tilt the frame past, and discover the drawers are backward.


INT. FLAT HALLWAY, CONTINUOUS

The wardrobe is wedged at the corner. Rue braces the door while Fenn checks whether his long arms can reach the handle. Mrs. Pogo measures the gap with string.

DEX: Turn it left.
RUE: Your left or mine?
FENN: The wardrobe is spinning.
DEX: Stop turning it.
RUE: We are not turning.
FENN: I am helping.
MRS. POGO: Inspection is soon.
DEX: The door is blocked.
MRS. POGO: I noticed.
RUE: It is a large wardrobe.
MRS. POGO: It is in the hall.
FENN: The hall is narrow.
DEX: The plan was wrong.
RUE: The plan is cardboard.
DEX: That's still a plan.
FENN: Can it dance?
MRS. POGO: It cannot.
FENN: Then no.
DEX: Lift the bottom.
RUE: Which bottom?
DEX: The heavy bottom.
MRS. POGO: Both ends are heavy.
FENN: The wardrobe has range.
RUE: It is stuck again.
DEX: We need a map.
RUE: I have one.
DEX: That is a map of Peru.
RUE: It shows a narrow pass.
FENN: We need a local map.
MRS. POGO: Remove the door.
DEX: The wardrobe door?
MRS. POGO: The flat door.
RUE: She is the landlord.
FENN: The hallway has two doors.
DEX: The bedroom door comes off.
MRS. POGO: That's acceptable.
RUE: I have the hinge pins.
FENN: I have the spoon.
DEX: Put down the spoon.
FENN: It was for leverage.
MRS. POGO: Use a screwdriver.
DEX: There is one in the drawer.
RUE: The drawer is in the wardrobe.
FENN: We have a second problem.

### Act three

The inspection pauses while the three roommates take the wardrobe apart. They each claim the other should have read the instructions. Mrs. Pogo asks who kept the instruction sheet. All three point at Dex. He admits he used it to protect the floor from scratches, then recycled it with the cardboard.

Rue finds a photograph from the sale listing. It shows the wardrobe's drawer handles on the other side. Fenn identifies the panel that must be flipped. Dex removes the screws, Rue keeps the pieces in labeled bowls, and Fenn holds the frame steady without making a dance move. The wardrobe comes together correctly.

They carry it into the bedroom using the doorway route Rue mapped. Dex puts his clothes away. Rue assigns a shelf for shared coats. Fenn leaves a small open space for a folding screen so he can practice turns without hitting the doors. Mrs. Pogo checks the hall and finds it clear.

She says the flat passes inspection, but she expects the roommates to keep the front door unblocked. Rue offers the neighbor his groceries. He has been standing outside long enough to finish a conversation with someone across the courtyard. Dex tells him the wardrobe is now in the bedroom. The neighbor says he never knew it had been in the hall.

### Tag

That night, Fenn dances in the bedroom. The wardrobe doors swing open with each turn. Dex closes them. They swing open again.

RUE: It likes the music.

Dex installs a little latch. Fenn turns once more. The doors stay shut, but a drawer slides open and releases one sock.

Mrs. Pogo, from the hallway: I heard that.

The roommates stare at the sock. Rue picks it up and adds it to the laundry basket.
