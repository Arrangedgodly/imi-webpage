# A Show About a Show

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: behind-the-scenes television documentaries, confessional interviews, and scripted spontaneity*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A documentary crew follows the cast of a beloved grove sitcom as they film its season finale, while the sitcom's own crew tries to stage candid moments for the documentary.

Cast: Sitcom lead Sela, a macaque; co-star Varo, a gibbon; director Pavi, a capuchin; documentarian Nemm, an orangutan; and boom operator Tekk, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Candid Take"

### Cold open

INT. SITCOM SET, DAY

[A kitchen set. Sela stirs an empty pot. Varo enters carrying a scripted pie.]

NEMM: What are you feeling as you begin the final episode?

SELA: I am waiting for the director to say action.

PAVI: Action!

VARO: I brought a pie.

SELA: The script says you brought a secret.

VARO: The pie is a secret.

[The pie falls apart. Tekk's boom dips into the frame.]

NEMM: That felt authentic.

PAVI: It was the pie.

### Act One

INT. SET HALLWAY

[Nemm interviews Pavi beside a painted door.]

NEMM: How do you create natural chemistry?

PAVI: I tell them where to stand.

NEMM: Doesn't that make it less natural?

PAVI: It makes the camera see it.

Tekk: The boom is in the shot.

PAVI: Move it.

Tekk: The documentary camera can see the boom moving.

NEMM: That's part of the process.

[Sela and Varo rehearse a hug. They stop when the cameras approach.]

SELA: Are we supposed to be candid?

VARO: The script says "embrace with genuine warmth."

SELA: Is there a warmer embrace?

VARO: We can try another take.

PAVI: The documentary team wants real emotion.

NEMM: We do.

PAVI: Then wait for the third take.

[They hug again. Tekk's boom taps Sela's head.]

SELA: That was real.

### Act Two

INT. CONTROL ROOM

[The documentary crew watches footage of the sitcom crew staging candid moments.]

NEMM: We need an honest moment.

TEKK: The show is on a set.

NEMM: The honesty can be about that.

PAVI: We need a pickup shot of the cast laughing in the kitchen.

SELA: We already laughed in the kitchen.

PAVI: That was the pie accident.

VARO: The pie was scripted.

PAVI: The collapse wasn't.

NEMM: Let's film the cast reacting to the collapse.

SELA: We already reacted.

NEMM: Then react again, but remember how it felt.

[The pie collapses again. The cast watches it silently.]

VARO: It doesn't feel the same.

PAVI: We need to recreate the feeling.

SELA: You cannot recreate a pie.

TEKK: We can use a second pie.

PAVI: We only have one.

[They lift the broken pie onto a plate. It slumps.]

NEMM: This is an intimate portrait of production.

SELA: It is a portrait of dessert.

### Pickup line

INT. SITCOM SET

[Pavi places a fresh pie on the table. The documentary camera moves closer.]

PAVI: We need the cast to laugh naturally.

SELA: The pie is empty.

VARO: It has a cardboard bottom.

NEMM: What would you do if it were a real pie?

SELA: I would eat it.

PAVI: That isn't the line.

VARO: The line says, "We can laugh about it now."

SELA: We can laugh about the cardboard.

[They do. The sitcom crew laughs too.]

NEMM: That is the moment.

PAVI: That is a setup.

TEKK: The boom is over the pie again.

PAVI: We need a new angle.

NEMM: The documentary has found the real laugh.

### Act Three

INT. SITCOM SET, EVENING

[The cast prepares the finale scene. The script has a line reading "Everyone shares the last slice."]

SELA: There is no slice.

VARO: The pie is gone.

PAVI: The script needs a resolution.

NEMM: What would the characters do?

SELA: They would order another pie.

VARO: The kitchen is not real.

PAVI: We can write in a bakery.

TEKK: The bakery is a painted door.

NEMM: The cast is finding its own ending.

SELA: We are finding the edge of the set.

[They look behind the false wall. The studio crew is visible.]

VARO: There is the real kitchen.

PAVI: Cut.

NEMM: Keep rolling.

SELA: We are not in the sitcom anymore.

NEMM: That is the documentary's finale.

PAVI: The documentary was supposed to be about our finale.

NEMM: It is now about how the finale got made.

VARO: What happens to the pie?

SELA: It has finished its season.

[They place the empty plate on the set. The camera stays on it.]

### Last take

INT. SET HALLWAY

[The cast waits while Pavi resets the kitchen. Nemm leaves a camera running.]

NEMM: Tell us when you're ready.

SELA: We are ready.

PAVI: I need one more pie.

VARO: The bakery is closed.

SELA: Then we will use the empty one.

[The cast sits at the table. The documentary crew catches them laughing before the cue.]

NEMM: That was unscripted.

PAVI: Keep it.

### Tag

INT. SITCOM SET, NIGHT

[Nemm asks for one final candid shot. Sela and Varo sit naturally on the fake couch.]

NEMM: What are you thinking?

SELA: That this couch is painted on.

[The couch prop tips over, revealing the painted front.]
