# The Transit Challenge Takes the Long Route

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel challenge programs that add arbitrary checkpoints to a journey that already has a working route*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: Two teams race across town by public transit, but the show's clue cards send them past the same station repeatedly.

Cast: Host Nara, a gibbon; team members Velo, a macaque, and Sumi, a capuchin; producer Odo, a baboon; and station clerk Tavi, a lemur.

Episode: "The Transfer Test"

COLD OPEN

INT. CENTRAL STATION - MORNING

Nara holds two route cards. Velo and Sumi wait at the platform. Odo watches the show clock.

NARA: Your clue is to find the only station with a clock.

TAVI: Every station has a clock.

ODO: The clue means the only clock we approve for the segment.

SUMI: Which one is that?

[THE STATION CLOCK TICKS. ODO POINTS TO A SECOND CLOCK ON THE PRODUCTION CART.]

VELO: They both tell the time.

ACT ONE

Velo and Sumi open the first clue card. It says "Take the line that crosses the river." Tavi points to a route map.

TAVI: The blue line crosses the river and stops at the market.

ODO: You need to take the scenic line for the camera.

SUMI: Does it reach the market?

ODO: It reaches the depot, then you transfer.

VELO: The blue line is direct.

NARA: The challenge rewards creative routing.

[THE TRAIN ARRIVES. SUMI BOARDS THE BLUE LINE. VELO WAITS FOR THE SCENIC LINE.]

ACT TWO

INT. DEPOT PLATFORM - LATER

Odo hands Velo a second card. Nara asks Sumi to hold her train so the teams can be reunited.

NARA: The clue is "return to where your journey began."

VELO: Central Station.

ODO: The studio starting mark is at the east entrance.

TAVI: That is a different side of the same station.

SUMI: I am already there.

[NARA TURNS THE MAP. THE EAST ENTRANCE IS ON THE OPPOSITE SIDE OF THE BLOCK.]

NARA: You have one minute to reach the mark.

VELO: We can walk around the station.

ODO: The show route requires a transfer.

TAVI: The pedestrian tunnel is open.

[VELO STARTS TOWARD THE TUNNEL. ODO ROLLS THE CAMERA CART IN FRONT OF HIM.]

ODO: We need a tracking shot.

VELO: Then roll beside me.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CENTRAL STATION - DAY

Sumi reaches the east entrance first. Velo and the camera crew emerge from the tunnel. Nara holds up a final clue.

NARA: Find the red bench and bring back the ticket.

SUMI: The red bench is right there.

VELO: What ticket?

TAVI: Your transit tickets.

[THE TEAMS CHECK THEIR POCKETS. ODO HAS THE CLUE ENVELOPE, WHICH IS RED.]

ODO: The clue said ticket.

NARA: Is the envelope the ticket?

TAVI: No.

SUMI: It is red, though.

[ODO PLACES THE ENVELOPE ON THE RED BENCH. IT SLIDES OFF.]

VELO: We can show our passes.

NARA: That counts.

ACT THREE

INT. CENTRAL STATION - PLATFORM

Nara stops the teams beside the blue-line platform. Odo checks the camera monitor, which shows an empty east entrance.

NARA: We need a replay of the transfer.

VELO: We took the direct train.

ODO: The camera missed the arrival.

TAVI: The station clock shows when you arrived.

NARA: Can the clock be our finish-line shot?

SUMI: It is not a finish line.

[NARA ASKS THEM TO WALK PAST THE CLOCK. THEY WALK BACKWARD TO MAKE THE SHOT LOOK LIKE A REWIND.]

VELO: We are back at the station again.

NARA: The replay gives the audience context.

TAVI: They saw them arrive the first time.

ODO: We need the camera shot for the recap.

SUMI: Then film the clock. It is accurate.

[ODO FRAMES THE CLOCK. ITS HANDS HAVE MOVED AHEAD ONE MINUTE.]

NARA: The replay is now later than the event.

TAVI: That is still the correct time.

INT. CENTRAL STATION - FINAL CHECKPOINT

Both teams arrive with their transit passes. Nara checks the stopwatch. Odo removes the extra route cards.

NARA: The winners took the direct blue line.

ODO: They skipped two checkpoints.

TAVI: They followed the route map.

SUMI: The train stopped at the market.

VELO: We bought the supplies we needed there.

NARA: You have completed the challenge.

[ODO CHECKS THE STATION CLOCK, THEN THE CAMERA CART CLOCK. THEY DIFFER BY A MINUTE.]

ODO: Which time counts?

TAVI: The train time.

NARA: What does the stopwatch say?

ODO: That the camera cart is late.

[THE CREW ROLLS THE CART ONTO THE TRAIN AFTER THE TEAMS HAVE BOARDED.]

NARA: The route is finally direct.

TAG

Nara hands the map to the teams. Odo folds it. The map unfolds again and covers the red bench.

SUMI: We have found the last checkpoint.

[THEY BOARD THE DIRECT TRAIN.]
