# The Great Acorn Caper

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: serialized heist dramas, intricate crew plans, and last-second escape reversals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A crew plans to retrieve a treasured acorn from a locked display case before it is auctioned, but the case is in the crew's own clubhouse and the key is attached to the plan.

Cast: Planner Vero, a gibbon; locksmith Sani, a capuchin; lookout Pru, a lemur; driver Tobo, a macaque; and clubhouse keeper Harl, a gorilla.

## Episode: "The Case with No Lock"

### Cold open

INT. CLUBHOUSE, NIGHT

[A glass display case stands in the center of a room. Inside is one acorn. Vero studies a hand-drawn blueprint.]

VERO: At midnight, the acorn moves.

SANI: Where?

VERO: Into the case.

HARL: It is already in the case.

PRU: The case is locked.

TOBO: Where is the key?

[Everyone looks at Sani. A key hangs from his belt.]

SANI: I am the locksmith.

HARL: You're also the keeper.

VERO: The plan has changed.

### Act One

INT. CLUBHOUSE

[Vero spreads a map on the floor. The map shows the clubhouse, with an arrow marked "IN."]

VERO: We enter through the skylight.

PRU: The skylight is open.

TOBO: Then we should enter through the door.

VERO: The door is visible from the street.

HARL: There is no street. This is a tree.

SANI: The display case has no lock.

VERO: The lock is in the plan.

SANI: I drew it on the case.

VERO: You designed the plan.

SANI: I wanted to look professional.

PRU: The acorn is behind glass.

TOBO: The lid lifts.

HARL: The lid is held down by a banana.

VERO: That's a security system?

HARL: It is a weight.

[Harl lifts the banana. The case opens.]

VERO: We have breached the first layer.

SANI: It was not a layer.

PRU: A patrol is approaching.

[Harl walks around the room holding a lantern.]

TOBO: The patrol is Harl.

HARL: I am checking the door.

VERO: We need a distraction.

PRU: I can knock on the window.

SANI: We are inside the clubhouse.

PRU: That will distract us.

### Act Two

INT. CLUBHOUSE, MOMENTS LATER

[The crew puts on dark masks. Vero whispers into a cup tied to a string.]

VERO: Phase two.

TOBO: The cup is not connected to anything.

VERO: It is a secure line.

SANI: I can use the key.

VERO: Not yet. We need the decoy.

PRU: What is the decoy?

VERO: A second acorn.

HARL: There are no second acorns.

PRU: There is a walnut.

VERO: A walnut is not an acorn.

TOBO: It is close enough from the roof.

[They place the walnut in the case. The glass lid closes.]

SANI: The case is now more secure.

HARL: The banana is still beside it.

VERO: The target remains unguarded.

[Harl picks up the walnut and cracks it.]

PRU: The decoy has been compromised.

HARL: It was a walnut.

VERO: The operation is exposed.

TOBO: By a snack.

SANI: We can still retrieve the acorn.

VERO: We must not leave the key behind.

SANI: The key is on my belt.

VERO: Then don't leave your belt.

[Sani bends to reach for the key, but his belt catches on the case handle.]

SANI: I am attached to the evidence.

PRU: The evidence is attached to you.

### Act Three

INT. CLUBHOUSE, MIDNIGHT

[Harl unlocks the case with the key. Vero reaches for the acorn, then stops.]

VERO: The acorn is the last one from the old tree.

HARL: It is a keepsake.

PRU: It was going to be auctioned.

HARL: For the roof repair.

TOBO: If we take it, there is no auction.

VERO: The plan was to steal it before it left.

SANI: We can steal the plan instead.

HARL: What would that do?

SANI: It would make the auction a surprise.

PRU: We could announce the story of the acorn and invite bids for the roof.

VERO: That is not a heist.

TOBO: It has a team, a target, and a deadline.

HARL: The deadline is tomorrow.

VERO: I see the new plan.

[They place the acorn on a velvet pad. Harl opens the clubhouse door. Neighbors gather outside.]

HARL: The acorn stays here. The auction raises money for the roof.

PRU: The first bid is one basket of leaves.

SANI: I bid two baskets.

TOBO: I bid a plank.

VERO: I bid the entire crew's labor.

HARL: Sold.

VERO: We have completed the operation.

SANI: We didn't take the acorn.

VERO: We took the plan somewhere better.

### Tag

INT. CLUBHOUSE, DAWN

[The crew patches the roof. The acorn sits in the case under a new sign: "NOT FOR SALE."]

PRU: The case is unlocked.

HARL: The banana is still on top.

[Everyone looks up as a drip lands inside the case.]
