# The Animation Special Dubs the Animals

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: behind-the-scenes animation specials that let the voice-over describe drawings before artists finish them*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Rali, a macaque; animator Pavo, a gibbon; voice actor Seli, a lemur; editor Oru, an orangutan; producer Kima, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. ANIMATION STUDIO - DAY

Seli voices a bear. The screen shows a blank page.

SELI: I have found the honey.

PAVO: I have not drawn the bear yet.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Kima tells the crew the special must show a finished scene. Oru loads a rough cut. It begins with Seli's voice, then shows Pavo sketching the bear's paw. Rali narrates that the bear is already climbing a tree. The drawing is still a circle.

PAVO: It is a head.

RALI: It is a very determined head.

Seli asks to record the line after she sees the animation. Kima says the narration has already been recorded. Oru plays the track; it describes a chase before the bear has legs.

ACT TWO

INT. EDITING BAY - LATER

Pavo draws a path for the bear. The animation shows the bear moving left. Seli voices a line about turning right. Oru asks for another take. Kima asks the animator to flip the drawing instead. Pavo says the bear's bandage is on one arm; flipping it moves the bandage.

[The editor reverses the audio so the line sounds backward. Rali calls it a mysterious bear language. Seli asks them to stop before the whole track is reversed.]

Pavo adds the bear's arm frame by frame. Kima asks him to speed it up for the special. The bear's paw appears in three places at once. Rali narrates that it has discovered a shortcut. Oru freezes the frame; the paw remains duplicated. Seli records a new line: the bear waves. The animation shows the bear reaching for honey.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Pavo finishes the sequence. The bear climbs, pauses and reaches the branch. Seli watches the action before recording. She says the line at the right moment. Kima asks for an alternate take with more excitement. Seli asks whether the bear has found the jar or only smelled it. Pavo points to the final drawing: the jar is still behind the branch.

INT. VOICE BOOTH - LATER

Seli watches the animatic while Pavo lays three paper panels on the table: climb, reach, open. Oru marks the audio track with removable tabs, but the line about finding honey sits before the jar appears. Kima says the special needs a finished clip before the segment ends.

SELI: Give me the picture before you ask for the voice.

Pavo slides the panels into order. The bear climbs; its paw reaches; only then does the jar enter frame. Rali tries to narrate over the test. Kima mutes the host mic. Rali holds up a card reading "I am still here." Oru asks Seli to record the three short lines against the drawings. She does, pausing at the reach. The tracks line up on the monitor, though the last panel is only a pencil outline. Pavo says he still needs to animate the jar opening. Kima removes the countdown graphic from the booth monitor and lets him work. Oru labels the take ROUGH SYNC, not FINAL.

Pavo lays the finished frames beside the voice track. The bear's paw reaches the jar exactly as Seli says "there it is." Kima asks for a louder gasp to match the graphic; Seli plays the line at the same volume and lets the pause carry the surprise. Rali tries to add a title card that says AMAZING DISCOVERY. Oru replaces it with the character's name. The animator flips through the drawings once, and the bear's motion reads from climb to reach without a jump cut.

ACT THREE

INT. ANIMATION STUDIO - AFTERNOON

The special plays in full. Rali introduces the artist, then stays quiet while the scene runs. Pavo's drawings appear in order. The bear climbs without a duplicated paw. Seli's voice arrives after the jar is visible. Oru checks that the soundtrack and drawing stay aligned.

At the end, the bear opens the jar. The label reads PEANUT BUTTER, not honey. Seli asks if she should change the line. Pavo says the character thinks it is honey. Rali calls it a revelation. Kima asks for a final shot of the reaction. The drawing is not ready, so they wait while Pavo sketches the bear's surprised face. The camera films the pencil moving instead of cutting to an unrelated finished scene.

TAG

INT. EDITING BAY - AFTER THE SPECIAL

Oru saves the aligned version. Kima labels the earlier cut ROUGH ANIMATION. Seli returns to the voice booth.

FINAL IMAGE: The finished bear looks at the jar while the credits list the animator beside the voice actor.
