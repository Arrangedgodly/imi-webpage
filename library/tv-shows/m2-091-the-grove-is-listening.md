# The Grove Is Listening

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation reality television and ominous sound editing*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A camera crew returns to an abandoned feeding station after dark to investigate a repeated tapping sound. Their dramatic theories collapse when they discover how ordinary night movement changes the soundscape.

Cast: Host Dara, a gibbon; sound technician Olo, a macaque; field biologist Sen, a capuchin; night keeper Pavi, a slow loris; camera operator Etti, a tamarin; and three local panelists.

## Episode: "The Tapping at Hollow Nine"

### Cold Open

[BLACK SCREEN. THREE SLOW TAPS. A LOW WIND RISES. CUT TO A CAMERA LIGHT SWEEPING ACROSS AN EMPTY FEEDING STATION.] 

DARA: For six nights, a sound has crossed this clearing. Three taps. A pause. Then three more. We came to find out who, or what, is tapping.

OLO: I counted four taps.

DARA: The audio mix has three.

OLO: The fourth one is on the left channel.

[SFX: FOUR TAPS, CLEARLY.] 

DARA: We will investigate after we establish the baseline.

SEN: That is the sound of a seed pod hitting the roof.

DARA: You have not seen the roof.

SEN: I can hear the seed pod.

[THE CREW LOOKS UP. A POD FALLS AND TAPS FOUR TIMES.] 

DARA: We will return to that clue.

### Act One

[DAWN. DARA STANDS BEFORE A MAP WITH A RED CIRCLE AROUND HOLLOW NINE.] 

DARA: Local accounts say this station has been empty since the feeding trays were removed. Yet every night, something taps from inside.

PAVI: The station is not empty. I keep a water bowl there for the night troop.

DARA: That sounds like an explanation.

PAVI: It is a fact.

OLO: We recorded movement at 1:12 a.m. A shadow crossed the wall.

SEN: A branch moved in front of the lantern.

DARA: Can you say that after we replay the clip?

[OLO PLAYS THE FOOTAGE. THE SHADOW SWEEPS ACROSS THE WALL, THEN A TAIL APPEARS AT THE EDGE OF FRAME.] 

DARA: A long shape, with a narrow end and a cuff.

SEN: That is a branch and Pavi's sleeve.

PAVI: I was carrying the bowl.

ETTI: My lamp caught the branch and your sleeve at the same time.

OLO: The audio also contains a whisper.

DARA: What does it say?

OLO: It says, "Set it down carefully."

PAVI: That was me.

DARA: We will need to determine why a water bowl sounds like a warning.

PAVI: It has a crack.

[SFX: BOWL SET DOWN. A LOW, HOLLOW RING.] 

OLO: That is the voice we recorded.

DARA: The station may be communicating through the bowl.

SEN: The bowl rings because it is cracked.

DARA: We should test that theory at night.

### Act Two

[NIGHT. A THERMAL CAMERA DISPLAY SHOWS A BRIGHT RECTANGLE.] 

DARA: We are using a thermal camera to detect any presence within the structure.

OLO: The camera is reading the lantern as a warm figure.

DARA: Could the figure be standing still?

SEN: The lantern is bolted to the wall.

DARA: We will approach without disturbing it.

[THEY TIPTOE TOWARD THE LANTERN. A NIGHT MONKEY LEAPS ACROSS THE RAFTER. THE CAMERA OPERATOR GASPS.] 

OLO: Movement at the ceiling.

PAVI: That is a night monkey. He uses the rafters to reach the water.

DARA: Did you know he would be here?

PAVI: He comes every night.

DARA: Then why did no one tell the crew?

PAVI: I did. You asked me not to reveal the answer before the night shoot.

OLO: We have the tapping again.

[SFX: THREE TAPS. PAUSE. THREE TAPS.] 

SEN: That rhythm is different from the seed pod.

PAVI: The night monkey is knocking seeds off the shelf before he drinks.

DARA: We can place a microphone closer.

SEN: Please do not move the shelf while he is near it.

[THE MONKEY TAPS A SEED POD AGAINST THE BOWL, THEN DRINKS.] 

OLO: The source is a seed pod.

DARA: The ghost has a snack.

SEN: There is no ghost.

DARA: The audience will decide.

### Act Three

[STUDIO. A PANEL OF THREE LOCALS WATCHES THE CLIPS.] 

DARA: Was Hollow Nine haunted?

LOCAL ONE: No. The seed pod taps the roof.

LOCAL TWO: The shadow was the branch.

LOCAL THREE: The whisper was Pavi.

DARA: And the repeated sound?

PAVI: A night monkey tapping a seed against a cracked bowl.

OLO: The audio file titled "UNEXPLAINED VOICE" is now titled "Pavi sets down bowl."

DARA: We will keep the dramatic music under the final explanation.

SEN: The music makes the water bowl sound frightening.

DARA: The bowl is not frightened.

[THE CREW RETURNS THE BOWL TO THE STATION. THE NIGHT MONKEY APPEARS, TAPS A POD TWICE, AND DRINKS.] 

PAVI: He has changed the rhythm.

OLO: Should I record it?

DARA: Only if we can say what it is.

SEN: A monkey drinking.

DARA: That is a compelling final reveal.

[THE THEME SWELLS. THE NIGHT MONKEY STOPS TO LOOK DIRECTLY INTO CAMERA.] 

DARA: We are leaving Hollow Nine. The night will continue, whether we are filming or not.

[SFX: ONE SEED POD TAPS THE BOWL. CREDITS ROLL.]
