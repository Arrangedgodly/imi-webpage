# The Weather Desk Apology

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: news panels that turn a harmless forecast miss into a full public accountability segment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A local weather show holds a formal hearing after predicting a brief shower that never reached the garden.

Cast: Anchor Kito, a macaque; meteorologist Leni, a gibbon; editor Odo, a baboon; gardener Piri, a capuchin.

Episode: "The Dry Patch"

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO - DAY

Kito sits beside a chart showing one cloud.

KITO: We predicted rain. The garden stayed dry.

Leni looks at the chart.

LENI: The cloud moved north.

Odo rings a tiny bell.

ODO: Accountability segment.

ACT ONE

Kito asks Leni to explain the forecast. She says the cloud carried moisture, but the wind shifted. The chart included a chance of a passing shower. Odo says the graphic used a blue raindrop icon.

LENI: The icon was a possibility.

KITO: It looked like a promise.

Piri calls in from the garden. She says the soil stayed dry and she watered the seedlings. Kito asks if the missed shower harmed the plants. Piri says no; she had a watering can.

Odo asks whether the show should apologize. Leni says she can apologize for the confusing graphic, not for the cloud moving.

ACT TWO

The panel shows the forecast again. The blue icon covers the entire garden map. Kito asks if the graphic could have been smaller. Odo says a small icon would not fill the screen.

Leni redraws the map with a narrow cloud track and a shaded area showing the chance of rain. Kito says that looks more accurate but less dramatic. Odo adds a red banner: "CLOUD RETREAT."

Piri asks if the show can report what happened without an apology. Kito says viewers deserve clarity. Leni describes the forecast update and the direction the cloud moved. Odo tries to add a dramatic sting. The sting plays while the cloud icon drifts off the screen.

KITO: The rain has left the building.

LENI: It never arrived.

ACT TWO-B

INT. GARDEN - LATER

The panel goes outside to check the soil. Piri presses one finger into the earth. It is dry near the path and damp beneath the mulch. Kito asks if the forecast should have included the difference. Piri says the garden has microclimates because of shade and slope.

Odo films her watering the dry row. She moves the hose to the next row. The panel asks if this is a correction to the weather. Piri says it is gardening.

Leni shows the new graphic on a tablet. It marks where rain fell and where it did not. The map is small enough to fit beside Piri's plants.

ACT TWO-C

Leni explains how she reads cloud speed, shade and wind direction. Kito asks whether the forecast can be certain. Leni says no; it gives a likelihood based on current observations. Odo asks if the graphic should show every uncertainty. Leni says it should show enough to help people decide, without filling the screen with symbols.

Piri points to the garden bed. The plants beneath the tree are damp, but the row in full sun is dry. Kito asks if that means rain fell on one side. Piri says no; the mulch held moisture in the shade. Odo calls it a split-screen mystery. Leni asks him to stop inventing a plot for the soil.

The crew records a short demonstration: check the bed, touch the soil, water only the dry row. Kito asks if they can use that in the evening report. Piri says yes, if the forecast stays separate from the gardening advice. Odo moves the red banner off the chart and writes "CHANCE OF SHOWERS" beside the narrow cloud path.

Odo asks Leni to repeat the forecast in one sentence. She says, "A narrow shower may pass north of the garden, but the beds remain dry." Kito repeats it for the lower-third caption. The wording fits without a red warning banner. Piri asks whether she should water at noon. She checks the soil again and says yes, but only the sunny row. The camera captures her turning the hose nozzle to a gentle stream.

ACT THREE

Back in the studio, Kito reads a clear correction: the shower missed the garden; the forecast had shown a chance of rain; gardeners should check their own soil. Leni says the graphic could have made uncertainty clearer. Odo removes the red banner.

Piri asks if the show can keep the weather desk practical. Kito says yes, unless a cloud files a complaint.

TAG

A dark cloud appears on the monitor.

Odo reaches for the bell.

Leni turns it off and checks the wind.
