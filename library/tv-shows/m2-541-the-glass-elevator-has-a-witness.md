# The Glass Elevator Has a Witness

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: legal procedurals that turn every incidental observer into a crucial eyewitness*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: ADA VALE, a gibbon; DEFENSE ATTORNEY, a macaque; DETECTIVE BRAM, a baboon; COURT CLERK NELL, a macaque; SECURITY GUARD ORI, an orangutan; WITNESS PIP, a capuchin; LUDO, the defendant, a baboon; JUDGE, a mandrill.

COLD OPEN

INT. COURTHOUSE - GLASS ELEVATOR - DAY

A glass elevator rises through the courthouse atrium. Pip, a capuchin courier, watches two lawyers silently argue through the walls. Detective Bram stands beside Pip, holding a paper cup.

BRAM: Did you see who took the blue envelope?

PIP: I saw the elevator mirror.

BRAM: That's not the same as seeing a person.

PIP: It is if you are a mirror.

The elevator stops. Its doors open. Bram hands Pip the paper cup as he steps out. Pip remains inside, holding it as though it's evidence.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

INT. COURTROOM - DAY

Judge sits above a packed courtroom. The defendant, a baboon named Ludo, is accused of taking a sealed blue envelope from a records desk. Ada Vale rises.

VALE: The state has one eyewitness.

Bram stands.

BRAM: The courthouse has six hundred people and one glass elevator. We interviewed the elevator.

JUDGE: The elevator cannot testify.

VALE: Then we interviewed the courier inside it.

Nell sets the elevator maintenance log and a separate still from the lobby security camera on the evidence table. The elevator camera was offline during the alleged theft; the lobby camera faced the glass shaft and caught a reflection.

DEFENSE ATTORNEY: We object to the building having a memory.

JUDGE: Sustained. The building may remember silently.

Pip takes the stand. He explains that the elevator's polished wall reflected a person carrying a blue envelope. He did not see the face. He did notice a left glove with a stitched yellow button.

VALE: Could the reflection have been distorted?

PIP: It made Detective Bram look taller.

Bram scowls at the jury. Vale displays a photograph of the courthouse elevator. The reflection shows Pip, Bram, and a blurry third figure.

DEFENSE ATTORNEY: The image proves nothing.

VALE: It proves the witness was in the elevator.

JUDGE: The court will not retry the elevator's architecture.

Nell brings the maintenance log closer. At 2:14, the elevator stopped between floors. A maintenance note reads: "Yellow button recovered from door track." Bram explains that the button came from Pip's courier bag, whose loose thread matches the strand found by the records desk. The defense attorney has no yellow button on either glove.

Ludo raises a hand.

LUDO: My gloves have blue buttons.

The courtroom gasps.

JUDGE: There is no reason to gasp at a button.

ACT TWO

INT. COURTHOUSE HALL - LATER

Vale and Bram examine the evidence photograph in the hall. The reflection looks like a figure with a yellow button, but the courthouse clerk points out that the button is on Pip's own courier bag, which appears backward in the glass.

NELL: The image is mirrored.

BRAM: We were looking at the wrong hand.

PIP: I did say it was a mirror.

The courier bag is returned to the evidence table. A second yellow button remains on the bag beside the empty stitching where the recovered button came loose. Its thread matches the strand found by the records desk. The strand is attached to Pip's bag, not Ludo's glove. Pip admits he delivered the envelope to the wrong desk, then placed it inside his bag while checking the label. He saw the reflection and assumed someone else had taken it.

VALE: You moved the envelope?

PIP: I thought it was my route packet.

LUDO: It was labeled COURT RECORDS.

PIP: My route packet was labeled COURT ROUTE.

JUDGE: The difference is one word.

PIP: It is a very important word.

Nell brings the lobby still into court. Pip hands Bram his cup back, then holds the bag beside a demonstration mirror. Bram stands next to him with the cup in his original hand. The mirror places the cup on the opposite side; the surviving yellow button appears next to Bram although it belongs to Pip's bag. Pip moves the bag away, and the button leaves Bram's reflected sleeve.

PIP: The mirror did not lie. It reversed.

BRAM: That is what a mirror does.

PIP: I said it in the elevator.

The jurors compare the mirrored photograph with the real courier bag. The loose thread matches exactly. Bram apologizes for calling the reflection an eyewitness. Judge tells him the elevator may remain a witness, but only to its own doors.

The envelope is found inside the courier bag. It was never stolen; it contained a copy of the court's room schedule. Vale asks the judge to dismiss the charge. Bram removes the yellow thread from the bag and places it beside the photograph.

ACT THREE

INT. COURTROOM - DAY

Judge dismisses the case and orders the records desk to use larger labels. Pip apologizes to Ludo and delivers the correct packet. Ludo accepts, then asks if the elevator can give him a ride home.

JUDGE: The elevator is not a witness anymore.

NELL: Maintenance cleared it this morning. It can return to carrying passengers.

Bram looks at the cup lid, which has folded in on itself. He tries to drink; the lid snaps shut.

BRAM: I may have evidence.

VALE: That is a coffee lid.

Bram removes it before taking a sip.

FINAL IMAGE: Pip rides the glass elevator with a new bag marked DELIVER ONLY TO THE ROOM ON THE LABEL.
