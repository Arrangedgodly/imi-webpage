# The Airport Needs a Single-Item Inspection

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel reality shows that turn a simple security check into a dramatic obstacle course*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Officer Tavi, a gibbon; traveler Pava, a lemur; host Odo, an orangutan; assistant Neri, a capuchin; line marshal Seli, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. AIRPORT SECURITY - MORNING

[Pava stands at the belt with one paper umbrella. Odo narrates from beside the scanner.] 

ODO: The traveler has one item and one chance to clear security.

TAVI: It is an umbrella.

[The umbrella opens inside the scanner tunnel. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Welcome to Gate Challenge, where every passenger is a journey and every tray is a turning point.

PAVA: The umbrella opened by itself.

TAVI: It is a spring umbrella.

[Neri holds the tray and looks at the queue. Seli points to the sign: "One item per tray."] 

SELI: Please place the umbrella in the tray.

PAVA: It will not fit closed.

ODO: Can you demonstrate the struggle again?

TAVI: She has already passed inspection.

[Odo asks for a close-up. Pava tries to compress the umbrella, but the clasp slips. It springs open and catches the paper tray, which rolls down the belt.] 

[Neri runs after it. The open umbrella catches the moving air and sails toward the metal detector. Tavi blocks it with one foot.] 

TAVI: The item has not passed security.

PAVA: It is still attached to the handle.

[Tavi folds the umbrella and hands it back. Pava holds the handle while she removes her other items.]

ACT TWO

[The line advances. Odo asks Pava to remove all items from her pockets. She produces a transit ticket, a seed, and a tiny map folded into a triangle.] 

ODO: The suspense is building.

PAVA: I am ready to board.

TAVI: The ticket stays with you. The seed goes in the tray.

[The scanner beeps. Tavi checks the image. The seed appears as a bright dot. Odo leans into frame.] 

ODO: A suspicious object.

TAVI: A seed.

PAVA: I carry it for the layover.

[She tucks the seed into the map's fold. The scanner image becomes a small white square.] 

TAVI: The map has a crease.

ODO: The plot has thickened.

[The map unfolds on the belt, revealing a drawing of the terminal. Neri recognizes the gate.] 

NERI: Your gate is across the hall.

PAVA: I know.

ODO: Can we send the umbrella first?

TAVI: No item travels without its passenger.

[Seli tries to retrieve the tray. The umbrella is still open and has hooked its handle around the belt divider. She rotates it, unhooking it with one careful turn.] 

SELI: It needs to face sideways.

PAVA: That is how it closes.

[She closes it. The tray continues without the item.] 

[Tavi places the umbrella in a second tray and sends it through the scanner again. This time it stays closed, but the paper handle tag catches on the tunnel edge.]

TAVI: The tag is stuck.

PAVA: It is attached to the handle.

ODO: We have a cliffhanger at the end of the belt.

[The line behind them grows longer. Seli places a sign on the floor directing passengers to the next lane. Neri walks down the queue, asking each person to keep one tray between their items.]

NERI: We can keep the belt clear if everyone uses one tray.

[Odo steps onto the marked lane to get a better camera angle. The next traveler waits with a single shoe.]

SELI: The host is in the queue.

ODO: I am gathering atmosphere.

TAVI: Gather it from behind the line.

[Pava reaches the tunnel exit and gently lifts the tag free. She closes the umbrella strap, then places it flat in the tray. It clears the scanner without opening.]

PAVA: It passed.

ODO: Can we have the drum roll?

Neri drums on the tray with two fingers. The sound is tiny.

ACT THREE

[Tavi hands the umbrella back to Pava. The security check is complete. Odo asks her to wait for the final question.] 

ODO: What did you learn about travel?

PAVA: Never open an umbrella indoors.

TAVI: That is not a security rule.

PAVA: It is a travel rule.

[They start toward the gate. The terminal doors open, and a breeze pulls the umbrella's strap loose. It flaps against Pava's shoulder.] 

ODO: The item has returned.

PAVA: It never left.

[Neri catches the strap and wraps it around the handle. Odo points out that the security camera has followed them into the concourse.] 

ODO: We have an unscheduled second checkpoint.

TAVI: We do not.

[The camera operator stays behind the security line. Odo holds the microphone forward, but the cord is too short. He turns back toward the lens.] 

PAVA: The show has missed its gate.

[Seli checks the board. Boarding has not started.]

SELI: You have time.

PAVA: I have a seat.

[She holds up her ticket. The seed falls from the map crease and lands in the tray.] 

TAG

TAVI: One item per tray.

PAVA: The seed is not traveling.

[The tray rolls back toward security by itself.]

ODO: The seed has made its connection.
