# The Travel Show Misses Its Own Street

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel programs that manufacture exotic discoveries while ignoring nearby local knowledge*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Davi, a macaque; producer Kiri, a capuchin; guide Suma, an orangutan; camera operator Telo, a lemur; sound recordist Pavo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. MARKET STREET - MORNING

Davi stands beside a painted sign reading OLD TOWN. Behind him is the studio parking lot.

DAVI: We have arrived at a remote corner of the city.

Suma points across the street to the market entrance. The crew has walked past it twice.

ACT ONE

EXT. MARKET STREET - CONTINUOUS

Kiri tells the crew that the segment needs an undiscovered destination. Suma says the market has been open for generations. Davi introduces it as a secret bazaar. A vendor waves from the doorway and calls Suma by name. Kiri asks him to wait until the introduction is finished.

DAVI: Few travelers know this place exists.

SUMA: It is on the bus map.

Telo frames Davi so the market sign appears behind him. The sign reads MARKET. Kiri asks Suma to turn it around. It is bolted to the post. Pavo records street sound. The bus announces the market stop through its speaker, drowning out Davi's whisper about hidden culture.

ACT TWO

INT. MARKET - LATER

Suma introduces the crew to a basket maker. Kiri asks for a phrase in the maker's language. The maker answers in the same language Davi speaks. Davi nods as though he has understood, then asks Suma to translate. Suma explains that the maker asked them to move away from the doorway.

[The crew steps aside. Two customers enter carrying shopping bags. Davi follows them, thinking they are part of a procession. They stop at the vegetable stall.]

DAVI: Our hosts have led us to a place of gathering.

SUMA: They came to buy tomatoes.

Kiri asks for a scenic overlook. Suma points to the second-floor balcony. The crew climbs the narrow stairs with cameras and lights. At the top, they find a notice: STAFF ONLY. Suma asks whether the crew has permission. Kiri looks at the schedule and sees the balcony listed as a scenic location. The owner unlocks the door and lets the crew film for two minutes. The view is the same street from above, including the studio van blocking the bus stop.

INT. MARKET - MOMENTS LATER

The basket maker demonstrates a handle weave. Davi asks if it is an ancient secret. The maker says it is a repair she learned from her aunt. Suma translates the explanation. Kiri starts to ask for a mysterious origin story, then notices the basket is being used to carry the crew's microphone batteries. Pavo hands the batteries back. The maker gives Davi a small basket to hold the map; it fits without being called ceremonial.

INT. MARKET - LATER

Davi tries a wrapped snack. He asks whether it is a special regional dish. The vendor says it is a sesame bun sold at breakfast. Kiri asks for a slower bite so the camera can capture the texture. Davi waits. The bun cools. Pavo records the silence. Suma says the morning rush is ending. Customers begin stacking chairs around the crew.

ACT THREE

EXT. MARKET STREET - AFTERNOON

Kiri asks Davi to deliver a closing reflection about the journey. He stands in front of the market sign, now facing the correct direction. A bus pulls up and blocks the camera. The driver opens the door and asks whether they are getting on. Suma says this bus runs to the next neighborhood. Davi begins his closing line, but the doors beep. He steps aboard, then realizes his map and microphone are still on the pavement.

SUMA: You have missed your own departure.

Davi steps back off the bus. The market vendor hands him the map. Pavo clips the microphone to his shirt. The crew asks the driver to wait. The driver says the schedule has room for one minute. Kiri wants a final take beside the sign. Suma suggests they film from the bus window instead. Davi boards with the map and mic. The market appears behind him as the bus moves.

DAVI: We found a place just down the road.

SUMA: We found the place we walked past.

The crew laughs. Kiri lets the exchange stay in the edit. The bus turns the corner. The camera catches the market sign one last time, then the city beyond it without a narration claiming discovery.

TAG

INT. EDIT SUITE - NIGHT

Kiri labels the episode THE MARKET AROUND THE CORNER. Davi asks if that sounds too ordinary. Suma places the bus map beside the keyboard; the market is marked in bold.

FINAL IMAGE: The map's red route circles the market, while the studio van is crossed out at the starting point.
