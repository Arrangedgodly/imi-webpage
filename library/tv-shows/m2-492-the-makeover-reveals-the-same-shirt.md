# The Makeover Reveals the Same Shirt

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: makeover programs that hide a participant's familiar clothes to manufacture a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Oda, a gibbon; stylist Pera, an orangutan; participant Liku, a macaque; producer Venn, a capuchin; camera operator Savi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. MAKEOVER STUDIO - DAY

A curtain parts. Liku stands in a new jacket over the same striped shirt she wore in the opening interview.

ODA: You look completely different!

LIKU: I am still wearing my shirt.

[The audience applauds on cue.]

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Venn tells Oda to save the reveal until after the commercial. Pera says Liku chose the jacket because it has roomy pockets. Liku turns out both pockets, displaying a comb and two oranges. The camera moves in for a detail shot. Venn asks Pera to remove the familiar shirt from the reveal. Pera says it belongs to Liku.

PERA: The makeover is the jacket and the haircut. She can keep her clothes.

ODA: We need a before and after.

Liku stands between two photographs. Both show the same shirt. One is labeled BEFORE. The other is labeled AFTER. Savi photographs the jacket from three angles, but every angle catches the striped collar. Venn places a scarf over the collar. Liku asks if it is part of the outfit. Pera says it was a styling choice for the shoot, not a requirement.

ACT TWO

INT. WARDROBE ROOM - LATER

Pera lays three scarves on a table. Liku chooses none. She likes the stripes and says she picked them at a market years ago. Oda asks if she wants a new shirt. Liku says she wants to show how the jacket works with the one she owns. Venn asks for a more dramatic answer. Liku repeats herself, with less drama.

[Savi films the jacket pocket. Liku places the comb inside, then retrieves it without taking off the jacket. Pera demonstrates a small cuff adjustment. The sleeve no longer covers Liku's hand.]

PERA: Now you can reach your keys.

LIKU: They were in my other pocket.

The crew laughs. Venn tells them that laugh was not in the script. He asks Savi to reset for the reveal. Liku leaves the room wearing the outfit. The camera rolls too early and records her walking back in to retrieve her market bag. Oda calls it an accidental second entrance. Liku asks why she is entering twice.

INT. MAKEUP MIRROR - MOMENTS LATER

Liku sits before the mirror. Pera trims one loose thread from the jacket cuff. Venn asks for a countdown to the reveal. Liku counts from three, then stops at one and asks whether she can decide when the curtain opens. Oda agrees. Savi marks a wider camera frame so the audience can see the whole outfit. Pera returns the comb to Liku's pocket and leaves the striped shirt exactly as it is.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Venn edits the before-and-after photographs. He crops the first to hide the jacket and the second to show it. The stripe remains in both. Savi adjusts the frame. Now the first picture shows Liku's face and the second shows her jacket but no face. Oda asks for a split screen. Pera places two full-length photos side by side. The camera catches the identical shirt, the new jacket and Liku's expression in both.

ACT THREE

INT. MAKEOVER STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Oda introduces Liku's reveal. The curtain parts. Liku steps forward in the jacket and striped shirt. Pera explains the pocket and cuff changes. Venn cues applause early; the audience claps while Liku is still crossing the stage. Oda tries to speak over them. Liku waits, then demonstrates the pockets. The audience quiets to watch.

LIKU: I can carry my comb and still find my keys.

Savi follows her hands instead of the jacket hem. Pera shows how the sleeve rolls once and stays. Oda asks for the dramatic turn. Liku turns, showing the back of the jacket. The curtain catches the hem and follows her. Venn tries to pull it free without entering frame. Pera unhooks a small loop and the curtain drops back into place.

Oda asks Liku to stand between the before and after photos. The images line up, striped shirt visible in both. Liku points out that the difference is the jacket, its useful pockets and a sleeve she can move in. The audience applauds without a cue. Venn glances at the applause monitor, then lets the camera stay wide.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Venn labels both portraits SAME SHIRT, NEW JACKET. Oda asks if the label spoils the reveal. Pera hangs Liku's jacket on the rack while Liku carries her market bag home.

FINAL IMAGE: The two photographs hang side by side, each showing Liku's striped shirt, while the jacket's empty pockets rest in the foreground.
