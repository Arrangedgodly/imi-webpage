# The Sheriff and the Side Gate

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: western serials, where a sheriff turns a broken side gate into a town-wide standoff*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Sheriff Odo investigates a gate that will not latch, while the ranchers on either side argue over who must close it.

Cast: Sheriff Odo, baboon; rancher Nara, macaque; rancher Piri, capuchin; blacksmith Vela, gibbon; town clerk Seli, lemur.

## Episode: "At the Swing of the Gate"

### Cold Open

[WOODEN GATE SWINGS OPEN IN THE WIND.]

ODO: Nobody cross that gate.

NARA: It is my gate.

PIRI: It opens onto my pasture.

SELI: It is a side gate.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[MAIN STREET. ODO STANDS BEFORE A NOTICE BOARD.]

Odo posts a notice: "Gate stays shut until further investigation." Nara says the gate will not latch. Piri says she has been leaving it open to move hay.

ODO: We have a breach.

NARA: We have a loose latch.

Vela examines it. The latch is bent, but the post is straight. Seli writes down the repair request.

PIRI: Who pays?

NARA: It is on the property line.

PIRI: The line runs through the hinge.

Odo asks each rancher to give a statement. Nara says she closed it in the morning. Piri says she found it open at noon. Vela asks if either saw an animal pass through.

NARA: I saw a goat.

PIRI: The goat was on my side.

ODO: We have a possible witness.

### Act Two

[THE SHERIFF HOLDS A FORMAL INQUIRY.]

Odo places a map on the table. He draws a line between the pastures and marks the gate. Nara says the gate swings toward her land. Piri says it should swing toward the road.

Seli asks which way the wind blows. They check the weather vane. It points east. Vela tests the gate; the wind pushes it open.

Vela straightens the latch. It closes once, then swings open again.

VELA: The latch is bent the other direction now.

ODO: The culprit has returned.

NARA: The wind did it.

PIRI: The wind does not own the gate.

ODO: The wind has no property papers.

Nara brings the goat to the gate. The goat nudges it and slips through the opening.

PIRI: There. The witness has demonstrated.

ODO: The witness is also the suspect.

Vela says the latch needs a stopper, not a stronger spring. Piri offers a short board. Nara has a spare hinge pin.

[THE SHERIFF CALLS A TOWN MEETING.]

Seli says the notice has confused people; some have been using the main gate instead. Nara says the side gate is the shortest route to the water trough. Piri says the main gate is easier for the hay cart.

ODO: We need one route for everyone.

VELA: A gate can serve two paths.

NARA: The post is on the boundary.

PIRI: The post is in the way.

Seli spreads the map on the table. The path bends around a shallow ditch. If the gate opens inward, it blocks the trough route; if it opens outward, it blocks the hay cart.

ODO: We have a second problem.

VELA: We can move the post.

NARA: That will cost a day.

PIRI: We can add a hinge on the other side.

Vela tests the gate's swing with a rope tied to the handle. It clears both routes if the stopper is placed higher.

### Act Three

[THEY REPAIR THE GATE TOGETHER.]

Vela installs the stopper. Nara holds the gate while Piri aligns the latch. Odo asks them to take turns closing it from both sides.

NARA: It catches from my side.

PIRI: It catches from mine too.

Seli updates the notice board: "Gate repaired. Please latch after use."

ODO: The investigation is complete.

PIRI: Who pays for the board?

NARA: I brought it.

PIRI: Who pays for the pin?

NARA: I brought it.

PIRI: Then I owe you.

NARA: You can repair the water trough.

PIRI: I already repaired it.

ODO: The debt is settled.

The goat pushes the gate. It closes and latches by itself.

VELA: The gate has learned.

NARA: The stopper holds it.

ODO: The gate has learned to use the stopper.

[THE SHERIFF RIDES PAST THE GATE.]

The latch holds through a gust. Odo removes his notice from the board. Seli replaces it with the repair instructions and a drawing of the stopper.

ODO: The town has a working gate.

NARA: The town has a sign.

PIRI: The gate is still the important part.

### Tag

[ODO RETURNS TO THE NOTICE BOARD.]

Seli has written a second notice: "Do not investigate the gate."

ODO: Who posted this?

SELI: The clerk.

PIRI: The clerk owns the board.

NARA: The gate is outside.

The goat walks through the gate. It latches behind him.

[SFX: LATCH CLICK, WESTERN THEME.]
