# The Morning Show Can't Find the Sunrise

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: morning television segments that keep smiling through obvious production mishaps*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: anchor Lali, a lemur; anchor Boro, a macaque; meteorologist Omi, an orangutan; producer Kiva, a gibbon; camera operator Satu, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. MORNING STUDIO - DAWN

A screen behind the desk reads GOOD MORNING. The stage is dark. Lali and Boro sit in front of a painted sunrise. The studio clock shows 6:00.

LALI: It is a beautiful morning.

[The backdrop rolls up, revealing a brick wall and a ladder.]

BORO: The sun has gone backstage.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Kiva asks the crew to keep smiling while Satu resets the sunrise backdrop. Omi begins the weather report. His map is upside down, with the north arrow pointing toward the desk. He turns it around; the magnets fall off and slide under the couch.

OMI: A mild front is approaching from the floor.

Lali introduces a cheerful garden segment. The garden cart is still in the loading bay. Boro fills time by describing a potted fern on the desk. Kiva asks him to call it a rare tropical specimen. The fern has a store label tied to its pot.

BORO: This fern was discovered in a shop yesterday.

Kiva cues the theme music. The music starts halfway through the melody, then stops when the sunrise backdrop catches on a cable. Satu frees it, and the painted sun appears upside down.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Kiva orders a new backdrop cue. Satu gives the camera operator a hand signal. The camera cuts to a close-up of Lali just as the painted sun rises behind her head like a halo. Boro points it out. Kiva says they can use it as a lifestyle feature.

LALI: I am not the sun.

KIVA: You are currently the brightest thing we have.

Lali asks the control room to keep that shot. The camera cuts to a weather map instead, showing a cartoon sun wearing her earrings. Omi removes the earrings and puts them on the desk. The graphic sun keeps them. Kiva asks for the map to be reset; Satu zooms in until one floating earring fills the screen.

Omi tries to show a radar animation. The graphic displays a large red dot over the studio. He says it is the weather station. Satu pans to a wet mop bucket beside the set. A stagehand has just cleaned the floor. Kiva tells the anchors to describe the dot as a patch of local showers.

[The anchors keep talking as the backdrop drops six inches. Lali holds it up with one hand while Boro continues the forecast. Omi moves the radar marker away from the studio. The dot follows his cursor, which is being controlled by a mouse on the floor.]

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

The garden cart arrives. It contains three seedlings and a watering can. The crew places it in front of the backdrop to hide the exposed brick. Boro asks the gardener to explain how to grow tomatoes. The guest says they are basil. Kiva tells the anchors to embrace the surprise. Lali reads the plant tag correctly. Boro calls it a flavorful tomato anyway. The gardener moves the basil tag into the camera frame.

ACT THREE

INT. STUDIO - 7:00

Kiva has one final cue: the sunrise animation. Satu switches to the wide shot. The screen counts down, then displays a night sky. Omi says the weather map is accurate; it is still dark outside. Lali turns to the studio window and sees dawn beginning beyond the parking lot. She asks for the camera to show the real sky. Satu rolls it toward the window, but a van backs into the frame and blocks the view.

BORO: Our sunrise is sponsored by parking.

The driver moves. The actual sky brightens. Kiva calls for the painted backdrop, but the cable slips again. Instead of holding it up, the crew lets it roll down. The real window remains visible. Omi gives the forecast from the glass: clear, cool, and already happening. Lali asks the gardener to take the basil home before the next segment. The anchors stop smiling long enough to laugh at the fern's store tag.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE BROADCAST

Kiva tapes the sunrise backdrop to the wall. The crew rolls the basil cart away. Boro wipes the desk where the watering can dripped. Omi returns the upside-down map to the graphics team.

FINAL IMAGE: The morning set shows the real pale sky through the window, while the painted sun leans against the wall with its label facing the camera.
