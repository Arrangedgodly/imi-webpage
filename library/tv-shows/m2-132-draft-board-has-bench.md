# The Draft Board Has a Bench

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports draft-night coverage that treats every selection as a franchise-defining revelation and fills time with speculative analysis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Analyst Vero expects a dramatic first pick for the canopyball league. Coach Lala needs a dependable repair crew for the team's loose court railing, but the broadcast keeps ranking players by potential.

Cast: Vero, a macaque analyst; Lala, a gorilla coach; Tuni, a lemur host; and Puck, a capuchin statistician.

## Episode: "With the First Pick, A Wrench"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A DRAFT BOARD HAS TWELVE NAMES. A SPOTLIGHT MOVES OVER THEM.]

TUNI: The first pick is moments away.

VERO: A decision that could reshape the whole season.

LALA: We need someone who can fix the loose railing.

[PUCK SLIDES A CARD INTO THE SLOT. IT READS "COURT MAINTENANCE CREW."]

TUNI: That name was not on the board.

VERO: A surprise pick.

LALA: They are already hired.

### Act One

[VERO POINTS TO A GRAPHIC OF PLAYER STATS.]

VERO: Candidate One has the longest reach. Candidate Two has the fastest sprint. Candidate Three has exceptional bench presence.

LALA: The railing has three loose bolts.

PUCK: I have the repair crew's schedule.

VERO: How does their schedule compare with our rivals?

PUCK: They arrive at ten.

[TUNI OPENS A MOCK ENVELOPE.]

TUNI: The room is holding its breath.

[PUCK LOOKS AT THE CAMERA. A FAN IS BLOWING THE PAPER ENVELOPE OPEN.]

PUCK: We should move the fan away from the board.

VERO: Could that be a metaphor for pressure?

LALA: It is a fan.

[TUNI CLOSES THE ENVELOPE WITH A CLIP.]

TUNI: With our first selection, the Grove team chooses... the maintenance crew.

[THE CROWD APPLAUDS. VERO LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.]

VERO: They have not played a game.

LALA: They have tools and a site plan.

### Act Two

[THE BROADCAST SHIFTS TO A "DRAFT ROOM" INTERVIEW. LALA SITS BESIDE THE BOARD.]

TUNI: Coach, what does the repair crew bring to the team?

LALA: A wrench, replacement bolts, and a level.

VERO: The level has tested well in practice.

LALA: It is a tool.

[PUCK SHOWS A BAR GRAPH WITH THREE BARS: "BOLTS," "WRENCH," "ON-TIME ARRIVAL." THE THIRD BAR IS TALLEST.]

VERO: Their punctuality score is elite.

[THE CREW ARRIVES. THEY INSPECT THE RAILING. ONE CREW MEMBER TAPS THE WOOD.]

VERO: What is the scouting report?

CREW LEAD: The bottom bolt is loose. The top brace is cracked.

PUCK: They identified two needs in one walk-through.

LALA: I could have shown them the bolts.

[THE CREW LEAD CHECKS THE POST AGAIN AND MEASURES THE SPACING.]

CREW LEAD: This section needs a brace. The other posts are secure.

TUNI: We have a live update.

VERO: The team has discovered a weakness in the structure.

LALA: They found a loose railing.

[THE WORKERS BLOCK OFF THE COURT AND REPLACE THE BRACE. PUCK TIMESTAMPS THE REPAIR.]

PUCK: Twenty-two minutes from arrival to safe inspection.

VERO: That is a record.

CREW LEAD: It is our standard process.

[THE CREW CLEARS THE COURT. LALA CHECKS EACH POST.]

[TUNI ASKS THE COACH TO EXPLAIN THE PICK TO VIEWERS. LALA STANDS NEXT TO THE COMPLETED REPAIR.]

LALA: We needed the court ready for practice. The crew fixed the brace and checked the posts.

VERO: No projection about future performance?

LALA: It is safe to play on now.

[THE TEAM WALKS ONTO THE COURT. ONE PLAYER TESTS THE RAILING WITH A HAND. IT DOES NOT MOVE.]

### Act Three

[THE DRAFT BOARD STILL HAS UNSELECTED PLAYERS. VERO ASKS LALA WHETHER SHE WILL USE ANOTHER PICK.]

LALA: We have a full roster.

TUNI: A bold decision.

VERO: The draft is still live.

LALA: The court is ready.

[PUCK ERASES THE MAINTENANCE CREW'S NAME FROM THE BOARD AND WRITES IT UNDER "COMPLETED WORK." THE PLAYER NAMES REMAIN.]

TUNI: Any final thoughts for the season?

LALA: We have practice at noon.

VERO: What about the rookie outlook?

LALA: I will watch the rookies at practice.

[THE CREW OPENS THE COURT. PLAYERS ENTER AND WARM UP.]

VERO: Could the team still trade for a player?

LALA: We have enough players. We needed a safe court.

TUNI: The broadcast has a trade desk.

LALA: The repair crew has gone back to work.

[PUCK CHANGES THE GRAPHIC TO SHOW THE COURT OPENING TIME.]

### Tag

[THE BROADCAST RETURNS TO THE BOARD. PUCK ADDS A SMALL TOOL ICON BESIDE THE MAINTENANCE CREW.]

VERO: They may be available in the next draft.

LALA: They have other jobs.

[THE SPOTLIGHT TURNS OFF. THE REPAIR CREW IS ALREADY GONE.]

VERO: We should update the board.

PUCK: The repair is complete.

TUNI: And the first pick?

LALA: The season starts with practice.

[THE BOARD GOES DARK. THE COURT LIGHTS COME ON.]
