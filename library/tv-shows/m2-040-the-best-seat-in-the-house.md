# The Best Seat in the House

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live variety television, celebrity banter, and music performances with sponsor breaks*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: The Canopy Variety Hour promises an elegant live concert from the oldest hollow in the grove, but every guest, prop, and camera operator has claimed a different seat.

Cast: Host Sena, a lemur; singer Kallo, a gibbon; comedian Tiff, a capuchin; sponsor representative Boro, a gorilla; director Yani, a macaque; and stagehand Vem, a baboon.

## Episode: "An Intimate Audience"

### Cold open

INT. OLD HOLLOW STUDIO, NIGHT

[A small stage is set inside a tree hollow. Rows of tiny stools face the stage. The audience, crew, and guests are all seated among them.]

SENA: Welcome to the Canopy Variety Hour, broadcasting live from our intimate studio.

YANI: The audience is sitting behind the cameras.

SENA: That's an intimate angle.

KALLO: I can't see the stage.

TIFF: You're on the stage.

KALLO: Then I can't see myself.

BORO: I have the sponsor seat.

VEM: The sponsor seat is on the stage.

BORO: That explains the view.

SENA: We'll begin with music.

[She gestures. Everyone looks at Kallo. Kallo is still trying to turn his stool.]

### Act One

INT. OLD HOLLOW STUDIO

[Yani whispers instructions to Vem. Vem quietly moves two stools. The whole front row shifts with them.]

YANI: We need the camera lane clear.

VEM: I moved the stools.

YANI: You moved the audience.

VEM: They were sitting on the stools.

SENA: Our first guest is singer Kallo.

KALLO: Thank you. I have prepared a song about finding a place to sit.

TIFF: Is it a ballad or a chair ad?

KALLO: It is a ballad with a chair sponsor.

BORO: The sponsor is a cushion maker.

SENA: Please save the sponsor mention for the break.

BORO: The cushion is already on my lap.

YANI: Camera one, tight shot on the singer.

KALLO: I am facing the back wall.

YANI: The back wall is where the camera is.

KALLO: Then my song is for the wall.

[Kallo sings a soft, wordless note. The hollow echoes it. A stool squeaks in harmony.]

TIFF: The room has joined the band.

KALLO: That was the chorus.

SENA: A beautiful chorus.

VEM: The squeaky stool is in the audience.

YANI: Move it off camera.

[Vem lifts the stool. The squeak follows.]

### Act Two

INT. OLD HOLLOW STUDIO, SPONSOR BREAK

[Sena faces camera. Boro stands beside a cushion.]

SENA: Tonight's broadcast is supported by soft cushions for hard branches.

BORO: They make sitting feel like landing in a warm nest.

TIFF: Can I try one?

BORO: During the break.

TIFF: The break is now.

BORO: I meant after the message.

SENA: Our next guest, Tiff, will perform a comic monologue.

TIFF: I wrote it while standing in line for this stool.

SENA: What is the subject?

TIFF: Seating arrangements.

YANI: We already have a stool joke.

TIFF: This is a different stool.

[Tiff steps to the microphone. The microphone is mounted on a stool.]

TIFF: Why did the guest bring a cushion to the show?

BORO: To support the program.

TIFF: That's my punchline.

BORO: It's in the sponsor agreement.

SENA: We have a live audience. Let them decide.

[The audience tries to clap. The stools squeak so loudly that the claps are lost.]

TIFF: They laughed with their furniture.

KALLO: I can do a harmony with that.

YANI: We are back from break in ten seconds.

SENA: We have not left the break.

YANI: The sponsor needs the cushion shot.

BORO: Here it is.

[He sits. The cushion slowly inflates, raising him above the other guests.]

TIFF: The sponsor is now the tallest guest.

BORO: That's a premium feature.

### Act Three

INT. OLD HOLLOW STUDIO

[The camera pulls back to reveal that every seat is occupied, including the camera platform.]

SENA: We promised an intimate audience. We have achieved it.

YANI: Camera two can't move.

VEM: The audience is sitting on it.

KALLO: I can sing from here.

TIFF: I can tell jokes from here.

BORO: I can sponsor from here.

SENA: The finale is a group song. Everyone who can see Kallo, join in.

[The audience turns toward Kallo. Half the stools squeak.]

KALLO: I will follow the room.

[He sings the same soft note. The squeaky stools answer in uneven harmony.]

TIFF: We have a chorus line.

YANI: The camera is still blocked.

SENA: Keep the shot. This is a live show.

YANI: The shot is all cushions.

BORO: That's good branding.

VEM: The stools are moving.

[The audience tries to stand. The stools stick to cushions and rise with them.]

SENA: We will take our final bow seated.

KALLO: A seated standing ovation.

TIFF: The stools are doing most of the work.

BORO: And the cushions are supporting the talent.

YANI: The camera got it.

SENA: Then we have a show.

### Tag

INT. OLD HOLLOW STUDIO, AFTER BROADCAST

[The crew begins stacking stools. One stool squeaks the opening note again.]

KALLO: The room wants an encore.

YANI: The broadcast has ended.

TIFF: The room doesn't have a camera.

BORO: The room has a sponsor cushion.

[Everyone sits down again.]

[The stools squeak together like a final chord.]
