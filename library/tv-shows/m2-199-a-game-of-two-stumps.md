# A Game of Two Stumps

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports documentary convention, recapping a historic contest whose score was two stumps and an unresolved argument over the boundary*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A documentary crew reconstructs the grove's first stumpball game, where two teams remember different rules.

Cast: Narrator Pavi, a capuchin; retired player Miri, a macaque; referee Odo, a baboon; and archivist Sela, a gibbon.

Episode: "The Boundary Line"

COLD OPEN

EXT. GROVE FIELD, MORNING

Pavi stands between two stumps.

PAVI: One match. Two stumps. No scorecard.

Miri holds a wooden ball. Odo points to a line in the dirt.

ODO: The ball crossed the boundary.

MIRI: It stopped before the boundary.

The camera zooms in. An ant walks across the line.

ACT ONE

INT. ARCHIVE ROOM, DAY

Sela finds a photograph of the old game. The boundary line is faint. Miri says her team won. Odo says the match ended in a tie. Pavi asks what happened to the ball. They all point in different directions.

Sela finds a note on the back of the photograph: "Ball rolled under stump. Game paused." The note is signed by both teams. Miri says the game resumed. Odo says it did not.

ACT TWO

EXT. GROVE FIELD, AFTERNOON

The crew recreates the match. Miri rolls the ball toward the line. Odo calls it out. Sela uses a measuring stick to mark the boundary. The ball rolls into a shallow rut and stops short. Pavi asks if the rut was there in the original game. Odo says the ground was uneven.

Miri moves the stump to make the court fair. Odo says the stump cannot move after play starts. Sela checks the photo; the stump appears in two different places. They may have moved it during the match. Pavi asks if there was a referee. Odo admits he was both referee and player.

MIRI: You called your own boundary.

ODO: I was closest.

They inspect the underside of the stump and find a groove where the ball often lodged. Pavi says the game may have paused more than once. The players agree that everyone took turns retrieving it.

ACT TWO-B

INT. ARCHIVE ROOM, LATE AFTERNOON

Sela finds a second photograph from the match. In it, the ball is under the stump, the players are taking turns retrieving it, and Odo is holding a whistle. Miri says the whistle was only for starting the game. Odo says he also used it to stop the match when the ball rolled away.

Pavi asks Sela to mark the exact boundary on the old field map. The ink has faded, but there is a line of stones around the playing area. Miri places the first stump inside the ring. Odo places the second outside it. They argue about which stone was moved. Sela compares the photographs and finds the same split rock in both frames.

SELA: The field was wider than we thought.

MIRI: Or the camera was closer.

Pavi asks for a reconstruction. Miri rolls the wooden ball and Odo waits to see where it stops. This time Odo does not call it out. He asks Sela to measure from the ball to the stone line. The ball is inside the boundary by a hand's width.

Odo agrees the old game could have continued. Miri says that still does not prove who scored. Sela suggests they leave the historical result unknown and make a new scorecard for the rematch. Pavi asks if the documentary can end without declaring a winner.

SELA: The record can be honest.

Pavi tears up a dramatic graphic that says "THE BIGGEST CONTROVERSY IN STUMPBALL." He replaces it with "BOUNDARY UNCLEAR; GAME CONTINUES."

ACT TWO-C

EXT. GROVE FIELD, SUNSET

Miri and Odo line up for the rematch, with Sela acting as a neutral referee. Pavi places the stumps inside the stone boundary. Miri rolls the ball; it stops beside the split rock. Sela checks the ground and confirms it is inside. Odo retrieves the ball without calling a point.

They play a short round with no scorekeeper. The ball rolls beneath the stump again. Odo and Miri both crawl after it and bump shoulders. They laugh, then agree to take turns retrieving it. Pavi asks if this will make better footage. Sela says it makes a fair game.

ACT THREE



EXT. GROVE FIELD, SUNSET

Sela draws a new boundary around the level part of the field. Miri and Odo roll the ball once each. Both shots stop inside the line. They decide the historical match cannot be scored, but they can play a rematch with clear rules and a neutral referee.

Pavi announces the rematch. The ball rolls under the stump. Odo begins to call it out, then laughs and crawls to retrieve it.

TAG

Pavi holds up the old photograph. The ant from the cold open is now on the boundary line.

PAVI: The only witness has not changed its position.

Sela moves the paper outside before the ant reaches the frame.
