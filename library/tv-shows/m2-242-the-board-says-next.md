# The Board Says Next

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: medical drama television, where a clinic's queue becomes a high-stakes ensemble crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: The clinic's appointment board jams on the word "next," sending staff and patients into a dramatic scramble to determine who is actually waiting.

Cast: Dr. Luma, a gibbon; nurse Odo, a baboon; receptionist Seli, a capuchin; patient Nima, a macaque; and orderly Pava, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Next One"

### Cold Open

[CLINIC WAITING ROOM. THE BOARD FLIPS, SHOWS "NEXT," THEN FLIPS BACK.]

SELI: The board says next.

ODO: Next what?

LUMA: Next patient.

NIMA: I have been next for twenty minutes.

PAVA: The board has not changed for twenty minutes.

[SFX: BOARD FLIPS AGAIN.]

SELI: It says next.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[DR. LUMA APPROACHES THE BOARD WITH A STETHOSCOPE.]

LUMA: The room is calm. The queue is not.

NIMA: I came in for a splinter.

ODO: I have three patients ahead of you.

NIMA: The board says next.

SELI: The board has been stuck on that word since the morning check-in.

Pava opens the board's side panel. A small card is wedged behind the flipper.

PAVA: I can remove it.

SELI: If you remove the card, the board may show the wrong name.

LUMA: We need a clear order, not a dramatic one.

Odo calls Nima into the examination room. Nima points at the board.

NIMA: It says next.

ODO: It says the same thing for everyone.

NIMA: Then I am not alone.

### Act Two

[A SECOND PATIENT ENTERS, HOLDING A SMALL TIN.]

PAVA: I brought the splinter that came out of my paw.

LUMA: Please keep it in the tin.

ODO: We have a specimen.

NIMA: Is the chair's splinter larger than mine?

LUMA: They are both small.

Seli tries to label the tins. She writes NIMA on both, then stops.

SELI: We need different names.

NIMA: Mine came from me.

PAVA: Mine came from the hallway.

LUMA: The splinter in the chair came from the armrest.

Odo checks the chair cover and finds a loose thread with a tiny wooden point. The clinic has three splinter sources, each needing a different fix.

ODO: We need a new armrest and a hallway sweep.

PAVA: And a tin for evidence.

LUMA: We are a clinic, not a detective office.

SELI: Then why are we keeping the splinters?

PAVA: The report needs evidence.

LUMA: The report needs a chair.

SELI: For the maintenance report.

[EXAM ROOM. LUMA EXAMINES NIMA'S PAW.]

LUMA: The splinter is small, but it is deep.

NIMA: Is it dangerous?

LUMA: Only if you keep pressing it against the armrest.

Nima looks at the armrest. A small point sticks out. Odo checks it and finds a splinter from the chair.

ODO: The clinic has a splinter problem.

NIMA: I told you I had one.

SELI: We have to update the waiting board.

Pava removes the stuck card. The board flips to "NIMA," then to "NEXT," then to "NIMA" again.

PAVA: It has learned one name.

LUMA: We need to reset it.

Seli re-enters the queue by mistake and receives a number. She is both receptionist and patient.

SELI: Who is at the desk?

ODO: You are.

SELI: I am waiting for the board.

Nima holds up the splinter. It is not his; it came from the chair. Luma sterilizes the spot and replaces the armrest cover.

NIMA: So my splinter was from the clinic?

LUMA: It was from the chair. You still have it.

NIMA: The chair is a patient now.

### Act Three

[WAITING ROOM. THE BOARD IS RESET. IT READS "NIMA," THEN "SELI."]

SELI: I am next.

ODO: You cannot treat yourself while checking in.

SELI: I can wait until my shift ends.

Pava calls the next patient. The board turns to "NEXT" again.

PAVA: It is stuck.

LUMA: The reset did not hold.

Seli opens the panel, finds the little card, and notices it is a printed instruction: "Flip to next after each patient." The card has been placed behind the mechanism.

SELI: It was the instruction sheet.

ODO: It was stopping the board from moving.

Luma calls Nima back to finish the check. She uses forceps to remove the chair splinter, cleans the puncture, and puts a small bandage on his paw. Odo takes the loose armrest to the supply room.

LUMA: Your hand is clear.

NIMA: And the waiting room?

SELI: Still waiting.

LUMA: The board is not the queue.

Seli uses paper slips to call patients in order. The room settles.

### Tag

[PAVA REPLACES THE BOARD WITH A HANDWRITTEN SIGN: "PLEASE WAIT HERE."]

ODO: It cannot get stuck.

SELI: It can be ignored.

NIMA: Next?

LUMA: You're done.

[SFX: A CHAIR CREAKS. EVERYONE LOOKS AT IT.]
