# One More Rep for the Camera

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised fitness programs that prescribe one routine to every body and keep adding reps for a dramatic countdown*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Fitness host Kori wants an energetic thirty-minute workout. Trainer Dena needs to offer modifications, while producer Puck keeps extending the set because the camera missed a dramatic finish.

Cast: Kori, a macaque host; Dena, a gorilla trainer; Puck, a capuchin producer; and Vela, a lemur participant.

## Episode: "The Camera Is Not a Coach"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. KORI FACES A ROW OF PARTICIPANTS. A TIMER SHOWS 30:00.]

KORI: Follow me for a full-body workout.

[THE TIMER STARTS. KORI DOES A JUMPING JACK.]

DENA: Warm-up first.

[KORI STOPS MID-JUMP.]

KORI: Right. Please warm up.

[PUCK POINTS TO THE CLOCK.]

PUCK: We are losing the opening energy.

DENA: We are gaining warm muscles.

[VELA STRETCHES. THE REST OF THE GROUP COPIES HER INSTEAD OF KORI.]

VELA: Are we warming up the ankles too?

DENA: Yes. Then we will begin the walking pattern.

KORI: Can the walking pattern have a beat?

[PUCK ADDS MUSIC. KORI STEPS TOO FAR, AND THE CAMERA PAN MISSES VELA. DENA SHORTENS THE STEP.]

DENA: Keep the movement small enough to repeat comfortably.

[THE GROUP MATCHES HER PACE. PUCK WIDENS THE SHOT INSTEAD OF ASKING THEM TO GO FASTER.]

### Act One

[DENA LEADS A SHOULDER ROLL. KORI DEMONSTRATES A FAST VERSION FOR CAMERA.]

KORI: Big movements, everyone.

DENA: Small comfortable range.

[VELA LIFTS HER ARMS TO SHOULDER HEIGHT. KORI LIFTS HIS OVERHEAD.]

PUCK: The wide shot needs everyone doing the same thing.

DENA: Everyone's shoulders are different.

[THE CAMERA PANS. TWO PARTICIPANTS FOLLOW DENA, TWO FOLLOW KORI. PUCK TRIES TO FRAME BOTH.]

KORI: We have a split routine.

DENA: We have people listening to the instructor.

[PUCK CUTS TO THE TIMER. IT SHOWS 27:00.]

PUCK: We need a faster pace.

DENA: We have time to breathe.

[KORI ATTEMPTS A SQUAT WHILE TALKING. HE LOSES HIS COUNT.]

KORI: Was that six or seven?

VELA: You said five twice.

[DENA SHOWS A CHAIR MODIFICATION. KORI SITS ON THE CHAIR, THEN STANDS.]

KORI: That was one.

DENA: That is how counting works.

### Act Two

[THE GROUP DOES A BALANCE EXERCISE. PUCK ADDS A COUNTDOWN GRAPHIC.]

PUCK: Five, four, three...

DENA: No countdown. Hold only while stable.

[VELA STEPS OUT OF THE POSE AND PLANTS BOTH FEET.]

VELA: I am stable here.

KORI: The segment needs a finish.

DENA: The finish is everyone standing safely.

[PUCK REWINDS THE CAMERA FEED. IT SHOWS KORI WOBBLING WHILE TALKING.]

PUCK: The take is usable if you fall toward the mat.

KORI: I am not falling.

DENA: Then keep both feet on the floor.

[THE GROUP MOVES TO A SEATED COOL-DOWN. PUCK ASKS FOR ONE MORE SET OF ARM RAISES.]

VELA: My arms are done.

DENA: Rest them at your sides.

KORI: The camera is still rolling.

VELA: Then it can film me resting.

[PUCK LOWERS THE CAMERA TO SHOW THE WHOLE GROUP. SOME PARTICIPANTS SIT, OTHERS STAND AND SHAKE OUT THEIR HANDS.]

KORI: We have reached the cool-down.

PUCK: The timer says four minutes.

DENA: The timer is not a coach.

[PUCK TURNS OFF THE COUNTDOWN GRAPHIC. THE PARTICIPANTS BREATHE AND LOWER THEIR ARMS.]

[DENA ASKS VELA TO SHOW A MODIFICATION FOR THE NEXT MOVE. VELA STANDS BESIDE THE CHAIR AND PLACES ONE HAND ON ITS BACK.]

VELA: This helps me balance.

KORI: Can I try the same version?

[HE HOLDS THE CHAIR AND LIFTS ONE FOOT A FEW INCHES. HE SETS IT DOWN AND SMILES.]

DENA: Good. Stop when you need to.

### Act Three

[KORI FACES THE CAMERA. THE GROUP SITS IN A CIRCLE.]

KORI: Today's workout had a warm-up, options, and a cool-down.

PUCK: We did not finish the planned count.

DENA: We finished the class.

[VELA DEMONSTRATES THE CHAIR SQUAT. KORI COUNTS EACH REP CORRECTLY.]

KORI: One, two, three.

[ON FOUR, PUCK CUES A CONFETTI BURST. IT LANDS ON THE PARTICIPANTS. DENA BRUSHES IT OFF.]

DENA: That is not part of exercise.

PUCK: It was a finish effect.

VELA: I finished the movement without it.

[THE PARTICIPANTS STAND, STRETCH, AND LEAVE AT THEIR OWN PACE.]

KORI: Should we tell viewers how many calories they used?

DENA: We did not measure that.

PUCK: We can put a number on the screen.

DENA: Do not invent a number.

[PUCK ERASES THE EMPTY CALORIE COUNTER FROM THE GRAPHIC.]

[DENA PUTS THE CHAIRS BACK IN THEIR ROW. VELA TAKES A BOTTLE OF WATER.]

KORI: See you at the next class.

VELA: I will check the routine first.

### Tag

[KORI TRIES TO REMOVE HIS MICROPHONE. PUCK CALLS FOR ONE MORE TAKE.]

KORI: The cool-down is over.

DENA: The camera can pack up now.

[PUCK LOOKS AT THE TIMER. IT IS OFF.]
