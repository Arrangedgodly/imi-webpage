# The Game Show Double-Books a Contestant

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: game shows that let camera graphics and scoring gimmicks overrule the people actually playing*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Tavi, a macaque; contestants Luma, a gibbon, and Peko, an orangutan; scorekeeper Seli, a lemur; producer Nori, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW STAGE - NIGHT

A split-screen shows Luma twice, once from the front and once from the side. The scoreboard awards her two points.

TAVI: Luma has taken the lead.

LUMA: I have not moved.

ACT ONE

INT. STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Nori says the camera split is part of the show's new interactive format. Seli checks the score sheet; the second image should not count as another player. Peko has no points because his camera is still focused on an empty podium. He waves. The monitor remains empty.

TAVI: Peko, are you with us?

PEKO: I am at the podium.

The camera finds him and the scoreboard awards Peko a point for appearing. Luma asks whether standing still is worth more than answering. Nori says the show is about quick reactions. Tavi reads the first question: name a tool that cuts paper. Luma says scissors. The screen awards the answer to her duplicate.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Seli traces the camera feeds. The split-screen was intended for a replay round, but Nori activated it before the game. She shuts off the duplicate image. Luma's score drops by two. Nori asks her to keep the points; the audience has already seen them. Seli says the score sheet is the official record.

[The next question asks which shape has three sides. Peko answers triangle. The camera shows only his empty podium; the score gives the point to Luma.]

Tavi asks the camera operator to show Peko. The operator pans too far and finds Nori behind a monitor. Nori ducks. The score adds a point to the producer. Seli erases it. A second monitor displays the previous total, and Tavi reads that aloud by mistake.

TAVI: We have twenty-four points.

SELI: We have four.

Peko asks for a paper scoreboard. Seli writes each answer by hand. Nori tries to keep the graphics by placing two paper scorecards side by side. The camera frames them as two contestants again. Seli folds one card in half. The double score disappears.

INT. STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

The show moves to a physical round. Contestants must place three rings on numbered pegs. Luma places all three correctly. The camera takes a close-up of her hands, then repeats the shot in a replay window. The scoreboard adds another point for the replay. Seli turns the monitor away from the scoring console. The point disappears.

Peko places two rings and misses the third. He tries again after the buzzer. Nori asks to count the second attempt. Seli says the round is over. Tavi says the answer can be a bonus only if both contestants agree. Luma nods. Peko asks for the ring back and lands it on the peg. The bonus point is recorded once.

INT. STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Tavi asks the camera operator to frame both contestants in one shot. The split-screen button is covered with a paper label. Luma and Peko answer a practice question. Seli records each response on the score sheet. Nori tests the replay window; it opens over the scoreboard, but the points do not change. Tavi thanks the crew for holding the graphic until the round ends. The operator gives a thumbs-up from behind the camera. Seli keeps the paper total beside her microphone.

ACT THREE

INT. GAME SHOW STAGE - NIGHT

The final round is one question each. Nori asks the camera operator to keep one shot per contestant. Seli checks the score sheet and announces a tie. Tavi gives each contestant a bell. They must ring when ready, then answer. Peko rings first and names a season. Luma rings once and names a color. Both answers are correct.

The display starts a double-screen animation. Seli pulls its power lead. The stage lights stay on, but the scoreboard goes dark. Tavi checks the paper totals. The contestants agree they each won a point in the final round. Seli declares a tie. Nori asks whether the audience will accept no single champion. Luma and Peko split the small trophy by taking opposite handles. The trophy stays intact; they carry it together.

TAG

INT. STAGE - AFTER THE SHOW

Seli writes one score per contestant. Nori labels the replay button REPLAY ONLY. Tavi tests the camera; one contestant appears once.

FINAL IMAGE: Luma and Peko carry the single trophy between them while the scoreboard displays two names and the same total.
