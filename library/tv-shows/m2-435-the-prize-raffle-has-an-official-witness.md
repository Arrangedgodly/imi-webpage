# The Prize Raffle Has an Official Witness

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised charity raffles that treat a small drawing as a national event*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; witness Nari, a lemur; ticket holder Pip, a capuchin; caller Vex, a macaque; stagehand Seli, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - EVENING

[A glass bowl, still beaded with rinse water, holds folded raffle tickets. Odo wears white gloves. Nari holds a clipboard.] 

ODO: We have sealed the tickets.

NARI: The lid is open.

[Odo puts the lid on. Pip reaches toward the bowl. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Welcome to the Annual Acorn Raffle, witnessed by the entire town and one official clipboard.

NARI: Every ticket was entered once.

VEX: I entered mine twice.

NARI: It says one ticket per person.

VEX: I used two pens.

[Odo turns the bowl. The tickets settle into a neat pile. He asks Seli to shake it. She does. The bowl's lid pops off and the folded tickets scatter across the studio floor.] 

ODO: We have broadened the draw.

NARI: We have dropped the tickets.

[Pip helps gather them. He puts each one into a separate paper cup. Nari checks the list against the tickets. Vex holds up one with a smudged number.] 

VEX: This could be mine.

NARI: It has a seven. Yours is a three.

VEX: The seven is shaped like a three from this angle.

ACT TWO

[Odo sets up a transparent drum. He inserts the tickets one by one. Seli rotates the handle. The drum stops after one turn.] 

ODO: The drawing is complete.

NARI: We did not draw a ticket.

[Seli has been holding the handle from the wrong side. She reverses it; the drum spins and a single ticket slides into the chute.] 

SELI: Now it is complete.

[Odo reaches for the ticket. Nari asks him to let the witness open it. He hands it to her. The paper is stuck to the chute.]

NARI: It is not coming out.

PIP: The drum has chosen to keep it.

[Vex takes a long wooden spoon from the prize table. He reaches through the chute. The spoon bends the ticket but frees it.] 

NARI: The witness has observed a retrieval.

ODO: Read the number.

NARI: The ink has run.

[The ticket is damp because the glass bowl had been washed. Vex offers his smudged seven.]

VEX: It matches now.

NARI: Your ticket is dry. This one is wet.

[Odo calls a break. The stagehand replaces the paper cup with a towel. Pip fans the ticket. It curls into a tube.] 

PIP: It is turning into a horn.

ODO: A celebratory instrument.

[Nari asks Seli to record each ticket number before the draw. Seli lays out an empty tray and places tickets in it one at a time.]

SELI: We have forty-two entries.

VEX: I have one entry and one alternate interpretation.

NARI: You have one ticket.

[Odo shakes the sealed transparent drum. The tickets tumble visibly. Nari checks the seal, then signs the clipboard.]

NARI: The draw is witnessed.

ODO: The drawing has not happened.

NARI: The process has.

[The bowl lid slips again. Seli catches it with her tail, keeping her hands on the clipboard. The tickets stay inside.]

VEX: The witness has prevented a second scattering.

SELI: I prevented the lid from hitting the floor.

[Odo turns the drum once more. The chute stops over the tray. A ticket sits in the mouth but does not fall.]

PIP: It is thinking.

NARI: It is stuck.

VEX: Is there a prize for patience?

NARI: There is a prize for ticket 19.

ACT THREE

[Nari unfolds the ticket carefully. It bears the number 19. She checks the list.]

NARI: Winner is ticket 19.

[Pip holds up his stub. It says 19. Odo steps toward him, but Vex also steps forward.] 

VEX: I am his official witness.

NARI: You are ticket 3.

VEX: I witnessed his ticket.

[Odo presents the prize: a basket of acorns. Pip takes it. Two acorns roll out through a hole in the bottom. Seli places a tray underneath.] 

PIP: The prize is leaking.

NARI: The basket is handmade.

[The remaining acorns stay in. Pip lifts the basket and uses the loose acorns as rattles. Odo begins the closing announcement.] 

ODO: This year's raffle was fair, transparent, and witnessed.

NARI: The bowl was open, the tickets scattered, and the winner was clear.

VEX: Can I have the two that escaped?

PIP: They are now separate prizes.

TAG

[The camera zooms on a single acorn under the table. Nari points to it.] 

NARI: We have one more witness.

[Odo sets the glass bowl over it.]

ODO: The draw is secure.
