# The Precinct Is Out of Evidence Tape

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedurals, ominous evidence reveals, precinct ensemble scenes, and detectives who call every minor incident a case*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A precinct's detectives investigate the disappearance of its evidence tape while a very ordinary office repair keeps altering the crime scene.

Cast: Detective Luma, a lemur; Detective Fenn, a capuchin; Chief Omi, an orangutan; clerk Nari, a macaque; and building custodian Vex, a gibbon.

## Episode: "The Tape Line"

### Cold Open

[PRECINCT EVIDENCE ROOM. A ROLL OF TAPE IS MISSING.]

LUMA: Nobody leaves until we find it.

VEX: I need to mop the floor.

Luma looks at the camera as if Vex has confessed.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

Chief Omi explains that the evidence tape is used to mark a loose cabinet door in the records room. It has been moved from the supply shelf, and the cabinet is still swinging. Luma and Fenn treat the missing roll as a serious case. Nari enters with an inventory sheet. She says the roll was last seen near the copier.

FENN: Who had access?

NARI: Everyone who has access to the copier.

The detectives question the staff. A patrol monkey says the copier jammed. A desk sergeant says the tape was too sticky for the cabinet. Vex walks through carrying a mop, leaving a clean stripe through the team's floor diagram. Luma blocks him from crossing their chalk line.

VEX: The floor was dirty before the diagram.

### Act Two

Fenn dusts the copier for fingerprints. The copier prints a page with black toner across his gloves. Luma asks whether the marks match anyone. Nari says the copier has been making smudges all week. Omi orders the repair technician to come after the tape is found, then notices the cabinet swings into the hallway and asks them to fix it now.

The detectives trace the tape's possible route through the building. It appears on a supply list, then disappears from a sign-out sheet. Nari notes the sheet was taped to the clipboard with a different roll. Luma pins the list to a board and draws a red string between the copier, cabinet, and mop closet. Fenn suggests they ask Vex. Luma says they have to follow the evidence.

Vex lifts the clipboard to mop beneath it. A strip of tape drops from the back. It is ordinary clear tape, not the missing evidence tape. The team leans closer. Omi tells them not to call it a breakthrough. The camera zooms in anyway.

FENN: We have identified a second tape.

NARI: It is office tape.

The repair technician arrives with a box of hinges. She opens the cabinet and sees the marked door. Omi says the tape is missing, so they cannot indicate where it sticks. The technician presses the door shut, and it stays closed. The tape has been used as a temporary hinge shim. She explains that the cabinet's screw had loosened, so someone wrapped tape around it to stop the rattling.

Luma asks who wrapped the screw. Vex says he did, to stop the noise during calls. He put the roll in the cabinet for safekeeping. The cabinet then shut on it. Fenn turns toward the cabinet, which is now fixed. The crew opens it and the tape rolls out onto the floor, leaving a straight line toward the mop bucket.

Nari replays the security camera footage. The picture shows Vex carrying a mop, but the angle makes the mop handle look like a second suspect. Luma asks for an enhancement. The blurry image becomes larger and less clear. Fenn studies it, then looks at the actual mop leaning beside the door. The handle has a strip of blue tape; the missing roll is red. He points out the difference, and Nari labels the footage 'custodian, routine cleaning.'

### Act Three

Omi decides the roll should be returned to the supply shelf, while the cabinet gets a proper hinge repair. Nari updates the inventory. Luma removes the red string from the board. Fenn takes a final photograph of the evidence tape beside the repaired cabinet. The detectives say the case is closed.

The copier prints another smeared page. Everyone turns at once. Nari lifts the sheet: it is a receipt for the new hinge. Omi tells them to log the repair, not launch another investigation. Luma places the evidence tape on the shelf, then checks the cabinet door. It remains shut.

FENN: What about the fingerprints?

NARI: That is toner.

FENN: We have solved a case and identified an office supply.

LUMA: Two separate achievements.

### Tag

[PRECINCT HALLWAY.]

Vex mops away the red string diagram's chalk marks. Luma starts to step around the clean stripe, then walks right through it. She leaves a wet footprint beside the repaired cabinet.

VEX: Should I mark the scene?

LUMA: No. Just mop it.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
