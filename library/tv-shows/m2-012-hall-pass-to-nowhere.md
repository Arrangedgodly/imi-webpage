# Hall Pass to Nowhere

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: teen school drama*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: At a high school built around a courtyard fig tree, two students race to save the school's quiet lunch spot from a well-meaning renovation plan.

Cast: Kira, a sharp macaque student council president; Mal, a capuchin who sketches instead of speaking in assemblies; Niko, a gibbon athlete who knows every roof route; Ms. Vale, a patient baboon counselor; and Principal Sedge, who wants the school to look good for a donor visit.

## Episode: "The Last Table Under the Fig"

### Cold open

EXT. SCHOOL COURTYARD, MORNING

A worker plants an orange cone beneath the old fig tree. Kira pulls it out. Niko puts it back. The bell rings. Students freeze, waiting for someone to make the first move.

KIRA: You cannot cone off lunch.

NIKO: I can cone off anything with a cone.

A fig drops onto the cone and knocks it over.

NIKO: The tree has chosen a side.

KIRA: The tree was here first.

### Act one

Principal Sedge announces a glass study pavilion for the courtyard. A donor tour is scheduled for Friday. Kira likes the idea of more places to study but sees that the plan covers the fig's roots and blocks the path to the library. Mal has sketched the courtyard from memory, including the route students with mobility aids use. At the student council table, Niko arrives with a glossy rival proposal for a climbing wall.

NIKO: I thought we agreed on a pavilion.

KIRA: We agreed on a place to study.

NIKO: This is a place to climb.

KIRA: It is a wall.

NIKO: Walls can be inspirational.

MAL: This one blocks the path.

NIKO: You drew that in red.

MAL: The path is red on the plan.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: The donor likes clean lines.

KIRA: Students need a clear path.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: The path is currently there.

NIKO: On the other side of the pavilion.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: The model does not show that.

MAL: The model does not show people.

The principal lifts the model. A tiny fig tree has been glued over a plastic patch of paving. Its roots are painted blue.

KIRA: Why are the roots blue?

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: It was the color available.

NIKO: I thought that was a river.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: It's a diagram.

### Act two

Kira surveys students in the lunch courtyard. One student reads under the fig because the cafeteria is too loud. Two dancers need the open paving. Niko's track team stretches by the wall. Mal silently hands Kira his drawing. The microphone at the assembly keeps cutting out, so she invites everyone to inspect the plan instead of giving a speech.

MS. VALE: The vote is tied.

KIRA: Count again.

MS. VALE: It is still tied.

NIKO: The climbing wall has a fan club.

KIRA: How many members?

NIKO: Four, including me.

MAL: I counted the whole courtyard.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: The donor arrives in an hour.

KIRA: Then let's show her the route.

A cart of library books rolls toward the pavilion stakes. Its wheels catch. Niko catches the handle before the cart tips. Mal points to the narrow gap on the drawing, then to the real path.

NIKO: That gap is smaller in person.

KIRA: The path is the same size.

NIKO: I meant the model.

MS. VALE: The model is still not a person.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: The donor asked for a surprise.

KIRA: Here's one. Students use the courtyard.

A donor steps into the yard. Sedge whispers that the students should follow the script. Niko takes the script, turns it over, and uses the blank back to draw a wider path.

NIKO: I would like to withdraw my wall.

KIRA: Why?

NIKO: It would make the path worse.

KIRA: That's a terrible rivalry twist.

NIKO: I can still beat you at relay.

MAL: Only if the path stays open.

### Act three

The donor is Sedge's aunt, who planted the fig tree when she attended the school. She wanted a covered study space but never asked for the tree to be removed. The letter explaining that request was in Sedge's folder the whole time. He admits he lost it among the donor materials and apologizes to the students.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: The letter says, "Leave the roots room."

KIRA: We can do that.

DONOR: I can fund a smaller canopy and a repaired bench.

NIKO: Does it have to be glass?

DONOR: It can be shade cloth.

MAL: The library path stays clear.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: Agreed.

The crew moves the cones. Students sign up for a rotating cleanup schedule. Mal's drawing becomes the new plan. The quiet reader claims the bench. The dancers mark their practice space with chalk. Niko's team stretches without climbing anything.

KIRA: You could still do the relay.

NIKO: I was building tension.

MS. VALE: You were holding a cone.

The bell rings. Nobody freezes. They sit, eat, read, and talk beneath the fig.

### Tag

At lunch, Sedge opens his folder again. A second letter falls out. It is a grocery list from his aunt.

PRINCIPAL SEDGE: Another secret.

KIRA: It says figs, tea, and tape.

NIKO: A cliffhanger with supplies.

MAL: We need tape for the drawing.

The principal hands it over. The camera holds on the orange cone, now holding down the edge of the plan.
