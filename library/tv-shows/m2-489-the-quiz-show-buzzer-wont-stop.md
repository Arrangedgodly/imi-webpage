# The Quiz Show Buzzer Won't Stop

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: quiz shows that trust dramatic buzzers and scoreboards more than contestants' actual answers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Sola, an orangutan; contestants Riff, a macaque, and Tena, a capuchin; judge Poro, a gibbon; stage manager Lemi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. QUIZ STAGE - NIGHT

The final question appears on the board: NAME A TOOL USED TO TIGHTEN A BOLT. Riff presses his buzzer. It sticks and sounds continuously.

SOLA: That is one very confident answer.

RIFF: I have not answered yet.

ACT ONE

INT. QUIZ STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Lemi unplugs Riff's podium. The buzzer continues from the stage speakers. Poro checks the score display. It has awarded Riff a point for every second the sound lasts. Tena's score remains zero despite her correct answer to the previous question.

TENA: The answer was a wrench.

SOLA: We will accept that once the buzzer is finished.

Poro finds the sound cue stuck on the control panel. He presses MUTE. The buzzer stops, and the audience applauds. The scoreboard adds a point to Tena for the applause. Riff asks whether applause is a tool. Sola says it is not in this category.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Lemi replaces the buzzer button. The new one is too sensitive; a camera cable brushes it and triggers a sharp tone.

Poro asks the floor crew to stop walking near the podiums. The crew freezes in place. A stage light swings on its motor and touches the button, triggering another point. Sola asks whether the light has a contestant number. Lemi secures the light arm, and the buzzer stays silent while the judges reset the score. Poro tests each contestant button with a single press. Both lights turn on once, then go dark. Sola reads the next question only after the scoreboard stops counting. The crew rolls the light stand farther from the podiums. Riff gives the button one final tap; the beep ends cleanly. Poro verifies the score by hand before Sola announces the winner. Sola holds the next question card: WHICH ANIMAL BUILDS A DAM? Riff says beaver. The buzzer sounds again, but this time it comes from Tena's station. The board marks her wrong before she speaks.

Poro checks the wiring and finds the contestant buttons reversed. He swaps the cables. Now Riff's button activates Tena's light. Lemi places a colored sticker on each podium. Sola asks the contestants to use their names instead of the lights. She reads a question about a tool for tightening a bolt. Tena answers "wrench." The scoreboard awards it to Riff.

RIFF: I am pleased to receive her point.

Lemi moves the score display so the judge can see the cable labels. Poro traces each line to the correct podium. He marks the fixed route with tape. The camera operator follows the tape across the floor, producing a close-up of Lemi's shoes instead of the players. Sola asks the director for a wide shot. The board shows the real scores: Riff 2, Tena 4.

INT. QUIZ STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

The control booth tests the final buzzer. Sola asks a practice question. Neither contestant presses. The buzzer sounds from the empty podium. Poro looks underneath it and finds a rubber ball wedged against the button. The ball rolls free. Lemi puts it in the prop bin, which has a sign reading BALLS AND OTHER ANSWERS.

ACT THREE

INT. QUIZ STAGE - NIGHT

The final question is worth one point. Sola tells the contestants to speak their answers if their buttons fail. She asks what tool tightens a bolt. Tena presses her button once. The light comes on. The buzzer gives a short beep and stops. Tena answers wrench. The display credits the answer correctly.

Riff's podium lights up too. He does not press it. The judge checks the buttons; the scoreboard remains still. Sola gives Riff the next question: what tool measures a board? He says ruler. The answer is correct. The lights stay on only for the active contestant. Tena wins by one point.

Sola reaches for the trophy. The buzzer chirps. Everyone freezes. Poro points to the score display, which has not changed. Lemi pulls the trophy's ribbon out from under the podium button. The chirp stops. Sola hands Tena the trophy. Tena sets it on the floor before the ribbon can snag again.

TAG

INT. STAGE - AFTER THE SHOW

Riff tests his button once. It makes one clean beep. Poro turns off the scoreboard. Lemi labels the fixed cable ends with contestant names rather than colors.

FINAL IMAGE: Tena leaves carrying the trophy, while the silent buzzer button sits in the prop bin beside the rubber ball.
