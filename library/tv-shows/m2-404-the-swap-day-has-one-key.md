# The Swap Day Has One Key

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-swap reality television, confessional interviews, reveal shots, and producers who forget practical logistics*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: Two families swap treehouses for a day, but the production crew brings only one key and keeps filming the locked door as a dramatic reveal.

Cast: Host Peli, a gibbon; home A owner Noro, an orangutan; home B owner Savi, a macaque; camera operator Tavi, a capuchin; producer Kemi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. TWO TREEHOUSES - MORNING

Peli stands between two rope bridges. Noro and Savi each hold out a hand.

PELI: Today, they swap homes.

KEMI: We have one key.

SAVI: Which home does it open?

Kemi tries it. It opens neither.

ACT ONE

Noro explains that his treehouse has a sliding panel latch. Savi says her home has a wooden key lock. The show packed the key for Savi's door but left it in the production van. Kemi asks Tavi to film a suspenseful close-up of the key in Peli's hand.

TAVI: It is not the right key.

PELI: It can still be a reveal.

Noro slides his panel open. Savi enters first. The camera follows her through the doorway, but the crew blocks the path with lights and a rolling monitor. Noro asks them to clear his threshold. They move one stand; the cable remains across the bridge.

ACT TWO

Savi explores Noro's treehouse. She finds a low sleeping platform, a climbing loop, and a shelf organized by size. Kemi asks for a confessional. Savi says the shelf is useful but she cannot reach the highest slot. Noro says he can lower the shelf before the swap begins.

SAVI: The show says the homes are ready.

NORO: The shelf was set for me.

Kemi films Noro lowering the shelf. Peli asks if this counts as a transformation. Noro says it is one shelf moving six inches. Peli calls it "a redesigned vertical storage experience."

Savi tries the climbing loop. It swings into the camera tripod. Tavi catches the tripod. Noro holds the loop steady while Savi finishes the move. The crew shifts the camera outside the loop's path.

ACT TWO-B

Noro arrives at Savi's house. The key is still in the van. Kemi tells the camera that the contestants face an unexpected barrier. Savi points to the back entrance, which opens with a latch from outside. Noro walks through it. Kemi asks for a reaction shot, but he is already inside.

NORO: This is a door.

SAVI: The front lock is for when we leave.

Noro checks the sleeping platform and the storage shelf. He likes the view but notices the fruit basket is hung high. Savi offers him a hook to lower it. Kemi asks them to wait for the key reveal. They do not need the key; the back latch is the normal entrance.

Tavi leaves to fetch the key from the van. The camera tracks him across the bridge. He returns with a ring containing six keys. They test them one by one. The fourth key opens the front door. Peli asks which one viewers saw earlier.

TAVI: None. That was a spare prop key.

Kemi asks both owners to stand in their new homes for a final reveal. Noro points out that the shelf has been lowered and the fruit hook moved, so the shots no longer match the "before" photos. Tavi sets the camera to split-screen. One side shows the old shelf; the other shows the new one. The split places the doors on opposite edges and makes it look as if both homes share a wall.

SAVI: We do not share a wall.

NORO: We share a bridge.

KEMI: Can you say that with a reaction?

Savi lifts the fruit basket from the new lower hook and checks that the hook holds. Noro tests the lowered shelf by reaching the top slot. He can reach it without climbing. Both nod. Kemi asks for one last confessional from each.

SAVI: The hook is useful.

NORO: The shelf is easier to reach.

Tavi gets a continuous wide shot of the bridge and each separate doorway. Kemi removes the split-screen graphic. The producers carry the lights off the bridge so the owners can cross safely.

ACT THREE

At the end of the day, Noro and Savi return to their own homes. Savi says she will use the lower shelf idea. Noro says he will add a hook for the fruit basket. Kemi asks them to describe the swap in one word.

SAVI: Helpful.

NORO: Brief.

PELI: The homes have been exchanged and returned.

Tavi hands the correct key ring to Savi and takes the prop key from Peli. Kemi collects the lights and cables. Both owners open their own doors with the usual latch.

TAG

Kemi locks the production case. The prop key does not fit. Tavi opens the case with its combination latch.

KEMI: We should label the keys.

FINAL IMAGE: The owners cross their own bridges as the crew packs the unused prop key into a box marked "NOT A HOUSE KEY."
