# The Ghost in the Sound Boom

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation reality shows that turn ordinary noises into evidence*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A ghost-hunting crew searches an empty theater for a spirit while the sound operator keeps accidentally haunting them with his own equipment.

Cast: Host Varo, a baboon; investigator Leni, a macaque; skeptic Omi, a capuchin; sound operator Fenn, a gibbon; theater caretaker Tavi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. OLD THEATER - NIGHT

Varo holds a recorder in the dark. A boom microphone swings above him.

VARO: We are alone in this theater.

FENN: I'm above you.

Varo jumps. The boom sweeps through the camera frame.

OMI: One voice. One operator. No ghosts.

ACT ONE

Leni stands by the curtain with a temperature reader. Tavi unlocks the exit doors and says the theater is empty except for the crew. Varo asks her to describe the legend.

TAVI: A stagehand heard footsteps after everyone had gone home.

VARO: The spirit still walks?

TAVI: The building has a loose floorboard.

Omi taps it. It creaks. Varo tells the camera the theater has answered them. Fenn moves the microphone away, and the creak disappears.

LENI: We can test the board with the microphone off.

VARO: The microphone is how we hear.

OMI: Our ears also work.

They step on the board together. It creaks. Varo asks if anyone else heard it. Everyone raises a hand.

ACT TWO

The crew enters a backstage corridor. Varo switches on an electromagnetic detector. It beeps near a wall outlet. Fenn plugs in the camera monitor; the beeping stops. Varo tells the camera the presence may be hiding.

OMI: It was a monitor.

VARO: A monitor with a history.

Tavi explains that a stage light hums when its dimmer is on. Fenn switches the light off. The hum disappears. Varo asks Leni to conduct a spirit-box session. She asks for a sign. The boom microphone dips down and brushes Varo's hood.

VARO: It touched me.

FENN: The boom has a loose hinge.

VARO: It has chosen me.

Omi holds the boom's base while Fenn tightens the hinge. The microphone stops drooping. Leni asks the crew to remain silent for one minute. The camera operator shuffles a foot. A chair squeaks.

VARO: We have a response.

TAVI: That's the chair I just moved.

Varo replays the audio. A faint word seems to say "stage." Tavi says she had just told them where the stage is. Omi compares the recording with the live room and hears the same syllable when Fenn says "stand here."

ACT TWO-B

Varo leads the group onto the stage. A spotlight clicks on. Everyone turns toward the empty seats. Tavi says the light is triggered by a timer for the next morning's rehearsal. Varo asks whether a ghost could set a timer.

TAVI: A technician set it yesterday.

VARO: Could a ghost be a technician?

OMI: Then the ghost has a shift schedule.

Fenn checks the boom monitor. A red meter jumps every time someone speaks. Varo goes quiet. The meter still jumps as Fenn whispers to himself. Varo catches the sound operator's reflection in a dark panel and thinks he sees a figure behind them.

VARO: There, in the glass!

FENN: That is me.

LENI: We can stand where you are and see it.

She steps beside Fenn. The reflection shows both of them. Omi uses a flashlight to check behind the panel; there is only a narrow equipment rack. A dangling headset sways from the rack as Fenn brushes it.

VARO: The headset is moving.

FENN: I brushed it.

VARO: The evidence is beginning to speak.

TAVI: It would help if you stopped asking the evidence leading questions.

ACT THREE

Leni asks for one final quiet recording. Fenn places the boom on a stand, locks its hinge and steps away. The crew waits. The theater settles. A single footstep echoes from the corridor.

VARO: We have a sound.

TAVI: That was my key ring. I hung it on the door.

The keys tap the handle again as the air vent starts. Omi records the second tap. Fenn turns off the vent; the keys stop moving. Leni writes the result on her field slate: loose board, timed spotlight, vent, key ring, boom hinge. Varo asks if he can call it a haunting anyway.

LENI: Call it a theater with a lot of equipment.

VARO: Our investigation found a sound boom.

OMI: And no ghost.

Varo nods to camera and announces the investigation complete. Tavi turns off the stage lights and locks the theater. No one hears a final mysterious noise.

TAG

The credits roll over the dark stage. The boom microphone slowly dips into frame. Fenn reaches in and tightens the hinge without turning on a light.

FINAL IMAGE: The microphone stays upright while the crew exits together, visible in the glass as ordinary reflections.
