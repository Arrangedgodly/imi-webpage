# The Secret Life of a Doorstop

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nature documentary narration applied to a common household object's routine work*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A documentary crew follows a wooden doorstop through one day of keeping a community hall door open.

Cast: Narrator Pava, a gibbon; caretaker Odo, a baboon; camera operator Miri, a capuchin; volunteer Nara, a macaque.

Episode: "Wedge of the Wild"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MORNING

Odo places a wooden wedge under the door.

PAVA (V.O.): At dawn, the wedge takes its position.

MIRI: It is holding the door.

NARA: The cart is coming through.

The door stays open as the cart rolls by.

ACT ONE

Pava narrates the wedge's territory: a patch of floor between the door and the wall. Odo says the wedge should stay close to the hinge, where it has leverage. Miri films it from floor level. Nara steps around it carrying a stack of chairs.

PAVA: A large migration passes nearby.

NARA: It is chair delivery.

The hall's air vent turns on. The door pushes against the wedge. It shifts slightly. Odo moves it closer to the hinge and tests the door. The wedge holds.

Miri asks whether the wedge can survive a heavier door. Odo says it has never been tested on the storage door. Nara says the storage door has a different shape.

ACT TWO

The crew follows the wedge to the storage room. Odo tries to place it under the door, but the gap is too narrow. The wedge does not fit. Pava calls this a change in habitat. Nara says it needs a different stop.

Odo returns the wedge to the hall door. A volunteer comes through with a tray. The wedge holds the door as she passes. She thanks Odo. Pava calls the exchange a mutual benefit.

The camera crew asks Odo to leave the hall for a dramatic tracking shot. He removes the wedge and carries it away. The door closes, cutting off the shot. Nara reopens the door. The crew resets.

PAVA: The wedge has vanished from its territory.

ODO: I put it in my pocket.

Miri asks if that counts as migration. Odo says it counts as storage.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

A gust comes through the open window. The door rattles. Odo's wedge slides again. Nara closes the window halfway. The door steadies. The wedge is repositioned. Pava says it is adapting to the changing conditions.

Miri asks for a close shot of the wedge's surface. The wood is worn smooth on one side. Odo says it has been used for years. Pava calls the grooves a record of its labor.

Nara says they should label the wedge so it does not get thrown away. Odo writes "DOORSTOP" on a tag. Miri films the label as if it were a discovery.

ACT TWO-C

Pava wants to know how many doors the wedge has held open. Odo estimates dozens, but has not kept a count. Miri suggests interviewing people who used the hall. Nara calls two volunteers. One remembers pushing a cart through while the door was held. Another says the wedge kept the door from slamming during a meeting.

Pava asks if the wedge has ever failed. Odo says it slid once on the polished floor. Nara says he moved it closer to the hinge and it held. The documentary crew stages a reenactment. Odo places the wedge too far away; the door pushes it aside. Nara corrects the placement. The wedge holds again.

Miri asks if the wedge should be upgraded. Odo says a rubber pad might help on the slick floor. Nara adds one to the bottom, then tests the door. It no longer slides. Pava calls the improvement a late-season adaptation. Odo says it is a small piece of rubber. The door stays open while the crew moves equipment out.

Before the camera leaves, Nara asks Odo to write the wedge's new location on the drawer label. He writes "doorstop" in large letters. The label points to the supply drawer, not to the wedge itself. Miri films the drawer closing. Pava asks if this counts as an ending. Odo says the wedge will be used again tomorrow, so the story continues. Nara puts the spare rubber pad in the drawer beside it.

ACT THREE

The last cart exits. Odo removes the wedge and places it in the supply drawer. The door closes quietly. The hall is empty.

PAVA: After a full day of effort, the wedge returns to rest.

NARA: It goes in the drawer.

TAG

Miri tries to open the drawer to get a marker. The wedge is holding it shut from inside.

ODO: The creature has found a new habitat.
