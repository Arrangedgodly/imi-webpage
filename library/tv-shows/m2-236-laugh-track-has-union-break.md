# The Laugh Track Has a Union Break

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sitcom production conventions that use canned audience reactions to instruct viewers when a joke has landed*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A sitcom scene stops every few lines because the laugh-track operator keeps taking breaks, forcing the actors to discover whether the jokes work in silence.

Cast: Actor Miri, a macaque; actor Sefu, a gibbon; director Kori, a capuchin; and laugh-track operator Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "The Empty Sofa"

COLD OPEN

INT. SITCOM LIVING ROOM SET - DAY

Miri sits on an empty sofa. Sefu enters holding one cushion.

SEFU: The sofa is full.

MIRI: I see one cushion.

[A LAUGH TRACK PLAYS.]

KORI: Cut. Odo, that was the setup.

ODO: I thought it was the line.

ACT ONE

Kori reviews the script. Odo sits by a soundboard with buttons marked LAUGH, APPLAUSE, and AWKWARD SILENCE.

KORI: The laugh should follow the punchline.

SEFU: The punchline is that the sofa is empty.

MIRI: The audience can see that.

ODO: I can give them a small laugh.

[HE PRESSES THE BUTTON. A HUGE ROAR PLAYS.]

KORI: That was not small.

ODO: The label says LAUGH.

KORI: The label does not say how much.

[THE CREW MOVES THE SOUND BOARD CLOSER TO THE STAGE. IT BLOCKS THE SOFA.]

ACT TWO

INT. SITCOM LIVING ROOM SET - LATER

Miri tries the line without stopping. Sefu waits for the laugh, but none comes.

MIRI: The sofa is full.

SEFU: There is no one sitting here.

[THEY LOOK AT THE EMPTY CUSHION.]

SEFU: Maybe it is full of air.

KORI: That is the joke.

ODO: I missed it. I was changing channels.

MIRI: We can try it again.

KORI: We cannot repeat the punchline for the audience.

SEFU: We just repeated it for the sound operator.

[ODO PRESSES THE AWKWARD SILENCE BUTTON. IT PLAYS A LONG, UNCOMFORTABLE PAUSE.]

KORI: That is the next beat.

ACT TWO-B

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTERNOON

Odo watches the scene on a monitor. Kori enters with the script. Miri and Sefu reset the sofa.

KORI: We will run it without canned reactions.

ODO: The show needs a laugh track.

KORI: The actors need a clean take.

[THE ACTORS REPEAT THE SOFA SCENE. A CREW MEMBER LAUGHS OFF CAMERA AT THE CUSHION FALLING.]

ODO: That laugh was not on my board.

KORI: It was real.

ODO: It came from the set.

SEFU: It came from the joke.

[ODO MUTES THE LAUGH TRACK. THE CREW MEMBER LAUGHS AGAIN WHEN MIRI SITS ON THE CUSHION.]

MIRI: I think that worked.

ACT TWO-C

INT. SITCOM LIVING ROOM SET - LATE AFTERNOON

Odo wheels in a small board of reaction buttons. Kori checks the scene list. Miri and Sefu reset the cushion.

ODO: We have laugh, big laugh, applause, and the sound of one person coughing.

KORI: We need none of those until after a line lands.

SEFU: What happens if the audience doesn't laugh?

ODO: I can press the pause button.

[HE PRESSES PAUSE. EVERYONE FREEZES.]

MIRI: The audience cannot see the button.

KORI: The scene can continue.

[ODO PRESSES PLAY. THE SET RESUMES. THE CUSHION SLIPS OFF THE SOFA AGAIN.]

SEFU: It is full of air.

[NO LAUGH TRACK PLAYS. THE CREW STAYS QUIET.]

MIRI: That was not funny.

SEFU: It was a little funny.

ODO: The board has a small laugh setting.

KORI: The board is off.

ODO: The small laugh is me.

[ODO CHUCKLES. THE ACTORS LOOK AT HIM.]

KORI: That is enough. Keep it human.

ACT TWO-D

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - EVENING

Kori listens to a clean take. Odo places his hands in his lap. Miri and Sefu perform the sofa scene again.

MIRI: The sofa is full.

SEFU: Of what?

MIRI: Cushions.

[SHE LIFTS THE CUSHION. A SMALL PILLOW FALLS OUT. THE CREW LAUGHS.]

ODO: I heard that from the hallway.

KORI: No button needed.

ODO: I can still take a break.

[THE SET'S TABLE LAMP TILTS. ODO CATCHES IT BEFORE IT FALLS.]

SEFU: That was funny.

MIRI: The lamp nearly landed on the sofa.

KORI: Keep the moment, not the sound effect.

ODO: Should I leave the board muted?

KORI: Yes. The audience can decide.

SEFU: What if nobody laughs?

MIRI: Then we play the scene and move on.

ODO: I can do that.

ACT THREE

INT. SITCOM LIVING ROOM SET - FINAL TAKE

The actors play the scene. The cushion slides off the sofa. Odo waits. The audience laughs softly.

KORI: Keep rolling.

SEFU: The sofa is full.

MIRI: Of what?

SEFU: Audience expectations.

[THE ACTUAL AUDIENCE LAUGHS. ODO DOES NOT PRESS ANY BUTTON.]

TAG

Odo presses the laugh button by mistake. The track plays after the actors have left.

KORI: The joke is over.

ODO: The laugh is still going.
