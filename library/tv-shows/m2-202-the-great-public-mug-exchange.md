# The Great Public Mug Exchange

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: public-access craft show convention, where a host repaints a mug that its owner uses to water seedlings*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A crafts host plans a decorative makeover for a chipped mug, while the owner keeps using it for practical garden work.

Cast: Host Piri, a capuchin; artist Nara, a gibbon; owner Kito, a macaque; and helper Sela, a lemur.

Episode: "A Cup of Color"

COLD OPEN

INT. PUBLIC ACCESS STUDIO, DAY

Piri holds up a plain mug. Kito enters with a tray of seedlings.

PIRI: Today we add color.

KITO: I use that for water.

He fills it and waters a seedling. Piri's paintbrush splashes.

ACT ONE

Nara presents paints and stencils. Kito says the mug needs to be easy to rinse. Piri suggests a delicate flower pattern. Sela asks whether paint can go inside the cup. Nara says no. They mark the inside with tape and paint a band around the outside.

Kito sets the mug down. A seedling leans over it, casting a leaf shadow across the wet paint. Piri calls it an accidental design. Nara says they should let the coat dry before touching it. Kito needs the mug, so Sela brings a second cup.

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO GARDEN, LATER

The mug is dry. Piri unveils it under a spotlight. Kito tries to lift it, but the handle has been painted too thick. Nara sands the handle lightly and smooths the edge. Piri calls the repair a surprise texture moment.

Kito uses the mug to water a tray. A drop runs down the side, leaving a faint streak. Nara wipes it off and explains the paint needs a clear protective coat. Piri asks if the mug can be displayed before the coat dries. Kito says the seedlings cannot wait. He uses another cup while the mug cures.

The crew arranges the mug beside a flower. The flower tilts toward the window. Sela rotates the shelf to give it light. The camera loses the mug behind the plant. Piri wants the flower moved. Kito says it needs the window.

ACT TWO-B

INT. PUBLIC ACCESS STUDIO, AFTERNOON

The crew films a close-up of the mug's repair. Piri asks Kito to demonstrate how he uses it. Kito pours water, but the mug's painted handle is still warm from the protective coat. Nara checks the finish and asks him to wait another hour. Piri says the program is only thirty minutes long. Sela checks the schedule and finds a gardening segment after theirs.

PIRI: We can borrow an hour from the garden show.

KITO: The seedlings cannot wait an hour.

Nara suggests they use a clean watering can for the seedlings while the mug cures. Kito agrees, but the show has no spare can. Piri looks at the decorated props. One is a plastic pitcher. Sela says it is not food-safe but can carry water to plants. They fill it and water the trays.

The camera follows Kito's careful pour. Piri adds narration about the mug's new color story. Kito says the paint should stay on the outside and the inside should remain plain. Nara adds that the protective coat must fully dry before it is used. Piri asks if they can add glitter. Nara says no; loose glitter could wash into the soil.

PIRI: We have a no-glitter clause.

Sela tapes the care instructions to the wall. A fan blows the paper away. Kito clips it to the mug's handle. It stays in place, and the crew gets a clear shot of the completed project.

ACT TWO-C

INT. STUDIO GARDEN, EVENING

The host asks Kito to explain the mug's chipped rim. Kito says he dropped it while carrying water to the seedlings. Nara sands the rough edge so it will not catch on cloth. Piri suggests painting over the chip. Kito wants the repair to remain visible so he remembers not to use the mug for drinking.

They compare the repaired mug with the plain spare. The spare pours faster but has no handle. Kito likes the repaired mug because the handle keeps his fingers dry. Sela adds a small label to the bottom: "For watering only." Piri asks whether the label counts as a design element. Nara says it is a useful note.

ACT THREE



The mug is finished and returned to Kito. He uses it to water the seedlings. The paint stays intact. Piri asks if he will display the mug. Kito says it is already in use. Nara notes that the cup is not food-safe for drinking because of the outside paint, but it is fine for garden water.

TAG

The host invites viewers to repaint their own household items. Sela reads the instruction card: test the surface and let it dry fully. Kito waters the plants with the mug. Piri asks if they can have it back for a close-up. Kito turns it around. The inside is plain.

PIRI: A clean finish.

KITO: A clean cup.
