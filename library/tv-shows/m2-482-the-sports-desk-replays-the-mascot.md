# The Sports Desk Replays the Mascot

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: postgame highlight shows that replay the most dramatic clip even when it has nothing to do with the game*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: anchor Varo, a macaque; analyst Tiko, a gibbon; producer Nemi, an orangutan; editor Pala, a lemur; mascot Bex, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SPORTS STUDIO - NIGHT

A screen reads FINAL SCORE: 3-2. The anchor points to the highlights monitor. It shows the team's mascot slipping on a damp towel before the game.

VARO: A stunning finish tonight.

TIKO: That was the mascot.

[The screen replays the slip in slow motion. The studio audience gasps, then laughs.]

ACT ONE

INT. SPORTS STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Producer Nemi says the network's goal highlight has corrupted. The only saved clip is the mascot's tumble. Analyst Tiko wants to discuss the last goal from memory. The anchor keeps asking for the replay.

VARO: We need a visual for the turning point.

TIKO: The turning point was a shot into the net.

Nemi tells editor Pala to find the file. Pala searches the archive and finds a clip labeled GOAL, but it shows Bex waving at the crowd. The file contains a slow-mo shot of the mascot and no goals.

[Bex enters the studio in costume. The camera operator frames the oversized head. Bex waves. The audience cheers.]

ACT TWO

INT. EDITING BAY - LATER

Pala finds the actual goal footage on a memory card. It begins with the scoreboard, then the shot, then the ball crossing the line. The producer wants a suspense build. Tiko asks to see the full play. Nemi clips the first two seconds to fit the segment. The clip now begins after the ball has already entered the goal.

TIKO: The goal has happened before the replay starts.

VARO: It is a very decisive shot.

Pala restores the beginning. The shot is clear, but the crowd blocks part of the camera. Nemi overlays the score graphic on the crowd, covering the ball. She moves the graphic. The ball disappears behind the analyst's name banner.

[Bex walks behind the monitor, waving. The control room camera automatically switches to the mascot feed. The studio audience applauds.]

INT. EDITING BAY - LATER

Pala builds a two-minute package from the available footage. The first shot is the mascot entering the court. The second is the mascot tumbling. The third is the actual goal, framed wide enough to include the player and the net. Nemi asks for slow motion on the shot. Tiko says it needs the pass that led to it. Pala adds the pass and trims the mascot clip to a short transition.

VARO: The replay has a beginning, middle, and end.

TIKO: And the score.

The control room plays it. The goal is clear, and Bex appears for one quick wave between the clips. The mascot's tumble is removed. Bex asks whether the fall is being shown. Nemi says no. Bex nods and asks if the damp towel has been picked up. The floor manager carries it away.

INT. SPORTS STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

The show goes live. Varo introduces the score and Tiko explains the final play. Pala rolls the clip once. The ball crosses the line. Bex enters the edge of the frame only after the players have left, waving at a child in the stands. The studio audience cheers. Nemi asks whether the mascot should return for the closing shot. Tiko says the game is the story; the mascot can wave during the sign-off. The floor director switches Bex's microphone off before the camera finds the players again.

ACT THREE

INT. SPORTS STUDIO - LATER

Nemi tells the crew to lock the goal camera and turn off automatic switching. Pala plays the uncut shot. The ball crosses the goal line. Tiko describes the defender's mistake and the attacker's quick pass. Varo summarizes the score. Bex sits on the bench off-camera and removes the mascot head to cool down.

VARO: That is the play that decided it.

[The mascot head rolls into frame. The camera does not switch. Pala holds the shot while Bex retrieves it. The audience laughs, and Nemi tells the control booth not to replay the tumble again.]

TIKO: We can mention the mascot without making it the match.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE BROADCAST

Pala labels the goal file correctly. Varo asks for a final replay of the score. The monitor starts showing Bex's wave again. Nemi switches it off. Tiko hands Bex a towel and asks if the mascot is okay. Bex nods and points to the damp towel that caused the slip.

FINAL IMAGE: The monitor displays the final score beside a still frame of the goal, while Bex carefully steps over the towel on the studio floor.
