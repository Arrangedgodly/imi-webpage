# The Advice Sofa Has Two Sides

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime advice television, competing expert opinions, dramatic reenactments, and studio audience polls*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: Two television advice hosts give opposite solutions to a caller's shared-sofa dispute, while the caller reveals the sofa has one movable cushion.

Cast: Host Pava, a macaque; host Odo, a baboon; caller Reni, a gibbon; audience guide Tavi, a capuchin; stage manager Nemi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. DAYTIME ADVICE STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Pava and Odo sit at opposite ends of a small sofa. Reni appears on the monitor.

RENI: My roommate always takes the sunny side of our sofa.

PAVA: Set a boundary.

ODO: Move the sofa.

RENI: It has wheels.

ACT ONE

Reni says she and her roommate share a reading room. The sofa sits by a window. Her roommate takes the sunny cushion each morning and leaves Reni in the shade. Pava says to ask for a schedule. Odo says to rotate the sofa every day. The studio audience votes for "schedule."

ODO: The audience has not seen the room.

PAVA: It is still an audience.

Tavi reads a viewer message suggesting they flip the cushion. Nemi brings a sofa cushion onto the stage. It is reversible, with a yellow side and a blue side. Pava says the color might establish ownership. Odo says it will not change the sunlight.

ACT TWO

The hosts stage a reenactment. Pava plays Reni's roommate and slides onto the sunny cushion. Odo plays Reni, then tries to move the sofa. The wheels roll; the sofa turns sideways and blocks the set entrance. Nemi stops it before it reaches a camera cable.

NEMI: We will not move the sofa while anyone is sitting on it.

PAVA: That is a useful note.

Odo asks Reni what happens if she arrives first. Reni says her roommate is already there, reading. Pava suggests that she bring her own chair. Reni says there is room for one sofa and two floor cushions. Odo says she can use the floor cushion.

RENI: I would like to sit on the sofa sometimes.

PAVA: The show supports your right to the sofa.

ODO: The show does not own the sofa.

ACT TWO-B

Reni describes the room's layout. The sun moves across the window in the morning and reaches the other cushion by late afternoon. Her roommate prefers the warm side because she reads before work. Reni prefers the shaded side because she draws. Both arrive at the same time. Tavi suggests they move the cushion rather than the whole sofa. Nemi lifts it; there are two sides.

TAVI: This cushion flips.

RENI: It is one cushion. We already turn it when we clean.

Odo asks if a daily flip would solve the dispute. Reni says no; both want the sunny side in the morning. Pava asks whether the roommate knows. Reni says they have never discussed it because she did not want to sound petty.

PAVA: We can model a conversation.

ODO: Without assigning the roommate a villain role.

Pava starts the reenactment, then stops and asks Odo to switch parts. He plays the roommate and says he did not realize Reni wanted the sunny spot. Reni smiles at the screen.

RENI: That would be a simpler conversation than this segment.

ACT TWO-B - STUDIO REENACTMENT

Pava wheels in a second sofa to demonstrate the schedule. Odo asks the audience to vote on which host deserves the sunny cushion. Pava sits down before he finishes the question. Odo pulls a paper sun across a track behind the sofa; it catches on a camera cable and drops onto the floor.

ODO: The sun has left the set.

PAVA: It has a call time.

Reni says the real window does not move, and the roommate is not a contestant. The audience guide gives both hosts a yellow card marked "MORNING" and a blue one marked "AFTERNOON." Pava claims yellow because it matches his tie. Odo claims blue because he is wearing it. Nemi rolls the studio sofa between them; its wheels squeak in alternating rhythm.

TAVI: The show has created a second sofa dispute.

PAVA: Then we should resolve this one before advising anyone.

They agree to alternate seats between segments. Odo stands to make room, and the cushion flips over by itself. The audience applauds the cushion. Reni laughs and says her roommate will find the schedule funnier than the reenactment.

ACT THREE

Pava asks Reni what she wants to do. Reni says she will ask her roommate to alternate mornings, then use the reversible cushion as a reminder. Odo asks if the sofa wheels need a brake. Reni says they lock. Nemi locks the studio sofa wheels and returns the cushion to the set.

RENI: I can ask for a turn without moving the room.

PAVA: That is your choice.

The audience votes again. This time the screen lists "talk," "schedule," "move sofa," and "flip cushion." The majority selects "talk." Odo asks if the show can finish without another reenactment. Pava agrees. Nemi signals the end of the segment.

TAG

The two hosts stand to leave. Pava has been sitting on the sunny side. Odo waits for her to move. They trade seats before the cameras turn off.

FINAL IMAGE: The sofa remains centered on the set, with its two-color cushion turned halfway between yellow and blue.
