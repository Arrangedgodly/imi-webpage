# The Long Way to the Waterfall

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel television, breathless location reveals, and host-led local interviews*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A travel host visits the famous Silver Drop Waterfall, which is currently a trickle, and tries to convince the crew that the nearby footpath is the real destination.

Cast: Host Dali, a capuchin; guide Orso, a gorilla; camera operator Lin, a lemur; local maker Beni, a macaque; and producer Vika, a gibbon.

## Episode: "The Hidden Wonder"

### Cold open

EXT. MOUNTAIN PATH, MORNING

[Dali stands before a large scenic reveal. The crew counts down.]

DALI: Three, two, one - welcome to Silver Drop!

[The waterfall trickles into a shallow pool.]

LIN: The camera is ready.

VIKA: The waterfall is smaller than the preview photograph.

ORSO: The photograph was taken during the rainy season.

DALI: This is the quiet season. Every drop has more time to be itself.

BENI: The sign says the waterfall is closed for cleaning.

DALI: Then we are here at a very intimate moment.

### Act One

EXT. PATH TO FALLS

[Dali turns to camera. Orso points at a wooden sign: "FALLS VIEW: 2 MINUTES."]

DALI: We begin our journey at the lower trail.

VIKA: The trail is shorter than the intro.

DALI: Its compactness is a feature.

LIN: We can use the drone shot.

ORSO: There is no drone.

LIN: We can use the high branch.

[Dali climbs a low branch. The camera follows from the ground.]

DALI: From up here, the vista expands.

BENI: You are three feet above the path.

DALI: A local perspective.

ORSO: The waterfall is over that ridge.

VIKA: The sign says two minutes.

ORSO: It is a slow sign.

DALI: We should hear from a local.

BENI: I make walking sticks.

DALI: Tell us about the falls.

BENI: They are small today.

DALI: And what does that teach us?

BENI: To bring a water bottle.

VIKA: That's practical.

DALI: Travel is about carrying practical lessons to beautiful places.

### Act Two

EXT. WATERFALL POOL

[The crew reaches the pool. Dali steps into the water. It is ankle deep.]

DALI: The water is crystal clear.

ORSO: It is shallow.

DALI: You can see every detail.

LIN: Including your reflection.

DALI: That is a bonus guest.

VIKA: We need a wide shot.

LIN: There is a branch across the pool.

[Dali tries to stand on the branch for a dramatic pose. It dips into the water.]

DALI: The falls are welcoming us.

ORSO: The branch is bending.

DALI: A flexible welcome.

BENI: You can sit on that rock.

DALI: I will reveal the waterfall from a seated angle.

[He sits. The camera frames the trickle behind his shoulder.]

DALI: There it is, the Silver Drop, sculpting the stone.

ORSO: It is landing in a bucket.

[The bucket fills with one drop.]

DALI: A patient artisan.

VIKA: The schedule says we have to visit the upper overlook.

ORSO: The path is blocked by a fallen tree.

DALI: Another unexpected detour.

BENI: The detour is the path.

DALI: I love when the path has a sense of humor.

### Local custom

EXT. TRAIL, MOMENTS LATER

[Dali stops beside a flat stone. Beni places a walking stick against it.]

DALI: This stone is known as the Traveler's Rest.

BENI: It is a stone by the trail.

DALI: Does it have a legend?

BENI: People sit on it.

DALI: For generations?

BENI: Since it was left here.

VIKA: Can we get a close-up of the carving?

BENI: There is no carving.

[Lin films a leaf resting on the stone.]

DALI: The leaf has left a mark.

ORSO: The leaf has just landed.

DALI: Nature is full of brief stories.

BENI: The stone is also good for resting.

DALI: Let's rest for one minute.

[Everyone sits. The crew's shoulders drop.]

LIN: This is the best shot of the day.

VIKA: We are resting on camera.

DALI: The audience can see how a journey feels.

### Act Three

EXT. LOWER PATH, AFTERNOON

[The crew walks to a small clearing where Beni has set up a stand with walking sticks.]

DALI: We have reached our final stop, an artisan's workshop.

BENI: It is a stand beside the path.

DALI: A workshop can be portable.

BENI: I make walking sticks from fallen branches.

ORSO: This one is a good length.

BENI: It helps you cross shallow water.

VIKA: We should have bought one before the falls.

DALI: The journey gave us context.

LIN: The footage is mostly us walking.

DALI: The walk is the story.

ORSO: The waterfall is still flowing.

DALI: And every drop is still arriving.

BENI: The cleaning crew is done. You can go closer now.

[They return to the waterfall. A worker has moved the bucket. The trickle falls directly into the pool.]

DALI: It has changed.

ORSO: The water is still the same.

DALI: The view is clearer.

VIKA: We have one minute for the sign-off.

DALI: Silver Drop is a place of quiet beauty, good walking sticks, and signs that tell the truth.

BENI: The sign said two minutes.

DALI: It was right.

LIN: The waterfall is behind you.

[Dali turns the wrong way.]

DALI: And now it is behind me.

### Tag

EXT. TRAILHEAD, EVENING

[Vika checks the travel map. Dali is already halfway down the trail.]

VIKA: The parking area is this way.

DALI: Then we have discovered one more path.

[Orso points to the sign. They are standing beside the parking area.]
