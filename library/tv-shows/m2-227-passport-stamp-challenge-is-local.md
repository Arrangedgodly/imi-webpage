# The Passport Stamp Challenge Is Local

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel competitions that make ordinary local trips sound like an international expedition*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A travel team crosses a familiar town to collect passport stamps, but the show's map treats every block as a new country.

Cast: Host Nera, a gibbon; traveler Kito, a macaque; guide Omi, a capuchin; producer Sela, a baboon; and baker Huna, a lemur.

Episode: "Across the Street"

COLD OPEN

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - DAY

Nera hands Kito a tiny passport. Omi points across the square to a bakery.

NERA: Your journey begins in the northern quarter.

KITO: That's the bakery.

OMI: It is three blocks away.

SELA: The show needs a sense of distance.

[KITO OPENS THE PASSPORT. IT HAS A MAP OF THE SAME SQUARE.]

ACT ONE

Omi leads the team toward a fountain. Sela films their feet. Nera narrates from a small cart.

NERA: We leave the center and enter the old market district.

KITO: It is on the same block.

OMI: The fruit stall has been here for years.

SELA: Stop for a location stamp.

[THE STALL OWNER STAMPS KITO'S PASSPORT WITH A SMALL APPLE.]

KITO: That is a sticker.

NERA: It marks the district.

ACT TWO

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

Omi unfolds a map. A red line goes from the fountain to the bakery. Sela has added a detour around a bench.

KITO: The bakery is across the street.

SELA: Our route goes past the clock, along the wall, and around the bench.

OMI: Why?

SELA: We need enough footage for the crossing.

[THEY WALK THE ROUTE. THEY ARRIVE AT THE BENCH.]

KITO: We are back at the fountain.

NERA: You have circled the central square.

KITO: That's not the bakery.

SELA: The map is rotated.

[OMI TURNS IT. THE BAKERY APPEARS ACROSS THE ROAD.]

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CROSSWALK - AFTERNOON

The team waits at the crosswalk. The walk signal changes. Sela asks for a dramatic countdown.

SELA: Three, two, one.

OMI: The light is already green.

KITO: We can cross safely now.

NERA: The passport needs one final stamp.

[THE BAKER COMES OUT AND STAMPS THE PASSPORT WITH A FLOURY HANDPRINT.]

BAKER: You came to the bakery.

KITO: We crossed one street.

SELA: The episode title is "Across the Street."

NERA: The journey has been completed.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. SIDE STREET - AFTERNOON

Sela points to a small path behind the bakery. Kito has the passport open. Omi checks the route map.

SELA: The next stamp is at the old post office.

KITO: It is across the street from the bakery.

OMI: The post office closed last year.

NERA: A closed border. A new challenge.

KITO: The building is now a shoe repair shop.

SELA: We can ask for a stamp.

[KITO KNOCKS. A COBBLER OPENS THE DOOR.]

KITO: We are collecting travel stamps for the show.

COBBLER: I repair shoes.

OMI: Do you have a stamp?

COBBLER: I have a date stamp for receipts.

NERA: A unique local mark.

[THE COBBLER STAMPS THE PASSPORT. THE INK SAYS "PAID." ]

KITO: I have not bought anything.

COBBLER: Then it is the wrong stamp.

SELA: It still marks the visit.

OMI: The show wants a proper travel stamp.

KITO: The baker already gave us a handprint.

NERA: A route can be remembered without a stamp.

[SELA TAKES A PHOTO OF THE CLOSED POST OFFICE SIGN. KITO TURNS THE PASSPORT TO THE BLANK PAGE.]

KITO: We can keep the photo with the map.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Omi spreads the map across the bench. Sela hands Kito a glue stick for the receipts and stamps.

OMI: We can attach the bakery handprint beside the route.

KITO: There is flour on the passport.

NERA: The flour suggests local cuisine.

KITO: It suggests I opened the book too soon.

[A PATCH OF FLOUR COVERS THE NEXT TWO PAGES. SELA BLOWS ON IT. THE FLOUR DRIFTS ONTO THE MAP.]

SELA: The route is now dusted.

OMI: The map already shows the way.

NERA: We can end the challenge here.

KITO: We can walk to the bus stop. It is our next real destination.

SELA: There is no stamp there.

KITO: There is a bus.

[THE BUS ARRIVES. THE TRAVELERS BOARD. THE MAP STAYS ON THE BENCH.]

ACT THREE

INT. BAKERY - LATE AFTERNOON

Kito buys a roll. Omi folds the map. Nera asks the baker for a travel tip.

BAKER: Watch the crosswalk and check the hours before you come.

KITO: That would have saved us the detour.

SELA: We needed the detour for the story.

OMI: The road was closed around the bench.

[THEY LOOK OUT. A WORKER HAS PUT A SMALL BARRIER AROUND THE BENCH.]

NERA: The route had a reason after all.

KITO: The map was still upside down.

TAG

Sela opens Kito's passport. The bakery handprint has smeared across every page.

KITO: Now the whole town is stamped.

[THEY CLOSE THE PASSPORT AND SHARE THE ROLL.]
