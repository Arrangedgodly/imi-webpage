# The River Rescue Is Waiting for a Camera

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality rescue shows that delay practical help for a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Tavi, a baboon; rescuer Pava, a macaque; rescuer Neri, a capuchin; field producer Lumo, a gibbon; local macaque Seli.

COLD OPEN

EXT. RIVERBANK - MORNING

[A small wooden footbridge has one loose plank. A paper bag is stuck on the far bank. Tavi faces camera while Pava reaches for it.] 

TAVI: In a moment, our team will attempt a daring retrieval.

PAVA: I can walk over and get it.

LUMO: Hold. We need the shot from the other side.

[The bag blows into the water. Theme sting.]

ACT ONE

TAVI: Welcome to River Rescue, where no situation is too small for a crane shot.

NERI: It's a paper bag.

SELI: My lunch is in it.

PAVA: I can reach it with this branch.

[She extends a stick. The bag drifts away from shore.] 

LUMO: Keep your arm in frame.

PAVA: I am trying to get the bag.

TAVI: Tell us what you are feeling.

PAVA: Damp.

[The camera operator steps onto a dry rock. It tilts slightly. Seli grabs the operator's strap and steadies them.] 

SELI: Watch your footing.

TAVI: We have our first rescuer in trouble.

SELI: I am holding a camera person.

LUMO: That's a usable B-story.

PAVA: The bag is moving toward the reeds.

NERI: I can climb down the bank.

TAVI: Before you do, describe the risk.

NERI: Slippery mud. Shallow water. A bag with lunch.

TAVI: More suspense.

NERI: The lunch might get soggy.

ACT TWO

[The producers set up a rope and a ring buoy. Pava checks the bank, then asks Seli to hold one end. Neri climbs down a low slope using her hands and feet, careful and quick.] 

TAVI: The rope is taut. The stakes are rising.

SELI: The rope is around my waist.

LUMO: It keeps the line clean.

SELI: It keeps me attached to the camera.

[The bag catches on a reed. Neri reaches it, but the paper tears. A sandwich container slides free and floats toward a rock.] 

PAVA: We have the food.

TAVI: Do we have the rescue?

NERI: We have lunch.

[The buoy rolls away because no one has tied it to the rope. Pava catches it with her tail, then pulls it back onto shore.] 

PAVA: The safety equipment has attempted an escape.

LUMO: Hold that for the recap.

[The sandwich is dry inside its sealed container. Seli opens it and checks the label.] 

SELI: This is not mine. Mine has the red stripe.

[They look upstream. A second bag is wedged under the bridge. Pava points to a pawprint in the mud.] 

PAVA: Yours came this way. Something carried it across.

[She notices a macaque on the bridge. It has a red-striped bag tucked against its chest. It peers down at the crew.] 

SELI: That's my cousin. She was keeping it from the water.

TAVI: We have an unexpected rescuer.

SELI: She's not in the show.

ACT THREE

[Pava calls up to the bridge. The cousin tosses the bag. It lands safely on the bank, then a gust rolls it toward the water again. Neri grabs it.] 

NERI: Lunch secured.

TAVI: Can we get a slow-motion shot?

PAVA: You just saw it.

LUMO: We can recreate the toss.

SELI: The bag is wet now. No retakes.

[Tavi faces camera. The crew kneels beside Seli while she opens the container. Inside is a bunch of berries and a folded note.] 

SELI: The note says, "I found this under the bridge."

TAVI: Our rescue had a rescue.

PAVA: Our team walked down a bank.

TAVI: And learned the true meaning of lunch.

[The cousin calls from above. Seli waves. A second sandwich bag drops from the bridge, tied to a string. Pava catches the string and lifts it.]

PAVA: Do we need another rescue?

SELI: That's her lunch.

TAVI: We can roll into the next episode.

[The camera operator notices the strap has caught on a reed at the water's edge. The lens tilts toward the ground.]

LUMO: We have lost the wide shot.

PAVA: Your strap is caught.

TAVI: Can we describe the danger?

LUMO: The camera might touch mud.

[The whole crew reaches for the strap. Seli stays on the bank and holds the rope while Neri frees it from the reed.]

SELI: The rope is for the bag, not the camera.

PAVA: It is a line. We can use it once.

[The strap comes free, leaving a reed caught on its buckle. Neri removes the reed and hands it to Seli.]

NERI: Evidence of the rescue.

TAVI: We have a clean ending.

SELI: My cousin got my lunch back before your ending.

TAG

[The camera pans to the empty riverbank. Lumo marks the spot with a small sign: "DANGER: DRAMATIC." A breeze lifts it into the water.] 

TAVI: Cut. We have a new call.
