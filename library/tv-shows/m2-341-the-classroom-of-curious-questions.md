# The Classroom of Curious Questions

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's educational programs that answer simple questions with elaborate set pieces*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A children's science host tries to explain why monkeys groom one another, but the studio keeps turning it into a competition.

Cast: Host Tavi, a gibbon; scientist Nara, a macaque; student Odo, a baboon; student Piri, a capuchin; puppet Dot, a lemur.

Episode: "What Is a Helpful Hand?"

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO TREEHOUSE - DAY

Tavi sits beside a toy comb and a mirror.

TAVI: Today we ask why monkeys groom.

Odo raises his hand.

ODO: Because someone's fur is crooked.

Piri smooths Odo's shoulder fur. Odo looks in the mirror.

ODO: It is still crooked.

Dot the puppet springs from a box wearing a paper crown.

DOT: A grooming emergency!

ACT ONE

Nara explains that grooming helps remove dirt and loose fur and can strengthen social bonds. Tavi says they will demonstrate with a friendly grooming circle. Odo asks if there is a winner. Tavi says no.

A scoreboard drops from the ceiling. Piri asks who installed it. Tavi says the studio team thought points would help children follow the lesson. Nara reads the score categories: "most hairs moved," "longest patience," and "best smile."

NARA: These do not measure care.

Odo reaches for the comb. Piri asks him to wait until the demonstration. Odo begins brushing his own arm. Dot calls this independent grooming.

ACT TWO

Nara shows how a monkey may carefully pick through fur, stopping if the other monkey moves away. Piri gently checks Odo's shoulder and removes a small leaf. Odo turns his back to her. She stops.

TAVI: Why did you stop?

PIRI: He turned away.

Nara says grooming works only when both animals are comfortable. The scoreboard flashes "Piri: one leaf." Tavi covers it with a cloth.

Dot pulls the cloth away. It reveals a bonus round: participants must groom while balancing a ball. Nara says that would make the action less careful. Odo tries anyway. He drops the ball and knocks over the mirror.

The crew resets the set. Tavi asks Odo what went wrong. He says he was watching the ball instead of Piri. Piri says she was distracted by the score. Nara asks them to try again without the scoreboard or props.

ACT TWO-B

INT. STUDIO TREEHOUSE - LATER

The children sit on the floor. Piri checks Odo's fur slowly, pausing whenever he shifts. Odo relaxes. He returns the grooming by gently picking a seed from Piri's sleeve.

DOT: The seed is the prize!

Nara says it is just a seed. Dot tries to put it on the scoreboard, but Tavi takes the board down. She tells the studio crew to leave the demonstration unscored.

Odo asks if grooming always means removing something. Nara says sometimes it is social contact; it can signal trust and attention. Piri asks if she may stop now. Odo says yes. She stops, and they sit together.

ACT TWO-C

Nara asks the students to compare grooming with scratching. Odo scratches his shoulder quickly; loose fur sticks to his paw. Piri says that is different from checking gently through the coat. Tavi asks Dot to demonstrate with a plush toy. Dot pulls the toy's fur so hard that the paper crown falls off. Nara shows how to pause and let a partner move away if uncomfortable.

Odo asks if grooming is always done by family. Nara says monkeys may groom familiar group members, but they can also groom themselves. Piri uses her fingers to remove a bit of dried leaf from her arm. The scoreboard flashes "solo points." Tavi unplugs it.

Dot asks how children can tell if a friend wants grooming. Nara says they can ask or notice if the friend moves away. Odo asks whether the show can include a "consent signal." Piri raises one hand to say "stop." Odo lowers his hand and leaves space. Tavi tells the viewers that kindness includes paying attention to a partner's signals.

The studio crew tries to bring back the balance-ball challenge. Nara refuses. She asks them to show a safe demonstration with no competition. They put away the ball, score board, crown, and extra props. The set becomes a quiet treehouse again.

ACT THREE

Tavi summarizes the lesson: grooming can keep fur clean and help monkeys bond, but it should be gentle and welcome. Odo asks if the mirror can stay. Nara says it can, as long as nobody uses it to judge who is neatest.

Dot removes the paper crown and offers it to the mirror. The crown falls off.

TAG

Odo asks whether the show can award one point for asking a good question.

Nara shakes her head.

PIRI: He can keep the seed.
