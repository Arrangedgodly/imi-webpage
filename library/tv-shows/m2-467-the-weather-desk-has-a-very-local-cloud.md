# The Weather Desk Has a Very Local Cloud

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local television weather forecasts, animated maps, and reporters who label a single garden sprinkler a storm system*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Meteorologist Jaro, a gibbon; anchor Vemi, a macaque; field reporter Pali, an orangutan; graphics operator Hemi, a baboon; gardener Toko, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL NEWS STUDIO - MORNING

A weather map shows a red cloud over one backyard. The anchor turns to the meteorologist.

VEMI: A severe cell is forming behind the station.

JARO: That is a garden sprinkler.

[The map flashes STORM ALERT. A small animated lightning bolt lands on a lawn chair.] 

HEMI: The software expanded the dot to fill the county.

PALI: I am live in the backyard. The sky is clear.

[The anchor puts on a raincoat. The sprinkler ticks as it rotates.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. BACKYARD - CONTINUOUS

Pali stands beside the gardener, who is watering a row of seedlings with a timer. The field reporter holds an umbrella, ready for a dramatic shot. Toko turns off the sprinkler while the anchor speaks through the remote feed.

VEMI: Are you experiencing dangerous rainfall?

TOKO: It is a sprinkler. I am testing the timer.

JARO: The local radar picked up the water droplets.

PALI: The droplets are landing on the camera lens.

[The field reporter wipes the lens. The umbrella blocks the sun and casts a dark shadow over the seedlings. Toko asks Pali to move it.] 

TOKO: The plants need light. The umbrella can stay closed.

[Pali folds the umbrella. The newsroom map zooms in on the backyard. The red warning box covers the street name.] 

VEMI: What is the current condition?

PALI: Clear skies and a sprinkler turned off.

HEMI: The map wants a weather symbol.

JARO: Use the sun icon. Do not call it a heat dome.

[The graphics operator changes the label to GARDEN WATERING, LOCAL.] 

ACT TWO

INT. NEWS STUDIO - LATER

The anchor asks the meteorologist to explain the warning. Jaro shows how the radar briefly detected the spray, then cleared when the sprinkler stopped. Hemi shows the map scale and explains that the first graphic zoomed too far. Vemi tries to add a red banner reading BREAKING.

JARO: Nothing broke.

VEMI: We can say the graphic broke.

HEMI: I resized it. It is fixed.

[The field reporter calls in. Toko says the seedlings are watered and the timer has been reset. Pali stands well clear of the sprinkler.]

PALI: We have a final update from the yard.

TOKO: The timer runs for ten minutes at dawn. No one is watering now.

[The anchor asks if the segment can run until the next commercial. Jaro says the forecast is complete. Vemi turns to camera, but the weather map still shows the lightning bolt over the lawn chair.] 

HEMI: That icon is from the old template.

[Hemi deletes it. The chair remains visible, dry and unstruck. The anchor takes off the raincoat.] 

[The anchor asks the field reporter whether the gardener needs another live check. Pali points to the timer, which is now shut off, and reads the posted schedule. Hemi clears the last red border from the graphic.] 

PALI: The water ran for ten minutes. The seedlings are watered. The sprinkler is off.

VEMI: So the alert is over.

JARO: It was not a storm alert. It was a map error.

[The host folds the raincoat and places it on the chair. Hemi saves the corrected local map under the name GARDEN SPRINKLER, preventing the severe-weather overlay from returning.] 

HEMI: The next forecast will begin with the county view.

PALI: The backyard can return to being a backyard.

ACT THREE

INT. LOCAL NEWS STUDIO - MIDDAY

The regular forecast returns. Jaro points to actual clouds moving toward the north county and gives the expected time of arrival. Hemi keeps the map at regional scale. Vemi asks Pali whether the backyard needs another live check. Pali looks through the studio window; the gardener has gone inside.

PALI: No. The sprinkler is off, and the report is complete.

VEMI: We will keep an eye on the forecast.

JARO: A forecast is not a promise. The north clouds may pass east.

[The map updates with a broad, clear arrow. No lightning bolt appears. Hemi places a small note beside the backyard location: WATER TIMER, NOT WEATHER.] 

VEMI: That is a useful note.

[The anchor sets the raincoat on a hanger. The meteorologist closes the local radar window and opens the county map.] 

TAG

[A single drop falls from the umbrella onto the desk. Vemi reaches for the raincoat, then notices Hemi's water glass has condensation. They both look at the glass.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The county weather map shows moving clouds while a closed umbrella hangs dry behind the desk.
