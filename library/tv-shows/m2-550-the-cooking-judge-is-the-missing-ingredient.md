# The Cooking Judge Is the Missing Ingredient

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competition mysteries, secret-ingredient reveals, and judges who mistake their own absence for a culinary clue*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: HOST, a baboon; CHEF TALA, a gibbon; CHEF FENN, a capuchin; JUDGE MORA, an orangutan; FLOOR RUNNER, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING STUDIO - DAY

Two chefs stand before hot plates. The judge's chair is empty. A covered dish waits on the table.

HOST: Today, the missing ingredient is our judge.

TALA: That is not an ingredient.

FENN: It could be if she's in the pantry.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The host explains the challenge: make a dish inspired by the word "absence." The chefs have twenty minutes. Judge Mora is missing because the floor runner cannot find her. Tala makes a broth with a hollow dumpling. Fenn sets an empty plate in the center of the table and begins arranging herbs around it.

HOST: The camera needs a dish.

FENN: The empty plate is the dish.

The runner returns, breathless, and says Mora's dressing room is empty. The host turns the search into a suspense segment. Tala asks if anyone checked the pantry. Fenn says the pantry is part of the competition and might contain the secret ingredient. The host sends both chefs to look, but the rules say contestants cannot leave their stations.

TALA: I cannot abandon the broth.

FENN: It is already about absence.

The countdown reaches fifteen minutes. The runner checks the studio call sheet. Mora was scheduled to judge after the first commercial break, not before it. The host says the chair has been empty for the entire shoot, so the story is already in motion.

ACT TWO

The producers reveal that Mora is in the control room watching the monitors. She stepped out of the studio to avoid being filmed before her judging segment. She returns through a side door holding a tasting spoon. The host asks why she did not answer when called.

MORA: I was off camera.

HOST: We had a mystery.

MORA: You had a schedule.

The chefs finish their plates. Tala presents the broth and explains she left room inside the dumpling for steam. Mora tastes it, then asks whether the absence is supposed to be visible. Tala cuts the dumpling open. It releases steam and collapses.

FENN: My plate has remained absent throughout.

Mora tastes the herb arrangement. She says it is not a dish but a garnish arranged around nothing. Fenn says the challenge asked for absence. The host asks the judge to decide whether a missing judge counts as the secret ingredient.

MORA: I am not on the menu.

The floor runner brings the call sheet to the counter and points out the planned judging time. Mora says the contestants followed the prompt, but the host invented a second puzzle. Tala's broth wins for turning a hollow center into a cooked dish. Fenn receives the "most literal plate" mention.

INT. JUDGING COUNTER - DAY

The host asks the chefs to describe their final plates without naming the missing judge. Tala explains that the dumpling's hollow center holds steam, but it collapsed when Mora cut it. She asks whether she can serve a second one. The host says a second plate would change the challenge. The floor runner checks the clock and says there is time for a reheat, not a remake.

TALA: I can refill the broth.

FENN: I can remove the herbs.

MORA: Neither action changes what you submitted.

The host asks the chefs to answer the same question: what makes a dish feel complete when one part is absent? Tala says the broth still has flavor and temperature. Fenn says an empty plate can make a viewer imagine anything. Mora says the judging criteria include taste, preparation, and presentation. Fenn's plate earns points for presentation but none for taste.

FENN: I expected a philosophical exception.

MORA: It is a cooking show.

The runner places the call sheet next to the timer. The host admits the show's suspense music began before the judge was scheduled to arrive. The floor manager changes the cue so the next episode will announce a real delay rather than a fake disappearance. Tala serves the remaining broth to the crew, while Fenn carries his plate to the sink.



ACT THREE

The host announces the winner. Mora sits in the judge's chair and stays there. The runner labels the schedule with a large red arrow. Fenn takes the empty plate to the dishwashing station.

FENN: Now it has something on it.

HOST: What?

FENN: Soap.

FINAL IMAGE: The judge's chair remains occupied as the host searches the call sheet for the next surprise.
