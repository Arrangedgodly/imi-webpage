# The View from the Wrong Side

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentary television, breathless destination narration, and hosts who describe a place before checking which side they are on*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A travel host films a picturesque overlook from the service road and accidentally guides viewers away from the actual viewpoint.

Cast: Host Mira, a macaque; field producer Odo, an orangutan; camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; park guide Neri, a capuchin; and driver Vex, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "The Hidden View"

### Cold Open

[FOREST ROAD. MIRA STANDS BEFORE A BUSH.]

MIRA: Behind me is the famous valley, waiting to be discovered.

TAVI: The valley is behind the camera.

MIRA: Then it is waiting to be discovered by viewers.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PRODUCTION VAN. ODO STUDIES A MAP.]

Odo explains that the crew has arrived at the north service road instead of the overlook. Mira says the trees create an intimate view. Neri offers to guide them to the marked path.

NERI: It is a six-minute walk.

MIRA: Can you make it feel remote?

NERI: It is near the parking lot.

Tavi frames a close-up of Mira looking thoughtful. Vex points out that the camera is aimed at the road sign marked OVERLOOK, but the sign is behind them.

VEX: We are standing with our backs to the view.

MIRA: That is a strong opening image.

Odo asks them to hike to the overlook before the weather changes. Mira carries a lantern and announces that the journey begins with a crossing of the "old forest road." They cross a paved service road.

### Act Two

[TRAIL. MIRA WALKS, FOLLOWED BY THE CREW.]

Neri leads the way. Mira stops at every marker to narrate it. The first marker says 100 METERS. Mira describes it as a milestone. At the next marker, Tavi cannot get a wide shot because the crew's tripod blocks the narrow path.

TAVI: We need to move the equipment.

MIRA: The equipment is part of the expedition.

NERI: The path is for walkers.

Odo carries the tripod around the crew. The camera records a slow-motion shot of Vex brushing leaves off his coat. Mira says the leaves are "guides from the canopy." Vex returns them to the path.

A light rain begins. Odo checks the radar. The storm is moving toward the overlook. Neri suggests they stop filming the walk and reach the covered shelter.

MIRA: We need the moment the view opens.

NERI: You can have it after the rain.

Mira continues. The trail bends, and the crew arrives at a maintenance shed with a painted landscape on the side. Mira steps in front of it and calls it a panoramic vista.

VEX: It is a mural.

MIRA: A vivid interpretation of the landscape.

Odo asks Mira to turn around and face the valley. She agrees, but asks for one more close-up of the mural. Tavi frames the painted tree, then pans to the real tree beside it. The two trunks line up, making the mural look like a continuation of the forest.

MIRA: The boundary between art and landscape has disappeared.

NERI: The boundary is the wall.

Vex notices the service road ends at a gate. A sign points to the public trail, but the crew's equipment cart cannot pass through. They leave the cart behind and carry the camera and tripod by hand. Mira insists the extra weight makes the hike more authentic.

TAVI: It makes the camera heavier.

They reach a fork. Mira chooses the path with the dramatic slope; Neri points to the level overlook path. The crew splits for one shot, then reunites at the same shelter. Odo marks the level route on the map.

### Act Three

[OVERLOOK SHELTER. RAIN FALLS BEYOND THE ROOF.]

Neri points to a small window. The real valley can be seen through the rain. Mira asks Tavi to frame the view without the glass reflections. Tavi moves outside, then discovers the best view is from the opposite side of the shelter.

TAVI: The camera was facing the parking lot.

ODO: We have spent the episode filming the wrong direction.

MIRA: We have shown how a place changes when you turn around.

Neri opens the shelter door, but the rain has stopped. The crew walks to the overlook platform. Mira stays quiet while Tavi records the full valley, the trail, and the service road below. Vex is visible near a sign pointing back toward the parking area.

NERI: We can reach the platform safely. Stay inside the rail.

MIRA: The view is larger than the narration.

Odo nods. Mira asks him to remove the scripted lines from the last segment. The crew records the wind, the birds, and one minute of silence.

### Tag

[PRODUCTION VAN. THE CREW WATCHES THE FOOTAGE.]

The footage shows a valley, rain, a shed mural, and Mira in front of a bush.

MIRA: We can start the show with the bush.

TAVI: The valley is better.

MIRA: The bush got us here.

VEX: The map got us here.

Neri folds the map and points the van toward the right road.

MIRA: Next week, we visit the south overlook.

ODO: This was the north overlook.

MIRA: Then the journey continues.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
