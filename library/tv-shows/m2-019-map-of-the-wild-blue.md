# Map of the Wild Blue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: animated adventure series*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A fearless young explorer and her cautious mapmaker friend race to return a lost cloud seed to the mountain garden before the dry season begins.

Cast: Pika, a young marmoset explorer; Sprocket, a thoughtful capuchin mapmaker; Tumble, a tiny lemur who can squeeze through narrow spaces; Aunt Rena, a mountain gardener; and the Wind, a gusting force that never speaks but keeps moving everyone's hats.

## Episode: "The Cloud Seed"

### Cold open

EXT. MOUNTAIN RIDGE, DAY

Pika holds up a silver seed in a glass jar. Wind snatches it. The jar bounces down the slope, skips a rock, and disappears into a ravine. A shiny icon appears in the corner of the picture. It spins once, then vanishes.

PIKA: The quest has begun.

SPROCKET: The quest is to retrieve it.

TUMBLE: Can the quest wait? My scarf is gone.

A second gust blows Sprocket's map open upside down.

PIKA: First, we follow the marker.

SPROCKET: That's my lunch wrapper.

### Act one

Aunt Rena's mountain garden depends on mist gathered at the ridge. The cloud seed came from an old weather shrine and helps channel water into the cistern. Pika dropped the jar while trying to pose for the expedition sketch. Sprocket checks his old map. A rockslide has covered the shortest trail, and a stream now cuts through the ravine.

SPROCKET: The map shows a bridge.

TUMBLE: It shows a branch.

PIKA: It used to be a bridge.

SPROCKET: It is on the other bank.

PIKA: We need three keys.

TUMBLE: Who said that?

PIKA: Every adventure says that.

SPROCKET: This is a map, not a game.

PIKA: Then why is there a glowing icon?

The icon is a reflection from Pika's jar. She shades it with her hand. The glow disappears. The trio takes the safer route through a cave, where thick moss marks the path taken by morning mist.

### Act two

Tumble squeezes into a narrow gap and finds the jar wedged behind a stone. Pika reaches for it. Pebbles begin to fall. Sprocket tells her to stop. He sets a branch as a lever while Tumble wraps the jar in his scarf. They lift the stone together and slide the jar free.

PIKA: We got the collectible.

TUMBLE: We got a glass jar.

SPROCKET: The seed is collectible.

PIKA: Do we get a badge?

TUMBLE: You can have my muddy scarf.

PIKA: I want the silver one.

TUMBLE: That is the seed.

SPROCKET: It belongs in the shrine.

PIKA: The shrine is the final level.

SPROCKET: It's a stone structure.

TUMBLE: The map says left.

SPROCKET: The map is still upside down.

They follow the air current to the shrine. Its stone funnel is blocked by gravel from the slide. A notch at the top holds the cloud seed. The side vent that once let mist enter is jammed by a slab.

PIKA: Put the seed in first.

SPROCKET: The vent is blocked.

PIKA: The seed should open it.

TUMBLE: It's a seed, not a key.

PIKA: Maybe it has a key form.

SPROCKET: It has a seed form.

TUMBLE: The slab is on a track.

Sprocket clears grit from the groove. They lift the slab together. Wind carries mist through the vent and across the stone funnel. Pika places the seed in its notch. Nothing happens. The breeze continues, and water begins to bead along the channel.

PIKA: Is that the victory music?

TUMBLE: It is a drip.

SPROCKET: The cistern is receiving water.

PIKA: A slow victory drip.

### Act three

Aunt Rena opens the cistern lid. The trickle will not fill the garden's needs, but it can keep young plants alive until the rains. She thanks the trio for clearing the vent. Pika admits she dropped the jar while posing. Sprocket says the old map needs the new trail marked. Tumble asks for a new scarf.

AUNT RENA: The route is on this map.

SPROCKET: It was covered by a rockslide.

AUNT RENA: Then cross it out.

PIKA: No collectible marker?

AUNT RENA: A warning mark.

TUMBLE: Can we get a sticker?

AUNT RENA: You can draw one.

They mark the safe route and the loose ledge. Sprocket writes that the seed channels moisture, not magic. Pika adds a note that it should stay in the shrine. Aunt Rena returns the jar to the tool shed.

### Tag

Pika makes a little badge from a wet leaf. It slides off her vest. Tumble pins it to his scarf.

PIKA: We completed the quest.

SPROCKET: We did useful work.

TUMBLE: And got no merchandise.

The leaf drops. Pika pins it back on with a twig.
