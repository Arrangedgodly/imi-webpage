# The Antique Show's Patina Is Still Wet

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: antique restoration competitions, dramatic appraisals, and hosts who confuse fresh glue with historic patina*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Vero, a gibbon; restorer Tavi, an orangutan; contestant Luma, a macaque; appraiser Piri, a baboon; stagehand Seko, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. RESTORATION STUDIO - DAY

A small wooden side table sits under a spotlight. One leg is loose; a strip of fresh glue shines at the joint. The host circles it slowly.

VERO: This table may be worth a fortune.

PIRI: It may be worth a repair.

LUMA: It is my grandmother's table. I want it steady enough to use.

TAVI: Then we should not polish the wet glue for the camera.

[The host reaches toward the leg. The restorer blocks the hand with a wooden ruler.] 

ACT ONE

INT. RESTORATION STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Tavi checks the underside and identifies a split at the joint. Luma says the leg wobbles when anyone sets down a cup. The host asks whether the table has a secret compartment. Piri examines the underside and finds no drawer or hidden panel.

VERO: Could it be a rare tea table?

LUMA: It is a side table.

PIRI: The maker's mark dates it to several decades ago. That is enough history without inventing a secret.

[The camera zooms toward the wet glue. Vero asks the restorer to call it an ancient resin. Tavi points to the bottle label, which says wood glue.] 

TAVI: This repair was made today. It has not cured.

LUMA: I applied it this morning and brought it in for advice.

VERO: A fresh mystery.

TAVI: A fresh joint. We should let it set before testing.

[The host moves a small cup toward the table for a dramatic balance test. Luma takes it away before it can be placed on the unstable leg.] 

ACT TWO

INT. WORKBENCH - LATER

Piri explains that the table's value depends on condition and maker, not on the show adding a glossy finish. Tavi removes the squeeze-out glue with a damp cloth while it is still soft, then clamps the joint. Luma helps hold the table level. Seko brings a padded stand so the piece can sit without pressure on the repaired leg.

VERO: How much will the appraisal rise?

PIRI: The repair has not changed its age. It may prevent further damage.

LUMA: I am not selling it.

VERO: But what if the camera wants a price reveal?

LUMA: The camera can reveal the clamp.

[The restorer positions the clamp and checks that it is snug, not crushing the wood. The host tries to pull it tighter for a close-up. Tavi stops the hand.] 

TAVI: A clamp is not a strength contest.

[The host asks the appraiser to give a range anyway. Piri says the maker's mark and condition support a modest value, but the loose leg must cure before a final inspection. The contestant chooses to keep the table and use it after the repair is checked.] 

[While the clamp sets, Vero rehearses a valuation reveal with a covered card. Piri asks the host to wait until the joint is inspected. Luma uses the time to photograph the maker's mark for her own records.]

LUMA: That mark was hard to see before you moved the lamp.

PIRI: A clear record helps a future owner understand the piece.

VERO: Will that be in the reveal?

LUMA: It can be in the episode, if you do not call it a hidden signature.

[The host turns the cover card around. It reads "MAKER'S MARK, NOT A SECRET COMPARTMENT." Tavi checks the clamp one final time and leaves it in place until the next day.]

ACT THREE

INT. RESTORATION STUDIO - NEXT DAY

The glue has cured. Tavi removes the clamp and checks the joint. The table stands on all four legs. Luma places an empty cup on the center, then moves it to the edge. The table does not wobble. The appraiser examines the finish without applying polish.

PIRI: Stable, with the original surface intact.

VERO: Then our mystery object is worth...

LUMA: A table I can use.

PIRI: And a modest resale estimate, if you ever choose to sell it.

[The host presents a card with the estimate. Luma turns it face down and places the cup on the table. Tavi asks Luma to wait a little longer before putting weight on the repaired corner.]

LUMA: I can wait. I have another table for today.

VERO: The suspense continues.

TAVI: The glue is cured. The recommendation is simply to avoid a heavy load until tomorrow.

[The stagehand removes the padded stand and stores the clamp. The table remains in place. Vero starts to announce another appraisal, but Piri closes the price book.] 

TAG

The host places a price card on the empty padded stand. It slides off. Luma catches it and sets it flat on the host's clipboard.

FINAL IMAGE: The restored table stands steady with one cup on top and no spotlight aimed at its glue joint.
