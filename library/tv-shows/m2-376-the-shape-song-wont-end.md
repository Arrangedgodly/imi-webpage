# The Shape Song Won't End

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: preschool television, endlessly repeated lessons, mascot interruptions, and songs that refuse to stop at the credits*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A children's TV host tries to teach three shapes, but the triangle mascot's jingle loops until the crew makes it part of the lesson.

Cast: Host Miri, a macaque; Pip the Triangle, a capuchin; Dot the Circle, a gibbon; Box the Square, an orangutan; director Nema, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. CHILDREN'S STUDIO - MORNING

Miri points to a triangle, a circle and a square on a board. A jaunty song begins.

MIRI: Today we will meet three shapes.

PIP: I am the shape with three corners!

The song repeats its opening line. Dot tries to speak over it.

DOT: I am the round one - 

NEMA (IN EARPIECE): The music cue is stuck.

ACT ONE

Miri asks each mascot to name itself. Pip answers first, then repeats his jingle. Dot rolls across the floor, but the music cues Pip's song again. Box waits beside the square card.

BOX: Should I introduce myself?

MIRI: Yes, Box.

BOX: I have four equal sides.

PIP: Three corners, three corners, triangle!

Nema raises the cue card for the circle segment. The triangle song starts again. Miri points at the board, where the triangle's card has slipped over the circle.

MIRI: Pip, can you stand by your own picture?

PIP: I can stand anywhere.

Pip walks to the circle card. The jingle plays. Dot begins rolling in the wrong direction and bumps the board. All three shape cards slide to the floor.

ACT TWO

Miri asks children at home to find a circle. Pip sings his song during the pause. Nema tries to pull the music cable. The song stops for half a second, then plays through a small speaker in Pip's costume.

PIP: I can hear it in my vest.

NEMA: The speaker is sewn into the pocket.

MIRI: We need to hear Dot and Box too.

Dot demonstrates a circle by rolling a hoop. Pip sings over it. Box holds up a square block. Pip continues singing. Miri gives Pip a hand signal to pause. He stops, and the room goes quiet.

MIRI: What shape is the hoop?

DOT: A circle.

MIRI: What shape is the block?

BOX: A square.

Nema cues the closing music. The speaker in Pip's vest starts the triangle song again. Miri turns the microphone toward the audience and the children clap on the beat. Pip keeps singing, now synchronized with them.

ACT TWO-B

Miri asks the three mascots to sort a box of blocks by shape. Pip puts every red block in the triangle bin. Dot rolls two circles into it. Box removes them and shows the shape cards.

BOX: Color is not the question.

PIP: My song is about triangles.

MIRI: The song can help us, but we have to look at the shapes.

Pip points to the triangle bin. He places one triangle block inside. Dot places two circles in the circle bin. Box stacks the square blocks. The speaker plays the jingle whenever Pip handles anything. He sets down a circle. The song starts. He moves a square. The song starts again. Finally, he holds still. It stops.

PIP: It thinks I am a triangle.

NEMA: It responds to the motion sensor on your vest.

Miri opens the vest pocket to expose the control board. Pip removes the vest and holds it still. The speaker finishes its current bar and stops because the motion sensor is no longer moving.

MIRI: Now we can hear the shapes.

A child in the studio audience holds up a triangle drawing. Pip points to it, keeping the vest motionless and silent in his other hand. Dot rolls the hoop beside a square cushion. Box rotates the cushion; it stays square.

DOT: The circle can roll.

BOX: The square can stack.

PIP: And the triangle can...

Pip puts the vest on the costume rack. The movement triggers the jingle again. Nema opens the pocket, removes the speaker battery, and tapes the switch in the off position. The jingle stops. Pip finishes without music.

PIP: Be a triangle.

MIRI: You don't have to sing it every time.

PIP: The vest kept asking.

The audience laughs. Nema tapes a label beside the switch: "THEME SONG - OFF." Pip asks if the jingle can be used only at the beginning and end. Nema says yes, but only when the director presses the studio cue.

ACT THREE

Miri reviews the three cards. Pip names the triangle without singing. Dot names the circle. Box names the square. The children clap. Nema does not restart the theme; the studio cue stays off.

MIRI: We learned three shapes, and we stopped when we were finished.

PIP: Can I sing one more time?

MIRI: After the credits.

Pip waits until Nema says the take is finished and assumes the camera is off. He sings one line. Nema points to the red live light. Pip freezes mid-note.

TAG

Dot rolls toward the exit. Box carries the blocks. Pip follows, humming his own tune and leaving the vest with Nema.

FINAL IMAGE: Pip walks out humming the triangle jingle live while the discarded vest stays silent in Nema's hand.
