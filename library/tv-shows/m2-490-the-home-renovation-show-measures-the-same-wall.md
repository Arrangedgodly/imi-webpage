# The Home Renovation Show Measures the Same Wall

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home renovation programs that repeatedly reveal a measured room as if the tape measure has uncovered a dramatic secret*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Deni, a gibbon; designer Mava, an orangutan; carpenter Koro, a macaque; homeowner Rill, a capuchin; camera operator Nala, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. LIVING ROOM - DAY

Deni holds a tape measure against one wall. The room is empty except for a sofa and a large potted fern.

DENI: We have a surprising dimension.

KORO: It is twelve feet.

[The camera zooms in on the tape. The number reads twelve feet.]

ACT ONE

INT. LIVING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Homeowner Rill wants a reading corner that leaves the door clear. Mava sketches a shelf, a lamp, and a chair. Koro measures the wall and says the shelf fits. Deni asks for a reveal. Nala films the wall, then pans back as if the measurement has changed.

RILL: The wall was twelve feet before the camera moved.

Deni explains that each segment needs a discovery. Mava proposes a second shelf. It blocks the door. Koro measures the doorway and points out the problem. Deni asks him to check again for a better angle. The doorway remains the same width. Rill tries to open the door; the shelf bumps it and spins the tape measure from Koro's hand.

ACT TWO

INT. DESIGN TABLE - LATER

Mava revises the plan. She places the shelf on the opposite wall and moves the chair beside the window. Rill tests the route from the door. The chair is in the way. Deni asks for a dramatic music cue while Mava moves it six inches. Nala films the chair from low down; the move appears enormous. Rill walks the route. It is clear.

DENI: We have solved the room.

The producer's voice from offscreen asks for a complication. Koro discovers that the tape measure has been stretched across the hallway by the camera crew. A grip steps over it, and the metal blade retracts with a loud snap. Everyone looks at the empty wall. Deni says the room has changed again.

Mava asks for the floor plan. Rill marks the shelf, chair, and clear doorway with removable tape. Koro measures the shelf once. It fits. Deni wants a close-up of the number, but Nala frames the end of the tape upside down. The display reads 21 instead of 12. Deni announces a surprise twenty-one-foot wall. Rill turns the tape over so the numbers face up.

RILL: We have a twelve-foot wall and a camera that needs glasses.

Nala adjusts focus. The number is clear. Deni asks for one more measurement, this time of the potted fern. Koro refuses; the fern is not part of the renovation. Mava puts the plant on the shelf to check the height. It fits, and Rill says the reading corner now has shade.

ACT THREE

INT. LIVING ROOM - AFTERNOON

The shelf is secured to the wall. The chair sits beside the window. The doorway opens fully. Rill walks in carrying a book, sits down, and reads. Deni begins the reveal music. Rill keeps reading. Mava points out the lamp is still unplugged. Koro connects it to the outlet without moving the furniture. The lamp turns on.

Deni asks Rill to react to the completed room. Rill closes the book and says it is comfortable. The camera crew waits for a bigger reaction. Deni asks whether the shelf could be a surprise. Rill points to the shelf, which has been in frame since the opening. Nala slowly pans to it anyway. Koro places the tape measure back on the shelf. It unrolls across the room, reaches the door, and stops just before the threshold.

KORO: Twelve feet. Still.

Deni tries to call for a second reveal. Mava hands him the floor plan instead. It shows the same shelf, chair, and clear path. Deni turns it to camera and reads the title: FINAL LAYOUT. Rill opens the book again. The crew lowers the music and records the quiet room tone.

TAG

INT. LIVING ROOM - AFTER THE SHOOT

Nala packs the camera. Koro retracts the tape measure. Deni asks if the next room has a surprise dimension. Rill points to the kitchen, where a narrow doorway is plainly marked on the plan. Deni studies the plan and asks if the doorway is expected to stay there. Koro folds the tape measure and walks out through it. The camera tries to follow, but Deni leaves the shot and holds the door open.

FINAL IMAGE: The tape measure rests coiled on the shelf beside Rill's book, while the camera frames the open doorway without a dramatic zoom.
