# The Courthouse Has a Studio Audience

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom reality shows that turn a small neighbor dispute into a verdict-first spectacle*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A televised neighborhood court hears a dispute over a shared garden hose, while the host pushes for a dramatic ruling before either neighbor finishes speaking.

Cast: Judge Pava, a macaque; host Rell, a gibbon; claimant Domi, a capuchin; neighbor Kesh, a baboon; and bailiff Tali, a squirrel monkey.

Episode: "The Hose Line"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY COURT STUDIO - DAY

Domi holds a coiled hose. Kesh stands beside an empty watering can. Rell turns to the camera.

RELL: One hose. Two gardens. Who crossed the line?

JUDGE PAVA: We have not heard the facts yet.

DOMI: The hose reached my garden first.

KESH: It is connected to my tap.

[TALI UNCOILS THE HOSE. IT REACHES BOTH PLANTS AND THE JUDGE'S BENCH.]

JUDGE PAVA: Please keep it away from the court reporter.

ACT ONE

Rell directs Domi to the witness chair. Pava checks the hose for labels.

DOMI: Kesh said I could water my plot before lunch.

KESH: I said I could lend the hose after I filled my can.

RELL: Did he say "before lunch" or "after lunch"?

KESH: I said after I filled my can.

DOMI: It was empty.

[TALI HOLDS UP THE CAN. A LITTLE WATER SLOSHES INSIDE.]

RELL: We have a contradiction.

JUDGE PAVA: We have a can with water in it.

DOMI: He filled it after I started watering.

KESH: I filled it while she was watering.

ACT TWO

INT. COMMUNITY COURT STUDIO - LATER

Rell puts two garden maps on an easel. One shows Domi's plot; the other shows Kesh's tap. The hose loops around the easel leg.

RELL: This diagram proves the hose crossed onto Domi's side.

JUDGE PAVA: The hose is connected to Kesh's tap. It has to cross the path.

DOMI: I put it back after watering.

KESH: It was still stretched across my beans.

RELL: Was there damage?

KESH: The hose flattened one bean stem.

DOMI: I moved it as soon as I saw.

[TALI LIFTS THE HOSE. A SINGLE BEAN LEAF IS CAUGHT IN THE LOOP.]

RELL: Exhibit A.

JUDGE PAVA: Exhibit A is a leaf.

RELL: The audience likes exhibits.

JUDGE PAVA: The court likes useful evidence.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COURT STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Pava asks Tali to lay the hose along the marked path. Rell adds dramatic music. The hose pulls a small planter off the set.

JUDGE PAVA: Stop the music.

RELL: The planter fell at the crucial moment.

TALI: The hose was wrapped around the stand.

DOMI: I did not pull it.

KESH: I did when I moved the watering can.

RELL: So the defendant caused the planter to fall?

KESH: The planter is a prop.

JUDGE PAVA: And nobody's garden was damaged by the fall.

[RELL LOWERS THE EXHIBIT CARD.]

DOMI: We can agree to lay the hose along the path.

KESH: And I will coil it after I fill the can.

Rell places a red buzzer between the neighbors. Pava puts the garden map away.

RELL: Before the ruling, each of you may make a final statement.

DOMI: I need water before the leaves droop.

KESH: I need the path clear so I can reach my beans.

RELL: Can either of you deliver that as a closing argument?

DOMI: I want to water before lunch.

KESH: I want the hose put back afterward.

[PAVA SLIDES THE BUZZER AWAY.]

JUDGE PAVA: Those are the facts and the agreement.

RELL: We have no audience vote?

JUDGE PAVA: The audience is not watering the garden.

TALI: The can is full now.

DOMI: Then I can start.

[DOMI SETS THE WATERING CAN BESIDE THE HOSE. KESH COILS THE HOSE ALONG THE EDGE OF THE STUDIO SET.]

KESH: It stays off the beans.

DOMI: I can fill the can first.

RELL: We have a live demonstration.

JUDGE PAVA: We have neighbors solving the problem.

RELL: You are settling before the verdict.

JUDGE PAVA: That is allowed.

ACT THREE

INT. COMMUNITY COURT STUDIO - FINAL RULING

Pava examines the hose and the watering can. Domi and Kesh stand on opposite sides of the bench.

JUDGE PAVA: Both neighbors may use the hose. It must stay off the beans, and the user coils it after filling the can.

RELL: No fine?

JUDGE PAVA: No proven loss.

KESH: I can water first today.

DOMI: Fine. I will use it after.

[THEY SHAKE HANDS. THE HOSE DROOPS OFF THE BENCH AND UNCOILS ACROSS THE STUDIO.]

TAG

Tali coils the hose. Rell tries to stand on the dramatic mark. The coil rolls away, leaving him beside the empty planter.

RELL: The hose has appealed.

JUDGE PAVA: The hose is not a party.
