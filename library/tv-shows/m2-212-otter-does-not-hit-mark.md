# The Otter Does Not Hit Its Mark

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nature documentary crews that stage animal behavior to meet a scripted camera schedule*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A wildlife crew wants an otter to slide down the same bank for a perfect shot, but their repeated resets make the crew the spectacle.

Cast: Narrator Seli, a gibbon; field producer Mako, a macaque; camera operator Diri, a capuchin; and local ranger Venn, a baboon.

Episode: "The Planned Slide"

COLD OPEN

EXT. RIVERBANK - DAWN

Diri aims a camera at a muddy bank. Mako holds a marker flag beside the water.

MAKO: The otter should enter from the left, slide down, and splash at the rock.

VENN: Otters do not take direction.

SELI: At first light, the otter approaches its usual route.

[Diri points the camera. A squirrel monkey walks into the frame, takes the marker flag, and carries it away.]

DIRI: The flag has moved.

VENN: The monkey is carrying it.

SELI: A sign of territorial competition.

VENN: It is a flag.

ACT ONE

Mako sets a second marker. Diri waits behind a fern. Seli records narration into a handheld microphone.

MAKO: We need the otter to cross the patch of light.

VENN: It is cloudy.

MAKO: The schedule calls for golden hour.

SELI: The sky has chosen a different lighting plan.

[Diri's microphone picks up a low rumble. Everyone freezes.]

DIRI: Otter vocalization?

VENN: My stomach.

MAKO: Can you wait until the take?

VENN: I can try.

[A REAL OTTER SURFACES, CARRIES A FISH, AND SWIMS PAST THE MARKER.]

SELI: The otter has caught its breakfast.

MAKO: It missed the slide.

VENN: It ate. That's the activity.

ACT TWO

EXT. RIVERBANK - LATER

Mako moves the marker beside the otter's path. Venn takes it away. Diri adjusts a long lens.

MAKO: The marker tells us where to frame.

VENN: It tells the animal where not to go.

MAKO: We are not asking it to do anything.

DIRI: It has turned around.

[THE OTTER CLIMBS OUT ON THE FAR BANK. MAKO MOVES QUICKLY TO FOLLOW, KNOCKING OVER A LIGHT STAND.]

SELI: The crew now enters the habitat.

VENN: The crew stays on the path.

MAKO: We need the shot from the other side.

VENN: The bridge is closed for repairs.

[MAKO LOOKS AT A HANDWRITTEN SIGN: "BRIDGE CLOSED." Diri pans toward the bridge, where two maintenance workers are tightening a rail.]

DIRI: We have a bridge repair sequence.

MAKO: We have an otter sequence.

SELI: The otter has disappeared.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. RIVERBANK - NOON

The crew waits behind a screen. Mako studies the shot list. Venn takes the marker flag and folds it into a small square.

MAKO: The list says feeding, grooming, then slide.

VENN: The otter ate. It groomed. It left.

DIRI: I have footage of the crew trying not to move.

MAKO: The crew is not the subject.

SELI: The ranger listens for movement along the bank.

VENN: I hear the otter in the reeds.

MAKO: Can we get the lens around the screen?

[DIRI EXTENDS A MONOPOD. IT CATCHES ON A VINE AND PULLS A LEAF INTO THE FRAME.]

DIRI: The leaf is in the shot.

VENN: It is a riverbank.

MAKO: We need a clean view.

SELI: The view is where the animal is.

[A SPLASH SOUNDS. THE CREW LEANS TOWARD THE SAME OPENING. MAKO'S SHOT LIST SLIPS INTO THE MUD.]

MAKO: The schedule is wet.

VENN: The otter is still dry.

ACT THREE

EXT. OBSERVATION BLIND - AFTERNOON

The crew settles inside the blind. Mako puts away the marker flags. Venn opens the viewing slit. The otter reappears beside the bank and slides down naturally into the water.

DIRI: Rolling.

MAKO: Wait for the light.

VENN: The otter is moving now.

SELI: The animal returns to the river after feeding.

[THE OTTER SLIDES, SPLASHES, AND VANISHES. THE TAKE IS CLEAR.]

DIRI: We got it.

MAKO: It hit the mark.

VENN: There was no mark.

SELI: The crew has learned to wait.

MAKO: Can we do one more for safety?

VENN: The otter is gone.

DIRI: The camera kept rolling.

SELI: It recorded the quiet after the slide.

MAKO: That might be the best part.

VENN: The river does not need a retake.

[THE CREW HOLDS STILL. A DROP OF WATER FALLS FROM MAKO'S LENS COVER.]

DIRI: That was not the river.

MAKO: Keep it in the cut.

TAG

Diri films the crew tiptoeing out of the blind. Mako steps on the stolen marker flag, which is now stuck in the mud beside his boot.

SELI: The elusive primate returns to its territory.

VENN: That is your reflection.

[MAKO STEPS OUT OF FRAME. THE OTTER'S EMPTY SLIDE REMAINS CENTERED.]
