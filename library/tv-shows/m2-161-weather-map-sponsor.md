# The Weather Map Has a Sponsor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cable weather coverage that turns routine forecasts into all-day breaking-news spectacles*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Meteorologist Fara must report a narrow band of ordinary rain while the station's producer stretches it into a daylong storm event for a patio-furniture sponsor. The live map keeps shrinking as the graphics department enlarges the danger.

Cast: Fara, a macaque meteorologist; Doss, a gorilla producer; Miki, a lemur field reporter; and Seli, a capuchin graphics operator.

## Episode: "Storm Desk, Light Drizzle"

### Cold Open

[WEATHER STUDIO. A MAP SHOWS ONE SMALL BLUE PATCH OVER A PARK. A GRAPHIC READS "BREAKING: RAIN." DOSS STANDS BESIDE A PATIO UMBRELLA.] 

FARA: A brief shower may cross the east park after lunch.

DOSS: We have a storm desk now.

SELI: The patch covers one playground.

DOSS: Widen it to the county.

[FARA POINTS TO THE LEGEND. SELI ZOOMS IN. THE BLUE PATCH NOW COVERS THE WHOLE SCREEN.] 

FARA: The scale is missing.

DOSS: We will put it back after the sponsor read.

[SELI'S MAP HAS A SECOND LAYER FOR COUNTY WARNINGS. DOSS PRESSES IT ON, TURNING THE SMALL BLUE PATCH INTO A RED BLOCK.] 

FARA: That is the advisory layer from last winter.

SELI: It was still open in the graphics file.

DOSS: The color is stronger.

[FARA SWITCHES BACK TO THE CURRENT MAP. DOSS HOLDS A SPONSOR UMBRELLA OVER THE SCREEN AS IF IT WERE A SHADE.] 

FARA: We should show the updated timestamp.

DOSS: It says 10:42.

FARA: The forecast says 12:30.

[DOSS TURNS THE UMBRELLA. IT BLOCKS THE TIMESTAMP INSTEAD.]

### Act One

[MIKI REPORTS FROM THE PARK. SHE STANDS UNDER A TREE WITH A CLEAR SKY BEHIND HER.] 

MIKI: It is dry here. The shower is still west of the river.

DOSS: Can you stand nearer a puddle?

MIKI: There are no puddles.

[SELI ADDS A BLUE CIRCLE OVER MIKI'S HEAD. A NEWS BUG COVERS HER UMBRELLA.] 

FARA: That is a radar loop, not her location.

DOSS: Keep the dramatic circle.

[MIKI OPENS A FOLDING UMBRELLA. A BREEZE TURNS IT INSIDE OUT.]

MIKI: The wind is enough to turn a prop umbrella inside out.

DOSS: We have a visual.

FARA: We have a light breeze.

[THE SPONSOR CARD READS "STAY DRY ON YOUR DECK." MIKI IS STANDING IN A PUBLIC PARK.]

[MIKI WALKS TOWARD THE MARKED RAIN GAUGE. THE CAMERA OPERATOR FOLLOWS, BUT DOSS CALLS HER BACK TO THE PATIO SET.]

MIKI: The gauge is the reason we are here.

DOSS: The chair is the sponsor's product.

MIKI: The chair is dry.

[SHE SETS A FOLDING CHAIR BESIDE THE GAUGE. A SINGLE DROP HITS THE CHAIR BACK, THEN SLIDES OFF.]

MIKI: There is your demonstration.

### Act Two

[DOSS SENDS A CREW TO THE RIVER BRIDGE. FARA CHECKS THE RAIN GAUGE. IT SHOWS A SMALL AMOUNT.] 

DOSS: We need a flood marker.

FARA: The river is within its normal channel.

[SELI ADDS A RED WARNING TRIANGLE. FARA TAKES THE REMOTE AND REMOVES IT. THE GRAPHIC RETURNS WHEN DOSS PRESSES A SECOND BUTTON.] 

DOSS: That is the severe-weather package.

FARA: It is not severe weather.

[MIKI WALKS THE BRIDGE. A SINGLE DROP LANDS ON HER NOTE CARD. SHE HOLDS IT UP TO CAMERA.]

MIKI: One drop. I can still read the route notice.

DOSS: Can you say, "It is moving fast"?

MIKI: It is moving at the speed of clouds.

[THE PRODUCER'S UMBRELLA BLOWS AWAY ACROSS THE FIELD. MIKI CATCHES IT BEFORE IT REACHES THE WATER.]

MIKI: Your umbrella has traveled farther than the shower.

### Act Three

[FARA TAKES OVER THE FULL SCREEN. SHE PUTS THE MAP BACK TO COUNTY SCALE AND SHOWS THE SMALL BLUE AREA, THE HOUR, AND THE ALTERNATE PARK ENTRANCE.] 

FARA: A short shower is likely at the east park after lunch. The rest of the county should stay dry.

DOSS: We are losing the storm event.

FARA: We are giving people a useful forecast.

[THE SPONSOR UMBRELLA DROPS INTO FRAME BEHIND FARA, DELIVERED BY MIKI. ITS CANOPY HAS TURNED INSIDE OUT. SELI'S SYSTEM AUTOMATICALLY LABELS IT "LOCAL WEATHER." ]

SELI: The map agrees with the umbrella.

DOSS: Take that graphic off.

[DOSS SWAPS THE SPONSOR CARD TO A CLOSE-UP OF THE UMBRELLA. THE AIR VENT TURNS THE CANOPY INSIDE OUT AGAIN AND KNOCKS THE CARD TO THE FLOOR.]

SELI: Should I label that "gust"?

FARA: Yes. A small gust in the studio.

[THE RAIN PATCH MOVES OVER THE PARK. MIKI OPENS HER OWN SMALL UMBRELLA AND CONTINUES THE REPORT.]

### Tag

[THE SPONSOR'S PATIO UMBRELLA IS ANCHORED INSIDE THE STUDIO. A VENT BLOWS IT INSIDE OUT. FARA TURNS THE RADAR TO THE STUDIO AND POINTS.] 

FARA: Now it is raining here.
