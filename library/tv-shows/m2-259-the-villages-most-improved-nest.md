# The Village's Most Improved Nest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: makeover reality television, where a nest is redesigned for visual impact while its builder prefers practical materials*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: A makeover crew transforms a nest for a dramatic reveal, but the resident keeps replacing decorative twigs with softer material and a better roof.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; designer Odo, baboon; nest builder Nara, macaque; resident Seli, capuchin; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "A Nest to Remember"

### Cold Open

[THE CREW STANDS BEFORE A SMALL NEST.]

PAVA: This nest needs a bold new look.

NARA: It needs a roof.

ODO: We can do both.

SELI: I need it to stay dry.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE TEAM MEASURES THE NEST.]

Odo suggests a wider entrance to make the nest feel open. Seli says the current entrance keeps rain out. Pava shows a board with color swatches: "Acorn Gold," "Moss Green," and "Storm Gray."

NARA: The nest is made of twigs.

PAVA: We can add a colorful lining.

SELI: The lining is scratchy.

Tavi films the "before" shot. Seli moves a soft leaf into the nest. Odo removes it for the camera.

SELI: That was my cushion.

ODO: It blocked the view.

NARA: The view is not what the nest is for.

Pava says they will preserve the nest's original character while adding a showpiece element.

### Act Two

[THE DESIGNERS BUILD A DECORATIVE ROOF.]

Odo weaves a large branch across the top. It looks dramatic but sags over the entrance. Seli cannot fit through.

SELI: The roof is too low.

ODO: It creates intimacy.

NARA: It creates an obstruction.

Pava suggests a reveal curtain. Tavi hangs a strip of fabric over the entry. It catches on a branch and pulls the roof sideways.

[SFX: TWIGS SHIFT.]

PAVA: The reveal has begun early.

SELI: The roof has moved.

Nara and Odo hold the branches while Seli climbs out. Nara asks Odo to remove one decorative limb. Odo says it is the visual centerpiece.

NARA: It is pressing on the nest.

ODO: We can trim it.

Seli pulls it free herself. The nest settles into its original shape.

[THE CREW REVIEWS THE BEFORE FOOTAGE.]

Tavi plays the original shot beside the new nest. Pava says the finished design needs a strong reveal. Nara asks the camera crew to show the roof from the side, where its slope is visible.

PAVA: The side angle hides the new trim.

SELI: The trim is not important.

ODO: We could add a branch arch.

NARA: The entrance already clears the branch.

Odo places a curved twig above the entry. Seli tries to pass under it; the twig catches on her shoulder. She backs out and removes it.

SELI: The arch is too low.

PAVA: It looked elegant.

NARA: It does not fit the resident.

Tavi films Seli returning the twig to the supply pile. Pava tries to restore it for the camera, but Seli hands it back again.

PAVA: The reveal is losing decoration.

SELI: The nest is gaining space.

### Act Three

[THE CREW REBUILDS WITH NATURAL MATERIALS.]

Nara uses smaller twigs for a roof that slopes away from the entrance. Seli tests it by pouring a small cup of water over the top. The drops run off.

PAVA: The water test is not part of the reveal.

SELI: It is part of the roof.

Odo adds a soft lining made of dry grass. Seli settles inside, then asks for more room near the side.

NARA: We can widen the sleeping curve without changing the entrance.

PAVA: The nest will look less symmetrical.

SELI: I sleep in it, not the camera.

Tavi films the finished structure. The roof works; the entrance stays narrow; the lining is soft.

PAVA: The makeover is complete.

SELI: I would like to put my leaf back.

Odo returns the leaf to the nest. Seli arranges it.

[PAVA STARTS THE REVEAL AGAIN.]

The crew places a gold cloth over the nest. Seli tugs it away before the camera counts down.

SELI: The nest should stay open.

PAVA: The audience needs a reveal.

NARA: The reveal is the roof.

Odo films the finished nest from the branch. Seli enters, turns around, and settles onto the soft lining. The structure holds without shifting.

PAVA: The after shot is complete.

TAVI: We should leave the nest alone now.

### Tag

[THE HOST FACES CAMERA.]

PAVA: A nest can be beautiful and functional.

NARA: It was functional before.

ODO: It is more colorful now.

SELI: It is still a nest.

Tavi pans to the branch the crew removed. It rests beside the tree like a tiny sculpture.

PAVA: We have a bonus reveal.

SELI: That is the branch you took off.

[SFX: CAMERA CLICKS. WINGS FLUTTER.]
