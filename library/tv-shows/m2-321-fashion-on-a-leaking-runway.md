# Fashion on a Leaking Runway

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fashion competitions, judging-panel suspense, last-minute garment reveals, and runway walks where practical needs get ignored*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A fashion competition asks designers to create rainwear, then scores their outfits beneath a roof that leaks on the judges.

Cast: Host Nari, an orangutan; designer Luma, a lemur; designer Fenn, a capuchin; judge Vex, a macaque; judge Omi, a gibbon; and stage manager Kett, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The Weather Look"

### Cold Open

[RUNWAY. A MODEL WALKS UNDER A DRIPPING CEILING.]

NARI: Tonight, fashion meets weather.

VEX: And the weather has found our judging table.

A drip lands in Omi's tea. He moves his cup. The next drip lands in it again.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The contestants receive a challenge: design rainwear using only fabric from the costume room and a small basket of repair materials. Luma makes a cape with a hood that folds into a pouch. Fenn makes a wrap that can be tied around a branch or a person. The judges ask both designers to explain their inspiration in one dramatic sentence.

LUMA: I dislike wet sleeves.

FENN: I wanted to keep working outdoors.

Kett writes down "a battle against the elements" and adds thunder to the recap. Nari asks the models to walk the runway. The ceiling drips harder. The first model's cape catches a drop and sheds it onto the floor. The second model uses Fenn's wrap as a towel to dry the judges' chairs.

VEX: We need to see the garment in motion.

OMI: It is moving the water off the chair.

### Act Two

Kett lowers the runway lights for a reveal. The dark makes the models slow down, so Vex asks for a dramatic walk. They take three careful steps and stop at the puddle. Nari tells the crew to bring out the stage fans, but the fans blow the rain sideways through a window. The models turn their backs to the gust. The capes work; the judging notes get damp.

Luma's hood has a hidden fold. She demonstrates it by popping the pouch open. It catches a stream from the ceiling and becomes a small bowl. The audience applauds. Vex asks if the bowl is a design feature. Luma says it is a temporary solution to a temporary ceiling.

Fenn's wrap has two ties so it can be secured around a branch. Kett asks him to show the garment's versatility. Fenn ties it to the runway rail, creating a dry awning over the scoring cards. Omi slides his chair beneath it. Vex stays outside the awning to finish his critique. A drip lands directly on his score sheet.

VEX: The piece has protected the paperwork.

FENN: The paperwork was in the rain.

Kett asks for a close-up of stitching. The camera zooms on the seam while a model walks past, blocking the judges. Nari calls for the full look. The camera widens just as the ceiling drip starts again. The crew uses Luma's cape as a temporary canopy over the camera monitor. Luma watches her own garment protect the broadcast of her garment. Nari asks the judges to inspect the seams. Vex kneels for a close-up and discovers the floor is wet. Omi offers him a dry corner of Fenn's wrap. Vex gives the garment a higher score for keeping his trousers out of the puddle, then insists that was not the reason.

### Act Three

The judges confer beneath the makeshift awning. Vex wants a winner based on silhouette. Omi chooses based on whether the clothing works. Nari asks each contestant to walk once more. The stage manager places a bucket under the leak; it sits at the end of the runway like a trophy. Both designers step around it.

Omi awards the challenge to Luma for the convertible hood, while giving Fenn the show's first practical design commendation. Kett asks for a surprise confetti burst. Nari says not while the ceiling is leaking. The confetti cannon fires anyway; wet paper sticks to the runway. Fenn ties his wrap from rail to rail to keep the debris from sliding under the audience seats.

The models take a final walk under the dry awning. The camera assistant asks whether the cape can cover one more monitor.

VEX: The winning look has changed the room.

LUMA: The room is still leaking.

The judges move the table under the awning. The model walks one last time, dry from hood to tail. Everyone else follows under the borrowed wrap.

### Tag

[EMPTY RUNWAY.]

A final drip falls into the bucket. The bucket rings like a bell. Kett writes "sound design" on the recap card.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
