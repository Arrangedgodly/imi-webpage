# The Great Treehouse Swap

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: property-swap reality shows that treat a change of homes as an identity crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Two monkey neighbors trade treehouses for a day and discover that each home was built around a different climbing style.

Cast: Host Miri, a gibbon; householder Odo, a baboon; householder Pava, a capuchin; builder Nara, a macaque.

Episode: "The Branch Between Us"

COLD OPEN

EXT. TWO TREEHOUSES - DAY

Miri presents two tiny houses. Odo's has broad platforms. Pava's has narrow climbing ropes.

MIRI: Today, they trade homes.

ODO: For how long?

MIRI: One day and one reveal.

Pava looks at Odo's wide ladder.

PAVA: Where is the rope?

ACT ONE

Odo enters Pava's home. The doorway is narrow, and the rope ladder swings. He turns sideways to pass through. Pava enters Odo's house and finds a wide platform that makes her feel exposed.

PAVA: I can see the whole forest.

ODO: That is the point.

Miri asks both residents to unpack a bag. Odo places his fruit on Pava's small shelf; it tips. Pava hangs her bag from the rope; it swings into the doorway. Nara watches the structures and asks them not to force the furniture.

Miri asks what each home says about its owner. Odo says Pava prefers climbing. Pava says Odo likes space. Miri asks if either feels transformed.

ODO: I feel stuck.

PAVA: I feel visible.

ACT TWO

Nara proposes small adjustments. She adds a low shelf in Pava's treehouse and a hanging basket in Odo's. The camera crew wants a dramatic wall removal, but Nara says both homes are stable and no wall needs to go.

Miri asks Pava to test the shelf. She places her fruit on it; it stays level. Odo tests the basket. He hangs one banana, then two. The basket sways but does not hit the doorway.

Miri reveals a new challenge: each neighbor must make a snack in the other's kitchen. Odo searches for a flat work surface. Pava's house has none. Nara places a board across two supports. Pava tries to reach Odo's fruit basket but cannot without climbing. Odo steadies the ladder while she takes a piece.

The snack challenge ends with both neighbors sharing fruit on the branch between the houses. Miri calls this a lifestyle merger. Nara calls it a short walk.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. SHARED BRANCH - AFTERNOON

The neighbors discuss what they would keep. Pava likes Odo's platform for resting. Odo likes her rope for exercise. They agree to keep the new shelf and basket but return to their own homes at sunset.

Miri says the format requires a final reveal. She suggests swapping the house signs. Odo says the signs are already labeled correctly. Pava says she does not want to confuse visitors. The camera crew changes the signs anyway, placing Pava's name on Odo's broad platform and Odo's name on the rope house.

Nara catches the mix-up before either resident goes inside. She switches them back.

ACT TWO-C

The camera crew asks each resident to describe the emotional cost of leaving home. Pava says she misses her rope ladder, especially the way it swings when she climbs. Odo says he misses the wide platform where he can turn without brushing a wall. Miri asks if they feel trapped in the wrong house. Pava says no; she has only been there for a few hours. Odo says the narrow doorway is inconvenient but not a crisis.

Nara checks the temporary shelf in Pava's treehouse. One support is loose because the trunk curves. She adds a second bracket and tests it with the fruit basket. It holds. In Odo's treehouse, Pava hangs a basket from a branch, but the branch bends when she adds too much weight. Odo removes one fruit and the branch returns to its position. They agree to keep the basket light.

Miri asks which home should get the bigger budget. Nara says neither needs a major renovation. The houses work for their owners. Miri proposes one shared walkway. Odo says they already meet on the branch between them. The camera crew films the branch as if it were a dramatic property boundary.

ACT THREE

Odo returns to the broad platform. Pava climbs the rope ladder to her own home. Each uses the improvement they chose. The houses remain distinct, and the neighbors have a shared snack spot on the branch between them.

Miri asks if the swap changed their lives.

PAVA: It changed where I put the fruit.

ODO: That is a lot of change.

TAG

The crew offers to swap the camera van next.

NARA: It has wheels. Leave it on the ground.
