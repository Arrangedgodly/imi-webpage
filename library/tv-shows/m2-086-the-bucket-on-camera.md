# The Bucket on Camera

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: serialized true-crime documentaries, dramatic evidence boards, and expert reenactments*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A documentary team investigates the disappearance of the grove's communal water bucket, only to discover that it has appeared in every interview as a seat.

Cast: Narrator Sori, an orangutan; investigator Pex, a baboon; archivist Rila, a lemur; witness Mavo, a macaque; and camera operator Deni, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Where Did the Bucket Go?"

### Cold open

INT. COMMUNITY HALL, NIGHT

[A chair stands under a spotlight. Sori narrates in a low voice.]

SORI: For three days, the bucket was missing.

PEX: What bucket?

RILA: The water bucket.

MAVO: It was beside the door.

DENI: The camera was sitting on it.

[Everyone looks at the camera. It is balanced on a metal bucket.]

SORI: The evidence is closer than we thought.

PEX: It was here the whole time.

SORI: We will examine the footage.

### Act One

INT. EDIT ROOM

[Rila plays a clip of Mavo's interview. He is seated on the bucket.]

RILA: On Monday, Mavo sat on the bucket.

MAVO: I was sitting on a stool.

PEX: The stool has a handle.

MAVO: It is a bucket.

SORI: The witness may have mistaken the object.

MAVO: I filled it with water.

DENI: The interview used the water for a set plant.

RILA: The bucket was present on camera.

PEX: Then it wasn't missing.

SORI: The question is why no one recognized it.

[They play footage of Pex interviewing Rila. The bucket appears beside her.]

PEX: Why is the bucket beside you?

RILA: I was using it as a table.

SORI: The bucket has changed roles.

DENI: It was never a table.

RILA: It held my notes.

PEX: The evidence board says "LAST SEEN: MONDAY."

SORI: We have a timeline.

MAVO: The bucket is behind the board.

[They move the board. The bucket is behind it.]

### Act Two

INT. COMMUNITY HALL

[The crew stages a reenactment. Deni places the bucket near the door.]

SORI: The bucket leaves the room.

DENI: It is still here.

PEX: We will recreate its disappearance.

[Rila moves the bucket behind a curtain.]

SORI: The room is suddenly without a bucket.

MAVO: The bucket is behind the curtain.

SORI: The witness has interrupted the reenactment.

PEX: We need a clean take.

[Rila moves the bucket again. The camera follows it.]

DENI: The camera operator has tracked the subject.

SORI: A crucial clue.

PEX: Who moved it?

RILA: I did. We needed the floor clear.

MAVO: I thought Pex took it.

PEX: I was using it for the evidence table.

SORI: Everyone used the bucket.

DENI: The bucket has appeared in every interview.

PEX: We need to ask the question differently.

SORI: What happened to the water?

[Mavo points to a potted plant.]

MAVO: We watered it.

RILA: The bucket was emptied.

DENI: The bucket was not missing. It was in use.

### Evidence review

INT. COMMUNITY HALL

[Pex pins photos to a board: the bucket by the door, beside the camera, and under Mavo.]

PEX: The bucket was in three places.

MAVO: It was moved between interviews.

RILA: The camera was on the bucket in two shots.

DENI: The camera was on a stool in one shot.

SORI: The timeline is incomplete.

PEX: We need a final witness.

[They interview Kett, an elderly groundskeeper, off camera.]

KETT: I carried the bucket to water the plant.

PEX: When?

KETT: During your interview.

SORI: The camera never showed Kett.

DENI: It showed the plant.

RILA: The plant is in the background of every interview.

MAVO: The bucket watered it.

PEX: The plant has been a silent witness.

SORI: It cannot speak.

KETT: It can grow.

[The crew compares the plant's leaves in each photograph. One leaf is visibly larger.]

PEX: We have established the order.

SORI: The bucket moved from the door to the plant, then to the camera.

DENI: The camera was last.

### Act Three

INT. COMMUNITY HALL

[The team records a conclusion. The bucket sits in the center of the room.]

SORI: The investigation found no theft.

PEX: The bucket moved from door to table to interview seat.

RILA: It carried water, notes, and the camera.

MAVO: It was never out of the building.

DENI: We should label it.

PEX: A label might change the evidence.

SORI: We can put a label on the handle.

RILA: It says "COMMUNITY BUCKET."

MAVO: That will help.

PEX: It may also create a new mystery if the label comes off.

SORI: Then we will keep the footage.

DENI: The footage shows me sitting on it.

PEX: You were sitting on the camera.

DENI: The camera was on the bucket.

SORI: The bucket has become part of the story.

MAVO: The bucket is the story.

### Tag

INT. COMMUNITY HALL, NEXT DAY

[The bucket is labeled and filled with water. The camera sits on a stool beside it.]

SORI: The case is closed.

[The camera operator picks up the bucket, and the label falls off.]
