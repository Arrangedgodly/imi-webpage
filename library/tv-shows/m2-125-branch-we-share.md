# The Branch We Share

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised family-reunion reveals that build suspense around ordinary records and treat every shared trait as proof of a dramatic secret*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Leko plans a surprise reunion between two neighboring macaque families whose old photo albums were filed together. Archivist Sura wants to check the labels before anyone announces a long-lost connection.

Cast: Leko, a macaque host; Sura, a lemur archivist; Nomi, a gorilla gardener; and Pavi, a capuchin photographer.

## Episode: "The Photograph in the Wrong Album"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A LARGE ENVELOPE SITS ON A PEDESTAL. LEKO WHISPERS TO CAMERA.]

LEKO: Tonight, one photograph may reveal a family secret.

[SURA OPENS THE ENVELOPE. IT CONTAINS A GROUP PHOTO OF A GARDEN CLUB.]

SURA: It reveals a garden club.

LEKO: But which family?

[SURA TURNS THE PHOTO OVER. THE BACK READS "NORTH GROVE CLUB, 1987."]

NOMI: My family lived in South Grove.

[LEKO SLOWLY TURNS TOWARD CAMERA.]

LEKO: The mystery deepens by one direction.

### Act One

[LEKO STANDS BETWEEN TWO CHAIRS. A CURTAIN HIDES PAVI.]

LEKO: Nomi, we have found an old portrait with someone who shares your smile.

NOMI: Everyone smiles the same way in a group photo.

[LEKO SHOWS THE PHOTO. ONE PERSON HAS THEIR EYES CLOSED.]

LEKO: There is a resemblance.

SURA: The person is squinting in the sun.

[PAVI ENTERS HOLDING THE ORIGINAL PHOTO ALBUM.]

PAVI: I found the label. It belongs to the community archive, not Nomi's family.

LEKO: Let us not rush to conclusions.

SURA: We already checked the label.

[LEKO ASKS NOMI TO SIT FOR A "REACTION SHOT." SHE SITS, THEN GETS UP TO READ THE DATE.]

LEKO: What are you feeling?

NOMI: I am trying to read the small print.

[PAVI HANDS HER A MAGNIFYING GLASS. LEKO MOVES THE CAMERA CLOSER, BUT THE GLASS MAGNIFIES HIS OWN EYE.]

NOMI: This was taken before I was born.

LEKO: A powerful clue.

SURA: A calendar.

### Act Two

[THE PRODUCTION TEAM PLACES TWO FAMILY TREES ON EASELS. ONE IS LABELED NORTH GROVE, THE OTHER SOUTH GROVE.]

LEKO: We have confirmed that both families grow figs.

NOMI: Most of the neighborhood does.

SURA: And the names in this album are members of the garden club.

[LEKO POINTS TO A PAIR OF IDENTICAL HATS IN THE PHOTO.]

LEKO: Could this matching headwear mark a hidden connection?

NOMI: They were club hats.

[PAVI CHECKS THE ARCHIVE BOX. THE HATS HAVE A RECEIPT TAPED TO THEM.]

PAVI: The club ordered twelve. Here is the invoice.

LEKO: We will reveal the result after the break.

[SURA TAKES THE TREES OFF THE EASELS AND PUTS THEM BACK IN THEIR FOLDERS.]

LEKO: You cannot remove the family trees during the reveal.

SURA: They are records, not props.

[THE CREW RETURNS FROM BREAK. LEKO OPENS A BOX WITH A SMALL KEY. INSIDE ARE MORE PHOTOS, ALL LABELED WITH CLUB EVENTS.]

LEKO: A deeper secret.

SURA: Three more gardening meetings.

NOMI: I can identify that one. We planted the north hedge.

[LEKO HOLDS UP A GROUP PHOTO. NOMI POINTS OUT HER GRANDFATHER IN A CLUB APRON.]

LEKO: Did he know the other family?

NOMI: He knew everyone who borrowed his spade.

SURA: The sign-out sheet is in the box.

[LEKO OPENS THE FOLDER. IT LISTS THE SPADE AS RETURNED ON MONDAY.]

NOMI: He joined the club after retiring.

LEKO: And the other family?

NOMI: They joined the same club.

### Act Three

[LEKO FACES BOTH FAMILIES, WHO HAVE BEEN WAITING OFF CAMERA.]

LEKO: We cannot confirm a hidden family relationship.

SURA: We can confirm the albums were stored together by mistake.

PAVI: The archive clerk copied the wrong label.

LEKO: Would you still like to meet?

NOMI: We have been neighbors for years.

[THE TWO FAMILIES GREET ONE ANOTHER. SOMEONE OFFERS A SEEDLING.]

LEKO: This is not the reveal we planned.

SURA: It is the one we documented.

[LEKO READS THE CLUB RECORD. THE PHOTOGRAPHER WAS LISTED AS "P. NOMA," NOT "N. POMA." PAVI POINTS TO THE SPELLING ON THE BACK OF THE PHOTO.]

PAVI: The name was also filed under the wrong letter.

LEKO: A second mystery.

SURA: A filing correction.

### Tag

[LEKO HOLDS UP THE TWO ALBUMS.]

PAVI: I will put one in the archive and one in Nomi's family box.

SURA: Check the labels first.

[PAVI READS THEM, THEN TURNS THE ALBUMS SO THE LABELS FACE OUT.]

LEKO: A satisfying ending.

NOMI: A useful shelf.

[THE FAMILIES CARRY THE PHOTOS TO THE COMMUNITY TABLE.]

LEKO: Next week, we investigate why the garden shed has two keys.

NOMI: One is for the shed. One is for the gate.

[SURA LABELS THEM. PAVI TAKES A PHOTO.]

LEKO: We may have to reschedule the mystery.
