# Rescue Route: Repeat

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised rescue reality shows, dramatic rope work, and replayed near-misses*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A rescue crew responds to a troop's stranded picnic basket on a low branch, but the broadcast's slow-motion replay makes a simple retrieval look like a cliffside emergency.

Cast: Rescue lead Nara, a gorilla; rope specialist Miko, a gibbon; field host Tavi, a macaque; camera editor Suli, a capuchin; and caller Rell, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Basket Over the Water"

### Cold open

EXT. CREEK BANK, DAY

[A picnic basket hangs from a low branch above a shallow creek. Tavi speaks solemnly to camera.]

TAVI: The basket is stranded above dangerous water.

NARA: The water reaches my ankles.

MIKO: The branch is two feet high.

SULI: We have a slow-motion replay ready.

[The basket slips. Miko catches it.]

TAVI: A daring rescue.

NARA: It was a basket.

### Act One

EXT. CREEK BANK

[Rell points to the branch. Nara checks the rope.]

RELL: I threw the basket to the far bank.

TAVI: Why?

RELL: I wanted to keep it out of the ants.

NARA: There are no ants.

RELL: There were ants in the ad.

MIKO: The basket is not heavy.

NARA: We can reach it from here.

TAVI: Our crew will use a safety line.

NARA: A safety line is not necessary.

MIKO: It is part of the procedure.

[He ties the rope around his waist.]

TAVI: The specialist prepares for a descent.

NARA: It is not a descent.

MIKO: The line is secure.

[He steps to the branch and reaches for the handle.]

### Act Two

EXT. CREEK BANK

[Suli shows a replay on a small monitor. Miko's hand reaches toward the basket in slow motion.]

TAVI: The moment of contact.

NARA: He has already picked it up.

SULI: We can replay it from another angle.

RELL: I can make it look farther away.

NARA: Don't.

[The replay zooms in until the basket fills the screen.]

TAVI: The basket appears to be floating over an abyss.

MIKO: It is floating over a creek.

NARA: Put the basket on the ground.

[He places it beside Nara. The rope remains tied around his waist.]

MIKO: I need to disconnect.

TAVI: A second challenge emerges.

NARA: The knot is on your back.

MIKO: I can't reach it.

RELL: I can untie it.

TAVI: The caller becomes the rescuer.

[Rell reaches around and frees the knot.]

SULI: We have another replay.

NARA: We do not need another replay.

SULI: The knot release was dramatic.

MIKO: It was a knot.

TAVI: The team has completed a double rescue.

NARA: We rescued a basket and a rope specialist from a rope.

### The rope protocol

EXT. CREEK BANK

[The rescue crew lays a rope along the ground before moving the basket.]

NARA: This is a hand line, not a cliff line.

MIKO: The camera makes it look longer.

TAVI: We should explain the safety steps.

SULI: Can I replay the knot check?

NARA: Not until we do it.

[They check the rope together. The basket is set safely on the ground.]

RELL: Is the rescue officially over?

NARA: Yes.

TAVI: We will now return to base.

[Suli presses replay. The monitor shows the basket slipping again.]

MIKO: The replay is showing the emergency after it ended.

TAVI: The audience needs context.

NARA: The context is that we already fixed it.

[The screen freezes on Nara holding a sandwich.]

SULI: That is a strong final frame.

### Act Three

EXT. PICNIC CLEARING

[The crew opens the basket. It contains sandwiches and a spare rope.]

RELL: I packed lunch.

NARA: You threw it over the creek.

RELL: I thought the other bank was closer.

MIKO: The basket is dry.

TAVI: The rescue is complete.

SULI: We should show the replay.

NARA: Show the whole sequence.

[The monitor plays the basket slipping, Miko catching it, and Rell untying the rope. A dramatic musical sting plays after each small movement.]

TAVI: A race against time.

NARA: It took twelve seconds.

MIKO: The replay takes two minutes.

RELL: We can eat during it.

[They sit down. Suli continues replaying the rescue.]

TAVI: The basket rests safely on dry ground.

NARA: The ground is damp.

TAVI: The basket rests safely on slightly damp ground.

MIKO: That is accurate.

SULI: I have one more angle.

NARA: No.

[Everyone opens a sandwich while the replay shows the catch again.]

### Viewer replay

INT. RESCUE BASE

[A viewer call comes through. Rell speaks on speakerphone.]

RELL: I watched the replay. The basket looked much higher.

NARA: It was over a creek.

RELL: The music made it seem like a canyon.

SULI: We can lower the music.

TAVI: Then the rope sounds louder.

MIKO: The rope was quiet.

[The editor lowers the music. The monitor shows a calm basket landing.]

RELL: That looks safer.

### Tag

EXT. CREEK BANK, AFTERNOON

[The rescue crew packs up. The basket is carried by hand.]

TAVI: No lives were lost.

NARA: No one was in danger.

[The replay monitor shows the basket in slow motion over the creek.]
