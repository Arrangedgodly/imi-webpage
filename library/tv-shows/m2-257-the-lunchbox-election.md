# The Lunchbox Election

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: teen school dramas, where a lost lunchbox sparks campaign rivalries, secrets, and a cliffhanger*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Two student candidates accuse each other of hiding a missing lunchbox, turning a classroom election into a campaign scandal.

Cast: Candidate Rumi, a macaque; candidate Pavi, a gibbon; student reporter Nara, a capuchin; teacher Odo, a baboon; hall monitor Seli, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Lunchbox Scandal"

### Cold Open

[CLASSROOM. AN EMPTY HOOK. TWO CAMPAIGN POSTERS.]

RUMI: My lunchbox is missing.

PAVI: I was campaigning.

NARA: Near the hooks?

PAVI: Everyone campaigns near the hooks.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE STUDENTS GATHER AROUND THE EMPTY HOOK.]

Nara asks Rumi when she last saw the lunchbox. Rumi says before the morning bell. Pavi says she saw the box beside the door but did not touch it.

RUMI: You were standing beside it.

PAVI: I was giving a speech.

RUMI: About improving storage.

PAVI: That is not a confession.

Seli checks the hook row. The lunchbox is not there. Odo says the election debate begins in ten minutes and asks the students to stay calm.

NARA: The debate is about honesty.

PAVI: Then we should have the debate.

RUMI: We cannot debate while my lunch is missing.

PAVI: It might be in the office.

RUMI: Who put it there?

Pavi looks at Nara. Nara says she was taking notes.

### Act Two

[HALLWAY. A CAMPAIGN LEAFLET LIES BESIDE A BENCH.]

Seli finds a smear of apple on the leaflet. Rumi says her lunchbox had an apple inside. Pavi says she handed out leaflets but did not carry fruit.

NARA: The smear links the campaign to the lunch.

PAVI: It links the leaflet to an apple.

Rumi notices that the smear points toward the classroom. They follow the trail. It ends at a recycling bin.

SELI: The evidence stops here.

ODO: Do not reach inside.

Rumi looks into the bin and sees a red corner. Odo uses a paper grabber to lift it out. It is a campaign flyer, folded around a sandwich.

PAVI: That is my sandwich.

RUMI: You said you had no fruit.

PAVI: I have a sandwich. That is not fruit.

NARA: The case has expanded.

Pavi admits she set down her lunch to hang her campaign poster. The lunchbox was already open. She did not take Rumi's.

[NARA HOLDS A PRESS CONFERENCE BY THE LOCKERS.]

NARA: I have recovered a leaflet from the hallway.

RUMI: You said it was evidence.

PAVI: It is a sandwich wrapper.

NARA: The wrapper carries a campaign slogan.

PAVI: "Better shelves for everyone." That is my slogan.

Rumi says the slogan appeared on the wall before the lunchbox went missing. Pavi admits she moved the poster because it was falling. Seli asks whether anyone saw her near the hooks.

PAVI: I was there. I said so.

RUMI: You also said you had no fruit.

PAVI: I still don't have fruit.

NARA: The missing apple links the lunchbox and the campaign.

PAVI: The apple was in Rumi's box.

Odo arrives with the classroom schedule. The debate is moved up because the gym is needed for an assembly. The candidates have four minutes to prepare.

RUMI: We cannot go on stage while the case is open.

PAVI: Then we take the case on stage.

NARA: That is not a debate format.

PAVI: It is now.

### Act Three

[DEBATE STAGE. THE CANDIDATES STAND AT PODIUMS.]

Nara asks each candidate what they would do about the missing lunchbox. Rumi says the school needs labeled hooks. Pavi says the hooks need a shelf underneath.

RUMI: A shelf would hide the boxes.

PAVI: A label would tell us whose box is whose.

ODO: Both are practical.

NARA: Can we return to the accusation?

Rumi says she saw Pavi near the hooks, but now realizes Pavi was hanging a poster. Pavi says she did not ask permission to use that wall. The candidates argue about campaign space.

SELI: The lunchbox is still missing.

Rumi checks beneath her podium. The lunchbox is under a campaign banner, where it was moved to clear the doorway.

RUMI: I put it there.

PAVI: You accused me.

RUMI: I forgot.

PAVI: Then you owe me an apology.

Rumi apologizes. Pavi accepts, then asks Rumi to help move the poster. The debate ends before the candidates deliver their closing statements.

### Tag

[THE BELL RINGS. STUDENTS LEAVE.]

Nara checks the lunchbox. The sandwich is safe, but the apple is gone.

RUMI: I ate it this morning.

PAVI: The evidence was consumed.

SELI: The case is closed.

NARA: The election is not.

Rumi and Pavi look at the empty voting box. It is missing.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
