# The Studio Quiz Locks Its Host Outside

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: game-show puzzle conventions, timed clues, and a host who enters the contestant route instead of the studio*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Host Varo, a baboon; contestant Rani, a macaque; contestant Bix, a gibbon; floor manager Luma, an orangutan; announcer Pip, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW HALL - DAY

A door clicks shut. Host Varo is on the wrong side of it, holding the questions.

VARO: I have locked myself out of my own show.

PIP (V.O.): Correct. That is worth no points.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The quiz show is filming a special in a studio built around three doors: the staff entrance, a contestant route, and a decorative door used as a clue prop. Host Varo arrives early, follows a trail of arrows for his entrance, and steps through the contestant route. The door closes behind him. His cue cards are now inside while contestants Rani and Bix wait on the set.

LUMA: The staff entrance is beside the lighting desk.

VARO: I followed the gold arrows.

LUMA: Those arrows lead contestants to the stage.

VARO: I feel included.

PIP (V.O.): Contestants, today's first clue is: what has a handle and opens?

RANI: A door.

BIX: A question.

PIP (V.O.): Both answers are under review.

The show starts its thirty-second clock. Rani tries the decorative clue door. It is a painted panel, not an entrance. Bix checks the contestant route from the studio side, but the latch opens only from the hall side. Luma keeps the real exit clear and asks the camera not to block the staff entrance.

LUMA: We need Varo back with his cards. We do not need anyone squeezing through scenery.

RANI: Is the clock counting?

PIP (V.O.): It is counting down.

BIX: The host is outside and the clock is inside. This is the first question with a location.

ACT TWO

Luma uses the crew intercom to talk Varo through the corridor. Varo can see three signs: STAFF, CONTESTANTS, and SET. A temporary arrow has been taped over STAFF and points toward the contestant door.

VARO: I trusted the arrow.

LUMA: The arrow is for the audience entrance, not the host.

VARO: It has the same handwriting as the staff sign.

LUMA: That is because the same person wrote both signs.

PIP (V.O.): New clue: which sign should a host follow?

RANI: The staff sign.

BIX: The one that says STAFF, not the one that points at a door.

PIP (V.O.): The judges accept both.

Rani asks Luma to stop the clock while they locate the corridor map. Luma pauses the game timer and hands the contestants a floor plan. It shows the staff entrance around the corner, but the show has a fake countdown clock displayed on a monitor by the contestant door. Bix spots the difference: the real timer has a small green pause mark; the monitor's clock is only a looping graphic.

BIX: The clock is pretending to be urgent.

RANI: Like the host?

VARO (INTERCOM): I can hear you.

LUMA: The real timer is paused. We are going to use the staff door.

Varo follows the actual STAFF sign, reaches the door, and passes his ID to Luma through the window before she buzzes him in. He enters with the cards, then takes a moment to re-stack them in order. One card has a question about a key. Another asks for the name of the door host just used.

VARO: The staff entrance.

PIP (V.O.): That answer is correct. No points, because it happened off the set.

RANI: Can the show resume?

LUMA: First we remove the arrow that points to the contestant route.

Varo peels the taped arrow off the STAFF sign. The adhesive leaves a gold triangle stuck to his sleeve. Bix quietly points it out. Varo asks whether the triangle can count as a wild card. Pip says only if it has a question printed on it.

ACT THREE

The production reopens the real game with the clock at thirty seconds. Rani buzzes first and answers a riddle about keys. Bix correctly identifies the key as a musical key, not a door key, and wins the round. Varo reads the card from the correct side of the set.

VARO: The host has learned to enter through the staff entrance.

RANI: That wasn't the question.

VARO: It is a bonus fact.

PIP (V.O.): Bonus facts do not score.

Bix wins the final prize, a brass desk bell. Luma places it on the contestant table, away from the doors. Varo asks if he can ring it to announce the show's return from break. Luma points out the audience is already in the room.

LUMA: Ring it once for the winner.

Bix rings the bell once. Varo hands the cue cards to Pip for the next segment and walks toward the staff exit. He pauses at the sign, reads it aloud, and enters the proper door without an escort.

FINAL IMAGE: The brass bell sits beside the paused timer while the host's discarded gold arrow is filed under WRONG DOOR.
