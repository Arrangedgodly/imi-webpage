# The Debate Under the Fig Tree

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local election debate programs and candidates whose slogans fail against practical questions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Two candidates debate who should manage a shared fig tree. Their campaign teams have prepared polished slogans, but the live questions concern shade, fallen fruit, and a branch that blocks the path.

Cast: Moderator Luma, a macaque; candidate Pella, a gibbon; candidate Varo, a capuchin; campaign aide Neri, a lemur; and residents of Fern Hollow.

## Episode: "A Platform for Every Branch"

### Cold Open

[A SMALL DEBATE STAGE IS BUILT BESIDE A FIG TREE. TWO PODIUMS ARE SET UNDER ITS LOWEST BRANCH.] 

LUMA: Welcome to the Fern Hollow debate. Tonight we ask who will manage the community fig tree.

PELLA: I am ready to lead from the branches.

VARO: I am ready to bring the tree back to the people.

[THE LOW BRANCH DROPS BETWEEN THE PODIUMS. BOTH CANDIDATES DUCK.] 

LUMA: Our first debate question has arrived early.

### Act One

[THE MODERATOR HOLDS UP A CARD. CAMPAIGN AIDE NERI STANDS BEHIND THE CAMERAS WITH A STACK OF SLOGAN CARDS.] 

LUMA: Pella, what is your plan for fallen fruit?

PELLA: A fair harvest, shared by all.

LUMA: Who gathers it?

PELLA: The community.

VARO: I propose a basket at the path and a weekly schedule.

PELLA: That is bureaucracy for fruit.

VARO: A basket prevents fruit from rolling into the stream.

[ONE FIG ROLLS DOWN THE PATH. A RESIDENT STOPS IT WITH A FOOT.] 

LUMA: The fruit has requested a basket.

NERI: Pella, read the next card: "Growth with dignity."

PELLA: Growth with dignity.

LUMA: What does it mean?

PELLA: It means the tree will grow with dignity.

VARO: Trees do not read campaign cards.

LUMA: Varo, your campaign promises "a branch within reach of every resident." How?

VARO: We will build a low platform.

RESIDENT: The path is already blocked by the low branch.

VARO: We can prune it.

LUMA: Who decides where to cut?

VARO: A trained gardener.

PELLA: A trained gardener costs more than a slogan.

### Act Two

[THE CANDIDATES WALK TO THE TREE FOR A LIVE DEMONSTRATION. THE CAMERA CREW FOLLOWS.] 

LUMA: The candidates will now show how they would handle the branch blocking the path.

PELLA: I will lift it.

[PELLA PUSHES THE BRANCH UP. IT DROPS BACK DOWN.] 

PELLA: The branch has rejected my proposal.

VARO: I will tie it back with this vine.

[VARO TIES THE BRANCH. THE VINE SNAPS.] 

VARO: The materials were not included in the budget.

RESIDENT: I have a longer vine from the garden shed.

LUMA: A resident has entered the policy discussion.

[THE RESIDENT HANDS VINE TO VARO. HE TIES THE BRANCH SECURELY TO A POST.] 

PELLA: That is a temporary fix.

RESIDENT: It is a safe path until the gardener arrives.

LUMA: The audience can now pass.

[THE CREW HAS TO MOVE A LIGHT STAND OUT OF THE PATH.] 

LUMA: The broadcast equipment is also blocking the path.

NERI: We did not prepare a slogan for that.

PELLA: We will clear it.

VARO: The crew should move the stand.

LUMA: Who is responsible?

[THE DIRECTOR POINTS TO THE CAMERA OPERATOR. THE OPERATOR MOVES THE STAND.] 

[NERI WAVES A CARD THAT READS "A FIG FOR EVERY FUTURE." THE WIND CATCHES IT AND FLAPS IT OVER THE CAMERA LENS.]

NERI: The card is for the closing statement.

LUMA: It is currently covering the live shot.

PELLA: I can reach it. [SHE CLIMBS THE PODIUM, GRABS THE CARD, AND THE PODIUM WOBBLES.]

VARO: Step down. A campaign card should not require a rescue plan.

PELLA: I am demonstrating branch access.

[THE RESIDENT HOLDS THE PODIUM STEADY WHILE PELLA DESCENDS.]

LUMA: The campaign has now required one resident, one vine, and a stable platform.

NERI: Can we call that a coalition?

RESIDENT: Call it a tripod. The third leg is missing.

[THE CREW ADDS A SANDBAG TO THE PODIUM. NERI TAKES BACK THE CARD.]

### Act Three

[THE CANDIDATES RETURN TO THE PODIUMS. THE LOW BRANCH HAS BEEN TIED OUT OF THE WAY.] 

LUMA: Final question. What is your first action if you win?

PELLA: I will listen to residents and build a harvesting schedule.

VARO: I will ask a gardener to inspect the tree and mark what can safely be pruned.

RESIDENT: Can we put up the basket now?

PELLA: Yes.

VARO: Yes.

LUMA: The candidates agree on a basket. The election continues tomorrow, but the path is open now.

NERI: Which slogan should we print?

PELLA: "Fruit for All."

VARO: "A Path That Works."

LUMA: Print both on the basket.

[THE RESIDENT PLACES THE BASKET UNDER THE TREE. A FIG DROPS INTO IT.] 

PELLA: My slogan has received the first donation.

VARO: The fruit did not vote.

LUMA: The ballot box is beside the camera stand.

[THE CAMERA OPERATOR MOVES THE STAND AGAIN, CLEARING THE WAY TO THE BALLOT BOX.]
