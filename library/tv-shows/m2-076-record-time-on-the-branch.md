# Record Time on the Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: record-breaking competition programs, official adjudicators, and endless category revisions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A team tries to set the record for the longest group grooming session, while the adjudicator keeps inventing new rules about what counts as grooming.

Cast: Host Tova, a macaque; contestant Lemm, a gibbon; contestant Pika, a capuchin; adjudicator Rell, a baboon; and timekeeper Doss, a gorilla.

## Episode: "One More Minute"

### Cold open

INT. RECORD STUDIO, DAY

[A large clock reads 00:00. Lemm and Pika sit beside each other, grooming carefully.]

TOVA: We are attempting the record for the longest cooperative grooming session.

RELL: The current record is four hours.

DOSS: The clock is not running.

TOVA: Why not?

RELL: The contestants have to begin.

PIKA: We began yesterday.

RELL: The record starts when the clock starts.

LEMM: We are still grooming.

DOSS: I will start the clock now.

[The clock begins. Rell adjusts his glasses.]

### Act One

INT. RECORD STUDIO

[Lemm picks a leaf from Pika's fur. Pika picks a seed from Lemm's sleeve.]

RELL: Are those actions part of the attempt?

LEMM: We are grooming.

RELL: The rules specify fur.

PIKA: The seed is in the fur.

RELL: It is in the sleeve.

TOVA: The adjudicator has identified a gray area.

DOSS: The clock is running.

RELL: I need to classify the sleeve.

LEMM: It is a sleeve.

RELL: Is it an accessory?

PIKA: It is part of my jacket.

RELL: The jacket is not part of the record.

TOVA: The record continues.

[The clock reaches one minute. Rell places a card on the floor.]

RELL: A grooming pause will reset the clock.

LEMM: We didn't pause.

RELL: You blinked.

PIKA: I can groom with my eyes open.

RELL: Blinking is not allowed.

DOSS: The written rules do not mention blinking.

RELL: The rules are in my binder.

TOVA: The binder is thicker than the rule sheet.

### Act Two

INT. RECORD STUDIO, LATER

[The clock shows 01:47. Lemm and Pika continue grooming. Rell circles them.]

RELL: The contestants have changed seats.

LEMM: We needed to reach the other side.

RELL: Position changes need approval.

PIKA: It is a cooperative session.

RELL: It is now a coordinated relocation.

TOVA: The record is facing a challenge.

DOSS: The clock continues.

[The clock reaches two hours. Rell stops it.]

LEMM: Why did you stop the clock?

RELL: We have a break.

PIKA: We did not request a break.

RELL: The adjudicator needs water.

DOSS: The rules permit official breaks.

RELL: We are not on break yet.

[He drinks water. Lemm and Pika continue.]

TOVA: The attempt continues during the official break.

RELL: It should not.

DOSS: The rules say only the contestants may pause.

RELL: I need a new rules page.

TOVA: The record may be broken by the paperwork.

LEMM: We are still grooming.

PIKA: I have removed three leaves.

RELL: Those were decorations.

TOVA: The studio set is losing its foliage.

### The official break

INT. RECORD STUDIO

[Doss turns over a card that reads "BREAK." Lemm and Pika stop.]

RELL: The attempt has paused.

DOSS: The rules say official breaks do not count against the record.

PIKA: We were still grooming.

RELL: You were not grooming.

LEMM: I was picking a leaf from Pika's ear.

RELL: That is personal grooming.

PIKA: What is the official definition?

[Rell opens the binder. Several pages fall out.]

RELL: I will need to consult the appendix.

TOVA: The contestants are waiting.

DOSS: The clock is also waiting.

RELL: The clock is stopped.

LEMM: We can resume when you find the word "ear."

### Act Three

INT. RECORD STUDIO, EVENING

[The clock reads 04:02. Rell studies the record certificate.]

RELL: The attempt is valid.

TOVA: The team has set a new record.

DOSS: Four hours and two minutes.

PIKA: Can we stop now?

RELL: You may stop.

[Lemm and Pika both stop grooming. The clock freezes.]

TOVA: A new record.

RELL: The certificate says "cooperative grooming."

LEMM: We cooperated.

PIKA: We groomed.

RELL: It also says no blinking.

DOSS: That rule was not in the official sheet.

RELL: I have added it.

TOVA: Can a record be updated after the event?

RELL: The adjudicator can revise the category.

PIKA: Then the record is four hours and two minutes of cooperative grooming with blinking.

LEMM: That is the record we set.

RELL: I will note the distinction.

DOSS: The clock has run longer than the program.

TOVA: We have achieved a record for television too.

[The studio clock keeps running after the contestants leave.]

### Tag

INT. RECORD STUDIO, NIGHT

[Rell sits beside the clock, reading a new rule sheet. The clock reads 04:03.]

RELL: I can set the record for reviewing the record.

[Doss restarts the timer.]
