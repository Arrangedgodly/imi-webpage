# Mystery at Willow Crossing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cozy village mystery*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: In a quiet village known for its flower show, a series of missing seed packets leads an amateur investigator to the only place everyone forgot to look: the community greenhouse's open roof vent.

Cast: Mrs. Bracken, a retired macaque schoolteacher; Pip, a young marmoset who runs the post office; Ada Finch, a gibbon gardener; Dr. Moss, a gentle baboon veterinarian; and Constable Reed, a lemur who prefers questions to accusations.

## Episode: "The Case of the Vanishing Marigolds"

### Cold open

INT. VILLAGE GREENHOUSE, MORNING

Ada opens the seed drawer. The marigold packets are gone. Mrs. Bracken pulls a corkboard from a canvas bag. Red string is already attached to one suspect's name.

ADA: Is that the mayor?

BRACKEN: He owns garden shears.

REED: So do most gardeners.

BRACKEN: We must begin somewhere.

PIP: The vent is open.

A bird hops across the roof and drops a seed husk. Mrs. Bracken writes "bird" on the board.

### Act one

Three seed packets have disappeared from the shared greenhouse: marigold, nasturtium, and poppy. Constable Reed checks the lock. It is undamaged. Mrs. Bracken interviews Ada while Pip listens from the post office steps. Village gossip has already blamed a new gardener who asked to borrow a different seed variety.

BRACKEN: Were you at the greenhouse?

ADA: I work here.

BRACKEN: At the hour of the theft?

ADA: I was watering plants.

REED: Did you see anyone?

ADA: A blackbird, two sparrows, and Pip.

PIP: I delivered a letter.

BRACKEN: What did the letter say?

PIP: It was sealed.

REED: That's not a clue.

BRACKEN: It could be.

PIP: It was from the seed club.

BRACKEN: A secret society?

PIP: A club that shares seeds.

REED: It has a sign-up sheet.

BRACKEN: Very secret.

The three enter the greenhouse. Mrs. Bracken inspects the cabinet with a magnifying glass. Ada points out the broken vent latch. A twig sits across the frame. The seed packets had been kept on the shelf below it.

### Act two

BRACKEN: The vent was open.

ADA: It sticks in damp weather.

REED: Someone could have entered.

PIP: Through the roof?

BRACKEN: The culprit may be nimble.

REED: Or small.

A bird lands on the sill and pecks at a paper strip. It flies to the willow. The group follows at walking speed. Mrs. Bracken asks Reed to mark the route on a map. Pip points to the nest directly above them.

BRACKEN: We will need a ladder.

REED: We have one in the shed.

BRACKEN: What if it is part of the plot?

REED: It is a ladder.

They find a nest lined with paper from the marigold packets. A few seeds are tucked between twigs. Dr. Moss, the veterinarian, arrives to check that the birds are safe. He asks the group not to touch the nest. The poppy packet remains missing.

MOSS: Look for husks near the ground.

PIP: There's one by the compost.

BRACKEN: The mouse did it.

REED: We haven't seen a mouse.

BRACKEN: I am revising my theory.

REED: That's allowed.

A trail of torn paper leads to a dry pocket in the compost bin. The poppy seeds are intact. A field mouse has eaten the paper and left the seeds alone.

### Act three

The committee repairs the vent latch and stores seeds in a closed cabinet. Dr. Moss moves the mouse toward the compost area, away from the greenhouse shelf. Ada apologizes to the new gardener for the rumor. The gardener agrees to help plant the remaining seeds.

ADA: We owe you an apology.

GARDENER: I wondered why everyone whispered.

BRACKEN: We have corrected the record.

REED: Please remove the red string.

BRACKEN: It is useful string.

PIP: It connects the mayor to a rake.

BRACKEN: That is his rake.

The flower show opens with uneven marigold rows. Children fill the gaps with extra seedlings. Ada receives a ribbon for shared work. Mrs. Bracken posts a note explaining that birds took paper for a nest and a mouse moved one packet to the compost. No one stole anything.

REED: The facts are on the board.

BRACKEN: The gossip is on the other board.

ADA: There is another board?

PIP: The bakery wall.

BRACKEN: We'll correct that too.

### Tag

At the show, Constable Reed finds a blue ribbon missing. Mrs. Bracken lifts her magnifying glass. A bird has woven it into a nest above the judge's hat.

REED: Do we open a case?

BRACKEN: Only if the bird enters the contest.

The bird chirps. Mrs. Bracken adds its name to the ballot.
