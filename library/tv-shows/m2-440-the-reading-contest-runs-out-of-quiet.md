# The Reading Contest Runs Out of Quiet

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: educational competition shows that score children on a behavior they cannot control*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; librarian Seli, a gibbon; contestant Pip, a capuchin; contestant Nari, a lemur; sound technician Vex, an orangutan; teacher Odo, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCHOOL LIBRARY - AFTERNOON

[Two contestants sit at separate desks. Mara holds up a card marked SILENCE. Vex wears headphones.] 

MARA: The quiet reading contest starts now.

[The library book return slot clunks. Pip turns a page.] 

VEX: The sound meter is already moving.

[Theme music.]

ACT ONE

MARA: Welcome to Read It Quiet, where students compete to make the least noise while learning.

SELI: Reading is not a competition.

MARA: The prize is a bookmark.

ODO: A very long bookmark.

[Pip opens a book. The pages stick together. Nari reads a line silently, then reaches to help him separate them.] 

VEX: We have a whisper.

NARI: I did not speak.

VEX: The meter picked up page friction.

[He shows the display. It has a needle and a paper roll, but no numbers.]

SELI: That is a tuning meter.

VEX: It is measuring reading ambience.

[Pip points at the book's illustration of a fruit tree. A real tree outside the window holds several birds. The birds call.] 

PIP: The birds are reading too.

MARA: No outside assistance.

ACT TWO

[The contestants move to the back table, farther from the window. Vex sets two foam mats beneath the chairs. Nari's chair rocks; she steadies it by gripping the edge.] 

NARI: This chair is loud.

VEX: The furniture has entered the contest.

[Odo whispers the rules: one point for every page read, minus a point for each sound. Seli asks how the show will know the readers understood the pages.] 

MARA: We ask one question at the end.

SELI: Then the scoring is about noise, not reading.

MARA: The title is right there.

[Pip opens his book. A small feather falls from between the pages. He picks it up and tucks it behind his ear. The bird outside hops to another branch.] 

PIP: I know where the feather came from.

VEX: Was that part of the story?

PIP: It is on the page.

[He shows the illustration. The book has a bird's nest. Nari glances at the window and sees a nest tucked under the gutter.] 

NARI: The real bird has a nest on the library.

MARA: Outside observations do not count.

SELI: They count as reading the room.

[The sound meter needle rises. Vex blames Nari's breathing. Nari holds her breath; Pip taps her arm. She inhales and the needle spikes.] 

VEX: We have a dramatic loss.

NARI: I was breathing.

[Odo tests the meter by tapping the table. The needle swings even higher.]

ODO: The table is louder than the contestants.

VEX: The table has a commanding presence.

[The contestants trade books for a two-minute speed round. Pip's book has a fold-out page that opens with a loud flap. Nari's book has a blank page where a drawing should be.]

NARI: Mine is missing an illustration.

SELI: The page turned over.

[Nari flips the page. The picture is there. The sound meter jumps.]

VEX: A major noise event.

PIP: We turned a page.

MARA: The meter says it is a tie.

SELI: The meter has a loose needle.

[She presses the meter's base. The needle stays still. Vex whispers a score to Mara.]

VEX: I recommend awarding the point to the louder book.

MARA: Books do not compete.

VEX: Then the louder desk.

[Odo moves the contestants' desks apart. A foam mat rolls under one leg and steadies it.]

ODO: The furniture has improved its score.

ACT THREE

[The final round begins. Mara asks each contestant to summarize what they read. Pip describes a bird building a nest; Nari describes a story about a stream.] 

MARA: Which contestant understood the book best?

SELI: Both of them read carefully.

MARA: Who was quieter?

[Vex rewinds the sound meter tape. It is a long strip of paper. He sees the meter recorded the return slot, the chair, and his own tapping, but almost none of the readers.] 

VEX: The quietest participant was the book.

NARI: The book did not have a mouth.

PIP: It still had a story.

[The outside birds call again. Pip answers with a small chirp, then stops.] 

MARA: That is a sound.

SELI: It is also a response to what he read.

[Odo awards each contestant a bookmark. The bookmarks are different lengths; Pip's reaches to the floor and catches under the desk leg. When he stands, the chair lifts with it.] 

PIP: My prize has pinned the furniture.

[Vex checks the meter. It stays still.]

VEX: The library is quiet now.

[The book return slot clunks again. Vex's meter spikes.] 

VEX: We have a winner.

MARA: The return slot?

VEX: It made the most noise and returned the most books.

TAG

[Seli places a sign on the table: "READING IS NOT SCORED." The sound meter needle moves when Mara puts down her microphone.] 

SELI: Add "and neither is the sign."

[Vex turns off the meter.] 

VEX: We have achieved silence.
