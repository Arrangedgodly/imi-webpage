# The Boat Show Launches the Model

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: boating programs that stage a dramatic sea trial before checking whether the boat is a scale model*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Piri, a capuchin; builder Rema, an orangutan; presenter Tavi, a macaque; producer Kalo, a lemur; harbor master Nima, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. BOAT SHOW SET - DAY

Piri stands beside a small wooden sailboat in a glass tank.

PIRI: We are ready for launch.

REMA: It is a model.

[The camera zooms in until the tank resembles open water.]

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Kalo asks Rema to describe the boat's full-size performance. Rema says she built it to scale and it has never carried a passenger. Tavi reads a card claiming it can cross the harbor in ten minutes. Rema asks who wrote the card. Piri says the sponsor provided it.

NIMA: The harbor is not in this studio.

Kalo points to the tank. Rema places the model in the water. It floats level. Piri announces a sea trial. Nima asks for the safety line. The boat is secured by a thin thread tied to the table.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Tavi prepares a wind machine. Rema asks him to keep it low; the model's sail is paper. Kalo wants a gust for drama. The fan blows the boat across the tank, straight into the glass. The hull is undamaged, but the sail folds.

PIRI: We have made contact with land.

REMA: You have pushed a model into a wall.

[The crew dries the sail. Nima asks if the tank can be moved to an open set. The tank is too heavy. Kalo proposes a camera projection of waves. Tavi projects a blue ocean onto the studio wall, behind the boat rather than beneath it.]

INT. SET - MOMENTS LATER

Rema patches the sail with a small paper strip. Piri asks whether it will hold. She clips the sail to a stand and uses a hand fan to test the patch in the air. The paper stays attached. Kalo asks for a gust; Nima keeps the wind machine unplugged. The boat stays on its stand.

Rema shows the scale markings. Tavi reads the sponsor card and finds it describes a full-size harbor launch. He replaces it with the model's actual dimensions. Nima draws a short route across the tank.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Kalo reads the production notes and learns the model was built for a design segment, not a sea trial. He asks Tavi to change the rundown. Tavi removes the dramatic launch countdown and writes FLOAT, STEER, RECOVER. Nima checks the safety thread. Rema looks over the patched sail; it is dry. The camera frame includes the tank rim and route card, so the model's scale is clear. Kalo asks the operator for a storm graphic. The operator leaves the blue studio backdrop plain. Tavi sets the timer beside the tank but does not start it. Rema places the model at the marked start point. The crew waits for the scheduled trial.

INT. BOAT SHOW SET - MOMENTS LATER

Kalo asks for a tighter lens so the tank looks larger. The camera zooms until the model fills the frame and the route card disappears. Rema steps into shot holding a ruler. It shows the tank is less than a meter wide. Piri asks Tavi to keep the ruler in frame; the scale is part of the test. Kalo slides a painted horizon behind the glass. The horizon line crosses through the boat's mast. Nima moves the backdrop back to its mark, but the camera still shows the tank edge. Kalo sighs and removes the fake horizon. Rema places a small buoy at the end of the measured route. The model remains on the start mark, ready for its single official crossing.


ACT THREE

INT. BOAT SHOW SET - AFTERNOON

The crew stages a proper model trial. Rema places the sailboat at the start. Nima holds the loose safety thread. Tavi starts a timer. Piri narrates the short crossing while the boat moves under the gentle fan. It reaches the far end and turns slightly. The timer records fourteen seconds.

PIRI: A successful harbor crossing.

REMA: A successful model test.

Kalo asks for a dramatic storm. Nima stops him and lifts the boat out of the tank. Rema checks the patched sail; it holds. The model is set on a stand beside the measured tank. Tavi updates the title card to MODEL SAILBOAT TRIAL. The sponsor card remains on a separate stand, no longer claiming a real harbor time.

TAG

INT. SET - AFTER THE SHOW

Kalo packs the wind machine. Nima coils the safety thread. Piri asks if the model can be launched again. Rema says after the sail dries.

FINAL IMAGE: The dry model rests on its stand beside the glass tank, with the short measured route drawn on a card.
