# The Talent Show Has One Entrant

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised talent competitions, audition montages, and producers who manufacture rivalry between people who never entered*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: HOST RHEA, a macaque; PRODUCER KEX, a baboon; CONTESTANT BO, an orangutan; JUDGE SURI, a gibbon; STAGEHAND TAVI, a capuchin; ANNOUNCER, a mandrill.

COLD OPEN

INT. TALENT STAGE - NIGHT

A spotlight finds Bo alone on the stage, holding a spoon and a glass of water.

HOST RHEA: Tonight, one performer will be eliminated.

PRODUCER KEX: We only have one performer.

HOST RHEA: Then we have half the contestants left.

Bo taps the spoon against the glass. A tone rings. The audience applauds.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

INT. GREENROOM - NIGHT

Kex stands before a screen showing the night's contestant list: BO. He adds three empty silhouettes beside Bo's name.

KEX: The viewers need a competition.

STAGEHAND TAVI: They also need contestants.

KEX: We have judges.

Suri enters holding three scorecards. One reads AMAZING, one reads NEEDS WORK, and one reads WATER.

SURI: I was told this was a cooking show.

Rhea explains Bo's act: he can identify any household object by its sound. Kex wants a backstory package. Bo taps the glass again, then taps a wooden spoon against a metal tray.

BO: Glass. Tray. Spoon.

KEX: That's not a backstory.

BO: I can tap out my backstory.

KEX: How long is it?

BO: Until someone stops asking.

ACT TWO

INT. TALENT STAGE - CONTINUOUS

The announcer introduces a "three-way showdown." Two stagehands wheel in empty chairs with contestant numbers taped to them. Rhea calls the first chair's name. The chair does not answer. She calls the second. Silence.

SURI: They have stage presence.

Bo performs. He taps a spoon against the glass, then a pebble against a bowl, then his fingernail against a wooden panel. The judges write their guesses. Suri says the final sound is a gourd. Bo reveals it is the stage manager's clipboard.

KEX: The clipboard was not approved as an instrument.

BO: It was approved as a clipboard.

The audience laughs. Kex signals for the rivalry package. The screen shows a montage of Bo staring at the two empty chairs. The voice-over says the other contestants "refused to back down." The chairs remain motionless.

RHEA: Bo, what do you say to your rivals?

BO: They have excellent posture.

Suri gives Bo the AMAZING card. Kex asks her to use the NEEDS WORK card for tension. Suri says the only thing needing work is the stage's soundproofing. Bo taps the clipboard. The sound carries into the greenroom, where a stagehand taps back. Bo listens, then repeats the pattern.

STAGEHAND TAVI: That was me.

BO: I know.

RHEA: Is this a duet?

BO: It is a sound check.

Kex asks the audience to vote between Bo and the chairs. Every vote appears under Bo's name because the empty-chair buttons were never connected. The host attempts to announce that the result is too close to call.

SURI: It is unanimous.

KEX: We need a suspenseful pause.

SURI: The pause was the empty chairs.

INT. GREENROOM - LATER

Kex orders the stagehand to bring out a golden chair for the final showdown. The host introduces it as the second contestant. Suri asks how the chair qualified. Kex says it survived every montage without changing expression. Bo sits on the chair, then taps its wooden arm. It makes a low hollow note.

BO: Oak. Slightly loose joint.

STAGEHAND TAVI: That chair is from the audience seating.

KEX: It has a compelling backstory.

Rhea asks Bo to perform one more sound. Bo taps the chair leg, then the trophy, then the metal latch on the stage door. The audience hears three notes. Suri writes a score. Kex tries to ask whether Bo can tap a dramatic rivalry. Bo plays two notes that sound identical.

SURI: They are both chairs.

KEX: One is the competitor.

BO: The chair is not signed up.

The producer points to the empty contestant buttons and asks the timekeeper to count the votes again. Every response appears under Bo's name. Rhea asks the audience to vote for the chair by holding up their hands. The chair wins no votes, but the camera operator accidentally frames the trophy beside it, making it look like a prize presentation.

SURI: We have a result.

KEX: We have a shot.

RHEA: We have one contestant.



ACT THREE

INT. TALENT STAGE - LATER

Rhea announces Bo as the winner and gives him a small trophy shaped like a microphone. Bo taps it against the spoon. The trophy rings at a new pitch.

BO: Metal. Hollow. Winner.

Kex tries to book Bo for a rematch against a second empty chair. Bo declines and signs up for the next show as the sound engineer.

FINAL IMAGE: Bo taps a mic stand to tune the show while the two numbered chairs wait under the eliminated-contestants sign.
