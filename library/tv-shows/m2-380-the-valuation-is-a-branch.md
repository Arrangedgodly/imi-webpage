# The Valuation Is a Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: startup pitch panels, inflated market claims, investor interruptions, and negotiations over a single percentage point*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: An orangutan entrepreneur pitches a clamp-on shade awning for park branches, but the investors keep arguing about whether the market includes every tree.

Cast: Founder Rono, an orangutan; host Pavi, a macaque; investor Teli, a gibbon; investor Sako, a baboon; investor Nemi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. PITCH STUDIO - NIGHT

Rono sets a small canvas awning on the table. He clamps it to a display branch. It opens over his head.

RONO: BranchShade gives a little shade where there is a branch.

SAKO: How many branches are in your market?

RONO: I brought one.

ACT ONE

Pavi introduces Rono's product: a lightweight clamp that holds a small canopy over a park bench, stall or picnic table. Rono demonstrates the clamp and opens the canopy. It shades only one side of the table.

TELI: Is it adjustable?

RONO: The clamp rotates. The fabric does not.

NEMI: What if the branch is too thick?

RONO: Then the clamp will not fit. We list the range on the package.

Sako asks for the total addressable canopy market. Rono shows a map with three parks and two outdoor markets. Sako says he wants the whole region. Rono says he has only tested the five locations on the map.

SAKO: A small map is not a big market.

RONO: It is a map of places that said yes.

ACT TWO

Teli tests the clamp on the display branch. It slips because the branch is polished plastic. Rono tightens the strap and explains it needs a firm surface. Nemi asks whether it works on a metal pole. Rono says a separate adapter is still being tested.

PAVI: That sounds like a second product.

RONO: It is a question I was asked.

Sako pulls a stack of blank order forms from under his chair. He says he can sell a million units through his cousin's tree service. Rono asks how many the service needs. Sako says he has not asked yet.

NEMI: I would rather hear the actual orders.

PAVI: We have one pilot agreement from the park.

RONO: For twelve canopies. Two are for the market stalls.

Teli asks to see one deployed. Rono clamps it to the studio display branch. The canopy opens and covers the investor panel's name placards. Sako pushes it aside; the stand tips. Rono catches the base and tightens the clamp.

RONO: The display branch is not anchored.

SAKO: The investors are.

ACT TWO-B

Nemi offers funding for a smaller share if Rono commits to testing the pole adapter. Teli offers less money for a larger share but wants to enter the outdoor market first. Sako offers a huge valuation in exchange for selling the product in every country with a tree.

RONO: Some trees are protected. Some places do not need shade. Some customers want a fixed awning.

SAKO: That limits the opportunity.

RONO: It describes the product.

Pavi asks Rono what he needs. Rono says he wants enough to manufacture the twelve units, test the pole adapter, and pay the local canvas maker for the first run. He declines Sako's offer. Teli asks if he will share the market list. Rono says yes, after checking the park's permission to share their contact details.

TELI: You are making a deal smaller.

RONO: I am making it fit.

Nemi asks about replacement fabric. Rono demonstrates how to unclip the canopy and swap the fabric panel. A corner catches on the studio branch. He asks Sako to hold the branch steady. Sako leans back; the entire stand begins to tip again. Nemi grabs its base while Teli catches the canopy.

ACT THREE

Nemi offers the requested funding for twelve percent, conditional on the park pilot and adapter test. Rono accepts. Teli says she would have offered the same terms, but Nemi got there first. Sako says his deal covered every tree. Rono reminds him that he declined it.

SAKO: You could have had a bigger valuation.

RONO: I could have had a bigger number.

Pavi announces the deal. The crew packs the sample canopy. Teli asks to buy one for her garden. Rono says she can order through the regular channel. She opens a phone app and finds the pilot product page not yet live.

TELI: When can I order it?

RONO: After we build it.

TAG

Sako studies the studio's fake tree and tries to clamp a business card to it. The clamp slides. Nemi points to the instruction label.

FINAL IMAGE: Rono carries the small awning offstage while the investors remain under the studio lights, debating the tree's market size.
