# The Soap Opera Ends at the Gate

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap operas that turn ordinary misunderstandings into cliffhangers at every commercial break*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: Two neighbors try to return a garden gate key, but every interruption turns the simple handoff into a season-long secret.

Cast: Mara, a macaque; Ivo, a gibbon; neighbor Tella, a capuchin; and narrator Sori, a baboon.

Episode: "The Key Between Us"

COLD OPEN

EXT. GARDEN PATH - DAY

Mara holds a small brass key. Ivo stands beside an open gate.

MARA: I found this by your shed.

IVO: That is the gate key.

MARA: I'll give it back.

[SORI STEPS BETWEEN THEM WITH A NARRATION CARD.]

SORI: But why was the key beside the shed?

IVO: It fell out of my pocket.

ACT ONE

INT. COMMUNITY TV SET - DAY

Tella watches playback on a monitor. Mara and Ivo stand on opposite sides of a paper garden.

TELLA: We need the audience to think the key opens a secret room.

MARA: It opens the gate.

TELLA: The gate can hide a secret.

IVO: It is a gate.

[THE KEY IS TOO LARGE FOR THE PAPER LOCK. IT FALLS THROUGH THE SET.]

SORI: The key has vanished.

MARA: It is under the stage.

TELLA: We can reveal it after the break.

ACT TWO

EXT. GARDEN SET - LATER

Mara looks under the gate. Ivo carries a watering can. Sori speaks from a platform.

SORI: Will the neighbors discover what the key unlocks?

MARA: The gate.

IVO: The gate is open.

TELLA: It needs to be locked for the scene.

[MARA CLOSES THE GATE. IVO TRIES THE KEY. IT TURNS.]

IVO: It works.

SORI: A shocking twist.

MARA: The key is returned.

TELLA: We still have twenty minutes in the episode.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COMMUNITY TV SET - AFTERNOON

Tella gives Mara a second key. Ivo checks his pockets. Sori holds a new card.

TELLA: This is the shed key. It looks almost the same.

MARA: The shed is over there.

IVO: I already have that key.

[SORI LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.]

SORI: Or does he?

[IVO TAKES A KEY FROM HIS POCKET AND OPENS THE SHED. IT IS FULL OF GARDEN TOOLS.]

TELLA: We have learned the shed has tools.

MARA: We can put the spare key back on the hook.

IVO: That would prevent this from happening again.

TELLA: There is no hook in the set.

[MARA HANGS IT FROM A NAIL. THE NAIL PULLS OUT OF THE CARDBOARD WALL.]

ACT TWO-C

INT. COMMUNITY TV SET - LATE AFTERNOON

Tella hands Sori a sealed envelope. Mara and Ivo stare at it.

TELLA: The network wants a secret for next week.

SORI: Is the secret inside the envelope?

TELLA: We do not know. It is a prop envelope.

MARA: We can open it.

IVO: It is sealed with tape.

[MARA PEELS THE TAPE OFF. THE ENVELOPE IS EMPTY.]

SORI: The secret is missing.

MARA: There was no secret.

TELLA: That is the secret.

IVO: We can write the key's name on the envelope.

SORI: The audience will ask why the envelope is empty.

MARA: We just answered that.

[TELLA PULLS A SECOND CARD. IT READS: "WHO TOOK THE SECRET?" SHE TURNS IT OVER. THE BACK IS BLANK.]

TELLA: We can save this question for the recap.

IVO: There is nothing to recap.

MARA: I returned the key. The gate opens. The shed has tools.

SORI: That is a complete story.

TELLA: It is only complete if we end the episode.

[SORI TAKES DOWN THE EXTRA CARD. THE EMPTY ENVELOPE FALLS OFF ITS STAND.]

MARA: The secret has left the set.

SORI: The audience may expect another question.

MARA: They can ask about the garden.

IVO: The gate opens. The shed has tools. The key needs a place to hang.

TELLA: Could we ask whether the gate will stay open overnight?

IVO: It will stay closed if I close it.

[IVO CLOSES THE GATE. THE BENT NAIL FALLS OFF THE CARDBOARD WALL AGAIN.]

MARA: The hook is the problem now.

TELLA: We can put the key on a ring and hang it on the inside latch.

SORI: We have one final scene.

ACT THREE

EXT. GARDEN SET - FINAL SCENE

Mara and Ivo stand by the open gate. Tella holds the bent nail and the two keys.

MARA: One key opens the gate. One opens the shed.

IVO: We can label them.

TELLA: That resolves the confusion.

SORI: But what is inside the shed?

IVO: A rake, a spade, and a watering can.

MARA: We know. We just opened it.

TAG

The gate swings shut. Mara holds up both keys.

MARA: Which one opens the gate?

IVO: The one labeled GATE.

[TELLA ADDS A LABEL. THE GATE SWINGS OPEN.]
