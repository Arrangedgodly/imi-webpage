# The Final Whistle Is a Squeak

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live sports broadcasts, instant replays, and overexcited play-by-play commentary*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A television crew covers the annual canopy relay, where a missing finish line turns a friendly race into an argument about what counts as a finish.

Cast: Commentator Vell, a baboon; commentator Suni, a lemur; runner Dara, a gibbon; runner Kopo, a macaque; referee Marn, a gorilla; and replay operator Tiko, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Line That Moved"

### Cold open

EXT. GROVE TRACK, AFTERNOON

[Two runners crouch at a start line. The finish line is a ribbon tied between two saplings. A breeze lifts the ribbon.]

VELL: The runners are ready for the annual canopy relay.

SUNI: The finish ribbon is moving.

VELL: The ribbon is showing pre-race excitement.

MARN: It is tied loosely.

KOPO: Is that the finish?

DARA: It is where the finish was.

[The ribbon blows across the track and wraps around Vell's microphone.]

SUNI: The finish has taken the lead.

VELL: We have an early contender.

### Act One

EXT. START LINE

[Tiko watches the monitor. Marn reties the ribbon.]

MARN: We need a clear finish.

VELL: The course is marked by cones.

SUNI: There is one cone.

VELL: A symbolic cone.

KOPO: How long is the race?

MARN: To the big tree.

DARA: Which big tree?

MARN: The one at the end.

SUNI: There are three trees at the end.

VELL: The competitors will decide.

MARN: That is not how races work.

[Tiko shows a close-up replay of the ribbon blowing away.]

TIKO: The replay confirms the ribbon crossed the line before the runners.

VELL: The ribbon is our current leader.

SUNI: It has no legs.

VELL: It has wind assistance.

DARA: Can we race to the stump instead?

MARN: The stump is stable.

KOPO: Is the stump the finish?

MARN: It is now.

### Act Two

EXT. GROVE TRACK

[Marn blows a small whistle. It produces a squeak. The runners start. Dara swings from low vines; Kopo bounds between cones. The camera chases them.]

VELL: Dara has taken the high route.

SUNI: Kopo is taking the direct route.

VELL: He has collided gently with the symbolic cone.

KOPO: The cone moved.

MARN: The cone is not the finish.

SUNI: We are approaching the stump.

[Tiko plays an instant replay. It shows Kopo crossing an imaginary line that the camera operator had drawn in the dirt.]

VELL: The replay shows Kopo across the line.

MARN: There was no line.

VELL: There is a line on the screen.

MARN: That's the camera frame.

SUNI: The camera frame has entered the competition.

[Dara arrives at the stump. Kopo catches up. Both grab the stump to steady themselves.]

VELL: A photo finish.

SUNI: They have both finished.

MARN: They have both touched the stump.

KOPO: The stump was wobbling.

DARA: We were holding it up.

MARN: You cannot finish by holding the finish.

### Replay booth

INT. COMMENTARY BOOTH

[Tiko freezes the image of Dara and Kopo holding the stump.]

TIKO: At this moment, the stump moves three centimeters.

VELL: Was it a gust?

SUNI: It was the runners.

TIKO: We can zoom in.

VELL: Do we have a camera angle from the stump?

TIKO: The stump camera is facing the tree.

SUNI: Can we reverse the footage?

TIKO: The stump camera has been recording itself.

VELL: A bold independent production.

SUNI: The replay shows both runners supporting the course.

TIKO: It also shows Marn's whistle flying past.

VELL: The whistle has entered the race.

SUNI: It is still squeaking.

[Tiko rewinds. The squeak repeats three times.]

TIKO: The replay is now the loudest competitor.

### Act Three

EXT. TRACK, MOMENTS LATER

[Marn lays the ribbon on the ground between the runners. The ribbon lifts in the breeze and circles the stump.]

MARN: We will use the tree's shadow as the finish line.

SUNI: The shadow is moving.

MARN: The sun is moving.

VELL: A late change to the rules.

DARA: We can finish when the shadow reaches the stump.

KOPO: When will that be?

MARN: Soon.

SUNI: The crowd is waiting.

VELL: The crowd is a troop on the branches.

[Several monkeys lean forward. One drops a peel. The peel lands just beyond the stump.]

SUNI: The peel crossed the line.

VELL: We have a new leader.

MARN: The peel is not a runner.

KOPO: We can run to it.

DARA: On your mark.

MARN: Wait. I need a whistle.

[He blows the whistle. It squeaks. Dara and Kopo both take off, stop, then walk to the peel together.]

VELL: They are approaching the finish in unison.

SUNI: They have chosen sportsmanship over speed.

KOPO: We can split the peel.

DARA: I already ate.

MARN: The race is officially a tie.

VELL: A thrilling conclusion.

SUNI: The final whistle was a squeak.

TIKO: The replay agrees.

MARN: The replay is showing my foot.

TIKO: You crossed the line first.

MARN: I was fixing it.

### Tag

EXT. TRACK, SUNSET

[The ribbon is tied around the stump. The runners and crew sit beneath it. The wind pulls it loose again.]

VELL: The finish has moved.

SUNI: It is heading for next year's race.

[The ribbon lifts from the stump and floats past the empty commentary booth.]
