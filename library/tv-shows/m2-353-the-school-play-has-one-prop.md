# The School Play Has One Prop

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: behind-the-scenes theater documentaries that inflate a small production into an awards-season crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Student actors share one cardboard moon across three scenes while a crew films their "creative conflict."

Cast: Director Etta, a gibbon; actors Seli, a macaque, and Tov, a capuchin; prop keeper Ralo, a baboon; documentarian Miri, a lemur.

Episode: "The Moon Must Travel"

COLD OPEN

INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM - AFTERNOON

Ralo carries a silver cardboard moon on a stick. Miri's camera tracks it.

ETTA: The moon belongs in scene two.

SELI: I need it in scene one.

TOV: It is in the finale too.

MIRI: We have discovered a creative conflict.

RALO: We have one prop and three entrances.

[THE MOON SLIPS UNDER A RISER. EVERYONE STARES INTO CAMERA.]

RALO: It is under there. I can reach it.

ACT ONE

Etta lays out the script. Seli finds the moon in a forest scene. Tov carries it in a dream. The company raises it for the final night sky. Miri asks what the prop means to them.

SELI: It means I need to hand it to Tov.

TOV: The dream has no handoff.

ETTA: We can add one behind the tree flat.

RALO: The flat is cardboard. There is no opening.

MIRI: Could the audience see the transfer? It might symbolize the shared labor of theater.

ETTA: The audience should see the play.

They pass the moon beside the flat. Tov reaches early and takes the stick while Seli still holds it. The moon bends.

RALO: Stop. The pole is cardboard too.

MIRI: Did the moon almost break?

RALO: No. It bent.

MIRI: Can you show us the moment again?

SELI: We can reinforce the pole.

ACT TWO

Ralo adds a sleeve around the stick. It fits through a notch behind the tree flat. The crew rotates the flat to hide the transfer; it now blocks the exit.

TOV: I can't get onstage.

ETTA: The moon has moved the doorway.

MIRI: That is an unexpected production challenge.

RALO: It is a flat turned the wrong way.

[THEY ROTATE IT. THE SLEEVE NOW FACES THE AUDIENCE.]

SELI: The moon has a visible handle.

TOV: It looks like a flagpole.

MIRI: What does a moon do when the audience sees how it works?

ETTA: It becomes a prop.

Ralo puts the notch behind the flat again and tapes a guide onto the back. They rehearse. Seli inserts the stick and exits; Tov reaches around and lifts it. The moon tilts sideways.

TOV: It is setting.

RALO: It is loose. Hold it straight while I tighten the sleeve.

MIRI: Is the tension emotional?

ETTA: It is adhesive.

ACT TWO-B

The cast runs the entrances. The moon clears the flat, but the finale handle is too short for three actors to hold. Ralo adds a second grip. During the lift, the moon spins once and lands in a laundry basket.

SELI: The moon has fallen into orbit.

RALO: It has fallen into lost-and-found.

Miri asks them to pause for an interview. Etta says they have seven minutes before the auditorium is needed by the choir. Miri follows the basket as Ralo carries it to the prop table.

MIRI: The moon is leaving the stage.

RALO: It is going to be reinforced.

[THE CAMERA OPERATOR BACKS INTO THE TREE FLAT. IT STARTS TO TIP. TOV CATCHES IT WHILE SELI HOLDS THE MOON.]

ETTA: That is the teamwork shot. Now put everything down safely.

ACT TWO-C

Ralo reinforces the grip with two strips of tape and a wooden dowel. The cast repeats the finale. Seli holds the dowel; Tov steadies the moon face; Etta cues the ensemble. The prop stays upright.

MIRI: We have reached the emotional resolution.

ETTA: We have finished the rehearsal.

MIRI: Those are close.

Before the show, Miri asks the cast to recreate the dropped-moon moment for the documentary. Ralo refuses to drop the prop again and hands her the laundry basket instead.

RALO: You can film the basket holding the moon safely.

MIRI: It needs a human moment.

SELI: We already had the human moment. Tov caught the flat.

TOV: My arms are still tired.

[ETTA CALLS PLACES. THE CAST TAKES THEIR MARKS. MIRI TRIES TO SQUEEZE A CAMERA BETWEEN THE TREE FLAT AND THE WING.]

ETTA: The camera has the moon's mark.

MIRI: I can move.

ETTA: Move now, please. The audience is seated.

[MIRI BACKS INTO THE PROP TABLE. RALO CATCHES THE MOON BEFORE IT FALLS.]

RALO: It has another close call.

MIRI: It has another angle.

ETTA: It has a show to do.

ACT THREE

The school play runs. Seli finds the moon, Tov carries it through the dream, and the ensemble raises it at the finale. The audience applauds. Miri keeps filming after the cast bows.

RALO: Put the moon away before the cardboard bends.

MIRI: We need one final image.

TAG

Miri frames the moon in the prop cupboard. The door swings shut, leaving the camera pointed at a blank shelf.

MIRI: We have found our final image.

SELI: It's the cupboard.

[MUSIC: SCHOOL PLAY THEME.]
