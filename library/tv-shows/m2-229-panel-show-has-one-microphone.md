# The Panel Show Has One Microphone

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television panel discussions that invite debate but give guests too little time and one shared microphone*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: Four local experts debate where to put a new bench, but the studio has only one working microphone and the host keeps changing the topic.

Cast: Host Dema, a macaque; planner Lali, a gibbon; gardener Kito, a capuchin; and caller Sela, a baboon.

Episode: "Bench Debate"

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - NIGHT

Dema sits between Lali and Kito. A single microphone stands in the middle. A diagram shows a park path.

DEMA: Tonight, where should the new bench go?

LALI: Near the shade tree.

KITO: Near the path entrance.

[THEY BOTH LEAN TOWARD THE MICROPHONE. THEIR HEADS BUMP.]

DEMA: We have a disagreement.

LALI: We have one microphone.

ACT ONE

Dema introduces the first round. Lali points to a spot under a tree. Kito points to an open patch by the path.

DEMA: Lali, your thirty seconds begin now.

LALI: The shade makes the bench comfortable in the afternoon.

[KITO TAKES A BREATH. THE TIMER BEEPS.]

DEMA: Kito, your turn.

KITO: People with walking sticks can reach it from the path.

LALI: The shaded area is also close to the path.

DEMA: Please wait for the rebuttal bell.

[DEMA RINGS A SMALL BELL. IT DOES NOT SOUND.]

ACT TWO

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - LATER

The show takes a caller. Sela is on speaker. Dema puts the mic between the two guests again.

SELA (ON PHONE): Could the bench go halfway between the tree and the entrance?

DEMA: That would be a compromise.

LALI: The middle is in direct sun.

KITO: We can add a canopy.

DEMA: The question is the bench location, not the canopy.

SELA: The caller line has the park map. It has a shaded section halfway to the entrance.

[LALI LOOKS AT THE MAP. KITO LEANS OVER HER SHOULDER.]

LALI: That spot works.

KITO: It is also level.

DEMA: We have agreement before the final round.

ACT TWO-B

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

A stagehand brings in a second microphone. Dema hands it to Lali. Kito still shares the first one with the host.

DEMA: Now each guest gets room to speak.

LALI: The bench should face the path.

KITO: The bench should face the shade.

[THEY TURN THEIR CHAIRS TO FACE DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS.]

DEMA: We have more microphones and less agreement.

SELA (ON PHONE): The map shows both sides of the bench.

LALI: It can face the path and sit under the tree.

KITO: The bench is long enough for two people.

DEMA: Is the debate over?

LALI: The bench still needs to be installed.

ACT THREE

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - CONTROL BOOTH

Vela and Kito review the segment monitor. Dema speaks through a headset. The panel guests can hear only the microphone nearest them.

DEMA: We need another round of debate before the park visit.

LALI: We settled on the middle spot.

KITO: The bench can face the path and catch shade.

DEMA: Let's debate whether it should face east or west.

SELA (ON PHONE): The sun moves. The shade moves too.

DEMA: That gives us a follow-up question.

KITO: The bench doesn't need to follow the sun.

[DEMA POINTS TO THE MICROPHONE. IT IS STILL ON THE BENCH OUTSIDE, PICKED UP BY THE REMOTE FEED.]

DEMA: We lost the studio feed.

LALI: The microphone is in the park.

DEMA: Can we bring it back?

SELA: The crew is still filming there.

[THE MONITOR SHOWS A CREW MEMBER SITTING ON THE BENCH.]

KITO: The bench has a user.

LALI: It is level and in the shade.

DEMA: We have a field test.

SELA: The crew member is reading.

DEMA: We will return to the debate after the test.

EXT. PARK - DAY

The bench is placed halfway between the tree and the path. Dema holds the two microphones. Lali and Kito test the seat.

KITO: The path is clear.

LALI: The shade reaches the end of the bench.

DEMA: Our panel has reached a verdict.

SELA: The bench is open to everyone.

[DEMA SETS THE MICROPHONES ON THE BENCH. A WALKER PICKS ONE UP BY MISTAKE.]

LALI: The microphone is blocking the seat.

KITO: I'll move it to the side.

DEMA: We need one final answer from the panel.

LALI: The bench works here.

KITO: People can sit, pass by, and stay in the shade.

[THE WALKER RETURNS THE MICROPHONE. DEMA PLACES IT ON THE GRASS BESIDE THE BENCH.]

DEMA: We can end the segment.

TAG

Dema returns to the studio with one microphone. The other is still at the park.

DEMA: Next week's topic is where to put the microphone.
