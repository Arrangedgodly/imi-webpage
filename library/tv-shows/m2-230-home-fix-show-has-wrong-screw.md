# The Home Fix Show Has the Wrong Screw

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-repair television that promises a one-tool fix before checking the actual hardware*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A host promises to fix a loose bench with one screwdriver, but the crew's prop kit contains only decorative tools.

Cast: Host Neri, a gibbon; carpenter Rava, a macaque; homeowner Toma, a capuchin; and producer Venn, a baboon.

Episode: "One Quick Tighten"

COLD OPEN

INT. WORKSHOP SET - DAY

Toma taps a loose bench. One screw shifts. Neri holds up a screwdriver.

NERI: One quick tighten and the bench is fixed.

RAVA: That screwdriver is for a different screw head.

VENN: The camera is ready.

TOMA: The bench wobbles when I sit.

[NERI TURNS THE SCREWDRIVER. IT SLIPS OUT OF THE SCREW HEAD.]

NERI: We have found the challenge.

ACT ONE

Rava checks the bench. Toma stands beside it. Venn rolls out a tool cart.

RAVA: This screw needs a square bit.

NERI: The show promised one tool.

RAVA: The right bit is one tool.

VENN: The kit has a flathead, a toy hammer, and a measuring tape.

TOMA: The bench is missing one bolt too.

NERI: Could we use the tape to hold it?

RAVA: No.

[TOMA SITS. THE BENCH WOBBLES. RAVA CATCHES THE SEAT BEFORE IT TIPS.]

RAVA: We need to stop using it until the bolt is replaced.

ACT TWO

INT. WORKSHOP SET - LATER

Venn places a container of mixed screws on the table. Rava sorts them. Neri keeps the camera on the tool cart.

NERI: We have a drawer of spare hardware.

RAVA: We have three bolts that look similar.

TOMA: I have the manual from the bench kit.

VENN: Can the manual be a sponsor prop?

TOMA: It says the bolt size.

[RAVA MEASURES THE BOLT. THE TAPE MEASURE SNAPS BACK AND KNOCKS OVER THE TOY HAMMER.]

NERI: We have a tool mishap.

RAVA: We have a tape measure.

TOMA: The manual says use the washer too.

NERI: The episode is called "One Quick Tighten."

RAVA: It is still one repair.

ACT TWO-B

INT. WORKSHOP SET - AFTERNOON

Rava finds the correct bolt and square bit. Venn reads the show card. Neri positions a camera for the close-up.

VENN: We need a dramatic turn of the screw.

RAVA: It needs to be snug, not forced.

NERI: Can you make the last turn slower?

RAVA: I will tighten it until the joint is secure.

[TOMA HOLDS THE BENCH STEADY. RAVA FITS THE WASHER AND TIGHTENS THE BOLT.]

TOMA: That feels solid.

VENN: Do not sit until the camera has the reveal.

TOMA: I am holding it.

NERI: We can show the before-and-after.

[RAVA PLACES A HAND ON THE SEAT AND TESTS IT. IT DOES NOT WOBBLE.]

ACT TWO-C

INT. WORKSHOP SET - MID-AFTERNOON

Toma brings the bench's instruction sheet. Rava lays out the correct bit, bolt, and washer. Neri looks for a way to frame the repair.

NERI: Can we show the bench before it wobbles?

RAVA: We already showed it. It needs the missing bolt.

TOMA: I found the bolt on the floor under the bench.

VENN: That would make a good reveal.

TOMA: It fell out yesterday.

NERI: Could we replay the moment?

RAVA: No. The bolt fell out before we arrived.

[TOMA HOLDS THE BOLT AGAINST THE EMPTY HOLE. IT FITS.]

TOMA: This is the right one.

RAVA: It needs a washer so the wood does not split.

VENN: The washer is not in the shot list.

NERI: Add it. We should show the whole repair.

[RAVA ADDS THE WASHER. TOMA HOLDS THE SEAT WHILE RAVA TIGHTENS THE BOLT.]

TOMA: That feels steadier already.

RAVA: The joint needs to be fully tightened before anyone sits.

NERI: Can we tease that after the break?

TOMA: I can wait. The bench has waited a day.

[VENN MOVES THE PROP CAMERA TO THE OTHER SIDE. THE BENCH LOOKS THE SAME.]

VENN: The angle changes the reveal.

RAVA: The repair does not.

TOMA: I can use the bench again.

NERI: Once we finish the test.

ACT THREE

INT. WORKSHOP SET - FINAL TEST

Toma sits on the repaired bench. It stays steady. Neri holds the screwdriver. Rava puts the spare toy tools back in their case.

NERI: One repair, one correct bit, and one bolt.

RAVA: And a washer.

TOMA: The bench is safe to use now.

VENN: Can we say one quick tighten?

RAVA: Not with the wrong screw.

TOMA: I will put it back by the wall.

[HE LIFTS THE BENCH. THE JOINT HOLDS.]

RAVA: It is ready for use.

TAG

Neri taps the bench. It remains still. Venn asks for a final close-up.

TOMA: The camera can sit there.

[THE CAMERA TRIPOD WOBBLES. RAVA HANDS VENN A LEVEL.]
