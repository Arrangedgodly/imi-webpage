# The Slowest Journey

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentary convention, with sweeping narration about a monkey moving only as fast as a tortoise crossing the grove*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A documentary crew follows a tortoise across the grove, while the narrator keeps describing a race no one entered.

Cast: Narrator Vela, a gibbon; camera operator Piri, a capuchin; guide Kito, a macaque; and Temba, a tortoise.

Episode: "Across One Clearing"

COLD OPEN

EXT. GROVE, DAWN

Vela whispers into a microphone as Temba moves along a path.

VELA: At first light, our traveler begins a dangerous journey.

Kito points to a shallow puddle. Temba walks around it.

KITO: The puddle is three steps wide.

Vela says the crossing could take hours. Temba reaches the other side.

ACT ONE

EXT. PATH, MORNING

Piri films Temba beside a fallen leaf. Vela describes the leaf as a barrier. Kito picks it up and places it aside. The crew follows as Temba continues. He pauses beside a patch of sunlight.

VELA: He gathers strength for the next leg.

KITO: He is warming his shell.

Piri asks if the shot should be sped up. Vela says the audience must feel the passage of time. They set up a time-lapse camera. A cloud moves over the sun. Temba stays still. Kito offers a leaf for shade. Temba moves two inches and stops.

ACT TWO

EXT. STREAM BANK, AFTERNOON

The path reaches a small stream. Temba approaches, then turns toward a flat rock. Vela says he is reconsidering his route. Kito explains that tortoises often pause to assess. Piri frames the rock as an epic overlook. Temba climbs onto it and rests.

The camera battery runs low. Piri asks if they should switch batteries. Vela says they cannot miss the moment. Kito replaces the battery while Piri keeps the lens trained on the empty rock. Temba has moved on, unnoticed, to a shaded patch nearby.

They search the frame and discover his tracks. Kito follows them to the shade. Vela narrates the discovery as a reunion. Temba continues toward the stream. The water is shallow, but the stones are slick. Kito places a branch at the edge as a handrail. Temba crosses on his own route beside it.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. STREAM BANK, LATE AFTERNOON

The crew reaches a small footbridge. Vela describes it as a perilous crossing. Kito points out the handrail. Temba stops before the first board. The crew waits. Piri zooms in, then asks Kito whether the tortoise needs help. Kito says Temba can choose his own route.

Vela continues narration about courage. Temba turns away from the bridge and walks along the bank to a shallow place. He pauses to sniff a wet stone. Piri changes angle to keep him in frame. The camera operator steps backward into a patch of mud. Kito pulls him out by the strap, leaving the camera safe but Piri's feet muddy.

PIRI: The traveler has found a new route.

KITO: The crew has found mud.

Temba crosses the stream at the shallow place. The water reaches only the edge of his shell. Vela asks for a dramatic reaction. Temba pauses, then continues to the other bank. Kito explains that the crossing was ordinary and safe. Piri asks if they can film his tracks. They follow them to a patch of warm soil, where Temba has already settled.

The crew sets down equipment quietly. Vela lowers the microphone. Piri turns off the camera. Kito asks whether they are done. Vela says they can record one natural sound: a bird calling from the nearby tree. The bird calls twice. Temba does not move.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. GROVE EDGE, SUNSET

Vela asks Kito for a concluding thought. He says Temba traveled at his own pace and used a safe crossing. Vela says that sounds less dramatic than the journey. Kito asks if the camera recorded the muddy path, the bridge and the shallow crossing. Piri reviews the footage. It shows each one clearly.

Vela writes a new narration: "The route was not dangerous. The challenge was waiting long enough to see it." Kito says that is accurate. Piri records it without music. A gust lifts the microphone cover and carries it a short distance. Kito catches it before it reaches the stream.

PIRI: The crew has had the closest call.

VELA: We are not the subject.

Kito places the cover back on the microphone. The camera stays fixed on Temba resting in the warm soil.

ACT THREE



EXT. GROVE EDGE, SUNSET

Temba reaches the destination: a patch of warm soil beneath a tree. Vela calls it the end of a grand expedition. Kito says the tortoise has traveled across one clearing. Piri reviews the footage. Most of it shows the crew changing batteries, lifting leaves and setting up equipment.

Vela asks for the final narration. Temba settles into the soil. Vela says he has found a place to rest. Kito says that is what he was doing all day.

TAG

EXT. GROVE EDGE, NEXT MORNING

The crew packs up. Temba remains in the warm spot. Piri films from a distance.

VELA: Our traveler has begun a new journey.

KITO: He has not moved.

VELA: The journey is internal.
