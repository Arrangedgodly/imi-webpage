# The Drip Under the Awning

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom television, dramatic evidence reveals, rapid objections, and judges who demand a clean final ruling*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Two market vendors sue each other over a drip beneath their shared awning, but the courtroom's demonstration rig is the source of the water.

Cast: Judge Savo, a baboon; claimant Leni, a macaque; respondent Odo, a gibbon; counsel Piri, a capuchin; bailiff Tavi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. TELEVISION COURTROOM - DAY

Judge Savo looks up as one drop of water lands on his bench.

SAVO: Is the courtroom leaking?

TAVI: The demonstration awning is above us.

PIRI: We are in the middle of a case about a drip.

ACT ONE

Leni says the awning over her fruit stall sends water onto her neighbor's display. Odo says the awning is shared and the gutter belongs to both of them. He has moved his stall twice to avoid the puddle. Leni brings a folded tarp; Odo brings a small drain diagram.

SAVO: We will hear one account at a time.

Leni steps forward. Odo begins speaking over her. Tavi rings the courtroom bell. Both stop. The bell itself drips onto the bench.

SAVO: The court will relocate the bell.

Piri asks Leni where the drip falls. Leni points to a photograph of the market floor. A puddle is marked with chalk. Piri asks if she saw Odo move the awning. Leni says she saw him pull the edge one afternoon.

ODO: I pulled the gutter clear of a leaf.

LENI: You pulled the edge toward my stall.

ACT TWO

Odo takes the stand and shows how the gutter is clipped to both awning frames. He says a large leaf blocked the outlet; he removed it and water drained toward the shared path. Leni says the flow washed two price cards off her table. Odo says the cards were outside the covered area.

SAVO: Do you have the cards?

LENI: I brought one.

She holds up a damp card marked "PEARS." Piri places it in an evidence sleeve. Savo asks why the card is still wet. Leni says she preserved it in the same bag.

PIRI: The evidence has remained wet.

SAVO: That is not a finding about the awning.

Tavi wheels in a model of the market. The awnings are made of cloth. A tiny gutter connects them. Odo points to the drain. Leni says the model is tilted; the drain is actually lower than shown. Tavi rotates the base. The model awning drips into a small cup.

SAVO: We have reproduced the event.

TAVI: We have reproduced a drip.

ACT TWO-B

Piri asks to test the demonstration awning. Tavi climbs the small platform and pours a measured cup of water into its channel. Water runs through the gutter and onto the courtroom floor. Savo raises a hand. Everyone steps back from the wet area.

SAVO: Stop the test.

TAVI: I am not pouring anything.

The studio sprinkler above the awning rig drips again. Tavi points to the ceiling. Piri looks up. A narrow stream lands in the gutter and passes through the model exactly as before.

ODO: That is the same direction as the market drain.

LENI: The market has no sprinkler.

SAVO: Correct. This demonstration cannot establish where your water came from.

Piri asks the courtroom technician to turn off the sprinkler. The drip stops. Leni says she still needs the gutter checked; Odo agrees but says they should ask the market manager to arrange it. Savo asks whether either vendor wants a ruling about the awning's ownership. Both say the clips are shared.

Tavi brings in the market's daily log. It notes a leaf was cleared from the gutter on Tuesday, signed by both vendors. Piri asks Leni if she recognizes the handwriting. Leni says it is hers. Odo says he initialed the same line.

PIRI: So both of you cleared the drain.

LENI: We did. The drip happened later.

ODO: After the studio installed its demonstration awning.

SAVO: The court is not hearing a claim against the television studio.

TAVI: The studio has a maintenance form.

Savo asks for the form. Odo points to a puddle beneath the platform. Piri objects to its relevance. Savo overrules her and orders the crew to place a bucket under the rig. The next drop lands with a loud plink.

SAVO: The court admits the sound, but not as evidence against either vendor.

ACT THREE

Savo rules that the awning clips and gutter are shared property, but the court cannot assign blame for the two lost price cards from the evidence presented. He orders the case dismissed without a damages award and advises both vendors to request a market inspection together. Tavi removes the wet demonstration model. Piri carries the evidence bag to the clerk.

LENI: The pears were still for sale.

ODO: I bought two after the rain.

SAVO: The court will not adjudicate the fruit transaction.

TAG

The bailiff places a bucket under the courtroom rig. Another drop lands inside it. Judge Savo hears the plink and looks toward the camera.

FINAL IMAGE: The evidence card "PEARS" hangs beside a maintenance tag on the studio awning, both now dry.
