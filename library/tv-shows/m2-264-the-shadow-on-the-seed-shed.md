# The Shadow on the Seed Shed

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal reality television, where a documentary host treats moving shadows as an intelligent presence*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: A night crew investigates a shadow crossing the seed shed, while the keeper keeps pointing out the motion sensor, the hanging rake, and the moon.

Cast: Host Vela, gibbon; sound tech Odo, baboon; biologist Nara, macaque; keeper Piri, capuchin; camera operator Seli, lemur.

## Episode: "The Shed That Watches"

### Cold Open

[SEED SHED AT NIGHT. A SHADOW PASSES ACROSS THE DOOR.]

VELA: Something has crossed the shed.

PIRI: That was the moon.

ODO: The audio caught a breath.

NARA: That was the motion sensor.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW STANDS OUTSIDE THE SHED.]

Vela explains that three nights in a row, a shape has moved across the door. Piri says the shed has a small window, and the moon crosses it as the night moves on.

VELA: Can you prove that?

PIRI: I can show you.

ODO: The sound has a low hum.

NARA: The motion sensor hums when it turns on.

Seli replays the recording. A beep sounds before each shadow.

VELA: The shed may be warning us.

PIRI: The beep is the light switch.

Odo asks Piri to turn off the sensor. The light goes dark. The shadow vanishes.

VELA: It knows we are watching.

NARA: The light is off.

### Act Two

[THE CREW SETS UP TWO CAMERAS.]

Vela asks Piri to wait outside while the team listens from the control table. A shadow moves again, this time from right to left. Odo says the direction changed.

NARA: The moon moved.

VELA: What if the presence has moved too?

Seli checks the second camera. The shadow is the hanging rake in front of the window. A passing cloud dims the light, then the rake shifts in the wind.

ODO: The rake has a long handle.

VELA: It could be reaching toward the door.

PIRI: I hung it there to dry.

Odo plays a sound he recorded: a tap, scrape, tap. Piri recognizes the sound as the shed latch bumping in the wind. Vela asks him to demonstrate.

[SFX: LATCH TAPS, SCRAPES.]

VELA: It sounds different in the playback.

NARA: The microphone is beside the door.

ODO: It sounded like footsteps.

PIRI: It is a latch.

[THE CREW SETS A LIGHT NEAR THE WINDOW.]

Vela asks Odo to recreate the shadow without the moon. Odo switches on the work light; the rake shadow appears across the door. Nara moves the light, and the shadow stretches.

VELA: It is reacting to us.

PIRI: You moved the lamp.

ODO: The angle changes the shape.

Seli marks the light's position with tape. When the camera moves to get a close shot, the shadow shifts again.

SELI: The camera light is adding a second shape.

VELA: Two presences?

NARA: Two lights.

The camera operator turns off her lamp. The extra shadow disappears.

ODO: Our equipment made the evidence.

VELA: The evidence remains unexplained.

PIRI: It is a rake.

Odo asks Piri why the latch taps at night. Piri demonstrates that the shed door is loose in its frame; a breeze makes it touch the post. The tap stops when he holds the handle.

VELA: The presence has been silenced.

PIRI: I am holding the door.

### Act Three

[THE TEAM ENTERS THE SHED.]

Piri switches on the lights. The rake is hanging from a nail, and the latch is loose. Nara checks the window and finds a small gap where the moonlight enters.

VELA: We have found the source.

ODO: We have found two sources.

NARA: And a sound.

Piri fixes the latch with a small strip of leather. The rake is moved away from the window. Seli films the door as the moonlight shifts.

[SFX: LATCH CLOSES QUIETLY.]

Vela asks for one final take of the shadow. They wait. A cloud passes; the shed darkens. The shadow returns, but now the crew can see it is the rake still hanging at the side wall.

VELA: The shape has come back.

PIRI: I moved it to the other wall.

NARA: We did not remove it.

ODO: Should we record the conclusion?

VELA: We should record the mystery.

NARA: We have explained it.

[THE CREW CHECKS THE NIGHT FOOTAGE.]

Seli reviews the final shot. The shadow is still there, but the crew can see the moonlit branch.

VELA: We cannot call it a ghost.

PIRI: You never could.

### Tag

[THE CREW OUTSIDE, MORNING.]

VELA: The shed is no longer watching.

PIRI: It never was.

Seli points her camera toward the host. Vela's shadow stretches across the shed door.

ODO: The shape has returned.

VELA: That is me.

NARA: You are standing in front of the light.

[SFX: CAMERA CLICKS. BIRDS.]
