# The Prank Was in the Prop Department

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: hidden-camera prank shows, fake reactions, and hosts who stay in character after the trick is obvious*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A hidden-camera crew tries to make a repair tech believe a talking toolbox is haunted, but the technician spots the speaker before the host finishes his spooky introduction.

Cast: Host Vema, a gibbon; target Horo, a macaque repair technician; producer Keli, a baboon; sound operator Nari, a capuchin; prop builder Selo, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. WORKSHOP SET - DAY

A toolbox sits on a bench. Its lid opens by itself. A voice says, "Return the wrench."

HORO (OFF): I am using the wrench.

Vema watches from a fake wall.

VEMA: The toolbox has spoken.

KELI: The repair tech has answered.

ACT ONE

The crew explains the prank to camera. A small speaker is taped under the bench. A string lifts the lid. Horo enters carrying a real screwdriver. He examines the toolbox, notices the string, and removes it.

HORO: This could catch someone's sleeve.

Selo hides behind a curtain. Nari activates the speaker. The toolbox says, "Do not open me." Horo looks toward the control booth.

HORO: The speaker is underneath.

Vema asks Keli whether Horo is supposed to be surprised. Keli says the segment has not started yet. Horo hears them and turns toward the camera.

HORO: You can come out. I have work to do.

ACT TWO

Vema steps out wearing a ghost sheet. The sheet has eyeholes at the wrong height. He walks into a lamp stand. Horo catches the lamp before it falls and lifts the sheet off Vema.

HORO: You cannot see through this.

VEMA: We are filming a prank.

HORO: You should not put string across a walkway.

Nari says the toolbox can keep talking if Horo plays along. Horo says he needs to finish repairing the drawer hinge. Keli asks if he can perform a mock reaction for the edit. Horo puts on a very serious face.

HORO: I am shocked that a box has a speaker.

VEMA: Can you be more shocked?

HORO: I am so shocked I have stopped noticing the speaker taped under the bench.

Nari turns off the voice. Selo retrieves the string and speaker. Horo asks if the toolbox is safe to use. Selo says it is a prop, with a loose lid. Horo tests the hinge and says it needs a proper latch.

ACT TWO-B

Keli tries to salvage the scene by staging a fake reveal. Vema asks Horo to open the box one more time. Horo says he will, after the prop crew clears the cables. Selo coils the speaker wire and removes the adhesive. Nari holds the lid while Horo tightens the loose hinge with the real wrench.

HORO: Now the lid stays open without a string.

VEMA: Can the box say something at the end?

HORO: It can say nothing. That would be an improvement.

Nari reconnects the speaker inside the control booth and plays, "Thank you for fixing me." Horo lifts the toolbox and turns it upside down. The speaker falls onto the workbench.

NARI: It was not mounted securely.

HORO: It was part of the prank.

VEMA: We can use that in the reveal.

HORO: Use the part where nobody trips over the wire.

Keli tries one last reveal shot. She asks Horo to open the toolbox while Vema waits behind the curtain. Horo checks that the floor is clear, then opens the lid. Vema jumps out too early and misses the camera mark.

VEMA: Did I surprise you?

HORO: You surprised the lamp.

Nari shows the monitor. The shot captured the lamp, the curtain, and the toolbox, but not Vema's entrance. Keli asks Horo to reset the scene. He says the hinge is fixed and the reveal is over.

HORO: The box is repaired. We can stop opening it.

Selo carries the spare string to the prop shelf and labels it. Keli removes the ghost sheet from the camera path.

ACT THREE

Keli announces the prank is over and asks Horo if he wants to stay for the show. Horo says he can help install a latch on the prop box, but he does not want a fake reaction edited into the segment. Keli agrees to show the setup and the failed surprise honestly. Vema retells the moment the toolbox spoke; the edit cuts to Horo pointing at the speaker.

VEMA: He saw through it immediately.

HORO: I saw a wire.

SELO: We can put the speaker inside a sealed panel next time.

HORO: Or put it in a place people expect sound equipment.

Selo installs a small latch. Horo tests it twice. The lid stays open, and no strings remain across the floor. Nari packs the speaker separately, labeled "AUDIO PROP."

TAG

Vema tries the ghost sheet again. It slips over his eyes. Horo offers to cut larger eyeholes. Vema says the show is over and walks away with the sheet still covering his face.

FINAL IMAGE: Horo closes the latched toolbox by hand while the unused speaker sits in its labeled case.
