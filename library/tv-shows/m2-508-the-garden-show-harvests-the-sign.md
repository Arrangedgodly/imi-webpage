# The Garden Show Harvests the Sign

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: gardening programs that stage a harvest reveal before the plants are ready*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Neri, a lemur; gardener Odo, an orangutan; producer Fali, a capuchin; soil specialist Jani, a macaque; camera operator Riko, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

Neri stands beside a raised bed. A sign reads TOMATO HARVEST TODAY. The plants are green and empty.

NERI: We have a basket of ripe tomatoes.

ODO: We have a basket.

[He holds it up. It is empty.]

ACT ONE

EXT. GARDEN - CONTINUOUS

Fali says the harvest reveal is already in the rundown. Odo explains the tomatoes need another week. Neri asks whether there are any ripe ones. Jani finds one small green tomato and shows it to the camera. Riko zooms in until the fruit fills the frame; the leaves disappear, making it look red under the set light.

JANI: It is still green.

FALI: Could it be a reflection?

Odo turns the tomato so the lens sees its color. Neri asks for the reveal basket anyway. Fali places the empty basket in front of the bed. Odo drops in a garden sign that says PLEASE WAIT. The camera frames only the basket.

ACT TWO

EXT. GARDEN PATH - LATER

Jani checks the calendar. The tomatoes were planted six weeks ago. Fali says the episode title cannot change because it is printed on the lower-third. Neri asks Odo to demonstrate pruning. Odo removes a dead leaf, then stops before cutting a healthy branch. Fali asks him to make the shot bigger. Odo holds up the dead leaf beside the empty basket.

Riko films a bee visiting a flower. Fali asks whether it can be called a pollination reveal. Jani explains that the bee is doing ordinary work and should not be disturbed. The camera follows the bee to the sign and loses it behind the cardboard word HARVEST. The sign falls forward and lands in the soil.

[Odo lifts the sign. A small tomato has grown behind it, but it is still green. Neri calls it a hidden crop. Jani says it was hidden by the sign.]

INT. GARDEN SHED - MOMENTS LATER

Fali looks for a basket to use in the closing shot. The shed contains seed trays, gloves, labels and a row of watering cans. Riko films each item as a series of discoveries. Odo reaches for the harvest basket, but Jani asks him to use the shallow tray so the green tomato will not roll. Fali asks whether that can look festive. Odo lines the tray with clean paper and places the tomato in the center.

EXT. GARDEN PATH - MOMENTS LATER

Jani writes the planting date on a second label. Neri reads it to camera. Fali asks if the label can say READY SOON. Odo turns the sign back to NEXT WEEK. Riko films the row without zooming into the unripe fruit. A neighbor walks through the gate with a basket, sees the green tomatoes and asks when to return. Odo points to the date. The neighbor nods and leaves the basket empty. Neri thanks them for checking the date instead of picking the fruit. The crew leaves the bed untouched. Fali checks that the sign is still readable from the path.

ACT THREE

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - AFTERNOON

Neri records a new introduction. The harvest segment becomes a check-in on the garden's progress. Odo shows the green fruit, the flower clusters and the newly watered bed. Jani explains how they will know when the tomatoes are ripe: color, firmness and time. Fali signals for the title graphic. It still reads HARVEST TODAY.

Neri picks up the sign and turns it around. The back reads NEXT WEEK. Riko frames the text beside the plant. Fali asks whether that spoils the reveal. Odo points to the fruit; it has not changed. Jani suggests putting a small blank label on the bed to mark the date. Neri writes the date in thick letters. The camera captures it clearly.

Fali removes the empty basket from the bed. Odo sets the tray with the green tomato on the garden table, not as a harvest but as a marker of what is growing. Neri reads the label: CHECK AGAIN NEXT WEEK. Riko widens the shot to show the whole bed. The garden looks alive without pretending it is finished.

TAG

INT. GARDEN SHED - EVENING

Fali crosses out HARVEST TODAY on the rundown. Odo hangs the basket back on its hook. Jani waters the rows. Neri checks the date.

FINAL IMAGE: The green tomato rests in a shallow tray beside a sign reading NEXT WEEK, while the empty basket hangs in the shed.
