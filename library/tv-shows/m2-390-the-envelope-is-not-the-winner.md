# The Envelope Is Not the Winner

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised awards ceremonies, acceptance-speech music cues, and envelopes opened for unnecessary suspense*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A regional awards show misplaces the winner's card inside its run-of-show envelope and keeps playing the exit music before anyone can finish a sentence.

Cast: Host Lela, a macaque; stage manager Omi, a baboon; nominee Tavi, a gibbon; nominee Naro, a capuchin; presenter Dena, an orangutan; orchestra leader Piri, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. AWARDS HALL - NIGHT

Dena opens an envelope. A folded schedule falls out; the winner card slips free, lands face down on the table, and stops at Omi's hand.

DENA: I have the order of the evening.

LELA: Is the winner in the order?

OMI: The winner is under the order.

ACT ONE

The award is for Best Short Garden Film. The two nominees, Tavi and Naro, sit in the front row. Omi checks the envelope. The schedule lists the host's entrance, the sponsor reel, and a musical interlude. She slides the winner card back beneath the envelope flap to keep it from blowing off the table.

OMI: We have not announced the winner.

LELA: We have announced several other things.

Dena opens the envelope more carefully. She removes the winner card, but the orchestra starts the acceptance music early. Tavi walks to the stage, thinking the award has been called. Lela stops him at the stairs.

TAVI: I heard music.

PIRI: I heard the cue.

NARO: Neither of us heard a name.

ACT TWO

Omi asks Piri to stop the orchestra. Piri lowers the baton. Lela announces the category and reads the nominees. Dena opens the card. A fan from the stage vent lifts it from her hand. Tavi catches it against his chest and turns the blank side toward the audience. He does not read the name and hands the card back to Dena.

TAVI: It is face down.

LELA: Thank you.

Piri starts the music again. Dena turns the card. She reads Naro's name. Naro stands, but the music swells so loudly that he cannot hear the stage manager's directions. He walks toward the side stairs instead of the center.

OMI: Center stairs.

NARO: I can't hear you.

Omi signals Piri to silence the orchestra. Naro reaches the stage. Lela hands him the award. Naro thanks the editor, the garden crew, and the person who kept the camera steady during a wind gust. Piri begins the exit cue after seven seconds.

NARO: I have one more person.

The music grows louder. Naro pauses. The orchestra stops when Piri sees Omi's hand signal.

ACT TWO-B

Naro says he wants to thank Tavi, who lent him a reflector for the film's final scene. Tavi waves. Lela looks at Omi, who points at the clock. The broadcast is behind schedule because the sponsor reel ran long. Lela asks Naro to finish his thought in one sentence.

NARO: Tavi held the light, and I held the camera.

PIRI: That is a complete sentence.

The audience applauds. Omi asks Dena to confirm the next envelope before opening it. Dena checks the label. It says "NEXT CATEGORY," not "WINNER." Inside is the run-of-show for the closing act. She holds up the label for camera.

DENA: We will read the card before playing music.

LELA: That is a helpful order.

Tavi asks whether Naro's award will be announced in the printed program. Naro says yes, but the program still lists the old title of his film. Omi writes a correction on the teleprompter. The cue display updates just as Lela turns back to camera.

LELA: The award goes to Naro for "The Garden at the Window."

The audience applauds once. Piri waits before lifting the baton. Naro holds the award steady with both hands so it does not tip.

ACT THREE

Omi checks the final cue sheet. Dena names the next presenter, the presenter reads the category, and the winner card is confirmed before the orchestra plays. Lela closes the broadcast on time. Piri starts the closing music only after the host finishes.

LELA: Thank you for joining us. The winners have their awards, and the envelopes have been returned to the table.

Omi stacks the used envelopes by category. The winning card goes into Naro's hand. Naro offers the reflector back to Tavi; Tavi says Naro can keep it until the next shoot.

NARO: I'll bring it back.

TAG

The camera stays on Omi as she seals one final envelope. She labels it "EMPTY." The orchestra starts a dramatic sting. She stops the music with one finger.

FINAL IMAGE: Naro holds his award beside the neatly stacked envelopes while the end credits roll without interruption.
