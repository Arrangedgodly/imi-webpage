# The Crown Was Made of Bark

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: prestige historical dramas, period reenactments, and solemn narration*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A historical television crew recreates the first election of the Old Grove's tree keeper, but the actors keep correcting the narrator with details that were left out of the official record.

Cast: Narrator Tavo, a baboon; historian Lessa, a lemur; actor Rami, a gorilla; actor Suli, a macaque; and prop keeper Pipp, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Keeper's Seat"

### Cold open

INT. REENACTMENT SET, DAY

[A grand hall is built from painted cardboard. Rami wears a bark crown. Suli holds a wooden ballot bowl.]

TAVO: In the distant past, the Grove chose its first keeper through a noble contest of strength.

LESSA: It was a vote.

TAVO: A vote was a contest of strength.

RAMI: I am the candidate.

SULI: I am the voter.

PIPP: The ballot bowl has a hole.

TAVO: History was full of uncertainty.

[The tokens fall through the bowl and land on the floor.]

LESSA: The vote has ended.

RAMI: It has not begun.

### Act One

INT. REENACTMENT SET

[Lessa checks a faded charcoal sketch. Tavo reads from a script.]

TAVO: The keeper was chosen by the council.

LESSA: The council chose where to hold the vote. The troop voted.

TAVO: The troop was the council.

LESSA: That is not in the records.

PIPP: The records are on a leaf.

LESSA: It is a large leaf.

RAMI: How did they count votes?

LESSA: They placed pebbles in bowls.

SULI: That is simple.

PIPP: Our bowl has a hole.

TAVO: We need a historically accurate bowl.

PIPP: I can patch it.

LESSA: With what?

PIPP: Another leaf.

[Tavo signals to camera.]

TAVO: The contestants face a great challenge.

RAMI: I am a candidate, not a contestant.

SULI: I have one pebble.

RAMI: I have a crown.

LESSA: The crown was made after the vote.

RAMI: Then why am I wearing it?

PIPP: It is the only crown in props.

TAVO: We will preserve the dramatic image.

LESSA: The crown was a basket lid.

[They all look at Rami's head. The crown has a handle.]

RAMI: I accept history's correction.

### Act Two

EXT. OLD GROVE SET

[The crew recreates the first public debate. Two actors sit on logs.]

TAVO: The candidates spoke passionately about the future.

SULI: I will keep the shade on the west branch.

RAMI: I will make the roots wider.

LESSA: The candidates were discussing where to plant the fig sapling.

TAVO: Politics begins with soil.

SULI: My plan has more water.

RAMI: My plan has better soil.

LESSA: The records say the sapling was already planted.

PIPP: Who planted it?

LESSA: No one knows.

TAVO: A mystery at the heart of our history.

RAMI: Could it have been planted by a bird?

LESSA: It could have been carried by a monkey.

SULI: That is less dramatic.

TAVO: We can show both possibilities.

[Pipp places a tiny seedling between the candidates. The bark crown slides over Rami's eyes.]

RAMI: I cannot see the seedling.

SULI: The crown is powerful.

LESSA: It is a basket lid.

RAMI: It blocks my view.

SULI: Maybe that is why the keeper needed a council.

TAVO: A profound insight.

LESSA: They were all sharing the work.

TAVO: The past was complicated.

PIPP: The set is falling.

[The painted hall begins to lean. Everyone holds it upright.]

RAMI: We are all supporting the history.

SULI: And the cardboard.

### Act Three

INT. REENACTMENT SET, EVENING

[The vote is staged with three bowls. Rami and Suli each place a pebble. Lessa, Pipp, and Tavo cast theirs.]

LESSA: The majority chooses Rami as keeper.

RAMI: What is my first duty?

LESSA: Plant the sapling.

RAMI: I thought it was already planted.

LESSA: It is a reenactment.

SULI: The sapling is real.

PIPP: We should put it outside.

TAVO: The camera is ready for the coronation.

LESSA: There was no coronation.

TAVO: We have a crown.

RAMI: It is a basket lid.

TAVO: A coronation can be modest.

[Rami lifts the bark crown. Pipp takes it and puts it over the sapling.]

PIPP: The sapling is keeper now.

SULI: It has no votes.

LESSA: It was planted by everyone.

RAMI: Then it represents the grove.

TAVO: A surprising ending.

LESSA: It is the recorded one.

TAVO: History has changed my script.

### Narrator's cut

INT. EDIT SUITE

[Tavo watches the footage beside Lessa. A dramatic title card reads "THE DAY THE GROVE CHANGED."]

TAVO: The music makes the vote feel historic.

LESSA: It was a Tuesday meeting.

TAVO: We can make Tuesday sound historic.

RAMI: My crown is falling over my eyes.

PIPP: It is a basket lid.

TAVO: The crown must remain in frame.

LESSA: The vote count is three to two.

TAVO: Can we make it three to one? The tension would be sharper.

LESSA: No.

TAVO: Then we will use a close-up of the pebble.

[Suli places a pebble on the editing desk.]

SULI: The pebble was not in the vote.

TAVO: It is now a symbol of the vote.

LESSA: It is a pebble.

### Tag

EXT. SET, SUNSET

[The actors move the sapling outside. The cardboard hall folds behind them.]

RAMI: The crown fits the tree.

SULI: The tree was here first.

[The sapling leans toward the fading sun.]
