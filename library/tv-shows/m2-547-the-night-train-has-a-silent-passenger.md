# The Night Train Has a Silent Passenger

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nocturnal mystery serials, ominous train corridors, and detectives who interpret every quiet passenger as a clue*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: CONDUCTOR VALE, a baboon; DETECTIVE MOSS, an orangutan; PORTER KIP, a gibbon; PASSENGER SERA, a macaque; DISPATCHER, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. NIGHT TRAIN - SLEEPING CAR - NIGHT

A figure sits in the last compartment. The corridor light flickers. Detective Moss approaches with a notebook.

MOSS: You have not spoken since the train left.

The passenger points to a sign: QUIET CAR.

MOSS: That is suspicious.

The conductor walks by, carrying a tray of tea. One cup rattles.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

Moss investigates the silent passenger as if solving a mystery. The train's manifest lists seat 12 as vacant, yet Sera sits there wearing a coat and holding a ticket. The conductor says the passenger boarded at the last station, but no ticket was punched.

VALE: I was checking the ticket when the lights blinked.

MOSS: For how long?

VALE: One blink.

MOSS: A generous window for a disappearance.

The porter says he saw a shadow move between cars. Moss asks whether it had a hat. Kip says every passenger has a hat at night. The dispatcher calls through the intercom to announce a stop, but the speaker crackles and the message is lost. Sera writes something on a napkin and pushes it toward Moss.

MOSS: A note.

SERA: [Writes: I am using a voice rest.]

Moss studies the writing. It is neat, ordinary handwriting. He asks whether the passenger is hiding from someone. Sera shakes her head and points to the tea tray. The conductor offers a cup. Sera declines and points to the rattling cup.

ACT TWO

The train crosses a bridge. Its wheels make a steady clatter. Moss places a glass of water on the compartment table and watches the ripples. The cup rattles in rhythm. Moss believes the train is sending a code. Kip says the dining car kettle is loose.

KIP: It rattles on every bridge.

MOSS: And every bridge has a message.

VALE: The railway has several bridges.

The conductor checks the compartment number against the manifest. Seat 12 is empty because it is a folding seat for the porter when he is off duty. Sera's ticket is for the next car, berth 21. She moved to seat 12 to help Kip sort a dropped stack of blankets and then stayed because the corridor was quieter.

MOSS: You were never assigned to this compartment.

SERA: [Writes: It had a door.]

Moss checks the corridor. The berth door is stuck shut; a folded blanket is wedged in its latch. The porter pulls it free. Sera returns to berth 21 and verifies her ticket. The conductor punches it. No one has vanished; the mystery was a misread seating chart and a quiet passenger who chose a calm place to rest.

The dispatcher repeats the delayed-stop announcement. Moss listens closely, then writes it down. The call is not a code. It says the train will arrive seven minutes late because a freight wagon is being moved at the junction.

INT. NIGHT TRAIN - DINING CAR - NIGHT

Moss interviews the porter beside the kettle. Kip demonstrates how the blanket stack fell when the train crossed the bridge. Sera helped him gather the blankets, then sat in the fold-down seat to rest. The berth number on her ticket was faint, and she had put her coat over the printed destination.

KIP: I saw her move into the quiet car.

MOSS: Did you tell the conductor?

KIP: I thought the conductor knew.

Vale shows Moss the punched ticket. The seat number is 21, not 12. Moss holds the ticket under the dining-car lamp. The mark is clear. Sera writes that she moved because berth 21's door was stuck and she did not want to wake the sleeping passenger in the next compartment.

MOSS: You left your berth because the door would not close?

SERA: [Writes: I tried the latch twice.]

The conductor checks the door and finds the blanket wedged under it. Kip recognizes it as one from the stack. He apologizes for leaving the blanket in the corridor. The dispatcher confirms no passenger was reported missing; the manifest had not been updated because the ticket punch jammed.

The crew clears the blanket, tests the latch, and marks Sera present in berth 21. Moss asks the dispatcher to repeat the arrival time. The dispatcher says the train is seven minutes late. Moss writes it down, then crosses it out when the station clock disagrees.

VALE: The station clock is two minutes fast.

MOSS: I am not asking it questions again.



ACT THREE

The porter repairs the loose kettle strap. The conductor updates the manifest and gives Moss a clean copy. Sera leaves a final note: "Thank you for not making the train stop." Moss folds it into his case.

MOSS: A quiet passenger can still be a witness.

VALE: To what?

MOSS: The timetable.

The train slows at the junction. The wheels clatter over the bridge once more. Moss reaches for his notebook but keeps his voice low.

FINAL IMAGE: Sera sleeps behind the repaired berth door while the conductor punches a ticket under a steady lamp.
