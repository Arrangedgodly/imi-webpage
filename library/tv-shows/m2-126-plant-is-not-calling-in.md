# Your Plant Is Not Calling In

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live call-in gardening shows that diagnose plants from blurry phone images and treat every leaf as a crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Fafa wants a dramatic plant rescue segment. Gardener Anu needs to help callers describe ordinary growing conditions before the studio tries another miracle spray.

Cast: Fafa, a macaque host; Anu, a gorilla gardener; Lomi, a lemur phone screener; and Puck, a capuchin camera operator.

## Episode: "The Yellow Leaf Hotline"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A POTTED FERN SITS ON A ROTATING TABLE.]

FAFA: The hotline is open. If your plant is in danger, call now.

[THE PHONE RINGS. LOMI ANSWERS.]

LOMI: What is the problem?

CALLER: My fern has a yellow leaf.

[FAFA GASPS. PUCK ZOOMS IN ON A GREEN LEAF.]

FAFA: The crisis is spreading.

ANU: Ferns grow new leaves.

[THE YELLOW LEAF FALLS OFF AND LANDS IN FAFA'S NOTES.]

FAFA: It has left the plant.

### Act One

[LOMI PUTS THE CALL ON SPEAKER.]

FAFA: Tell us where the plant lives.

CALLER: By a window.

FAFA: Is the window north, south, east, west, or emotionally complicated?

ANU: How much light does it get?

CALLER: Morning light for an hour.

[FAFA HOLDS UP A BOTTLE LABELED "INSTANT GREEN."]

FAFA: We have a treatment.

ANU: We have a question. How often do you water it?

CALLER: When the soil feels dry.

ANU: That sounds reasonable. Does the pot drain?

[THE CALLER CHECKS. THE POT HAS NO HOLE.]

CALLER: It is sitting in a decorative bowl.

[ANU ASKS THEM TO REMOVE THE INNER POT. WATER POURS INTO THE BOWL. PUCK FILMS IT.]

FAFA: The plant was underwater.

ANU: The roots were too wet. Let the bowl drain and keep the plant in indirect light.

[FAFA LOWERS THE SPRAY BOTTLE.]

FAFA: The rescue is less dramatic than the label.

### Act Two

[THE NEXT CALLER IS PUT THROUGH. A PHOTO APPEARS ON SCREEN: A HOUSEPLANT WITH ONE LEAF TURNED TOWARD THE WINDOW.]

FAFA: This plant is leaning. Is it asking for help?

ANU: It is growing toward light.

LOMI: The caller says it was moved last week.

FAFA: The plant has not adjusted.

ANU: Give it time and turn the pot gradually.

[FAFA TURNS THE POT A FULL CIRCLE. THE LEAVES BRUSH THE PHONE CORD.]

ANU: A quarter turn is enough.

[PUCK MARKS THE POT WITH A SMALL PIECE OF TAPE. FAFA SPINS IT AGAIN TO SHOW THE CAMERA.]

ANU: Now we have to find the original position.

[THE PHONE RINGS AGAIN. LOMI ANSWERS.]

CALLER: My seedling has not grown in two days.

FAFA: We are dispatching the rescue kit.

ANU: It has been two days.

[FAFA OPENS A BOX. IT CONTAINS A MAGNIFYING GLASS, A RULER, AND THE INSTANT GREEN SPRAY.]

ANU: Keep a simple growth note. Seedlings do not need hourly reports.

LOMI: The caller has been measuring it every hour.

FAFA: That is commitment.

ANU: That is a ruler pressed into the soil.

[FAFA REMOVES THE RULER FROM THE POT. THE SEEDLING STRAIGHTENS.]

FAFA: The plant was waiting for room.

ANU: The ruler was leaning on its stem.

[FAFA PLACES THE RULER ON THE TABLE AND CROSSES OUT "EMERGENCY" ON THE CALL SHEET.]

[FAFA OPENS THE PROP BOX AND TAKES OUT A SMALL FAN.]

FAFA: Could a breeze help the fern recover?

ANU: It is not ill.

[PUCK TURNS THE FAN ON. THE FERN'S NEW SHOOT BENDS AWAY FROM THE AIR. ANU SWITCHES THE FAN OFF.]

ANU: It prefers still air.

FAFA: The plant has a preference?

ANU: It has leaves that move when a fan blows.

[LOMI MAKES A NOTE TO KEEP THE FAN OUT OF THE RESCUE KIT.]

### Act Three

[ANU PLACES THE FERN BESIDE THE STUDIO WINDOW. THE SOIL DRAINS INTO A TRAY.]

FAFA: We have three calls and no emergencies.

ANU: We have three callers who understand their plants better.

[THE FIRST CALLER SENDS AN UPDATED PHOTO. THE FERN HAS A NEW GREEN SHOOT.]

FAFA: A comeback.

ANU: Normal growth.

[FAFA TAKES THE INSTANT GREEN BOTTLE OFF THE TABLE AND READS THE LABEL.]

FAFA: "Results may vary."

ANU: Especially if the plant is already green.

[LOMI PUTS THE BOTTLE IN A PROP BIN. PUCK MOVES THE CAMERA TO SHOW THE DRAINAGE TRAY.]

FAFA: Next week: why a cactus does not want a bath.

ANU: Please do not announce a rescue.

[ANU CHECKS THE DRAINAGE TRAY. IT IS EMPTY.]

FAFA: We have a clear tray and a calmer fern.

ANU: We have a drained pot.

[LOMI WRITES THE SIMPLE CARE STEPS ON THE CALL SHEET FOR THE NEXT SEGMENT.]

### Tag

[FAFA WATERS THE STUDIO FERN. ANU STOPS HER.]

ANU: Check the soil first.

[FAFA TOUCHES IT. IT IS STILL WET.]

FAFA: The plant has requested a pause.

[THE PHONE RINGS. LOMI LETS IT RING ONCE, THEN ANSWERS.]

LOMI: What does the soil feel like?
