# The Little Lantern Rescue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rescue drama*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A volunteer rescue team tracks a lost young monkey through fog near the harbor, then learns the most useful clue came from the child herself.

Cast: Captain Ena, a capable baboon; Moss, a calm lemur medic; Tuck, a young capuchin radio operator; Lio, a gibbon searcher; and Mimi, a curious macaque child who has wandered from her family.

## Episode: "The Light in the Fog"

### Cold open

EXT. HARBOR PATH, NIGHT

A lantern swings in the fog. Captain Ena hears a faint tapping from the rocks. She stops the team and signals silence. A small voice calls from the other side of a low seawall.

MIMI: I can see your shoes.

Tuck looks down. His bright yellow boots glow in the lantern light.

### Act one

Mimi's family reports her missing after she left the harbor market to follow a dog. The volunteer rescue team checks the last known route and asks the harbor workers what they saw. No one reports an injury or a fall. The fog has thickened, and the tide is rising. Ena divides the crew into pairs and sets a check-in time.

Lio and Tuck follow the path toward the lighthouse. Moss stays near the family and asks them to describe Mimi's habits. Her brother says she carries a small lantern and taps it against railings when she is bored. Their mother says Mimi knows the way home but sometimes takes shortcuts through the old boat yard.

Ena asks workers to close the yard gates and keep vehicles still. A cart driver offers to search by sound. Moss says moving carts could make it harder to hear. The team uses a low whistle pattern to signal between checkpoints. Tuck records each reply on the radio map.

### Act two

Lio finds small footprints near the boat yard but loses them where the stone path meets wet sand. He notices a lantern reflection on a warehouse wall. The team approaches slowly and calls Mimi's name. She answers from behind the seawall, then taps her lantern twice against the stone.

Mimi is safe but cannot climb over the wall because the stones are slippery. Moss checks the tide and sees the water is rising along the lower path. Ena tells Lio to bring a low step ladder from the rescue van. She warns him not to jump down to Mimi; the wall could shift and trap them both.

Tuck stays on the radio with Mimi. He asks her what she can see. She describes the yellow boots, a red rope, and a broken boat sign. Tuck looks at the map and identifies the corner of the old net shed. He tells Ena their position. Mimi says she followed the dog until it ducked through the yard gate, then the fog hid the market lights.

The dog appears on the wall and shakes water onto Lio. It belongs to a harbor keeper, who recognizes its collar. The keeper secures the dog and tells the team the nearest safe ladder is stored inside the net shed. The gate is locked, but he has the key.

### Act three

Ena and Lio set the ladder against the seawall from the dry side. Moss checks the stones while Tuck maintains contact with Mimi. The ladder rests on a stable section. Ena climbs down one rung at a time, keeping three points of contact. She reaches Mimi and asks if she feels hurt or cold. Mimi says her knees are wet and she is embarrassed.

Ena wraps her in a blanket and guides her up the ladder. Moss checks her hands and feet, then returns her to her family. Her mother hugs her, and her brother holds the lantern. Mimi says she chose the seawall because she could hear waves and thought they would lead back to the harbor.

The team reviews the call. They note that Mimi stayed in one place, answered when called, and used her lantern to signal. The family agrees to add a small whistle to her coat and practice a meeting point for busy market days. Ena asks them not to scold her until everyone is warm and rested. Her mother says she was too worried to think about scolding.

The rescue crew returns to the station. Tuck updates the map with the net shed ladder location. Lio dries the dog-shaped water marks off his sleeve. Moss checks the tide chart and posts a note about the slippery seawall.

### Tag

The next afternoon, Mimi visits the station with her family. She taps her lantern twice. Tuck answers over the radio with two clicks.

MIMI: I know that signal now.

TUCK: So do we.

The dog wags its tail against the door and knocks three times.
