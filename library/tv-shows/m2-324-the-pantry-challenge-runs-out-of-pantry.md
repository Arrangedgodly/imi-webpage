# The Pantry Challenge Runs Out of Pantry

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competitions, timed pantry challenges, frantic tasting panels, and hosts who announce twists after contestants have started cooking*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: Two cooks must create a meal from a tiny pantry basket, but the show's surprise twists keep removing the ingredients they need.

Cast: Host Zuzu, a squirrel monkey; cook Miri, a macaque; cook Odo, a capuchin; judge Nari, an orangutan; judge Vex, a gibbon; and prop assistant Tavi, a lemur.

## Episode: "Three Ingredients, Four Judges"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO KITCHEN. A TIMER COUNTS DOWN.]

ZUZU: You have twenty minutes and three ingredients.

MIRI: There are four ingredients.

Zuzu looks in the basket. One is a timer.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The contestants find apples, oats, and yogurt in their baskets. Zuzu says the pantry challenge is about creativity. Odo asks whether salt counts as an ingredient. The rules card says there is a twist, but not what it is. Nari and Vex sit at the tasting table while Tavi films close-ups of the baskets.

Miri plans baked apple cups. Odo plans chilled oat patties with yogurt. The clock starts. Zuzu announces that no knives may be used because the sponsor's demonstration blade is missing. Tavi finds it under the timer. The cooks agree not to use it anyway.

A producer wheels in a garnish cart that holds only empty ramekins. Odo arranges them in a circle around his plate.

Nari asks how they will present their dishes. Miri says in bowls. Vex says the plate should tell a story. Odo puts one oat patty upright like a tiny door. Vex asks what the door symbolizes. Odo says it keeps the yogurt from running off.

### Act Two

With twelve minutes remaining, Zuzu announces a second twist: contestants may use the emergency pantry. The pantry contains napkins and a bowl. Miri uses the bowl to mix oats. Odo uses a napkin to keep the cutting board from sliding. The crew applauds the creative use of supplies. Vex asks whether the napkin is edible. Odo tells him it is a napkin.

Miri heats the apples, but the timer has been set to count upward. She checks the wall clock and corrects the time. Odo discovers his portable burner is not plugged in. He switches to a no-cook dish. The camera crew asks him to pretend the burner stopped dramatically. Odo looks at the burner, then continues shaping patties.

Zuzu takes a card from the twist bowl. It says "change one dish with a mystery ingredient." Tavi opens the cooler and reveals a second container of yogurt. Nari says the ingredient is not mysterious. Zuzu asks the producer for a new card. The new card says "cook with confidence." The contestants already are.

The producer rolls a dramatic clock graphic onto the monitor. Its countdown runs backward, then pauses at 00:00 while the kitchen timer still has six minutes left. Zuzu declares the graphic a warning. Tavi asks whether he should restart it. Miri says she needs the burner, not a warning. The producer changes the graphic to NOW COOKING, though neither contestant has stopped cooking.

Vex circles the kitchen, asking both cooks what they fear most. Miri says the apples may be too hot. Odo says the judges might expect a garnish. Vex gives them each a sprig of parsley from his lapel. It is plastic. Miri puts hers on the side of the plate, and Odo uses his as a tiny flag beside the oat door.

### Act Three

The cooks present their food. Miri's apple cups are warm and soft, with oats packed inside. Odo's oat patties are crisp at the edges and served with yogurt. The judges taste both. Vex pauses before his verdict long enough for Zuzu to flip through the rules card twice.

NARI: Miri balanced the sweetness.

VEX: Odo gave the dish a useful shape.

ZUZU: Who wins?

Nari says Miri takes the challenge for her cooked apples, while Odo wins the sponsor's bowl for using every ingredient. The prize basket is empty except for a timer. Odo turns it over; it is a kitchen timer that counts down from one minute. He winds it and places it beside the judges. It rings before Vex can give a second speech.

The cooks share the remaining apples with the crew. Tavi asks for a close-up of the final plate, but Zuzu points to the empty bowls. The camera captures the food already eaten.

### Tag

[STUDIO KITCHEN.]

Zuzu announces a surprise final challenge: wash the dishes in thirty seconds. The sink is unplugged from the water supply for the set. Miri hands her a napkin.

MIRI: Emergency pantry.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
