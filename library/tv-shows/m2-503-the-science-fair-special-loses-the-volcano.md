# The Science Fair Special Loses the Volcano

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: science fair broadcasts that demand spectacle from projects designed to explain a clear idea*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Pera, a lemur; student Beni, a macaque; student Ralo, an orangutan; judge Nima, a gibbon; producer Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCIENCE FAIR STAGE - DAY

Beni stands beside a model volcano. A title card reads BIGGEST ERUPTION WINS.

BENI: My project shows how water pressure moves through layers.

[The volcano emits one small bubble of foam.]

ACT ONE

INT. STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Tavi asks Beni to make the eruption taller. Judge Nima says the project is about pressure, not height. Ralo presents a bridge made from paper strips. The bridge holds a stack of books. Pera asks whether it can hold the host. Ralo says it is designed for books, not hosts.

Pera steps near the bridge. Ralo moves it farther back. The camera zooms on the books instead of the bridge. Tavi asks for a dramatic collapse. Ralo removes one book; the bridge stays up. He removes another. It still holds. Nima asks him to explain the load. The producer signals the camera to get ready for a crash.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Tavi sets a countdown graphic for the volcano. Beni says the experiment should be done at room temperature. The graphic counts down anyway. The foam bubbles over the tray and spills onto the title card. The camera operator reaches for a cloth. Tavi asks for slow motion. Nima lifts the card before it dissolves.

BENI: The liquid is spreading because the tray is tilted.

Pera looks at the table leg, which rests on a folded rundown sheet. She straightens the sheet. The tray levels and the foam stops creeping toward the cable. Tavi asks whether they can call it a second eruption. Nima asks him to move the title card out of the liquid.

[The camera focuses on a paper tower at the next table. Ralo's bridge enters the frame behind it, still standing. Pera asks Ralo to explain why the tower fell. The tower is not his project.]

INT. FAIR FLOOR - MOMENTS LATER

The students move their exhibits to the judging line. Beni places the volcano tray on a level board. The foam settles. Ralo demonstrates the bridge again, adding books one at a time. Tavi turns on the countdown. Nima switches it off and asks the crew to record the demonstration at normal speed.

Pera asks the students to state their results. Beni shows the layer marks inside a clear tube. Ralo points to the bridge supports. Their explanations fit within the segment. Tavi asks for a surprise reveal; Beni turns the tube around and shows an unexpected air pocket. The audience leans forward.

INT. FAIR FLOOR - MOMENTS LATER

Judge Nima visits each table with a clipboard. Beni explains the air pocket in the tube. Nima asks him to show the mark where the water stopped. Beni points to the line. At Ralo's table, Nima places a second book on the bridge. The paper sags but does not tear. Tavi asks whether this can be called a collapse. Nima removes the book; the bridge rises again. Pera asks the students to explain what the test showed, and lets them finish before the camera cuts. Nima signs both result cards. The ribbons go to separate hooks.

ACT THREE

INT. SCIENCE FAIR STAGE - AFTERNOON

Nima awards the clarity ribbon to Beni and the structure ribbon to Ralo. Tavi asks if there is a single grand prize. Nima says the categories are separate. The host's trophy table has only one slot. Pera tries to place both ribbons on the same stand; one slides off.

Ralo folds the paper bridge and sets it beside the books. Beni pours the remaining water from the tube into the tray. The foam does not rise again. Pera asks the viewers which display was more exciting. Nima points to the experiment cards, each with a question, a test and a result. The cameras finally show both exhibits at once.

Tavi changes the lower-third from BIGGEST ERUPTION WINS to TWO PROJECTS, TWO FINDINGS. He asks Beni if the volcano can erupt again. Beni says the project is packed away. Ralo offers the paper bridge for the closing shot, but asks everyone not to lean on it. Pera stands back. The bridge holds one last book.

TAG

INT. STAGE - AFTER THE FAIR

Tavi stores the countdown graphic. Nima returns the ribbons. Pera lifts the title card out of the damp tray and reads its blank side.

FINAL IMAGE: The volcano sits quietly beside the folded bridge, while both ribbons hang from separate hooks.
