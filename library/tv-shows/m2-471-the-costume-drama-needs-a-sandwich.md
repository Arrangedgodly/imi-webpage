# The Costume Drama Needs a Sandwich

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: prestige period dramas, elaborate costume continuity, and cliffhangers staged around an ordinary lunch break*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: actor Vali, a macaque; actor Eko, an orangutan; director Sura, a baboon; continuity supervisor Nemi, a gibbon; prop keeper Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - DAY

A drawing room contains a velvet chair, a tea tray, and a letter sealed with red wax. Vali wears a coat with a gold cuff. Eko holds a script. The director watches a monitor.

VALI: I cannot accept this letter.

EKO: You accepted it in yesterday's scene.

NEMI: Yesterday's scene is set after lunch. This is before lunch.

[The prop keeper enters carrying a tray with sandwiches. Sura looks toward the monitor.] 

SURA: The sandwich cannot appear in the period drama.

TAVI: The sandwich is for the cast.

[The letter's red wax seal falls off the envelope and lands on the tea tray.] 

ACT ONE

INT. DRAWING ROOM SET - CONTINUOUS

The continuity supervisor checks the scene order. The letter is opened only after the character refuses it; the director wants a second take with a different cuff position. Nemi says the gold cuff must stay on Vali's left sleeve in every shot. Tavi places the seal back on its marked dish.

NEMI: Cuff left, letter unsealed for the first take, cup on the table.

SURA: Can we add a close-up of the wax?

[Between takes, Tavi checks that the old loose seal is on its marked dish and the envelope has no wax left. He presses fresh stage wax onto the envelope for the next take; Nemi records the reset on the continuity slate.]

NEMI: Only after Vali opens the letter.

EKO: I can turn toward the window before the line.

VALI: The window is painted.

[The actor turns anyway. The camera catches the painted view. Sura asks the camera operator for a more distant shot that hides the seam.] 

SURA: We need the room to feel expensive.

NEMI: It is rented by the hour.

[The director calls action. Vali refuses the letter, Eko insists the family must read it, and the tea tray rattles when the actor rests a hand on it. Tavi steadies the tray between takes.] 

ACT TWO

INT. GREEN ROOM - LATER

The lunch break arrives. The cast sits at folding tables outside the set. Sura asks for one more scene before the meal; Nemi points out that the script places a two-hour time jump between scenes. The actors cannot perform the afternoon scene while continuity records a pre-lunch sleeve.

SURA: Could lunch happen off camera and keep the scene going?

NEMI: The characters are in 1890. The cast is eating now.

VALI: My character has been holding a sealed letter for six takes.

EKO: He has also been standing by the tea tray.

[Tavi hands Vali a sandwich wrapped in paper. Nemi checks that crumbs stay away from the gold cuff and costume coat. The prop keeper sets a separate plate for the wax seal.] 

TAVI: Lunch is not a prop.

SURA: It could become one in the next episode.

NEMI: Then it needs to be on the call sheet.

[The cast eats. Sura asks for the sandwich to be removed before the camera returns to the set. Tavi stores the leftovers on the green room cart; the original loose seal remains on its marked dish.]

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - AFTER LUNCH

[Nemi checks the collar of Vali's coat before the take. The gold cuff is on the correct sleeve, but the buttons are fastened in a different order than the previous shot. The continuity supervisor corrects them while Tavi keeps the letter closed.] 

NEMI: Three buttons, left cuff, wax facing camera.

SURA: We can hide the buttons in shadow.

NEMI: Or fasten them consistently.

[The actor straightens the coat. Eko rehearses the line once without the letter so the paper does not rustle over the dialogue. Sura lowers the music cue that had been covering the words.] 

EKO: We will hear the postponement clearly.

VALI: And then we can finally sit down.

[The director moves the velvet chair into the frame, making sure it does not block the camera's view of the letter.] 

ACT THREE

INT. PERIOD DRAMA SET - LATER

The crew resumes with the later scene. Nemi changes the continuity slate to AFTER LUNCH. Vali now wears a different cuff. He breaks the fresh seal and opens the letter; Eko holds the letter open. The director wants a cliffhanger on the new wax seal. Nemi checks the script: the letter reveals that the tea party has been postponed, not a family scandal.

VALI: The party is postponed until Thursday.

EKO: We have two days to prepare.

SURA: Can we end on the seal?

NEMI: The fresh seal broke when Vali opened the letter. It is on the dish.

[The actor holds up the letter. Tavi passes the broken fresh seal to Nemi, who places it beside the open envelope for the matching close-up. The camera records the wax, then the actors' faces.] 

SURA: Cut. That is our cliffhanger.

VALI: A postponed tea party.

EKO: Two days of suspense.

[The director calls wrap. Nemi checks the cuff, the letter, and the seal against the continuity notes. Tavi returns the tea tray to the prop shelf.] 

TAG

Tavi carries the cast's leftover sandwiches past the drawing room. Sura looks at the tray, then at the empty set. Nemi labels it LUNCH, NOT PERIOD PROP.

FINAL IMAGE: The red wax seal rests beside the open letter as the costume rack rolls away from the drawing room set.
