# Professor Puddle's Very Small Question

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's educational television, cheerful repetition, puppet interruptions, and lessons that grow too elaborate for a simple experiment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A children's science host plans to teach floating and sinking, but the puppet's production notes turn one tub of water into a chaotic demonstration.

Cast: Professor Puddle, a gibbon; puppet Pipkin, a squirrel monkey; assistant Nari, an orangutan; camera operator Vex, a macaque; and student guest Fenn, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Will It Float?"

### Cold Open

[BRIGHT STUDIO. A TUB OF WATER SITS ON A TABLE.]

PROFESSOR PUDDLE: Today, one question: will this pebble float?

PIPKIN: Maybe if it believes.

The pebble drops straight to the bottom.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

Professor Puddle welcomes the audience and introduces Fenn, who brought a pebble from the school garden. Pipkin interrupts to ask whether the pebble has a name. Fenn calls it Pebble. The puppet asks if Pebble has a family. Nari says the rock has no role in today's science question.

PUDDLE: We can make a careful test.

PIPKIN: Can we make it sing first?

Vex rolls a graphic onto the screen: THE MYSTERY OF THE DEEP. It shows a tiny pebble surrounded by cartoon fish. Nari asks him to remove the fish because there is only a tub. Vex reduces the graphic to a blue ripple, which remains on screen after the camera has moved to the table.

Fenn drops Pebble into the water. It sinks. Professor Puddle explains that it is denser than the water. Pipkin asks if the tub is sad. Puddle says the tub is a container. Pipkin hugs it anyway, making the water wobble across the table.

### Act Two

The show moves to a second test: a cork. It floats. Pipkin insists the cork is surfing. Nari keeps it still with a spoon. Vex adds beach music, then a title card reading CORK'S BIG WAVE. The cork drifts into a corner of the tub. Puddle retrieves it with a net and explains that the shape and material matter.

Pipkin asks if a spoon will float. Nari says it depends on the spoon and sets a metal spoon in the water. It sinks. The puppet asks whether the spoon can have a second chance. Nari turns it bowl-up; it still sinks. Puddle tries a wooden spoon. It floats, and Pipkin calls it a boat. Fenn places the cork on the wooden spoon. Both stay at the surface until Pipkin leans over the tub and splashes them.

PIPKIN: The boat had a storm.

NARI: The storm was you.

Puddle summarizes the test: objects behave differently in water, and careful observation helps. Fenn records the results on a card: pebble down, cork up, spoon depends on material. Pipkin tries to add a fourth column for personality. Nari gives the puppet a pencil, and he writes 'splashy' beside his own name. The card goes on the studio wall, where the camera can show the actual observations. Vex signals that the show should repeat the lesson. The camera returns to the empty tub; the props are on the floor. Nari reaches for them, but the cue card says keep smiling. She recovers the cork with a net while facing the camera.

PUDDLE: We can ask one more question. Does this sponge float?

NARI: It does, unless you squeeze it.

Pipkin squeezes the sponge; water sprays onto Vex's graphic monitor. The blue ripple disappears. Vex tries to keep the background graphic alive by holding up a blue card. It covers the sponge and most of his face.

### Act Three

The group resets the demonstration with a clear tub and three objects: pebble, cork and wooden spoon. Puddle asks the audience to predict what will happen. Fenn says the pebble sinks, the cork floats and the wooden spoon floats. They test each one. The results match. Pipkin is disappointed there is no surprise, so Nari shows that a sealed plastic cup can float when empty and sink when filled with water.

Pipkin tries to fill the cup with air. Nari explains that air is already inside it. Fenn adds a little water, and the cup sits lower. The demonstration is clear. The puppet salutes the cup's bravery. Puddle asks the viewers to try the activity with an adult, using a safe tub and objects that can get wet.

The show closes. Vex finally restores the ripple graphic. It appears over the end credits, where it covers the studio address. Nari moves the card aside. Pipkin peeks around it and says good-bye to Pebble, now dry on a towel.

### Tag

[AFTER RECORDING.]

Pipkin asks if the towel will float. Nari tests one corner in the tub. It floats, then absorbs water and sinks. The puppet gives it a tiny medal for trying.
