# The Debate Show Runs Out of Speaking Time

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised debates, countdown clocks, split-screen interruptions, and panels that award points for using the full minute*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Moderator Seli, a macaque; panelist Pavi, a baboon; panelist Loro, a gibbon; timekeeper Oda, an orangutan; floor producer Kemi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. DEBATE STUDIO - NIGHT

Two panelists sit before a screen that reads PUBLIC BENCH DESIGN. A countdown clock starts at 60 seconds. The moderator holds a card marked OPENING STATEMENT.

SELI: Tonight we debate whether the park bench should face the path or the pond.

PAVI: The answer depends on where the bench is installed.

LORO: We have both been given the same bench diagram.

[The clock reaches 58. A buzzer sounds. The timekeeper raises a sign: TOO MUCH AGREEMENT.] 

ACT ONE

INT. DEBATE STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

The moderator asks each panelist for an opening position. Pavi wants the bench facing the path so visitors can see the sign. Loro prefers the pond so people can watch ducks. Seli asks them to identify the disagreement. Oda resets the clock.

PAVI: I want the sign visible.

LORO: I want the view visible.

SELI: A breakthrough.

[The graphic operator displays two boxes labeled YES and NO. Both panelists lean toward the YES camera. Kemi asks the control booth to split the screen evenly.] 

KEMI: The graphic makes it look like one answer is winning.

ODA: Neither has spoken yet.

[The host asks whether the bench can rotate. Pavi says the maintenance crew would need room around it. Loro says the park already has two benches, one faces each direction. The countdown hits zero and a horn blares.] 

LORO: We have answered the question.

SELI: There are forty seconds left in the segment.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The panelists review the bench diagram. The debate producer asks Seli for a conflict card. Kemi finds a note that says THE BENCH IS NOT INSTALLED YET. The moderator turns it toward the camera. The studio audience murmurs.

PAVI: Then we are debating a proposed location.

LORO: We should ask park visitors where they want it.

SELI: The segment brief says we choose tonight.

KEMI: The location vote is scheduled for next week's community meeting.

[The countdown clock resets to 90 seconds. Seli asks the panelists for a closing argument. Oda hands them a card that reads ONE SENTENCE EACH. Pavi summarizes the path view; Loro summarizes the pond view.]

PAVI: Put the bench near the path so the posted map can be read.

LORO: Angle it toward the pond so people can rest and watch the water.

[The moderator asks them to respond to each other. They agree both conditions could be met if the bench is placed at the curve. Kemi points to the diagram's curve, where both the sign and pond are visible.] 

SELI: We have found a location.

ODA: The timer is still running.

[Kemi places a miniature bench and a paper map on the desk. The panelists show how a person approaching from the path can see the notice while seated facing the pond. Seli asks them to keep the model within the camera frame.] 

PAVI: Angle the bench a little toward the curve.

LORO: Then both the water and the sign are visible.

ODA: We have found a third option without changing the question.

[The graphic operator updates the diagram. The countdown begins again, but Seli stops it before it reaches zero.] 

SELI: The segment is complete. The meeting will decide the placement.

[The timekeeper puts the buzzer down and writes the meeting location on the studio board. The audience sees the date rather than a score.] 

ACT THREE

INT. DEBATE STUDIO - NIGHT

The panelists state that the park meeting will decide the exact placement. The show graphic changes from YES/NO to PATH/POND. The moderator tries to start a final lightning round, but Oda turns the clock off. Kemi hands Seli the meeting notice and asks the host to put the date on screen.

SELI: The public meeting is Thursday at six, at the park shelter.

PAVI: We can bring the diagram.

LORO: And listen to people who use the bench.

[The audience claps. The timekeeper lifts the buzzer but leaves it silent. The producer asks whether there should be a winner. Seli points to the words COMMUNITY DECIDES on the screen.] 

SELI: The next step belongs to the park users.

KEMI: That is the agenda, not a tie.

[The moderator ends the segment. The panelists take their copies of the diagram, and the stage crew removes the debate podiums.] 

TAG

The countdown clock flashes zero. Oda unplugs it. The studio lights stay on for the next show.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty debate set shows one bench diagram and a meeting notice, with no winner banner.
