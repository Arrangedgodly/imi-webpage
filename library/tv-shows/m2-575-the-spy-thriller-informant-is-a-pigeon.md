# The Spy Thriller Informant Is a Pigeon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: spy thrillers, coded messages, and handlers who mistake ordinary pigeon behavior for espionage*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Agent Sela, a macaque; handler Varo, an orangutan; courier pigeon Piri, a pigeon; analyst Tavi, a gibbon; station chief Odo, a baboon; animal keeper, floor crew and studio director (nonspeaking).

COLD OPEN

EXT. ROOFTOP - NIGHT

A pigeon lands on a windowsill and coos. Agent Sela listens through an earpiece.

SELA: The informant has arrived.

VARO: That is a pigeon.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The spy series is filming a nighttime operation on a studio rooftop. The production has put a tiny red ribbon on Piri, a homing pigeon from the animal unit, and the script says she will deliver a coded message. Agent Sela interprets every coo as a warning. Handler Varo asks whether the note attached to the bird is from the actual message team or the animal keeper.

SELA: Two short coos, one long. That is the code.

TAVI: That is her normal call when she lands.

ODO: The paper tube is clipped to her tracking tag. It holds the keeper's note, not a spy message.

VARO: What does the red ribbon signify?

TAVI: It means she is camera-ready. The green one means rehearsal.

SELA: The network has trained a bird for fieldwork.

ODO: The network has trained the bird to follow a familiar perch. She has not been near any fieldwork.

The show wants to use a dramatic close-up of Piri's leg tag. Sela lifts her gloved hand to retrieve it, but Odo stops her and asks the animal handler to open the paper holder after Piri settles in the carrier. The bird pecks a crumb from the sill and flutters onto the wrong perch.

SELA: She is avoiding the extraction.

TAVI: She saw the food bowl.

VARO: The food bowl is a decoy.

ODO: It is her dinner.

ACT TWO

The keeper guides Piri into the carrier with a seed cup. Once she settles, Sela's team opens the paper tube clipped to the tracking tag and places the tube on the table. The note reads CHECK PERCH LATCH, written by the animal crew after the latch slipped during rehearsal. The so-called coded message is a maintenance reminder. Sela insists that the phrase may have a second meaning, so Tavi checks the set map and finds the perch is, in fact, missing one screw.

SELA: The pigeon delivered a warning about the perch.

ODO: The keeper wrote the warning and attached it to the tracking tag.

VARO: Could the screw be a signal?

TAVI: It is a screw. I can replace it.

The floor crew tightens the latch. Piri watches them from the carrier, then hops onto the newly secured perch. The show camera zooms toward her beak as she pecks the red ribbon.

SELA: She is destroying the evidence.

ODO: She is grooming the ribbon because it is loose.

TAVI: The evidence is in the tube, which is on the table, with the maintenance note.

Sela asks the analyst to run a code-break sequence. Tavi decodes it as a list of ordinary handling instructions: secure the perch, remove ribbon after the shot, return Piri to the animal unit. The studio director wants Sela to say the pigeon passed an intelligence test. Odo refuses; Piri has followed familiar cues and received no spy training.

VARO: What if we call her an asset?

ODO: Call her a bird. She has an owner and a care plan.

Piri lifts off from the perch and flies back into the carrier when the handler places a seed cup inside. The studio crew gets its dramatic landing shot. Sela asks whether she has returned with another message. The handler checks the tag and finds that the loose ribbon has slipped free of the bird and hangs from the carrier hook.

TAVI: The ribbon is on the hook, not the bird.

SELA: We have lost the visual code.

ODO: A loose decoration came off before it could tangle her feet.

ACT THREE

The production changes the scene. Instead of claiming Piri is a spy, Sela narrates the actual operation: the animal handler confirms the bird is comfortable and the perch is secured, then explains that the tracking tag will be removed before her return trip. Varo receives the real script note from the writer: the fictional courier in the episode is a human agent who walks onto the roof in the following episode.

VARO: The informant was never the pigeon.

SELA: We have been interrogating the wrong character.

TAVI: You have been listening to a bird.

The crew places the note in the continuity binder, not on Piri. The handler removes the tracking tag and unhooks the loose ribbon from the carrier latch, then places the ribbon on the prop table under a COSTUME ONLY label. Piri accepts a seed inside the carrier. The handler closes its door for the trip back to the animal unit.

FINAL IMAGE: Piri departs in her carrier while Agent Sela studies the perch-latch reminder as if it still might have a secret.
