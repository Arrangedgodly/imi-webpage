# Crafts in the Commons

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: public-access craft programs whose hosts overcomplicate simple materials and live television*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A neighborhood craft host attempts to make a leaf-print banner on a tiny public-access set. The live pledge clock, an eager young monkey, and a shortage of paper turn one simple project into an elaborate broadcast crisis.

Cast: Host Marn, a capuchin; camera operator Seli, a spider monkey; caller Pip, a squirrel monkey; floor manager Oru, a baboon; and a young monkey visiting the studio.

## Episode: "Prints of the People"

### Cold Open

[THE SET HAS A TABLE, PAPER, A BOWL OF PAINT, AND A LIVE DONATION METER.]

MARN: Welcome to Crafts in the Commons, where a leaf becomes a memory.

ORU: We have three minutes before the studio lease pledge.

MARN: A leaf becomes a memory on a deadline.

[SELI LIFTS A LEAF. A TINY MONKEY REACHES OVER THE TABLE AND TAKES IT.]

MARN: Our first volunteer has arrived.

### Act One

[MARN SHOWS A SAMPLE PRINT. IT IS A SINGLE GREEN SMUDGE.]

MARN: Press the leaf gently into paint, then onto paper. The veins make the pattern.

ORU: The pledge meter is not moving.

MARN: The art needs room to breathe.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO THE METER. IT READS $0.]

SELI: We are supposed to remind viewers to call.

MARN: We are supposed to make art.

[THE PHONE RINGS.]

PIP: I called to say my leaf is too big for the paper.

MARN: Fold the paper.

PIP: Now the leaf is too big for the folded paper.

MARN: Turn it diagonally.

PIP: Now it is a different leaf.

ORU: That counts as a second craft.

[THE LITTLE MONKEY RETURNS WITH A SMALLER LEAF AND A PAINTBRUSH.]

MARN: That is a strong choice.

ORU: Please do not add a new segment.

MARN: We have an emerging artist.

### Act Two

[MARN PRESSES THE LEAF TO PAPER. THE PAINT BOWL SLIDES TOWARD THE EDGE.]

SELI: Bowl.

[MARN CATCHES IT WITH ONE HAND, STAMPING THE LEAF WITH THE OTHER. THE PRINT IS A NEARLY PERFECT HANDPRINT.]

MARN: That is not a leaf.

ORU: It is a hand.

MARN: We will call it collaborative.

[THE DONATION METER BEEPS. A VIEWER HAS PLEDGED $8.]

SELI: The handprint raised eight dollars.

MARN: The hand of the community.

ORU: We need sixty dollars to renew the room.

PIP: Can I pledge if my picture is just a fold?

MARN: A fold is a choice.

PIP: Then I pledge one dollar.

[THE METER RISES TO $9. THE LITTLE MONKEY PRINTS A LEAF, THEN PRESSES HIS WHOLE PALM IN THE PAINT.]

MARN: A bold second mark.

ORU: We cannot let him paint the equipment.

[THE MONKEY REACHES FOR THE CAMERA CABLE. SELI MOVES IT AND GIVES HIM A SCRAP OF PAPER.]

SELI: Here. The cable is not a canvas.

MARN: Today we discovered a boundary and a medium.

[MARN LAYS OUT A SECOND SHEET AND INVITES THE CREW TO MAKE A PRINT.]

MARN: Viewers can try along at home. Put a scrap of paper under the leaf so the table stays clean.

ORU: We are out of scrap paper.

SELI: We can use the old pledge cards.

ORU: Those have phone numbers on the back.

MARN: Then the print will have a way to call us.

[SELI TURNS THE CARDS FACE DOWN. THE LITTLE MONKEY PICKS ONE UP, SEES THE NUMBER, AND PRESSES A PAINTED LEAF OVER IT.]

MARN: A private number protected by public art.

ORU: The card is upside down.

MARN: The print has no required orientation.

PIP: My leaf tore the paper.

MARN: Tape it from behind.

PIP: I used the front.

[THE PAPER NOW HAS A STRIP OF TAPE THROUGH ITS CENTER.]

SELI: It looks like a river.

PIP: It is the path from my house to the tree.

MARN: Then we have a map.

ORU: And a second pledge.

[THE DONATION METER RISES BY FOUR DOLLARS. MARN COPIES PIP'S RIVER DESIGN ONTO THE MIDDLE OF THE BANNER.]

MARN: One tiny repair has become the center of the design.

[THE PHONE LINES LIGHT UP. SELI PUTS EACH NEW PLEDGE AMOUNT IN FRAME BESIDE A HANDPRINT. AS THE PRINTS SPREAD ACROSS THE BANNER, THE METER CLIMBS TO $59.]

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK HAS THIRTY SECONDS LEFT. MARN HOLDS UP THE BANNER, NOW COVERED IN SMALL LEAF PRINTS.]

MARN: Our project is a commons banner. Each print marks a place someone cared for.

ORU: And the pledge meter is at fifty-nine dollars.

PIP: I have another dollar.

[THE METER CLICKS TO SIXTY. ORU TURNS OFF THE LEASE COUNTDOWN.]

ORU: The room is renewed for another month.

MARN: The banner belongs to the viewers.

SELI: Where should we hang it?

[THE LITTLE MONKEY CLIMBS THE CURTAIN AND HANGS THE BANNER OVER THE SET CLOCK.]

MARN: That is not the wall.

ORU: We cannot reach it before the credits.

[THE BANNER COVERS THE CLOCK. MARN LOOKS TO CAMERA.]

MARN: Next week, we will make a step stool. Please pledge for the wood.

[THE METER BEEPS. A VIEWER PLEDGES ONE DOLLAR.]
