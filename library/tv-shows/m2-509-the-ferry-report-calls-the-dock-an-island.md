# The Ferry Report Calls the Dock an Island

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel news segments that overstate a small route delay as an expedition into the unknown*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Pavi, a gibbon; reporter Sela, a macaque; captain Toma, an orangutan; map operator Reni, a lemur; passenger Kori, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. FERRY DOCK - MORNING

Pavi faces a map showing a dotted line across the harbor. The ferry sits behind him, tied to the dock.

PAVI: We are about to cross into uncharted waters.

CAPTAIN TOMA: The route is on the sign.

ACT ONE

EXT. DOCK - CONTINUOUS

Sela reports that a loose rope delayed departure by three minutes. Reni's map shows the ferry halfway across the bay. Toma says the vessel has not left the dock. Pavi asks the camera to hold on the map; Reni drags the boat icon back. The route line follows it and loops around the dock.

KORI: Is this ferry running?

Toma says yes, after the rope is secured. Sela asks for a dramatic shot of the rope. A deckhand ties it correctly and puts the line away. Pavi says the crossing is imminent. The ferry remains tied while passengers board.

ACT TWO

INT. FERRY CABIN - LATER

The crew enters the cabin for a live report. Kori sits beside the window and points to the opposite shore. Pavi asks whether the destination feels distant. Kori says it is a short ride and she can see her house. Reni's map updates; the ferry icon appears on land. Sela reports a navigational mystery.

Toma looks out the window. The boat has not moved; Reni's location feed is set to the dock's maintenance cart. She finds the tracker clipped to the cart handle. The operator rolls it toward the gangway. The map shows the ferry advancing at walking speed.

[The deckhand lifts the tracker off the cart and clips it to the ferry rail. The map jumps across the harbor and back again.]

Pavi asks whether the ferry has crossed twice. Toma says it is still tied. Sela interviews a passenger about the delay. The passenger asks if the interview can wait until after boarding. The crew steps aside. A family carrying a stroller walks through the shot and onto the ramp.

EXT. DOCK - MOMENTS LATER

The captain unties the ferry. The horn sounds once. Reni's map shows the right location, but the route graphic starts at the wrong end. Pavi describes the crossing as a bold journey from the island to the mainland. Toma says both docks are on the mainland. Kori says the water is the harbor.

INT. FERRY CABIN - MOMENTS LATER

Reni compares the tracker log with the captain's departure sheet. The tracker moved before the ferry because it was attached to the cart; the vessel did not leave early. Sela reads the corrected times aloud. Pavi asks whether the route graphic can show the actual harbor, not a dramatic ocean. Reni draws the two docks and labels them. Kori points out the bus connection. The crew films her following the sign without a narrator calling it an expedition. Toma keeps the ferry tied until every passenger is aboard. He checks the ramp before departure. Sela waits until the last passenger clears the dock. The route card remains visible. No animation plays.

ACT THREE

EXT. HARBOR - LATE MORNING

The ferry moves across the water. Sela reports the short crossing and a clear landing. Pavi keeps the map on screen. Reni leaves the icon alone. The boat reaches the opposite dock. The crew walks off with the passengers. The camera follows Kori as she reaches the bus stop and checks the route map.

Pavi asks for a closing reaction. Kori says the ride was smooth and the transfer is nearby. Reni's graphic attempts to draw an island circle around the dock. She shuts it off. Toma signs the arrival log. Sela reads the crossing time from the actual clock: seven minutes.

PAVI: We have reached our destination.

TOMA: We are still in the same city.

The crew turns to the dock sign, which lists both stops under CITY FERRY. Pavi asks Reni to use the route name instead of an island graphic. She puts up a simple line with two docks and a seven-minute mark. Kori boards the bus. The ferry remains behind her, tied at the opposite dock.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE REPORT

Pavi reviews the segment. The map shows the tracker on the boat, the two docks, and the actual route. Sela removes the phrase UNCHARTED WATERS from the rundown.

FINAL IMAGE: The ferry crosses between two marked docks on a straight line while the studio map stays still.
