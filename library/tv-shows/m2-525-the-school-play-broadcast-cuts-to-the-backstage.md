# The School Play Broadcast Cuts to the Backstage

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: school-play broadcasts that cut to backstage camera feeds during the most important lines*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: director Pavi, a lemur; student actor Kori, a macaque; stage manager Dena, an orangutan; camera operator Feli, a capuchin; narrator Ranu, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM - EVENING

Kori steps into a spotlight and begins the play's most important speech. The broadcast cuts to a backstage shot of a cardboard moon.

KORI: I came here to find-

[The moon fills the screen.]

ACT ONE

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - CONTINUOUS

Feli says the camera labels were swapped. Pavi asks her to switch to the stage camera. The feed shows a close-up of Dena's clipboard. She covers the shot with her hand. Ranu narrates the scene while the audience watches the empty stage monitor.

DENA: The actors are on stage.

Pavi signals the booth. The picture cuts to the curtain, then to the orchestra pit. Kori continues the speech, unheard on the broadcast. The live audience applauds at the end. Ranu says the speech was moving, though none of the viewers heard it.

ACT TWO

INT. BACKSTAGE - LATER

Dena checks the camera board. Stage left is labeled STAGE, but its feed points to backstage. The backstage camera is labeled HOUSE. Feli traces the cables to a switcher. A volunteer has plugged the feeds into the wrong inputs. Pavi asks the crew to correct them before the next scene.

[The volunteer swaps the cables. The monitor now shows the stage, but mirrored. Kori enters from the wrong side of the screen and appears to walk backward.]

Ranu tries to describe the blocking. Dena asks him to stop. Pavi asks the actor to rehearse the entrance once. The camera follows Kori to backstage again. Feli manually selects the stage camera. The picture holds, but the audio comes from the backstage mic. It captures someone whispering the next cue.

INT. AUDITORIUM - MOMENTS LATER

Dena moves the mic to the stage feed. Feli checks the picture and sound with Kori's line. Kori speaks from the stage. The monitor shows the actor and the audio meters move. The two match. Pavi asks the class to continue the play from the next cue, not repeat the missed speech.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Feli tests the stage feed with Dena's cue: one clap, then a spoken line. The monitor shows Dena on stage and the audio meter peaks once for the clap. Pavi asks Kori to hold position until the camera is ready. Kori waits in the wing, not in the scene. Ranu removes the narration track from the playback queue. The camera operator practices staying on the stage as an actor crosses from left to right. The picture and sound remain matched.

Dena calls the cast for the next cue. Pavi tells the booth to follow the actors, not the backstage movement. A stagehand rolls the cardboard moon into its mark. The stage feed catches the student who steadies it, then returns to Kori. No one inserts a cutaway. Feli labels the working camera STAGE - LIVE. Pavi watches Kori finish the scene from the audience monitor, not the backstage feed. The picture stays live to the bow. Ranu lets the audience applause come through without narration. Dena thanks the class from the stage, and the broadcast ends on the real curtain call. The play continues without another rehearsal of the missed speech.

ACT THREE

INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM - NIGHT

Kori reaches the important speech again in the final scene. The camera stays on stage. The picture is correct, but Ranu begins narrating over the line. Pavi turns off the narration mic. The viewers hear Kori's speech, the actor's pause, and the audience's response.

Feli does not cut away when Dena signals from backstage. The cardboard moon tips slightly. A student catches it without leaving the scene. The audience laughs; Kori continues. The play reaches its ending. The camera stays wide enough to show all the students bowing. No one switches to the empty stage or the clipboard.

Pavi thanks the cast and the crew. Ranu asks whether they should replay the missed speech. Dena says the broadcast recorded it correctly this time. The students leave the stage together. Feli keeps the stage feed on until the final actor exits, then switches to a shot of the empty curtain.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE PLAY

Dena relabels both camera feeds. Feli tests the stage mic once. Ranu removes the narration cue from the play's final scene.

FINAL IMAGE: The last frame shows the cast bowing on the real stage, with the cardboard moon upright behind them.
