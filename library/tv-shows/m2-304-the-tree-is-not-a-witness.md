# The Tree Is Not a Witness

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom procedural television, dramatic witness reveals, and lawyers who ask questions the evidence already answered*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A neighborhood court hears a dispute over a branch that fell across a shared path, while every witness insists the tree should explain itself.

Cast: Judge Omi, an orangutan; advocate Nera, a macaque; advocate Vex, a capuchin; witness Luma, a gibbon gardener; witness Rell, a ring-tailed lemur cyclist; and clerk Palla, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The Branch on the Path"

### Cold Open

[COURTROOM. A LARGE BRANCH SITS BESIDE THE BENCH.]

VEX: The tree blocked the path.

NERA: The wind moved the branch.

OMI: We are not putting the tree on trial.

PALLA: The tree has not entered a plea.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[COURTROOM. THE JUDGE TAPS A WOODEN BLOCK.]

Judge Omi hears the complaint. Rell says he found the branch across the bike path and had to walk around it. Luma says she had warned the park crew that the limb was cracked. Vex argues that the path should have been closed sooner.

VEX: The evidence is the branch.

NERA: The branch is evidence of a branch.

Omi asks Palla to place it on the evidence table. The branch is too long, so one end sticks into the jury row. Everyone shifts their chairs. Palla rotates it; it knocks over the water pitcher.

OMI: The evidence has affected the court.

Rell says the branch appeared after a wind gust. Vex asks if he actually saw the branch fall. Rell says no, but he heard a loud crack. Luma says she was pruning another tree at that moment and heard the same crack.

VEX: You were near the scene.

LUMA: I was in the park.

NERA: The park is not a single room.

### Act Two

[WITNESS STAND.]

Vex calls the tree as a witness. Judge Omi refuses. Vex asks Luma to describe the branch's condition. She says the limb had a split and needed to be removed, but the crew's work order was scheduled for the next morning.

LUMA: The branch was weak. The wind finished the break.

VEX: So the park crew knew it might fall.

LUMA: We knew we needed to inspect it. I did not know when it would fall.

Nera presents a photo of a warning cone beside the path. Rell says the cone was moved out of the way by someone on a scooter. Vex suggests the scooter rider was the defendant. Rell says it was a delivery cart and the rider wore a helmet with feathers.

OMI: Is a feathered helmet identifying evidence?

PALLA: There are several in town.

Vex asks to call a surprise witness. The door opens. A young macaque enters holding a branch saw.

YOUNG MACAQUE: I was the one who moved the cone. I needed to get my cart through.

The courtroom gasps. The young macaque says the cone was on the path because the crew had marked a cracked limb. He moved it but left a note on the cone. Palla finds the note inside the branch.

PALLA: It says "Path closed until limb check."

VEX: The note was inside the branch?

PALLA: No. It was underneath the cone.

### Act Three

[COURTROOM. EVIDENCE IS REARRANGED.]

Judge Omi asks everyone to describe what they personally saw. Rell saw the fallen branch. Luma saw the cracked limb. The young macaque moved the cone. No one saw the tree fall. The court concludes the path closure was clearly marked, but the sign was moved before the incident.

OMI: This is not a case against the tree or the gardener. The path should be cleared, and the warning cone should be replaced.

VEX: Who moved it?

YOUNG MACAQUE: I did. I will help put it back.

The court orders a new reflective sign, a maintenance check for the trees along the path, and a public reminder not to move hazard markers. Luma asks the park crew to prune the remaining split branches before opening the path. Rell says he can help direct cyclists around the closure.

Nera asks whether the court should hold the branch. Omi says it can go to the park woodpile.

PALLA: The evidence will be released to firewood.

The town crew begins clearing the path. Rell points out the reflective strip on the replacement sign, while Luma shows the young macaque how to set the cone beyond the branch rather than in the riding lane. Vex attempts to interview the tree from the sidewalk; Judge Omi closes the courtroom door before he can call it as a witness again.

### Tag

[COURTHOUSE COURTYARD.]

The town's tree committee meets under a healthy oak. Vex asks whether it can be called as a witness in the next case. Omi taps the wooden block.

OMI: The tree is not on the witness list.

A leaf falls onto the judge's robe. Palla picks it up and puts it in the evidence box.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
