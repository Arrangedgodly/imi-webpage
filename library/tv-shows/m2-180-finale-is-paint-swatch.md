# The Finale Is a Paint Swatch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home makeover finales that delay the reveal and prioritize reaction shots over the finished room*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Designer Sumi has renovated a neighborhood reading room, but host Taro keeps delaying the reveal for dramatic reaction shots and sponsor mentions. Librarian Gena is waiting to reopen the room before the evening book club arrives.

Cast: Taro, a macaque host; Sumi, a gorilla designer; Gena, a langur librarian; Dali, a capuchin producer; and Osi, a red colobus camera operator.

## Episode: "Open the Door Already"

### Cold Open

[READING ROOM ENTRY. A CURTAIN COVERS THE DOOR. GENA HOLDS A STACK OF BOOKS. TARO FACES THE CAMERA.] 

TARO: Tonight, one room will change forever.

GENA: The book club starts at seven.

DALI: We need the reveal first.

[SUMI PULLS THE CURTAIN. IT CATCHES ON A DOOR HANDLE AND REVEALS ONLY A PAINT SWATCH.] 

TARO: The transformation has begun.

GENA: That is one wall.

### Act One

[THE CURTAIN IS RESET. DALI ASKS GENA TO CLOSE HER EYES. SHE DOES, WHILE HOLDING THE BOOKS.] 

TARO: Imagine the room you have always wanted.

GENA: I want a clear path to the shelves.

[SUMI SHOWS THE FLOOR PLAN. THE NEW SHELVES LEAVE A WIDE AISLE AND A LOW TABLE FOR CHILDREN.] 

DALI: Can we add a surprise chair?

SUMI: The room is full.

[THE CAMERA CREW SETS A REACTION MARK ON THE FLOOR. IT SITS IN THE MIDDLE OF THE AISLE. GENA MOVES IT TO THE WALL.] 

GENA: The path needs to stay open.

TARO: It is a reveal mark.

### Act Two

[THE CREW TRIES TO FILM THE DOOR OPENING IN SLOW MOTION. THE DOOR HITS A STACK OF BOXES LEFT BY THE SET TEAM.] 

SUMI: The boxes are supposed to be in storage.

DALI: We need a dramatic entrance.

GENA: We need the shelves clear.

[THEY MOVE THE BOXES. ONE CONTAINS SPARE CURTAIN FABRIC. DALI PULLS IT OUT TO BUILD A SECOND REVEAL. IT WRAPS AROUND THE CAMERA OPERATOR.] 

TARO: The room is hiding its secrets.

OSI: I am wrapped in the curtain.

[DALI PULLS THE FABRIC FREE. AS IT COMES AWAY, THE LOW TABLE APPEARS BEHIND THE HANGING DRAPE. SUMI FOLDS THE FABRIC INTO THE STORAGE BIN.]

SUMI: That was the missing piece.

TARO: The reveal was under the reveal.

GENA: The table was in the room plan.

[GENA SETS A PICTURE BOOK ON IT. DALI MOVES A CAMERA LIGHT SO IT DOES NOT SHINE INTO THE CHILDREN'S SEATS.]

[GENA CUTS THE POWER TO THE AUTO-REVEAL TRACK. SUMI OPENS THE DOOR BY HAND. THE FINISHED ROOM IS BRIGHT, WITH A READING TABLE AND SHELVES.] 

GENA: The book club can come in.

DALI: We still need the sponsor shot.

[THE SPONSOR PAINT SWATCHES ARE ON THE TABLE. GENA SLIDES THEM INTO A DRAWER TO MAKE SPACE FOR THE BOOKS.] 

### Act Three

[THE CREW FILMS THE REVEAL AGAIN. TARO ASKS GENA TO TURN AROUND SLOWLY. SHE WALKS TO THE SHELVES AND PUTS THE BOOKS AWAY.]

[DALI SHOWS A SET OF REACTION CARDS: "TEARS," "GASP," AND "HUG." GENA SETS THEM FACE DOWN AND PUTS THE BOOKS IN ALPHABETICAL ORDER.] 

TARO: We need one big feeling.

GENA: I feel relieved the shelves are ready.

SUMI: The bench is level, too.

[SUMI POINTS OUT THE SMALL BENCH BUILT UNDER THE WINDOW. GENA SITS AND OPENS A BOOK. THE CREW TRIES A CLOSE-UP. THE LAMP REFLECTS ON THE WINDOW AND BLOWS OUT THE CAMERA EXPOSURE. GENA TURNS IT A FEW DEGREES. THE PAGE IS LEGIBLE AGAIN.]

DALI: That was an actual reveal.

TARO: That was not the reaction shot.

GENA: The books are home.

[DALI POINTS TO THE REACTION MARK. GENA TURNS A PAGE WITHOUT LOOKING UP.]

SUMI: Does the light work?

GENA: Yes.

[DALI RETRIEVES A PAINT SWATCH FROM THE DRAWER AND PLACES IT ON THE BENCH FOR THE CLOSE-UP. A CHILD ARRIVES EARLY AND SITS ON IT. THE SWATCH STICKS TO THE CHILD'S COAT.]

TARO: We have our first guest.

GENA: The club is starting early.

[THE CREW MOVES CAMERAS OUT OF THE AISLE. THE CHILD OPENS A BOOK. THE ROOM IS READY.] 

[DALI STARTS A FINAL SPONSOR READ. THE FIRST ADULT CLUB MEMBER ARRIVES AND ASKS WHERE TO PUT A COAT. GENA OPENS THE CLOSET THAT SUMI BUILT BESIDE THE ENTRY.]

GENA: Right here. It is out of the reading path.

TARO: The room is already in use.

[DALI STOPS THE SPONSOR READ. THE CLUB MEMBERS CARRY BOOKS TO THE LOW TABLE WHILE THE CREW LIFTS ITS LIGHT STANDS CLEAR OF THE DOOR.] 

### Tag

[TARO LIFTS THE CURTAIN FOR A FINAL REVEAL. IT FALLS OVER THE DOOR AND COVERS HIS HEAD.] 

GENA: The room is open.

[SUMI PULLS TARO FREE. THE LAST BOOK CLUB MEMBERS WALK PAST THE CURTAIN INTO THE ROOM.]
