# Presto, Primate!

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised magic competitions, secret mechanisms, and judges' impossible reveal demands*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A young magician performs a disappearing-fruit trick on a live competition show, while the judges keep asking for a reveal that would spoil the trick.

Cast: Host Ovi, a baboon; contestant Simo, a capuchin; judge Leda, a lemur; judge Rook, a gorilla; and stagehand Mina, a macaque.

## Episode: "The Fig That Vanished"

### Cold open

INT. MAGIC COMPETITION STAGE, NIGHT

[Simo places a fig beneath a brass cup. Ovi introduces the act.]

OVI: The audience will see a miracle.

LEDA: I want to see how it works.

ROOK: I want to see both sides.

SIMO: Then there will be no miracle.

[He lifts the cup. The fig is gone.]

OVI: Incredible.

MINA: The fig is in your sleeve.

OVI: That is not part of the act.

LEDA: It is now part of the reveal.

### Act One

INT. BACKSTAGE

[Simo prepares three cups. Mina stands beside the curtain.]

SIMO: The trick needs misdirection.

MINA: The host is the misdirection.

OVI: I heard that.

SIMO: You are meant to talk while I move the fig.

OVI: I can talk.

LEDA: I will watch your hands.

ROOK: I will watch the cups.

SIMO: The audience should watch the cup.

MINA: Which cup?

SIMO: The brass one.

OVI: We have three brass cups.

SIMO: They are all the same.

LEDA: That is suspicious.

[The stage manager signals them on.]

### Act Two

INT. MAGIC COMPETITION STAGE

[Simo places a fig under the center cup. The judges lean forward.]

SIMO: Keep your eyes on the cup.

LEDA: I am watching the cup.

ROOK: I am watching the fig.

OVI: I am watching the audience.

MINA: I am watching the curtain.

[Simo gestures. A fig appears on Rook's chair.]

ROOK: It appeared beside me.

LEDA: You were watching the fig.

ROOK: I was watching the cup too.

SIMO: That is the trick.

LEDA: How did it get there?

SIMO: If I tell you, it is no longer a trick.

ROOK: The audience wants to know.

OVI: The audience wants to be surprised.

LEDA: I am the judge. I want a score.

OVI: Score the surprise.

LEDA: I cannot see the mechanism.

SIMO: You are not supposed to.

[Ovi takes one cup and lifts it. Nothing is underneath.]

OVI: This cup is empty.

MINA: That is the one you just lifted.

OVI: I have revealed it.

LEDA: You revealed an empty cup.

ROOK: The fig is still on my chair.

### Mechanism check

INT. STAGE, MOMENTS LATER

[Leda asks to inspect the brass cups. Simo lines them up.]

LEDA: I want to see the underside.

SIMO: Then you will see the underside.

[She lifts a cup. The fig is taped to it.]

ROOK: We have found the mechanism.

SIMO: That is a backup fig.

MINA: The real fig is still missing.

OVI: The trick continues.

Leda lifts the second cup. A note reads "LOOK AT ROOK."

ROOK: Why am I in the trick?

SIMO: You watched the fig.

[The fig appears in the host's sleeve again.]

OVI: It has returned to me.

MINA: You put your arm through the cup.

OVI: I was part of the act.

LEDA: The act has three figs now.

SIMO: The show supplied them.

### Act Three

INT. STAGE

[The judges confer. Simo waits beside the cups.]

LEDA: We award points for skill, timing, and presentation.

ROOK: I award points for the fig arriving on my chair.

OVI: The winner is Simo.

LEDA: I still do not know how he did it.

SIMO: That means the trick worked.

ROOK: Can you make the fig appear in the trophy?

SIMO: No.

OVI: Can you make the trophy disappear?

SIMO: No.

MINA: The trophy is bolted to the stage.

ROOK: That is a good safety measure.

[Ovi carries the trophy toward Simo. The fig slides off Rook's chair and lands on the trophy.]

OVI: The fig has won.

LEDA: Does it receive a prize?

SIMO: It receives a very good review.

MINA: The fig is bruised.

ROOK: It has been through a lot.

LEDA: The judges request one final reveal.

SIMO: I can show you the cup.

[He lifts the cup. The fig is under it again.]

OVI: It has returned.

SIMO: It never left the stage.

### Second performance

INT. STAGE

[The judges return to their seats. Simo places an empty cup on the table.]

SIMO: I will make the clue disappear.

LEDA: What clue?

SIMO: The one on the cup.

[She looks down. The note has vanished.]

ROOK: It is on the table.

MINA: The cup is empty.

OVI: The judges have been distracted.

LEDA: We were looking at the note.

SIMO: That was the trick.

ROOK: I want points for finding it.

### Tag

INT. BACKSTAGE

[Mina picks up the fig. It is still sitting on the trophy.]

MINA: The trick is over.

[The stage light goes out; the fig glows faintly from a sticker underneath.]
