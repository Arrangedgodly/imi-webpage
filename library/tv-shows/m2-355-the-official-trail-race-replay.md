# The Official Trail Race Replay

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports documentaries that turn a modest neighborhood race into a legacy-defining event*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A camera crew reviews a three-runner trail race whose only controversy is a disputed turn around a stump.

Cast: Narrator Vale, a gibbon; runner Sola, a macaque; runner Kett, a capuchin; runner Doro, a baboon; race steward Miri, a lemur.

Episode: "One More Lap"

COLD OPEN

EXT. PARK TRAIL - MORNING

A small bell rings. The runners jog around a tree stump. Vale narrates over slow-motion footage of a leaf falling.

VALE: At exactly nine twelve, one leaf changed the course of the race.

SOLA: It fell on my shoulder.

KETT: It fell beside you.

DORO: I stepped over it.

[MIRI HOLDS UP A STOPWATCH.]

MIRI: The race lasted four minutes.

ACT ONE

The crew interviews each runner beside the stump. Sola says she took the inside path. Kett says the inside path was blocked by the stump. Doro insists all three took the same route, though he stopped to tie a shoelace.

VALE: There is no official footage of the disputed turn.

MIRI: There is footage. The camera operator was behind the water barrel.

[THE VIDEO PLAYS. IT SHOWS THE BARREL, THEN A RUNNER'S SHADOW.]

VALE: A silhouette at the crucial moment.

MIRI: It was me carrying the camera.

The runners line up for a replay. Vale asks them to repeat the race at a pace suitable for the soundtrack. They jog slowly around the stump while dramatic strings swell.

SOLA: This is not the pace I ran.

KETT: The music is faster than us.

ACT TWO

Miri places chalk arrows on the path. The crew films a close shot of each runner's shoes crossing the line. Doro has two different shoes. He says one is for traction and one was the clean pair by the door.

VALE: Equipment choices reveal character.

DORO: The left shoe was dry.

Sola demonstrates the inside turn. She bumps the stump with her knee and stops to check it. Kett takes the outer loop. Vale calls it a tactical retreat.

KETT: I went around the obstacle.

MIRI: That is what the route says.

The crew slows the footage. A leaf drifts over the finish line after the racers have passed. Vale says the leaf may have crossed first. Miri points to the time stamp: the camera was recording the prior afternoon.

VALE: It is archival footage.

MIRI: It is yesterday's leaf.

ACT TWO-B

The runners gather around a scoreboard. Vale wants a final verdict. Miri checks the race sheet: Sola first, Kett second, Doro third. Doro says he was held up by his shoe. Kett argues the stump made the inside path shorter.

MIRI: The course was marked before the race.

SOLA: I followed the marks.

KETT: I followed the ground.

DORO: I followed my shoe.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS ON DORO'S UNTIED LACE. HE TIES IT.]

VALE: The final obstacle returns.

DORO: The final obstacle is a lace.

ACT TWO-C

Miri measures the path with a cord. The inside and outside routes are the same length because the stump sits beside a shallow loop. Kett says the loop looked shorter. Sola admits she thought the race had one lap, while the sign said two. She completed two; the others stopped after one.

MIRI: We have a real scoring error.

VALE: At last, a reversal.

KETT: I stopped because the bell rang.

MIRI: The bell was a warm-up signal.

DORO: I stopped to tie my shoe.

SOLA: I kept running because the sign said two laps.

Miri checks the route sheet and confirms the sign had been turned around. Sola wins the official two-lap race. Kett and Doro agree to run the missing lap together for the camera.

ACT TWO-D

Miri lays the runners' times beside the course map. Kett asks why his second lap was not counted. Miri says the start bell was a warm-up cue, not the official start.

KETT: It rang like a start bell.

DORO: It rang twice. That usually means the race begins.

MIRI: It means the bell was tested twice.

[THE CREW REPLAYS THE FOOTAGE. THE CAMERA POINTS AWAY AS THE BELL SOUNDS.]

VALE: The decisive moment is just outside the frame.

SOLA: I heard it and started anyway.

MIRI: You ran both laps. The corrected sheet gives you the win.

KETT: I will run the missing lap with Doro.

DORO: After I tie this shoe.

[HE SITS DOWN. THE CAMERA ZOOMS ON THE LACE.]
ACT THREE

At the finish, Miri presents the corrected race sheet. Sola receives the official ribbon for completing both laps. Kett and Doro shake her hand, then ask Vale to stop replaying the one-lap warm-up as if it were the final race.

VALE: The result is settled.

MIRI: The sign now faces the right way.

SOLA: And the leaf is still just a leaf.

TAG

The leaf lands on the scoreboard and covers Sola's name.

VALE: The course remains unpredictable.

MIRI: It is still a leaf.

[MUSIC: SPORTS DOCUMENTARY THEME.]
