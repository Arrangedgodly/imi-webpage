# The Full Nest Hotel

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: luxury hotel-review programs, impossible guest standards, and reveal tours*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A hotel inspector arrives at the Full Nest Hotel during a storm, when the only available room is a carefully staged display nest that the staff insist is a premium suite.

Cast: Inspector Vela, a macaque; manager Doru, an orangutan; porter Kipp, a capuchin; guest Sila, a gibbon; and host Nemi, a lemur.

## Episode: "Room with a View"

### Cold open

INT. FULL NEST HOTEL LOBBY, EVENING

[Nemi introduces the hotel to camera. Rain taps the roof. A sign reads "NO VACANCY."]

NEMI: Tonight we review the finest nest in the valley.

VELA: Is there a room available?

DORU: We have one.

KIPP: It is the display nest.

DORU: It has a view.

SILA: It is in the lobby.

NEMI: A social suite.

VELA: Is it private?

DORU: It has a curtain.

[Kipp pulls the curtain. It covers only one side.]

### Act One

INT. DISPLAY NEST

[Vela inspects the nest while Nemi narrates. Sila carries a small bag.]

VELA: How many guests fit?

DORU: One, comfortably.

KIPP: Two, if they are very friendly.

SILA: I have a reservation for one.

DORU: The booking system placed you here.

SILA: I booked the upper branch.

KIPP: The upper branch has a leak.

DORU: The display nest is the premium alternative.

NEMI: It offers easy access to the lobby.

VELA: I can hear everyone.

DORU: The hotel has a lively atmosphere.

SILA: I can see the front desk.

DORU: Our guests enjoy attentive service.

[Vela tests the mattress. It rebounds and launches a pillow into the lobby.]

NEMI: The bed has a springy feel.

VELA: The bed has a spring.

KIPP: The nest was built over a bent branch.

SILA: Is breakfast included?

DORU: It is included in the lobby.

NEMI: A convenient communal meal.

VELA: It is a bowl of figs on the desk.

### Act Two

INT. LOBBY, LATER

[The storm grows louder. Doru uses a bowl to catch a drip.]

DORU: The roof leak is contained.

VELA: The bowl is in the lobby.

DORU: It is a water feature.

NEMI: Guests can enjoy the sound of falling rain indoors.

SILA: It is dripping on my bag.

KIPP: We can move your bag.

SILA: There is nowhere dry.

DORU: We can move the display nest.

[They try. The nest is attached to a signpost.]

KIPP: The nest is part of the display.

DORU: The display can travel.

[The signpost tips and lands across the lobby. The nest hangs safely from it.]

NEMI: The suite now has a canopy.

VELA: It has a signpost through the center.

SILA: The sign says "WELCOME."

DORU: We have made your welcome more direct.

VELA: I would like to see the upper branch room.

KIPP: The stairs are wet.

SILA: We can climb the outside.

DORU: The outside is also wet.

NEMI: We can review the staircase from here.

[Vela steps on the first stair. It squeaks. Everyone listens.]

VELA: Is that the floor?

KIPP: It is the stair.

DORU: The hotel has an audible route.

SILA: It tells you where the guest is.

### Act Three

INT. LOBBY, NIGHT

[Vela looks at the room. Doru sets a dry blanket in the display nest.]

VELA: The room is small, the curtain is partial, and breakfast is a bowl on the desk.

DORU: Correct.

VELA: The staff handled the storm by moving the nest.

KIPP: The nest moved the signpost.

SILA: I have stayed in worse rooms.

NEMI: What is your rating?

VELA: Four stars for the staff, two for the roof, and one for the spring bed.

DORU: That is seven stars.

VELA: It is a five-star scale.

NEMI: We have reached a new maximum.

SILA: I will stay in the display nest.

DORU: It is already occupied.

SILA: I am the guest.

DORU: The signpost is the guest.

[The signpost leans. The nest tilts toward the lobby.]

KIPP: I can put a wedge beneath it.

VELA: Use the welcome sign.

NEMI: The hotel has repurposed its own welcome.

[Doru props the signpost. The nest settles.]

### Room service

INT. LOBBY

[Nemi carries a tray with one fig and a cup of water.]

VELA: Is this room service?

DORU: Yes.

VELA: I ordered tea.

KIPP: We are out of tea.

VELA: Can you bring me some?

KIPP: The kitchen is upstairs.

DORU: We can deliver it by hand.

[Nemi passes the tray to Kipp, who passes it to Doru, who passes it back to Nemi.]

SILA: The tea is on a journey.

NEMI: It is arriving at the suite.

VELA: The suite is six feet away.

### Tag

INT. LOBBY, DAWN

[The storm has stopped. The display nest is level. Sila sleeps in it while the signpost reads "WELCOME BACK."]

NEMI: Checkout is at noon.

VELA: I checked out.

DORU: The nest has not.

[The signpost remains beside the sleeping guest while rain drips into the bucket.]
