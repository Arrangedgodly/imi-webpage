# The Safety Demonstration Is Not an Action Scene

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: airline safety videos that add cinematic danger, overcomplicate simple instructions, and distract passengers from the actual demonstration*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Director Pera wants a dramatic in-flight safety segment. Cabin lead Doro needs to show passengers where the exits and seatbelts are, while producer Puck keeps adding smoke effects and action music to a routine demonstration.

Cast: Pera, a macaque director; Doro, a gorilla cabin lead; Lemi, a lemur passenger volunteer; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "The Aisle Is Clear"

### Cold Open

[MOCK AIRPLANE CABIN. DORO STANDS IN THE AISLE HOLDING A SEATBELT.]

DORO: To fasten, insert the metal tab into the buckle.

[PUCK STARTS ACTION MUSIC. A RED LIGHT FLASHES.]

DORO: There is no emergency.

PERA: The demonstration needs stakes.

[LEMI TRIES THE BELT. THE MUSIC CRESCENDOS AS IT CLICKS.]

PERA: Can we have another take where the strap is harder to fasten?

DORO: The belt should work normally.

PUCK: We can give it a dramatic snag.

[PUCK ADDS A PIECE OF VELCRO TO THE BELT. DORO REMOVES IT BEFORE THE NEXT TAKE.]

DORO: Demonstrate the buckle as it is.

[LEMI FASTENS IT AND RELEASES IT ONCE. THE CAMERA GETS A CLEAR VIEW.]

LEMI: It is fastened.

[PUCK ADDS A THUNDER SOUND.]

DORO: That was the buckle.

### Act One

[THE CREW RESETs. DORO POINTS TO THE EXIT MARKERS.]

DORO: The nearest exits are marked by green signs.

[PERA ASKS PUCK TO DIM THE LIGHTS. THE EXIT SIGNS GLOW.]

LEMI: I can see them.

[PUCK ADDS A FOG MACHINE. THE SIGNS DISAPPEAR IN HAZE.]

DORO: Turn that off.

PUCK: It represents uncertainty.

DORO: It blocks the exit signs.

[PUCK TURNS OFF THE FOG. DORO SHOWS THE AISLE AND THE TWO NEAREST EXITS.]

PERA: Can you run to one?

DORO: Passengers should walk, not run.

[LEMI WALKS TO THE EXIT. PUCK PLAYS A SLOW-MOTION SOUND EFFECT.]

LEMI: I am walking.

### Act Two

[DORO DEMONSTRATES THE OXYGEN MASK WITH A MODEL. PUCK ADDS A DRAMATIC COUNTDOWN GRAPHIC.]

DORO: If masks deploy, pull one toward you and place it over your nose and mouth. Secure your own mask before helping someone else.

[PERA ASKS LEMI TO HELP DORO FIRST. DORO HOLDS UP A HAND.]

DORO: I will demonstrate my mask first.

PUCK: The rescue would be more moving if she helped you.

DORO: The instruction is clear.

[LEMI FITS HER MODEL MASK. THE STRAP COVERS ONE EYE.]

LEMI: I cannot see the demonstration now.

[DORO ADJUSTS THE STRAP. PUCK MOVES THE CAMERA TO SHOW THE FIT.]

PERA: Can we have a close-up of the oxygen flow?

DORO: It is a model. There is no oxygen in it.

[PUCK PLUGS IN A TUBE. IT MAKES A WHISTLING SOUND.]

LEMI: That sounds like the tea kettle.

[DORO REMOVES THE TUBE AND PUTS THE MODEL MASK BACK ON ITS STAND.]

[PUCK PARKS A PROP SUITCASE IN THE AISLE TO ADD DEPTH TO THE FRAME. DORO MOVES IT INTO THE OVERHEAD BIN.] 

DORO: Keep the aisle clear.

PERA: That is a useful detail.

LEMI: The suitcase fits up there.

[PUCK CLOSES THE BIN. IT DOES NOT LATCH. DORO REPOSITIONS THE BAG AND CHECKS THE BIN AGAIN.]

DORO: It must be secure before we continue.

### Act Three

[THE CREW FILMS A CLEAN TAKE. DORO SHOWS THE BELT, EXIT SIGNS, AND MODEL MASK. THERE IS NO MUSIC.]

DORO: Follow crew instructions, keep the aisle clear, and read the safety card in your seat pocket.

[LEMI OPENS THE SEAT POCKET. IT IS FULL OF UNUSED PROP CARDS.]

LEMI: Which one is the real card?

[DORO TAKES OUT THE OFFICIAL CARD AND HOLDS IT OPEN.]

DORO: This one. The demonstration explains the main steps; the card has details for this aircraft.

[PUCK REMOVES THE PROP CARDS. PERA APPROVES THE TAKE.]

PERA: Should the passengers practice the brace position?

DORO: We can demonstrate it on the safety card, using the actual instructions for this aircraft.

[LEMI HOLDS UP THE CARD. THE CORRECT PICTURES FACE THE CAMERA.]

PUCK: No music cue?

DORO: The words are enough.

### Tag

[THE MOCK CABIN IS RESET. PUCK HOLDS A SMALL FOG MACHINE.]

PUCK: Can we use this in the next segment?

DORO: Only if we are filming fog.

[PUCK PUTS IT AWAY. THE AISLE REMAINS CLEAR.]

LEMI: The demonstration is ready for the next flight.

DORO: It is ready for the crew to review against the aircraft card.

[PERA SAVES THE CLEAN TAKE. PUCK REMOVES THE MUSIC TRACK FROM THE EDIT.]

PERA: We will show it without action music.

DORO: The instructions should be easy to hear.

[LEMI CHECKS THE EDIT ON A SMALL MONITOR. THE WORDS ARE CLEAR AND THE EXIT SIGNS ARE VISIBLE.]
