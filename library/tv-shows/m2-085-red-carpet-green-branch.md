# Red Carpet, Green Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live awards broadcasts, red-carpet interviews, and suspenseful envelope reveals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: The Canopy Screen Awards are underway, but the nominees keep leaving the carpet to help the stage crew recover a drifting branch that supports the camera platform.

Cast: Host Miri, a lemur; presenter Daro, a mandrill; nominee Vola, a gibbon; nominee Kett, a macaque; and stagehand Piri, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Branch Award"

### Cold open

EXT. AWARDS CARPET, NIGHT

[A red carpet runs beneath a camera platform. A branch supporting one corner shifts in the wind.]

MIRI: Welcome to the Canopy Screen Awards.

DARO: The nominees are arriving.

VOLA: The carpet is moving.

KETT: The camera platform is moving.

PIRI: The branch is loose.

MIRI: We have a live event.

DARO: We have a live branch.

[The branch rolls. The camera tilts toward the carpet.]

MIRI: Please remain in your seats.

VOLA: We are standing.

### Act One

EXT. CARPET

[The nominees pose for photographs. Piri kneels to tie the branch.]

MIRI: Vola, who are you wearing tonight?

VOLA: A woven jacket I made from fallen leaves.

MIRI: What inspired it?

VOLA: The branch that is moving toward me.

[The branch rolls past. Vola catches it.]

DARO: A dramatic entrance.

KETT: It is part of the platform.

PIRI: I need a rope.

DARO: We have a red carpet.

PIRI: It is not a rope.

MIRI: The carpet can be tied around the branch.

[They roll the carpet into a bundle. It unrolls as the branch rolls back.]

KETT: The carpet has returned.

VOLA: It wants a close-up.

MIRI: We will continue inside.

### Act Two

INT. AWARDS HALL

[The audience sits. The stage has a new support made from a stack of award boxes.]

DARO: The nominees for Best Short Scene are Vola and Kett.

MIRI: We will reveal the winner after the commercial.

PIRI: We are not taking a commercial break.

DARO: We can have a dramatic pause.

[The envelope sticks to the presenter card.]

KETT: The card is damp.

VOLA: The branch is wet.

PIRI: The branch is outside.

KETT: It came in with the carpet.

DARO: The envelope is sealed.

MIRI: Can we open it?

DARO: Not until the music swells.

[The orchestra begins too early. The envelope tears, revealing no card.]

MIRI: The envelope is empty.

DARO: That is suspense.

KETT: The winner is not in it.

PIRI: The card is under the podium.

[They lift the podium. The card is stuck to its base.]

DARO: The winner is Vola.

VOLA: I was holding the branch.

MIRI: You can accept while holding it.

### Red carpet replay

EXT. CARPET

[Miri interviews the nominees beneath a banner. Piri's rope is still tied to the branch.]

MIRI: The carpet is back in place. Vola, what did you think when the camera platform tilted?

VOLA: I thought the camera would fall.

DARO: That is a very honest answer.

KETT: I thought the camera was getting a better shot.

PIRI: I was trying to hold the branch.

MIRI: You were holding the production together.

PIRI: I was holding the branch.

[A small camera drone passes overhead.]

DARO: Our aerial camera is ready.

VOLA: The wind is coming from the east.

PIRI: The branch is loose again.

MIRI: The carpet is moving.

[They pin the carpet with award boxes. The drone lands on one box.]

KETT: The carpet is now a landing pad.

DARO: That is a premiere.

MIRI: The awards will begin once the platform is stable.

PIRI: It is stable.

[The platform shifts a fraction.]

DARO: The platform has one more note.

### Envelope check

INT. AWARDS HALL

[Daro tests the envelope. The card is still stuck to the podium.]

DARO: The winner's name is on the card.

MIRI: Can we read it?

DARO: The lighting is dramatic.

PIRI: I have freed the card.

VOLA: Who won?

PIRI: The card is blank.

KETT: We can vote on the nominees.

MIRI: The audience is already voting.

[The camera platform tilts again. The branch makes a soft creak.]

### Act Three

INT. AWARDS HALL, LATER

[Vola stands at the microphone with the branch tied to the carpet.]

VOLA: I want to thank the stage crew.

PIRI: The branch is stable now.

KETT: The camera platform is level.

DARO: The awards can continue.

MIRI: We have a special award for outstanding support.

[She presents a small trophy to the branch.]

KETT: Is the branch the winner?

MIRI: It held up the camera platform.

PIRI: It almost dropped it.

DARO: It gave us a memorable ceremony.

VOLA: We can plant it in the grove.

KETT: It is already a branch.

MIRI: Then it has a future.

[The audience applauds. The trophy is tied to the branch.]

### Tag

EXT. AWARDS CARPET, NIGHT

[The branch rests beside the carpet. A camera zooms in on it.]

MIRI: The branch is our final nominee.

[The camera platform tilts again.]
