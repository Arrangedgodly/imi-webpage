# The Train Travel Show Reports from Yesterday's Platform

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel broadcasts that follow a prerecorded schedule after the live train has departed*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Rali, a lemur; reporter Odo, a gibbon; station agent Mena, an orangutan; producer Kiko, a macaque; camera assistant Davi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. RAIL PLATFORM - MORNING

Rali stands beside an empty track. A graphic reads TRAIN ARRIVING NOW.

RALI: Here comes the express.

MENA: It left twenty minutes ago.

ACT ONE

EXT. PLATFORM - CONTINUOUS

Kiko says the station feed is on a delay. Odo reports from the far platform; he sees the next train boarding. Davi points the camera at a clock that is five minutes slow. Rali announces the delayed train as current. Mena holds up the station's live board, which shows the next departure at platform three.

Kiko asks her to move the sign out of frame. Mena refuses. A prerecorded chime sounds from Rali's monitor, followed by a boarding announcement for the train that has already departed. The few waiting passengers look at the empty track.

ACT TWO

INT. STATION CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Odo traces the video delay to a replay buffer. Kiko wants a live cut, but the screen begins replaying Rali's opening from the previous hour. Rali watches herself predict the train's arrival. Davi sets a handheld camera on the platform and calls out the actual time. The feed reaches the booth without delay.

Mena points to platform three again. The crew follows her sign and finds the arriving local train. Rali asks the driver for a quote. Kiko cues a dramatic arrival sting. It plays after the train has stopped and the doors have opened. Odo starts his report while passengers board around him.

INT. PLATFORM THREE - MOMENTS LATER

Davi keeps the camera behind the yellow line. Kiko asks Rali to tell viewers the express is still coming. She says it has departed and gives the next live departure. Mena checks the board, then the platform sign; both show platform three. Odo interviews a passenger who says they nearly went to the empty track after seeing the stale graphic.

Rali asks the passenger to point to the correct platform. They point to the sign immediately behind her. Kiko lowers the arrow graphic, which had pointed toward the old platform. The arrow now points directly at Rali's elbow.

ACT THREE

EXT. PLATFORM THREE - LATER

The next train pulls in. Mena confirms the route and departure time on the live board. Odo reports from the platform, while Rali reads the board only after Mena checks it. Kiko asks Davi to show the old delay graphic beside the live feed. Davi places them side by side; Rali's earlier prediction appears next to the train already gone.

Rali says the express has left, the local is boarding, and platform three is correct. Kiko wants to replay the arrival. Mena reminds him that a replay must be labeled. Odo holds up a card reading RECORDED, and Davi puts it in the corner of the screen. The train doors close. Rali steps clear of the platform edge; the camera stays on the sign rather than chasing the last carriage.

Kiko cues the departure sting as the train pulls away. This time it lands on time. Rali thanks Mena and asks Odo for the next live board reading. He answers the time and platform without looking at the stale monitor. Kiko removes the delayed graphic from the rundown. The crew walks toward the station entrance, where the clock is five minutes slow; Mena sticks an accurate time card beside it.


The departure board updates while Odo is speaking. Platform three now lists the express as delayed, and the local train is ready to depart. Rali asks Mena to confirm the route change. Mena checks the station radio, then reads the updated board. Kiko tries to keep the old arrival graphic on screen because it is already animated. Davi points the camera at the live board, where the new time is visible. Odo takes the RECORDED card off the previous replay and changes it to DELAYED. Rali corrects her line before the train pulls away. A passenger turns toward the platform named on the graphic; Mena directs them to the correct track. Kiko removes the animation rather than adding another arrow. The camera captures the updated sign and the passenger heading the right way.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE REPORT

Davi labels the replay buffer with its delay. Kiko bins the old platform arrow. Mena checks the live board before signing out.

FINAL IMAGE: The accurate platform sign stays in frame while the old prediction plays, clearly marked RECORDED.
