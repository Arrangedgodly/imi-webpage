# The Projection Has a Projection

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: election-night television graphics that project winners from partial returns and compete for spectacle*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Anchor Luma is covering a small council election when producer Boro asks her to call the race before all precincts report. Data analyst Seki tries to keep the live map accurate while the studio keeps adding victory banners and countdowns.

Cast: Luma, a macaque anchor; Boro, a capuchin producer; Seki, a gorilla data analyst; and Toma, a langur precinct reporter.

## Episode: "Hold the Banner"

### Cold Open

[NEWS STUDIO. TWO CANDIDATE PHOTOS FACE EACH OTHER. ONE PRECINCT HAS REPORTED. BORO HOLDS A GIANT VICTORY BANNER.] 

LUMA: We have one precinct in. The count is close.

BORO: Can we call it?

SEKI: No. The west precinct has not reported.

[THE BANNER OPENS EARLY AND COVERS LUMA'S MAP.]

LUMA: The banner has called it.

[SEKI ROLLS THE MAP DOWN. THE VICTORY BANNER COVERS THE POLL WORKERS' STATUS PANEL TOO.]

SEKI: We need to see which precincts are reporting.

BORO: The banner is a stronger image.

LUMA: It is also covering the numbers.

[TOMA CALLS IN FROM THE WEST PRECINCT. BENEATH THE BANNER, THE PHONE LIGHT FLASHES UNANSWERED.] 

LUMA: We have a call from the west.

BORO: Can it wait until the reveal?

SEKI: The west has a third of the ballots.

### Act One

[SEKI SHOWS THE RESULTS TABLE. THE CANDIDATES ARE SEPARATED BY THREE VOTES.]

SEKI: We have only one third of the ballots.

BORO: The graphic needs a color.

LUMA: Use gray until we know.

[BORO TURNS THE GRAY MAP BRIGHT RED. THE SCREEN LABELS THE RACE "TOO CLOSE TO CALL" AND THEN ADDS A COUNTDOWN CLOCK.] 

SEKI: The clock is not part of the count.

LUMA: It is counting down to the next update.

[BORO HOLDS UP TWO NEW GRAPHICS: "SURGE" AND "SHOCKER." LUMA TAKES BOTH AND SETS THEM FACE DOWN.]

[BORO PUTS A COLOR-CODED MAP ON THE FLOOR FOR A CAMERA PAN. THE MARKERS COVER THE STUDIO EXIT. SEKI MOVES THEM AGAINST THE WALL.] 

SEKI: We need the map, not the obstacle course.

BORO: The overhead shot looks impressive.

[LUMA POINTS THE CAMERA AT THE TABLE OF VERIFIED FORMS INSTEAD. THE GRAPHIC AUTOMATICALLY LABELS IT "EARLY VICTORY PARTY." ]

LUMA: That is a paperwork table.

[THE PHONE LIGHT FLASHES AGAIN. TOMA'S CALL HAS DROPPED. BORO CUES A REMOTE INTERVIEW WITH A PARTY SPOKESPERSON WHO IS STILL WAITING FOR RESULTS.] 

SPOKESPERSON: We are waiting for the certified total.

BORO: Can you say "historic night"?

SPOKESPERSON: It is Tuesday.

[SEKI RESTORES THE CALL TO TOMA. SHE HAS THE SIGNED FORM. THE MAP UPDATES, BUT THE EAST CANDIDATE'S LEAD SHRINKS FROM THREE TO TWO.] 

LUMA: The new count changed the margin.

BORO: It changed the banner too.

### Act Two

[TOMA REPORTS FROM THE WEST PRECINCT. A LINE OF VOTERS IS STILL INSIDE.]

TOMA: The poll workers are counting the final ballots.

BORO: Can you ask how the race feels?

TOMA: It feels like counting.

[SEKI CHECKS THE REPORTING FORM. THE PRECINCT TOTAL IS MISSING A SIGNATURE.]

SEKI: We cannot add it until the form is verified.

[BORO STARTS A BREAKING NEWS STING. THE STUDIO LIGHTS FLASH. TOMA'S REPORT CONTINUES ON THE SCREEN, WHERE SHE IS QUIETLY WAITING WITH THE POLL WORKERS.] 

LUMA: The music says emergency. The report says wait.

[TOMA GETS THE SIGNATURE. SEKI ENTERS THE NUMBERS. THE MAP CHANGES COLOR, BUT THE LEAD IS STILL ONLY TWO VOTES.] 

BORO: Now can we call it?

SEKI: There are still ballots to review.

[BORO ROLLS THE VICTORY BANNER BACK INTO ITS TUBE. IT SNAGS ON THE STUDIO RAIL AND UNFURLS AGAIN.] 

### Act Three

[THE FINAL VERIFIED TOTAL ARRIVES. SEKI CHECKS THE FORM AGAINST THE TABLE. LUMA READS THE RESULTS WITHOUT A SOUND EFFECT.] 

LUMA: The east candidate has won by two votes.

BORO: We have the banner ready.

SEKI: Make sure the name matches the certified result.

[BORO OPENS THE BANNER. IT HAS THE OTHER CANDIDATE'S NAME. HE ROLLS IT UP BEFORE IT REACHES THE CAMERA.] 

BORO: The correct banner is in the archive box.

LUMA: We can use the map.

[THE VERIFIED MAP SHOWS THE FINAL COUNT. TOMA HOLDS UP THE COMPLETED FORM FROM THE PRECINCT. THE GRAPHICS TEAM PUTS A SMALL LABEL ON SCREEN: "CERTIFIED." ]

[BORO UNROLLS THE CORRECT BANNER. THE CANDIDATE'S NAME IS RIGHT, BUT THE RACE NUMBER IS WRONG.] 

SEKI: That is the count from the prior update.

[BORO TURNS THE BANNER AROUND. THE BACK IS BLANK. LUMA HOLDS UP THE VERIFIED MAP AGAIN.] 

LUMA: We have a result and a map. We can put the banner away.

### Tag

[BORO FINDS THE WRONG BANNER STILL HALF-UNROLLED UNDER HIS DESK. HE USES IT TO COVER THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK.] 

BORO: The next update is tomorrow.

SEKI: The clock can stop now.
