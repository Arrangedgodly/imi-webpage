# Five Stars for the Soup

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: restaurant review television, where critics rate a soup by its bowl, steam, and dramatic arrival*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A food critic visits a village cafe and gives the soup five different scores before tasting it.

Cast: Critic Piri, capuchin; chef Nara, macaque; host Odo, baboon; server Seli, gibbon; camera assistant Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Slurp Score"

### Cold Open

[CAFE TABLE. A BOWL OF SOUP STEAMS.]

ODO: The critic is ready.

PIRI: I am ready to inspect the bowl.

NARA: The soup is getting cold.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CAMERA CIRCLES THE BOWL.]

Piri rates the bowl's shape, the spoon's weight, the table's height, and the steam's direction. Odo asks when the soup will be tasted.

PIRI: We rate presentation first.

SELI: The presentation is a bowl.

TAVI: The spoon is clean.

Piri asks Nara what ingredients are in the soup. Nara says squash, water, and herbs from the garden.

ODO: Is it locally sourced?

NARA: The garden is behind the cafe.

PIRI: A strong origin story.

Seli asks if the soup can be served. Piri asks for a close-up of the steam. The camera fogs.

TAVI: The lens has steamed.

### Act Two

[THE SOUP IS MOVED TO A COOLER SPOT.]

Odo places a small fan beside the table. The steam disappears. Piri says the soup has lost its atmosphere.

PIRI: We need visible warmth.

NARA: We can warm it again.

SELI: Then it will take longer.

Odo turns off the fan. The steam returns but blocks the camera view. Tavi moves the camera to the side. Piri rates the steam highly.

PIRI: Four stars for aroma.

NARA: You have not tasted it.

PIRI: I can smell it.

Seli brings a second bowl for herself. She tastes it and says it needs salt. Nara adds a pinch. Piri finally takes a spoonful.

[PIRI INSPECTS THE SOUP'S TEMPERATURE.]

Piri asks Odo to bring a thermometer. Odo returns with a meat thermometer from the cafe kitchen. Nara says the soup needs to be stirred before the reading.

PIRI: The surface is cooler.

NARA: The bowl is wide.

SELI: The camera shows a skin forming.

PIRI: That affects texture.

NARA: It is a thin film.

Odo stirs the soup. The steam rises directly into the camera again. Tavi shifts the light; the steam becomes visible without covering the lens.

TAVI: The angle makes a difference.

PIRI: I rate the angle four stars.

NARA: You are reviewing lunch, not the set.

Piri takes another spoonful and notices that one side is warmer than the other. Nara explains that the bowl was not stirred after serving. She mixes it properly and serves a second spoonful.

PIRI: The flavor is more even.

SELI: We have found the real issue.

ODO: It was the stir.

PIRI: The spoon has contributed.

Seli asks if the soup needs a garnish. Nara adds a small herb leaf. Piri says it improves the color, but it does not change the flavor.

[SELI ASKS FOR A SECOND SCORE.]

Piri rates the soup's temperature four stars, the bowl five, and the recipe four. Odo asks if the combined rating is the average.

PIRI: I do not average categories.

NARA: Then the soup has no single score.

PIRI: The show needs a number.

Seli writes each score on a card. The cards cover the menu. Tavi moves them to the camera monitor, where they reflect in the lens.

TAVI: The scorecards are in the shot.

ODO: They are the result.

NARA: The result is a bowl of soup.

Piri asks for the recipe. Nara says the quantities depend on how much squash they had that morning. Odo wants a fixed version for viewers. Nara lists the ingredients but refuses to pretend every pot tastes identical.

PIRI: That is not a precise recipe.

NARA: It is soup.

### Act Three

[PIRI LOOKS AT THE SCORECARD.]

Piri says the soup has good texture, a gentle herb finish, and a clear squash flavor. Odo asks for a numerical score. Piri gives the soup four stars.

NARA: What would make it five?

PIRI: I need to taste it again.

Piri takes another spoonful and revises the score to five. Odo asks if the bowl also gets five stars.

PIRI: The bowl is not the soup.

Seli says the soup was served in the same bowl. Piri says the bowl helped the soup arrive.

### Tag

[THE CREW PACKS THE CAMERA.]

Odo asks for the final rating. Piri gives the cafe five stars for soup and three for camera fog.

TAVI: The lens is clear now.

PIRI: Then the camera gets four.

NARA: The soup is finished.

[SFX: SPOON TAPS BOWL, THEME.]
