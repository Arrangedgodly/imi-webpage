# The One-Minute Advice Show

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime self-help programs, expert panels, and oversimplified advice segments*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A self-help host promises to solve every audience problem in sixty seconds, while the callers keep asking questions with more than one answer.

Cast: Host Lemi, a capuchin; expert Sora, a gorilla; caller Venn, a gibbon; audience helper Pala, a macaque; and timekeeper Bix, a baboon.

## Episode: "One Tip for Everything"

### Cold open

INT. DAYTIME STUDIO, DAY

[A digital clock counts down from 60 seconds. Lemi smiles at the camera.]

LEMI: Welcome to The One-Minute Advice Show. We have one minute to fix your day.

SORA: We have one minute for the segment.

LEMI: We can fix a lot in a minute.

BIX: The clock is running.

VENN: I haven't asked my question.

LEMI: Ask quickly.

VENN: My branch is leaning.

SORA: Is it attached to the tree?

VENN: Yes.

LEMI: Tip one: don't push it.

[The clock hits zero.]

BIX: We have reached the end of the advice.

### Act One

INT. STUDIO

[The clock resets. Pala hands Lemi a stack of audience cards.]

LEMI: Our next caller has trouble getting everyone to listen.

PALA: The caller is the whole audience.

SORA: Listening is a skill.

LEMI: Tip two: use a clear voice.

VENN: I can hear you.

LEMI: Good.

VENN: The branch is still leaning.

SORA: We should inspect the root.

BIX: That's more than one tip.

LEMI: We can summarize it. Check the root.

SORA: That is a good start, not a full solution.

LEMI: The show is called One-Minute Advice.

SORA: Not One-Minute Solutions.

LEMI: We can say that in the closing.

[Pala gives Lemi another card.]

LEMI: This viewer has trouble sharing a nest.

PALA: Two adults are using one nest.

SORA: They should talk about space.

LEMI: Tip three: make more space.

SORA: There is a limit to the branch.

BIX: Thirty seconds.

LEMI: Tip four: take turns.

PALA: They already take turns.

LEMI: Then take better turns.

SORA: That is not advice.

LEMI: It is a phrase with confidence.

### Act Two

INT. ADVICE DESK

[Venn calls again, holding the leaning branch. The studio clock begins counting down.]

VENN: I tried not to push. It leaned farther.

SORA: Is the soil loose?

VENN: Yes.

SORA: Can you brace it?

VENN: I can use this small stake.

LEMI: Tip five: use a stake.

SORA: First check that the stake won't damage the roots.

LEMI: Tip six: be careful.

BIX: The clock says ten seconds.

VENN: How do I keep it from leaning while I check?

LEMI: Hold it.

SORA: That is a temporary measure.

LEMI: Tip seven: temporary measures can buy time.

BIX: The time is gone.

[The clock freezes at zero. Venn continues holding the branch through the screen.]

LEMI: We have a caller in our studio.

SORA: He is on a video feed.

PALA: The branch has entered the show.

LEMI: We can demonstrate bracing with the studio set.

[Sora lifts a decorative branch. The entire background shifts.]

SORA: The set is also leaning.

BIX: We have a second problem.

LEMI: Tip eight: don't lean on the set.

### Act Three

INT. STUDIO, LATER

[Sora props the decorative set with a wooden stand. The clock is unplugged.]

SORA: The real answer takes longer than a minute. We need to check the soil, use a support, and watch how the branch responds.

LEMI: That is three steps.

SORA: Yes.

LEMI: The show can handle three steps.

BIX: We are out of time.

LEMI: We can take the time from tomorrow's show.

PALA: Tomorrow's show is also one minute.

VENN: My branch is stable now.

SORA: Did you brace it?

VENN: I asked my neighbor to hold it while I tightened the soil.

LEMI: Tip nine: ask for help.

SORA: That is good advice.

LEMI: We have saved the day.

BIX: The clock is still at zero.

LEMI: We can end on the clock.

PALA: The clock has not moved.

LEMI: It has served its purpose.

[The branch on set shifts. Everyone steadies the backdrop together.]

SORA: We should inspect that root.

LEMI: After the show.

BIX: The show is over.

LEMI: Then it is after the show.

### The advice card

INT. STUDIO

[Pala holds up a viewer card. The clock has been replaced by a sand timer.]

PALA: This caller wants to know how to stop interrupting.

LEMI: Tip ten: wait your turn.

SORA: You interrupted me to say that.

LEMI: I did.

BIX: The timer is almost empty.

PALA: The caller is still speaking.

LEMI: We can answer after they finish.

[Everyone waits. The caller continues from the monitor. The sand runs out.]

BIX: We are out of time.

SORA: We listened to the whole question.

LEMI: That was the advice.

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, EVENING

[Lemi points to a sign reading "ADVICE IS NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR DOING THE THING."]

LEMI: Our final tip is to read the sign.

SORA: The sign says "Don't lean on it."

[The backdrop tips gently into the frame.]
