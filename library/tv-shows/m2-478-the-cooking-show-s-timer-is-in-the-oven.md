# The Cooking Show's Timer Is in the Oven

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live daytime cooking television, ingredient close-ups, and hosts who try to race an oven timer*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Rima, a gibbon; chef Talo, an orangutan; assistant Peka, a macaque; floor manager Nori, a baboon; camera operator Vali, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING STUDIO - MORNING

A tray of rolls sits in a warm oven. The host peers through the glass. A countdown clock on the studio wall reads 03:00.

RIMA: Three minutes until our golden breakfast rolls are ready.

TALO: The oven timer says twelve minutes.

PEKA: The studio clock is for the segment.

[The host turns the wall clock away from camera. The oven beeps once; the rolls are not ready.] 

ACT ONE

INT. COOKING STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Chef Talo demonstrates kneading a small dough sample. Rima asks for a dramatic pull-apart shot. The camera operator focuses on the flour bowl instead of the dough. Peka resets the camera mark and keeps the hot tray behind the counter line.

TALO: The rolls need time to rise and bake. The segment cannot change that.

RIMA: Can we show the ingredients while we wait?

NORI: Yes, but do not imply the rolls are finished.

[The host lists flour, yeast, milk, and salt. Talo notes that the yeast was activated before the broadcast. The producer asks for an instant version. Talo shows the risen dough and explains that the earlier preparation is already done.] 

RIMA: Viewers will want the rising to happen now.

TALO: It happened before the camera turned on.

[The chef places dough in the pan and opens the oven only after the heat is reduced. The wall clock's countdown reaches zero. Nori holds up a card reading SEGMENT BREAK.] 

ACT TWO

INT. COOKING STUDIO - LATER

The wall clock is reset to five minutes. The oven's timer is still running. Rima proposes using a tray of finished rolls as a reveal. Talo says those are yesterday's samples and asks Peka to identify them as such. Peka labels the plate SAMPLE - NOT TODAY'S BATCH.

RIMA: We can taste the sample while the real rolls bake.

NORI: That is fine if it is labeled.

[The host cuts a roll. The sample is dry from sitting out. Talo explains the texture but does not pretend it came from the live oven. The camera operator catches Rima trying to hide the label with a napkin.] 

VALI: The label is part of the frame.

RIMA: It ruins the reveal.

TALO: It clarifies the sample.

[The host moves the plate into the foreground and reads the label. Peka checks the oven timer. Six minutes remain. Rima asks Talo to describe the aroma. Talo points to the open window; the smell is from the studio breakfast area.] 

TALO: The current rolls are still baking.

[The assistant closes the window so the studio smell does not confuse the demonstration. The host tries a second time, describing the ingredients and oven temperature rather than claiming a smell.] 

[Nori asks for a final demonstration of the dough recipe card while the rolls cool. Talo reads the measured amounts aloud; Rima reaches for a decorative spoon that is not on the ingredient list. Peka points to the real measuring cup.] 

PEKA: Use the marked cup. The spoon is only for serving.

RIMA: Our props have jobs.

TALO: The cup measures; the spoon serves.

[The camera frames the card and the tools. The host returns the decorative spoon to its tray instead of measuring with it. The crew can now repeat the recipe without using the studio countdown as a timer.] 

ACT THREE

INT. COOKING STUDIO - NOON

The oven timer beeps. Talo checks the rolls with a thermometer and confirms they are baked through. Nori uses heat gloves to remove the tray and places it on a cooling rack. The host starts a countdown to the first bite, but Talo asks for a short cooling period.

RIMA: The oven says done. Why wait?

TALO: The tray is hot, and the rolls are too warm to handle.

[The host sets the countdown for two minutes. Peka moves the studio clock out of frame, leaving the cooling rack visible. The rolls cool. The chef breaks one open and shows the crumb; no steam burns anyone.] 

PEKA: This batch came from the oven we have been timing.

VALI: Clear shot of the real result.

[The host tastes a cooled piece and nods. The crew labels the recipe card with the full baking time, not the studio countdown. Talo places the remaining rolls in a basket for the crew.] 

RIMA: The segment ended in five minutes. The rolls took longer.

NORI: Both clocks were accurate.

TAG

The wall countdown reaches zero again. Everyone ignores it while the oven timer remains off.

FINAL IMAGE: The recipe card lists the real baking time beside the cooling rack and finished rolls.
