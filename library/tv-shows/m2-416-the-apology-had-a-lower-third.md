# The Apology Had a Lower Third

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised press conferences, prepared statements, lower-third captions, and officials who answer a different question*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A park director holds a live press conference after the station's map marks a closed footbridge as open, but the caption writer keeps making the apology sound like an award.

Cast: Director Pavo, a baboon; reporter Reni, a macaque; map technician Tavi, an orangutan; caption operator Neli, a capuchin; host Odo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. PARK PRESS ROOM - DAY

Pavo stands at a lectern. The screen reads: "DIRECTOR RECEIVES MAP AWARD."

PAVO: I am here to correct a map.

NELI: The lower third is from yesterday.

TAVI: It is still on the map.

ACT ONE

Odo welcomes viewers to a live press briefing. The park's online map showed a footbridge as open after a routine inspection closed it. The barrier stayed in place; no visitors crossed. Pavo says the map has been corrected. Reni asks who approved the change.

PAVO: The map update came from our route feed.

RENI: Who approved the route feed?

PAVO: I did.

The screen updates: "DIRECTOR APPROVES ROUTE." Neli says she can change the caption. Pavo asks for the full map. Tavi rolls it out across the table. The arrow to the bridge is printed over a fold, making it point toward the closed route.

ACT TWO

Reni asks whether the barrier was visible at the trailhead. Tavi says it was, but the digital map showed an open path. The station has added a red closure mark online and placed the same mark at the physical map board. Pavo tries reading the prepared statement. It says, "The route display did not reflect the current inspection status."

RENI: Did the park publish an incorrect map?

PAVO: Yes.

The lower-third caption now reads "PAVO SAYS YES." Odo asks if he can take a follow-up. Reni asks whether the park will inspect the other bridges. Pavo says they already did, and all are open. Tavi points at the map where the other routes have green marks. Neli changes the caption to "OTHER BRIDGES OPEN."

ACT TWO-B

Pavo takes questions from two reporters. One asks if the closed bridge will reopen tomorrow. Tavi says the inspection is scheduled for Friday, and the bridge will stay closed until it passes. Pavo repeats the answer. The caption operator writes "BRIDGE OPENS FRIDAY." Tavi corrects her.

TAVI: Inspection is Friday. Opening is not guaranteed.

NELI: I will change it.

The caption now reads "BRIDGE INSPECTION FRIDAY." Reni asks whether the park will notify visitors before they reach the footbridge. Pavo says the closure will appear at the trailhead and online. Tavi raises the printed sign, which says "CLOSED FOR INSPECTION."

RENI: That is clear.

PAVO: We should have updated both maps at the same time.

Odo asks for a final statement. Pavo steps away from the lectern and points directly to the corrected map. The TV camera zooms on his hand; he moves it so viewers can see the whole route. Reni follows the line from the parking lot to the safe alternate path.

ACT TWO-B - CAPTION CHECK

Reni asks Pavo to walk through what a visitor sees at the trailhead. Tavi holds up the old printed map; Neli's live caption reads "PARK DIRECTOR TAKES A HIKE." Pavo stops at the model barrier.

PAVO: This is where the route ends today.

NELI: The caption is describing the shot.

RENI: It is also describing a hike.

Neli changes it to "FOOTBRIDGE CLOSED FOR INSPECTION." The words stretch across two lines and cover the red closure symbol. She shortens the caption to "BRIDGE CLOSED" and moves it beneath the map. Pavo points to the alternate trail, but the camera crops it out. Odo widens the frame.

TAVI: Now the detour is visible.

PAVO: Good. A correction should be readable.

A reporter asks whether visitors will be turned away. Pavo says the trail remains open to the overlook by the alternate path, while the bridge remains closed. Reni asks Neli to put both facts on screen. Neli types; the first try says "OPEN BRIDGE ALTERNATE." She deletes it and writes a full sentence. No applause cue plays.

ACT THREE

Pavo reads a brief apology: the digital map was wrong, the barrier remained closed, and the park should have synchronized its route updates. Tavi confirms that the other bridges were inspected. Reni asks where visitors can check current closures. Pavo names the park's printed board and website. Neli writes the same information on screen without adding a title or award.

PAVO: We will keep the bridge marked closed until the inspection is complete.

RENI: Thank you.

The press briefing ends. Tavi folds the paper map along its seam. The arrow now points to the alternate route. Odo thanks the reporters and steps away from the lectern. The caption board goes blank.

TAG

Neli types one last caption: "PRESS CONFERENCE ENDS." The screen displays it below an empty lectern.

FINAL IMAGE: The corrected map hangs beside the closed-bridge sign, both showing the same route.
