# The Quiz Show with One Answer

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: game shows that manufacture suspense even when every contestant knows the answer*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Contestants compete to name the only object on the stage, while the host stretches each clue into a dramatic reveal.

Cast: Host Miri, a capuchin; contestant Odo, a baboon; contestant Leni, a gibbon; judge Nara, a macaque; stagehand Kito, a lemur.

Episode: "What Is on the Table?"

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME STUDIO - DAY

A small wooden spoon sits under a glass dome. Miri gestures to it.

MIRI: Contestants, can you name this mystery object?

ODO: A spoon.

LENI: A wooden spoon.

MIRI: We have two strong answers.

ACT ONE

Miri explains the rules. Contestants get points for correct answers, dramatic pauses, and confident posture. Nara says the score sheet contains no category for posture. Miri adds it in pencil.

Kito hides the spoon under a cloth. Miri reads the first clue: "It is used in a kitchen." Both contestants buzz.

ODO: Spoon.

LENI: Wooden spoon.

Miri asks them to wait for the full clue. The second clue is "It can stir a pot." They buzz again. Miri says the answer will be revealed after the commercial break.

Nara asks if there is a commercial. Kito says they have one sponsor message about a basket.

ACT TWO

After the break, Miri gives the final clue: "It is made of wood and has a handle." Odo says it is a spoon. Leni says it is the same spoon as before.

Miri asks them to lock in their answers. They press the buttons. Odo's buzzer is louder. Miri calls that a sound bonus. Nara refuses to score it.

Kito lifts the cloth. The spoon is visible. Miri asks the audience to guess. Everyone says "spoon." The applause sign lights up late.

MIRI: The anticipation is at a peak.

LENI: The object is not moving.

Miri asks the contestants to explain their reasoning. Odo says he recognized it immediately. Leni says she did too, but she added "wooden" for detail.

ACT TWO-B

The final round is a wager. Contestants may risk all points on the identity of the object. Odo wagers one point. Leni wagers her entire score. Nara asks if either can lose points for answering correctly. Miri checks the rules; there is no penalty.

The spoon is placed back under the dome. Miri gives a countdown from ten. Odo says they already answered. She counts anyway. Leni asks if the show can end early. Miri reaches nine.

ACT TWO-C

Nara asks the contestants to write their answers on cards instead of buzzing. Odo writes "spoon" and Leni writes "wooden spoon." Miri asks if the more detailed answer earns extra points. Nara says the rules mention accuracy, not length.

MIRI: Then we need a tiebreaker.

NARA: The rules do not have one.

MIRI: We can add one.

NARA: You can. The audience cannot.

Kito tests the buzzers. Odo presses his; it is silent. Leni presses hers; it buzzes twice. Kito opens the panel and finds the wires crossed. The show has been awarding answers by volume, not speed.

ODO: I thought I was first.

LENI: You were louder.

Miri says a second recording will settle it. She plays the raw track: the delayed buzzer overlaps Odo's answer, while Leni answers after Miri repeats the question. The recording cannot establish which buzzer rang first.

MIRI: We have evidence and no winner.

NARA: We have two correct answers.

The host reaches for the prize basket. It contains one basket of fruit and one empty basket. She sets them aside until the score is final. Miri tries to add a final clue, but Odo names the spoon before she finishes reading it. Nara stops the countdown and closes the question cards.

ODO: The answer is still a spoon.

LENI: Even without the clue.

Kito removes the buzzers from the table. Miri announces that the next recording will use hand-raising so the answer cannot be hidden under a sound effect.

ACT THREE

The dome lifts. The spoon remains on the table. Nara awards each contestant one point for a correct answer; the faulty buzzers make the order impossible to score.

Miri declares a tie and awards the game to the spoon for appearing throughout the show. Nara writes "spoon" on the scorecard. Kito puts it away in the kitchen drawer.

Nara gives each contestant half of the fruit basket. Leni leaves the empty basket in the prop room; Odo carries his share of fruit to the kitchen.

TAG

Miri returns with a fork.

MIRI: New mystery.

ODO: Fork.

MIRI: We are cutting to commercial.
