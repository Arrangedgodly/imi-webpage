# The Crate at the Checkout

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: retail mockumentaries about staff mission statements, staged product demos and the strange behavior of shoppers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A small market prepares for a corporate visit. The staff rehearse customer-service slogans, but the customers and a troop of monkeys keep using the displays in practical ways the company did not plan.

Cast: Manager Ves, a capuchin; cashier Nara, a macaque; stocker Fip, a gibbon; corporate observer Dax, a baboon; and shoppers.

## Episode: "Customer Journey"

### Cold Open

[THE STORE TEAM STANDS BEFORE A DISPLAY OF FRUIT.]

VES: Remember the phrase: "Every shelf tells a story."

NARA: What story does this empty shelf tell?

[FIP PUSHES A CART PAST WITH THREE CRATES.]

FIP: The delivery is late.

### Act One

[DAX ARRIVES WITH A TABLET.]

DAX: I am here to observe the customer journey.

VES: We have a path marked through the store.

[THE PATH IS BLOCKED BY A LOW DISPLAY.]

DAX: Customers should flow smoothly.

NARA: They cannot flow through the display.

[DAX TAKES NOTES. A SHOPPER MOVES THE DISPLAY ASIDE.]

SHOPPER: I need to reach the apples.

VES: That was not an approved layout change.

SHOPPER: It is now.

[FIP STACKS THE CRATES. A SMALL MONKEY CLIMBS INTO AN EMPTY ONE.]

DAX: We need to prevent unauthorized use of packaging.

NARA: The crate is empty.

MONKEY: It is also comfortable.

### Act Two

[VES DEMONSTRATES A NEW LOYALTY CARD.]

VES: Each purchase earns a leaf. Ten leaves earn a fruit basket.

SHOPPER: Can I collect the leaves without the app?

DAX: The app is part of the customer journey.

NARA: The customer is standing here.

[THE SHOPPER PRESENTS A PAPER CARD WITH NINE STAMPS.]

VES: One more purchase and you get the basket.

[THE SHOPPER BUYS A SINGLE FIG. NARA STAMPS THE CARD AND HANDS OVER A FRUIT BASKET FROM UNDER THE COUNTER. DAX ASKS TO KEEP THE CARD FOR HIS SCANNER.]

DAX: We should scan it.

NARA: The scanner is unplugged.

[DAX LOOKS AT THE TABLET. THE APP WILL NOT LOAD.]

DAX: We have a temporary issue.

[THE SMALL MONKEY TAKES THE DISPLAY BASKET AND CLIMBS INTO IT.]

FIP: That is not the prize basket.

MONKEY: It is a basket.

[DAX SETS UP A MOCK CUSTOMER INTERVIEW BESIDE THE FRUIT DISPLAY.]

DAX: What brings you to this store?

SHOPPER: I need figs.

DAX: What emotion does the fig create?

SHOPPER: Hunger.

NARA: That is a useful emotion.

[DAX TURNS TO FIP.]

DAX: How does the display tell a story?

FIP: It tells me which crate to unload first.

[DAX LOOKS AT HIS TABLET. THE TABLET SCREEN GOES DARK.]

DAX: The survey has timed out.

VES: The store has not.

[A CHILD REACHES FOR AN APPLE. THE DISPLAY IS TOO HIGH.]

[NARA MOVES THE APPLE BASKET LOWER.]

SHOPPER: Thank you.

DAX: That was a customer-centered adjustment.

NARA: It was an apple basket.

[FIP REARRANGES THE CRATES SO THE EMPTY ONE IS AT FLOOR LEVEL.]

MONKEY: This one is for sitting.

DAX: The seating experience is not in the plan.

VES: It is in the store now.

[THE STAFF PRACTICES A CORPORATE GREETING.]

VES: Welcome to our market. How can we help you today?

NARA: Do you need a basket?

SHOPPER: Yes.

[FIP HANDS OVER A BASKET. A MONKEY HAS LEFT A FIG INSIDE.]

SHOPPER: Is this included?

FIP: It is not on the shelf.

DAX: We need a process for found fruit.

NARA: It belongs to the monkey.

[THE MONKEY TAKES THE FIG AND CLIMBS INTO THE EMPTY CRATE.]

VES: We could add a sign.

DAX: The sign needs corporate wording.

NARA: "Please leave the fruit where it is."

[DAX TYPES IT INTO HIS TABLET. THE BATTERY DIES AGAIN.]

DAX: I will remember it.

DAX: I will note the crate as an informal seating area.

VES: It is an empty crate.

DAX: I will record that the basket was awarded. The paper card has completed the customer journey ahead of the app.

### Act Three

[THE CORPORATE VISIT ENDS. DAX REVIEWS THE STORE PATH.]

DAX: The customers moved displays, used paper cards, and carried the basket away.

VES: The store still served them.

NARA: The path is clear now.

[FIP MOVES THE EMPTY CRATES TO THE BACK. THE MONKEY REMAINS IN ONE.]

DAX: We should add a policy about crates.

VES: We should add a cushion.

[THE SHOPPER RETURNS WITH THE FRUIT BASKET. NARA HANDS OVER THE PAPER CARD.]

SHOPPER: The stamps worked.

DAX: We will evaluate the paper process.

NARA: It already worked.

[THE MONKEY CARRIES THE EMPTY CRATE TO THE EXIT AND SETS IT DOWN.]

VES: Is that an unauthorized departure?

FIP: It is a crate leaving the store.

DAX: I will record it as a customer interaction.

[THE SHOPPER PICKS UP THE BASKET. THE MONKEY CLIMBS INTO IT, THEN CLIMBS OUT WHEN IT TILTS.]

NARA: That is why the prize basket has a handle.
