# The Podium Is on a Timer

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised political debates, strict answer clocks, instant rebuttals, and candidates speaking past a simple question*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Three candidates debate where to put a public bench while the moderator's podium timer keeps stopping whenever anyone says "community."

Cast: Moderator Sava, a macaque; candidate Boro, a baboon; candidate Lemi, a gibbon; candidate Odi, a capuchin; timer operator Piri, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. DEBATE STAGE - EVENING

Sava reads the first question: "Where should the town place its new bench?" Boro begins answering. The timer stops.

BORO: We need a community - 

PIRI: The word paused the clock.

LEMI: Is "bench" allowed?

The clock resumes. Odi presses his microphone button, but Boro's podium lights up.

ACT ONE

The candidates each propose a location. Boro wants the sunny side of the plaza. Lemi wants the shade beside the fountain. Odi wants the bus stop, where older residents currently stand. Sava asks them to explain how they will keep the walkway clear.

BORO: The walkway is central to our community plan.

Piri stops the timer.

BORO: Can I say it in a different order?

PIRI: You can say "plan."

LEMI: My plan adds a bench by the fountain, with a gap for wheelchairs and carts.

ODI: Mine adds one at the bus stop. We can paint the curb line so the queue leaves room.

Sava asks Boro to respond. Boro says he supports benches but not benches that look like they are deciding where people sit. The audience applauds. Odi asks what that means. Boro says he will clarify in his closing statement.

ACT TWO

The moderator announces rebuttals. Each candidate gets twenty seconds. Piri starts the countdown. Lemi asks Boro if his plaza site has shade. Boro says it has shade in late afternoon. Odi asks where people wait before then. Boro starts to answer; the clock stops on "community" again.

BORO: The clock has a bias against my vocabulary.

PIRI: The clock is a word detector.

SAVA: The question was about shade.

BORO: I will add an awning.

LEMI: That's an additional cost.

BORO: We have an awning budget.

ODI: The question is whether the bench goes where people already wait.

The debate graphic shows three dots on a map. Piri moves the dot at the plaza to align it with a narrow path. The dot covers the only walkway. She moves it back; the screen labels it "ACCESS ROUTE." Sava asks for a diagram. Lemi draws a square, a fountain, a wide route and a bench. Odi adds the bus stop and a queue line. Boro adds a tiny tree to the plaza and says it provides shade.

BORO: The tree is projected shade.

LEMI: Is it planted?

BORO: It is in the plan.

ACT TWO-B

An audience member asks whether there will be one bench or three. The candidates look at each other. Sava says the funding approved one. Odi suggests putting it at the bus stop this year, then seeking funds for the other two. Boro says a modular bench can be moved between locations.

LEMI: By whom?

BORO: Public works.

PIRI: They are not in the studio.

Sava asks for a final answer: where will each candidate place the single bench? Lemi says at the fountain, with a clear route. Odi says the bus stop, where the queue forms. Boro says the plaza, with an awning and a marked access lane. His timer starts, but the "community" detector shuts it down again.

BORO: I will use the other word. Neighbors.

The timer continues. He uses up the remaining seconds describing the awning. Sava stops the round before he can finish.

ACT THREE

The candidates step to the center stage for a final audience question. The audience asks them to pick one plan and one cost. Boro chooses the plaza with a painted access lane. Lemi chooses the fountain. Odi chooses the bus stop and says the queue paint can be done with the bench installation. Each gives one estimate. Piri checks the clock; none says "community."

SAVA: We have heard three locations, three costs, and one bench.

The studio audience votes on a mock ballot. The bus stop wins. Sava clarifies that it is not an election, only the audience's preference for the proposed bench site. Odi wins the final quick poll. Boro says he will support the site if public works confirms the route is clear. Lemi says the fountain remains a second choice.

TAG

The lights go down. Piri removes the word detector from the timer. Sava asks her to keep the clock running for the station signoff.

PIRI: It will run unless someone says "community."

SAVA: We can manage that.

The live signoff begins. Boro says "community" by habit. The timer stops with one second left.

FINAL IMAGE: The candidates stand frozen under the debate clock while Piri reaches up to restart it.
