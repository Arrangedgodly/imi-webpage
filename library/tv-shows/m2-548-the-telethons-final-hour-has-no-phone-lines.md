# The Telethon's Final Hour Has No Phone Lines

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live charity telethons, celebrity interruptions, and pledge totals that only rise during commercial breaks*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: HOST DARA, a gibbon; TECH KIP, a capuchin; GUEST STAR RUMI, an orangutan; CALLER NELL, a macaque; FLOOR DIRECTOR, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. TELETHON STUDIO - NIGHT

A giant pledge meter reads 4,000. Host Dara smiles into camera. The phone bank is silent.

DARA: We have raised four thousand. Can we reach five?

The floor director whispers, "The lines are down." Dara smiles wider.

DARA: We can reach five by thinking about it.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The final hour of the community telethon begins. Rumi enters wearing a glittering cape and asks where the phone bank is. Dara points to a row of volunteers staring at dead headsets. Kip is under the switchboard with a flashlight.

RUMI: I am here to answer calls.

KIP: The calls are not here.

Dara tells the camera the phone lines are "experiencing a brief dramatic pause." The floor director holds up a sign: KEEP SMILING. Rumi sits at the phone bank and lifts the receiver. It has no cord.

DARA: That is a prop phone.

RUMI: It has a dial.

KIP: It is from the museum.

The pledge meter flickers from 4,000 to 40,000, then back to 4,000. Dara asks whether the new total is real. Kip says the meter is connected to the studio's applause button, not the donation system. Rumi claps once; the total jumps.

RUMI: I have raised ten thousand with my hands.

DARA: Please do that again.

KIP: Please don't.

ACT TWO

Kip traces the phone bank problem to a patch cord unplugged so the crew could silence a buzzing headset. The cord is connected to the wrong board. He can restore the lines, but the patch panel is behind the pledge meter. The floor director asks him to wait for a cut. Dara refuses to pause the broadcast. She moves the meter aside, but it is wheeled onto a cable and goes dark.

FLOOR DIRECTOR: The number is gone.

DARA: We can give the number a moment.

RUMI: I can hold the sign.

Rumi props the meter up while Kip connects the cord. Nell's call comes through. She says she wants to pledge five dollars but has been listening for forty minutes.

DARA: We are grateful for every minute.

NELL: I have only five dollars.

DARA: We are grateful for five dollars.

The phone bank wakes up. Volunteers answer actual calls. The meter starts working, but it takes a full minute to update. Rumi keeps holding it and slowly sinks under its weight. Dara asks viewers to wait for the confirmed total. The floor director signals for applause; nobody claps.

INT. PHONE BANK - NIGHT

The volunteers take turns testing each headset. One line rings, then another. The floor director asks Dara to announce a matching challenge from a local bakery. Dara reads the sponsor card, but the pledge meter still shows 4,203.

DARA: The bakery will match the next hundred dollars.

RUMI: Is that a donation or a dessert?

KIP: It is both, if the bakery confirms.

The floor director calls the bakery line. No answer. Dara keeps the camera on the pledge volunteers while Kip reroutes the call. The line reaches a recorded message saying the bakery closes at nine. It is 9:07. Rumi suggests the crew buy the leftover bread and auction it. The floor director says that would require a separate receipt.

NELL: I can pledge five more dollars if it goes to the repair fund.

DARA: The pledge goes to the roof repair fund.

NELL: Good. I thought the meter was for the phone lines.

The volunteers laugh. The floor director lowers the KEEP SMILING sign. Kip checks that all six headsets are connected to the donation board, not the applause channel. He moves the prop phone into the archive box and labels it NOT CONNECTED. The host repeats the total only after the system confirms it. The pledge meter rises by five, then by twenty, and waits.



INT. STUDIO FLOOR - NIGHT

A viewer sends a text pledge. The volunteer reads it aloud, but the host mistakes the message for a product review of the silent prop phone. Kip shows her the screen and corrects the total. The floor director asks to replay the earlier false jump. Dara refuses and reads the confirmed number instead.

DARA: We will only show the total the board can verify.

RUMI: Can we show the phone?

KIP: Only in the archive.



ACT THREE

The confirmed pledges reach 4,203 dollars. Dara thanks every caller and explains that the earlier 40,000 was the applause button. Rumi finally puts the meter down. Kip tapes the patch cord in place and labels it DONATIONS ONLY.

RUMI: Can we raise the total now?

KIP: Only with pledges.

Dara takes the museum phone off the desk and puts it in a donation box.

FINAL IMAGE: The pledge meter ticks up by five dollars as Nell's call ends and the dead prop phone stays silent.
