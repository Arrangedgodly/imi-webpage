# The Case of the Missing Shade

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedural television, where officers investigate who moved a patch of shade from a picnic area*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: A quiet park complaint becomes a procedural investigation when a bench no longer sits in the shade. The officers reconstruct the sun, the tree, and a squirrel with excellent timing.

Cast: Detective Miri, a macaque; Officer Odo, a baboon; witness Luma, a gibbon; park keeper Pavi, a capuchin; technician Nara, a lemur.

## Episode: "Shade at Noon"

### Cold Open

[PARK BENCH. MIRI MEASURES A SHADOW WITH A RULER.]

MIRI: At ten, the bench was shaded. At noon, it was not.

ODO: The shade is missing.

PAVI: The sun moved.

MIRI: We have a suspect.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE OFFICERS STAND BY A TREE WITH A CHALK OUTLINE ON THE GROUND.]

MIRI: The complainant says someone moved the shade.

LUMA: I said the bench was too hot.

ODO: Did you see anyone near it?

LUMA: A squirrel.

MIRI: Describe the squirrel.

LUMA: Small. Brown. In a hurry.

NARA: That describes most squirrels.

Pavi shows them a park map. It marks the tree and bench but not the position of the sun. Miri asks Nara to reconstruct the shadow at ten.

NARA: The sun was lower. The shadow reached the bench.

MIRI: And at noon?

NARA: The sun was higher.

ODO: So the shade moved.

PAVI: The shade did not move. The light changed.

Miri writes "light changed" on the board under "possible motive."

### Act Two

[THE TEAM SETS A CAMERA ON A TRIPOD.]

Odo places a marker at the tip of the tree's shadow. Nara records the position. They wait for five minutes. The shadow shifts several inches.

MIRI: The suspect is moving.

ODO: We have a time-lapse.

LUMA: The tree is standing still.

PAVI: The shade follows the branches.

Miri asks Luma to sit on the bench. The sun reaches her face. She moves to the other end; a sliver of shade covers one shoulder.

LUMA: It is cooler here.

ODO: The bench has a hiding place.

NARA: The shade is not a physical object.

MIRI: It leaves no fingerprints.

Pavi finds a leaf on the bench. Miri treats it as evidence. Nara says the leaf fell from the tree above them.

MIRI: We need to test whether it moved itself.

Odo puts the leaf under a fan. It slides across the bench.

ODO: The evidence has shifted.

PAVI: The fan did it.

MIRI: We have identified an accomplice.

[THE DETECTIVES RETURN TO THE PARK OFFICE.]

Miri draws the time-lapse on a board. The tree's shadow moves east; the bench stays still until Pavi relocates it. Nara marks the squirrel's path in a different color.

MIRI: The complainant reported missing shade at noon.

PAVI: The shade moved because the sun moved.

ODO: The squirrel occupied it briefly.

LUMA: And the bench has been relocated to my preferred spot.

Miri asks whether the squirrel should receive a warning. Pavi says the squirrel is allowed to sit on the bench.

ODO: We cannot ticket wildlife.

MIRI: We can issue a verbal notice.

NARA: The squirrel is not listening.

The squirrel darts away with a leaf. Miri files the leaf under "evidence returned to nature."

### Act Three

[THE TEAM REVIEWS THE TIME-LAPSE ON A TABLET.]

The shadow moves across the ground as the sun travels. At one point, a squirrel crosses the frame and pauses in the shade.

MIRI: The squirrel occupied the missing shade.

LUMA: It sat there for a moment.

ODO: We have a witness and a suspect in the same shot.

Pavi adjusts the bench a few feet toward the tree. The bench is now shaded at noon, but it blocks the path.

NARA: We have solved one complaint and created another.

Miri asks Luma where she wants the bench. Luma points to a spot beside the path that has morning shade.

LUMA: I mostly come before lunch.

PAVI: We can set the bench there.

Odo moves it. The team measures the walkway; enough space remains for visitors to pass.

MIRI: The bench is restored to the shade.

NARA: The shade is not restored. The bench is moved.

MIRI: The report can say "complaint resolved."

### Tag

[THE NEXT MORNING. MIRI SITS ON THE BENCH WITH A NOTEBOOK.]

ODO: The shade is back.

MIRI: The sun is lower.

PAVI: The bench is still where we put it.

A squirrel sits on the bench beside Miri. She shifts over.

MIRI: The suspect has returned.

LUMA: It is a park.

MIRI: Case closed.

ODO: Until the sun moves again.

MIRI: The sun is not under investigation.
