# The Flatmate Accord

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: ensemble sitcoms about shared apartments, escalating household rules and tiny domestic territory*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Four flatmates try to divide one sunny window ledge among their plants, snacks, and favorite resting spots. A written agreement only makes the negotiations more theatrical.

Cast: Sola, a gibbon; Puck, a macaque; Deni, a capuchin; and Fara, a slow loris.

## Episode: "The Ledge Is Neutral Ground"

### Cold Open

[THE FLATMATES STARE AT A NARROW SUNNY LEDGE. THREE POTS AND A BOWL OF FRUIT ARE BALANCED THERE.]

SOLA: We need a schedule.

PUCK: I was sitting there.

DENI: You were asleep there.

FARA: The plants were here first.

[A POT TIPS. EVERYONE CATCHES SOMETHING DIFFERENT.]

### Act One

[DENI UNROLLS A CHART ON THE FLOOR.]

DENI: Morning for plants, noon for fruit, afternoon for sitting.

PUCK: Where do I sit in the morning?

DENI: Somewhere else.

PUCK: That was my current plan.

SOLA: We should rotate.

FARA: The sun does not rotate around our chart.

[SOLA MOVES A POT TWO INCHES. A SQUARE OF SUNLIGHT SHIFTS OFF THE LEAVES.]

FARA: The plant has lost its sun.

SOLA: I was making room.

FARA: Room is not the same as light.

[PUCK SETS THE FRUIT BOWL DOWN. IT COVERS THE CHART.]

PUCK: Now the schedule has shade.

DENI: We can write a ledge charter.

### Act Two

[THEY HOLD A MEETING AROUND THE WINDOW. DENI READS THE CHART ALOUD.]

DENI: Clause one: no occupant shall remain beyond their allotted hour.

PUCK: The plant cannot read clocks.

SOLA: I can move it when its hour is over.

FARA: You moved it during its light.

SOLA: Then plants get the first hour.

PUCK: The fruit will spoil if it waits until noon.

DENI: We will assign a fruit monitor.

PUCK: I volunteer.

FARA: You ate the monitor's badge.

[PUCK REMOVES A PAPER LABEL FROM HIS MOUTH.]

PUCK: It was attached to the bowl.

DENI: We also need a dispute process.

SOLA: If there is a dispute, ask the plant.

FARA: The plant is not a mediator.

[THEY ALL TURN TO THE POT. A LEAF DROOPS TOWARD THE SUN.]

PUCK: It has made a motion.

[THE FLATMATES TRY TO MEASURE THE LEDGE. DENI HOLDS A RULER WHILE SOLA MOVES EACH POT.]

DENI: The largest pot takes half the ledge.

FARA: It takes the sunny half.

PUCK: The fruit bowl takes the corner.

SOLA: There is no room for a person.

PUCK: I can perch.

[PUCK SITS ON THE WINDOW SILL. THE SILL CREAKS.]

FARA: The lease says not to sit on the outside ledge.

PUCK: I am on the inside.

SOLA: We should check the lease.

[DENI FINDS THE LEASE UNDER THE PLANT TRAY. A RING OF WATER HAS BLURRED THE PAGE.]

DENI: It says "window area is shared."

FARA: That settles nothing.

PUCK: It settles that the lease is also living here.

[THEY SHIFT THE TRAY. A SMALL SHOOT IS GROWING THROUGH A DRAIN HOLE.]

SOLA: The plant has a roommate.

FARA: It needs another pot.

DENI: We can put the shoot on the table until we find one.

PUCK: It will have no sun.

SOLA: Then we can all use the ledge for five minutes while it gets a pot.

[THE FOUR FLATMATES STAND SHOULDER TO SHOULDER IN THE LIGHT. THEY BLOCK THE SUN FROM THE PLANTS.]

FARA: This is not helping.

PUCK: The plants have asked for privacy.

[EVERYONE STEPS BACK AT ONCE AND BUMPS INTO THE FRUIT TABLE.]

[THE FRUIT TABLE WOBBLES. PUCK CATCHES IT, THEN MOVES THE BOWL TO THE FLOOR.]

PUCK: The table has its own territory.

DENI: And the plant has the sunny ledge.

FARA: I can move the shoot to the spare pot.

SOLA: We have a spare pot?

[FARA PULLS A SMALL POT FROM UNDER THE SOFA. A SOCK IS GROWING IN IT.]

FARA: We have a spare sock.

### Act Three

[THE FLATMATES REMOVE THE CHART AND PLACE A SMALL WOODEN TRAY ON THE LEDGE.]

SOLA: Plants can sit at the back. Fruit goes on the tray. People can use the sill when nothing is in danger of falling.

DENI: No schedule?

FARA: We can look at the sun and decide.

PUCK: I will look at the fruit.

[PUCK PICKS UP A FIG, THEN SETS IT ON THE TRAY. THE TRAY BENDS.]

DENI: It is not strong enough for fruit.

SOLA: Then fruit stays on the table.

PUCK: The table has no sun.

FARA: That may help the fruit last longer.

[THEY MOVE THE FRUIT BOWL DOWN. THE POT, NOW UNOBSTRUCTED, RECEIVES A FULL STRIPE OF LIGHT.]

DENI: The plant has the ledge.

PUCK: The plant has the charter.

[THE POT'S LEAF BRUSHES PUCK'S NOSE AS HE LEANS IN.]

PUCK: It has also claimed my seat.

SOLA: You can file an appeal.

[PUCK SIGNS THE CHART WITH A STICKY FIG SEED AND TACKS IT TO THE FRIDGE.]
