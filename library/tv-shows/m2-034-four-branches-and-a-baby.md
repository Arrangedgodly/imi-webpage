# Four Branches and a Baby

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: multi-camera family sitcoms, mistaken deliveries, and sentimental act breaks*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: The residents of a crowded communal tree must care for a visiting macaque infant after a mix-up at the nursery, while each adult insists they are the only one who knows how to hold a baby.

Cast: Dada, an elder orangutan; Miri, a young macaque; Obo, a gibbon musician; Kess, a capuchin cook; Tavi, a baboon botanist; and infant Nori, a macaque.

## Episode: "The Basket on the Landing"

### Cold open

INT. COMMUNAL TREE, MORNING

[A wicker basket sits on the landing. A tiny tail sticks through a blanket. The residents gather around it.]

MIRI: That is my basket.

KESS: Your basket is breathing.

MIRI: I borrowed it from the nursery.

OBO: The nursery borrowed it from me.

TAVI: The nursery borrowed a baby?

DADA: No one borrowed a baby.

[The blanket moves. Everyone steps back at once.]

KESS: I have prepared mashed fruit.

MIRI: Did you prepare a spoon?

KESS: I prepared fruit.

[The baby squeaks. The residents lean in.]

OBO: We should call the nursery.

DADA: We will. First, find out whose baby this is.

MIRI: How?

DADA: Ask it.

### Act One

INT. COMMUNAL TREE, KITCHEN

[The group sits around the basket. Nori sleeps. Kess brings a bowl of fruit puree.]

TAVI: I checked the nursery ledger. The last name is smudged.

MIRI: It begins with "No".

OBO: Nori?

DADA: It says "Nori," not "no."

KESS: I thought it said "no fruit."

MIRI: That would be a very unusual nursery rule.

TAVI: The baby has a yellow ribbon.

OBO: So does my music case.

KESS: Why does your music case have a baby ribbon?

OBO: It got tangled during the spring concert.

DADA: The ribbon belongs to the basket. It doesn't tell us who owns the baby.

MIRI: I can hold Nori.

KESS: I can feed Nori.

TAVI: I can identify Nori's species.

MIRI: We know the species.

TAVI: I can identify it with confidence.

[They all reach for the basket, then stop.]

DADA: One person at a time.

KESS: I am holding the puree.

OBO: I am holding the blanket.

TAVI: I am holding the ledger.

MIRI: I am holding my arms out.

DADA: Then you are the only one ready.

[Miri lifts Nori. Nori immediately grips her fur.]

MIRI: That is a strong hand.

DADA: The baby has made a choice.

KESS: The baby chose Miri's fur.

OBO: That's a family resemblance.

### Act Two

INT. COMMUNAL TREE, MUSIC NOOK

[Obo rocks the basket while Miri checks the nursery. The basket begins to sway like a small boat.]

OBO: I am using a gentle rhythm.

KESS: You are playing a drum solo.

OBO: The baby likes it.

TAVI: The basket is moving.

OBO: That is the rhythm.

TAVI: The basket is moving toward the stairs.

[Obo catches it with his foot. Nori laughs.]

OBO: Nori laughed.

KESS: Nori laughed at your foot.

OBO: My foot has stage presence.

[Miri returns with a nursery worker's note.]

MIRI: The baby belongs to the small troop on the east branch.

DADA: Does the note say when they'll arrive?

MIRI: It says "after morning snack."

KESS: What time is that?

TAVI: When the snack is over.

KESS: The snack is not over.

DADA: We can keep Nori until then.

MIRI: I think Nori should have a nap.

OBO: The music nook is quiet.

KESS: Your drum is still there.

OBO: I will move the drum.

[He carries the drum away. A second drum rolls after him.]

TAVI: That is the drum's baby.

OBO: It followed me.

DADA: Leave the drums with the family.

### Act Three

INT. COMMUNAL TREE, LANDING

[An adult macaque, LENA, arrives carrying an empty basket. Nori reaches toward her. Miri keeps holding the baby.]

LENA: There you are, little one.

MIRI: You are Nori's parent?

LENA: Aunt. The nursery asked me to pick Nori up.

DADA: We found the note.

LENA: I wrote it. The ink ran when the basket got wet.

TAVI: The baby arrived safely.

LENA: And very well fed?

KESS: I prepared fruit puree.

LENA: Nori can't eat that yet.

KESS: I prepared it for the adults.

OBO: I played the lullaby.

LENA: Nori hates music.

[Obo slowly lowers the drum.]

OBO: Nori laughed at my foot.

LENA: Nori laughs at feet.

MIRI: I don't want to hand Nori over yet.

DADA: You can walk with them to the east branch.

MIRI: Could I visit tomorrow?

LENA: Of course. Nori likes meeting new relatives.

MIRI: I am not a relative.

DADA: You held the baby. That is how it starts.

KESS: Should I pack the puree?

LENA: Pack a snack for yourself.

TAVI: I will bring the ledger.

LENA: No need.

TAVI: It has a useful page about the nursery.

DADA: Leave the ledger here.

[They all prepare to leave. Obo lifts the basket.]

OBO: I can carry the baby.

MIRI: I have Nori.

OBO: I can carry the blanket.

KESS: I have the blanket.

TAVI: I have the note.

DADA: And I have the door.

### Tag

EXT. EAST BRANCH, AFTERNOON

[Miri, Dada, Kess, Tavi, and Obo return home. The empty basket is tied to Obo's back.]

KESS: Why are you carrying the basket?

OBO: The basket laughed at my foot.

[The others look at the basket. A small squeak comes from inside: Nori's toy.]

[The empty basket rocks gently on Obo's back.]
