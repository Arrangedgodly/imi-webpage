# The Family Challenge of Carrying Water

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: family competition shows, where arbitrary scoring makes an ordinary garden task more difficult than it needs to be*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A family competes to water a row of seedlings, but the host keeps changing scoring rules to manufacture suspense.

Cast: Host Miri, a gibbon; parent Kito, a macaque; cousins Pava and Odo, squirrel monkeys; scorekeeper Seli, a lemur; and the camera crew.

Episode: "Keep It in the Bowl"

COLD OPEN

EXT. GARDEN COURSE, DAY

Miri displays a cracked wooden bowl. Odo tilts it; water leaks through the seam and trickles into a microphone cable.

MIRI: Today, one family will conquer the garden.

PAVA: The garden is six seedlings.

MIRI: Six seedlings with a dramatic past.

The bowl empties. Miri looks at camera.

MIRI: And our first twist is already wet.

ACT ONE

EXT. START LINE, MOMENTS LATER

Seli lays out three legal containers: a folded leaf cup, a hollow reed, and a gourd with a fitted cap. Each contestant gets a measured ladle of water. Miri explains that the winner delivers the most water before the sunset bell. Seli holds up the rule card to the lens.

KITO: At least the plants have a clear goal.

MIRI: The goal is clear. The journey has three surprise rounds.

Odo grabs the leaf cup and sprints. The cup folds in his hand; half the water splashes on his feet. Miri awards him a "commitment splash point." Seli checks the card. No such point appears.

SELI: I can score what happened. I can't score what you just invented.

Miri moves the finish flag two steps down the path so the camera can see the seedlings behind it. Kito stops at the new line.

KITO: The finish moved after we started.

MIRI: The course is evolving.

SELI: A course can evolve only if we announce its new habitat.

Pava tests the reed. A thin stream reaches the first seedling, then leaks from a seam. Kito patches the seam with plant fiber. Seli checks the rules; repairs are allowed. Miri asks the camera for a close-up of the tiny knot as if it were a championship trophy.

ACT TWO

EXT. GARDEN COURSE, AFTERNOON

The relay round begins. The family must pass one container between markers. Pava chooses the gourd because it holds more water. Miri reveals a new rule: the container must remain visible to the camera throughout the relay.

Odo swings the gourd around his shoulder like a prize. Its cap pops loose. Seli catches the cap before it rolls into the seedlings, while Kito catches the gourd. A little water spills.

MIRI: A costly cap error!

SELI: No points for a cap error. It was equipment.

MIRI: Then I award the cap one point for bravery.

Pava asks whether she can carry the gourd with both hands. Miri says yes, but the camera operator can only follow at walking pace. Odo races ahead, then has to return because the microphone cable stops him at the marker. Miri calls this a dramatic reversal. Seli says it is a cable.

Kito suggests they pass the gourd hand to hand, keeping the cap secure. Miri says the method lacks suspense. She requires each contestant to announce a personal strategy before passing it.

PAVA: My strategy is to give the water to the plants.

ODO: Mine is to win enough points that the plants will respect me.

KITO: Mine is to get home before this becomes a reunion special.

The relay proceeds slowly. Each monkey states a different strategy, but the water stays in the capped gourd. Miri turns to camera and whispers that a score change is coming. Seli hears her.

SELI: The rules are printed on the card. You can't invent a bonus at the finish.

MIRI: I can if the bonus is emotionally earned.

SELI: The plants are not judging you.

MIRI: We haven't asked them.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. SEEDLING BED, LATE AFTERNOON

The family reaches the garden. Seli measures the water left in the gourd and the amounts delivered through the reed and leaf cup. Pava has delivered the most. Odo has the best sprint time. Kito kept the gourd from falling when its cap came loose.

MIRI: We're tied.

SELI: We are not. Pava wins by the measure you announced.

Miri lowers her voice and asks Seli to add the splash point. Seli points to the rule card, still damp but readable. The word "most" is circled; no splash category is printed. Miri tries to persuade Pava to accept a sudden bonus round. Pava asks the host to carry the leaking bowl through the course herself.

MIRI: As a contestant?

PAVA: As a person with water.

The camera operator hands Miri the cracked bowl from the prop tray. It leaks immediately. Seli announces that the family must choose: wait for a new container and miss the sunset bell, or use the actual measured water now. They choose the gourd.

ACT THREE

EXT. SEEDLING BED, SUNSET

The family pours the gourd over the four seedlings Pava has not already watered. Seli records Pava's individual win and the family's completed garden challenge. Miri looks at the three separate names on the scorecard, unable to frame one triumphant close-up.

Miri looks into camera.

MIRI: An emotional victory, achieved without a twist.

SELI: You can still do the tag.

Miri hands Odo the empty gourd. He raises it triumphantly; the loose cap drops into the bowl with a plunk.

ODO: The cap won.

MIRI: I knew it had a story.

TAG

The crew packs up. The finish flag rolls down the slope. Miri runs after it with the rule card. Seli calls that there is no bonus for chasing a flag.

MIRI: Not yet.

[SFX: FLAGPOLE TAPS THE GROUND. THEME MUSIC.]
