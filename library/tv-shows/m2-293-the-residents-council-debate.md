# The Residents' Council Debate

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: political debate television, where candidates debate who should repair a communal rain barrel*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Three candidates debate how to repair a leaking rain barrel, but their proposals all depend on someone else carrying the water.

Cast: Moderator Seli, capuchin; candidate Odo, baboon; candidate Nara, macaque; candidate Piri, gibbon; clerk Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Who Will Carry the Barrel?"

### Cold Open

[RAIN BARREL. WATER DRIPS FROM A SEAM.]

SELI: The council must decide how to repair it.

ODO: I propose a committee.

NARA: I propose a patch.

PIRI: I propose moving the barrel.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CANDIDATES STAND AT PODIUMS.]

Seli explains the rules: opening statement, rebuttal, and one audience question. Odo says the barrel should be inspected by a committee. Nara says the seam can be patched. Piri says the barrel should be moved to flatter ground.

TAVI: The ground is already flat.

PIRI: It looks flat.

NARA: The barrel tilts because the stand is uneven.

ODO: A committee can assess the stand.

SELI: We need a proposal today.

Odo asks how the repair will be funded. Tavi says there is a roll of patch tape in the shed.

ODO: We must approve the tape.

NARA: The tape is already approved.

PIRI: Then we need to decide who applies it.

### Act Two

[THE AUDIENCE QUESTION IS READ.]

A resident asks who will carry water while the barrel is being repaired. Odo says a temporary bucket can be used. Nara says she can carry water from the stream. Piri says the stream is farther from the garden than the well.

SELI: How many trips?

PIRI: Six.

ODO: We should convene a working group.

NARA: We should patch the barrel before it empties.

Tavi checks the seam. The leak is a small crack near the bottom. Nara says the barrel must be empty before it can be dried and patched.

ODO: That changes the plan.

PIRI: The barrel is nearly empty already.

TAVI: It has enough water for two buckets.

Seli asks the candidates to explain how they will use the remaining water. They all say the garden should keep it.

[THE CANDIDATES ARE ASKED ABOUT RESPONSIBILITY.]

Seli asks who will empty the barrel before the patch is applied. Odo says the committee can assign the work. Nara says she will do it if the others hold the tub. Piri says the tub is too small.

TAVI: There is a second tub in the shed.

ODO: That requires approval.

NARA: The shed key is on the hook.

ODO: We need an access procedure.

PIRI: The procedure is to take the key.

Tavi retrieves the second tub. Odo proposes a rotation schedule for carrying water. Nara asks how many households use the barrel. Piri says four. Seli asks how many buckets each needs.

TAVI: Two each.

ODO: Eight trips.

NARA: We have four people.

PIRI: Two trips apiece.

Odo asks whether candidates should carry the water during the debate. Seli says the audience may want a demonstration. They lift the tub together; it is heavier than expected.

### Act Three

[THE CANDIDATES MOVE THE WATER TO A TEMPORARY TUB.]

Nara and Piri carry two buckets to the tub. Odo steadies the barrel while Tavi tilts it. The water is moved without spilling. They dry the seam and apply the patch.

SELI: The debate has become a repair.

ODO: The proposal is in progress.

PIRI: The committee is here.

NARA: The patch is holding.

The candidates refill the barrel with water from the tub. The leak stops. Odo says the council should approve a maintenance schedule. Nara agrees, as long as someone is assigned to check the stand.

PIRI: I will check it after rain.

TAVI: I can mark the level.

[THE CLERK WRITES THE AGREEMENT.]

Odo signs that the committee will check the stand next week. Nara signs that she will inspect the patch after the next rain. Piri offers to keep the spare tub beside the barrel.

TAVI: The key goes back on the hook.

ODO: The procedure is complete.

SELI: The water is available again.

[THE CANDIDATES RETURN TO THE PODIUMS.]

Seli asks each candidate for a closing statement. Odo says the committee will meet next month. Nara says the patch should be checked after rain. Piri says the spare tub will stay near the barrel.

SELI: The residents will know who is responsible.

TAVI: The agreement is written down.

ODO: The debate has a result.

NARA: The barrel is still a barrel.

PIRI: It is a working barrel.

### Tag

[THE BARREL IS FULL.]

Seli asks who won the debate. Tavi says the barrel did.

ODO: The council still needs a decision.

NARA: The decision is to keep checking the seam.

PIRI: And the stand.

[SFX: WATER DRIPS ONCE, THEN STOPS.]
