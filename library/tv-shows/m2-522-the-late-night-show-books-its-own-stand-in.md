# The Late-Night Show Books Its Own Stand-In

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night interviews that keep a stand-in in the chair after the guest arrives*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Omi, a gibbon; guest Rena, an orangutan; stand-in Luko, a capuchin; producer Pali, a macaque; bandleader Savi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. LATE-NIGHT SET - NIGHT

Omi welcomes guest Rena. The camera shows Luko sitting in the guest chair.

OMI: We are delighted to have you here.

RENA: I am standing behind the curtain.

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Pali says the guest camera is still on the rehearsal stand-in. Luko was asked to test the chair and has not left. Rena enters from the stage door. The camera stays on Luko. He waves. The audience applauds him by mistake.

LUKO: I can leave if you want.

Omi asks him to stay until the camera is fixed. Rena sits on the couch beside the host. Pali asks Savi to play the guest entrance music again. The band begins the show's theme. Omi and Rena wait through the full tune.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The director checks the camera labels. The monitor marked GUEST points to the empty chair; the one marked STAND-IN shows Rena. Pali switches them. The guest camera now shows Luko's reflection in a framed poster. He has moved to the audience seats, where he is holding a cue card.

[Omi asks Rena about her new book. The camera cuts to Luko, who silently mouths the title from the card. Rena pauses, waiting for the question. Omi asks it again. Luko mouths it again.]

Savi stops the band from playing a transition sting. Pali tells the camera operator to follow the person speaking, not the chair. The operator pans to Rena. She answers the question. The camera cuts to Luko when he laughs. Omi asks if he has read the book. Luko shakes his head and leaves the frame.

INT. SET - MOMENTS LATER

Rena tells a story about signing books at a station. Luko returns carrying a paper cup and sits in the stand-in chair. The camera finds him. Pali waves him away. Rena says the chair might be comfortable. Omi asks the floor manager to remove it. The chair is bolted to the stage for the reveal shot. They leave it empty.

INT. GREEN ROOM - LATER

Pali asks Luko to leave the guest chair empty until Rena arrives. Luko sits in the audience and hands the cue card to Savi. The band rehearses the guest sting once. Omi asks them to stop before the interview begins. Rena walks in through the correct door. The camera operator follows her to the couch. Pali checks the monitor: one guest, one frame. Luko remains visible in the audience, quietly reading the book jacket. Savi plays no music while the interview is underway.

The rehearsal camera still labels the spare chair as GUEST. Pali places a small tag on it reading STAND-IN. Omi asks whether the tag should be in the shot. Rena says it can stay. Luko returns the cue card to the desk and takes his seat. When Omi asks the first question, the camera stays on Rena. She finishes her answer before the host moves to the next topic. The show has no mistaken introduction left to correct. The director cues the band only for the opening and goodbye. Pali checks both camera labels against the actual seats, then lets Rena finish the final answer without switching shots. Luko returns the empty chair to storage. Savi checks that the camera is following the guest rather than the spare seat. The bandleader closes the cue book.

ACT THREE

INT. LATE-NIGHT SET - NIGHT

The interview continues with Rena on the couch. Omi asks about the book's main character. Rena answers directly to the host. The camera remains on her. Luko watches from the audience, holding the cue card upside down. Savi plays a soft underscore. Pali signals him to stop; Rena's answer is already funny without music.

Omi introduces the book cover. The camera shows the correct title. Luko raises his hand from the audience. Omi asks if he has a question. Luko asks whether the stand-in chair can be used in another segment. Pali says the prop department needs it. The camera operator keeps the frame on Rena while the chair is rolled away behind them.

TAG

INT. SET - AFTER THE INTERVIEW

Luko returns the cue card to the desk. Pali relabels the camera monitors with colored tape. Omi thanks Rena by name as she exits.

FINAL IMAGE: The guest chair is empty, the camera points at the correct guest, and the stand-in sits safely in the audience.
