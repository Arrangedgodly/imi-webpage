# The Puppet Show Loses the Cue Cards

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's television that relies on scripted call-and-response cues even when the puppets derail the timing*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Mina, a macaque; puppet Pipkin, a capuchin; puppet Grub, a gibbon; stage manager Lala, an orangutan; caller Jojo, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. CHILDREN'S STUDIO - DAY

Mina holds a card that reads ASK WHERE THE STAR WENT. The puppet stage is empty.

MINA: Where did Pipkin go?

[From under the desk, Pipkin answers.]

PIPKIN: I am right here.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Lala whispers that the puppets are supposed to appear through the blue curtain. Pipkin has gone behind the set to retrieve a dropped button. Grub enters through the wrong opening and asks whether they are on air. Mina smiles and says they are.

MINA: Can you help us find the star?

GRUB: Which one?

Mina looks at the cue card. It says STAR. Grub points to the star painted on the set, then the star on Mina's shirt. Pipkin returns holding the missing button. Lala asks Mina to continue the scripted question. Mina asks where the button went.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Lala rewrites the cards with larger lettering. The new card says ASK PIPKIN ABOUT THE BUTTON. Mina reads it aloud before Pipkin appears. He enters holding a small tin and says he has found three buttons. None matches the one from his jacket. Grub asks the children watching to count them. The tally graphic shows four.

MINA: We have three buttons.

GRUB: The screen says four.

The fourth button is printed on the cue card. Lala hides the card behind the desk. The tally drops to three. Mina asks viewers to help pick the matching button. Pipkin tries each one against his jacket. All three are too large. He checks the sleeve and finds the original button still attached on the other side.

PIPKIN: I found it before we started.

The stage manager holds up a note that says KEEP THE MYSTERY. Mina tells the audience the search is over. Grub says they have not counted the buttons again. Mina counts to three. The tally graphic changes to five, showing the stars in the painted sky.

INT. PUPPET STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Lala has removed the counting graphic. Jojo calls in to say their younger sibling likes the song. Mina asks which song. Jojo hums the theme. Grub begins singing the closing verse too early. Pipkin joins in at the wrong note. The band follows them, leaving Mina and the host microphone behind.

INT. PUPPET STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Mina opens the button tin and lets Pipkin choose a spare for the jacket. He selects one the same color. Grub counts the stitches with the audience, but starts at zero and ends at four. Mina shows the card with three visible stitches. Lala removes the tally monitor from the set; it had continued adding numbers whenever Grub moved his hands. Mina asks Grub to keep still while Pipkin threads the needle. Grub holds both hands against the puppet stage. The host reads the repair steps from memory, and the studio monitor stays blank. Pipkin ties the thread with a small knot and shows Mina the sewn button. She checks it once, then folds the jacket sleeve down. Grub lets go of the cue cards; Lala catches them before they reach the floor. The puppets agree to finish the repair before singing.

ACT THREE

INT. CHILDREN'S STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Mina sets aside the cue cards and asks Pipkin and Grub what they want to show. Pipkin demonstrates how to sew on a button. Grub tries to help by holding the thread, but keeps turning the jacket inside out. Mina asks the viewers to count the stitches. The tally graphic remains off.

Pipkin passes the needle to Mina with the cap on. She threads it once and sews the button in place. Grub suggests they test it with a tug. It holds. He tugs again; Mina stops him before the jacket stretches.

MINA: One test is enough.

Jojo calls back to say they counted three stars and one button. The studio audience claps. Lala raises a card that says ASK FOR A WAVE. Mina asks the puppets to wave goodbye. Pipkin waves with the newly repaired sleeve. Grub waves with both hands, then realizes he is holding the host's cue cards. They flutter down across the puppet stage.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Lala gathers the cards. The top one reads WHERE DID THE STAR GO? Pipkin places it beside the painted star on the set, then looks beneath the desk to make sure it has not moved.

FINAL IMAGE: Mina waves beside the blue curtain as Pipkin and Grub wave from the wrong openings, all three in frame.
