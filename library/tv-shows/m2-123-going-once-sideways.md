# Going Once, Going Sideways

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live auction programs that turn ordinary lots into suspenseful bidding wars and keep raising the stakes after the sale*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Auction host Della wants a thrilling bidding segment for a box of retired park signs. Clerk Piko needs the correct signs returned to the parks before morning. The audience bids on lots the city intended to recycle, forcing the crew to repair its own catalog.

Cast: Della, a macaque auctioneer; Piko, a lemur clerk; Rumo, a gorilla parks worker; and Tavi, a capuchin bidder.

## Episode: "The Sign Goes to the Highest Bidder"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A BOX OF OLD PARK SIGNS SITS ON A PEDESTAL. DELLA HOLDS UP A SIGN READING "PATH CLOSED."]

DELLA: Who will give this sign a second life?

TAVI: Is the path closed?

RUMO: The path is open. That sign is outdated.

[DELLA TURNS THE SIGN AROUND. THE BACK READS "OPEN."]

DELLA: A two-sided opportunity.

PIKO: We have to return the signs to the correct park.

DELLA: After the bidding.

[THE AUCTION BELL RINGS. THE SIGN FALLS OFF THE PEDESTAL.]

DELLA: It is a dramatic lot.

RUMO: It needs a new screw.

[PIKO SETS THE SIGN UPRIGHT AND WRITES "CHECK MOUNT" ON THE INVENTORY CARD.]

### Act One

[DELLA STANDS BESIDE THE BOX. PIKO READS THE LOT LIST.]

PIKO: Lot one, three trail markers from North Grove. Lot two, two wetland signs. Lot three, one blank board.

DELLA: A blank board is a canvas for your future.

RUMO: It is a board for a missing sign.

[TAVI RAISES HIS NUMBER.]

DELLA: We have an opening bid of five.

TAVI: Five for the board?

DELLA: Five for possibility.

[PIKO CHECKS THE INVENTORY. THE BOARD HAS A MOUNTING HOLE AND A FADED ARROW.]

PIKO: It was a direction sign.

DELLA: Possibility with an arrow.

[TAVI BIDS SIX. A SECOND BIDDER RAISES A PADDLE FOR SEVEN. DELLA BUILDS SUSPENSE.]

DELLA: Do I hear eight? Eight for the board that once guided a generation?

RUMO: It guided people to a picnic shelter.

[THE BELL RINGS. THE LOT SELLS FOR SEVEN.]

PIKO: That bidder works for the parks department.

DELLA: They will treasure it.

### Act Two

[THE WINNER APPROACHES THE TABLE. RUMO CHECKS THE SIGN'S BACK.]

RUMO: This one belongs at the east entrance.

PIKO: The lot was labeled North Grove.

DELLA: Then the sale is an adventure.

[TAVI RAISES HIS PADDLE AGAIN.]

TAVI: I want to bid on the adventure.

PIKO: The signs are not supposed to be sold.

DELLA: We announced the auction.

RUMO: The parks department marked them for recycling, not resale.

[PIKO OPENS THE ORIGINAL DELIVERY LIST. A SPLASH OF INK COVERS THE PARK NAMES.]

PIKO: The labels ran in the rain.

DELLA: That gives each lot an air of mystery.

TAVI: I bid one coin to learn where it goes.

DELLA: Do I hear two?

PIKO: You cannot bid on information that belongs on the label.

[TAVI LOWERS HIS PADDLE. DELLA TURNS THE CAMERA TOWARD THE MAP.]

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. DELLA STARTS A BID. RUMO ROLLS OUT A MAP OF THE PARKS.]

RUMO: The mounting holes match the posts. North Grove has square bolts. The wetland path has round bolts.

[PIKO CHECKS THE SIGNS. THE GROUP SORTS THEM BY BOLT HOLES. DELLA TRIES TO KEEP THE AUCTION MOVING.]

DELLA: Can we bid on the map?

RUMO: It belongs to the city.

DELLA: The map is not for sale.

TAVI: Then I will stop bidding.

### Act Three

[THE CREW RE-READS THE LOT LIST. DELLA STRIKES THE HAMMER.]

PIKO: Should I list the final sale price?

DELLA: There was no sale.

PIKO: Then I will list the final return count.

RUMO: All the signs are accounted for.

[DELLA LOOKS AT THE PEDESTAL. IT IS EMPTY.]

DELLA: The auction is closed.

PIKO: Nothing has been sold.

DELLA: We sold a lesson in reading labels.

RUMO: We can put the signs back before dark.

[TAVI CARRIES A STACK. DELLA CARRIES THE MAP. PIKO MARKS EACH SIGN IN THE DELIVERY LOG. ONE SIGN STILL HAS NO MATCH.]

TAVI: I thought auctions ended with a winner.

DELLA: We have a winner. The city gets its signs back.

RUMO: The city did not bid.

DELLA: It won by default.

DELLA: This last one says "Lookout."

RUMO: The old lookout was removed.

PIKO: The sign can go to recycling.

[DELLA LOOKS AT THE AUDIENCE.]

DELLA: Any bids on responsible disposal?

[NO PADDLES RISE. RUMO PUTS THE SIGN IN THE RECYCLE BIN.]

### Tag

[DELLA CLOSES THE SHOW. TAVI HOLDS UP HIS BIDDER PADDLE.]

TAVI: Can I bid on the paddle?

PIKO: It belongs to the station.

DELLA: Going once?

[PIKO TAKES THE PADDLE BACK.]

PIKO: Sold to the equipment cupboard.
