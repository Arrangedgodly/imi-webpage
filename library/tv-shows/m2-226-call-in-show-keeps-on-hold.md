# The Call-In Show Keeps You on Hold

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live call-in programs that promote audience participation but keep callers waiting through the host's monologue*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A community advice show invites callers to ask one question, but the producer keeps putting them on hold to fit the sponsor reads.

Cast: Host Vela, a macaque; producer Tavi, a gibbon; caller Ralo, a capuchin; caller Miri, a baboon; caller Nema, a squirrel monkey; and floor runner Odo, a lemur.

Episode: "You're Live After This"

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - MORNING

Vela smiles at the camera. Two phone lights blink. Tavi raises a finger to indicate the sponsor slot.

VELA: Call us with your question. We have time for everyone.

TAVI: We have time for one caller after the sponsor.

VELA: We have two callers.

TAVI: One caller and one sponsor.

[THE PHONE RINGS AGAIN. ODO POINTS TO THE LIGHT.]

ACT ONE

Vela answers the first call. Ralo's voice comes through a speaker.

RALO (ON PHONE): My neighbor has a ladder in my yard. Should I ask him to move it?

VELA: Yes. You can ask politely.

TAVI: That's a short answer.

RALO: I can also just carry it back. It's empty.

VELA: That sounds reasonable.

TAVI: We need more tension. Ask whether the ladder is part of a boundary dispute.

RALO: It's a ladder.

[TAVI PLAYS A DRAMATIC MUSIC STING.]

VELA: We will return to Ralo after the break.

RALO: I am still here.

ACT TWO

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - LATER

The second caller, Miri, is on hold. Vela reads a card. Odo signals that the line is clear.

VELA: Miri, you're on the air. What is your question?

MIRI (ON PHONE): I wanted to ask how to keep my porch mat from sliding.

TAVI: Can we frame that as a safety concern?

MIRI: It slides when I step on it.

VELA: You could use a mat with a grip backing.

TAVI: That answer is also short.

MIRI: It is a short mat.

[TAVI TURNS ON THE HOLD MUSIC. IT DROWNS OUT MIRI'S VOICE, BUT HER MICROPHONE STAYS LIVE.]

VELA: Miri, are you still there?

MIRI: I can hear the music.

TAVI: The caller is listening to the sponsor message.

MIRI: I am listening to a flute.

ACT TWO-B

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Vela puts both callers on speaker. Ralo and Miri answer at once.

RALO: I moved the ladder.

MIRI: I put a rubber pad under the mat.

VELA: Both problems are solved.

TAVI: The lines are crossing. We can make that a debate segment.

RALO: We are not discussing the same problem.

MIRI: My mat is fixed.

[ODO SWITCHES OFF THE HOLD MUSIC. THE TWO CALLERS CAN HEAR EACH OTHER CLEARLY.]

MIRI: Did you need the ladder?

RALO: No. My neighbor asked me to keep it out of the rain.

VELA: The callers have helped each other.

TAVI: We can call that a listener panel.

VELA: We can call it two people talking.

ACT TWO-C

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - LATE AFTERNOON

Tavi checks the phone console. Vela watches the caller lights blink in sequence.

TAVI: We have one caller who has been waiting since the first segment.

VELA: Put them on.

NEMA (ON PHONE): I heard the whole show while I was on hold.

TAVI: That is the program.

NEMA: I thought I was listening to hold music.

VELA: What was your question?

NEMA: How do I get the show to stop putting me on hold?

TAVI: You could hang up.

NEMA: Then I would not be on the air.

[THE CALLER LIGHT GOES DARK.]

VELA: Did they hang up?

ODO: No. The line was transferred to the studio speaker.

[NEMA'S MICROPHONE IS LIVE THROUGH A MONITOR ON THE OTHER SIDE OF THE SET. HER RETURN FEED STILL CARRIES THE HOLD FLUTE.]

NEMA: I can hear you and the flute together.

TAVI: We have a routing issue.

VELA: We can let the caller finish.

NEMA: I was going to say the hold music is too loud.

ODO: I can lower it.

[ODO TURNS DOWN THE MUSIC. THE PHONE LIGHT STOPS BLINKING.]

VELA: We have answered the loud part of the question.

ACT THREE

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - FINAL CALL

The phone lights blink again. Vela looks at Tavi. He tries to point at the sponsor card, then at the callers.

VELA: We have time for one more question.

ODO: The caller is ready.

NEMA (ON PHONE): How do I get off hold?

TAVI: We can put them through after the message.

VELA: No. You're live now. Press the button marked "OFF HOLD."

[ODO SWITCHES NEMA'S RETURN FEED FROM THE HOLD CHANNEL TO THE LIVE PROGRAM. THE HOLD MUSIC STOPS.]

NEMA: Thank you.

TAG

The phone rings again. Tavi points at the sponsor card. Vela points to the caller light.

VELA: We have time for both.

[TAVI TAKES A DEEP BREATH AND ANSWERS THE SPONSOR LINE HIMSELF.]

VELA: Another caller is still waiting.
