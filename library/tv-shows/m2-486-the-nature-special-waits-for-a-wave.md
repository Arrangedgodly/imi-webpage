# The Nature Special Waits for a Wave

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: wildlife documentaries that narrate ordinary animal behavior as a grand, mysterious ordeal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: narrator Sumi, a lemur; field host Boro, a macaque; camera operator Yani, an orangutan; keeper Fenn, a gibbon; capybara Momo.

COLD OPEN

EXT. CONSERVATION POND - DAWN

A camera points at Momo, who sits beside a pond and chews a reed. A dramatic score swells. Boro crouches behind a bush, wearing a headset and holding a clipboard.

SUMI (V.O.): At first light, the capybara approaches the water's edge.

[Momo stands and takes one step toward the pond.]

BORO: Cut. We need the approach from farther away.

ACT ONE

EXT. POND - MORNING

Yani rolls the camera back to a mark. Momo is still beside the water, chewing the same reed. Boro asks him to wait until Sumi's narration describes the mist. The mist has already lifted. Fenn opens a spray bottle to make a light veil; the breeze blows it onto the camera lens.

SUMI (V.O.): The animal senses a change in the air.

MOMO: The spray bottle is pointed at me.

Boro says the sound was unusable. Yani shows him the clean take: Momo heard the bottle, looked over, and went back to chewing. Sumi calls it a cautious withdrawal. Momo has not moved.

BORO: We need a choice. Water or reeds.

FENN: He chose reeds.

Boro asks for a second camera angle. Yani sets a long lens on the opposite bank. It frames a duck crossing behind Momo. The director marks the duck as a surprise visitor. Fenn points out it crosses every morning.

ACT TWO

INT. FIELD EDIT TENT - LATER

Sumi records narration beside the rough cut. Boro asks her to make Momo's stillness sound like suspense. She tries three versions. The first says he is waiting. The second says he is listening. The third describes the reed's nutritional value. Boro says none has a turning point.

SUMI: He has been eating breakfast for twelve minutes.

BORO: The audience expects a discovery.

Yani plays the moment Momo drops the reed, picks it up, and turns it around. Boro leans forward. Sumi records a line about a sudden change in feeding strategy. Fenn explains that Momo picked up the end he had not chewed. Boro keeps the line and adds a close-up of the reed as if it were evidence.

[The crew returns to the pond. Boro places a small marker flag beside the water to define the edge of the shot. Momo pulls it out and uses it to scratch his shoulder.]

SUMI (V.O.): The capybara claims the territory.

FENN: That is a camera flag.

Boro asks Yani to get a reaction shot. Yani pans to the empty patch where the flag was. Momo scratches, then drops the flag. The crew tries to replace it between takes; Momo removes it again each time. Sumi's narration calls each removal a deliberate signal. Fenn finally puts the flag away.

The assistant director asks for one more angle from the far bank. Yani moves the tripod, and the camera captures Boro crouching behind a bush with the clipboard over his head. Sumi records a line about a hidden observer. Boro hears it through his headset and slowly lowers the clipboard.

ACT THREE

EXT. POND - AFTERNOON

Boro announces one last take, with no props. The camera rolls. Momo chews a new reed, stands, and walks into the pond until the water reaches his belly. The crew stays silent. Sumi narrates the crossing in a reverent whisper. Momo turns around, walks back out, and sits in the same patch of shade.

BORO: That is the ending.

YANI: The camera was rolling the whole time.

Boro watches the footage. It shows the walk, the water, and the return in one unbroken shot. He asks Sumi for a closing line. She starts to describe a vast migration. Momo shakes water from his fur, splashing the sound blanket and Boro's clipboard. Sumi stops recording. The camera captures Boro trying to keep the clipboard dry while still holding his solemn pose.

BORO: Was that a behavior?

FENN: It was water.

Sumi records a new final line: Momo returns to his chosen resting place. Yani plays it against the shot. The line ends just as Momo sits down and resumes chewing. Boro asks for another take. Sumi switches off the narration microphone.

TAG

INT. EDIT TENT - EVENING

The finished segment plays. It uses the clean crossing, Momo's reed, and the splash. Boro watches the splash twice, then asks if the duck can get a title card. Fenn says it has already gone home.

FINAL IMAGE: Momo chews quietly on the monitor while the title card reads THE ORDINARY AFTERNOON, accompanied by a thunderous orchestral hit.
