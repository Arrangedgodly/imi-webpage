# The Weather Is Sponsored by a Towel

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local news television, where sponsor mentions compete with urgent weather updates*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A local station tries to report a sudden rainstorm while its sponsor insists the weather segment feature a new line of towels.

Cast: Anchor Seli, capuchin; meteorologist Nara, macaque; producer Odo, baboon; sponsor representative Piri, gibbon; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Breaking Rain"

### Cold Open

[NEWS DESK. RAIN HITS THE WINDOW.]

SELI: We have a storm moving across the village.

PIRI: Sponsored by SoftNest Towels.

NARA: It is a weather alert.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[NARA STANDS BESIDE A MAP.]

Nara explains that rain is approaching from the west and the path near the stream may flood. Odo asks her to keep the map visible. Piri places a folded towel beside the monitor.

PIRI: Our sponsor can absorb the situation.

SELI: We need viewers to know which path to avoid.

PIRI: The towel is available in four colors.

NARA: The west path is closed.

Piri unfolds the towel in front of the map. The weather symbols disappear.

ODO: The sponsor has covered the forecast.

PIRI: It is a demonstration.

Nara moves the towel. The map shows rain over the stream and clear ground at the hill.

### Act Two

[THE STUDIO PHONE RINGS.]

A caller asks if the lower bridge is safe. Nara says it is not. Piri asks whether a towel could mark the closure. Seli says a sign would be clearer.

PIRI: The towel is bright.

SELI: The towel is not a road sign.

Odo lays the towel across a chair to show its size. It slides to the floor. Tavi retrieves it and hangs it on the chair back.

NARA: We should not use equipment that can blow away.

PIRI: It is weighted at the corners.

A gust through the door lifts the towel and covers the camera lens.

TAVI: The forecast is no longer visible.

SELI: Neither is the sponsor.

Piri tries to fold it smaller. The camera catches the towel as a colorful square.

[THE PRODUCER INTERRUPTS WITH A NEW SPONSOR CARD.]

Odo holds up a card that says "Rain? No Problem." Piri says the station must read it before the alert. Nara says the bridge closure needs to be repeated now because the water has risen.

PIRI: We have already said the bridge is closed.

NARA: The water has risen since then.

SELI: The update takes priority.

Odo asks the sponsor to wait until after the warning. Piri agrees but offers to place the towel over the desk during the next segment.

TAVI: It will cover the microphone.

PIRI: We can fold it.

TAVI: It is a towel. It will still absorb sound.

Seli tests it. Her voice becomes muffled when the towel is near the mic.

SELI: The sponsor is suppressing the news.

NARA: Put it in the cabinet.

Piri folds it into the cabinet. The door will not close because it is too full of other sponsor samples. Odo removes a branded mug, making room.

ODO: We have a storage update.

[THE CREW RECHECKS THE WEATHER MAP.]

Nara updates the map with a red line showing the closed bridge and a blue line for the hill route. Seli repeats the warning. Piri stands outside the camera shot with the towel folded under one arm.

PIRI: Can I show the towel now?

SELI: After the warning.

PIRI: You have said the warning twice.

NARA: The stream is still rising.

Odo looks out the window. Rain has slowed, but water is flowing over the lower path. Piri puts the towel down and helps Tavi pin a printed closure notice to the board outside.

TAVI: This is a better use for the towel.

PIRI: The towel is not a sign.

SELI: The paper is.

They secure the notice and return inside. The sponsor card stays in the cabinet.

### Act Three

[NARA RETURNS TO THE WEATHER MAP.]

She clearly states that the lower path and bridge are closed until the rain passes. Odo puts a printed notice on the station's website and asks Tavi to display it at the entrance.

PIRI: Can we mention the towels after the alert?

SELI: Yes, after the update.

PIRI: SoftNest Towels absorb-

NARA: The stream is rising.

PIRI: Right. The stream is rising.

Seli repeats the closure information. Piri waits until the end, then holds up a towel beside the desk without covering the map.

PIRI: The towel is available in four colors.

ODO: It is also available after the storm.

NARA: We have a dry route along the hill.

### Tag

[STUDIO. THE RAIN STOPS.]

Piri folds the towel and puts it away. Seli faces camera.

SELI: The lower path remains closed.

PIRI: The towel remains dry.

TAVI: The camera is clear.

[SFX: WEATHER THEME, WINDOW DRIPS.]
