# The Platform Was Behind the Camera

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised rail travelogues, sweeping departure shots, and hosts who narrate the platform instead of catching the train*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A travel host attempts to film a dramatic train departure from the wrong platform while the station guide tries to keep the crew on schedule.

Cast: Host Vali, a gibbon; guide Neri, a macaque; cameraman Odo, a baboon; station keeper Piri, an orangutan; guest traveler Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. RAIL STATION - MORNING

Vali faces a train as it pulls away. Odo pans after it. Neri points behind the camera.

NERI: Our train is on platform two.

VALI: That was platform one.

PIRI: And that train left.

ACT ONE

Neri holds a route card showing the crew's departure from platform two. Odo's camera cable stretches toward platform one. Vali asks for a final shot of the station clock. Piri says the clock is above platform two. Odo rotates the camera; the train on platform two is already boarding.

VALI: We have one minute for the introduction.

NERI: We have one minute before the doors close.

Tavi, a traveler, enters with a small suitcase. He says he is on the same route. Vali asks him to stand beside the departure board for an interview. Neri checks the board; the platform number has changed from two to three. Piri confirms a late track adjustment.

ACT TWO

The group walks to platform three. Vali narrates the transfer as an unplanned discovery. Neri says it is a normal station announcement. Odo tries to film the train's front, but a cleaning cart blocks the view. Piri moves the cart off the yellow safety line. Odo zooms in on a station map and covers the route number with his hand.

VALI: We are learning to read the station.

TAVI: We could also listen to the announcement.

Piri points to the updated board. The train is due in two minutes. Neri asks Tavi if he has used this line before. Tavi says yes and knows the stop for the market. Vali asks him to say it again for the camera. He does, but Odo's microphone is pointed at the train speaker.

The station speaker announces the platform change again. Vali begins a voice-over about the journey, but Neri holds up the route card to the camera. It shows the same number as the board. Odo tilts the lens down and catches only the bottom half of the sign.

NERI: We need the platform number in frame.

ODO: I can widen the shot.

He steps back. The station clock, platform sign and train all fit. Vali asks Tavi to point out the market stop on the map. Tavi traces the line with one finger, then folds the map back along its crease so the route remains visible.

TAVI: This is the transfer we need.

VALI: We are no longer guessing.

Piri checks the train destination board against Tavi's map. They match. Neri asks Vali to wait until boarding is complete before recording the introduction.

VALI: I can introduce the route from the train.

NERI: That is where we are going.

ACT TWO-B

The station announces boarding. Odo asks Vali to hold still for a dramatic departure shot. Vali begins describing the route while Neri and Tavi board. Piri calls that the crew must board now. Vali finishes the sentence, turns, and discovers the camera cable is wound around the bench leg.

VALI: We are delayed by our own shot.

Odo unhooks the cable. The train doors remain open. Tavi reaches from inside and takes the tripod. Neri steadies the camera while Piri signals the conductor that the crew is boarding. Vali steps aboard last. The doors close only after all five are inside.

Neri opens the route card. The train is headed to the market, but the show planned to film the final shot before boarding. Vali asks if they can film from the window. Piri says the windows are too reflective. Odo tries a low angle; his reflection becomes the host.

ODO: I am now the travel presenter.

VALI: You can narrate the map.

ACT THREE

Tavi points out the market stop. The crew exits together and films the station sign from the platform. Vali gives the actual route and thanks the station staff for the transfer announcement. Neri keeps the camera cable coiled away from the boarding area. Piri checks the platform board for the return train.

VALI: We reached our destination after a change of platform.

NERI: And one missed train.

TAVI: The next train was the right one.

The crew interviews a fruit seller, then packs the equipment before the return announcement. Vali starts another sweeping introduction, but Odo has the camera on the route sign and the train is approaching.

TAG

Piri calls the platform number. The crew turns together and walks toward the right train without stopping for a shot.

FINAL IMAGE: The route card lies beside the camera monitor, which shows the station sign rather than the crew's reflection.
