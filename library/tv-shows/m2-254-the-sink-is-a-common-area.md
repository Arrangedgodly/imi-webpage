# The Sink Is a Common Area

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: roommate sitcoms, where a shared kitchen conflict escalates into a formal apartment summit*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Three flatmates negotiate over who left a cup in the sink, while a fourth keeps using the sink as a nest-material staging area.

Cast: Flatmates Nara, macaque; Odo, baboon; Piri, capuchin; and visiting cousin Tavi, gibbon.

## Episode: "Cup of Conflict"

### Cold Open

[APARTMENT KITCHEN. ONE CUP IN THE SINK.]

NARA: Whose cup?

ODO: Not mine.

PIRI: I use a bowl.

TAVI: I put a leaf in it.

NARA: Then it is a leaf cup.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE FLATMATES GATHER AROUND THE SINK.]

Nara says the cup has been there since breakfast. Odo says he washed every cup he used. Piri says the cup is still clean enough to drink from. Tavi says the leaf was not a leaf, but a strip of bark.

NARA: Why is bark in the sink?

TAVI: I was rinsing it.

ODO: The sink is not a workshop.

TAVI: It is a basin.

Piri suggests assigning one sink shelf to dishes and one to nest materials. Odo says that is two sinks. Nara points to the drainboard, which is currently occupied by Odo's toolbox.

ODO: The toolbox is drying.

NARA: It is full of screws.

ODO: They were wet.

Piri places the cup on the drainboard. A screw rolls into it.

PIRI: The cup has caught a tool.

TAVI: It is useful.

### Act Two

[THEY HOLD A HOUSE MEETING.]

Odo proposes a rotating sink schedule. Nara says the cup does not need a schedule; it needs washing. Piri washes it. The water splashes onto Tavi's bark strip.

TAVI: My material is wet again.

NARA: It was in the sink.

TAVI: I was rinsing it.

ODO: We need clear zones.

They put colored tape around the counter: blue for dishes, green for tools, yellow for nesting material. The tape crosses the sink and drainboard.

PIRI: The blue zone is too small.

NARA: The whole sink is blue.

ODO: The screws are in the sink.

Tavi moves the bark strip to the green zone by mistake. Odo moves the toolbox there too. The zones overlap.

NARA: The system has failed.

PIRI: The cup is clean.

TAVI: But where should it go?

Odo suggests a cup shelf. Nara says there is already a shelf. Piri points to a single empty hook.

[THE HOUSEMATES DRAW A MAP OF THE KITCHEN.]

Nara labels one counter "food" and the other "tools." Odo says the toolbox cannot stay on the counter if they are cooking. Tavi says the bark strip needs to dry near the window.

TAVI: The window is over the sink.

NARA: The sink is for washing.

PIRI: We can put the bark on a towel.

Odo lays out three towels. One is already damp from the spill. He puts the bark on the dry one and places the damp towel in the laundry basket.

TAVI: Now the bark has a drying zone.

NARA: And the sink is clear.

ODO: We should number the towels.

PIRI: We do not need a towel system.

Odo writes numbers on masking tape and sticks it to each towel.

NARA: You have labeled the towels.

ODO: They were unassigned.

Tavi takes the numbered towel and folds it into a small pad for the bark. The label ends up underneath.

TAVI: The towel has been assigned.

### Act Three

[THE FLATMATES TEST THE NEW HOOK.]

Piri hangs the cup. Tavi hangs the bark strip on the hook beside it. Odo adds a screwdriver. The hook bends.

[SFX: METAL CREAK.]

NARA: The hook has reached capacity.

ODO: We should not overload it.

TAVI: The cup, bark, and screwdriver all fit.

PIRI: They do not hang evenly.

The hook slips. Nara catches the cup. Tavi catches the bark. Odo catches his screwdriver. Piri catches the hook itself.

NARA: One hook was not enough.

ODO: A schedule might help.

TAVI: We can use the hook in turns.

PIRI: The hook is a shared resource.

Odo says the cup can stay on the shelf, the bark can drain on a towel, and the toolbox belongs in the closet. They move each object to its proper place.

NARA: We have restored the kitchen.

TAVI: The sink is empty.

ODO: The sink is for washing.

Nara asks everyone to empty the sink before bed. Odo moves the toolbox to the closet. Tavi takes his bark strip to the window ledge.

PIRI: The kitchen is clear.

NARA: The cup is on the shelf.

### Tag

[THE FLATMATES RELAX IN THE LIVING ROOM.]

Nara sees a cup on the coffee table.

NARA: Whose cup?

Everyone points at Odo.

ODO: I am still drinking from it.

[SFX: SIP, LAUGHTER.]
