# The Case of the Missing Objection

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom legal dramas, surprise evidence, and dramatic cross-examinations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A sharp trial lawyer must prove who moved the community fountain's only bench before the ceremonial opening, while the judge refuses to let the case become a public works meeting.

Cast: Advocate Neri, a lemur; defendant Boro, a gorilla; prosecutor Chik, a macaque; Judge Vassa, an orangutan; court clerk Pippin, a capuchin; and witness Loma, a baboon gardener.

## Episode: "The Bench Beneath the Banner"

### Cold open

INT. COMMUNITY COURT, MORNING

[A small courtroom. A folded park map sits on the evidence table. Boro stands beside an empty bench-shaped outline chalked on the floor.]

CHIK: The defendant is accused of moving the fountain bench.

BORO: I moved it three steps.

CHIK: Without permission.

BORO: It was in the splash zone.

VASSA: This court will determine whether the bench was moved, and why.

NERI: Your honor, we have a witness.

PIPPIN: The witness has arrived with a wheelbarrow.

LOMA: I brought the bench.

[Everyone turns. The bench is in the wheelbarrow.]

VASSA: We will take a short recess.

NERI: Before opening statements?

VASSA: The bench has arrived before the facts.

### Act One

INT. COMMUNITY COURT

[The wheelbarrow is parked beside the witness box. Loma sits on the bench.]

VASSA: Prosecutor, proceed.

CHIK: Mr. Boro, did you move the bench?

BORO: Yes.

CHIK: Did anyone ask you to?

BORO: The fountain asked me.

NERI: Objection. The fountain cannot speak.

VASSA: Sustained. The fountain may not testify.

CHIK: Why did you move it?

BORO: Water was hitting the seat.

CHIK: Did you notify the town?

BORO: I left a note.

PIPPIN: I entered the note as exhibit one.

[He unfolds a damp scrap reading "BENCH WET. MOVED."]

CHIK: The note has no signature.

BORO: The water erased it.

NERI: The note is wet because the problem was wet.

VASSA: Counsel, do not argue with the paper.

CHIK: I call Loma, the gardener.

LOMA: I saw Boro moving the bench.

CHIK: Was the fountain running?

LOMA: Yes.

CHIK: Was the bench in the splash zone?

LOMA: Yes.

CHIK: Then why is Boro on trial?

LOMA: The opening ceremony had a photograph scheduled.

NERI: Did the photographer need the bench?

LOMA: He needed an empty space for the mayor.

VASSA: A bench was moved for a photograph?

CHIK: The town has standards.

BORO: The mayor was standing where the water lands.

### Act Two

INT. COURTROOM

[Chik places a large park map on the evidence table. It unfolds across the judge's desk.]

CHIK: Exhibit two: the official fountain plan.

NERI: The plan has the bench on the north side.

CHIK: Correct.

BORO: The fountain faces north.

CHIK: Also correct.

BORO: Then the bench was in the splash zone.

CHIK: The map does not show splashing.

NERI: It also doesn't show the fountain's water.

VASSA: Does anyone have a current map?

PIPPIN: I have a hand drawing.

[He unfolds a drawing. It is mostly a circle with arrows.]

VASSA: What are the arrows?

PIPPIN: The water.

CHIK: I move to dismiss the drawing.

NERI: It is the only evidence showing the water.

VASSA: I will allow it as a map and as a drawing.

LOMA: I can demonstrate.

VASSA: No water in my courtroom.

LOMA: I have a dry demonstration.

[She pours pebbles from one cup into another to show the fountain's direction. One pebble rolls under the bench.]

PIPPIN: The pebble has escaped.

CHIK: Is the pebble evidence?

VASSA: It is now.

NERI: Your honor, the bench is not the issue. The town placed it where people could not sit.

CHIK: The town planned for a ceremony.

NERI: A public bench should serve the public.

VASSA: Counsel, this is becoming a meeting.

CHIK: I object to the word "public."

VASSA: On what grounds?

CHIK: It is broad.

VASSA: Overruled.

### Act Three

INT. COMMUNITY COURT

[Neri stands beside the wheelbarrow. Boro points to the chalk outline.]

NERI: The bench was moved three steps. The wheelbarrow shows where it went. The water shows why.

VASSA: The court finds that Boro moved the bench.

BORO: I told you that.

VASSA: The court also finds that the bench was wet, the note was sincere, and the ceremony plan was foolish.

CHIK: Can the court order a new bench location?

VASSA: The court can order the town to test the seat before inviting the public.

PIPPIN: Shall I record the verdict?

VASSA: Yes. And record that the pebble is returned to the fountain.

[The pebble rolls out from under the bench and stops at the judge's foot.]

NERI: It has returned on its own.

VASSA: Then the pebble is dismissed.

LOMA: Where should the bench go?

VASSA: Beside the tree, outside the spray.

BORO: I can move it.

CHIK: With permission?

VASSA: With a note signed in dry ink.

PIPPIN: I have a pencil.

NERI: The pencil is wet.

PIPPIN: I will use the back of the map.

### Tag

EXT. COURTHOUSE STEPS

[Boro and Loma set the bench beside the shade tree. Chik sits. A sprinkler turns on and sprays the empty chalk outline.]

CHIK: The courtroom map did not show this.

VASSA: It is not a courtroom matter anymore.

[The sprinkler shuts off; the bench rests in the shade.]
