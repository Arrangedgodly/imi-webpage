# A Little More Leaf

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: makeover reality shows that treat personal style as a crisis and mistakes a primate's natural markings for flaws*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A style expert offers a makeover to a shy monkey before a community portrait. The crew tries to transform her fur and favorite branch, but her troop prefers her natural look and simple grooming.

Cast: Stylist Neli, a gibbon; subject Savi, a macaque; cousin Peta, a capuchin; and photographer Juno, a baboon.

## Episode: "Ready for the Picture"

### Cold Open

[THE CAMERA SHOWS A MIRROR WITH LIGHTS.]

NELI: Today we reveal a new Savi.

SAVI: I am already here.

[PETA HANDS SAVI A LEAF COMB.]

PETA: And your hair is fine.

### Act One

[NELI LAYS OUT RIBBONS, BEADS, AND A BRIGHT CAPE.]

NELI: We could add a pop of color.

SAVI: I like the patch above my eye.

NELI: We can highlight it.

SAVI: It is a natural patch.

[THE MAKEUP CREW APPROACHES. SAVI STEPS BACK.]

JUNO: We need the portrait by sunset.

PETA: We can take it on her favorite branch.

NELI: The reveal is supposed to happen in a studio.

SAVI: The studio has no branch.

### Act Two

[NELI DRAPES THE CAPE OVER SAVI. IT COVERS HER ARMS.]

SAVI: I cannot climb in this.

NELI: It is for the portrait.

[PETA HELPS REMOVE THE CAPE.]

PETA: The portrait can show who she is.

[NELI BRUSHES SAVI'S FUR. SAVI GROOMS NELI'S EAR IN RETURN.]

NELI: I have been made part of the makeover.

SAVI: You had a leaf stuck there.

[JUNO TAKES A TEST PHOTO. THE FLASH STARTLES THE TROOP.]

JUNO: No flash.

NELI: We can use the window light.

[THEY MOVE OUTSIDE. THE LIGHT IS WARM AND SOFT.]

[THE CREW BRINGS A RACK OF CLOTHES TO THE BRANCH.]

NELI: The cape was not comfortable. We have a lighter scarf.

SAVI: I do not need it.

PETA: She can decide after she tries it.

[SAVI HOLDS THE SCARF. A BREEZE LIFTS IT FROM HER HAND AND WRAPS IT AROUND A BRANCH.]

NELI: The branch has chosen the accessory.

SAVI: The branch can keep it.

[JUNO TAKES A TEST PHOTO WITH THE SCARF IN THE BACKGROUND.]

JUNO: The picture has a nice line.

SAVI: It looks like a ribbon on the tree.

NELI: We can leave it there.

[PETA GROOMS A TINY LEAF OUT OF SAVI'S FUR. SAVI RETURNS THE GROOMING.]

PETA: That was the only change we needed.

NELI: You both look ready.

SAVI: We were ready before the clothes arrived.

[THE CREW REMOVES THE RACK FROM THE PATH. THE TROOP MOVES CLOSER TO THE BRANCH.]

[SAVI INSPECTS THE FINAL PHOTO.]

SAVI: My eye patch is in the picture.

JUNO: The light makes it clear.

NELI: Do you want the branch behind you?

SAVI: Yes. It is where I rest with my troop.

[PETA LOOKS AT THE IMAGE.]

PETA: There is a leaf in the corner.

SAVI: Leave it.

NELI: The photo will not match the studio plan.

JUNO: It matches the person.

[THE CREW PRINTS THE PHOTO. THE TROOP PASSES IT AROUND.]

PETA: We can hang it by the sleeping branch.

SAVI: That is a good place.

[THE CAPE FALLS OFF THE CAMERA AND LANDS ON THE GROUND. NELI FOLDS IT AND PUTS IT AWAY.]

[THE PHOTOGRAPH IS HUNG ON THE COMMUNITY WALL.]

NELI: The makeover episode is complete.

SAVI: We did not change anything.

PETA: We changed the place for the picture.

JUNO: And the light.

[SAVI TOUCHES THE PHOTO, THEN TURNS TO JOIN HER TROOP.]

NELI: The final look is the one Savi chose.

SAVI: I chose my branch and my troop.

NELI: That is a complete look.

[JUNO PACKS THE LIGHTS. THE TROOP REMAINS ON THE BRANCH.]

[SAVI RETURNS THE SCARF TO THE BRANCH. THE TROOP SETTLES AROUND HER.]

[NELI PACKS THE BRUSHES AND RIBBONS.]

NELI: We can return these to the studio.

PETA: Leave the leaf comb here.

[SAVI SETS THE COMB ON THE BRANCH. A JUVENILE TAKES IT AND BEGINS GROOMING.]

SAVI: It has a new owner.

JUNO: The portrait is finished.

[THE CREW CARRIES THE CAMERA DOWN THE PATH. THE TROOP REMAINS IN PLACE.]

NELI: We came to change a look and found a good light.

### Act Three

[SAVI SITS ON HER BRANCH. HER TROOP GATHERS BEHIND HER.]

NELI: Are you ready for the reveal?

SAVI: I am ready for the picture.

[JUNO TAKES THE PHOTO. IT SHOWS SAVI'S PATCH AND THE TROOP'S HANDS RESTING ON THE BRANCH.]

NELI: You look like yourself.

PETA: That is the point.

[THE CAMERA SHOWS THE BEFORE AND AFTER. BOTH PHOTOS LOOK NEARLY THE SAME.]

NELI: I will call this a natural finish.

SAVI: Call it a good day.

[THE TROOP LEANS IN TO SEE THE PICTURE. A SMALL MONKEY PULLS THE CAPE OVER THE CAMERA.]
