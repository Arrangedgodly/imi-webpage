# Night Vision Finds the Night Crew

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation shows that recast every ordinary sound as evidence of a haunting*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: A crew arrives at an old theater to investigate a ghostly tapping heard every night. Investigator Suma wants a supernatural reveal; caretaker Danu knows the stage crew's work schedule but cannot get the show to stop calling him a witness to the unknown.

Cast: Suma, a macaque investigator; Danu, a gorilla theater caretaker; Peli, a capuchin sound technician; Varo, a lemur host; and Tali, a red colobus stage painter.

## Episode: "The Tap at Eleven"

### Cold Open

[OLD THEATER. LIGHTS OUT. A TAP-TAP-TAP SOUNDS ABOVE THE STAGE. VARO WHISPERS INTO A CAMERA.] 

VARO: Something has answered us.

DANU: The fly rail clicks when the curtain warms.

[ANOTHER TAP. SUMA SWINGS A FLASHLIGHT TOWARD THE BALCONY. A PAINTBRUSH DROPS FROM A CATWALK INTO A BUCKET.] 

SUMA: The entity left a tool.

DANU: The painter did.

[THE CREW HEARS A DOOR CREAK. PELI PLAYS IT BACK THROUGH A SPEAKER, MAKING IT SOUND LIKE A GROWL.] 

### Act One

[SUMA SETS A MOTION CAMERA IN THE AISLE. PELI POINTS A MICROPHONE AT THE STAGE CURTAIN.] 

SUMA: We will capture whatever moves.

DANU: The night crew needs to finish painting.

VARO: Are they part of the investigation?

DANU: They are on the schedule.

[THE TAPPING RETURNS. THE CAMERA FLASHES. ON THE SCREEN, A SHADOW MOVES ACROSS THE WALL.] 

VARO: A figure!

[THE SHADOW GROWS AS DANU CARRIES A LADDER PAST THE WORK LIGHT.]

DANU: That is my shadow.

SUMA: Could you repeat the pass?

DANU: I need to put the ladder away.

[THE SHOW'S GRAPHIC LABELS THE VIDEO "UNIDENTIFIED PRESENCE." DANU POINTS AT HIS OWN REFLECTIVE VEST.] 

### Act Two

[VARO INTERVIEWS EACH CREW MEMBER IN THE LOBBY. PELI PLAYS BACK THEIR FOOTSTEPS AT HALF SPEED.] 

VARO: Did you hear the three taps?

TALI: I was tapping the brush handle to clear paint.

DANU: They repaint the trim every fall.

SUMA: Could the ghost be imitating the painter?

TALI: I was in the room.

[PELI'S AUDIO METER JUMPS WHEN A CART ROLLS OVER A FLOORBOARD. THE SCREEN FLASHES RED.] 

PELI: A strong spike.

DANU: That board squeaks under the cart.

[THE CART ROLLS BACK OVER THE BOARD. IT SQUEAKS AGAIN, THIS TIME IN A RHYTHM THAT SOUNDS LIKE THE SHOW'S THEME SONG. VARO LOOKS INTO CAMERA.] 

VARO: The theater may be singing.

DANU: The wheel is bent.

[SUMA ASKS PELI TO SET A TRAP OF BELLS NEAR THE FLY RAIL. DANU MOVES THEM OFF THE LADDER PATH.]

DANU: Nobody works under a tripwire.

[SUMA PUTS A RECORDER ON AN EMPTY SEAT. PELI PLAYS BACK TEN SECONDS OF ROOM TONE. THE FILTER TURNS A DISTANT WHISPER INTO A DEEP GROWL.] 

VARO: It said, "Leave this place."

TALI: I said, "Leave that brace there."

[DANU SHOWS THE RECORDER THE PAINTER'S BRACE, WHICH IS HOLDING A PROP WALL UPRIGHT. SUMA TAKES THE FILTER OFF.]

SUMA: The wall is not haunted. It is still wet.

PELI: The sound effect was convincing.

TALI: The paint is not.

[VARO CAREFULLY WALKS AROUND THE BRACE. HIS SHOES SQUEAK ON THE SAME FLOORBOARD. THE CREW'S METER SPIKES AGAIN.] 

VARO: I have produced the same evidence.

### Act Three

[THE CREW RETURNS TO THE STAGE AT ELEVEN. THE PAINTERS HAVE LEFT. DANU TURNS ON THE WORK LIGHTS AND SHOWS THE CURTAIN TRACK.]

DANU: When the fabric warms, the metal rail shifts and taps the support.

[THE RAIL TICKS THREE TIMES. SUMA RECORDS IT. PELI PLAYS BACK THE SOUND. THE CREW'S FILTER ADDS A LOW RUMBLE AND AN ECHO.] 

VARO: We have our evidence.

SUMA: We have the source.

[THE CAMERA'S NIGHT-VISION MODE TURNS DANU'S REFLECTIVE VEST BRIGHT WHITE. HIS MOVEMENT MAKES THE FIGURE APPEAR TO GLOW.] 

VARO: The witness is luminous.

DANU: The vest is reflective.

[PELI DISABLES THE FILTER. THE SOUND GOES BACK TO A TAP. SUMA GIVES THE THEATER THE RAW CLIP FOR ITS MAINTENANCE RECORD.]

[VARO ASKS SUMA FOR A FINAL LINE. SUMA CHECKS HER NOTES, BUT VARO TURNS ON THE NIGHT-VISION CAMERA AND HIS EYES REFLECT BACK AS TWO BRIGHT DOTS.] 

VARO: The investigator has become the evidence.

SUMA: You are standing in front of the lens.

[DANU OPENS A WORK LIGHT. THE REFLECTION DISAPPEARS. THE CREW RECORDS THE RAIL TICK ONCE MORE, WITHOUT MUSIC, FILTER OR A SHADOW GRAPHIC.]

PELI: That is all the sound contains.

SUMA: We can put the raw recording next to the maintenance note.

[VARO HOLDS UP BOTH ITEMS FOR THE CAMERA. THE SHOW'S LOWER THIRD STILL SAYS "GHOST CAUGHT ON TAPE." DANU REACHES OVER AND COVERS THE WORD "GHOST" WITH A PAPER LABEL: "CURTAIN RAIL."]

### Tag

[THE CREW PACKS UP. THE BENT CART WHEEL CROSSES THE FLOORBOARD ONE LAST TIME. VARO FLINCHES AT THE SQUEAK.] 

DANU: The ghost is leaving.

[VARO WALKS BACKWARD INTO THE OPEN PROP CLOSET AND THE DOOR CLICKS SHUT.]
