# Ape Space

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: workplace mockumentary convention*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Premise: At IMI's regional office for licensing monkey-made entertainment, the staff sell laughs by the crate while quietly fighting over who gets credit for them. Manager Daryl believes every camera is a chance to become beloved.

Cast: Daryl Baboon, regional manager and amateur morale architect; Nia Capuchin, payroll clerk with a dry eye for fraud; Kit Macaque, junior writer and reluctant prankster; Len Gibbon, veteran rights negotiator who communicates in sighs; Bev Langur, receptionist and keeper of the office keys.

## Episode: "The Morale Invoice"

### Cold open

INT. IMI REGIONAL OFFICE, MORNING

Daryl wheels a glass case into the bullpen. Inside is one yellow banana on a velvet pillow. A card reads: FIRST ANNUAL TEAM PEEL.

DARYL: This is our new morale award. It rotates weekly.

KIT: Does the banana rotate?

DARYL: The honor rotates. The banana remains as a symbol.

NIA: It has a barcode.

DARYL: The symbol is traceable.

He unveils a second card, with his own name typed on it. Everyone stares at the lens.

### Act one

The day begins with a mandatory morale meeting. Daryl has ordered matching T-shirts that say IMI: WE MAKE MONKEY BUSINESS. The sleeves are too short for Len, who has long arms and a contract folder tucked under one elbow.

DARYL: The shirt makes us one troop.

LEN: It makes me look like a napkin.

Daryl explains that the Team Peel will be awarded to the employee who best embodies collaboration. He has entered himself, citing the time he lent Kit a stapler.

KIT: You took it back while I was using it.

DARYL: A complete lending cycle.

Nia asks for the cost center. Daryl says morale has no price. Nia turns her monitor toward him. The invoice says $1,240 for one banana, one case, and "leadership aura consultation." The consultant's name is Daryl Baboon.

DARYL: The consultant was a different Daryl.

NIA: It says your employee number.

DARYL: He was a very similar Daryl.

Bev tells the camera that this happens every quarter. "Sometimes the inspiration has a receipt. Sometimes it has a parking ticket."

### Act two

Nia freezes the invoice until finance confirms the vendor. Daryl recruits Kit to make a morale video proving the award is worth it. Kit films staff describing their favorite office moment. Len describes the time the copier jammed during a rights negotiation and forced everyone to read the contract aloud, word by word, until the machine got embarrassed and released the pages.

The footage is good. Daryl wants to add a heroic voice-over and shots of himself carrying the case. Kit says that would turn a staff video into a campaign ad. Daryl asks if those are different genres.

Meanwhile, Len discovers the banana has been sitting under a grow lamp in the supply closet. He worries the award will ripen into an employment dispute. He shows Nia a clause from the union handbook: perishable gifts become property of the person who catches them after they fall.

NIA: You can't unionize a fruit.

LEN: I don't have to. The fruit has representation.

He opens a drawer and produces a tiny paper tie with "Counsel" written on it.

Kit sets up an interview with Daryl in front of the glass case. Daryl practices a modest expression. It looks like he has smelled a leak.

DARYL: I don't need to win. I just need the team to see me not winning.

KIT: That's the only version of winning you planned?

### Act three

Nia returns with finance on speaker. The invoice is real, but the aura consultant is not. Daryl used his own name while trying to create a new vendor. He says he wanted to get the office a small treat. Nia points out that the banana costs more than Bev's chair.

Bev: My chair has been here since the office was a fax machine.

The staff begin voting. Daryl insists he won't campaign, then stands by the ballot box holding the stapler Kit had borrowed. Kit takes it from him and drops in a ballot for Bev, who found the case, checked the label, and made sure nobody ate the award before payroll reviewed the invoice.

Bev wins. Daryl hands over the glass case. She accepts it and immediately removes the banana, which has developed one brown spot.

BEV: The case can rotate. The banana is going in the break room.

Len asks whether that constitutes a transfer of property. Bev tells him to draft a memo while he chews.

### Tag

The case sits empty on Bev's desk. Daryl has placed a new card inside: NEXT WEEK'S SYMBOL: Bev's emergency snack drawer.

Nia sticks the overdue invoice beneath it. The camera finds Daryl watching the empty display.

DARYL: Maybe the real morale award was the documentation we created.

NIA: The documentation says you owe us twelve hundred and forty dollars.

DARYL: Morale has a price.
