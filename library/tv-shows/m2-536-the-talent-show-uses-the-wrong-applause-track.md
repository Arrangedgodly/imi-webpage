# The Talent Show Uses the Wrong Applause Track

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised talent contests that cue applause before a performer has finished*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Vela, a lemur; singer Omi, a gibbon; judge Ralo, an orangutan; sound operator Savi, a macaque; producer Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. TALENT STAGE - NIGHT

Omi steps to the microphone. Before she sings, an enormous applause track erupts.

VELA: The audience loves her already.

OMI: I have not started.

ACT ONE

INT. STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Savi says the applause pad is linked to the entrance light. Tavi wants to keep the burst; the show needs energy before the first note. Ralo asks Omi to sing without the track. She begins a quiet verse. The recorded crowd cheers over her first line.

Savi hits MUTE. The audience sound stops, but a second track plays a standing ovation. Vela thanks the audience for rising. No one in the seats has moved. Ralo asks for silence so he can hear the singer's pitch. Tavi points to the ratings monitor, where applause levels are charted beside the song.

ACT TWO

INT. SOUND BOOTH - LATER

Savi follows the cable from the applause pad. It is plugged into the entrance cue and the judge's buzzer. Ralo presses his score button; a crowd gasps. Vela asks if that means the judges are surprised. Ralo says he has not scored yet. Tavi asks Savi to isolate the tracks. She disconnects the applause cue and leaves the room microphone on.

Omi repeats the verse. A cough from the audience carries clearly. Tavi grimaces and asks for the canned crowd back. Savi says the room mic is how they hear the performer. Ralo asks Omi to sing the chorus. She does, and the audience claps at the natural pause. Savi keeps the room mic open. The console's applause meter barely moves. Tavi holds up a sign reading BIGGER REACTION.

INT. TALENT STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Omi sings the final verse. Tavi slides the sign into her sightline. She reaches the last note and holds it while the prerecorded crowd begins again. The track cuts her off. Ralo turns the applause fader all the way down; the note hangs in the room. The audience claps after she releases it. Vela starts to call it a tie, but no contestant has been scored yet.

INT. SOUND BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Savi shows Omi the applause fader and the room microphone. The live room level rises when the audience claps; the canned track stays disconnected. Tavi asks her to keep a tiny applause sting ready for the result. Ralo says he will decide the score before any result sting. Vela tries an introduction without music. She forgets the guest's name and looks down at her card. Tavi presses the cue pad, and the canned crowd bursts out. Everyone looks at him. He removes his hand and turns the pad face down. Savi reroutes the score buzzer to a silent tally light so it cannot summon an imaginary gasp.


ACT THREE

INT. TALENT STAGE - LATER

Tavi asks for a close-up of the fader. Savi leaves it at zero, and Vela turns her cue cards face down so she cannot accidentally prompt another cheer.

Ralo asks Omi to perform the final chorus once more. Savi leaves the canned tracks disconnected. Tavi tries pressing the entrance light; no applause plays. He switches it twice. The stage stays quiet until Omi sings. Her voice fills the room. At the last note, she lowers the microphone and waits. The audience applauds on its own.

Ralo gives his score and explains that he heard the whole song. Vela announces the result, then waits for the audience response instead of cueing it. Tavi watches the ratings monitor; its applause graph is nearly flat until the real clapping begins. He points to it and calls the segment a technical success. Omi says the song was the performance. Savi adds that she can now tell the ending from the beginning. Ralo hands Omi the ribbon. Tavi looks for the applause button, then leaves it alone. Vela thanks the audience for their actual applause. The room responds again, at its own pace. Savi records the sound on the room mic. Tavi asks whether the crowd track should be restored for the winner photo. Ralo says the audience is already there. The camera takes one quiet frame before another spontaneous clap.

TAG

INT. SOUND BOOTH - AFTER THE SHOW

Savi labels the applause pad ENTRANCE ONLY. Tavi puts it in a drawer. Ralo returns the score card.

FINAL IMAGE: Omi holds her ribbon beneath a silent applause meter as the real audience claps beyond the frame.
