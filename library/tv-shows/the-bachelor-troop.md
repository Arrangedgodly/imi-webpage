# The Bachelor Troop

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating reality competition convention*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Premise: On a vine-covered resort set, a single eligible mandrill meets hopeful suitors while producers chase a finale tidy enough to fit into a sponsor's ad break.

Cast: Bex Mandrill, a bicycle mechanic seeking a partner who likes quiet mornings; Riff the producer, forever holding a clipboard; Lolo, a blunt botanist; Mint, a charming but homesick drummer; Tavi, an earnest baker; Jo, the sound operator who sees all.

## Episode: "The Ceremony of Excellent Questions"

### Cold open

INT. PALM VILLA, NIGHT

Bex stands beside a table of roses. Riff whispers that the camera needs a clean reaction. Bex looks down the row of suitors.

BEX: Before I give anyone a rose, can someone tell me where the laundry room is?

Every suitor points in a different direction. One points at the camera.

### Act one

The day's group date is called "Build Your Future." The contestants receive planks, twine, and a drawing of a bench. Riff says it symbolizes commitment. Jo, the sound operator, asks where the screws are. Riff says screws create a competitive advantage.

Bex pairs with Lolo, who reads the sketch and immediately identifies it as a shelf. Mint and Tavi build something that looks like a small boat. Bex asks what they want from a relationship. Lolo says she'd like someone who can admit when a plan is wrong before it becomes furniture.

Across the lawn, Mint tells Tavi he misses his sister's band and the little club where they play. Tavi listens instead of asking him to describe his feelings in a moonlit confession booth. Mint is surprised by the silence.

Riff pulls Bex aside and suggests she create a jealousy beat by giving Mint extra time. Bex asks if the producers want her to date someone or stir up the hedge. Riff says both can fit in a half-hour.

### Act two

During the picnic, Lolo challenges Bex to explain why she is on the show. Bex says she likes fixing bikes but most dates treat a puncture as a personality flaw. Tavi arrives with a basket of buns and confesses she applied because her mother sent in the form without telling her. She is enjoying meeting Bex, though she did not enjoy the helicopter entrance.

The helicopter entrance was a surprise from the production team. Bex tells them the rotor noise scared the resort's nesting swifts. Riff insists the birds signed a location release. Jo says the release has a wingprint.

That evening, the contestants gather for the rose ceremony. Riff hands Bex a card with three names highlighted. Bex sets it aside and asks each person one question: what ordinary thing do you hope to share with someone? Lolo says morning coffee in a garden. Mint says walking home after a gig, when the city is empty. Tavi says a kitchen where nobody calls a burnt loaf a metaphor.

Bex looks toward the cameras and asks for a minute without them. Riff refuses at first, then sees that Jo has turned the boom mic off. He gives in.

### Act three

Off camera, Bex tells the group she cannot promise a perfect ending on schedule. She can promise to listen. Mint says he needs to go home and help his sister prepare for a tour. He does not want a rose as consolation. He wants to leave with the number of the drummer who played the resort's welcome song.

Jo raises a hand. "It's me. I played it because the hired band got lost."

Mint grins. Riff sees the moment and instinctively calls for a close-up. Jo points the boom at him until he stops.

Bex gives roses to Lolo and Tavi. She tells them the choice is not a contract. Lolo says she prefers a repair order. Tavi accepts the rose and asks whether the kitchen is open.

The crew wraps before sunrise. Producers expected a cliffhanger. They instead have two dates planned and one contestant leaving with a musician's phone number. Riff tries to label it a twist.

BEX: It was a conversation.

RIFF: Conversations test well if we add a storm.

### Tag

The next morning, Tavi flips a pancake in the villa kitchen. It lands on the floor. Lolo picks it up, examines it, and declares it salvageable for the compost.

Bex opens the laundry room door. Behind it is a rose, taped to a washing machine, with a note from Mint: "Tell Jo to call me after load-in."

The camera stays on Bex smiling, then pans to Riff writing "laundry-room cliffhanger" on his clipboard.

At checkout, the crew asks Bex to record a line about finding love where she least expected it. Bex looks at the open laundry door, then at the clipboard. She says she found a place to wash clothes without hiding from the camera. Riff tries the line again with more wonder in his voice. It sounds like a detergent ad.

LOLO: You can keep the first take.

Bex asks both remaining dates whether they want another week on set or a normal afternoon in town. They choose town. The producers scramble to book a van, and Jo asks the driver to leave room for her drum kit. Riff requests a shot of them boarding separately, then gives up when no one can remember which seat produces the better story.

On the ride out, Tavi shares a bun with the camera operator. Lolo maps a walk to the garden shop. Bex listens as they plan, relieved that nobody is asking what it symbolizes.
