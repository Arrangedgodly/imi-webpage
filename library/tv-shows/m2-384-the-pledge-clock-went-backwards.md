# The Pledge Clock Went Backwards

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: public television telethons, matching gifts, goal clocks, and hosts who promise one last update every hour*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A telethon wants to raise money for a reading-room window, but its countdown clock keeps adding time whenever the host mentions a matching gift.

Cast: Host Bava, a macaque; pledge coordinator Rumi, a capuchin; caller Lalo, a gibbon; technician Seli, a baboon; librarian Tavi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. TELETHON STUDIO - EVENING

A digital clock counts down from ten seconds. Bava holds up a pledge card.

BAVA: We need one more pledge to reach our goal.

The clock jumps back to one hour.

SELI: The matching gift extended the timer.

BAVA: We have one more hour.

ACT ONE

Tavi explains the goal: replace a cracked window in the neighborhood reading room. The old window is boarded up while the frame is inspected. Rumi answers calls and records pledges on a board. Lalo calls in with a donation of bookshelves.

LALO: I can deliver them tomorrow.

RUMI: The drive is raising funds for a window.

LALO: The shelves will help too.

Bava asks if the bookshelves count toward the cash target. Rumi says they will be listed as an in-kind donation. Seli's clock advances to forty-five minutes. Bava announces a matching gift from a local hardware store. The clock jumps back to an hour.

BAVA: The deadline has become more generous.

ACT TWO

The call board lights up. One caller pledges paint. Another offers to help install the window. Rumi tries to keep cash, materials and volunteer time in separate columns. Bava announces all three as "support for the room."

TAVI: We still need enough cash for the window and fitting.

BAVA: The audience is giving in every way.

The clock jumps back another hour when a donor offers a second match. Seli disconnects the timer from the matching screen. It stops moving. Bava tells the viewers they have all night. Tavi says the studio rental ends at midnight.

RUMI: We need to tell people the actual deadline.

LALO: I can bring the shelves before noon.

TAVI: The room will be open then, if the contractor finishes measuring.

Bava turns to camera and says the window quote is higher than the first estimate. Rumi shows the revised quote. The cash total is close but short. She asks Tavi whether the reading room can operate with the board in place for another week. Tavi says yes, if the frame is secure and the contractor agrees.

Another caller offers to loan a ladder. Rumi asks whether the contractor has requested one. Tavi says the crew brings its own equipment. Bava starts to announce the ladder's value as a pledge. Rumi shakes her head and marks it as an offer, not cash or a confirmed need.

RUMI: We can thank the caller without adding it to the total.

LALO: I can keep the ladder at home.

SELI: The clock is not moving.

BAVA: Our honesty has not extended the deadline.

RUMI: That's correct.

ACT TWO-B

The final match offer arrives: every cash pledge made before midnight is doubled up to the remaining amount. Seli programs the clock correctly. It counts down. Bava tries to urge donors without promising unlimited time.

BAVA: We have twelve minutes. If you want to support the window, the pledge line is open until midnight.

Rumi takes a call. A donor pledges enough to cover the remaining cost. The match doubles it. Rumi confirms the pledge and updates the cash total. Tavi checks the revised quote and says they have enough for the window and installation.

TAVI: We have reached the amount we need.

BAVA: Should we keep raising money?

RUMI: We can close the window drive and tell people about next month's book fund.

Lalo asks if he can still deliver the shelves. Tavi says yes, after confirming that they fit the room. Seli marks the in-kind pledge separately from cash. Bava thanks the callers and announces the total once.

ACT THREE

The clock reaches zero. Bava reads the final sum. Tavi says the contractor will order the window in the morning and the reading room will keep its temporary board until installation. Rumi closes the pledge line. Lalo confirms delivery of the shelves for the following day.

LALO: I will measure them before I load the truck.

TAVI: Thank you. The doorway is narrow.

The studio applauds. Bava begins to announce another matching gift, then stops when Seli points at the zeroed clock. He lowers the pledge card.

BAVA: The drive is over. We will share the installation date after it is scheduled.

TAG

The broadcast credits start. The timer flashes "MATCH AVAILABLE" and begins counting upward. Seli unplugs it. The display goes dark.

FINAL IMAGE: Tavi carries the boarded-window sign out of the studio while the cash total remains on screen, fixed and final.
