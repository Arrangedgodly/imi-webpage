# The Tunnel Is Not a Deadline

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised dog agility competitions that prize fast obstacle runs over clear cues and an animal's comfort*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Dema wants a photo-finish agility round. Handler Luro wants his dog to learn the course at a safe pace, while judge Pavi keeps adding bonus obstacles to make the broadcast more exciting.

Cast: Dema, a macaque host; Luro, a gorilla handler; Pavi, a lemur judge; Niko, a capuchin course marshal; and Momo, Luro's mixed-breed dog.

## Episode: "One Obstacle at a Time"

### Cold Open

[INDOOR ARENA. A DOG SITS BESIDE LURO. A TUNNEL, LOW HURDLE, AND WEAVE POLES FORM A SHORT COURSE.]

DEMA: This round will test speed, control, and courage.

LURO: It is an introduction round.

PAVI: The timer starts on the first step.

[MOMO SNIFFS THE START LINE. THE TIMER BEEPS.]

NIKO: Momo has not started.

DEMA: We are already building tension.

[LURO REWARDS MOMO FOR LOOKING AT HIM. MOMO SITS.]

PAVI: That is not an obstacle.

LURO: It is a good start.

### Act One

[LURO WALKS MOMO TO THE LOW HURDLE. MOMO SNIFFS IT, THEN STEPS OVER.]

DEMA: A clean clearance.

PAVI: No jump.

LURO: The bar is low. Momo can choose.

[MOMO WALKS AROUND THE NEXT HURDLE. NIKO MOVES THE BAR LOWER.]

DEMA: Would a toy motivate a jump?

LURO: We can reward Momo after the course.

[DEMA HOLDS UP A BALL. MOMO WALKS TO HER AND SITS BESIDE HER SHOES.]

PAVI: That was not the planned route.

LURO: Momo chose a person.

[DEMA RETURNS THE BALL TO LURO, WHO PUTS IT AWAY.]

NIKO: We can adjust the course.

PAVI: Will that count as a penalty?

DEMA: The camera needs a leap.

LURO: Momo is learning where the obstacles are.

[MOMO REACHES THE TUNNEL. IT BACKS AWAY.]

PAVI: A refusal.

LURO: It is checking the tunnel.

[LURO SITS BESIDE THE ENTRANCE AND TOSSES A TREAT INSIDE. MOMO LOOKS IN, THEN WALKS THROUGH. THE TIMER HAS BEEN RUNNING FOR TWO MINUTES.]

DEMA: The longest tunnel entrance in competition history.

NIKO: Momo went through.

### Act Two

[PAVI ADDS A BELL AT THE END OF THE COURSE. A PRIZE PEDESTAL WAITS JUST OFF THE FINISH LINE.]

PAVI: Ring the bell for a bonus point.

LURO: Momo has not practiced with a bell.

DEMA: It is a festive touch.

[MOMO HEARS THE BELL AND TURNS AWAY FROM THE FINISH. LURO CALLS ITS NAME. MOMO RETURNS TO HIM.]

LURO: Good check-in.

PAVI: The course is incomplete.

[MOMO WALKS TO THE WEAVE POLES. IT SNIFFS EACH ONE, THEN STEPS AROUND THE FIRST.]

NIKO: We can widen the poles.

[PAVI MEASURES THEM. THE SPACING IS TOO CLOSE FOR MOMO'S SIZE. NIKO MOVES THEM APART.]

DEMA: The arena has adjusted to its competitor.

PAVI: Does that affect the score?

LURO: It affects whether Momo can pass through safely.

[MOMO WALKS BETWEEN THE POLES. IT DOES NOT WEAVE. LURO PRAISES IT AND ENDS THE RUN.]

PAVI: Can we try again now that the spacing is fixed?

LURO: Not today. We can practice before the next event.

[MOMO DRINKS WATER. NIKO REMOVES THE TIMER FROM THE ARENA FLOOR.]

[LURO SHOWS THE CREW THE COURSE NOTES FOR NEXT WEEK. THE TUNNEL WILL BE SHORTER AND THE WEAVE POLES WILL BE WIDER.]

PAVI: Can we keep the same timer?

LURO: We can keep a timer for the program. It will not decide whether Momo is ready.

[DEMA CHANGES THE GRAPHIC FROM "FASTEST RUN" TO "PRACTICE COURSE."]

### Act Three

[THE JUDGES REVIEW THE SCORECARD. PAVI HAS MARKED THREE REFUSALS AND TWO MISSED OBSTACLES. MOMO RESTS BESIDE THE WATER BOWL WHILE LURO HOLDS THE LEASH LOOSELY.]

PAVI: Momo did not touch the bell.

LURO: The bell was not part of the original course.

DEMA: Should we score the run that was planned or the run we aired?

NIKO: We can note the course changes on the card.

[PAVI WRITES "NEW RECORD: LONGEST TUNNEL ENTRANCE" ON A GOLD CARD.]

PAVI: It is technically a record.

DEMA: Can we present it as the fastest tunnel entrance?

LURO: No.

[MOMO HEARS THE WORD "PRESENT" AND WALKS TO THE EMPTY PODIUM, WHERE A CAMERA CREW HAS PLACED A PLUSH TOY. MOMO SITS BESIDE IT, LOOKING AT LURO.]

DEMA: The contestant has found the prize.

LURO: That toy is a lure. Please move it off the course.

[NIKO REMOVES THE PLUSH. MOMO FOLLOWS THE TOY TWO STEPS, THEN STOPS AT LURO'S HAND SIGNAL. THE CAMERAS KEEP ROLLING ON THE UNEXPECTED PAUSE.]

PAVI: We could award a composure trophy.

LURO: We could end the run and let Momo rest.

[DEMA TRIES TO PUT A MEDAL ON MOMO'S COLLAR. MOMO TURNS TO SNIFF IT, AND THE MEDAL DROPS INTO THE WATER BOWL WITH A PLOP.]

DEMA: The prize has entered the water feature.

NIKO: There is no water feature.

[PAVI CROSSES OUT THE MADE-UP RECORD AND WRITES "PRACTICE RUN: COMPLETED AT THE DOG'S PACE." THE SCORECARD NOW HAS ROOM FOR THE COURSE CHANGES AND THE DATE OF THE NEXT SESSION.]

PAVI: That is the run we actually saw.

LURO: Momo can try the tunnel again next week if he wants to.

[THE DOG DRINKS. THE CREW REMOVES THE EXTRA BELL AND PEDESTAL.]

### Tag

[DEMA PLACES THE SOAKING MEDAL ON THE SCOREBOARD TO DRY. MOMO WALKS PAST THE TROPHY PEDESTAL AND SITS BESIDE LURO.]

DEMA: No photo with the record?

LURO: Momo has finished work.

[NIKO RAISES THE SCOREBOARD FOR A PICTURE. THE CARD SAGS UNDER THE WET MEDAL, HIDING THE WORD "RECORD" AND LEAVING "PRACTICE RUN" IN FRAME.]
