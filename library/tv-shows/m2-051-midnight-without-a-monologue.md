# Midnight Without a Monologue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows, celebrity interviews, and house-band interruptions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A talk-show host tries to interview an acclaimed tree climber, but the house band interrupts every answer with a fanfare meant for the next segment.

Cast: Host Rala, a macaque; guest Enno, a gibbon climber; bandleader Mip, a capuchin; sidekick Dara, a baboon; and floor director Savi, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Guest Who Finished a Sentence"

### Cold open

INT. MIDNIGHT SET, NIGHT

[Rala sits behind a desk. Enno sits in the guest chair. The house band plays a loud flourish.]

RALA: Welcome back. My guest has climbed the tallest tree in the valley.

ENNO: Thank you for having me.

[Mip plays another flourish.]

RALA: Did you hear that?

ENNO: I did.

DARA: The band heard you.

RALA: We will continue after this musical event.

SAVI: That was not a break.

MIP: It felt like one.

### Act One

INT. MIDNIGHT SET

[Rala turns to camera. Dara places a bowl of fruit on the desk.]

RALA: Enno, how did you begin climbing?

ENNO: I started with a low branch-

[Mip plays a triumphant drum roll.]

RALA: The band is excited.

ENNO: I was explaining my first climb.

MIP: The first climb deserves a roll.

DARA: The guest has not finished the sentence.

MIP: I can play softly.

[The band plays an even louder whispery trill.]

RALA: That was not softer.

MIP: It was a quiet instrument.

SAVI: The show clock says we have four minutes.

RALA: We have time for three questions.

ENNO: I have an answer for the first one.

RALA: What did you learn from the climb?

ENNO: To test a branch before putting my weight on it.

[Mip plays a suspense chord.]

DARA: The branch passed.

MIP: It was a tense answer.

ENNO: I am still speaking.

RALA: We will let him finish.

[The band holds one long note.]

ENNO: I learned to climb carefully.

[The note ends.]

RALA: Excellent. We have a complete answer.

### Act Two

INT. MIDNIGHT SET, MOMENTS LATER

[Rala flips through cue cards. Mip watches the clock.]

RALA: Our next segment is "Guess That Fruit."

ENNO: Am I part of that?

RALA: You may guess.

DARA: The fruit is in the bowl.

[Enno reaches for it. Mip plays a drum roll. Enno waits.]

RALA: Guess.

ENNO: It is a pear.

MIP: Correct.

DARA: The band has a fruit song.

MIP: We have not rehearsed it.

RALA: Play one note.

[Mip plays a single note. A pear rolls off the desk.]

DARA: The pear is in the game.

SAVI: We are losing the set.

RALA: We need a quiet interview corner.

[The crew moves two stools to the side. The band follows.]

ENNO: Will the band follow us everywhere?

MIP: We are the house band.

ENNO: Then I am the house guest.

RALA: Tell us about the summit.

ENNO: At the top, I could see the river, the whole grove-

[Mip plays a fanfare. The lights flash.]

SAVI: That was the closing theme.

RALA: We are not closing yet.

DARA: The band thinks we are.

MIP: I received the wrong cue.

RALA: What cue do you need?

MIP: "Do not play."

RALA: That is an unusual musical direction.

MIP: I can perform it.

[The band sits silently. Everyone waits.]

ENNO: From the summit, I could see the river.

RALA: That sounds peaceful.

ENNO: It was. I stayed for a while.

[Mip raises a drumstick, then lowers it.]

### Act Three

INT. MIDNIGHT SET

[Rala gives Mip a card reading "NO MUSIC UNTIL THE GUEST FINISHES."]

RALA: Enno, what was the hardest part?

ENNO: The final climb. The upper branches were narrow, and the wind moved them. I had to stop and feel the branch before going on. That taught me patience.

[The band remains silent.]

RALA: Thank you.

ENNO: You're welcome.

DARA: We have achieved an interview.

MIP: I had to hold a note in my mind.

SAVI: You can play it now.

[Mip plays a small, gentle chord. Enno smiles.]

ENNO: That fits the story.

RALA: The band was waiting for the right moment.

MIP: I can play quietly when I know why.

DARA: Can you play the closing theme?

MIP: Yes.

RALA: Not yet.

[Enno notices the pear at the edge of the desk. He catches it before it falls.]

RALA: Quick reflexes.

ENNO: I am used to moving branches.

MIP: Should I play?

RALA: One note.

[Mip plays one note. The audience applauds.]

### Tag

INT. MIDNIGHT SET, LATER

[The band plays the closing theme. Rala's cue cards flutter away. Enno catches them midair.]

RALA: We should have interviewed him about juggling.

ENNO: I don't juggle.

[The band plays a fanfare anyway.]
