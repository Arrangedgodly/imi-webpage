# The Replay Booth Replays the Replay

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcasts that use endless slow-motion replays to challenge an already obvious call*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: During a friendly tree-climbing race, the broadcast team replays a harmless finish so often that the runners begin to age in the booth.

Cast: Commentator Miri, a gibbon; analyst Kito, a macaque; runner Tavi, a capuchin; runner Boro, a gorilla; and replay operator Sela, a lemur.

Episode: "Was the Tail Over the Line?"

COLD OPEN

EXT. PARK TRACK - DAY

A chalk line marks the finish of a short tree-climbing race. Tavi and Boro cross together. Miri speaks from a booth.

MIRI: It's a tie.

KITO: We need the replay.

[Sela runs the finish again. Tavi's tail crosses a fraction before Boro's hand.]

KITO: The tail is not a scoring limb.

MIRI: The tail is part of the runner.

ACT ONE

INT. COMMENTARY BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Sela freezes the replay. The frame shows both runners at the line.

KITO: Slow it down.

[SHE SLOWS THE VIDEO UNTIL THE RUNNERS MOVE ONE PIXEL AT A TIME.]

MIRI: Tavi's foot is behind the line.

KITO: Boro's hand is on it.

Sela: The timing sensor recorded a tie.

KITO: Could we zoom in?

[SHE ZOOMS IN. THE IMAGE BECOMES A FIELD OF SQUARES.]

MIRI: We are looking at the pixels.

KITO: We are looking for certainty.

ACT TWO

EXT. PARK TRACK - LATER

Tavi and Boro wait at the finish. Sela carries a tablet. Miri approaches with the replay result.

TAVI: Are we running again?

MIRI: The broadcast is reviewing the finish.

BORO: We can share the medal.

KITO (ON SPEAKER): We need one winner for the graphic.

TAVI: The race had one finish.

BORO: We both crossed.

[Sela plays the replay on her tablet. The video shows the runners crossing, then the replay booth looking at the tablet.] 

MIRI: We are replaying the replay.

KITO: That gives us a second angle.

Sela: It is the same angle on a smaller screen.

EXT. PARK TRACK - MIDDAY

The runners stand beside the finish line. Kito brings the tablet over. Miri marks the chalk with a fresh line.

KITO: We can run the finish again without the race.

TAVI: We crossed together.

BORO: The sensor made one beep.

MIRI: The sensor records the timing chip.

KITO: Does it record the tail?

MIRI: There is no tail chip.

[SELA PLAYS THE FINISH ON THE TABLET. THE RUNNERS CROSS THE FRAME TOGETHER.]

TAVI: The video and the sensor agree.

KITO: But the replay looks close.

BORO: It looks like a tie.

MIRI: The result is a tie.

[KITO DRAWS A CIRCLE AROUND THE PHOTO FINISH. THE CIRCLE COVERS BOTH RUNNERS.]

KITO: The camera sees them together.

TAVI: Then let us both take a medal.

MIRI: The awards are still in the booth.

BORO: We can carry them.

[THE RUNNERS WALK OFF SIDE BY SIDE. KITO HOLDS THE TABLET UP, STILL WATCHING THE REPLAY.]

ACT TWO-B

INT. COMMENTARY BOOTH - AFTERNOON

Kito draws a diagram of the finish line. Miri holds two identical medals. Sela has a new clip queued.

KITO: We can compare the shadows.

MIRI: The sun moved between camera frames.

KITO: Then compare the shoes.

[THE REPLAY SHOWS TWO SHOES. THEY ARE BOTH ON THE LINE.]

SELA: The sensor reads the bib, not the shoes.

KITO: The bibs crossed together.

MIRI: The result has not changed.

KITO: Can we have one more angle?

SELA: The camera is fixed.

KITO: We can check the finish sensor log.

[SELA PULLS UP THE LOG. IT SHOWS ONE BEEP FOR BOTH TIMING CHIPS.]

MIRI: The sensor recorded them together.

KITO: Could the sensor be wrong?

SELA: It was tested before the race.

KITO: Can we test it again?

[SELA CROSSES THE LINE. THE SENSOR BEEPS ONCE. KITO CROSSES BESIDE HER. IT BEEPS ONCE AGAIN.]

MIRI: The system works.

KITO: The runners were still close.

SELA: The times are identical to the hundredth of a second.

MIRI: The race is over.

ACT THREE

EXT. PARK TRACK - AWARD CEREMONY

Miri holds the two medals. Kito remains in the booth. Tavi and Boro stand together.

MIRI: The judges confirm the tie. Each runner gets a medal.

TAVI: Could the replay stop now?

[Sela presses STOP. The video freezes on the replay booth.]

BORO: The broadcast has been watching itself.

KITO: We were checking the result.

MIRI: The result was a tie.

[THE MEDALS HANG SIDE BY SIDE. THE CAMERA PANS TO THE STILL FRAME OF THE BOOTH.]

TAG

Kito reviews the medal ceremony. He presses slow motion.

MIRI: That is us waving.

KITO: I am checking whether we waved at the same time.

MIRI: You did.

KITO: We can return to the race.

MIRI: It ended when they crossed.
