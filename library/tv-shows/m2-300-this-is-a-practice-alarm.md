# This Is a Practice Alarm

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live emergency broadcast television, where a scheduled fire drill is narrated as a breaking crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A television crew covers a routine fire drill at the community hall, but the host keeps calling it an emergency and the staff must get the audience outside calmly.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; safety lead Nara, macaque; manager Odo, baboon; usher Piri, gibbon; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Exit to the Left"

### Cold Open

[HALL. A BELL SOUNDS ONCE.]

SELI: We have an alarm.

NARA: It is the scheduled drill.

ODO: The audience is looking at us.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW STANDS BESIDE THE EXIT MAP.]

Nara explains the drill to the audience. Odo asks whether the broadcast should continue. Seli says they are live and should narrate the evacuation.

PIRI: We need to lead people out.

TAVI: The camera cable crosses the aisle.

NARA: Move the cable first.

Seli begins a dramatic report about an alarm. Nara interrupts and says the audience should use the marked exit on the left. Odo repeats the instruction.

ODO: This is a practice alarm. Please walk calmly to the left exit.

The audience stands. One person asks if the show is over. Piri says it will continue after the drill.

### Act Two

[THE AISLE BEGINS TO CLEAR.]

Tavi moves the camera cable to the wall. The camera angle now faces the stage, not the exit. Seli asks him to keep filming.

TAVI: I cannot film and move the cable at the same time.

NARA: Leave the camera.

Seli says the audience needs to see the exit. Odo points at the lit sign. Piri walks toward the door and checks that it opens.

PIRI: The exit is clear.

ODO: The audience is moving.

A guest stops to collect a coat. Nara asks them to leave it and return after the drill. Odo says the coat is part of the scene. Nara says it is not.

SELI: We have a delay.

NARA: We have one person retrieving a coat.

Tavi sets the camera down. The crew escorts the guest toward the exit. Everyone walks at a steady pace.

[NARA CHECKS THE EXIT LIST.]

Odo checks names against the attendance sheet. Seli asks if they should call roll on the broadcast. Nara says the list is for staff, not viewers.

SELI: We need to confirm everyone is out.

PIRI: We can count the people in the courtyard.

TAVI: There are more people than were in the hall.

Odo starts counting, but two guests enter from the far gate to ask about the noise.

ODO: We have new arrivals.

NARA: They were outside already.

Seli asks if they should include them in the drill. Nara says the goal is to clear the hall, not the courtyard. Odo returns to the door and checks every row.

PIRI: The room is empty.

SELI: We have an all-clear.

NARA: Wait for the alarm to stop before returning.

The bell rings twice, then pauses. Seli asks whether two rings mean the drill is over. Nara checks the posted instructions. The all-clear signal is one continuous tone.

TAVI: The alarm is not finished.

ODO: We will keep waiting.

### Act Three

[THE HALL IS EMPTY.]

Piri checks the room. Nara checks the exit path. Odo confirms everyone is outside. The alarm stops.

[SFX: BELL STOPS.]

[THE ALL-CLEAR TONE SOUNDS ONCE.]

The crew waits until the tone ends. Nara confirms the hall can reopen. Seli asks whether to replay the alarm; Odo says the show needs a dramatic recap, but Piri points to the audience calmly returning through the marked exit.

NARA: The drill is complete. The cable is secured, and the route is clear.

SELI: We have one report, not a replay.

Tavi films the exit map. Odo tries to stand beside the arrow for a closing shot, then realizes he is blocking the route and steps aside.

TAVI: Clear frame.

ODO: Clear exit.

PIRI: The audience can sit down now.

Seli delivers one calm signoff. The sign above the exit stays lit while the camera cuts away.

The live monitor shows a red BREAKING banner. Odo asks Tavi to leave it up for the replay. Nara reaches over and changes the caption to SCHEDULED DRILL.

ODO: It looks less urgent.

NARA: It was never urgent.

Piri demonstrates the clear aisle by walking through it without stopping. Odo follows, then catches himself before turning the demonstration into another report.

TAVI: We have our closing shot.

SELI: Use the calm one.

### Tag

[THE DOORS OPEN AGAIN.]

The audience returns to their seats. One guest has left a coat on the chair. Odo picks it up.

ODO: The coat has been evacuated.

NARA: It was never in danger.

SELI: The broadcast is over.

[SFX: APPLAUSE SIGN CLICKS ON, THEN OFF.]
