# Low Tide, High Tales

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fishing show*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: Two friends host a gentle fishing program that focuses less on record catches and more on learning how the river works when their favorite pool runs dry.

Cast: Host Pez, an easygoing macaque; Nori, a precise gibbon angler; Tully, a young baboon learning to cast; Old Ebb, a turtle river guide; and the show's producer, Clack, a nervous capuchin who wants a dramatic fish story.

## Episode: "The Pool That Moved"

### Cold open

EXT. RIVERBANK, MORNING

Pez points toward the usual fishing pool. It is mostly dry. Nori stares at a narrow stream cutting through the reeds.

PEZ: The river has moved our set.

Tully casts anyway. His line lands in a puddle beside his boot. Clack calls for a close-up of the puddle.

### Act one

The old fishing pool has filled with gravel after a storm. Clack asks the hosts to describe last season's biggest catch. Pez calls it the one that got away.

Old Ebb points out a new pool downstream of a fallen tree. He asks them to check the current and leave room for fish to pass.

Tully snags a reed and snaps his line. Nori helps him tie a new knot.

### Act two

From above, the hosts spot fish near the roots and ripples farther out. Nori casts near the seam where fast water meets still water.

Nori releases a tug before setting the hook. Ebb points out that a shallow pool behind the tree will heat up as the day brightens.

They move to a shaded bank. Tully casts again, this time landing just upstream of the seam. A small trout takes the lure. Tully reels slowly. Pez helps him keep the rod tip up. The fish reaches the bank. They measure it, photograph it, and release it into the deeper channel.

Clack asks them to call it a record. Tully says it was his first fish without a snag, which is enough.


[The crew reaches the new pool.]
PEZ: The old bank is gone.
NORI: The water found another route.
TULLY: Do fish know?
EBB: They are already there.
CLACK: Can we call it a migration?
PEZ: It's a river bend.
CLACK: The title needs drama.
NORI: Try "water moved."
TULLY: That's honest.
EBB: Check the current seam.
TULLY: It runs fast here.
NORI: Cast upstream.
TULLY: My line caught reeds.
PEZ: Don't yank it.
TULLY: I'm loosening it.
CLACK: We missed the fish.
NORI: We caught a lesson.
CLACK: Does that weigh anything?
EBB: Less than a camera.
TULLY: The lure is clear.
NORI: Try the shaded edge.
TULLY: Is it deep enough?
EBB: We'll test with a pole.
PEZ: Slow water by the roots.
TULLY: I have a bite.
NORI: Lift the rod.
TULLY: I'm not pulling hard.
PEZ: Let it run.
CLACK: We need a close shot.
EBB: Give the fish space.
TULLY: It's at the bank.
NORI: Measure it quickly.
TULLY: It is small.
PEZ: It is a fish.
TULLY: I caught it safely.
EBB: Now release it.
CLACK: Can we keep the ending?
PEZ: Keep the whole ending.
NORI: Pack out that line.
TULLY: And the bottle?
EBB: Take both.
CLACK: The pool is empty.
PEZ: The pool is resting.

### Act three

They explain the changed river and leave the fallen tree in place. The new channel carries food and fish. Ebb says anglers should read the water before casting.

They pack out line and a crushed bottle. Clack asks if the episode has enough action. Pez says the fish has better things to do than appear on television.

Nori shows Tully a safer way to cast beside the brush. He lands the lure in the water and does not catch anything. He is pleased anyway. They close the episode with the sound of the river over the microphone. Clack tries to add dramatic music, but the song makes the current sound like a parade. He leaves the natural sound instead.

### Tag

The crew packs up. Tully's line catches on a reed again. He sighs and untangles it without pulling.

NORI: You learned something.

TULLY: I learned reeds are patient.

A small fish jumps in the distance. Clack turns the camera too late, and the shot holds on empty water.

### Extra scene

The crew checks the riverbank before packing. Tully finds a short piece of monofilament caught in a root and removes it carefully. Nori shows him how to coil it so it cannot snag a bird or turtle. Clack asks whether the cleanup belongs in the closing montage. Tully says it belongs in the bin. They sort the line and the bottle into separate bags, then take them back to town. Old Ebb points out that the river has carried branches and leaves into the new pool, but the crew should not clear natural cover just to make casting easier. The hosts mark a safe place to stand and leave the pool as they found it. Pez says the day had one small catch, one broken line, and a better map of the water. Clack says he can work with that. He labels the episode "The Pool That Moved" and adds a note to return after the next storm, when the channel may have changed again.
