# The Garden Competition Bans the Hose

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality gardening contests, dramatic judging montages, and challenges with rules revealed after the plants are watered*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: HOST, a baboon; GARDENER PIP, a macaque; GARDENER OMI, an orangutan; JUDGE, a gibbon; PRODUCER, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

The host reveals a sign: NO HOSES. Pip holds a watering can. Omi holds a hose with the nozzle removed.

HOST: Today's challenge is to grow a garden without a hose.

OMI: This hose has no nozzle.

JUDGE: It is still a hose.

The producer points toward the timer. The countdown starts before anyone has planted a seed.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The contestants receive one small plot, one bag of seeds, and a measured amount of water. Pip studies the soil. Omi begins arranging decorative stones into a dry river. The host narrates each move as if a championship is at stake.

HOST: Pip chooses restraint. Omi chooses geology.

OMI: The stones are part of the garden.

JUDGE: The rule says plants only.

Omi removes the stones and hides them beneath the bench. Pip pours water slowly into a shallow tray. The producer asks for a close-up, and the camera zooms on a single seed.

PRODUCER: This is the season's most important seed.

PIP: There are twelve.

The judge checks the water measure. Omi's watering can has a leak; it drips onto the path and not into the plot. Omi tilts the can further, making the leak worse.

OMI: I am watering the route.

PIP: The route is growing moss.

ACT TWO

The host announces a surprise rule: contestants may collect dew but cannot bring water from outside the marked station. Pip places broad leaves beneath the garden frame overnight. Omi sets out cups but accidentally catches a drip from the greenhouse gutter, which is outside the boundary. The judge confiscates the water.

OMI: It came from the sky.

JUDGE: It came from the gutter.

Pip's leaves gather only a little dew. She moves them under the open frame, where morning sun warms them. The drops slide into a bowl. Omi tries to imitate the technique with a towel, wringing the moisture into a cup. The towel was damp before the challenge, so the judge rejects the water.

PRODUCER: We have a reversal.

OMI: We have a towel.

The producers show a montage of seeds sprouting. Pip's patch has two shoots. Omi's has one sprout and a circle of carefully arranged stones beneath the bench. The judge discovers the stones and asks why they were hidden.

OMI: They are now a root border.

JUDGE: They are still rocks.

The contestants have one minute to water. Pip uses her bowl of dew. Omi has no water left. The host asks if Omi wants to borrow Pip's. Omi refuses, then notices the leaking can has filled a tiny puddle at its base. The judge confirms it came from Omi's measured ration. Omi gathers the puddle with a spoon and places the water around the sprout.

INT. GARDEN JUDGING TENT - DAY

The judge asks both contestants to explain how they used their measured water. Pip shows the bowl and lists each watering. Omi shows the leaking can and tries to call the puddle a rain reserve. The judge points to the rule card. Water must come from the assigned allotment or be collected from natural dew.

OMI: The puddle came from my can.

JUDGE: Then it belongs to your allotment.

The producer asks for a close-up of the puddle. Omi holds the spoon under the drip and collects the last drop. The judge accepts it. Pip's plot has two shoots but one has grown sideways toward Omi's stones. Omi asks whether the plants can compete across the boundary. The judge checks the line markers and says roots may cross, but tools may not.

PIP: My shoot has wandered.

OMI: It likes the stones.

The host asks each gardener to make a final presentation. Pip describes her dew collection and points to the leaves. Omi describes the muddy path, then adds that the stones were hidden only because the first rule banned ornaments. The judge says the rule banned stones, not paths. The producer starts a dramatic replay of Omi hiding the stones. Omi asks to see the footage. It shows the stones clearly beneath the bench.

OMI: That is a very good hiding place.

JUDGE: It was visible on camera.

The producer ends the replay before showing the exact moment the stones were placed. The judge scores only the gardens, not the montage.



ACT THREE

At judging, Pip wins for the strongest plants and accurate use of the dew. Omi wins the "best path" prize, even though the path is muddy. The host announces that next week's challenge will involve rain barrels. Omi immediately places the hose on a shelf labeled DISQUALIFIED.

PIP: It could water the barrels.

JUDGE: Not until after the rules are read.

FINAL IMAGE: Pip's two shoots stand in morning light while Omi's carefully arranged stones surround the one surviving sprout.
