# The Map Room Holds a Press Conference

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cable news panels that argue over a map instead of the place it represents*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Anchor Sela, a macaque; analyst Pava, a lemur; mayor Vex, an orangutan; cartographer Nari, a gibbon; runner Pip, a capuchin; resident caller and hallway visitor, local villagers.

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL NEWS MAP ROOM - NIGHT

[A large town map hangs on a wall. Pip holds a rolled-up street map. Sela faces camera beside a long wooden pointer.] 

SELA: We have breaking news about the town square.

PAVA: The square is on the map.

[The map falls off the wall. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

SELA: Welcome to Mapline, where everyone has a route and no one agrees on the scale.

VEX: The square should be moved closer to city hall.

NARI: The map is fixed to the streets.

VEX: The camera is fixed to the map.

[Pava points to a small blue circle.] 

PAVA: That is the fountain.

VEX: It is too close to the park.

NARI: The fountain is in the square. The park is across the road.

[The crew unfolds a new map. It is a hand-drawn map made by Pip, with a large fountain and a tiny city hall.] 

SELA: This map is not to scale.

PIP: The fountain is bigger because it splashes.

VEX: We need a neutral map.

ACT TWO

[Nari pins the official map to the wall with four magnets. Vex uses a pointer to show a proposed walking route. The pointer rolls down the map and ends at the bakery.] 

VEX: The route has changed.

PAVA: The pointer has gone downhill.

[The magnets slip. Nari presses one corner back. The map stays up, but the north arrow points to the floor.] 

SELA: We are live.

NARI: We are sideways.

[Pip rotates the map. The town appears upside down to the camera. Vex continues his statement without noticing.] 

VEX: This proposal is gaining ground.

PAVA: The map is upside down.

VEX: The ground has moved in the frame.

[The mayor asks for public comment. Nari opens the floor. Pip unfolds his map and marks the route to the fountain. It passes the bakery, the library, and the square.] 

PIP: This is how I walk there.

VEX: Your map has too many stops.

PIP: It has the places I go.

[The studio phone rings. Sela takes the call from a resident standing in the square. The caller says the fountain is working and splashing toward the benches.] 

SELA: We have a live update.

NARI: The map cannot show a splash.

[The crew turns the official map toward the camera. A blue water stain from a leaking marker spreads across the square.] 

PAVA: The map is now wet.

VEX: The square is leaking into the studio.

[Pava asks for a map with the new fountain route. Nari pins Pip's hand-drawn map beside the official one. The route on Pip's map avoids the wet bench and passes the bakery.]

SELA: Which route is the town using?

PIP: The one with dry feet.

VEX: We cannot change a municipal route based on a bakery.

NARI: People walk by the bakery.

[The phone rings again. This time the caller is standing outside the studio door. Pip opens it. A resident points to the map and asks where the square is.]

PIP: You are here.

[He taps the correct spot. A magnet drops from the wall. The map curls shut at the center.]

NARI: The official map is not staying up.

VEX: It needs stronger backing.

PAVA: It needs a clear route.

[They hold the paper flat while Sela tapes its corners. The tape covers the north arrow. Vex rotates the page until the arrow points up.]

VEX: Now we have orientation.

PIP: The bakery is still there.

[The resident uses Pip's map and leaves through the hall. The studio camera follows, exposing the actual town square visible through the window.]

SELA: We have a live location.

ACT THREE

[The group goes to the actual town square. The fountain is splashing lightly; a maintenance worker has placed a bucket near one bench. Nari wipes the map stain and marks the bench.] 

NARI: The water reaches this far.

VEX: The route proposal is still under review.

PAVA: The benches need moving away from the fountain.

[The crew moves one bench. Pip lays a broom handle along the path to check the clearance. It fits between the fountain and the bakery cart.] 

PIP: The route is clear.

SELA: We have a practical update.

VEX: Can we have a debate about it?

NARI: We just did.

[The fountain splashes again. Vex's paper map gets wet. Pip offers his hand-drawn map, which has the bakery, library, and actual bench positions.] 

VEX: This one is more useful.

PIP: It is not to scale.

NARI: It is to the places.

[The anchor signs off. Vex folds the official map; the damp square sticks together and forms a small paper tube.] 

TAG

[Pip uses the tube to point toward the exit.]

PIP: The route is this way.

SELA: We have one final map update.
