# The Cliffhanger Has a Reset Button

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: serialized action dramas, repeated cliffhangers, recap montages, and scenes that stop just before a simple choice*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: An action serial films its weekly cliffhanger at a fake ravine, but the actors keep making the rescue choice before the director can cut away.

Cast: Hero Tavi, a capuchin; engineer Luma, a macaque; rival Koro, a gibbon; director Sava, a baboon; script supervisor Neri, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO RAVINE SET - DAY

Tavi clings to a painted ledge above a shallow blue mat. Luma reaches for his hand. Sava raises a finger.

SAVA: Stop there. We need the cliffhanger.

TAVI: I can reach the ledge.

LUMA: You are holding the rope.

Neri checks the script. It says, "Will he take her hand?"

ACT ONE

The crew resets the rope. Luma stands at the top of the fake ravine. Tavi returns to the ledge. Koro arrives as the rival who is meant to choose whether to cut the rope. Sava explains that the scene ends before anyone decides.

KORO: The rope is not attached to anything.

SAVA: It looks attached from camera three.

Neri points to a visible knot tied around a sandbag. The camera angle hides the bag. Tavi is lowered three inches, then Luma reaches down. She grabs his hand and pulls him up.

SAVA: Cut! You resolved the cliffhanger.

LUMA: I pulled him up.

TAVI: I was there.

ACT TWO

The crew resets again. Sava asks them to hold their positions longer. The camera tracks across the painted cliff, revealing its edge and a studio ladder. Neri tells Sava the ladder appears in the frame. Sava asks the prop team to move it. The ladder rolls behind a fake boulder, then rolls back when the set floor tilts.

KORO: The ladder is returning to the plot.

SAVA: We can cover it with the fog machine.

The fog machine starts and fills the set. Luma cannot see Tavi's hand. She asks him to call out. Tavi says he is safely on the mat. Neri asks the crew to stop the fog. The vent fan blows it toward camera one, where it hides the fake boulder.

SAVA: This is almost a crisis.

Neri: It is a set.

Sava asks the actors to play the scene without solving it. Luma holds her hand over the ledge. Tavi says, "I can let go." Koro offers to cut the rope, then reads his next line and lowers the prop knife.

KORO: I will not cut the rope.

LUMA: Then take my other hand.

ACT TWO-B

The actors try another take. This time Tavi catches Luma's wrist; Koro steps in to help. Sava calls cut just before they lift him. Neri checks the footage and says the scene already shows the rescue starting. Sava argues that viewers need a question for next week.

NERI: The question is whether they get off the set before the fog clears.

SAVA: That is not the story.

TAVI: It is the room we are in.

The studio door opens. A crew member rolls in the next episode's "collapsing bridge" section: two boards balanced on cardboard boxes. Sava orders it moved out of frame. Koro steps backward and bumps the boxes; the boards wobble. Luma braces them with one hand while Tavi reaches down from the ledge to steady the prop. Neri calls for the crew to secure the bridge.

SAVA: Hold! That is a perfect cliffhanger.

NERI: The boards are supported now.

KORO: We keep resolving things.

Neri asks for a reaction shot from each actor. Tavi looks at the camera and says he is safely standing on a blue mat. Luma says she can see the rope is tied to a sandbag. Koro points to the next week's cardboard bridge and asks whether it has a safety rail. Sava tries to cue ominous music, but the orchestra has already packed its instruments.

SAVA: We need a sound for the end.

NERI: The crew can leave the room.

The crew pauses between takes. They secure the bridge boxes, coil the spare rope and fold the ladder out of the camera frame. The rescue rope stays anchored to the sandbag for the final shot. Sava writes "DO NOT LEAN" on a card and tapes it to the bridge prop.

TAVI: That is the clearest cliffhanger we have had.

ACT THREE

Sava reviews the take. The rescue is clear, the bridge is stable, and the scene can end with the characters choosing to leave together. He allows one final shot: Tavi climbs onto the ledge, Luma anchors the rope, and Koro carries the prop knife away. They walk off the set while the camera follows.

SAVA: We have a scene ending.

NERI: And a beginning for next week.

TAVI: What is next week's problem?

Sava points at the cardboard bridge.

SAVA: The bridge.

KORO: It is made of boxes.

SAVA: We will build a better one.

TAG

The recap voice-over begins while the crew breaks down the set. It summarizes the rescue, the rope, and the bridge. Neri lowers the microphone.

NERI: They can watch the episode.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera holds on the safely folded rope as the recap narration tries to make it sound dangerous one last time.
