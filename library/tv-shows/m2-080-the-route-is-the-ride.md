# The Route Is the Ride

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: transit makeover documentaries, public feedback segments, and optimistic infrastructure launches*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A crew tries to launch a new canopy shuttle route, but the route circles the same tree because the mapmaker refuses to choose a terminal.

Cast: Planner Nema, a gibbon; driver Koro, a gorilla; host Lavi, a lemur; rider Toma, a macaque; and inspector Pex, a baboon.

## Episode: "One Loop Too Many"

### Cold open

EXT. SHUTTLE STOP, MORNING

[A small leaf-cart shuttle rolls up. A sign reads "NEW ROUTE - EVERYWHERE."]

LAVI: Today we launch the grove's first shuttle.

TOMA: Where does it go?

NEMA: Everywhere.

KORO: The route starts here and returns here.

TOMA: That is nowhere.

NEMA: It is a loop.

PEX: The route is approved.

KORO: It has not moved yet.

[The shuttle circles the tree once and returns to the stop.]

LAVI: The route is now underway.

### Act One

INT. SHUTTLE

[Koro drives. Nema holds a laminated map. Toma boards with a basket.]

TOMA: Does this stop at the market?

KORO: It passes the market.

NEMA: It has not been added as a stop.

TOMA: Can I get off?

KORO: We can request a stop.

TOMA: I am requesting.

NEMA: There is no stop request button.

LAVI: The shuttle has a flexible route.

PEX: It follows the map.

[Koro looks at the map. The route is drawn as a circle around one tree.]

KORO: This is the route.

NEMA: The map was printed in a circle.

TOMA: We are back at the start.

LAVI: The first loop is complete.

### Act Two

EXT. MARKET

[Toma steps off the shuttle. The driver waits. Nema marks the map.]

TOMA: I needed to get to the market.

NEMA: You are at the market.

TOMA: The shuttle brought me in a circle.

KORO: It passed here three times.

LAVI: The service is frequent.

PEX: The route schedule says once every hour.

KORO: We passed the stop three times in ten minutes.

PEX: That is a service improvement.

NEMA: We need a second stop.

TOMA: Put it by the market.

NEMA: Then the loop will be longer.

KORO: We can turn at the market.

PEX: There is no turn-around space.

TOMA: The market has a plaza.

LAVI: We have found a natural terminal.

[Nema marks the plaza. The shuttle tries to turn. Its basket wheel catches on a fruit stand.]

KORO: The terminal needs more space.

TOMA: The fruit stand is movable.

[They move the stand. The shuttle turns and returns to the stop.]

### Route audit

EXT. MARKET PLAZA

[Pex asks riders to rate the new shuttle route. Toma holds a chalkboard.]

PEX: How many stops did you use?

TOMA: One.

PEX: How many stops are on the map?

NEMA: Two.

PEX: How many rides did the shuttle make?

KORO: Three loops.

LAVI: The route is growing.

TOMA: The shuttle went around the tree three times before I got off.

NEMA: We can add the market as a stop.

KORO: It is already a stop.

PEX: The sign says "Everywhere."

TOMA: That is not a stop.

[They replace the sign with "TREE - MARKET - TREE."]

LAVI: The route is now specific.

NEMA: It is also shorter.

KORO: The shuttle can make more trips.

### Act Three

EXT. SHUTTLE STOP

[The route now goes from the tree to the market and back. A small group boards.]

LAVI: The new route is ready.

PEX: The map has two points.

NEMA: The start and the market.

TOMA: That is enough.

KORO: The route takes six minutes.

PEX: The schedule says twelve.

KORO: It includes a stop for loading fruit.

TOMA: The fruit is already loaded.

NEMA: The route is accessible.

PEX: The step is high.

[Koro lowers the shuttle's leaf ramp. Toma rolls the basket aboard.]

LAVI: We have solved the boarding issue.

TOMA: The shuttle is going to the market?

KORO: Yes.

TOMA: I am already at the market.

KORO: You can ride back to the tree.

TOMA: Then the route works both ways.

NEMA: The shuttle has a direction.

PEX: The map points both directions.

LAVI: A two-way route.

KORO: We have only one shuttle.

TOMA: It can turn around.

[The shuttle reaches the plaza and turns smoothly.]

### Schedule board

INT. SHUTTLE STOP

[Nema writes a schedule. Koro points to the shuttle.]

KORO: The route is short enough to run every ten minutes.

PEX: The schedule says hourly.

TOMA: I missed the shuttle by one minute.

LAVI: The timetable is optimistic.

NEMA: I can update it.

[The pencil breaks.]

KORO: The shuttle is already here.

TOMA: Then I will get on.

PEX: The schedule has not been approved.

NEMA: The passengers have approved it.

### Tag

EXT. TREE STOP, AFTERNOON

[The shuttle arrives at the tree stop. Toma steps down with an empty basket.]

LAVI: The route has connected the grove.

TOMA: The fruit is still at the market.

[Koro rings the shuttle bell and heads back.]
