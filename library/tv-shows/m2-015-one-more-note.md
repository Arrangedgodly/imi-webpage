# One More Note

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: talent competition television*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A neighborhood talent contest struggles to fit a wide range of acts into a tidy finale, until the contestants decide to make room for one another on the same stage.

Cast: Host Vee, a polished macaque; Judge Sola, an exacting parrot who plays piano; Judge Tamm, a warm baboon drummer; Rolo, a young gibbon dancer; Ina, a capuchin who performs shadow puppets; and the Three Sticks, an elderly trio of langurs who play rhythm with branches.

## Episode: "The Seven Minute Finale"

### Cold open

INT. COMMUNITY HALL, NIGHT

Vee opens the curtain. The stage is empty except for a microphone stand and one fallen leaf. She checks the rundown.

VEE: Our first act is delayed by a missing costume. Our second act is delayed by the first act's costume.

The Three Sticks walk on carrying their branches. One branch has a costume hat on it.

### Act one

The live final has seven minutes before the hall opens for the food pantry. Rolo needs a wide floor, Ina needs darkness, and the Three Sticks need time for their slow opening. The producer wants one act cut.

Sola warns Rolo about the front edge. Ina refuses to cut the moonrise, and the trio keeps its quiet opening.

Backstage, Vee tries to rearrange the acts. Rolo can perform before the screen goes up. Ina can project shadows onto a hanging cloth. The trio can play behind it. The technical crew says they only have one safe lamp and a short cable. Sola asks why there is a spotlight for the judges but no spare for the acts.

### Act two

The producer tests shortened solos. Rolo misses a turn; Ina's moon appears too early; the trio loses its rhythm. Tamm stops rehearsal.

He says the show has asked everyone to become a smaller version of themselves. Vee proposes a single shared finale. Rolo can dance around the screen while Ina moves the puppets. The Three Sticks can provide the rhythm. The performers test the plan. Rolo's spin sends a shadow puppet skittering off the cloth. Ina catches it and improvises a character chasing a dancer.

Sola points out that the song and dance overlap too much. She plays a slower pulse on the piano. The trio responds with a soft branch tap. Rolo finds a smaller movement that fits the beat. The shadow moon rises behind them. The act takes six minutes and forty seconds.

The producer worries that a shared act will blur the winner. The equipment grant can still be voted on. Ina needs lights, Rolo wants a sprung floor, and the trio wants benches.


[Backstage, the producer holds a stopwatch.]
VEE: Seven minutes for four acts.
ROLO: My dance takes three.
INA: My story takes four.
STICKS: Our song takes five.
VEE: That is twelve.
PRODUCER: Cut an act.
TAMM: Cut the stopwatch.
SOLA: We can overlap the music.
ROLO: I need a clear floor.
INA: I need a dark screen.
STICKS: We need to hear one another.
VEE: Can the screen be smaller?
INA: It can be a sheet.
ROLO: Can the dance be shorter?
SOLA: The turn is the ending.
ROLO: Keep the turn.
TAMM: Lose the extra lap.
ROLO: That lap is dramatic.
INA: So is the moon rising.
VEE: The moon can rise sooner.
INA: It needs a story first.
STICKS: We can start softly.
SOLA: Then the dance enters.
ROLO: I can follow your beat.
TAMM: Don't follow me. Listen.
ROLO: That is harder.
TAMM: Good.
PRODUCER: Who is the winner?
VEE: The audience will vote.
PRODUCER: We need one name.
INA: Put all our names.
STICKS: We made one act.
ROLO: Then it has one name.
SOLA: The group.
TAMM: The group can use benches.
VEE: And lights.
PRODUCER: Is that a prize?
VEE: It is useful.

### Act three

The live performance begins. Rolo dances in the light of the paper moon. Ina guides the puppet through a story about a traveler learning to follow a rhythm without leading it. The Three Sticks create the beat, sometimes holding still so the dancer can listen. Near the end, Rolo offers Ina the center of the stage. She moves the puppet into the foreground and lets him take the next turn.

The judges give their notes. Sola says the timing had one rough patch, which made the shared work feel real. Tamm says nobody disappeared behind the others. Vee opens the audience vote. The three solo acts would have competed against one another, but the shared finale has led to a tie between all three.

The hall's committee awards the equipment grant to the group. They will split it between portable floor panels, two stage lamps, and benches. The producer asks Vee to announce one winner. Vee says the committee already has. He repeats the name of the group and the audience cheers.

The performers thank the crew, then help fold the screen and carry the branches to storage. Rolo asks Ina if she wants to make a longer piece later. Ina says yes, if the story can keep its quiet beginning. The trio says they can provide a rhythm that leaves space.

### Tag

The next morning, the hall's food pantry opens. One of the new benches stands by the entrance. The Three Sticks sit on it, tapping out a gentle beat while volunteers unload boxes.

A child dances along the line of crates. Vee, in an apron, tries to follow.

SOLA: The turn is after the second tap.

VEE: I knew the show needed another rehearsal.
