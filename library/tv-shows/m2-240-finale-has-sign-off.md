# The Finale Has a Sign-Off

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live variety-show finales that prolong the goodbye with repeated thank-yous and one last segment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A host tries to end a community variety show, but every farewell triggers a new scheduled segment.

Cast: Host Runi, a gibbon; floor manager Vela, a macaque; performer Seli, a capuchin; and stagehand Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "Good Night, Again"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - NIGHT

Runi stands beneath a banner. Seli holds a ukulele. Vela watches a countdown clock.

RUNI: Thank you for joining our show. Good night.

VELA: We still have the closing number.

SELI: I thought that was the closing number.

[THE CLOCK SHOWS FIVE MINUTES REMAINING.]

ACT ONE

Runi returns to the host chair. Vela hands him a card. Odo moves a prop drum offstage.

RUNI: We have one final song.

SELI: I can play it now.

VELA: First, we thank the volunteers.

[THE VOLUNTEERS WAVE. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK RESETS TO TEN MINUTES.]

RUNI: Why did the clock reset?

VELA: The thank-you segment has its own time.

ACT TWO

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - LATER

Seli plays a short song. Runi begins to sign off. Odo brings in a cake with sparklers.

RUNI: That is all for tonight.

ODO: There is a cake presentation.

SELI: The cake was in the green room.

VELA: It is the show's final surprise.

[THE SPARKLERS GO OUT BEFORE THE CAKE REACHES THE TABLE.]

RUNI: The surprise has happened.

VELA: We still need the applause.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. Vela points to another card.]

ACT TWO-B

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Runi looks at the new card. It says "ONE LAST MESSAGE." Seli sits beside him. Odo begins rolling the cake away.

RUNI: This really is our last message.

VELA: The director has another message.

[THE SPEAKER CRACKLES. A VOICE SAYS, "THANK YOU, GOOD NIGHT."]

SELI: The director has signed off.

RUNI: We can sign off too.

VELA: Wait for the credits.

[THE CREDITS BEGIN. THE CAST WAVES. THE CLOCK RESETS AGAIN.]

RUNI: The credits are still running.

VELA: We need the cast thank-you.

ODO: We already thanked them.

ACT TWO-C

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - LATE NIGHT

The audience has left. Vela checks the rundown. Runi holds the sign-off card. Seli puts away the ukulele.

VELA: There is still a final sponsor thank-you.

RUNI: The sponsor is not in the building.

ODO: The card says to thank the sponsors.

SELI: We can read it once.

RUNI: Thank you to the sponsors. Good night.

[THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK RESETS TO FIVE MINUTES.]

VELA: That was the sponsor close. We still have the station close.

RUNI: The station has already gone off air.

ODO: The studio light is still on.

SELI: We can turn it off.

VELA: The light cue is in the closing segment.

[ODO FLIPS THE SWITCH. THE ROOM GOES DARK, EXCEPT FOR THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK.]

RUNI: The clock is still running.

VELA: The final cue needs the host.

SELI: The ukulele is packed away.

ODO: The plug is behind the banner.

[ODO LIFTS ONE CORNER OF THE BANNER. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK CONTINUES.]

ACT TWO-D

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Vela passes out the final rundown card. Runi sits in the host chair. Seli has repacked the ukulele. Odo holds the banner away from the clock cord.

VELA: After the sponsor thank-you, we introduce the closing song.

SELI: The song was already performed.

VELA: The schedule lists an encore.

RUNI: Did the audience ask for one?

VELA: The schedule asked.

ODO: The audience is gone.

[VELA POINTS TO THE EMPTY CHAIRS.]

VELA: We can play the encore to camera.

SELI: I packed the instrument.

RUNI: We can say the encore was canceled.

VELA: That leaves a gap in the rundown.

ODO: The gap is where the show ends.

[VELA CROSSES OUT THE ENCORE. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK ADDS TWO MINUTES.]

RUNI: The clock does not follow the rundown.

VELA: It has its own schedule.

SELI: I am ready to go.

[ODO PUTS THE BANNER DOWN. IT COVERS THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK.]

RUNI: We have canceled the encore.

SELI: We can give the audience the actual ending.

ACT THREE

INT. COMMUNITY STUDIO - FINAL SIGN-OFF

[RUNI LIFTS THE BANNER OFF THE CLOCK. VELA CHECKS THE COUNTDOWN.]

Runi steps to the door. Vela reads the countdown. Seli and Odo stand behind him with the cake.

RUNI: Good night. The studio is closed.

VELA: The clock is still on.

RUNI: Take it with you.

[ODO UNPLUGS THE CLOCK. ITS BACKUP BATTERY KEEPS THE DISPLAY GLOWING. VELA LIFTS IT; THE RESET BUTTON UNDERNEATH ADDS FIVE MINUTES.]

SELI: We can leave now.

[THEY WALK OUT. VELA FOLLOWS, CARRYING THE CLOCK.]

TAG

The studio is empty. A recorded voice says, "One last thank-you." No one returns.
