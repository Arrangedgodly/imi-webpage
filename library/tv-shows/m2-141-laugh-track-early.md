# The Laugh Track Is Early

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: multi-camera sitcoms that cue laughter before the punch line and hold actors in place for audience reactions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Director Sela needs a clean sitcom scene about flatmates sharing one coat hook. Sound operator Pavi keeps triggering the laugh track before the characters finish their lines, until the actors begin waiting for the wrong cue.

Cast: Sela, a macaque director; Pavi, a lemur sound operator; Rumi, a gorilla actor; and Tavi, a capuchin actor.

## Episode: "Hold for the Laugh"

### Cold Open

[APARTMENT SET. RUMI HANGS A COAT ON THE ONLY HOOK. TAVI ENTERS WITH A SECOND COAT.]

TAVI: Is this hook free?

RUMI: It is holding my coat.

[LAUGHTER PLAYS BEFORE THE LINE ENDS. RUMI STARES AT THE SOUND BOOTH.]

SELA: Pavi, you are early.

PAVI: I thought the audience was with him.

TAVI: I have not finished asking.

[THE LAUGHTER TRACK PLAYS AGAIN. BOTH ACTORS WAIT.]

SELA: And now it is late.

### Act One

[SELA RESETS THE SCENE. PAVI PLACES A FINGER ON THE SOUND BOARD.]

SELA: Start after Tavi asks the question.

PAVI: Which question?

TAVI: The one I have not asked yet.

[PAVI PLAYS A TINY LAUGH. RUMI LOOKS AT THE BOOTH.]

SELA: No sound until the end of a line.

PAVI: What if the line has a funny middle?

SELA: We can hear it first.

SELA: We go through the exchange once without cues.

RUMI: The coat is already on the hook.

TAVI: Then I can use the chair.

[RUMI MOVES THE CHAIR. IT HAS A SIGN ON IT: "RESERVED FOR LAUGH TRACK."]

TAVI: Where do I sit?

PAVI: The audience will laugh at that.

SELA: They cannot laugh at a chair sign before they see it.

[PAVI PLAYS A SHORT LAUGH. TAVI TURNS TO CAMERA.]

TAVI: I heard it.

RUMI: The chair heard it too.

[LAUGHTER PLAYS. THE ACTORS LOOK AT THE CHAIR.]

SELA: We are filming a sitcom, not interviewing the furniture.

### Act Two

[THEY TRY THE SCENE AGAIN. TAVI TAKES THE COAT OFF THE HOOK.]

TAVI: There. Now it is free.

[RUMI REACHES FOR THE HOOK. THE LAUGH TRACK PLAYS.]

RUMI: I have not said anything.

PAVI: The hook looked funny.

SELA: A prop does not deliver a line.

[TAVI HANGS BOTH COATS ON THE SAME HOOK. THEY SLIDE TO THE FLOOR.]

TAVI: We have shared it.

RUMI: We have dropped it.

[THE LAUGH TRACK PLAYS. RUMI PICKS UP THE COATS. TAVI TAKES THE CHAIR. PAVI PLAYS A BIGGER LAUGH.]

TAVI: I am sitting down.

SELA: Let him sit.

[TAVI SITS. THE CHAIR SQUEAKS. PAVI CUES LAUGHTER. TAVI STANDS BACK UP.]

RUMI: The chair has more lines than I do.

[PAVI CUES A BIGGER LAUGH. THE ACTORS WAIT FOR IT TO END. THE SOUND CONTINUES FOR SEVEN SECONDS.]

SELA: We need a shorter laugh.

PAVI: That was the full audience.

TAVI: We have not invited the audience yet.

[PAVI OPENS THE SOUND LIBRARY AND SEES THE FILE IS LABELED "FULL AUDIENCE." ]

TAVI: The audience thinks I am leaving.

PAVI: They thought the squeak was the joke.

SELA: The squeak is a chair.

[A WARM-UP AUDIENCE TAKES ITS SEATS. PAVI EXPLAINS THAT THE LAUGHTER WILL FOLLOW THE ACTORS, NOT LEAD THEM.]

PAVI: If a joke lands, wait for the room.

TAVI: And if the room stays quiet?

SELA: Keep doing the scene.

[THE AUDIENCE NODS. RUMI HANGS ONE COAT ON EACH HOOK.]

PAVI: I will keep my hand away from the board until the line ends.

TAVI: Thank you.

[THE CAST STARTS THE TAKE. PAVI WAITS THROUGH THE WHOLE EXCHANGE.]

[THE AUDIENCE PRACTICES THE SCENE WITHOUT THE SOUND BOARD. THEY WAIT UNTIL THE LAST LINE, THEN LAUGH TOGETHER.]

PAVI: That was the cue.

SELA: That was the audience.

[THE ACTORS RESET THE COATS AND BEGIN THE TAKE.]

### Act Three

[SELA MOVES THE CHAIR OFF SET. TAVI AND RUMI PUT TWO COAT HOOKS ON THE WALL.]

TAVI: One hook for each coat.

RUMI: The scene is solved.

[THE LAUGH TRACK PLAYS.]

SELA: That cue is right, but the line is not.

PAVI: I can wait for the scene.

[THEY FILM AGAIN. TAVI HANGS HIS COAT ON THE NEW HOOK. RUMI HANGS HIS ON THE OTHER. THEY BOTH LOOK AT CAMERA.]

RUMI: We can live together.

TAVI: As long as we label the hooks.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS AFTER THE LINE. PAVI DOES NOT PRESS A BUTTON.]

SELA: Good. Keep that timing.

### Tag

[THE CAST TAKES A BREAK. PAVI PLAYS THE LAUGH TRACK ONCE.]

SELA: That is not a cue.

PAVI: I was checking the volume.

[RUMI TRIES THE NEW HOOKS. THE COATS STAY UP. TAVI TESTS THE CHAIR OFF SET; IT DOES NOT SQUEAK.]

TAVI: That was not a joke.

PAVI: It was a test.

[THEY LOOK AT THE HOOKS. ONE IS LABELED "TEST." THE LAUGHTER PLAYS.]
