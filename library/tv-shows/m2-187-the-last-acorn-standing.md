# The Last Acorn Standing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival competition reality television, where producers force a sensible troop to treat seed storage as a dramatic elimination game*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A group of forest residents competes to protect a single acorn through a windy afternoon, while the rules keep changing to create suspense.

Cast: Host Rell, a gibbon; contestants Miri, a macaque, Odo, a baboon, and Pava, a squirrel monkey; field medic Seli, a lemur.

Episode: "Windfall"

COLD OPEN

EXT. CLEARING, DAY

Rell holds up an acorn. The contestants stand behind a chalk line.

RELL: One acorn. Three teams. Only one can take it home.

Seli checks the acorn for cracks.

SELI: It is already assigned to the nursery.

Rell asks her to step outside the frame. Miri notices a squirrel moving acorns nearby.

MIRI: There are more acorns.

RELL: This is the last one for the challenge.

The wind blows the acorn out of his hand. All contestants chase it. Seli catches it in a leaf cup.

ACT ONE

EXT. CLEARING, LATER

Rell explains the first challenge: build a shelter for the acorn using only materials from the ground. Odo gathers sticks. Pava chooses a hollow log. Miri uses a broad leaf and two stones. The judges inspect each shelter. Rell says the leaf shelter is too exposed, the log is too comfortable, and the stick shelter is not cinematic.

Seli asks whether the acorn should be planted instead. Rell says planting is the finale. The contestants each add one material. A gust lifts the leaf and reveals the acorn has rolled into a shallow hole. Miri covers it with soil.

MIRI: It has found a place to stay.

Rell calls this an unexpected twist.

ACT TWO

EXT. CLEARING, AFTERNOON

A producer's card announces a new rule: contestants must retrieve the acorn without touching the ground. Odo climbs a tree and reaches down. Pava swings from a vine. Miri examines the hole and asks why they need to move it.

Rell says the rules require a retrieval. Miri uses a long branch to point at the spot, then asks Seli whether the acorn is safe. Seli says the soil is good but too dry. The contestants fetch water. Rell says the challenge is now a hydration test.

Odo carries water in a cupped leaf. Pava finds a small stream and uses a hollow stem. Miri drips water onto the soil. The producer's card changes: "One contestant must be eliminated." The contestants look at the acorn, then at Rell.

PAVA: We can all go home.

Rell asks them to vote. They vote to eliminate the new rule. Seli says the rule has no badge, so it cannot be removed from the course. Miri moves the acorn's marker to a safe spot and invites the group to help shade it.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CLEARING, LATE AFTERNOON

The producer announces a bonus challenge: the teams must construct a windbreak without using any materials already in the shelter. Odo gathers more sticks. Pava asks whether the windbreak can simply go around the nursery. Rell says the camera needs a visible build.

Miri uses broad leaves and sets them low, leaving room for air to pass. Odo builds a taller wall. It catches the wind and falls over. Seli checks the acorn marker and shows them the soil is drying. The contestants agree to use their leaves to shade the planting spot instead of building walls.

Rell asks each contestant to say what they would do if they won. Odo says he would take the acorn home. Seli reminds him it belongs to the nursery. Pava says she would help water the saplings. Miri says she would remove the elimination rule from the challenge.

The wind rises. The contestants shield the marker with the leaf shelter they built in Act One. Rell asks them to compete for who can hold it longest. They sit on the corners together so it does not blow away.

MIRI: Nobody wins if the shelter flies off.

Odo finds a smooth stone and places it at the edge. The shelter stays. The producer gives them a new card: "Choose one contestant to send home." The group folds the card into a paper shade and places it beside the marker.

ACT THREE


EXT. NURSERY, SUNSET

The contestants bring the marked soil to the nursery bed. Seli records the planting location. Rell insists on a dramatic countdown. The seed is already in the soil, so the countdown happens over a watering can. Odo pours. Pava places a small marker. Miri shields it from a gust.

Rell announces one winner: the acorn. The contestants applaud. Seli says the nursery team will care for it. Rell asks if they can do a reunion episode when it sprouts.

TAG

EXT. NURSERY, NEXT MORNING

A small squirrel monkey sits beside the marker. It asks where the acorn went. Seli points to the soil. Rell appears with a camera.

RELL: The champion returns.

The squirrel begins digging. Everyone quickly covers the bed with a wire basket.
