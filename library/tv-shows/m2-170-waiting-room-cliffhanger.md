# The Waiting Room Cliffhanger

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: medical dramas that turn routine waiting-room delays into life-or-death cliffhangers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: A television crew is embedded at a small animal clinic for a medical drama. Producer Nox wants a dramatic emergency montage, but receptionist Uma keeps asking the crew to leave the check-in path clear while the actual patients wait calmly with their caregivers.

Cast: Uma, a macaque receptionist; Dr. Sori, a gorilla veterinarian; Nox, a capuchin producer; Beni, a lemur camera operator; and animal patients with their caregivers.

## Episode: "The Beeping Monitor"

### Cold Open

[CLINIC WAITING ROOM. A FISH TANK FILTER GURGLES. THE LABEL PRINTER BEEPS BEHIND THE COUNTER. NOX TURNS TO CAMERA.]

NOX: One beep could change everything.

UMA: That is the printer saying the label is ready.

[THE PRINTER SPITS OUT A LABEL. NOX TAKES A DRAMATIC STEP BACK.] 

NOX: The diagnosis has arrived.

DR. SORI: It is a name tag for the carrier.

[BENI PANS TO A DOG SLEEPING BESIDE ITS OWNER. THE DOG DOES NOT WAKE.]

[NOX ASKS UMA TO ANNOUNCE A CODE. SHE CHECKS THE CLINIC BOARD AND CALLS THE NEXT APPOINTMENT BY NAME. THE OWNER STANDS, AND THE DOG WAKES TO FOLLOW.]

NOX: There was no alarm?

UMA: It is their turn.

[DR. SORI OPENS THE EXAM ROOM DOOR. THE DOG WALKS IN WITH ITS OWNER. THE CAMERA STAYS OUTSIDE WITH NOX.]

NOX: We have lost the patient.

BENI: The patient is in the exam room.

### Act One

[NOX REARRANGES THE WAITING ROOM CHAIRS TO MAKE A NARROW CORRIDOR FOR THE CAMERA.] 

UMA: Please keep the walkway open.

NOX: We need a clear shot of the door.

UMA: The patients need a clear path to the door.

[DR. SORI CARRIES A SMALL CARRIER INTO AN EXAM ROOM. NOX CUES A FAST HEARTBEAT SOUND EFFECT. THE OWNER'S CAT INSIDE THE CARRIER PURRS.] 

NOX: The sound is wrong.

BENI: It is the cat.

[NOX TURNS THE EFFECT OFF. THE PURR REMAINS.]

### Act Two

[THE CLINIC IS QUIET. NOX ASKS UMA TO ANNOUNCE A LONG DELAY.]

NOX: Could you say the wait is unbearable?

UMA: The next appointment is five minutes late.

[AN OWNER SIGNS A FORM. THE PEN RUNS OUT OF INK. NOX CUES A SUSPENSE STING.] 

OWNER: Does anyone have another pen?

[DR. SORI HANDS OVER A PEN. THE FORM IS SIGNED.] 

NOX: The crisis is over.

UMA: It was a pen.

[THE PRINTER BEEPS AGAIN. NOX JUMPS. UMA TAKES THE LABEL AND PUTS IT ON A FILE.]

[ANOTHER LABEL PRINTS CROOKED, WITH THE PATIENT'S NAME UPSIDE DOWN. UMA PEELS IT OFF AND REPRINTS IT. NOX TRIES TO BUILD A CLIFFHANGER AROUND THE TURNING PAPER.]

NOX: Will the name be right side up?

UMA: Yes.

[SHE STICKS THE LABEL STRAIGHT. THE CAREGIVER TAKES THE FILE AND WALKS INTO THE EXAM ROOM. NOX'S CAMERA FOLLOWS THE LABEL INSTEAD OF THE PEOPLE.]

UMA: Ready for the next patient.

[NOX TRIES TO MOVE THE CAMERA THROUGH THE CHECK-IN LINE. BENI'S SHOULDER BUMPS A WATER BOWL. DR. SORI CATCHES IT BEFORE IT SPILLS.]

DR. SORI: We need the floor clear.

NOX: We need motion.

UMA: The waiting room has enough motion.

[A SMALL DOG STARTS BARKING AT THE CAMERA CART. NOX CUES A STING. UMA MOVES THE CART AWAY FROM THE DOOR, AND THE DOG SETTLES BESIDE ITS OWNER.] 

DR. SORI: The dog was alerting its person.

NOX: Can we replay the bark?

BENI: The owner has gone into the room.

[NOX TURNS OFF THE AUDIO BUTTON. THE ROOM RETURNS TO THE AQUARIUM GURGLE AND PRINTER HUM.] 

### Act Three

[THE EXAM ROOM DOOR OPENS. DR. SORI COMES OUT WITH A CAREGIVER AND A CALM CAT IN A CARRIER.] 

NOX: Is the patient stable?

DR. SORI: The cat is ready to go home.

[NOX CUES A REVEAL. BENI'S CAMERA FINDS THE CAT PAWING AT THE CLOSED CARRIER DOOR. THE CAREGIVER WALKS PAST THE REVEAL MARK TOWARD THE CHECKOUT COUNTER. NOX TURNS THE CAMERA TO FOLLOW AND CAPTURES ONLY THE BACK OF THE CARRIER.]

UMA: The follow-up is scheduled for next week.

NOX: That is the ending?

DR. SORI: It is the plan.

[NOX BEGINS A VOICE-OVER ABOUT A MYSTERY ILLNESS. DR. SORI HOLDS UP THE CLINIC'S BASIC CHECKOUT SHEET.]

DR. SORI: It was a routine check. The cat is going home.

[NOX LOOKS AT THE CAMERA. BENI TILTS IT DOWN TO THE CHECKOUT SHEET, WHERE THE NEXT APPOINTMENT IS ALREADY PRINTED.] 

[THE PRINTER BEEPS. NOX REACHES FOR A SOUND EFFECT, BUT THE LABEL IS READY. HE HANDS IT TO UMA.]

UMA: Thanks.

[THE CAMERA CREW MOVES ITS CHAIRS BACK AGAINST THE WALL. A PATIENT AND CAREGIVER WALK THROUGH THE CLEAR PATH TO THE EXIT.] 

### Tag

[NOX LOOKS AT HIS AUDIO BOARD. THE BUTTON MARKED "HEARTBEAT" IS NOW LABELED "PRINTER." HE PRESSES IT. IT MAKES A SMALL PAPER-RATTLE SOUND.] 

NOX: The suspense is printing.
