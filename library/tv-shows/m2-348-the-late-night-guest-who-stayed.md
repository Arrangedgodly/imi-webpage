# The Late-Night Guest Who Stayed

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows, where the host keeps a guest onstage for one more segment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A talk show guest tries to leave after her interview, but the host keeps inventing new segments around her ordinary trip home.

Cast: Host Miri, a baboon; guest Nara, a macaque; bandleader Odo, a gibbon; producer Piri, a capuchin.

Episode: "One More Question"

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO - NIGHT

Miri interviews Nara about her new garden guide. Nara closes the book.

MIRI: One more question.

NARA: I have to catch the shuttle.

Odo plays a dramatic chord. Piri signals that the shuttle leaves in five minutes.

ACT ONE

Miri asks Nara about her favorite plant. Nara says the plum tree. Miri asks for a story. Nara says she planted it with her aunt. The audience applauds. Miri asks whether Nara will stay for the "favorite leaf" segment.

NARA: I do not have a favorite leaf.

MIRI: We'll find one.

Piri whispers that the shuttle departs in four minutes. Miri tells the audience they will take a short break. Odo plays a longer chord than needed.

ACT TWO

Miri brings out a tray of leaves. Nara identifies the plum leaf by its shape. Odo asks if the band can play a leaf theme. He plays a few notes. Nara says the leaves are from different plants.

Miri announces a surprise game: Nara must guess which leaf came from her own garden. She recognizes it by a small tear near the edge. Miri asks if she can keep the leaf. Nara says it belongs to the garden.

Piri checks the clock. The shuttle has already left. Nara says there is another one in twenty minutes. Miri offers to call a cart. Nara says she can walk.

Miri asks if they can do a walking interview. Nara agrees to answer one question on the way out. Miri follows her to the studio door, then adds a "goodnight handshake" segment. Nara shakes hands and starts walking again.

ACT TWO-B

INT. STUDIO EXIT - TWENTY MINUTES LATER

Piri stands by the studio exit with the shuttle schedule. Nara waits at the threshold with her closed garden guide. Miri is at the desk, tracking the curb camera; the next shuttle is now five minutes away.

PIRI: The shuttle is five minutes away. We have one chance to get Nara outside before the audience question becomes a segment.

MIRI: It is already a segment. The question is on a card.

NARA: I can answer it while walking.

MIRI: The audience cannot see you walk if the camera stays here.

NARA: Then let the camera move.

[THE CAMERA OPERATOR PUSHES THE CAMERA. ITS CABLE CATCHES ON ODO'S PIANO BENCH.]

ODO: The exit has acquired a soundtrack.

MIRI: Odo, play us a walking cue.

ODO: I can play a short one.

[HE PLAYS THREE NOTES. MIRI RAISES THE AUDIENCE QUESTION CARD.]

MIRI: The question is, "What is the guest's next destination?"

NARA: The shuttle stop.

MIRI: The audience has guessed correctly.

PIRI: They guessed before the question was finished.

MIRI: That is a record. We need a winner's close-up.

NARA: I am still walking.

[THE CAMERA ROLLS WITH HER. THE CABLE TIGHTENS. ODO'S BENCH SLIDES SIX INCHES, AND HE KEEPS PLAYING TO STAY ON BEAT.]

ODO: The band is moving too.

MIRI: Then this is our first mobile audience award.

PIRI: The shuttle is at the curb now.

MIRI: One final question: what do you hope the driver knows about your destination?

NARA: That it is the shuttle stop, not a studio segment.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. MIRI LOWERS THE CARD.]

[THE SHUTTLE DRIVER TAPS THE HORN. MIRI LOOKS AT THE CURB MONITOR.]

MIRI: We have a live sound from the destination.

NARA: That is the driver telling us the door is open.

ODO: I can match the horn with a chord.

PIRI: Do not. The driver will think the shuttle is part of the band.

[ODO PLAYS A CHORD. THE DRIVER WAVES, MISTAKING IT FOR A SIGNAL.]

MIRI: The shuttle has acknowledged the music.

NARA: It has acknowledged the door.

PIRI: If we hold it any longer, the next segment will be an empty parking space.

MIRI: That would be a first for late-night television.

ACT THREE

EXT. STUDIO CURB - NIGHT

Nara steps onto the shuttle. Piri holds the door while the camera operator stops at the curb, short of its cable. Miri waves from the studio entrance rather than calling the guest back.

NARA: Good night. I made the shuttle.

MIRI: And we made our first interview that arrived at its destination.

PIRI: We could have done that five minutes ago.

MIRI: Then the shuttle would have missed its close-up.

TAG

The shuttle pulls up outside the studio.

Nara boards. Miri waves through the window.

PIRI: You could have let her go five minutes ago.

MIRI: Then we would have missed the ending.

[MUSIC: LATE-NIGHT THEME.]
