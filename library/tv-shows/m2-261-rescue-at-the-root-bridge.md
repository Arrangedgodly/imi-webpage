# Rescue at the Root Bridge

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rescue drama television, where a stuck cart wheel is treated as an urgent cliffside extraction*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: The rescue crew responds to a cart wedged on a root bridge, while the host narrates the operation as a high-risk extraction despite the shallow stream below.

Cast: Rescue lead Nara, macaque; medic Odo, baboon; spotter Piri, capuchin; host Vela, gibbon; cart owner Seli, lemur.

## Episode: "Wheel in the Water"

### Cold Open

[ROOT BRIDGE OVER A SHALLOW STREAM. A CART WHEEL IS CAUGHT.]

VELA: The cart is on the edge.

SELI: It is on the bridge.

NARA: Nobody move the cart.

ODO: The water is ankle deep.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[NARA SETS A SAFETY LINE.]

Nara checks the bridge roots and asks Seli to step off the cart. Seli does so. Odo checks her for injury.

ODO: No injury.

VELA: The patient is stable.

SELI: I am the cart owner.

Piri places a marker beside the wheel and says the cart's axle is caught between two roots. Nara asks for a strap. Vela asks the camera operator to zoom in on the drop.

NARA: There is no drop.

VELA: The stream is below.

NARA: The stream is shallow.

Seli says the cart carries a crate of apples. Odo asks if the apples are secure.

SELI: They are in a basket.

The basket slides slightly. Nara asks everyone to hold position while Piri secures the load.

### Act Two

[THE CREW PREPARES A PULLING STRAP.]

Nara attaches the strap to the cart frame. Piri anchors the other end to a tree. Odo tests the tension. The cart does not move.

VELA: The rescue has stalled.

NARA: The strap is not tight yet.

ODO: It needs a second anchor.

Seli says she can lift the wheel while Nara pulls. Nara declines; Seli should stay clear until the cart is stable.

SELI: I know the cart.

NARA: And I know the bridge.

Piri finds a loose root that can be moved by hand. Nara asks him not to pull it, since it supports the bridge. Vela narrates the decision as "a critical structural choice."

PIRI: We can lift the cart six inches.

NARA: Then we can roll the wheel backward.

Odo adds a wooden block under the frame. The cart shifts. Apples press against the basket's side.

SELI: The cargo is moving.

ODO: The basket is held.

Vela asks for a close-up of Seli's face. Seli looks at the cart, not the camera.

[THE RESCUE CREW CHECKS THE BRIDGE FROM BOTH BANKS.]

Piri places two markers to show where the cart can roll. The rear wheel is stable, but the front wheel is wedged against a root. Odo asks Seli to confirm the basket is tied down.

SELI: It is tied to the cart.

NARA: The cart is what we are moving.

Seli unties the basket and passes it to Odo. One apple rolls free. Piri catches it before it reaches the water.

PIRI: Loose cargo secured.

Vela asks if the rescue team will use the winch. Nara says a winch would pull the cart sideways. They need to lift the frame just enough to clear the root.

ODO: The strap is too high.

NARA: Move it beneath the axle.

Piri adjusts it. The cart shifts slightly, then stops.

SELI: It moved.

NARA: The wheel is still caught.

Vela calls this a critical setback. Odo asks for a second block to keep the frame level.

### Act Three

[NARA AND PIRI COUNT TO THREE.]

They lift the frame just enough for Odo to place a plank beneath the wheel. Nara eases the strap. The wheel rolls backward and comes free.

[SFX: WOOD SCRAPE, CART SETTLES.]

VELA: The extraction is complete.

SELI: The cart is back on the bridge.

NARA: The bridge is stable. Walk the cart slowly.

Seli pushes it across. The basket stays in place. Odo checks the apples; none have fallen.

ODO: No loss.

PIRI: No injury.

NARA: No bridge damage.

Vela asks if Seli has anything to say about the rescue. Seli says she will use the wider trail next time. Nara points to the map; the wider trail is marked beside the bridge.

SELI: I thought it was longer.

NARA: It is.

### Tag

[THE TEAM STANDS BESIDE THE CART ON LEVEL GROUND.]

VELA: Another successful rescue.

ODO: No one needed rescuing.

SELI: The cart needed a mechanic.

NARA: It needed a clear path.

PIRI: The wheel did.

NARA: The wheel is fine.

Seli rolls the cart away. A loose apple falls into the basket.

VELA: A final complication.

SELI: That apple was already loose.

[SFX: BASKET RUSTLES. STREAM FLOWS.]
