# The Case of the Silent Noticeboard

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedurals that deploy forensic teams and elaborate theories for a harmless mystery*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Detectives investigate why every notice on the community board has disappeared overnight.

Cast: Detective Nara, a macaque; Detective Odo, a baboon; technician Pava, a gibbon; witness Kito, a capuchin.

Episode: "The Board Went Blank"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MORNING

Nara and Odo stare at an empty noticeboard.

ODO: The board was full last night.

NARA: Now it is blank.

Pava enters carrying a stack of paper.

PAVA: I took everything down to repaint it.

Odo puts on gloves.

ODO: Do not contaminate the scene.

ACT ONE

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - CONTINUOUS

Pava says the board had old tacks, faded notices, and a loose corner. She removed the papers before repainting. Odo asks whether she had permission. Pava shows a work order signed by the hall manager.

Nara examines the board. The paint is wet. She asks why the crew is investigating if there is a work order. Odo says they need to verify no notice is missing.

Kito arrives to post a meeting announcement. He says he left a sign about the garden cleanup yesterday. Pava finds it in the paper stack. Kito confirms it is intact.

ODO: The first item has been recovered.

NARA: It was never lost.

Pava says the papers are sorted by date. The newest are on top. Odo checks the pile for fingerprints. Nara tells him not to touch wet paint.

ACT TWO

INT. STORAGE ROOM - LATER

The detectives interview the hall manager by phone. He confirms the repainting was scheduled. Odo asks whether the work order could be forged. The manager says he wrote it.

Pava shows the paint tray. The color is the same as the board's original color. Odo asks if the repainting was a cover. Nara points out that the paint is still wet and the brush is beside the wall.

Kito says he saw Pava carrying a stack of papers. Odo calls him an eyewitness. Kito says he helped carry them. Nara says that makes him a participant.

The detectives compare the paper stack to last week's photo of the board. Every notice is present. One flyer is folded in half. Odo asks if it was altered. Pava says it was too large for the tray and had to be folded for storage.

Kito notices the board has a new cork strip at the bottom. Pava says the old strip was peeling. She replaced it. Odo checks the strip for a hidden message. It is cork.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

Pava dries the board. Nara returns the notices one at a time. Odo insists they be placed in exact chronological order. Kito says the events are sorted by date already. The detectives use thumbtacks to secure the papers. One tack is bent. Odo baggies it.

NARA: It is a bent tack.

ODO: It may have held a clue.

Pava asks if she can finish repainting. Odo says the board is still under observation. Nara reads the work order again and closes the case file.

Pava turns over each notice to confirm it has no hidden writing. Nara asks if there is any reason to suspect a crime. Odo says the board was cleared without their knowledge. The hall manager's work order resolves that. They compare the date of the order with the calendar; it was posted three weeks earlier, beneath the old paint schedule. Kito says he read the notice and forgot. Odo calls that a lapse in memory. Nara asks him to stop turning every explanation into a suspect. Pava finds one tack on the floor and places it back in the supply cup. Odo asks if it is the same bent tack from the board. Pava shows that this one is straight. The forensic kit remains unopened. Nara signs the closure report, and Odo files the intact work order with it.

Kito asks if the case is closed. Nara says the papers were never missing. Odo files the report without a sting.

The notice is back in place.

ACT THREE

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

The board is restored. The garden cleanup notice is back where Kito posted it. Pava signs off on the repair. Odo stamps the report "NO CRIME."

Kito asks if he can post a new notice. Odo says yes, but first it must be logged. Kito hands him a paper announcing tomorrow's meeting about the board. Odo places it at the top.

TAG

Nara looks at the board.

NARA: It is full again.

ODO: We should keep it under observation.
