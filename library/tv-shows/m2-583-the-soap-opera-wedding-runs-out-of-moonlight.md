# The Soap Opera Wedding Runs Out of Moonlight

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: soap-opera cliffhangers, secret entrances, and a lighting cue that threatens to reveal the wrong secret*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Celeste, an orangutan bride; Milo, a gibbon groom; director Vex, a baboon; lighting tech Kiri, a macaque; actor Piri, a capuchin playing the aunt.

COLD OPEN

INT. SOAP SET CHAPEL - NIGHT

Milo lifts the veil. Celeste's face is in shadow.

MILO: I can finally see the truth.

The moonlight lamp clicks off.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The long-running soap opera is filming a wedding finale on a chapel set. Celeste and Milo are playing characters who have hidden a family secret for thirty episodes. Director Vex wants the audience to think the wedding may be interrupted by Celeste's aunt. Actor Piri is in the wings holding the aunt's dramatic letter. Lighting tech Kiri warns that the moonlight lamp is on a timer and will shut down before the vows.

VEX: We need one last stare before the blackout.

KIRI: It is not a blackout cue. The lamp overheats if we leave it on.

CELESTE: My character can be surprised in ordinary light.

MILO: My character has never been surprised in ordinary light.

PIRI: I am the aunt. I have arrived with a letter.

VEX: Stay behind the door until the music swells.

PIRI: The door is painted on.

The chapel has one real entrance behind a pillar. Vex tells Piri to wait there while he directs Celeste and Milo to the altar. Piri walks to the painted door, reads the label "EXIT," and tries the handle. The handle is also painted.

PIRI: I have found the family secret. It is a flat wall.

VEX: Hold for the reveal.

KIRI: Hold for the lamp check.

ACT TWO

Kiri turns the moonlight lamp off and examines its cooling fan. The lamp is safe, but its automatic timer has already recorded one overheat warning. Vex wants to restart it for the scene. Kiri says it needs to cool before the next use. The director can keep the blue effect with a reflective panel, as long as nobody places it near the warm housing.

CELESTE: Could the secret letter arrive in the kitchen scene instead?

VEX: The wedding is the finale.

MILO: The wedding can end without an electrical moon.

PIRI: I can enter through the real door holding the letter.

VEX: The aunt must appear where the audience expects the painted door.

PIRI: The audience expects the door to open.

Kiri takes a cue card and redraws the entrance blocking. Piri will enter behind the altar pillar, read the letter, and stop before the camera line. The reflector will catch a soft blue wash from a cool set light, not from the overheated lamp. Vex tries the new frame on the monitor. It looks less like a moon and more like a refrigerator.

VEX: The blue is too domestic.

KIRI: The blue is safe.

MILO: My character has lived in this house for thirty episodes. A refrigerator is emotionally plausible.

Celeste reads the letter. It reveals that the aunt kept the wedding rings in a drawer, not that the bride and groom are relatives. Vex says it lacks enough shock. Piri proposes a second page with a genuine family confession. Celeste asks whether it is true in the story.

PIRI: It says the aunt sold the chapel's silver spoons to buy a dramatic hat.

CELESTE: That is a secret, and it explains the hat.

VEX: The letter can change everything.

MILO: It can change the cutlery inventory.

The cast rehearses the entrance. Piri uses the real doorway, the reflector gives a blue edge, and Milo turns at the cue instead of staring into darkness. Vex watches the take without asking for a second interruption.

ACT THREE

The wedding scene begins. Celeste and Milo exchange vows. Piri enters through the actual chapel door with the letter. She reads the confession about the spoons and reveals a feathered hat from under her shawl.

PIRI: I sold the silver to buy this. I regret nothing except the spoon with the bent handle.

CELESTE: That spoon was the family heirloom.

MILO: Then we shall begin married life with one fewer spoon.

VEX: Hold. That is the cliffhanger.

KIRI: It is the ending of this episode. The next one can search the pantry.

Celeste and Milo finish the vows. The set light gives them a cool blue edge. The moonlight lamp stays off until it cools, and Kiri tags it for maintenance after the take. Vex lowers the music instead of adding another thunderclap.

PIRI: Should I keep the hat on?

CELESTE: It is the only piece of silverware-adjacent evidence.

MILO: Let it be our something borrowed.

The cast exits through the real chapel door. Piri carries the letter and hat with her; the painted entrance stays flat.

FINAL IMAGE: The newlyweds hold hands beneath the safe blue set light while one bent spoon glints on the signing table.
