# The Auction Has a Reserve Spoon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised antiques auctions, breathless appraisals, surprise bidding, and hosts who confuse family value with resale price*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: An auction show tries to sell a chipped serving spoon, while its owner and the appraiser disagree about what makes it valuable.

Cast: Host Vex, a macaque; appraiser Miri, a lemur; owner Odo, an orangutan; bidder Tavi, a capuchin; and auctioneer Palla, a gibbon.

## Episode: "Going Once"

### Cold Open

[AUCTION STUDIO. A SPOON SITS ON A VELVET PILLOW.]

VEX: This spoon could change the market.

ODO: It measures soup.

Palla lifts it. The pillow folds in half and drops the spoon into a bowl.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

Miri examines the spoon under a magnifier. It is stainless steel, slightly bent, with a blue paint spot on the handle. Odo says it belonged to his grandmother and was used to serve stew at family picnics. Vex introduces the object as a possible culinary heirloom. Miri estimates that a similar spoon would sell for a few coins at a secondhand shop.

VEX: The estimate is the beginning of the story.

MIRI: It is also the estimate.

Palla asks for a dramatic close-up. The camera light reflects off the spoon and blinds the bidders. Miri rotates the spoon until the glare disappears. Vex calls the move a restoration. Odo says she simply turned it around. The camera zooms until the spoon fills the screen. The blue paint spot looks like a continent. Vex calls it a map. Odo says it came from the picnic table. Tavi, sitting among the bidders, raises his number because he wants the bowl in which the spoon landed.

### Act Two

The bidding begins. Palla announces an opening price above Miri's estimate. Nobody bids. Vex asks Odo how he feels watching his grandmother's object enter the market. Odo says he is mostly worried that the velvet pillow will slide off the display table. It slides; Miri catches it.

Tavi bids one coin. Palla asks whether anyone will top the offer. Vex points to a bidder who has not raised a paddle. The bidder shakes their head. Palla describes the silence as a heated contest. Odo asks if he can withdraw the spoon. Vex says the episode needs a final bid. Miri notes that the owner can choose not to sell.

Tavi explains that he wants the spoon because the handle fits his old picnic bowl, which has a chip in the matching spot. Odo recognizes the bowl from a family photo. He asks to see it. Tavi holds up a picture: the spoon is beside a bowl painted blue around its rim. The spot on the spoon came from that bowl during a picnic cleanup.

VEX: A matching set.

MIRI: Two objects that were used together.

Odo offers to sell the spoon only if Tavi brings the bowl to the community picnic next week. Palla tries to restart bidding, but Tavi accepts the condition. Vex asks the appraiser to value the pairing. Miri says she can assess the spoon's market price, not the family's agreement.

The production displays two numbers: ESTIMATED VALUE and PICNIC VALUE. The first is small; the second has no numeric amount. Vex tries to make the second look like a record sale. Miri crosses out the graphic and writes "not for sale" beside it. Palla calls the bid closed. Tavi pays Odo the one coin and receives the spoon in a paper sleeve rather than a velvet box.

Before the sale closes, Miri asks to inspect the spoon's bend. It still rests flat enough to serve soup, but it will not sit straight on the velvet pillow. Palla turns the pillow over and uses the firm underside as a display stand.

### Act Three

The show stages its closing shot. Odo puts the spoon beside Tavi's bowl. The handle fits the chip. Tavi says he will bring both to the picnic. Vex asks Odo for a final reflection. Odo says the spoon is worth more at the table than under a camera. Miri approves the appraisal as a statement about use, not price.

Palla asks the audience to applaud the sale. They do, then look to see whether the spoon has been packaged. Tavi carefully wraps it in a napkin. The napkin has the same blue stripe as the bowl. Vex says the show has found a hidden clue. Odo recognizes it as a napkin from the same picnic set.

MIRI: Please do not start another auction.

VEX: We have no more objects.

Palla rolls the velvet pillow away before it can become a lot.

### Tag

[STUDIO.]

Vex picks up the empty bowl. Miri tells him it belongs to the production kitchen. He puts it down gently.

VEX: Not everything has a price.

PALLA: The bowl rents by the hour.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
