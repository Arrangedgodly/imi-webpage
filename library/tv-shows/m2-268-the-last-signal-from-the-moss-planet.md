# The Last Signal from the Moss Planet

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: science-fiction anthology television, where an alien message turns out to be a moss bed drying out*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: A night crew detects a rhythmic light under a fallen log and prepares for first contact, while the field botanist identifies a drying patch of moss.

Cast: Host Varo, baboon; technician Nara, macaque; botanist Seli, capuchin; scout Odo, gibbon; camera operator Piri, lemur.

## Episode: "The Green Pulse"

### Cold Open

[FOREST AT NIGHT. A GREEN LIGHT PULSES UNDER A LOG.]

VARO: Something is signaling from beneath the forest floor.

SELI: It is moss.

NARA: The light is pulsing.

ODO: I can see it.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW STANDS BESIDE THE LOG.]

Varo asks Nara to record the signal. She counts a pulse every few seconds. Seli explains that the moss glows faintly where the ground is damp.

VARO: The planet is communicating.

SELI: The moss is reflecting light from the camera.

PIRI: I have turned off the camera light.

The glow fades. Odo switches on a lantern; the green spot returns.

ODO: The message has resumed.

NARA: The lantern is shining on the moss.

Varo asks Seli to translate. Seli says the moss is dry on one side and needs water. Varo asks if it could be an instruction from an unknown intelligence.

SELI: It could be a sign that the ground is dry.

### Act Two

[THE CREW SETS A SMALL CUP OF WATER BESIDE THE LOG.]

Nara pours a little water near the moss, not directly on it. The green glow becomes brighter in the lantern light.

VARO: It has answered.

SELI: Water changed how the light reflects.

ODO: The response is immediate.

Seli lifts a corner of the log to check the damp soil. A beetle walks out. The crew jumps back.

[SFX: LOG SHIFTS.]

VARO: A visitor from the planet.

SELI: A beetle from under the log.

Nara asks everyone to step away so the beetle can return. Varo suggests an interview. Seli says the beetle is not part of the production.

Odo places the lantern at different angles. The glow changes from bright green to nearly invisible.

PIRI: The signal is directional.

NARA: The light is directional.

[THE CREW CARRIES A SECOND LANTERN TO THE LOG.]

Varo asks Odo to shine it from the opposite side. The moss no longer glows. Nara moves the light slowly; the green reflection appears only at one angle.

VARO: The signal has a preferred direction.

SELI: Reflection depends on where you stand.

PIRI: We can mark the angle.

Seli puts a small stone at the camera position. Odo moves one step left. The glow vanishes. He returns to the stone; it reappears.

ODO: We have found the exact location.

NARA: We have found where the camera light works.

Varo calls for a dramatic close-up. Piri's lens catches moisture on the log, making a bright orb.

VARO: A second signal.

SELI: That is a drop of water on the lens.

Nara wipes the lens. The orb disappears. Varo still wants to interview the moss, so Seli asks what question he would ask.

VARO: Why are you glowing?

SELI: The lantern.

VARO: The answer is too short.

Seli points out that the field note can be longer than the answer. She records the moisture, angle, and light source in the notebook.

### Act Three

[THE CREW RECORDS A FINAL EXPERIMENT.]

They cover the lantern with a cloth. The glow disappears. They uncover it; the moss shines again. Seli asks Varo to announce the result plainly.

VARO: We found a green light beneath the log.

SELI: Caused by moss reflecting our lantern.

VARO: And the beetle?

SELI: It went back under the log.

Odo asks if they should mark the location for future observation. Seli places a small wooden marker near the damp patch, outside the moss.

NARA: The site is recorded.

VARO: First contact has been established.

SELI: No.

VARO: First observation, then.

[THE CREW PACKS THE CAMERAS.]

Seli checks the log and finds the observation note was recorded under "light test."

VARO: Can we title it "Message from the Moss"?

SELI: We can title it "Moss Reflects Light."

ODO: That is less mysterious.

NARA: It is more accurate.

Piri turns off the lantern and lets the crew leave the log undisturbed.

### Tag

[THE CAMERA LIGHT TURNS OFF.]

The forest is dark. A faint glow remains for a moment, then fades.

PIRI: The signal is gone.

SELI: The lantern is off.

VARO: We will return at dawn.

Odo picks up the lantern and walks away. Its light sweeps across the moss again.

VARO: It has returned.

SELI: We are carrying the source.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS FADE, NIGHT INSECTS.]
