# Call Now, the Fern Is Listening

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night product demonstrations that create urgency and implausible uses for ordinary household objects*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Host Doro must sell a self-watering planter before the midnight shopping slot ends. Horticulturist Vela tries to explain its actual limits as the producer keeps adding countdowns, bundles, and celebrity endorsements from a fern that cannot speak.

Cast: Doro, a ring-tailed lemur host; Vela, a mandrill horticulturist; Kiko, a capuchin producer; and Fern, a potted fern with a prominent leaf.

## Episode: "The Thirsty Hour"

### Cold Open

[SHOPPING STUDIO. A FERN SITS ON A TABLE BESIDE A CLEAR WATER RESERVOIR. A COUNTDOWN CLOCK SHOWS 18 MINUTES.] 

DORO: This planter waters itself while you are away.

VELA: It releases water gradually through a wick. You still fill the reservoir.

[KIKO POINTS A CAMERA AT THE FERN.]

KIKO: Can it endorse the bundle?

DORO: The fern is our guest expert.

VELA: It is a plant.

[KIKO PLACES A TINY MICROPHONE IN THE SOIL. THE LEAF SAGS OVER IT.] 

DORO: Fern says, "Check the water level." 

VELA: That is the sign I brought.

### Act One

[DORO HOLDS UP THE PLANTER AND A BOX OF EXTRA WICKS.]

DORO: Buy one planter and receive three replacement wicks.

VELA: For different pots, not for emergencies.

KIKO: Our graphic says "Never Water Again." 

VELA: That is false.

[KIKO CROSSES OUT THE WORD "NEVER" AND WRITES "OCCASIONALLY." THE GRAPHIC NOW READS "OCCASIONALLY WATER AGAIN."]

DORO: That is less urgent.

VELA: It is more true.

[THE CLOCK TICKS LOUDLY. KIKO ADDS A SECOND CLOCK FOR THE BONUS OFFER. IT COUNTS DOWN AT A DIFFERENT SPEED.]

DORO: Which clock is real?

KIKO: The one nearest the camera.

VELA: Neither clock controls the wick.

[DORO TESTS THE RESERVOIR. IT IS EMPTY. HE READS THE FILL LINE AND POURS WATER TO IT.]

### Act Two

[KIKO PLACES THE FERN ON A SMALL TURNTABLE. A LABEL READS "WORKS WHILE YOU TRAVEL."]

DORO: Take it on a weekend trip.

VELA: Keep the planter upright and don't leave it in a hot car.

[KIKO SPINS THE TURNTABLE. WATER SLOSHES UP THE SIDE, BUT THE LID HOLDS. DORO STEADIES THE POT.] 

DORO: Our product survives a studio spin.

VELA: We did not test it for travel.

[KIKO WHEELS IN A SUITCASE. THE RESERVOIR FITS INSIDE, BUT THE FERN DOES NOT.]

DORO: The plant has exceeded its carry-on allowance.

[VELA REMOVES THE PLANT AND SHOWS HOW THE WICK TOUCHES THE SOIL. KIKO SLIDES A SECOND WICK THROUGH THE DRAINAGE HOLE. IT DROPS ONTO THE FLOOR.] 

VELA: One wick is enough for this pot.

KIKO: We need a bonus demonstration.

DORO: We have demonstrated the bonus.

[A FLOOR RUNNER BRINGS A SMALL CACTUS FOR THE NEXT DEMONSTRATION. DORO STARTS TO POUR WATER INTO THE RESERVOIR. VELA STOPS HIM AND CHECKS THE CARE CARD.]

VELA: This cactus needs a different watering schedule.

KIKO: Can we say the planter handles every plant?

VELA: It holds water. It does not make decisions for you.

[DORO SETS THE CACTUS ASIDE AND POINTS OUT THE FILL LINE. KIKO PLACES A GLOWING STICKER OVER IT.]

DORO: That sticker hides the line.

[KIKO PEELS IT OFF. THE STICKER CLINGS TO HIS SLEEVE. HE FINISHES THE SEGMENT WITH "LIMITED OFFER" ON HIS CUFF.]

VELA: The label says minimum and maximum. Follow those marks.

DORO: We should sell the instructions with the planter.

KIKO: The instructions are already in the box.

[DORO OPENS A SAMPLE BOX. THE CARE CARD IS BURIED UNDER THREE PROMO COUPONS. VELA MOVES THE CARD TO THE TOP.]

VELA: Put the instructions where the buyer can find them.

KIKO: Then the coupons can be the bonus.

DORO: A bonus that does not cover the fill line.

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK REACHES ZERO. KIKO HITS A BUTTON. A NEW CLOCK APPEARS WITH 12 MINUTES LEFT.] 

DORO: The offer has been extended.

VELA: It was supposed to end at midnight.

KIKO: This is a different time zone.

VELA: The studio has one clock and two time zones now?

[DORO TURNS THE TIMER TOWARD THE CAMERA. IT SAYS "12:00:00" WITH NO LABEL.]

DORO: For tonight, the honest deal is one planter, one wick, and instructions that tell you when to refill it.

VELA: And use a pot suited to the plant.

[THE PHONE LIGHTS FLASH. KIKO HOLDS UP A CARD READING "LINES OPEN." DORO PICKS UP THE PHONE.] 

DORO: Hello, caller. Yes, the fern cannot confirm your order.

[KIKO TRIES TO ADD A "FERN-APPROVED" STICKER TO THE BOX. VELA PEELS IT OFF AND PUTS THE CARE CARD IN ITS PLACE.] 

### Tag

[THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK RETURNS TO ZERO. KIKO COVERS IT WITH A SMALL SIGN: "REFILL WHEN LOW."]

DORO: No limited-time mystery?

VELA: The mystery is why the fern has a microphone.

[THE FERN'S LEAF DROOPS OVER THE MICROPHONE BUTTON. ALOUD, THE SPEAKER PLAYS A SINGLE SOIL-MOISTURE BEEP.]

KIKO: We have an endorsement.
