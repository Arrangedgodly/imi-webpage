# The Segment That Kept Morning

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: morning television, cheerful segment pivots, live countdowns, and guests interrupted by the next topic*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A local morning show promises a rooftop garden guest three minutes to recruit volunteers, then keeps interrupting him with increasingly small studio segments.

Cast: Host Kiri, a macaque; cohost Velo, a gibbon; guest Domo, an orangutan gardener; weather reader Pex, a capuchin; floor director Nemi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. MORNING STUDIO - 6:58 A.M.

Kiri smiles at the camera beside a banner reading "GOOD MORNING." Velo holds a mug. Nemi raises a two-minute card.

KIRI: We have a lovely guest who needs volunteers for the rooftop garden.

NEMI: We have two minutes before weather.

VELO: The garden is on a roof. The weather is relevant.

ACT ONE

Domo sits at the studio table with a small planter and a sign-up sheet. He says the roof garden needs help moving soil boxes on Saturday. Kiri asks whether volunteers need experience. Domo says no, just closed shoes and a free hour. Velo leans toward the camera.

VELO: This is a straightforward request.

NEMI: Can you make it more exciting?

KIRI: Is there a surprise?

DOMO: There is soil.

Nemi raises a card: "SHOW THE BEFORE PHOTO." Kiri asks Domo to hold up the photo. It shows an empty rooftop. The screen behind them displays the garden after a gust knocked down the shade cloth. Domo points out the different day and says the roof is already repaired.

DOMO: That photo is from last week.

Pex enters with a weather map. Velo asks if there is rain on Saturday. Pex says it will be dry and breezy. Domo asks if the crew can bring clips for the shade cloth. Pex says the forecast does not include clips.

ACT TWO

Kiri reads a viewer comment asking if the garden grows tomatoes. Domo says there are herbs and beans, but no tomatoes yet. Velo asks him to taste a leaf. Domo refuses to eat from a plant that has not been identified on camera. Kiri asks if the garden can supply breakfast.

DOMO: It supplies a place to work.

Nemi signals a mug segment. Velo lifts his mug. Kiri holds up hers. Domo's planter sits between them. The camera zooms on the mugs and crops out the guest.

KIRI: Our mugs are locally made.

DOMO: Is mine available?

VELO: Yours is a planter.

KIRI: We have a beautiful new mug.

Domo points to the sign-up sheet. The mug segment has covered it with a saucer. Nemi moves the saucer, then raises a card: "THREE QUICK QUESTIONS." Kiri asks whether Domo is a morning person. He says he starts at six. Velo asks if he drinks tea. He says after work. Kiri asks if the garden has a mascot. Domo says the building's pigeons watch from the rail.

KIRI: Pigeons?

DOMO: They do not volunteer.

ACT TWO-B

Pex returns with a four-day forecast. Nemi asks him to stand beside the garden sign. The weather screen fills the monitor and hides the guest. Domo asks if the show can return to the volunteer invitation. Kiri lowers the weather card. Velo asks how many helpers are needed. Domo says four, to carry two soil boxes and secure the repaired shade cloth.

NEMI: We have forty seconds.

KIRI: How do viewers sign up?

DOMO: Come Saturday at nine in the morning. Meet at the east stair entrance, 18 Cedar Street. Closed shoes, one hour, and please scan the code or call the community center if you need directions.

The camera zooms to the QR code. It focuses on Velo's mug instead. Nemi nudges the lens; the code comes into view. One studio staffer scans it and signs up. Kiri asks if that counts. Domo says it does. Nemi signals the weather transition again. Pex politely waits until Domo finishes the address.

ACT THREE

Kiri repeats the Saturday time and meeting place. Velo puts down the mug and signs the volunteer sheet. Pex asks if he can bring clothespins. Domo says they need weather-rated clips; Pex can bring those if he has them. The show closes the segment without a jingle.

NEMI: We are over by twenty seconds.

KIRI: We have a volunteer.

VELO: Two volunteers.

Nemi looks at the camera, then points at herself. Domo hands her the pen. She signs too, explaining she wants to see the roof the show has mentioned all morning.

TAG

The next segment is a kitchen demonstration. Kiri arrives holding the planter, then realizes it is not the ingredient basket. She carries it back to Domo.

FINAL IMAGE: Domo leaves with three signed names and the planter, while the morning show resumes its weather map over an empty chair.
