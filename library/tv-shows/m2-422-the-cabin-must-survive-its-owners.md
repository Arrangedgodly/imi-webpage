# The Cabin Must Survive Its Owners

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home survival reality television that makes ordinary nesting and territory into an elimination contest*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Rell, a gibbon; builder Toma, a macaque; builder Vika, a capuchin; field producer Jori, a lemur; wildlife consultant Moss, a slow loris.

COLD OPEN

EXT. WOODLAND CLEARING - MORNING

[A camera crew points toward a half-built shelter. Vika sits on the roof, weaving grass through the rafters.] 

RELL: Two builders. One cabin. One night in the wilderness.

MOSS: This is a public picnic shelter.

[The camera swivels. A sign behind him reads "PARK SHELTER - OPEN DAILY." Theme music strikes.]

ACT ONE

RELL: Welcome to Last Cabin Standing, where a roof is only a roof if a camera can find it.

TOMA: We are making one dry place to sleep.

VIKA: We are making a nest platform above the damp ground.

RELL: And which one is the winning strategy?

MOSS: They can use the shelter. It's already built.

JORI: The show needs a build.

[She hands each builder a bundle of branches. Toma examines the shelter's existing bench. Vika tests a low branch, then chooses the higher side of the clearing.] 

TOMA: The bench is flat and dry.

VIKA: The roof is already above it.

RELL: But can the cabin survive its owners?

TOMA: The owners are standing here.

[The crew places two flags at opposite ends of the shelter.]

JORI: These mark your territories.

MOSS: The shelter is shared park property.

VIKA: Then the flags are decorative.

[She picks up one flag and uses its cord to lash a branch to the existing roof frame. Toma starts stacking branches around the bench to block the wind.]

ACT TWO

[The builders work. Vika climbs the shelter's outer post with practiced balance. Toma tries to keep his stack from slipping.] 

RELL: Vika is gaining height. Toma is gaining mass.

TOMA: The mass is a windbreak.

VIKA: The height is a place to sit without wet feet.

[The branch Vika has tied is secure, but the show crew hangs a red ribbon from it for a "danger marker." The ribbon catches on her hand as she reaches across. She freezes, then calmly untangles it.] 

VIKA: Your warning is blocking the work.

JORI: It is visually important.

MOSS: A red ribbon is not a safety plan.

[Rain begins. Toma's branch wall softens and falls inward. Vika's raised platform remains dry, but a camera cable drips onto it. She moves the cable to a lower branch.] 

RELL: The cabin has taken a hit.

TOMA: Your crew put a hole in the roof to mount a camera.

JORI: We can patch that in post.

MOSS: Water is not in post.

[Vika gestures to the open public shelter.]

VIKA: We should all move inside.

RELL: If both teams use one roof, how do we judge the winner?

TOMA: By who stays dry.

[He lays his remaining branches across the shelter's open side as a screen. Vika ties them with grass. Her quick knots hold. Toma places the bench beneath them. The crew huddles under the extended roof.]

ACT THREE

[Rain stops. Rell steps out from beneath the roof to deliver the verdict, but the ground around the cameras is muddy. Jori holds a clipboard over her head.] 

RELL: Each builder has created a viable cabin. The judges will score shelter, ingenuity, and dramatic hardship.

MOSS: The existing roof gets the shelter points.

RELL: We can't award the park.

TOMA: It was already keeping us dry.

VIKA: The grass knots held the camera cable. The camera cable held the red ribbon. The ribbon pulled down the extra flag.

[The flag drops onto Rell's podium. He picks it up.] 

RELL: The cabin has claimed the host.

JORI: That's the elimination marker.

[The crew raises the flag. A squirrel darts through the clearing and steals the ribbon from it. Everyone watches the squirrel climb the shelter post.] 

MOSS: Local resident has won the territory challenge.

RELL: Can we get that in close-up?

[The squirrel settles on Vika's dry platform, nibbling a twig. Vika shifts aside without complaint.] 

VIKA: It was available.

TOMA: I concede the upper floor.

[The crew packs their gear under the shared roof.]


[The show crew sets up a winner's platform beside the shelter. The ground is muddy, so the platform sinks on one side.]

JORI: We need a level stage for the final shot.

TOMA: Put it on the bench.

VIKA: The bench is part of the shelter.

RELL: We can make it the award podium.

[Moss tests the platform with one foot. It tilts. Vika knots a scrap of grass around its leg, while Toma wedges a flat branch underneath.]

MOSS: The builders have repaired the production.

JORI: Can we call it a challenge?

TOMA: It was a platform.

TAG

RELL: Next week, we test whether the cabin can survive a second night.

MOSS: The shelter is open all season.

[The squirrel drops a twig onto Rell's cue cards.]

RELL: And the reviews are already in.
