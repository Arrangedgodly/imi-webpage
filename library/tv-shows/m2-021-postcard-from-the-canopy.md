# Postcard from the Canopy

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentary*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A travel host visits a rainforest village with a camera crew, but the residents ask her to stop calling everyday places hidden gems and learn the paths people actually use.

Cast: Lani, a curious macaque host; Taro, her local gibbon guide; Esi, a village capuchin who runs a food stall; Mo, a quiet spider monkey carpenter; and Dex, the camera operator who prefers practical directions to scenic narration.

## Episode: "The Road with No Viewpoint"

### Cold open

EXT. FOREST PATH, MORNING

Lani faces the camera beside a sign that reads "SCENIC OVERLOOK, 2 KM." Taro points in the opposite direction.

TARO: The village is that way.

Lani gestures toward the overlook. The path ends at a mudslide. Dex lowers the camera.

DEX: We could film the village instead.

### Act one

The planned sunrise route washed out in a storm. Taro offers the lower path to the school, market, and ferry.

Esi meets them at her food stall. She sells breakfast to workers heading to the ferry. Lani asks what makes the village special. Esi says the stew is made with roots from the hillside and that the ferry only runs three times a day. She would like the show to include the ferry schedule, because visitors keep missing it and asking residents to carry them across.

Lani records a scenic introduction. She calls the market a hidden gem. Esi asks who hid it. Lani pauses, then tries again. She says the market is where people buy what they need before work. Dex films the stall's chalkboard menu and the ferry times. The shot is less sweeping, but it tells visitors what to do.

### Act two

The crew follows the river path past the school and Mo's carpentry yard. Mo repairs a bridge rail that visitors lean on for photographs.

Lani asks if the bridge can be fixed before filming. Mo says it can, but the town needs replacement bolts. Dex asks if they can help carry the timber. The crew sets the camera down and moves boards with Mo. Lani learns the bridge is maintained by a shared fund. Lani buys replacement bolts at the local hardware shop.

The crew reaches the ferry. It is a narrow boat with a low roof. Dex has to fold the tripod. The ferryman asks everyone to keep their bags away from the edge. Lani asks how long the crossing takes. The answer is six minutes in calm weather and longer if the current is strong. She records the information without calling it an adventure.

On the other bank, the crew reaches the alternate viewpoint. It looks over the canopy, but the village is not visible. Lani asks Taro if this is the place he meant. He says yes, but locals rarely come unless they are counting storm damage. She takes a short shot, then asks to return to the market before the ferry leaves.


[Taro turns the crew away from the washed-out ridge.]
LANI: Is the overlook ahead?
TARO: The route has collapsed.
DEX: We have another path.
LANI: Why wasn't it in the plan?
TARO: It is the village road.
LANI: That sounds better.
DEX: It has real directions.
TARO: Mind the slick board.
LANI: The sign says "viewpoint."
TARO: It points to the school.
LANI: Is that an error?
TARO: It gets children there.
DEX: The road is busy.
LANI: We should move aside.
TARO: The ferry is coming.
LANI: How often does it run?
TARO: Three times a day.
DEX: We'll show the schedule.
ESI: And the breakfast stall.
LANI: What should visitors know?
ESI: Don't block the queue.
LANI: Anything else?
ESI: Ask before filming people.
DEX: We can film the menu.
ESI: The prices are current.
LANI: This is useful.
TARO: It is ordinary life.
LANI: That's the story.
MO: The bridge rail is loose.
DEX: Can we help carry wood?
MO: After I check the footing.
LANI: Not before?
MO: I like my toes.
TARO: So does the camera.
DEX: The ferry is boarding.
LANI: No second take.
ESI: The boat won't wait.

### Act three

At the stall, Esi serves the crew a bowl of stew. Lani describes its ingredients and asks permission before filming the cooking. Esi says yes, as long as the recipe credit names her grandmother. Dex records her explaining how she checks the roots for bitterness before adding them.

The episode's closing segment lists practical information: when the ferry runs, which path is usable after rain, where visitors can buy food, and which bridge rail has been repaired. Lani says the village is not a backdrop waiting for travelers. It is a place people work and live, and a camera should not make their routes harder.

Taro thanks her for changing the plan. Lani thanks him for telling her the first route was closed before they carried the tripod up the ridge. Dex says he told her too. Lani admits she heard him after she finished the opening line.

The crew boards the ferry. Esi waves from the dock. The stew is gone, the camera is dry, and the repair bolts are in Mo's toolbox. Lani asks if they should shoot a second take of her goodbye. Dex says the ferry is already leaving.

### Tag

Lani holds a postcard toward the lens. It shows the repaired bridge and the market path.

LANI: Send this to someone who needs directions.

Taro turns the card over. There is no stamp.

TARO: The post office is behind the food stall.

They walk back together.
