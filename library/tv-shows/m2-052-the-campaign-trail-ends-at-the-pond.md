# The Campaign Trail Ends at the Pond

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local political campaigns, televised debates, and optimistic stump speeches*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: Two candidates compete to become keeper of the public pond, but a televised debate reveals that neither has visited the pond since the campaign began.

Cast: Candidate Rumo, a mandrill; candidate Nessa, a macaque; moderator Pell, an orangutan; campaign helper Jip, a capuchin; and voter Sali, a gibbon.

## Episode: "A Better Shore"

### Cold open

EXT. POND DEBATE STAGE, EVENING

[A platform stands near a pond. Rumo and Nessa face the audience. The water is low.]

PELL: Welcome to the debate for pond keeper.

RUMO: I will restore the pond to its former glory.

NESSA: I will make the shoreline accessible.

SALI: Have either of you been here this season?

[Both candidates pause.]

JIP: We have a prepared answer.

PELL: Please answer the question.

RUMO: My schedule has been full.

NESSA: I have been studying the shoreline from a distance.

SALI: The distance is the next tree.

### Act One

EXT. DEBATE PLATFORM

[Pell reads from a card. The candidates hold campaign signs.]

PELL: Candidate Rumo, how will you address the dry bank?

RUMO: We will bring water.

PELL: From where?

RUMO: A nearby source.

NESSA: The nearby source is the stream.

PELL: Candidate Nessa, what is your plan?

NESSA: We will plant reeds to protect the edge.

SALI: The reeds are already planted.

NESSA: Then we will protect them.

RUMO: My plan protects the water.

PELL: How?

RUMO: With a fence.

SALI: The pond is a shared watering place.

RUMO: The fence would keep it safe.

NESSA: It would keep everyone out.

JIP: We can revise the fence proposal.

RUMO: I will keep the fence but add a gate.

PELL: That is a door in a fence.

RUMO: A very accessible fence.

[The audience murmurs.]

PELL: Candidate Nessa, you promised a boardwalk.

NESSA: Yes.

PELL: Where would it begin?

NESSA: At the bank.

SALI: Which bank?

NESSA: The dry one.

SALI: The whole pond is low.

### Act Two

EXT. POND SHORE

[The debate pauses. Sali leads both candidates down to the water. Their shoes sink into mud.]

SALI: This is where the pond is used.

RUMO: The mud is deeper than it looks.

NESSA: The reeds are holding the edge.

JIP: We can move the platform.

PELL: The debate is still live.

SALI: Then debate the pond from here.

[They stand by a shallow inlet. A frog hops onto Rumo's campaign sign.]

RUMO: My sign is being used.

NESSA: The frog supports your platform.

PELL: What would you do first?

RUMO: I would check the water inlet.

NESSA: I would ask the neighbors to help clear it.

SALI: The inlet is blocked by a fallen branch.

JIP: We can clear that today.

PELL: Is that a campaign promise?

SALI: It's a branch.

[They lift it together. Water trickles into the pond.]

RUMO: The pond is filling.

NESSA: It was only a small blockage.

PELL: Why wasn't it cleared earlier?

SALI: We were waiting for someone to ask.

RUMO: I have been talking about water for weeks.

SALI: You were talking about water from the next tree.

### Act Three

EXT. DEBATE PLATFORM, NIGHT

[The candidates stand beside the now-flowing inlet. The moderator sets the cards down.]

PELL: We will each have one closing statement.

RUMO: I have heard the pond more clearly tonight.

NESSA: The best plan is to keep the inlet open, protect the reeds, and let the neighbors use the shore.

SALI: That's the first answer that includes the neighbors.

JIP: The campaign signs are muddy.

RUMO: We can clean them.

NESSA: We can use them to mark the path.

PELL: Are you both withdrawing your campaigns?

RUMO: No.

NESSA: We are still running.

SALI: You are standing still.

RUMO: We can run the maintenance crew together.

NESSA: The winner can choose the schedule.

PELL: The keeper is chosen by the neighborhood vote.

SALI: The vote is tomorrow.

JIP: We should print new signs.

RUMO: Make them say "Clear the inlet."

NESSA: And "Share the shore."

PELL: That sounds like one campaign.

RUMO: It is two candidates on one platform.

SALI: Be careful with the platform. It is still muddy.

[The candidates step down from the stage and help move it away from the water.]

### Tag

EXT. POND, MORNING

[A small group clears leaves from the inlet. Rumo and Nessa work beside Sali. Their campaign signs are propped up as a dry seat.]

SALI: This is more useful than a debate.

PELL: We can still broadcast it.

[The camera pans from the pond to the two candidates carrying the platform away.]
