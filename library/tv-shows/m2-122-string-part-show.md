# The String Is Part of the Show

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's puppet shows where every mishap becomes a lesson while adults insist the puppets are fully independent*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Nema wants the puppet Mip to learn a lesson about sharing. The puppet's loose string tangles with a set piece, and the children in the studio audience keep correctly identifying the stagehand's hand.

Cast: Nema, a lemur host; Mip, a cloth puppet; Odo, a macaque stagehand; and Lali, a young capuchin audience helper.

## Episode: "Can You Hold the Ladder?"

### Cold Open

[COLORFUL SET. NEMA STANDS BESIDE A CARDBOARD TREE. MIP PEEKS FROM BEHIND IT.]

NEMA: Mip, what do you need?

MIP: I need a ladder.

[A LADDER FALLS OVER. MIP'S STRING CATCHES ON THE TOP RUNG.]

MIP: I need a shorter ladder.

NEMA: A shorter ladder can still help.

LALI: Someone is pulling your ear.

[ODO'S HAND VANISHES BELOW THE SET.]

MIP: My ear has learned to let go.

### Act One

[NEMA TURNS TO THE AUDIENCE.]

NEMA: Today we are learning how to ask for help. Mip wants to hang a paper star on the tree.

NEMA: What could Mip use?

LALI: A helper.

MIP: I could use a helicopter.

NEMA: Do we have one?

[ODO'S HAND HOLDS UP A PAPER HELICOPTER. IT HAS A STRING TIED TO THE ROTOR.]

MIP: The helicopter needs help too.

[ODO SETS IT DOWN BEFORE THE STRING CAN TANGLE.]

MIP: I can do it alone.

[THE LADDER SLIDES. ODO'S HAND CATCHES IT FROM BELOW.]

NEMA: The ladder needs a helper.

MIP: I was helping it stay on the floor.

LALI: You need somebody to hold the bottom.

[NEMA INVITES LALI TO THE SET. ODO MOVES BEHIND THE CARDBOARD TREE.]

LALI: I can hold it.

[ODO'S ARM EMERGES FROM THE TREE, HOLDING THE LADDER.]

LALI: The tree has an arm.

NEMA: That is a special tree.

ODO: [FROM BEHIND THE SET] The tree has excellent grip.

MIP: I will climb.

NEMA: Puppets do not climb real ladders.

MIP: Then I will pretend to climb.

[ODO LIFTS MIP THREE INCHES. THE STRING WRAPS AROUND THE PAPER STAR. THE STAR PULLS DOWN A BRANCH.]

[NEMA ASKS THE CHILDREN TO POINT TO THE SAFE PLACE TO STAND. THEY POINT TO THE FLOOR, THE BENCH, AND THE CRAFT TABLE.]

NEMA: The floor is right. The bench is stable. The craft table is for paper.

[MIP'S STRING PULLS THE PAPER STAR TOWARD THE TABLE. ODO STEPS OUT AND UNHOOKS IT.]

LALI: Odo helped before the ladder fell.

NEMA: The show is teaching all of us.

MIP: The show could teach me how to untie knots.

[ODO HOLDS UP A KNOT CARD. IT IS A PICTURE OF A BOW.] 

MIP: I know that one. It goes on a present.

### Act Two

[THE SET CREW RESETS THE BRANCH. ODO CHECKS THE STRING.]

NEMA: When something gets tangled, we stop and ask for help.

MIP: I am asking the tree.

LALI: Ask Odo.

[ODO'S FACE APPEARS ABOVE THE CARDBOARD TREE.]

ODO: I can help untangle the string.

MIP: The tree talks.

ODO: It has a name tag.

[ODO MOVES THE STAR AWAY FROM THE STRING. THE BRANCH STAYS IN PLACE.]

NEMA: Now we can hang it together.

MIP: I hold the star, you hold the ladder, and the tree holds the set.

[THE CARDBOARD TREE TIPS FORWARD. ODO STEPS OUT AND CATCHES IT.]

LALI: The tree is not holding the set.

NEMA: Thank you for noticing. Odo, can you secure the base?

[ODO ADDS A SANDBAG BEHIND THE TREE. THE SET STOPS WOBBLING.]

MIP: Now I can climb.

NEMA: You can reach from the floor.

[MIP'S STRING LIFTS HIM. HE TOUCHES THE STAR TO THE BRANCH.]

MIP: I did it.

LALI: We did it.

MIP: We did it, and nobody had to stand on the ladder.

[THE PAPER STAR IS HUNG. MIP'S STRING IS FREE, AND ODO CHECKS THAT THE CLIP IS CLOSED.]

NEMA: What did the helpers do?

LALI: I held the ladder. Odo fixed the base. Mip kept the star from falling.

MIP: We all had a job.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. MIP BOWS; HIS STRING LIFTS HIM TOO HIGH, AND ODO GENTLY LOWERS HIM BACK TO THE BENCH.]

### Act Three

[NEMA SITS WITH MIP ON A BENCH. LALI HOLDS THE PAPER STAR.]

NEMA: What did Mip learn?

MIP: Ask for help before your string gets tangled.

NEMA: And what can a helper do?

MIP: Hold the ladder steady.

[THE LADDER ROLLS AWAY ON ITS WHEELS.]

LALI: Put the brakes on first.

[ODO REACHES UP AND LOCKS THE WHEELS.]

NEMA: Good spotting. What do we need to say?

MIP: Thank you, Odo.

ODO: You are welcome.

MIP: Also, my ear is still attached.

[ODO CHECKS THE STRING AND GIVES A THUMBS-UP.]

### Tag

[THE SHOW'S CLOSING SONG BEGINS. THE CARDBOARD TREE WAVES.]

NEMA: The tree is waving goodbye.

LALI: Odo is waving the tree.

[ODO'S HAND EMERGES AGAIN.]

MIP: The tree has learned to ask for help.
