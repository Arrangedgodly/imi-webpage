# Jury of the Jungle

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: panel comedy and quiz show convention*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: Four comedians compete to answer questions about animal facts, but the host's scoring system rewards good listening and changes whenever the panel gets too confident.

Cast: Saffy, a mandrill host with a hand bell; Mox, a fast-talking macaque comedian; Didi, a quiet loris with startlingly dry jokes; Len, a gibbon who makes elaborate diagrams; and Bo, a young baboon audience helper who knows the answer to everything except the one being asked.

## Episode: "The Question with No Prize"

### Cold open

INT. GAME STUDIO, NIGHT

Saffy rings her bell. A board reads: HOW MANY LEGS DOES A SPIDER HAVE?

MOX: Eight.

DIDI: Depends how many it has lost.

SAFFY: Correct, and the prize is a picture of a spider.

Len reveals the picture. It is a drawing of a very large paw. Bo in the audience raises his hand to correct the drawing. Saffy points him toward the answer desk.

### Act one

The first round asks whether all bats live in caves. Mox says yes. Didi points out that some roost in trees and buildings. Saffy awards her a point and Mox a paper cave.

Len draws a bat map over the scoreboard. Bo spots a fruit bat in a tree. Mox calls its sleeping position uncomfortable. Didi says he sleeps in a hammock.

The next question asks why flamingos stand on one leg. Len starts a long theory about balance and water temperature. Saffy asks him to show his sources. He pulls a book from beneath the podium, then realizes it is the studio's snack menu. Bo says flamingos may stand that way to conserve body heat, but scientists are still learning. The answer is accepted because he says what they know and what they do not.

### Act two

The next round asks Mox to explain how bees communicate. He dances a figure eight and faces the curtain.

Len diagrams the waggle dance and explains how bees use the sun and landmarks. Mox buzzes through the explanation. He gets zero points and a paper flower.

The panel debates whether a tail is a fifth hand. Didi explains that some monkeys can grip with theirs. Mox tries it with his hat and knocks over a vase.

The score board slips under Len's map. Saffy pauses the game and asks the panel to help find it. They check behind the curtain, under the bell table, and inside Mox's paper cave. Bo finds it taped to the back of the board. The points are correct, but the prize list has a line for "one free banana" crossed out.


INT. GAME STUDIO, ROUND TWO

Saffy sets the broken buzzer on the floor. Ruth gives each contestant a hand bell. Mox tests his twice before the question begins.

SAFFY: True or false?
MOX: I choose loud.
DIDI: That is not an answer.
LEN: I brought a chart.
BO: It is upside down.
LEN: The chart is migrating.
SAFFY: The chart gets a point.
MOX: For being lost?
DIDI: It is a map.
SAFFY: Next, a bat question.
MOX: All bats use caves.
BO: Some live in trees.
MOX: I meant indoor trees.
DIDI: Those are plants.
SAFFY: No point for that.
LEN: My diagram has wings.
BO: That is a leaf.
LEN: The leaf is a wing.
SAFFY: Explain bees.
MOX: They dance directions.
DIDI: To food, relative to sun.
MOX: I danced toward the curtain.
BO: The food is backstage.
LEN: Here is the diagram.
SAFFY: It's covering the score.
LEN: The score is behind it.
DIDI: The score is zero.
SAFFY: Next is the tail.
MOX: Mine holds a hat.
BO: Some tails can grip.
MOX: My hat is proof.
DIDI: Your hat fell.
MOX: Gravity disputed it.
SAFFY: Ask a real question.
MOX: Do parrots laugh?
DIDI: How do bats navigate?
LEN: When do herds turn?
BO: These are good questions.
SAFFY: The prize is research.
MOX: Can I keep the flower?
BO: It is paper.
MOX: It won't wilt.
DIDI: You finally noticed.

### Act three

The final round asks each comedian to make up a question they genuinely want answered. Mox wonders whether parrots know when a joke is funny. Didi asks how baby bats learn to find their way back to a roost. Len asks how animals decide when to change a migration route. Saffy admits she wants to know how sloths keep track of time while moving so slowly.

The show cannot answer all of them. The panel chooses one to research for next week's program. Bo picks the baby bats, because he once saw one land on the wrong branch and look surprised. Saffy awards the points to the question, not the person who asked it.

Mox objects that the question cannot spend the prize. Saffy hands the prize to Bo, who chooses a field trip to the bat center over a banana. Mox says he would also pick the field trip, then whispers that he will bring a banana in his pocket.

Didi closes with a joke about a bat that joined a choir because it could always find the note in the dark. It is not a great joke. The panel laughs because she knows it, and Mox gives her the paper flower.

### Tag

After the taping, Bo asks Saffy if the picture of the spider can be fixed. She gives him a marker. He draws eight legs onto the large paw.

The spider now has eight toes and an extra thumb.

DIDI: That spider is ready for work.

Saffy rings the bell. The audience has already gone home.
