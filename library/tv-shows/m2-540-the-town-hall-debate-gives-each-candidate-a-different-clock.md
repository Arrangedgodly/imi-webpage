# The Town Hall Debate Gives Each Candidate a Different Clock

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live debate broadcasts that use mismatched timers and moderator interruptions to manufacture conflict*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: moderator Lela, a gibbon; candidate Daro, an orangutan; candidate Nima, a macaque; timekeeper Suma, a lemur; producer Kavi, a capuchin; audience questioner Veli, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. TOWN HALL SET - NIGHT

Daro speaks at a podium. His timer shows one minute. Nima's shows three.

LELA: Please keep your answers brief.

DARO: I have thirty seconds left.

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Suma says the clocks were started at different times. Kavi asks her to leave them; the graphic makes the debate look heated. Lela gives Daro a warning bell. The bell rings while Nima speaks. Nima stops. Lela thanks Daro for yielding time. Daro says he was not speaking.

Veli asks why one candidate got less time. Kavi tells Lela to move to the next question. She says the clock needs to be reset first. Suma switches off both podium displays. Their separate broadcast graphic remains cached on the control-room preview. The audience boos the silence. Kavi cues a dramatic sting.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Suma compares the timer cables. Daro's clock is connected to the moderator mic; Nima's is connected to the camera tally light. Whenever the camera cuts to Nima, her timer advances. Kavi asks if they can keep the wide shot and the running clock. Suma says that would still count the audience and moderator's turns against the candidates.

Lela asks for a paper time card. She gives each candidate a card with the same two-minute allotment. Kavi asks Suma to make the numbers count down on screen. Suma refuses until she can verify both cards. Veli asks a question about the town's ferry schedule. Daro starts his answer. Kavi changes the camera to Nima; her cached broadcast graphic clock begins running again even though she is silent.

INT. TOWN HALL SET - MOMENTS LATER

Suma unplugs the timer display. Lela gives each candidate the same card and starts a kitchen timer where both can see it. Veli repeats the question. Daro answers until the bell rings. Lela asks him to finish the sentence; Suma stops the timer during the moderator's follow-up. Nima answers with her own allotted time. The bell rings once at the same point. Kavi asks for an interruption shot. Lela says no; both candidates have finished.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Suma places the paper cards beside the dead displays. Daro and Nima each receive one card with two minutes printed in the same type. Kavi asks the graphics operator to animate the numbers anyway. Suma checks them against a kitchen timer; the graphic loses a second on every loop. She deletes it. Lela asks whether the room can see the warning bell. Veli holds it up, then sets it where both candidates can see it. Kavi asks for separate warning times to build suspense. Lela gives the bell to Suma, who rings it only at the agreed halfway mark during a rehearsal. Both cards show equal time remaining.


ACT THREE

INT. TOWN HALL SET - LATER

Veli asks which candidate will fund the ferry schedule board. Daro describes a public notice at the pier. Nima proposes adding the time to the town website. Lela gives each an equal turn. Suma runs the kitchen timer and holds up a matching card at the halfway point. Neither candidate is cut off by a camera switch.

Kavi asks Lela to ring the warning bell early for a more tense exchange. She leaves it on the table. Daro finishes his answer just before the timer ends. Nima uses the full turn and explains how the schedule would be updated. Veli asks a follow-up; Lela gives the same short time to both. Suma blanks the old broadcast graphics; both podium clocks remain dark. Suma shows the paper cards to the audience. Both candidates have used the same number of minutes.

Kavi cues the dramatic sting again. The sound plays over an empty podium because the candidates have stepped down. The audience laughs. Lela invites them back to answer one final question, with a single short, equal closing turn each. They return, take identical cards and wait for Suma's signal. The moderator reads the question without interruption. The candidates answer in turn, and the kitchen timer is reset between them and rings at each identical agreed limit. Kavi reaches to restart it; Suma removes the battery. Lela thanks the audience and ends the debate on the written schedule, not a winner graphic.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE DEBATE

Suma labels both timers OUT OF SYNC and packs the paper cards. Kavi saves the empty podium shot as the closing bumper.

FINAL IMAGE: Two equal time cards sit side by side while the mismatched clocks remain dark.
