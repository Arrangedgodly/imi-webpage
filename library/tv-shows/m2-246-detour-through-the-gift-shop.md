# Detour Through the Gift Shop

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentary conventions, where the host insists every planned stop is a hidden gem*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Host Seli takes viewers on a journey across the village square, but producer Odo keeps redirecting the itinerary toward his sponsor's gift shop.

Cast: Host Seli, a capuchin; producer Odo, a baboon; guide Nara, a macaque; camera operator Pava, a gibbon; vendor Luma, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Long Way to the Fountain"

### Cold Open

[SELI STANDS BESIDE A SIGN: FOUNTAIN, 20 STEPS.]

SELI: Today we explore a destination most travelers overlook.

NARA: The fountain?

SELI: The route to the fountain.

ODO (OFF): First, a brief stop at the gift shop.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW WALKS THREE STEPS AND REACHES A GIFT SHOP.]

ODO: This is a hidden gem.

NARA: It has a sign.

LUMA: It is my shop.

SELI: What makes it special?

LUMA: It sells cups, postcards, and a small shelf of seed packets.

ODO: A cultural destination.

LUMA: The fountain is across the square.

Seli asks Luma to explain the seed packets. Luma says the seeds grow local flowers. Odo asks her to call them "journey souvenirs."

LUMA: They are seeds.

SELI: Where do they grow?

LUMA: In soil.

NARA: That is a useful travel fact.

Pava films a close-up of the postcard rack. The postcard shows the fountain.

SELI: We have reached the destination before the journey.

ODO: The postcard is a preview.

### Act Two

[THE CREW FOLLOWS A PATH AROUND THE SQUARE.]

Odo says the direct route is too ordinary. He leads them behind a bench and around a tree.

SELI: This is the scenic route.

NARA: It adds twelve steps.

ODO: It adds context.

Pava's camera catches a low branch, and she ducks. Seli keeps speaking while trying not to hit it.

SELI: The village canopy offers natural shade.

NARA: It also has branches at face height.

The path ends at a short fence. Odo says they can use it to reveal the fountain dramatically. Nara points to the gate.

NARA: We can open this.

ODO: That would end the suspense.

Seli opens the gate. The fountain is directly behind it.

SELI: Our journey has arrived.

ODO: We have not done the market loop.

LUMA: The market is closed.

ODO: Then we can admire the outside.

They walk the perimeter. Luma explains that vendors leave early on market days so they can bring produce home.

[THE CREW STOPS AT A BENCH. ODO OPENS A FOLDER OF SPONSOR NOTES.]

ODO: The sponsor wants one more stop.

SELI: The episode is about the fountain.

ODO: The cup shop is a partner.

NARA: It is closed.

Odo suggests filming the cup outside the shop. Seli asks Luma if she would like to be in the shot. Luma says she has already closed and is carrying the seed packets home.

PAVA: We can film the packets on the bench.

LUMA: They are for planting, not display.

Seli asks Odo whether the segment can end with the fountain instead. He says the sponsor expects a product mention.

SELI: Then mention the seeds. They are sold by the shop.

LUMA: They are locally grown.

Odo reads the label. It says "Plant at home."

ODO: A destination you can take with you.

NARA: A seed packet.

Seli holds the packet beside the fountain, then hands it back to Luma.

### Act Three

[THE CREW RETURNS TO THE FOUNTAIN.]

Seli tries to introduce the fountain, but Odo asks her to stand in front of it and describe the water.

SELI: It is flowing.

NARA: It is a fountain.

PAVA: The camera is low. It makes the water look tall.

Seli steps aside. The camera shows the full fountain, including a small puddle at the base. Odo calls it a "reflecting pool."

LUMA: It is a puddle.

ODO: It reflects.

Nara tests the route back to the shop. A cart blocks the walkway. She moves it to the side with the vendor's permission.

NARA: The direct path is clear now.

SELI: The journey has improved.

ODO: We should revisit the gift shop for the closing.

LUMA: I have closed.

Odo looks at the camera. Seli says the segment is ending at the fountain. Odo tries to hand her a branded cup.

SELI: I can hold it.

NARA: Don't drink while walking.

Seli sets it on the bench.

### Tag

[THE CREW STANDS BESIDE THE SIGN TO THE FOUNTAIN.]

SELI: From the shop, it is twenty steps.

NARA: It was twenty steps this morning.

ODO: And now?

SELI: Now it is a return journey.

Odo points toward the gift shop. The sign says "CLOSED."

LUMA: The hidden gem has gone home.

[SFX: FOUNTAIN SPLASHES. CAMERA PANS TO THE OPEN PATH.]
