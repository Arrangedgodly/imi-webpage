# The Pledge Drive Sets a Record for Pauses

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: public television pledge drives that interrupt a program to announce progress before any pledge has arrived*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Mena, a lemur; host Doro, a macaque; phone captain Pali, an orangutan; producer Kesi, a capuchin; caller Varo, a gibbon; phone volunteer Tavi, a capuchin; graphics operator Odo, a lemur; program director Rumi, an orangutan; second caller, voice only.

COLD OPEN

INT. PLEDGE STUDIO - NIGHT

Mena smiles beside a phone bank. The progress meter is empty.

MENA: We have reached an exciting milestone.

DORO: No one has called yet.

[The meter flashes GOAL REACHED, then returns to zero.]

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Kesi says the meter is running a test animation. Pali checks the phone bank. Every line is silent. Doro asks the crew to demonstrate how a pledge works. Pali picks up a phone; it rings on the wrong desk. Kesi answers the call. It is the studio kitchen asking whether the hosts want tea.

MENA: We are grateful for your support.

Kesi tells the kitchen they will call back. The phone captain looks at the empty pledge cards. The hosts begin reading benefits of membership: a tote bag, a mug, and a calendar. Doro picks up the mug. It has last year's dates.

ACT TWO

INT. PHONE BANK - LATER

A call arrives. Pali answers and hears Varo asking whether the tote bag is large enough for sheet music. Pali checks the sample. The tote is small. Mena asks if the caller would like the mug instead. Varo says he wants to pledge, but only if he can hear the program without the hosts talking over it. Pali prepares one card and asks Varo to choose an amount while she checks the phone routing.

Doro pauses. Kesi points to the countdown clock, which is set to ten minutes. The hosts wait. The phone bank rings again, but the line goes to the kitchen. Pali redirects the incoming kitchen inquiry to reception, then passes Varo's still-open line to volunteer Tavi, who confirms his name and asks the amount. Varo chooses a modest monthly pledge. Pali writes the single pledge on the card and places it on the desk.

[The progress meter moves one notch. Kesi asks the graphics operator not to celebrate yet. The operator plays a fanfare anyway; the meter jumps to GOAL REACHED.]

Mena turns the sound down. Doro reads the pledge total from Pali's card. The meter shows six times the actual amount because the graphic multiplies by the six tote bags in storage. Kesi corrects the multiplier from six to one in a single edit; the meter now matches the pledge card.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Varo asks when the local music program will return. Mena starts to explain the station's mission, then stops when he repeats his request. Kesi lowers the pledge banner. Pali prepares to close the pledge line and hands the broadcast control to program director Rumi for the scheduled return.

INT. PHONE BANK - MOMENTS LATER

A second call comes through before the pledge line closes. The caller asks for a tote bag but makes no pledge. Pali marks the inquiry as a request, not a donation. Pali then closes the line. The progress total stays at one pledge. Kesi leaves the meter on the corrected single-multiplier setting and does not reset it.

ACT THREE

INT. PLEDGE STUDIO - NIGHT

The local music program returns once. The hosts stay quiet through its first verse. The phone bank remains closed during the broadcast. The program director checks the studio monitor and confirms the sound is clear. The teleprompter advances to an unsent milestone card while the song continues. Mena starts to read; Doro gently turns the prompter away. Kesi clears the card, and the music carries on without restarting. 

DORO: No need to interrupt a verse to announce a card.

When the song finishes, Mena gives the donation website in the closing credit slot, leaving space for the address to appear. Doro holds the tote bag beside the calendar, checking that the dates match. Kesi checks that the display still shows the single pledge already recorded. The hosts applaud once, softly, and the meter stays accurate.

Pali puts the single pledge card in the stack. Doro asks if they can call the one donation a record. Mena says it is a record of one pledge. Kesi changes the screen label to CURRENT TOTAL and locks the test animation. Doro reaches toward the fanfare button, then stops when he sees the one-card total. The hosts do not announce a second milestone.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Pali files one pledge card and one inquiry note. The phone bank stays quiet. Mena returns the mug to the prop shelf. Doro places the tote bag beside the calendar.

FINAL IMAGE: The progress meter shows one recorded pledge as the closing-credit music plays, with the tote request filed separately.
