# The Toy Train Appraisal Has a Missing Track

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: collectibles appraisal programs, dramatic value reveals, and experts asked to price an incomplete toy set*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Piri, a gibbon; collector Dema, a macaque; appraiser Talo, an orangutan; producer Viki, a baboon; camera assistant Noro, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. APPRAISAL STUDIO - DAY

A small toy train sits on a table. It has one engine, two cars, and a loop of track with one gap. Piri holds a velvet cloth over the price card.

PIRI: This rare train may be worth more than a real train ticket.

TALO: It is missing one track piece.

DEMA: I brought the box. Maybe it is inside.

[The host lifts the empty box. A packing list clings to the underside.] 

ACT ONE

INT. APPRAISAL STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Talo checks the maker's mark on the engine and dates the set. Dema explains that the train belonged to an aunt and was used at holidays. The producer asks if there is a hidden compartment in the box. Noro photographs the maker's mark and the missing-track gap.

TALO: The engine is original. The cars match the set. The track is incomplete.

PIRI: Does that increase the value?

TALO: It means it is incomplete.

DEMA: I want to know whether the engine can still run.

[The appraiser checks the battery compartment; there is no battery. The host offers a fresh battery from the production kit. Talo confirms the engine can be tested without forcing the wheels.] 

TALO: We can test it on a straight section. Not on the open loop.

[The assistant finds one straight track piece in the box, along with the missing-piece checklist. The piece is not the missing curve; it is an accessory.] 

PIRI: A new discovery.

Noro: It was listed in the box inventory.

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO TABLE - LATER

The battery is installed and the engine moves along the straight track. It stops at the end. Piri asks whether the train is now complete. Dema says it needs the missing curved piece to close the loop. Talo explains that a replacement may be available, but it could affect the set's collectibility if it is not an original part.

DEMA: I would rather keep the gap than pretend it is complete.

VIKI: We need a big reveal. Can we hide the value card under the train?

TALO: The card does not belong under the train.

[The producer sets the cloth over the value card. The host lifts it too early. The card reads ESTIMATE PENDING CONDITION. Piri puts the cloth back.] 

PIRI: We have to wait for the condition report?

TALO: Yes. I need to check the engine's wiring and the track pieces individually.

[The expert inspects each piece and notes a chip on one car. The camera assistant shows the chip in focus. Dema recognizes it as an old scratch from the toy box, not a fresh break.] 

TALO: The chip is stable. Do not sand or repaint it.

[The producer asks if they can announce a top auction price. The appraiser declines to make a guarantee; the card will show an estimated range and a note that the set is incomplete.] 

[The appraiser asks Dema to read the packing list. It names the engine, two cars, the track loop, and one curve. The curve is checked off, but the matching piece is not in the box. Noro checks the studio table again and finds only the straight accessory.] 

DEMA: I remember the curve from my aunt's living room.

TALO: It may be packed with another set, but we should not assume.

[The host reaches for the velvet cloth, then folds it away instead of covering the gap. Dema photographs the incomplete loop for the parts catalog.] 

PIRI: The missing piece is the story.

DEMA: It is also just missing.

ACT THREE

INT. APPRAISAL STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Talo gives Dema the written estimate. It lists the original engine and cars, the incomplete track, and the stable chip. The host reads the range without adding a dramatic number. Dema says the set will stay in the family and asks for a source for a compatible replacement curve.

TALO: I can provide a parts catalog. A replacement should be clearly marked as a replacement.

PIRI: So there is no sale today.

DEMA: No sale, and no mystery.

[The host removes the velvet cloth from the table. The train remains on the straight track, powered off. Noro places the parts catalog beside the estimate. The producer changes the shot list from PRICE REVEAL to CONDITION NOTES.] 

VIKI: Can we still use the train shot?

Noro: The engine can roll once more on the straight section.

[Talo places the battery back in its case and closes the engine compartment. The collector wraps the train in the cloth and leaves the gap visible.] 

TAG

[Piri lays a toy ticket beside the estimate. It is too large to fit in the train. The host folds it into quarters and hands it to Dema.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The collector carries away the original train and estimate, while the empty track gap remains on the studio table.
