# Orbiting the Snack Table

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: space mission control television, where a crew tracks a snack crumb floating through a simulated spacecraft*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A classroom space mission loses one snack crumb in its cardboard capsule, and control treats the search as an orbital emergency.

Cast: Commander Odo, baboon; pilot Piri, capuchin; engineer Nara, macaque; mission host Seli, lemur; flight controller Tavi, gibbon.

## Episode: "Crumb in Orbit"

### Cold Open

[CARDBOARD CAPSULE. A CRUMB FLOATS PAST THE WINDOW.]

SELI: We have an object in orbit.

ODO: It is a crumb.

PIRI: It is moving toward the control panel.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[CONTROL TABLE WITH A MAP OF THE CAPSULE.]

Tavi marks the crumb's last position. Odo says the capsule must remain sealed. Nara asks why a snack crumb is inside the spacecraft.

PIRI: It came from the snack bag.

ODO: The snack bag was secured.

NARA: It was folded.

SELI: The mission continues while we investigate.

Tavi asks the crew to use a soft brush to move the crumb away from the controls. Piri reaches for it, but the crumb drifts just out of reach.

PIRI: It has changed course.

ODO: The crumb is in zero gravity.

NARA: The capsule is on the floor.

Odo says the room is the simulated space environment. Nara points out that a fan is pushing air through the cardboard window.

### Act Two

[THE CREW CLOSES THE WINDOW.]

The crumb settles on the capsule wall. Seli narrates the landing. Odo asks Piri to retrieve it with a spoon. The spoon is too large to fit through the hatch.

ODO: We need a smaller tool.

Tavi offers tweezers. Nara says the crumb could be crushed. Piri uses a folded paper strip to slide it toward the hatch. It catches on a seam.

PIRI: The surface has trapped the object.

SELI: Is the mission compromised?

NARA: The panel is fine.

ODO: We have a containment issue.

Seli asks the crew to remain calm. She opens the snack bag outside the capsule and finds the missing crumb trail leading to the window seam.

PIRI: There are more crumbs.

TAVI: We have an asteroid field.

NARA: It is a folded snack bag.

[THE CONTROL TEAM RUNS A SIMULATION.]

Seli asks Piri to report the crumb's trajectory. Piri points to the map, but Odo has rotated the board so the capsule's front is now labeled "up."

PIRI: The object moved left.

ODO: Left from which side?

NARA: It moved toward the hatch.

SELI: That is the important direction.

Odo uses a string to show the crumb's path. The string catches on the cardboard window and pulls it loose.

[SFX: CARDBOARD TEAR.]

ODO: The hull has been compromised.

NARA: The window is cardboard.

Piri replaces it with a clear plastic sheet. The crumb is still stuck at the seam. Tavi suggests turning the capsule slowly so gravity brings it toward the hatch.

ODO: We do not have gravity controls.

NARA: We can tilt the box.

They tilt one corner. The crumb rolls a few inches and stops against tape. Seli narrates a controlled landing. Odo says the object has resisted capture.

PIRI: It is stuck to tape.

NARA: We can remove the tape after the surface is clear.

Tavi cuts the tape carefully. The crumb rolls to the tray.

### Act Three

[THE CREW REMOVES THE BAG FROM THE CAPSULE.]

Tavi seals the seam with tape. Odo uses a small brush to sweep crumbs into a cup. Piri retrieves the original crumb without crushing it. The control panel remains clear.

SELI: The object is contained.

ODO: The cabin is secure.

NARA: We should inspect the snack bag before the next mission.

PIRI: It was part of the mission supplies.

Tavi opens the bag outside the capsule and shakes it gently over a tray. Several more crumbs fall out.

TAVI: The supply container was leaking.

ODO: The capsule was never in danger.

SELI: The mission report will say "snack containment tested."

Piri places the crumb in a small sample jar. Odo says they have collected one specimen. Nara points to the tray full of crumbs.

NARA: We have collected many.

Before the tag, Piri checks the capsule floor for any remaining crumbs. Nara closes the snack bag with a clip.

PIRI: The cabin is clear.

NARA: The supplies are sealed.

### Tag

[THE CREW SITS AROUND THE TABLE.]

PIRI: Can we eat the samples?

TAVI: They have been logged.

ODO: The mission is over.

Seli looks at the capsule window. A crumb is stuck to the outside.

SELI: We have a second object in orbit.

NARA: It is on the floor.

[SFX: BRUSH SWEEPS, THEME OUT.]
