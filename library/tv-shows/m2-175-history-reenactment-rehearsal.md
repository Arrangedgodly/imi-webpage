# The Great History Reenactment Rehearsal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: historical reenactment programs that fill gaps in the record with invented certainty*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Historian Oru is filming an episode about the first bridge built across a local creek. Producer Vela wants a dramatic scene in which the builder predicts the future, while archive assistant Nemi keeps reminding the crew which details are documented and which are guesses.

Cast: Oru, a gorilla historian; Vela, a capuchin producer; Nemi, a macaque archive assistant; and Pali, a lemur actor playing the builder.

## Episode: "The Bridge Was a Bridge"

### Cold Open

[REENACTMENT SET. PALI STANDS BESIDE A SMALL WOODEN MODEL BRIDGE. VELA HOLDS A SCRIPT.] 

VELA: Say, "I know this bridge will unite the valley for generations."

PALI: The record says the builder asked for more nails.

ORU: That is all we know from the letter.

[NEMI HOLDS UP A TRANSCRIPTION. VELA POINTS TO A BLANK SPACE AFTER THE REQUEST.] 

VELA: There must have been a bigger thought.

NEMI: The paper ends.

### Act One

[THE CREW BUILDS A SET OF THE CREEK. PALI WEARS A TOOL BELT. VELA ADDS A THUNDER MACHINE.] 

VELA: The storm makes the stakes clear.

ORU: The bridge was built in summer.

[THE THUNDER MACHINE RUMBLES. THE MODEL CREEK'S PAPER WATER SHAKES. PALI'S TOOL BELT DROPS A WOODEN HAMMER.] 

PALI: Do I still ask for nails?

NEMI: Yes.

[VELA ASKS FOR A PREDICTION. PALI LOOKS AT THE BRIDGE PLAN.] 

PALI: I predict I will need more nails.

[THE SOUND TECH ADDS A CHORAL STING. ORU TAKES THE SOUND BOARD AND TURNS IT DOWN.]

[VELA ASKS PALI TO STAND ON A ROCK AND DELIVER THE PREDICTION TO THE HORIZON. PALI STEPS UP, BUT THE ROCK IS A PAINTED FOAM PROP THAT SLIDES.] 

PALI: I cannot stand on that.

NEMI: The builder would have stood on the bridge, not this rock.

[THE CREW REMOVES THE ROCK. VELA ASKS FOR A LOW CAMERA ANGLE INSTEAD. THE CAMERA SEES ONLY THE BRIDGE MODEL'S LEGS.] 

ORU: It was a small bridge.

VELA: It needs to look important.

NEMI: The letter gives us the importance.

### Act Two

[NEMI COMPARES THE BRIDGE PLAN TO THE SET. THE CREW HAS BUILT THE RAILING ON THE WRONG SIDE.] 

NEMI: The drawing puts the rail on the river side.

VELA: The audience needs to see the actor's face.

ORU: Then move the camera.

[THE CAMERA TRACKS AROUND THE MODEL. IT RUNS INTO THE THUNDER MACHINE. VELA MOVES THE MACHINE; IT TIPS, AND THE PAPER CREEK GETS WET.] 

PALI: The water has reached the bridge.

NEMI: The water is from the prop machine.

[THE CREW PAUSES WHILE THE PAPER DRIES. VELA ADDS A LINE TO THE SCRIPT: "THE SKY TESTS THE BUILDER." ORU CROSSES IT OUT.] 

ORU: We have no evidence of a storm.

VELA: We have a wet set.

NEMI: We have a wet set because of the thunder machine.

[THEY RESET THE RAILING TO MATCH THE PLAN. PALI HOLDS IT WHILE NEMI CHECKS THE MEASUREMENTS.]

[NEMI OPENS THE ARCHIVE FOLDER TO CHECK THE ORIGINAL PLAN. THE CREW HAS LEFT THE DOCUMENT'S SCALE BAR UNDER A CUP OF TEA. SHE LIFTS THE CUP; A WET RING COVERS THE MEASUREMENTS.] 

NEMI: We need another copy.

VELA: Can the stain be part of the story?

ORU: The stain is from this afternoon.

[THE CAMERA FILMS THE CLEAN DIGITAL SCAN INSTEAD. PALI HOLDS THE MODEL BRIDGE STEADY WHILE THE RAILING IS REPOSITIONED.] 

PALI: It matches now.

[VELA SETS UP THE FINAL SHOT: PALI WILL PLACE THE LAST PLANK, THEN TURN TOWARD THE SUNSET. THE SUNSET SCREEN SLIDES OFF ITS TRACK AND REVEALS A STUDIO WALL.] 

PALI: I can place the plank.

ORU: The turn is not in the record.

[NEMI MOVES THE MODEL UNDER A WORK LIGHT. PALI SETS THE PLANK. THE CAMERA HOLDS ON THE BRIDGE AS IT IS, WITHOUT THE SUNSET.] 

VELA: We need an ending image.

NEMI: The completed bridge is an ending image.

[THE CREW LOWERS THE SUNSET SCREEN. IT CATCHES THE MODEL'S ROPE AND PULLS THE LITTLE BRIDGE SIDEWAYS. PALI CATCHES IT BEFORE IT FALLS.] 

PALI: The bridge has survived production.

### Act Three

[THE CAMERA ROLLS AGAIN. PALI PLACES THE LAST MODEL PLANK. HE LOOKS AT THE LETTER AND READS THE DOCUMENTED REQUEST.] 

PALI: We need more nails.

[THE CREW IS SILENT. VELA WAITS FOR A DECLARATION. ORU NODS TO THE CAMERA OPERATOR, WHO PANS TO THE FINISHED BRIDGE.] 

ORU: That is the moment we can support.

[THE PROGRAM GRAPHIC TRIES TO READ "THE WORDS THAT CHANGED HISTORY." NEMI REPLACES IT WITH "LETTER FROM THE BUILDER, YEAR 1842." ]

VELA: It is less sweeping.

NEMI: It is on the page.

[THE REENACTMENT CREW PUTS AWAY THE THUNDER MACHINE. PALI CARRIES THE BRIDGE MODEL TO THE ARCHIVE TABLE, WHERE IT FITS BETWEEN THE ORIGINAL PLAN AND THE TRANSCRIPTION.] 

### Tag

[VELA READS THE LAST LINE OF THE LETTER: "SEND THE NAILS BY TUESDAY." ]

VELA: A cliffhanger.

ORU: A delivery date.
