# The Whisk Is Missing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: baking competition television and precision challenges disrupted by actual kitchen conditions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Three contestants race to bake a fig tart while the host keeps changing the judging criteria. Their strongest competitor is a macaque who cannot resist tasting every ingredient.

Cast: Host Vela, a lemur; contestants Nori, a capuchin; Bram, a gibbon; Kiko, a macaque; and judge Sava, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The Tasting Spoon"

### Cold Open

[THREE MIXING BOWLS SIT ON A COUNTER. KIKO TASTES THE DOUGH.]

VELA: The challenge is a fig tart with a crisp crust.

KIKO: It needs salt.

NORI: You already tasted it?

KIKO: I checked the balance.

[THE TIMER STARTS. KIKO TAKES ANOTHER BITE.]

### Act One

[THE CONTESTANTS ROLL DOUGH AT THREE WORKSTATIONS.]

VELA: Bakers, you have forty minutes. Judges will score flavor, structure, and presentation.

SAVA: A tart must hold a clean slice.

BRAM: I can make a perfect lattice top.

NORI: I will fold the edge by hand.

KIKO: I will taste the filling.

VELA: You have enough filling to taste.

KIKO: I have a very precise palate.

[NORI MEASURES FLOUR. BRAM ARRANGES DOUGH STRIPS. KIKO LICKS A SPOON, THEN SETS IT BACK IN THE BOWL.]

SAVA: Kiko, use a clean spoon.

KIKO: This spoon is clean.

SAVA: It has a bite mark.

[KIKO SWITCHES SPOONS. THE TIMER BEEPS AT TEN MINUTES.]

### Act Two

[THE OVEN DOOR OPENS. BRAM'S LATTICE HAS SLIPPED TO ONE SIDE.]

BRAM: The pastry moved.

VELA: The pastry has a point of view.

NORI: My tart is browning too fast.

SAVA: Turn the tray.

[NORI TURNS THE TRAY. KIKO REACHES FOR A FIG. HIS ELBOW KNOCKS FLOUR INTO THE FILLING.]

KIKO: It needs a little more fruit.

NORI: It needs less of your elbow.

[THE HOST PUTS A BOWL OF FIGS ON THE JUDGING TABLE.]

VELA: For the second round, each baker must add a surprise ingredient.

SAVA: That was not in the rules.

VELA: It is a twist.

SAVA: A twist is not a rule.

BRAM: I have honey.

NORI: I have cinnamon.

KIKO: I have a fig.

SAVA: You had three figs.

[KIKO LOOKS AT THE EMPTY BOWL.]

[THE CONTESTANTS COOL THEIR TARTS ON A RACK. KIKO MOVES HIS PLATE TO THE BACK.]

NORI: Why are you hiding yours?

KIKO: It has one bite missing. The camera keeps finding it.

VELA: Cameras are meant to find things.

SAVA: It also has a clean edge.

[KIKO TURNS THE TART. A SECOND BITE MARK IS VISIBLE ON THE OTHER SIDE.]

KIKO: That is from the tasting test.

SAVA: You tested the crust twice.

KIKO: It was a blind test.

BRAM: Your eyes were open.

KIKO: My eyes do not judge texture.

[NORI OFFERS KIKO A SMALL PIECE OF HER CRUST.]

NORI: Here. Compare it without taking it from the plate.

[KIKO TAKES THE PIECE, CHEWS, AND NODS.]

KIKO: Yours is crisp. Mine is softer.

SAVA: That is a useful comparison.

VELA: We have a baking lesson in our competition show.

SAVA: It is not a lesson. It is judging.

VELA: We can have both.

[THE TIMER BEEPS. THE TARTS ARE READY.]

[SAVA CUTS A SMALL SLICE FROM EACH TART.]

SAVA: The pastry holds together. The filling is warm, but not runny.

VELA: And the surprise?

SAVA: I reject the surprise round. The rules were clear at the start.

BRAM: I used honey anyway.

SAVA: It was on your ingredient list.

KIKO: I thought we needed a surprise.

NORI: You surprised us by leaving enough tart to judge.

[KIKO LOOKS AT HIS SMALLER TART.]

KIKO: I can bake another one.

SAVA: Next time, bake first and taste after.

KIKO: I will borrow your timer and keep my hands off the fruit.

SAVA: That is a good start for a baker.

NORI: Start with one tart.

### Act Three

[THE TARTS ARE PRESENTED. BRAM'S LATTICE IS CROOKED, NORI'S EDGE IS EVEN, AND KIKO'S IS A SMALLER CIRCLE.]

SAVA: Nori's crust is crisp. Bram's filling is balanced. Kiko's tart has one bite missing.

KIKO: That was a structural test.

VELA: The judges have decided the winner is Nori.

NORI: Thank you.

KIKO: The bite came from my tart.

SAVA: The winner's tart is still here.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO KIKO'S PLATE. HE HAS EATEN A CORNER.]

VELA: Kiko wins the tasting spoon.

KIKO: Is it edible?

SAVA: It is for next time.

[KIKO PUTS IT IN HIS POCKET. IT CLINKS AGAINST THREE OTHER SPOONS.]

NORI: How many tasting spoons do you have?

KIKO: Enough to judge the next season.

[THE TIMER DINGS. BRAM'S CROOKED LATTICE SLIDES OFF THE TART AND LANDS PERFECTLY ON THE WINNER'S PLATE.]
