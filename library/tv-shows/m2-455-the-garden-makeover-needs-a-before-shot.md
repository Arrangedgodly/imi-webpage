# The Garden Makeover Needs a Before Shot

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: makeover reality shows, artificial before-and-after contrasts, and hosts who try to keep a mess for the camera*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Nala, a macaque; gardener Omi, a baboon; homeowner Teri, a gibbon; camera operator Fenn, an orangutan; producer Jassa, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - DAY

A tidy garden has labeled beds, a watering can, and a small bench. Nala addresses camera as Jassa holds up a sign reading BEFORE.

NALA: This forgotten patch is ready for transformation.

TERI: It was weeded this morning.

OMI: The beds are planted and labeled.

JASSA: We need a before image.

FENN: The camera already recorded the garden.

JASSA: It looks good. We need it to look unfinished.

[The host lifts the bench cushion, hoping to reveal a mess. It is clean. Omi moves the cushion back.] 

NALA: Our makeover begins with a challenge.

TERI: The challenge is keeping the seedlings watered.

[The show logo appears over the watering can.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. GARDEN PATH - CONTINUOUS

Jassa gives the team a makeover list: add a decorative arch, repaint the bench, and introduce a surprise water feature. Omi asks where the water feature drains. Jassa suggests a blue bowl; Teri says the beds need the water.

OMI: The plants have drip lines. We should not block them with an arch.

NALA: Could the arch go over the path?

TERI: The cart needs to pass through.

[Omi rolls the garden cart along the path. It clears the sides by only a few inches. The host tries to hold a flower basket above the cart as a reveal prop.] 

FENN: If the arch narrows the path, the cart will not get through.

JASSA: The reveal can show a wider angle.

TERI: The path will still be the same width.

[The producer removes the arch from the plan. Nala asks for a dramatic close-up of the seedlings. Omi checks the soil and turns on the drip line. Water reaches each row.] 

NALA: The garden is thriving.

JASSA: That sounds like the after shot.

OMI: It is the current shot.

ACT TWO

EXT. BENCH - AFTERNOON

The team repaints the bench with the existing color so it matches the garden's posted maintenance plan. Jassa wants a contrasting color for television. Teri says the bright paint could stain clothes before it dries. Fenn tests the shot with a folded cloth over the lens hood.

JASSA: We can call the wet paint a design moment.

TERI: People sit here after watering.

NALA: We can put up a sign until it dries.

OMI: The bench can stay closed overnight. The garden opens tomorrow.

[They apply the approved paint. Nala places a small sign at the path entrance. The cameraman frames the sign so it looks like a dramatic warning.] 

FENN: The sign says, "Wet paint. Please use the other bench."

JASSA: Can we say, "The garden has changed forever"?

TERI: The garden has one freshly painted bench.

[The producer looks at the current footage. The before shot already shows a thriving garden; the after shot shows the same garden with a clean bench and watered beds.] 

JASSA: There is no transformation.

OMI: There is maintenance. It takes a season, not one afternoon.

NALA: We could reveal the sign.

FENN: It is facing the camera already.

ACT THREE

EXT. GARDEN ENTRANCE - NEXT MORNING

The paint has dried. Teri removes the sign and checks the bench. Omi tests the drip line once more. Jassa wants to add a ribbon to the watering can for the finale; Nala ties it to the handle without covering the nozzle.

TERI: The bench is ready. The path is clear. The beds are watered.

JASSA: The before shot will not look worse.

NALA: We can show what changed: a painted bench and a reliable watering line.

FENN: I can cut between yesterday's sign and today's open seat.

[The host opens the garden gate. The camera follows Teri carrying a small tray of seedlings to a bed. Omi points out where the new plants go; Teri places them in the prepared spaces.] 

OMI: The makeover is not finished until those roots take.

JASSA: That may take weeks.

TERI: You can come back then.

[The producer reluctantly marks the calendar. Nala sits on the bench only after Teri invites her. The paint remains dry.] 

[The host begins a dramatic ribbon cutting at the garden gate. Omi hands Nala garden shears, then points to the actual drip line.] 

OMI: Those are for the dry stems, not the ribbon.

NALA: I can cut the ribbon with regular scissors.

TERI: Or we can open the gate without a ceremony.

[The host sets the shears down and opens the gate. Teri rolls the cart through the clear path. The camera follows it all the way to the beds.] 

FENN: The cart fits. That is a useful reveal.

TAG

Jassa holds up the BEFORE sign beside the AFTER sign. Both frame the same garden. The crew laughs and lowers the signs.

FINAL IMAGE: A neighbor enters through the clear path and sits on the newly painted bench while the seedlings wait in their beds.
