# The Perfect Loaf, Almost

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: baking competition convention, where precision judging collides with primate food handling and an oven that needs rotating trays*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: Three contestants bake a loaf for a judging panel, but the host's exact recipe ignores how monkeys actually handle dough.

Cast: Host Piri, a capuchin; contestants Odo, a baboon, Miri, a macaque, and Sela, a gibbon; judge Tavi, a squirrel monkey.

Episode: "Proof of Patience"

COLD OPEN

INT. BAKING TENT, MORNING

Piri presents a bowl of dough. Odo reaches in with both hands. Piri stops him.

PIRI: The recipe says knead gently.

ODO: My hands are large.

A small piece of dough sticks to his palm and stretches into a ribbon.

TAVI: The dough has chosen a contestant.

ACT ONE

The bakers receive identical ingredients and one hour. Sela measures flour by eye. Miri uses a cup. Odo uses the bowl. Tavi asks if the measurements are exact. Piri says the camera wants to see a clean counter. Miri points out the flour keeps drifting in the breeze.

Sela covers the bowl with a leaf to let the dough rise. Piri says the challenge requires a cloth. She uses the leaf anyway, explaining it keeps the dough from drying. Odo's dough rises too quickly near the warm oven. He moves it to the shade. Piri calls that an unexpected strategy.

ACT TWO

INT. BAKING TENT, LATER

The contestants shape their loaves. Odo makes a round loaf but his tail brushes the flour. Miri forms a small braided loaf. Sela uses a smooth stone to roll the dough flat. Piri asks if the stone is an approved tool. Sela says it is a clean kitchen stone.

The oven has two shelves. The lower shelf runs hotter. Tavi asks everyone to rotate their trays halfway through. Odo does not want to open the oven because the show clock is counting down. Miri opens it carefully. A wave of heat escapes and fogs the camera lens.

PIRI: The oven has made its own weather.

Tavi wipes the lens. Sela rotates her tray, then notices Odo's crust is dark on one side. She offers him a lower rack. He accepts. Piri asks whether helping a competitor violates the rules. Tavi says the rules require a finished loaf, not a secret.

ACT TWO-B

INT. BAKING TENT, AFTERNOON

The oven temperature drops. Piri says the challenge clock cannot stop. Tavi explains that the tent flap keeps blowing open and sending cool air through the oven. The contestants use a weighted mat to hold the flap down. Odo offers to use the broom as a prop, but Sela asks for something that will not block the doorway.

Miri checks the loaves. The braid is browning faster on the left. She rotates it. Odo has shaped his loaf too thick in the middle. Tavi tells him to lower the oven rack and extend the bake time. Odo says the rules allow only one hour. Piri says a good contestant respects the clock. Sela points to the temperature chart, which shows the oven was below target for ten minutes.

Tavi adjusts the remaining time. The contestants agree to wait for the center to finish rather than pull the loaves early. Piri asks the camera to capture the tension. The camera fogs again. Odo holds a clean cloth over the lens while Piri wipes it.

SELA: We can finish the bake without a close-up.

PIRI: We can finish the close-up later.

Miri and Odo swap trays halfway through. Sela keeps the leaf cover on the unbaked dough until the oven is free. Tavi checks the crust by tapping each loaf. Odo's sounds hollow. Miri's gives a softer knock. Sela's is even.

ACT TWO-C

INT. BAKING TENT, LATE AFTERNOON

The judges inspect the loaves before tasting. Tavi asks each contestant what they would change. Odo says he would let the center bake longer. Miri says she would rotate the tray sooner. Sela says she would keep the leaf cover ready for the next batch.

Piri asks if they can reshoot the reveal. The first camera take shows the loaves, but not the bakers. The second shows Piri blocking the oven. Tavi suggests placing the camera beside the table instead of behind the oven. They do. The lens stays clear, and the crew captures the loaves cooling on a rack.

ACT THREE



The loaves are judged. Odo's loaf is browned unevenly but soft inside. Miri's braid is neat. Sela's loaf has a flat bottom from the stone. Tavi tastes each and asks what the bakers learned. They say the oven needs rotating trays and the leaf worked as a cover.

Piri announces the winner: Sela, for a loaf that baked evenly. She offers the smooth stone back to the kitchen. Odo asks if the dough ribbon can be baked too. Tavi says it is still raw.

TAG

The bakers share slices. Piri attempts to cut a perfect square. The crust cracks. Tavi takes a piece with a naturally shaped edge.

TAVI: The recipe did not mention geometry.
