# The Documentary Interviews the Bench

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: documentary series that use solemn narration and multiple camera angles to elevate an ordinary public object*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A documentary crew profiles a park bench as if it were a famous landmark, while visitors keep using it for its ordinary purpose.

Cast: Narrator Seli, a gibbon; interviewer Kito, a macaque; visitor Pava, a capuchin; and caretaker Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "Where We Sit"

COLD OPEN

EXT. PARK - MORNING

A wooden bench sits beneath a tree. Seli narrates over a slow camera move.

SELI: For generations, this bench has watched the park change.

ODO: It was installed last spring.

KITO: We can start with the caretaker's account after the establishing shot.

[PAVA SITS ON THE BENCH AND OPENS A BOOK.]

ACT ONE

Kito interviews Odo beside the bench. A camera operator circles it slowly.

KITO: What has the bench witnessed?

ODO: Joggers, lunch breaks, and the sprinkler repair.

SELI: The bench has borne witness to the quiet rhythms of the park.

ODO: It is a place to sit.

KITO: Can you point to a memorable day?

ODO: The day they repainted it.

[ODO SHOWS A PAINT CHIP.]

ACT TWO

EXT. PARK - LATER

The crew records Pava reading. Kito asks her to describe the bench.

KITO: What does it mean to you?

PAVA: I needed to sit down.

SELI: A moment of rest, framed by the lives of those who pass.

PAVA: I am reading.

[THE CAMERA PUSHES IN ON THE BOOK. THE BENCH DISAPPEARS FROM FRAME.]

KITO: Could you move a little to the left?

PAVA: Then I cannot read in the shade.

ODO: The shade moves across the bench each afternoon.

SELI: The light has its own schedule.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. PARK - AFTERNOON

Kito arranges three interview chairs around the bench. Odo removes them.

KITO: We need a panel discussion about the bench.

ODO: People use it one at a time or sit together.

PAVA: I can answer questions while I sit.

[THE CREW SETS UP A MICROPHONE. IT BLOCKS THE BENCH.]

KITO: We have lost the subject.

ODO: The subject is behind the microphone.

SELI: The bench has become obscured by the machinery of observation.

PAVA: Could you move it?

[THEY MOVE THE MICROPHONE. PAVA RETURNS TO HER BOOK.]

ACT TWO-C

EXT. PARK - LATE AFTERNOON

Odo notices a loose slat. Kito sets down his camera. Seli begins a serious narration.

ODO: This board needs a screw tightened.

KITO: Can we film the repair?

ODO: I can do it after the interview.

PAVA: I am done reading. You can fix it now.

SELI: A small change threatens the bench's long service.

ODO: The slat is loose. The frame is sound.

[ODO TIGHTENS THE SCREW WITH A HAND TOOL. THE BENCH STOPS CREAKING.]

KITO: That took six seconds.

SELI: The bench has been restored.

ODO: It was not broken.

PAVA: It is quieter now.

[KITO ASKS PAVA TO SIT AGAIN FOR A SECOND INTERVIEW. SHE SITS, THEN GETS UP TO LET AN ELDERLY WALKER TAKE THE SHADED SPOT.]

KITO: We have another visitor.

SELI: The bench welcomes a new chapter.

ODO: The bench is being used.

PAVA: I can read at home.

ACT TWO-D

EXT. PARK - EVENING

Kito sets up for a final interview. Odo checks the bench. Seli places a light near the tree.

KITO: We have three different stories about the same bench.

ODO: The paint, the shade, and the loose screw.

SELI: Each one tells us something about the place.

PAVA: The bench is still a bench.

[THE WALKER WAVES. ODO WAVES BACK.]

KITO: Can we film the wave once more?

PAVA: I am going home.

SELI: The wave has already been recorded.

ODO: The bench will still be here tomorrow.

[THE CREW PACKS THE LIGHT AND TRIPOD. PAVA LEAVES HER BOOKMARK ON THE SEAT, THEN RETURNS TO TAKE IT.]

KITO: We have the closing shot.

SELI: The bench is empty again.

ODO: Someone else will sit there later.

KITO: Should we wait for them?

PAVA: You can film the next person when they arrive.

ACT THREE

EXT. PARK - SUNSET

Seli narrates as the sun moves behind the tree. Odo checks the bench slats. Kito packs the camera.

SELI: The bench remains in place, waiting for the next visitor.

ODO: I will wipe it down before closing.

KITO: We have a final question. Does the bench have a message?

[PAVA CLOSES HER BOOK AND STANDS.]

PAVA: It says, "Please do not stand on me."

ODO: That sign is on the back.

[THE CAMERA TURNS. A SMALL SIGN IS ATTACHED TO THE BACK OF THE BENCH.]

TAG

Seli faces the camera beside the empty bench.

SELI: The bench has nothing more to say.

[ODO WIPES IT.]
