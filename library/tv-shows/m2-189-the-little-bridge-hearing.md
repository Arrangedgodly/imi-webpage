# The Little Bridge Hearing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom reality convention, where a neighborhood argues over a footbridge that animals already cross safely*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: Residents testify about a small footbridge that the committee wants to replace with a larger, more impressive bridge.

Cast: Judge Sela, a lemur; advocate Doma, a baboon; witness Pavi, a capuchin; engineer Nara, a gibbon; and clerk Tavi, a macaque.

Episode: "The Crossing"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY HALL, DAY

Sela calls the hearing to order. Doma unveils a drawing of a large bridge with banners and lanterns.

SELA: What is wrong with the current bridge?

Doma points to it: a narrow plank over a small creek. Pavi walks across it carrying a fig basket.

PAVI: It works.

The camera zooms in on the plank. A leaf floats beneath it.

ACT ONE

INT. HEARING ROOM, LATER

Doma says the bridge lacks a ceremonial entrance. Nara explains that the existing plank is sturdy and low enough for animals to cross safely. Tavi reads the proposal. It includes three stairs and a toll booth.

PAVI: Who collects the toll?

Doma says the toll would pay for lantern oil. Sela asks why the bridge needs lanterns in daylight. Doma says it could also host evening events. Nara notes the creek is shallow and the crossing is used mostly in the morning.

A resident testifies that a new bridge might block the path. Doma says the path can be rerouted. Tavi unrolls a map. The proposed detour crosses the resident's garden.

ACT TWO

EXT. CREEK, AFTERNOON

The court visits the bridge. Pavi walks across again. Judge Sela asks Nara to test the plank. Nara presses each end and checks the supports. The bridge remains firm. Doma steps onto it with a stack of ceremonial plans. The papers catch the breeze and scatter.

Pavi catches one paper before it reaches the water. It is a drawing of the proposed toll booth. The plan lists a fee of one fig per crossing. The residents begin calculating how many crossings they make each day. Doma says the fee could be waived for locals. Tavi asks who counts as local.

Doma admits the booth was added to pay for the bridge's decorations. Sela asks whether the decorations are required for safe crossing. Doma says they are not. Nara points out the creek bank is muddy; stairs would make it harder to reach the water.

Pavi places a flat stone beside the plank as a dry step. Residents test it. The crossing is now easier without changing the bridge.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CREEK BANK, LATE AFTERNOON

Doma asks to demonstrate how a grand bridge would improve the crossing. Nara sets up two chairs to represent the toll gate and a rolled blanket for the bridge. Pavi walks between them. He must climb over the blanket, pass the chairs and stop to pay a fig.

PAVI: It takes longer than the plank.

Doma says the demonstration lacks lanterns. Tavi adds two paper lanterns. One catches on the blanket, pulling it into the stream. Nara retrieves it before it gets wet. Sela asks whether the new design requires more maintenance. Doma admits the stairs, toll booth and lanterns would each need regular checks.

A young resident brings a walking stick to test the current plank. Nara confirms the support is stable but one end could use a new brace. Pavi offers a dry stone for the muddy approach. They place it beside the plank and test again. The crossing is steady, and the stone keeps feet out of the mud.

Doma asks if the court will let him present the bridge drawing anyway. Sela says he can display it as a proposal, with a note explaining why residents chose maintenance instead. Tavi pins the drawing on a board. The wind folds it over, covering the toll booth. Doma folds the rest of it and turns the blank side outward for the inspection schedule.

ACT THREE


INT. COMMUNITY HALL, EVENING

Sela rules that the bridge does not need replacement. The committee can maintain the supports and keep the approach clear. Doma asks if the lanterns can be saved for the hall. Sela approves if they do not block the path.

Nara writes a short inspection schedule. Pavi volunteers to check the plank after heavy rain. Tavi records the plan in the minutes. Doma folds the elaborate bridge drawing and uses its blank back to make a maintenance sign.

TAG

EXT. CREEK, NEXT MORNING

Pavi crosses with a basket. Doma waits on the other side holding a lantern. It is morning, so the lantern is not lit.

PAVI: You can carry the sign.

Doma joins him. The bridge holds both.
