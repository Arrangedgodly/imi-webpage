# The Harbor Weather Desk

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local weather broadcasts that turn ordinary conditions into color-coded breaking news*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A tiny rain shower forces a harbor weather team to choose between a dramatic map and the actual window.

Cast: Forecaster Leni, a capuchin; anchor Odo, a baboon; camera operator Seli, a macaque; dock keeper Pava, a gibbon; field reporter Tavi, a lemur.

Episode: "A Cloud Over the Pier"

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL STUDIO - MORNING

A weather map fills the screen. Odo points to a red storm band.

ODO: Breaking weather. The harbor may receive a brief shower.

LENI: The cloud is the size of the pier.

ODO: Our map has given it a red outline.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO THE WINDOW. SUNLIGHT FILLS THE ROOM.]

ACT ONE

Leni checks the forecast. A small cloud is moving over the water. Odo asks whether it can be called a storm. She says it can be called rain. Odo turns the map from green to yellow to red while Seli adjusts the camera.

SELI: The colors are changing faster than the sky.

ODO: The map is live.

LENI: The map is a drawing.

Tavi reports from the pier. He holds an umbrella and says the boards are dry. Odo asks him to describe conditions as they may become.

TAVI: They may stay dry.

ODO: Please give viewers a reason to keep watching.

PAVA: The tide is coming in at its normal pace.

ODO: Can we call that a surge?

PAVA: It is the tide.

ACT TWO

Leni moves a cloud icon across the touchscreen. It freezes over the mountains. Odo calls it a dramatic shift. Leni taps; the cloud does not move.

LENI: The forecast is stuck.

ODO: The cloud has stalled.

Seli brings over a paper map. Odo asks for a live graphic. Seli holds it behind him, upside down. Leni rotates it; the harbor is now on top of the studio map.

TAVI: A drop hit the lens.

[SFX: CAMERA WIPER CLICKS.]

SELI: The camera does not have a wiper.

[THE DROP SLIDES OFF THE LENS.]

ODO: We have cleared the view.

Leni says the shower is reaching the pier. Pava calls the dock workers under the awning. Tavi opens his umbrella, but wind flips it inside out. He lowers it and steps under shelter.

TAVI: The umbrella is now a bowl.

PAVA: Don't use it to collect rain. The wind is still changing.

ACT TWO-B

Back in the studio, Odo asks for a severe-weather banner. Leni says the rain has stopped. The map still glows red and the crawl says "HARBOR ALERT." Odo reads it gravely.

LENI: That message was scheduled for the storm last month.

ODO: The system does not know that.

SELI: It is showing yesterday's date.

Odo tells viewers to remain calm and check the date on their screens. Leni switches the map to green. The screen turns blue and labels the harbor a "weather-free zone."

PAVA: The clouds are back.

ODO: We need another update.

LENI: We need the current forecast. Light showers may pass, then clear skies.

ACT TWO-C

Tavi returns, wet at the shoulders. He stood outside to report while everyone else used the awning. Pava hands him a towel. Odo asks if this is a field emergency.

TAVI: It counts as being wet.

PAVA: And now you have a towel.

[ODO PUTS THE RED WEATHER BANNER ACROSS THE DESK. THE WET TOWEL DRIPS ON IT, MAKING THE INK RUN.]

LENI: The alert is dissolving.

ODO: Can we save it for the archive?

PAVA: It was last month's alert.

A fresh drop taps the studio window. Odo raises the alert banner again, but Leni points to the small cloud passing offshore. Seli turns the camera to the live view instead of the red graphic.

ODO: Are we still breaking?

LENI: The cloud is breaking apart.

PAVA: The pier is open. The workers are back outside.

TAVI: I have returned the umbrella. It is right side out now.

[SFX: UMBRELLA OPENS; A TRICKLE OF WATER FALLS FROM ITS FOLDED CANOPY INTO A BUCKET.]

SELI: The field report is still dripping.

TAVI: That is the umbrella drying, not the weather.[A GREEN ALERT CRAWL APPEARS: "NO WEATHER EVENT." ODO READS IT WITH A SERIOUS FACE.]

ODO: We have confirmed that no weather event is taking place.

LENI: That is not news.

ODO: It is a clear update.

SELI: The camera is still recording the window.

PAVA: You can see the pier again.
ACT THREE

Leni gives the final forecast plainly: a passing shower at the harbor and mostly clear skies for the afternoon. Odo summarizes without changing the colors. Tavi leaves the umbrella at the station to dry.

TAG

The sun comes out. Odo puts on sunglasses. A drop falls from the awning onto his nose.

ODO: Breaking update.

LENI: That is one drop.

[MUSIC: WEATHER THEME.]
