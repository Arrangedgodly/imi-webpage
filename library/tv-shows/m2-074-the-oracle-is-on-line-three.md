# The Oracle Is on Line Three

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised psychic hotlines, ominous prop readings, and caller suspense*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A TV oracle promises to reveal callers' futures from leaves, but every prophecy describes the caller's current room with increasingly dramatic language.

Cast: Oracle Mela, a macaque; host Soro, a gorilla; caller Evi, a capuchin; caller Palla, a lemur; and producer Venn, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Leaf Has Seen You"

### Cold open

INT. ORACLE STUDIO, NIGHT

[A phone rings. Mela places a leaf on a glass table.]

SORO: The oracle is ready to see your future.

EVI: Will I travel?

MELA: I see a path.

EVI: Where?

MELA: Under your feet.

EVI: I am standing in a hallway.

MELA: The future is very close.

[The studio lights dim. A phone rings again.]

VENN: Line three is waiting.

SORO: The mystery deepens.

### Act One

INT. ORACLE STUDIO

[Palla calls from home. Venn gestures for Mela to draw another leaf.]

PALLA: Will I get a new job?

MELA: I see a chair.

PALLA: I have a chair.

MELA: The chair is important.

PALLA: It is where I sit at work.

SORO: The oracle sees an employment path.

VENN: Can you make the answer more specific?

MELA: I see a desk.

PALLA: I have a desk too.

MELA: The desk is near the chair.

SORO: A powerful alignment.

[Palla holds the phone out. The camera shows a desk and chair.]

PALLA: You are describing my room.

MELA: I see what surrounds you.

VENN: We can offer an extended reading.

PALLA: What does it cost?

VENN: That is line two.

PALLA: I called line three.

VENN: The future has several lines.

### Act Two

INT. STUDIO

[Evi calls back. Mela turns over a new leaf. A small seed is stuck to it.]

EVI: The path under my feet is still a hallway.

MELA: A threshold is a journey.

EVI: I walked to the kitchen.

MELA: The kitchen was in your future.

SORO: The oracle is never wrong.

EVI: She told me I would find a cup.

MELA: Did you?

EVI: I found a cup.

VENN: The reading is confirmed.

EVI: The cup was in my hand.

MELA: Your hand has been holding the future.

[The camera zooms on the leaf. The seed falls onto the table.]

SORO: The oracle has received a sign.

MELA: The seed is a beginning.

VENN: What will it become?

MELA: A plant.

SORO: A mysterious plant.

MELA: If you put it in soil.

VENN: Can the caller take it home?

EVI: I am not in the studio.

VENN: The seed can travel by mail.

MELA: It must not be mailed.

SORO: Why?

MELA: The envelope has a hole.

[The camera zooms on a damaged envelope.]

### The paid reading

INT. CONTROL BOOTH

[Producer Venn hands Soro a card marked "EXTEND CALL."]

VENN: Offer the caller a deeper prophecy.

SORO: The caller asked about a job.

VENN: Tell her a shadow blocks the path.

MELA: There is a phone cord across the floor.

[The cord lies between Mela and the camera.]

VENN: That is not the kind of shadow we mean.

PALLA: I can hear you from line three.

VENN: The extended reading costs two shells.

PALLA: I am not paying to hear about a cord.

MELA: The leaf says the caller will not pay.

SORO: The oracle has predicted the cancellation.

VENN: We can offer a discount.

PALLA: I am hanging up.

[The phone line goes quiet. The studio card reads "CALLER LOST."]

### Act Three

INT. ORACLE STUDIO

[Venn holds the seed. Soro waits for a final prophecy.]

SORO: One last caller.

PALLA: I am still on line three.

VENN: Your reading is complete.

PALLA: I never got the answer about my job.

MELA: I see a new opportunity.

PALLA: What is it?

MELA: You could become a caller on the show.

PALLA: Is that a job?

VENN: It is unpaid.

PALLA: Then it is not a new job.

SORO: The future is full of surprises.

PALLA: The future should pay wages.

MELA: The leaf says you will leave the call.

PALLA: I am hanging up.

[The line clicks.]

SORO: The oracle predicted the ending.

VENN: The caller ended the call.

MELA: The leaf is silent now.

[The studio phone rings. Venn answers.]

VENN: Line three.

EVI: I planted the seed.

MELA: Where?

EVI: In the hallway.

MELA: It needs light.

EVI: It is next to the window.

SORO: A sign from the future.

MELA: A seed by a window.

### Final call

INT. ORACLE STUDIO

[The phone rings. Mela picks up.]

MELA: I see a missing comb.

CALLER: I am holding the comb.

MELA: The future has returned it to you.

CALLER: It was under the phone.

SORO: A completed journey.

VENN: Can we offer an extended reading?

[The caller hangs up.]

MELA: The line has gone quiet.

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, LATE NIGHT

[Mela places a plant pot beside the phone. The phone rings. She lets it ring.]

SORO: The oracle is unavailable.

VENN: The future can call back.

[The oracle leaves the phone unanswered as dawn light reaches the plant.]
