# The Lantern Festival Live

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live festival broadcasts, celebrity hosts, and countdowns to synchronized releases*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A live television crew tries to coordinate the grove's lantern release, but every lantern is tied to a different tree and no one has the same countdown.

Cast: Host Dema, a macaque; coordinator Lora, a gibbon; lantern maker Ketu, a capuchin; elder Nari, a gorilla; and field reporter Sola, a lemur.

## Episode: "Three, Two, Maybe"

### Cold open

EXT. FESTIVAL GROVE, NIGHT

[Lanterns hang from branches across the grove. Dema stands by a countdown clock.]

DEMA: We are seconds from the grand release.

LORA: The lanterns are tied to the trees.

KETU: They are meant to be carried.

NARI: The rain may come.

SOLA: The crowd is ready.

DEMA: Ten, nine, eight-

[Half the lanterns are already released. The rest remain tied.]

DEMA: The release is happening early.

LORA: Some are early. Some are still here.

### Act One

EXT. FESTIVAL STAGE

[Lora holds a clipboard. Ketu checks the lantern strings.]

LORA: Every lantern should be released on the same signal.

KETU: There are twelve strings.

NARI: There are twelve trees.

SOLA: The audience has lanterns too.

DEMA: We need a countdown everyone can hear.

[The microphone squeals.]

DEMA: The countdown is now louder.

LORA: The eastern grove cannot hear us.

SOLA: I can tell them.

DEMA: You are on the west side.

SOLA: The signal can travel.

KETU: The lantern strings cannot.

[Nari tugs one string. A lantern rises and then stops, caught in a branch.]

NARI: The tree has kept this one.

DEMA: A dramatic delay.

LORA: The knot is stuck.

KETU: Pull the other end.

[The lantern drops gently into Ketu's hands.]

### Act Two

EXT. EASTERN GROVE

[Sola reports live from a cluster of lanterns. The wind begins to blow.]

SOLA: We are ready on the east side.

DEMA: Stand by for the countdown.

SOLA: I cannot hear the countdown.

DEMA: We will flash the lights.

LORA: The lights are already flashing.

KETU: That is the camera.

NARI: The rain is coming.

DEMA: We need to release the lanterns before the storm.

LORA: The lanterns are not fireproof.

KETU: They are paper.

SOLA: The crowd can carry them to the covered grove.

DEMA: That is not the televised release.

NARI: It is safer.

DEMA: The audience came for the sky.

LORA: The sky is getting wet.

[They move the lanterns under a large canopy. The camera follows, catching people carrying them in a loose procession.]

DEMA: The festival has become mobile.

SOLA: The lanterns are moving together.

KETU: This was the original plan.

LORA: The original plan said "release."

KETU: It said carry them to the clearing.

NARI: We can lift them under the roof.

DEMA: We need a countdown.

SOLA: Count us in.

### Signal check

EXT. FESTIVAL GROVE

[Lora sends a runner to the east side with a hand bell. Sola carries a lantern.]

LORA: We will use the bell as the signal.

SOLA: The crowd can hear it.

DEMA: The microphone will not pick it up.

NARI: The audience can see the lanterns.

KETU: I made a larger lantern for the camera.

[The large lantern fills the frame and blocks the smaller ones.]

DEMA: We have lost the festival.

SOLA: We have one giant lantern.

LORA: We can move it behind the canopy.

NARI: The big lantern can light the path.

DEMA: The release can be seen from the clearing.

KETU: The clearing is getting wet.

### Act Three

EXT. COVERED GROVE

[The crowd holds lanterns at shoulder height. The rain begins outside. Dema reads from the countdown card.]

DEMA: Ten, nine, eight-

LORA: The east side is ready.

SOLA: The west side is ready.

KETU: The maker is ready.

NARI: The elder is ready.

DEMA: Three, two, one!

[The crowd lifts the lanterns. Their lights glow under the canopy. The trees outside are dark.]

SOLA: The lanterns are shining.

DEMA: They did not go into the sky.

NARI: They are still together.

KETU: The rain would have soaked them.

LORA: The crowd kept them dry.

DEMA: The camera has captured the release.

SOLA: They were not released.

DEMA: They were released from the ground.

NARI: They are still tied to the handles.

DEMA: The festival is now a suspended display.

LORA: We can end the broadcast here.

DEMA: We need one closing line.

KETU: The lanterns have found a safe place.

DEMA: The grove has found its light.

### Sign-off

EXT. COVERED GROVE

[The wind lifts the canopy edge. Ketu holds it down while the crowd shields the lanterns.]

DEMA: The picture is shaking.

LORA: The canopy is secure.

SOLA: The east side can still see us.

NARI: The lights are steady.

DEMA: Then we will close from here.

KETU: The lanterns are dry.

[The wind settles. The canopy holds.]

### Tag

EXT. COVERED GROVE, LATER

[The crowd carries the lanterns home. Dema's microphone remains under the canopy, glowing.]

SOLA: The broadcast is still live.

DEMA: The festival continues.

[The crowd carries lanterns through the covered grove while the live camera glows on.]
