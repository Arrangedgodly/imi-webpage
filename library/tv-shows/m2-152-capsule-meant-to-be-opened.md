# The Capsule Was Meant to Be Opened

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television time-capsule specials that promise shocking secrets and treat ordinary historical records as a grand reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Host Lala wants to surprise a neighborhood with a time capsule opened on camera. Archivist Mero wants to follow the date written on the box and keep the contents dry while producer Puck asks everyone to guess what is inside.

Cast: Lala, a macaque host; Mero, a gorilla archivist; Rumi, a lemur neighbor; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "The Box from 1996"

### Cold Open

[COMMUNITY HALL. A SEALED METAL BOX SITS ON A TABLE. LALA HOLDS A SHOVEL.]

LALA: After thirty years, we will discover what the neighborhood wanted us to know.

MERO: The label says "Open in 2026."

[PUCK STARTS OMINOUS MUSIC.]

RUMI: It is 2026.

LALA: The suspense is complete.

[THEY LIFT THE LID. INSIDE IS A PAPER ENVELOPE.]

LALA: Is it safe to open?

MERO: We should photograph the box and record the condition first.

PUCK: Can we hold the envelope up to the light?

MERO: No. It is old paper.

[MERO SETS THE ENVELOPE ON A CLEAN BOARD. RUMI READS THE DATE ON THE OUTSIDE.]

PUCK: A hidden message.

MERO: It is the inventory list.

### Act One

[PUCK HOLDS UP A SCHOOL PHOTOGRAPH.]

LALA: Who is this young macaque?

RUMI: That is me.

LALA: Were you expecting to be in the capsule?

RUMI: I put the photo there.

[MERO CHECKS THE INVENTORY.]

MERO: The list says one class photo, one bus map, and a note from the hall committee.

PUCK: Could we save the note for the end?

MERO: It is dated with the other items.

[LALA ASKS RUMI WHAT THE BUS MAP MEANS. RUMI POINTS TO A ROUTE THAT NO LONGER RUNS.]

LALA: Did the bus stop outside the hall?

RUMI: At the corner, if it was not full.

PUCK: Was that a secret?

RUMI: It was the schedule.

[MERO TAKES A PHOTO OF THE MAP BEFORE TURNING IT OVER. THE BACK HAS A HAND-DRAWN WALKING ROUTE.]

RUMI: That was the shuttle to the old market.

LALA: A lost route.

MERO: A discontinued shuttle.

### Act Two

[PUCK PLACES EACH ITEM UNDER A CAMERA. HE ASKS THE NEIGHBORS TO GUESS ITS IMPORTANCE.]

PUCK: What did the map reveal about the neighborhood's secret plans?

RUMI: We wanted a bus stop by the market.

MERO: The meeting minutes include the vote.

[LALA OPENS THE MINUTES. A PAGE IS STUCK TO THE BOTTOM OF THE BOX.]

LALA: The reveal is sealed.

MERO: The paper is damp.

[THEY SEPARATE THE PAGES CAREFULLY. THE MINUTES SHOW THAT THE SHUTTLE WAS FUNDED FOR ONE SUMMER.]

LALA: Could the vote have changed the neighborhood?

RUMI: It helped people reach the market for one summer.

MERO: We can say that without adding a hidden motive.

[PUCK REMOVES THE GRAPHIC "THE PLAN THEY HID" AND REPLACES IT WITH "SUMMER SHUTTLE MINUTES."]

PUCK: Can we call it a temporary experiment?

RUMI: We called it the summer shuttle.

[LALA ASKS WHETHER THE NEIGHBORHOOD SHOULD BRING THE ROUTE BACK. MERO SAYS THE CURRENT TRANSIT MAP IS AVAILABLE ON THE COMMUNITY WEBSITE. RUMI SAYS THE OLD MARKET IS NOW A SCHOOL.]

LALA: The past has changed.

MERO: The buildings changed.

[PUCK TRIES TO HOLD THE OLD MAP NEXT TO THE MODERN ONE. THE PAPER TEARS. MERO SLIDES IT ONTO A SUPPORT BOARD.]

### Act Three

[MERO READS THE FINAL NOTE ALOUD. IT THANKS THE NEXT NEIGHBORS FOR KEEPING THE HALL OPEN.]

LALA: That is a message for us.

RUMI: The hall is still open.

PUCK: Is this the emotional reveal?

MERO: It is the note's purpose.

[THE NEIGHBORS DECIDE TO ADD A NEW CAPSULE WITH CURRENT TRANSIT INFORMATION, A PHOTO, AND A COPY OF THE INVENTORY.]

RUMI: We should include what we want future neighbors to know.

LALA: What is useful now?

MERO: The hall hours, the current route map, and the opening date.

[PUCK WRITES EACH ITEM ON THE INVENTORY. RUMI ADDS A PHOTO OF THE CURRENT VOLUNTEERS.]

LALA: When should it be opened?

RUMI: In thirty years.

MERO: Write the exact year on the label.

[PUCK ADDS A COUNTDOWN GRAPHIC. MERO COVERS IT WITH THE OPENING DATE.]

[PUCK TRIES TO CLOSE THE NEW CAPSULE BEFORE THE INVENTORY IS FINISHED. THE LID CATCHES THE PAPER.]

MERO: Stop. The record is still in the hinge.

PUCK: The closing shot needs a clean seal.

RUMI: Then the clean shot can wait for the record.

[MERO LIFTS THE LID. A SMALL CORNER OF THE INVENTORY HAS FOLDED UNDER IT. HE FLATTENS IT ON THE TABLE.]

LALA: What should we write about today?

RUMI: That we almost sealed the list before we finished listing things.

PUCK: That sounds like a blooper.

MERO: It is the most accurate entry so far.

[PUCK REACHES FOR THE OMINOUS MUSIC BUTTON. RUMI TURNS THE MIXER TO A QUIET ROOM TONE.]

RUMI: The next people should hear the hall as it sounds when nobody is teasing a reveal.

[THEY RECORD THE ROOM: A CLOCK TICKS, SOMEONE STACKS CHAIRS, AND A DISTANT BUS BRAKES AT THE CORNER. PUCK LOOKS AT THE CAMERA, THEN LETS THE SOUND RUN.]

### Tag

[THE OLD MAP IS PLACED IN A CLEAR ARCHIVE SLEEVE. LALA HOLDS UP THE INVENTORY LIST.]

LALA: No shocking secrets?

MERO: No. Just records.

[PUCK TURNS OFF THE OMINOUS MUSIC.]
