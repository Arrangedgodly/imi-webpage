# The Medical Drama Has a Splinter

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: hospital dramas that give minor injuries the pacing and music of a season-ending crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A clinic team treats a tiny splinter while the television director keeps asking for an urgent monitor close-up.

Cast: Dr. Kori, a macaque; nurse Piri, a gibbon; patient Sefu, a capuchin; and director Daro, a baboon.

Episode: "Under the Surface"

COLD OPEN

INT. CLINIC EXAM ROOM - DAY

Sefu holds up one finger. A small splinter is visible. Daro gives a dramatic cue from the doorway.

DR. KORI: It is a splinter.

DARO: The music says otherwise.

NURSE PIRI: We can clean the area and remove it.

SEFU: I got it while moving a crate.

[DARO PLAYS A LOW, TENSE CHORD.]

ACT ONE

Piri sets out tweezers and a small bandage. Kori checks Sefu's hand.

DR. KORI: The splinter is shallow.

DARO: Is there a complication?

DR. KORI: There is a clean towel.

SEFU: I can hold still.

PIRI: You can rest your hand here.

[SEFU PLACES HIS HAND ON A PILLOW. Daro pushes a heart monitor closer.]

DARO: The monitor is not connected.

DARO: It needs to be in the scene.

ACT TWO

INT. CLINIC HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Kori and Piri discuss whether the splinter should be removed. Sefu waits in the exam room.

Daro: We need a hallway consultation.

DR. KORI: We already know what to do.

PIRI: The tweezers are ready.

Daro: What if the splinter is a clue about the crate?

DR. KORI: It is a piece of wood.

PIRI: The crate is wooden.

[DARO MOVES THE MUSIC CUE TO A LOUDER SPEAKER. THE PATIENT HEARS IT.]

SEFU: Is something wrong?

DR. KORI: No. We are removing the splinter.

INT. CLINIC EXAM ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Kori asks Sefu to lower the sleeve. Piri points the light at the fingertip, away from the camera.

DR. KORI: Would you prefer the camera to stay back?

SEFU: Yes. I can sit still if it is not right over my hand.

DARO: The close-up is the scene's tension.

PIRI: The patient's comfort is part of the visit.

[DARO MOVES THE CAMERA BACK. THE MONITOR STILL SHOWS THE HAND CLEARLY.]

DR. KORI: The splinter is visible. We can remove it with tweezers.

SEFU: Will it take long?

PIRI: It should be quick.

DARO: We could add a countdown.

DR. KORI: We will not rush the patient.

[SEFU NODS. PIRI CLEANS THE TWEEZERS. KORI WAITS FOR HIM TO REST HIS HAND.]

ACT TWO-B

INT. CLINIC EXAM ROOM - LATER

Kori uses the tweezers. Piri holds a light. Daro zooms in on Sefu's fingertip.

PIRI: Ready?

SEFU: Ready.

[THE SPLINTER COMES OUT. SEFU BLINKS.]

DARO: We need the monitor to beep.

DR. KORI: The patient is fine.

PIRI: I will clean the spot and put on a bandage.

SEFU: That was quick.

DARO: Can we do the removal once more for a cleaner shot?

DR. KORI: The splinter is already out.

PIRI: We should not repeat a procedure for the camera.

SEFU: I am ready for the bandage.

[PIRI CLEANS THE SPOT AND COVERS IT. KORI CHECKS THAT NO WOOD REMAINS.]

DR. KORI: The splinter is out. The visit is nearly finished.

DARO: I missed the dramatic pull.

SEFU: I did not feel it.

PIRI: That's a good result.

[DARO LOOKS AT THE CLOCK.]

DARO: We have six minutes of episode left.

DR. KORI: We can show the patient going home.

ACT THREE

INT. CLINIC FRONT DESK - AFTERNOON

Sefu picks up a care card. Kori explains the basic aftercare. Piri checks the hand.

DR. KORI: Keep the spot clean. Come back if it becomes red or painful.

SEFU: I can do that.

PIRI: The bandage is in place.

[DARO CUES THE TENSE MUSIC AGAIN. IT PLAYS OVER THE PRINTER AS THE RECEIPT COMES OUT.]

SEFU: Is the printer all right?

DR. KORI: It is finishing the visit.

SEFU: Can I go back to the crate?

DR. KORI: You can return to your day.

DARO: Could we film a follow-up check in an hour?

PIRI: There is no reason for another visit.

[SEFU SIGNS THE EXIT FORM AND LEAVES. DARO HOLDS THE DOOR OPEN.]

DARO: The patient is cleared.

DR. KORI: The patient is done.

PIRI: The room is ready for the next appointment.

[DARO STARTS THE TENSE MUSIC. THE ROOM IS EMPTY.]

DARO: We need a closing beat.

DR. KORI: The visit is finished.

PIRI: The printer has stopped.

[THE MUSIC CONTINUES FOR TWO NOTES, THEN DARO TURNS IT OFF.]

PIRI: We can use the room for the next patient.

TAG

Daro rewinds the splinter removal. It happens in one second.

Daro: We can slow it down.

PIRI: The patient is already home.
