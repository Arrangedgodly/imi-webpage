# The Baking Show of One Loaf

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: competitive baking programs, where simple kitchen mistakes trigger elaborate judging ceremonies*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: Three contestants bake one shared loaf, but the judges insist on crowning a winner for each step.

Cast: Host-contestant Odo, a baboon; baker Nara, a macaque; baker Leni, a gibbon; judge Piri, a capuchin; timekeeper Kito, a lemur.

Episode: "Proof of Patience"

COLD OPEN

INT. BAKING TENT - MORNING

Three monkeys stand around one bowl of dough.

ODO: Today, you will bake together.

NARA: Then who wins?

PIRI: Whoever proves the dough.

The dough rises while they argue. Kito rings the timer.

ACT ONE

The contestants are asked to knead the dough as a team. Nara presses it gently. Leni folds the far side. Odo calls this a dramatic collaboration. Piri scores their hand movements.

NARA: The dough is not a dance partner.

PIRI: It is for television.

Kito says there is still time for the dough to rest. Odo demands a signature garnish. Leni asks if they can shape the loaf first. Piri says the format requires a surprise round. She reveals a basket of raisins.

The recipe card says raisins are optional. Nara asks if optional means they can leave them out. Piri says it means they can decide on camera. Odo adds one raisin. Leni adds two. Nara says too many will make the loaf uneven. They count the raisins.

ACT TWO

The loaf is divided into three equal sections so each contestant can shape one part. Nara makes a smooth roll. Leni braids her section. Odo tries a spiral but the dough sticks to the board. He adds flour; the spiral becomes a small hill.

Piri tastes a pinch of raw dough, then stops herself. Odo asks if that is allowed. Piri says it is a judging instinct. Nara brushes the extra flour away. Leni braids the sections together so the loaf does not separate in the oven.

Kito announces a technical challenge: move the loaf onto the tray without breaking it. Everyone lifts from a different side. The loaf stretches and droops. Nara tells them to slide the tray under it. They do; the loaf remains intact.

Odo asks the camera to focus on the near disaster. Leni asks the crew to stop filming and let them get the loaf into the oven. Odo says the show needs suspense. Piri says the suspense can come from the bake.

ACT TWO-B

INT. BAKING TENT - LATER

The loaf rises in the oven. The contestants wait. Odo asks who is leading. Piri says there is no score for waiting. Nara says the dough needed proofing time. Leni watches the oven window. One side browns faster.

Kito says they can rotate the tray. Nara turns it carefully. The oven door does not close because the tray handle sticks out. Leni pushes the handle in. The loaf has risen almost to the top.

ODO: The bake is alive.

PIRI: It is dough.

ACT TWO-C

The dough rests while the crew prepares the oven. Piri asks each contestant to predict the bake. Nara says the loaf will brown at the edges first. Leni says the braid may cook unevenly where the strands cross. Odo predicts a golden crust because the show needs a confident answer.

Kito sets a timer. Odo wants to check the loaf every minute. Nara says opening the oven will drop the temperature. Piri asks if a closed oven creates suspense. The crew waits behind the glass. One side browns faster. Leni says the tray needs another quarter turn. Kito points out the handle is still outside the door. Nara uses an oven mitt to push it fully in, then rotates the tray.

Odo asks the camera to capture the steam when the door opens. Piri says they should let the loaf rest before cutting. Odo says that delays the tasting. Nara explains that a hot loaf can compress if cut too soon. Kito sets a second timer. The contestants wait as the crust cools. Odo tries to declare the wait an elimination round, but Piri says everyone stays.

ACT THREE

The loaf comes out golden. Piri cuts a slice. The braid holds the shape; the spiral is visible on top; the smooth roll gives the center a soft crumb. Every contestant's choice contributed.

PIRI: The winner is the loaf.

Odo asks if they can still award a ribbon. Piri ties it around the bread basket, not the loaf. The contestants share slices. Nara says the raisins are evenly spread. Leni says they should write down the method so they can make it again.

TAG

Kito rings the timer after the loaf is already gone.

ODO: A dramatic finish.

NARA: A late one.
