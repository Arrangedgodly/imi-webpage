# The Daring Dozen

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: stunt competitions, safety disclaimers, and slow-motion victory laps*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A group of performers compete to complete a series of harmless stunts, but the host keeps adding dramatic safety checks that take longer than the stunts.

Cast: Host Bessa, a baboon; contestant Liri, a lemur; contestant Jono, a gibbon; safety lead Mako, a gorilla; and replay editor Tavi, a macaque.

## Episode: "The Leaf Leap"

### Cold open

EXT. STUNT COURSE, DAY

[A short gap separates two low platforms. A soft mat lies beneath them.]

BESSA: The contestants will leap from one platform to the other.

MAKO: The platforms are one foot apart.

LIRI: I can step across.

JONO: I can jump.

BESSA: The challenge begins with a safety inspection.

[Tavi plays a slow-motion replay of the platform.]

BESSA: The gap looks larger in slow motion.

MAKO: It is still one foot.

### Act One

EXT. COURSE

[Mako checks the mat, the platforms, and the contestants' shoes.]

MAKO: The mat is secure.

BESSA: Is the mat ready?

MAKO: It is a mat.

LIRI: We are ready.

MAKO: I need to test the landing.

[He steps across the gap and lands on the mat.]

JONO: That was not a jump.

BESSA: It was a controlled crossing.

LIRI: My turn.

[She hops across. The crowd applauds.]

BESSA: A daring leap.

MAKO: She stepped.

TAVI: The replay will make it look bigger.

JONO: I will jump.

[He leaps, lands, and bounces slightly on the mat.]

BESSA: A spectacular flight.

MAKO: The mat bounced.

JONO: It helped.

BESSA: The contestants have advanced to the second challenge.

LIRI: What is it?

BESSA: Cross the same gap while carrying a leaf.

LIRI: I can do that.

MAKO: What kind of leaf?

### Act Two

EXT. COURSE

[Tavi brings three leaves of different sizes. Bessa examines them.]

BESSA: One is broad, one is narrow, and one is curled.

MAKO: All are soft.

LIRI: I'll take the broad leaf.

JONO: I'll take the curled one.

BESSA: The wide leaf may catch air.

MAKO: There is no wind.

[The wind machine turns on.]

MAKO: There is now wind.

LIRI: The leaf is pulling me.

JONO: Mine is spinning.

BESSA: The challenge is becoming airborne.

MAKO: Nobody is airborne.

[Tavi's replay shows Liri's foot briefly off the ground.]

BESSA: She has flown.

LIRI: I jumped.

MAKO: That is not flight.

BESSA: The replay suggests a takeoff.

MAKO: The mat pushed her.

JONO: Should I jump again?

MAKO: Not while the fan is on.

BESSA: We have a weather delay.

LIRI: We are indoors.

BESSA: The wind is part of the course.

[Mako unplugs the fan. The leaves settle. Jono crosses with his curled leaf.]

### Safety conference

EXT. STUNT COURSE

[Mako gathers the contestants and host beside the platforms.]

MAKO: The next stunt will be a backward leap.

LIRI: The platforms are one foot apart.

JONO: I can step backward.

BESSA: The title says "Daring Dozen."

MAKO: The safety rules say do not leap without seeing the landing.

TAVI: We have a replay of the test.

MAKO: The replay shows the mat.

BESSA: The mat has become a character.

LIRI: We can move the platforms farther apart.

MAKO: Then it is a different stunt.

JONO: We can move them closer.

MAKO: Then it is not a stunt.

BESSA: The countdown is ready.

MAKO: The safety team is not.

[The wind fan starts again. Mako turns it off.]

### Act Three

EXT. FINISH PLATFORM

[Bessa announces the winner. Mako stands beside a safety sign.]

BESSA: The champion is Jono, for the highest leap.

LIRI: He jumped one foot.

JONO: I carried the leaf.

MAKO: Both contestants completed the course safely.

TAVI: The replay shows Jono's leap lasting three seconds.

MAKO: The leap lasted less than one second.

BESSA: We added the slow-motion segment.

LIRI: Then I would like the replay trophy.

BESSA: We have one trophy.

JONO: We can share it.

MAKO: The trophy has a wide base.

BESSA: It is safe to hold.

[They lift the trophy. It is a small rubber leaf.]

LIRI: Is that the prize?

BESSA: It is flexible.

MAKO: It is safer than a metal trophy.

TAVI: We will show the winning moment one more time.

LIRI: You have shown it twelve times.

BESSA: The audience wants to see it again.

JONO: The audience has seen it in slow motion, reverse motion, and from above.

MAKO: We have not shown the mat inspection.

BESSA: Roll the mat inspection.

[Tavi plays footage of Mako stepping over the gap.]

### Tag

EXT. STUNT COURSE, EVENING

[Mako removes the mat. The platforms are moved together until they touch.]

BESSA: The course is now impossible to fail.

[The contestants step across without looking.]
