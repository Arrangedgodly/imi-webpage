# Mend It, Don't End It

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: instructional craft programs, makeover reveals, and last-minute supply substitutions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A patient craft host helps repair the troop's torn parade banner, while the producer keeps demanding a bigger reveal and the only matching fabric is the host's apron.

Cast: Host Omi, a capuchin; assistant Lell, a lemur; caller Bess, a macaque; producer Doma, a gorilla; and stitcher Haru, a gibbon.

## Episode: "A Banner for the Parade"

### Cold open

INT. CRAFT STUDIO, DAY

[A large green banner has a tear down the middle. Omi holds up thread.]

OMI: Today we will mend, not end, this banner.

DOMA: Can the reveal be more dramatic?

LELL: The banner is torn.

DOMA: Add suspense.

HARU: The tear is six feet long.

OMI: We can patch it with cloth and a running stitch.

DOMA: What if we use glitter?

OMI: The parade is outside.

DOMA: Glitter can travel.

[The banner slides off the table and covers Doma.]

DOMA: That is a reveal.

### Act One

INT. CRAFT STUDIO

[Omi lays the banner on the table. Lell sorts needles into a shallow bowl.]

OMI: First, align the torn edges.

HARU: The fabric is frayed.

OMI: We can fold the frayed edge under.

LELL: The thread keeps tangling.

OMI: Let the spool unwind gently.

[The spool rolls across the table and drops to the floor.]

DOMA: That is good television.

LELL: It is under the camera.

DOMA: Then the camera will go under.

OMI: We will cut a patch from spare fabric.

HARU: The spare fabric is the wrong shade.

OMI: A contrast patch can look intentional.

DOMA: Can it be a star?

OMI: It can be a square.

DOMA: A square star.

[Haru cuts a patch. The scissors snip the tablecloth.]

LELL: We have a smaller tablecloth now.

OMI: We will use that strip as the patch.

DOMA: The craft is expanding.

### Act Two

INT. STUDIO, LATER

[Omi sews along the banner. Doma whispers to the camera operator.]

DOMA: We need a transformation reveal.

OMI: We are mending the banner on camera.

DOMA: Can you cover it first?

OMI: It is already on the table.

LELL: I have an apron.

OMI: The apron is green.

DOMA: Is it the same green?

HARU: It is a slightly different green.

DOMA: That is contrast.

OMI: The apron is yours.

LELL: I can spare it.

OMI: We will cut a piece from the apron.

LELL: I just said I can spare it.

[Omi unpicks a pocket from the apron. A small acorn falls out.]

LELL: That was my snack.

DOMA: Now the patch has a story.

HARU: We can sew around the pocket instead.

OMI: Good idea. The pocket can hold a small note.

LELL: Or a small acorn.

DOMA: The banner now has a pocket?

OMI: It is a repair.

DOMA: We can reveal it as a feature.

[Omi stitches the pocket into place. The banner lies flat.]

OMI: A backstitch will secure the corners.

HARU: The needle is bent.

LELL: I have another.

DOMA: Hold for the reveal.

### Viewer question

INT. CRAFT STUDIO

[Omi reads a note from a viewer. Doma holds the patched banner at arm's length.]

OMI: Bess asks how to repair a tear without matching cloth.

HARU: Use a contrasting patch and secure all four corners.

DOMA: Can it be a triangle?

OMI: It can be any shape that covers the tear.

LELL: Could it be a pocket?

OMI: Only if the fabric can carry the weight.

DOMA: What should it hold?

LELL: A small note that says, "Do not pull."

[The banner sags as Doma pulls the pocket.]

HARU: The note is working.

### Act Three

INT. CRAFT STUDIO

[Omi lifts the banner. The patch is neat, but the pocket bulges slightly.]

OMI: The tear is closed, and the fabric is supported.

DOMA: Three, two, one - reveal!

[The crew pulls away a cloth. The banner is already visible.]

DOMA: That was too fast.

HARU: The repair is finished.

LELL: The pocket holds the acorn.

OMI: We can add one line of stitching to keep it closed.

DOMA: The audience needs a moment.

[Everyone waits. The acorn rolls out.]

LELL: We need the extra stitch.

OMI: We will sew it down.

DOMA: The reveal has a sequel.

HARU: The banner is ready for the parade.

OMI: We used cloth, thread, and an existing apron.

LELL: My apron has one pocket now.

DOMA: The banner has one pocket.

HARU: The stitch is strong.

[Haru gently shakes the banner. The patch holds. The acorn stays in place.]

DOMA: The banner is transformed.

OMI: It is repaired.

DOMA: The transformation is the repair.

LELL: Can we go to the parade?

OMI: After we return the scissors.

DOMA: Can we make that a reveal?

OMI: No.

### Tag

EXT. PARADE ROUTE, DAY

[The banner passes by. The patched pocket holds one acorn. Lell's apron has a single pocket.]

LELL: I can still carry a snack.

[The banner waves over the camera.]
