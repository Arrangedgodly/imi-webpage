# A House With Two Exits

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: house-hunting television that sells lifestyle fantasies before measuring practical routes and animal needs*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Two home seekers tour a sunny hollow with a real-estate host. They want different things: one wants a quiet sleeping branch, the other wants several exits and room for a troop.

Cast: Agent Nemi, a gibbon; buyer Kori, a macaque; buyer Vela, a langur; and nest inspector Ro, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Open-Plan Hollow"

### Cold Open

[THE GROUP STANDS BEFORE A HOLLOW TREE.]

NEMI: This home has charm, natural light, and a private entrance.

KORI: What about the back?

NEMI: The back is a tree.

[VELA CIRCLES THE TRUNK AND FINDS A SECOND OPENING.]

### Act One

[THE TOUR BEGINS. NEMI POINTS TO A SUNNY BRANCH.]

NEMI: This is the breakfast nook.

KORI: It is where I sleep.

NEMI: It can be both.

RO: The branch has a crack.

[RO TAPS THE BRANCH. A PIECE OF BARK FALLS.]

NEMI: A character detail.

KORI: A maintenance issue.

[Vela checks the hollow. It is deep but narrow.]

VELA: My troop needs room to turn around.

NEMI: It has an open floor plan.

RO: It has one floor.

[KORI LOOKS AT THE EXIT.]

KORI: I need a quiet place away from the path.

VELA: I need to be able to leave without crossing the sleeping area.

### Act Two

[THE GROUP TOURS A SECOND TREE WITH A WIDE ENTRANCE.]

NEMI: This property has generous access.

[THE TREE IS BESIDE A STREAM. THE ENTRANCE IS LARGE, BUT THE GROUND IS WET.]

RO: The roots hold water after rain.

VELA: There are two exits.

KORI: There is also a puddle.

[NEMI OPENS A DRAWER BUILT INTO THE DISPLAY.]

NEMI: The staging includes storage.

[THE DRAWER IS FULL OF DECORATIVE ACORNS.]

KORI: Who put these here?

NEMI: They are curated.

RO: They are blocking the drain.

[RO REMOVES THE ACORNS. WATER DRAINS OUT.]

[NEMI SHOWS THE BUYERS A THIRD LISTING ON A TABLET.]

NEMI: This one has a panoramic view.

[THE GROUP CLIMBS TO A HIGH BRANCH. IT OVERLOOKS A BUSY PATH.]

KORI: You can hear every visitor.

VELA: The view is excellent.

RO: The branch moves in the wind.

NEMI: It has a lively feeling.

KORI: It has no shelter.

[THE WIND BLOWS A LEAF INTO NEMI'S FACE.]

NEMI: This home offers a natural breeze.

RO: And a loose roof.

[Vela checks the branch joints. One is held by a vine.]

VELA: This is not ready for a large troop.

NEMI: We can discuss a repair credit.

KORI: We should discuss the safety before the view.

[NEMI TURNS THE TABLET AROUND. THE LISTING PHOTOS SHOW THE BRANCH WITHOUT THE VINE.]

RO: The photos were taken from the other side.

NEMI: The camera makes the branch look stronger.

KORI: The camera does not hold us.

[THEY DESCEND. RO ADDS A NOTE TO THE LISTING: "INSPECT THE UPPER BRANCH."]

[THE BUYERS RETURN TO THE SECOND TREE WITH THE TROOP.]

VELA: The upper platform can be raised above the damp roots.

KORI: The back exit is wide enough for everyone.

RO: We can install a simple latch on the drawer.

[NEMI HANDS OVER A REPAIR LIST.]

NEMI: These are the agreed changes.

KORI: We will inspect them before moving.

[THE TROOP TESTS THE ROUTE AGAIN. A SMALL MONKEY OPENS THE DRAWER, FINDS NO ACORNS, AND CLOSES IT.]

VELA: It works.

RO: The latch still needs a second screw.

[NEMI WRITES IT DOWN.]

NEMI: We will update the listing once the repairs are done.

RO: That will be a more accurate photograph.

[THE BUYERS KEEP THE REPAIR LIST ON THE KITCHEN WALL.]

NEMI: I will schedule the inspection after the platform is raised.

KORI: We will check the work first.

### Act Three

[THE BUYERS STAND BETWEEN THE TWO TREES.]

NEMI: Which home feels right?

KORI: I want the first hollow, if the branch is repaired.

VELA: I want the second, if we can raise the sleeping platform above the wet roots.

RO: Both repairs are possible.

NEMI: You can customize.

KORI: We can inspect before we move in.

VELA: We should ask the troop to test both paths.

[THE TROOP WALKS THROUGH THE SECOND TREE'S EXITS. ONE MONKEY USES THE DRAWER AS A STEP.]

RO: The drawer needs a latch.

NEMI: I will add it to the listing.

[THE BUYERS AGREE TO SHARE THE SECOND TREE AFTER THE ROOTS ARE DRIED AND THE PLATFORM IS RAISED.]

NEMI: This home has room for everyone.

KORI: After the repairs.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON THE LISTING CARD. IT READS "MOVE-IN READY." RO CROSSES OUT "READY" AND WRITES "AFTER WORK."]
