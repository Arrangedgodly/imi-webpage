# The Button That Won

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: studio game show*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: Three contestants race to answer questions and hit a buzzer, then discover the grand prize depends on a rule nobody can explain.

Cast: Host Bardo, a cheerful baboon; Ena, a quick capuchin contestant; Tull, a careful lemur; Finn, a confident macaque; and Ruth, the stage manager who knows where every prop came from.

## Episode: "Press If You Know"

### Cold open

INT. GAME SHOW SET, NIGHT

The host asks which animal can taste with its feet. Finn slams the buzzer.

FINN: A frog.

The buzzer plays a goat bleat. The audience laughs. Ruth walks onstage and unplugs the button.

RUTH: It has been doing that since rehearsal.

### Act one

On "Quick Think," contestants answer nature questions for a travel voucher. Ena, Tull, and Finn stand behind red buzzers. Bardo asks which bird can fly backward. Finn's buzzer bleats first.

Ruth finds Finn's button wired to a goat sound, Tull's silent, and Ena's set to play the theme. The judges cannot see who moved first.

The first round continues with a manual bell. Ena wins a point. Tull gives a thoughtful answer about how migrating birds use landmarks and the sun. Finn says the answer is too long. Bardo asks him to explain the route without using the word "basically." Finn takes longer than Tull.

### Act two

The second round asks contestants to match animal sounds. Ruth rolls out a panel of buttons, each labeled with a sound. The contestants hear a recording and guess the animal. The first sound is a frog. Finn hits the goat button by mistake. The second sound is a goat. He hits the frog button. Ena identifies both correctly, while Tull names a bird the producers do not have on the board.

The scoreboard gives ten points to the audience. Ruth says they have already won by sitting down. Ena suggests scoring accurate answers instead.

The bonus rewards buzzing before a question ends. Tull objects. Finn guesses "snake" after hearing "tail." The goat buzzer bleats.


[The buttons misfire. Ruth unplugs the control board.]
BARDO: We will use hand bells.
RUTH: Here are three.
ENA: Mine has a crack.
TULL: Mine has no handle.
FINN: Mine is a dinner bell.
BARDO: All bells are welcome.
RUTH: Don't ring until the question ends.
FINN: That's slower.
TULL: It is also fair.
BARDO: Which bird flies backward?
ENA: Hummingbird.
FINN: I knew that first.
RUTH: Your hand was down.
FINN: My thought was up.
TULL: Does thought score?
BARDO: We will ask the judges.
RUTH: No, we won't.
ENA: What is the prize?
BARDO: A travel voucher.
RUTH: The route is discontinued.
BARDO: Then a fruit basket.
FINN: Does it include bananas?
RUTH: It includes the sponsor's fruit.
TULL: That could be anything.
BARDO: Final question.
RUTH: Ask it clearly.
BARDO: What helps bats navigate?
TULL: Sound and landmarks.
ENA: Some use echolocation.
FINN: Can I ring now?
BARDO: You already answered.
FINN: I meant the bell.
RUTH: The bell is mine.
TULL: Who won?
BARDO: The audience got ten points.
RUTH: The scoreboard did that.
ENA: Give them the basket.
FINN: I'll share the bananas.
BARDO: That is the correct answer.

### Act three

Ruth replaces the faulty control box with three hand bells. Each contestant rings only after the full question. The last question asks what a group of monkeys does when a fruit tree is too high. Ena answers that they may climb, jump, or wait for the fruit to fall. Tull adds that different species move in different ways. Finn says he waits for someone else to get the fruit.

Tull wins by one point. The travel voucher, however, is only valid on a route that the airline stopped flying last month. Ruth reads the fine print and says the prize cannot be used. Bardo offers the show's backup prize: a basket of locally grown fruit. Finn asks if he can keep the goat buzzer. Ruth says it belongs to the studio.

The contestants decide to share the fruit with the audience. Ena takes the first piece to a child in the front row. Tull gives the voucher to Ruth so she can ask the sponsor for a different route. Finn takes a pear and admits he enjoyed the questions once he stopped trying to beat the sound effects.

Bardo closes the show by asking viewers to write in with better questions and working buttons. Ruth labels the broken buzzer "goat" and puts it on a shelf. The manual bells go back into the prop cupboard, where they should have been from the beginning.

### Tag

After the taping, Ruth presses the old button once. It plays a goat bleat.

A goat outside answers.

FINN: It wants the job.

Ruth unplugs the button again.

Before leaving the set, Ena explains that the trip voucher should list available routes and dates, rather than a destination the sponsor no longer serves. Ruth writes the note for next season. Finn asks if the show can give away a working buzzer. Ruth says she will check the prop inventory, then hands him a pear for the ride home. He takes the goat button off the table and makes one last bleat. The audience, still in the studio, answers with a chorus of goat sounds. Bardo bows. The stage crew begins stacking chairs while the contestants share the rest of the fruit.
