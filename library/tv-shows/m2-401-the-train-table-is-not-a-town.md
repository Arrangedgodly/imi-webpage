# The Train Table Is Not a Town

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: toy-review television, unboxing reveals, oversized product claims, and demonstrations staged for camera*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A toy-review host tests a train set advertised as an entire town, while the model train keeps leaving the only station in the package.

Cast: Host Davi, a macaque; reviewer Boro, a gibbon; child guest Neli, a capuchin; floor producer Omi, a baboon; camera operator Seli, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. REVIEW STUDIO - DAY

Davi opens a box marked "COMPLETE TOWN." Inside are a loop of track, one train and a cardboard station.

DAVI: We have a town.

NELI: Where are the houses?

BORO: They may be in the next box.

There is no next box.

ACT ONE

Boro reads the package description: the set includes a train, circular track, station and scenery. Davi points to the cardboard station. Neli asks if the train stops there. Boro places the train on the track and switches it on. It races around the loop and passes the station without slowing.

NELI: It did not visit the town.

DAVI: It saw the town.

OMI: We need a close-up of the station.

Seli zooms in. The train blurs past and blocks the station from view. Boro tries to slow it with the controller. The train stops at the opposite side of the loop.

BORO: It has chosen another district.

ACT TWO

Omi asks Neli to build the scenery while Boro reviews the controls. The cardboard trees have folded bases. Neli stands one up; it falls against the station. She unfolds the base and locks it. Davi asks whether the set has a bridge. Boro checks the box and finds a paper archway. It is too low for the train.

NELI: The bridge is for a picture.

BORO: It is still a bridge.

Omi directs Seli to frame the train crossing the arch. The lens can't see the track. Seli lifts the camera; now the train looks tiny. Davi asks for the product claims. Boro says the train runs on a loop and the station is a model. There is no working switch, passenger figure or alternate route.

DAVI: Can we call it a complete town?

BORO: We can call it a train set with a station.

ACT TWO-B

Neli asks if she can make a second station from the cardboard insert. Boro checks the instructions. The insert is not designed as a toy, and the set has no spare track. Omi suggests placing it beside the loop. Neli makes a platform from a fold in the packaging and sets it outside the track, leaving a safe gap.

NELI: This station is waiting for a train.

The train whips by. Davi asks if they can have a passenger. Neli draws a small ticket on a scrap of paper and lays it on the platform. Boro says it is not part of the set. Omi points to the overhead camera; the ticket looks official.

BORO: The train still doesn't stop.

NELI: We can stop it with the controller.

She slows the train and stops it beside the cardboard station. The motor clicks. Neli puts the paper ticket beside the platform. Davi turns the controller forward; the train departs immediately. The ticket remains.

Neli asks to test the track connection. One piece sits slightly higher than the next. She lifts the train off before it reaches the join and presses the rails together. Boro runs the train around the loop again. It crosses without stopping, then makes a clicking sound at the bridge arch.

NELI: The arch is touching the roof.

BORO: The train is too tall for the bridge.

Neli removes the archway and places it beside the station. The train passes. Davi asks if the product should include a scale figure. Boro says it includes no figures. Neli puts the paper ticket on the platform and waits until the train stops manually beside it.

NELI: That is as close as it gets.

The camera zooms in on the ticket while the train remains still. Omi signals a cut. Seli pulls back to show the entire loop and the empty cardboard town border.

DAVI: We have completed the whole journey.

NELI: It went around and stopped once.

ACT THREE

Boro gives the set a practical review: the track loop is easy to assemble, the train runs quickly, and the station is decorative. Neli adds that the scenery needs its bases unfolded and the bridge does not fit over the track. Davi reads the correction aloud: "A train set with one station." Omi removes the giant town graphic from the screen.

DAVI: Would you recommend it?

BORO: If you want a loop and a cardboard station.

NELI: I would add more track before promising a town.

The crew packs the set. Neli keeps the paper ticket for another toy. Boro folds the station back into its box. Davi holds the package beside the corrected product title.

TAG

Seli films a final close-up. The train circles once and stops exactly at the station because Boro has unplugged it.

FINAL IMAGE: The toy sits boxed under a smaller label reading "ONE LOOP, ONE STATION," while Davi's hand stays on the unplugged controller.
