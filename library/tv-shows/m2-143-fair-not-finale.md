# The Fair Is Not a Finale

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised science fairs that reward explosive demonstrations, dramatic rankings, and instant results over careful explanations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Toma wants a spectacular science-fair finale. Judge Rina wants each student to explain a repeatable test, while producer Puck keeps asking for a dramatic reveal before the results are ready.

Cast: Toma, a macaque host; Rina, a gorilla science teacher; Lila, a lemur student; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "The Results Need Another Day"

### Cold Open

[SCHOOL GYM. LILA STANDS BESIDE THREE PAPER TOWELS AND A CUP OF WATER.]

TOMA: Which paper towel absorbs the most?

LILA: I need to measure each one.

[PUCK STARTS A COUNTDOWN CLOCK.]

PUCK: We have thirty seconds to the reveal.

RINA: The test takes five minutes.

TOMA: Can we reveal the timer?

[LILA DIPS THE FIRST TOWEL. WATER DRIPS ONTO THE FLOOR.]

RINA: We also need a tray.

[PUCK SLIDES A TRAY UNDER THE TABLE.]

TOMA: What if we add food coloring so the result is more visible?

RINA: It will not change the absorbency, but we should keep the test simple.

LILA: I can put a mark on the cup to measure the starting level.

[PUCK HANDS HER A RULER. LILA SETS IT BESIDE THE CUP AND RECORDS THE LINE.]

### Act One

[LILA MARKS THREE TOWEL SQUARES. RINA CHECKS THE LABELS.]

RINA: Same size, same water amount, same wait time.

TOMA: Can we add a drumroll?

LILA: It might make me lose count.

[PUCK STOPS THE DRUMROLL. TOMA HOLDS A CARD READING "WHICH BRAND WILL WIN?"]

LILA: I am comparing absorbency, not brands.

RINA: Read the hypothesis.

LILA: The thicker towel may absorb more water.

[THE TIMER STARTS. LILA DIPS THE FIRST SQUARE, THEN THE SECOND.]

TOMA: The tension is rising.

RINA: The water level is changing.

[PUCK ZOOMS IN ON THE CUP. TOMA LEANS TO SEE.]

TOMA: I cannot tell who is ahead.

RINA: This is not a race.

### Act Two

[LILA WEIGHS THE WET TOWELS ON A SMALL SCALE. THE DISPLAY READS 18, 19, AND 18.]

TOMA: We have a winner by one gram.

RINA: We have one result.

LILA: I should repeat the test.

[PUCK BRINGS OUT A GOLD MEDAL. RINA SETS IT ASIDE.]

RINA: We need another sample from each towel.

TOMA: Could we give a preliminary trophy?

LILA: It is too close to call.

[THE SECOND ROUND BEGINS. THE FIRST TOWEL TEARS WHEN LILA LIFTS IT. THE WATER SPILLS INTO THE TRAY.]

TOMA: An upset.

RINA: The sheet tore. We should note it.

[LILA RECORDS THE RESULT. PUCK POINTS TO THE CLOCK.]

PUCK: We are behind schedule.

RINA: Science does not change because the program is short.

[THEY REPEAT THE TEST. THE THICKEST TOWEL HOLDS MORE WATER, BUT ONLY BY A SMALL AMOUNT. LILA WRITES DOWN THE NUMBERS.]

TOMA: Can you make the result more dramatic?

LILA: I can show the chart.

[THE CHART HAS THREE BARS OF SIMILAR HEIGHT.]

TOMA: Viewers like a clear gap.

LILA: The bars are close because the results are close.

RINA: Keep the scale labeled.

[PUCK CHANGES THE CHART AXIS TO START AT ZERO. THE BARS LOOK EVEN CLOSER.] 

TOMA: That is less dramatic.

RINA: It is more honest.

[THE STUDENTS SET UP THEIR BOARDS FOR THE VISITORS. RINA CHECKS THAT EACH ONE SHOWS THE METHOD AS WELL AS THE RESULT.]

RINA: Which sample did you repeat?

LILA: The second one.

TOMA: The audience can ask after the demonstration.

[PUCK TAKES THE TIMER OFF THE TABLE.]

### Act Three

[TOMA FACES THE CAMERA. LILA STANDS BESIDE HER CHART.]

TOMA: The thick towel absorbed the most in our tests.

RINA: It was a small difference, and one sample tore. More trials would help.

LILA: My results support the hypothesis a little.

[PUCK PUTS THE GOLD MEDAL BACK IN ITS BOX.]

TOMA: We do not have a winner?

RINA: We have a careful report.

[LILA ADDS THE TEST DATE AND WATER AMOUNT TO THE DISPLAY. RINA CHECKS THAT THE SCALE IS ZEROED.]

TOMA: The fair is open. Judges can ask questions.

[THE AUDIENCE APPROACHES THE BOARD. ONE VISITOR ASKS IF THE TOWEL WORKS ON A SPILL. LILA SAYS SHE TESTED WATER, NOT JUICE.]

VISITOR: Could you test juice next?

LILA: We would need permission to use the gym floor and a new set of towels.

RINA: She can plan a follow-up test with the right cleanup materials.

[PUCK ROLLS THE TRAY TO THE SINK. TOMA HOLDS THE DISPLAY AWAY FROM THE WATER.]

### Tag

[PUCK HOLDS UP THE MEDAL.]

PUCK: Can we award it for best chart?

RINA: The chart is legible.

[LILA TAKES THE MEDAL AND HANGS IT ON THE "SUPPLIES" HOOK.]

TOMA: No winner shot?

LILA: I can show the data.

[RINA TAKES A PHOTO OF THE BOARD FOR THE FAIR RECORD. THE CAMERA HOLDS ON THE NUMBERS.]

LILA: We need it for next year's fair.
