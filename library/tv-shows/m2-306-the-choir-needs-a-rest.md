# The Choir Needs a Rest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality choir competitions, rehearsal montages, emotional backstories, and judges who demand a bigger final note*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A neighborhood choir tries to prepare a single song for the town festival while its conductor keeps adding high notes that the singers cannot reach.

Cast: Conductor Nala, a macaque; singer Vex, a gibbon; singer Palla, a capuchin; accompanist Omi, an orangutan; judge Tavi, a ring-tailed lemur; and stage manager Rell, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The High Note"

### Cold Open

[REHEARSAL ROOM. NALA RAISES BOTH HANDS.]

NALA: On the final note, everyone rises.

PALLA: In pitch or from the chairs?

NALA: Both, if possible.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[CHOIR ROOM. PIANO PLAYS.]

The choir rehearses a simple folk song. Vex sings a clear middle harmony. Palla hums lower. Nala asks everyone to finish with a high note that sounds triumphant.

PALLA: I cannot reach that note.

NALA: Reach emotionally.

OMI: I can change the key.

NALA: The key is part of the arrangement.

Rell announces that the competition's judges expect a memorable finale. Tavi says the choir needs an emotional arc. Nala tells each singer to share a reason they joined the choir. Vex says he likes singing with neighbors. Palla says rehearsals are near the snack table. Omi says the piano needs tuning.

Nala asks Vex to sing his line alone for a close-up. He sings it comfortably, but the edit monitor shows only his face. Palla says the choir is meant to sound like a group. The producer asks for a reaction shot of Nala listening. She closes her eyes and nods with theatrical intensity.

PALLA: You look like you are hearing a secret.

NALA: I am hearing Vex sing.

The production team adds a backstory card about Palla's first rehearsal. It says she joined after moving to town, though Palla says she has lived here all her life. Rell removes the card. The camera asks Omi to describe the piano's journey. Omi says it was tuned last Tuesday.

TAVI: That is not much of a journey.

OMI: It was out of tune. Now it is in tune.

The choir attempts a rehearsal with the camera crew standing among them. The crew's clapping keeps falling on the wrong beat. Vex stops, gives each camera operator a part to sing, and the harmony improves. Nala watches the monitor. For the first time, the framing includes the whole choir.

### Act Two

[MONTAGE: REHEARSAL, SNACK BREAK, REHEARSAL.]

The choir tries the high note with added drums, a cymbal, and a descending slide. Each effect makes the ending less clear. The drummer overshoots the beat. Palla's tail catches a cymbal mallet. Omi changes the key, but Nala changes it back.

The judge visits rehearsal and asks Nala to explain the finale. She describes a towering note that represents the choir's journey. The singers try it once. Vex reaches it; Palla produces a squeak; Omi's piano gives a small rattle. The judge asks whether the note is part of the song or part of the television edit.

NALA: It was in the original arrangement.

OMI: The arrangement is a page of notes. We can change it.

Nala asks each singer to mark the highest comfortable note. Their ranges overlap, but not at the pitch she chose. She moves the final chord down. The choir sings together, and the producer asks for a slow-motion shot of the page turning.

PALLA: The page is not the ending.

Rell uses the page as a fan while the singers rest. The camera catches the moment and labels it "the breath before the finale."

NALA: We need a big finish.

VEX: We need to finish together.

Tavi listens from the front row. He says the best part is the harmony before the high note. Nala says audiences expect a climax. Palla demonstrates a lower closing chord that everyone can sing comfortably.

The chord sounds warm and full. Nala asks for one more take. The choir sings it again, this time while standing in a circle. Their voices blend.

### Act Three

[STAGE AT THE FESTIVAL.]

Nala leads the choir onto the stage. The crowd is ready. The band begins the arranged version, but the cymbal cue comes too early. Omi stops playing and signals the choir to hold. Vex hums the first pitch; Palla joins; the full choir begins a cappella.

They sing the folk song as rehearsed, without the added high note. The last chord lands together. The audience applauds. The judges confer and ask whether the choir intentionally changed the arrangement.

NALA: We returned to the version everyone could sing.

TAVI: That was the strongest ending.

Rell asks whether they won. Tavi says the judges gave them the award for best ensemble. Nala thanks the singers rather than taking the microphone alone.

PALLA: Can we sing one more song?

NALA: After a rest.

### Tag

[BACKSTAGE.]

Nala tries the high note by herself. It cracks. Omi lowers the key. Nala sings the note comfortably and smiles.

NALA: That is the note.

VEX: It is lower.

NALA: It is ours.

The choir walks toward the snack table, still humming the final chord.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
