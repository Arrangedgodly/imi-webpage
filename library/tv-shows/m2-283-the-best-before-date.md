# The Best Before Date

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: investigative consumer television, where a host treats a best-before label as a forensic countdown*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Consumer host Seli investigates why the shop's pantry labels are confusing, but the crew keeps treating ordinary date marks as a food emergency.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; food expert Nara, macaque; manager Odo, baboon; shopper Piri, gibbon; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Date on the Jar"

### Cold Open

[COUNTER. A JAM JAR WITH A LABEL.]

SELI: This jar expires tomorrow.

NARA: It says best before tomorrow.

ODO: We should evacuate the pantry.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[SELI FACES THE CAMERA.]

Seli says viewers have called about pantry labels. Nara explains that a best-before date is about quality, while a use-by date is about safety.

PIRI: This jar is best before tomorrow.

ODO: The date is approaching.

NARA: It is jam.

SELI: We need to examine it.

Piri says the jar is unopened. Odo places it in a clear evidence tray. Nara checks the lid; it is sealed and not bulging.

NARA: It appears fine.

SELI: Can we open it?

NARA: We can inspect the label first.

Tavi zooms in. The label says "Best before: 10/12." The month and day are unclear.

ODO: It could be December.

PIRI: It could be the twelfth day of October.

SELI: The mystery deepens.

### Act Two

[THE CREW CHECKS THE PANTRY LOG.]

Odo finds a receipt showing the jar was purchased last week. Nara says that does not clarify the date format. Seli asks the store manager.

ODO: The label printer uses month and day.

PIRI: Then it is October twelfth.

NARA: We are not in October yet.

SELI: We are.

The crew checks the wall calendar. It is October eleventh. Odo says the jar is indeed best before tomorrow.

SELI: The countdown is real.

NARA: The date is a quality guide.

Piri asks whether the jar can be used after the date. Nara says quality may decline, but the label is not an alarm. Odo asks if they should taste it live.

NARA: Only if the seal is intact and the jar looks normal.

They inspect it again. The lid is flat, no leaks, and no visible mold. Seli asks the camera to zoom in for the reveal.

[THE SHOP FLOOR IS REVIEWED.]

Nara checks several other jars. Some show month and day; others spell out the month. Odo says the labels come from different suppliers. Seli asks if the store can use one format.

ODO: We can request it.

PIRI: A shopper could read the wrong date.

NARA: We can put a guide beside the shelf.

Tavi prints a small card explaining "best before" and "use by." Odo places it near the jam. The card covers a price label.

SELI: We have clarified the date and hidden the price.

PIRI: The card needs to move.

They shift it to the shelf edge. It falls over. Nara clips it upright to the display rail.

NARA: Now it is visible.

ODO: The jam is still where it was.

SELI: The investigation has changed the shelf, not the jar.

### Act Three

[THE JAR IS OPENED.]

Nara checks the smell and texture, then spreads a small amount on a cracker. She says it looks and smells normal. Piri tastes a tiny bit and says it tastes like jam.

SELI: The pantry has survived.

ODO: We have a quality report.

NARA: The date tells us when the manufacturer expects best quality, not that the jar becomes unsafe at midnight.

Piri asks how the shop can make labels clearer. Odo proposes writing the month in letters rather than numbers. Seli says the store should do that on future jars.

Tavi films the label being replaced with "Best before: October 12."

[SELI REVIEWS THE FINAL SEGMENT.]

The camera shot shows the jar's date, the explanatory shelf card, and the intact lid. Seli says viewers should check storage instructions as well as the printed date.

NARA: The label is one part of the information.

PIRI: The jar is still sealed.

ODO: The price is visible again.

Tavi moves the card a little farther from the price sticker. The card stays upright.

SELI: The pantry is organized.

NARA: The shelf is clearer.

SELI: The label is still a label. Nara, please put the guide back.

Nara tries to slide the jar onto the shelf, but the card blocks its space. She turns the card sideways; it falls flat. Tavi props it against the price rail, and Seli puts the jar back behind the date guide.

TAVI: Now the card points to the date.

PIRI: And the jar has its shelf back.

### Tag

[THE CREW CLEARS THE COUNTER.]

Seli places the jar back on the pantry shelf. Odo keeps the evidence tray.

NARA: We don't need that.

ODO: It is a tray.

PIRI: It has a label now.

SELI: The jar is not a case.

[SFX: JAR SETS DOWN, THEME OUT.]
