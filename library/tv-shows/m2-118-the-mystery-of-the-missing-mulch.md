# The Mystery of the Missing Mulch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cozy village mysteries that expand a petty garden problem into a web of elaborate clues and social suspicion*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: In the village garden, a pile of mulch disappears before the planting fair. Amateur sleuth Aunt Luma questions every neighbor, until the mulch itself gives away its destination.

Cast: Aunt Luma, a macaque; gardener Fenn, a capuchin; shopkeeper Viri, a gibbon; and neighbor Odo, a baboon.

## Episode: "Bark at Midnight"

### Cold Open

[THE GARDEN BED IS EMPTY. A LITTLE MULCH REMAINS ON THE PATH.]

LUMA: Someone has taken the mulch.

FENN: It was here yesterday.

LUMA: We have a mystery.

[ODO WALKS BY WITH MULCH ON ONE SHOE.]

LUMA: And a suspect.

### Act One

[LUMA OPENS A NOTEBOOK.]

LUMA: Who was near the garden last night?

VIRI: I closed the shop at dusk.

ODO: I was home.

FENN: I moved the wheelbarrow.

LUMA: Where?

FENN: To the shed.

[LUMA INSPECTS THE WHEELBARROW. IT IS EMPTY.]

LUMA: The vessel has returned without its cargo.

VIRI: The wheelbarrow is not a vessel.

LUMA: Every clue has a character.

### Act Two

[LUMA FOLLOWS MULCH BITS TO THE SHED. THE DOOR IS OPEN.]

LUMA: The trail ends here.

FENN: The mulch was damp. It stuck to the wheel.

[ODO ARRIVES WITH A BAG OF MULCH.]

ODO: I brought this from the compost heap.

LUMA: You took it from the garden?

ODO: No. I made it from leaves.

[THE GROUP EXAMINES THE BAG. IT CONTAINS LEAVES AND BARK.]

VIRI: Then where did the original pile go?

[FENN POINTS TO A NEWLY MULCHED BED.]

FENN: I moved it to cover the roots before rain.

LUMA: You moved the evidence.

FENN: I moved the mulch.

[LUMA LOOKS AT THE CLOUDS. RAIN BEGINS.]

[LUMA QUESTIONS THE NEIGHBORS AT THE VILLAGE TEA TABLE.]

LUMA: Viri, did you hear anything last night?

VIRI: I heard the wheelbarrow squeak.

FENN: That was me.

LUMA: At what hour?

FENN: After the evening meal.

ODO: I heard rain.

LUMA: It had not rained yet.

ODO: I heard the forecast.

[LUMA WRITES "PREDICTED RAIN" IN HER NOTEBOOK.]

VIRI: You have written down a weather report as a clue.

LUMA: The weather can explain a motive.

FENN: The roots were dry.

LUMA: Then the mulch moved before the rain.

[ODO SHOWS THE MULCH BITS ON HIS SHOE.]

ODO: This is from my compost bag, not the garden pile.

[LUMA SNIFFS THE BARK.]

LUMA: Different leaves.

VIRI: The trail is telling us the truth.

LUMA: The trail is telling us two truths.

[FENN CARRIES THE EMPTY WHEELBARROW PAST. ITS WHEEL SQUEAKS.]

LUMA: There is the sound you heard.

FENN: I should oil it.

LUMA: After we finish the case.

[THE VILLAGERS GATHER TO PREPARE THE GARDEN FOR THE PLANTING FAIR.]

FENN: The rain made the bed ready.

LUMA: We still need to spread the remaining mulch.

[ODO POURS HIS HOMEMADE MULCH INTO A SECOND BED.]

VIRI: The planting fair has two beds now.

LUMA: A mystery can lead to a useful discovery.

[THE WHEELBARROW WHEEL SQUEAKS AGAIN.]

FENN: I will oil it before the next trip.

[HE OILS THE WHEEL. THE SOUND STOPS.]

LUMA: The case has a second resolution.

ODO: The wheel was not missing.

LUMA: No, but it was a suspect sound.

[LUMA ADDS A FINAL ENTRY TO HER NOTEBOOK: "CAUSE: MULCH MOVED FOR ROOTS."]

VIRI: The village will remember the case.

LUMA: The garden will remember the mulch.

FENN: And I will remember to write a note before moving anything.

[FENN WHEELS THE MULCH CART BACK TO THE SHED. THE WHEEL ROLLS SILENTLY.]

LUMA: A quiet suspect.

FENN: A repaired wheel.

[THE VILLAGERS RETURN TO THEIR GARDEN WORK.]

LUMA: I will close the notebook and open the garden gate.

[THE VILLAGERS CARRY THE LAST BAG OF MULCH TO THE FAIR BED.]

FENN: This should cover the roots.

ODO: There is enough for both beds now.

LUMA: The case began with an empty space.

VIRI: Now the garden is ready.

[THEY SMOOTH THE MULCH AROUND THE PLANTS. LUMA CLOSES HER NOTEBOOK.]

LUMA: No more clues.

[THE WHEELBARROW ROLLS QUIETLY PAST.]

### Act Three

[THE VILLAGERS GATHER UNDER THE TOOL SHED ROOF.]

LUMA: The mulch was not stolen. It was relocated to protect the roots.

FENN: I should have left a note.

ODO: I can add more from the compost heap.

VIRI: I will bring a sign for the planting fair.

[LUMA WRITES "MULCH MOVED TO ROOT BED" IN HER NOTEBOOK.]

LUMA: The mystery is solved.

[THE RAIN WASHES THE LAST MULCH BITS OFF ODO'S SHOE.]

ODO: My alibi is clean now.

LUMA: The mud will remember.

[EVERYONE LAUGHS. THE GROUP CARRIES THE EXTRA MULCH TO A SECOND BED.]
