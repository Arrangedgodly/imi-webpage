# Bridgewatch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rescue dramas that stage urgent cliffhangers around a missing animal and a limited-response crew*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A storm leaves a young monkey stranded on the far side of a creek. The rescue team keeps receiving urgent calls, but the animal has already found a safe branch and is waiting for help across a shallow crossing.

Cast: Rescuer Anu, a langur; team lead Bo, a baboon; caller Meli, a macaque; and young Rook, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Other Side"

### Cold Open

[RAIN FALLS. A RADIO CRACKLES.]

MELI: We have a monkey on the far bank.

BO: Is the water rising?

MELI: No. He is holding a branch.

[THE CAMERA CUTS TO ROOK, SITTING SAFELY IN A LOW TREE.]

ANU: He looks comfortable.

BO: We are still responding.

### Act One

[THE CREW ARRIVES WITH ROPE AND A FLOAT.]

BO: We need to assess the current before crossing.

ANU: The water is shallow by the stones.

MELI: Rook tried to cross earlier and slipped.

ROOK: I got wet.

BO: Did you hurt yourself?

ROOK: No.

[THE CREW CHECKS THE BANK. ANU TESTS THE STONES WITH A POLE.]

ANU: The crossing is safe if we go one at a time.

BO: We will secure a line anyway.

[THE ROPE IS FASTENED TO A TREE. ROOK WATCHES.]

ROOK: Is the rope for me?

ANU: It is for the rescuers.

ROOK: Then I can wait.

### Act Two

[ANU CROSSES WITH THE ROPE. BO HOLDS THE LINE. THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN.]

BO: We are in position.

ANU: The branch is stable. Rook can climb down by himself.

ROOK: I can come to you.

ANU: Take your time.

[ROOK CLIMBS TO A LOWER BRANCH, THEN TO A STONE. HE STOPS.]

ROOK: That stone is slippery.

ANU: Good catch. Use the next one.

[ROOK TAKES THE NEXT STONE AND REACHES THE BANK.]

MELI: He made it.

BO: The rope was ready if needed.

[THE RADIO CRACKLES AGAIN.]

VOICE: Another call at the north path.

BO: We have a safe transfer. We will check it.

[ROOK TAKES A FRUIT FROM HIS POCKET.]

ANU: Where did you get that?

ROOK: I brought a snack.

[THE CREW REPLAYS THE RADIO CALL FOR THE CAMERAS.]

BO: "A monkey stranded on the far bank."

MELI: He was waiting in a tree.

ROOK: I could see the team.

ANU: What made you stop there?

ROOK: The branch was dry. The stones were wet.

BO: That was a good choice.

[THE PRODUCER HANDS BO A CARD THAT READS "HIGH-RISK RESCUE."]

BO: We do not need this card.

[HE SETS IT DOWN AND ASKS ROOK TO POINT OUT THE SLIPPERY STONE.]

ROOK: That one.

ANU: We can mark it so the next person takes the safer route.

[THE TEAM PLACES A SMALL MARKER BESIDE THE STONE.]

MELI: We can also move the branch from the path.

[ROOK SHOWS THEM THE STICK HE DRAGGED AWAY.]

BO: The rescue is complete. Now we prevent the next call.

[A CAMERA OPERATOR ASKS ROOK TO REPEAT THE CROSSING FOR A CLOSE-UP.]

ROOK: I already crossed.

BO: We will not ask you to do it again for the camera.

ANU: The first crossing is the story.

MELI: And the safe route is marked.

[THE PRODUCER PUTS AWAY THE REPLAY CARD.]

BO: We can film the team checking the path.

[ANU TESTS EACH STONE WITH THE POLE. ROOK WATCHES FROM HIS FAMILY'S SIDE.]

ROOK: The wide stone is dry.

ANU: It is a better step.

[THE TEAM TAKES THE GEAR BACK ACROSS ONE AT A TIME.]

MELI: No one needs to hurry.

[THE RADIO STAYS QUIET. BO NODS AT THE CAMERA.]

BO: That is a good sign.

### Act Three

[THE CREW WALKS ROOK BACK TO HIS FAMILY. THEY CHECK HIS HANDS AND FEET.]

BO: No injuries.

MELI: His family is here.

[ROOK RUNS TO HIS MOTHER, WHO EMBRACES HIM.]

ANU: The crossing was manageable, but the storm made the stones slick.

BO: We will mark the safe route and clear the branch debris.

[THE TEAM PLACES A SMALL WARNING SIGN BESIDE THE CREEK.]

ROOK: Can I keep the rope?

BO: No. The rope stays with the team.

ROOK: Can I keep the sign?

MELI: The sign stays by the creek.

[ROOK HANDS BACK THE SIGN. HE USES THE ROPE'S TAIL TO DRAG A STICK AWAY FROM THE PATH.]

BO: That is not how we use rescue equipment.

ANU: But the path is clear.

[THE TEAM PACKS THE ROPE. THE RADIO CALLS AGAIN.]

VOICE: The north path has a loose branch.

BO: We are on our way.

[ROOK'S FAMILY WAVES. THE CREW LEAVES AT A WALKING PACE.]
