# Unclaimed, but Not a Clue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: lost-and-found segments that turn ordinary unclaimed objects into sentimental mysteries with dramatic owner reveals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Bina wants a suspenseful reveal for the station's lost umbrella. Custodian Ralo needs the items labeled and returned before the waiting room closes.

Cast: Bina, a lemur host; Ralo, a gorilla custodian; Pex, a macaque clerk; and Nori, a capuchin commuter.

## Episode: "The Umbrella with No Name"

### Cold Open

[STATION LOST-AND-FOUND. BINA HOLDS A CLOSED UMBRELLA INSIDE A CLEAR BAG.]

BINA: Its owner may be closer than we think.

RALO: It was left by the north bench.

[PEX READS THE TAG.]

PEX: The tag says "North bench, Tuesday."

BINA: A clue.

RALO: A location.

[NORI ENTERS AND POINTS TO THE UMBRELLA.]

NORI: That is mine.

[BINA TURNS TO CAMERA.]

BINA: The mystery has a witness.

NORI: It is raining. Can I have it back?

### Act One

[BINA INVITES NORI TO SIT AT A SMALL TABLE. RALO OPENS THE LOGBOOK.]

BINA: Before we reunite you, tell us what the umbrella means to you.

NORI: It keeps rain off my head.

PEX: That is its intended use.

[BINA ASKS PEX TO SHOW THE OTHER UNCLAIMED ITEMS: A SCARF, A WATER BOTTLE, AND A SINGLE GLOVE.]

BINA: Which of these has the deepest story?

RALO: The glove has been here one day.

BINA: A short story.

[PEX HOLDS UP THE GLOVE. IT IS A SMALL WORK GLOVE WITH A NAME LABEL INSIDE.]

PEX: The print shop sent someone over to claim it.

RALO: We called them.

[BINA PUTS THE GLOVE DOWN AND RETURNS TO THE UMBRELLA.]

BINA: How did you recognize it?

NORI: The wooden handle has a scratch from the bus door.

[PEX CHECKS THE FORM. THE DESCRIPTION MATCHES.]

BINA: Is there a way to verify it is your umbrella?

NORI: The scratch is right there.

[PEX COMPARES THE FORM WITH THE HANDLE. THE SCRATCH MATCHES THE DRAWING.]

PEX: Verified.

BINA: That was quick.

RALO: The form has a box for the description.

### Act Two

[THE CREW SETS UP A "REVEAL" CURTAIN. BINA STANDS ON ONE SIDE; NORI WAITS ON THE OTHER.]

BINA: When the curtain opens, you will see the object that has traveled back to you.

RALO: The umbrella has been in the room all morning.

[PEX PULLS THE CURTAIN. IT CATCHES ON THE UMBRELLA HANDLE AND TIPS THE TABLE.]

NORI: That is the scratch I mentioned.

[BINA PICKS UP THE UMBRELLA. A SMALL RECEIPT FALLS FROM THE BAG.]

BINA: Another clue.

PEX: It is the claim ticket.

RALO: The desk issued it after NorI described the umbrella.

[NORI TAKES THE TICKET AND SIGNS THE LOG.]

BINA: Do you have anything to say to viewers?

NORI: I am walking to the bus stop.

[BINA ASKS IF SHE CAN WALK WITH NORI FOR AN EXIT SHOT. RALO POINTS OUT THE RAIN IS STILL FALLING.]

BINA: I could stand under the umbrella.

NORI: If you want to get wet on one side.

[BINA TRIES TO FIT BESIDE NORI. THE UMBRELLA TILTS AND COVERS THE CAMERA LENS.]

RALO: One claim, one umbrella.

[BINA STEPS BACK INTO THE STATION.]

RALO: You can use the umbrella.

NORI: It is one umbrella.

[BINA LOOKS AT HER LARGE HAT. RALO HANDS NORI THE UMBRELLA.]

[THE CLAIM WINDOW CLOSES. RALO TURNS THE SIGN AROUND. IT READS "CLOSED FOR INVENTORY."]

BINA: What if another owner arrives?

PEX: They can return tomorrow with a description.

RALO: We are here at nine.

[BINA CHECKS HER SCRIPT. THE NEXT LINE SAYS "A SURPRISE REVEAL." SHE CROSSES IT OUT AND WRITES "CHECK THE LABEL."]

### Act Three

[PEX CHECKS THE REST OF THE ITEMS. THE SCARF HAS A NAME TAG; THE WATER BOTTLE HAS A SCHOOL STICKER; THE GLOVE IS ALREADY CLAIMED.]

PEX: The shelf is almost clear.

BINA: No unresolved objects?

RALO: There is one lunch container without a label.

[BINA OPENS IT. A NOTE INSIDE SAYS "PLEASE THROW AWAY."]

BINA: A message from the owner.

RALO: A disposal instruction.

[PEX PUTS IT IN THE BIN. BINA READS THE LOST-AND-FOUND PROCESS TO CAMERA: BRING A DESCRIPTION, SIGN THE LOG, AND CHECK THE CLAIM WINDOW.]

BINA: Next time, we investigate a hat.

RALO: It is on your head.

[BINA REMOVES HER HAT AND CHECKS THE LABEL.]

[RALO CHECKS THE CABINET ONE LAST TIME. THE CLAIM WINDOW IS EMPTY.]

RALO: The shelves are clear.

PEX: The log is filed.

[BINA LOOKS AT THE EMPTY PEDESTAL.]

BINA: It is a satisfying empty space.

### Tag

[THE STATION DOOR OPENS. NORI RETURNS, DRY.]

BINA: Was the walk emotional?

NORI: It was rainy.

[PEX SETS THE CLAIM TICKET IN A FILE. RALO CLOSES THE CABINET.]

NORI: I forgot my claim ticket.

[PEX HANDS IT OVER. BINA WAITS FOR A REUNION MUSIC CUE. NONE PLAYS.]

BINA: It has found its owner again.

RALO: It was on the desk.
