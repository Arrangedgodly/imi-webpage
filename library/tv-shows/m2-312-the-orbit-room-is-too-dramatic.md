# The Orbit Room Is Too Dramatic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: space-station adventure television, blinking control panels, clipped countdowns, and crews who narrate routine maintenance as a cosmic emergency*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: The Night Orbit crew tries to replace a loose antenna cable before a scheduled broadcast, while the captain turns every beep into a threat from outer space.

Cast: Captain Nala, a capuchin; engineer Vex, a macaque; pilot Omi, an orangutan; science officer Rell, a gibbon; and station computer Palla, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "The Red Light"

### Cold Open

[ORBIT ROOM. A PANEL BEEPS.]

NALA: Red light! We have a crisis!

VEX: The light is red because the panel is open.

OMI: The panel is open because we are repairing it.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[CONTROL ROOM.]

The station's antenna cable has loosened during routine rotation. Captain Nala orders the crew to prepare for signal loss. Rell reads the manual, which recommends tightening the connector and checking the seal.

NALA: Is the manual certain?

RELL: It has a diagram.

PALLA: The broadcast is scheduled in six minutes.

Vex begins the repair. Nala watches the panel and narrates the voltage. Omi controls the station's rotation. The cabin turns slowly, but the camera cuts to close-ups of blinking lights, making the movement seem faster.

NALA: We are spinning out of control.

OMI: We are moving at the scheduled orbit speed.

### Act Two

[VEX INSIDE THE SERVICE PANEL.]

The cable connector is loose but intact. Vex tightens it. The red light turns amber. Nala calls this a partial recovery. Palla says the broadcast signal is stable. Nala orders a second scan for hidden threats.

Rell's sensor detects a small object outside the station. The crew goes silent. The camera zooms in on the object: a spoon that slipped from the galley tray.

NALA: An unidentified metallic object.

OMI: It is a spoon.

RELL: It is still unidentified until we bring it inside.

Vex uses the retrieval arm. The spoon rotates slowly. Nala instructs him to secure it with the outer claw. Vex catches it on the first attempt, but the claw's grip is loose. The spoon slips and floats toward the station.

PALLA: We have lost the object.

NALA: We have lost a spoon.

The crew recovers it. Omi returns to the scheduled orbit. Rell checks the airlock seal. Nala asks for a dramatic countdown before the station's antenna test.

The test requires the outer antenna to point toward a relay satellite. Omi rotates the station by a few degrees, while Vex watches the cable from the service hatch. Nala insists on using the backup panel, whose label has faded. Rell reads the manual and identifies the correct switch by its shape.

RELL: The round switch controls the relay.

NALA: Are we certain?

VEX: We tested it on the ground.

Palla counts down. At zero, the relay connects. The signal appears on the monitor. Nala prepares a victory announcement. Omi warns that the station is still rotating, so the antenna will need one final adjustment.

Nala holds her speech while Vex makes the adjustment. The signal remains stable. The crew checks the cable strain, secures the spoon and cup, and closes the service panel. Rell logs the successful test in the station's routine maintenance record.

The ground station asks for a final voice check. Nala reads her prepared line about a historic day. The signal carries only the first word, "Historic," before cutting out. Vex checks the panel: the antenna is fine, but the ground station's microphone is muted. Omi waits while the ground operator unmutes it. The crew repeats the test with a short message: "Signal received."

RELL: That is enough information.

NALA: It is not enough for a finale.

PALLA: The broadcast is not a finale.

The crew sends the technical report instead of the speech. Nala folds her paper and clips it beside the maintenance manual, where it will not float into the equipment bay.

### Act Three

[ANTENNA TEST.]

The antenna is stable. The signal reaches ground control. Palla announces that the broadcast is ready. Nala delivers a solemn speech about the crew's courage, but Vex asks to be credited for tightening the connector.

VEX: It was a quarter turn.

NALA: A quarter turn that saved the signal.

RELL: The signal would have paused briefly.

NALA: It would have paused in the vastness of space.

Omi puts the spoon in a drawer with a latch. The crew begins the scheduled broadcast. The camera pulls back, showing the space station moving calmly around the planet.

### Tag

[GALLEY.]

Nala reaches for a cup. The cup floats away. Vex catches it and clips it down.

NALA: A new emergency.

VEX: A new latch.

Palla's computer voice confirms that the cup is secured. The crew drinks in silence while the station keeps its orbit.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
