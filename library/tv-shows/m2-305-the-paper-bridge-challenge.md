# The Paper Bridge Challenge

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's science competition television, countdown builds, exaggerated demonstrations, and judges who mistake a noisy collapse for useful data*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: Two student teams build paper bridges over a model stream, but the host keeps changing the weight test to make the finale more dramatic.

Cast: Host Tavi, a gibbon; student Mira, a capuchin; student Odo, a macaque; judge Palla, an orangutan; teacher Neri, a ring-tailed lemur; and floor assistant Vex, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The Great Span"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO TABLE. TWO PAPER BRIDGES.]

TAVI: Which bridge can hold the most acorns?

PALLA: We are testing washers.

TAVI: The acorns are for the close-up.

MIRA: Do acorns count as washers?

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[BUILD TABLES. CLOCK STARTS.]

The teams have twenty minutes to build a bridge from paper, tape, and two blocks. Mira and Odo fold strips into triangles. The rival team rolls paper into a tube. Tavi narrates every fold as a breakthrough.

TAVI: A bold crease! A daring turn!

NERI: It is a triangle.

Vex places the models over the stream and tests them with washers. Mira's bridge holds six. The rolled bridge holds four, then collapses with a loud slap.

Tavi asks the teams to explain their construction choices. Mira says folding the paper distributes weight across several supports. The rival team says rolling the paper keeps the center stiff. Odo demonstrates the difference by pressing one finger on each bridge. One bends evenly; the other twists.

PALLA: We should record the shape as well as the maximum load.

TAVI: Can we call the twist a surprise?

NERI: We can call it a twist.

Odo asks whether the model stream has current. Vex turns on a small pump. Water moves beneath the bridges and shakes the table. The rolled bridge has not been anchored, so it drifts downstream. Tavi narrates the movement as a bridge disaster. Mira reaches to catch it, but Palla asks them to observe first. The bridge bumps against the bank, remains intact, and stops.

MIRA: The bridge floated.

ODO: The contest did not ask for a floating bridge.

PALLA: We have discovered another property to test, if we keep it separate from the load test.

TAVI: A dramatic failure.

PALLA: It held four washers.

Odo points out that the collapsed bridge was not taped at the ends. The team rebuilds it with folds and one strip of tape. Tavi announces a surprise challenge: the bridges must now hold a toy boat.

MIRA: The rules said washers.

TAVI: The boat is a thematic extension.

### Act Two

[THE BOAT TEST.]

Vex places a small wooden boat on Mira's bridge. It holds. He adds a second boat. The bridge bends but stays up. Tavi asks for a larger boat.

PALLA: The bridge is sized for the model stream.

TAVI: We need the ultimate test.

Neri says the ultimate test should be a repeatable weight. Palla agrees. She asks each team to add washers one at a time, recording when the bridge bends and when it breaks.

Odo's team measures the paper width. Mira notices that the blocks are not the same distance apart; one has shifted. She resets the span. The bridge now holds more weight. Tavi tries to call this a twist.

TAVI: The bridge has changed its fate.

MIRA: We changed the distance.

Palla asks the other team to repeat their trial. Their first bridge collapses at four washers; their reinforced bridge holds seven. The show has two useful results and no single champion.

### Act Three

[FINAL LOAD TEST.]

Tavi brings in a bag labeled THE BIG ONE. It contains mixed acorns, toy boats, and washers. Palla rejects it because the weights differ. Tavi opens the bag and discovers it also contains a rubber frog from another set.

VEX: That frog is not a weight.

TAVI: It can be a visual aid.

MIRA: It can sit in the boat.

The teams agree to test one bridge with exactly ten washers. It bends but holds. Odo wants to add another. Mira stops him and asks to record the result first. Palla awards both teams a ribbon: one for strength and one for careful testing.

TAVI: The finale has two winners.

PALLA: The contest has two results.

Neri asks the students to explain how triangles distribute the load. Odo shows the folded supports. The other team shows the rolled tube. Both designs work when the span is measured and the ends are taped.

### Tag

[AFTER THE SHOW.]

Tavi places the toy boat on the stronger bridge and films a slow-motion shot. The bridge holds. Vex removes the rubber frog from the stream.

VEX: The frog has no data.

TAVI: The frog has a close-up.

Palla labels the bridge model with its measurements. The students carry their designs back to class, leaving the stream table clear for cleanup.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
