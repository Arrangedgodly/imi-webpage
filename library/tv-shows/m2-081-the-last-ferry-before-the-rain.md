# The Last Ferry Before the Rain

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television disaster dramas, countdown rescues, and experts arguing over maps*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A storm approaches the river crossing, and the ferry crew must move a stranded troop to the high bank before the water rises, while the control room keeps reporting a ferry that is safely docked.

Cast: Captain Sula, a gorilla; navigator Reni, a macaque; deckhand Pavo, a capuchin; weather reporter Liri, a lemur; and dispatcher Kett, a baboon.

## Episode: "Waterline"

### Cold open

INT. FERRY CONTROL ROOM, EVENING

[A map shows the ferry at the dock. Rain begins tapping the window.]

KETT: The ferry is secured.

LIRI: It is in the middle of the river.

KETT: The map shows it at the dock.

RENI: The map is from yesterday.

SULA: How many are waiting on the far bank?

PAVO: Six adults and one very large basket.

KETT: The ferry is secured.

SULA: Prepare to launch.

### Act One

EXT. FAR BANK

[The ferry rocks beside a muddy landing. Pavo ties a rope to a post.]

SULA: We have one trip before the water rises.

RENI: The current is moving faster.

PAVO: The basket is stuck in the mud.

LIRI: The rain has reached the lower path.

SULA: Load the passengers first.

PAVO: They are carrying the basket.

SULA: The basket can stay.

PASSENGER, O.S.: It has the seedlings.

RENI: We need those seedlings on the high bank.

SULA: Then we load the basket.

PAVO: The ferry is tilting.

RENI: The basket is heavier than it looks.

SULA: It has soil and a clay pot inside.

LIRI: The weather is worsening.

KETT: The dock remains clear.

LIRI: We are not at the dock.

### Act Two

INT. FERRY CABIN

[The passengers board. The basket is secured. The current pushes the boat sideways.]

SULA: Pavo, release the rope.

PAVO: The knot is wet.

RENI: Use the safety knife.

PAVO: The safety knife is in the dry box.

SULA: Where is the dry box?

PAVO: Under the wet map.

RENI: The map is soaked.

KETT: The ferry remains at the dock.

LIRI: Dispatcher, update your map.

KETT: The map is the official record.

SULA: The river is not reading it.

[The ferry pulls free. The basket shifts. Pavo grabs the rope.]

PAVO: The seedlings are moving.

RENI: Keep the basket centered.

SULA: We have a wave coming.

LIRI: The weather report says light rain.

[The wave splashes into the boat.]

LIRI: The report is now updated.

KETT: The map still shows the dock.

SULA: Tell the map to hold on.

### Control-room update

INT. FERRY CONTROL ROOM

[Kett redraws the river. The map is pinned down with a mug.]

KETT: I have moved the ferry symbol.

LIRI: It is on the high bank.

KETT: The map is right-side up now.

RENI: It shows the current going upstream.

KETT: The arrows are decorative.

SULA: Make them point the way the water moves.

KETT: I can add another arrow.

LIRI: We need to report the stranded passengers are safe.

KETT: The report says they are still waiting.

RENI: The report is from before the crossing.

KETT: I will update it after the ferry returns.

SULA: The ferry is staying on the high bank.

KETT: Then I will send the report by runner.

LIRI: The runner is already here.

### Act Three

EXT. HIGH BANK, NIGHT

[The ferry reaches the high bank. The passengers step off. Pavo carries the basket to dry ground.]

RENI: All passengers are safe.

SULA: The seedlings are intact.

LIRI: The river has risen over the lower path.

KETT: The ferry is at the dock.

SULA: The ferry is at the high bank.

KETT: I will update the record.

RENI: The old map is stuck to the desk.

KETT: I can peel it off.

[The map tears. The ferry symbol remains at the dock.]

KETT: The ferry is still at the dock.

LIRI: The boat is beside me.

SULA: We need a new map.

KETT: I can draw one.

RENI: Include both banks.

KETT: I will start with the dock.

SULA: Start with the river.

[Pavo plants the seedlings in a raised box. The rain eases.]

PAVO: The roots will be above the waterline.

RENI: The crossing is clear.

SULA: The ferry will stay here tonight.

KETT: The map says it is at the dock.

SULA: Then the map can sleep there.

### Weather bulletin

INT. FERRY CABIN

[Liri records a final update into a handheld radio.]

LIRI: The far bank has been evacuated before the rain.

KETT: The ferry is at the dock.

SULA: The ferry is at the high bank.

RENI: The radio can report both.

KETT: It can report one location at a time.

LIRI: Then report the passengers first.

PAVO: The seedlings are planted above the waterline.

SULA: That is the important location.

### Tag

EXT. HIGH BANK, DAWN

[The river is calm. The ferry rests at the high bank, beside the new seedling box.]

LIRI: The storm has passed.

KETT: The map is dry.

[He unfolds it. It still shows the ferry at the dock.]
