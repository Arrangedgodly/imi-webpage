# The Village Picks a New Mascot

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: community branding shows that turn an animal mascot audition into a committee meeting*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Vex, a macaque; candidate Pip, a capuchin; candidate Nari, a lemur; designer Seli, a gibbon; council chair Mara, a baboon; child, a macaque, offstage voice; resident, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. TOWN HALL - DAY

[A costume rack holds a smiling acorn, a river otter, and an oversized bell. Vex faces the camera.] 

VEX: Today we choose the face of our village.

MARA: We need a mascot for the harvest fair.

[Pip climbs onto the costume rack to reach a high hat. The rack tips. Theme sting.]

ACT ONE

VEX: Welcome to Town Spirit, where the mascot is chosen by the people and designed by whoever has a sewing machine.

SELI: The acorn represents the orchard.

NARI: The river otter represents the river.

PIP: What does the bell represent?

MARA: The clock tower.

PIP: Does it ring?

VEX: Only during the finale.

[The candidates try on costume pieces. Pip's acorn head is too large to see through. Nari wears the otter tail; it drags on the floor. Vex places the bell over his own head and cannot hear questions.] 

MARA: We should test the costumes before the vote.

ACT TWO

[The candidates parade down the town hall aisle. Pip walks carefully, feeling for the wall with one hand. Nari turns and her tail sweeps a ballot box off the table.] 

NARI: I am sorry.

VEX: The otter has disrupted the vote.

SELI: The tail is too long.

[She pins it shorter. Pip's acorn head slides forward; he props it up with both hands. A child in the audience points to the gap where his face should be.] 

CHILD (OFF): I can see his nose.

PIP: I can see you too.

[The bell costume rings every time Vex nods. The council asks him a question; the bell rings before he answers.] 

MARA: Do you support the mascot?

[The bell rings.] 

VEX: I do not know what it answered.

[The contestants trade props. Pip wears the otter tail, Nari holds the acorn sign, Vex puts on the small bell as a hat. The combinations work better.]

SELI: The tail is balanced on Pip.

PIP: I have a strong grip.

NARI: The sign is readable now.

MARA: The bell is not covering Vex's face.

[Before the vote, the crew sends each candidate to greet a small group of residents. Pip wears the acorn head; Nari carries the sign; Vex rings the bell.]

VEX: We should make a strong first impression.

[The acorn head blocks Pip's view. He follows Nari's voice and stops just short of the first resident.]

PIP: I brought the harvest.

RESIDENT: I cannot see you.

[He lifts the head enough to reveal his face. The costume slips backward and rests around his neck.]

PIP: Now I can see you.

NARI: The sign is readable from here.

[She holds it too close to the camera. The lens focuses on the painted acorn and blurs the people.]

SELI: Take two steps back.

[The bell costume rings as Vex nods at the gardener. A flock of pigeons lifts from the roof and settles near the candidate table.]

MARA: The audience has grown.

VEX: Can they vote?

MARA: They can choose where to sit.

[The pigeons perch on the acorn head, which Pip has placed on the floor. The mascot has become a bird stand.]

PIP: The acorn has attracted a flock.

SELI: It is the only costume with a flat top.

[Vex tries to raise the bell to ring them away. Mara stops him.]

MARA: They are part of the village.

VEX: They are not registered.

NARI: Neither is the bell.

ACT THREE

[The vote begins. Each resident places a bean in one of three jars. A child drops a bean into the acorn jar. A gardener puts one in the otter jar. Mara counts them.] 

MARA: It is a tie.

VEX: We will need a runoff.

PIP: Can the mascot be a team?

SELI: We have a shared design.

[They assemble the costumes: Pip wears the shortened tail, Nari carries the acorn sign, and Vex wears the bell as a badge. The assembled look resembles a parade crew rather than one mascot.] 

MARA: Does it represent the village?

NARI: It represents the people carrying the props.

PIP: And the animal wearing the tail.

[Vex tries to ring the bell for the vote. It gives a tiny, muffled ding against his chest. The audience laughs.] 

VEX: The town has chosen a sound.

MARA: The town chose a combination.

[They take the mascot through the front door. The acorn sign catches on the doorway, and Nari turns it sideways to fit. The otter tail clears after Pip backs up.]

SELI: We have a route test.

PIP: The mascot can get outside.

VEX: That is the most important quality.

TAG

[The fair begins. A child waves to the mascot. Pip waves back; the giant acorn head tips forward again.] 

NARI: It is waving too hard.

MARA: The village has a face.
