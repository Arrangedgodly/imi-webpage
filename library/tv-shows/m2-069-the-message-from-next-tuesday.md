# The Message from Next Tuesday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: time-travel anthology series, mysterious broadcasts, and paradox-heavy explanations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A radio operator receives a message from next Tuesday warning the crew not to send another message, but every attempt to decode it creates more copies of the warning.

Cast: Operator Leni, a macaque; engineer Varo, a gibbon; archivist Tett, a capuchin; visitor Sula, a baboon; and narrator Om, an orangutan.

## Episode: "Don't Send This"

### Cold open

INT. SIGNAL ROOM, NIGHT

[A radio crackles. Leni writes down a message.]

LENI: "Do not send this message."

VARO: From whom?

LENI: It says Tuesday.

TETT: Today is Monday.

OM: The future speaks through static.

SULA: Tuesday is tomorrow.

LENI: It says next Tuesday.

VARO: That is a week away.

[The radio crackles again. A second message arrives: "Stop writing this down."]

TETT: I already wrote it down.

OM: The future has been recorded.

### Act One

INT. SIGNAL ROOM

[Varo opens the radio casing. Tett stacks copies of the message.]

VARO: The transmitter is receiving a signal from the future.

SULA: How do you know?

VARO: The message says Tuesday.

LENI: It also says not to send it.

TETT: We have not sent it.

VARO: We need to reply.

LENI: The message specifically says not to.

VARO: That is why we should ask why.

OM: The question itself may alter the timeline.

SULA: We can write the question and wait.

TETT: The archive has six copies now.

LENI: Why six?

TETT: Each time the radio repeats, I make a copy.

VARO: Stop making copies.

TETT: The archive policy requires duplicates.

LENI: This is a warning, not a seed catalog.

TETT: Warnings are important.

[The radio crackles. "Do not archive this message."]

TETT: Too late.

### Act Two

INT. ANTENNA PLATFORM

[Leni climbs a ladder to adjust the antenna. Varo stays below with the radio.]

LENI: I can rotate the antenna.

VARO: The signal is coming from the east.

LENI: The future is in the east?

VARO: The compass points east.

SULA: The compass needle is stuck.

VARO: The antenna is pointing at the weather vane.

LENI: The weather vane points north.

OM: Time has turned upon itself.

TETT: I have found a note inside the radio.

LENI: What does it say?

TETT: "Do not open the radio."

VARO: That is the note I wrote on Monday.

SULA: It is Monday.

VARO: Exactly.

[He examines the note. It is in his own handwriting.]

LENI: Did you write it today?

VARO: I will have to check.

TETT: The message says not to send it. We should preserve the original.

SULA: What if the original is the copy?

OM: A paradox takes root.

LENI: We can shut off the transmitter.

VARO: Then the receiver will still receive.

LENI: We can unplug the radio.

[She pulls the plug. The radio continues to crackle.]

SULA: The message is coming from the battery.

VARO: The radio has no battery.

TETT: It has a seed pod in the compartment.

### Act Three

INT. SIGNAL ROOM, LATER

[Varo removes the seed pod. The radio stops. Leni places the pod on the table.]

VARO: The pod was pressing the signal button.

TETT: It is round and fits the compartment.

SULA: There is no message from next Tuesday.

OM: There is still a warning.

LENI: The warning came from a radio that was being pressed by a seed pod.

VARO: We can return the pod to the archive.

TETT: The archive is closed.

SULA: Today is Monday.

TETT: It is always closed on Monday.

LENI: What about next Tuesday?

TETT: The archive will be open.

VARO: We should not send the message.

LENI: We will file it instead.

TETT: How many copies?

LENI: One.

TETT: The archive requires two.

SULA: Put one in the radio.

[They place the pod beside the note. The radio is silent.]

OM: The future has gone quiet.

LENI: The future is a week away.

VARO: We have time to label the pod.

TETT: "Do not press."

[The pod rolls toward the radio. Everyone catches it.]

### Continuity check

INT. SIGNAL ROOM

[Tett plays the recorded message backward. It sounds like a faint squeak.]

TETT: The archive has a reverse-listening procedure.

LENI: It sounds like a chair.

VARO: The radio has no chair.

SULA: It is the stool leg rubbing against the signal table.

[Varo moves the stool. The squeak stops.]

OM: The future has withdrawn its voice.

TETT: I need to note the stool in the file.

LENI: Note that it was moved.

TETT: To where?

SULA: The floor.

TETT: The archive needs a location.

VARO: "Beside the table."

TETT: That is clear.

[The radio gives a brief squeak.]

LENI: Is the message back?

VARO: It is the stool.

TETT: I will make a copy anyway.

### Tag

INT. SIGNAL ROOM, NEXT TUESDAY

[Tett opens the archive. A copy of the warning falls out.]

TETT: I did make two copies.

[The radio gives one small click.]
