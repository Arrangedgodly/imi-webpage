# The Cooking Contest Plates the Timer

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competitions that reward a ticking clock and dramatic plating over whether the food is ready*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Kima, a macaque; chef contestant Doro, an orangutan; chef contestant Vani, a lemur; judge Peli, a gibbon; producer Ranu, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING SET - DAY

A timer counts down. Doro checks a loaf in the oven. Ranu places a tiny plate on the judging table.

KIMA: Ten seconds to plate.

DORO: It needs twenty more minutes.

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Ranu says the segment must end before the next commercial. Vani has finished a salad, but the dressing is still in a bottle. Peli asks the contestants to serve what is ready. Kima announces a plating sprint. Doro leaves the loaf in the oven and sets out the ingredients for a later slice. Ranu says the camera needs a finished dish.

Vani pours dressing over the salad. The bottle cap falls into the bowl. She fishes it out with a fork. Kima calls it a surprise garnish. Peli declines to taste until Vani replaces the salad. Ranu waves the timer card at the camera.

ACT TWO

INT. KITCHEN SET - LATER

Doro asks for more oven time. The timer operator resets the countdown to thirty seconds. Ranu tells Kima to call it a final round. Peli checks the loaf through the oven window; its center is still raw. Vani starts a new salad in a clean bowl, measuring dressing into a spoon instead of pouring from the bottle.

KIMA: The clock says we have eight seconds.

PELI: The loaf says we have a thermometer.

Ranu sends in a runner with a tray of decorative parsley. The runner places it on the raw loaf, which sags under the weight. Doro removes the parsley and puts the loaf back in the oven. Kima asks whether the parsley can be scored for presentation. Peli says it has not been served.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Ranu asks Peli to judge the contestants on camera while the food cooks. Peli refuses. Kima records a teaser: "Who will win the plate?" She holds up Vani's empty salad bowl. Vani brings a fresh bowl to the table. The camera zooms so tight it shows only the dressing spoon. Ranu asks for a dramatic sizzle. Doro opens the oven; there is no sizzle, only a timer beep. Peli asks the sound operator not to add one.

ACT THREE

INT. COOKING SET - AFTERNOON

The loaf finishes baking. Doro checks its center with a thermometer, lets it rest, and slices it. Vani's salad is dressed evenly. Peli tastes both dishes. Ranu asks Kima to hold the trophy beside the timer for a suspense shot. Kima sets the timer down; it keeps beeping against the trophy base.

Ranu points to the seconds on the clock and asks if the shorter cook can earn an efficiency bonus. Peli checks the oven log; Doro waited for the loaf to bake through, while Vani used the countdown only to mark her prep. The timer cannot decide the score. Kima moves it off the judging table. Peli awards points for the food: Doro's loaf is cooked through, and Vani's salad is balanced. Vani wins by one point. Ranu asks the judge to make the winner's plate look taller. Vani stacks the salad bowl on the trophy pedestal by mistake. The camera cuts to her hand as she lifts it back off.

Kima announces the result while Peli holds the actual trophy. Ranu reaches for the timer to trigger another dramatic beep. Peli turns it off. Vani accepts the trophy, then gives Doro the last warm slice. Doro asks whether that counts as a tie. Peli says it counts as lunch. The contestants sit at the set table and eat. Ranu moves the next episode's countdown clock out of the food frame.


Peli asks both contestants to identify their dishes before tasting. Vani's bowl is labeled SALAD, and Doro's plate says BREAD, READY AT 2:10. Ranu asks for a blind judging reveal. Kima covers the labels with two napkins, then mixes up which napkin belongs to which plate. Peli lifts each one and reads the actual dish. Ranu wants the contestants to pretend surprise. Vani says she has been standing beside her salad the whole time. The camera operator catches Ranu silently moving the timer away from the plates. Peli tastes the food only after it is served at a safe temperature. Doro offers Vani the knife used to slice the loaf; she sets it beside the plate rather than handing it across the hot oven door.

TAG

INT. COOKING SET - AFTER THE SHOW

Kima puts the timer in a drawer. Peli labels the leftover loaf with its baking time. Ranu takes the decorative parsley back to props.

FINAL IMAGE: The trophy stands beside two empty plates while the timer, silent at last, displays the actual oven-rest time.
