# The Perfect View Is Behind the House

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: house-hunting reality television, where buyers reject practical homes for a perfect view that is blocked by a tree*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Agent Pava tours two prospective buyers through three homes, but they judge every room by what they can see through the kitchen window.

Cast: Agent Pava, gibbon; buyer Nima, macaque; buyer Odo, baboon; camera host Seli, capuchin; current resident Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Window Seat"

### Cold Open

[SMALL HOUSE. NIMA LOOKS THROUGH THE KITCHEN WINDOW.]

NIMA: The view is a tree.

ODO: The tree is blocking the view.

TAVI: The tree is in the yard.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PAVA STANDS OUTSIDE THE FIRST HOUSE.]

Pava introduces a cottage with a large kitchen and a small yard. Nima checks the window. Odo opens a cupboard. Tavi demonstrates how the door sticks unless lifted slightly.

PAVA: It has character.

NIMA: It has a sticky door.

ODO: The window faces a tree.

TAVI: The tree gives shade.

Seli asks the buyers what they need. Nima says a quiet place to read. Odo wants room to store tools. Pava points out a low shelf beside the window.

ODO: The tools do not fit.

NIMA: The reading chair fits.

ODO: The chair blocks the door.

Tavi lifts the door as he opens it. The door clears the floor.

PAVA: A simple adjustment.

NIMA: We want a door that opens without instructions.

### Act Two

[SECOND HOUSE. THE WINDOW FACES A CLEAR HILL.]

Nima likes the view. Odo checks the shed; it is large enough for tools. The kitchen, however, is narrow.

ODO: We can store everything in the shed.

NIMA: The shed is outside.

PAVA: It is a short walk.

NIMA: The flour is not a tool.

Seli asks them to imagine breakfast. They try to fit two stools in the kitchen. One blocks the cupboard.

ODO: The stools are too wide.

NIMA: The view is beautiful.

ODO: We are not eating the view.

A bird lands on the window ledge and drops a seed. The seed rolls into the narrow gap between the sill and the cabinet.

NIMA: We have a cleaning problem.

PAVA: We have a bird visitor.

NIMA: We have a gap.

[PAVA SHOWS THE BUYERS A FLOOR PLAN.]

Nima marks where she would place a reading chair. Odo marks where he would store tools. Their two marks overlap near the kitchen doorway.

ODO: The chair blocks the tool cupboard.

NIMA: The cupboard is not in the first house.

PAVA: It is in this one.

Tavi suggests moving the chair to the wide sill. Nima tries it. The chair fits, but the book blocks the window latch.

NIMA: I cannot open the window.

ODO: I can move the chair six inches.

NIMA: Then it blocks the cupboard again.

Seli asks if the sill can hold the book instead. Nima tests it; the book lies flat, and the chair moves to the side. Odo opens the cupboard fully.

ODO: We have found a layout.

PAVA: The house is working.

TAVI: We did not change the house.

NIMA: We changed where things go.

Seli films the room from the doorway. The view through the window looks clear, and the walking path stays open.

### Act Three

[THIRD HOUSE. THE KITCHEN WINDOW FACES A SMALL GARDEN.]

The house is smaller, but its kitchen has a wide sill. Odo finds a built-in tool cupboard near the back door. Nima checks the view; a climbing vine covers part of the window.

TAVI: The vine can be trimmed.

NIMA: I like the shade.

ODO: We can move the chair to the side.

PAVA: This house offers a compromise.

Seli asks them to test the kitchen. Nima sets down a book on the sill. Odo stores a wrench in the cupboard. The door opens without sticking.

NIMA: The tree does not block the whole view.

ODO: I can reach the tools.

PAVA: Is this the one?

Nima and Odo look at each other. Odo says yes, but only if the vine stays. Tavi says the vine is on a trellis and can be trained around the window.

### Tag

[HOST SITS ON THE KITCHEN STOOL.]

SELI: What sold you on this house?

NIMA: The chair fits.

ODO: The tools fit.

TAVI: The vine fits.

PAVA: And the view?

Nima looks through the window. The branch sways aside, revealing a patch of the hill.

NIMA: It moves.

ODO: The view has opened up.

TAVI: The tree was never blocking it.

PAVA: The buyers have chosen the third house.

ODO: The view is an extra.

NIMA: The chair is not.

PAVA: The kitchen is ready.

ODO: The tools can stay in the cupboard.

TAVI: The vine stays.

[SFX: WINDOW OPENS, BIRDS.]
