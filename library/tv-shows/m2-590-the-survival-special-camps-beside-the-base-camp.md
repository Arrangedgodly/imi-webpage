# The Survival Special Camps Beside the Base Camp

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival television, manufactured scarcity, dramatic supply reveals, and a resourceful primate who notices the production camp*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Host Sela, a gibbon; naturalist Bram, a mandrill; field producer Nari, a macaque; camera operator Odo, an orangutan; ranger Kiri, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. WOODED TRAINING CLEARING - DAY

Sela stands beside a tent and whispers to camera.

SELA: We are alone in the wilderness.

Kiri rolls a food cart behind her.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The survival special is filming in a managed woodland training area. Host Sela is supposed to demonstrate how to select a safe campsite. Field producer Nari wants a dramatic shot of the cast discovering their supplies are missing. Naturalist Bram points out that the supplies are on the marked cart behind the tent.

NARI: The cart is outside the frame.

BRAM: It is still part of the site.

SELA: We can pretend the forest ate it.

KIRI: The forest did not take your lunch. I moved the cart to keep the path clear.

Odo pans the camera toward a stand of trees. In the far background, a portable restroom and a ranger station are visible. Nari asks him to tilt up. Odo says he can, but then the audience will see the low branches that mark the trail boundary.

ODO: The boundary is here. We stay inside it.

NARI: The shot needs isolation.

BRAM: The area is managed. We should explain that.

Sela opens the map and identifies the base camp, the safe path, and the emergency radio point. Nari wants the map kept secret until the final act. Kiri says the crew must know the route before they film.

ACT TWO

The producers stage a "lost supplies" scene. Sela opens an empty basket and gasps. Bram looks behind the tent and finds the food cart. The camera captures him discovering a labeled box of snacks and bottled water.

SELA: This is not what I expected to find.

BRAM: You knew where the cart was.

SELA: I knew the cart existed. I did not know it had apples.

NARI: Can we use that as a reveal?

KIRI: You can show the supplies and explain the plan.

Bram shows how to check a marked trail, keep food sealed, and use a radio for help. Sela opens a flint kit and tries a demonstration, but the practice area is damp. Kiri reminds her this segment is a demonstration, not a reason to build a fire in the woodland. The show has a designated fire tray at base camp.

NARI: A flame would make the scene feel rugged.

BRAM: A wet patch of dirt would make it feel wet.

Sela closes the flint kit and carries it back to the fire tray at base camp, beside the ranger station. Kiri checks that the tray is clear and that the nearby water bucket is full. Odo films from the marked camera point. A breeze pushes the tent flap over the emergency radio. Bram moves the flap and checks that the radio still works.

BRAM: We have contact with the ranger station.

SELA: Does that ruin the feeling of being alone?

KIRI: It means the safety plan works.

Nari asks Sela to walk farther from camp for a "discovery" shot. Kiri points to the path boundary. Sela instead follows the marked trail to a sign explaining the woodland's managed use. Bram reads it on camera.

ACT THREE

The segment shifts from pretend survival to a real demonstration of the site plan. Sela secures the tent flap, Bram identifies the radio point, and Odo records the visible trail markers. Nari says the scene has lost its sense of peril. Sela asks what the audience should learn from a fake emergency.

SELA: We can be prepared without pretending we are stranded.

BRAM: The route, radio, water, and ranger station are all part of the story.

NARI: Can we at least make the supply cart surprising?

KIRI: Put it where people can find it. That is its job.

Odo frames the food cart, then the trail map. Sela explains that the crew is using a designated training area and that visitors should stay on its marked paths. The fire kit stays resealed because the weather is damp. Nari looks at the monitor and sees the base camp clearly, with the woodland behind it.

NARI: It looks like a real place, not a crisis.

SELA: It is a real place. We can show how to use it.

Bram packs the demonstration items back into their labeled cases. Kiri confirms the radio is returned to the station point. The crew walks together along the marked path to base camp. Sela carries the empty basket; Nari keeps glancing inside, hoping an unexpected apple might improve the shot.

FINAL IMAGE: The supply cart sits in full view beside the map, while the cast follows the marked trail back to the ranger station.
