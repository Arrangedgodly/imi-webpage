# The Campground Has a Quiet Hour

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality competitions that score campers for silence in a noisy natural setting*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Sela, a macaque; camper Nari, a lemur; camper Pip, a capuchin; ranger Vex, a gibbon; sound judge Odo, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. CAMPGROUND - DAWN

[A sign reads QUIET HOUR: 6 TO 7 A.M. Pip tiptoes past a tent. A bird calls from above.] 

ODO: The bird has broken the silence.

VEX: The bird is not camping.

[The campers look up. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

SELA: Welcome to Silent Site, where campers earn points for making no noise.

NARI: We are in a forest.

PIP: It is going to make sounds.

[Odo holds a sound meter. Its needle moves when a leaf falls on the table.] 

ODO: The forest is already competing.

VEX: We can only score campers.

[The rules give a point for every quiet minute. Sela explains that contestants may not speak, rustle wrappers, move chairs, or wake the sleeping host.] 

SELA: Our first challenge is to open breakfast without a sound.

[Nari opens a paper bag. It crackles loudly. Pip catches the bag, but the crackers inside crumble.] 

PIP: We have a quiet snack.

NARI: We have crumbs.

ACT TWO

[The campers compete in a silent tent setup. Pip holds a tent pole while Nari threads a sleeve over it. The pole slips and springs upright. The tent fabric catches the wind and flaps loudly.] 

ODO: Minus one point.

PIP: The tent made the noise.

SELA: The tent is part of your campsite.

[Vex moves the tent behind a stand of trees. The sound meter drops. A group of macaques appears at the edge of the clearing and begins grooming.] 

NARI: They are quiet.

[One macaque finds a tick and chirps. The group shifts. Odo's meter rises.] 

ODO: The wild card has entered.

VEX: They are not contestants.

ODO: The rules do not say that.

[Nari lays out a blanket. A seed drops from Pip's pocket and rolls downhill. They chase it without speaking. It stops beside Odo's shoe.] 

PIP: I have retrieved the seed.

[He whispers. The meter detects his breath.] 

ODO: Whispering is sound.

PIP: I was not speaking loudly.

SELA: The rules are too broad.

[The host wakes in the tent and steps on a dry leaf. It snaps. Odo subtracts a point from Sela.] 

SELA: I did not move during the round.

VEX: The host has lost a point while asleep.

[Sela divides the quiet circle into three zones: sleeping, eating, and listening. Odo places the sound meter in the middle.]

SELA: The campers may choose a zone.

PIP: What if I need to move?

VEX: Movement has a sound score.

[Pip tries to stand without lifting the chair. The chair stays still, but his knees crack. Odo points at the meter.]

ODO: The contestant has made a sound.

PIP: That was my knees.

NARI: The forest has knees too.

[The nearby macaques shift closer to the food table. One pulls a leaf from another's shoulder. The grooming call is tiny, but Odo's meter picks it up.]

ODO: The troop has lost a point.

VEX: They never signed up.

SELA: They did not agree to be scored.

[Odo turns the meter away from the troop. It now points toward the stream, where water bubbles over stones. The number rises.]

ODO: The stream is winning.

VEX: We cannot award a ribbon to water.

NARI: You can award it the listening zone.

ACT THREE

[The finalists enter the quiet circle. Nari and Pip sit still. The forest grows louder as morning begins: birds call, leaves move, and the nearby macaques groom.] 

ODO: We are measuring silence.

NARI: There is no silence here.

PIP: There is space between sounds.

[Vex checks the meter. It is recording his own ticking watch. He removes it. The needle drops, then rises with the birds.] 

VEX: The quiet hour is over.

SELA: Who won?

ODO: No one. The forest scored highest.

[The campers smile. The sound meter registers their breathing.] 

ODO: Minus half a point each.

NARI: We accept a tie.

[They pack the crinkly breakfast bag into the recycling box. Pip shakes out crumbs for the birds. The macaque troop leaves the clearing, still grooming as it goes.] 

VEX: They have completed the course.

SELA: They were never in the course.

ODO: They have the high score anyway.

[Vex calls for the final count. A bird lands on the tent peg and sings twice.]

ODO: Another sound.

SELA: The contest is over.

[Pip lifts the peg slowly and the bird leaves without disturbing the tent.]

PIP: It got quiet again.

TAG

[The host announces the next challenge: "Silent Campfire." A log cracks in the fire ring.] 

VEX: The log has been disqualified.
