# The Night the Acorn Moved

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation reality shows that treat normal animal behavior as supernatural evidence*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A camera crew investigates a seed that appears to move through an empty clearing overnight.

Cast: Host Odo, a baboon; investigator Pava, a gibbon; skeptic Nara, a macaque; night watcher Kito, a capuchin.

Episode: "The Restless Acorn"

COLD OPEN

EXT. CLEARING - NIGHT

A single acorn sits on a flat stone. The camera zooms in. A breeze rolls it a few inches.

ODO: Something has disturbed the acorn.

NARA: The wind.

PAVA: We have not ruled out a ghost.

Kito turns on the lantern. The acorn is no longer on the stone.

ACT ONE

EXT. CLEARING - MORNING

Odo addresses the camera. The crew found the acorn on the ground. It appeared on the stone after the night watch, then was gone by morning. Pava calls this a pattern.

Nara checks the ground around the stone. A squirrel trail runs past it. Kito says he saw a squirrel at dawn, carrying something in its mouth.

ODO: Was it the acorn?

KITO: It could have been.

Odo asks whether the crew should set a trap. Nara says no. They can observe from a distance. Pava sets a motion camera facing the stone. Odo adds a thermal camera and a recorder for unexplained sounds.

NARA: It is a seed on a rock.

ODO: It has a history now.

ACT TWO

EXT. CLEARING - NIGHT

The crew watches a monitor. At midnight, the acorn sits still. At 1:00, the camera image flickers. Odo claims the acorn has moved. Nara points out the angle changed because Kito adjusted the tripod.

The crew hears scratching. Pava whispers that it may be a presence. Kito says the sound is from the camera strap tapping the stand. He ties the strap down.

At 2:15, a squirrel approaches the stone. It takes the acorn and carries it out of frame. Odo raises a hand to stop the playback.

ODO: We need to identify the entity.

NARA: It is a squirrel.

PAVA: Could it be a spirit using a squirrel?

Nara asks the crew to keep the animal in view. The squirrel returns with a second acorn and places it on the stone. It sniffs, takes that one too, then leaves.

Kito checks the camera. The first acorn was not on the stone at the start of the night. It had been placed there by Odo, who set up the demonstration and forgot to mark it.

ODO: I was establishing the scene.

NARA: You planted the evidence.

Odo says he was testing whether the camera could see an acorn in the dark. The squirrel found the test object.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CLEARING - BEFORE DAWN

Pava says the crew can still investigate why the squirrel uses the stone. Nara suggests it may be a convenient place to pause. Kito notices a small crack in the stone. The squirrel puts an acorn in the crack and taps it until the shell opens.

The crew watches from behind a tree. The squirrel eats the kernel and leaves the shell. Odo narrates in a solemn voice. Nara tells him to describe what they can see.

ODO: A squirrel opens an acorn on a rock.

PAVA: It is less haunted than our teaser.

Kito shows them the motion footage. The acorn only moves when the squirrel touches it or when the wind crosses the clearing.

Odo sets a second acorn on the stone to test the motion camera. Nara tells him not to bait the squirrel. He removes it. The squirrel arrives anyway, sniffs the stone, and looks toward the crew's hiding place. Pava holds still while Odo whispers that the animal may sense their presence. Nara points out that Odo is breathing loudly into the microphone. He lowers it. The squirrel takes a kernel from the crack and leaves the shell. Kito records the actual sound of the shell tapping stone. Pava asks if they can use that as the episode's suspense sting. Nara says yes, if they identify it as an acorn opening. Odo changes the title card a second time, from "SQUIRREL BREAKFAST" to "HOW AN ACORN OPENS."

ACT THREE

EXT. CLEARING - MORNING

The crew removes the extra equipment and returns the rock to its place. Odo tells the audience the mystery has a natural explanation. Pava says the squirrel's behavior is more interesting than the ghost theory. Nara asks Odo to correct the episode title card.

He changes "RESTLESS ACORN" to "SQUIRREL BREAKFAST."

TAG

A small leaf rolls across the stone.

Odo turns on the camera.

NARA: That is the wind.

Odo switches the camera off and follows the crew.
