# The Awards Show Opens the Wrong Envelope

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: awards broadcasts that stretch a card reveal into suspense even when the presenter has the wrong envelope*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: presenter Rema, a macaque; stage manager Oru, a gibbon; nominee Peli, an orangutan; envelope keeper Seli, a lemur; announcer Kavi, a capuchin; bandleader Deni, a squirrel monkey.

COLD OPEN

INT. AWARDS STAGE - NIGHT

Rema holds an envelope marked BEST SET DESIGN. Behind her, the screen reads BEST COSTUME. The band sustains one suspense chord. Oru steps into her eyeline and whispers that the envelope is wrong. Rema keeps smiling at the camera.

REMA: The winner is...

ORU: Not this one.

[The chord continues. The camera pushes close on the envelope until the category label fills the screen.]

ACT ONE

INT. STAGE WINGS - CONTINUOUS

Seli checks the rack. The costume and set-design envelopes have traded places. The opened card in Rema's hand names the set-design winner, but she has not read it aloud. Peli is nominated in both categories and sits in the front row, watching the screen alternate between the two titles.

KAVI: While the academy confirms the envelope, here is a word from our sponsor.

ORU: We are live. Do not invent a commercial break.

Kavi begins the sponsor copy anyway. Deni's band holds the chord beneath every sentence. The sponsor's name is swallowed by the brass. Rema asks Deni to stop. The chord stops for half a beat, then resumes when he mistakes Oru's clipboard tap for a cue.

REMA: Can we have one quiet second to sort paper?

Deni drops his baton. The band cuts off together. In the sudden silence, Seli reads the label aloud and hands Oru the correct costume envelope.

ACT TWO

INT. WINGS - LATER

Oru places a rack beside the podium and labels its slots with large category cards. Seli matches each sealed envelope to the rundown while Kavi reads the labels. A camera operator tries to get a suspense close-up of each one. The shot makes BEST SUPPORTING DESIGN look like BEST SET. Oru moves the rack out of the lens; the camera follows and frames the bandleader's cuff, where FOUR BARS is written in marker.

KAVI: We have four bars of suspense available.

DENI: I can spend them slowly.

Oru sets a four-bar limit for the final reveal. Kavi says the audience-choice vote is still tied. The control screen changes from a number to a spinning wheel. It spins so long that the crew begins clearing the stage behind it. Rema walks into the wings, then returns when the wheel stops on a tie again.

REMA: Is there a rule for this?

SELI: There is a second ballot in the envelope marked TIE.

Oru finds the tie-break envelope in the correct slot. He checks its seal and places it on a separate stand. The camera zooms toward it. Seli covers it with her hand until the audience-choice segment is called. Deni raises his baton. Oru gives him four fingers, then folds one down. Deni plays three bars and stops. The camera catches him staring at his own unfinished chord.

INT. STAGE WINGS - MOMENTS LATER

The screen shows the live audience vote. Kavi reads the finalists in order. Rema points to the right envelope before it reaches her. Seli checks its category in front of the camera, then passes it to Oru. He confirms the label and gives it to Rema. The audience choice winner is announced once. The tie-break envelope stays sealed.

ACT THREE

INT. AWARDS STAGE - LATER

Seli hands Rema the correct costume envelope. Rema opens it and reads Peli's name. Peli stands, but Oru sees the camera dolly blocking the aisle. He signals the camera operator to lock the shot and clear the path. Peli crosses to the podium. Deni begins the suspense chord, remembers the four-bar limit, and cuts it off after four measures. The audience applauds.

REMA: Congratulations. You may thank the people who made the costumes.

PELI: They are sitting behind the camera.

[The camera turns too late. The costume team waves from the back row. Peli points toward them; the director holds the wide shot instead of cutting away.]

Oru brings the set-design envelope to Rema. Seli verifies the category one last time. The winner is a small theater collective seated near the back. They take the side stairs and reach the podium without crossing the camera track. Rema opens the card and reads their name. Deni starts a chord, then stops himself. The collective thanks its workshop crew and leaves the microphone in place for the next presenter.

TAG

INT. STAGE - AFTER THE SHOW

Kavi turns the tie-break envelope over. A sticky note on the back says KEEP SEALED UNLESS TIED. Oru removes it and sticks it to the rack. Seli locks the categories in order. Deni practices a one-second chord and ends it cleanly.

FINAL IMAGE: Rema holds up the now-empty rack while the screen displays ENVELOPES: ALL ACCOUNTED FOR.
