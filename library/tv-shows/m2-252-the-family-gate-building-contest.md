# The Family Gate-Building Contest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: family competition reality television, where relatives race to build a gate while the host keeps changing the scoring*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Two families compete to build a gate across a garden path, but the best design is the one the judges can actually open.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; team leader Piri, capuchin; builder Nara, macaque; builder Tavi, gibbon; judge Seli, lemur; assistant Luma, macaque.

## Episode: "Open Sesame"

### Cold Open

[BACKYARD COURSE. TWO PILES OF WOOD.]

ODO: You have one hour to build a gate.

PIRI: Does it need to open?

ODO: We will reveal that after the break.

NARA: Then what are we building?

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE FAMILIES PICK THEIR MATERIALS.]

Odo explains the scoring: sturdiness, creativity, and speed. Seli adds that the gate must open and close.

ODO: That was not on the card.

SELI: It is a gate.

Team Piri builds a wide swinging gate. Team Tavi builds a narrow sliding panel. Nara measures the path and places the hinges. Tavi asks whether they can make the gate higher.

TAVI: A tall gate looks impressive.

NARA: It has to clear the ground.

PIRI: Our gate can do both.

Odo starts a countdown. The families race to cut boards. Luma brings the wrong saw, one with a dull blade.

LUMA: This is the spare.

PIRI: It does not cut.

ODO: It adds a challenge.

Seli takes the saw and sharpens it. The countdown continues.

### Act Two

[THE GATES TAKE SHAPE.]

Team Tavi's panel slides but catches on the path. Nara suggests raising the bottom rail. Tavi says that will leave a gap.

TAVI: A goat could get through.

SELI: We have no goat in the rules.

ODO: We do now. The gate must keep out a goat.

Piri adds a lower board. The gate becomes heavier. It swings shut too fast and knocks over a bucket.

[SFX: GATE SLAMS, BUCKET FALLS.]

PIRI: Our gate is aggressive.

TAVI: It is enthusiastic.

Odo says speed earns a bonus. Seli says safety is worth equal points. Odo adds a bonus for design. Seli adds a deduction if the gate blocks the path.

NARA: The scoring is changing faster than the gate.

Luma hands both teams a level. The level shows Tavi's posts lean slightly. Tavi tries to straighten them while Piri holds the crossbar.

[THE TEAMS COMPARE THEIR GATES.]

Odo asks the contestants to rate each other's designs. Piri says Tavi's latch is neat but the panel is narrow. Tavi says Piri's gate is wide but needs a second hinge.

ODO: Peer scores count double.

SELI: That rule was not on the card.

ODO: We have a new round.

Nara removes one hinge and shows Tavi how the gate swings. Tavi fits a wider latch from the scrap pile. The combined gate now opens easily and closes securely.

LUMA: You have built one gate from both plans.

PIRI: Does that make us teammates?

TAVI: We were on opposite sides of the same path.

Seli tests the hinge with the broom. It opens, swings back, and catches the latch every time. Odo tries to award points for the broom's performance.

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK SHOWS TEN MINUTES.]

Both teams test their gates. Team Piri's gate opens inward but hits the water barrel. Team Tavi's gate clears the barrel but will not latch.

SELI: A gate needs to open, close, and stay closed.

ODO: The challenge is nearly over.

PIRI: We can move the barrel.

TAVI: The barrel belongs there.

Nara shifts the hinge position. The gate now swings clear of the barrel. Tavi trims one corner of the latch so it catches.

[SFX: GATE OPENS, THEN CLICKS SHUT.]

Luma tests it with the broom handle. The latch holds. Odo gives the teams a final minute to decorate. Piri paints a bright stripe; Tavi writes "PUSH" on the gate.

NARA: It opens both ways.

TAVI: Then the sign should say "PUSH EITHER WAY."

Seli tests the gate. It opens smoothly and closes behind her.

### Tag

[THE JUDGES STAND BEFORE THE GATE.]

ODO: The winner is Team Piri for creativity.

SELI: Team Tavi for function.

LUMA: They built one gate together.

ODO: Then we have a tie.

PIRI: What is the prize?

Odo hands them a ribbon. It gets caught in the latch.

TAVI: The gate has eaten the prize.

NARA: The gate is working.

[SFX: LATCH CLICKS. RIBBON FREES.]

SELI: We award it to the gate.

PIRI: Can the gate keep the ribbon?

TAVI: Only if it does not block the latch.

Odo ties the ribbon to the post instead.

ODO: The prize is now on the winning structure.

LUMA: And out of the way.
