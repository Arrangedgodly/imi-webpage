# The Kids' Quiz Show Has a Question from the Audience

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's television quiz shows, prewritten questions, and hosts who cannot control a curious studio audience*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Dori, a baboon; contestants Leni, a macaque, and Pavo, a gibbon; question keeper Sumi, an orangutan; audience helper Reka, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. CHILDREN'S STUDIO - DAY

Two contestants sit at a low table with answer paddles. The host holds a question card. A small audience watches from behind a painted garden backdrop.

DORI: Today's quiz is about things that grow.

LENI: Plants?

PAVO: Also hair.

[The audience laughs. The question keeper flips the card. The question reads: WHAT GROWS ON A TREE?] 

DORI: Our first question is very specific.

[A child in the audience raises a hand.] 

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Reka signals that the audience is not part of the quiz. The host continues with the printed questions. Leni answers apple; Pavo answers nest. The question keeper says both can be right depending on what the question asks. Dori awards one point to each contestant.

DORI: That is a tie.

SUMI: It is the first question.

[The audience helper asks everyone to keep questions until the end. The child keeps a hand raised. Dori moves to the next card: WHICH PART OF A PLANT TAKES IN WATER?]

PAVO: Roots.

LENI: Roots.

SUMI: Correct, one point each.

[The scores are written on a board: Leni 2, Pavo 2. The audience child lowers their hand to wave at the camera. The host waves back and loses the next card behind the lectern.] 

DORI: The question has disappeared.

SUMI: It is under the lectern.

[The host retrieves it; the child raises a hand again.] 

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO - LATER

The contestants answer two more questions. Leni identifies a seed; Pavo identifies a leaf. Each receives one point. The board now reads Leni 3, Pavo 3. Dori announces a tiebreaker from the final card. The audience child is still waiting to ask a question.

DORI: This tiebreaker is worth three points.

SUMI: The card says one point.

DORI: The graphic says three.

[The question keeper checks the show rules. One point is correct. The stage manager changes the graphic before the host reads the question.] 

SUMI: What do roots do?

LENI: They hold the plant and take in water.

PAVO: They can also store food.

SUMI: Both are correct. No tiebreaker.

[The audience applauds. The host looks at the raised hand, then asks Reka if the child may ask a question after the score is settled. Reka checks the schedule and nods.] 

DORI: First, we confirm the score. Three points each.

[Reka checks with the floor manager and returns the microphone to the audience row. The child asks whether a seed can grow without soil. Sumi says the question is outside the scored quiz, then asks if the contestants want to answer as part of the show.] 

LENI: Some seeds can sprout on a damp paper towel.

PAVO: They still need water and light after that.

DORI: That is a good audience answer, but the score stays three each.

[The question keeper writes AUDIENCE QUESTION on a separate card, not on the score board. Reka takes the microphone back and thanks the child. The host does not add surprise points.] 

SUMI: The quiz is closed. The question was still welcome.

[The contestants carry their pots to the edge of the set and avoid the microphone cable.] 

ACT THREE

INT. STUDIO - AFTERNOON

The host declares a tie and gives each contestant a small plant pot. The question keeper confirms the score card matches the board. Only then does Dori invite one audience question. Reka brings the microphone to the child and reminds the crew to keep the cable behind the audience row.

AUDIENCE CHILD: If both players answer correctly, why do you have a winner?

DORI: We do not. They tied.

PAVO: We can grow our plants together.

LENI: Mine can sit by the window.

[The host asks the contestants to show their pots. The child asks whether plants need music. Sumi says it depends on the plant and the light, but the show has no test for music. The host turns the question into a bonus only if the contestants want to answer.] 

DORI: Do you want a bonus question?

LENI: Not today.

PAVO: We have our plants.

[The host accepts the answer. The camera shows the tied score and two pots. Reka returns the microphone to its stand, with the cable coiled safely.] 

TAG

The audience child raises a hand again. Dori looks at the cue cards, then the clock, and waves goodbye instead of starting another round.

FINAL IMAGE: Two tied contestants carry their plant pots off set while the score board remains at three each.
