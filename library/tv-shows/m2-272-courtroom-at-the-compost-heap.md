# Courtroom at the Compost Heap

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised small-claims court, where a dispute over whose leaves belong in the compost becomes an adversarial hearing*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Judge Pava hears two gardeners argue over a wheelbarrow of leaves that both claim to have gathered.

Cast: Judge Pava, gibbon; claimant Odo, baboon; respondent Nara, macaque; bailiff Piri, capuchin; clerk Seli, lemur; garden-club treasurer Fenn, squirrel monkey.

## Episode: "Leaves of Evidence"

### Cold Open

[COURTROOM. A WHEELBARROW OF LEAVES SITS BETWEEN TWO PODIUMS.]

PAVA: Who owns these leaves?

ODO: I raked them.

NARA: I moved them.

PIRI: The leaves are still in the wheelbarrow.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CLERK READS THE CLAIM.]

Seli says Odo claims the leaves because he raked them from the path. Nara claims them because she moved them into the compost area. Pava asks what remedy they want.

ODO: I want credit.

NARA: I want the barrow back.

PAVA: These are different claims.

Piri measures the pile. The leaves reach the top of the wheelbarrow and spill over one side.

PIRI: The evidence is overflowing.

Seli says the wheelbarrow belongs to the garden club. Odo says he borrowed it. Nara says he left it by the path for two days.

ODO: I was waiting for the leaves to dry.

NARA: They were already dry.

PAVA: The court will hear testimony on the condition of the leaves.

### Act Two

[ODO PRESENTS A RAKE AS EVIDENCE.]

Odo shows that the rake has leaves caught between its teeth. Nara says the leaves are from the path, but she also added a handful from beneath the oak.

PAVA: How many leaves did you add?

NARA: One handful.

PIRI: We have a mixed pile.

Odo asks to separate his leaves. Pava allows him to try. He sorts by shape, but the leaves are all similar.

ODO: These are mine.

NARA: That one has an oak mark.

ODO: The oak is beside the path.

Seli enters a photograph of the pile before it was moved. The picture shows the same wheelbarrow under the oak.

PAVA: The leaves were already together.

NARA: I did not combine them. I moved the barrow.

ODO: You moved the barrow and the leaves.

PAVA: The barrow's ownership is not before the court.

Piri starts to push the wheelbarrow. Pava stops him; the exhibit must remain in place.

[THE COURT CALLS FENN, THE GARDEN CLUB TREASURER.]

The treasurer explains that the wheelbarrow is shared and that members often leave tools near the compost heap. Pava asks whether there is a rule about who receives credit for gathered leaves.

TREASURER: We usually thank whoever did the work.

ODO: I did the raking.

NARA: I moved the barrow to the heap.

PAVA: Then both contributions are clear.

Odo says the board only has space for one name. The treasurer offers a second board, but it is already full of watering schedules.

PIRI: We can shorten the names.

NARA: We can write smaller.

Odo writes "O." and Nara writes "N." The treasurer says nobody will know what the letters mean. Pava asks them to add the action beside each name.

TREASURER: This is a garden board, not a court transcript.

PAVA: It can still be accurate.

Seli finds a blank strip at the bottom and places it under the schedule.

### Act Three

[THE JUDGE REVIEWS THE EVIDENCE.]

Pava finds that Odo gathered the leaves and Nara moved the barrow into the compost area. Both actions helped the garden. She orders the leaves to be composted, the barrow returned to the club, and each gardener to receive credit on the garden board.

ODO: We share the credit?

PAVA: You share the leaves.

NARA: I can write the sign.

Odo says he should write it because he raked. Nara says she has neater handwriting. Pava asks them to write it together.

They take a marker. Odo writes "Odo gathered" and Nara adds "Nara moved." The sign is too large for the board and covers the compost schedule.

SELI: The court has created a new dispute.

PAVA: The schedule is not evidence.

Seli moves the sign below the schedule. Piri wheels the leaves toward the heap.

Before the tag, Pava asks Odo and Nara to sign the ledger. Odo signs first, then Nara adds the compost date.

ODO: The leaves are ready to use.

NARA: After they break down.

PAVA: The court will not supervise the compost.

ODO: We can check it next week.

NARA: The garden club can.

### Tag

[THE GARDEN BOARD.]

The sign reads "Odo gathered, Nara moved, the garden used."

ODO: That is accurate.

NARA: And the barrow is back.

PIRI: The heap has received the evidence.

[SFX: LEAVES RUSTLE.]

PAVA: Court is adjourned.
