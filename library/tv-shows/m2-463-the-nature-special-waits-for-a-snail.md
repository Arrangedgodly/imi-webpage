# The Nature Special Waits for a Snail

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: wildlife documentaries, portentous narration, and camera crews that demand dramatic movement from tiny animals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Narrator Koro, a baboon; field host Leni, a gibbon; camera operator Mavi, an orangutan; naturalist Pali, a macaque; sound recordist Varo, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

A snail rests on a broad leaf beside a damp garden path. Koro narrates from a voice booth. Leni crouches nearby; Mavi has the camera on a tripod.

KORO: At dawn, the forest's fastest predator begins its pursuit.

PALI: It is a garden snail.

LENI: What is it pursuing?

PALI: The leaf is in the shade. It will move when the leaf warms.

[The snail remains still. Koro's narration continues over a close-up.] 

KORO: The chase has begun.

MAVI: Nothing has moved.

[The theme music swells, then loops because the snail remains in frame.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. GARDEN PATH - CONTINUOUS

The crew waits. Leni asks Pali whether the snail has a destination. Pali explains that it is moving toward a damp patch, but the path is only a few centimeters away. Koro asks to record a more urgent narration.

KORO: The creature approaches its perilous crossing.

PALI: It is crossing a leaf vein.

VARO: I can hear the wind in the microphone. I cannot hear a chase.

[The sound recordist holds a small wind cover over the microphone. Mavi keeps the lens steady. Leni begins a whispered play-by-play; Pali asks her not to block the light.] 

LENI: One eye stalk has turned.

PALI: That is the snail checking its surroundings.

KORO: A bold reconnaissance.

MAVI: Please stop telling the snail what it intends.

[The host proposes setting down a piece of fruit to make the snail move faster. Pali says the snail is not being fed for the camera. Leni takes the fruit back to the crew's lunch bag.] 

ACT TWO

EXT. GARDEN PATH - LATER

The snail reaches the edge of the leaf. Its foot stretches toward the damp path. Mavi adjusts the tripod rather than zooming too close. The recordist marks the time. Koro records another narration take, this time describing the waiting crew.

KORO: Four patient professionals observe one small traveler.

PALI: That is accurate.

LENI: It is also our episode.

[The snail lowers onto the path. Mavi captures the movement in a single shot. Leni asks whether they should cheer. Pali says the snail is not competing. The host quietly waves a tiny flag behind the camera, trying to add a finish line.] 

MAVI: The flag is in frame.

LENI: It is not a finish line.

[The crew moves the flag out of the shot. The snail continues toward the damp patch. Koro asks for one final dramatic word. Pali tells him the snail has reached the wet soil.] 

KORO: Arrival.

PALI: It is now eating a leaf.

[The host asks whether the snail has won. The naturalist explains that it has simply reached a suitable place. The camera stays on the snail as it feeds; nobody touches the leaf.] 

[As the crew packs up, Varo asks the host for one more reading of the wind, with no music underneath. Koro tries to whisper dramatically, but the sound recordist holds up a finger.] 

KORO: The microphone is capturing the breeze and nothing else.

VARO: Now it is capturing your whisper too.

[The narrator steps back from the mic and gives the field crew room to finish. Mavi lowers the camera only after the snail has moved out of the leaf's shadow.] 

ACT THREE

EXT. GARDEN PATH - AFTERNOON

The crew reviews the footage. The snail's entire crossing takes several minutes. Koro's narration calls it a journey; Pali corrects him once, then allows the shot to play without voice-over. Mavi trims only the time spent adjusting focus, not the snail's movement.

LENI: We have the crossing and the feeding.

VARO: We also have a clean recording of the wind.

KORO: Should I add a cliffhanger?

PALI: The snail is not going to a cliff.

[The host unrolls the shot list. It includes a sunrise, a chase, and an aerial view. Mavi crosses out the chase and aerial view; the snail never left the garden bed.] 

MAVI: We filmed what happened here.

KORO: Then I will narrate what we know.

[The naturalist names the species from the field guide and returns the book to its case. The host asks if they can revisit the snail tomorrow. Pali says only if it remains in the same public garden bed and the crew does not disturb it.] 

TAG

The snail reaches the edge of the damp patch. Koro whispers, "To be continued." Pali points out that the scene is complete; Koro changes the line to, "The snail continues eating." 

FINAL IMAGE: The camera holds a quiet close-up of the snail feeding while the crew waits without music.
