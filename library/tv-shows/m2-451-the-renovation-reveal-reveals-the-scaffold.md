# The Renovation Reveal Reveals the Scaffold

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home renovation competitions, surprise reveals, impossible deadlines, and hosts who insist every wall needs a reaction shot*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Salo, a gibbon; carpenter Miri, a macaque; designer Bop, a baboon; inspector Tavi, an orangutan; camera operator Luma, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. RENOVATION SET - DAY

A half-built reading nook sits behind a curtain. A bright sign reads REVEAL IN TEN MINUTES. The inspector holds a level. The host stands in front of the curtain, facing the camera.

SALO: In ten minutes, this bare corner becomes a calm place to read.

MIRI: The shelf is still on the floor.

BOP: The shelf is a concept.

TAVI: A concept cannot hold a book.

LUMA: Camera ready from the control booth. I need the curtain to stay closed.

SALO: We will reveal the surprise after the break.

MIRI: The surprise is that the room is unfinished.

[The curtain slips open one inch. Salo blocks it with an oversized sponsor pillow.]

SALO: And we are out.

ACT ONE

INT. RENOVATION SET - CONTINUOUS

Miri sets the shelf against the wall and checks the brackets. Tavi checks the studs on the exposed frame. Bop lays out a row of decorative cushions. Luma comes onto the set to move a camera mark away from the saw horses, then returns to the booth.

TAVI: This bracket is not anchored. The shelf cannot be loaded yet.

BOP: It will be behind the plant.

TAVI: Plants do not support shelves.

MIRI: I can anchor it, but the stud is here. The camera track runs through that spot.

LUMA: The track can move. The reveal shot can't be from inside the shelf.

SALO: Every great room has a reveal angle.

MIRI: It also has a wall.

[Miri and Tavi mark the stud, move the track, then fasten the bracket. The host asks Luma to rehearse the curtain pull. Luma refuses until the shelf is secure.]

BOP: We can make the bookshelf look taller if we put the plant on top.

TAVI: The plant goes on the floor.

BOP: The floor is not in the shot.

LUMA: It will be if the plant tips.

[Salo practices a gasp into the camera. Bop whispers that the gasp should land after the sponsor line. The host repeats it with more volume. The echo makes the shelf rattle. Miri tightens one screw.]

MIRI: The shelf is secure. The host is not.

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Luma watches the monitor from the control booth and speaks to the set crew through her headset as they set books on the shelf. Salo holds the sponsor pillow in place. Bop asks Miri to hide the tools behind the curtain. Tavi sees the curtain brush against a loose cable and stops the reveal rehearsal.

TAVI: That cable cannot be under the rug. Someone will trip on it.

BOP: It is the power lead for the glowing sign.

LUMA: Then the sign goes dark until the cable is secured.

SALO: The reveal sign is part of the reveal.

MIRI: The actual room is part of the renovation.

[Tavi routes the power cable along the wall, tapes it flat at two points, and checks that the curtain clears it. Luma watches the monitor and tells the set crew the line is clear through her headset. The camera operator switches off the sign. The host looks at the dark letters, then turns to the camera.] 

SALO: Our dramatic countdown is temporarily in shadow.

TAVI: The cable is taped down, and the curtain clears it. Now it is safe.

[The inspector tests the shelf with one book, then two. It holds. Bop puts a tiny plant on the floor and reluctantly admits the room looks larger without it on the shelf.]

BOP: I dislike how reasonable the floor is.

MIRI: It has been here the whole build.

[The control booth calls. The sponsor pillow covers the one remaining unpainted patch. Salo asks to leave it there. Miri removes the pillow and paints the patch with a small brush.]

ACT THREE

INT. RENOVATION SET - EVENING

The team stands outside the curtain. Tavi signs the inspection card. Luma checks the camera frame and sees the curtain covers the room but not the exit. Salo holds the cord, prepared for a dramatic pull.

SALO: Viewers, we missed the ten-minute reveal by most of the day. The sponsor timer was only for the break; this nook took eight hours.

MIRI: It has been built, inspected, and furnished.

BOP: And it has exactly one decorative cushion.

TAVI: The shelf is anchored. The cable is secured. The exit remains clear.

[Salo pulls the cord. The curtain opens. A modest reading nook appears: one chair, a secured shelf, a lamp, and the plant on the floor. The camera holds on the shelf rather than Salo's face.] 

SALO: Where is the gasp?

LUMA: It was blocking the bookshelf.

BOP: The room looks finished.

MIRI: It is finished. The audience can use it now.

[The inspector places a book on the shelf. It stays. Salo starts another countdown, then sees that the room is already open.] 

SALO: No second reveal. The deadline is over; please enjoy the reading nook.

TAG

Salo sits in the chair and opens the sponsor pillow, which is actually a cushion cover. He tries to read the sponsor copy aloud. Miri hands over a book instead.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera frames the secured shelf and open book while the host quietly reads without a countdown.
