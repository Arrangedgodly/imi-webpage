# The Reunion Surprise Is Already Here

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime reunion shows that manufacture emotional suspense even when the guests recognize each other*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; producer Vex, an orangutan; guest Leni, a gibbon; guest Neri, a capuchin; stage manager Pavi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. DAYTIME STUDIO - MORNING

[A curtain hides a small bench. Leni waits on the left mark; Neri waits on the right. Mara holds a card reading "SURPRISE REUNION."]

MARA: Leni, someone from your past is here.

LENI: Neri?

[Behind the curtain, Neri sneezes.]

MARA: We have not revealed anyone yet.

[Theme music rises.]

ACT ONE

MARA: Welcome to Together Again, where every reunion starts with a question and ends with a hug we can see from the balcony.

VEX: The audience must not know who is behind the curtain.

PAVI: They can hear her sneeze.

VEX: We can add a drum hit.

[The band plays a loud sting. Neri sneezes again.]

LENI: Neri has a dusty costume.

MARA: How could you possibly know?

LENI: She sneezes before a costume change.

[Vex signals Pavi to keep the curtain shut. Neri's tail slips beneath it and curls around the bench leg.] 

MARA: Leni, what would you say if you saw your friend?

LENI: I would ask why she is behind a curtain.

MARA: And how would that make you feel?

LENI: Like I was on television.

[Vex sends Pavi to check the cue card. Pavi walks behind the curtain and comes back holding Neri's scarf.] 

PAVI: The reveal is wearing blue.

VEX: Put the scarf back.

ACT TWO

[Mara interviews Leni about the last time she saw Neri. The story was yesterday, at the studio cafeteria. Vex asks for a dramatic detail.] 

LENI: She borrowed my spoon.

MARA: And you have never seen it again?

LENI: I saw it in her hand five minutes ago.

[The camera cuts to Neri. She holds up the spoon. The audience laughs.] 

VEX: This is a major development.

PAVI: It is a cafeteria spoon.

VEX: It is the object that connects them.

[Neri pushes the curtain aside. Vex pulls it closed. The fabric wraps around his arm and pins him to the bench.] 

NERI: Can I come out now?

MARA: We are building to the reveal.

NERI: I have been in the green room for forty minutes.

VEX: We are building to the exact right moment.

[Neri begins grooming her sleeve. Leni notices the movement in the curtain and imitates the gesture. Neri pauses. Leni pauses. They both start again in time.] 

MARA: What are you doing?

LENI: Grooming.

NERI: She has a leaf on her collar.

[Leni reaches around the curtain and removes a small leaf from Neri's collar without looking. The crowd applauds.] 

VEX: They have touched before the reveal.

PAVI: The reveal has happened.

[Vex asks the audience to hold the reveal card high. It is wider than the bench and covers the curtain seam.]

VEX: The secret guest is still secret.

PAVI: We can see her shoes.

[The cue card tilts. Neri reaches under the curtain and straightens one corner. Leni reaches from the other side and straightens the opposite corner. Their hands meet.]

MARA: We have another connection.

LENI: We share a curtain.

NERI: And a spoon.

[Vex lowers the card. Its glued letters peel off and stick to the curtain, spelling "SURPRISE" backward.]

VEX: The word has turned around.

PAVI: It knew the guests already knew.

ACT THREE

[Mara stands center stage and asks Leni to turn toward the curtain. Vex gives Pavi a countdown card. Pavi holds up three fingers, then two. Neri's hand appears through the curtain at one.] 

MARA: Leni, open your heart to the unknown.

LENI: I know exactly who it is.

MARA: Then open the curtain.

[Leni pulls one edge. Vex pulls the other. The curtain catches on his sleeve and drops over both of them. Neri steps forward and lifts it from their heads.] 

NERI: There. Surprise.

LENI: You have my spoon.

NERI: I washed it.

[She returns it. Leni examines the handle.] 

LENI: You bent it.

NERI: You left it in the drawer.

MARA: We have a reunion and a dispute.

VEX: Can we bring out the oversized check?

PAVI: There is no prize.

VEX: There is an apology card.

[He hands Neri a card. It reads "SORRY I KEPT YOUR SPOON." Leni turns it around; the back reads "PLEASE RETURN IT."] 

LENI: That's not an apology.

NERI: It is the spoon's itinerary.

TAG

[Mara points both guests toward the exit. They groom each other's shoulders as they walk.] 

VEX: The reunion is complete.

PAVI: We still have to return the curtain.

[The curtain follows Vex, still caught on his sleeve.] 

MARA: He is having another reunion.
