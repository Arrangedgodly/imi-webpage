# Lines Tight at the Dock

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fishing shows whose hosts promise a trophy catch but must learn to read weather, water and animal behavior*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Host Daro promises viewers the biggest catch of the season. A patient local fisher knows the fish are sheltering under the dock, and the crew's loud production plan keeps scaring them away.

Cast: Host Daro, a macaque; fisher Uma, a capuchin; camera operator Len, a gibbon; and juvenile monkey Sol.

## Episode: "The Quiet Bite"

### Cold Open

[A BOAT ROCKS BESIDE A DOCK. DARO HOLDS UP A HUGE EMPTY NET.]

DARO: Today we catch a legendary river fish.

UMA: The fish are under the dock.

DARO: Then we will bring them to the boat.

[A LOUD THEME SONG PLAYS. WATER SPLASHES. UMA LOOKS UNDER THE DOCK; THE FISH SWIM AWAY.]

### Act One

[THE CREW SETS UP A CAMERA, A LIGHT, AND A BANNER READING "MONSTER CATCH."]

LEN: The banner is blocking the view.

DARO: The viewers need the promise.

UMA: The fish need quiet.

SOL: I can see one by the post.

[LEN ADJUSTS THE CAMERA. THE LIGHT REFLECTS ON THE WATER.]

UMA: Turn off the light.

DARO: We have a bright production.

UMA: The fish have bright eyes.

[THE LIGHT GOES OFF. SOL POINTS TO A SMALL RIPPLE.]

DARO: There it is. How do I catch it?

UMA: Wait for the line to settle.

[DARO THROWS THE LINE. IT SPLASHES LOUDLY.]

UMA: That is not settled.

### Act Two

[DARO REELS IN THE LINE. THE BAIT IS GONE.]

DARO: The fish stole my bait.

UMA: The fish ate the bait.

DARO: We have a criminal.

LEN: No, we have a hungry fish.

[SOL CLIMBS DOWN TO THE LOW DOCK AND SITS STILL.]

UMA: Sol, stay behind the rail.

SOL: I am watching the water.

[THE FISH RETURNS. IT NIBBLES THE BAIT. DARO REACHES FOR THE ROD TOO QUICKLY.]

UMA: Not yet.

[THE FISH SWIMS AWAY.]

DARO: I let it go.

UMA: You startled it.

DARO: The moment was building.

UMA: The fish does not know the show schedule.

[THE CREW TAKES DOWN THE BANNER. LEN HOLDS THE CAMERA LOW AND STEADY.]

[UMA USES A SMALLER HOOK AND A FLOAT. DARO WATCHES HER HANDS.]

DARO: Is this a special technique?

UMA: It is a small hook for a small fish.

DARO: The viewers expect a monster.

UMA: The river has not promised one.

[SOL POINTS AT THE FLOAT. IT HAS DRIFTED TOWARD A POST.]

SOL: It is moving.

UMA: The current is taking it around the post.

[DARO REELS A LITTLE. THE FLOAT TURNS BACK.]

DARO: I can feel the river.

UMA: You can feel the line.

[THE LINE TUGS. DARO WAITS. UMA NODS. HE LIFTS THE ROD GENTLY AND BRINGS UP A SECOND SMALL FISH.]

DARO: A second catch.

UMA: Check it, then return it.

[THE FISH IS RELEASED. DARO LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.]

DARO: Two fish, both released. The biggest catch may have been restraint.

UMA: That is a good title.

[THE CREW WATCHES THE FISH SWIM INTO THE SHADE.]

DARO: We came to catch the river's biggest fish.

UMA: You caught two fish and returned both.

DARO: The river has a different ending than the one I planned.

LEN: We can film the water instead.

[LEN TURNS THE CAMERA TOWARD THE CURRENT. THE BANNER LIES ON THE BANK.]

SOL: The banner is getting wet.

[DARO PICKS IT UP AND FOLDS IT.]

DARO: The monster catch can wait. We have enough for today's show.

UMA: The river is still here tomorrow.

UMA: And a good cast leaves the fish in the river.

[DARO WRITES "QUIET BITE" ON THE SHOW SLATE. UMA CARRIES THE ROD BACK.]

UMA: The best part of fishing was waiting quietly.

DARO: I will put that in the next opening, after the weather report.

### Act Three

[THEY WAIT IN QUIET. THE LINE TUGS. DARO REELS GENTLY AND BRINGS UP A SMALL FISH.]

DARO: We have a catch.

UMA: Keep it in the water while we check it.

[UMA MEASURES THE FISH, THEN RETURNS IT TO THE RIVER.]

DARO: It was smaller than the legend.

UMA: It was a fish.

SOL: It came back under the dock.

[THE FISH SWIMS AWAY. DARO LOOKS AT THE EMPTY NET.]

DARO: We can call this a release.

LEN: It is the only accurate title.

DARO: Tomorrow we try again, quietly.

UMA: We do not need to catch anything to understand the water.

DARO: Can we still have a theme song?

[THE MUSIC STARTS SOFTLY. A FISH SPLASHES UNDER THE DOCK.]

UMA: That was the fish leaving.

DARO: It has excellent timing.

[THE CREW PACKS UP WITHOUT THE BANNER. SOL WAVES TO THE WATER.]
