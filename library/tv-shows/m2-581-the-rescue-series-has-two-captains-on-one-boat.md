# The Rescue Series Has Two Captains on One Boat

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rescue-reality television, competing command shots, and a training crew whose two captains keep steering for the camera*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Captain Sena, a macaque; Captain Odo, an orangutan; producer Vex, a baboon; deckhand Tavi, a gibbon; safety officer Kiri, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. HARBOR TRAINING DOCK - DAY

A small rescue boat pivots slowly in a circle.

VEX: The harbor is difficult today.

KIRI: The harbor is still.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The rescue series is filming a practice run in a marked harbor lane. Sena is the assigned captain at the helm. Odo is the navigation captain, responsible for calling the buoys. Producer Vex wants two dramatic command close-ups and has told both captains to "take charge" at the same time.

SENA: Odo, call the first buoy.

ODO: Red buoy to port.

TAVI: We are turning toward the dock.

SENA: I have the helm.

ODO: I called the buoy.

VEX: Both of you say it again, with more urgency.

KIRI: We are not in an emergency. This is a drill.

Vex asks the camera operator for a tighter shot. The camera zooms past both captains and films a coiled rope. Tavi checks the training schedule and reads the next mark: hold position, then simulate a safe approach to the floating target.

TAVI: We have not reached the target.

VEX: Can the target come closer?

KIRI: We can tow it to the marked practice point before the next take.

Sena brings the boat to neutral. Odo continues calling turns as though the boat is moving. It slowly swings with the current and rotates toward the dock. Kiri checks the stern line; it is still tied to the training float.

KIRI: We are turning because the line is attached.

SENA: I thought I was correcting the drift.

ODO: I thought I was directing a correction.

VEX: It looks excellent from this angle.

KIRI: It looks like a boat tied to its own camera mark.

ACT TWO

Kiri asks everyone to pause while she secures the boat. She points out the stern line is for holding position at the dock and must be released before the drill begins. Tavi holds the camera cable clear of the cleat. Sena confirms neutral and Odo checks the water around the stern.

SENA: Neutral confirmed.

ODO: Stern clear.

KIRI: I will release the training line. No one engages until it is aboard.

Vex wants to film the rope dropping in slow motion. Kiri refuses because the line can snag. She hands the free end to Tavi, who places it in the marked bin. The boat stops pivoting. Vex asks the captains to repeat their earlier commands for coverage.

SENA: I have already given the command.

ODO: And I have already called the buoy.

VEX: Could we get a second version?

KIRI: We can rehearse on the dock without moving the boat.

Sena explains their roles: she steers, Odo navigates, and each one confirms the other's instruction. Odo will not call another heading until Sena says she is ready. Tavi holds up the laminated route card with the first buoy highlighted.

ODO: Red buoy to port when you are clear.

SENA: I am clear. Taking the marked lane.

KIRI: Training line aboard. Safety lane is open.

The boat moves forward at walking speed. Vex asks for a dramatic rescue call, but the target is still being towed to the practice point. The camera catches Tavi dragging the float from shore with a long pole.

TAVI: Target arriving.

VEX: Make it look like it needs help.

KIRI: It is a floating practice marker. It can wait its turn.

ACT THREE

At the marked point, the crew stops the boat and checks its position. Sena keeps the helm. Odo calls the next buoy once. Their coordinated approach lets Tavi place the rescue loop around the floating marker without leaning over the gunwale. The marker comes aboard safely.

SENA: Practice target secured.

ODO: Route remains clear.

KIRI: The drill is complete. Return to the dock at idle speed.

VEX: Can we have a final shot with both captains pointing in opposite directions?

SENA: Not while we are steering.

ODO: We can point at the two names on the duty board.

Tavi pins the duty board inside the cabin: SENa - HELM; ODO - NAVIGATION. Vex frames the shot. The letters are easy to read, though one is hidden behind the camera cable until Kiri reroutes it along the rail.

KIRI: Now the picture shows the jobs, not a crisis.

VEX: It needs more tension.

SENA: The boat is safely tied, the lane is clear, and the take is finished.

ODO: That is a clean ending.

The crew ties the boat to the proper dock cleat only after the motor is off. Kiri checks the knot. Vex starts to ask for another pass, then notices the camera is still recording the duty board.

FINAL IMAGE: Two captain names hang over one quiet boat, with a single helm and a clear harbor lane.
