# The Sheriff of the Unmarked Trail

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: western serials that frame minor disputes as showdowns at the frontier*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A trail marshal investigates who moved the sign at a fork, while two travelers argue over which path reaches the water.

Cast: Marshal Pava, a gibbon; traveler Odo, a baboon; traveler Kito, a macaque; trail keeper Nara, a capuchin.

Episode: "Showdown at the Fork"

COLD OPEN

EXT. TRAIL FORK - DAY

A sign lies face down in the dust. Odo and Kito stand on opposite sides.

ODO: The water is west.

KITO: It is east.

Pava rides in on a small cart.

PAVA: Nobody moves until the sign speaks.

ACT ONE

Nara arrives with a toolbox. The sign is not damaged; its post has loosened. Odo says someone turned it. Kito says the wind pushed it over. Pava asks each to state their case.

Odo followed the old trail map, which shows water to the west. Kito followed the painted arrow, which pointed east. Nara checks the map; it was printed before the pond path was built.

Pava calls for a standoff. Nara asks her not to draw her badge; the badge is pinned to her vest. Pava says the town expects a marshal to look decisive.

ACT TWO

Nara lifts the sign and checks its arrows. One arrow points east to the pond. A second, smaller arrow points west to the old well, now closed. Odo says the west arrow was hidden by dust. Kito says the east arrow was covered by the sign when it fell.

Pava asks if anyone tampered with the sign. Nara finds a fresh scrape at the base. Kito says he tried to lift it earlier but could not. Odo admits he turned it to read the west arrow. The post came loose.

Pava says the mystery is solved. Odo and Kito argue over who caused the fall. Nara says both touched the sign, and both should help reset it.

They dig a wider hole. The ground is dry and hard. Kito brings water from the canteen to soften the soil. Odo holds the post upright while Nara packs the dirt. Pava ties a rope to the cart to keep the sign straight.

ACT TWO-B

The sign is upright but tilts toward the east. Pava declares the trail has taken sides. Nara uses a level. Odo steps back to check the sightline. The sign points cleanly toward both trails.

Nara paints "POND" over the east arrow and "CLOSED WELL" over the west. Kito suggests removing the old arrow. Nara says keeping the warning helps travelers understand the layout.

ACT TWO-C

Pava asks Odo and Kito to walk both trails and verify the distances. The pond is east, twelve minutes away. The old well is west and is closed for repairs. Odo admits he had been following a map that no longer matched the town. Kito admits he moved the sign after it fell because the arrow was hard to read.

Nara makes a second small marker for the trail to the closed well. It says "WELL CLOSED, RETURN TO FORK." Pava says the message needs a warning symbol. Nara paints one. Odo asks if the marshal should patrol the fork. Pava says she can pass through on her regular route; no standoff is needed.

They set the post deeper and tamp the dirt around it. Kito tests it by pushing gently from both sides. It stays upright. Odo checks that travelers can read the arrows from the approach. Pava asks him to stop calling the sign a witness.

ACT TWO-D

EXT. TRAIL FORK - SUNSET

Pava has placed two stools on opposite sides of the sign. The camera crew rolls in a fan to make the dust move.

PAVA: This is the final showdown. Each traveler will make one statement before the sign is questioned.

NARA: The sign is not a witness. We already know who moved it.

ODO: I turned it so I could read the arrow.

KITO: I tried to lift it after it fell. I couldn't.

PAVA: The frontier is full of complicated admissions.

NARA: Both of you will help hold the post while I tighten the bracket.

[PAVA PLANTS HER BOOTS BETWEEN THEM. THE FAN BLOWS HER HAT OVER THE SIGN.]

PAVA: Nobody cross the line!

KITO: The line is a coil of rope.

ODO: And your hat is on the pond arrow.

[PAVA RETRIEVES HER HAT. THE CAMERA OPERATOR ROTATES THE STOOLS TO KEEP HER CENTERED, TURNING THE SHERIFF AWAY FROM BOTH TRAVELERS.]

PAVA: I am facing the wrong direction.

NARA: So was the sign.

PAVA: Then let the record show I have restored order from a stool.

ACT THREE

Odo and Kito shake hands and agree to walk to the pond together. Pava says there will be no duel today. Nara says there was never a reason for one. The travelers head east.

Pava tips her hat to the camera.

TAG

The wind blows the sign slightly.

PAVA: Nobody move.

NARA: It is on a new post.
