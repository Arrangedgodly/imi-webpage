# The Acorn Is in the Envelope

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: high-pressure game shows, where a simple guessing game gains more rules each round*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Host Odo turns a hidden-acorn guessing game into a televised tournament whose contestants keep trying to learn the rules.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; contestant Lira, capuchin; contestant Seli, gibbon; announcer Pavi, macaque; stagehand Nara, lemur.

## Episode: "Final Answer"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. THREE IDENTICAL ENVELOPES SIT ON PEDESTALS.]

ODO: One envelope contains an acorn.

LIRA: Can we choose one?

ODO: After the warm-up round.

SELI: What's the warm-up?

ODO: Guess which envelope contains the acorn.

PAVI: The rules are clear.

NARA: The acorn is on the table beside the envelopes.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[ODO STANDS BEFORE A GIANT SCOREBOARD.]

ODO: Welcome to "Final Answer." Correct guesses win a point. Incorrect guesses also win a point if they are confidently delivered.

LIRA: How do we know if we're confident?

ODO: You must answer before the bell.

SELI: What if the bell rings first?

ODO: Then it was an incorrect answer.

Pavi rings the bell while Seli is reading the rules.

PAVI: Time.

SELI: I had not started.

ODO: The clock is part of the game.

Lira chooses envelope one. It is empty. Seli chooses envelope two; a folded card inside reads "Choose again." She asks if that is a clue.

ODO: It is an instruction.

LIRA: So the card is a contestant?

ODO: Only if it answers.

Nara moves the third envelope to the scoreboard pedestal. Odo says the envelopes cannot change places during a round. Nara says she was setting up the next one.

### Act Two

[THE SCOREBOARD LIGHTS UP. IT SHOWS LIRA: 1, SELI: 1, ENVELOPE: 0.]

ODO: The envelope is behind.

LIRA: It hasn't guessed.

ODO: The envelope is under pressure.

Seli asks for a lifeline. Odo offers "Ask the host." She asks whether the acorn is in envelope three.

ODO: I can neither confirm nor deny the envelope's contents.

LIRA: That sounds like yes.

ODO: It is not yes.

Pavi rings the bell. Odo awards Lira a point for a confident interpretation. Seli objects that she did not answer.

ODO: You have a point for objecting.

SELI: I would like another objection.

ODO: One per contestant.

Nara brings out a magnifying glass for the final round. The glass is too small to read the envelope labels. Lira turns the envelope over and hears something roll inside.

LIRA: I hear the acorn.

ODO: Sound clues are not permitted.

SELI: They are in the envelope.

ODO: The envelope is not a contestant.

Pavi rings the bell. The scoreboard adds a point to the envelope.

[SCOREBOARD RESET. THREE ENVELOPES RETURN TO THE PEDESTALS. AS ODO TURNS TO READ THE LIGHTNING-ROUND CARD, NARA TAKES THE ACORN FROM THE TABLE AND SLIPS IT INTO ENVELOPE ONE. THE CAMERA CATCHES HER HAND; THE CONTESTANTS ARE LOOKING AT THE SCOREBOARD.]

Nara announces a lightning round. Contestants must point to the envelope they think contains the acorn before Odo rings the bell.

LIRA: Can we hear the envelope first?

ODO: You may not touch, lift, sniff, shake, or address the envelopes.

SELI: Can we look at them?

ODO: Looking is permitted.

Pavi rings the bell early. Lira points to envelope one. Seli points to envelope three. Nara rotates the pedestal to align them, and the envelopes change places.

ODO: The contestants have lost their selections.

NARA: I moved the stand, not the envelopes.

LIRA: My answer was the left one.

SELI: Left from whose side?

Odo adds a rule that answers must include a direction and a landmark. The game has become a map-reading contest.

### Act Three

[THE ENVELOPES ARE SET BACK IN A ROW.]

Odo says the final round is worth five points. Contestants must choose an envelope, then explain why they chose it without mentioning the contents.

LIRA: I choose the one that made a sound.

ODO: You mentioned the contents.

LIRA: I mentioned the sound.

ODO: The sound came from the contents.

SELI: Then no explanation is possible.

ODO: You may describe your feelings.

SELI: I feel that envelope three is heavier.

ODO: That is an observation.

Pavi rings the bell. Seli wins a point. Lira holds envelope three up to the light, but Odo says shadows are not clues.

Nara asks whether they can finally open the envelopes. Odo says yes, the round is over. Lira opens envelope one; it contains an acorn. Seli opens envelope two; it contains the "Choose again" card. The third envelope is empty.

LIRA: The acorn moved.

NARA: I picked it up from the table and put it in envelope one while you reset the board.

ODO: That violates the rules.

NARA: The rules said the acorn would be in an envelope.

ODO: At the start of the show it was.

### Tag

[THE SCOREBOARD DISPLAYS NARA: 5.]

PAVI: We have a new winner.

ODO: The stagehand was not playing.

NARA: I moved the acorn.

ODO: Then you win the prize.

He hands her the envelopes.

NARA: I already have three.

[SFX: BELL RINGS. SCOREBOARD GOES DARK.]
