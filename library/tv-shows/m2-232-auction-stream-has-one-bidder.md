# The Auction Stream Has One Bidder

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised auctions that stretch bidding drama around a modest object with a clearly stated value*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: An auction host tries to build suspense around a hand-carved coat hook, but the only bidder needs it for tomorrow's market stall.

Cast: Auctioneer Varo, a gibbon; bidder Neli, a macaque; appraiser Kito, a capuchin; and camera operator Pavo, a baboon.

Episode: "Going Once"

COLD OPEN

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - EVENING

A wooden coat hook sits on a velvet stand. Varo raises a gavel.

VARO: We begin at two shells. Do I hear three?

NELI: I bid two.

VARO: We have a bid. The room is on the edge of its seat.

KITO: There is one seat in the room.

[PAVO ZOOMS IN ON AN EMPTY CHAIR.]

ACT ONE

Varo turns the hook to show its grain. Kito studies a small card.

KITO: The hook was made by a local carpenter last week.

VARO: A piece of modern craftsmanship.

NELI: It is for my stall. I need to hang aprons.

VARO: The story deepens.

NELI: It has one hook.

KITO: The maker's mark is on the back.

[VARO TURNS IT OVER. A STICKER SAYS "MADE TUESDAY."]

VARO: A precise mark.

ACT TWO

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - LATER

Pavo arranges three paddles on empty chairs. Varo addresses the camera. Neli raises her paddle.

VARO: We have a second bid.

NELI: I am still at two.

VARO: The paddle was raised.

NELI: I am moving it because my elbow is tired.

[KITO TAKES THE PADDLES OFF THE CHAIRS.]

KITO: There is one actual bidder.

VARO: The audience at home may join online.

PAVO: The stream has one viewer, and it is our director.

VARO: We have one watcher.

NELI: Can I buy the hook?

VARO: Not until the bidding closes.

ACT TWO-B

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Kito reads the appraisal card. Varo prepares a dramatic countdown. Neli checks the clock.

KITO: The fair price is between two and three shells.

VARO: We have an estimate.

NELI: I can pay two.

VARO: Do I hear three?

NELI: No.

[THE COUNTDOWN MUSIC STARTS. NELI PUTS ON HER COAT.]

VARO: Once. Twice.

NELI: I need to get to the market.

VARO: The lot is still open.

KITO: There is no reserve price. The current bid is two.

[VARO LOOKS AT THE CARD.]

VARO: Sold for two shells.

ACT TWO-C

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - AFTER THE SALE

Pavo reads a message from the stream. Kito checks the auction terms. Neli waits beside the empty stand.

PAVO: A viewer asks if the hook comes with wall screws.

VARO: We have an interested bidder.

KITO: The auction is closed.

NELI: I have bought it.

VARO: We could reopen the lot.

NELI: You said sold.

[VARO POINTS TO THE GAVEL. IT HAS SLID OFF THE TABLE AND HUNG FROM ITS OWN HANDLE ON THE COAT HOOK.]

PAVO: The hook is now displaying the auction tool.

KITO: It has held the gavel.

VARO: A demonstration of quality.

NELI: I still need it for the stall.

KITO: The hook is sturdy enough for an apron.

PAVO: The viewer asks whether they can buy the gavel.

VARO: The gavel is not for sale.

[THE GAVEL SLIPS OFF THE HOOK AND LANDS ON THE VELVET STAND.]

VARO: We have another lot.

NELI: No. I have paid for the hook.

KITO: The paperwork is complete.

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Kito writes the sale price on a small card. Varo places it beside the empty stand.

VARO: The item sold for two shells after a suspenseful pause.

KITO: It sold for two shells because that was the bid.

PAVO: The viewer asks if the price included shipping.

NELI: I will carry it to the market myself.

VARO: A local delivery solution.

[NELI LIFTS THE HOOK. THE STAND IS LIGHTER THAN THE PACKING MATERIAL.]

KITO: The object is smaller than the box.

VARO: That is often true at auction.

PAVO: The stream has ended.

VARO: Do we hear another bid?

PAVO: No. The viewer has logged off.

NELI: The hook is mine now.

KITO: The sale is complete.

VARO: The hammer has completed the sale.

[NELI CARRIES THE HOOK OUT. VARO PICKS UP THE GAVEL AND CHECKS THE EMPTY STAND.]

ACT THREE

EXT. MARKET STALL - MORNING

Neli hangs an apron on the hook. It holds. Kito checks the fastening. Varo appears with the gavel.

VARO: The lot has found its new home.

NELI: It is on the stall.

KITO: The screw is secure.

[VARO LIFTS THE GAVEL TO BEGIN ANOTHER COUNTDOWN. NELI HANGS IT ON THE HOOK.]

NELI: It can hold the gavel too.

TAG

Varo stares at the empty stand in the studio.

VARO: Do I hear another bid?

[PAVO LOWERS THE CAMERA. NO ONE IS IN THE ROOM.]
