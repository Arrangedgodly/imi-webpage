# The Hemline Has Evidence

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fashion makeover competition convention, where judges demand a transformation but overlook the contestant's useful repair*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A television makeover team insists a practical rain cape needs glamour, while its wearer keeps asking for a dry shoulder.

Cast: Vela, a macaque host; Mino, a gibbon stylist; Piri, a capuchin tailor; and Seli, a baboon gardener.

Episode: "Runway Forecast"

COLD OPEN

INT. MAKEOVER TENT, DAY

Seli enters wearing a patched rain cape. Vela circles her with a clipboard.

VELA: We will transform your look.

SELI: It already keeps rain off.

Mino drapes a bright scarf over the cape. A gust blows it into the mud.

PIRI: The first accessory has left the competition.

ACT ONE

Mino shows Seli three outfits: a leaf-green poncho, a woven vest and a long trailing sash. Seli asks which has pockets. Mino says the sash has movement. Piri checks the weather report; rain is due before the runway.

Vela announces the judges will score silhouette, color story and reveal. Seli asks if they score waterproofing. Vela says that is not part of the brief.

Seli's old cape has a small tear at the shoulder. Piri offers to mend it. Mino insists the makeover must be visible. He stitches a bright patch over the tear. The patch is shaped like a sun and catches the camera light.

ACT TWO

EXT. RUNWAY, AFTERNOON

The contestants walk the runway. Seli's patched cape flaps in the wind, making her look as if she has wings. The judges applaud. A drizzle begins. The other contestants' accessories droop. Seli's cape sheds water.

VELA: The look is weather-aware.

MINO: The fabric was chosen for its function.

Vela asks Seli to pose. Seli turns to check the garden plots, where seedlings need cover. Her cape stretches over two trays. Mino objects that she has blocked the runway. Piri sees the plants are protected.

PIRI: The accessory has become a shelter.

The judges deliberate. One says the cape's silhouette is too practical. Another likes the sun patch. Seli says the patch marks the repaired shoulder and helps her find the tear next time.

ACT TWO-B

INT. MAKEOVER TENT, LATER

The judges ask for a second look under studio lights. Mino brings out a feathered shoulder cape and positions Seli in front of a mirror. She reaches for her garden shears, but the garment's long sleeve catches on the handle. Piri frees it and checks the stitching.

PIRI: This outfit cannot survive a normal afternoon.

MINO: It is meant for a reveal.

Seli asks to see the rain forecast. Vela says the forecast is not part of the makeover. A drop lands on the tent roof. Seli pulls her cape closed, while the feathered cape begins to sag.

Mino tries to hold the feathers in place with clips. The clips slide down the wet fabric and land in a shoe. Piri pins the patched cape's loose edge and shows how the repair leaves space for Seli's arm to move.

SELI: I need to reach the plants without opening the cape.

Vela asks if this could be called a performance feature. Seli demonstrates by kneeling and lifting a tray. The sun patch on her shoulder shows clearly against the wet gray cloth. The camera operator gets the shot without asking her to turn.

Mino suggests a gold trim. Piri measures the edge and finds it would cover the repair. Seli says the visible patch is useful because she can check it each morning. Vela writes this down as "personal detail." The judges ask Seli if she wants a complete transformation. She looks at the cape, then at the wet garden trays outside.

SELI: I want a second patch for the other shoulder.

Piri cuts a matching shape from the remaining cloth. Mino helps hold the cape while Piri sews it. It is a simple, visible change, and Seli can still move freely.

ACT THREE


INT. MAKEOVER TENT, EVENING

Vela announces the winner will be chosen for the most dramatic reveal. Seli removes the bright scarf and shows the patched cape. The judges ask her to turn around. The back of the cape has a small loop where she hangs her garden shears.

VELA: That is not part of the look.

SELI: It is part of the job.

The rain strengthens. Mino picks up the woven vest to cover the camera monitor. Piri offers the sash to tie the tent flap. The whole crew uses the makeover pieces to protect equipment and seedlings. Vela admits the practical look has won the evening.

TAG

EXT. GARDEN, NIGHT

Seli walks through the rows in her patched cape. Mino follows with the bright scarf now tied around a watering can. Vela announces a new challenge: create a rainproof look from the wardrobe closet. Piri opens the closet. A leak drips onto the garments.

PIRI: The next makeover is for the closet.
