# The Spelling Bee Writes the Word Before It

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised spelling competitions that reveal letters in graphics before contestants finish answering*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Rema, a macaque; contestant Suli, a lemur; contestant Varo, an orangutan; pronouncer Kiki, a gibbon; graphics operator Tavi, a capuchin; judge Kori, a squirrel monkey.

COLD OPEN

INT. SPELLING STAGE - NIGHT

Suli steps to the microphone. The board behind her spells ELEPHANT in giant letters.

REMA: Your word is elephant.

SULI: I can see it.

[The graphic operator hides the board under a curtain.]

ACT ONE

INT. SPELLING STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Tavi says the letter animation is meant to follow the answer, not lead it. Rema asks him to restart the graphic. Kiki pronounces the word again. Tavi pulls the curtain aside for the test. The audience board begins spelling before she finishes the first syllable. Varo asks if the contest has become a reading test.

VARO: Should I look at the board or listen?

REMA: Listen first.

Tavi covers the audience board with the curtain. The side-stage preview monitor stays on so the control room can test graphics without showing contestants. Rema tries to continue. Kiki gives Suli the word lantern. Suli asks for a definition and origin. Before Kiki answers, the side-stage preview monitor displays LANTERN - FROM THE FRENCH. Its reflection is visible in the polished podium top. Kiki says the preview has given away the answer and the wrong origin.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Tavi traces the graphics feed. The animation is linked to the rehearsal recording. He disconnects the animation feed. The audience board remains covered; the separate side-stage preview still shows yesterday's contestant names from its local cache. Rema asks him to clear the preview cache. Tavi clears it; the side-stage monitor displays a large zero.

Kiki gives Varo the word "unfurl." The zero remains. Varo spells it correctly. Tavi tries to load the score graphic manually. It displays Varo's name beside a crown before Rema announces the result. Rema permits one off-air score test before covering the audience board again. Tavi sends the manual score to that board and draws the curtain open; it catches on the microphone stand and stops halfway, revealing only the crown.

[The contestants move their podiums to block the graphic. The camera cuts to a wide shot, which shows the board reflected in the polished floor.]

Rema asks the contestants to turn away from the floor. Kiki pronounces another word. Suli asks for the number of syllables. The test score still shows a large three. Suli mistakes it for the syllable count, and Kiki has to explain that the number belongs to the score test. Tavi closes the audience-board curtain, moves the preview monitor off the polished podium and covers the podium top with a matte cloth. The reflection disappears; the cloth slips and covers the pronouncer's shoes.

INT. STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Kiki places the word cards face down on a stand. She draws one and reads it only after Tavi clears the manual score and confirms that the screens are blank. Rema asks for a definition; the graphic remains dark. Suli spells "copperplate" correctly. Varo asks for a sentence. Kiki gives one about a sign painter. The side-stage preview stays dark. Kiki puts the word cards in a closed box between turns.

INT. SPELLING STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Kiki gives Rema the tie-break jar. Rema checks the seal; Tavi's cable is no longer connected to the display. Suli and Varo face the audience instead of the board. Kiki explains that both must spell the same word without seeing each other's slate. The contestants turn their backs. The audience watches the empty board. Tavi reaches for a marker to write the word, then stops when Kiki reminds him that the tie-break word must stay in the sealed jar until needed. Neither contestant asks him to confirm the letters.

ACT THREE

INT. SPELLING STAGE - LATE NIGHT

The final round begins with no animations. Rema gives Varo a word. He asks for the definition, listens, and spells it correctly. Suli gets the next word. She asks for the language of origin. Kiki answers. Tavi watches the dark board and keeps both hands away from the controls.

Suli spells the word correctly. The audience applauds. Tavi reaches for the score graphic. Rema signals him to wait. Judge Kori totals the scores from the answer slates. Varo and Suli are tied. Kiki draws the tie-break word from the sealed jar and gives it to both contestants. They write their spellings on boards facing away from the camera. They reveal together; both are correct.

REMA: We have co-champions.

Before the last camera cue, Tavi checks the isolated preview monitor. A correction to Rema's name appears in the local cache. Rema tells him to correct the host card before the audience board is reopened. He types the right spelling on the preview, shows it to her, then clears the cache. The audience board stays covered through the certificate presentation. Tavi switches off its power at the wall. The show ends with the two contestants holding their paper certificates side by side; no new graphic appears.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE SHOW

Tavi labels the graphics cable AFTER ANSWERS. Kiki places the tie-break jar on the shelf. Rema checks the empty screen before she leaves.

FINAL IMAGE: Two blank name cards rest on the podiums while the powered-off spelling board stays dark behind the contestants' certificates.
