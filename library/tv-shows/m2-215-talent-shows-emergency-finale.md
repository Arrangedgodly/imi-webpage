# The Talent Show's Emergency Finale

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: talent competitions that manufacture suspense by delaying the result and demanding one more performance*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A small talent-show finale stalls while the judges debate categories, forcing the contestants to perform around the countdown.

Cast: Host Sela, a gibbon; judge Pavi, a macaque; judge Odo, a baboon; and contestants Runi, a capuchin, and Tali, a squirrel monkey.

Episode: "One More Minute"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - NIGHT

A large countdown clock reads 00:30. Sela stands with an envelope. The judges whisper behind a table.

SELA: The winner is in this envelope.

PAVI: We need to decide if the juggling counts as a circus act.

ODO: It was three beanbags.

[THE CLOCK HITS ZERO. IT BEEPS.]

SELA: The winner is...

RUNI: Can I put down the beanbags?

ACT ONE

Runi and Tali sit on a bench. Pavi holds the score sheets. Odo compares two numbers.

PAVI: The scores are tied.

TALI: Then we both win?

ODO: The format requires one winner.

SELA: We can have a tie-breaker.

RUNI: What do we do?

PAVI: One final performance.

TALI: We already performed.

[SELA STARTS A NEW COUNTDOWN. IT SHOWS TEN MINUTES.]

ACT TWO

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - BACKSTAGE

Runi stacks the beanbags in a small crate. Tali marks the floor where her dance ended. Pavi enters with new score cards.

PAVI: The second round needs a clear winner.

RUNI: We both did the extra performance.

TALI: The audience liked both.

PAVI: The judges need to separate technical skill from stage presence.

ODO: The beanbags landed in the crate every time.

PAVI: One bounced out.

RUNI: It hit the table leg.

[RUNI PUTS THE BEANBAG BACK IN THE CRATE. IT BOUNCES OUT AGAIN.]

TALI: That is the table leg's performance.

PAVI: It cannot compete.

SELA: We could have a teamwork round.

ODO: That would make the contestants a team.

PAVI: We are looking for one winner.

[SELA LOOKS AT THE COUNTDOWN. IT HAS BEEN RESET TO TEN MINUTES.]

SELA: The clock is longer than the finale.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - SIDE STAGE

The judges create a tie-breaker card. Runi and Tali wait with their props.

PAVI: One contestant will perform while the other holds the score sign.

TALI: Which one of us goes first?

ODO: The card says contestant one.

RUNI: There is no contestant number.

SELA: You can draw for it.

[TALI DRAWS A CARD. IT SAYS "TIE-BREAKER." RUNI DRAWS THE OTHER. IT ALSO SAYS "TIE-BREAKER."]

PAVI: The cards were printed from the same sheet.

ODO: We need a different challenge.

RUNI: I can juggle while Tali dances.

TALI: We already did that together in rehearsal.

SELA: Then the audience has seen the team version.

PAVI: But the rules say one contestant at a time.

[THE AUDIENCE STARTS A QUIET RHYTHM. TALI STEPS TO IT. RUNI JUGGLES IN TIME.]

ODO: They are performing together without the judges' card.

PAVI: We cannot score the audience.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - LATER

Runi juggles three beanbags while Tali does a short dance. The judges watch. The clock counts down.

PAVI: The juggling is more difficult.

ODO: The dance is more expressive.

PAVI: We cannot compare skill to expression.

SELA: Can you compare the audience reaction?

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS BOTH PERFORMANCES.]

ODO: They sound equal.

PAVI: We should ask for another round.

RUNI: My hands need a break.

TALI: My music is over.

SELA: We have a special question from the judges.

PAVI: What inspired your performance?

TALI: I wanted to dance.

ODO: What inspired the juggling?

RUNI: The beanbags were on the floor.

[THE CLOCK BEEPS.]

ACT THREE

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - FINALE

Sela opens the envelope. Inside is a card reading "AUDIENCE CHOICE." She turns it over. The back is blank.

SELA: We have the result.

PAVI: There is no name on the card.

ODO: The audience chooses.

SELA: The audience is still applauding both.

Runi and Tali step forward together.

RUNI: We can share the prize.

TALI: What is the prize?

[ODO LIFTS A SMALL TROPHY. IT HAS TWO HANDLES.]

PAVI: The trophy is made for two winners.

SELA: The format said one.

ODO: The trophy says two.

[THE JUDGES HAND IT TO BOTH CONTESTANTS. THE TROPHY TIPS, AND THEY BALANCE IT BETWEEN THEM.]

SELA: Please hold it steady for the photograph.

RUNI: I am holding the left handle.

TALI: I have the right.

PAVI: The trophy is level.

ODO: The winners are level too.

[THE FLASH GOES OFF. THE JUDGES LOOK AT THE PHOTO. BOTH CONTESTANTS ARE SMILING; THE JUDGES ARE BLURRED FROM LEANING IN.]

SELA: That is our winner photo.

TAG

The clock starts another countdown. Sela reaches for it.

SELA: We have one more minute.

RUNI: For what?

PAVI: The recap.

[TALI TAKES THE TROPHY AND WALKS OFF. THE CLOCK KEEPS COUNTING DOWN TO AN EMPTY STAGE.]
