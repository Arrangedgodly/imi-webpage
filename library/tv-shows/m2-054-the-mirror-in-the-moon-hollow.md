# The Mirror in the Moon Hollow

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fantasy anthology series, ominous prophecies, and magical-object rules*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A moonlit mirror in the old hollow appears to show every visitor their future, until a practical young keeper notices that it reflects the entrance rather than the person.

Cast: Keeper Mava, a lemur; traveler Joss, a macaque; elder Soro, an orangutan; mirror voice Venn, a gibbon; and messenger Kito, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Face in the Glass"

### Cold open

INT. MOON HOLLOW, NIGHT

[A silver mirror stands beneath a vine. Joss approaches. The reflection appears to show a crown.]

JOSS: I see myself as ruler.

MAVA: You are looking at the crown behind you.

JOSS: The mirror has revealed my destiny.

SORO: The mirror has revealed the display shelf.

[The mirror glows. A low hum fills the hollow.]

VENN: One who gazes shall learn what waits.

KITO: It is the lantern buzzing.

VENN: One who speaks shall lose the mystery.

MAVA: The lantern is low on oil.

### Act One

INT. MOON HOLLOW

[Soro gives Joss a smooth pebble.]

SORO: Place the pebble before the mirror.

JOSS: What will it reveal?

MAVA: Your future pebble.

JOSS: The mirror has no humor.

MAVA: It has no face.

VENN: The mirror sees beyond the present.

KITO: It sees the entrance.

JOSS: Why do you keep saying that?

KITO: Because the mirror points at the door.

[The mirror reflects a shadow moving outside.]

JOSS: A dark figure approaches.

MAVA: That is my shadow.

JOSS: Your future shadow?

MAVA: I am standing in front of the lamp.

SORO: Prophecies are often misunderstood.

MAVA: This one is a reflection.

JOSS: Then why does it glow?

KITO: We polished it with moon oil.

MAVA: There is no moon oil.

KITO: The bottle says "moon oil."

MAVA: It is cooking oil.

[The mirror hums again.]

VENN: The seeker shall carry a burden.

JOSS: I knew it. I will bear a heavy crown.

MAVA: The burden is the mirror frame.

Soro: It is very heavy.

### Act Two

INT. MOON HOLLOW, LATER

[Mava moves the mirror to face the opposite wall. The reflection now shows only stone.]

JOSS: The future has gone blank.

MAVA: The mirror is facing a wall.

VENN: The path is hidden.

KITO: The wall is in front of it.

SORO: Perhaps the wall is a metaphor.

MAVA: It is a wall.

[Joss looks into the mirror from the side. The reflection shows the entrance and a small shelf.]

JOSS: I see a doorway.

MAVA: That is the entrance.

JOSS: It is the same doorway I entered.

MAVA: A powerful return.

KITO: The mirror predicts that you will leave.

JOSS: I came here to ask whether I should travel.

SORO: The mirror says you have already arrived.

MAVA: It says you are standing in a room.

VENN: One who doubts the vision may miss the lesson.

MAVA: One who believes the frame may miss the door.

[The mirror slips in its stand. Everyone catches it.]

JOSS: It predicted danger.

MAVA: It predicted the mirror was loose.

SORO: That is a useful prophecy.

KITO: We can tighten the base.

MAVA: We can use a wedge.

JOSS: The pebble?

MAVA: Exactly.

[They wedge the pebble under the mirror frame. It stands straight.]

### Act Three

INT. MOON HOLLOW, DAWN

[Joss studies the mirror. The reflection shows all five characters and the open doorway.]

JOSS: I have decided to travel.

SORO: Did the mirror tell you?

JOSS: No. I did.

MAVA: The mirror has shown your future.

JOSS: Which part?

MAVA: The part where you walk through the door.

KITO: The door is open.

VENN: The seeker leaves with knowledge.

JOSS: I leave with a mirror wedge.

SORO: The pebble is a poor souvenir.

JOSS: It held the mirror steady.

MAVA: Then it served its purpose.

[The mirror gives one last flicker. The reflection shows a crown again.]

JOSS: There is the crown.

MAVA: That is the cooking pot on Soro's head.

SORO: I was keeping it warm.

### The second vision

INT. MOON HOLLOW, LATER

[Kito places a bowl of fruit before the mirror.]

KITO: We need to know which fruit is ripe.

VENN: The mirror reveals what is hidden.

MAVA: The fruit is under the bowl.

JOSS: That is not a vision.

KITO: It is a very useful answer.

[Soro lifts the bowl. All the fruit is green.]

SORO: The future is unripe.

MAVA: The orchard needs sunlight.

JOSS: Could the mirror show whether the sun will come out?

MAVA: The sun is already outside.

VENN: The light returns when the seeker opens the door.

[Kito opens the door. Sunlight falls across the bowl.]

KITO: The mirror was right.

MAVA: The door was open.

### Tag

EXT. MOON HOLLOW, MORNING

[Joss walks out. In the mirror, the open doorway stays empty. Kito turns the mirror to face the wall.]

KITO: The future is private now.

MAVA: The wall is still there.

[The lantern buzzes softly.]
