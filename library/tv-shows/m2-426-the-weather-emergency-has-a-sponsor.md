# The Weather Emergency Has a Sponsor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local news weather coverage where sponsorship and graphics compete with useful information*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Anchor Sela, a macaque; meteorologist Tavi, a gibbon; field reporter Pavi, an orangutan; camera operator Leni, a capuchin; gardener Nari, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL NEWS STUDIO - MORNING

[A weather map shows a small patch of rain over the town garden. Sela points to it with a branded umbrella.] 

SELA: We have a weather alert, brought to you by DrySoon Towels.

TAVI: It is a light shower.

[The sponsor logo covers the map. Theme music swells.]

ACT ONE

SELA: Welcome back. Tavi, how serious is this weather?

TAVI: A brief shower, then clearing.

SELA: Should viewers prepare?

TAVI: If you are outside, bring an umbrella.

[The umbrella opens. It catches on the studio lights and pulls Sela two steps sideways.] 

SELA: The conditions are moving indoors.

TAVI: The umbrella is moving.

[Field reporter Pavi appears on a split screen from the garden. Leni holds the camera under a small tent.] 

PAVI: I am standing beside the town's rain gauge.

NARI: You are under my tarp.

PAVI: The gauge reads one mark.

TAVI: That is a tenth of a cup.

PAVI: We have already prepared the storm graphic.

[Sela sends a towel from the sponsor. It blows across the studio floor, sticking to the umbrella's wet edge.]

SELA: DrySoon Towels are absorbing the news.

ACT TWO

[In the garden, Nari sets her seedlings beneath the natural drizzle. Pavi asks her to describe the impact.]

NARI: The soil is damp. The plants are fine.

PAVI: Can you point to something at risk?

NARI: The paper weather board. It is about to melt.

[The board is made of cardboard. Pavi lifts it; the corner tears. Leni zooms in.]

PAVI: We have structural damage.

NARI: You have a soggy sign.

[Back in studio, Tavi updates the map. The small patch moves east. Sela places the sponsor umbrella over the map screen, blocking the town.]

TAVI: Please move the umbrella.

SELA: It is the sponsor's product.

TAVI: It is also over the forecast.

[The studio fan blows. The umbrella turns inside out. Sela struggles to collapse it; it flips back open and sprays water from its folds onto the desk.] 

SELA: We have a localized indoor shower.

TAVI: The umbrella was wet.

[The sponsor lower-third changes from "DRYSOON" to "DRIP SOON" when water hits the keyboard.] 

[Leni feeds Pavi the updated map. Pavi holds it upside down, turning the rain patch into a storm over the ocean.] 

PAVI: The weather has moved offshore.

TAVI: Turn the map around.

ACT THREE

[The rain stops. Nari lifts the tarp. The seedlings are upright. Pavi tries to recover the torn board with tape; the sign flaps in the breeze and sticks to his jacket.] 

PAVI: I am wearing the alert.

NARI: It says "chance of showers."

PAVI: The town is on my back.

[In studio, Sela reads the corrected forecast. The umbrella lies open on the floor with a towel draped across it.]

SELA: The shower has passed. Streets are damp; no flooding reported.

TAVI: And the garden has received a tenth of a cup.

SELA: DrySoon Towels provided one dry spot.

TAVI: That was a camera bag.

[On split screen, Pavi removes the weather sign from his back. It is now dry where his jacket covered it and soggy at the edges.]

PAVI: The forecast has a clear center and damp margins.

NARI: That is the cardboard.

[The studio fan stops. The sponsor umbrella slowly closes itself, trapping the towel.] 

SELA: We have lost the sponsor.

TAVI: The weather has improved.

[Leni tries to keep the camera dry with a cloth hood. The hood blocks the weather map reflected in the studio window.]

TAVI: I can't see the map.

SELA: The sponsor towel is doing its job.

TAVI: It is doing your job.

[She hands Sela the cloth. Sela tries to wipe the umbrella and accidentally wipes the map screen, leaving a clean square surrounded by rain graphics.]

SELA: We have a clear patch.

TAVI: That is a fingerprint.

[They compare the clean square to the actual town map. The rain patch has moved east, away from the garden. Leni points to the live feed.]

LENI: The tarp is dry now.

PAVI: I can take it down.

NARI: Leave it over the seedlings. The next shower is later.

PAVI: But the dramatic weather is over.

NARI: The garden still needs shade.

[The crew folds the tarp, but wind catches it like a sail. Pavi holds one corner while Nari secures the other to a fence post. The tarp settles neatly above the seedlings.]

TAVI: That is a useful shot.

SELA: Can the towel sponsor it?

LENI: The towel is still inside the umbrella.

TAG

[The studio screen displays tomorrow's forecast: SUNNY. A single drop from the umbrella lands on the sun icon.] 

SELA: Tomorrow, the sun is sponsored by Shade.

TAVI: Don't cover the map.
