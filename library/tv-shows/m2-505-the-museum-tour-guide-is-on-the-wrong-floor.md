# The Museum Tour Guide Is on the Wrong Floor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live museum tour broadcasts that follow the guide's headset feed instead of the guide's actual location*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: guide Neri, a lemur; host Toma, a macaque; producer Sela, a capuchin; camera operator Garo, an orangutan; visitor Omi, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - DAY

Toma faces a blank display case. Neri speaks through his earpiece.

NERI (V.O.): To your left, you will see the blue pottery.

TOMA: I see a wall.

[The camera pans. The pottery is behind him.]

ACT ONE

INT. GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

Sela says the headset feed is on a seven-second delay. Neri is guiding a real group on the floor below. Toma asks her to describe the next exhibit. The audio arrives after the camera has moved on. He turns left; the camera turns right. Garo follows Toma, filming the back of his head as he searches for the pottery.

NERI (V.O.): The bowl was recovered near the river.

TOMA: We have found it.

Toma points at a trash bin. Garo follows the gesture. Neri says the bowl is in the case behind them. Sela tells the crew to stop using the delayed headset.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Sela routes Neri's feed to the studio monitor. The delay remains. She asks Neri to give a visual cue. Neri raises a blue card. Toma sees it on the monitor and turns toward the camera. Garo points the lens at the card instead of the gallery. The monitor shows the card three seconds after Neri lowers it.

[Visitor Omi enters the gallery carrying a folded map. He asks Toma where the ancient pottery is. Toma looks at his headset, then at the case directly behind him. He answers correctly, but the delayed audio repeats his previous wrong directions.]

Toma tries to speak over the delay. His voice overlaps Neri's tour. Sela asks them to use names before each line. Neri says "Guide," Toma says "Host," and both keep talking. Garo mutes the return feed. The gallery becomes quiet, but the studio loses the tour audio entirely.

INT. GALLERY - MOMENTS LATER

Omi unfolds the museum map. The current exhibit is marked on the reverse side. Toma turns the map over and finds the floor plan. Neri appears at the far end of the gallery with her real tour group. She waves to the camera and walks toward the pottery case. Toma greets her in person. Sela tells Garo to keep both of them in the shot.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Sela clears the buffer and checks the headset with a short count. Neri says one, then two. Toma hears both immediately. Garo points his camera toward the pottery case before the next line begins. Omi asks Neri the date of the bowl. She answers without a delay; Toma repeats it only after she finishes. The tour group remains visible behind them, moving at the pace of the explanation. Sela records the live feed without adding music or captions over Neri's words. Toma gives the visitor the map and waits while she finds the stairs. Garo keeps both the guide and the pottery in frame as the group moves. The producer signals for a cut, but Sela holds the shot until the last visitor exits.

ACT THREE

INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Neri gives the explanation live, without the delayed headset. Toma repeats the key date after she finishes each point. Omi checks the map and asks where the next exhibit is. Neri points to the stairs. The camera follows her gesture and captures the correct gallery. Garo holds the shot while the group moves.

Sela switches off the delayed feed. The earpiece continues to play the old audio from its buffer. Toma hears himself direct visitors to the trash bin. He removes the earpiece and hands it to Sela. She waits for the audio to end, then powers down the pack. The room stays quiet. Neri finishes the tour at the pottery case. Omi thanks her and turns toward the stairs.

Toma asks for a closing line. Neri stands beside him in the same frame. He points to the exhibit only after looking at it. Garo captures the bowl, the guide and the visitor consulting the map. The producer does not ask for a dramatic turn or another direction cue. The group exits toward the next floor together.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE TOUR

Sela labels the headset BUFFERED AUDIO ONLY. Toma checks the gallery behind him before introducing the next case. Garo waits for Neri's real hand signal.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera holds on the pottery display as the guide and host stand beside it, both facing the correct direction.
