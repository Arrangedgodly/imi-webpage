# The Canopy Climb-Off

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised athletic qualifiers, expert commentary, and photo-finish replay disputes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: Two climbers compete to reach the top branch, but the course's finish bell is attached to a branch that moves whenever anyone touches it.

Cast: Host Luri, a lemur; climber Beko, a gibbon; climber Nala, a macaque; judge Taro, a baboon; and replay analyst Mivi, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Touch the Bell"

### Cold open

EXT. CLIMB COURSE, DAY

[A bell hangs from a high branch. Beko and Nala stand at the base of a tree.]

LURI: The first climber to ring the bell wins.

TARO: The branch is secure.

NALA: It is moving.

BEKO: The bell is moving with it.

MIVI: The replay camera is ready.

LURI: The competitors are ready.

[The branch swings. The bell rings by itself.]

TARO: We have a false start.

LURI: The bell has begun without them.

### Act One

EXT. CLIMB COURSE

[Beko climbs quickly, using a series of low branches. Nala takes a slower route.]

LURI: Beko is in the lead.

MIVI: The replay shows Nala started first.

TARO: The bell rang before the start.

NALA: We are already climbing.

TARO: The start was the whistle.

LURI: I have a whistle.

[The whistle gives a small squeak.]

BEKO: Was that the start?

LURI: That was the warm-up.

TARO: We will restart.

NALA: The branches have moved.

BEKO: The tree is growing.

TARO: The course must remain identical.

MIVI: It is a tree.

LURI: The replay team has a question.

MIVI: Are roots part of the course?

TARO: Yes.

MIVI: Then both climbers started on the course.

### Act Two

EXT. UPPER BRANCH

[Beko reaches the bell. He touches it. The branch bends away.]

BEKO: The bell is moving.

NALA: You have to follow the branch.

LURI: Beko has nearly reached the finish.

MIVI: The replay shows his hand touched the bell.

TARO: The branch moved before contact.

BEKO: I have not rung it.

NALA: The bell is above you.

BEKO: I can see that.

[The branch swings. Nala climbs past Beko and touches the bell. It rings.]

LURI: Nala wins!

TARO: The branch moved again.

MIVI: The replay shows Nala's hand on the bell.

BEKO: I was holding the branch.

NALA: The branch would have swung away.

TARO: The finish may not be valid.

LURI: Can the bell be removed?

TARO: It is the finish.

NALA: The tree is the course.

MIVI: The bell is part of the tree.

### Qualifying run

EXT. TRAINING TREE

[Nala and Beko practice while Mivi reviews a speed chart.]

MIVI: The course time is twelve seconds.

BEKO: My first climb was eleven.

TARO: The bell rang early.

NALA: The bell didn't ring. The branch did.

LURI: We can use a starting buzzer.

TARO: We have a whistle.

[He blows it. It squeaks.]

BEKO: Is that the start?

TARO: It is the whistle.

NALA: We need a clearer signal.

MIVI: The replay microphone captured the squeak.

LURI: It will be our official sound.

BEKO: Can we practice with the actual bell?

TARO: The bell is not for practice.

[The wind rings the bell.]

NALA: The bell has started.

TARO: The bell is not the start.

MIVI: The replay agrees.

### Camera calibration

INT. REPLAY BOOTH

[Mivi aligns the start and finish marks on a screen.]

MIVI: The camera frame includes the bell and the branch.

TARO: It must include the finish.

LURI: The bell moves outside the frame.

MIVI: I can widen the shot.

TARO: Then the branch looks smaller.

NALA: The branch is the course.

MIVI: I will use two cameras.

[One camera shows the bell; the other shows the climbers.]

### Act Three

EXT. BASE OF TREE

[Taro gathers the competitors. Mivi projects the replay onto a hanging cloth.]

TARO: We need a fair result.

BEKO: Nala touched the bell.

NALA: Beko held the branch.

LURI: The replay shows both of them at the finish.

MIVI: The image is blurred.

TARO: We can use a sensor.

MIVI: The sensor is on the bell.

TARO: It says the bell was touched.

NALA: By me.

BEKO: And the branch was held by me.

LURI: Then we have a shared finish.

TARO: The rules allow one winner.

MIVI: We can change the rules for next year.

TARO: We cannot change them now.

NALA: We can ring the bell together.

BEKO: I will hold the branch.

[They climb together. Beko steadies the limb; Nala rings the bell.]

LURI: We have two finishers.

TARO: The result is a tie.

MIVI: The replay confirms two hands.

TARO: It also confirms the bell was already ringing.

[The bell continues to sway after they climb down.]

### Tag

EXT. COURSE, SUNSET

[The bell swings in the breeze. Taro removes it from the branch.]

LURI: The course is closed.

[The bell rings once in Taro's hand.]
