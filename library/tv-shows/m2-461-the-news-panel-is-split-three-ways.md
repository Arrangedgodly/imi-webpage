# The News Panel Is Split Three Ways

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cable news panels, split-screen arguments, and graphics that assign a position before anyone speaks*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Anchor Meli, a gibbon; analyst Doru, a baboon; analyst Peka, an orangutan; graphics operator Tavi, a macaque; field reporter Suni, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. NEWS STUDIO - LATE AFTERNOON

A screen shows a city fountain with a banner: FOUNTAIN FLOOD? Three boxes appear beside the picture. One says YES, one says NO, one says MAYBE.

MELI: Tonight, a major panel debate about a fountain.

DORU: The fountain is off for maintenance.

PEKA: The sidewalk is dry.

[The graphics screen gives Doru a blue border and Peka a red border. Tavi looks at the control desk.] 

TAVI: The borders are assigned by seating position.

MELI: Can we switch them? The disagreement looks uneven.

SUNI: I am beside the fountain now. It is dry.

[The panel music continues while no one disagrees.] 

ACT ONE

INT. NEWS STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Meli asks for the two analysts to debate whether the fountain being off is newsworthy. Doru says it is a regular maintenance window. Peka says the maintenance crew posted the schedule. The screen splits into three panels: dry fountain, schedule sign, and studio anchor.

MELI: Our viewers are seeing both sides.

SUNI: There are three views, and all show the same update.

[The map replaces the background image, but the YES/NO banner remains as a lower-third. It labels the fountain as a lake. Tavi zooms out; the building outline reveals a small plaza fountain.] 

DORU: That is the location.

PEKA: It is not a lake.

MELI: Can we call it an urban water event?

SUNI: You can call it a fountain.

[The host asks for a live shot. Suni holds up the posted maintenance schedule. The crew uses a split screen with the studio and field; the graphic places Suni under a tag reading OPPOSITION.] 

SUNI: I am reporting, not opposing.

TAVI: The tag came from the previous segment's template.

[Meli looks at the camera and asks Tavi to remove the label. Tavi clears it; the screen goes blank for two seconds, then returns with four new labels.] 

MELI: We have more labels than panelists.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The graphics operator opens the layout editor. Meli follows and puts on a headset so she can continue the live panel from the booth. Doru and Peka remain at the desk and begin discussing whether the schedule is readable from the sidewalk. Suni compares it with the printed map.

DORU: It says the fountain will reopen at six.

PEKA: That is today. It is 5:40.

SUNI: The crew is still behind the barrier. They are testing the pump.

[The panelists point to a small countdown on the schedule. The host asks whether the debate should continue until six. Doru says they can wait. Peka suggests showing the work crew's update.]

MELI: But we promised a heated debate. [She speaks into the headset; the studio audience hears her voice from the panel speakers.]

TAVI: We can heat the studio. We cannot heat the fountain.

[The control room plays an old clip of the fountain spraying. It is from last summer. Suni asks that it be labeled archival. Tavi adds the date and moves it off the live screen.] 

SUNI: The current fountain is still off.

PEKA: The old footage is not evidence about tonight.

[The producer requests a prediction graphic. Doru refuses to predict a scheduled reopening. Tavi creates a plain timeline: maintenance, test, reopen. Meli asks if it can be more suspenseful; Tavi adds a blinking dot that advances as the clock moves.] 

ACT THREE

INT. NEWS STUDIO - 6:00 PM

The live camera returns to the fountain. The crew finishes testing the pump and clears the barrier. The fountain turns on. Suni confirms the water is flowing normally and reads the posted notice. No flooding occurs.

MELI: The two sides have reached a conclusion.

DORU: There were not two sides.

PEKA: There was a maintenance schedule.

[Tavi clears the YES/NO lower-third and the map labels with one CLEAR PANEL command. The studio screen switches to one wide view of the fountain. Meli is still speaking from the control booth through her headset. The host tries to keep the panel discussion open.] 

MELI: Should we continue debating the result?

SUNI: You can sign off.

[The control booth shows the next segment title, which reads WEATHER. Tavi presses the weather preset. The screen displays the forecast without assigning anyone a position.] 

TAVI: Clean transition ready.

MELI: We now move to weather, where clouds are not required to pick a side.

[Meli signs off the discussion. Tavi holds the clean, full-frame fountain view for three seconds before the transition.] 

MELI: One full picture, one update, and no assigned colors.

TAG

Tavi stays in the control booth after the weather handoff, clicks RESET, then loads a new training preset labeled COMEDY SPLIT SCREEN. The studio display divides Doru and Peka down the middle. They step together into the same box, then wave to Suni.

TAVI: New preset tested. The live panel graphics remain cleared.

FINAL IMAGE: The fountain runs in one full-frame picture as the studio panel goes dark.
