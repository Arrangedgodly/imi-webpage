# The Unfinished Lesson

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's educational television programs that turn every mishap into a labeled learning objective*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A cheerful learning show tries to teach young viewers how seeds travel. The host's strict lesson plan collides with an actual troop of curious juveniles who keep moving the props.

Cast: Host Nimo, a gibbon; teacher Pella, a capuchin; young learners Tavi and Jun, macaques; and puppet Acorn, voiced by the host.

## Episode: "Where Did the Seed Go?"

### Cold Open

[BRIGHT STUDIO. A GIANT MAP SHOWS A SEED WITH AN ARROW POINTING NORTH.]

NIMO: Today we learn that seeds travel.

PELLA: Some are carried by wind, water, or animals.

[THE SEED PROP ROLLS OFF THE TABLE AND UNDER A CURTAIN.]

NIMO: Our seed has chosen a route.

### Act One

[NIMO CRAWLS UNDER THE CURTAIN, STILL FACING THE CAMERA.]

NIMO: Can you guess where the seed went?

TAVI: It went under there.

NIMO: Correct. That is one way to travel.

JUN: It rolled.

PELLA: Gravity helped.

NIMO: Our learning objective is wind.

[PELLA SETS A FAN ON THE TABLE. NIMO PUTS PAPER LEAVES IN FRONT OF IT.]

NIMO: When the wind blows, a seed may go far.

[TAVI TURNS THE FAN ON HIGH. THE PAPER LEAVES FLY INTO THE AUDIENCE.]

NIMO: Some seeds travel to new places.

PELLA: These are paper.

JUN: They landed on me.

NIMO: You are the habitat.

### Act Two

[ACORN PUPPET APPEARS FROM A LOG.]

ACORN: I am a seed. How do I travel?

NIMO: You could float in water.

[PELLA SETS THE PUPPET BESIDE A CLEAR BASIN. TAVI DROPS IT IN. IT FLOATS.]

ACORN: I am going to the ocean.

PELLA: The basin is three inches wide.

ACORN: My journey is still important.

[THE BASIN TIPS AS JUN LEANS FORWARD. PELLA CATCHES IT.]

NIMO: We learned to keep water on the table.

PELLA: We learned to hold the basin.

TAVI: What about animals?

NIMO: Animals carry seeds in fur or eat fruit and later drop seeds elsewhere.

[ACORN IS PLACED ON A PATCH OF FAKE FUR. TAVI CARRIES THE FUR ACROSS THE SET.]

ACORN: I am traveling with a very soft animal.

JUN: It is the rug.

ACORN: A rug is an animal that stays still.

PELLA: That is not the lesson.

NIMO: It is a useful question.

[PELLA POINTS TO A ROW OF PICTURE CARDS: WIND, WATER, ANIMAL.]

PELLA: We can match each seed to the way it travels.

[NIMO HOLDS UP A DRY POD. IT BURSTS OPEN AND SCATTERS SEEDS ACROSS THE SET.]

NIMO: Some plants send seeds out when the pod opens.

TAVI: The pod sent them at us.

JUN: That one went in your shoe.

[NIMO TURNS HIS FOOT. A SEED IS STUCK TO HIS TOE.]

NIMO: The lesson has reached the host.

PELLA: Seeds can stick to fur or clothing.

[NIMO PULLS OFF THE SEED. IT BOUNCES INTO THE PICTURE CARD FOR WATER.]

ACORN: I have arrived at the water card.

TAVI: But you did not float.

ACORN: I can be carried.

PELLA: A person carried it. The seed did not choose.

NIMO: We should show the difference between traveling by wind and being carried by a hand.

JUN: You carried the seed.

NIMO: Yes. That is the difference.

[THE PUPPET DISAPPEARS BELOW THE TABLE. NIMO'S ARM REMAINS VISIBLE AS HE REACHES FOR IT.]

ACORN (OFF): I am traveling under the table.

PELLA: That is a route. It is not seed dispersal.

NIMO: We will make a separate chart for studio travel.

[PELLA TAKES THE STUDENTS TO A SMALL GARDEN BOX BESIDE THE SET.]

PELLA: Here is a seed that sprouted after rain.

TAVI: It traveled in the water and stayed here.

NIMO: It traveled, landed, and began growing.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO THE BOX. THE LITTLE MONKEY HAS CARRIED ACORN THERE AND SET THE PUPPET BESIDE THE SPROUT.]

ACORN: I have found a new home.

PELLA: That seed is not yours.

NIMO: We should return the puppet before the real seedling gets stepped on.

### Act Three

[THE CLASS LOOKS FOR THE ORIGINAL SEED PROP. IT IS IN JUN'S HAIR.]

JUN: I carried it.

NIMO: A seed can travel by catching on an animal.

PELLA: Or by sticking to a student who leaned over the table.

[THE FAN SWITCHES ON AGAIN. THE SEED FALLS FROM JUN'S HAIR AND LANDS IN THE BASIN.]

ACORN: I am traveling by water now.

NIMO: Today we learned that one seed can take more than one route.

PELLA: And that we should unplug the fan before cleanup.

[NIMO UNPLUGS THE FAN. THE PUPPET'S LEAFY HAT BLOWS OFF FROM THE AIR CONDITIONING VENT.]

ACORN: I am traveling by vent.

NIMO: We will label that route next week.

[THE CLASS FOLLOWS THE HAT OFFSCREEN.]
