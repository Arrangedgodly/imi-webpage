# The Morning Exercise Show Mirrors the Wrong Side

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fitness broadcasts that prioritize synchronized camera choreography over the instructor's actual demonstration*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Tavi, a macaque; instructor Nala, an orangutan; producer Vero, a capuchin; camera operator Piri, a lemur; guest Luma, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. FITNESS STUDIO - MORNING

Nala demonstrates a simple side stretch. The monitor shows the opposite arm moving.

TAVI: Follow the screen and stretch left.

NALA: I am stretching left.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Piri has flipped the camera image so the audience can mirror the instructor. Vero asks for an arrow graphic to clarify. The arrow points to Nala's other hand. Luma follows the arrow and nearly turns her back to the camera. Nala lowers her arm and asks everyone to reset.

Vero says the show must keep moving. Tavi does the stretch facing the camera, but he turns the wrong way. Nala demonstrates again slowly. Piri switches off the mirrored monitor. The graphic remains, pointing at empty space.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Vero asks Piri to add left and right labels. He overlays two arrows, one labeled LEFT and one labeled ALSO LEFT. Nala asks him to use the instructor's perspective consistently. Piri points to the camera; the audience perspective flips the frame. Luma holds up her own left hand and watches the monitor. It appears on the right side.

LUMA: My arm did not switch sides.

Tavi asks for a countdown to the next stretch. Vero plays a five-second clock. Nala has not finished explaining the movement. The countdown reaches zero and the studio music begins. Tavi starts a squat; Luma is still holding her arm up. The camera cuts to her, then the host, then a close-up of the arrows.

INT. FITNESS STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Nala asks the crew to freeze the graphic. Piri freezes the whole video. Tavi remains mid-squat on the monitor. Vero wants to use the still as the show's new logo. Nala says she is demonstrating a stretch, not a statue. Piri clears the freeze and turns off the arrows.

ACT THREE

INT. FITNESS STUDIO - LATER

Nala stands with her back to the monitor and gives the movement in spoken directions: raise the left arm, lean gently right, then return to center. Luma follows the words. Tavi looks at his own hands instead of the graphic. Piri frames the instructor and guest side by side without mirroring either image. Vero asks whether the segment can still look synchronized. Nala holds the stretch until Luma is ready; they move together at their own pace.

Tavi begins the next count too early. He sees Luma still returning to center and lowers his fingers. Nala finishes the movement; then both performers begin the next one. Vero tries to restart the countdown. Piri reaches over and removes the clock graphic from the monitor. Tavi does not announce a score or a calorie total. He asks Luma how the stretch felt. She says the directions finally matched what her arms were doing.

Vero asks for one last mirrored pose. Nala turns toward the camera and raises her left hand. The monitor shows her left hand on the same side of the frame. Tavi copies the wrong arm out of habit. Luma gently points him to the correct one. He switches, and the three hold the pose without an arrow.


Vero asks if the show can display a calorie score for each pose. Nala says the stretch is not a contest and asks the crew to remove the scoreboard. Piri switches to the wide camera. The old monitor still shows the frozen left-right arrows from rehearsal, so Tavi reaches for the blank screen control. Vero tells him to leave the image live. He turns the monitor toward the wall instead. Luma asks for the spoken directions again. Nala gives them once, with a pause between the lift and the lean. The host follows the same pace as the guest. Vero tries to cue energetic music over the pause; Piri lowers the level so Nala's words remain clear. When the movement ends, Luma nods before Tavi moves on. Before that, Vero asks Piri to turn the monitor around for the viewers at home. Piri swivels it, and the mirrored arrows face the studio crew instead. Tavi reaches toward his right hand, catches the reflection, then waits for Nala's spoken cue. Luma points at the same hand. Vero writes NO SCORE on the run sheet.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SEGMENT

Piri labels the camera feed DIRECT VIEW. Vero removes the left-right arrows from the rundown. Nala rolls up the unused mirrored backdrop.

FINAL IMAGE: Tavi, Nala and Luma stretch in the same direction while the blank monitor reflects their actual arms.
