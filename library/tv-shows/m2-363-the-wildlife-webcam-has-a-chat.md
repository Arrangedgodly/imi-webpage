# The Wildlife Webcam Has a Chat

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live nature streams, where viewers demand constant action from animals on their own schedule*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: When chat viewers complain that a macaque is doing nothing at a salt lick, a nature station's producers chase engagement until the audience becomes the wildlife.

Cast: Pava, a gibbon host; Nema, a macaque field naturalist; Odo, a baboon chat moderator; Kito, a capuchin camera operator; Seli, a lemur viewer calling in.

COLD OPEN

INT. STREAM CONTROL ROOM - DAWN

A monitor shows a sunlit rock. A macaque's tail enters the edge of frame, pauses, and disappears.

PAVA: We have movement.

ODO: The chat says the stream froze.

KITO: The timecode is advancing.

PAVA: Can we advance the animal?

NEMA: That's not a button.

Odo opens a bright poll: "Is nature buffering?" The stream receives its first donation: a viewer paid to make the poll go away.

ACT ONE

Pava settles at the studio desk, facing a live feed of an empty rock.

PAVA: Today's salt-lick watch is presented by RockTime. We recommend not licking your monitor.

NEMA: I recommend not licking rocks that haven't been checked by the field team.

ODO: The chat wants a camera move.

KITO: The tripod is behind a boundary. I can pan from here, not approach.

PAVA: I mean? Our last nature show had a migration. This has a mineral deposit.

PAVA: It is a popular mineral deposit.

Nema points to the chat. A user suggests adding dramatic music. Pava asks Kito to add a drum roll. Kito accidentally plays the sound of a doorbell. The macaque's tail reappears, flicks toward the camera, then withdraws.

ODO: The viewer count doubled.

PAVA: The tail is our lead animal.

NEMA: That is the animal's tail. It does not know it has a role.

ACT TWO

The macaque steps into view, tastes the salt lick, and sits. Pava leans toward the monitor. Odo adds a label: "SALT TASTING: PART ONE." Nema removes it.

PAVA: Could we call this a tasting flight?

NEMA: It has touched one rock.

PAVA: A single-origin tasting.

KITO: I'm holding a wide frame. The animal has room to move.

PAVA: Can we push in for a close-up?

NEMA: The macaque is already visible. Zooming closer will crop out the tree it may climb.

The macaque licks the rock, then scrapes its cheek with a long finger. Odo highlights the gesture with a replay graphic and calls it "the cheek maneuver." Nema swats the control panel before the graphic can add an arrow.

ODO: It was a helpful arrow.

NEMA: It pointed to the macaque's eye.

PAVA: The chat is asking whether the animal is bored.

NEMA: It is chewing. Boredom is your interpretation.

Seli joins a call-in window. She says she has been watching for forty minutes and the animal has been sitting for twenty-nine.

SELI: I waited through a commercial, a weather alert, and your mascot asking me to subscribe. Nothing happened.

PAVA: Did you notice the tail flick?

SELI: I was entering my password.

ACT TWO-B

The macaque stands, looks directly toward the hidden camera, and grooms one arm. Pava tells the control booth to prepare a breaking-news banner. Odo types: "MONKEY MAKES EYE CONTACT." Nema blocks the screen with a field map.

NEMA: It is checking a reflection. The lens is catching the sky.

KITO: I can tilt the camera so it sees less reflection.

PAVA: Keep the eye contact. People are sharing clips.

The macaque leans toward the ground and picks a tick from its fur. Kito widens the shot. Odo adds a red circle around the moment. Seli calls out, through the studio, that the arrow covered the macaque's paw.

SELI: The red circle is larger than the event.

NEMA: Turn off the graphics. This is grooming, not a championship.

PAVA: If we go quiet, the audience may leave.

NEMA: Then let them leave. The animal is not performing for them.

Odo removes the captions, poll and donation ticker. The viewer count drops. Pava stares at the monitor. The macaque climbs the tree and disappears above the frame.

PAVA: It's gone.

NEMA: It climbed. The camera stays where it is.

KITO: We can still hear the leaves moving.

The chat begins typing guesses about which branch the macaque chose. Odo switches off the chat overlay but leaves a small moderation window open. A flood of viewers asks for the camera to pan up. Kito holds the frame.

ACT THREE

The macaque swings across a branch at the edge of the image, pauses to scratch its shoulder, then drops behind the rock. Pava starts to announce the moment, but Nema taps her headset. Pava waits. The macaque walks out of frame.

PAVA: The macaque has left the visible area. We are keeping the camera on the habitat.

SELI: I saw it. It was brief, but I saw it.

NEMA: That's a good time to stop watching if you need to.

Seli logs off voluntarily. Odo removes the live poll and closes the donation panel. A single chat message remains: "I saw the leaf move too." Pava asks Kito whether he can keep the stream running unattended. He nods and removes the host desk microphone from the feed.

PAVA: No host voice. No countdown. Just the habitat.

NEMA: We are done here.

The live view stays on the empty rock. A leaf drops onto it. Kito does not zoom. Odo types "leaf" into the internal log, not the public chat.

TAG

INT. CONTROL ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Pava watches the silent feed. The subscriber counter rolls upward by one.

PAVA: The leaf has an audience.

NEMA: The leaf isn't reading the chat.

FINAL IMAGE: The leaf tumbles off the rock in a gust; the camera holds its wide frame while the control room quietly debates whether wind counts as a guest star.
