# Worth More Than a Walnut

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: antiques appraisal programs that build suspense around provenance and estimated value*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: An antiques expert visits a community hall to appraise a carved walnut bowl. The owner believes it belonged to a royal troop, but the bowl's real history is humbler and more useful.

Cast: Appraiser Sela, a capuchin; owner Tavi, a gibbon; researcher Bo, a macaque; and auctioneer Fenn, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Bowl in the Attic"

### Cold Open

[A BLACK CLOTH IS PULLED FROM A WOODEN BOWL.]

SELA: This may be the rare bowl of King Morrow.

TAVI: It has been used to hold walnuts.

BO: It has a date carved underneath.

[SELA TURNS THE BOWL. THE DATE READS LAST SPRING.]

### Act One

[SELA SETS THE BOWL ON A PADDED TABLE.]

SELA: Where did you find it?

TAVI: In the hall cupboard.

SELA: How long was it there?

TAVI: Since the last potluck.

FENN: The provenance is compelling.

BO: It has a label: "Return to kitchen."

[SELA LIFTS THE BOWL TO EXAMINE THE CARVING.]

SELA: There is a crown shape here.

TAVI: That is a walnut stain.

SELA: A royal stain?

BO: A walnut stain.

### Act Two

[BO LOOKS THROUGH THE HALL RECORDS.]

BO: The bowl was made by the craft group.

TAVI: I remember them carving it.

SELA: Then it is not an antique.

FENN: What is the value?

SELA: As an antique, very little. As a sturdy serving bowl, perhaps twelve walnuts.

TAVI: It held twenty.

[THE BOWL IS PUT ON A SCALE. IT TIPS, SPILLING WALNUTS.]

SELA: The market is volatile.

FENN: We can auction it.

TAVI: The hall needs it for the next potluck.

SELA: That is a different kind of value.

[THE CREW PLACES A CARD BESIDE THE BOWL: "ESTIMATED VALUE: 12 WALNUTS."]

[SELA EXAMINES THE INSIDE OF THE BOWL WITH A SMALL LIGHT.]

SELA: The grain is even. Whoever made it knew how to sand the surface.

TAVI: The craft group used a shared tool.

BO: The tool checkout book is still in the cupboard.

FENN: Can the book prove the bowl's history?

BO: It can prove who borrowed the carving knife.

[BO FINDS A SIGNATURE: "TAVI, TWO HOURS."]

TAVI: I made the first cut.

SELA: That gives the bowl a maker, not a monarch.

FENN: The appraisal is losing its mystery.

SELA: Its usefulness is increasing.

[THE CREW FILMS A CLOSE-UP OF THE CARVING. IT LOOKS LIKE A CROWN FROM ONE ANGLE AND A WALNUT FROM THE OTHER.]

SELA: The mark may have been intended as decoration.

TAVI: We were making bowls, not royal objects.

BO: Did anyone ask for a crown?

TAVI: The hall's old sign had one.

[FENN LOOKS AT THE SIGN. IT IS A DRAWING OF A WALNUT.]

FENN: It is difficult to appraise a symbol.

SELA: It is easy to appraise a bowl that holds food.

[THE CREW SETS UP A REVEAL SHOT WITH THE BOWL UNDER A CLOTH.]

FENN: We are ready to announce the value.

TAVI: You already did.

FENN: The audience has not heard it.

[SELA LIFTS THE CLOTH. THE SIGN STILL READS "TWELVE WALNUTS."]

SELA: This bowl is worth more in the kitchen than at auction.

BO: The next potluck needs it tomorrow.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS ON THE SIGN. A WALNUT ROLLS OFF THE TABLE.]

TAVI: We should raise the estimate.

SELA: The walnut is not included.

[BO PUTS THE WALNUT BACK IN THE BOWL.]

[TAVI TAKES THE BOWL BACK TO THE KITCHEN. THE CAMERA STAYS ON THE EMPTY PAD.]

SELA: The appraisal is complete.

FENN: The bowl has returned to service.

BO: That is the highest valuation we can give it.

SELA: The bowl will continue to serve the hall.

[THE BOWL IS SET ON THE KITCHEN SHELF BESIDE THE WALNUTS.]

BO: That is where it belongs.

### Act Three

[THE APPRAISAL IS FILMED AGAINST A BACKDROP OF THE HALL.]

SELA: We cannot verify a royal connection. The bowl was made locally last spring.

TAVI: It has held soup, fruit, and a lost mitten.

BO: The mitten is still inside.

[BO PULLS OUT A MITTEN.]

FENN: The bowl has a diverse portfolio.

SELA: It is worth keeping.

TAVI: It stays in the kitchen.

[THE CREW PACKS UP. FENN TRIES TO CARRY THE BOWL, BUT TAVI FILLS IT WITH WALNUTS.]

FENN: It is heavier now.

TAVI: It has gained value.

SELA: Do not appraise the nuts.

[FENN SETS IT DOWN. A WALNUT ROLLS UNDER THE TABLE.]

BO: We have found a rare loose walnut.

SELA: Estimated value: one walnut.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON THE CARD. A SMALL MONKEY TAKES THE WALNUT AND EATS IT.]
