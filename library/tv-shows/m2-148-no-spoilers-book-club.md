# No Spoilers Before the Book Club

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised book clubs that praise every selection as life-changing, reveal the ending, and let the host's summary replace discussion*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Lera wants a dramatic book-club episode about a mystery novel. Librarian Boro wants to discuss what readers noticed without spoiling the ending for people who have not finished.

Cast: Lera, a macaque host; Boro, a gorilla librarian; Nimi, a lemur reader; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "The Last Page Stays Closed"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. LERA HOLDS UP A CLOSED BOOK.]

LERA: This book changed everything.

BORO: It is a mystery novel.

[PUCK SHOWS A TITLE CARD: "THE BUTLER IS THE BROTHER."]

NIMI: I have not finished it.

[PUCK QUICKLY COVERS THE CARD WITH A BOOK JACKET.]

LERA: We will discuss the ending after the break.

BORO: We can discuss the first three chapters now.

### Act One

[THE PANEL SITS AROUND A TABLE. NIMI HOLDS HER PLACE WITH A PAPER BOOKMARK.]

LERA: What did you think of the detective?

NIMI: The detective keeps asking the same question.

BORO: That may be a clue.

PUCK: Can we show the final page while they talk?

BORO: No. The page contains the ending.

[PUCK PUTS THE BOOK UNDER THE TABLE. THE CAMERA STILL SHOWS IT IN A REFLECTION.]

NIMI: I can see the title.

LERA: What if we turn it around?

[PUCK TURNS THE BOOK OVER. THE BACK COVER HAS A PLOT SUMMARY.]

BORO: That gives away the first half.

[THEY SET THE BOOK FACE DOWN. LERA ASKS NIMI WHAT SHE EXPECTS NEXT.]

LERA: What makes the detective suspicious?

NIMI: The coat was dry after the storm.

BORO: That is a useful observation.

[PUCK WRITES "DRY COAT" ON A BOARD, THEN ADDS A QUESTION MARK.]

LERA: Can we call that a clue?

BORO: We can ask why it matters.

[NIMI SAYS THE DETECTIVE SHOULD CHECK WHERE THE COAT WAS HUNG. LERA DOES NOT SAY WHETHER SHE IS RIGHT.]

NIMI: The detective will check the greenhouse.

LERA: That is where the brother is hiding.

NIMI: I was guessing.

### Act Two

[THE SHOW RETURNS FROM BREAK. A GUEST READER, ODO, JOINS BY VIDEO.]

LERA: Did you guess the ending?

ODO: I guessed the gardener.

BORO: That is not the ending.

[PUCK PLAYS A PRE-RECORDED CLIP OF A GUEST SAYING, "I KNEW IT."]

NIMI: That clip is from another book.

PUCK: It tested well.

[BORO OPENS HIS COPY TO CHAPTER FOUR. HE READS A SHORT PASSAGE WITHOUT REVEALING THE PLOT.]

ODO: I noticed the detective asks the same question at the end of each chapter.

BORO: The order changes.

NIMI: Could the order matter?

LERA: We can compare the first and fourth questions without reading the answer.

[THEY WRITE BOTH QUESTIONS ON CARDS. THE WORDS MATCH; THE PUNCTUATION DOES NOT.]

BORO: Notice how the detective asks the same question but changes the order.

NIMI: I missed that.

LERA: Could you summarize the rest?

BORO: Not without spoiling it.

[PUCK PUTS UP A COUNTDOWN: "THREE MINUTES UNTIL THE REVEAL." BORO ERASES IT.]

BORO: The discussion is the reveal.

[NIMI SAYS SHE WANTS TO FINISH THE BOOK BEFORE HEARING MORE. LERA CLOSES HER COPY.]

LERA: We can wait.

### Act Three

[THE PANEL TAKES QUESTIONS FROM VIEWERS. ONE ASKS IF THE BOOK IS WORTH READING.]

NIMI: I am not finished, but I like the clues.

BORO: I liked the pacing.

LERA: I liked the cover.

NIMI: I like that we can talk about the clues without knowing the ending.

BORO: The next meeting can include everyone who finishes.

[PUCK MOVES THE ENDING CARD OUT OF THE CAMERA FRAME.]

PUCK: The cover artist is joining us after the segment.

[BORO ASK HOW THEY WILL KNOW WHEN THE BOOK IS FINISHED.]

BORO: You reach the last page.

NIMI: Without skipping to it.

[PUCK REMOVES THE ENDING CARD FROM THE MONITOR. LERA TURNS THE BOOK FACE UP AND OPENS TO THE FIRST CHAPTER.]

LERA: Next week, we will discuss the whole book after everyone has had time to read it.

[LERA OPENS HER BOOK TO THE PAGE SHE MARKED. THE BOOKMARK IS STILL THERE.]

LERA: I can pick up where I stopped.

PUCK: Should I put that in the recap?

BORO: There is no recap next week.

### Tag

[PUCK HOLDS UP A PAPERBACK. ITS LAST PAGE IS STUCK TO THE BACK COVER.]

NIMI: That is a damaged copy.

BORO: It needs a new binding, not a plot twist.

[PUCK SETS IT BESIDE THE REPAIR CART.]

BORO: We can replace the binding before the next club meeting.

NIMI: Will the repaired copy keep the same ending?

BORO: The pages stay in order.

[PUCK LABELS THE COPY "REPAIR" AND PUTS IT ON THE CART.]
