# Glue It Before the Credits

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: public-access craft television, where a host keeps adding decorative steps to a simple paper lantern*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Craft host Piri tries to make a paper lantern before the program ends, while the crew adds ribbons, stamps, and a surprise tassel.

Cast: Host Piri, capuchin; co-host Odo, baboon; guest maker Nara, macaque; camera helper Seli, gibbon.

## Episode: "One Sheet, One Lantern"

### Cold Open

[CRAFT TABLE. PAPER, GLUE, RIBBON.]

PIRI: We will make a lantern in ten minutes.

NARA: The glue needs time to dry.

ODO: We have eight minutes.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PIRI HOLDS UP A RECTANGLE OF PAPER.]

Piri demonstrates folding the paper into strips. Odo asks if the lantern can be personalized. Nara says it can, but the cuts must stay attached at the edge.

ODO: I can add a star.

NARA: After folding.

Seli asks for a close-up. Piri holds the paper too near the lens, and the pattern disappears.

PIRI: The camera is too close.

SELI: The detail is important.

NARA: We need to cut evenly.

Odo cuts through the edge. The paper separates into strips.

ODO: The lantern is now a tassel.

Piri says they can start again. The clock reads seven minutes.

### Act Two

[THE CREW TRIES A SECOND SHEET.]

Nara folds carefully and marks the stopping line. Odo adds a ribbon before the cuts are made. The ribbon gets caught in the scissors.

PIRI: We have a ribbon in the cutting path.

ODO: It was for decoration.

NARA: It is now part of the problem.

Seli asks for a segment break. Piri says the audience can follow the steps if they stay on screen. The camera shows the fold, the line, and the cuts. The lantern begins to curl when opened.

NARA: It needs a firm base.

ODO: We can use a cup.

PIRI: The show is about paper.

NARA: The lantern needs support.

Piri places the paper cylinder over a cup. It stands upright.

[PIRI CHECKS THE TIME.]

The paper lantern still needs to dry. Odo asks if they can speed it up with the fan. The fan blows the handle sideways, and the lantern spins on the cup.

ODO: It is rotating.

NARA: It is not stable.

Seli moves the fan farther away. Piri asks for a decorative stripe. Nara says paint will soak through the paper. Odo offers a marker. It bleeds into a dark patch.

PIRI: The design is spreading.

NARA: The paper is thin.

Odo suggests putting a second sheet inside. Nara says it will block the light. Piri cuts a narrow strip from spare paper and tests it around the base. It stiffens the cylinder without covering the opening.

SELI: We have reinforced the lantern.

ODO: Can we add ribbon now?

NARA: One ribbon, away from the wet glue.

Odo ties the ribbon to the handle. It droops over the side. Seli asks for a close shot, but the camera cannot see through the decoration.

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK SHOWS TWO MINUTES.]

Odo adds a paper handle. The glue is still wet. Piri asks the camera to hold the finished shot while Nara steadies the handle.

SELI: The credits are ready.

PIRI: We are not finished.

NARA: The lantern works.

ODO: It needs a tassel.

PIRI: We have no time.

Odo offers the first torn sheet. Nara attaches it as a fringe. It droops against the wet glue.

PIRI: That is not a tassel.

NARA: It is a strip of paper.

Seli turns off the overhead light. The lantern casts a shadow, but there is no candle inside.

[THE CAMERA FINALLY GETS A CLEAR VIEW.]

Nara asks Odo to remove the loose ribbon. He unties it, but the wet knot sticks to his fingers. Seli moistens it with a drop of water; the glue softens and releases.

ODO: The decoration has been removed.

PIRI: We have saved the lantern.

NARA: The handle still needs to dry.

Piri holds it above the cup while the glue sets. The lantern leans to one side. Odo props it with two pencils.

SELI: The studio has become a drying rack.

NARA: The lantern needs support until the glue holds.

The credits begin to roll in the preview monitor. Piri asks whether the finished craft can be carried outside. Nara says not until the handle dries. Odo announces a "lantern launch" for the next episode.

PIRI: It is not ready to launch.

ODO: It will be ready for the credits.

### Tag

[THE CREW HOLDS THE LANTERN UP.]

PIRI: We have made a paper lantern.

ODO: And a tassel.

NARA: The glue is still wet.

The handle slips. Everyone catches the lantern.

[SFX: PAPER RUSTLES, CREDITS MUSIC.]
