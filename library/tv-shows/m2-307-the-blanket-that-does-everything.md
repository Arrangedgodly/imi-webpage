# The Blanket That Does Everything

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-shopping television, enthusiastic demonstrations, countdown offers, and products claimed to solve problems they were never made for*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A home-shopping host tries to sell one ordinary blanket as a multipurpose invention, while the floor crew keeps using it for the job it was actually designed to do.

Cast: Host Palla, a gibbon; co-host Odo, an orangutan; product tester Rell, a macaque; caller Vex, a capuchin; and floor director Neri, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "One Blanket Left"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A FOLDED BLANKET ON A PEDESTAL.]

PALLA: This blanket keeps you warm, hides a chair, and provides a dramatic entrance.

ODO: It is a blanket.

Neri pulls it off the pedestal and wraps it around a cold camera operator.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[CAMERA ONE. PALLA FACES VIEWERS.]

Palla announces the Miracle Fold Blanket, available for one hour at a special price. Odo demonstrates how it folds around a cushion. Rell tries it as a cape, but the fabric drags on the floor and catches his heel.

PALLA: It is wearable.

RELL: It is still attached to the chair.

Odo shows that it can be used as a picnic cloth. Neri places a prop basket on it. The blanket slides off the table, taking the basket with it. The fruit rolls across the set.

ODO: It can also move a picnic.

Palla introduces a third use: a room divider. They hold it between two chairs. The blanket is too short. Rell stands behind it and can still be seen from the waist up.

PALLA: It creates privacy at eye level.

RELL: My feet are visible.

Palla calls the product hotline. Vex phones in from home and asks whether the blanket can cover a drafty window. Odo says yes, if it is clipped in place. Neri clips it to the studio window, where the breeze stops.

### Act Two

[DEMONSTRATION TABLE. A FAN BLOWS.]

The crew tests the blanket against a fan. It billows like a sail. Palla says it is useful for indoor sailing. Odo explains that the studio fan is at maximum speed. Rell tries to hold the blanket down and is pulled across the stage.

PALLA: It is lightweight.

RELL: It is towing me.

Odo tries the blanket as a picnic canopy. He clips two corners to the display stand and stretches the other end across a chair. It creates shade for the product table, but the fan starts again and the blanket lifts like a sail. Neri stands on one corner to hold it down.

PALLA: It creates a shaded outdoor room.

NERI: We are indoors.

The fan stops. The blanket drops over the product display again. Odo suggests testing it as a cushion cover instead. Rell wraps the pedestal, which immediately looks like a gift.

RELL: It is now a present.

PALLA: The blanket is the present.

Neri unties the corners and returns the fabric to its original shape. The camera operator asks for a break; the studio is cold under the air vent. Palla offers the blanket without making a sales pitch. The operator accepts it and warms her hands.

Neri turns off the fan. The blanket falls over the display camera. The broadcast loses its picture. Palla continues speaking, assuming viewers can still see the demonstration.

PALLA: As you can see, the fabric is soft.

ODO: They cannot see.

Palla asks Neri to remove it. The blanket is wrapped around the tripod. The crew carefully frees the camera. The fabric remains intact, but the tripod is slightly crooked.

Vex calls again. He asks if the blanket can be used for warmth. Odo says that is its main purpose. Palla repeats the price. Neri points out the blanket is now the only warm object in the studio. The camera operator is still wearing it.

### Act Three

[STUDIO. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK READS 00:30.]

Palla announces the last chance to order. A second blanket is brought out. The floor crew uses it to carry the rolling fruit back to the table. It works. Rell tries to lift the basket by its handles; the blanket keeps the fruit from spilling.

RELL: That is a useful demonstration.

PALLA: It is an unplanned demonstration.

Odo shows the blanket's stitched edge and care label. The label says "wash cold, dry flat." Palla turns it to camera. The teleprompter asks her to call it a "care instruction experience."

PALLA: It can be washed cold and dried flat.

The offer timer reaches zero. Vex has ordered one blanket. He says he wants it for the couch, not for sailing. Palla confirms the order. The studio crew folds the demonstration blanket and returns it to the pedestal.

### Tag

[STUDIO AFTER BROADCAST.]

Neri drapes the blanket over the camera operator's shoulders. Odo sits down with the product card. Palla asks if they should make another use list.

RELL: It is a blanket. We can sit under it.

PALLA: That is a strong customer testimonial.

The camera operator nods, warm at last.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
