# The Case of the Silent Bell

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedural dramas, forensic clue montages, and escalating suspect interviews*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: Detective Nara investigates the disappearance of the bell clapper from the town's warning bell, while every witness insists the silent bell is still working.

Cast: Detective Nara, a macaque; forensic tech Sovo, a lemur; Sergeant Hobb, a baboon; witness Eki, a capuchin; and bell keeper Duma, a gorilla.

## Episode: "No Ring, No Warning"

### Cold open

EXT. TOWN SQUARE, DAWN

[The warning bell hangs over the square. Duma pulls its rope. The bell makes no sound.]

DUMA: It rang yesterday.

HOBB: It is silent today.

EKI: I heard it last night.

NARA: What time?

EKI: When I was asleep.

NARA: Then how did you hear it?

EKI: I woke up after.

[The bell sways. A small wooden clapper is missing.]

NARA: Nobody leaves the square.

SOVO: The square has four exits.

HOBB: Then nobody leaves through the exits.

### Act One

INT. TOWN HALL, MORNING

[Nara stands before a board with photographs of the bell, rope, and three suspect footprints.]

NARA: We have a missing clapper, one rope, and tracks in the dust.

HOBB: The footprints lead to the banana stand.

NARA: That is not a clue.

SOVO: The print has five toes.

NARA: Most of us do.

SOVO: It is a very common suspect.

HOBB: I interviewed Eki.

NARA: What did you learn?

HOBB: Eki heard the bell last night.

NARA: We knew that.

HOBB: Eki was asleep.

NARA: We knew that too.

HOBB: The interview was complete.

[Sovo presents a fiber sample.]

SOVO: I found a blue thread on the bell rope.

NARA: Who wears blue?

HOBB: The entire town wears blue on market day.

NARA: Today is market day.

HOBB: The thread is from my uniform.

NARA: Why were you at the bell?

HOBB: I was ringing it.

NARA: Did it ring?

HOBB: No.

NARA: Then we have a witness and a motive.

HOBB: I have no motive.

NARA: That is exactly what a motive would say.

### Act Two

INT. EVIDENCE ROOM

[Sovo lays out three objects: a seed pod, a small wooden peg, and a chalk mark.]

SOVO: The clapper may have been removed with a tool.

NARA: This peg fits the bell's hinge.

HOBB: That is not the clapper.

SOVO: It was near the bell.

NARA: Everything in the square is near the bell.

SOVO: We have to start somewhere.

[Nara examines the peg. It has a strip of blue cloth wrapped around it.]

NARA: This thread is from Hobb's sleeve.

HOBB: I leaned on the bell rope.

NARA: You said you pulled the rope.

HOBB: I did both.

NARA: Sergeant, why did you take the clapper?

HOBB: I didn't.

SOVO: The peg was used to hold a sign.

NARA: What sign?

[Hobb unfolds a sign: "BELL UNDER REPAIR."]

HOBB: The clapper was loose. I took it off so it would not fall.

NARA: Where is it?

HOBB: I put it in the repair box.

SOVO: The box is empty.

HOBB: It was there yesterday.

NARA: Who opened the box?

HOBB: The bell keeper.

DUMA: I took the clapper home to polish it.

NARA: Without telling anyone?

DUMA: The bell was under repair.

NARA: The sign says that.

DUMA: I put the sign there.

SOVO: The clapper is at your home?

DUMA: It is in a jar by the door.

HOBB: Why?

DUMA: It was dusty.

NARA: You brought a bell clapper home in a jar.

DUMA: It is safer than a pocket.

### Act Three

INT. TOWN HALL

[Duma brings in a jar. The clapper inside is clean. Nara places it on the evidence table.]

NARA: The bell was silent because the clapper was being polished.

DUMA: It is shiny now.

HOBB: The warning system has been out for a day.

DUMA: The town has a loudspeaker.

SOVO: The loudspeaker is also quiet.

NARA: Why?

HOBB: I unplugged it to move the bell rope.

NARA: The warning system was entirely silent because every part was being repaired.

DUMA: We can put it back.

NARA: We'll test the bell before reopening the square.

[Hobb attaches the clapper. Duma pulls the rope. The bell rings once, then the clapper falls into the jar.]

SOVO: It is still loose.

DUMA: It needs a smaller pin.

NARA: Do we have one?

HOBB: The seed pod might fit.

SOVO: The seed pod was evidence.

NARA: It can be evidence and a temporary pin.

[Hobb fits the pod. Duma rings the bell. It rings clearly.]

EKI: I heard that.

NARA: You are awake now.

EKI: I can confirm it.

HOBB: Case closed.

NARA: The bell is fixed. The case is closed.

### Tag

EXT. TOWN SQUARE, AFTERNOON

[The bell rings. Eki wakes from a nap on a nearby bench.]

EKI: I heard it last night.

NARA: It was this afternoon.

[The bell rings again as the seed pod spins on the rope.]
