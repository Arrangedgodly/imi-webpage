# The Clapper Under the Threshold

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television detective procedurals, suspect interviews, evidence walls, and last-minute laboratory results*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A detective investigates a museum bell that failed to ring during opening, only to find that the missing sound is still hanging on the wall.

Cast: Detective Roka, a baboon; Detective Meli, a macaque; curator Tavi, an orangutan; guard Pavo, a gibbon; conservator Eri, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. CITY MUSEUM - MORNING

The front door opens. The museum's brass bell hangs silent above it. Tavi points to the bell. Roka pulls the cord. Nothing.

ROKA: The bell is missing its voice.

MELI: The bell is still here.

PAVO: I heard the door open.

ACT ONE

Tavi explains that the bell rang every morning until yesterday. The museum opens in an hour. Roka asks who last heard it. Pavo says he was on the afternoon shift. Eri polished the bell. Tavi locked the doors. Meli examines the pull cord and notices a clean strip through the dust.

MELI: Someone touched it.

TAVI: We pull it every day.

ROKA: We have a routine disturbance.

Meli interviews Pavo in the guard booth. He says he heard a faint click, not a ring. Roka draws three suspects on a whiteboard: curator, guard, conservator. Meli points out all three are employees.

ROKA: The museum is small.

ACT TWO

Eri says the bell's clapper was loose, so she removed it for cleaning and placed it on a padded tray. The tray is empty. Roka asks whether she took it home. Eri says no. Tavi says the museum's only other brass object is an old compass. Roka pins a photo of it on the evidence wall.

MELI: Why is the compass on the wall?

ROKA: It was in the room.

Tavi opens the exhibit case. The compass is there. Pavo replays a security clip. It shows Eri entering with the clapper, Tavi carrying a stack of brochures, and Pavo opening the front door. The footage is grainy. Roka asks the lab for enhancement.

MELI: The clip is from yesterday's opening.

ROKA: We need an establishing shot.

The monitor enlarges a blurry frame until the people disappear. Meli asks Pavo to open the door again. It swings, and the bell stays quiet. She watches the cord. It is tied in a loop around the handle.

Roka lays out the clapper tray, the security still, and the looped cord. He asks Eri when she last saw the clapper. She says after polishing it, before the museum closed. Tavi remembers moving the brochures across the threshold while the door was open.

ROKA: Did you hear anything fall?

TAVI: I heard brochures hit the floor.

MELI: That is a different sound.

Roka replays the guard-booth audio. A soft metal click follows the brochure rustle. Pavo says it could be the door latch. Meli asks him to open the door slowly while she listens beside the threshold. The latch clicks first; a second, softer click follows as the door settles.

PAVO: Two clicks.

ROKA: One is the latch. What is the other?

Meli looks down at the narrow channel under the door and points to the dust line that stops halfway across.

ACT TWO-B

Roka calls Eri back to the gallery. She brings the empty padded tray. Meli says the clapper was removed, but no one reported it missing because the tray was left in the restoration room. Eri says she returned the clapper to the bell but did not reattach the retaining pin. The clapper slid off when the door swung and is likely in the wooden threshold channel.

ROKA: Is this a theft?

ERI: It is a loose part.

PAVO: I can check the threshold.

MELI: Stop. The door is still swinging.

Pavo holds the door open. Tavi crouches and sees a brass edge beneath the channel. Eri removes the threshold cover with a small tool and lifts out the clapper. It is intact. She fits it back inside the bell and secures the pin. Roka asks for a dramatic test.

TAVI: We should test it once, then open normally.

ACT THREE

Meli pulls the cord. The bell rings clearly. The detectives drop the suspect board. Roka erases the compass photograph. Pavo resets the security camera and holds the door while Tavi checks the pin. Eri puts the used tray in the restoration room with a label reading "EMPTY - RETURNED."

ROKA: The sound has been restored.

MELI: The clapper was under the door.

ROKA: The case is closed.

TAVI: The museum is open.

Visitors enter. The bell rings once for each person, then the cord catches on a visitor's hat brim. Tavi frees it without disturbing the bell.

TAG

Roka looks at the evidence wall. The compass photo remains pinned crooked.

ROKA: We need a new case.

MELI: We need a level.

FINAL IMAGE: Eri hangs the brass clapper's empty tray on the restoration room wall while the museum bell rings normally behind her.
