# The Studio Mat Keeps Folding

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: morning fitness television, cheerful countdowns, camera-friendly routines, and hosts who keep adding one more move*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A live exercise host tries to teach a simple balance routine, but the studio mat keeps folding under the camera crew's marks.

Cast: Trainer Nala, a macaque; host Pavo, a gibbon; guest Reka, an orangutan; camera operator Tavi, a capuchin; floor producer Omi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. FITNESS STUDIO - MORNING

Nala stands on a mat. Pavo begins the countdown.

PAVO: Five, four, three, two, one.

The mat folds in half beneath the tripod. Tavi grabs the camera.

NALA: We are starting on the floor.

ACT ONE

Omi tapes down the mat's corners. Nala demonstrates a seated shoulder stretch. Pavo mirrors her and swings his tail into the microphone stand. Tavi moves the stand beyond the mat. Omi says the show has thirty seconds left in the warm-up.

PAVO: We have just begun.

OMI: The clock counts the introduction.

Reka joins them for a balance routine. She stands with feet apart and raises one arm. Pavo asks if the camera can show her face. Tavi pans upward; the viewers can no longer see her feet. Nala asks for a wider shot. Tavi backs up, and the tripod lands partly on the mat.

ACT TWO

The mat folds at the edge. Reka steps off and moves the tripod onto the studio floor. Omi asks them to reset the camera marks. Nala says the mat is too small for three people and a tripod. Pavo asks if they can add a "power pose" while the crew finds another mat.

NALA: We can wait.

PAVO: We have a live segment.

REKA: We can use the floor. It is level.

Omi checks the surface, clears a loose cable and has the crew move the mat off the exercise area. Nala leads a seated breathing sequence. Pavo looks at the countdown clock and tries to speed it up by counting every second twice.

PAVO: One, one, two, two.

NALA: That is not how time works.

Tavi frames all three participants without the mat. The camera view includes their full bodies and the marked cable-free space. Reka adjusts her stance so her tail does not sweep the equipment. Omi tells the crew to keep the original wide shot.

ACT TWO-B

Nala demonstrates a simple standing balance: feet apart, one heel raised, a hand on a chair if needed. Reka raises one heel and holds steady. Pavo does the same, but the chair rolls away. He grabs its back before it reaches the camera.

PAVO: I have found the optional support.

REKA: The chair needs a brake.

Omi locks the chair wheels. Nala invites viewers to stop if uncomfortable and choose the seated version. Pavo tries to add a second balance pose. Nala asks him to keep the routine to one move. He lowers his heel and waits.

PAVO: I am holding the pose.

NALA: You are standing still.

REKA: The camera can stay wide.

Tavi gives a thumbs-up from behind the lens. The mat remains folded by the wall and the cable remains outside the clear floor. The crew performs the standing version once, then the seated version. No one loses balance. Pavo's tail curls around the chair leg, keeping it from rolling.

Omi holds up a new cue card: "ONE MORE MOVE." Pavo starts to lift both arms. Nala looks at the clock; only ten seconds remain. Reka asks whether the camera can show the chair brake before the close. Tavi pans down. The close-up fills the screen with one locked wheel.

PAVO: Is the wheel part of the routine?

NALA: It is part of keeping the chair still.

Omi asks for the group shot. The camera has zoomed in too far. Tavi widens the frame and reveals Pavo still holding the pose. Nala counts him down once. He lowers his arm and stands with both feet on the floor.

REKA: That is a good ending.

PAVO: I was ready for another one.

ACT THREE

Nala closes the routine and thanks Reka for showing the chair-supported version. Pavo asks whether they have time for one more stretch. Omi shakes her head and points to the closing clock. Pavo accepts the cue and lowers his arms.

PAVO: That is our morning movement.

NALA: And we stopped on time.

Reka rolls the locked chair to the storage wall. Tavi folds the mat and sets it upright, away from the camera path. Omi checks that the cable is clear before the next crew enters.

TAG

Pavo turns to leave and starts another countdown. He catches himself on "three," smiles at Nala, and walks off without beginning a new routine.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty studio floor stays clear as the credits roll, while the folded mat leans securely against the wall.
