# Inside the Bag Factory

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: factory-tour programs that narrate ordinary production as high-stakes innovation and keep interrupting workers for host reactions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Sari wants a dramatic tour of a reusable-bag workshop. Floor lead Beko wants the crew to stay behind safety lines and let workers finish an order before the pickup.

Cast: Sari, a macaque host; Beko, a gorilla floor lead; Lemi, a lemur stitcher; and Puck, a capuchin camera operator.

## Episode: "One Seam at a Time"

### Cold Open

[WORKSHOP FLOOR. LEMI GUIDES CLOTH UNDER A SEWING MACHINE. SARI WEARS A SAFETY VEST.]

SARI: We are witnessing a precision operation.

BEKO: She is sewing a seam.

[SARI LEANS TOWARD THE MACHINE. BEKO POINTS TO THE YELLOW LINE.]

BEKO: Visitors stay behind the line.

SARI: I am getting close to the craft.

LEMI: Please do not stand over my elbow.

[PUCK ZOOMS IN FROM BEHIND THE LINE. THE CAMERA SHOWS A SEAM, A NEEDLE, AND THE EDGE OF BEKO'S BOOT.]

SARI: The work is happening around us.

### Act One

[BEKO SHOWS THE CREW A STACK OF CUT CLOTH. THE ORDER SHEET LISTS FIFTY BAGS.]

SARI: Each bag begins as a blank canvas.

LEMI: It begins as a rectangle.

[LEMI FOLDS THE CLOTH AND MARKS THE HANDLE.]

SARI: And this is where the transformation starts.

BEKO: The cutting pattern is on the template.

[PUCK ASKS TO FILM THE SCISSORS FROM ABOVE. BEKO MOVES THE TEMPLATE AWAY FROM THE EDGE.]

SARI: Is this a design decision?

BEKO: It keeps the pattern aligned.

LEMI: If we cut the handles at different angles, the bags will not match.

[SARI PICKS UP A SCRAP OF CLOTH. BEKO SHOWS HER THE MARKED BOUNDARY FOR MATERIALS.]

BEKO: Scraps go in the bin, not on the floor.

[SARI PUTS THE SCRAP IN THE BIN. PUCK FILMS THE LABEL INSTEAD OF THE SCISSORS.]

BEKO: Keep the camera stand behind the tape.

[PUCK BACKS UP. THE STAND CATCHES A CART HANDLE.]

PUCK: The cart moved.

LEMI: The cart was parked.

[BEKO ROLLS THE CART TO THE SHIPPING AREA. THE CREW WAITS.]

SARI: An unexpected detour in our process.

BEKO: We had to move a cart.

### Act Two

[LEMI STITCHES THE HANDLES. SARI WATCHES FROM THE SAFE SIDE.]

SARI: How do you know the handle will hold?

LEMI: We test one sample before the run.

[BEKO PULLS ON A TEST BAG. THE SEAM HOLDS. SARI ASKS FOR A MORE DRAMATIC TEST.]

SARI: Could we put something heavier in it?

BEKO: The test is already done.

[PUCK BRINGS A BOX OF METAL PARTS. BEKO STOPS HIM.]

BEKO: That would damage the sample.

SARI: What if it held?

LEMI: Then we would have to make a new sample.

[THE CREW FILMS THE WORKERS CHECKING EACH HANDLE AND COUNTING COMPLETED BAGS. THE PICKUP TRUCK ARRIVES EARLY.]

SARI: Can the driver wait for a dramatic finish?

BEKO: The driver needs a loading estimate.

LEMI: Ten minutes for the final twelve bags.

[BEKO GIVES THE DRIVER THE ESTIMATE. PUCK CUES A COUNTDOWN GRAPHIC. SARI TURNS IT OFF WHEN A STITCHER NEEDS TO REPLACE A NEEDLE.]

BEKO: We have thirty-eight. The truck can wait ten minutes.

SARI: A countdown.

BEKO: A pickup time.

[LEMI CONTINUES SEWING. SARI ASKS HER HOW SHE FEELS ABOUT THE DEADLINE.]

LEMI: I want the last row stitched straight.

[PUCK FILMS THE ROW. ONE BAG HAS A CROOKED HANDLE. LEMI FIXES IT BEFORE ADDING IT TO THE STACK.]

### Act Three

[THE LAST BAG IS FINISHED. BEKO COUNTS THE ORDER. THERE ARE FIFTY.]

PUCK: The camera missed the final count.

BEKO: We can show the tally sheet.

SARI: No need to recount the bags?

LEMI: We have a written count and the labeled cartons.

[BEKO SIGNS THE PICKUP FORM. THE DRIVER LOADS THE LAST BOX.]

SARI: The team has completed an incredible run.

LEMI: We finished the order.

BEKO: The bags go to the school library.

[SARI PICKS UP ONE BAG AND OPENS IT. SHE LOOKS INSIDE.]

SARI: What is the secret feature?

LEMI: It holds books.

[THE WORKERS LOAD THE BAGS INTO THE TRUCK. PUCK FILMS FROM BEHIND THE SAFETY LINE.]

SARI: Is there a final inspection?

BEKO: We checked the count and seams.

LEMI: The school asked for no tags inside the bags.

[THE WORKERS CHECK FOR TAGS BEFORE CLOSING THE CARTONS. ONE TAG IS REMOVED AND RECYCLED.]

SARI: We have seen the whole process, from cloth to pickup.

BEKO: And no one crossed the line.

[SARI STEPS BACK, CHECKS HER VEST, AND THANKS THE FLOOR CREW.]

[BEKO CHECKS THAT THE LOADING AREA IS CLEAR. SARI REMOVES HER SAFETY VEST AND RETURNS IT TO THE RACK.]

SARI: We have seen the complete process.

LEMI: The next order starts tomorrow.

### Tag

[PUCK SHOWS THE FINAL SHOT: A ROW OF BAGS ON A TABLE.]

SARI: Can we add a slow-motion reveal?

LEMI: They are already on the table.

[THE CREW TURNS OFF THE MACHINE AND CLOCKS OUT.]
