# The Reveal Is Behind the Door

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home makeover shows, before-and-after reveals, dramatic countdowns, and designers who confuse surprise with consent*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A makeover crew redesigns a shared treehouse without asking its residents, then must stage a reveal that the residents can actually use.

Cast: Host Vex, a macaque; designer Nari, an orangutan; carpenter Fenn, a capuchin; resident Omi, a gibbon; resident Pip, a squirrel monkey; and reveal producer Rell, a mandrill.

## Episode: "Open the Door"

### Cold Open

[TREEHOUSE. A HUGE CURTAIN COVERS A SMALL DOOR.]

VEX: In three minutes, this family sees their new home for the first time.

OMI: We live here. We have seen it this morning.

Rell pulls the curtain anyway. It catches on the door and covers Vex.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The crew arrives to update the treehouse kitchen and reading nook. Omi asks for shelves that can hold heavy books. Pip asks for a low counter where he can prepare fruit. Nari presents a sleek design with a single glossy shelf mounted above everyone's reach.

NARI: It opens the room.

PIP: It closes my kitchen.

Fenn tests the shelf and discovers the branch flexes. He recommends two supports. Rell says supports look like work and asks whether they can hide them behind a reveal wall. Fenn explains that a reveal wall would be a wall, which is not part of the brief.

Vex interviews each resident in front of a board. Omi points to a sketch of a low work surface. Pip points to a drawing of a book shelf. The production labels both images DREAMS. Rell tells the editor to add a piano swell.

### Act Two

The crew begins building. Nari changes the colors from green to white because the camera reflects green. Omi asks where his books will go during painting. Rell says the reveal will happen before they come back inside. Omi points out that the books are still in the room.

Fenn constructs two sturdy supports. Nari paints them the same color as the branch. They look neat, but Rell says viewers will miss them. He asks Fenn to put a dramatic arch over the low counter. The arch blocks the doorway. Pip crawls under it, then cannot stand up beneath the counter.

PIP: This is a beautiful tunnel.

FENN: It is not supposed to be a tunnel.

Vex films a "before" walk-through. Rell asks Omi to pretend to be surprised by the old room. Omi says the room is exactly as he remembers it. They roll the camera again. This time Omi pretends to gasp, but his book slips from the shelf and hits the floor mid-gasp. The sound effect arrives three seconds late.

Nari proposes a second shelf, fitted lower. Fenn secures it to the supports. Pip tests it with a bowl and a pile of fruit. The shelf holds. Omi places books on the upper ledge and can reach them using a small step. The room works, but the crew has used the curtain to cover the paint cans, so the dramatic entrance is blocked.

Rell wants a countdown. He asks the residents to wait in the garden while the crew rearranges their chairs for camera symmetry. Omi agrees only if the books stay where he placed them. Pip picks up the fruit bowl and takes it outside. Rell calls this a continuity problem. Pip says it is lunch.

### Act Three

Rell rolls the reveal music. The residents enter through the doorway without a curtain. Nari shows the strong shelf supports, the low counter, and the step for reaching the book ledge. Omi tests the shelf with a thick atlas. Pip chops fruit at the counter. Both works are visible in the same wide shot.

VEX: What is your first reaction?

OMI: I can reach my books.

PIP: I can reach my fruit.

Rell asks for bigger reactions. The residents repeat their actions: Omi reaches a book, Pip reaches a pear. Vex begins a countdown anyway. On three, nothing changes. Rell adds a burst of confetti, which lands in the fruit bowl.

PIP: Now the pears are decorated.

Nari removes the confetti and shows that the supports keep the branch steady. The crew ends the reveal with a shot of the residents using the room. Rell asks them to hold still. Omi keeps reading; Pip keeps slicing. The footage is less symmetrical and more useful.

### Tag

[THE NEXT DAY.]

Rell visits to film a follow-up. He brings a second curtain, which is immediately used to shade the fruit from the sun. Vex begins a countdown, but nobody pauses.

OMI: You can film the room as it is.

Rell lowers the curtain and discovers he is holding the wrong end. It becomes a tablecloth.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
