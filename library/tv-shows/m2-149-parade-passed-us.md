# The Parade Has Passed Us

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live parade broadcasts that fill dead air with sponsor chatter, overlong introductions, and camera shots pointed away from the procession*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Pavi wants a lively parade broadcast. Camera operator Ralo needs to keep the floats in frame as they pass the reviewing stand, but producer Seli keeps cutting to sponsor banners and guest interviews.

Cast: Pavi, a macaque host; Ralo, a gorilla camera operator; Seli, a lemur producer; and Nima, a capuchin parade marshal.

## Episode: "Wave When You See the Camera"

### Cold Open

[STREET. A PARADE FLOAT APPROACHES. PAVI FACES CAMERA.]

PAVI: Here comes the canopy club, carrying its handmade bridge model.

[SELI POINTS TO A SPONSOR BANNER BEHIND THE CAMERA.]

SELI: We need the sponsor shot.

[RALO PANS AWAY. THE FLOAT PASSES OUT OF FRAME.]

PAVI: And we have missed the bridge.

NIMA: The parade kept moving.

[THE CAMERA PANS BACK. THE NEXT BAND IS APPROACHING.]

PAVI: We have a band coming down the street.

SELI: Read the sponsor message now.

RALO: The band is already here.

[NIMA RAISES A HAND TO SIGNAL A SAFE GAP. THE BAND PASSES. SELI WAITS UNTIL THE LAST DRUMMER CLEARS BEFORE SPEAKING.]

### Act One

[THE CREW RESETS. RALO FRAMES THE ROUTE. SELI HOLDS UP A CARD: "THANK OUR SPONSOR."]

PAVI: The audience should know who made the broadcast possible.

RALO: The float is coming from the right.

[SELI STARTS THE SPONSOR READ. THE FLOAT ENTERS FRAME.]

PAVI: Our sponsor keeps the camera powered as the canopy club arrives.

[THE FLOAT PASSES. RALO HOLDS THE SHOT. THE BAND FOLLOWS WITH A DRUM LINE.]

PAVI: The musicians are keeping a steady beat.

SELI: Can we interview the drum captain?

NIMA: The captain is marching.

[SELI RUNS ALONGSIDE THE BAND WITH A MICROPHONE. SHE ASKS A QUESTION. THE CAPTAIN CANNOT HEAR OVER THE DRUMS.]

DRUM CAPTAIN: After the route.

[SELI FALLS BEHIND. RALO KEEPS THE BAND IN FRAME.]

### Act Two

[THE NEXT FLOAT IS THE LIBRARY CART. PAVI READS ITS INTRODUCTION.]

PAVI: The reading room is on wheels, with books from the neighborhood collection.

[SELI HOLDS UP A CARD: "ASK ABOUT THE SPONSOR TABLE."]

PAVI: What is your favorite part of the float?

NIMA: The books are secured with straps.

[THE FLOAT TURNS. A BOX SLIDES A FEW INCHES. NIMA STEPS FORWARD AND TIGHTENS THE STRAP.]

NIMA: Now it is ready for the corner.

[SELI ASKS RALO TO GET A CLOSE-UP OF THE SPONSOR LOGO. RALO MOVES THE CAMERA. THE LIBRARY CART PASSES OUT OF FRAME AGAIN.]

SELI: We have the logo and the crowd.

PAVI: We do not have the turn.

NIMA: The cart made the turn safely.

[RALO TILTS THE CAMERA TO THE ROUTE MAP. IT SHOWS THE CART'S PATH AROUND THE CORNER.]

PAVI: We have the logo.

NIMA: You missed the cart turning.

[THE PARADE MARSHAL SIGNALS THE CREW TO STAY BEHIND THE BARRIER. RALO MOVES BACK. SELI PLACES A SMALL SPONSOR FLAG ON THE CAMERA CART.]

NIMA: The barrier is here for the float turning radius.

SELI: I thought it was for the camera.

NIMA: It is for people and floats.

[SELI TAKES THE FLAG OFF THE CART AND STANDS BEHIND THE BARRIER.]

RALO: The flag is blocking the lens.

SELI: It is the sponsor flag.

RALO: It is in the shot.

[PAVI REMOVES THE FLAG AND HOLDS IT BESIDE THE BANNER.]

### Act Three

[THE LAST FLOAT APPROACHES. THE CAMERA FRAMES THE FULL STREET. SELI STANDS QUIETLY.]

PAVI: The final float is the community garden, with seedlings for the new beds.

[THE FLOAT REACHES THE STAND. NIMA POINTS OUT THE PLANTS ARE TIED SECURELY. RALO FOLLOWS IT THROUGH THE TURN.]

SELI: We have time for a closing sponsor message.

PAVI: After the float passes.

[THE FLOAT PASSES. RALO HOLDS THE SHOT ON THE EMPTY ROUTE. THE CROWD WAVES.]

SELI: There is no float to film now.

PAVI: We can show where it went.

[NIMA POINTS TO THE ACCESSIBLE VIEWING AREA AND THE CLEAR EXIT PATH. THE CROWD STARTS TO LEAVE.]

PAVI: That is the end of the procession. The sponsor banner will remain on screen while we share the route map.

[NIMA SHOWS THE MAP WITH ACCESSIBLE VIEWING AREAS MARKED.]

[PAVI ASKS NIMA WHETHER THE EXIT ROUTE IS CLEAR. NIMA CHECKS THE MAP AND THE STREET.]

NIMA: The west gate is open. The east gate is for floats.

PAVI: We will put that on the closing card.

[SELI HANDS THE MAP TO A VISITOR WHO ASKS FOR THE BUS STOP.] 

### Tag

[THE CREW PACKS UP. THE SPONSOR FLAG IS FOLDED.]

SELI: We got the banner, the guest interview, and the last float.

RALO: We also got most of the parade.

PAVI: Next year, we keep the camera on the street.

[NIMA CARRIES THE ROUTE MAP TO THE INFORMATION TABLE.]
