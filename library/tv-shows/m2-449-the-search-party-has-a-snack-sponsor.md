# The Search Party Has a Snack Sponsor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: adventure reality series that pauses a missing-object search for branded refreshments*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Odo, a baboon; organizer Seli, a lemur; tracker Vex, a macaque; volunteer Pip, a capuchin; sponsor rep Mara, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. HILLSIDE TRAIL - DAY

[A group stands beside an empty trail-marker post. Odo holds a snack bar toward camera; a loose wrapper covers the search map legend.] 

ODO: The search party begins after this brief sponsor pause.

PIP: The map is under the snack wrapper.

[The empty marker post stands beside the snack box. Theme sting.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Welcome to Find It Fast, where every missing item has a search map and a sponsor.

SELI: We are looking for the blue trail marker.

MARA: And thanking Trail Crunch for keeping the team energized.

VEX: The search grid is on this map.

[He opens the map. A snack wrapper covers the legend.] 

VEX: We have a clue.

PIP: That's the wrapper.

[The team moves toward the first search square. Seli sets a marker at each completed section. Odo asks them to describe their feelings.] 

VEX: Focused.

PIP: Hungry.

SELI: We have snacks.

PIP: Those are for the sponsor shot.

ACT TWO

[The search party checks the trail edge. Vex follows footprints, but they lead back to the snack table. Pip has walked between the table and the trail, leaving a loop of prints.] 

VEX: The trail doubles back.

PIP: I carried the box up here.

MARA: That is not a search clue.

[The sponsor rep hands Odo a bar. He unwraps it and drops a crumb. A bird picks it up and flies toward the missing marker.] 

ODO: The wildlife has joined the search.

SELI: It is following a crumb.

[The bird lands on a low branch and pecks at a blue object caught in the leaves.] 

PIP: The marker is in the tree.

VEX: We have an aerial lead.

[He climbs the trunk, using a lower branch for balance. The branch bends but holds. He reaches the marker, then realizes the sign is attached to a vine.] 

VEX: The marker has roots.

SELI: It is tied to the tree.

[Mara asks the crew to hold a snack break while Vex untangles it. The wrapper blows away. Seli chases it, then returns with the wrapper. A small map corner with the legend clings to its sticky inside.] 

SELI: We found the missing map legend.

ODO: A second recovery.

[Before the reveal, Seli retraces the team's route on the map. The first square is beside the snack table, the second at the bend, and the third under the tree.]

SELI: We followed the path in order.

VEX: The map legend was missing.

[She places the recovered legend beside the route. It shows blue for river path and green for nature trail. The marker on the tree shows green.]

PIP: We found it. The sign was facing the wrong trail.

MARA: The post is in the correct place.

[Odo asks the sponsor rep to hold a bar beside the marker for the closing shot. Mara stands between the snack and the sign.]

MARA: The sponsor does not own the trail.

ODO: It supplied the snacks.

SELI: It can appear after the search, not over the marker.

[Odo steps back. The camera now captures the full post, the route map, and the search team. The bar wrapper flutters in front of the lens.]

PIP: The wrapper is hiding the result.

[Mara catches it and folds it into a pocket. The marker is visible again.]

VEX: That is the clean shot.

SELI: We should leave it there.

ACT THREE

[Vex frees the marker from the vine. The blue face turns toward the river path; the green face points toward the nature trail.]

SELI: Same post, corrected face.

PIP: We can finish the route.

MARA: The snack box was blocking it at the start.

[Odo looks at the camera.] 

ODO: Our search has revealed the power of perspective.

SELI: And a bird found the marker.

[The team turns the marker blue-side out and secures it to the post. The bird returns, pecks the last crumb from the snack table, and flies off.] 

PIP: The sponsor break helped.

MARA: The crumbs helped.

[The volunteers fold the search map. Vex marks the trail square complete, while Seli removes the snack box from the post.] 

ODO: The search party is finished.

VEX: We should check the next marker.

SELI: It is behind you.

[Odo turns. The next marker is visible in the clear trail.] 

TAG

[The sponsor rep hands out the remaining bars. Pip reads the wrapper.] 

PIP: "Find the blue mark."

MARA: We did.

ODO: We can run it again with the green side.
