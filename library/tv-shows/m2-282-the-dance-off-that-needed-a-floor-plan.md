# The Dance-Off That Needed a Floor Plan

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised dance competitions, where the judges score a group routine before the dancers agree on their stage positions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Two dance teams rehearse for a televised competition, but the stage marks keep moving and the host keeps asking for a dramatic entrance.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; choreographer Nara, macaque; dancer Odo, baboon; dancer Seli, capuchin; stage manager Tavi, lemur; judge Vex, squirrel monkey; judge Luma, tamarin.

## Episode: "Find Your Mark"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. TWO TAPE MARKS. FOUR DANCERS.]

PAVA: Take your positions.

ODO: Which positions?

NARA: The marks moved.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[TAVI MEASURES THE STAGE.]

Tavi says the tape marks were placed for the camera, not the dancers. Nara explains the routine: two steps forward, a turn, then a partner pass.

PAVA: We need a big opening.

NARA: The dancers need room.

Odo takes the left mark. Seli takes the right. The camera operator asks them to switch so both faces are visible.

ODO: The stage has changed.

TAVI: The stage has not moved.

Seli turns; she bumps Odo's elbow. They both stop.

NARA: The pass needs a wider gap.

Pava asks for another take. The dancers walk to their marks. One piece of tape peels up and sticks to Odo's foot.

PAVA: Odo has taken the mark.

ODO: I have taken one step.

### Act Two

[THE CREW MOVES THE TAPE.]

Nara places the marks farther apart. Tavi measures the center line. Pava says the camera will not see the dancers if they stand too far back.

PAVA: Move forward.

NARA: Then the turn is too close to the edge.

Seli suggests turning diagonally. Odo tries it; his tail swings into the camera stand.

TAVI: The stand is inside the dance area.

PAVA: It is a fixed camera.

NARA: It is in the way.

The crew shifts the camera. The light stand now blocks the left mark. Tavi takes it down and resets the tape. Odo asks if the judges will score the stage layout.

PAVA: They score timing, energy, and story.

NARA: The story is a dance.

SELI: The stage is the page.

Odo asks if the tape can be colored. Tavi brings out red tape, but it looks like a boundary line. The dancers hesitate before crossing it.

[THE JUDGES WATCH A RECORDING OF THE ROUTINE.]

Judge Vex says the dancers appeared hesitant at the beginning. Nara says they were waiting for a clear floor mark. Judge Luma says the ending needs more height.

PAVA: Could Odo lift Seli?

NARA: There is no lift in the choreography.

ODO: I can lift her.

SELI: You will need to know where I land.

Tavi marks a safe landing zone with tape. The judges ask for a demonstration. Odo lifts Seli only a few inches and sets her down gently, but the camera angle makes it look higher.

PAVA: That reads well on screen.

NARA: It is a small lift.

SELI: I can step down.

Odo lowers her. The judges ask if the team can add a turn. Nara says the routine already has a turn near the edge. The team repeats it with the camera moved farther back. The movement is clear without adding new steps.

### Act Three

[THE TEAM RUNS THE ROUTINE WITHOUT THE CAMERA.]

Nara counts the steps. Odo and Seli pass cleanly. They turn and return to their marks. Pava asks for a more dramatic finish.

NARA: The finish is a still pose.

PAVA: Can the pose be bigger?

ODO: It can be wider.

The dancers widen their stance, but Odo's foot lands on the edge of the stage. Tavi moves the tape inward. They try again; the pose is balanced.

Pava calls for the camera. The stand is now clear of the movement area. The dancers complete the routine on count.

PAVA: That was the performance.

NARA: That was the rehearsal.

TAVI: The tape marks are stable.

Seli checks each mark and presses it down.

[THE JUDGES GIVE THEIR FINAL NOTES.]

They praise the clear spacing and the unbroken pass, but ask for the ending to face the audience. Nara rotates the final pose, and the dancers try it again.

SELI: We can see everyone.

ODO: No one is at the edge.

TAVI: The camera is outside the dance area.

PAVA: The routine is ready for the broadcast.

Nara tells the team not to add another turn. Odo says he will keep the ending simple. Seli marks the center and asks the judges to stand clear.

### Tag

[THE JUDGES ENTER.]

Pava asks them to score the routine. The judges say they need to see it performed.

ODO: We just performed.

NARA: We rehearsed.

PAVA: That distinction is important.

Tavi points to the tape. One mark has lifted again.

TAVI: The floor plan is not stable.

[SFX: DANCE MUSIC STARTS, TAPE PEELS.]
