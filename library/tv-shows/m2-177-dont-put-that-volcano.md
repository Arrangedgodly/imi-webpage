# Don't Put That in the Volcano

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's science television that treats every demonstration as a spectacular explosion*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Science host Kelu plans a quiet lesson about how rocks absorb heat. Producer Pips keeps asking for a bigger reaction, while lab teacher Anu tries to keep the studio classroom intact and the young audience focused on what the experiment shows.

Cast: Kelu, a macaque host; Pips, a capuchin producer; Anu, a gorilla teacher; and Rika, a langur student assistant.

## Episode: "The Hot Rock Test"

### Cold Open

[CLASSROOM STUDIO. A TRAY OF DARK AND LIGHT STONES SITS BESIDE A SAFE HEAT LAMP. PIPS ROLLS IN A TOY VOLCANO.] 

KELU: Today we will compare how two rocks warm under the same lamp.

PIPS: Can the volcano erupt?

ANU: The lesson is about heat absorption.

[PIPS PULLS A LEVER. THE VOLCANO SPITS PAPER CONFETTI ACROSS THE TABLE.]

RIKA: The confetti is not hot.

KELU: Correct. It is a prop.

### Act One

[ANU SETS A THERMOMETER BESIDE EACH ROCK. RIKA WRITES THE STARTING TEMPERATURES ON A BOARD.] 

KELU: We will check both after five minutes.

PIPS: Can we speed it up?

ANU: No. The timer is part of the test.

[PIPS SWITCHES ON A SECOND LAMP. RIKA TURNS IT OFF AND CHECKS THAT THE TWO ROCKS ARE STILL UNDER THE SAME LIGHT.]

RIKA: If they get different heat, the comparison changes.

[KELU ASKS RIKA TO READ THE TIMER. PIPS CUES A RED LIGHT WHEN SHE SAYS "FIVE MINUTES," AS IF THE NUMBER IS A DANGER ALERT.] 

RIKA: It is the timer.

ANU: We are checking the same interval for both rocks.

[PIPS MOVES THE TOY VOLCANO CLOSER TO THE LAMP. ITS PAPER CONE STARTS TO WARP. ANU MOVES IT BACK TO THE PROP CART.] 

PIPS: The model is reacting.

KELU: The model is paper.

[PIPS POINTS THE CAMERA AT THE CLASS. THE CHILDREN ARE WATCHING THE THERMOMETERS, NOT THE VOLCANO.]

RIKA: The numbers are changing slowly.

PIPS: Can we get a reaction shot?

ANU: They are looking at the experiment.

[ONE CHILD HOLDS UP A DRAWING OF THE TWO ROCKS WITH THE SMALL TEMPERATURE DIFFERENCE. THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN. PIPS ADDS A GIANT FLAME GRAPHIC OVER IT, COVERING THE DRAWING.] 

KELU: We have lost the result.

[PIPS PUTS A DRAMATIC SOUNDTRACK UNDER THE TIMER. THE TRACK BUILDS TO A CLIMAX WHILE THE SECOND HAND MOVES.] 

KELU: The rocks are still sitting there.

ANU: That is what we want them to do.

### Act Two

[THE TIMER RINGS. RIKA READS THE TEMPERATURES. THE DARK ROCK IS WARMER. PIPS UNVEILS A GRAPHIC: "THE ROCK THAT WON." ]

RIKA: It is a difference, not a race.

KELU: The dark surface absorbed more energy from the lamp.

[PIPS TURNS THE TOY VOLCANO TOWARD THE TABLE. ITS PAPER CONFETTI BLOCKS THE THERMOMETERS.]

[RIKA CLEARS THE CONFETTI AND WRITES THE NUMBERS ON THE BOARD. PIPS ASKS FOR A BAR GRAPH WITH A HUGE DIFFERENCE. SHE DRAWS THE ACTUAL SMALL GAP BETWEEN THE VALUES.] 

PIPS: That is a very short bar.

RIKA: The difference is small.

[ANU CHECKS THE THERMOMETERS. BOTH HAVE COOLED BY THE SAME AMOUNT WHILE THE GRAPHIC WAS BEING MADE.] 

KELU: Now we have another observation.

ANU: Move the model away from the results.

[PIPS LIFTS THE VOLCANO. A PAPER SMOKE PLUME CATCHES ON A LIGHT STAND AND HANGS OVER THE CLASSROOM LIKE A CLOUD.] 

KELU: The volcano has escaped its lesson.

[RIKA PULLS THE PLUME FREE. IT UNFOLDS INTO A LONG PAPER STRIP THAT READS "SPONSOR MESSAGE." ]

RIKA: It was an advertisement.

PIPS: It has a science theme.

### Act Three

[ANU RESETS THE TABLE. KELU EXPLAINS THAT THE TEST APPLIES TO THESE ROCKS UNDER THIS LAMP, NOT EVERY ROCK EVERYWHERE.] 

KELU: We have one observation. We can test again with other materials.

[PIPS TRIES TO ADD A GIANT "BREAKTHROUGH" GRAPHIC. RIKA HOLDS UP THE ACTUAL DATA BOARD. THE GRAPHIC SHRINKS TO FIT BESIDE THE TWO TEMPERATURES.] 

RIKA: The results are small enough to read.

[THEY PLACE THE ROCKS ON A COOLING TRAY. PIPS PUTS THE TOY VOLCANO ON THE PROP CART, BUT IT SPRINGS A CONFETTI PUFF WHEN THE WHEEL HITS A BUMP. THE PAPER PLUME COVERS THE CAMERA LENS.]

[RIKA PEELS THE PAPER OFF THE LENS. THE CAMERA SHOWS THE STUDENTS WRITING THEIR OWN OBSERVATIONS INSTEAD OF WATCHING THE CONFETTI. PIPS MOVES THE PROP CART OUT OF FRAME.] 

KELU: We have enough for today's test.

ANU: The class can repeat it with different stones next week.

### Tag

[ANU REMOVES THE CONFETTI. THE FINAL SHOT SHOWS THE DATA BOARD, TWO ROCKS, AND ONE TOY VOLCANO TURNED TOWARD THE WALL.] 

PIPS: It is ready for the next experiment.

RIKA: It can wait until we have one.
