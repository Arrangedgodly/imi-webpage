# The Case of the Vanishing Drum

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedurals that inflate a small, nonviolent mystery into a forensic command operation*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Detectives investigate a missing practice drum from the community room. Their elaborate evidence routine reveals it was carried outside by a monkey who needed a louder signal to find his troop.

Cast: Detective Suli, a macaque; Detective Bex, a gibbon; analyst Kora, a capuchin; caller Ren, a langur; and the community drummer, Pata.

## Episode: "Three Taps at the Window"

### Cold Open

[POLICE LIGHTS FLASH ACROSS AN EMPTY ROOM. A DRUMSTICK LIES ON THE FLOOR.]

SULI: The drum is gone.

BEX: No sign of forced entry.

KORA: I found a crumb.

SULI: Bag it.

[KORA HOLDS UP A TINY DRIED FIG.]

BEX: It may be the suspect's snack.

### Act One

[THE DETECTIVES TAPE OFF THE ROOM. SULI PUTS ON GLOVES.]

SULI: Last seen at six, beside the window.

BEX: The door log shows one person leaving.

KORA: Pata left at six-ten.

SULI: Did Pata take the drum?

BEX: Pata says the drum was already gone.

SULI: We need a timeline.

[KORA SETS DOWN A CHART. THE LINES FORM A SMALL SPIRAL.]

KORA: At six, the drum was by the window. At six-ten, Pata left. At six-twelve, a sound was reported outside.

BEX: What kind of sound?

KORA: Three taps, then a pause.

SULI: A coded message.

BEX: Or someone testing a drum.

[THEY EXAMINE THE WINDOW. A FEW SEEDS ARE ON THE SILL.]

SULI: The suspect may have climbed out.

BEX: The window is too narrow for the drum.

SULI: Then the drum left first.

### Act Two

[THE DETECTIVES INTERVIEW PATA IN THE COMMUNITY ROOM.]

PATA: I put the drum near the window because the evening rehearsal was outside.

SULI: Why did you leave it unattended?

PATA: I went to get water.

BEX: Did anyone ask to borrow it?

PATA: Ren asked for a signal sound.

KORA: What was the signal?

PATA: Three taps, so their troop could find the clearing.

SULI: Did Ren take the drum?

PATA: Ren took it outside, then left it under the tree.

[THE DETECTIVES LOOK AT EACH OTHER.]

SULI: We have a suspect.

BEX: We have a witness.

[THEY HEAD OUTSIDE. A SMALL MONKEY IS TAPPING THE DRUM UNDER A TREE.]

KORA: There is the signal.

SULI: Approach carefully.

[THEY WALK SLOWLY. THE MONKEY STOPS, WATCHES THEM, THEN TAPS THE DRUM TWICE.]

BEX: It is not a threat.

SULI: It is a message.

KORA: The troop answered from the branches.

[THEY HEAR A RUSTLE. THREE MONKEYS COME DOWN FROM THE TREE.]

[KORA RECONSTRUCTS THE TIMELINE WITH THREE PAPER CIRCLES.]

KORA: First tap at six-twelve. Second at six-thirteen. Third at six-fourteen.

SULI: A pattern.

BEX: Or a drum being tapped three times.

KORA: Each sound came from a different distance.

SULI: The caller may have been moving.

BEX: Or the monkey was walking with the drum.

[THEY FOLLOW THE SEED TRAIL TO THE SIDE DOOR. THE DOOR IS OPEN.]

SULI: No forced entry.

BEX: You already said that.

SULI: I am confirming the observation.

KORA: There are small handprints on the lower edge.

BEX: That fits a monkey, not a burglar.

SULI: We have not ruled out a very small burglar.

[THEY HEAR THE THREE-TAP SIGNAL OUTSIDE. SULI REACHES FOR HER RADIO.]

KORA: Wait. The troop answered.

[THE SOUND COMES FROM THE TREE. THE DETECTIVES TURN.]

[THE CREW TAKES A PHOTOGRAPH OF THE DRUM IN ITS NEW LOCATION.]

KORA: We should document where it belongs.

PATA: It belongs by the window for rehearsal.

SULI: And outside for signals, with permission.

BEX: We can write both uses on the sign.

[THE SIGN NOW READS "COMMUNITY DRUM: ASK BEFORE BORROWING. RETURN AFTER USE."]

KORA: The report has a resolution.

SULI: And the evidence has a sound.

### Act Three

[THE DETECTIVES RETURN THE DRUM TO PATA.]

PATA: Ren borrowed it to call the troop back.

SULI: The instrument was moved without the room's checkout form.

PATA: There is no form.

KORA: We can make one.

BEX: Or we can ask before borrowing.

[THE COMMUNITY ROOM ADDS A SIMPLE SIGN: "ASK PATA BEFORE TAKING THE DRUM."]

SULI: The case is closed.

KORA: What about the fig crumb?

[THE SMALL MONKEY TAKES THE FIG FROM THE EVIDENCE BAG.]

BEX: That is evidence of a snack.

SULI: And the snack has left the scene.

[PATA TAPS THE DRUM THREE TIMES. THE TROOP ANSWERS FROM OUTSIDE.]

PATA: They know where we are.

SULI: We will file the report.

KORA: Under music or missing property?

SULI: Both. We have a form now.

[BEX HOLDS UP THE SIGN. IT HAS BEEN HUNG UPSIDE DOWN.]
