# The Sound Guessing Show Has a Clue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: studio quiz shows that disguise familiar sounds with dramatic clues and misleading hints*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A sound-identification game show adds so many clues to a simple household noise that the contestants begin guessing the studio itself.

Cast: Host Kori, a macaque; contestant Sefu, a gibbon; contestant Lali, a capuchin; and sound engineer Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "What Made That Noise?"

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - NIGHT

Kori stands beside two contestants. Odo waits behind the soundboard.

KORI: Name the sound. Here is your first clue: it happens when something is moved.

[THE SOUND OF A DOOR CREAKING PLAYS.]

SEFU: A door.

KORI: Correct, but can you be more specific?

LALI: The studio door.

[THE STUDIO DOOR CREAKS OPEN. ODO PEEKS OUT.]

KORI: Too specific. We are looking for a door.

ACT ONE

Kori draws a curtain across a set of mystery objects. Odo cues a sound.

KORI: This sound is associated with a morning routine.

[A TOOTHBRUSH BUZZES.] 

LALI: Electric toothbrush.

KORI: It could be. But what else makes a similar sound?

SEFU: The soundboard fan.

[THE FAN WHIRS. ODO TURNS IT OFF. THE STUDIO GETS HOTTER.]

KORI: You have introduced a new sound.

ODO: You asked for another possibility.

KORI: The game expects a single answer.

ACT TWO

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - LATER

The contestants put on headphones. Kori holds a clue card.

KORI: The sound is small, metallic, and often found in a pocket.

[A SET OF KEYS JINGLES.]

SEFU: Keys.

LALI: The host's key ring.

[KORI CHECKS HIS POCKET. THE KEYS JINGLE AGAIN.]

KORI: The answer is a key ring, but not mine.

ODO: It was my keys in the recording.

KORI: The clue did not say whose keys.

SEFU: How can we know the answer if the clue leaves out the useful part?

KORI: That is where instinct helps.

LALI: I instinctively hear keys.

KORI: You are close.

ACT TWO-B

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - LATER

Odo brings out a box labeled "MYSTERY OBJECT." Kori turns to camera. The contestants get a buzzer each.

KORI: This sound is made by something used in a room, but not every room.

[A RATTLE PLAYS.]

SEFU: A window latch.

LALI: The studio door handle.

KORI: The intended answer is a tambourine.

ODO: That was my prop box.

KORI: The clue says room.

ODO: The box is in a room.

LALI: So is the door.

[THE DOOR HANDLE RATTLES. A STAGEHAND TRIES TO ENTER WITH A COIL OF CABLE.]

KORI: We have another valid answer.

ODO: We have a stagehand.

SEFU: Can he be a contestant?

STAGEHAND: I have a cable.

KORI: Is that the answer?

STAGEHAND: It is what I am carrying.

[THE AUDIENCE BUZZES. THE CONTESTANTS AND STAGEHAND ALL LOOK AT THE BUZZERS.]

KORI: The audience has guessed the sound.

ACT TWO-C

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - EVENING

Kori sits in the contestant chair. Sefu holds the clue card while Lali operates the soundboard.

SEFU: The sound is made of three short taps.

[LALI PLAYS THREE TAPS.]

KORI: Someone knocking.

LALI: Someone drumming on the soundboard.

[SHE TAPS THE BOARD. THE SAME SOUND PLAYS.]

SEFU: Both answers fit.

KORI: Only one answer is on the card.

ODO: The card says "knock."

KORI: The sound engineer is also knocking.

[LALI TAPS AGAIN. ODO KNOCKS ON THE BOX. THEY SOUND IDENTICAL.]

KORI: I withdraw my answer.

SEFU: Are you allowed to do that?

KORI: I am hosting.

[THE AUDIENCE RINGS A SMALL BELL. Kori looks toward Odo.]

KORI: Is that a clue?

ODO: That is the audience.

ACT THREE

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - FINAL ROUND

Odo plays one last sound: a soft, repeated click. The contestants lean in.

SEFU: I hear a metronome.

LALI: I hear a camera lens.

KORI: The clue is still loading.

ODO: The clue is on the card.

[KORI TURNS THE CARD OVER. THE OTHER SIDE SAYS "FINAL ROUND."]

KORI: We have reached the final round.

KORI: It is found in a place where people gather.

SEFU: A chair leg.

LALI: The camera tripod.

[THE CAMERA TRIPOD CLICKS AS Diri shifts it.]

ODO: It is the studio set moving.

KORI: The answer we had was a clock.

SEFU: The sound was not a clock.

KORI: It is now. We have adjusted the clue.

[THE CLOCK TICKS LOUDLY. THE CAMERA SLOWLY PANS TO THE WALL CLOCK.]

LALI: We guessed the set, the room, and the audience.

KORI: And you were both correct about a sound.

TAG

Odo plays a celebratory fanfare. Kori raises his hand.

KORI: Is that the winning sound?

ODO: It is the exit music.

[THE CONTESTANTS LEAVE. THE HOST STAYS, WAITING FOR THE NEXT CLUE.]
