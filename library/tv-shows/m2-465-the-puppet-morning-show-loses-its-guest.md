# The Puppet Morning Show Loses Its Guest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's morning television, surprise guests, and hosts who fill dead air with increasingly large puppets*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Lilo, a gibbon; cohost Pako, a macaque; puppet Bim, a baboon; booking producer Eri, an orangutan; floor assistant Sefu, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. MORNING SHOW SET - DAWN

A bright set has a chair for a guest, a small table, and a puppet stage. The host faces the camera. The guest chair is empty.

LILO: Our special guest is almost here.

ERI: The guest canceled last night.

PAKO: Who are we waiting for?

ERI: Nobody. The segment is still on the schedule.

[The puppet pops up from behind its stage.] 

BIM: I am here.

LILO: You are the surprise guest.

BIM: I was on the call sheet as the puppet.

[The opening music starts again. Lilo gestures toward the empty chair.] 

ACT ONE

INT. MORNING SHOW SET - CONTINUOUS

Eri gives the hosts a replacement rundown: puppet song, weather map, craft demonstration, sign-off. Lilo asks if Bim can be interviewed as a celebrity guest. Pako points out that Bim is scheduled to sing after the interview slot.

LILO: We can move the song.

ERI: The musician is not here yet.

BIM: I know one song.

PAKO: Is it the one you sing every day?

BIM: It is my only song.

[The hosts ask Bim about the empty guest chair. The puppet says the chair is comfortable but empty. Lilo tries to make the missing guest a mystery.] 

LILO: Could the chair be holding a clue?

BIM: It is holding a cushion.

[The floor assistant checks the chair for a hidden microphone and finds none. Eri removes the chair from the camera frame so viewers do not think the guest is late.] 

SEFU: The chair can go back to wardrobe.

LILO: Then where will our guest sit?

PAKO: There is no guest.

ACT TWO

INT. SET - LATER

The morning show reaches the craft segment. Pako shows viewers how to fold a paper bird. Lilo asks Bim to be the bird's voice. The puppet refuses because it is a frog. Pako folds one paper bird and hands it to Bim, who holds it upside down.

BIM: This bird is flying toward the table.

PAKO: It is upside down.

BIM: Then it is landing.

[The host laughs, then asks the puppet whether it misses having a guest. Bim says the set crew is present and working. Eri points to the countdown clock and asks them to move on.] 

ERI: We have four minutes before weather.

LILO: Can we stretch the paper bird into a game?

PAKO: Only if we have rules.

[They set one rule: the folded bird must land in a basket from a taped mark. Lilo tries to throw it from across the set. Sefu moves the basket closer so the game can be played safely. Bim drops its bird, which lands on the table instead.] 

BIM: Mine has chosen the desk.

[The paper bird slides into the guest chair, now back in frame. Eri quickly removes the chair again. Pako places the bird in the basket by hand, ending the game.] 

[The floor assistant calls a short reset because a paper bird has landed near a cable. Sefu moves the bird and secures the cable along the edge of the set. Lilo tries to use the pause for a second guest tease.] 

LILO: Perhaps the bird knows who is coming.

BIM: It knows where the basket is.

[The host looks at the bird, then at the empty chair. Eri crosses the guest cue off the rundown for good and gives the weather map its full screen.] 

ACT THREE

INT. SET - END OF SHOW

The weather map appears. Pako points to the day's forecast. Lilo asks if the missing guest might arrive for the sign-off. Eri confirms the guest is not coming and crosses the empty-chair cue off the rundown. The puppet performs its one song, short and clear.

BIM: Ribbit in the morning, ribbit in the day.

PAKO: That was the song.

BIM: It has two verses.

LILO: We have time for both.

[The puppet sings the second verse with the same two notes. The floor assistant lowers the puppet stage curtain. The host thanks the crew and viewers, then signals the end rather than inventing another segment.] 

LILO: We had a craft, a forecast, and one frog song.

ERI: And no guest.

PAKO: The chair has been returned to wardrobe.

[The hosts turn the set lights down. Bim stays on the puppet stage until Sefu carries it off.] 

TAG

A crew member places a handwritten card on the empty chair mark: GUEST SEGMENT CANCELED. Lilo removes it before the camera cuts.

FINAL IMAGE: The puppet stage is dark, the guest chair is off set, and the hosts wave goodbye.
