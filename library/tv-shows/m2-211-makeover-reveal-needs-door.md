# The Makeover Reveal Needs a Door

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home renovation reality shows that prioritize a dramatic reveal over the people who still need to use the house*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A renovation crew promises to unveil a transformed treehouse kitchen, but the dramatic camera wall blocks the only entrance.

Cast: Host Pella, a macaque; carpenter Runo, a gibbon; resident Kiri, a capuchin; and producer Dax, a baboon.

Episode: "Before and After the Door"

COLD OPEN

INT. TREEHOUSE KITCHEN - MORNING

Kiri opens the kitchen door. A camera track runs across the entry. She cannot get through with a basket of greens.

KIRI: Is this the new wall?

DAX: It is the reveal wall.

RUNO: It is not attached to the treehouse.

PELLA: When we pull it away, the audience sees the whole room at once.

KIRI: I need to get to the stove.

[RUNO LIFTS THE WALL. THE TRACK ROLLS AND BLOCKS THE DOOR AGAIN.]

ACT ONE

Pella stands in the kitchen with a paint card. Runo measures the doorframe. Kiri holds the basket.

PELLA: The makeover theme is open concept.

KIRI: I asked for more shelf space.

DAX: Shelf space is less visual.

RUNO: We can add a shelf above the counter.

KIRI: The old shelf is already full of dishes.

PELLA: Could we clear it for the before shot?

KIRI: The dishes are used at breakfast.

[DAX TAKES A STACK OF BOWLS OFF THE SHELF. A CUPBOARD DOOR SPRINGS OPEN AND THE LIDS TUMBLE OUT.]

RUNO: The cupboard needs a latch.

PELLA: We found a second problem. Great television.

KIRI: It was a problem before the camera arrived.

ACT TWO

INT. TREEHOUSE KITCHEN - AFTERNOON

Runo installs a short shelf. Pella marks the floor for the reveal. Dax places a white cloth over the stove.

KIRI: I cook on that stove.

DAX: We need a clean shot.

KIRI: Then clean it.

[RUNO FITS A LATCH TO THE CUPBOARD. KIRI OPENS AND CLOSES IT. THE LIDS STAY IN PLACE.]

RUNO: The latch works.

PELLA: Can we film it opening in slow motion?

KIRI: It opens normally.

PELLA: Normal is hard to tease in a promo.

DAX: I can make a dramatic sound cue.

[HE PLAYS A LOW THUNDER ROLL. THE CUPBOARD OPENS. A SPOON FALLS OUT AND CLINKS ON THE FLOOR.]

KIRI: That is why we needed the latch.

RUNO: I will add a hook for the spoon.

PELLA: Now we have an act break.

ACT TWO-B

INT. TREEHOUSE KITCHEN - MID-AFTERNOON

Runo clears space under the counter. Kiri tests the stove. Dax rolls in a camera cart, which catches on the entrance track.

DAX: We need the cart inside for the before-and-after shot.

KIRI: It does not fit through the door.

PELLA: Can we remove the handle?

RUNO: The handle is welded on.

DAX: Then we can remove the door.

KIRI: The door is how I get in.

[RUNO SLIDES THE CAMERA CART BACK OUT. PELLA TRIES TO FRAME THE KITCHEN THROUGH THE OPENING.]

PELLA: The view is narrower without the cart.

KIRI: It is the doorway.

RUNO: We can film from the porch. The shelf and cupboard both show.

DAX: The porch is not in our lighting plan.

KIRI: It is in the morning sun every day.

[DAX MOVES A LIGHT STAND OUTSIDE. IT CASTS A SHADOW ACROSS THE CAMERA TRACK.]

DAX: The light is blocking the entrance.

KIRI: Move the light.

PELLA: That's the same note as the shelf.

DAX: Fine. We can go handheld.

[DAX PICKS UP THE CAMERA. THE TRACK IS NOW CLEAR. KIRI WALKS THROUGH THE DOOR WITH HER BASKET.]

KIRI: That is the shot I needed.

ACT THREE

INT. TREEHOUSE KITCHEN - LATE AFTERNOON

Dax reassembles the reveal wall and track for the final shot. The crew gathers behind it. Kiri stands outside holding the same breakfast basket. Pella raises both hands for the cameras.

PELLA: In three, two, one.

[THE WALL ROLLS ASIDE, BUT THE WALL'S UPPER EDGE SNAGS ON THE NEW SHELF. THE WALL TILTS. RUNO CATCHES IT.]

DAX: Hold the reveal.

KIRI: I cannot get in.

RUNO: I can roll the wall two inches left.

PELLA: The shot will lose symmetry.

KIRI: The door will gain a person.

[RUNO SHIFTS THE REVEAL WALL. THE WALL CLEARS THE DOOR. KIRI WALKS IN AND SETS DOWN HER BASKET.]

PELLA: Look at that open space.

KIRI: I can reach the counter.

RUNO: And the latch keeps the lids from falling.

KIRI: That's the change I wanted.

RUNO: The cupboard stays closed when the camera is gone.

DAX: Can we leave the camera track?

KIRI: No.

TAG

Kiri places bowls on the new shelf. Pella asks her to pose beside the reveal wall. Kiri moves it into the corner, then starts preparing breakfast eight hours late.

DAX: We need a final wide shot.

KIRI: Film from the doorway.

[THE CAMERA ROLLS BACK. THE DOOR REMAINS OPEN.]
