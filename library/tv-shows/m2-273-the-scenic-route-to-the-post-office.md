# The Scenic Route to the Post Office

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentary television, where a host takes a long detour to make a five-minute delivery appear epic*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Host Seli travels across the village to mail one postcard, while producer Odo keeps adding scenic stops to the itinerary.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; producer Odo, baboon; guide Nara, macaque; camera operator Pava, gibbon; shopkeeper Tavi, lemur; post-office clerk Vela, squirrel monkey.

## Episode: "One Stamp, Many Miles"

### Cold Open

[SELI HOLDS A POSTCARD. A SIGN POINTS TO THE POST OFFICE.]

SELI: The post office is just across the square.

ODO: We will take the scenic route.

NARA: It is raining.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW STANDS BESIDE A VILLAGE MAP.]

Odo draws a route around the square, past the bakery, the fountain, and the hill path. Nara says the direct route is five minutes. Odo says the scenic route will give the episode a beginning, middle, and end.

SELI: The postcard only needs a stamp.

PAVA: The camera needs a view.

Tavi gives them a rain cover for the camera. The cover blocks the lens.

PAVA: I cannot see.

ODO: It is weatherproof.

NARA: It is opaque.

They remove the cover and walk under a shop awning. Seli describes the bakery as a "local landmark." Tavi says it sells bread.

SELI: What is the story of this bakery?

TAVI: It opens at dawn.

Odo asks the baker to pose with a loaf. The baker is busy and points toward the post office.

### Act Two

[THE CREW REACHES THE FOUNTAIN.]

Seli wants to film the postcard beside the water. Odo says the fountain represents the village's history. Nara says the postcard is getting damp.

PAVA: The water gives us atmosphere.

NARA: The paper is softening.

Seli tucks it inside her jacket. Odo asks for a walking shot around the fountain. The crew circles it once, then realizes they have returned to the same bench.

ODO: We have completed a loop.

NARA: We have lost three minutes.

PAVA: We have gained a shot.

A gust lifts the map. Nara catches it. The route's ink has smudged, and the post office symbol is gone.

SELI: The destination has disappeared.

NARA: It is across the square.

Odo asks Tavi for directions. Tavi points straight ahead. Seli follows the direct route, but Odo moves to film the hill path.

ODO: The view is better from above.

NARA: The mailbox is lower.

[THE CREW TAKES A DETOUR PAST THE HILL.]

Odo says the hill path offers a view of the whole village. Nara says the post office is about to close. Seli agrees to a single establishing shot.

PAVA: We need the camera on the panorama.

NARA: We need a stamp.

The wind lifts Seli's jacket, and the postcard nearly slides out. She presses it flat against the map.

SELI: The postcard is trying to depart early.

ODO: It knows its destination.

They take the shot. The camera captures the village, but a branch blocks the post office from view. Pava moves around the branch; the camera catches only the hillside.

NARA: The landmark is not in frame.

ODO: The journey is.

Seli turns the camera toward the actual route. The post office is visible below them. They descend directly, and Odo asks for a closing line.

SELI: We are almost there.

NARA: That is the most useful narration so far.

### Act Three

[THE CREW ARRIVES AT THE POST OFFICE.]

The office is about to close. Seli asks for a stamp. Clerk Vela leans through the service window and places one on the counter. Tavi holds the postcard flat while Seli writes the address and sticks the stamp on.

PAVA: Can you say what the journey meant to you?

SELI: It meant I should have taken the direct route.

ODO: We need a reflective closing.

NARA: The post office is closing.

Seli places the postcard in the slot. It sticks halfway because the paper is damp. She presses it gently until it drops inside.

[SFX: POST SLOT CLACK.]

SELI: The message has left us.

NARA: The postcard is in the box.

Odo asks them to return to the fountain for the final shot. Tavi says the office is closed and they should go home. Seli agrees.

Before leaving the office, Seli checks the postcard slot to make sure the card went through. It has fallen into the box.

SELI: The delivery is complete.

ODO: The journey has a receipt.

NARA: The receipt is in the camera bag.

PAVA: We can film it tomorrow.

### Tag

[THE CREW WALKS BACK ACROSS THE SQUARE.]

ODO: The trip is complete.

SELI: The delivery is complete.

PAVA: And the scenic route?

NARA: We are on it now.

Odo turns the map right side up. The direct path is marked in bold.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS, RAIN EASES.]
