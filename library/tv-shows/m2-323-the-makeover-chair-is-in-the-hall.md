# The Makeover Chair Is in the Hall

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime makeover shows, emotional reveal speeches, surprise renovations, and hosts who mistake possessions for clutter*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A makeover crew tries to transform a retired librarian's reading room, but its surprise reveal keeps hiding the books that make the room useful.

Cast: Host Palla, a gibbon; librarian Omi, an orangutan; designer Neri, a capuchin; organizer Vex, a macaque; and camera assistant Tavi, a lemur.

## Episode: "A Room for Reading"

### Cold Open

[READING ROOM. A CHAIR IS COVERED WITH A CLOTH.]

PALLA: Omi has waited years for a fresh start.

OMI: I asked for a lamp.

Palla pulls the cloth. The lamp is missing; the chair is wearing it.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

Omi retired from the library and now reads in a small room at home. She asks for a brighter lamp and a safe place for her catalog of folktales. Neri wants to paint the walls pale yellow. Vex suggests removing the old shelves because they look crowded on camera. Omi says the shelves hold books, not decoration.

Palla records an interview about Omi's dream room. Omi describes a comfortable chair, a strong reading light, and room for a cup of tea. The editor adds a string score under each item. When Omi mentions tea, Palla asks her to repeat the line with a wistful look. Omi repeats it while holding the empty cup.

Vex starts sorting books into piles: keep, donate, and maybe. Omi corrects him: the books are organized by region and should stay together. He moves one shelf, then cannot find the matching volume. The camera shows an empty space where the set's vase has been placed.

### Act Two

The crew paints one wall, covering a pencil mark Omi uses to remember where a shelf fits. Neri asks for a new measurement. Omi points to the old mark under the paint. Vex says the reveal needs a clean wall. Omi says the shelf needs a level wall. Neri measures twice and finds the painted shelf bracket is crooked.

They move the chair for the big reveal, but Vex insists on a wide shot. The chair blocks the shelves. Tavi moves the camera. Now the lamp is behind the chair. Palla asks for a more emotional angle. Omi arranges the books herself, leaving an open gap for the volume Vex misplaced.

VEX: The space makes the room feel calm.

OMI: It is where the missing book goes.

The crew finds the volume in the donation pile, under Vex's clipboard. He says the sort was still in progress. Omi carries it back and places it exactly where it belongs. Neri tests the new lamp and discovers the shade points at the camera instead of the page. The reflected light makes every book spine unreadable on screen.

Palla asks Omi to wait outside while they finish. Omi agrees, but only after she marks the shelf order on a card. Vex turns the card into a reveal prop and pins it on the chair. The cloth slides off; the lamp swings and illuminates the floor. Tavi lifts the lamp, turns it toward the chair, and plugs it into the wall outlet that was hidden behind the set's decorative plant.

Vex tries to create a before-and-after comparison. He puts the chair back in its original spot, then finds that the lamp cord cannot reach the outlet from there. Neri asks him to stop moving the furniture. The crew photographs the room from the doorway, where both the lamp and the bookshelves fit into one frame. Palla calls it a simpler shot. Vex asks if the shot can be more moving. Omi rolls her chair into position and opens a book.

### Act Three

The reveal begins. Palla counts down from five. Omi enters and sees the chair beside the lamp, the shelves restored, and the catalog card on the table. She sits, adjusts the shade so it lights her book, and reaches for her tea. The camera moves to a close-up of her face. Omi turns the lamp toward the page again.

OMI: Now I can read.

Vex asks for a reaction to the color. Omi says she can see the words. Neri explains that the shelves are anchored and the chair has space to move without striking them. Palla asks whether the room feels new. Omi says it feels like the room she requested, with a brighter lamp.

The crew gives her a plant as a finishing touch. Omi finds it blocks the outlet. Vex moves it to the windowsill, where it does not cover anything. Tavi keeps the camera wide, showing the whole reading corner instead of a close-up of the plant.

### Tag

[AFTER THE CREW LEAVES.]

Omi turns a page. The lamp stays aimed at the book. The chair, books, tea and plant are all in their places. Offscreen, Palla asks whether they can film the moment again.

OMI: You can film the next chapter.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
