# The Cooking Contest's Missing Taste Test

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competitions, suspenseful judging edits, and contestants forced to identify dishes by labels after the food is gone*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Dela, a baboon; chef Momo, a macaque; chef Riku, a gibbon; judge Pava, an orangutan; stage manager Tika, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING STUDIO - NIGHT

Two covered bowls sit on the judging table. The host holds a card marked FINAL TASTE. A tiny spoon rests beside it.

DELA: One dish wins the golden ladle. The judges have tasted both.

PAVA: We have not tasted either.

DELA: The edit says you did.

TIKA: That was yesterday's take. The bowls are empty now.

[The judges lift the lids. Both bowls contain only garnish. The chefs look at their own stations, where one pot of soup remains.] 

MOMO: Mine is still on the warmer.

RIKU: Mine is beside the cutting board.

DELA: Then we have a mystery.

[Music swells. The host looks into camera while the stage manager removes the empty bowls.] 

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Tika checks the call sheet. The judges tasted the soups in the previous segment, but the crew cleared them before recording the winner announcement. The two pots are both covered and labeled with masking tape, but the labels are turned toward the wall.

PAVA: I remember tasting ginger, toasted oats, and something smoky.

MOMO: That was mine. The oats were toasted separately.

RIKU: Mine had roasted mushroom and a little plum.

PAVA: I remember plum.

DELA: The suspense is unbearable.

TIKA: We can replate a spoon from each pot and confirm the winner.

DELA: A taste test? On a cooking show?

[The host asks the camera operator for a dramatic close-up of the ladle. The camera operator focuses on the labels instead. One label says M, the other R, but the pot positions have changed.] 

RIKU: I moved mine when the warmer beeped.

MOMO: I swapped stations to get the cutting board.

TIKA: So the letters identify chefs, not locations.

[The stage manager writes the pot positions on a board and keeps each chef beside the correct pot. The host asks whether the judges can identify the dishes blind. Pava agrees, if nobody adds music between bites.] 

ACT TWO

INT. JUDGING TABLE - LATER

Pava tastes the first soup from a fresh spoon. The host watches for a reaction. Pava's eyebrows rise. Dela signals the camera operator to zoom in. Pava raises one hand to pause the shot.

PAVA: I am swallowing.

DELA: The reaction is arriving.

PAVA: The soup is warm. Give me a moment.

[The host tries to stretch the pause with a recap. Tika turns off the recap monitor. Riku and Momo stand at their stations, unable to see which bowl is being served.] 

PAVA: This one has roasted mushroom, plum, and black pepper. Riku's soup.

RIKU: Correct.

[Pava tastes the second spoon. Momo watches the judge rather than the camera.]

PAVA: Ginger, toasted oats, and smoked pepper. Momo's soup.

MOMO: Correct. I served the oats separately because they soften quickly.

DELA: Both answers are correct. We need a winner.

TIKA: The judging card says flavor, texture, and temperature. Pava gave each dish a score on all three.

[The host reaches for a score card but picks up a sponsor coupon. Tika hands over the correct card. Pava compares the totals: Momo has one point more for texture; Riku has one point more for temperature. Flavor is tied.] 

ACT THREE

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - NIGHT

Pava explains that the combined scores are tied. The rules say the winner is the dish with the higher flavor score; if flavor is also tied, the chefs share the prize. Dela starts to ask for a sudden-death garnish challenge. Tika points to the printed rule card.

DELA: The card is very clear.

TIKA: It was clear before the recap, too.

RIKU: We can share the golden ladle.

MOMO: We can alternate weeks.

PAVA: The award goes to both chefs. The dishes were distinct, and both met the brief.

[The host lifts the golden ladle. It is attached to the table by a display cord. Tika unclips it and gives each chef one handle to hold for the photo.] 

DELA: A shared win, decided by the actual rules.

MOMO: And an actual taste test.

RIKU: Can we keep our soups warm now?

TIKA: Yes. The cameras are off.

[The chefs return to their stations. The host tries to announce a final twist, then sees the crew packing the lights and stops.] 

TAG

Dela tastes one remaining spoonful from each pot and looks at Pava for a score. Pava points at the dishes, then at the shut-off camera.

FINAL IMAGE: The chefs carry their own labeled pots away while the golden ladle rests between them on the counter.
