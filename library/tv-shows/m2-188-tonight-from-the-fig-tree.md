# Tonight from the Fig Tree

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk show convention, where celebrity interviews, desk bits and musical guests are staged around an uncooperative tree*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A talk show broadcasts from a fig tree after the studio desk cannot fit through the grove entrance.

Cast: Host Piri, a capuchin; sidekick Kito, a macaque; guest Miri, a gibbon arborist; and bandleader Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "Live at Five Leaves"

COLD OPEN

EXT. FIG TREE, DUSK

Piri sits behind a small desk propped against the trunk. A branch drops a fig into the cup beside him.

PIRI: Our first sponsor is the tree.

Kito reaches for the fig. A leaf lands on his head.

KITO: The tree has notes.

The band plays a short intro. A bird answers from the canopy. Odo stops the band.

ODO: We have competition.

ACT ONE

Piri introduces Miri, an arborist who is there to inspect the tree. He asks whether the tree has been preparing for its television debut. Miri says she is checking for a damaged limb. Kito asks if the branch is nervous. Miri taps it gently and explains that it is healthy, but the desk is leaning against it.

Piri asks the crew to move the desk. They lift it, but a leg catches in the roots. The audience volunteers to help. The desk comes free and rolls downhill. Odo catches it before it hits the band.

PIRI: Our desk has made a dramatic exit.

MIRI: It was a hazard.

Piri tries an interview while standing. He asks Miri what advice she would give a young tree. Miri says it needs room to grow and should not carry a desk. Kito adds that the tree should hire an agent.

ACT TWO

EXT. TREE, LATER

The show moves to a flat rock. Piri announces a comedy segment: "Things the Tree Has Dropped." Kito lists a fig, a leaf, and the desk. The audience laughs at the desk. Piri says the tree has an excellent sense of timing.

Miri checks the branch again and finds a small cluster of fruit ready to fall. She places a basket beneath it. The camera operator asks if the basket can be branded. Miri says it should remain clear so the fruit lands safely. Piri asks if the basket has a name. Kito suggests "the guest bucket."

Odo starts a musical number. A bird lands on the basket handle, shifting it. Miri steadies the basket. Odo changes the song to a softer rhythm. The bird chirps along, and the band follows its pattern.

Piri interviews the bird by asking whether it enjoys the show. The bird flies away. Kito says it has left a review.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. FIG TREE, EARLY EVENING

Piri takes questions from the studio audience. A resident asks how the crew plans to keep the desk dry if rain comes. Miri points out that the desk is still leaning against the tree. Kito suggests bringing it indoors. The desk's leg is wedged in a root, so the band helps lift it free without scraping the bark.

Odo asks the bird to return for a musical encore. The bird remains in the canopy. The band tries to copy its call, but each instrument produces a different note. Miri says the bird is not a musical guest; it is nesting nearby. The crew moves the lights farther away.

Piri asks if the show can continue without a desk. Miri says a host can stand. Kito places a small stool on the flat rock. The stool is stable, but when Piri sits, the microphone cable loops around his foot. He stands and unwinds it carefully.

The camera operator frames the stream and tree. Piri's closing questions are interrupted by the actual sounds of the grove: water, leaves and a branch creak. Odo plays softly beneath them. The bird answers once, then falls quiet. Piri waits rather than speaking over it.

PIRI: That is a good place to stop.

ACT THREE


EXT. TREE, NIGHT

Piri closes the show. The desk is now on stable ground, away from the trunk. Miri finishes her inspection and says the tree is healthy. The crew thanks the tree by removing the lights from its branches. A final fig drops into the basket.

PIRI: Our guest has given us a parting gift.

MIRI: The fruit was ripe.

Piri offers it to the audience. Everyone shares it. Odo plays the closing music. The bird calls once from above, ending the tune.

TAG

EXT. TREE, NEXT DAY

Piri returns to the tree with a small stool. Kito places it carefully on the rock.

KITO: The tree has requested no desk.

PIRI: The tree is not the producer.

A leaf drops onto the stool. Piri moves it before sitting.
