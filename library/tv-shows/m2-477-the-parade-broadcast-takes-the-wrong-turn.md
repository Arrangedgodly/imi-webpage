# The Parade Broadcast Takes the Wrong Turn

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live parade coverage, commentators who narrate a route from outdated maps, and producers who cannot interrupt a moving float*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Anchor Luma, a macaque; commentator Horo, a baboon; route marshal Seli, an orangutan; camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; float driver Peko, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. CITY PARADE ROUTE - DAY

A flower float approaches an intersection. The anchor points to a map on the broadcast table. The commentator watches the float through binoculars.

LUMA: The float is approaching the north turn.

SELI: It is approaching the south turn.

HORO: Our map says north.

[The float pauses at the intersection. The driver checks the route marshal's sign.] 

PEKO: The arrow points south.

[The anchor flips the map; it is upside down. The opening music continues while the crowd waits.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. INTERSECTION - CONTINUOUS

Seli holds up the current route card and directs the float south along the barrier. Tavi pans the camera to show the card and the float's direction. Horo continues describing the old route from last year's map.

HORO: We now see the float turning north.

TAVI: It is turning south.

LUMA: The current route card says south.

[The commentator checks his notes. His script calls the float a boat. Luma crosses out the word and hands the paper back.] 

HORO: The boat is now proceeding along dry pavement.

LUMA: It is a float.

HORO: The float is proceeding along dry pavement.

[The crowd applauds. The driver follows the marshal's signal and clears the corner. The camera operator keeps the road and float in view; the anchor lays the outdated map aside.] 

ACT TWO

EXT. BROADCAST PLATFORM - LATER

A second float approaches. The route marshal checks the street clearance. The commentator asks whether the first float should be replayed in slow motion so the audience can see the turn. The anchor says the first float has already passed and the broadcast is live.

LUMA: We will not replay the turn. The next float is here.

HORO: The flower boat returns.

TAVI: No, the next float is a drum stage.

[The second float's drummers strike a short rhythm. Horo tries to talk over the sound, but the anchor waits until the performance ends. The route marshal signals the drummers to continue moving.] 

SELI: The intersection is clear. Keep rolling.

[The driver moves the float forward at walking pace. A confetti strip catches on the wheel guard. Seli signals a stop in the marked holding space. Peko clears the strip without stepping into the crowd lane; Tavi keeps camera on the stopped float until it moves again.] 

LUMA: The float is stopped for a safe clearance.

HORO: Our parade has a brief pause.

[The confetti strip is removed and discarded in the crew bin. The marshal checks the route ahead and signals the float onward.] 

[The anchor switches to a street camera to check the next turn. Seli holds up a reflective route marker for the camera and points the driver toward the clear lane. Horo reads a sponsor card for a moment, then realizes the crowd cannot hear over the drums.] 

LUMA: We will wait until the music ends before speaking.

HORO: The route is open; the float can continue.

[The drum float moves past the broadcast stand. Tavi follows it with a wide shot so the route marshal stays in frame. The anchor checks the updated map against the intersection sign. Both show the same direction.] 

LUMA: The current map and the marshal agree.

SELI: That is the route we are following.

[The anchor moves the old map beneath the desk and pins the corrected one to the top.] 

HORO: Our viewers can now see the same turn the driver sees.

[The camera assistant wipes a spot of confetti from the lens. The broadcast stays live as the parade reaches the finish, and the commentator holds the binoculars down rather than calling a replay.] 

ACT THREE

EXT. PARADE FINISH - AFTERNOON

The flower float reaches the finish line by the current route. The anchor and commentator summarize the route using the corrected map. Horo identifies the south turn correctly. Luma points out that the route map on the broadcast table has been rotated and updated.

LUMA: The parade completed the listed route.

HORO: The flower float turned south at the square.

SELI: Correct.

[The driver parks in the designated staging area and switches off the engine. The drummers finish their set. The crew waits for the route marshal to clear the lane before crossing to the platform.] 

TAVI: One route, one finish, no replay needed.

[The anchor removes the outdated map from the table and folds the current card. The commentator packs away his binoculars.] 

TAG

[Horo looks at the map upside down again. Luma turns it right side up and points to the finish, then the anchor ends the broadcast.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The flower float rests in the staging area while the route marshal folds the correct map.
