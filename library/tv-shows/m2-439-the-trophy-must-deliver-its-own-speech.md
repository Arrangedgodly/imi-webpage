# The Trophy Must Deliver Its Own Speech

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: awards broadcasts that give the trophy more camera time than the winners*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Runi, a baboon; winner Nari, a lemur; presenter Vex, a macaque; stage manager Pip, a capuchin; announcer Sela, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. AWARDS STAGE - NIGHT

[A gold cup sits on a pedestal. Runi turns it toward the camera. Vex and Nari wait in the wings.] 

RUNI: The award is ready.

PIP: The winner has not arrived.

[The spotlight stays on the cup. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

RUNI: Welcome to the Annual Acorn Awards, honoring achievement, effort, and dramatic pauses.

SELA: The winner of Best Orchard Plan is Nari.

[Nari steps out. Vex reaches for the cup first, then notices her hand.] 

VEX: I was making sure it was secure.

NARI: It is a trophy.

PIP: It has a loose handle.

[The handle turns as Nari lifts it. She puts it back on the pedestal. Runi asks for applause. The audience claps. The trophy wobbles in time.] 

RUNI: A standing ovation.

NARI: The audience is seated.

RUNI: We have room to grow.

ACT TWO

[Vex introduces the nominee reel. The clip shows Nari planning irrigation rows, then ends on a shot of a watering can. The can drips onto the trophy's base.] 

NARI: The water is spilling.

PIP: It is on the pedestal.

[He lifts the trophy. A damp ring remains beneath it.] 

VEX: The trophy has left a mark.

RUNI: A lasting impression.

[Nari examines the base. The award has a small felt pad that is peeling. She presses it down. Vex turns the cup toward camera again.] 

NARI: It is upside down.

VEX: The logo faces the audience.

[The cup's handle blocks the logo. Runi rotates it. The pedestal rotates too, dragging the damp ring across the stage.] 

PIP: The award is signing the floor.

[Nari tries to speak into the microphone. Vex passes her an oversized cue card with a long acceptance speech.] 

NARI: I have one sentence.

VEX: We have airtime.

NARI: The new irrigation plan keeps water in the orchard rows.

RUNI: Can you thank the sponsor?

NARI: The sponsor gave us the hose.

VEX: And the trophy.

PIP: The trophy is not in the orchard.

[Sela interviews the other nominees beside the stage. One says the orchard plan should win; another points to the trophy's loose handle.]

SELA: What do you think makes an award meaningful?

PIP: It should stay together until the winner gets it.

VEX: It should look good on the broadcast.

NARI: It should remind us what we did.

[The crew rolls out a second pedestal for the next category. It has four wheels, one of them unlocked. Pip holds it steady while Sela puts a sandbag behind the wheel.]

PIP: We have secured the stage.

RUNI: That is our next award.

NARI: For stage safety?

VEX: We have not announced the category.

[The cue cards list "Best Dramatic Pause." Runi asks the nominees to hold a pause while the camera changes lenses. Nari sets the trophy on the pedestal; the cup wobbles again.]

NARI: The handle is stable. The base is not.

[She shifts the felt pad beneath it. The cup stops moving. The camera operator finishes the lens change.]

RUNI: That was the best pause.

VEX: The award goes to the felt pad.

ACT THREE

[The trophy's loose handle falls sideways. Nari catches it before it hits the floor.] 

NARI: This needs a screw.

VEX: The award is showing vulnerability.

[She fixes the handle with a small tool from her pocket. The crowd claps. The cup stops wobbling.] 

RUNI: We have a repair montage.

NARI: I tightened one screw.

[Vex asks her to repeat the action for a camera close-up. Nari hands the tool to Pip instead.] 

NARI: He can do it. He is stage manager.

PIP: I am holding the curtain.

[The curtain starts to fall. Pip lets go with one hand, catches the fabric, and the trophy slides on the pedestal. Nari steadies the base. Vex reaches for the cup, but stops when Sela shakes her head.] 

SELA: Let the winner hold it.

[Nari lifts the trophy again. It is stable now. She faces the audience, not the camera.] 

NARI: Thank you. The orchard gets the water. The cup stays here.

[The audience applauds. Runi tries to hand her the microphone for a longer speech. Nari sets the trophy on the floor beside the stage and returns to help Pip with the curtain.] 

VEX: The winner has left the award.

NARI: I will pick it up after the curtain is fixed.

TAG

[The camera zooms in on the trophy. Sela moves it out of frame to show the stage crew lifting the curtain.] 

RUNI: Best supporting trophy.

PIP: It has no speech.
