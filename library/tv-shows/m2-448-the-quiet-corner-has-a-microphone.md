# The Quiet Corner Has a Microphone

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-library makeover programs that add equipment to places designed to be quiet*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Runi, a gibbon; librarian Mara, a macaque; volunteer Nari, a lemur; sound technician Vex, an orangutan; reader Pip, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. LIBRARY - MORNING

[A sign reads QUIET CORNER. Pip sits beneath it, turning a page. A microphone lowers from above.] 

PIP: Is this for the book?

VEX: It is for the quiet.

[The microphone squeals. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

RUNI: Welcome to Book Nook Reveal, where we turn a quiet corner into a production set.

MARA: This is where people read.

RUNI: We are installing soft lighting, a sound meter, and a page-turn camera.

NARI: Why would a page need a camera?

VEX: Readers want to see the page turn.

[Pip opens a book. The camera follows the page. It zooms too close and shows only a letter.] 

PIP: The story is under the camera.

MARA: The camera needs to move.

[The crew rolls in a padded chair. It blocks the shelf. A patron reaches for a book and bumps the chair.]

NARI: The quiet corner is full of equipment.

ACT TWO

[The team tests the sound meter. It reacts to the microphone, the fluorescent lights, and Pip's slow page turn. Vex suggests putting the meter outside the room.] 

VEX: We can measure the quiet from the hall.

MARA: That measures the hall.

[The microphone hangs above the chair. Runi asks Pip to read silently while the camera captures his expression. He becomes aware of the lens and freezes.] 

PIP: I cannot read while being watched.

RUNI: The audience is watching you read.

PIP: The audience is not above my head.

[Vex adjusts the microphone. It swings toward the shelf and catches a dust cover. A row of books tilts outward.] 

MARA: Stop the arm.

[Vex stops it. Nari slides the books back into place. She notices the shelf is too high for Pip and moves the padded chair to reach the top row.] 

NARI: This chair is useful here.

RUNI: We can move the lighting to the other side.

[The crew shifts the set. The lamp shines directly on the books and reflects off their plastic covers.] 

PIP: The glare is louder than the room.

MARA: We can take the covers off.

[They remove one plastic cover. The book opens easily. The microphone picks up a single page turn; Vex celebrates.] 

VEX: We have captured the sound of reading.

[Mara opens the library doors for a short test. Two readers approach the quiet corner. One carries a thick book; the other has a thin booklet.]

RUNI: Please tell us what you think of the new space.

[The first reader sits in the padded chair. Its casters roll toward the shelf. Nari wedges a rubber doorstop beneath one wheel.]

NARI: It is quiet now.

[The second reader reaches for a book in the top row. Pip brings over the step stool. The sound meter needle jumps as he sets it down.]

VEX: That counts against the room.

MARA: He is helping a reader.

[The reader takes the book and thanks Pip in a whisper. The meter jumps again.]

VEX: We have a second sound.

PIP: A thank-you.

RUNI: The room is working.

[The camera microphone swings down to capture the thank-you. It taps the top shelf, knocking loose a bookmark. Nari catches it and returns it to the book.]

NARI: The equipment needs a quiet corner of its own.

[They park the sound-meter cart outside the library. The overhead boom microphone stays in place, switched to standby. Through the doorway, the camera sees readers turning pages without a meter in the room.]

MARA: That is the room we wanted.

RUNI: The reveal is the quiet.

ACT THREE

[The library opens. Readers enter the quiet corner. Runi introduces the camera and asks for a silent reading sample. A reader sits down and begins to turn pages.] 

MARA: The overhead microphone is still on.

VEX: It is recording the quiet.

[A phone rings in the hallway. Everyone looks toward it. Vex's meter spikes. Pip reaches up to the hanging microphone cable and switches it off.] 

PIP: We can hear the book without measuring it.

[The readers settle. The padded chair is now beside the high shelf. The camera has been moved out of the corner, pointed at an empty book stand.] 

NARI: Is the makeover finished?

RUNI: The corner is still quiet.

MARA: And the books are reachable.

[The librarian shows Runi the new sign: QUIET CORNER - PLEASE READ. The camera operator sets the light aside.]

VEX: We have one final shot.

MARA: No.

[He lowers the lens. A reader turns a page. The microphone remains off.]

TAG

[Runi whispers toward the camera.] 

RUNI: A quiet success.

[The equipment cart's wheel squeaks as it rolls away.] 

PIP: The cart is still on the show.
