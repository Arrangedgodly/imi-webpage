# The Farm Report Confuses the Goat with the Host

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live farm broadcasts whose lower-third labels keep following the camera instead of the interview subject*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Kavi, a lemur; farmer Dena, an orangutan; reporter Rali, a macaque; graphics operator Poro, a capuchin; goat Button, a goat.

COLD OPEN

EXT. FARMYARD - MORNING

Rali interviews Dena beside a fence. The screen labels Dena as BUTTON, FARM GOAT.

RALI: How old is Button?

DENA: I am Dena.

[Button bleats off camera.]

ACT ONE

EXT. FARMYARD - CONTINUOUS

Poro says the labels follow whichever person is nearest the lens. Kavi asks the crew to switch them. The camera moves to Button. The screen labels the goat as DENA, FARMER. Rali tries again, asking Dena about the spring pasture. Button walks between them. The label switches to Rali.

DENA: The pasture is ready.

KAVI: Thank you, Rali.

Rali asks Poro to freeze the labels. He freezes the picture too. The camera stops recording. Button chews the grass beside the monitor.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL VAN - LATER

Poro checks the name graphics. They are linked to face tracking. The goats keep crossing between the camera and the guests. He switches to manual labels. The label stays on Dena when the camera pans to Button. Kavi reads the farmer's name while Button fills the screen.

[Button nudges the tripod. The image tilts. Poro corrects the camera, and the label tracks the movement to the fence post.]

Rali asks Dena how many goats are in the pasture. Dena answers four. The camera counts three, then a fourth goat appears behind the hay cart. The graphic displays FIVE because the face tracker counted Dena. Poro turns off the count overlay. Kavi asks Button to demonstrate eating grass. Button walks away from the camera, leaving the host to interview a bale of hay.

INT. FARMYARD - MOMENTS LATER

Dena and Rali stand in front of the fence. Poro types their names into separate lower-thirds. Kavi introduces Dena correctly. The graphic shows Rali's name under the farmer. Poro swaps the fields. Now the title reads RALI, FARMER. Dena points to the button on the screen. Poro clicks it; the text changes to BUTTON, REPORTER.

INT. CONTROL VAN - MOMENTS LATER

Poro turns off automatic labels and tests the lower-thirds on a monitor. He types DENA and RALI in separate boxes. The names stay attached to their positions, even when Button walks across the frame. Kavi asks the control room to show the pasture count. Poro leaves the count off until Dena confirms it. Rali checks the gate latch with Dena. Button noses it again, but it stays clipped. The host waits for the farmer to finish speaking before adding a question. Dena says the gate is secure. The camera holds on the clip instead of switching to the goat's face. Button wanders back into frame and chews the edge of the FARMER card. Poro lifts it out of reach and keeps the name graphic accurate. Kavi finishes the report by thanking Dena and Rali by name. Poro saves the manual labels, and the goat's caption remains blank until the farmer identifies her.

ACT THREE

EXT. FARMYARD - AFTERNOON

Poro uses static caption cards held by the camera assistant. One reads FARMER DENA; one reads REPORTER RALI. They sit below the frame, each person holding their own card for the introduction. Kavi asks Dena about the pasture. Rali holds the reporter card and asks the question. Button enters and takes Dena's card in her teeth.

DENA: I will need that back.

Button lets go. The card is damp but readable. Poro places it on the fence rail. The camera frames Button with no name. Kavi says the goat is Button. Rali confirms. Poro adds a small caption only after Dena points to the animal.

The farm report continues. Dena explains that the new fence keeps the goats out of the seed bed. Button tests the fence gate with her nose. It opens slightly. Dena closes it and clips the latch. Rali asks whether Button often checks the gate. Dena says she does when the crew is nearby. Kavi starts to call it a breakthrough. Button turns away to eat.

TAG

INT. CONTROL VAN - AFTER THE REPORT

Poro saves the labels as manual text. Kavi checks the names on each card. Rali wipes the damp edge of Dena's sign. Dena walks the goats back toward the pasture. Rali checks the frame before the closing shot. Button stays behind the latched gate, and the name cards remain with the people who spoke.

FINAL IMAGE: The screen displays FARMER DENA and REPORTER RALI correctly, while Button stands beyond the latched gate without a label.
