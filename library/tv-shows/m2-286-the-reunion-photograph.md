# The Reunion Photograph

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sentimental family reunion specials, where the host insists on recreating an old group photo despite changed family positions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A reunion crew recreates a decades-old family photograph, but the family members no longer fit the original poses or remember who stood where.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; matriarch Nara, macaque; cousin Odo, baboon; cousin Piri, gibbon; photographer Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Stand Where You Were"

### Cold Open

[OLD PHOTOGRAPH ON A BOARD. FIVE MONKEYS IN A ROW.]

SELI: We will recreate this picture.

NARA: The tree is gone.

ODO: I was shorter.

PIRI: I was not in the picture.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE FAMILY GATHERS IN THE YARD.]

Tavi places the old photograph on an easel. Seli asks each relative to match a pose. Nara points out that the old picture was taken beside a tree that has since been removed.

SELI: We can use the fence.

NARA: The fence is in the wrong place.

ODO: I can stand on a box.

PIRI: I can stand behind Odo.

TAVI: Then I cannot see you.

Seli studies the photograph. She says the most important feature is the family looking toward the camera. Nara says they were looking at a bird in the original.

### Act Two

[THE CREW TRIES THE FIRST POSE.]

Odo stands in the center. Nara takes his old spot. Piri holds a small branch to represent the missing tree. The branch blocks Seli's face.

SELI: The tree is taking my place.

NARA: It is a branch.

ODO: The picture has changed.

Tavi asks them to match the old positions exactly. Odo sits on a stool, but the stool is too low. Nara kneels; her shadow covers Piri.

PIRI: I am in the dark.

SELI: Move toward the light.

PIRI: Then I am not in the original spot.

Tavi takes a test photo. Everyone looks serious. The old photo shows them laughing.

[THE CREW REVIEWS THE OLD PHOTOGRAPH.]

Seli asks each relative where they were standing. Nara points to the left edge. Odo says he was behind the tree. Piri says she was not born yet.

ODO: You were represented by the branch.

PIRI: I was not a branch.

TAVI: The camera timer went off before everyone was ready.

Nara remembers that Odo had been holding the camera while Tavi ran into the picture. The old image was taken by accident when Odo dropped the shutter button.

SELI: That is why everyone is looking away.

NARA: We were watching the camera fall.

Odo says the recreation should include a falling camera. Tavi refuses to drop it. They try setting the timer and placing the camera on a low wall.

The timer is too short. Piri is still walking when it clicks. The photo catches her halfway through the frame.

PIRI: I am in this one.

SELI: You are moving.

NARA: That is how you arrived at the reunion.

Tavi checks the image. Everyone is laughing, and Piri is blurred but visible.

ODO: It is not like the original.

TAVI: It is like this afternoon.

[THE FAMILY SETS UP THE TIMER AGAIN.]

Tavi puts the camera on the wall and sets a longer delay. Odo asks everyone to hold their positions. Piri says she wants to stand closer to Nara this time.

SELI: The old picture has a gap between you.

PIRI: The old picture was before I arrived.

NARA: The new one can include the whole family.

They move together. Odo stands behind the branch, then steps to the side so he will be visible. The camera timer counts down. A bird lands on the fence; everyone looks toward it.

[SFX: TIMER BEEP, CAMERA SHUTTER.]

Tavi checks the image. It shows everyone in the frame and the bird on the fence.

TAVI: This one has the missing tree's substitute.

ODO: The bird was not in the old photo.

NARA: It is here now.

### Act Three

[THE FAMILY DISCUSSES THE ORIGINAL MOMENT.]

Nara remembers that the bird flew away just before the picture. Odo says he was trying to catch it. Piri points out that she was not born yet. Nara squints at the old photograph and discovers she has been remembering the younger Tavi as Piri.

TAVI: We can take a new photo.

SELI: The special is about recreating the old one.

NARA: We cannot recreate the old day.

Seli asks everyone to stand where they naturally would. Odo steps beside Nara. Piri stands near Tavi. They begin to laugh about the old picture.

Tavi sets the timer again and joins the family. The shutter catches them looking at one another rather than the camera.

### Tag

[THE NEW PHOTO IS PLACED BESIDE THE OLD ONE.]

Seli says the recreation is complete. Nara says it is a different picture.

SELI: It is a family picture.

ODO: The tree is still missing.

PIRI: We could plant one.

[SFX: CAMERA SHUTTER, BIRDS.]
