# The Museum Curator's Big Reveal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: museum documentary television, where a curator stages a dramatic reveal of a newly cleaned stone*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A curator plans a grand reveal for a stone from the riverbank, but visitors keep identifying it as the rock they already know.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; curator Pava, gibbon; conservator Nara, macaque; guard Odo, baboon; visitor Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Stone Under the Cloth"

### Cold Open

[MUSEUM DISPLAY. A STONE IS COVERED BY A CLOTH.]

PAVA: This artifact has been hidden for centuries.

TAVI: I saw it in the river yesterday.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[SELI STANDS BESIDE THE DISPLAY.]

Pava says the stone was recovered from the river and cleaned for exhibition. Nara says the stone was found near the path and has no known age. Odo says visitors must not touch it.

SELI: What makes it important?

PAVA: It is part of the river's history.

NARA: It is a stone.

TAVI: It is flat on one side.

Pava asks for a dramatic countdown. Seli counts from five. Tavi says it is the same stone he used to skip across the water.

PAVA: That cannot be confirmed.

NARA: The stone has no marks.

Odo removes the cloth. The stone is gray and smooth.

### Act Two

[THE VISITORS LOOK AT THE DISPLAY.]

One visitor asks if the stone was used as a tool. Nara says they do not know. Pava says it may have been part of an ancient trail marker. Odo asks her not to speculate.

PAVA: We can say it could have been.

NARA: We can say it was found by the river.

Seli asks the camera to show the label. It reads "Unknown stone, origin uncertain." Pava says the label lacks drama.

Tavi points out a small scratch on the bottom. Nara examines it; the scratch is a recent mark from the display stand.

ODO: We have new evidence.

NARA: We have a scratch from the stand.

Pava asks if the stone can be polished. Nara says no; cleaning further may damage its surface.

[THE CURATOR PREPARES A SECOND DISPLAY.]

Pava wants a dramatic lighting reveal. Nara says the stone's surface should not be heated by the display lamp. Odo tests the light with his hand and finds it warm.

PAVA: Can we use a lower setting?

NARA: Yes, if the case stays cool.

Tavi takes a reading. The display is still too warm. They move the lamp farther away, but the stone's shadow disappears.

SELI: The reveal is less dramatic now.

PAVA: The stone is still visible.

NARA: We should protect the object.

Odo adds a small reflector to direct existing light into the case. The stone remains visible without the hot lamp.

TAVI: The display is balanced.

Pava asks Nara to explain the stone's uncertain history. Nara says it was found near the river, borrowed from the nature club, and has no proven age or purpose.

PAVA: Can we call it an ancient river stone?

NARA: We can call it a stone found by the river.

SELI: That is less exciting.

NARA: It is what we know.

### Act Three

[THE CURATOR WRITES A NEW LABEL.]

Pava proposes: "The river's silent witness." Nara says that is a poetic title, not a fact. They agree to add a note explaining that visitors may recognize the stone from the riverbank.

Tavi asks if the museum can return it. Odo says the exhibit was borrowed from the local nature club. Seli checks the loan card and reads that it belongs back at the stream after one week.

PAVA: Then the reveal is temporary.

NARA: The stone was never lost.

[THE TEAM CHECKS THE LOAN AGREEMENT.]

Pava asks whether the stone can be displayed beside a photo.

NARA: A photo would show where it was found.

ODO: The photo can be dramatic.

SELI: We can show the river without claiming the stone is ancient.

Pava agrees to use a simple image of the bank. Odo helps place the label where visitors can read it without leaning over the case.

TAVI: The display is ready.

PAVA: The reveal has a context.

NARA: The context is the river.

They stand back from the case. The stone is visible, the label is clear, and the lamp stays cool.

### Tag

[ONE WEEK LATER, THE DISPLAY CASE IS EMPTY.]

Seli says the borrowed artifact has returned to its original place. Tavi picks up a second stone from the bank.

TAVI: Is this one the exhibit?

NARA: No.

Pava reaches for the cloth, but Nara stops her and checks the empty case latch. Odo reads the returned loan card aloud, then turns it over to reveal a blank side.

ODO: The rock has no sequel.

PAVA: The river may disagree.

PAVA: We may need a new episode.

[SFX: STONE SKIPS ACROSS WATER.]
