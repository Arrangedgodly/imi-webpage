# The Reveal Behind the Reveal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation reality shows with surprise walls, impossible schedules, and repeated room reveals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A renovation crew promises one dramatic reveal, but the homeowners keep discovering that the new wall blocks the only safe route to their sleeping branches.

Cast: Host Della, a gibbon; builder Mako, an orangutan; homeowner Suri, a macaque; designer Fenn, a capuchin; camera assistant Tavi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. TREEHOUSE - MORNING

Della stands beside a bare wall marked with blue tape.

DELLA: We will turn this room into a restful reading nook.

SURI: This is where we sleep.

FENN: The reading nook is on the other side.

Mako holds up a wall panel that blocks a branch opening.

MAKO: This is the other side.

ACT ONE

Fenn unfolds a drawing of a built-in shelf. Suri points to an opening in the trunk wall. It is the route she and the young macaques use to reach their sleeping platform.

SURI: The shelf goes here. The opening stays clear.

FENN: The camera's best reveal angle is from that opening.

SURI: You can film from the floor.

DELLA: We promised a surprise transformation.

MAKO: It can be a surprise without blocking the doorway.

Tavi tries to film from the floor, but Della's reveal banner covers the shot. Fenn rotates the design board. The shelf fits below the opening but no longer reaches the painted ceiling stripe.

FENN: The stripe is part of the concept.

SURI: The branch is part of the house.

Della asks the camera assistant for a before shot. Tavi sweeps the camera across the existing room. A sleeping mat, reading cushion and small ladder are already there.

DELLA: Can we move the ladder for the before?

SURI: You can move it for the shot. Put it back before night.

ACT TWO

Mako measures the wall opening. Fenn suggests sliding the shelf three inches to the left. Mako says that would cover a support notch. Della asks whether they can make it look as if the shelf nearly covers it, then reveal the notch later.

MAKO: We should build around the support.

DELLA: Could the support be a surprise?

SURI: It is holding up the platform.

Fenn changes the plan. He sketches a narrow shelf that wraps around the support and leaves the route open. Della asks for a hidden door moment. Suri demonstrates how she climbs through the opening. Her tail catches a measuring tape; she unhooks it with one hand and continues.

TAVI: That was a better reveal than the door.

DELLA: We need a reveal after construction.

MAKO: First we need the construction.

Mako cuts a test panel. It fits, but the paint is a different shade. Fenn tries three swatches. The camera crew films each color as if it were a major decision. Suri chooses the shade closest to the existing bark. Della asks her to hold a swatch beside her face for a reaction shot.

SURI: I don't have a reaction to paint.

DELLA: Could you be surprised?

SURI: I would be surprised if this takes one day.

ACT TWO-B

The shelf is assembled. Mako sets it against the wall, leaving the route and support notch clear. Suri climbs up and tests the opening. Her tail hooks a loose bracket, which she catches before it falls.

SURI: Tighten this one.

MAKO: Good catch.

DELLA: Can you do that after the reveal? The cameras are ready.

SURI: No one should use it before the bracket is secure.

Mako fastens it. Tavi tests the shelf with two books and a small cup. Fenn checks the edges for splinters. Della asks everyone to leave the room so they can film the reveal with a closed door. There is no door. Tavi holds a board across the opening while Della counts down.

DELLA: Three, two, one. Reveal!

Tavi drops the board. The room looks exactly the same except for the shelf. Della waits for cheers. Suri enters, walks to the shelf and places her book on it. Then she climbs to the sleeping platform through the clear opening.

SURI: The route works.

FENN: And the books are reachable.

DELLA: Could we do a second reveal from the other side?

TAVI: There is no other side. It's a tree.

ACT THREE

The crew films one final walk-through. Mako checks the bracket, Suri uses the opening twice, and Fenn places the reading cushion beneath the shelf. Della asks for one line about the transformation.

SURI: We have a shelf, a clear branch route, and the same sleeping place.

DELLA: Can you say it like you just saw it?

SURI: I helped build it.

Della accepts that take. The crew carries out the leftover panel and the measuring tape. Tavi removes the fake reveal frame from the opening. Suri sets her book on the shelf and turns out the work light.

TAG

At the monitor, Della watches the continuous take from ACT THREE: Suri sets the book on the shelf, checks that it stays, then climbs through the opening and settles on the platform.

DELLA: Could we replay the shelf entrance?

MAKO: It is the only entrance.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera holds on the quiet room as Suri's tail curls around the book's corner and keeps it from sliding off the new shelf.
