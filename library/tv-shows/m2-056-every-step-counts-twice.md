# Every Step Counts Twice

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised dance competitions, judges' scorecards, and dramatic rehearsal montages*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A dance team prepares a routine for the Grove Showcase, but the judges penalize them for synchronized tails, unsynchronized tails, and any movement they cannot name.

Cast: Coach Vela, a gibbon; dancer Kimi, a lemur; dancer Rono, a macaque; judge Soro, an orangutan; judge Palla, a capuchin; and host Dax, a mandrill.

## Episode: "The Tail Counts"

### Cold open

INT. SHOWCASE STAGE, NIGHT

[The dancers freeze in a dramatic pose. Their tails point in four different directions.]

DAX: The Grove Showcase is down to its final routine.

SORA: I see a strong finish.

PALLA: I see four tails.

KIMI: We practiced the ending.

RONO: The ending moved.

VELA: The ending is a pose.

DAX: Judges, score?

[Soro raises a card. Palla holds up a card that is upside down.]

PALLA: I give it a six.

DAX: It says nine.

PALLA: The score is still in motion.

### Act One

INT. REHEARSAL ROOM

[Coach Vela draws a formation on the floor with chalk. Kimi and Rono study it.]

VELA: We enter in a line, turn on the beat, and end facing the audience.

KIMI: Which beat?

VELA: The drum.

RONO: The drum has two beats.

VELA: Use the second.

KIMI: What does the first beat do?

VELA: It builds anticipation.

RONO: Our tails move on the first beat.

VELA: Then your tails build anticipation.

[They rehearse. Kimi turns; Rono swings through the formation.]

KIMI: You are in my space.

RONO: I followed the chalk.

VELA: The chalk is the map, not the floor.

RONO: It is drawn on the floor.

KIMI: We can make a wider circle.

VELA: The stage is small.

KIMI: Then we make smaller circles.

RONO: My tail is still in the large circle.

VELA: We will feature the tail.

### Act Two

INT. COMPETITION STAGE

[Dax introduces the routine. A band begins a steady rhythm. The dancers move in a clean pattern.]

DAX: The team has trained for weeks.

SORA: Their footwork is precise.

PALLA: The tails are loose.

[The dancers turn. Kimi's tail brushes a ribbon from the set.]

KIMI: That was part of the routine.

RONO: Was it?

KIMI: It is now.

[They use the ribbon as a prop, looping it through the formation.]

VELA: Keep the beat.

RONO: The beat has changed.

DAX: The band has entered the performance.

SORA: The dancers are adapting.

PALLA: The ribbon is winning.

[The ribbon catches a drumstick. The drummer stops. The dancers continue in silence.]

KIMI: Should we stop?

RONO: The judges are watching.

KIMI: They were watching before.

VELA: Finish the routine.

[The dancers tap their feet and tails against the stage, creating a new rhythm.]

DAX: An unexpected percussive section.

SORA: They have turned a mishap into choreography.

PALLA: They have turned choreography into a mess.

### Score review

INT. JUDGES' BOOTH

[Soro and Palla watch the rehearsal on a monitor. A slow-motion replay freezes Kimi's tail mid-swing.]

SORA: That move had control.

PALLA: It had a tail.

SORA: The tail is part of the dancer.

PALLA: The score sheet has no box for tail.

SORA: Add a box.

[Palla draws a box. It covers the score.]

PALLA: Now I have nowhere to write.

SORA: Write on the back.

PALLA: The back is for the bonus round.

SORA: There is a bonus round?

PALLA: There is now.

[They rewind the footage. Rono appears upside down.]

PALLA: The dancer is falling.

SORA: It is a turn.

PALLA: I will score the turn.

SORA: You have to score the whole routine.

PALLA: Then the routine needs a larger scorecard.

### Act Three

INT. JUDGES' TABLE

[The dancers bow. The band tries to restart the song, but the drummer's stick is still caught in the ribbon.]

DAX: Judges, your final scores.

SORA: Nine for timing and teamwork.

PALLA: Seven for ribbon control.

VELA: The ribbon was not in the original routine.

PALLA: It was in the performance.

KIMI: What is the total?

DAX: Sixteen.

RONO: Out of?

DAX: The scoring system is still being explained.

SORA: The team wins the showcase.

PALLA: The ribbon receives an honorable mention.

VELA: We will keep the ending pose.

KIMI: Which direction should our tails point?

PALLA: The direction that doesn't hit the lights.

RONO: That is a useful note.

DAX: The dancers have earned a place in the final.

KIMI: We already performed the final.

DAX: The final is next week's show.

VELA: Then we will practice the same routine.

PALLA: We will judge it again.

RONO: We may point our tails differently.

SORA: That is growth.

### Tag

INT. REHEARSAL ROOM, LATER

[The team repeats its ending pose. All tails point in the same direction and knock over the chalk.]

VELA: We are synchronized.

KIMI: The floor has moved.

[The dancers applaud the chalk.]
