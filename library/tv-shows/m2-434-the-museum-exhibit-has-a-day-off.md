# The Museum Exhibit Has a Day Off

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: workplace mockumentaries that give institutional procedures to inanimate exhibits*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Director Pava, a lemur; guard Vex, a macaque; curator Tavi, a gibbon; intern Pip, a capuchin; sculptor Runi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. CITY MUSEUM - MORNING

[A clay statue of a seated monkey faces a bench. A paper sign on the plinth reads "OUT TO LUNCH." Pip checks his watch.] 

PIP: The statue is not due back until one.

VEX: It has never moved.

[The statue's clay hand drops an inch. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

PAVA: Welcome to Museum Day, the documentary about the people who keep history in one place.

TAVI: The sculpture was made by Runi for the new gallery.

RUNI: It was supposed to be outside.

TAVI: The weather was bad.

RUNI: It is made of clay.

[Vex shows the sign to camera.] 

VEX: We do not know why the exhibit requested a lunch break.

PIP: The sign came from the staff room. It says "BACK AT ONE."

PAVA: We have a mystery.

[The camera turns toward the statue. Its hand is lower than before. Runi examines the clay.] 

RUNI: The clay is still soft. The arm is sagging.

VEX: It has had a long morning.

ACT TWO

[The crew decides to stage a staff interview beside the statue. Pava asks Vex about visitor flow. A child enters and copies the statue's seated pose. Another visitor sits on the bench beside it.] 

PAVA: The exhibit is connecting with its audience.

TAVI: It is a bench. Visitors sit there.

[The child offers the statue a banana. Pip tries to explain that it is clay. Vex steps in to protect the work; his badge catches the banana peel.] 

VEX: I have received the offering.

RUNI: Put it in the compost.

PAVA: Can we keep it in frame? The statue's lunch has arrived.

[The camera assistant places a small tray under the clay hand. The hand's sag increases, lowering the tray onto the bench.] 

PIP: It is moving again.

TAVI: Gravity is moving it.

VEX: The exhibit has left the plinth.

[The statue stays on the plinth; only the clay wrist has bent. Runi supports the arm with a temporary foam block.] 

RUNI: I need it to cure overnight.

PAVA: We can film the repair as an emotional return.

RUNI: The arm just needs support.

[The crew interviews Runi. Pava asks how it feels to give the statue a day off. Runi answers that the clay must dry. Vex holds the paper sign beside it.] 

VEX: Does it want the same hours tomorrow?

RUNI: The studio is closed tomorrow.

[After the interview, the crew follows Runi to the loading door. The statue is still in the gallery, but the camera frames it so the door seems far away.]

PAVA: Are we leaving the exhibit behind?

RUNI: I am checking whether it fits through the door.

[Vex clears the hall. Pip rolls the plinth a short distance; one caster squeaks. A visitor imitates the squeak by making a small sound.]

PIP: The gallery is speaking.

TAVI: The wheel needs oil.

[The visitor sits on the bench and copies the clay figure's pose. Runi adjusts the statue's elbow by a fraction. The visitor mirrors the adjustment.]

RUNI: That is why the pose works. It invites people to sit.

PAVA: Can we film the moment they connect?

TAVI: They are both sitting quietly.

PAVA: Exactly.

ACT THREE

[At one, Pip removes the sign. The statue's hand is now held in place by the foam support. Vex announces that the exhibit has returned to work.]

VEX: The gallery is open again.

TAVI: The gallery never closed.

[Visitors pass by. One places a banana on the bench. Runi picks it up and gives it to Pip.] 

RUNI: This one is for the staff.

PAVA: We have a warm, human ending.

RUNI: We are monkeys.

[The crew moves the bench to make more room. The statue remains on its plinth. Pip places the "BACK AT ONE" sign on the bench. A visitor reads it and sits down.] 

VEX: The bench is taking a break.

TAVI: The bench is still here.

[The visitor opens a book. Vex tries to interview them, but they turn a page without looking up.] 

PAVA: We have lost the subject.

PIP: It has found the exhibit.

[Pava asks the gallery visitors to vote on whether the statue should return outside. One visitor points to the bench; another points to the clay figure.]

PAVA: We have a divided audience.

TAVI: They are choosing where to sit.

[Runi rolls the plinth back beside the bench. The squeaky caster quiets when the wheel turns forward.]

RUNI: It can stay here until the clay is dry.

VEX: The exhibit has negotiated an extension.

TAG

[The next morning, the statue is dry and upright. The sign on the plinth now reads "BACK AT ONE."]

VEX: The museum has a schedule.

RUNI: I put it there.
