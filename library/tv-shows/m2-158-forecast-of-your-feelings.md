# The Forecast of Your Feelings

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality reunion episodes that demand dramatic confrontations after the participants have already made peace*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Two former roommates return for a reunion special after resolving their argument over a shared treehouse. Host Vika wants a feud for the teaser; the roommates keep trying to discuss the practical repair they completed together.

Cast: Vika, a macaque host; Joss, a gibbon carpenter; Miri, a lemur botanist; and Tump, a capuchin segment producer.

## Episode: "The Branch Between Them"

### Cold Open

[REUNION SET. JOSS AND MIRI SIT IN SEPARATE CHAIRS. A GRAPHIC READS "THEY HAVE NOT SPOKEN IN YEARS." MIRI WHISPERS TO JOSS.]

MIRI: Did you bring the new hinge?

JOSS: It is in my bag.

VIKA: Tonight, they face the truth.

MIRI: We spoke this morning.

[TUMP PLAYS A LOW DRUM STING. JOSS OPENS HIS BAG AND A METAL HINGE CLINKS ONTO THE FLOOR.] 

VIKA: The first crack in the silence.

JOSS: That is hardware.

### Act One

[THE HOST SHOWS AN OLD CLIP OF THE ROOMMATES ARGUING ABOUT A TREEHOUSE LADDER. THE CLIP ENDS WITH BOTH REACHING FOR THE SAME TOOLBOX.] 

VIKA: Miri, what did you feel when Joss took the ladder down?

MIRI: I was relieved. It was splitting at the rung.

JOSS: We rebuilt it last spring.

[TUMP SHOWS A GRAPHIC: "THE LADDER THAT ENDED IT ALL." MIRI TURNS THE SCREEN AROUND. THE BACK READS "SAFETY REPLACEMENT." ]

MIRI: The old ladder is now a planter.

VIKA: Could we see the moment you stopped speaking?

JOSS: We did not stop speaking.

MIRI: We stopped using the old group chat.

### Act Two

[TUMP WHEELS OUT TWO EMPTY STOOLS TO SYMBOLIZE THE DISTANCE BETWEEN THEM. JOSS AND MIRI MOVE THE STOOLS TO THE SAME SIDE OF THE SET SO THEY CAN LOOK AT A TREEHOUSE PHOTO.] 

VIKA: What does this empty space mean to you?

JOSS: It means there was no chair here.

MIRI: We put the chairs together because the camera blocks the picture.

[TUMP ROLLS A SECOND GRAPHIC: "THE MOMENT THEY REUNITED." ]

MIRI: We met to repair the roof.

VIKA: Was that difficult?

JOSS: The roof was slippery.

MIRI: We used a proper ladder this time.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. VIKA LOOKS AT THE TELEPROMPTER, WHICH SAYS "ASK ABOUT BETRAYAL."]

VIKA: Did either of you feel betrayed?

MIRI: No.

JOSS: We agreed to have someone check the structure every season.

[TUMP PUTS A SINGLE RED CUSHION ON ONE STOOL TO SUGGEST A FIGHT. MIRI USES IT TO LEVEL THE WOBBLY CHAIR.] 

[TUMP CUES AN OLD CLIP OF A DOOR SLAM. JOSS AND MIRI WATCH IT, THEN LOOK AT THE REUNION SET'S SWINGING DOOR.]

JOSS: The old door had a loose hinge.

MIRI: We fixed that too.

VIKA: Did it feel like a final break?

JOSS: It felt like a loud door.

[THE SOUND TECH PLAYS THE SLAM AGAIN. THE REUNION SET'S DOOR SWINGS OPEN AND BUMPS THE CUE BUTTON, TRIGGERING A THIRD SLAM. MIRI GETS UP AND HOLDS IT OPEN WHILE JOSS TIGHTENS THE HINGE SCREW WITH HIS POCKET TOOL.]

TUMP: The past is repeating itself.

MIRI: The sound cue is repeating itself.

[THE TECH DISABLES THE BUTTON. VIKA PUTS DOWN THE PROMPT CARD ABOUT BETRAYAL.]

### Act Three

[JOSS PULLS A SMALL REPAIR PLAN FROM HIS BAG. MIRI ADDS THE NEW HINGE AND A LIST OF TREES TO TRIM AWAY FROM THE ROOF.]

VIKA: You have brought a plan to the reunion?

JOSS: You invited us to talk about the treehouse.

MIRI: The west branch is touching the roof. We can trim it after nesting season.

VIKA: Is there any unresolved tension?

MIRI: The step stool is missing.

[JOSS LOOKS UNDER HIS CHAIR. THE STEP STOOL IS UNDER VIKA'S DESK, HOLDING THE LIGHTING CABLES CLEAR OF THE FLOOR.] 

JOSS: We found it.

TUMP: Can we frame this as a shocking discovery?

VIKA: No. We have a repair plan, a found stool, and an audience that now knows where the cable goes.

[TUMP CHANGES THE LOWER THIRD TO "NEXT CHECK: SPRING." THE OLD CONFLICT GRAPHIC STAYS ON A MONITOR BEHIND THEM.] 

[VIKA TRIES ONE LAST TIME TO TURN THE STOOLS BACK TO FACE EACH OTHER. THE CABLE FROM THE CAMERA SNAGS THE EMPTY CHAIR. JOSS AND MIRI LIFT THE CABLE CLEAR TOGETHER, WITHOUT INTERRUPTING THEIR REPAIR PLAN.]

VIKA: You have no trouble sharing the set.

MIRI: We are trying to share the floor.

### Tag

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON THE TITLE "THEY HAVE NOT SPOKEN IN YEARS." JOSS REACHES OVER AND TURNS THE MONITOR OFF.] 

JOSS: We have to go check that hinge.

MIRI: I brought the wrench.

VIKA: Can we get one last dramatic look?

[THEY BOTH CHECK THE HINGE, NOT THE CAMERA.]
