# The Buzzer Knows Your Name

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: quiz shows with oversized buzzers, dramatic pauses, and contestants who answer before the question ends*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Three contestants compete on a quiz show whose buzzers have been wired to the wrong names, lights, and one confetti cannon.

Cast: Host Mela, a macaque; contestant Doro, a gibbon; contestant Pex, a capuchin; contestant Vali, an orangutan; scorekeeper Nemi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. QUIZ STUDIO - NIGHT

Mela stands beside three colored podiums. Doro presses the blue button. A spotlight shines on Vali.

VALI: I have not buzzed.

MELA: The light says you have.

NEMI: The light is wired to the wrong button.

ACT ONE

Mela explains the rules: ring in after the question, answer correctly for points, and do not press the button during the dramatic pause. Doro tests the buzzer once. It makes a duck quack.

DORO: Is that my sound?

MELA: It is now.

Pex presses his button. The scoreboard changes DORO's name to PEX. Nemi reverses the names on the display. Vali's button plays a cymbal crash and drops a paper ribbon over her head.

VALI: I will wait for the question.

MELA: That is excellent sportsmanship.

ACT TWO

Mela asks the first question: "What shape has three sides?" She finishes the sentence. Doro buzzes. The duck quacks; Vali's spotlight turns on.

DORO: Triangle.

NEMI: Correct. The score goes to Doro. The light remains on Vali.

MELA: Our audience can follow the points even if the lights cannot.

Pex asks if he can answer before the next question because he knows what it will be. Mela refuses. He presses anyway. A small confetti popper fires behind the set. Nemi writes "early" beside his name.

MELA: The question is, which animal builds a dam?

Pex buzzes before she finishes. The popper fires again, this time into the judges' chairs. He answers "beaver" correctly but loses the point for ringing in early.

PEX: The confetti agreed with me.

MELA: The confetti was not asked.

The next question asks for a season between winter and summer. Vali waits through the whole sentence, then presses her button. Doro's duck sounds. Vali answers "spring." The scoreboard awards Pex the point, but Nemi marks Vali on the manual tally.

NEMI: The button lights, sounds and score assignments are now three separate systems.

ACT TWO-B

Nemi moves the wires beneath the podiums. Mela keeps asking questions so the round continues. Doro presses for "the largest ocean"; Pex's name flashes. He answers "Pacific," and the answer is correct. Nemi gives Doro the points and circles the proper name with a marker.

MELA: We are not using the screen to determine the result.

VALI: What is the screen for?

NEMI: Atmosphere.

Mela asks a question about a fruit with a peel and a crown. Vali waits until the end, presses, and receives a spotlight. She answers "pineapple." The scoreboard lights every letter in her name except the first one.

VALI: I seem to be missing a beginning.

NEMI: Your points are present.

Pex has one point left and promises not to buzz early. Mela asks: "What is the name of our host?" Pex stares at the button, then slowly folds his hands. Doro's buzzer quacks from the table without being touched.

DORO: I did not press it.

MELA: The show has rung in for you.

Nemi marks Doro as first to answer the unscored practice prompt. The answer is Mela, but no points change. Pex reaches for his button, then stops himself.

Mela calls for a lightning round. Each contestant must identify a sound. A kettle whistles from behind the screen. Vali waits for the cue, presses, and gets Pex's name on the scoreboard.

VALI: I can hear the kettle.

NEMI: You have the right to answer.

VALI: It is a kettle.

MELA: Correct. The points go to Vali.

The next sound is a handbell. Doro presses, and the spotlight lands on Nemi, who is holding the bell. Pex starts to answer, sees the sound came from the stage, and asks whether that disqualifies the question.

MELA: The sound is part of the question.

PEX: The stage is part of the show.

Nemi taps the bell again by accident. All three contestants press their buttons. A shower of paper leaves falls from the ceiling. Nemi adds one point to each and tells the crew to stop the sound effects.

ACT THREE

Nemi manually checks the scores. Doro has three points, Vali has four, and Pex has one. Mela announces Vali as winner. The scoreboard illuminates Vali's podium; the quacking sound plays; a final confetti popper fires above Pex.

MELA: Our scorekeeper has the correct result.

NEMI: The prize is a small trophy and a receipt.

She hands Vali the trophy. Vali presses her button by accident while placing it down. A spotlight lands on Nemi.

NEMI: I do not receive the trophy.

VALI: But you did keep score.

NEMI: That is the job.

Pex offers to help unplug the podiums. Vali points out which cord powers the lights. Mela says they can end the broadcast manually. Nemi does so by flipping a labeled switch. All three buzzers sound at once as the power cuts.

TAG

The set goes dark. Doro's buzzer makes one last duck noise from the off position.

DORO: Is that still my sound?

FINAL IMAGE: Nemi carries the trophy away while the blank scoreboard flashes "CONGRATULATIONS, VALI" in the dark.
