# This Box Contains a Demonstration

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised home-shopping shows that add bundles and countdowns to simple products*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Shopping host Rumi must sell a compact clothesline before the broadcast window ends. Producer Tegg keeps adding unrelated accessories while engineer Sali tries to demonstrate how the line actually folds and locks.

Cast: Rumi, a macaque host; Tegg, a capuchin producer; Sali, a gorilla product engineer; and Vem, a lemur caller.

## Episode: "The Folding Line"

### Cold Open

[SHOPPING STUDIO. A FOLDED CLOTHESLINE SITS ON A TABLE BESIDE A HUGE COUNTDOWN CLOCK.] 

RUMI: It fits on a balcony and folds flat for storage.

TEGG: Add the bonus towel set.

SALI: It is not included.

[TEGG SLIDES A STACK OF TOWELS ONTO THE TABLE. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK IS NOW BLOCKED.] 

RUMI: We cannot see the offer timer.

TEGG: The towels are dramatic.

### Act One

[SALI OPENS THE LINE AND SHOWS THE LOCK. RUMI PULLS THE TENSION BAR.]

SALI: It must click into the side bracket before hanging clothes.

TEGG: Can it hold a wet blanket?

SALI: The label gives the weight limit.

[TEGG HANGS A STAGE BLANKET ON THE LINE. IT SAGS. THE LINE STAYS IN PLACE, BUT THE CAMERA TRACKS THE SAGGING FABRIC LIKE A SUSPENSEFUL DROP.] 

RUMI: That is heavier than the recommended load.

TEGG: It looked impressive.

[SALI TAKES THE BLANKET DOWN. TEGG ADDS A SECOND BONUS: A PLASTIC BASKET. IT WILL NOT FIT INTO THE STORAGE BOX.] 

RUMI: The basket is larger than the product.

TEGG: It makes the bundle feel generous.

### Act Two

[THE PHONE LINE LIGHTS. VEM ASKS WHETHER THE CLOTHESLINE FITS BESIDE A WINDOW. SALI CHECKS THE MEASUREMENTS ON THE SAMPLE FRAME.] 

VEM: My balcony is narrow. Will it block the door?

SALI: It needs the clear width printed on the card.

TEGG: Tell them it fits anywhere.

RUMI: We can show the dimensions on screen.

[TEGG PUTS UP AN ANIMATED HOUSE. THE CLOTHESLINE EXPANDS TO FILL THE WHOLE BALCONY AND COVERS THE DOOR.] 

RUMI: That graphic answers the question badly.

[SALI SETS A SMALLER MOCK BALCONY ON THE TABLE. THE LINE FITS WHEN ITS SUPPORTS ARE INSIDE THE MARKED AREA. TEGG ADDS A POTTED TREE, A LOUNGE CHAIR, AND A TABLE. THE LINE NO LONGER FITS.] 

SALI: Those are not part of the test.

TEGG: They are part of the lifestyle.

[THE LINE COLLAPSES AGAINST THE CHAIR. RUMI MOVES THE EXTRA PROPS OFF THE MODEL.]

RUMI: The product fits. The set does not.

[SALI SHOWS HOW THE LINE FOLDS INTO ITS STORAGE CASE. TEGG TRIES TO PUT THE SAMPLE TOWELS AND BASKET INSIDE TOO. THE LID BOUNCES OPEN.]

SALI: The case holds the line.

TEGG: The bundle needs to fit together.

RUMI: It does not come with the towels.

[TEGG REMOVES THE TOWELS. THE CASE CLOSES. HE PUTS THE TOWELS IN THE STUDIO LAUNDRY CART, WHICH IMMEDIATELY ROLLS AWAY DOWN THE RAMP.] 

TEGG: The bonus has gone to laundry.

[RUMI RETRIEVES THE CART. SALI RESETS THE LINE AND LOCKS IT INTO THE BRACKET.] 

### Act Three

[THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK REACHES ZERO. TEGG PRESSES A BUTTON. IT RESETS TO 15 MINUTES.] 

TEGG: We have a special extension.

SALI: The line is still the same size.

[RUMI GIVES THE OFFER CLEARLY: ONE FOLDING LINE, THE LOCKING BRACKET, AND A SIZE CARD. SHE REFUSES THE UNRELATED TOWEL BUNDLE.] 

RUMI: If it fits your space, you can order it. Check the measurements first.

[THE CALLS ARRIVE. TEGG HOLDS THE PLASTIC BASKET OVER THE PHONE BANK, BUT IT COVERS THE BUTTONS. VEM CALLS BACK TO ASK FOR THE DIMENSION CARD.]

VEM: That is the part I needed.

[THE ORDER LIGHTS FLASH. SALI FOLDS THE DEMONSTRATION LINE. THE BASKET IS TOO LARGE TO FIT THE BOX, SO TEGG USES IT TO CARRY THE PHONE HEADSETS TO THE NEXT STATION.]

[A CALLER ASKS WHETHER THE LINE WORKS IN A BATHROOM. SALI POINTS TO THE OUTDOOR-USE LABEL. TEGG STARTS TO SAY "ANYWHERE," THEN SEES THE LABEL IN CAMERA.]

TEGG: It works in the places listed.

RUMI: That is a strong answer.

[THE NEXT CALLER ASKS IF THE LINE CAN HANG A WINTER COAT. SALI CHECKS THE LABEL AND SHOWS THE WEIGHT LIMIT. TEGG BEGINS TO ADD A COAT RACK TO THE BUNDLE.] 

SALI: The line is for drying clothes, within the marked limit.

RUMI: The coat rack is not part of this product.

[TEGG PUTS THE COAT RACK BACK IN THE PROP CLOSET. THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK IS VISIBLE AGAIN.]

TEGG: We have four minutes left.

RUMI: The line folds in four seconds.

[SALI FOLDS IT. THE LOCK SNAPS SHUT. TEGG TRIES TO MAKE A BONUS REVEAL FROM THE CLOSED CASE. NOTHING MOVES.] 

SALI: That is how it stores.

### Tag

[THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK TICKS DOWN. TEGG POINTS TO IT. RUMI UNPLUGS IT.] 

RUMI: The offer can end when it ends.

SALI: The line folds either way.
