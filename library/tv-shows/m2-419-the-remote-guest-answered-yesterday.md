# The Remote Guest Answered Yesterday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live television remote interviews, satellite delay, split-screen reactions, and hosts who keep talking over the guest*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A host interviews a capuchin instrument maker from a distant workshop, but the satellite delay makes every answer arrive after the next question.

Cast: Host Rava, a macaque; remote guest Piri, a capuchin; studio director Odo, a baboon; remote camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; graphics operator Neli, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. TELEVISION STUDIO - MORNING

Rava faces a split screen. Piri sits in a workshop. Rava waves; Piri waves five seconds later.

RAVA: Good morning, Piri.

PIRI: Good morning, Rava.

Rava is already looking at Odo.

ACT ONE

Odo explains there is a seven-second satellite delay. Rava asks Piri what he makes. Piri answers after the next question has started. Neli adds a spinning graphic of a hand-carved flute; it covers Piri's face.

PIRI: I make bamboo whistles and flutes.

RAVA: We will hear one shortly.

PIRI: I am holding one now.

The answer arrives over Rava's next line. She stops speaking. Piri waits, listening to the delay in his earpiece. Odo raises a card that says "PAUSE."

ACT TWO

Rava tries again. She asks how long it takes to carve a flute. Piri starts answering while she begins a sponsor introduction. The lines overlap. Rava apologizes, but Piri hears the apology before the question.

PIRI: It takes about an hour for a small one.

RAVA: What is your favorite part?

PIRI: I said an hour.

Odo tells the host to wait after asking. Rava asks the question and counts silently. Neli displays a graphic showing Piri's workshop, but labels it "STUDIO B." Tavi shakes his head at the remote camera. The label changes to "PIRI'S WORKSHOP."

RAVA: We can see your tools.

PIRI: I can see the label.

ACT TWO-B

Piri demonstrates a bamboo whistle. He turns the flute to show the carved holes, but the video freezes with his tail covering the instrument. Rava asks him to repeat the movement. Piri does not hear the request until the image returns. He has already set the flute down.

PIRI: I put it on the bench.

RAVA: Could you pick it up?

PIRI: I heard that one.

Piri picks it up slowly, turning it so the remote camera can follow. Tavi keeps the frame wide. Neli removes the spinning graphic. Rava waits seven seconds, then asks how the instrument is tuned. Piri answers that he trims the end until it makes the intended note. He plays a short clear tone.

The studio hears the tone later. Rava holds her reaction until it arrives. Odo marks the delay on a cue card. Neli displays the caption "A SHORT CLEAR TONE" before the sound comes through. Rava looks at the screen, then back at Odo.

RAVA: We have captioned the future.

ODO: We have captioned the buffer.

ACT TWO-B - THE QUESTION WITH A GAP

Odo gives Rava three cards: ASK, WAIT, and LISTEN. She asks Piri what tool he uses to smooth a flute, then holds up WAIT. The studio stays quiet. Seven seconds pass. The control room starts the end music, but Odo waves it down.

PIRI: A strip of fine leaf. It follows the curve without scraping the bamboo.

RAVA: Thank you. I was about to ask a second question before the first one arrived.

PIRI: I could hear the sponsor music starting on my monitor.

Rava asks him to show the leaf strip. Piri holds it beside the flute, then waits for Tavi's framing card. The camera catches both objects. Neli delays the caption until the answer is actually heard. The caption appears at the same time as Piri's final word. Odo gives a small silent thumbs-up.

PIRI: That is the first caption that arrived with me.

RAVA: We are making progress at exactly one speed.

A brief audio drop blanks the studio sound. Rava does not fill it. Piri sees the question card and keeps the strip still. When sound returns, Rava repeats only the part lost, and Piri answers once. The producer points to the clock; the segment is running long. She holds up another card marked "KEEP GOING" instead of cutting him off.

ACT THREE

Rava asks one final question: where can viewers find Piri's work? She waits. Piri names his workshop's market stall and says he will be there on Saturday. Rava repeats the location only after his answer has arrived. Neli puts the information on screen without covering the guest. Tavi holds up a card saying "END INTERVIEW" so Piri can see it after the delay.

RAVA: Thank you for joining us.

PIRI: Thank you for waiting.

Odo cuts the remote feed after Piri waves. The studio screen goes black. Rava keeps her hand up for five more seconds, then lowers it.

TAG

The show returns to the remote feed. Piri is packing up. Rava's earlier wave arrives on his monitor; he waves back.

FINAL IMAGE: Rava's hand is down in the studio while Piri's delayed wave fills the screen.
