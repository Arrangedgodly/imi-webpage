# The Five-Star View Is a Loading Screen

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: luxury travel review shows, staged room tours, star ratings, and hosts who praise amenities they have not tested*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A travel host reviews a remote lodge, but the promised panoramic view is hidden by fog and the crew keeps rating the brochure instead.

Cast: Host Luma, a lemur; lodge manager Omi, an orangutan; travel producer Vex, a macaque; camera operator Tavi, a capuchin; and guest reviewer Miri, a gibbon.

## Episode: "The View Suite"

### Cold Open

[LODGE BALCONY. WHITE FOG FILLS THE FRAME.]

LUMA: This room has a breathtaking view.

TAVI: I can see your sleeve.

Luma moves her arm. The frame stays white.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The crew arrives at a mountain lodge to review its premium suite. Omi welcomes them and says the balcony overlooks a valley when the weather is clear. Vex has already printed a five-star graphic. Miri asks whether they can review the actual stay first. Vex says the show needs a strong opening.

Luma begins a room tour. She opens the curtains to reveal fog. The camera cuts to a stock photograph of blue mountains. Miri asks if the image is from the lodge. Tavi checks the production folder; it is from a different country. Vex says the image captures the spirit of the view. Omi says the mountain is in the other direction.

The group tests the amenities. The kettle works, the bed is firm, and the room has a boot dryer. Luma praises the robes, which are still folded. Miri asks her to try one. The robe is too long and drags across the floor. Omi shows where guests can hang it to dry. Luma says the room includes interactive storage.

### Act Two

Vex proposes a panoramic reveal at sunset. Omi checks the weather and says the fog may remain all evening. Vex asks him to open the balcony doors so the clouds can move. Omi says they are open already. Tavi films the fog for a full minute. Nothing changes. Luma narrates a changing landscape while Miri watches a blank monitor.

Miri reviews the room honestly: it is warm, quiet, and clean; the view is unavailable today. Vex asks whether she can call the fog atmospheric. She says it is weather. He tries a star rating. Luma gives the suite five stars for the kettle. Miri gives the view no rating because she cannot see it. Vex suggests an average. Omi says there is no score for the view on a foggy day; guests can ask about forecast and room orientation.

The fog lifts for a second and reveals one small rock ledge. Vex asks whether that counts as a view. Miri says it counts as a rock ledge. The camera operator labels the shot honestly before the clouds cover it again.

The crew tours the dining room. The menu lists mountain soup, but the kitchen is serving lentil stew. Luma asks if the stew is inspired by the mountain. Omi says it is made with local lentils. Vex wants a dramatic close-up. The stew arrives with a spoon that reflects the studio light back into the lens. Tavi lowers the camera. Miri tastes the soup and says it is good. Luma reaches for the wrong spoon and takes a bite of the decorative salt bowl.

OMI: That is not the soup.

LUMA: It is a very strong first course.

The dining room has a board with local trail conditions. The main trail is closed due to mud. Vex asks if there is a safer alternative. Omi points to a short paved lookout walk beneath the lodge. Miri suggests they visit it only if the fog lifts. Vex requests a shot of the crew hiking. Omi offers a walk around the building instead. The camera follows them for half a lap before Vex calls it an expedition.

### Act Three

The fog thins briefly at dawn. Miri steps onto the balcony and sees a narrow slice of valley. Tavi frames a wide shot, then waits as clouds move again. Luma resists calling it a full panorama. Omi says the clear periods are usually short. The crew records one honest shot and labels it with the time and weather.

Miri gives the lodge four stars for comfort and service, with a note that the view depends on conditions. Luma agrees the room was good even when the scenery was hidden. Vex deletes the five-star graphic and replaces it with a weather icon. Omi thanks them. The crew packs the unused robe and returns it to the closet.

### Tag

[REVIEW MONITOR.]

Vex plays back the footage. The stock mountain image appears again for a moment. Tavi freezes the frame; a tiny copyright label is visible in the corner.

VEX: That is not our lodge.

MIRI: It is now our evidence.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
