# The Variety Hour Ran Out of Acts

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live variety television, rapid act changes, countdown cards, and hosts forced to fill unexpected silence*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A live variety hour loses its final act when the acrobat's prop ladder goes missing, leaving the host to recruit the stage crew as performers.

Cast: Host Miri, a gibbon; acrobat Lako, a macaque; drummer Venn, a capuchin; mime Tavi, an orangutan; floor director Seli, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. LIVE VARIETY STUDIO - NIGHT

Miri announces the final act. Lako steps onstage and looks for his ladder. A countdown card flashes behind the camera.

LAKO: My ladder is gone.

MIRI: We have thirty seconds.

SELI: The ladder is in the percussion segment.

ACT ONE

Venn's drums are set on platforms. Lako's ladder is between them, labeled "ACROBAT." Venn says the set crew moved it to make room for his cymbal. Seli asks the stagehands to bring it back. The cymbal stand blocks the aisle. Miri keeps speaking to camera.

MIRI: Our performers are preparing a surprise.

TAVI: It is a rearrangement.

Tavi uses mime to gesture at the ladder. The audience thinks he is performing. Venn taps a soft rhythm on the cymbal stand while the crew clears a path. Lako holds the ladder still so it does not tip.

ACT TWO

The ladder returns to the stage with one rubber foot missing. Lako tests it and refuses to climb until the foot is replaced. Seli checks the prop shelf; no spare foot is there. Venn offers a folded cloth to steady it.

LAKO: That would slide.

MIRI: We can cut to a close-up.

LAKO: The ladder still needs a foot.

Seli finds a rubber pad under the cymbal case. It fits the ladder. Lako tests it again. Venn's drums are now on the floor, and he can still play. Miri asks whether the act can begin. The audience starts clapping to Venn's test rhythm.

VENN: I have an audience.

TAVI: You have a sound check.

ACT TWO-B

The countdown is down to ten seconds. Seli checks the schedule and says the final act has one minute, not three. Lako says he will do the low balance routine only, with the ladder secured. Miri introduces him. He climbs one rung, balances, then steps down. Venn adds a steady drumbeat. Tavi mimes holding the ladder; the audience applauds.

MIRI: We have a trio.

LAKO: We have a ladder and a beat.

Seli holds the ladder base. Venn plays three beats. Lako performs the balance once, then steps down. Tavi drops his mime pose. He was actually holding the second side. The audience applauds again.

TAVI: That was not the mime part.

MIRI: It was the best part.

The music cue for the final act fails to start. Venn continues his rhythm. Lako bows. Seli confirms the ladder is safe to return to the prop shelf. The host asks the crew to take one final bow; they hold up a hand to say they are carrying equipment.

The audience calls for an encore. Miri checks the clock and asks Venn whether he can play one short rhythm without opening the cymbal case. Venn taps the lid twice. Lako makes one careful step in place, while Tavi silently mimes the ladder being set upright. The audience laughs and claps. Seli raises the "END" card. Miri does not ask them to repeat it.

MIRI: That is the encore.

VENN: It fit in the time we had.

LAKO: The ladder stayed in storage.

Tavi freezes in his mime pose until Seli points toward the exit. He bows while walking backwards, then stops when he reaches the clear aisle. The crowd applauds the safe, low-key finish.

Miri checks the floor monitor. The next segment's title card is already on screen. She turns it face down and introduces the crew instead of starting another act. Venn closes the cymbal case. Lako carries the ladder by its side rails, and Seli walks ahead to keep the path clear.

MIRI: We are finished for tonight.

SELI: We are on time.

ACT THREE

Miri thanks each performer by name and says the final number was shorter than planned. Lako explains the prop issue, then says the repair is complete. Seli returns the rubber pad to the labeled shelf. Venn packs the cymbal and drums. Tavi resumes his mime, silently demonstrating how the ladder was moved.

MIRI: Is that an encore?

TAVI: It is the route to storage.

The show ends on time because Miri shortens her closing speech. The audience applauds the crew as they carry the ladder offstage.

TAG

Miri looks at the schedule. The next episode has no ladder act.

MIRI: Good.

FINAL IMAGE: Seli hangs the ladder on its marked rack while Venn taps one quiet beat on the cymbal case.
