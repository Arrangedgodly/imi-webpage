# The Farm Has One Basket for the Harvest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rural competition series that awards points for a harvest before anyone can carry it*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Odo, a gibbon; grower Nari, a lemur; grower Pip, a capuchin; judge Vex, an orangutan; field reporter Seli, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY FARM - MORNING

[A single wicker basket sits between two rows of tomatoes. Odo holds a stopwatch.] 

ODO: Growers, collect as much produce as you can in five minutes.

NARI: We have one basket.

PIP: We can take turns.

[The stopwatch starts. They both reach for the handle.]

ACT ONE

ODO: Welcome to Harvest Dash, the agricultural contest where the basket is shared and the score is not.

VEX: Each tomato is worth one point.

SELI: The basket is worth zero.

[The growers pick tomatoes. Nari places each one in carefully; Pip tries to fit more by arranging them in a pyramid. One tomato rolls out.] 

PIP: The stack is unstable.

NARI: The basket is full.

ODO: The clock has four minutes.

VEX: There is still room if you use the corners.

[Nari removes one tomato to make space. Pip catches it, places it in his cheek pouch, and keeps picking.] 

SELI: That tomato is no longer in the basket.

PIP: It is in the harvest.

ACT TWO

[The basket gets heavy. Nari and Pip lift it together, but one handle is loose. Vex marks it "one point for transport."] 

NARI: We need to empty it into the crate.

ODO: The challenge is to fill the basket.

PIP: It is full.

VEX: The judge has not scored it yet.

[They wait. The tomato pyramid settles and a tomato drops into Vex's lap. He looks at the scoreboard.] 

VEX: That is a point for the judge.

SELI: The judge is not harvesting.

[Odo starts the clock again for the "carry round." The growers lift the basket; the handle comes loose, but Nari catches it. Tomatoes spill into the row. Pip uses both hands to scoop them up.]

PIP: We have gained ground.

NARI: We have put them back where they grew.

[The camera follows the scattered tomatoes. One remains attached to the vine; another is bruised. Nari sets the bruised one aside. Pip says the rules count every tomato.]

VEX: Is a bruised tomato half a point?

SELI: It's still food.

[They transfer the good fruit into a sturdy crate. The basket is empty again.]

ODO: The harvest has vanished.

NARI: It is in the crate.

[Vex brings out a scale to weigh the harvest. The empty basket weighs almost as much as the tomatoes.]

VEX: We should subtract the basket.

ODO: The official rules count the load as carried.

NARI: Then we are measuring the basket and the fruit together.

[The scale needle swings. Pip tries to steady it with one finger, then holds still. The pointer settles at a number.]

PIP: It is heavier than the empty basket.

SELI: We can record the difference.

VEX: That would make the numbers smaller.

NARI: They would be accurate.

[Odo holds up the scorecard. One column says "quantity," the other "care." Vex tries to write the same number in both.]

SELI: The scorecard is upside down.

[Vex rotates it. The number stays the same.]

VEX: It is still a low score.

PIP: The tomatoes are still good.

ACT THREE

[Vex inspects the crate. The tomatoes are sorted by ripeness, the bruised fruit is set aside for sauce, and the plants remain intact.]

VEX: I cannot score the crate. The challenge was a basket.

PIP: We filled it twice.

NARI: We also carried the harvest without dropping it.

[Odo checks the official rules. They say the winner is the grower who brings the harvest to the table.] 

ODO: The table is over there.

[They carry the crate together. Vex follows, keeping the scoreboard clear. The final tomato in Pip's cheek pouch pops out and lands in the crate.] 

PIP: Extra harvest.

SELI: One point.

VEX: Shared point.

[The crate reaches the table. Odo raises the stopwatch, but it has stopped.]

ODO: We have no time.

NARI: We have tomatoes.

[The two growers divide the harvest into smaller baskets for the neighborhood stand. Vex tries to take the large wicker basket as a trophy; its handle comes off.] 

VEX: I have won the basket.

SELI: You have won the loose handle.

[Seli writes the final count on a chalkboard. The chalk snaps, leaving the total unfinished.]

VEX: We cannot announce an incomplete score.

NARI: We can add the last digit.

[Pip holds the chalk. His fingers are sticky from tomatoes, so the chalk leaves a faint print beside the number.]

PIP: The score has a handprint.

TAG

[Odo announces next week's event: "The Watering Can Relay." Nari and Pip lift a watering can together. Vex reads the rules before the whistle.] 

VEX: The can is also shared.

[The whistle blows. Nothing happens; it is a watering can, not a race horn.]
