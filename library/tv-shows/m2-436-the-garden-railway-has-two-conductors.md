# The Garden Railway Has Two Conductors

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel series that sells a tiny scenic ride as an epic journey*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Seli, a baboon; conductor Nari, a lemur; guest Pip, a capuchin; gardener Vex, a macaque; camera operator Odo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. BOTANICAL GARDEN - DAY

[A miniature train sits on a garden track. A sign says "ONE LAP: THREE MINUTES." Seli speaks into camera.] 

SELI: We are setting out on an unforgettable journey.

NARI: It goes around the pond.

[The train whistle squeaks. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

SELI: Welcome to Farther Than It Looks, our travel series for destinations close to home.

PIP: Is this the whole train?

NARI: It is a locomotive and two cars.

VEX: The route is one hundred and forty meters.

SELI: Over several emotional landscapes.

[Odo follows the train with a long camera lens. The view makes the pond look distant. Pip boards the first car. Vex stays behind to guard the flower beds.] 

PIP: Are you coming?

VEX: Someone must watch the ferns.

SELI: He has chosen the stationary itinerary.

[The train moves. A squirrel crosses the track. Nari stops the locomotive. Seli describes the pause as a border crossing.] 

SELI: We have encountered local traffic.

NARI: The squirrel has the right of way.

ACT TWO

[The train rounds the pond. Seli asks Pip what he sees. He points to a patch of clover.] 

PIP: The same clover from the station.

SELI: The landscape has repeated.

NARI: We are on a loop.

[The train approaches a low branch. Nari ducks. Seli's tall hat brushes a leaf and stays behind on the branch.]

SELI: The journey has taken my hat.

PIP: We can go back for it.

SELI: A return journey would add depth.

[At the station, Vex has moved a watering can away from the track. Its handle catches on a rose bush. He pulls gently, and the can tips, watering one section of the rail bed.] 

VEX: We have improved the scenery.

NARI: The track is damp.

[The train slows at the wet section. Pip disembarks and helps Vex place a handful of gravel beside the rail. The squirrel returns, carrying a flower petal, and crosses again.] 

SELI: We have been granted passage.

PIP: The squirrel crossed twice.

[Before the final lap, Nari checks every passenger's seat latch. Seli asks Odo to film the safety briefing as an expedition checklist.]

NARI: Keep hands inside the car. Wait for the train to stop before you board.

SELI: And what is the most dangerous part of the route?

NARI: The platform edge.

PIP: I thought it was the branch.

[The squirrel appears beside the platform, nibbles a fallen seed, and slips under the hedge.]

SELI: The local traffic has cleared.

[Vex lays a small board across the damp patch by the track so no one has to step on wet gravel. Odo films it as a bridge.]

ODO: We have crossed a river.

VEX: It is three inches wide.

PIP: The bridge is shorter than the train.

[They test the board. It holds. Nari removes it after the train passes so it does not remain across the track.]

NARI: The route is clear again.

SELI: Our journey has required engineering.

VEX: I moved a board.

ACT THREE

[The train arrives back at its starting point. Seli steps down and asks Odo to film the horizon. The camera points across the pond to the station they just left.] 

SELI: We have completed the circuit.

NARI: It is the same station.

SELI: It is a different view of the station.

[Pip retrieves Seli's hat from the branch. He has to lean out of the car, using the train's handrail for balance. Nari stops the locomotive while he reaches.] 

PIP: I have the hat.

SELI: It has traveled farther than I have.

[Vex presents a route map. A short spur to the rose garden appears as a tiny line beside the pond loop. Odo's camera had made it look like a valley.] 

VEX: The landscape has been compressed.

NARI: It is a garden bed.

SELI: From the car, it felt farther away.

[Odo compares his wide shot with the map. The rose bed fills half the screen.] 

ODO: We have a destination shot.

PIP: It is next to the station.

[Nari helps Seli retrieve her hat from the branch. The train remains stopped at the platform while the crew folds the map.] 

SELI: The railway has taken us around the world.

VEX: Around the pond.

SELI: The pond is a world.

TAG

[The locomotive whistles while waiting at the platform. The squirrel settles in the empty car and grooms one paw.] 

NARI: That passenger knows the route.

SELI: It is a local.
