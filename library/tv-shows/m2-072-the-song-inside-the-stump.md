# The Song Inside the Stump

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: music-documentary series, archival discovery montages, and artist-origin myths*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A documentary crew searches for the source of a mysterious rhythm in the grove, while every musician insists the sound is proof of their own artistic influence.

Cast: Director Lulo, a gibbon; percussionist Mera, a macaque; singer Tavi, a lemur; sound archivist Oko, a baboon; and carpenter Doro, a gorilla.

## Episode: "The First Beat"

### Cold open

EXT. OLD STUMP, DAWN

[A low thump echoes through the grove. Lulo leans into camera.]

LULO: For generations, musicians have asked where the first beat began.

MERA: The stump is hollow.

TAVI: I heard it first.

OKO: The archive has a recording from last week.

DORO: That was me hammering a loose plank.

LULO: The legend has a new source.

### Act One

INT. ARCHIVE HOLLOW

[Oko plays a scratchy recording. The rhythm sounds like two knocks and a pause.]

OKO: This is the oldest recording we have.

MERA: That is my drum pattern.

TAVI: I wrote a song with that rhythm.

DORO: I have been tapping that plank for years.

LULO: Each artist claims the beat.

MERA: I learned it from my grandmother.

TAVI: My grandmother heard it from the rain.

DORO: The rain hits the loose plank.

OKO: The recording has a bird call in the background.

LULO: A witness from nature.

MERA: The bird is keeping time.

TAVI: The bird is interrupting me.

[Oko plays the recording again. The bird call lands between the knocks.]

OKO: The bird repeats every three beats.

MERA: Then the bird is part of the song.

TAVI: Or part of the forest.

LULO: The archive remains divided.

### Act Two

EXT. STUMP

[The crew sets up microphones around the stump. Doro taps the plank. Mera listens with her eyes closed.]

DORO: Knock, knock, pause.

MERA: It sounds the same.

TAVI: I can sing over it.

LULO: Tell us what the rhythm means.

TAVI: It means the stump is waiting.

DORO: It means the plank is loose.

OKO: The microphone picks up a deeper tone from inside the hollow.

MERA: There is another sound.

[They listen. A small echo answers the knocks.]

LULO: The stump replies.

DORO: It is the inside of the stump.

TAVI: It has a voice.

MERA: The echo returns after one beat.

OKO: We can record the response.

[The crew plays the knocks through a speaker. The stump echoes them. Tavi sings. The echo follows her.]

TAVI: It is singing back.

DORO: It is echoing.

LULO: The line between a reply and an echo is thin.

MERA: We can build a rhythm around it.

TAVI: I will add a melody.

DORO: I will tighten the plank.

[He taps the plank again. The beat changes.]

MERA: That is a different rhythm.

DORO: The plank is tighter.

LULO: The original sound has vanished.

OKO: We recorded it before the repair.

TAVI: Then the song is in the archive.

### Listening session

INT. ARCHIVE HOLLOW

[Oko slows the recording. The knocks become uneven. The crew leans toward the speaker.]

OKO: The second knock is slightly late.

MERA: The first beat was leading.

TAVI: The melody can follow.

DORO: The loose plank was bouncing after the hammer.

LULO: We have the source, the echo, and the musicians' versions.

OKO: We also have the original recording on a tiny reel.

MERA: Is it safe to play?

OKO: It is old, not cursed.

[The reel turns. The two knocks and pause return.]

TAVI: It still sounds like a song.

DORO: It sounds like a plank.

MERA: Both can be true.

### Act Three

INT. SMALL PERFORMANCE HOLLOW, NIGHT

[The musicians gather. Mera plays the old rhythm. Tavi sings a short melody. Doro taps a new, steady beat on the repaired plank.]

LULO: The crew will not decide who created the first beat.

OKO: We can credit the stump, the plank, the rain, and the bird.

MERA: The bird did not consent.

TAVI: We can call it "Traditional."

DORO: The plank is new now.

LULO: The old recording will preserve the sound.

OKO: I will add a note that the plank was loose.

MERA: That takes away the mystery.

DORO: It keeps the floor from wobbling.

TAVI: We can leave both versions in the program.

LULO: The origin is a sound shared by everyone who heard it.

MERA: That is a documentary answer.

TAVI: It is also a song.

[The group plays together. The stump echoes the final note.]

DORO: The echo is back.

OKO: The plank is tight.

MERA: Then the sound came from the stump.

TAVI: Or from the room.

LULO: The legend continues.

### Tag

EXT. OLD STUMP, DAWN

[A bird lands on the stump and taps twice. The crew turns toward it.]

DORO: That's the bird.

[The bird taps a third time and flies away.]
