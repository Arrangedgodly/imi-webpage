# The Time Travel Show Rewrites Tuesday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: time-travel dramas that use continuity rules to postpone a simple decision*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A temporal detective visits the same Tuesday three times to recover a lost key, while the show's continuity supervisor keeps changing which version counts.

Cast: Detective Pava, a gibbon; continuity lead Odo, a macaque; witness Rini, a capuchin; and narrator Kesh, a baboon.

Episode: "Tuesday, Again"

COLD OPEN

INT. CLOCKWORK STATION - DAY

Pava holds a brass key. Odo checks a timeline chart. Rini stands beside a wall clock.

PAVA: I found the key before it was lost.

ODO: Then it cannot be the key you found after it was lost.

RINI: It is the only key.

KESH: On Tuesday, time loops around the missing key.

PAVA: Can I put it back on the hook?

ODO: Not until we confirm which Tuesday this is.

[THE CLOCK CHIMES. EVERYONE LOOKS AT IT.]

ACT ONE

Odo points to three entries on the chart: Tuesday morning, Tuesday afternoon, and Tuesday again.

ODO: You must return to the morning, before Rini takes the key.

RINI: I never took it.

PAVA: Then who found it?

RINI: You did.

KESH: The detective and the witness are now suspects.

PAVA: That seems like a lot for one key.

ODO: The timeline requires tension.

[THE CLOCK CHIMES AGAIN. THE HAND MOVES BACK ONE MINUTE.]

RINI: We have the same conversation twice.

ODO: The second time has a different angle.

ACT TWO

INT. CLOCKWORK STATION - LATER

Pava places the key on the table. Odo puts a paper cup over it.

PAVA: If it stays here, it cannot be lost.

ODO: The cup creates a new timeline branch.

RINI: The key is under a cup.

KESH: In the original version, the key vanished beneath a cup.

PAVA: Then we know where it is.

[ODO LIFTS THE CUP. THE KEY IS THERE.]

ODO: This is a continuity error.

RINI: It is the same key.

ODO: The prop department made a second one.

[HE HOLDS UP A DUPLICATE KEY. THE TWO KEYS ARE IDENTICAL.]

PAVA: Which one is lost?

ODO: The one in the previous scene.

RINI: It was under the cup.

ACT TWO-B

INT. CLOCKWORK STATION - EVENING

Kesh reads a recap card. Pava points to the wall hook. Odo has added arrows to the timeline chart.

KESH: Previously, the key was lost, found, covered, uncovered, and duplicated.

PAVA: The hook is right here.

RINI: It is empty.

ODO: If we hang the key, the show ends.

PAVA: That is why I want to hang it.

[THE CLOCK CHIMES. THE KEY DUPLICATES SLIP OFF THE TABLE AND LAND ON THE FLOOR.]

RINI: Now both keys are lost.

ODO: We can call that a cliffhanger.

PAVA: We can pick them up.

ACT THREE

INT. CLOCKWORK STATION - NIGHT

Odo draws a line from Tuesday morning to Tuesday afternoon. Rini holds both brass keys. Kesh records the recap.

ODO: If the key is on the hook in the morning, the later scene cannot show it missing.

PAVA: Then start the story after the key was moved.

RINI: I moved it to the table so the hook could be fixed.

KESH: That was not in the recap.

RINI: I told the crew.

ODO: We were shooting the clock.

[PAVA HANDS ONE KEY TO ODO. HE HANGS THE OTHER ON THE HOOK.]

PAVA: Now the hook is fixed and the spare is in your hand.

ODO: We need to show how the key got there.

RINI: I carried it.

KESH: Time travel is a complicated explanation for carrying a key.

ODO: The show has a time machine.

PAVA: The time machine is a door with a clock above it.

[THE CLOCK CHIMES. THE DOOR OPENS. IT LEADS TO A STORAGE CLOSET.]

RINI: That is the prop closet.

ODO: It is the closet in Tuesday's past.

PAVA: We can use the key on that door.

RINI: It is not locked.

INT. CLOCKWORK STATION - FINAL SCENE

Rini picks up both keys. Odo crosses out the timeline chart. Pava hangs one key on the hook and gives the other to Odo.

PAVA: Keep the spare in the drawer.

ODO: That closes the loop.

KESH: Tuesday reaches its end.

RINI: It is still Tuesday.

[THE CLOCK CHIMES. THIS TIME, THE HAND MOVES FORWARD.]

TAG

Odo opens the drawer and places the spare key inside. He closes it. The clock ticks forward.

ODO: The next episode begins on Wednesday.

PAVA: Keep the drawer shut.

KESH: That is the shortest recap we have recorded.

RINI: We kept the key in one place.

[ODO HOLDS THE DRAWER CLOSED WHILE THE CLOCK CONTINUES FORWARD.]
