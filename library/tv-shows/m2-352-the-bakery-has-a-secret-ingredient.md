# The Bakery Has a Secret Ingredient

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: baking competitions that turn ordinary substitutions into suspenseful eliminations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A cinnamon shortage interrupts a televised swirl-roll challenge.

Cast: Host Sola, a macaque; contestants Piri, a capuchin, and Odo, a baboon; judge Kett, a gibbon; pantry runner Nema, a lemur.

Episode: "A Pinch of Panic"

COLD OPEN

INT. BAKING TENT - MORNING

A timer shows forty minutes. Sola holds up an empty cinnamon jar.

SOLA: The signature ingredient is missing.

PIRI: The recipe says cinnamon or another warm spice.

ODO: The word "or" has changed the entire competition.

NEMA: We have nutmeg and cardamom. There is pepper too, but keep it away from the dough.

KETT: The pepper has not been invited.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON THE EMPTY JAR.]

ACT ONE

Sola announces a cinnamon swirl bake. Piri chooses cardamom. Odo reaches for nutmeg and honey. Nema checks the recipe card and says the dough already contains sugar, so honey would be a second sweetener. Odo argues that honey can be a glaze.

ODO: Then it is not in the dough.

KETT: It will still be sweet.

ODO: What if I call it a shine?

KETT: I will call it a rule violation.

SOLA: We have our first controversy.

PIRI: We have a label and a list.

Sola turns toward the camera and calls the scene a pantry crisis. Nema finds the cardamom exactly where the label says. The crew adds ominous music as Piri opens the jar.

PIRI: It smells like cardamom.

SOLA: Can you say that with uncertainty?

PIRI: No.

[ODO SNEEZES INTO HIS FLOUR BOWL. A CLOUD RISES.]

ODO: My spice has arrived dramatically.

NEMA: That is flour.

ACT TWO

The bakers roll their dough. Piri makes a neat spiral. Odo brushes honey onto his dough. It sticks to the brush and folds over itself; he lifts it and the spiral turns into a knot.

ODO: I have invented a braided swirl.

KETT: You have invented a knot.

SOLA: Judge, is a knot a swirl?

KETT: If I can see the center.

Odo pulls the dough apart. Honey stretches between his fingers and glues them together. Nema brings a bowl of water. Odo dips both hands and releases the dough, which slides onto the tray.

PIRI: Your knot is now a roll.

ODO: It survived the trial.

Sola asks the camera to focus on Piri's tray. The timer blocks the lens. Nema moves it; the timer beeps as Piri puts the rolls in the oven.

SOLA: The clock has begun its final countdown.

PIRI: It began forty minutes ago.

KETT: That is why it is a timer.

ACT TWO-B

Odo's rolls come out pale. He asks for one more minute. Piri's rolls are ready, but Kett asks her to let them cool. Sola covers both trays with cloths to create suspense.

NEMA: The rolls need air.

SOLA: The audience needs suspense.

NEMA: They can wait without steaming the pastry.

[THE CLOTH SLIDES OFF ODO'S TRAY. ONE ROLL STICKS TO IT.]

ODO: My glaze has attached itself to the reveal.

KETT: Lift it gently.

[ODO LIFTS. THE ROLL COMES FREE, LEAVING A HONEY PRINT ON THE CLOTH.]

SOLA: A signature!

ODO: A laundry problem.

ACT TWO-C

Kett tastes Piri's cardamom roll and Odo's nutmeg roll. Odo presents his tray with the honey-marked cloth folded under it. Kett checks the middle of each roll.

KETT: Piri's spiral is even. Odo's has a crisp crust and an unusual shape.

ODO: A knot.

KETT: A knot that baked through.

Sola asks for the dramatic verdict. Kett awards Piri the challenge for balance and names Odo's knot the episode's most persistent pastry. Odo accepts the ribbon and tries to pin it to the cloth; it sticks.

[KETT HOLDS UP THE HONEY-MARKED CLOTH.]

KETT: The roll stuck to the cloth because the glaze was still warm.

ODO: So my bake has a printed proof.

PIRI: It has a cloth-shaped hole.

SOLA: Could the bakers repair the presentation before judging?

NEMA: The food is ready. The table is not.

[ODO USES A SPATULA TO LIFT THE ROLL. IT SITS UPRIGHT, BUT ITS BOTTOM IS FLAT.]

KETT: A stable base. A bold choice.

ODO: I designed it to stay on the plate.

PIRI: It was stuck there.

SOLA: The audience will decide what happened.

KETT: The judges will decide what it tastes like.

ACT THREE

Nema returns the spices to the labeled shelf. Sola says next week's mystery ingredient will be announced on air. Nema shows the recipe card, which already lists apples.

TAG

Odo opens the emergency spice box. Inside is a note: "Please stop opening this for television."

ODO: Is the note edible?

KETT: It is certainly seasoned.

[MUSIC: BAKING SHOW THEME.]
