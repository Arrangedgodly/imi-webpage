# The House Has a Secret Doorbell

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: real estate television tours that call every ordinary feature a hidden luxury*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A real estate host tries to sell a modest treehouse as a luxury home by renaming its practical features, while the owner keeps demonstrating what they actually do.

Cast: Host Vela, a gibbon; agent Piko, a capuchin; owner Duri, an orangutan; camera operator Nemi, a macaque; buyer Olo, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. TREEHOUSE ENTRY - DAY

Vela stands beside a brass bell attached to the door.

VELA: This is the secret arrival chime.

DURI: It is a bell. Pull the string when you arrive.

Piko points to a folded ladder.

PIKO: And this is our retractable grand staircase.

OLO: It is a ladder.

ACT ONE

Vela begins the tour. The listing calls the home a "canopy retreat with private circulation." Duri opens a window and explains that it lets in air. Vela calls it a "breeze portal." Nemi tries to film the treetop view, but a branch brushes the lens.

VELA: The trees are close enough to touch.

DURI: This one touches the camera.

Piko shows Olo a storage shelf. He calls it a curated pantry. Duri opens it; there are three jars and a bundle of dried fruit. Olo asks if the jars come with the house. Duri says no, they are her jars.

PIKO: The pantry is negotiable.

DURI: The jars are not.

Vela leads them to the bedroom nook and describes the view as a sunrise-facing suite. Olo notices the branch opening in the wall.

OLO: Is that a doorway?

DURI: It is the way I reach my sleeping platform.

VELA: A private canopy passage.

ACT TWO

Piko demonstrates the ladder. It drops from the platform and taps him on the head. He says that is a hands-free welcome. Duri shows how to lower it safely and hooks it into place. Olo climbs one rung, then notices that the ladder blocks the front door when fully extended.

OLO: Can two people pass?

DURI: One person can climb. The other waits by the bell.

VELA: A built-in pause for mindful arrival.

Nemi asks Duri to ring the bell for the sound shot. The bell is so quiet that no one hears it over Vela's narration. Duri pulls it again; the camera operator looks up only because a branch falls on his hat.

NEMI: I saw the bell move.

VELA: Visual alert, auditory serenity.

Olo asks about the roof. Duri demonstrates a woven rain cover and says she ties it down before a storm. Piko calls it an all-season canopy. A breeze lifts one corner; Duri grabs the tie while Piko keeps selling the feature.

PIKO: It is also an interactive weather display.

DURI: Hold this end.

Olo and Piko hold the tie. Nemi moves the camera to show the roof from above. The angle reveals the other tie is loose. Duri secures it and checks the knot.

ACT TWO-B

Vela asks Olo to name his favorite feature. Olo says he likes the tree view but needs a quieter bell and a safer ladder. Piko offers an upgraded brass bell and an extra rung kit. Duri says the current bell works if you pull it firmly, and the ladder is designed for one climber at a time.

OLO: Can I test the bell without the camera crew talking?

VELA: Of course.

Everyone falls silent. Olo pulls the bell. It rings clearly. Nemi's camera tilts toward it.

OLO: That is a bell.

PIKO: A surprisingly effective one.

Duri folds the ladder back into its hook. Olo asks if there is room to bring up a chair. Duri says a small stool fits through the opening; a large chair will not. Vela calls this "selective furniture access."

OLO: The listing says spacious.

DURI: The platform is spacious for sleeping. Not for a dining set.

PIKO: We can amend the listing.

VELA: We can call it a cozy dining zone.

OLO: No, just say it fits a stool.

ACT THREE

Olo decides the home suits him. He asks to keep the view and the branch opening, and agrees to use the ladder one at a time. Piko corrects the listing: treehouse, sleeping platform, air window, bell, ladder, storage shelf. Vela reads the revised details on camera without adding "secret," "grand," or "interactive."

VELA: The home is available with the features you saw.

DURI: And the jars stay with me.

OLO: Understood.

Piko hands him a plain paper listing. Vela begins an outro. Behind her, Duri rings the bell once to signal that the camera crew should leave. They all turn toward the door.

TAG

Nemi asks for one final shot of the view. The branch taps the lens again. He adjusts the camera instead of calling it a feature.

FINAL IMAGE: Olo climbs down the folded ladder after Duri, leaving the bell hanging still beside the honest listing.
