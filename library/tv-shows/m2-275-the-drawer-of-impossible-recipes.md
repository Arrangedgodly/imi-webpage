# The Drawer of Impossible Recipes

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking demonstration television, where the host reads a recipe without noticing that the listed ingredients contradict one another*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Chef Seli follows a recipe card that calls for both a dry batter and a cup of water, while host Pava refuses to admit the card is wrong.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; cook Seli, capuchin; tester Odo, baboon; card reader Nara, macaque; runner Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Batter That Wouldn't"

### Cold Open

[COUNTER. A RECIPE CARD READS: "ADD WATER. KEEP DRY."]

NARA: The recipe says to add water.

SELI: It also says keep the batter dry.

PAVA: We will follow both instructions.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PAVA ADDRESSES THE CAMERA.]

Pava calls the recipe "a family favorite." Nara reads the ingredients: flour, oats, water, berries, and a note that says "do not add liquid." Seli asks which instruction is newer.

TAVI: The card has two different inks.

ODO: One line may be a correction.

PAVA: We will discover the true recipe together.

Seli mixes the flour and oats. Nara pours a cup of water nearby. Pava tells her not to add it yet. Odo checks the batter; it is dry and crumbly.

SELI: It will not hold together.

PAVA: The recipe says it will.

NARA: The recipe is paper.

### Act Two

[SELI TESTS A SMALL BATCH.]

Seli adds a spoonful of water. The mixture clumps. Pava says the batch is no longer dry. Odo forms a small cake and places it on the tray.

ODO: The shape holds.

PAVA: The instruction is compromised.

NARA: The batter is working.

Tavi finds a second card in the drawer. It says "Add water to bowl; keep the dry ingredients separate." Pava says that is a different recipe.

SELI: It is the same dish.

PAVA: It is a recipe variant.

Nara compares the handwriting. One card is from the cooking club; the other is a shopping list.

NARA: This line says "Keep dry" because it was a reminder for storage.

Odo tastes the small cake. It is bland.

ODO: It needs berries.

PAVA: The berries are listed.

Seli folds them in. Pava asks for a dramatic close-up of the batter.

[THE CAMERA FOLLOWS THE CARD TO THE DRAWER.]

Nara opens the drawer and finds a stack of recipe cards stuck together with flour. One card says "keep dry," another says "add water," and a third lists berries but no flour.

TAVI: These were stored together.

PAVA: The recipes have been combined.

SELI: We should separate them before cooking.

Odo lines the cards up by handwriting. He identifies the flour list as Seli's recipe, the water note as Pava's reminder, and the berry note as Nara's shopping list.

PAVA: So the family favorite belongs to all of us.

NARA: It belongs to the card.

Odo asks whether the recipe could be made without berries. Seli says yes, but the result would be plain. Pava wants to add more berries; Seli says too many will make the batter wet.

PAVA: The recipe calls for berries.

SELI: One handful.

TAVI: The shopping list says "berries for everyone."

NARA: That is not a measurement.

Pava takes a handful, then another. Seli takes the second handful back.

### Act Three

[THE CREW BAKE THE FULL BATCH.]

Seli follows the corrected card: mix dry ingredients, add water gradually, then fold in berries. Pava narrates each step. Nara moves the shopping list away from the counter.

SELI: The batter is holding together.

ODO: The cake is ready.

PAVA: We have solved the recipe.

NARA: We have read both sides of the card.

Tavi puts the tray in the oven. The timer is set for twenty minutes. Pava asks if they should check it after ten.

Seli says not to open the oven yet. Odo watches the timer and announces each minute. Pava asks for one more description of the recipe's history. Nara says it came from the drawer.

PAVA: A family archive.

NARA: A drawer.

Odo checks the oven timer. The dial has counted down to zero.

ODO: The timer reached zero. Are the cakes ready?

Seli opens the oven and tests one cake with a clean skewer. It comes out dry. Odo reaches for the tray without mitts; Seli blocks his hand and gives him the heat-safe cloth.

SELI: The timer is permission to check, not to grab.

ODO: The cloth has no recipe.

NARA: It has one job.

Odo uses the cloth to lift the tray. The cakes have finished.

Seli checks them and removes the tray. Pava asks if the recipe card should be laminated.

NARA: Only after we separate the notes.

TAVI: We can put each recipe in its own sleeve.

PAVA: A full drawer renovation.

### Tag

[THE BAKED CAKES COOL ON A RACK.]

Pava tastes one. It is soft and berry-filled.

PAVA: The recipe is perfect.

SELI: The recipe was confusing.

ODO: The cake is not.

Tavi returns the cards to the drawer, keeping them separate.

NARA: Add labels.

PAVA: A new recipe project.

SELI: After lunch.

[SFX: DRAWER CLOSES, TIMER BEEPS.]
