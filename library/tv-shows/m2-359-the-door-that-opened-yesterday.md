# The Door That Opened Yesterday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: anthology science fiction that treats a household malfunction as a threat to the timeline*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A storage-room door opens onto yesterday's version of the same room, but only when someone forgets to latch it.

Cast: Researcher Nemi, a lemur; caretaker Odo, a baboon; visitor Sela, a macaque; narrator Vale, a gibbon.

Episode: "The Second Shelf"

COLD OPEN

INT. COMMUNITY ARCHIVE - NIGHT

Odo opens a storage door. Inside, a basket sits on the second shelf. Sela looks behind him; the same basket is already in her hands.

SELA: Why is my basket in there?

ODO: It was in there yesterday.

[THE DOOR CLOSES. A CLOCK TICKS BACK ONE MINUTE.]

VALE: In a room where every object has a place, one basket has found two.

ACT ONE

Nemi arrives with a notebook and asks them to recreate the event. Odo opens the door. The basket is on the shelf, but Sela's hands are empty. She places her basket on the table. Nemi asks her not to touch anything.

SELA: It is my basket.

NEMI: It may be a duplicate.

ODO: It has one handle. That is the same basket.

Nemi checks the clock. It reads the correct date. She checks the latch; it catches only if the door is pushed firmly. Vale's narration calls the latch a boundary between two timelines.

ACT TWO

Sela tests the door. She closes it gently; it opens again. Odo says he has been asking everyone to push until it clicks. Nemi labels the basket on the shelf "YESTERDAY" and the one on the table "TODAY."

SELA: Both have the same banana stain.

NEMI: A temporal signature.

ODO: It came from lunch.

[NEMI PLACES A PAPER CLIP ON THE TABLE. THE DOOR IS SHUT. WHEN IT OPENS, THE CLIP IS IN THE STORAGE ROOM.]

VALE: The anomaly has crossed the threshold.

NEMI: The shelf slopes toward the door. It slid when the floor shook.

Sela stomps once. The paper clip moves. Odo asks whether the basket also slid. Nemi says the basket was already on the shelf; the room has no second timeline.

ACT TWO-B

The crew adds a red warning light above the door. It flashes whenever the latch is loose. Vale narrates a containment failure. Sela pushes the door until it clicks; the light turns off. Odo pulls the handle. The door stays shut.

ODO: The timeline is sealed.

NEMI: The latch is engaged.

Sela asks how the basket got into her hands. Odo says he placed it there when he came in, then forgot and saw its twin on the shelf. Nemi checks the camera footage. It shows Odo moving the basket, but the shot is partly blocked by the warning light.

VALE: The archive has recorded its own uncertainty.

NEMI: The camera is pointed at a lamp.

ACT TWO-C

Nemi moves the lamp. The footage now clearly shows Odo carrying the basket out and setting it on the table. The basket on the shelf is another woven basket with an identical banana stain.

SELA: We have two baskets.

ODO: One from the picnic. One from storage.

VALE: The mystery is solved, but the universe remains unsettled.

NEMI: The universe is fine. The labels were identical.

Odo places a new tag on each basket. The crew asks him to repeat the discovery for a dramatic reveal. He opens the door; it latches properly, and nothing changes.

ACT TWO-D

Nemi places a second tag on the storage basket and a third on the shelf. Sela reads each one aloud. The crew's red warning light keeps blinking, even though the latch is fixed.

NEMI: The light is still wired to the door sensor.

ODO: Then the timeline is still open.

SELA: Or the switch is bent.

[SELA PRESSES THE SENSOR. THE LIGHT GOES OUT.]

VALE: A second anomaly has appeared.

NEMI: The light switch needed adjustment.

Odo tries to produce a dramatic version for the episode. He opens the door in slow motion. It catches halfway and knocks the warning lamp off its stand.

ODO: I have disturbed the timeline.

SELA: You have bumped the lamp.

Nemi replaces the lamp with a smaller one on the shelf, out of the door's path. The room stays shut. The baskets remain labeled.
ACT THREE

The archive uses different labels and fixes the latch. Sela takes her picnic basket. Odo leaves the storage basket on its shelf. Nemi removes the warning light; Vale insists on one last shot of the quiet door.

TAG

The door opens a crack. Odo pushes it until the latch clicks.

VALE: Yesterday has been contained.

NEMI: That is today's repair.
