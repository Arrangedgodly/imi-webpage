# The Greener Branch Challenge

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: eco-reality competitions, sustainability scoreboards, and sponsor-friendly green claims*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: Two teams compete to make the grove's community kitchen more sustainable, but the program's scoreboard awards points for labels rather than actual reuse.

Cast: Host Meli, a lemur; team lead Sora, a macaque; team lead Tumo, a gorilla; judge Pex, a capuchin; and sponsor liaison Davi, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Zero-Waste Kitchen"

### Cold open

INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN, DAY

[A scoreboard shows both teams at zero. Sora holds a reusable bowl. Tumo holds a pile of leaves.]

MELI: The greener branch challenge begins now.

SORA: We will reuse the bowls.

TUMO: We will compost the leaves.

DAVI: The sponsor has provided new green bowls.

PEX: They are single-use.

DAVI: They are labeled "eco."

SORA: The bowls are plastic.

DAVI: The label is green.

MELI: The scoreboard is ready.

[The scoreboard awards the sponsor two points.]

### Act One

INT. KITCHEN

[Sora stacks the existing bowls. Tumo sorts leaves and peelings.]

PEX: Each team has one hour to reduce waste.

SORA: We can wash and reuse these.

TUMO: We can put scraps in the compost bin.

DAVI: The sponsor bowls are made from recyclable plastic.

MELI: Do they need to be recycled?

DAVI: Eventually.

SORA: We already have bowls.

DAVI: These are brighter.

TUMO: The compost bin is full.

PEX: We can turn it.

[They rotate the bin. The scraps fall out.]

MELI: The waste has moved.

TUMO: We need another bin.

DAVI: The sponsor has green bins.

SORA: Are they reusable?

DAVI: They are very green.

PEX: That is not an answer.

### Act Two

INT. KITCHEN, LATER

[Sora washes bowls at a basin. Tumo tries to carry the compost outside.]

TUMO: The path is blocked by the sponsor display.

MELI: The display is part of the challenge.

DAVI: It shows the green bowls.

SORA: The bowls are blocking the compost route.

PEX: The scoreboard says Sora has one point for "visible reuse."

SORA: The bowls are clean.

PEX: It only counts visible labels.

TUMO: My compost has no label.

DAVI: We can label it.

TUMO: It is compost.

MELI: The label says "future soil."

DAVI: That is a sponsor phrase.

[The team labels the compost bin. The scoreboard awards Tumo five points.]

SORA: The compost didn't change.

PEX: The label improved its score.

TUMO: Can the dirt wear a label?

DAVI: We have a sticker sheet.

[The sticker sticks to a banana peel.]

SORA: We can reuse the peel as food for the compost.

PEX: The scoreboard gives no points for that.

MELI: The challenge is about waste.

PEX: The scoreboard is about points.

### Scoreboard audit

INT. KITCHEN

[Pex checks the point totals. The sponsor bowls are stacked beside the compost.]

PEX: The scoreboard gave the new bowls two points each.

SORA: There are six bowls.

DAVI: That is twelve points for green design.

TUMO: The bowls were used once.

MELI: The compost label got five points.

TUMO: The compost was used twice.

PEX: The scoreboard is rewarding labels.

SORA: We can put a green label on the reused bowls.

DAVI: That earns more points.

SORA: Then the program measures stickers, not waste.

[They peel the stickers off the scoreboard and place them on the compost bin.]

PEX: The score has changed.

MELI: Did the kitchen change?

TUMO: No. The stickers moved.

DAVI: That is still a measurable outcome.

### Act Three

INT. KITCHEN, EVENING

[The teams present their results. The reused bowls are stacked. The compost is now in an unlabeled corner.]

MELI: Team Sora reused the existing bowls.

SORA: We used the same bowls for every meal.

MELI: Team Tumo composted the scraps.

TUMO: The bin is full again.

DAVI: We can provide a larger bin.

PEX: It is made of new plastic.

DAVI: It is green plastic.

MELI: The winner is the team that made the least new waste.

PEX: The scoreboard says the sponsor display wins.

SORA: The display was delivered in a new crate.

TUMO: The crate can hold the bowls.

MELI: The teams have found a use for the crate.

DAVI: The sponsor logo is still visible.

PEX: That is not the same as reducing waste.

DAVI: Can the logo be composted?

TUMO: It is painted on.

SORA: We can reuse the crate as storage.

MELI: Then the teams tie.

PEX: The scoreboard says they lose.

MELI: The scoreboard is plugged into the sponsor display.

[The lights on the scoreboard turn off.]

### Tag

INT. KITCHEN, NEXT MORNING

[Sora carries a stack of old bowls. Tumo empties compost into a garden bed. The green scoreboard is reused as a shelf.]

DAVI: The scoreboard is no longer visible.

MELI: It is holding the bowls.

[One bowl rolls off and lands in the compost.]
