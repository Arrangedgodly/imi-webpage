# The Orchard Has a Secret Ingredient

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: competitive cooking shows that treat an ordinary ingredient reveal as a season-defining mystery*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Pavi, a macaque; cook Leni, a gibbon; cook Odo, an orangutan; judge Tavi, a baboon; floor producer Suri, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. ORCHARD KITCHEN - DAY

[Two covered bowls sit under spotlights. Pavi faces camera with a card. Leni and Odo stand at separate counters.] 

PAVI: Today, the cooks will compete using one secret ingredient.

TAVI: It is a pear.

PAVI: The secret is that it is a pear.

[Odo lifts one lid. A pear rolls off the counter and into his apron pocket.]

ODO: I have secured the mystery.

[Theme music swells.]

ACT ONE

PAVI: Welcome to Orchard Kitchen, where every basket has a backstory and every timer is too loud.

SURI: We have twenty minutes. The ingredient is still hidden.

TAVI: It is a pear.

PAVI: Judge Tavi will be surprised by the presentation.

[Leni smells the covered bowl without lifting it.]

LENI: Pear.

PAVI: You cannot know that.

LENI: The leaves are sticking out.

[She removes one leaf. It is attached to a pear stem. Suri rushes to cover the clue with a napkin.]

SURI: The reveal must remain intact.

ODO: I know what it is. My pouch found it.

[Odo reaches into the apron pocket, takes out the pear, and slips it up his sleeve. He turns; the pear drops from the sleeve into a mixing bowl.]

PAVI: A shocking development.

TAVI: The pear entered the bowl.

PAVI: We will revisit that moment after the sponsor message.

SURI: We have no sponsor message.

PAVI: Then tell them what is at stake.

TAVI: Lunch.

ACT TWO

[The cooks begin. Leni slices pears for a tart. Odo stirs pear slices into stock for soup. In a small pan, he thickens a spoon of pear jam with syrup, then brushes the sticky glaze over a second whole pear to make a garnish. When the camera swings toward him, he hides the glazed pear under a towel. Steam from the soup pot dampens the towel and presses it into the tacky glaze. Odo turns to stir the soup.]



ODO: I am protecting the reveal.

LENI: The reveal happened when you dropped it.

ODO: That was a preview.

[Suri films a close-up of a pear core. Pavi steps between the camera and the cutting board.] 

PAVI: Viewers at home, do not adjust your screens. The fruit is still a mystery.

TAVI: It is a pear core.

PAVI: The core is not the whole pear.

[Odo's soup boils. He hears a rustle and lifts the towel. The damp cloth has pressed into the glazed pear and sticks to it. Steam has warmed the glaze. Odo peels the cloth away; the softened skin slips off in a spiral. He lays the peel on a plate like a ribbon while Pavi watches.]

ODO: The pear has changed.

LENI: It is warm.

ODO: It has undergone a transformation.



PAVI: A twist no one predicted.

TAVI: It is a peel.

[The twenty-minute timer rings. Leni's tart is done. Odo's soup is mostly broth; one pear slice floats in the center like an island. He parks the glazed garnish on a plate beside the pot.]


SURI: We need a tasting shot of the ingredient.

LENI: It is in every shot.

PAVI: Judge Tavi will taste blindfolded.

TAVI: Why?

PAVI: For suspense.

ACT THREE

[Judge Tavi sits at the tasting table. Pavi carries in Leni's tart. Odo carries his soup on a tray with the glazed garnish on a side plate. He has slipped a third whole pear from the ingredient crate into his apron pocket, where it bulges.]


TAVI: Odo, why do you have another pear?

ODO: The first one was a decoy.

TAVI: We saw it fall into the bowl.

ODO: That was a decoy decoy.

[Leni serves a slice of tart. Tavi tastes it.]

TAVI: Balanced, clear, and recognizably pear.

PAVI: A bold reveal.

[Odo serves the soup. The pear island slips off the spoon into the bowl with a soft plop.]

TAVI: The pear is floating.

ODO: It is the garnish staying in character.

[Tavi tastes the broth, then looks at Pavi.]

TAVI: This needs salt.

ODO: I was saving it for the reveal.

TAVI: The salt is on your counter.

[Odo sets down his soup, reaches to his counter for the shaker, and adds salt to his own bowl.]

PAVI: Then the secret ingredient has been revealed three times.

[Odo reaches into his apron and finds the third pear. He puts it on the table. Leni slides the winning tart away from the spill.]

LENI: The fruit was never a secret. The timer was.

TAVI: The timer ran the full twenty minutes. Leni's tart is baked through, and Odo's soup needed salt.

PAVI: Who wins?

TAVI: Leni wins the Golden Pear Spoon. Odo wins the broth ladle, with the note: season before serving.

[Suri stops the timer. Its alarm keeps ringing; the switch is loose.]


[As the crew resets for a wide shot, Suri slides the empty bowl to the center of the counter.]

SURI: The bowl needs to look important.

LENI: It is empty.

PAVI: An empty bowl can hold expectation.

TAVI: Or nothing.

[Odo sets the third pear into the bowl. It rolls once, then settles against the rim. The camera follows it in a slow close-up.]

PAVI: We have a new reveal.

TAVI: It is still a pear.

ODO: This one knows its mark.

TAG

PAVI: Join us next week for another ingredient reveal.

[Odo opens the pantry. A row of pears is labeled "NOT THE SECRET."]

TAVI: We know what those are.

ODO: Not until the premiere.
