# The Crown Has a Seed Stuck in It

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: historical costume dramas that make every ceremony sound like a prophecy*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Queen Nari, a lemur; courtier Vex, an orangutan; chamberlain Pavi, a macaque; musician Pip, a capuchin; wardrobe keeper Seli, a baboon; orchard groundskeeper Fara, a gibbon; palace macaque Bibi, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

INT. PALACE HALL - MORNING

[A gold crown sits on a velvet pillow. Pip plays a solemn chord on his open-ended wooden recorder. Nari reaches for the crown, but Seli lifts it first.] 

SELI: There is a seed stuck inside.

VEX: A sign from the ancestors.

[The seed rolls off the pillow and drops through the recorder's open bell. SFX: A seed rattles inside the wooden tube. Theme music.]

ACT ONE

NARI: The coronation is at noon. I will wear the crown after it is cleaned.

VEX: The royal chronicle says a seed foretells a season of change.

PAVI: The chronicle is a dinner menu. The seed was in the crown after breakfast.

VEX: The palace archives are mysterious.

[Seli turns the crown upside down. A small seam of dried fruit is visible along the inner band.] 

SELI: Someone wore it while eating.

NARI: It was on a display head overnight.

PIP: The display head has no mouth.

VEX: Perhaps it had a previous life.

[Nari puts on a plain practice circlet. Vex asks her to rehearse the oath.]

NARI: I will keep the palace gardens watered and the paths clear.

VEX: The ceremonial version says "guard the kingdom's flowing lifeblood."

PAVI: That means water.

VEX: The court does not say "water."

ACT TWO

[Seli cleans the crown with a soft brush. Pip plays a three-note fanfare. The seed in his instrument rattles between the notes.] 

PIP: The crown has added percussion.

VEX: A royal omen.

NARI: It is a seed in the recorder.

[Seli asks Pip to remove it. He turns the instrument upside down. The seed does not fall out. He taps it, and it rolls deeper into the tube.] 

PIP: It has entered the chamber.

PAVI: The chamber is the recorder.

[Vex begins drafting an announcement: "A golden seed, fallen from the crown, heralds a new age." Nari says the announcement should note that the crown is being cleaned. He refuses to make the story ordinary.] 

VEX: People remember a prophecy.

NARI: People remember whether the ceremony begins on time.

[The palace doors open. Orchard groundskeeper Fara enters carrying a basket of fruit. Palace macaque Bibi follows, having carried the cleaning brush away. It sits near the crown, grooms its wrist, then inspects the velvet pillow.] 

PAVI: The royal animal has returned.

NARI: There is no royal animal.

[Seli retrieves the brush. Bibi touches the crown and plucks a strip of dried fruit from the inner band.] 

SELI: There was more breakfast in the lining.

VEX: The prophecy deepens.

PAVI: The breakfast tray was in the wardrobe room.

[Seli sets a gold saucer beside the crown. Pip taps the recorder, and the seed drops into it.]

VEX: It should be sealed in the royal archive.

NARI: It should be planted after the ceremony.

PAVI: The archive has no soil.

[Fara brings a pot from the orchard and sets it near the throne. Pip marks the saucer so no one mistakes the seed for a snack.]

PIP: We can remember where it is.

VEX: A symbol with a location.

NARI: A seed.

[The court watches as Fara sets the pot aside. One drop from the watering can lands on Vex's sleeve, where a breakfast crumb is stuck.]

VEX: The sign is already affecting the court.

ACT THREE

[The coronation starts. The seed rests in the gold saucer beside Pip. Nari approaches the crown. Vex reads his dramatic announcement.] 

VEX: From the gold of the crown, a seed has come forth. A season of change begins.

NARI: We found one seed and a strip of dried fruit in the lining. The crown has been cleaned. The garden will be watered this afternoon.

[The court listens. Pavi turns to Fara, who lifts the fruit basket. Bibi reaches for an apple, then stops when Seli offers a small bowl.] 

SELI: The fruit belongs in the bowl.

PAVI: A new order.

VEX: The court has witnessed a sign.

NARI: The court has witnessed good housekeeping.

[She places the crown on her head. It fits. Pip plays the fanfare. Pip takes the seed from the saucer and hands it to Fara.] 

PIP: It should be planted.

VEX: In the palace garden, beneath the royal oak.

NARI: We have no royal oak. We have an orchard.

[Fara plants the seed in the waiting pot. Vex places a small marker beside it reading "THE SEED OF CHANGE." Nari turns the marker around and writes "APPLE."]

VEX: The chronicle will need both names.

PAVI: It can have one line.

TAG

[At the banquet, Vex raises a cup to toast the new reign. A crumb rolls out of his sleeve and lands in the bowl of fruit.] 

NARI: Another omen?

VEX: A snack.

[He eats the crumb.]

PAVI: The age of change has begun.
