# The House at the End of the Vine

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation series, eerie reenactments, and ominous voiceovers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A crew investigating a supposedly haunted treehouse discovers that its nightly knocking is caused by a practical neighbor who has been trying to return a ladder.

Cast: Investigator Edda, a lemur; sound expert Pox, a baboon; host Viri, an orangutan; neighbor Tev, a macaque; and camera assistant Lolo, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Three Knocks at Hollow Nine"

### Cold open

INT. HOLLOW NINE TREEHOUSE, NIGHT

[The room is dark. Three knocks echo. Viri speaks into the camera.]

VIRI: Every night at the same hour, Hollow Nine calls to someone.

POX: That was three knocks.

EDDA: It came from the east wall.

LOLO: The east wall is a window.

VIRI: A window can be haunted.

[Another three knocks. The camera shakes.]

POX: The sound moved.

EDDA: It is now at the door.

[The door opens. Tev stands outside with a ladder.]

TEV: Is this yours?

VIRI: We are live.

TEV: I know. I have been knocking for four nights.

### Act One

INT. HOLLOW NINE TREEHOUSE, NIGHT

[Tev leans the ladder against the wall. The crew surrounds him.]

VIRI: Why do you have our ladder?

TEV: I found it in my yard.

LOLO: The yard is far below.

TEV: It fell from this tree.

POX: The ladder's metallic resonance could explain the knocking.

EDDA: You were carrying it?

TEV: I was putting it back.

VIRI: Why not call us?

TEV: I did. The number on the sign is for the studio.

LOLO: That is the production office.

TEV: I left a message.

VIRI: The production office thought you were a viewer.

TEV: I was a viewer. I was also returning a ladder.

POX: When did you first hear the knocking?

TEV: When I knocked.

EDDA: The haunting began when the ladder went missing.

TEV: The ladder was never missing. It was in my yard.

VIRI: We have a timeline.

LOLO: It starts before the ladder fell.

POX: The timeline is also haunted.

### Act Two

INT. TREEHOUSE, LATER

[The crew has placed microphones around the room. Pox wears headphones. Edda examines a loose panel.]

POX: At 9:03, a soft tapping came from behind the wall.

EDDA: At 9:04, the house creaked.

VIRI: At 9:05, I felt a chill.

TEV: It is cold outside.

VIRI: That may be the source of the chill.

LOLO: The camera has picked up a shadow.

EDDA: Where?

LOLO: Behind Viri.

[Viri spins around. A large fern stands behind him.]

VIRI: The shadow has leaves.

POX: The fern is moving.

TEV: I moved it to get the ladder through.

EDDA: We will record the movement again.

TEV: Why?

VIRI: For the reenactment.

TEV: I can move it now.

VIRI: We need the light to be more ominous.

[They dim the lantern. The fern shadow stretches across the wall.]

POX: The figure is tall and branching.

EDDA: It has no face.

TEV: It is a fern.

VIRI: The camera is ready.

TEV: What am I supposed to do?

LOLO: Carry the fern and pause at the door.

TEV: I carried it once.

VIRI: This time, look troubled.

TEV: I am troubled. I am in a treehouse at night carrying a fern.

[He walks slowly. The shadow looms.]

POX: The footage is chilling.

EDDA: The sound is a leaf brushing the wall.

POX: We can add a low hum.

TEV: Please don't make it sound like me.

### Act Three

EXT. HOLLOW NINE TREEHOUSE, NIGHT

[The crew hangs outside the treehouse, listening. Tev sits on his porch below.]

VIRI: We have one final test. Tev will knock on the trunk, and our team will identify the sound.

TEV: I have already returned the ladder.

VIRI: We still need the test.

TEV: Fine.

[Tev knocks three times. The treehouse creaks. A loose bucket swings and taps the wall three times.]

EDDA: The bucket.

POX: The bucket has a hollow tone.

LOLO: It is tied to the ladder hook.

VIRI: The knocking came from a bucket?

TEV: The bucket fell from your balcony when the ladder did.

EDDA: That explains the nightly pattern.

POX: It also explains the metallic overtone.

VIRI: What about the cold spot?

TEV: The window is open.

[He closes it. The wind stops.]

VIRI: The house is no longer calling.

EDDA: It was asking for maintenance.

TEV: The house was asking for its ladder.

LOLO: We should return the bucket.

VIRI: It is part of the evidence.

TEV: It is my bucket.

VIRI: Then the haunting has a resolution.

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, DAY

[The crew watches the finished episode on a monitor. A title card reads "HOLLOW NINE: THE LADDER RETURNS."]

POX: We should investigate the next house.

EDDA: The next house has a loose porch step.

VIRI: That sounds promising.

[The porch step creaks in the studio.]

LOLO: It is our own set.

[The crew's porch step creaks once more as the studio lights fade.]
