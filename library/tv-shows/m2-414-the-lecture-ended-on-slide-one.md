# The Lecture Ended on Slide One

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised university lectures, unreadable slides, and presenters who repeat the opening while the broadcast clock runs down*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A botanist's lecture broadcast keeps jumping to its final slide, forcing the lecturer to teach from the actual seed pod on the desk.

Cast: Professor Neri, a gibbon; host Rava, a macaque; technician Odo, a baboon; student guest Piri, a capuchin; camera operator Tavi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. UNIVERSITY LECTURE HALL - DAY

Professor Neri advances a slide. It reads "THANK YOU."

NERI: We have not started.

RAVA: We are already grateful.

Odo presses the remote. The slide returns to "THANK YOU."

ACT ONE

Neri is scheduled to give a short television lecture on how seed pods open and spread seeds. She holds a dry pod and shows its two halves. The screen changes to a photograph of the lecture hall from last year. Piri asks whether the remote is skipping ahead. Odo checks the battery; it is new.

ODO: The advance button sends two pulses each time.

TAVI: The case is bent against it. I can see the edge pressing down.

Rava asks Neri to continue while Odo loosens the remote case. Neri explains the pod opens as it dries and releases seeds. She demonstrates with a paper model. The projector returns to "THANK YOU" before she finishes.

ACT TWO

Odo opens the battery cover to relieve the pressure on the warped case. Neri advances one slide. It shows a diagram, but the camera zooms too close and crops off the labels. Tavi backs the camera away; now the lecture screen is too small to read. Rava asks for the labels to appear on a lower-third graphic.

RAVA: We can add the words to the broadcast.

NERI: The words are already on the slide.

PIRI: The viewers cannot see them.

Neri brings the pod to the document camera. Tavi frames the object. Odo sets the remote aside. The screen remains on "THANK YOU," but the seed pod is now clear. Neri separates the two halves and points out the seeds inside.

NERI: The dried pod opens along this seam.

RAVA: Is that the thank-you slide?

NERI: It is the object.

ACT TWO-B

Piri asks how far the seeds travel. Neri says it depends on the plant and conditions; the demonstration pod does not launch them across a room. Rava asks whether they can show a slow-motion seed flight. Odo says the lecture hall has a paper model, not a seed-launching rig.

PIRI: We can roll the paper seed across the table.

NERI: We can show the opening without pretending it flew.

Neri folds the paper model and lets it open. One paper seed slides onto the floor. Tavi keeps the camera on the table while Odo retrieves it. Rava asks for the next slide. Odo presses the remote once and waits. The image changes to the correct diagram, then immediately advances to "QUESTIONS."

ODO: The bent case is still holding the button down.

RAVA: We are close to the question period.

NERI: I have one more point.

Piri asks whether the seeds are safe to touch. Neri says these are dried teaching specimens and should stay on the table. She places them in a clear dish. The camera finally captures the labels as Tavi angles the lens away from the bright stage lights.

ACT TWO-C

Rava opens the audience microphone. A student asks whether the dried pod always splits into two halves. Neri says this specimen does, but plants vary. The camera pushes close until the pod fills the frame and the professor disappears.

TAVI: We have a lecture with no lecturer.

RAVA: Can you tell us what the label says?

NERI: It says the pod came from the teaching garden, not that every pod behaves exactly alike.

Odo presses the remote. The screen jumps to THANK YOU again. The audience laughs, and Rava begins thanking Neri. The professor holds up the actual pod like a hand raised in class. The room quiets. She explains that the demonstration is limited: it shows the seam and the dry split, while the diagram shows a different species. Tavi widens the lens so both object and professor are visible.

PIRI: We can read the label and see the sample.

RAVA: The screen can wait.

Odo turns the remote face down. It advances nothing. Neri sets the sample in its dish and answers one more question about how the seed is protected from damp air.

ACT THREE

Neri summarizes how the pod opens and shows the seed dish once more. Odo removes the remote batteries and puts the bent case in his tool bag. Tavi uses the desk keyboard to select the diagram. It stays up. Rava invites Piri to ask the closing question. Piri asks whether the paper model behaves exactly like a real plant.

NERI: It demonstrates the opening motion, not the full life cycle.

The broadcast clock reaches the end. Rava tries to thank the professor. The projector does not change slides. Odo leaves it on the diagram while the camera holds a wide view of the lecture hall.

TAG

After Neri packs the pod, Rava presses the final-slide key at the desk. The projector displays "THANK YOU." Neri looks up.

NERI: I have now started and finished.

FINAL IMAGE: The slide stays on "THANK YOU" while the real seed pod is packed safely in its labeled dish.
