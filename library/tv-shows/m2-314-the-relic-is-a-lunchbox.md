# The Relic Is a Lunchbox

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: archaeology documentaries, dramatic artifact close-ups, and hosts who call every old object a lost civilization*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A documentary crew excavates an old garden plot and finds a metal lunchbox that its former caretaker buried for safekeeping.

Cast: Host Vex, a gibbon; archaeologist Nari, an orangutan; field assistant Palla, a macaque; local historian Odo, a capuchin; and camera operator Tavi, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "The Buried Feast"

### Cold Open

[EXCAVATION SITE. A BRUSH CLEARS DIRT.]

VEX: Beneath the soil lies a secret from the past.

PALLA: It is a lunchbox.

VEX: A mysterious container.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[FIELD TENT. A MAP IS UNROLLED.]

Nari explains that the garden plot belonged to the old community center. Vex tells the camera they are searching for evidence of an unknown settlement. Odo says the center closed only twelve years ago.

VEX: History can happen quietly.

PALLA: So can lunch.

The crew digs along a marked line. Palla uses a small brush to uncover a metal corner. Vex asks for a dramatic pause before they lift it. Nari checks the soil around the box, then asks Palla to photograph it in place.

VEX: We have found a relic.

NARI: We have found a lunchbox. We do not know how old it is yet.

Tavi zooms in on a latch. Vex narrates that the mark may be a symbol. Nari says it is a sticker shaped like an apple.

### Act Two

[EXCAVATION CONTINUES.]

The crew cleans the box carefully. Inside are a spoon, a cloth napkin, and a note saying "Back after the garden shift." Vex reads it as a coded message. Odo recognizes the handwriting as belonging to the community center's former gardener.

ODO: My aunt worked here. She used to bury her lunch when the squirrels raided the table.

VEX: A defensive storage system.

Nari examines the box. The corrosion suggests it was buried recently, not centuries ago. Vex asks for a reenactment. Palla pretends to be a squirrel. Tavi films a slow-motion paw reach. The squirrel is played by Vex's own hand, wearing a felt tail.

NARI: The evidence does not show a squirrel.

VEX: The reenactment does.

The production assistant wheels in a graphic labeled ANCIENT PANTRY. Nari asks who approved it. Vex says the graphic is only a possibility. Odo points at the lunchbox's faded school-fair sticker, still visible through the dirt.

ODO: The school held its fair eleven years ago. I sold jam here.

VEX: Could the jam have been ceremonial?

ODO: It was on toast.

Tavi frames Nari's gloved hand beside the sticker. The shot makes the small lunchbox look enormous. Vex asks for a scale reference, so Palla holds up a ruler. Vex rejects it because it has centimeters and asks for a carved stone. Nari sets the ruler back beside the box.

NARI: A ruler is a scale reference.

PALLA: The stone would be another object we have to explain.

Vex records a voice-over describing a sealed chamber. Palla lifts the hinged lid, and the chamber is a single compartment with a loose spoon. The boom operator lowers a dramatic bell tone. It rings each time the spoon rolls.

VEX: The chamber answers us.

NARI: The spoon is on a slope.

Odo contacts his aunt. She says she buried the lunchbox during a storm and forgot where she put it. She is still alive and lives nearby. The crew invites her to the site.

### Act Three

[THE GARDENER ARRIVES.]

Odo's aunt opens the lunchbox and recognizes the napkin. She laughs at the note and says she had planned to retrieve it after the rain, but the garden shed moved. Vex asks what the object means to history.

AUNT: It means I was hungry.

Nari explains the box's age and the garden's previous use. The crew will preserve the note in the local archive and return the lunchbox to its owner. Vex asks if they can still call it a relic.

NARI: You can call it a recent object with a known owner.

VEX: That is less mysterious.

PALLA: It is more accurate.

The aunt offers the crew fresh sandwiches. Vex asks Tavi to film the handoff as a historical reenactment. Nari says they can film it as a lunch break.

### Tag

[FIELD TENT.]

Vex examines a second shiny object. It is the crew's spoon, which Palla dropped by the camera bag. He starts to narrate. Nari takes it away.

NARI: We know where that came from.

VEX: History is everywhere.

PALLA: So are our things.

The team packs up, carrying the lunchbox above ground.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
