# The Pet Adoption Hour Introduces the Cat to the Host

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: adoption broadcasts that stage a cute on-camera meeting before learning what the animal prefers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Pema, a macaque; adoption counselor Oru, an orangutan; guest Jali, a lemur; camera operator Savi, a gibbon; producer Tavi, a capuchin; cat Button, a cat.

COLD OPEN

INT. ADOPTION STUDIO - DAY

Button sits in an empty guest chair. Pema reads a card.

PEMA: Tell us what you love about the cat.

ORU: We should first let the cat settle.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Tavi wants a close-up of Button's face. Savi moves the camera closer. Button steps off the chair and walks behind the set. Pema continues the introduction to the empty cushion. Oru checks the cat's carrier; it is open, with a blanket and a familiar toy inside.

Jali arrives for an adoption meeting. Tavi asks them to sit beside the cat. Oru says Button needs space. Jali waits on the far side of the table. The camera zooms on the empty chair again. Tavi whispers that the segment has no cat in it.

ACT TWO

INT. QUIET ROOM - LATER

Oru brings Button's blanket and toy to the quiet room. The cat comes out from behind the carrier and sniffs the toy. Jali sits on the floor at a distance. Pema asks what the cat is like at home. Oru says Button takes time to approach new people. Tavi asks Jali to hold a treat toward the camera. Jali places it on the floor and sits back. Button approaches when no one reaches for her.

Savi keeps the camera wide. The monitor shows Button sniffing the treat, then backing away. Tavi asks for another take. Oru says the moment is real and should stay. Jali asks if the cat likes quiet rooms. Oru says she usually chooses a covered bed. They lower a small fabric tent beside the carrier.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Pema conducts the interview without bringing Button to the set. Jali describes how they would make a quiet space at home. Tavi displays a heart graphic beside the host's chair. Savi changes to a shot of the empty cat chair, with Button nowhere near it. Pema notices and asks for the real meeting footage. The monitor shows the treat on the floor and Jali sitting still. Button is visible at the edge.

INT. ADOPTION STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Tavi asks Pema to read a viewer question from the card: "Will the cat go home tonight?" Oru says the application, reference check and home visit come first. Pema starts to announce a match, then crosses out the word on her script. Savi frames the counselor and the blank application form. Jali asks whether Button's blanket can travel with her if the application is approved. Oru says they can discuss that later; today the blanket stays with the cat. Tavi tries to roll the cat chair into the quiet room. Button ducks into her tent, so Oru leaves the chair outside.


ACT THREE

INT. QUIET ROOM - AFTERNOON

Savi asks whether they should show the adoption form. Oru sets it beside Jali, not beside the cat. Tavi removes the "tonight" banner from the studio monitor.

Oru checks Button's body language and leaves the carrier door open. Jali asks whether they can offer a hand for the cat to sniff. Oru says to keep the hand low and still. Jali waits. Button comes forward, sniffs, then steps back into the tent. Tavi asks if that counts as a successful adoption. Oru says it counts as a first meeting.

Jali asks what happens next. Oru explains the shelter's usual follow-up and home visit. Pema asks if Jali still wants to apply. Jali says yes, after the regular process. Tavi lowers the heart graphic and replaces it with a card reading MEETING, NOT A PROMISE. Savi films Button from across the room as she returns to her blanket. No one lifts her for a final shot.

Pema asks Jali what they learned. They say Button decides when to approach. Tavi starts to cue applause, then looks at Oru. Oru shakes his head; the cat is resting. The crew lowers the lights and stops moving the camera. Jali leaves the application with the counselor and steps out quietly. Button stays in the tent, with her toy near the entrance. Oru closes the room door gently after checking that the carrier remains open. Tavi removes the empty guest chair from the broadcast frame.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE MEETING

Pema thanks the shelter team. Savi labels the clip FIRST MEETING. Tavi stores the unused cat-chair graphic.

FINAL IMAGE: Button sleeps in her tent beside the open carrier while the interview chair remains empty on the monitor.
