# The Auction Show Has a Current Tenant

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: property auction programs, dramatic valuation reveals, and hosts who mistake a lived-in room for an empty set*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Ralo, a baboon; owner Simi, a macaque; appraiser Henn, an orangutan; camera operator Vuli, a gibbon; producer Teka, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SMALL MUSIC SHOP - DAY

A camera crew stands inside a neighborhood music shop. A monkey behind the counter sorts sheet music into a crate. Ralo holds an auction paddle.

RALO: This charming property is going under the hammer.

SIMI: The shop is still open.

TEKA: We booked a valuation segment, not an auction.

HENN: The sign behind you says APPRAISAL TODAY.

[The host turns the paddle. It is actually a cardboard cue card that reads ESTIMATE.] 

RALO: We will reveal the winning bid after the break.

SIMI: There is no bidding.

[The theme music plays as Simi rings the shop's bell. A customer enters.] 

ACT ONE

INT. MUSIC SHOP - CONTINUOUS

The appraiser checks the lease, the display shelves, and the shop's inventory list. Simi points out that the building belongs to a landlord and the instruments belong to the business, not to the television crew. Teka asks the camera operator to get a wide shot without showing customers' faces.

VULI: The counter is occupied.

RALO: That gives the property character.

SIMI: It gives the shop a cashier.

HENN: We are estimating the fixtures and the shop equipment. The property itself is not for sale.

[The host peels a price sticker from a clarinet case and holds it like a reveal card. Henn takes the sticker back and asks Simi whether the instrument is part of the inventory.] 

SIMI: It is on consignment. The maker gets paid if it sells.

RALO: So it has a dramatic backstory.

SIMI: It has a contract.

[The crew walks through the shop. Ralo narrates the narrow aisle as a secret passage. Simi carries a small delivery cart through it and shows that the cart fits if the tripod is moved.] 

VULI: I will move the tripod. The path stays clear.

TEKA: Can we get a shot of the door opening dramatically?

SIMI: The door opens every time a customer arrives.

[The doorbell rings. A customer asks for sheet music. Ralo offers to point them toward the display, but Simi serves them while the crew waits.] 

ACT TWO

INT. BACK OFFICE - LATER

Henn gives an estimate for the fixtures, shelving, and equipment. The host wants a single high number. Simi asks that the report separate equipment from the lease, since the shop does not own the building. Henn revises the page into clear categories.

RALO: The reveal needs one bold number.

HENN: One number would combine things that are not being sold together.

SIMI: I need to know what the shelves and sound equipment are worth for insurance. The lease stays with the landlord.

[The host tries to unveil a blank board. Teka hands him the itemized sheet. He unfolds it; it has several lines and no dramatic total.] 

RALO: The audience may be confused.

HENN: The categories are labeled.

[The camera operator frames the page. Simi checks the shop floor while the customer tests a music stand. The stand wobbles. Simi tightens its knob before handing it over.] 

SIMI: That is not part of the appraisal. It is part of the sale.

RALO: Could the stand be the surprise item?

SIMI: It is a music stand.

[The producer asks the appraiser to value the shop's sign. Henn explains that it is removable and remains with the business. Simi says they will keep it. Ralo switches the paddle to a new cue card: OPEN FOR BUSINESS.] 

ACT THREE

INT. MUSIC SHOP - AFTERNOON

The crew prepares a final shot of the itemized estimate. Simi tapes a copy to the back-office wall and returns to the counter. The host asks whether the shop has a future buyer. Simi says the business is staying open and has no auction scheduled.

RALO: Then the property has no winner?

HENN: It was never being auctioned.

TEKA: The segment is an appraisal, and the report is complete.

[The host puts away the paddle. A customer pays for a music book. The camera operator films only the counter and the receipt changing hands.] 

SIMI: That is a real transaction.

RALO: A small sale with no cliffhanger.

SIMI: We have more customers.

[The doorbell rings again. Teka asks the crew to clear the entrance. Ralo steps aside and lets the next customer in. Henn signs the final page and gives the report to Simi.] 

TAG

Ralo stands by the open sign and whispers that bidding is closed. Simi flips the sign to OPEN.

FINAL IMAGE: Simi serves a customer at the counter while the appraisal report hangs in the back office.
