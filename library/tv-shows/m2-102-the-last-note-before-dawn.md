# The Last Note Before Dawn

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: talent competition television with dramatic backstories and judges who overstate small performances*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A local talent show expects a spectacular finale, but the contestants perform simple skills with great seriousness. The producers keep asking for bigger emotion until a real primate talent changes the running order.

Cast: Host Rumba, a gibbon; judges Piko, a macaque, and Fara, a capuchin; contestants Meli, a langur, and Odo, a squirrel monkey; stagehand Tern, a tamarin.

## Episode: "One More Encore"

### Cold Open

[THE STAGE IS LIT WITH A SINGLE SPOTLIGHT. ODO HOLDS A PEANUT.]

RUMBA: Tonight, one performer will win the golden leaf.

ODO: I can open this peanut.

[ODO CRACKS IT WITH A STONE. THE CROWD APPLAUDS.]

RUMBA: A moment of courage.

### Act One

[BACKSTAGE, MELI PRACTICES A SOFT WHISTLE.]

RUMBA: What is your talent?

MELI: I can whistle the first three notes of the dawn chorus.

RUMBA: Can you add a story?

MELI: The birds sing at dawn.

RUMBA: What did you overcome?

MELI: A loud camera crew.

[THE CREW FALLS SILENT.]

TERN: You have two minutes.

MELI: I need one quiet minute.

[THE JUDGES LOOK TO THE PRODUCER. THE PRODUCER SIGNALS FOR A DRAMATIC MUSIC BED.]

PIKO: I want to hear the sound without a score.

FARA: So do I.

[THE MUSIC STOPS.]

### Act Two

[MELI STEPS INTO THE SPOTLIGHT. SHE WHISTLES THREE NOTES. THE AUDIENCE LISTENS. A BIRD OUTSIDE ANSWERS.]

RUMBA: Was that part of the act?

MELI: No.

[THE BIRD SINGS AGAIN. MELI WHISTLES THE SAME NOTES. THE BIRD ANSWERS WITH A DIFFERENT PATTERN.]

FARA: The performer has found a duet partner.

PIKO: We should not ask the bird to audition.

[ODO ENTERS WITH HIS PEANUT.]

RUMBA: Your turn.

ODO: I can crack another one.

[HE SETS THE PEANUT DOWN, FINDS A SMALL STONE, AND CRACKS IT. THE PEANUT SPLITS IN TWO.]

RUMBA: A second triumph.

ODO: I can also share.

[HE HANDS HALF TO THE STAGEHAND.]

TERN: I am working.

ODO: You can eat between cues.

[TERN TAKES THE HALF. THE JUDGES LOOK AT ONE ANOTHER.]

[THE PRODUCER HANDS RUMBA A CARD THAT READS "PERSONAL SACRIFICE."]

RUMBA: Meli, what did you give up to prepare?

MELI: My quiet morning.

RUMBA: Can you say it with more emotion?

MELI: I would like my quiet morning back.

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. THE PRODUCER CROSSES OUT THE CARD.]

PIKO: That was honest.

FARA: It also made room for the bird to answer.

[ODO PRACTICES CRACKING A PEANUT WITH THE STONE. THE STONE BOUNCES INTO THE WING.]

ODO: I need a smaller stone.

TERN: We have a prop peanut that opens with a string.

ODO: That would not be my talent.

RUMBA: The judges will score technique and stage presence.

PIKO: The act is safe and clear.

FARA: The stage presence is sharing.

RUMBA: We still need a winner.

PIKO: The show can award both without ranking a bird.

[THE PRODUCER RIPS UP THE CARD MARKED "SUDDEN ELIMINATION."]

[RUMBA READS THE FINAL CARD.]

RUMBA: Our final question is, what will each of you do tomorrow?

MELI: Listen to the dawn chorus.

ODO: Practice cracking a peanut without sending the stone offstage.

TERN: I will put a mat under the work table.

PIKO: Those are excellent plans.

FARA: None require a spotlight.

[RUMBA LOWERS THE CARD.]

RUMBA: We may need a quieter show next season.

[THE PRODUCER BRINGS OUT A GIANT GOLDEN LEAF TROPHY. IT IS TOO WIDE FOR THE STAGE DOOR.]

TERN: We cannot get it backstage.

RUMBA: Can we turn it sideways?

[THE TROPHY TURNS, BUT ITS STEM CATCHES ON THE CURTAIN.]

PIKO: The award is too large for the room.

FARA: We can leave it by the window.

[THE BIRD OUTSIDE PECKS THE TROPHY. THE LEAF ROCKS.]

MELI: We should give it a smaller perch.

### Act Three

[THE FINAL VOTE. RUMBA HOLDS UP TWO GOLDEN LEAF CARDS.]

RUMBA: The judges have chosen a shared award for listening and sharing.

PIKO: Meli made room for a sound beyond the stage.

FARA: Odo made sure the crew got a snack.

RUMBA: The prize is a golden leaf for each.

[TERN BRINGS OUT THE AWARDS. THE BIRD OUTSIDE SINGS AGAIN.]

MELI: It deserves a leaf.

PIKO: The bird was not a contestant.

FARA: We can leave the award on the windowsill.

[THE TROPHY SLIDES OFF THE SILL. ODO CATCHES IT WITH HIS FREE HAND.]

RUMBA: A final display of talent.

ODO: I caught a falling leaf.

TERN: That is not the award leaf.

[ODO LOOKS AT THE TROPHY, THEN HANDS IT TO MELI.]

MELI: We can share it.

[THE BIRD SINGS THREE NOTES. THE JUDGES RAISE THEIR CARDS TO GIVE IT AN HONORABLE MENTION.]
