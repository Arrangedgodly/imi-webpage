# The Story Hour Reads the Last Page First

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's reading programs that spoil a story's ending before the guest has opened the book*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Nima, a macaque; librarian Oda, an orangutan; child guest Feli, a capuchin; page-turner Pali, a lemur; producer Ranu, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. READING STUDIO - DAY

Nima holds a large book closed. The screen behind him reads THE HERO RETURNS HOME.

NIMA: Let's find out what happens.

FELI: The screen told us.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Ranu says the title card came from the final page. Oda asks the graphics crew to cover it. Pali lifts a curtain over the monitor, but the camera frames the reflection in the book's glossy cover. The ending is still visible, upside down.

Nima asks Feli to choose a story. She selects one with a red kite on the cover. Pali opens it from the back by mistake. The last page shows the kite caught in a tree. Ranu asks him to close the book before anyone sees more. The camera has already captured the illustration.

ODA: We can still enjoy how it gets there.

FELI: I want to see the kite first.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Ranu asks the graphics operator to replace the ending card with a question mark. The card reads THE HERO RETURNS HOME? Feli says the question mark does not hide anything. Oda suggests a neutral card with only the story title. The crew uses the cover art. The red kite appears again, now with a large arrow pointing toward the tree.

[The host starts the story. Pali turns pages too quickly. The camera sees the hero leaving home, then the last page again. Nima asks him to slow down. Pali flips backward and lands on the kite illustration.]

Feli offers to hold the book open while Oda reads. Nima asks her not to show the ending. She places a bookmark at the first page and holds the cover upright. The book opens to the middle. Oda reads the hero's first attempt to fly the kite. Ranu asks the camera for a reaction shot. It shows Feli holding the book backward, with the final page facing the audience.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Oda turns the book around. She asks Feli to choose the page-turning speed. Feli says one page at a time. Pali follows her finger along the line. The story reaches the storm, but the sound effect plays a triumphant fanfare. Ranu says the audio file is labeled ENDING. Oda stops reading until Pali finds the rain cue. The fanfare continues for three seconds after he mutes it.

INT. READING STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Feli asks to turn the pages herself. Pali sits beside her and waits for her signal. Oda reads a paragraph about the kite pulling against its string. The studio fan makes the paper flutter. Ranu reaches for the fan switch, but Feli holds the page down and finishes the sentence. Nima asks if the wind is part of the story. Feli says it is part of the studio. The crew switches off the fan and the page stays open. Pali asks if Feli wants to keep reading. She reads one line, then hands the book to Oda for the next page. Ranu leaves the ending card blank.

ACT THREE

INT. READING STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Nima asks Feli whether she wants to know what happens next. She says yes, but wants the page to stay covered until Oda reads it. Pali holds a blank card over the page. Oda reads the hero climbing the hill to retrieve the kite. The camera shows the words as she reaches them. The kite is freed, then carried home.

The final page says the hero returns home. Ranu starts the ending graphic. Oda covers the monitor with the book's back cover. Feli closes the book herself. Nima asks what she liked best. She says the part where the hero tries three times before reaching the tree. The host asks if she knew the ending already. She says the screen told her, but the story had more in it.

Pali marks the correct page order with small tabs. Ranu removes the spoiler card. The crew repeats the closing shot with the book closed, title forward, and no summary text behind it. Feli carries the book to the reading shelf after the camera stops.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Ranu deletes the ending graphic. Nima turns the monitor off. Oda places the book with the cover facing out.

FINAL IMAGE: The closed book rests on the shelf beside a blank screen, its kite illustration visible without an arrow or spoiler.
