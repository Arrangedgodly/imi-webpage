# The Royal Kitchen Has a Seating Chart

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: palace costume dramas, formal banquets, and etiquette plots driven by seating diagrams*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-019*

Cast: STEWARD OMI, an orangutan; PRINCESS TALI, a macaque; CHEF, a baboon; PAGE, a capuchin; AMBASSADOR, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. PALACE KITCHEN - EVENING

The steward pins a seating chart to the wall. The chef holds a tray of soup bowls.

STEWARD OMI: The ambassador sits beside the princess.

PAGE: Which princess?

OMI: The one on the chart.

TALI: That is me.

OMI: The chart has you at the pantry.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The palace prepares for a treaty dinner. Omi explains that the seating chart determines who speaks first, who receives the silver spoon, and who may reach the bread. Tali has been placed beside the kitchen door, where the servants carry dishes through. The ambassador is assigned to the head of the table, beside an empty chair labeled TEMPORARY.

TALI: Who is temporary?

OMI: We do not know.

The chef asks where to place the soup. Omi points to the chart. It shows the soup in the center seat. The page says the chart is an old draft from last year, when the banquet had one fewer guest. The ambassador arrives early and sits in the temporary chair. The page tries to move him, but the ambassador says the chair is comfortable.

AMBASSADOR: I prefer this seat.

OMI: It is not approved.

CHEF: The soup is getting cold.

ACT TWO

The princess offers to swap seats with the ambassador so he can stay in the chair he chose. Omi says a swap requires two initials and a witnessed revision. The page brings an inkpot but spills a drop onto the chart. A stain covers the location of the bread basket.

TALI: We can put bread anywhere.

OMI: Not if it is ceremonial bread.

The chef refuses to serve the soup until the seating chart is final. He has arranged bowls in order from mild to spicy. The ambassador wants the mild bowl; the chart assigns him the hottest. The page offers to switch the bowls, but Omi says the label cards must also be exchanged. The kitchen becomes a staging area of chairs, trays, and place cards.

The princess asks the ambassador which seat he wants. He says he wants one where he can hear her proposal. Tali chooses the chair beside her, then moves the temporary chair to the head of the table. Omi checks the treaty protocol. It allows the guest of honor to choose a seat if both sides agree.

OMI: That is not on the chart.

TALI: It is in the treaty.

The page reads the clause aloud. Omi studies it, then marks the chart. The chef places the mild soup in front of the ambassador and leaves the ceremonial bread in the center where everyone can reach it. The ambassador asks whether the empty chair should remain. Omi says it is now reserved for the chart.

INT. BANQUET HALL - EVENING

The guests arrive before the new chart is dry. Omi has marked each seat in pencil. The ambassador follows the chart to the temporary chair and finds Tali already sitting there. The page offers to move the chair, but Tali asks the ambassador to sit beside her instead. The chef waits with soup in two covered bowls.

AMBASSADOR: I came to discuss the treaty, not the furniture.

OMI: The furniture is where the treaty will be discussed.

The page starts carrying place cards from one end of the table to the other. The cards are copied in two languages; one translation has swapped "east" and "west." The ambassador notices the error because his place card points him toward the kitchen. Omi asks the page to stop moving chairs until the labels are checked. The chef covers the soup again so it stays warm.

TALI: Read the place cards aloud.

The page does. The ambassador confirms his name and seat. Tali confirms hers. Omi removes the temporary label, folds it in half, and uses the blank side to mark the bread basket's place. The chef serves the mild soup to the ambassador and the spicy bowl to Tali, whose chart now matches the agreed seating arrangement.

OMI: We have a table.

AMBASSADOR: We have an agreement about the table.



ACT THREE

The dinner begins. Tali speaks first, the ambassador answers, and the treaty discussion proceeds without incident. The chef serves the correct bowls. Omi updates the seating diagram as the meal continues, moving each place card only after its guest has sat down.

PAGE: Is the chart final?

OMI: It is current.

FINAL IMAGE: The steward sits with the chart at one end of the table while the page brings the bread basket to everyone else.
