# Red Alert for a Small Cloud

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rolling television weather alerts, ominous maps, and anchors who turn ordinary clouds into breaking news*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A weather desk sees one small cloud cross the town and must decide whether to issue the station's loudest alert.

Cast: Anchor Vala, a macaque; meteorologist Runi, a gibbon; field reporter Tesk, a capuchin; graphics operator Omi, a baboon; caller Piri, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. WEATHER DESK - DAY

Omi puts a tiny gray cloud over a street map. An alert siren blares. The map turns red.

VALA: We interrupt regular programming for weather.

RUNI: It is a cloud the size of a fruit basket.

VALA: A fruit basket can still interrupt programming.

ACT ONE

Vala asks Runi to confirm the cloud's track. Runi compares the live radar with the window. Outside, sunlight stripes the desk. Omi zooms the map until the cloud covers three neighborhoods and the station parking lot.

OMI: It is growing.

RUNI: The graphic is growing. The cloud isn't.

VALA: We need to tell people what it may do.

RUNI: It may shade the east path for six minutes. There is no rain in the forecast.

Vala turns to camera with grave concern. She announces a Red Alert. A floor assistant wheels in a box of emergency ponchos, then realizes there is no rain and parks beside the studio door.

VALA: Stay with us as the cloud approaches.

RUNI: It is already over the park.

ACT TWO

Tesk reports from the east path. A macaque troop sits beneath a tree. The cloud's shadow crosses the path. A jogger puts on sunglasses, then takes them off. No one else reacts.

TESK: I am standing where the shade begins.

VALA: Do you feel a change in conditions?

TESK: The sun is temporarily behind a cloud.

VALA: Is the cloud moving toward you?

TESK: It moved over me. Now it moved past me.

The cloud passes over Tesk. The camera iris darkens the shot; the studio panics.

OMI: We have a sudden drop in visibility.

RUNI: The camera adjusted its exposure.

VALA: Tesk, can you still see the street?

TESK: Yes. The street is where it was before.

Piri calls in from a picnic bench. She says she is holding her hat on her head because a light breeze has started. Runi checks the wind reading: gentle. Vala asks if that is a precursor to a stronger gust.

RUNI: We have no sign of that.

VALA: What should residents do?

RUNI: Continue what they were doing. If you planned to carry an open bowl, keep it steady.

OMI: I have a bowl graphic.

RUNI: Don't put it on the warning map.

ACT TWO-B

The cloud reaches the station's satellite dish and briefly shades it. The weather map loses its live feed. Omi throws up a scrolling banner: "CLOUD DISRUPTS WEATHER COVERAGE." Vala reads it with concern. Runi checks the backup data and sees the feed is operating again.

RUNI: The signal returned. That was a dish shadow, not a storm.

VALA: Does the cloud know it has interrupted our coverage?

TESK: I asked. It did not respond.

Tesk follows at a walking pace as the cloud passes over a bench, then a playground. Children duck into the shade, then resume climbing. One child asks Tesk to hold a snack while she climbs. The camera turns toward it.

VALA: A possible food-related consequence.

TESK: She gave me the snack to keep it off the ground. I am not eating it.

Omi zooms the cloud over the weather map. It becomes enormous, swallowing the station logo and all the labels. Runi walks to the graphic screen and draws a small circle around the actual cloud.

RUNI: The alert should reflect what is happening outdoors.

VALA: We can revise the category.

OMI: The siren only has loud and louder.

VALA: Turn it off.

Omi presses the button. The siren keeps playing from a second speaker under the desk. Tesk reaches through the camera's view from the field monitor and mimes pulling a plug. Runi finds it and disconnects the sound.

ACT THREE

Runi issues an updated bulletin: brief shade, a mild breeze, no rain expected. Vala explains that the earlier Red Alert was based on an overgrown graphic and withdraws it. Omi removes the red map and returns the street labels to normal size. Tesk reports that the cloud has moved beyond the park.

PIRI (PHONE): Should I bring the picnic inside?

RUNI: If you're comfortable in the shade, you can stay where you are.

Piri says she will finish her sandwich. Vala signs off with the forecast. No siren plays. The ponchos are returned to storage.

TAG

As the show closes, Omi puts a sunny forecast graphic on screen. Another tiny cloud drifts past the station window.

OMI: Do I alert the desk?

RUNI: You can alert the desk that it is cloudy outside.

VALA: Quietly.

FINAL IMAGE: The map remains calm while Omi labels the little cloud with a small gray dot and nothing more.
