# The Soap Opera of the Missing Bowl

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap conventions, where a missing household bowl inspires secret alliances and repeated dramatic revelations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A family's beloved serving bowl vanishes before dinner, and each relative suspects a different branch of the troop.

Cast: Miri, a macaque aunt; Odo, a baboon cousin; Sela, a gibbon grandmother; and Tavi, a capuchin neighbor.

Episode: "As the Fruit Turns"

COLD OPEN

INT. FAMILY KITCHEN, DAY

Sela opens the cupboard. The coconut bowl is gone. A dramatic chord sounds from the radio.

SELA: The bowl has left us.

Odo enters carrying a smaller bowl.

ODO: It is only in the wash.

The radio chord plays again. A monkey turns it off.

ACT ONE

Miri interviews each family member. Odo says he used the bowl for fruit and placed it near the sink. Sela remembers it on the table. Tavi saw a shadow carrying something round across the yard.

MIRI: A bowl-shaped shadow?

TAVI: I thought it was a hat.

They visit the sink. The bowl is not there. A wet ring marks the counter. Odo says the ring could be evidence. Sela says it is water. Miri photographs it anyway.

ACT TWO

EXT. BACKYARD, AFTERNOON

The family follows muddy footprints. They lead to a small monkey's play area, where a toy is covered by a cloth. Odo lifts the cloth. There is no bowl, only a round stone. Sela says the stone is from the garden, not the kitchen.

Miri suspects Tavi because he was the last visitor. Tavi insists he was carrying a basket. The basket has a circular indentation, but it could fit a melon or a bowl. The family asks the neighbor's parrot, who repeats "take it away" in a dramatic voice. Sela recognizes the phrase from her own instruction to move the dirty dishes.

They go to the dish rack. The bowl is upside down beneath a towel. Odo says the case is solved. Miri asks how it got there. Tavi admits he carried it from the table to the sink and forgot to uncover it.

ACT TWO-B

INT. FAMILY KITCHEN, AFTERNOON

Miri stages a dramatic confrontation between Odo and Tavi. She places the empty place where the bowl usually sits at the center of the table. Odo accuses Tavi of taking the bowl. Tavi says he carried it to the sink. Sela says both statements can be true.

MIRI: Someone is hiding something.

SELA: The towel is hiding the bowl.

Odo lifts the towel, but only reveals a stack of plates. The family gasps. A small monkey has stacked the plates on top of the bowl. Tavi tries to lift them all at once. The stack wobbles. Sela catches the top plate while Odo slides the bowl free.

MIRI: The bowl was beneath the evidence.

TAVI: The evidence was dishes.

The parrot repeats "beneath the evidence" from the window. Miri asks the bird if it witnessed the placement. The parrot says, "wash up." Sela recognizes her own voice. She had said it while assigning cleanup.

Odo carries the bowl to the dish rack. Tavi dries it. Miri asks for a final statement from each suspect. Odo says he should have checked the sink. Tavi says he should have taken the towel off. Sela says the bowl should have a shelf with its name written on it.

They label a cupboard shelf "COCONUT BOWL." The label sticks to the bowl instead. Miri peels it off and places it on the shelf.

ACT TWO-C

INT. FAMILY KITCHEN, LATE AFTERNOON

The family checks the rest of the dishes. The plates are stacked, the small bowl is back on the counter, and the towel is hung to dry. Miri wants a final clue for the audience. Odo finds a round water mark on the counter and points to it as new evidence.

SELA: That is from the coconut bowl.

MIRI: Or a second bowl?

Tavi pours a little water on the ring. It spreads into the shape of the bowl's base. The family tests the small bowl beside it. The small bowl leaves a narrower ring. The evidence confirms the coconut bowl was on the counter before Tavi carried it to the sink.

MIRI: The timeline is complete.

Odo rewinds the family's retelling on a small kitchen tablet. The audio plays everyone saying the bowl was in a different place. Sela switches it off and places the bowl on the labeled shelf. The family gathers fruit for dinner.

ACT THREE



INT. FAMILY KITCHEN, EVENING

The bowl is clean and dry. Sela fills it with figs. Miri says the investigation has reunited the family. Odo says they were all in the kitchen the whole time. The parrot repeats "whole time" and flaps its wings.

SELA: The bowl is home.

Tavi sets the figs on the table. A smaller bowl rolls off the counter. Everyone turns dramatically.

TAG

The family catches the small bowl before it hits the floor. The radio plays one last chord.

MIRI: Not again.

Odo turns off the radio and places the bowl in the cupboard.
