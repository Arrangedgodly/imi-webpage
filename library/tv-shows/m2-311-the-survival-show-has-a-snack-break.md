# The Survival Show Has a Snack Break

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival competition television, dramatic resource challenges, and producers who hide the obvious supplies to preserve suspense*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A survival show strands two primate contestants beside a stream with shelter materials, food, and a picnic blanket, while the host keeps trying to turn ordinary preparation into a cliffhanger.

Cast: Host Rell, a macaque; contestant Odo, a gibbon; contestant Palla, a capuchin; field safety lead Neri, an orangutan; and camera operator Tavi, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "The Great Crossing"

### Cold Open

[STREAM BANK. A BASKET SITS BESIDE A SIGN.]

RELL: The contestants have only what they can find in the wild.

NERI: The basket is full of supplies.

RELL: It was placed here by the production team.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[CONTESTANTS ENTER THE CLEARING.]

Rell announces a challenge: build a shelter and cross the stream before sunset. Odo and Palla inspect the area. They find a stack of boards, a coil of rope, and a food basket marked "crew snacks." Neri says the stream is shallow but slippery.

PALLA: We can walk across at the stones.

RELL: The challenge is to build a crossing.

ODO: The sign says "cross the stream," not "build a bridge."

Tavi zooms in on the water. Rell says the contestants are facing danger. Neri points out that the stream is ankle-deep. The camera follows a leaf floating toward the bank as if it were a rescue signal.

### Act Two

[THE CONTESTANTS PLAN.]

Odo wants to lay boards over the rocks. Palla wants to use the rope as a handline. Rell asks which choice shows more ingenuity. The contestants test the boards; one rolls. Palla ties the handline around a tree. The rope is too short, so they move the anchor tree closer.

TAVI: The team is adapting.

RELL: Can we call that a setback?

NERI: It is a shorter tree.

Palla crosses using the handline. Odo follows, holding the board. On the far bank, they build a simple roof from a tarp and two branches. Rell announces a surprise weather event. Tavi sprays a mist bottle. Neri objects that it is not raining.

RELL: It represents rain.

PALLA: We have a tarp.

ODO: And shelter.

The contestants finish before the countdown reaches halfway. Rell asks them to continue gathering resources. They collect dry sticks, check the trail, and share the food basket.

Rell adds a final obstacle: they must cross the stream carrying the supply crate. Odo says the crate can stay on the near bank while the crew carries the food. Rell reminds him that the challenge is scored on teamwork. Palla suggests passing the crate across with the rope. They tie it securely, guide it over the shallow water, and keep it level.

The crate catches on a rock. The host starts a dramatic countdown. Neri tells the crew to stop pulling so the current can loosen it. Palla shifts the rope angle, and Odo lifts the crate clear. Nothing breaks, and the snacks remain dry.

RELL: The contestants have overcome the stream.

NERI: The stream was shallow.

Tavi films the crate landing safely. Rell asks for a slow-motion replay of the rope passing the rock. Neri checks the bank for loose gear and finds only one empty wrapper.

Rell asks which contestant won the crossing. Odo says the crate was carried by both of them. Palla says the rope was tied by both of them. The show awards each contestant one half of the wooden spoon-shaped medal.

### Act Three

[CHALLENGE REVIEW.]

Rell reveals that the basket was intended as a hidden reward, but its label was visible. The "wild food" is a stack of wrapped sandwiches for the crew. Palla offers one to Tavi, whose camera has been running without a break. Neri confirms that everyone has water and the crossing is safe.

RELL: The official challenge is not complete until I sound the victory horn.

He blows the horn. A flock of birds leaves the trees. Odo and Palla lower the tarp so it does not flap. The host asks whether they feel triumphant.

ODO: We finished the task.

PALLA: We took a snack break.

Neri announces that both contestants win because the challenge was cooperative, not competitive. Rell asks the camera to capture a final shot of the empty basket.

### Tag

[STREAM BANK AT SUNSET.]

The crew packs the equipment. Tavi places the mist bottle back in the kit. Rell asks whether the contestants learned that survival is unpredictable.

PALLA: The stream was predictable.

ODO: The host was not.

Rell steps on the rolling board and slides one foot into the water. Neri offers him the handline.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
