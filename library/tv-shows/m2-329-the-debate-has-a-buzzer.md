# The Debate Has a Buzzer

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised public debates, timed rebuttals, audience applause cues, and candidates who answer the moderator instead of each other*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: Two candidates debate how to share a community orchard, while the moderator's timer and applause signals become the biggest obstacles.

Cast: Moderator Nari, an orangutan; candidate Pip, a squirrel monkey; candidate Odo, a capuchin; timekeeper Vex, a macaque; and audience captain Luma, a lemur.

## Episode: "Orchard Forum"

### Cold Open

[COMMUNITY HALL. A BUZZER SOUNDS.]

NARI: We have not started yet.

VEX: The timer is ready.

Pip and Odo sit down. The buzzer sounds again when they touch their microphones.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The candidates are discussing how neighbors should share a small orchard: fixed picking days or an open first-come system. Nari explains that each will get a minute for an opening statement and thirty seconds to answer. Vex tests the timer; it buzzes after ten seconds. He says he can count the remaining time with his tail. Luma, assigned to cue applause, asks whether applause counts against the clock.

Pip supports posted picking days so older neighbors and families can plan. Odo supports open access, with a basket limit so one group cannot take everything. Nari asks them to respond to each other's proposal. Both turn toward her and repeat their own points. The camera cuts between them as if they were disagreeing directly.

NARI: Look at the other candidate when you answer.

PIP: Odo, your basket limit needs a size.

ODO: Pip, your schedule needs a way to handle rain.

The audience applauds. Luma has pressed the cue button by mistake, and a small light flashes on the podium.

### Act Two

Vex gives each candidate a rebuttal. Pip asks Odo whether open hours might favor neighbors who arrive first. Odo says the schedule can reserve a morning for residents who need more time. Odo asks Pip whether fixed days could waste ripe fruit in the rain. Pip suggests posting a rain date in advance. Vex turns off the buzzer before they can finish the practical discussion, then apologizes and lets them continue. The buzzer is so loud that both stop mid-sentence. Nari says the next round will use a bell instead. Odo rings it once to test the tone. Pip replies that the bell itself can become the time limit. Nari asks them not to design the debate equipment.

A resident question asks how the orchard will handle fallen fruit. Pip says volunteers can sort it for compost. Odo says people may take it for cooking, as long as the tree is not damaged. Nari asks for a follow-up. A second resident asks whether the candidates are sharing the question. Vex points to the timer; it has stopped at 00:30. He tries to restart it, and it counts upward.

Pip uses the extra time to explain a rain date. Odo uses it to describe a basket size. The camera operator, thinking the round is over, starts the closing credits. Nari gestures to cut them. The credits freeze over Odo's face, labeling him as "Assistant Sound Mixer." Odo reads the credit and asks whether it comes with a basket.

Luma tries to cue applause for the policy point. The button activates the studio's applause track, which continues through a quiet moment. Vex disconnects the speaker. The audience stops clapping at once; the real people were waiting for the recording to stop. Nari asks Luma to use a hand signal instead. She lifts both hands, and everyone mistakes it for a standing ovation cue. The candidates stand, then sit again when she waves them down.

The debate returns to the orchard. Pip and Odo agree that a calendar can list picking days and rain dates, with open access to any fruit left after the scheduled window. Each wants to claim the compromise as a victory. Nari asks them to state which part came from the other person. Pip credits Odo's basket limit. Odo credits Pip's rain date. The camera finds they are finally facing each other.

### Act Three

The candidates give closing statements. Vex holds up a handwritten clock, because the timer's screen has gone blank. The bell stays silent. Nari gives each speaker equal time by pointing at a large wall clock. Luma cues the audience with a hand sign that everyone understands.

Pip says the orchard calendar will include open hours and a rain date. Odo says the basket limit will keep fruit available. They each promise to post the plan at the community garden. The audience applauds in real time. The moderator closes the forum before the credits can start early again.

### Tag

[HALLWAY.]

Vex takes the timer to the equipment desk. It beeps as soon as he puts it down.

NARI: That one can moderate the next debate.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
