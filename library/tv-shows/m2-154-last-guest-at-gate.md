# The Last Guest Is Still at the Gate

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: elimination reality competitions that manufacture suspense by withholding obvious results*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: A competition show has one remaining contestant, a tortoise who has been waiting at the entrance since the first episode. Host Jori wants to reveal the result in a dramatic finale, while floor manager Venn tries to get the actual obstacle course cleared and the crew home.

Cast: Jori, a macaque host; Venn, a gorilla floor manager; Tula, a tortoise contestant; and Pip, a squirrel scorekeeper.

## Episode: "The Dramatic Final Walk"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A GIANT LED SIGN READS "FINAL TWO." ONE SPOTLIGHT IS LIT. TULA SITS AT A STARTING GATE WITH HER NUMBER PINNED UPSIDE DOWN.]

JORI: Tonight, one contestant will take home the trophy.

PIP: There is one contestant.

JORI: And she faces the ultimate challenge.

[TULA LOOKS DOWN A SHORT CARPETED HALLWAY. A SMALL PAPER FLAG MARKS THE FINISH.]

TULA: Is the floor dry?

VENN: I checked.

[THE SOUND DESIGNER PLAYS A THUNDERCLAP. TULA FLINCHES. A STAGEHAND DROPS A CUP OF CONFETTI.] 

JORI: The tension is already rising.

PIP: The confetti is falling.

### Act One

[JORI TURNS TO THE CAMERA. PIP HOLDS A SCORECARD WITH ONLY ONE NAME.]

JORI: After weeks of fierce competition, we have reached the final two.

PIP: Tula and the space where the other finalist's name should be.

VENN: The other contestant left in episode three.

JORI: The audience deserves a full ceremony.

[TULA RAISES A HAND.]

TULA: Could someone turn the starting bell down? It has been ringing for twenty minutes.

[VENN UNPLUGS THE BELL. IT CONTINUES RINGING FROM A SPEAKER UNDER THE PODIUM.]

PIP: The bell has a backup bell.

VENN: Why?

JORI: A sound cue can never be too prepared.

[VENN PULLS THE SPEAKER PLUG. THE BELL STOPS. JORI WAVES TO THE EMPTY BLEACHERS.] 

### Act Two

[THE COURSE IS A LOW BALANCE BEAM, A CURTAIN TUNNEL, AND THE FINISH FLAG. VENN INSPECTS THE BEAM.]

VENN: The beam is dry. The tunnel is open. The route is clear.

JORI: But can Tula overcome the pressure?

TULA: I would like to begin.

[JORI SIGNALS FOR A COMMERCIAL BREAK. PIP HOLDS UP A CARD: "WE WILL BE RIGHT BACK." THE CAMERA LIGHT IS STILL ON.] 

PIP: We are not going anywhere.

JORI: We need a cliffhanger.

TULA: I am at the start.

[THEY ROLL A DRUM RISER. VENN STRIKES THE DRUM ONCE; A MALLET BOUNCES INTO THE TUNNEL. TULA WAITS WHILE VENN RETRIEVES IT.]

VENN: Ready now.

[TULA CROSSES THE BEAM SLOWLY AND STEADILY. JORI WHISPERS EXCITEDLY INTO A MICROPHONE THAT IS STILL MUTED.] 

PIP: The viewers cannot hear your whisper.

JORI: They can see my suspense.

[TULA REACHES THE TUNNEL. A PRODUCTION ASSISTANT HOLDS THE CURTAIN OPEN LIKE A ROYAL ARCH.]

TULA: Thank you. That is easier.

[AT THE END OF THE TUNNEL, A CREW MEMBER PLACES A SECOND FINISH FLAG. PIP CHECKS THE COURSE MAP.]

PIP: There is no second finish.

JORI: It is a surprise twist.

VENN: It is the exit sign. The finish is on the other side.

[JORI MOVES THE FLAG. THE LIGHTING CREW FOLLOWS IT AND LEAVES TULA IN A DARK PATCH. VENN ROLLS A WORK LIGHT OVER.]

TULA: I can see the actual finish now.

PIP: The contestant has located the course better than production.

[TULA CONTINUES. JORI ASKS FOR A SLOW MOTION SHOT. THE CAMERA OPERATOR WALKING BACKWARDS NEARLY BUMPS INTO THE TROPHY TABLE. VENN CATCHES THE TROPHY BEFORE IT FALLS.]

VENN: We have one contestant and one trophy. Protect both.

[THE STAGEHAND ROLLS OUT A RED CARPET. ITS EDGE CATCHES THE FINISH FLAG. TULA STOPS WHILE VENN FREES THE FABRIC, THEN CONTINUES WHEN THE PATH IS CLEAR.]

JORI: The carpet almost changed the course.

PIP: The carpet is not a contestant.

### Act Three

[TULA REACHES THE FINISH FLAG. THE SIGN FLASHES "FINAL TWO" AGAIN. PIP UNPLUGS IT AND HOLDS UP THE SCORECARD.]

PIP: We have one entrant and one completed course.

JORI: The winner is...

VENN: Tula.

JORI: Let me build to it.

TULA: I have been standing on the podium since lunch.

[JORI LIFTS THE TROPHY. IT IS BIGGER THAN TULA. VENN TILTS IT SO SHE CAN HOLD THE BASE.]

JORI: You endured the course, the wait, and the repeated announcement.

TULA: The course was fair. The wait had more obstacles.

[PIP REMOVES THE UNUSED SECOND MEDAL FROM THE DISPLAY. JORI HANDS TULA THE TROPHY.]

### Tag

[THE CREW STARTS TO STRIKE THE SET. THE SCOREBOARD STILL READS "FINAL TWO." TULA PUTS HER TROPHY ON THE BUTTON. IT CHANGES TO "CHAMPION." ]

PIP: There. A result.

JORI: Can we get a close-up?

[TULA LIFTS THE TROPHY. THE BUTTON SPRINGS BACK TO "FINAL TWO."]

VENN: I am taking the sign home.
