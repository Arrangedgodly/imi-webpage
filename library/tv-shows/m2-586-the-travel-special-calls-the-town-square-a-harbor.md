# The Travel Special Calls the Town Square a Harbor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel-show location reveals, voice-over pickup shots, and a guide forced to narrate one fountain as three destinations*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Guide Luma, a capuchin; host Bram, a mandrill; camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; editor Sela, an orangutan; local keeper Odo, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - DAY

Host Bram walks toward a stone fountain.

BRAM: We have arrived at the northern harbor.

Odo points to a sign reading TOWN SQUARE.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The travel special has filmed a series of destinations in the town square because the camera van arrived late. Host Bram is supposed to introduce the harbor, the old market, and the palace garden. Guide Luma says the square contains only one fountain and the three backdrops are portable panels. Editor Sela wants to use the location footage anyway.

SELA: We have three segments, three outfits, and one fountain.

LUMA: We have one square and three facts.

BRAM: The camera makes it look far away.

TAVI: It makes the fountain look closer. I used a wide lens.

Odo is the keeper of the square. He moves the portable harbor panel away from the real map sign. Bram walks in front of it, then turns to reveal a painted dock. Luma refuses to describe the fountain as the harbor. She points to the actual river on the town map, several streets away.

LUMA: The harbor is two blocks east. This is the square.

BRAM: Can we say "harbor atmosphere"?

LUMA: You can say the gulls flew over from the harbor.

Odo indicates a stone plaque with the fountain's date and builder. Bram says the plaque looks like a market marker. Luma reads the inscription. It says the fountain was built by the town's first stone mason and repaired last spring.

ACT TWO

Sela sets up a voice-over pickup at the edit table. She wants to use Bram's walking shot three times with new narration. Tavi sees the same blue scarf in every take and notices Bram's shoes are muddy only in the supposed palace segment. The chronology would make him walk from the square to the harbor, back to the square, then through the garden without a cut.

TAVI: The shoes tell us the take order.

BRAM: My shoes are versatile.

LUMA: The town has one public garden. It is uphill and the fountain is not in it.

SELA: Could we label the fountain a "gateway to the garden"?

ODO: It is a fountain.

Bram proposes filming actual transitions: walk from the square to the market, then to the harbor, then finish at the garden. Sela checks the schedule and sees there is time if she uses one continuous route and drops the extra costume change. Bram worries that the special will lose its grand reveal.

LUMA: The places are the reveal.

The crew walks to the market. Tavi records the roofline, Bram interviews a vendor about the morning produce, and Luma explains the market's opening hours. Bram tries to call it a secret bazaar. The vendor says it has a large sign and has been open for forty years.

VENDOR ODO: We are not hidden. We are open.

BRAM: The secret was the excellent turnips.

Luma corrects the route map so it shows east toward the harbor. The crew reaches the dock, where Bram describes the ships and the tide. No fountain appears in the shot. Sela records a clean voice-over identifying the harbor and the current weather.

ACT THREE

The final segment is filmed in the garden. Bram walks past real beds and asks the keeper what visitors should notice. Odo describes the plants and asks that viewers stay on the path. Bram starts to repeat the market introduction, catches himself, and names the garden.

BRAM: We have arrived at the palace garden.

LUMA: It is the public garden. The palace is closed.

BRAM: We have arrived at the public garden, which has better access and no palace queue.

SELA: That is a useful correction.

Bram films an outro beside a modest water basin. Luma points out it is a bird bath, not the fountain. Bram checks the shot and agrees to use the town square footage only in the square segment.

BRAM: We can tell the truth and still travel.

TAVI: The camera van may be surprised.

The edit team builds the episode using the actual route: square, market, harbor, then garden. The fountain appears once, behind its correct plaque. The blue scarf is removed from the shot where Bram stands by the river, then returned for the garden outro. Sela labels the map with the real distances.

FINAL IMAGE: A folded town map shows four stops in a clear line, while the fountain appears only at the town square.
