# The House Hunt for a Hammock

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: house-hunting shows that treat ordinary preferences as deal-breaking demands*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A host searches for a new resting spot for a monkey who wants a hammock, shade, and a short walk to fruit.

Cast: Host Odo, a baboon; buyer Piri, a gibbon; scout Nara, a macaque; tree inspector Kito, a capuchin.

Episode: "Location, Location, Branch"

COLD OPEN

EXT. ORCHARD - DAY

Odo presents three branches on a map.

ODO: Piri wants shade, a hammock hook, and fruit nearby.

PIRI: I want one good branch.

KITO: The branch must be strong.

ACT ONE

The first branch is wide and sunny. Piri likes the view but says there is no shade. Odo calls it a deal-breaker. Nara points to a nearby tree with shade, but it is farther from the fruit.

The second branch has a hook but is low to the ground. Kito checks it for strength. It bends when he hangs a test weight. Piri says she does not want to swing into the path.

Odo asks if she can compromise on the fruit distance. Piri says she can carry a snack. Odo writes "fruit access essential" on his clipboard.

ACT TWO

The third branch is shaded and strong, but it has no hook. Nara offers to install a safe hanging strap around the branch. Kito checks the bark and says the strap will not dig into it. Piri tries the branch by sitting first, then testing the strap.

Odo asks if the spot has enough drama for the reveal. Piri says it has a good view of the orchard. Odo asks her to describe how she feels. She says comfortable.

Nara shows a small basket hook nearby. Piri can hang fruit there without putting weight on the branch. Odo says the hook is an unexpected amenity. Kito checks that it is outside the hammock's swing path.

ACT TWO-B

Piri tests the hammock. It hangs level. She swings gently and stops before the tree trunk. Kito watches the knots. Nara adjusts one strap so it sits flat. Odo asks if the spot has a private entrance. Piri points to a path.

The camera crew tries to add a "secret room" reveal by placing a curtain between branches. Piri asks them to remove it; it blocks the breeze. Nara takes it down. The branch stays shaded and open.

ACT TWO-C

Piri asks Kito to explain the maintenance card. It says to check the strap after rain and remove the hammock during strong winds. Odo asks if those conditions require a house inspector. Kito says the owner can check the knots herself. Nara demonstrates how the strap can be loosened without scraping the bark.

The camera crew brings in a hanging lantern and asks if it can complete the outdoor suite. Piri declines; the branch is already comfortable. Odo wants a dramatic reveal, so he pulls back a curtain from the tree trunk. It covers nothing. Nara folds it and carries it away.

Odo presents a proposed upgrade list: lantern, privacy curtain, second fruit hook, and a footpath sign. Piri crosses out the lantern and curtain; both would crowd the branch. She keeps the one basket hook. Kito says the maintenance card is enough for the owner to check the strap after rain. Nara folds the extra list and gives it to the camera crew to take home. They pack the props they brought without adding anything to the tree.

ACT TWO-D

EXT. ORCHARD - AFTERNOON

The production crew sets up a reveal curtain across the path to the shaded branch. Nara watches the wind tug the fabric. Kito has a safety checklist. Piri holds the hammock straps.

ODO: The buyer should discover the room one flap at a time.

PIRI: There is no room. It is a branch.

ODO: That's exactly why we need the curtain.

NARA: The wind is pushing it into the hammock line.

[KITO TESTS THE STRAP. THE CURTAIN CATCHES, PULLING THE HAMMOCK SIDEWAYS.]

KITO: Stop. The anchor is secure; the camera fabric is not.

PIRI: Take it down. I want to see the branch before I sit.

ODO: If we remove the reveal, what will the audience wait for?

PIRI: Me to test the knot.

[THE CREW FOLDS THE CURTAIN. PIRI SITS, LEANS BACK, THEN SWINGS GENTLY. THE BRANCH HOLDS.]

NARA: That was the reveal.

ODO: It had no music.

KITO: I heard the strap settle.

ODO: We can add music after.

PIRI: You can add music while I eat the fruit I brought.

ACT THREE

Piri chooses the third branch. She places a small amount of fruit in the basket hook and settles into the hammock. The spot meets her needs without a renovation. Odo asks if she is ready to sign the papers. Nara hands her a maintenance card instead.

PIRI: I can rest here.

KITO: The tree is sound.

TAG

Odo lies in the hammock beside her.

PIRI: You do not live here.

ODO: I am previewing.

[MUSIC: HOUSE-HUNT THEME.]
