# The Space Station Reality Show Holds a Vote

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality competition formats, audience voting, and contestants who discover that the producer is counting floating objects*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Host Nara, a gibbon; commander Tavi, an orangutan; contestant Riko, a capuchin; contestant Sena, a macaque; producer Vex, a baboon; flight engineer Odo, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. ORBITAL HABITAT - COMMON ROOM

A red voting light blinks. A spoon floats past the camera.

NARA: Tonight, one crew member will be eliminated.

TAVI: From the show or the station?

NARA: The show.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The reality series is filming a week-long teamwork challenge aboard a training habitat that simulates low gravity. Host Nara tells the contestants that the audience has voted. Producer Vex has counted five floating items and concluded that the crew has failed its cleanup challenge.

VEX: The audience saw a spoon, a sock, a pen, a cup, and a bag drifting through the room.

SENA: The spoon and cup are food-service equipment. Odo logged them in the station inventory.

RIKO: The sock is mine. It was clipped to the laundry line.

TAVI: The pen is tethered to the checklist board.

VEX: The bag was not tethered.

ODO: It was a ballast sample in the experiment rack. The rack moved while we replaced the filter.

NARA: The audience voted for the least tidy crew member.

TAVI: We are not in the station. This is a training set. The items are staged for the challenge.

VEX: Then the vote is still real.

NARA: We can ask the audience to vote again.

TAVI: We should first identify which items belong to the task, and which belong to the simulation.

SFX: Velcro straps peel; a cup bumps softly against a wall.

ACT TWO

Vex wants the crew to carry out the elimination scene before the live window closes. Odo says the loose ballast sample needs to be secured before the next fan test. The contestants disagree over whether the audience is judging their teamwork or their dramatic reactions.

RIKO: I can catch the bag.

ODO: Do not chase it. Turn off the fan and wait for it to drift toward the net.

SENA: The fan switch is outside the common room. I will go there with Tavi.

NARA: Can we keep filming?

TAVI: Yes, if we keep the camera on the room and stop asking the crew to vote while equipment is moving.

SFX: Fan shuts down. The bag drifts gently into a mesh net. Odo clips it to the experiment rack.

VEX: The audience may think that was the elimination.

NARA: The vote is about the person, not the bag.

RIKO: Then I vote to eliminate the word "eliminate."

SENA: We can use "selected for the next challenge."

VEX: The show needs stakes.

TAVI: The stakes are real enough. If the filter replacement is delayed, we lose our scheduled fan test.

Odo checks the rack; the ballast sample has a label showing its mass and inventory number. The pen remains on its tether, but the camera angle made it look loose. The sock was detached because the laundry line clip was only halfway closed. Riko retrieves it and fastens the clip until it clicks.

RIKO: The sock is secured.

VEX: The audience has voted for Riko as the least tidy.

RIKO: Because I was the only one whose sock was actually loose.

SENA: The vote can still determine who leads the next challenge. That is a real role, not an expulsion.

NARA: We have the results. Riko will lead the filter team.

RIKO: I accept. I want the checklist and a second clip for the sock.

VEX: Does this mean the drama is over?

TAVI: The filter is still uninstalled.

Before the replacement starts, Vex asks to record a confessional about the vote. Riko puts the checklist on camera and explains the loose sock, the labeled ballast sample, and the tethered pen. The audience can see that the spoon and cup are clipped to the service rail. One snack wrapper drifts toward the intake grille; Sena catches it in the mesh net and puts it in the waste pouch before it reaches the filter housing.

VEX: That is another floating item.

SENA: It was not in the station inventory. It was trash, and it is now contained.

NARA: The vote was about tidiness. We have one actual untidy item and a crew that caught it safely.

ACT THREE

The crew begins the filter replacement. Odo calls out each step; Riko reads the checklist; Sena secures the panel. Vex asks the host to announce another vote, but Nara tells him the audience will vote after they see the completed task. The station team finishes the installation and starts the fan test.

SFX: Filter panel clicks shut. Fan turns on. The clipped tools stay in place.

ODO: Test is stable. No floating items.

VEX: Does the show have a winner?

NARA: The crew completed the filter job. The vote selected Riko to lead it. That is the result.

RIKO: Can the next challenge involve less sock surveillance?

SENA: It can involve the supply drawer.

VEX: There is a spoon in it.

TAVI: The spoon is part of the inventory.

NARA: The audience will have to vote on something else.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew secures the supply drawer while the voting light blinks beside a spoon labeled STATION EQUIPMENT.
