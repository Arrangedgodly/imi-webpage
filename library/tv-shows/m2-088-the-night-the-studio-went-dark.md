# The Night the Studio Went Dark

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live broadcast specials, blackout coverage, and anchors filling dead air*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A live studio loses power during its anniversary special, and the hosts try to continue the show using the only light source available: a glowing fruit display.

Cast: Anchor Leni, a macaque; host Varo, a gibbon; lighting chief Pex, a baboon; guest gardener Miri, an orangutan; and floor runner Tavi, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Still On the Air"

### Cold open

INT. STUDIO, NIGHT

[A bright anniversary set. Leni reads a cue card. The lights cut out.]

LENI: We are experiencing a brief interruption.

VARO: We are still on the air.

PEX: The cameras are off.

TAVI: The emergency camera is on.

MIRI: The fruit display is glowing.

[The dark set is lit by several luminous figs.]

LENI: We have a visual.

VARO: We have a special.

PEX: We have no electricity.

### Act One

INT. STUDIO

[Tavi carries a glowing fig to the desk. Varo holds the cue cards near it.]

LENI: We will continue with our anniversary interview.

MIRI: I am ready.

VARO: We have been on air for ten years.

LENI: Nine years.

VARO: The tenth season.

MIRI: I brought a plant for the anniversary.

PEX: The plant needs a light.

MIRI: It is bioluminescent.

PEX: The plant is not glowing.

MIRI: The fruit is glowing.

VARO: Can we use the fruit as a spotlight?

[Tavi holds the fig too close. The camera overexposes the image.]

LENI: We are now broadcasting a bright circle.

PEX: The camera is working.

VARO: The show has become a circle.

MIRI: We can place the fruit farther away.

[The image returns. The fruit is still visible, but the hosts are in shadow.]

LENI: Please stay with us.

VARO: We will answer questions from the audience.

PEX: The audience cannot see us.

TAVI: They can see the fruit.

### Act Two

INT. STUDIO HALLWAY

[The crew moves through the dark hallway carrying fruit. A second camera follows.]

MIRI: The plants need water.

PEX: The water pump is off.

LENI: Can we water them by hand?

MIRI: Yes.

VARO: We can make that a segment.

TAVI: The anniversary special is now a garden show.

LENI: We can talk about how the studio began.

MIRI: We began in a hollow with one lantern.

VARO: The lantern is in storage.

PEX: The storage door needs power.

TAVI: I can open it.

[He pulls a manual latch. The door opens. Inside are old props, including a hand-cranked projector.]

LENI: We can show old footage.

PEX: The projector needs a lamp.

MIRI: The glowing figs can light the film.

VARO: That is not how projectors work.

PEX: It is how this one works.

[They crank the projector. It displays a shadowy image of the crew's first set.]

LENI: We started with one table and a lantern.

VARO: The table is still here.

TAVI: It is the desk.

LENI: The studio has grown.

PEX: The power has gone.

### Backup broadcast

INT. CONTROL ROOM

[Pex turns a hand crank on the emergency generator. The studio monitor flickers.]

LENI: Is the signal back?

PEX: The camera is up. The lights are not.

VARO: Can we broadcast from the fruit table?

PEX: The backup camera can run for five minutes.

MIRI: The glowing figs can light the set.

LENI: Then we will do the interview by the plants.

[They move to the garden display. Varo asks Miri about the anniversary.]

VARO: What is the first plant you remember growing here?

MIRI: A fig tree.

LENI: Is it still in the grove?

MIRI: It is the old tree outside the studio.

PEX: The roots run under the building.

VARO: The studio was built around the garden.

MIRI: The garden was here first.

[The light flickers. The interview continues.]

### Act Three

INT. STUDIO

[The hosts return to the desk. The projector shows old clips. The crew gathers around the glowing fruit.]

LENI: Our anniversary special is still live.

VARO: We have learned we can keep going with less light.

PEX: We still need the power.

MIRI: The plants can wait.

TAVI: The camera can record the fruit.

LENI: The audience has stayed with us.

VARO: We cannot see them.

LENI: They can see us.

[The lights flicker back on. Everyone squints.]

PEX: The power has returned.

VARO: We are back to regular broadcasting.

MIRI: The fruit display looks ordinary now.

TAVI: The camera is still on the old footage.

LENI: Keep that shot.

VARO: We are live with our past.

PEX: The show is ten years old.

LENI: Nine.

VARO: Ten seasons.

MIRI: We can celebrate both.

[The crew places the glowing fruit in the garden display.]

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, LATER

[The cameras are off. One fig still glows beside the desk.]

TAVI: Is the special over?

PEX: The lights are on.

LENI: Then we can sign off.

[The fig goes dark.]
