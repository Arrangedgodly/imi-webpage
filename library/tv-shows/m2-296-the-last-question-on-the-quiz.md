# The Last Question on the Quiz

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: quiz show television, where the host adds bonus rounds after the contestants have already won*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Two contestants tie in a village quiz, and host Nara keeps inventing extra questions until the winner is the person who wants to go home.

Cast: Host Nara, macaque; contestant Piri, capuchin; contestant Odo, baboon; announcer Seli, gibbon; scorekeeper Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Final, Final Question"

### Cold Open

[QUIZ TABLE. SCOREBOARD SHOWS 10-10.]

NARA: We have a tie.

ODO: Ask the final question.

PIRI: We have answered all the questions.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[SELI ANNOUNCES A BONUS ROUND.]

Seli says the category is village landmarks. Piri names the old fountain. Odo names the west gate. Both answers are correct.

TAVI: The rules say closest answer wins.

NARA: The question asked for a landmark.

PIRI: Then we are still tied.

Nara asks for a second bonus: name the number of steps from the fountain to the gate. Odo says twenty. Piri says nineteen. Tavi counts on the map; the answer is twenty.

ODO: I win.

PIRI: The map uses the long route.

NARA: We need a tiebreaker.

### Act Two

[THE CONTESTANTS DRAW A PICTURE.]

Nara asks each contestant to draw the fountain. Piri draws the water. Odo draws the stone rim. Seli asks the audience which is more recognizable.

SELI: Both pictures show a fountain.

TAVI: We have another tie.

Nara proposes a speed round. She asks what sound the bell makes at noon. Odo says "ring." Piri says "twelve rings." Tavi checks the bell log; it rings twelve times.

PIRI: That is still a correct answer.

NARA: We need one winner.

ODO: We can share it.

NARA: The prize is one basket.

PIRI: We can divide the basket.

TAVI: The basket is empty.

[THE HOST ANNOUNCES A SECOND BONUS ROUND.]

Nara says the tie will be broken by a final question. Piri asks whether "final" means actually final. Nara says yes. Tavi brings the question card. It is blank.

TAVI: We have no question.

NARA: We can write one.

ODO: That gives the host an advantage.

PIRI: Let the scorekeeper write it.

Tavi asks: "How many sides does a triangle have?" Both contestants answer three.

NARA: Correct.

ODO: We remain tied.

PIRI: The triangle has not helped.

Nara asks for a sudden-death challenge. Each contestant must stack three cups without knocking them over. Odo's cups wobble; Piri's hold steady.

TAVI: Piri wins.

ODO: The challenge was not on the original rules.

NARA: The tie is broken.

PIRI: I don't want the basket.

Odo says he can use the basket to carry the trophy. Nara asks if that makes him the winner. Tavi says the basket is not part of the scoring.

Seli asks the studio audience to vote on whether the show should stop. Everyone raises a hand. Nara says the audience has decided.

### Act Three

[THE HOST OPENS THE PRIZE BASKET.]

The basket contains a quiz trophy shaped like a question mark. It is too large to fit through the studio door. Odo tries to turn it sideways. Piri supports the base.

NARA: The prize is stuck.

SELI: The show is also stuck.

Tavi finds a wider exit door. They carry the trophy through together. Nara says the contestants have both won for cooperation.

ODO: That was not a quiz answer.

PIRI: It was a solution.

[THE SCOREKEEPER READS THE RULE CARD.]

Tavi reads aloud: one point per correct answer, no bonus rounds. Nara raises the card toward the camera. Odo reaches for the buzzer; Piri moves it to the far end of the table.

TAVI: Ten points each. The tie stands.

NARA: The host cannot add another question.

ODO: The audience gets to decide.

Seli asks for a show of hands. Everyone raises a hand against another round. Nara turns the scoreboard to the camera: TIE. Odo tries to add a tiny bonus star beside it; Tavi covers the chalk with the rule card.

PIRI: We both won ten points.

ODO: I have one basket.

PIRI: It is empty.

NARA: Put the question cards in it. The school can use them for its next quiz.

Odo loads the used cards into the basket. The oversized question-mark trophy catches on the doorframe as he lifts it; Piri helps him tilt it through the wide exit. The basket stays behind as a card holder.

SELI: Contestants tied, prize delivered, show finished.

NARA: No extra question.

ODO: Not even one about the door?

PIRI: No.

The buzzer sounds once as Odo accidentally leans against it. The crew takes it out of reach.

### Tag

[THE CONTESTANTS STAND BESIDE THE TROPHY.]

Odo asks if they are ready for another round. Piri is already walking toward the exit.

NARA: The quiz ended in a tie. Piri wins the only race left: getting home before the credits.

PIRI: Finally, a question with an exit.

Odo raises the buzzer, but Tavi switches it off. Piri crosses the studio door first. Nara holds up the tie card as the camera catches her leaving the game behind.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES, APPLAUSE.]
