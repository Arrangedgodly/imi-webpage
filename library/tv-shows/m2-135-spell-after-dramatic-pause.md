# Spell It After the Dramatic Pause

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised spelling bees that add suspense music, elaborate word origins, and repeated requests for sentence use*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Mako wants a tense spelling-bee finale. Contestant Tali wants to spell the words before the music, camera zooms, and host's long definitions make everyone forget the letters.

Cast: Mako, a macaque host; Tali, a lemur contestant; Ben, a goose finalist; Oru, a gorilla pronouncer; and Pex, a capuchin scorekeeper.

## Episode: "The Word Is Still Open"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. TALI STANDS AT A MICROPHONE. BEN WAITS AT THE OTHER PODIUM. A SCOREBOARD SHOWS TWO CONTESTANTS LEFT.]

MAKO: Your word is "cocoon."

TALI: May I have the definition?

ORU: A protective covering made by certain insects.

MAKO: And in a sentence?

ORU: The moth rested inside its cocoon.

[TALI SPELLS THE WORD CORRECTLY. THE CROWD APPLAUDS. PEX RINGS A BELL.]

MAKO: We will confirm after the commercial.

TALI: You rang the bell.

PEX: It was the correct bell.

### Act One

[THE NEXT ROUND BEGINS. MAKO OPENS A LARGE ENVELOPE.]

MAKO: Your word is "migrate."

TALI: May I have the definition?

ORU: To move from one region to another, often seasonally.

MAKO: And in a sentence?

ORU: The geese migrate in autumn.

[TALI BEGINS TO SPELL. THE MUSIC SWELLS.]

TALI: M-I-G-R-A-T-E.

[PEX HOLDS UP A GREEN CARD. MAKO TURNS TO CAMERA.]

MAKO: A perfect spelling.

[THE MUSIC CONTINUES AFTER THE WORD IS OVER.]

TALI: Is there a bonus letter?

MAKO: There is not.

[THE SCOREBOARD FLASHES. ONE LIGHT STAYS ON.]

MAKO: The studio is on the edge of its seat.

TALI: I am standing on a mark.

[THE FRONT ROW ADJUSTS IN THEIR CHAIRS. PEX CHECKS THE BOARD.] 

PEX: The last light is only the battery indicator.

MAKO: We have suspense and a low battery.

[PEX CHANGES THE BATTERY. THE SCOREBOARD SHOWS TALI'S POINT.]

PEX: The point is posted. Please stop interviewing the battery.

### Act Two

[ORU OPENS THE NEXT ENVELOPE.]

ORU: Your word is "acorn."

TALI: Could you use it in a sentence?

ORU: The squirrel carried an acorn.

MAKO: How far did the squirrel carry it?

ORU: To its nest.

MAKO: Was the nest high or low?

ORU: That is not part of the sentence.

[THE CROWD LAUGHS. TALI SPELLS THE WORD. MAKO ASKS FOR A REPLAY. PEX PRESSES A BUTTON. THE SCREEN SHOWS THE SCOREBOARD, NOT TALI.]

PEX: The replay camera is pointed at the wrong mark.

[TALI STEPS TO THE MARK. THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN UNTIL ONLY ONE EYE IS VISIBLE.]

MAKO: Can you look more uncertain?

TALI: I already spelled it.

[BEN TAKES HIS TURN AT THE MICROPHONE.]

ORU: Your word is "receipt."

BEN: R-E-C-E-I-T.

[PEX SHOWS THE RED CARD. BEN SHRUGS AND RETURNS TO HIS PODIUM.]

MAKO: One final word will decide the winner. Tali must spell it correctly.

MAKO: The finalists have studied for months.

TALI: I practiced the word list.

MAKO: What inspired your journey?

TALI: I want to spell words correctly.

[ORU OPENS THE FINAL ENVELOPE. PEX SETS A GLASS OF WATER BESIDE THE MICROPHONE.]

ORU: Your word is "tether."

TALI: Definition, please.

ORU: A rope or chain used to tie an animal to a point.

MAKO: And in a sentence?

ORU: The goat's tether was too short.

[THE MUSIC STARTS BEFORE TALI SPEAKS.]

TALI: Could we turn the music off?

PEX: The button is under the table.

[PEX CRAWLS UNDER. THE MUSIC GETS LOUDER.]

TALI: T-E-T-H-E-R.

[PEX EMERGES AND SHOWS THE CORRECT CARD.]

[ORU CHECKS THE REMAINING WORD CARDS. ONE IS UPSIDE DOWN.]

ORU: The next exhibition word is not for scoring.

TALI: Can I read it?

ORU: It says "congratulations."

MAKO: Could we save it for the finale?

TALI: We already had a finale.

[PEX TURNS THE CARD RIGHT SIDE UP AND SETS IT IN THE BOX.] 

### Act Three

[MAKO HOLDS THE TROPHY. TALI WAITS.]

MAKO: We have a winner.

[THE CROWD APPLAUDS. MAKO KEEPS THE PAUSE GOING.]

TALI: Is there another word?

MAKO: No. You won.

TALI: Then can I sit down?

[PEX HANDS HER THE TROPHY. THE SCOREBOARD SHUTS OFF. ORU CLOSES THE ENVELOPE BOX.]

ORU: The words are finished.

MAKO: We still need a winner's interview.

TALI: I listened to the word, asked for the definition, and spelled it.

MAKO: A concise reflection.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN. TALI HOLDS UP THE TROPHY TO BLOCK THE LENS.]

MAKO: Tell us what the trophy means to you.

TALI: It means the spelling round is over.

ORU: That is accurate.

[PEX TAKES THE TROPHY TO THE TABLE SO TALI CAN DRINK WATER.]

[THE CAMERA LIGHT TURNS OFF. TALI EXITS THE STAGE WITH THE TROPHY.]

PEX: We should print the word list for next time.

ORU: Use large type.

MAKO: And a longer pause?

[TALI LEANS BACK THROUGH THE WINGS, HOLDING HER TROPHY.]

TALI: No.

### Tag

[MAKO READS THE NEXT SEASON'S PROMO CARD.]

MAKO: More words, more suspense, and more dramatic pauses.

ORU: Fewer sentences, please.

[PEX SWITCHES OFF THE MUSIC BEFORE IT STARTS.]
