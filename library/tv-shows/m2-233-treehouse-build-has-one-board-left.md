# The Treehouse Build Has One Board Left

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: competition build shows that impose a reveal deadline before checking whether the materials fit*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: Two teams build a small treehouse reading nook, but the host's dramatic deadline ignores the tree's uneven branches.

Cast: Host Sela, a macaque; builder Venn, a gibbon; builder Omi, a capuchin; judge Ralo, a baboon; and tree keeper Tavi, a lemur.

Episode: "One Board Short"

COLD OPEN

EXT. PRACTICE GROVE - MORNING

A tree stands beside two stacks of boards. Sela starts a countdown clock.

SELA: You have two hours to build a reading nook.

TAVI: The tree has a low branch on the left.

VENN: We can build around it.

OMI: The plan shows an empty corner.

[THE TEAM HOLDS THE PLAN AGAINST THE TREE. THE BRANCH COVERS THE DRAWN CORNER.]

ACT ONE

Venn measures the trunk. Omi sorts the boards by length. Ralo checks a scorecard.

RALO: Points for height, symmetry, and a dramatic entrance.

TAVI: The roots are shallow here.

SELA: Can you move the platform farther up?

TAVI: The tree should not carry the whole structure.

OMI: We can make a freestanding base beside it.

VENN: Then it will not be a treehouse.

[THEY HOLD UP A SHORT BOARD. IT REACHES FROM THE TRUNK TO A SUPPORT POST.]

ACT TWO

EXT. PRACTICE GROVE - LATER

The builders assemble a low platform around the tree without attaching it. Sela moves the camera. Ralo watches the clock.

SELA: The platform needs to look taller.

VENN: It needs to sit level.

OMI: The ground slopes toward the creek.

TAVI: The roots are below that side.

RALO: The tallest build wins.

OMI: A tall platform would lean.

[THEY PLACE A LEVEL ON THE FRAME. THE BUBBLE SLIDES TO ONE SIDE.]

VENN: We need to adjust the feet.

SELA: That takes time.

TAVI: It takes less time than rebuilding a fallen platform.

[OMI SHIMS ONE FOOT WITH A THIN BOARD. THE BUBBLE CENTERS.]

ACT TWO-B

EXT. PRACTICE GROVE - AFTERNOON

One full-length board remains. Venn wants it for the entry step. Omi wants it for a roof brace.

VENN: The step is too short.

OMI: The roof needs another support.

RALO: A roof makes a stronger reveal.

TAVI: The leaves already shed most of the rain.

SELA: We need a final choice.

[THEY SET THE BOARD ACROSS THE ENTRANCE. IT BRIDGES THE STEP AND SUPPORTS THE ROOF EDGE.]

OMI: It does both jobs.

VENN: It is not centered.

RALO: It is stable.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. PRACTICE GROVE - FINAL HOUR

Ralo walks around the structure. Sela sets a camera inside the nook. Tavi checks the tree's bark.

RALO: The other team built twice as high.

SELA: Their platform is in the next grove.

VENN: How does it look?

RALO: It has a dramatic ladder.

OMI: Can it fit a book?

RALO: I did not check.

[SELA TRIES TO SET THE CAMERA ON THE PLATFORM. THE LENS POINTS AT THE LOW BRANCH.]

SELA: The branch is in frame.

VENN: Move the camera?

TAVI: The branch shades the platform in the afternoon.

OMI: Then the branch is part of the design.

RALO: The scoring card says symmetry.

VENN: The tree has uneven limbs.

SELA: We can set the bench at an angle that leaves both branches clear.

[THEY TURN THE LOW BENCH. IT FITS BETWEEN THE SUPPORT POSTS.]

TAVI: Now the doorway is clear.

OMI: And the tree remains untouched.

RALO: The judges can sit and read.

SELA: The reveal is ready.

ACT TWO-D

EXT. PRACTICE GROVE - SUNSET

Ralo tests the platform with a stack of books. Venn holds the support post while Omi checks the entrance.

RALO: The platform supports the books.

VENN: It supports the people too, once we complete the final check.

TAVI: The weight sits on the posts, not the tree.

SELA: Can we film the judges stepping in?

TAVI: After I finish checking the base.

[TAVI PRESSES EACH SUPPORT FOOT. NONE SHIFT.]

OMI: The platform is ready.

RALO: We can score it now.

SELA: The other team is still building.

VENN: Their deadline is the same.

[A DISTANT SAW STOPS. THE OTHER TEAM CALLS THAT IT NEEDS ANOTHER BOARD.]

RALO: We have finished with one board left.

OMI: We used the last full board.

VENN: The short pieces became the steps.

ACT THREE

EXT. PRACTICE GROVE - FINAL INSPECTION

Ralo steps onto the platform. It stays level. Tavi checks that no branch is bearing the weight.

RALO: The build is safe and useful.

SELA: The winning entry is the one that fits the tree.

VENN: It is a reading nook, not a tower.

OMI: There is room for two books and two people.

[TAVI SETS TWO BOOKS ON THE PLATFORM. THE LOW BRANCH SHADES THEM.]

TAG

Sela starts a new timer for cleanup. The builders carry the leftover scraps away.

RALO: The build is finished.

SELA: Now we have a dramatic teardown.
