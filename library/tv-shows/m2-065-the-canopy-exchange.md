# The Canopy Exchange

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: financial market broadcasts, urgent ticker graphics, and confident expert panels*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A business-news panel tries to predict the price of figs during harvest week, while the ticker reports only the number of figs currently being eaten on set.

Cast: Anchor Dena, a macaque; market analyst Goro, a baboon; orchard owner Meli, an orangutan; ticker operator Pex, a capuchin; and field reporter Luma, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Fig Futures Report"

### Cold open

INT. CANOPY EXCHANGE STUDIO, MORNING

[A red ticker runs beneath the desk: "FIGS EATEN: 1." Dena points to a graph.]

DENA: Good morning. Fig futures are down.

GORO: They are not down. They are on the table.

MELI: The price is steady.

PEX: The ticker says two figs eaten.

DENA: We have a market movement.

LUMA: I am live in the orchard.

DENA: What is the outlook?

LUMA: The trees are full.

GORO: The market may be oversupplied.

MELI: The fruit is not for sale yet.

### Act One

INT. STUDIO

[Goro studies a graph with a single line. Pex updates the ticker.]

DENA: Analyst, what is driving the market?

GORO: Expectation, scarcity, and a very active lunch hour.

MELI: There is no scarcity. The harvest starts tomorrow.

GORO: The market is reacting to the possibility of scarcity.

MELI: The figs are still on the trees.

DENA: Is there a futures contract?

GORO: There will be.

MELI: Who is selling it?

GORO: I am working on that.

PEX: The ticker says "figs eaten: three."

DENA: We have a new high.

MELI: The crew is eating the inventory.

GORO: That is consumption.

DENA: Can we see the graph?

[The graph shows a straight line.]

LUMA: The line has not moved.

GORO: It is consolidating.

MELI: It is a marker on a board.

DENA: We will take a quick look at the currency market.

PEX: We have one shell and a smooth pebble.

DENA: Which is stronger?

GORO: The pebble has gained weight.

MELI: It is the same pebble.

### Act Two

EXT. ORCHARD

[Luma stands beside a fig tree. The wind shakes a branch. One fig falls into a basket.]

LUMA: A fig has entered the basket.

DENA: Is that a sell signal?

LUMA: It is a ripe fig.

GORO: The drop suggests volatility.

MELI: The tree is shedding fruit.

LUMA: The farmer is collecting it.

DENA: How many figs remain?

LUMA: I have not counted.

GORO: That uncertainty is moving the market.

[Back in studio, the ticker reads "FIGS EATEN: 4."]

DENA: Pex, why does the ticker show another fig?

PEX: I ate one.

MELI: You are the ticker operator.

PEX: I was testing the supply.

GORO: A live market check.

MELI: You have eaten the data.

DENA: We need an outside perspective.

[The studio phone rings. Dena answers.]

DENA: We have a caller.

CALLER, O.S.: What is the price of one fig?

GORO: It depends on the market.

CALLER: I am standing beside a tree.

MELI: Then ask the tree.

DENA: The tree has no phone.

CALLER: It has figs.

GORO: We have reached a new price discovery phase.

### Market close

INT. STUDIO

[The crew has set a bowl of figs beneath the graph. Each time the graph moves, someone takes one.]

DENA: The line has dropped.

PEX: Two figs have been eaten.

GORO: The market is correcting.

MELI: The crew is eating the chart.

DENA: We have a strong sell signal.

MELI: You have an empty bowl.

LUMA: The orchard is not empty.

GORO: We should move outside.

[The panel carries the graph to the orchard. A fig falls into it.]

PEX: The market has gained one.

DENA: We have seen an organic rebound.

MELI: The fig fell off a tree.

GORO: It has entered the exchange.

### Act Three

INT. STUDIO

[The panel returns. The ticker now reads "FIGS EATEN: 6." Pex sets down an empty plate.]

DENA: Our final report: the harvest begins tomorrow.

MELI: The figs will be picked when they are ripe.

GORO: The futures market will open after we determine the supply.

DENA: And the ticker?

PEX: I am switching it to "FIGS PICKED."

[The ticker changes. A crew member reaches for a fig on the desk. The number jumps.]

DENA: It says one.

MELI: One fig has been picked.

GORO: The ticker is accurate.

DENA: Does that mean prices are up?

MELI: It means someone picked a fig.

GORO: It may also mean the first trade.

MELI: It was picked for lunch.

DENA: The market is hungry.

PEX: I have another plate.

MELI: No. Leave the fruit in the orchard until it's ready.

[The crew returns the remaining figs to Meli's basket.]

DENA: We will continue the story tomorrow.

GORO: After the opening bell.

MELI: The bell is at the door.

PEX: The door is open.

[The studio bell rings as the last fig is carried away.]

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, LATER

[The ticker reads "FIGS EATEN: 0." Goro points to the flat graph.]

GORO: The market is stable.

[The graph falls off the wall.]
