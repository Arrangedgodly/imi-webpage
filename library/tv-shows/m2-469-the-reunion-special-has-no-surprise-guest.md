# The Reunion Special Has No Surprise Guest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reunion specials, emotional recap montages, and hosts who announce people already seated onstage*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Mera, a gibbon; former castmate Jori, a macaque; former castmate Ulo, an orangutan; producer Venn, a baboon; stage manager Tavi, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. REUNION STAGE - NIGHT

Jori and Ulo sit in two chairs beneath a banner reading TEN YEARS LATER. The host stands between them. A third chair is empty.

MERA: We have one surprise guest joining us tonight.

TAVI: That chair is for the cue cards.

VENN: We no longer have a guest. Their train was canceled.

JORI: Then why is there a chair?

[The stage manager removes the empty chair and puts the cue cards on the host's desk. The host looks into the camera.] 

MERA: Our surprise is that the guest chair was never a guest.

[The reunion theme starts too early. The former castmates look at each other while the music plays.] 

ACT ONE

INT. REUNION STAGE - CONTINUOUS

The host begins with a montage of old scenes. The montage includes Jori leaving a room, Ulo opening a door, and a dramatic shot of a bowl. Mera asks what viewers should remember. Jori says the bowl was a prop, and Ulo says the door never worked.

MERA: Did the door cause tension between you?

ULO: It was a set door. It stuck.

JORI: I had to hold it for three takes.

[The producer signals for a heartfelt pause. The castmates start laughing about the door. The host asks if this is an unresolved conflict.]

JORI: It was resolved when the stage crew fixed the hinge.

TAVI: We have the maintenance note.

[The stage manager produces the old work order. Mera asks if the guests have stayed in touch. They say yes. The host checks the cue cards for a dramatic reveal; the next card only says COFFEE.] 

MERA: We could ask what you discussed over coffee.

ULO: The old set door.

ACT TWO

INT. GREEN ROOM - LATER

The producer tries to film a confessional about the missing guest. Tavi tells Venn not to frame the train delay as a betrayal. The host asks Jori and Ulo to each describe the other in one word. They give the same word: patient.

VENN: Can one of you say it with more suspicion?

JORI: No.

ULO: We are actually friends.

[The crew plays another montage. A laugh from the audience is heard before the joke in the clip. Tavi checks the audio track and finds the laugh cue placed a beat early.] 

TAVI: The audience laugh belongs after the line.

MERA: We can replay the montage.

JORI: Or we can keep talking.

[The host sets aside the remote and asks what they have done since the show. Jori describes building a community theater; Ulo says he repairs set doors there. The producer asks if they have a rivalry. Ulo shows a small wrench; Jori shows a paintbrush.] 

JORI: He fixes the door. I paint it.

ULO: We share the work order.

[Venn brings out two confessionals prepared for the absent guest. Mera starts to read the questions, but Jori asks to answer about the door repair instead. Ulo agrees to describe the community theater's current production.] 

MERA: We could ask each of you what the other changed in your life.

JORI: He showed me how to fix a hinge.

ULO: She showed me how to paint without covering the latch.

[The producer signals for a reaction shot. Tavi leaves the camera on both castmates in a two-shot. They look at the old clip of the door sticking, then smile.] 

VENN: That is not the dramatic reveal we planned.

JORI: It is what we remember.

ACT THREE

INT. REUNION STAGE - NIGHT

Mera closes the interview without waiting for the missing guest. Jori and Ulo stand together and walk toward the backstage door. It opens smoothly. The host calls after them with a question about a new season, but the stage manager signals that the segment has ended.

MERA: Would you return for another reunion?

JORI: If there is a real conversation.

ULO: And a door that opens.

[The host turns to camera. The producer starts the planned surprise music, but Tavi cuts it off before another announcement.] 

VENN: We are not adding a guest who is not here.

MERA: Then the special has reached its ending.

[The crew stacks the chairs and removes the banner. The cue card that promised a surprise is turned over; the reverse side reads THANK YOU.] 

TAG

The host announces that the old door is open. Jori and Ulo have already walked through it. Mera waves to the empty doorway, then follows them offstage.

FINAL IMAGE: The reunion set stands empty except for the fixed door, which remains open.
