# The Cabin Has a Comment Section

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation shows that let remote viewers vote on structural decisions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Renovation host Osa is live-streaming repairs to a mountain cabin and lets viewers vote on materials. Builder Kavi has to keep the structure weatherproof while the poll keeps choosing stylish options that are wrong for the cabin's exposed ridge.

Cast: Osa, a macaque host; Kavi, a gorilla builder; Leni, a langur owner; and Puck, a capuchin livestream producer.

## Episode: "Vote Before the Rain"

### Cold Open

[OPEN CABIN PORCH. DARK CLOUDS GATHER OVER THE RIDGE. OSA HOLDS A PHONE ON A GIMBAL WHILE KAVI SECURES A TARP.]

OSA: Viewers are choosing our roof color.

KAVI: The roof is still leaking.

PUCK: The poll has two choices: copper glitter or cloud white.

LENI: We need a flashing strip at the chimney.

[THE CHAT VOTES FOR COPPER GLITTER. A SAMPLE SHINGLE GLINTS IN THE LIGHT. KAVI SETS IT DOWN AND COVERS THE OPEN SEAM.] 

OSA: The people have spoken.

KAVI: The water is still coming in.

### Act One

[INSIDE, LENI PUTS A BOWL UNDER A DRIP. PUCK POINTS THE CAMERA AT IT AND ADDS A HEART ICON TO THE STREAM.]

PUCK: The bowl is trending.

LENI: It was my mixing bowl.

KAVI: I need to inspect the flashing before we buy material.

OSA: Can the chat pick a color for the bowl?

LENI: The bowl goes back to the kitchen.

[THE CHAT VOTES TO KEEP IT UNDER THE LEAK. LENI TAKES IT AWAY. A DROP LANDS ON PUCK'S LAPTOP.]

PUCK: The poll has a consequence.

KAVI: The roof has a consequence.

[KAVI SHOWS THE VIEWERS A LOOSE PANEL AND EXPLAINS THE REPAIR. OSA OPENS A SECOND POLL: "FIX IT OR ADD A SKYLIGHT?" THE VOTE TILTS TOWARD SKYLIGHT.] 

LENI: The last owners installed a skylight. It leaked for three winters.

PUCK: The chat wants before-and-after footage.

KAVI: The before is the leak. The after is the leak stopped.

[OSA POINTS THE CAMERA AT THE CEILING. A DROP LANDS ON THE LENS. PUCK WIPES IT WITH A CLOTH, AND THE VIEWER COUNT JUMPS WHEN THE IMAGE CLEARS.] 

OSA: Apparently the audience also wanted the camera dry.

LENI: We all did.

### Act Two

[THE RAIN STARTS. KAVI AND LENI SET A LADDER ON LEVEL GROUND AND SECURE IT. OSA HOLDS THE PHONE UNDER AN UMBRELLA.]

[THE CHAT VOTES TO PLACE THE LADDER ON THE PORCH RAIL FOR A BETTER ANGLE. KAVI DISABLES THE POLL AND SETS THE FEET ON THE GROUND.]

KAVI: The ladder stays level.

PUCK: The audience says the rail looks sturdier.

LENI: The audience is not standing on it.

[OSA SETS THE PHONE ON A DRY SHELF. THE CHAT CONTINUES TO VOTE ON CAMERA ANGLES, BUT THE CREW KEEPS THE LADDER IN THE SHOT FROM THE SIDE.] 

[PUCK SETS A TIMER FOR THE LIVE POLL. IT COUNTS DOWN TO ZERO, THEN CONTINUES INTO NEGATIVE NUMBERS.]

PUCK: The result is delayed.

OSA: The clock says the poll ended.

LENI: The roof did not wait for a poll.

[KAVI FINISHES THE REPAIR BEFORE THE POLL CLOSES. OSA READS THE FINAL VOTE: THE CHAT CHOSE "OTHER." LENI HOLDS UP THE REPLACED FLASHING.]

LENI: Other was the right answer.

OSA: The audience wants a skylight.

KAVI: We need a dry roof first.

PUCK: The vote is 62 percent skylight.

LENI: This is not a roof survey. It is a cabin.

[KAVI REMOVES A LOOSE BOARD AND FINDS WATER RUNNING ALONG THE CHIMNEY. HE POINTS TO THE DAMAGED SEAL.]

KAVI: This flashing has to be replaced before any finish goes on.

OSA: Can we ask the viewers which sealant?

KAVI: We need the one rated for the roof material and the weather.

[PUCK PUTS UP A POLL WITH TWO BRANDS. KAVI CHECKS THE LABELS AND SELECTS THE COMPATIBLE ONE. THE CHAT CHOOSES THE OTHER.] 

PUCK: The losing sealant has more votes.

LENI: It is for indoor tile.

[OSA TURNS THE PHONE TOWARD THE PRODUCT LABEL. THE CHAT CHANGES ITS VOTE.]

### Act Three

[KAVI REPLACES THE FLASHING AND TESTS THE SEAM WITH A HOSE. THE WATER RUNS OFF THE ROOF, AWAY FROM THE CABIN. THE STREAM CHAT SENDS ROOF EMOJIS.] 

OSA: The viewers chose the repair.

KAVI: They chose after seeing the damage.

PUCK: The glitter shingle poll is still open.

LENI: We can discuss color once the roof is sound.

[OSA CLOSES THE POLL AND PUTS THE PHONE DOWN. SHE HELPS LENI CARRY THE LADDER INSIDE. PUCK'S SCREEN DISPLAYS A NEW VOTE: "WHERE SHOULD THE PHONE GO?" ]

PUCK: The chat picked the bowl.

[LENI TAKES HER MIXING BOWL FROM THE KITCHEN AND SETS IT ON THE COUNTER, FAR FROM THE DRIP. THE CHAT VOTE DOES NOT MOVE IT.] 

### Tag

[THE RAIN STOPS. OSA PICKS UP THE COPPER GLITTER SAMPLE. IT IS STILL DRY AND SPARKLY.]

OSA: The roof can wait for the next poll.

KAVI: The roof is done.

[THE CHAT SENDS A VOTE TO ADD A SECOND STORY. LENI CLOSES THE LAPTOP BEFORE THE VOTE FINISHES.]
