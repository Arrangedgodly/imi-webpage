# The Perfect Porridge Challenge

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competition television, where judges demand a perfect dish while changing the rules mid-bake*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Two cooks have twenty minutes to prepare porridge, while host Odo and judge Pava keep adding presentation rules that have nothing to do with breakfast.

Cast: Host Odo, a baboon; cook Nara, a macaque; cook Seli, a capuchin; judge Pava, a gibbon; assistant Tavi, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Golden Spoon"

### Cold Open

[KITCHEN SET. TWO BOWLS OF OATS.]

ODO: Your challenge is simple: make porridge.

NARA: With oats?

ODO: We will reveal the ingredients after the break.

SELI: The oats are already on the table.

TAVI: So is the water.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CLOCK STARTS.]

ODO: You have twenty minutes to create an unforgettable breakfast.

NARA: Does it need to be edible?

PAVA: We will judge flavor, texture, and narrative.

SELI: What is the narrative of porridge?

PAVA: It begins as oats and becomes something else.

Nara adds water to a pot. Seli heats the pan. Odo walks between them with a clipboard.

ODO: The judges are looking for innovation.

NARA: I am making the recipe on the packet.

ODO: That is a bold choice.

Tavi reads the packet aloud: two cups of oats, four cups of water, simmer until soft. Pava says the dish needs a surprise element.

SELI: The oats are the surprise.

PAVA: I expect a reveal.

Nara stirs. Seli adds a pinch of salt. Odo asks if they can plate the porridge in a carved fruit shell. Tavi says the shell has a crack.

### Act Two

[THE CLOCK READS TEN MINUTES.]

Pava walks along the counter. She asks the cooks to explain their process while continuing to stir.

PAVA: Nara, what are you feeling?

NARA: The spoon is sticking.

PAVA: And emotionally?

NARA: I am stirring.

Seli's pot bubbles over. Tavi lowers the heat. Odo calls it a setback. Seli lifts the lid; the porridge has thickened.

SELI: It is ready.

PAVA: It is early.

SELI: The oats are soft.

PAVA: But the clock has ten minutes.

ODO: The challenge is not over until the timer ends.

NARA: What am I supposed to do with finished porridge?

PAVA: Develop it.

Seli adds dried berries. Nara adds chopped fruit. Pava asks for a garnish that represents the cooks' journey.

NARA: My journey was from the pantry to this stove.

TAVI: Mine was from the sink to the counter.

Odo sets out decorative spoons. One is too large for the bowls and tips Nara's porridge onto the counter.

[SFX: BOWL SLIDES.]

NARA: The spoon has changed my dish.

PAVA: It has challenged your plating.

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK SHOWS TWO MINUTES.]

Nara cleans the spill. She divides her porridge into two bowls, one warm and one cooled slightly. Odo says the judges must taste both. Pava asks which version tells the stronger story.

NARA: The warm one is breakfast. The cool one is waiting for breakfast.

SELI: Mine has berries and salt.

PAVA: We expected a crunchy element.

Tavi offers a small handful of toasted seeds. Pava says the ingredient table has been closed.

TAVI: They are from the pantry.

ODO: Were they on the challenge list?

TAVI: The oats were not on the list either.

Odo checks the card. It says only "Make porridge." He agrees the seeds are allowed. Seli sprinkles a few over her bowl. Nara declines, saying her dish is finished.

The timer rings. The judges taste the bowls. Pava holds up two score cards, but both are blank.

PAVA: We need to agree on the scoring categories.

ODO: The categories were flavor, texture, and narrative.

PAVA: I would like to add spoon compatibility.

NARA: The spoon tipped my bowl.

TAVI: It is too large.

SELI: Then the spoon should be judged.

Odo places the spoon beside the bowls. Its handle is longer than the counter.

ODO: It has presence.

NARA: It has fallen off the table twice.

Pava asks the crew to test whether it can rest on the serving tray. Tavi turns it sideways; it fits.

TAVI: The spoon works on the tray.

PAVA: We have a plating solution.

Pava asks each judge to record a score. She praises Seli's crunch and Nara's texture. Odo asks for the narrative.

NARA: I followed the recipe.

SELI: I made mine thicker.

TAVI: Both are porridge.

PAVA: The decision is difficult.

### Tag

[THE JUDGES HUDDLE.]

Odo announces a tie. The prize is a golden spoon. It is too large to fit into either bowl.

NARA: The spoon is not useful.

SELI: It is decorative.

PAVA: It tells a story.

Tavi uses it to stir the remaining pot. The porridge loosens to a smooth consistency.

ODO: The spoon has changed the texture.

NARA: We can serve it now.

[SFX: BOWLS SET DOWN.]

PAVA: The real winner is breakfast.

SELI: Breakfast was the challenge.

ODO: We have completed the episode.

TAVI: And the porridge.

[SFX: SPOON TAPS POT, THEME OUT.]
