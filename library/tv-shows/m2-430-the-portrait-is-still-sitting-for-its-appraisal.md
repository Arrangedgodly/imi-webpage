# The Portrait Is Still Sitting for Its Appraisal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: antiques appraisal programs that assign a price before asking who owns the object*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Pavi, a macaque; appraiser Sela, a lemur; collector Vex, an orangutan; conservator Tavi, a gibbon; portrait sitter Rini, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. APPRAISAL STUDIO - DAY

[A gilded portrait stands on an easel. Rini sits beside it in the same red sash and holds the same pose. Pavi leans toward camera.]

PAVI: Is the painting valuable, or is the person in it?

SELA: The person is here.

[The portrait blinks. Theme music strikes.]

ACT ONE

PAVI: Welcome to Heirloom Hour, where every object has a price and every owner has a story.

VEX: I inherited the portrait from my aunt.

RINI: I painted it last week.

VEX: My aunt inherited you with the frame.

TAVI: You are not an object.

VEX: I brought the receipt.

[He presents a handwritten card. It says "One painting, one frame, one sitter."]

SELA: This is not a receipt. It is a list.

PAVI: Lists can become evidence.

[Rini adjusts her sash. The portrait's painted sash shifts at the same time because the canvas is propped against her shoulder.]

TAVI: The portrait is moving.

RINI: I am moving.

PAVI: The appraisal has begun.

[Sela inspects the frame with a small magnifier. The frame has fresh paint along one edge. She looks at Vex.]

SELA: Is this antique?

VEX: The frame was in my aunt's attic.

RINI: I built it from a cabinet door.

VEX: The attic was old.

ACT TWO

[Tavi turns the frame around. A hinge and a short handle are attached to the back.]

TAVI: This opens like a cupboard.

RINI: It was a cabinet door.

VEX: It is a rare convertible portrait.

SELA: It is a repurposed frame.

[Pavi holds a card reading "What would you sell it for?" Vex whispers a number to him.]

PAVI: Vex believes the portrait is worth four hundred coins.

RINI: The paint cost three.

VEX: The story increases the value.

TAVI: Whose story?

[Vex says his aunt found the portrait at a fair. Rini says Vex's aunt asked her to sit for it. Sela looks at the painted face, then at Rini's pose.]

SELA: It is not a family heirloom. It is a commission.

VEX: My aunt owned it.

RINI: She gave it back to me when she saw the frame.

[The frame opens. Inside is a folded note and a pair of costume earrings. Rini takes them out.] 

RINI: Those are mine too.

VEX: They were stored in the painting.

TAVI: They were stored in the cabinet.

PAVI: We have uncovered a second layer.

SELA: We have opened a door.

[Tavi lays a soft cloth under the frame and lifts it from the easel. A strip of felt falls from the bottom edge.]

TAVI: This is not a hanging wire.

VEX: It is a custom support.

RINI: It is the felt I put there so the cabinet would not scrape the floor.

[Sela turns the frame slightly. A pencil mark on the back reads "Kitchen."] 

SELA: That label narrows the provenance.

PAVI: The portrait has traveled from an attic to a kitchen.

RINI: It went from the pantry to the studio.

VEX: The pantry is in the kitchen.

TAVI: Then it did not travel very far.

[The camera zooms in on the pencil mark. Pavi presents it like a signature.]

PAVI: A maker's mark.

RINI: A reminder to put the door back.

ACT THREE

[Sela writes an appraisal. Vex stands by the portrait, ready for a large number. Pavi turns a monitor toward camera.]

PAVI: The estimated value is...

SELA: The canvas is worth the cost of its materials. The frame is worth less than a new one. The sitter's time is not for sale.

VEX: What about the historical value?

SELA: It has a clear record: painted last week, by Rini, for Vex's aunt.

RINI: Can I have the portrait back?

VEX: The show cannot appraise an object it does not own.

TAVI: The owner is asking.

[Vex looks at the receipt, then at the portrait.]

VEX: I am not keeping it. I was hoping it would be valuable.

RINI: It is useful. The cabinet needs a door.

[She lifts the portrait from the easel and fits its hinged frame over a small prop cupboard. It closes neatly. The painted face now hangs on the front of the cupboard.]

PAVI: We have found its market value.

SELA: It is a door.

VEX: It is an antique door.

TAVI: It was a cabinet door last week.

[The cupboard opens. Rini takes out a brush and paints a small number on the edge.] 

RINI: Three coins for the paint.

VEX: I will buy it.

RINI: You already have a receipt.

TAG

[Pavi asks Rini to sit for a second appraisal. She closes the cupboard door on the camera.]

PAVI: We will return after the commercial.

[From inside the cupboard, the portrait's painted eyes look through the hinge gap.]

SELA: That is the highest estimate yet.
