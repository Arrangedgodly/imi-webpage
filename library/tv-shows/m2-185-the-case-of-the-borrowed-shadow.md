# The Case of the Borrowed Shadow

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedural convention, investigating a harmless shadow that appears to move between neighbors' yards*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A patrol pair investigates claims that a shadow has been stolen, only to find the neighborhood's tree and sun have changed position.

Cast: Officer Odo, a baboon; Officer Nara, a gibbon; resident Pavi, a capuchin; and gardener Kito, a macaque.

Episode: "The Missing Shade"

COLD OPEN

EXT. VILLAGE LANE, MORNING

Pavi points to a sunny patch beside his water dish.

PAVI: My shade is missing.

Odo examines the ground. Nara asks when he last saw it.

PAVI: Yesterday. It was attached to the tree.

A small cloud passes overhead. A shadow sweeps across the lane. Odo radios in a moving suspect.

ACT ONE

EXT. PAVI'S YARD, LATER

Nara interviews Pavi. He says the shade covered his dish every afternoon. Odo measures the sunny patch with a ruler. The measurements change as the sun moves.

NARA: The scene is not staying still.

Odo marks the tree's position on a map. The tree is rooted firmly. Pavi asks if it can be questioned. Nara knocks on the trunk; a woodpecker answers.

Kito arrives with pruning shears. He trimmed one low branch to keep the path clear. The branch used to cast shade over Pavi's dish.

ODO: We have a motive.

KITO: I had a path to clear.

ACT TWO

EXT. VILLAGE LANE, AFTERNOON

The officers reconstruct the timeline. Nara places a bowl in the old shade spot. Odo watches the light. The shadow slides away as the afternoon advances. Pavi says it used to move too, but he moved the dish with it.

NARA: You followed your shadow.

PAVI: I thought it followed me.

Odo asks Kito whether the branch can be returned. Kito says it was deadwood and has been safely stacked for mulch. The officers inspect the stack. A small lizard rests in the shade of the pile.

ODO: The shadow has been recovered.

NARA: It is attached to the wood.

Pavi asks if he can move his dish under the remaining branches. Kito shows him a safe spot that stays shaded longer. Odo photographs the new location. His camera casts a shadow across the dish.

PAVI: Now you are the suspect.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. VILLAGE LANE, LATE AFTERNOON

Nara and Odo interview other residents. A squirrel monkey says the shade used to cover a bench. A tortoise says it reached the water dish only in the afternoon. Odo draws a timeline. Nara notes that both accounts can be true because the sun moves.

Odo asks Pavi to stand in the old shade spot at the same time as yesterday. Pavi agrees. He shifts the dish beneath the remaining tree limb. The shadow reaches it for a short while, then slides away. Pavi follows it with the dish.

NARA: That is how the shade went missing.

PAVI: I was making room for it.

Kito checks the cut wood pile. The removed limb is already marked for mulch. He says he could leave some of it beside the path for the lizard. Odo asks whether the lizard filed a request. The lizard crawls into the wood pile.

ODO: Request received.

Nara asks if the officers should restore the branch. Kito explains that the limb was dead and unsafe, so it cannot be attached again. He can keep the path clear and leave a small shade patch over the water dish. Pavi agrees to move the dish each afternoon rather than expect it to stay shaded.

Odo prepares a witness statement for the lizard. It has no signature. Nara marks the report "nonviolent environmental misunderstanding." Pavi asks if the case is closed. Odo says they need to verify the new dish placement at sunset.

EXT. PAVI'S YARD, SUNSET

The officers return. The dish is shaded by a safe branch. The lizard remains in the wood pile. Odo takes a photograph, but his long shadow covers the dish. Nara moves him one step to the side. The shade line and the dish now match.

ACT THREE


EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, EVENING

The officers file a report: no theft, one pruned branch and a moved water dish. Pavi places the dish beneath a safe branch. Kito spreads the deadwood as mulch. Nara checks the path is clear. Odo measures the shade again, but the sun is setting.

ODO: It has disappeared again.

NARA: Night is not a crime.

TAG

EXT. LANE, NEXT MORNING

Pavi finds the dish in sunlight and moves it to the new spot. Odo watches from a patrol cart.

ODO: The victim has learned to follow the evidence.

Nara points to Odo's long shadow stretching across the path.

NARA: Your suspect is back.
