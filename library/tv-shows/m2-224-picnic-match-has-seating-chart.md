# The Picnic Match Has a Seating Chart

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating competition shows that manufacture romantic tension through games instead of letting participants talk*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: Two monkey contestants are paired for a picnic, but the producers keep swapping their seats and prompt cards until they simply share the same blanket.

Cast: Host Luma, a gibbon; contestant Kori, a macaque; contestant Piri, a capuchin; and producer Velo, a baboon.

Episode: "The Perfect Pairing"

COLD OPEN

EXT. MEADOW SET - DAY

Kori and Piri sit at opposite ends of a blanket. A small basket sits between them. Luma holds a card.

LUMA: You have five minutes to discover one thing you share.

KORI: We both like quiet picnics.

PIRI: We both brought a book.

VELO: We need more tension. Swap seats.

[THEY MOVE TO OPPOSITE SIDES OF THE BASKET.]

LUMA: Now ask the question on your card.

KORI: What is your favorite kind of weather?

PIRI: Calm.

ACT ONE

Velo hands the contestants new cards. Luma watches the clock.

PIRI: The card says, "Describe your ideal partner as a landscape."

KORI: I would rather describe a picnic spot.

LUMA: That is close enough.

KORI: A shady bench beside the creek.

PIRI: I brought a map of a place like that.

[THEY OPEN THE MAP. IT IS UPSIDE DOWN.]

VELO: We need a reveal.

KORI: The map is upside down.

PIRI: The creek is still on it.

VELO: Can we film the discovery again?

LUMA: The contestants have already discovered it.

ACT TWO

EXT. MEADOW SET - LATER

Velo adds two colored cups to the blanket. The cups are labeled YES and MAYBE.

LUMA: Choose a cup that describes your connection.

KORI: I don't know Piri yet.

PIRI: We just talked about where we like to read.

VELO: The show needs a choice.

KORI: Maybe.

PIRI: Maybe.

[Velo looks at the cards. Both contestants have chosen the same cup.]

VELO: We only have one MAYBE cup.

LUMA: They can share it.

VELO: The camera will miss the individual choices.

PIRI: We can each hold it.

[THEY BOTH HOLD THE CUP. IT TIPS, AND A LITTLE WATER SPILLS ON THE BLANKET.]

KORI: Now we have something to clean up together.

ACT TWO-B

INT. PRODUCTION TENT - MOMENTS LATER

Velo reviews footage. Luma brings the damp blanket inside. Kori and Piri sit on folding chairs outside the tent.

VELO: The conversation is too calm.

LUMA: They agreed on books, weather, and a picnic spot.

VELO: Could we ask if they felt a spark?

KORI: I felt a draft.

PIRI: The blanket is wet.

LUMA: We can move the interview under the tent.

VELO: The tent is full of cameras.

[KORI AND PIRI LIFT THE BLANKET TOGETHER AND CARRY IT TO A DRY PATCH OF GRASS.]

LUMA: That was a choice.

VELO: It was a reset.

KORI: We moved because the blanket was wet.

PIRI: We can still have the picnic.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. MEADOW SET - LATE AFTERNOON

Luma sets two mugs beside the basket. Kori and Piri return with the dry blanket. Velo asks them to sit on separate marks.

VELO: One mark for each contestant. The camera needs both faces.

KORI: The shade is in the middle.

PIRI: We can move the blanket there.

LUMA: The show has a question about shared interests.

KORI: We both brought books.

PIRI: We both read outside.

VELO: The answer needs a little more drama.

LUMA: They are answering the question.

[VELO SHIFTS THE MARKS APART. THE CAMERA TRACK IS IN THE WAY.]

KORI: The track has the best seat.

PIRI: It is blocking the basket.

[THEY MOVE THE BASKET TO THE CENTER. VELO MOVES IT BACK TO THE MARKS.]

KORI: The fruit is rolling.

[A PEAR ROLLS DOWN THE BLANKET AND STOPS AGAINST THE CAMERA TRACK.]

VELO: We have a moment of uncertainty.

PIRI: The pear is fine.

LUMA: We can sit on the blanket and share the fruit.

VELO: The show has not reached the choice segment.

KORI: We can choose to eat.

[KORI CUTS THE PEAR IN HALF. PIRI TAKES ONE PIECE.]

VELO: That was not on the card.

ACT THREE

EXT. MEADOW SET - SUNSET

The producer has left. Luma asks the contestants if they want to continue the picnic.

LUMA: The cameras are off. Would you like to sit together?

KORI: Yes.

PIRI: Let's use the dry side of the blanket.

[THEY SIT SIDE BY SIDE. THEY OPEN THEIR BOOKS.]

VELO: We should ask them to look at each other.

LUMA: The cameras are off.

TAG

Kori and Piri read quietly. Velo turns on a small monitor and watches them from inside the tent.

VELO: They are still sitting together.

LUMA: That was the answer.
