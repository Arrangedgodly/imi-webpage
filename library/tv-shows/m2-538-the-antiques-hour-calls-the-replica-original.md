# The Antiques Hour Calls the Replica Original

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: appraisal programs that build suspense around an object's price before checking its provenance*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Rena, a lemur; appraiser Toma, an orangutan; guest Neli, a macaque; archive clerk Piri, a gibbon; producer Vano, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. ANTIQUES SET - DAY

Neli places a painted teapot on the table. Toma lifts it carefully.

RENA: Is this a priceless heirloom?

PIRI: It is the museum shop replica.

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Vano asks Piri to wait until after the appraisal reveal. Piri has found a label on the bottom: REPRODUCTION, 1998. Toma checks the glaze and agrees it is a modern copy. Rena turns the teapot so the label faces the camera. Vano turns it back.

Neli says the original belonged to her grandmother. Rena asks if she brought it. Neli says it is in a padded box at home. Vano asks the crew to film the replica as though it were the heirloom. Toma refuses to estimate a price for an object that is not there.

ACT TWO

INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - LATER

Piri brings the museum catalog. The original teapot was donated years earlier, but the catalog photograph shows a blue flower on the handle. Neli's replica has a red one. Rena asks whether that means the original is worth more. Toma says the color alone does not establish provenance.

Vano sets a large number card on the table. It reads $12,000. Piri checks the station's prop inventory; the card belongs to a previous episode about a clock. Toma replaces it with a blank card. Rena asks the camera to zoom in on the empty space where the price would be.

INT. ANTIQUES SET - MOMENTS LATER

Neli opens the padded box she brought from home. Inside is the original teapot, wrapped in cloth. Toma asks Piri to compare the handle with the catalog photo. The flower is blue. Neli explains that her grandmother bought the red-flowered copy for everyday use. Vano asks for a dramatic unwrapping. Neli removes the cloth herself, revealing the maker's stamp on the base.

Piri checks the donation catalog again. The museum record shows the original was returned to the family for conservation. Toma asks Neli whether she wants a market appraisal or help confirming the object. She asks for identification first. He records the stamp and condition without naming a price.

INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Piri reads the catalog number aloud while Toma checks the stamp against the photograph. The number matches. Vano switches the monitor to the price-card feed, which still shows the clock's $12,000 estimate. Rena turns the screen away from Neli. Neli asks if the teapot should go back to the museum. Piri says the record identifies it as a family loan, not a sale. Toma notes the cracked glaze on the handle and records it on the condition form. Vano offers a repair montage; Neli asks that the crack stay visible in the record.


ACT THREE

INT. ANTIQUES SET - AFTERNOON

Piri brings the provenance form to the table. Toma compares the stamp, the handle and the conservation record. The original matches the archive photograph. The replica stays on a separate stand with its label visible. Rena asks whether the price card can now be shown. Toma says the segment is about identifying the piece; a price would require another appraisal and Neli's consent.

Vano tries to hold up the $12,000 card. Piri takes it from him and turns it over; the back reads CLOCK. Rena asks if the replica has any value. Toma says it is a souvenir copy, and Neli says she uses it for tea. She pours from it into a cup; the lid rattles. The original remains in its padded box. Vano reaches for a second price card, but Piri has removed all the numbers from the set.

Toma writes the catalog reference on a card for Neli. She thanks him and folds the conservation record into the padded case. Rena introduces the object as her grandmother's teapot, with its identity confirmed and no auction estimate. Vano asks if viewers will stay without a dollar figure. The camera operator pans to the rattling replica lid, and the audience laughs. Toma hands Neli the case, not the prop price card. She closes it before carrying the original off set. The replica stays on the side table for the next cup.

TAG

INT. SET - AFTER THE APPRAISAL

Piri files the catalog copy. Vano returns the clock's number card to props. Rena sets the replica beside the tea cups.

FINAL IMAGE: The original rests in its padded case while the everyday copy pours tea beneath a sign reading REPLICA.
