# The Flatmates' Quiet Evening

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: apartment sitcoms, where every roommate's plan for a quiet night depends on the others staying quiet*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Four flatmates agree to a quiet evening, but each has a different idea of what "quiet" means and every activity travels through the thin walls.

Cast: Nara, macaque; Odo, baboon; Piri, capuchin; Seli, gibbon.

## Episode: "Shh"

### Cold Open

[APARTMENT. A SIGN READS "QUIET NIGHT."]

NARA: I am going to read.

ODO: I am going to practice drums quietly.

PIRI: I have a loud book.

SELI: I am going out.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[NARA SITS ON THE COUCH WITH A BOOK.]

Odo taps a practice pad in his room. Nara can hear it through the wall. Piri opens a bag of crunchy snacks. Seli returns because it is raining.

NARA: We agreed to be quiet.

ODO: This is quiet drumming.

PIRI: The bag is loud, not me.

SELI: The rain is louder.

Nara asks if everyone can move to separate rooms. The apartment has two rooms and a kitchen. Odo moves to the kitchen; Piri moves to the hallway; Seli takes the couch.

NARA: I was already on the couch.

SELI: You can read in your room.

NARA: Odo is drumming there.

### Act Two

[THE FLATMATES TRY A QUIETNESS TEST.]

Seli suggests that each person make one sound and rate it. Odo taps the pad once. Piri opens the snack bag. Nara turns a page. Seli closes the window.

ODO: The page was the loudest.

NARA: The bag was louder.

PIRI: The rain was loudest.

SELI: We need a quieter activity.

Odo tries to practice with soft sticks. The sticks make no sound, so he cannot keep the rhythm. Piri pours snacks into a bowl, but the bowl clinks against the counter. Nara uses a bookmark instead of turning pages, but the book closes.

NARA: The quiet is preventing reading.

SELI: You can read silently.

NARA: I was.

Odo moves the practice pad to the closet. The closet door creaks. Piri puts the bowl on a folded towel. The clinks stop.

[THE FLATMATES TRY TO CHOOSE A QUIET ACTIVITY.]

Seli suggests cards. Odo shuffles the deck, and every shuffle makes a soft flutter. Piri deals. The cards tap the table. Nara asks if the game can be played without the cards.

ODO: Then it would be a different game.

PIRI: We can play silently.

Seli asks how they will know whose turn it is. They agree to point. Odo points at Nara; Nara points at Piri; Piri points at Seli. Everyone waits.

NARA: Is it my turn?

ODO: We are pointing.

PIRI: I thought that meant pass.

SELI: We have no rule for passing.

Odo writes a simple rule on a note: "Take a turn, then point to the next player." The pencil scratches loudly. Nara suggests writing with a soft marker. The marker squeaks.

PIRI: The rules are louder than the game.

They switch to charades. Odo acts out a drum solo with his hands, but nobody guesses it. Piri says "weather." Nara says "laundry." Seli guesses "quiet drumming," and Odo nods.

### Act Three

[THE ROOM IS QUIET.]

Everyone returns to the living room. Nara reads. Odo practices using finger taps on his knee. Piri eats from the padded bowl. Seli looks out the window.

NARA: This works.

ODO: I can still feel the rhythm.

PIRI: I can eat without the bowl ringing.

SELI: The rain has stopped.

Odo starts to tell a joke, then whispers. Nobody hears him. He repeats it louder. Nara asks him to write it down instead.

ODO: It is not funny on paper.

PIRI: Read it silently.

Odo writes the joke and passes it around. Nara smiles. Piri laughs out loud, then covers her mouth.

SELI: We have broken the quiet.

PIRI: The joke was good.

[THE FLATMATES TRY THE GAME AGAIN.]

They point to the next player without speaking. Piri guesses Odo's mime correctly. Odo smiles and tries to clap, then stops before making a sound.

NARA: You can clap.

ODO: I do not want to break the quiet.

SELI: You can do both.

Odo claps once. The apartment echoes.

Nara closes her book. The joke is still written on the note.

PIRI: Can we keep it?

ODO: It is the quietest joke I know.

SELI: Then it fits the evening.

### Tag

[THE FLATMATES SIT TOGETHER.]

Nara closes her book softly. Odo stops tapping. Piri finishes the snacks. Seli opens the window.

[SFX: RAIN STARTS AGAIN, LOUD.]

NARA: The apartment was quiet.

ODO: The weather was not.

PIRI: We can move to the closet.

SELI: It creaks.

[SFX: CLOSET DOOR CREAKS. LAUGHTER.]
