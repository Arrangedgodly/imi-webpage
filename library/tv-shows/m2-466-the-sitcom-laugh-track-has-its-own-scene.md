# The Sitcom Laugh Track Has Its Own Scene

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: multi-camera sitcom tapings, eager laugh tracks, and scenes whose canned audience reacts to stage directions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: actor Neri, a macaque; actor Pavo, a baboon; actor Seli, a gibbon; director Omi, an orangutan; sound editor Kett, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SITCOM KITCHEN SET - NIGHT

Three actors stand around a table. A refrigerator door hangs open. The director watches from a monitor; the sound editor has a laugh track control pad.

NERI: The refrigerator is empty.

PAVO: That is because you left the groceries in the car.

[LAUGH TRACK: LOUD LAUGHTER.] 

SELI: That was a stage direction.

KETT: The track is set to react to pauses.

OMI: It should react to jokes.

[The laugh track starts again as Neri closes the refrigerator.] 

ACT ONE

INT. SITCOM KITCHEN SET - CONTINUOUS

The actors rehearse the scene. Neri plays a roommate who forgot to buy food; Pavo plays the roommate who saved a bowl of fruit. Seli is meant to enter with a grocery bag. The laugh track fires whenever anyone looks at the monitor.

NERI: I have one apple.

PAVO: I have one bowl.

[LAUGH TRACK: APPLAUSE.] 

SELI: The bowl is empty.

KETT: It heard the pause.

OMI: We need the timing, not the button testing.

[The sound editor lowers the track level. Omi asks the actors to run the scene once without canned laughter. Neri reaches for the apple; Pavo moves the bowl. They both grab it and bump hands.] 

NERI: We can split it.

PAVO: It is a plastic prop.

[LAUGH TRACK: TINY GIGGLE.] 

KETT: That one was from the apple joke.

OMI: The scene has no apple joke.

[The editor removes the cue from the board. The actors continue with a bowl of paper grocery receipts instead.] 

ACT TWO

INT. SITCOM KITCHEN SET - LATER

Seli enters with the grocery bag, but the bag is empty. The director asks whether this can be the twist. Seli says the bag is empty because the prop team forgot to load it. Omi signals for a pause. Kett finds the prop list and asks the stagehand to bring the pretend groceries.

PAVO: The store sold you an empty bag?

SELI: The store was closed.

NERI: You went to a closed store?

[The laugh track bursts into applause before the punchline. The actors freeze. Kett checks the cue and discovers the applause button is linked to the bag rustle.] 

KETT: The bag is apparently a celebrity.

OMI: Disconnect that cue.

[The stagehand fills the bag with prop vegetables and closes it. Seli enters again. The bag makes a soft rustle; the laugh track stays quiet. The actors continue.]

SELI: I brought groceries from the market.

PAVO: The market is closed.

SELI: It opened again in the morning.

NERI: You went to the future?

[The laugh track gives one natural laugh, then stops. The director looks at the editor, who keeps hands off the pad.] 

[The sound editor asks the actors to mark the pause before the final line. The refrigerator door squeaks; the laugh track bursts into applause again. Omi stops the take and asks the crew to inspect the hinge.] 

OMI: That sound is not a punchline.

KETT: It is also not a laugh. The cue is still mapped to the door.

[The editor clears the cue. Neri opens and closes the refrigerator once; the door stays quiet. Pavo tests the bowl on the table, and it remains empty.] 

PAVO: The scene has one apple and no surprise food.

SELI: That is what the script says.

[Omi calls for the actors to reset from their marks. The editor keeps the laugh track muted until the dialogue starts.] 

ACT THREE

INT. SITCOM KITCHEN SET - NIGHT

The actors run the scene for camera. The refrigerator closes, the bag is set on the table, and the plastic apple stays in the bowl. The laugh track is programmed to fire only after the lines that tested well. The sound editor has a manual mute switch.

NERI: I bought one apple.

PAVO: You said you bought groceries.

NERI: One apple is a grocery.

[The audience laughs. The bag rustles after Seli sets it down; the track remains silent. Omi gives the editor a thumbs-up.] 

SELI: At least we can all share the bowl.

[The actors reach for the apple at once. The director cuts before they touch it, saving the prop.] 

OMI: That is the take.

KETT: The laugh cue landed after the line, and nowhere else.

PAVO: Can we keep the apple?

SELI: It is plastic.

[The set assistant takes the apple away. The laugh track briefly plays one stray chuckle as the refrigerator closes. Kett mutes the channel before the actors react.] 

TAG

The cast waits in silence for the next scene. The sound editor tests the mute switch; there is no laughter. Everyone nods and walks off set.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty kitchen set remains quiet while the laugh track control pad sits switched off.
