# The Unmasked Ukulele

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: masked-singer competitions, celebrity guessing panels, and reveal-night cliffhangers*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: Judges try to identify Noma, the live singer inside a pineapple costume, but vague clues make them insist the voice belongs to an eliminated musician.

Cast: Host Rumi, a baboon; judges Lala, a lemur, and Bex, a macaque; masked singer Noma, an adult gibbon; stage manager Oko, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Who Is Under the Pineapple?"

### Cold open

INT. MASKED SINGING STAGE, NIGHT

[A performer in a pineapple suit sings a clear melody. The judges gasp. The costume has a leaf crown.]

RUMI: We have reached the reveal.

LALA: It could be a famous drummer.

BEX: The clues say the singer has fur.

LALA: All our contestants have fur.

BEX: That narrows it to everyone.

RUMI: Pineapple, remove the mask!

[The performer lifts the pineapple top. A smaller pineapple mask is underneath.]

OKO: That is not the reveal mask.

RUMI: We will return after the commercial.

### Act One

INT. JUDGES' BOOTH

[The judges sit behind a board of clue cards. Oko sorts masks in a crate.]

RUMI: Judges, make your final guesses.

LALA: I believe Pineapple is Sello, the drum player.

BEX: Sello was eliminated yesterday.

LALA: That was his public identity.

BEX: The elimination was public.

LALA: Exactly. He would hide.

RUMI: My guess is the flute teacher.

OKO: There is no flute teacher.

RUMI: Then the clue is working.

[The clue screen lights up: "I once climbed a ladder."]

BEX: That could be anyone.

LALA: Not me.

BEX: You climbed the set ladder at rehearsal.

LALA: I was looking for a snack.

RUMI: Another clue: "I have performed under a tree."

OKO: We are in a forest.

BEX: This is the least helpful clue show.

RUMI: The clue team calls it poetic.

### Act Two

INT. BACKSTAGE

[Noma sits on a stool in the pineapple costume. Oko checks the zipper from the outside.]

OKO: You can remove the mask in the changing room.

PINEAPPLE: The changing room is locked.

OKO: Why?

PINEAPPLE: The key is in the prop crate.

[Oko opens the crate. A smaller pineapple mask sits inside.]

RUMI: The reveal mask has a reveal mask.

LALA: The contestant has layers.

BEX: The clue card says the singer has performed under a tree.

RUMI: That points to Sello.

LALA: Sello was eliminated yesterday.

BEX: Perhaps the voice has returned.

[Oko steadies the costume as it turns on the stool.]

RUMI: The costume is spinning.

OKO: The stool has a rotating base.

PINEAPPLE: I can hear you.

RUMI: The microphone is on.

PINEAPPLE: It is also warm in here.

LALA: The voice sounds familiar.

BEX: It sounds like someone from Sello's band.

RUMI: We need one more performance.

[The band begins. Noma sings live while the band accompanies her. A leaf falls from the costume and lands on a judge's desk.]

BEX: The costume is shedding.

LALA: A natural performer.

OKO: It is made of paper leaves.

RUMI: The panel is getting closer.

### Act Three

INT. REVEAL STAGE

[The judges sit. Noma stands under a spotlight in the pineapple suit. A lock of fur sticks out through the zipper.]

RUMI: Judges, your last guesses.

LALA: Sello.

BEX: The flute teacher.

OKO: The zipper is open.

RUMI: Pineapple, remove the mask!

[Noma removes the pineapple top, then steps out of the suit. She is a gibbon in a green stage jacket.]

LALA: Noma from Sello's band.

NOMA: I sang backup for him. I also played the ukulele.

BEX: The clues pointed to Sello.

NOMA: The clues said I performed under a tree. The whole band did.

RUMI: And the ukulele?

[Noma lifts the small ukulele from the costume.]

NOMA: It was a prop. The band was playing live, and I was singing.

OKO: I put the zipper pull on the inside.

NOMA: That is why it was hard to remove.

LALA: I guessed the right band and the wrong performer.

BEX: You guessed the flute teacher.

LALA: I had a broader theory.

RUMI: Judges, what is your final score?

BEX: Ten for the voice, two for the clues.

LALA: Ten for the reveal and one for the pineapple.

RUMI: Noma wins the round.

[Noma bows. The band plays a bright ukulele chord.]

### Scoring after the reveal

INT. JUDGES' BOOTH

[Rumi checks the scorecard. Noma places the ukulele beside it.]

RUMI: The scorecard has a box for the contestant and a box for the costume.

BEX: It has no box for the clue writer.

LALA: The clue writer should receive a pineapple.

OKO: I wrote the clues.

BEX: Then you knew the answers.

OKO: I knew what I wrote. I did not know how the judges would interpret it.

RUMI: The format lets the judges guess from hints.

LALA: The hints were too broad.

BEX: You guessed an eliminated singer.

LALA: The hints said under a tree.

NOMA: We were under a tree.

[The audience laughs. Oko adds "specificity" to the clue card.]

RUMI: The next clue will be more precise.

OKO: It will say "Noma."

BEX: That gives it away.

LALA: The panel will still guess Sello.

[The judges hand Noma the trophy. The ukulele's string twangs.]

### Tag

INT. BACKSTAGE

[The empty pineapple suit sits on a stool. Oko pins the contestant name tag to the costume's front.]

LALA: Who was in the pineapple?

BEX: Noma.

LALA: I knew that.

[The stool rotates slowly. The empty suit faces the camera.]
