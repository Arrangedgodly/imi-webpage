# Tonight, With Taro

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows, desk bits and celebrity interviews that mistake interruption for spontaneity*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A late-night host tries to keep a polished interview moving while a live desk segment, a squeaky chair, and a guest's practical honesty derail the planned punch lines.

Cast: Host Taro, a macaque; guest Dr. Iva, a capuchin botanist; bandleader Kesh, a gibbon; and stage manager Pru, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Chair Has Notes"

### Cold Open

[THE BAND PLAYS A LOUD INTRO. TARO SWIVELS IN A CHAIR. IT SQUEAKS.]

TARO: Tonight we have a leading botanist, an exclusive desk report, and a chair with a recurring role.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS AGAIN.]

KESH: It is the only guest who has been on time.

### Act One

[TARO SITS BEHIND A DESK. A MUG, THREE CARDS, AND A SMALL POTTED PLANT ARE ARRANGED PERFECTLY.]

TARO: Welcome. We begin with headlines. A local fig tree has produced a large crop.

[THE BAND PLAYS A FANFARE.]

TARO: Scientists call this a seasonal event. We call it an opportunity for a dramatic graphic.

[A GRAPHIC SHOWS A FIG WITH A CROWN.]

TARO: Joining us is Dr. Iva, who studies fruiting trees. Tell us what made the crop so large.

IVA: Rain, sunlight, and pollination.

TARO: Any secret ingredient?

IVA: The tree had room to grow.

TARO: The crowd wants a secret ingredient.

IVA: The crowd can water a tree.

[APPLAUSE SIGN FLASHES. NO ONE CLAPS.]

TARO: Let's meet the plant.

[HE LIFTS THE POT. SOIL SPILLS ON THE DESK.]

IVA: It is dry.

TARO: We have brought a thirsty guest onto television.

[PRU SLIDES A CUP OF WATER ONSTAGE. TARO POURS IT INTO THE POT.]

IVA: That is enough. The drainage hole is blocked.

[TARO TILTS THE POT. WATER LEAKS ACROSS HIS NOTE CARDS.]

TARO: The plant has reviewed my monologue.

### Act Two

[THEY MOVE TO TWO GUEST CHAIRS. IVA SITS. THE CHAIR SQUEAKS.]

TARO: What is your latest discovery?

IVA: A young fig tree grows better when animals leave some fruit behind.

TARO: So your finding is that sharing helps.

IVA: My finding is that seeds spread when animals carry them.

TARO: Can you make that sound more surprising?

IVA: A monkey eats a fig, walks, and drops a seed.

[TARO LOOKS TO CAMERA.]

TARO: I did not expect to be implicated.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS WHEN IVA LEANS FORWARD.]

TARO: Your chair has a follow-up.

IVA: It needs oil.

TARO: Do you have a scientific theory about the noise?

IVA: The hinge is dry.

[THE BAND PLAYS A MYSTERY STING.]

TARO: Could it be the building settling?

IVA: It settles every time I sit down.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS. KESH STRIKES A CHORD IN TIME.]

KESH: We have a rhythm section.

TARO: Can you play us out?

IVA: I came to discuss trees.

TARO: And now you've joined the band.

IVA: No, I have not.

[TARO HANDS HER A SMALL SHAKER. SHE SETS IT ON THE TABLE.]

[TARO PULLS A DRY CARD FROM UNDER THE DESK.]

TARO: We prepared a quick game called "Name That Leaf."

IVA: There are thousands of leaves.

TARO: This one is from our potted guest.

IVA: It is a fig leaf.

TARO: Correct. How did you know?

IVA: I study fig trees.

TARO: We have made this game difficult for our guest by inviting an expert.

[THE BAND PLAYS A WRONG ANSWER BUZZER.]

IVA: The buzzer is not a leaf.

KESH: It is a very loud leaf.

[TARO HOLDS UP THE WET CARD. THE INK RUNS.]

TARO: The answer is now "blue."

IVA: That is water damage.

TARO: Our show is committed to evidence.

[PRU REPLACES THE CARD WITH A TOWEL.]

### Act Three

[TARO RETURNS TO THE DESK. THE CARDS ARE WET AND THE PLANT SITS IN A TRAY.]

TARO: We have time for one final segment: "Expert or Guess?"

IVA: I was told this was an interview.

TARO: We can still interview you while guessing.

[PRU HOLDS UP A CARD: "WHY DO LEAVES FALL?"]

TARO: Expert or guess?

IVA: Seasonal change, water balance, and the tree's own growth cycle.

TARO: Guess?

IVA: The tree is tired of the desk.

[TARO LOOKS AT THE PLANT. A LEAF DROPS ONTO HIS SHOE.]

TARO: We have a second opinion.

IVA: That leaf was already loose.

TARO: The guest has corrected the plant.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS. KESH PLAYS THE SAME THREE NOTES.]

TARO: Our time is up. Dr. Iva, thank you for explaining your work without a secret ingredient.

IVA: Thank you for not watering the desk again.

[TARO SETS THE MUG DOWN. THE CHAIR SQUEAKS AS HE STANDS.]

TARO: Our final guest has requested the last word.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS ONCE MORE.]

KESH: It says oil.

[TARO AND IVA LOOK AT THE CHAIR. PRU WHEELS IT OFFSTAGE WHILE THE BAND PLAYS THE EXIT MUSIC.]
