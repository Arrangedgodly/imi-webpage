# The Family Relay

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: family competition programs where teamwork is measured by a scoreboard and every chore becomes a challenge*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: The Mottle family competes in a televised household relay, but their instincts as a troop keep turning individual tasks into shared work.

Cast: Mother Kiri, a macaque; father Sol, a gibbon; youngsters Vee and Toma; host Fenn, a mandrill; and scorekeeper Ria, a capuchin.

## Episode: "Pass the Basket"

### Cold Open

[THE FAMILY STANDS AT A STARTING LINE. A BASKET OF LAUNDRY SITS BESIDE THEM.]

FENN: The fastest family wins a golden fruit bowl.

KIRI: We need to fold these towels.

TOMA: We need to keep the basket from falling.

[THE BASKET TIPS. THE FAMILY CATCHES IT TOGETHER.]

FENN: That was not the first event.

### Act One

[THEY BEGIN THE RELAY. THE FIRST TASK IS TO SORT CLOTHES BY COLOR.]

RIA: Yellow on the left. Blue on the right.

VEE: This one has both.

SOL: Put it in the middle.

FENN: There is no middle basket.

[KIRI MAKES A THIRD PILE.]

RIA: That is outside the rules.

KIRI: It is still sorted.

[THE FAMILY FINISHES. THE SCOREBOARD GIVES THEM ZERO POINTS.]

TOMA: The shirt is still in the middle.

FENN: The next task is carrying fruit without dropping it.

### Act Two

[SOL CARRIES A BOWL. KIRI WALKS BESIDE HIM. THE HOST COUNTS DOWN.]

FENN: Three, two, one.

[THE BOWL SLIPS. VEE CATCHES IT, THEN TOMA CATCHES VEE.]

RIA: They used two extra hands.

FENN: The task required one carrier.

KIRI: The fruit stayed in the bowl.

[THEY REACH THE NEXT STATION: A STACK OF BLOCKS.]

RIA: Build the tallest tower in one minute.

[THE CHILDREN STACK BLOCKS. SOL HOLDS THE BASE. KIRI BRINGS A WIDE BLOCK.]

FENN: No assistance from outside the lane.

KIRI: We are one family.

FENN: For the contest, you are four contestants.

[THE TOWER WOBBLES. THE FAMILY HOLDS IT UP. THE TIMER BEEPS.]

[THE HOST ADDS A BONUS EVENT: SET THE TABLE FOR FOUR.]

FENN: Each family member places one item.

[KIRI PUTS DOWN FOUR CUPS. SOL ADDS FOUR NAPKINS. VEE BRINGS A BOWL OF FRUIT.]

RIA: That is three turns.

TOMA: I brought the spoons.

[THEY SET THE SPOONS BESIDE THE CUPS. THE TABLE IS COMPLETE.]

FENN: The family used four team members.

KIRI: There are four of us.

FENN: The event was meant to test one person's speed.

SOL: A table is for everyone.

[THE SCOREBOARD ADDS NO POINTS. THE GOLDEN FRUIT BOWL IS MOVED TO THE NEXT STATION.]

RIA: The prize has left the table.

TOMA: We can carry it together.

[FENN TAKES THE BOWL AND HOLDS IT ABOVE HIS HEAD.]

FENN: I am carrying it alone.

[THE BOWL TILTS. RIA STEADIES IT.]

RIA: Now we are both carrying it.

[THE FINAL TASK IS TO FOLD A FITTED SHEET.]

FENN: One contestant, one sheet, thirty seconds.

[THE FOUR FAMILY MEMBERS GRAB DIFFERENT CORNERS. THE SHEET SNAPS OPEN AND COVERS THE CAMERA.]

RIA: We have lost the shot.

SOL: We have found the corners.

[KIRI TUCKS ONE SIDE. VEE AND TOMA FOLD THE OTHER. THE SHEET IS NEAT.]

FENN: The timer is still running.

KIRI: It is folded.

FENN: You had help.

TOMA: It is a family task.

[THE SCOREBOARD ADDS A POINT, THEN TAKES IT AWAY.]

RIA: The scorekeeper is undecided.

FENN: I am waiting for a ruling.

[THE GOLDEN BOWL IS SET ON THE TABLE AGAIN. THE FAMILY PLACES THE FOLDED SHEET UNDER IT SO IT STOPS WOBBLING.]

[THE FAMILY CARRIES THE BASKET TO THE FINISH LINE. THIS TIME, ITS HANDLE HOLDS.]

FENN: The relay is complete.

RIA: The family is still working together.

KIRI: That is how we carry things.

[RIA ADDS THE TEAM PHOTO TO THE SCOREBOARD. THE FAMILY STANDS BESIDE IT.]

FENN: The score is final.

KIRI: The towels are folded.

FENN: The basket stayed upright.

### Act Three

[THE SCORES ARE TIED. THE FINAL TASK IS TO CARRY THE LAUNDRY BASKET TO THE FINISH.]

FENN: One carrier only.

[KIRI LIFTS THE BASKET. THE HANDLE SNAPS. SOL, VEE, AND TOMA EACH GRAB AN EDGE.]

RIA: That is four carriers.

FENN: The rules say one.

[THE BASKET IS ALREADY OVER THE LINE.]

KIRI: We finished the task.

FENN: You finished it together.

[THE SCOREBOARD FLASHES "NO VALID WINNER." THE GOLDEN BOWL SITS ON A HIGH SHELF.]

TOMA: Can we have the towels?

RIA: Those were never prizes.

[THE FAMILY FOLDS THE TOWELS. FENN TRIES TO LIFT THE BOWL, BUT ITS HANDLE IS TOO SMALL.]

FENN: I need help carrying the trophy.

[KIRI AND SOL TAKE ONE SIDE EACH.]

RIA: That is not allowed.

[FENN LOOKS AT THE CAMERA, THEN HOLDS THE BOWL WITH BOTH HANDS.]
