# Orbiting Orchard

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: science-fiction space rescue series and tense bridge-command scenes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: The crew of the survey ship Canopy One races to rescue a drifting seed pod greenhouse before its artificial gravity shuts down for good.

Cast: Captain Vela, a chimpanzee; navigator Oru, a gibbon; engineer Saff, a capuchin; botanist Pell, a macaque; and ship's computer VOX, a baboon over an intercom.

## Episode: "The Gravity of the Situation"

### Cold open

INT. CANOPY ONE BRIDGE, SPACE

[Stars shine through the viewport. A red warning light blinks. Pell's seed pod floats past the ceiling.]

VELA: Why is there a seed pod on the bridge?

PELL: The gravity is failing in the greenhouse.

ORU: We are two minutes from the station.

SAFF: The pod is moving toward the engine vent.

VOX: The engine vent is closed.

SAFF: It is closed now.

[The pod gently taps the vent and drifts away.]

VELA: Prepare the rescue line.

ORU: Which way is down?

VOX: Down is currently a matter of ship preference.

### Act One

INT. BRIDGE

[Vela straps into a chair. Oru studies a star map turned sideways.]

VELA: Report.

ORU: The greenhouse pod has detached from the station and is orbiting us.

PELL: It contains six seedlings and one very worried beetle.

SAFF: The pod's gravity wheel stopped turning.

VELA: Can we restart it remotely?

SAFF: We can ask it politely.

VELA: Ask.

VOX: The wheel has declined.

ORU: We need to dock with the pod.

VELA: Navigate us in.

ORU: The map is upside down.

VELA: Turn it around.

ORU: The stars don't turn with it.

PELL: The pod is drifting into the shadow of the moon.

VELA: Can the seedlings survive?

PELL: They can survive in low gravity, but not without light.

SAFF: The beetle is also losing its floor.

PELL: The beetle has six legs. It can make its own floor.

VOX: The beetle has attached itself to the camera.

VELA: Keep it there.

### Act Two

INT. AIRLOCK

[Saff and Pell wear light suits. A tether connects them to the ship. The rescue pod bumps gently against the airlock.]

SAFF: Pressure is equal.

PELL: The hatch is stuck.

SAFF: It is not stuck. It is latched.

PELL: The latch is on the other side.

SAFF: That is where we are going.

[They turn the pod slowly. The hatch opens. Seedlings and a watering cup float out.]

PELL: Catch the plants.

SAFF: I have the cup.

PELL: The cup has water.

SAFF: Then I have the most important plant.

[The water floats into a sphere. Pell traps it in a clear bag.]

PELL: The plants are dry.

SAFF: The water is in a ball.

PELL: A useful ball.

[The beetle crawls across the tether.]

PELL: The beetle has found us.

SAFF: It appears to be in command.

VOX: The gravity wheel is jammed by a loose screw.

PELL: Can we reach it?

SAFF: The wheel is behind the seed rack.

PELL: Move the rack carefully.

[They shift the rack. The seedlings drift away, then cling to Pell's sleeve.]

PELL: They are using me as soil.

SAFF: Hold still.

PELL: I am holding still.

SAFF: Your tail is moving.

PELL: It is maintaining balance.

### Act Three

INT. GREENHOUSE POD

[Pell and Saff reach the gravity wheel. Saff turns it by hand. It creaks and stops.]

SAFF: The wheel is bent.

PELL: Can the seed tray be used as a brace?

SAFF: It is a tray.

PELL: It is a flat piece of strong material.

SAFF: That is a brace.

[They wedge the tray against the wheel. It turns once, then continues slowly.]

VOX: Artificial gravity restored to fifteen percent.

PELL: The seedlings are settling.

[The plants drift toward the soil trays. The beetle crawls onto a leaf.]

SAFF: We have a stable pod.

VELA: Bring it into the ship.

ORU: The docking path is clear.

PELL: The beetle is still on the camera.

VOX: The beetle has received a crew designation.

VELA: What designation?

VOX: Captain Beetle.

SAFF: It outranks me.

VELA: It saved the camera.

ORU: And the plants.

PELL: The plants were on the camera.

SAFF: Then it saved the entire mission.

[The pod locks into the ship. The crew enters the bridge. The seedlings sit upright in their trays.]

VELA: Mission complete.

ORU: The moon is behind us.

VOX: The beetle is still in command.

PELL: Captain Beetle has requested a leaf.

VELA: Grant the request.

### Tag

INT. GREENHOUSE POD, LATER

[The beetle rests on the leaf. A small label beside the tray reads "CAPTAIN'S QUARTERS."]

SAFF: The label is crooked.

PELL: The captain likes it that way.

[The gravity wheel turns smoothly.]
