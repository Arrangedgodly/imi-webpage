# Escape from the Prop Closet

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised escape-room competitions, ticking clocks, and misleading clue reveals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: Three contestants race to escape a studio prop closet, but the clues are so literal that the host's dramatic hints keep slowing them down.

Cast: Host Boro, a baboon; contestant Nella, a macaque; contestant Suf, a capuchin; contestant Viri, a gibbon; and clue designer Kett, an orangutan.

## Episode: "The Door Is the Answer"

### Cold open

INT. PROP CLOSET, NIGHT

[A red clock counts down from 20:00. The door has a large handle and a sign reading "PULL."]

BORO: Three contestants are locked in a room full of clues.

NELLA: The door says pull.

SUF: Is that a clue?

VIRI: It might be a trick.

KETT: The clock has started.

NELLA: We should pull the handle.

BORO: The contestants have found their first mystery.

[Nella pulls. The door does not open.]

SUF: It is locked.

KETT: The key is in the room.

### Act One

INT. PROP CLOSET

[The contestants search shelves. A large key hangs on the wall.]

BORO: Can they find the first clue?

VIRI: I found a key.

SUF: It is hanging on a hook.

NELLA: It says "KEY."

KETT: That is the label.

BORO: The puzzle deepens.

NELLA: This is the key.

SUF: It may be a decoy.

VIRI: We can try it in the lock.

BORO: The contestants are taking a risk.

[The key turns but the door stays shut.]

NELLA: The key works.

SUF: The handle says pull.

VIRI: The door might be pushed.

NELLA: We pulled already.

KETT: The lock is open.

BORO: The challenge has multiple layers.

NELLA: There is a chain across the door.

SUF: We need the chain key.

VIRI: It is the key we used.

NELLA: The chain is a prop.

SUF: It has a little lock.

[They tug. The chain falls off its hook.]

BORO: The contestants have solved the chain.

KETT: The chain was never locked.

### Act Two

INT. PROP CLOSET, LATER

[The clock reads 10:00. A shelf holds a box marked "CLUE."]

BORO: They have found a mysterious box.

NELLA: It says "CLUE."

SUF: It could be empty.

VIRI: It is a box.

[Nella opens it. Inside is a card reading "Look behind you."]

BORO: The clue sends them in a new direction.

[They all turn. The door is behind them.]

NELLA: We can see the door.

SUF: We should look behind the door.

VIRI: There is a mop.

KETT: The mop is part of the next puzzle.

BORO: A shocking reveal.

NELLA: The mop has a key taped to it.

SUF: Another key?

VIRI: It is labeled "EXIT."

NELLA: We have an exit key.

[They use it. The door opens one inch, then stops.]

SUF: The door is blocked.

KETT: A chair is in the hallway.

BORO: The contestants face their greatest obstacle yet.

NELLA: We can move the chair.

VIRI: The chair has a cushion.

SUF: Is the cushion a clue?

NELLA: It is a cushion.

[They slide the chair aside.]

### Bonus lock

INT. PROP CLOSET

[Kett places a small box on the shelf. It is marked "BONUS." The clock resumes.]

KETT: The bonus room contains a prize.

NELLA: The door is open.

SUF: The clock is running.

VIRI: We could take the prize and leave.

BORO: The contestants have discovered a shortcut.

NELLA: Is the prize the key?

KETT: It is a small mirror.

SUF: We already have a mirror in the hall.

KETT: This one is for the bonus.

[They look into the mirror. It reflects the open door.]

VIRI: The prize is the exit.

### Act Three

INT. STUDIO HALLWAY

[The contestants walk out. The timer stops at 04:12. Boro waits beside the door.]

BORO: How did they solve the room?

NELLA: We read the labels.

SUF: We tried the keys.

VIRI: We moved the chair.

KETT: The room took less time than expected.

BORO: We had a bonus riddle planned.

NELLA: What was it?

KETT: "What has a handle and opens a door?"

SUF: The door.

KETT: Correct.

VIRI: We answered that in the first minute.

BORO: The audience didn't see the answer.

NELLA: The audience saw us pull the handle.

BORO: We can use a slow-motion replay.

[They watch a monitor. Nella pulls the handle. The words "PULL" flash dramatically.]

SUF: The clue was on the door.

KETT: We should remove the labels next time.

NELLA: Then the show becomes harder.

BORO: That is the idea.

VIRI: The timer is still running.

[The timer resumes at 04:12.]

NELLA: Why is it counting down?

KETT: There is another room.

SUF: We already escaped.

BORO: This is the twist.

[The contestants look at a second door. It is marked "STUDIO EXIT."]

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, LATER

[The second door swings open. The crew carries the prop closet inside.]

BORO: The contestants have escaped.

KETT: The room has not.

[The clock stops at zero.]
