# The Bell Rings for Everyone

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: teen school dramas, hallway confrontations, and overly meaningful locker discoveries*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: On the last day before the school talent assembly, a new student tries to return a borrowed comb without triggering rumors that he has joined every club.

Cast: Kiri, a young adult macaque; Navo, a young adult gibbon; Tess, a young adult capuchin; Principal Ralu, an orangutan; and hall monitor Dren, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Comb Between Us"

### Cold open

INT. CANOPY ACADEMY HALLWAY, MORNING

[Kiri opens locker 12. A comb falls out. Three students look over.]

TESS: You found the comb.

KIRI: I left the comb there.

NAVO: So it is yours?

KIRI: I borrowed it from the grooming club.

TESS: You were in the grooming club?

KIRI: I returned a comb.

NAVO: The whole school is talking.

KIRI: The whole school should get a comb.

[The bell rings. Nobody moves.]

DREN: That was the bell.

TESS: It sounded different today.

DREN: It is the same bell.

NAVO: Nothing is the same.

### Act One

INT. CLASSROOM

[Principal Ralu stands beside a talent assembly sign-up sheet. Kiri sits with the comb in a pencil case.]

RALU: The assembly needs one final act.

TESS: The dance club is ready.

NAVO: The percussion club is ready.

KIRI: I am not in either club.

TESS: You brought a comb.

KIRI: That is not a talent.

NAVO: You could comb a rhythm.

KIRI: It is a plastic comb.

RALU: Creativity comes from limitations.

DREN: The sheet says every student may volunteer.

KIRI: I already volunteered to return the comb.

RALU: That is responsible, but not a stage act.

TESS: We can make it a stage act.

NAVO: We can perform the journey of the comb.

KIRI: It traveled from a locker to a classroom.

TESS: A powerful arc.

NAVO: The comb was lost. Then it was found.

KIRI: It was in my locker.

TESS: Conflict.

[The sign-up sheet passes around. Kiri's name appears under "solo." She erases it.]

RALU: Why did you erase your name?

KIRI: I didn't write it.

DREN: The sheet has five names.

NAVO: One is written on the back.

RALU: That is the same name in reverse.

KIRI: I am only returning the comb.

### Act Two

INT. HALLWAY

[Students gather near locker 12. Tess whispers to Navo. Dren watches.]

TESS: Kiri has an announcement.

KIRI: I do not.

NAVO: This is the moment before the reveal.

KIRI: The reveal is the comb.

TESS: Maybe the comb belonged to a mysterious senior.

KIRI: It belongs to the grooming club.

DREN: The rumor board says Kiri has been chosen for a solo.

KIRI: I didn't choose anything.

NAVO: The board has a list.

KIRI: The list is a sign-up sheet.

TESS: That's how rumors start.

DREN: It also says the comb is a symbol.

KIRI: It is a comb.

[The students gasp softly.]

RALU: Let Kiri speak.

KIRI: I borrowed this from the grooming club because I spilled sap on my fur. I returned it to the nearest safe place. I am not joining five clubs or performing a solo.

TESS: That is a clear speech.

NAVO: It could be the opening of the act.

KIRI: I am not doing an act.

DREN: The bell is about to ring.

[The bell rings early. The students move in three different directions.]

TESS: The bell has chosen the scene.

KIRI: The bell is broken.

RALU: It is on the wrong schedule.

DREN: I can fix it.

NAVO: Does the bell belong to a club?

DREN: The bell belongs to the school.

KIRI: Then return it to the school.

### Act Three

INT. AUDITORIUM

[The assembly begins. Ralu steps to the microphone. Kiri sits in the front row with the comb.]

RALU: Our final act will be an honest announcement from a student.

KIRI: Principal, I did not volunteer.

RALU: You may say no.

KIRI: Then I say no.

RALU: Thank you. That was an honest announcement.

TESS: The audience applauded.

NAVO: It was a short act.

KIRI: It was not an act.

DREN: The schedule has a five-minute gap.

KIRI: We can use it to fix the bell.

RALU: The bell is already fixed.

[The bell rings. It is still early.]

DREN: I fixed the sound, not the schedule.

TESS: The timing is dramatic.

NAVO: The bell could close the show.

KIRI: It should close the hallway, so everyone goes to class.

RALU: That is what a school bell does.

TESS: We have been standing here too long.

NAVO: I should return to percussion.

KIRI: You are in percussion?

NAVO: I borrowed a drum.

TESS: He has been carrying it all week.

NAVO: I have not joined the club.

RALU: Then this is a good day for everyone to return what they borrowed.

[Students begin handing over combs, drums, and one oversized pencil.]

### Tag

INT. HALLWAY, AFTERNOON

[Kiri closes locker 12. A second comb slips out.]

KIRI: Whose is this?

DREN: Put it in lost and found.

TESS: The mystery continues.

NAVO: But the bell is on time.

[The bell rings. Everyone goes to class.]
