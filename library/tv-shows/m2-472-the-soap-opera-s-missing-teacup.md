# The Soap Opera's Missing Teacup

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap operas, ominous close-ups, and domestic mysteries stretched across commercial breaks*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Mira, a macaque; Daro, a baboon; Evi, a gibbon; caretaker Poro, an orangutan; producer Leki, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SOAP OPERA PARLOR - DAY

Mira holds a saucer. The matching teacup is missing. Daro stands beside the window, staring at a curtain.

MIRA: The cup was here this morning.

DARO: And now it is gone.

EVI: The tea is still warm.

[The camera zooms in on the empty saucer. The theme swells.] 

PORO: It is in the kitchen.

[The actors freeze. The producer lifts a stop sign.] 

LEKI: That reveal is scheduled for after the break.

ACT ONE

INT. PARLOR - CONTINUOUS

The actors restart from their marks. Mira asks Daro where he was when the cup disappeared. Daro says he took it to the window. Evi says the window is closed. The caretaker brings the teacup from the kitchen on a tray.

MIRA: Why did you take the cup to the kitchen?

PORO: To refill it.

DARO: Why was it on the saucer?

PORO: I had not poured yet.

[The camera zooms in on the teapot. Leki asks the actors to make the exchange feel more suspicious.] 

MIRA: You refilled my cup without asking?

PORO: You asked me to make tea.

EVI: I witnessed the whole thing.

[The director cues thunder. The stage lights flicker, revealing a cable being moved behind the curtains. Mira looks at it.] 

MIRA: Is the storm also in the kitchen?

LEKI: The storm is a sound cue.

ACT TWO

INT. PARLOR - LATER

The producer wants a cliffhanger before the commercial. Evi points to the empty saucer and says the cup must be nearby. Daro searches behind the curtain; it is empty. Mira opens a drawer and finds a teaspoon. The actors all gasp.

MIRA: That is a spoon.

DARO: Whose spoon?

PORO: It belongs in the drawer.

[The camera cuts to a close-up of the teaspoon, then the saucer, then the cup still sitting on the kitchen tray. The producer asks for another close-up. The actors refuse to repeat the spoon reveal.] 

EVI: The cup is visible in the shot behind us.

[The director checks the monitor. The tray can be seen on a side table, just outside the actors' sightline. Leki asks Mira to turn and discover it.] 

MIRA: I can see the cup. It is on the tray.

DARO: It has returned.

PORO: It never left the building.

[The producer looks at the commercial timer. The cup is discovered before the break, so the cliffhanger must change. Evi suggests a new question: who poured the tea? Poro lifts the teapot.] 

[The producer sets a second saucer beside the tray to create a wider shot. Mira notices the painted blue ring differs from the cup in the kitchen. Evi checks the prop list and confirms only one blue-lined cup is used in the scene.] 

MIRA: I will take the cup that matches the saucer.

DARO: There is only one cup.

PORO: The other one is a sugar bowl.

[The camera pushes toward the sugar bowl. Leki stops it before the bowl is mistaken for a second teacup. The actors remove the extra saucer and return to the single marked set.] 

LEKI: One cup, one saucer, one take.

[The sound operator mutes the thunder cue and leaves the room tone on.] 

ACT THREE

INT. KITCHEN SET - AFTERNOON

The cast moves to the kitchen set. The caretaker pours tea into the cup. Mira confirms it is the right cup; a painted blue line matches the saucer. Daro asks whether anyone else touched it. Evi admits she brought it to the kitchen while clearing the table, then forgot to tell them.

EVI: I put it by the kettle. I thought you saw me.

MIRA: I did not. I was watching the curtain.

DARO: Why were you watching the curtain?

MIRA: It was making a storm noise.

[The actors look at the cable. The sound operator switches off the thunder cue. The curtain stops fluttering. The camera pulls back from the teacup to show the entire set.] 

LEKI: The cup story is over.

DARO: What about the tea?

PORO: It is ready.

[The caretaker sets the cup on the saucer. Mira takes a sip. The blue line on the cup aligns with the painted ring on the saucer. The actors settle at the table without another gasp.] 

TAG

[The storm cue plays once as the cast drinks tea. Everyone looks at the sound booth, not at the cup. Leki cuts the cue.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The teacup rests on its matching saucer beside the switched-off storm speaker.
