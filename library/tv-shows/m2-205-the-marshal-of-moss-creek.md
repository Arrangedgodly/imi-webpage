# The Marshal of Moss Creek

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: western serial convention, where a town marshal investigates a harmless creek diversion caused by a beaver dam*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A frontier marshal must resolve a creek dispute between a beaver family and residents whose footpath has gone muddy.

Cast: Marshal Sela, a lemur; trail boss Odo, a baboon; beaver engineer Pavi, a capuchin; and deputy Nara, a gibbon.

Episode: "The Dry Crossing"

COLD OPEN

EXT. MOSS CREEK, MORNING

Odo steps onto the trail and sinks ankle-deep in mud. He raises a hand to the sky.

ODO: The creek has crossed the line.

Pavi appears beside a small beaver dam. It holds back water from the path.

PAVI: The water is right where we left it.

Sela rides up on a low cart. The cart wheel sinks.

ACT ONE

Sela examines the path. The beavers have built a dam near a low crossing. The residents need the path to reach the orchard. Pavi says the dam keeps a pool near the beaver lodge. Odo suggests moving the beavers. Nara points out they are already moving branches.

Sela asks everyone to show the water route. Pavi draws a channel in the sand. Odo's map shows the path in a straight line. Nara says the path was placed there after a storm, not before the dam.

ACT TWO

EXT. CREEK BANK, AFTERNOON

Sela convenes a town meeting at the crossing. Odo wants a bridge. Pavi says a bridge would cast shade over the pool. Nara suggests a stepping-stone path farther upstream. Odo worries people will get wet. Sela tests the route. It is shallow, but the stones are slick.

Pavi offers a branch from the dam as a handrail, but Sela says it belongs to the structure. He fetches a fallen branch from the bank. Odo sets it in place. Residents cross one at a time. The trail remains muddy, but the new route is dry.

A young beaver adds a twig to the dam. Water shifts slightly away from the path. Pavi says the creek is adjusting. Odo watches the water line recede and asks if the case is closed. Sela says the route needs checking after rain.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CREEK BANK, LATE AFTERNOON

Nara interviews the residents who use the crossing. One carries baskets to the orchard before sunrise. Another leads young monkeys along the path after school. Both say the muddy patch is difficult, but neither wants the beaver pool drained.

Sela asks Pavi to show where the water enters the dam. He points to a narrow channel that can be cleared without damaging the main wall. Odo asks whether the channel caused the mud. Pavi says it diverted some water, but the path also slopes toward the stream.

ODO: The path is guilty of gravity.

NARA: We cannot arrest the hill.

They clear leaves from the channel entrance. Water flows into the pool, away from the path. The mud begins to dry. Pavi checks the dam supports. Sela keeps one branch in place and moves only the loose leaves. The beaver family returns and adds a twig to the upper edge.

Odo sees the new twig and reaches for it. Pavi stops him. Sela explains the dam needs small repairs, just like the path. The residents agree to leave the structure intact and check the channel after storms.

ACT TWO-C

INT. TOWN HALL, EVENING

Sela draws a simple map: dam, channel, dry crossing, and orchard trail. Odo proposes a watch schedule. Nara asks who will check the water before dawn. A young resident volunteers because they already walk to the orchard then. Pavi offers to show the beavers' safe side of the bank.

The town signs the map. Odo stamps it with the marshal's seal, which leaves a muddy paw print. Sela turns the paper over and copies the route neatly. Pavi asks if the case is closed. Sela says the water can change, but the plan is clear.

ACT TWO-D

EXT. CREEK, EVENING

Nara walks the route with a basket to confirm it stays dry. Odo follows with a spare marker stake. The stepping stones are secure, and the trail has begun to firm. Pavi watches the pool level while Sela records the channel position. A beaver adds one twig, then swims away.

ODO: The witness has modified the scene.

SELA: The witness lives here.

They leave the dam undisturbed and return by the dry crossing.

ACT THREE



The town marks a shared crossing above the dam. Odo takes his muddy boots to the water. Sela writes a schedule for checking the stepping stones. Pavi returns to the beaver lodge with the fallen branch.

TAG

Nara rides the cart across the dry route. Odo follows on foot. A small puddle splashes onto his boot.

ODO: The creek has made a final statement.

Sela hands him a cloth.
