# The Root Room Reveal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-design competition series, reveal countdowns, and judges' impossible criteria*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: Two design teams transform a hollow into a rain shelter, but the judging panel's demand for "open, covered, rooted, and portable" forces them to redesign around the tree itself.

Cast: Host Lira, a lemur; designer Pava, a gibbon; designer Koro, a macaque; judge Nemm, an orangutan; and judge Baxi, a capuchin.

## Episode: "A Roof with Roots"

### Cold open

INT. TREE HOLLOW, MORNING

[A wide root crosses the floor. Rain drips through a hole in the roof.]

LIRA: Two teams will compete to make this space livable.

NEMM: It must be open but private.

BAXI: Covered but full of light.

PAVA: The tree is part of the room.

KORO: The room is part of the tree.

LIRA: You have six hours.

[The root shifts. A bowl rolls across the floor.]

NEMM: The room has already moved.

### Act One

INT. TREE HOLLOW

[Pava unfolds a plan. Koro measures the root.]

PAVA: We can build a raised sleeping platform above the root.

KORO: The root supports the tree. We shouldn't cover it.

NEMM: The design brief says maximize floor space.

BAXI: And preserve the tree's natural features.

PAVA: The root is the natural feature.

KORO: The root is also the floor.

LIRA: The judges will return at the reveal.

NEMM: I want a hidden storage wall.

BAXI: I want an open view.

PAVA: The wall will block the view.

KORO: The view will reveal the storage.

[They begin building a platform. The platform catches on the root.]

PAVA: The root is wider than the plan.

KORO: The plan was drawn around the root.

PAVA: The root moved.

KORO: Roots don't move.

[The platform slides. The bowl rolls again.]

### Act Two

INT. TREE HOLLOW, AFTERNOON

[The roof still leaks. Koro holds a broad leaf above a sleeping mat. Pava tests a woven canopy.]

NEMM: The judges want more light.

BAXI: And less rain.

PAVA: A clear roof panel would solve both.

KORO: It would be hard to attach.

PAVA: We can tie it to the root.

KORO: The root is not a hook.

[The canopy droops. Water runs into a bucket.]

LIRA: The design is not ready.

NEMM: It needs a reveal.

BAXI: It needs a drain.

KORO: We can channel the water through a hollow branch.

PAVA: Then the branch becomes a gutter.

NEMM: Is that a design feature?

BAXI: It is a drain.

PAVA: We can decorate it.

KORO: It should stay clear.

[The teams attach the branch. Rainwater flows through it and fills the bucket.]

LIRA: The shelter is dry.

NEMM: The light is blocked.

BAXI: The sleeping mat is bright.

KORO: Because the canopy is open on that side.

NEMM: It is open but private?

PAVA: We can use vines as a curtain.

BAXI: That is a curtain.

### Inspection test

INT. TREE HOLLOW

[Nemm walks beneath the canopy. A drop lands on the judge's shoulder.]

NEMM: The room has a leak.

PAVA: The gutter is working.

BAXI: The drop missed the bucket.

KORO: We can shift the bucket.

[Nemm moves it. The branch gutter follows and lands over the bucket.]

NEMM: The room adjusted.

LIRA: The reveal has changed.

PAVA: The root keeps the canopy up.

KORO: The curtain gives privacy.

BAXI: The bucket catches the rain.

NEMM: Then the room has three working features.

LIRA: And one moving judge.

### Act Three

INT. TREE HOLLOW, EVENING

[Lira gathers the judges. A cloth covers the finished shelter.]

LIRA: Are you ready for the reveal?

NEMM: We are ready to judge.

BAXI: I am ready to see the roof.

[The cloth drops. The root is visible, the canopy is tied to it, and a branch gutter carries rain into the bucket.]

NEMM: The room is open.

BAXI: The room is covered.

NEMM: The view is private.

BAXI: The root is preserved.

PAVA: We built around the tree.

KORO: And the tree held the canopy.

NEMM: What about portable?

KORO: The curtain can move.

NEMM: The room cannot.

LIRA: The challenge said shelter, not cabin.

NEMM: It did say portable.

BAXI: The bucket is portable.

[Nemm moves the bucket. The branch gutter follows and pulls one vine loose.]

PAVA: The roof is moving.

KORO: The root is holding it.

NEMM: The design passes.

LIRA: Which team wins?

BAXI: Both teams solved a different part.

NEMM: Then they must share the prize.

PAVA: What is it?

LIRA: A second bucket.

KORO: We needed that at the start.

### Tag

INT. TREE HOLLOW, NIGHT

[The rain eases. The bowl stays still beneath the new gutter.]

NEMM: I give the room four stars.

BAXI: The root gets five.

[The root holds the canopy as rainwater runs neatly into the bucket.]
