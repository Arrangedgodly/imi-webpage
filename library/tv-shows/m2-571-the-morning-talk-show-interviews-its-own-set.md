# The Morning Talk Show Interviews Its Own Set

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime talk-show intimacy, set reveals, and producers who treat furniture as a recurring guest*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Host Tavi, a gibbon; producer Luma, a macaque; stagehand Odo, an orangutan; guest Kiri, a capuchin; floor manager Vex, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. DAYTIME STUDIO - MORNING

A host sits on a couch marked HOME. Behind him, a painted window shows a garden that is plainly a canvas.

TAVI: Today we meet the set that makes our guests feel at home.

The couch squeaks. Tavi looks at it.

TAVI: Did you hear that?

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The show is filming a special about its own studio. Producer Luma wants the couch to be a warm recurring character. Guest Kiri is an interior designer who came to discuss the studio's tiny green room. Stagehand Odo has been told to move the couch after every segment, but no one has shown him the floor marks.

LUMA: We need a reveal. Tavi, ask the couch how long it has been on the show.

TAVI: It has no mouth.

LUMA: The couch has a microphone under the cushion.

VEX: That is the floor manager's talkback mic.

KIRI: I am here to explain why the green room chair blocks the coat closet.

TAVI: Welcome. First, I must interview the couch.

KIRI: Is that in my contract?

TAVI: No. But the couch has been here longer than I have.

The couch squeaks again as Odo tests its wheels. Luma's headset lights up.

LUMA: That was the cue. Tavi, ask it what it thinks of the studio.

TAVI: Couch, what do you think of the studio?

VEX: It thinks the host is standing on the cable.

Odo lifts Tavi's shoe off a wire. The studio speaker stops relaying the rubbing noise from the talkback mic.

TAVI: It has strong opinions about footwear.

KIRI: It has a wheel pressing on the audio cable.

Vex points out the floor marks to Odo. Odo rolls the couch toward them. The couch rolls too far and stops against the painted garden.

ODO: The wall is not a wall.

TAVI: We found that out during the first season.

LUMA: Please don't mention the garden wall on camera.

KIRI: The green room is behind that wall?

ODO: The green room is through the real door. The garden is canvas.

TAVI: The set is being very candid today.

ACT TWO

Luma insists the couch needs its own close-up. Kiri points out that the actual guest problem is the green room furniture. Odo measures the room and finds that the single chair is wider than the doorway when turned sideways. Vex wants to preserve the studio's appearance, so he asks Odo to rotate it without moving the camera.

VEX: Turn it in place. The shot is live.

ODO: It is a chair, not a compass.

KIRI: The door opens inward. Move the coat rack first.

TAVI: We have reached a design solution.

LUMA: We have not reached the couch interview.

The producer rolls the couch toward camera. Its loose caster catches the taped cable ramp. The couch twists, revealing the hidden talkback microphone and two snack wrappers beneath it.

VEX: Those wrappers are not mine.

TAVI: You were the last one sitting there.

VEX: I was checking the cushion.

KIRI: The design issue is also under the cushion.

Odo disconnects the talkback lead before moving the couch. Vex checks that the cable is clear. They replace the faulty caster with a spare and roll the couch back without dragging any wires.

ODO: The couch is now quiet.

TAVI: Have we lost our guest?

KIRI: I am still here. I have a plan for the green room.

Kiri asks Odo to remove the coat rack, turn the chair diagonally, and mark the chair legs with tape so the next crew can restore the walkway. Vex tests the closet door. It opens fully. The chair still fits, and the camera sees the room rather than the blocked exit.

LUMA: The room looks less crowded. Can we still make the couch the star?

KIRI: The couch belongs in the studio. It doesn't need to be in every room.

TAVI: A powerful distinction.

Odo wipes the replacement caster, routes the microphone cable along the floor edge, reconnects it, and tests the route from set to storage. The couch rolls once, stops on the tape, and stays there.

ACT THREE

The camera returns to the couch for the promised close-up. This time Tavi sits without a microphone wire beneath his foot. The couch makes no sound. Luma waits for a cue that does not come.

TAVI: Couch, would you like to add anything?

VEX: The couch has declined to comment.

KIRI: It has a very good boundary.

Odo checks the tape marks against the chair already positioned in the green room, opens the closet, and verifies the exit path. He photographs the finished setup for the next crew. Luma asks the camera to show the clear doorway rather than the couch.

LUMA: Fine. The room is the story.

TAVI: The room says thank you.

FINAL IMAGE: The couch sits quietly on its marked floor position while the green-room door swings all the way open.
