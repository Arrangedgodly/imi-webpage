# The News Desk Has a Kite

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local breaking-news broadcasts that stretch a minor event into continuous live coverage*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A neighborhood news crew covers a kite stuck in a tree as breaking news until the wind resolves the story and the station keeps reporting.

Cast: Anchor Rova, a macaque; field reporter Sumi, a gibbon; camera operator Tavi, a capuchin; and editor Odo, a baboon.

Episode: "Developing in the Canopy"

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL NEWS STUDIO - DAY

Rova sits before a map. A red banner flashes: BREAKING. Odo holds up a note.

ROVA: We interrupt our gardening segment for breaking news. A kite is stuck in a tree.

ODO: It has been stuck for seven minutes.

ROVA: We go live to Sumi at the park.

EXT. PARK - CONTINUOUS

Sumi stands beneath an oak. A kite rests in a low branch. Tavi frames it.

SUMI: The kite is caught about two meters up. The owner is nearby.

ROVA (ON MONITOR): Is there any danger?

SUMI: Not at present.

[TAVI ZOOMS IN UNTIL THE KITE FILLS THE FRAME.]

ACT ONE

EXT. PARK - LATER

Sumi asks the owner, Lali, what happened. Lali holds a spool of string.

LALI: The wind pulled it into the branch. I was going to wait for it to fall.

SUMI: Can you safely reach it?

LALI: No. I can wait.

ROVA (ON MONITOR): We are hearing patience from the scene.

ODO (IN STUDIO): We need a second angle.

[TAVI MOVES TO THE OTHER SIDE OF THE TREE. IT LOOKS EXACTLY THE SAME.]

TAVI: The kite is still caught.

SUMI: The owner is still waiting.

ROVA: This is an ongoing situation.

ACT TWO

INT. NEWS STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Rova stands beside a graphic showing the tree and a red arrow. Odo updates the map. Tavi returns from the field with a leaf stuck to his shoulder.

ROVA: We now have an exclusive update. The kite has rotated slightly.

ODO: The wind changed direction.

SUMI (ON MONITOR): The owner says it is still her kite.

LALI (ON MONITOR): It is still the blue one.

ROVA: Has the string moved?

SUMI: The string is slack.

ROVA: Is the branch moving?

SUMI: The branch is moving in the wind.

ODO: We can show the leaf diagram again.

ROVA: We need a fresh development.

[TAVI HOLDS UP A LEAF. IT HAS THE SAME SHAPE AS THE ONE IN THE EARLIER SHOT.]

TAVI: The tree has shed a leaf.

ROVA: A possible sign the kite may come free.

SUMI: It is a leaf.

EXT. PARK - MID-AFTERNOON

Lali sits beneath the tree. Sumi checks the string from the ground. Rova remains on the studio monitor.

SUMI: The string is tangled around one small branch.

ROVA: Can the owner pull it free?

LALI: I do not want to snap the branch.

SUMI: A park worker is on the way with a long hook.

ROVA: We have a rescue plan.

LALI: It is a kite. It can wait.

[TAVI MOVES THE CAMERA TO FRAME LALI AND THE KITE TOGETHER. THE KITE FLUTTERS AND TURNS UPSIDE DOWN.]

TAVI: It is moving.

ROVA: Is the kite free?

SUMI: No. The wind turned it over.

ROVA: The situation has changed.

LALI: It is still in the tree.

[A DOG WALKS THROUGH THE SHOT WITH A STICK. TAVI PANS TO FOLLOW IT.]

ROVA: Have we lost the kite?

TAVI: The dog is moving.

SUMI: The kite is still in the branch.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. PARK - LATE AFTERNOON

Sumi films the kite from the ground. Lali sits on a bench. A park worker approaches with a long pole.

PARK WORKER: I can lift the branch a little. The owner can take the kite down.

SUMI: We have help arriving.

ROVA (ON MONITOR): Is the pole approved?

PARK WORKER: It is a branch hook.

TAVI: We need a close shot.

[THE WORKER LIFTS THE BRANCH. THE KITE SLIDES OFF AND LANDS IN LALI'S HANDS.]

SUMI: The kite is down.

ROVA: We have a resolution.

ODO: Should we return to the gardening segment?

ROVA: Stay with us. The owner is winding the string.

ACT THREE

INT. NEWS STUDIO - EVENING

Rova faces the camera. The red banner still flashes. Odo has updated the map to show the kite back on the ground.

ROVA: The kite has been recovered with no injuries and no damage to the tree.

ODO: We can close the story.

ROVA: One final question. Will the owner fly it again?

LALI (ON MONITOR): Tomorrow, if there is less wind.

[THE STUDIO MAP'S RED ARROW DROPS OFF ITS VELCRO.]

ROVA: We have another development.

TAG

Tavi retrieves the arrow. Odo takes down the breaking banner. Rova turns to the gardening segment.

ROVA: The kite story has ended.

ODO: The arrow has landed safely.

[THE NEWS MUSIC PLAYS.]
