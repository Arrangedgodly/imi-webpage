# The One-Take Wonder

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: single-take television specials, hidden cuts, and casts forced to improvise around mistakes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A studio crew attempts to film a full episode in one continuous take, but every hidden transition exposes a new set problem before the cast can finish the scene.

Cast: Director Loma, a baboon; actor Neri, a macaque; actor Piko, a capuchin; camera operator Sari, a gibbon; and set builder Voro, a gorilla.

## Episode: "No Cut"

### Cold open

INT. STUDIO, DAY

[Sari holds a camera on a shoulder rig. Loma whispers the opening cue.]

LOMA: We will film the whole episode in one take.

NERI: What if something goes wrong?

LOMA: We keep going.

PIKO: What if the set falls?

VORO: It won't.

[The kitchen wall tips gently.]

SARI: We are rolling.

LOMA: We are still rolling.

NERI: The wall has moved.

LOMA: Stay in character.

### Act One

INT. KITCHEN SET

[Neri stirs a pot. Piko enters carrying a tray.]

NERI: I have made soup.

PIKO: The script says it is a surprise.

NERI: The soup is a surprise.

PIKO: Is the pot hot?

VORO: It is empty.

LOMA: Keep going.

SARI: The camera needs to move to the doorway.

[She walks backward. The kitchen wall follows on wheels.]

NERI: Why is the wall moving?

PIKO: The room is changing.

LOMA: That is a dramatic transition.

VORO: I am supposed to move it after the scene.

LOMA: Keep moving it.

[The wall rolls into a treehouse set. The actors turn.]

PIKO: We are now outside.

NERI: The soup has traveled.

### Act Two

INT. TREEHOUSE SET

[The camera circles the actors. A branch prop blocks the lens.]

SARI: I cannot see the actors.

VORO: The branch is part of the set.

LOMA: Move around it.

SARI: The camera cable is tangled.

NERI: The story says we are lost.

PIKO: We are lost in the set.

LOMA: That is excellent improvisation.

[They move toward a painted doorway. It is only a flat wall.]

PIKO: The door won't open.

NERI: I will find another way.

[She pushes the wall. It falls forward into a garden set.]

VORO: That was not the planned transition.

LOMA: We have discovered a new location.

SARI: The camera is still rolling.

NERI: The garden is empty.

PIKO: The soup is still on the tray.

LOMA: We need the next line.

NERI: What is the next line?

PIKO: "We have reached the end."

LOMA: We are halfway through the episode.

### Continuity pass

INT. STUDIO

[The camera circles the fallen wall. Piko searches for a cue card.]

PIKO: I have lost my next line.

LOMA: Say what the character would say.

PIKO: The character would ask where the soup went.

NERI: The soup was never here.

VORO: The pot was on the table.

[The camera pans to the pot. It has rolled behind a fake tree.]

SARI: We need to keep moving.

LOMA: The shot cannot stop.

NERI: I will retrieve the pot.

PIKO: That is not in the script.

NERI: It is in the scene now.

[She reaches behind the tree, retrieves the pot, and returns without breaking frame.]

LOMA: Keep that.

SARI: The audience saw the whole reset.

### Act Three

INT. GARDEN SET

[The cast stands among fake plants. The camera light blinks. Sari is running out of battery.]

SARI: We have three minutes of camera power.

LOMA: We need to finish the story.

NERI: The soup is our only prop.

PIKO: We can share it.

VORO: The pot is empty.

NERI: Then the surprise is that there is no soup.

PIKO: I was hoping for soup.

LOMA: The characters can discover the empty pot together.

[They look inside. The camera tilts down.]

NERI: We were looking for a meal.

PIKO: We found a set.

VORO: We can use the garden to grow food.

LOMA: That is the resolution.

SARI: We have one minute.

NERI: We will plant the soup.

PIKO: You cannot plant soup.

NERI: We can plant the pot.

VORO: The pot has a hole.

LOMA: The episode is ending.

SARI: The camera is dying.

[The battery light turns red. The cast looks into the lens.]

NERI: We made one take.

PIKO: We also made three sets fall down.

VORO: Two sets.

LOMA: The wall was already loose.

### Closing cue

INT. STUDIO

[The crew reaches the final mark. Loma holds up one finger for the last line.]

LOMA: Everyone look toward the camera.

NERI: We have been looking at the camera.

PIKO: It has been looking at us.

SARI: The battery is nearly empty.

VORO: The set is still standing.

[The kitchen wall falls behind them.]

LOMA: We have one final image.

SARI: The camera is still rolling.

### Tag

INT. STUDIO

[The camera shuts off. The crew stands among the fallen walls.]

SARI: Did we get the ending?

LOMA: We got the whole episode.

VORO: In one take.

[The empty pot rolls into frame.]
