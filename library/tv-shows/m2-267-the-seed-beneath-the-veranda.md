# The Seed Beneath the Veranda

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap opera television, where a family secret is revealed through an incorrectly labeled seed packet*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: A seed packet bearing the wrong name throws the garden club into a dramatic dispute over who planted the last row of beans.

Cast: Matriarch Luma, lemur; gardener Seli, capuchin; sibling rivals Odo and Nara, baboon and macaque; courier Piri, gibbon.

## Episode: "The Bean We Left Behind"

### Cold Open

[VERANDA. A SEED PACKET LIES ON THE TABLE.]

LUMA: These are not beans.

SELI: The packet says beans.

ODO: The handwriting is Nara's.

NARA: I have never seen that packet.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE FAMILY GATHERS AROUND THE GARDEN TABLE.]

Luma says she planted the last row of beans before the festival. Odo says he watered them. Nara says she delivered the packet but did not open it. Seli says the packet contains peas.

NARA: How do you know?

SELI: The seeds are round.

ODO: Beans can be round.

LUMA: These have a green spot.

Piri arrives with a note from the garden club. It says the new row was planted by "N. Odo."

ODO: It could mean Nara or me.

NARA: It is your initials.

ODO: My name is Odo.

NARA: Your middle name is Nara.

Everyone turns to Odo. He says the note was written by the clerk, who often reverses names.

### Act Two

[THEY INSPECT THE GARDEN ROW.]

The row is sprouting, but each plant is different. Seli identifies peas, beans, and one pumpkin seedling. Luma says she planted only beans.

ODO: Someone mixed the packets.

NARA: The packet has a split seam.

Piri finds a second packet beneath the veranda. It is labeled "beans" but contains sunflower seeds.

LUMA: The garden has two secrets.

SELI: It has one spilled packet.

Nara says she stored the packets in a tin. Odo recalls borrowing the tin to hold screws. Nara says the seeds may have spilled when he returned it.

ODO: I returned the tin.

NARA: Without checking inside.

ODO: The tin was closed.

Piri opens the tin. A screw rolls out and lands in the sunflower row.

[THE FAMILY COMPARES THE PACKETS.]

Seli lays the packets beside each other. One has a green stripe, the other a green drawing. Piri says she delivered both from the garden club and cannot recall which was on top.

NARA: You carried them?

PIRI: In the same basket.

ODO: Then they could have mixed.

LUMA: The labels are different.

ODO: I poured from the tin.

Nara asks why Odo was using the seed tin for screws. He says the toolbox was wet and the metal tin had a lid. Luma says the seeds were also in the tin and could not be separated once mixed.

NARA: That explains the row.

ODO: I did not know the tin held seeds.

PIRI: It had a label.

ODO: The label was under the tin.

SELI: The family secret is the label.

Luma flips the tin. It reads "BEANS" in large letters. Odo says he did not see it because the label was on the bottom.

LUMA: The tin was upside down.

NARA: So were the seed packets.

Piri turns the packets upright. The word "PEAS" is clear.

### Act Three

[THE FAMILY HOLDS A GARDEN CLUB HEARING.]

Luma asks each person to say what they planted. Odo admits he poured the loose seeds into the row, thinking they were beans. Nara had written her name on the packet to claim the garden tools, not the seeds.

NARA: I did not plant the row.

ODO: I thought you had.

LUMA: I planted the beans.

SELI: Then the row is yours.

ODO: The pumpkins are mine.

NARA: You did not plant a pumpkin.

ODO: I moved the tin.

Piri reads the packet labels. One is upside down; "peas" looks like "beans" when turned around.

LUMA: The handwriting was not the problem.

NARA: The orientation was.

They mark the rows with clear signs. Odo takes the sunflower seedlings to a new bed. Nara returns the screws to the tool box.

The family writes new labels on the garden markers. Odo places the screw tin on the tool shelf, label side up.

SELI: The mystery is solved.

ODO: The beans can grow.

LUMA: In the garden.

NARA: The beans are still growing.

### Tag

[THE FAMILY SITS ON THE VERANDA.]

LUMA: The garden is in order.

PIRI: The labels are right side up.

ODO: And the pumpkin?

SELI: It is still a pumpkin.

Nara hands Odo a fresh packet marked "beans." He opens it carefully and checks inside.

NARA: You can plant these.

ODO: Together?

LUMA: The row needs water.

[SFX: WATERING CAN, DRAMATIC MUSIC.]
