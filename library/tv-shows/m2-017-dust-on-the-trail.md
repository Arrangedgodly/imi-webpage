# Dust on the Trail

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: western serial*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: In a dry frontier town, a traveling water cart disappears, and a young marshal learns that the real trouble began when the town stopped sharing its well schedule.

Cast: Marshal Ada, a steady capuchin; Rook, an aging orangutan blacksmith; Lyle, a nervous prairie dog courier; Sal, a sharp-tongued macaque shopkeeper; and Cinder, a horse who refuses to cross a wooden bridge.

## Episode: "The Empty Water Wagon"

### Cold open

EXT. MAIN STREET, HIGH NOON

The water wagon sits in the middle of town with its barrels empty. A tumbleweed rolls past and gets stuck under a wheel. Marshal Ada steps out of the shade.

ADA: Who took the water?

Cinder snorts. A small trail of hoofprints leads back to the well, then vanishes at the bridge.

### Act one

The public well has a household schedule. The wagon returned from the far pasture empty. Sal blames the ranchers; Lyle says the barrels were full when he left.

The schedule is crossed out and rewritten. The bakery and ranchers changed their turns without telling the pasture crew.

At the bridge, Cinder refuses to cross. A board is missing. Rook finds the barrel seams are dry; Lyle confirms they were full at the crossing.

### Act two

At the far pasture, water has drained through a crack in the trough. A cloth patch softened in the sun.

Sal says the cloth patch was hers. She fixed the trough last month and did not tell anyone because she thought the town would blame her for the leak. Ada tells her the repair needs proper wood and a new iron band. Rook can make the band if the town shares the work. Sal says she will provide the boards.

Ada calls a town meeting. The barrels stayed full until the trough, where a patch failed. The schedule changes were never shared.

A rancher says the well belongs to whoever can reach it first. Ada points out that the town built the well together. Lyle says he can carry messages on a fixed route if the schedule is posted clearly. Sal offers a chalk board with room for changes and initials. Rook says he will repair the bridge before anyone sends a heavy cart over it.


[At the well, Ada holds the schedule board.]
ADA: Who changed these times?
SAL: The bakery needed dawn.
RANCHER: We needed the trough.
LYLE: The marks overlap.
ADA: The wagon returned empty.
ROOK: The barrels were sealed.
CINDER: Hrrm.
ADA: Cinder heard the bridge.
LYLE: The boards are loose.
ROOK: I'll bring planks.
SAL: I'll bring nails.
RANCHER: Who gets water first?
ADA: The schedule decides.
RANCHER: The schedule is wrong.
LYLE: We can post a new one.
SAL: With everyone's initials.
ADA: Write the time clearly.
ROOK: Not in chalk.
SAL: I have a board.
CINDER: Hrrm.
ADA: The trough is cracked.
RANCHER: We patched it.
ROOK: With cloth?
SAL: It was temporary.
ADA: It leaked through.
RANCHER: We lost the water.
LYLE: Not to a thief.
ADA: To a hole.
ROOK: I can band it.
SAL: I have boards.
RANCHER: I'll help fit them.
ADA: Good. No speeches.
LYLE: I can carry notices.
CINDER: Hrrm.
ADA: She wants a bucket.
SAL: Give her one.
RANCHER: The first turn is yours.
ADA: The first turn is shared.
ROOK: That is fair.

### Act three

The townsfolk repair the bridge with new planks. Cinder tests it first, one hoof at a time. She crosses after Ada walks beside her. Rook fits an iron band around the trough. Sal brings boards for the base, and the ranchers help set it level. The next water delivery reaches the pasture without leaking.

At the well, the town agrees on two morning turns and one evening turn. Any change must be written and signed. Lyle carries the revised schedule to the pasture and posts a copy at the general store. Ada says the rule does not solve every shortage, but it makes the next disagreement easier to see.

Sal returns to her shop. She hangs a water bucket outside for travelers, free to drink. The ranchers agree to refill it after the evening turn. Lyle receives a small bell for his courier cart, so people hear him coming before he reaches the bridge. Cinder has the bell attached to her harness and shakes her head until it rings.

Ada rides through the town at dusk. The empty wagon is now filled with clean water. She stops at the public well and checks the board. The schedule is clear, with no names crossed out. She leaves the chalk beside it.

### Tag

The tumbleweed rolls down the main street again. It reaches the repaired bridge and stops at the sign: "CROSS SLOWLY."

Cinder noses it aside. It rolls into Sal's doorway.

SAL: We have a new customer.

She places a cup of water on the floor. The tumbleweed rolls past it.

That evening, Lyle carries the revised schedule to the far pasture. He reads each name aloud while the ranchers check their marks. Nobody argues over the first turn. Cinder drinks from the repaired trough, then shakes her bell once. Ada hears it from the street and smiles. The town has not found more water, but it has stopped losing a share through the cracked trough and a schedule nobody trusted.
