# The Trophy with the Wide Base

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports studio panels that turn small equipment failures into a legacy debate*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A youth climbing champion finishes her route, but the sports network cannot award her the trophy because it will not fit through the gym door.

Cast: Nara, a macaque sports anchor; Odo, a baboon replay analyst; Piri, a capuchin climber; Tavi, a lemur referee; Seli, a gibbon trophy maker.

COLD OPEN

INT. CLIMBING GYM - AFTERNOON

Piri reaches the final hold. Her tail steadies her as she taps the bell. The timer stops. The crowd cheers. Tavi raises both hands.

TAVI: Route complete. Piri wins by eleven seconds.

NARA (TO CAMERA): History has been made. We will now wait for history to fit through the door.

Seli rolls in a mountain-shaped wooden trophy. Its base wedges in the gym entrance. The wheels keep turning. The trophy stays put.

SELI: I can reverse it.

ODO: We are going to replay the entrance from three angles.

PIRI: Can I get my medal while the mountain warms up?

ACT ONE

INT. SPORTS BOOTH - CONTINUOUS

Odo replays Piri's finish. Nara points to the screen. He enlarges her last foothold until it fills the monitor.

NARA: At this size, the foothold is almost a second competitor.

ODO: Her left foot is steady. Her right foot is calm. The official clock says she won.

NARA: Then the question is whether a title is official before its trophy arrives.

PIRI: The bell rang. My hands were on the top hold. My name is on the scorecard.

TAVI: Correct on all three.

SELI: The base is four inches wider than the doorway. I can take off the plaque, but the base still won't pass.

NARA: Was that width intentional?

SELI: It represents the breadth of achievement.

PIRI: It represents a door problem.

Nara asks Odo to compare the width with last year's cup. Odo opens a graphic reading THE WIDENING OF VICTORY. The trophy silhouette grows across the screen and covers Nara's face.

NARA: I have been eclipsed by the award.

ACT TWO

INT. GYM FLOOR - MOMENTS LATER

The camera crew wheels its lights closer. Tavi draws a chalk line at the door and makes everyone step back from the trophy.

TAVI: No pushing. We protect the walls and the finish.

NARA: Our panel is split. One side says trophy should adapt. The other side says doorway should recognize excellence.

PIRI: The doorway is not on the team.

SELI: I could remove two carved peaks. That would narrow it.

PIRI: I like the peaks. Could we carry it through the wider loading door?

TAVI: Awards must be presented where the climbing wall is visible.

PIRI: The wall is visible from the loading bay.

Tavi checks the laminated ceremony guide. A bright sticker marks a preferred camera angle. She peels it off. The camera operator swivels toward them.

TAVI: This says preferred. It does not say required.

NARA: A disputed sticker. Let's go to the replay.

PIRI: We don't need a replay of a sticker.

Odo lays tape on the floor to trace a route through the side corridor. The trophy fits if it is held upright and turned at the drinking fountain. Seli checks each corner. Nara narrates the move like a championship climb.

NARA: Seli approaches the fountain. The base is stable. The left peak is clear. A small rotation could decide everything.

SELI: I build these. I know how to turn a wooden object.

ODO: Her hands are chalked. No grip adjustment.

Seli turns it. The trophy passes. Odo throws confetti from the replay booth. It lands in the fountain and floats past the trophy like tiny flags.

ACT THREE

INT. GYM FLOOR - LATER

The crew brings the trophy through the side corridor and onto the ceremony platform beside the climbing wall. One foot rocks on the platform.

TAVI: Hold. The stand is uneven.

NARA: We have a wobble in the medal zone.

PIRI: I can hold the trophy while Seli fixes it.

SELI: You already did the climbing.

Seli slips a felt pad under one corner. The trophy steadies. She attaches the plaque with Piri's name. Tavi checks the official result once more, then passes Piri a medal. Piri accepts it and hangs it around her own neck.

NARA: Officially awarded. The champion has her medal, and the trophy is standing.

ODO: We have footage of the route, the doorway, and the final four inches.

PIRI: Keep the route footage. Sell the four inches to the door company.

Seli gives Piri a small wooden foothold carved from leftover material. Piri clips it to her gym bag.

TAG

INT. SPORTS BOOTH - EVENING

Nara stands beside a graphic of the trophy. Odo shrinks it until it fits in the corner of the screen.

NARA: The award is now within broadcast limits.

ODO: We have to enlarge it for the replay.

The trophy graphic expands over the score. Nara ducks beneath the screen.

FINAL IMAGE: Piri exits through the wide loading door with her medal shining; the trophy remains by the wall, and Odo's on-screen arrow points toward the nearest carpenter.
