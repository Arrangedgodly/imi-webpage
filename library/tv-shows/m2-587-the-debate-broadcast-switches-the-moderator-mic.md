# The Debate Broadcast Switches the Moderator Mic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised debate rules, split-screen interruptions, and microphones that make the moderator sound like a candidate*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Moderator Odo, a mandrill; candidate Luma, an orangutan; candidate Vex, a baboon; audio engineer Suri, a macaque; stage runner Pip, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. DEBATE STAGE - NIGHT

Odo leans toward his microphone.

ODO: We begin with the first question.

LUMA: I can answer that.

VEX: So can I.

Odo's mic is silent; both candidates' mics are live.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The town's televised debate is about transit plans. Moderator Odo has a strict speaking clock, and candidates Luma and Vex each have an opening statement. Audio engineer Suri discovers the console's labels were switched after a rehearsal. The "MODERATOR" slider controls a floor lamp, while the "AUDIENCE" slider controls Odo's microphone.

SURI: I have found the problem.

ODO: Is that your opening statement?

SURI: It is a technical update.

PIP: The floor lamp just got a microphone.

VEX: I would like to address the floor.

LUMA: The floor has had more uninterrupted time than we have.

Odo asks Suri to mute all channels and test them one at a time. The debate clock is paused. The candidates agree not to use the accidental microphone during the test. Vex whispers a practice answer anyway, and the audience hears it through a monitor return.

VEX: I was only checking.

SURI: You were checking at full volume.

LUMA: The test is now part of the debate.

ACT TWO

Suri places a test card in front of each channel. Pip holds a colored marker so the camera can distinguish which light is being checked. Odo speaks into his mic; the floor lamp stays dark. Luma speaks into hers; the correct red indicator lights. Vex's mic works, but his timer is still wired to the moderator channel and begins counting down while Odo is silent.

ODO: My question has been given a deadline.

SURI: I will reconnect the timer after the mic test.

PIP: The lamp has not answered any questions.

Suri changes the console labels physically rather than relying on the old tape. She tests each channel again, then routes the timer to its separate display. Vex asks whether the lost seconds will be credited to his opening statement. Odo says no opening statement has begun.

LUMA: Then let us start fairly.

VEX: We should also agree not to interrupt each other.

ODO: The speaking clock will do that.

SURI: I can cut the channel when the time ends.

VEX: Can you cut the clock instead?

Suri tests the cut-off. The stage runner's mic is muted; Pip raises the colored marker and says nothing. The audience sees the test but hears only the correct channel. The debate begins with one minute each. Luma describes her transit plan and gives a route with two stops. Vex argues for a different route but accidentally cites the old stop count.

LUMA: That route has three stops.

VEX: The plan says two.

ODO: The printed map says three.

VEX: Then the map has offered a different plan.

The moderator asks each candidate to state the cost and timeline. Luma says the route will require a year of construction. Vex says his proposal is faster, but admits one bridge must first be surveyed. The clock cuts him off as his minute ends. He looks at the timer and accepts the signal.

ACT THREE

Odo gives each candidate one closing statement. This time his microphone works, the candidates' channels light correctly, and the timer cuts off each speech at its limit. No one gets extra seconds from the lamp. Suri watches the meters; Pip keeps the test card ready in case a channel misbehaves.

ODO: The broadcast is balanced. The discussion is now yours.

LUMA: I support publishing the stop list before construction.

VEX: I support surveying the bridge before promising a date.

PIP: The audience can compare both plans.

SURI: And they heard the moderator.

After the show, Suri labels the corrected console and locks the debate preset. Odo asks whether the floor lamp will ever have a microphone. Pip taps it; the lamp makes no sound. Before they leave, Suri checks the recording meters and confirms each speaker is on the right track. Odo asks for the test card, then signs it beside the console so the next crew can verify the labels before going live.

VEX: It had a better speaking clock than I did.

LUMA: It stayed within its limit.

The candidates leave the stage without a closing argument about the audio equipment. Odo signs off, and Suri turns off the channels in order.

FINAL IMAGE: The console displays three labeled microphones and a separate silent timer, while the floor lamp glows without interrupting.
