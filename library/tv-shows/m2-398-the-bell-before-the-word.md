# The Bell Before the Word

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised spelling contests, suspenseful pauses, word-origin detours, and contestants whose buzzers ring too early*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A spelling final is delayed when the stage bell rings before the pronouncer gives a word, sending the host into an unscripted vocabulary segment.

Cast: Host Vali, a gibbon; finalist Kemi, a macaque; finalist Taro, a capuchin; pronouncer Odo, a baboon; scorekeeper Neri, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. SPELLING STUDIO - NIGHT

Odo stands at the microphone. The stage bell rings. Both finalists step forward.

KEMI: I have not heard the word.

TARO: Neither have I.

VALI: The bell has entered the contest.

ACT ONE

Neri checks the scoreboard. Neither contestant has a point. The bell sits on a small table beside a red button. Vali asks if it is supposed to ring automatically. Odo says it is a hand bell for ending a round. A stagehand touched the table when moving a light.

NERI: We will remove the bell until it is needed.

VALI: The contest needs a bell.

NERI: The bell needs a table that does not shake.

Kemi asks Odo for the first word. Odo says "lantern." Kemi asks for the origin and definition. Odo gives both. She spells the word correctly. The scoreboard flashes Taro's name, and his buzzer quacks.

TARO: I did not answer.

VALI: The equipment has guessed.

ACT TWO

The round continues. Taro receives "intermission." He asks for the word in a sentence. Vali reads one from the script, but the sentence includes a word he has not learned. Taro asks for that word too. Odo gives him "curtain." Taro spells it correctly. Neri updates the manual tally while the screen adds a spinning curtain graphic.

VALI: The graphic is still closing.

NERI: It is waiting for a cue.

The bell rings again when the curtain animation finishes. Kemi steps forward by reflex. Odo points out that no word was given. Neri turns off the animation. The bell stops.

Odo gives Kemi "apricot." She asks for the language of origin and the definition. Odo answers. The overhead camera lowers for a close-up of the word card, blocking Kemi's view. Vali lifts the camera with one hand and continues reading. The audience applauds. Kemi spells the word correctly.

KEMI: A-P-R-I-C-O-T.

NERI: Correct. The point goes to Kemi.

ACT TWO-B

The score is tied. Taro receives "silhouette." He asks Odo to repeat it and use it in a sentence. Odo does. The word card blows off the table as the studio fan starts. Neri catches it. Vali calls a brief pause while the card is replaced under a paperweight.

TARO: I can spell it without the card.

ODO: You may begin when ready.

Taro spells it correctly. The bell stays silent. Kemi then receives "marionette." She asks for the definition, origin, and a sentence. Odo begins explaining its history. Vali looks at the broadcast clock, which has reached the end of the allotted segment.

VALI: We have time for the spelling.

KEMI: M-A-R-I-O-N-E-T-T-E.

Neri checks the answer sheet. It is correct. The scoreboard awards the point to Kemi, but an automatic graphic crowns Taro. Neri turns off the display and announces the manual result. Kemi wins by one point.

The closing graphic places the trophy in front of the finalists. Taro asks if it is part of the prize. Neri checks the card and says the winner receives a dictionary; the trophy belongs to the studio. Vali asks the finalists to hold their places for the camera. The fan shifts the word cards again, and Kemi catches them before they reach the floor.

TARO: The bell is not on the prize list.

KEMI: The bell is on its own table.

Neri moves the table farther from the fan. Odo checks the word cards and lays them flat under a clear cover. The next contestant will see the words without the cards blowing into the audience. Vali removes the extra camera light that had been aimed at the bell.

VALI: We can finish without a bell.

NERI: We can finish with the score.

ACT THREE

Vali presents Kemi with a small word dictionary. Taro congratulates her and asks to borrow it for a word he missed. Kemi hands it to him. Odo places the bell on a separate table with rubber feet. The table does not wobble.

VALI: A contest decided by spelling.

NERI: And a bell that now waits its turn.

The studio applauds. The automatic curtain graphic starts again, but no bell rings. The red button remains covered with a label reading "MANUAL."

TAG

Vali reads one final word for the credits: "quiet." The audience stays silent. Odo closes the dictionary.

FINAL IMAGE: The bell rests on its stable table while Kemi and Taro walk off together, debating the pronunciation of "marionette."
