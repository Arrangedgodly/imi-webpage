# The Panel Show Debates a Bench

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: debate panels that turn a straightforward local decision into an endless argument over framing and airtime*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: moderator Pali, a macaque; councilor Enu, an orangutan; planner Sava, a lemur; caller Riko, a gibbon; producer Toma, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. DEBATE STUDIO - NIGHT

A photograph shows a park bench facing a pond. The screen reads: SHOULD THE BENCH TURN LEFT OR RIGHT?

PALI: Tonight, we hear both sides.

SAVA: It currently faces the path.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Toma tells the panel that the bench is a local issue. Enu says it should face the pond. Sava says the path must remain clear for wheelchairs and strollers. Pali asks each speaker to state a position in one sentence. The lights flash for the first caller before the question arrives.

RIKO (OVER PHONE): I just want to know where the bench is.

Pali says it is beside the pond. The screen changes to BREAKING: BENCH LOCATION CONTESTED. Toma asks the graphics team to remove the alert. They change it to DEVELOPING STORY. The alert stays.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The panel asks for the actual park plan. Sava brings one. It shows a bench pad with two possible anchor points. The first faces the pond and narrows the path. The second faces diagonally toward both the water and the walkway. Enu says the view matters. Riko says he sits there to wait for the bus and wants to see both directions.

Pali asks which position fits the plan. Toma cues a poll graphic. It offers LEFT and RIGHT, but the plan's option is diagonal. The callers split evenly. The poll animation keeps counting after the phone lines close. The screen shows 99 percent for each choice.

[Enu stands to point at the drawing. The camera follows his finger to the corner of the map and cuts off his face. Pali asks him to sit back down.]

ACT TWO - CONTINUED

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Sava places two cardboard seat models on the desk. One faces the pond; one angles toward the path. Pali asks Riko to choose. Riko says he cannot tell from a model on a television desk. Toma suggests a live remote. The camera crew walks to the park, but the bench is occupied by two people eating lunch. They ask the crew to wait until they finish.

Enu begins a closing statement. The timer runs out. Pali gives the last twenty seconds to the sponsor instead of the planner. The sponsor sells cushions. Riko calls back to say the bench has room for both sides if it is angled. Toma asks whether the call can count as a vote. Pali says it can count as information.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

The remote producer tells the panel to keep debating until the poll reaches one hundred percent. Toma asks Sava to repeat the clearance measurement. She holds the plan beside the camera. The poll sticks at ninety-nine percent and a sliver. Enu reaches for the tablet, but Pali switches it off. The crew carries the panel chairs toward the park so the discussion can happen beside the actual bench. The camera operator brings the poll tablet but leaves its countdown disconnected. Pali asks the groundskeeper to stand beside the map.

ACT THREE

EXT. PARK - NEXT DAY

The panel meets beside the bench. Sava marks the diagonal position with chalk. Enu sits down and can see the pond. Riko sits at the other end and can see the bus path. Pali asks whether the compromise is dramatic enough for television. Toma's screen graphic labels the bench a historic center point.

A groundskeeper moves the bench six inches to avoid a puddle. The panel freezes. The camera zooms in. Sava checks the path width; it remains clear. Enu asks the groundskeeper whether the new angle is better. The groundskeeper says it is easier to mow around. Riko gives a small nod and sits again.

The studio panel is now outside. Pali asks each person to state their final position. Enu supports the diagonal angle. Sava supports it because it meets the clearance plan. Riko supports it because the bus stop is visible. Toma asks for one more poll. Pali turns off the graphic tablet.

PALI: We have a bench, a plan and a place to sit.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - NIGHT

The panel returns to the desk. The screen still reads LEFT OR RIGHT. Toma changes it to DIAGONAL. The poll graphic shows one option and no countdown.

FINAL IMAGE: The park bench faces the pond and path at once, while the panel's chairs are finally angled toward each other.
