# The Hospital Drama Diagnoses a Missing Sock

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: medical procedurals, urgent jargon, and a team of primate clinicians whose patient is a sock that went missing in the laundry*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Chief Resident Sela, a macaque; Doctor Varo, an orangutan; Nurse Pip, a capuchin; Lab Tech Niko, a baboon; Patient's Owner Daro, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. HOSPITAL LAUNDRY - NIGHT

A hamper is wheeled under a dramatic light. Pip lifts a single blue sock with two fingers.

PIP: We have lost one.

A monitor beeps.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The hospital's medical drama unit is filming a midnight case in the laundry room. Daro says the blue sock went into a pair at the start of the shift, but only one returned from the wash. Chief Resident Sela wants to treat the missing sock as a critical case. Doctor Varo asks for a count of all laundry bags before ordering a dramatic scan.

SELA: When did the loss occur?

DARO: Before the dryer cycle. I put in a pair. I got one back.

VARO: Does the patient have a pulse?

PIP: It is fabric.

SELA: The monitor shows a flat line.

NIKO: That is the lint filter indicator.

The room goes quiet. Sela asks Niko to run an inventory of the laundry cart. Niko finds three blue socks, two red socks, and one towel. Daro insists the missing sock is not one of those.

DARO: Mine has a yellow stripe at the cuff.

PIP: The sock on the table has no stripe.

SELA: We may have a second patient.

VARO: Or a laundry mix-up.

SFX: Washing machine beeps; door unlocks.

PIP: The cycle is complete. I will open the drum after it stops.

VARO: Stand back. The drum may contain complications.

PIP: It contains wet towels.

NIKO: There is a sock behind the drum gasket.

SELA: Is it the missing one?

DARO: It has a yellow stripe.

PIP: It is stuck to the gasket. The machine cannot spin until it is removed.

ACT TWO

The show wants to reveal the sock with a surgical instrument. Varo prepares forceps, but Nurse Pip stops him and switches off the machine. Niko unplugs the washer at the wall after confirming the drum has stopped. Only then does Pip reach into the accessible gasket channel with a gloved hand and lift the sock free.

PIP: We are not using forceps. The cuff is caught in the fold.

SELA: The patient is stable.

VARO: The patient is still a sock.

DARO: That is my sock.

Sela compares the yellow stripe with Daro's laundry label. The sock is intact but damp. Daro says the dryer cannot run until the gasket is checked. Niko finds the washer manual taped behind the detergent shelf; the sock must have slipped through the door fold before the cycle began.

NIKO: The gasket is clear. There is no tear.

PIP: I can test the empty drum on a rinse cycle before anyone adds clothes.

VARO: We should call a specialist.

SELA: The maintenance technician is already on the way. We can wait and keep the machine off.

The dramatic monitor beeps again. Varo turns toward it.

VARO: The patient's heart!

NIKO: It is the door-open alarm. The dryer beside it is finished.

PIP: I will silence that alarm once I close the dryer door.

Daro asks for a second sock, but the rest of the laundry is still wet. The show crew begins to roll in a bright examination lamp. Sela moves it away from the wet floor and asks the camera to show the instruction label. The label identifies two separate machines and one shared alert panel.

DARO: Could the pair be reunited?

NIKO: The matching sock is still in the clean basket. It was folded under the towel.

PIP: I found it. Yellow stripe, same label.

SELA: The pair is complete.

VARO: We have ruled out amputation.

DARO: Nobody said amputation.

VARO: The trailer editor may have.

ACT THREE

The maintenance technician checks the gasket and signs the washer back into service. Niko runs the empty rinse cycle while Pip waits by the controls. The door alert stays quiet, the lint filter light is cleared, and the laundry chart now lists every bag by color.

SELA: The sock is recovered, the machine is safe, and the patient may return to the drawer.

PIP: After it dries.

VARO: We should do a follow-up episode.

DARO: Only if the other sock goes missing.

NIKO: It is in the basket beside you.

Daro lifts the matching sock and holds it next to the damp one. They match. The monitor beeps once as the machine finishes its rinse.

FINAL IMAGE: A complete pair of blue socks sits beside the washer while the dramatic monitor is unplugged.
