# The Break-Room Documentary

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: workplace mockumentaries, reaction interviews, and awkward office silence*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A camera crew follows staff at the Canopy Permit Center on the day the break room's only table is scheduled to be moved upstairs.

Cast: supervisor Dara, a gorilla; clerk Fenn, a gibbon; runner Yip, a squirrel monkey; facilities lead Osa, a baboon; and interviewer Cam, a macaque.

## Episode: "The Table Goes Up"

### Cold open

INT. CANOPY PERMIT CENTER, MORNING

[A low table sits in the break room. Osa examines it with a tape measure. Everyone watches.]

OSA: The table is too wide for the stairwell.

DARA: It has been in this room for years.

OSA: The stairwell has also been here for years.

FENN: We can turn it sideways.

YIP: I tried. It became wider.

[Everyone looks at the camera.]

DARA: The table is going upstairs today.

FENN: Why?

DARA: The upstairs room needs a table.

YIP: This room also needs a table.

OSA: We have one table.

[Long silence.]

### Act One

INT. BREAK ROOM

[Cam interviews staff one at a time beside the table.]

CAM: What does the table mean to you?

FENN: It is where I eat lunch.

CAM: Anything else?

FENN: I also read the notices.

CAM: How does it feel to lose it?

FENN: I haven't lost it. It is moving upstairs.

[Cut to Dara's interview.]

DARA: A table is a table. Staff can eat wherever they sit.

[Cut to Yip's interview. He sits on the floor beside the table.]

YIP: The table is a good height for me.

CAM: Why are you on the floor?

YIP: The chair is upstairs.

[Cut to Osa in the hallway measuring the stairwell.]

OSA: If we take the legs off, the table will fit.

DARA: Can we take the legs off?

OSA: The legs are holding it up.

DARA: Could we take them off one at a time?

OSA: It would not be a table.

### Act Two

INT. STAIRWELL

[The crew follows Osa and Fenn as they attempt to move the table. It catches at the first landing.]

FENN: It won't turn.

OSA: We need to angle it.

YIP: The angle is already steep.

DARA: We have to be strategic.

FENN: The table is stuck.

DARA: We have to be more strategic.

[They try lifting one end. The table rises, then slides back.]

YIP: It has returned to its original position.

OSA: Gravity is consistent.

CAM: Can you explain the process to the camera?

OSA: We are moving a table up the stairs.

CAM: What makes it difficult?

OSA: The stairs.

DARA: And the table.

FENN: And the people holding it.

[They all lower the table. Silence.]

DARA: Let's take a break.

FENN: In the break room?

DARA: Yes.

YIP: The table is in the stairwell.

DARA: We can stand around it.

[They gather around the stuck table. Osa places a lunch container on it.]

OSA: This is now a break area.

CAM: Does the table feel different here?

FENN: It feels like it has a view.

YIP: It blocks the stairs.

DARA: We can eat quickly.

### Act Three

INT. STAIRWELL, AFTERNOON

[The supervisor holds a new note: "UPSTAIRS TABLE NEEDED." Osa reads it.]

OSA: It says the upstairs room needs a table.

DARA: It doesn't say this table.

FENN: We have a folding table in storage.

YIP: It fits upstairs.

CAM: Why didn't anyone mention it earlier?

DARA: I thought this was the folding table.

OSA: This is a dining table.

FENN: They look similar under the notice.

DARA: So we moved the wrong table.

[Everyone looks at the camera.]

YIP: The folding table is upstairs now.

OSA: I moved it yesterday.

FENN: What is upstairs?

OSA: A table.

DARA: And what is down here?

OSA: Another table.

CAM: The office has two tables?

DARA: The office has one table in each room.

FENN: That is how rooms work.

[Osa begins moving the stuck table back down. It glides easily.]

YIP: It fits going down.

OSA: The stairs are helping.

DARA: We will keep it here.

FENN: And lunch?

DARA: We can eat at the table.

YIP: The table has returned to its original position.

### Confessional

INT. RECORDS ROOM

[The crew asks each worker one final question. A stack of forms leans toward the lens.]

CAM: What did the table teach you?

FENN: That lunch can happen without sitting.

YIP: That the stairs have a landing.

OSA: That a table can fit one direction and not another.

DARA: That the staff can share a table.

CAM: Will you move the table again?

DARA: Only if the note is current.

FENN: How can we tell?

OSA: The ink is still wet.

[Osa touches the note. Ink marks her finger.]

OSA: It is current.

DARA: Then we should move it.

YIP: Which table?

[Everyone looks toward the lens.]

CAM: We will follow up next week.

### Tag

INT. BREAK ROOM, EVENING

[The table is back in place. Osa reads a new note: "PLEASE MOVE THIS TABLE UPSTAIRS."]

OSA: This note is old.

DARA: How old?

OSA: It is written on the back of tomorrow's note.

[The crew looks into the camera.]

[The table stays downstairs while Osa pins tomorrow's note beside today's.]
