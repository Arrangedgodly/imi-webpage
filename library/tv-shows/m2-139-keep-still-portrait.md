# Keep Still for the Portrait Round

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised art competitions that impose arbitrary time limits, crowd artists with judges, and score likeness before a portrait is finished*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Piri wants a fast portrait challenge. Painter Suma needs her subject to sit still long enough to work, while judge Dalo keeps asking for dramatic reveals before the paint is dry.

Cast: Piri, a macaque host; Suma, a lemur painter; Dalo, a gorilla judge; and Nix, a capuchin stage assistant.

## Episode: "The Face Is Still Moving"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. SUMA SETS A CANVAS ON AN EASEL. PIRI SITS ON A STOOL.]

PIRI: You have ten minutes to paint me.

SUMA: Will you sit still?

PIRI: I will provide natural energy.

[THE TIMER STARTS. PIRI WAVES TO THE CAMERA. SUMA DRAWS A WAVING HAND.]

DALO: A strong opening gesture.

SUMA: That is not the face.

[PIRI TURNS TO LOOK AT DALO. THE TIMER BEEPS.]

### Act One

[SUMA STARTS AGAIN. PIRI FACES FRONT.]

PIRI: You may begin.

[SUMA MIXES PAINT. PIRI LEANS FORWARD TO SEE THE PALETTE.]

SUMA: Please stay where you are.

PIRI: I am helping you see me.

DALO: Is interaction allowed?

NIX: The rules say "live subject."

[SUMA PAINTS PIRI'S EAR. PIRI TURNS TOWARD THE AUDIENCE. THE EAR NOW POINTS THE WRONG WAY.]

PIRI: I heard someone laugh.

SUMA: The ear is on the other side now.

DALO: That could be a bold interpretation.

SUMA: It is a continuity error.

[NIX HOLDS UP A MIRROR SO PIRI CAN CHECK HIS POSE. PIRI TURNS TO LOOK INTO IT. SUMA SETS THE MIRROR DOWN.]

DALO: The portrait is developing a new perspective.

SUMA: The subject moved.

PIRI: I heard applause.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS AGAIN. PIRI TURNS TO WAVE. NIX PUTS A SMALL "HOLD STILL" SIGN BESIDE THE CAMERA.]

### Act Two

[THE TIMER RESTARTS. PIRI SITS WITH BOTH HANDS ON HIS KNEES. DALO WHISPERS TO NIX.]

DALO: Can we see the canvas?

NIX: Not until the reveal.

DALO: The audience needs progress.

[NIX SHOWS A SMALL MONITOR. IT DISPLAYS THE CANVAS UPSIDE DOWN.]

PIRI: That is a very long neck.

SUMA: It is upside down.

[NIX ROTATES THE MONITOR. THE PORTRAIT HAS A LARGE FOREHEAD AND A SMALL CHIN.]

DALO: We can score the forehead separately.

SUMA: I have not painted the chin yet.

PIRI: Can you make it more like the camera view?

SUMA: The camera view changes every time you wave.

[THE STAGE LIGHT SHIFTS AGAIN. PIRI'S SHADOW COVERS THE CANVAS. NIX ASKS THE LIGHT OPERATOR TO LOCK THE POSITION.]

DALO: Strong character.

PIRI: My forehead is not that large.

SUMA: The light is from above.

[THE STAGE LIGHT SHIFTS. PIRI'S SHADOW MOVES ACROSS HIS FACE. SUMA CHANGES THE SHADING. PIRI STARTS TO SPEAK.]

SUMA: If you talk, your mouth moves.

PIRI: I will be silent.

[THE TIMER TICKS. PIRI'S TAIL SLIDES OFF THE STOOL. HE SHIFTS TO CATCH IT.]

DALO: A dramatic pose change.

SUMA: A balance correction.

[NIX PLACES A CUSHION UNDER THE STOOL. PIRI SITS AGAIN.]

[DALO ASKS THE AUDIENCE TO SCORE THE PAINTING. SOMEONE CALLS OUT A NINE. PIRI TURNS TO WAVE AGAIN.]

SUMA: The portrait is already finished.

PIRI: I heard applause.

DALO: We will score the finished work, not the reaction.

[NIX LOWERS THE AUDIENCE SCORECARDS UNTIL THE PAINTING IS VISIBLE.]

### Act Three

[THE TIMER SOUNDS. SUMA PUTS DOWN HER BRUSH. DALO ASKS FOR THE REVEAL.]

PIRI: Do I need to stay still now?

SUMA: The painting is done.

DALO: The judges have not finished scoring.

SUMA: Then judge the painting, not the timer.

[NIX CHECKS THE PAINT. THE CANVAS IS DRY ENOUGH TO TURN TOWARD THE AUDIENCE.]

DALO: We are judging likeness, technique, and stage presence.

SUMA: The portrait is still wet.

PIRI: I can hold still for the drying period.

DALO: The round has ended.

[SUMA TURNS THE CANVAS. PIRI LOOKS AT IT. THE PORTRAIT SHOWS HIS FACE WITH HIS HEAD TURNED TOWARD THE AUDIENCE.]

PIRI: That is the moment I waved.

SUMA: It is the moment you held still long enough.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. DALO SCORES THE PAINTING. NIX PUTS THE TIMER AWAY.]

DALO: Best movement, best color, and best recovery from a moving subject.

PIRI: I was the subject.

DALO: You moved.

[SUMA WRITES THE DATE ON THE BACK OF THE PORTRAIT. NIX ADDS THE SUBJECT'S NAME.]

PIRI: Will you paint another one?

SUMA: If the subject can sit still.

[PIRI SITS. HE DOES NOT TURN UNTIL SUMA PUTS THE BRUSH DOWN.]

### Tag

[PIRI SITS FOR A FINAL PHOTO. SUMA HOLDS UP THE PORTRAIT.]

NIX: Everyone look at the camera.

[PIRI TURNS TOWARD THE AUDIENCE. SUMA PHOTOGRAPHS THE BACK OF HIS HEAD.]

PIRI: Is that the entry?

SUMA: It is a study in movement.

DALO: It is a photograph of a head.

[NIX PLACES THE CAMERA ON A TRIPOD AND ASKS EVERYONE TO FACE FORWARD.]

SUMA: I have my next entry.
