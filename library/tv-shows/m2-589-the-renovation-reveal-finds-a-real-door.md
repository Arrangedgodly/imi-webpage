# The Renovation Reveal Finds a Real Door

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home renovation reveals, before-and-after montages, and a contractor who discovers the camera wall blocks the actual doorway*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Renovation host Rumi, a tortoise; builder Kiri, a macaque; homeowner Odo, an orangutan; camera director Vex, a baboon; carpenter Tavi, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SMALL LIVING ROOM - DAY

Vex cues the big reveal. Rumi pulls a curtain aside. A painted wall stands where the room's door should be.

RUMI: We opened up the space.

ODO: You covered the door.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The renovation show has transformed Odo's crowded living room. Builder Kiri installed shelves and moved a sofa, but camera director Vex added a scenic wall to hide cables. The wall now blocks the real doorway from the camera angle. Host Rumi wants to reveal the finished room before checking how Odo gets through it.

RUMI: The colors are brighter, the shelves are higher, and the room feels bigger.

ODO: I have been entering from the kitchen.

KIRI: The real door is behind that flat.

VEX: The flat keeps the shot clean.

TAVI: It also blocks the route.

Odo shows the narrow walkway between the sofa and the scenic wall. A basket of blankets makes the gap too tight. Tavi moves the basket and asks whether the wall is secured. Vex says it is a temporary flat on rolling feet. Kiri says the feet are not locked.

KIRI: Nobody leans on it until we secure the brakes.

RUMI: Can we call that the first reveal?

VEX: The first reveal is the wallpaper.

ACT TWO

Kiri locks the flat's wheels and measures the doorway. The wall still hides the door. She asks Vex to move the camera track and turn the flat toward the window, where it can remain outside the walking path. Vex worries the shot will reveal the cable bundle. Tavi offers to fasten the cable to the track with clips, then checks that the clips do not pinch it.

TAVI: The route can stay open, and the cable can stay contained.

VEX: We lose the painted fireplace.

ODO: The fireplace is painted on.

RUMI: It looked very warm.

Kiri releases the wheel brakes, and she and Tavi move the scenic flat carefully. Odo watches the doorway and confirms it swings freely. The scenic wall now sits beside the window, leaving the door visible. Vex asks for the reveal music again, but the crew is still routing the camera cable. Rumi holds the music cue.

RUMI: The music waits until the doorway works.

Odo checks the door swing from the kitchen. The door clears the sofa, and the scenic flat appears beside the open route from the living room camera angle. The room is still compact, but the walkway is clear. Tavi checks the flat's brakes and adds a visible "SCENIC PANEL - DO NOT LEAN" tag. Vex says the tag is ugly.

KIRI: It tells the crew what the panel is.

VEX: Could it be a decorative tag?

ODO: It can be useful.

ACT THREE

Before the reveal, Kiri asks Odo to test the route with the items he actually carries. He walks through with his morning cup, turns at the shelf, and returns with a folded blanket. The door does not hit the sofa, and the blanket basket stays outside the walking line. Vex suggests another camera move; Tavi checks the cable clips and keeps the track beside the window. Rumi waits until Odo confirms the route works twice in opposite directions, while the furniture stays in its finished arrangement.

The camera makes one slow pass from the open doorway across the living room. The shelves hold books and a bowl of fruit. The sofa remains clear of the path, and the scenic wall frames the window without covering the exit.

RUMI: The room is ready.

ODO: I can reach the shelves and get out.

VEX: The shot has fewer surprises.

TAVI: The door is the surprise. It was behind a wall.

Rumi asks Odo what he likes best. He points to the shelf where he keeps his morning cup. The show does not claim the room is larger; the path simply works better. Kiri shows the locked brakes on the flat and the clipped cable route for the crew.

KIRI: The panel stays here, the door stays clear, and nobody has to squeeze behind the sofa.

VEX: Can I add a fake door to the panel for symmetry?

ODO: No.

RUMI: The design vote is unanimous.

The reveal music plays once. Odo steps through the real doorway carrying his cup. The camera stays inside on the open path. Vex quietly lowers the spare reveal curtain he has been holding behind the sofa.

FINAL IMAGE: The scenic wall frames the window while the actual door stands open beside a clear walkway.
