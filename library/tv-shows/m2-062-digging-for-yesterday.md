# Digging for Yesterday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: archaeology documentaries, sensational artifact reveals, and expert disputes over fragments*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: An archaeology crew searches an ancient grove for a ceremonial bowl, while every artifact they uncover turns out to belong to the present-day snack tent.

Cast: Host Edda, a lemur; field archaeologist Tolo, a baboon; conservator Nima, a macaque; local guide Boro, a gorilla; and student digger Piki, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Vessel Beneath the Tent"

### Cold open

EXT. GROVE DIG SITE, MORNING

[A shovel uncovers a round wooden rim. Edda leans into camera.]

EDDA: We may have found the ceremonial vessel of the first grove council.

BORO: That is the snack tent table.

TOLO: The table is beneath the soil.

NIMA: It is still attached to the tent.

PIKI: I can see a leg.

EDDA: A remarkable clue.

BORO: It is the same table we ate on yesterday.

EDDA: History is closer than we think.

### Act One

EXT. DIG SITE

[Tolo brushes dirt from the table leg. Piki uses a small brush on a piece of cloth.]

TOLO: This layer is from several seasons ago.

BORO: The tent was moved last month.

TOLO: The ground can preserve recent history.

NIMA: The artifact has a fresh scratch.

TOLO: A ceremonial mark?

NIMA: Someone dragged it.

PIKI: I dragged it to this spot.

EDDA: The student has confessed to moving the artifact.

PIKI: I thought it was a table.

EDDA: It is also a table.

TOLO: We need to document the find before we disturb it.

BORO: Can we take our lunch off it?

NIMA: Not until we finish the documentation.

BORO: It is lunchtime.

EDDA: This is the tension of archaeology.

### Act Two

EXT. SECOND TEST PIT

[The crew digs beside a large root. Piki finds a small metal spoon.]

PIKI: I found an ancient tool.

TOLO: What material?

PIKI: Metal.

NIMA: It is a spoon.

EDDA: A ritual spoon?

BORO: It is from the snack tent.

TOLO: How can you tell?

BORO: It has a sticker with the snack tent's number.

EDDA: The ancients numbered their spoons?

NIMA: The sticker says "Property of Boro."

BORO: That is my name.

TOLO: This might be a replica.

BORO: It was in my cup yesterday.

EDDA: A connection between past and present.

NIMA: Or between the spoon and the cup.

[The crew uncovers a small ceramic shard. It has a blue stripe.]

TOLO: Now this may be ancient.

BORO: It looks like the mug I dropped.

NIMA: The mug was dropped this morning.

TOLO: We are excavating today's events.

EDDA: The past is still forming.

### Act Three

EXT. DIG SITE, AFTERNOON

[The crew maps the finds: table, spoon, mug shard, and a small paper napkin.]

TOLO: We have evidence of a communal meal.

BORO: We had lunch here.

EDDA: We have confirmed the site was used for gathering.

NIMA: We have also confirmed that the snack tent needs better storage.

PIKI: The napkin has a note on it.

EDDA: What does it say?

PIKI: "Move the table before digging."

TOLO: That is an instruction.

BORO: I wrote it yesterday.

EDDA: The note has survived the excavation.

NIMA: We should place the artifacts in a display case.

TOLO: The table is too large.

BORO: We could put it back at the snack tent.

EDDA: The most important discovery is often practical.

TOLO: We have not found the ceremonial bowl.

PIKI: We have a ceramic shard.

NIMA: From the mug.

TOLO: The shard may still tell us how people drank.

BORO: From a mug.

EDDA: A humble vessel, captured in time.

[They carry the table back. The napkin flutters off and lands on the marked dig.]

### The deeper layer

EXT. TEST PIT, AFTERNOON

[Tolo marks a layer on the dig wall. A strip of plastic twine appears.]

TOLO: The layer changes here.

NIMA: The twine is modern.

EDDA: It may have been carried by the roots.

BORO: I tied the tent with that twine.

PIKI: The table is connected to the tent.

TOLO: Then the artifact belongs to a temporary structure.

EDDA: We cannot dismiss it too quickly. It has been underground.

BORO: It went under the soil when we moved the tent.

NIMA: The rain washed dirt over it.

Tolo: Could the rain have preserved the table?

NIMA: It could have covered the table.

EDDA: That is still a story about the past.

[The crew uncovers a plastic chair leg.]

PIKI: I found another object.

BORO: That's my chair.

TOLO: The site has a complete seating arrangement.

EDDA: Perhaps an ancient gathering.

BORO: Or yesterday's lunch.

NIMA: The chair has a fresh banana stain.

EDDA: A feast may have taken place.

TOLO: We should test it.

[He touches the stain. It smears.]

TOLO: The feast was recent.

EDDA: History has a short timeline.

### Tag

EXT. DIG SITE, SUNSET

[Edda narrates over the empty trench. Boro sets a basket of snacks on the table.]

EDDA: The team leaves behind a question: what else lies beneath?

BORO: The tablecloth.

[Tolo begins to dig.]
