# Last Place, Best Season

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports documentary series that turns a losing youth team into a prestige comeback narrative*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A documentary crew follows the Fern Hollow seedball team through its final match. The team is last in the league, but the coach cares more about safe play and shared snacks than the rankings.

Cast: Coach Miri, a macaque; captain Odo, a capuchin; player Tess, a gibbon; commentator Venn, a lemur; and documentary producer Rell, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Game They Finished"

### Cold Open

[AN EMPTY FIELD AT SUNSET. A TROPHY CASE HOLDS NO TROPHIES. MIRI SETS A BOWL OF SEEDBALLS ON THE BENCH.] 

RELL: This season began with a question: can the last-place seedball team rise above its record?

MIRI: We can practice tomorrow.

RELL: The final match is tonight.

MIRI: Then we can play tonight.

[THE TEAM ARRIVES, CARRYING A WATER BOWL AND A BAG OF FRUIT.] 

RELL: They do not look like a team preparing for a comeback.

ODO: We are waiting for the field to dry.

TESS: The west side is muddy.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON THE MUD.] 

RELL: A challenge is forming.

### Act One

[THE TEAM HUDDLES NEAR THE SIDELINE. THE COMMENTATOR STANDS BEFORE A GRAPHIC: 0 WINS, 7 LOSSES.] 

VENN: Seven matches, seven defeats. Tonight they face the undefeated Canopy Comets.

RELL: Coach, what is your strategy?

MIRI: Keep the ball low, watch your footing, and pass to the teammate who is ready.

RELL: Are you changing your lineup?

MIRI: No. Everyone who came to practice plays.

ODO: I cannot throw far with my left arm.

MIRI: Then use your right. We will stand closer.

TESS: The Comets have a tall striker.

MIRI: We can let the ball roll around him.

[THE COMETS ENTER IN MATCHING JACKETS. THEIR CAPTAIN PRACTICES A HIGH THROW.] 

RELL: The opposition looks focused.

VENN: They are warming up with a coordinated chant.

COMETS: Win the point! Win the point!

[THE FERN HOLLOW TEAM CHEWS FRUIT.] 

RELL: Our side is eating.

MIRI: We eat before we play.

### Act Two

[THE REFEREE SIGNALS THE START. THE BALL ROLLS ACROSS THE FIELD. ODO PASSES TO TESS, WHO TAPS IT BACK.] 

VENN: The Comets intercept and throw. The ball lands beside the muddy patch.

TESS: Stop. The field is too slippery there.

[THE REFEREE BLOWS A WHISTLE. THE CAMERA CREW RUSHES TOWARD THE MUD.] 

RELL: A possible turning point.

MIRI: The game is paused. Let the groundskeeper mark the edge.

COMETS CAPTAIN: We could play around it.

ODO: The sideline is safe. We can shift the boundary.

MIRI: We will ask the referee.

[THE REFEREE MOVES TWO CONES, MAKING A NARROWER FIELD.] 

VENN: The conditions have changed. The score remains zero-zero.

RELL: Coach, the team may lose the advantage.

MIRI: We did not have an advantage. We have a safe field now.

[THE GAME RESUMES. A COMETS PLAYER SLIPS ON A DRY LEAF, CATCHES THEMSELF, AND LAUGHS.] 

TESS: Is everyone okay?

COMETS PLAYER: Yes. The leaf surprised me.

ODO: We can move the loose leaves off the sideline during the next break.

MIRI: Do not leave the field unattended. Ask the referee.

[THE REFEREE CALLS A WATER BREAK. PLAYERS CLEAR LEAVES TOGETHER.]

[DURING THE BREAK, RELL ASKS EACH PLAYER TO NAME THE MOMENT THEY KNEW THE SEASON COULD CHANGE.]

RELL: Odo?

ODO: When Tess said the west side was muddy. We changed the boundary before somebody fell.

RELL: That is not usually the turning point in a sports documentary.

ODO: It changed where we could pass.

RELL: Tess?

TESS: When the Comets helped clear the leaves. They saw the same slippery spot.

[RELL GLANCES AT A PRODUCER, WHO HOLDS UP A CARD: "SAY HEART." ]

RELL: Could you describe the heart of the team?

TESS: We share fruit after practice.

MIRI: And we wash the bowls before we leave.

[ODO LIFTS THE WATER BOWL. A SEEDBALL IS STUCK TO ITS BASE. HE PEELS IT OFF AND HANDS IT TO THE REFEREE.]

REFEREE: That is equipment, not a snack.

RELL: The camera will follow the bowl.

MIRI: The camera can help carry it.

[RELL TAKES THE BOWL. THE PRODUCER'S CARD DROPS INTO IT, FACE DOWN.]

### Act Three

[THE FINAL MINUTE. THE SCORE IS 1-1. A SEEDBALL ROLLS TOWARD ODO.] 

VENN: Odo has the ball. He can take the shot.

ODO: Tess is open.

[TESS SCORES. THE REFEREE BLOWS THE FINAL WHISTLE.] 

VENN: Fern Hollow wins two-one.

RELL: We have the comeback.

MIRI: We finished the match.

RELL: The camera caught the winning point.

MIRI: It also caught everyone checking the field and sharing water.

TESS: We can put the muddy cones back when it dries.

COMETS CAPTAIN: We can help.

RELL: The story began with a losing record and ends with a win.

ODO: We lost seven times before this.

MIRI: We will practice tomorrow either way.

[THE TEAM AND THE COMETS SHARE FRUIT BESIDE THE FIELD. THE CAMERA PULLS BACK, LEAVING THE TROPHY CASE EMPTY.]
