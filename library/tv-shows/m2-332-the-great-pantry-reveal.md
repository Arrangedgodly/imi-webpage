# The Great Pantry Reveal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation reality shows, where every shelf becomes an emotional reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A renovation crew rebuilds a pantry for a family of monkeys, but the host keeps delaying the reveal while the residents need to store food.

Cast: Host Piri, a capuchin; carpenter Odo, a baboon; resident Nima, a gibbon; resident Kito, a macaque; camera assistant Seli, a lemur.

Episode: "Open Concept Acorns"

COLD OPEN

INT. PANTRY - DAY

Piri stands beside an empty shelf. Nima carries a basket of acorns.

PIRI: Today, this pantry gets an open-concept transformation.

KITO: It has no door.

PIRI: It will have a reveal.

Odo knocks on the wall. A small shelf falls forward. Nima catches it, then puts the acorns on the floor.

NIMA: The reveal can happen after lunch.

ACT ONE

INT. WORKROOM - LATER

Odo shows the plan. The pantry will have three shelves, a clear label system, and a dramatic sliding ladder. Kito asks if the ladder is necessary. Piri says it gives the room aspiration.

Odo measures the wall. The pantry is narrow. The ladder blocks the entrance when open. Nima suggests using lower shelves so every family member can reach them. Piri asks if the camera can get a wide shot.

SELI: Not unless we remove the wall.

KITO: Please do not remove the wall.

Piri says they will keep the wall and build a tall cabinet. Nima says she needs to reach the food without climbing. Odo adjusts the shelf heights. Piri asks for an emotional before shot. The residents stand outside the pantry and describe how the fallen shelf interrupted breakfast.

KITO: I put the basket on the floor.

NIMA: I caught the shelf.

PIRI: And how did it feel?

NIMA: Heavy.

ACT TWO

Odo installs the shelves. Piri asks Kito to choose a finish. Kito picks plain wood because it is easy to clean. Piri suggests a special stain called "Harvest Glow." Nima says the pantry is dark even with the light on.

Odo adds a small lamp. It is powered by a switch near the doorway. Seli checks the camera and finds that the light reflects off the shelf labels. Odo angles the lamp down. Now it lights the baskets but not the floor. Nima asks for a simple ceiling bulb. Odo installs one.

Piri introduces the sliding ladder. Odo shows it rolling along the wall. When he pushes it, the ladder blocks the pantry entrance and pins the food basket inside.

KITO: The ladder has trapped lunch.

Odo removes the ladder. Piri says the scene lacks a transformation moment. Nima carries the basket out through the doorway after Odo moves the ladder. The crew agrees the ladder is unnecessary.

ACT TWO-B

INT. PANTRY - AFTERNOON

The shelves are set at three heights. The lowest shelf holds snacks, the middle shelf holds family meals, and the upper shelf holds empty jars. Kito tests each level. He can reach all three except the highest.

KITO: We need a step stool.

Odo builds a short sturdy stool and places it near the cabinet. Nima tries it. It wobbles because one leg rests on a floor seam. Odo adds a felt pad under the leg. It stabilizes.

Piri asks the residents to stand back while the reveal is filmed. Nima says the food cannot stay on the floor much longer. Piri says they need a final sweep shot. Kito asks if they can move the basket back in before the camera pans. Piri agrees, but asks them to carry it slowly for atmosphere.

Piri prepares to film the final walk-through, but the crew discovers the step stool has been moved to the pantry doorway. Kito says he used it to reach the upper shelf. He puts it back beside the cabinet. Nima asks if the camera can show the low shelf first, since that is where breakfast lives. Piri agrees and films the family taking food without climbing. Odo tightens the final screw. The ladder is left outside as a prop for the "before" shot, but Nima uses it to reach a fruit branch on the path. Piri follows with the camera, then calls her back for the reveal. Nima says the pantry is finished; the fruit is not part of the renovation.

ACT THREE

The pantry is stocked with labeled baskets. Nima places acorns on the low shelf. Kito stores the breakfast jars in the middle. Odo shows the step stool beside the upper shelf. Piri turns the camera toward the room.

PIRI: From a crowded, unsafe corner to a calm, organized space.

A basket tips from the low shelf. Nima catches it, then puts it back with heavier items at the bottom. The pantry stays orderly.

NIMA: Better.

KITO: I can reach breakfast.

Odo checks the shelf brackets and tightens one screw. The renovation is complete without removing the wall or adding a ladder.

TAG

Piri asks the family to stand in the doorway for one final shot.

KITO: We are using the pantry.

Nima opens the cabinet and takes an acorn.

PIRI: The reveal is live.

NIMA: The snack is also live.
