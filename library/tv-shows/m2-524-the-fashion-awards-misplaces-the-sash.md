# The Fashion Awards Misplaces the Sash

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fashion awards that build a red-carpet climax around an accessory nobody can find*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Suma, a macaque; designer Rava, a gibbon; presenter Odo, an orangutan; stage manager Peli, a lemur; producer Neri, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. AWARDS STAGE - NIGHT

Odo opens a case. It is empty.

ODO: And the award goes to...

Suma holds up a card reading BEST SASH.

ACT ONE

INT. STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Peli says the sash was sent to the dressing room. Rava says she wore it during rehearsal. Neri asks for a suspense shot. The camera zooms into the empty case. Suma announces the nominees while Odo searches behind the podium. The award itself is present; only the sash is missing.

RAVA: The garment is in the wardrobe.

Neri asks if they can award the sash separately. Peli says the category is best accessory. Odo lifts the award, which is shaped like a ribbon. The camera plays a triumphant sting. The audience claps before the presenter reads the winner.

ACT TWO

INT. DRESSING ROOM - LATER

Peli and Rava check the garment rack. The sash is not there. Neri asks the nominees to describe it. One says blue; another says silver. The wardrobe assistant brings a blue ribbon from a costume box. It is a measuring tape. Suma holds it up to camera, mistaking it for the missing sash. The audience applauds again.

[Odo returns to the stage with a garment bag. He unzips it; a long red sash is tied around the hanger to mark the rack. It is not the award sash.]

Peli checks the wardrobe list. The award sash was used as a length guide during alterations and is clipped to the sewing table. Rava says she can recognize it by a repaired hem. They return to the workroom. The camera crew follows, but the live feed remains on the empty stage. Suma continues narrating the search while the screen shows Odo's empty chair.

INT. WORKROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Rava finds the sash beneath a stack of fabric. The repaired hem is visible. Neri asks her not to put it on until the winner is announced. Rava hands it to Peli, who carries it carefully back to the stage. Odo follows with the award.

INT. WARDROBE WORKROOM - LATER

Rava compares the repaired sash with the sketches. The design includes a small stitched notch near the hem. Peli checks it against the award entry. The sash is the design sample, not the stage-worn piece. Neri asks whether it should be presented as a replica. Rava says it is the original sample and belongs to the collection. Odo removes the sample from the show rack and places it in a garment sleeve.

Suma asks for a close-up of the notch. The camera finds it, then tilts toward the measuring tape still on the table. Peli moves the tape to the tool box. Odo says the accessory's shape is simple enough to show without a dramatic zoom. The crew sets the award beside the labeled sleeve. Neri takes down the red sash that was tied to the wrong hanger. The workroom list now identifies the award, sample and costume piece separately. Rava initials the correct line. Neri places the sample sleeve beside the award record. Suma removes the wrong red sash from the camera frame. Odo checks the case lock. Rava confirms the wardrobe sample is back on its hanger. Peli checks the award's label before the crew packs it.

ACT THREE

INT. AWARDS STAGE - NIGHT

Suma opens the correct envelope. Rava wins the accessory award for the sash design. Odo gives her the award. Peli brings the recovered sash, but Rava says it belongs to the wardrobe department. The winner accepts the trophy without wearing the missing accessory.

Neri asks for a final carpet walk. Rava carries the sash over her arm. The camera follows the repaired hem. At the stage edge, a thread catches on the case. Peli frees it without pulling. The sash is folded and placed back in the case. The award stays with Rava.

Suma asks whether the segment can have a final reveal. Odo opens the case to show it is empty again. Rava holds up the trophy beside the correctly tagged garment bag. The audience applauds after the moment, not before.

TAG

INT. WORKROOM - AFTER THE SHOW

Peli pins a label to the sash: WARDROBE PROPERTY. Neri removes the measuring tape from the awards tray. Suma reads the category card correctly.

FINAL IMAGE: The trophy stands on stage while the sash rests folded on the wardrobe table, its repaired hem facing up.
