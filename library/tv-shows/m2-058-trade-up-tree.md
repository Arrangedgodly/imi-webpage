# Trade Up the Tree

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: barter reality shows, escalating trades, and dramatic deal reveals*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A barter host starts with one acorn and tries to trade up to a sturdy footbridge for the community, but every trader wants the bridge for a different reason.

Cast: Host Toma, a gibbon; trader Sella, a macaque; trader Goro, a baboon; builder Nix, a gorilla; and appraiser Miri, a capuchin.

## Episode: "One Acorn to a Bridge"

### Cold open

EXT. MARKET CLEARING, DAY

[Toma holds up a single acorn. A camera zooms close.]

TOMA: We start with one acorn and end with a footbridge.

MIRI: That is a large jump.

TOMA: It is a trade-up.

SELLA: I will trade a rope for the acorn.

TOMA: How long is the rope?

SELLA: Long enough to be useful.

GORO: I will trade a basket.

TOMA: Is the basket useful?

GORO: It can hold an acorn.

NIX: The acorn already does that.

MIRI: The first deal is tied.

### Act One

EXT. MARKET CLEARING

[Toma places the acorn on a leaf. Sella offers a rope. Goro offers a basket.]

TOMA: We have a choice between rope and basket.

MIRI: The rope can help build the bridge.

NIX: The basket can carry bridge parts.

GORO: It can also carry snacks.

SELLA: The rope can also carry snacks.

TOMA: Does the rope have a handle?

SELLA: It is a rope.

[Toma trades the acorn for the rope. Sella hands it over.]

TOMA: We have traded up to a rope.

MIRI: The rope has a knot.

NIX: It is a loop.

TOMA: The loop is a feature.

[They carry the rope to Goro, who offers a sturdy basket.]

GORO: I will trade this basket for the rope.

TOMA: What is the basket made of?

GORO: Woven branches.

NIX: The basket could become part of the bridge railing.

MIRI: It could also hold the rope.

TOMA: We will trade.

[They exchange the rope for the basket. The basket has a hole.]

MIRI: The basket leaks.

GORO: It is a ventilation hole.

NIX: The bridge needs a gap for water to pass.

TOMA: We have traded to a basket with a purpose.

### Act Two

EXT. RIVER EDGE

[Nix examines the basket. A few branches are missing.]

NIX: I can use it as a model for the bridge railing.

TOMA: What will you trade for it?

NIX: I can offer two bridge planks.

MIRI: Those are already part of the bridge.

NIX: They are not attached yet.

SELLA: I need the basket for my market stall.

TOMA: What can you trade for the planks?

SELLA: A sign.

TOMA: What does it say?

SELLA: "Bridge."

MIRI: That is a sign for the bridge.

NIX: We already know where the bridge is.

TOMA: The show requires a trade.

MIRI: It requires a useful trade.

[They look at the river. A small footbridge is half-built, with two planks resting on the banks.]

SELLA: I can trade the sign for a bucket.

GORO: I have a bucket.

TOMA: What do you want?

GORO: The basket.

SELLA: I want the sign.

MIRI: We have a cycle.

TOMA: We can trade the basket for two planks, the planks for a sign, the sign for a bucket, and the bucket for the basket.

NIX: We will end where we started.

TOMA: But with more camera angles.

### Act Three

EXT. RIVER EDGE, SUNSET

[Nix lays the two planks across the narrowest part of the river. The crew tests them carefully.]

NIX: The bridge is stable.

TOMA: What did we trade?

MIRI: The acorn became a rope, then a basket, then the idea of planks.

SELLA: The basket is still useful at my stall.

GORO: I will trade it for the rope.

TOMA: The trade-up is complete.

NIX: We did not trade the basket for a bridge.

MIRI: We used the basket to plan the railing.

TOMA: The show is about value.

SELLA: The bridge is valuable because it lets people cross.

GORO: The basket is valuable because it carries fruit.

NIX: The rope is valuable because it holds things together.

MIRI: And the acorn?

TOMA: It started the episode.

SELLA: It can grow a tree.

TOMA: That is a bigger trade-up.

[They place the acorn beside the bridge. It rolls toward the water. Goro catches it in the basket.]

GORO: The basket has found its purpose.

NIX: It can carry the acorn to the other side.

TOMA: We have our final trade.

MIRI: What did we trade?

TOMA: An acorn for a crossing.

SELLA: We still have the acorn.

TOMA: Then the trade is a success.

### Tag

EXT. RIVER, EVENING

[The group crosses the new footbridge. Goro carries the basket; the acorn rests inside.]

MIRI: The basket is part of the bridge now.

GORO: It is not attached.

[He steadies the basket as the group steps onto the far bank.]
