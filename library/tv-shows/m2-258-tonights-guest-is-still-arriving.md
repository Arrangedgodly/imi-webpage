# Tonight's Guest Is Still Arriving

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows, where the host keeps filling airtime while a slow-moving guest makes a careful entrance*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Host Odo tries to keep a live talk show moving while guest Tavi, a slow loris, makes his way across the stage to the chair.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; guest Tavi, slow loris; bandleader Piri, capuchin; announcer Nara, macaque; floor manager Seli, gibbon.

## Episode: "The Long Walk On"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. ODO SITS AT THE DESK. THE GUEST CHAIR IS EMPTY.]

NARA: Please welcome Tavi.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. TAVI ENTERS FROM THE BACK OF THE STUDIO.]

ODO: He's on his way.

PIRI: He is already on the stage.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE BAND PLAYS A SHORT FANFARE.]

Tavi moves slowly toward the guest chair. Odo starts the interview from his desk.

ODO: Welcome, Tavi. How are you tonight?

TAVI: I am well.

ODO: What do you think of the studio?

TAVI: I can see the desk.

Seli gestures for Odo to fill the time. Odo tells a joke about the band, then asks Piri for a musical interlude. The band plays eight bars. Tavi reaches the couch.

ODO: We have made it to the first conversation.

TAVI: I have not sat down yet.

Nara tries another applause cue. The audience claps; Tavi pauses to listen.

ODO: The applause has interrupted the guest.

TAVI: I heard it.

### Act Two

[ODO LEAVES THE DESK AND WALKS TOWARD TAVI.]

Odo asks if Tavi would like help. Tavi says he is fine. Odo offers a hand; Tavi declines it and moves one step closer to the chair.

ODO: We can take a commercial break.

SELI: We have already taken two.

NARA: The audience has been clapping between them.

Piri plays a longer melody. Tavi moves in time with the music, but the band speeds up. Tavi stops.

PIRI: I was following you.

TAVI: You were playing faster.

ODO: Let's ask the audience to keep time.

Nara claps slowly. The crowd follows. Tavi takes another step. Odo asks him what his new project is.

TAVI: A small bridge.

ODO: Is it nearly finished?

TAVI: I have collected the wood.

ODO: That's a start.

TAVI: It is a careful start.

Seli tells Odo not to rush the guest. Odo asks about the bridge's location. Tavi says near a stream. Odo asks if it can be built before the show's end.

TAVI: Not if I stop to answer.

[ODO PULLS A QUESTION CARD FROM THE DESK.]

ODO: What is the most difficult part of building a bridge?

Tavi considers while shifting one hand on the chair.

TAVI: Getting the first plank to stay in place.

ODO: And the second?

TAVI: It rests on the first.

Seli signals that there are two minutes left. Odo begins another question, but the audience applauds again. Tavi waits until the clapping ends.

ODO: You could have answered over them.

TAVI: I could not hear the question.

Piri plays a short chord to mark the pause. Tavi smiles and taps the armrest in time.

PIRI: The guest has joined the band.

TAVI: The band has joined the waiting.

Odo asks if Tavi would return to the show. Tavi says only if the chair stays in the same place and the applause is shorter.

### Act Three

[TAVI REACHES THE CHAIR.]

The audience applauds. Tavi pauses, then sits. Odo asks him to tell the story of the bridge. Tavi explains that the stream is narrow, but the stones are slippery. He wants the bridge to give smaller animals a dry crossing.

ODO: What is your deadline?

TAVI: When the bridge is ready.

NARA: That is not a date.

TAVI: It is a condition.

Odo asks whether the band can help carry wood. Piri volunteers. Seli says the band has instruments to pack.

PIRI: We can carry them and the wood.

TAVI: One trip at a time.

Odo tries to set up a dramatic reveal. He asks Tavi to show a sketch, but the paper is folded into a tiny square. Tavi opens it slowly. It is a simple drawing of two planks and a handrail.

ODO: It is not a grand bridge.

TAVI: It crosses the stream.

The audience applauds. Tavi takes a breath and folds the paper again.

### Tag

[ODO RETURNS TO HIS DESK.]

ODO: We have had our guest.

TAVI: I am still here.

ODO: Stay as long as you like.

Seli signals the show is out of time. The band plays the closing theme. Tavi stands and begins the slow walk back across the stage.

NARA: He is leaving.

PIRI: He is on his way.

[SFX: BAND THEME FADES, APPLAUSE CONTINUES.]
