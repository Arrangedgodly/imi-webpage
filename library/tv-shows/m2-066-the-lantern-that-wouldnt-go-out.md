# The Lantern That Wouldn't Go Out

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: anthology horror television, ominous narration, and twist endings*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A night watchman inherits a lantern that supposedly reveals a visitor's greatest fear, but the only figure appearing in its light is the watchman carrying it.

Cast: Watcher Oru, a macaque; lantern keeper Vess, a gibbon; night visitor Mina, a lemur; narrator Tavo, an orangutan; and groundskeeper Kett, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Light in the Shed"

### Cold open

INT. OLD TOOL SHED, NIGHT

[A lantern burns on a table. Oru opens the shed door. A tall shadow stretches across the wall.]

TAVO: Some lights show the way. Others show what waits.

ORU: Who is that?

VESS: It is your shadow.

ORU: It is taller than I am.

VESS: The lantern is low.

[Oru lifts the lantern. The shadow shrinks.]

TAVO: The light has changed the shape of the fear.

KETT: The light has changed the shape of the shadow.

### Act One

INT. TOOL SHED

[Vess hands Oru the lantern. Its glass is smudged.]

VESS: Keep the wick trimmed.

ORU: What happens if I don't?

VESS: It smokes.

TAVO: It reveals a hidden image.

KETT: It needs cleaning.

ORU: Is it haunted?

VESS: It belongs to the night watch.

ORU: That isn't an answer.

VESS: It is the only answer I have.

[Mina arrives, holding a bucket.]

MINA: I heard someone calling from the shed.

ORU: I didn't call.

MINA: The lantern makes a voice.

KETT: The wind is blowing through the vent.

TAVO: A whisper from within the walls.

VESS: The vent is open.

[Oru closes the vent. The sound stops.]

ORU: The lantern is quiet now.

TAVO: The silence is more unsettling.

### Act Two

EXT. PATH TO THE SHED

[Oru walks with the lantern. A shadow moves behind the trees.]

ORU: Someone is following us.

MINA: It is moving when you move.

KETT: That is because it is your shadow.

ORU: What about the shape over there?

[The group turns. A large figure stands against the shed wall.]

TAVO: The visitor has arrived.

VESS: That is the raincoat on the peg.

MINA: It looks like a tall baboon.

KETT: I am a baboon.

MINA: It looks like two of you.

[Kett removes the coat. The shadow splits in two.]

ORU: The lantern has shown us a double.

KETT: The coat has two sleeves.

TAVO: Every clue has an ordinary explanation.

VESS: That does not make the dark less dark.

[The lantern flickers. A second light appears outside.]

ORU: There is another lantern.

MINA: The light is coming from the water bucket.

KETT: The glass is reflecting our flame.

TAVO: The reflection watches silently.

ORU: I can see myself.

VESS: You are holding the lantern.

ORU: I thought it would show my fear.

KETT: It shows your face.

ORU: My face is afraid.

### The keeper's account

INT. TOOL SHED, LATER

[Vess opens a small drawer. Inside are several trimmed wicks and a notebook.]

VESS: My teacher kept a log of every lantern problem.

ORU: What did the last entry say?

VESS: "Clean the glass. Close the vent. Move the raincoat."

KETT: That sounds familiar.

TAVO: The old keeper knew of the haunting.

MINA: The old keeper knew the shed.

[Oru reads the next line.]

ORU: "If the lantern flickers, trim the wick."

VESS: He wrote that every night.

ORU: Did it ever reveal anything?

VESS: It revealed soot.

TAVO: The log offers no comfort.

KETT: It offers instructions.

[The lantern flickers again. Vess trims the wick. The light steadies.]

MINA: The shadow is gone.

TAVO: The watcher is alone.

ORU: I am not alone. You are all standing here.

KETT: We are also holding the door open.

### Act Three

INT. TOOL SHED, LATER

[The lantern suddenly goes out. The room is dark. Oru freezes.]

ORU: The light is gone.

MINA: The wick has slipped.

TAVO: The darkness closes in.

VESS: I have a match.

KETT: We are outside the shed. The door is open.

ORU: The lantern won't go out.

VESS: It has gone out.

[Oru strikes a match. The lantern relights. A large shadow fills the wall.]

TAVO: The watcher confronts its final terror.

ORU: It is the coat again.

KETT: I took it off the hook.

[The coat falls. Oru jumps back. The match goes out.]

MINA: You are afraid of a coat.

ORU: I am afraid of what I cannot see.

VESS: Then look where the light falls.

[Oru raises the lantern. The wall is empty.]

KETT: The coat is on the floor.

TAVO: The darkness returns.

ORU: The lantern is back on.

VESS: It needs a new wick.

ORU: I will trim it before the next watch.

### Tag

EXT. SHED, DAWN

[Oru sets the lantern on the table. The sunrise shines through the open door.]

TAVO: The night keeps its secrets.

KETT: The day shows the shed.

[The lantern's shadow is short and ordinary.]
