# Case of the Squeaky Gate

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: police procedural*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: Two patient city officers investigate a string of early-morning gate openings that have been waking a neighborhood, only to discover the mystery has no criminal and a very determined goat.

Cast: Officer Mina, a macaque who notices small details; Officer Odo, a baboon who knows the local routes; Sergeant Pell, a no-nonsense langur; Mr. Pru, a retired goat herder; and Lilt, a young goat with a talent for latches.

## Episode: "Three Clicks Before Sunrise"

### Cold open

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, DAWN

A gate opens with a shriek. A row of sleeping birds lifts from the fence. Officer Odo arrives and finds a cabbage leaf pinned beneath the latch.

ODO: The suspect left a message.

Mina sniffs it.

MINA: The suspect left breakfast.

### Act one

Residents have filed four noise complaints about the garden gate opening before sunrise. No tools are missing and no damage has been found. Officer Mina interviews the neighbors. One heard three clicks, then the squeak. Another heard a bell. A third says the gate opened on its own. Sergeant Pell asks the officers to determine whether the gate is being tampered with or simply needs repair.

Odo checks the lock. It catches twice, then releases if the handle is lifted. The hinge squeaks because rain washed oil from the pin. The cabbage leaf is fresh, with tooth marks. Mina finds small hoof prints on the damp path, leading to a gap in the fence.

At the station, Pell reviews the reports. A delivery driver says he saw a shadow near the gate. The officers find that the shadow was a tree branch moving in the dawn wind. Odo wants to file a noise citation against the branch. Pell says the department does not cite trees.

### Act two

Mina and Odo set up a quiet watch from the garden shed. They see a small goat enter through the fence gap. It reaches the gate, lifts the handle with its nose, and pushes through. The gate makes three clicks and a squeal. The goat heads straight for the cabbage patch.

The officers follow a bell to Lilt, who eats a leaf and nudges the gate shut. Her owner, Mr. Pru, says she escaped through storm damage.

A resident appears, angry that the officers have not arrested anyone. Mina explains that Lilt is not a person and has not damaged property. Odo says the gate does need repair, and the department can help contact the garden committee. The resident says the complaints were about sleep, not theft. Sergeant Pell asks whether the officers can resolve a noise problem without pretending it is a crime.

Mr. Pru offers to bring a panel to close the fence gap. The garden committee has a maintenance kit, but its key is kept by a volunteer who works nights. The officers call the volunteer and arrange a handoff. Mina makes a temporary marker so the morning gardeners can see the loose latch. Odo removes the cabbage from the hinge.


[At the garden, Mina watches the gate. A goat noses the latch.]
MINA: There is our early caller.
ODO: It has no badge.
LILT: Maaa.

### Act three

The committee meets at the garden that afternoon. They repair the fence and replace the hinge pin with a quieter one. Lilt watches from Pru's side of the fence. She noses at the gate handle, but it no longer lifts easily. Pru fits a simple latch guard, then tests it himself to make sure residents can still open it.

The neighbors share a number for maintenance reports. Pell says the city will handle future repairs, not patrol every hinge. Odo writes the final report: no theft, no trespass charge, gate repaired, goat returned to owner. Mina adds that the cabbage patch lost one leaf and gained a story.

Pru thanks the officers. Lilt steps toward him, then slips through a gap in the temporary panel. Odo catches her before she reaches the road. The garden committee realizes the panel was measured to keep a dog out, not a goat. They add a lower board. Lilt tests it once, finds no opening, and returns to Pru's yard.

The officers leave as the gardeners begin their morning work. The gate opens quietly. A bird lands on it and stays. Sergeant Pell hears the silence over the radio and asks if the case is closed. Mina says the gate is quiet. Odo says the goat is home. Pell marks both items resolved.

### Tag

At the station, Odo hears a squeak in the hallway. He stops, investigates, and finds the breakroom cabinet door.

MINA: Should we call maintenance?

ODO: We are maintenance today.

He oils the hinge. The cabinet opens without a sound. Inside, someone has left a cabbage leaf on his lunch plate.
