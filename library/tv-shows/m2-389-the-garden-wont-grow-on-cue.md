# The Garden Won't Grow on Cue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: gardening television, before-and-after edits, fast-growth time lapses, and hosts who demand instant results*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A gardening show films a seedbed before lunch and promises a fast reveal, but its host must wait for the actual growing season.

Cast: Host Meli, a gibbon; gardener Toko, a macaque; camera operator Savi, a capuchin; editor Pavo, a baboon; caller Reni, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

Meli holds a packet of seeds above a patch of soil.

MELI: By the end of this segment, we will have a garden.

TOKO: We will have a planted bed.

PAVO (IN EARPIECE): We need a before and after.

SAVI: The after takes three weeks.

ACT ONE

Toko marks rows in the soil. Meli asks for the quickest-growing seed. Toko chooses a local leafy green and reads the planting instructions. Meli asks if they can shorten the spacing to make the bed look full.

TOKO: They need room to grow.

MELI: Can we film from a low angle?

TOKO: You can film the soil.

Savi adjusts the camera. Meli pours seeds into the first row. Toko stops her before the packet empties.

TOKO: A pinch per space, not the whole packet.

MELI: We have avoided a seed emergency.

Toko covers the seeds, waters the bed lightly and places a small label at the row. Meli looks for a sign of growth. Savi shows her the camera monitor: brown soil, a label and Meli's shoes.

MELI: The transformation is subtle.

ACT TWO

Pavo asks for a time-lapse. Savi sets a camera to record the bed. Toko explains the watering schedule and the need to keep the path clear. Meli asks if she can place a decorative bench in front of the bed for the reveal. Toko says the bench would block the watering can.

MELI: Could we move it after the reveal?

TOKO: The reveal would be behind the bench.

Reni calls from home. She says she planted the same seeds two days ago and sees nothing. Meli starts to reassure her that the garden may appear by the end of the show. Toko says germination takes time and depends on conditions. Reni asks whether she should dig up a seed to check.

TOKO: Leave the row undisturbed and follow the packet's watering guidance.

MELI: Can we show a calendar graphic?

PAVO: It will take the whole screen.

Savi puts up a calendar. Meli circles three weeks, then asks Pavo to speed it up in post. Pavo fast-forwards the image; the plants remain the same height because the time-lapse camera has only been recording for ten minutes.

ACT TWO-B

The crew returns three weeks later. The bed has sprouted, but several seedlings are sparse. Toko checks the soil and says a bird took some seeds from the edge. Meli asks whether the crew can fill the gaps with more seed before filming. Toko says they can reseed the bare spaces, label the new row, and show which plants came up first.

TOKO: We should not pretend they all grew at once.

PAVO: The edit can show a split screen.

MELI: What if we call this the first harvest?

TOKO: The plants are still small.

Reni calls again. Her seeds sprouted on the same week. She holds a tray to the camera and says she thought the show would make the garden grow faster. Meli apologizes for the promise. Toko explains the crew did not speed the plants; only the video.

RENI: That explains the calendar.

MELI: We can film your tray next time, with your permission.

RENI: After I water it.

ACT THREE

Toko reseeds the sparse spaces and adds a fresh label. Meli announces the bed is growing, not finished. Savi captures a wide shot of the first leaves and the neighboring beds. Pavo offers a split-screen of the before and after. Meli asks to keep the full timeline visible instead of compressing the three weeks into a few seconds.

MELI: We planted, waited, and now there are leaves.

TOKO: That is the whole story.

PAVO: We have enough for a segment.

The crew packs away the bench prop. Toko waters the soil; Meli holds the camera shade so it does not fall over the seedlings. She watches a leaf move in the breeze and does not call it a harvest.

TAG

Pavo starts the time-lapse. A cloud crosses the sun. The image darkens, then brightens.

FINAL IMAGE: Meli points out one new sprout at the bed's edge as the camera holds the shot long enough to see it is still small.
