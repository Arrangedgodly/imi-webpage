# The Pottery Contest Glazes the Wrong Bowl

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: craft competitions that make a last-second judging mix-up look like a dramatic elimination*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Pali, a capuchin; potter Rema, an orangutan; potter Tavi, a macaque; judge Doro, a gibbon; producer Leki, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. POTTERY STUDIO - DAY

Two similar bowls sit on a judging table. Pali lifts one.

PALI: Tavi's bowl has won.

TAVI: That is Rema's.

[The label beneath the bowl reads REMA.]

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Leki says the bowls were switched during the camera setup. Doro checks the score cards. Tavi's bowl has a blue glaze; Rema's has a green band. The bowl in Pali's hand has both. Rema says it is the practice piece. The main bowls are still on the drying rack.

DORO: The practice bowl is not in the contest.

PALI: It is in the close-up.

Leki asks for a suspense sting while the crew locates the entries. The sting repeats every five seconds. Rema and Tavi wait beside their wheels, holding their hands still. The music makes the quiet room sound like a final round.

ACT TWO

INT. DRYING RACK - LATER

Doro inspects the bowls. One is still damp. Tavi points to his initials on the foot ring. Rema's bowl has a small thumbprint in the clay. Leki asks the camera operator for a close-up of the mark. The camera reveals that the thumbprint belongs to Tavi; he helped Rema center the clay earlier.

[The host lifts the practice bowl again. Its label sticks to the bottom and rises with it. Leki peels it away. The label now reads SAMPLE.]

Pali asks whether the judge can determine authorship by the glaze. Doro says glaze is not a signature. Rema explains she used the green band to cover a small uneven edge. Tavi says he used the blue glaze to bring out the bowl's ridges. The score cards are accurate, but the objects on the table are not.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

The crew returns the practice piece to a separate shelf. The contestants carry their finished bowls to the table themselves. Doro checks each foot ring and reads the initials. Leki turns off the suspense sting. Pali begins a new introduction, but the camera is still aimed at the empty table. He holds up both bowls while the operator resets.

INT. POTTERY STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

The contestants carry their finished bowls to the judging table themselves. Leki turns off the suspense sting, but Pali asks for one final drumroll before Doro has checked the names. Doro stops him with a raised hand. Rema's bowl is damp at the foot, so she places it on a cork pad; Tavi sets his on a second pad.

DORO: Let the clay sit. We have time to read the labels.

The segment clock flashes FINAL DECISION. Leki asks Pali to announce a winner while Doro compares the foot rings with the cards. Pali looks into the camera and begins, "The champion is..." Doro slides the practice bowl, still marked SAMPLE, to a separate shelf. Pali has to wait while Doro reads each maker's name aloud. Tavi asks if the ribbon can wait too. Leki lowers it from the camera frame. The bowls are now on the table with their makers' names visible; no score has been read. Pali asks the camera operator to zoom in on the ribbon instead. Doro moves it back between both score cards. The suspense music starts again, then stops when Leki pulls its cue card from the console.

ACT THREE

INT. POTTERY STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Doro judges the bowls in their correct places. Rema's green band is even. Tavi's blue glaze follows the ridges. The judge awards separate marks for shape, finish and control. The contestants tie. Pali looks toward Leki for a tie-breaker. She points to the contest rules: the judges' scores are final.

Tavi asks if they can share the ribbon. Rema says one ribbon can be cut in half. Doro hands it to them; they unfold it and discover it is a long sash. Pali drapes it between the two bowls. The camera finally captures both pieces with the right labels.

Leki asks for a final reveal shot. Rema lifts her bowl, checks the label and sets it down. Tavi does the same. No one picks up the practice piece. Doro signs both score cards and gives each potter a copy. The contestants carry their own bowls to the padded transport shelf.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE CONTEST

Leki marks the practice shelf NOT FOR JUDGING. Pali removes the suspense music cue from the rundown. Doro checks that both labels remain under the right bowls.

FINAL IMAGE: The green and blue bowls sit side by side with matching score cards, while the sample remains on its separate shelf.
