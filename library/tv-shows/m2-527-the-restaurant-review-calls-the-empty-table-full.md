# The Restaurant Review Calls the Empty Table Full

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: restaurant review segments that declare a packed dining room while filming before service*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Tavi, a macaque; reviewer Leni, a lemur; chef Oda, an orangutan; producer Riko, a capuchin; server Pali, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. RESTAURANT - AFTERNOON

Tavi faces a dining room with every chair empty.

TAVI: This place is buzzing with energy.

PALI: We open in two hours.

ACT ONE

INT. RESTAURANT - CONTINUOUS

Riko says the segment must show a dinner rush. Oda is still preparing the menu. Leni asks to taste the soup. Pali sets one bowl on a table. The camera frames it against the empty chairs. Tavi calls it a beloved neighborhood gathering spot.

ODa: There are no guests yet.

Riko places a bouquet on each table. The flowers block the view of the empty room. Leni asks to see the menu. The printed copy lists three dishes that have been removed. Oda crosses them out. The host keeps reading them.

ACT TWO

INT. KITCHEN - LATER

Leni watches Oda prepare the soup. The camera operator asks for the chef to repeat the garnish. Oda plates one bowl, then waits for the camera reset. The soup cools. Riko asks for a steaming close-up. Pali brings a kettle; the steam fogs the lens.

[The restaurant doors open. A real customer arrives early and asks for a table. Riko wants them to wait until filming ends. Pali offers the customer a seat by the window. The camera follows the customer and finally records someone dining.]

Tavi introduces the guest as a regular. The customer says it is their first visit. Leni asks what they ordered. The customer points to the current menu. Oda has prepared the dish, but the host is still describing the crossed-out items. Riko takes the old menu card from Tavi.

INT. DINING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The customer tastes the soup and says it is good. Leni asks what stands out. The customer says the toasted mushrooms. The camera holds on the bowl and the diner, not the bouquet. Oda brings the updated menu. Tavi reads the correct dishes. Riko asks him to describe the room as lively. The customer is eating quietly.

INT. KITCHEN PASS - LATER

The dining room now has guests, but two tables remain open. Riko asks Tavi to stand where he can hide them. Leni moves his mark so the camera sees the room honestly. Pali carries a fresh soup bowl from Oda's kitchen to the table; the camera follows the server instead of the host's bouquet.

Tavi reads the current menu. He starts to add that every table is full, then sees Pali seating another couple at an open one. Riko removes the word FULL from the graphic. Leni tastes the soup and notes the toasted mushrooms. The kitchen bell rings. Tavi waits for it to stop before asking Oda about the recipe. Oda answers from the pass, then turns back to the orders. The crew resets its wide shot without moving the guests or staging applause. Leni keeps her review notes closed until she has finished tasting.

Riko asks the camera assistant to pan across the room. One table is still open. Tavi starts to call it the busiest night of the season, but Leni taps the empty chair in frame. Pali seats a walk-in at that table. The take now shows a restaurant gaining customers at its own pace. Oda sends the new guest the current menu, and Riko removes the canned applause cue from the sound board.

ACT THREE

INT. RESTAURANT - EVENING

The restaurant opens for service. Pali seats two more guests. Leni returns to the table and reviews the soup and the current menu. Tavi no longer claims the room is full. Riko tries to roll a second camera toward the entrance, but the tripod blocks the server's path. Pali moves it behind the host mark.

Oda brings out a fresh bowl, not the cooled one from the first take. Leni tastes it. The broth is hot. She describes the flavor and the toasted garnish. A couple at the next table orders the same dish. Tavi asks Oda how long the recipe takes. Oda answers without a rehearsed history. The kitchen bell rings. Pali picks up two plates and carries them into the dining room.

Riko cues a wide shot. The room is not packed, but it is serving guests. Leni says the restaurant is open, the menu is current, and the soup is worth ordering. Tavi asks if the phrase "buzzing with energy" can stay. The kitchen bell rings again. Pali says that is the actual source of the buzz.

TAG

INT. RESTAURANT - AFTER SERVICE

Riko removes the bouquets. Pali stacks the unused menus. Oda keeps one current copy beside the pass.

FINAL IMAGE: The restaurant has three occupied tables and one steaming bowl, while the old menu card lies crossed out on the counter.
