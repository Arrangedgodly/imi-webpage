# The Finale Needs Another Finale

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality-competition finales that fill the winner announcement with recaps, sponsor segments and manufactured suspense*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Cast: Host Pavi, a macaque; sculptor Leni, a gibbon; sculptor Odo, an orangutan; judge Rali, a capuchin; judge Tavi, a baboon; director Neri, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. COMPETITION STUDIO - NIGHT

Two clay weather vanes wait on stands beneath a cloth. Pavi holds an envelope against his heart. Neri points to the live camera.

PAVI: After a season of struggle, someone will finally learn what is inside this envelope.

NERI: The sponsor voucher. We haven't judged the vanes yet.

Pavi turns the envelope around. Its back reads FREE FAN WITH PURCHASE.

PAVI: A season of struggle and excellent value.

ACT ONE

Leni uncovers a bird vane with a broad tail. Odo reveals an arrow with a polished counterweight. Rali places two floor marks at equal distances from the unpowered test fan. Tavi holds a card saying DIRECTION, NOT SPEED.

RALI: One official demonstration. The fan runs for twelve seconds. A vane must turn toward the air source and hold its direction.

ODO: Mine turns very freely.

TAVI: So does a revolving door. It doesn't tell us the weather.

Pavi gestures to the recap screen. It shows Leni making a bowl in week one. The bowl fills the entire screen while the actual finalists wait.

PAVI: Leni began with a vessel for possibility.

LENI: It was for soup.

The montage cuts to Odo holding a lump of clay.

PAVI: Odo stared into an uncertain future.

ODO: I was looking for my carving tool. It was under your shoe.

Neri advances the clip. Pavi's shoe appears, crushing the handle. He steps in front of the screen.

PAVI: Some memories belong to the contestants.

Rali asks both sculptors to check their bearings before the official demonstration. Leni removes a packing thread from her stand. Odo wipes a fleck of clay from his pivot. Tavi checks the floor marks; neither fan nor vane is running.

ACT TWO

A stage assistant rolls a branded fan between the camera and the contestants. Pavi produces a card for the sponsor segment.

PAVI: Tonight's breeze is brought to you by the fan that never lets you down.

Neri points to the unplugged test fan. Pavi points instead to the enormous sponsor model. Its face carries a logo larger than either vane.

NERI: That is a display unit. The approved test uses this one.

Pavi switches on the sponsor fan for his product shot. Its gust lifts his cue cards. Odo catches the edge of his stand before it slides off its floor mark. Leni holds her bird steady.

TAVI: We are still in the advertisement. No result counts.

PAVI: The sponsor has given us an exciting rehearsal.

NERI: The sponsor has given us a paper chase.

Pavi crawls after a card. The camera follows him beneath the table, where the discarded recap script says PAUSE FOR EMOTION. He reads it while kneeling on the cable cover.

PAVI: I am feeling a great deal.

Rali disconnects the branded fan. Neri rolls it behind the curtain, restores both stands to the measured marks and checks the approved fan's low setting. The contestants step behind the judging line. Odo reaches toward his counterweight, then remembers the demonstration is about to begin and withdraws his hand.

ACT TWO-B

Neri places a twelve-second timer where contestants and camera can see it. Pavi reaches for the recap button. Tavi lays the rules card over it.

PAVI: We could remember the journey before the breeze.

TAVI: The breeze is the journey's destination.

RALI: Official demonstration starts now.

Neri starts the timer and the approved fan together. Leni's bird turns toward the fan and settles. Odo's arrow spins once, overshoots and stops facing away from the air source.

Pavi whispers a dramatic commentary. Neri raises a finger to her lips. The audience hears the soft bearing clicks and the fan. Odo looks from his arrow to the rules card. He keeps his hands behind the line.

ODO: I optimized the spin.

LENI: I gave mine somewhere to stop.

The timer reaches twelve. Neri turns off the fan. Rali and Tavi record their scores without moving either stand. Pavi lifts the voucher envelope.

PAVI: Could our champion be hidden in here?

NERI: Your champion would have to be a discount fan.

ACT THREE

The judges compare their cards. Rali writes Leni's name on the result slip and folds it. Pavi empties the voucher onto the table so Rali can put the actual result into the envelope.

RALI: Direction was the rule. Leni's vane held it.

TAVI: Odo's bearing moved well, but his arrow did not indicate the source.

Pavi opens the envelope and announces Leni. She accepts a brass weathercock trophy. Odo congratulates her, then studies his arrow without requesting another scored attempt.

PAVI: Before our winner speaks, a montage of her reaction.

LENI: I haven't reacted yet.

NERI: Let her do it live.

Leni thanks Odo for lending her a shaping tool. He lifts his undamaged tool for the camera and puts it safely in his apron. Pavi looks down at his own shoes before thanking the judges and closing the broadcast.

TAG

The camera light goes out. Pavi reaches for the recap button one last time. Tavi removes the rules card. Beneath it Neri has unplugged the button's cable.

PAVI: Can we have a recap of the unplugging?

Neri hands him the sponsor voucher.

FINAL IMAGE: Pavi fans himself with FREE FAN WITH PURCHASE while Leni carries her brass weathercock trophy offstage.
