# The Voice Behind the Curtain

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: talent competition television, where judges build suspense over a performer hidden behind a curtain*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Three judges try to identify a singer by voice, but the contestant keeps changing the song to suit the curtain's acoustics.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; judge Odo, baboon; judge Nira, macaque; contestant Seli, capuchin; stagehand Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Who Is That Singer?"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. A BLUE CURTAIN HIDES THE CONTESTANT.]

PAVA: Behind this curtain is a voice that could change the competition.

ODO: Is it a high voice?

NIRA: We haven't heard it yet.

SELI (BEHIND CURTAIN): I can hear you.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PAVA STANDS WITH A SCORECARD.]

PAVA: Our judges will hear one song, then guess the singer's identity.

TAVI: The singer asked for a microphone.

PAVA: They have one.

TAVI: It is behind the curtain.

Odo says the voice sounds familiar before the performance begins. Nira tells him he is guessing too early. The curtain rises two inches, then drops.

PAVA: A dramatic hint.

SELI: The curtain is caught on my sleeve.

Tavi frees it. The singer begins a short melody. The room echoes, making the voice sound deeper.

ODO: It is someone with a low register.

NIRA: Or a room with an echo.

### Act Two

[THE CURTAIN IS CLOSED AGAIN. THE JUDGES WRITE NOTES.]

Pava asks the contestant to sing a different song. Seli chooses a humming tune, but the microphone stand is too tall. Tavi adjusts it. The hum becomes clearer.

NIRA: The voice is brighter now.

ODO: The singer changed.

SELI: I moved closer to the microphone.

PAVA: The contestant is revealing clues.

Odo guesses it is the stagehand. Tavi protests that she has not sung. Nira guesses Seli, who remains behind the curtain.

NIRA: The singer sounds like Seli.

SELI: I am Seli.

PAVA: That is one clue.

Tavi points out the curtain is vibrating against the speaker, adding a low buzz. He moves the speaker. The voice changes again.

ODO: Now I hear the singer clearly.

NIRA: Same voice, different setup.

[THE JUDGES TAKE A SHORT BREAK.]

Odo asks Nira how she guessed Seli. Nira says the contestant matched her breath to the room's echo and paused to hear the tail of each note.

NIRA: The singer listened before changing the melody.

ODO: I was listening too.

NIRA: You guessed me, the stagehand, and then the curtain.

ODO: The curtain sounded like a bass singer.

Tavi holds up the curtain and lets it settle. It makes no sound. Seli sings one note from behind it; the fabric vibrates.

PAVA: We should judge the performer, not the room.

ODO: The room is part of the performance.

Seli says she would like to sing the original song from the beginning, without the curtain. Pava agrees, but the microphone is still too high. Tavi lowers it; the judges move their chairs closer so they can hear clearly.

### Act Three

[THE JUDGES STAND TO MAKE THEIR FINAL GUESSES.]

The stagehand brings three score paddles, one for each judge. Odo picks the one marked "mystery," Nira selects "voice," and Pava accidentally chooses "curtain."

TAVI: The paddles were stacked out of order.

PAVA: I have committed to my answer.

ODO: Can you change it?

PAVA: The show has a rule against changing answers.

NIRA: You just invented that rule.

PAVA: It is now a rule.

Seli asks whether she can sing one final note before the guesses are revealed. Odo says the judges must lock in first. The microphone picks up the curtain rustling behind her.

ODO: The curtain is trying to influence the vote.

TAVI: It is hanging still.

NIRA: The fan is on.

Pava switches off the fan. The rustle stops, but Seli's voice now sounds different without the room's movement. The judges hear the same singer, clearly. Pava asks each judge to lock in an answer. Odo writes "Seli," Nira writes "Seli," and Pava writes "the curtain."

PAVA: I choose the curtain because it has been performing all night.

Seli steps out. She is holding the curtain edge so it will not fall.

SELI: I changed songs because the echo was strange.

TAVI: The curtain was adding the bass note.

ODO: The curtain is a fourth performer.

NIRA: Does it receive a score?

PAVA: It does if it can sing on its own.

Tavi lets go. The curtain drops, and the stage lights make it rustle.

[SFX: CURTAIN RUSTLE.]

PAVA: That is a strong finish.

### Tag

[THE CURTAIN IS RAISED AGAIN. THE JUDGES ARE GONE.]

SELI: Did I win?

TAVI: You performed.

SELI: Did the curtain win?

TAVI: It is still on stage.

[SFX: APPLAUSE SIGN CLICKS ON. CURTAIN RUSTLES.]
