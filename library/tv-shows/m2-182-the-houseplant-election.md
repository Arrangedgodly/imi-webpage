# The Houseplant Election

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: municipal campaign sitcom convention, where candidates make promises to a single leafy voter who cannot vote*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: Two residents compete to become greenhouse captain by promising more shade to the one plant that cannot cast a ballot.

Cast: Doma, a baboon candidate; Lali, a gibbon candidate; Pavi, a macaque debate moderator; and Fernanda, a potted ficus with no dialogue.

Episode: "A Leaf in Every Pot"

COLD OPEN

INT. GREENHOUSE, MORNING

Pavi places a ballot box beside a potted ficus. Doma hangs a banner: SHADE FOR ALL. Lali arrives with a smaller banner: MORE LIGHT, LESS WAITING.

PAVI: The ficus is the only registered voter.

DOMA: We have a strong ground game.

LALI: We have a strong pot game.

A leaf drops into the ballot box.

PAVI: Early voting has begun.

ACT ONE

INT. GREENHOUSE, LATER

Pavi moderates the candidate debate. Doma promises a canopy over every plant. Lali promises to rotate the pots every morning so each side gets sunlight.

LALI: A fair greenhouse is one where every leaf sees the sun.

DOMA: A safe greenhouse is one where no leaf is scorched.

Pavi asks them to explain how they will protect Fernanda, the ficus. Doma moves the banner to create shade. Lali moves the ficus toward the window. They both reach for the pot at once, turning it in place.

PAVI: The candidate positions are changing.

A small monkey in the audience asks if the plant can vote for itself. Pavi says the ballot only allows one mark. Lali suggests the plant can drop a leaf. Doma calls that an endorsement.

ACT TWO

INT. GREENHOUSE, AFTERNOON

Doma builds a tiny canopy from a paper sign. It blocks the window entirely. Lali says the ficus needs light. Doma cuts a square opening in the sign. The canopy collapses onto the ballot box.

LALI: The voter has been covered.

DOMA: The voter is protected.

Pavi opens the ballot box. Inside are two leaves and a campaign flyer. Neither leaf has a mark. The flyer says, "Vote for a better greenhouse." Pavi asks which candidate submitted it. Both deny it, then recognize their own handwriting.

A leak begins above the ficus. Lali moves the pot under a dry shelf. Doma catches the drip in a bowl. Pavi says the debate has become a maintenance meeting. The candidates realize the greenhouse captain must also check vents, water trays and shade cloth.

DOMA: I will check the vents.

LALI: I will rotate the pots.

They work together. The ficus sits safely beneath the shelf. Pavi asks if either candidate still wants the job. They both raise a hand.

ACT TWO-B

INT. GREENHOUSE, LATE AFTERNOON

Pavi runs a test to see whether Fernanda responds to campaign promises. Doma reads his platform aloud while Lali rotates the pot toward the window. Each time Doma promises shade, the leaves move slightly because the paper canopy flaps. Each time Lali promises sunlight, the leaves remain still.

DOMA: The plant is signaling.

LALI: The sign is blocking the air.

They remove the sign and place it on the floor. A leaf unfolds. Pavi asks if that is an endorsement. The candidates argue about who noticed it first. Pavi says the leaf's movement cannot be assigned to either campaign.

Doma proposes a town hall with the other plants. Lali gathers three pots. One is a cactus. Pavi says it can participate, but no one should touch it. Doma asks the cactus if it supports canopy coverage. It does not respond.

LALI: That is a silent constituent.

They practice a stump speech. Doma promises to protect every pot from falling. As he speaks, a watering can tips near the ficus. Lali catches it, and Doma moves the plant to dry ground. Pavi asks which candidate did the work. Doma and Lali both say, "We did."

ACT THREE


INT. GREENHOUSE, EVENING

Pavi opens the ballot box. There are three leaves, one campaign flyer and a small beetle. He counts the leaves as votes, then stops.

PAVI: The voter has not marked a preference.

Doma proposes a coin toss. Lali says they can share the captaincy. They agree to alternate weekly and keep a shared checklist. Pavi writes the result on a sign and places it beside the ficus.

LALI: The election has produced cooperation.

DOMA: The campaign promised that.

Pavi adjusts the sign so it does not shade the plant. A new leaf unfolds.

TAG

INT. GREENHOUSE, NEXT MORNING

Pavi returns to find a leaf resting on the checklist. He picks it up and checks the schedule.

PAVI: Fernanda has voted for Monday.

Doma and Lali arrive at the same time, carrying two watering cans.

DOMA: We have a tie.

LALI: The plant has requested a recount.
