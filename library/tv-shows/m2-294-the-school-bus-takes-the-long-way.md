# The School Bus Takes the Long Way

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality travel competition television, where students race a school bus to the same one-block destination*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A camera crew follows two teams trying to reach the library before the bell, but the bus route and walking route both include a closed gate.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; driver Nara, macaque; walker Seli, capuchin; navigator Piri, gibbon; timekeeper Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "First to the Library"

### Cold Open

[BUS IDLES BESIDE A SIGN: LIBRARY, 100 METERS.]

ODO: The race begins now.

NARA: The gate is closed.

SELI: The path is open.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE TWO TEAMS SET OUT.]

Odo says the bus team will take the road while Seli and Piri walk. Tavi starts the timer. The bus moves slowly through the narrow lane, and the walkers move ahead.

NARA: We cannot pass the cart.

ODO: We are still in the race.

PIRI: We are already at the path.

Seli says the walking route is shorter, but the gate is locked. Piri checks the sign; it says "open from the other side."

ODO: We have a twist.

NARA: It is a gate.

Tavi asks if the bus can use the side entrance. Nara says the side entrance is for deliveries.

### Act Two

[THE WALKING TEAM LOOKS FOR AN ALTERNATE ROUTE.]

Seli follows the fence and finds a small footpath. It leads past the garden, but a hose crosses the path.

PIRI: We need to step over it.

ODO: The race is delayed.

NARA: It is not the bus team.

Seli lifts the hose so Piri can pass. The bus reaches the corner and finds another sign: "No buses beyond this point."

NARA: We cannot continue.

ODO: The bus has been eliminated.

TAVI: The rules did not say that.

Nara reverses carefully to the turnout. Odo asks her to take the main road. She says the library is closer by the footpath.

[THE TEAMS COMPARE THEIR ROUTES.]

Nara checks the bus map. The street route is longer because the road bends around the garden. Seli checks the walking map; the footpath is shorter but has no sign at the gate.

PIRI: We can add a sign.

ODO: The sign would help next time.

NARA: We need to arrive today.

Odo asks the driver to take a smaller alley. Nara refuses because the bus cannot turn safely. Tavi checks the vehicle dimensions against the narrow entrance.

TAVI: The bus is wider than the alley.

ODO: Can we remove the side mirrors?

NARA: No.

Seli and Piri walk to the gate. It is locked from their side, but they can see a latch on the other side. Piri reaches through the bars but cannot reach it.

SELI: The path is open, but the gate is not.

PIRI: We need the key.

Tavi calls the library. The librarian says she has the gate key and can meet them at the entrance. Odo asks if this counts as a shortcut.

NARA: It is the route we should have taken.

### Act Three

[THE BUS TEAM PARKS AT THE TURNOUT.]

Seli and Piri arrive at the locked library door ten minutes before opening. The bus cannot pass the closed gate, so Nara parks at the marked turnout and walks the last stretch with Odo.

SELI: We reached the library.

PIRI: The door is still locked.

ODO: We can start the ending now.

TAVI: The rule says the race ends when the library opens.

Odo asks Tavi to change the lower-third graphic to say WALKERS WIN. Tavi refuses; the clock still shows ten minutes. Nara checks the gate latch while Piri tapes a hand-drawn arrow to the sign, pointing toward the footpath.

NARA: The bus route is closed.

ODO: The bus is at the finish.

TAVI: It is at a turnout.

The school bell rings at the scheduled opening. The librarian unlocks the door. Piri touches the threshold first; Seli enters beside her. Odo tries to call the bus a co-winner, but Nara points out that it never reached the library.

SELI: The walking team wins.

PIRI: The bus is still useful tomorrow.

Odo asks the camera for a dramatic bus arrival. Nara walks back to the turnout rather than drive through the gate. The horn sounds once as the bus reverses into its legal parking space.

TAVI: That is the bus's finish.

ODO: The walkers get the ribbon.

Seli ties it to the library's footpath sign.

### Tag

[THE FOOTPATH SIGN FLAPS IN THE WIND.]

The ribbon points toward the open library door. The bus waits beyond the gate.

NARA: Tomorrow's route is clear.

ODO: Tomorrow's route will be shorter.

[SFX: PAGE TURNS.]
