# The Timer Locked the Exit Graphic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised escape-room contests, countdown graphics, hint penalties, and hosts who narrate doors instead of clues*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: Two teams race to open a prop vault, but the show's on-screen countdown freezes at ten seconds and keeps the host from calling the result.

Cast: Host Savi, a macaque; team North, including Leko, a gibbon, and Piri, a capuchin; team South, including Ralo, a baboon, and Tavi, an orangutan; puzzle designer Nema, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME STUDIO - NIGHT

Two teams stand before a painted vault door. A screen counts down from ten seconds and stops.

SAVI: The teams are still racing.

NEMA: The graphic is not.

Leko turns a wooden dial. The vault clicks open.

ACT ONE

Savi explains the challenge: find three colored tiles, arrange them by size, and turn the dial. The first team to open its prop vault wins. Team North found two tiles. Team South has found all three but cannot agree on the order.

RALO: Blue is first.

TAVI: The card says small to large.

RALO: Blue is small.

Nema says the tiles are all the same size. The clue refers to the symbols on them: a seed, a sprout and a tree. Piri turns her tile over and sees the symbols. The teams recognize the sequence.

ACT TWO

Team North arranges seed, sprout, tree. The dial turns, but the vault remains shut. Leko asks for a hint. Savi says hints add fifteen seconds. The frozen screen does not change. Nema asks them to check the side of the tiles.

PIRI: There are arrows here.

LEKO: The arrows point around the circle.

Ralo looks at the clock and asks for his hint. The timer remains at ten seconds. Savi asks the control room to restore it. The clock flashes ten, then displays "EXIT GRAPHIC PENDING." The teams continue solving while Nema checks the dial labels.

NEMA: The vault is openable. The timer is not part of the lock.

ACT TWO-B

Team South turns the dial: seed, sprout, tree. It opens. Team North uses the arrows to rotate the tiles to face the door. Their sequence matches. The vault opens. Ralo reaches inside and finds a small key. Tavi asks what it unlocks. Nema says it opens the prize cabinet after the game. The teams still have to decide who finished first.

SAVI: We need the timer.

NEMA: We need the dial sensor logs.

The sensor log shows Team South's latch opened first, followed by North's a few seconds later. The frozen countdown graphic still reads ten. Savi asks if they should replay the finish. Nema says the log is sufficient. Ralo hands the key to Savi rather than trying to open the prize cabinet early.

RALO: We are waiting for the result.

PIRI: That is new for a race.

ACT TWO-B - THE SECOND LOCK

Nema reveals a second latch behind the first vault door. The key from Team South fits a small slot, but it will not turn. The frozen timer begins pulsing red, making both teams look up instead of at the clue plate. Savi asks them to describe the screen for the recap.

LEKO: It says ten.

RALO: It has said ten for the whole race.

PIRI: The clue plate has three footprints, each facing a different direction.

The teams line up their seed, sprout and tree tiles under the matching footprints. Leko reaches for the key again. Tavi rotates the plate a quarter turn, and an arrow appears behind it. North catches the direction first; South recognizes that the symbols must face outward. They fit the key, pull together, and open the latch. Inside is a bell wired to the finish sensor.

NEMA: The bell records the completion. The clock only decorates it.

SAVI: We have been racing a decoration.

The bell rings. The studio timer remains frozen. Ralo tries not to look at it; the red pulse reflects in his eyes. Piri covers the display with a blank clue card so the teams can listen to the bell again. The crew confirms the first ring on the audio track, giving the result a physical source instead of a graphic.

ACT THREE

Savi announces Team South as the winner by the latch record. Team North accepts the result. The host opens the prize cabinet with the key. Inside are two small medals, one for each team, and a card explaining that the game was designed for both teams to succeed.

LEKO: We both opened the vault.

NEMA: That was the point of the puzzle.

RALO: We won the race.

TAVI: We solved it together after the race.

The show gives Team South the time prize and Team North the completion medal. Nema resets the tiles for the next round. Savi asks the control room to replace the broken timer before the next recording.

TAG

The graphic changes from ten to zero after the studio has emptied. Savi walks back on camera and points at it.

SAVI: We have a new winner.

FINAL IMAGE: The vault stands open while the frozen clock restarts over the empty set.
