# The Courtroom Cooking Show Sentences a Pie

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom cooking competitions, solemn evidence, timed judging, and chefs who object to their own crusts*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Judge Miro, a mandrill; chef Leda, a macaque; opposing chef Bram, an orangutan; bailiff Piri, a capuchin; host Sela, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO COURTROOM KITCHEN - DAY

A pie sits beneath a glass dome. Judge Miro taps a wooden spoon like a gavel.

MIRO: The court will determine whether this crust is flaky beyond reasonable doubt.

The pie gives a soft, wet sag.

BRAM: I object to the timing.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The cooking show has fused a courtroom set with a baking contest. Leda's berry pie is cooling on the evidence table. Bram says it collapsed before the judge tasted it. Host Sela wants a dramatic verdict before the commercial break. Bailiff Piri is trying to keep the contestants from moving the pie while the crust sets.

SELA: Chef Leda, how do you plead?

LEDA: I plead for ten minutes of cooling.

MIRO: That is not one of the available answers.

PIRI: It is the only answer that keeps the filling from running.

BRAM: The pie is already leaning toward the jury.

Sela points to a monitor showing a slow-motion replay of the sag. The replay has been zoomed in until the crust looks like a landslide.

SELA: Viewers deserve to see the evidence.

LEDA: That is not the evidence. That is one berry escaping through steam.

Miro asks Piri to mark the pie's current position. Piri places a little numbered tent card beside the dish, then discovers the card is reflected in the dome as if it were another berry.

PIRI: We have a witness who is a reflection.

MIRO: Is the witness under oath?

PIRI: The reflection is silent.

BRAM: Convenient.

Leda says the filling must rest before anyone cuts it. Bram calls that a delay tactic. Sela asks the production to start a countdown clock. Piri points out that the clock is counting up from zero because someone reversed the control.

SELA: We can call it a suspense clock.

MIRO: The court prefers clocks that tell time.

ACT TWO

Miro asks each chef to present evidence. Bram has a photograph of a perfectly level pie from before the oven. Leda has a temperature reading from the center of the filling. The numbers do not disagree; they describe different moments.

BRAM: The pie looked level when it entered the oven.

LEDA: It was supposed to rise and settle.

MIRO: So the photograph proves the pie existed.

SELA: Could we call the photo a character witness?

PIRI: Only if it can be cross-examined.

The cooking judge begins a long question about butter temperature. The question ends with, "And where were you at 4:12?" Leda answers, "Washing the berries," while Bram admits he was checking the camera monitor instead of his oven timer. His second pie is still inside the oven.

BRAM: I was watching the angle, not the clock.

MIRO: The angle has not been charged with anything.

Piri hears the timer chirp. Bram rushes to the oven, but the set's courtroom partition blocks his path. Piri swings the partition open and discovers the door marked COURT EXIT only leads to a pantry.

PIRI: The bailiff has found the pantry.

SELA: Is that where the jury is?

PIRI: It is where the paper towels are.

Bram removes his pie. Its edge is slightly dark, but it holds its shape. He tries to cover the dark edge with a decorative leaf. Miro stops him and asks for the condition of the pie as baked.

MIRO: We are judging the pastry, not a disguise.

LEDA: Then let both pies cool on the rack. The clock says four minutes.

SELA: The audience has watched three commercial breaks.

MIRO: The audience can watch a pie cool. That is still more honest than a countdown that counts upward.

ACT THREE

The pies rest on a wire rack. Sela tries to fill the quiet with a recap of the evidence, but Miro makes her stop before she narrates the crust into changing. Piri reads the actual timer: four minutes have passed. Leda cuts one slice. The filling holds; the bottom crust is crisp. Bram cuts his. It holds too, though the edge is dark.

BRAM: I withdraw the accusation of collapse.

MIRO: The court appreciates a corrected statement.

SELA: Then the verdict is?

MIRO: The berry pie is not guilty of collapse. It is guilty of needing a cooling rack before testimony.

LEDA: I accept the verdict.

PIRI: Should I escort the rack from the courtroom?

MIRO: Escort the rack to the sink after the judging.

Sela looks at the jury table. It is empty; the studio had placed three fruit bowls there as stand-ins.

SELA: The jury has reached a unanimous decision to eat dessert.

MIRO: The fruit bowls cannot vote.

Leda and Bram each serve a slice to the camera crew. Bram keeps the dark edge for himself rather than hiding it. The cooking show announces that next week's case will concern a soufflé and an unreliable witness timer.

FINAL IMAGE: The gavel rests beside two cooling racks while Miro eats the slice marked EXHIBIT A.
