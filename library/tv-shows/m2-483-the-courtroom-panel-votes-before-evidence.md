# The Courtroom Panel Votes Before Evidence

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: courtroom panel shows that flash a verdict graphic before the witnesses have finished speaking*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Mella, an orangutan; advocate Rina, a macaque; witness Tavi, a lemur; caller Sela, a capuchin; graphics operator Poro, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. TV COURT SET - DAY

A monitor reads VERDICT: GUILTY. The host looks at the empty witness chair. Tavi enters carrying a flowerpot.

MELLA: We have not heard the case.

PORO: The graphic has.

[The monitor changes to NOT GUILTY, then to MISTRIAL.]

ACT ONE

INT. TV COURT SET - CONTINUOUS

Rina explains the dispute: a neighbor's wind chime keeps ringing during a recorded meditation hour. Tavi owns the chime. Sela called the show asking for a ruling. Mella asks Tavi to explain when it rings. He says it rings when the wind moves. Poro's graphic displays DISTURBING THE PEACE.

RINA: He has not finished answering.

[Poro freezes the graphic. Mella asks Tavi to demonstrate the chime. Tavi sets the flowerpot on the witness table. A small bell is tied to its rim.]

TAVI: It is a wind chime for the plant.

Sela says the bell wakes her during meditation. Mella asks whether she has asked him to move it. Sela says she has not, because the chime's placement is part of the mystery.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Poro's graphics keep appearing early. Mella calls for a pause. The producer tells Poro to use the "dramatic reveal" button only after the evidence. He presses it to confirm. The screen flashes GUILTY again.

Poro: It is very sensitive.

Mella moves the button to the far side of the console. Poro's sleeve catches the cable and drags the monitor graphic into the frame. The courtroom audience sees a verdict card sliding across the set.

[The host turns the monitor toward the wall. Rina asks Sela if she has tried closing the window during meditation. Sela says she has, but the bell continues whenever the air conditioner starts.]

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

The producer asks Poro to add a poll graphic: WHO IS AT FAULT? Mella says the neighbors have not yet agreed on a solution. Poro puts up YES and NO buttons anyway. The studio audience presses both. The screen shows a tie. Rina asks Poro to remove the poll and display the chime's recorded sound. Tavi swings it once. The bell rings softly. Mella asks the audience whether they would rather hear it during a quiet hour or on the porch. The room raises hands; most choose porch.

Poro begins to flash a verdict. Mella stops him with a raised palm. Rina asks Tavi if the chime can be moved without damaging the plant. Tavi removes the small hook and shows it is attached with a screw, not nailed into the pot.

INT. TV COURT SET - CONTINUOUS

Rina asks Tavi why the chime hangs near Sela's window. Tavi says it is tied to the flowerpot so he can hear when the plant's leaves move in the breeze. Sela says the bell interrupted her quiet hour three times. Mella asks whether the quiet hour was posted. Sela admits it was on a note inside her apartment, not on the shared porch. The audience reacts as the host waits, instead of the graphics operator.

INT. TV COURT SET - MOMENTS LATER

Sela returns to the witness chair with a small desk fan. She switches it on. The chime rings. Poro's screen flashes LIABLE before the fan reaches full speed. Mella turns the monitor around. Rina asks Sela to describe the sound she hears in her apartment. Sela says it is the bell and the fan, not the plant. Tavi offers to move the chime to his own sill, where he can still hear the leaves move. Sela asks whether he can use a felt loop instead of the metal hook. Tavi tests one from his pocket; the bell stays quiet when the fan blows. Poro reaches for the graphic button, then asks which label to use. Mella tells him to wait for the neighbors to finish.

ACT THREE

INT. TV COURT SET - AFTERNOON

Tavi places the chime beside the window. The air conditioner switches on. The bell rings. Mella asks Tavi to move the chime to the covered porch during Sela's quiet hour. Tavi agrees. Sela agrees to tell him the schedule. Poro prepares a final graphic. He waits until Mella finishes the decision.

MELLA: The chime can stay. The two neighbors will agree on where it hangs during the quiet hour.

[The screen displays AGREEMENT REACHED. The panel applauds. Poro's hand hovers over the verdict button but does not press it.]

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Poro tests the verdict button with the monitor disconnected. The screen still flashes GUILTY on the practice slate. He turns the slate over and sees it has a red sticky note reading GUILTY on the back. He peels it off and labels the button WAIT.

FINAL IMAGE: The wind chime hangs on the covered porch, and the monitor displays AGREEMENT REACHED without a countdown.
