# The Glue Gun Gazette

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: public-access crafts programs and overambitious live demonstrations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A basement craft show's host promises to fix a viewer's window with cardboard. Her neighbors rescue the project while the station tries to rescue its pledge total.

Cast: Dotty, an ambitious capuchin host; Clem, a baboon camera operator; Bix, a macaque phone volunteer; Mrs. Twig, a langur knitter; Orla, a loris with a drafty window.

## Episode: "The Cardboard Window"

### Cold open

INT. BASEMENT STUDIO, AFTERNOON

Dotty lifts a cardboard birdhouse. Its drawbridge drops into Clem's tea.

DOTTY: Today we make something weatherproof.

CLEM: The forecast is inside my mug.

A ceiling drip punches through the roof. Bix catches the ruined house with his tail.

BIX: A viewer has pledged five dollars if we stop making houses.

DOTTY: Put them down for our window special.

### Act one

A sign reads LIVE. Another reads LIVE AGAIN, FOR VIEWERS WHO MISSED THE FIRST LIVE. Bix turns the second sign over.

DOTTY: Our sponsor, Bargain Box Warehouse, has donated all the materials you see here.

CLEM: One box.

DOTTY: A versatile box. It previously contained a microwave, so it has experience with warmth.

The phone rings. Bix holds the receiver against his ear and the pledge bell against his foot.

ORLA, ON PHONE: I need something to stop the draft around my window.

DOTTY: Excellent. We will build you an entire window.

ORLA: I have a window. The glass works. The air around it doesn't.

DOTTY: Our project can be adapted for beginners.

CLEM: She has described a gap. We are offering a building.

Dotty unfolds the box. A packing label covers the part she calls the frame. She cuts a viewing hole directly through the sponsor's logo.

BIX: The warehouse is on line two.

DOTTY: Tell them we're expanding brand visibility.

BIX: They can now see through their name.

Dotty announces that the demonstration takes ten minutes. Clem points at the wall clock. Its minute hand has fallen off.

DOTTY: We use workshop time.

Mrs. Twig arrives with her knitting bag. Dotty gives her the role of grateful homeowner.

MRS. TWIG: I own a perfectly good window. You can have grateful skeptic.

### Act two

Orla arrives carrying measurements and a piece of waxed cloth. Dotty welcomes her as a celebrity guest. Bix starts applause on the tape deck. It plays a bowling tournament.

ORLA: My sill is wider at one end. I measured both.

DOTTY: We usually reveal that complication after the commercial break.

CLEM: We don't have commercials.

DOTTY: Then we've been solving problems too early for years.

Mrs. Twig studies the measurements. She suggests a small removable insert for the gap. Dotty holds up her entire cardboard window.

DOTTY: This has architectural ambition.

ORLA: My landlord has architectural responsibility. I've called him. I just want the curtains to stop lifting until he comes.

Dotty places the grand window against a chair. It folds around the seat like a hat. Mrs. Twig takes the narrow slat Orla brought from a crate.

MRS. TWIG: Shall we make the thing she asked for?

BIX: Another pledge. Twenty dollars if we do.

CLEM: That's our largest conditional donation since the macrame treadmill.

They make a small frame on the table. Dotty tries to narrate every movement. Mrs. Twig's tail repeatedly crosses the close-up.

DOTTY: The camera needs to see the joint.

MRS. TWIG: The joint needs me to hold it.

Clem raises the camera. The shot reveals the studio's painted backdrop, which also has a cardboard window.

ORLA: Yours has a gap too.

CLEM: That's where the lens goes. Our view is permanently obstructed by television.

The paint light flickers. Clem unplugs it and opens the basement door. Daylight reaches the table.

DOTTY: Is that our lighting upgrade?

CLEM: The sun waived its appearance fee.

### Act three

They take the insert to Orla's apartment, carrying the camera because its cable is shorter than the distance to the van. Dotty announces an outside broadcast while still halfway downstairs.

DOTTY: We are coming to you from a homeowner's journey.

ORLA: Mostly the landing. Watch the loose shoe.

The insert fits. Orla leaves a pull loop where she can reach it. Dotty unveils the improvement by drawing back the curtain. Nothing moves.

DOTTY: We'll do that again. I expected a bigger reaction.

ORLA: The curtain staying still is the reaction.

CLEM: We have our first successful shot of nothing happening.

Mrs. Twig photographs the damaged sill for the landlord. Bix phones the studio. The warehouse has offered another box if they stop calling the first one structural.

DOTTY: Tell them tomorrow's episode is storage.

BIX: They want to know what we're storing.

DOTTY: Today's ambitions.

Back in the basement, Dotty shows a second demonstration insert. Orla phones to say her room is quieter. Bix rings the pledge bell. It falls off its stand.

MRS. TWIG: Next project?

CLEM: Repairing the equipment we use to announce repairs.

### Tag

Dotty returns the collapsed birdhouse to the shelf. A sparrow enters through the missing roof. Bix puts a tiny pledge envelope beside it.

DOTTY: Our first resident subscriber.

The sparrow takes the envelope for its nest.

CLEM: It chose materials over programming.

Dotty reaches for another box. Mrs. Twig quietly removes the glue gun.
