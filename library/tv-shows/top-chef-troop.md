# Top Chef Troop

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competition convention*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Premise: In the IMI test kitchen, cooks race to turn a mystery crate of ingredients into television while the judges pretend the sponsor's logo is a garnish.

Cast: Chef Panko, patient host and former cafeteria cook; Suri, an exacting tamarin baker; Bo, a confident capuchin who seasons by instinct; Mx. Peel, the show's judge and brand liaison; Ed, the exhausted pantry runner.

## Episode: "The Mystery Crate"

### Cold open

INT. TEST KITCHEN, MORNING

Chef Panko opens the mystery crate. A fog of dry ice rolls across the counter. Inside: lentils, bruised pears, one can of sweet corn, and a branded travel mug.

PANKO: The mug is not an ingredient.

Mx. Peel: The mug is the sponsor.

### Act one

The cooks have forty minutes to make a dish inspired by "the taste of ambition." Bo immediately reaches for the corn. Suri asks what ambition tastes like. Panko says he once tasted it in a staff meeting and it was mostly lukewarm tea.

Ed announces that the pantry is locked until the cooks identify which crate item belongs to the sponsor. Mx. Peel says the mug must appear in every shot. Panko points out the mug is stainless steel. Mx. Peel suggests a beverage reduction.

Bo builds a lentil cake with a pear glaze. He says the corn will give it a bold finish. Suri makes pear hand pies and uses lentils as a savory filling. She asks Ed for salt. The pantry door stays shut. Ed tells her the salt is in the crate, hidden beneath the mug. Suri turns the mug over and finds a sticky note: "Ask nicely."

SURI: Pantry, may I please have salt?

ED: Granted.

He opens the door. The crew applauds the first successful pantry negotiation of the season.

### Act two

The judges sample Bo's cake. It has a handsome shape and tastes like a sweet bean has made a poor decision. Mx. Peel praises the color and asks whether Bo can make the mug visible in the plating. Bo arranges it beside the cake like a trophy.

Suri's hand pies are crisp, warm, and careful. Mx. Peel asks if they could be shaped like the mug. Suri says that would be a different pastry. Panko agrees. He asks what she wanted the dish to say. Suri says, "I can make something good from the ingredients I have, even if the table keeps asking me to promote a cup."

Mx. Peel tastes the filling again, then writes a note. The sponsor rep whispers that she could use more excitement. Panko asks the rep to define excitement in grams.

The cooks face a sudden challenge: make a second dish with whatever remains. Ed discovers that the pantry has been open the whole time; the latch was installed backward. He reports that he has spent the season escorting cooks through the door one at a time because he thought the lock was part of the rules.

Bo uses the leftover lentils to make a soup. Suri turns the bruised pear skins into a sharp relish. Panko taste-tests both, then admits the best idea of the day came from the runner who thought the door was locked.

### Act three

At judging, Mx. Peel announces Bo's cake is attractive, but the corn overwhelmed the pear. Suri's pies win the challenge. Panko adds that the second dish showed better judgment than the first: use what is there, and stop pretending a container is food.

Bo accepts the loss without sulking. He asks Suri how she kept the pastry from tearing. She shows him a little fold, and he tries it on the last scrap of dough. It holds.

Mx. Peel presents Suri with a branded travel mug. Suri says thank you, then pours tea into it and drinks. The mug is finally used as a mug.

PANKO: A daring choice.

The sponsor rep asks whether they can shoot the pour again. Panko says only if the tea is still hot.

### Tag

Ed stands by the pantry door with a new sign: "Push." He opens it, then steps aside for the crew.

Behind him, Bo and Suri share the last hand pie. It is cold. Bo says the filling is better that way.

Panko looks into camera: "Ambition tastes like leftovers when you wait too long."

After the cameras stop, Suri shows Bo how she folded the pastry around the filling. He tries twice before making an even edge. The first attempt goes to Ed, who has been on his feet since dawn and does not care how it looks. Mx. Peel asks the crew to leave one pie untouched for a sponsor still. Suri offers the travel mug instead. The rep sighs, then takes a clean shot of tea steam against the steel.

PANKO: That is a product demonstration.

MX. PEEL: Don't make me say it was my idea.

Ed tapes the "Push" sign to the pantry door. It falls off. He flips it over and writes "Pull" on the back. The door opens on the first try. The crew applauds again, this time for the hinge.
