# The School's Best Science Fair

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: school competition television, where a science fair prize is awarded before the projects are finished*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Host Pava covers a science fair with a dramatic prize ceremony, but the judges cannot score projects until students finish demonstrating them.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; student Nara, macaque; student Odo, baboon; student Seli, capuchin; judge Tavi, lemur; teacher Luma, gibbon.

## Episode: "And the Winner Is..."

### Cold Open

[GYM. A TROPHY STANDS BESIDE THREE UNFINISHED PROJECTS.]

PAVA: The winner will be announced shortly.

TAVI: I have not seen the projects.

NARA: Mine is still drying.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PAVA STANDS BEFORE A BANNER.]

The fair opens. Odo demonstrates a pulley system, but the string slips off the wheel. Seli's paper bridge holds one seed, then bends under a second.

PAVA: Strong contenders.

LUMA: They are testing their projects.

TAVI: I need to see what each one does.

Nara brings a tray of water and a paper boat. Her project is a small water filter made from gravel, sand, and leaves. Pava asks if it is safe to drink.

NARA: It removes leaves and dirt.

TAVI: That does not mean it is safe to drink.

Pava starts to announce "a breakthrough." Luma stops her.

### Act Two

[THE JUDGES MOVE BETWEEN TABLES.]

Tavi asks Odo to show how the pulley lifts a weight. Odo rethreads the string. It works, but the weight hits the table.

ODO: The pulley reduces the effort.

TAVI: It also needs a clear path.

Seli tests the paper bridge with one seed, then two. It bends but does not collapse.

SELI: It can hold a little weight.

PAVA: Can it hold the trophy?

SELI: No.

Nara pours muddy water through the filter. The water comes out clearer but still cloudy. Pava asks if it is now drinkable.

NARA: We would need to test it further.

TAVI: Good answer.

Pava whispers to Luma that the show needs a winner. Luma says the fair is not a race.

[THE JUDGES COMPARE THEIR SCORE SHEETS.]

Pava says the show usually crowns one winner. Tavi explains that the projects solve different problems, so a single score would hide what each student achieved.

PAVA: Viewers want a result.

LUMA: They have results.

ODO: My pulley lifted the weight.

SELI: My bridge held two seeds.

NARA: My filter caught the dirt.

Tavi asks them to show the limits. Odo says the string must be centered. Seli says the bridge needs a dry table. Nara says the water still needs testing.

PAVA: We can announce three winners.

LUMA: That is not the same as three categories.

PAVA: It is close.

The producer asks which ribbon goes with which project. Pava puts the "strongest" ribbon on Nara's filter. Nara moves it to Seli's bridge. Odo reads "clearest explanation" and hands it to Nara.

ODO: We have corrected the prizes.

PAVA: The ceremony is back on.

[THE STUDENTS DEMONSTRATE THEIR PROJECTS AGAIN.]

Odo centers the pulley and lifts the weight smoothly. Seli adds a second support to the paper bridge. Nara pours a small amount of muddy water through the filter and catches the output in a clear cup.

PAVA: The water is almost clear.

NARA: It still needs testing.

TAVI: The cup helps us see that.

Luma asks each student to name one thing they would not claim. Odo says the pulley does not make work disappear. Seli says the bridge is not ready for heavy objects. Nara says the filter does not make water safe by itself.

PAVA: These are careful conclusions.

SELI: We can still be proud.

PAVA: Of course.

Odo asks whether the trophy can be used for the next experiment. Tavi says the base is too narrow to hold the weight.

### Act Three

[THE TROPHY REMAINS ON THE TABLE.]

The judges ask each student what they would change. Odo says he will add a stopper. Seli says she will use more supports. Nara says she will add a second filtering layer and test the water.

TAVI: These are all useful experiments.

PAVA: But who wins?

LUMA: The students who learned what to test next.

Pava announces awards by category: clearest explanation, strongest bridge, and most promising filter. Each student gets a ribbon. The trophy remains unclaimed.

ODO: The trophy is still here.

SELI: It can be the next project.

NARA: We could test how much weight it holds.

Pava looks at the trophy. It is heavy and has a narrow base.

PAVA: That is a new experiment.

### Tag

[THE STUDENTS SET THE TROPHY ON A LOW TABLE.]

The base wobbles. Tavi steadies it with a paper shim.

TAVI: We have improved the display.

PAVA: And found the next science fair project.

[SFX: TROPHY SETTLES, GYM APPLAUSE.]
