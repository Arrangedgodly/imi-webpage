# The Finale Starts Before the Finale

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: recap-heavy competition finales and hosts who rehearse their triumphant announcement before anyone has actually won*
*By: IMI Television Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Varo, a gibbon; contestant Nima, a macaque; contestant Olo, an orangutan; judge Piri, a baboon; director Sela, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. COMPETITION STUDIO - NIGHT

[Varo stands before an enormous envelope. Nima and Olo wait beside trays of prepared sculpture parts. Piri writes NIMA 24, OLO 23 on a board. Sela checks her headset.]

VARO: After twelve weeks, tonight we discover the champion. The answer is inside this envelope.

[Nima peers inside it.]

NIMA: There is a sandwich wrapper.

SELA: That was your lunch envelope. The blank result form is on my clipboard.

VARO: An unexpected twist before the final challenge.

PIRI: An expected consequence of eating beside the props.

[Theme music. Varo folds the wrapper very slowly, giving it a dramatic close-up.]

ACT ONE

[The two contestants assemble wind-powered sculptures. Nima chooses a wide base and two light sails. Olo chooses a narrow base, one large sail and a counterweight.]

PIRI: Five points are available. One each for secure sails, a level base, free rotation, and staying upright through a complete turn. The fifth requires that turn in twelve seconds or less.

VARO: Everything you have ever done depends on this moment.

OLO: My laundry does not.

NIMA: My tax return probably does not either.

SELA: The fan is off while they build. Varo, introduce their choices.

VARO: Nima has chosen a wide base, reflecting her lifelong search for stability.

NIMA: Reflecting the wide sail.

VARO: Olo has chosen a counterweight, reflecting a burden only he can understand.

OLO: I understand it weighs forty grams.

[Varo tries to lift the counterweight meaningfully. It remains attached to Olo's completed base. Olo gently takes his hand away. Piri checks both sculptures, awarding the first two points each.]

PIRI: Secure sails, level bases. Two each. Hands away for the fan test.

[Sela starts the low fan setting. Both sculptures turn freely and remain upright. Olo's completes a turn in eleven seconds; Nima's takes fourteen. Piri stops both timers. Sela switches the fan off.]

PIRI: Four points to Nima, five to Olo. The season totals are twenty-eight each.

VARO: Olo wins by winning more points just now!

PIRI: The season total determines the title. They are tied.

VARO: A shocking arithmetic twist.

NIMA: Addition.

ACT TWO

[Sela turns a board around. It displays the posted tie rule: while the sculpture rotates, place one paper marker on it. The marker must remain through a complete rotation after release. If both succeed, the title is shared.]

OLO: Can we use different places on our sculptures?

PIRI: Any part of your own sculpture. The fan setting stays the same.

[Varo approaches the recap screen.]

VARO: First, we must remember all twelve weeks.

SELA: The audience remembers. They watched them.

VARO: What about week four?

NIMA: You played it three times last week.

[Varo presses RECAP. A tiny clip shows his own introduction. He presses it again; the introduction repeats. Sela removes the remote from his hand.]

SELA: That button loops the host package. It contains no contestants.

VARO: It contains their emotional guide.

OLO: It contains a person pointing at us.

[Sela places the remote beside the empty envelope. She brings the shared trophy from a side shelf to the center table. Its two handles are polished; the nameplate is blank.]

VARO: Nima and Olo, your shared trophy!

PIRI: Possible shared trophy. They have not taken the test.

SELA: I have a blank result form. Nobody writes a winner before the result.

[Varo holds the blank form beside his face.]

VARO: The suspense is literally blank.

NIMA: A promising improvement on the recap.

[Piri hands each contestant one equal paper marker. Nima studies the flat portion of her base. Olo checks a ledge beside his counterweight. They stand at their own tables, markers in hand.]

ACT THREE

[Sela starts the fan at the same low setting. The sculptures begin rotating. Varo inhales for an announcement; Sela holds up WAIT.]

PIRI: Place your markers while the sculptures are moving. I will count each full rotation from the moment you let go.

[Nima lowers her marker onto the wide base and releases it. Piri marks its starting position. Olo places his marker on the counterweight ledge; it slides toward the lip, catches, and stays. His hands retreat. Piri marks that starting position too.]

VARO: Olo's marker has fallen!

OLO: It has moved three millimeters. Kindly move your commentary farther.

[Nima's marker completes its turn. Piri raises one finger. Olo's completes its own turn a moment later. Piri raises the other. Sela switches the fan off.]

PIRI: Both succeeded. Under the posted rule, Nima and Olo share the season title.

[Sela writes both names on the result form. Piri signs it. Varo reaches for the envelope, remembers the wrapper, and chooses to read the card openly.]

VARO: Our champions are Nima and Olo!

[They lift the trophy together. The crowd applauds. Varo attempts to stand between them for the camera; the handles leave no room. He steps aside, and both champions fit the frame.]

NIMA: That is a useful wide shot.

OLO: Both contestants included.

TAG

[Varo starts an additional concluding speech. Sela hands him his lunch wrapper.]

VARO: And now, the final final revelation...

SELA: You left half your sandwich.

[He checks the wrapper, finds the sandwich, and finally stops talking.]

FINAL IMAGE: The champions carry one trophy together while the host finishes his sandwich beside a silent recap remote.
