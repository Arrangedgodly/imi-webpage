# The Council of the Shared Bench

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: political debate television, where candidates argue over who controls a single park bench*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Three neighborhood representatives debate the future of one shared bench, while the moderator keeps adding podiums to the park.

Cast: Moderator Piri, capuchin; candidate Nara, macaque; candidate Odo, baboon; candidate Luma, gibbon; town clerk Seli, lemur.

## Episode: "A Seat for Everyone"

### Cold Open

[PARK BENCH. THREE CANDIDATES STAND BESIDE IT.]

PIRI: Tonight's question: who owns the bench?

NARA: The park.

ODO: The people.

LUMA: The bench.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CANDIDATES TAKE THEIR PLACES.]

Piri explains the rules: each candidate gets one minute, then one rebuttal. Odo asks if the bench is a candidate. Seli says it has not registered.

NARA: The bench is used by everyone.

ODO: I use it every morning.

LUMA: I sit there on market days.

PIRI: How will you make sure everyone gets a turn?

NARA: A schedule.

ODO: People do not need appointments to sit.

LUMA: We could install a second bench.

Seli checks the budget. There is no money for another bench.

PIRI: What about repairing this one?

Seli says one slat is loose. Odo sits down to demonstrate. The slat dips.

ODO: The bench is unstable.

NARA: You were leaning.

ODO: I was demonstrating.

### Act Two

[THE DEBATE MOVES TO THE PARK PATH.]

Piri asks candidates to propose a plan. Nara suggests marking one end for older residents. Odo says that could reserve the best shade. Luma proposes moving the bench under the tree.

Seli checks the maintenance map. The tree roots cross the path where Luma wants the bench.

LUMA: We can move it a little.

NARA: The path is narrow.

ODO: The bench is already in the shade.

PIRI: The shade moves.

ODO: We have discussed this before.

Seli places chalk marks on the ground. The candidates carry the bench to each mark, but it blocks the path at all three. They shift it toward the grass. The bench wobbles.

NARA: We need to level the ground.

LUMA: I can bring flat stones.

ODO: Stones can roll.

PIRI: The debate has become a construction meeting.

[THE MODERATOR INVITES QUESTIONS FROM THE PARK.]

A squirrel raises its paw from the tree. Piri asks for its question.

NARA: The squirrel cannot speak.

ODO: We can infer the question.

LUMA: It wants to sit on the bench.

Seli asks the candidates how they would accommodate a small animal. Odo proposes a separate ledge. Nara points out that the bench already has room for a squirrel at one end.

PIRI: Does that count as a seat?

SELI: It is part of the bench.

NARA: The squirrel is using it now.

Odo stands to make room. The squirrel hops onto the bench and takes his place.

ODO: I have yielded my seat.

LUMA: The bench is in use.

Piri asks whether the squirrel should have priority. Seli says the park is open to everyone, including animals, and the debate has no authority to evict it.

PIRI: The squirrel has ended the debate.

### Act Three

[THE CANDIDATES WORK ON THE BENCH TOGETHER.]

Seli finds a loose slat. Odo holds the bench while Nara tightens the bolt. Luma places flat stones under one leg. Piri asks whether the candidates will share the work.

NARA: We are already sharing it.

ODO: The bench should have a sign.

LUMA: What should it say?

ODO: "Please take turns."

NARA: That sounds like a rule.

PIRI: What about "A seat for anyone who needs it"?

Seli writes the sign. They attach it to the bench. It is too large and blocks the view, so they move it to a post.

NARA: The bench is repaired.

LUMA: The path is clear.

ODO: The shade is partial.

PIRI: And who owns the bench?

Seli points to the park sign.

SELI: The town.

The candidates ask the squirrel to leave the bench so the broadcast can finish. It stays still, grooming its tail.

Seli turns to the camera.

SELI: The voters have made their choice.

PIRI: We did not count votes.

NARA: We do not need to.

Odo looks at the squirrel, then moves to sit on the grass.

ODO: The seat is taken.

Piri asks the squirrel to leave a minute for the closing credits.

PIRI: The camera is still rolling.

ODO: The bench is not.

### Tag

[THE SQUIRREL HOPS BACK INTO ITS TREE. THE CANDIDATES RUSH TO THE VACANT BENCH.]

PIRI: We have reached a consensus.

ODO: There is only room for two.

NARA: Luma can sit on the grass.

LUMA: The bench is for everyone.

They all try to sit at once. The bench creaks but holds.

[SFX: WOOD CREAK.]

PIRI: The bench has cast the deciding vote.

[SFX: APPLAUSE SIGN, BIRDS.]
