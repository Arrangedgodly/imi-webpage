# The Night Shift at Banyan Clinic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: high-stakes hospital dramas and overnight emergency-room episodes*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: During one stormy night, the Banyan Clinic staff treat a patient whose symptoms keep changing whenever he hears the word "rest."

Cast: Dr. Sefa, a chimpanzee physician; Nurse Luli, a capuchin; Dr. Arlo, an orangutan surgeon; Pogo, a young macaque patient; and Marn, a baboon orderly.

## Episode: "Code Banana"

### Cold open

INT. BANYAN CLINIC, NIGHT

[Rain rattles the leaf roof. The emergency bell rings. Luli wheels in Pogo, who is wrapped in a blanket and holding a branch.]

SEFA: What happened?

LULI: He says he can't put the branch down.

POGO: If I let go, the room will tilt.

ARLO: The room is level.

POGO: You are standing on the wall.

ARLO: I'm standing on the floor.

MARN: The floor is where the chart says it is.

SEFA: That's not a diagnosis.

LULI: His paws are gripping so hard his fingers have filed themselves into the bark.

POGO: It's a good branch.

SEFA: Put him in exam three. And nobody say "rest."

POGO: Why?

SEFA: We don't know yet.

[The monitors beep in a dramatic rhythm. Pogo squeezes the branch.]

### Act One

INT. EXAM THREE

[Arlo studies the branch while Sefa checks Pogo's pulse. Luli writes on a chart that keeps sticking to her elbow.]

ARLO: His pulse is steady.

SEFA: His grip is not.

LULI: I asked him to relax and he climbed onto the lamp.

POGO: The lamp was moving.

MARN: It was hanging from a cord.

SEFA: When did this start?

POGO: At the gym. The coach said, "Take a rest."

LULI: And?

POGO: I heard "take a nest."

ARLO: You built a nest on the climbing frame?

POGO: I improved the frame.

SEFA: The coach asked him to stop. Pogo heard "top."

LULI: He's been climbing every surface since lunch.

ARLO: His muscles are exhausted.

POGO: They can be exhausted after I finish this branch.

SEFA: We need a safe place to loosen his grip.

MARN: The waiting room has a padded chair.

POGO: The chair has no branch.

LULI: I can attach a small branch to the chair.

SEFA: That would make it a branch chair.

ARLO: It is a better chair.

### Act Two

INT. WAITING ROOM

[Luli ties a short branch across a padded chair. Pogo tests it with one hand. The chair rolls away.]

POGO: It is moving.

MARN: It has wheels.

SEFA: Hold the chair.

[Arlo holds the chair. Pogo grips the branch. The chair slowly spins.]

LULI: His hands are still locked.

ARLO: What if we ask him to release one finger at a time?

POGO: I can do that.

[He releases one finger, then immediately uses it to pull the chair closer.]

SEFA: Progress.

LULI: The chart says he was at the gym for six hours.

POGO: I was training for the branch relay.

MARN: The branch relay is tomorrow.

POGO: I know. I was starting early.

ARLO: Your shoulders need a break.

POGO: Don't say it.

ARLO: I said "break."

POGO: The branch is breaking?

SEFA: Nobody said the branch was breaking.

[The branch gives a tiny creak. Everyone freezes.]

LULI: That was the chair.

MARN: It was the branch.

SEFA: Code Banana!

MARN: We have no banana.

ARLO: I called it for the monitor.

[The monitor flashes a close-up of a tiny splinter.]

LULI: The patient has a splinter.

ARLO: The branch has a splinter. The patient is holding it.

SEFA: This is why we need the specialists.

MARN: I am the specialist. I brought tweezers.

[He takes out an enormous pair of kitchen tongs.]

SEFA: We need a different approach. Pogo, what happens if you hold something softer?

POGO: It won't support me.

ARLO: It only needs to support your hand.

LULI: I have a rolled blanket.

[She offers the blanket. Pogo takes it with his free hand, then wraps it around the branch.]

SEFA: That is not what I meant.

POGO: Now the branch is comfortable.

MARN: The patient has improved the hospital equipment.

### Act Three

INT. EXAM THREE, LATER

[Sefa stands at the end of Pogo's bed. Arlo adjusts a lamp for a heroic close-up. Luli brings the padded chair.]

SEFA: The diagnosis is branch lock.

ARLO: I diagnosed a branch-related grip.

SEFA: I named it.

ARLO: My diagnosis had more syllables.

LULI: The chair from the waiting room has arrived.

MARN: It arrived without a patient.

[The wheeled chair glides past the doorway again.]

POGO: It's taking the branch.

SEFA: Nurse, intercept that chair.

[Luli catches it with her foot. Arlo reaches for the branch. Pogo holds tighter.]

ARLO: We need a dramatic release.

SEFA: We need a pillow.

MARN: I have one.

[He presents a tiny pillow, sized for a doll.]

POGO: That is not for my head.

MARN: It is for the branch.

[Luli places the branch across the pillow on the chair. Pogo's fingers uncurl to keep the pillow from sliding.]

LULI: He has released it.

SEFA: Call the family.

ARLO: He is the family.

POGO: I can still hold the branch.

SEFA: You can. The question is whether the branch can hold you.

[The monitor shows the chair rolling away in slow motion.]

MARN: The answer is no.

SEFA: We have our final image.

POGO: Can I have a regular pillow now?

ARLO: Two.

MARN: The chart says one.

SEFA: Put the second pillow in the close-up.

### Tag

INT. HALLWAY, DAWN

[Marn returns the padded chair. It rolls past Pogo's room, carrying the branch like a patient.]

MARN: The branch is ready for its checkup.

POGO: Does it have a chart?

LULI: It has a very good grip.

ARLO: Let's not start another shift.

[The chair stops at the nurses' station. A tiny bell rings.]

[The chair sits at the nurses station beside sleeping Pogo.]
