# Who Moved the Penguin Cam?

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: wildlife series that treat camera access and narration as more important than the animals' routines*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: A film crew is following a colony of penguins at a public aquarium. Narrator Ralu wants an intimate close-up of one penguin's daily path, while keeper Nima asks the crew to keep its equipment outside the animals' route.

Cast: Ralu, a mandrill narrator; Nima, a macaque aquarium keeper; Jo, a capuchin camera operator; and Kiri, a gibbon educator.

## Episode: "The Long Walk to Lunch"

### Cold Open

[AQUARIUM PENGUIN HABITAT. A PENGUIN WALKS FROM A ROCK TO A FEEDING STATION. JO TRACKS IT WITH A CAMERA.] 

RALU (V.O.): Against all odds, the explorer crosses the continent.

NIMA: It is a ten-meter walk to lunch.

[JO'S TRIPOD STANDS IN THE CLEAR ROUTE. NIMA MOVES IT BEHIND A BARRIER.]

JO: The angle is worse from here.

NIMA: The path is clear from here.

[KIRI SHOWS THE CREW A SMALL MARKER WHERE THE PENGUINS' ROUTE MEETS THE FEEDING AREA. JO PLACES A SECOND CAMERA THERE, OUTSIDE THE BARRIER.] 

KIRI: This angle records the walk without blocking it.

RALU (V.O.): The observer has withdrawn to the perimeter.

[JO'S MONITOR SHOWS THE PENGUIN CROSSING THE FRAME. RALU ASKS FOR A LONGER SHOT; JO SAYS THE BATTERY WILL LAST IF THEY STOP MOVING THE TRIPOD.] 

NIMA: The animal does not need a second take.

### Act One

[THE CREW SETS A LONG LENS BESIDE A GLASS PANEL. JO ADDS A FAKE ROCK TO HIDE THE CAMERA.] 

KIRI: That rock is part of the exhibit sign.

JO: It makes a natural frame.

[THE SIGN TILTS AND SLIDES DOWN THE GLASS. A STAFF MEMBER CATCHES IT.] 

NIMA: The penguins use that area for feeding.

RALU (V.O.): The camera has disturbed the landscape.

JO: I can move back.

[JO MOVES BACK. THE CAMERA CABLE CROSSES THE WALKWAY. KIRI POINTS TO THE CABLE COVER.] 

KIRI: Run it under the mat.

[THE CREW REPOSITIONS THE CABLE. THE PENGUIN RETURNS TO THE ROCK AND WALKS THE SAME ROUTE.] 

### Act Two

[RALU RECORDS A NEW NARRATION: "THE PENGUIN HESITATES, UNSURE OF ITS DESTINY." THE PENGUIN STOPS TO SHAKE WATER FROM ITS FEATHERS.] 

NIMA: It is drying off.

JO: Can we wait for the step?

NIMA: You can wait from behind the barrier.

[THE CAMERA ROLLS. THE PENGUIN WALKS TO THE FEEDING STATION AND EATS. RALU'S VOICE OVER DESCRIBES A RISKY FORAGING MISSION. KIRI POINTS TO THE FEEDING SCHEDULE POSTED ON THE WALL.]

[THE PENGUIN FINISHES AND WALKS BACK. RALU STARTS TO CALL IT A RETURN JOURNEY; NIMA HOLDS UP THE ROUTINE CARE BOARD, WHICH SHOWS THE SAME ROUTE FOR EVERY FEEDING.] 

RALU (V.O.): It returns along the path it came by.

JO: We have that on one battery.

[JO PUTS THE SPARE BATTERY BACK IN THE CASE. THE CAMERA OPERATOR'S OWN SHADOW CROSSES THE GLASS, AND THE CREW STEPS BACK ANOTHER METER.]

[THE PENGUIN PAUSES AT THE FEEDING STATION. NIMA WAITS UNTIL IT MOVES AWAY BEFORE SHE CROSSES TO THE CARE BOARD. JO KEEPS THE CAMERA ON THE ANIMAL, WITHOUT FOLLOWING NIMA INTO ITS PATH.] 

KIRI: We have the whole visit.

RALU (V.O.): The crew has remained outside the route.

KIRI: It is lunch time.

[JO TRIES A LOW ANGLE THROUGH THE GLASS. THE CAMERA PICKS UP THE REFLECTION OF THE CREW INSTEAD OF THE PENGUIN. RALU'S NARRATION CALLS THE REFLECTION A SHADOWED RIVAL.] 

NIMA: That is Jo's jacket.

JO: I will use the side panel.

### Act Three

[THE CREW SETS THE CAMERA OUTSIDE THE ROUTE AND RECORDS THE PENGUIN'S NORMAL WALK, FEED, AND RETURN TO THE ROCK. RALU READS A PLAINER VOICE-OVER.] 

RALU (V.O.): The penguin walks to the feeding station, eats, and rests on the warm rock.

KIRI: That is the behavior we observed.

[JO PLAYS BACK THE FOOTAGE. THE FIRST CLIP SHOWS THE TRIPOD IN THE WAY. THE NEW CLIP SHOWS THE CLEAR PATH. NIMA KEEPS THE FIRST CLIP AS A REMINDER FOR NEXT TIME.] 

JO: We can frame the animal without framing ourselves.

[RALU RECORDS ONE MORE TAKE. A CREW MEMBER SNEEZES OFF CAMERA. THE PENGUIN TURNS ITS HEAD TOWARD THE SOUND. RALU'S VOICE OVER STARTS A GRAND LINE, THEN STOPS.]

RALU (V.O.): The explorer has noticed the crew.

[NIMA WAITS UNTIL THE CAMERA CREW MOVES BEHIND THE BARRIER. THE PENGUIN RETURNS TO ITS ROCK. JO REVIEWS THE SHOT AND FINDS THAT THE GLASS REFLECTION IS GONE.] 

JO: We got the routine.

KIRI: And left the route clear.

### Tag

[THE CAMERA RECORDS THE EMPTY HABITAT AFTER THE PENGUINS MOVE OUT OF VIEW. THE NARRATOR WHISPERS.] 

RALU (V.O.): The landscape remains.

NIMA: The penguins are around the corner.
