# The News Panel Has One Chair

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cable news panels, urgent banners, pundit cross-talk, and hosts who bring on experts without enough seats*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A local news panel debates a mysterious park sound, but its guests keep changing positions as the studio runs out of chairs and facts.

Cast: Anchor Luma, a ring-tailed lemur; field reporter Odo, a capuchin; sound expert Neri, an orangutan; resident witness Tavi, a gibbon; and segment producer Kett, a macaque.

## Episode: "Breaking at the Bandstand"

### Cold Open

[NEWS DESK. A RED BANNER READS: PARK SOUND SHOCKS CITY.]

LUMA: At this hour, a noise was heard near the bandstand.

NERI: It was a woodpecker.

Luma looks into camera as if the answer has not yet aired.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

Kett introduces the panel: a sound expert, a witness, and a reporter at the park. There are only two chairs at the desk. Neri and Tavi sit; Luma stays standing, then is handed a small stool. The banner changes to WOODPECKER? EXPERTS DIVIDED.

NERI: The rhythm is consistent with pecking.

TAVI: It sounded like someone knocking on a door.

Odo reports from beside the bandstand. A woodpecker has been tapping a hollow post. He holds up a phone recording. Kett asks him to replay it later so the studio can discuss the sound first. Odo stands beside the post while a woodpecker continues tapping behind him.

Luma asks whether the city should investigate. Neri says the parks crew can inspect the post for damage. Tavi says she heard the same sound last week and assumed the band was practicing. The lower third reads: BAND PRACTICE OR BIRD BUSINESS?

### Act Two

Kett brings in another guest by video call: the parks supervisor, who confirms there is no emergency. The studio feed freezes on his face while he says, "It is a bird." Luma thanks him, but the screen remains frozen, showing his mouth open. Kett says the video is still live. Luma turns away from it; the supervisor reappears and says she can turn back.

Odo inspects the post on camera. He finds a small crack in the wood, so the park staff will put a marker around it and repair it before the next concert. Kett changes the banner to CRACK IN POST: DANGER? Neri says a repair is sensible, but the panel must distinguish routine maintenance from danger. Luma reads the banner and asks whether the bird is a suspect.

TAVI: The bird was at work.

NERI: It has no known connection to the crack.

Kett asks each guest to face a different camera. Odo points toward the park feed, Tavi turns to the anchor, and Neri looks directly at the lens. The screen divides into three close-ups, each showing a guest looking somewhere else. Luma asks whether the panel can answer one question together. Their microphones switch on one at a time, so every answer begins after the next person has taken the floor.

Luma asks for a timeline. Odo explains that the pecking started after a seed lodged in the post. He uses a twig to remove it, and the sound stops. The studio listens to the silence. Kett cues an urgent music bed over it, making the studio sound like an approaching storm.

LUMA: The noise has stopped, but the story continues.

Neri asks what the story is now. Kett hands her a card: "What residents should know." She says residents should avoid the marked post until the repair crew finishes. Tavi asks if the birds need a public statement. Kett makes a new graphic: WOODPECKER SILENT - WHAT NEXT?

The studio chair rolls away under Luma. She catches it with her tail. Kett calls the chair movement breaking news, and the banner switches to ANCHOR IN MOTION. Luma stays seated on the floor, still reading the update. The crew brings her a stable chair, but it belongs to the remote guest and has his name taped to it.

### Act Three

The parks supervisor is back on video. He confirms the post is marked and the repair is scheduled for the afternoon. The woodpecker has moved to a nearby tree. Neri repeats that the sound came from pecking, not a signal or explosion. The panel now agrees on the facts, so Kett asks each guest to make a final prediction.

TAVI: The repair crew will fix the post.

ODO: The bird will tap somewhere else.

LUMA: We will keep watching this story.

Kett starts to raise the banner, but Luma lowers it. Instead, she displays a simple caption: "Post marked for repair; no danger reported." The guests leave the set. A final tap comes from the desk: Kett is knocking to get the camera operator's attention.

LUMA: We have another sound.

NERI: It is a producer.

### Tag

[CONTROL ROOM.]

Kett orders the banner removed. The graphics operator accidentally displays "PRODUCER CONFIRMED." Kett taps the monitor. The caption changes to "PRODUCER STILL TAPPING."

[CUT TO BLACK.]
