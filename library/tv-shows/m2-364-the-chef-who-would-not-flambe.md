# The Chef Who Would Not Flambe

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: elimination cooking contests, surprise ingredients, countdown edits, and judges demanding spectacle*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A cooking show's final challenge becomes a battle between a careful capuchin chef and producers who keep trying to make the soup catch fire.

Cast: Rumi, a capuchin chef; Bex, a macaque rival; Host Talo, a baboon; Judge Iva, an orangutan; Director Niko, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - NIGHT

Five minutes remain. Talo sweeps into frame holding a covered basket.

TALO: Your secret ingredient is... the ingredient we did not tell you about.

He lifts the lid. Pumpkins roll across the counter. Rumi catches one with her tail; Bex traps two under a pot.

BEX: Is the basket supposed to be this lively?

RUMI: They were stacked on a ramp.

NIKO (IN EARPIECE): Better. Get a pumpkin to roll toward the judges.

ACT ONE

Talo describes the challenge: serve a warm dish using pumpkin, oats and pantry basics. Niko's camera circles the contestants as though they are facing a duel.

TALO: The clock begins when the music hits.

RUMI: The clock is already running.

TALO: The music will catch up.

Bex chops pumpkin quickly, tapping her knife like a drum solo. Rumi slices evenly and starts simmering oats in a pot. Niko signals for a close-up of a knife. The camera operator gives Rumi's gently bubbling pot the dramatic zoom instead.

NIKO: Why is it not sizzling?

RUMI: It is soup.

Bex asks for a flare torch. The assistant presents a hand torch for melting sugar. Bex refuses it and builds a decorative pumpkin stack. Talo narrates as the stack wobbles.

TALO: A tower of ambition.

BEX: A stack of garnish.

The stack collapses into Bex's bowl. Judge Iva looks up from her tasting table.

ACT TWO

The producers add a new card: "Surprise twist: the judges will taste in sixty seconds." The actual clock reads four minutes. Talo announces a sudden time change. Rumi lowers the heat and stirs. Bex puts her bowl in the oven and asks if the judges can taste from there.

IVA: I will not put my face in an oven.

NIKO: Ask them to explain their inspiration.

TALO: What is your inspiration, Rumi?

RUMI: Pumpkin and oats taste good together.

TALO: Can you make that more personal?

RUMI: I was hungry.

Niko cues a rain sound effect. No rain is in the kitchen. Talo waves his hand for thunder. A thunderclap plays as Rumi adds salt. She jumps; the spoon flies and lands in the sink.

RUMI: I need a clean spoon.

NIKO: Stay in the shot.

RUMI: I need a clean spoon in the shot.

The assistant brings one. Bex's oven timer rings. She pulls out a hot tray; steam fogs the lens. Talo calls it a dramatic reveal, but the camera operator wipes the glass while the timer clock keeps counting down.

ACT TWO-B

Talo announces the judges will score aroma, texture and presentation. Niko sets a small pedestal on each station to make the plates appear taller. Rumi's bowl wobbles atop it. She slides it back down onto the counter.

RUMI: It is soup. It should sit on a table.

BEX: My garnish stack might hold a bowl.

She tests it. The pumpkin stack collapses again, this time in slow motion on the replay monitor. Iva tastes Bex's pumpkin-and-oat cakes. They are cooked, though the tallest one has a visible toothpick.

IVA: The top cake is held up by equipment.

BEX: It was leaning.

IVA: I am judging food, not architecture.

Rumi brings her bowl. Iva tastes. Talo asks whether the aroma transports her. Iva takes another spoonful.

IVA: It transports me to the next bite.

Niko signals for more steam. A technician brings a kettle. Iva asks him to take it away; Rumi's soup is already hot. He backs up, carrying the kettle like a trophy.

ACT THREE

Iva awards the challenge to Rumi for even texture and balanced seasoning. Bex accepts the result, then points out that her cakes stayed upright only because of the toothpick. Iva names that an avoidable garnish hazard and asks her to remove it from the serving plate.

BEX: Can I keep the toothpick for the walk back?

TALO: We have a winner and a safety note.

NIKO: Can we redo the announcement with a bigger reaction?

RUMI: I can lift the spoon.

She lifts the spoon. It drips soup on the counter. A camera rushes in. Rumi hands the crew a towel, then serves the remaining pot to Bex and Iva. Talo announces one winner and one shared bowl.

TAG

The credits begin. Niko tries to roll a pumpkin down the counter for the closing shot. It stops against the towel.

NIKO: Can we get the roll again?

RUMI: You can eat it.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew shares the cooked pumpkin while the dramatic elimination music plays over a very ordinary spoon being washed.
