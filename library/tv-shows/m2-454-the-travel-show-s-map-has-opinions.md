# The Travel Show's Map Has Opinions

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel documentaries, overconfident hosts, and cinematic route changes that ignore the actual ferry schedule*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Kavi, a gibbon; local guide Eno, an orangutan; camera operator Seli, a macaque; producer Rami, a baboon; ferry clerk Paku, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

EXT. HARBOR WALK - MORNING

Kavi presents a folded map to the camera. Behind him, a ferry pulls away from the dock.

KAVI: Our journey begins with the last ferry of the morning.

ENO: It left five minutes ago.

RAMI: Can we say it is waiting for us dramatically?

PAKU: It is on the water, following its route.

SELI: I have the departure in frame.

[Kavi turns the map upside down and points toward a hill.] 

KAVI: Then our journey takes the scenic high road.

ENO: That road ends at a locked maintenance gate.

KAVI: Scenic and exclusive.

[The gate sign reads CLOSED. The opening theme begins and stops abruptly.] 

ACT ONE

EXT. DOCK - LATER

Paku shows the next ferry time on a posted schedule. Eno traces the walking route to a public lookout while Rami proposes an insert shot of the missed boat.

RAMI: Viewers need to feel our loss.

ENO: We can feel it from here. The next ferry is at eleven.

KAVI: What if we race along the coast and meet it there?

PAKU: It stops at the same dock. The coast path does not reach the island.

[The host walks to a sign showing the island route. The map is upside down; the camera operator rotates the map, not the camera.] 

SELI: This compass mark is pointing south.

KAVI: I thought the arrow was the destination.

ENO: It marks north. The destination is the dot. The dock is the square.

[The host asks the producer to add a dotted line from the square to the dot. Rami draws a line through a cliff symbol. Eno points out the actual footpath along the shore.] 

ENO: The line goes around the headland. We can walk it in twenty minutes.

RAMI: Can we make that feel remote?

ENO: The path has a handrail and benches.

KAVI: A comfortable expedition.

[They set off. Seli keeps the camera on the marked path. Kavi pauses at each bench to deliver a dramatic reflection.]

ACT TWO

EXT. SHORE PATH - MIDMORNING

Kavi and Eno reach a split in the path. One sign points to the ferry dock; another points to the lookout. The host tries to improvise a route through a closed bird nesting area. Eno stops him before he leaves the path.

ENO: We stay on the marked route. The nesting area is closed.

KAVI: Could the camera zoom through the fence?

SELI: It can show the sign. It cannot show a route we are not taking.

RAMI: We could narrate the forbidden path as if we walked it.

ENO: Then it would be a fiction segment.

[The producer considers this, then sets the script aside. Kavi reads the public sign and describes the lookout's view from the path. A gull calls above them; Seli pans upward but keeps the camera steady.] 

KAVI: Here we see a bird using the sky.

ENO: And we are using the path.

[The camera battery warning appears. Seli switches to a spare battery. Rami asks the host to repeat the last line with more wonder. Kavi repeats it while seated on a bench, looking at the real harbor.] 

KAVI: A mapped route can still surprise you.

ENO: The sign told us where it went.

ACT THREE

EXT. FERRY DOCK - LATE MORNING

They return to the dock before the next departure. Paku confirms that the ferry will board in three minutes. Kavi folds the map along its printed creases. The host asks the camera to frame the boat behind him without standing in the boarding lane.

RAMI: We have the emotional arrival and a departure shot.

SELI: One shot. Then we board.

KAVI: Our expedition ends where it began.

ENO: The walk was the journey. The ferry is transportation.

[The ferry horn sounds. Rami wants a second take of the horn for drama. Paku points to the boarding line. Kavi lowers the map and joins it.]

KAVI: We have reached our destination on foot and our next destination by ferry.

PAKU: That is what the schedule says.

[They board with the crew. Kavi checks the map and hands it to Eno to fold correctly. The camera records the shoreline receding from the safe deck.] 

TAG

Rami asks if the missed ferry can be added as a flashback. Seli points to the footage already recorded. It shows the ferry departing, and the group choosing the marked path.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew rides the scheduled ferry as the map rests north-up on Kavi's lap.
