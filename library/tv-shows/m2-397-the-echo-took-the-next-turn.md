# The Echo Took the Next Turn

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised science demonstrations, enthusiastic hosts, oversized graphics, and experiments interrupted by the sponsor clock*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A science host tries to demonstrate an echo, but the studio delay repeats her instructions before the experiment can finish.

Cast: Host Rena, a gibbon; lab assistant Pavo, a baboon; student guest Tavi, a capuchin; graphics operator Seli, a macaque; producer Omi, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. SCIENCE STUDIO - DAY

Rena holds a foam ball beside a microphone. She says, "Echo." The studio speakers repeat, "Echo, echo, echo."

RENA: That was not the experiment.

PAVO: The delay is set to three seconds.

TAVI: It answered you.

ACT ONE

Rena introduces the demonstration: sound waves reflect off a hard surface and return to the listener. The microphone is aimed at a padded wall. She says a word. Nothing repeats. Pavo switches the speaker monitor on; it repeats her word immediately.

RENA: That is the studio monitor, not an echo.

OMI: The segment clock is running.

Tavi asks how far away a wall needs to be for an echo to be heard. Rena explains that the sound must travel out and back with enough delay to be distinct. Seli puts an animated arrow on screen. The arrow moves from Rena to the wall, then circles back through the camera.

TAVI: It went behind the camera.

SELI: The path needed to fit in the frame.

ACT TWO

Pavo wheels in a hard panel. Rena stands at a marked distance and claps once. A clear clap returns. Tavi measures the delay with a stopwatch. The studio speakers add a second, third, and fourth clap. Rena stops.

RENA: Turn off the monitor delay.

PAVO: It is off.

Seli checks the sound board. A prerecorded applause button is stuck down. The claps continue in a neat loop. Tavi reaches for the button, but Seli asks him to leave the controls to the crew. Pavo unplugs the applause pad. It stops.

OMI: Can we have one clean clap?

Rena claps once. The hard panel returns one distinct clap. Tavi smiles and claps again. The echo follows. Omi starts the sponsor clock, which displays a countdown from sixty seconds.

ACT TWO-B

Tavi asks whether soft surfaces absorb sound. Rena brings a fabric curtain over the panel. She claps; the echo is faint. Pavo adds a second curtain. The room becomes quiet. Omi says the audience must still hear them, so they take the cloth away. Seli's sound graphic shows a wave bouncing repeatedly between the panel and a drawing of a curtain.

RENA: The drawing is not the test.

SELI: The camera cannot see sound.

TAVI: It can see the curtains.

Rena asks Tavi to stand behind the panel and clap once. The sound returns clearly. Tavi moves to the side, where it is softer. Pavo moves the panel to keep both in frame. It blocks the host. Rena speaks from behind it.

RENA: Can you still hear me?

TAVI: Yes.

The speakers repeat, "Yes, yes." Omi looks at the audio desk. Seli points to the small delay meter; it is active again. Pavo traces the cable and finds it plugged into the graphics monitor. He disconnects it. The repeated word stops.

RENA: We should check one more surface.

TAVI: The hard panel or the curtain?

RENA: The empty corridor.

They move the microphone to the doorway. Rena says "hello" once. The sound returns after a short pause. The studio monitor remains silent. Pavo writes down the interval with a stopwatch, but the graphic reads the digits backward and overlays them across Rena's face.

SELI: I can flip the timer.

RENA: Please leave the number off the screen.

Omi asks if they can end with a fast recap. Seli advances the arrows too quickly; the wave flies off the screen and reappears behind the host. Tavi points to the actual corridor, where the sound has already faded.

TAVI: That is where it went.

OMI: We have enough footage.

ACT THREE

Rena asks Tavi to say one word toward the hard panel. He says, "Hello." One echo returns. He turns toward the padded wall and repeats the word. There is no distinct echo. Seli removes the animated arrows from the screen. Omi lets the clock run out without adding a final graphic.

RENA: We heard the sound return from the hard panel.

TAVI: The curtain softened it.

PAVO: The speakers repeated it when the delay was on.

RENA: And that was not the wall.

The studio applauds once. The applause pad remains unplugged. Rena asks the crew to keep it that way until the next experiment.

TAG

Tavi leaves the studio and calls back, "Goodbye." A faint echo returns from the corridor. He turns around, but no one is there.

FINAL IMAGE: Rena points at the empty corridor while the graphics screen labels the return sound "CORRIDOR," with no arrows.
