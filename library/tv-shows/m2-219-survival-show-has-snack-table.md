# The Survival Show Has a Snack Table

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival competitions that manufacture hardship while crew support sits just outside the frame*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A survival-game host orders contestants to forage for lunch while the production crew's catered table keeps appearing in the background.

Cast: Host Kiri, a macaque; contestants Ralo, a gibbon, and Mena, a capuchin; field medic Omi, a baboon; and crew cook Sela, a lemur.

Episode: "Lunch in the Wild"

COLD OPEN

EXT. FILMING CLEARING - DAY

Ralo and Mena stand beside an empty cooking pot. Kiri faces the camera.

KIRI: The contestants must find food using only what the forest provides.

[BEHIND HER, SELA OPENS A COOLER. A STACK OF SANDWICHES IS VISIBLE.]

MENA: What is in the cooler?

KIRI: That is crew support.

RALO: We are hungry.

ACT ONE

Kiri hands the contestants a map. Omi checks their water bottles.

KIRI: You have thirty minutes to forage.

MENA: Are we allowed to pick the berries?

OMI: Only the marked safe plants.

RALO: Where are the markers?

[THE WIND BLOWS TWO MARKER FLAGS OVER. SELA RUNS TO CATCH THEM.]

KIRI: The forest is unpredictable.

SELA: The flags are on stakes.

MENA: That seems predictable.

KIRI: You can use the map.

RALO: The map marks the snack table.

[THE MAP HAS A SMALL ICON LABELED "PRODUCTION BASE." IT IS NEXT TO THE CLEARING.]

ACT TWO

EXT. TRAIL - LATER

The contestants find edible leaves and a fallen seed pod. Kiri follows with a camera crew. Omi checks the leaves.

OMI: These are safe after washing. The pod is empty.

MENA: We can make a salad.

RALO: We need water to wash it.

KIRI: The challenge is to find a water source.

[THE CREW WALKS PAST A COOLER. A TAPED SIGN READS "DRINKING WATER." KIRI BLOCKS THE CAMERA.]

KIRI: That is for crew.

OMI: Contestants need water too.

SELA: I can bring a bottle from the base.

KIRI: The challenge is about survival skills.

RALO: Washing leaves is a survival skill.

[OMI HANDS THEM TWO WATER BOTTLES.]

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CLEARING - AFTERNOON

The contestants prepare a small salad at the pot. Sela serves sandwiches to the crew behind a screen. A wrapper blows into frame.

MENA: Our leaves are ready.

RALO: They need a plate.

KIRI: You can make one from a leaf.

[THEY PLACE THE SALAD ON A BROAD LEAF. THE LEAF FOLDS UNDER THE WEIGHT.]

MENA: The plate has collapsed.

OMI: Use the bowl.

KIRI: That is the challenge pot.

RALO: We can eat from it.

[SELA'S COOLER LID SLAMS. EVERYONE LOOKS.]

KIRI: Please stay focused.

MENA: We are focused on lunch.

ACT THREE

EXT. CLEARING - LATE AFTERNOON

Omi lays out the washed leaves and seed pod. The cooler stands behind the crew tent. Kiri holds the challenge scorecard.

KIRI: Each meal needs one found ingredient and one cooking method.

MENA: We found leaves and washed them.

RALO: The pod is empty, but it can hold the leaves.

OMI: You may use the pot. The fire remains supervised.

[RALO SETS THE POT ON THE CAMP STOVE. SELA HANDS HIM A SMALL PAN, THEN HIDES A STACK OF PLATES BEHIND THE TENT.]

KIRI: Production is not allowed to help.

SELA: I am helping myself. I am crew.

MENA: That seems like a useful distinction.

OMI: The contestants are allowed safe equipment. This is not a test of eating random plants.

KIRI: We want to show resourcefulness.

RALO: We are making a warm leaf dish with the ingredients we found.

[THE STOVE FLAME GOES OUT. SELA STEPS FORWARD WITH A MATCH, THEN KIRI HOLDS UP A HAND.]

KIRI: That is crew support.

OMI: I can relight the stove safely.

MENA: The crew can keep the sandwiches.

SELA: I would like that.

EXT. FILMING CLEARING - FINAL CHALLENGE

Kiri asks the contestants to present their meal. They show the washed leaves and empty pod. Omi confirms the food is safe.

KIRI: You have survived the lunch challenge.

RALO: We found leaves and water.

MENA: The crew found sandwiches.

KIRI: They are working.

SELA: So are the contestants.

[SELA OFFERS THE CONTESTANTS THE UNUSED SANDWICHES. KIRI LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.]

KIRI: The challenge has ended. Everyone may eat.

RALO: Do we still have to forage tomorrow?

OMI: Tomorrow's task is a supervised trail walk.

MENA: Will there be a snack table?

SELA: The crew will have one.

KIRI: We will label it clearly this time.

SELA: The label will say "snacks."

OMI: That is the first sensible rule today.

TAG

Kiri holds up the map. The "PRODUCTION BASE" icon is now circled.

KIRI: Tomorrow's challenge is finding the camera crew.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO THE SNACK TABLE.]
