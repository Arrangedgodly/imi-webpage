# The Flashback That Ate Tuesday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sitcom flashback episodes that reuse old scenes to avoid resolving the current plot*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: The residents of a treehouse clubhouse are trying to fix a stuck hatch before their meeting begins. Producer Kiki keeps inserting old flashbacks whenever the characters approach the solution, until the audience remembers more about last season's snack table than the current door.

Cast: Jojo, a macaque club president; Fera, a lemur carpenter; Kiki, a capuchin producer; and Matu, a gorilla treasurer.

## Episode: "Remember the Great Chair?"

### Cold Open

[CLUBHOUSE. JOJO PULLS THE HATCH. IT DOES NOT MOVE.]

JOJO: The meeting starts in ten minutes.

[THE SCREEN FREEZES. A GRAPHIC READS "THREE WEEKS EARLIER." THE CAST SITS AROUND A SNACK TABLE.] 

KIKI (V.O.): Remember when the door worked?

FERA: It was a different door.

[THE FLASHBACK ENDS. THEY ARE BACK AT THE STUCK HATCH.] 

MATU: We have lost two minutes.

### Act One

[FERA CHECKS THE HINGE. IT IS PINNED BY A WEDGED FOLDING CHAIR.] 

FERA: The chair is caught under the hatch.

JOJO: We can move it from the side.

[KIKI CUES A FLASHBACK: THE SAME CHAIR AT LAST YEAR'S PICNIC, HOLDING DOWN A TABLECLOTH.] 

MATU: That chair was never at the clubhouse.

KIKI: It is the same model.

[THE FLASHBACK CHAIR SPINS. KIKI ADDS A LAUGH TRACK. THE CAMERA RETURNS TO THE PRESENT. THE HATCH STILL DOES NOT MOVE.]

JOJO: Can we lift the actual chair?

FERA: On three.

[THEY LIFT THE HATCH. KIKI FREEZES THE IMAGE BEFORE THE CHAIR SLIDES OUT.]

KIKI (V.O.): But remember when Fera found the toolbox?

[FLASHBACK: FERA OPENS THE TOOLBOX. IT IS EMPTY. MATU HOLDS UP THE SCREWDRIVER, WHICH IS IN HIS HAND.] 

MATU: That was six months ago.

KIKI: It explains why the toolbox is empty.

[BACK IN THE PRESENT, FERA USES HER OWN SCREWDRIVER TO REMOVE THE WEDGED CHAIR. JOJO OPENS THE HATCH. KIKI TRIES TO FREEZE THE FRAME AGAIN.] 

JOJO: The door is open now.

KIKI: I know. I need a transition.

### Act Two

[THEY LIFT THE HATCH SLIGHTLY. THE CHAIR SLIDES FREE. KIKI FREEZES THE PICTURE AGAIN.] 

KIKI (V.O.): But before they opened it, they had to choose a snack.

[FLASHBACK: MATU HOLDS TWO EMPTY BOWLS. JOJO REACHES FOR ONE. THE IMAGE FREEZES.] 

MATU: That was a budget meeting.

KIKI: The bowls made it fun.

[BACK IN THE PRESENT, THE HATCH LOWERS. FERA MOVES THE CHAIR AWAY. JOJO TESTS THE HANDLE. IT OPENS.] 

JOJO: There. We are in.

[KIKI CUES ANOTHER FLASHBACK. THE SCREEN SHOWS A PAST BIRTHDAY CAKE. FERA WALKS IN FRONT OF THE PROJECTOR, BLOCKING IT.] 

FERA: The hinge needs oil before anyone uses the hatch again.

MATU: I have the maintenance line in the budget.

[KIKI HOLDS UP A CARD: "ONE LAST FLASHBACK." JOJO TAKES IT AND WRITES "AFTER THE MEETING." ]

[THE SCREEN BEGINS TO PLAY THE OLD SNACK-TABLE SCENE. MATU UNPLUGS THE PROJECTOR. THE FREEZE FRAME HOLDS ON A BOWL OF EMPTY NUTS.] 

MATU: The snacks are not part of today's minutes.

KIKI: They were popular.

FERA: The meeting is happening.

### Act Three

[THE MEMBERS ENTER THE CLUBHOUSE. THE HATCH OPENS AND CLOSES SMOOTHLY. KIKI PLAYS A FLASHBACK OF THE CHAIR JAMMING IT.]

JOJO: That happened four minutes ago.

KIKI: A callback.

MATU: It is an accurate record of the obstruction.

[THE MEETING BEGINS. JOJO PUTS THE CHAIR IN THE STORAGE CORNER. KIKI TRIES TO INSERT THE OLD SNACK-TABLE SCENE. FERA SWITCHES OFF THE PROJECTOR.]

FERA: We need the room for the meeting.

KIKI: The audience likes the past.

JOJO: The past can wait until the hatch is fixed.

[KIKI'S EDITOR RUNS IN WITH A NEW FLASHBACK CARD. IT READS "THE TIME THE PROJECTOR BROKE." FERA CHECKS THE PROJECTOR. IT IS ALREADY OFF.] 

FERA: The projector is working.

MATU: It is off because the meeting started.

[KIKI PLUGS THE PROJECTOR BACK IN. THE OLD FLASHBACK FREEZE FRAME OF THE SNACK BOWL RETURNS, NOW PROJECTED ONTO THE CLUB'S NOTICE BOARD AND COVERS THE MEETING AGENDA.] 

JOJO: That is the wrong screen.

[MATU MOVES THE PROJECTOR TO A BLANK WALL. KIKI FINALLY GETS HER CALLBACK; IT SHOWS THE EMPTY AGENDA BEFORE THEY WRITE THE HINGE TASK ON IT.] 

KIKI: That is the beginning of the story.

FERA: It is the agenda.

[MATU READS THE MINUTES. THEY VOTE TO OIL THE HINGE AND MOVE THE CHAIR AFTER EACH EVENT. THE PROJECTOR SCREEN REMAINS BLANK.]

[KIKI LIFTS A HAND TO CUE ANOTHER FLASHBACK, THEN SEES THE MEETING AGENDA. SHE PUTS HER HAND DOWN.]

### Tag

[AFTER THE MEETING, KIKI PLAYS A FLASHBACK OF THE MEETING. IT SHOWS THE CAST WATCHING THE PROJECTOR SCREEN.] 

JOJO: We were in the room.

KIKI: It is a very recent callback.
