# The Holiday Special Must Continue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sentimental holiday television specials, where the host delays the finale until a scripted miracle arrives*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: The community lantern walk is ready to begin, but host Pava keeps delaying it for a scripted miracle and an inspirational speech.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; organizer Nara, macaque; lighting lead Odo, baboon; child guest Seli, capuchin; camera assistant Luma, lemur.

## Episode: "One More Glowing Moment"

### Cold Open

[THE CROWD HOLDS UNLIT LANTERNS.]

NARA: We are ready to start.

PAVA: We need a miracle first.

ODO: We have batteries.

SELI: The lantern is glowing.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE HOST STANDS BEFORE THE CROWD.]

Pava says the special will not begin until a magical light appears. Nara says the lanterns are charged and the route is clear. Odo checks each battery.

PAVA: The audience expects a miracle.

NARA: The audience expects a walk.

LUMA: The cameras are ready.

PAVA: We need a speech about togetherness.

Seli says she can read the route directions. Pava asks her to wait for the right emotional moment.

SELI: The right moment is when people can see.

Odo switches on the lanterns. They glow evenly. Pava says the light is too practical.

### Act Two

[THE CREW TRIES TO STAGE A MIRACLE.]

Luma places a reflective sheet behind the tree. The lantern light shines brighter. Pava calls it a star descending.

NARA: It is a sheet.

ODO: The light is bouncing off it.

SELI: It looks like a bright patch.

Pava asks Seli to say, "I believe." Seli refuses; she says she believes the battery works.

Odo tests the spare battery. It is dead. Pava says the failure can become a dramatic setback.

NARA: We have extras.

PAVA: The story needs darkness before light.

NARA: The crowd is standing in the dark now.

Seli points to the route marker, which is not illuminated. Odo moves a lantern beside it. The path becomes visible.

[THE ORGANIZERS CHECK THE ROUTE MAP.]

Nara finds that one stretch of the route passes beneath low branches. She asks Odo to raise the lanterns so people can see the ground, but the branches catch the handles.

ODO: We need a lower grip.

SELI: Then the light will point at the path.

PAVA: The lights should face the crowd.

NARA: The crowd needs to see where it is walking.

Pava tries holding a lantern high. The beam shines into the camera and blinds Luma's shot.

LUMA: I cannot see the route.

PAVA: The light is beautiful.

LUMA: The lens is white.

Odo adjusts the lantern angle. The light now covers the path without hitting the camera. The crowd follows the marked route. One child stops to tie a shoe; Nara pauses the group until the child is ready.

PAVA: This is a quiet moment.

SELI: It is a shoe lace.

NARA: We do not leave anyone behind.

Pava begins to narrate the pause as a lesson in community, then gets tangled in a low branch. Odo frees her lantern handle.
### Act Three

[THE CROWD BEGINS THE WALK.]

Pava tries to stop them for a speech. Nara says the walk is already underway. Seli leads the group at a safe pace. Odo checks the lanterns as they pass.

PAVA: We have not delivered the inspirational message.

NARA: You can speak while we walk.

Pava begins a speech about shared warmth, but the crowd moves faster than expected. She has to walk to keep up.

PAVA: Together, we move forward-

SELI: Watch the curb.

Pava steps over the curb and continues.

PAVA: Together, we look after one another-

Odo replaces a loose battery. The lantern stays lit.

PAVA: Together, we keep the lights on.

Nara says the event is nearly over. Pava says they need a finale. Seli holds up her lantern; everyone else raises theirs. It is a simple bright row.

Pava reaches the hall with the rest of the group. She turns back to make sure the last lantern has been collected.

PAVA: We have all arrived.

NARA: The walk is complete.

PAVA: The story is not.

SELI: The special is.

LUMA: The doors are closing.

ODO: The batteries are off.

SELI: The light is still in the camera.

Pava looks at the camera and starts another speech, but the power indicator goes dark.

### Tag

[THE WALK ENDS AT THE COMMUNITY HALL.]

PAVA: That was our miracle.

NARA: It was a line of lanterns.

ODO: The batteries worked.

SELI: And we finished on time.

Pava turns to the camera and begins one final speech. The crowd has already gone inside.

PAVA: Together, we-

LUMA: The special has ended.

[SFX: DOOR CLOSES, LANTERNS CLICK OFF.]
