# The Superhero Sidekick Auditions for the Cape

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: superhero franchise casting specials, origin-story auditions, and a sidekick who wants credit for practical hero work*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Producer Vela, an orangutan; hero Captain Comet, a macaque; sidekick Zip, a capuchin; casting director Tavi, a gibbon; stunt coordinator Rafi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. STUDIO STAGE - DAY

A dramatic cape swoops overhead and lands on a chair. Zip points to it.

ZIP: I can wear that.

CAPTAIN COMET: It is still under contract.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The studio is filming a franchise special to cast a new sidekick for Captain Comet's next movie. Producer Vela has invited Zip to audition for the hero's cape, though Zip has spent the previous films designing escape routes and rescuing extras from collapsing sets. Casting director Tavi wants to hear a fresh origin story. Stunt coordinator Rafi wants to test the cape's safety clasp.

VELA: Tell us how Zip got the power to fly.

ZIP: I don't fly. I hold the ladder.

TAVI: That's not an origin story.

ZIP: It is how the balloon rig stopped swaying.

CAPTAIN COMET: Zip has saved me from my own cape three times.

VELA: The audience expects a dramatic reveal.

RAFI: The audience also expects the cape not to catch on the lighting truss.

Rafi checks the clasp and finds the cape's fabric loop is stitched too close to the buckle. Vela asks the camera to frame it as a secret weapon. Rafi says it is a snag hazard and must be fixed before the audition. Zip offers to demonstrate the cape's turn radius without attaching it to a performer.

ZIP: Put the cape on the dummy. I'll walk the marked path.

TAVI: The audition is for acting.

ZIP: Then I will act like I'm walking a marked path.

ACT TWO

Captain Comet wears the cape for the flying scene. The fabric catches on a floor fan, and the cape pulls the hero sideways. Rafi stops the fan and releases the magnetic clasp. Zip moves the camera cable away from the runway and shows Tavi where the harness line crosses the stage.

CAPTAIN COMET: That was supposed to be a heroic landing.

ZIP: It was a sideways landing.

VELA: Can we keep the stumble? It looks like a funny new power.

RAFI: Not until we correct the snag. The fan is off, the cable is clear, and the clasp opens under light pressure.

Tavi asks Zip to perform the scripted entrance. Zip enters through the wrong door because the set sign points toward a painted tunnel. The crew flips the sign, but the sign itself covers the real exit. Rafi has them move the prop and tape a clear path for the stunt team.

TAVI: The character needs a memorable entrance.

ZIP: The side door is memorable. It's where we carried the collapsed moon.

CAPTAIN COMET: That was not in the film.

ZIP: It was in the crew report.

Vela asks Zip to tell the audience about the moon rescue. Zip describes holding a ladder while Captain Comet climbs down from a tilting set. The camera tries to cut to a dramatic reenactment; Rafi declines because the moon prop has a loose wheel. Instead, they interview the prop handler, who confirms Zip braced the ladder and called for a stop.

VELA: Can we give Zip a power name?

ZIP: I can spot a loose wheel.

CAPTAIN COMET: That's a power.

RAFI: It is a skill. We should credit the skill and keep the safety check.

Tavi gives Zip a new audition, asking for a line that fits the character. Zip stands beside the repaired cape, points to the clearly marked walkway, and says the line without pretending to fly.

ZIP: If the hero is in the air, I will keep the landing clear.

TAVI: That is good dialogue.

ZIP: It is also a direction.

ACT THREE

The cape clasp is repaired and tested on the dummy. Captain Comet runs the flying scene with a clear route. Zip works from the ground, keeping the harness line untangled and alerting the crew when the fan drifts toward the landing pad. The take succeeds without a snag.

Vela announces Zip as the new sidekick, not as a flying hero but as the person who gets the team safely through each stunt. Zip asks for a costume with pockets for the cable markers. Vela agrees to add them after the fitting, and Tavi updates the character credit.

CAPTAIN COMET: You have a name now.

ZIP: Does it come with pockets?

TAVI: The name is Sidepath. The pockets are a costume note.

RAFI: The cape goes back on its stand, unclipped from the dummy.

FINAL IMAGE: Zip walks the clear landing route while Captain Comet's repaired cape hangs safely on its marked stand.
