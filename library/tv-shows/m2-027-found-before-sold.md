# Found Before Sold

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: house-hunting reality show*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A young couple tours three homes with an agent who keeps emphasizing charm, while they focus on whether the spaces work for their noisy family and their elderly aunt.

Cast: Agent Beryl, a fast-talking macaque; Kimo and Lala, a capuchin couple; Aunt Omi, a slow loris who walks carefully; their twins, Fizz and Foz; and the camera crew, who keep asking the family to react at the doorway.

## Episode: "A Kitchen Big Enough for All"

### Cold open

INT. FIRST HOUSE, DAY

Beryl opens a door into a narrow kitchen. The family squeezes in. Aunt Omi stays in the hallway.

BERYL: Cozy, isn't it?

KIMO: It has one chair.

Lala opens a cabinet. A loaf of bread falls out. The twins catch it together.

### Act one

Kimo and Lala want a home near school and the clinic, with a room for Aunt Omi and a kitchen where family can cook. Beryl picked three homes. Lala asks to see the bathroom first.

The first home has a narrow kitchen and a high tub. Aunt Omi tests the step. The listing has no roof repair record.

### Act two

The second has a large kitchen, garden, and ground-floor bedroom. Lala measures the doorway. Kimo finds a damp patch beneath the sink.

Beryl says the seller will address the leak. Kimo asks whether that promise is in writing. The camera crew requests a reaction shot. Lala says she is concerned, not excited. The producer asks for a more emotional line. Lala says a leak can damage cabinets and attract mold. The producer keeps that take.

The third home has a ramp, broad kitchen, and small bedroom. Its roof is sound, but heating costs more and the bus adds fifteen minutes.


[At the second house, Aunt Omi measures the bathroom threshold.]
BERYL: This kitchen is enormous.
LALA: How wide is the doorway?

KIMO: I brought a tape.
AUNT OMI: The step is high.
BERYL: It adds character.
AUNT OMI: It adds a risk.

KIMO: The road is loud.
LALA: And the sink leaks.

BERYL: It can be.

BERYL: We have three choices.

### Act three

The family sits at the kitchen table of the third house. They compare the actual tradeoffs. The first home needs a safer bathroom and a roof inspection. The second offers space but has a leak and road noise. The third fits their daily needs but costs more to heat and adds time to the school route.

Beryl admits she chose homes that looked good on camera. The family asks her to find one more option with a wide kitchen, a ground-floor bedroom, and a route to school that the twins can manage. The producer says they have already toured three. Beryl says house hunting should answer the family's questions, even if it takes another day.

The additional home is modest and needs paint. It has a wide kitchen, a bedroom near the bathroom, and a quiet path to school. Aunt Omi tests the ramp. Kimo checks the heating system records. Lala asks about the roof and sees a recent repair receipt. The twins find a small alcove where they can keep backpacks out of the walkway.

The family makes an offer after a second inspection. The seller agrees to fix a loose handrail before closing. Beryl says they chose the house because it supports ordinary routines. The camera asks for a celebratory doorway reaction. Aunt Omi walks through first, turns, and holds the door open for everyone else.

### Tag

The twins place a loaf of bread on the new kitchen table. It stays put. Kimo checks the cabinet, then laughs.

LALA: We bought the house with a bread shelf.

Aunt Omi sits in the chair by the doorway and watches the family unpack.


### Additional moment

A week later, the camera crew visits after the family has moved in. Kimo has installed a low shelf for lunch bags. Lala pins the school bus schedule beside the door. Aunt Omi says the ramp is useful even when her knees feel fine, because it keeps the entrance open for everyone carrying groceries. The twins place their boots under the bench instead of in the walkway. Beryl checks the kitchen doorway once more and asks whether they would change anything. Lala says she would add a second hook for coats. Kimo finds one in the toolbox. No one asks for a reaction shot. The family hangs the hook, then sits down to eat.
