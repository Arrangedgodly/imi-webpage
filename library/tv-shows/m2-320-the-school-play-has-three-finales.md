# The School Play Has Three Finales

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live school-play broadcasts, sentimental recaps, backstage panic, and television cuts that miss the actual performance*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A local TV crew broadcasts a school play but keeps cutting to commentary, nearly missing the students' carefully rehearsed finale.

Cast: Director Palla, a gibbon; student actor Fenn, a capuchin; student actor Miri, a macaque; announcer Vex, an orangutan; camera operator Omi, a ring-tailed lemur; and stage manager Zuzu, a squirrel monkey.

## Episode: "Please Stay on Stage"

### Cold Open

[SCHOOL GYM. A PAINTED MOON HANGS ABOVE A CARDBOARD BOAT.]

VEX: We are live with the moving story of a boat that seeks the moon.

PALLA: The boat is tied to the gym curtain.

The curtain starts to open, carrying the boat sideways.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The students are performing an original play about a boat captain returning a borrowed moon lantern. Palla has blocked every entrance carefully. The TV crew arrives with two cameras: one aimed at the actors and one aimed at Vex. Vex wants a pre-show interview about the students' dreams. The students are waiting in costume behind the curtain.

FENN: My dream is to say the first line before the curtain opens.

VEX: And how does that feel?

FENN: The curtain is moving.

Zuzu grabs the rope and stops it. Omi checks the stage camera. Its view is blocked by the painted boat, which has been parked center stage for the opening. Palla asks the camera team to use the side aisle. Omi refuses because that aisle is reserved for audience reaction shots.

### Act Two

Vex introduces the show and asks the audience to hold their applause for the broadcast. Palla gives the opening cue. The boat swings out, but the camera cuts to Vex describing the boat. On stage, Miri lifts the lantern and delivers the first scene's key line to an empty camera.

MIRI: The moon was borrowed, not captured.

Omi switches to the stage feed too late. Vex whispers to the camera that viewers can catch up during a recap. He begins retelling the scene while Fenn is already rowing the cardboard boat across the gym. The sound of the oar squeaking competes with Vex's narration.

Palla signals for a close-up on the lantern. Omi zooms in so far that the moon disappears and only the handle remains. Zuzu holds up a sign reading WIDE SHOT. Omi changes angles, then lands on a close view of Vex's reaction. Vex tries to look moved. His paper cue card covers his face.

Between scenes, Vex interviews the actors about the play's message. Fenn says the captain should return borrowed things. Miri adds that the captain also needs to untie the boat before leaving. The crew puts a sentimental music bed under her answer. Behind them, the painted moon lantern begins rolling down the ramp. Zuzu catches it with a stage hook and puts it back in place.

Vex announces a special behind-the-scenes look. Omi points the camera backstage, where Palla is resetting the boat. A caption calls the scene the emotional journey continues. Zuzu tries to remove the caption with a long pole, but the pole itself appears in close-up and receives a name label from the graphics team. The students wait in a line while the broadcast explains that they are waiting.

PALLA: The audience already knows. They can see us.

VEX: We should tell them what they are seeing.

PALLA: They are seeing a line. The actors stand still waiting for the next scene. Palla whispers that the broadcast is showing the reset. Vex turns to camera and calls it an exclusive rehearsal moment. Fenn waves from the stage and accidentally pulls the boat's rope; the curtain opens early again.

### Act Three

Palla halts the broadcast. She asks Omi to keep the camera wide and Vex to stop narrating over the dialogue. Omi moves to the side aisle. The audience does not get a reaction shot; they can see the whole stage. Zuzu checks the rope and unhooks the boat from the curtain.

They start the finale. Fenn rows the boat to the moon. Miri returns the lantern to its hook. The captain says the moon is a guest, not a prize, and rows home. Omi keeps the camera on the actors through the last line. The audience applauds exactly when the scene ends.

Vex begins to introduce a recap, then hears Palla announce the curtain call. He stays quiet as the students bow. The broadcast finally shows the whole cast together. Fenn waves; the boat's oar falls off and lands on the gym floor. The audience laughs, and the students bow again.

### Tag

[AFTER THE SHOW.]

Vex attempts a final interview. The cast is carrying the scenery away. He asks for one last emotional thought. Miri carries the lantern past the camera.

MIRI: Return borrowed things before cleanup.

Omi films her walking out. Vex steps into the shot, then moves aside and lets the stage remain visible.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
