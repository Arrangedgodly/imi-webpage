# The Forecast Has a Pollen Plot

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local morning lifestyle television that forces every community event into a cheerful segment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Morning host Bibi is assigned a spring-garden segment and wants a bright live shot. Botanist Toma needs the station to acknowledge a heavy pollen count before the visiting choir arrives, while producer Squeak keeps changing the background to preserve the show's sunny mood.

Cast: Bibi, a macaque host; Toma, a slow loris botanist; Squeak, a capuchin producer; and Nella, a spectacled langur choir director.

## Episode: "Blooming on Schedule"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO GARDEN SET. PAPER FLOWERS FRAME A WEATHER SCREEN. TOMA ENTERS WEARING A MASK AND CARRYING A REAL POTTED TREE.]

BIBI: It is a perfect morning to plant joy.

TOMA: The pollen count is high enough to trigger the school notice.

[SQUEAK SWITCHES THE SCREEN FROM POLLEN MAP TO A SUN WITH SUNGLASSES.]

SQUEAK: Our color palette is yellow.

BIBI: Can we keep both maps up?

SQUEAK: The sun is already on the sponsor wall.

[TOMA PUTS THE POT DOWN. A YELLOW CLOUD OF POLLEN DRIFTS ACROSS THE SET. BIBI SNEEZES INTO A PAPER FLOWER.]

BIBI: The set is participating.

### Act One

[TOMA UNROLLS A REGIONAL MAP. SQUEAK MOVES A LIGHT PANEL TO COVER THE RED ZONE.]

TOMA: The north garden should be closed until the wind shifts.

BIBI: The choir is due here in ten minutes.

SQUEAK: We can call it a surprise flower shower.

TOMA: It is not water.

[BIBI READS THE SAFETY NOTICE. SQUEAK HOLDS UP A CARD READING "SPRING IS IN THE AIR." THE CARD COVERS THE NUMBER.]

BIBI: Our caption has blocked the count.

SQUEAK: It is a very legible caption.

TOMA: The count is the information.

[THEY SHIFT THE CARD. A STUDIO FAN BLOWS IT INTO THE POTTED TREE. TOMA TURNS OFF THE FAN.]

BIBI: We have a live audience outside.

TOMA: Then we tell them where not to stand.

### Act Two

[THE CHOIR ARRIVES AT THE STUDIO DOOR. NELLA IS HOLDING A STACK OF MUSIC FOLDERS.]

NELLA: We were told the garden was a safe entrance.

SQUEAK: The garden is beautiful from this angle.

NELLA: Our singers are wheezing from that angle.

[TOMA POINTS THEM TO THE COVERED LOADING BAY. SQUEAK PLACES A PAPER SUN AT ITS ENTRANCE.]

BIBI: The choir can wait inside while we move the tree.

SQUEAK: If we move the tree, the segment loses its bloom.

NELLA: If we stay here, the alto section loses its voices.

[TOMA TAKES THE TREE TO A SEALED PROP ROOM. BIBI HELPS NELLA MOVE THE MUSIC STANDS. SQUEAK FOLLOWS, STILL HOLDING THE SUN.]

SQUEAK: Could you walk through the garden for one establishing shot?

NELLA: We can sing from behind this door.

[THE CHOIR SINGS A SHORT WARMUP. A DUSTING OF POLLEN FALLS ON SQUEAK'S SUN.]

BIBI: Even the sunshine has symptoms.

[THE WEATHER DESK CALLS THROUGH BIBI'S EARPIECE. TOMA POINTS TO THE WIND ARROW, WHICH NOW FACES THE STUDIO ENTRANCE.]

TOMA: The wind shifted. The loading bay is clear, but the garden entrance is worse.

SQUEAK: We just moved the camera there.

NELLA: We can move the choir, but we cannot sing while carrying the stands.

[BIBI HANDS HER MICROPHONE TO SQUEAK AND HELPS THE CHOIR ROLL THE STANDS INSIDE. SQUEAK'S CAMERA TRACKS BIBI, LEAVING THE EMPTY PAPER GARDEN CENTER FRAME.]

SQUEAK: The shot has no host.

BIBI: It has the map and the notice. Keep those visible.

[SQUEAK LOWERS THE SUNNY BACKDROP. THE POLLEN MAP FILLS THE SCREEN.]

[A CAMERA OPERATOR ATTEMPTS TO FRAME THE FLOWER BED THROUGH THE GLASS DOOR. TOMA MOVES THE TRIPOD BACK BEHIND THE SAFETY LINE.]

TOMA: The warning applies to the doorway too.

SQUEAK: We have lost the close-up.

BIBI: We have kept the choir breathing.

### Act Three

[THE LIVE SEGMENT STARTS FROM THE LOADING BAY. TOMA HOLDS THE POLLEN MAP BESIDE BIBI. NELLA STANDS WITH THE CHOIR IN THE CLEAR HALLWAY.]

BIBI: We are moving today's planting demonstration indoors because the pollen count is high.

SQUEAK: Could we say "high enthusiasm"?

TOMA: No. The count is measured.

NELLA: Our concert is still on. The garden is not the only place with flowers.

[BIBI SHOWS THE SCHOOL NOTICE. SQUEAK TRIES TO PUT THE SUN GRAPHIC OVER IT, BUT THE SCREEN AUTO-CROPS THE SUN OUT AND LEAVES THE NOTICE CENTERED.]

BIBI: That graphic has made its own editorial choice.

SQUEAK: We will keep it.

[TOMA GIVES THE VIEWERS THE CURRENT COUNT AND A LINK TO THE PUBLIC ALERT. THE CHOIR PERFORMS A BRIEF, CLEAR NOTE.]

### Tag

[AFTER THE BROADCAST, SQUEAK PEELS THE PAPER SUN OFF THE DOOR. THE BACK IS COVERED IN POLLEN.]

SQUEAK: It is still sunny.

TOMA: It is still a paper circle.

[BIBI HANDS SQUEAK A MASK. HE PUTS IT ON, THEN HOLDS UP THE SUN.]

BIBI: Tomorrow, we do the rain barrel segment indoors.
