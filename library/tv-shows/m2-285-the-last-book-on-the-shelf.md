# The Last Book on the Shelf

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: library workplace mockumentaries, where staff try to shelve a popular book while every patron asks for it*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A small library has one copy of a new adventure book, and staff try to return it to the shelf without starting another queue.

Cast: Librarian Odo, baboon; assistant Nara, macaque; patron Piri, capuchin; patron Seli, gibbon; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Hold, Please"

### Cold Open

[LIBRARY DESK. ONE BOOK.]

ODO: The book is available.

PIRI: I would like to borrow it.

SELI: I have a hold on it.

NARA: The book is on the desk.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE LIBRARIANS CHECK THE COMPUTER.]

Nara says the book was returned this morning. Odo says the hold list has three patrons. Piri says he was first to ask. Seli says she placed a hold yesterday.

ODO: The system says it is available.

NARA: It is physically here.

SELI: I am next on the hold list.

PIRI: I am here now.

Tavi films the screen. It shows "available" and "hold pending." Odo says the computer has two answers.

NARA: We need to scan it.

The barcode reader beeps but does not update the status. Nara wipes it and scans again.

[SFX: BEEP.]

ODO: The book is still available.

### Act Two

[THE STAFF CHECK THE RETURN CART.]

The book's slip shows that it was returned to the wrong branch. Nara says it should be routed to another library. Seli says the hold is at this branch. Piri asks if he can read it while they decide.

ODO: The book must be checked in first.

PIRI: It is already checked in.

NARA: The system has not recorded it.

Tavi asks Odo to restart the terminal. The screen goes blank. Piri holds the book while Nara checks the printed return sheet.

SELI: I can wait.

PIRI: I can read while waiting.

NARA: The book is on hold.

PIRI: For whom?

Seli raises her hand.

[THE STAFF TRY A PAPER HOLD SYSTEM.]

Nara creates a list with names and membership numbers. Seli writes her name, then Piri writes the same name with a different number.

NARA: We need the number to separate you.

PIRI: I use my middle name on the account.

SELI: I use it as my first name.

ODO: The computer has only one name field.

Tavi suggests adding a note to the record. Odo opens the patron profile and finds the note field is full of old reminders.

ODO: We cannot add another note.

NARA: We can clear outdated notes.

Seli asks which notes are outdated. One says "return umbrella." Another says "call about noisy printer."

PIRI: The printer is still noisy.

Tavi prints a short form for patrons to update their display names. The form asks for three names and has only two lines.

SELI: The form is also confused.

Odo checks the hold list and confirms Piri's membership number is older. Seli says she can wait for the next copy.

NARA: There is no second copy.

Seli looks at the shelf, where an older edition sits. She asks if she can borrow that one instead.

[THE LIBRARIANS CHECK THE RETURNS SHELF.]

Nara searches for another copy, but finds only a reservation slip. Odo says the book is still at the neighboring branch. Piri can borrow it later if he returns this one on time.

PIRI: Then Seli can have this copy next.

SELI: I will take the older edition today.

TAVI: The two editions have different covers.

ODO: The story is the same.

Seli chooses the older edition. The staff update the hold list by membership number, then print the transfer request for the newer copy.

NARA: The system now knows who is next.

PIRI: And which book?

Odo checks the label. It says "adventure novel, volume two," but the shelf has volume one.

ODO: The next problem is the shelf label.

SELI: We can fix it tomorrow.

### Act Three

[THE SYSTEM RESTARTS.]

Nara scans the book again. The terminal updates: it belongs to another branch, but Seli's hold is active. Odo prints a transfer slip.

PIRI: So I cannot borrow it?

ODO: Not yet.

SELI: I can borrow it when it reaches my turn.

NARA: The system says the hold is for Piri.

Everyone looks at Piri. He had placed the hold under his middle name, which is Seli.

PIRI: My middle name is Seli.

SELI: That is also my name.

ODO: The record has a duplicate.

Tavi asks which patron should receive the book. Nara checks the membership numbers; Piri's hold is older by one day. Seli accepts the result.

### Tag

[THE BOOK IS SCANNED OUT TO PIRI.]

Piri takes the book. Seli asks to be next in line. Odo writes her name on the hold slip.

SELI: Use my membership number.

NARA: We will.

[SFX: LIBRARY STAMP, QUIET ROOM.]
