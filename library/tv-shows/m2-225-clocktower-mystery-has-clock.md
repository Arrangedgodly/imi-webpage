# The Clocktower Mystery Has a Clock

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: mystery serials that stretch a simple clue across episodes by withholding obvious information*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: Three village detectives investigate why the clocktower stopped, while the show keeps cutting away before anyone checks the clock mechanism.

Cast: Detective Eri, a macaque; Detective Venn, a gibbon; caretaker Pavo, a capuchin; and narrator Sela, a baboon.

Episode: "At the Stroke of Four"

COLD OPEN

EXT. VILLAGE CLOCKTOWER - DAY

The clock face reads 4:00. Eri studies it. Venn holds a magnifier. Pavo carries a toolbox.

ERI: The clock stopped at four.

VENN: A suspicious time.

PAVO: It stopped because the winding weight is down.

SELA (V.O.): But why did the clock stop?

ERI: We should wind it.

VENN: First, we must inspect the scene.

ACT ONE

INT. CLOCKTOWER - MOMENTS LATER

The detectives climb the stairs. Pavo follows with his toolbox. The winding weight rests on the floor.

VENN: The clock has a weight.

ERI: It is on the ground.

PAVO: It needs to be raised.

SELA (V.O.): The detectives have found an object that may hold the answer.

ERI: It is the clock's weight.

VENN: Could someone have lowered it?

PAVO: The gear is jammed. The weight reached the bottom when it stopped.

[ERI TRIES TO TURN A GEAR. IT DOES NOT MOVE.]

ACT TWO

INT. CLOCKTOWER - LATER

Venn finds a feather between two gears. Eri lifts it with tweezers. Pavo looks at the mechanism.

VENN: A clue.

PAVO: A bird feather.

ERI: The window is open.

SELA (V.O.): The detectives now face a new mystery.

PAVO: I can remove it.

VENN: What if it belongs to the clock's previous owner?

PAVO: It is caught in the gear.

[HE REMOVES THE FEATHER. THE GEAR TURNS A LITTLE, THEN STOPS AGAIN.]

ERI: There is another obstruction.

PAVO: A loose screw.

VENN: The plot thickens.

PAVO: The screw is holding the gear.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. CLOCKTOWER - AFTERNOON

Sela records a recap. Eri and Venn stand beside the door. Pavo holds the loose screw.

SELA: Previously, our detectives discovered a fallen weight, a feather, and a screw.

ERI: The screw came out of the gear.

VENN: We need to know where it came from.

PAVO: This empty hole.

[HE POINTS TO THE HOLE.]

SELA: We will return after the break.

ERI: The screw goes in there.

VENN: We are close to the answer.

ACT TWO-C

INT. CLOCKTOWER - LATE AFTERNOON

Eri and Venn stand beside the gear. Pavo holds a small tray of screws. Sela records a recap from the landing.

SELA: The detectives return to the clock's mechanism.

ERI: We are still here.

VENN: We should examine the feather's direction.

PAVO: The feather fell out when I removed it.

VENN: It could point toward the culprit.

PAVO: It points toward the floor.

[PAVO USES A BRUSH TO SWEEP THE GEAR. A SECOND FEATHER FLOATS DOWN FROM THE OPEN WINDOW.]

ERI: Another clue.

PAVO: The bird has a nest outside.

VENN: Is it the same bird?

SELA: We will reveal that after the break.

ERI: We can close the window.

PAVO: The clock needs air circulation.

VENN: The window is open because the latch is broken.

[PAVO LIFTS THE WINDOW AND SLIDES THE LATCH INTO PLACE. THE FEATHER STOPS BLOWING IN.]

SELA: The wind has changed.

ERI: The window has closed.

ACT TWO-D

INT. CLOCKTOWER - EVENING

Pavo places the screws in a row. Eri and Venn compare them with the gear holes.

VENN: Which screw belongs to the clock?

PAVO: The one that fits the empty hole.

ERI: That is a clear test.

SELA: The detectives have narrowed the suspects to three screws.

PAVO: There are two screws.

[VENN HOLDS UP A TINY WOOD CHIP.]

VENN: The third suspect is a splinter.

ERI: It came from the railing.

PAVO: And it did not stop the clock.

VENN: The screw fits the hole exactly.

PAVO: It is the right size.

ERI: Why did it come loose?

PAVO: The clock shakes when the weight drops.

[PAVO TURNS THE GEAR BY HAND. THE LOOSE SCREW VIBRATES ON THE TRAY.]

SELA: The mechanism has provided a demonstration.

VENN: The mystery now has a sound.

PAVO: It needs a wrench.

ACT THREE

INT. CLOCKTOWER - FINAL REPAIR

Pavo replaces the screw and winds the weight. Eri listens. Venn watches the hands move.

PAVO: The clock is running.

ERI: It was a loose screw.

VENN: A simple cause with a long investigation.

SELA: The village will hear the bell again.

[THE CLOCK STRIKES FOUR. THE VILLAGERS LOOK UP.]

TAG

The clock strikes four again an hour later. Venn looks at Eri.

VENN: Is the mystery back?

PAVO: That is the next hour.
