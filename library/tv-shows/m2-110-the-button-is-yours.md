# The Button Is Yours

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: game shows that turn simple household knowledge into elaborate timed spectacle*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: Contestants answer questions about familiar objects while the host adds timers, buzzers, and bonus rounds. A macaque contestant knows the objects by using them, but the show keeps rewarding theatrical guesses.

Cast: Host Tilli, a gibbon; contestants Pomo, a macaque, and Leta, a capuchin; scorekeeper Fenn, a baboon; and floor assistant Nib, a tamarin.

## Episode: "Name That Household"

### Cold Open

[THE SET HAS A TABLE WITH A BOWL, A BRUSH, A CUP, AND A SMALL KEY.]

TILLI: Four objects. One thousand points.

POMO: A bowl, a brush, a cup, and a key.

[THE BUZZER SOUNDS.]

TILLI: Too fast. You have to wait for the question.

### Act One

[THE CONTESTANTS STAND AT TWO BUZZER PODIUMS.]

TILLI: What object holds water and can be carried in one hand?

LETA: A bucket.

POMO: A cup.

FENN: The answer is cup.

TILLI: One point to Leta for enthusiasm.

POMO: I was right.

TILLI: But the question was not finished.

POMO: It ended.

[TILLI LOOKS AT THE CUE CARD.]

TILLI: This is a timed answer show.

NIB: The timer did not start.

FENN: I will start it now.

[A BUZZER RINGS. THE TIMER BEGINS COUNTING DOWN.]

TILLI: Which item removes loose material from fur?

POMO: A brush.

LETA: A cloth.

TILLI: The accepted answer is brush.

POMO: I use one every morning.

TILLI: You are not allowed to bring experience onto the set.

### Act Two

[TILLI REVEALS THE BONUS ROUND: A COVERED BOX.]

TILLI: Guess the mystery object by touch.

[LETA REACHES IN AND FEELS A ROUND OBJECT.]

LETA: A fruit.

POMO: It is a cup with a fig inside.

[THEY OPEN THE BOX. IT CONTAINS A CUP AND A FIG.]

TILLI: The answer was "a cup with a fig inside."

FENN: Pomo said that.

TILLI: He said it before the timer.

POMO: The timer was not running.

NIB: I can start it earlier next time.

[THE CREW SETS UP A SECOND MYSTERY BOX. A SMALL MONKEY CLIMBS ONTO THE TABLE AND TAKES THE FIG.]

TILLI: We have lost the object.

POMO: It has been claimed.

LETA: Can we buzz in?

[THE MONKEY EATS THE FIG AND DROPS THE CUP.]

TILLI: The object is now a cup without a fig.

FENN: That was not one of the listed answers.

[THE SHOW RUNS A PRACTICE ROUND. NIB SETS THE OBJECTS BACK ON THE TABLE.]

TILLI: This round is about common tools. The first object is used to hold a door open.

POMO: A wedge.

LETA: A chair.

[TILLI LIFTS THE BOWL. A SMALL WOODEN WEDGE IS UNDER IT.]

TILLI: The answer is wedge.

FENN: We have no wedge in the prize list.

NIB: It was holding the table steady.

[THE TABLE WOBBLES. THE CUP ROLLS TOWARD THE EDGE. POMO CATCHES IT.]

POMO: The cup needs the table.

TILLI: Contestants may not touch the props.

POMO: Then the props need to stay put.

[THEY RESET THE TABLE. NIB PLACES THE WEDGE UNDER THE SHORT LEG.]

FENN: The table is stable.

TILLI: The wedge has won the practice round.

LETA: Does it get a bowl?

TILLI: We will consult the prize department.

[THE SMALL MONKEY RETURNS, CARRIES THE BOWL BACK, AND SETS IT ON THE WEDGE.]

POMO: The object knows its use.

[TILLI REMOVES THE WEDGE FROM THE PRIZE TABLE AND GIVES IT BACK TO NIB.]

NIB: The table is still mine to set up.

FENN: And the wedge is not a prize.

TILLI: We will label it equipment and keep it beside the table for next time.

NIB: I will keep the wedges beside the podiums.

### Act Three

[TILLI REARRANGES THE OBJECTS. FENN READS THE FINAL QUESTION.]

FENN: Which item can open the small cabinet under the table?

[THE KEY IS SET BESIDE A BRUSH.]

POMO: The key.

LETA: The brush?

[THE CABINET OPENS. INSIDE IS A SECOND BOWL.]

TILLI: The correct answer was hidden in the cabinet.

POMO: Then the key opened it.

FENN: The bowl was the prize.

TILLI: The winner is Leta, by one theatrical guess.

LETA: I would like the bowl.

[THE SMALL MONKEY TAKES THE BOWL AND CARRIES IT OFF.]

TILLI: The prize has been claimed by an audience member.

POMO: I would like the brush. I know how to use it.

[TILLI HANDS HIM THE BRUSH.]

TILLI: You may have one household item.

POMO: That is all I asked for.

[THE TIMER BEEPS. NIB STARTS IT AFTER THE ROUND HAS ENDED.]

FENN: We have a new record for the slowest start.

TILLI: Contestants, please wait until the buzzer before leaving.

[EVERYONE WALKS OFF. THE TIMER CONTINUES COUNTING DOWN.]
