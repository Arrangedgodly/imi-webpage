# The Weather Desk Forecasts Rain Indoors

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: weather broadcasts that trust a dramatic studio graphic more than the window outside*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Sela, a lemur; meteorologist Roko, an orangutan; graphics operator Pavi, a macaque; producer Teni, a capuchin; camera operator Miri, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. WEATHER STUDIO - MORNING

Sela stands under a graphic of a storm cloud. The studio floor is dry. Outside the window, sunlight fills the plaza.

SELA: Severe rain is already falling across town.

ROKO: Then why is the camera operator holding sunglasses?

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Pavi says the graphic comes from last night's rehearsal. Teni wants to keep it because the storm map has lightning. Roko checks the current radar on a tablet. The rain line is moving east and will miss the city. Sela asks him to find a dramatic angle. Roko points to a small green patch representing clear skies.

Teni asks Pavi to animate the rain over the whole map. Pavi clicks the wrong layer. Blue drops fall over the studio windows. Sela holds out her palm and catches nothing. Miri pans to the dry glass, then back to the storm graphic.

SELA: We have rain in the forecast and nowhere else.

ACT TWO

INT. GRAPHICS BOOTH - LATER

Roko brings the station's rooftop camera feed. The roof is dry, but the feed label reads FLOOD CAM. Teni asks him to use the dramatic clip from yesterday. Roko says it is a sprinkler test. Pavi adds thunder. The sound arrives before the animated lightning, then repeats while the map sits still.

MIRI: The thunder is coming from the booth.

Pavi reaches for the speaker volume. Teni asks him not to mute the sponsor's weather jingle. Roko turns the display to the live radar. Teni's animated storm shrinks to a small patch over the hills. Sela steps toward the map and blocks the only raindrop.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Roko offers Sela the umbrella prop. She opens it indoors, and the ceiling sprinkler releases a short rehearsal spray over the set. Pavi immediately draws a rain icon over the studio. Roko points at the sprinkler control panel; the water has stopped. Teni asks for a slow-motion replay. Miri keeps the camera on the dripping umbrella while Sela closes it. The studio's one wet spot lands on the weather desk.

ACT THREE

INT. WEATHER STUDIO - LATER

Roko places the live radar beside the window feed. The clouds are still over the hills; the plaza remains bright. Teni asks Sela to give the storm warning anyway, because the sponsor bought a rain graphic. Sela reads the actual forecast: clear through noon, showers in the foothills after three. Pavi removes the citywide rain layer but leaves a tiny cloud over the hills.

The map updates. The cloud drifts toward the edge of the screen and covers the sponsor logo instead. Teni asks Pavi to move it. Roko says the cloud is following the radar. Sela turns to the window and confirms the sunshine. She keeps the umbrella closed. Miri frames her with the clear plaza behind her and the accurate map beside her. The sponsor jingle starts; the ceiling sprinkler clicks on for another rehearsal drop. Sela holds up the closed umbrella. One drop hits its handle, and she looks toward the sprinkler.

SELA: Showers after three, apparently.

Teni asks if they can call it a studio microclimate. Roko says they can call maintenance. Pavi turns off the sprinkler graphic. Sela reads the two-part forecast once more, naming the city and the foothills separately. The camera holds on the window, where the sun stays out.


Teni receives a message from the sponsor asking the show to mention umbrellas in every region. She reads the note aloud. Roko opens a local map and points to the neighborhoods outside the storm line. Sela gives a forecast for those streets without adding the umbrella warning. Pavi begins an animated rain loop over the map, but Miri turns the studio camera toward the clear window. The mismatch is visible. Teni tells Pavi to keep the regional map up; the sponsor's umbrella logo is small enough to fit beneath it. The sprinkler clicks again. Roko points to the ceiling sensor, and Sela asks the floor crew to cut the rehearsal water before they issue another forecast.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE FORECAST

Miri hangs the umbrella to dry. Roko tapes the live radar label over the old rehearsal label. Teni asks Pavi to save the storm animation for fiction night.

FINAL IMAGE: The dry city forecast fills the monitor while one drop falls from the umbrella onto the sponsor's unused thunder button.
