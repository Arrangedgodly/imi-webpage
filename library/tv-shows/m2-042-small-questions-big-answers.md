# Small Questions, Big Answers

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's educational television, demonstrative experiments, and cheerful recap songs*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A friendly science host tries to explain gravity with a simple demonstration, but the studio's spring floor turns every experiment into a jumping contest.

Cast: Professor Tella, a chimpanzee; assistant Nimo, a squirrel monkey; child guests Rafi, a young gibbon, and Una, a macaque; and stagehand Gogo, a baboon.

## Episode: "Why Do Things Come Down?"

### Cold open

INT. LITTLE LAB STUDIO, DAY

[A ball rests on a table. A banner reads "ASK A SMALL QUESTION." Nimo puts the ball on the floor.]

TELLA: Today we ask, why do things come down?

RAFI: Because the floor is under them.

UNA: Because someone lets go.

NIMO: Because I dropped the ball.

[The ball bounces high and lands on the table again.]

TELLA: That is an excellent question from the ball.

GOGO: The studio floor has a spring.

TELLA: The floor is demonstrating energy.

RAFI: It is demonstrating jumping.

### Act One

INT. LITTLE LAB STUDIO

[Tella draws Earth on a board. The chalk slides into a groove.]

TELLA: Gravity is a pull that keeps us near our planet.

UNA: Is gravity pulling the ball?

TELLA: Yes.

NIMO: Then why did the ball go up?

GOGO: The floor pushed it.

TELLA: The floor can push back after we press down.

RAFI: Can we test that?

TELLA: We can drop a soft ball from a low height.

[She holds a foam ball above the floor and releases it. It bounces to the ceiling.]

UNA: That is not low.

TELLA: The floor has a strong opinion.

NIMO: The ceiling caught it.

GOGO: The ceiling is padded.

TELLA: We have discovered that surfaces can affect what happens after an object falls.

RAFI: Does gravity work on bananas?

TELLA: It works on everything with mass.

NIMO: I have a banana.

[He lets go. It bounces, rolls, and stops against the chalkboard.]

UNA: It rolled sideways.

TELLA: Gravity pulled it down. The round shape helped it roll.

RAFI: So gravity isn't the only thing.

TELLA: Correct. We can test ideas one at a time.

GOGO: I have a second floor panel.

TELLA: Is it a spring panel?

GOGO: It is a flat panel.

NIMO: Can I jump on it?

TELLA: We are testing gravity.

NIMO: I can jump and gravity can watch.

### Act Two

INT. LITTLE LAB STUDIO, MOMENTS LATER

[The children stand on the flat panel. Tella holds a feather and a small stone.]

TELLA: Which will fall faster?

RAFI: The stone.

UNA: The feather.

TELLA: We will drop them together and observe.

[She drops them. The stone lands first. The feather drifts slowly.]

NIMO: The stone wins.

TELLA: Air pushes against the feather more because it is light and spread out.

RAFI: If we fold the feather?

[Tella folds it. They drop it with the stone. They land close together.]

UNA: They nearly arrived together.

TELLA: The shape changed how air affected it.

GOGO: The spring panel is back.

TELLA: Why?

GOGO: It was underneath the table.

NIMO: The table has been bouncing all day.

[Tella taps the table. It gives a small bounce.]

TELLA: We found our extra force.

RAFI: So if I stand on the spring panel?

TELLA: You will go up when it pushes back.

[The children step onto it together. They hop a few inches.]

NIMO: Gravity brought us down.

UNA: The panel sent us up.

TELLA: And your knees bent to make the landing gentle.

RAFI: Can gravity pull a question down?

TELLA: A question is an idea. Ideas don't have weight.

NIMO: This one does.

[He holds up a large card that reads "WHY?"]

TELLA: That is a card. Gravity can pull the card.

[He drops it. The card floats down onto Gogo's head.]

GOGO: The answer has arrived.

### Act Three

INT. LITTLE LAB STUDIO

[Tella sets three objects on the table: the ball, the folded feather, and the question card.]

TELLA: Let's recap. What pulls objects toward Earth?

CHILDREN: Gravity!

TELLA: What can change how an object moves through the air?

CHILDREN: Its shape!

TELLA: What pushed the ball back up?

CHILDREN: The spring floor!

NIMO: And what makes a good experiment?

TELLA: A clear question, a safe test, and careful observation.

GOGO: Also checking what is under the table.

RAFI: Can we do another experiment?

TELLA: Next time, we will ask why shadows move.

UNA: Is the sun going to jump?

TELLA: The sun stays where it is. We move around it.

NIMO: I will bring a small shadow.

TELLA: Shadows do not fit in backpacks.

NIMO: I will bring a small backpack.

[The children laugh. The question card slips off Gogo's head and lands on the spring panel. It bounces back up.]

RAFI: The question came back.

TELLA: Some questions do.

### Tag

INT. LITTLE LAB STUDIO, LATER

[Nimo sings a simple recap while sweeping.]

NIMO: Gravity pulls us down, the spring floor says "boing."

GOGO: The floor is not part of the song.

NIMO: It is part of the show.

[The ball bounces once more, gently into a basket.]
