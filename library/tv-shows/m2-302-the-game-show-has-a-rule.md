# The Game Show Has a Rule

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live television game shows, hidden rule reveals, suspenseful buzzers, and contestants rewarded for noticing the instructions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A quiz show's host keeps adding rules after each answer, until the contestants discover that the real game is to make him read the rule card.

Cast: Host Vela, a gibbon; contestant Palla, a ring-tailed lemur; contestant Rook, a macaque; judge Omi, an orangutan; and announcer Tavi, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Last Rule"

### Cold Open

[GAME SHOW SET. TWO BUZZERS.]

VELA: The first contestant to buzz wins.

PALLA: Is that the only rule?

VELA: Yes.

Rook buzzes. A second buzzer sounds from under the desk.

TAVI: The desk has won.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[STAGE. AUDIENCE APPLAUDS.]

Vela welcomes the contestants to The Last Rule, a quiz show with simple questions and a grand prize: a golden bell. The first question asks for the number of sides on a triangle. Palla answers three. Vela says she is correct, but the game also requires the contestant to name a color.

PALLA: The triangle is blue.

VELA: Correct again.

Rook asks where the color rule is written. Vela reveals a small card behind the podium. Omi reads it aloud. It says contestants must answer the question, name a color, and ring the bell.

PALLA: There is no bell at our desks.

Vela points to the bell under his chair. It rings when he shifts his weight.

### Act Two

[THE QUESTIONS CONTINUE.]

The next question asks which direction the sun rises. Rook says east, then names green. He has no bell. Palla tries tapping her buzzer; it squeaks like a bell. Vela rejects the sound because it is not the official bell.

OMI: The rule card does not say the bell must be official.

Vela turns the card around. The back has another rule: contestants may not read the card.

Rook asks whether that rule is itself on the card. Omi says yes. Vela says reading the card violates the rule against reading the card.

The audience laughs. Tavi announces a commercial break. During the break, Palla and Rook inspect the desk. They find a cord leading from Vela's chair to the hidden bell. Whenever he sits, it rings.

PALLA: The host has been playing the sound effect.

ROOK: Does the host know the rule?

Vela returns, holding the golden bell. He says the contestants must now answer a question without using a color. Palla asks whether that is a new rule. Vela says it was always a rule, but the card was folded.

The audience gets a chance to play. Vela asks how many wheels are on a bicycle. A spectator answers two. The host asks for a color; the spectator says green. The hidden bell does not ring because the contestant did not buzz first. Tavi explains that the desk buzzer is connected to the bell, while Omi checks the wiring.

OMI: The buzzer rings the bell whether a contestant is right or wrong.

VELA: It creates suspense.

PALLA: It creates noise.

Rook asks for a question with only one rule. Vela gives him a simple arithmetic problem. Rook answers correctly, but Vela tries to ask for a color. Omi raises the plain rules card, which says the bonus color is optional.

Vela admits he added the color round because the show needed a visual moment. Palla points out that the golden bell is already a visual moment. The audience starts ringing it whenever Vela begins a new rule.

Omi unfolds the card. It lists no such rule. Vela says the rule is on the opposite side. The card has no opposite side.

### Act Three

[FINAL QUESTION.]

Vela asks what an animal uses to hear. Rook answers "ears." Palla names blue, then makes a bell sound by tapping her tail against the desk. Vela says her answer is invalid because the sound did not come from a bell.

Omi stops the game. He reads the full rule card into the camera. It says contestants must answer questions correctly. The rest is a host's handwritten note: "Add suspense."

VELA: That note is part of the show.

PALLA: It is not part of the rules.

Vela tries to defend the twists, but the audience begins asking to see the card. Rook buzzes. The hidden bell rings. Palla buzzes. Her desk squeaks. Omi rings the golden bell.

OMI: We have three sounds and one correct answer.

Vela gives the bell to Palla and Rook to share. He agrees to read the rules before the next episode. Tavi asks whether the rule card can be shown on camera. Vela says yes, as long as it is folded dramatically.

### Tag

[STAGE AFTER THE SHOW.]

Vela opens a new envelope. It contains one rule: "Do not add any rules." He reads it aloud. The chair bell rings.

VELA: That rule has a sound effect.

Omi removes the cord from under the chair. The audience hears one quiet click.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
