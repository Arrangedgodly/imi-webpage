# The Case of the Missing Marble

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: teen detective serials, moody hallway reveals, suspect montages, and clues that become dramatic after someone turns on the lights*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: Three students investigate a missing blue marble from the school's display case, but every witness saw it in a different place because the glass reflects the hallway.

Cast: Student detective Mira, a macaque; partner Rell, a capuchin; art student Tavi, a gibbon; teacher Omi, an orangutan; and hall monitor Palla, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "Blue at the Bell"

### Cold Open

[SCHOOL HALL. A GLASS CASE STANDS OPEN.]

MIRA: The blue marble has vanished.

RELL: We should turn on the lights.

Tavi flips the switch. The marble is on the shelf.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[HALLWAY. STUDENTS GATHER.]

The school display case holds a blue marble used in a physics demonstration. Mira begins an investigation. Palla says the case was closed before lunch. Tavi says she saw the marble missing after lunch. Omi says the marble was on the opposite side of the shelf this morning.

Mira asks each witness to mark their position on a floor plan. Palla stood near the doorway, Tavi stood by the trophy cabinet, and Omi looked through the glass from the classroom. Their sightlines cross at different angles. Rell tapes string between the points, creating a web across the hallway.

PALLA: The school is now inside the case.

MIRA: The floor plan is helping.

TAVI: The string is blocking the hallway.

They remove the string. Mira interviews the student who polished the case. The student says the marble was under the label card before lunch, but the label was moved to make room for a new exhibit. Palla says she moved it and forgot to tell anyone.

MIRA: We have a time window.

RELL: We have three reports.

MIRA: That is a time window.

The pair interview each witness. Palla says she saw someone near the case. She describes a tall silhouette with a long arm. Rell realizes it was the reflection of a student carrying a ruler. The suspect, Tavi, says she saw the marble roll away but did not touch it.

TAVI: It rolled toward the back of the shelf.

MIRA: The shelf is flat.

TAVI: The case is tilted.

They check with a level. The case leans slightly toward the wall.

### Act Two

[ART ROOM. THE TEAM RECONSTRUCTS THE SCENE.]

Mira and Rell use a wooden bead to reproduce the roll. It moves toward the back of the display case. The case is too shallow for the marble to disappear, so they suspect a false back. Palla provides a key, but Omi says the case is supposed to stay locked.

They open it. The back is solid. Tavi notices a blue reflection in the glass. The marble is on the shelf, but a poster behind the case reflects over it, making it look like part of the picture.

MIRA: The marble did not disappear.

RELL: It disappeared from view.

MIRA: That is different.

The school bell rings. Students flood the hallway. The display case vibrates slightly as the door closes. The marble rolls behind a label card. Mira asks everyone to stop moving. She uses a ruler to nudge the card aside. The marble is revealed.

Palla updates the label with the marble's name and the date of the demonstration. Rell checks that the case is level. Tavi leaves the hallway light on.

PALLA: The clue was the reflection.

OMI: The clue was the label covering it.

TAVI: The reflection made the label look like the shelf.

Mira pins a diagram to the case showing where to look for the marble. The diagram itself reflects in the glass and doubles the marble again. Palla moves it to the side.

### Act Three

[SCIENCE CLASS.]

Omi explains that the marble's blue color and glass reflection confused the viewers. The students use a flashlight at different angles. The reflection shifts while the marble stays still. Mira and Rell present their findings to the class, but Mira has staged the reveal with a dark hallway and a spotlight.

RELL: We already know where it is.

MIRA: The audience needs a dramatic reconstruction.

TAVI: They can just turn on the lights.

Mira turns on the lights. The marble is immediately visible. She accepts the simpler ending. Palla locks the case, checks that the label is not in front of the marble, and leaves the key with Omi.

### Tag

[HALLWAY AFTER SCHOOL.]

Rell asks if another mystery is ready. Tavi moves the blue marble to a clearly labeled drawer. Mira stares at an empty display case.

MIRA: It has vanished again.

Palla switches off the lights. The marble's reflection glows from the hallway poster.

RELL: Not again.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
