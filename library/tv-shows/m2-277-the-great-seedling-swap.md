# The Great Seedling Swap

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality competition television, where gardeners trade seedlings under a strict and confusing swap format*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Four gardeners compete in a seedling swap, but the host's rules keep changing while the plants need water and shade.

Cast: Host Nara, macaque; gardener Odo, baboon; gardener Seli, capuchin; gardener Tavi, gibbon; judge Piri, lemur.

## Episode: "Trade Before Noon"

### Cold Open

[GARDEN TABLE. FOUR SEEDLING POTS.]

NARA: You may trade one plant.

ODO: With whom?

SELI: The rules don't say.

PIRI: The plants are already thirsty.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CONTESTANTS STAND BEHIND THEIR POTS.]

Nara says each gardener must trade one seedling before the noon bell. Odo asks if the swap must be equal. Piri says the goal is to give each plant a better growing place.

SELI: I have a tomato seedling.

TAVI: I have a bean.

ODO: I have a sunflower.

NARA: I have an empty pot.

PIRI: That is not a seedling.

Nara gives the empty pot to Odo. He plants the sunflower in it and says he has made a swap with the host.

NARA: Hosts cannot compete.

ODO: Then the empty pot is still available.

### Act Two

[THE CLOCK SHOWS ELEVEN.]

Seli offers the tomato to Tavi in exchange for the bean. Tavi asks if the bean needs more shade. Piri says it does. Seli's tomato needs sun. They try placing the pots on opposite sides of the table.

[SFX: POTS SLIDE.]

The tomato leans toward the window. The bean curls around a support stick. Nara asks whether the plants can choose.

PIRI: They need different conditions.

ODO: The swap must be between gardeners.

SELI: Then we can trade spots, not plants.

Nara says the rules specified a plant. Odo points out the card says "one plant." Piri checks the back; it says "or one place."

NARA: There are two sides of the card.

TAVI: We have been reading only one.

The gardeners rotate the pots. Odo places the sunflower in the shade; it droops. Piri moves it back.

[NARA ANNOUNCES A BONUS ROUND.]

Each gardener must explain why the plant was traded. Odo says he gave up the sunflower's spot because it was too shaded. Seli says the tomato needed more light. Tavi says the bean required a support stick.

PIRI: These are not trades. These are placements.

NARA: The round is called "Trade Before Noon."

ODO: Then we have traded locations.

NARA: The card says trade a plant.

Tavi reads the reverse side again. It says "or swap one location." The host had been holding the card upside down.

SELI: We have been following the wrong side.

PIRI: The plants have been in the right spots.

Odo asks whether the empty pot can count as a trade. Nara says it has no plant. Odo offers to exchange it for compost. Seli gives him a scoop, and he fills the pot.

ODO: I have traded an empty pot for compost.

PIRI: That is a trade.

NARA: The plant-trading challenge is complete.

[THE PRODUCER CHECKS THE CONTEST CLOCK.]

The station bell gives its quarter-hour warning. Nara says the noon swap deadline has not arrived yet. Piri asks whether they have time for one final trade. Nara says they have until the noon bell, but the gardeners must stop when it rings.

A sudden shower begins. Odo covers the sunflower with a broad leaf. Seli moves the tomato under the eave. Tavi props the bean against the support stick.

ODO: The plants have been reassigned.

PIRI: That is not a competition rule.

NARA: It is a garden response.

Piri checks the rule card and finds a third line: "After the swap, water each seedling." Odo fills his pot, but it drains through a hole.

ODO: The pot cannot hold water.

SELI: It should have drainage.

NARA: The pot is doing its job.

Tavi puts a tray under it. The runoff goes to the bean plant.

### Act Three

[THE NOON BELL RINGS.]

Each gardener confirms the final location. Seli gives Tavi the tomato; Tavi puts it by the window. Tavi gives Seli the bean; she puts it beside the support stick. Odo keeps the sunflower in the sun. Nara takes back her clean spare pot; Odo keeps the pot filled with compost.

PIRI: Every seedling has a place.

NARA: And we have completed the swap.

ODO: I still have an empty pot.

SELI: You can trade it for compost.

NARA: Hosts cannot trade.

PIRI: The compost is not a contestant.

### Tag

[THE GARDENERS WATER THE PLANTS.]

Nara asks who won. Piri says the seedlings did. Odo asks whether that is a prize.

NARA: It is a result.

Tavi's bean curls around the empty pot's handle.

SELI: The pot has been chosen.

ODO: It has been adopted.

[SFX: WATERING CAN.]
