# The Choir Broadcast Has One Microphone

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live music broadcasts that promise intimate ensemble sound through one badly placed microphone*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: choir director Rema, a macaque; singer Oru, an orangutan; singer Pali, a lemur; audio engineer Tavi, a gibbon; host Sela, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. CONCERT HALL - EVENING

A choir of twelve stands on a stage. One microphone sits directly in front of the empty conductor's stand.

SELA: We are ready for a beautiful ensemble performance.

[The choir begins. The microphone records the stand creaking.]

ACT ONE

INT. HALL - CONTINUOUS

Tavi checks the audio meter. The choir is barely audible. He moves the microphone closer. It picks up Oru's breathing and the front row's page turns. Rema asks the singers to begin again. Sela introduces the song over the warm-up.

REMA: Please let us finish the warm-up first.

Sela begins again after they finish. Tavi places the microphone at the edge of the stage. The sound captures one singer and the ventilation fan. Pali sings a test note; the fan drowns it out. Tavi switches off the fan. The hall becomes hot, and the choir starts fanning itself with sheet music.

ACT TWO

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Tavi suggests recording from the balcony. Sela asks if the broadcast can still feel close. He raises the single microphone on a long boom. The boom blocks the camera's wide shot. Rema asks the choir to spread out. The singers do. The boom follows the front row and misses the back row.

[Oru sings the bass line. The microphone moves toward him. Pali sings the high note; the boom swings away too late.]

Sela tells the director to use the shot that shows the whole choir. Tavi sets the microphone between two singers. It catches a whispered count-in from Rema and a cough from Oru. The host asks if they can remove the cough in editing. Tavi says the microphone has recorded more breathing than singing.

INT. HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Rema marks a semicircle on the floor. The choir stands inside it. Tavi places the microphone above the center, angled down. The camera shows the boom. He lowers it behind the conductor's stand; now the stand blocks the center singers. Rema turns the stand sideways. The microphone sees the choir, but Sela cannot see the conductor.

Sela tries to conduct from beside the camera. She comes in one beat late. Rema stops the choir. Tavi says the single microphone can capture them if they sing together, but the camera angle must change. The director switches to a high wide shot and lets the boom remain visible at the edge.

INT. HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Rema asks Tavi to test the microphone with one note from each row. Oru sings from the back. Pali answers from the front. The levels are uneven, so Tavi moves the stand between them and asks the choir to keep their places. The camera frames the conductor and the full semicircle. Sela reads a short introduction, then steps away before Rema raises her hands. The choir waits for the downbeat without anyone speaking over it. Tavi tests the meter and leaves the microphone in place. The choir repeats its first note softly. Rema cues the entrance with her hand, and the singers follow together. Tavi captures the note without moving the boom. The final chord is clean. It rings evenly.

ACT THREE

INT. CONCERT HALL - NIGHT

The choir performs the song once, with the microphone over the center and the camera wide. The first verse sounds balanced. On the chorus, the singers turn their heads toward Rema. The microphone catches the full harmony. Sela sits quietly off to the side. Tavi watches the meter but leaves the controls alone.

At the final chord, one singer's music folder slips to the floor. The microphone captures the thump. The audience applauds. Sela asks whether they can retake the ending. Rema says the choir has finished. Tavi listens to the recording; the chord is clear, and the thump comes after it. He keeps the take.

Sela asks Rema to identify the piece. Rema names the song and composer. The host begins a sentimental narration about an intimate sound. The microphone remains pointed at the choir as they gather their music. The paper rustle fills the hall. Sela stops narrating and lets them leave in silence.

TAG

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - AFTER THE PERFORMANCE

Tavi labels the recording ONE MIC - FULL CHOIR. Sela watches the playback without speaking. Rema closes the last music folder.

FINAL IMAGE: The microphone hangs above the empty conductor's stand, recording the applause fading through the hall.
