# The Village Votes on a Very Small Fountain

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local political broadcasts that stage a major debate around a tiny public works project*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Mayor Mara, a macaque; councilor Vex, an orangutan; resident Nari, a lemur; clerk Pip, a capuchin; plumber Odo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. VILLAGE COUNCIL ROOM - EVENING

[A tabletop fountain burbles into a cup. Vex stands beside a scale model of the village.] 

VEX: Tonight we decide whether the fountain belongs in the square.

NARI: It is in the square.

[The fountain's pump stops.]

PIP: Now it belongs in the minutes.

[Theme music.]

ACT ONE

MARA: The repair budget is three coins. The fountain needs a new hose.

VEX: The question is whether we approve the hose, the basin, and a ribbon-cutting ceremony.

ODO: The hose is one coin. The basin is already there.

VEX: We should consider the full civic impact.

NARI: The impact is a small splash.

[Vex presents two posters. One shows a fountain with four tiers. The other shows a fountain labeled "PAUSE."] 

VEX: Option A is expansion. Option B is reflection.

MARA: Option B is the same fountain with a label.

PIP: I vote for water.

[He raises his hand. Vex writes "uncommitted" beside his name.] 

PIP: I said water.

VEX: We need a clear mandate.

ACT TWO

[The council moves outdoors for a live segment. The tabletop fountain has been placed on a bench. Odo examines the pump. Nari asks the group to step back while he checks the hose.] 

ODO: The intake is blocked by one leaf.

VEX: We cannot rush to a conclusion.

[Odo lifts the leaf with a twig. The fountain burbles again. The stream lands in the cup.]

PIP: It works.

VEX: We have not heard from the garden committee.

NARI: I am the garden committee. The water is good for the seedlings.

[She moves a seed tray under the drip. Vex moves it away to preserve the camera's frame. A sunbird lands on the bench and drinks from the fountain.] 

MARA: The public has arrived.

VEX: Is the bird registered to vote?

ODO: It knows where the water is.

[The bird splashes. Vex's poster gets wet at the bottom. The word "PAUSE" blurs into "PUDDLE."] 

VEX: The sign has changed position.

NARI: The water has changed the sign.

[The council attempts a roll-call vote. The fountain's pump sputters between each name. Pip's hand remains raised.]

MARA: Pip, your vote?

PIP: Water.

VEX: Recorded as "in favor of further discussion."

ACT THREE

[Odo fits a new hose. The pump starts and sends a small, steady stream into the basin. Mara asks for the vote. Vex tries to raise the two posters, but both are damp and stick together.] 

VEX: The options have formed a coalition.

NARI: They are two wet pieces of paper.

MARA: All in favor of keeping the repaired fountain?

[Everyone raises a hand, including Odo. The bird raises one wing. Vex does not raise his; he is holding the posters apart.] 

PIP: The bird voted.

VEX: We need to verify its address.

[The bird flies away. A single drop lands in Vex's open palm.] 

VEX: It has submitted water evidence.

MARA: The fountain stays. We will spend one coin on the hose and keep two for something that actually needs tiers.

[The stream arcs into the cup. The cup fills and tips, watering the seed tray. Nari slides the tray back under the drip.] 

NARI: The fountain has approved the garden plan.

ODO: I installed the hose.

NARI: The hose has implemented it.

[Odo sets the old hose he removed beside the model village. One end is too short to reach the painted fountain.]

ODO: The real hose fits. The model hose does not.

VEX: We should fund a scale expansion.

MARA: We are voting on a repair, not a miniature.

[The camera zooms in on the model. The painted fountain is three times the size of the houses.]

PIP: Is that the very small fountain?

NARI: The model makes it look important.

VEX: Exactly. Scale is a matter of perspective.

[The bird returns to the tabletop and drinks from the cup. It knocks a tiny paper flag into the basin. Vex tries to retrieve it with a long ruler; the ruler tips the model and sends a little wooden tree rolling across the table.]

MARA: The expansion plan has lost a tree.

ODO: The repair needs one hose and a clear intake.

VEX: Is there a ribbon for the opening?

NARI: We can cut the hose box string.

[Odo passes Vex the string. Vex holds it across the basin. The pump resumes, and the water trickles beneath it, soaking the string until it slips from his fingers.]

VEX: The ribbon has been officially opened.

TAG

[Vex returns to the council table and updates the minutes.] 

VEX: Motion passed, subject to public comment.

[The fountain burbles. He looks down at the wet page.] 

PIP: Public comment received.
