# The Commentary Booth Is Too Close

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports broadcast television, where commentators narrate a quiet practice drill as if it were a championship final*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Two commentators cover a routine passing drill, but their dramatic play-by-play makes the players stop to listen.

Cast: Commentator Odo, baboon; commentator Seli, capuchin; coach Nara, macaque; players Piri, a gibbon, Luma, a lemur, and Vex, a macaque; producer Tavi, a lemur.

## Episode: "Practice, Live"

### Cold Open

[FIELD. PIRI PASSES A BALL TO NARA. LUMA AND VEX WAIT BESIDE THE CONES TO JOIN THE DRILL.]

ODO: A historic exchange.

SELI: The ball has traveled three feet.

NARA: We are practicing.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE COMMENTARY BOOTH OVERLOOKS A SMALL FIELD.]

Tavi tells Odo and Seli the broadcast is meant to cover a simple passing drill. They have one microphone and no scoreboard.

ODO: We can create suspense.

SELI: Four people are on the field.

NARA: Three players and a coach.

PIRI: The coach is counting us too.

Odo describes the first pass as a bold opening. Nara returns the ball with one foot. Odo says she has "answered the challenge."

NARA: We are warming up.

SELI: The ball is still in bounds.

Piri passes to Nara again. The ball rolls slowly and stops short. Tavi asks the commentators not to shout; the players can hear them.

ODO: They need to hear our analysis.

NARA: We do not.

### Act Two

[THE COACH SETS CONES IN A TRIANGLE.]

Nara asks the players to pass around the cones without touching them. Seli narrates the route accurately. Odo adds a rivalry story between the cones.

ODO: The red cone has held the center all morning.

SELI: The red cone is a marker.

ODO: And the blue cone is closing in.

Piri passes the ball. It hits the red cone. The cone tips over.

ODO: A major upset.

NARA: Reset the cone.

Piri resets it, then passes to the blue cone by mistake. The ball stops beside it.

SELI: The play has ended.

ODO: The defense has held.

Nara asks the commentators to keep the players focused. Odo promises to lower his voice, then whispers dramatically into the microphone.

ODO: The ball approaches the triangle.

Piri stops mid-run and looks up at the booth.

PIRI: Is there a game?

NARA: There is a drill.

[NARA SETS UP A PRACTICE CONE DRILL.]

The team now asks Odo and Seli to commentate from the field while walking the route. Odo begins a dramatic call as he approaches the cone. He trips over the marker and catches himself.

ODO: A near collision!

NARA: The cone was in the marked space.

SELI: The commentator has entered the play.

Piri asks Odo to pass the ball to her. He does, but the ball rolls between his feet. He pauses to invent a replay description.

NARA: Keep moving.

ODO: I need to describe what happened.

PIRI: It is still happening.

Seli demonstrates the proper route around the cone. She narrates at a normal volume and completes the drill.

SELI: Pass, receive, return.

NARA: That is all we need.

Odo tries the same sequence. He adds a flourish, and the ball taps the cone.

ODO: An unexpected deflection.

NARA: Reset the cone.

### Act Three

[THE TEAM ADDS A FOURTH CONE.]

Nara changes the drill: players must call the receiver's name before passing. Odo says this will create a strategic layer. Seli asks if he can identify the players first.

ODO: Number one is approaching.

NARA: We do not wear numbers.

PIRI: I'm Piri.

SELI: Piri receives the pass.

Odo adds sound effects with his mouth. Tavi switches his microphone off. Odo continues making the sounds silently, visible through the booth glass.

Nara asks the team to complete the drill without commentary. They pass cleanly around all four cones. The ball returns to the starting point.

NARA: Good work. That is the drill.

PIRI: Can we do it again?

NARA: Yes.

Seli says the players have completed a successful sequence. Odo asks whether there is a winner.

NARA: The goal is practice.

ODO: Everyone wins.

The coach marks a line for the commentary booth. Odo and Seli move their chairs behind it. Their voices no longer interrupt the players.

ODO: We are outside the field.

SELI: The players can still hear us.

NARA: Only if you shout.

ODO: I will whisper.

Piri passes the ball, and Odo raises one hand but says nothing. Seli smiles at the camera.

SELI: Silence is also a broadcast choice.

### Tag

[THE COMMENTATORS STAND BESIDE THE FIELD.]

SELI: The broadcast is over.

ODO: The players are beginning another round.

TAVI: We can keep recording.

NARA: From farther away.

Odo moves the booth chair back one inch.

NARA: Farther.

Odo moves another inch.

PIRI: We can hear you.

[SFX: BALL ROLLS, COMMENTATORS WHISPER.]
