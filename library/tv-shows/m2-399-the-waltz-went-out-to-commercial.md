# The Waltz Went Out to Commercial

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised dance competitions, judge score paddles, slow-motion replays, and commercial breaks timed to a dramatic pause*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: Two monkey dancers reach the final turn of a televised waltz when the sponsor break cuts their music, leaving the judges to score the silence.

Cast: Host Mavi, a macaque; dancers Reko, a gibbon, and Suli, a capuchin; judge Odo, a baboon; judge Tavi, an orangutan; bandleader Nema, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. DANCE STUDIO - NIGHT

Reko and Suli sweep across the floor. Their waltz music stops mid-turn. They continue three silent steps and finish in a bow.

MAVI: We have reached the commercial break.

NEMA: The music has reached it too.

ACT ONE

Mavi introduces the final round. Each pair dances for ninety seconds. Odo and Tavi will score timing, control, and use of the floor. Reko and Suli ask if the music will be uninterrupted. Nema says it is recorded and will be played through the full track.

REKO: Then why did it stop?

MAVI: The sponsor clock is set to sixty seconds.

SULI: The dance is ninety.

Mavi apologizes and asks them to start again. Nema restarts the track. The pair moves through the first half. Mavi watches the clock, then signals the camera for a close-up of their feet. The camera cable crosses the dance floor. Suli steps over it without breaking rhythm.

TAVI: Good control.

ODO: The cable is not part of the score.

ACT TWO

The dancers rehearse the final turn. Suli asks Reko to keep his arm lower so her tail clears his sleeve. Nema cues the music. The sponsor clock appears at forty seconds. The melody reaches the same point as before; the track cuts off again.

MAVI: That was the break cue.

NEMA: That was the second half of the song.

Odo raises a ten paddle. Tavi holds up a seven. The numbers are for the previous performance, but Mavi thinks they have judged the rehearsal. Neri, offstage, signals to lower the paddles. Odo lowers his; Tavi leaves hers up.

TAVI: I have not scored the final.

MAVI: We are building suspense.

SULI: We are building another rehearsal.

ACT TWO-B

Nema moves the sponsor clock to the end of the song. The pair performs again. This time the music plays to the end, but the studio lights switch to a commercial graphic. The floor turns blue. Suli's steps remain visible; Reko's jacket blends into the backdrop.

REKO: I have become the floor.

SULI: Keep turning. I can see your hands.

They finish together. Odo praises their timing. Tavi says the lighting made one partner disappear during the final turn. Mavi asks the judges whether they can score the dance separately from the broadcast lighting.

TAVI: Yes. The dance was clear; the camera was not.

Odo gives Reko and Suli an eight for timing, a nine for control, and an eight for floor use. Tavi gives them nine, nine, and eight. Mavi asks the band to play the ending once more, without dancers, so the technical crew can reset the lights.

Before the judges total the cards, Nema asks the dancers to show the final eight counts without music so she can line up the camera cut. Reko and Suli count under their breath. Reko starts early; Suli catches his wrist and waits for the downbeat. They finish together without moving from their marks.

NEMA: That is the transition we lost to the commercial.

SULI: We did not lose it. We danced it.

MAVI: The audience did not see it.

TAVI: They can see it now.

Piri gives a close-up of their feet, then a wide shot that includes both dancers. Odo asks the crew to leave the transition in the broadcast replay. The sponsor clock lights up, but Nema removes its power cable before it can cut the picture.

NEMA: We have the ending and the full performance.

REKO: Then the next dance can start on time.

ACT THREE

The judges total the scores on paper. Reko and Suli earn fifty-one points and win the round. Mavi announces the score once. The audience applauds; Nema plays a short closing phrase only after the result is read.

SULI: We finished the whole dance.

REKO: We also finished a silent ending.

MAVI: The silent ending is not a scored category.

Odo asks production to move the cable off the floor before the next broadcast. Tavi asks that the lights stay white through the final turn. Nema marks the full-length track in the cue sheet. The sponsor clock starts after the performance, not during it.

TAG

As the credits roll, Mavi tries to thank the judges. The sponsor clock reaches zero and displays "PLEASE HOLD." Odo keeps his score paddle lowered.

FINAL IMAGE: Reko and Suli leave the floor together while the final waltz phrase plays without interruption behind them.
