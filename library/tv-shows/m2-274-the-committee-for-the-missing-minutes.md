# The Committee for the Missing Minutes

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: panel discussion television, where experts debate why a meeting starts late while the meeting itself keeps being delayed*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A panel investigates why the village meeting always begins late, but every panelist arrives with another theory and another agenda item.

Cast: Host Nara, macaque; timekeeper Odo, baboon; panelist Piri, capuchin; panelist Seli, lemur; audience questioner Tavi, gibbon.

## Episode: "Five Minutes Past"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO CLOCK SHOWS 6:05. EMPTY CHAIRS.]

NARA: We are here to discuss why meetings start late.

ODO: The meeting has not started yet.

PIRI: That is the problem.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE PANELISTS TAKE THEIR PLACES.]

Nara introduces the topic. Odo says the clock is five minutes fast. Piri says the meeting notice listed 6:00. Seli says the chairs were still being arranged at 6:00.

NARA: Then the delay began before the meeting.

ODO: The room is part of the schedule.

PIRI: We should begin with the agenda.

SELI: The agenda is not printed.

Tavi brings in a stack of pages. They are blank on one side and have last week's notes on the other.

TAVI: I printed the new agenda on the back.

NARA: What is the first item?

TAVI: Why meetings start late.

ODO: We are already discussing it.

Nara asks the panel to vote whether the discussion counts as the meeting. Odo says not until the chair calls it to order.

### Act Two

[THE CLOCK ADVANCES.]

Piri argues the meeting is late because people wait for everyone to arrive. Seli says starting without someone wastes time because they must repeat the discussion. Odo says the clock is not the cause.

PIRI: We need a start signal.

ODO: The chair's gavel.

NARA: The gavel is missing.

Tavi finds it under the stack of blank agendas. He taps it once. The sound is too quiet for the studio.

[SFX: SOFT TAP.]

SELI: We did not hear it.

ODO: It was a gavel tap.

PIRI: It was a table tap.

Nara asks Tavi to hit it harder. The gavel knocks a cup over. Water spreads across the agenda pages.

NARA: We now have a water problem.

ODO: Put it under new business.

Tavi hands Seli a cloth. She wipes the table while Piri asks whether cleaning counts as meeting time.

[THE PRODUCER OPENS THE FLOOR TO AN AUDIENCE QUESTION.]

Tavi asks whether meetings would start on time if the agenda were shorter. Piri says the agenda is already short. Seli holds up the blank page.

NARA: The agenda is not printed.

ODO: It is on the back.

PIRI: The back has last week's notes.

TAVI: Those notes say the meeting started late.

Seli reads the previous minutes. The first item is "Discuss why meetings start late." The meeting began at 6:12 and lasted until 6:40.

ODO: We spent twenty-eight minutes on the delay.

PIRI: And made no plan.

NARA: We have made a plan now.

Tavi asks whether the plan is in the minutes. Seli writes it on the blank side. The ink bleeds through and covers the prior notes.

SELI: The record has changed.

ODO: The old record is still legible.

NARA: We are not on the panel.

NARA: We are discussing it.

### Act Three

[THE PANEL AGREES ON A TRIAL.]

They will start at 6:00 even if not everyone is seated. Odo sets the clock by the station bell. Seli marks the chairs. Piri places the agenda on the table. Nara calls the meeting to order.

NARA: The meeting is now in session.

ODO: It is 6:07.

PIRI: We are seven minutes late.

SELI: The trial starts next week.

TAVI: Then today's meeting is late.

Nara asks if they can use the remaining time to discuss a shorter agenda. Odo proposes removing the opening announcements. Piri says the announcements contain the start time. Seli suggests posting the time outside.

NARA: Then people can arrive before we begin.

ODO: That is a new agenda item.

The panel votes to post the notice earlier and begin at the stated time. Tavi writes it down.

[THE PANEL CHECKS THE CLOCK.]

Odo adjusts the clock to the station bell. The time is now accurate.

NARA: We can begin next week at six.

PIRI: We should arrive at 5:55.

SELI: That is the plan.

NARA: We will start at six.

ODO: The clock is now accurate.

PIRI: The panel is adjourned.

### Tag

[STUDIO CLOCK SHOWS 6:15.]

NARA: We have resolved the issue.

ODO: The meeting has ended.

PIRI: It started seven minutes late and ended on time.

SELI: Which time?

Tavi points to the clock. Its second hand has stopped against the glass.

TAVI: It stopped at 6:15.

[SFX: GAVEL TAP, THEME.]
