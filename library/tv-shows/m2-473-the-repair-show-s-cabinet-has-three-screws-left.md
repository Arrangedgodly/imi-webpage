# The Repair Show's Cabinet Has Three Screws Left

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live home-repair television, improvised fixes, and countdown clocks that pressure hosts to finish before the glue dries*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Pavi, a gibbon; builder Mako, an orangutan; caller Jori, a macaque; safety lead Toma, a baboon; camera operator Eni, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. REPAIR SHOW SET - DAY

A small cabinet has one loose hinge. Three screws sit on a tray. The host holds a screwdriver and watches a countdown reading FIVE MINUTES.

PAVI: We will repair this cabinet before time runs out.

MAKO: It needs one screw tightened.

TOMA: The door is open and supported. Put the screwdriver down until I check the hinge.

[The host places the screwdriver on the table, but the clock keeps ticking.] 

ACT ONE

INT. REPAIR SET - CONTINUOUS

The builder checks the hinge and sees one screw is missing. The other two are snug. The caller joins by video and asks whether the cabinet can hold heavy bowls. Mako asks for the cabinet's model and wall position before answering.

JORI: It stands on the floor against a wall.

MAKO: Then the hinge carries the door, not the cabinet's weight. We should replace the missing screw with the same size.

PAVI: Can we use a longer screw for extra strength?

TOMA: Not without checking what is behind the panel.

[The safety lead checks the back of the cabinet and confirms the standard screw length. The host points to the timer.] 

PAVI: Three minutes.

MAKO: The cabinet does not know that.

[The builder aligns the hinge and threads the screw by hand so it does not cross-thread. The camera operator zooms in, then pulls out when the host's sleeve blocks the lens.] 

ENI: I need room for the hand and the hinge.

ACT TWO

INT. REPAIR SET - LATER

The screw is seated. Mako tests the door while Toma supports the cabinet. It closes but catches slightly at the bottom. The host declares the repair done. Jori says the cabinet door at home also rubs the floor.

MAKO: This door is catching because the hinge shifted while the screw was loose.

PAVI: We have one minute left.

TOMA: We need to loosen the hinge, align the door, and retighten all three screws. The timer does not change the steps.

[The host begins the countdown music. The safety lead turns it down. Mako uses a spacer to hold the door aligned while tightening the screws in order. Jori watches the demonstration from the screen.] 

JORI: Do I tighten all three or only the new one?

MAKO: All three, after the door is aligned. Do not force the old screws if they turn freely.

[The host tries to summarize the instructions while Mako works. The camera operator asks for silence to record the tool sounds. The host whispers the countdown. The door is aligned and closes without rubbing.] 

PAVI: The cabinet is fixed.

TOMA: Test it once more, then stop.

[The builder opens and closes the door once. The hinge stays secure. The host raises the screwdriver for a final flourish but Toma asks him to put it down.] 

[Jori asks whether the hinge should be checked again after the cabinet is moved. Mako says the test belongs on a stable floor, so they set it back at its original spot. Toma checks that the test area is clear.] 

MAKO: We are not testing it while someone lifts it.

PAVI: The camera can follow the move after the segment.

[The builder opens and closes the door once at the marked spot. It stays aligned. Jori confirms the cabinet is the same model as the caller described, and Toma asks that the label remain visible for the recording.] 

JORI: I can compare the screw size before I buy one.

TOMA: And stop if the hinge shifts.

[The host tries to turn the test into a final countdown. The producer removes the countdown card from the table.] 

ACT THREE

INT. REPAIR SHOW SET - AFTERNOON

The caller describes the hinge and confirms the cabinet is on the floor. Mako tells Jori to use the same size replacement screw and align the door before tightening the set. Toma asks the caller to avoid overloading the door. The host turns the countdown off.

JORI: The repair makes sense. I can check the cabinet label first.

PAVI: We have finished with twenty seconds on the clock.

TOMA: The clock was not part of the repair.

[The crew returns the screwdriver to its rack. The builder labels the spare screws by size. The cabinet's door remains closed; no one hangs weight on it for a stunt.] 

MAKO: The hinge is stable. We can move to the next project.

PAVI: What is next?

ENI: A chair with a loose leg.

[The host looks at the timer, then at Toma. The safety lead removes the countdown sign before the next segment begins.] 

TAG

[The repaired cabinet door stays closed as the crew rolls it away. The host reaches for the countdown, finds only the blank stand, and nods.] 

FINAL IMAGE: Three labeled screws rest in the tray beside the stable cabinet hinge.
