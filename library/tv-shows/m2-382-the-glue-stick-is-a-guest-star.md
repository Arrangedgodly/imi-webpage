# The Glue Stick Is a Guest Star

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: instructional craft television, cheerful step-by-step narration, and hosts who improvise when materials disappear*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A craft host plans to teach paper lanterns, but a missing glue stick forces her to choose between the perfect demonstration and a workable fold.

Cast: Host Piri, a capuchin; assistant Boma, an orangutan; camera operator Vali, a macaque; voice-over Neri, a gibbon; floor producer Seko, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. CRAFT STUDIO - DAY

Piri holds up a paper lantern. It falls apart in her hands. The glue stick rolls off the table and under a monitor.

PIRI: We are making a lantern.

BOMA: The glue has left the program.

SEKO: We have three minutes until the recorded instructions start.

ACT ONE

Piri opens a second kit. It contains paper, string, scissors and no glue. The voice-over begins anyway.

NERI (VOICE-OVER): Apply a small amount of adhesive to the top edge.

PIRI: We don't have adhesive.

NERI (VOICE-OVER): Smooth the edge firmly.

PIRI: That is a different step.

Vali changes the camera angle so the empty glue space is outside the frame. Boma folds the paper into a fan and clips its ends together with a paper fastener. Piri turns it into a cylinder. It holds, but leans sideways.

PIRI: That's a lantern with a question.

SEKO: Can we use tape?

BOMA: The tape is holding the camera cable.

ACT TWO

Neri's voice-over continues from a prerecorded track. It describes placing a handle. Piri cuts a strip of paper. Her scissors are blunt, and the strip comes out jagged. Boma tries trimming it, then stops when the paper begins to tear.

BOMA: We can use string.

PIRI: The string is for hanging the lantern.

VALI: We could show both versions.

SEKO: The instructions promise one easy method.

PIRI: It is easy if the materials are here.

Boma pulls the fallen glue stick out from under the monitor. The cap is still on. Piri opens it, but the glue has dried into a hard nub. Neri's voice-over says to use a "generous line." Piri holds up the stick. It makes a faint scratch on the paper.

PIRI: The guest star is unavailable.

Neri's track continues: "Press for ten seconds." Piri counts to ten while holding the paper. The cylinder opens at eight. She tries again with a paper clip. The clip holds one seam, but catches on the handle.

ACT TWO-B

Boma unfolds the paper and makes two diagonal cuts, stopping short of the edge. He interlaces the strips instead of gluing them. Piri watches, then follows the pattern. The paper forms a lantern shape. It is wider at one end.

PIRI: Does it need to be symmetrical?

BOMA: It needs to stay together.

Vali films the fold. Seko asks for a close-up of the missing glue. Piri moves the dried stick into frame beside the stable lantern.

PIRI: Here is the kit we expected. Here is the fold that works without it.

NERI (VOICE-OVER): For a smooth finish, apply a generous line of adhesive.

PIRI: The track is from yesterday.

SEKO: We can't stop the recording.

Piri turns the finished lantern and demonstrates how the paper interlocks. Boma attaches the string through two punched holes. Vali lifts the lantern; it stays closed. The voice-over finally reaches the closing credits and thanks the audience for using the glue stick.

Seko holds up a card for the next segment: "SECOND MATERIAL TEST - PAPER BAG." Neri's recorded track resets to its opening as Seko reads a viewer question: "Can I make this with a paper bag?" Boma tries the fold on a brown bag. Its bottom seam opens, and the bag flattens. He folds the bottom inward and clips it shut with the paper fastener.

BOMA: This is a different material, so test the fold.

PIRI: We can show one bag version, but not promise every bag will hold.

The second segment's recording says, "Press the glued seam until it is secure." Piri asks Vali to cut that audio. Vali points out that the camera is still live. Piri looks into lens and demonstrates the fastener slowly, using the same two cuts and interlocking strip.

PIRI: If you don't have a fastener, fold the top edge over itself.

The brown lantern holds its shape. Seko tries to hang it from the set hook. The hook is too high, so Boma lowers it before the paper handle stretches.

ACT THREE

Piri records a new voice-over: "If your glue is missing or dry, use the interlocking fold, then test the handle before hanging." Seko asks if she can repeat the original line, so both takes can be used. Piri agrees, but only after Vali marks which track is current.

PIRI: We made a paper lantern with a fold, not glue.

BOMA: The paper fastener stays with the lantern. The tape still secures the camera cable.

Vali checks the lantern handle. It holds. Seko leaves the cable tape in place and turns the monitor toward the finished lantern.

TAG

Piri turns off the studio lights. The paper lantern glows faintly from the window.

PIRI: It is not lit from inside.

BOMA: It is reflecting the studio sign.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera holds on the lantern while Neri's recorded voice thanks the glue stick for its contribution.
