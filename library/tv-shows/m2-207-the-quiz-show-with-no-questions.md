# The Quiz Show with No Questions

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: panel game show convention, where contestants win points for correcting the host's confusing instructions instead of answering questions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A quiz show host has lost the question cards, so contestants earn points by pointing out that there are no questions.

Cast: Host Tavi, a capuchin; contestants Pava, a gibbon, and Odo, a macaque; scorekeeper Sela, a lemur; and announcer Rell, a baboon.

Episode: "Final Answer Pending"

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO, DAY

Rell announces round one. Tavi opens the card box. It is empty.

TAVI: What is the capital of the grove?

PAVA: There is no city.

Sela rings the bell.

ACT ONE

Tavi tries another prompt: "Name something that grows on a tree." Odo answers "a branch." Tavi says the expected answer was "leaves." Sela checks the rulebook, which says any correct answer counts. Odo gets a point.

Tavi asks for a bonus question. Rell hands him a card from the wrong stack. It contains stage directions: "Contestants look surprised." Pava looks surprised. Tavi awards a point for following instructions.

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO, LATER

The contestants compete to identify categories. Tavi reads a card that says "Question goes here." Pava says it is a placeholder. Sela says placeholders do not count. Rell searches the prop cabinet and finds a card with a drawing of a banana. Odo identifies it as a banana, then asks if it is a question.

Tavi invents a riddle: "What has a beginning, middle and end?" Pava says a story. Odo says a trail. Both answers are accepted. Sela explains that the show has no criteria for the bonus round.

Rell tries to improvise a tie-breaker: "Who can tell me what I am thinking?" Pava asks if he is thinking about missing cards. Rell looks down at the empty box. Tavi awards her a point.

ACT TWO-B

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO, AFTERNOON

Sela finds the missing question cards in the recycling bin. The questions are intact, but the category labels have been cut off. Tavi says they can still use the questions. Pava objects that without categories, contestants cannot prepare. Odo says the show was meant to be a surprise.

Rell reads a question about a fruit that grows in a bunch. Odo answers "figs." Pava answers "grapes." Tavi says both grow in bunches depending on the tree. Sela checks the card: it asks for "a fruit that hangs from a stem." Both answers fit. The contestants tie.

The next card asks them to name the sound of a door closing. Pava says "thunk." Odo says "click." Rell demonstrates with three doors: one creaks, one thuds, and one slides silently. Sela gives a point to each contestant for a different accurate answer.

Tavi announces a speed round. The contestants must answer before the timer. He asks what comes after three. Both contestants say four. The timer rings before Sela can award points. Rell says the timer is now the winner. Sela checks the rules and sees the timer is not listed as a contestant.

ACT TWO-C

INT. SCORE TABLE, LATE AFTERNOON

Sela reconstructs the score from the cards. Each answer has a mark, but the marks are in three different colors. Tavi says one color means correct; another means funny; the third means unclear. Rell says he used the wrong pencil. Sela sorts the answers and awards points only for correct responses.

The score is tied again. Pava suggests a final question everyone can answer: what does the show need? Odo says questions. Tavi says a full card box. Sela says a paper label on the question drawer. Rell writes "QUESTIONS" on the box and puts the cards back in it.

ACT TWO-D

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO, EVENING

The host tests each buzzer before the final round. Odo rings his by leaning on it. Pava presses hers once. The sound is identical. Sela writes a new rule: answers must be spoken before either buzzer sounds. Tavi tries the rule with a practice question. Both contestants answer at once, and the audience applauds.

Rell asks if applause counts as a response. Sela says it is not on the scorecard. Tavi puts the buzzer box away and hands each contestant a chalkboard. Pava writes her answer. Odo writes his. They reveal both boards at the same time, and both show the same answer.

ACT THREE



Sela totals the scores. Pava wins by identifying the missing questions. Tavi gives her a trophy shaped like a question mark. It is upside down. Odo asks if that means the answer is uncertain. Sela turns it upright and declares the game complete.

TAG

Rell finds the question cards beneath the host desk. He asks if they should play another round. Tavi looks at the clock and says the show is over. The studio audience asks a question: "Can we go home?"

Sela rings the bell.
