# The Quest for the Slightly Bent Spoon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: animated adventure serials, where a team seeks a legendary object that turns out to be a kitchen utensil*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: An adventuring crew follows an old map to recover the Spoon of Dawn before its ceremonial breakfast, but the spoon is still in the pantry.

Cast: Captain Nima, a macaque; map-reader Piri, a capuchin; engineer Odo, a baboon; and keeper Luma, a gibbon.

## Episode: "The Spoon of Dawn"

### Cold Open

[ANIMATED HILLS. A MAP UNFURLS, THEN ROLLS BACK UP.]

NIMA: The spoon is near.

PIRI: The map says east.

ODO: The map is upside down.

LUMA: The pantry is west.

NIMA: We will take the high road.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW STANDS AT A FORK IN A PATH. A SIGN POINTS TO PANTRY AND HILL.]

PIRI: The legend says the spoon was hidden beyond the three hills.

LUMA: The pantry is beyond the kitchen.

NIMA: We have traveled too far to turn back.

Odo checks the map. It has a drawing of a spoon next to a bowl. Piri says the bowl symbolizes a lake.

ODO: The bowl is labeled "breakfast."

PIRI: It could be a lake called Breakfast.

Nima leads them up the hill. A loose branch blocks the path. Odo moves it aside.

[SFX: BRANCH THUD.]

NIMA: The mountain has tested us.

LUMA: The branch fell from a tree.

PIRI: The tree is part of the mountain.

### Act Two

[THE CREW REACHES THE SECOND HILL.]

Piri compares the map to the landscape. The spoon symbol points down toward a nearby shed.

PIRI: The artifact is underground.

LUMA: The shed has a basement.

ODO: It has a pantry door.

NIMA: The door is guarded.

Luma opens the door. It squeaks. The crew jumps back.

NIMA: A warning.

LUMA: A hinge.

Odo applies a drop of oil. The door opens quietly. Inside, they find shelves of bowls, cups, and spoons.

PIRI: The chamber of utensils.

LUMA: The pantry.

Nima searches the shelf for a golden spoon. Luma points to a slightly bent spoon beside the oats.

NIMA: It is not golden.

ODO: It is slightly bent.

PIRI: The legend described a crooked path.

LUMA: It described the spoon.

[THE CREW REACHES THE TOP PANTRY SHELF.]

A round flour crock blocks the path. Nima asks Odo to turn its spoon-shaped handle.

NIMA: The final gate.

LUMA: That is a flour crock.

PIRI: The map shows a stone arch.

Odo pulls. The lid sticks, then opens. A wooden spoon rolls out and taps the shelf.

[SFX: CROCK LID, WOODEN TAP.]

NIMA: The guardian has left a weapon.

LUMA: That is the spoon rack.

PIRI: It has a wooden handle. It could be ceremonial.

Nima picks it up and looks into the pantry below. They see the bowl, oats, and a rack of spoons.

ODO: The treasure chamber is visible.

LUMA: It is the pantry.

NIMA: We must enter with care.

Luma opens the door fully. The crew steps inside. Nima checks each shelf, asking if the objects are ancient. Luma says the bowls were bought last year; Odo recognizes the bent spoon from breakfast.

PIRI: The object has been in use all along.

NIMA: Then someone knew the legend.

LUMA: It is my kitchen.

Piri finds a second spoon, perfectly straight, and compares it with the bent one.

PIRI: One is ordinary. One has survived a bend.

NIMA: The trial has tested its strength.

ODO: It was bent in the drawer.

Luma confirms that the drawer sticks. Nima asks if that makes the spoon more valuable. Luma says it makes the drawer less convenient.

### Act Three

[THE CREW EXAMINES THE SPOON UNDER A LANTERN.]

Nima asks if the spoon is the Spoon of Dawn. Luma says it is the spoon used at breakfast. Odo tests it in a bowl of porridge. It scoops normally.

NIMA: It has no special power.

LUMA: It can serve breakfast.

PIRI: That is a useful power.

The crew hears a bell from the dining room. The ceremonial breakfast is beginning. Nima carries the spoon. The path back down is shorter than the way up, but Piri insists they take the high road to preserve the story.

ODO: The breakfast will get cold.

PIRI: The story will stay warm.

Nima takes the short path. They arrive at the dining room just in time. The spoon is placed beside the bowl.

### Tag

[THE CREW SITS AT THE TABLE.]

NIMA: The quest is complete.

LUMA: The spoon is where it belongs.

PIRI: The map was accurate.

ODO: It was upside down.

PIRI: It pointed to the right kitchen.

[SFX: SPOON TAPS BOWL.]

NIMA: The Spoon of Dawn has spoken.

LUMA: It is breakfast.
