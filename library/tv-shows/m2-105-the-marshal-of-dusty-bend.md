# The Marshal of Dusty Bend

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: western serials with saloon standoffs, hard-edged narration and frontier justice over a small shared resource*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: The people of Dusty Bend gather to decide who can use the only shade tree at noon. A dramatic marshal's showdown is complicated by a troop of monkeys who value the tree for grooming and resting.

Cast: Marshal Vale, a baboon; deputy Lio, a gibbon; rancher Mox, a capuchin; traveler Jun, a macaque; and the town troop.

## Episode: "Noon at the Cottonwood"

### Cold Open

[WIND BLOWS DUST ACROSS A SINGLE TREE. MOX STANDS WITH ONE HAND ON THE TRUNK.]

VALE: This town ain't wide enough for two claims on one shade.

JUN: I did not claim it. I stopped walking.

[MANY MONKEYS DROP FROM THE BRANCHES AND STARE AT THEM.]

VALE: Looks like the tree has spoken.

### Act One

[THE TOWN GATHERS OUTSIDE THE GENERAL STORE. A PAPER NOTICE READS "SHADE ROTATION: NOON."]

LIO: Mox says he planted the tree.

MOX: I watered it when it was small.

JUN: The troop has been resting here all season.

VALE: We will hear both sides before the sun reaches the mark.

[Mox points to a dry patch of dirt.]

MOX: I marked my claim with a stone.

[JUN MOVES THE STONE, MAKING ROOM FOR A WATER BOWL.]

JUN: I moved it so the bowl would not tip.

MOX: That is my mark.

LIO: It is now a bowl stand.

VALE: Nobody draws a weapon over shade.

MOX: I have no weapon.

JUN: He has a fence post.

[MOX LOOKS AT THE POST HE CARRIED.]

MOX: It is for the fence.

VALE: Put it down until the town decides.

### Act Two

[VALE SETS THREE CHALK LINES UNDER THE TREE: WEST, CENTER, EAST.]

VALE: We can divide the shade by time.

LIO: The shadow will move.

VALE: Then the line moves with it.

JUN: The troop grooms in the morning and rests at noon.

MOX: I need the shade to fix my roof.

VALE: You can work before noon.

MOX: The roof is hottest then.

[THE TROOP SHIFTS BRANCHES. A PATCH OF SHADE MOVES OVER MOX'S ROOFING MATERIAL.]

LIO: The shade is already changing.

JUN: We could move the work table here.

MOX: The tree is on the town side of the fence.

VALE: The tree is on the ground.

[MOX TRIES TO LIFT THE TABLE. A MONKEY SITS ON IT.]

MOX: I cannot move the table with a passenger.

JUN: It is not a passenger. It lives here.

[THE MONKEY PICKS UP A ROOFING SHINGLE AND CARRIES IT TO A BRANCH.]

MOX: That is my shingle.

VALE: The tree has a claim on it now.

LIO: Marshal.

VALE: I am considering the evidence.

[THE DEPUTY LAYS A TOWN MAP ON A BARREL.]

LIO: The tree is in the center of the public path. Anyone can use the shade.

MOX: I put up a fence to keep the cattle away.

JUN: The fence blocks the troop from reaching the water bowl.

VALE: Could the fence move one post to the west?

MOX: It would leave a gap.

LIO: A gap for people to walk through.

[MOX MEASURES THE SPACE WITH HIS BOOT. THE TROOP WATCHES FROM THE TREE.]

MOX: One post would be enough.

JUN: The bowl can sit beside it.

[THEY MOVE THE POST. A MONKEY CLIMBS ONTO IT AND USES IT TO REACH THE LOW BRANCH.]

VALE: The fence is now a ladder.

MOX: It is still a fence.

LIO: It is doing two jobs.

MOX: I did not hire it for that.

[THE TROOP SETTLES ABOVE THEM. MOX LOOKS UP, THEN SHAKES HIS HEAD.]

MOX: I will repair the fence tomorrow.

VALE: The post stays movable.

MOX: The post stays where the shade needs it.

### Act Three

[THE TOWN MARKS A WORK AREA BESIDE THE TREE AND A REST AREA UNDER IT.]

VALE: Mox gets the shade at noon to repair his roof. The troop keeps the upper branches. The water bowl stays in the center.

MOX: That is not how I pictured the claim.

JUN: You still get the shade.

MOX: And the troop gets the shingles?

[JUN RETRIEVES THE SHINGLE AND HANDS IT BACK.]

JUN: We can leave the building materials on the other side.

[MOX SETS THE POST AS A RACK FOR TOOLS. THE TROOP CLIMBS UP, LEAVING THE TABLE CLEAR.]

LIO: The town has reached a settlement.

VALE: No shots fired, no fences moved.

MOX: I still need a new mark.

[HE DRAWS A CHALK CIRCLE AROUND THE WATER BOWL.]

JUN: That marks the bowl.

MOX: It is a start.

[THE WIND BLOWS THE CHALK AWAY. THE MONKEYS CROWD INTO THE SHADE. VALE LOOKS INTO THE SUN.]

VALE: We will reopen this case tomorrow at noon.
