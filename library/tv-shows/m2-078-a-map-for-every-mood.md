# A Map for Every Mood

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: lifestyle mapping shows, personalized itineraries, and hosts who overread ordinary paths*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A cheerful host guides viewers on a route for every mood, but the mapmaker insists the paths are simply the same path walked at different speeds.

Cast: Host Ravo, a gibbon; mapmaker Nala, a macaque; hiker Timo, a capuchin; local expert Sori, an orangutan; and field producer Venn, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Path for a Big Feeling"

### Cold open

EXT. TRAILHEAD, MORNING

[Ravo stands beside a sign with three arrows pointing along the same trail.]

RAVO: Today we find the perfect path for every mood.

NALA: There is one path.

TIMO: I feel adventurous.

RAVO: Take the left arrow.

SORI: It points to the same trail.

RAVO: The trail has many emotional entrances.

VENN: The camera is ready.

NALA: The map is upside down.

[They turn the map. All arrows still point the same way.]

### Act One

EXT. TRAIL

[Ravo walks briskly. Nala follows with a compass.]

RAVO: For confidence, walk with your shoulders high.

TIMO: I feel tired.

RAVO: The tired path is over there.

NALA: There is no other path.

SORI: You can rest under that tree.

RAVO: The tree is a reflective pause.

TIMO: It's shade.

VENN: We can film a montage.

[They walk in a line. Venn films Timo's feet.]

RAVO: Each step is a choice.

NALA: Each step is forward.

SORI: Unless you turn around.

RAVO: The route for regret.

TIMO: I regret taking the map.

NALA: It is a map of the trail.

TIMO: The trail is right here.

RAVO: The map is guiding you inward.

NALA: The map is guiding you to the creek.

### Act Two

EXT. CREEK CROSSING

[The trail reaches a shallow creek. Three stepping stones are placed in a row.]

RAVO: This is the path for courage.

TIMO: I can jump across.

NALA: The stones are for walking.

SORI: The water is ankle-deep.

RAVO: The water has a deep meaning.

TIMO: I am already across.

[He walks through the water. His feet are wet.]

VENN: The crossing was bold.

TIMO: It was short.

NALA: We should mark the stepping stones on the map.

RAVO: The map already has them.

NALA: They are drawn on the other bank.

RAVO: It is a future map.

SORI: It is reversed.

[They turn the page. A small drawing of the creek appears.]

RAVO: The map has revealed the path.

NALA: I drew it this morning.

TIMO: We are standing on the path.

RAVO: The path for presence.

VENN: The show needs another mood.

SORI: We can choose a quiet mood.

RAVO: The quiet route is the same route with less talking.

NALA: That is a practical suggestion.

### Map test

EXT. CREEK TRAIL

[Venn holds the camera while Nala asks Ravo to guide them back to the studio.]

NALA: Which way is home?

RAVO: Choose the path for confidence.

TIMO: That is the creek again.

SORI: We are on the other side now.

RAVO: Then choose the path for adventure.

NALA: There is only one path.

RAVO: The map will tell us.

[Nala rotates the map. A leaf is stuck over the trail mark.]

TIMO: The map is covered.

RAVO: The path is asking us to trust ourselves.

SORI: The path is asking us to remove the leaf.

[Nala lifts it. The trail is visible.]

NALA: Home is straight ahead.

RAVO: A very direct feeling.

### Act Three

EXT. TRAILHEAD, AFTERNOON

[The group returns to the same sign. Ravo changes the arrows so they point toward different parts of the trail.]

RAVO: We have completed three journeys.

NALA: We completed one walk.

TIMO: I crossed the creek, saw the tree, and came back.

SORI: That is a complete trip.

RAVO: Which mood was your favorite?

TIMO: Dry feet.

RAVO: A peaceful mood.

NALA: We should leave the sign as it was.

VENN: The map needs a final reveal.

[They unfold it. It is the same map, with Ravo's handwritten notes in the margins: "bold branch," "quiet creek," "regret turn."]

NALA: Those are not paths.

RAVO: They are ways to experience the path.

TIMO: I experienced it by walking.

SORI: The map helped us find our way back.

NALA: The trailhead never moved.

RAVO: The host has learned something.

TIMO: What?

RAVO: Not every mood needs a different arrow.

VENN: That's our closing thought.

NALA: Can we go home now?

RAVO: The route for home is behind us.

### Tag

EXT. TRAILHEAD, EVENING

[The map flaps in the wind. It points toward the same path.]

TIMO: I will take the dry-feet route.

[He walks toward the creek.]
