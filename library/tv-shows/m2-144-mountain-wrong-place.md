# The Mountain Is in the Wrong Place

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: model-railroad programs that narrate intricate scenery as history while the train repeatedly misses its switch*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Toma wants a sweeping tour of the Grove Valley model railway. Engineer Seli needs to fix a misaligned switch before the train reaches it, while producer Puck keeps adding scenery that blocks access to the track.

Cast: Toma, a macaque host; Seli, a gorilla railway builder; Rami, a lemur historian; and Puck, a capuchin camera operator.

## Episode: "The Scenic Detour"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A MODEL TRAIN APPROACHES A MOUNTAIN. IT STOPS AT A SWITCH.]

TOMA: Our train is about to cross the valley bridge.

SELI: It is on the wrong track.

[PUCK MOVES A MODEL TREE TO HIDE THE SWITCH.]

RAMI: The bridge is behind the mountain.

TOMA: A dramatic reveal.

[THE TRAIN ROLLS BACKWARD.]

SELI: I have not turned it on.

### Act One

[SELI LIFTS THE TRAIN OFF THE TRACK. SHE CHECKS THE SWITCH.]

SELI: The point rail is not seating.

TOMA: What does that mean for the journey?

SELI: It cannot take the left branch.

RAMI: The left branch was added last month.

TOMA: Is it historically accurate?

RAMI: The valley did not have a mountain there.

[PUCK PLACES A SMALL CASTLE ON THE MOUNTAIN.]

PUCK: We needed a landmark.

SELI: It covers the access hatch.

[PUCK LIFTS THE CASTLE. A TINY BRIDGE IS STUCK TO ITS BASE.]

TOMA: Can we keep the bridge attached for the tour?

SELI: It has to connect the tracks.

RAMI: The bridge used to sit by the station.

[PUCK MOVES IT. THE WIRE UNDERNEATH PULLS THE MOUNTAIN. SELI STOPS THE TRAIN BEFORE IT CAN ROLL.]

SELI: We need to secure the scenery before running the motor.

PUCK: The bridge has moved.

RAMI: The bridge is magnetic.

### Act Two

[THE CREW TRIES TO SHOW THE TRAIN'S ROUTE. TOMA NARRATES.]

TOMA: The locomotive leaves the old station, passes the river, and climbs toward the high pass.

[THE TRAIN REACHES THE SWITCH AND TAKES THE WRONG ROUTE. IT ENTERS A CIRCLE AND RETURNS TO THE STATION.]

RAMI: The high pass is behind the tree.

TOMA: A mysterious loop.

SELI: The switch is still misaligned.

[PUCK SETS A CAMERA ON A SMALL TRIPOD. THE TRIPOD BLOCKS THE TRACK.]

PUCK: I will get a low shot.

[THE TRAIN BUMPS THE TRIPOD AND STOPS.]

TOMA: The mountain railway has encountered an obstacle.

SELI: It encountered camera equipment.

[PUCK MOVES THE TRIPOD TO THE TABLE EDGE. RAMI POINTS OUT THAT THE TRAIN IS NOW RUNNING IN REVERSE ON CAMERA.]

RAMI: The engine is going backward.

TOMA: The story is moving forward.

SELI: The train is stopped.

SELI: The line needs to stay clear.

[TOMA ASKS FOR A SECOND TAKE. SELI REMOVES THE TRIPOD AND REALIGNS THE SWITCH. RAMI MOVES THE CASTLE OFF THE MOUNTAIN.]

RAMI: This was a pasture, not a fortress.

TOMA: A simpler history.

RAMI: A more accurate map.

[RAMI SHOWS TOMA THE ORIGINAL TRACK PLAN. THE TOWN, STATION, AND BRIDGE ARE MARKED IN PENCIL.]

TOMA: Could the voice-over follow the plan?

RAMI: Yes. We should stop calling the ridge a mountain.

SELI: And keep the switch accessible.

[PUCK ADDS THE PLAN AS A SMALL INSET GRAPHIC WITH A LABEL: "MODEL ROUTE."]

[SELI SHOWS THE CREW HOW TO REACH THE SWITCH THROUGH THE ACCESS HATCH. PUCK REMOVES THE TREE THAT BLOCKED IT.]

SELI: Now I can check the point rail without lifting the mountain.

TOMA: The route is open for the next run.

[RAMI PLACES THE HISTORIC MAP NEXT TO THE MODEL.]

### Act Three

[SELI RUNS THE TRAIN AGAIN. IT PASSES THE SWITCH AND CROSSES THE BRIDGE. TOMA FOLLOWS IT WITH THE CAMERA.]

TOMA: The train has reached the high pass.

RAMI: The pass is a low ridge.

TOMA: The train has reached the low ridge.

[THE TRAIN STOPS AT THE STATION. SELI CHECKS THE SWITCH. IT IS STILL ALIGNED.]

TOMA: Is the route ready for a second train?

SELI: Not until I clear the first one and check the points.

[PUCK ASKS FOR A CLOSING SHOT. RAMI REMOVES A FALSE SIGN THAT READS "MOUNTAIN PASS." THE REAL STATION NAME IS VISIBLE UNDER IT.]

SELI: The track works.

PUCK: We have our sweeping shot.

[THE CAMERA PANS TO SHOW THE WHOLE MODEL, INCLUDING THE WIRES, ACCESS HATCH, AND TOOLBOX.]

TOMA: Can we hide the toolbox?

SELI: Not if we need to reach the switch.

RAMI: The railway has to be maintained.

### Tag

[THE TRAIN SITS ON THE TRACK. TOMA READS THE FINAL CARD.]

TOMA: The route is complete.

[PUCK PLACES THE CASTLE BESIDE THE MODEL, NOT ON THE MOUNTAIN.]

RAMI: That is where it belongs.

[SELI SWITCHES OFF THE MOTOR.]

TOMA: We will return after the next track inspection.

SELI: The inspection is on Saturday.

RAMI: The mountain is still in the wrong place.

[PUCK SETS THE MODEL TREE BACK BESIDE THE TRACK, NOT OVER THE SWITCH.]
