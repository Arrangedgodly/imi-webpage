# The Lantern Parade Starts Before Dark

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: festival broadcasts that demand a dramatic nightfall while local animals follow their own schedules*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-015*

Cast: Host Runi, a baboon; parade marshal Pava, a lemur; lantern keeper Tavi, a gibbon; drummer Neri, a capuchin; camera operator Vex, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - LATE AFTERNOON

[Colorful paper lanterns hang from a line. The sun is still bright. Runi faces camera.] 

RUNI: The annual lantern parade begins at sunset.

PAVA: The sign says six.

[The clock reads 5:42. Neri tests the drum; a flock of birds lifts from the trees.] 

ACT ONE

RUNI: Welcome to Evening Lights. We are live from the village green, where night is expected shortly.

TAVI: The lanterns need candles.

PAVA: The electric lanterns are charged.

TAVI: They are not lanterns. They are paper shades on a string.

[The crew sets out battery lights. Vex adjusts the camera exposure. The display makes the sky look darker.] 

VEX: We can bring the evening forward in camera.

PAVA: The parade starts when the sun sets.

RUNI: The audience is waiting.

[The audience is mostly families and a troop of macaques grooming near the fountain. One macaque takes a lantern and holds it up to the light.] 

NERI: The lantern is not lit yet.

RUNI: We have an early participant.

TAVI: That's the lantern keeper's helper.

ACT TWO

[The camera crew dims the picture. In the frame, dusk arrives. Outside the frame, the sun remains high. Pava asks the children to walk to the staging mark while the crew adjusts the camera.] 

PAVA: For rehearsal, carry the lanterns to the staging mark. The parade begins at six.

[The macaque troop follows the scent of fruit from a nearby stand, carrying their lanterns with them. The line of paper shades sways.]

RUNI: We have a spontaneous procession.

PAVA: They are going to the snack table.

[The troop pauses to groom. Their lanterns hang from the branches above them. Tavi guides the children back to the staging mark.]

TAVI: These need to stay on the poles.

[The wind pulls one lantern loose. Neri catches it with her drum strap. Vex films the rescue and asks for a close-up.] 

VEX: A dramatic save in the golden hour.

PAVA: The golden hour is still bright.

[The battery lights blink on. The paper shades glow faintly, almost invisible in daylight. Runi asks the crew to hold a black backdrop behind them. It blows away.] 

RUNI: Night has escaped.

[The crew runs a lighting rehearsal. Tavi turns on the lanterns in a row. One battery light blinks, then goes dark.]

TAVI: This one is not charged.

PAVA: We have a missing lantern.

RUNI: We have a light that needs a battery.

[The macaque helper carries the dark lantern to the charging table. The others follow, leaving the parade line empty.]

VEX: The lanterns are migrating again.

PAVA: They are returning to the charger.

[Neri taps a steady rhythm to gather the children. They line up by height and pass the glowing lanterns forward, keeping their arms clear of the branches.]

TAVI: That is a safer line.

RUNI: It is also a better camera shot.

PAVA: We should not move them for the shot.

[Vex steps back to show the full line. He bumps the camera tripod, which tilts toward the sun. He catches it and points the lens back to the lanterns.]

VEX: The sun has been framed out.

ACT THREE

[The clock reaches six. The sun is still above the trees, but the parade begins as scheduled. Neri plays a measured beat; children carry the lanterns along the path. The troop of macaques follows, now carrying fruit from the stand.] 

PAVA: The parade is moving.

TAVI: The lights are on.

VEX: Can we dim the sky one more stop?

[He adjusts the exposure. The lanterns glow brightly, but the people become silhouettes. Runi's face disappears.] 

RUNI: The village has vanished.

PAVA: The village is carrying the lanterns.

[The camera returns to natural exposure. The lanterns look small, but the crowd and the bright sky are visible. Tavi helps a child lift a shade upright. Neri plays louder as the first natural shadow crosses the green.] 

RUNI: The night is arriving.

PAVA: It is five minutes past six.

[The sun lowers behind the hill. The lanterns become bright against the darkening sky. The macaques settle on a fence rail, sharing fruit and holding one lantern between them.] 

TAVI: They have found the best seat.

VEX: We have our finale shot.

PAVA: The parade is over. Let them watch.

TAG

[Runi announces that the final lantern will be turned off. The macaque holding it closes one hand around the switch and keeps it glowing.] 

RUNI: That is the last light.

PAVA: It has a keeper.
