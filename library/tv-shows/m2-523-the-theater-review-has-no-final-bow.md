# The Theater Review Has No Final Bow

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: theater-review programs that demand a tidy verdict while the production is still changing*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: critic Vela, a lemur; host Daro, a macaque; director Nima, an orangutan; stage manager Tavi, a gibbon; producer Sela, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. REVIEW SET - NIGHT

Vela holds a program. Daro asks for her verdict.

VELA: I have not seen the final scene.

TAVI: It is still being rehearsed.

ACT ONE

INT. SET - CONTINUOUS

Sela says the review segment must air before the play opens. Nima has changed the ending that afternoon. Daro asks Vela to review what she has seen. She describes the first act, then asks to see the new ending. Tavi opens the curtain; the cast is still moving a painted tree.

Daro begins his verdict anyway. The teleprompter reads MUST-SEE. Vela asks who wrote it. Sela says it is the default card. Nima asks the crew to stop moving the tree until the interview is over. The tree keeps rolling because its wheel is unlocked.

ACT TWO

INT. BACKSTAGE - LATER

Vela asks Nima about the change. The original ending had the hero leave. The new ending keeps the hero on stage to apologize. Nima says the actor found a clearer choice in rehearsal. Sela asks whether Vela can call it a bold revision. Vela says she has not seen it performed.

[Tavi locks the tree's wheel. It stays still. The stage manager hands Vela a marked script with the new pages.]

Daro asks whether the audience should know the ending. Vela says a review can discuss the acting without summarizing every turn. Sela asks for a star rating. Vela asks if the show has a rating scale. Daro points to five stars on the screen. The graphic has six, because one is duplicated.

INT. REVIEW SET - MOMENTS LATER

The cast performs the final scene. The hero reaches the exit, turns back and apologizes. The tree is still. Vela watches without speaking. Daro holds a rating card under the desk. Sela signals him not to reveal it before the scene ends. The actor completes the apology. The cast waits for the director's note; Nima asks them to try the last line once more.

INT. BACKSTAGE - MOMENTS LATER

The actor who played the hero asks Nima whether the turn back toward the exit was clear. Nima says the audience saw it, but the actor's pause was stronger before the apology. Vela asks whether the final bow is part of the scene or the review. Tavi explains that the cast bows after the curtain call, not during the final line. Daro asks the actor to demonstrate the difference. The actor finishes the line, pauses, and bows only after Tavi closes the curtain.

Sela asks Vela for a one-word recommendation. Vela says "wait." Daro types WAIT on the teleprompter. It scrolls away before he can read it. The screen replaces it with MUST-SEE again. Vela turns the monitor toward the camera and points to the default setting. Nima asks the control booth to clear the template. The rating stars disappear, leaving the review text. Vela says she will return after opening night; the play deserves a review of the performance the audience actually sees. Daro leaves the star card face down. Tavi rolls the locked tree offstage after the curtain closes. Sela saves the review for opening night. Vela gives Nima the marked script. The director places the changed ending pages in the correct order. Daro removes the six-star template from his desk. The tree is stored upright. Safely. The cast exits.

ACT THREE

INT. REVIEW SET - LATER

Vela gives her review. She praises the actor's pause before the apology and says the shifting ending is worth seeing live. She declines to rate an unfinished production. Daro asks whether that means she disliked it. Vela says it means the run is still in progress. The teleprompter changes to UNDECIDED. Sela asks the graphics operator to replace it with REVIEW IN PROGRESS.

Nima asks the actor to perform the last line again. Vela watches. The actor delivers it without turning toward the exit. Nima nods. Tavi closes the curtain. Daro asks for a final bow. The curtain opens again, but the cast remains in rehearsal clothes. They bow to the camera crew and the rolling tree.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE REVIEW

Sela files the rating card blank. Daro leaves the review in the rundown for after opening night. Vela returns the program to Nima.

FINAL IMAGE: The six-star graphic is gone, and the stage tree stands locked while the cast rehearses behind the curtain.
