# The Reunion Has a Timeline

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality-show reunion specials, selective flashbacks, and hosts who demand a feud after it has ended*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: Contestants from a birdhouse-building show return to settle who moved the ladder, but the reunion edit has removed the one person holding it.

Cast: Host Rala, a macaque; builder Toma, an orangutan; designer Eki, a capuchin; judge Venn, a baboon; floor manager Seli, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. REUNION STUDIO - NIGHT

Rala sits between Toma and Eki. A giant screen shows a ladder falling out of frame.

RALA: Toma, you said Eki moved the ladder.

TOMA: I said the ladder moved.

RALA: Eki, did you move the ladder?

EKI: No. Seli was holding it.

Seli steps into frame behind the set wall, holding a ladder.

SELI: I am holding it right now.

ACT ONE

Rala welcomes viewers to the reunion of Build a Better Birdhouse. Toma and Eki explain that during the finale their ladder rolled, their roof panel slid, and both contestants blamed each other's timing. Venn says the judges awarded the win to Toma for a weatherproof roof.

RALA: But first, the clip.

The screen plays Toma pointing to Eki. The clip cuts to Eki carrying a roof panel. Rala freezes the frame on Eki's hand near the ladder.

EKI: I was holding the panel.

TOMA: It looked like you were reaching for the ladder.

EKI: The edit cropped out my tail, which was under the panel.

RALA: Viewers, what do you think?

Venn points at the screen. A side camera shows Seli holding the ladder's lower rail while Eki carries the panel.

VENN: We have another angle.

ACT TWO

Rala asks Toma to explain why he accused Eki. Toma says he saw the ladder shift just as Eki passed. Eki says she warned Toma that its wheel brake was loose. Toma admits he did not hear her because the show's dramatic music was loud.

RALA: Were you angry?

TOMA: I was trying to keep the roof panel from sliding.

RALA: Were you angry while keeping it from sliding?

TOMA: I had both hands full.

The screen shows the argument in slow motion. Rala asks for it to be replayed with the sound up. The edit adds a thunderclap as Toma grabs the panel. Venn says the thunder was not recorded during filming.

VENN: That is a sound effect.

RALA: It makes the moment feel important.

SELI: It made the ladder hard to hear.

Eki demonstrates the birdhouse roof panel. She lifts it, slides its tabs into place, and turns it so rain runs off the back. Toma says the first version had no gutter. Eki shows him the seam he missed in the rush. Toma says the judges never asked them to build a gutter.

RALA: We may have a second disagreement.

VENN: The roof was judged on coverage and stability. Both panels passed.

ACT TWO-B

The audience asks about the finale's missing screws. Rala lifts a clear evidence box containing two screws and a tiny wrench. Toma says the screws were in the kit. Eki says the wrench had rolled beneath a bench. Seli confirms she retrieved it before judging.

RALA: Did anyone suspect sabotage?

TOMA: I suspected a wheel brake.

EKI: The wheel brake was loose.

RALA: Were you upset at each other?

EKI: We were trying to finish a birdhouse on television.

TOMA: I should have checked the ladder before we blamed each other.

Rala looks to the camera for a confession. Eki and Toma both ask to watch the corrected angle instead. The screen plays the entire moment: Seli holds the ladder, Eki carries the panel, Toma secures the roof. The panel slips when a studio fan starts. No one touches the ladder.

SELI: That fan was added for the dramatic wind shot.

VENN: The outdoor wind was not dramatic enough.

RALA: We have found our unseen antagonist.

SELI: The fan was switched on by production.

RALA: The production fan has declined to comment.

ACT THREE

Venn announces that Toma's birdhouse won and Eki received the judges' award for clever roof joinery. The crew brings the finished houses onto the reunion set. A small finch enters the open window and lands on the edge of Eki's model.

EKI: It's not meant for birds. It's a display model.

TOMA: The real one is outside, under the eave.

The finch hops to Toma's house and pecks at its roof. Rala asks if it has chosen a winner. Venn says the bird is inspecting a construction detail. Toma opens the side panel so it can leave. The finch flies out through the studio door.

RALA: An exit interview?

SELI: It wasn't contracted.

Rala thanks both builders. Toma and Eki agree to build a shared feeder for next season, with a locked ladder and no fan. Rala asks if they can do a dramatic handshake. They shake hands, then immediately let go to keep the model stable.

TAG

The screen plays a final slow-motion replay of the handshake. It zooms in until the two hands fill the frame.

RALA: Was it a truce?

EKI: It was a handshake.

FINAL IMAGE: Seli wheels the ladder offstage while the corrected full clip plays once, without thunder or captions.
