# Spin the Acorn

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: high-energy daytime quiz shows, bonus rounds, and audience giveaways*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A quiz-show host tries to keep a live audience excited while the prize wheel refuses to stop on any category printed on it.

Cast: Host Bix, a macaque; contestant Lolo, a capuchin; contestant Venn, a gibbon; scorekeeper Umi, a lemur; and audience captain Doro, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Wheel Knows"

### Cold open

INT. STUDIO, DAY

[A giant wheel turns slowly. Every wedge says "ACORN." Bix holds a microphone.]

BIX: Welcome to Spin the Acorn, the quiz show where knowledge can win you a prize.

LOLO: What is the prize?

BIX: We reveal that after the bonus round.

VENN: Is the bonus round before the main game?

BIX: It is after the intro and before the end.

UMI: The wheel is still turning.

DORO: It has not stopped since rehearsal.

BIX: We call that suspense.

LOLO: I call it a wheel.

[The wheel makes a squeaking sound and speeds up.]

BIX: Contestants, ready?

VENN: I am ready when the wheel is.

### Act One

INT. STUDIO

BIX: First question. What do monkeys use to crack an acorn?

LOLO: A stone.

BIX: Correct! Your score is one.

UMI: The scoreboard says "acorn."

BIX: That is the category.

VENN: My question?

BIX: Name a tree that grows acorns.

VENN: Oak.

BIX: Correct! Your score is also acorn.

UMI: The scoreboard is not changing.

DORO: The audience is ready to cheer.

BIX: Audience, make some noise!

[The audience makes a polite rustle.]

BIX: Good energy. We can use that.

LOLO: How many points do I have?

BIX: You have one acorn.

LOLO: Is that a point or a prize?

BIX: The wheel decides.

[The wheel spins. It slows, then accelerates again.]

VENN: The wheel knows something.

BIX: The wheel knows the answer is in the question.

UMI: The wheel is attached to a motor.

BIX: The motor is part of the wheel's journey.

### Act Two

INT. BONUS ROUND SET

[A smaller wheel is rolled out. It also says "ACORN" on every wedge.]

BIX: Bonus round! Answer three questions while the wheel spins.

LOLO: It has the same answer on every wedge.

BIX: That is why speed matters.

VENN: Ask us the questions.

BIX: What color is an acorn?

LOLO: Brown.

BIX: Correct. What does an acorn grow into?

VENN: An oak tree.

BIX: Correct. What is the best way to celebrate winning an acorn?

LOLO: Eat it?

BIX: We cannot accept that answer. The sponsor is an acorn collector.

DORO: Can the audience help?

BIX: Audience, choose "celebrate" or "contemplate."

[The audience rustles. One spectator coughs.]

BIX: The audience has chosen a third option: "mildly surprised."

UMI: The wheel has stopped.

[The wheel points to ACORN.]

BIX: The result is in!

LOLO: We knew the result.

BIX: But did you know it would stop?

VENN: It has stopped on the only thing written on it.

BIX: That is a historic first.

### Act Three

INT. STUDIO

BIX: Contestants, your prize is an acorn.

LOLO: Each?

BIX: One acorn to share.

VENN: Is it a large acorn?

UMI: It is a normal acorn.

DORO: The audience would like to see it.

[Bix presents the acorn on a velvet cushion.]

BIX: This acorn was gathered from a healthy oak and polished by our prize team.

LOLO: It is still in its shell.

BIX: Naturally.

VENN: What does the winner do?

BIX: The winner takes the acorn home.

LOLO: I will give it to Venn.

VENN: I will give it to the audience.

DORO: I will plant it in the studio garden.

BIX: Then everyone wins.

UMI: Who gets the score?

BIX: The scoreboard says "acorn."

UMI: That is not a score.

BIX: It is the only category.

[The wheel begins turning again.]

VENN: The wheel has another question.

LOLO: What is it?

BIX: I don't know. The wheel is asking.

DORO: It wants us to go home.

BIX: The studio is closed.

UMI: The wheel is still turning.

BIX: We will answer tomorrow.


### Audience round

INT. STUDIO, MOMENTS LATER

BIX: We have time for one audience question.

DORO: Can I ask the wheel?

BIX: The wheel is not a contestant.

[The wheel squeaks as if disagreeing.]

UMI: It has stopped again.

BIX: What category?

UMI: Acorn.

BIX: The wheel has chosen itself.

LOLO: Does that mean it wins the prize?

VENN: The prize is an acorn.

DORO: Then the wheel can plant it.

BIX: Wheels don't plant things.

UMI: This wheel has a small garden attached.

[They inspect the wheel. A paper pot is taped to its side.]

BIX: That is a sponsor display.

DORO: It has soil in it.

LOLO: The sponsor is an acorn collector and a gardener.

VENN: The wheel is an enterprise partner.

BIX: We did not rehearse this.

UMI: We also did not rehearse the wheel stopping.

DORO: It stopped on the prize.

BIX: The wheel has won an acorn.

[Umi places the acorn into the paper pot. The audience gives a soft, respectful rustle.]

### Tag

INT. STUDIO, NIGHT

[Umi places the acorn in a small pot. The wheel turns in the background.]

UMI: There. A prize that can grow.

[The wheel stops. Every wedge still says ACORN.]

[The acorn sits in its pot as the wheel points to itself.]
