# Please Do Not Restore It Live

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live restoration shows that promise a dramatic reveal on a broadcast schedule despite slow, careful conservation work*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Conservator Suma wants to clean a community hall's painted sign properly. Host Doro promises viewers the whole restoration will be finished before the hour ends. The crew's safer plan reveals that the sign is already useful, just not shiny.

Cast: Suma, a lemur conservator; Doro, a macaque host; Bex, a gorilla stage manager; and Piko, a capuchin camera operator.

## Episode: "The Before-and-After Reveal"

### Cold Open

[COMMUNITY HALL. A WOODEN SIGN READS "READING ROOM." DORO STANDS BESIDE IT.]

DORO: By the end of the hour, we will reveal a completely transformed sign.

SUMA: It needs dusting and one loose screw tightened.

DORO: That is the before.

[BEX STARTS A COUNTDOWN CLOCK AT 60:00.]

BEX: We have exactly one hour.

SUMA: The finish needs to dry overnight.

[DORO SMILES AT CAMERA. THE CLOCK TICKS LOUDLY.]

### Act One

[PIKO FRAMES THE SIGN. DORO HOLDS A CLOTH.]

DORO: Our expert will begin with a dramatic wipe.

SUMA: No. First we check whether the paint is stable.

[DORO STOPS THE CLOTH.]

DORO: A test patch?

SUMA: A small test patch.

[SUMA DABS ONE CORNER WITH A DRY BRUSH. A LITTLE DUST COMES OFF. THE LETTERS REMAIN.]

DORO: The transformation begins.

SUMA: The cleaning begins.

[BEX MOVES THE TIMER TO 45:00.]

BEX: We should see more progress by the next segment.

SUMA: We can clean the frame while the test patch dries.

[DORO SHOWS A BOX OF POLISH.]

DORO: This promises a brilliant shine.

SUMA: It is for metal.

DORO: The sign has a nail.

SUMA: The nail is not the sign.

[Piko ZOOMS IN ON THE LOOSE SCREW. SUMA TIGHTENS IT WITH A HAND TOOL.]

### Act Two

[THE CREW RETURNS FROM A COMMERCIAL BREAK. THE SIGN LOOKS ALMOST THE SAME.]

DORO: We asked viewers to guess the final color.

SUMA: We are not repainting it.

DORO: The poll says gold.

SUMA: The test says leave the paint alone.

[BEX REMOVES A GOLD BACKDROP THAT PIKO HAD PLACED BEHIND THE SIGN.]

PIKO: I can get a before-and-after shot from the camera roll.

SUMA: Use the same angle.

[PIKO MATCHES THE FIRST FRAME. THE DIFFERENCE IS DUST ON THE LEDGE AND THE SIGN NOW HANGING LEVEL.]

DORO: It is a subtle reveal.

SUMA: It is an accurate one.

DORO: Viewers may be wondering where the reveal is.

SUMA: We found a loose screw and confirmed the paint is stable.

DORO: Can we uncover the lettering?

SUMA: It is not covered.

[BEX ROLLS A CLOTH OVER THE SIGN AS A REVEAL CURTAIN. THE CLOTH CATCHES ON THE NAIL.]

BEX: It is caught.

SUMA: Lift it up, don't pull sideways.

[THE CLOTH COMES FREE. A SMALL FLAKE OF OLD PAINT FALLS INTO SUMA'S HAND. EVERYONE FREEZES.]

DORO: Is that a problem?

SUMA: It is why we tested first.

[PIKO MOVES THE CAMERA CLOSER. SUMA PLACES THE FLAKE IN A PAPER ENVELOPE.]

DORO: Can we tell viewers the flake came from underneath the sign?

SUMA: It came from the lower corner.

DORO: The corner where history meets the frame.

SUMA: The corner where the cloth snagged.

[BEX WRITES THE LOCATION ON THE ENVELOPE. PIKO ZOOMS OUT TO INCLUDE THE LABEL.]

DORO: Could we put the flake back for the reveal?

SUMA: No.

[DORO LOOKS AT THE COUNTDOWN. IT SHOWS 18:00.]

DORO: Our hour is almost over.

SUMA: Restoration is not a race.

BEX: The timer was a production choice.

### Act Three

[SUMA CLEANS THE FRAME AND LABELS THE PAINT FLAKE. DORO SITS BESIDE HER.]

DORO: Could we call this a reveal?

SUMA: We can call it a sign that is secure and clean.

BEX: That is exactly what the hall needed.

[PIKO SHOWS THE BEFORE SHOT. THE SIGN LEANS. THE AFTER SHOT IS LEVEL.]

DORO: The difference is visible.

SUMA: Because we matched the angle.

DORO: The sign still reads "Reading Room."

SUMA: It did before.

DORO: What changed?

SUMA: The screw is secure. The frame is clean. The paint is documented.

[BEX TURNS OFF THE COUNTDOWN CLOCK.]

BEX: We can show the after without a big reveal.

[DORO FACES THE CAMERA.]

DORO: The sign can return to the hall today. The paint will be monitored, and any repairs will happen after testing.

[Piko SHOOTS A STEADY CLOSE-UP. THE LETTERS ARE CLEAR. THE HALL ORGANIZER CARRIES THE SIGN BACK TO ITS PLACE.]

### Tag

[DORO HOLDS THE POLISH BOTTLE. SUMA TAKES IT AND PUTS IT BACK IN THE METALWORK BOX.]

BEX: We can end on a clean shot.

DORO: With a sparkle?

SUMA: With the sign facing the door.

[PIKO TURNS THE SIGN SO VISITORS CAN READ IT.]

DORO: Maybe next week we restore the box.

SUMA: We can dust it.
