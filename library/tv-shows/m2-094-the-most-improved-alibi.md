# The Most Improved Alibi

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime soap operas, cliffhangers and revelations delivered at impossible volume*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: At the Palm Court, a missing silver comb turns a routine hair appointment into a family betrayal. Three relatives reveal secrets, then discover the comb was in plain sight.

Cast: Zella, a macaque salon owner; Ro, her gibbon sister; Mott, their capuchin cousin; and Aunt Bibi, a mandrill who remembers every slight.

## Episode: "A Parting So Deep"

### Cold Open

[THE SALON DOOR SWINGS OPEN. ZELLA HOLDS AN EMPTY VELVET TRAY.]

ZELLA: The comb is gone.

RO: It cannot be gone. It was here when I sat down.

MOTT: You sat down on the tray.

RO: I would remember crushing family silver.

[THE CAMERA PUSHES IN ON A COMB TUCKED BEHIND RO'S EAR.]

ZELLA: Someone in this room has betrayed me.

### Act One

[AUNT BIBI ENTERS WEARING A ROBE AND CARRYING A CUP OF TEA.]

BIBI: I heard there was a betrayal. I brought my own cup.

ZELLA: Auntie, you gave me that comb when I opened the salon.

BIBI: I gave you a comb. I gave Ro the matching brush.

RO: Which is also missing.

MOTT: It is in your hand.

[RO LOOKS DOWN. SHE HAS BEEN HOLDING THE BRUSH.]

RO: I am overwhelmed by the family resemblance.

ZELLA: The comb was the only thing that made this salon feel like home.

BIBI: You have a mirror, three chairs and a sink.

ZELLA: The sink is rented.

MOTT: I saw someone near the tray before lunch.

ZELLA: Who?

MOTT: I was looking at the pastry plate.

[THE SOAP MUSIC RISES.]

### Act Two

[RO TAKES THE COMB FROM BEHIND HER EAR. EVERYONE GASPS.]

RO: I can explain.

ZELLA: You hid it?

RO: I tucked it there when I came in. It is where I keep a pencil during appointments.

BIBI: You have no pencil.

RO: I had one yesterday.

MOTT: We have solved the comb. We should tell the customers their appointments can begin.

ZELLA: The comb has been found, but the trust is still missing.

RO: We were searching for ten minutes.

ZELLA: Ten minutes can change a family.

BIBI: It can also change a set of bangs.

[THE SALON BELL RINGS. A CLIENT ENTERS WITH A LEAF STUCK TO THEIR HEAD.]

CLIENT: Is someone free to trim this?

ZELLA: Not until I know who moved the tray.

MOTT: I moved it to make room for the tea.

BIBI: You said you never touched it.

MOTT: I said I never took the comb.

[ZELLA TURNS TO MOTT. THE MUSIC STABS.]

ZELLA: You withheld a crucial detail.

MOTT: I moved a tray.

BIBI: In this family, that is how every inheritance dispute begins.

[AUNT BIBI SITS IN THE CLIENT'S CHAIR AND INSPECTS THE COMB.]

BIBI: This was not the comb I gave you. Mine had a chipped tooth.

ZELLA: It does have a chipped tooth.

BIBI: The other end.

[ZELLA TURNS IT OVER. A SMALL CHIP IS VISIBLE.]

RO: I knew that.

MOTT: You have been wearing it in your hair.

RO: I knew it from a distance.

ZELLA: If it is the family comb, it belongs in the salon drawer.

BIBI: I thought it was meant for you.

ZELLA: It is. I use it here.

[ZELLA PLACES THE COMB IN A DRAWER. RO SLIDES THE DRAWER SHUT WITH HER HIP.]

RO: There. No one can mistake it for a tray.

MOTT: The tray is still under the tea cup.

[THE CUP WOBBLES. MOTT CATCHES IT, BUT THE SAUCER FALLS INTO HIS LAP.]

BIBI: Another betrayal.

MOTT: The saucer betrayed gravity.

ZELLA: We will discuss it after the client gets a trim.

CLIENT: I only came in for a leaf.

RO: In this family, a leaf is never only a leaf.

[THE CLIENT LOOKS AT THE SALON MIRROR.]

CLIENT: I have two different sideburns.

ZELLA: The comb is back, but we will need a second appointment.

CLIENT: Book it under betrayal.

### Act Three

[ZELLA SETS THE COMB ON THE COUNTER. RO SETS THE BRUSH BESIDE IT. MOTT MOVES THE TEA CUP AWAY.]

ZELLA: The tray can stay here. The tools will stay in the drawer.

RO: And I will keep my ear empty.

BIBI: Until the next appointment.

[RO PLACES A FOLDED APPOINTMENT CARD BEHIND HER EAR. EVERYONE SHOUTS.]

RO: It is a card.

MOTT: We have learned nothing.

ZELLA: We have learned that our new client is still waiting.

[ZELLA TRIMS THE LEAF FROM THE CLIENT'S HEAD. IT FALLS INTO THE EMPTY TRAY.]

BIBI: Another thing has gone missing.

MOTT: It is under the leaf.

[THE MUSIC SWELLS AS ZELLA LIFTS THE LEAF. THE COMB IS SAFE. THE CLIENT LEAVES WITH A PERFECTLY ROUND PATCH.]
