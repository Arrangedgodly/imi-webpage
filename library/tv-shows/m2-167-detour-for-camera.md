# Detour for the Camera

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: travel reality shows that describe ordinary local routes as dangerous discoveries*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Travel host Pavo is filming an episode about a village's new footpath and wants to call it a forbidden trail. Guide Niri knows it is a signed public route, but production keeps detouring into muddy scenery to make the journey seem perilous.

Cast: Pavo, a macaque host; Niri, a gorilla guide; Rell, a capuchin camera operator; and Suna, a red colobus resident who maintains the path.

## Episode: "The Hidden Shortcut"

### Cold Open

[TRAILHEAD. A CLEAR SIGN READS "RIVER PATH, 1.2 KILOMETERS." PAVO STANDS BESIDE IT WITH A MAP UPSIDE DOWN.] 

PAVO: We have found a route locals do not want visitors to know about.

NIRI: The sign is new. We put it up last week.

[RELL TURNS THE SIGN AWAY FROM CAMERA.] 

PAVO: Can you say it was difficult to find?

SUNA: It is at the bus stop.

[THE BUS PULLS AWAY. PAVO WAVES AT IT, MISSING THE CAMERA'S OPENING SHOT.]

[RELL SETS UP A HANDHELD CAMERA. PAVO ASKS HIM TO WALK BACKWARD THROUGH THE TRAIL ENTRANCE. NIRI TAKES THE CAMERA AND TURNS IT OFF WHILE THEY CHECK THE ROUTE MAP.] 

NIRI: We should know where the crew is going before filming.

PAVO: The audience wants discovery.

SUNA: The route has been open to school groups for years.

[PAVO OPENS THE MAP. IT HAS A CLEAR KEY AND BENCH SYMBOLS. HE HOLDS IT UPSIDE DOWN AGAIN.] 

RELL: You are facing the water.

### Act One

[THE CREW WALKS THE SIGNED PATH. NIRI CARRIES A MAP. PAVO STEPS OVER A SMALL ROOT AND GASPS.] 

PAVO: The forest has placed an obstacle in our way.

NIRI: It is a root.

[RELL FILMS A LOW ANGLE. THE ROOT LOOKS LIKE A LOG. PAVO STEPS AROUND IT AND WALKS INTO A PUDDLE.]

PAVO: The path has struck back.

SUNA: That puddle drains into the gravel ditch.

[RELL ASKS FOR A SECOND TAKE. PAVO STEPS OVER THE ROOT CORRECTLY BUT STOPS AT THE PUDDLE TO POSE. A DROP FROM THE TREE LANDS ON HIS HAT.] 

PAVO: The forest has marked me.

NIRI: It is water from a leaf.

[SUNA HOLDS OUT A CLOTH. PAVO WIPES THE HAT AND CONTINUES ALONG THE DRY EDGE.] 

[RELL ASKS SUNA TO WALK THROUGH THE PUDDLE AGAIN. SHE POINTS TO THE DRY EDGE AND KEEPS GOING.] 

SUNA: The route is dry on this side.

PAVO: The shortcut has options.

[NIRI SHOWS THE CREW A BENCH AND A DISTANCE MARKER. PAVO ASKS IF THEY CAN CALL IT AN ABANDONED WAYSTATION.] 

NIRI: It is a public bench.

### Act Two

[THE CAMERA CREW LEAVES THE MARKED PATH TO FILM A STEEP MUD SLOPE. PAVO FOLLOWS THEM. NIRI STAYS ON THE SIGNED ROUTE.] 

PAVO: Our guide has vanished.

NIRI: I am at the next marker.

[RELL'S CAMERA SLIDES IN THE MUD. SUNA SETS A TRAIL-CLOSURE CONE NEAR THE SLOPE.]

[PAVO TRIES TO CROSS THE CLOSED SLOPE TO REACH THE CAMERA. NIRI POINTS TO THE DETOUR SIGN. PAVO TAKES IT; THE CREW FOLLOWS AND THE CAMERA STAYS DRY.] 

PAVO: We have been rerouted.

NIRI: We are on the signed route.

RELL: We have lost the muddy shot.

SUNA: You still have the bridge.

SUNA: That bank is closed until it dries.

PAVO: Does this increase the danger?

SUNA: It tells people where not to walk.

[THE CREW REJOINS NIRI AT A BRIDGE. A SIGN GIVES THE SAFE CROSSING LIMIT. PAVO ASKS FOR A DRAMATIC CROSSING SHOT. NIRI CROSSES FIRST AND WAITS.] 

NIRI: The route is open. Keep the camera inside the rail.

[RELL PANS OVER THE BRIDGE. THE SHOT SHOWS THE RIVER AND THE CLEAR HANDRAIL. PAVO CROSSES WITH BOTH HANDS FREE.] 

PAVO: I expected more of a challenge.

SUNA: The challenge was getting the path maintained.

### Act Three

[THE CREW REACHES THE VILLAGE MARKET. PAVO READS THE MAP RIGHT SIDE UP. IT SHOWS THE SAME ROUTE THEY HAVE JUST WALKED.] 

PAVO: We crossed the river and reached the market without a detour.

RELL: The footage has no chase scene.

NIRI: It has a route people can use.

[RELL SHOWS A CLIP OF PAVO IN THE PUDDLE. PAVO ASKS FOR IT TO BE THE EPISODE'S CLIMAX. SUNA POINTS TO THE SHOT OF HER PLACING THE CLOSURE CONE.] 

SUNA: The dramatic part was closing the slope before someone slipped.

PAVO: The cone is not dramatic.

[THE CREW FILMS THE CONE, THEN THE SIGNED PATH AND BENCH. PAVO RECORDS A NEW INTRO WITH THE MAP IN THE CORRECT ORIENTATION.]

### Tag

[THE BUS RETURNS. PAVO BOARDS IT. THE DRIVER POINTS TO THE SAME PATH SIGN THROUGH THE WINDOW.] 

PAVO: The secret route has a bus stop.

NIRI: That is how you got here.
