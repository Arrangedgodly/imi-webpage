# Order by the Moon

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-shopping television, where a host pitches ordinary fruit baskets as essential lunar-age technology*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Host Seli tries to sell a woven fruit basket using increasingly grand demonstrations, while caller questions expose that the basket is simply a basket.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; product expert Odo, baboon; caller Nara, macaque; stage assistant Pavi, gibbon; floor manager Luma, lemur.

## Episode: "The Basket You Need"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO TABLE. A BASKET HOLDS THREE APPLES.]

SELI: This basket has changed my life.

NARA (ON PHONE): How?

SELI: It holds fruit.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[SELI HOLDS THE BASKET UP TO CAMERA.]

SELI: Woven by hand, light enough to carry, strong enough for a generous harvest.

ODO: It has a handle.

PAVI: The handle is loose.

LUMA: We have four minutes before the live slot.

Seli asks Odo to demonstrate how many apples fit. He adds one, then another, until the basket is full.

ODO: Nine apples.

NARA: I have a bag that holds twelve.

SELI: But does your bag have a story?

NARA: It has a receipt.

Pavi tightens the handle. The basket creaks.

PAVI: It is ready.

Odo lifts it. One apple falls out through a gap in the weave.

SELI: A convenient fruit exit.

NARA: That seems inconvenient.

### Act Two

[THE SHOW GOES LIVE.]

Seli introduces the basket as a "portable orchard." Odo carries it across the set. The handle loosens again.

ODO: The basket is still strong.

NARA: It is dropping apples.

SELI: The apples are demonstrating gravity.

Luma signals for a demonstration. Pavi places a cloth liner in the basket. The apples stay inside, but the liner covers the woven pattern.

SELI: Now it is a portable orchard with privacy.

NARA: Why would fruit need privacy?

ODO: To protect it from observation.

Nara asks if the basket can be washed. Odo says it should be wiped, not soaked. Seli calls it "low-water living." Pavi says the basket is made of plant fibers and water can weaken them.

SELI: We value responsible care.

NARA: Can it carry a water jug?

ODO: We have not tested that.

SELI: We will not test that live.

[THE PHONE LINES OPEN FOR VIEWER QUESTIONS.]

A caller asks whether the basket can hold a live fish. Odo says it has not been tested. Seli calls that a "future feature." Nara asks why anyone would carry a fish in a fruit basket.

NARA: Where is the water supposed to go?

SELI: We do not recommend liquid use.

PAVI: The base is woven, not sealed.

Odo tries to fold the basket flat. It springs back into shape.

ODO: It is resilient.

LUMA: It is not collapsible.

SELI: The basket remembers its form.

NARA: It is made to hold things.

A second caller asks if the basket comes with a lid. Luma says there is no lid. Odo holds up a cloth and drapes it over the top.

ODO: A cover is included.

LUMA: That is a tablecloth.

SELI: A multipurpose accessory.

Nara asks whether she can take the fruit out before the basket is returned to the kitchen. Odo says yes, and begins unloading it.### Act Three

[ODO SETS THE BASKET ON A SMALL SCALE.]

Seli announces a limited-time offer. Luma says there is no offer; the basket is a sample from the craft table.

LUMA: We have one basket.

SELI: One exclusive basket.

NARA: Is it for sale?

LUMA: It belongs to the kitchen.

Odo examines the handle and says it needs another stitch. Pavi repairs it with twine. Seli continues the pitch, now describing the basket as "artisanally reinforced."

NARA: How much does it cost?

SELI: That depends on the number of apples.

ODO: It costs nothing. We borrowed it.

Seli pauses. The camera stays on her.

SELI: Then we are showing you a borrowed basket.

LUMA: Please return it before lunch.

The crew places the basket back on the craft table. Nara says she might make one with a stronger handle. Odo offers to show her the weave.

Seli asks Odo to put the basket back where it belongs. He takes it to the kitchen, then returns to finish the show.

ODO: The product has gone home.

NARA: The product is still a basket.

Nara asks whether the borrowed basket should be credited on screen.

SELI: We can thank the craft table.

ODO: And return the fruit.

### Tag

[STUDIO TABLE IS EMPTY.]

SELI: We are sold out.

LUMA: We had one basket.

ODO: It is back in the kitchen.

NARA: What are you selling next?

SELI: A chair.

LUMA: The chair belongs to the studio.

[SFX: CAMERA LIGHT CLICKS OFF.]
