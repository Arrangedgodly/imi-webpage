# The Parade Float That Needed a Rescue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: rescue reality shows that manufacture suspense around a harmless, solvable mishap*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A parade crew's small leaf-shaped float sticks at a narrow gate while a rescue host arrives with unnecessary equipment.

Cast: Host Jori, a macaque; float builder Ketu, a gibbon; route marshal Lani, a capuchin; driver Boro, a baboon; camera operator Seli, a lemur.

Episode: "The Narrowest Gate"

COLD OPEN

EXT. PARADE ROUTE - MORNING

A compact leaf float stops at a gate. Jori runs toward it carrying a coil of rope.

JORI: We have a float in distress.

LANI: It is two inches too wide.

KETU: If we turn the handlebars, it fits.

JORI: We cannot risk an uncontrolled turn.

[BORO TURNS THE HANDLEBARS. THE FLOAT PIVOTS A FEW INCHES AND STOPS SHORT.]

Boro keeps the brake on. Jori begins a countdown, but Ketu tells him to hold the shot while she checks the wheel. A ribbon trails near the axle; she does not pull until Boro confirms the float is locked.

KETU: The decoration is caught. We have to clear it before trying again.

LANI: The route stays closed until the wheel is free.

Jori tells the crew to reset the shot. Lani says no: the attempt is over until the wheel is safe.

ACT ONE

Seli asks everyone to reset for a dramatic arrival. Boro keeps the float braked where it stopped, on the gate side of the start line. Jori narrates the approach as if the wheels might fail. Lani points out that the brake is locked and the ground is level.

JORI: Every rescue begins with an assessment.

LANI: Assessment complete. Turn the handlebars.

KETU: The leaf face is mounted on a swivel. I can rotate it after the gate.

Jori unfolds a rescue map. It depicts the entire parade route but not the gate. He calls for a support team. The support team is two volunteers holding a banner; they are already there.

ACT TWO

The crew measures the gate. Jori asks for a close-up of the tape measure. Seli holds it. The tape retracts suddenly and clicks against the post.

JORI: We have a snapping line.

LANI: It is a tape measure.

BORO: The float is still parked.

Jori asks Ketu to inspect the axle. Ketu reaches under the float and frees the ribbon she found caught around one wheel. Jori calls this a critical extraction.

KETU: The ribbon came from our decoration.

JORI: It was a hazard.

LANI: It was trailing. Now it is tied up.

[THE CAMERA CREW ZOOMS ON THE EMPTY WHEEL.]

Seli asks for the float to remain still while she changes batteries. Jori says they cannot pause a rescue. Lani points to the still camera icon. Jori waits while she replaces the battery.

ACT TWO-B

The crew checks the gate clearance with the float parked at the start line. Ketu rotates the leaf face sideways and measures the narrowest point. Boro keeps the brake set while Lani marks the safe angle with chalk.

JORI: If we have not passed the gate, can we still call this a rescue?

LANI: We can call it preparation.

Seli frames the chalk marks with the working camera. Jori steps into the close-up, covering both marks with his boots. She tilts down and waits.

SELI: Your warning has hidden the plan.

JORI: I can deliver it from the other side.

[HE STEPS OVER THE MARKS. THE CAMERA FINALLY SHOWS THEM.]

KETU: The decoration clears if we turn it here.

BORO: And the wheels stay straight.

Lani checks that the ribbon is tied above the wheel. Jori asks the volunteers to stand beside the route rather than around the float. They step back, leaving the gate and the audience path clear.

JORI: We have a plan.

LANI: We have a marked turn and a free wheel.

Ketu looks at the clock. The parade begins in ten minutes. Jori reaches for his rope coil, then leaves it on the ground.

KETU: The float is ready when the parade is ready.

ACT TWO-C

The float remains braked at the start line while the banner volunteers take their places. Seli asks Jori to step aside so she can film the whole leaf. Jori stands at the edge but keeps narrating. [JORI PICKS HIS ROPE UP FROM THE GROUND AND SLINGS IT OVER HIS SHOULDER FOR THE INTERVIEW.]

His rope coil slips from his shoulder and catches the gate latch.

JORI: I have become entangled in the scene.

LANI: The rope is on your sleeve.

[JORI LIFTS HIS ARM; THE COIL FALLS AWAY.]

JORI: I am free.

KETU: You were never tied.

Jori says the event needs one genuine emergency. Lani points to the parade whistle, which has rolled under the float. They all kneel, but Ketu reaches it first. Jori looks into camera and says the whistle was recovered safely.

ACT TWO-D

Jori tries to interview the crew about how they felt when the float was stuck. Ketu says she was checking clearance. Boro says he was holding the brake. Lani says she was keeping spectators out of the route. Jori looks to Seli for a more emotional angle.

JORI: Did you fear the camera battery might fail?

SELI: It did fail. I changed it.

JORI: Did you fear the parade would not start?

KETU: We had ten minutes. We used four.

[JORI BENDS TO RETRIEVE THE COIL. LANI GETS THERE FIRST AND LAYS IT BESIDE HIS FOLDED MAP.]

JORI: We have a second rescue.

LANI: We have a rope.

Seli pans to the float as the gate swings closed behind them. The route marshal checks the latch and leaves it open for the parade. Jori asks if the gate can be called the final obstacle.

LANI: Only if you stop calling everything an obstacle.

ACT THREE

The parade begins. The leaf float passes the gate with its face turned sideways, then rotates toward the crowd. Jori walks beside it without rope or map. The children wave; the route stays clear.

TAG

Jori steps through the gate and turns his shoulders sideways anyway.

LANI: You fit normally.

JORI: I wanted the camera to get the profile.
