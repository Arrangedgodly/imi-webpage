# A Season of Short Passes

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: sports documentaries that manufacture redemption arcs from modest team progress*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A documentary crew follows a cooperative ball team through a single match. Filmmaker Sol wants a comeback story so badly that his staged interviews and replays interfere with the game.

Cast: Coach Rema, a baboon; captain Jori, a gibbon; player Kito, a macaque; filmmaker Sol, a capuchin; the referee; and the two teams.

## Episode: "One More Pass"

### Cold Open

[BEFORE THE MATCH, THE TEAM PRACTICES NEAR THE SIDELINE. SOL SETS A CAMERA ON A TRIPOD.]

SOL: Tell me what you overcame.

JORI: The ball rolled under a bench.

SOL: And what did that teach you?

KITO: To pick it up.

[REMA PASSES THE BALL. KITO STOPS IT WITH HIS FOOT.]

REMA: Tomorrow's lesson is to keep your eyes on the receiver.

SOL: Can you say that while looking into the lens?

REMA: No. I am coaching.

[THE TEAM FINISHES WARMUPS. SOL ADDS A CARD TO HIS CAMERA: "COMEBACK MOMENT."]

### Act One

[THE REFEREE STARTS THE MATCH. THE OTHER TEAM SCORES FIRST.]

SOL: The setback has arrived.

JORI: It is one point.

SOL: We need a reaction shot.

[HE STEPS ONTO THE SIDELINE WITH HIS CAMERA. KITO TRIES TO PASS AROUND HIM.]

REMA: Sol, behind the line.

SOL: I need the team's faces.

REMA: They need the field.

[SOL MOVES BACK. JORI RECEIVES THE BALL, PASSES TO KITO, WHO SCORES.]

SOL: That's our turnaround. Can you do it again for a clean angle?

JORI: We are still playing.

[THE OTHER TEAM TAKES THE BALL. THEY SCORE WHILE SOL TURNS THE CAMERA.]

### Act Two

[THE SCORE IS 2-1. SOL WALKS BESIDE JORI, STILL FILMING.]

SOL: Tell the camera what the team is feeling.

JORI: We are defending.

SOL: Could you stop for a second?

JORI: No.

[JORI RUNS TO THE BALL. KITO IS OPEN, BUT SOL'S TRIPOD BLOCKS THE PASS LANE.]

KITO: Tripod!

[SOL GRABS IT. THE PASS GOES WIDE.]

REMA: Equipment stays behind the line.

SOL: We lost the shot.

REMA: We lost the pass.

[SOL CARRIES THE TRIPOD TO THE BENCH. THE TEAM RESETS. JORI PASSES TO KITO, WHO SCORES. THE SCORE IS 2-2.]

SOL: We have the comeback.

REMA: We have a tie.

[SOL FLIPS HIS CARD TO "VICTORY." THE TEAMMATES LOOK AT HIM.]

JORI: The match is not finished.

[THE TEAM TAKES A QUICK WATER BREAK AT THE SIDELINE. SOL HOLDS UP A NEW QUESTION CARD.]

SOL: What has this season taught you about loss?

KITO: The score is 2-2.

SOL: Could you say it as a reflection?

KITO: We need to watch their left runner.

[REMA DRAWS A LINE IN THE DIRT.]

REMA: Their passes come through here. Jori, cover the open side. Kito, stay ready for a short pass.

SOL: That sounds tactical, not emotional.

JORI: It can be both.

[SOL TURNS THE CAMERA TOWARD THE TEAM. THE REFEREE BLOWS THE WHISTLE.]

REMA: Back in.

[THE TEAM RETURNS TO THE FIELD. SOL'S QUESTION CARD FALLS INTO THE WATER BOWL. KITO PULLS IT OUT AND HANDS IT BACK.]

SOL: It says "What did you sacrifice?"

KITO: We sacrificed a dry card.

[THE PLAY RESUMES. JORI BLOCKS THE LEFT RUNNER AND TAPS THE BALL TO KITO.]

[THE REFEREE SIGNALS TWO MINUTES LEFT. SOL SETS UP A SLOW-MOTION CAMERA AT THE CORNER.]

REMA: Do not cross the line.

SOL: I am clear.

[THE CAMERA'S LEG SLIDES INTO THE FIELD. KITO STOPS THE BALL TO AVOID IT.]

KITO: Move your leg.

[SOL RETRACTS THE TRIPOD. THE OTHER TEAM STEALS THE BALL AND SCORES. THE SCORE IS 3-2.]

### Act Three

[THE WHISTLE ENDS THE MATCH. SOL LOWERS THE CAMERA.]

SOL: We can film a final shot with the team walking off as if the season has turned around.

REMA: The season has not turned around. We lost three-two.

JORI: We tied it before the tripod blocked the lane.

KITO: Then we lost after the tripod moved into the field.

SOL: That makes the tripod part of the story.

REMA: It makes the tripod part of the equipment bag.

[SOL FOLDS THE TRIPOD. A LEG IS BENT.]

SOL: I need a closing image.

[THE TEAM PICKS UP THE BALLS AND THE WATER BOWL. JORI CARRIES ONE END OF THE EQUIPMENT BAG. SOL TAKES THE OTHER.]

SOL: Is this the walk-off?

REMA: It is the cleanup.

[THE BAG CATCHES ON A BENCH. SOL AND JORI PULL; THE BALLS ROLL OUT AND STOP AGAINST THE TRIPOD.]

KITO: That is your final shot.

[SOL FILMS THE BALLS AGAINST THE CROOKED TRIPOD.]

SOL: We will call it "The Cost of the Comeback."

REMA: Call it "Put the balls away."

[THE TEAM HELPS SOL GATHER THEM. THE SCOREBOARD REMAINS AT 3-2.]
