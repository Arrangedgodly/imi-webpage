# The Repair Show Lost Its Screw

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised repair programs, overhead tool shots, sponsor cutaways, and hosts who promise a fix before diagnosing the fault*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A repair-show host promises to fix a rolling studio stool in one segment, but the missing screw turns up in the sponsor's display tray.

Cast: Host Seli, a gibbon; repairer Odo, an orangutan; guest Nari, a macaque; camera operator Pava, a capuchin; sponsor liaison Tavi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. REPAIR STUDIO - DAY

Nari sits on a rolling stool. It glides away from the table. Odo catches the backrest.

SELI: We can fix that.

NARI: It moved before I sat down.

TAVI: We have a sponsor segment in four minutes.

ACT ONE

Odo checks the stool's wheels and brake. One caster is loose. Seli asks for a close-up of the tool kit. Pava films the sponsor's glossy screwdriver set instead of the stool.

ODO: I need the stool stopped, not a product shot.

PAVA: The product shot is on the list.

Odo locks two wheels and marks the loose caster. He asks for a matching screw. The tool tray has three sizes, none of them right. Tavi says the sponsor's demonstration kit includes extra screws. Odo asks to see them. Tavi turns the tray toward camera and a small screw rolls into a groove.

ACT TWO

Seli narrates the repair steps. She says Odo will remove the caster and check its mounting plate. Odo asks her to stop before the next step; he needs to keep the wheel assembly together. Pava leans in for a macro shot. The camera blocks Odo's hand.

ODO: I cannot see the screw.

PAVA: The audience can.

ODO: They cannot tighten it.

Seli moves the camera. Odo removes the caster. The screw is missing. Nari says she heard it fall when the stool rolled. Tavi checks the floor and finds a screw stuck to the wheel magnetically. It is the wrong length.

NARI: That is the one from the display kit.

TAVI: It fell from the sponsor tray.

Seli asks whether they can use it for the repair. Odo says the length is wrong and returns it to Tavi. He checks the stool's parts pouch. It is empty. Nari says the stool's label lists the caster model. Odo compares the remaining screws to the label's diagram.

ACT TWO-B

Pava zooms in on the diagram. It is upside down. Seli turns the manual around. Odo identifies the correct screw from the parts pouch's folded inner flap. It was stuck in the fold. He checks its size with the caster, then fastens the wheel. Nari holds the stool still. The screw seats properly and the caster locks.

ODO: Now we test the brake.

NARI: Sitting or standing?

ODO: First, an empty roll test.

He pushes the stool lightly. It travels a foot, then stops at the locked caster. Nari sits on it and confirms it stays in place. Seli asks for the before-and-after shot. Pava shows the before clip; the stool rolls out of frame. The after shot keeps it centered.

SELI: We have a repair.

TAVI: And the sponsor set is missing one screw.

Odo gives him the screw from the tray. It is the wrong one.

Tavi places the sponsor tray beside the stool and labels the empty compartment. Seli asks if every screw in the kit can be used for every wheel. Odo says the lengths and threads differ. The sponsor card on the monitor still claims "universal fit."

ODO: That applies to the tool handle, not the screws.

PAVA: Can we update the product card?

TAVI: We can update the wording.

Seli changes the caption to "multi-bit driver set." Nari compares the correct fastener with the wrong display screw. The head shapes match; the lengths do not. She hands the display screw back to Tavi, who returns it to the labeled tray. Odo rolls the stool once more with its brake open, then locks it before Nari sits.

NARI: I can tell which wheel is fixed now.

ACT THREE

Odo explains that the caster is tightened, the brake works, and the stool stays put when locked. He advises Nari to use the brake before sitting and not to substitute mismatched hardware. Tavi removes the sponsor tray from the bench and checks each compartment. Seli thanks the sponsor without claiming its screws fit every stool.

NARI: It is nice to sit without traveling.

SELI: Our segment is complete.

The stool remains in place. Odo hands Nari the manual and places the spare parts pouch back on the underside. Pava films the final close-up of the locked caster, then widens out before it can dominate the shot.

TAG

Seli asks the crew to roll the stool to storage. Nari unlocks the brake first. The stool rolls away under control.

FINAL IMAGE: Tavi puts the sponsor tray back on its shelf, this time with every screw accounted for.
