# A View from the New Nest

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: architecture reality television, where designers insist on a scenic window that makes the nest drafty*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A design team adds a picture window to a tree nest, but the resident keeps choosing shade, cover, and a small entrance.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; designer Odo, baboon; resident Nara, macaque; builder Seli, capuchin; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Panoramic Nest"

### Cold Open

[TREE NEST. ODO HOLDS UP A WINDOW FRAME.]

ODO: Imagine the view.

NARA: I can see the tree.

PAVA: We could see more.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE DESIGNERS SHOW A MODEL.]

Pava says the nest feels closed in. Nara says the small entrance keeps the wind out. Odo presents a wide opening with a branch railing.

NARA: The opening is too large.

ODO: It creates space.

SELI: It also exposes the sleeping area.

Pava says the camera needs a view of the interior. Nara says the camera does not live there.

Tavi films from outside. The branch frame blocks the lens. Odo wants to remove one side branch.

NARA: That branch supports the nest.

Seli tests it; the roof shifts slightly.

SELI: We should not cut it.

### Act Two

[THE TEAM TRIES A REMOVABLE FRAME.]

Odo builds a frame from a fallen branch. It fits over the entrance but blocks the path to the inner chamber. Nara enters and bumps her shoulder.

NARA: The frame is in the way.

PAVA: It creates a grand entry.

NARA: I need to get through with leaves.

Seli widens the lower edge. The frame now has a gap, but the top is unstable. Tavi asks for a reveal shot. Odo holds the frame with one hand; it tilts.

ODO: We need a permanent attachment.

SELI: We need no frame.

Pava asks Nara what she wants. Nara says a small ledge outside where she can place leaves while entering. Seli adds a narrow strip, leaving the entrance unchanged.

[THE TEAM MEASURES THE NEST.]

Odo brings a tape measure and asks how wide the window could be. Nara says she does not want a window. Pava asks if she can at least choose the frame color.

NARA: The tree is brown.

PAVA: We have bark brown, branch gray, and leaf green.

SELI: Those are not window colors.

Tavi shows the camera how the proposed opening would look from outside. It makes the nest appear larger, but the view points directly at the neighbor's branch.

NARA: I do not want to look into their nest.

ODO: We can angle the frame.

SELI: Then the supports are uneven.

Odo adjusts one side. The frame tilts. Nara says the opening should stay small because the wind comes from that direction. Seli tests with a leaf; it blows into the nest.

PAVA: The air movement is significant.

NARA: That is why I like the small entrance.

Pava asks whether the nest needs any change at all. Nara says the ledge would help when she carries leaves. Seli builds a temporary ledge from a fallen twig. Nara tests it with a leaf and enters smoothly.

TAVI: The resident has demonstrated the design.

ODO: We have a before and after.

NARA: The before is the same nest without the ledge.

### Act Three

[THE RESIDENT TESTS THE LEDGE.]

Nara places one leaf on the ledge, then enters without hitting her shoulder. The branch holds. Pava asks for the wide-window reveal, but the camera can only see the ledge and the entrance.

PAVA: It is a smaller transformation.

NARA: It works.

ODO: The view is still there.

NARA: It is the same view.

Seli asks if they can film from the branch. Tavi moves the camera to the side; the nest is visible without cutting any supporting wood.

[THE CAMERA RECORDS THE FINISHED NEST.]

Pava asks Nara to describe the view. Nara says the same tree is still there. Odo asks if the ledge can hold a second leaf. Nara tests it; two leaves fit.

ODO: We have doubled capacity.

NARA: It is still a small ledge.

SELI: The nest remains private.

Tavi films from the branch without entering the opening. The crew packs the wide frame back into the truck. Pava asks if they can donate it to another project.

PAVA: It could fit a larger nest.

NARA: Ask the resident first.

Nara checks the ledge once more. It remains stable.

SELI: No window required.

PAVA: The resident has chosen the view.

### Tag

[THE CREW LOOKS OUT FROM THE BRANCH.]

Pava says the design is complete. Nara brings a leaf to the ledge and enters.

ODO: We have increased storage.

NARA: One leaf.

[SFX: BRANCH CREAKS, BIRDS.]
