# The Curtain Came Down Before the Scene

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live television theater, camera cutaways, stage cues, and curtains dropped at the wrong dramatic moment*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A live broadcast of an original stage play loses its curtain cue halfway through the final scene, forcing the actors to finish with the audience watching the crew.

Cast: Actor Nemi, a gibbon; actor Pava, a macaque; actor Tavi, an orangutan; director Seli, a baboon; camera operator Odo, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. LIVE THEATER - NIGHT

Pava speaks onstage. The curtain begins to descend. The actors are still mid-scene.

PAVA: I have not returned the crown.

NEMI: The curtain thinks you have.

Seli waves both arms from the wing. The curtain stops just above their heads.

ACT ONE

The broadcast is halfway through an original play about a gardener who finds a lost crown in the orchard. Nemi plays the gardener; Pava plays the town keeper; Tavi plays the orchard guide. The curtain cue is meant for the end of the act. Odo's camera has cut to a close-up of the curtain rope instead of the actors.

SELI: Camera two, back to stage.

ODO: The rope was moving.

SELI: The actors are moving.

The actors wait in character. Pava holds the crown and asks Nemi where it belongs. Nemi answers that it belongs to the town hall, not the orchard. Tavi says the town hall is locked for the night. Seli signals for them to continue.

ACT TWO

The curtain is raised. Pava repeats the last line so the audience can follow. Odo zooms in too far; viewers see only the crown. Nemi crosses the stage to the town hall prop door. It opens onto a painted wall. She looks back at Seli.

NEMI: The door is painted shut.

TAVI: The guide has the key.

Tavi reaches into the guide's pouch and finds a paper map, a pencil and no key. He reads the map and says the town hall is at the other end of the orchard. Pava tries to pass the crown through the painted door. The prop catches on a frame. Nemi frees it before the cardboard bends.

PAVA: We can carry it to the hall.

NEMI: The stage set does not go that far.

ACT TWO-B

Odo changes to a wide camera. The frame now shows the stagehand moving a bench. The audience sees the whole crew. Seli signals a blackout, but the lights stay on. Nemi uses the bench as a cart and places the crown on it. Pava and Tavi wheel it toward the edge of the set. The live audience laughs as the actors stay in character.

TAVI: The orchard path is longer than I remember.

PAVA: The town hall has moved.

NEMI: The set has stopped.

Seli sees the curtain is tangled with a decorative vine. She gestures to Odo to hold the wide shot. The crew lifts the vine from the track. The curtain rises fully. No one cuts away. Nemi carries the crown toward a small table that represents the town hall, but keeps it in her hands. Pava asks whether the town keeper has authority to accept it. Tavi says the orchard guide witnessed where it was found.

TAVI: I can witness it.

PAVA: Then the record is complete.

The stage manager whispers that the broadcast is still live. Odo asks whether to cut to the audience. Seli shakes her head and signals for a wide shot of the entire set. Nemi holds the crown out toward Pava but keeps her grip until Tavi steps beside them as a witness. The audience follows the action without narration. A prop hand reaches toward the town hall table, then waits for the actor's cue.

TAVI: The orchard has been counted.

PAVA: The town can keep the crown.

NEMI: Then let it be placed in the hall.

Odo keeps all three actors in frame. Seli lowers her cue cards and lets the scene continue. The curtain remains raised, and the audience sees the prop crew hold the vine clear of the track.

ACT THREE

The play reaches its intended final beat. Nemi hands the crown to Pava. Pava places it on the town hall table. Tavi closes the prop map. The actors hold their positions for the applause. The curtain stays up. Seli signals them to bow, and they do.

NEMI: The crown is home.

PAVA: The scene is complete.

Odo changes from the close shot to a wide view of the bows. The crew stays visible at the edge of the stage. The audience applauds the actors and the stagehands. Seli gives one cue to lower the curtain; the track is clear now, and it closes smoothly.

TAG

As the end credits roll, Odo zooms in on the curtain rope. Seli points at the stage. He switches back to the actors as they retrieve the prop crown.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty stage is framed wide, curtain closed, with the crown safely carried off by the prop crew.
