# The Reef Has a Schedule

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nature documentaries that impose narration and human order on animal routines*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-004*

Premise: A documentary crew sets out to capture the dramatic daily migration of a reef troop. The animals keep moving for practical reasons while the narrator insists every snack and grooming stop is a grand ritual.

Cast: Narrator Vela, a mandrill; camera operator Tuck, a marmoset; field guide Ranu, a langur; and reef macaques Kito, Mina, and Jo.

## Episode: "The Long Crossing"

### Cold Open

[DAWN. THREE MACAQUES CROSS A LOW ROCK LEDGE, CARRYING FRUIT.]

VELA (V.O.): At first light, the troop begins its perilous journey across the reef.

RANU: The fruit tree is on the other side.

VELA (V.O.): Each carries provisions against an uncertain future.

[KITO DROPS A FIG, TURNS AROUND, AND PICKS IT UP.]

TUCK: They have reversed course.

VELA (V.O.): The journey has encountered its first test.

### Act One

[THE CREW HIKES BEHIND THE TROOP. VELA RECORDS NARRATION INTO A MICROPHONE.]

VELA: Ranu, how far is this crossing?

RANU: About twenty steps.

VELA: Twenty steps in the language of the reef.

RANU: Twenty steps in any language.

[MINA STOPS TO GROOM JO'S SHOULDER.]

VELA (V.O.): The troop pauses to reinforce its bonds before entering exposed territory.

RANU: Mina is removing a burr.

VELA: The burr is a threat to the group.

RANU: It is a burr.

[MINA FINDS A SECOND BURR. JO LEANS CLOSER.]

TUCK: Should we film this?

VELA: This is an intimate exchange. Keep the lens wide.

### Act Two

[THE TROOP REACHES A NARROW GAP BETWEEN ROCKS. KITO TESTS THE SURFACE WITH A HAND.]

VELA (V.O.): Kito approaches the chasm, where one wrong step could end the crossing.

RANU: It is a shallow tide pool.

KITO: The stone is slick.

MINA: Use the higher edge.

[THE MACAQUES WALK AROUND THE POOL. THE CREW SETS UP A DRAMATIC CRANE SHOT.]

VELA: We need the crossing from above.

TUCK: The crane will block the path.

VELA: Can we place it just here?

RANU: That is where they are walking.

[THE TROOP ARRIVES AND WAITS. JO TAKES A FIG FROM THE CREW'S SNACK BAG.]

TUCK: Jo has borrowed my lunch.

VELA (V.O.): A bold exchange with a neighboring species.

TUCK: I am also a monkey.

RANU: The camera crew is part of the troop now.

VELA: Then we must behave with care.

[VELA MOVES THE CRANE. JO RETURNS HALF THE FIG AND KEEPS THE OTHER HALF.]

VELA: A negotiated settlement.

[THE CREW TRIES TO FILM A SLOW-MOTION SHOT OF THE TROOP LEAVING THE TREE.]

TUCK: They are going the other way.

VELA: Perhaps the evening migration has reversed.

RANU: They are heading to the water bowl.

[MINA STOPS AT THE BOWL, SPLASHES WATER ON HER ARMS, THEN CLIMBS BACK UP.]

VELA (V.O.): The troop prepares for an arduous return across the reef.

RANU: The bowl is three steps from the tree.

VELA: It is still a return.

[TUCK SETS DOWN THE CAMERA TO MOVE A CASE OUT OF THE WAY. JO CLIMBS ON THE CASE AND PEERS INTO THE LENS.]

TUCK: Jo, that is not a branch.

VELA: A young observer studies the strange metal fruit.

RANU: The camera lens is not fruit.

[JO TAPS THE LENS CAP. TUKC CLICKS IT SHUT.]

TUCK: The cap protects the glass.

VELA: The troop has tested our defenses.

RANU: Jo has found a button.

[JO TAPS THE CAP AGAIN. TUCK MOVES THE CAMERA TO HIS SHOULDER.]

VELA: We will continue at a respectful distance.

[THE CREW CHECKS THE FOOTAGE. JO APPEARS IN THE REFLECTION OF EVERY SHOT.]

TUCK: We have filmed the troop from the troop.

VELA: A rare view from within.

RANU: Jo is standing on your camera case.

[JO STEPS OFF THE CASE. TUCK RETRIEVES IT.]

TUCK: Our equipment has been returned.

VELA: The crossing has taught us to travel lightly.

### Act Three

[THE SUN SETS. THE TROOP REACHES THE TREE AND SETTLES INTO BRANCHES.]

VELA (V.O.): After a day of tests, the troop has reached its destination.

RANU: They walked across the rocks in the morning and ate in the afternoon.

VELA: The footage tells a larger story.

TUCK: The footage tells us the crane was in the way.

[MINA GROOMS KITO. KITO FALLS ASLEEP HOLDING A FIG.]

VELA (V.O.): As night falls, the troop returns to its ancient ritual of rest.

RANU: Kito fell asleep while eating.

VELA: The ritual is complete.

[THE FIG SLIPS FROM KITO'S HAND. JO CATCHES IT BEFORE IT FALLS.]

VELA (V.O.): The journey continues tomorrow.

TUCK: To the same tree?

VELA: We will let the reef decide.

[THE TROOP SHIFTS ONE BRANCH OVER. THE CREW PICKS UP THE CRANE AND FOLLOWS.]
