# The Great Nest Swap

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation reality shows that promise oversized makeovers and staged reveals to residents who only need a workable space*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A renovation host promises a family a luxury nest, but the residents keep stopping the crew from adding features that would make their actual home harder to use.

Cast: Host Tavi, a capuchin; designer Nara, a gibbon; builder Odo, a macaque; residents Miri and Pava, macaques; arborist Sela, a lemur; and the camera crew.

Episode: "Room for a Reveal"

COLD OPEN

EXT. GROVE, MORNING

Tavi stands before a rendering of a nest with a balcony, hammock and fruit shelf.

TAVI: We are giving this family a whole new lifestyle.

Miri points up at the existing nest.

MIRI: We asked for the loose rung fixed.

The rendering unfurls and covers the path. Odo has to lift it off a fallen branch.

ACT ONE

EXT. NEST TREE, LATER

Nara measures the ladder. One rung is loose, and the entry is crowded with dry leaves. Sela checks the tree and says the supporting limbs are healthy, but the proposed balcony would add weight to an already busy branch.

Tavi asks if a smaller balcony would work. Sela asks what the family needs. Pava says she wants room to carry leaves through the opening. Miri says the ladder needs one secure rung and the bedding needs to stay dry.

Nara sketches a wider entrance. Tavi adds an attached fruit shelf. Miri says fruit belongs in the nearby tree, not beside the nest. Tavi adds a leaf hammock. Pava says she needs to sleep, not swing.

ACT TWO

EXT. GROVE, AFTERNOON

Odo repairs the rung. Tavi asks him to stop so the camera can catch a dramatic hammer close-up. Odo's tool taps the rung once. Tavi asks for another take. Sela says the rung is secure and repeated hammering will loosen it.

Nara widens the entrance with a light branch. Pava tries carrying a mouthful of leaves through it. The decorative shelf blocks one side. She takes the shelf off. The camera crew replaces it for the reveal shot; Pava removes it again.

PAVA: I need a doorway, not a display.

Tavi unveils the leaf hammock. He hangs it from the tree limb, but it covers the ladder. Miri climbs around it and the hammock swings into the rendering. Odo catches the paper before it falls.

Sela asks the crew to clear the ladder. Tavi says the hammock is the episode's feature. Sela points to the family waiting with their bedding. Tavi folds the hammock and asks the designer to find a new place. Nara uses it as a temporary cover for the dry leaves while Odo works.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. NEST TREE, LATE AFTERNOON

The camera crew stages the reveal. They ask Miri and Pava to wait behind the tree. Tavi cues music and pulls away a cloth. The nest is hidden behind the oversized rendering. Pava pushes the paper aside and steps through the clear entrance.

PAVA: This is better.

Tavi asks her to repeat the reveal with more surprise. Pava says she was already surprised they built a shelf in the doorway. She carries the leaf bundle inside. It fits. Miri climbs up behind her, checks the rung, and shifts the dry bedding into place.

Odo shows the fixed rung. Nara checks the widened entry from both sides. Sela checks the nearby branch and confirms nothing is tied to it. The residents move in and out without the hammock, shelf or balcony. The camera operator keeps trying to frame the discarded accessories as if they were part of the plan.

Tavi asks if the family wants a luxury upgrade. Miri points to the rendering and says they can reuse it as a sign for the crew's equipment area. Nara flips it over and writes "KEEP PATH CLEAR" on the blank side.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. GROVE, LATE AFTERNOON

The camera crew tries one last accessory: a hanging basket for fruit. Pava carries leaves through the entrance. The basket swings into her path. She removes it and carries it to the fruit tree. Nara asks the crew to stop staging equipment in the doorway. Odo sets the unused accessories beneath the fruit tree beside the rolled rendering.

ACT THREE

EXT. NEST TREE, SUNSET

The family sits inside the nest. The entrance is open, the rung is solid, and the leaves are dry. Tavi announces a reveal. Miri opens the door flap herself. There is no music, and the crew can see the family comfortably arranged.

Pava puts one fallen leaf on the discarded fruit shelf. A squirrel monkey takes it and climbs away. Odo follows with the folded hammock.

TAG

The crew packs the rendering into its case. It does not fit. Sela turns the paper sideways, and it slides in easily.

TAVI: We made the space work.

MIRI: You folded the picture.
