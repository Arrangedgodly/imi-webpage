# The Checkout Line Is Moving

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: retail workplace mockumentary, where staff try to speed up a checkout line while a customer insists on returning a receipt*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A small shop's checkout line jams when a receipt printer keeps printing blank paper, and every shopper has a different definition of "next."

Cast: Manager Odo, baboon; cashier Nara, macaque; stocker Piri, capuchin; shopper Seli, gibbon; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Next in Line"

### Cold Open

[SHOP COUNTER. A RECEIPT PRINTER SPITS OUT A LONG BLANK STRIP.]

NARA: The printer is working.

ODO: It has printed nothing.

SELI: I need to return this receipt.

PIRI: It is blank.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE LINE HAS THREE SHOPPERS.]

Odo asks Nara to restart the printer. Nara opens the paper roll and finds the paper inserted backward.

NARA: It is facing the wrong way.

ODO: The printer has been giving us blank receipts all morning.

PIRI: The line has been moving.

SELI: I am next.

PIRI: I was next.

SELI: You stepped aside to get a basket.

PIRI: I stayed in line.

Odo looks at the floor markers. There are two arrows, one pointing toward the counter and one toward the exit.

ODO: We have a traffic issue.

Nara asks Seli what she wants to return. Seli says she has a receipt for a jar, but the jar is at home.

NARA: We need the item.

SELI: The receipt proves I bought it.

ODO: The receipt is blank.

### Act Two

[THE CAMERA FOLLOWS PIRI TO THE STOCKROOM.]

Piri brings a new paper roll. The printer takes it, but the strip unspools onto the floor.

[SFX: PAPER ROLLING.]

PIRI: The line has gained a tail.

NARA: That is not the receipt.

Odo asks the shoppers to wait while he fixes the printer. Seli hands him the blank receipt and says the item was a jar with a blue lid. Odo asks if she can describe the jar's contents.

SELI: It was empty.

PIRI: Then we have no item to inspect.

SELI: The jar is at home.

Nara points out that the store return policy requires the item and a readable receipt. Odo says he can make an exception. Nara asks him to sign the form.

ODO: The form is in the drawer.

Piri opens the drawer; it is full of blank receipts.

PIRI: The printer has filled the drawer.

NARA: Those are not forms.

[ODO HOLDS A STAFF HUDDLE BESIDE THE REGISTER.]

Odo says they need a better way to separate receipts from return forms. Nara suggests a labeled tray. Piri finds a tray, but it is already being used for items waiting to be stocked.

PIRI: We can use the basket.

NARA: Customers need baskets.

ODO: Then the blank receipts can go in the drawer.

PIRI: The drawer is full of blank receipts.

Nara pulls a long strip from the drawer. It unrolls across the counter and curls around the scanner.

[SFX: PAPER UNROLLS.]

NARA: We need to stop the printer first.

Odo reaches for the power switch. The line advances while he is behind the counter. Seli's place has been taken by another shopper.

SELI: I was next.

PIRI: You went home.

SELI: I came back with the jar.

ODO: We can process the return when your turn comes.

Seli looks at the jar, then joins the back of the line. Nara gives her a number card. The card says "1," but another shopper already has number one.

NARA: We have duplicate numbers.

PIRI: The number cards are from last week.

Odo collects them and writes new numbers on the blank receipts. The receipts are too long, so he tears them into smaller tickets. Nara labels one tray "RETURN" and another "STOCK."

ODO: This is a system.

NARA: It is two trays.

PIRI: Two trays and the line.

### Act Three

[THE PRINTER IS RELOADED CORRECTLY.]

Nara runs a test. The printer produces a readable receipt. Odo asks Seli to bring the jar back later. Seli says she will.

ODO: We can hold your place in line.

PIRI: We cannot hold the whole line.

SELI: I will go home and get the jar.

NARA: You will need to join the line again.

Seli steps out. Piri directs the next shopper to the counter. The line moves.

ODO: We have solved the receipt problem.

NARA: And the queue problem.

Piri removes the blank strips from the floor. One shopper asks if the long strip can be used as wrapping paper.

PIRI: It has no print on it.

ODO: It is still paper.

Tavi films Nara scanning a jar. The printer produces a clear receipt.

### Tag

[THE STORE IS QUIET.]

Seli returns with the jar and the readable receipt she found folded inside its empty paper sleeve at home. The line has one person.

NARA: You can go next.

PIRI: I was next.

SELI: I left the line.

ODO: The line has reset.

Seli joins the back.

[SFX: PRINTER BEEPS, PAPER TEARS.]
