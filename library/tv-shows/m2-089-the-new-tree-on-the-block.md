# The New Tree on the Block

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: neighborhood reality shows, boundary disputes, and community makeover meetings*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A new sapling is planted between two neighboring homes, and a reality crew films the residents' competing claims to the patch of shade it will create in twenty years.

Cast: Neighbor Vessa, a macaque; neighbor Oru, a gibbon; host Pala, a lemur; arborist Miko, a gorilla; and camera mediator Tett, a baboon.

## Episode: "Whose Shade Is It?"

### Cold open

EXT. SHARED CLEARING, DAY

[A tiny sapling stands in the middle of a clearing. Vessa and Oru hold opposite ends of a ribbon.]

PALA: The neighborhood has one new tree.

VESSA: It is beside my home.

ORU: It is beside my home.

MIKO: It is planted between them.

TETT: The cameras need a clear shot.

[The ribbon is tied around the sapling. It bends.]

MIKO: Don't tie the ribbon to the tree.

PALA: The community has already decorated it.

### Act One

EXT. CLEARING

[Pala interviews Vessa on one side of the sapling, then Oru on the other.]

PALA: Vessa, what do you hope the tree will bring?

VESSA: Shade in the afternoon.

PALA: Oru?

ORU: Morning shade.

MIKO: The tree is small. It has no shade.

VESSA: It will grow.

ORU: I want the branch facing my side.

MIKO: Trees do not choose a side.

TETT: The map shows the boundary line.

[He holds a map. The line runs directly through the sapling.]

VESSA: It is on my side.

ORU: The line is crooked.

TETT: The map is hand drawn.

PALA: The dispute is growing.

MIKO: The tree is not.

### Act Two

EXT. CLEARING, LATER

[The neighbors build small benches on either side of the sapling. Pala narrates.]

PALA: The residents have created two seating areas.

VESSA: Mine faces west.

ORU: Mine faces east.

MIKO: The tree needs water, not benches.

TETT: The crew has a hose.

PALA: We can water the sapling.

[They turn on the hose. Water sprays both benches and the camera.]

VESSA: The hose is pointed at my bench.

ORU: It is pointed at the tree.

TETT: The camera is getting wet.

MIKO: Move the benches closer.

VESSA: That would put them on the line.

ORU: The line is under the tree.

MIKO: The benches can share the shade.

VESSA: There is no shade yet.

ORU: We can sit in the future shade.

PALA: The neighbors are making progress.

TETT: The camera has lost focus.

[They move both benches to the center. The sapling is now between them.]

### Twenty-year forecast

EXT. CLEARING

[Miko stretches a measuring tape between the homes. Pala narrates to camera.]

PALA: The sapling is five feet tall and growing.

MIKO: It is two feet tall.

TETT: The camera angle makes it taller.

VESSA: The future shade will reach my porch.

ORU: It will reach my porch first.

MIKO: Neither porch is under the tree.

PALA: The neighbors are considering future property lines.

VESSA: We can trim the branches.

ORU: We can move the benches.

MIKO: You should not trim a tree for shade before it makes shade.

[The camera crew moves a reflector beside the sapling. Both neighbors sit in its shadow.]

VESSA: That is the shade we wanted.

ORU: It is from the camera.

PALA: The production has provided a preview.

### Camera meeting

EXT. CLEARING

[Tett asks the neighbors to stand on their marked sides. The camera tripod sits directly over the sapling.]

TETT: We need a clean shot of both houses.

MIKO: Move the tripod away from the roots.

PALA: The shot will lose the tree.

VESSA: The tree is between us.

ORU: The camera is between us.

TETT: I can use a wider lens.

[The wide shot includes both homes, the sapling, and the camera crew.]

PALA: The neighborhood is in the frame.

MIKO: That is the whole clearing.

TETT: We have found a shared view.

### Act Three

EXT. CLEARING, SUNSET

[Miko removes the ribbon and checks the sapling. The neighbors stand on either side.]

MIKO: The tree needs room to grow.

VESSA: Then the benches should move back.

ORU: We can share the clearing.

VESSA: We can share the tree.

PALA: The community has reached an agreement.

TETT: What happens when the branches grow over one house?

MIKO: We trim them safely.

VESSA: Who decides the trim?

ORU: The arborist.

MIKO: I am the arborist.

PALA: The cameras have captured the resolution.

TETT: The tree is still small.

PALA: The agreement will last.

VESSA: We will meet again when there is shade.

ORU: In twenty years?

MIKO: If you keep watering it.

[The neighbors take turns holding the hose. The sapling stands between the benches.]

### Tag

EXT. CLEARING, NEXT MORNING

[The cameras return. The sapling has grown one new leaf.]

PALA: The neighborhood is changing.

TETT: The leaf is on Oru's side.

MIKO: Leaves do not choose a side.

[The camera pulls back, leaving the sapling between both homes and out of the frame's center.]
