# The Marshal of Moth Gulch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: episodic television westerns, standoffs, and moral speeches at sunset*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A peacekeeping marshal must solve a dispute between two neighboring ranches before their runaway goats turn the town's main street into a stampede.

Cast: Marshal Cora, a mandrill; rancher Jeb, a gorilla; rancher Vale, a gibbon; deputy Moss, a capuchin; and shopkeeper Nell, a baboon.

## Episode: "A Goat Between Them"

### Cold open

EXT. MAIN STREET, MOTH GULCH, DAY

[A bell rings. Jeb and Vale face each other across a watering trough. Between them stands a goat wearing a tiny hat.]

JEB: That goat crossed onto my land.

VALE: It was following the shade.

JEB: The shade was on my land.

VALE: The goat does not know property lines.

CORA: Nobody reaches for anything.

MOSS: They are holding paper maps.

CORA: Those can be dangerous.

NELL: The goat has eaten the map.

[Everyone looks down. The goat chews.]

VALE: Now it knows the land.

JEB: It knows paper.

CORA: We will settle this at the marshal's office.

[The goat walks into the office.]

CORA: I said the marshal's office.

### Act One

INT. MARSHAL'S OFFICE

[The goat stands behind Cora's desk. A wanted poster hangs crookedly.]

CORA: Deputy, get the goat outside.

MOSS: Which side?

CORA: The side without the desk.

MOSS: That's the jail.

CORA: Then put the goat in the waiting room.

JEB: The goat belongs to Vale.

VALE: The goat belongs to neither of us. It appeared yesterday.

NELL: It ate three of my shop signs.

CORA: Does it have a brand?

MOSS: It has a hat.

JEB: I gave it the hat.

VALE: You gave a goat a hat?

JEB: It looked cold.

CORA: This is not about the hat. It's about the crossing.

VALE: The creek dried up. The goat wanted water.

JEB: My trough is full.

VALE: Your trough is on my old trail.

JEB: The trail is on my land.

CORA: We have two claims and one goat. I suggest we test whose side it prefers.

MOSS: How do we test that?

CORA: We offer it hay from both sides.

[They place hay near each rancher. The goat eats Cora's desk paper.]

NELL: It prefers paperwork.

CORA: That's an unusual preference.

### Act Two

EXT. DRY CREEK, LATE AFTERNOON

[Cora and the ranchers walk the goat to the creek. Moss carries a bucket. Nell brings a shop bell.]

CORA: The boundary used to follow the creek.

JEB: It still does.

VALE: The creek is a line of dust.

MOSS: A very dry line.

NELL: The town bell says water delivery is coming.

JEB: I have water.

VALE: Your trough is behind a fence.

JEB: The fence keeps goats out.

[The goat slips under the fence.]

JEB: It did that yesterday.

VALE: It can do it again.

CORA: This fence isn't keeping anyone safe.

JEB: The goat can get hurt.

VALE: The goat can get thirsty.

CORA: Then move the trough to the creek path. Both ranches can use it.

JEB: That means I share my water.

VALE: I can repair the trough.

JEB: You can?

VALE: I repaired the old trail bridge.

MOSS: You did not tell anyone.

VALE: It was too small for a parade.

NELL: I can bring a bucket.

CORA: And I will write an agreement.

JEB: On what?

[Cora looks at the goat, still chewing the edge of the map.]

CORA: Not on paper.

### Act Three

EXT. MAIN STREET, SUNSET

[The trough has been moved to the center of town. Vale tightens a wooden brace. Jeb pours in water. The goat drinks.]

JEB: The brace is solid.

VALE: The water is clear.

CORA: The goat is drinking from both ranches.

MOSS: The goat has no opinion about the agreement.

CORA: It has an opinion about the water.

NELL: The town can use the trough too.

JEB: If they bring their own buckets.

NELL: Fair.

VALE: What about the boundary?

CORA: We will mark it along the old creek, but the water stays shared.

JEB: What if the goat crosses again?

CORA: Then it is getting a drink.

MOSS: That seems simple.

CORA: Most problems are simple after someone stops trying to own the shade.

[Nell rings the shop bell. The goat bolts after it.]

NELL: The bell is not a watering trough.

JEB: It thinks it is lunch.

VALE: It ate the map. It can eat a bell.

CORA: Nobody chase it. It will come back for water.

[The goat returns, trailing the bell rope.]

### Tag

INT. MARSHAL'S OFFICE, EVENING

[Cora pins a blank notice to the wall. The goat eats one corner.]

MOSS: We need a sign.

CORA: Make it out of wood.

MOSS: The goat may chew it.

CORA: Then make two.

[The goat noses the new wooden sign, then walks past it to the water.]
