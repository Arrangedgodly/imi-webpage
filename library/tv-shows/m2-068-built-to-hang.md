# Built to Hang

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: engineering build competitions, countdown clocks, and judges' destructive stress tests*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: Two teams compete to build a safe hanging bridge across a narrow creek, but the judge keeps testing the models with heavier objects than the rules allow.

Cast: Host Mela, a gibbon; engineer Soro, a gorilla; engineer Peki, a capuchin; judge Davi, a baboon; timekeeper Nix, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Load Test"

### Cold open

EXT. CREEK BUILD SITE, DAY

[Two teams stand beside bundles of rope and branches. A large stone sits beside Davi.]

MELA: You have one hour to build a bridge that can carry one monkey.

Soro: One monkey at a time?

DAVI: That is the standard.

PEKI: Why is there a stone?

DAVI: The stone will test the bridge.

NIX: It is heavier than one monkey.

DAVI: It is a very thorough test.

MELA: The clock starts now.

[The stone rolls toward the creek. The teams chase it.]

### Act One

EXT. BUILD SITE

[Soro lays two branches across the creek and ties them with vines.]

SORO: We make a wide platform.

PEKI: A wide platform takes more rope.

SORO: It will distribute the load.

PEKI: The load is one person.

NIX: You have fifty minutes.

MELA: Team two is using woven vines.

PEKI: A woven surface will flex.

SORO: Flex can absorb a step.

PEKI: Or drop a foot.

DAVI: I will test the first bridge.

SORO: It is not finished.

DAVI: The clock is running.

[Mela asks Davi to wait. Nix holds up a timer.]

NIX: You have forty-five minutes.

PEKI: The judge has already tested the unfinished bridge.

DAVI: I stood beside it.

MELA: The test was observational.

[The first bridge sags under Davi's foot. Soro catches him.]

SORO: The anchor needs a deeper knot.

PEKI: The bank is soft.

SORO: We can wrap the vine around a root.

PEKI: The root is moving.

### Act Two

EXT. CREEK, LATER

[The woven bridge is complete. Peki steps onto it. It dips, then holds.]

PEKI: It flexes but stays level.

Soro: Good work.

DAVI: Now we test the stone.

PEKI: The rules said one monkey.

DAVI: The stone is a stand-in.

NIX: It does not look like a monkey.

DAVI: It has mass.

MELA: We can use the stone after the human-scale test.

DAVI: There are no humans here.

MELA: Then after the monkey-scale test.

[They roll the stone onto the bridge. The weave stretches. The stone stops in the middle.]

SORO: It is holding.

PEKI: The stone is stuck.

DAVI: The bridge has trapped the load.

NIX: Is that a failure?

DAVI: It is a complication.

[The stone shifts. Everyone grabs the rope. The bridge remains intact.]

MELA: Team two is under tension.

PEKI: The bridge is under tension.

SORO: We need to unload it carefully.

DAVI: The rules say the bridge must hold.

PEKI: It is holding. The stone is not moving.

NIX: Thirty seconds.

MELA: The clock is still running.

Soro: We can slide it to the far bank.

PEKI: The far bank is lower.

[They pull the rope together. The stone slides off onto the bank.]

DAVI: A successful stress test.

PEKI: It was not a permitted test.

DAVI: It stressed us too.

### Act Three

EXT. CREEK, SUNSET

[Both bridges are side by side. Mela brings a small basket across each one.]

MELA: The basket weighs less than a monkey, but it tests the surface.

DAVI: We can also add the stone.

PEKI: We have already tested the stone.

SORO: Let's test the basket first.

[The basket crosses the woven bridge. It holds. The branch bridge bends and returns.]

NIX: Both bridges pass.

DAVI: We need a winner.

MELA: The rules say build a safe bridge.

DAVI: The show format says one winner.

PEKI: The bridge is not a competition.

SORO: The teams can connect the two designs.

[They tie the platforms together, creating one wide crossing.]

MELA: The combined bridge is stable.

DAVI: Who wins?

NIX: The creek.

PEKI: The monkeys who cross.

[Davi tests the bridge with one foot. It holds.]

DAVI: I will accept the result.

MELA: You can cross now.

DAVI: I was not part of the weight limit.

SORO: You are one monkey.

DAVI: The stone was my stand-in.

PEKI: Then cross with it.

### Final inspection

EXT. CREEK

[Mela carries a basket of branches across the combined bridge. One branch falls into the water.]

MELA: The bridge holds.

DAVI: The branch did not.

PEKI: The bridge is for crossing, not storing lumber.

NIX: The test is complete.

DAVI: I would like to try the stone again.

SORO: You can carry the stone across.

[Davi steps onto the bridge. The teams hold their breath.]

MELA: The bridge is steady.

DAVI: I am steady.

[He reaches the far bank and sets down the stone.]

### Tag

EXT. CREEK, EVENING

[Davi steps onto the bridge carrying the stone. The bridge holds. The teams watch.]

DAVI: It passes.

NIX: The stone is still in the middle.

[The bridge dips. Davi freezes.]
