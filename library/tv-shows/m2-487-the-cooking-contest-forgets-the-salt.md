# The Cooking Contest Forgets the Salt

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking competitions that turn a small pantry mistake into a timed elimination crisis*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Nala, a macaque; contestant Piki, a capuchin; contestant Duma, an orangutan; judge Sero, a gibbon; floor producer Tavi, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING SET - DAY

Two covered dishes sit beneath the lights. A digital clock counts down from ten minutes. Nala lifts a silver cover. Piki's soup is steaming. Duma's rice is neat in a small bowl.

NALA: The pantry has one missing ingredient.

[The camera zooms on the empty salt crock.]

ACT ONE

INT. COOKING SET - CONTINUOUS

Tavi announces that the salt delivery is delayed. Nala tells the contestants they have ten minutes to finish without it. Piki tastes the soup and says it needs salt. Duma tastes the rice and says it does not. Sero asks them to cook, not perform their reactions for the camera.

Sero asks each contestant to describe the dish without mentioning the missing ingredient. Piki describes the onions and lemon. Duma points to the crisp rice edge. Nala tries to announce a surprise twist, but the contestants have already plated. The clock rings anyway. Tavi turns it off at the wall and carries the decorative prop away. Nala calls it a pantry twist. Duma declines a victory lap.

PIKI: The soup is a little flat.

NALA: A bold opening statement.

Tavi signals the camera to push in on Piki's face. Piki tries the soup again. The camera circles him while he waits for an opinion. The clock ticks loudly through the sound system. Duma's station has no music, so Nala asks the band to play suspense. The band starts a dramatic beat as Duma rinses rice.

ACT TWO

INT. PANTRY WINDOW - LATER

A runner arrives with a sealed box. The contestants stop cooking while Tavi checks whether it contains salt. The box holds paper napkins. Nala asks them to continue. Piki reaches for lemon. The producer calls for a close-up of the empty salt crock. Duma uses a strip of roasted seaweed to season the rice. The camera misses the move because it is still on the crock.

SERO: We are judging the food, not the container.

Nala asks Duma to repeat the seasoning for the camera. Duma declines; the rice is already mixed. Tavi hands the camera operator a spoon and asks him to capture the finished bowl instead. The operator frames the spoon so tightly that the judges disappear.

[The clock reaches two minutes. Piki adds lemon and toasted onions. Duma fans the rice with a tray. The fan sends napkins across the set and into the soup.]

PIKI: The garnish has arrived.

Nala asks for a safety pause. The clock keeps running in the background. Sero reaches for the stop button, but Tavi says it is a decorative prop. Nala calls time herself. The clock continues to count down. Duma turns it around, revealing a second clock on the reverse side. Both clocks are running at different speeds.

ACT THREE

INT. JUDGING TABLE - LATER

The judges taste both dishes without salt. Sero says Piki's soup is bright but needs a small pinch. Piki points out that there was no salt available. Nala tries to frame the missing crock as a surprise challenge. Sero says the challenge was to make dinner, not to explain an empty shelf.

Duma's rice has a crisp edge from the tray fanning. The judges like it. Tavi brings the napkin box to the judging table and discovers a small salt packet taped to its side. The delivery runner has used the box as a temporary carrier. Everyone looks at the packet. Nala proposes a sudden bonus round.

SERO: The dishes are already cooked.

PIKI: We could season tomorrow's lunch.

Duma splits the packet between them. Piki adds a few grains to one spoonful and tastes it. The soup improves. He stops there. Duma sprinkles a little over the remaining rice, then returns the packet to Tavi. Nala asks the judges to rank the contestants. Sero awards both a ribbon: Piki for the best recovery and Duma for the best rice. Nala tries to make one ribbon into a tie-breaker; it tears down the middle.

TAG

INT. COOKING SET - AFTER THE SHOW

Tavi places the empty salt crock in the pantry window. The delivery runner finally arrives with a full container. Nala asks for a dramatic reveal. Sero carries the container straight to the shelf without waiting for the cue.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera pushes in on the salt crock; offscreen, the crew eats the leftover soup and rice without a host announcing a winner.
