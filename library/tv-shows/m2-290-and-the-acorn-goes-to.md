# And the Acorn Goes To

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised award ceremonies, where the host delays the winner announcement for speeches, music cues, and envelope drama*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: The village awards its annual golden acorn to the best garden, but the host keeps delaying the result to build suspense and the envelope contains the wrong category.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; presenter Odo, baboon; gardener Nara, macaque; gardener Seli, capuchin; stage manager Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Golden Acorn"

### Cold Open

[STAGE. AN ENVELOPE ON A PODIUM.]

PAVA: The winner is...

ODO: The envelope is upside down.

TAVI: That is the back.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[PAVA STANDS BEFORE THE CROWD.]

Pava welcomes the garden club. The award is for the best community plot. Odo says the winner's name is inside the envelope. Tavi says there are three envelopes, each labeled with a different category.

PAVA: We will reveal the most dramatic one first.

NARA: The audience is waiting.

SELI: The plants are also waiting for water.

Odo lifts the envelope. It is sealed with a large sticker. Tavi says the sticker is only for decoration. Pava asks the band to play suspenseful music.

[SFX: SHORT DRUM ROLL.]

The band plays too long, and the audience starts clapping to stop it.

PAVA: We have a musical interruption.

### Act Two

[THE PRESENTERS CHECK THE ENVELOPES.]

Odo opens the first envelope. It says "Best Compost." Pava says that is not the top award. Nara says she won best compost last year and already returned the trophy.

NARA: It was a wooden bucket.

SELI: The award can be reused.

Tavi checks the next envelope. It is labeled "Most Improved Plot." Pava says they should save it for a touching story.

ODO: The winners are waiting.

Pava asks Nara and Seli to describe their gardens. Nara says she planted beans in a narrow bed. Seli says her plot has sunflowers and a path. Odo asks which garden is more beautiful.

NARA: Beauty is not on the score sheet.

PAVA: It is in the audience's hearts.

SELI: The score sheet says healthy plants, accessible paths, and shared harvest.

Tavi finds the score sheet under the podium. The categories are clear.

[THE STAGE MANAGER CHECKS THE WINNER LIST.]

Tavi finds a second page with the final scores. Nara and Seli are tied on healthy plants, paths, and shared harvest. Odo says the audience vote should break the tie. Pava asks the audience to clap for Nara's plot, then for Seli's.

The clapping is equally loud.

PAVA: We have a second tie.

ODO: The crowd cannot decide.

TAVI: The score sheet has a tie-breaker: "best use of rainwater."

Nara built a small barrel by her bean bed. Seli has a shallow basin under the sunflower canopy. Both captured water.

NARA: My barrel is covered.

SELI: My basin is easier to reach.

Pava says they should show each design. Odo pours water into the barrel, but the spout is blocked by a leaf. Nara clears it. The water runs into a watering can.

PAVA: A strong demonstration.

Seli tips her basin toward the bed. The water flows into a channel and reaches the sunflower roots.

TAVI: Both gardens used rainwater well.

ODO: We have a tie-breaker that ties again.

Pava asks whether the gold acorn can be awarded for teamwork. Tavi checks the rules; shared awards are allowed if there is a tie.

PAVA: The ceremony has found its ending.

### Act Three

[THE CROWD SETTLES.]

Pava says they will announce the award. Odo opens the final envelope. It contains the schedule for next year's event.

ODO: This is not the winner.

TAVI: We used the wrong envelopes.

NARA: Which one has the winner?

Seli checks the podium and finds a card beneath the microphone. It lists both gardens as tied. Pava says a tie is not dramatic enough.

SELI: It is the result.

ODO: We can present two acorns.

TAVI: We have one golden acorn.

Nara says they can split the trophy. It is made of wood, but Odo says breaking it would be bad ceremony. Seli suggests a shared display on the garden board.

PAVA: The award will rotate between the two plots.

NARA: That is practical.

SELI: We can take turns.

[THE NAME CARDS ARE SET BESIDE THE TROPHY.]

Pava asks the gardeners to say a few words. Nara thanks the garden club. Seli thanks the rain barrel.

ODO: The acorn will visit both plots.

TAVI: It can travel in a small box.

### Tag

[THE GOLDEN ACORN IS PLACED BETWEEN TWO NAME CARDS.]

Odo reads the winners aloud. Pava begins a speech, but Tavi plays the closing music.

PAVA: I will finish after the break.

NARA: There is no break.

[SFX: MUSIC SWELLS, THEN CUTS.]
