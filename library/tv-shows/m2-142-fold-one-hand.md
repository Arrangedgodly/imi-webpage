# Does It Fold in One Hand?

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television product tests that dramatize ordinary features and stage impractical demonstrations instead of checking the advertised claim*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Sumi wants to test a folding chair's "one-hand close" claim. Engineer Koro needs to read the instructions and check the safety latch before anyone sits or folds it.

Cast: Sumi, a macaque host; Koro, a gorilla engineer; Lila, a lemur studio assistant; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "Fold, Then Sit"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A FOLDING CHAIR SITS ON A MARKED PLATFORM. SUMI HOLDS UP THE BOX.]

SUMI: This chair claims to fold with one hand.

KORO: It says, "Release the side latch, then fold."

SUMI: We will see.

[SUMI PULLS THE SEAT. THE CHAIR DOES NOT MOVE.]

PUCK: The camera caught a suspenseful moment.

KORO: The latch is still engaged.

[LILA RELEASES THE LATCH. THE CHAIR FOLDS EASILY.]

SUMI: A surprise result.

KORO: The instruction worked.

### Act One

[PUCK PUTS A SPOTLIGHT ON THE CHAIR. A GRAPHIC READS "ONE-HAND CHALLENGE."]

SUMI: Can our host fold it blindfolded?

KORO: That is not the claim.

SUMI: Can we add a timer?

KORO: The claim does not mention speed.

[LILA READS THE LABEL. IT REQUIRES TWO HANDS TO OPEN SAFELY.]

SUMI: Can we call that a bonus feature?

KORO: It is a safety instruction.

PUCK: Could the label appear after the test?

LILA: The label was on the chair the whole time.

[SUMI MOVES THE BOX SO THE LABEL IS VISIBLE. IT READS "KEEP FINGERS CLEAR OF THE HINGE."]

LILA: It folds with one hand, but opens with two.

SUMI: That sounds less exciting.

KORO: It is the instruction.

[SUMI PULLS THE LATCH WITH ONE HAND AND FOLDS THE CHAIR. SHE HOLDS IT UP FOR THE CAMERA.]

PUCK: Now sit on it.

KORO: It is folded.

SUMI: We can unfold it first.

[LILA OPENS THE CHAIR WITH TWO HANDS AND CHECKS THE LATCH. SUMI SITS.]

### Act Two

[PUCK PLACES THREE HEAVY BOXES BESIDE THE CHAIR.]

PUCK: We can test the maximum weight.

KORO: The label gives a limit. We do not need to exceed it.

SUMI: Could we test it with one of the boxes?

KORO: We can use a safe load within the limit.

[LILA READS THE SPECIFICATION. THE STUDIO SCALE IS SET TO ZERO. KORO PLACES A SMALL WEIGHT ON THE SEAT.]

SUMI: It has passed the box test.

KORO: It has held a safe test weight.

[PUCK ASKS SUMI TO STAND ON THE SEAT FOR A DRAMATIC RESULT.]

KORO: Do not stand on the chair.

SUMI: I will not.

[PUCK MOVES THE SPOTLIGHT. THE CHAIR LOOKS LIKE A STAGE. SUMI STEPS BACK. LILA REMOVES THE BOXES.]

LILA: The test is complete.

SUMI: Can we compare it with the old chair?

KORO: Only if the old chair is also inspected.

[THE OLD CHAIR HAS A LOOSE FOOT. KORO TAGS IT FOR REPAIR.]

SUMI: Does that mean the new chair wins?

KORO: We tested one feature of it. We did not compare durability.

[PUCK ADDS A SCORE GRAPHIC. IT SHOWS "FOLD: WORKS" AND "DURABILITY: NOT TESTED."]

LILA: That is clearer than a trophy.

[SUMI PUTS THE TROPHY BACK IN THE PROP BOX.]

PUCK: The trophy is also a folding chair.

KORO: It is a chair-shaped trophy.

[LILA CHECKS ITS LEGS. ONE IS LOOSE. SHE TAGS IT FOR REPAIR.]

[KORO WRITES A FINAL NOTE ON THE TEST SHEET: "FOLDING FEATURE CONFIRMED; OTHER USES NOT TESTED."]

SUMI: That is a clear result.

LILA: It tells us what we know and what we do not.

[PUCK PUTS THE TEST SHEET BESIDE THE BOX INSTEAD OF THE TROPHY DISPLAY.]

### Act Three

[SUMI FACES THE CAMERA. THE CHAIR IS OPEN AND LOCKED.]

SUMI: The chair folds with one hand after the side latch is released. It opens with two hands, and the latch must be checked before sitting.

KORO: That is the full result.

PUCK: No explosion?

LILA: No.

[SUMI FOLDS THE CHAIR. IT STAYS FOLDED. LILA OPENS IT AND CHECKS THE LATCH AGAIN.]

SUMI: The feature works as described.

KORO: Use it according to the label.

[PUCK ERASES THE TIMER GRAPHIC. THE SCREEN SHOWS THE INSTRUCTIONS.]

SUMI: Would you recommend it?

KORO: If you need a folding chair and use it as directed.

LILA: And check that the latch is secure.

[SUMI FOLDS IT ONCE MORE, THEN SETS IT DOWN WITHOUT SITTING ON IT.]

[A STUDIO ASSISTANT BRINGS IN ANOTHER MODEL WITH A DIFFERENT LATCH. KORO ASKS FOR ITS MANUAL BEFORE TESTING.]

SUMI: We will read the instructions first.

[SUMI TRIES TO CARRY THE CHAIR AND BOX AT ONCE.]

KORO: Put the box down first.

[SUMI SETS IT DOWN.]

KORO: The box is flat on the floor.

SUMI: And I have two hands free.

### Tag

SUMI: The instruction works again.
