# The Hospital Drama's Dramatic Curtain

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: medical dramas, urgent diagnosis scenes, rival specialists, and cliffhangers built around a curtain that will not close*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Doctor Pali, a macaque; Doctor Venn, a baboon; Nurse Sita, a gibbon; technician Oko, an orangutan; patient Neri, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. HOSPITAL WARD - NIGHT

A curtain hangs half-open around a bed. Neri sits upright, holding a small paper cup. Doctor Pali rushes in; Doctor Venn enters from the other side with a portable lamp.

PALI: The curtain is not closing.

VENN: How long has it been open?

NERI: Since I asked for privacy.

SITA: The track catches at the corner. The patient is stable.

[Both doctors turn to the monitor, which displays a normal pulse. The dramatic monitor beep is only the cup dispenser signaling an empty stack.]

PALI: A second alarm.

SITA: The cup dispenser is empty.

VENN: We need a differential.

NERI: I need a curtain.

[The camera pushes in on the curtain hook as the theme sting plays.] 

ACT ONE

INT. WARD HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Pali and Venn stand at opposite ends of the curtain track. Sita explains that a wheel popped off its runner. Oko arrives with a tool tray and a folded replacement curtain.

OKO: The track is intact. One roller is missing.

PALI: Could this be a systemic failure?

OKO: It is a missing roller.

VENN: I have seen this before. The curtain refuses treatment until it knows its history.

SITA: The curtain is fabric.

[The doctors debate whether to replace the track. Pali points to a stain on the curtain. Venn declares it could be a sign of a bigger problem. Neri lowers the cup and looks directly at them.]

NERI: It is tea. I spilled it while waiting.

PALI: The patient remembers the event.

VENN: A promising sign.

SITA: Neri never lost consciousness.

[Oko removes the old roller from the track. It rolls under a supply cart. The doctors both dive for it, colliding shoulder to shoulder. Neri uses the call button to summon Sita, not the doctors.]

NERI: Could somebody bring me another cup?

SITA: Yes. And you two, stop diagnosing the curtain.

ACT TWO

INT. SUPPLY ALCOVE - LATER

Oko brings a replacement roller. Venn insists on a dramatic consultation before installation. Pali asks for an X-ray of the track; Oko holds up a ruler. The track measures straight. Sita returns with a fresh cup and asks Neri whether the new position is comfortable.

NERI: I can see the hallway, the clock, and two doctors arguing over a wheel.

PALI: Is the view causing discomfort?

NERI: The arguing is.

VENN: We can reduce the noise.

SITA: You can stop talking.

[The doctors fall silent. Oko clips the roller into place and slides the curtain along the track. It stops before the corner again because a paper chart is caught behind the rail. Oko removes the chart and reads it.]

OKO: This is a menu from last night.

NERI: It was tucked there when the curtain jammed.

PALI: The chart was obstructing the patient's care.

SITA: The chart is a menu.

VENN: A menu of possible causes.

[Oko slides the curtain again. It closes smoothly. The two doctors each reach for the handle to present the result. Sita gently moves them aside and lets Neri decide the curtain position.]

ACT THREE

INT. HOSPITAL WARD - NIGHT

The curtain is open halfway. Neri places the cup on the bedside table. Sita asks if the patient wants it closed all the way; Neri says halfway is fine. Oko checks that the roller stays in its track, then puts the tool tray away.

PALI: The curtain's condition has improved.

VENN: We should monitor it overnight.

OKO: It is a curtain. The roller is installed.

SITA: Patient's chart is stable. Curtain is working.

[The camera moves toward the doctors, expecting a solemn close-up. Neri points to the empty cup dispenser.]

NERI: The only emergency now is cups.

[Pali and Venn race to the dispenser. Both arrive at once and reach for the refill box. Sita takes the box from them and restocks the dispenser. The doctors are left holding the empty cardboard sleeve.] 

PALI: We nearly lost the supply.

SITA: We had a replacement in the cabinet.

VENN: A close call.

NERI: A normal Tuesday.

[An overhead light flickers. The doctors both reach for their stethoscopes. Oko points to the switch by the bed. Sita toggles it; the light steadies.] 

PALI: The room responded to treatment.

OKO: The switch was loose.

VENN: We should observe it for recurrence.

SITA: I tightened the plate. The room is fine.

[The camera begins a dramatic push toward the monitor. Neri covers the display with the menu from earlier.]

NERI: Please do not give the cup dispenser a close-up.

PALI: Agreed. We have no evidence it is ill.

VENN: The dispenser has been through enough tonight.

[Sita rolls the empty chart cart away from the curtain track. Oko tests the curtain once, and Neri gives a small nod from the bed.]

TAG

The dispenser gives one quiet click as a new stack settles. Both doctors turn toward it. Sita raises one finger, and they remain still.

FINAL IMAGE: Neri sips from a full cup behind a curtain that closes smoothly when asked.
