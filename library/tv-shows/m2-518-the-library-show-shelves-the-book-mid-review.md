# The Library Show Shelves the Book Mid-Review

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: book-review broadcasts that interrupt a discussion to meet a rigid shelf-and-segment schedule*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Vela, a macaque; reviewer Oru, an orangutan; librarian Neri, a lemur; floor manager Pali, a capuchin; caller Savi, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. LIBRARY STUDIO - DAY

Oru holds up a book. Pali points at a cart labeled RETURNS.

ORU: I have not finished the review.

PALI: The shelf segment starts now.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Vela asks Oru to finish his point about the ending. Pali wheels the returns cart in front of the camera. Neri says the book has not been checked out; it is a studio copy. Pali scans it anyway. The library computer reports it overdue by 400 days.

NERI: This book belongs to the station.

The host asks whether the review can continue. Oru says the main character changes in the last chapter. The cart begins rolling toward the return slot. Pali chases it, leaving the host alone with the book jacket.

ACT TWO

INT. LIBRARY STACKS - LATER

Neri checks the barcode. The book was entered as a library copy during a previous segment, then placed back in the studio. Pali says the overdue warning is a good cliffhanger. Vela asks if they can clear it before the next guest. Neri edits the record. The computer asks for a return date. Pali types today. It requests a checkout date too.

[Oru continues the review while walking between shelves. Vela follows with a microphone. Pali follows with the cart. The camera films a tracking shot of the group moving in a slow circle around the same shelf.]

Savi calls to ask whether the book is worth reading. Oru says he can answer after discussing the last chapter. Vela begins an answer for him. Neri asks her to let the reviewer finish. Pali presents the cart again, this time from the opposite aisle. The books are still on it.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Neri clears the book's false overdue record. The screen still shows a red alert from the earlier scan. Pali asks her to turn it off. She removes the book from the returns list, but the alert remains on the broadcast graphics. Vela tries to cover it with a review quote. The quote is too long and fills the entire screen.

INT. LIBRARY STACKS - MOMENTS LATER

Neri finds an actual library copy on the shelf and checks its record. It is available, but the barcode is easy to confuse with the studio copy. She puts a bright green sleeve around the library copy and keeps the studio copy on the review cart. Pali reaches for the red overdue card; Neri stops him before he scans anything else.

NERI: One copy is part of the set. One copy can leave with a reader.

Vela asks Pali to put the two copies in separate camera frames. He places the library book by the checkout desk and the studio book on its labeled shelf. The red alert is still on the broadcast screen. Pali tries to cover it with a large review quote; the quote fills the screen and hides the book titles. Oru asks him to remove the quote. Pali lowers the card. The alert remains, but the titles are visible.

Savi calls again and asks whether the library copy is available. Neri checks the green sleeve and says yes, after the review. Pali sets the empty returns cart outside the frame. Vela asks Oru for the last page. He closes the studio copy without revealing it. The crew resets the camera for one uninterrupted closing take; nobody scans either book during the setup.

ACT THREE

INT. LIBRARY STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Oru finishes his review by saying the last chapter changed how he understood the first. Vela asks whether the viewer should borrow the title. Neri points to the studio copy on its marked shelf, then to the library copy in the green sleeve. Pali scans only the green-sleeved copy. The computer gives a current due date. Savi enters, signs the checkout slip and takes that copy with her.

The red overdue alert remains on the monitor. Pali removes the false station record and refreshes the graphic once. The alert clears. Oru keeps the studio copy on its shelf; Vela reads the title card without hiding the book. Savi asks what happens in the final chapter. Oru smiles and says he will not spoil it. Savi leaves with the borrowed copy, and the camera holds on the studio copy's clean shelf label.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SEGMENT

Pali wheels the empty returns cart away. Neri checks the shelf label. Vela asks Oru if the next book is ready. He says he has not opened it yet.

FINAL IMAGE: The reviewed book rests on its marked studio shelf, while the returns cart passes out of frame without it.
