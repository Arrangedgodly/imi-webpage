# The Countdown Started Yesterday

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live New Year countdowns that rely on unsynced clocks, stretch filler segments, and assume every viewer reaches midnight at once*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Luma wants a synchronized midnight special. Producer Puck has sold two countdown peaks to a sponsor and plans to replay the same countdown for a second region. Timekeeper Odo needs to keep the live clock accurate as that plan collides with local midnight.

Cast: Luma, a macaque host; Odo, a gorilla timekeeper; Vela, a lemur clock technician; and Puck, a capuchin producer.

## Episode: "Ten Seconds, One Time Zone"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. TWO LARGE CLOCKS SHOW DIFFERENT TIMES. LUMA HOLDS A GLITTERING HAT.]

LUMA: We are just ten seconds away from midnight.

ODO: The wall clock says twelve minutes.

VELA: The display is not synced.

[PUCK TURNS THE CLOCK FACE TOWARD CAMERA. THE HANDS FALL OFF.]

PUCK: The clock is on break.

LUMA: Then we have time for one more musical number.

ODO: We have time to set the clock.

### Act One

[VELA CONNECTS THE DIGITAL CLOCK TO AN OFFICIAL TIME SIGNAL. PUCK PLAYS A COUNTDOWN BED.]

LUMA: Welcome to our worldwide celebration.

PUCK: The sponsor bought a second midnight peak. We will replay this countdown when the next region reaches twelve.

ODO: Your recording says "Welcome, River Ward." The next region is Hill Ward.

LUMA: We have not reached midnight here yet.

[PUCK SHOWS A MAP WITH ONE TIME ZONE HIGHLIGHTED. THE REST OF THE MAP IS BLANK.]

PUCK: We have viewers in many places.

VELA: They will reach midnight at different times.

LUMA: Then we can say good evening to everyone.

[THE STUDIO AUDIENCE PRACTICES A CHEER. THEY SHOUT BEFORE THE COUNTDOWN.]

LUMA: Please wait for the final second.

AUDIENCE MEMBER: We heard the band.

PUCK: The band is not a time signal.

[ODO HOLDS UP A CARD READING "WAIT FOR ZERO." THE AUDIENCE PRACTICES A QUIET WAVE INSTEAD.]

LUMA: Save it for zero.

AUDIENCE MEMBER: Sorry.

ODO: That was a practice cheer.

PUCK: Can we use it in the final?

VELA: It was early.

### Act Two

[THE CLOCK SIGNAL DROPS. VELA CHECKS THE CABLE.]

LUMA: Our countdown is paused.

ODO: It is not counting down.

[PUCK FILLS THE GAP WITH A RECAP OF THE YEAR. HE SHOWS FOOTAGE OF THE STUDIO CLOCK FALLING.]

LUMA: We learned to check our time sources.

VELA: We learned to secure the clock.

[THE CABLE RECONNECTS. THE DIGITAL DISPLAY JUMPS FORWARD BY THREE MINUTES.]

PUCK: We are now at eleven fifty-eight.

ODO: That is closer.

LUMA: We can begin the countdown early and hold on zero.

VELA: The official signal has not reached zero.

[PUCK SETS THE CLOCK BACK TO FIT THE SPONSOR CUE. THE DISPLAY NOW DIFFERS FROM THE OFFICIAL SIGNAL BY THREE MINUTES.]

ODO: We are on air with the wrong time.

[THE AUDIENCE STARTS COUNTING DOWN. ODO HOLDS UP THE WAIT CARD. PUCK KEEPS THE MUSIC PLAYING.]

LUMA: Could we add a minute to the special?

ODO: Midnight will still arrive when it arrives.

PUCK: We can fill the extra minute with a look back at the year.

VELA: We already showed the clock falling.

[PUCK PUTS THE RECAP FOOTAGE AWAY AND ASKS THE BAND FOR A SHORT SONG.]

ODO: Do not change the time to make the segment fit.

[THE AUDIENCE CHEER FADES. LUMA REMOVES THE GLITTER HAT AND SETS IT DOWN.]

LUMA: We can talk with the musicians while we wait.

[THE BAND PLAYS A SHORT PIECE. PUCK BEGINS A LONG INTRODUCTION.]

VELA: The time is moving.

PUCK: I am shortening the introduction.

### Act Three

[VELA RESTORES THE DIGITAL CLOCK TO THE OFFICIAL SIGNAL. ODO CHECKS BOTH.]

ODO: One minute to midnight.

LUMA: We are ready.

[PUCK ASKS THE AUDIENCE TO HOLD THEIR CHEER UNTIL ZERO. VELA PLACES THE GLITTER HAT ON THE TABLE.]

PUCK: Do we have the correct time zone on the display?

VELA: Yes. Local time at the studio.

ODO: The signal is steady.

[LUMA CHECKS THE COUNTDOWN CARD. IT HAS TEN NUMBERS, NOT ELEVEN.]

LUMA: We are ready.

LUMA: Ten, nine, eight...

[THE CLOCK COUNTS DOWN. EVERYONE FOLLOWS THE SAME DISPLAY.]

AUDIENCE: Three, two, one!

[THE OFFICIAL SIGNAL HITS MIDNIGHT. THE CROWD CHEERS. CONFETTI FALLS, BUT THE FAN BLOWS IT BACKWARD INTO THE BAND.]

PUCK: The sponsor wants the same countdown again at the next region's midnight.

ODO: The recording already says this region. Hill Ward reaches midnight in another hour.

LUMA: We will go live with their actual time instead.

[PUCK SELECTS THE HILL WARD LIVE CLOCK AND CUES THE RECORDING IMMEDIATELY. THE SCREEN SAYS "HAPPY NEW YEAR, RIVER WARD" ABOVE HILL WARD'S 11:00 DISPLAY. THE BAND STOPS PLAYING.]

VELA: That is not midnight there.

[VELA CUTS THE TAPE AND RESTORES THE STUDIO'S LIVE CLOCK. IT READS 00:00. PUCK HOLDS A SPONSOR CUE CARD MARKED "SECOND PEAK: IMMEDIATELY."]

PUCK: The sponsor bought two midnights. They did not buy an hour between them.

LUMA: Happy New Year.

ODO: Here.

[LUMA ASKS ODO TO CHECK THE OFFICIAL SIGNAL ONE LAST TIME. IT MATCHES THE STUDIO DISPLAY.]

ODO: The clocks agree.

VELA: The prop clock does not.

[PUCK REMOVES THE PROP CLOCK FROM THE SET. THE DIGITAL DISPLAY STAYS VISIBLE.]

[PUCK CHECKS THAT THE COUNTDOWN CARD IS STORED WITH THE RIGHT DATE. VELA SAVES THE TIME-SIGNAL LOG.]

PUCK: We will use a new card for the next region.

LUMA: And their local clock.

### Tag

[PUCK SHOWS A MAP WITH OTHER TIME ZONES.]

PUCK: The sponsor has one more countdown card.

ODO: Use the live signal when it is their midnight.

[PUCK SETS THE REPLAY CARD ASIDE. THE BROKEN WALL CLOCK DROPS ANOTHER HAND.]

LUMA: That one is not on the schedule.

[ODO LABELS THE CLOCK "PROP TIME" WHILE VELA COVERS THE BROKEN CLOCK WITH THE REPLAY CARD.]
