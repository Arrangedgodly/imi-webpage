# The Puppet Has a Handprint

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: children's puppet television, hidden puppeteers, shadow reveals, and hosts who try to keep every hand offscreen*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A puppet's shadow keeps revealing the puppeteer, so a children's program must decide whether to hide the hand or let it join the scene.

Cast: Host Rava, a macaque; puppet Momo, a capuchin; puppeteer Teli, a gibbon; lighting operator Odo, an orangutan; director Piri, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

INT. PUPPET STUDIO - MORNING

Rava introduces Momo, a small puppet monkey. A huge hand shadow appears on the backdrop.

RAVA: Momo has brought a friend.

MOMO: It is my hand.

TELI: It is my hand.

PIRI: We need to move the light.

ACT ONE

The program's scene is about Momo building a paper kite. Rava asks him to choose a shape. Momo picks a square. Teli's fingers remain visible below the puppet's felt arm. Odo adjusts the light, but the fingers become a larger shadow on the wall.

RAVA: The shadow has grown.

MOMO: It is bigger than I am.

PIRI: Move the light higher.

Odo raises the lamp. The backdrop brightens. Momo disappears into the glare, leaving only Teli's hand visible.

ACT TWO

Piri asks Teli to move the puppet behind the screen. Momo's voice continues while the puppet is hidden. Rava asks where he went. Teli says he is behind the screen. Odo moves a second light to the side; the hand shadow crosses the set like a tree branch.

MOMO: I can see the shadow.

RAVA: Can you build the kite?

MOMO: I can hold the paper.

Teli brings Momo back into frame. The square kite folds in half. The hand shadow presses the fold. Rava asks whether Momo wants help. The puppet nods; Teli's hand nods too. Piri tells Odo to cut away, but the camera remains on the set.

ACT TWO-B

Rava asks the puppeteer to place the kite on the floor. Momo stands beside it. Teli's fingers become visible again. Odo adjusts the light so the puppet and puppeteer's hands appear at the same scale. The shadow no longer towers over Momo.

PIRI: It looks like there are two characters.

MOMO: There are two of us.

TELI: I help move him.

RAVA: Then you can help fold the kite.

They fold the paper together. Momo holds one corner; Teli presses the crease. Odo points the light toward the backdrop, and the shadow displays the same kite shape. The image reads clearly. Piri asks whether the program still works if viewers see the puppeteer.

RAVA: The puppet is the character. Teli is the person who moves him.

MOMO: We can build a kite together.

The puppet and hand raise the finished kite. The shadow resembles a bird. Momo tilts his head. The puppet shadow tilts too. Rava follows the moment with the camera instead of cutting away.

ACT TWO-B - THE SHADOW TAKE

Rava asks for one clean take of the kite scene. Odo positions the lamp so Teli's hand is hidden. Momo picks up the paper, but the light now throws an enormous puppet shadow across the wall. Piri calls cut.

PIRI: The hand is gone. The shadow has applied for its own close-up.

Odo lowers the lamp. The shadow shrinks, then lands across the kite. Momo tilts his head; the silhouette tilts too. Teli tries to hold the puppet still while the paper flaps in a vent. The kite slips from Momo's grasp and drifts toward the edge of the set. Teli's visible fingers catch it.

MOMO: You saved the kite.

TELI: I was already in the picture.

Rava asks whether they want to hide the hand for the final take. Momo looks at the shadow, then at Teli. They decide the scene is about making the kite, so the hands can share the work. Piri marks the blocking: Momo holds the paper, Teli folds the corner, and the puppet's shadow makes the tail. Odo moves the lamp so the shadow stays attached to the object instead of climbing the wall. The light finally behaves like part of the set rather than a second cast member.

ACT THREE

The scene continues. Momo takes the kite to the outdoor set. Teli operates him from below the frame; Odo angles the light so the hand shadow falls outside the backdrop. The kite lifts in the breeze, then dips. Momo's felt hands hold the string. The program ends with the puppet waving goodbye and Teli's hand giving a small wave beside him.

PIRI: We have the ending.

RAVA: And the shadow stayed on the backdrop.

MOMO: It can be part of the story.

TAG

Piri checks the camera monitor. The shadow of the boom microphone is visible above the kite.

PIRI: We have one more guest.

FINAL IMAGE: Momo and Teli hold the paper kite together as the boom shadow hovers overhead, clearly part of the set.
