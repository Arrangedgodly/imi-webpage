# Three Angles and a Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live sports replay shows, controversial finish debates, and commentators who slow every motion to a crawl*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A tree-climbing relay ends in a photo finish, and the broadcast crew discovers that all three camera angles show the branch moving instead of the finish line.

Cast: Anchor Naro, a gibbon; analyst Seli, a baboon; field commentator Tavi, a macaque; referee Piri, a capuchin; racer Doma, an orangutan; rival captain Sumi, a macaque.

COLD OPEN

EXT. CLIMBING COURSE - DAY

Doma crosses the finish and taps the bell. The crowd cheers. Piri raises one hand, then the other.

NARO: We have a finish and a question.

SELI: The bell rang.

PIRI: The bell rang after the tail crossed.

ACT ONE

The relay course runs up a short climbing wall, across a fixed branch, and down a ladder. Doma's team finished first. A rival team claims she touched the bell before her whole body crossed the tape. Tavi reports from the course while Naro asks Seli to review the footage.

TAVI: Doma crossed the tape, then rang.

SELI: The front camera shows a blur.

NARO: The side camera?

SELI: It shows the branch.

ACT TWO

The broadcast replays the finish in slow motion. A gust moves the branch. Doma's tail swings past the tape, then her hand reaches for the bell. Piri says the rule requires the racer's torso to cross before the bell may be rung.

NARO: What counts as torso?

PIRI: The torso is the middle of the body.

SELI: We can draw a line on the replay.

She draws a line. It lands over Doma's shoulder. Tavi asks for another angle. The camera from behind shows the bell, the branch, and a commentator standing in the way.

TAVI: The camera sees my shoulder.

NARO: That is a fourth angle.

TAVI: It is the angle of my shoulder.

ACT TWO-B

Piri explains that the finish sensor records the tape crossing. The sensor beeped before the bell. The official race clock shows Doma's team first by half a second. The rival team asks to hear the beeps. Seli plays them; the first is the sensor, the second is the bell.

SELI: Sensor. Bell.

NARO: The order seems clear.

PIRI: It is clear under the written rule.

Doma's teammate says the branch shifted and made the tape swing. Piri checks the wind marker. A gust registered during the finish. Seli overlays the wind reading; the graphic covers the racers entirely. Naro asks her to shrink it.

NARO: I would like to see the sport.

Seli shrinks the graphic, then slows the clip once more. In slow motion, Doma's tail passes the tape before her torso, but the sensor is mounted at torso height and registers only when her shoulder reaches it.

SELI: The sensor cannot be triggered by the tail.

PIRI: Correct. The rule and device agree.

The scoreboard still reads "PROVISIONAL." Naro asks for a final word from the rival team's captain, Sumi, a macaque. She says she did not see the sensor, only the tail passing the tape. Doma waits beside the finish, holding the bell rope away from her tail.

SUMI: I want to know the rule before the next relay.

PIRI: The torso must cross the sensor line. Then the racer rings the bell.

DOMA: I heard the bell and kept moving.

NARO: Did you know the result was in question?

DOMA: I heard the replay music.

Seli cues the replay once at full speed. The crowd can see the tape and sensor light blink before the bell. The camera then follows Doma down the ladder. The rival team watches the screen rather than the commentators.

SUMI: The order is clear at full speed.

TAVI: The official called it provisional until the sensor log came through.

Piri shows the log on a handheld display. It lists the finish sensor, bell switch, and race clock in sequence. The screen also shows Seli's replay button pressed seven times.

SELI: I was checking the same moment.

NARO: We were all checking the same moment.

Piri asks the course crew to mark the sensor line more visibly before the next heat. Sumi agrees and asks that the wind shield not block the view from the side camera. Tavi draws a smaller shield around the sensor mount. The crew tests it; it stays below the branch and leaves the tape visible.

SUMI: That would help the next team.

DOMA: And you still get the second-place ribbon.

SUMI: We came to race, not to argue about the ribbon.

ACT THREE

Piri confirms Doma's team as the winner. The rival team accepts the ruling, asking the course crew to add a wind shield before the next race. Naro begins the postgame analysis. Seli prepares one final replay and shows it at normal speed.

TAVI: That looked like a race.

NARO: The slow motion made it look like a moving branch.

PIRI: The branch moved. The torso crossed. The bell rang.

Doma receives the relay ribbon. Her team hangs it on the branch, which bends under the weight. She removes the ribbon and pins it to a board instead.

TAG

Naro asks for a close-up of the ribbon. The camera zooms in until it fills the frame.

FINAL IMAGE: Seli rewinds the clip to the moment before the race, while the course crew ties down the finish tape.
