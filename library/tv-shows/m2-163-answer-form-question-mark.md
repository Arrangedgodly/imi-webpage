# Answer in the Form of a Question Mark

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: high-stakes quiz tournaments where buzzer speed matters more than listening to the full clue*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Contestant Nuri knows every bird call in the forest but keeps buzzing before host Vale finishes the clue. Stage manager Redd wants a record-setting final; scorekeeper Olli tries to preserve the actual question and scoring rules.

Cast: Vale, a macaque host; Nuri, a toucan contestant; Olli, a gorilla scorekeeper; and Redd, a capuchin stage manager.

## Episode: "Fastest Finger, Wrong Bird"

### Cold Open

[QUIZ STUDIO. THREE CONTESTANTS FACE LIGHT-UP BUZZERS. VALE HOLDS A CLUE CARD.] 

VALE: This forest bird has a blue crest and...

[NURI BUZZES.]

NURI: Kingfisher.

VALE: I had not finished.

REDD: The buzzer made a great sound.

OLLI: The answer is incorrect. The clue is incomplete.

[REDD REWINDS A SPARKLING BUZZER ANIMATION. IT PLAYS WHILE OLLI HOLDS UP A RED CARD.] 

### Act One

[VALE STARTS A NEW CLUE. NURI HOLDS BOTH WINGS STILL.]

VALE: The bird nests in a tree cavity and uses its long bill to reach fruit...

[NURI BUZZES AGAIN.]

NURI: Toucan.

VALE: Correct, but the rule says wait until I finish.

NURI: I recognized the beak.

OLLI: The last phrase was the distinguishing clue.

[REDD ADDS A COUNTDOWN GRAPHIC OVER THE QUESTION. IT COVERS VALE'S CARD.]

VALE: We cannot read the clue.

REDD: The clock makes it tense.

[OLLI TAKES THE REMOTE AND REMOVES THE GRAPHIC. HE SETS A SMALL BELL BESIDE VALE.] 

OLLI: The contestants need to hear the whole clue first.

[REDD REPLACES THE SCREEN WITH A SPLIT TIMER: ONE SIDE COUNTS DOWN FROM FIVE, THE OTHER FROM SEVEN.]

NURI: Which clock decides when the clue ends?

REDD: The faster one.

OLLI: The clue ends when the host finishes reading.

[VALE READS AN ENTIRE SAMPLE QUESTION. NURI'S WING TREMBLES OVER THE BUTTON. OLLI COVERS IT WITH THE SCORECARD UNTIL THE LAST WORD.]

NURI: I heard the whole sentence.

VALE: And the answer?

NURI: I know now that I need the last part.

[REDD SETS A PRACTICE CLUE ON THE NEXT CARD. NURI KEEPS HIS WING ON HIS LAP UNTIL VALE FINISHES.]

VALE: This mammal uses echolocation to navigate in darkness.

[NURI WAITS. THE CLOCK'S BEEP STARTS EARLY. HE DOES NOT BUZZ. VALE FINISHES THE CLUE.]

NURI: Bat.

OLLI: Correct. The buzzer was not needed until the clue ended.

[REDD PEELS THE SPARKLING ANIMATION OFF THE BUZZER BUTTON. IT LEAVES A SMALL STICKY STAR ON HIS THUMB.]

### Act Two

[THE FINAL ROUND BEGINS. A CHIME PLAYS BEFORE EACH QUESTION. NURI'S WING HOVERS OVER THE BUZZER.]

VALE: Which bird...

[NURI BUZZES BEFORE THE CHIME FINISHES. THE BUZZER SOUNDS LIKE A TOOT. NURI ANSWERS "TOUCAN." ]

OLLI: The clue has not begun.

NURI: I heard a bird sound.

REDD: Can we count it as a musical response?

OLLI: No.

[VALE WAITS. THE CHIME ENDS.]

VALE: Which bird is known for drumming against a tree trunk to find insects?

[NURI WAITS. ANOTHER CONTESTANT BUZZES AND ANSWERS WOODPECKER CORRECTLY.]

NURI: I knew that.

OLLI: You also heard the full clue.

[REDD GIVES NURI A SOFT FOAM COVER FOR THE BUZZER. THE COVER MUFFLES THE SOUND BUT DOES NOT STOP THE BUTTON.]

NURI: That is not a rule change.

VALE: It is a softer buzzer.

### Act Three

[THE LAST QUESTION IS WORTH DOUBLE POINTS. REDD PLACES A GOLDEN BUZZER ON THE DESK.]

REDD: The first press wins the whole round.

OLLI: That is not on the rules card.

VALE: The posted rule is double points for a correct answer.

[REDD PULLS THE GOLDEN BUZZER AWAY. NURI FOLDS HIS WINGS IN HIS LAP.]

VALE: This bird's call is a series of short notes often heard near wetland reeds. Listen to the recording.

[THE RECORDING PLAYS COMPLETELY. NURI WAITS UNTIL IT ENDS, THEN PRESSES.]

NURI: Reed warbler.

OLLI: Correct.

[THE SCOREBOARD GIVES NURI THE POINTS, NOT A BONUS FOR SPEED. THE OTHER CONTESTANTS CLAP.]

NURI: I had to listen all the way through.

VALE: That is the game.

[REDD PUSHES A NEW BUTTON MARKED "INSTANT REVEAL." OLLI LOOKS AT THE RULES CARD, THEN SWITCHES IT OFF.]

REDD: It would have shown the answer sooner.

OLLI: The host had already given the answer.

[NURI'S BUZZER LIGHT REMAINS ON. NURI WAITS, THEN PRESSES IT ONLY AFTER VALE SAYS "NEXT QUESTION." THE SOUND IS A QUIET TOOT.]

NURI: I wanted to see if it was fixed.

VALE: It is now part of the recording.

[REDD STARTS A SLOW-MOTION REPLAY OF NURI'S HAND WAITING. THE GRAPHIC SHOWS THE WING HOVERING FOR FOUR SECONDS.] 

### Tag

[REDD HOLDS THE GOLDEN BUZZER LIKE A TROPHY. IT STARTS TO BEEP. OLLI TAKES OUT THE BATTERY.] 

OLLI: The recording has ended.

NURI: I waited.
