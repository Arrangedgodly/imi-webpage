# Flip This Hollow

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation reality television, where a makeover crew promises to transform a tree hollow without consulting its resident*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: A renovation crew arrives to turn a quiet tree hollow into an "open-concept" retreat, while the resident woodpecker keeps putting the walls back.

Cast: Host Vela, a macaque; designer Piri, a gibbon; carpenter Odo, a baboon; resident Tavi, a woodpecker; camera operator Nara, a capuchin.

## Episode: "The Open-Concept Hollow"

### Cold Open

[EXT. OLD TREE. A SAW WHINES. TAVI SLAPS A WING AGAINST THE BARK.]

TAVI: Stop cutting the wall.

ODO: We are opening the room.

TAVI: It is a tree.

VELA: After the break, we'll reveal a spacious hollow with improved airflow.

TAVI: After the break, I'll close it again.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[HOST VELA STANDS BESIDE A MODEL TREE WITH A LARGE PAPER DOOR.]

VELA: Tavi asked for a more comfortable home. Our team heard one phrase: "more space."

TAVI: I said more space for me. I did not say remove the back wall.

PIRI: The old hollow feels cramped because the entrance is small. We can widen it and bring in natural light.

TAVI: I sleep in there.

PIRI: Then you will have a better view.

Odo taps the trunk. The sound changes near the hollow.

ODO: The wood is solid around the entrance.

TAVI: That is where I pecked a side chamber.

VELA: A hidden feature.

TAVI: A bedroom.

Nara asks for a before shot. Vela stands in the hollow while Odo takes measurements. Tavi flies in carrying a strip of bark and blocks the camera.

NARA: We can't see the room.

VELA: That's part of the reveal.

TAVI: It is part of the wall.

Piri draws a design on a board: "OPEN PLAN / SUNLIT / STREAMLINED." Tavi pecks a small square into the board.

TAVI: Window.

PIRI: We can put the window on the side.

TAVI: That is the nest chamber.

### Act Two

[THE CREW HAS SET UP A SAW AND A PORTABLE LIGHT.]

VELA: We are removing just enough bark to create a welcoming entry.

TAVI: The entry is welcoming to me.

ODO: We have to be careful. We won't cut the supporting wood.

TAVI: You are cutting my doorway.

Piri holds up a color sample. The options are "birch white" and "bark neutral."

TAVI: The tree is already brown.

PIRI: We call that "earth tone."

Odo makes one shallow cut. A small piece of bark falls. Tavi catches it and tries to fit it back into the opening.

TAVI: You made a hole.

ODO: A small one.

VELA: The camera needs a clear angle.

TAVI: The hole is for air, not cameras.

Nara offers a side view. The shot shows the ledge and the hollow without cutting another strip. Vela asks for a reveal angle from below.

TAVI: There is a nest there.

NARA: The camera is not going in.

Piri lowers the light. The hollow returns to shadow.

Odo sets the bark strip against the opening for a camera test. A draft catches it and sends it fluttering into the hollow. Tavi flies in, retrieves it, and blocks the gap again.

[SFX: SAW STOPS. PECKING.]

ODO: The resident is rebuilding.

VELA: Is this a protest?

TAVI: It is a draft.

Piri suggests a removable screen. Odo frames a small ledge instead of widening the opening. Tavi tries it. The ledge is too narrow for a landing.

TAVI: My feet don't fit.

PIRI: We can extend it.

TAVI: Or stop.

The camera operator asks the host to repeat the line "We are opening the room." Vela repeats it while Tavi pecks at the new ledge.

VELA: We are opening the room.

TAVI: I am closing it.

NARA: We have two competing slogans.

### Act Three

[THE HOST'S MODEL NOW HAS A SMALL SIDE LEDGE. THE TREE IS INTACT.]

VELA: Our renovation team has created a landing without changing the hollow's structure.

TAVI: The entrance is the same size.

ODO: The ledge gives you a place to pause before going in.

Tavi lands on it. One corner wobbles. Odo adds a short brace made from a fallen branch.

TAVI: That is stable.

PIRI: Could we add a cushion?

TAVI: The tree is the cushion.

Vela begins the reveal speech. The camera pushes in as Tavi enters the hollow. He returns with the loose bark strip and plugs the unused gap by the camera light.

VELA: A dramatic new finish.

NARA: The shot is dark.

TAVI: My home is dark.

Odo switches off the light. The crew steps away from the tree.

VELA: The renovation is complete.

TAVI: No. The visit is.

[CUT TO TAG.]

### Tag

[THE TEAM SITS BESIDE THE TREE WITH A SIGN: "AFTER." TAVI ADDS A SECOND SIGN: "PLEASE DO NOT RENOVATE."]

PIRI: We can film the after shot.

TAVI: You can film the sign.

[SFX: A CAMERA SHUTTER. TAVI PECKS THE WORD "AFTER" OFF THE MODEL.]
