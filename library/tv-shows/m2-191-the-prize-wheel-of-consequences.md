# The Prize Wheel of Consequences

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime game show convention, where a prize wheel offers increasingly inconvenient rewards and a contestant cannot leave empty-handed*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A contestant wins a wheel spin, but each prize creates a new job before the next round.

Cast: Host Miri, a macaque; contestant Tavi, a capuchin; announcer Rell, a gibbon; and stagehand Seli, a lemur.

Episode: "Spin to Win"

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO, DAY

Rell spins the wheel. It stops between a basket of figs and a broom.

MIRI: The winner receives both!

TAVI: Do I get to choose?

The wheel wobbles and lands on "BROOM PLUS." The audience applauds.

ACT ONE

Miri explains the first game: answer a riddle, win a spin. Tavi solves it. Rell spins the wheel. Tavi wins a watering can. Seli hands it over. Water drips from the spout onto the studio floor.

MIRI: You have also won a cleanup task.

Tavi uses the broom to sweep the spill. The broom was the second prize. Miri calls it a bonus challenge. Tavi asks if he can keep the watering can. Seli says it belongs to the studio garden.

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO GARDEN SET, LATER

Tavi wins another spin and receives a potted plant. The plant is heavy. Rell helps carry it to the garden set. The pot is cracked, so soil spills across the floor. Tavi asks for a tray. The wheel spins on its own and stops at "TRAY." Seli gives him a tray.

Miri says the show has delivered exactly what Tavi needs. Tavi puts the plant on the tray and carries it outside the set. The audience sees a working flower bed beyond the painted backdrop. He asks why the set plant was indoors.

Seli says it was a prop. Tavi offers to plant it for real. Miri calls that the final prize.

ACT TWO-B

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO, LATE AFTERNOON

Miri introduces a team round. Tavi and Seli must carry the potted plant to the garden set without dropping soil. Rell spins the wheel for a route. It lands on "SCENIC WALK." The shortest path is straight across the studio, but the scenic route loops behind the audience and through the curtain.

Tavi carries the pot. Seli guides the leaves around the curtain. The plant catches on the fabric. Rell calls it a suspenseful obstacle. Miri tells him to lift the curtain rather than add another spin. He does. The team reaches the garden with the plant intact.

Seli shows the audience that the pot has a drainage hole. Water drips onto the set. The wheel spins again and lands on "MOP." The prize is a cleanup tool, not a prize at all. Tavi mops the floor while Seli puts the plant in its bed. Miri asks if they still want a wheel spin.

TAVI: Can the plant stay here?

The audience applauds. Miri says the plant has won a permanent place in the garden. Rell checks the wheel's rules. It says the contestant must keep any prize won. Seli points out the plant was already in the studio garden before the show. Rell spins anyway. The wheel stops on "TAKE IT HOME."

Tavi and Seli look at the plant. The audience groans. Rell looks at the small print and discovers the prize is a picture of the plant, not the plant itself. He hands them a postcard. They place it beside the real flower bed.

ACT TWO-C

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO, EVENING

Rell explains the last wheel prize: a mystery box. Tavi asks if it contains a prize or another task. Miri says the rules do not say. Seli spins the wheel, which lands on MYSTERY BOX. Rell opens the box and finds a note: "Please return this box to storage."

MIRI: The prize is responsibility.

Tavi carries the box backstage. Seli checks that the box is empty. It is not; there is a small loose screw inside. She hands it to the stagehand, who recognizes it from the wheel's base. He tightens the wheel. The wheel stops wobbling.

Rell spins it again to test the repair. It lands on "ONE MORE SPIN." The audience groans. Miri says the show has run long enough. Rell tries to spin anyway, but the repaired wheel stops cleanly on a blank space. The audience cheers.

ACT THREE



EXT. STUDIO GARDEN, EVENING

Tavi places the plant in a safe spot. Seli patches the pot and uses it for another seedling. Rell spins the wheel for the final prize. It lands on "A FRIENDLY GOODBYE." Miri says Tavi has won a farewell. Tavi asks if there is a prize to take home. The wheel wobbles again and lands on a small basket of figs.

MIRI: Now you can leave with something.

Tavi takes one fig and leaves the basket for the garden crew. He waves to the audience. Seli follows him with the broom.

TAG

INT. STUDIO, NIGHT

The crew resets the wheel. It spins and lands on "BROOM PLUS." Rell picks up the broom.

RELL: The house has won.

He sweeps the set while Miri reads the next contestant's name.
