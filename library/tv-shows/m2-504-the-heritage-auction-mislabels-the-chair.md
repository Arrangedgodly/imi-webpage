# The Heritage Auction Mislabels the Chair

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: auction broadcasts that assign dramatic provenance before checking the catalog card*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: auction host Rima, a macaque; cataloger Odo, a gibbon; bidder Fena, an orangutan; bidder Tavi, a lemur; producer Kalo, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - NIGHT

A plain wooden chair sits under a spotlight. The screen reads ROYAL THRONE, 1820.

RIMA: We open bidding at one hundred.

ODO: That is a dining chair from 1982.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Kalo tells the crew to keep the title card until the first bid. Odo checks the inventory sheet. The chair was donated by a community hall and used in the old meeting room. Rima asks whether a mayor once sat in it. Odo says probably; the hall had several mayors. Kalo asks him to say "historic seat."

ODO: It was a chair where people sat.

Tavi raises a paddle. The screen adds a gold crest. Fena asks what the chair is made of. Odo says pine and varnish. The camera zooms on a scratch near the back leg. Rima calls it a royal mark. Odo points out the scratch is shaped like a grocery cart.

ACT TWO

INT. CATALOG TABLE - LATER

Odo finds a photograph of the chair in the community hall. The photo shows a row of identical chairs. Kalo asks the graphics team to crop out the others. The picture becomes a close-up of the same scratch. Rima describes it as a hand-carved insignia. Fena says she has seen that mark on chairs at the library.

[The studio audience studies the image. Tavi raises his paddle to ask whether the chair folds. Rima mistakes it for a bid and raises the price.]

ODO: That was a question.

Rima asks the bidders to hold their paddles down. The timer continues counting upward. Kalo says the show needs urgency. Odo reads the catalog entry in full: pine chair, re-varnished, minor scratch, community hall donation. The screen changes to VINTAGE ROYAL CHAIR. Odo turns the monitor toward the camera and points to the actual description.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Fena asks whether the chair is safe to sit on. Odo checks the legs and says yes. Rima invites her to test it. Fena sits down. The chair is sturdy. Tavi sits in another identical chair beside her. The camera widens and reveals five matching chairs waiting for the next lots.

INT. CATALOG ROOM - LATER

Odo compares the chair's underside with the donation record. A paper tag is stapled beneath the seat, dated 1982. Kalo asks whether that is old enough to call rare. Odo says the date tells when it entered the hall, not who made it. Rima rehearses the corrected lot description. She calls it a wooden chair donated by the community hall. Tavi checks the matching chairs; each has the same mark from the cart. The camera records the tag and the plain legs. Rima asks the crew to remove the crown graphic before the lot opens. Kalo peels away the gold crest. The monitor still shows the chair's scratch in close-up, so he switches to the full item view. Odo places the donation record beside the seat, and the bidders can read it before Rima starts the auction. Tavi lowers his paddle until the description is finished.

ACT THREE

INT. AUCTION STUDIO - LATE NIGHT

Kalo removes the royal title card. Rima restarts the lot with the correct description. Bidding opens at ten. Tavi bids fifteen. Fena bids twenty. The timer counts down. The screen shows a picture of the community hall, not a crown. The audience recognizes the chairs and begins naming rooms where they have seen them.

Rima asks Odo whether any important person used the chair. Odo says the hall hosted committee meetings, school concerts and a weekly chess club. Fena bids thirty. Tavi bids thirty-five, then sits back down in his own chair. Rima waits. No one raises a paddle. The lot closes at thirty-five.

Fena asks if she can buy a second matching chair. Odo checks the inventory. There are four more. Kalo asks Rima to add a surprise premium for the set. Odo says the catalog lists them individually. Rima offers all five at the listed price per chair. Fena buys two for a reading group. Tavi buys one for his workshop. The screen displays the correct description.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE AUCTION

Odo peels the gold crest from the monitor. Kalo saves the graphic template under PLAIN FURNITURE. Rima sits in one of the remaining chairs and reads the next catalog card.

FINAL IMAGE: The community chairs line up beneath their accurate lot numbers, while the monitor shows the scratch labeled MINOR SURFACE MARK.
