# Life on the Lower Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: wildlife documentaries, hushed narration, and dramatic animal tracking*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A wildlife crew films a troop preparing for the rainy season, only to discover that the quiet narrator has been following the crew and not the animals.

Cast: Narrator Eron, a mandrill; field camera Lita, a lemur; sound recordist Piki, a capuchin; guide Boro, a gorilla; and young macaque Tavi.

## Episode: "The Patient Collector"

### Cold open

EXT. LOWER BRANCH, DAWN

[The camera glides across dew. Eron narrates in a whisper.]

ERON: At first light, the troop begins its long search for food.

LITA: We are filming the troop.

PIKI: The troop is filming us.

[Several macaques peer into the lens.]

ERON: The young macaque approaches cautiously.

TAVI: You have been following me all morning.

ERON: This is a rare moment of trust.

TAVI: I am looking at the camera.

ERON: The natural world offers no explanations.

Boro: The camera has a strap on it.

[The troop walks off. Eron continues whispering.]

### Act One

EXT. FIG GROVE, MORNING

[Lita sets the camera behind a fern. Piki places a microphone near a nest.]

ERON: We must remain unseen.

Boro: You are standing beside a bright orange equipment case.

ERON: It blends with the morning.

LITA: The case says "CREW."

ERON: It is a local word.

TAVI: The camera is pointed at the fig tree.

LITA: We are hoping to film the troop eating.

TAVI: We eat on the other side.

PIKI: Could you eat here?

TAVI: Why?

ERON: We need to observe behavior without influencing it.

TAVI: You just asked me to move lunch.

ERON: A natural conversation.

[Boro places a fig on the equipment case.]

PIKI: What are you doing?

BORO: The troop is hungry. The case is flat.

ERON: A dominant male claims the territory.

BORO: I am putting down a fig.

ERON: Territory is often established through food.

TAVI: He is sharing the fig.

ERON: Cooperation can also signal strength.

LITA: Eron, the camera is still on the case.

### Act Two

EXT. WATERING HOLLOW, AFTERNOON

[The crew follows a line of damp prints. Eron whispers as if narrating a chase.]

ERON: The tracks lead toward water. Our subjects move with instinctive precision.

PIKI: These are our boot prints.

LITA: I walked this way to test the lens.

ERON: The trail has become confused.

TAVI: You can hear the stream from here.

ERON: The troop detects danger.

BORO: The danger is the loose plank.

[The plank rocks. Eron freezes.]

ERON: The narrator becomes still.

LITA: You are on camera.

ERON: I must preserve the mystery.

[Boro steps over the plank. Tavi swings to the other side. Eron tries to follow and gets his tail caught in the microphone cord.]

PIKI: Hold still.

ERON: The narrator has been captured.

TAVI: By a cord.

ERON: A cunning snare.

PIKI: It's a microphone cable.

ERON: We should not name it. The scene is stronger without names.

[Boro frees Eron. The crew reaches the water hollow. The troop is grooming in the shade.]

LITA: This is the shot we wanted.

ERON: A family ritual begins.

TAVI: We are picking burrs from each other's fur.

ERON: A delicate act of care.

TAVI: You could join us.

ERON: I am observing.

BORO: You have burrs on your sleeve.

ERON: The forest has marked me.

### Act Three

EXT. EQUIPMENT CLEARING, EVENING

[The crew packs cases. The troop gathers around the fig Boro left earlier.]

ERON: As the sun lowers, the troop returns to its shelter.

TAVI: We are eating the fig.

ERON: A final meal before the night.

BORO: There are more figs on the tree.

ERON: A resource-rich habitat.

LITA: We have hours of footage. Which moment is the story?

ERON: The story is the troop's resilience.

PIKI: Or how we kept filming the wrong side of the branch.

ERON: The audience does not need to know.

TAVI: I watched the monitor. You filmed yourselves walking in circles.

LITA: We thought the marks belonged to an animal.

BORO: They were your prints.

ERON: A rare view of the camera crew's migratory path.

TAVI: You can use the footage if you show us eating.

ERON: We will show the troop in its natural habitat.

TAVI: And the crew?

ERON: We will show one brief glimpse.

[He looks toward the camera. Lita is reflected in the lens, holding a fig.]

LITA: That's not a glimpse. It's the whole camera.

ERON: The lens has become a mirror.

PIKI: We can end on the troop moving together.

BORO: They are already leaving.

ERON: The day concludes as the troop travels toward its shelter.

TAVI: We are going to the fig tree.

ERON: The habitat provides.

### Tag

EXT. FIG TREE, DUSK

[The troop swings away. The crew follows. Eron walks into a low branch and stops.]

ERON: The narrator has encountered an obstacle.

TAVI: The branch was there all day.

[The camera pans to the unbothered troop disappearing into leaves.]
