# The Parade of Lost Pets

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: morning news magazine convention, where a host treats a wandering tortoise as a breaking celebrity story*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A slow tortoise walks through a local news set, and the anchors keep interrupting each other to report its progress.

Cast: Anchor Piri, a macaque; cohost Vela, a gibbon; field reporter Odo, a baboon; and Temba, a tortoise.

Episode: "Crossing Live"

COLD OPEN

INT. LOCAL NEWS SET, MORNING

Piri reads the headlines. A tortoise appears behind him, crossing the studio floor.

PIRI: We have breaking movement.

Vela turns around. Temba continues toward the weather map.

VELA: He has entered the forecast.

The map falls over. Piri asks for a field report.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO HALLWAY, LATER

Odo crouches beside Temba with a microphone. He asks where the tortoise is going. Temba keeps moving. Odo walks alongside him, matching his pace.

ODO: We are following a developing story.

Vela gives a studio update: Temba has covered four floor tiles. Piri asks if he has reached the exit. Odo says the exit is twelve tiles away. The control room plays dramatic music. Temba stops beside a warm patch of light.

ACT TWO

INT. STUDIO, MIDMORNING

The crew lays a small path of leaves toward the door, hoping Temba will follow. Sela, the animal handler, says he should choose his own route. Piri asks if they can move the camera to keep him in frame. Vela shifts the news desk. Temba turns toward the new shadow of the desk.

Odo reports that the tortoise has changed direction. Piri asks if the desk is interfering. Vela says the desk has no comment. Temba resumes toward the exit. A cable lies across the floor. Sela moves it safely, and the camera operator lifts the stand.

The anchors discuss the journey's impact on studio traffic. A producer walks behind them carrying coffee and waits for Temba to pass. Piri calls it a congestion event. Vela says the studio has never seen such a slow commute.

ACT TWO-B

INT. STUDIO HALLWAY, LATE MORNING

Temba reaches the doorway, where a thick mat creates a small rise. Odo arrives with a ramp. The anchors ask him to hold it at an angle for the camera, but Sela says a shallow slope will be easier. They lay the ramp flat. Temba pauses, extends one foot and tests it. He crosses without help.

Piri narrates the crossing as a breakthrough. Vela points out Temba has already crossed similar ground twice. Piri says this is the first time in high definition. The camera operator changes lenses. Temba stops moving while the crew adjusts focus. Odo asks if the broadcast will wait.

ODO: He has not signed a release.

Sela says the tortoise is calm and not distressed, but the crew should not block his route. They move the lights and cables to the side. Vela asks whether the story can end once Temba reaches the door. Piri says they need one more live update.

EXT. STUDIO THRESHOLD, NOON

Temba approaches the outside mat. A breeze lifts it. Sela puts a small stone on each corner, leaving the walking path clear. Temba continues. Piri asks Odo if they should call this a finish line. Odo says it is a mat.

ACT TWO-C

INT. STUDIO, LATE MORNING

The anchors ask Sela to explain how a tortoise should be handled on a busy set. She demonstrates by clearing cables and moving the desk away from the doorway. Piri asks if the audience can see a close-up of Temba's shell. Sela says the shell is not the story; the safe route is.

Vela reviews the field notes. Temba crossed three floor tiles, rested in a warm patch and continued toward the open door. Odo corrects her: he also paused near the weather map because it shaded the floor. Piri asks if the map can be included in the recap. Vela says the map is not the location.

A production assistant brings a new headline: "Tortoise Escapes Studio." Sela reads it and crosses out "escapes." She writes "walks out with the door open." The wording fits the facts but will not fit on the banner. Piri says they can abbreviate it. Vela declines and uses a plain strip of paper.

ACT THREE




EXT. STUDIO DOOR, NOON

Temba reaches the door. Sela opens it and waits. He crosses the threshold onto a sunlit mat. Odo gives the final report.

ODO: The tortoise has exited the building.

Piri says the story is over. Vela asks if they should follow him outside. Sela says the tortoise is headed toward a quiet garden and the crew should leave him alone.

TAG

INT. STUDIO, AFTERNOON

Piri reads the next headline. A small snail moves across the weather map behind him. Vela looks at it.

VELA: We have a developing forecast.

Piri turns the map back upright.
