# The Ghost Is a Lighting Cue

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: paranormal investigation programs, night-vision reveals, dramatic equipment readings, and crews who frighten themselves with production gear*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A paranormal crew investigates a supposedly haunted theater, where every eerie signal comes from ordinary backstage equipment.

Cast: Investigator Vex, a macaque; sound technician Nari, an orangutan; skeptic Odo, a capuchin; theater caretaker Miri, a lemur; camera operator Tavi, a gibbon; and the stage manager, heard over radio.

## Episode: "The Cold Spot"

### Cold Open

[THEATER STAGE. A SPOTLIGHT TURNS ON BY ITSELF.]

VEX: We are not alone.

MIRI: That light is on a timer.

Vex turns to camera as the light turns off.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The crew enters the old theater after closing. Miri explains that the building is being checked for a loose cable before the school play. Vex says local reports describe a cold spot near the balcony. Odo says the balcony door does not seal. Nari checks her sound meter, which immediately beeps when Tavi walks past with his battery pack.

VEX: The device has detected a presence.

NARI: It detected Tavi's battery.

Tavi moves away. The meter stops. Vex asks him to walk back through the frame more slowly. Tavi does, and the meter beeps again. Vex calls the signal responsive. Odo points to the glowing battery indicator.

Miri takes them to the balcony. A draft moves the curtain, which brushes the microphone. Through headphones, the flutter sounds like a whisper. Vex asks the crew to enhance it. Nari says it is a curtain. Tavi plays the recording louder, and it sounds more like a curtain.

### Act Two

The investigators set up a night-vision camera. Nari warns that the automatic focus light may turn on. Vex begins a solemn question to the unseen presence. The focus light shines directly into Odo's face. Odo blinks and walks toward it, making the image look like a floating eye.

VEX: Did you see that?

ODO: I saw the camera.

The crew moves to the wings. A rope swings above the stage. Vex asks if a spirit is pulling it. Miri says the rope controls the curtain and the ventilation fan is on. She switches off the fan. The rope stops, but a sandbag settles with a thud. Vex jumps. Odo examines the pulley and finds the knot is loose.

The sound meter beeps again, this time near a prop cabinet. Nari opens it and finds a small radio used by the stage manager. It is receiving a cue from the booth. Miri switches it off. A faint voice says "places" from the speaker just before the battery dies. Vex stares into camera.

VEX: Something wants the theater to begin.

MIRI: It is the stage manager.

The crew hears three knocks from the wall. Odo taps back. The wall answers with two knocks. Miri explains that the school drum room is next door. Vex asks the drummer to repeat the pattern for verification. The drummer plays a whole practice phrase, rattling a stack of paint cans in the theater closet. The cans roll into the hall one by one, like slow, metallic footsteps.

Tavi follows them with the camera. Vex narrates each can as an approaching shadow. Nari stops the shot and labels the audio file "paint cans." Vex changes it to "possible footsteps, source unknown." Nari asks if he wants to inspect the closet. He says he wants to preserve the mystery. Odo opens the door; the last can is resting against a mop.

### Act Three

Before the final walk-through, Vex insists on one last equipment test. Nari sets the sound meter on the stage and asks everyone to remain still. It reads zero. Tavi whispers 'ghost' from behind the curtain, and the meter jumps because his belt radio is still on. Vex asks whether the reading could be emotional. Nari unclips the radio and places it on the table.

Miri leads the crew through a safety check: secure the pulley, close the balcony door, and tell the stage manager the radio is low on power. Nari records a clean reading with the battery pack outside the theater. The sound meter stays quiet. The cold spot disappears when the door is closed.

Vex asks whether anything unexplainable remains. The spotlight switches on. Everyone turns. Miri checks the timer; it is scheduled to light the stage for the caretaker's morning inspection. Vex finally admits the show has found a working theater, not a ghost. Odo asks whether they can still call the battery a presence. Nari holds up the meter; its screen is now blank.

### Tag

[CONTROL BOOTH.]

The crew signs off. The radio clicks once from the booth.

STAGE MANAGER (RADIO): Did anyone move my paint cans?

VEX: We have a new case.

Miri points at the camera and gestures that the cans are still in the hallway.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
