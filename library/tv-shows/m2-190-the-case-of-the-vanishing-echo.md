# The Case of the Vanishing Echo

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: detective serial convention, where investigators pursue a sound that only occurs when the investigator stops talking*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A village detective investigates an echo that disappears whenever the team tries to demonstrate it.

Cast: Detective Nara, a gibbon; partner Odo, a baboon; sound keeper Pavi, a capuchin; and witness Miri, a macaque.

Episode: "A Sound in the Hollow"

COLD OPEN

EXT. ROCKY HOLLOW, MORNING

Miri calls into a small hollow. Her voice returns a beat later. Nara arrives and asks her to repeat it. Miri calls. Nothing answers.

ODO: The suspect has gone silent.

A bird calls from the ridge. Its sound returns from the hollow.

ACT ONE

EXT. HOLLOW, LATER

Nara interviews Miri. She says the echo appears when she is alone. Odo asks if it has a name. Miri says she calls it "again." Pavi sets up a microphone and asks Nara to speak. The microphone catches her words but no echo.

NARA: It may be shy.

ODO: Or selective.

Pavi plays back the recording. The echo is there, faint beneath the bird's call. Nara asks him to isolate it. He removes the bird sound, then the wind, then Nara's question. The echo disappears from the track.

ACT TWO

EXT. RIDGE, AFTERNOON

The team tests different locations. Odo calls into a tunnel. His voice returns loudly. Nara records it as a second witness. Pavi says it is the same sound bouncing off a hard wall.

They move to the hollow. Miri stands aside while Nara calls. The echo answers. When Odo joins in, the sound changes. Pavi says their calls overlap. Nara asks Odo to step back. The echo returns, but only after Odo stops describing it.

ODO: I have found the pattern.

NARA: You were quiet.

A small monkey climbs the ridge and drops a seed. It clicks against the rock. The sound bounces across the hollow. Pavi follows the click to a narrow stone gap. The gap points toward a nearby cliff.

ACT TWO-B

INT. EVIDENCE ROOM, LATE AFTERNOON

Pavi compares the two recordings. The first includes the echo because the microphone faced the ridge. The second was pointed toward the hollow's side wall and caught only a direct voice. Nara draws the sound path on a paper map. Odo thinks the echo is moving, so he asks Pavi to play the clip while he walks around the room.

ODO: I can track it.

PAVI: You are tracking the speaker.

Odo circles the table anyway. The sound appears behind him each time he passes the speaker. Nara asks him to hold still. They play the recording once more. The echo comes after Miri's call, but before Odo's answer.

NARA: It is not choosing a witness. It is arriving late.

Miri remembers the hollow changes sound depending on where she stands. She takes them back to the ridge. Pavi marks the microphone direction with a strip of cloth. Nara stands on the original call spot and repeats the word "again." The echo returns from the cliff. Odo calls from beside her. His voice returns too, but overlaps hers.

ODO: We have two statements.

NARA: We have two voices.

A bird calls from the ridge again. Pavi records the sound without anyone speaking. The bird's echo is clear. Nara asks whether the bird can be added to the case file. Odo writes "uncooperative witness," then crosses it out and writes "natural cause." Pavi checks the recorder and sees the batteries are low. The crew decides to capture one final clean take before sunset.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. HOLLOW, LATE AFTERNOON

The crew sets a second microphone on a flat stone. Odo insists on standing behind it so he can monitor the suspect sound. Nara asks him to move to the call mark. He steps three paces to the left. Miri calls once. The echo returns. Odo starts to comment, but Pavi raises one finger. They wait in silence.

A bird lands on the ridge and calls again. Pavi records it. Odo stays quiet long enough to hear a second, softer echo from the opposite wall. Nara asks him what he heard.

ODO: A bird, then the hollow answering.

NARA: That's the whole case.

The recorder captures a leaf scraping against the microphone. Pavi checks the device and removes the leaf. Odo asks if it should be entered as evidence. Nara says it is part of the setting, not a suspect.

Pavi takes down the extra microphone, leaving the area undisturbed. Miri repeats her call once from the marked spot. The echo arrives and fades. Nobody speaks over it.

ACT THREE



EXT. HOLLOW, SUNSET

Nara reconstructs the sound path. The hollow reflects calls only when they come from one angle. Miri stands on the marked spot and calls. The echo returns. Odo calls from the wrong side and hears nothing.

NARA: The echo was never missing.

MIRI: It was waiting for the right question.

Pavi records the sound cleanly. Odo asks if they can replay it for the case file. Pavi presses play. The recording is silent because he muted the input channel.

TAG

EXT. VILLAGE LANE, NIGHT

Nara and Odo walk home. Odo calls, "Case closed." The hollow answers from behind them.

NARA: The case is still open.

Odo turns to speak, but the echo has already stopped.
