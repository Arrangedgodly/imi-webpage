# The Survival Show Builds a Shelter on Set

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival competitions that manufacture wilderness hardship from a carefully controlled studio set*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-017*

Cast: host Raku, a macaque; contestant Eni, an orangutan; contestant Falo, a gibbon; field producer Tesa, a lemur; set technician Voro, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. SOUNDSTAGE - DAY

Raku stands in a fake forest beside two contestants. Rain falls from a sprinkler. A studio light glows through the painted trees.

RAKU: You are alone in the wilderness.

[The stagehand with the sprinkler waves from a ladder.]

ACT ONE

INT. SOUNDSTAGE - CONTINUOUS

Tesa explains the challenge: build a shelter before sunset. The sun is a lighting unit on a track. Eni gathers branches from a prop bin. Falo finds a tarp folded beside the camera dolly. Raku asks them to survive without equipment. Voro wheels the tarp out of frame, then returns it when the sprinkler starts soaking the sound cables.

ENI: We could make a roof.

RAKU: First tell us how desperate you feel.

Eni says she is damp. Falo points to the exit sign. Tesa says the door is not part of the challenge. Raku asks the camera operator for a dramatic close-up of the contestants' hands. The camera frames a stagehand passing them a roll of gaffer tape.

ACT TWO

INT. SOUNDSTAGE - LATER

The contestants build a shelter from the prop branches. The branches are hollow foam and collapse when Falo leans one against another. Eni asks for cord. Tesa says they must find it. Voro brings a coil from the toolbox, then notices the label and takes it back. Raku narrates that supplies are scarce.

[The set's artificial wind machine turns on. It blows the painted leaves off a nearby tree. One leaf lands in Raku's hair.]

RAKU: The forest is testing us.

FALO: The fan is testing the set.

Eni uses two foam branches as a frame and ties them with the drawstring from her jacket. Falo holds the tarp over the top. It works until the sprinkler cycles. Water pools in the middle and drops on Raku's cue cards. He calls a storm warning. Voro switches off the sprinkler, but the rain continues on the other half of the stage. Tesa checks the controls; two zones are on different timers.

INT. PROP STORAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Tesa checks the shelter challenge list. The printed card says contestants must build with materials on the ground. Voro points to the prop bin and asks whether the ground includes the stage floor. Tesa crosses out the phrase and writes available set materials. Raku tries to add a lightning effect. The lighting operator asks for a cue number. Raku cannot find it in the rundown, so the operator keeps the sky calm. Eni and Falo test their roof from underneath; it holds without the fan.

INT. CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Raku asks the director to reset the weather. Tesa points out that the shelter is still standing. Voro gives the contestants a dry sandbag to weigh down the tarp. The camera catches him doing it. Raku tells him to stay out of frame. Voro exits, then the tarp lifts in the fan and settles over the camera monitor instead.

ACT THREE

INT. SOUNDSTAGE - AFTERNOON

The sunset light begins to move across the painted trees. Raku announces that the challenge ends when the sun disappears. Eni and Falo finish their shelter and invite the host inside. The roof is low. Raku tries to crouch but his microphone pack catches on a branch. He backs out, taking the tarp with him.

ENI: The shelter is for two contestants.

RAKU: The host is not a contestant.

Falo asks Voro to stop the fan. Voro switches it off. The set becomes quiet. The shelter stays upright. Tesa calls the challenge complete. Raku wants a final shot of hardship, so he sits outside in the sprinkler zone. The water starts again. The contestants remain dry under the tarp.

Raku asks who won. Tesa says both teams completed the build. He asks for a tiebreaker. Eni offers the host a towel; Falo offers the dry sandbag. Raku takes the towel and uses it to cover his cue cards. The cards are already soaked. The contestants sit inside the small shelter and wave at the camera as the sunset light clicks off.

TAG

INT. SOUNDSTAGE - AFTER THE SHOOT

Voro rolls the trees back to storage. The contestants fold the tarp for the next scene. Raku points to the stage door and asks whether it leads to a river. Tesa opens it; it leads to the loading dock.

FINAL IMAGE: The shelter stands under work lights, with the host's damp cue cards drying on the foam roof.
