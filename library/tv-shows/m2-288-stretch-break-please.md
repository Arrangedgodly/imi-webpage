# Stretch Break, Please

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: morning fitness television, where a host's increasingly complicated stretches require more floor space than the studio has*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: Fitness host Nara demonstrates a gentle stretch routine, but producer Odo keeps asking for a bigger movement and the camera crew runs out of room.

Cast: Host Nara, macaque; producer Odo, baboon; instructor Seli, capuchin; camera operator Piri, gibbon; studio assistant Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "Reach the Morning"

### Cold Open

[SMALL STUDIO. NARA RAISES ONE ARM.]

NARA: Stretch gently to the side.

ODO: Can you reach farther?

SELI: The wall is there.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[NARA STANDS ON A MAT.]

Nara leads a slow warm-up. Odo says viewers expect energy. Seli explains that a stretch should feel comfortable, not painful.

PIRI: The camera can zoom in.

ODO: A wide shot looks more athletic.

Tavi lays out a second mat. It covers the studio's path to the door.

NARA: We need room to move.

SELI: We also need a clear exit.

Odo asks Piri to move the camera. It bumps the light stand. The light flickers.

ODO: We have a dramatic effect.

NARA: We have a loose cable.

### Act Two

[THE CREW TRIES A BALANCE POSE.]

Seli lifts one foot and holds the pose. Odo asks her to extend both arms. She wobbles. Nara tells her to put her foot down.

ODO: The shot ended too soon.

SELI: I was losing balance.

PIRI: I can keep the camera on the mat.

Tavi tapes the cable along the wall. The camera angle improves, but now Odo stands in front of the mirror.

ODO: We need the mirror for depth.

NARA: We need the mirror for form.

Seli demonstrates a smaller stretch. The movement is clear and steady. Odo asks for a more challenging version. Nara says viewers can choose their own range.

PIRI: The broadcast has two levels.

TAVI: Gentle and optional.

[THE CREW CHECKS THE ROUTINE CARD.]

Piri reads the next movement: reach up, lower arms, rotate shoulders. Odo asks if they can add a side bend. Nara says the routine is for beginners.

ODO: We need a climax.

SELI: A stretch is not a race.

Tavi demonstrates the shoulder roll. It is small and controlled. Odo exaggerates the movement until his arms hit the light stand.

NARA: Keep your range comfortable.

ODO: The camera barely saw that.

PIRI: The camera saw your elbow.

Seli asks the crew to test the mat spacing. Each person takes one arm's length. The studio is too narrow for everyone to extend at once, so they arrange themselves in a staggered line. Odo wants the front position; Nara says it blocks the host.

TAVI: The back row can still be seen.

They shift Odo to the side. The light stand is now clear, and the routine can continue.

### Act Three

[THE HOST LEADS A FINAL ROUTINE.]

Nara asks everyone to stand with feet apart and reach only as far as comfortable. Odo follows, keeping his arm within range. Seli holds the pose. Piri frames the group without showing the cramped corners.

NARA: Breathe in, then slowly return.

ODO: That is the final movement?

NARA: It is the complete stretch.

Tavi checks the studio. The mats are clear of the door. The cable is taped down. The light stays steady.

Seli asks Odo to try the stretch once more without directing the camera.

ODO: I can do that.

He leans too far; Nara reminds him to ease back. Odo returns upright.

[THE PRODUCER INVITES VIEWERS TO FOLLOW ALONG.]

Nara demonstrates each movement slowly. Odo tries to mirror her, but keeps turning the wrong direction when she faces the camera. Seli moves behind him and gives a hand signal for left.

ODO: My left or the viewer's left?

NARA: Your left.

ODO: The camera reverses it.

PIRI: The camera does not reverse the movement.

Tavi turns the preview screen around. Odo sees himself and adjusts. The movement is now clearer, but he tries to keep watching the monitor while balancing.

NARA: Look at a steady point, not the screen.

ODO: The host is the steady point.

SELI: I am moving.

Odo stops. Nara asks everyone to lower their arms and reset. The team repeats the stretch without watching the preview. This time all four finish together.

Before the tag, Odo asks if the routine can be repeated tomorrow. Nara says yes, as long as the floor remains clear and the movements stay gentle.

SELI: We can keep the mat ready.

ODO: And move the camera back.

### Tag

[THE MUSIC FADES.]

Piri says the camera has plenty of room now. Odo asks if the next show can include a jump.

NARA: Only if the ceiling is higher.

TAVI: It is not.

[SFX: MAT ROLLS, THEME OUT.]
