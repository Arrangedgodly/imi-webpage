# The Surprise Guest Is the Host

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: reality competition convention, where a host pretends to be a mystery contestant and the crew must avoid revealing the obvious*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A host disguises herself as a contestant in a cooking challenge, but everyone recognizes her voice and production habits.

Cast: Host Miri, a macaque; producer Odo, a baboon; contestants Nara, a gibbon, and Piri, a capuchin; and camera operator Tavi, a squirrel monkey.

Episode: "Who Is Under the Apron?"

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING TENT, DAY

A contestant enters wearing a large apron and a leaf mask. Odo whispers to the camera.

ODO: A mystery guest has joined the competition.

The masked contestant says, "Welcome back to the show." Everyone looks at Miri.

ACT ONE

Odo introduces a surprise recipe: fruit skewers. The mystery contestant rearranges the prep station exactly as Miri does during every episode. Nara asks if the guest has hosted before. The contestant says no, then reminds everyone to look at the sponsor board.

Piri says Miri's voice is unmistakable. Odo insists the identity must remain secret until judging. Tavi frames the apron from the waist down. The contestant uses a secret host hand signal to ask for more time. Nara notices and points to the clock.

ACT TWO

INT. COOKING TENT, LATER

The competitors assemble their skewers. Miri's mask slips, revealing one eye. She turns away from the camera. Piri asks if the mystery guest can taste the fruit. Miri says the host cannot taste during a challenge. Odo says this is a rule for contestants, not hosts.

Nara creates a pattern with alternating colors. Piri uses a single fruit on each stick. Miri builds a tower and calls it a vertical garden. The tower leans. She steadies it with a napkin from the sponsor table.

Tavi pans to Odo, who is trying to signal the crew to keep the mask on. Nara reads the label inside the apron; it says "Miri." Odo claims the apron belongs to someone with the same name.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COOKING TENT, AFTERNOON

The judges ask the contestants to prepare a second dish using the same ingredients. Miri says the surprise contestant should go first. Odo says the mystery guest cannot receive a special advantage. Miri's apron pocket contains a host cue card, which falls onto the floor.

Tavi reads it aloud: "Ask whether the dish needs more color." Miri tries to take it back. Nara says her skewer needs color, not another question. She adds one bright berry between the pale fruit. Piri stacks fruit in a stable row and ties the skewers with leaf strips. Miri builds another tower, but the napkin support slides.

The contestants ask for equal time. Odo stops the clock while Piri retrieves the fallen napkin. Miri says hosts should not pause the clock for their own mistakes. The crew restarts the timer and clears the station. The contestants finish with their own designs.

Piri's skewer is simple and easy to carry. Nara's pattern is bright and balanced. Miri's tower has collapsed into a fruit bowl. She tastes it and admits it is easier to eat now.

MIRI: I have made a salad.

TAVI: It is still on the challenge table.

Odo asks if the host can compete. Miri says she should return to hosting. She removes the apron and joins the judges' table, leaving the contestants to present their plates.

ACT TWO-C

INT. CONTROL BOOTH, LATE AFTERNOON

The crew reviews footage. Tavi shows Miri how her host hand signal appeared in every shot. Odo asks if they should hide the cue card. Miri says they should show it as part of the challenge. The contestants watch the replay and laugh when the mask slips.

Nara asks if the show can let contestants choose their own plating style. Odo updates the rule card: presentation is scored for stability, clarity and edible fruit, not for copying the host. Piri signs it. Miri reads the new rule aloud and places it beside the clock.

ACT TWO-D

INT. COOKING TENT, EVENING

Miri takes the host microphone back. She says the judges will score the dish, not the mask. Nara and Piri each present their finished skewers. The host's cue card remains on the floor where the camera can see it.

ACT THREE



The judges taste each skewer. Miri is disqualified for receiving a host hint when the clock was paused. She removes the mask and admits she wanted to know what contestants experience. Nara wins the round with a sturdy skewer that can be carried without falling apart.

TAG

Miri returns to the host station. She asks Odo to reveal the surprise guest. Odo points to the apron on the chair. It waves in the fan.

MIRI: The mystery is still under wraps.

The camera shows the name label.
