# The Review Needs a Charger

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: television product reviews, unboxing rituals, sponsor disclaimers, and rating charts with too many categories*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-013*

Logline: A tech-review host unboxes a portable lantern but cannot test its battery because the show forgot to charge it.

Cast: Reviewer Dena, a gibbon; cohost Pavi, a macaque; producer Odo, a baboon; test editor Liko, a capuchin; sponsor voice, an orangutan.

COLD OPEN

INT. REVIEW STUDIO - DAY

Dena opens a box and lifts a portable lantern. She presses the power button. Nothing happens.

DENA: It is portable.

PAVI: It is not on.

ODO: We can test the packaging.

ACT ONE

Odo reads the sponsor disclaimer. The lantern was supplied for review; the channel keeps its score independent. Pavi asks whether it came with a cable. Dena finds one in the box, but no adapter. Liko checks the studio drawer. It contains three adapters that fit none of the ports.

PAVI: We have reviewed accessories before.

DENA: We should review this one after charging it.

Odo says the battery indicator may show charge if held for five seconds. Dena holds the button. A small red light flashes, then disappears. Pavi asks if that counts as a feature. Dena says it counts as a red light.

ACT TWO

The crew places the lantern on the table and reviews its exterior. Dena checks the handle, rubber base and printed instructions. Pavi opens the safety leaflet and reads that the lantern should be charged before first use. Odo asks if they can rate the leaflet.

PAVI: It is folded into eight panels.

DENA: The diagrams are clear.

PAVI: Score: six for clarity, two for folding difficulty.

The sponsor voice interrupts with a prerecorded message claiming the lantern has "up to twelve hours" of battery life. Dena asks whether that depends on brightness. The message does not say. Odo tries to replay it, but presses the studio's applause button instead. The audience claps for the claim.

ACT TWO-B

Liko finds the correct adapter. The lantern charges for fifteen minutes during a station break. When the show resumes, Dena powers it on. It glows brightly, then dims after a minute. Pavi checks the setting; it is on low. Dena switches to high. The light lasts for another moment, then goes out.

PAVI: Our studio may be too bright to see the beam.

DENA: We can test it in the dark booth.

They move to a small blackout tent. Dena powers on the lantern. It illuminates the instructions, a chair, and Odo's snack. Odo reaches into the light and takes a cracker. The lantern dims again.

PAVI: We have measured one minute of light.

DENA: After fifteen minutes of charging. We cannot claim a full battery test.

Liko finds that the adapter has the wrong voltage. The proper adapter is still in the box, nested under the folded cardboard insert. Odo says the insert was part of the unboxing. Dena points to the label: "CHARGER INCLUDED."

Pavi turns over the rating chart. It has separate boxes for brightness, runtime, portability, style, and "unboxing delight."

PAVI: We can score portability. It carried one cracker.

DENA: We can score the handle. The battery is still untested.

Liko finds a small note on the package: the twelve-hour claim applies at the lowest setting with a full charge. Odo says the studio tested it at high brightness after fifteen minutes. Dena crosses out the provisional runtime box rather than writing a number.

ODO: The sponsor wants a score tonight.

DENA: The sponsor supplied a lantern, not a stopwatch.

Pavi tries to carry the unlit lantern through the blackout tent to see whether the handle catches. It swings and taps the tent frame. Liko checks the hook, then adds "handle: secure" to the chart. The lantern battery remains unscored.

PAVI: Could we give it a point for arriving in a box?

DENA: The box is not the product.

ACT THREE

The review team reconnects the correct charger and leaves the lantern to charge overnight off-camera. Dena gives a provisional score for build and controls, but marks battery life as untested. Pavi asks if they should hold the review until the full test. Dena says yes. Odo looks at the broadcast schedule; there is no segment ready for the empty slot.

PAVI: We can review the box again.

DENA: We already reviewed the box.

Liko records the lantern's serial number and labels it "TEST IN PROGRESS." The sponsor message plays one more time. Odo presses the applause button by mistake. Pavi switches it off before the audience reacts.

TAG

The next morning, Dena returns with the fully charged lantern. She turns it on in the blackout tent and starts a timed test. The clock begins. She does not announce a score yet.

FINAL IMAGE: The lantern stays lit as the camera pulls back to reveal the review team quietly timing it with a kitchen timer.
