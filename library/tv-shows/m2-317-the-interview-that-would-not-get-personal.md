# The Interview That Would Not Get Personal

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: late-night talk shows, celebrity anecdotes, audience reaction shots, and hosts who keep adding props when a guest gives a direct answer*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A late-night host tries to turn a practical guest's repair story into a confession, while the guest keeps repairing the set.

Cast: Host Palla, a gibbon; guest Miro, an orangutan clockmaker; bandleader Zuzu, a macaque; floor director Kett, a capuchin; and audience plant Vex, a ring-tailed lemur.

## Episode: "Tell Us More"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. PALLA SITS BEHIND A DESK. THE DESK CLOCK IS LOUD.]

PALLA: Tonight, a guest with a story that will make you laugh, cry, and check the time.

MIRO: The clock is running four minutes fast.

Palla glances at camera. Kett silently holds up a card reading EMOTION.

PALLA: And we are already on a journey.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

Miro arrives carrying a small clock and a toolbox. He has come to explain how he repaired the bell at the neighborhood library. Palla asks for the funniest part. Miro says a moth had nested in the bell housing and the repair was simple. Palla waits for a childhood memory. Miro opens his toolbox and removes a tiny screwdriver.

MIRO: The moth was not a metaphor.

Zuzu plays a soft musical sting. The audience applauds. Miro looks under his chair; a screw has fallen out of the set. He picks it up and places it beside his tools.

Palla says every clock has a secret. Miro says some clocks have dust. Palla asks whether his father taught him to fix them. Miro replies that his aunt taught him and corrects the host's cue card, which says "father's watch." Kett changes cards.

PALLA: We love a twist.

MIRO: It is the same story with the right relative.

### Act Two

Palla asks for a visual aid and produces a large cardboard clock. Miro points out the hands are glued in place. Palla asks him to demonstrate the repair on it anyway. He removes the hands, aligns the minute hand, and discovers the cardboard face is upside down. The audience gasps on cue.

Kett cues Vex to laugh. Vex laughs one beat late, then continues through a serious answer. Palla tries a different angle: was there a moment when Miro nearly gave up? Miro says the library door was locked, so he came back the next day. Palla leans toward the camera.

PALLA: The door was closed on him.

MIRO: It was closed on everyone. It was a library.

Zuzu improvises a suspenseful bass line. Miro hears another loose screw rattling in the desk and asks Palla to hold the lamp. He opens a panel. Inside is a pile of host cue cards, a wrapped sandwich, and a spring that has been bouncing against the wood. Palla calls it a hidden chamber. Miro removes the spring and returns the sandwich to her.

Palla asks Miro to tell the story as if it changed his life. Miro thinks. He says the library bell now rings on time, and a child stopped missing story hour. Palla nods, then prompts him to cry. Miro studies the studio clock.

MIRO: The child was happy. I was paid in tea.

PALLA: Can the tea be symbolic?

MIRO: It was hot.

The camera moves to a tight close-up. Miro gently turns it away from his face so the viewers can see his hands repairing the desk. He shows Palla how to tighten the loose hinge. The desk stops squeaking. The audience tries to applaud quietly because the host is leaning on the desk.

### Act Three

Palla asks for one final surprise. Miro winds the repaired studio clock. It begins chiming at the correct time, interrupting the band. Zuzu stops playing, then joins the chime with a tiny keyboard. Palla tries to talk over them, but each time she begins, a clock chimes on a different wall.

Kett checks the schedule and signals the show is out of time. Palla asks Miro for a last line. He says the show can end when the clock says so. The clock chimes. The band hits a celebratory chord. Vex's audience laugh starts on time for once.

PALLA: Our guest fixed the desk, the clock, and my interview.

MIRO: I tightened one hinge.

Palla presents him with a souvenir mug. Its handle wobbles. Miro takes out the screwdriver and tightens it before accepting.

### Tag

[AFTER THE SHOW.]

Palla attempts to tell the mug's moving story to camera. The handle stays firmly in place. She looks at Miro, who has already packed his tools.

PALLA: Could we get one more emotional take?

MIRO: You can have the mug.

Zuzu plays the clock chime. The camera holds on Palla's silent reaction until she finally laughs at herself.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
