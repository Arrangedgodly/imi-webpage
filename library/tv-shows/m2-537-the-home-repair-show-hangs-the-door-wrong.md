# The Home Repair Show Hangs the Door on the Wrong Side

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: renovation broadcasts that unveil a room before testing whether its new fixtures work*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Kalo, a capuchin; carpenter Dena, an orangutan; homeowner Miri, a macaque; designer Pavo, a gibbon; producer Leka, a lemur.

COLD OPEN

INT. DEMO HOUSE - DAY

Kalo pulls a new door open. It swings into the sink and stops.

KALO: A beautiful transformation.

MIRI: I cannot get into the kitchen.

ACT ONE

INT. DEMO HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Leka says the reveal has been filmed already. Dena points out that the door hinges are on the wrong side. Pavo says the design plan called for a left-hand swing. Miri opens the old floor plan; the sink was moved after the plan was printed.

Kalo asks her to pretend she is surprised. Miri tries to walk through the doorway. The door hits the sink again. Dena props it open, but now it blocks the refrigerator. Leka asks for a close-up of the brass handle. The camera records the handle while Miri waits in the hall.

ACT TWO

INT. WORKROOM - LATER

Dena checks the frame and measures the available clearance. The door can swing outward, but it would block the narrow hall. Miri asks whether they can move the hinges to the other side. Pavo offers a sliding door. The track would cross the cabinet lights. Leka says the reveal montage already shows brass hardware and a dramatic swing.

Kalo reads the plan's room label: "Sink - north wall." The sink is on the south wall now. Pavo turns the page upside down, hoping to match it. It aligns for the pantry and fails at the window. Miri points out that the plan is from before the kitchen was rebuilt.

INT. DEMO HOUSE - MOMENTS LATER

Dena removes the door from its hinges and places it on padded blocks. Pavo holds the swing diagram next to the actual frame. Leka asks them to reinstall it for another reveal. Dena says the frame needs a new hinge plate on the opposite jamb. Miri marks the clear path from doorway to sink with masking tape. Kalo follows the tape, steps backward and lands in the camera light.

INT. HALLWAY - MOMENTS LATER

Miri walks the marked path with a box of dishes. Pavo holds the new plan against the wall. Leka asks her to pause for a sponsor shot, but the box is heavy. Dena stops the take so Miri can set it down. Kalo tries the doorway himself, carrying a stack of folded towels. The door clears the stack and opens fully. Leka asks whether he can repeat the walk for a reveal; Dena says the first test showed the clearance. Miri lifts her box again and returns to the kitchen.


ACT THREE

INT. DEMO HOUSE - AFTERNOON

Dena fits the hinge plate on the opposite jamb. Miri checks the hall clearance while the door is still supported on blocks. They lift it back into place and test the swing once. It opens into the empty wall without blocking the sink or refrigerator. Miri walks through carrying a basket; the door stays clear.

Leka asks for a grand reveal with the old montage. Kalo plays it on the monitor: the door swings into the sink, then a title card says DREAM KITCHEN. Pavo pauses the footage. Miri points to the collision. Leka deletes the old reveal and records a new take. Kalo opens the door once, holds it clear, and lets Miri carry the basket through. Dena shows the relocated hinge plate to the camera. Pavo updates the plan with the new sink location and the door swing. The old diagram no longer matches, so Leka crosses out its title card.

The crew checks the walking path from the hall to the counter. The door does not hit the light, the sink or the basket. Kalo asks Miri for a surprised reaction. She says she is pleased that the door opens. Pavo removes the staged brass close-up from the edit. Dena packs the old hinge screws into a labeled envelope. Leka rewinds the new footage and watches the doorway stay clear. This time she lets the shot run through Miri's entrance instead of cutting away to the handle. Kalo announces the room is ready to use, not merely ready to photograph. Miri hangs her keys on the hook beside the door.

TAG

INT. WORKROOM - AFTER THE SHOW

Dena sets the old plan aside. Pavo pins the updated drawing beside it. Leka keeps the collision footage in the blooper reel.

FINAL IMAGE: Miri opens the door with one hand and carries the basket through with the other, while the obsolete plan points its arrow at the wrong wall.
