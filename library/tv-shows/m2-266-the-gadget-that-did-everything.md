# The Gadget That Did Everything

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: daytime technology demo television, where a host pitches a multi-use gadget that is less useful than its separate tools*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Host Seli unveils a multipurpose branch tool that promises to sweep, stir, hook, and measure, but every function interferes with the next.

Cast: Host Seli, capuchin; inventor Odo, baboon; tester Nara, macaque; caller Pava, gibbon; floor assistant Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "One Tool, Four Jobs"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO TABLE. A BRANCH WITH A SPOON TIED TO ONE END.]

SELI: This tool can do four jobs.

NARA: It is a branch with a spoon.

ODO: Exactly.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[SELI HOLDS THE TOOL BESIDE A DISPLAY.]

Seli shows the "BranchMate," designed to sweep crumbs, stir a pot, hook a basket, and measure a shelf. Odo says it is lightweight and handmade.

NARA: What is the measurement scale?

ODO: The branch is one branch long.

TAVI: That is not a scale.

Pava calls in and asks whether the spoon can be removed for washing. Odo says the knots are permanent.

PAVA: Then how do you clean it?

ODO: You rinse the whole tool.

NARA: The branch gets wet.

SELI: It is designed for outdoor use.

TAVI: We are in a studio.

### Act Two

[THE FIRST DEMONSTRATION: SWEEPING.]

Nara sweeps crumbs from the table. The spoon end catches the cloth and pulls it off the table.

[SFX: CLOTH FALLS.]

SELI: The tool has cleared the surface.

NARA: The cloth was on the surface.

Odo demonstrates stirring a pot. The spoon is too small to reach the bottom, and the branch taps the rim.

ODO: It is stirring and keeping time.

Seli moves to the hook demonstration. The branch reaches a basket on a high shelf, but the spoon catches the handle and tips the basket over. Tavi catches the basket.

TAVI: We have one function left.

SELI: Measuring.

Odo lays the branch beside a shelf. It is longer than the shelf, so he bends it. The branch snaps.

[PAVA ASKS FOR A LIVE CALL-IN TEST.]

She wants to know whether the BranchMate can reach a cup on the top shelf. Odo uses the hook end, but the spoon slides inside the cup and makes it difficult to lift.

PAVA: I can hear it clinking.

SELI: The tool is making a sound.

NARA: The cup is not moving.

Odo adjusts his grip. The branch bends, the spoon slips free, and the cup stays on the shelf. He tries the other end. It hooks the cup handle and brings it down, but the spoon taps the glass.

[SFX: CUP CLINK.]

PAVA: It worked.

NARA: It almost dropped the cup.

ODO: Almost is not dropped.

Tavi asks them to try the tool on a lower shelf. Nara hooks the basket neatly, but the spoon catches a loose ribbon tied to its handle. The basket is tangled.

NARA: One tool has found another job.

SELI: Untangling.

ODO: We did not promise that.

Tavi frees the ribbon by hand.
### Act Three

[THE CREW EXAMINES THE BROKEN TOOL.]

Nara asks which function failed. Odo says the sweep worked if the cloth is removed, the spoon can stir a small cup, the hook works if the basket is light, and the measurement is approximate.

SELI: It does four jobs.

NARA: None of them well.

Pava calls back and asks if the tool comes with a warranty. Odo says the branch is replaceable.

PAVA: The spoon is tied on.

TAVI: The branch is split.

Odo unties the spoon. It works normally. Seli asks whether the show can sell the spoon separately.

ODO: The spoon came from the kitchen.

NARA: Then it belongs in the kitchen.

Seli holds the remaining branch and tries to sweep with it. The crumbs move in a small pile.

[THE CREW CHECKS THE TOOL'S PACKAGE.]

Tavi finds the box it came in. The label says "For indoor and outdoor use," but the pictures show separate tools: a broom, spoon, hook, and ruler.

TAVI: These are four different tools.

SELI: The BranchMate combines them.

NARA: It also combines their problems.

Odo puts the pieces back into the box. The spoon fits in the kitchen drawer, the branch in the garden bin, and the string in the repair basket.

ODO: We have restored the tools.

PAVA: The show has one product left.

SELI: A drawer.

### Tag

[THE TOOL IS DISASSEMBLED ON THE TABLE.]

Seli places the spoon in a cup, the branch in the garden bin, and the string in a drawer.

SELI: We have four useful objects.

NARA: They are not one tool.

ODO: They are one demonstration.

PAVA: Will there be another version?

SELI: Only if we find a better branch.

[SFX: CUP SET DOWN, THEME OUT.]
