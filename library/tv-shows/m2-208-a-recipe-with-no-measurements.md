# A Recipe with No Measurements

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cooking show convention, where the host demands exact quantities for a dish made by observation and practiced hands*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A cooking host tries to standardize a fruit mash recipe that the elders make by feel, while the television cards keep blowing away.

Cast: Host Sela, a lemur; cook Odo, a baboon; assistant Miri, a macaque; and card holder Pavi, a capuchin.

Episode: "A Pinch and a Pour"

COLD OPEN

INT. COOKING HUT, DAY

Sela holds a recipe card. Odo puts figs into a bowl by hand.

SELA: How many figs?

ODO: Enough for the bowl.

A breeze lifts the card and it sticks to the wet bowl.

ACT ONE

Sela asks Odo to repeat each step using cups and spoons. Odo mashes the figs, then adds water until the mixture loosens. Miri measures the water. Pavi holds the card away from the breeze.

PAVI: The card says one half cup.

ODO: It depends on how ripe the figs are.

Sela says television viewers need numbers. Odo shows her two batches: ripe fruit needs less water; dry fruit needs more. Miri writes both instructions on the card.

ACT TWO

The crew makes a third batch. Sela follows the card exactly. The mash is too runny. Odo adds more fruit. The camera operator asks if they can hide the correction. Sela says the recipe should explain it. Pavi rewrites the card: begin with a small amount, then add until it holds a spoon.

Miri tastes the mixture and suggests a pinch of salt. Odo adds it. Sela asks for a measurement. Miri shows the size of a pinch with two fingers. Pavi writes "small pinch" on the card.

ACT TWO-B

INT. COOKING HUT, AFTERNOON

Sela asks Odo to demonstrate the recipe for a second camera. He begins by choosing figs. The camera assistant holds up a card that says "three figs." Odo picks three small fruits, but the ripe ones make more mash than the dry ones. Miri weighs the bowl. The batch is still thicker than the first.

ODO: The fruit size changes the result.

PAVI: We can list a range.

They repeat the recipe with larger figs. Pavi writes "three to five, depending on size." Sela asks if a viewer can tell when enough water has been added. Odo tips the bowl. The mash slides slowly, then settles. Miri says that is the right texture. Pavi sketches the movement on the card.

Sela wants a measurement for the salt. Miri places a pinch between her fingers, but some grains fall onto the counter. Odo sweeps them back into the bowl. Pavi writes "a little, then taste." Sela asks if tasting should happen before or after the salt. Odo says both.

The crew films the mixing close-up. The bowl turns as Odo stirs. Piri reaches in to hold it steady. Odo asks him to use a damp towel underneath, so the bowl will not slide. Sela notes that the practical tip is more useful than the camera angle.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. STUDIO GARDEN, LATE AFTERNOON

The crew tests the recipe outside. The fruit is less ripe than the indoor batch. Odo mashes it with a smooth stone. Miri adds water one spoon at a time. Pavi records the amounts, but the mix is still stiff. Sela asks them to check the texture rather than keep adding water.

Odo folds the mash over itself. It loosens. Miri tastes it and asks for one more pinch of salt. Pavi writes the adjustment. A young monkey asks whether the recipe can be made without salt. Odo says yes; the flavor will be different. Sela adds an optional note instead of changing the main method.

The card blows off the table. Miri catches it before it lands in the garden bed. They weight it with a clean stone and keep filming. Odo shows how to rinse the mixing bowl before the fruit dries to the sides. Piri asks if cleanup can be a final segment. Sela says the kitchen is already ready for the next cook.

ACT TWO-D

INT. COOKING HUT, EVENING

Pavi checks the recipe card against the final bowl. He reads the steps aloud while Odo demonstrates. Miri confirms the texture, and Sela signs off on the optional salt note. The card now gives cues for ripeness, water and taste.

ACT THREE



Sela presents the final recipe. Odo makes a bowl without measuring cups. Miri reads the updated card aloud. The recipe now includes signs to watch for, not just numbers. Sela says the show has learned to cook with the ingredients it has.

TAG

The crew finishes the dish. Pavi asks if the recipe card can be laminated. The bowl tips and covers it with mash. Odo wipes the card clean with a leaf.

ODO: The recipe is still readable.

SELA: And seasoned.
