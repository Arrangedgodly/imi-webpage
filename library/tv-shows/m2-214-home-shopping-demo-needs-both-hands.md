# The Home Shopping Demo Needs Both Hands

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live shopping television that keeps adding product claims while the demonstration becomes harder to perform*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A host demonstrates a clever basket that folds flat, but the sales script keeps adding accessories until it cannot fold.

Cast: Host Mira, a capuchin; product maker Tavi, a macaque; floor director Rell, a gibbon; and caller Omi, a baboon.

Episode: "The Basket That Goes Flat"

COLD OPEN

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - MORNING

Mira holds a folding basket. Tavi presses two clips. The basket folds flat.

MIRA: One basket, two clips, and no struggle.

RELL: You have twelve seconds left in the segment.

TAVI: It folds like this.

[THE BASKET LIES FLAT.]

MIRA: That's the whole demonstration.

RELL: The script has four benefits left.

ACT ONE

Mira opens the basket and sets it on the table. Tavi places a rolled scarf inside.

MIRA: It carries groceries, laundry, or yarn.

TAVI: It folds flat when empty.

RELL: Could we say it folds flat with the scarf inside?

TAVI: It does not.

MIRA: It folds flat after you take things out.

[RELL HANDS HER A PRODUCT CARD. IT ADDS: "INCLUDES HANDLE WRAP."]

MIRA: The handle wrap is a separate accessory.

RELL: The card says included.

TAVI: That is a printing error.

MIRA: We will not say it is included.

ACT TWO

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - LATER

The basket sits beside three accessories: a lid, a liner, and a carrying strap. Rell clips each onto it as Mira speaks.

MIRA: The optional lid keeps light items inside.

TAVI: The liner is washable.

RELL: The strap makes it hands-free.

MIRA: The basket is no longer empty.

TAVI: It cannot fold with those attached.

[RELL TRIES TO FOLD IT. THE STRAP TANGLES WITH THE LID.]

MIRA: We should remove the accessories first.

RELL: The script says one-motion fold.

TAVI: The basket folds in one motion. The accessories do not.

[THE PHONE LINE LIGHTS UP.]

MIRA: We have a caller.

OMI (ON PHONE): Does it fold flat with the lid on?

MIRA: No. You remove the lid, then fold it.

RELL: The card says one motion.

OMI (ON PHONE): That is two motions.

MIRA: Correct.

ACT TWO-C

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - LATE MORNING

The basket sits on a turntable. Rell holds up a card. Tavi checks each clip by hand.

RELL: We have a viewer question. How much weight can it hold?

TAVI: We tested it with a bag of oranges, up to twelve pounds.

MIRA: The card says twenty.

TAVI: The card is wrong. Twelve is the tested amount.

RELL: We can say "strong enough for everyday use."

MIRA: That is true if we say the tested weight.

[RELL PUTS A HEAVY PROP ROCK IN THE BASKET. THE HANDLE STRETCHES. TAVI REMOVES THE ROCK.]

TAVI: The basket is not designed for rocks.

MIRA: It is designed for groceries and laundry.

RELL: The rock is in the demonstration kit.

TAVI: Then it should be in the storage bin.

[TAVI FOLDS THE EMPTY BASKET FLAT. IT FITS UNDER THE TABLE. RELL TRIES TO PUT THE PROP ROCK ON TOP. THE TABLE WOBBLES.]

MIRA: We have a separate table problem.

ACT THREE

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - NOON

Omi calls back. Mira answers on speaker. Rell stands behind a row of accessories.

OMI (ON PHONE): If the liner is wet, can I fold it inside the basket?

TAVI: Let the liner dry first. Then fold it separately.

RELL: The product page says "folds as one."

MIRA: We can correct the page.

OMI (ON PHONE): I would rather know that before ordering.

TAVI: That is fair.

RELL: Do we still have a special offer?

MIRA: The basket and the optional liner are separate prices.

[RELL REMOVES THE COMBINED PRICE SIGN. UNDER IT IS A SMALL CARD SHOWING EACH PRICE CLEARLY.]

OMI (ON PHONE): That makes sense.

MIRA: We can show the actual items without stacking them.

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO - FINAL DEMO

Tavi removes the lid and strap. Mira folds the basket flat in one clean motion.

MIRA: Here is the basket with no accessories. It folds flat.

TAVI: Here is the basket with the liner. It still folds if you take the liner out first.

RELL: Can we mention the carrying strap?

MIRA: It helps carry the open basket.

[RELL PUTS THE STRAP ON HIS SHOULDER. THE BASKET SWINGS AND BUMPS THE PRODUCT CARD OFF ITS STAND.]

OMI (ON PHONE): Does it come with the card?

TAVI: The card is not included.

MIRA: The basket is.

TAG

Mira puts the folded basket on a shelf. Rell tries to add the lid, liner, and strap to the bundle.

TAVI: Keep the accessories separate.

RELL: The offer looks smaller.

MIRA: The truth fits in the basket.

[THE BASKET REMAINS FOLDED. THE ACCESSORIES SIT BESIDE IT.]
