# Last Troop Standing

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival reality competition convention*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Premise: On a remote island, contestants compete to build a safe camp while producers keep trying to turn sensible cooperation into a rivalry.

Cast: Vale, a calm langur arborist who reads ground before climbing; Saff, a competitive tamarin baker who hates damp firewood; Omi, a retired orangutan ferry captain with a bad knee; Jun, a young macaque park ranger who knows the island's tide pools; Producer Fenn, an off-camera voice who keeps asking for a dramatic choice.

## Episode: "The Dry Side"

### Cold open

EXT. ISLAND BEACH, MORNING

The four contestants stand beside their packs. A crew member hands them a map and points at a hilltop. Producer Fenn calls that the best place for camp.

Jun studies the sand, then watches the waves lick a line of driftwood.

JUN: The tide will reach the hill's foot before dinner.

FENN: Can you say that while looking concerned?

Omi sits on a rock and tests his knee.

OMI: I am concerned about lunch.

### Act one

The contestants' first task is to find a dry place and build a shelter before the next tide. Fenn announces a twist: only one team can win the tarp. The four look at each other. Vale says they can split into groups and compare sites before using anything. Fenn asks if they are forming an alliance. Saff says they are forming a roof.

Vale checks the ridge for loose branches and finds a stand of young trees whose roots are shallow. She rejects it. Jun notices a low rock shelf above the high-water line, sheltered from wind by a grove of palms. Omi says it will hold a shelter if they leave a channel for rain runoff. He draws the channel in wet sand with a shell.

Saff scouts for dry fuel. Most branches are soaked. She finds a fallen palm frond under an overhang and uses its dry center to catch a spark. She is good with fire, but her hands shake from hunger. She admits she skipped breakfast to keep her pack light. Jun gives her half a dried fruit packet. Saff accepts it and stops pretending she is fine.

The producers place a sealed box at the ridge. Fenn says it contains a reward for the contestant who reaches it first. Jun points out the path is steep and slick. Vale says they should finish the shelter before anyone climbs. Fenn asks if Vale is willing to risk losing the reward. Vale says she is willing to avoid carrying an injured person down the hill.

### Act two

The rain begins. The contestants build a lean-to over the rock shelf. Omi shows them how to angle the palm fronds so water runs into the channel instead of under the bedding. Vale ties the supports with cord from her pack. Saff starts the fire beneath the overhang but leaves it too close to the dry fronds. Smoke collects under the roof.

Jun notices a gust pushing smoke back into the shelter. He opens a gap on the windward side. The fire settles. Fenn asks Saff whether she will accept a penalty for needing help. She says she would accept a penalty for burning down the only dry place on the island. Fenn asks her to repeat that with more suspense.

A camera operator slips on the hill path while moving toward the reward box. Omi and Vale help him back to level ground. The crew's medic checks his ankle. Producer Fenn asks the contestants if the delay has changed their strategy. Omi says yes, they now have a medic's boot print to avoid.

When the rain eases, Jun checks the tide pools. He finds small shellfish but warns the group not to eat the bright ones. Saff spots a crab near the rocks and wants to reach for it. Jun stops her. He shows the pincers and explains that the crab is guarding a burrow. They gather edible mussels instead, taking only a small number from each patch.

A second challenge card arrives. It offers a dry blanket to the first person to bring back three marked shells. Vale reads the tide chart and says the marked shells sit below the water line. Omi says no prize is worth entering a pool at a rising tide. Saff asks if the blanket can be used by all four. The card says it belongs to one contestant.

### Act three

The group decides not to enter the pool. They send Jun to explain the tide to the crew, while Saff and Vale finish cooking and Omi keeps the fire covered. Fenn protests that no one has taken the challenge. Jun replies that the challenge was to make a choice, and they did.

The production team checks the shells and confirms the marked area is unsafe. The blanket is brought to camp anyway, and the rules are changed so the group can use it as a shared dry layer. Fenn records a short interview asking whether the contestants trust each other. Saff says trust is easier when nobody is making her prove it by getting wet.

They eat the mussels and a little flatbread Saff cooked on a heated stone. Omi gives the first piece to the camera operator, whose ankle is wrapped. Vale asks the crew to move their equipment away from the rain channel. The camera operator thanks her and says the new angle catches the whole camp.

At dusk, the tide reaches the ridge exactly where Jun predicted. The shelter stays dry. The contestants mark the safe route with driftwood and leave the beach clearer than they found it. Fenn reads the next day's instruction: the winner will be the person who can demonstrate a solo shelter. The four ask whether one person may watch the others. Fenn says that is allowed. Saff says good, because she has several notes about his knots.

### Tag

At dawn, Producer Fenn stands under the shelter with a mug. A drop of water lands on his clipboard. He looks up. The roof holds. Omi points to a small hole in the far corner, nowhere near the camp beds.

OMI: That is where the suspense goes.

The crew laughs. Fenn asks for a take without the laugh. The contestants hand him a palm frond and show him how to patch it.
