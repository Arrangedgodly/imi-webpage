# Buy It Before It Grows

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live home-shopping television, limited-time offers, and improbable product demonstrations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: A home-shopping host demonstrates a self-growing branch hook, but the hook sprouts faster than the crew can remove its props.

Cast: Host Gela, a macaque; product inventor Bunni, a capuchin; caller Toma, a gibbon; floor director Voss, a gorilla; and camera operator Liri, a lemur.

## Episode: "The Hook That Hooks"

### Cold open

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO, DAY

[A small wooden hook sits beside a phone bank. A new green shoot curls around the display.]

GELA: Welcome to Canopy Finds. Today we offer the branch hook that grows with your needs.

BUNNI: It attaches to any tree.

VOSS: It is already attached to the table.

BUNNI: That is a demonstration.

LIRI: The hook is growing.

GELA: It is flourishing.

[The shoot wraps around the price card.]

GELA: The offer is available for a limited time.

VOSS: The card is unreadable.

GELA: Then the time is more limited.

### Act One

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO

[Bunni shows a diagram of the hook. The branch has added two leaves since the intro.]

BUNNI: The hook starts small and grows as you hang more items.

GELA: What can it hold?

BUNNI: A hat, a satchel, a small basket, or a light branch.

VOSS: Can it hold the camera?

BUNNI: The camera is not on the list.

GELA: Call now and receive a second hook.

VOSS: We have one hook.

GELA: The second hook is a promise.

LIRI: The first hook has wrapped around the microphone stand.

GELA: That gives you hands-free sound.

BUNNI: It is a natural design.

VOSS: We need to demonstrate the hat.

[Gela hangs a hat on the hook. The branch bends. The hat swings in a circle.]

GELA: Look at that elegant rotation.

BUNNI: The hook is stretching.

VOSS: The hook is pulling the table.

GELA: The set is mobile.

[The table begins to move toward the phone bank.]

LIRI: Camera two, follow the table.

GELA: You are watching our exclusive rolling demonstration.

### Act Two

INT. PHONE BANK

[Toma calls in. Voss answers and hands Gela a note.]

GELA: We have Toma on the line. What do you think of the hook?

TOMA: I bought one last week.

GELA: And how is it working?

TOMA: It is growing through my scarf.

BUNNI: That means it has found a secure attachment.

TOMA: I want to take the scarf off.

GELA: You can return it.

TOMA: The hook has grown through the return label.

VOSS: Can you bring it here?

TOMA: It has reached my neighbor's tree.

GELA: The product is expanding your storage space.

BUNNI: That is not the intended use.

VOSS: We should cut the offer.

GELA: The offer is still live.

LIRI: The hook has reached the camera cable.

BUNNI: It grows toward support.

VOSS: It is pulling the camera.

GELA: We will take a quick sponsor break.

[The camera tilts. The hook's new leaves fill the frame.]

TOMA: I can still hear you.

GELA: Stay on the line.

TOMA: I am holding on to the scarf.

BUNNI: Do not pull. The branch may bend.

VOSS: Or the camera may fall.

GELA: This is why we include a care card.

VOSS: The care card is the price card.

GELA: It says "water weekly."

BUNNI: It should say "trim regularly."

### Live caller

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO

[The phone rings again. Voss answers and listens.]

VOSS: The caller says the hook is growing toward the window.

GELA: Is there a window?

VOSS: Yes.

BUNNI: It needs light.

GELA: Our product has found a sunny location.

VOSS: It is blocking the fire exit.

BUNNI: It is not supposed to grow indoors.

GELA: The care card says "water weekly."

VOSS: There is a watering can beside it.

[The branch reaches toward the can.]

GELA: It has recognized its care partner.

VOSS: I am moving the can outside.

### Act Three

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO, LATER

[Voss uses garden shears to snip the branch from the set. The studio table is free. Gela holds the small wooden base.]

GELA: The hook can be returned to its original size.

BUNNI: It can be trimmed.

VOSS: And it cannot be connected to a microphone, camera, or scarf.

GELA: Those are now included in the warning.

LIRI: Toma's hook is free.

TOMA: It is not free. It has my scarf.

BUNNI: We will send a pruning kit.

TOMA: Please send scissors first.

GELA: We have one final offer. Buy the branch hook and receive a pair of pruning shears.

VOSS: Do the shears grow?

BUNNI: No.

GELA: They are a limited model.

TOMA: I will take the shears.

GELA: We have a confirmed order.

VOSS: You have a caller asking for scissors.

GELA: A very valuable caller.

[The hook sprouts one new leaf from its wooden base.]

BUNNI: It is still growing.

VOSS: We should move the set.

GELA: The show must go on.

VOSS: The table has roots now.

GELA: Then the table is a showroom.

### Tag

INT. SHOPPING STUDIO, EVENING

[The crew lifts the price card. A vine has grown through it.]

LIRI: It says "call now."

GELA: Then now is a good time.

[The phone rings. Voss unplugs it. The table rings too.]
