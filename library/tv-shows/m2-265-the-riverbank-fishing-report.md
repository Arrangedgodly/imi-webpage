# The Riverbank Fishing Report

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: fishing television, where hosts celebrate a catch but the actual fish is always released*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-009*

Premise: Host Odo presents a fishing report with expert Pava, but every cast lands in the reeds and the only successful catch is an old boot.

Cast: Host Odo, baboon; expert Pava, gibbon; angler Nara, macaque; camera operator Seli, capuchin; park guide Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "The Big One"

### Cold Open

[RIVERBANK. NARA HOLDS A BOOT ON A LINE.]

ODO: We have a catch.

NARA: It is not a fish.

PAVA: It is old.

TAVI: Please do not put it back in the river.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[ODO STANDS IN FRONT OF A RIVER MAP.]

Odo promises a report on the best fishing spot. Pava explains that fish gather in calmer water near the reeds. Nara says she knows the spot, but the camera crew wants a wide shot from the bridge.

SELI: The bridge gives us a view of the whole river.

NARA: It gives us a view of the current.

ODO: The current is part of the report.

Nara casts toward the calm pool. Her line catches a reed. Odo narrates the snag as "a powerful resistance."

NARA: The reed is holding the hook.

PAVA: Let the line go slack.

Nara does. The line frees itself. Odo asks if she will cast again.

NARA: I will try from the bank.

### Act Two

[THE CREW MOVES DOWNSTREAM.]

Tavi warns them not to step on the soft bank. Odo says they need to get close to the water for a dramatic angle. Seli sets the camera farther back.

SELI: We can see the pool without standing in it.

Pava shows Nara how to keep the hook away from the reeds. Nara casts, and the float moves with the current.

ODO: The fish is taking the bait.

PAVA: That is the current.

NARA: The float is moving downstream.

Odo asks for a close-up. Seli zooms in, but the camera loses focus. The float catches on a branch and stops.

ODO: The fish has stopped.

PAVA: The float is caught.

Nara reels it in carefully. A leaf is attached to the hook.

NARA: The river has sent us a sample.

Tavi asks them to keep the bank clean. Odo says the leaf is a prop now.

[PAVA SHOWS THE CREW HOW TO RELEASE THE SMALL FISH NARA HAS JUST LANDED.]

The fish must stay in the water as much as possible. Nara wets her hands before lifting it. Odo asks her to pose for a still photograph.

NARA: I need to return it.

SELI: We have the shot.

PAVA: Keep the fish level. Do not squeeze it.

Odo holds the camera steady. Nara lowers the fish into the water; it swims away.

ODO: It has escaped.

PAVA: It has been released.

Tavi asks Odo to collect the line and hook before they leave. The hook catches on the cloth of his sleeve. Nara helps free it.

NARA: The gear needs to be stored safely.

ODO: We have another rescue.

SELI: The hook is out.

PAVA: The report should include that.

### Act Three

[THE CREW TRIES ONE LAST CAST.]

Nara casts into the open pool. The float sinks slightly, then rises. She reels in a small fish, holds it briefly for the camera, then returns it to the water.

[SFX: WATER SPLASH.]

ODO: A successful catch.

NARA: A safe release.

PAVA: The fish was not injured.

SELI: We have the shot.

Odo asks whether he can hold the old boot for the closing. Tavi says it belongs in the cleanup bag, not on camera. Odo asks if the boot has a name.

TAVI: It is a boot.

NARA: It has no partner.

PAVA: Perhaps it has traveled alone.

[THE CREW PACKS ITS EQUIPMENT.]

Pava checks that the fish has moved into the current. Nara reels in the empty line and removes the leaf from the hook.

NARA: The pool is clear.

ODO: The episode has one catch.

PAVA: And one release.

Tavi collects the boot in a separate bag so it can be disposed of properly. Odo asks if they can keep it as a trophy.

TAVI: It is litter.

SELI: The riverbank is cleaner than when we arrived.

NARA: That is the best result.

Odo turns to camera for the closing. Behind him, the float drifts past with another leaf.

ODO: Another catch?

PAVA: Just a leaf.

### Tag

[THE CREW PACKS UP.]

Odo reads the day's report: two fish caught and released, one leaf recovered, one boot removed from the bank.

ODO: A very successful morning.

NARA: The fish is still in the river.

TAVI: And the boot is in the cleanup bag.

SELI: The river is cleaner.

[SFX: FLOAT SPLASHES ON WATER, THEME OUT.]
