# The Wildlife Documentary Team Loses Its Narrator

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: wildlife documentary voiceover conventions, dangerous close-ups, and a narrator whose script belongs to a different animal*
*By: IMI TV Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-020*

Cast: Narrator Vex, an orangutan; field director Mira, a macaque; sound recordist Odo, a gibbon; camera operator Nessa, a capuchin; habitat keeper Rafi, a baboon.

COLD OPEN

EXT. WOODLAND BLIND - DAWN

The camera sees a small animal move through leaves. Vex begins in a grand whisper.

VEX: Here, the mountain tiger stalks its prey.

Rafi whispers through the intercom.

RAFI: That's a squirrel.

CUT TO TITLE.

ACT ONE

The documentary team is filming the sanctuary's spring wildlife special. Vex has received a narration card labeled TIGER, while Mira's camera shows the woodland squirrel enclosure. Mira pauses the take and asks Vex to read the animal's actual identification sign. Nessa keeps the camera behind the blind; the team is not allowed to cross the habitat fence.

MIRA: What does your card say?

VEX: It says, "The mountain tiger moves silently through its range."

ODO: The animal in frame is making a great deal of noise.

SFX: Leaves rustle, then a squirrel chatters.

VEX: The tiger is warning us.

RAFI: The squirrel is warning another squirrel that you have described it incorrectly.

Nessa zooms out. A red-tailed squirrel sits on a stump beside a feeder. Vex flips the card over and sees the correct card beneath it. The editor stacked the narration cards in reverse order.

VEX: Then the tiger card belongs to the next segment.

MIRA: The next segment is a moth.

Nessa keeps filming. The squirrel grabs a seed, holds it with both paws, and attempts to bury it in the wooden platform. The platform is not soil. The seed bounces into the feeder tray.

VEX: A clever hunter stores its prize.

MIRA: It is a squirrel caching food.

VEX: The line still works.

ODO: It does not. A moth does not hunt like a tiger, and this squirrel is not a tiger.

ACT TWO

Mira asks Odo for a clean field recording without narration. He moves the mic toward the blind window, but a cable catches on the tripod leg. Nessa stops the camera before the tripod shifts. Odo routes the cable around the leg and tests the microphone from the safe side of the barrier.

ODO: Cable clear. I can hear the squirrel chewing.

MIRA: Keep the commentary off until we confirm the sound.

VEX: The sound is a twig snapping.

RAFI: It is the feeder lid. The squirrel is pushing it open.

SFX: Feeder lid taps; seed rattles inside.

Nessa records the full action. The squirrel pushes the lid, takes one seed, and retreats behind the stump. Another squirrel appears and takes the next seed. They do not fight. Vex watches the footage and wants to describe the second animal as a rival.

VEX: The rival challenges the first for control of the food supply.

RAFI: They take turns. One waits behind the stump.

MIRA: We have footage of the waiting animal. No challenge, no chase.

VEX: Can I call it a tense negotiation?

MIRA: You can call it two squirrels using one feeder.

Odo plays the raw sound. The feeder lid and chewing are clear, but the script's dramatic tiger growl sits on a separate narration track. Mira asks him to mute that track rather than trying to cover it with bird sounds.

ODO: Field audio is clean. Narration is isolated.

Nessa checks the camera slate and confirms the shot belongs to the squirrel enclosure, not the nearby nocturnal house. The editor's card for the moth had been placed on top of this clip.

Nessa reads the enclosure slate aloud and photographs it for the edit log. Rafi checks the next habitat sign. It says MOTH HOUSE - CLOSED UNTIL DUSK. No animal from that room is in the morning footage.

ACT THREE

Vex records a new narration card after Mira verifies each detail. The team shows the squirrel using the feeder, then the second animal taking a seed after the first leaves. The edit keeps the real chewing sound and removes the tiger growl. Nessa labels the clip with the species and enclosure time.

VEX: The red-tailed squirrel stores a seed beneath the feeder platform, retrieves it when the lid moves, and leaves room for another squirrel.

MIRA: That's what we saw.

VEX: It is less dramatic than the tiger.

RAFI: The squirrel has not requested a more dramatic narrator.

The crew records one final wide shot from behind the blind. The squirrel moves off under the leaves, and the feeder lid settles without another animal entering frame.

FINAL IMAGE: The narration card marked TIGER is turned face down while the squirrel clip plays with its own sound.
