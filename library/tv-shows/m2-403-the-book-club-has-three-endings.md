# The Book Club Has Three Endings

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised book clubs, spoiler warnings, quote cards, and panelists who summarize different editions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-014*

Logline: A television book club argues over how a novel ends, until the host discovers the panelists were given three different editions.

Cast: Host Nera, a macaque; critic Odo, a baboon; librarian Tavi, an orangutan; reader Piri, a capuchin; producer Velo, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. BOOK CLUB SET - NIGHT

Nera faces three panelists and a screen reading "SPOILERS AFTER THIS POINT." Odo holds up a closed book.

ODO: The ending is a ferry crossing.

TAVI: Mine ends at a hill.

PIRI: My copy has no ending.

Nera looks at Velo. He checks the clock and starts the theme music.

ACT ONE

The book is "Map of the Quiet Orchard," a fictional novel about a cartographer who leaves the capital. Nera asks each panelist to describe the final choice. Odo says the cartographer boards a ferry. Tavi says the cartographer climbs the hill and returns the map. Piri says the last chapter stops mid-sentence.

NERA: We have three readings of one book.

VELO: That is a strong panel topic.

TAVI: It may also be three books.

Nera asks the camera to show each cover. Odo's is a paperback, Tavi's is a library edition with a blue dust jacket, and Piri's is a large-print version missing its final pages. Velo says they all have the same title.

ACT TWO

Odo opens to the last page. Nera reads the quote card: "She stepped aboard, carrying the map." Tavi says that sentence appears halfway through her copy. Nera looks at the page number and sees it is chapter nine, not the ending. Odo's book has the same page numbering, but a different chapter order.

PIRI: My print stops at chapter eight.

NERA: Why did we invite you to discuss the ending?

PIRI: You invited me to discuss the book.

Velo starts a viewer poll: "Which ending did you get?" The screen offers ferry, hill and unfinished. Votes come in for all three. Nera asks whether the publisher issued alternate endings. Tavi says one library copy has pages bound out of order. Odo flips his book; chapter nine is followed by the ferry scene, then the hill scene.

ODO: Perhaps both are intended.

TAVI: The hill chapter has a page number that goes backward.

Nera asks for the quote cards. Velo brings three cards, each with a different final sentence. Odo reads the ferry line first. Tavi objects that it belongs after the hill chapter. The screen puts the ferry card before the hill card. Nera walks to the monitor and moves them into the order Tavi describes.

NERA: We have been showing the ending before the decision.

VELO: It makes a better teaser.

PIRI: It made a different story.

Odo asks the library for its catalog record by phone. Tavi reads the call number from her copy. The librarian confirms it is the regular edition, with the pages bound out of order. Odo turns his book around and follows the chapter numbers instead of the page tabs.

ODO: The plot is going forward.

TAVI: The pages are going backward.

The studio audience chuckles. Velo flips the quote cards face down until the panel is ready to discuss the correct sequence.

ACT TWO-B

Tavi compares the signatures on the pages. She discovers the ferry scene is chapter eleven, the hill scene is chapter ten, and Odo's copy had them bound in reverse order. Piri's copy is missing the last two chapters entirely. Nera asks Velo to find the publisher's reading guide. He brings a card with the correct order and a quote from the closing paragraph.

NERA: We have a sequence.

PIRI: Does the cartographer take the ferry?

TAVI: After climbing the hill.

Odo reads the chapter transition aloud. The cartographer uses the map to find the ferry landing, then decides to return to the orchard by boat. The panelists agree the ferry is the actual ending, but the hill changes how the journey feels. Nera asks the audience to vote again; the poll still says "unfinished" for Piri's edition.

PIRI: That is accurate.

ACT THREE

Nera gives one final summary: the character climbs the hill, finds the route, and takes the ferry back. Tavi says the order matters. Odo apologizes for presenting his misplaced pages as an intentional ending. Piri borrows the complete library copy so she can read the missing chapters.

VELO: We have resolved the ending.

NERA: And identified a binding problem.

The panel closes with each guest holding the book open to the correct page. Odo takes the quote card down. Nera removes the spoiler warning from the screen after the summary is complete.

TAG

Velo hands Nera a script with its pages out of order. She turns to the last page and finds the opening greeting.

NERA: We have another mystery.

FINAL IMAGE: The panelists trade copies, each checking the page numbers before the camera cuts away.
