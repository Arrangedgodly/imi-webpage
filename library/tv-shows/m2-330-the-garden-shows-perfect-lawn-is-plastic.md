# The Garden Show's Perfect Lawn Is Plastic

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: garden makeover television, instant lawn reveals, expert tips delivered over time-lapse footage, and hosts who treat neatness as the only goal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: A garden show tries to replace a neighborhood's messy pollinator patch with perfect turf, until the camera crew discovers the lawn they filmed is artificial.

Cast: Host Miri, a gibbon; gardener Omi, an orangutan; landscape designer Vex, a macaque; camera operator Fenn, a capuchin; and neighbor Nari, a lemur.

## Episode: "A Neater Yard"

### Cold Open

[BACKYARD. A ROLL OF GREEN TURF IS UNFURLED.]

MIRI: In one afternoon, this garden will be transformed.

NARI: That is plastic grass.

Vex pats the roll. It squeaks.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

The crew arrives to film a makeover of Omi's backyard. Omi wants a path through the garden so he can reach his vegetables without stepping on seedlings. Vex proposes a neat square lawn and a row of identical shrubs. Nari, a neighbor who volunteers with the community pollinator group, says the existing flowers attract bees and butterflies. Miri asks whether there is room for both a path and the flowers.

Omi points out the muddy shortcut he takes to the vegetable beds. Vex marks a straight paved path across the flower patch. Omi asks to curve it around the blooms. Vex says a curve is difficult to show in a before-and-after split screen. Fenn measures the route and suggests stepping stones. He sets a camera dolly on the path; the wheels sink into the mud.

VEX: The ground is part of the challenge.

OMI: The ground is why I asked for a path.

### Act Two

The show uses time-lapse footage to make the work look immediate. Fenn films the crew placing one stone, then Vex asks him to speed the footage until the path appears in seconds. The stones skip across the image. In real time, Omi checks that they are level. Nari adds low flowers around the edges, leaving a clear route to the vegetables.

Vex rolls out the plastic lawn. It covers the muddy corner but also blocks a drain. Water from the hose pools on top. Miri steps onto it; the turf squeaks under her feet. The camera captures her startled expression. Vex calls it a spontaneous reaction shot. Nari lifts the edge and finds the drain. Omi asks for a section of real gravel so water can flow through.

The crew removes the roll. Underneath, several tiny flowers have been flattened. Nari and Omi prop them upright, then add mulch. Vex asks whether the makeover still has a dramatic reveal. Miri says the path and healthy beds are the reveal. Vex arranges three potted shrubs in a line for the camera. One pot blocks the gate. Omi moves it to the corner, where it still gets sunlight.

The producers film an expert tip about watering. Vex tells Omi to water every plant on the same schedule. Omi explains that the vegetables and flowers need different amounts. Nari points to a small rain barrel beside the shed. Fenn asks her to repeat the advice while the camera rolls. Nari gives one clear instruction, then notices a bee landing on the flowers. The camera follows the bee, leaving Vex speaking to an empty frame.

MIRI: The visitor has chosen the garden.

VEX: Can we get a close-up of the host?

Fenn moves the camera back to Miri. The bee remains on the flower beside her, visible in the shot. Miri says they can keep filming without disturbing it. Vex tries to cue music for the reveal, but the speaker plays a lawn-mower sound effect from a previous episode.

### Act Three

Omi walks the curved stepping-stone path to the vegetable beds. The stones stay level. Nari points out that flowers remain on both sides, and the drain is clear. The garden is tidy where it needs to be and lively where it helps the plants. Vex looks for the plastic lawn roll. It is stored behind the shed, labeled "set dressing." He admits that it was never a real garden solution.

The crew shoots a before-and-after comparison. The old shot shows the muddy shortcut; the new shot shows Omi walking over the stones. Vex asks him to pause at the end and look amazed. Omi reaches the beds and picks a ripe tomato. Miri keeps the camera on him instead of switching to Vex's prepared reveal card.

Vex wheels the plastic lawn roll into frame for one last green background. The camera dolly catches its loose edge. As Fenn pushes, the roll unwinds behind him, laying a bright artificial strip over the path and around Omi's ankles. The squeak gives Vex the dramatic sound cue he wanted. Omi steps free without crushing a flower, takes the roll, and winds it back up. Fenn guides the dolly after the retreating loose end, then sets its wheel brake beside the shed.

MIRI: That is the fastest installation we have filmed.

NARI: And the shortest lawn.

Vex looks at the shot monitor. The frame shows Omi holding the roll, the clear path, and the real flowers. He reaches for the plastic again, then sees the bee cross the lens and leaves the camera wide. The final image is Omi carrying his tomato through the garden while Fenn holds the rolled turf far from the drain.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
