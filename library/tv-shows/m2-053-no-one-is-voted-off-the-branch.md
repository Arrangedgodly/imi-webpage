# No One Is Voted Off the Branch

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: survival reality competitions, dramatic confessionals, and surprise eliminations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-002*

Premise: Contestants on a wilderness competition learn to build shelter, find water, and endure a surprise elimination ceremony that no one can remember agreeing to.

Cast: Host Karo, a mandrill; contestant Beni, a gibbon; contestant Lusa, a macaque; contestant Rix, a capuchin; and field medic Omi, an orangutan.

## Episode: "The Shelter Challenge"

### Cold open

EXT. COMPETITION CLEARING, DAWN

[A camera drone circles a group of contestants. Karo holds a wooden bowl filled with smooth stones.]

KARO: Today, one contestant will leave the branch.

BENI: Which branch?

KARO: The competition branch.

LUSA: Where is it?

KARO: We are standing on it.

RIX: This is the ground.

OMI: It is a clearing.

KARO: A dramatic clearing.

[The bowl tips. All the stones roll away.]

LUSA: The elimination has begun.

KARO: We will retrieve the stones after the challenge.

### Act One

EXT. SHELTER SITE

[Karo points to three bundles of vines and leaves.]

KARO: Your task is to build a shelter before sunset.

RIX: Can we use the trees?

KARO: The trees are part of the environment.

OMI: They are also trees.

BENI: I can weave the vines between two trunks.

LUSA: I can make a roof from broad leaves.

RIX: I will make a door.

KARO: A door is not required.

RIX: A shelter with a door is a home.

KARO: Home is not the objective.

LUSA: What is the objective?

KARO: Survive the night.

OMI: We have blankets.

KARO: The blankets are for the crew.

[The contestants build a low shelter. Beni ties a crossing vine. Lusa layers leaves. Rix sets a door frame against the opening.]

KARO: Rix is building an unnecessary entrance.

RIX: I am building privacy.

BENI: The roof is slipping.

LUSA: The leaves are too dry.

OMI: I brought water.

KARO: The water is for the challenge.

OMI: It is for the contestants.

KARO: The challenge is for the contestants.

OMI: Then give it to them.

### Act Two

EXT. SHELTER SITE, AFTERNOON

[A sudden gust blows the leaf roof off. The cameras pan dramatically. Karo looks concerned.]

KARO: The shelter has suffered a setback.

BENI: The wind moved the roof.

LUSA: We can lower it and tie the corners.

RIX: The door is still standing.

KARO: The door is alone.

RIX: It is doing its best.

OMI: The sky is clear now.

KARO: The weather can change.

LUSA: The weather does not watch the show.

KARO: We do not know that.

[They lower the roof. The door frame holds the ridge. The shelter takes shape.]

BENI: The frame is steady.

RIX: The door is helping.

KARO: You may have to choose between comfort and safety.

OMI: We have built both.

KARO: The rules say each team may use three bundles.

LUSA: We used three.

KARO: The door uses a fourth vine.

RIX: The vine was on the ground.

KARO: The ground is a resource.

BENI: Then the door is made from the ground.

KARO: I will review the footage.

LUSA: We have until sunset.

KARO: The sunset is already underway.

[The sun lowers behind the camera rig.]

OMI: That is a studio light.

KARO: The light is part of the environment.

### Act Three

EXT. ELIMINATION CIRCLE, NIGHT

[The contestants sit by the shelter. Karo returns with the stone bowl, now empty.]

KARO: It is time for the elimination.

BENI: Who is leaving?

KARO: The contestant with the fewest stones.

LUSA: We never voted.

KARO: The cameras recorded you looking at the bowl.

RIX: I was looking at the stones on the ground.

OMI: We picked them up.

KARO: We found no votes.

BENI: Then nobody has the fewest.

KARO: That is technically correct.

LUSA: Does that mean we all stay?

KARO: We can do a tie-breaker.

OMI: The challenge is over.

KARO: We have a dramatic schedule.

RIX: The shelter is ready. We can sleep.

[They enter the shelter. The roof holds. The door swings shut gently.]

BENI: It works.

LUSA: The wind is outside.

RIX: Privacy.

KARO: The crew cannot film inside.

OMI: Then the episode is over.

KARO: The audience still needs a result.

BENI: The result is a shelter.

KARO: The winner is the shelter.

LUSA: Can the shelter take home the prize?

KARO: It is already home.

### Confessional

INT. SMALL TENT

[Beni sits before a camera. The tent is so small that the lens touches his nose.]

BENI: I am confident in the shelter.

KARO: The roof has one loose edge.

BENI: The roof has many edges. One is loose.

KARO: Are you worried?

BENI: I am worried about the camera in the shelter.

[The camera slowly backs out. Its strap catches the door frame.]

KARO: The camera is leaving the shelter.

BENI: It has been eliminated.

[The camera comes back in, still recording.]

KARO: The camera has returned.

BENI: The branch is a forgiving place.

### Tag

EXT. CLEARING, DAWN

[Karo sits alone beside the stone bowl. A camera points at him. The contestants sleep under the leaf roof.]

KARO: The host has been eliminated by the schedule.

[He picks up a stone. It rolls away.]
