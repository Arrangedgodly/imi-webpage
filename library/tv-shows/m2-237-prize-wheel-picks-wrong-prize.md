# The Prize Wheel Picks the Wrong Prize

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: game shows that put a spinning wheel between a contestant and the answer they already know*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-008*

Premise: A contestant answers every trivia question correctly, but the prize wheel keeps awarding increasingly inconvenient objects.

Cast: Host Pavo, a macaque; contestant Runi, a gibbon; contestant Neri, a capuchin; and floor manager Doma, a gorilla.

Episode: "Spin for Knowledge"

COLD OPEN

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - NIGHT

Pavo stands beside an oversized prize wheel. Runi faces him. A card reads "Name a mammal that lays eggs."

RUNI: The platypus.

PAVO: Correct. Spin for your prize.

[THE WHEEL TURNS AND LANDS ON "PAPER CLIP." ]

RUNI: That is not a prize.

PAVO: It is a useful object.

ACT ONE

Doma hands Runi a small envelope. It contains one paper clip. Neri waits at the other podium.

NERI: Do I get a question?

PAVO: First, Runi must identify the largest planet.

RUNI: Jupiter.

PAVO: Correct. Spin again.

[THE WHEEL LANDS ON "MYSTERY BOX." Doma brings out a box. It contains a smaller box.]

RUNI: Is the prize inside?

PAVO: It is a nested prize experience.

[Doma opens the smaller box. It contains another paper clip.]

ACT TWO

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - LATER

Neri gets a question about the capital of a made-up planet. Runi holds two paper clips and the mystery boxes.

NERI: That planet does not exist.

PAVO: Correct. Spin for your prize.

[THE WHEEL LANDS ON "LARGE TROPHY." A STAGEHAND CARRIES IN A TROPHY THAT IS TOO WIDE FOR THE DOOR.]

DOMA: We cannot get it through.

PAVO: Can you angle it?

[THE TROPHY TILTS AND BLOCKS BOTH PODIUMS.]

NERI: It is bigger than the game.

PAVO: The prize is aspirational.

RUNI: I would like to trade my boxes for a pencil.

ACT TWO-B

INT. PRIZE STAGING AREA - AFTERNOON

Doma sorts prizes. Pavo checks a chart. The paper clips have become a chain connecting the boxes.

DOMA: The wheel has six sections and only one useful prize.

PAVO: The wheel decides.

DOMA: We can add a section for "choose your prize."

PAVO: That would remove the suspense.

NERI: We already know the answers. We do not know what we will win.

[THE WHEEL SPINS BY ITSELF WHEN DOMA SETS IT DOWN. IT STOPS ON "PAPER CLIP." ]

RUNI: Again?

DOMA: The wheel has a favorite.

ACT TWO-C

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - LATE AFTERNOON

Doma removes the wheel cover. Pavo checks the pegs along the edge. Neri reads the prize labels aloud.

NERI: Paper clip, mystery box, large trophy, pencil, wheel, and paper clip again.

RUNI: There are two paper clip spaces.

PAVO: That makes the wheel fair.

DOMA: It makes the paper clip twice as likely.

PAVO: The audience enjoys suspense.

RUNI: I would enjoy a pencil.

[DOMA MOVES THE PENCIL SPACE BESIDE THE CLIP. PAVO SPINS THE WHEEL. IT LANDS BETWEEN THE TWO.]

PAVO: We have an uncertain result.

DOMA: It is between spaces.

NERI: Can we ask the question again?

PAVO: The question was answered correctly.

RUNI: I said Jupiter.

PAVO: The wheel decides the prize.

[THEY MEASURE THE WHEEL. THE POINTER IS WIDER THAN THE GAP BETWEEN SPACES.]

DOMA: The pointer covers both prizes.

NERI: Then the wheel has selected two.

PAVO: It has selected neither.

DOMA: We can make the pointer narrower.

[DOMA REPLACES IT WITH A PENCIL. THE PENCIL LANDS ON THE PAPER CLIP SPACE.]

RUNI: It has chosen the paper clip again.

ACT TWO-D

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - EVENING

Runi and Neri answer a practice question about a local bird. Both answer correctly. Pavo asks them to wait for the final round.

NERI: Is there a prize we can share?

DOMA: The paper clips are small enough.

PAVO: The final spin decides the prize.

[DOMA PLACES THE PAPER CLIPS BESIDE THE WHEEL. THE WHEEL STARTS TO TURN AS HE WALKS AWAY.]

RUNI: It is moving again.

NERI: We can wait for it to stop.

DOMA: We should move the podium away.

[PAVO HOLDS THE WHEEL STILL WITH ONE HAND. THE POINTER SETTLES BETWEEN MYSTERY BOX AND PAPER CLIP.]

PAVO: We will wait to spin until the final round.

ACT THREE

INT. GAME SHOW STUDIO - FINAL ROUND

Pavo asks one last question. Runi answers correctly. Neri answers correctly too. The wheel spins.

PAVO: The winner gets the grand prize.

[THE WHEEL LANDS ON "MYSTERY BOX." Runi opens it. Inside is a card reading "TAKE HOME THE WHEEL."]

RUNI: How do I carry it?

DOMA: We can take it apart.

PAVO: That is the prize.

NERI: I will take the paper clips.

TAG

The wheel spins once more after the set is empty. It lands on "PAPER CLIP." Doma turns off the lights.
