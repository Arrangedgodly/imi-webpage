# The Clockmaker's Quiet Hour

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: restoration reality television, where a clockmaker must repair a village clock while the host demands a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-010*

Premise: A repair crew tries to restore the town clock before the hour, but the host keeps asking them to pause for interviews and close-up shots.

Cast: Host Pava, gibbon; clockmaker Odo, baboon; apprentice Nara, macaque; caretaker Seli, capuchin; camera operator Tavi, lemur.

## Episode: "One More Tick"

### Cold Open

[THE CLOCK FACE SHOWS 11:52. THE PENDULUM IS STILL.]

PAVA: At noon, the clock must ring.

ODO: It needs a new pin.

NARA: The tools are on the bench.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[THE CREW STANDS BESIDE THE CLOCK.]

Pava says the town has not heard the bell in three days. Odo explains that the pendulum stopped because a retaining pin slipped. Nara has the replacement part.

SELI: The clock needs to be opened.

PAVA: Can we show the damaged pin first?

ODO: It is in the clock.

TAVI: The camera cannot fit in there.

Pava asks Odo to remove the panel. Odo says the clock must be stopped before the mechanism is handled. Seli holds the ladder while Nara passes up the wrench.

PAVA: The tension is building.

NARA: The ladder is stable.

ODO: We need quiet to hear the gear.

Pava signals the crew to stop talking. The camera motor whirs loudly.

### Act Two

[THE PANEL IS OPEN.]

Odo finds the worn pin. It is bent but not broken. Nara asks if it can be straightened. Odo says the metal is too weak; it must be replaced.

PAVA: Can we keep the old pin for the reveal?

ODO: I need it as a reference.

PAVA: The reveal is after the repair.

NARA: We could film the old pin now.

Tavi takes a close-up. The camera light reflects off the gear.

ODO: Turn that off.

PAVA: The image is too dark.

SELI: The clock is dark inside.

Tavi switches to a small lamp and angles it at the workbench. Odo removes the old pin and fits the new one. The gear moves but catches.

NARA: The wheel is not centered.

ODO: The pin is seated. The wheel is bent.

Pava asks if this is a setback. Odo says it is a second repair.

[THE CLOCK IS SET TO A TEST TIME.]

Nara asks Odo to set the hands to 11:59. Seli closes the panel, and Odo starts the pendulum. It ticks for three beats, then stops.

ODO: The new pin has slipped.

NARA: It is still in place.

Odo checks the wheel and finds that the pendulum weight is catching the frame. The pin is secure, but the weight is too low.

PAVA: We have another setback.

SELI: We have a clearance issue.

Nara measures the space between the weight and the frame. Odo raises the weight one notch. The pendulum swings cleanly.

TAVI: The repair needed two adjustments.

PAVA: Is this still the same episode?

ODO: It is the same clock.

Seli asks them to listen without speaking. The tick-tock is uneven; every fourth beat is softer.

ODO: The clock needs oil.

NARA: The gears are dry.

Pava asks if they can oil it on camera. Odo says yes, but only a tiny amount. Nara applies a drop to the pivot, and the sound evens out.

PAVA: The clock is ready.

TAVI: We still have to close the panel.

### Act Three

[THE CLOCK HANDS APPROACH NOON.]

Nara holds the gear steady while Odo adjusts the wheel. Seli winds the mechanism slowly. The pendulum swings once, then again.

[SFX: TICK. TOCK.]

Pava begins the reveal narration. The clock strikes twelve before she finishes.

[SFX: TWELVE BELLS.]

PAVA: The clock has returned.

ODO: It is keeping time.

NARA: The pin held.

Seli asks if they can close the panel. Odo secures the screws. Pava wants one more shot of the gears turning. Tavi says the panel is closed.

PAVA: Could we open it again?

ODO: No.

[THE CREW TESTS THE BELL.]

Odo sets the clock to noon. The hands reach the top, and the bell rings evenly. Pava asks the team to hold for the reaction shot, but the bell continues for the full twelve strokes.

TAVI: We have a complete chime.

NARA: The mechanism is steady.

The team checks the wall clock against the town bell. Both show noon.

PAVA: The reveal is complete.

ODO: The repair is complete.

### Tag

[THE CREW STANDS OUTSIDE.]

Pava asks if the clock will keep working. Odo says it will as long as the pin holds and someone winds it.

NARA: We can set a schedule.

SELI: The clock tells the time.

TAVI: It also tells us when the crew is late.

[SFX: ONE STEADY TICK.]
