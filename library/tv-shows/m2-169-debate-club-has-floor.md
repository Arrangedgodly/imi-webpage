# Debate Club Has the Floor

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised panel debates that reward interruption and false balance over useful exchange*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Moderator Ebo is assigned a televised debate about whether the village hall should install a ramp. Producer Vika insists on equal time for a guest who opposes the plan, although that guest has come only to ask where to park his delivery cart.

Cast: Ebo, a macaque moderator; Vika, a capuchin producer; Jami, a langur access planner; and Tilo, a gorilla delivery worker.

## Episode: "Both Sides of the Door"

### Cold Open

[DEBATE SET. TWO PODIUMS FACE EACH OTHER. JAMI HOLDS A HALL PLAN. TILO STANDS BESIDE A HAND CART.] 

EBO: Tonight, should the hall install a ramp?

TILO: I am here to ask where the ramp will go. I deliver chairs.

VIKA: We need two opposing views.

JAMI: The plan has the measurements.

[VIKA PUTS TILO AT THE SECOND PODIUM AND GIVES HIM A RED CARD.]

TILO: What does this card mean?

EBO: Apparently, you are against the ramp.

[VIKA STARTS THE DEBATE CLOCK BEFORE EBO HAS ASKED A QUESTION. THE CLOCK COUNTS DOWN WHILE TILO ROLLS HIS CART TO THE SIDE.] 

TILO: Is this the question?

EBO: It is the timer.

TILO: I am not answering a timer.

[JAMI TAKES THE RED CARD AND USES IT TO MARK THE DOOR SWING ON THE HALL PLAN.]

JAMI: The ramp can start here if we move the podiums.

VIKA: The podiums are part of the debate.

EBO: They are not part of the entrance.

### Act One

[JAMI SHOWS THE HALL ENTRANCE. THE CURRENT STEP IS HIGHER THAN THE CART'S FRONT WHEELS.] 

JAMI: The ramp will connect the sidewalk to the doorway. The landing stays clear of the doorswing.

VIKA: Tilo, rebuttal?

TILO: I need to turn the cart around at the corner.

[VIKA STARTS A TIMER. IT GIVES JAMI 30 SECONDS AND TILO 30 SECONDS.] 

JAMI: I can add a turning space beside the landing.

TILO: That would help.

VIKA: You agree?

TILO: Yes.

[VIKA LOOKS AT THE PRODUCER'S MONITOR. A GRAPHIC READS "DEBATE HEATS UP." EBO CROSSES OUT "HEATS UP" AND WRITES "PLAN UPDATED." ]

### Act Two

[THE CREW TAKES THE PODIUMS TO THE HALL. THE RAMP IS MARKED IN TAPE ON THE FLOOR. VIKA ASKS FOR A WALKING SHOT.] 

VIKA: Jami, demonstrate the controversy.

JAMI: There is no controversy in the drawing. We are checking clearance.

[TILO PUSHES HIS CART THROUGH THE MARKED TURN. ONE WHEEL CATCHES THE PODIUM BASE. THE RED CARD DROPS INTO THE CART.] 

TILO: This is why I asked about turning space.

EBO: That is a concrete point.

VIKA: Can you say it louder?

TILO: The podium is in my way.

[THE CREW MOVES THE PODIUM. JAMI ADJUSTS THE TAPE MARKS. THE CART NOW MAKES THE TURN WITHOUT CATCHING.] 

JAMI: We have the clearance.

VIKA: We need a rebuttal.

TILO: I would like to deliver the chairs.

[VIKA PUTS UP A POLL: "RAMP OR STAIRS?" THE AUDIENCE VOTES FOR RAMP. TILO ROLLS THE CART OVER THE MARKED ROUTE.]

[THE POLL'S CLOSE-UP GRAPHIC COVERS THE TAPE MARKS. JAMI MOVES IT OFFSCREEN SO THE COMMITTEE CAN SEE THE PLAN.] 

VIKA: We are losing the vote.

EBO: The committee can still see the drawing.

[TILO ROLLS BACK THROUGH THE TURN. THE CART PASSES CLEARLY. VIKA TRIES TO ASK HIM TO REPEAT THE PASS WITH A MORE WORRIED EXPRESSION. HE KEEPS A NORMAL PACE.] 

TILO: I am delivering the chairs.

### Act Three

[THE HALL COMMITTEE LOOKS AT THE UPDATED PLAN. EBO ASKS EACH GUEST FOR A FINAL STATEMENT.] 

JAMI: We have adjusted the landing and confirmed the cart can turn.

TILO: I can get the chairs inside without lifting them over the step.

VIKA: We have two strong positions.

EBO: We have one proposal and a delivery route that works.

[THE RED CARD IS STILL IN THE CART. TILO PULLS IT OUT AND USES IT TO LEVEL A WOBBLY CHAIR.] 

TILO: It is useful after all.

[VIKA TRIES TO RESET THE PODIUMS FOR A CLOSING SHOT. THE CART IS ALREADY PARKED IN THE WIDE TURNING SPACE. THE CAMERA CAN'T FIT BOTH PODIUMS. EBO MOVES THE PODIUMS ASIDE.]

[VIKA BRINGS OUT A SECOND TIMER FOR THE CLOSING STATEMENTS. IT STARTS AT ZERO AND COUNTS UP.]

VIKA: We can make this feel like overtime.

EBO: The meeting ends when the committee has the drawing.

[JAMI HANDS THE PLAN TO THE COMMITTEE CHAIR. TILO ROLLS THE EMPTY CART OUT THROUGH THE CLEAR TURNING SPACE. THE SECOND TIMER CONTINUES COUNTING AFTER EVERYONE HAS LEFT THE SET.]

### Tag

[THE SHOW'S LOWER THIRD READS "THE RAMP QUESTION: SOLVED?" TILO ROLLS THE FIRST CHAIR THROUGH THE DOOR.]

TILO: The chair is inside.

JAMI: That is the answer.
