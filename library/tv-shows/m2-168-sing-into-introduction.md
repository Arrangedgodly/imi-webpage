# Sing Into the Introduction

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised talent auditions that bury the performer under elaborate contestant packages*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-006*

Premise: Singer Maru arrives at a talent competition to perform a short lullaby. The show producer keeps expanding her introductory biography until the allotted performance window is gone, and host Tavi has to decide whether the broadcast is about the contestant or the contestant's dramatic backstory.

Cast: Tavi, a macaque host; Maru, a langur singer; Yeki, a capuchin producer; Boro, a gorilla stagehand; and Auntie Nala, Maru's gibbon aunt.

## Episode: "Please Welcome the Package"

### Cold Open

[AUDITION STAGE. MARU STANDS AT A MICROPHONE. A SCREEN BEHIND HER SHOWS A CHILDHOOD PHOTO.] 

TAVI: Please welcome Maru.

[YEKI CUES A VIDEO PACKAGE. A PIANO PLAYS AS A SERIES OF PHOTOS APPEARS.] 

MARU: I thought I was singing now.

YEKI: First the story.

BORO: The microphone is live.

[MARU SAYS "HELLO" INTO IT. THE VOICE ECHOES THROUGH THE EMPTY STUDIO.] 

### Act One

[YEKI RUNS THE PACKAGE AGAIN. A GRAPHIC READS "SHE SANG TO HERSELF." ]

MARU: I sang in the choir.

YEKI: We need a struggle.

MARU: The choir practiced on Wednesdays.

TAVI: That is a schedule.

[YEKI ADDS A SHOT OF A CLOSED MUSIC ROOM. BORO OPENS THE DOOR TO REVEAL A ROOM FULL OF CHAIRS AND A PIANO.] 

BORO: They are rehearsing in there now.

YEKI: Can we use it as a memory?

MARU: It is today.

[THE PRODUCER CUTS THE PACKAGE TO A CLOSE-UP OF MARU'S HANDS. MARU HOLDS THE MICROPHONE STEADILY.]

[YEKI PUTS A SMALL TOY PIANO ON THE STAGE TO REPRESENT MARU'S CHILDHOOD. MARU MOVES IT SO THE CAMERA CAN SEE THE REAL PIANO BEHIND HER.] 

MARU: I learned the song from my aunt.

TAVI: Is she here tonight?

MARU: She is sitting in the front row.

[THE CAMERA TURNS TO AUNTIE NALA, WHO WAVES. YEKI CUES A SAD VIOLIN STING. NALA LAUGHS AND WAVES AGAIN.]

YEKI: The music is supposed to be emotional.

MARU: She is happy.

[YEKI ASKS NALA TO STAND IN A SPOTLIGHT. NALA MOVES HER CHAIR TO THE FRONT ROW MARK AND PATS THE EMPTY SEAT BESIDE HER FOR MARU.]

NALA: I can watch from here.

TAVI: That is a good seat.

[THE SPOTLIGHT IS TOO BRIGHT FOR THE CAMERA. BORO SHADES IT WITH A PIECE OF BLACK FOAM. YEKI TRIES TO CUE THE SAD STING AGAIN; THE BAND PLAYS THE LULLABY'S FIRST CHORD INSTEAD.] 

MARU: That's the tune.

[SHE HUMS THE NOTE TO THE BAND. YEKI'S PACKAGE AUDIO STARTS OVER IT, AND TAVI CROSSES THE AUDIO TRACK OFF THE CUE SHEET.] 

TAVI: How long is the song?

MARU: Ninety seconds.

YEKI: The introduction is four minutes.

TAVI: Then the show is about the introduction.

### Act Two

[THE RUNNING CLOCK SHOWS FIVE MINUTES LEFT. YEKI PLAYS A VOICE-OVER ABOUT MARU'S "JOURNEY." SHE STANDS ON THE MARK, WAITING.] 

MARU: May I start?

YEKI: The package needs its final reveal.

[BORO ROLLS IN A PLUSH PILLOW TO REPRESENT THE LULLABY. IT BLOCKS THE CAMERA'S VIEW OF MARU.]

MARU: That is larger than the microphone.

[TAVI MOVES THE PILLOW OFFSTAGE. YEKI PLAYS ANOTHER CLIP, THIS TIME A SLOW-MOTION SHOT OF MARU WALKING DOWN A HALLWAY.] 

TAVI: The audience has seen her walk here.

YEKI: They have not seen what it means.

MARU: It means the stage is at the end of the hallway.

[THE BAND BEGINS HER INTRODUCTION MUSIC, BUT YEKI CUES THE PACKAGE AUDIO OVER IT. THE TWO TRACKS CLASH. TAVI SIGNALS BOTH TO STOP.]

[YEKI ASKS FOR A SLOW-MOTION WALK TO THE MICROPHONE. MARU STAYS AT THE MARK AND CHECKS THE STAND HEIGHT.]

MARU: The microphone is too high.

BORO: I can lower it.

[BORO TURNS THE CLAMP. THE STAND DROPS TOO FAR AND THE MICROPHONE NEARLY TOUCHES THE FLOOR. MARU CATCHES IT BY THE HANDLE.] 

TAVI: That is the most dramatic movement so far.

TAVI: We have two introductions and no song.

[BORO PULLS THE PACKAGE CABLE. THE SCREEN GOES BLACK.] 

### Act Three

[TAVI FACES THE CAMERA. YEKI HOLDS UP A CARD READING "FINAL REVEAL." ]

TAVI: Maru is here to sing. We will hear her song now.

[YEKI STARTS TO PLAY A FINAL CHILDHOOD CLIP. BORO TAKES THE REMOTE AND PUTS IT ON THE TABLE. MARU SINGS A QUIET LULLABY. THE ROOM GOES STILL.]

[WHEN SHE FINISHES, THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. YEKI LOOKS AT THE CLOCK, WHICH STILL HAS A MINUTE LEFT.] 

YEKI: We have time for the package ending.

MARU: I can sing the last line again.

[TAVI NODS. MARU REPEATS THE FINAL LINE. THE CAMERA STAYS ON HER FACE INSTEAD OF THE SCREEN.] 

### Tag

[BACKSTAGE, YEKI OPENS THE PACKAGE FILE. IT IS EIGHT MINUTES LONG. BORO HAS RENAMED IT "AFTER THE SONG." ]

YEKI: The title is good.

TAVI: We can use the song in the next segment.
