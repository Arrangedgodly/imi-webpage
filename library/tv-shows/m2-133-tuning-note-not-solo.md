# The Tuning Note Is Not a Solo

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised orchestra competitions that rank performers during tuning and turn rehearsal corrections into dramatic eliminations*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Evi wants a high-stakes finale for the Grove Orchestra Challenge. Conductor Boro needs the ensemble to tune together before the guest performance, but the judges keep scoring individual noises.

Cast: Evi, a macaque host; Boro, a gorilla conductor; Seli, a lemur violinist; and Tavi, a capuchin judge.

## Episode: "One Note at a Time"

### Cold Open

[ORCHESTRA PIT. MUSICIANS TUNE THEIR INSTRUMENTS. EVI STANDS BESIDE A SCOREBOARD.]

EVI: The competition begins now.

[SELI PLAYS A SINGLE A. TAVI RAISES A SCORECARD.]

TAVI: Strong opening. Eight points.

BORO: We are tuning.

EVI: The contest is already underway.

[THE ORCHESTRA PLAYS DIFFERENT NOTES. TAVI'S SCORECARD FLIPS IN THE SOUND.]

TAVI: I did not press anything.

EVI: Your scorecard is responding to the music.

BORO: It is responding to air from the brass section.

[THE SCORECARD FLAPS AGAIN. TAVI HOLDS IT CLOSED.]

TAVI: We will judge after the rehearsal.

EVI: That is not the format.

BORO: It is the schedule.

### Act One

[BORO RAISES HIS BATON.]

BORO: Everyone listen to the oboe.

[THE OBOIST PLAYS. A TRUMPET ANSWERS. A CELLO ADDS A LOW NOTE.]

EVI: The ensemble is building suspense.

BORO: It is tuning.

[SELI ADJUSTS HER STRING. IT SLIPS FLAT.]

TAVI: A bold dissonance.

SELI: The peg moved.

[THE STAGEHAND BRINGS A SMALL TOOL. EVI ASKS HIM TO WAIT UNTIL THE JUDGES DISCUSS THE PERFORMANCE.]

EVI: What did the note make you feel?

TAVI: Curious.

BORO: It needs to be in tune before it can make you feel anything.

[SELI ADJUSTS THE PEG. THE NOTE MATCHES THE OBOE.]

EVI: A dramatic recovery.

BORO: A successful adjustment.

### Act Two

[THE ORCHESTRA BEGINS THE FIRST MOVEMENT. THE JUDGES HOLD UP SCORECARDS AFTER EACH PHRASE.]

EVI: We are evaluating precision, expression, and stage presence.

BORO: Please wait until the movement ends.

[TAVI RAISES A CARD MID-PHRASE. SEVERAL MUSICIANS LOOK OVER. THE TEMPO SLOWS.]

BORO: Eyes on the music.

[THEY CONTINUE. SELI PLAYS A CLEAN SOLO. EVI POINTS TO HER.]

EVI: A star moment.

SELI: It is the melody in the score.

TAVI: Could you play it with more surprise?

SELI: I have rehearsed it the same way for weeks.

[THE ENSEMBLE REACHES A REST. TAVI SCORES THE SILENCE.]

TAVI: Strong use of space.

BORO: It is a rest.

EVI: The orchestra is giving us a dramatic pause.

[THE MUSICIANS RESUME. A MUSIC STAND SLIPS. SELI CATCHES IT WITHOUT MISSING A NOTE. THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN.]

EVI: A test of commitment.

SELI: A loose stand.

[BORO STOPS THE GROUP AND TIGHTENS THE CLAMP. HE CHECKS THE STAND BEFORE RESTARTING.]

[THE ORCHESTRA PAUSES WHILE BORO CHECKS THE MUSIC STANDS. EVI ASKS WHETHER THE NEXT SECTION IS A SOLO ROUND.]

BORO: It is a duet between violin and cello.

TAVI: Can the judges hear each part separately?

SELI: You can hear them together.

[THE DUET BEGINS. TAVI PUTS DOWN HIS SCORECARD AND LISTENS. EVI WAITS FOR HIM TO RAISE IT. HE DOES NOT. THE MUSIC RESOLVES INTO THE FULL ENSEMBLE.]

EVI: We had a quiet judge reaction.

TAVI: I was listening.

### Act Three

[THE GUEST PERFORMANCE BEGINS. THE ORCHESTRA PLAYS THE PIECE WITHOUT INTERRUPTION. THE JUDGES WAIT UNTIL THE FINAL CHORD.]

EVI: The scores are in.

TAVI: We have a tie between Seli's solo and the tuning note.

SELI: The tuning note was not a solo.

BORO: The ensemble has completed the piece together.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. EVI ASKS THE JUDGES TO SELECT A WINNER. TAVI LOOKS AT THE PARTS.]

TAVI: The stands were steady, the strings were tuned, and the rest was quiet.

EVI: Can that be a score?

TAVI: It is what the music needs.

[SELI HANDS TAVI THE SCORECARD. IT HAS ONLY ONE BOX TO CHECK: "LISTENED."]

TAVI: The best sound was when everyone played the same pitch.

BORO: That is the orchestra.

[THE SCOREBOARD REPLACES INDIVIDUAL NUMBERS WITH A SINGLE LINE: "IN TUNE."]

[AFTER THE PIECE, BORO CHECKS THE PROGRAM. THE NEXT REHEARSAL IS SCHEDULED FOR TUESDAY.]

BORO: We will tune before the guests arrive.

EVI: Will the judges be there?

TAVI: We can sit quietly.

EVI: Should we hand out scorecards?

BORO: Hand out programs.

[PUCK PASSES THE PROGRAMS DOWN THE ROW. EACH ONE HAS THE FULL SET LIST.]

[SELI PACKS HER VIOLIN. THE SCOREBOARD SHOWS THE NEXT CONCERT DATE, NOT A RANKING.]

[BORO GIVES THE ORCHESTRA THE NEXT REHEARSAL DATE. SELI PUTS HER MUSIC IN A FOLDER.]

SELI: We can tune together before the audience arrives.

TAVI: And I can listen before scoring.

[THEY PACK UP IN TIME WITH THE LAST CHORD.]

### Tag

[THE MUSICIANS PACK THEIR INSTRUMENTS. EVI HOLDS UP A CARD.]

EVI: One final question. Who won?

SELI: The audience got to hear the music.

[TAVI LOWERS THE CARD. THE ORCHESTRA PLAYS A SHORT, MATCHED CHORD.]
