# The Meadow That Moved

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: nature documentary convention, where a small group of grazing monkeys appears to migrate across the screen because the camera keeps relocating*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-007*

Premise: A documentary crew tracks a troop of monkeys in a meadow, but the animals are only moving between patches of shade.

Cast: Narrator Tavi, a gibbon; camera operator Piri, a capuchin; field guide Nara, a macaque; and troop leader Vela, a baboon.

Episode: "Migration of the Afternoon"

COLD OPEN

EXT. MEADOW, MORNING

Tavi whispers over a wide shot of monkeys under a tree.

TAVI: The troop prepares for a journey.

Nara points to a nearby shady patch. The monkeys move three body lengths and sit down.

NARA: They changed seats.

ACT ONE

Piri sets a camera at the first tree. The monkeys move into the shade of a rock. Tavi narrates a bold departure. Nara explains the sun has shifted. Piri carries the camera to the rock. The monkeys move back to the tree.

TAVI: The migration reverses.

NARA: The shade moved.

ACT TWO

The crew sets up two cameras. Vela leads the troop to a patch with fallen fruit. Piri films from a distance, but a tall grass stem covers the lens. He trims nothing; Nara asks him to move the camera rather than disturb the grass.

Tavi records a narration about changing terrain. The troop eats fruit and rests. One juvenile climbs a small mound to see over the grass. The others follow, then return to the tree when the mound is sunny.

ACT TWO-B

EXT. MEADOW, AFTERNOON

Tavi asks Nara to identify the troop's destination. Nara watches Vela lead the group toward a low stone wall, where a narrow band of shade has formed. Piri sets a camera beside the wall. The troop keeps moving because the wall is still warm from the sun.

Vela leads them to the tree line. Piri moves the camera and sets it on a rock. The lens points toward an empty patch of grass. Nara notices a cluster of small fruits under a bush. The troop stops to eat. Tavi narrates a seasonal food discovery, but Nara says the fruit is present every day.

A young monkey carries a fruit to the warm rock, then returns to the bush for another. The crew tracks the individual. Piri asks if it is collecting provisions for a long migration. Nara says it is feeding its infant nearby. The crew shifts the camera back to the bush to avoid filming too close to the nest.

TAVI: We have found the reason for the route.

NARA: Food and shade.

Piri records a wide shot of the meadow and the tree line. It shows the troop moving between patches without crowding any one place. Nara explains how the shade changes with the sun and why the troop moves little by little.

ACT TWO-C

EXT. FIELD STATION, LATE AFTERNOON

The crew reviews the footage. The first shot makes the troop appear to travel across the entire meadow. The second reveals the camera moved first. Tavi asks Piri to show the tripod marks on the ground. There are four sets, all pointing at the same tree.

Piri suggests keeping the narration about migration because the music works well. Nara asks him to leave the music out. She points to a small monkey returning to the same resting branch. Tavi rewrites the script: "The troop shifts with the shade and gathers fruit along its familiar route." Piri records the line without whispering.

They check the nest area from a respectful distance. The infant remains hidden, and the adults feed nearby. Nara asks the crew to pack the cameras before sunset so the troop can use the clearing undisturbed.

ACT TWO-D

EXT. MEADOW, SUNSET

The troop moves from the tree to a patch of shade behind a boulder. Piri places the camera on a fixed stand and lets the animals pass through the frame. No one follows them with a lens. Tavi describes the grass, the fruit and the changing light without calling it a migration.

Nara asks him to include the troop's pauses. The monkeys rest, groom and share fruit before moving again. Piri captures a young monkey grooming another beside the rock. Tavi calls it a social stop. Nara explains the troop is not merely traveling; it is staying connected while choosing cooler places.

The camera remains still for several minutes. Vela leads the group back toward the familiar tree as the sun lowers. The crew packs up from the field station, leaving the meadow clear.

ACT THREE



The crew makes a map of shade patches and observes from a fixed spot. The troop uses the tree, rock and mound throughout the afternoon, with no long journey. Tavi rewrites the narration to describe how the troop adjusts to heat and food.

TAG

Piri checks the camera. A leaf has fallen over the lens, making the whole meadow look green. Tavi says the habitat has expanded.

Nara lifts the leaf. The monkeys are still sitting in the shade.
