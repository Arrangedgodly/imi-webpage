# Announcements After the Weather

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: local community-calendar broadcasts that race through event listings, cram in corrections, and treat routine notices like breaking news*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-005*

Premise: Host Pavi wants to fit every neighborhood announcement into one segment. Producer Lumo keeps adding late corrections, while sign-language interpreter Dena tries to make the information understandable.

Cast: Pavi, a macaque host; Lumo, a lemur producer; Dena, a gorilla interpreter; and Tavi, a capuchin camera operator.

## Episode: "The Room Changed"

### Cold Open

[STUDIO. A BOARD SHOWS FIVE COMMUNITY EVENTS. PAVI READS QUICKLY.]

PAVI: The choir meets in Room Three, the repair cafe is in Room Two, and the garden meeting is-

[LUMO SLIDES A NEW CARD INTO FRAME.]

LUMO: The garden meeting moved to Room Four.

PAVI: Room Four.

[LUMO SLIDES ANOTHER CARD.]

LUMO: Room Four is being painted. It moved to the courtyard.

DENA: Which courtyard?

[LUMO CHECKS HIS NOTES.]

LUMO: The west one.

[TAVI ZOOMS IN ON THE BOARD. THE ROOM NUMBERS ARE UPSIDE DOWN.]

### Act One

[PAVI TAKES A BREATH. DENA SIGNS THE ANNOUNCEMENTS BESIDE HIM.]

PAVI: Saturday, nine a.m., repair cafe in Room Two. Bring one household item that can be safely carried.

[LUMO HOLDS UP A CARD.]

LUMO: It is now in the courtyard.

PAVI: Which courtyard?

LUMO: The west courtyard.

DENA: The west courtyard is closed for painting.

[LUMO CHECKS AGAIN.]

LUMO: East courtyard.

[TAVI CHANGES THE MAP. THE ARROW POINTS OUT OF THE STUDIO.]

PAVI: We can update the map after this announcement.

DENA: Viewers need the correct room before they leave home.

[LUMO WRITES "EAST COURTYARD" ON A CARD. THE MARKER RUNS OUT OF INK.]

LUMO: I have no ink.

PAVI: We have a marker.

DENA: It is not working.

[PAVI TRIES THE MARKER. IT MAKES A PALE LINE. TAVI HOLDS THE CARD UNDER A LIGHT.]

DENA: I cannot interpret a pale line.

LUMO: I can read it if I tilt the card.

PAVI: That is not how viewers will see it.

[TAVI FINDS A BLACK MARKER. HE TESTS IT ON THE BACK OF THE CARD. IT WORKS. THEY WRITE THE LOCATION AGAIN.]

PAVI: The announcement is visible if you know what it says.

### Act Two

[THE NEXT CARD READS "COMMUNITY CHOIR, SUNDAY." LUMO ADDS A CORRECTION.]

LUMO: Rehearsal is in Room Three.

PAVI: Is Room Three open?

LUMO: It is booked for the repair cafe.

DENA: The repair cafe moved outside.

PAVI: Which courtyard?

[LUMO LOOKS AT THE NOTES. DENA POINTS TO THE EAST COURTYARD MAP.]

LUMO: The east courtyard.

PAVI: Then the choir has Room Three.

[THE CAMERA CUTS TO A SPLIT SCREEN WITH THE CHOIR LEADER. THE LEADER HOLDS UP A SIGN: "ROOM 3 - SIDE DOOR."]

CHOIR LEADER: Use the side door. The main hall floor is wet.

PAVI: Please enter by the side door.

[LUMO ADDS A NEW CARD: "CLOTHING SWAP, SUNDAY, MAIN HALL."]

DENA: The main hall floor is wet.

LUMO: That event moved to Room One.

PAVI: Room One is the choir's storage room.

DENA: It is also the east entrance.

[THE THREE OF THEM LOOK AT THE MAP. TAVI FLIPS IT RIGHT SIDE UP. ROOM ONE IS IN THE WEST WING.]

PAVI: We will pause and verify the locations.

[LUMO CALLS THE COMMUNITY CENTER. HE WAITS ON HOLD WHILE PAVI SITS QUIETLY.]

DENA: The schedule can wait until we know where people should go.

### Act Three

[LUMO RETURNS WITH A CONFIRMED LIST. HE REMOVES THE OUTDATED CARDS.]

PAVI: Did you check the courtyard with the custodian?

LUMO: Yes. The paint crew has left a walkway open.

DENA: Is there step-free access?

LUMO: The west ramp is open, and the side entrance is marked.

[TAVI ADDS THE RAMP SYMBOL TO THE MAP. DENA CHECKS THAT IT IS LARGE ENOUGH TO READ.]

LUMO: Repair cafe, east courtyard. Choir, Room Three through the side door. Clothing swap, Room One. Garden meeting, next Saturday.

PAVI: That is four clear announcements.

DENA: And one event moved to next week.

[TAVI ADDS A LARGE, SIMPLE MAP. THE ARROWS POINT TO EACH LOCATION.]

PAVI: We will post the list after the broadcast and leave it up at the center entrance.

[LUMO HANDS DENA A CLEAN CARD. SHE CHECKS EACH LOCATION BEFORE HE HOLDS IT UP.]

DENA: Confirmed.

[PAVI READS THE LIST SLOWLY. DENA SIGNS EACH EVENT. NO NEW CARDS ARRIVE.]

[PAVI HOLDS UP THE FINAL EVENT CARD. DENA CHECKS THE SIGNED MAP BESIDE IT.]

PAVI: The community calendar is now confirmed.

LUMO: I will call the center before adding any late changes.

[TAVI SETS THE CAMERA TO SHOW BOTH THE CARD AND THE ACCESSIBLE ROUTE MAP.]

### Tag

[THE BROADCAST ENDS. LUMO PICKS UP A LATE NOTE.]

LUMO: The clothing swap is in Room Two.

PAVI: The confirmed list says Room One.

DENA: We will check before changing it.

[LUMO SETS THE NOTE DOWN. THE CAMERA FADES.]
