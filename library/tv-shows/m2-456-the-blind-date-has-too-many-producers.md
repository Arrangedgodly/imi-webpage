# The Blind Date Has Too Many Producers

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: dating reality shows, producer-manufactured coincidences, and confessionals that turn every small pause into a cliffhanger*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-016*

Cast: Host Davi, a baboon; date one Loro, a macaque; date two Sumi, a gibbon; field producer Kett, an orangutan; sound mixer Pali, a capuchin.

COLD OPEN

INT. CAFE SET - EVENING

Loro and Sumi sit across from each other at a small table. A director's chair and two production headsets are visible behind a fake plant. Davi stands just outside the frame.

LORO: Are we supposed to pretend we met in line?

SUMI: We met in the casting room. You asked where the exits were.

LORO: I wanted to know if the room had a back door.

[The camera pulls wider, revealing Kett holding a sign reading SPONTANEOUS MEET-CUTE.] 

KETT: Can we try that again with a pastry?

SUMI: Why?

KETT: The audience likes pastries.

[The studio bell rings for the segment. The host waves to camera as both daters stare at him.] 

ACT ONE

INT. CAFE SET - MOMENTS LATER

Davi introduces the date and promises a natural evening. Kett gives each participant a card with suggested topics: childhood dreams, favorite travel mishap, and whether they believe in fate. The sound mixer clips microphones to their collars.

DAVI: Ignore the crew. The camera is a friend.

SUMI: Is it a friend who keeps asking us to repeat the pastry entrance?

KETT: The first version had a chair squeak.

LORO: That was the chair's authentic response.

[The host asks them to begin with a toast. A server brings water, not sparkling cider as the cue sheet expected. Kett reaches to swap the glasses; Sumi stops the hand.] 

SUMI: Water is fine.

LORO: We can toast with water. It is what we asked for.

DAVI: A refreshing twist.

SUMI: It is a glass of water.

[The dates talk about hobbies. Loro says he likes repairing small clocks; Sumi says she grows herbs on a balcony. Kett signals Davi to ask whether either activity reflects their romantic compatibility.] 

DAVI: Do clocks and herbs share a deeper meaning?

LORO: They both need attention.

SUMI: And neither improves when someone narrates over it.

ACT TWO

INT. CONFESSIONAL BOOTH - LATER

The show asks each dater to record a reaction. Kett wants them to describe a moment of instant chemistry. Loro says the conversation is comfortable. Sumi says the crew keeps interrupting. The producer asks whether the interruption itself could be the chemistry.

SUMI: No.

KETT: We can retake your answer with more mystery.

LORO: She gave you a clear answer.

[The crew returns them to the cafe for a surprise challenge: choose a dessert for each other. Only one dessert remains, a plain pear. Kett asks the server to bring a covered tray. The host makes a suspenseful announcement.] 

DAVI: Your choices may reveal your compatibility.

SUMI: We both have the same pear.

LORO: We can share it.

[They slice the pear with the clean table knife and ask the server for another plate. Davi tries to turn the shared pear into a symbolic moment. Sumi hands one half to Loro without looking at the camera.] 

LORO: Thank you.

SUMI: You are welcome.

KETT: Can we get a close-up of the exchange?

PALI: The camera battery is low. We need to stop for a minute.

[The crew stops recording. Loro and Sumi continue talking without the host. The audio mixer leaves the mics on only for safety and does not feed the conversation to the set.] 

ACT THREE

INT. CAFE SET - NIGHT

The host asks whether the date will continue. Loro says he would like to meet again without the cameras. Sumi says yes, but proposes the public herb market on Saturday. Kett tries to ask whether it will be a dramatic second date. Davi blocks the microphone gently.

DAVI: The participants can set their own plan.

KETT: We need a closing reveal.

SUMI: We are going to the market. That is the reveal.

LORO: Saturday, at the south entrance, after the morning crowd.

[The sound mixer confirms the invitation is captured. Kett starts a countdown for a kiss shot, then sees both daters wave goodbye instead. Davi lets the goodbye stand.] 

DAVI: They have made a plan. We will not add one.

[The crew packs the fake plant. Kett reaches to move the water glasses but leaves them for the server. The date card is removed from the table.] 

TAG

Davi walks past the camera and asks if anyone wants the remaining pear. The crew declines; Sumi takes it for the walk home.

FINAL IMAGE: Loro and Sumi leave through the real cafe door while the set lights go dark behind them.
