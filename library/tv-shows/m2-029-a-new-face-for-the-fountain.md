# A New Face for the Fountain

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: makeover show*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A design team renovates a neglected neighborhood fountain, but the residents care more about fixing its water access and keeping its old mosaic than about a dramatic reveal.

Cast: Host Celeste, a stylish macaque; Ro, a practical baboon plumber; Nimmi, a capuchin mosaic artist; Mr. Tallow, an elderly gibbon gardener; and the Fountain Friends, a rotating group of residents who use the square.

## Episode: "Keep the Blue Tiles"

### Cold open

EXT. TOWN SQUARE, DAY

Celeste pulls a cover from the fountain. The water sputters, sprays her jacket, and stops. Behind her, a resident fills a bottle from a public tap.

CELESTE: We wanted a splashy reveal.

RO: We got one.

Mr. Tallow taps a blue tile near the basin. It comes loose in his hand. Nimmi asks him to hold it carefully.

### Act one

The show plans new stone and planters. Residents want the fountain repaired, a ramp to the tap, and shade. Mr. Tallow says local children made the mosaic. The designer prefers clean stone.

Ro finds a corroded valve and cracked basin. The budget covers a valve and liner, not a rebuild. Nimmi asks to inspect the mosaic before painting.

Residents use the tap, bench, and bird basin. A wheelchair user cannot reach the tap from the current path.

### Act two

Nimmi carefully removes loose tiles and finds the original artist's initials under a layer of grime. The blue mosaic is faded but intact. She proposes cleaning and regrouting it instead of replacing it. The production asks for a before-and-after shot with a large wall panel. Nimmi says the tiles are the actual artwork, and the panel would cover them.

Ro replaces the valve and seals the basin. The pump works, but the tap pressure is weak. He discovers that the supply pipe narrows behind the wall, likely from mineral buildup. He can clear the pipe, but the team must shut off water to the square for an hour. Celeste asks whether that will affect the reveal. Mr. Tallow says people can wait an hour. He has a thermos.

The crew builds a short ramp to the public tap. The first version is too steep. Ro measures the rise and length, then adjusts the path so it is easier to approach. The production worries it will take space from the flower bed. The residents vote to shrink the bed and keep the route clear.

Nimmi cleans the mosaic with a soft brush and replaces missing grout. The blue tiles brighten, but retain small chips and cracks. She says the wear shows where children used to sit along the edge. Celeste asks her not to hide the marks. They place a small plaque naming the school art club that made the pattern.


[Ro shuts off the pump. Nimmi lifts a loose blue tile.]
CELESTE: We have our reveal.
RO: We have a leak.
NIMMI: This tile is original.
MR. TALLOW: The children made it.
CELESTE: Can it be replaced?
NIMMI: It can be cleaned.
RO: The valve is corroded.
CELESTE: Is that dramatic?
RO: It is expensive.
FRIEND ONE: The tap is too high.
FRIEND TWO: The path is too steep.
CELESTE: What do you need?
FRIEND ONE: A lower route.
MR. TALLOW: And shade.
NIMMI: Keep the mosaic visible.
PRODUCER: The new stone looks bright.
NIMMI: It covers the blue tiles.

CELESTE: Can we keep filming?
RO: After the water is off.
MR. TALLOW: I brought a thermos.
FRIEND TWO: I brought a tape measure.

CELESTE: That takes flower space.
FRIEND ONE: Move the flower bed.

PRODUCER: Should they applaud?
CELESTE: They came for water.
NIMMI: The birds came too.
RO: No leak.
CELESTE: Then we are done.

### Act three

The water returns. Ro tests the tap and basin for leaks. The fountain runs quietly, without the high spray the show planned. A resident fills a bottle using the new ramp. Mr. Tallow waters the flowers from a bucket. Birds gather at the basin and drink.

Celeste gathers the residents for the reveal. There is no giant cover. The camera follows people arriving, using the tap, resting on the bench, and tracing the mosaic with their eyes. The producer asks for applause. The Fountain Friends clap once, then continue with their day.

The finished square has a repaired pump, a clear path, cleaned blue tiles, and a smaller flower bed. Celeste says the makeover changed how easy it is to use the fountain. Ro explains the valve and pipe repair so the caretaker can maintain it. Nimmi leaves a brush and grout notes with the school art club. Mr. Tallow agrees to water the new plants, but only if volunteers share the work.

The crew asks residents what they think. One says the fountain sounds better. Another says the ramp makes the square feel like it belongs to more people. A child asks whether the birds count as residents. Mr. Tallow says they have been here longer than the flower beds.

### Tag

At sunrise, the fountain runs. Celeste returns to film a final shot, but a pigeon walks across the frame and drinks from the basin.

RO: The reveal has a guest.

Nimmi says the pigeon was there yesterday too.
