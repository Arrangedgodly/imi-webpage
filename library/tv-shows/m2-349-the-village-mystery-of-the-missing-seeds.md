# The Village Mystery of the Missing Seeds

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: cozy village mysteries that inflate a small garden mix-up into a network of suspects*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-012*

Premise: A packet of seeds disappears from the village greenhouse, and the amateur detective interrogates everyone who came near the potting table.

Cast: Detective Leni, a gibbon; gardener Kito, a macaque; baker Odo, a baboon; post carrier Pava, a capuchin; greenhouse keeper Nara, a lemur.

Episode: "Seeds Among Friends"

COLD OPEN

INT. GREENHOUSE - MORNING

Leni examines an empty envelope.

LENI: The seeds are missing.

KITO: I planted them yesterday.

Leni gasps. Odo drops a pie.

ODO: A planting scandal.

ACT ONE

Nara explains that the packet was opened and the seeds were sown in a tray. Kito confirms he planted them, but Leni asks why the envelope is empty. Kito says he threw it into the paper bin.

Leni calls for a village inquiry. She interviews Odo, who visited the greenhouse to deliver a pie. He says he never touched the seeds. Pava brought a parcel and placed it on the table. She says it was a new pot.

Leni finds soil on the table and calls it evidence. Nara says it came from the seed tray. Leni asks if the pot has fingerprints. Nara says everyone handles pots.

ACT TWO

Leni checks the paper bin. The envelope is there, folded shut and marked "sunflower." A dark line of soil stains one corner.

LENI: The packet is empty, but the case is not.

KITO: I opened it beside the tray.

LENI: You admit access to the scene.

KITO: I admit planting seeds. I do not know where the second packet went.

Odo says the pie was for next week's greenhouse opening. Pava confirms the date on the calendar, then points out that the ribbon and sign are still rolled up. Leni asks whether anyone moved the tray. Pava recalls shifting it from the table to the window when the pie was delivered; Kito says he later moved it back so he could water.

LENI: A trail of movements. At last, a proper village mystery.

NARA: Those are ordinary gardening tasks.

LENI: Ordinary tasks are how mysteries hide.

ACT TWO-B

Leni lays a garden map on the potting table and makes each witness retrace one move. The paper map covers the tray, so Odo lifts it by one corner while Pava reads the labels. Kito recognizes the square where the sunflower tray was last set down, but the bench there is empty.

LENI: Then where is the tray now?

NARA: On the lower shelf. I put it there when the watering can dripped.

LENI: You moved evidence.

NARA: I moved a tray away from a puddle.

Leni inspects the wet ring on the shelf and finds tiny grains caught in the wooden seam. Kito says they are spilled soil, not seeds. She marks the seam with a paper flag and asks everyone to step back. The group obeys, except Odo, whose pie keeps sliding toward the edge.

ACT TWO-C

Nara lifts the tray from the lower shelf. Under the magnifying glass, the first green shoots are visible. Kito checks the seed jar, then the tray label: the packet was planted here, while the spare seeds stayed in the jar. The soil grains on the shelf came from the tray's drainage holes, not a thief's pocket.

LENI: The missing packet became a row of seedlings.

KITO: The packet was never missing. The empty envelope was.

PAVA: And the jar stayed on the shelf the whole time.

Leni marks the tray's location on the garden board. Odo asks to sign as lead investigator. Nara hands him the pencil, but only after he sets the pie on a clear corner of the table. The pie slides anyway; Pava catches it before it lands in the seed tray.

ACT TWO-D

INT. GREENHOUSE - LATE AFTERNOON

The camera operator sets a dramatic lamp beside the seed tray. Nara moves it away from the young shoots.

NARA: They need daylight, not a spotlight.

LENI: The evidence must be visible to the viewers.

KITO: It is visible if you stop putting props in front of it.

[LENI MOVES THE LAMP. ITS CORD TUGS THE EMPTY ENVELOPE OFF THE TABLE. PAVA CATCHES IT.]

PAVA: The packet has made another escape.

LENI: Was it pushed?

PAVA: By the cord you asked the crew to run through the paper bin.

ODO: That is an accomplice with a plug.

NARA: It is a lamp cord. Pull it out before it knocks over the tray.

[THE CAMERA OPERATOR UNPLUGS IT. Leni studies the empty envelope, then puts it beside the labeled seed jar rather than in the evidence box.]

LENI: The seeds are accounted for. The lamp and its cord are not evidence.

KITO: Can we finish watering now?

LENI: Yes. The case has reached its growing season.

ACT THREE

Leni announces that there was no theft: the empty packet had been planted, the spare seeds remained in their labeled jar, and the tray had simply been moved to avoid a leak. Odo gives the pie to the greenhouse crew. Pava marks the tray's location on the schedule.

LENI: The mystery was a packet becoming a garden.

KITO: That is what seeds do.

TAG

Leni finds a crumb beside the seed tray.

She leans in.

NARA: It is pie.
