# The Sewing Show Loses the Sleeve

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: live makeover craft shows that keep cutting fabric to fit a segment clock*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-018*

Cast: host Lela, a lemur; tailor Boro, an orangutan; guest Kima, a macaque; producer Savi, a capuchin; assistant Rito, a gibbon.

COLD OPEN

INT. SEWING STUDIO - DAY

Kima holds a jacket with one sleeve folded inside. A timer reads THREE MINUTES.

LELA: We will transform this garment before the break.

BORO: We should first find the other sleeve.

ACT ONE

INT. STUDIO - CONTINUOUS

Savi says the sleeve is probably in the fabric bin. Rito checks. He finds a curtain, two aprons and a sleeve from a different jacket. Lela asks Kima to model the garment. It fits, but the cuff is too long. Boro pins it in place. Savi asks him to cut immediately for visual impact.

BORO: I need to measure it first.

LELA: The clock is moving.

Kima points to a label inside the jacket. It says the sleeve was removed for repair and stored in the studio. Rito finds a paper tag in the bin but no sleeve. Savi asks the crew to search off camera. The timer reaches zero. Lela says the transformation is almost complete.

ACT TWO

INT. WARDROBE ROOM - LATER

Boro asks Kima what she wants changed. She wants the cuff shortened and the missing sleeve found. Savi proposes a contrasting fabric. Kima says she likes the jacket as it is. Lela offers a dramatic reveal with a scarf. Kima declines. Boro pins the cuff and marks the seam. The camera zooms on the scissors before he has cut anything.

[The crew searches the storage rack. Rito finds the missing sleeve attached to a hanger behind the lighting stand. It is still connected by a long basting thread to the jacket lining.]

RITO: The sleeve was here the whole time.

SAVI: That is a twist.

Boro checks the seam. The sleeve is intact; it was tucked inside the jacket. Lela asks whether they can say they discovered it. Kima turns the lining out and shows the thread. The camera captures the ordinary repair that needs to be done.

INT. STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Boro unpicks the basting thread. He shortens the cuff with a measured fold and stitches it by hand. The segment clock reaches zero again. Savi asks the timer operator to pause. The operator has no pause button, so Rito holds a card in front of the display. It reads STILL SEWING.

INT. WARDROBE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Rito returns the spare sleeve to the rack. It is the wrong color and has a label for another costume. Savi asks if they can attach it for the camera. Boro checks the seam and says it will not match. Kima puts the jacket on and moves both arms. The repaired cuff stays in place. Lela asks for a walking shot; Kima crosses to the table and reaches for the spool without catching the sleeve. The timer graphic counts down again, but no one looks at it. Rito places the recovered sleeve on its labeled hanger, not on Kima's jacket. Boro checks the finished seam once and returns the needle to its case. Kima keeps both sleeves. Savi turns off the timer, and the studio goes quiet. No cue sounds. Here.

ACT THREE

INT. SEWING STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Boro finishes the cuff and presses the seam. Kima tries on the jacket. The sleeve sits at her wrist. She moves her arm, reaches into the pocket and lifts a spool of thread from the table. The other sleeve remains in place. Lela asks for a turn. Kima turns once and shows the jacket's lining.

Savi asks Boro to add a decorative patch for a stronger before-and-after. He asks Kima first. She says no patch. Lela tries to cue applause. The studio audience claps when Kima says she can wear the jacket again. The clock graphic still shows three minutes because Rito has left the card over it. He removes it. The timer immediately resets to three.

Kima asks to keep the matching sleeve that had been inside the jacket. Boro folds it and puts it in her pocket. Lela asks whether the segment has a final reveal. Kima says the reveal is that both sleeves were always part of the garment. Savi accepts the line and drops the dramatic sting.

TAG

INT. STUDIO - AFTER THE SHOW

Rito labels the storage hanger JACKET SLEEVE. Boro returns the scissors to the sewing table. Savi unplugs the segment clock.

FINAL IMAGE: Kima exits wearing the jacket, both sleeves visible, while the stopped timer sits beside a neatly threaded needle.
