# The Mayor in the Mirror

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: political satire*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV*

Premise: A mayor campaigning for a second term discovers that the town's giant mirror sculpture has been installed backwards, reflecting the council instead of the citizens.

Cast: Mayor Bramble, a smooth-talking macaque; Kit, a young civic clerk; Doss, a skeptical baboon street cleaner; Mira, an orangutan sculptor; and Councilor Pip, who agrees with whoever spoke last.

## Episode: "A Better Reflection"

### Cold open

EXT. TOWN SQUARE, DAY

Mayor Bramble unveils the new civic mirror. A sheet falls away. The mirror faces the council building and reflects the mayor's office windows.

BRAMBLE: A symbol of transparency.

Doss sweeps the steps. His reflection appears behind the mayor, holding a broom like a scepter.

DOSS: It already has a cleaner government.

### Act one

The mayor commissioned the mirror for his reelection campaign. The plan says it should face the square, but the contractor turned it toward the council building.

Bramble calls the mistake intentional. Pip agrees. Doss points out that the mirror blocks the route from the bus stop to the market.

The sculptor, Mira, says the mirror's base can rotate if the council approves a new anchor. She designed it to reflect people in the square, not officials at their desks. Bramble asks whether a reflection of the council might inspire confidence. Mira says the council already has windows.

### Act two

Bramble schedules a press event. Doss marks a clear path in chalk, but the mirror covers one corner.

The council debates whether moving the mirror will look like admitting a mistake. Pip proposes changing the plaque to read "The Council Looks Outward." Bramble likes the phrase and asks Kit to add it to the press release. Kit reads the original plan aloud. It clearly says the mirror should face the square.

Mira asks residents what they want. A child wants to see her face; a vendor wants to check her fur; Doss needs room for his cart.

The anchor bolts cannot be moved until the base is unloaded. The crane operator has gone to another job. Mira says she can help rotate the sculpture with a manual winch, but the council must close the square for a few hours. Bramble wants to postpone until after the election rally. The vendors say the market is this weekend.

### Act three

Bramble cancels the press event and asks the council to help clear the square. Pip volunteers to carry the chalk buckets. Doss redirects foot traffic around the fountain. Mira and the crew loosen the anchors, then turn the mirror toward the open plaza. The move takes longer than expected because the stone base has settled unevenly.

When the mirror faces the square, residents can see themselves beside one another. The bus route is clear again. The vendor checks her fur and finds a leaf stuck above her ear. The child stands in the center and waves. The reflection waves back.

Bramble gives a brief statement. He says the installation did not match its plan, and the town helped correct it. He removes the campaign banner from the mirror and asks Kit to post the full minutes, including the complaints. Pip says he agrees with the decision. Doss asks whether Pip agreed before or after Bramble spoke.

Pip considers this. He says he agreed after hearing the residents, which is a new experience. Mira pins the original plan to the public notice board. The town's market committee begins setting up stalls around the sculpture. Bramble's reelection posters remain on the lampposts, but one has been covered by the clear-path map.

### Tag

Bramble passes the mirror alone and pauses to inspect his reflection. Behind him, a crowd uses the square. He steps aside so they can see themselves.

Doss sweeps one leaf from the base.

DOSS: The mirror is doing more listening now.

After the market closes, Kit brings the mayor a copy of the comments residents left beside the notice board. Several ask for benches and a lamp near the bus stop. Bramble circles the requests and sets them on the next council agenda. He tells Kit to leave the public comments visible, even the one that calls him "Mayor of His Own Reflection." Kit asks whether the comment needs a spelling correction. Bramble says no. Doss walks by with his broom and points out that the mirror now catches the bus arriving, so waiting passengers can see it before it turns the corner.
