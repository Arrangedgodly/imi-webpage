# The Ball Never Leaves the Court

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: televised sports commentary, slow-motion replays, and rule reviews that turn ordinary bounces into disputed history*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-011*

Premise: The Canopy Doubles final is delayed by a camera crew, a curled tail, and a ball that keeps rolling under the net.

Cast: Commentator Vex, a gibbon; analyst Omi, an orangutan; players Nari, a macaque, and Palla, a capuchin; referee Rell, a ring-tailed lemur; and replay operator Tavi, a mandrill.

## Episode: "Point of Contact"

### Cold Open

[INDOOR COURT. THE BALL ROLLS UNDER THE NET.]

VEX: A brilliant pass!

OMI: The point has not started.

PALLA: The ball was looking for shade.

[CUT TO TITLE.]

### Act One

[COMMENTARY BOOTH. CAMERAS TRACK THE PLAYERS.]

The Canopy Doubles final begins. Vex introduces the teams as rivals who have trained for months. Omi says they are neighbors who have played twice. Nari and Palla warm up by tapping the ball back and forth. Nari's tail curls around the racket handle, changing the grip.

Rell warns that tails cannot touch the ball. Palla asks whether a tail can hold the racket. The rulebook says the racket may be held by any part of the player.

VEX: That could be a strategic advantage.

OMI: It could also be a tangled tail.

The first serve hits the net and rolls under it. Rell calls a let. Vex announces a dramatic near-point. Omi says it did not count. The audience claps anyway.

### Act Two

[COURT. SECOND SERVE.]

Nari serves. The ball bounces on Palla's side, then hits the court line. Palla returns it and misses. Rell awards the point to Nari. Tavi plays the replay in slow motion. From one angle, the ball appears to touch the line. From another, it appears to land outside.

VEX: We have a line call.

OMI: We have two camera angles and one ball.

Palla challenges the call. Rell reviews the replay. The footage shows the ball bouncing exactly on the painted line.

RELL: The ball is in.

PALLA: I thought it was out.

NARI: It was on the line.

PALLA: That is why there is a line.

The teams reset. The camera crew moves to the opposite side. The camera stand partly blocks the sideline. Rell asks them to move. Tavi shifts it and accidentally starts a second replay.

VEX: The match is now occurring twice.

OMI: Only the replay is occurring.

Nari's tail taps the ball while she adjusts her grip. Rell calls a fault. Nari says she did not touch it; the tail touched the ball by accident. Palla asks for a replay. The footage shows a tiny brush. Rell awards the point to Palla.

Vex analyzes the replay frame by frame. Tavi zooms in until the image becomes a blur of fur and yellow ball. Omi asks him to return to the original view. The replay confirms a touch, but does not show whether Nari intended it.

RELL: Intent does not change the fault.

NARI: I will keep my tail behind the line.

PALLA: Mine is already behind me.

The players take a short break. The scoreboard technician discovers the score has been entered twice because Tavi replayed the point. Rell checks the paper scorecard, corrects the display, and calls for play to resume. Vex narrates the correction as a major momentum shift.

OMI: The numbers are back to the actual score.

VEX: The match has returned to reality.

### Act Three

[FINAL GAME. SCORE TIED.]

The players are tied at the final point. Vex lowers his voice for the decisive moment. Omi asks him not to narrate over the serve. Nari serves. The ball clips the net, lands on Palla's side, and rolls toward the corner. Palla reaches it, but her tail catches on the net post. She pulls free, returns the ball, and it bounces twice before Nari can reach it.

Rell calls the point for Palla. The crowd applauds. Nari asks for the replay. The camera shows the ball bounced twice, but the second bounce happened after Palla returned it. The point stands.

NARI: You won the rally.

PALLA: I almost got tangled in the net.

NARI: You got untangled.

Rell presents the trophy. The players share it because the event's doubles award recognizes both partners. Vex tries to summarize the match as a fierce rivalry. Omi says it was a close game between two teams who kept helping each other retrieve the ball.

### Tag

[COURT AFTER THE MATCH.]

Tavi retrieves the ball from beneath the net. It rolls back under. Vex begins another dramatic commentary. Omi lifts the net to stop it.

VEX: A bold defensive move.

OMI: I am fixing the net.

The players leave the court with the shared trophy. Rell checks the line paint before the next match.

[CUT TO BLACK.]
