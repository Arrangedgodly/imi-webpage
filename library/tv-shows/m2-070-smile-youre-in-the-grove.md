# Smile, You're in the Grove

*Format: TV show parody*
*Parody target: hidden-camera prank shows, elaborate setups, and overlong reveal reactions*
*By: IMI Television Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-TV-003*

Premise: A prank-show crew rigs a fake broken branch to surprise a park keeper, but the keeper has already noticed the cameras and prepares a counter-prank.

Cast: Host Jori, a macaque; park keeper Venn, a gorilla; prank planner Seli, a lemur; camera operator Tebb, a capuchin; and sound mixer Naru, a baboon.

## Episode: "The Branch That Wouldn't Break"

### Cold open

EXT. PARK PATH, DAY

[A large branch hangs low across the path. A sign reads "DO NOT TOUCH." Venn approaches with a broom.]

JORI: We are about to surprise Venn with a fake branch collapse.

SELI: The branch is made of foam.

TEBB: The camera is hidden in the birdhouse.

NARU: The birdhouse is facing the wall.

[The branch drops gently onto a leaf pile. Venn stops, looks at the birdhouse, and walks away.]

JORI: That was the reaction?

SELI: He didn't see it.

VENN: I saw it.

### Act One

EXT. PARK PATH

[Jori steps out from behind a tree. Venn sweeps leaves into a pile.]

JORI: Smile, you're in the Grove!

VENN: I know.

JORI: You knew about the prank?

VENN: The birdhouse had a red light.

TEBB: That's the recording light.

VENN: It was pointed at the wall.

SELI: We had a second camera.

VENN: I saw the reflection in the puddle.

NARU: The sound mic was in the bush.

VENN: The bush was moving.

JORI: The setup took three days.

VENN: The branch fell once.

SELI: We can do another take.

VENN: The branch is made of foam.

Jori: We will build a better prank.

VENN: The path is closed for maintenance.

SELI: We can pretend the path is open.

VENN: The sign says closed.

JORI: That is our clue.

### Act Two

EXT. PARK TOOL SHED

[Seli rigs a sign to fall when Venn opens the shed. Tebb hides behind a barrel.]

SELI: When he opens the door, the sign falls.

VENN: The door is already open.

JORI: Why did you leave it open?

VENN: The sign is blocking the hinge.

[He lifts the sign. A foam branch rolls out of the shed.]

TEBB: The branch has escaped.

NARU: We have a rolling sound effect.

[He makes a drumroll with his fingers. The branch rolls toward Jori.]

JORI: Watch out!

[Jori jumps onto a bench. The branch stops against the broom.]

VENN: Are you okay?

JORI: Yes.

VENN: You are the one who got pranked.

SELI: We can use that.

JORI: No. We need a reaction shot from Venn.

VENN: I can react.

[He looks at the foam branch and gasps very slightly.]

JORI: Bigger.

VENN: It is foam.

JORI: Imagine it is a real branch.

VENN: I work with real branches.

NARU: The gasp is clean.

### The second setup

EXT. TOOL SHED

[Seli rigs a harmless bucket of leaves above a chair. Jori sits in the chair.]

SELI: Venn will open the shed door and the leaves will fall.

JORI: The chair is mine.

SELI: You're supposed to be behind the camera.

JORI: You said the setup was ready.

[The bucket tips. Leaves fall on Jori.]

NARU: We have a reaction.

JORI: That was not the target.

VENN: You said the prank was for me.

JORI: It was.

VENN: The leaves are on you.

JORI: The camera can see both of us.

TEBB: The hidden camera is pointed at the floor.

### Act Three

EXT. PARK PATH, LATER

[Venn asks Jori to inspect a new "danger" sign. Jori approaches cautiously.]

JORI: Is this a prank?

VENN: I don't know.

[He points. The sign says "CAMERA CREW PARKING ONLY." Jori laughs.]

SELI: The keeper has pranked the host.

JORI: This is not the official prank.

VENN: It is the only one that worked.

TEBB: The camera has been caught on the sign.

NARU: The sound is just leaves.

JORI: We can cut to the reveal.

VENN: What is the reveal?

JORI: The reveal is that the prank show crew got pranked.

SELI: That is the show.

JORI: We need a big reaction.

VENN: You want me to gasp?

JORI: Yes.

[Venn lifts the sign. A small basket of apples is underneath.]

VENN: I have snacks.

JORI: That is a good reveal.

SELI: Were the apples part of the prank?

VENN: No. They are for the crew.

JORI: That's kind.

VENN: I knew you would be here all day.

### Tag

INT. EDIT ROOM, NIGHT

[Jori watches the footage. The best reaction is his jump from the bench.]

JORI: We will call it "The Host Takes a Fall."

[On the monitor, Venn quietly sweeps the foam branch into the leaf pile.]
