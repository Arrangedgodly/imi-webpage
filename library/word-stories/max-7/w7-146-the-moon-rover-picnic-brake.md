# The moon rover picnic brake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space vehicles lack practical brakes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Driver Halo, Builder Iris, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Halo parked the moon rover beside a hill for lunch. It rolled down while the monkey peeled bananas. Iris chased it with a rope and tied it to a rock. Halo found the parking brake loose under his seat. They fixed the bolt, then tested it on a small slope. The rover stayed still. Halo put the rope in the tool box and ate his lunch on flat ground before the next drive.
