# The ginger hut lease

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Edible houses in the woods*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Baker Bess, Hans Hal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hal found a hut made of cake and bit the wall. Bess came out with a rent bill. The monkey said he thought it was a free sample. Bess showed him the plain door and asked him to mend the hole before rain came. Hal baked a brick of oats and bananas. It fit snugly. Bess hung a Sample sign on a tray outside, and Hal became her first regular guest.
