# The tiny ray rescue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Shrink rays solve the wrong problem*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hank, Mayor Miri, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hank shrank a jammed cart to get it through the gate. The bananas inside became so small that the monkeys needed a lens to eat them. Mayor Miri asked him to undo the ray. Hank grew the cart back outside, then took off its wheels and carried it through on its side. Miri paid him with one full banana. Hank used the ray only on the empty gate key, which fit his pocket much better.
