# The case of the extra tail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mysteries solved by costume inspection*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Laurel, Actor Alder, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Alder walked into the hall with two tails. Laurel feared a strange spell. The monkey actor showed her one tail, then the other, both moving in time. She asked him to turn round. A string led from the false tail to his wrist. Alder was testing a costume for a two headed beast. Laurel helped hide the string under his fur. They ate bananas after the test, and the spare tail became a curtain tie.
