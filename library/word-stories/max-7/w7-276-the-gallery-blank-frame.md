# The gallery blank frame

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Modern art values empty gestures*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Artist Fenn, Guest Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fenn hung an empty frame and called it the essence of bananas. Fern asked the monkey artist why the real fruit sat hidden in a drawer. Fenn said the gap invited thought. Fern put one banana in the frame and let guests draw its shape on scrap paper. They stayed longer than before. Fenn served the fruit after the session and hung the art next day, with the same frame around the newest sketch.
