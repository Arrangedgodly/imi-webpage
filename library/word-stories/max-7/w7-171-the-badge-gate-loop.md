# The badge gate loop

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Access controls lock out their owners*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Guard Pine, Worker Elm, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Elm left his badge on his desk behind the locked gate. Pine asked the monkey worker to show it before entry. Elm pointed through the bars, where the badge lay in plain sight. Pine could see it but could not reach. Elm hooked it with his tail through the rail and held it up. Pine opened the gate. They put a spare badge locker outside and shared bananas while they fitted a simple lock.
