# The morale plant

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate wellness replaces needed resources*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Cori, Clerk Cypress, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cori gave Cypress a plant to boost morale. The monkey clerk had asked for a new lamp, since his desk sat in the dark. The plant drooped there too. Cypress moved it to the bright window and took the window desk as well. Cori noticed that both looked better. He bought a lamp for the old desk and kept the plant by the sun. Cypress named it Request and watered it at lunch each day.
