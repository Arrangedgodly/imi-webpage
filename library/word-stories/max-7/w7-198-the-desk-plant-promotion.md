# The desk plant promotion

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Managers promote visible proxies*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss River, Clerk Rain, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

River praised Rain for the tallest plant in the office. Rain wanted credit for her work, but the monkey boss watched only the leaves. She moved the plant to his desk and left a file summary on hers. River watered the plant all week and found it quite hard. He read the summary at last and gave Rain the lead role. She took the files to the next floor and left him clear notes about water.
