# The mirror takes a day off

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic mirrors and vanity*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Queen Bea, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Queen Bea asked her mirror who had the best fur. The mirror gave no reply. Dot, the monkey who cleaned it, found a leaf stuck over its mouth. Bea pulled the leaf away. The mirror asked who had the best manners. Bea brought Dot a chair and some bananas while she worked. The mirror praised the clean glass. Bea liked that answer enough to stop asking about her own fur.
