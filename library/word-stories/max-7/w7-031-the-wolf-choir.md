# The wolf choir

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Disguised predators at doors*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Wolf Walt, Kids Kira, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Walt wore a wolf suit to test the door guard. Kira asked him to sing the family song before she let him in. His voice was so rough that all the young monkeys laughed. He showed his bare tail, but Kira still said no. Walt fetched his lute and learned the tune from a window. At dusk she opened the door. He brought bananas, and they gave him the low part in their choir.
