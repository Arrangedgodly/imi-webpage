# A slipper for the wrong foot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical ballroom footwear*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Moss, Prince Jem, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Moss left one glass shoe at the ball. Prince Jem toured the woods with it, sure he would find his bride. Each monkey tried a foot. Moss tried his tail. The shoe fit that best, since he had worn it as a tail bell all night. Jem felt a bit silly. They hung the shoe over the gate and rang it when fresh bananas came to the hall.
