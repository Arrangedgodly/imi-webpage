# The clue under the hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detectives misplace their own evidence*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Detective Dale, Assistant Ari, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dale found a key beside the empty fruit box. He hid it in his hat, then checked the room for hours. Ari asked the monkey to take a break and brush his fur. The key fell onto the desk. It opened the box in the next room, where all the bananas sat safe from ants. Dale thanked Ari and made a tray for clues. His hat held only his head on the next case.
