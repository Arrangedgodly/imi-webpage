# The rescue robot spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Hero gadgets with narrow purposes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hilda, Builder Bolt, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bolt built Hilda a robot that could carry any spoon. Hilda wanted it to rescue a cart full of bananas. The machine brought her one spoon and sat down. The monkey hero tied the spoon to a rope, hooked the cart axle, and pulled. Bolt saw his robot needed a broader job list. He taught it to hold rope next. Hilda kept the spoon in her kit because it had already earned its place.
