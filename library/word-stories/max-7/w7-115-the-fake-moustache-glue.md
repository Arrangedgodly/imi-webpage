# The fake moustache glue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Disguises become permanent obstacles*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Moss, Barber Bea, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Moss glued a false lip patch over his monkey nose. It hid his face so well that Bea would not let him into her shop. He tried to state his secret name, but the glue sealed his mouth. Bea handed him warm water through the window. Moss melted the glue and peeled the hair away. Bea trimmed his real fur and fed him bananas. Moss used a loose hat for his next guise instead.
