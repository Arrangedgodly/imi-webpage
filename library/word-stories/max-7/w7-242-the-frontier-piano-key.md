# The frontier piano key

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western bars rely on one dramatic tune*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Player Lark, Owner Laurel, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark played one tense tune each time the saloon door opened. Laurel wanted a gentler song for lunch, but his piano had only six working keys. The monkey opened the lid and found bananas stacked against the strings. Laurel had stored them there in the cool dark space. They moved the fruit to a cabinet and tuned the freed keys. Lark played a happy lunch song, and the door could open with no hint of a duel.
