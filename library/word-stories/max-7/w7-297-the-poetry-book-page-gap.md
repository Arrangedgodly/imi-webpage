# The poetry book page gap

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Poets use blank space excessively*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Poet Fenn, Binder Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fenn placed one word on each page of his poem. Fern needed a thick cover for the monkey book, though the poem lasted only a minute aloud. She laid the pages side by side on one sheet and asked him to read it. The rhythm stayed the same. Fenn chose that version and spent the saved paper on another poem. Fern bound both in a thin book, which fit beside the bananas in his small travel bag.
