# The star map stain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space explorers follow flawed maps*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Pilot Pia, Navigator Nix, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nix spilled tea on the star map. Pia steered toward the brown spot and found no planet. The monkey pilot checked through the window and saw that home lay just below them. Nix wiped the map clean with his tail. The stain stayed, so he marked it Tea, Not Star. They landed at the proper port and bought bananas. Pia kept the marked map to remind them where a snack break should be.
