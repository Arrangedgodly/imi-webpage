# The radio silence cue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Radio drama needs understandable sounds*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Vale, Sound Wren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vale acted a grim silent glare in the radio studio. Wren waited, but the monkey fans heard only empty air. She asked him to speak one short line or rustle the note in his hand. Vale tore the prop note and said he would not sign. The scene became clear without a face to see. Wren saved the glare for the cast photo, then served bananas while Vale tried keeping his paper close to the mic.
