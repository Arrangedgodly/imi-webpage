# The team spirit costume

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Mandatory employee enthusiasm*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Bram, Worker Wil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bram gave each worker a bright team cape. Wil wore his over his monkey fur, but the cape dragged through glue at the packing bench. Every box stuck to him. Bram tried to help and stuck his own cape there too. Wil cut both capes free and used the cloth as packing pads. The boxes shipped safely. Bram ordered plain aprons next time and gave each worker bananas instead of another speech about team spirit.
