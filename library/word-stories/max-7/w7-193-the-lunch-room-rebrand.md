# The lunch room rebrand

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate rebranding ignores function*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Myrtle, Cook Moss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Myrtle renamed the lunch room the Fuel Hub. Moss asked for a new stove because the old one would not heat soup. The monkey boss ordered a shiny sign instead. Moss served cold soup beneath it. Myrtle took one taste and called the repair crew. They fixed the stove that day. Moss hung a small card under the sign that said Hot Lunch At Noon. All staff used that name, since it told them when to come.
