# The jewel ice cube

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Stolen jewels hidden in drinks*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Dune, Host Hana, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hana lost a blue gem at the feast. Dune checked every monkey pocket and found only crumbs. His own drink had a blue ice cube. He let it melt in a dish, and the gem fell out. Hana had dropped it while filling the ice tray. Dune gave it back and drank plain water. Hana served bananas in dry bowls so no one had to wait for dessert to thaw.
