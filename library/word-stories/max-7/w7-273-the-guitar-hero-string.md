# The guitar hero string

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Musicians favor poses over equipment care*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Guitarist Cedar, Tech Cora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cedar struck a grand pose and broke his guitar string. Cora handed the monkey a spare while the crowd waited. He tried the pose again, but she pointed at the sharp edge on his guitar bridge. They filed it smooth and fitted the string. Cedar played a simple tune and did not twist the guitar behind his head. The crowd sang along. Cora got bananas in the wings, and Cedar kept the spare strings beside a small file in his case.
