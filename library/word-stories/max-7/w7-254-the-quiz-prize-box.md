# The quiz prize box

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Game shows offer unsuitable grand prizes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Host Dell, Player Dara, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara won the quiz and got a huge stone bowl. The monkey player lived in a small tree hut. Dell asked her to pose beside the prize while her tail shook as she tried to lift it. Dara asked to swap it for seeds. The show agreed. She planted bananas and sent a photo when the first fruit grew. Dell used that image in the next prize list, where each gift now had a choice of size.
