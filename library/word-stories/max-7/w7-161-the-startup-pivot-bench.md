# The startup pivot bench

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Constant business pivots*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Fern, Builder Bly, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern planned an app to find seats. Bly built a bench for the demo. The monkey founder changed the plan to a snack app, so Bly put bananas on the bench. Fern changed again to a fitness app. Bly asked guests to walk to the bench for lunch. They paid for the food and sat down happily. Fern kept the bench service and dropped the app. Bly wrote the price on one plank in large clear letters.
