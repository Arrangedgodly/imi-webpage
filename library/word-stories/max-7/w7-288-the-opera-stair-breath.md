# The opera stair breath

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Opera grandeur ignores practical stamina*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Singer Reed, Director Rue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Reed climbed six flights before singing his grand aria. The monkey had no breath left when Rue raised the baton. Rue asked him to arrive earlier or use the lift. Reed came early next night, warmed his voice slowly, and sang the full tune. Rue moved the heavy costume up above in advance. Reed could eat bananas after the show instead of gasping on the stairs, and the guests heard every word of the final song.
