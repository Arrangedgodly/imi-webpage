# The reunion show missing chairs

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Television reunions focus on conflict*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Host Jade, Guest Jun, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade booked eight guests and six chairs for the reunion show. Jun refused to fight over a seat. The monkey fetched two crates and put soft pads on them. Jade tried to ask who hated whom, but Jun asked who could help move the set after filming. All eight raised hands. They did the work before tea and bananas arrived. Jade aired that scene, which got more praise than the old clips of their rows.
