# The hidden hero badge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret identities and costume changes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hen, Friend Fay, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hen changed into her hero suit behind the fruit stall. Fay saw her tail under the curtain and knew at once. The monkey begged her to keep the secret. Fay agreed, but the suit had no room for the bananas Hen had just bought. Fay lent her a plain bag. Hen saved a kite from a roof and came home with the fruit. Fay kept the badge safe while Hen washed the dusty suit.
