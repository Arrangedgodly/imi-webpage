# The invisible ship ticket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cloaked spacecraft and parking rules*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Pilot Pea, Guard Gil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pea hid her ship with a cloak beam to avoid the port fee. Gil, a monkey guard, heard her tail drag down the ramp. He put a ticket on the empty air. Pea could not find it until she turned the beam off. She paid the fee in bananas and asked for a visible parking post. Gil gave her one. Pea left the beam off at the port and found her ship without bruised toes.
