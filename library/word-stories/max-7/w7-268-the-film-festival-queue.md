# The film festival queue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Prestige film events impede viewing*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Director Sable, Usher Snow, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sable brought a short film to the film fair. The entry speech took longer than the film, and the monkey viewers waited with tired tails. Snow cut the speech to one line and started the reel. All guests saw the full story before the hall closed. Sable thanked Snow and served bananas outside after the credits. At the next event she printed her notes on a small card, leaving the screen for the film itself.
