# The slow sprint camera

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Photo finishes in racing*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Runner Rue, Judge Finn, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rue trained for the sprint, but the race camera could see only tails. Every monkey curled a tail over the line before their feet arrived. Judge Finn could not choose a winner. Rue set a second line behind the first and asked each runner to touch it with a foot. They raced again without tricks. Rue came third and won bananas for fixing the rules. Finn used the tail photo as a funny poster.
