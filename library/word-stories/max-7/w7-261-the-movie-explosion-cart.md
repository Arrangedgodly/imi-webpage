# The movie explosion cart

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action films prioritize spectacle over sense*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Director Lark, Stunt Laurel, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark wanted Laurel to leap from a cart as smoke burst behind it. The monkey stunt actor asked where the cart would stop. Lark had planned only the leap. Laurel fitted a brake and marked a soft landing patch. They tested the route without smoke before filming. The shot looked grand and no fruit spilled from the prop crate. Laurel ate the bananas after the scene, while Lark added a stop point to every later stunt plan.
