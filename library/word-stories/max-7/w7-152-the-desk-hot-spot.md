# The desk hot spot

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Hot desk policies*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Manager Mina, Worker Wade, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina chose hot desks by daily vote. Wade got the desk beside the stove and singed his monkey fur. Mina said hot meant shared, but Wade asked for a cooler spot. They rolled the desk to the window and marked seats with clips instead of heat. Wade hung bananas from his clip during lunch. Mina banned food on desks, then took one because she had not brought a snack of her own.
