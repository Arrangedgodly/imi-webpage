# The moon cheese trial

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fables about impossible food*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Otto, Sage Tess, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Otto saw the moon in a pond and thought it was cheese. He told the other monkeys to drain the pond. Sage Tess put a bowl of water beside it. The moon shone in that too. Otto tried a spoon and got only water on his fur. Tess gave him cheese on a leaf with ripe bananas. He ate that and left the moon in the pond so fish could have a lamp.
