# The permit for a chair

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Licensing ordinary office furniture*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Inspector Iris, Clerk Rowan, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rowan bought a chair but could not sit until Iris cleared it. Iris tested the legs with her monkey hands, then asked for a diagram of the tail hole. Rowan stood to draw it. Iris sat to review it and found the seat quite safe. Rowan asked if that counted as a test. Iris stamped the form and stood up. Rowan finally sat, ate bananas, and used the cleared diagram to make a cushion that fit the hole.
