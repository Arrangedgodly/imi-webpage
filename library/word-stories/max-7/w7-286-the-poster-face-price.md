# The poster face price

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Advertising images omit actual products*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Designer Pine, Seller Pax, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pine made a poster with a huge monkey face and no fruit. Pax asked how guests would know he sold bananas. Pine said the face looked warm. Pax set a real bunch beside the draft and asked for its shape on the page. Pine drew it and added the price. More guests came to the stall that day. He kept the face small in the corner, where it could smile without hiding what was for sale.
