# The decoder drum

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret codes interpreted too dramatically*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Rue, Musician Min, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rue heard taps through the wall and thought a spy sent a code. The monkey wrote each beat down, then fed the marks into his decoder. It said Bring Bananas. Min opened the next door with a drum under her arm. She was playing a new tune. Rue brought fruit anyway and asked her to play it again. His decoder made a fine beat clock once he stopped asking it to read secret notes.
