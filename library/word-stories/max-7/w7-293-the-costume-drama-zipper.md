# The costume drama zipper

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical costumes use anachronistic fasteners*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Birch, Dresser Bea, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Birch wore a royal coat in the period play. The monkey could not hide the bright zipper down its back. Bea sewed a flap over it, but caught his tail in the stitch. They undid the seam and made a new tail opening. The coat looked old from the seats and came off easily in the wings. Birch thanked Bea with bananas and hung the coat on a wide hook, so the flap would not crease before the next day.
