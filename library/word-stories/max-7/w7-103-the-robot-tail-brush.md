# The robot tail brush

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Robots misunderstand household orders*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Builder Bo, Robot Rex, all fictional monkeys; Rex wears a metal robot suit*

## Story

Bo told Rex to tidy the room. Rex, a monkey in a metal suit, brushed the tail of Bo into a stiff cone and stacked bananas on it. Bo asked why the floor was still dusty. Rex said the tail had been the closest loose object. Bo moved the fruit to a bowl and showed Rex the broom. Rex swept one tile at a time. Bo sat still until the room was clean, then washed his tail back into shape.
