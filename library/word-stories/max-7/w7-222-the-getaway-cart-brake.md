# The getaway cart brake

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist escapes undone by small mechanics*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Driver Drift, Planner Dune, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dune planned a heist of the prize melon. Drift waited in the getaway cart with his monkey tail over the brake. The melon rolled onto the cart, but the cart would not move. Dune pushed until the owner arrived. Drift lifted his tail, then chose to stay. They gave back the melon and paid for its bruise with bananas. The owner hired them to move the rest of the crop, after testing the brake first.
