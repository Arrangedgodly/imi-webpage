# The printer jam chart

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Root cause charts replace repairs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Linden, Clerk Lilac, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Linden drew a chart to explain why the printer jammed. Lilac opened its tray and found a twig inside. The monkey clerk pulled it out with her tail and printed the chart. Linden studied the page, which blamed low morale. Lilac taped the twig over those words. He laughed and rewrote the cause as Wood In Tray. They posted the new chart beside the machine and kept all plant pots on a second shelf.
