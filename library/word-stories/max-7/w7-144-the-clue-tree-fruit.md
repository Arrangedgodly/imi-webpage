# The clue tree fruit

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mystery clues are mistaken for snacks*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Grove, Scout Greer, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Grove hung red tags on limbs to mark a trail. Greer thought the tags meant ripe bananas and picked every bunch along it. He took the tags off as he went. The monkey sleuth came back to find no markers but a full basket. Greer showed him each branch he had climbed. Grove drew the route on paper while they walked. They found the lost camp tent beside the stream and ate the fruit there, with the map tucked safely under a stone.
