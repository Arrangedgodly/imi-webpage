# The midnight parcel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir suspense around mundane deliveries*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Gray, Baker Bram, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bram hired Gray to guard a parcel until twelve. The monkey sleuth kept it under his coat and watched every shadow. At twelve, Bram asked for it back and opened a box of cake candles. He had wanted them safe until his secret party. Gray lit each one and carried the cake inside. The guests cheered. Gray got a slice with bananas and a dry place to hang the coat he had kept shut all night.
