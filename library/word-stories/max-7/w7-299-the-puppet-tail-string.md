# The puppet tail string

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Puppetry rigs entangle performers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Puppeteer Haze, Helper Heath, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Haze tied puppet strings to her monkey tail for a new trick. The puppet danced, then pulled her back into the curtain. Heath held the stand and helped untie her. They put the strings on a small hand frame instead. Haze could bow without moving the puppet now. The show ended with a neat dance and no torn cloth. Heath gave the puppet a wooden banana, while Haze ate the real bananas after the curtain fell.
