# The startup bell schedule

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Productivity apps interrupt productive work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Dahlia, Coder Dax, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dahlia put in a bell that rang when Dax should change tasks. It rang while the monkey fixed a line of code, so he lost his place. Dax cut power to it and fixed the fault. Dahlia said the tracker showed no work done. Dax opened the working app on her screen. She tried it and rang the bell by hand in praise. They kept it for jobs done and let the clock handle lunch.
