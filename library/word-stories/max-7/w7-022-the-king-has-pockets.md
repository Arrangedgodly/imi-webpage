# The king has pockets

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Invisible royal clothes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: King Ned, Weaver Opal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Opal sold King Ned a suit that no one could see. Ned walked out in his fur and asked where to put his keys. The monkey weaver offered a bag. Ned asked why he had paid for a suit with no pockets. Opal gave back the bananas and sewed him a plain coat. At the parade Ned held up each pocket for the crowd. They cheered the coat because it could hold their lost keys.
