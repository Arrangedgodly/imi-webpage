# The lunch receipt ruler

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Expense audits obsess over tiny sums*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Auditor Sage, Clerk Sol, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sage checked the lunch receipt with a ruler. Sol had spent one coin extra on bananas. The monkey auditor asked for a note on the extra cost. Sol showed her the large fruit and the smaller one on sale. Sage weighed both and found the larger was cheaper per bite. She cleared the receipt after an hour. Sol offered her half his lunch, and Sage said she would weigh costs before paper next time.
