# The album cover soup

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Musicians confuse style with content*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Singer Dawn, Artist Dell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dawn chose a cover showing a bowl of soup for her album of love songs. Dell asked the monkey how it linked to the music. Dawn said the colors matched her fur. Dell played the songs and drew two tails linked instead. Dawn liked that better and kept the soup picture for a recipe card. The album sold well. Dell ate bananas at the launch and gave guests the soup card as a useful extra.
