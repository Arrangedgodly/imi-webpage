# The wolf stamp

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: False alarm fables*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Bo, Wren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo cried that a wolf had come. No wolf came, but all the monkeys left their work. Wren gave him a red stamp to use for real danger. Bo stamped his own fur just to test it. The mark would not wash off. When a wolf did pass, Bo held up his red tail. The troop shut the gate, then paid him bananas to guard it with a plain bell.
