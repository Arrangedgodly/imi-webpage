# The sitcom door key

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Sitcom friends enter without knocking*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Alf, Neighbor Nell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Alf burst into the flat every day for the sitcom. Nell finally changed the lock. The monkey actor stood outside while the laugh track played to no one. He knocked and asked to come in. Nell opened the door and offered bananas. The scene worked better with one real pause. Alf kept a knock in every later script, and Nell could finish her tea before his daily grand entry began.
