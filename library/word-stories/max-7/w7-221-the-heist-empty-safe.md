# The heist empty safe

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist crews steal before checking contents*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Plume, Picker Puck, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Plume drew a grand plan to steal a locked safe. Puck opened it in seconds, and the monkey crew found only dust. The bananas had been moved to the pantry because a safe had no airflow. Plume paid the owner for the broken lock and asked to work off the rest. Puck fitted pantry latches instead. They earned fair wages by keeping fruit safe, and no longer hauled a steel box through the woods at night.
