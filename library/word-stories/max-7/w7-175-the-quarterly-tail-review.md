# The quarterly tail review

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Performance reviews measure irrelevant traits*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Willow, Worker Wisp, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Willow rated Wisp by how often his tail moved at work. The monkey clerk could file pages without moving it at all. Willow gave him low marks despite the empty inbox. Wisp tied a ribbon to a fan so it waved beside his chair. Willow praised his energy, then noticed the trick. They counted the neat stacks instead. Wisp got his fair score and a bowl of bananas, while the ribbon waved over the fan.
