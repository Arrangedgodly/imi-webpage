# The salad trophy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Health food contests with absurd prizes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Sage, Judge Jay, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sage made a salad in the shape of a trophy. Jay gave the monkey a real trophy for it. Sage tried to eat that one and found it much too hard. Jay said prizes were for shelves. Sage had no shelf, only a small hut full of bananas. He traded the trophy for seeds and grew a garden. At the next contest he served bowls and asked for an empty sack as his prize.
