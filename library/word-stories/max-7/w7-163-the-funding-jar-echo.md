# The funding jar echo

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Investors praise empty ventures*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Gale, Investor Ike, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gale brought an empty jar to Ike and called it a new fruit hub. Ike tapped it and liked the echo. The monkey founder asked for cash to make more empty jars. Ike instead put bananas inside and asked Gale to sell them. Gale sold every jar at the market that day. He brought the bills back without a fancy name. Ike funded the next crop, and Gale used the echo jar to store small coins.
