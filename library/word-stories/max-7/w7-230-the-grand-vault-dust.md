# The grand vault dust

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist payoff becomes cleaning debt*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Opal, Guard Orin, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Opal broke into the old vault and found years of dust. Orin closed the door behind the monkey and asked who would pay to clean the mess. Opal put down his bag and picked up a broom. He swept until the floor shone, then mended the latch. Orin opened the door and gave him a receipt for the repair debt now paid. Opal used his bag to carry bananas home instead of stolen gold.
