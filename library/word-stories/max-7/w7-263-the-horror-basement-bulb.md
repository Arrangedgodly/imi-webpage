*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*

## Story

Nix went down into the cellar with a dying torch. Nell held a box of fresh bulbs beside the monkey actor. The script called for dark scenes, but Nix could not see the stairs. He fitted a new bulb before the camera rolled. The scene still felt eerie because the door creaked behind him. Nell stayed close with the spare torch. They found the prop bananas on the bottom shelf and did the shot without falling over them.
