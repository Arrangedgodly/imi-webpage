# The x ray lunchbox

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Super vision reveals mundane secrets*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hex, Guard Gage, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hex used his x ray gaze to check a sealed box at the gate. He saw long curved shapes and cried that it held secret weapons. Gage opened the box to show bananas. The monkey hero blushed beneath his mask. Gage asked him to read the label before using his gift. Hex checked the next box and found towels. He carried both boxes to the hall and used plain sight to serve lunch.
