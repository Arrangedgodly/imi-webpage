# The calendar chair block

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Calendar tools and workplace territory*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Manager Flora, Clerk Finch, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Flora booked every chair in the planner so her team would have space. Finch arrived alone and found the monkey hall empty but fully booked. He sat on the floor to mend a file box. Flora came in and tripped over his tail. Finch showed her the unused chairs. She cleared all but the two needed for a call. Finch sat at last, and the file box stayed safely beneath the desk.
