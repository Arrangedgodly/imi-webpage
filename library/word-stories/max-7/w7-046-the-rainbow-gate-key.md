# The rainbow gate key

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Quest objects hidden in riddles*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Rin, Wizard Wil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wil told Rin that a key lay at the end of the rainbow. Rin crossed fields but the colors moved with her. The monkey came back wet and cross. Wil looked at his own wall and saw the key hung under a rainbow artwork. He had left out that part. Rin opened the garden, picked bananas, and painted a map on the gate. Wil now used that map when he gave advice.
