# The ice rink broom

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports surfaces need humble maintenance*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Skater Sia, Keeper Kip, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia tried a grand spin on the rink and hit a leaf. Her monkey tail swung out and she sat down hard. Kip offered to sweep before her next turn. Sia took the broom and pushed it while she skated a simple circle. The rink cleared, and she found a steady rhythm. Her final spin worked. Kip gave her bananas, and the broom got a place beside her shiny cup in the display case.
