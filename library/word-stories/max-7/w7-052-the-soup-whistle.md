# The soup whistle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Soup chefs who forbid noise*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Cook Cleo, Pip, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo asked for silence in her soup shop. Pip slurped once and got sent outside. The young monkey heard the pot whistle behind him. Cleo ran out to scold the sound, but Pip pointed at the stove. The pot was too full. He held a bowl while she poured some out. They added bananas to cool it. Cleo put a bell by the door and let guests ring it for more soup.
