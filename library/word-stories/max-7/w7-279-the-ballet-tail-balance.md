# The ballet tail balance

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Dance traditions ignore performers bodies*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Dancer Iris, Coach Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Iris tucked her tail into a belt for the ballet. The monkey tipped to one side on each turn. Ivy asked why she hid the tail, and Iris pointed to a rule in the old book. Ivy undid the belt and let her try again. Iris spun neatly with her tail held out. They changed the rule and marked the safe space around each dancer. Iris ate bananas after class, her tail resting freely over the bench.
