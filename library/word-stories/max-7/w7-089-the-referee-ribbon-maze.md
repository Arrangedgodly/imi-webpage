# The referee ribbon maze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports rules become overcomplicated*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Ref Rex, Runner Rose, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex tied ribbons across the track to mark each rule. Rose could not see a clear way through. The monkey referee blew his whistle when her tail touched one ribbon, then tripped on another himself. Rose helped untie them and laid one ribbon along the outside edge. Rex kept the rules on a card in his pocket. The race began late, but all the bananas stayed on the prize table until it ended.
