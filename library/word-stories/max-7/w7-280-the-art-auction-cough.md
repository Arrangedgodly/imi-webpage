# The art auction cough

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Art auctions mistake accidental sounds for bids*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Auctioneer Jade, Guest Jun, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jun coughed at the auction and won a giant picture. The monkey guest had meant to bid on nothing. Jade paused the sale and asked for a clear raised hand. Jun drank water and kept both hands on his knees. Another guest bought the picture with a real bid. Jade placed number cards at each seat for later bids. Jun bought bananas in the lobby, where a price tag could not mistake his cough for a promise.
