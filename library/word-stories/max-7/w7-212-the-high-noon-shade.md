# The high noon shade

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western showdowns and inconvenient heat*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sheriff Sunny, Rival Rust, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sunny called Rust to a duel at noon. Both monkeys stood in the dust with dry mouths and hot fur. Rust asked if they could settle the cattle fence dispute under a tree. Sunny agreed. They drew the field border in the shade and split the cost of a new gate. No shots fired. They ate bananas at one, then spent two hours fitting hinges while the sun moved off the road.
