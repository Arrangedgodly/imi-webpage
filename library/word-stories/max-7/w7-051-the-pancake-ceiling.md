# The pancake ceiling

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking contests and showy flips*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Chip, Judge Jo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Chip flipped his pancake so high it stuck to the ceiling. Judge Jo waited with her fork. The monkey chef climbed a shelf, but his tail knocked flour over the stove. Jo handed him a mop. Chip swept the cake down onto a clean tray and served it with bananas. Jo gave him a prize for floor care. He hung it at eye level and kept each new flip below it.
