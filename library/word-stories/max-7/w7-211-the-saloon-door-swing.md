# The saloon door swing

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western entrances become hazards*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sheriff Sage, Owner Oak, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sage strode through the saloon doors, which swung back and caught his monkey tail. Oak helped him free it before the piano tune ended. Sage wanted the doors fixed before the next grand entry. Oak fitted soft edges and a catch that held them open. Sage tested the walk slowly, then ordered bananas at the bar. He kept one hand on the door when he left, so the next guest could come in safely.
