# The frozen yogurt race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Kitchen contests depend on temperature*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Frost, Judge Jade, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Frost made yogurt so cold that the spoon froze to his fur. Jade waited for a taste while the monkey hopped around the kitchen. He held his tail near a warm lamp until the spoon fell free. Jade asked for small cups that thawed faster. Frost filled them and added bananas at the last moment. The judges ate before the next freeze. Frost won a warm pair of gloves and used them to carry the cups.
