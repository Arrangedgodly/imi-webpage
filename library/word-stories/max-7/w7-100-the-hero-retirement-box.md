# The hero retirement box

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroes pass on their gear*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hale, Student Star, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hale packed his hero mask for his rest. Star wanted it, but the box also held bills for every broken window Hale had left behind. The young monkey read them and took a broom instead. Star swept the glass from the last rescue while Hale paid for repairs. They ate bananas on the new porch. Hale gave Star the mask at sunset, and Star put a repair fund beside it before she planned her first patrol.
