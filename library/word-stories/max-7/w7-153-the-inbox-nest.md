# The inbox nest

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Unread email overload*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Corin, Boss Brin, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Corin printed every unread note and built a nest beneath his desk. Brin wanted the monkey to answer them all. Corin sorted the pages by date and found most asked about a feast held last year. He filed those in a box and handled the three new queries. Brin gave him bananas for lunch. Corin used the empty nest as a foot rest, and Brin stopped sending each note to every desk.
