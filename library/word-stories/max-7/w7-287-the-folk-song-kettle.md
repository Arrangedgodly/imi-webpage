# The folk song kettle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Folk music turns every object into lore*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Singer Quill, Cook Quartz, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Quill wrote a ballad about the old kettle. The monkey claimed it had crossed seas and lasted through wars. Quartz showed him the shop receipt from last spring. Quill rewrote the song about a kettle that made good tea each day. The chorus was short enough for guests to join. Quartz served bananas during the encore. Quill sang the true price as a joke, and all guests knew what the useful little kettle had really done.
