# The spy pigeon basket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Carrier animals ignore mission priorities*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Dove, Keeper Dovea, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dove sent a message by pigeon, but the bird landed at the fruit stall. Dovea found it pecking bananas beside the monkey agent. Dove saw he had used a page covered in fruit stains. He wrote a clean copy and tucked it into a dry tube. Dovea sent the bird again. It reached the right hut before dusk. Dove washed his desk and ate the ripe fruit far from his paper stack.
