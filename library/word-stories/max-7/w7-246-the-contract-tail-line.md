# The contract tail line

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Contracts omit necessary physical details*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Lawyer Pine, Client Pax, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pax signed a deal to sit for a picture. The bench had no tail gap, and the monkey could not keep still. Pine read the deal and found it offered only a seat. Pax asked the painter to cut a notch or refund his fee. The painter cut it, then did the picture in an hour. Pine added Tail Space to the next deal and sat at ease, holding bananas that the painter could leave out if he wished.
