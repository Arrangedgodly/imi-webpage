# The lawyer footnote tower

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal writing obscures simple claims*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Lawyer Dell, Client Dax, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dell wrote a brief with more notes below than text. Dax could not find the request from the monkey lawyer for his missing cart. Dell read the pages aloud until his voice failed. Dax drew the cart on one sheet and wrote Give It Back below. Dell added proof that Dax owned it and filed that instead. The court gave back the cart, full of bananas. Dell kept the long brief under his desk to raise the short leg.
