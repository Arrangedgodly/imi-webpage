# The comic strip tail panel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Comics compress action too aggressively*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Artist Sable, Reader Snow, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sable drew a monkey leap across three panels, but the tail was gone in the middle one. Snow asked how the hero could land without balance. Sable added a small panel where the tail curled round a branch. The scene made sense, and Snow laughed at the banana peel waiting below. Sable drew the hero as he caught that too. The strip ended with both feet on dry ground and the peel in a bin, where the next reader could see it.
