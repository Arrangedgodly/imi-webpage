# The cheese cloud

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Molecular cooking in plain kitchens*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Chase, Guest Gem, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Chase made a cheese cloud for Gem and served it in an empty bowl. The monkey guest sneezed and blew the cloud into the vent. Chase stared at his dish. Gem asked for cheese she could hold. He cut a slice, added bananas, and set it on a plate. She paid the full price. Chase kept the cloud machine for party fog and put a napkin over the vent before his next test.
