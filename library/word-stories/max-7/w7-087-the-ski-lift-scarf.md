# The ski lift scarf

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Winter sports gear causes mishaps*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Skier Sky, Lift Liz, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sky wore a scarf twice as long as her tail. It caught on the ski lift and pulled her monkey cap off. Liz stopped the lift before the knot grew tight. Sky cut the scarf into short pieces and wrapped each pole handle. Her hands stayed warm on the next run. Liz used the spare piece to line her lunch box. Both ate bananas at the bottom, with no loose wool near the gears.
