# The oath echo

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Court witnesses misunderstand formal language*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Elm, Witness Ember, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Elm asked Ember to repeat the oath. The monkey said every word, even the request to say it again. The court clerk tried not to laugh. Elm asked one plain query about the lost fruit box. Ember said that she had placed it in the shade behind the gate. The clerk fetched the bananas and checked her account. Elm wrote a clearer oath for the next hearing, without a line that sounded like part of the pledge.
