# The train bandit timetable

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western bandits miss mundane schedules*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Bandit Kestrel, Clerk Kit, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kestrel waited by the track to rob the noon train. Kit walked past with bananas and asked what the monkey was doing. Kestrel pointed at the rails. Kit showed him a notice that the route had closed last year. Kestrel took his mask off and helped Kit clear weeds from the old train stop. The town opened a fruit stall there. Kestrel sold tickets for cart rides instead, keeping each time on a sign he could read.
