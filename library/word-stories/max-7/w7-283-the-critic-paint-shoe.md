# The critic paint shoe

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Art critics leave physical marks*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Critic Mira, Painter Moss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mira called the picture dull while she stepped in wet blue paint. The monkey critic left prints across the gallery floor. Moss handed her a mop before he spoke about the review. Mira cleaned each print and noticed how the blue changed under varied lights. She wrote a more careful piece about those colors. Moss hung it near the final canvas. Mira left her shoes outside the next studio and brought bananas instead of mud on her soles.
