# The sand castle king

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fragile kingdoms and pride*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: King Kip, Builder Uma, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kip ruled a sand castle and charged monkeys bananas to enter. Uma warned him that the tide would soon reach the gate. Kip ordered a taller wall. Uma dug a ditch instead and led the water around the yard. The castle still fell at dusk, but the guests had dry fur. Kip gave them their fruit back. Next day he made a sand park with free gates and a tide sign.
