# The red cloak laundry

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Woodland stranger warnings*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Red Ren, Aunt Uma, Wolf Wes, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ren took soup to Aunt Uma in her bright red cloak. Wes, a monkey in a wolf mask, tried to lure her down a dark path. Ren knew him by the strip of fur near his nose. She asked him to carry the soup while she washed her cloak in the stream. He waited so long that he got hungry. At the hut they shared soup and bananas, and he hung up his mask.
