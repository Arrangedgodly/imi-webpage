# The mural window block

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Public art fails its surrounding building*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Painter Ember, Clerk Elm, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ember painted a bright mural over the shop front, even the window. Elm could no longer see the monkey guests arrive. Ember peeled the paint off the glass and framed the window with vines instead. Guests could see bananas inside, and Elm could wave them in. The mural still looked lively along the wall. Ember signed it beside the clear pane and drew future wall plans on one sheet and window plans on another.
