# The three small inns

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Three houses and a hungry wolf*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Ash, Elm, Oak, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash built an inn of straw. Elm used sticks. Oak used brick. Their monkey guests ate bananas in all three. A great wind blew down the first two inns, and all the guests ran to Oak. His door was too small for the crowd. Ash wove a broad mat roof and Elm built a long bench in the yard. Oak sold his bricks to buy tea for the new open inn.
