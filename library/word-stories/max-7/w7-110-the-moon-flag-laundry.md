# The moon flag laundry

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space achievements meet chores*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Captain Cal, Crew Cia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cal planted his flag on the moon, but Cia noticed it was a wet towel. The monkey captain had grabbed it from the wash rack. Cal wanted to leave it as a grand symbol. Cia needed it to dry her fur. They took a photo, folded the towel, and drew a flag on a flat stone. At home they ate bananas beneath the photo, while the real towel dried on its proper line.
