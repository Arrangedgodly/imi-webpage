# The mute button tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Video meetings and muted speakers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Manager Briar, Clerk Bri, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Briar asked Bri to give a report on the call. The monkey spoke for ten minutes with her mic off. Each peer waved a tail at the screen, but Bri thought they liked her speech. Briar held up a card that said No Sound. Bri turned the mic on and gave one clear line with the sales total. The call ended early. Briar kept the card on his desk beside the mute switch.
