# The goose on a break

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Golden egg greed*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: May, Goose Gil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May kept a goose that laid gold eggs. Gil was a monkey in a feather cape, and the eggs were brass balls from his shed. May asked for ten more each day. Gil put down his cape and showed her his sore hands. May helped polish the old balls. They sold them as door bells and split the bananas they earned. The goose nest became a soft chair for both to rest.
