# The stolen replica spoon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heists target valueless replicas*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Thief Glint, Curator Gem, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Glint stole a silver spoon from the gallery. Gem found the monkey hiding it under his fur at the gate. She showed him the real spoon, locked in a glass case. His was a wooden model painted gray. Glint gave it back and asked how to carve one himself. Gem put him in the shop for a lesson after he paid the fine. He made a plain spoon and used it to eat mashed bananas at home.
