# The noir window blind

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Brooding detective offices obstruct vision*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Quill, Client Quartz, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Quill kept the blinds shut to make his office look grave. Quartz came to ask about a missing cart, but the monkey sleuth could not read the receipt in the gloom. She opened one blind. The cart stood outside the window, where the driver had parked it. Quill gave the fee back and helped carry the bananas in. He kept the blind open for the next client, saving the dark room for naps after work.
