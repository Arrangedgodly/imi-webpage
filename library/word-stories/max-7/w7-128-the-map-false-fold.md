# The map false fold

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Treasure maps misread by eager hunters*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Demi, Guide Gil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Demi dug beneath an old tree where the map showed a cross. Gil saw that the map had folded over on itself. The monkey hunters opened it flat and found the cross marked a tool shed. Inside lay a note that told them to dig by the river. They found a chest of bananas there, left by the camp cook. Demi ate one and put the map in a stiff frame for the walk home.
