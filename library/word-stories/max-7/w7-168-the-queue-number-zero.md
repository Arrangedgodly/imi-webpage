# The queue number zero

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Waiting systems create endless waits*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Kestrel, Guest Moss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kestrel gave Moss ticket Zero and called One through Ten. The monkey guest sat all morning with bananas getting soft in his bag. Moss asked when Zero would come. Kestrel checked the board and found it started at One. She served him next, then printed a new first ticket. Moss shared his soft fruit with the waiting guests. Kestrel put Zero in a frame to show that a neat count must still include the first person.
