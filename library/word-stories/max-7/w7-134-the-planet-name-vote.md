# The planet name vote

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Explorers argue over discovery names*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Captain Cove, Scout Seren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cove named the new planet after himself. Seren said no because she had found it first. The monkeys argued until their fuel gauge flashed. They landed and saw trees full of bananas beside a clear pond. Cove wrote Snack on the map while Seren filled the fuel tank at a pump by the pond. She liked the name. They left a small flag with the pond marked on it, then flew home with fruit and enough fuel for a calm trip.
