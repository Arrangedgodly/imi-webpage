# The soap opera letter

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Soap operas delay simple revelations*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Enid, Actor Echo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Enid found a letter that could settle the plot. Echo urged the monkey to hide it until the next season. Enid read it aloud instead. It was a receipt for bananas, not a secret wedding. Echo stared at the wrong prop in her hand. The crew found the right letter beneath a cup and shot the scene again. Enid put all letters in marked trays, so a lunch bill could never run the plot twice.
