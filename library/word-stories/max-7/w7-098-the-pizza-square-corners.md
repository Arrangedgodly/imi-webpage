# The pizza square corners

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Food traditions meet geometry*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Pru, Guest Gabe, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pru made square pizzas, but Gabe said real pizza must be round. The monkey chef cut off the corners and put them on a tray. Gabe ate his circle, then asked for the corners too. Pru charged him for a whole square. He paid and laughed. She named the corner tray after him and served bananas beside it for dessert. Gabe came back with friends who each ordered a square and kept every crisp edge.
