# The getaway disguise cart

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heists conceal the wrong thing*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Oak, Driver Orla, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Oak painted his cart to look like a bush for the getaway. Orla could not see the path through its thick leaves. The monkey driver stopped at the gate before it crashed. Oak cut a clear window and saw the bush would never fool anyone on wheels. They dropped the heist and sold the cart as a garden shade wagon. Orla took plants in it all summer, with bananas beneath the leafy roof for lunch.
