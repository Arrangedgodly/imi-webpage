# The theater ghost rope

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Theater superstitions obscure loose equipment*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Lark, Stagehand Laurel, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark blamed a ghost when the curtain rose by itself. Laurel traced the rope to a snag under the stool used by the monkey actor. Each time Lark leaned back, his tail pulled the rope. Laurel freed it and moved the stool. Lark bowed to the empty wings as an apology to the ghost he had named. The curtain stayed down until the right cue. Laurel ate bananas during the break without needing to watch a haunted knot.
