# The spy bubble trail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret agents leave obvious traces*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Mist, Guard Maple, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mist wore soap shoes to slide quietly across the hall. Each step left bubbles behind the monkey spy. Maple traced the trail and found him under a desk, peeling bananas. Mist said he was testing a stealth route. Maple gave him a mop and helped dry the floor. They were done before anyone slipped. Mist swapped the soap with soft felt and tested one quiet step at a time, while Maple looked for new trails.
