# The bear chair repair

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Intruders and three household sizes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Goldie, Bear Ben, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Goldie tried each chair in the bear hut. She was a monkey guest, but had lost her invite in the wind. One chair broke beneath her tail. Bear Ben came home and found her mending it with rope. He read the invite that stuck to his boot. The chair was his gift to her, though he had built it too small. They made a wider seat and ate bananas while the glue dried.
