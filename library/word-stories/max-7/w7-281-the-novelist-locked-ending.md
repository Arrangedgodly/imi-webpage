# The novelist locked ending

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Writers confuse mystery with incompletion*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Writer Kestrel, Editor Kit, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kestrel wrote a mystery with no final chapter. Kit asked the monkey writer where the stolen bananas had gone. Kestrel said the reader should decide. Kit found three clues that led to the pantry and asked him to open its door on the page. Kestrel wrote the scene, and showed that the cook had stored the fruit safely. Kit took the book. Kestrel left one tiny riddle about the pantry key for the next story, which had its own ending.
