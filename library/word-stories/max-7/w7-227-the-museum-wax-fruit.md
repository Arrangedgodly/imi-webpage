# The museum wax fruit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Laser security creates theatrical tension*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Thief Keen, Guard Kia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Keen crawled beneath red beams to reach a display of gold bananas. Kia watched the monkey from the door. She turned the beams off and pointed to the open gift stall. The gold fruit was wax, sold for one coin each. Keen paid for one and put it in his bag. Kia let him leave after he wiped his muddy crawl marks off the floor. He kept the wax fruit on his desk, still whole.
