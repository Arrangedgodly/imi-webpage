# The golf sand picnic

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Golfers refuse to leave bunkers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Golfer Glen, Caddie Cora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Glen hit his ball into the sand pit six times. Cora offered the monkey a shorter club, but he wanted to prove his power. His next swing dug a hole beside the ball. Cora spread a cloth over it and opened the bananas. Glen sat down at last. After lunch he used the short club and cleared the sand. Cora raked the pit smooth and kept the long club as a picnic pole.
