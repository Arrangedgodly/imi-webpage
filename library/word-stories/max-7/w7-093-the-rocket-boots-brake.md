# The rocket boots brake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Super speed without control*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Harry, Mechanic Mox, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Harry wore rocket boots to reach fires fast. He flew past a smoky shed and landed in a pond. Mox pulled the monkey out by his tail. The boots had a start button but no brake. Mox added one and made Harry train on the grass. The next smoke came from a grill. Harry stopped beside it, brought water for the cook, and stayed to eat bananas while the boots cooled in the shade.
