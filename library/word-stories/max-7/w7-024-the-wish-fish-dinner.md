# The wish fish dinner

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wish granting fish and greed*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Fisher Fae, Fish Milo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fae caught a fish that offered wishes. It was Milo, a monkey in a blue suit, hiding from his dish chores. Fae wished for a grand meal. Milo led her home and cooked beans with bananas. She wished for clean plates. He sighed and washed them with his tail wrapped round the brush. Her last wish was for him to join her at the table. He did, still damp, and ate his share.
