# The jump pole lunch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Athletic equipment confused with food tools*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Jumper Jem, Coach Kim, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jem used a tall pole to leap over a bar. Kim saw him stop halfway and pick bananas from a branch. The monkey said his grip needed a snack. Kim moved the bar to an open field. Jem jumped cleanly, then missed the branch where he had planned to land. Kim placed a soft mat there. Jem won the event and ate his fruit on the ground, with the pole flat beside him.
