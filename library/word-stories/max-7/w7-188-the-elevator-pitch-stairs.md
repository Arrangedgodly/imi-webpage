# The elevator pitch stairs

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Startup pitching ignores actual users*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Iris, Buyer Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Iris tried out her lift pitch all week. Ivy worked above in a hall with no lift. The monkey founder climbed six flights and arrived too winded to give the speech. Ivy handed her water and asked what she sold. Iris said Light Bags. Ivy bought one to carry bananas down the stairs. Iris used the money to make more bags and put the speech away until she could say it in one breath.
