# The cowboy cart ride

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western heroes lack proper mounts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Rider Reed, Stable Stella, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Reed arrived at the stable in full cowboy gear. Stella had no horse, only a cart pulled by two monkeys. Reed refused to ride until he saw the cart shade and the bananas in its basket. He sat down and held his hat over his tail. The ride reached town faster than his planned walk. Reed paid both pullers and helped them oil the wheels before taking the cart back along the dusty lane.
