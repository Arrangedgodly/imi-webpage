# The donkey song ban

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Musician animals seek a home*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Donkey Dot, Mayor Max, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot sang so loudly that the town asked her to leave. She was a monkey in a donkey hood and took the news badly. Mayor Max heard her voice shake loose ripe bananas from a high tree. He hired her to sing in the grove each morning. Dot could now choose any song she liked. The town slept late, the fruit crew wore ear plugs, and the hood became a sack for the crop.
