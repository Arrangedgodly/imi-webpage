# The roof rain cover

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist crews fail to identify targets*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Thief Moon, Roofer May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Moon saw a metal box on the roof and planned to steal it. May caught the monkey lifting it and showed him the vent beneath. It was a rain cover, not a safe. Moon put it back before the storm arrived. May asked him to help fit the loose screws as payment for the damage. He did, then bought bananas at the market. The roof stayed dry and he looked inside boxes before making plans from then on.
