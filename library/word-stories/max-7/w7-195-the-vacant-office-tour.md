# The vacant office tour

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Startups boast unused space*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Orchid, Guest Olive, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orchid showed Olive a vast office for his new fruit firm. Only one monkey worked there, peeling bananas in a corner. Olive asked how rent got paid. Orchid said future growth would cover it. Olive moved the table into a small stall outside and watched the first guests buy fruit. Orchid ended the office lease when it expired. His grand tour now took two steps, leaving more time to peel and sell the actual stock.
