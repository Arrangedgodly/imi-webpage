# The jury lunch lock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Court procedures ignore ordinary needs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Cedar, Juror Clover, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cedar sent the jury to a locked room to decide the case. Clover found bananas on the table but no water. The monkey juror knocked until the clerk came. Cedar feared they wanted help with the verdict. Clover held up an empty cup. The clerk brought a jug and opened the side kitchen. The jury did its work with clear heads. Cedar kept a small needs list beside the rules for every later session.
