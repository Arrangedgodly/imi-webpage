# The key mold crumble

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist gadgets fail basic material tests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Locke, Baker Lio, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Locke made a copy of the vault key out of dough. Lio baked it, but the teeth curled in the oven. The monkey planner tried to flatten them and broke the key in half. Lio served both bits with bananas and asked why he had not asked for a real key. Locke went to the owner and applied for work. He earned a legal key to the store room, and Lio baked plain rolls for his first shift.
