# The burnt toast portrait

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Celebrity chefs excuse failed food*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Toast Tom, Guest Gia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tom called his burnt toast a bold new dish. Gia took one bite and coughed black dust onto her fur. The monkey chef said that was the style. Gia scraped the toast and used the dark bits to draw his face on a plate. Tom laughed, then made fresh toast at low heat. He served it with bananas and put her art on the menu. The new dish was called Take Your Time.
