# The appeal ladder

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal appeals confused with physical heights*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Lawyer Jun, Client Jade, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade asked Jun to take her case to a higher court. The monkey lawyer rented a room on the top floor and carried the files up. Jade asked where the new judges were. Jun checked the notice and learned higher meant an appeals panel across town. He gave back the room key and took the files there. Jade bought bananas for the walk. Jun kept the proper address on the front page, ahead of every grand legal phrase.
