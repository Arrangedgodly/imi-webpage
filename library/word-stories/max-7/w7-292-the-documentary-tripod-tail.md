# The documentary tripod tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Documentaries claim authenticity through poor equipment*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Filmmaker Ash, Subject Alder, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash filmed Alder sorting bananas with a shaky camera. He said the wobble felt honest. The monkey subject asked viewers to see his hands clearly. Ash set the camera on a tripod, but one leg stood on his own tail. He moved it, tested the frame, and filmed with no shake. Viewers could tell ripe fruit from green. Ash kept one shaky shot of the market outside and used the calm view for every step of the sorting lesson.
