# The distant planet mud

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Explorers name ordinary discoveries*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Scout Sky, Captain Cam, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sky found a strange brown sea on a new planet. Cam said the monkey must bring a sample home. Sky dipped his tail in and got stuck. Cam pulled him free with a rope. The sea was deep mud, much like the patch behind their hut. They marked it on the map and took a jar anyway. Back home they grew bananas in it, which earned the planet a useful name, Garden.
