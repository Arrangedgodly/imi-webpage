# The memo margin war

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office edits consume the work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Writer Wren, Editor Eve, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren wrote a memo about a broken tap. Eve changed its margins six times. The monkey writer watched water drip onto a crate of bananas while the page passed back and forth. Wren took a wrench to the tap and stopped the leak. Eve cleared the seventh margin just as he was done. Wren sent a one line note that said Fixed. Eve kept it on her desk because no one needed to edit its shape.
