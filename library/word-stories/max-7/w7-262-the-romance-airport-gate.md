# The romance airport gate

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film romances depend on missed departures*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Mira, Actor Moss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mira ran to the airport to confess her love before Moss left. The monkey found him at the wrong gate, eating bananas. His flight had moved, and he had not read the board. Mira gave the speech while they walked to the right gate side by side. Moss thanked her and agreed to call. The film boss kept the scene because Moss reached his flight on time, with no need to halt a plane on the runway.
