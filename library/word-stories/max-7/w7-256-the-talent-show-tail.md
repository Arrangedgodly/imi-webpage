# The talent show tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Talent contests prize spectacle over skill*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Singer Fia, Judge Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fia sang well, but Fern wanted a bigger finish. The monkey tied ribbons to her tail and spun while holding the final note. She grew dizzy and forgot the words. Fern gave her water and asked for a simple song. Fia sat on a stool and sang it cleanly. The crowd sat still, then cheered. Fern gave her a plain mic and bananas as the prize, leaving the loose ribbons for the stage curtain.
