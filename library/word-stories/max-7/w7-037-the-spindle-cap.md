# The spindle cap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A forbidden spinning tool*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Rose, Aunt Eve, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose found a spindle in the attic, despite a royal ban. The monkey touched its point and got a small prick. Aunt Eve heard her yelp and brought a bandage. No one fell asleep. Rose asked why the king had banned all sewing. Eve showed her a cap that kept the sharp tip covered. They made caps for every spindle and used the saved cloth to wrap bananas for the winter fair.
