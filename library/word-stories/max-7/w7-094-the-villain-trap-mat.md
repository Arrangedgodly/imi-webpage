# The villain trap mat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Elaborate traps fail on practical details*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Villain Voss, Hero Holly, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Voss built a pit under a rug to catch Holly. The monkey hero saw that the rug sagged over a hole when her tail brushed it and stepped around it. Voss leaned down to check the edge and fell in himself. Holly lowered a basket of bananas, then a ladder. Voss ate before he climbed out. They filled the pit with soil and planted a tree. Holly kept the rug for the shade beneath it.
