# The bread door wedge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Perfect loaves become too hard*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Baker Bram, Guest Greta, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bram baked a loaf that rang like a bell when Greta knocked on it. The monkey baker said the crust proved his skill. Greta could not cut it even with a saw. Bram sliced a softer loaf and served it with bananas. They used the hard loaf to hold the door open while the kitchen cooled. Guests praised the fresh air. Bram put less heat in the oven and kept the bell loaf by the door.
