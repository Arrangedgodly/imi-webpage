# The night vision lamp

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Advanced spy gear defeated by simple light*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Dusk, Guard Gray, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dusk wore night goggles to enter a dark hall. Gray turned a lamp on, and the monkey agent saw only glare. Dusk took the goggles off and found Gray holding his lost bag of bananas. There was no secret vault, just the coat room. Dusk thanked him and checked the room number on his note. He went to the next door with plain eyes and left the goggles in their padded case.
