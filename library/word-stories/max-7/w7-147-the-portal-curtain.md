# The portal curtain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dimensional doors mistaken for ordinary doors*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Mage Orbit, Guest Opal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orbit built a portal behind his kitchen curtain. Opal stepped through to fetch bananas and landed in the tool shed. The monkey mage had linked the wrong doors. Opal came back with a rake and asked where lunch was. Orbit moved the link to the pantry, then hung a clear sign on the curtain. They ate side by side in the kitchen. The rake stayed by the back door, which led to the garden without magic.
