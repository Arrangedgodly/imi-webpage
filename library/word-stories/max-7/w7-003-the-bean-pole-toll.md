# The bean pole toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Giant beanstalk quests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Nell, Giant Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nell grew a bean stalk so tall that clouds caught in its leaves. Giant Gus charged each monkey three bananas to climb it. Nell saw that he used the toll to buy boots that crushed her beans. She set a small stall at the root and sold bean soup. Gus climbed down for lunch. His boots sank in the mud, so he stayed to help pick beans in bare feet.
