# The office trophy cupboard

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Award culture crowds out tools*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Dove, Clerk Dune, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dove gave Dune a trophy each month for tidy work. Soon the awards filled the shelf where the monkey kept his tools. Dune put the awards in a cabinet and asked for no more. Dove said praise should be visible. Dune showed him the clean desk and the files in order. Dove gave him a day off instead. Dune cleaned one small cup before leaving and used it to hold his best pens.
