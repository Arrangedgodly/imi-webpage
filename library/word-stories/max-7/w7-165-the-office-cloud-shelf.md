# The office cloud shelf

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Digital jargon hides physical problems*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Hazel, Clerk Clover, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hazel told Clover to put all files in the cloud. The monkey clerk carried the boxes to a high shelf where steam from the kettle rose. Paper curled in the damp air. Hazel meant an online store, but had given no login. Clover moved the boxes to a dry cabinet and asked for the account. Hazel wrote it down but kept the code safe. They sorted files side by side over bananas, then moved only the needed pages online.
