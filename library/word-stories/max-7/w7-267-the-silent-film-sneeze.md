# The silent film sneeze

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Silent films and live sound*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Reed, Pianist Rue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Reed played a silent hero with a solemn face. His monkey fur tickled his nose, and he sneezed during the take. Rue matched the sneeze with a sharp piano chord. The crew laughed and kept filming. Reed turned it into a scene where the hero blows dust off a lost gold map. Rue played a light tune as he found the bananas marked on it. The final film had no speech, but the sneeze still got the biggest laugh.
