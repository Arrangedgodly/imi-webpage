# The growth chart roof

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Startup growth measured poorly*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Faye, Analyst Ash, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Faye showed a chart where sales rose through the roof. Ash checked the scale and found it counted peels instead of bananas. The monkey founder had called every peel a new client. Ash weighed the actual fruit and drew a smaller chart. Faye first frowned, then saw that the stock would last a week. They sold the peels as compost and counted that money once. The new chart fit neatly on one page.
