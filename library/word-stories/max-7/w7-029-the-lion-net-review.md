# The lion net review

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Small helpers save large rulers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Lion Leo, Mouse Min, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Leo wore a lion mane and laughed at tiny Min. Then his mane caught in a net while he chased bananas. Min was a small monkey with neat claws. She cut one knot and let him crawl out. Leo offered her a place on his royal staff. Min asked for a stool at the fruit stall instead. He built it that day, and she could reach the ripe bunches without his help.
