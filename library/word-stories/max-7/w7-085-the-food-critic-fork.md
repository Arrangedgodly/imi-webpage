# The food critic fork

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Critics value presentation over taste*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Critic Cliff, Cook Cia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cliff gave Cia low marks because her fork was bent. The monkey cook bent it back, but the critic found a dent in the spoon. Cia set a bowl of bananas beside him without any metal tools. Cliff ate with his hands and forgot to grumble. He wrote that the meal had a fine rustic style. Cia framed the review next to the tool rack, where the bent fork held spare towels.
