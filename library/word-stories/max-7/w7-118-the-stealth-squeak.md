# The stealth squeak

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spies with noisy equipment*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Vale, Mechanic May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vale wore silent shoes for a night mission. His monkey tail creaked against the shiny floor instead. May heard him from the shop and handed him a cloth. Vale wrapped his tail, crossed the hall, and found the lost key beside the sink. May had left it there while washing bananas. Vale gave it back, then removed his shoes. His bare feet were quiet enough, and the cloth became his regular tail towel.
