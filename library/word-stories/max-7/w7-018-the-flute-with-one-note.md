# The flute with one note

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magical music and pest removal*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Piper Hal, Mayor Liv, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hal said his flute would rid the town of pests. He could play only one note. The monkeys came out to ask him to stop. Mayor Liv saw their tails twitch at each squeak and had an idea. She put them in a line to sweep the street in time. Hal played until each crumb was gone. The pests left for lack of food, and Hal earned bananas for a short song.
