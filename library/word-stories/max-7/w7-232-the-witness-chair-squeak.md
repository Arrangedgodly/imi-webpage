# The witness chair squeak

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom drama treats noise as testimony*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Birch, Witness Bea, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea sat in the witness chair and it creaked. Birch thought the sound meant the monkey was nervous. Bea shifted her tail, and the chair creaked again. She stood to give her account clearly. The clerk found a loose bolt beneath the seat. Birch paused the hearing while it was fixed. Bea then sat without a sound and told the same facts. The judge weighed those facts alone, with no note about the faulty chair.
