# The bell tower whisper

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ghost mysteries with sound clues*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Della, Keeper Kian, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Della heard low voices in the bell tower and went up to face a ghost. Kian, a monkey keeper, stood below with a basket of bananas. Della found a pipe that carried his voice to the top. He had asked if she wanted lunch. She spoke into the pipe and asked for two bananas. Kian sent them up in a bucket. Della labeled the pipe Lunch Call and left the ghosts out of her report.
