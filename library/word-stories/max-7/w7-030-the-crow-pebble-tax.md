# The crow pebble tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Clever thirst solutions*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Crow Cam, Wells Willa, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cam wore black wings and dropped stones in a jug to raise its water. Willa watched the monkey work for an hour. Each stone cost him a banana at the stone stall. She tipped the jug into a shallow bowl. Cam drank, then took the stones back and got his bananas back. He kept one smooth stone as a weight so his new bowl would not blow away in the wind.
