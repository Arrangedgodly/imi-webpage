# The acorn budget

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Winter food fables*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Ant Amy, Grass Gil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Amy stored acorns for winter. Gil played songs and stored none. The monkeys met when snow sealed the nut shed. Amy could not reach its latch through the drift. Gil tapped a tune on the roof until the loose snow slid off. Amy opened the shed and shared nuts and bananas. Gil played while she counted the sacks. In spring he built a drum with a small food bin inside it.
