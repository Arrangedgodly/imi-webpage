# The moon base broom

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Superhero lairs with chores*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Haze, Cleaner Clio, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Haze built a secret base on the moon. Clio came to clean it and found dust in every crack. The monkey hero had no broom, only a ray gun. Clio taped dry leaves to the gun and swept the floor. Haze tried to fire it, but Clio took out the power cell first. They packed bananas in the clean kitchen. Haze made a chore chart and put his own name on the first shift.
