# The office quiet jar

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Workplace noise control*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Bea, Clerk Creek, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea charged a coin when someone made noise. Creek dropped a mug and owed three coins for the crash. The monkey asked how to report a fire under the quiet rule. Bea opened her mouth, then shut it. Creek put a bell beside the jar and offered one free ring for urgent news. Bea agreed and gave back the coins. They filled the jar with bananas instead, though each peel made a small rustle at lunch.
