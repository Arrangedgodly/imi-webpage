# The silver trail snail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detectives follow misleading shining trails*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Drake, Gardener Greta, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Drake saw a silver trail near the locked fruit shed. The monkey thought a thief had dragged a chain there. Greta lent him a lens, and he traced the trail to a snail. It had crossed a wet leaf in the moon glow. Drake opened the shed with Greta and found the bananas safe. He moved the snail to a damp patch beside the fence. Greta gave him the lens to check small facts first.
