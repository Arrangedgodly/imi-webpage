# The giant shade hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Size based fairy tale quests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Poe, Giant Meg, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Giant Meg wore a hat that hid the sun from the village. Poe, a monkey tailor, climbed its brim to ask her to move. She could not see where her feet went. Poe cut eye holes in the hat, then tied its top with his tail. Meg saw the huts and stepped round them. She paid him with bananas and used the spare cloth to shade the village in hot weather.
