# The cape caught in the bus

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Superheroes arrive with dramatic capes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hal, Driver Di, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hal leaped onto the bus to save it from a loose wheel. His cape caught in the door, leaving the monkey stuck on the step. Di stopped the bus and freed him. Hal used the cape to wipe grease off the axle while Di fixed the bolt. They drove to the shop at walking speed. The riders paid Hal with bananas. He cut his cape short and kept a wrench in the hem.
