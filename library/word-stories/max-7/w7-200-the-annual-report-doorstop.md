# The annual report doorstop

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate reports have unintended practical uses*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Spruce, Clerk Stone, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Spruce printed a thick annual report for each worker. Stone could not find the desk space to open hers. The monkey used it to hold the windy door open while fruit carts rolled in. Spruce came to demand that she read it. Stone pointed to the front page, which said Remove Blocks To Sales. He let the book stay. They read the key totals on one sheet and sold all the bananas before the door swung shut.
