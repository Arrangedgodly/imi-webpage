# The cake tower brace

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Baking shows and towering cakes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Baker Baz, Host Hope, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Baz stacked cakes taller than his head. Hope asked how he would cut them for the judges. The monkey pushed his tail through the middle as a brace, which made the top cake tilt. Hope slid small plates beneath each layer. Baz served a whole row of flat cakes instead. The judges could taste them all without ladders. Baz put a tiny ladder beside one cake and won the prize for best tower.
