# The crown in the well

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Frog prince bargains*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: King Kit, Frog Finn, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

King Kit dropped his crown in a well. Finn, a monkey in a frog suit, said a kiss would bring it back. Kit peered down and saw the crown caught on a nail. He lent Finn his long tail as a rope. Finn fetched it, but the wet suit stuck to his fur. Kit paid him bananas and a dry towel. Finn quit the frog act and opened a tool shop.
