# The bonus fruit scale

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Employee rewards hide unfair measures*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Marsh, Clerk Mari, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Marsh paid bonuses by fruit weight. Mari got a bunch of green bananas, while senior staff got soft ripe ones. The monkey weighed her share and found it heavier but too hard to eat. She brought a ripe bunch to compare. Marsh tested a slice of each and saw the problem. He let workers choose when to collect their fruit. Mari waited three days and got a ripe bunch at the same fair weight.
