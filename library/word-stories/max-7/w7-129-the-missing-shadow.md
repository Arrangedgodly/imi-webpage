# The missing shadow

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Supernatural mystery with stage lighting*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Dora, Lamp Lyle, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dora woke with no shadow and feared a spell. Lyle checked the monkey room and found lamps on every wall. Their light hid the dark patch behind her feet. He turned all but one lamp off. Dora saw her shadow stretch across the floor. She fed Lyle bananas for his help, then asked him to move the lamps into the garden. Her night party had bright paths, and each guest kept a shadow.
