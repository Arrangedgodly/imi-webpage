# The spell that kept time

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sleeping castle enchantments*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Belle, Mage Lux, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mage Lux cast a sleep spell on the hall. Belle stayed awake because she was outside peeling bananas. The monkey cooks slept over cold pans, and no one could serve lunch. Belle wound the old clock and set its bell for noon. The chime woke the hall. Lux said the spell should last years. Belle pointed to the lunch list. He changed it to a nap spell and joined the queue.
