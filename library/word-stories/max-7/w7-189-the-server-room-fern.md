# The server room fern

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office decoration disrupts equipment*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Jade, Tech Jun, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade filled the server room with ferns to make it calm. Jun saw water drip onto a cable while he checked the fans. The monkey tech moved the pots outside and dried the floor. Jade asked why the room looked bare again. Jun showed her the hot air path each fern had blocked. They made a garden by the break room instead. Jade watered it there, and Jun could hear the servers humming without a drip.
