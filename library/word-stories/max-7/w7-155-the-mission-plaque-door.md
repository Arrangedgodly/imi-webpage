# The mission plaque door

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate mission statements block work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Basil, Clerk Clio, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Basil hung a huge mission plaque over the door. Clio could not carry boxes of bananas through without tilting her monkey head. Basil said the words would inspire the team. Clio asked him to read them from the hall. He could see only the frame. They moved the plaque to a side wall and cleared the door. Clio got the boxes in before noon. Basil wrote Keep Door Clear on a smaller card by the hinge.
