# The queen of crumbs

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: A tiny kingdom demands tribute*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Queen Qia, Baker Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Qia claimed every crumb on the bakery floor as royal land. Bo swept the crumbs up before she could build her palace. The monkey queen sat on his broom and refused to move. Bo gave her a tray where crumbs could stay dry. Qia made a tiny castle on it and charged ants a leaf to enter. Bo served bananas beside her tray, and the ants left his good bread alone.
