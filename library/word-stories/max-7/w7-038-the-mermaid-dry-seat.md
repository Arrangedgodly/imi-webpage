# The mermaid dry seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wishes to live on land*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Mira, Sailor Sid, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mira wore a fish tail in the lake show. She wished to sit on land, but the tail made each chair tip. Sailor Sid brought a stool with a slot for it. The monkey slid in and ate bananas without falling. Mira kept the fish tail for shows and her own tail for walks. Sid put wheels on the stool so she could roll from the lake to lunch in one smooth trip.
