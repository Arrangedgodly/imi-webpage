# The proud pond swan

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ugly duckling revelations*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Duck Dax, Swan Sue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dax wore a duck cap to fit in at the pond. Swan Sue said his brown fur spoiled the flock photo. The monkey took his cap off and held it over the lens as a shade. The glare went away. Sue saw that the photo looked much better. She asked Dax to stand beside her for a new one. He did, with bananas in his cap, and every bird leaned toward the snack.
