# The oven timer duet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking against a dramatic clock*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Cook Kit, Singer Sam, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kit had ten minutes to bake buns on the show. Sam sang the last count so slowly that the monkey thought he had an hour. His buns burned while Sam held the last note. Kit sliced off the dark crust and made crunchy bits for soup. He served bananas as dessert. Sam got a real clock for the next round. Kit asked him to sing only when the food had left the oven.
