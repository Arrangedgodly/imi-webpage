# The time sheet tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Time tracking creates unpaid work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Nova, Clerk Night, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nova made Night record every minute on a time sheet. The monkey spent half an hour noting how long the notes took. Nova asked why filing had slowed. Night showed her the loop of entries about entries. They set one box for the full filing task and one for lunch. Night filled both after work and went home on time. Nova could read the simple sheet in just one minute.
