# The starship tail seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space design ignores body shape*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Pilot Pearl, Builder Beck, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Beck made a ship seat with no tail hole. Pearl sat in it and could not reach the buttons. The monkey builder cut a slot, but loose foam clogged the air vent. Pearl held a bag under the seat while Beck brushed the foam out. They tested each switch before takeoff. The ship flew well, and Pearl ate bananas in comfort. Beck added tail slots to every seat on his plan that evening.
