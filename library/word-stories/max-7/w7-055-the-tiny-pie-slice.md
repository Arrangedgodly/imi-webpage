# The tiny pie slice

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking judges demand equal portions*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Pie Pam, Judge Jed, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pam baked one pie for seven judges. Jed said each slice must weigh the same. The monkey used a scale, but crumbs kept falling into her fur. Pam cut the pie into small cubes and gave each judge a bowl. Jed still wanted a grand slice for the photo. Pam shaped his cubes into a wedge and put bananas beside it. He took the photo, then ate the same share as the rest.
