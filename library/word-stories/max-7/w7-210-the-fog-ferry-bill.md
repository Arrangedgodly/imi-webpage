# The fog ferry bill

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir chase scenes miss transport rules*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Frost, Captain Tide, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Frost chased a shadow onto the ferry without paying. Tide stopped the boat and asked for his fare. The monkey sleuth pointed at the shadow, but it came from a coat on the rail. Frost paid and helped Tide haul the coat in before it blew away. A guest claimed it at the far dock. Frost ate bananas on the ride back and kept his ticket visible in the brim of his hat.
