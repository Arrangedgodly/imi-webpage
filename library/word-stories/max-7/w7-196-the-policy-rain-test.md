# The policy rain test

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Rules fail under physical conditions*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Pebble, Inspector Poppy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Poppy told Pebble to post all permits outside. Rain soaked them until the ink ran. The monkey clerk could no longer prove the fruit stall was legal. Poppy came back to issue a fine, but her own note got wet. Pebble lent her a clear sleeve and put one on each permit. Poppy voided the fine and added Dry Cover to the rule. The bananas stayed under the awning while every form stayed clear beside them.
