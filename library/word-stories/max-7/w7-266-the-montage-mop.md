# The montage mop

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Training montages omit necessary cleanup*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Quill, Cleaner Quartz, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Quill trained for the fight in a fast montage. He threw ropes, sacks, and towels across the monkey gym while music played. Quartz stopped the shot when a sack rolled under his foot. They cleaned the floor and filmed a final sweep in the montage. Quill reached the match with a clear space to train. Quartz got a screen credit, and the bananas stayed on a shelf where no flying towel could strike them.
