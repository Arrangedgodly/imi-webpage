# The moon lunch leak

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space missions with food emergencies*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Pilot Pax, Cook Cora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pax flew to the moon with a crate of bananas. Cora found a leak in the food bag and called the monkey pilot back to the cabin. He patched it with his mission badge, but now no one could read his name. Cora wrote it on his helmet in sauce. They landed safely and shared lunch beside the ship. Pax kept the stained helmet as proof that the cook had saved the trip.
