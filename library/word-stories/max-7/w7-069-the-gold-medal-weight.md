# The gold medal weight

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Winning burdens sports heroes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Lifter Lou, Coach Cole, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lou trained to lift the largest weight in the hall. He won a gold medal so heavy it pulled his neck down. The monkey asked Cole to help him wear it. Cole put it on a stand, but the stand sank into the soft floor. Lou used the medal as a door wedge and hung a small leaf on his chest. He could carry his bananas again, which felt like a better prize.
