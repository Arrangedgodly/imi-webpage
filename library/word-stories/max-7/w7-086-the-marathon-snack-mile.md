# The marathon snack mile

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Distance races and feeding stations*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Runner Ria, Marshal Mars, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ria stopped at every snack stand during the long race. By the final mile the monkey carried more bananas than she could eat. Mars asked her to put the fruit down so she could finish. Ria gave each tired runner one and kept only a peel bag. She crossed last with a light tail and a full smile. Mars gave her the spare medal for best cleanup, then helped her carry the peel bag home.
