# The western sunset delay

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Cowboys insist on cinematic timing*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Rider Mesa, Partner Moss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mesa wanted to ride home at sunset for a fine outline. Moss asked the monkey to leave sooner because their bananas would bruise on the dark road. Mesa waited until the sun dipped, then could not see a rut. The cart wheel sank. Moss lit a lantern and helped push it free. They reached home late and mashed the bruised fruit for the next meal. Mesa set the next trip before dusk, with the lantern packed under his seat.
