# The superhero trailer chair

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Movie trailers exaggerate ordinary events*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Editor Thorn, Actor Thyme, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Thorn cut a trailer that made Thyme lifting a chair look like saving the world. The monkey actor asked what the full movie would show. Thorn watched the source reel and saw only a room move. He recut the trailer as a funny tale about finding space for guests. Viewers came for that story and enjoyed it. Thyme carried bananas to the first show, then put the same chair beside the table where people could really sit.
