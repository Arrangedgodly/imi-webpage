# The jewel swap label

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist teams mix up their replicas*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Jewel, Maker Juno, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jewel packed two gems, one real and one false, for a swap at the fair. Juno put both in plain boxes. The monkey planner could not tell which held which. She asked the seller to test them and told the seller her plan before any swap. The seller kept the real gem and paid Juno for the fine model. Jewel sold bananas to cover the fine, then labeled every box in the shop before going home.
