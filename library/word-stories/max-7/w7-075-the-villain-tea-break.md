# The villain tea break

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroes interrupt theatrical villain speeches*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Herb, Villain Vee, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vee stood on a crate to declare his plan to steal all the bananas. Herb waited in peace until the monkey villain lost his voice. Herb offered tea. Vee sat down and said he wanted fruit for his hungry guests. Herb led him to the market, where the bruised fruit cost little. Vee bought enough and gave up his grand plot. Herb carried the crate home so Vee could use it as a tea table.
