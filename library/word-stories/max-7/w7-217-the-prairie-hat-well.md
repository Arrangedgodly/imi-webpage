# The prairie hat well

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western hats solve the wrong problem*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Cowboy Clay, Farmer Fenn, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Clay lost his broad hat down the well. Fenn offered a hook, but the monkey decided to go down like a hero. His tail tangled in the rope before he reached the water. Fenn pulled him up and used the hook herself. The hat came back full of water and one banana leaf. Clay let it dry on the fence. He bought Fenn bananas and kept the hook beside the well for future rescues.
