# The fire drill form

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Safety procedures prioritize documentation*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Officer Fern, Clerk Fox, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern told Fox to fill a drill form before leaving the hall. The monkey clerk smelled smoke from the toaster and took the troop outside first. Fern carried the blank forms after him. They counted all staff by the gate, then called the kitchen crew. A burnt bun was the only loss. Fox filled the form in the yard. Fern moved the pen box outside so the next safe exit could stay clear.
