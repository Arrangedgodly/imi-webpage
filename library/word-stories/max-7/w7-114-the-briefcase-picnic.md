# The briefcase picnic

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy exchanges at public benches*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Fern, Agent Fay, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern left a case on the park bench for Fay. A gust blew it open, and bananas rolled across the path. The monkey agents crawled after them as if they had to inspect the paving stones. Fay caught the last fruit with her tail. They sat down and ate because the case had held only lunch. Fern put a latch on it before the next swap. Fay brought a cloth to cover the bench.
