# The poetry slam whisper

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Poetry contests reward loud delivery*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Poet Haze, Host Heath, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Haze wrote a poem about a lost spoon. Heath told the monkey to shout it to win the slam. Haze tried, but her voice broke before the last line. She asked for a quiet reading instead. The room hushed, and guests heard where the spoon had gone, into the banana mash. Haze pulled it from the prop bowl at the end. Heath gave her the prize for the clear story, not the volume.
