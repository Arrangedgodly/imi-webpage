# The spoon draft

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Team selection in cooking tournaments*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Coach Cal, Spoon Sue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cal picked cooks by how fast they could stir. Sue used her tail and won a place on the monkey team. In the final, the soup needed slow care. The fast cooks spilled it onto their fur. Sue tied a bell to her spoon and kept it quiet. Cal made the team follow her pace. They served smooth soup with bananas and put the silent spoon above the cup they had won.
