# The space opera wig

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Science fiction costumes frustrate actors*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Vale, Costume Wren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vale wore a vast silver wig as ruler of the stars. The monkey could not fit through the ship door. Wren took the wig off and tested the doorway with a small cap. Vale could enter, sit, and reach the buttons at last. Wren put the silver hair on the throne behind him. It looked grand in the wide shots. Vale ate bananas without hair in his mouth, and the ship scenes ended on time.
