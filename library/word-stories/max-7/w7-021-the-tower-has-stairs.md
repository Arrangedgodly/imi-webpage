# The tower has stairs

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rescues from tall towers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Rae, Prince Cal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rae lived in a tower and let down her long tail when Prince Cal came. He climbed halfway, then sneezed from her fur. Rae hauled him in and showed him the stairs behind a curtain. The monkeys had missed them for years because the curtain said Keep Out. Cal took the sign down. Rae opened a stair cafe and sold bananas at each landing. Cal used the lift, which was a plain bucket.
