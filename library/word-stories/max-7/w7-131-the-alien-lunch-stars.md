# The alien lunch stars

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space gift exchanges with ordinary rules*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Pilot Pine, Trader Tia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tia gave Pine a sealed alien cube. The monkey pilot opened it and found six bananas, each wrapped in foil. He thought the foil was a rare space metal. Tia said it was just there to keep lunch fresh. Pine folded it into stars and hung them in his cabin. He sent Tia a thank you note and gave the empty cube back. She filled it again, this time with a spoon beside the fruit.
