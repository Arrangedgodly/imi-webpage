# The team photo tail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Athletes insist on perfect publicity*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Captain Clem, Photo Phil, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Clem held the team still for a photo before the match. Phil could not fit every monkey tail in the frame. Clem curled his tail up, and all the others copied him. Now their heads hid behind the fur. Phil asked them to sit on the bench with legs to one side. He took one quick picture. The team reached the field in time and won. Clem kept the photo beside the bowl of victory bananas.
