# The pool lane knot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Competitive swimming mishaps*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Swimmer Sid, Ref Rae, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sid tied his tail to a float so he could rest between laps. During the race it caught the lane rope. The monkey kept going in one small circle. Ref Rae halted the race and cut the knot. Sid used the float to hold bananas at the pool edge instead. He swam the full course with a free tail. Rae gave him a plain ribbon and kept the shears close for the next event.
