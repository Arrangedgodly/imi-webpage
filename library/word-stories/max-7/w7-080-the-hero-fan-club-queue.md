# The hero fan club queue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Celebrity hero autograph lines*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hero, Fan Fenn, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hero spent all day signing cards while Fenn waited at the end of the line. A monkey shouted that a branch blocked the road. Hero feared the fans would leave if he stepped away from his desk. Fenn took the pen and signed a card that said Back Soon. Hero moved the branch and brought bananas for the queue. Fenn got the first card when he came back, with a real leaf on it.
