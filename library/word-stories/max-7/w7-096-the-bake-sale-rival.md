# The bake sale rival

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Competition becomes mutual aid*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Baker Bina, Baker Burt, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bina and Burt held rival bake sales across the lane. Each monkey lowered prices until a cake cost less than a crumb. Then rain drove all their guests away. Bina had a tent but no dry chairs. Burt had chairs but no tent. They joined the stalls and sold warm buns with bananas. Both earned enough to pay for flour. They kept one shared price sign and took turns holding it when the wind rose.
