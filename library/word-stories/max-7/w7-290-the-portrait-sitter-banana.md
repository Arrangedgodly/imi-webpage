# The portrait sitter banana

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Portrait painters ignore their sitters comfort*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Painter Thorn, Sitter Thyme, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Thorn asked Thyme to hold one pose for hours. The monkey sitter gripped bananas until the fruit went soft. Thorn liked the solemn face, but Thyme was simply hungry. They paused for lunch and swapped the fruit with a wooden model. Thyme held it lightly while Thorn did the picture. The face now looked calm. Thorn listed breaks in his next booking, and Thyme took the real fruit home without another bruise.
