# The break room spoon vote

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Bureaucracy over small supplies*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Cedar, Boss Brook, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cedar asked for a spoon in the break room. Brook formed a panel to choose its shape. The monkeys met for weeks while their soup grew cold. Cedar carved a spoon from a fallen branch and ate lunch. Brook asked if it met the new policy. Cedar handed him a bowl of bananas and the spoon. Brook used it without reading the policy. The panel closed, and the branch gave them three more spoons for the drawer.
