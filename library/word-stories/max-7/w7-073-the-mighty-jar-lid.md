# The mighty jar lid

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Super strength with tiny limitations*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hugo, Cook Cat, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hugo could lift a bridge, but he could not open a jam jar. Cat watched the monkey strain until his fur stood up. She tapped the lid with a spoon and ran warm water round its rim. Hugo turned it with one finger. He ate jam on bananas, then asked to learn the trick. Cat made him wash the spoon first. Hugo added a small tap move to his list of great powers.
