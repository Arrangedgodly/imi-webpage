# The tumbleweed toll

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western towns monetize scenery*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Mayor Mesa, Rider River, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mesa charged a coin for each dry weed that crossed the main road. River saw the monkey town funds swell as wind blew the weeds back and forth. No one could afford to drive a cart through. River caught the weeds in a fence and sold them as dry fuel. Mesa removed the toll when she saw the clear lane. They used the dry fuel to bake bananas at the town feast and kept the road open from then on.
