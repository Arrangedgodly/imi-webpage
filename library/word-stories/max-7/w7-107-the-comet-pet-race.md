# The comet pet race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space pets with dangerous speeds*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Keeper Kit, Comet Coco, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kit kept Coco as a pet comet in a glass bowl. Coco was a monkey with a silver cape who ran in circles so fast the bowl shook. Kit asked him to stop for bananas. Coco stopped at once, and the bowl fell quiet. They moved the game outside to a wide field. Kit drew a slow orbit round the picnic cloth. Coco could race each lap and still sit down for a snack.
