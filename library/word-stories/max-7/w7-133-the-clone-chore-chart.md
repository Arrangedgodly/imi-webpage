# The clone chore chart

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Clones multiply trouble instead of labor*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Scientist Suri, Clone Sal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Suri made a clone to do her chores. Sal was another monkey who also wanted a clone to do his chores. Suri stopped the machine before it made a third. They split the list, with Suri washing dishes and Sal on broom duty. The work ended sooner than they had hoped. They ate bananas on the clean floor. Suri changed the machine label to Friend Maker and kept the switch off unless a guest truly wished to stay.
