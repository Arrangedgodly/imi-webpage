# The meeting to end meetings

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office meeting culture*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Manager Mabel, Clerk Cole, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mabel called a meeting to cut talks. Cole brought a clock and a bag of bananas. Every monkey got one minute to speak, but Mabel used hers to list the agenda. Cole turned the clock round so she could see it. Mabel stopped before the bell. They cut the next meeting and used its hour to fix the loose shelf. Cole put the clock there, with enough space beside it for lunch.
