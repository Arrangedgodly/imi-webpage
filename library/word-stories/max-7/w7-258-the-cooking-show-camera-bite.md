# The cooking show camera bite

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: TV cameras interfere with food preparation*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Chef Herb, Camera Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy moved her camera close to film Herb taste the stew. The monkey chef knocked the lens with his spoon and left a red smear. Herb cleaned it with a soft cloth while the stew bubbled. Ivy filmed the pot from the side instead. The viewers saw the food clearly with no spoon in the way. Herb served bananas after filming, and Ivy put a safe space mark on the floor for the next shoot.
