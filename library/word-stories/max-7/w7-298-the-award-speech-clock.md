# The award speech clock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Arts awards speeches exceed audience patience*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Grove, Host Gilda, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Grove thanked every monkey he had ever met in his award speech. Gilda saw the hall clock pass twelve while his list still grew. She handed him a card with five blank lines. Grove chose the names from this film and closed with one clear thanks to the crew. The crowd clapped at last. Gilda served bananas before all guests left, and Grove sent the other thanks by letter in the morning.
