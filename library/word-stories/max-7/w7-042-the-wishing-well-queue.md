# The wishing well queue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Coins for magical wishes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Wes, Keeper Kay, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wes threw a coin in the well and wished for bananas. Kay, the monkey keeper, fished the coin out to pay for rope. Wes said the wish had gone wrong. Kay showed him the broken bucket. Wes tied his tail to the rope and helped haul water for the fruit trees. A month later they picked a full crop. Kay gave him the first bunch and kept the well free of coins.
