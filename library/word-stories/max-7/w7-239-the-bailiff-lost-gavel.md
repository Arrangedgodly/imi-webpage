# The bailiff lost gavel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal symbols hide in plain sight*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Bailiff Iris, Judge Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Iris lost the gavel just before court. Ivy refused to begin without it. The monkey bailiff checked every drawer and found only bananas. Ivy tapped a wooden handle on her desk while she waited. Iris pointed at the head beneath the papers. It was the gavel she had held all morning. Ivy cleared her desk and began the case. Iris put a small tray beside her hand, clearly marked for the gavel alone.
