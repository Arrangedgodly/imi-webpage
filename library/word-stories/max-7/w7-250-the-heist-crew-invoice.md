# The heist crew invoice

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Criminal planning has ordinary costs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Sable, Builder Stone, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sable hired Stone to build a tunnel for a heist. Stone sent a bill larger than the value of the stolen bananas. The monkey planner checked each charge and found digging, boards, and cart hire all cost real money. Sable stopped the theft and paid Stone for the work done. They turned the short tunnel into a cool fruit cellar with consent of the owner. Sable rented shelf space there and earned the money back over a season.
