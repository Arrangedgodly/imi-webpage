# The policy bookshelf tilt

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate rules physically obstruct work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Rose, Clerk Reed, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose printed each policy and stacked the binders on one shelf. Reed heard the wood crack above his monkey desk. He moved the bananas and files out of the way just before it fell. Rose wanted a rule about shelf weight. Reed asked her to remove old rules first. They kept three slim binders and sent the rest to the paper mill. Reed built a sturdy shelf and left half of it empty, where the fruit could sit safely.
