# The drum solo clock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Musicians extend solos beyond usefulness*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Drummer Birch, Singer Bea, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Birch played a drum solo so long that Bea missed the last bus. The monkey singer sat by the stage with her bag of bananas. Birch finally stopped when the hall clock chimed. Bea asked him to use that chime as a limit next time. They walked home side by side and shared the fruit. At the next show his solo ended in four minutes. Bea caught her bus, and Birch could pack his drums before the hall closed.
