# The spy clock fast

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Timed missions with wrong clocks*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Reed, Watchmaker Wren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Reed raced to the tower before noon to stop a secret trade. The monkey arrived early and found no one but Wren mending clocks. His watch was an hour fast. Wren fixed it while Reed shared his bananas. At noon a cart came to swap old clocks for new ones. Reed checked its permit and let it pass. He sent a report about the false alarm, with the correct time on every page.
