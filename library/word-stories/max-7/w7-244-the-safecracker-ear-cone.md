# The safecracker ear cone

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist specialists overstate their skills*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Picker Nix, Owner Nell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nix pressed a cone to the safe to hear its clicks. Nell asked the monkey why he had not tried the key she gave him. Nix said his craft needed time. The cone picked up the kitchen timer instead. Nell opened the safe with her key and took out the shop coins. Nix said he had guessed every sound. He gave the fee back and helped Nell count the coins before they bought bananas.
