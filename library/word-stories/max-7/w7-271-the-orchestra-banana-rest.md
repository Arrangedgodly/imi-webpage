# The orchestra banana rest

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Orchestras misinterpret musical rests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Conductor Ash, Player Alder, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash told Alder to take a rest during the slow passage. The monkey player lay down on the stage with bananas by his tail. Ash stopped the band and told him that rest meant silence for a few beats. Alder sat back up and counted them. The tune flowed well on the next try. After the concert Ash marked a real break on the plan. Alder ate then, with his horn safe in its case.
