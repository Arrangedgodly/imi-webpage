# The bass note floor

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Music performances reveal unsafe stages*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Bassist Ember, Builder Elm, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ember struck one low note and felt the stage shake. The monkey bassist stopped before the next chord. Elm checked beneath the boards and found a loose support. They moved the crowd to the yard while he fixed it. Ember played on a solid crate, with her tail clear of the strings. The outdoor show sounded good. Elm ended the repair before rain came, and the bananas served at the break stayed under the tent.
