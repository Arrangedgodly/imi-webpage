# The locked room window

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Impossible crimes with simple exits*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Detective Dan, Host Hope, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope locked a room with bananas inside. When she opened it, the fruit was gone. Dan checked the lock, then the ceiling, then his monkey hat. Hope asked why he had not checked the open window. Dan climbed out and found the fruit on a ledge where wind had blown it. He brought it back dry in his hat. Hope shut the window and put a small latch on it before dinner.
