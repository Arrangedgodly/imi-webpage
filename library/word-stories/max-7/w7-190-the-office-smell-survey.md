# The office smell survey

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Surveys delay obvious repairs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Kelp, Cleaner Kit, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kelp sent a survey about a bad smell near the desks. Kit, the monkey cleaner, found a rotten banana beneath the fridge. He removed it before anyone filled the survey out. Kelp still wanted data, so Kit wrote One Old Fruit on the form. The next survey asked if the room smelled better. Kit opened a window and went home. Kelp cut the third survey and added a weekly sweep under the fridge instead.
