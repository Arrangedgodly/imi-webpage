# The pitch deck banana

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Startup pitches favor slide count*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Finn, Investor Ivo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Finn made fifty slides for his fruit service pitch. Ivo asked what the service did. The monkey founder flipped through charts until his lamp rig failed. Finn took one banana out of his bag and peeled it for Ivo. That was the whole service. Ivo paid for lunch and asked what it cost to run. Finn wrote the costs on one sheet of paper. He sold the lamp rig and bought better fruit, leaving the slide deck in its box.
