# The heist rope job

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist equipment finds honest use*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Haven, Climber Hazel, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Haven tied ropes across the yard to reach a roof safe. Hazel climbed one, but the monkey found the roof held only an empty water tank. Haven had misread the plan. They took the ropes down and said sorry to the owner. She needed wash lines in the same yard. Hazel fitted them neatly and Haven washed the dirty cloths. The owner paid them bananas for the work, and the tank stayed empty until the next rain.
