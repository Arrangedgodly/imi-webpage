# The training video pause

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Mandatory training cannot account for reality*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Trainer Sable, Clerk Snow, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sable played a safety film about lifting fruit boxes. Snow saw a real box lean off the shelf behind him. The monkey clerk paused the film and caught it. Sable scolded her for missing the safety lesson, then noticed the box over his head. He helped her lower it. They used that box for the next live lesson and put the film away. Snow taught the proper grip with her tail clear of the floor.
