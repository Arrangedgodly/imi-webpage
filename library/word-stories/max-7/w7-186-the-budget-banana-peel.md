# The budget banana peel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Budget cuts cost more than they save*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Grove, Clerk Gilda, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Grove cut the bin budget to save coins. Gilda stacked banana peels in a corner because the monkey office had no bins left. Grove slipped on one and broke a desk leg. Gilda paid for a cheap bin from the spare coin jar and swept the floor. Grove added the desk repair to his savings chart. The total fell below zero. He renewed the bin budget and thanked Gilda for the clean walk to his chair.
