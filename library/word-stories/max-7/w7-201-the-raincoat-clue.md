# The raincoat clue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir detectives brood over weather*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Slate, Client Coral, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Slate stood in the rain to look grim while Coral waited indoors. The monkey sleuth soaked his notes and could no longer read the address of her missing cart. Coral gave him a towel and drew the route on the back of a dry bill. Slate traced it to the shed next door. The cart sat under a tarp, full of bananas. He wheeled it back and wrote his report by a warm stove.
