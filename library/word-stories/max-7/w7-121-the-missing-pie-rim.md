# The missing pie rim

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detectives overlook visible evidence*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Detective Dex, Baker Bina, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dex looked for a stolen pie. Bina showed him a pie dish with just a ring of crust left. The monkey found crumbs on his own tail. He had sat on the sill deep in thought and eaten the pie there. Dex paid Bina in bananas, then helped bake a new one. He closed the case with a plain note about his snack. Bina served him lunch before she hired him for another search.
