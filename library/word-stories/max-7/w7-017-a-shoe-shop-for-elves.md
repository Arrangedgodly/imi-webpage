# A shoe shop for elves

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Helpful night workers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Cobbler Dex, Elf Ona, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dex left leather out for elves to sew at night. Ona, a monkey in an elf cap, made six shoes, but all had holes for tails. Dex found them useless until rain flooded the lane. He wore one on his tail and kept his fur dry. He sold the rest as tail boots. Ona got fair pay in bananas, and Dex moved the sewing shift to day so she could sleep.
