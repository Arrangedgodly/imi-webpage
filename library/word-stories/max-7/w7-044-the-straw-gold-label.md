# The straw gold label

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Impossible spinning bargains*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Spinner Sal, Imp Ian, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ian offered to spin straw into gold if Sal guessed his name. Sal, a monkey with a seed shop, asked why anyone wanted gold straw. Ian said it sold well for nest trim. Sal painted a bundle yellow and labeled it Plain Straw. Birds bought that instead. Ian told her his name without a guess and asked for work. She paid him bananas to paint labels, where his fine small brush proved useful.
