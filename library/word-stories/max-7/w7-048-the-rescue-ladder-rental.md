# The rescue ladder rental

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Princess rescue contests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Princess Plum, Knight Noel, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Plum offered a prize to anyone who could reach her balcony. Noel rode up with a ladder, but every knight wanted to borrow it. The monkey charged one banana each and watched them climb. Plum came down the same ladder to join the queue for snacks. She gave Noel the prize for making a safe route. He used it to buy more ladders, and Plum ran the desk that lent them out.
