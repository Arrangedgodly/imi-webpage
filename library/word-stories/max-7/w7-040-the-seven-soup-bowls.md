# The seven soup bowls

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Helpful woodland hosts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Snow Sia, Dwarf Dell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia found seven bowls in a small hut and filled them with soup. Dell came home with six other monkeys, all in dwarf hats. They had meant the bowls for paint. Sia offered to wash them, but Dell tasted the soup and asked her to stay. They painted the hut after lunch. Sia used mashed bananas to make yellow paint for the door, which smelled so good that bees came to visit.
