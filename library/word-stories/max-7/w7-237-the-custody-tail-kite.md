# The custody tail kite

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal custody disputes over shared objects*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Grove, Owners Glen and Gale, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Glen and Gale both claimed a kite. Grove asked each monkey to hold one end of its string. Their tails pulled apart until the paper tore. Grove stopped them and ordered a joint repair. Glen patched the paper while Gale tied a new tail. They flew it side by side at the field after court. Grove split the fee in half and ate bananas on the bench while the shared kite stayed high above them.
