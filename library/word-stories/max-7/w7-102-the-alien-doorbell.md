# The alien doorbell

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: First contact with confusing customs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Scout Sia, Alien Avo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia heard a ring outside her ship. Avo stood there with a plate of bananas and a long green hat. The monkey scout thought the hat was an alien weapon. Avo took it off and showed more fruit inside. He had come to trade hats for spoons. Sia gave him one spoon and took the hat. They rang the bell twice for friends, then sat on the ramp and ate.
