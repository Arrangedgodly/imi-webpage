# The western duel coin

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Duel rituals conceal simple disputes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sheriff Rust, Shopkeeper Rain, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rust called for a duel after Rain gave him the wrong coin. The monkey seller set both coins on the counter and showed they had the same value. Rust stared at the varied faces and finally read the rims. He put his hand away from the empty holster. Rain sold him bananas at the proper price. Rust carried a small coin chart after that, and the town settled coin rows at the counter, leaving the dusty square to lizards.
