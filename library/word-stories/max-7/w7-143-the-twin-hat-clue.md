# The twin hat clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Identical twins confuse investigations*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Flynn, Twins Finn and Fawn, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Flynn accused Finn of taking the red hat. Fawn wore it at the other end of the room. The monkey twins had traded seats while Flynn checked his notes. Flynn asked each to stand by a window and stay there. Finn showed a blue hat in his bag. Fawn gave the red one back to its hook. They shared bananas and labeled their bags, so Flynn could count objects and see who had moved.
