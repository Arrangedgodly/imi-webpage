# The gold pan dinner

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Gold rush greed finds domestic value*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Prospector Pip, Cook Pru, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip panned the creek all day and found no gold. The monkey brought the empty pan to Pru, who filled it with beans and bananas. Pip ate and noticed that the heavy pan stayed warm. He sold similar pans to the camp cooks instead of digging another hole. Pru tested each one over the stove. By winter Pip had a steady trade, and the creek ran clear where he no longer stirred the mud.
