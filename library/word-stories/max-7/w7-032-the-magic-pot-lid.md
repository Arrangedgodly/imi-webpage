# The magic pot lid

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Endless porridge disasters*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Penny, Mage Mel, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mel gave Penny a pot that made hot oats on command. Penny forgot the stop word, and oats filled the monkey hut. She slid the lid under the stream and used it as a chute to the yard. Friends came with bowls and bananas. Mel came back to find a feast in the yard. He said the stop word was Enough. Penny carved it on the lid, then asked for one last small bowl.
