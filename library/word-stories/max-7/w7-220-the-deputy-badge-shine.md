# The deputy badge shine

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western deputies mistake polish for duty*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Deputy Dew, Sheriff Silt, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dew rubbed his badge until it flashed across the town. Silt asked the monkey to find a missing fruit cart. Dew kept at it, sure the shine would scare any thief. Silt traced the cart tracks to a loose wheel by the creek. Dew used the badge pin to hold a patch in place while Silt fixed the axle. They brought the bananas home. Dew let the badge stay scuffed, since it had finally done useful work.
