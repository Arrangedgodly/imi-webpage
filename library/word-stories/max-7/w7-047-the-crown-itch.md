# The crown itch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal curses with practical cures*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Prince Pax, Witch Wye, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pax put on a cursed crown and felt an awful itch. Wye said only a rare herb could end it. The monkey prince took off the crown to scratch his fur. The itch stopped. Wye found rough wire inside the rim and blushed. She padded it with wool. Pax wore it for the feast, then hung it on a hook. He paid Wye in bananas for the pad and skipped the herb quest.
