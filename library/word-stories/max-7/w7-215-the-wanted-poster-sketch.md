# The wanted poster sketch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western wanted posters resemble innocents*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sheriff Rowan, Artist Ash, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash drew a wanted poster from a vague report. Every monkey in town had brown fur and a long tail, just like the sketch. Rowan held no one. He asked Ash to add the missing red scarf and broken boot. Only one guest wore both. The guest had lost his luggage, not stolen it, and showed the claim tag. Rowan gave back the bag of bananas and asked for written facts before the next poster.
