# The vault echo rehearsal

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist crews practice in inappropriate venues*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Planner Echo, Guard Ember, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Echo tried his vault speech in a tunnel. Ember heard each word through the vent by the monkey guard desk. She came down with a map and showed Echo where the sound went. Echo gave up the theft plan and offered a tunnel tour instead. Ember checked the route and sold tickets for daytime visits. Echo earned bananas from guests who liked the echo. He kept all talks away from the old vault wall.
