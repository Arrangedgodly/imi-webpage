# The secret handshake ache

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy rituals become impractical*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Dawn, Agent Dell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dawn and Dell devised a secret hand grip with twelve turns and a tail twirl. The monkeys forgot the last twist and tied their tails in a knot. Dawn reached for bananas with one free hand while Dell read the rules. They undid the knot by turning the other way. Their new hello was one plain nod. Dell burned the old rules in the stove and used the warm pan to cook a snack before they left for work.
