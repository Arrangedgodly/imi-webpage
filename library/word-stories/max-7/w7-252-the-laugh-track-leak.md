# The laugh track leak

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Sitcom laughter overrides actual dialogue*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Bea, Sound Sol, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea gave a sad speech while Sol played a loud laugh track. The monkey actor stopped and pointed to the wrong tape. Sol rewound it, but laughs still filled the room. Bea cut power to the speaker and spoke in silence. The crew heard the scene clearly at last. They ate bananas during the reset. Sol labeled each tape by mood, and Bea let him test the sad one before she began her next line.
