# The vanishing ink list

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mystery notes with domestic explanations*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Detective Dawn, Cook Chet, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dawn saw blank pages where Chet had written the meal list. The monkey sleuth blamed secret ink. She warmed a page over the stove, but no words came back. Chet showed her the sponge he had used to wipe sauce off the book. He had wiped the pencil too. Dawn wrote a fresh list on a board. Chet hung it above the sink, and they ate the bananas the old list had omitted.
