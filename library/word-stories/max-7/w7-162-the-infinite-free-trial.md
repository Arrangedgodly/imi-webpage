# The infinite free trial

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Subscription traps consume stock*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Founder Frost, User Uma, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Frost offered a free trial of his banana club. Uma signed up each week with a new hat and got more fruit. The monkey founder ran out of stock before anyone paid. Frost asked her to show her tail instead of her hat. Uma said it was the same tail every time. She paid for one month and helped sort the signups. Frost kept the free sample to one banana, which was still enough to taste.
