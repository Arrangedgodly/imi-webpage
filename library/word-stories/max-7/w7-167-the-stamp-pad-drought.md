# The stamp pad drought

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Administrative tools halt useful work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Juniper, Clerk Lark, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Juniper could not issue fruit permits because the stamp pad was dry. Lark asked the monkey to sign each form instead. Juniper said names lacked the right shape. Lark drew a small square around each signed name. The permits went out and the bananas reached the market before noon. Juniper ordered fresh ink that day. When it came, she used the stamp only on final forms and kept her pen beside it for dry days.
