# The mascot penalty

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Team mascots interfere with play*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Mascot Moe, Captain Cass, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Moe wore a giant banana suit to cheer the team. His monkey tail dragged behind and tripped Cass near the goal. The referee sent Moe to the bench. Moe cut a hole for his tail, then made small flags from the spare cloth. He waved them from his seat and kept score with bananas. Cass scored twice. Moe gave the team the fruit and folded the suit into a tidy bag for the flags.
