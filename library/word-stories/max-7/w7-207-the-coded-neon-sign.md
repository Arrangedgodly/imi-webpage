# The coded neon sign

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir detectives read patterns into faults*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Grim, Owner Opal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Grim watched a neon sign blink over the club. He read its flashes as a secret warning. Opal, the monkey owner, found him writing dots and lines on a napkin. She showed him a loose wire behind the sign. Grim held the ladder while she fixed it. The sign lit up. It said Open. Grim went inside, ordered bananas, and used the napkin to wipe his muddy shoes before sitting at a table.
