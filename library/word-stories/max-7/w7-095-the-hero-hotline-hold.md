# The hero hotline hold

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Urgent calls meet waiting systems*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hugh, Caller Calla, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Calla rang Hugh because her kite was stuck in a tree. A taped voice told the monkey to hold. Calla put the phone down and built a stool from her fruit crates. Hugh picked up just as she freed the kite. She asked him to help put the crates back instead. He arrived with bananas and did the heavy lifting. Hugh cut the hold message and kept a pencil by the phone to note each real job.
