# The shadow on the wall

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir suspicion built on silhouettes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Soot, Clerk Ember, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Soot saw a giant shadow at the bank and called Ember to lock the safe. The monkey clerk turned the lamp and found a small moth on its glass. Soot put his hat over the insect and carried it outside. Ember counted the coins and found none missing. Soot wrote Moth on the case file. He kept the lamp farther from the wall, and the bank opened in time to buy the morning bananas.
