# The courtroom wig vent

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Judges dress codes cause practical trouble*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Heath, Clerk Holly, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Heath wore a huge white wig in court. The monkey judge leaned forward and blocked the desk fan. Holly saw the papers heat and curl beside his lamp. She asked him to move the wig, but he called it a symbol of the law. Holly turned the fan toward his face. The wig blew off onto the filing rack. Heath wore a small cap from then on and let the clerk keep cool while noting each case.
