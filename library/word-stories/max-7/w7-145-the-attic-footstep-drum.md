# The attic footstep drum

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Haunted houses with mundane noises*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Hark, Musician Harp, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hark heard heavy steps in the attic each night. He climbed up with a lamp, his monkey tail stiff with fear. Harp sat there beating a drum with her feet while she ate bananas. She played after dark because the attic was cool. Hark moved a chair and rug up to soften the sound. Harp played him a quiet tune. The house stayed calm. Hark slept till dawn with his lamp off.
