# The epic speech wind

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical film speeches meet actual weather*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Oak, Director Orla, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Oak gave a vast speech on a hill while wind tugged his monkey cape. Orla could hear none of the words. Oak tried yelling, then lost his hat down the slope. The crew filmed his stance without sound and taped the speech indoors later. Oak fetched the hat and wrapped the bananas inside it for lunch. Orla kept the wind in the final scene, but the viewers could finally hear what the hero had said.
