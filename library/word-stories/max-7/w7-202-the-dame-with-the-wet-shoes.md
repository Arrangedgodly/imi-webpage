# The dame with the wet shoes

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir clients hide obvious motives*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Storm, Client Pearl, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pearl asked Storm to find her stolen shoes. The monkey sleuth saw wet prints from her chair to the door. Pearl had put the shoes by the stove, then lost track of them. Storm fetched them but found the heels warped by heat. He lent her flat boots for the walk home. Pearl paid him with bananas and bought new heels the next day. Storm kept a shoe rack far from his own stove after that case.
