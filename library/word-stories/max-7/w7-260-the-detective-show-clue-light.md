# The detective show clue light

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Crime television hides evidence in darkness*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Kestrel, Lighting Kit, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kestrel checked a dark room for the prop clue. Kit dimmed the lamp further to make the scene tense. The monkey actor stepped on the clue and broke it. Kit turned the light up and fetched a spare prop clue. They cleared the broken bits, reset the clue, and shot again with enough light to see. Kestrel solved the case on camera. Kit saved the darkest lamp setting for the credits, after every actor left.
