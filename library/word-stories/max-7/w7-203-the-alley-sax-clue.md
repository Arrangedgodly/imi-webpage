# The alley sax clue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir soundtracks reveal practical facts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Steel, Player Sax, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Steel heard a sad sax tune in the alley each night. He thought it marked a secret trade. The monkey watched from behind a crate of bananas until Sax opened the back door. Sax played there because his room had thin walls. Steel asked him to play inside with a rug by the door. The sound grew soft. Steel gave Sax the empty crate to sell for a mute. Both could sleep after the late night tunes.
