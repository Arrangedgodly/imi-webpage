# The pop star mirror

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Pop performers chase perfect images*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Singer Dawn, Dresser Dell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dawn wanted every strand of fur in place before her concert. Dell combed the monkey for an hour while the guests waited. One curl refused to lie flat. Dell tied a small ribbon around it and opened the stage door. Dawn sang with the curl waving above her brow. Fans copied the ribbon next day. Dawn kept the combing short from then on and ate bananas with Dell in the spare time before each show.
