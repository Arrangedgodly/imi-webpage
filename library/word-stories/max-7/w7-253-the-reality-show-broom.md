# The reality show broom

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Reality contests stage trivial conflicts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Host Chet, Guest Gilda, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Chet told Gilda to fight another guest over the last broom. The monkey guest took a dust pan and swept with dry leaves instead. Chet said that made poor drama. Gilda showed him the clean floor and asked for a task worth a prize. He gave the guests a shelf to build as a team. The show aired their work with no staged row. Gilda won bananas for the neatest shelf and used the broom only later.
