# The stage sword of foam

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Stage battles need sensible prop design*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Actor Cedar, Prop Cora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cedar swung a metal sword and knocked a lamp off the stage. Cora caught it before the monkey could begin his next move. She made a foam sword and painted it gray. Cedar tried the same fight slowly, keeping well clear of the lights. The viewers saw a grand duel, but every strike was soft. Cora stored the real sword in a locked rack and used the spare foam to line the box of prop bananas.
