# The sidekick map

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroes ignore practical sidekicks*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Heath, Sidekick Sid, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Heath charged into the cave while Sid checked the map. The monkey hero reached a wall and struck a bold pose. Sid found a side door that led to the stolen bananas. Heath tried to claim the rescue, but his cape was still caught on a nail inside. Sid freed him and shared the fruit. Heath gave Sid the map badge and asked him to lead the next mission while he carried bags.
