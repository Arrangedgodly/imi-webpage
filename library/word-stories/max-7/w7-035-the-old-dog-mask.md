# The old dog mask

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Learning new tricks fables*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Dog Don, Pup Pat, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Don said he was too old to learn new tricks. Pat tied a dog mask over his monkey face and asked him to fetch a stick. Don refused until the stick held a bunch of bananas. He brought it back with his tail. Pat tried that too and fell on his nose. Don taught him how to curl his tail first. Pat gave him the fruit and asked for a slower lesson next time.
