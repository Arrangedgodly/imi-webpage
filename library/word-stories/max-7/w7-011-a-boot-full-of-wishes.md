# A boot full of wishes

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Three careless wishes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Sol, Fairy Rue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fairy Rue gave Sol three wishes. Sol wished for more bananas, then for a boot to hold them all. The boot grew so large that it blocked his hut. His monkey tail stuck beneath the heel. Rue waited for his last wish. Sol asked for the boot to have wheels. He rolled it to the square and made a fruit cart. Rue bought the first bunch and got a free ride.
