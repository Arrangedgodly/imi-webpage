# The blue fur case

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detectives infer crime from odd color*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Davy, Painter Paz, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Davy saw blue fur on Paz and asked who had hurt him. The monkey painter showed him a mural of the sea. He had leaned into the wet paint as he reached for bananas. Davy fetched soap and helped wash his tail. The blue faded, but one patch stayed on his ear. Paz kept it until the mural dried. Davy wrote the case up as Paint Spill and used the spare soap to clean his desk.
