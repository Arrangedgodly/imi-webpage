# The short cut race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Slow and steady races*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Tess, Rook, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rook raced Tess round the lake. His monkey feet flew while Tess took small steps. He found a short cut through a shed full of bananas and stopped to eat. Tess reached the last bend and sat down. A cart had tipped and blocked the route to the line. Rook came out with a full mouth and helped her shift it. They crossed at once, and shared the prize bench for a nap.
