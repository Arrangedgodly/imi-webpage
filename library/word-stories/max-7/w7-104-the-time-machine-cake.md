# The time machine cake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Time travel to fix tiny regrets*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Timer Tess, Baker Ben, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess used her time machine to save a burnt cake. She went back one minute, then tripped over her own tail and missed the oven again. The monkey tried twice more and found three copies of herself in the kitchen. Ben gave each Tess a job. One watched the clock, one washed dishes, and one sliced bananas. The first Tess baked a fresh cake. They ate four slices before the machine drew them back into one.
