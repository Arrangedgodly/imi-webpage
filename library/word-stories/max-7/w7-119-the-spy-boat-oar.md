# The spy boat oar

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Chases ruined by missing equipment*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Birch, Pilot Plum, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Birch leaped into a boat to chase a secret courier. Plum pushed off, then found the oars missing. The monkeys used flat boxes from their lunch as paddles. They reached the other boat at the dock. The courier had brought their oars back from the repair shop. Birch took them and paid with bananas. Plum kept the boxes as spare paddles, after washing all the soft fruit out of their corners.
