# The jazz solo map

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Jazz improvisation loses the shared tune*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Sax Nix, Drummer Nell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nix strayed so far from the melody that Nell could not follow. The monkey drummer held a steady beat while Nix looked for the chorus. At last Nell tapped the opening phrase on a small bell. Nix heard it and joined the song again. They wrote the key changes on one card for the next show. After the set they ate bananas, and Nix kept the bell close as a simple way back home.
