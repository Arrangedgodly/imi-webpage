# The tennis moon shot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Overpowered sports serves*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Ace Amy, Coach Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Amy hit a tennis ball into the clouds. Dot praised her power, then asked how to win a point without a ball. The monkey tried a softer serve. It rolled under the net and hit a crate of bananas. Dot used a lid as a low target. Amy hit it ten times, then served over the net at last. The lost ball fell on the court at dusk and scored nothing at all.
