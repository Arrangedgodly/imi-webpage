# The goalie fruit basket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports heroes with improvised gear*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Goalie Gus, Coach Nell, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus had no gloves for the big match. Nell gave him two fruit baskets. The monkey caught each ball, but bananas fell out at every save. The crowd ate them and cheered. At the last kick, a peel lay beside his foot. Gus used his tail to sweep it clear and caught the ball cleanly. Nell bought real gloves with the prize coins. Gus kept a basket by the goal for snacks at the break.
