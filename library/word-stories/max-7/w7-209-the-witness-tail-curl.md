# The witness tail curl

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Noir lie detection misreads habits*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Flint, Witness Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Flint thought Fern lied because her tail curled when he asked about the safe. The monkey witness said her chair was too close to the hot pipe. Flint moved it and asked again. Her tail relaxed, and she named the clerk who had changed the lock. Flint checked the receipt and found a legal repair. Fern helped him file the case. He gave her the cool chair and put a guard over the pipe.
