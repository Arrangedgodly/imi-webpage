# The sculpture tail crack

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Artists prize scale over safety*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Sculptor Grove, Builder Gilda, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Grove made a tall stone monkey with a thin tail. Gilda saw a crack near its base before the opening. Grove wanted guests to see it as it was. Gilda roped the area off and tested the tail with a padded pole. It broke into the soft grass. Grove carved the stump smooth and set the fallen tail beside it. The show opened safely, with bananas on a low table well away from the heavy stone pieces.
