# The bowling tail lane

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Trick shots in bowling*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Bowler Belle, Lane Lee, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Belle rolled balls with her tail to impress the crowd. One ball curved into the snack stand and crushed the bananas. Lane Lee closed her lane until she learned to aim. The monkey used soft balls and empty cups in the yard. By dusk she could hit one cup at a time. Belle swept the snack stand, made a fresh fruit mash, and rolled a plain strike with both hands the next day.
