# The wise owl glasses

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wise advisers who cannot see*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Owl Odo, Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Odo gave advice from a high branch while wearing an owl hood. Fern asked where her lost bananas had gone. He pointed to a cloud. Fern saw them on the branch beside his tail. The monkey had worn the hood back to front and could see only through its ear holes. She turned it round and gave him the fruit. Odo checked that he could see his own feet before each new advice session.
