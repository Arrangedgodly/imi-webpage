# The gumshoe gum

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Detective nicknames become literal problems*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Grit, Cleaner Gale, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Grit stepped in gum outside the fruit stall. Each monkey foot stuck to the next, and he could not follow the thief. Gale brought cold water and a flat scraper. Grit freed his shoes and found the fruit seller had taken the bananas inside to avoid rain. There had been no theft. He paid Gale for her work and helped sweep the stall. His new case notes began with a check of the weather.
