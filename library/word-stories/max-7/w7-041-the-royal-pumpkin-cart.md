# The royal pumpkin cart

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Midnight transformation deadlines*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Cindy, Fairy Fay, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fay turned a pumpkin into a cart for Cindy. At twelve it would turn back. Cindy, a monkey who hated waste, drove to the ball with bowls and a spoon. When the clock struck, she parked beside the kitchen. The wheels became seeds and the cart became a large pumpkin. She made soup for the late guests and packed bananas for the walk home. Fay admired the empty peel at dawn.
