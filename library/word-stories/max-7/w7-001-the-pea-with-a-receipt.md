# The pea with a receipt

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal sensitivity tests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Pip, Queen Ada, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Queen Ada hid a pea beneath the guest bed. Pip, a small monkey, felt no lump at all. His tail did find a receipt that said the bed cost ten bananas. He took the receipt to Ada and asked why a fine bed had loose springs. Ada tried it herself. One spring poked her fur. She hired Pip to mend the bed and served the pea in soup.
