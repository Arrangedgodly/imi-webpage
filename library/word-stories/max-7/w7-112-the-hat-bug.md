# The hat bug

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spy devices hidden in clothing*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Ash, Clerk Clay, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash put a tiny sound bug in the hat of Clerk Clay. The monkey clerk lent his hat to a fruit seller, so Ash heard only prices all day. He went to fetch the bug and found it stuck to a bunch of bananas. Clay helped him take it off with a spoon. Ash paid for the damaged fruit. His report said the market had fresh stock, which was true and useful to the cook.
