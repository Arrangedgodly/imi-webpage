# The gravity switch snack

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Science fiction switches solve daily chores*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Scientist Sol, Guest Gem, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sol turned gravity down to clean beneath his desk. Gem came in with bananas, and the fruit floated to the roof. The monkey guests chased it with their tails but could not reach. Sol tied a spoon to a cord and hooked each bunch down. He turned gravity back up before serving lunch. Gem asked for a plain broom next time. Sol kept the cord, which also pulled dust from behind the shelf.
