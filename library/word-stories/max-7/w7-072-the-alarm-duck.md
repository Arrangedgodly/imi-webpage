# The alarm duck

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroes respond to every siren*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hett, Duck Dan, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hett heard a loud quack and flew to the pond. Dan, a monkey in a duck suit, was testing the village alarm. Hett saved him from a puddle he meant to sit in. Dan made a sign that said Test Day. Hett helped hang it beside the bell and waited for a real call. When a cart tipped, they both went to lift it. The driver gave them bananas and a quiet thank you.
