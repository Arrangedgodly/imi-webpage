# The sequel spare door

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Movie sequels preserve unresolved hazards*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Director Pine, Actor Pax, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pine wanted the broken door left hanging so a sequel could use it. Pax saw the hinge wobble over the monkey crew. He propped the door safely and asked to film a repair scene. Pine agreed, though he feared it would end the story. The viewers liked seeing the hero finish the job. Pax carried the spare hinge home, and Pine planned a new sequel about a new door instead of leaving this one unsafe.
