# The exhibit spoon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Lawyers argue over obvious objects*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Lawyer Fenn, Judge Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fenn showed a spoon as proof that a cake had been stolen. Fern asked how a spoon proved that claim. The monkey lawyer showed crumbs, but the crumbs were from his own lunch. Fern set the spoon aside and asked for the shop record. It showed the cake had been sold and paid for. Fenn cleaned the spoon and gave it back to the cook. He ate bananas with his hands while he rewrote the report.
