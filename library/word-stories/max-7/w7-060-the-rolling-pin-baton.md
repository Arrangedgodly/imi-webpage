# The rolling pin baton

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Relay cooking contests*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Baker Bea, Runner Ren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea gave Ren a rolling pin for the kitchen relay. Ren raced so fast that dough wrapped round his monkey tail. He reached the next table with no dough left on the tray. Bea dusted his tail with flour and rolled it over the sheet. The dough came off in a neat spiral. They baked it with bananas inside. The judges asked for the recipe, so Bea drew a tail beside the rolling pin.
