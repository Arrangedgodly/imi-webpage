# The lake gives back a spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Honest woodcutter rewards*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Axe Ari, Lady Eda, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari dropped his axe in the lake. Eda rose from the reeds with a gold spoon and asked if it was his. The monkey said no. She brought a silver fork. He said no again. At last she found the axe, stuck in a log beside the bank. Ari freed it with his tail. Eda gave him the spoon for his care. He used it to serve her mashed bananas on shore.
