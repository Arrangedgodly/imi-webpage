# The secret tunnel sign

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Hidden bases need directions*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Alder, Guest Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Alder made a hidden tunnel to his spy hut. Gus could not find it and spent an hour going round the grove. The monkey agent wanted to keep the route secret, but his lunch guest had all the bananas. Alder hung a small sign that said Roots with an arrow toward the hatch. Gus read it and knocked. After lunch they changed the sign to a plain leaf mark that both could read from the path.
