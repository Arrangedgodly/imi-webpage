# The crane lunch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Unequal dinner vessels*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Fox Flo, Crane Kai, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Flo served soup on flat plates, though Kai had a long paper beak for the play. Kai planned revenge with tall jars. The monkey cooks stopped when both spilled soup on their fur. Flo found wide cups and Kai took his beak off. They ate in peace, then used the jars for flowers. Their guests brought bananas and asked to keep the plates, which made fine hats for the next play.
