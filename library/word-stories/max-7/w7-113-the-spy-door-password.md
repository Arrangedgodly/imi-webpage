# The spy door password

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret agents forget their own codes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Elm, Guard Glen, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Elm reached the secret door but forgot the code. Glen offered a hint, saying it was a yellow fruit. The monkey tried lemon, then mango, then pear. His tail brushed a crate of bananas beside the wall. Glen pointed at it. Elm spoke the right word and entered. Inside, he changed the code to Tail so he would always have a hint with him. Glen moved the fruit crate into the kitchen.
