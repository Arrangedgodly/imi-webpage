# The dragon needs a shelf

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dragon hoards and brave knights*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Sir Nim, Drake, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sir Nim rode to a cave to face a dragon. Drake was a large monkey with a smoke pot and wings of cloth. His heap of bananas kept falling on the pot, so the cave smelled burnt. Nim put down his lance and built a shelf. Drake stacked the fruit on it and took off the wings. Nim earned a medal for fire safety, though he asked for a chair instead.
