# The window height rule

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Regulations ignore actual users*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Inspector Maple, Clerk Oak, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Maple ordered the service window raised to keep tails off the counter. Oak, a short monkey clerk, could no longer see guests. He stacked boxes of bananas to reach it. Maple saw the boxes wobble and asked him to stop. Oak moved the window lower and added a rail for tails instead. Maple tested it with her own tail and signed the change. The fruit went back into storage, and Oak served guests from the floor.
