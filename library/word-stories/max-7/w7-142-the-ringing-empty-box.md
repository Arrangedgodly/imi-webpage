# The ringing empty box

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mysterious packages and hidden sounds*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Flint, Clerk Cora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

A box rang on the counter though no one had put a bell inside. Flint held it to his ear and shook it while Cora watched. The monkey finally opened the bottom flap. A small clock had slipped between the layers of card. Cora stopped its alarm and found the shop receipt tucked beside it. Flint packed the bananas in a new box. Cora took the clock home, where it woke her in time to open the shop.
