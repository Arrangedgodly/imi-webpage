# The chess cape

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports rituals and lucky outfits*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chess Chet, Rival Ora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Chet wore a cape to every chess match. It made his monkey tail itch, but he thought it brought luck. Ora saw the cape brush three pawns off the board. They stopped and put the pieces back. Chet took the cape off and used it as a shade over their bananas. He lost the match by one move. Ora lent him her notes, and he studied them in the cool shade he had made.
