# The remote office branch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Remote work equipment*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Manager Amber, Worker Arbor, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Amber sent Arbor a desk for remote work. The monkey lived in a tree, where the flat desk could not fit between the limbs. Arbor hung its drawers from ropes and used one as a lap tray. Amber saw him work well on a video call. She asked where the desk top had gone. Arbor aimed the camera below. It was a roof over the mail box, keeping every letter dry.
