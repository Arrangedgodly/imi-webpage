# The space suit sneeze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Protective suits cause new problems*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Pilot Petal, Medic Mica, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Petal sneezed inside her space suit and could not wipe her nose. Mica told the monkey to come into the air lock before taking her helmet off. Petal waited through the slow cycle, then found a feather stuck to her fur. Mica removed it and cleaned the glass. Petal kept bananas outside the suit for lunch. Her next walk went well, with no feather and a clear view of each moon rock.
