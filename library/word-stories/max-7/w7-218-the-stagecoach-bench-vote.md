# The stagecoach bench vote

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western passengers challenge dramatic drivers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Driver Dust, Guest Della, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dust drove the coach so fast that Della bounced off her seat. The monkey guests voted to stop until he slowed down. Dust claimed the mail needed speed. Della showed him the letters shaken open in the rack. He tied them with a cord and set a slower pace. The coach reached town with every page intact. Della bought bananas for the guests, and Dust checked the wheels for loose bolts, then joined them.
