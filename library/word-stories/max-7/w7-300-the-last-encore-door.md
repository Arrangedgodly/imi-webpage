# The last encore door

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Concert performers ignore venue closing time*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Singer Iris, Keeper Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Iris took a third encore while Ivy waited to lock the hall. The monkey crowd wanted more, but the last bus would soon leave. Iris sang one brief verse, thanked all guests, and opened the side door. Guests reached the stop in time. Ivy helped her pack the mic and cables. Iris left bananas in the crew room as thanks and put a clear final song on the next program, so claps could end without another endless encore.
