# The muddy paw clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mystery clues identify the wrong suspect*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Detective Dot, Gardener Gale, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot found muddy prints by the empty banana shelf. She accused Gale, whose monkey feet were always dirty. Gale showed her a fresh broom he had used to clean the shed. Dot swept the floor and found a loose wheel track under the prints. The fruit cart had rolled away through the open gate. They fetched it as a team. Dot washed her own muddy feet before writing the report and sharing the bananas.
