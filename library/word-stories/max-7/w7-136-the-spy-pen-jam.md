# The spy pen jam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Gadget pens cannot do ordinary work*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Aura, Clerk Creek, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Aura used a pen that could shoot rope, flash light, and play songs. Creek asked her to sign for a parcel of bananas. The monkey pressed the wrong switch and tied the parcel to the desk. Creek lent her a plain pencil. Aura signed, cut the rope, and put the pen back in its case. She used the parcel string to tie a pencil onto the case so it would always be close.
