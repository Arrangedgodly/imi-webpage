# The cactus fence gap

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western property claims cause silly conflicts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Rancher Thorn, Neighbor Moss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Thorn planted cactus along his border. Moss could no longer bring bananas through to the shared market. The monkey rivals argued until Thorn caught his own tail on a spine. Moss trimmed a clear path and helped pull the thorn out. They built one gate between the cactus rows. Thorn kept his fence, Moss kept the route, and both could carry crates without needing thick rugs over their fur on every trip.
