# The coach chalk storm

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Complex sports plans*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Coach Craig, Player Pia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Craig filled a board with arrows for the final play. Pia could not see which way to run. The monkey coach wiped half the lines away, then half again. Only one arrow stayed. Pia ran that way and found a basket of bananas beside the field. Craig had drawn on the lunch map by mistake. They ate, then drew the play on the proper board. Pia scored before the chalk dust settled.
