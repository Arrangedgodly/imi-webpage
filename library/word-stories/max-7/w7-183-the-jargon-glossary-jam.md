# The jargon glossary jam

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate language conceals simple tasks*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Ewan, Clerk Ember, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ewan asked Ember to align the fruit task flow. The monkey clerk asked if he meant sort the bananas. Ewan said the task was part of a grander plan. Ember put ripe fruit in one bin and green fruit in another. Sales stopped wasting soft stock. Ewan praised the new bins. Ember wrote Sort Fruit beside the phrase in his word book. The next clerk read that line and got to work without needing a long meeting.
