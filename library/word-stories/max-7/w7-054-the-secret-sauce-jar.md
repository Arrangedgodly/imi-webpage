# The secret sauce jar

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Family recipe secrets*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Cora, Aunt Ann, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cora would not share her secret sauce with Aunt Ann. She hid the jar in a hollow stump. A monkey troop mistook it for glue and used it on a kite. Cora found the kite stuck to the tree, with a smell of bananas. Ann helped soak the joints in warm water. Cora wrote the sauce name on a fresh jar and gave Ann the recipe. The kite flew after lunch with no sticky tail.
