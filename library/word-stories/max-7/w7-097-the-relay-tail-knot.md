# The relay tail knot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Teamwork rituals impede races*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Runner Rai, Runner Rem, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rai and Rem tied tails to prove their team bond. The monkeys could run in a line but not turn at the track bend. They fell into the soft grass beside the bananas. Rem untied the knot and handed Rai a short stick instead. Rai ran his leg, passed the stick, and cheered from the edge. Rem crossed the line without hauling a friend. They tied a knot in the prize ribbon and hung it over the stick.
