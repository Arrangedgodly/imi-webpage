# The secret menu tail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Exclusive restaurants and password dishes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Chef Cher, Guest Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cher kept a secret menu behind the counter. Gus guessed every code he knew, but none worked. The monkey leaned on his tail and knocked the menu onto the floor. It listed plain beans and bananas at half price. Cher said the secret was meant for regular guests. Gus came back each week, paid for lunch, and washed his own bowl. Cher put the menu on the wall so new guests could join him.
