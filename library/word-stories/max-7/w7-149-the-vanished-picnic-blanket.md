# The vanished picnic blanket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Disappearing objects and wind clues*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Sleuth Lake, Host Lila, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lila set bananas on a blanket and went for cups. The blanket was gone before she came back. Lake checked the monkey yard and saw a square patch of shade under a tree. The wind had lifted the blanket into its limbs. Lake climbed up while Lila held a basket below. The fruit fell safely into it. They tied stones to each corner of the blanket and ate their picnic beneath the same tree.
