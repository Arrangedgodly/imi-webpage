# The museum audio nap

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Museum tours overexplain exhibits*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Guide Oak, Visitor Orla, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Oak taped a long audio guide for one small bowl. Orla heard it until the monkey fell asleep on a bench. Oak found her there beside a bag of bananas. He cut the guide to a short account of who made the bowl and how it was used. Orla woke and tried the new version. She could now tour the whole room before lunch. Oak kept extra facts in a booklet for guests who chose to stay longer.
