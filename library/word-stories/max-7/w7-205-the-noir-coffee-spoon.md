# The noir coffee spoon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Hardboiled detectives cannot manage coffee*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Sleuth Smoke, Waiter Wren, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Smoke took his coffee black and cold to look tough. Wren saw the monkey shiver and added hot water. Smoke argued until his tail stopped shaking. He finally asked for a spoon and milk. Wren served bananas with the cup. Smoke solved the case of a missing coat while he warmed up, finding it on the hook behind him. He left a fair tip and wore the coat on the rainy walk home.
