# A troll with a ticket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bridge trolls and toll riddles*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Troll Tom, June, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tom sat under the bridge and charged a riddle as toll. June, a monkey bus driver, had twenty guests who all gave the same wrong guess. Tom would not let them pass. June handed him a ticket and a bunch of bananas. He rode across with them to see where the bus went. At the far bank he found a riddle club and stayed there, leaving the bridge free for traffic.
