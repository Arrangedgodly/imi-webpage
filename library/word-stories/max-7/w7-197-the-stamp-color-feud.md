# The stamp color feud

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Departments fight over office symbols*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Quill, Clerk Quartz, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Quill used a red stamp, but Quartz would accept only blue ones. The monkey clerks sent a fruit permit back and forth until the page turned purple. The cart waited outside with bananas going soft. Quill signed a fresh page and Quartz signed it too. The cart left. They put both stamps in a drawer and used plain names from then on. The purple page became a desk mat to show how much ink their silly feud could waste.
