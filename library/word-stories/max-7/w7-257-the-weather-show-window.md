# The weather show window

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Broadcasts trust scripts over visible weather*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-006*
*Cast: Host Gale, Producer Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gale read a report of clear skies while rain hit the studio window. Gus pointed at the script, asking the monkey to keep going. Gale set the page down and looked outside. She told viewers to bring coats, then opened the window to show the rain. Gus fetched a towel for the wet floor. They amended the report and moved the bananas away from the sill. The next script left a blank for what Gale saw herself.
