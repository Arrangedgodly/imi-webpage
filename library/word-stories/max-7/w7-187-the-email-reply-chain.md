# The email reply chain

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Reply all chains reward noise*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Holly, Boss Heath, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Heath emailed one query to the troop. Holly replied, then every monkey replied to thank her. The inbox filled while the work waited. Holly sent a last note that asked for no more thanks. It caused another dozen replies. Heath walked to each desk and asked them to stop. He wrote the answer on the wall for anyone who needed it. Holly shut her mail app and did the job before the kettle boiled.
