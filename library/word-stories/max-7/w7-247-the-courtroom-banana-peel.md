# The courtroom banana peel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Evidence creates new hazards*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Reed, Lawyer Rue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rue brought a banana peel to prove the hallway floor was unsafe. The monkey lawyer left it by the witness chair. Reed nearly slipped and ordered it put in a sealed box. Rue said sorry and showed the photo taken before the fall. The court used that image and kept the floor clear. Reed ruled that the hall needed a bin. Rue paid for a second bin beside his own desk, where loose proof had begun to pile.
