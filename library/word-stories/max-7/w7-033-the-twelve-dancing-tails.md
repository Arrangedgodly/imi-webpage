# The twelve dancing tails

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret royal dancing*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Princess Pia, Guard Glen, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia and her sisters wore out their shoes each night. Guard Glen hid by the door to find out why. The monkeys used the shoes as drums while their tails danced. Pia feared the king would ban the noise. Glen lined the soles with soft moss and helped them set a quiet beat. The king slept well, the shoes lasted, and Glen got bananas for his first lesson in tail dance.
