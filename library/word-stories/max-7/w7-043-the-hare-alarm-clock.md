# The hare alarm clock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Overconfident racers nap*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Hare Hank, Tortoise Ty, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hank took a nap during the race and set an alarm in his fur. Ty walked past, heard it ring, and thought a monkey needed help. He woke Hank, who sprang up and ran the wrong way. Ty called him back to the trail. They came in late, after the prize bananas had gone soft. Hank mashed them into cake. Ty cut two large slices and they ate beneath the silent clock.
