# The spy magnet spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magnetic gadgets collect unintended objects*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-003*
*Cast: Agent Frost, Cook Flora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Frost turned on a pocket magnet to catch a stolen key. Every spoon in the kitchen flew toward his monkey coat. Flora asked him to turn it off before her soup got cold. Frost caught the spoons in a bowl as they fell. The key landed among them. He opened the pantry and brought out bananas for dessert. Flora let him keep one wooden spoon, which did not move when he tested the magnet again.
