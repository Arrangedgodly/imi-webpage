# The court banana scale

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal debates over portion fairness*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-005*
*Cast: Judge Alder, Claimant Ash, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash sued over a small banana in his lunch box. Judge Alder asked the monkey to weigh it beside the large one given to his friend. The large banana had a thick peel and less fruit. Ash weighed only the fruit and found his own share larger. He dropped the claim. Alder split both bananas between them for lunch and ordered the stall to list edible weight, so guests could compare the actual shares.
