# The marble egg mother

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mistaken magical eggs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Mum Mae, Chick Cora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mae found a marble egg and sat on it for days. Cora, a young monkey, brought her bananas while she waited for it to hatch. At last Cora saw a small hole through the stone. It was a bead from the royal curtain. Mae gave it back and got a soft cushion as thanks. She sat on that instead. Cora used the cushion box as a nest for toy birds that hatched on time.
