# The shield soup lid

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroes defend with famous shields*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-002*
*Cast: Hero Hazel, Cook Cam, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hazel blocked hail with her shiny shield. The monkey crowd stayed dry, but Cam could not find the lid for his soup pot. Hazel looked at the handle on her shield and saw his name on it. She gave it back before the soup boiled over. Cam lent her a broad tray for the next storm. Hazel served bananas from it when the sky cleared and kept it out of the dish rack.
