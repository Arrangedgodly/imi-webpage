# The lost form folder

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Forms require proof of missing forms*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Clerk Laurel, Guest Flint, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Flint lost the form needed to request a new form. Laurel asked for the old form as proof. The monkey guest showed his empty folder. Laurel stared at it until Flint offered her bananas from his other bag. She ate one and thought again. Laurel wrote Lost on a fresh sheet and let Flint sign it. He got the form he needed. She kept the empty folder as a place to put rules that needed review.
