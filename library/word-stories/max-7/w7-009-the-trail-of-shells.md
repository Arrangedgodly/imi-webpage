# The trail of shells

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost children and edible trails*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Lark, Reed, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark and Reed left bits of banana on the path to mark their way home. Birds ate the bits. The young monkeys reached a pond and saw no trail. Reed cracked nuts and placed the shells in pairs on stones. Lark climbed a tree to spot their roof. They laid more shells on the path toward it and came home at dusk. Next day they took a map and kept the bananas for lunch.
