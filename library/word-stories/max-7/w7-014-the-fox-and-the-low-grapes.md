# The fox and the low grapes

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sour grapes fables*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-001*
*Cast: Fox Fen, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fen wore a fox mask and claimed he could reach any fruit. A bunch of grapes hung just above his tail. He said they must taste bad. Ivy, a young monkey, placed a box below the vine and picked one. It was sweet. Fen climbed the box too, but it tipped. Ivy held it still. Fen shared the grapes and traded his mask for a basket that could hold fruit but could not boast.
