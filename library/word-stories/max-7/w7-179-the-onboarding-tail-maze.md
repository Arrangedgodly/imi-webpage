# The onboarding tail maze

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: New hire training overload*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W7-004*
*Cast: Boss Cedar, Newcomer Nettle, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cedar gave Nettle a stack of guides on her first day. The monkey could not see the door over the pages. She set the stack down and asked where to put her coat. Cedar found the coat hooks hidden behind a poster about welcome habits. He moved it, then led her through the office with one short map. Nettle filed the guides and took her first real task, sorting mail that had waited all morning.
