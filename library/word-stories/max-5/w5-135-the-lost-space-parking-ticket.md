# The lost space parking ticket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space travel bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip left his ship on a small rock. He came back with his troop and found a fine on the door. The fee was one moon stone. Pip chose a small rock to pay. Dot said he could not take the rock his ship sat on. Pip gave the guard his stone stud. The guard took it, and Pip held his shirt shut all the way home.
