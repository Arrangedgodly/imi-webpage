# The goose accountant

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Golden goose treasure*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May owned a goose that laid gold eggs. She got a grand nest for it. The troop came to see her gold. May wiped her fur and held up an egg in one paw. Her tail swept a pile of bills. The nest cost more than the eggs. She sold the nest and got straw. The goose liked that, and May could at last pay for toast.
