# The decoy decoy

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist double cross trope*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip hired Rex as a decoy for the gem theft. Rex hired Gus to play the decoy in his place. Gus sent May, who sent a doll. The troop put the doll by the gate. The guard held it and asked for its name. Pip took the gem. He then had to steal the doll too, since it held all the keys in its tiny felt paw.
