# The case of the printed tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office security investigation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

A tail mark was on a memo. Dot asked the troop to line up. Rex said no. He was the boss. Dot held the memo up to his chair. The ink was the same as the seat. Rex had sat on the memo to hide it. Dot gave him a clear chair. He hid no more notes under it, but his hat soon grew very full and fell off.
