# The quest for a spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Epic enchanted relic hunt*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip went to find a lost spoon. He went past a bog and wore mud in his fur. His tail saved him from a deep hole. At last he found a chest. He pried its lid up with one paw. The troop saw all the gold and cried out. Pip shut the lid. He still had soup to eat. The spoon lay under the chest, where it held one short leg still.
