# The team building raft

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate retreat exercise*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex asked the troop to build a raft from junk. Dot tied seats to a desk. Gus added the clock. May put the files on top. The raft could float. Rex asked what they knew now. Dot said their work place made a fine boat. They set sail while Rex ran along the bank. He held the task list and asked them to come back for a talk.
