# The fantasy sword size

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Fantasy heroes choose oversized weapons*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Dell, Edie, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dell got a huge sword for the quest film. Edie asked him to draw it, but his paws could not reach the whole way. Dell gave its case a tilt and the sword hit the stage floor. Edie gave him a short blade made of wood. The furry hero could walk and bow with no help. The shot was bold when she took it from low down. Dell hung the huge sword over the prop shelf. Its only job now was to look grand at the back of the shot.
