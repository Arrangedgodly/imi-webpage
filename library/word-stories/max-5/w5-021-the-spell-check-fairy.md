# The spell check fairy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy granting a boon*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy cast a spell to make Pip bold. She said cold in error. Frost grew on his fur. His tail stuck to a rail. The troop got hot soup while Ivy tried again. She said gold. Pip shone, but still shook. He held his paw over her mouth and asked for a coat. Ivy gave him one. He faced the dark wood in warm plaid.
