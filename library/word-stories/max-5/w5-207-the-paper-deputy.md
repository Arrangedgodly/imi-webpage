# The paper deputy

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western deputy trope*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip names Gus his aide. Gus asks for a badge. Pip has only one, so they share it on odd days. The troop takes cases on the wrong day. Dot draws a new badge on paper. Gus wears it with pride till rain turns it blue. Pip gives him the real one and wears the paper badge. In the next storm he sits under a tree. He gives out soggy notes.
