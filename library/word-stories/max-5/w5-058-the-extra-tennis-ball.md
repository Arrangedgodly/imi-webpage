# The extra tennis ball

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports cheating scandal*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex wins each point with a fresh ball. The troop sees a sly trick. Dot peers into his pouch. It is empty. Rex puts up both paws with a grin. Gus looks at his tail. It holds a tube of balls out of sight. Rex gets a new job. He now feeds balls to the whole troop and wins no games at all.
