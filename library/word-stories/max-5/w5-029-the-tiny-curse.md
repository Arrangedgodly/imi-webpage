# The tiny curse

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Curse of shrinking*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Rex, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy puts a spell on Rex to make him small. He hides in her fur and nips her tail. The troop can hear his laugh but can not see him. Ivy asks him to stop. Rex asks for a crumb in each paw. She gives him a whole cake. He says the spell must stay till he can eat it all.
