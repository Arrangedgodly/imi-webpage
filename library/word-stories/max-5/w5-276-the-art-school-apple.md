# The art school apple

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Art classes overvalue abstract concepts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Sol, Tess, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sol gave an art class with an apple on a desk. Tess drew it as a box, sure that bold ideas would win stars. Sol asked her to bite the apple. Her furry paws held the round fruit, and her teeth left a round mark. Tess drew that shape next. The class could see what she had seen. They ate the fruit after the class. Sol put the box on the wall too, with a label that said Box, not Apple.
