# The frog exam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic transformation test*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: May, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May had to turn a cup into a frog. She cast her spell and heard a croak. The troop gave a cheer. Ivy asked her to lift the cup. A real frog sat below it. May had found it in the pond. Ivy said the test was not done. May made the frog a cup at last, then gave tea to a very dazed class.
