# The rose allergy

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic flower gift*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex gives Ivy a rose at their first date. Her nose gives a blast and his hat blows off. The troop grabs it. Rex gets one more rose, but Ivy gives a snort again. Dot gives him a paper rose. Ivy grins and puts it in a vase. Rex asks why it is so much nicer. Ivy says it stays fresh and never makes her nose try to leave her face.
