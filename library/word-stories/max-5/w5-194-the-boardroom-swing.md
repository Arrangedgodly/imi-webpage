# The boardroom swing

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Executive ergonomics satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex used a swing as his chair. The troop had to push him while he spoke. Dot gave one hard push, and Rex flew out the door. He went on with his talk in the hall. The troop heard his voice grow faint. Dot said the talk was done. Rex came back at lunch and spoke well of their drive. He had to push his own swing after that.
