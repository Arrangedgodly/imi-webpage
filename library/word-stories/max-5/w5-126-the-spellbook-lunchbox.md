# The spellbook lunchbox

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school forbidden book*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy gave Pip a book he must not open till noon. Pip put it in his lunch box. At noon he took it out and found jam on each page. The troop tried to read the words, but each word ran into the next. Pip cast one and made a huge loaf. Ivy kept the book. It was now the best lunch text in class.
