# The clue with teeth

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: False threatening evidence*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May finds bite marks on the gate. Dot asks the troop to show their teeth. Each grin is too small. Gus shows them a saw. The marks match its teeth. May had asked him to make the gate lower, then lost track. Dot says the case is done. Gus asks if she means the gate. He has cut its latch in half.
