# The laser maze dust

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action security maze*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May faces a maze of red beams. The troop tells her to crawl. Dot sees dust in the air and asks for a broom. May uses the broom while Dot turns off each lamp. The beams fade. Rex comes in to test his new alarm and finds a very clean floor. He hires May to sweep. She gets a key to every vault and will never have to crawl again.
