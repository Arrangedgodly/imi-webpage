# The jewel in the soup

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Hidden royal treasure*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Rex, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy finds a red gem in her soup. She holds it up with her paw. The troop roars. Her tail waves, and her fur glows in the light. Rex says the gem once sat in his crown. He takes it and puts it on his head. The gem pops. Red juice runs down his nose. Ivy asks for her plum back.
