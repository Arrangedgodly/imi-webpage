# The spell in the jam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted food*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy makes jam that lets each eater sing. Gus tries it, and his voice fills the room. Each note makes his chest shake. His tail keeps time. The troop holds paws over their ears. Ivy puts the lid back on the jar. The jar sings too. Gus takes it on stage and lets it do all the hard high notes.
