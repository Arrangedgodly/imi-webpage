# The sea chest lock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate chest mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot found a chest with seven locks. The troop tried seven keys. None fit. Bo held it up to shake it. The base fell off, and coins fell out. Dot saw that the locks were just glued to the lid. But the gold was real. Bo put each coin back and tied the chest shut with rope. No one could find the knot again.
