# The pillow shield

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroic armor quest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus held a soft pad to block each blow. The troop sent him to face a stern guard. His tail shook, and his fur stood up. The guard swung a staff. The staff tore the pad and sent fluff out. Fluff flew past his paw. The guard had to snort so hard he lost the gate key. Gus came home with the key and a flat pad.
