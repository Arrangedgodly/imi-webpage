# The telescope queue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space observation mishap*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May set up a scope to view a rare star. The troop stood in line all night. Each guest saw one round light. At dawn Dot put her paw over the front. The light shone on. May had left the lens cap on, and a small lamp lit the glass from the back. The troop asked for coins back. May gave each one a turn to view a real star at night.
