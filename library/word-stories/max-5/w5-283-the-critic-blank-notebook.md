## Story

Bo wrote a harsh piece about a play while the stage was still dark. Cora saw his notes from her seat. She asked the furry guest what the play was about. Bo did not know. Cora lent him a clean page and asked him to watch the first scene. He found two jokes and wrote them down. His final piece gave those parts high marks and named one slow scene. Cora kept the old page as proof that a neat line could be full of empty air.
