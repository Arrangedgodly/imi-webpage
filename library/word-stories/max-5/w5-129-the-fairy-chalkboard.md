# The fairy chalkboard

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school classroom rebellion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Ivy, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo wrote a spell on the class slate. The words slid off and ran out the door. The troop ran after them into the yard. Ivy told Bo to write a stop spell. He wrote stop, and the words lined up by the fence. Bo used them to spell out lunch. Ivy gave a sigh and let the class eat while she swept the last word off the path.
