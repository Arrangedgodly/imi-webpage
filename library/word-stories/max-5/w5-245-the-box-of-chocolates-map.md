# The box of chocolates map

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic gift investigation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex gives Ivy a map in place of candy. She opens it and sees a trail. The troop goes to a tree, then a bench, then the cafe. Rex waits there with the candy. Ivy asks why he made her walk so far. Rex says he spent all his coins on candy and had none left for a cab. Dot hands him the fare for a bus and eats the first sweet.
