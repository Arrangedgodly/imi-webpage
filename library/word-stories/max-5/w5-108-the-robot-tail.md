# The robot tail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Helpful robot goes wrong*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus made a robot to brush his fur. It did a fine job till it saw his tail. It saw the tail as a rope and tied it to the chair. The troop set Gus free. Dot used a doll to teach the robot about tails. Now it combs the doll all day. Gus sits next to it and waits for his turn.
