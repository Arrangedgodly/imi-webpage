# The muddy alibi

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: False alibi detective trope*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned says he was out in the rain when the cake was lost. His fur is full of mud. Dot puts one paw in the mud. It is warm and has a sweet scent. The troop sees Ned lick his cuff. He used cake mix to fake the rain. Dot leads him to the bath. Now he has a real need to be wet and no cake.
