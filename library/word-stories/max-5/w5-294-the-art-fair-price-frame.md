# The art fair price frame

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Art sellers confuse expensive framing with artistic worth*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ian, Lana, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ian put a small piece of art in a huge gold frame at the fair. Lana asked what it cost. The furry host named a high price based on the frame. Lana liked the art but had no wall space for all the gold. Ian took it out and gave a price for the paper alone. Lana paid for it and put it in a plain book. Ian used the empty frame round his stall sign. Its shine led the crowd to the real art for sale below.
