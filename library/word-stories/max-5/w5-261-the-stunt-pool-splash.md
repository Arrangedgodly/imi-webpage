# The stunt pool splash

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action films hide basic stunt planning*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ash, Bina, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ash had to leap into a pool for the chase film. Bina asked how deep it was. Ash posed on the rail but did not check. Bina put in a pole and found only a foot of water. The furry actor came down and let her fill it first. They tried a leap from a low step, then did the full shot. Ash hit the water with a great spray and no sore paws. Bina put the depth on a sign and used the pole to hang their wet coats.
