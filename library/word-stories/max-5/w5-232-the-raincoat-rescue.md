# The raincoat rescue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic rescue trope*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus sees Dot stand in the rain and gives her his coat. She gives a nod and runs home. The troop roars for his kind heart. Gus stays in the rain with his tail round his bag. Dot comes back with a spare coat. It is tiny. Gus wears it on his head. Dot says that is where all his best ideas seem to leak out, and they use a large shade hat.
