# The empty suggestion box

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate feedback theater*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May asked the troop for ideas. She put a box by the door. No notes came in. Dot saw that the slot was too short for notes. May said the box was fine since no one said it was not. Dot put a coin in the slot. May tried to get it out and broke the box. Dot wrote make a wide slot on the new lid.
