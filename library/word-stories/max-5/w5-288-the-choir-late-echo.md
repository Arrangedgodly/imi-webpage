# The choir late echo

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Choirs perform in unsuitable acoustic spaces*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ewan, Flo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ewan led the choir in a stone hall. Flo sang the last note, then heard it come back after the next verse began. The echo made the furry troop lose their place. Ewan tried a quick beat, which made more noise. Flo hung thick rugs on the back wall. They sang once more, and the words came in order. Ewan kept the rugs for each show. Flo took one home after the final song. It was her own rug, and her floor felt cold.
