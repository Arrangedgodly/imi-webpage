# The cloud steps

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Journey to a sky kingdom*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May ties ropes to make tall steps up to a cloud. The troop holds the base. Her tail curls round each rung, and her fur fills with mist. She puts a paw out at the top. The cloud moves. May sways over the town. She lets go above a pool. The troop calls it a bad quest, but May sells seats for the ride.
