# The rock band shoe beat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Rock musicians mistake gear for rhythm*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Max, Nora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Max got heavy boots to make his drum beat sound tough. Nora heard him stomp off time at the drum. His tail hit a plate at each wrong step. Nora set a small clock by the drum and asked him to tap in plain socks. Max found the beat again. He kept the boots by the door for the walk home and did the show in socks. Nora gave him a loud cheer. That was the only extra crash for the final tune to end well.
