# The golf hole thief

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Golf course caper*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex hits a ball at the hole. It rolls out each time. The troop looks on as he tries again. Dot bends to look into the hole. Pip has put his tail in it to keep worms warm. Rex asks him to move. Pip lifts his tail, and six balls fall in at once. Rex marks six wins on his card. Dot takes away his pen.
