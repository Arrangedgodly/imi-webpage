# The western film dust bag

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Westerns manufacture dramatic dust*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Jem, Kara, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jem rode a prop cart in the old west film. Kara shook dust from a bag to make the scene look wild. The furry actor got dust in his nose and could not say his line. Kara swept the track and let the cart make a small cloud on its own. Jem spoke with a clear voice at last. They gave his hat a rinse after the shot. Kara kept the dust bag for the empty road scene. No one would have to take air from the cloud she made there.
