# The love quiz referee

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic compatibility test*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo and Ivy take a quiz to see if they match. The troop reads each query aloud. Bo likes rain. Ivy likes sun. Bo likes pie. Ivy likes soup. Dot asks what both like. They say sleep. Dot ends the quiz and gives them two soft pads. They nap while the troop has a row about the score. When they wake, they agree not to take a quiz till they have eaten lunch.
