# The giant boot garden

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Giant at the boundary*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus sees a huge boot by the road. The troop fears its owner will crush their huts. Gus rubs mud from the toe with one paw. His tail hooks a lace. Seeds fall in his fur. No owner comes. They fill the boot with soil and plant peas. When the giant seeks his boot, Gus asks him to wait till the peas are ripe.
