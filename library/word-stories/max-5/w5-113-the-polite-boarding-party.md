# The polite boarding party

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate ship boarding*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May put her paw on a ship rail and asked to come on board. Its troop said yes. She asked for the gold. They said no. May gave a bow and went back to her boat. Gus asked where the loot was. May held up a cake. The ship had given her tea, and she had found the gold was just a cheap foil trim.
