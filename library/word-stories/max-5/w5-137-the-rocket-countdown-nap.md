# The rocket countdown nap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space launch countdown*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex set a timer for lift. The troop saw the timer count down. At zero, a bell rang, but the ship did not move. Rex had used his nap timer. Dot asked where the real ship key was. Rex had it under his bed. He went to bed to fetch it and fell into sleep. The troop flew at last with their chief snug in his bunk.
