# The jazz club soup spoon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Jazz performances mistake incidental noises for music*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ezra, Gale, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ezra sang jazz by the food stand. Gale let a spoon fall in a bowl. The furry band took the clang as a cue and chose a new key. Gale held the spoon up to show the cause. Ezra used the new key for one verse, then came back to the tune. Gale moved the bowls away from the stage. The next clang meant soup was ready. The band took a real break and ate from quiet cups prior to the final set.
