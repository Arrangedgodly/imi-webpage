# The asteroid ball game

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space recreation accident*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex hit a ball from one rock to the next. The troop stood ready to catch it. The ball went round the rocks and came back to Rex. He hit it again. Dot said he was alone in the game. Rex asked her to throw from the other side. Both balls hit the same net and stuck. The troop had to pull them free and start a new sport.
