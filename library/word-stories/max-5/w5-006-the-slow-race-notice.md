# The slow race notice

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Race against a slow rival*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo raced a snail to the gate. He ran so fast his fur blew back and his tail flew high. The troop gave a cheer. Bo put one paw on the gate and took the prize. The snail gave him the race rules. They had to reach their own home next. Bo had a long way left to run. The snail had its own home and won.
