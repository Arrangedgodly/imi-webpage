# The apology form

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate accountability satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex lost the troop pay. He said sorry and gave Dot a form. She had to sign to say he was sorry. Dot asked where the pay was. Rex gave her a new form to ask for a form. Dot made him sign the first form. She wrote that he owed them his own pay. He gave that pay to the troop. Rex had to fill out six forms to ask for it back.
