# The love letter rent

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic letters gone astray*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip sends a love note to May. He says her smile is worth all the gold in town. The troop reads it in error and feels he has found a mine. They dig under the porch of May. Dot asks what they seek. Pip shows her the note. May comes out with a bill for the porch. Pip sees the price of a smile when it comes with six bills to fix a porch.
