# The silent comet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space documentary expedition*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip led the troop to hear a comet sing. They wore ear cups and sat in space for hours. No song came. Dot asked Pip who said a comet sings. He gave her a book of poems. She gave him a drum and asked him to make the score. The comet went past to a fine beat. Pip sold the drum track with his quiet film.
