# The moon cheese lease

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space resource dispute*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus went to the moon to get Swiss. The troop took bread. Gus found only stone, but he had told them to leave the Swiss at home. Dot set a slice of rock on her bread and took a fake bite. Gus asked how it was. Dot said it could use salt. They went home, where Gus had to buy Swiss for each empty slice.
