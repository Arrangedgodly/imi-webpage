# The troll toll book

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bridge toll guardian*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo asked for a toll at his span. Each guest had to tell a joke. Gus gave him one about a tail in a pie. Bo had a laugh and let the troop pass. Then he saw Gus come back with fake fur and a new hat. Gus told the same joke. Bo put a paw out for coins. His span now had a fee for old jokes.
