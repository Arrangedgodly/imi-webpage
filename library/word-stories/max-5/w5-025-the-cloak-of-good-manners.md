# The cloak of good manners

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted heroic garment*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot wore a cloak that said "May I" each time she moved. The troop liked it at first. Then her tail hit a door. "May I?" She gave her fur a rub. "May I?" She held a paw to eat. "May I?" Dot gave the cloak to a shop bell. It said "May I" when pals came in. At night she shut the shop and heard it say "May I" to each moth.
