# The royal bath lake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal luxury wish*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex wants a bath as wide as a lake. The troop digs one, and rain fills it. Rex dips a paw in. He jumps back. He puts his tail in and pulls it right out. The lake is far too cold. Dot hands him a pot. Rex heats one cup of lake water and takes a royal bath in his old bowl.
