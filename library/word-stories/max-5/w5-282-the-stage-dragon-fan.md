# The stage dragon fan

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Theater effects fail to consider performers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Axel, Bree, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Axel wore a beast head in the troop play. Bree blew smoke into it with a fan. The furry actor could not see his cue. He gave a roar at the wrong king and fell in a prop well. Bree cut the smoke and got him out. They put the fan by the back wall so smoke would flow past his head. Axel saw the next cue and gave his roar on time. Bree left the fan on low after the show to cool the hot suits.
