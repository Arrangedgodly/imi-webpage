# The stage rain bucket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Theater realism creates avoidable mess*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Hank, Kira, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hank put real water on the stage for the storm scene. Kira slid as she went on it, and her tail held the rail. The furry actor asked for a dry floor. Hank used blue cloth and a sound tape for the next storm. Kira could walk and say her lines with ease. The crowd knew it was rain, but no one got wet. Hank used the water left in his pail to mop the floor after the show. No actor had to cross it then.
