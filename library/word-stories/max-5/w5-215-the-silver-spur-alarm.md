# The silver spur alarm

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western intimidation trope*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned wears loud spurs to scare a thief. The troop hears him come from miles away. A thief hides each time the spurs ring. Dot wraps them in cloth. Ned grabs the thief. No one hears him come back. He rings a spoon on his badge. The town comes out to cheer. Dot takes the spoon back and asks him to wash the spurs while she makes soup.
