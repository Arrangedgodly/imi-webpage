# The court sketch artist

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom media satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May drew the judge for the news. The troop saw a stern face with huge ears. The judge said the image was wrong. May made the ears small. He still said no. Dot asked what he liked. He asked to look pure. May put a halo on his head. The troop had a good laugh. The judge ruled that he was less pure with a hoop over his head.
