# The meeting room booking

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate resource conflict*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot got a room for the troop. Rex got the same room for his wet hat. Dot put the hat on a chair and began the talk. Rex came in to claim his slot. The hat sat at the head of the table. The troop made it chair. It gave no long talks and cast a fine shade over the pie. Rex asked to join its team.
