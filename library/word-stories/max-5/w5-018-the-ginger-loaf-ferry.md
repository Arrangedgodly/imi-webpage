# The ginger loaf ferry

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Runaway baked hero*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

A loaf ran from the oven and dared the troop to catch it. Pip used his tail to swing ahead. Dot held out one paw, but the loaf got past her. It came to a pond and asked Gus for a ride. Gus had flour in his fur and a boat full of buns. The loaf took one look, went home, and chose a cool shelf.
