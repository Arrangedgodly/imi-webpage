# The space elevator button

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space transport etiquette*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus rode a lift up to the stars. The troop had hit each knob to see what it did. The lift had a stop at every cloud. Dot asked them to stop. Gus put his paw over the knobs, but his tail hit the one for down. They rode all the way home. Next time Dot gave the troop toy knobs and kept the real ones in her pouch.
