# The chair promotion

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office hierarchy satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip got a new rank at work. His desk was small, but his chair grew tall. The troop had to look up to speak to him. Pip could not reach his files. Dot put steps by his chair. He went down each time a task came in. By dusk he gave the chair his rank and sat on a stool to do the work.
