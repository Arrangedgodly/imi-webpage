# The duel at five past noon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western showdown punctuality*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex asks Bo to meet at noon for a duel. The troop waits by the clock. Bo comes late by five ticks. Rex says the duel is void. Bo says the clock is fast. Dot takes both hats and asks them to fix the clock first. They climb the tower and find a loose gear. At noon next day they argue over who did more work, then go for lunch.
