# The rocket pet door

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space ship creature cargo*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May took her pet frog onto the ship. It leapt at the red door knob. The troop ran to stop it. Dot hid the knob with a cup. The frog sat on the cup for the whole trip. When they got home, it would not move. May gave it a fly. Dot cut a small pet door in the main door. The frog leapt out for the fly, and May freed the knob at last.
