# The change control fan

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office process bottleneck*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus asked to turn on a fan. Ned said each new step must have a form. Gus wrote it out while the troop got hot. Ned asked Dot to test the form. Dot used it as a fan and sent it round. The troop got cool. Ned said no new step was now due. Gus kept blank forms by each desk and put the fan away to make room for them.
