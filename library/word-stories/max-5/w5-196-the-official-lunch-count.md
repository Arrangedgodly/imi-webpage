# The official lunch count

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Workplace attendance fraud*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May used the lunch count to see who came to work. Gus took two bowls and wore a new hat for the next count. May put his name down twice. Dot asked why the firm had more staff than desks. Gus put one bowl on the floor. May gave the floor a staff badge. It was never late and never asked for leave. Gus kept its lunch.
