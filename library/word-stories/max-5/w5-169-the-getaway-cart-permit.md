# The getaway cart permit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Caper logistics satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot chose to flee fast in a cart. Ned asked for a pass to park it by the vault. She wrote out the form while the troop took the gold. The guard put his seal on it and gave her a spot far down the hill. Dot moved the cart. The troop went out with the gold at noon, in a legal march led by a cart with a pass.
