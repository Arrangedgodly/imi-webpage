# The spell for toast

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school lesson*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy told the class to turn bread into toast. Pip waved his wand and said roast. His whole desk grew hot. The troop let air in the door to drive smoke out. Ivy gave him a plain pan. Pip made toast in it while the rest did magic. His was the only meal free of frogs, fire, or bits of desk.
