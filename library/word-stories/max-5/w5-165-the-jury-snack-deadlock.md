# The jury snack deadlock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Jury deliberation satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop sat as a jury. They all said yes to the case at once. Then they had to pick a lunch. Gus asked for pie, and May for soup. Pip chose cake, which made both sides cross. Dot sent a note to the judge. It said we are stuck. He sent in ten plain rolls. The troop gave their reply at once and asked never to serve as a jury again.
