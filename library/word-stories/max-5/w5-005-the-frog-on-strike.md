# The frog on strike

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal frog transformation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

A frog asked Ivy for a kiss. Ivy said she could give him a plum. He took it and asked for more. Soon the troop sat by the pool with fruit in each paw. Ivy wiped juice from her fur and hid her tail. The frog would not turn into a lord. He liked free lunch far more than he liked a crown.
