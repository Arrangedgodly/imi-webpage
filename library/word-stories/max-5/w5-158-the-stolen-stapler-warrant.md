# The stolen stapler warrant

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office noir investigation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned lost his clip tool and sent Dot to the desk. She saw one bent pin in the rug. The troop kept quiet as she read the pin trail to Rex. His pants were full of them. He had used the clip tool to fix his hem. Dot took it back with a paw. Rex asked if he could keep the pins. His pants were now at risk.
