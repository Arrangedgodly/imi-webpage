# A pea with a plan

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Princess and pea test*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot slept on a stack of beds to prove her rank. A pea lay at the base. She felt no lump, but her tail hung down and her paw hit the floor. At dawn the troop found green vines all in her fur. The pea had grown up the beds. Dot won the test, then made the royal chef mow her room.
