# The star that blinked

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space distress signal*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May saw a star blink three times. She sent her troop to help. Their ship flew close and found Ned in a tin shed. He had run out of light bulbs. He waved a torch to ask for a spare. May gave him a bulb and asked about the star. Ned held up a glass. He had hoped to read by the stars.
