# The chalk clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: School detective mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot finds white dust on the floor. A cake is gone, so she tests each paw for chalk. The troop lines up. Bo has white fur and must go first. Dot tries one speck. It is flour. She goes to the oven and finds the cake still there. Bo asks her to say sorry. Dot gives him the first slice and a clean class slate.
