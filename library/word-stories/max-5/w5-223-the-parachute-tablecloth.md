# The parachute tablecloth

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action escape equipment*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo leaps from a tower with a cloth on his back. The troop gasps. Dot says it is her lunch cloth, not a chute. Bo lands in a cart of soft hay. He holds the cloth up as proof his plan won. Dot shows him the hay. Bo hires the cart for each new leap. His next stunt must wait since the cart is busy with hay for the cows.
