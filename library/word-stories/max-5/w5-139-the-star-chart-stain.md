# The star chart stain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space navigation comedy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned spilt soup on the star chart. The troop had to steer by the red dots. Each dot led them to the same old world. Dot tried one. It was a red bean. Ned wiped the chart and found the true path below. The ship got home at last. Dot made a fresh chart on a plate and told Ned that he could eat those stars at home.
