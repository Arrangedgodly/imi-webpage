# The whistle in the cake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Whistleblower detective parody*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot hears a high note at the bake sale. Each time Ned takes a bite, it sings again. She asks him to open his mouth. There is a small pipe in his cake. The troop knows whose it is. Rex has lost his pipe for the race. He must now ask Ned to chew when it is time to start.
