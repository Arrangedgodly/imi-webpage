# The knight of the leaf

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroic armor initiation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo wears a leaf as his badge. He tells the troop that he will fear no storm. His fur is dry and his tail is high. The first gust rips the leaf away. Bo grabs it with one paw and folds it into a boat. He sets it in the ditch. Now he is lord of the fleet, and the storm gives him free wind.
