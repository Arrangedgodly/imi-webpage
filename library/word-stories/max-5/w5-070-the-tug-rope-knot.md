# The tug rope knot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Tug of war final*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop makes two teams to play tug. Pip ties his tail to the rope for more grip. Dot tells him to use his paws. He says his plan is smart. Each team pulls. Pip flies up and lands on a low limb. His tail holds the rope there as both teams let go. The game ends as a swing game, and Pip needs the first push.
