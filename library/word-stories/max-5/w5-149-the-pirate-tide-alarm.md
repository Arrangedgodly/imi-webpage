# The pirate tide alarm

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sea travel scheduling*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus tied a bell to the dock to warn of high tide. The troop heard it ring all night. At dawn Dot saw a crab pull its rope. Gus freed the rope and let the crab go. The tide rose with no sound, and the ship blew out. Dot gave the crab a job to ring the bell only at noon. It rang when he got lunch.
