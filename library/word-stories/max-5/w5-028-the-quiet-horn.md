# The quiet horn

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Heroic call to arms*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned buys a horn to call his troop on a quest. He blows so hard his teeth ache. No sound comes out. Dot puts a paw in the horn and finds a sock. Ned pulls it out with his tail. Now the horn works. The troop comes up to the hill. The quest is to find the owner of a very ripe sock.
