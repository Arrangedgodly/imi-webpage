# The dance film close shot

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Dance films crop away the actual choreography*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Hal, Joss, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hal shot Joss as she did her dance, but kept the frame tight on her face. The furry star asked him to watch the film. He saw a smile and no steps at all. Joss asked for a wide shot with her tail and both paws in view. Hal moved the lens back and shot the whole dance again. Fans could copy the steps now. He kept one close shot for the final bow. Her feet were still then, and her face could have its turn.
