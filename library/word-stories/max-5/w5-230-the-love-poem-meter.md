# The love poem meter

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic poetry competition*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned sends a poem for Dot. Each line ends with love. The troop says it needs rhyme. Ned swaps every last word to dove. Dot reads it and asks where all the birds came from. Ned goes to buy a dove as proof. The bird eats his poem. Dot likes the bird more than the verse, and Ned asks it to write his next love note.
