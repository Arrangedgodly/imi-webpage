# The cake procurement trial

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Workplace purchasing bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, Ned, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May asked for cake for the staff feast. Ned said she had to get three bids. She asked the troop for bids. Gus said cake is nice. Dot said cake is round. Bo said cake is gone. Ned put each bid in a file and gave his stamp. May held out the empty tin. Bo had given the most exact quote of all.
