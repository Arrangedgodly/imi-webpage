# The mime glass test

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Mime performers confuse illusion with real obstacles*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Val, Willa, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Val mimed a glass wall at the fair. Willa tried to hand the furry actor a coin, but Val would not leave his pose. She put the coin on the floor and went away. Val bent down to get it. The crowd saw him pass the wall he had made. He gave a shy bow. On the next round he mimed a door in the wall and used it to take each coin. Willa came back with tea and used that door to give it to him.
