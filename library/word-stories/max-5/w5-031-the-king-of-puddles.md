# The king of puddles

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Small kingdom ambition*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex calls a pool his realm. He puts a flag in it with his paw. The troop bows, but Dot grins into her fur. Rex curls his tail on his new seat. Then the sun dries the pool. Rex moves his flag to a cup of tea. Dot sips it. His next realm has a lid and a sign that says do not sip.
