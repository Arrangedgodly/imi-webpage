# The rooftop chase lift

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action chase shortcut*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus runs after Bo over the roofs. The troop roars as he leaps a gap. Dot takes the lift and waits at the next roof. She grabs Bo by his tail when he lands. Gus asks why she did not make the leap. Dot says the lift was free. Gus pays for his torn shoes, and Dot rides down with Bo, who asks if the lift can be used to flee too.
