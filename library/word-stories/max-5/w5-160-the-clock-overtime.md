# The clock overtime

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Workplace timekeeping loophole*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus put his name in at dawn and left at dusk. Rex paid him for one hour. The clock had died. The troop knew Gus had done the work, but Rex asked for a time stamp. Dot put the dead clock on the desk of Rex. She asked him to fix it in the paid hour. He spent all night at it and gave Gus his wages prior to his next try.
