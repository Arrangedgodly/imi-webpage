# The folk singer rhyme

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Folk songs force rhymes at the expense of truth*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Rai, Rosa, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rai sang that the mill fell down just to rhyme with town. Rosa ran the mill and asked the furry bard to come see it. The walls stood firm, and the wheel spun in the creek. Rai wrote a new verse about grain and flour. He kept the rhyme with carts that came into town. Rosa gave a cheer at the next show and had bread for the troop. Rai tried more ways to rhyme prior to his song about the road, which also still lay firm and would not crack for a verse.
