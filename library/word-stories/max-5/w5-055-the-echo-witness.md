# The echo witness

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Unreliable witness mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip hears a voice shout thief in the cave. He leads the troop in with a torch. No one is there. He calls out who said that. The cave asks him the same thing. Pip goes red under his fur. Dot taps his paw and tells him to ask a fresh query. He asks where the pie is. Gus gives a laugh in the dark. The sound leads them to Gus and his pie. Dot lets the cave ask him for a slice.
