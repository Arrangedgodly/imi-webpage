# The news show fake crowd

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: News programs mistake stock footage for live reporting*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Jude, Neve, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jude put a tape of a large crowd at the back of the news set. The furry host said the town plaza was full. Neve saw a wool coat in the tape, but the live day was hot. She asked Jude to show the real view. Two fans and a cart stood in the plaza. Neve told that true count on air. Jude wrote the date on the old tape and kept it for past clips. The next live count came from the plaza lens with no old coats in view.
