# The decoy beard

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Caper disguise mishap*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo wore a false beard to enter the vault. His troop wore wigs. Dot used glue to hold each beard in place. At the door a fan blew the wigs off. The guard let them in as a choir on tour. Bo sang while Dot took coins. They could not flee till the guard heard the whole song. Bo had to hum the last verse since the words were in his lost wig.
