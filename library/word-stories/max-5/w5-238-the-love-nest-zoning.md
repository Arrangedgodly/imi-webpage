# The love nest zoning

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic shared home*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy and Ned build a nest in a tree. The troop calls it a love nest. Dot reads the town rules and says it needs a pass. Ned says birds need none. Dot asks him to prove he is a bird. Ned sings, but Ivy says he can not fly. They move the nest to the floor and call it a rug. Dot says yes to the rug, which keeps the mud off their feet.
