# The missing chess king

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Board game mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo finds his king gone from the board. The troop looks under the rug. Dot spots a tiny crown in his fur. His tail had swept the king off the board and into his hood. Bo puts it back. Ned says the move is wrong. Bo nods and puts the king in his hood. From there it rules a warm realm with no foes in sight.
