# The disappearing clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Vanishing evidence mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot cuts a clue in ice to keep it fresh. The troop goes with her to the crime scene. The ice melts in her paw. Now she has no clue and a wet map. Pip asks what the clue said. Dot says it said keep cool. The lost cake was in the cold room all along. She puts her next note on paper and keeps it dry.
