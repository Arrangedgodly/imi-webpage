# The treasure hammock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Buried treasure quest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo dug for gold on an isle. His troop dug till the pit was deep. Dot found a cloth chest at the base. It held a net and two hooks. Bo was sad till Dot hung the net in a tree. It made a fine bed. Bo lay in it and told the troop to dig some more. They put dirt in the pit and went for a swim.
