# The ball that went home

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Baseball equipment antics*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip hits a ball out of the park. The troop runs to fetch it. The ball rolls into a hole and stays there. Dot looks in and sees Bo at rest with it under his head. He used it as a soft bed last night and wrote his name on it. Pip buys a new ball. Bo asks him to sign a lease if he wants to use the old ball again.
