# The anchor garden

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate ship stuck in harbor*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop could not lift the iron hook. Gus drew the rope, but it was taut. Dot went down to look. Roots had grown round the iron. The ship had sat in port for weeks. Bo got a saw and freed it. Gus held up the iron hook with green vines on it. He set sail at last, with tiny vines hung on the bow.
