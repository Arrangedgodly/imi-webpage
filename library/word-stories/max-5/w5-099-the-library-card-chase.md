# The library card chase

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Missing identity document*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip loses his card in the book room. Dot asks where he last used it. Pip says at lunch. The troop goes to the cafe and sees the card under a short table leg. It makes the table level. Dot pulls it out. Soup tips out. Pip gives it back and pays cash for his next book. The clerk gives him a card with four tiny legs.
