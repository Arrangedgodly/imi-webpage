# The trophy that rang

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Stolen sports trophy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex can not find his cup after the race. The troop looks in each bag. Dot hears a ring from the shed. Pip has hung the cup from a rope to call the troop for lunch. Rex asks for it back. Pip gives it to him. Now Rex hears a ring each time he moves his paw. He finds the lost spoon still tied in the cup.
