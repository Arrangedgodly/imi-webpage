# The magic graduation robe

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school ceremony*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo wore a robe that grew as he did. At the class feast he ate three cakes. The robe grew right over the hall. The troop fell over its hem. Ivy asked him to stand up. Bo could not find the cuffs. Dot cut a hole in the top, and Bo poked his head out. The robe was now the feast tent, and Bo sat in it till wash day.
