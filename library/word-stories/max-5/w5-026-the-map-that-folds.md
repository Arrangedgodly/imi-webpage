# The map that folds

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Quest map prophecy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip has a map of the whole realm. He folds it to fit his paw. Now the sea lies next to the seat. The troop takes a boat to the king. Pip has salt in his fur and a wet tail. The king asks how they got past his walls. Pip opens the map. They all land in the royal bath.
