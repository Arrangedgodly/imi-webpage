# The bank robber bond

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Caper financial inversion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo went to the bank to ask for gold. The clerk asked if he would like a loan. Bo said no, but the troop liked that plan more. Dot read the terms and said the loan cost too much. Bo put his sack on the desk and left. The clerk put forms in it. Bo got a bill for the time spent on his loan. He paid it with his own gold.
