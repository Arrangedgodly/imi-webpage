# The limbo judge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dance sports rivalry*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo bends under a low bar. His tail hits it. The troop says he lost. Bo asks if the tail rule is in print. Dot gives him the rules. He folds the page into a tube and uses it as a new bar. Now his tail can brush it with no harm. Dot bends under it once and takes the prize cup while Bo reads the rules.
