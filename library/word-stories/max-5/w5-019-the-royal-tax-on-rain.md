# The royal tax on rain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Greedy king decree*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex taxed each drop of rain. The troop set jars out to count the debt. Pip kept his tail dry, but his fur got wet. He took one paw full of rain to the king. Rex asked for the fee. Pip asked for a slip for each drop. By noon Rex had no ink left. He made rain free and put a tax on ink.
