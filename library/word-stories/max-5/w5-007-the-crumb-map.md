# The crumb map

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost children in a forest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip left bits to mark his trail. Dot ate them as they went. By dusk their troop was lost. Pip waved his tail and shook his fur. Dot put a paw on a tree. It had jam on its bark. She had wiped her paws on each tree after each snack. They went home by the jam trail and had soup for tea.
