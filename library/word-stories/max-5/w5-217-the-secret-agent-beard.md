# The secret agent beard

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy disguise satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo wears a false beard to spy on Rex. The troop says he is too easy to spot. Dot gives him a plain coat to use. Bo goes to the cafe and sits near Rex. Rex asks why he is there. Bo says for tea. Rex gives him a job since that is the first clear reply he has heard all day. Bo tells the troop that the enemy now pays his wages.
