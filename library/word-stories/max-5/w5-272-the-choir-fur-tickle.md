# The choir fur tickle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Choirs prioritize costumes over vocal comfort*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Nim, Opal, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nim wore a plume ruff for the choir show. It made his fur itch, and he gave a cough in the first verse. Opal asked him to take it off, but Nim felt he would look plain. She gave him a blue scarf that lay flat. Nim sang the next verse with a firm voice. The troop heard the tune at last. Opal hung the plume ruff on a music stand as trim. It could look grand there and stay far from each neck, so the whole choir could sing in peace.
