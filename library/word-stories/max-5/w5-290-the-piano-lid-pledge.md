# The piano lid pledge

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Musicians protect image while neglecting instruments*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Finn, Hope, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Finn kept the piano lid shut so his grand coat could lie on it. Hope asked the furry star how the sound would reach the hall. Finn hit a chord, and it came out dull. Hope hung the coat on a hook and put the lid up. The next chord rang clear. Finn did the whole show in his plain vest. The crowd did not miss the coat. Hope put its hook by the door, far from the keys and lid, for each later night.
