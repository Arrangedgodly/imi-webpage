# The brave broom

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Animated household servant*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot asks a broom to sweep the hall. It tries to clean her tail as well. She holds a paw in front of her face and sends the broom out the door. The troop sees it clean the path, the hill, and the whole town. Dot gives it a tiny brush. At last it sits down to clean the dust from its own straw.
