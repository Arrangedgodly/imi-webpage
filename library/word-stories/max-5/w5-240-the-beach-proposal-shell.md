# The beach proposal shell

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic proposal gone wrong*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus puts a ring in a shell for Dot. The troop waits close by. A crab takes the shell and runs off. Gus runs after it down the beach. Dot looks on till he comes back with the crab in a pail. She opens the shell and finds the ring. The crab finds a new home in the empty ring box. Dot says yes to both the ring and the tiny guest.
