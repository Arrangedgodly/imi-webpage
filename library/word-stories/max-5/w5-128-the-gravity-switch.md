# The gravity switch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space station control error*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned found a red lever on the ship. He hit it to make tea. The troop rose from their seats and hit the roof. Ned hung by his tail and tried to reach the lever again. Dot threw a shoe at it. They fell back down. The tea was still cold, so Ned wrote do not use for tea on the red lever.
