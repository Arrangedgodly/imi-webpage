# The train tea whistle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western railroad rivalry*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned owns a train that makes no noise. The troop fails to see it at the stop. Dot lends him a bell. He rings it and gets every cow in town to trail it. The train moves at a crawl with cows on its rails. Ned asks for a pipe to use. Dot gives him a tea pot. The train now stops for tea at every stop, and the cows stay home for milk.
