# The museum arrow loop

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Museum signage traps visitors in repetitive routes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Don, Etta, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Don put signs on the art hall floor to lead the troop round the rooms. Etta read them and came back to the same bowl six times. Her tail drew a dusty ring by it. Don saw one sign aimed the wrong way. He gave it a turn and took the whole route with Etta. They found the exit at last. Don swept the ring away and gave Etta free entry next month. He added a small map she could hold in her paw on the next tour.
