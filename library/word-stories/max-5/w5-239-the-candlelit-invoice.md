# The candlelit invoice

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic expensive dinner*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex takes May to a dim cafe. The troop waits out front to hear how it goes. Rex asks for the best meal for all. He can not see the bill in the dark. May sets a wick on fire and reads it aloud. Rex goes pale. Dot gets a big lamp from the road. The bill is for a whole troop. Rex has asked for a meal for all, and they come in to help him eat.
