# The poster tiny date

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Designers hide critical information in decorative layouts*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ed, Faye, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ed made a vivid sign for the troop show. Faye could read the huge title from afar but could not find the date. Ed held his paw by tiny print near a blue star. Faye came close, her fur flat on the wall. She asked for a large date and a small star. Ed drew it in bold type and moved the star to the edge. The crowd came on the right day. Ed kept a copy by his desk to test each new date from afar.
