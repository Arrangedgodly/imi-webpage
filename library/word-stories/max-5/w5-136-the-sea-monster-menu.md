# The sea monster menu

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate monster encounter*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop saw a huge fin near the ship. Gus held out a fish to make peace. The fin went away, then came back with a card. It had a menu on it. Dot read that the fin was a sign for a cafe below the sea. Gus asked for lunch. A net rose with hot fish in it, plus the fish Gus had sent to pay for his meal.
