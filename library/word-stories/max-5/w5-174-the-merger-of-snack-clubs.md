# The merger of snack clubs

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate merger satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus ran a pie club. May ran a soup club. Rex made them one club to save money. The troop now got one bowl with pie in soup. Dot said she liked one at a time. Rex said she had fear of new ideas. She sold the bowls as dog food and used the coins to buy two lunch tins. Rex gave the firm his best sales chart yet. It had a dog on it.
