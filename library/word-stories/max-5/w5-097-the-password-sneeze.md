# The password sneeze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Secret club investigation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot needs a code word to enter the club. She hears Gus snort at the door. The troop lets him in. Dot gives a snort too, but gets no reply. She peers in the lock hole. Gus has dust in his fur from the attic. The guard knows he found the lost games. Dot gets a game and gets in with a clean nose and dry lips.
