# The magic hat filing cabinet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard school storage*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip put all his class notes in a magic hat. He asked for his sums, and a hare came out. He asked for his magic, and got a new hare. The troop found a whole class of hares in the hat. They had used his notes for their desks. Pip gave them roots in trade. They gave back his work with red marks where he had got each sum wrong.
