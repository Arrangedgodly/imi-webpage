# The treasure island picnic tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate buried treasure reward*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy dug up a chest on the isle. The troop gave a cheer at the gold in it. Then they saw a bill for harm to the grass. Ivy owed more gold than she found. Dot put the grass back in the hole. The bill grew green and fell apart. Ivy kept the gold, but now she had to water the hole each day so the bill would stay small.
