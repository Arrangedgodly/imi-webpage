# The case of the double hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mistaken identity mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot sees a thief in a tall red hat. She stops Rex, who wears the same hat. He waves at May, who wears one too. The troop has a whole box of them. Dot goes back to the scene and finds red paint on a leaf. Rex has red paint on his tail. He wore the hat on it to fool her. Dot asks him to raise both paws and his tail. The lost cake drops out.
