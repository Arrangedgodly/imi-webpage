# The footprints on the roof

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Impossible intrusion mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot sees paw marks in a cake on the roof. No steps lead up the hut. The troop fears a thief with wings. Gus says he threw the cake up there to cool. Dot finds a pie fork stuck in it. Its five tips made the marks when Gus threw the cake. Gus gets his cake and Dot gets a slice for the clue to a crime with five metal toes.
