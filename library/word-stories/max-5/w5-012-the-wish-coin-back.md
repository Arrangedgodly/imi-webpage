# The wish coin back

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Three magic wishes*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy paid for a lamp and made a wish for gold. A gold fish came out. She made a wish for a crown. A crown of weeds fell on her fur. Her tail shook. The troop said to try once more. Ivy put her paw on the lamp and made a wish for a coin back. A coin fell out. She got a plain lamp that did what lamps ought to do.
