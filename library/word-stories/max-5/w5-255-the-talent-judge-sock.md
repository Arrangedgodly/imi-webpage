# The talent judge sock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Talent contests focus on arbitrary image details*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Flo, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Flo sang a clear tune, but Gus gave her low marks for her plain socks. Flo asked the judge to wear fine socks and sing it too. Gus put a gold pair on his paws, held the mic, and made a rough peep. The crowd loved the joke. Gus said the socks did not help. Flo sang once more and got fair marks. She kept the plain socks for the last round, with her tail round the stool so no stage cloth could trip her.
