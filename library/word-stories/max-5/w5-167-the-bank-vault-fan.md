# The bank vault fan

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist with unexpected obstacle*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo chose to steal a gem from a vault. His troop wore black and crept in. The alarm was quiet, but a loud fan blew their hats away. Dot ran for hers and hit the alarm lever. Bo asked why she let the plan fail. Dot said the hat was on loan. They left the gem and stole back only their own hats, then paid the bank a mop fee.
