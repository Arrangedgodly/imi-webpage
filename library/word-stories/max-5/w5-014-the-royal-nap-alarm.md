# The royal nap alarm

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sleeping beauty curse*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop said a spell had made Rex sleep for years. Pip went to wake him with a kiss, but found the whole troop at rest too. Fur lay on each chair. Tails hung from the lamps. Pip put his paw on a gong. He hit it once. Rex sat up and asked for tea. The spell had no power. The royal lunch had just been much too large.
