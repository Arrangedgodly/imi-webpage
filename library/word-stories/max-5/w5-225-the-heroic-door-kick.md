# The heroic door kick

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action entrance cliche*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus kicks the door to burst into a room. It does not move. The troop asks him to try again. Dot reads the sign that says pull. Gus pulls, but the door still stays shut. Dot shows him the latch. Gus lifts it and gets in at last. Rex has left for lunch. Gus sits in the empty room and asks Dot to film his exit this time.
