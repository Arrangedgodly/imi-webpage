# The cricket tea break

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cricket match disruption*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex bats a ball into the tea pot. The troop calls it out. Rex says the pot is still on the field. Dot pours the tea and the ball rolls into a cup. Gus sips his tea and spits the ball back onto the pitch. The judge halts the game to give a rule. The rest drink all the tea while he reads.
