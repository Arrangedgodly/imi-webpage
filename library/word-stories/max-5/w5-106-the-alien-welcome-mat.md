# The alien welcome mat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: First contact etiquette*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy put a mat by the ship door. It said wipe paws. A new guest had ten feet and no paws. The troop had to wait while he wiped each toe. Ivy took the mat away. The guest went in and left a trail of mud. Ivy wrote a new sign. It said take off boots. The guest left ten boots by the tea tray.
