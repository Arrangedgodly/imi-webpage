# The high jump crown

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: High jump sports final*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo tries to jump over a pole. His tail hooks it on each try. The troop says he must tuck his tail. Bo does, but now he lands flat. Dot puts the pole on the grass and asks him to lift it. He lifts it past his head. He wins the high lift prize. The jump judge asks where the pole went.
