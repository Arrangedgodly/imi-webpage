# The hero with a receipt

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Reward for a rescue*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip saves a crown from a well. He hands it to May with a wet paw. His tail drips, and his fur reeks of moss. May gives him any prize he wants. The troop waits for him to ask for gold. Pip asks for a fence round the well. May nods. Next week he saves her fence from the same well.
