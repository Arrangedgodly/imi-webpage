# The explosion countdown clock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action ticking bomb trope*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex sets a timer to scare the troop in a drill. Pip runs in to cut a wire. Dot reads the label. It is an egg timer. Rex says he meant to test their nerve. The timer rings, and Gus comes with eggs. Pip holds the cut wire. His eggs are cold since he cut the stove wire. Dot gives him an egg and asks for the wire back.
