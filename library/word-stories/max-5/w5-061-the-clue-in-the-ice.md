# The clue in the ice

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Frozen evidence mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot finds a key in a block of ice. The troop needs it to open the snack shed. She holds it near the fire. The ice melts, but the key melts to a thin curl. It was made of sugar. Pip says he froze it for a prank. Dot asks him to fetch the real key. It is in his hat. He now keeps all candy far from the fire.
