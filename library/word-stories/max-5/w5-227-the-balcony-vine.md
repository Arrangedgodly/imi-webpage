# The balcony vine

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic balcony serenade*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo sings under the high porch of Dot. His song goes on so long that the troop can not sleep. Dot lets down a vine to pull him up. Bo feels it means she loves him. She hands him a cup of tea and shuts the door. Dot asks him to sip it while it is hot. His lips stay busy. The troop rests while Bo looks at the cup and waits for more tea.
