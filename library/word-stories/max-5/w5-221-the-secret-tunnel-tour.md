# The secret tunnel tour

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action infiltration route*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned digs a shaft under the vault. The troop goes after him. At the end they find a sign for tours. Dot has made the shaft a tour stop. Ned says she broke the plan. Dot holds up the tour fees. They have made more than the vault holds. Ned fills the shaft in for a high price tour where each guest must guess what was once under the soil.
