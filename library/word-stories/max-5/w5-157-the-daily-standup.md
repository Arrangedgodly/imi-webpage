# The daily standup

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Pointless workplace meeting*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop stood for a daily work chat. Rex said no one could sit till each task was clear. Pip asked what his task was. Rex said to state his task. Dot asked who chose it. Rex said they would talk of that next week. Gus put seats under each tail. The troop sat at once. Rex named it a bold pilot and made a new chart.
