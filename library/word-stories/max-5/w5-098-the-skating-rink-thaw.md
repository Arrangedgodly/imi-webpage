# The skating rink thaw

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ice skating final*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned makes a rink in his yard. The troop has steel on each shoe. They wait for the judge. The sun rises, and the rink melts. Dot finds a pair of shoes. She puts them on and leaps into the pool. Ned calls it a new sport. They award her a medal for best leap. The judge comes dry and goes home with mud up to his knees.
