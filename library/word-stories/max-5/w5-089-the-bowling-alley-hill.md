# The bowling alley hill

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bowling lane mishap*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May rolls a ball down the lane. It turns left and hits the shoe rack. The troop tries again with a new ball. The same thing takes place. Dot puts one paw on the floor. The lane tilts to the door. Gus set one leg of it on a plum. They take out the plum. May bowls a great game while Gus eats the plum.
