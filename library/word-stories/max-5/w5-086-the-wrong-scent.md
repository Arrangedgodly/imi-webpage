# The wrong scent

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detective tracking by smell*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot takes a trail of roast nuts. She hopes to find the lost snack box. The troop waits at the shed. Dot finds a bath, where Rex has nut soap in his fur. He shows her the box by the tub. He used its lid as a tray. Dot finds the nuts in the soap dish and asks Rex which ones he ate.
