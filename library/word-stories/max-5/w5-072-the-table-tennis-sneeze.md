# The table tennis sneeze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Unusual sports advantage*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May hits a tiny ball. Ned blows it back with a loud snort. The troop calls foul. Ned says his nose is a kind of bat. Dot reads the rules and finds no ban on noses. May puts dust on the ball. Ned gives a snort so hard the table slips away. The judge lets him keep his nose, but asks him to use a clean bat.
