# The record label rush

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Record labels prioritize release dates over readiness*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Gus, Iona, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus asked Iona to sing her new song that night. The furry star had only half the words. Gus said the rest could wait for the next disc. Iona sang one verse and sat down in the long empty pause. Gus heard how odd it was. He gave her a week to end the tale. Iona wrote about the lost kite at home with its owner. The song came out whole. Gus kept a blank disc on his desk to show what a rush could yield.
