# The pirate eye patch swap

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate costume confusion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned put on an eye patch to look bold. He hit a mast. The troop gave him a cane, but he hit a rail next. Dot found that Ned had put the patch over his good eye. He moved it to the other side and saw the whole deck. Now he could steer the ship, while Dot used the cane to hang his wet socks.
