# The red hood feast

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Red riding hood encounter*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot wore a red hood to take bread to May. A sly guest met her in the wood and asked where she went. Dot held her paw to her lips. Her tail hid the bread bag. The troop had sent her with a quiz for spies. The guest got each guess wrong. At the feast, May gave him a cloth and wiped bits off his fur.
