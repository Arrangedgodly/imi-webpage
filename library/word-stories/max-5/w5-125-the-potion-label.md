# The potion label

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic potion mix up*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Gus, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus made a green drink for his class. Ivy said it would make fur glow. The troop took a sip and got shiny lips. Gus read his label. It said lips, not fur. Ivy made him write each label twice next time. Gus made plain water and gave it two tags. The class read both and still asked which spell would make them shine.
