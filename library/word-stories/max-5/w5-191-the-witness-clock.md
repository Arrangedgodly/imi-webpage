# The witness clock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom physical evidence*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip took a clock to court to prove the time of the theft. The judge asked it to speak. Dot wound the clock. It rang at noon. Pip said that was the time of the theft. The judge wound it again. It rang at noon once more. The troop gave a sigh. Pip took back the clock and gave the judge his lunch bell. Court ended early for soup.
