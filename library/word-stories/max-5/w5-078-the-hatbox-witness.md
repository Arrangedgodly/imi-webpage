# The hatbox witness

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Witness concealed in luggage*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May says she saw the thief from a hat box. Dot asks how she got in. May says she slept there. The troop looks at the tiny box. May can fit one paw in it. Dot asks her to show the view. May puts her eye to the hole. She sees a face on a sign. The thief is a model for cheap hats. The lost coins roll out when May lifts the hat box.
