# The two picnic blankets

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic planning conflict*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo and Dot each plan a meal. The troop helps carry their bags. They reach the hill and lay two mats side by side. Bo has bread but no jam. Dot has jam but no bread. They join the meals and sit close. Rex says the match is fine. Then rain falls. Both lack a cover. The troop uses one tree while Bo and Dot each get a mat to wrap round their head.
