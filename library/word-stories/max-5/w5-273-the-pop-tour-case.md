# The pop tour case

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Pop tours transport unnecessary props*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Odo, Pia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Odo put ten shiny balls in cases for his pop tour. Pia asked where the drums and mics would fit. The furry star waved at a tiny gap by the van door. Pia took one case lid off. It could hold all the stage gear if the balls came out. Odo kept one ball and left the rest at home. The show began on time with clear sound. Pia put the spare empty cases under the stage, where they held coats and left the aisle free for the crowd.
