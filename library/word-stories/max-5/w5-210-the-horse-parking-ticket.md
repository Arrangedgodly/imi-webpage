# The horse parking ticket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western town regulations*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo ties his horse by the bank. Ned puts a fine on its mane. The troop asks what rule it broke. Ned says the horse took two white spots. Dot tests the spots with a rope. One is just large for a hat. Bo puts his hat there and keeps the horse in the other one. Ned asks the hat for rent, and Dot calls for a new mayor.
