# The boat race anchor

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rowing competition mishap*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned rows hard, but his boat does not move. The troop calls out hints from the bank. He pulls with both paws and wraps his tail round the seat. Dot dives in to look. A rope holds the boat to a tree. Ned frees it and races ahead. He wins the race, then comes back for his lunch, which still hangs from the tree.
