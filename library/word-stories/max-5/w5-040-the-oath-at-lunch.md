# The oath at lunch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Knightly vow*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus vows to guard a pie till the troop comes home. He sets his paw on his chest. His fur is neat and his tail is still. A fly lands on the crust. Gus eats that bit to save the pie. A new fly lands. By dusk only the dish is left. Gus waits proud by it. No fly has hurt the dish.
