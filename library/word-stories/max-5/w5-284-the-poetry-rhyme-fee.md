# The poetry rhyme fee

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Poetry clubs impose arbitrary formal rules*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Cy, Dora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cy ran a verse club and took a coin for each line that did not rhyme. Dora read a poem about rain on her fur. The last word was Roof. Cy asked for a rhyme. Dora put a pail under the leaky club roof. Its drips rang in the pause. The troop kept time with their paws. Cy used the fees to fix the leak and ended the rhyme tax. Dora read the poem again next week in a dry room, with no need for a pail.
