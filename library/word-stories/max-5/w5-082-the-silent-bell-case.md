# The silent bell case

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Village alarm mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The bell does not ring at noon. Dot asks each one in the troop where they were. Pip says he was in the bell tower. He hoped for a quiet nap, so he put wool in the bell. Dot pulls out the wool with one paw. The bell rings. Pip sits up in alarm. Dot puts the wool in a sack and gives it to him.
