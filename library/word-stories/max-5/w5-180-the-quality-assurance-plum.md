# The quality assurance plum

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Quality control absurdity*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned tried each plum to test its taste. He took one bite to see if it was ripe. The troop sent back each half eaten plum. Dot told Ned to use just one plum for the test. He did, but that plum was sour. He wrote sour on each crate and paid less for them all. Dot found him at lunch with sweet plums and no test log.
