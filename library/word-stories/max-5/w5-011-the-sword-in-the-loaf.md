# The sword in the loaf

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sword in the stone*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned drew a blade from a loaf. The troop named him as king. He wiped flour from his fur with one paw and held his tail high. The baker came out and asked for his knife. Ned gave it back. The troop still bowed. The baker made him king of the wash bowl, where he ruled ten bowls and a spoon with jam on it.
