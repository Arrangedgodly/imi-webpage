# The royal soup trial

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Trial to win a royal hand*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May sets a test for each brave beau. Each must eat her soup. Gus dips one paw in the bowl and loses some fur. His tail goes stiff. The troop begs him to stop. He asks May for bread to cool the soup. She gets a loaf. He finds her lost ring in it and wins her nod, plus lunch for the troop.
