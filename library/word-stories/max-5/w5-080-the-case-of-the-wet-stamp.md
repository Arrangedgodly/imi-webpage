# The case of the wet stamp

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mail theft mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus says a thief took his note. Dot looks in the post bag. It is full of wet paper. The troop used the bag as a rain hood. Gus holds up his paw and says his note was dry. Dot reads his name on the hood. The ink has run, but his words are clear. He asked the troop to bring him a dry coat.
