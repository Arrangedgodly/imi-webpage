# The bean fare

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic beanstalk quest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus sold a cow for a sack of beans. The beans grew a stalk up to a cloud. Gus went up with his tail round each leaf and dew in his fur. A huge paw held out a fare chart. The cloud troop ran a lift now. Gus had no coins, so he gave the lift a scrub and rode down with a mop as his prize.
