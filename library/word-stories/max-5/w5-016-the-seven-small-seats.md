# The seven small seats

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Guest in a tiny cottage*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May found a hut with seven seats. She sat on one and broke it. Her tail sent two more down. A nail tore her coat. The troop came home to find her on the floor. May held a bent peg in her paw. She fixed each chair, then made one big bench. The troop sat on it and asked her to break the table too.
