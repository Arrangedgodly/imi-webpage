# The red hat date

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic mistaken identity*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy waits for Rex in a red hat. The troop wears red hats too, so Rex goes to the wrong table. Gus eats the cake Rex gave him. Dot asks Rex to read the names on the place cards. Rex finds Ivy and gives her a bloom. Ivy says she likes cake more. Gus takes back the empty dish and asks for the bloom in trade. Ivy sends both to the cake shop.
