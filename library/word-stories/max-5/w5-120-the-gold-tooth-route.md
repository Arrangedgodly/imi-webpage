# The gold tooth route

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate treasure hiding*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip hid a coin under a loose tooth. No thief would look there. At lunch he bit a hard nut, and the tooth came out. The troop ran round the deck to find it. Dot found a nut with a coin in it. Pip had spat the coin into its shell. He put the coin in a pouch and let the tooth lie under his soft bed for a fair trade.
