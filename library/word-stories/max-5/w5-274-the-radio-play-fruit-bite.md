# The radio play fruit bite

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Radio dramas forget incidental live sounds*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Poe, Quin, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Poe ate an apple while he read a royal tale on radio. Quin heard the crisp bite and felt sure a door had split. The furry actor tried to speak with his mouth full. Quin ended the take and gave him a plate out of the booth. Poe ate the rest, wiped his paws, and came back to read the tale in a clear voice. Quin taped one bite for a later food stall scene. The king could now rule in peace until the play asked him to eat.
