# The glass gets a day off

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic glass vanity*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex asked his glass who was best. The glass said Dot. Rex wiped his fur and tried again. It said Dot once more. He hid his tail and hit the floor. The troop came to see the fuss. Dot stood right in front of the glass. Rex moved her aside. The glass said it could see a wall now, and the wall was quite nice.
