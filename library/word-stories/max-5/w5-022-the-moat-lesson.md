# The moat lesson

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Castle siege quest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned dug a moat to keep pals out. Soon his troop could not get in for lunch. He waved a paw from the wall. His tail sank and his fur smelt of mud. Dot tied a rope to a cart full of pals and threw Ned the loose end. Ned drew so hard the cart flew over the moat. He had pals at last, all in his soup.
