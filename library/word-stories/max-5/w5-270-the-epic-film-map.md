# The epic film map

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Epic films sacrifice geographical sense*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Leo, Mina, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Leo held up a map to show where his army would march. Mina saw the route cross the page edge and jump to the other side. The furry actor had put two maps the wrong way round. Mina gave one a turn and found the road ran by a lake. Leo gave a new talk about the safe shore path. The troop took that route in the next shot. Mina made a neat fold, and the final fight could begin in a place that fans knew from the tale.
