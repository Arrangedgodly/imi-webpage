# The ring size chart

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic proposal logistics*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip asks May for her ring size. She feels he means the show ring where she works. The troop helps him gauge it. Pip buys a hoop as wide as a hut. He bends a knee to offer it. May grins and uses it for her next show. Dot gives Pip a tiny ring to try again. May says yes, but asks that the big one stay since it earns their rent.
