# The cafe seat shuffle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic chance meeting*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, May, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip plans to meet May at a cafe. The troop has taken every seat. Dot moves them round so Pip can sit next to May. Gus ends up with three cakes, and Bo loses his soup. By the time Pip sits down, May has gone to pay. Dot moves the whole troop to the till. Pip and May meet there and go to a new cafe to eat in peace.
