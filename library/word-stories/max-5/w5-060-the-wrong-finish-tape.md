# The wrong finish tape

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Marathon finish confusion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May runs past the red tape with a cheer. The troop gives a cheer too. Gus asks why she is in his yard. The tape marks his new paint. May has red lines in her fur. She looks down the road and sees the true goal. She runs there. The judge gives her a prize for best lines, while Gus hangs wet paint signs high up.
