# The news desk tail peek

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: News broadcasts conceal impractical sets*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Hal, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hal read the news at a desk too high for his stool. Ivy saw only the top of his fur on the TV. Hal tried to sit on his tail as a pad, but it slid out. Ivy took a firm box and set the stool on it. Hal read the next item with his whole face in view. A guest asked why he was so tall. Hal let them see the box after the show. Ivy put a safe step by it for each guest to use.
