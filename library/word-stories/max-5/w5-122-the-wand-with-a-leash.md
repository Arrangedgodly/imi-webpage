# The wand with a leash

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school equipment*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Ivy, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo lost his wand each day. Ivy tied it to his paw with a cord. At class he cast a spell to make it fly. The wand rose and took Bo with it. The troop held his tail. Bo hung over the desk till Ivy broke the spell. She tied the wand to the desk next time. Now Bo takes his desk to every class.
