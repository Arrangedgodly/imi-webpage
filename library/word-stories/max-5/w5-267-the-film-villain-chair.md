# The film villain chair

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Movie villains sit in impractical thrones*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Hugh, Iris, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hugh was a cruel lord in a tall black chair. Iris asked him to turn to face the hero. The chair legs made a noise and held his tail tight. Hugh moved only an inch, then gave a yelp. Iris freed his tail, then put the chair on a board that could roll. Hugh could glide round for the next take. His grim face was good, but the board gave a creak. Iris oiled its axles. Hugh gave his final line at last, with no extra sound to make him seem like a cart full of mice.
