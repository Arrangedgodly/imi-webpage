# The musical bus fare

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Musicals interrupt mundane transactions with songs*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Kip, Lila, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kip sang for five whole tunes while he tried to board a bus in the stage show. Lila stood with her paw on the fare box. The furry star did a dance on the step but did not pay. Lila asked for the coin first. Kip paid, sat down, and sang the rest from his seat. The scene could move at last, with the bus on its way to town. Lila got a short solo at the next stop about the rule that all tails must stay clear of the doors.
