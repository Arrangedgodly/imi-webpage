# The slow light beam

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Futuristic transport*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned built a beam to send fruit to the moon. The troop stood by to test it. He put a plum in the beam. The plum shone, then fell off the stand. That was all it did. Dot read the box. Ned had paid for a lamp. He kept it to light his desk and sent the plum by mail. It got there far less shiny.
