# The crown of ants

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Animal monarch contest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip wins a crown of leaf bits at the fair. The troop lifts him high. His tail curls with pride. Then his skin grows itchy. He feels ants on each paw. The crown was their old roof. Pip sets it on a stump. He makes a fresh crown from paper. The ants vote him king again, for his fine grasp of home care.
