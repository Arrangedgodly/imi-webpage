# The false safe alarm

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist nervous specialist*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned heard a click from the safe and ran. The troop fled with him. Dot had a look. It was a toy clock set for lunch. She took the gold and met Ned by the gate. Ned said he had saved them from a trap. Dot gave him the clock as his cut. He kept it shut all day for fear of the bell. It rang at lunch in spite of that.
