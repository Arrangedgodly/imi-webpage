# The remote work vine

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Remote work misunderstanding*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Ned, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo asked to work from a tree. Ned said yes if he could still reach the staff. Bo ran a vine from the tree to each desk. The troop gave tugs to send news. A hard tug drew Ned out of his chair. Bo wrote it down as a plea for help. Ned let him stay in the tree but asked that all new notes come by paper plane.
