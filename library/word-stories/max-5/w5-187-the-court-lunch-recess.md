# The court lunch recess

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Judge scheduling satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The judge said take a break. The troop ran to the yard to play. The judge meant a pause to read the file. Dot found him by the glass door as he eyed their game. He asked what the score was. Dot said tied. The judge took a bat and hit a home run. They came back to court with a clear score and mud on the case file.
