# The gallery snack label

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Art galleries create uncertainty about ordinary objects*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Zed, Ada, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zed set fruit on a stand at the art show. Ada asked if it was art or a snack. The furry host said it could be both. Ada took a grape, and Zed cried that the work was not whole now. She put it back, but the bite mark was there. Zed wrote a new label about bites. Ada paid for the grape and asked for a clear food table. Zed made one by the door. The next guest could eat there and leave the art alone.
