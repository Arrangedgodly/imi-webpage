# The gold sack receipt

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist inventory control*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot took gold from a vault but left a bill. The troop asked why. Dot said the owner must know how much was gone. The owner read it and found she had taken one coin too few. He sent the spare coin by post. Dot said the mail fee cost more than the coin. The owner sent her a rare stamp. Dot now had a stamp worth more than all the gold. She left the gold and took just the stamp books after that.
