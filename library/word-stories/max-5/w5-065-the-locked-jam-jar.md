# The locked jam jar

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Impossible theft puzzle*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May locks a jar of jam in a glass case. At dawn the jam is gone. Dot tests the lock and finds it sound. The troop fears magic. Dot finds a straw in a tiny hole in the case. It leads up to the roof. Gus sits there with a red paw and a full belly. May ties a bell to the straw. At his next sip, the whole troop comes for jam.
