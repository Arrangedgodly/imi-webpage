# The tail on the witness stand

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom witness oath*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May swore to tell the truth. Her tail wound round the court chair. The judge asked if she saw Rex take the pie. May said she saw a red hat. The troop all wore red hats that day. Dot held up the pie tin. May sent her tail to the tin. The judge let her eyes rest and asked her tail to state its case.
