# The dance shoe glue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Dancers trust lucky shoes over maintenance*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Wes, Yara, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wes wore old shoes to each dance. He felt they gave him luck. Yara saw a sole peel away as he spun. His tail held a rail so he could sit to mend it. The glue took time to set. Yara lent him a plain spare pair for the show. Wes did each step well and saw that the skill was his, not in the old soles. He put the old shoes on a shelf at home. The new pair fit his paws with no need for glue.
