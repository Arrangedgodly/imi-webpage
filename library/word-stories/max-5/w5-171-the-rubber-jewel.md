# The rubber jewel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Art theft authenticity twist*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May stole a ruby from a hall. Her troop met her by the van. Dot let it fall. It went to the far side of the road. It was a soft prop. May went back to ask why. The owner said the real gem had been gone for weeks. May said she would help find it if she could keep the ball. They hired her to guard the empty case all day.
