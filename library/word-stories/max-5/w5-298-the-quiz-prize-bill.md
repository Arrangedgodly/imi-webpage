# The quiz prize bill

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Game shows hide practical costs of winnings*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Jules, Nola, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jules won a huge piano on the quiz show. Nola asked the furry guest to sign for it. Jules saw that cart hire to get it home cost more than his rent. He asked for a small drum. Nola read the prize list and let him pick one that fit in his bag. Jules hit it on the bus home, then quit when asked. Nola added cart costs to the prize cards. The next guest knew the full price prior to using a pen to claim a shiny prize.
