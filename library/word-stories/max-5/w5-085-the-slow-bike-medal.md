# The slow bike medal

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cycling race loophole*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip rides hard with his tail round the seat. He goes past the troop, but his wheel comes off. He hauls the bike to the goal on his back. The judge says he must ride it. Pip sits on it and asks Dot for a push. She gives him a push over the line. He wins last place and gives Dot the bell from his old bike.
