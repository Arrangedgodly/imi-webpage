# The pole vault picnic

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Athletic equipment mishap*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo bends a pole to leap over a bar. His tail hooks a flag, and he flies into the snack tent. The troop runs to see if he is hurt. Bo sits in a bowl of jelly with both paws full of buns. He asks for one more try. Dot moves the snack tent. Bo now asks if he can skip ahead to the medal meal.
