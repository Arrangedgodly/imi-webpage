# The locksmith souvenir

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist technical expert*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus could open any lock. The troop paid him to open a safe. He did it in one try and took the key as his fee. Dot asked why he kept it. Gus said he had a box of keys at home. He could not open that box. The troop gave him a new box with no lock, and Gus gave back the fee to pay for it.
