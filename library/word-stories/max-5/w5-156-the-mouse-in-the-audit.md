# The mouse in the audit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Audit evidence mixup*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot found a hole in the files. Rex said the staff kept files badly. The troop swept each desk for the lost sums. Gus found a mouse at rest on torn paper. Dot put it in a box with a blank form. The mouse ate the form. Rex said the audit was now done. Dot wrote that the chief had let the sole clue eat the proof.
