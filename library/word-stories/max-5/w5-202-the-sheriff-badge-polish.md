# The sheriff badge polish

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western lawman vanity*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip wears a badge so shiny the town shuts its eyes. The troop asks him to dull it. Pip says a law chief must shine. Dot puts mud on the badge. Pip wipes it off at once. A thief rides past while he buffs it. Pip sees the thief in the badge and gives chase. He keeps a small muddy spot on it for his next case.
