# The sunset ride invoice

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western heroic ending*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex rides west as the red sun sinks. He has saved the town. The troop waves him off. After five ticks he rides back. His horse is owned by Dot, and she wants it home prior to dark. Rex tries to state why he must leave. Dot gives him a broom. He can ride that all night if he wants. Rex walks home, and the broom has a fine view of the red sun.
