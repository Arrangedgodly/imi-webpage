# The elevator alibi

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate caper mistaken identity*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip used a lift ride as his alibi. He said he was mid ride when the coins were lost. Dot read the lift log. The troop had used it all day, but Pip had never hit a knob. He had been on the roof of the car. Dot found the coins under his tail. Pip said he was just there to hold them down so they would not roll.
