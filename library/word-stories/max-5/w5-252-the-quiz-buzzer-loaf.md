# The quiz buzzer loaf

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Quiz shows and malfunctioning buzzers*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Bex, Cole, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bex knew each quiz fact, but her bell was mute. Cole told her to clap if she knew the next one. Her soft claps could not reach him. Bex hit a spoon on a loaf tin from the snack desk. The clang rang clear. Cole gave each guest a tin so the round was fair. Bex won by one point. She took the cash prize and got warm bread. They ate it from the same tins, and Cole kept one as a spare bell for the next show.
