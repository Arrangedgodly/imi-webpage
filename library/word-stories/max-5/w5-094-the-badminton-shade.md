# The badminton shade

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Weather obstructs a match*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy hits a cork with a plume into a stiff wind. It lands in a tree. The troop calls for a new serve. Ivy looks up and sees six more corks on the same limb. She puts her tail round the trunk and gives it a shake. All seven fall onto the court. Dot hits them back one at a time. The troop plans to play under a roof next week.
