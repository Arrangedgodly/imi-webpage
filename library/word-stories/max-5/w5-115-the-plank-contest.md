# The plank contest

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Walking the plank reversal*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus told Pip to walk the plank. Pip put his tail round the rail and made the plank into a slide. The troop gave a clap. Gus tried the slide too and fell into the sea. Pip used the plank to pull him out. Gus asked for one more turn. The ship sat in port all day while each crew mate tried to land feet first.
