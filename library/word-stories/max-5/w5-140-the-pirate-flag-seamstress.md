# The pirate flag seamstress

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate intimidation fails*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May sewed a skull on her flag. She hoped the troop would seem scary. The skull had a grin by error. Each ship waved and came close for tea. May tried to sew a frown. Her tail shook, and the skull got one shut eye. She gave up and made the ship a cafe. The happy flag got more gold than any raid had done all year.
