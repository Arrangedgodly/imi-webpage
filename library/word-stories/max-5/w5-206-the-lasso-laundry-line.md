# The lasso laundry line

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western roping contest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May joins the rope game. The troop ties hoops for each throw. May fails at each hoop but hooks a pile of wet coats. She lifts them up onto the rope. They dry in the sun while Rex wins the prize. Dot pays May to wash her coats too. Rex asks what he won. His prize is a clean shirt, and May gets the money used to pay for it.
