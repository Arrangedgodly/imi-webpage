# The ship in the bottle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate magical imprisonment*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex put a tiny ship in a glass flask. Then he cast a spell to make his troop fit in it. They went round the cork and had no way out. Dot used her tail to haul the mast up. The mast poked the cork out. They all grew big again. Rex kept the flask as a vase. Dot put a large rose in it to block the port.
