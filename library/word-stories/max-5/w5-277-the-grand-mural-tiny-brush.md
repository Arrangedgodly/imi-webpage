# The grand mural tiny brush

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Public artists ignore practical scale*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Tom, Una, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tom took a tiny brush to paint a mural on the town hall. Una saw him make one dot per hour while his tail held the paint can. She gave him a wide brush for the broad blue sky. Tom used it, then saved the tiny one for bird eyes. The mural was done by dusk. Una asked him to sign it, and he chose the tiny brush for that final mark. The troop could now see both the whole sky and the small birds from the lane.
