# The saloon milk duel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western bar confrontation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus asks for a very stiff drink in town. The troop goes still. Dot gives him warm milk with five heaps of sugar. Gus tips some on his hat and chair, then asks for more. Soon he is stuck to the seat. Rex gives him a drink to free him. It is plain water. Gus says the town is soft, but his hat stays on the floor since the sugar has glued it there.
