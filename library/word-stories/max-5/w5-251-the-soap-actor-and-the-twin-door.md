# The soap actor and the twin door

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Soap opera surprise twins*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Alf, Bea, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Alf was a lost twin in a soap show. Bea stood by the door, set to gasp when he came in. Alf came too soon. Bea gave her gasp to an empty chair, for his tail was stuck on a coat peg. The crew had to stop. Bea freed him, then wrote Enter on the right page. They did the scene once more. Alf came in on cue. Bea gave a real gasp this time, since he wore her coat and all her hats at once.
