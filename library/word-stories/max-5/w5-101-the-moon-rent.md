# The moon rent

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space settlement bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip put his flag on the moon. His troop had a fine new home. Then Dot came out of a cave with a bill. She had got there first and owned the land. Pip had no gold, so he gave her his flag. Dot used it as a mop. The moon was dusty, and rent was now one clean cave each month.
