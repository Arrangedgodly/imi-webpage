# The court of crumbs

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom expert witness*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip sued Gus for the last slice of cake. The judge asked for proof. Dot held up a crumb. Gus said a crumb could come from any cake. The troop gave a nod. Dot asked Gus to open his mouth. He had blue icing on his teeth. Gus said that meant only that he liked blue. The judge told both sides to bring whole cakes for a fair test.
