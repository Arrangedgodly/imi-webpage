# The spell that swept names

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school identity mixup*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Ivy, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo cast a spell to clean the class list. It took each name right off. The troop could not reply at roll call. Ivy asked Bo who he was. He did not know. Dot found the names in a dust pan. She put them back by size. Bo was Gus for one day. He liked the large lunch, but Gus asked for his small boots back.
