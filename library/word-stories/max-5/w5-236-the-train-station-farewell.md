# The train station farewell

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic departure cliche*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip runs to stop the train May is on. The troop waves from the track. Pip yells that she must stay. May leans out and asks why. He holds up her lunch. She left it on the table. May takes the bag with her tail and gives him a kiss. The train goes. Pip finds her pass in his paw. It had stuck to the bag. He has to run after the train once more.
