# The captain hat tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate rank rivalry*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex wore the high hat on the ship. He said that made him boss. The troop piled hats on their heads to claim his rank. Dot came with no hat at all. She could see under the sail, so she held the wheel while the rest hit the rails. Rex gave her the wheel. She sold the hats to buy a chart of small reefs.
