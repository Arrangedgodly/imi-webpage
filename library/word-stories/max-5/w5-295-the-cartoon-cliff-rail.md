# The cartoon cliff rail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Cartoons disregard safety even within practical staging*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ivan, Lena, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivan drew a hero who ran off a cliff and hung in the air. Lena asked why his tail could not grab a tree. Ivan said the fall had a joke in it, his furry face grave. Lena drew a tree in the next panel and let the tail catch it. Ivan added an odd face as the hero swung back to the path. The strip still had a joke. The hero could do his task now. Lena drew a rail in the last panel for the next safe trip.
