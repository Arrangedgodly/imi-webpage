# The baton in the bread

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Relay race mix up*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Gus, May, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May gives the baton to Bo. He runs to the goal, but the judge says no. Bo holds a loaf. The troop mixed up the snack bag and the race bag. May runs back to fetch the baton. She finds Gus with jam on it. The judge waits while they wash it, then gives the troop a prize for a very slow clean pass.
