# The wanted poster typo

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western outlaw identity*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

A sign calls Pip a bread thief. The troop spots him by the bread shop. Dot asks why his face is on the wall. Pip reads the sign. The baker meant to call him bread chief. One word is wrong. Dot makes a new sign. Pip gets the job but still wears a mask. Flour dust makes him look a lot worse than the face on his old sign.
