# The ski hill queue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Winter sports contest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned puts wax on his skis. He races past the troop and over the hill. At the base he finds a long queue. No one can walk back up the slick snow. Ned has waxed the whole slope with his skis. Dot lends him a rope. He goes up the tree and uses his tail to haul each skier up. The prize is a broom.
