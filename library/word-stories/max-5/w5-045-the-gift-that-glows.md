# The gift that glows

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted gift*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned gets a stone that glows at night. He shows the troop and puts it under his bed. His fur glows blue, and his tail casts a long shade. He hides his eyes with a paw. He can not sleep. Dot buys the stone to light her shed. Ned gets a plain dark rock for his bed and has the best night in years.
