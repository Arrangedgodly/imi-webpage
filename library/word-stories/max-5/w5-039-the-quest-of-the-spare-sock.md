# The quest of the spare sock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Quest for a missing relic*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo seeks the mate to his sock. He asks each one of the troop to lift a paw. No luck. His tail drags in mud and his fur fills with burrs. At dusk he sits on a stump and sighs. The sock hangs on his own tail. He puts it on at last, then finds he has lost the other one.
