# The map on the sail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate treasure map*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex drew a map on his sail so he could not lose it. The troop set off to find the gold. Wind blew in the sail, and the map went round a pole. Rex could see only half. Dot cut a small map from paper. Rex said that might blow away. Dot put it in a box. Rex did not know maps could fit in boxes.
