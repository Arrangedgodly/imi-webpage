# The spy film cuff

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy heroes confuse elegance with functionality*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Glen, Hana, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Glen wore gold cuffs in the spy film. One cuff held his tail in a snag as he tried to get the coded note. Hana had to wait as he spun in his chair. They took a break to put plain cloth cuffs on his coat. Glen read the note, then left the room with no snags. Hana kept the gold pair in a prop box for a close shot only. Glen asked for the cloth ones in each chase. A free tail gave him more speed than a bit of gold on his paws.
