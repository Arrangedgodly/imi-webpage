# The castle with round discs

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Moving enchanted home*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned makes a fort on discs. The troop rolls it up a hill to gain a grand view. Ned has dust in his fur and a sore paw. His tail slips off the brake. The fort rolls down to the lake. It bobs. Ned calls it a royal barge. Dot asks for a ride, then notes that the seat is now the drain.
