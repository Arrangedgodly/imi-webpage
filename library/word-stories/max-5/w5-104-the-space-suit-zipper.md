# The space suit zipper

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Spacewalk emergency*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo wore his new suit to step out of the ship. His tail stuck out of the back. The troop could not close the seal. Dot put a pipe over the tail and fixed a cap on it. Bo went out and waved. He used his new tail pipe to hook lost bolts. Dot made him chief of all ship tool work.
