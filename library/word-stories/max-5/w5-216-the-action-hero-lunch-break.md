# The action hero lunch break

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action countdown parody*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip has ten ticks to stop a loud alarm. The troop eyes the clock. He leaps over boxes and slips under a gate. Dot uses the open side door. She turns off the alarm and puts lunch on the table. Pip comes in with torn pants and a grand pose. Dot asks him to carry the soup. He says his knees need a stunt aide for that scene.
