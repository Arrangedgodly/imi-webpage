# The dragon candle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dragon guarding treasure*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot met a beast with smoke in its nose. The troop hid, but Dot held up a cake. She asked it to light the wick. It blew once. Her fur got warm and her tail rose. She gave a glad clap. The beast had hoped to guard gold. Soon it stood at the cake shop, with a line of cakes and a sign for tips.
