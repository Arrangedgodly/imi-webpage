# The case of the red moon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Misleading visual clue*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May sees a red moon above the shed. She calls Dot to solve the odd sight. The troop comes in fear. Dot walks up to it with a stick. The moon hangs from a cord. It is a red globe Rex got for his play. May asks why it has teeth. Rex says the crowd got bored with his plain old moon last week.
