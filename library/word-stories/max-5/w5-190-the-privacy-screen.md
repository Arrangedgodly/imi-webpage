# The privacy screen

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office privacy misunderstanding*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May asked for a cloth to hide her work. Ned gave her one full of holes. The troop could read each note. Dot hung a coat over it. Ned said that was wrong. May wrote her next notes on the coat. Ned could not read the back, but wore it to see his boss. His boss saw May ask for more pay in huge red words all over his back.
