# The costume coat hem

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Costume departments use dramatic accessories impractically*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Karl, Oona, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Karl wore a long royal coat on stage. Oona had to walk after him with its tail end in her paws. It would catch on seats if she let go. The furry actor saw her lose all her own cues. They put the coat hem up with pins until it came to his knees. Karl could walk alone, and Oona could play her role. The coat was still grand from afar. After the bow Karl took the pins out, and Oona hung it flat for the next show.
