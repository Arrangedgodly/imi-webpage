# The fairy census

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy invasion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot hears a voice in a rose. It asks for milk. She sets a cup by the rose. Next day ten cups stand on the step. The troop helps her fill them. Dot gets milk on her fur, tail, and paw. She asks how many fair folk live there. One voice says one. It has ten tiny cats. Dot sets out a cow and goes back to bed.
