# The pirate fruit treaty

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate territorial dispute*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo and Gus both took the same isle. Both crews met on the beach. Dot drew a line down the sand. Bo got the trees, and Gus got the brook. Gus got fish, but Bo had no water for fruit. They gave one cup for one plum. At dusk the tide took the line away. They kept the trade since both liked their meal.
