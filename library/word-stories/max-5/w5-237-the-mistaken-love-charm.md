# The mistaken love charm

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic magical charm*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo buys a charm to make Dot love him. He wears it to the troop feast. Dot asks him to pass the salt. Bo feels the charm has won. She asks Gus the same thing. Gus gets no nod. Dot sees the charm and reads its tag. It says charm for soup. Bo puts it in his bowl. The soup is fine, and he asks for one more date with it.
