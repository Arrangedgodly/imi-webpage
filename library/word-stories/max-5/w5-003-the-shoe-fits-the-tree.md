# The shoe fits the tree

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost glass slipper*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May left a glass shoe at the ball. Rex took it round the troop to find its mate. Each paw was too wide. Each tail got in the way. May saw him try the shoe on a limb and had to laugh till her fur shook. It fit the tree just right. Rex gave the tree a dance, while May went home in boots.
