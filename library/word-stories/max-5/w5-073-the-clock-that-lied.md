# The clock that lied

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Time based mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop says Pip stole a plum at noon. The clock stood at noon when the plum was lost. Pip says he was at the pond. Dot opens the clock and finds a plum pit in its gears. The chef used the clock as a bin. Its hands have been stuck for days. Pip goes home with his name clear and a job to clean the clock.
