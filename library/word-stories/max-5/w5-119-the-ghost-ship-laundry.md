# The ghost ship laundry

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Haunted pirate ship*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May saw white forms move on a dark ship. The troop hid below deck. May took a lamp on board. The forms were wet coats on a line. Wind blew them up as if alive. She found Ned as he tried to fold them. May used her tail to hold the line still. The troop came back, and each got a clean shirt in place of a scary tale.
