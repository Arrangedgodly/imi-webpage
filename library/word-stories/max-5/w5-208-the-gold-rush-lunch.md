# The gold rush lunch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western prospecting fever*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex finds a gold flake in the brook. The troop runs to pan for gold. Dot tries one flake and says it is corn. Gus has lost his lunch in the brook. Rex says corn can still be sold. The troop fills sacks and heads to town. Gus meets them with a bill for his lunch. The gold rush ends in a long talk about who owns the bits.
