# The encore note card

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Performers fail to distinguish demand from closure*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Kent, Pru, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kent sang a last song each time he heard claps. Pru tried to leave the hall, but the furry star began again when she gave a clap on her way out. She held a card that said Good Night. Kent bowed and let the song end. The troop could go home at last. Pru put the card by the mic for the next show. Kent used it after one extra tune, then gave a small wave. The crowd could clap at the close and still walk out of the open door.
