# The open door policy

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Management slogan literalized*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex said his door was never shut. The troop went in to ask for more pay. Rex shut it at once. Dot took off the hinge pins to keep his word true. Wind blew his notes into the hall. Rex said he would pay more if she fixed the door. Dot put the pins back but kept the door wide open. The troop lined up for more pay.
