# The horror prop squeal

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Horror soundtracks exaggerate harmless events*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Clem, Dara, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Clem took the lid off a box in the scary film. Dara put on a loud wail. The box held a plain comb. Clem leapt so hard his tail hit the lid. The crew had to fix it. Dara used a quiet creak on the next take. Clem saw the comb, gave his fur a brush, and found a key in its case. The scene had a real clue now. Dara saved the loud wail for the last door, where the key did not fit and the hero had cause to fear.
