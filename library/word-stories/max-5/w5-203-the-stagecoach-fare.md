# The stagecoach fare

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western travel robbery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus stops a coach to take its gold. The coach chief says the coach has only bills. The troop looks in the sacks. Each sack has rent bills. Gus tries to leave, but Dot says he made the coach stop and must pay the late fee. Gus hauls it to town on his own. He gets there with no gold and a new job as a horse with a hat.
