# The game show prize goat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Game shows neglect winners practical circumstances*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ned, Ora, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned won a goat suit on the game show. Ora asked him to pose in it. The suit had four leg holes but no tail hole. Ned could move only in short hops. Ora bent down to cut a slot as the crew stood by. Ned could walk with ease, then gave a loud stage bleat that made Ora leap from her chair. They kept that take as the last gag. Ned took the suit home for the troop play next year, with his tail free to sway.
