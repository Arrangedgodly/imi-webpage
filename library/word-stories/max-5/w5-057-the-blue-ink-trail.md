# The blue ink trail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detective follows footprints*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May finds blue marks on her clean floor. She calls Dot to solve the case. Dot looks for blue paws in the troop. No one has them. But Pip has a blue tail. He used it as a brush to make a map. Dot reads the trail as lines on a map. It leads to the milk jar, which Pip has put on the roof. Dot gets it and hands Pip a mop to draw his next map.
