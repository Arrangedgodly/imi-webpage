# The briefcase maze

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal brief mixup*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned put his notes in a case in a case. He said that made them safe. At court the troop saw him open each lock. Dot asked for the key to the tiny case. Ned said it was in the large one. The judge took a break while they had a look. Dot found the notes on top of the first case. Ned had left them there to make a label.
