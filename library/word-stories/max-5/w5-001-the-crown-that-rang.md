# The crown that rang

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Chosen royal heir*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip found a gold crown in a tree. Each time he put it on, it rang like a bell. The troop came to bow, then held their ears. Pip hid his tail in his fur and tried to look grand. Dot gave the crown a tap with her paw. A bee flew out. Its hive had a new king, and that king owed rent.
