# The matching scarf

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic couple fashion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip gives May a scarf to match his own. The troop feels it looks sweet. Dot finds that both wraps are parts of one long strip. Pip and May can not walk apart. They try a date, but each takes a fork to a cake at the far end. Dot cuts the scarf in two. May eats plum, Pip eats pear, and both agree that two short wraps make lunch much less messy.
