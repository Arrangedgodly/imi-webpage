# The tumbleweed post office

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western frontier postal service*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot ties a note to a dry weed. Wind rolls it to town. The troop goes with it to see if it gets to the post hut. It rolls past the door and into the yard of Rex. Rex reads the note. It asks him to keep weeds out of the lane. Dot holds out her paw for a mail fee. Rex pays with a new weed and asks her to send his reply.
