# The wish for silence

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wish gone wrong*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot asks a star for quiet. The troop falls mute. Her tail makes no swish, and her paw makes no tap. Even her fur can not swish. Dot likes it till the tea boils. She can not hear the pot. She waves for her next wish. Sound comes back just as the pot tips. The star gives her a mop as a free third wish.
