# The crater cake

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Moon exploration discovery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex found a round hole on the moon. The troop dug down and found a cake tin. Rex said the moon must be one huge cake. Dot took a bite of a rock and spat it out. Pip came from the ship with flour on his fur. He had tried to bake a cake out there. It flew away, and only the tin came back.
