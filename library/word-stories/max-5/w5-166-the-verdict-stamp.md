# The verdict stamp

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom clerical error*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex made a stamp that said wrong to save the court time. Dot used it on the first file. It was a claim for a leaky roof. The troop heard the judge blame the roof. Rex said the stamp was fine and the file was wrong. Dot took him out in the rain. He found all the proof he could want on his wet fur. He told the crew to fix the roof.
