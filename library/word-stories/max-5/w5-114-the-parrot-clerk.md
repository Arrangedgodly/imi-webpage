# The parrot clerk

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate pet repeats secrets*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned had a bird that spoke each word he said. The troop loved it till Ned named the spot where he hid his gold. The bird told the whole port. Ned wrote a false place on a card and told the bird to read it aloud. The bird said read it aloud. It knew the trick. Ned paid it one seed to keep each hint for a day.
