# The love song rehearsal fee

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic serenade business*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned hires Gus to teach him a love song. The troop hears him sing. Dot asks who the song is for. Ned says May. May comes in with a bill for the room they are using. She owns the music hall. Ned sings to pay his rent. May takes the song but bills him for the echo. Gus gives Ned a small song of just one verse to learn next time.
