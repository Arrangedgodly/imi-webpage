# The zero inbox contest

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Productivity metric gaming*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex gave a prize for an empty inbox. The troop raced to clear their mail. Gus put his box in a bin. Dot sent all hers to Rex. May made a new box with no lid, so notes fell out. Rex won the prize, since his desk broke under all the notes. His box now lay flat under the floor, where no mail could fit.
