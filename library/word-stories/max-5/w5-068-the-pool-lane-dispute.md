# The pool lane dispute

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Swimming competition rules*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus and Bo race in the pool. Gus wins, but Bo says his tail hit the line first. The troop reads the rules. They say first paw wins. Dot lays a plank over the pool. Gus walks over it on his paws. Bo swims below it. Gus wins again. The judge adds one word to the rules, and the plank is now a swim ramp.
