# The rocket salad

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Launch fuel confusion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May put green fuel in her ship. It would not rise. The troop had a look in the tank and found pea soup. Gus had used the fuel jar as a lunch pot. May took out the soup with a cup. Gus gave her the real fuel from his lunch flask. The ship rose at once. Gus sat by each bowl to check it prior to tea time.
