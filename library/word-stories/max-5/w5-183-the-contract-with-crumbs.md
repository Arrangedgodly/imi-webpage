# The contract with crumbs

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal document dispute*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip put his name on a deal at lunch. Jam stuck two pages shut. The troop read just the first page. Dot got them apart at dusk. The next page said Pip owed Gus a pie each week. Gus gave a grin. Dot held the page by the fire. More jam fell off. A third page fell out. It said Gus had to bake each pie. Gus got his apron.
