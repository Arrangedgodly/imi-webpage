# The villain chair warranty

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Villain luxury lair*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex spins in his tall chair to greet the troop. The chair spins on and on. Dot tries the brake, but it snaps off. Rex yells that he will rule the world once each turn. Pip puts a broom in the wheel and stops him. Rex asks who broke his chair. Dot hands him the sales slip. His next plot is to get his coins back from the chair shop.
