# The lunch bell thief

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: School mystery revelation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The lunch bell is gone. The troop waits at their desks with empty bowls. Dot sees Ned wave both paws at the cook. He has tied the bell to his tail. Each wag rings it, but the thick door stops the sound. Dot opens the door. The cook comes with soup. Ned tries to sit down, and the bell calls for lunch all over again.
