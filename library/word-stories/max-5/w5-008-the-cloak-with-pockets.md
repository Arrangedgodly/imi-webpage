# The cloak with pockets

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Invisible clothing scam*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Rex, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex got a cloak no one could see. He wore it to meet the troop. His fur was cold, and his tail shook. Ned gave him a real coat. Rex put his paw into its deep pouch and found a note from the cloak shop. It said the cloak was free, but the pouch cost gold. Ned used the note to light the stove.
