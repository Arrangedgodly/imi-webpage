# The science film blinking panel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Science fiction relies on useless blinking interfaces*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Finn, Gia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Finn sat at a panel full of lamps in the space film. Gia asked him to press the key that would start the ship. None had a label. The furry actor took a guess and a lamp went out by his tail. Gia ended the take and wrote Start on one key and Stop on the next. Finn had a clear task now. The scene went well in one take. Gia left the other lamps off, so fans could see which key sent the ship on its way.
