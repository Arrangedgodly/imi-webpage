# The dance card auction

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic ballroom mixup*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May puts names on a dance card. Rex buys it as a list of debts. He asks each one in the troop for a coin. Dot reads the card and takes his paw. They dance till Rex is dizzy. May says he must dance five times more. Rex gives the card back for free and asks to sit down. Dot puts his last coin in the music box.
