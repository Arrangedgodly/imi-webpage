# The badge reader audit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Workplace access control*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip got a badge to enter work. The gate would not let him in. He held it with his paw, then his tail. No luck. The troop waved from the glass door. Dot came out and read his badge. It said guest. Pip had done work there for years. Rex wrote old guest on it. Pip still had to knock each day to get past the gate.
