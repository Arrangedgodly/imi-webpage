# The secret admirer stamp

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic anonymous gift*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Ned, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy gets a box from a shy fan. It holds a plum and no name. The troop tries to guess who sent it. Dot looks at the stamp. It has a paw print made by Ned. Ivy gives Ned a hug for the plum. He says he sent a whole crate. Dot takes a trail of pits to Gus. Ivy says Gus saved her from a belly ache, then asks for the crate to store her notes in.
