# The soap show clock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Soap operas stretch time until plots lose sense*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Jake, Mara, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jake shot a soap scene in which Mara had to wait for her tea. Five shows went by and the cup still had not come. The furry actor asked if her guest must have a dry mouth for a month. Jake put tea in the next scene and let her drink. Mara could talk about the lost ring at last. Jake kept a clock on the set after that. Each long pause had to leave room for the plot to move. The tea pot had a real place by the stove.
