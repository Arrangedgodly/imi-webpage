# The travel host toll lesson

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Travel shows create false exotic drama*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Lou, Mae, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lou said a plain toll booth was a lost altar on his tour show. Mae, who ran the booth, asked the furry host for two coins. Lou tried a magic chant but did not pay. Mae kept the gate shut until he did. The crew shot the whole scene with the real booth name in view. Lou saw his error and got a map. His next show told the troop how to cross the creek, with no chant and no fake tale about the old toll booth.
