# The dry paw clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Locked room mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rain falls all day. A pie is gone from the hut. Dot looks at each paw in the troop. Only May has dry fur. Dot feels sure she has her thief. May shows her a hole in the roof. Her bed is wet too. She dried her paws at the fire, where the chef ate the pie right out of the pan. Dot puts the empty pan in jail and gives May a towel.
