# The invisible ink test

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school examination*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot wrote all her work in ink no one could see. Ivy held the page up to the light. It was blank. The troop said Dot must have won. Ivy gave her a prize no one could see. Dot felt the empty box with one paw. She asked for plain ink next time. Ivy gave her a real pen and kept the blank prize for the next test.
