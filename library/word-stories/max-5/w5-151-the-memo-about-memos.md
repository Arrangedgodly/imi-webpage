# The memo about memos

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office communication overload*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex sent a memo to ban memos. Dot sent a reply to ask if notes back were still fine. The troop got both and sent kind notes. By noon Rex had a desk full of notes. He gave a talk to clear it up. Gus wrote the talk down and sent it to each desk. Rex put his tail over the inbox and went out for tea.
