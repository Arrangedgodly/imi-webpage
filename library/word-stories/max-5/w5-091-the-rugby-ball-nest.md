# The rugby ball nest

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Oval ball sports confusion*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo sees an odd egg on the field. He puts it in his fur to keep it warm. The troop hunts for their ball. Dot asks Bo what he found. He shows her the egg. She says it is their rugby ball. Bo will give it back if they do not drop it. The whole game is now a very soft game of catch.
