# The puppet crown swap

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Puppet theaters complicate simple costume changes*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Eli, Fern, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Eli used a new doll for each royal role in the play. Fern had to pull ten furry kings on cords at the back of the small stage. Their tails tied knots in the cords. Eli took a pause and moved them apart. Fern used one doll and a new crown for each king. The troop knew each role by its hat. Eli put the spare dolls by the door, where young fans could pose with them. No cords could tie knots in those tails now.
