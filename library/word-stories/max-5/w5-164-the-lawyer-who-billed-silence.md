# The lawyer who billed silence

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal fee satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned said he could win the case with one word. Pip paid him a coin for each pause in court. Ned sat quiet. The troop saw the judge wait. At last Dot said yes. Ned stood and said no. He had used his one word. The judge asked him for proof. Ned said his one word was used up and sent Pip a bill for the pause.
