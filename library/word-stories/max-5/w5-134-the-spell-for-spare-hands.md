# The spell for spare hands

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic multiplication mishap*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: May, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May asked Ivy for extra hands to wash the bowls. Ivy gave her four. May held more bowls, but her cuffs did not fit. The troop gave her a huge coat. Each new hand got stuck in a pouch. May could not reach the bowls. She asked Ivy to take them back. Ivy left one spare hand to hold the towel while May had a wash in her old shirt.
