# The spell for neat desks

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school classroom cleanup*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Ivy, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy cast a spell to make the desks neat. Each desk shut up to form a small cube. The troop had no place to sit. Bo put a cube in his paw and shook it. The desk rose up and threw his books. Ivy told the troop to give each cube a soft tap. They shut their desks after class and took the whole room home in their lunch bags.
