# The floating soup

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Zero gravity dining*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus made soup on the ship. He took off the lid, and a warm blob rose into the air. The troop tried to catch it in bowls. Gus held a spoon in each paw but got no soup. Dot set a fan to blow. The soup blew into the air vent. Gus set cups below it. Lunch came from the roof with a fine gust.
