# The vow in the wind

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic wedding ceremony*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, May, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May and Bo write vows on loose cards. Wind takes the cards with no time to read them. Dot runs for the pages. She gets a store list and a rent bill in their place. May reads that she will buy milk. Bo vows to pay rent. The troop roars since those vows are of some use. Dot finds the real vows later and uses them to wrap the cake they take home.
