# The royal image itch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal image commission*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex sits for a grand image. He keeps his tail rigid and his paw on a gold ball. His skin grows itchy. He moves. The troop tells him to hold still. Dot draws each pose on a fresh sheet. Rex sees a blur of arms when she flips them. He buys the whole stack and calls it his first royal film show.
