# The missing goal

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports equipment mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The troop comes to play ball, but the goal is gone. Pip finds two long marks in the mud. The marks lead to a shed. Gus sits there with the goal round his bed. He says it keeps flies off. Pip finds the net full of fruit. Gus is glad to move his bed to the field. Now each goal earns a free plum.
