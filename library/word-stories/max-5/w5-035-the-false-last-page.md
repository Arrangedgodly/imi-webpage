# The false last page

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy tale happily ever after*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May reads that her tale must end with a feast. She cooks for the troop and wipes her fur clean. Her tail holds the spoon as her paw turns the last page. There is one more page. It says wash up. May tears it out and makes a paper crown. At the feast, she names Gus king of all the dirty bowls.
