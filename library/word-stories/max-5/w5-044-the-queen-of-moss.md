# The queen of moss

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Nature royal transformation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy makes a crown from moss. It grows down her fur and round her tail. Soon she can just lift a paw. The troop soaks her each day. She asks them to stop, but they think it is royal music. Dot hears the words and cuts the moss. Ivy is free. The troop keeps the crown on a stump, where its reign is far less noisy.
