# The fake wedding cake

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic fake engagement*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May and Gus act as bride and groom so they can get cake. The troop gives gifts too. Dot asks where the vows are. Gus says he is still at work on them. May asks for a slice while they wait. Dot gives them a cake made of wood. It suits the false vows. May and Gus buy real cake, share it with the troop, and keep the wood one as their table.
