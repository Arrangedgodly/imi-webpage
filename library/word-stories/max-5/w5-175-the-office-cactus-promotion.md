# The office cactus promotion

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Absurd workplace awards*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned gave a prize to the one who asked least. The troop had a row over the prize. Dot chose the plant by the door. It had asked none. Ned gave it a gold badge and its own desk. The plant asked for a large pot. The troop gazed at it. Gus came out from under the desk with a hose and said the new chief could use water.
