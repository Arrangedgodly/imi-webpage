# The comet hairbrush

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space souvenir trouble*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Dot, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy took a brush from a comet. The brush made her fur glow. Her troop could see her all night. She liked it till she tried to hide in a game. Dot found her in the dark shed at once. Ivy gave the brush to the lamps in the hall. Now the lamps glow, and Ivy can hide till her tail pokes out.
