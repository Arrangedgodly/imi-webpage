# The ice cream reunion

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic reunion scene*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May meets Ned after years apart. The troop hopes for a grand talk. Ned gives her an ice cream. May takes it and asks what he has done all these years. Ned says he has sold ice cream. Dot asks why he never wrote. Ned holds up his messy paws. May buys him a spoon and a pen. He puts his first note on the paper wrap while she eats his whole stock.
