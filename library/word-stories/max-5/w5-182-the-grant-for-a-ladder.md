# The grant for a ladder

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Grant application bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo asked for steps to fix the roof. Rex said he must prove the roof was bad. Bo sent a photo from below. Rex asked for one from above. The troop made a stack of boxes. Bo went up with his phone. The stack fell, but he got his shot. Rex paid for the steps but said no to the bill for the boxes. Bo sent a photo of those too.
