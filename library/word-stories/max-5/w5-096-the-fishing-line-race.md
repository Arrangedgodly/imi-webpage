# The fishing line race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Competitive angling parody*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus hooks a huge fish. He pulls so hard his tail grips the bank. The troop helps. At last a boot comes out of the lake. Dot reads a tag in it. Pip says he lost that boot last week. Gus says he still wins the best catch. Pip says the boot must go on a scale. He takes out the huge stone he hid in it.
