# The romance rose cart

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romance films treat expensive gifts as automatic love*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Bram, Cia, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bram got a cart of roses for Cia in the love film. Cia had no room for them in her small flat. The furry actor left the cart in her door in hope of a kiss. Cia asked him to move it so she could go to work. Bram gave the roses to the park crew and went with her to the bus. The scene ended with a wave, not a kiss. He kept one rose in a cup where her tail would not knock it over.
