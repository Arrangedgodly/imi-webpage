# The door with tests

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted door quiz*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Pip, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo keeps a door that gives a quiz. Pip knows the right reply, but the door tests him. Pip rubs his fur and wraps his tail round the knob. The troop waits in the rain. Bo puts his paw on a hinge and asks the door to state its proof. The door has none. Bo gives it a book to study. It opens to let him turn each page.
