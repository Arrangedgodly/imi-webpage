# The flying broom closet

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic school flying lesson*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-003*
*Cast: Ned, Ivy, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned chose a broom from the class shed. He rode it to the sky. The troop saw the broom shake free. Ned clung to a cloud and held on. Ivy sent up a mop. Ned flew home on it and asked why the broom had fled. It still had its price tag. The shop asked for it back till he paid the bill.
