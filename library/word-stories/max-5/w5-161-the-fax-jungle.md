# The fax jungle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Obsolete office technology*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo sent a fax to the next room. The troop heard a grind, then saw paper climb the wall. Bo had fed the fax a long roll. Dot took its tail end and tore it free. The fax had split hello into five parts and put one part on each page. Rex put all five pages on the wall as proof that his firm used less paper.
