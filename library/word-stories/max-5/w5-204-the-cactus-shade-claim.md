# The cactus shade claim

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western land dispute*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo buys a patch of land with one plant. He says the troop can rent its shade. At noon the shade is tiny. Dot lies flat and fits under it. Rex tries and gets a thorn in his tail. Bo asks for a fee to pull it out. Dot says she can pay him with shade from her hat. By dusk Bo gives the land in trade for the hat. The troop gets the plant as well.
