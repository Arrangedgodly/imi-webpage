# The novel chapter cake

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Authors delay endings to extend a series*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Ames, Bess, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ames wrote a tale about a baker who lost her cake. Bess read each part and asked where it was. Ames added a new quest each time, his pen firm in a furry paw. At last Bess drew a cake on the final page and wrote Found It. Ames saw how tired she was. He wrote a real close at the bake shop door. The cake was there on a cart. Bess paid for the book and made a cake to eat while he wrote his name on the cover.
