# The tea expense

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Expense claim satire*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, May, Ned, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May paid for tea and filed a claim. Ned asked for proof. She gave him the bill. He asked who had drunk it. The troop held up their mugs. He asked if the tea aided work. Dot put cold tea on his stuck desk lock. It swung open. Ned let the claim pass as tool care and asked if they could fix his loud chair with bread.
