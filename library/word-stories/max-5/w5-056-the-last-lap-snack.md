# The last lap snack

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Endurance race rivalry*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus leads the race till the last lap. He sees a fruit stall and stops for lunch. The troop races past. Gus asks the chef to pack his meal in a paper cone. He uses his tail to hold it as he runs. At the line he is last, but all the rest want a bite. He gives out bites in trade for each prize bow.
