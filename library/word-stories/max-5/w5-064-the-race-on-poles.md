# The race on poles

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Underdog track contest*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip ties long poles to his feet to beat the tall troop in a race. He takes huge steps till a paw slips. He lands on his back while the poles run down the slope. Dot grabs them at the line. The judge says the poles win. Pip asks them to speak. They stay mute, so he takes the prize for them and buys a pair of flat shoes.
