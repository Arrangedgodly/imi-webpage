# The nature film tame roar

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Wildlife films stage animal danger*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Cam, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cam shot a film about the wild woods. He asked Dot for a great roar. Dot gave a soft peep. Cam put a roar on tape, but it went on while Dot sat still and took fleas from her fur. Dot asked for the real sound. Cam shot her paws as she took seeds from a shell. The film used that quiet scene. Fans tried her neat trick at home. Cam put the roar tape away and paid Dot a whole bowl of seeds.
