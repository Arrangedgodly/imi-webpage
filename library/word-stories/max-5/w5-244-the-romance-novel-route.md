# The romance novel route

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic fiction meddles with life*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot reads that love must start in a storm. She goes out with the troop when rain falls. Bo stays at home and makes tea. Dot comes back wet and cross. Bo gives her a towel. She reads the next page, where the hero gives a towel. Dot shuts the book. Bo asks for it to level his chair. Their first date is tea by a chair held level by a soggy love tale.
