# The lost library hush

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Quiet place detective case*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dot hears a faint chirp in the book room. The troop can not read in peace. She looks on each shelf and finds no one. May shows her the sign that says hush. A toy wheel turns at its back when the wind blows. Dot shuts the pane. The sound stops. Then the room gets hot. The troop opens the pane and reads books about noisy gears.
