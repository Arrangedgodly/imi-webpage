# The series recap chain

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Television recaps replace the actual story*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Paz, Rue, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Paz shot a recap for the new show. Rue asked for a recap of that one, since it went on too long. The furry crew made a third clip to sum up the last two. Soon the day was gone and no new scene was done. Paz drew the last plot on one small card. Rue held it in view for ten beats, then began the new scene. The troop saw the new tale at last. Paz filed the old clips and kept the card for next week.
