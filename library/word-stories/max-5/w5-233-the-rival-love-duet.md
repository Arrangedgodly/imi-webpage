# The rival love duet

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic musical rivalry*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, May, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo and Rex both sing for May. The troop roars at each tune. May asks them to sing as one so she can judge. Each voice jars, and the panes shake. Dot shuts the glass and turns the duet into a quiet show. May likes the moves and hires both to mime at her shop. Bo and Rex vie to stay quiet. Their next duet has no words at all.
