# The hoop in the tree

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Basketball court relocation*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus puts a hoop high in a tree. The troop lobs balls but can not score. Dot goes up and finds a nest in the hoop. She moves the nest to a safe limb and drops the hoop. Gus says the game is too easy now. Dot puts him in the nest. He must keep each ball out. By dusk he asks to join the team on the grass.
