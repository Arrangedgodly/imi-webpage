# The copier portrait

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office machine prank*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus put his face on the copy box. The troop saw his nose on each memo. Rex asked who had made the new logo. Dot said the box had. Rex asked for more. Gus tried to clean the glass, but Rex said stop. He liked his new logo. Gus now had to put his face on the glass each week. He lost all taste for the prank by June.
