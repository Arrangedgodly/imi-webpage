# The legal dictionary dinner

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtroom jargon parody*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gus spoke in court with a book of long words. The judge asked what he meant. Gus read a word twice as long. The troop slept on the bench. Dot took the book and asked Gus to use words he used at home. Gus said pie. The judge knew that at once. He split the pie in half and used the book as a tray for both sides.
