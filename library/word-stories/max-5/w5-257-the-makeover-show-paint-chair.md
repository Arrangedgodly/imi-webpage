# The makeover show paint chair

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Makeover shows prioritize looks over usability*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Jay, Kit, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jay ran a show that gave old rooms a new look. Kit asked for a chair with a tail slot. Jay made the old chair gold but left the seat solid. Kit sat on its edge and fell off. Jay tried it and felt the same pinch in his fur. They cut a neat slot and made its edges blunt, then put on fresh paint. Kit sat in it for the last shot. The gold shone, but the slot gave her cause to smile.
