# The lease on the desk

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate asset monetization*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex made the troop pay rent for each desk. They did work on their laps. Rex then asked rent for seats. Dot went up a tree and did work on a limb. Rex asked rent for the tree. Dot asked for proof he owned it. He went to fetch the deed. The troop moved to the next tree. They sent Rex a bill for shade while he read his deed.
