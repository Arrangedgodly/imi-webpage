# The sitcom couch move

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Sitcom sets preserve impossible furniture*
*By: IMI Seven Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-006*
*Cast: Dax, Eve, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dax asked to move the couch on the set so he could walk to the door. Eve said it must stay for the wide shot. Dax tried to hop over it, but his tail hit Eve with a thump. They had to stop the take. Eve moved the couch just a bit. The shot was the same, and Dax had a path. He came in with ease. The joke came from his odd hat now, and Eve could drink her tea with no tail in her cup.
