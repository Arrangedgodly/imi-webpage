# The tower delivery

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Locked tower rescue*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Gus, May, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May lived in a tall tower. She let her tail hang down so Gus could climb. He had a cake in one paw. Bits got in her fur, and the cake fell on the troop below. May went down to taste it. Gus gazed at the open door. All week he had come up by the wall. The door had been for cake carts all along.
