# The wagon wheel feud

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Western family feud*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-005*
*Cast: Dot, Gus, May, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May and Bo fight over one wagon wheel. The troop can not solve the claim. Dot cuts the wheel in half. Both say no to their share. Dot says that shows they both care for the wheel. Gus says it shows a half wheel can not roll. Dot glues it back up and keeps it from both. She makes a swing with it and makes both sides pay for rides.
