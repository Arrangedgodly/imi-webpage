# The north wind scarf

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wind and sun fable*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-001*
*Cast: Dot, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

The wind tries to pull a scarf off Bo. Bo grips it with one paw and holds his tail tight. His fur stays warm. The sun tries next. Bo opens the scarf but puts on a shade hat. The troop bets on who will win. Dot gives Bo a plum. He takes off the scarf to make a fruit bag. Dot keeps the bets.
