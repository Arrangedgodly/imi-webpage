# The safe with a window

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist design flaw*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-004*
*Cast: Dot, Rex, Bo, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex built a safe no thief could crack. He made a pane so the troop could view the gold. Dot asked if the glass was tough. Rex said no one had tried it yet. Bo hit it with a spoon. The gold fell out. Rex added a cloth and said the flaw was fixed. Dot put the spoon back and took her gold to a new bank.
