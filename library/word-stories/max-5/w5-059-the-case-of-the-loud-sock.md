# The case of the loud sock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Noise complaint mystery*
*By: IMI Five Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W5-002*
*Cast: Pip, Dot, all fictional monkeys*

## Story

A chirp keeps the troop awake. Dot looks at the gate, the beds, and the roof. Pip walks with her. Each time he moves, the chirp moves too. She lifts one of his feet and finds a toy duck in his sock. Pip says he put it there to keep it safe. Dot gives the duck a nest. It gives her one last chirp.
