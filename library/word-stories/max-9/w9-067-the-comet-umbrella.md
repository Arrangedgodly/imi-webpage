# The comet umbrella

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space hazard*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Cleo, Joss; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo opened an umbrella to shield their spaceship from a comet. Joss said space had no rain. A shower of tiny stones hit the splendid cloth. Cleo held its handle with her tail while Joss steered. The cloth tore, but the ribs caught a shining stone. At home the museum bought it. Joss bought Cleo a new umbrella. She opened it indoors to check the ribs. A second stone dropped into the banana bowl and paid for their next trip.
