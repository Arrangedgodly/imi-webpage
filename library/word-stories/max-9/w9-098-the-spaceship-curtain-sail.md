# The spaceship curtain sail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space sailing*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Nan, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan lost the spaceship engine near a dusty planet. Poe hung an enormous curtain outside to catch sunlight. The ship drifted toward a dangerous cloud of rocks. Nan wrapped her tail round the curtain rope and tilted it. They sailed between the stones and reached a repair dock. Poe sold the curtain as a solar sail. Nan bought a fresh engine and new curtains. She kept one rope tied to her seat because the sun did not charge for fuel.
