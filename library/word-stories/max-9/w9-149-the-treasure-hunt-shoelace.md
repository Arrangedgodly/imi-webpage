# The treasure hunt shoelace

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: School treasure hunt*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Dara, Sol; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara followed clues to a treasure beneath the banana tree. Sol raced ahead with an enormous shovel. His shoelace caught a root, and he fell beside the last clue. Dara pulled him up with her tail. A chocolate coin lay inside a tiny box. Sol wanted half for finding the hole. Dara gave him half for digging it, then ate hers. They filled the hole together. The teacher added a final clue asking hunters to leave the tree roots safe.
