# The pirate cork parade

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate blockade*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Ned, Ria; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned blocked the harbor with an enormous chain. Ria wanted to sell bananas across the bay. She tied corks to each bunch and floated them under the chain, steering with her tail from a small raft. Ned called this a dangerous breach. Ria offered him a cork fee. He accepted, then found every boat copying her fruit rafts. By dusk the chain guarded an empty harbor. Ned sold it to Ria, who used it to anchor her new floating shop.
