# The ownership vine invoice

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Intellectual property*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Hugo, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hugo claimed ownership of the knot he used to hang bananas. Nia tied the same knot in a tree swing. Hugo sent a contract demanding payment for each twist. Nia untied the swing and held its ends in her tail. She charged him for testing his knot. Hugo refused. Nia let go of the loose ends. The swing fell, spilling his lunch basket. Nia offered to tie it back for one banana. Hugo paid, then crossed out his invoice. The knot kept its shape, and they kept their coins.
