# The detective ink tail

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Forgery mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Esme, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Esme found a forged note asking for ten bananas. Bo called the detective and locked the splendid fruit room. The note had a long ink smear. Esme compared it with each monkey tail, then noticed her own black tip. She had written a shopping list while painting the fence and forgotten to sign it. Bo opened the fruit room. Esme washed her tail before writing the next list. The detective charged one banana for the ink stain on his desk.
