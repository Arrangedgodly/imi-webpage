# The love letter ink frog

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic mix up*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Jade, Kip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade wrote Kip a beautiful letter beside the pond. A frog jumped into the ink and hopped across the page. Kip read its marks as a terrible storm warning and packed a boat with bananas. Jade met him at the dock, her tail holding the stained letter. She explained the frog. Kip unpacked the boat except for one picnic basket. They rowed to a quiet island. The frog rode in the empty ink cup and left its next letter on a plain rock.
