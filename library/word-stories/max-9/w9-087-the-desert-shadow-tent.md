# The desert shadow tent

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Desert rescue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Hugo, Flo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hugo lost his tent in an enormous sandstorm. Flo stretched banana leaves across their packs to make shade. Hugo chased the tent shadow he saw on a dune, but it moved with him. Flo caught his tail and made him sit. The shadow came from their own rescue balloon. She waved a leaf. The pilot lowered a ladder and served pineapple juice on the way home. Hugo kept the leaf roof. His next tent had a bright rope tied to it.
