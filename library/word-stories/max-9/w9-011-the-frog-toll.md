# The frog toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Enchanted prince*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Ada, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ada kissed the frog at the bridge. It stayed a frog and charged her a banana. Len said the tale promised a prince. The frog held up a contract, then pointed to the crown on its head. Ada read the fine print with her tail holding the page flat. The prince could choose his own shape. She paid for a return ticket instead. At breakfast the frog kissed her coin and hoped for gold.
