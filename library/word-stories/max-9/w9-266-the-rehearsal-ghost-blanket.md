# The rehearsal ghost blanket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Ghost play*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Pia, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia played a ghost during rehearsal. Tom washed the costumes beside the banana stage. Her blanket shrank, leaving her tail bare. The director wanted a spooky entrance, but the tail made the audience laugh. Pia used it to lift a chair without showing her hands. Tom added a plain sheet to the blanket. Pia kept the tail trick beneath it. The show scared no one, but every child asked how the chair floated. Tom sold the answer as a lesson in hidden tail tricks after the curtain fell.
