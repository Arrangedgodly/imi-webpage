# The hood with pockets

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Forest errand tale*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Red, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Red wore a hood with enormous pockets to carry bananas to Gus. A wolf asked which tree held her grandpa. Red gave it a map upside down. It ran straight into a pond. When she reached Gus, her pockets shook. The wet wolf had climbed from the pond and hidden in one. Gus offered it breakfast on the porch while Red stitched both pockets shut. The wolf went home with a full belly and a map it could finally read.
