# The marketing hollow fruit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Marketing stunt*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Etta, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Etta sold a banana with no pulp as a premium light snack. Gus counted earnings while customers found only peels. The marketing team claimed air was the new fruit. Etta tried to show it with her tail holding a peel open. A customer blew through it like a horn. Soon the shop rang with protests in loud notes. Gus refunded every sale. Etta renamed the stock party horns. This time she showed the empty center before taking any coins.
