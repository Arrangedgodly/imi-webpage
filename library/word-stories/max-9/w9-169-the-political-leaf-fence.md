# The political leaf fence

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Border dispute*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Uma, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma drew a political border through the banana grove. Cal built a fence from leaves. Each monkey had to pay a crossing fee to reach the water. A gardener moved the water bucket to each side in turn, using his tail as a handle. Uma taxed the bucket. Cal objected that the official map showed only land. The gardener planted the bucket on the border. Uma and Cal argued until thirst made them share it. The leaves dried and blew away.
