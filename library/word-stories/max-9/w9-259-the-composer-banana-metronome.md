# The composer banana metronome

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Music practice*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Ira, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira composed a song with a banana swinging as a metronome. Len, the musician, played beneath the tree. The fruit ripened and swung more slowly. Ira blamed Len for losing the beat. He caught the banana with his tail and showed the soft stem. Ira fetched a clock, but its ticking spoiled the song. Len tapped a steady beat on the trunk instead. Ira wrote it down before the fruit fell. They ate the old metronome and practiced with a plain wooden weight.
