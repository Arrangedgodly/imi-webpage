# The waterfall rope receipt

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: River crossing*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Tia, Mo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tia rented a rope to cross a waterfall. Mo read the contract while she tested its splendid knots. Halfway across, a bell rang on the bank. Their rent had expired. Tia hooked her tail round the rope and stayed put while Mo argued. The owner demanded another banana. Mo rolled one across the rope to Tia, who ate it and threw back the peel. The owner slipped into his own safety net. Mo took the receipt and extended the lease.
