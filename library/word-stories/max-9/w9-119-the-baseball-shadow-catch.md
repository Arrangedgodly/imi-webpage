# The baseball shadow catch

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports illusion*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Toma, Jade; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Toma chased a baseball across an enormous field. Jade shouted that he was following its shadow. He stopped beneath a banana tree and held his tail out. He caught the ball in his curled tail while its shadow slid away. Jade called the catch splendid. A dangerous wind sent the next hit toward the pond. Toma watched the shadow again but kept his eyes up. He caught the ball before it splashed. Jade painted a sun on his glove to remind him where to look.
