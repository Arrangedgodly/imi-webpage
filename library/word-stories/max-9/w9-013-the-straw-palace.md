# The straw palace

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Three houses tale*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Nico, Tess; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nico built a palace from straw. Tess built hers from banana crates. A terrible wind blew Nico into Tess through the open door. The king ordered a stone palace by breakfast. Nico had no stone, so he tied his tail to the crates and stacked them round the throne. Tess sold tickets to watch the wind test. It blew through the gaps. The king declared fresh air a royal asset and paid them in fruit.
