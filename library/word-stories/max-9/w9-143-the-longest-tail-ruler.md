# The longest tail ruler

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Measurement contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Vera, Kip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera entered a longest tail contest. Kip stretched his tail along an enormous ruler. Vera beat him by one inch. A detective noticed her tail curved behind a banana crate. Inside lay a spare rope tied to its tip. Vera said the rope was a tail coat. The judge measured both tails without clothes. Kip won, then lent Vera his prize scarf. She wore it round her neck, where the rules allowed any length she liked.
