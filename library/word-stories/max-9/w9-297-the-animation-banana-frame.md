# The animation banana frame

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Animation export*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Ari, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari finished an animation about a banana boat. Tom, the director, wanted an enormous frame around it for the festival. Ari used her tail to carry a wooden frame to the screen. It blocked half the picture. Tom meant a title frame inside the film, not wood outside. Ari added one with the boat name and took the wood home. The audience saw the whole voyage. Tom helped turn the spare frame into a window for the studio of Ari, where it finally showed a useful view.
