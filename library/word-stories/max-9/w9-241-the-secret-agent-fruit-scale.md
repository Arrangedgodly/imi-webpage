# The secret agent fruit scale

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy exchange*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Sia, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia met a dangerous agent at the banana stall. Rex weighed fruit to hide their secret exchange. Sia set a splendid envelope on the scale. Rex put a banana beside it, but the needle rose too far. The envelope held a gold key, not paper. Sia curled her tail round it before a customer saw. Rex demanded payment for the fruit used as cover. She gave him the banana. He kept the peel as a receipt, then sold the scale because he no longer knew what a normal sale weighed.
