# The beautiful duel rose

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic duel*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Nan, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan sent Sid a beautiful rose. He mistook its sharp thorns for a dangerous duel challenge and arrived with an enormous sword. Nan held the rose in her tail and asked for a vase. Sid planted his sword in the soil as a stake. They tied the rose to it and watered the roots. His rival arrived expecting a duel. Nan handed him a watering can too. The three spent the afternoon fixing the garden, and Sid left his sword where the rose needed it.
