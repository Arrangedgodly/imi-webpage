# The efficient complaint drum

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Public feedback*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Mina, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina gave citizens a drum for protests about banana taxes. Sid praised the efficient system because he could hear it from his desk. Soon the whole town pounded it day and night. Sid wrapped the drum in blankets, but the sound shook his chair. Mina held the sticks in her tail and asked each citizen for one sentence. She wrote them on leaves for the committee. Sid read the leaves, lowered the tax, and returned the drum to the music school.
