# The broadcast banana poll

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Audience polling*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Vera, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera asked the broadcast audience to vote for their favorite fruit. Rex, the director, counted banana peels mailed to the studio. One grower sent an enormous sack. Vera held it in her tail and noticed every peel came from the same bunch. Rex wanted to count all votes. Vera limited each sender to one reply. Bananas still won, but by less. The grower got his sack back with a note asking him to sell fruit instead of mailing breakfast waste to a camera.
