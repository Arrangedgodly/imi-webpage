# The tiny quest

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Epic quest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Hazel, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hazel packed a splendid sword for her quest. Tom packed breakfast. The map led through dark roots to a chest no bigger than a walnut. Hazel tried to pry it open with her sword, but the blade bent. Tom used the tip of his tail. Inside lay a tiny crown and a plea to guard an ant king. Hazel stuck her bent sword in the soil as a fence. Tom fed the ants banana crumbs.
