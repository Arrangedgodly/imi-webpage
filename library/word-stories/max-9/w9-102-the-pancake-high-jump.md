# The pancake high jump

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports record*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Bo, Nola; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo trained for the high jump on a splendid banana branch. Nola cooked pancakes beneath him. Each time he jumped, she raised a pancake on a fork. Bo cleared the bar and landed beside breakfast. At the meet he stopped halfway because no pancake waited below. Nola waved the fork from the finish mat. He sailed over the bar and ate his prize. The judges measured the jump, then ordered Nola to lower the fork before the next event.
