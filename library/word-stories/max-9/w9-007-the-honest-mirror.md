# The honest mirror

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic mirror*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Rose, Kip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose asked the magic mirror who had the finest tail. It said Kip, who had spilled breakfast on his. Rose called this a terrible error. The mirror showed Kip using his sticky tail to lift a trapped beetle. Rose wiped her own tail, then used it to pull a small monkey up a steep branch. That night the mirror named Rose. Kip asked who had the cleanest floor. The mirror went dark.
