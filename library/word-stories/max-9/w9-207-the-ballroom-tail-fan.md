# The ballroom tail fan

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Society romance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Gail, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail carried a beautiful fan to the banana ball. Rex read its motions as secret love signs. When she opened it, he bowed. When she closed it, he fled. Gail used her tail to catch his sleeve. She was hot, not proposing marriage. Rex confessed he had studied a splendid guide to fan speech. Gail lent him the fan to cool down. They danced after he stopped reading it. The guide spent the evening propping open a window that actually needed air.
