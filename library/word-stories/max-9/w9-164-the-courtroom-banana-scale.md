# The courtroom banana scale

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Court justice*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Orla, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla weighed court evidence on a balance. Rex put a banana on one side and his contract on the other. The fruit pulled the scale down. He claimed this proved the debt too heavy. The courtroom judge asked him to eat the banana. Rex peeled it with his tail and took a bite. The scale balanced. Orla wrote that he had paid in fruit. Rex kept the peel as a receipt, which the clerk required him to dry before filing.
