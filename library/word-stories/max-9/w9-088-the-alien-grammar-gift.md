# The alien grammar gift

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Alien translator*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Dara, Remy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara gave an alien a splendid speech box. Remy tested it by asking for a banana. The box replied that he was a banana. The alien carried him toward its spaceship pantry. Dara grabbed his tail and changed the box dial. Now it said he owned a banana. The alien bowed and offered a fruit basket as tribute. Remy left the dial there until Dara asked for her share. He learned the word ours before they ate the basket.
