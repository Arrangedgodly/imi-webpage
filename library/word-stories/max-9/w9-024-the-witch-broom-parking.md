# The witch broom parking

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Witch flight*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Zia, Rufus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia landed her broom in the royal banana tree. Rufus found three more brooms jammed beside it. The witches had come for a splendid feast but could not fly home. Zia carefully looped her tail round a branch and climbed to the top. She tied each broom to a leaf tag, then called its owner by name. Rufus held the ladder. Last came the queen, who had parked her broom upside down and lost her hat in the roots.
