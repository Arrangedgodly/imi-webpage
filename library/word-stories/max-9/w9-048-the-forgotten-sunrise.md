# The forgotten sunrise

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Stolen sun*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Esme, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Esme had forgotten where she hid the sun after her magic show. Cal searched the cupboard with a candle. The banana grove had gone dark. Esme shook her splendid hat, but only socks fell out. Cal felt warmth under the table. He lifted the cloth with his tail and found the sun inside a melon bowl. Esme set it free through the window. The whole village woke late, except the baker, who had baked by the bowl.
