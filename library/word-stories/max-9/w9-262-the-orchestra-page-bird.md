# The orchestra page bird

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Concert page turner*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Lark, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark hired a bird to turn pages for the orchestra. Gus sat in the audience with banana chips. The bird flew toward his snack halfway through the score. Lark turned the page with her tail while playing one note with her hand. Gus shared a chip and sent the bird back. Lark gave it a seed after each correct page. The concert ended in tune. Gus kept the chip bag closed, and the bird got a written menu clipped beside the music.
