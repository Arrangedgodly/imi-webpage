# The ogre shoe shop

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ogre bargain*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Sol, Bea; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sol opened a shoe shop in a hollow tree. Bea sold laces woven from strong vines. An enormous ogre asked for shoes by breakfast, but Sol had only monkey sizes. Bea looped her tail through a lace and measured the feet. Sol cut two banana boats into soles. The ogre stepped into the stream and floated off smiling. Bea put a sign on the tree offering shoes for wet days. Sol charged extra for oars.
