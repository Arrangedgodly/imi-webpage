# The royal river fee

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Small kingdom*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Cato, Joy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cato declared himself king of a puddle beneath the banana tree. Joy built an enormous dock from twigs. A terrible drought shrank the realm before breakfast. Cato ordered rain, but the sky ignored him. Joy carried a bowl of water in her tail and poured it into the puddle. Cato named her royal river. She charged him one banana per bowl. By sunset he gave her the crown and went to fetch water from the pond.
