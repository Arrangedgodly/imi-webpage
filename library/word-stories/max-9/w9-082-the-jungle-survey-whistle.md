# The jungle survey whistle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Explorer survey*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Otis, Fia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Otis marked a jungle path with a splendid whistle call. Fia wrote each call on a banana leaf. A bird copied the notes from a far tree. Otis followed it and walked in a circle. Fia caught his tail before he crossed their own tracks again. She whistled a new tune. The bird copied that too. Their discovery became a bird lesson. Fia tied leaves to the path, and Otis saved his whistle for the march back home.
