# The filmmaker fruit credits

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film credits dispute*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Uma, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma listed every helper in the film credits. Poe, the filmmaker, wanted to credit each banana eaten on set too. The names filled an enormous paper roll. Uma held it with her tail while the audience waited for the lights. Poe insisted the fruit had powered the acting. Uma wrote one line thanking the banana growers and rolled up the rest. The audience clapped and left before midnight. Poe kept the unused roll to write the next script, which had enough pages without naming his lunch.
