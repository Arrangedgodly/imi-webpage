# The waterfall crown

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost royal crown*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Uma, Zed; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma found a crown behind a waterfall. Zed put it on and demanded a treasure. Water poured from the crown into his eyes. Uma read the marks inside. It belonged to the king of rain. She tied it to her tail and swung into the banana grove, where the trees had wilted. The crown watered each root. Zed became royal bucket keeper. He still wore it on feast days, with a leaf tucked over his nose.
