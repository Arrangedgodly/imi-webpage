# The campaign root tour

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Campaign optics*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Orla, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla toured the banana farms for her campaign. Gus carried the official camera. Orla posed with clean boots beside an enormous root. A farmer asked her to lift a water bucket. She wrapped her tail round its handle and slipped in mud. Gus lowered the camera. Orla told him to take the picture anyway. She finished watering the row and washed her boots after. The committee photo won votes because the bucket had reached the trees, even if the mayor had sat in mud.
