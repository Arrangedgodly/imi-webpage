# The boardroom banana bell

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Management*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Wren, Kip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren rang a boardroom bell whenever someone had a useful idea. Kip proposed longer meetings to hear more ideas. Wren rang the bell. No one could hear the next speaker. Kip wrapped his tail round its clapper and waited. The business council stared in silence until the banana cart arrived. Everyone spoke at once about lunch. Wren removed the bell and wrote each idea on paper. Kip got one approved meeting rule. Lunch came before the talk, and the bell called only the cart.
