# The secretary double lunch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Scheduling satire*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Zia, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia booked two clients for lunch with Gus at the banana firm. Her secretary chart showed two empty hours, but they were the same hour on different pages. Gus used his tail to hold both soup bowls and asked the clients to share a table. They found they sold the same crates and argued over the business contract. Zia brought one common order sheet. Gus bought half from each. She fixed the chart, then booked herself a single quiet lunch with no crates.
