# The lost tower lift

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Rescue princess*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Fia, Reed; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fia waited atop a tower with a basket of bananas. Reed called for her to lower her tail. She had spent yesterday tying it into a splendid braid, so she lowered the basket instead. Reed climbed inside. Halfway up, he sneezed and shook the rope. Fia lowered him again and sent down a note asking for stairs. Reed built them from tree limbs. They ate on the first step. Fia untied her braid so the birds could take its loose ribbons.
