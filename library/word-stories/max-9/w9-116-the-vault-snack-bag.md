# The vault snack bag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Vault event*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Polly, Remy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Polly carried a splendid vault pole to the meet. Remy used it to hang the team bananas. When Polly ran, her tail caught the snack bag. She rose with fruit swinging below and landed past the mat. A dangerous landing, said the judge. Remy moved the mat farther out. Polly tried again without the bag and fell short. They lowered the bar and counted her safe jump. Remy served bananas only after every pole stood in the rack.
