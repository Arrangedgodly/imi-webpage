# The inventory tail stamp

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Warehouse audit*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Gil, Rue; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gil counted inventory in the banana store. Rue stamped each crate for the business audit. Gil dipped his tail in ink to work faster. He swung past five crates and marked them twice. Rue found twice as much fruit on paper as on the shelves. Gil proposed selling the paper surplus. Rue handed him a cloth and made him erase each spare mark. He counted again with his hands. The audit passed, and the cloth became the only stock they could honestly add.
