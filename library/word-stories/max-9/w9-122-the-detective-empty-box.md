# The detective empty box

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Missing gift mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Zia, Pip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia wrapped an enormous box for Pip. At breakfast it stood empty under the tree. Pip hired a detective to find the gift. Zia saw a trail of ribbon up a branch. Her gift was a hammock, and a bird had used its loose strings to haul it into a nest. Pip climbed with his tail round the branch and lowered it. The bird kept the wrapping. Pip napped in his hammock while the detective billed him for one tree climb.
