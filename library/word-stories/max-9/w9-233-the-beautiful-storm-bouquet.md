# The beautiful storm bouquet

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic grand gesture*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Jade, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade brought Bo a beautiful bouquet through a terrible storm. Rain stripped every flower, leaving bare stems. She tucked the stems in her tail and climbed to his banana porch. Bo had built a vase large enough for the planned gift. Jade spoke with one word, sorry. Bo filled the vase with warm tea and put the stems in a pot. They drank while planting them. By spring the porch bloomed. Jade brought a small cup on the next wet visit instead.
