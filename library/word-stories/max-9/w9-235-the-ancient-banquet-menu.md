# The ancient banquet menu

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical feast*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Lark, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark restored an ancient banquet for the enormous royal birthday. Sid read the menu in a new tongue. It called for banana leaves, which Lark cooked into a bitter stew. A messenger found the next line, which said use them as plates. Lark poured the stew into a garden pot and served fresh fruit on new leaves. Sid edited the menu while holding it steady with his tail. The king praised the old recipe. Lark showed him the full page before anyone asked for more stew.
