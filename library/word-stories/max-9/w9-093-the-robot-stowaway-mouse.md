# The robot stowaway mouse

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Robot security*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Nia, Wes; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nia built a robot to guard their spaceship. Wes found a mouse eating the banana stores. The robot refused to catch it because it wore a splendid crew badge made from foil. Nia took the badge with her tail. The robot chased her instead. Wes made a new badge for Nia and marked the mouse as a guest. The robot brought it one crumb each day. Nia locked the stores and hired the mouse to test the door gaps.
