# The broadcast silent guest

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Talk show*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Hope, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope invited Nia to the broadcast banana show. The director told Nia to answer briefly. Hope asked about her enormous garden. Nia nodded. Hope asked about her harvest. Nia nodded again. Hope wrapped her tail round the cue card and read the card text aloud. Nia thought briefly meant no words. She finally described the garden, and viewers learned how to grow fruit. Hope changed the card to short answers welcome. Nia returned next week with a banana and a story, both small enough for the show.
