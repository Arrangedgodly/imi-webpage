# The cave lantern refund

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cave adventure*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Cobb, Una; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cobb bought a splendid lantern for his cave trip. Una bought bananas. In the cave the lantern went dark. Cobb shook it, and a firefly crawled out demanding breakfast. Una gave it fruit, but it flew away. She traced the wall with her tail until they reached daylight. Cobb took the lantern back to the shop. The seller offered fresh fireflies. Una asked for a candle. Cobb got a refund, which the firefly tried to eat through his pocket.
