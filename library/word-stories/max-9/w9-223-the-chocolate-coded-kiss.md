# The chocolate coded kiss

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy romance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Zia, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia sent Bo a chocolate kiss with a secret message inside. He ate the message with the sweet. The dangerous meeting place vanished from memory. Zia waited at the banana dock with her tail round an enormous rope. Bo visited every sweet shop until he found the wrapper printer. The printer recalled the message and sent him to the dock. He arrived late with a fresh box. Zia made him read every wrapper before eating. Their next message came on a plain card too large to fit in his mouth.
