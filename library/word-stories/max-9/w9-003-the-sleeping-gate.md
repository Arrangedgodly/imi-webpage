# The sleeping gate

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sleeping kingdom*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Tuck, Wren; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tuck found the castle asleep, including its gate. Wren shook the bars, but they only snored. Their breakfast was inside. Tuck made a generous offer of one banana if the gate would wake. It dreamed of fruit and opened its mouth. Wren slipped through with her tail round a bar and fetched the baskets. They left a banana in the hinge. The gate woke and thought lunch had grown in its teeth.
