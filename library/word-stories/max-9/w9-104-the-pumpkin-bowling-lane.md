# The pumpkin bowling lane

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports equipment*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Dax, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dax made a bowling lane from smooth banana trunks. Meg brought an enormous pumpkin as her ball. It rolled once and split in half. Dax counted no points. Meg put each half on her feet and slid down the lane, tail held high. The pins fell. The referee called this a dangerous new sport. Dax rebuilt the lane as a slide and charged a peel per ride. Meg kept the seeds to grow fresh foot cups for next season.
