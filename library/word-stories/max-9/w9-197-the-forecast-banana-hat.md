# The forecast banana hat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Financial guru*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Bea, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea sold a forecast based on the angle of her banana hat. Rex bought it for his business council. The hat drooped, so she predicted falling sales. Rex stopped selling fruit and bought low shelves. Bea raised the hat with her tail and predicted growth. Rex raised the shelves again. A secretary asked for actual orders. Rex had sold none while moving shelves. He returned the forecast, put fruit at eye level, and sold Bea one fresh banana to hold up her hat.
