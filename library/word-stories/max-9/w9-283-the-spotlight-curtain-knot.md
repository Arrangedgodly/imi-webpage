# The spotlight curtain knot

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Stage mechanics*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Jade, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade opened the curtain for the spotlight scene. Sid had tied its rope to an enormous banana basket to keep it taut. Jade pulled with her tail, lifting fruit above the audience. One banana fell into a lap. The director paused the show while Sid lowered the basket. Jade replaced it with a plain weight. The scene began again. The lucky viewer kept the fruit, but Sid moved the rest to the lobby and sold them only after the curtain closed.
