# The animation tree roots

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Animated scenery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Rose, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose drew a banana tree for an animation. Hal moved its leaves in each frame. The roots wandered too, making the tree chase the actors. Rose asked the director to stop the drift. Hal used his tail to pin the root guide beneath the paper. The tree stood still, but its moving leaves still looked hungry. Rose wrote a scene where it caught a falling monkey. Hal kept the playful leaves. The finished film gave the tree one useful rescue and a firm place to stand.
