# The contract rain signature

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal formality*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Uma, Kip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma signed a contract on a banana leaf during rain. Kip watched the ink wash away. The ownership dispute returned at once. Uma claimed the tree, and Kip claimed the ground. They stood beneath it with their tails linked to stop another wet sheet escaping. A gardener offered to plant a new tree on the ground owned by Kip. Uma kept the old one. They signed on dry bark indoors. The gardener took the leaf home, where he added it to the compost beneath his roses.
