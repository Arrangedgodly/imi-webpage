# The historical banana mint

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Royal treasure*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Orla, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla minted coins shaped like bananas for the king. Bo counted the enormous royal treasure. A messenger reported that hungry citizens had bitten every coin. Orla used her tail to hold one beside a real banana and saw the problem. She punched a hole through each new coin so it looked less like fruit. Bo strung them on vines. Citizens stopped biting them and bought food instead. The king said his coins looked cheap. Orla showed him the tooth marks on the old gold.
