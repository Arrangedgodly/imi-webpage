# The moon passport peel

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space border*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Alba, Finn; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Alba reached the moon border without her passport. Finn had used it to wrap bananas. The guard demanded a splendid seal before their spaceship could land. Alba curled her tail round the paper and pressed the fruit flat. Its brown marks looked like a face. The guard compared them with Finn and waved him through. Alba had to wait. Finn sent back a fresh passport with a banana stamp. The guard smiled and admitted her for the fruit festival.
