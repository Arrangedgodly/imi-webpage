# The committee final basket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Organizational closure*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Esme, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Esme chaired the committee for banana waste. Tom brought an enormous empty basket as a proposal. They discussed its shape until ripe fruit spoiled outside. Esme carried the basket with her tail into the grove and filled it. Tom opened the gate for hungry neighbors. Every banana went. The committee asked for minutes proving the plan. Esme set the empty basket on the table. Tom wrote one sentence stating that the fruit had been eaten. They closed the meeting before another bunch fell.
