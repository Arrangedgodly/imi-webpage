# The gnome hat map

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Hidden kingdom*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Lulu, Moss; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lulu found a tiny map sewn inside a splendid hat. Moss said it led to a gnome kingdom. They followed it through roots, over a log, and under the banana stall. Lulu carefully lifted a board with her tail. Beneath it sat a gnome counting crumbs. The map was his hat lining, and he wanted it back. Lulu traded it for a tour. The kingdom had one chair. Moss had to reign while standing outside.
