# The enchanted door bell

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Quest gate*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Eli, Mo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Eli reached a splendid door in the mountain. Mo rang its bell with his tail. A voice asked them to state their quest. Eli wanted the golden banana. Mo wanted breakfast. The door opened for Mo and shut in front of Eli. Mo returned with a banana on a plate. Eli called this cheating. The door said it had run a cafe for years and wished heroes would read the menu. Eli ordered toast and got a seat.
