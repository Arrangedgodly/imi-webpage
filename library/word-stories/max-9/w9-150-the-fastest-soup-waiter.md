# The fastest soup waiter

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Speed contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Elm, Mina; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Elm entered a speed contest with an enormous tray of banana soup. Mina warned that the judges wanted running, not serving. Elm balanced the tray on his tail and sped along the track. Each judge took a bowl as he passed. By the finish his tray was empty, but the clock showed a record. The committee argued over whether soup helped him run. Mina offered a second trial. Elm brought empty bowls and won again, though the judges liked the first race better.
