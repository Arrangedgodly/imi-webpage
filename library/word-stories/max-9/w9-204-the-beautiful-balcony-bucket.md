# The beautiful balcony bucket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Balcony romance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Dex, Rose; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dex sang beneath Rose and her beautiful balcony. Rose lowered a bucket for his love note. He put a banana inside because his hands shook too much to write. Rose peeled it and sent the bucket back with seeds. Dex planted them beside the splendid wall. Weeks later vines reached the balcony. He climbed with his tail round the stems. Rose met him halfway, carrying a watering can. They wrote their first note together on a leaf asking the gardener for a wider pot.
