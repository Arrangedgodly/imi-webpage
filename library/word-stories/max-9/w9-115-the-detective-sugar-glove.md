# The detective sugar glove

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Food theft mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Otis, Aya; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Otis found sugar on a splendid glove where the missing banana tart had stood. Aya offered to help the detective. Otis sniffed each monkey hand, finding no sugar. Aya used her tail to lift the glove. It had stretched wide, with a heel mark inside. Someone had worn it on a foot. They followed sweet tracks to the cook, who had stepped on sugar while carrying the tart to cool outside. Otis returned the glove. Aya washed the porch and got the first cooled slice.
