# The sleeping star hammock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Celebrity culture*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Hope, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope booked a superstar for the banana festival. Hal built an enormous hammock backstage. The audience waited while the star slept. Hope rocked the hammock with her tail to wake him, but the gentle motion deepened his nap. Hal carried it onto the stage. The audience watched the sleeping star in silence, then clapped when he snored. Hope sold tickets as a quiet rest show. Hal lowered the hammock after one hour. The star woke, took a banana, and thanked them for a good set.
