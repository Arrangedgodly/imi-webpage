# The warranty rain desk

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Consumer law*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Hope, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope bought a desk with a lifetime warranty. Len placed it in the banana grove for meetings. Rain dissolved its glue, and the drawers fell onto his tail. Hope took the contract to the seller. The seller said lifetime meant the life of the desk, which had ended. Hope set the loose drawers on his counter and asked him to certify the death. He refused to sign. She returned the next day with a working desk rebuilt from the drawers and claimed the warranty lived too.
