# The leapfrog finish spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Playground contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Pia, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia played leapfrog across an enormous field. Sid placed a splendid spoon at the finish to mark the breakfast prize. Pia jumped over him with her tail curled high. He jumped over her and landed on the spoon. It flipped banana mash onto his nose. Pia stopped to laugh, so Sid crossed first. He demanded the prize. The judge pointed at his nose. Sid ate it, then shared the bowl with Pia and planted a flag at the next finish.
