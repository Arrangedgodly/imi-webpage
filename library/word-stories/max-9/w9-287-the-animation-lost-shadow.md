# The animation lost shadow

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Animation continuity*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Nan, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan made an animation in which a monkey climbed a banana tree. Bo drew the shadow beside the enormous trunk. Halfway up, the shadow climbed into a different tree. Nan asked the director to choose one source of light. Bo pinned a lamp guide beneath the paper with his tail. The shadow followed the monkey correctly. Nan kept the stray version for a dream scene. The audience saw both, but the dream began with closed eyes and ended before the real climb resumed.
