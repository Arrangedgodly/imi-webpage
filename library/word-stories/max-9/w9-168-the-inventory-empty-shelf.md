# The inventory empty shelf

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Retail metrics*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Tess, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess praised the banana shop for selling all its inventory. Poe pointed to the empty shelf. Their earnings report showed no cash because a loose hinge had tipped every bunch into the street. Tess saw customers gathering fruit below. She climbed down with her tail round the rail and set up a stall beside the pile. Poe fixed the hinge. Tess sold the last banana and filed a corrected report. The shelf stayed empty, but this time the cash box did not.
