# The secretary lunch gate

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office gatekeeper*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Koa, Vera; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa hired Vera as secretary for his banana firm. Her official task was keeping visitors from his lunch. Vera tied her tail across the door and asked each guest to book a time. Sales fell because no clients got through. Koa demanded an efficient plan. Vera booked him a lunch hour and served bananas only during that hour. Clients returned. Koa tried to eat during a meeting, but Vera booked the banana for noon and sent it back to wait.
