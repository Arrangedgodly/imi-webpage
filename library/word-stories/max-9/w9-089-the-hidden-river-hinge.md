# The hidden river hinge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost river*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Jade, Elm; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade traced a lost river beneath the mountain. Elm carried bananas and a splendid spade. They found a stone door that hummed with water. Jade pushed it with her tail, and the river rushed out through a narrow gap. Elm could not shut it. A crocodile floated past with the missing hinge pin in its mouth. Jade traded a banana for the pin. They fixed the door, opened it slowly, and gave the village a river with a tap.
