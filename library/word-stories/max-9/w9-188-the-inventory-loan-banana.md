# The inventory loan banana

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Accounting satire*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Pia, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia loaned Len a banana to improve his inventory count. Len loaned it back to improve hers. Their business reports showed two full shelves while one fruit traveled between them. An auditor watched Pia swing it across the lane with her tail. He asked who could eat it. Neither wanted to ruin the count. The auditor split it in half and recorded one meal shared. Pia and Len closed the false shelves. Their next reports listed one empty plate and no debt.
