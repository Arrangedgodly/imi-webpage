# The rehearsal missing king

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Costume fitting*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Zia, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia waited for the king actor at rehearsal. Gus stood in the costumes room with an enormous crown stuck over his eyes. Zia pulled it free with her tail. He could see, but the crown no longer fit. The director gave him a paper crown and let the heavy one sit on the throne. The audience accepted both symbols. Gus finished his lines without a stumble. Zia measured every hat after the show and wrote actor size beside each role, above the banana lunch list.
