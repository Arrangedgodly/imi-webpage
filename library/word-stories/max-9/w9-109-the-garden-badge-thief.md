# The garden badge thief

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Child detective club*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Ira, Flo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira wore a detective badge in the banana grove. Flo lost her splendid garden prize and asked him to find it. Ira quizzed every monkey except himself. Flo noticed his badge had green paint under its gold foil. He had borrowed her prize for his costume. Ira peeled the foil off with his tail and gave it back. Flo charged him one hour of weeding. He solved his next case wearing a paper badge with his own name on it.
