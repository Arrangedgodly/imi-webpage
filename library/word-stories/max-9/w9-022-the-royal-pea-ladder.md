# The royal pea ladder

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal sensitivity test*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Ava, Finn; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ava stacked eight beds above a pea to test a royal guest. Finn climbed the tower with his tail round a post. At breakfast he said he had slept badly. Ava reached for the splendid crown, but Finn pointed to a loose bed leg. It rocked all night. She fixed it with the pea. Finn slept well the next night. Ava gave him the crown for finding the fault and kept the pea under the leg.
