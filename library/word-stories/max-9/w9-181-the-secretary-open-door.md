# The secretary open door

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office culture*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Ivy, Lou; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy announced an open door policy at the banana firm. Lou, her secretary, removed every office door. Wind scattered the contracts through the trees. Ivy climbed after them with her tail wrapped round a branch. A corporate visitor walked into the pantry and ate her lunch. Lou said access had improved. Ivy rehung the doors and set one hour for visits. Lou put that hour on a sign. The visitor returned at noon, carrying a banana to replace the one he ate.
