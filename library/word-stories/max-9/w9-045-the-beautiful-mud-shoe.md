# The beautiful mud shoe

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Beauty spell*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Lena, Otis; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lena bought a spell to make her shoes beautiful. Otis spilled mud on them before the royal ball. She rubbed the shoes with her tail, but they began to sing. The witch had enchanted the mud instead. Lena went to the ball barefoot, carrying the shoes in a basket. The splendid court band lost its tune. Her shoes sang the right notes. The prince asked for a dance, then hired her basket for the rest of the night.
