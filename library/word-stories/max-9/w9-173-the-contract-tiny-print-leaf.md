# The contract tiny print leaf

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal fine print*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Ari, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari signed a contract to buy an entire banana grove. Meg read the tiny print with a detective lens. It covered a grove painted on a leaf. Ari held the leaf in his tail and demanded real fruit. The seller offered a painted banana. Meg took the leaf to the art market. A buyer paid twice the grove price for its detail. Ari accepted, then bought one actual tree. Meg checked the roots before letting him reach for the pen.
