# The historical royal shoes

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Period drama*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Uma, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma sewed enormous shoes for the royal parade. Hal wore them to seem taller than the visiting prince. A messenger warned that the banana gate stood low. Hal ducked, caught a shoe on a root, and fell into the arms of Uma. She used her tail to untie the tall soles. The prince bowed to Hal at his real height. Uma sold the removed blocks as foot rests. Hal kept plain shoes for the next parade and hired Uma to measure gates before crowns.
