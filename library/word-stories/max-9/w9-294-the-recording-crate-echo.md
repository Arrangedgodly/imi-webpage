# The recording crate echo

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Audio effect*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Vera, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera wanted an echo in her recording. Cal built a tunnel from banana crates beside the studio. Vera sang into it while Cal held the far end with his tail. The echo sounded thin because the crates had enormous gaps. Cal lined them with dry leaves and tried again. The sound grew warm. The musician liked it better than a machine effect. Vera kept the tunnel for one song, then returned the crates before the grower needed them for a real harvest.
