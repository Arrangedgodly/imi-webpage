# The testimony parrot clerk

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Witness credibility*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Vera, Bram; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera gave testimony about a stolen banana. Bram, the court clerk, wrote it down while a parrot repeated every word. The judge asked for a second witness. The parrot spoke, and Bram recorded it as another vote. Vera used her tail to draw a cloth over its cage. It repeated nothing. Bram realized both accounts came from her. The official record kept one witness. The judge hired the parrot to announce lunch, then regretted it when lunch was called twelve times.
