# The insurance umbrella hole

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Claims satire*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Dara, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara claimed insurance for a torn umbrella at the banana stand. Len read her contract and said a hole let wind pass, reducing risk. Dara placed the umbrella above his desk. Rain fell through onto the policy pages. Len wrapped his tail round the papers and ran indoors. He approved the repair. Dara patched the cloth and offered him dry shelter. Len paid one banana for a seat. He rewrote the policy without the word hole and kept it away from the roof.
