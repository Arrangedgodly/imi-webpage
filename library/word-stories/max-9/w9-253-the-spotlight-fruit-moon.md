# The spotlight fruit moon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Theater illusion*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Cal, Ivy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cal hung a banana beneath the spotlight to make a moon on stage. Ivy rehearsed her speech to the audience beneath its glow. Heat softened the fruit until it slipped from the hook. Ivy caught it with her tail and ate one bite without breaking her line. Cal dimmed the light. The play now had an eclipse. Afterward the audience praised the effect. Cal used a wooden moon for the next show, while Ivy packed a real banana to eat only after her speech.
