# The star chart blanket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space navigation*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Etta, Pax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Etta stitched a star chart into an enormous blanket. Pax spread it over the spaceship controls to choose their route. He sneezed, moved three stars, and sent them toward the laundry moon. Etta pinned the cloth down with her tail. The laundry crew cleaned their dusty suits and pressed the chart flat. Pax found the right route at last. Etta charged him a banana for the trip. He paid two and asked her to stitch a compass on his sleeve.
