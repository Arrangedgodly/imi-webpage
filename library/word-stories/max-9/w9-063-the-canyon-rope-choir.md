# The canyon rope choir

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cliff expedition*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Nell, Remy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nell had to cross a canyon with a messenger bag. Remy tied a splendid rope between two banana trees. Nell walked onto it, tail high for balance, and heard a low note. Wind played the rope like a string. Remy tightened it until the tune rose. Nell sang along and crossed in time with the beats. They left the rope there. Each new traveler paid a banana to join the song. Remy bought ear plugs with the first fee.
