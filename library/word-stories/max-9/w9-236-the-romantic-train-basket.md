# The romantic train basket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Train station farewell*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Mina, Gil; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina ran beside a train with a beautiful banana basket for Gil. He reached out, but her tail caught the platform rail. The basket landed in an enormous mail cart. Gil jumped off before leaving and helped her free it. Mina thought she had ruined his grand journey. He had only bought a ticket to deliver her birthday cake from the next town. They opened both baskets on the bench. The train left, carrying one empty cake box the porter had mistaken for luggage.
