# The marble mayor race

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: School race election*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Koa, Mina; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa entered the marble race to choose the school mayor. Mina polished an enormous marble until it shone. Koa picked a small chipped one and nudged it with his tail. Mina rolled ahead but got stuck in a narrow groove. Koa went past and won. At breakfast he ordered wider grooves for everyone. Mina used her marble as a tool to smooth the track. Koa made her parks chief and promised to leave polishing out of the next race.
