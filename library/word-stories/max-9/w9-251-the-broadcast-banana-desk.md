# The broadcast banana desk

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: News satire*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Ada, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ada read the broadcast news from a banana crate. Rex, the director, ordered a taller desk to look serious. Ada stacked crates until only her ears showed. Rex raised the camera. Her tail knocked the top crate, spilling fruit into view. Viewers sent notes praising the honest farm report. Ada lowered the desk and reported actual banana prices. Rex kept one tall crate for weather maps, but he nailed it to the floor before letting anyone forecast a gust.
