# The composer borrowed hum

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Song ownership*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Pia, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia heard Tom humming beside the banana grove and wrote the tune for her orchestra. Tom, a gardener, heard his tune at the concert. Pia called it an original song. Tom hummed the last bar before the musicians reached it. The audience noticed. Pia lowered her tail baton and named him as composer. Tom said his grandma had taught it to him. Pia credited the family tune and kept her new score. Tom came to the next show with grandma and two seats they had earned.
