# The mayor stump seat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Political office*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Fern, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern ran for mayor on a promise of more seats. Hal counted votes beneath the banana tree. Fern won, then found the official chair was a stump. The committee had sold every other chair to pay for the election. Fern wrapped her tail round the stump and stood. She ordered the campaign signs cut into benches. Hal worried that the paint still praised her. Fern turned each sign face down. The town got seats, and no one had to sit on a promise.
