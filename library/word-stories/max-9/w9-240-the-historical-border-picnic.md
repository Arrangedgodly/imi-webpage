# The historical border picnic

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Peace treaty*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Rose, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose drew an ancient border between two banana kingdoms. Dex placed an enormous stone on the line. A messenger arrived with a treaty saying the stone must be shared. Neither kingdom could lift half of it. Rose wrapped her tail round a vine and tested its weight. Dex brought lunch while she thought. They spread a cloth over the stone and sat on opposite sides. The treaty became a picnic pact. The stone stayed whole, and both kings learned to pass the fruit bowl across it.
