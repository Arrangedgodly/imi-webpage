# The detective bridge squeak

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Noise mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Orla, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla heard a squeak each night from the splendid bridge. Poe hired a detective before the noise spoiled breakfast. Orla hung below the bridge by her tail and watched the joints. A loose bolt moved when the banana cart rolled across. Poe had blamed mice. They tightened the bolt and found a mouse family asleep beneath it. Orla left them a quiet leaf bed. Poe paid the detective for the wrench he had brought, rather than the mouse trap.
