# The frozen mast flag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Arctic voyage*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Elsie, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Elsie sailed north to see the midnight sun. Tom tied bananas to the mast so they would not freeze on deck. They froze on the mast instead, making an enormous yellow flag. Elsie carefully chipped one free with a spoon held in her tail. The flag cracked and dropped fruit into the soup pot. A passing ship hailed their strange banner. Tom sold it hot banana soup. Elsie watched the sun while counting the coins in her warm mittens.
