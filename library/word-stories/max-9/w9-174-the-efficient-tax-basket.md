# The efficient tax basket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Tax administration*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Bea, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea taxed every banana entering the village. Sid built an enormous basket to speed the count. Bea called it efficient and jumped inside to stamp each fruit with her tail. Traders began bringing empty peels. Sid counted them as bananas under the official rule. Bea opened the basket and found no tax meal. She changed the rule to edible fruit. Sid ate one to test it and had to pay. Bea kept him at the desk with his lunch sealed outside.
