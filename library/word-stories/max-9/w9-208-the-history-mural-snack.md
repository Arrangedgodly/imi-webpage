# The history mural snack

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical epic*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Hope, Lou; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope painted the founding battle on an enormous wall. Lou posed with a spear and banana shield. A messenger brought news that the battle had never happened. The founders had argued over fruit, then shared it. Hope lowered her brush. Lou used his tail to turn the shield into a tray. Hope painted the meal instead, with the spear holding a canopy. The council approved the mural. Lou asked for a fresh banana shield because standing through history had made him hungry.
