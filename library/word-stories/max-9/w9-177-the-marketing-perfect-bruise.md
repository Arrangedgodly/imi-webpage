# The marketing perfect bruise

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Product branding*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Esme, Gil; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Esme sold bruised bananas under a marketing plan called soft gold. Gil counted earnings from each crate. A customer asked for firm fruit. Esme pointed to the premium shelf, where the same bananas cost twice as much. Gil lifted a bunch with his tail and revealed the bruise beneath the label. The customer bought one at the old price. Esme peeled the rest and made mash. Gil sold it honestly as soft food. The labels became lids for the mash cups.
