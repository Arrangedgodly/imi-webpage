# The courtroom peel oath

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Court evidence*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Dex, Ivy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dex brought a banana peel to the courtroom as proof of a fall. Ivy asked him to swear the peel had caused it. He placed a hand on the contract and his tail on the peel. Down he went. Ivy objected that the court floor had caused this second fall. The judge sealed the peel in a jar and awarded Dex a mop. Ivy appealed for a safer oath table. The judge moved it beside the sink and made them both wash.
