# The library footprint pie

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Library mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Gil, Vera; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gil found muddy prints leading to a splendid library shelf. Vera said someone had stolen the cookbook. Gil dusted the marks with flour held in his tail. They formed a trail to the kitchen. The cook had taken the book to make a pineapple pie but dropped it behind the oven. Vera retrieved it with a hook. Gil ate pie while the cook cleaned the marks. He returned the book with one new page showing how to wash library floors.
