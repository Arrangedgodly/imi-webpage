# The ancient statue hat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical monument*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Cleo, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo built an enormous statue of an ancient king. Rex added a banana crown to show his wealth. A messenger warned that birds would eat it. Cleo climbed with her tail round the stone arm and replaced the crown with carved fruit. The birds nested inside the hollow hat instead. Rex wanted them removed before the unveiling. Cleo cut a small door in the back for cleaning. The town praised the king for giving birds a home, which was kinder than anything he had done in life.
