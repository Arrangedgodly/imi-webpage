# The missing race whistle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mystery at meet*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Cal, Ivy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cal lost the whistle for the banana race. Ivy searched each enormous basket. A sharp note came from a tree, then another from the table for the judges. Cal followed both and found nothing. Ivy curled her tail round a branch and spotted a bird with the whistle. It dropped the whistle into the soft cake at breakfast. Cal blew it, but only crumbs came out. Ivy clapped to start the race. The bird won the crumb cleanup without a whistle.
