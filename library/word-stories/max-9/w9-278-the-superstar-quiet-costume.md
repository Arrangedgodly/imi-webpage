# The superstar quiet costume

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Celebrity disguise*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Esme, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Esme hid a superstar in a gardener costume. Tom led him through the audience after the banana concert. The star bowed to everyone, giving himself away. Esme held his sleeve with her tail and handed him a watering can. He watered the flowers instead of posing. Fans asked the gardener for help with their plants. The star gave plain advice and reached the gate safely. Tom booked a garden tour for the next day, but Esme warned him to leave all stage bows at home.
