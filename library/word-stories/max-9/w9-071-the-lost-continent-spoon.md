# The lost continent spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost world*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Ada, Ben; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ada found an ancient continent on a spoon handle. Ben packed bananas for the splendid voyage. They sailed across a pond and found a tiny island shaped like the map. Ada planted a flag with her tail. The ground shook. A turtle rose beneath them and paddled toward shore. Ben called their discovery a moving continent. Ada gave the turtle a banana peel and took down the flag. Their next map came on a plate, which stayed still.
