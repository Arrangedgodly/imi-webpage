# The chalk race raincoat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Track contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Sia, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia drew lanes for the race with splendid blue chalk. Len brought an enormous raincoat to cover them overnight. At breakfast he lifted it by the sleeves. The chalk stuck to its lining. Sia had no time to redraw every lane. She laid the coat flat, opened its buttons, and made the runners follow its blue stripes. Len used his tail to hold one corner. The race ended beside the pocket, where Sia had hidden bananas for the winners.
