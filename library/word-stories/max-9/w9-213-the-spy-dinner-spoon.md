# The spy dinner spoon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy gadget*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Mina, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina brought a secret listening spoon to dinner with Rex. He discussed the stolen banana plans while stirring soup. The spoon sent each slurp to an enormous speaker outside. Guards rushed in, fearing a dangerous beast. Mina slipped the spoon into her tail and pointed at the soup pot. Rex admitted he had hidden the plans beneath it. The guards took the plans back. Mina ate with a plain spoon afterward, though the speaker still echoed one last noisy gulp.
