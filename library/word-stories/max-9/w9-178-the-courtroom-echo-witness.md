# The courtroom echo witness

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Court procedure*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Fia, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fia accused Rex of taking her bananas. The courtroom stood beneath an enormous echo arch. Each charge sounded twice, and the judge called this strong testimony. Rex shouted no, which echoed too. Fia wrapped her tail round a curtain rope and drew cloth across the arch. The echo stopped. Rex showed the fruit receipt from her own shop. Fia had lost track of his order. She withdrew the charge. The judge kept the curtain and billed the arch owner for making every trial twice as long.
