# The recording banana mute

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Recording technology*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Ari, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari recorded Bo playing a banana leaf flute. The recording stayed silent though the cheeks of the musician puffed red. Ari checked the wire with her tail and found the mute switch beneath his lunch basket. Bo moved it, but the leaf had dried and cracked. Ari handed him a fresh leaf from the grove. The tune returned. Bo played one short song before the next leaf dried. Ari saved that take and put lunch on a separate shelf, where no sandwich could silence the music.
