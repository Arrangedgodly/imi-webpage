# The orchestra wind fan

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Concert effects*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Wren, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren set a fan beside the orchestra to keep musicians cool. Cal arranged the audience under banana leaves. The fan blew sheet music into the trees. Wren caught one page with her tail and played its last bar over and over. Cal fetched the rest with a ladder. The audience hummed the repeating tune until the song returned. Wren turned the fan toward the empty wall. Cal clipped each page to its stand and charged no one for the extra verse the wind had written.
