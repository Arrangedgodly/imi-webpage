# The raft with brakes

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: River expedition*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Liam, Rue; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Liam built a raft to reach a waterfall. Rue warned that the map showed a terrible drop. Liam tied banana crates beneath the raft and called them brakes. At the edge Rue hooked her tail round a branch. The raft swung into the bank and the crates broke. Bananas drifted over the fall. Liam climbed down to fetch them and found a quiet pool. Rue built a path to it. Their next raft trip began at the bottom.
