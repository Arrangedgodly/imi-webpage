# The broadcast guest tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Interview spectacle*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Sia, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia spoke with Bo on the broadcast banana hour. The director asked for no movement in the shot. Bo wrapped his tail round the chair leg and answered calmly. Sia tried to shift the chair closer to the camera. Bo moved with it and knocked the enormous fruit bowl over. The director stopped filming. Sia untied the tail and gave Bo a chair with arms. He held those instead. The interview resumed without spills, though Sia let the first question be why tails needed room too.
