# The moon dust bakery

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lunar science*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Ava, Gil; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ava tested moon dust inside the spaceship oven. Gil had hidden banana dough there for supper. The mixture rose into an enormous loaf that filled the cabin. Ava pushed it away with her tail while Gil opened the vent. The loaf squeezed out like a soft comet. They followed it down to the moon and cut slices. Ava wrote that moon dust helped bread rise. Gil wrote that the oven needed a lock and a much clearer meal schedule.
