# The alien tail socket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: First contact*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Mara, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mara met an alien beside her banana tree. Gus offered it a splendid meal. It pointed at their tails, then at an empty socket on its spaceship. Mara refused to plug herself in. Gus stuck a vine into the hole. Lights blinked, and the ship rose one inch. The alien gave them a fruit tree in thanks. Mara planted it far from the house. When it grew, each branch had a socket. Gus bought very long cords.
