# The dragon tooth fairy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy helper*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Lark, Dot; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark put a dragon tooth under her leaf pillow. Dot warned that a terrible fairy might come. Lark stayed awake with her breakfast banana. At dawn a tiny fairy tugged at the tooth, but it weighed more than her cart. Lark looped her tail round it and helped. The fairy offered a coin. Lark asked for a brush instead. Next day she cleaned the dragon teeth. The fairy hired a larger cart.
