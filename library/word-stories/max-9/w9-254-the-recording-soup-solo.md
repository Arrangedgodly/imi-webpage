# The recording soup solo

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Studio production*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Dex, Rose; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dex recorded Rose singing beside the banana kitchen. The recording picked up an enormous slurp from the cook. Dex tried to erase it, but it matched the beat. Rose used her tail to tap the rhythm on a pot and sang again. The audience bought the song as soup jazz. The cook asked for a credit. Dex printed his name on the record sleeve. Rose kept the pot in the studio, and the cook learned to slurp only when the red lamp glowed.
