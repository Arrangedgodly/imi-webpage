# The dungeon orchestra

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Captive musician*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Vera, Ben; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera heard an orchestra beneath the castle. Ben said the terrible king had locked up the players. They climbed down using their tails and found six guards banging spoons on banana bowls. A rat had stolen their keys, and the noise kept it away from their lunch. Vera played a soft tune on a reed. The rat came to listen. Ben took the keys from its nest. The guards hired the rat to turn their sheet music.
