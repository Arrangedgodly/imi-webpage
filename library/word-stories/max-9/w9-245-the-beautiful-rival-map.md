# The beautiful rival map

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic rivalry*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Wren, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren and Bo both loved a beautiful map maker. Wren brought an enormous pearl. Bo brought a banana and a compass. The map maker asked them to mark a new path through the grove. The pearl from Wren rolled down the hill, and her tail caught it beside a hidden spring. Bo used his compass to chart the spring. The map maker thanked both and married neither. Wren kept the pearl, Bo ate the banana, and the new path let them visit each other without getting lost.
