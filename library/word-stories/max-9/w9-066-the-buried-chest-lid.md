# The buried chest lid

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Treasure island*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Wren, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren dug for treasure under a palm. Rex had counted nine steps from a waterfall. Their spades struck wood. Wren hooked her tail into a crack and tugged, but the lid rose with their whole camp on it. The island was a giant chest. Rex wanted to open it. Wren pointed to the tents, banana trees, and sleeping crew. They shut the lid and dug a smaller hole. Rex found a coin and agreed it was quite enough.
