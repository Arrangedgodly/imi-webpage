# The composer rain bucket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Found sound music*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Fia, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fia wrote a song using rain on banana leaves. Poe, the musician, set buckets beneath each leaf to deepen the notes. An enormous storm filled them before the audience arrived. Fia tipped one with her tail and spilled water over the stage. Poe emptied the buckets into the garden and moved the show beneath a roof. They tapped dry leaves with small sticks. Poe made a recording before rain drowned their tapping. Fia wrote the garden watering into the setup sheet, far from the actual score.
