# The budget empty fountain

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Civic budget*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Wren, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren cut the water budget for the official fountain. Bo filled its bowl with bananas to honor the saving. Citizens ate the display, leaving an enormous empty bowl. A committee praised the cheap monument. Wren sat inside it with her tail curled round the drain and heard rain begin. The bowl filled. Bo sold the collected water to nearby gardens. Wren restored a small water budget and moved the banana stall beside the fountain. The monument finally served more than a speech.
