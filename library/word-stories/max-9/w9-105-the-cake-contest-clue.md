# The cake contest clue

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Baking mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Etta, Seth; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Etta entered a chocolate cake in the village contest. Seth tasted a splendid slice before judging began. Someone had swapped the cake for banana mud. Etta found tiny claw marks in the icing. She followed them with her tail sweeping dust away. A squirrel had dragged the real cake under the stage. Seth lifted the boards. Etta served the untouched half and won for flavor. The squirrel kept the mud cake, which made a fine wall around its nuts.
