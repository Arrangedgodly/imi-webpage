# The detective shell phone

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Private eye*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Ivy, Sol; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy installed a shell phone in her detective office. Sol called to report a stolen banana. She heard only waves. Sol had spoken into a shell by the pond, not a phone. Ivy followed his wet tracks and found him beside an enormous basket. His banana lay beneath the lid. She lifted it with her tail. Sol paid her fee in fruit. Ivy kept the shell on her desk because its quiet waves helped her think between real cases.
