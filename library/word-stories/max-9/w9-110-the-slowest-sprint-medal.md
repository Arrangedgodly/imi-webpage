# The slowest sprint medal

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Underdog sports*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Jem, Ria; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jem entered the sprint with an enormous lunch basket. Ria said it would slow him down. Jem tied his tail round its handle and ran anyway. Halfway through, the lead runner stopped with a cramped leg. Jem shared a banana, then helped him walk. Everyone passed them. Ria gave Jem the kindness medal at breakfast. Jem hung it on his basket. At the next race the whole team brought snacks, and no one wanted to leave him behind.
