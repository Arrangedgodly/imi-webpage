# The general pineapple cannon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Battle epic*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Bo, Vera; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo loaded a pineapple into the army cannon. Vera wanted a warning shot across the enormous enemy camp. Bo pulled the cord with his tail. The fruit landed in the enemy soup pot and splashed every officer. They waved white spoons and asked for the recipe. Vera crossed the field with salt and bananas. Both armies ate beneath one tent. Bo sold the cannon to a farm for launching seed. Vera kept the spoons as medals and retired before the next meal.
