# The captain salt chart

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sea navigation*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Arlo, May; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Arlo drew a chart in salt on the deck. May warned that a dangerous wave would erase it. He built an enormous fence round the chart and tied his tail to the mast. The sea stayed calm, but a crew member spilled soup. Salt ran into every port mark. May tasted the soup and found it good. She sold bowls to the crew while Arlo drew a new chart on cloth. He marked the soup stall as their safest harbor.
