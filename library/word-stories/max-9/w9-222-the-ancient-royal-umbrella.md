# The ancient royal umbrella

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Royal procession*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Wren, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren carried an ancient umbrella above the king. Gus guarded the enormous banana cart behind them. A messenger warned of rain, but the umbrella had holes shaped like royal stars. Wren used her tail to spread a plain leaf beneath it. The king stayed dry and praised the stars for good weather. Gus pointed to the dripping leaf. Wren lowered it so the king could see. He ordered plain umbrellas for everyone and kept the star one indoors above a dry chair.
