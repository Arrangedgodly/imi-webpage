# The swamp boot ferry

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Swamp expedition*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Pia, Seth; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia stepped into the swamp and lost a boot. Seth pulled her out by the tail. Their splendid map said dry land lay beyond the reeds. Pia found her boot floating toward a crocodile. She threw it a banana instead. Seth tied a vine to the boot and used it as a tiny ferry for their packs. They crossed on roots. The crocodile followed the fruit to shore, where Pia traded it the other boot for safe passage.
