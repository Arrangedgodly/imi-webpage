# The mermaid branch seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mermaid bargain*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Ria, Dax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ria heard a mermaid asking for a seat in the banana tree. Dax built a splendid swing above the pool. The mermaid offered a pearl but could not reach the swing. Ria carefully lowered it with her tail until it skimmed the water. Dax pushed from the bank. The pearl rolled into the mud. Ria left it there. Each day the mermaid brought fish for Dax and songs for Ria. Ria gave her bananas in return and lowered the swing for each meal.
