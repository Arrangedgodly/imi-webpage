# The cinematic tiny giant

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film scale trick*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Lark, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark filmed a cinematic giant among tiny banana houses. Hal, the director, knelt in the set with his tail behind the painted hills. The audience test laughed because his ears touched the cardboard sky. Lark moved the camera lower and cropped the ears. Hal looked enormous at last. He sneezed and flattened three houses. Lark filmed the sneeze as the final mistake of the giant. Hal rebuilt the houses after shooting and kept his nose behind the hills during every later close view.
