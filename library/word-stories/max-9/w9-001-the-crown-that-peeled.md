# The crown that peeled

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Chosen king*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Pip, Moss; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pip drew the gold crown from a stone and found a banana stuck beneath it. Moss bowed. The monkeys in the trees bowed too, until Pip bit the fruit. The crown slipped over his eyes. A terrible dragon had stolen their breakfast, Moss said. Pip could hear the dragon snore behind the stone. He fed it the peel, which made it sneeze up six baskets of fruit. Pip gave back the crown. A king should see his lunch.
