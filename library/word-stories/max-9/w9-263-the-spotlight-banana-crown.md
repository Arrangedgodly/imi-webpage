# The spotlight banana crown

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Stage costume*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Mina, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina wore a banana crown beneath the spotlight. Rex designed the costumes for a serious royal play. A monkey in the audience smelled ripe fruit and asked for a slice. Mina kept speaking, but Rex lowered a curtain with his tail. He replaced the crown with painted wood and served the fruit during the break. The audience returned calmer. Mina finished the speech without a snack request. Rex made the next royal feast from wooden bowls, though the actors ate real food afterward.
