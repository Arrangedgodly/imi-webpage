# The pirate anchor orchard

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate escape*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Lulu, Dax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lulu dropped an enormous anchor to hide from a dangerous pirate. Dax climbed the mast with his tail and saw the foe pass. When they tried to leave, the anchor would not rise. It had hooked a sunken banana tree from the harbor garden. Lulu cut the chain and tied a buoy to it. She sold maps to the sunken orchard. Dax led tours in a glass boat. The pirate came back, bought a ticket, and forgot the chase.
