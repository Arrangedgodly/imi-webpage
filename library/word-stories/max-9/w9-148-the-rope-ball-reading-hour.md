# The rope ball reading hour

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Playground rules*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Cleo, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo wanted rope ball beneath the splendid banana tree. Tom preferred a quiet reading seat. Their committee met on its branches. Cleo tied the ball rope to her tail and showed how little room it needed. Tom ducked as it swept his book away. The committee split the day into play hours and quiet hours. Cleo retrieved the book. Tom built a seat above the ball reach. Each took a banana break when the other hour began.
