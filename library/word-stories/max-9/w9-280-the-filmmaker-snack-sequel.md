# The filmmaker snack sequel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Sequel satire*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Gail, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail made a film about one stolen banana. Rex, the filmmaker, demanded a sequel with an enormous bunch. Gail wrote a chase, but every actor stopped to eat the props. Rex wanted more action. Gail filmed the meal and the argument over who should wash the bowls. The audience laughed at the new ending. Rex asked for a third film. Gail wrapped her tail round the empty basket and said the next story needed fruit first. He bought a bunch and hired a prop guard.
