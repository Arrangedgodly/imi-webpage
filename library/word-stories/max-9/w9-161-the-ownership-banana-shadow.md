# The ownership banana shadow

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Property law*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Lark, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark sold Sid ownership of a banana tree shadow. Sid held meetings beneath it and charged rent. At noon the shadow shrank, leaving his clients in the sun. Lark said the contract promised a shadow, not a fixed size. Sid used his tail to hang a cloth from the tree. The new shade covered all his seats. Lark demanded cloth rent. Sid pointed to the sky. He owned the shadow, and the cloth had only made it wider. Lark sat outside.
