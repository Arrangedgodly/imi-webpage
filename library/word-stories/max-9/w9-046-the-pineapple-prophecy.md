# The pineapple prophecy

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ancient prophecy*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Remy, Sia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Remy found a prophecy carved in a pineapple. Sia read that the king would fall when the fruit was ripe. They rushed to the splendid throne with their tails linked for speed. The king reached for the fruit and slid off his chair. Its leg had broken. Remy caught him while Sia fixed the chair with a stone. They ate the prophecy. The king ordered softer fruit for the next royal warning and a chair with four sound legs.
