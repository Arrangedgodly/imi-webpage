# The cinematic golden lunch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film color grading*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Wren, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren made the cinematic sunset gold by painting the banana set yellow. Sid, the director, painted the lunch bowls too. Actors refused to eat from wet paint. Wren caught a bowl with her tail and saw the mistake. She moved real lunch off set and used wooden food for the scene. The audience admired the golden feast. Sid ate plain bananas behind the camera. Wren kept separate signs for props and meals, so no new color plan could turn dinner into scenery again.
