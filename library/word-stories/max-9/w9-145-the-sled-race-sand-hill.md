# The sled race sand hill

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Winter sports*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Zia, Ned; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia waited for snow to test her splendid sled. Ned pointed to the sand hill beyond the banana grove. She tied her tail round the sled rail and slid down. Sand piled at its nose and stopped her halfway. Ned brought an enormous board to spread the weight. The next run reached the bottom. They held a race at breakfast. The winner got a banana, while Zia kept a jar of sand to prove her winter sport needed no winter.
