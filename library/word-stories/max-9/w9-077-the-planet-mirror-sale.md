# The planet mirror sale

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Planet exploration*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Nova, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nova found a planet that mirrored each move. Hal waved his tail, and a splendid monkey waved back. They offered the mirror crew bananas. The other crew offered theirs too. Nova stepped closer and hit glass. The planet was a showroom window inside a spaceship. Hal found a price tag on the frame. They bought one mirror for the ship gym. Nova logged the discovery as useful furniture. Hal trained his tail with a partner who never missed a turn.
