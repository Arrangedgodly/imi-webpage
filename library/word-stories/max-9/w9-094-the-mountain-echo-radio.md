# The mountain echo radio

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Mountain rescue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Tuck, Vera; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tuck called for help from a mountain ledge. Vera heard his voice through her splendid radio. Each reply came twice, so she thought two monkeys were trapped. Tuck threw a banana peel down to mark his spot. Vera climbed up with a rope held in her tail. She found one monkey and a deep echo cave. Their discovery drew singers from every valley. Tuck sold seats on the ledge. Vera kept the radio for real rescue calls and charged for echoes.
