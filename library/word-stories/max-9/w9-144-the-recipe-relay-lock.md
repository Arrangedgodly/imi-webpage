# The recipe relay lock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cooking contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Wren, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wren locked the secret recipe in an enormous tin before the cooking relay. Cal carried it to the next team with his tail round the handle. He lost the key under a banana root. Wren had to bake from memory. Her chocolate loaf rose crookedly, but it tasted rich. Cal found the key after judging ended. The tin held only a blank sheet. Wren had forgotten to write the recipe. Cal wrote it down while they ate the winning loaf.
