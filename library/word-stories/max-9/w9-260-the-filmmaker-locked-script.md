# The filmmaker locked script

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film secrecy*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Jade, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade locked the film script in a banana crate. Poe, the filmmaker, lost the key before shooting. The actors waited in costumes. Jade hooked her tail through a gap and pulled out one page, but it held only the final line. Poe filmed that first. The actors invented scenes leading toward it while Jade fetched the spare key. When the crate opened, their version worked better. Poe kept the old script as scrap paper and paid the actors for writing the middle.
