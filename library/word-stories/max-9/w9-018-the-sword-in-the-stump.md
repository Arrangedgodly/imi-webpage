# The sword in the stump

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Chosen hero*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Elm, Joss; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Elm tugged the sword in the stump with both hands. Joss tried her tail. Neither could free it. A messenger said whoever pulled it out would rule the forest. Elm noticed a splendid latch beneath the bark. He opened the stump like a box. The sword fell into his lap. Joss found the old king inside, eating bananas. He had hidden there to nap. Elm shut the latch and chose a quiet branch of his own.
