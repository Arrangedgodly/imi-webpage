# The domino courtroom

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Game dispute*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Rose, Evan; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose and Evan argued over a domino game at the splendid banana cafe. Rose said his tail had moved a tile. Evan claimed the table shook. They took the board to a courtroom made of crates. The judge tested the table and spilled its pineapple drink. One leg rested on a loose peel. Rose removed the peel. Evan replaced the tile. They finished the game while the judge charged them a drink fee and ate the peel as evidence.
