# The moat soup

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Castle siege*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Otto, June; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Otto guarded the castle moat with a splendid spear. June came to rescue the royal banana trees, but the moat smelled delicious. A witch had turned it into soup. June dipped her tail, yelped, and fetched a spoon. Otto refused to leave his post until June showed him that the post was now a carrot. They ate a path across. The king thanked them, then asked why his bridge had no gravy.
