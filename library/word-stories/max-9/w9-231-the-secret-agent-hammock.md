# The secret agent hammock

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy surveillance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Hope, Lou; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope watched a dangerous spy from an enormous banana branch. Lou sold hammocks below. Hope hid in one with her tail round the rope and waited for the secret exchange. Lou rocked the hammock to attract buyers. Hope fell asleep. The spy bought a hammock. His stolen packet fell into the display one as he lifted his purchase. Hope woke on the evidence. She returned the packet to court. Lou charged for the nap, and Hope ordered a chair for every future watch.
