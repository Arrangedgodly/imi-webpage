# The committee empty cup

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Budget satire*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Ira, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira sought funds for a banana stand. Meg chaired the committee and offered drinks. Every cup was empty because the budget had cut water. Ira held his cup in his tail and asked for rain instead. The committee approved a rain study, hired a weather clerk, and bought an enormous cloud chart. Meg looked at the cost. Ira opened the window during a shower. Their cups filled for free. Meg cancelled the study and funded a roof for the stand.
