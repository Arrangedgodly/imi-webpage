# The historical dinner trumpet

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Royal announcement*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Zia, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia sounded the royal trumpet for an enormous feast. Dex waited to carry banana soup into the hall. A messenger slipped him the wrong signal card. He entered during the war anthem. The generals stood and saluted the soup. Zia caught the card with her tail and stopped playing. Dex served them before telling them of the error. They agreed to discuss peace after lunch. Zia wrote a clearer card for dinner and kept every anthem in a separate box far from the kitchen.
