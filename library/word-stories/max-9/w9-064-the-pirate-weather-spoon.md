# The pirate weather spoon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sailing superstition*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Tess, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess stirred her tea with a spoon that foretold storms. Bo hung it beside the banana mast. It rattled all night, so they stayed in port. Each day it warned of dangerous seas, though the bay looked splendid. Tess caught Bo tapping it with his tail. He feared sailing because he could not swim. She taught him in the shallows. The spoon fell silent. On their first voyage it bent in a real gale and stirred soup very badly.
