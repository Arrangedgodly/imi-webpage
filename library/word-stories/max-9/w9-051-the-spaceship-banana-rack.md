# The spaceship banana rack

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space mission*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Orla, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla strapped bananas to the spaceship wall. Dex forgot to strap himself. When the engine stopped, he floated past the splendid rack and grabbed fruit with his tail. Each banana sent him spinning faster. Orla caught his belt with a long hook. She tied him beside the rack until the ship landed. The moon guard asked what cargo they carried. Dex said fresh fruit and one bruised pilot. Orla sold the guard a bunch and kept Dex tied down until the hatch opened.
