# The fishing contest hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Angling contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Dara, Finn; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara cast her line toward an enormous fish. Finn cast his toward her splendid hat. He caught it and reeled her off the bank. Dara grabbed a banana root with her tail and stayed dry. The fish took her bait while she freed the hook. Finn offered his pineapple lunch as an apology. Dara used its leaves to repair her hat. The judge counted no fish for either monkey, but gave Finn a warning for fishing above the water.
