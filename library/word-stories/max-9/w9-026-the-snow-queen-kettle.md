# The snow queen kettle

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Frozen kingdom*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Una, Beck; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Una found the banana grove trapped in ice. Beck brought a splendid sword to fight the snow queen. Una brought a kettle. She poured warm water over one stem, and the fruit came loose. The queen arrived to demand silence. Her teeth shook too much for speech. Una offered tea and breakfast. Beck used his sword to stir the pot. The queen thawed the grove in return for a kettle she could carry home.
