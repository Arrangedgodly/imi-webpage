# The beautiful duel cake

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic rivalry*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Pia, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia and Gus agreed to a duel for a beautiful poet. Each brought an enormous banana cake as a weapon because the poet loved sweets. Pia lifted hers with her tail. Gus dropped his before the first strike. The poet caught a slice and asked why they planned to waste dinner. Pia lowered her cake. They shared it beneath a tree while the poet read a new verse about foolish feasts. Gus baked a second cake as an apology and carried it with both hands.
