# The action alarm banana

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Heist alarm*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Koa, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa guarded an enormous banana vault with a dangerous alarm. Rex tossed a peel over the beam to test it. The bell rang, and Koa ran outside while Rex stayed beside the fruit. Koa realized her mistake and returned with her tail round the door handle. Rex offered the test receipt. Koa changed the alarm to lock the door before ringing. During the next test Rex got trapped inside with no fruit key. Koa let him out after he paid for the banana he ate.
