# The dangerous royal portrait

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical intrigue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Nan, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan painted an enormous royal portrait. Cal stood guard because a dangerous rival wanted to steal the image of the king. Nan saw a shadow slip through the banana window. She caught its sleeve with her tail. The visitor carried a tiny brush and claimed to be the king. Cal checked his face against the half done portrait. Nan had painted the nose wrong. The king fixed it himself, then paid her. Cal guarded the finished portrait and asked Nan to paint visitors only after checking their names.
