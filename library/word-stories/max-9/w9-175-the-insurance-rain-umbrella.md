# The insurance rain umbrella

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Risk management*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Cleo, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo bought insurance against rain at her banana stall. Tom sold umbrellas beside her. When clouds gathered, he covered her roof for free. The stall stayed dry, so her claim failed. Cleo accused him of stealing her payout. Tom offered an umbrella sales fee instead. She sold six before the storm ended. Her business earned more than the claim. Cleo renewed the policy for wind, then tied every umbrella handle to the counter with her tail while Tom held the roof.
