# The juggling contest soup

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Talent contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Bess, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bess juggled bananas at the splendid fair. Hal entered with an enormous soup pot. He tossed it once, caught it with his tail, and spilled soup over the stage. Bess slipped but caught every banana before landing. The judge called the act dangerous and stopped the show. Hal mopped the boards. Bess put her fruit in bowls and served it with his soup. Their booth earned enough to buy Hal three small juggling balls that held nothing wet.
