# The superstar stage fruit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Celebrity rider*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Orla, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla booked a superstar who demanded only green bananas. Gus bought an enormous crate, then the fruit ripened before the show. Orla painted one peel green, but the star spotted her stained tail. Gus offered the ripe fruit plainly. The star tasted one and liked it. The audience got the rest at the break. Orla erased green from the contract and wrote fresh instead. Gus planted the painted peel in compost far from the food table, where color no longer counted as flavor.
