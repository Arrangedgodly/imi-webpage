# The branch office root fee

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate expansion*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Cal, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cal opened a branch office high in the banana tree. Nia handled the contract with the root owner. Cal promised efficient service because clients could swing straight in. The first client climbed the trunk and asked for a chair. Cal offered a branch. Nia used her tail to hold the client steady through the meeting. The root owner charged rent for each handhold. Cal moved the office to the ground and kept the branch address on his letters for prestige.
