# The moon cheese crater

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Moon landing*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Nova, Finn; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nova landed on the moon and found an enormous wedge of cheese. Finn called the discovery home, then tried to plant a flag. The pole sank to its base. Nova looped her tail round it and pulled him out of a soft crater. They laid banana leaves over the hole to spread their weight. Finn took one cheese sample. Back home it melted in his pocket. Nova sold the stained flag as proof that space travel required better lunches.
