# The cave of lost hats

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cave explorer*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Evan, Sol; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Evan entered a cave to seek buried treasure. Sol carried a lantern and a banana lunch. The ceiling dripped on their hats, which stuck fast when they looked up. The discovery was a cave full of honey. Sol climbed his shoulders and used her tail to peel each hat free. They carried one sticky hat home. The village bees followed it. Evan sold honey by the spoon and hired Sol to keep the cave map away from bears.
