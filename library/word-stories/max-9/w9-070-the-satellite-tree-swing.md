# The satellite tree swing

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space station*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Lou, Dara; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lou put a satellite above the banana grove. Dara used an enormous vine to anchor its shell. Each orbit pulled the vine across the trees and swept fruit into their baskets. Lou called it a science mission. Dara used her tail to release one knot before the roots came loose. The satellite flew free, taking their basket with it. Lou sent a second craft to fetch lunch. Dara picked the next crop by hand and charged him for the launch.
