# The action hero cloak rack

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Secret identity*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Orla, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla hung her hero cloak in the banana cafe. Hal took it for a splendid table cover and set dinner on it. A dangerous call for help came from the grove. Orla pulled the cloak with her tail, spilling soup, then raced outside. Hal followed with a clean sheet. She rescued a monkey from a broken branch and used the sheet as a sling. Orla left her cloak at home next time. Hal kept the sheet near the door, where useful cloth mattered more than a secret costume.
