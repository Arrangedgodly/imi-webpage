# The ancient map rug

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical conquest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Fia, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fia marched an enormous army across an ancient map rug. Len moved the banana supply markers with his tail. The rug rolled up at one edge, trapping the enemy flag. Fia declared victory. Len unrolled it and showed the flag still there. The army had not left camp. A messenger brought a peace offer from the real enemy. Fia accepted before anyone had to walk. Len hung the rug on the wall, where no one could mistake a curled corner for conquered land.
