# The action cape turnstile

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Masked hero*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Dara, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara chased a dangerous robber in her splendid hero cape. Hal operated the market turnstile. The cape caught while Dara swung over the gate with her tail. The robber escaped, but his stolen banana basket caught the cape too. Hal turned the gate backward and freed both. Dara returned the basket to the vendor. She shortened the cape before the next chase. Hal kept its spare cloth as a flag to warn heroes where the gate stood, even when they insisted on leaping.
