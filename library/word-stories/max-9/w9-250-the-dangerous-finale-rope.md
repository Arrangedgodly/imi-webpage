# The dangerous finale rope

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action finale*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Dara, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara reached the dangerous end of her chase on an enormous rope bridge. Tom held the stolen banana chest on the far bank. She swung beneath the bridge by her tail and cut his escape rope. He could still walk home, so he shrugged. Dara realized the theft had no grand finale. She showed him the court order and opened the chest. Tom returned every bunch. Dara found a short path around the gorge and moved the bridge ropes to mark it. Tom carried the posts under her watch.
