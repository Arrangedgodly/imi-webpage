# The corporate ladder roots

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Career ladder*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Zia, Lou; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia climbed the corporate ladder in a banana tree. Lou had nailed it to the trunk for the official promotion race. At the top Zia found a sign reading chief of roots. She climbed down, tail sore, and asked where the office stood. Lou pointed beneath the soil. Zia dug a room beside the roots and sold cool drinks to passing workers. Lou demanded a share because he owned the ladder. Zia showed him the root sign and hired him to serve below the roots.
