# The pirate deck chessboard

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate duel*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Ira, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira dared a dangerous captain to play a game for his treasure. Meg painted the deck as a chess board. Waves made the pieces slide, so Ira held his king with his tail. The captain claimed this broke the rules. Meg tied every piece to the deck with fine vines. Neither player could move. Ira offered a draw and a banana meal. The captain accepted. Meg sold him the vine chess set as a perfect game for rough seas.
