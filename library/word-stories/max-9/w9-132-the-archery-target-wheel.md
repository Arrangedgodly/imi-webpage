# The archery target wheel

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Target sport*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Jade, Otto; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade shot arrows at a splendid target in the banana grove. Otto moved it on a wheel to save her a walk. A dangerous gust sent the wheel downhill. Jade dropped her bow and hooked her tail round its axle. Otto caught her hands. They hauled the target back to flat ground. The arrows pointed toward every tree they had passed. Jade used them to mark a walking course. Otto locked the wheel and waited for her final shot.
