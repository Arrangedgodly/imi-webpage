# The siege ladder supper

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: War epic*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Sia, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia led an enormous siege ladder toward the castle. Hal carried banana soup for the troops. A messenger shouted that the gates were open for supper. Sia refused to drop her ladder after carrying it all day. Hal used his tail to turn it sideways and set bowls on its rungs. They entered with a long serving tray. The castle guard thanked them for the feast. Sia accepted a peace ribbon and asked for a cart before moving the ladder home.
