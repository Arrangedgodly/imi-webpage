# The locked banana locker

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Locked room mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Kip, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kip locked his bananas in an enormous locker. Nia heard him shout when they vanished. A detective checked the latch and found no mark. Nia pushed her tail through the vent and touched a cold plate. Kip had locked the fruit in the school oven by mistake. The cook had moved it before baking bread. He returned a tray of warm bananas. Kip ate them, then painted his real locker blue so hunger would not guide him wrong again.
