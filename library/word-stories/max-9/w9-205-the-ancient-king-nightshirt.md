# The ancient king nightshirt

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical disguise*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Etta, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Etta smuggled Sid from the palace in an ancient king costume. His enormous crown caught the door, and his tail tangled in the cape. Guards hurried toward them. Etta stripped off the costume, leaving Sid in his sleep shirt. A messenger asked why the laundry boy had royal clothes. Etta gave him the bundle and said it needed washing. The guards carried it away. Sid escaped through the banana garden, where Etta charged him one crown coin for repairing her torn disguise bag.
