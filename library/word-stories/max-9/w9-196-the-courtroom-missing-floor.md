# The courtroom missing floor

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal technicality*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Ari, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari sued Nia for dropping bananas on his office floor. The courtroom heard testimony from the cleaner. Nia said the office had no floor because it stood on an open branch. Ari pointed to the ground far below. The judge asked whether his contract included the ground. Ari searched the page with his tail holding it steady. It covered one branch only. The judge dismissed the case and advised a basket. Ari bought one from Nia, who promised never to drop that.
