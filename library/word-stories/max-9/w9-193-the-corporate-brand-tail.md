# The corporate brand tail

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Brand management*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Vera, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera painted the tail of every worker gold for the corporate banana brand. Hal objected that paint made his tail slip from branches. The business office offered gloves, which fit hands but not tails. Vera watched him fall into a soft crate. She washed the paint off with her own tail holding the cloth. Hal climbed safely again. Vera put the gold on crate labels instead. The office claimed a new brand vision. Hal asked for a label on his glove box reading not needed.
