# The pirate apology flag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate truce*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Lark, Joss; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark raised a splendid black flag above her boat. Joss had washed it with a red shirt, so it turned pink. Their dangerous rivals laughed from the dock. Lark tried to lower it with her tail, but the rope snapped. Joss wrote sorry on the flag in banana paste. The rivals came aboard to ask what she meant. Lark said she was sorry for taking their soup pot last week. They accepted the pot back and stayed for soup beneath the pink flag.
