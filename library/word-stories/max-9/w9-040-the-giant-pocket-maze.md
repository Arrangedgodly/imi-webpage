# The giant pocket maze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Labyrinth quest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Koa, Elsie; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa entered an enormous maze to find Elsie. He tied a vine round his tail and left bananas at each turn. A hungry giant ate the fruit trail. Koa heard Elsie call from above. The maze lined a pocket worn by the giant, and she had climbed a seam. Koa carefully followed her voice. They escaped through a button hole. The giant found a vine in his pocket and hung his keys on it, ending the maze for good.
