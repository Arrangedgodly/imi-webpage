# The courtroom soft gavel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Judicial authority*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Koa, Rue; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa made a courtroom gavel from a banana. Rue warned that the official bench needed a firmer tool. Koa struck it once, splashing the witness book. The court laughed, and the witness forgot his lines. Rue wiped the book with her tail and asked him to speak plainly. He admitted the story was false. Koa kept the soft gavel for nervous guests but used a wooden one for rulings. Rue kept a cloth beneath both and no fruit near the ink.
