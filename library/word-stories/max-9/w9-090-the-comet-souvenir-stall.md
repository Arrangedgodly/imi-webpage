# The comet souvenir stall

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space tourism*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Mina, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina opened a souvenir stall on a comet. Cal built an enormous counter from banana crates. The comet circled the sun before any guests arrived. Mina tied her tail to the stall and waited through another orbit. Cal finally put a sign on the spaceship lane. Guests came, but each wanted a postcard sent home. Mina used the crates as mail bins. Cal sold the empty counter as a comet relic. Their new shop had stamps and no waiting room.
