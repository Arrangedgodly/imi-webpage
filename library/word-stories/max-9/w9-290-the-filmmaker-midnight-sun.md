# The filmmaker midnight sun

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film schedule*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Rose, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose scheduled a sunrise scene for midnight. Poe, the filmmaker, wanted to shoot before the banana market opened. The director lit an enormous lamp behind a painted hill. Rose used her tail to move the sunrise slowly, but a real owl crossed the frame. Poe called it proof of dawn. Rose added a rooster sound after filming. The audience believed the scene. Poe credited the owl as night watch, while Rose booked the next real sunrise early enough to get it before the fruit carts arrived.
