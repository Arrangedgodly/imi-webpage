# The insurance tree branch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Insurance dispute*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Jade, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade bought insurance for her banana tree against storms. Poe wrote the contract on a leaf. A wind tore the leaf away but left the tree intact. Jade claimed the policy had suffered storm damage. Poe denied it, saying the leaf was only paper. Jade climbed with her tail round a branch and fetched another leaf. She wrote a bill for his lost contract. Poe paid one banana to end the dispute and printed the next policy on cloth he could safely file indoors.
