# The volcano bath ticket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Volcano explorer*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Fern, Dax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern climbed an enormous volcano to study its smoke. Dax packed a towel and a pineapple. They reached a warm pool, where a guard demanded bath tickets. Fern showed her science pass. The guard charged extra for steam. Dax dipped his tail and found the water cold. The smoke came from a kettle behind a rock. Fern pulled its plug. The guard refunded their fee. They ate fruit on the crater rim and watched real clouds drift past.
