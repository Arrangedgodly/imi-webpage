# The action hero soap rope

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Escape scene*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Fern, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern hung above a dangerous pit on a rope. Tom stood on the splendid ledge with the stolen banana chest. Fern caught the rope with her tail and tried to climb, but it slid through her hands. Tom had washed it with soap to remove his prints. Fern swung toward a dry root instead. She climbed out and rubbed the soapy rope over the chest. It slipped from the hands of Tom. Fern caught the chest, returned the fruit, and sent Tom a bill for soap.
