# The ancient chariot brake

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Historical race*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Lark, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark drove an ancient chariot through the enormous arena. Dex carried bananas to feed the horses after the race. A dangerous bend sent the wheels sideways. Lark wrapped her tail round the rail and pulled a vine brake. The chariot stopped, but the horses kept going. Dex lured them back with fruit. Lark finished on foot with the chariot behind her. The crowd applauded the first safe stop it had seen. Dex sold brake vines outside the gate while the horses ate.
