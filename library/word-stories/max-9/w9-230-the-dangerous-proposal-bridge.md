# The dangerous proposal bridge

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Adventure romance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Gail, Kip; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail planned to propose on a dangerous rope bridge. Kip carried an enormous banana cake across it. The bridge swayed when Gail knelt. Kip caught her tail and asked her to stand. She offered the ring halfway upright. He accepted, then proposed moving the cake to shore. They crossed holding hands and ate beneath a tree. Gail kept the bridge ticket beside the ring box. Kip kept a firm bench for every later romantic speech, with enough space for the cake too.
