# The permanent acting chief

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office promotion*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Mina, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina became acting chief while Tom took a banana break. Her promotion lasted six months because he never returned from the grove. The official board wanted a permanent chief but could not dismiss a monkey on break. Mina carried the lunch bell up the tree in her tail. Tom came down to eat. The board thanked him for returning and ended his leave. Mina kept her job. Tom got a new role timing breaks, with his own bell far from the fruit.
