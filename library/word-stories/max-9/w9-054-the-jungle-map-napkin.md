# The jungle map napkin

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Lost city expedition*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Toma, Eden; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Toma found a map to an ancient treasure on a cafe napkin. Eden packed a pineapple and a coil of vine. They climbed trees for three days before the marks led them back to the cafe. Toma held the napkin against the window with his tail. The map was a stain from spilled soup. Eden peeled the fruit beside the cook. Beneath its rind lay a gold ring the cook had lost. Toma claimed the soup had been right.
