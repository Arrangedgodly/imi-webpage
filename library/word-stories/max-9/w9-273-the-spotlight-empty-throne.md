# The spotlight empty throne

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Stage blocking*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Zia, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zia aimed the spotlight at the royal throne. Sid, the director, ordered the actor to stand far away for a lonely scene. The audience could see only the empty chair. Sid whispered for more emotion. Zia used her tail to turn the lamp toward the actor instead. His face told the scene clearly. Sid moved the throne beside him for the final line. Zia marked both places with chalk, so the next lonely king would still have enough light to be seen.
