# The secret agent glue glove

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy gadget failure*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Ari, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari wore a dangerous glue glove to climb the enemy wall. Nia waited below with an enormous banana cart. The glove stuck to the wall, and Ari could not move. Nia looped her tail round his boot and passed up warm water. Ari washed the glue free but slipped toward the cart. Nia caught him in the soft fruit. They returned with no secrets and a lot of mash. Ari sold the glove recipe to a furniture shop, where sticking in place was useful.
