# The cinematic banana chase

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film spectacle*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Etta, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Etta planned a cinematic chase through the banana market. Gus, the director, wanted a real cart crash. Etta tied the cart to her tail and stopped it before the fruit stall. Gus said nothing exciting happened. She filmed the stop close up, with bananas falling toward the lens. The audience gasped at the safe scene. Gus approved the cut. Etta returned every banana to the seller and billed the film for the bruised one she had eaten between takes.
