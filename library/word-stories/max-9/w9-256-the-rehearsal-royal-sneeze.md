# The rehearsal royal sneeze

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Stage tragedy*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Fern, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern practiced a royal death in rehearsal. Sid arranged costumes beneath the banana stage. Fern lay still, but dust from the crown made her sneeze. The audience of helpers laughed. Sid cleaned the crown and wrapped its lining in a leaf. Fern died quietly on the next run. The director missed the sneeze and asked for it back. Fern changed the play to a royal nap. Sid wrote one sneeze into the script and kept the dusty crown locked away.
