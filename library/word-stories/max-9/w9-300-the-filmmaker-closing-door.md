# The filmmaker closing door

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film ending trope*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Dara, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara filmed the final scene at a banana cafe. Rex, the filmmaker, wanted the hero to close the door as the audience heard music. The tail of the hero held the handle too tightly, pulling the whole door from its enormous hinge. Dara stopped the camera. Rex laughed and wanted to keep the crash. Dara repaired the door first, then filmed a gentle close. She used the crash after the credits as a rehearsal outtake. The cafe reopened next morning, with its door working and no actor blocking breakfast.
