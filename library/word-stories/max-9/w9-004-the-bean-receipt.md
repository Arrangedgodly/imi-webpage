# The bean receipt

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic beans*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Bo, Fern; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo traded a banana cart for magic beans. Fern demanded a receipt. The seller had vanished, so they planted the beans beside his sign. A beanstalk rose into a cloud, taking the sign with it. Bo climbed with his tail round each stalk. A giant demanded rent. Fern showed the sign, which promised free lodging. The giant sighed. He gave them breakfast, then climbed down to change his sign.
