# The historical clock march

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Military history*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Cleo, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo ordered an enormous march at sunrise. Rex wound the ancient clock before serving bananas. It rang at midnight, waking every soldier. Cleo marched them across the yard, then saw the stars. She wrapped her tail round the bell cord to stop it. Rex found a mouse nest holding the hour hand back. They moved the nest to a box and let the troops sleep. At real sunrise the clock rang once. Cleo held the parade after breakfast and kept a window beside the dial.
