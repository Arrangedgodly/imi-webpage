# The science fair rain hat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Science contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Ned, Bea; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ned built a rain hat for the science fair. Bea brought an enormous watering can to test it. Water ran off the brim and soaked his tail. Ned claimed the invention worked because his ears stayed dry. Bea tested it beside the banana plants. The brim watered a full ring of roots. Ned changed his sign from rain hat to garden tool. He won second prize and a watering can. Bea bought the hat for dry ears on wet days.
