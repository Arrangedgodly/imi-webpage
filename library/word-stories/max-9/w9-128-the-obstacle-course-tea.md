# The obstacle course tea

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Obstacle race*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Fia, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fia built an enormous obstacle course through the banana grove. Cal carried tea to the judges at its far end. He climbed the wall, crossed the ropes, and used his tail to steady each cup. The judges gave him a splendid time score. Cal said he had not entered. Fia handed him a medal at breakfast. He wore it on his tea tray. When the racers asked his secret, he told them hot tea made falling a very poor choice.
