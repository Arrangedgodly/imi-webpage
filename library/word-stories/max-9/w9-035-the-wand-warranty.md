# The wand warranty

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic wand*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Gail, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail bought a wand that promised splendid spells. Poe used it to turn banana peels into shoes. The shoes walked away without them. Gail carefully read the warranty. It covered spells only while the wand was dry. Poe had dipped it in soup. Gail wrapped her tail round the shoes and pulled them back. They put the wand in the sun. Once dry, it turned the shoes into peels. Poe wore leaves home and kept the receipt.
