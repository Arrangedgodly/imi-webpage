# The river map fish

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: River science*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Jem, Ria; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jem tagged a fish to map a waterfall. Ria held the splendid net with her tail. The fish swam upstream instead and returned with a banana peel. Jem called it a failed study. Ria followed it to a hidden grove above the falls. They found ripe fruit hanging over the river. Jem revised his map and released the fish. Ria left one banana on the bank each week. The fish kept the peel and ignored every new tag.
