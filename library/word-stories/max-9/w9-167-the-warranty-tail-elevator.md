# The warranty tail elevator

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Office transport*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Sia, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia installed an elevator in her tree office. Hal signed the warranty before checking its size. It carried one banana at a time. Sia tried to ride with her tail outside, and the door refused to close. A secretary sent their lunch up fruit by fruit while Hal climbed the trunk. The supplier said the lift met all terms because the contract promised a small lift. Sia moved it to the pantry. Hal wrote his next order while standing beside a ruler.
