# The recording copy cat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Audio mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Koa, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa heard a second voice in her recording. Len, the musician, blamed an enormous echo in the banana studio. Koa moved blankets around the walls with her tail. The voice remained. A cat sat behind the mixer, meowing each time she sang a high note. Len gave it a bed in the lobby. Koa sang alone and missed the strange harmony. She recorded one short cat chorus on its own. Len credited the cat, which accepted its fee in fish rather than fruit.
