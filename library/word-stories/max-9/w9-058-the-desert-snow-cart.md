# The desert snow cart

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Desert explorer*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Dune, Cora; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dune pushed an enormous cart of snow into the desert. Cora said they should have brought water. Dune claimed the snow would save their banana crop at the distant camp. By noon the cart held a puddle. Cora carefully bent her tail round the drain plug and held it shut. They reached camp with warm water and a wet map. Dune planted the banana shoots. Cora kept the cart to carry shade cloth, which weighed much less than snow.
