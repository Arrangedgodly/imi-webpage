# The robot moon garden

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Colonist garden*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Rose, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose sent a robot to plant an enormous grove on the moon. Dex gave it banana seeds and a shovel. When they arrived, the robot had planted the shovel in neat soil. A spaceship brought more seeds, but the robot buried its ramp too. Rose tied her tail round the control lever and held it still. Dex changed the task to dig, then plant seeds. The grove grew. The robot kept watering the shovel until Rose gave it a flower pot.
