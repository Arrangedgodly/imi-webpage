# The business nap merger

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Corporate merger*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Nan, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan merged her banana shop with Rex and his pillow stall. Their business promised fruit and rest in one place. The corporate office ordered every worker to nap while eating. Rex fell asleep with a banana in his tail. Nan woke him before it dropped. Customers waited beside empty tills. They split the hours into meals and naps. Sales recovered, but the office demanded a single product. Nan stuffed pillows with dry banana leaves. Rex sold them without asking anyone to chew.
