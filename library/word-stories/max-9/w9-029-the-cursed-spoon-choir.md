# The cursed spoon choir

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Cursed object*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Jem, Ora; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jem stole a spoon from the witch kitchen. It sang whenever he tried to eat breakfast. Ora warned that a terrible curse could last forever. Jem tied the spoon to his tail and climbed to the grove. Each time he scooped banana mash, the song shook ripe fruit from the branches. Ora fetched baskets. By noon the witch came to reclaim it. Jem offered her half the crop. She left the spoon and asked for the tune.
