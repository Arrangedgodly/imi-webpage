# The hover ship tail basket

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action rescue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Ira, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira flew a hover ship above the enormous banana grove. Meg hung from its rescue basket with her tail through the mesh. The pilot tried to lift a trapped worker, but the basket held too much fruit. Ira ordered Meg to dump the bananas. She passed them to the worker first, then cleared the basket. A dangerous branch broke as the lift rose. Meg caught its vine and steadied them. They landed with one worker holding the saved fruit, and he shared his bunch with them.
