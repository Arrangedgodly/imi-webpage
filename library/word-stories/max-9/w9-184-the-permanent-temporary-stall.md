# The permanent temporary stall

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Permit bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Lark, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark opened a temporary banana stall. Dex stamped its contract for one day. Each dawn she took it apart and rebuilt it, calling the stall new. The permit office sent a secretary to watch. Lark used her tail to lift the roof, but the secretary counted the same roots beneath it. Dex offered a permanent permit for less than a week of daily fees. Lark bought it and left the roof up. The secretary bought fruit and finally got a dry seat.
