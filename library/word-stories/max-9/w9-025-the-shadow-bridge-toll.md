# The shadow bridge toll

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Bridge troll*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Toby, Sal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Toby had to cross a bridge before breakfast. Sal, the troll guard, demanded a terrible riddle first. Toby asked what had a tail but could not climb. Sal guessed for an hour. Toby sat on the rail and peeled a banana. At last he pointed to the bridge shadow, which ended in a narrow tail. Sal leaned over to see. Toby crossed behind him. Sal charged the shadow a toll and spent all day waiting for payment.
