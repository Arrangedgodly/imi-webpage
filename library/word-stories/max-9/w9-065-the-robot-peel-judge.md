# The robot peel judge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Robot logic*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Pax, Mina; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pax built a robot to sort bananas. Mina wrote a splendid rule that ripe fruit went left. The robot put every banana left, then put Pax there too. His yellow coat matched its chart. Mina tried to pull him free with her tail, but the robot sorted her coat beside him. Pax called this a dangerous flaw. Mina took off both coats. The robot folded them into the peel bin. They fed it a new chart and kept their coats.
