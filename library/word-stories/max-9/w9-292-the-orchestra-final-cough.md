# The orchestra final cough

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Concert etiquette*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Tess, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess warned the orchestra audience not to cough during the quiet song. Len, the musician, swallowed a banana chip and felt a cough rising. He held it until his face turned red. Tess noticed and stopped the music with her tail raised. Len coughed safely and drank water. The audience joined him in one enormous burst. Tess gave them a pause, then began again. The quiet song stayed quiet because no one had to pretend breathing was less important than the score.
