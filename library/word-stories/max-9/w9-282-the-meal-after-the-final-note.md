# The meal after the final note

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Musical notation*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Ira, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira wrote a rest into the orchestra score. Cal placed bananas beside each musician to fill the pause. The audience heard peeling during the silence. Ira lifted her tail to stop the noise, but everyone chewed more softly instead. Cal moved the fruit backstage. The next rest stayed quiet. After the final note Ira lowered her arm, and the players rushed for lunch. Cal wrote a meal break after the score so no one had to guess whether a rest meant food.
