# The ghost crew wash day

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ghost pirates*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Ivy, Reed; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy boarded a ghost ship to search for treasure. Reed carried a lantern in his tail. Pale shapes flapped along the deck and moaned about breakfast. Ivy touched one and found a wet sheet. The pirate crew had hung laundry on the ropes, then hidden below to scare guests away from their bananas. Reed raised his lantern and found red socks tangled among the pink sheets. Ivy offered soap in trade for fruit. The pirates accepted and asked her to keep their secret.
