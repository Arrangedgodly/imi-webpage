# The climbing trophy ribbon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Climbing contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Meg, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Meg raced Gus up an enormous tree. The trophy hung beneath a splendid banana bunch. Gus reached it first, but his tail snagged the ribbon and tied him to the branch. Meg climbed up and freed him. She carried the trophy down while he carried the fruit. The judge could not tell who had won. Meg proposed a shared prize. Gus accepted on condition that the ribbon stayed on the ground. They used it to mark a safer finish line.
