# The spotlight paper hero

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Stage illusion*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Uma, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma played a hero beneath the spotlight. Rex built an enormous dragon from banana crates and paper. Its head fell forward before the battle. Uma caught it with her tail and called the dragon tired. The audience laughed as she helped it sit. Rex moved the hidden strings to let it bow. Uma offered it a wooden fruit instead of drawing her sword. The director kept the new ending. Rex braced the neck after the show so kindness would not depend on a broken hinge.
