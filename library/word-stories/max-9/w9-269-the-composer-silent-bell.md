# The composer silent bell

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Minimalist music*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Tess, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess wrote a song for a bell with no clapper. Len, the musician, waited beneath the banana tree. He shook the bell, producing nothing. Tess called the silence planned. A goat rang its neck bell nearby and spoiled the pause. Len caught the silent bell in his tail and moved the concert indoors. The audience sat quietly for one minute, then asked for a tune. Tess put a clapper inside and wrote one. Len played it outside, where the goat joined only after the final note.
