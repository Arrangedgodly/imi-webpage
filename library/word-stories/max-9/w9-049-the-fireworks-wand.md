# The fireworks wand

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal wizard test*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Jade, Lou; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade had to light the royal candles to earn a wizard hat. Lou handed her a fireworks wand by mistake. She pointed it at the splendid cake. Sparks burst from the bananas and chased the king under a table. Jade wrapped her tail round a wet cloth and caught each spark. Lou put them in jars. The candles stayed dark, but the jars lit the hall. Jade earned her hat. Lou earned a job cleaning the royal ceiling.
