# The waterfall chase ferry

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action pursuit*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Vera, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera fled a dangerous chase toward a waterfall. Dex waited with an enormous ferry full of bananas. She landed aboard, tail hooked round a rope. The pursuers stopped at the bank because the ferry charged a fare. Dex offered them a group rate. Vera paid him double to leave at once. He took both offers and crossed with everyone. At the far bank Vera showed the court order clearing her name. Dex returned the fare to the pursuers in bananas and kept the fee from Vera for the quick ride.
