# The costume contest fern

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Costume prize*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Nan, Remy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan dressed as a fern for the contest. Remy wore a dangerous dragon mask. Nan stood so still that a gardener tried to water her. She wrapped her tail round his can and asked for a pause. Remy laughed, then sneezed smoke from banana flour hidden in the mask. The judge gave Nan best costume and Remy best sneeze. Nan went home in plain clothes. Remy kept sneezing until the gardener washed the flour from his nose with the prize can.
