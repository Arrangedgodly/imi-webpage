# The pudding relay baton

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Relay race*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Ari, Dot; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari brought a pudding cup instead of a baton to the relay. Dot called this a dangerous mistake. He ran his lap with the cup balanced on his tail. Dot took it and sped beneath the banana trees. The splendid pudding spilled at each turn. Their team finished with an empty cup. The judges gave them no medal, but every runner asked for the recipe. Ari sold it to the cafe and bought a proper baton with the first coins.
