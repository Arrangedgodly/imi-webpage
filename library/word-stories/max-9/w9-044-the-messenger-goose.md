# The messenger goose

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal summons*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Etta, Gil; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Etta hired a messenger goose to call Gil to the palace. The goose ate her splendid seal and swallowed the note. Gil found it honking beside his banana tree. Etta arrived on foot, tail full of spare paper. She offered the goose a bowl of water. It coughed the note into the bowl, where the ink ran. Gil guessed that the queen wanted soup. He brought a pot. She had wanted supper, so he got a royal job.
