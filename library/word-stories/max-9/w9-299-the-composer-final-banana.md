# The composer final banana

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Artistic inspiration*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Cleo, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo could not finish the orchestra song. Poe, the musician, waited with an enormous banana basket. Cleo tried a loud ending, then a soft one. Neither fit. Poe peeled a banana with his tail and dropped the skin into a bin. Its soft tap matched the last beat. Cleo wrote one simple note and stopped. The audience heard the finished song that night. Poe brought no basket on stage, only a small drum for the note a peel had taught them.
