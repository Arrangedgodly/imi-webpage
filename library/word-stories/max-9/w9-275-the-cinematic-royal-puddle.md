# The cinematic royal puddle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film spectacle*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Bea, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea filmed a cinematic ship battle in a puddle. Hal, the director, wanted an enormous wave. He dropped a banana crate into the water. Both toy ships sank, and the camera got wet. Bea wiped the lens with her tail and built a slow wave using a flat board. Hal filmed close to the surface. The audience believed it was an ocean. Bea returned the toys to their owner. Hal kept the board and left every heavy crate far from the next puddle.
