# The explorer boot museum

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Archaeology*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Hope, Zia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope dug up a splendid boot beneath the banana grove. Zia called it evidence of an ancient king. Hope brushed it with her tail and found yesterday written inside. The village cobbler had thrown it out after a leak. Zia had already sold museum tickets. Hope filled the boot with water to show why the king had lost it. Guests liked the display. The cobbler opened a repair booth next door and bought every ticket holder a dry sock.
