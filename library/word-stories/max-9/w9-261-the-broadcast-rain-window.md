# The broadcast rain window

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Weather broadcast*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Koa, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa forecast rain on the broadcast news. Meg, the director, sprayed the window to give him proof. Koa used his tail to wipe a clear patch and saw sunshine. He told viewers to check their own windows. Meg stopped spraying and moved the camera outside the banana grove. Real clouds arrived by the next hour. Koa reported them without a hose. Meg kept the sprayer for plants and put the forecast desk far enough away that no wet glass could pretend to be a sky.
