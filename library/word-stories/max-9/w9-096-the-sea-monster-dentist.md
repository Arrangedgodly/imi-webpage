# The sea monster dentist

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sea monster threat*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Cato, Orla; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cato sailed into an enormous mouth while seeking a treasure island. Orla stopped the boat with her tail round a tooth. The sea beast groaned. A pineapple had wedged beneath its tongue. Cato pushed the fruit loose with an oar. The beast spat them toward shore, then followed for more care. Orla opened a dockside tooth clinic. Cato sold soft banana meals. The beast paid with pearls and kept its mouth shut whenever a boat needed a quiet crossing.
