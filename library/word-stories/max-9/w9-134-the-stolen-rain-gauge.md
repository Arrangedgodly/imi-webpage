# The stolen rain gauge

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Science mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Lark, Tuck; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lark lost the rain gauge from her splendid garden. Tuck offered his detective badge. They found banana peels beside the empty post. Tuck suspected the cook, but Lark followed drops up a tree with her tail brushing leaves aside. A monkey gardener had borrowed the gauge as a flower vase. He thought it measured stem height. Lark took it back and gave him a jar. Tuck solved the next case by asking before he dusted the whole garden for prints.
