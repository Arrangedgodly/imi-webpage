# The time machine lunch bell

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Time travel*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Kit, Aya; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kit built a time machine from a splendid clock he bought yesterday and a banana crate. Aya climbed in with her tail round the bell. They meant to see next year, but the machine stopped at lunch. Kit tried again. Lunch again. Aya read the dial. He had bought a kitchen timer. They sold rides to hungry monkeys, each ending beside a full bowl. Kit never reached next year, but he knew the exact minute the cook served every meal.
