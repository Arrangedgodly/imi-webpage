# The promotion ladder toll

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Workplace ambition*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Tess, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess climbed an office ladder for her promotion. Poe charged a banana toll on each rung. Tess paid until she reached the official top, where a secretary asked for the final fee. Tess had no fruit left. She swung down by her tail and gathered fallen bananas beneath the ladder. Poe called them company property. Tess asked why he had dropped each payment. He returned her fees. She bought a plain ladder and climbed to a different office with lunch intact.
