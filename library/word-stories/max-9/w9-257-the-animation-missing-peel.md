# The animation missing peel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Animation continuity*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Gail, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail drew an animation of a monkey slipping on a banana peel. Tom checked each frame for the director. The peel vanished halfway through, leaving the monkey falling on clean ground. Gail curled her tail round the pencil and drew it back in. Tom added a frame of a cleaner catching the falling monkey. The monkey landed safely in the next drawing. Gail liked the new scene better. Their film became a short tale about catching mistakes, and the cleaner got the biggest applause.
