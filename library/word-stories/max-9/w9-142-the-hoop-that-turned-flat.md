# The hoop that turned flat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Basketball contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Uma, Dex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma threw balls through a splendid hoop tied to a banana tree. Dex set an enormous prize basket below it. A dangerous wind turned the hoop flat. Uma tossed the ball high, but it rested on the rim. Dex climbed with his tail round the branch and tilted the hoop. The ball fell through onto the prizes. Uma won a squashed banana. They moved the hoop lower, tied its frame tight, and ate the prize before testing it again.
