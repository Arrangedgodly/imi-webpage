# The rehearsal audience mirror

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Acting exercise*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Mina, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina practiced to a mirror during rehearsal. Rex arranged an enormous banana basket as her pretend audience. She bowed, saw her image bow back, and started the speech again. Rex covered the mirror with his tail holding a cloth. Mina finished without waiting for a reply. The director gave her a real audience that night. They clapped after her final line. Mina bowed once and left. Rex uncovered the mirror only after she went backstage, where a second bow could not restart the play.
