# The cave guide hat light

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Expedition guide*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Bram, Sia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bram sold cave tours using a splendid light on his hat. Sia brought bananas for the guests. A low ceiling knocked the hat into a stream. Bram followed its glow and left the group behind him. Sia stretched her tail across the path to keep them still. She heard a waterfall ahead and called Bram back. He hired her as guide. The hat drifted into the gift shop below, where it sold for more than the tour had earned.
