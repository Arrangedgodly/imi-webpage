# The council tail census

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Population survey*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Nan, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan ran a census by counting tails in the banana grove. Gus filed the official total. A committee found the town had doubled overnight. Nan had counted each tail once going up a tree and once coming down. Gus proposed two votes per citizen to match the ledger. Nan crossed out the spare count instead. She marked each monkey hand with washable ink. Gus tried to get a second mark on his foot. Nan gave him a mop and one vote.
