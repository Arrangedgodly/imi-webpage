# The dangerous lullaby

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Siren song*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Bryn, Flo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bryn learned a dangerous song that put foes to sleep. Flo plugged her ears with banana bits and guarded him at the castle. Bryn sang at the gate. The guards slept, but so did his splendid horse. It lay across the path. Flo tied her tail round the gate latch and hauled it open. Bryn hummed in his own sleep. Flo dragged him home in a cart and made him practice with the song book shut.
