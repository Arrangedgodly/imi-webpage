# The banana forecast fan

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Business forecast*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Ada, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ada presented a forecast for the banana firm. Bo set an electric fan behind her chart to cool the board. The paper rose with every gust, making sales seem steep. Ada held it down with her tail. The committee frowned at the flat line. Bo turned the fan higher. Ada unplugged it and opened the sales ledger. The firm had sold one fan and no bananas. The board voted to pivot into fans. Ada billed them for the chart paper.
