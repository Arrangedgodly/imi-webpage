# The football hollow ball

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Moss, June; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Moss kicked an enormous football into the banana grove. June caught it with her tail and heard a rattle. The referee suspected a dangerous trick. Moss opened the seam and found a tiny bell. A goat had swallowed their old ball and the coach had bought a pet toy as a spare. June tied the bell to the goal post. Each score rang it. The coach kept the new rule, and the goat learned to wait beside the fence.
