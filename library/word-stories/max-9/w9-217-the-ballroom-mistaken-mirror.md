# The ballroom mistaken mirror

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic mistaken identity*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Rose, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose met a beautiful dancer in the ballroom mirror. Gus stood behind her with a splendid banana bouquet, trying to speak. Rose practiced her greeting at the glass until he thought she loved her own image. He left the bouquet on a chair. Rose saw him go and caught his sleeve with her tail. She had been too shy to turn around. Gus admitted the same. They danced facing each other. The mirror watched alone, showing the empty bouquet chair.
