# The unicorn mud bath

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pure magical beast*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Arlo, Meg; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Arlo sought a unicorn in the grove. Meg found hoof marks beside the banana pond. A splendid horn poked from the mud, but its owner refused to rise. It had spent yesterday posing for royal coins and wanted a rest. Arlo cleaned the horn with his tail while Meg held a towel. The unicorn let them ride home on one condition. They had to splash through every puddle. The royal painter met them at the gate and fainted.
