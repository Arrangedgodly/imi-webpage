# The recording door squeak

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Studio sound*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Nan, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nan wanted silence for her recording. Bo oiled every door in the banana studio. One still squeaked because his tail pressed its hinge each time he entered. Nan recorded the squeak alone and asked him to repeat it in rhythm. The musician used it as a sharp beat beneath her song. Bo got a sound credit. He moved his tail before opening the door afterward. Nan kept the squeak file, but the studio finally had quiet when she asked for it.
