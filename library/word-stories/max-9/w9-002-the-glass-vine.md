# The glass vine

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Glass slipper tale*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Nell, Bram; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Nell had a splendid tail, but the glass shoe would not fit it. Bram tried every monkey in the palace. Nell asked why a tail needed a shoe. The prince had dropped it while climbing a vine. Nell took the shoe up the tree and caught rain in it. At breakfast the thirsty prince came for a drink. He found Nell beside his lost cup and asked to share her branch.
