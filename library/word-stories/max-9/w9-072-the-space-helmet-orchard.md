# The space helmet orchard

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Space survival*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Zed, Joy; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Zed cracked his helmet beside the spaceship. Joy had one spare, but it held an enormous banana seedling. She moved the plant into a pot while Zed held his breath. Its roots clung to the glass. Joy used her tail to ease them free. Zed put on the helmet and breathed the scent of wet soil. Back home he planted the seedling in the yard. Joy kept the cracked helmet as a dome and grew herbs beneath it.
