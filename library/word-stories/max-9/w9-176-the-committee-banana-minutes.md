# The committee banana minutes

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Meeting bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Dara, Pax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara recorded minutes for the banana committee. Pax asked why one hour of meetings filled twelve pages. Dara had written every sixty seconds as a separate event. Pax used his tail to turn the heavy stack. The official archive had room for one shelf. Dara cut the record to decisions, but there had been none. She filed a blank page. Pax proposed ending the meeting on time. Dara wrote that down and closed the book before anyone could discuss it.
