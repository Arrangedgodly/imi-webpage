# The orchestra tail bow

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Concert mishap*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Bo, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bo led the orchestra with his tail as a bow. Nia placed the audience under the banana trees. Bo bowed low, and his tail struck the drum. The musicians took it as the opening beat. He stood, and the cymbals crashed. Bo stopped moving until Nia handed him a plain stick. The music settled into its proper tune. At the end he bowed again, but Nia held his tail behind the podium. The drummer still played one final note just in case.
