# The love potion soup ladle

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic magic*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Esme, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Esme bought a beautiful love potion for Cal. The cook poured it into the enormous banana soup pot by mistake. Every guest praised the soup and hugged the ladle. Esme lifted it with her tail, and the crowd followed her to the sink. Cal brought plain water and washed the ladle. The spell faded. He asked Esme to supper without any potion. She accepted. The cook kept the empty bottle to hold salt and locked the soup recipe well away from the magic shelf.
