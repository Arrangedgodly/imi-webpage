# The anniversary tree ring

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic gift*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Uma, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Uma asked Poe for a yearly ring. He counted tree rings in the banana grove and brought an enormous stump. Uma used her tail to roll it onto the porch. It blocked the door. Poe offered to carve it smaller, but she noticed the smooth top made a fine table. They ate pineapple there and marked one ring for each year together. Poe carved a tiny ring from the spare wood. Uma wore it and kept the table for gifts that needed more room.
