# The glacier picnic flag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Polar explorer*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Polly, Seth; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Polly planted a splendid flag on the glacier. Seth put their breakfast beneath it while they mapped the coast. An hour later the flag had moved. Polly accused a rival crew. Seth followed banana peels across the ice and found their flag on a seal. Polly had stuck it in a harness left by a fisher. She used her tail to untie it. They shared their last banana and mapped the seal route as a very wet road.
