# The captain cork eye

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate disguise*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Patch, Lila; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Patch wore a cork over one eye to look like a dangerous pirate. Lila steered their splendid boat while he posed on the bow. He could not see the reef. Lila wrapped her tail round his waist and pulled him down before they struck it. The cork fell into a leak and stopped the water. Patch left it there. At port the crew asked about his pirate mark. He pointed at the deck and said his eye guarded the ship.
