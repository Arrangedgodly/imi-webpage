# The contest jar frog

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Guessing contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Vera, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera filled a splendid jar with banana chips for a guessing contest. Len counted them through the glass. At breakfast the count had changed. Vera searched for a thief and found a frog inside the jar. It had climbed in through the loose lid and eaten chips. Len caught it with his tail and set it by the pond. Vera counted the chips again. Len won the prize by guessing fewer. The frog entered no guess but kept its free meal.
