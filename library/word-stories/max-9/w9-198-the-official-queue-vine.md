# The official queue vine

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Public service queue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Cleo, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo installed an official queue vine at the permit desk. Sid held it with his tail to keep monkeys in line. A committee praised the smooth system. Then the vine grew overnight, carrying the queue round three trees and through the banana stall. Guests arrived with full baskets and blank forms. Cleo cut the vine to desk length. Sid planted the spare section in a garden. Its fruit paid for a second clerk, which shortened the queue better than any knot had.
