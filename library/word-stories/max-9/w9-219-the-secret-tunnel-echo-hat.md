# The secret tunnel echo hat

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Spy escape*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Tess, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess escaped through a secret tunnel with proof that Len had stolen banana funds. Len chased the sound of her boots. Tess hung a splendid hat beside an echo hole and flicked it with her tail. The sound drew Len down a dangerous side path. He reached a dry well and found only his own echo. Tess left the records at the court, then returned with a rope. Len climbed out and paid her for the rescue. She took back her hat, which he had stolen too.
