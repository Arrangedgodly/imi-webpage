# The singing prize silence

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Talent contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Sal, Uma; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sal practiced a splendid song for the contest. Uma watched from a tree, holding a banana in her tail. On stage Sal lost his voice. Uma held her tail behind his back and tapped the beat. He waved his hands to show the song instead. The audience copied each move. A messenger brought him the prize for best silent act. Sal took a sip of water and thanked them aloud. The judge asked if he could keep the thanks silent too.
