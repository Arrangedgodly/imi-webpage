# The rehearsal borrowed beard

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Costume comedy*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Cleo, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo wore a long beard for rehearsal. Gus had made it from banana fibers for the costumes box. The tail of Cleo caught the beard during her royal bow, pulling it from her chin. The director demanded more glue. Gus shortened the beard instead. Cleo could bow safely, but the audience laughed at its uneven ends. She played a king who cut his own hair badly. Gus added a tiny wooden comb to the crown, and the laugh became part of the scene.
