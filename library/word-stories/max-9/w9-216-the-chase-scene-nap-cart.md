# The chase scene nap cart

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action chase*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Pia, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia chased a dangerous thief through the banana market. Cal drove an enormous nap cart across her path. She leaped onto it, tail wrapped round the rail, and bounced off a pillow toward the thief. He stopped to catch his stolen hat. Pia caught him and returned the hat to its owner. Cal billed the town for one crushed pillow. Pia paid from the reward. She kept the pillow in her chase kit, where its soft corner proved more useful than another hard helmet.
