# The beautiful rain song

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic serenade*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Tess, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess sang Cal a beautiful song beneath the banana trees. Cal leaned from a splendid window and listened. Rain filled the song box that played her backing tune. Tess lifted it with her tail, but the music slowed to a growl. Cal came down with a towel and sang the missing notes himself. They carried the box inside together. Tess kept the wet score as a reminder of their first duet. Cal dried it flat and wrote a simpler tune for weather they could not control.
