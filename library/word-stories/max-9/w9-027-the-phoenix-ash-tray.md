# The phoenix ash tray

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Immortal pet*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Dara, Wes; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara bought a phoenix to warm her tree house. Wes built an enormous perch. The bird burst into flame beside the banana bowl and left a pile of ash. Dara carefully swept it into a tray with her tail. At dawn it hatched again beneath a baked banana. Wes wanted a cage. Dara set out a fresh tray instead. Soon the village lined up for hot fruit. The phoenix got a new perch and a strict closing hour.
