# The chess king umbrella

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Chess contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Rex, Sia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex entered a chess contest under the banana trees. Sia brought an enormous umbrella because the sky looked dangerous. Rain began as Rex moved his king. Sia tilted the umbrella with her tail and struck the table, sliding every piece across the board. Rex lost his winning move. The judge set up the last known position, but no one agreed where the pawns belonged. Sia used leaves to draw it from memory. Rex won, then bought a board with small holes for each piece.
