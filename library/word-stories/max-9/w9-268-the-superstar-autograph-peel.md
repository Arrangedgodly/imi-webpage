# The superstar autograph peel

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Celebrity souvenir*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Sia, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia sold banana peels signed by a superstar. Cal waited in the audience line for one. The ink ran as the peel browned. Sia tried drying it with her tail fanning the air, but the signature cracked. Cal asked for plain paper instead. Sia printed a note saying the star had eaten this banana. The star laughed and signed both note and paper. Cal kept the paper, ate his own banana, and used the peel as compost without expecting a name to last in soil.
