# The beautiful castle letterbox

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Courtship misunderstanding*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Bea, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea waited for Sid and his beautiful love letters. Each morning her splendid castle box held banana peels instead. She climbed inside with her tail round the lid and found letters wedged in the back. Sid had fed the palace pet through the same slot. Bea read the letters, then wrote one asking for two boxes. Sid built them side by side and painted hearts on hers. The pet learned to open only the fruit lid. Bea finally got dry letters and a quiet morning.
