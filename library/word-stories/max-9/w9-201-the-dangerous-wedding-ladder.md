# The dangerous wedding ladder

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic rescue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Ada, Gil; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ada climbed a dangerous ladder to rescue Gil before his arranged wedding. He sat in the banana tower wearing a splendid coat. Ada hooked her tail round the sill and asked him to jump. Gil said he wanted the wedding but feared the stairs. Ada lowered the ladder into the hall. He climbed down one rung at a time and thanked her. The bride met them with cake. Ada stayed to hold the ladder while the priest fetched his hat from the tower.
