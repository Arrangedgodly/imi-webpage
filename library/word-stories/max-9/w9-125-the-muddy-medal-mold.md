# The muddy medal mold

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports fraud mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Cleo, Bram; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo won a splendid gold medal, but Bram found mud inside it. A detective checked the podium. The medal maker had used clay to save gold and forgotten to bake it. Cleo wrapped the wet medal in banana leaves with her tail. She took it back and asked for one that would last. The maker fired the clay and painted it gold. Bram called it cheap. Cleo hung it proudly because it still marked the race she had won.
