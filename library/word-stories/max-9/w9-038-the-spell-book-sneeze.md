# The spell book sneeze

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Forbidden spell*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Alba, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Alba opened the forbidden book on a splendid branch. Sid sneezed into its pages. A spell jumped out and turned the bananas blue. Alba carefully checked the index for a cure. Sid sneezed again, and the fruit turned gold. They fed one to a crow. It stayed a crow and asked for more. Alba closed the book before Sid took another breath. They sold golden fruit at the fair and used the money to buy him a cloth.
