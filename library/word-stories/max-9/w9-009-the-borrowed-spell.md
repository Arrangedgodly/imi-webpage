# The borrowed spell

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Wizard apprentice*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Fenn, Kit; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fenn borrowed a spell to clean the monkey tower. Kit read the warning, but Fenn had waved his tail. Every banana peel grew a broom. They swept the beds into the yard and the wizard into a bucket. Kit held up the splendid spell book. Fenn found the word reverse on its cover. He turned the book round. The brooms swept the beds home, then took breakfast from the table as their pay.
