# The golden egg pan

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Golden goose*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: May, Orin; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

May found a golden egg beneath her banana tree. Orin brought a splendid pan and tried to fry it. The egg dented the pan. May tapped the shell with her tail and heard a tiny voice asking for shade. They built a nest under leaves. At breakfast a golden goose hatched and ate every banana peel. Orin sold the dented pan as a nest bowl. May kept the goose, which laid plain eggs with shiny yolks.
