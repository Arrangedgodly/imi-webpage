# The chocolate shield

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Knight trial*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Ari, Nola; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari made a chocolate shield for the royal trial. Nola warned that the dragon had a terrible sweet tooth. Ari held the shield high as the dragon breathed fire. It melted over his tail. The dragon stopped and licked the shield instead. Nola tossed bananas into the warm pool of sweets. The judges tasted them and called the trial a draw. Ari opened a stall beside the arena. His next shield came with a spoon.
