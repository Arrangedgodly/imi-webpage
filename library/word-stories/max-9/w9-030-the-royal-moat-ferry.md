# The royal moat ferry

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Hero rescue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Rex, Pia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rex rode to rescue Pia from a tower. The bridge had fallen, and his horse refused to swim. Pia carefully lowered a basket on a vine. Rex put his splendid sword inside. She sent it back with a banana and a fare sign. He climbed in, tail curled round the rope. Pia hauled him across and opened a ferry service. Rex stayed to sell tickets. The horse got a season pass and a seat with a wider door.
