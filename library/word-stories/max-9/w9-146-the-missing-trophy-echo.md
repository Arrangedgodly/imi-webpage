# The missing trophy echo

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: School mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Ari, Rue; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari lost the splendid singing trophy from the banana hall. Rue searched with her detective lens. Each tap on the wall gave a bright ring. Ari had hung the trophy inside the drum to make his last song louder. He opened the drum with his tail and found it wedged against the skin. Rue loosened the frame. The trophy came free, but the drum sounded dull. Ari thanked her with a quiet song and kept the prize on a shelf.
