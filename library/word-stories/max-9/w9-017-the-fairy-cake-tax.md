# The fairy cake tax

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Fairy curse*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Mina, Cobb; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Mina baked a cake for the fairy queen. Cobb ate its banana crown before she came. A terrible curse made his tail grow a cake at each sneeze. Mina feared a flood of frosting, but Cobb found the cakes delicious. He sneezed into empty pans until the queen arrived. She saw the bare first cake and the full table. Cobb paid her tax in cake. The queen lifted the curse because she had run out of plates.
