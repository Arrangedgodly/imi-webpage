# The pirate compass cough

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate voyage*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Kira, Abe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Kira stole a compass from an enormous pirate ship. Abe hid it in a banana jar. The needle pointed toward the jar lid instead of north. Kira shook it and heard a cough. A tiny messenger sat inside, trying to breathe. She opened the case with her tail. He had been hired to point toward gold but wanted shore leave. Kira sailed where he asked. His home was a bank. She returned the compass and opened an account.
