# The diving board banana

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Swimming contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Hope, Pax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hope polished an enormous diving board for the meet. Pax ate his breakfast banana at its far end. The peel slid beneath his feet and sent him into the pool. Hope held up a splendid score card. Pax said he had not meant to dive. She asked him to do it again. He tied his tail round a rail and placed the peel under one foot. The board bent, then bounced him high. Pax won the meet and lost his lunch.
