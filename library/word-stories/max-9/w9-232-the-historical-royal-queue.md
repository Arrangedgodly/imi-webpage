# The historical royal queue

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Royal ceremony*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Ira, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira crowned Tom during an ancient ceremony beneath the banana trees. The enormous crown slid over his ears. A messenger called the next prince, and Tom refused to move. Ira lifted the crown with her tail and found the kings were only in a practice pageant. Tom had taken the script as a real order. Ira gave him a paper crown to keep. He joined the queue again and waited for his cue, which now came with a clear practice sign.
