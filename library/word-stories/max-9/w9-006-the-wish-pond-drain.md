# The wish pond drain

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Three wishes*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Milo, Ash; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Milo wished the pond would fill with bananas. It did, and Ash fell into the heap. Milo wished for a splendid boat, but it sat on top of the fruit. The pond spirit said he had one wish left. Ash poked a tail through the boat drain and pulled up a plug. Water rushed in beneath their breakfast. Milo wished for forks. They sailed home with fruit for the village and a boat full of lunch.
