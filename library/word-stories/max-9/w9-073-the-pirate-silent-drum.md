# The pirate silent drum

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pirate raid*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Milo, Bea; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Milo led a raid with a splendid drum. Bea said its noise would warn the enemy. Milo wrapped banana leaves round the skin and struck it with his tail. The drum gave a soft cough. They crept across the deck, then found the enemy waiting with tea. A messenger had heard the cough and called a doctor. Milo admitted he wanted their gold. Bea bought their old coins instead. The doctor charged them for a quiet drum lesson.
