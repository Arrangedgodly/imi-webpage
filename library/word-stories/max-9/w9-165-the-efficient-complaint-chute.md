# The efficient complaint chute

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Customer service*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Pia, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Pia built a chute for unhappy customers at the banana firm. Len called it efficient because every letter vanished at once. The business council praised the clear desks. Pia heard paper rustle under a tree and followed the chute with her tail. It emptied into the public garden. She gathered the letters and read one aloud at the next meeting. Len had sent it himself about the cold office. Pia closed the chute, fixed the heater, and hired Len to read mail.
