# The pumpkin coach seat

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Magic carriage*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Cleo, Ned; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Cleo needed a coach to reach the moon feast. Ned turned a pumpkin into one with a borrowed wand. It looked splendid until Cleo sat down and sank into soup. Ned read the wand label carefully. It made meals, not wheels. Cleo tied her tail to a vine, swung to the feast, and carried the pumpkin with her. The guests had brought dry bread. Her soup won the royal prize. Ned rode home in the empty rind.
