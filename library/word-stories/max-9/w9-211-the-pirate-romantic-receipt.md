# The pirate romantic receipt

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Adventure romance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Koa, Aya; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Koa rescued Aya from a dangerous pirate boat. She demanded a receipt for the bananas he had thrown overboard to lighten it. Koa offered an enormous ruby as a token of love. Aya sold it at port and bought twice the fruit. He thought she had rejected him. She invited him aboard their repaired boat and showed two lunch baskets. Koa tied the mast rope with his tail. They sailed together, keeping all future love tokens small enough to fit beside the food.
