# The spelling bee hive

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: School contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Bryn, Kit; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bryn entered a spelling bee beside the banana grove. Kit carried an enormous word book. Bryn spelled beehive, and a swarm rose from the next tree. Kit held the book above them with her tail. The judge asked for butterfly. Bryn refused to speak. Kit showed that the swarm had moved because the lunch cart hit its branch. Bryn finished the word. No butterfly came. He won a ribbon, which Kit tied to the cart as a warning flag.
