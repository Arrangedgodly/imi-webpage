# The marathon wrong ribbon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports finish*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Wes, Nell; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Wes ran the marathon toward a splendid yellow ribbon. Nell waited by the real finish with a banana basket. Wes crossed a ribbon tied round a dangerous tree stump. It marked a hole. He fell waist deep and caught a root with his tail. Nell pulled him free. They finished last together. Wes asked to keep the ribbon as a trophy. The gardener made him fill the hole first, so his trophy came with a spade and muddy hands.
