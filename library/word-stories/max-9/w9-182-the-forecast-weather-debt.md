# The forecast weather debt

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Public finance*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Jade, Cal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Jade borrowed against the banana harvest due next year. Cal wrote a forecast promising sunshine. The committee spent the coins on a marble fountain. A drought came, and the harvest shrank. Jade used her tail to clear the fountain drain. Its bowl held enough water to save the driest trees. Cal revised his report to claim the fountain was a farm tool. Jade sold its marble lions and paid the debt. The fountain stayed, with plain pipes and no forecast pinned above it.
