# The crocodile moat lease

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Royal monster pact*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Tia, Evan; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tia leased the moat to a crocodile who promised to guard the palace. Evan planted banana trees along the bank. The splendid beast ate the fruit and let every guest across its back. Tia read the lease aloud. It had agreed to guard the palace from hunger. Evan laughed, but Tia asked for the rent. The beast opened its mouth and showed a gold coin beneath its tongue. They paid a dentist to fetch it and kept the tenant.
