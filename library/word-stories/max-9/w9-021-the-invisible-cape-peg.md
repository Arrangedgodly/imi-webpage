# The invisible cape peg

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Invisibility cloak*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Puck, Nia; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Puck wore a splendid cape that made him vanish. Nia could still see his banana, floating through the hall. He ate it to hide, then dropped the peel on a guard. The guard followed the peel trail to the pantry. Puck hung the cape on a peg and asked Nia to hide him. She carefully put a bowl on his head. The guard found an empty cape and a strange bowl with a tail. He arrested the bowl.
