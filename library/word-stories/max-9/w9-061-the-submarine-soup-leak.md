# The submarine soup leak

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Undersea mission*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Bea, Otto; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea took a submarine below the reef. Otto packed banana soup in an enormous flask. A leak sent bubbles past the window, and Bea ordered him to seal it. Otto poured soup into the crack. A crab plugged the hole with its claw to catch the meal. Bea rose toward shore while Otto fed the crab through the crack. They bought it a bowl before it let go. The repair shop charged them for one shell and two soups.
