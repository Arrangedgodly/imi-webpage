# The dangerous catapult picnic

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Action siege*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Vera, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Vera loaded a dangerous catapult with an enormous banana basket. Sid planned to launch her over the wall with it. Vera curled her tail round the frame and refused until he tested the basket alone. It sailed over and landed in the royal feast. Guests cheered for fresh fruit. Sid launched a note asking for the gate to open. Vera walked through with the empty sling in her hands. The king hired them to bring fruit but insisted that all workers use the door.
