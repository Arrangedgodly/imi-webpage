# The tennis tail knot

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sports rule loophole*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Fern, Lou; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Fern played tennis with her splendid tail round the racket. Lou claimed she had two hands free and an unfair edge. The referee tied their hands behind them. Lou tried to use his tail too, but he had tied it in a knot to hold a pineapple snack. Fern waited while he untied it. They played a long match, then split the fruit. The referee made a new rule allowing snacks only between games, with tails fully untied.
