# The asteroid pet leash

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Asteroid mining*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-002*
*Cast: Gail, Koa; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail found treasure inside a small asteroid. Koa drilled a hole, and the rock began to roll away. Gail caught it with a leash held in her tail. Its shell cracked open to reveal a moon snail. Koa called the discovery a mining failure. Gail offered the snail banana leaves from their ship garden. It followed them home and ate the stone steps. Koa sold the smooth dust. Gail moved the leash to a very large stake.
