# The detective peel clock

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Detective clue*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Ada, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ada found a banana peel beside the broken clock. Rex called a detective. She measured the peel with her tail, then checked the enormous clock hands. One hand smelled of fruit. Rex admitted he had used it to reach lunch on a high shelf. Ada fitted it back and wound the clock. It ran twelve minutes fast. Rex offered to climb up and fix it. Ada moved the bananas lower and made him keep both feet on the floor.
