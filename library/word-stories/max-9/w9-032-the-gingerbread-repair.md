# The gingerbread repair

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Candy cottage*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Bess, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bess found a splendid cottage whose sweet cake wall had a crack. Hal bit the loose edge. The witch charged him for damage. Bess used her tail to spread banana paste in the crack and carefully pressed the piece back. Hal offered to mend the roof. The witch handed him a bag of sweets and watched every bite. By dusk the cottage stood firm. Hal paid for one window he had eaten. Bess earned a room with a plain wooden door.
