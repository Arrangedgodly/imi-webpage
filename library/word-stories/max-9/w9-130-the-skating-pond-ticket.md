# The skating pond ticket

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Ice sports*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Hugo, Aya; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hugo trained on an enormous frozen pond. Aya sold seats on the bank and served pineapple cubes. At noon the ice softened. Hugo used his tail to hold a low branch while Aya pulled his skates free. The crowd demanded their money back. Aya rolled the benches to a shaded pond that still had firm ice. Hugo skated a short show there. He bowed from the bank this time. Aya kept one cube on the ice as a warning test.
