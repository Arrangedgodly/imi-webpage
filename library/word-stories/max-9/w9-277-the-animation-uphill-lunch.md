# The animation uphill lunch

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Animation physics*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Dara, Len; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Dara drew an animation of a banana rolling uphill. Len, the director, said the movement broke physics. Dara added a monkey tail pushing it from behind the rock. Len approved, but the tail belonged to no visible actor. Dara drew a hungry monkey on the next frame. The audience now saw a plan rather than a mistake. The monkey reached the summit and ate the fruit. Len kept the final close view, where the tail rested and the hill no longer mattered.
