# The detective flour moon

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Night mystery*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Tess, Bram; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Tess saw a splendid white moon mark on the banana shed. Bram called the detective because the lock was broken. Tess found flour inside the mark. She curled her tail round the latch and opened the door. The baker stood within, covered in dough. He had slipped against the wall and broken the lock while seeking his breakfast fruit. Bram gave him a key and a bill. Tess washed the moon away before the village started a hunt for ghosts.
