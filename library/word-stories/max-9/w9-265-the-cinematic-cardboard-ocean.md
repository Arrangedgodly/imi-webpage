# The cinematic cardboard ocean

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Film set illusion*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Orla, Sid; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Orla built a cinematic ocean from blue cardboard. Sid, the director, floated banana boats on its flat waves. An actor stepped through the sheet and got stuck. Orla caught his tail and pulled him back. Sid liked the dramatic whirlpool, but Orla refused another fall. She filmed a toy boat sinking into a safe bowl instead. The audience believed the storm. Orla folded the ocean into storage, and Sid paid for a real railing around every hole before the next scene.
