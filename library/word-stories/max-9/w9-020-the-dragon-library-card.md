# The dragon library card

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Dragon hoard*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Ivy, Mel; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ivy climbed into a dragon cave to fetch a stolen book. Mel waited outside with breakfast. The dragon curled round an enormous heap of books but could not turn a page with its claws. Ivy used her tail to hold the book open. Mel brought the banana bowl inside. The dragon lent them the book after the last page. Ivy made it a library card. Its first fine covered three scorched pages and one melted spoon.
