# The kite contest anchor

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Flight contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Lena, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lena flew a splendid kite shaped like a banana. Gus tied a pineapple to its tail for balance. The kite never left the ground. Lena cut the string holding the pineapple with a shell and gave the fruit to the judges. Her kite rose above the trees. Gus held the loose string in his own tail and ran beneath it. The judges awarded best flight and best snack to different monkeys. Gus asked for another prize for carrying the heaviest kite tail.
