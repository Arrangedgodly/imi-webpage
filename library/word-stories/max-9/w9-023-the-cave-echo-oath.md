# The cave echo oath

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Sacred quest oath*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Hugo, Mae; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Hugo swore to bring a treasure home from the cave. Mae heard the echo swear the same oath. They carried bananas through the dark. At a fork Hugo asked which way to go. His echo shouted both ways. Mae carefully tapped each wall with her tail. One wall rang like a drum. Behind it lay a chest full of bells. They took one home. The echo kept the rest and rang them whenever it felt lonely.
