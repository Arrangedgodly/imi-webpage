# The election banana ink

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Ballot design*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Gail, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail printed ballots with banana ink for the official election. Bo dried them across branches. Rain washed away every candidate name. The committee still wanted voting by dusk. Gail used her tail to pin plain leaves beside the box and asked voters to write a name. Bo counted them aloud. One leaf voted for rain. Gail set it aside, then found twelve more. The town elected a gardener who promised a roof over the next ballot line. Rain got no office.
