# The pie crust disguise

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Pie contest*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-003*
*Cast: Gail, Rex; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Gail entered a splendid banana pie in the fair. Rex baked a chocolate cake and hid it beneath a thin crust. The judge cut both pies. Gail saw brown crumbs on the knife and objected. Rex claimed cake was only thick pie filling. Gail wrapped her tail round the rule sheet and read it aloud. Pies needed a top and a bottom crust. Rex added a crust beneath his cake. The judge accepted it and required a much larger fork.
