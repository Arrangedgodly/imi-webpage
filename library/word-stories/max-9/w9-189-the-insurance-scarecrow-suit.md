# The insurance scarecrow suit

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Liability*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Rose, Bo; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose dressed a scarecrow in her official insurance suit to guard bananas. Bo saw it and filed a claim for a broken branch. Rose explained that straw could not approve payment. Bo asked why it wore the badge. She took the badge back with her tail and checked his branch. Wind had broken it, as his contract covered. Rose paid the claim. Bo gave the scarecrow a plain hat. Birds returned, so Rose hired Bo to guard fruit in plain clothes.
