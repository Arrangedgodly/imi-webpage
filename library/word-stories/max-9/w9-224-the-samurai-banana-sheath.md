# The samurai banana sheath

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Sword duel*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Ari, Poe; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ari faced Poe in a dangerous duel beneath an enormous banana tree. Ari reached for his sword, but the sheath held only fruit. The cook had swapped it for a lunch tube. Poe laughed and lowered his blade. Ari peeled the banana with his tail and offered half. Poe accepted, then admitted his sword was wooden. They had both come to look brave rather than fight. The cook fetched the real sword to cut bread. Ari carried the fruit sheath home and left the blade in the kitchen.
