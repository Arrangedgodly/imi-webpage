# The testimony tail curtain

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Legal stagecraft*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Sia, Tom; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Sia built a curtain behind the courtroom witness seat. Tom said it would make testimony seem serious. Sia pulled it open with her tail to reveal the witness and his banana snack. The audience clapped. The judge ordered silence. Tom lowered the curtain at the wrong moment, hiding the witness before his answer. Sia took the rope away and left the curtain open. The witness answered plainly. Tom got a job at the theater, where his official duty was waiting for the final line.
