# The knight laundry flag

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Gallant knight*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Lio, Nan; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Lio tied a splendid flag to his lance and rode toward the foe. Nan noticed it was her bedsheet. She chased him through the banana grove, holding his tail to slow him. A messenger met them at the border and mistook the sheet for a peace flag. Both armies stopped to talk. Nan demanded soap before any treaty. The generals washed her sheet in the stream. Lio went home with peace and a lance full of wet socks.
