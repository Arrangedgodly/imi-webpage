# The royal dungeon window

*Format: Children's story parody*
*Parody target: Prison escape*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-001*
*Cast: Ira, Hope; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Ira got locked in a dungeon for eating royal bananas. Hope brought an enormous file hidden in breakfast. Ira used it on the bars until his tail ached. Then Hope noticed the window had no glass, only thin bars across the lower half. Ira climbed over them and dropped into the garden. He returned the file to the cook. The king ordered higher bars. Hope planted a banana tree beneath them and sent Ira a ladder bill.
