# The romantic apology bench

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Romantic repair*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-005*
*Cast: Bea, Gus; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea promised Gus a beautiful apology after missing dinner. She carved a splendid bench in the banana grove and wrote sorry across its seat. Gus sat before the paint dried. Bea wiped his coat with her tail, making the stain wider. He laughed and asked for dinner instead. They ate on the ground while the bench dried. Bea repainted the word on a sign above it, where no coat could touch. Gus used the bench next day and found a meal basket waiting beneath it.
