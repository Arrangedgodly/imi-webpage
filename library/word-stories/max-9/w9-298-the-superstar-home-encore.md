# The superstar home encore

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Encore ritual*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-006*
*Cast: Bea, Hal; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Bea called for the superstar encore after the banana concert. Hal had already packed the sound box and gone home. The audience clapped longer. Bea followed his tracks with her tail carrying the stage lamp. Hal sat at his kitchen table, eating an enormous supper. Bea asked for one more song. He sang quietly while washing his bowl. She recorded it with his consent and played it outside the hall. The audience left satisfied, and Hal finished supper without putting his stage coat back on.
