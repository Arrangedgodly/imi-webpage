# The campaign soup spoon

*Format: Story parody*
*Parody target: Election campaign*
*By: IMI Nine Letter Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-W9-004*
*Cast: Rose, Dax; all fictional monkeys*

## Story

Rose promised a spoon for every monkey in her campaign. Dax ordered spoons from a corporate supplier. The town had no soup bowls. Rose served banana mash on leaves, but the spoons tore them. Dax held a leaf flat with his tail while Rose tried again. The town voted her out and kept the spoons. She opened a bowl stall across from the council. Dax handled orders. Every new policy lunch brought her more trade than the campaign ever had.
