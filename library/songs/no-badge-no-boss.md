# No Badge, No Boss

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: fast punk rebellion and scrappy three-chord defiance*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
The guard said, "Show your badge before you sit."  
Mine fell apart; I'd chewed a bit.  
The door went beep, the lights went red.  
I held my lunch above my head.  
The manager spoke of policy and grace.  
I asked if grace had a locker space.  
The scanner blinked; the scanner froze.  
The whole shift smelled like burnt toast.  

## Chorus
No badge, no boss, no beep in my ear.  
We clock our own lunch break here.  
No badge, no boss, we kick the door.  
It opens out, then locks once more.  

## Verse 2
We took the stairs and found a mop.  
It leaned like it had had enough.  
The break room clock ran backward fast.  
We shouted, "Make this minute last!"  
The printer spat a page that said, "Please wait."  
We taped it to the fire escape gate.  
The night crew brought a tambourine.  
We made the loudest quiet scene.  

## Chorus
No badge, no boss, no beep in my ear.  
We clock our own lunch break here.  
No badge, no boss, we kick the door.  
It opens out, then locks once more.  

## Bridge
The guard came back without his cap.  
He said the scanner needed a nap.  
We passed him half a sandwich crust.  
He sat beside us, shook off dust.  
The manager unplugged the red light sign.  
We drew a lunch break on the line.  
No one got fired, no one got rich.  
We fixed the scanner with a switch.  

## Final chorus
No badge, no boss, but bring your name.  
We'll share the room and share the blame.  
No badge, no boss, no beep in my ear.  
We clock our own lunch break here.  
No badge, no boss, the door swings wide.  
The guard can sit on the inside.  
The printer rests; the mop stands tall.  
We're back to work, together, all.  

## Ending
The night rolled on, the coffee cooled.  
The scanner blinked, then acted fooled.  
We punched the clock and took our pay.  
The lunch bell rang the punk rock way.  

*Performance note: Keep the guitars rough and the chorus short enough for a tired night crew to shout.*
