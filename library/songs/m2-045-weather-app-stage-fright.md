# The Weather App Has Stage Fright

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: earnest singer-songwriter confessionals and the fragile bedroom-recording aesthetic*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Intimate acoustic guitar with audible room noise; each interruption should be treated as part of the take.*

Verse 1
I wrote a little song at dawn beneath my laundry line,  
The sock clipped to the window made a steady tambourine.  
I whispered every lyric like the walls could hear my doubt,  
The weather app said "cloudy" and then loudly read it out.  
I stopped the take, I cleared my throat, I found a quieter key,  
A monkey in the next room said, "The forecast sounds like me."  

Chorus
This is my room, this is my truth,  
This is a lamp with a crooked tooth.  
I sing one note, the floorboards sigh,  
The weather app replies, "Rain by five."  
I'll leave it in; it's part of the song,  
The quiet gets interrupted all along.  
This is my room, this is my truth,  
And someone just microwaved soup.  

Verse 2
I tried a take at midnight, soft enough to wake the cat,  
The cat walked on the keyboard and recorded four bars flat.  
My cousin said the silence was the strongest part by far,  
Then plugged a little fan in and blew dust across the guitar.  
I saved the scratch recording; its roughness felt sincere,  
Until the file was named "Untitled Final Final New Version Here."  

Bridge
The neighbor drills a shelf; I hold the note in place,  
The kettle starts a harmony and steams across my face.  
I sing the chorus once again, the window starts to rain,  
The app says, "Chance of weather," and I thank it for the refrain.

Ending
I post the take with every buzz and clatter left intact,  
The comments say the soup sound is the most authentic track.
