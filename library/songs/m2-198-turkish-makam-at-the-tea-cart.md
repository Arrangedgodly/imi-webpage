# The Tea Cart's Long Note

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: makam melodic ornament, microtonal bends, and a tea vendor stretching one order into an epic musical exchange*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Use a plucked lute and graceful bends; let the kettle whistle answer the melody.*

[Verse 1]
A vendor pushed his tea cart through the courtyard after noon,
A gibbon asked for mint tea; he replied in rising tune.
The kettle gave a whistle that curved around the square,
The vendor bent one note so long the cups began to stare.

[Refrain]
Pour it warm, pour it slow, let the little kettle sing,
Turn the note and turn the tray; make the teaspoons ring.
Pour it warm, pour it slow, let the courtyard hear,
One small cup can take the afternoon from here.

[Verse 2]
A second guest said, "Less mint." The vendor shaped the phrase;
The lute replied in ornaments that wandered through the haze.
The tea went cold while all the guests admired the turn;
The kettle steamed a quicker line to make the teacups yearn.

[Refrain]
Pour it warm, pour it slow, let the little kettle sing,
Turn the note and turn the tray; make the teaspoons ring.
Pour it warm, pour it slow, let the courtyard hear,
One small cup can take the afternoon from here.

[Bridge]
He poured at last; the cup was full, the note came to a rest.
The guest asked for a refill; the lute began its quest.
The vendor turned the cart to leave, still humming down the lane;
The kettle whistled from behind and called him back again.

[Final Refrain]
Pour it warm, pour it slow, let the little kettle sing,
Turn the note and turn the tray; make the teaspoons ring.
Pour it warm, pour it slow, let the courtyard hear,
The tea is hot, the song is done, the cart is staying here.

[Ending]
The guests drank in silence. The kettle kept the last note.
