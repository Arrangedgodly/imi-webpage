# Gospel Choir at the Car Wash

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel call-and-response, soaring choir entries, and an overconfident soloist seeking a testimony*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Organ, handclaps, and a rising choir; foam cannons answer the soloist's calls.*

Verse 1
The mandrill drove his dusty car into the wash at nine,
He raised both hands and cried, "This soap will make me shine!"
The organ struck a chord; the foam began to rise,
The attendant ducked behind the till to guard the price.

Chorus
Wash it clean, let the brushes roll,
Send that mud beyond the toll.
Call out loud and answer bright,
Let the suds lift up the night.

Verse 2
The choir called, "How clean?" He said, "The cleanest in the land!"
The brushes spun around him like a gospel marching band.
He testified to every scratch and claimed each one was gone,
The dryer blew his little paper testimony on.

Chorus
Wash it clean, let the brushes roll,
Send that mud beyond the toll.
Call out loud and answer bright,
Let the suds lift up the night.

Bridge
The gibbon manager called, "The cycle's done, come through!"
The mandrill cried, "One more refrain; my shine is not yet true!"
The choir sang an amen; the conveyor rolled him back,
He emerged in foam and confetti with a coupon on his back.

Verse 3
He strutted to the exit, but the hose began to sing,
The choir took its cue and made a chorus of the spray.
He bowed before the vacuum; it swallowed up his tie,
The attendant sold the tie back as a souvenir to buy.

Chorus
Wash it clean, let the brushes roll,
Send that mud beyond the toll.
Call out loud and answer bright,
Let the suds lift up the night.

Ending
He left without his tie but with a polished shopping cart;
The choir baptized his rear wheel and billed him by the quart.
