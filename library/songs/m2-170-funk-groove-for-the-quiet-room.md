# Funk Groove for the Quiet Room

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: funk bass showcases, synchronized band breaks, and a bass player who makes every household sound part of the groove*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Syncopated bass and tight horn stabs; let the squeaking chair become a recurring rhythmic hook.*

Verse 1
The band was told to play a quiet tune at noon,
The bass player brought his amp and tuned it like a spoon.
A capuchin sat down and made the chair go squeak,
The bass found that small rhythm and began to speak.

Chorus
Squeak, bump, slide to the side,
Let that bassline take a ride.
Squeak, bump, keep it neat,
Quiet room got a funky beat.

Verse 2
The drummer tapped a pencil; the horn section joined,
The chair became the lead sound and the bassline coin.
The librarian came in and asked for lower volume,
The band played softer but the squeak began to bloom.

Chorus
Squeak, bump, slide to the side,
Let that bassline take a ride.
Squeak, bump, keep it neat,
Quiet room got a funky beat.

Bridge
The chair was pulled away; the groove began to fade,
The singer found a squeak inside the folding shade.
They opened up the shade; the window made a click,
The band used every sound but kept the volume slick.

Verse 3
The librarian joined in with one small finger snap,
The whole room moved together without a single clap.
The bass played low enough for pages to stay still,
The chair was fixed with felt and lost its squeaky thrill.

Chorus
Squeak, bump, slide to the side,
Let that bassline take a ride.
Squeak, bump, keep it neat,
Quiet room got a funky beat.

Ending
They left the chair in silence, but the groove stayed in the hall,
The bass player returned at dusk to play one note for all.
