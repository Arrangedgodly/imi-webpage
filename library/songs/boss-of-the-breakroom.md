# Boss of the Breakroom

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: boastful rap braggadocio and luxury-flex imagery*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
I roll in at ten with a mug from the fair.  
My badge has a scratch and remarkable flair.  
I own the good spoon; I control the tea.  
The vending machine recognizes me.  
I stack two napkins like a downtown tower.  
I microwave soup with executive power.  
My chair has one wheel that squeaks on the beat.  
I slide to the fridge with a champion's feet.  

## Hook
Breakroom boss, put respect on the plate.  
I label my leftovers and secure the gate.  
My lunch is a legend, my fork is chrome.  
I keep one tiny hot sauce bottle at home.  

## Verse 2
I negotiated Monday's last vanilla yogurt.  
The lid came loose; I still kept order.  
I traded two crackers for half a lime.  
My deal closed early, right on time.  
The copier jammed; I handled the scene.  
I shook the tray like a slot machine.  
I got one page and a paper cut.  
The interns yelled, "Boss, you're never shut up."  

## Hook
Breakroom boss, put respect on the plate.  
I label my leftovers and secure the gate.  
My lunch is a legend, my fork is chrome.  
I keep one tiny hot sauce bottle at home.  

## Bridge
Then a new hire brought a casserole pan.  
The smell filled the room; I revised my plan.  
I offered my spoon, my chair, and my lime.  
She said, "Take a bowl, there's more this time."  
I tried to act cool, but burned my tongue.  
The whole room laughed as the microwave sung.  
I passed the napkins down the line.  
My throne became a table, and that felt fine.  

## Final hook
Breakroom boss, bring a plate to the crew.  
The biggest flex is there's enough for you.  
My lunch is a legend when shared at home.  
I guard no fork; I brought my own.  
Breakroom boss, let the hot sauce ride.  
We save one bowl for whoever walks inside.  
The copier can jam; the soup can spill.  
We eat together, and the room stands still.  

## Ending
I left my badge beside the door.  
The casserole pan came back once more.  
The fridge hummed low, the clock ran late.  
I washed the spoon and closed the gate.  

*Performance note: Deliver the verses like an absurdly expensive victory lap, then soften when the casserole arrives.*
