# Dueling Dance Cards

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: ballroom competition medleys and judge-table etiquette*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Alternate tango pauses with salsa brass; judges hold up numbered cards only at the end.*

## Verse 1
At the Civic Hall, the dance card filled by seven.  
A pair of lemurs practiced turns beneath the exit sign.  
Their rivals brought a fan and practiced looks.  
The bandleader shuffled charts and checked the hooks.  
The tango couple stepped as if the floor might crack.  
The salsa pair sent laughter traveling back.  
The judges sat with clipboards straight and stern.  
A ceiling fan kept making every ribbon turn.  

## Chorus
Take your step, then give them space.  
A good dance leaves room for another face.  
Take your step, don't own the floor.  
The band can always play one measure more.  

## Verse 2
The first pair bowed and cleared the center line.  
The next pair entered right on time.  
A beginner lost a shoe, then found the beat.  
The audience made a path for her retreat.  
She tied the lace and rejoined the set.  
The judges smiled, though none had scored her yet.  
The band switched styles before the final round.  
Every pair made room when new feet touched the ground.  

## Bridge
The scores came in; the rivals tied at eight.  
They split the ribbon by the side-stage gate.  
The fan became a prop for both to share.  
They danced one last turn, equal in the glare.  

## Chorus
Take your step, then give them space.  
A good dance leaves room for another face.  
Take your step, don't own the floor.  
The band can always play one measure more.  

## Ending
They folded up the cards and put the chairs away.  
The band played freely on the walk home through the day.
