# The Protest Singer Petitions for a Sound Check

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: righteous folk protest concerts and procedural campaigns for the unglamorous equipment that makes a voice audible*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Insistent acoustic strumming and stomps; the first petition is sung without a microphone, with a group response.*

[Verse 1]
I came to sing for everyone beneath the market clock,  
I set my guitar on its strap and stood beside the stock.  
The microphone was silent and the speaker gave no sound,  
The people heard the pigeons and the carts that rolled around.  
I called for change so fiercely that the fruit stall turned to see,  
The fruit stall had a price board larger than my plea.  
I wrote a little petition with a pen I found that day,  
"Please test the microphone before you send the crowd away."  

[Chorus]
Raise your voice, sign your name,  
Let the switch receive the blame.  
Hear us now across the square,  
Our demands need power somewhere.  
Raise your voice, join the line,  
The technician gets a copy when he's got the time.  

[Verse 2]
The merchant signed the paper and the baker signed it too,  
The technician read the heading while he checked what he could do.  
He found the mixer muted from the morning's spoken show,  
He opened up the channel and the little lamps began to glow.  
My voice reached every corner with the testing word of "One,"  
The pigeons left the fountain when the loud response was done.  
I thanked him through the microphone and held the paper high,  
The baker asked if signing meant that he could have a try.  

[Chorus]
Raise your voice, sign your name,  
Let the switch receive the blame.  
Hear us now across the square,  
Our demands need power somewhere.  
Raise your voice, join the line,  
The technician gets a copy when he's got the time.  

[Bridge]
I sang the songs I'd brought along about a better town,  
The baker stayed to listen after closing shutters down.  
We left the signed petition on the technician's chair,  
He wrote "Sound check at nine next week" and pinned it by the stair.  

[Final Chorus]
Raise your voice, sign your name,  
Next week's sound check has a claim.  
Hear us now across the square,  
The mixer waits; the technician's there.  
Raise your voice, join the line,  
The baker's brought a second verse about the price of twine.
