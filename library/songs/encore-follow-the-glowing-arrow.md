# Follow the Glowing Arrow

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Novelty dance songs and repetitive emergency evacuation instructions*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Bouncy hand percussion and a call-and-response leader; keep the safety steps clear.*

[Call]  
When the alarm goes beep-beep, what do we do?  
[Response]  
Stop the dance, take a breath, let the guide lead you.  

[Verse 1]  
The monkeys in the breakroom were rehearsing for the show.  
The dance captain dropped the clipboard. Nobody followed him.  
A floor guide raised an arrow. "Walk this way, along the wall."  
The troop filed out together, leaving cups beside the stall.  
One monkey tried a spin-step. The guide said, "Keep it plain."  
He saved his fancy footwork for the open yard again.  

[Chorus]  
Follow the glowing arrow, walk, don't run.  
Stay with your floor guide till the count is done.  
Leave your bag and mug behind. Keep the exit lane in view.  
When the alarm says move along, that's what we do.  

[Verse 2]  
The manager came hustling with a laptop tucked inside.  
The guide said, "Leave the laptop. Come and stand beside the sign."  
A visitor was missing, so they told the marshal's crew.  
They didn't re-enter to retrieve a jacket or a shoe.  
The all-clear came from wardens, not a text alert or guess.  
Then everyone went back in, no one ran ahead to press.  

[Chorus]  
Follow the glowing arrow, walk, don't run.  
Stay with your floor guide till the count is done.  
Leave your bag and mug behind. Keep the exit lane in view.  
When the alarm says move along, that's what we do.  

[Bridge]  
No moonwalk through the stairwell. Keep your hands free, face ahead.  
The safety guide is speaking. Hear the words that they have said.  
A dance can wait till later. A head count comes right now.  
The captain ties her tap shoes and takes a quiet bow.  

[Final Chorus]  
Follow the glowing arrow, walk, don't run.  
Stay with your floor guide till the count is done.  
Leave your bag and mug behind. Keep the exit lane in view.  
When the alarm says move along, that's what we do.  

[Outro]  
At the next drill, the monkey troop knows where to go.  
They save the dance for after, when the wardens say it's so.
