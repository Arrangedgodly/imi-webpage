# Concrete Forecast

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: UK grime clashes and stark tower-block storytelling*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Sparse electronic pulse, clipped delivery, and a crew answering from the stairwell.*

## Verse 1
Rain hit the estate like coins against a tray.  
A young gelada watched the clouds erase the day.  
Her bus card cracked beside the corner shop.  
She rapped a route while waiting for the rain to stop.  
The beat came from a phone with one chipped side.  
Her cousin made the bass with a bin lid wide.  
No studio, no lights, no borrowed chain to show.  
Just names of every stairwell that the whole crew knows.  

## Chorus
Concrete forecast, cold wind, hard ground.  
We put our own weather in the sound.  
Concrete forecast, grey sky, bright eye.  
The block writes its truth while the clouds roll by.  

## Verse 2
The corner shopkeeper nodded through the glass.  
He knew each kid by nickname, not by class.  
A cyclist cut the corner, splashed their shoes.  
The crew made that mistake a hook they couldn't lose.  
One verse named the leak above the upstairs bed.  
One named the landlord's letter nobody had read.  
They took the mic to the youth club Friday night.  
A dozen mums came early, saved the front row right.  

## Bridge
The host asked for bravado, fists and flashing rings.  
The lead said, "We brought facts and a few true things."  
The crowd went quiet, then the stairwell crew replied.  
Every hand rose level, no one had to hide.  

## Chorus
Concrete forecast, cold wind, hard ground.  
We put our own weather in the sound.  
Concrete forecast, grey sky, bright eye.  
The block writes its truth while the clouds roll by.  

## Ending
The rain let up; the bus arrived late.  
They took the long way home and kept the beat straight.
