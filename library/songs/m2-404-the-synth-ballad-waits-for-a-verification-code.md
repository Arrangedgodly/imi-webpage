# The Synth Ballad Waits for a Verification Code

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: yearning synth-pop ballads about distance and unanswered messages, focused on a late login verification code*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Warm synthesizer pads, gated snare and aching vocals; each delayed notification triggers a hopeful chord change.*

[Verse 1]
I lit the room in violet to record our song tonight,  
The session on my laptop asked me first to prove my right.  
It sent a little number to the phone beside my hand,  
I watched its empty screen as though it held a distant land.  
My drummer waited patiently with sticks across his knees,  
The bassist said to check the bars; the room had only three.  
A message lit the silence and I reached with open palms,  
It was my aunt requesting photographs of all my plants.  

[Chorus]
Come to me, light my screen,  
You are six small numbers somewhere far beyond this scene.  
Answer me, cross the night,  
The session cannot open till your digits reach the light.  
Come to me, do not fade,  
The whole band knows the chorus of the login serenade.  

[Verse 2]
I pressed the send-again request and paced beside the door,  
The drummer tapped the table for a hopeful count of four.  
The first code came a minute late; I entered it with care,  
The newer one had canceled it; rejection filled the air.  
The bassist held the phone aloft beside the kitchen pane,  
The second code arrived at last, then third arrived again.  
I waited till the messages were sorted in a row,  
The newest one unlocked the song; the drummer whispered, "Go."  

[Chorus]
Come to me, light my screen,  
You are six small numbers somewhere far beyond this scene.  
Answer me, cross the night,  
The session cannot open till your digits reach the light.  
Come to me, do not fade,  
The whole band knows the chorus of the login serenade.  

[Bridge]
We played the song in one good take and saved it on the drive,  
My aunt had sent another note; the plants were still alive.  
I sent their photograph at last and put the phone aside,  
The producer called for playback; we were finally inside.  

[Final Chorus]
Come to me, light my screen,  
The producer wants the yearning that the waiting put in me.  
Answer me, cross the night,  
We leave the little message sound beneath the final light.  
Come to me, do not fade,  
My aunt has heard the ballad and requests a plant parade.
