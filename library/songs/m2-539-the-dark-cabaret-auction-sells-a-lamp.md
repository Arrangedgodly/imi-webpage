# The Dark Cabaret Auction Sells a Lamp

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dark cabaret performers making sinister promises about a perfectly ordinary secondhand light fixture*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Minor-key piano, theatrical whisper and a slow refrain; the buyer checks the lamp switch during the bridge.*

[Verse 1]
A lamp stood in the auction hall beneath a velvet shade,  
I sang about the secrets that its yellow light had made.  
It once had lit a hallway in the flat across the lane,  
I called it an enchanted eye that knew the city's pain.  
The auction clerk described the cord and little switch instead,  
The buyer asked if it had been rewired, as the ticket said.  
I sang about its haunted glow and memories of night,  
The clerk plugged in the cord to show an ordinary light.  

[Refrain]
Glow, little witness, glow through the gloom,  
The lamp knows where the hallway is inside the sitting room.  
Glow, little witness, let the shadows fall,  
The buyer needs a reading light, not secrets on the wall.  
Soft shade, long cord, hold the hidden flame,  
The maker's little label has an entirely normal name.  

[Verse 2]
The buyer read the label and she checked the swivel head,  
She wanted it beside a chair to help the books she read.  
The clerk switched off the lamp before the bidding could begin,  
I sang a low lament about the darkness held within.  
The buyer made one modest bid; another raised it two,  
She raised hers one more time and got the lamp as she meant to do.  
I helped her carry it outside and kept the cord rolled tight,  
She said the lamp would have a new and very useful night.  

[Refrain]
Glow, little witness, glow through the gloom,  
The lamp knows where the hallway is inside the sitting room.  
Glow, little witness, let the shadows fall,  
The buyer needs a reading light, not secrets on the wall.  
Soft shade, long cord, hold the hidden flame,  
The maker's little label has an entirely normal name.  

[Bridge]
She tested it at home beside the chair she'd meant to use,  
It lit the little pages of a book of local news.  
She sent the auction clerk a photograph of where it stood,  
I wrote another verse about the darkness in the wood.  

[Final Refrain]
Glow, little witness, let the shadows fall,  
The buyer has a bright lamp and no shadow on her wall.  
Soft shade, long cord, hold the hidden flame,  
She hums my haunted chorus but gives the lamp its normal name.  
Glow, little witness, glow through the gloom,  
The clerk hires me to sing about the vacuum in the next room.
