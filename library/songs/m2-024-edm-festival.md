# Hands Up for the Water Truck

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: EDM festival buildup, drops, and crowd-control production*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Build with filter sweeps and snare rolls, but leave space for the real water truck announcement.*

## Verse 1
The festival field had melted into dust.  
A ring-tailed lemur wore a neon vest and trust.  
The DJ raised both hands and held the crowd.  
The speakers teased a drop that promised loud.  
Then someone shouted, "Water truck is coming through!"  
The DJ cut the bass and pointed where to queue.  
The crowd made room, a corridor of light.  
The truck rolled gently through the orange night.  

## Chorus
Hands up, then clear the lane.  
Make a path for the water train.  
Hands up, let the cool spray rise.  
The best drop comes from the sky.  

## Verse 2
The driver wore earplugs, steady at the wheel.  
A volunteer waved flags to guide each turn with zeal.  
The crowd took cups and stepped aside.  
The bass returned in waves as people dried.  
The headliner built a tower out of sound.  
The whole field bounced upon the trembling ground.  
The lemur checked the exits, kept a route in sight.  
He danced again when all the rows were right.  

## Bridge
A snare roll rose, then stopped before the blast.  
The DJ thanked the crew who filled the tanks so fast.  
The lights came up; the crowd could see each face.  
The drop arrived with room enough for space.  

## Chorus
Hands up, then clear the lane.  
Make a path for the water train.  
Hands up, let the cool spray rise.  
The best drop comes from the sky.  

## Ending
At closing, every cup went in the bin.  
The truck went home; the music faded thin.
