# The Romantic Crooner Feeds the Parking Meter

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: suave romantic crooning that treats every passing minute as precious, while the singer's parked car demands those minutes in coins*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Gentle swing piano and warm brass; the parking meter's coin clink interrupts the tender phrases exactly on the offbeat.*

[Verse 1]
I met you at a little café where the afternoon could shine,  
I said that every moment was a jewel within our time.  
The waiter brought our coffee and I reached to touch your hand,  
My phone announced the parking time was reaching its last stand.  
I said I'd be a moment and I stepped out to the street,  
The meter took my little coins with clicks upon the beat.  
I came back to the table with a very tender bow,  
You said the precious moments were more expensive now.  

[Chorus]
Stay a while, let the day go slow,  
There is nothing but our hearts and an hourly fee to know.  
Stay a while, let the world be kind,  
The meter has a metal heart that will not let me lose time.  
Soft smile, warm cup, hold the afternoon,  
I shall feed it one more coin before the next romantic tune.  

[Verse 2]
We spoke of distant travels and the little things we'd seen,  
The phone gave out another warning halfway through a dream.  
You said the car was running this rather lovely date,  
I said the meter's appetite was growing very great.  
We finished off our coffee and we walked toward the square,  
I moved the car to parking with a flat fee waiting there.  
We strolled along the riverside without another call,  
The metal heart had finally stopped conducting it all.  

[Chorus]
Stay a while, let the day go slow,  
There is nothing but our hearts and an hourly fee to know.  
Stay a while, let the world be kind,  
The meter has a metal heart that will not let me lose time.  
Soft smile, warm cup, hold the afternoon,  
I shall feed it one more coin before the next romantic tune.  

[Bridge]
You wrote a little verse about a date without a car,  
I took the train to meet you at the next café not far.  
The clock inside the station told us when the trains would go,  
We chose a later one and let the loving evening grow.  

[Final Chorus]
Stay a while, let the day go slow,  
The parking meter keeps the coins I gave it long ago.  
Soft smile, warm cup, hold the afternoon,  
The train is leaving later; we can finish our whole tune.  
Stay a while, let the world be kind,  
You tuck the folded timetable beneath your cup and mine.
