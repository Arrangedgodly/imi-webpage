# The Robot's Charleston

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: electro-swing, vintage dance steps over a modern beat, and a cafe robot that keeps misreading orders as choreography*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Mix brushed jazz percussion with a bright electronic beat; let the robot's service chime become the hook.*

[Verse 1]
At the robot cafe, the gibbon swung in time,
A brushed snare met the synthesizer's bright old-fashioned rhyme.
The service bot approached with trays of tea and pie,
It spun a Charleston circle and then tilted to the side.

[Chorus]
Swing it, beep it, step to the left,
Robot's dancing with a tray in its chest.
Swing it, beep it, glide through the room,
The coffee's getting cold but the floor's got room.
Swing it, beep it, step to the left,
Robot's dancing with a tray in its chest.
Swing it, beep it, glide through the room,
The coffee's getting cold but the floor's got room.

[Verse 2]
A macaque ordered cappuccino, one sugar and a spoon,
The robot heard "Charleston" and spun beneath the moon.
It balanced all the cups while swinging on one wheel,
The barista marked the order with a jazzy little squeal.

[Chorus]
Swing it, beep it, step to the left,
Robot's dancing with a tray in its chest.
Swing it, beep it, glide through the room,
The coffee's getting cold but the floor's got room.
Swing it, beep it, step to the left,
Robot's dancing with a tray in its chest.
Swing it, beep it, glide through the room,
The coffee's getting cold but the floor's got room.

[Bridge]
The bot's alarm said "Order up," then played a trumpet line,
The customers all answered with a heel-toe turn in time.
It served each drink correctly, but the cups were on parade;
The manager joined the chorus and forgot to call last order.

[Final Chorus]
Swing it, beep it, step to the left,
Robot's dancing with a tray in its chest.
Swing it, beep it, slow down the room,
The coffee's now delivered; save the dance for noon.
Swing it, beep it, step to the left,
Robot's dancing with a tray in its chest.
Swing it, beep it, glide through the room,
The cafe keeps on swinging with a little more room.

[Ending]
The robot charged the bill in eight-counts, not in cash.
