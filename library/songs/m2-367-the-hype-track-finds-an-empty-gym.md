# The Hype Track Finds an Empty Gym

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: athletic hype songs that demand collective triumph from people doing unrelated exercises*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Aggressive brass, stomping drums and shouted responses; allow the lone cleaner to answer without enthusiasm.*

[Verse 1]
I came before the sunrise with a jersey and a dream,  
The lobby speakers promised I could lead a winning team.  
I slapped my hands together and I called for everyone,  
The cleaner raised his mop and said the day had not begun.  
I yelled about the mountain we would conquer by the night,  
He pointed to the treadmill and requested shoes wiped right.  

[Chorus]
Who's with me? Who will rise?  
Put that fire inside your eyes.  
Break the barrier, win the day,  
Move the laundry trolley safely out the way.  
Who's with me? Make some noise,  
The cleaner hasn't joined the team but has a mop of choice.  

[Verse 2]
A visitor arrived to do a little gentle stretch,  
I asked him for a battle cry, he looked at me quite vexed.  
Another came to pedal while she read the morning news,  
I called her our left winger; she refused the role I'd choose.  
I jogged between the machines and sang about our golden goal,  
The cleaner blocked my victory lap to keep the mop in control.  

[Chorus]
Who's with me? Who will rise?  
Put that fire inside your eyes.  
Break the barrier, win the day,  
Move the laundry trolley safely out the way.  
Who's with me? Make some noise,  
The cleaner hasn't joined the team but has a mop of choice.  

[Bridge]
He asked me to lift buckets to the cupboard by the door,  
I called the job a team drill and I carried nearly four.  
The visitors helped move the cart; the floor was clear at last,  
I raised my fists above my head and praised our mighty cast.  

[Final Chorus]
Who's with me? Who will rise?  
Put that fire inside your eyes.  
Break the barrier, win the day,  
The cleaner hands a brush to me and gently walks away.  
Who's with me? Victory's near,  
I cheer with a scrubbing brush; the cleaner's disappeared.
