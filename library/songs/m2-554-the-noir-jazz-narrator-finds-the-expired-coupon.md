# The Noir Jazz Narrator Finds the Expired Coupon

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jazz-noir story songs treating a commonplace receipt-wallet search as a hardboiled investigation*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Walking bass, muted brass and a half-spoken narrator; the shop clerk supplies the anticlimactic date.*

[Spoken Verse 1]
The city had a secret in the pocket of my coat,  
A coupon for a sandwich, with a very little note.  
The clerk at the counter said he couldn't take the deal,  
I watched his face for clues about the truth he might conceal.  
He pointed to the date line at the bottom of the square,  
The coupon had expired last month; the answer waited there.  
I lit no cigarette, I bought a coffee for the scene,  
The case was getting colder; my sandwich stayed unseen.  

[Refrain]
Midnight paper, small reward,  
The coupon has a date that the detective has ignored.  
Midnight paper, price to pay,  
The bargain took the early train and left before today.  
Low brass, dark coat, let the little shadows creep,  
The sandwich costs the usual price, however close I keep.  

[Spoken Verse 2]
I searched the little wallet for another printed clue,  
A newer coupon waited with a discount still quite true.  
The clerk checked all its dates and rang the sandwich through,  
I paid the smaller price for it, as customers could do.  
I sat beside the window where the rain began to fall,  
The sandwich was the answer to the mystery of it all.  
The clerk brought me a napkin for the crumbs upon my knee,  
He said the coupon's date was plain to anyone but me.  

[Refrain]
Midnight paper, small reward,  
The coupon has a date that the detective has ignored.  
Midnight paper, price to pay,  
The bargain took the early train and left before today.  
Low brass, dark coat, let the little shadows creep,  
The sandwich costs the usual price, however close I keep.  

[Bridge]
I put the old coupon in a folder marked "Case Closed,"  
The newer one was used and had no secrets left to pose.  
The clerk heard my recording on the shop's small radio,  
He asked if noir detectives ever read the dates below.  

[Final Refrain]
Midnight paper, small reward,  
The clerk has got a new advert upon the sandwich board.  
Low brass, dark coat, let the little shadows creep,  
I read the date before the price and take the meal to keep.  
Midnight paper, price to pay,  
The napkin has a voucher; I investigate it straight away.
