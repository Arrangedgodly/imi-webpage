# The Club Remix Answers the Wrong Number

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dance remixes of found speech and producers who sample a mundane telephone call into a huge emotional hook*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: House beat with chopped spoken phrases; spoken opening and closing are dry, while every hook repeats to the beat.*

[Spoken Intro]
Hello? Is this the dental clinic?  
No, you've reached the studio.  
I'm calling to move Thursday.  
Thursday what?  
My appointment. At two.  
I'm afraid we cannot do that.  

[Verse 1]
The producer had a telephone beside the mixing board,  
A caller rang the studio while he was cutting chords.  
She wanted her appointment moved from Thursday afternoon,  
The wrong last digit brought her to a very different tune.  
He asked her if her words could be a little vocal part,  
She said her daughter loved to dance and gave him leave to start.  
He wrote her number on a card and checked her chosen name,  
She called the clinic back while he built the music frame.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Move Thursday, move Thursday,  
I cannot do that, I cannot do that.  
At two, at two, at two, at two,  
The beat can keep an appointment with you.  
Wrong number, right sound,  
The appointment goes up while the bass comes down.  

[Verse 2]
He cut the phrases into time and added bright piano,  
He kept the "dental clinic" in a little spoken shadow.  
The club began to dance beneath a colored ceiling fan,  
He called her back to play the track according to his plan.  
She said she'd moved the booking to a Monday just at ten,  
He asked her for a second take to use the news again.  
She told him he could use it if he put her name in print,  
He made a new recording of her very helpful hint.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Move Thursday, move Thursday,  
I cannot do that, I cannot do that.  
At two, at two, at two, at two,  
The beat can keep an appointment with you.  
Wrong number, right sound,  
The appointment goes up while the bass comes down.  

[Breakdown]
Monday, Monday, Monday at ten,  
The dentist sees the patient and the bass comes in again.  
Monday, Monday, Monday at ten,  
The caller gets a credit and a share of what we earn.  

[Spoken Outro]
Her daughter bought the single and played it on the train.  
The caller found the clinic without dialing us again.  
Our studio has changed the message underneath the tone:  
For teeth, press no buttons. You have called a different phone.
