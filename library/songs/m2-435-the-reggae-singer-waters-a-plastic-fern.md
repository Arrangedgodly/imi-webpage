# The Reggae Singer Waters a Plastic Fern

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: relaxed reggae songs about growth and good care, sung to a houseplant that has never been alive*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Soft offbeat guitar, gentle bass and a warm refrain; a watering can rattles while the plant remains completely unchanged.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a leafy fern to bring some green into my room,  
I placed it by the window where the afternoon could bloom.  
I gave it water every week and sang a little tune,  
Its leaves remained the same bright green from April into June.  
My neighbor said its pot looked damp and asked about the light,  
I moved it to the sunny side and sang to it at night.  
I waited for a little frond to show that it had grown,  
The fern stayed in its perfect pose beside the telephone.  

[Chorus]
Grow, little green, take your time,  
I have brought the water and a little sunshine rhyme.  
Rest, little leaf, do not fear,  
I will keep you company through every quiet year.  
Grow, little green, reach the sky,  
You have held the same position since the day that you arrived.  

[Verse 2]
My sister came to visit and she dusted off a leaf,  
She found a molded seam along the middle underneath.  
The label on the bottom said to wipe it with a cloth,  
The pot contained a plastic fern with painted pebbles on top.  
She took the watering can away and drained the little tray,  
I sang about my faithful friend who'd never grown a day.  
The neighbor brought a cutting from a real plant by her chair,  
I put it in a pot of soil and gave it water there.  

[Chorus]
Grow, little green, take your time,  
I have brought the water and a little sunshine rhyme.  
Rest, little leaf, do not fear,  
I will keep you company through every quiet year.  
Grow, little green, reach the sky,  
You have held the same position since the day that you arrived.  

[Bridge]
The cutting made a new leaf and I took a photograph,  
The plastic fern stood next to it; my sister gave a laugh.  
I kept them both beside the light with different care to do,  
The live one gets the watering; the fake one gets a dust or two.  

[Final Chorus]
Grow, little green, take your time,  
The cutting has a new leaf and the fern still looks quite fine.  
Rest, little leaf, do not fear,  
The fern requires no chorus but it likes to keep me here.  
Grow, little green, reach the sky,  
My plant collection has two jobs: one grows, one stays dry.
