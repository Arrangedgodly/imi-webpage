# Beatbox in the Aquarium Tunnel

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: a cappella beatboxing, layered vocal loops, and performers imitating every instrument*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Build a vocal loop from bass hum, clicks, and bubbles; let the aquarium's filtration system steal the downbeat.*

Verse 1
A capuchin steps in the tunnel with a microphone and grin,  
He makes a snare from cheek and tongue and lets the bubbles in.  
A lemur adds a bass line, a gibbon clicks the beat,  
The fish all turn in circles when the rhythm hits repeat.  
The singer layers every sound until the loop is tight,  
The pump goes glug behind the glass and steals the chorus right.  

Chorus
Bmm-tss, bubble-bop,  
Hold the loop and let it drop.  
Click-ka, boom-ka, hum below,  
Make the little current flow.  
Bmm-tss, bubbles rise,  
Fish are watching through their eyes.  
Build it up, then cut the track,  
The filter brings the whole beat back.  

Verse 2
The dolphin keeper taps a code against the glass for food,  
The beatboxer answers with a sound that's almost rude.  
The keeper says, "That's feeding time," and opens up the hatch,  
The band makes spoon and bucket sounds to keep the groove attached.  
The filter hum gets louder, so the singer turns it down,  
The silence lasts one measure, then the pump resumes its sound.  

Bridge
They try a softer rhythm, all the fish begin to sway,  
The keeper checks the water and says, "That's quite okay."  
The crowd starts clapping gently, the bass line settles in,  
The octopus taps eight soft beats against a practice fin.  

Chorus
Bmm-tss, bubble-bop,  
Hold the loop and let it drop.  
Click-ka, boom-ka, hum below,  
Make the little current flow.  
Bmm-tss, bubbles rise,  
Fish are watching through their eyes.  
Build it up, then cut the track,  
The filter brings the whole beat back.  

Ending
The capuchin unplugs the mic, the bubbles keep the beat,  
The aquarium hums along and asks for a repeat.
