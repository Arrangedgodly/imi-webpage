# The Last Bus Has a Backbeat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: doo-wop group vocals and the romanticized last-train farewell*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: A five-part doo-wop stack starts sweet and grows into a transit-platform shuffle; keep the conductor's spoken count crisp.*

Verse 1
At the station clock the hands both lean like tired little trees,  
Our lemur band has missed the last bus by a minute and three.  
The conductor taps his clipboard, says the route is closed for night,  
So we harmonize beneath the sign until the sign says right.  
The schedule curls in midnight air; the platform lights blink blue,  
We ask the sleeping ticket booth if it can sing us through.  

Chorus
Doo-wah, wait for the wheel to turn,  
Doo-wah, watch the timetable burn.  
If the bus won't come, we'll hum right here,  
With four-part hope and a fifth part fear.  
Doo-wah, don't wave the driver down,  
He's sweeping circles round the town.  

Verse 2
A capuchin with a tuba case says, "I know a faster lane,"  
He marches us behind the depot through a sprinkling of rain.  
We find the bus at its wash rack, its windows bright with soap,  
The driver hears our velvet chord and hands us mops and rope.  
We scrub the route map clean enough to read the final stop,  
It says the bus is parked right here, its schedule on the mop.  

Bridge
The moon is just a platform lamp with one loose screw of light,  
The driver says we missed the bus, but earned a ride at five.  
We fold our coats for pillows; the tuba case can be a seat,  
The dawn bus starts on time, then waits for us to find the beat.  

Chorus
Doo-wah, wait for the wheel to turn,  
Doo-wah, watch the timetable burn.  
If the bus won't come, we'll hum right here,  
With four-part hope and a fifth part fear.  
Doo-wah, don't wave the driver down,  
He's sweeping circles round the town.  

Ending
At sunrise all our voices fit the engine's gentle purr,  
The driver waves us on the bus, then asks us not to sing at her.
