# The Chiptune Maze Has One Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: retro video-game chiptunes with looping stages, simple sound cues, and a triumphant level-clear jingle*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use square-wave tones and a repeating eight-bit bass; let the maze loop restart the melody until the player finds the single exit.*

[Verse 1]
The screen lit at midnight with a maze of blocks and stars,  
A capuchin took the joystick and leaned against the bars.  
The hero wore a pixel cape and carried a key,  
The first turn led to where he was, as clear as it could be.  
A macaque tapped the arrow; the same wall came around,  
The chiptune played its two-note loop and bounced across the sound.  
The player made a map on paper, drawing every square,  
The hero crossed the same blue hall and found the same blue stair.  

[Chorus]
Turn left, beep twice, the pixels glow,  
The maze will keep you where you go.  
Find one door, collect one key,  
The victory jingle waits for thee.  
Turn right, step small, the score climbs high,  
The game has drawn a loop and called it sky.  

[Verse 2]
They tried the upper passage, which returned them to the start,  
The soundtrack played its opening, heartening the loop.  
A gibbon watched the screen: "The wall is moving too,"  
The hero's little cape blinked red, then flickered back to blue.  
The player pressed the pause key; the screen displayed "RESUME,"  
The maze resumed at once and played the same small tune.  
A second player joined with a written chart,  
The chart showed one blank square where the exit should have start.  

[Chorus]
Turn left, beep twice, the pixels glow,  
The maze will keep you where you go.  
Find one door, collect one key,  
The victory jingle waits for thee.  
Turn right, step small, the score climbs high,  
The game has drawn a loop and called it sky.  

[Bridge]
They pressed down at the corner where the map had shown no wall,  
A hidden doorway opened with a tiny pixel fall.  
The hero found the keyhole, turned the key and walked through,  
The jingle played one note too soon, then finished right on cue.  

[Final Chorus]
One door, one key, the pixels shine,  
The exit's drawn beyond the line.  
The loop is done, the score is clear,  
The player has escaped the screen this year.  
One door, one key, the chiptune flies,  
The map has found the square the game denies.  

[Ending]
The screen said "Level Complete." The player still held the map.
