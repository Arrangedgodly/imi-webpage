# The Christmas Single Arrives in July

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: holiday pop singles released so early that their wintry imagery becomes impossible to perform*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Sleigh bells, cheerful pop drums and a choir fanning itself between lines.*

[Verse 1]
The label booked our snowy shoot beside the summer fair,  
The drummer wore a woolen hat and wrung out all his hair.  
The singer leaned on plastic snow that melted in the sun,  
The sleigh was parked beside a stand of melons sold for one.  
The chorus wore their mittens while the ice cream truck went by,  
The producer said the album had to launch before July.  

[Chorus]
Jingle through the heatwave, sparkle in the shade,  
Hang a little mistletoe beside the lemonade.  
Wish upon a snowflake painted on the sky,  
All I want for Christmas is a fan this July.  
Wrap it up and keep it cool,  
The sleigh bells sound beside the public swimming pool.  

[Verse 2]
We filmed a frosty window with a can of shaving foam,  
The singer rubbed his scarf against a sweating garden gnome.  
The reindeer were two bicycles with antlers on the bars,  
The manger held a picnic basket full of cooling jars.  
The producer wanted breath clouds as the lovers said goodbye,  
We stood before the freezer but the lens refused to lie.  

[Chorus]
Jingle through the heatwave, sparkle in the shade,  
Hang a little mistletoe beside the lemonade.  
Wish upon a snowflake painted on the sky,  
All I want for Christmas is a fan this July.  
Wrap it up and keep it cool,  
The sleigh bells sound beside the public swimming pool.  

[Bridge]
We dropped the scarves and ran outside to sing beneath the trees,  
The chorus kept the bells but lost the artificial freeze.  
We filmed a summer singalong with ribbons on the hose,  
The producer marked it WINTER as the sprinkler soaked his nose.  

[Final Chorus]
Jingle through the heatwave, sparkle in the shade,  
Hang a little mistletoe beside the lemonade.  
Wish upon a snowflake painted on the sky,  
All I want for Christmas is a fan this July.  
Wrap it up and keep it cool,  
The video debuts as BACK TO SCHOOL.
