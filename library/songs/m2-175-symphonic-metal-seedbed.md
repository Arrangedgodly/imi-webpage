# Symphonic Metal at the Seedbed

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: symphonic metal bombast, choir-and-guitar builds, and an apocalyptic battle staged over a row of seedlings*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Distorted guitars, double-kick drums, and a huge choir; stage a cataclysmic swell as the garden hose uncoils.*

Verse 1
The gibbons raised the garden beds beneath a stormy sky,
The metal band arrived with banners flying high.
The choir sang of thunder as the seed trays were set,
A capuchin held a trowel like a sword to meet the threat.

Chorus
Rise, seedlings, rise from the soil,
Break the dark and claim the loam.
Drums of thunder, strings of fire,
Guard the roots and build them home.

Verse 2
The hose began uncoiling with a serpent's twist,
The drummer hit a double beat; the gardeners made a fist.
They fought the looping rubber, held the nozzle to the ground,
The choir shouted "Water!" as the spray went spinning round.

Chorus
Rise, seedlings, rise from the soil,
Break the dark and claim the loam.
Drums of thunder, strings of fire,
Guard the roots and build them home.

Bridge
The lead guitar screamed a note; the hose gave one more kick,
A gibbon planted both feet down and tied it to a stick.
The storm cloud passed above them, no thunder came to pass,
The band kept up the battle while the seedlings reached the grass.

Verse 3
They watered every row and turned the nozzle low,
The chorus sang a final chord to help the green things grow.
The gardeners laid their weapons down - the trowel and the rake,
The metal band took off its cape and shared a garden cake.

Chorus
Rise, seedlings, rise from the soil,
Break the dark and claim the loam.
Drums of thunder, strings of fire,
Guard the roots and build them home.

Ending
The hose was tied, the roots were wet, the battle drums fell still,
A giant radish rose at dawn and broke the garden hill.
