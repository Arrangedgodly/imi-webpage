# Gate Twelve, Take Your Time

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: smooth R&B slow jams, melismatic notes, and a late airport announcement that keeps interrupting the singer's reunion*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the groove smooth and restrained; let the overhead announcement cut across each held note.*

[Verse 1]

He waited for his sister from another distant land.
The keyboard played a mellow chord; the airport lights were blue,
He held a note so long the boarding call came through.
