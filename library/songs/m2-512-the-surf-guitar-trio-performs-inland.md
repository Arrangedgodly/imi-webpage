# The Surf Guitar Trio Performs Inland

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: surf-rock fantasies and musicians selling a coastal mood at a landlocked town festival*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Reverb guitar, rolling drums and shouted refrain; the nearest body of water is a very small ornamental pond.*

[Verse 1]
We drove our van to Maple Town to play the summer green,  
The posters promised surf guitar and waves they'd never seen.  
The town lay far from any coast beyond a range of hills,  
The only water in the square fed daffodils and mills.  
A little fountain made a spray beside the village band,  
The singer put his surfboard prop upon the very dry land.  
The mayor asked if everybody needed swimming gear,  
We told him all the waves would be within the sound he'd hear.  

[Hook]
Ride, ride, let the echo roll,  
The nearest beach is very far but music has a shore.  
Ride, ride, let the bright strings break,  
The fountain has a ripple that is smaller than a lake.  
Blue sky, dry ground, keep the rhythm high,  
The board stays on the stage; the daisies stay quite dry.  

[Verse 2]
We played a rolling instrumental as the crowd began to sway,  
The reverb made a little coast that filled the summer day.  
The fountain keeper kept his small pump running by the square,  
Its little splashes met the drums beneath the warming air.  
The mayor brought a deck chair from the shed behind the hall,  
He sat beside the fountain while the echo answered all.  
We sang about a giant wave that nobody could find,  
A duck upon the little pond seemed satisfied behind.  

[Hook]
Ride, ride, let the echo roll,  
The nearest beach is very far but music has a shore.  
Ride, ride, let the bright strings break,  
The fountain has a ripple that is smaller than a lake.  
Blue sky, dry ground, keep the rhythm high,  
The board stays on the stage; the daisies stay quite dry.  

[Bridge]
The duck swam round the pond as we began the last refrain,  
The fountain keeper asked if we would play next year again.  
He said he'd bring a larger deck chair for the mayor's feet,  
The singer packed the surfboard in the van beside the seat.  

[Final Hook]
Ride, ride, let the echo roll,  
Maple Town has got a duck that understands our soul.  
Blue sky, dry ground, keep the rhythm high,  
The surfboard rides the motorway beneath the inland sky.  
Ride, ride, let the bright strings break,  
The next poster shows our board beside the duck's small lake.
