# A Symphony for Falling Acorns

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: symphonic tone-poem conventions, rising orchestral motifs, and an impatient squirrel waiting for a single acorn to fall*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Start with quiet strings, build through brass and timpani, then end with one tiny plucked note.*

Verse 1
The orchestra assembled under oak trees in the park,
The conductor lifted both hands as the morning turned to dark.
A squirrel sat beneath the bough and waited for an acorn,
The violins began to whisper of a storm before the dawn.

Chorus
Rise, strings, rise, let the branches sway,
Brass roll in and carry the day.
Timpani thunder, winds take flight,
One acorn falls in the velvet night.

Verse 2
The horns announced a distant wind; the woodwinds gave a sigh,
The squirrel looked up every time a leaf went drifting by.
The cellos built a mountain out of four repeating notes,
The conductor held the silence as the solo flute arose.

Chorus
Rise, strings, rise, let the branches sway,
Brass roll in and carry the day.
Timpani thunder, winds take flight,
One acorn falls in the velvet night.

Bridge
The percussion rolled like thunder; the audience leaned in,
The squirrel raised a little basket and prepared its chin.
A tiny acorn dropped at last, then bounced beyond the stand,
The full orchestra struck a chord; it rolled into the band.

Verse 3
The conductor pointed left; the violas followed through,
The basses stepped around the nut and played a softer cue.
The squirrel retrieved the acorn and sat beneath the tree,
The orchestra resolved the storm to one warm melody.

Chorus
Rise, strings, rise, let the branches sway,
Brass roll in and carry the day.
Timpani thunder, winds take flight,
One acorn falls in the velvet night.

Ending
The final note was very small, the squirrel took it home,
The orchestra bowed beneath the tree and let the branches roam.
