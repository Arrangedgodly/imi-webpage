# The Swing Band Lost the Count

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: big-band swing, brass-section call-and-response, and a drummer who keeps adding bars to the ending*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Brassy swing with walking bass; the drummer adds a two-bar tag each time the singer tries to finish.*

Verse 1
The band began a swing tune at the ballroom's edge,
The trumpets blew a bright reply; the saxes made a pledge.
The singer sang, "We'll end at eight," and pointed to the door,
The drummer played two extra bars and brought them back for more.

Chorus
Swing it left, swing it right,
Count the bars and hold on tight.
When we reach the final two,
Drummer, let the ending through.

Verse 2
The dancers formed a circle and the bass began to walk,
The trombone slid a phrase beneath the singer's talk.
The band hit seven measures, then the drummer hit the break,
The singer bowed at eight; the band began to wake.

Chorus
Swing it left, swing it right,
Count the bars and hold on tight.
When we reach the final two,
Drummer, let the ending through.

Bridge
The drummer said, "The tag is what the people came to hear,"
The singer said, "They came for cake; the cake is over there."
The drummer played a fill; the band began to spin,
The cake cart rolled across the floor and joined the violin.

Verse 3
The dancers caught the cart and brought the cake to rest,
The drummer found the count again and played his very best.
They hit the final chord at nine, exactly as planned,
The crowd clapped once, the drummer stopped, the singer shook his hand.

Chorus
Swing it left, swing it right,
Count the bars and hold on tight.
When we reach the final two,
Drummer, let the ending through.

Ending
The cake was cut, the band packed up, the drummer kept one stick,
He tapped a tiny two-bar tag and made the lights all flick.
