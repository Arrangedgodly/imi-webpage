# The Metal Singer Steams the Milk

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: heavy metal vocal intensity applied to coffee-making rather than epic destruction*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Down-tuned guitars, rapid drums and a growled lead; the steam wand provides a very real hiss between phrases.*

[Verse 1]
The coffee bar hired our singer for the morning after tour,  
He wore his black band shirt behind the tiny café door.  
A customer requested foam upon a little drink,  
He raised the metal pitcher from the counter by the sink.  
He growled about the molten sea and fire within the night,  
The steam wand made a loud reply beneath the counter light.  
The customer looked hopeful as the pitcher gave its hiss,  
She said the milk was all she needed from the fiery abyss.  

[Chorus]
Steam, metal pitcher, roar to the sky,  
The customer has ordered a cappuccino nearby.  
Steam, metal pitcher, let the hot cloud rise,  
The foam should meet the coffee, not obscure the singer's eyes.  
Dark shirt, hot milk, give the little cup its crown,  
The bar manager asks him to bring the volume down.  

[Verse 2]
He poured the milk quite carefully and made a little leaf,  
The customer admired the shape beneath her quiet relief.  
The next order was a black coffee with nothing else inside,  
He put the pitcher down and let the milk routine subside.  
He sang a shorter growl about the darkness of the brew,  
The manager asked if he could simply call the order through.  
He called the name quite clearly and the drink reached its right hand,  
The café kept its rhythm with no touring metal band.  

[Chorus]
Steam, metal pitcher, roar to the sky,  
The customer has ordered a cappuccino nearby.  
Steam, metal pitcher, let the hot cloud rise,  
The foam should meet the coffee, not obscure the singer's eyes.  
Dark shirt, hot milk, give the little cup its crown,  
The bar manager asks him to bring the volume down.  

[Bridge]
He wrote a new song later with the steam hiss in its break,  
The band recorded coffee sounds beside the cooling cake.  
The manager came to hear it at the little listening night,  
She asked if every order had to meet a dragon fight.  

[Final Chorus]
Steam, metal pitcher, let the hot cloud rise,  
The cover has a silver leaf beneath a little dragon's eyes.  
Dark shirt, hot milk, give the little cup its crown,  
The singer calls the orders with no growling in the town.  
Steam, metal pitcher, roar to the sky,  
The band sells little mugs before the guitars start nearby.
