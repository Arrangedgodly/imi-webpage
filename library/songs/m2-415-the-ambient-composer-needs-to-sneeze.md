# The Ambient Composer Needs to Sneeze

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ambient listening sessions with solemn attention to minute sounds and performers who cannot survive their own extended silence*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Sparse sustained synthesizer notes, whispered vocals and long rests; reserve the only loud transient for the sneeze.*

[Verse 1]
I dimmed the room to blue and played a single gentle tone,  
The listeners closed their eyes and heard the quiet of their own.  
I set a feather on the stand to signify the breeze,  
Its dust rose softly upward and I felt the urge to sneeze.  
I held my breath behind the desk and waited for the wave,  
The synthesizer held its note; my nose would not behave.  
I reached toward a tissue with a very careful hand,  
The audience heard its rustle and admired the texture planned.  

[Chorus]
Still air, slow light, let the small sounds flow,  
There is something in my breathing that the room should never know.  
Deep room, long tone, listen to the space,  
The silence has a tickle sitting right behind my face.  
Still air, slow light, let the evening drift,  
My nose has got a climax that the score did not predict.  

[Verse 2]
The tone grew slowly quieter; the listeners leaned near,  
I pinched my nose a moment; I could feel the sneeze draw near.  
The tissue waited ready as the feather shook again,  
I turned away from all the mics and sneezed into the tissue in my hand.  
The room heard every echo from the far uncarpeted wall,  
The listeners opened wide their eyes; the tone became quite small.  
One critic wrote a sentence about sudden human breath,  
The others sat in silence till my final chord had left.  

[Chorus]
Still air, slow light, let the small sounds flow,  
There is something in my breathing that the room should never know.  
Deep room, long tone, listen to the space,  
The silence has a tickle sitting right behind my face.  
Still air, slow light, let the evening drift,  
My nose has got a climax that the score did not predict.  

[Bridge]
The critic asked the meaning of the burst across the hall,  
I told him it was dust; he said that dust explained us all.  
I put the feather in a bag and cleaned the little stand,  
He called my humble cleaning an extension of the plan.  

[Final Chorus]
Still air, slow light, let the small sounds flow,  
The next concert has no feather and a freshly polished floor.  
Deep room, long tone, listen to the space,  
The critic waits for dust again; I keep a tissue near my face.  
Still air, slow light, let the evening drift,  
He says the lack of sneezing is my most disturbing shift.
