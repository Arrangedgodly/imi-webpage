# Committee of the Dead

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: theatrical gothic rock and melodramatic supernatural narration*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
At midnight the attic committee called.  
The old house groaned; the wallpaper peeled.  
Three ghosts arrived with minutes from before.  
They floated through the ceiling, missed the door.  
The chair was a bust with one cracked eye.  
The treasurer counted dust as supply.  
I brought a candle and a list of fees.  
The ghosts all rattled in agreement, pleased.  
One ghost was an ape with a silver cane.  
He knocked the rhythm down the windowpane.  

## Chorus
We convene when the clocks forget.  
We approve one more regret.  
Raise the gavel, dim the hall.  
The living never call us at all.  

## Verse 2
The first motion asked to haunt the pantry.  
The second asked for warmer shrouds.  
The secretary was a crow in a vest.  
It stamped each form against my chest.  
The ghost accountant raised a pale hand.  
He said the dues were paid in sand.  
The chair began to moan in C.  
The neighbors banged the ceiling angrily.  
The ghost ape swung from the chandelier.  
He dropped the budget in my beer.  

## Chorus
We convene when the clocks forget.  
We approve one more regret.  
Raise the gavel, dim the hall.  
The living never call us at all.  

## Bridge
Then upstairs came a toddler's sleepy cry.  
The ghosts all froze and looked at I.  
The chair removed its dusty crown.  
The treasurer put the forms back down.  
They crept upstairs with no dramatic flair.  
One ghost tucked blankets, soft and square.  
The crow unlatched the nursery door.  
The house stopped groaning, just once more.  

## Final chorus
We adjourn when the clocks forget.  
We can save one child from regret.  
Lower the gavel, warm the hall.  
A quiet house is home to all.  
We convene when the child sleeps well.  
We file our forms in a teacup shell.  
Raise no moan, disturb no dream.  
The moon keeps watch above the beam.  

## Ending
The attic door clicked shut at dawn.  
The ghostly minutes were withdrawn.  
The crow flew off to find its vest.  
The house sat still and took a rest.  

*Performance note: Sing with dark theatrical weight, but make the ghosts careful and gentle when the child wakes.*
