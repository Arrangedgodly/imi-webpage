# Ska at the Crossing Guard

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ska's offbeat guitar chops, brisk horn stabs, and bouncing stop-start energy*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Upstroke guitar and bright horns; all singers freeze whenever the crossing signal clicks.*

Verse 1
A squirrel monkey wore a vest beside the painted lane,
He clicked the little crossing light and waited through the rain.
The band arrived on every offbeat, horns held up in air,
They froze at "wait," then skanked in place as walkers gathered there.

Chorus
Step on green and stop on red,
Horn goes high above your head.
Bounce one beat, then hold it tight,
Ska can cross when signs say "right."

Verse 2
The first light changed; the brass took off; the pedestrians moved slow,
A gibbon danced a zigzag line and blocked the zebra row.
The guard blew one long whistle; every trumpet answered back,
The crossing signal blinked in time and joined the band's attack.

Chorus
Step on green and stop on red,
Horn goes high above your head.
Bounce one beat, then hold it tight,
Ska can cross when signs say "right."

Bridge
The singer cried, "A final chorus!" right before the red,
The whole band locked its knees and held one note instead.
A stroller rolled between them; every musician stayed,
The guard waved all the walkers through while trumpets softly played.

Verse 3
The commuters formed a rhythm line and crossed without delay,
The band still bounced at either curb, forbidden to have sway.
The light turned green; they took one step; the red froze them once more,
The guard collected tips in hats and opened up a score.

Chorus
Step on green and stop on red,
Horn goes high above your head.
Bounce one beat, then hold it tight,
Ska can cross when signs say "right."

Ending
At last the band crossed after hours, when traffic lights went dim;
The crossing guard sold tickets for the world's slowest ska hymn.
