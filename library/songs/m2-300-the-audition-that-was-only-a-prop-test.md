# The Audition That Was Only a Prop Test

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: talent-show auditions that advertise star-making but spend the slot testing props and stage cues*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Show-tune audition patter with an anxious piano vamp; the hopeful singer keeps adapting as each prop test steals the spotlight.*

**Verse 1**
Milo waits behind the curtain with a song he wrote at dawn.  
The host says, "You're our next big star," then calls the curtain on.  
A stool arrives, a cane, a hat, a chair with squeaky legs.  
The judges want to test the props before the singer begs.  
Milo hits his opening note; the cane falls with a clatter.  
The pianist stops to tune the chair; the song is not the matter.  

**Chorus**
Sing, Milo, sing, though the spotlight's on the chair;  
Show the judges every prop and prove the legs are square.  
Clap for the stool, give the cane a bow;  
The audition starts tomorrow, if the props are ready now.  

**Verse 2**
The hat rolls down the runway; Milo catches it in time.  
The judges score his balance as a daring pantomime.  
The chair squeaks on the chorus; they ask him for the cause.  
He oils the joint and sings a note that earns a round of applause.  
The cane is used to cue the lights; the stool becomes a stand.  
The singer holds the curtain while the stage crew clears the band.  

**Chorus**
Sing, Milo, sing, though the spotlight's on the chair;  
Show the judges every prop and prove the legs are square.  
Clap for the stool, give the cane a bow;  
The audition starts tomorrow, if the props are ready now.  

**Bridge**
The judges ask his name at last; the piano starts again.  
Milo sings the opening phrase; the room grows still, then joins.  
The host looks at the clock and says the slot is through.  
The crew has heard the whole song while testing every cue.  

**Ending**
They give him one small card: "Return for the final round."  
Milo bows, the stool stays still, the cane is safely bound.  
He leaves the stage humming, while the judges take their seats.  
The props get their own curtain call; Milo keeps the beat.
