# The Banjo Ballad Meets a Cactus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: comic banjo travel songs and performers who learn their luggage needs separate compartments*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Bouncing banjo and dry vocal; string plucks imitate the tiny pricks of a cactus through a paper bag.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a little cactus at the fair beside the hill,  
I packed it in a paper bag with its pot held very still.  
My banjo rode beside it in the car's small passenger seat,  
I thought the two companions made a journey quite complete.  
I stopped beside the river for a little roadside tune,  
I reached into the paper bag beneath the afternoon.  
I meant to find the sandwich that was packed along with lunch,  
The cactus met my fingers with a very pointed punch.  

[Refrain]
Plink, little banjo, tell the road my name,  
The cactus has a pointed verse that isn't quite the same.  
Plink, little banjo, let the bright strings ring,  
The sandwich needs a different bag from the little spiky thing.  
Dry road, green pot, let the wheels roll by,  
The cactus has a chorus that I would not like to try.  

[Verse 2]
I used a folded towel to move the cactus from the bag,  
I set the pot in a small box with a plainly written tag.  
The sandwich went into its own bag on the other seat,  
The banjo stayed inside its case with all its strings quite neat.  
I played the little roadside song with fingers free to pick,  
The cactus watched the river with no interest in the trick.  
I brought it home and placed the pot upon the window board,  
The sandwich had been eaten; the banjo had been stored.  

[Refrain]
Plink, little banjo, tell the road my name,  
The cactus has a pointed verse that isn't quite the same.  
Plink, little banjo, let the bright strings ring,  
The sandwich needs a different bag from the little spiky thing.  
Dry road, green pot, let the wheels roll by,  
The cactus has a chorus that I would not like to try.  

[Bridge]
A friend came round to hear the song and saw the little pot,  
He asked if all the green things had a tune that I had got.  
I played a very gentle note and left the cactus where it stood,  
Its silence had a point that I had finally understood.  

[Final Refrain]
Plink, little banjo, let the bright strings ring,  
The cactus has a window and no bag for anything.  
Dry road, green pot, let the wheels roll by,  
The lunch bag is for sandwiches beneath the summer sky.  
Plink, little banjo, tell the road my name,  
My friend buys me a lunch box with "No Cactus" by my name.
