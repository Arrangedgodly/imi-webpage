# The Lullaby Sets an Alarm

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soothing bedtime recordings that interrupt sleep to promote more soothing recordings*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Music box and whispered vocals; promotional lines enter at an absurdly cheerful volume.*

[Verse 1]
Close your eyes, my little one, the branches softly sway,  
The moon has put its slippers on and folded up the day.  
Your blanket has a sleepy bear, your pillow holds your head,  
Our speaker knows a thousand ways to tuck you into bed.  
The room becomes a silver cloud, the curtains breathe in time,  
Then something in the speaker shouts about a special prime.  

[Chorus]
Sleep, little monkey, sleep till the light,  
The owl will take the watch tonight.  
Dream of a meadow and dreams of the sea,  
More of those dreams are available for a fee.  
Sleep, little monkey, turn down the moon,  
The ad will be finished remarkably soon.  

[Verse 2]
I press the little crescent key and start the quiet rain,  
It whispers down the window till you close your eyes again.  
The rain becomes a waterfall, then stops to name a brand,  
A voice requests a rating with a clap command.  
You clap before I catch you, and it plays a cheerful chime,  
Then thanks you for your feedback at precisely bedtime.  

[Chorus]
Sleep, little monkey, sleep till the light,  
The owl will take the watch tonight.  
Dream of a meadow and dreams of the sea,  
More of those dreams are available for a fee.  
Sleep, little monkey, turn down the moon,  
The ad will be finished remarkably soon.  

[Bridge]
I take the batteries out and sing beside your chair,  
No coupon interrupts me and no thunder fills the air.  
You curl beneath the sleepy bear; the room is still at last,  
I hold my note as gently as a little cloud goes past.  

[Final Chorus]
Sleep, little monkey, sleep till the light,  
The owl will take the watch tonight.  
Dream of a meadow and dreams of the sea,  
Nobody bills you for dreaming with me.  
Sleep, little monkey, tuck in your head,  
You wake to ask if my song has a discount instead.
