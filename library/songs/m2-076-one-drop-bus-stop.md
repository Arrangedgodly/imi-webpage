# One Drop at the Bus Stop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: reggae one-drop rhythm, offbeat guitar, and relaxed lyrics that refuse to hurry*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Warm bass, clipped offbeat guitar, and a single dropped snare beat; the queue advances at its own pace.*

Verse 1
A macaque waits for Route 8 beneath the shelter shade,  
The board says "Due in five"; five minutes slowly fade.  
The guitar clicks offbeat, the bassline rolls along,  
The bus arrives in seven, so we stretch another song.  
A capuchin asks, "Are we late?" The driver says, "Not yet,"  
The whole line sways in easy time beside the transit set.  

Chorus
One drop, one step, move along,  
Let the bassline hold the song.  
No rush, no shove, make some room,  
The bus will come and so will noon.  
One drop, one step, wait your turn,  
The shelter light will soon return.  
One drop, one step, board with care,  
The driver needs a little air.  

Verse 2
A stroller blocks the doorway, the line begins to bend,  
The driver calls the back row up; the passengers descend.  
We move the bags and fold the chairs, we keep the rhythm slow,  
The bass player climbs aboard and lets the downbeat go.  
The bus goes one block, stops again; a pigeon claims the lane,  
The driver waits; we hum the groove and wave it off the pane.  

Bridge
The road clears up, the wheels roll on, the shelter fades behind,  
The conductor counts the stops in time, the route stays kind.  
The singer says, "We're almost there," though no one asked him when,  
The whole bus answers, "Take your time," and starts the hook again.  

Chorus
One drop, one step, move along,  
Let the bassline hold the song.  
No rush, no shove, make some room,  
The bus will come and so will noon.  
One drop, one step, wait your turn,  
The shelter light will soon return.  
One drop, one step, board with care,  
The driver needs a little air.  

Ending
The bus arrives on schedule, the driver checks the clock,  
The passengers keep swaying till the next stop on the block.
