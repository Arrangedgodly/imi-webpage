# The Marching Band Takes the Escalator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: triumphant marching-band anthems that confuse synchronized progress with going anywhere*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Brass march with snare cadence; the band's feet stop while the moving stairs keep the tempo.*

[Verse 1]
Our captain led the band inside the mall's enormous door,  
The mayor had booked a welcome song upon the upper floor.  
We formed a perfect column by the shop that sold perfume,  
The tuba filled the passage and the cymbals shook the room.  
The captain raised his baton for a proud ascending march,  
An escalator carried us beneath a plastic arch.  
He shouted, "Keep the left foot first!" We stood upon the stairs,  
Our knees were very disciplined; our feet stayed where they were.  

[Chorus]
Up, up, up we go,  
Flags above the shoe shop and the brass below.  
Left, right, hold the line,  
Moving without marching is a most unusual sign.  
High, high, lift the tune,  
We shall reach the mayor and salute him very soon.  
Up, up, hear us play,  
The trumpet has reached menswear; the drum is by the cafe.  

[Verse 2]
The little steps approached the top; the captain walked ahead,  
The tuba couldn't clear the rail, so he turned it on its edge.  
The tail of our procession crossed a moving metal seam,  
The drummer lost his little hat; it dropped beside the beam.  
The band stepped off in sections and assembled by a chair,  
We saw the mayor's welcome stand; the mayor wasn't there.  
A shop assistant pointed to the ground-floor garden gate,  
We'd followed last week's floor plan and arrived a full floor late.  

[Chorus]
Up, up, up we go,  
Flags above the shoe shop and the brass below.  
Left, right, hold the line,  
Moving without marching is a most unusual sign.  
High, high, lift the tune,  
We shall reach the mayor and salute him very soon.  
Up, up, hear us play,  
The trumpet has reached menswear; the drum is by the cafe.  

[Bridge]
The captain chose the downward stairs and gave a mighty nod,  
We rode behind a shopper with a potted rubber plant.  
She kept a better spacing than our well-rehearsed bassoon,  
The mayor heard us coming down and hummed our climbing tune.  

[Final Chorus]
Down, down, down we go,  
Passing every shop again with banners hanging low.  
Left, right, hold the line,  
The shopper with the rubber plant has kept excellent time.  
High, high, lift the tune,  
We reached the mayor's garden and began at half past noon.  
Up, up, hear him say,  
"The lift was round the corner; you have made my shopping day."
