# Brand New Banana Day

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: relentlessly cheerful corporate jingles and slogan writing*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
At Banana Better, every peel has purpose.  
We measure smiles in quarterly service.  
Our fruit arrives in gold foil sleeves.  
The CEO hands out motivational leaves.  
We scan each bunch for brand alignment.  
We call a bruise "a flavor assignment."  
The break room wall says, "Reach your best."  
The bananas have been laid to rest.  
The macaque staff all wear green ties.  
The tail-coil trend began with supplies.  

## Chorus
It's a brand new banana day!  
We peel with pride and invoice on time.  
Put our friendly logo in your tray.  
Your snack can join our growth paradigm.  

## Verse 2
The slogan team met inside a glass cube.  
They chose three words, then formed a focus group.  
The interns clapped when the fruit was unveiled.  
The sticker slipped; the presentation failed.  
One banana rolled beneath the copier.  
The chair declared a new stakeholder.  
The printer made a spreadsheet of the floor.  
The janitor asked what this was all for.  
One langur swung down to retrieve the snack.  
His lanyard caught, so the board swung back.  

## Chorus
It's a brand new banana day!  
We peel with pride and invoice on time.  
Put our friendly logo in your tray.  
Your snack can join our growth paradigm.  

## Bridge
The janitor cut through the meeting haze.  
He peeled the fruit and gave us praise.  
"It tastes like lunch," he said, and sat.  
The board removed its welcome mat.  
The CEO asked for the market share.  
He got one bite and forgot to care.  
The focus group stopped taking notes.  
They passed the bunch and loosened their coats.  

## Final chorus
It's a brand new banana day!  
No logo needed on every bite.  
Put the whole ripe bunch in the tray.  
Let the meeting end before tonight.  
It's a brand new banana day!  
We peel, we share, we clean the floor.  
Our friendly sticker can stay away.  
The fruit tastes good; we need no more.  

## Ending
The slogan hung beside the door.  
The janitor ate one, then four.  
The copier rested, warm and slow.  
The brand had somewhere else to go.  

*Performance note: Smile too brightly through the first chorus, then let the staff sing the plain truth in the finale.*
