# Quickstep on the Ferry Ramp

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ballroom quickstep footwork and competition judges who score speed before the floor is clear*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Bright quickstep piano and crisp heel taps; each judge's call interrupts the dancers at a worse moment.*

**Verse 1**
At Harbor Hall, the ferry ramp is polished for the dance.  
Two lemurs take the ballroom frame and start their quickstep prance.  
The judge calls, "Travel down the floor; keep every turn precise."  
A deckhand wheels a luggage cart across their measured line.  
They dodge the bags, they cross the beat, they never break their hold.  
The judge writes down a perfect score, the deckhand says, "Too bold."  

**Chorus**
Quick, quick, step; turn, turn, glide;  
Keep your partner at your side.  
Heel, toe, rush, then wait for the ramp to clear;  
The fastest dance in town is standing right here.  

**Verse 2**
The cart returns with folded chairs and blocks the second turn.  
They skip around the luggage and wait their second turn.  
He calls for more extension; they stretch from heel to toe.  
A ferry whistle sounds the cue; the ramp begins to go.  
They keep their ballroom posture as the floor tilts to the bay.  
The judge scores their recovery; the captain asks them to stay.  

**Chorus**
Quick, quick, step; turn, turn, glide;  
Keep your partner at your side.  
Heel, toe, rush, then wait for the ramp to clear;  
The fastest dance in town is standing right here.  

**Bridge**
The deckhand parks the cart; the captain locks the ramp.  
The couple checks the surface, wipes a shining patch.  
They dance one final figure on the marked and level ground.  
The judge stops his stopwatch; the ferry makes no sound.  

**Ending**
The partners bow; the bags stay put; the chairs are stacked away.  
The judge awards a ribbon for the quickest stationary display.  
They step ashore; the ramp goes up; the ferry leaves the place.  
They wave from the pier and keep their quickstep pace.
