# The Drum-and-Bass DJ Stamps the Library Books

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rapid drum-and-bass breakbeats and DJs who confuse a repetitive library task with an invitation to headline*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Fast broken drums and low bass; a library date stamp supplies the snare while a librarian supplies the quiet vocal cue.*

[Verse 1]
I volunteered on Tuesday at the library's returns,  
The librarian showed the date stamp and the way its handle turns.  
I stamped the first returning book and heard a tidy crack,  
The second book gave out a thump; I started building a track.  
I tapped the cover twice before the stamp came down again,  
The librarian said the date was all the book should gain.  
I told her that the rhythm had a very special drive,  
She pointed to the reading room; I kept the beat inside.  

[Chorus]
Stamp that date, roll that break,  
There is only one small mark that every book should take.  
Fast hands, soft voice, keep the counter clear,  
The readers have a quiet tune that nobody wants to hear.  
Stamp that date, hold the bass,  
The loudest little DJ has a silent working place.  

[Verse 2]
I took the pattern home that night and built a layered beat,  
The books stayed in the library; my samples were complete.  
I asked the librarian for the sound of one clear stamp,  
She made it after closing with a desk recorder in hand.  
I sped the little sample up and gave it drums below,  
The club crowd heard a date stamp racing through my opening show.  
The librarian came as a guest and stood beside the stage,  
She asked how many books I'd stamped; I showed her the record sleeve page.  

[Chorus]
Stamp that date, roll that break,  
There is only one small mark that every book should take.  
Fast hands, soft voice, keep the counter clear,  
The readers have a quiet tune that nobody wants to hear.  
Stamp that date, hold the bass,  
The loudest little DJ has a silent working place.  

[Bridge]
She put my borrowed headphones on and watched the dancers jump,  
The bass dropped underneath her stamp; the room became a thump.  
She checked her watch at closing time and tapped the exit sign,  
The dancers gave the librarian a perfectly obedient line.  

[Final Chorus]
Stamp that date, roll that break,  
The librarian has heard the beat her quiet counter makes.  
Fast hands, soft voice, now the evening's done,  
She asks the encore's due date; I point to closing time.  
Stamp that date, hold the bass,  
Tomorrow I stamp quietly with the loud tune in its place.
