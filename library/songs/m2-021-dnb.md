# Parcel Run at Dawn

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: drum and bass velocity, breakbeat switches, and courier culture*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Fast breakbeats, a bass line that ducks under the vocals, and breathless but clear phrasing.*

## Verse 1
The courier baboon clipped a light onto his pack.  
The city hadn't woken; he took the river track.  
A snare roll chased him past the shuttered bakery.  
The bass dropped low beneath the tramway's melody.  
He carried one cello, wrapped in striped blue cloth.  
The address said a rooftop, though the stairs fell off.  
He found a fire ladder bolted to the wall.  
The drummer split the break; his boom woke the hall.  

## Chorus
Run the route, let the breakbeat fly.  
Keep the parcel level, keep one eye on the sky.  
Run the route, every corner cuts.  
The city beats fast, but the cello stays shut.  

## Verse 2
A cellist met him on the upper landing.  
She checked the seal, then gave him thanks for standing.  
The skyline caught the first light in its panes.  
The courier caught his breath, then heard the trains.  
She opened up the case and checked each string.  
One had slipped, but none were broken by the swing.  
She tuned it there and played a rising phrase.  
The courier tapped a rhythm through the dawn's first haze.  

## Bridge
The fastest feet don't make the music play.  
A careful hand can save the whole long day.  
The drummer pulled the tempo back a hair.  
The cellist offered cash; he asked for a chair.  

## Chorus
Run the route, let the breakbeat fly.  
Keep the parcel level, keep one eye on the sky.  
Run the route, every corner cuts.  
The city beats fast, but the cello stays shut.  

## Ending
The courier signed the pad; his tip was one long solo.  
He got no cash, just front-row seats to the cello.
