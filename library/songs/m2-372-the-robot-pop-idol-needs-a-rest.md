# The Robot Pop Idol Needs a Rest

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: polished pop performances that describe mechanical exhaustion as a perfectly choreographed new dance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Bright electronic pop with precise harmonies; battery alerts become little counter-melodies.*

[Verse 1]
Our idol took the stage beneath a thousand silver lights,  
His polished little joints performed a hundred perfect rights.  
He spun before the camera and he pointed to the sky,  
A yellow light beneath his collar caught the manager's eye.  
The crowd began the chorus as his shoulders slowed their beat,  
The manager announced a brand-new shuffle of the feet.  

[Chorus]
Power down the dance floor, light up every face,  
There's a little spark of magic in this very special place.  
Slow your hands and slow your feet,  
It's a brand-new kind of very economical beat.  
Hold that pose and make it shine,  
The charge beside his collar says he's running out of time.  

[Verse 2]
His backing dancers copied every little fading move,  
The drummer dropped the tempo to protect the borrowed groove.  
The manager requested just another perfect spin,  
The idol leaned against the set and let a warning in.  
The crowd held up their phones to watch the latest sleepy step,  
His eyelids slowly shuttered and his shoulders lost their pep.  

[Chorus]
Power down the dance floor, light up every face,  
There's a little spark of magic in this very special place.  
Slow your hands and slow your feet,  
It's a brand-new kind of very economical beat.  
Hold that pose and make it shine,  
The charge beside his collar says he's running out of time.  

[Bridge]
The stagehand led him to a stool beside a charging lead,  
He rested while the dancers took the chorus up to speed.  
The manager declared the break a very bold new theme,  
The crowd sat down to join him in a synchronized daydream.  

[Final Chorus]
Power down the dance floor, light up every face,  
There's a little spark of magic in this very special place.  
Slow your hands and slow your feet,  
It's a brand-new kind of very economical beat.  
Hold that pose and make it shine,  
His most successful dance is sitting down for quite some time.
