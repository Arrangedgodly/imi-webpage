# Flamenco for the Flying Fan

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: flamenco song conventions, heelwork, dramatic guitar rasgueado, and a fan that refuses to stay in its dancer's hand*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Guitar rasgueado, palmas, and crisp heelwork; the fan should flutter in response to each dramatic turn.*

Verse 1
A lemur wore a red shawl and a fan with painted lace,
She stamped across the courtyard with a fiercely focused face.
The guitarist struck a sharp chord; the dancer raised her hand,
The fan flew past the fountain and was caught by the band.

Chorus
Stamp the heel and turn around,
Let the fan fly through the sound.
Clap the beat and hold the pose,
Watch the painted petals close.

Verse 2
She spun beside the orange tree; the fan returned in flight,
It landed on the guitarist, who kept the rhythm right.
The drummer clapped a double beat; the dancer made a bow,
The fan slipped from her fingers and went sailing toward the plow.

Chorus
Stamp the heel and turn around,
Let the fan fly through the sound.
Clap the beat and hold the pose,
Watch the painted petals close.

Bridge
The band chased after silk and lace, then stopped upon a clap,
The fan had landed in a basket on the gardener's lap.
He tossed it back with careful aim; it opened in the air,
The dancer caught it by its rim and swept it through her hair.

Verse 3
She stamped a final pattern; the courtyard answered loud,
The fan stayed in her hand as she bowed before the crowd.
The guitarist played a closing turn, the clappers kept the time,
The gardener took one heel-step and claimed the final line.

Chorus
Stamp the heel and turn around,
Let the fan fly through the sound.
Clap the beat and hold the pose,
Watch the painted petals close.

Ending
The fan was fixed with ribbon; the shawl was folded right,
The gardener kept the rhythm going long into the night.
