# The Barbershop Quartet Takes a Number

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: barbershop quartets harmonizing simple requests into elaborate delays*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Four unaccompanied voices; the bass reads queue numbers under increasingly long held chords.*

[Verse 1]
We walked into the barber shop with sunshine in our hair,  
The barber held his scissors and he pointed to a chair.  
Our lead requested just a trim, the tenor sang it high,  
The baritone enriched the word; the bass supplied a sigh.  
The barber tried to ask which one was first beneath the comb,  
We held a chord so long he thought we'd never leave for home.  

[Chorus]
Take a number, take a seat,  
Make the little waiting room a harmony suite.  
Short on top and long on song,  
The barber has another client coming along.  
Ring that chord and let it shine,  
We have sung through number seven and we're number nine.  

[Verse 2]
The barber called the tenor up; the lead said he was first,  
The baritone began a bridge describing how he'd thirst.  
The bass produced a water flask and sang a lower note,  
The barber draped a cutting cape around the singer's throat.  
We stood around the occupied chair to hold a perfect chord,  
The barber couldn't reach his ears and gently begged the lord.  

[Chorus]
Take a number, take a seat,  
Make the little waiting room a harmony suite.  
Short on top and long on song,  
The barber has another client coming along.  
Ring that chord and let it shine,  
We have sung through number seven and we're number nine.  

[Bridge]
He trimmed the tenor's whiskers while the other voices hummed,  
Then combed the lead and baritone before the final drum.  
The bass sat down in silence and the barber smiled at last,  
He cut one careful snip before the bass sang something vast.  

[Final Chorus]
Take a number, take a seat,  
Make the little waiting room a harmony suite.  
Short on top and long on song,  
The barber has another client coming along.  
Ring that chord and let it shine,  
He sends us out untrimmed on one side, four heads in a line.
