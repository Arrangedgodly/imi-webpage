# The Shanty Crew Counts the Luggage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rousing sea shanties slowed by a cruise ship's baggage rules*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Unaccompanied lead with a stomping crew response; sing the baggage weights with terrible solemnity.*

[Verse 1]
We gathered at the harbor with a mighty thirst for foam,  
The captain packed a compass and a cushion from his home.  
The mate brought seven jackets for the storms we hoped to meet,  
The cook rolled in a cupboard with a kettle at his feet.  
The porter stood beside the plank and weighed each little case,  
The wind was in our whiskers and the tide was in our face.  
He marked the cook's enormous load and handed him a bill,  
The crew began a hauling song; the cupboard stood there still.  

[Chorus]
Heave away, weigh again,  
Count the bags of mighty men.  
Hoist that trunk, mind that fee,  
Nothing sails for free upon the sea.  
Pull together, pull it right,  
We leave when every case is lashed down tight.  

[Verse 2]
The captain wore his cushion tucked beneath his braided coat,  
The mate put seven jackets on and waddled toward the boat.  
The cook declared his cupboard was a much beloved pet,  
The porter asked what breed it was; he hadn't chosen yet.  
We took the kettles from the shelves and hid them in our hats,  
The cupboard shed three pounds and all the harbor's nesting rats.  
The porter weighed it once again and sadly shook his head,  
The captain said we'd sail without the furniture instead.  

[Chorus]
Heave away, weigh again,  
Count the bags of mighty men.  
Hoist that trunk, mind that fee,  
Nothing sails for free upon the sea.  
Pull together, pull it right,  
We leave when every case is lashed down tight.  

[Bridge]
The cook unpacked his cupboard on the stones beside the pier,  
He gave the harbor workers mugs and brewed them something clear.  
The captain slipped the mooring rope, then left the helm for tea,  
The mate removed six jackets and returned to number three.  

[Final Chorus]
Heave away, weigh again,  
Count the bags of mighty men.  
Hoist that trunk, mind that fee,  
Nothing sails for free upon the sea.  
Pull together, pull it right,  
The empty boat departs while we reopen tea all night.
