# Merengue at the Roller-Rink Cleanup

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: merengue's steady partner steps and dance marathons that keep going after the rink closes*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Brisk merengue groove; the floor manager's cleanup calls should land over the beat without ending the dance.*

**Verse 1**
The roller rink is closing; the lights turn amber low.  
A pair of macaques keeps dancing in a neat and measured flow.  
The manager brings a mop and asks them to clear the floor.  
They step around the handle and keep moving toward the door.  
The band plays one more chorus; the skates roll side to side.  
The mop head follows every turn, a partner on the glide.  

**Chorus**
Step-step, turn-turn, keep the rhythm tight;  
The rink is closed, but the beat is bright.  
One more turn, one more sweep, the floor begins to shine;  
The dancers and the mop head share a closing line.  

**Verse 2**
A second pair joins in, though their skates are in their hands.  
They slide in socks across the boards and join the band.  
The manager marks the wet spots with a cone and calls, "Stay clear."  
The dancers use the cones as partners, circling far and near.  
A wheel slips off a skate; the drummer catches time.  
The owner kneels to fix the axle, tapping out the rhyme.  

**Chorus**
Step-step, turn-turn, keep the rhythm tight;  
The rink is closed, but the beat is bright.  
One more turn, one more sweep, the floor begins to shine;  
The dancers and the mop head share a closing line.  

**Bridge**
The band cuts off; the skaters stop; the mop is set upright.  
The manager dries the last wet patch beneath the exit light.  
The loose wheel clicks into place; the skater checks the brake.  
They roll one careful lap and finish at the gate.  

**Ending**
The rink goes dark; the dancers leave their skates in hand.  
The mop is rinsed and hung up straight beside the band.  
The manager locks the door; the floor is smooth and dry.  
A final wheel spins once, then stops beneath the sky.
