# Rap Battle with the Rubber Cart

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rap-battle bravado, rapid internal rhymes, call-and-response boasts, and a rival whose cart squeak steals the beat*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Heavy kick and crisp hi-hats; the cart's squeak must land between the rival's rhymes.*

Verse 1
A capuchin MC stepped up to the market floor,
He flexed his gold shoelaces and kicked the swinging door.
He rapped about his rhythm, his speed, his mighty flow,
Then pushed a rubber cart and heard it squeak below.

Chorus
Squeak on the beat, keep the wheels in line,
Talk big, roll quick, but the squeak is mine.
Step to the mic, make a boast, then retreat,
The cart got bars and it owns this beat.

Verse 2
The gibbon rival answered with a rhyme about his shoes,
His cart went squeak-squeak-squeak and interrupted every cue.
The crowd began to bounce whenever wheels went past,
The MC tried to out-rhyme it; the squeak came back too fast.

Chorus
Squeak on the beat, keep the wheels in line,
Talk big, roll quick, but the squeak is mine.
Step to the mic, make a boast, then retreat,
The cart got bars and it owns this beat.

Bridge
The vendor said, "No battle here; my peaches need their space,"
The rivals tried to park the cart and keep a steady pace.
They loosened up the axle; the squeak dropped half a tone,
The crowd booed the quiet wheels and asked for one more moan.

Verse 3
The MC rolled it backward, then the wheel began to sing,
The rivals shared the handle and made the cart a ring.
They used the squeak as percussion, each verse on the right,
The peaches sold out early in the middle of the night.

Chorus
Squeak on the beat, keep the wheels in line,
Talk big, roll quick, but the squeak is mine.
Step to the mic, make a boast, then retreat,
The cart got bars and it owns this beat.

Ending
The judges gave the cart a mic and gave the rappers fruit,
The cart performed an encore with a very squeaky boot.
