# The Watermelon Parade

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soca carnival energy, call-and-response hooks, and a parade float built around a fruit that keeps rolling off its platform*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Drive the percussion forward; use the crowd response when the watermelon rolls through the dancers.*

[Verse 1]
The band lined up on Main Street, bright feathers in the sun,
A watermelon float rolled out before the drums begun.
A gibbon pulled the handle; the float began to sway,
The melon slipped its ribbon and went bouncing down the way.

[Chorus]
Roll, melon, roll, we follow your line,
Shake to the left and turn on time.
Roll, melon, roll, the crowd's in a wave,
We dance behind the fruit that we came out to save.

[Verse 2]
The drummers chased the melon with a rhythm in their feet,
The dancers moved around it, keeping clear of every street.
A capuchin reached down to catch it near the square,
It bounced into a feather fan and sailed up in the air.

[Chorus]
Roll, melon, roll, we follow your line,
Shake to the left and turn on time.
Roll, melon, roll, the crowd's in a wave,
We dance behind the fruit that we came out to save.

[Bridge]
The melon landed safely in a basket by the band,
The drummer played a slower beat; the dancers formed a hand.
They lifted up the basket and resumed the parade,
The fruit was now the centerpiece, the platform freshly made.

[Final Chorus]
Roll, melon, roll, now stay in your place,
We dance in a circle and give you some space.
Roll, melon, roll, the crowd's in a wave,
We dance beside the fruit; it no longer needs saving.

[Ending]
The melon rode to the square and received its own sash.
