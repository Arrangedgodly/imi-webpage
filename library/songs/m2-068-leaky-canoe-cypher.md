# The Cypher for the Leaky Canoe

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rap-battle cyphers, escalating boasts, and punchlines that must land on the beat*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Dry boom-bap beat with a crowd response; each verse should boast bigger while the canoe keeps sinking lower.*

Verse 1
A macaque steps into the circle with a paddle held in style, with my paddle held in style,  
I rhyme so fast the riverbank has listened for a mile.  
My opponent wears a life vest and a helmet made of tin,  
He says his canoe's the fastest; I can see the water in.  
He boasts about his trophies, I boast about my flow,  
Then both our boats take on a splash and drift a little low.  

Chorus
Talk your talk, stay on the beat,  
Keep your feet inside the seat.  
Drop a line, the crowd says "Whoa!"  
Don't drop gear into the flow.  
One more rhyme, one more boast,  
The leak is winning, not the host.  
Talk your talk, make it clean,  
Patch the hole between the scenes.  

Verse 2
He says he can outpaddle me with one hand tied behind,  
I say I can repair a seam and keep my verse in time.  
The crowd says "Ooh," the bass drum thumps, the canoe takes on a wave,  
We trade a rhyme for duct tape and the dry bag that I save.  
The judge leans in to hear the punchline, hears a steady drip,  
He calls the battle even and directs us to the ship.  

Bridge
We dock beside the boathouse, both competitors are soaked,  
The coach checks all the seams and says the boast was badly stoked.  
We fix the little rivet, then we test the hull once more,  
The crowd chants "Patch that canoe," from shore to storage door.  

Chorus
Talk your talk, stay on the beat,  
Keep your feet inside the seat.  
Drop a line, the crowd says "Whoa!"  
Don't drop gear into the flow.  
One more rhyme, one more boast,  
The leak is winning, not the host.  
Talk your talk, make it clean,  
Patch the hole between the scenes.  

Ending
We shake hands in the boat shed, the judge records the score,  
The winner is the repair kit, we all rap by the oar.
