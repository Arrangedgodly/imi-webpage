# Polka for the Loading Dock

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: brisk polka dance bands and their unstoppable oom-pah momentum*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Accordion and tuba drive a fast polka; the dancers keep trying to pause for sensible dock safety instructions.*

Verse 1
The forklift's backing up, the band begins to play,  
The dock crew starts a polka in the marked pedestrian way.  
A capuchin with a clipboard says, "The loading zone is clear,"  
The accordion says "oom-pah" and the crates begin to veer.  
We step to left, we step to right, then spin around the sign,  
The safety cones are dancing in a very crooked line.  

Chorus
Oom-pah, keep your toes behind the stripe,  
Oom-pah, don't waltz beside the pipe.  
Turn your partner, check the pallet, count to four and stop,  
The polka's got a chorus, but the forklift has the dock.  
Oom-pah, let the warning buzzer play,  
Oom-pah, we'll dance another day.  

Verse 2
The tuba takes a corner wide; it bumps a stack of lids,  
They roll like silver saucers under all the dancing kids.  
The foreman calls a safety break; the fiddler takes a bow,  
The dancers form a safety line and show us where to go.  
We move the crates one meter back, we chalk the proper space,  
Then dance inside the taped-off square at a polka pace.  

Bridge
The loading bell rings twice; we freeze upon the beat,  
A mouse with tiny earmuffs carries labels on his feet.  
The driver waves us through, the dock is clear and dry,  
We bow to the accordion and let the pallets fly.  

Chorus
Oom-pah, keep your toes behind the stripe,  
Oom-pah, don't waltz beside the pipe.  
Turn your partner, check the pallet, count to four and stop,  
The polka's got a chorus, but the forklift has the dock.  
Oom-pah, let the warning buzzer play,  
Oom-pah, we'll dance another day.  

Ending
The band packs up the tuba and the cones return to place,  
The foreman taps his clipboard; we polka out with grace.
