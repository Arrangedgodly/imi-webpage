# The Heroic Tenor Buys a Train Sandwich

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operatic declarations of appetite and heroes demanding grandeur from the limited menu on a moving train*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Operatic recitative, piano and a trolley bell; the attendant speaks plain menu choices over the grand vocal.*

[Recitative]
What banquet waits upon this journey?  
What fragrant feast shall warm the day?  
The trolley wheels approach my carriage.  
The attendant asks what I will pay.  
I call for roasted golden plenty,  
A platter worthy of a king.  
She holds two little wrapped triangles,  
And asks which filling I am ordering.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Bread, little bread, contain my longing,  
Cheese, little cheese, receive my heart.  
I crossed the plains expecting banquets,  
The lunch comes in a very modest part.  
Rise, mighty hunger, name your portion,  
The trolley holds the limits of my art.  

[Verse]
I choose the cheese and tomato for a feast beneath the sun,  
She scans it on the little till and gives me only one.  
I peel the wrapper carefully and breathe the great bouquet,  
The passenger beside me asks if I will sing all day.  
I take a very measured bite and find the bread quite good,  
The attendant moves the trolley to the next row as she should.  
I cannot call her back to praise the balance of the cheese,  
My mouth has got the evidence and needs a moment's peace.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Bread, little bread, contain my longing,  
Cheese, little cheese, receive my heart.  
I crossed the plains expecting banquets,  
The lunch comes in a very modest part.  
Rise, mighty hunger, name your portion,  
The trolley holds the limits of my art.  

[Recitative]
The train approaches my own station.  
I fold the wrapper neatly in my hand.  
The attendant returns with the trolley,  
Her afternoon procession nearly planned.  
I thank her for the little feast she sold me,  
She asks if I would like a cup of tea.  
I choose a small one with a little milk in,  
The passenger can hear her speak to me.  

[Final Aria]
Bread, little bread, contain my longing,  
Cheese, little cheese, receive my heart.  
The tea has got a lid upon its longing,  
Its little lid stays fast as train and platform part.  
Rise, mighty hunger, name your portion,  
The receipt is in the pocket near my heart.  

[Spoken Tag]
On the platform I unfold the bill.  
I paid for a sandwich, a drink, and a refill.  
The attendant gave the tea a second little pour,  
A generous encore with no tenor asking more.
