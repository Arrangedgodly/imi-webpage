# The Boy Band Has a Fire Drill

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: boy-band choreography, synchronized poses, and dramatic mid-song costume reveals*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Glossy pop harmonies and crisp dance breaks; the fire marshal interrupts the formation without breaking tempo.*

Verse 1
Five macaques step together in a line of silver shoes,  
We point toward the camera on the beat we always use.  
The lead sings, "You're the reason," with a hand across his heart,  
The marshal holds a clipboard and says, "Please don't block the exit part."  
We spin to face the spotlight, then we freeze in perfect V,  
The marshal asks which door is clear; we all look at the key.  

Chorus
Step left, lock right, turn and shine,  
Keep the chorus in a line.  
Don't make a pyramid near the stairs,  
The fire marshal needs fresh air.  
Hands up, hands down, clear the lane,  
We'll hit the pose when safe again.  
Step left, lock right, turn and shine,  
The exit sign is not a sign.  

Verse 2
The bass drops out, the jackets split, the sequins start to rain,  
We planned a quick reveal, but one sleeve is caught again.  
Our drummer finds a silver star stuck underneath his shoe,  
He keeps the steady backbeat while the stagehands pull it through.  
We march the fans toward exits in our practiced dance-step flow,  
The marshal says, "That's useful," then asks us all to go.  

Bridge
The alarm was just a test; the crew assembles in the hall,  
The lead attempts a hair flip and gets static on the wall.  
We sing the safety chorus in five-part harmony,  
The marshal gives a thumbs-up and hands out water free.  

Chorus
Step left, lock right, turn and shine,  
Keep the chorus in a line.  
Don't make a pyramid near the stairs,  
The fire marshal needs fresh air.  
Hands up, hands down, clear the lane,  
We'll hit the pose when safe again.  
Step left, lock right, turn and shine,  
The exit sign is not a sign.  

Ending
The final pose is orderly, each singer spaced apart,  
The marshal leaves the building first and takes our glitter chart.
