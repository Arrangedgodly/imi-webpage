# Ambient Music for the Busy Beaver

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ambient minimalism, long silences, and gallery audiences searching for the intended listening position*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Soft sustained synthesizer and room tone; leave long rests, but let the beaver's construction noises become the real composition.*

Verse 1
A macaque artist floats one note through the gallery at noon, through the gallery at noon,  
The audience stands very still beneath a paper moon.  
The artist says the silence is the center of the piece,  
A beaver in the corner brings a branch and asks for lease.  
He sets it by the speaker, then he taps the wooden frame,  
The note begins to tremble, and the curator says his name.  

Chorus
Breathe in, listen low,  
Let the quiet come and go.  
Hold the pause, leave space to hear,  
The beaver's building over here.  
One soft tone, one branch, one more,  
The gallery has a second door.  
Breathe out, let it fade,  
The new wall's self-made.  

Verse 2
The artist asks the beaver if he understands the art,  
He says, "I hear a river," then he starts to build a chart.  
The curator says the pond is not included in the show,  
The beaver tests the room tone with a gentle tail-tap flow.  
The speakers hum a deeper note, the audience leans in close,  
The beaver adds a branch support beneath the gallery post.  

Bridge
The gallery gets quiet; the wall is nearly done,  
The artist calls it minimal, the beaver calls it fun.  
A visitor asks where the music's coming from tonight,  
The beaver points to all the sticks and says, "That one sounds right."  

Chorus
Breathe in, listen low,  
Let the quiet come and go.  
Hold the pause, leave space to hear,  
The beaver's building over here.  
One soft tone, one branch, one more,  
The gallery has a second door.  
Breathe out, let it fade,  
The new wall's self-made.  

Ending
The show closes at six, the beaver takes his branch away,  
The silence fills the gallery and stays there all the day.
