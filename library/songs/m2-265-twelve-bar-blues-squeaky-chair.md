# The Twelve-Bar Blues of the Squeaky Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: twelve-bar blues, bent-note laments, and turning a minor inconvenience into a life's burden*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a slow shuffle, blue guitar bends, and a weary vocal; let the chair squeak answer each complaint.*

[Verse 1]
I sat down in my chair and heard a squeak from underneath,  
I leaned a little left and heard it answer with its teeth.  
Oh, that squeak has followed me from breakfast into noon,  
It sings a little solo like a rusty spoon.  
A baboon said, "Try oil," I said, "That sounds too easy,"  
I played a sad guitar lick and made the whole room queasy.  
The chair cried "ee-ow" whenever I would shift,  
Now even all the clocks are keeping time with my lament.  

[Chorus]
Squeak, chair, moan for me,  
You've got the blues in C and E.  
Squeak, chair, tell the room my pain,  
One little hinge can bring a storm of rain.  
Squeak, chair, don't you quit,  
I wrote twelve bars about this bit.  

[Verse 2]
The gibbon brought a wrench and turned a screw around,  
The squeak became a whisper, then it made no sound.  
I missed the little chorus that had haunted every day,  
I nudged the seat to call it back; it would not have its say.  
The room was now too quiet for my mournful guitar tune,  
I tapped the leg against the floor and summoned up the groove.  
The chair stayed silent, solid, patient as can be,  
So I sang the blues about the wrench that stole my key.  

[Chorus]
Squeak, chair, moan for me,  
You've got the blues in C and E.  
Squeak, chair, tell the room my pain,  
One little hinge can bring a storm of rain.  
Squeak, chair, don't you quit,  
I wrote twelve bars about this bit.  

[Bridge]
The wrench went in the toolbox; the guitarist bent a string,  
The singer found a new complaint about the lack of swing.  
The chair held steady through the verse, the chorus and the tag,  
The blues found one more little thing: a folded music rag.  

[Final Chorus]
Quiet chair, stay strong and true,  
The blues can find another thing to do.  
Quiet chair, don't creak for me,  
I need a rest before the next key.  
Quiet chair, hold up the band,  
The wrench is waiting just offhand.  

[Ending]
The chair never squeaked again; the singer leaned on the mic stand.
