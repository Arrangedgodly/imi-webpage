# The Canoe Pulling Work Song

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: call-and-response work songs that coordinate a crew's shared pulling rhythm*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a grounded hand drum and a strong lead call; let the response keep returning the canoe to the same muddy patch until the crew changes tactics.*

[Verse 1]
The canoe had settled sideways on the muddy river bend,  
A baboon called the pulling beat; a gibbon joined the end.  
The crew took hold of ropes and leaned together in the sand,  
The lead sang "Pull it forward!" and they pulled with all the band.  
The canoe moved half a meter, then the rope went slack,  
A capuchin called "Again!" and the crew pulled it right back.  
They answered every measure with a rising steady sound,  
The canoe had not escaped; it had circled halfway round.  

[Chorus]
Pull together, hold the line,  
Count the beat and pull in time.  
Heave and haul, the water calls,  
The river waits beyond the walls.  
Pull together, don't let go,  
The canoe will move when we all know.  

[Verse 2]
The lead called "One more pull!" and the crew bent to the task,  
The stern rose from the mud; the bow slipped under grass.  
They changed their angle slightly, turned the rope around a tree,  
The call became a rhythm and the answer came in three.  
The lead cried "Forward!" loudly; the crew pulled toward the bank,  
The canoe swung from the shallows and bumped against a plank.  
The drummer changed the pattern; the rope moved clean and slow,  
The boat slid into open water with a gentle undertow.  

[Chorus]
Pull together, hold the line,  
Count the beat and pull in time.  
Heave and haul, the water calls,  
The river waits beyond the walls.  
Pull together, don't let go,  
The canoe will move when we all know.  

[Bridge]
They passed the rope around once more to steady the canoe,  
The lead called "Release!" and every hand knew what to do.  
The boat held level in the current, the crew climbed in,  
The drummer set the paddle beat and let the trip begin.  

[Final Chorus]
Pull together, keep the line,  
Paddle easy, travel fine.  
Heave and haul, the water calls,  
The bend is open, past the walls.  
Pull together, ride the flow,  
The canoe is moving where we want to go.  

[Ending]
The lead stopped singing; the crew kept rowing on the beat.
