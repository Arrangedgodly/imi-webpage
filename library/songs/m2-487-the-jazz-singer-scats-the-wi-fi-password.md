# The Jazz Singer Scats the Wi-Fi Password

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: improvised jazz syllables and performers who cannot distinguish a whimsical vocal phrase from a useful sequence of characters*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Walking bass and brushed drums; the singer scats the password, while the host insists on a spoken exact spelling.*

[Verse 1]
The café booked a jazz trio to play beside the stove,  
I sang a little opening as the walking bass line drove.  
The host asked for the password to connect his music pad,  
The manager supplied a card with every letter that she had.  
I read it like a chorus: "Blue cup seven, doo be day,"  
The host typed every syllable and couldn't find a way.  
The manager said "BlueCup7" with a very patient face,  
I sang a little ornament and added "shoo" in place.  

[Scat Chorus]
Doo-bap, blue cup, seven, seven, sky,  
The host has typed the whole refrain and doesn't know just why.  
Shoo-be, doo-be, turn the little line,  
The password has eight characters; I offer forty-nine.  
Bop-bop, stop-stop, let the bass go by,  
The manager would like the extra syllables to die.  

[Verse 2]
The host put down his little pad until the solo ceased,  
The manager placed the printed card beside his cup of tea.  
He typed the letters exactly with their capitals in place,  
The pad connected to the café with a small approving face.  
He opened up the score he needed for the second tune,  
The bassist played the opening as the light filled up the room.  
I sang the melody quite clearly till the middle solo came,  
Then gave the password back its extra syllables and name.  

[Scat Chorus]
Doo-bap, blue cup, seven, seven, sky,  
The host has typed the whole refrain and doesn't know just why.  
Shoo-be, doo-be, turn the little line,  
The password has eight characters; I offer forty-nine.  
Bop-bop, stop-stop, let the bass go by,  
The manager would like the extra syllables to die.  

[Break]
She changed the café password at the ending of the week,  
The host received a new card with no tune that he could speak.  
I asked to see it quickly for a possible encore,  
She put it in his pocket and she closed the office door.  

[Final Scat]
Doo-bap, blue cup, seven, seven, sky,  
My old refrain connects to nothing, however hard we try.  
Shoo-be, doo-be, turn the little line,  
The host has got the current one; the obsolete one's mine.  
Bop-bop, stop-stop, let the bass go by,  
A customer enjoys the song but asks for proper Wi-Fi.
