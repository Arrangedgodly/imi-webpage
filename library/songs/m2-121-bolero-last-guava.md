# Bolero for the Last Guava

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bolero ballad conventions, patient romantic repetition, and a fruit seller who keeps moving the last guava farther down the counter*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Slow bolero guitar and brushed percussion; stretch each refrain until the seller moves the guava and changes its price.*

Verse 1
The fruit stand closed at sunset; one guava still remained,
A gibbon sang a bolero while the evening breeze complained.
He asked the seller softly if the fruit might come his way,
She slid it past the oranges and charged him for delay.

Chorus
Guava, wait, don't roll away,
Stay until the end of day.
One slow song, one gentle plea,
Save the sweetest fruit for me.

Verse 2
He sang of leaves and moonlight, then repeated every line,
The seller moved the guava past the pears and changed the sign.
The price went up by one seed while his long note held the air,
The seller charged him rent for all the notes suspended there.

Chorus
Guava, wait, don't roll away,
Stay until the end of day.
One slow song, one gentle plea,
Save the sweetest fruit for me.

Bridge
The guitar returned to minor; he knelt beside the stall,
The seller rolled the guava toward the far end of the wall.
He reached to catch it, missed by half a hand, and sang again,
The seller sold his silence back and charged for the refrain.

Verse 3
He stopped the song and paid the price before it rose anew,
The seller placed the guava in a bag of green and blue.
His bolero had been lovely; it had cost him every seed,
He carried home one fruit and practiced speed for what he'd need.

Chorus
Guava, wait, don't roll away,
Stay until the end of day.
One slow song, one gentle plea,
Save the sweetest fruit for me.

Ending
The stand was closed, the guava safe, the guitar softly played,
Next time he would buy first, then sing the serenade.
