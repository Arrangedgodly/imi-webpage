# The Opera of the Stuck Window

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: grand opera arias, melodramatic vocal holds, and implausible emotional stakes*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: A full operatic soprano rides piano and strings; stretch the high note until the janitor raises the window.*

Verse 1
O window, stubborn window, release the summer breeze,  
My soprano shakes the curtains and rattles both the keys.  
The audience in the library turns pages through my woe,  
The librarian whispers, "Please sing softer; people study below."  
I raise my arms, the orchestra swells, the latch refuses still,  
A gibbon in the balcony says, "Try lifting up the sill."  

Chorus
Ahhhh, let the casement rise!  
Ahhhh, I plead to painted skies!  
My heart is trapped behind this pane,  
My voice can summon wind and rain.  
Ahhhh, the ending waits for me,  
Until the janitor finds the key.  

Verse 2
I sing of lost horizons and a room without a breeze,  
The librarian hands me a small can labeled "Squeak Release."  
I reach a note so high and long, the chandeliers all shake,  
A book slides from the shelf and lands beside a rake.  
The janitor arrives at last, with oil and modest pride,  
He turns the handle half a turn; the window opens wide.  

Bridge
The orchestra cuts off mid-chord, the audience can breathe,  
The janitor says, "It was stuck because you tugged the sleeve."  
My aria falls to speaking range, my tragic brow unbends,  
The librarian says, "That is the first verse that makes sense."  

Chorus
Ahhhh, let the casement rise!  
Ahhhh, I plead to painted skies!  
My heart is trapped behind this pane,  
My voice can summon wind and rain.  
Ahhhh, the ending waits for me,  
Until the janitor finds the key.  

Ending
I sing one final note of thanks, the window swings once more,  
The librarian asks for silence; I whisper through the door.
