# The Musical Hero Misses the Ticket Inspector

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: lavish film-musical travel songs whose hero becomes so absorbed in choreography that the actual journey's requirements disappear*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Sweeping strings, lively percussion and a joyful ensemble; the ticket inspector's practical refrain cuts across each grand dance flourish.*

[Verse 1]
I boarded with a suitcase and a heart prepared to soar,  
The train began to carry me beyond the station door.  
I saw my love reflected in the window's passing light,  
And sang about the journey that would reunite us tonight.  
My fellow travelers clapped along with fingers on their knees,  
I danced a little step beside the luggage racks with ease.  
The inspector walked toward me with a scanner in his hand,  
I took him for a dancer and invited him to stand.  

[Chorus]
Come away, cross the land,  
The whole carriage has a rhythm that can join us hand in hand.  
Come away, let love shine,  
The inspector needs my ticket and I give him another line.  
Move with me, hear the wheels,  
The journey has a chorus and a very ordinary bill.  

[Verse 2]
He asked again quite clearly for the ticket on my phone,  
I sang the word "ticket" as if love were taking me home.  
The passengers stopped clapping and the rhythm thinned to air,  
The inspector held his scanner with a patient little stare.  
I fetched the phone from my coat and opened up the code,  
He checked it, thanked me kindly and continued down the coach.  
The fellow at the window asked when we would start again,  
I said the bridge was coming after my embarrassed refrain.  

[Chorus]
Come away, cross the land,  
The whole carriage has a rhythm that can join us hand in hand.  
Come away, let love shine,  
The inspector needs my ticket and I give him another line.  
Move with me, hear the wheels,  
The journey has a chorus and a very ordinary bill.  

[Bridge]
My love met me at the platform with her ticket in her palm,  
She said she'd heard my singing from the carriage rolling on.  
I asked if she would dance with me beneath the station clock,  
She pointed to the exit gates; we scanned before we walked.  

[Final Chorus]
Come away, cross the land,  
We have cleared the ticket barriers and now can hold each hand.  
Come away, let love shine,  
The inspector waves from farther down and knows my paid-up line.  
Move with me, hear the wheels,  
My love has brought the choreography; I bring the ticket still.
