# The Bolero Outlasts the Birthday Candles

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: slow romantic birthday serenades whose elaborate introductions delay the actual celebration*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Slow bolero rhythm, muted trumpet and a vocalist holding every affectionate syllable.*

[Verse 1]
We brought the cake at seven with its candles burning bright,  
Our uncle brought his trumpet for the sweetest song that night.  
He played a long introduction and the singer closed his eyes,  
The birthday girl leaned closer as the frosting lost its rise.  
He sang her name in syllables that traveled round the room,  
The wax became a little lake beneath the roses' bloom.  

[Chorus]
Many happy moments, many lovely years,  
Let the music lengthen every memory that appears.  
May your days be gentle, may your nights be sweet,  
The cake is getting warmer by the rhythm of the beat.  
Take a breath and make a wish,  
We have nearly sung the words that let you reach the dish.  

[Verse 2]
The singer praised her kindness to a string of long guitar,  
The trumpeter replied as if the kitchen were a star.  
Her sister held the cutting knife and watched the candles lean,  
The youngest cousin whispered that the wish must stay unseen.  
The singer took a second verse describing all her grace,  
The birthday girl held one blown candle up beside his face.  

[Chorus]
Many happy moments, many lovely years,  
Let the music lengthen every memory that appears.  
May your days be gentle, may your nights be sweet,  
The cake is getting warmer by the rhythm of the beat.  
Take a breath and make a wish,  
We have nearly sung the words that let you reach the dish.  

[Bridge]
She blew the rest out quietly and cut a generous slice,  
The trumpet paused for breathing; she offered him a slice.  
Our uncle put his trumpet down and took the cake instead,  
The singer saw the plates go round and shortened what he said.  

[Final Chorus]
Many happy moments, many lovely years,  
Let the music lengthen every memory that appears.  
May your days be gentle, may your nights be sweet,  
The cake is going quickly and there's very little to eat.  
Take a breath and make a wish,  
The singer ends the final note and finds an empty dish.
