# The Math-Rock Band Divides the Pizza

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: math-rock's intricate rhythmic subdivisions and bands that apply their favorite odd meters to an ordinary shared meal*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Angular guitars, changing accents and clipped vocals; the pizza cutter ticks across the counter between phrases.*

[Verse 1]
We ordered one large pizza when rehearsal finally ceased,  
The drummer said that seven slices had the nicest beat.  
The bassist wanted five parts with a pause before the third,  
The guitarist drew a diagram; the singer didn't hear a word.  
He ate one little triangle while we marked the circle's rim,  
The diagram became outdated very quickly because of him.  
The drummer counted missing crust and changed his pattern twice,  
The delivery rider waited for the money, not advice.  

[Chorus]
One, two, three, cut across the beat,  
There are four quite hungry people and a circle full of cheese.  
Five, six, pause, start the count anew,  
Somebody has eaten something while the diagram grew.  
One, two, three, let the fractions fall,  
The pizza doesn't care what time signature we call.  

[Verse 2]
We paid the rider promptly and he left us to divide,  
The bassist took a second slice and set the score aside.  
The guitarist said the pattern should account for every bite,  
The singer said the pizza would be cold by closing time.  
The drummer put the cutter down and handed plates around,  
We took the pieces nearest us without a single sound.  
The guitarist wrote a final bar with one long resting chord,  
Then ate the crust in silence while the others cleared the board.  

[Chorus]
One, two, three, cut across the beat,  
There are four quite hungry people and a circle full of cheese.  
Five, six, pause, start the count anew,  
Somebody has eaten something while the diagram grew.  
One, two, three, let the fractions fall,  
The pizza doesn't care what time signature we call.  

[Bridge]
The drummer saved the diagram and played its rhythm next,  
The bassist followed carefully; the guitarist looked perplexed.  
The singer put a line above the complicated score,  
"Order two next rehearsal. We should probably eat more."  

[Final Chorus]
One, two, three, cut across the beat,  
The next rehearsal has two pizzas and four people who can eat.  
Five, six, pause, let the count go free,  
The diagram has found a place underneath the extra cheese.  
One, two, three, let the fractions fall,  
Our next record is called Lunch and has the simplest meter of all.
