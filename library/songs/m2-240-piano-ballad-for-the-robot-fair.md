# One Chord to Start

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dramatic piano ballads, swelling key changes, and a science-fair robot that needs one simple instruction repeated*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Begin with a single piano chord and build to a grand finish; let the robot's encore beep follow the final pause.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque brought a robot to the school fair hall,
It blinked beneath the banner and did not move at all.
The judge asked for a demonstration; the lights were turning gold,
The pianist played one gentle chord and waited in the cold.

[Chorus]
One chord to start, one note to stay,
The robot hears the music, then it rolls away.
One chord to start, one pause to keep,
The ballad grows while the wheels still sleep.
One chord to start, one note to stay,
The robot hears the music, then it rolls away.
One chord to start, one pause to keep,
The ballad grows while the wheels still sleep.

[Verse 2]
The singer raised a high note; the robot's eye lit blue,
The audience leaned forward as the bass line slowly grew.
The judge asked for a turn; the robot did not bend,
The pianist played a bridge that sounded like the end.

[Chorus]
One chord to start, one note to stay,
The robot hears the music, then it rolls away.
One chord to start, one pause to keep,
The ballad grows while the wheels still sleep.
One chord to start, one note to stay,
The robot hears the music, then it rolls away.
One chord to start, one pause to keep,
The ballad grows while the wheels still sleep.

[Bridge]
The macaque pressed a button; the robot moved an inch,
The choir held a harmony; the judge began to flinch.
It rolled across the table and stopped beside the prize,
The ballad hit its key change; the robot blinked its eyes.

[Final Chorus]
One chord to start, one note to stay,
The button woke its wheels, and now it rolls away.
One chord to start, one pause to keep,
The robot won the prize while the pianist lost sleep.

[Ending]
The robot played one beep; the crowd called it a song.
