# Gregorian Chant at the Laundromat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Gregorian chant and solemn monastic repetition applied to a jammed washer*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Slow unison chant with a resonant laundry-room echo; the spin cycle supplies the drone.*

Verse 1
At midnight in the laundromat the capuchins stood still,
Their robes were folded neatly on a plastic window sill.
The washer held one sock and hummed a note of deep concern,
The attendant pressed the button marked "Do not make it turn."

Chorus
O washer, turn, but turn with care,
Release the sock that circles there.
O washer, hear our solemn plea,
Return the pair in harmony.

Verse 2
They sang the plea in Latin vowels, low and long and round,
The machine replied with one wet thunk, a most persuasive sound.
A lemur choir began the verse again from line one,
Each repetition raised the rent on minutes not yet done.

Chorus
O washer, turn, but turn with care,
Release the sock that circles there.
O washer, hear our solemn plea,
Return the pair in harmony.

Bridge
The attendant tried to stop the hymn; the choir held a note,
The washer's timer counted down while water filled its throat.
A macaque reached to lift the lid, but paused at sacred "amen";
The sock emerged, then vanished in the foam and went around again.

Verse 3
They changed the chant to "open now," in ever-deeper tone,
The washer popped the lid and flung one sock across the stone.
Its twin sat in a pocket, dry, inside the cantor's sleeve;
The choir bowed to both of them, too solemn to believe.

Chorus
O washer, turn, but turn with care,
Release the sock that circles there.
O washer, hear our solemn plea,
Return the pair in harmony.

Ending
The monks left with one clean sock apiece, no pair to reconcile,
The washer kept their echo and sang "spin" for quite a while.
