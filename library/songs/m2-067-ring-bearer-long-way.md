# The Ring Bearer Takes the Long Way

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: wedding processional crescendos, ring-bearer suspense, and sentimental ceremony pacing*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Start with a stately processional organ; let the tempo stall politely every time the ring bearer stops to inspect a flower.*

Verse 1
The guests are seated, candles glow, the organ starts to play,  
The bride waits by the garden gate, the groom looks down the way.  
A tiny macaque in a sash has rings upon a pillow,  
He spots a beetle on the path and follows it through willow.  
The organ holds a noble chord; the guests all turn to see,  
The usher whispers, "Keep it moving," softly as can be.  

Chorus
Walk the aisle, take it slow,  
Keep the ribbon tied in bow.  
Step by step, don't chase the bee,  
The vows are waiting patiently.  
Walk the aisle, hold them tight,  
Don't inspect each candlelight.  
The organ's grand, the flowers sway,  
The ring has got a wedding day.  

Verse 2
He reaches halfway, then he stops to count the garden stones,  
The couple smiles and waves him on; the organ changes tones.  
A squirrel tosses petals, one lands upon his nose,  
He sneezes once, the pillow tips, but neither ringlet goes.  
The best man starts to help him, then the officiant says wait,  
The child must bring the rings himself; the aisle is not a race.  

Bridge
He reaches both the couple, offers rings upon the cloth,  
The bride says, "Thank you, little friend," the guests all laugh and cough.  
The vows begin, the organ swells, the candles shine anew,  
The bearer finds the beetle waiting by shoe number two.  

Chorus
Walk the aisle, take it slow,  
Keep the ribbon tied in bow.  
Step by step, don't chase the bee,  
The vows are waiting patiently.  
Walk the aisle, hold them tight,  
Don't inspect each candlelight.  
The organ's grand, the flowers sway,  
The ring has got a wedding day.  

Ending
The couple says their vows at last, the guests rise as they leave,  
The bearer gets a tiny cake and one loose flower sleeve.
