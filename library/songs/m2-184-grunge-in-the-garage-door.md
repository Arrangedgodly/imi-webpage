# Grunge at the Garage Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: grunge's fuzzed guitars, weary vocals, and the emotional weight assigned to an amplifier that will not open*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Fuzz guitar and loose, heavy drums; build from quiet verse to noisy chorus, then unplug safely.*

Verse 1
The band stood in a garage with the door shut tight,
The amp had lost its signal in the middle of the night.
The singer said, "We're trapped here," though the side door opened wide,
The drummer hit a slow beat while the guitarist sighed.

Chorus
Pull the cord, let the door fall,
Static singing through the wall.
Fuzz the note and drown the sound,
We are stuck but still around.

Verse 2
The cable crossed the pedal and the plug had slipped away,
The guitar made a hollow hum where the power should have stayed.
A capuchin replaced it, the amp began to roar,
The singer turned the volume up and shook the garage door.

Chorus
Pull the cord, let the door fall,
Static singing through the wall.
Fuzz the note and drown the sound,
We are stuck but still around.

Bridge
The neighbors tapped the ceiling with a broom against the beat,
The band mistook the warning for a crowd out in the street.
They played the chorus louder till a fuse began to glow,
The bass player unplugged it and the room went soft below.

Verse 3
The garage door was never locked; the cord was hanging near,
The band had blocked the entrance with their cases and their gear.
They moved the amps outside and played the song at low,
The neighbors came to listen when the fuzz began to flow.

Chorus
Pull the cord, let the door fall,
Static singing through the wall.
Fuzz the note and drown the sound,
We are stuck but still around.

Ending
The band left room beside the door for everyone to pass,
The final chord rang softly through the open pane of glass.
