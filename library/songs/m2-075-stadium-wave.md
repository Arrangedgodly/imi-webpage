# Stadium Wave on the Wrong Side

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: stadium-rock anthems, audience call-and-response, and the choreographed crowd wave*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Big drums and sustained guitar chords; the crowd wave should keep getting misdirected by the ushers.*

Verse 1
The stadium lights are blazing, the band has struck a chord,  
A gibbon in the upper seats waves both arms toward the floor.  
The crowd begins a rising wave that travels to the left,  
It hits a section full of ushers, who direct it to the desk.  
The singers hold a power note, the scoreboard flashes bright,  
The wave turns back and rolls again against the traffic light.  

Chorus
Raise it up, pass it on,  
Wave across the rows till dawn.  
Left side, right side, don't collide,  
The ushers are along for the ride.  
Hands up high, then bring them down,  
Let the sound go round the town.  
Raise it up, pass it on,  
We missed the beat but kept it strong.  

Verse 2
A macaque starts a chant for seats behind the goal,  
The drummer answers with a fill that shakes a paper bowl.  
The wave reaches the concession stand; the cashier ducks below,  
A basket of soft pretzels begins to gently go.  
The usher saves the basket, then joins in with the crowd,  
The singer points at section eight and tells them to be loud.  

Bridge
The wave returns to section one, the band repeats the line,  
A custodian holds up a sign: "Please keep the aisle clear."  
The crowd obeys, the path stays open, the final notes ring out,  
The stadium sings one quiet chord and files the team report.  

Chorus
Raise it up, pass it on,  
Wave across the rows till dawn.  
Left side, right side, don't collide,  
The ushers are along for the ride.  
Hands up high, then bring them down,  
Let the sound go round the town.  
Raise it up, pass it on,  
We missed the beat but kept it strong.  

Ending
The band packs up, the wave rolls once through an empty row,  
The cleaner raises one last hand, then tells the lights to go.
