# The Chanting Choir Records the Voicemail

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: solemn chant arrangements whose spacious phrasing makes a telephone greeting far longer than the machine allows*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Unaccompanied unison chant with a resonant room sound; a blunt electronic beep ends each overambitious phrase.*

[Verse 1]
The abbey had a telephone upon a little stand,  
The visitors kept calling for directions through the land.  
The choirmaster requested that we record a greeting there,  
He said our voices carried peace and calm into the air.  
We gathered round the handset in a single measured line,  
And sang the word "Welcome" with a long and solemn climb.  
The machine gave out a sharp beep as we reached the final vowel,  
It saved our first five seconds and our greeting ended foul.  

[Chorus]
Call, gentle friend, hear the ringing bell,  
We are trying to describe the way to find our quiet cell.  
Wait, gentle friend, let the long note fall,  
The little machine gives twenty seconds to us all.  
Call, gentle friend, hear the rising choir,  
We have used the whole recording on the word before "arrive."  

[Verse 2]
The choirmaster said to shorten every opening refrain,  
We took a breath together and began the song again.  
We sang "Turn left at the orchard" but the left took far too long,  
The caller heard the orchard without learning where we'd gone.  
The porter wrote a plain short script and held it to the light,  
It gave the gate's address and visiting hours outright.  
We spoke it all in twenty seconds, clearly and at ease,  
The choirmaster said the ending could contain a little peace.  

[Chorus]
Call, gentle friend, hear the ringing bell,  
We are trying to describe the way to find our quiet cell.  
Wait, gentle friend, let the long note fall,  
The little machine gives twenty seconds to us all.  
Call, gentle friend, hear the rising choir,  
We have used the whole recording on the word before "arrive."  

[Bridge]
The porter saved the spoken script without a closing hymn,  
The first arriving visitor said he had followed it.  
He asked about the choir he had heard in last week's call,  
We sang the whole long greeting in the abbey's echoing hall.  

[Final Chorus]
Call, gentle friend, hear the ringing bell,  
The porter gives directions and the choir sings as well.  
Wait, gentle friend, let the long note fall,  
The greeting is a concert if you reach the abbey hall.  
Call, gentle friend, hear our welcome rise,  
The machine remains quite silent for the rest of our replies.
