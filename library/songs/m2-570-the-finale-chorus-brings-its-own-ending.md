# The Finale Chorus Brings Its Own Ending

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical theatre finales that promise one last song and find the company still waiting for its curtain cue*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Full company, bright piano and brass; a visible conductor gives the one final cutoff, and the curtain descends only after it.*

[Verse 1]
The company assembled for the last song of the night,  
The dancers took their places in the warm concluding light.  
The lead sang of tomorrow while the chorus sang of home,  
The conductor found a chorus from an older show in Rome.  
It had been left from last night when that show was on to play,  
The stage manager checked the cue and let the curtain stay.  
The company could see her in the wing beside the door,  
She held a little card that said, "There isn't any more."  

[Chorus]
One last song, one last bow,  
The company has come to where the ending happens now.  
One last song, let it ring,  
The curtain needs the cutoff after everybody sings.  
Raise the hands, bring them down,  
The conductor has an ending and the cast can leave the town.  

[Verse 2]
The conductor closed the extra page and gave the cadence clear,  
The pianist watched his lifted hand and knew the end was near.  
The chorus held the final chord until his fingers closed,  
The stage manager called the curtain as the quiet company posed.  
The audience applauded as the curtain met the floor,  
The company went back to greet them once, then sang no more.  
The lead received a flower and the drummer took his sticks,  
The dancer checked the call sheet for tomorrow's start at six.  

[Chorus]
One last song, one last bow,  
The company has come to where the ending happens now.  
One last song, let it ring,  
The curtain needs the cutoff after everybody sings.  
Raise the hands, bring them down,  
The conductor has an ending and the cast can leave the town.  

[Bridge]
We sing these words tomorrow when we reach the closing scene,  
The extra page is filed away where old rehearsals lean.  
The stage manager has marked the cue in bright and simple ink,  
The conductor checks the score before the company can blink.  

[Final Chorus]
One last song, one last bow,  
The score has got its proper end and everyone knows how.  
Raise the hands, bring them down,  
The company can leave the stage and change its evening gown.  
One last song, let it ring,  
The curtain drops on time; backstage the kettle starts to sing.
