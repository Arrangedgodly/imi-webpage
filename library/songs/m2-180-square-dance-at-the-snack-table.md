# Square Dance at the Snack Table

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: square-dance caller commands, partner swaps, and dancers who turn a snack table into the center of every formation*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Fiddle, banjo, and caller; keep the square formations clear until the chorus sends everyone toward the snack table.*

Verse 1
The caller lined eight dancers in a square beside the wall,
The fiddle played a lively tune and beckoned one and all.
"Swing your partner, do-si-do, then promenade on through,"
The monkeys made a tidy turn and found the table too.

Chorus
Circle left, then circle right,
Pass the crackers, share a bite.
Dosado, the cups are near,
Square dance snacks for everyone here.

Verse 2
"Now trade your partner," called the host, "and allemande your way,"
The dancers crossed the floor and took the plates away.
A tamarin held the lemonade and tried to keep it straight,
The fiddle sped the rhythm up; the cups began to skate.

Chorus
Circle left, then circle right,
Pass the crackers, share a bite.
Dosado, the cups are near,
Square dance snacks for everyone here.

Bridge
The caller shouted, "Grand right and left!"; they turned around the spread,
They circled every chair and came back to the bread.
The snack table was not in the square, it stood beside the band,
So they moved it to the center, careful, hand by hand.

Verse 3
The dancers made a smaller square and left a walkway wide,
The fiddle slowed the tune so trays could safely slide.
They shared the last two crackers, then danced the closing round,
The caller called "home place!" and every cup sat down.

Chorus
Circle left, then circle right,
Pass the crackers, share a bite.
Dosado, the cups are near,
Square dance snacks for everyone here.

Ending
The band packed up its fiddle, the table stayed in place,
The dancers left in pairs with crumbs upon their lace.
