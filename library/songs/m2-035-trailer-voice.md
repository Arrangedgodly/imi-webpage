# Coming This Fall, the Laundromat

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: movie trailer voice tropes and cinematic sound design*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: A gravelly trailer narrator battles the washing machine's spin cycle for dramatic space.*

## Verse 1
In a town where every sock goes missing, one monkey will search.  
The trailer voice rolled deep above the laundromat perch.  
A washer spun like thunder; the dryers glowed like dawn.  
The hero wore a bathrobe and one slipper on.  
He found a red thread caught beneath the lint.  
The narrator warned, "This clue will change the spin."  
A close-up showed his face reflected in the glass.  
The ticket clerk was sweeping when he asked to pass.  

## Chorus
Coming this fall, the load of a lifetime.  
One lost sock, one search, one washing-line climb.  
Coming this fall, prepare for the rinse.  
The spin cycle hits with a terrible sense.  

## Verse 2
His sister held the basket like a shield.  
The villain was a dryer with a broken seal.  
It tossed each pair in circles through the night.  
The hero found the vent and made it right.  
The machine released a mountain of unmatched.  
The narrator paused, then said, "The case is hatched."  
A sock slid down and landed on his head.  
The trailer cut to silence, then the title card went red.  

## Bridge
The film was rated G for gentle tumble scenes.  
The critic called the plot a little too detergent-clean.  
The hero gave his sister back her pair.  
The narrator saved his deepest voice for folding there.  

## Chorus
Coming this fall, the load of a lifetime.  
One lost sock, one search, one washing-line climb.  
Coming this fall, prepare for the rinse.  
The spin cycle hits with a terrible sense.  

## Ending
It premiered on a Tuesday, tickets half-price.  
The audience brought their laundry, which was nice.
