# The Counting Choir Gets a Goose

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cheerful children's counting songs and presenters who cannot keep the lesson steady while a goose supplies extra answers*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Simple original melody, bright piano and clear group vocals; the goose honks once between every two sung numbers.*

[Verse 1]
We gathered by the pond to sing a counting song at nine,  
The teacher brought ten wooden stars and put them in a line.  
We sang the first two numbers with a smile upon each face,  
A goose came from the reeds and made a honk inside the space.  
The youngest singer called it three and pointed to the bird,  
The teacher said to count the stars, not every sound we heard.  
The goose came closer to the group and honked beside the board,  
The choir sang four together; it supplied a fifth chord.  

[Chorus]
One little star, two little stars,  
The goose adds one honk where the next beat starts.  
Three little stars, four in a row,  
We count the wooden stars; the goose keeps time below.  
Five little stars, six little lights,  
The bird supplies the chorus and the choir sings it twice.  

[Verse 2]
The teacher moved the counting board onto a garden chair,  
The goose remained below it with a very noisy stare.  
We sang the numbers slower as she pointed one by one,  
The bird kept honking proudly in the quiet gaps we'd sung.  
The youngest singer asked if we could give the goose a verse,  
The teacher said to count the honks but do the stars first.  
We reached the ten stars clearly and we sang their numbers through,  
Then waited for the goose to honk; it only offered two.  

[Chorus]
One little star, two little stars,  
The goose adds one honk where the next beat starts.  
Three little stars, four in a row,  
We count the wooden stars; the goose keeps time below.  
Five little stars, six little lights,  
The bird supplies the chorus and the choir sings it twice.  
[Bridge]
We counted all ten stars and clapped the final time,  
The goose gave one soft honk and settled by the sign.  
The teacher packed the wooden stars; the board was going home,  
The goose honked once behind her, then paddled off alone.  

[Final Chorus]
One little star, two little stars,  
The goose adds one honk where the next beat starts.  
Three little stars, four in a row,  
We count the wooden stars; the goose keeps time below.  
Five little stars, six little lights,  
Tomorrow we count the bird if it returns in sight.
