# Boogie-Woogie at the Bell Shop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: boogie-woogie piano, walking bass, and a bell repair shop where every repaired chime changes the dancers' feet*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Driving left-hand piano bass and bright right-hand runs; each fixed bell adds a rhythmic answer.*

Verse 1
The bell shop opened early; its door gave one dull clang,
A macaque rolled the piano in and tuned the boogie gang.
The repairer tapped a handbell; it answered with a squeak,
The pianist played a rolling bass and made the brass sound speak.

Chorus
Boogie by the bell, let the low notes roll,
Shake your feet and wake the sleepy toll.
Fix one chime and add it to the band,
Every little bell can lend a hand.

Verse 2
A gibbon brought a clock bell with a spring that would not ring,
The repairer turned the screw; the piano made it sing.
A capuchin began to dance, but stepped into a tray,
The tray rang like a cymbal and joined the boogie play.

Chorus
Boogie by the bell, let the low notes roll,
Shake your feet and wake the sleepy toll.
Fix one chime and add it to the band,
Every little bell can lend a hand.

Bridge
They fixed the shop's old doorbell; it chimed in double time,
The walking bass kept stepping while the bells supplied the rhyme.
The clock bell struck the hour, the handbell answered twice,
The tray rang once by accident and sounded rather nice.

Verse 3
The customers reclaimed their clocks, the repairer swept the floor,
The band played one more chorus by the bell shop's open door.
They left the tray in silence, the bells returned to shelves,
The pianist played the bass line all the way back home himself.

Chorus
Boogie by the bell, let the low notes roll,
Shake your feet and wake the sleepy toll.
Fix one chime and add it to the band,
Every little bell can lend a hand.

Ending
The doorbell rang a final time, the piano answered low,
The bell shop closed at five o'clock, but kept a boogie glow.
