# The Moon Base Has a Trailer Theme

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: blockbuster science-fiction trailer music with booming slogans and escalating countdowns*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Begin with low cinematic drums; let the narrator oversell mundane lunar maintenance before a sharp brass sting.*

[Verse 1]
The moon base had a leaky seal on hatch number nine,  
A gibbon wore a silver suit and checked the warning sign.  
The trailer voice declared, "One crew, one moon, one wrench!"  
The crew looked at the toolbox and began to make a bench.  
A capuchin said, "It is a gasket, not a war,"  
The music dropped a thunder hit and shook the galley door.  
The camera made a slow push toward the rubber ring,  
The narrator called it "the last thing keeping out the spring."  

[Chorus]
This season, on a moon near you,  
One brave crew must tighten screw two.  
When the pressure falls, the lights go red,  
They'll save the base before they make the bed.  
The seal is small, the stakes are tall,  
The wrench will answer destiny's call.  

[Verse 2]
The lead monkey checked the manual; it showed the proper size,  
The trumpet played a warning as the wrench began to rise.  
The mechanic turned it gently; the seal sat in its groove,  
The orchestra kept building, waiting for a bigger move.  
The crew secured the hatch and ran the pressure check,  
The number held, the warning light went off above the deck.  
The trailer voice proclaimed, "The universe is safe!"  
The crew went back to dinner and split a packet cake.  

[Chorus]
This season, on a moon near you,  
One brave crew must tighten screw two.  
When the pressure falls, the lights go red,  
They'll save the base before they make the bed.  
The seal is small, the stakes are tall,  
The wrench will answer destiny's call.  

[Bridge]
A second alarm began to beep beside the galley sink,  
The crew all turned in slow motion while the drums went "thunk."  
It was the tea machine demanding that its tray be clean,  
The narrator said, "Coming soon: the sequel to the scene."  

[Final Chorus]
This season, on a moon near you,  
One brave crew will rinse cup two.  
When the tea drips down, the lights turn green,  
The smallest cleanup makes the biggest screen.  
The seal is tight, the crew can dine,  
The trailer has been longer than the repair time.  

[Ending]
The title card appeared; it read "Based on a Very Small Fix."
