# Bayou Picnic Shuffle

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: zydeco accordion drive, rubboard percussion, and a picnic dance that turns a runaway pie into the bandleader*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Push the accordion forward; use the rubboard to imitate pie tins sliding across a table.*

[Verse 1]
Down by the bayou, the picnic spread was wide,
A lemur set the pies beside the waterside.
The accordion started with a bounce in its bellows,
And the dancers shook their shoulders with the rhythm in their elbows.

[Chorus]
Slide, pie, slide, let the rubboard chatter,
Turn, pie, turn, make the tin pans clatter.
Zydeco on the bank, let the whole crowd sway,
The pie is leading now; give the band some space.

[Verse 2]
A gust lifted one pie and it slid off the tray,
It spun around the table and it rolled toward the bay.
The fiddler chased behind it; the drummer tapped the tin,
The pie made one full circle and returned with a grin.

[Chorus]
Slide, pie, slide, let the rubboard chatter,
Turn, pie, turn, make the tin pans clatter.
Zydeco on the bank, let the whole crowd sway,
The pie is leading now; give the band some space.

[Bridge]
A capuchin stepped forward, tried to catch it by the crust,
The pie slipped through his fingers and rolled up in a gust.
The dancers made a circle; they kept the path clear,
The pie stopped at the bandstand and bowed to the rear.

[Final Chorus]
Slide, pie, slide, let the rubboard chatter,
Turn, pie, turn, make the tin pans clatter.
Zydeco on the bank, let the whole crowd sway,
The pie is leading now; give the band some space.

[Ending]
They cut the pie in slices; the accordion took the plate.
