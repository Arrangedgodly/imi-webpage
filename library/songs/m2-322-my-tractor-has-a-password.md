# My Tractor Has a Password

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country heartbreak songs redirected into a farm equipment software lockout*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Steel guitar and a steady country shuffle; the backing singers pronounce each password symbol.*

[Verse 1]
You left me in September with the keys beside the door,  
I thought I'd drive the tractor like a hundred times before.  
I climbed up in the leather seat and gave the key a turn,  
The screen requested characters I never thought to learn.  
It wanted one big letter and a number after that,  
I tried our anniversary; it drew a little cat.  
That cat was there to tell me I had failed another test,  
While rain approached the barley growing taller than my vest.  

[Chorus]
My tractor has a password, my heart has a hole,  
The wheels are full of diesel but the screen won't let them roll.  
An underscore between us, an asterisk above,  
I cannot plow a single row without resetting love.  
Come home with your recovery code, or send it in the post,  
The tractor knows your mother's name, the thing I miss the most.  

[Verse 2]
I called the dealer's hotline with my muddy mobile phone,  
A cheerful recorded banjo said I wasn't all alone.  
It asked me where I'd first been kissed; I answered by the barn,  
The screen rejected countryside and wouldn't hear my yarn.  
My neighbor brought a shovel and a flask of bitter tea,  
He said his horse had never asked a question quite like these.  
I said my horse had eaten all the paperwork last June,  
He looked up at the thunderheads and offered me a spoon.  

[Chorus]
My tractor has a password, my heart has a hole,  
The wheels are full of diesel but the screen won't let them roll.  
An underscore between us, an asterisk above,  
I cannot plow a single row without resetting love.  
Come home with your recovery code, or send it in the post,  
The tractor knows your mother's name, the thing I miss the most.  

[Bridge]
Your letter came at sundown: "Try the name behind the seat."  
I found a tiny sticker underneath the faded pleat.  
The password was the tractor's name, the one we'd called it then,  
I typed in LITTLE DAISY, and it asked me to change again.  
I added seven asterisks and one enormous B,  
The engine finally coughed awake and did a dance for me.  

[Final Chorus]
My tractor has a password, my heart has a hole,  
The wheels are full of diesel and at last they're free to roll.  
An underscore between us, an asterisk above,  
I plowed one muddy furrow with a newly coded love.  
I wrote it on the steering wheel, the rain washed off the note,  
Now Daisy wants the maiden name of my neighbor's goat.
