# The Punk Band Reads the Minutes

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: anti-establishment punk songs trapped in the band's own committee procedure*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Fast distorted guitars; stop abruptly whenever the secretary demands a recorded vote.*

[Verse 1]
We met behind the laundromat to overthrow the rules,  
Four monkeys dressed in safety pins and borrowed welding tools.  
Our singer kicked the folding chair, declared the system dead,  
The bassist asked to put that in the minutes before we shred.  
The drummer had a motion for a louder second verse,  
The secretary struck it out for language getting worse.  
We voted on the spelling of the slogan on our van,  
Then formed a working party for a truly rebel plan.  

[Chorus]
Smash the state when the minutes are approved,  
Kick the door when the motion has been moved.  
Rip it up, keep a copy for the file,  
Rage against the system in a recognized style.  
No more rulers, no more chains,  
Except the chair who calls our names.  
One, two, three, four, wait for the floor,  
The treasurer has something to report before the roar.  

[Verse 2]
We booked a basement concert for a riotous revolt,  
The bass amp ate the kettle lead and blew a little bolt.  
The singer yelled "Anarchy!" and climbed upon a crate,  
The drummer checked the bylaws and declared the meeting late.  
A fan proposed a circle pit, the chair requested space,  
We drew a diagram and gave each dancer numbered place.  
The secretary wanted all objections in advance,  
We passed a resolution to permit spontaneous dance.  

[Chorus]
Smash the state when the minutes are approved,  
Kick the door when the motion has been moved.  
Rip it up, keep a copy for the file,  
Rage against the system in a recognized style.  
No more rulers, no more chains,  
Except the chair who calls our names.  
One, two, three, four, wait for the floor,  
The treasurer has something to report before the roar.  

[Bridge]
The youngest fan unplugged our mic and shouted through a cone,  
"I came here for a concert, not a meeting of your own!"  
We passed the cone among us and the basement shook at last,  
The secretary took the notes so nobody talked too fast.  

[Final Chorus]
Smash the state when the minutes are approved,  
Kick the door when the motion has been moved.  
Rip it up, keep a copy for the file,  
Rage against the system in a recognized style.  
No more rulers, no more chains,  
Except the chair who calls our names.  
One, two, three, four, we tore up the score,  
Then voted that we'd tape it back together for the tour.
