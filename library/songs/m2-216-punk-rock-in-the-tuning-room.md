# Tuning Is a Revolution

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: hardcore punk urgency, shouted group hooks, and a band that starts the rebellion before its instruments are tuned*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the guitars raw and fast; stop abruptly when the drummer calls for tuning.*

[Verse 1]
The capuchin band broke into the basement at eight,
The guitarist tuned a string while the crowd filled the gate.
The drummer shouted "Ready!" with his sticks in the air,
The bassist shouted "Not yet!" and pointed at a chair.

[Chorus]
Tune it up, tune it loud, tune it till it's right,
Raise the amp, count to four, start the show tonight.
Tune it up, tune it loud, don't rush the refrain,
The revolution can wait for the string to stay in tune again.

[Verse 2]
The crowd began to pogo while the guitarist held one note,
The keyboard lost its cable and the singer lost his coat.
The stagehand found the cable in a pile of tangled wire;
The singer took the coat back and the crowd began to tire.

[Chorus]
Tune it up, tune it loud, tune it till it's right,
Raise the amp, count to four, start the show tonight.
Tune it up, tune it loud, don't rush the refrain,
The revolution can wait for the string to stay in tune again.

[Bridge]
The drummer struck a tuning fork; it rang above the din,
The band all stopped and listened as the crowd came filing in.
They tuned each string in silence, then they played the opening chord,
The crowd had gone for water, but the stage crew asked for more.

[Final Chorus]
Tune it up, tune it loud, tune it till it's right,
Raise the amp, count to four, start the show tonight.
Tune it up, tune it loud, the strings have found their place,
The crowd came back from water and began a slower pace.

[Ending]
The singer yelled "Encore!" The guitarist tuned again.
