# Please Do Not Tap the Case

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: museum audio-guide narration turned into musical docent instructions*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: A measured docent sings into a handheld guide; visitors answer in whispers.*

## Verse 1
Welcome to the hall of ordinary tools.  
Please keep your hands away from objects used by mules.  
This brass spoon was carried by a traveling cook.  
Its owner left no name, just one recipe book.  
The audio guide clicks, then speaks in gentle rhyme.  
A squirrel monkey docent keeps a measured time.  
She points toward the label, where the dates are plain.  
A child leans too near; she asks him back again.  

## Chorus
Listen close, walk slow, let the glass stay clear.  
The past can tell a tale when we give it room to appear.  
Listen close, step back, don't tap the display.  
The objects have been waiting here a very long day.  

## Verse 2
The visitors pass maps and a cracked blue cup.  
The guide explains how floods once filled the lower floor up.  
A school group hums along with every cue.  
One kid asks what people used before the glue.  
The docent says they mended things with thread and pine.  
A curator adds, "And patience, most the time."  
The group writes questions on a paper card.  
The audio track ends, but leaving isn't hard.  

## Bridge
A museum is a room where questions learn to stay.  
The docent lowers her voice and lets the silence play.  
The child waves goodbye to one old wooden shoe.  
The shoe can't wave back, though it seems to want to.  

## Chorus
Listen close, walk slow, let the glass stay clear.  
The past can tell a tale when we give it room to appear.  
Listen close, step back, don't tap the display.  
The objects have been waiting here a very long day.  

## Ending
The school bus pulled away at half past two.  
The spoon stayed still, with more to tell the next group through.
