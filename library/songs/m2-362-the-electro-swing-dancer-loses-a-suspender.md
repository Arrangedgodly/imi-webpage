# The Electro-Swing Dancer Loses a Suspender

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: retro dance revivals whose immaculate fashion cannot survive the modern bass drop*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Sampled brass over a heavy electronic beat; one elastic snap replaces the snare.*

[Verse 1]
I wore my finest striped suit to the club behind the tram,  
My shoes were mirror polished and my hat announced the man.  
The brass recalled a golden age, the bass disturbed the floor,  
I did a little Charleston till my knees required more.  
I kicked across the backbeat and I raised a hand in style,  
My right suspender snapped and hit a speaker down the aisle.  

[Chorus]
Swing it old and drop it new,  
Let the brass remember while the bass remembers you.  
Kick your heels and hold your hat,  
Keep one hand on everything that's coming unattached.  
Step in time, land in grace,  
Something from the golden age is bouncing off the bass.  

[Verse 2]
I tried to dance one-handed while I held my waistband tight,  
The left suspender kept me looking half of what was right.  
The DJ chopped a trumpet phrase and doubled up the beat,  
I hopped beside a velvet chair to reorganize my feet.  
The bouncer offered string to make a temporary belt,  
I tied it with a flourish and returned where music dwelt.  

[Chorus]
Swing it old and drop it new,  
Let the brass remember while the bass remembers you.  
Kick your heels and hold your hat,  
Keep one hand on everything that's coming unattached.  
Step in time, land in grace,  
Something from the golden age is bouncing off the bass.  

[Bridge]
The elastic on the speaker kept vibrating with the track,  
The DJ sampled every snap and played the rhythm back.  
The crowd performed my lopsided step and cheered my little bow,  
My trousers stayed exactly where the string allowed them now.  

[Final Chorus]
Swing it old and drop it new,  
Let the brass remember while the bass remembers you.  
Kick your heels and hold your hat,  
The crowd demands the sample of the strap that went unattached.  
Step in time, land in grace,  
The DJ pays my tailor for the rights to that embrace.
