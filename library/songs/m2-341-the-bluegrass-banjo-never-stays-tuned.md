# The Bluegrass Banjo Never Stays Tuned

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: breakneck bluegrass instrumental breaks delayed by endlessly circulating tuning requests*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Bright mandolin and banjo rolls; every instrumental break begins with the wrong tuning note.*

[Verse 1]
We stood upon a wagon at the county harvest fair,  
The fiddler took a tuning note and tossed it in the air.  
The banjo player caught it, turned a peg and struck a string,  
The mandolinist heard it and adjusted everything.  
The bassist matched the mandolin and plucked a solemn low,  
The fiddler heard the bassist and forgot what he should know.  

[Chorus]
Pick it fast and pick it clean,  
Find the note that's hiding in between.  
Pass it round and pass it through,  
Tune to me and I'll tune back to you.  
We can race a thunderstorm and beat it to the noon,  
As soon as all five strings agree to start the tune.  

[Verse 2]
The banjo found a bright new pitch, the fiddler found a third,  
The mandolinist followed till the bassist barely heard.  
The crowd began to clap a beat, the drummer joined the fun,  
We hadn't got a drummer, but the baker brought a drum.  
He beat a flour bucket and the wagon boards began,  
The banjo's loosened peg slipped while the fiddler played a span.  

[Chorus]
Pick it fast and pick it clean,  
Find the note that's hiding in between.  
Pass it round and pass it through,  
Tune to me and I'll tune back to you.  
We can race a thunderstorm and beat it to the noon,  
As soon as all five strings agree to start the tune.  

[Bridge]
The baker held a tuning fork against his wooden tray,  
We matched it once together and the band began to play.  
The banjo flew like sparrows and the fiddler raced the sun,  
The baker sold his flour cakes before the break was done.  

[Final Chorus]
Pick it fast and pick it clean,  
Find the note that's hiding in between.  
Pass it round and pass it through,  
Tune to me and I'll tune back to you.  
We can race a thunderstorm and beat it to the noon,  
The banjo buys a cake and uses its fork to tune.
