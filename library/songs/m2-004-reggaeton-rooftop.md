# Rooftop Signal

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: reggaeton dembow production and boastful dance commands*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Keep the bass clipped and dry, then let the roof crew answer each cue.*

## Verse 1
The rooftop water tank wore a silver crown.  
Our young macaque DJ plugged the speakers down.  
No velvet rope, just laundry on a line.  
Auntie brought a folding chair and claimed the prime.  
The beat went boom, then ticked along the tar.  
The neighbor's parrot shouted, "That is much too far!"  
The DJ lowered bass, adjusted every cone.  
The parrot tried the rhythm from its weather vane throne.  

## Chorus
Move with the roof, let the warm night roll.  
Hands to the sky, but keep one near the pole.  
Move with the roof, let the water tank glow.  
The block knows the beat, so the block says go.  

## Verse 2
A lemur in a helmet brought a bucket drum.  
Two capuchins arrived with snacks for everyone.  
The landlord climbed the stairs to check the noise.  
He found his nephew dancing with the plumber's boys.  
A clothesline snapped, one sock sailed through the air.  
The whole crowd ducked, then cheered the sock's career.  
The DJ mixed its landing with a clean four count.  
The parrot gave a whistle from the gutter's spout.  

## Bridge
The lights across the avenue blinked one by one.  
The street below had finished; our set had just begun.  
No guest list, no camera, no promoter's deal.  
Just neighbors taking turns to choose the next cut's feel.  

## Chorus
Move with the roof, let the warm night roll.  
Hands to the sky, but keep one near the pole.  
Move with the roof, let the water tank glow.  
The block knows the beat, so the block says go.  

## Ending
At sunrise every speaker rested by the stair.  
The parrot kept the beat while dawn dried up the air.
