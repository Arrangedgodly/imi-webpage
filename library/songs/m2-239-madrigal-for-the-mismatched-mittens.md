# Madrigal for the Mismatched Mittens

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Renaissance madrigal counterpoint, ornate word painting, and a laundry line whose mittens keep arriving in the wrong pair*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Sing the parts in interwoven counterpoint; let each section name a different mitten color.*

[Verse 1]
The lemur choir assembled by the laundry line at dawn,
The alto found a mitten with its partner mitten gone.
The sopranos sang of winter; the tenors sang of snow,
The basses traced the clothesline where the mismatched colors flow.

[Chorus]
One red hand, one blue hand, spinning in the breeze,
Find the other mitten among the frozen trees.
One red hand, one blue hand, pass the tune along,
A pair may be a duet, but a mismatch sings a song.

[Verse 2]
A macaque held the clothespins and arranged them in a ring,
The counterpoint grew tangled as the sleeves began to swing.
The alto found a yellow one; the bass found one of green,
They sang each mitten's history as the clothesline turned between.

[Chorus]
One red hand, one blue hand, spinning in the breeze,
Find the other mitten among the frozen trees.
One red hand, one blue hand, pass the tune along,
A pair may be a duet, but a mismatch sings a song.

[Bridge]
The wind removed one mitten and sent it down the street,
The choir followed singing in a perfectly measured beat.
It landed on the wash basket, where the missing half was found,
The two mittens met at last and pulled the clothesline round.

[Final Chorus]
One red hand, one blue hand, resting by the door,
They do not match in color, but they match in warmth and more.
One red hand, one blue hand, sing the final song,
A pair may be a duet, and a mismatch can belong.

[Ending]
The choir bowed in four parts; the mittens waved as one.
