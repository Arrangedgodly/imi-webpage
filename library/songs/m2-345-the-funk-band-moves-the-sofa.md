# The Funk Band Moves the Sofa

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: funk songs promising effortless groove while the band undertakes awkward manual work*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Slap bass, clipped guitar and brass stabs; every furniture collision lands on the backbeat.*

[Verse 1]
Our bassist bought a purple sofa wider than his room,  
He called us up to bring it home and promised us a groove.  
We met him on the pavement with our jackets pressed and bright,  
The sofa blocked the doorway and the doorway held it tight.  
The trumpeter said lift it; the drummer said go low,  
Our singer found a rhythm but no place for it to go.  

[Chorus]
Get down, get up, get round that bend,  
Move a little closer to the heavy end.  
Slide that leg, mind that wall,  
Put a little groove beneath the backbeat of the hall.  
Get down, get up, feel that strain,  
We've found the pocket but we're wedged in it again.  

[Verse 2]
We took the little feet off and we turned it on its side,  
The bassist kept on counting while the trumpeter replied.  
The drummer dropped his corner for a sudden little fill,  
The sofa hit the skirting with a syncopated thrill.  
The singer wanted everybody moving as a team,  
He stretched a mighty note while the upholstery caught a seam.  

[Chorus]
Get down, get up, get round that bend,  
Move a little closer to the heavy end.  
Slide that leg, mind that wall,  
Put a little groove beneath the backbeat of the hall.  
Get down, get up, feel that strain,  
We've found the pocket but we're wedged in it again.  

[Bridge]
The neighbor opened up her door and offered us a hand,  
She turned the sofa gently where we'd thought it couldn't stand.  
We slid it through the doorway to the beat of four relieved,  
Then sat upon the cushions and admired what we'd achieved.  

[Final Chorus]
Get down, get up, get round that bend,  
Move a little closer to the heavy end.  
Slide that leg, mind that wall,  
Put a little groove beneath the backbeat of the hall.  
Get down, get up, the bassist makes a plea,  
The sofa's in the room, but his bass is underneath.
