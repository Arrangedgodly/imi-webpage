# The Protest Song Is on the Sponsor Stage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: commercially sponsored protest anthems with advertising woven into their revolutionary chorus*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Strummed folk-rock guitar and a crowd chorus; the stage announcer inserts an invented lemonade brand.*

[Verse 1]
We climbed the stage with banners and a song to change the town,  
The sponsor hung a bigger banner, nearly pinned ours down.  
I sang about the price of bread and all the rents we'd pay,  
The announcer offered lemonade at half price for the day.  
Our drummer painted FREEDOM on the skin across his drum,  
They added FIZZCO just below before the crowds could come.  

[Chorus]
Raise your voice and raise your hand,  
We have come to take a stand.  
Every neighbor gets a say,  
Try the citrus special on the way.  
Let the streets resound with song,  
Fizzco asks you kindly not to sing for very long.  

[Verse 2]
I asked the crowd to join me when the chorus reached its height,  
The giant screen displayed a bottle glowing in the light.  
The audience sang justice as the bubbles drifted by,  
The announcer called the moment an improvement in supply.  
Our bassist tried to hide the logo with his little hat,  
The camera swung around him and enlarged it where he sat.  

[Chorus]
Raise your voice and raise your hand,  
We have come to take a stand.  
Every neighbor gets a say,  
Try the citrus special on the way.  
Let the streets resound with song,  
Fizzco asks you kindly not to sing for very long.  

[Bridge]
We unplugged the amplifiers and we walked into the square,  
The crowd took up the melody and sang it in the air.  
The sponsor sent a trolley out to sell its bottled cheer,  
The wheels squeaked in the rhythm and the drummer drew it near.  

[Final Chorus]
Raise your voice and raise your hand,  
We have come to take a stand.  
Every neighbor gets a say,  
The trolley is our drum for the remainder of the day.  
Let the streets resound with song,  
The announcer begs us for his wheels and has to sing along.
