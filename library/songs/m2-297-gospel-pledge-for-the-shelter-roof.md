# Gospel Pledge for the Shelter Roof

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel-style fundraising telethons that turn every pledge into a bigger pledge before the roof is repaired*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Full choir call-and-response, warm organ and handclaps; aim the joke at escalating pledge pitches, not at the musical tradition.*

**Verse 1**
The shelter's roof has one small leak above the coat-room door.  
The choir sets up a pledge-night stand beside the wooden floor.  
A capuchin host says, "Every coin can mend a tile."  
The first guest gives a button; the choir sings for a while.  
The roof fund jar is empty; the rain begins to tap.  
The pianist plays a rolling chord; the bucket catches drops.  

**Chorus**
Give what you can, let the bucket ring;  
One little pledge can do a thing.  
Hands together, voices rise, the choir is strong and clear;  
The roof needs one good patch before the storm gets here.  

**Verse 2**
The host asks for a hundred; the choir sings, "Amen."  
A donor offers fifty, then the host asks once again.  
The choir builds a mighty swell; the pledge board fills with zeros.  
A drummer adds a thunder roll; the guests all eye the ceiling.  
A roofer steps inside and asks to see the leak.  
The host says, "After one more song," the roofer says, "This week."  

**Chorus**
Give what you can, let the bucket ring;  
One little pledge can do a thing.  
Hands together, voices rise, the choir is strong and clear;  
The roof needs one good patch before the storm gets here.  

**Bridge**
The choir lowers every note; the roofer climbs the ladder.  
He seals the seam above the door; the bucket stops its patter.  
The host asks for a larger gift; the guests all shake their heads.  
The pledge board holds its modest sum; the roof holds over beds.  

**Ending**
They sing a soft thanksgiving; the rain slides down the pane.  
The shelter stays dry through the night; no one asks again.  
The host puts up the pledge jar with a label, "Roof complete."  
The choir leaves by the back door and shares a quiet treat.
