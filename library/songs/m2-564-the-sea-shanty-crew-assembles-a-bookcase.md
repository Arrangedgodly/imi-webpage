# The Sea Shanty Crew Assembles a Bookcase

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: work shanties that assign maritime grandeur to the parts of flat-pack furniture*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Unaccompanied lead and crew response; hauling beats mark screws being turned, and the final chorus uses the finished shelf as a gentle drum.*

[Verse 1]
We gathered in the captain's room to build a little shelf,  
The captain said his charts could not arrange their books themselves.  
He spread the boards upon a rug and named them bow and stern,  
The mate unfolded diagrams and told us how to turn.  
Our strongest sailor hauled a plank above his shoulder high,  
The cook said, "Put it on the rug; the lamp is standing by."  
We laid the long sides flat again and checked the numbered holes,  
The mate assigned the screws their places, sparing ropes and poles.  

[Hauling Chorus]
Heave, my crew, but keep the cupboard still,  
The shelf has little wooden pegs and needs no sailor's skill.  
Heave, my crew, turn the screw around,  
We tighten with the little key before it leaves the ground.  
Port and starboard, page and spine,  
The captain wants his atlas on the shelf by suppertime.  

[Verse 2]
The backing went on squarely with its painted side inside,  
The captain asked where ballast goes to keep the shelves upright.  
The mate checked on the fitting pack and found a wall-fastening brace,  
We used the maker's drawing to identify its place.  
A carpenter from down the quay secured it to the wall,  
The cook held back the captain's charts until she'd checked them all.  
The captain put his atlas in and tried to name the shelf,  
The mate said, "Call it Bookcase; let it speak for its own self."  

[Hauling Chorus]
Heave, my crew, but keep the cupboard still,  
The shelf has little wooden pegs and needs no sailor's skill.  
Heave, my crew, turn the screw around,  
We tighten with the little key before it leaves the ground.  
Port and starboard, page and spine,  
The captain wants his atlas on the shelf by suppertime.  

[Bridge]
We sang the closing shanty with the bookcase standing sound,  
The captain put a brass ship bell upon the shelf we'd found.  
The cook laid out the supper and the carpenter sat down,  
We sang a shorter grace before the dishes made their round.  

[Final Hauling Chorus]
Heave, my crew, let the supper smell,  
The atlas has a berth beneath a very little bell.  
Heave, my crew, leave the screws alone,  
The bookcase doesn't need to sail; its home is quite its own.  
Port and starboard, page and spine,  
The captain files the building plan beneath the shipping line.
