# Salsa for the Soup Spoon

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: salsa orchestra arrangements and kitchen dance-floor flirtation*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Let the piano sprint, the horns interrupt, and the spoon player take the final bow.*

## Verse 1
At Café Cereza, lunch arrived in steaming bowls.  
A red-tailed monkey spun between the dinner rolls.  
She wore a linen jacket, sharp as folded fans.  
The waiter brought a spoon and lost all his plans.  
The conga player drummed upon an empty tin.  
The piano chased the bass around the room again.  
She dipped the spoon; the broth made one small wave.  
The whole band paused, as if the soup could save them.  

## Chorus
Turn, little spoon, let the cinnamon shine.  
Step through the steam on a quick three-two line.  
Turn, little spoon, keep the table in time.  
Tonight even soup has a place in the rhyme.  

## Verse 2
A capuchin in spectacles requested extra lime.  
He tapped his heel and tried to copy her time.  
His napkin fell, his chair began to slide.  
She caught him with a spin and set him by her side.  
The cook came through the doorway bearing one big pan.  
He had a wooden ladle and a seasoned plan.  
They traded steps beside the service window's glow.  
The salsa band turned kitchen traffic into show.  

## Bridge
No roses on the table, no candle in a glass.  
Just steam upon the window and a waiter counting cash.  
She asked him if he'd dance when closing time was near.  
He said, "I'll need a lesson," and she pulled him clear.  

## Chorus
Turn, little spoon, let the cinnamon shine.  
Step through the steam on a quick three-two line.  
Turn, little spoon, keep the table in time.  
Tonight even soup has a place in the rhyme.  

## Ending
The bowls were washed, the chairs were stacked away.  
The spoon still spun inside her pocket on the way.
