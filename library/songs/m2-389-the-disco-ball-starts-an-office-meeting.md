# The Disco Ball Starts an Office Meeting

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: disco anthems promising release from daily life, captured by an office manager's agenda*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor bass, bright strings and handclaps; the manager reads meeting items in perfectly rhythmic spoken breaks.*

[Verse 1]
We hung a mirrored disco ball above the break-room floor,  
The working week was finished; we had locked the office door.  
Our accountant brought the speakers and a pocket full of lights,  
The interns pushed the tables back and stretched into the night.  
The manager came dancing with a clipboard on her hip,  
She said she'd only join us for a single little sip.  
Then pointed to the glitter on the ceiling overhead,  
"While everyone's together, let's discuss the quarter ahead."  

[Chorus]
Spin, spin, glitter on the wall,  
Every little mirror has a meeting to recall.  
Move, move, loosen up your shoes,  
The manager has scheduled time for departmental news.  
Shine, shine, let the music play,  
We are dancing through the minutes of the closing working day.  
Spin, spin, never miss a beat,  
Please approve the motion while you're moving both your feet.  

[Verse 2]
The drummer on the record laid a steady rolling kick,  
She raised a staffing question and our answers came quite quick.  
The bassist slid an octave as she asked who'd take the notes,  
The intern did a hand wave; she recorded it as votes.  
Our accountant tried a hustle to escape behind the bar,  
She handed him a budget and he didn't get too far.  
She cleared a space for questions with a finger in the air,  
The chorus came back louder; we pretended not to hear.  

[Chorus]
Spin, spin, glitter on the wall,  
Every little mirror has a meeting to recall.  
Move, move, loosen up your shoes,  
The manager has scheduled time for departmental news.  
Shine, shine, let the music play,  
We are dancing through the minutes of the closing working day.  
Spin, spin, never miss a beat,  
Please approve the motion while you're moving both your feet.  

[Bridge]
The intern took the clipboard and declared the meeting closed,  
Our accountant changed the record to a song the manager knows.  
She left the agenda by the cups and claimed the center square,  
The intern filed the minutes: "Everybody shook their hair."  

[Final Chorus]
Spin, spin, glitter on the wall,  
The manager has found a groove beyond the quarterly call.  
Move, move, loosen up your shoes,  
Her dancing got unanimous approval from the crew.  
Shine, shine, let the music play,  
We have adjourned the meeting till the start of Monday's day.  
Spin, spin, never miss a beat,  
She puts "team-building disco" in next week's expense sheet.
