# Beatbox for the Folding Chairs

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: solo beatboxing performances that layer kick, snare, and bass sounds into a one-person loop*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Build a live vocal loop one sound at a time; let the chair-folding rhythm overwhelm the beatboxer's carefully layered pattern.*

[Verse 1]
The hall was closing after dance class on a Tuesday night,  
A capuchin began to beatbox underneath the light.  
He made a kick drum with his chest, a snare with cheek and tongue,  
The empty room returned the sounds and made the echo young.  
The caretaker rolled in chairs and set them in a stack,  
Each folding frame made clack-clack-clack and answered from the back.  
The beatboxer laid a bass line, smooth and low and deep,  
The chairs replied with metal clicks that scattered every beat.  

[Chorus]
Bum, tss, ka, make the rhythm grow,  
One mouth lays a beat in a row.  
Clack, fold, stack, the chairs reply,  
The room keeps time as the echoes fly.  
Bum, tss, ka, build it again,  
The chair beat enters at the end.  

[Verse 2]
He looped his first three sounds and added one more layer,  
The caretaker folded faster, filling every chair.  
The bass went "brr," the snare went "tck," the hinges answered "clack,"  
The loops began to tangle up and all came tumbling back.  
A gibbon moved a chair away; the rhythm lost its frame,  
The beatboxer tried to fill the gap and called it part of the game.  
The caretaker set the last chair down with careful grace,  
The room held one final echo in an empty space.  

[Chorus]
Bum, tss, ka, make the rhythm grow,  
One mouth lays a beat in a row.  
Clack, fold, stack, the chairs reply,  
The room keeps time as the echoes fly.  
Bum, tss, ka, build it again,  
The chair beat enters at the end.  

[Bridge]
The singer asked the caretaker to try the beat once more,  
They folded chairs together by the storeroom door.  
The mouth made kick and snare; the chair made clack and click,  
The final loop was perfect till the last chair shut too quick.  

[Final Chorus]
Bum, tss, ka, the hall is clear,  
The folding beat has disappeared.  
Clack, fold, stack, then wheel away,  
The sound can rest until another day.  
Bum, tss, ka, the echo ends,  
The beatboxer and chair are friends.  

[Ending]
The final sound was one chair clack, in perfect time with "goodnight."
