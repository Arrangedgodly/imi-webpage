# The Hair-Metal Star Books a Tiny Stage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: hair-metal arena swagger, huge stage gestures and performers who carry stadium choreography into a small café*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Big guitar chords, high vocals and enormous backing harmonies; every planned arena gesture is reduced to fit the café.*

[Verse 1]
The booking said a hundred seats; I pictured flashing steel,  
A runway through the audience and smoke around my heels.  
The venue was a café with a stage beside the till,  
My platform boots stood on it; there was little room left still.  
I asked about the runway and the owner pointed down,  
The route went past the toilets and the counter serving buns.  
My guitarist put his speaker underneath a spare tea tray,  
The drummer brought one little snare; the kit would have to wait.  

[Chorus]
Raise your hands, let me hear,  
The nearest audience can touch the silver fringe beside my ear.  
Big hair, bright light, shake the whole wide room,  
The smoke machine is outside so the coffee keeps its bloom.  
Raise your hands, make it roar,  
My stadium has a hundred seats and one small café door.  

[Verse 2]
I tried my opening leap and almost brushed the ceiling shade,  
The guitarist held a chair in case my landing went astray.  
I changed the leap to one small step and sang the opening note,  
The owner set a glass of water near my shining coat.  
The audience came closer as the drummer brushed his snare,  
The guitarist made the softer chords still glitter through the air.  
I sang a huge high chorus with no runway to explore,  
The coffee drinkers cheered me and requested three songs more.  

[Chorus]
Raise your hands, let me hear,  
The nearest audience can touch the silver fringe beside my ear.  
Big hair, bright light, shake the whole wide room,  
The smoke machine is outside so the coffee keeps its bloom.  
Raise your hands, make it roar,  
My stadium has a hundred seats and one small café door.  

[Bridge]
The owner asked us back again with half the gear we brought,  
I kept my enormous hairstyle but I shortened every walk.  
My guitarist named the tour The Very Intimate Storm,  
The drummer put his kit on sale and kept the little snare warm.  

[Final Chorus]
Raise your hands, let me hear,  
The café has my photo and a booking twice a year.  
Big hair, bright light, shake the whole wide room,  
The smoke machine earns nothing; it is waiting in my room.  
Raise your hands, make it roar,  
The encore has a pastry break before I give them more.
