# The Pelican's Poker Face

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: smoky lounge torch songs and the mysterious nightclub singer persona*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Slow lounge piano with brushed snare; the singer's deadpan delivery should crack only when the beak gets stuck.*

Verse 1
The room is dim, the lamps wear shades, the band has learned to sigh,  
A pelican in velvet steps beneath a painted sky.  
He sings of secrets kept in silk, of whispers after two,  
Then catches on the microphone the name tag from his shoe.  
The piano holds a minor chord, the drummer holds his breath,  
The singer's beak stays level as he chews the word "regret."  

Chorus
I keep my cards beneath my wing,  
I never show the hand I bring.  
The world may guess, the lamps may glow,  
I only tell what fish should know.  
No one can read this velvet face,  
No one can find my secret place.  
I keep my cards beneath my wing,  
And lose them when I start to sing.  

Verse 2
A mouse at table seven asks if sorrow has a taste,  
The pelican leans close to him and says, "A little paste."  
He meant to say "a little bass," the band was playing low,  
The mouse writes down the menu and requests it to-go.  
The singer croons a smoky note, a feather drifts astray,  
It lands inside the tip jar where the coins begin to sway.  

Bridge
He tells the room his heart's locked up behind a silver door,  
The bartender says, "That's the freezer; don't lean there anymore."  
The pianist changes key to help the story find its place,  
A fish escapes the garnish tray and kisses his black lace.  

Chorus
I keep my cards beneath my wing,  
I never show the hand I bring.  
The world may guess, the lamps may glow,  
I only tell what fish should know.  
No one can read this velvet face,  
No one can find my secret place.  
I keep my cards beneath my wing,  
And lose them when I start to sing.  

Ending
The set ends cool, the singer bows, his nametag shines like flame,  
The mouse asks for the freezer door; the pelican signs his name.
