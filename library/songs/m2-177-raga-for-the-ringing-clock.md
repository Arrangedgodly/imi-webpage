# Raga for the Ringing Clock

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: raga performance structure, patient melodic development, and a clock that keeps trying to hurry the tala*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Begin with a spacious alap, then introduce the tabla cycle; the clock should chime independently.*

Alap
A quiet string awakens in the room before the dawn,
One note leans toward the window; another carries on.
The lemur holds the phrase until the note is clear,
The clock chimes once too early, then pretends it wasn't here.

Chorus
Let the melody arrive,
Let the rhythm find its way.
Clock may ring at five to six,
We will not be led astray.

Verse 1
The tabla enters softly, taps a circle in the air,
The singer turns the melody with patience and with care.
The clock chimes every quarter; the tabla keeps its cycle,
A macaque moves the clock away and sets it by a bicycle.

Chorus
Let the melody arrive,
Let the rhythm find its way.
Clock may ring at five to six,
We will not be led astray.

Bridge
The clock chimes in the hallway; the listeners start to count,
The drummer holds the tala while the notes begin to mount.
The bell rings once, the singer bends a phrase around the sound,
The clock chimes two more times, but cannot turn the round.

Verse 2
The performance grows in color, then returns to one low string,
The drummer drops the final beat; the room becomes still.
The clock has reached its hour and gives a little ring,
The raga keeps its own pace, unfolding by its will.

Chorus
Let the melody arrive,
Let the rhythm find its way.
Clock may ring at five to six,
We will not be led astray.

Ending
They set the clock back on the wall and let the final tone remain,
The singer bows; the clock chimes once, then waits to ring again.
