# Canopy Weather Report

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: tropical pop production and rainforest field-recording textures*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Layer hand percussion, bird calls, and warm bass; avoid turning the forest into a postcard.*

## Verse 1
The field recorder caught a frog beside the stream.  
A howler monkey checked the levels on the screen.  
The producer built a beat from rain on broad leaves.  
A parrot gave a warning call that nobody believes.  
The song was for the canopy weather desk.  
The forecast named a storm, then said, "Expect a rest."  
The bass arrived like distant thunder on the ridge.  
The frog kept croaking near the fallen bridge.  

## Chorus
Canopy weather, rain in the air.  
Listen to the forest; it has things to share.  
Canopy weather, clouds moving through.  
The green keeps changing, and the forecast does too.  

## Verse 2
The crew climbed down to mark a washed-out trail.  
They found the measuring post had started to lean pale.  
A biologist recorded where the river changed its bed.  
The producer lowered music so the notes could spread.  
The parrot's call rang clear above the rain.  
They left a wider path beside the cane.  
The crew packed up before the river rose.  
The weather desk played back the storm in gentle prose.  

## Bridge
The forest isn't backing music for a scene.  
It has its own loud hours, fierce and green.  
The producer took the extra synth away.  
The frog and rain were enough to close the day.  

## Chorus
Canopy weather, rain in the air.  
Listen to the forest; it has things to share.  
Canopy weather, clouds moving through.  
The green keeps changing, and the forecast does too.  

## Ending
The field recorder dried beside the tent.  
The last bird call was not an instrument.
