# The Countertenor Calls the Neighbor Dog

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: virtuoso high-voice recitals and singers whose warm-ups closely resemble a familiar pet-training cue*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: High light solo voice and delicate piano; a dog arrives at the open garden gate when the singer repeats one note.*

[Verse 1]
I warmed my upper register beside the garden chair,  
A high and clear descending phrase went drifting through the air.  
The neighbor's dog came trotting to the open little gate,  
He sat beside the roses with a very hopeful wait.  
The neighbor came to fetch him and she heard the little tone,  
She said her recall whistle had that very shape alone.  
I sang the phrase a second time; the dog came back once more,  
My glorious top note had a useful meaning next door.  

[Refrain]
High voice, clear call, let the bright notes ring,  
The neighbor's dog has heard a tune that means a different thing.  
High voice, clear call, let the warm notes stay,  
The roses have an audience that doesn't want to stray.  
Small paws, raised ears, hold the little air,  
My scale has got a listener who expects his owner there.  

[Verse 2]
I took the warm-up indoors and I shut the garden door,  
The neighbor led her dog back home to his familiar floor.  
The piano gave a different phrase for me to rise and fall,  
The neighbor's dog stayed home without the old descending call.  
I sang the afternoon recital at the town's small hall,  
The neighbor came without her dog; she sat halfway down the hall.  
She said the high notes sounded fine without the old warm-up,  
Her dog was home enjoying quite a different evening's luck.  

[Refrain]
High voice, clear call, let the bright notes ring,  
The neighbor's dog has heard a tune that means a different thing.  
High voice, clear call, let the warm notes stay,  
The roses have an audience that doesn't want to stray.  
Small paws, raised ears, hold the little air,  
My scale has got a listener who expects his owner there.  

[Bridge]
She asked if I could record the little call for her phone,  
I sang it once inside the room, with all the doors closed alone.  
She used it as a reminder to take her dog out on time,  
The dog came to her pockets when he heard the little rhyme.  

[Final Refrain]
High voice, clear call, let the warm notes stay,  
The phone has got a countertenor for the dog-walk part of day.  
Small paws, raised ears, hold the little air,  
The neighbor keeps the recording and the dog knows where to stare.  
High voice, clear call, let the bright notes ring,  
She pays my tiny royalty in cake before the spring.
