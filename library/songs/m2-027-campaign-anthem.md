# The Candidate for Shade

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: campaign anthem conventions, applause cues, and civic stump-speech promises*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Big snare and brass cue the crowd; the candidate answers questions between hooks.*

## Verse 1
The town hall filled with folding chairs and fans.  
A mandrill candidate brought maps and rubber bands.  
He promised every sidewalk would have shade.  
The crowd asked who would water trees he'd made.  
He paused and read the budget from his notes.  
The band held back the huge, triumphant quotes.  
A child raised her hand beside the aisle.  
She asked if branches could reach her school in a while.  

## Chorus
Plant the trees, then mind the roots.  
Show the cost and bring the boots.  
Plant the trees along the way.  
A promise grows by what we do each day.  

## Verse 2
The rival brought a chart in shades of green.  
The candidate asked what the empty rows might mean.  
They checked the plan with gardeners from the park.  
One street had pipes too close beneath the bark.  
They moved the planting to the bus stop square.  
The neighborhood came out to dig and share.  
No marching band could lift the heavy dirt.  
The candidate went home with mud upon his shirt.  

## Bridge
The campaign song had promised shade by June.  
The saplings need a few more summers to grow soon.  
The child still walks beneath the noon-day glare.  
But now there are young trees beside the stair.  

## Chorus
Plant the trees, then mind the roots.  
Show the cost and bring the boots.  
Plant the trees along the way.  
A promise grows by what we do each day.  

## Ending
The brass band played at planting day, not polling night.  
The shade arrived in years, and that felt right.
