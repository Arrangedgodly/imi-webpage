# Swing Shift at the Roller Rink

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: big-band swing, dance-floor callouts, and the bandleader's unstoppable count-in*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Brass-heavy swing with walking bass; the rink manager's safety announcements should land like crisp band breaks.*

Verse 1
The bandleader lifts his baton, the trumpets give a shout,  
The rollers hit the polished floor and take the traffic out.  
A gibbon swings around the rink with elbows like a gate,  
The manager blows a whistle and says, "Please skate the figure eight."  
The saxophone runs up the scale, the bass walks down the lane,  
The floor guard rolls beside the band and sweeps away a chain.  

Chorus
Swing, glide, circle round,  
Keep your wheels upon the ground.  
Pass the cones and mind the wall,  
Leave some room for one and all.  
Brass goes high and bass goes low,  
Watch the little skaters go.  
Swing, glide, don't collide,  
The band has got the right of side.  

Verse 2
A trumpet player tries a spin and clips a paper sign,  
The sign says "Slow in corners," now it dangles from his horn.  
The band keeps playing swing as all the skaters turn,  
The manager says, "The fire lane is not a place to learn."  
A lemur tries a backward roll, then sees the snack bar light,  
She brakes beside the counter and orders fries outright.  

Bridge
The bandleader calls a break; the rink becomes serene,  
The manager sets the cones where the narrow turns have been.  
The skaters take a water stop; the sax gets one last blast,  
The guard says, "That was lovely, now please roll out at last."  

Chorus
Swing, glide, circle round,  
Keep your wheels upon the ground.  
Pass the cones and mind the wall,  
Leave some room for one and all.  
Brass goes high and bass goes low,  
Watch the little skaters go.  
Swing, glide, don't collide,  
The band has got the right of side.  

Ending
At closing, all the skates are stacked, the band puts brass away,  
The guard skates one small circle, then clocks out for the day.
