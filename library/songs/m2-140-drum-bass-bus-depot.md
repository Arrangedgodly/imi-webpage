# Drum and Bass at the Bus Depot

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: drum-and-bass breakbeats, impatient MC hype, and a delayed departure board mistaken for the next drop*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Fast breakbeats and rolling bass; sample the departure announcement between verses. The driver answers the MC's hype with two plain horn notes.*

Verse 1
The band set up beside the depot where the buses took their rest,
A macaque MC screamed "Express!" with both hands on his chest.
His breakbeat ran at twice the speed the timetable allowed,
Each numbered stop became a drop; "I missed mine!" yelled the crowd.

Chorus
Drop the bass and hold the bus,
All the waiting riders fuss.
Next stop, next drop, coming soon,
Board says "DELAY"; we call that tune.

Verse 2
The board still read "Departing" though the last bus pulled away,
The singer sampled "Please stand back" and called the track "Delay."
A gibbon driver tapped the horn, a plain two-note refrain,
The crowd demanded "One more drop!" He drove around again.

Chorus
Drop the bass and hold the bus,
All the waiting riders fuss.
Next stop, next drop, coming soon,
Board says "DELAY"; we call that tune.

Bridge
The clerk proposed a faster board; the MC cried "Too clean!
I need its dated rattle for my underground machine."
The driver checked the loading bay and left the mic to squeal,
The bassist rode the luggage rack to get the moving feel.

Verse 3
The drummer chased the departing bus, still playing double time,
The driver sold him one small seat and charged for every chime.
The MC told the empty yard, "Our bass will set the pace!"
"It's set," replied the ticket clerk. "You lost your boarding place."

Chorus
Drop the bass and hold the bus,
All the waiting riders fuss.
Next stop, next drop, coming soon,
Board says "DELAY"; we call that tune.

Ending
The horn became the hottest track; the driver took a bow,
The board refreshed behind the MC: "BOARDING OVER NOW."
