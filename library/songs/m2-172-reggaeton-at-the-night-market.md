# Reggaeton at the Night Market

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: reggaeton dembow pulse, call-and-response hooks, and dance-floor confidence that makes even a fruit scale join in*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Steady dembow beat and warm bass; the market scale clicks on every backbeat.*

Verse 1
The macaques set a speaker by the lantern stand,
A tamarin brought the mangoes, stacked them by hand.
The singer hit the rhythm, the crowd began to sway,
The market scale clicked back and weighed the beat away.

Chorus
Dum-tak, turn it round,
Let the market make the sound.
Dum-tak, move your feet,
Even scales can catch the beat.

Verse 2
The fruit seller called, "One kilo!" and the bass went low,
The dancers leaned to listen, then the scale said "No."
A mango tipped the basket, rolled into the lane,
The crowd chased after fruit and danced it back again.

Chorus
Dum-tak, turn it round,
Let the market make the sound.
Dum-tak, move your feet,
Even scales can catch the beat.

Bridge
The singer raised a hook and asked the crowd to sing,
They shouted "More bananas!" till the vendor rang.
He weighed the fruit in rhythm, one tray at a time,
The scale's small clicking number became the perfect rhyme.

Verse 3
The market cleared its walkway, the dancers made a ring,
The basket stayed in place while every fruit could swing.
The vendor packed the mangoes before the dawn,
The beat kept rolling softly as the lanterns shone.

Chorus
Dum-tak, turn it round,
Let the market make the sound.
Dum-tak, move your feet,
Even scales can catch the beat.

Ending
The scale was unplugged at closing; the dancers gave a cheer,
The vendor took the speaker home and played the beat next year.
