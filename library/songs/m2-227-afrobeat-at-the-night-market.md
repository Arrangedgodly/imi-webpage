# Mango on the One

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Afrobeat layers, interlocking guitar and horn patterns, and a night market where every stall adds a competing groove*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Layer horns, guitar, bass, and percussion gradually; let the crowd become the final rhythm section.*

[Verse 1]
The macaque band arrived when the market lamps turned gold,
The bass began a steady line, the horns replied in bold.
The fruit seller tapped a crate; the drummer caught the beat,
The spice stall shook its jars and made the rhythm sweet.

[Chorus]
Mango on the one, pepper on the two,
Horn says "come this way," the guitar says "through."
Mango on the one, lanterns in a row,
Every little market stall is making the music grow.
Mango on the one, pepper on the two,
Horn says "come this way," the guitar says "through."
Mango on the one, lanterns in a row,
Every little market stall is making the music grow.

[Verse 2]
A gibbon played guitar behind the kettle's hiss,
The kettle added steam and made a whistle to the mix.
The fruit stall rolled a mango; it bounced against a pan,
The whole row found the groove before the drummer ran.

[Chorus]
Mango on the one, pepper on the two,
Horn says "come this way," the guitar says "through."
Mango on the one, lanterns in a row,
Every little market stall is making the music grow.
Mango on the one, pepper on the two,
Horn says "come this way," the guitar says "through."
Mango on the one, lanterns in a row,
Every little market stall is making the music grow.

[Bridge]
The night watch called for closing; the band took one more turn,
The lanterns dimmed in sequence while the cooking fires burned.
The vendors closed their tables, then played with spoons and bowls;
The market made a chorus from its very moving stalls.

[Final Chorus]
Mango on the one, pepper on the two,
Horn says "stay a moment," the guitar says "through."
Mango on the one, lanterns all aglow,
The stalls are packed away, but the rhythm's still below.

[Ending]
The drummer took one mango home, and tapped it in the morning.
