# Drumline at the Empty Stadium

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: stadium drumline warmups that keep building for a crowd that has not arrived*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Tight snare rudiments grow into a full-field cadence; the echoing stands become the loudest audience member.*

**Verse 1**
The stadium doors swing open; no one takes a seat.  
A gibbon captain lifts the sticks and calls a marching beat.  
The snare line answers cleanly, the bass drum shakes the gate.  
The echo comes back louder than the players' first refrain.  
They add a tenor roll, a cymbal crash, a second line of sound.  
The empty upper deck returns it, thunder rolling round.  

**Chorus**
Roll it, hit it, let the cadence climb;  
The bleachers are applauding in a half-second time.  
One drum, two drums, the whole field hears the call;  
The echo is the biggest fan inside the empty hall.  

**Verse 2**
The team arrives to practice and mistakes the sound for cheers.  
They wave to all the empty seats and jog around the tiers.  
The drumline plays a victory march; the coach starts to respond.  
A ball rolls through the tunnel and returns upon the bond.  
The captain asks the band to stop; the stadium answers, "Stop."  
They freeze, then hear the final echo make one extra pop.  

**Chorus**
Roll it, hit it, let the cadence climb;  
The bleachers are applauding in a half-second time.  
One drum, two drums, the whole field hears the call;  
The echo is the biggest fan inside the empty hall.  

**Bridge**
They change to softer practice taps and close the far-off doors.  
The echo shrinks from upper deck to empty locker floors.  
The coach gives one small clap; the drummer answers once.  
The sound comes back politely, no thunder and no runs.  

**Ending**
The team takes to the field; the players stretch in line.  
The drumline packs its sticks away and leaves before the sign.  
The stadium holds one final beat beneath the roof.  
A janitor claps once to test it; the echo proves the proof.
