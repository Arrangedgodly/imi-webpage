# Minimalist Song for the Wind Tunnel

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: minimalist music's patient repetition and lab demonstrations that mistake sustained process for a finished result*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Use one repeated two-note figure; gradually add voices and wind noise, but let the test operator decide when the experiment is complete.*

**Verse 1**
A wind tunnel hums one note, one note, one note.  
The test crew holds a paper kite and watches how it floats.  
A monkey sings two notes in a slow and even row.  
The fan begins to turn; the kite begins to go.  
The singer adds a second line, repeating every bar.  
The operator checks the gauge and asks how long they are.  

**Chorus**
One note, two notes, let the air repeat;  
One step, two steps, keep the steady beat.  
The kite goes up, the kite comes down;  
The same small phrase goes round and round.  

**Verse 2**
The crew adds one more fan; the paper kite lifts high.  
The singer keeps the two-note loop beneath the testing sky.  
A ribbon flaps, a gauge needle moves; the engineer takes notes.  
The chorus hums the same two tones; the draft blows off their coats.  
The test is meant to end at ten, but every clock runs slow.  
The operator says, "One more minute," and the voices grow.  

**Chorus**
One note, two notes, let the air repeat;  
One step, two steps, keep the steady beat.  
The kite goes up, the kite comes down;  
The same small phrase goes round and round.  

**Bridge**
The operator stops the fan; the kite settles to the floor.  
The singers hold one final tone, then add no more.  
The gauge is read, the test is logged, the room is still again.  
The paper kite rests on the table, no longer in the wind.  

**Ending**
The crew files out; the fan is off; the humming fades away.  
One note remains inside the duct, then leaves with end of day.  
The kite's thin paper holds its shape; the test is signed complete.  
The singer leaves on quiet feet and counts the final beat.
