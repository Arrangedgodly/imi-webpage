# Four-Part Harmony at the Lost and Found

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: barbershop quartet close harmony, comic tags, and perfectly synchronized resolutions*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Four close voices with a ringing final tag; let the lost-item list grow faster than the harmony can keep up.*

Verse 1
We stand beside the counter, four singers in a square,  
The tenor holds a mitten, the bass has found a chair.  
The lead begins a welcome, the baritone joins in,  
The clerk says, "Please describe the hat before you start to sing."  
A capuchin says, "Blue one," then the tenor finds a shoe,  
The quartet locks a seventh chord and asks if that is blue.  

Chorus
Ring it, bring it, name the thing,  
Found and waiting, hear us sing.  
A scarf, a lunchbox, one left glove,  
We harmonize the goods we love.  
Round the chord and hold the note,  
Check the pocket, check the coat.  
Ring it, bring it, make it clear,  
Your missing hat is over here.  

Verse 2
The clerk unfolds a velvet cape, the lead says, "Not my size,"  
The bass tries on a paper crown and blocks the exit sign.  
The tenor finds a sandwich wrapped in yesterday's review,  
The baritone sings "Who left this?" in a resonant A-two.  
The owner comes to claim it; she can name the filling's spice,  
The quartet sings her item number once, then sings it twice.  

Bridge
A glove is found, its partner lost; the clerk checks every bin,  
The bass keeps holding one long note until the glove walks in.  
It's on the tenor's other hand; the harmony holds fast,  
The clerk says, "Please take both of them; the counter's closing past."  

Chorus
Ring it, bring it, name the thing,  
Found and waiting, hear us sing.  
A scarf, a lunchbox, one left glove,  
We harmonize the goods we love.  
Round the chord and hold the note,  
Check the pocket, check the coat.  
Ring it, bring it, make it clear,  
Your missing hat is over here.  

Ending
We sing our final perfect chord, the clerk turns out the light,  
The quartet finds four matching hats, and leaves them there all night.
