# The Chiptune Hero Finds the Battery Drawer

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: retro game victory themes and musicians treating a powerless handheld console as an epic final boss*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Square-wave accompaniment enters after the batteries are changed; the opening is sung as imitated electronic beeps.*

[Verse 1]
The hero of my handheld game had reached the final door,  
I planned to sample victory and make the speakers roar.  
I pressed the little power switch to find the glowing screen,  
The batteries were empty and the screen showed nothing green.  
I searched the desk for potions and a powerful new blade,  
The drawer held two fresh cells beside the paper clips.  
Two fresh cells in a packet waited under paperclips,  
The dragon in the silent game was far from any trips.  

[Hook]
Beep, little hero, beep to the dawn,  
The battery icon has been empty all along.  
Jump, little hero, conquer the floor,  
The greatest hidden treasure lies inside the second drawer.  
High score, bright sword, let the small world shine,  
Two fresh cells will buy us a little playing time.  

[Verse 2]
I changed the little batteries and shut the plastic plate,  
The game lit up and put the hero back beside the gate.  
I fought the final dragon with the jump button and the shield,  
The tune began its victory across the little field.  
I ran the recorder from the speaker to the track,  
The melody was tiny but the bass could fill its back.  
I built a song around it with a rising little line,  
The console had its credits while the studio finished mine.  

[Hook]
Beep, little hero, beep to the dawn,  
The battery icon has been empty all along.  
Jump, little hero, conquer the floor,  
The greatest hidden treasure lies inside the second drawer.  
High score, bright sword, let the small world shine,  
Two fresh cells will buy us a little playing time.  

[Bridge]
The crowd asked for the secret to the hero's final flight,  
I showed the little drawer beneath the studio's bright light.  
They wanted a lost level and a magical new key,  
The packet had a price tag and a plain expiry date to see.  

[Final Hook]
Beep, little hero, beep to the dawn,  
The fresh little batteries have kept the chorus on.  
High score, bright sword, let the small world shine,  
The dragon has been beaten; the receipt is also mine.  
Jump, little hero, conquer the floor,  
The sequel has a charger and requires a different drawer.
