# The Bubblegum Star Blows a Small Bubble

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: relentlessly sweet bubblegum pop videos that promise an enormous visual stunt from a performer who cannot produce it*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bright keyboards, handclaps and cheerful vocals; leave a tiny pop sound where a huge dramatic bubble is promised.*

[Verse 1]
The video director made a set of pink and white,  
The dancers wore enormous bows beneath a heart-shaped light.  
He gave me gum and told me that the final shot would show,  
A bubble large enough to put the whole chorus in its glow.  
I chewed and tried to blow one while the cameras stood quite near,  
A bubble like a little pea appeared, then disappeared.  
The dancers held their smiling pose; the director looked quite pale,  
He said we'd need a bigger ending if the single was to sell.  

[Chorus]
Pop, pop, sugar sweet,  
The bubble is a little thing beside this giant beat.  
Pink sky, bright smile, make the chorus glow,  
The video has twenty dancers and a bubble that won't grow.  
Pop, pop, let it fly,  
My tiny little ending has a very tiny sky.  

[Verse 2]
He brought a larger wad of gum; I couldn't sing around it,  
The dancers puffed their cheeks in time and tried to dance without it.  
I took the extra gum away and tried a smaller piece,  
The little bubble made a pop that fell between the beats.  
The editor enlarged the shot until my face filled all the screen,  
The bubble looked a little bigger but my chin became a scene.  
The dancers asked to see it and they laughed in perfect time,  
The director kept the tiny pop and put it on the final line.  

[Chorus]
Pop, pop, sugar sweet,  
The bubble is a little thing beside this giant beat.  
Pink sky, bright smile, make the chorus glow,  
The video has twenty dancers and a bubble that won't grow.  
Pop, pop, let it fly,  
My tiny little ending has a very tiny sky.  

[Bridge]
The fans began a challenge to make bubbles quite as small,  
The dancers showed their failures in a clip beside them all.  
The director ordered smaller bows for next week's second song,  
He said a little ending was the look we'd wanted all along.  

[Final Chorus]
Pop, pop, sugar sweet,  
The bubble has a fan club and a tiny merchandise suite.  
Pink sky, bright smile, make the chorus glow,  
The dancers blow small bubbles when the little pop says go.  
Pop, pop, let it fly,  
The next video has a giant bow; I ask the director why.
