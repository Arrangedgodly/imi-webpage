# The Swing Band at the Bus Pass Window

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: big-band swing's brassy call-and-response and suave bandleader patter*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a walking bass and punchy brass answers; the bandleader should croon as the ticket window slowly closes.*

[Verse 1]
The band arrived at lunchtime at the city's transit hall,  
A gibbon held a trumpet; a baboon held a pass for all.  
The clerk behind the window said, "One at a time, please,"  
The saxes played a shuffle and the brass began to tease.  
The leader made a finger snap, the drummer brushed a beat,  
The line moved one small step along the polished marble street.  
A macaque reached the counter as the window started down,  
The band played a big entrance while the clerk was out of town.  

[Chorus]
Swing, swing, pass me through,  
The brass can make a line feel new.  
Snap your fingers, tap your shoe,  
The ticket window's closing too.  
Swing, swing, stay in line,  
The band has passes; are they all in time?  

[Verse 2]
The clerk returned with forms to fill and asked for every name,  
The leader sang each member's part as if it were a game.  
The saxophone went "doo," the trombone answered "wah,"  
The clerk read every number while the drummer went "ba-da."  
A capuchin had brought the wrong route card from the hall,  
The trumpet played a solo to announce the mix-up call.  
They took the right form, got their passes, and stepped aside,  
The band struck up a walking tune and let the next folks ride.  

[Chorus]
Swing, swing, pass me through,  
The brass can make a line feel new.  
Snap your fingers, tap your shoe,  
The ticket window's closing too.  
Swing, swing, stay in line,  
The band has passes; are they all in time?  

[Bridge]
The leader tipped his hat and said, "We paid our proper fare,"  
The clerk held up the passes and counted them with care.  
One pass was for the saxophone, one was for the bass,  
One was for the trumpet stand, reserved a window place.  

[Final Chorus]
Swing, swing, now board the train,  
The brass is leaving in the rain.  
Snap your fingers, tap your shoe,  
The clerk has stamped the whole band through.  
Swing, swing, take your time,  
The band has passes; now they're in line.  

[Ending]
The trumpet boarded first; the music stand missed the bus.
