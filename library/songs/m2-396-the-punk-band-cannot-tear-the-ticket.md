# The Punk Band Cannot Tear the Ticket

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: furious punk anthems against authority, defeated by durable packaging and practical ticket technology*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Fast distorted guitars and gang vocals; leave a sudden silent gap for the ticket's tiny scanner beep.*

[Verse 1]
We stood outside the venue with our jackets full of pins,  
The singer waved a ticket like the revolution wins.  
He said he'd tear the system into seven little scraps,  
Then tried to rip his ticket while the drummer clapped his hands.  
The ticket was a plastic card the venue used again,  
He pulled it till his face went red; it would not even bend.  
The bassist offered scissors from a pocket in his coat,  
The door attendant told him, "It is borrowed. Read the note."  

[Chorus]
Rip it up, shout it out,  
There is something very sturdy that we need to talk about.  
Fight the wall, kick the line,  
We are standing at the entrance fifteen minutes past our time.  
Rip it up, make them hear,  
The plastic has no feelings and it doesn't show us fear.  
Big noise, small card, break the spell,  
We have brought our mighty fury to a thing that scans quite well.  

[Verse 2]
The singer called the card a symbol of a soulless age,  
The attendant said, "Without it you cannot get to the stage."  
The drummer took his sticks out and proposed a little roll,  
The bassist found the email with our booking and our role.  
It said the card was reusable to cut the paper waste,  
The singer stopped his pulling and adopted thoughtful grace.  
He said he'd save the planet by returning it intact,  
The attendant said, "That's lovely. Now your set is running back."  

[Chorus]
Rip it up, shout it out,  
There is something very sturdy that we need to talk about.  
Fight the wall, kick the line,  
We are standing at the entrance fifteen minutes past our time.  
Rip it up, make them hear,  
The plastic has no feelings and it doesn't show us fear.  
Big noise, small card, break the spell,  
We have brought our mighty fury to a thing that scans quite well.  

[Bridge]
She scanned the card and took it back; the gate swung open wide,  
We carried our rebellion to the waiting crowd inside.  
The singer wrote a furious song about the scanner's beep,  
The crowd demanded shorter sets so they could get some sleep.  

[Final Chorus]
Rip it up, shout it out,  
The toughest thing we met tonight is safely sorted out.  
Fight the wall, kick the line,  
The attendant has a cupboard full of little cards like mine.  
Big noise, small card, break the spell,  
Our chorus has a scanner beep the fans all know quite well.  
Rip it up, make them hear,  
We bought the door attendant lunch; she booked us for next year.
