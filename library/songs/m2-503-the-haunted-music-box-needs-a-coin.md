# The Haunted Music Box Needs a Coin

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: spooky music-box songs and singers treating a coin-operated attraction as a supernatural presence*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Thin celesta and whispered voice; insert a coin before each return of the tune.*

[Verse 1]
A music box stood waiting at the fairground's little gate,  
A painted dancer circled on a small revolving plate.  
I sang about a lonely spirit caught behind the glass,  
The tune stopped in the middle as the next few people passed.  
I pressed my face against the pane and asked what made it sleep,  
The owner pointed to the slot where little coins could keep.  
One coin bought half a minute of the dancer and the tune,  
The lonely spirit's schedule had a very earthly noon.  

[Refrain]
Turn, little dancer, turn in the dark,  
The silver coin can wake your very ordinary heart.  
Turn, little dancer, circle in the light,  
Your thirty seconds finish before the last note of the night.  
Thin tune, pale face, hold the little air,  
The owner has a bucket full of coins beside his chair.  

[Verse 2]
I dropped a coin into the slot to catch the song again,  
The dancer started turning to a very short refrain.  
I finished my long verse just as the little plate stopped dead,  
The owner said the half-minute had gone exactly as he said.  
I wrote a shorter chorus that could fit inside the time,  
I gave the slot a second coin and sang the little rhyme.  
The plate reached its whole circuit and the song reached its last beat,  
The people by the fairground gate applauded something neat.  

[Refrain]
Turn, little dancer, turn in the dark,  
The silver coin can wake your very ordinary heart.  
Turn, little dancer, circle in the light,  
Your thirty seconds finish before the last note of the night.  
Thin tune, pale face, hold the little air,  
The owner has a bucket full of coins beside his chair.  

[Bridge]
The owner taped my short words on the window of the box,  
I wrote the last note's timing underneath the painted rocks.  
The children sang the little chorus after putting in their coin,  
The painted dancer turned around and had no voice to join.  

[Final Refrain]
Turn, little dancer, turn in the dark,  
The fairground prints the short song on a little postcard mark.  
Thin tune, pale face, hold the little air,  
The owner has my byline on the glass beside his chair.  
Turn, little dancer, circle in the light,  
My haunting has a royalty of one free spin a night.
