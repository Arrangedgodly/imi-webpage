# The Harp Duet Tunes Through the Interval

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: elegant harp-and-voice recitals and a singer filling every necessary tuning pause with another claim of effortless beauty*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Flowing harp and poised soprano; the harp stops for several practical tuning corrections between sections.*

[Verse 1]
The harpist checked her strings beneath the warming stage light,  
I sang about a perfect moon upon a perfect night.  
A string had drifted slightly from the pitch that it should keep,  
She paused to use the tuning key while I sang seas of sleep.  
I filled the little silence with a verse about the stars,  
She asked if I could stop so she could hear which note was ours.  
I held the next line in my mouth and let her test the tone,  
The harpist found the proper pitch when left to hear alone.  

[Refrain]
Flow, little strings, flow through the air,  
The singer has a moon to sell and moonlight everywhere.  
Flow, little strings, keep the silver light,  
The harpist has a tuning key to make the note quite right.  
Still voice, small turn, let the quiet stay,  
The perfect night needs one short pause before it flows away.  

[Verse 2]
We played the next song smoothly with the strings all singing clear,  
The audience heard the melody we'd meant for them to hear.  
The interval began and she checked the strings once more,  
I spoke to all the audience beside the stage's door.  
I said the harp was effortless and lived on golden air,  
She showed the tuning key to those who stood beside her chair.  
They watched her make a small correction on a silver note,  
The golden air had hard work behind the story that I wrote.  

[Refrain]
Flow, little strings, flow through the air,  
The singer has a moon to sell and moonlight everywhere.  
Flow, little strings, keep the silver light,  
The harpist has a tuning key to make the note quite right.  
Still voice, small turn, let the quiet stay,  
The perfect night needs one short pause before it flows away.  

[Bridge]
We finished all the second half with both our parts in line,  
The audience gave the harpist flowers underneath the sign.  
I gave her time to check the final chord before our encore,  
She said a little silence was worth a verse or more.  

[Final Refrain]
Flow, little strings, keep the silver light,  
The tuning key has helped us make the last chord right.  
Still voice, small turn, let the quiet stay,  
I count the little pause before the next song flows away.  
Flow, little strings, flow through the air,  
The program lists the tuning key beside the harpist's chair.
