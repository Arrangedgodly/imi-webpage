# The Waltz Instructor Learns the Rolling Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: waltz instructional recordings and dancers turning smooth movement into accidental travel across a room*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Three-quarter piano with a calmly counting instructor; the learner rolls away while demonstrating the seated footwork.*

[Instructor]
One, two, three, breathe and turn.  
One, two, three, let the body learn.  
Keep your shoulders loose and light.  
The learner answers, "Mine feel right."  

[Verse 1]
We filmed a simple seated lesson for a rainy winter day,  
The learner brought an office chair to help him find the way.  
He tapped the count upon the floor with both his polished shoes,  
The castors rolled him slowly back with every move he'd choose.  
I spoke about the graceful turn and floated through the room,  
The learner sailed behind me toward the cupboard and the broom.  
He asked if traveling backward was included in the dance,  
I said the chair had added its own unauthorized chance.  

[Counting Refrain]
One, two, three, let the music flow,  
The seated demonstration has somewhere else to go.  
One, two, three, keep your shoulders free,  
The learner's at the cupboard where the audience cannot see.  
Turn your head and hold the line,  
The chair has done a traveling step ahead of lesson nine.  

[Verse 2]
We stopped the little camera and we rolled him to his mark,  
He stood up from the rolling chair beside the camera mark.  
We fetched a steady dining chair and set it in the place,  
He sat and tapped the three-count with a very settled face.  
The camera caught the whole step with the learner in the frame,  
The cupboard got no second dance and stayed exactly the same.  
We filmed the standing version with the chairs put by the door,  
The learner waltzed beside me on the ordinary floor.  

[Counting Refrain]
One, two, three, let the music flow,  
The seated demonstration has somewhere else to go.  
One, two, three, keep your shoulders free,  
The learner's at the cupboard where the audience cannot see.  
Turn your head and hold the line,  
The chair has done a traveling step ahead of lesson nine.  

[Outro]
We labeled both the lessons with the chairs that they should use,  
The learner kept the rolling take among the clips he'd choose.  
He showed it at his birthday to a room of laughing friends,  
The dining chair stayed steady while the bonus lesson ends.  
One, two, three, breathe and turn,  
Some lessons have a cupboard that the dancers have to learn.
