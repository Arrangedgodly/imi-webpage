# Ode to the Missing Chair

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: orchestral anthems, swelling strings, and ceremonial overstatement*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Begin with a lone oboe; bring in the brass as the hall staff search under tables.*

## Verse 1
The mayor's great hall held speeches every spring.  
Tonight the orchestra had no chair for the king.  
A ring-tailed lemur checked the seating chart twice.  
The trumpet player offered him a folding stool, quite nice.  
The oboe played a question through the empty hall.  
The violas answered from behind the curtain wall.  
A stagehand found four cushions and a crate.  
The conductor raised her baton, prepared to dedicate.  

## Chorus
Rise, strings, for furniture out of place.  
Brass, proclaim the lost seat's case.  
Drums, announce its modest wooden frame.  
Tonight the chair will earn a name.  

## Verse 2
The chair was in the loading bay, beside a potted fern.  
A porter had wheeled it there and promised to return.  
The orchestra swelled as the search party crossed the floor.  
The king arrived on time and waited by the door.  
He saw the chair, then picked it up himself.  
He carried it past flags and placed it on the shelf.  
The cellos grew majestic, the horns let out a blast.  
The lemur said, "It is a chair. At last."  

## Bridge
The mayor began a speech about the nation's spine.  
The king sat down and said, "This seat is fine."  
The conductor kept her arms aloft another bar.  
The hall staff laughed, then struck the final chord afar.  

## Chorus
Rise, strings, for furniture out of place.  
Brass, proclaim the lost seat's case.  
Drums, announce its modest wooden frame.  
Tonight the chair will earn a name.  

## Ending
The chair went back to storage when the guests departed.  
The orchestra left smiling, slightly under-started.
