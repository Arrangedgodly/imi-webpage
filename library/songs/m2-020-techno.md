# Warehouse Clockwork

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: minimal techno repetition and industrial dance-floor culture*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Let the kick build by tiny changes; keep the steel factory sounds in the mix.*

## Verse 1
The old clockworks sat quiet beyond the freight yard gate.  
A troop of macaques arrived at quarter after eight.  
They brought two decks, a lamp, and cables wound in pairs.  
The caretaker unlocked the doors and showed the stairs.  
A kick drum met the pulse of pipes below.  
The factory's clock began to count the slow.  
One loop returned, then shifted half a shade.  
The dancers moved like gears the night had made.  

## Chorus
Round again, one more turn.  
Keep the pulse, let the bearings learn.  
Round again, less is clear.  
Stay inside the sound you hear.  

## Verse 2
A mechanic came to check the boiler's hum.  
She stayed to hear the second rhythm come.  
The DJ filtered out a clank, then brought it back.  
The room answered in shoes upon the track.  
No laser cut the dark, no video wall.  
A single work light painted shadows on the wall.  
At 2 a.m. the pipes went still.  
The dancers kept the pulse by tapping on the sill.  

## Bridge
A pattern can be patient, but it must keep room to breathe.  
The DJ dropped the kick and let the silence weave.  
The crowd stood still, then heard the clocks restart.  
The next beat came like a small repaired heart.  

## Chorus
Round again, one more turn.  
Keep the pulse, let the bearings learn.  
Round again, less is clear.  
Stay inside the sound you hear.  

## Ending
The factory lights came on at half past five.  
The crew packed up; the clocks kept time alive.
