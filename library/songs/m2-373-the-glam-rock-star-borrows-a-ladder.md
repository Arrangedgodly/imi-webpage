# The Glam Rock Star Borrows a Ladder

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: glam-rock entrance songs whose enormous costumes prevent the star from reaching the stage*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Stomping drums, glittering guitar and a crowd chant; the ladder's hinges click under every heroic ascent.*

[Verse 1]
I wore my silver platform boots and wings of purple light,  
My hair was full of tiny stars, my cape could cover night.  
The entrance stairs were narrow and my boots were very wide,  
The drummer played my welcome as I waited on the side.  
I raised one arm toward heaven and the feathers hit a beam,  
The stagehand fetched a wider ladder for the mighty dream.  

[Chorus]
Rise, little star, rise above the floor,  
Let the glitter lead you to the stage once more.  
Shine, little star, let the crowd believe,  
The stagehand has a handful of your feathers up his sleeve.  
Reach for the sky, reach for the light,  
Please keep both your platform boots upon the rung tonight.  

[Verse 2]
I stepped upon the lowest rung and lifted up my head,  
The wings got caught between the sides and several feathers shed.  
The crowd began my anthem though I hadn't reached the mic,  
The bassist sang the opening with a very different bite.  
I took the wings off slowly and I passed them to the crew,  
The stagehand wore them backwards and the crowd approved the view.  

[Chorus]
Rise, little star, rise above the floor,  
Let the glitter lead you to the stage once more.  
Shine, little star, let the crowd believe,  
The stagehand has a handful of your feathers up his sleeve.  
Reach for the sky, reach for the light,  
Please keep both your platform boots upon the rung tonight.  

[Bridge]
I climbed the final rungs and took my place beneath the beams,  
My cape was still below me in the hands of other dreams.  
The stagehand spread the borrowed wings and climbed the other stair,  
The crowd turned all their glitter toward the feathers in his hair.  

[Final Chorus]
Rise, little star, rise above the floor,  
Let the glitter lead you to the stage once more.  
Shine, little star, let the crowd believe,  
The stagehand has the costume and the spotlight up his sleeve.  
Reach for the sky, reach for the light,  
I hold his ladder steady for the encore every night.
