# Hallelujah at Platform Nine

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel choir call-and-response, organ swells, and a late bus promoted to lead soloist*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Build from handclaps to full choir; give the bus horn the final response.*

[Verse 1]
The night bus missed its minute; the depot clock said two,
A choir of capuchins had one more hymn to do.
Their organ hummed a question; the benches answered low,
The conductor raised one hand and said, "We'll make a chorus grow."

[Call and Response]
LEAD: Will the bus come rolling?
CHOIR: When the headlights find the lane.
LEAD: Will it bring our neighbors?
CHOIR: And the bags that rode in rain.
LEAD: Is that horn a solo?
CHOIR: It is singing through the pane.
LEAD: Will we keep on waiting?
CHOIR: We can harmonize the strain.

[Verse 2]
The organ swelled to Sunday size; the driver heard the sound,
He honked one note in answer as he turned the corner round.
The choir mistook the brake hiss for a second tenor part;
The driver climbed aboard to ask who'd tuned the parking cart.

[Call and Response]
LEAD: Will the bus come rolling?
CHOIR: When the headlights find the lane.
LEAD: Will it bring our neighbors?
CHOIR: And the bags that rode in rain.
LEAD: Is that horn a solo?
CHOIR: It is singing through the pane.
LEAD: Will we keep on waiting?
CHOIR: We can harmonize the strain.

[Bridge]
The doors swung wide; the choir filed in, still clapping on the beat.
The driver asked for quiet, but the seats supplied a seat.
They sang the route announcements in a four-part round;
Each stop arrived on cue beneath a glorious sound.

[Final Call and Response]
LEAD: Has the bus come rolling?
CHOIR: Yes, the doors are open wide.
LEAD: Are the singers boarding?
CHOIR: With the organ by our side.
LEAD: Who will sing the last note?
CHOIR: Let the horn reply outside.

[Ending]
The bus pulled off; its horn held one long note. The choir gave it five stars.
