# Doo-Wop at the Window Wash

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: doo-wop close harmonies, nonsense syllables, and a lead singer who turns a cleaning shift into a street-corner serenade*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Finger snaps and stacked doo-wop harmonies; each squeaky window joins the bass part.*

Verse 1
The macaques came to wash the windows at the corner store,
They stacked their cloths and buckets by the painted loading door.
The lead sang, "Doo-wah, glass so bright," while bubbles filled the pail,
A gibbon gave a high note when a squeegee left a trail.

Chorus
Doo-wah, shine and sway,
Squeegee to the left, then spray.
Shoo-bop, clear the pane,
Let the sunshine sing again.

Verse 2
They scrubbed the lower windows in a smooth four-part round,
The bass went "brrm" each time the cloth came sliding down.
Their harmony was perfect till the owner raised the blind,
The glass reflected all the singers, doubled line by line.

Chorus
Doo-wah, shine and sway,
Squeegee to the left, then spray.
Shoo-bop, clear the pane,
Let the sunshine sing again.

Bridge
A capuchin began to sing to keep the strokes in time,
The store's alarm replied with two notes in the same rhyme.
The owner stepped outside and asked them to turn it off,
They held a chord so steady that the alarm began to cough.

Verse 3
They finished every window but the one behind the sign,
The owner moved the sign and showed a pawprint in the grime.
The crew wiped it away; the crowd sang "Shoo-bop, clean!"
The pawprint reappeared as soon as someone touched the screen.

Chorus
Doo-wah, shine and sway,
Squeegee to the left, then spray.
Shoo-bop, clear the pane,
Let the sunshine sing again.

Ending
They charged no fee for pawprints; the store put up a sign,
"Please tap to leave a handprint," and the glass became a shrine.
