# The Spare Chair's Wedding

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: klezmer wedding music, clarinet trills, joyful dance circles, and a spare chair promoted to honored guest*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Play quick clarinet ornaments over a buoyant bass; let the chair's scrape become a comic rhythm.*

[Verse 1]
At the wedding hall, a gibbon set a chair beside the band,
The guests all made a circle and the clarinet began.
A capuchin came dancing, his tail swept through the floor,
The chair slid down the aisle and stopped beside the door.

[Chorus]
Oy, turn the chair, let the clarinet fly,
Spin it around as the dancers go by.
Oy, lift the chair, don't let it depart,
The spare seat's got a place in the newlyweds' hearts.

[Verse 2]
The bride and groom were waiting; the chair rolled into place,
The clarinet went trilling as it joined the wedding pace.
A macaque grabbed the backrest, then she twirled it like a partner,
The band played faster still, and the chair became a starter.

[Chorus]
Oy, turn the chair, let the clarinet fly,
Spin it around as the dancers go by.
Oy, lift the chair, don't let it depart,
The spare seat's got a place in the newlyweds' hearts.

[Bridge]
The chair's leg caught the rug, the music stopped at once,
The guests all held their poses like a very careful dance.
They lifted up the chair, the rug lay flat again,
The clarinet came back with a joyful little bend.

[Final Chorus]
Oy, turn the chair, let the clarinet fly,
Spin it around as the dancers go by.
Oy, lift the chair, don't let it depart,
The spare seat's got a place in the newlyweds' hearts.

[Ending]
The chair was saved a slice and sat beside the band.
