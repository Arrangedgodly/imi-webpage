# The Bossa Nova Singer Waits for the Bus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: relaxed bossa nova travel songs and the singer whose unhurried rhythm cannot change a timetable*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Soft guitar, brushed drums and a lead with an increasingly precise eye on the bus stop display.*

[Verse 1]
I sang about the warm blue coast beside the stop for nine,  
My guitar case lay by my feet; the morning looked divine.  
The clock upon the shelter showed a minute left to go,  
I lingered on a lovely note and sang it soft and slow.  
The bus pulled up beside me with its doors held open wide,  
I finished one more gentle line before I tried to ride.  
The driver shut the doors before my song could reach its end,  
I watched the bus depart and called the sea my patient friend.  

[Refrain]
Softly rolls the morning, gently shines the bay,  
The bus has got a timetable that takes it on its way.  
Softly plays the guitar, slowly falls the rhyme,  
The driver has a route to run and leaves the stop on time.  
Blue sky, warm breeze, let the daylight stay,  
My ticket has another bus in twenty minutes' sway.  

[Verse 2]
I checked the board for nineteen; it would take the coast road too,  
I closed the case and watched the road beneath the gentle blue.  
A traveler asked me for a song to pass the little wait,  
I sang a short refrain and stopped before it grew too late.  
The nineteen turned the corner; I had packed away the strings,  
I stood beside the shelter with my case and other things.  
The doors slid open quietly; I climbed aboard at once,  
The driver punched my ticket; I sat down without response.  

[Refrain]
Softly rolls the morning, gently shines the bay,  
The bus has got a timetable that takes it on its way.  
Softly plays the guitar, slowly falls the rhyme,  
The driver has a route to run and leaves the stop on time.  
Blue sky, warm breeze, let the daylight stay,  
My ticket has a coastward seat; I'm on my way.  

[Bridge]
The coast was there at noon beneath a very patient sun,  
I played the long slow chorus when the traveling was done.  
The traveler from the shelter took a seat upon the sand,  
He kept the return timetable folded neatly in his hand.  

[Final Refrain]
Softly rolls the morning, gently shines the bay,  
The song can take its time while we have nowhere else to stray.  
Softly plays the guitar, slowly falls the rhyme,  
The traveler taps his timetable at half past closing time.  
Blue sky, warm breeze, let the daylight stay,  
I finish before the last bus carries both of us away.
