# Fado for the Empty Birdbath

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: fado's saudade and the temptation to turn every ordinary absence into an eternal lament*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Intimate Portuguese-guitar-like strings and a measured vocal; keep the melancholy sincere while the practical cause keeps intruding.*

**Verse 1**
At dusk, old Tavi sits beside a birdbath, dry and bare.  
He sings of wings that once came down to drink the evening air.  
The guitar bends one aching note; a finch looks from the wall.  
It hops around the empty bowl and chirps, "No water at all."  
Tavi sings of summer's end, of feathers lost to night.  
His neighbor brings a watering can and asks him to move right.  

**Chorus**
Ai, the bowl is empty, the fountain has gone still;  
Ai, the birds are waiting on the roof above the hill.  
Sing low for what has left us, sing long for what may be;  
The tap is just behind the shed, if someone finds the key.  

**Verse 2**
The key is on the garden hook, beneath a folded glove.  
Tavi sings about the vanished flock, the ache of absent love.  
A finch lands on the handle; the watering can tips down.  
A trickle fills the little bowl and darkens all the ground.  
He sings of rain remembered and the thirst of every tree.  
The finch takes one quick sip and stares impatiently.  

**Chorus**
Ai, the bowl is empty, the fountain has gone still;  
Ai, the birds are waiting on the roof above the hill.  
Sing low for what has left us, sing long for what may be;  
The tap is just behind the shed, if someone finds the key.  

**Bridge**
His neighbor turns the tap off; the bowl is full enough.  
The finch drinks, then splashes water; Tavi sings the rough.  
The song swells toward forever, but the bird flaps to the hedge.  
He follows with the watering can and clears a leafy edge.  

**Ending**
At dusk, the birds return again; the bowl reflects the sky.  
Tavi lets the final guitar chord fade without goodbye.  
The tap is closed, the key is hung, the garden breathes at ease.  
A finch sings one small note; Tavi answers in the breeze.
