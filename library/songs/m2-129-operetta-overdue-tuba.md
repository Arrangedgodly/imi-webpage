# Operetta of the Overdue Tuba

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: light operetta conventions, mistaken identity, and a tuba player who has to deliver the instrument before the final ensemble number*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Light orchestral accompaniment with patter dialogue and a lyrical refrain; keep the tuba's bass entrance as the final reveal.*

Verse 1
A gibbon in the theater found a tuba by the stair,
A note was tied around it: "Please return this instrument with care."
The stage manager asked the players, "Whose tuba did we find?"
The violinist said, "Not mine," and the oboe shook its mind.

Chorus
Where is the tuba, who knows its place?
The final number needs its bass to lead the chase.
Search the wings and check the stand,
The missing sound is in the band.

Verse 2
A capuchin in costume claimed the tuba as a prize,
He puffed into the mouthpiece and produced a small surprise.
The stage manager cried, "That's not your part; the chorus starts at eight!"
The capuchin marched down the hall, still playing at the gate.

Chorus
Where is the tuba, who knows its place?
The final number needs its bass to lead the chase.
Search the wings and check the stand,
The missing sound is in the band.

Bridge
A tenor heard the low note and mistook it for a train,
He boarded the prop cart and rolled out through the rain.
The tuba player chased him, with the score tucked in his hat,
The cart reached the orchestra pit and stopped beside the cat.

Verse 3
The tuba returned to its player; the tenor found his feet,
The conductor raised the baton for the final comic beat.
The bass line joined the melody and gave the chorus ground,
The cat sat on the prop cart and listened to the sound.

Chorus
Where is the tuba, who knows its place?
The final number needs its bass to lead the chase.
Search the wings and check the stand,
The missing sound is in the band.

Ending
The curtain fell, the tuba bowed, the note was loud and clear,
The prop cart stayed behind the line, the cat remained to hear.
