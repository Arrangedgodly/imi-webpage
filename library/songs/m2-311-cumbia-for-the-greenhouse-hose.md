# Cumbia for the Greenhouse Hose

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cumbia's rolling dance groove and greenhouse demonstrations that turn watering into a parade*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Rolling cumbia rhythm with accordion and hand drum; each watering pass sweeps the dancers into a new row.*

**Verse 1**
The greenhouse opens early; the hose is on the floor.  
A cumbia band starts warming up beside the glassy door.  
A lemur lifts the nozzle; the band begins to sway.  
The plants all catch the silver spray and glisten in the day.  
The gardener says, "Keep to the path; the seedlings need their space."  
The dancers make a curving line and circle round the place.  

**Chorus**
Roll, roll, hose line, turn around the bed;  
Cumbia moves the water while the leaves nod overhead.  
Step, step, spray, don't soak the seedling row;  
The band keeps up the rhythm while the greenhouse starts to grow.  

**Verse 2**
The nozzle turns too far; a mist reaches the accordion.  
The player dries the keys and plays a wetter variation.  
The gardener marks the dry spots with a chalk line on the ground.  
The dancers cross the line in time and water all around.  
A sprinkler starts behind them, adding syncopated drops.  
The band counts every puddle; the floor refuses stops.  

**Chorus**
Roll, roll, hose line, turn around the bed;  
Cumbia moves the water while the leaves nod overhead.  
Step, step, spray, don't soak the seedling row;  
The band keeps up the rhythm while the greenhouse starts to grow.  

**Bridge**
The gardener shuts the valve; the final drops fall slow.  
The dancers wring their sleeves and help the seedlings grow.  
The hose is coiled in circles; no one crosses through.  
The accordion dries; the last beat is clean and true.  

**Ending**
The doors swing wide; the band steps out into the square.  
The plants stand watered, leaves all bright beneath the air.  
The hose stays on its hook, the path is dry and clear.  
The gardener sways one quiet step; the plants can hear.
