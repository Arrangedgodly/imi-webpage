# A Round for the Echo Trail

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: traditional singing rounds that rely on precise staggered entrances and cascading imitation*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Sing the first phrase plainly, then layer each entrance in a clean round; let the echo trail return the line at the wrong interval.*

[Verse 1]
A troop set out at morning on the echo trail,  
A gibbon sang one simple line to test the valley's rail.  
The macaques joined behind him as the phrase went round,  
A capuchin came in too soon and doubled every sound.  
The ranger gave a hand signal: wait until the bend,  
The singers saw the gesture and began the phrase again.  
The first voice reached the ridge, the second reached the stream,  
The third voice hit the picnic shed and woke a sleeping team.  

[Chorus]
Sing it once, then let it go,  
The hillside will return the words you know.  
Wait a beat, then join the line,  
A round should travel orderly and fine.  
Sing it once, then listen near,  
The echo has a verse that sounds sincere.  

[Verse 2]
They tried a shorter phrase about the trail's blue sign,  
The first group sang it clearly, the next group sang it twice.  
The ranger tapped the tempo on a walking stick,  
The echo copied every tap and answered far too quick.  
A baboon entered early, the gibbon entered late,  
The whole round met together at the narrow wooden gate.  
The singers held one vowel and it bounced from wall to wall,  
The echo sang the ending before they'd sung at all.  

[Chorus]
Sing it once, then let it go,  
The hillside will return the words you know.  
Wait a beat, then join the line,  
A round should travel orderly and fine.  
Sing it once, then listen near,  
The echo has a verse that sounds sincere.  

[Bridge]
The leader asked for silence; every singer stopped,  
The valley answered silence with a loud and echoing "stop!"  
They waited out the answer till the last sound disappeared,  
Then sang in staggered order as the timing engineer.  

[Final Chorus]
Sing it once, then let it fly,  
The echo meets the mountain and returns across the sky.  
Wait a beat, then join the line,  
The voices now are orderly and fine.  
Sing it once, then take a bow,  
The hillside knows the ending now.  

[Ending]
The final echo arrived on time, then asked for the encore.
