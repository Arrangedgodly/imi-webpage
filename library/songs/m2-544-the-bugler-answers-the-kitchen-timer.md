# The Bugler Answers the Kitchen Timer

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: brisk bugle calls and musicians interpreting every household beep as a command to perform*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Clear solo bugle between sung verses; the kitchen timer has a completely different two-beep pattern.*

[Verse 1]
The village bugler practiced in the courtyard by the hall,  
He played the morning signal and the little evening call.  
The kitchen timer sounded through the window at his side,  
He answered with a bugle phrase and stood up full of pride.  
The cook leaned out to ask him if he meant to call the crew,  
He said a signal always needed something sounding through.  
She said the timer marked the bread she'd placed to rise,  
The kitchen had its own small call beneath the cooking skies.  

[Refrain]
Call, little bugle, let the morning rise,  
The kitchen has a loaf of bread beneath its warming skies.  
Call, little bugle, answer what you hear,  
The cook would like the oven checked before the crew comes near.  
Bright brass, short breath, hold the little note,  
The timer doesn't need the bugle answer that you wrote.  

[Verse 2]
The cook took out the bread and placed it on the cooling rack,  
The bugler put his horn beside the small rehearsal pack.  
She set the timer once again to make another tray,  
He waited for the beep and let the kitchen have its say.  
The village crew arrived at noon according to the clock,  
He played the meal call when the cook had set the bread in stock.  
The crew sat down to lunch with every plate upon the board,  
The timer had its own voice and the bugle kept its chord.  

[Refrain]
Call, little bugle, let the morning rise,  
The kitchen has a loaf of bread beneath its warming skies.  
Call, little bugle, answer what you hear,  
The cook would like the oven checked before the crew comes near.  
Bright brass, short breath, hold the little note,  
The timer doesn't need the bugle answer that you wrote.  

[Bridge]
The cook asked him to write a tune for next year's bread fair,  
He wrote two short responding phrases for the oven's air.  
She chose the silent gap between them for the tray's own chime,  
The bugler waited patiently until his written time.  

[Final Refrain]
Call, little bugle, hold the little note,  
The timer has a voice that needs no bugle in its throat.  
Bright brass, short breath, let the bread cool down,  
The cook sells warm loaves after the bugle calls the town.  
Call, little bugle, let the morning rise,  
The oven gets a solo that the bugler does not try.
