# Banjo on the Clothesline

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bluegrass picking circles, fast instrumental breaks, and a washday contest judged by stray socks*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Trade banjo and fiddle breaks; use a clothespin click as the count-in.*

[Verse 1]
Old Mae the macaque strung a line from post to post,
She washed the band's blue shirts and hung each one to boast.
The banjo player picked beside the soapbox on the ground;
A clothespin snapped the count-in and the washday became sound.

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, hang it high, let the shirts flap free,
One sock's on the clothesline, one sock's on my knee.
Pick it quick, pass the peg, keep the rhythm clean,
The fastest little laundry band this valley ever seen.

[Verse 2]
The fiddle took a solo; a shirt spun like a wheel.
The dogwood caught a handkerchief and joined the string-band reel.
Mae called out for matching socks; the players searched the yard,
They found one in the fiddle case and one beneath Mae's hat.

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, hang it high, let the shirts flap free,
One sock's on the clothesline, one sock's on my knee.
Pick it quick, pass the peg, keep the rhythm clean,
The fastest little laundry band this valley ever seen.

[Bridge]
A gust lifted every shirt and made a flying tent;
The banjo player ducked below and kept the tempo bent.
Mae clipped down the corners; the audience stomped along;
The clothesline held the harmony, the clothespins held the song.

[Final Chorus]
Pick it quick, hang it high, let the shirts flap free,
Two socks found each other; the third stayed mystery.
Pick it quick, pass the peg, the wash is almost clean,
The fastest little laundry band this valley ever seen.

[Ending]
Mae sold the unmatched sock a banjo ticket for next week.
