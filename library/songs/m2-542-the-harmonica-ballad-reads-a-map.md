# The Harmonica Ballad Reads a Map

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: traveling harmonica ballads and musicians whose portable instrument leaves their hands free for every other distraction*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Acoustic guitar and voice with neck-rack harmonica breaks; the map rustles over the instrumental solo.*

[Verse 1]
I wore my harmonica holder for a walk beside the bay,  
I sang about a winding road that led so far away.  
The holder kept my hands quite free to read the map I brought,  
I followed every dotted line beside the tune I'd taught.  
The harmonica took a breath whenever I looked down,  
I sounded two wrong notes while searching for the next small town.  
A walker asked if all the bends were written in my tune,  
I said the map and melody had met too very soon.  

[Refrain]
Road bends left, tune bends right,  
The holder keeps the harmonica very near my sight.  
Road bends left, map goes down,  
A little wrong note shows me where I ought to find the town.  
Free hands, full page, let the harbor wind,  
The music needs a moment that the map has left behind.  

[Verse 2]
I stopped upon a little bench to read the route ahead,  
The holder stayed beside my chin with no notes being said.  
I folded up the map and took the road toward the pier,  
I played a clean harmonica phrase with all the way quite clear.  
The walker kept beside me till the road split by the stream,  
We took two different turning paths beyond the shared small theme.  
I reached the town I'd meant to find and played beside the square,  
The map stayed folded in my pocket while the notes filled up the air.  

[Refrain]
Road bends left, tune bends right,  
The holder keeps the harmonica very near my sight.  
Road bends left, map goes down,  
A little wrong note shows me where I ought to find the town.  
Free hands, full page, let the harbor wind,  
The music needs a moment that the map has left behind.  

[Bridge]
I wrote a song about the map and all its little bends,  
The walker heard a recording that I sent to all my friends.  
She said the road was shorter than the solo that I'd made,  
I cut the solo down before the map was on display.  

[Final Refrain]
Road bends left, let the harbor wind,  
The tune has got a proper break before the walk begins.  
Free hands, full page, let the little notes roam,  
The map has got a separate verse that brings me back home.  
Road bends left, tune bends right,  
The walker sends a shorter map to help me through the night.
