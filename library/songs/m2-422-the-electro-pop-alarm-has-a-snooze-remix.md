# The Electro-Pop Alarm Has a Snooze Remix

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: relentlessly cheerful electro-pop wake-up songs and remixes that extend a tune beyond the listener's endurance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bright synth bass and cheerful layered vocals; each snooze restarts the opening hook in a slightly more exhausted voice.*

[Verse 1]
I set my favorite chorus as the alarm beside the bed,  
Its hopeful little message filled the dreams inside my head.  
At seven in the morning it announced a brilliant day,  
I pressed the little snooze control and sent the song away.  
It came back nine minutes later with its optimism whole,  
I answered with a pillow and a less ambitious goal.  
The hook returned at eighteen past; I knew its opening line,  
The singer sounded happy, but the happiness wasn't mine.  

[Chorus]
Rise and shine, start anew,  
The singer has a perfect day that's waiting here for you.  
Rise and shine, touch the sky,  
My arm emerges from the quilt to send the chorus by.  
Bright beat, fresh start, hear the morning say,  
The first eight bars have made this the most repetitive day.  

[Verse 2]
At half past seven I got up and brewed a little tea,  
The alarm sang from the bedroom though the bed was quite empty.  
I'd pressed the snooze once more instead of switching it off clean,  
The singer had no knowledge of my kettle or routine.  
I walked back in and stopped it with a firm and final touch,  
The room grew beautifully quiet; I had heard that song too much.  
I changed tomorrow's wake-up sound to one plain chiming bell,  
The singer's hopeful morning could belong to someone else.  

[Chorus]
Rise and shine, start anew,  
The singer has a perfect day that's waiting here for you.  
Rise and shine, touch the sky,  
My arm emerges from the quilt to send the chorus by.  
Bright beat, fresh start, hear the morning say,  
The first eight bars have made this the most repetitive day.  

[Bridge]
That evening at a concert I could hear the song in full,  
The singer reached a second verse I'd never heard at all.  
He said the day had disappointments and the sky could turn to gray,  
I wished he'd started there instead of shouting rise all day.  

[Final Chorus]
Rise and shine, start anew,  
The singer has a second verse that finally sounds true.  
Bright beat, fresh start, let the chorus fly,  
I stand among the audience without a quilt nearby.  
Rise and shine, touch the sky,  
He calls again for encore; I reach for snooze this time.
