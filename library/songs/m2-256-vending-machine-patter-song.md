# The Vending Machine Patter Song

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Broadway patter songs, with fast syllables, escalating lists, and a final rhyming flourish*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Deliver verses at a nimble theater pace; slow only when the vending machine refuses to choose a snack.*

[Verse 1]
A capuchin approached the machine to purchase something small,  
The button labels danced around the panel on the wall.  
The macaque clerk explained the rows: "The crackers sit at five,  
The nuts at seven, gum at eight, and juice is four-oh-five."  
He tapped the cracker button; it displayed "selection made,"  
Then showed the crackers hanging where no hand could reach the shade.  
He tapped again; the machine began to hum and blink,  
The clerk recited every code before he had a chance to think.  

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, press a key,  
B7 brings a snack to me.  
Drop the coin, watch the tray,  
One more button, now it's on its way.  
Pick it quick, keep the pace,  
The vending machine is running this place.  

[Verse 2]
The clerk listed every option, sorted by their size,  
The buyer asked for water, then the panel changed its mind.  
The screen read "OUT OF ORDER," then "WELCOME, PLEASE SELECT,"  
The coin slot took a token and returned a lost receipt.  
The clerk pressed the service code; the door began to buzz,  
A bag of pretzels dropped as if the machine had heard the fuss.  
The buyer caught the packet, the clerk bowed with a spin,  
Then the machine said "THANK YOU" and asked them both to log in.  

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, press a key,  
B7 brings a snack to me.  
Drop the coin, watch the tray,  
One more button, now it's on its way.  
Pick it quick, keep the pace,  
The vending machine is running this place.  

[Bridge]
They read the printed manual; it listed every jam,  
The clerk kept up the patter while the buyer ate the ham.  
The final line said "pull the lever if the screen is dim,"  
They pulled it; three more snacks came tumbling out for him.  

[Final Chorus]
Pick it quick, press a key,  
B7 brought the whole machine to me.  
Drop the coin, watch the tray,  
The pretzels and the manual got away.  
Pick it quick, take a bow,  
The clerk is still explaining how.  

[Ending]
The machine kept the coin; the clerk kept the receipt.
