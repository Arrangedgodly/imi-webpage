# Sleep Mode, Little Joystick

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soothing lullaby convention applied to a noisy arcade cabinet that refuses to close its game*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Keep a soft rocking pulse; let the machine answer in tiny electronic chirps.*

[Verse 1]
The arcade lights were dimming; the night guard yawned at two,
A macaque named Jo tucked a blanket round the queue.
One cabinet kept on blinking, though the last player was gone;
It played its little victory tune and would not power down.

[Chorus]
Sleep now, bright machine, let your pixel eyelids close,
Dream of quiet mazes where no bonus carrot grows.
Sleep now, bright machine, the night shift's almost through,
I'll hum a low soft lullaby and count the beeps with you.

[Verse 2]
Jo pressed the sleep-mode button; the cabinet said, "Play!"
A tiny tune looped gently, then demanded one more day.
The guard brought out a pillow; the screen displayed a star;
The star began to snore in time, not very quietly by far.

[Chorus]
Sleep now, bright machine, let your pixel eyelids close,
Dream of quiet mazes where no bonus carrot grows.
Sleep now, bright machine, the night shift's almost through,
I'll hum a low soft lullaby and count the beeps with you.

[Bridge]
Jo unplugged the cabinet; it hummed from stored-up cheer.
The guard put on ear muffs; the star blinked once, then disappeared.
A final little chime came from the prize slot down below;
Jo tucked a token in its tray and whispered, "There you go."

[Final Chorus]
Sleep now, bright machine, your pixel eyelids close,
Dream of quiet mazes where the carrot never glows.
Sleep now, bright machine, the night shift's almost through,
I'll leave one token by your screen in case you dream anew.

[Ending]
The cabinet slept. Jo woke when the victory music stopped.
