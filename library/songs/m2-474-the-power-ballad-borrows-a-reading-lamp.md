# The Power Ballad Borrows a Reading Lamp

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: arena power ballads and singers demanding a dramatic beam in venues equipped for a much smaller task*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Slow piano rising into loud guitars; an adjustable library lamp supplies the supposed arena spotlight.*

[Verse 1]
The hall had room for thirty and a stage the size of bed,  
I told the sound technician I required a beam ahead.  
The opening needed darkness with my lonely face in white,  
He pointed at the ceiling's ordinary reading light.  
The librarian found a lamp beside the borrowing desk,  
She clamped it to the music stand and said she'd done her best.  
I leaned into its little pool and sang of endless skies,  
The light showed every printed word and both my tired eyes.  

[Chorus]
Light me up against the night,  
Let the tiny lamp show all my sorrow in the light.  
Lift me high above the rain,  
The clamp must stay on this one stand to hold my mighty pain.  
Hear the heart that will not fall,  
The darkness has a switch beside the catalogue wall.  

[Verse 2]
I reached the rising chorus and I lifted both my hands,  
My face left the little beam; the light stayed on the stand.  
I leaned back down to sing the next long, devastating cry,  
The librarian adjusted it a little toward the sky.  
The crowd could see my face again and all the words beneath,  
One reader mouthed the final line while smiling through her teeth.  
I tried a walk across the stage; the lamp could not come too,  
I finished at the music stand where all my notes were due.  

[Chorus]
Light me up against the night,  
Let the tiny lamp show all my sorrow in the light.  
Lift me high above the rain,  
The clamp must stay on this one stand to hold my mighty pain.  
Hear the heart that will not fall,  
The darkness has a switch beside the catalogue wall.  

[Bridge]
The crowd applauded warmly and the librarian took the clamp,  
A reader at the desk had been awaiting his own lamp.  
I gave it back and thanked her for the beam upon my face,  
The reader opened chapter two within the little space.  

[Final Chorus]
Light me up against the night,  
The reader gets his chapter in the very same white light.  
Hear the heart that will not fall,  
He asks me to sing quietly beside the catalogue wall.  
Lift me high above the rain,  
I write my tragic encore on the form to book again.
