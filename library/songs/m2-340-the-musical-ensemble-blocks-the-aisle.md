# The Musical Ensemble Blocks the Aisle

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: supermarket musical numbers in which shoppers become a choreographed ensemble*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Broadway brass and tap shoes; a squeaking shopping cart supplies the opening motif.*

[Verse 1]
I came to buy a loaf of bread, a carton and some peas,  
The baker caught my squeaking wheel and started singing these.  
The cashier kicked her heels together, stacked the till receipts,  
The shelf boy danced with oranges along the narrow streets.  
A chorus formed beside the milk and swung the freezer wide,  
My carton sat behind six singers stepping side to side.  

[Chorus]
There's a place for everybody in the wonderful store,  
Everybody has a place, but please don't block the door.  
Give a little, take a little, reach across the line,  
The milk is right behind your arm and I would like what's mine.  
Sing the highest price in town,  
Could the chorus put the grocery baskets down?  

[Verse 2]
The butcher sang a tender note about his family trade,  
The baker built a staircase from the loaves he'd freshly made.  
I tried to wheel between the dancers on the final count,  
They lifted up my shopping cart and held it like a mount.  
My peas went flying into baskets all around the floor,  
The shelf boy made a tap routine to carry them once more.  

[Chorus]
There's a place for everybody in the wonderful store,  
Everybody has a place, but please don't block the door.  
Give a little, take a little, reach across the line,  
The milk is right behind your arm and I would like what's mine.  
Sing the highest price in town,  
Could the chorus put the grocery baskets down?  

[Bridge]
The manager came running with the closing bell in hand,  
The dancers lowered me at last and lined up as a band.  
I took my bread and milk and peas to pay and leave the scene,  
The cashier sang the total while the chorus formed a screen.  

[Final Chorus]
There's a place for everybody in the wonderful store,  
Everybody has a place, but please don't block the door.  
Give a little, take a little, here's my card to pay,  
The curtain is a freezer blind; please roll it out the way.  
Sing the highest price in town,  
The encore is my receipt with every item written down.
