# Last Dance at the Laundry

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: glittery disco grooves and glamorous dance-floor drama*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
The laundromat disco ball was made of foil.  
It spun above the baskets and the boil.  
Miss Dottie wore a jumpsuit made of sheets.  
The dryers kept a steady four-on-beats.  
I shook my quarters like a maraca line.  
The lint trap flashed a warning sign.  
The owner dimmed the lights at half past nine.  
We danced between the bleach and detergent shine.  

## Chorus
Spin, baby, spin, let the cotton fly.  
Turn that hamper till the socks run dry.  
One more groove before the rinse cycle ends.  
The night is young, the machine is our friend.  

## Verse 2
A sequined stranger stepped into machine four.  
They found a tiny sock on the floor.  
"It's mine," I said, and struck a pose.  
They handed it back with a clothespin rose.  
The manager arrived with soap in hand.  
He tried to stop us, then joined the band.  
We spun him round beside the change machine.  
His apron flashed the brightest we had seen.  

## Chorus
Spin, baby, spin, let the cotton fly.  
Turn that hamper till the socks run dry.  
One more groove before the rinse cycle ends.  
The night is young, the machine is our friend.  

## Bridge
Then someone yelled, "The washer's full of foam!"  
The suds rolled out and tried to find a home.  
We made a conga line around the flood.  
The drummer tapped a mop against the stud.  
The lights came up; our shoes all squeaked.  
The dance floor looked like powdered milk.  
We grabbed the towels and formed a chain.  
The foam retreated down the drain.  

## Final chorus
Spin, baby, spin, let the towels fly.  
Turn that hamper till the floor runs dry.  
One more groove, then fold with your friends.  
The night is young till the quarters end.  
Spin, baby, spin, with a lint-free heart.  
Pair every sock before we part.  
The owner keeps the foil ball up high.  
Next Friday we will make it fly.  

## Ending
The last load tumbled, soft and warm.  
We carried home our rescued uniform.  
The dryer clicked, the streetlights shone.  
Miss Dottie danced the whole way home.  

*Performance note: Give the bass a rubbery bounce; the crowd choreography should use laundry baskets as props.*
