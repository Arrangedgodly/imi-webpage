# The Yodeling Champion Meets the Echo Judge

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: competitive alpine yodeling and judging panels that mistake echoes for extra contestants*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Bright accordion and buoyant yodel breaks; a second voice behind the room repeats only the final syllables.*

[Verse 1]
The contest faced a mountain with the judges in a row,  
I cleared my throat and raised my hat and let the high notes go.  
The valley answered every phrase a second later on,  
The chairman made another entry: "Echo, carry on."  
He asked the unseen singer to come forward for a score,  
The mountain gave his last two words and offered nothing more.  
I pointed to the rocky wall that made the sound come back,  
He said a hidden partner needed membership and a plaque.  

[Refrain]
Yo-de-lay, let the far hills say,  
They heard the entry once and sang the ending anyway.  
Yo-de-lo, hear the numbers grow,  
The mountain has a rival now and nowhere left to go.  
A silver cup for singing and a second for the stone,  
I came to make a solo but the valley won't leave me alone.  

[Verse 2]
The chairman called a test round with the other singers mute,  
I gave a little yodel; back it came, precisely cute.  
He docked the unseen singer points for copying my phrase,  
The valley copied "copying" and earned a second raise.  
He shouted that the rules were clear and copies weren't allowed,  
The mountain gave that sentence to a very happy crowd.  
The other entrants turned around and faced the rocky rise,  
They all began to yodel and the judge closed both his eyes.  

[Refrain]
Yo-de-lay, let the far hills say,  
They heard the entry once and sang the ending anyway.  
Yo-de-lo, hear the numbers grow,  
The mountain has a rival now and nowhere left to go.  
A silver cup for singing and a second for the stone,  
I came to make a solo but the valley won't leave me alone.  

[Bridge]
The chairman moved the finals to the school behind the trees,  
The plaster walls gave nothing back except a gentle breeze.  
I won the solo trophy with a clean, untroubled sound,  
The judge announced my name and heard no voice the second round.  

[Final Refrain]
Yo-de-lay, let the far hills say,  
The judge has sent the mountain its own invitation day.  
Yo-de-lo, hear the numbers grow,  
It can't attend the banquet so we'll take the meal to go.  
A silver cup for singing and a second for the stone,  
I ate my dinner quietly; the chairman ate his own.
