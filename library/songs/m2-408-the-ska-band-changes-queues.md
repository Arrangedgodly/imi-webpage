# The Ska Band Changes Queues

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: buoyant ska dance anthems with restless movement, performed by shoppers who cannot choose a checkout line*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Choppy offbeat guitar, lively horns and a bouncing chorus; each queue change gets a short brass flourish.*

[Verse 1]
We took the band to buy some bread before the soundcheck call,  
The bassist chose the shortest line beside the freezer wall.  
The drummer saw the second queue was moving rather fast,  
We shuffled with our shopping toward the end of it at last.  
The first line lost three customers while ours began to stall,  
A price check on some peaches brought a silence over all.  
The trumpet player pointed to a newly opened till,  
We danced across the aisle again; it closed for changing bills.  

[Chorus]
Step aside, shuffle through,  
There's a quicker-looking queue beside the one we're moving to.  
Pick it up, set it down,  
We have carried one small loaf around the whole half of the town.  
Step aside, let it swing,  
The shopper who stayed still has bought a basketful of things.  

[Verse 2]
The guitarist chose a checkout with a single little cart,  
The owner bought twelve gift cards and requested them apart.  
The trombone found a register with nobody in sight,  
Its light was off; the cashier had gone home for the night.  
The bassist put our bread down in the first queue once again,  
The drummer kept his feet still; we had reached a tired refrain.  
A shopper who had seen us make our circuit of the room,  
Said, "Every time you move away, the old line finishes soon."  

[Chorus]
Step aside, shuffle through,  
There's a quicker-looking queue beside the one we're moving to.  
Pick it up, set it down,  
We have carried one small loaf around the whole half of the town.  
Step aside, let it swing,  
The shopper who stayed still has bought a basketful of things.  

[Bridge]
We kept our place for seven minutes, paid and took the loaf,  
The cashier hummed our little tune and asked if we were local.  
We told her where the gig would be; she bought a ticket there,  
That night she took the shortest line and reached the front with care.  

[Final Chorus]
Step aside, shuffle through,  
The cashier gets the front row and the band is coming due.  
Pick it up, set it down,  
We have packed the bread backstage and put the restless feet to sound.  
Step aside, let it swing,  
The queue at our own merchandise is still the slowest thing.
