# The Chamber Choir Sings the Wrong Menu

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: solemn chamber-choir performances whose impressive diction conceals the wrong text*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Four-part concert harmony; treat every item on the café menu as an ancient sacred inscription.*

[Verse 1]
The choir took their places in the candle-colored hall,  
The conductor bowed to silence and the audience gave their all.  
He raised his hands above the score, the singers took a breath,  
The first line praised a toasted bun with butter underneath.  
The alto checked her folder and the bass checked page thirteen,  
The words were from the café where the choir had lately been.  

[Chorus]
O toasted bun, O golden crust,  
In butter and in crumbs we trust.  
O soup of day, O tea of night,  
Let every sandwich rise into the light.  
Sing clear, sing true, pronounce the thyme,  
The special comes with salad for a very modest dime.  

[Verse 2]
The conductor saw their worried looks and urged them to persist,  
The tenor sang the pudding with a very tender twist.  
The bass described the breakfast deal in dignified low tones,  
The soprano found a high note in the phrase about the scones.  
The audience leaned forward, moved by every little blend,  
The café cook stood at the back and wished the song would end.  

[Chorus]
O toasted bun, O golden crust,  
In butter and in crumbs we trust.  
O soup of day, O tea of night,  
Let every sandwich rise into the light.  
Sing clear, sing true, pronounce the thyme,  
The special comes with salad for a very modest dime.  

[Bridge]
The cook came to the lectern with the program we'd mislaid,  
The chorus title sat beneath the cafe's lunch parade.  
The choir turned the paper over, found the sacred score,  
Then sang the menu's specials as an encore once more.  

[Final Chorus]
O toasted bun, O golden crust,  
In butter and in crumbs we trust.  
O soup of day, O tea of night,  
Let every sandwich rise into the light.  
Sing clear, sing true, pronounce the thyme,  
The café serves an encore and the choir joins the line.
