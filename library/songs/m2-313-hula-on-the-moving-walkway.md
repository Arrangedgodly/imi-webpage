# Hula on the Moving Walkway

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: airport announcements that compete with gentle travel music and turn every delay into a scenic invitation*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Gentle ukulele-like rhythm and hula sway; the walkway keeps moving while the singer's calm narration ignores the gate change.*

**Verse 1**
A little band plays island tunes beside the airport lane.  
The moving walkway glides along beneath the roof of rain.  
A gibbon sings of palm trees while the travelers roll their bags.  
The speaker says, "Your gate has changed," and lifts a dozen flags.  
The dancers sway toward Gate 4; the sign says Gate 8.  
The singer says, "Take your time," the boarding call says, "Wait."  

**Chorus**
Sway with the belt, let the luggage flow;  
The plane is at Gate 8, but the song says go slow.  
Step on, step off, the signs all rearrange;  
The walkway keeps on gliding through the airport gate change.  

**Verse 2**
A traveler follows ukulele toward a paper palm.  
He passes through the same shop twice; the singer stays quite calm.  
The band repeats its welcome line; the gate display goes black.  
A porter turns the sign around and sends the crowd back.  
The hula dancers drift in place, their hands in ocean arcs.  
The airport cart rolls past them, carrying all the marks.  

**Chorus**
Sway with the belt, let the luggage flow;  
The plane is at Gate 8, but the song says go slow.  
Step on, step off, the signs all rearrange;  
The walkway keeps on gliding through the airport gate change.  

**Bridge**
The band stops playing; everyone can hear the call.  
The gate is now at 4; the palm is just a wall.  
They step off the moving belt and turn their bags around.  
The singer gives one final chord, then leaves without a sound.  

**Ending**
The travelers board; the walkway carries on alone.  
The band packs up its ukulele and heads for home.  
A paper palm leans by the gate, its leaves point toward the sky.  
The speaker calls one last change; the paper palm stands by.
