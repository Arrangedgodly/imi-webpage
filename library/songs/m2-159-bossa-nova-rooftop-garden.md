# Bossa Nova in the Rooftop Garden

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bossa nova's cool understatement, soft syncopation, and breezy lyrics about effortless leisure*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Quiet guitar, brushed percussion, and airy vocals; the sprinkler keeps politely interrupting the groove.*

Verse 1
A tamarin brought a hammock to the rooftop garden rail,
He strummed a lazy bossa while the basil leaves set sail.
The sprinklers clicked on gently; he pretended not to see,
A droplet tapped his shoulder in a calm syncopated key.

Chorus
Softly now, let the green leaves sway,
Water comes to visit anyway.
Sip your lime and lean back slow,
Let the little garden show.

Verse 2
The singer sang of easy days with no appointment planned,
The water rose around his chair and dampened both his hands.
A lemur on the balcony called, "Your hammock's taking rain!"
He answered, "That is atmosphere," and strummed the chord again.

Chorus
Softly now, let the green leaves sway,
Water comes to visit anyway.
Sip your lime and lean back slow,
Let the little garden show.

Bridge
The gardener arrived in boots and turned the valve to off,
The singer sang a tender note; the pipe began to cough.
His hammock swung above the pots, the herbs below grew wet,
The tune stayed cool while every plant acquired a private set.

Verse 3
He floated over parsley in a hammock full of rain,
The lemur sold umbrellas from a folding garden stand.
The singer's quiet record hit the rooftop number one,
The sleeve came with a packet marked "Grow your own bossa sun."

Chorus
Softly now, let the green leaves sway,
Water comes to visit anyway.
Sip your lime and lean back slow,
Let the little garden show.

Ending
The sprinkler stayed off; the hammock toured the neighborhood by crane,
The singer wrote a sequel called "A Slightly Damp Remain."
