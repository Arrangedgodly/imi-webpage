# The Shoegaze Band Swaps Its Shoes

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: shoegaze stage habits, densely obscured vocals, and musicians who pay more attention to pedals than to what they are wearing*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Dense guitar wash, restrained drums and near-whispered vocals; the band notices footwear only in the quiet break.*

[Verse 1]
We stared down at the pedalboards beneath the smoky light,  
The guitars made a cloudy wall that filled the room that night.  
I'd changed into my stage shoes in the narrow dressing room,  
My bandmate changed beside me while the drummer checked the tune.  
I looked down at a bright red shoe instead of my own black,  
My bandmate had one black shoe with a very different back.  
We'd taken half each other's pair while hurrying through the hall,  
The colored pedals held our eyes; we hadn't looked at all.  

[Refrain]
Look down, drift away,  
The red shoe has a different part to play.  
Look down, hear the rain,  
One heel is far too loose within the distant guitar pain.  
Clouds above the pedalboard, shadows round the feet,  
The shoes have got a duet that the band did not complete.  

[Verse 2]
We let the last long chord decay until the quiet break,  
The drummer told the audience the pause we had to take.  
We sat upon the stage edge with the guitars put aside,  
We took off the wrong shoes and traded them to either side.  
I wore two black shoes once again; she wore her red pair right,  
The drummer tapped the count to bring us back into the light.  
We stared down at the pedalboards and played the next long wave,  
The audience watched the shoes more closely than the sounds we gave.  

[Refrain]
Look down, drift away,  
The red shoe has a different part to play.  
Look down, hear the rain,  
One heel is far too loose within the distant guitar pain.  
Clouds above the pedalboard, shadows round the feet,  
The shoes have got a duet that the band did not complete.  

[Bridge]
We packed the shoes in separate bags before we left the stage,  
The drummer put the shoe break on the set list's second page.  
The next crowd asked about it when the quiet section came,  
We showed them both our matching pairs beneath the colored frame.  

[Final Refrain]
Look down, drift away,  
The shoes now have the same part to play.  
Clouds above the pedalboard, shadows round the feet,  
The drummer buys mismatching socks to keep the break complete.  
Look down, hear the rain,  
Our first official merchandise is laces with the band's name.
