# The Bossa Nova Sunbed Follows the Shade

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: breezy bossa nova songs about effortless leisure, performed during a surprisingly laborious search for a shady deck chair*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Nylon-string guitar and light percussion; sing calmly while chair legs scrape in an increasingly busy counter-rhythm.*

[Verse 1]
I placed my chair beneath a palm beside the sparkling pool,  
The morning had a gentle breeze; the tiles were bright and cool.  
I opened up a paperback and poured a lime-touched drink,  
The shade slipped from my shoulder while I barely had a blink.  
I moved the chair a little left and settled with a sigh,  
The sun climbed slightly higher and the shadow wandered by.  
The lifeguard watched my furniture creep slowly round the tree,  
I said that I was resting; she looked skeptical of me.  

[Chorus]
Soft sun, slow song, let the afternoon be,  
There is shade beside the palm but it keeps moving away from me.  
Cool glass, calm breeze, easy little day,  
I have dragged my chair a hundred feet to keep the heat away.  
Soft sun, slow song, settle in the blue,  
The chair has seen the whole pool while my book stays on page two.  

[Verse 2]
I followed round the palm until the chair blocked half a stair,  
The lifeguard pointed to the roofed veranda over there.  
I said the palm looked lovely in the picture I had planned,  
She said the roof stayed where it was; the shade was on demand.  
I made one final circle for a photo by the trunk,  
My drink sloshed on the tiles; my camera nearly sunk.  
The towel caught it safely; I picked it up with care,  
Then wheeled the chair beneath the roof and found a place to spare.  

[Chorus]
Soft sun, slow song, let the afternoon be,  
There is shade beside the palm but it keeps moving away from me.  
Cool glass, calm breeze, easy little day,  
I have dragged my chair a hundred feet to keep the heat away.  
Soft sun, slow song, settle in the blue,  
The chair has seen the whole pool while my book stays on page two.  

[Bridge]
I took the perfect photograph from underneath the roof,  
The palm stood in the distance like a little leafy proof.  
My friends admired the peaceful day and asked me where I stayed,  
I sent them all a map that showed the route I'd used for shade.  

[Final Chorus]
Soft sun, slow song, let the afternoon be,  
The roof has been more faithful than that restless little tree.  
Cool glass, calm breeze, easy little day,  
The lifeguard mopped the citrus splash I tracked along the way.  
Soft sun, slow song, listen to the view,  
Tomorrow I shall finish page three underneath the roof.
