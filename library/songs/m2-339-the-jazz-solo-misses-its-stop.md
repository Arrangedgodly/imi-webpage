# The Jazz Solo Misses Its Stop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: extended jazz solos that ignore every signal to finish*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Swing trio with a saxophone solo; the drummer replaces fill patterns with the rhythm of a bus engine.*

[Verse 1]
Our saxophonist caught a phrase and chased it through the night,  
The pianist read the timetable beneath a yellow light.  
The final bus was half past ten, the club was near the stop,  
The drummer pointed to his watch and played a gentle chop.  
The saxophone went climbing like a vine around the moon,  
Our bassist packed his instrument and whistled half the tune.  

[Chorus]
Take it home, take it down, take the corner to the street,  
Put that golden melody beneath your traveling feet.  
Turn the final phrase around and bring it to a close,  
The bus driver knows the schedule, but he doesn't know those notes.  
One more bar, one more bend,  
That's a lovely place to end, my friend.  

[Verse 2]
The pianist closed the piano lid; the saxophone went on,  
The cleaner swept beneath his shoes; the seated crowd was gone.  
He found a little higher note and held it like a bird,  
The drummer rattled bus keys but the solo never heard.  
The bus arrived outside the door, its lights were glowing green,  
Our bassist made a low trombone of sounds he'd never seen.  

[Chorus]
Take it home, take it down, take the corner to the street,  
Put that golden melody beneath your traveling feet.  
Turn the final phrase around and bring it to a close,  
The bus driver knows the schedule, but he doesn't know those notes.  
One more bar, one more bend,  
That's a lovely place to end, my friend.  

[Bridge]
The saxophonist walked outside still playing through his grin,  
The drummer paid the driver and he waved the solo in.  
The doors closed on the fading chord; the player took a seat,  
The engine supplied a bass line and he found a brand-new beat.  

[Final Chorus]
Take it home, take it down, take the corner to the street,  
Put that golden melody beneath your traveling feet.  
Turn the final phrase around and bring it to a close,  
The bus driver hears the solo as he passes where it goes.  
One more bar, one more bend,  
He rides beyond his house and starts the turnaround again.
