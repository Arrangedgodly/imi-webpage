# Merengue Around the Fountain

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: merengue's brisk two-step pulse, repeating brass riffs, and a dancer who treats every interruption as another turn*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Bright brass and fast two-step percussion; add a water splash at the end of every phrase.*

Verse 1
The capuchin stepped in the plaza where the fountain met the sun,
The band struck up a merengue and the dancers joined as one.
A lemur spun her shawl around and drifted near the spray,
A drop fell on the trumpet, but the riff went on to play.

Chorus
Paso, paso, turn around,
Keep the rhythm, mind the ground.
Paso, paso, bright and clear,
Dance the fountain closer here.

Verse 2
The fountain's water pulsed in time, a jet rose on the beat,
The dancers stepped in quick two-counts and skipped around the street.
A macaque tried to close the valve; the handle spun too fast,
The band kept up the tempo as the water hit the brass.

Chorus
Paso, paso, turn around,
Keep the rhythm, mind the ground.
Paso, paso, bright and clear,
Dance the fountain closer here.

Bridge
They reached to catch the runaway spray with buckets from the stand,
The mayor joined the dance and grabbed the nearest hand.
They braced the valve and closed it once; the water settled down,
The band's last riff sent drops of silver dancing round the town.

Verse 3
The dancers formed a circle and gave the fountain space,
The band played one more chorus with a slower, careful pace.
The plaza dried by noon; the music carried through,
The mayor danced a merengue with a mop and bucket too.

Chorus
Paso, paso, turn around,
Keep the rhythm, mind the ground.
Paso, paso, bright and clear,
Dance the fountain closer here.

Ending
The mayor bowed and held his mop beneath the noonday sun,
The band demanded one more splash; he said the dance was done.

Spoken tag
The fountain is fixed. The mop has a solo booking outside the gate.
