# Foam on the Windshield

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: power ballads, soaring key changes, dramatic vocal belts, and a car-wash brush that keeps taking the spotlight*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Build from a soft verse to an oversized final chorus; let the car-wash alarm cut off the belt.*

[Verse 1]
A lemur drove into the wash with dust upon the hood,
The radio played one slow song that promised something good.
He held the wheel like destiny and watched the bubbles fall,
A giant brush came sweeping in and covered up it all.

[Chorus]
Foam on the windshield, light in the sky,
Hold to the feeling as the water rolls by.
Foam on the windshield, hands on the wheel,
This is the moment when the chrome starts to heal.

[Verse 2]
The singer raised a note so high the soap began to sway,
The side brush spun in harmony and pushed the car away.
A capuchin waved from the next lane, holding one wet shoe,
The wash machine changed its cycle and began the chorus too.

[Chorus]
Foam on the windshield, light in the sky,
Hold to the feeling as the water rolls by.
Foam on the windshield, hands on the wheel,
This is the moment when the chrome starts to heal.

[Bridge]
The rinse came down like rain; the singer held the note,
The dryer roared a counterline and shook the soap afloat.
The final key change rose; the wash alarm cut in,
The car door opened slowly and the chorus met the wind.

[Final Chorus]
Foam on the windshield, light in the sky,
Hold to the feeling as the water rolls by.
Foam on the windshield, hands on the wheel,
The car is clean now; the brush gets the deal.

[Ending]
The singer drove away; the brush kept spinning still.
