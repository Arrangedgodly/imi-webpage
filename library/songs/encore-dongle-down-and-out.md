# Dongle Down and Out

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Blues songs about obsolete technology and workplace dependence*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Slow electric blues, a tired harmonica, and a single cable nobody can identify.*

[Verse 1]  
Woke up this morning, my monitor was black.  
The meeting starts at nine, but the picture won't come back.  
I got a little dongle with a plug from years ago.  
It fits one ancient laptop that we don't allow to go.  

[Chorus]  
Dongle down and out, lost behind the drawer.  
You were here last Tuesday, now you ain't here no more.  
I got the blues, got the blues, and a slide deck on the floor.  
Dongle down and out, I can't present no more.  

[Verse 2]  
I asked the tech desk monkey, "Can you find that tiny friend?"  
He opened up a cabinet that appeared to have no end.  
There lay a gray adapter, with a label from before.  
It said, "For projector B," though B was gone in twenty-four.  

[Chorus]  
Dongle down and out, lost behind the drawer.  
You were here last Tuesday, now you ain't here no more.  
I got the blues, got the blues, and a slide deck on the floor.  
Dongle down and out, I can't present no more.  

[Verse 3]  
The monkey plugged it sideways. The screen began to glow.  
My slides appeared in portrait, then they started moving slow.  
The title page said "Strategy." The last page said "Goodbye."  
The dongle gave one little blink and let out one long sigh.  

[Bridge]  
The office bought a new cord with a code and an app.  
It needs a login, update, and a firmware nap.  
I found my old adapter in a box of surplus cords.  
It plugged right in and saved the day. No passwords or rewards.  

[Final Chorus]  
Dongle back in town, plugged into the wall.  
You survived the cabinet, the labels, and it all.  
I got the blues, got the blues, but the screen is shining bright.  
Dongle down and out no more. We're working through the night.  

[Outro]  
I wrap it in a napkin, put it in my bag.  
I sign its loan-out ledger. It finally gets a tag.
