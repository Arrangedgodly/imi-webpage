# Fugue Through the Revolving Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Baroque fugue entrances, counterpoint, and a quartet that keeps entering through the wrong door*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Four independent voice entries on the same subject; stage the revolving door as a repeated bass ostinato.*

Subject
Turn through the door, then return once more,
Find the room, find the room.

Verse 1
The soprano entered singing through the revolving door,
The tenor followed after, then came back once more.
The bass began the subject from the lobby on the beat,
The alto reached the hallway but returned to meet the feet.

Chorus
Turn through the door, then return once more,
Find the room, find the room.
Round and round, four voices sound,
Find the room, find the room.

Verse 2
The usher held the door and pointed down the hall,
The quartet kept returning, so he pointed through them all.
A gibbon took the subject in a lower, steady tone,
The revolving door replied and sang the bass alone.

Chorus
Turn through the door, then return once more,
Find the room, find the room.
Round and round, four voices sound,
Find the room, find the room.

Bridge
The usher stopped the doorway with a wedge beneath the frame,
The singers reached the concert hall but could not place its name.
They checked the printed program, then each took a separate seat,
The fugue resolved in harmony; the usher kept the beat.

Verse 3
The singers kept revolving when the concert had begun,
The quartet sang the subject as the audience joined the fun.
They rose to make their exit, each in proper order now,
The usher lifted up the wedge and took a bow.

Chorus
Turn through the door, then return once more,
Find the room, find the room.
Round and round, four voices sound,
Find the room, find the room.

Ending
The quartet left together through the regular door,
The usher joined the subject and returned once more.
