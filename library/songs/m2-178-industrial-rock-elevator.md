# Industrial Rock in the Elevator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: industrial rock sampling, mechanical percussion, and a band that turns an elevator's routine noises into its entire anthem*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Distorted guitar over sampled motor hum, door chime, and button clicks; keep the elevator safe and stationary during setup.*

Verse 1
The band set up inside the freight lift, cables on the floor,
A capuchin struck a wrench and found a heavy roar.
The gibbon sampled door chimes; the macaque hit the wall,
The elevator answered with a hum beneath them all.

Chorus
Clank, hum, rise and fall,
Hit the button, hear the call.
Clank, hum, ride the sound,
Doors stay open, band stays ground.

Verse 2
The motor made a rhythm as the lift began to move,
The band was still inside it, trying to keep the groove.
The stagehand stopped the elevator, asked them all to clear,
They said, "We're making music," then the floor came near.

Chorus
Clank, hum, rise and fall,
Hit the button, hear the call.
Clank, hum, ride the sound,
Doors stay open, band stays ground.

Bridge
The technician unplugged the lift and showed the proper panel,
The band sampled every button like a mechanical channel.
The safety light was blinking; the drummer missed the beat,
They packed their gear and moved the show to an empty street.

Verse 3
They played the motor's echo from a speaker by the door,
The elevator stayed still and carried cargo as before.
The building's tenants danced along and pushed the call-button twice,
The band made one more record from the humming device.

Chorus
Clank, hum, rise and fall,
Hit the button, hear the call.
Clank, hum, ride the sound,
Doors stay open, band stays ground.

Ending
The band unplugged the sampler when the lift came into view,
The elevator made its chime and took the stairs' old cue.
