# The String Quartet Reserves a Quiet Carriage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: genteel chamber music touring culture and performers who expect silence everywhere except around their own instruments*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Plucked strings under polite, clipped singing; gradually replace rehearsal sound with the train's rhythmic clatter.*

[Verse 1]
We booked four seats together for a journey through the hills,  
The first violin packed sandwiches, the cellist packed the bills.  
The carriage said QUIET in a neat and solemn sign,  
We praised the train's acoustics and unpacked a little line.  
The viola drew a gentle bow across the open string,  
The lady in the next seat looked up sharply at the ring.  
Our leader said, "Rehearsing, just a little passage here,"  
She pointed to the quiet sign and placed her book quite near.  

[Chorus]
Hush, hush, listen to the train,  
The window has a rhythm and the wheels have a refrain.  
Soft, soft, do not draw the bow,  
The carriage has an audience that doesn't want a show.  
Hush, hush, keep the music small,  
We have paid for transportation, not a concert hall.  
Four parts, four seats, quiet as can be,  
The cellist has a sandwich in the place of middle C.  

[Verse 2]
We put away the instruments and hummed beneath our breath,  
The lady closed her book again; the leader grew quite red.  
We tried to mime the fingering with scores upon our knees,  
The violist turned a page too loud; we froze in perfect peace.  
The train began a climbing run; the wheels gave out a beat,  
The cellist counted silently and tapped inside his sleeve.  
We heard a distant coupling knock, a whistle near the bend,  
Our leader wrote a passage called The Passenger's Revenge.  

[Chorus]
Hush, hush, listen to the train,  
The window has a rhythm and the wheels have a refrain.  
Soft, soft, do not draw the bow,  
The carriage has an audience that doesn't want a show.  
Hush, hush, keep the music small,  
We have paid for transportation, not a concert hall.  
Four parts, four seats, quiet as can be,  
The cellist has a sandwich in the place of middle C.  

[Bridge]
We played the piece that evening in the venue by the square,  
The lady from the carriage had a front-row ticket there.  
She heard the train inside the strings and gave a little cheer,  
Our leader bowed politely: "You commissioned this, my dear."  

[Final Chorus]
Hush, hush, listen to the train,  
The lady wants an encore of the carriage's refrain.  
Soft, soft, now we draw the bow,  
She has paid to hear us here and gladly hears the show.  
Four parts, four seats, quite a pleasant day,  
We book the quiet carriage for the journey home again.  
Hush, hush, no rehearsal spree,  
Our next commissioned masterpiece is Sandwiches in D.
