# Musical Saw at the Foggy Pier

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical-saw virtuosity and the expectation that every tune must become an eerie solo*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: The saw sings in wavering glissandi; the harbor crew keeps trying to use its notes as practical signals.*

**Verse 1**
At Foggy Pier, old Nella bends a saw against her knee.  
She draws the bow; a ghostly wail drifts out across the sea.  
The gulls all freeze, the harbor master drops his paper chart.  
A sleepy tugboat answers with a mournful little start.  
Nella means to tune a hymn, but bends the note too far.  
The dockhand hears a foghorn and goes hunting for a star.  

**Chorus**
Wheee, says the saw, and the mist replies,  
A gull puts on its woolen disguise.  
Wheee, says the bow, and the boats turn round;  
One eerie note has moved the whole sound.  

**Verse 2**
The harbor master waves his flag: "A ferry needs a lane."  
The saw bends up a rising note; the ferry backs again.  
Nella tries a cheerful scale, a little dancing tune.  
The tugboat thinks it's calling home and ties itself to June.  
A seal applauds with flippers while the passengers all sway.  
The dockhand sells the fog a ticket for the matinee.  

**Chorus**
Wheee, says the saw, and the mist replies,  
A gull puts on its woolen disguise.  
Wheee, says the bow, and the boats turn round;  
One eerie note has moved the whole sound.  

**Bridge**
The master asks for one short beep, one signal, nothing more.  
Nella bends the blade a hair; the note rolls down the shore.  
The ferry hears the single pitch and finds the channel light.  
The seal conducts the gulls away; the tugboat docks all right.  

**Ending**
She packs the saw; the fog lifts up; the pier resumes its trade.  
A gull still sings the final note, though no one taught the bird.  
Nella bows; the harbor claps, the ropes are coiled and dry.  
The foghorn answers from the bay with one last ghostly sigh.
