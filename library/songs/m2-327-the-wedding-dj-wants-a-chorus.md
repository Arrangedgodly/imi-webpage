# The Wedding DJ Wants a Chorus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: wedding dance singles built around commands that interrupt an actual wedding ceremony*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Bright disco bass and call-and-response commands; the officiant answers over the rhythm.*

[Verse 1]
The couple reached the altar by the lilies in the hall,  
The DJ took the silence as an invitation call.  
He played his latest single with a whistle and a clap,  
The groom began his wedding vow and lost it in the rap.  
The bride looked at the booth and raised one sternly painted brow,  
He thought that meant the left-side guests should all stand up right now.  
The officiant requested one small pause before the ring,  
The DJ found his pause effect and made the whole room sing.  

[Chorus]
Slide to the left if you cherish the bride,  
Slide to the right with your uncle beside.  
Clap for the future, stomp for the past,  
Hold up the ring, but don't throw it too fast.  
Everybody turn and everybody say,  
We would like the vows to happen sometime today.  

[Verse 2]
The ring-bearing capuchin was waiting by the aisle,  
The DJ told him forward, then reverse it with a smile.  
He danced back to the kitchen with the pillow on his head,  
The chef returned him gently, singing all the words instead.  
The bride unplugged the speaker and the hall at last was still,  
The DJ sang the chorus with considerable skill.  
The groom removed his headphones and placed flowers in his hands,  
He couldn't clap the next command; he stood beside the stands.  

[Chorus]
Slide to the left if you cherish the bride,  
Slide to the right with your uncle beside.  
Clap for the future, stomp for the past,  
Hold up the ring, but don't throw it too fast.  
Everybody turn and everybody say,  
We would like the vows to happen sometime today.  

[Bridge]
The couple said their promises, the rings were safely worn,  
The guests applauded once as if a quieter world were born.  
The bride restored the speaker lead and pointed to the floor,  
The DJ held the flowers up and looked around for more.  
He tossed them to the ceiling; all the dancers cleared the way,  
They landed in his open arms; the bride announced bouquet.  

[Final Chorus]
Slide to the left if you cherish the bride,  
Slide to the right with your uncle beside.  
Clap for the future, stomp for the past,  
Now the ring is on a finger and the vows are done at last.  
Everybody turn and everybody say,  
Our DJ caught his own bouquet; he booked himself for May.
