# The Acoustic Confession Needs a Shorter Cable

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: intimate singer-songwriter performances that insist on spontaneity while depending on elaborate equipment*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Fingerpicked guitar and whispered vocals; pause for the cable being coiled across the wooden floor.*

[Verse 1]
I sang about simplicity beside a single light,  
My guitar needed nothing but the silence of the night.  
The audience sat close enough to hear my little breath,  
A fifty-meter cable made a nest beneath my feet.  
I said we'd share a moment with no distance in between,  
The stagehand crossed the front row with a blinking little screen.  

[Chorus]
Just my heart and just this room,  
Just the strings beneath the moon.  
Nothing here between us but the little song I play,  
And the cable that the stagehand has to move out of the way.  
Just my heart and just your ear,  
Could the monitor be closer so I know that you can hear?  

[Verse 2]
I told a tender story about leaving things behind,  
The stagehand brought a second mic to catch my state of mind.  
I asked for less equipment while I pointed to the mixer,  
Then asked him for more reverb and a slightly warmer filter.  
The cable looped around my stool and tied it to the stand,  
I tried to lean toward someone and the stool denied the plan.  

[Chorus]
Just my heart and just this room,  
Just the strings beneath the moon.  
Nothing here between us but the little song I play,  
And the cable that the stagehand has to move out of the way.  
Just my heart and just your ear,  
Could the monitor be closer so I know that you can hear?  

[Bridge]
The stagehand offered one short lead and put the rest away,  
I sat among the listeners and began again to play.  
The room was full of wooden sound; no monitor remained,  
I closed my eyes and sang the verse I'd nearly lost beneath the cable chain.  

[Final Chorus]
Just my heart and just this room,  
Just the strings beneath the moon.  
Nothing here between us but the little song I play,  
Could we try a wireless pack? This short lead makes me stay.  
Just my heart and just your ear,  
I ask for freedom from the lead; the stagehand hides a tear.
