# Captain Peel and the Missing Map

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: adventure-musical show tunes and nautical bravado*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
I am Captain Peel, the fearless chart reader.  
I know every shoal from the bay to the bar.  
I once sailed twelve feet in a pickle jar.  
The map was in my pocket at breakfast, I swear.  
Now the pocket has a hole and some hair.  
The crew wants direction, the compass wants lunch.  
We're drifting in circles around one small bunch.  
Our macaque lookout grips the rope with his tail.  
He reports one cloud shaped like a whale.  

## Chorus
Point me north, point me true.  
I've got brave things left to do.  
Raise the flag and set the sail.  
I can captain with no trail.  

## Verse 2
We found a clue inside a biscuit tin.  
The first mate drew an arrow in gravy.  
The cook called it modern, the crew called it wavy.  
I marched to the bow with my spyglass held high.  
I saw my own hat reflected in the sky.  
The cabin boy spotted a gull with a string.  
It wore my lost map like a napkin ring.  
Our lemur cook began to beat his chest.  
Then drummed the tin to guide us west.  

## Chorus
Point me north, point me true.  
I've got brave things left to do.  
Raise the flag and set the sail.  
I can captain with no trail.  

## Bridge
The gull dropped the chart in our soup.  
The ink ran south, the coastline ran west.  
The crew all agreed the soup looked best.  
I tasted the broth and found an island.  
The cook marked the spot with a spoon in hand.  
We steered for the island at half a knot.  
It was a sandbar, but we liked the spot.  

## Final chorus
Point me north, point me true.  
There's warm soup waiting for the crew.  
Raise the flag and set the sail.  
We can find our way by tale.  
Point me home, point me slow.  
Our map has soup stains where we go.  
The gull can keep the ring we gave.  
We'll draw a chart for every wave.  

## Ending
The cook kept watch beside the pot.  
The crew went home with salt and bread.  
I wore the map upon my head.  

*Performance note: Use a bold, brassy lead and a crew that answers every boast with a practical correction.*
