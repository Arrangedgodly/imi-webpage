# The Duet Separates the Stereo Channels

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic stereo duets and singers whose great declarations appear in opposite ears of a broken headphone set*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Two voices alternate hard left and right until the sound engineer makes the practical mono mix.*

[Voice A]
I called your name across the sea; did you hear my faithful cry?  
[Voice B]
I heard a little breathing and a gull go drifting by.  
[Voice A]
I sang the whole first verse for you beneath the evening star.  
[Voice B]
My left ear hears no singer; it can only hear guitar.  

[Verse]
We made a tender duet with a voice in either ear,  
The engineer arranged the parts so love could travel clear.  
The listener's old headphones had a broken left-hand wire,  
She heard one half of longing with the other half entire.  
She called the local radio to ask about the track,  
The host played it in stereo and heard the missing half come back.  
The listener held the working ear against her quiet face,  
The host agreed that half a love song left a very empty place.  

[Refrain, alternating]
A: Answer me, answer me, let the longing fly.  
B: I am singing right beside you; can you hear my small reply?  
A: Answer me, answer me, let the night be kind.  
B: The working ear has kept the parts my channel had assigned.  
Both: Two hearts, one song, let the voices meet,  
The headphones have a broken wire beneath the little seat.  

[Verse]
The engineer made a mono mix with both our voices there,  
The host sent it to the listener to test beside her chair.  
She heard the opening question and the answer come behind,  
The gull still drifted through the track; the words were now combined.  
She thanked the host for finding all the phrases she had missed,  
Her partner bought new headphones and they put them on a list.  
We kept the wide version for two working earphones, clear,  
The mono mix held both our hearts without the missing years.  

[Refrain, together]
Answer me, answer me, let the longing fly,  
I am singing right beside you; you can hear my small reply.  
Answer me, answer me, let the night be kind,  
The working ear has every part the two of us assigned.  
Two hearts, one song, let the voices meet,  
The listener has new headphones; the gull is still complete.  

[Coda]
We sent the engineer a card with two names in one line,  
The radio plays the mono mix at half past eight and nine.  
The listener knows each singer and can follow every phrase,  
Her partner takes one earbud for the gull on windy days.
