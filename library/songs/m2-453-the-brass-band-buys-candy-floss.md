# The Brass Band Buys Candy Floss

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jubilant brass-band fairground marches and musicians who buy a snack requiring the same mouths as their instruments*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Bright brass march and cheerful group vocals; individual instruments drop out whenever their players take another sweet bite.*

[Verse 1]
We played beside the carousel upon the village green,  
The trumpet led a bright tune through the bustle of the scene.  
The candy seller gave the band a pink and fluffy gift,  
The players took their paper sticks and gave their mouths a lift.  
The trumpeter ate a little bite as his next entrance came,  
The tuba player did the same and left the bass quite plain.  
The drummer kept the march alive while half the band chewed through,  
The captain raised his baton; the horns had dropped out too.  

[Chorus]
March, brass, march, keep the little tune,  
The sugar has a solo that has silenced half the room.  
Bright bells, pink clouds, let the fairground sway,  
The candy needs the same mouths that the horns would like to play.  
March, brass, march, bring the bass along,  
The drummer is the whole band till the candy floss is gone.  

[Verse 2]
The captain stopped between the songs and pointed to the stand,  
He said the sweets could wait until the final number planned.  
We set the candy safely on a row of empty chairs,  
The drummer played the opening and the horns rejoined the air.  
The children danced in circles while the tuba kept the ground,  
The trumpet held its bright high part and never missed a sound.  
The candy seller waved at us and kept her stall quite near,  
We bowed at the conclusion and could finally eat right here.  

[Chorus]
March, brass, march, keep the little tune,  
The sugar has a solo that has silenced half the room.  
Bright bells, pink clouds, let the fairground sway,  
The candy needs the same mouths that the horns would like to play.  
March, brass, march, bring the bass along,  
The drummer is the whole band till the candy floss is gone.  

[Bridge]
The tuba player finished first, then lifted up his horn,  
He tried to play a fanfare with a pink floss cloud adorning.  
The drummer kept the march alive with taps against his stand,  
The captain laughed and called the tune the sweetest in the band.  

[Final Chorus]
March, brass, march, now the tune is done,  
The little fairground orchestra can eat its pink cloud sun.  
Bright bells, pink clouds, let the fairground sway,  
The captain writes "Snacks after" on tomorrow's music page.  
March, brass, march, bring the bass along,  
The candy seller buys a drum and plays our waiting song.
