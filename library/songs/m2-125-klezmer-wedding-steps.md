# Klezmer on the Wedding Steps

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: klezmer wedding-band conventions, clarinet swoops, and the wedding party whose dance circle keeps moving the cake table*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Clarinet lead, fiddle, and bouncing tuba; build from a stately entrance to a joyful dance while keeping the cake level.*

Verse 1
The wedding party gathered on the steps beneath the vine,
The clarinet began to climb, the fiddle took the line.
A baboon groom led the dance; his partner made a turn,
The cake table started rolling as the clarinet did burn.

Chorus
Dance the circle, mind the tray,
Keep the wedding cake in place today.
Clarinet can swoop and soar,
Feet should stay away from frosting on the floor.

Verse 2
The guests formed one wide circle; the tuba made a bounce,
The table rolled beside them with a soft and careful ounce.
A gibbon caught the corner, a macaque held the stand,
The clarinet flew upward while the cake remained unmanned.

Chorus
Dance the circle, mind the tray,
Keep the wedding cake in place today.
Clarinet can swoop and soar,
Feet should stay away from frosting on the floor.

Bridge
The band slowed down the rhythm and the dancers took a seat,
They locked the table's little wheels and tapped a steadier beat.
The couple cut the cake at last; the crowd began to cheer,
The clarinet played one high note that reached the branches near.

Verse 3
The bride and groom resumed the dance, the table held its ground,
The guests made room around the cake and filled the yard with sound.
The frosting stayed unmarked; the plates went hand to hand,
The wedding party danced until the moonlight crossed the land.

Chorus
Dance the circle, mind the tray,
Keep the wedding cake in place today.
Clarinet can swoop and soar,
Feet should stay away from frosting on the floor.

Ending
The band packed up its instruments; the cake was nearly gone,
The table stayed exactly where the vows were carried on.
