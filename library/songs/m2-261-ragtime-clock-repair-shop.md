# Ragtime at the Clock Repair Shop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ragtime piano's jaunty syncopation and repeated left-hand stride*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Keep a lively two-beat bass and clipped right-hand syncopation; let the clocks steadily disagree with the pianist.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque brought a pocket watch to Mister Gibbon's shop,  
Its hands were stuck at seven, but its ticking would not stop.  
The piano bounced a ragtime against the ticking spring,  
The clockmaker pried the back off; every gear began to sing.  
A wall clock struck at nine o'clock, the mantel struck at ten,  
The cuckoo bird popped out and tried to join the band again.  
The pianist held a cheerful grin, though time was running wild,  
The clockmaker said, "Your watch is fast." The pianist only smiled.  

[Chorus]
Tick-tock, ragtime, keep the seconds light,  
The hands are dancing left and right.  
Wind the spring and play the tune,  
The shop will close sometime this afternoon.  
Tick-tock, ragtime, don't ask when,  
The clocks will tell us different times again.  

[Verse 2]
A capuchin came in to fix a kitchen timer's bell,  
It rang before the pasta boiled and rang again as well.  
The drummer kept a steady beat; the timer skipped a bar,  
The cuckoo bird came out once more and landed on the jar.  
The clockmaker tuned the mainspring; the piano played a run,  
The pocket watch began to tick, then stopped when it was done.  
He wound it one more quarter-turn; the hands began to race,  
It claimed tomorrow morning on its tiny golden face.  

[Chorus]
Tick-tock, ragtime, keep the seconds light,  
The hands are dancing left and right.  
Wind the spring and play the tune,  
The shop will close sometime this afternoon.  
Tick-tock, ragtime, don't ask when,  
The clocks will tell us different times again.  

[Bridge]
They set each clock to noon and let the bells all ring,  
The pianist played a tag that made the cuckoo sing.  
The watch kept perfect time for one whole minute more,  
Then sounded like an alarm and rolled behind the door.  
He fetched it with a broom and set its hands at half past two,  
The pianist checked his wristwatch: "Mine says that time too."  

[Final Chorus]
Tick-tock, ragtime, time to say goodbye,  
The watch is fixed and the clocks comply.  
Wind the spring and play the tune,  
The shop will close precisely after noon.  
Tick-tock, ragtime, let the hands align,  
The cuckoo's still a minute behind.  

[Ending]
The pianist stopped on time; the shop clocks disagreed.
