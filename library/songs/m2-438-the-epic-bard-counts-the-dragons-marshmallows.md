# The Epic Bard Counts the Dragon's Marshmallows

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: epic fantasy folk ballads that promise a mighty dragon battle and receive a petty campsite dispute instead*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Solemn lute, hand drum and a heroic narrative voice; the dragon answers in an embarrassingly small sung aside.*

[Verse 1]
I followed brave Sir Rowan to a mountain black with smoke,  
The dragon at its summit wore a splendid copper cloak.  
I tuned my lute for battle and prepared a solemn verse,  
The knight held up a skewer and began to state his curse.  
"You ate the whole marshmallow bag I brought for camping here,"  
The dragon said, "I left you two. They sit beside your gear."  
The knight said two was hardly fair for all our camping meals,  
My mighty song of conquest grew quite difficult to feel.  

[Chorus]
Rise, bright blade, face the fire,  
The knight would like the dragon to replace his sweets entire.  
Sound, low lute, tell the ancient tale,  
The treasure has a sugar coat and came in a little bag for sale.  
Rise, bright blade, claim the mountain's crown,  
The dragon's shopping budget keeps the mighty battle down.  

[Verse 2]
The dragon opened up a chest of coins beside its bed,  
And asked how many packets would replace what it had fed.  
The knight took two small silver coins and rode toward the town,  
We went along behind him with the unused weapons down.  
He bought four bags of marshmallows and returned before the dark,  
The dragon lit our little fire with one quite modest spark.  
We shared the sweets in portions and I sang about the pact,  
The knight said keep the shopping trip but leave the prices out of that.  

[Chorus]
Rise, bright blade, face the fire,  
The knight would like the dragon to replace his sweets entire.  
Sound, low lute, tell the ancient tale,  
The treasure has a sugar coat and came in a little bag for sale.  
Rise, bright blade, claim the mountain's crown,  
The dragon's shopping budget keeps the mighty battle down.  

[Bridge]
The castle heard my ballad and requested tales of gore,  
I said the dragon shared dessert; the king demanded more.  
I gave him every detail of the knight's long shopping queue,  
The king fell asleep before the change; the queen bought marshmallows too.  

[Final Chorus]
Rise, bright blade, face the fire,  
The kitchen has a skewer fit for every courtly squire.  
Sound, low lute, tell the ancient tale,  
The dragon has a standing order at the little sweet shop's sale.  
Rise, bright blade, leave the mountain's crown,  
The kingdom runs on sugar now; its swords are all set down.
