# The Lullaby Parent Falls Asleep First

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tender bedtime lullabies whose soothing promises work on the exhausted performer before the intended listener*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Soft fingerpicked guitar and hushed vocals; the alert child's practical questions gradually take over the melody.*

[Verse 1]
I tucked the little blanket round the pillow and the bear,  
Then sat beside the bedside with a song prepared to share.  
I sang about the moonlight and the boats upon the sea,  
The child asked why a sailor wouldn't simply go to sleep.  
I said the sailor watched the stars to find a way to steer,  
The child requested every star that could be seen from here.  
I sang their names quite slowly, though I only knew a few,  
My eyelids met each other while the child supplied the moon.  

[Chorus]
Sleep, little one, drift on the tide,  
The bear will keep you company with moonlight at your side.  
Rest, little one, quiet and deep,  
I have nearly reached the ending but my voice has gone to sleep.  
Soft, little room, hold the night,  
The child is asking questions and the parent lost the light.  
Sleep, little one, close the day,  
There is somebody beside the bed who cannot stay awake.  

[Verse 2]
The child tapped my shoulder and I woke to start again,  
I sang about a sleepy fox asleep outside its den.  
The child asked where its blanket was and who had brought it tea,  
I said the fox liked leaves; the child said leaves were cold to me.  
I tried a quiet humming part with no new facts inside,  
The child began conducting with the bear held very high.  
My humming turned to breathing and my head began to lean,  
The child gave the bear my chair and sat upright to sing.  

[Chorus]
Sleep, little one, drift on the tide,  
The bear will keep you company with moonlight at your side.  
Rest, little one, quiet and deep,  
I have nearly reached the ending but my voice has gone to sleep.  
Soft, little room, hold the night,  
The child is asking questions and the parent lost the light.  
Sleep, little one, close the day,  
There is somebody beside the bed who cannot stay awake.  

[Bridge]
The other parent found us with my head against the wall,  
The child said, "I have settled them. They hardly fussed at all."  
I woke and took my pillow to the bedroom down the hall,  
The child requested silence now; my snoring shook the wall.  

[Final Chorus]
Sleep, tired one, drift on the tide,  
The bear has heard the whole thing from the quiet bedside.  
Rest, tired one, quiet and deep,  
The child has found the rhythm and is finally going to sleep.  
Soft, little room, hold the night,  
Tomorrow I will try to sing before the fading light.  
Sleep, tired one, close the day,  
The lullaby has two performers; neither gets much pay.
