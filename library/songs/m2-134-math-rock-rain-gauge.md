# Math Rock at the Rain Gauge

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: math-rock time signatures, shifting instrumental patterns, and a rain gauge reader who insists the storm must be counted in odd numbers*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Angular guitars change meter between phrases; keep the rain-gauge clicks in an uneven pattern.*

Verse 1
The band played seven beats, then five, then seven once again,
A macaque read the rain gauge from the window by the den.
The guitarist changed the measure; the drummer moved the stand,
The rain tapped out a different count across the garden land.

Chorus
Count the drops in five and seven,
Let the crooked beat keep stepping.
Turn the measure, shift the line,
Rain can play in odd-time time.

Verse 2
The gibbon wrote down inches, then converted them to beats,
The measure changed at every bar; the bass skipped all the seats.
A capuchin held the gauge up high and called out "Three!"
The band played four more measures and disagreed on key.

Chorus
Count the drops in five and seven,
Let the crooked beat keep stepping.
Turn the measure, shift the line,
Rain can play in odd-time time.

Bridge
The storm passed over; gauges filled across the yard,
The band kept changing meters till the counting got too hard.
They stopped to check the water line and wrote the total down,
The rhythm held its pattern while the rain moved out of town.

Verse 3
They played the final figure in a steady four-four bar,
The rain gauge showed a normal depth, not far above the mark.
The drummer closed the count book and the guitarist let it ring,
The macaque read the rainfall without counting every string.

Chorus
Count the drops in five and seven,
Let the crooked beat keep stepping.
Turn the measure, shift the line,
Rain can play in odd-time time.

Ending
The storm moved on, the chart was clear, the band packed up its gear,
The gauge stayed upright while the odd beat disappeared.
