# The Pirate Ballad Finds a Gift Voucher

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: swashbuckling pirate ballads whose buried treasure promises boundless wealth and delivers a restricted retail offer*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Stamping sea rhythm, fiddle and a rough narrative voice; the first mate reads voucher restrictions with increasing disbelief.*

[Verse 1]
We followed Captain Brindle to an island on the chart,  
The treasure lay beneath a tree with roots shaped like a heart.  
We dug through sand till evening and uncovered one brass chest,  
The captain raised a mighty cheer and put the spade to rest.  
He opened up the lid to find a little printed card,  
It promised twenty percent off at the harbor's sailcloth yard.  
The first mate read the smaller lines and lowered his proud head,  
"A minimum purchase applies, and discounted stock is exempt."  

[Chorus]
Gold, gold, bring the treasure high,  
The captain has a coupon with a date that's drawing nigh.  
Sail, sail, leave the island free,  
The fortune has a minimum spend much greater than we dreamed.  
Gold, gold, let the old chest shine,  
Its contents have a shopping list and one restrictive line.  

[Verse 2]
The captain said our canvas needed mending at the bow,  
The voucher might be useful if we bought some sailcloth now.  
We sailed into the harbor and we took it to the yard,  
The merchant checked the date and said we'd barely made the card.  
We bought the cloth we needed and received the promised price,  
The first mate counted savings; it was enough for lunch twice.  
The captain put the voucher in the chest to save its fame,  
The merchant put a PAID stamp on it with our vessel's name.  

[Chorus]
Gold, gold, bring the treasure high,  
The captain has a coupon with a date that's drawing nigh.  
Sail, sail, leave the island free,  
The fortune has a minimum spend much greater than we dreamed.  
Gold, gold, let the old chest shine,  
Its contents have a shopping list and one restrictive line.  

[Bridge]
We stitched the sail and ate our lunch upon the harbor wall,  
The chest now held our sandwiches and worked quite well for all.  
The captain drew a new chart with a little printed key,  
"Treasure may be practical. No guarantees of jewels at sea."  

[Final Chorus]
Gold, gold, bring the treasure high,  
The sail is holding steady and the coupon's time has passed by.  
Sail, sail, leave the harbor free,  
The chest protects the sandwiches that travel now with me.  
Gold, gold, let the old chest shine,  
The first mate buries the receipt; the captain makes a new sign.
