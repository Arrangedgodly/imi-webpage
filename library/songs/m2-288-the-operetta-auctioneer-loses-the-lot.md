# The Operetta Auctioneer Loses the Lot

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operetta patter, auction-room virtuosity, and sung dialogue that outruns the objects being sold*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Bright patter verse over a lilting waltz; auctioneer and bidders interrupt one another in precise, comic counterpoint.*

**Verse 1**
At the estate sale, Miri takes the rostrum in a hat.  
She sings, "A lamp, a chair, a jar, a rug, a cricket bat."  
The bidders chirp in triplets; the clerk records each bid.  
A macaque raises both his hands; the clerk asks what he did.  
He meant to fix his collar, but the hammer starts to fall.  
Miri sings a faster number to outpace them all.  

**Chorus**
Bid-a-dee, bid-a-dah, the lot is nearly gone;  
Bid-a-dee, bid-a-dah, the clerk has written wrong.  
The lamp goes left, the jar goes right, the chair begins to spin;  
The auctioneer keeps singing while the buyers shuffle in.  

**Verse 2**
A little velvet box appears; the label says "one key."  
The chorus sings in counterpoint: "Belongs to lot number three."  
The clerk says, "Lot three's a cupboard," the bidder says, "It's mine."  
Miri's voice leaps up a scale to keep the bids in line.  
The key fits in the cupboard lock, but opens up the door.  
Inside sits every item that they just sold before.  

**Chorus**
Bid-a-dee, bid-a-dah, the lot is nearly gone;  
Bid-a-dee, bid-a-dah, the clerk has written wrong.  
The lamp goes left, the jar goes right, the chair begins to spin;  
The auctioneer keeps singing while the buyers shuffle in.  

**Bridge**
The clerk checks every numbered tag; the cupboard holds the stock.  
Miri stops the waltz and finds the key inside her sock.  
She sings, "The sale is postponed until we sort the room."  
The bidders hum the refrain and clear the auction room.  

**Ending**
The lamp is sold, the chair is back, the jar is on the floor.  
Miri sings the final lot: "One key, one open door."  
The clerk puts down the hammer; the buyers file away.  
The cupboard stays unlocked until the end of day.
