# The Finale Chorus Has to Catch a Ferry

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: all-cast musical finales and performers who want to prolong the curtain call after the last transport has begun boarding*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Full ensemble harmony with piano; a dock bell replaces the last flourish as cast members walk off in singing pairs.*

[Verse 1]
We played a summer musical beside the island pier,  
The audience stood cheering at the ending of the year.  
We bowed and sang a reprise of the promises we'd made,  
The ferry horn came over us before the notes could fade.  
The captain had a timetable and passengers to take,  
The stage manager pointed at the boat across the lake.  
We took our final curtain call and sang another chord,  
The dock attendant rang a bell to say we ought to board.  

[Chorus]
One more song, one more bow,  
The ferry's taking passengers and wants to take us now.  
One more song, hold the light,  
The last trip leaves the island before anyone says goodnight.  
Hands together, voices high,  
Our harmonies need passage to the mainland by and by.  

[Verse 2]
The stage crew shut the scenery and left it stored behind,  
The cast collected costumes and the bags that they could find.  
The manager led two singers down the walkway to the boat,  
We sang the final verse while carrying each ordinary coat.  
The captain counted everyone and shut the boarding gate,  
Our pianist was aboard with us, the keyboard in its crate.  
We sat upon the ferry as the island moved away,  
The chorus took a quieter form to end the summer play.  

[Chorus]
One more song, one more bow,  
The ferry's taking passengers and wants to take us now.  
One more song, hold the light,  
The last trip leaves the island before anyone says goodnight.  
Hands together, voices high,  
Our harmonies need passage to the mainland by and by.  

[Bridge]
The captain asked for silence when he called the mainland pier,  
We held the long finale note inside where none could hear.  
The boat reached its destination and we walked across the ramp,  
The manager checked the head count underneath a harbor lamp.  

[Final Chorus]
One last song, one last bow,  
The captain has delivered every member safely now.  
Hands together, voices high,  
Tomorrow we collect the set and give the island our goodbye.  
One last song, let the ending stay,  
The captain heads for mooring; we sing from land as he sails away.
