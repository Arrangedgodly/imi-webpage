# The Salsa Band Gets a Rotating Table

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: restaurant salsa nights whose spinning meal service becomes part of the choreography*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Piano montuno, congas and call-and-response vocals; let the serving carousel squeak on the offbeat.*

[Verse 1]
We played beside a dining table big enough for eight,  
The waiter spun its center tray to bring around each plate.  
The singer saw the turning and he called it perfect time,  
The pianist made a circling phrase to match the steady line.  
The diners reached for salsa as the trumpet climbed above,  
The singer thought they called for dancing and began to sing of love.  

[Chorus]
Turn it round, bring it near,  
Let the little pepper bowl appear.  
Step outside and step within,  
The dinner keeps on circling while the congas make it spin.  
Turn it round, make it stay,  
We would like to eat before you send the plates away.  

[Verse 2]
The singer led a dancer round the table with a flourish,  
The waiter tried to serve the rice; we danced around his flourish.  
The trumpet blew a short bright phrase, the center tray sped faster,  
A diner caught the pepper bowl before a small disaster.  
The pianist slowed the rhythm so the hungry hands could choose,  
The singer called it tension and refused to lose his blues.  

[Chorus]
Turn it round, bring it near,  
Let the little pepper bowl appear.  
Step outside and step within,  
The dinner keeps on circling while the congas make it spin.  
Turn it round, make it stay,  
We would like to eat before you send the plates away.  

[Bridge]
The waiter stopped the center tray and served from either side,  
The singer paused his footwork and put all his pride aside.  
The diners finished eating, pushed their chairs beside the wall,  
Then took the dance floor with the band and answered every call.  

[Final Chorus]
Turn it round, bring it near,  
Let the little pepper bowl appear.  
Step outside and step within,  
The dancers keep on circling while the congas make them spin.  
Turn it round, make it stay,  
The singer eats his dinner while the dancers lead the way.
