# Washboard Waltz at the Lifeguard Tower

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jug-band washboard rhythms and beach bands that turn every clatter into a solo*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Swinging jug-band feel; the washboard answers every safety announcement with a competing rhythm.*

**Verse 1**
Benny sets the lifeguard flag beside the rolling sand.  
He taps his washboard twice to test the shoreline band.  
The bucket plays a bass note; spoons go rattling bright.  
A crab begins to shuffle when the tide rolls out of sight.  
The whistle sounds for swimmers; Benny scrapes a snare reply.  
The swimmers form a conga line and wade in shoulder-high.  

**Chorus**
Scritch-a-scratch, keep the tide in time,  
Jug goes bloop and the spoons all chime.  
Flag up high means stay on the sand;  
The washboard keeps conducting the whole beach band.  

**Verse 2**
A child has lost a sandal; it is drifting past the pier.  
Benny plays a rescue riff; the crab says, "Not from here."  
He points the flag toward the shore, but every dancer spins.  
The drummer scrapes a warning while the chorus shouts, "Begin!"  
The washboard's wooden frame gets sand in every groove.  
Benny shakes it clean; the beach begins to move.  

**Chorus**
Scritch-a-scratch, keep the tide in time,  
Jug goes bloop and the spoons all chime.  
Flag up high means stay on the sand;  
The washboard keeps conducting the whole beach band.  

**Bridge**
He sets the board down, blows one note, and raises the red flag.  
The band stops mid-scrape; the crab retrieves the bag.  
The sandal lands beside the tower, dry and neatly placed.  
Benny taps a gentle rhythm while the swimmers leave with haste.  

**Ending**
The flag comes down; the tide turns out; the beach is clear and wide.  
Benny packs his spoons away and lets the washboard ride.  
The crab keeps one small shell as an honorary drum.  
The next wave claps the final beat; the band is welcome home.
