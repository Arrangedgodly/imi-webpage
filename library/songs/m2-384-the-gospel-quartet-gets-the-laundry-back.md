# The Gospel Quartet Gets the Laundry Back

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jubilant gospel quartet arrangements applied to the smallest possible everyday deliverance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Handclaps, organ, and exuberant call-and-response; sing the lost-sock revelations with increasing conviction.*

[Verse 1]
We brought our robes to wash them at the laundromat next door,  
The bass man held the quarters; I collected socks from four.  
The washer took our garments with a dignified slow turn,  
We watched through its round window till the water ceased to churn.  
The lock released, we opened up, and counted every sleeve,  
The tenor's left sock wasn't there; he found it hard to believe.  
He raised his empty ankle like a question to the sky,  
The alto hit a mighty chord; a customer walked by.  

[Chorus]
Bring it back, bring it back,  
There is something missing from the bottom of the sack.  
Lift your voice, clap your hands,  
We are waiting for a sock beside the detergent stand.  
Bring it back, let it show,  
We have searched the rubber seal and looked behind the hose.  
Sing it high, answer low,  
The tenor cannot lead the walk with only one warm toe.  

[Verse 2]
The owner checked the drum and said the dryer might reveal,  
A sock that rode inside a robe and skipped our counting deal.  
We fed the dryer quarters and we took four little seats,  
The bass man's robe went round and round against the gentle heat.  
The tenor watched the window like a prophet watches clouds,  
The alto sold our next show's date to half the waiting crowd.  
The owner brought her tambourine from underneath the till,  
We gave the spin a chorus as it climbed the windowsill.  

[Chorus]
Bring it back, bring it back,  
There is something missing from the bottom of the sack.  
Lift your voice, clap your hands,  
We are waiting for a sock beside the detergent stand.  
Bring it back, let it show,  
We have searched the rubber seal and looked behind the hose.  
Sing it high, answer low,  
The tenor cannot lead the walk with only one warm toe.  

[Bridge]
The dryer stopped; the bass man shook his robe before the door,  
A warm blue sock slipped from the hood and landed on the floor.  
The tenor wore it on his foot, the owner rang the bell,  
Then held up seven other socks: "Whose stories shall we tell?"  

[Final Chorus]
Bring it back, bring it back,  
There is something found beside the bottom of the sack.  
Lift your voice, clap your hands,  
We are booked next Friday evening by the detergent stand.  
Bring it back, let it show,  
The tenor has his pair again; the owner runs the show.  
Sing it high, answer low,  
Our new supporting choir is the row of dryers below.
