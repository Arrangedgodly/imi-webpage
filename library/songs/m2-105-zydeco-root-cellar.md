# Zydeco at the Root Cellar

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: zydeco dance-band conventions, rubboard rhythm, and a cellar party interrupted by a crate of runaway yams*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Accordion and rubboard drive a brisk two-step; add crate thumps as improvised percussion.*

Verse 1
The root cellar party started when the accordion cried,
A lemur took the rubboard and began to scrape in time.
A crate of yams was stacked beside the doorway by the wall,
The first one rolled across the floor and started up the hall.

Chorus
Scrape and squeeze, stomp and slide,
Keep the yams from taking flight.
Step to the left and swing around,
Bring the crate back underground.

Verse 2
The fiddle chased the yam along; the tuba marked the floor,
The dancers formed a moving fence beside the cellar door.
The capuchin who owned the crate came running down the stair,
He tried to catch one rolling yam and danced it through the air.

Chorus
Scrape and squeeze, stomp and slide,
Keep the yams from taking flight.
Step to the left and swing around,
Bring the crate back underground.

Bridge
They stacked the yams in rhythm, one thump for every beat,
The rubboard scraped a warning as the crate rolled to their feet.
The band played faster, faster, till the last yam took a spin,
It landed in the basket and the dancers let it in.

Verse 3
They served a warm yam supper and invited every guest,
The band played one more two-step as the cellar took a rest.
The crate was tied with sturdy twine and set beside the stair,
The owner danced around it twice to show it he still cared.

Chorus
Scrape and squeeze, stomp and slide,
Keep the yams from taking flight.
Step to the left and swing around,
Bring the crate back underground.

Ending
The accordion shut its bellows; the rubboard went to bed,
One yam rolled out at daybreak and danced beneath the shed.
