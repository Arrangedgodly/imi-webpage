# The Tragic Baritone Buys a Replacement Button

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tragic operatic laments focused on tiny losses that can be remedied at an ordinary haberdashery*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Low operatic voice and dramatic piano; the shop assistant speaks calmly between the lamenting lines.*

[Verse 1]
A button fell from my best coat beside the station square,  
I heard its little tapping and I turned to find it there.  
It rolled beneath a rubbish bin and vanished out of sight,  
I sang about a broken world beneath the morning light.  
My coat hung open at the point where trust had come undone,  
A shop assistant heard me from the doorway in the sun.  
She asked if I would like to buy a matching button there,  
I told her this one carried all the history I could bear.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Gone, little button, gone from my heart,  
The fabric holds a little thread where we were pulled apart.  
Gone, little button, lost to the ground,  
The shop assistant has a tray with many more around.  
One stitch, two tears, let the broken coat remain,  
The replacement has four holes and might lessen all my pain.  

[Verse 2]
She matched the color carefully against the coat I wore,  
The size was just the same as all the buttons left before.  
She sold me one spare button and a needle with some thread,  
I sang about the old one while she showed me what she said.  
I sewed the new one through its holes upon the little chair,  
The coat closed at the proper point with all its warmth quite there.  
The lost one's tragic absence had a practical new face,  
I took the coat outside with every button in its place.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Gone, little button, gone from my heart,  
The fabric holds a little thread where we were pulled apart.  
Gone, little button, lost to the ground,  
The shop assistant has a tray with many more around.  
One stitch, two tears, let the broken coat remain,  
The replacement has four holes and might lessen all my pain.  

[Bridge]
The cleaner found my old one when he moved the rubbish bin,  
He left it with the shop assistant, wearing its old grin.  
She rang me at the number on the receipt I'd left that day,  
I came back for my history and put the spare away.  

[Final Aria / Refrain]
Found, little button, found by the broom,  
The replacement keeps its job until another loss finds room.  
One stitch, two tears, let the warm coat meet,  
The old one rides inside my pocket, very near the receipt.  
Found, little button, let the long grief cease,  
I sing a duet with both of them and buy a second piece.
