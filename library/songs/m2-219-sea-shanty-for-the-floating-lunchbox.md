# The Lunchbox Afloat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sea shanty call-and-response, hauling rhythms, and a deck crew that rescues a floating lunchbox with more ceremony than the cargo*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Let the chorus feel like a steady haul; the lunchbox bell should answer the crew.*

[Verse 1]
The macaque crew set sail at dawn to clean the harbor light,
A lunchbox fell from the galley shelf and bobbed into the white.
The captain saw it floating, then he raised his hand to call,
"Lower down the rescue rope; the lunch is worth us all."

[Chorus]
Haul away, haul away, the lunchbox rides the foam,
Haul away, haul away, we'll bring the biscuits home.
Pull together, pull together, the tide can roll and toss,
We saved a box of sandwiches and not a single sauce.

[Verse 2]
The gibbon crewman threw a net; it landed on the rail,
The cook leaned over after it and caught it by the tail.
The box rang like a bell when waves began to beat,
The sailors hauled in rhythm to the lunchbox's repeat.

[Chorus]
Haul away, haul away, the lunchbox rides the foam,
Haul away, haul away, we'll bring the biscuits home.
Pull together, pull together, the tide can roll and toss,
We saved a box of sandwiches and not a single sauce.

[Bridge]
The captain opened up the lid; the crackers stayed dry,
The pickles floated out and formed a little fleet nearby.
They rescued every pickle with a spoon and fishing line,
The crew declared the galley had survived a perfect brine.

[Final Chorus]
Haul away, haul away, the lunchbox rides the foam,
Haul away, haul away, we'll bring the biscuits home.
Pull together, pull together, the harbor light is near,
The crew can eat their lunch now; the sandwiches are here.

[Ending]
They tied the lunchbox to the mast; it rang for every bite.
