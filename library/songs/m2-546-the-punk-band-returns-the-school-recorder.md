# The Punk Band Returns the School Recorder

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: punk reunions and performers whose rebellion still carries unfinished school-equipment obligations*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Fast guitar, rough unison voices and a recorder squeak between phrases.*

[Verse 1]
We played a school reunion with our jackets full of pins,  
We sang about refusing all the systems and their sins.  
The former music teacher stood beside the little stage,  
She recognized the singer from a list upon a page.  
He'd borrowed one school recorder when he was fourteen,  
Its little missing number had remained within the scheme.  
She asked if he could bring it back before the closing bell,  
He said he'd built a solo with the borrowed thing as well.  

[Chorus]
No rules, no ties, let the loud chord roar,  
The teacher wants the recorder that we borrowed long before.  
No rules, no ties, shout the whole thing strong,  
The inventory has a number that outlived our protest song.  
Fast beat, old debt, let the little whistle cry,  
The borrowed recorder is an institution's property nearby.  

[Verse 2]
The singer found the recorder in the van beside the amp,  
He played its tiny solo underneath the school hall lamp.  
The teacher checked the number on its little plastic side,  
The missing school recorder had come back from quite a ride.  
He gave it to her after all the music had been done,  
She marked it as returned and said the solo sounded fun.  
The bassist asked if school rules meant the band had failed its part,  
She said returning borrowed things did not dissolve their art.  

[Chorus]
No rules, no ties, let the loud chord roar,  
The teacher wants the recorder that we borrowed long before.  
No rules, no ties, shout the whole thing strong,  
The inventory has a number that outlived our protest song.  
Fast beat, old debt, let the little whistle cry,  
The borrowed recorder is an institution's property nearby.  

[Bridge]
He bought his own recorder at the shop beside the square,  
The teacher sent the return receipt to thank us for the care.  
He put her on the guest list for the night beside the mill,  
She asked if he could make the next recorder solo less shrill.  

[Final Chorus]
No rules, no ties, let the loud chord roar,  
Our recorder has a receipt and owes the school no more.  
Fast beat, old debt, let the little whistle cry,  
The teacher has a spare one for the next school year nearby.  
No rules, no ties, shout the whole thing strong,  
She gives the solo seven out of ten inside our protest song.
