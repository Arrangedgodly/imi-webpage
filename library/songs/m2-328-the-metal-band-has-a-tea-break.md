# The Metal Band Has a Tea Break

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: apocalyptic heavy metal imagery interrupted by a meticulous backstage tea ritual*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Galloping bass, double-kick drums and a growled verse that becomes delicate at the tea order.*

[Verse 1]
We ride the burning horizon with our banners made of bone,  
The drummer wears a helmet made from someone else's throne.  
Our singer lifts a chain above the trembling front-row seats,  
Then checks the clock beside the amp and calls for something sweet.  
The kettle waits backstage beneath a poster of the end,  
The bassist wants a biscuit and the guitarist wants a blend.  
We lay our axes carefully upon a padded chair,  
The singer takes his demon mask off just to smell the air.  

[Chorus]
Boil, boil, kettle of doom,  
Steam shall rise across the room.  
Milk before the final roar,  
Pass the little sugar bowl once more.  
Let the darkness steep for three,  
No one storms the gates without a cup of tea.  

[Verse 2]
The crowd demands the funeral march of every fallen king,  
The drummer checks his saucer for a little stain or ring.  
Our singer writes a setlist on a napkin by the pot,  
The guitarist reads his future in a cup that's much too hot.  
The stagehand brings the curtain up; our masks are on the tray,  
The audience can see the milk arranged in neat array.  
Our bassist drops an octave as he reaches for the tongs,  
The singer growls to ask where every sugar cube belongs.  

[Chorus]
Boil, boil, kettle of doom,  
Steam shall rise across the room.  
Milk before the final roar,  
Pass the little sugar bowl once more.  
Let the darkness steep for three,  
No one storms the gates without a cup of tea.  

[Bridge]
The guitarist strikes a power chord; his spoon vibrates away,  
It lands upon the drummer's snare and makes a bright display.  
The crowd mistakes the teapot for the skull of some old foe,  
The singer points its spout at them and lets the warm steam go.  

[Final Chorus]
Boil, boil, kettle of doom,  
Steam shall rise across the room.  
Milk before the final roar,  
Pass the little sugar bowl once more.  
Let the darkness steep for three,  
Our second encore is a signed receipt for chamomile tea.
