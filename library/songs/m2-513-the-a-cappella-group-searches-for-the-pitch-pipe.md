# The A Cappella Group Searches for the Pitch Pipe

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: a cappella ensembles proud of musical independence while depending on one small starting device*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Unaccompanied five-part harmony with several hesitant starts; the pitch pipe sounds once before the proper take.*

[Verse 1]
We advertised five voices and no instruments at all,  
We stood beneath the cameras in the studio's little hall.  
The leader took a steady breath and looked inside her coat,  
The pitch pipe wasn't in the pocket where she found the note.  
The bass supplied a starting tone the tenor found too low,  
The alto hummed another one that made the chorus slow.  
We said we could sing anywhere with nothing but our skill,  
The camera waited patiently beside the windowsill.  

[Refrain]
Only our voices, only our hearts,  
The five of us are ready with our separate little parts.  
Only our voices, only the air,  
One small plastic starting thing has gone away somewhere.  
Hum me a beginning, give the tune its name,  
The confident announcement hasn't helped us start the frame.  

[Verse 2]
The soprano looked beneath the scarves beside the dressing chair,  
The pitch pipe lay beneath a hat with all its notes quite there.  
The leader blew the proper pitch and all five singers met,  
We sang a clean first verse with all our little parts reset.  
The camera caught the whole tune with no instrumental sound,  
The leader put the pipe beside the hat when we had bowed.  
The producer kept the opening with the pipe upon the tape,  
She said it made a funny scene before the voices took their shape.  

[Refrain]
Only our voices, only our hearts,  
The five of us are ready with our separate little parts.  
Only our voices, only the air,  
One small plastic starting thing has gone away somewhere.  
Hum me a beginning, give the tune its name,  
The confident announcement hasn't helped us start the frame.  

[Bridge]
We asked her for a version with the starting note removed,  
She said the proper song would have the cut that we'd approved.  
The outtake got a separate clip with all the false starts there,  
Its little pitch pipe gathered more applause than we could share.  

[Final Refrain]
Only our voices, only our hearts,  
The little pipe now has a credit before any singer starts.  
Hum me a beginning, give the tune its name,  
The pipe has got a labeled pouch attached upon the frame.  
Only our voices, only the air,  
The soprano buys a spare one that she leaves beside the chair.
