# Enka at the Last Platform

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: enka's expressive vibrato, dramatic railway farewells, and a singer whose beloved is only changing platforms*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Enka-style bends and sustained vibrato over shamisen and gentle drum; hold each farewell longer than the announcement.*

Verse 1
The lemur watched the platform as the evening train drew near,
A paper ticket trembled in her paw beside a tear.
She sang a long farewell as the whistle crossed the air,
Her friend had moved to platform two, but she was waiting there.

Chorus
O train, roll gently through the rain,
Carry my goodbye again.
O train, do not leave me behind,
The platform change is hard to find.

Verse 2
The station loudspeaker called the track, but she could not hear,
The vibrato climbed above the train and filled the station near.
The gibbon guard approached and waved a yellow flag,
She thought it was a handkerchief and clutched her travel bag.

Chorus
O train, roll gently through the rain,
Carry my goodbye again.
O train, do not leave me behind,
The platform change is hard to find.

Bridge
Her friend came back from platform two and called her by her name,
The singer held the final note; the guard called out the same.
The train doors closed; the friend stood waiting on the stair,
The song had made a small delay but no one had gone anywhere.

Verse 3
They walked across the station with the ticket held between,
The singer sang a softer line beneath the departure screen.
The train rolled out without them; the next one would arrive,
They shared a cup of tea and kept their plans alive.

Chorus
O train, roll gently through the rain,
Carry my goodbye again.
O train, do not leave me behind,
The platform change is hard to find.

Ending
The friend stayed for the tea; the song rose one more time,
The train returned at sunrise, and they boarded on the chime.
