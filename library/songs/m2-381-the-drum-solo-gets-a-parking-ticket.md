# The Drum Solo Gets a Parking Ticket

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: arena rock drum solos that treat every ordinary interruption as a spectacular musical cue*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Huge tom fills, a shouted rock chorus, and a parking warden whose ticket printer supplies the final rhythm.*

[Verse 1]
I wheeled my kit outside the club to give the crowd some air,  
The pavement shook beneath my kick; my cymbals split the square.  
I parked the van beside the drums, its sliding door spread wide,  
And started on a solo with my roadie trapped inside.  
The loading sign said fifteen minutes; I had played for twenty-three,  
A warden tapped my shoulder and I shouted, "Join in, please!"  
She held a little printer like a shaker in her hand,  
I pointed at the snare and cried, "The lady joins the band!"  

[Chorus]
Roll, roll, roll through the night,  
Thunder in the gutter and the parking meter light.  
Crash, crash, let the city hear,  
I have got a solo and a fine for standing here.  
Rock the block, shake the street,  
Everybody's clapping while the warden prints receipts.  
Roll, roll, bring the finish round,  
My expensive little encore makes a very tidy sound.  

[Verse 2]
She told me I could argue at the office on the form,  
I thought she meant a concert hall and booked another storm.  
My roadie waved the parking app; the screen had gone to sleep,  
I'd used his charging cable for a glowing bass-drum beat.  
The crowd demanded one more fill; the warden checked her watch,  
I promised just a short one, then invented forty bars.  
She tucked the ticket underneath the windscreen's little blade,  
My band came out with sandwiches and asked how much we'd made.  

[Chorus]
Roll, roll, roll through the night,  
Thunder in the gutter and the parking meter light.  
Crash, crash, let the city hear,  
I have got a solo and a fine for standing here.  
Rock the block, shake the street,  
Everybody's clapping while the warden prints receipts.  
Roll, roll, bring the finish round,  
My expensive little encore makes a very tidy sound.  

[Bridge]
I stopped the kit, we packed the van, the crowd returned indoors,  
The warden tore her copy off and tapped a little four.  
That printer had a better groove than half my fancy fills,  
I asked her for a guest spot; she said, "First you pay the bill."  

[Final Chorus]
Roll, roll, roll through the night,  
My drummer's pride is bruised beneath the dashboard light.  
Crash, crash, now the van can leave,  
The ticket gets the headline on our next promotional sleeve.  
Rock the block, shake the street,  
The warden never tours but keeps the tightest little beat.  
Roll, roll, bring the finish round,  
I bought a paper rhythm at the highest price in town.
