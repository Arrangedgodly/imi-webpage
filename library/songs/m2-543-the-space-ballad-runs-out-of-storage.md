# The Space Ballad Runs Out of Storage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: electronic space ballads and singers discovering that infinite lyrical horizons still occupy finite disk space*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Slow synth pads, soaring voice and a spoken computer message; the final recording begins after old duplicates are cleared.*

[Verse 1]
I sang of endless galaxies and worlds beyond the sun,  
The recording stopped at halfway with my opening still undone.  
The computer showed a message that its little drive was full,  
The endless worlds were taking up a very finite spool.  
I looked inside the folder named "The Final Cosmic Take,"  
A hundred older versions lay beside the one I'd make.  
The galaxies had numbers from one through ninety-nine,  
Each had its own vast heavens and the same first little line.  

[Chorus]
Far beyond the stars, far beyond the blue,  
The little drive has no room for the endless worlds I knew.  
Far beyond the stars, hear the cosmos call,  
A hundred final versions have occupied it all.  
Wide sky, small disk, let the distant voices roam,  
The old takes need a separate place before the new ones have a home.  

[Verse 2]
I copied all the old takes to an archive drive outside,  
I checked that they played clearly before clearing space inside.  
The computer had room again to hold the current song,  
I sang the endless galaxies without the stop along.  
I kept the finished version in a plainly named small place,  
The archive held the old worlds with their numbers full of space.  
The listener heard the ending that had been delayed before,  
The cosmos had a closing chord and needed nothing more.  

[Chorus]
Far beyond the stars, far beyond the blue,  
The little drive has no room for the endless worlds I knew.  
Far beyond the stars, hear the cosmos call,  
A hundred final versions have occupied it all.  
Wide sky, small disk, let the distant voices roam,  
The old takes need a separate place before the new ones have a home.  

[Bridge]
I named the archive "Old Worlds" with a date upon the side,  
A label held more useful truth than endlessness could hide.  
My friend asked for the first take with its tiny wrong high note,  
I found it by its number in the separate place I wrote.  

[Final Chorus]
Far beyond the stars, hear the cosmos call,  
The little drive has one new song and not the versions all.  
Wide sky, small disk, let the distant voices roam,  
The archive has the old worlds in their own numbered home.  
Far beyond the stars, let the new track play,  
The first wrong note becomes a bonus in the release next day.
