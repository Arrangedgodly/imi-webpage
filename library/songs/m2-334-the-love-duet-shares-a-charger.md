# The Love Duet Shares a Charger

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sentimental pop duets that make a shared phone charger sound like a lifelong romantic commitment*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Two earnest singers trade lines over soft piano; the battery warning supplies a third harmony.*

[Verse 1: Singer A]
I waited by the window with the thunder drawing near,  
Your message said you loved me, but the screen was hard to hear.  
My battery was dying with your portrait on its face,  
I brought the only charger to our favorite little place.  
You came with twenty roses and a phone at one percent,  
I laid the cable on the table, wondering what you meant.  

[Chorus: Both]
One plug, two hearts, one socket in the wall,  
We have to share the current if we want to hear the call.  
Take an hour, give an hour, promise you will stay,  
Don't unplug the future when you have to walk away.  
One plug, two hearts, a cable made for one,  
Our love will last until the charging light is done.  

[Verse 2: Singer B]
I thought that we were equal till you claimed the first half hour,  
You said your travel ticket needed slightly more than power.  
I needed our anniversary photograph to load,  
You needed maps to navigate the winding garden road.  
We held the cable between us like a ribbon at a dance,  
The waiter brought a splitter and invited us to chance.  

[Chorus: Both]
One plug, two hearts, one socket in the wall,  
We have to share the current if we want to hear the call.  
Take an hour, give an hour, promise you will stay,  
Don't unplug the future when you have to walk away.  
One plug, two hearts, a cable made for one,  
Our love will last until the charging light is done.  

[Bridge: Singer A, then Singer B]
The splitter had two branches, neither fitted either phone,  
We laughed and watched the screens go dark, no longer quite alone.  
You drew the garden on a napkin; I remembered where to turn,  
We stayed until the candles had no more of night to burn.  

[Final Chorus: Both]
One plug, two hearts, one socket in the wall,  
We walked away together, never waiting for a call.  
Take an hour, give an hour, promise you will stay,  
We leave the cable where it lies and greet the coming day.  
One plug, two hearts, the waiter waves us back,  
We left our phones beneath the bill beside his sugar stack.
