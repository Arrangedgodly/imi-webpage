# Tango of the Tumble-Dry

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ballroom tango drama, sharp pauses, and partners treating a runaway laundry basket as a romantic rival*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Use a dramatic bandoneon pulse; stop whenever a sock crosses the floor.*

[Verse 1]
At the laundromat, two lemurs met beneath the clock,
They planned a perfect tango while the washers softly rocked.
A basket rolled between them, full of towels, warm and white;
They bowed as if it challenged them to duel beneath the light.

[Chorus]
Step close, turn slow, don't let the red sock go,
The basket is our rival and it knows the tango.
Pause sharp, bow low, the spin cycle says hello,
We'll dance around the laundry till the final buzzer blows.

[Verse 2]
A sleeve hooked on a chair leg and the basket made a sweep;
The partners chased it sideways with a dignity to keep.
They crossed and spun around it, toes tapping on the tile;
The dryer door swung open like a judge who'd watched a while.

[Chorus]
Step close, turn slow, don't let the red sock go,
The basket is our rival and it knows the tango.
Pause sharp, bow low, the spin cycle says hello,
We'll dance around the laundry till the final buzzer blows.

[Bridge]
They caught the basket gently, freed the sleeve, and cleared the floor;
A single sock slid out and made a grand pirouette once more.
The washer beeped the ending; they let the sock take its bow.

[Final Chorus]
Step close, turn slow, don't let the red sock go,
The basket was our rival and it stole the show.
Pause sharp, bow low, the spin cycle says hello,
We'll fold the clothes at sunrise; let the tango finish slow.

[Ending]
They left as dancing partners. The sock stayed for another load.
