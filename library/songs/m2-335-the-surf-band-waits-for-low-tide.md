# The Surf Band Waits for Low Tide

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: eternally sunny surf songs performed by musicians who have never managed to reach the water*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Twanging guitar and close beach-pop harmonies; let the tide-table reader interrupt the drum fills.*

[Verse 1]
We waxed our boards at sunrise and we combed our seaside hair,  
We loaded up the little van and drove the road out there.  
Our singer wore a shell necklace, the drummer wore a tan,  
The bassist brought a tide chart in a waterproof tin can.  
We reached the beach at seven, found the water near the wall,  
The bassist said to wait because it hadn't dropped at all.  

[Chorus]
Ride that wave when the numbers agree,  
There's a column for the shore and a column for the sea.  
Surf all day when the calendar is right,  
We'll spend the morning harmonizing high and dry and bright.  
Catch that curl, catch that sun,  
Our parking meter eats the coins before the day is done.  

[Verse 2]
We practiced on the picnic bench beside the changing shed,  
Our drummer balanced on his board and hit the rails instead.  
The lifeguard asked us where we'd learned to cut a breaking wave,  
Our singer hummed a guitar lick and tried to look quite brave.  
The bassist checked his chart again and tapped a printed line,  
We had the tide for somewhere inland, ninety miles from brine.  

[Chorus]
Ride that wave when the numbers agree,  
There's a column for the shore and a column for the sea.  
Surf all day when the calendar is right,  
We'll spend the morning harmonizing high and dry and bright.  
Catch that curl, catch that sun,  
Our parking meter eats the coins before the day is done.  

[Bridge]
The lifeguard lent a proper chart and pointed down the sand,  
We carried all our boards across the newly opened land.  
We laid them at the waterline and made our biggest cheer,  
Then found our drummer's borrowed board was only painted gear.  

[Final Chorus]
Ride that wave when the numbers agree,  
There's a column for the shore and a column for the sea.  
Surf all day when the calendar is right,  
We'll spend the evening renting something that can float tonight.  
Catch that curl, catch that sun,  
The van is full of seaweed and our album art is done.
