# Stick It on the Chorus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bubblegum pop hooks, shiny sticker collectibles, and a lyric sheet rewritten by an overenthusiastic label campaign*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Keep the hook bright and simple; use a peel sound as percussion and let the band reclaim the lyric.*

[Verse 1]
A label sent the band a sheet with every word approved,
A pink star sticker covered half the line they had rehearsed.
Milo, a capuchin, peeled it off; the chorus now said "glow,"
The drummer stuck one on his snare and watched the tempo slow.

[Chorus]
Stick it on the chorus, make the whole room shine,
Peel it off the paper, keep the beat in time.
Star on every jacket, star upon the door,
We came in with a chorus; now the sticker wants one more.


[Verse 2]
The producer wrote a new refrain: "Our brand is bright and neat."
A star went on the lyric page and slid beneath the beat.
The singers lost the sponsor line and sang the sound they knew;
The audience sang back too, though the sticker book said two.

[Chorus]
Stick it on the chorus, make the whole room shine,
Peel it off the paper, keep the beat in time.
Star on every jacket, star upon the door,
We came in with a chorus; now the sticker wants one more.


[Bridge]
The bright star hit the cymbal; the cymbal caught the light.
The producer stuck one on his tie and called the take "just right."
The band peeled every sticker off the pages and the floor;
The drummer placed them on the case and shut the label door.

[Verse 3]
They sang the first old chorus, every word in its own place;
The label tried to join them, with a star stuck to its face.
Milo held up one last sticker; the crowd all leaned to see.
He placed it on the kick drum, and it bounced into the key.

[Final Chorus]
Stick it on the chorus, make the whole room shine,
Peel it off the paper, keep the beat in time.
Star on every jacket, star upon the door,
We came in with a chorus; now the sticker wants one more.


[Ending]
The band stuck the final star on its album case, not the lyrics.
