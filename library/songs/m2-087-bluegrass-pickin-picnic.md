# Bluegrass Pickin' Picnic

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bluegrass picking contests, virtuosic instrumental breaks, and an emcee who cannot keep the squirrels from borrowing the picks*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Banjo, fiddle, upright bass, and handclaps; leave a break for the squirrels to answer with stolen picks.*

Verse 1
The band set up at noon beneath the walnut tree,
A banjo-playing macaque said, "Come try your speed with me."
The fiddler raised her bow; the bass began to thump,
A squirrel took the banjo pick and hid it in a stump.

Chorus
Pick it fast and pick it clean,
Keep the walnut off the string.
Fiddle bright and bass down low,
Let the little fingers go.

Verse 2
The first contestant played a run that curled around the tune,
The second played it twice as fast and finished far too soon.
The judges held their scorecards up; the squirrel held three picks,
It played a tiny walnut roll with nimble little clicks.

Chorus
Pick it fast and pick it clean,
Keep the walnut off the string.
Fiddle bright and bass down low,
Let the little fingers go.

Bridge
The emcee asked for silence so the judges could confer,
The squirrel shook a pick tin; the crowd applauded her.
The banjo player chased it, but the bass player said, "Stay!"
The squirrel joined the bandstand and picked the beat away.

Verse 3
They gave the squirrels a basket full of picks to keep them still,
The band played one last breakdown on the orchard's gentle hill.
The banjo ran like water; the fiddle found its place,
The squirrel held the time while everybody played the race.

Chorus
Pick it fast and pick it clean,
Keep the walnut off the string.
Fiddle bright and bass down low,
Let the little fingers go.

Ending
The judges chose the full ensemble, not the fastest hand,
The squirrel kept one pick as the newest in the band.
