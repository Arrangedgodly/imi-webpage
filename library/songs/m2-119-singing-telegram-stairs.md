# The Singing Telegram Took the Stairs

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: singing telegram conventions, surprise serenades, and a delivery monkey who keeps finding recipients on different floors*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Upbeat piano and handclaps; each verse is a sung delivery announcement followed by a quick stairway transition.*

Verse 1
A macaque took a telegram and climbed the tower stair,
It said, "Your friend sends birthday songs and hopes you'll meet him there."
He sang the greeting at the landing; no one answered back,
The note had named the lower floor; he retraced his track.

Chorus
Singing up the stair, singing down the hall,
A message with a melody can reach them all.
Knock once, bow twice, deliver every line,
The telegram arrives in rhythm and in time.

Verse 2
He found a gibbon on the second floor, who'd lost a birthday hat,
The singer gave the message; she replied, "I'm not the recipient of that."
He checked the paper, found the name was written upside down,
He sang the message backward, then the gibbon turned around.

Chorus
Singing up the stair, singing down the hall,
A message with a melody can reach them all.
Knock once, bow twice, deliver every line,
The telegram arrives in rhythm and in time.

Bridge
He climbed to floor eleven; the elevator was asleep,
The paper slipped from his hand and tumbled down the deep.
A capuchin caught the telegram and sang the final cue,
The birthday friend came from below and sang the chorus too.

Verse 3
They found the named recipient by the lobby's potted tree,
He'd been waiting with a cake and said, "I heard you sing for me."
The sender's note was upside down, but every word was right,
The birthday monkey sang along and shared the cake that night.

Chorus
Singing up the stair, singing down the hall,
A message with a melody can reach them all.
Knock once, bow twice, deliver every line,
The telegram arrives in rhythm and in time.

Ending
The singer reached the street at last, his message safely read,
The stairs received one final note before he went to bed.
