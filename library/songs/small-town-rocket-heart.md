# Small-Town Rocket Heart

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: power ballad longing, key changes, and enormous promises*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
I saw your face in the hardware store glass.  
You bought a wrench; I let the moment pass.  
I carried two paint cans into the night.  
My heart went louder than the traffic light.  
The radio promised the moon was in reach.  
I sang that promise to an empty beach.  
My town has one stop sign and one small pier.  
I'm a macaque mechanic, grease on my sleeve.  
My tail holds the wrench while I grieve.  

## Chorus
I will build a rocket from the things we saved.  
I will cross the dark and meet you brave.  
If you say the word, I'll leave the ground.  
If you need a ride, I'll turn around.  

## Verse 2
The school bus driver lent me a ladder.  
We welded the frame behind the feed store.  
The welding mask made me look like folklore.  
At dusk the engine gave a nervous cough.  
You came in boots with a thermos of tea.  
You asked if I'd built this thing for me.  

## Chorus
I will build a rocket from the things we saved.  
I will cross the dark and meet you brave.  
If you say the word, I'll leave the ground.  
If you need a ride, I'll turn around.  

## Bridge
The engine roared; the bolts began to shake.  
I saw your hands, not some faraway star.  
We killed the switch before we went too far.  
You said you liked this town, but hated the rush.  
The rocket cooled; the crowd went home.  
The moon came up above the old mill's dome.  

## Final chorus
I won't build a rocket just to run away.  
We'll walk by the shoreline at the end of day.  
If you say the word, I'll meet you there.  
The sky can hold its breath and spare.  
I'll keep that rocket by the feed store shed.  
We'll plant some flowers where the sparks once spread.  
If you need a ride, I'll turn around.  
If you want to stay, we'll stay in town.  

## Ending
The wrench sat quiet beneath the seat.  
No launch command split the blue.  
The small-town sky had room for two.  

*Performance note: Build toward a huge final lift, then let the band fall away for the decision to stay.*
