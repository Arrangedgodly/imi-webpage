# The Late Night Sandwich

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: intimate acoustic confessionals and kitchen-table storytelling*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
At 2:13 the fridge light found my face.  
I stood in socks, unsure of my place.  
The bread bag folded like a tired sail.  
The mustard jar was down to a trail.  
I held a slice beneath the humming bulb.  
The house kept quiet, full of stubborn hum.  
My phone displayed a message I had missed.  
I set it down and made a list.  
I'm an old langur who hates the dawn.  
My tail held the bread while I yawned.  

## Chorus
I made a sandwich for the long way through.  
Two pieces of bread and a thought of you.  
The kitchen knows what I cannot say.  
It keeps one small light on till day.  

## Verse 2
I found your note beside the chipped blue bowl.  
It said, "Please buy more cheese," with a smile.  
I laughed alone, then gave the room a nod.  
The pickle jar sat like a patient god.  
I stacked the slices, folded them in two.  
The counter held the shape of what we knew.  
I took one bite and tasted home.  
The clock made sure I wasn't alone.  

## Chorus
I made a sandwich for the long way through.  
Two pieces of bread and a thought of you.  
The kitchen knows what I cannot say.  
It keeps one small light on till day.  

## Bridge
The morning came in pale and slow.  
I heard the neighbor's sprinkler start to go.  
I wrapped the rest in waxed paper clean.  
I left it where the note had been.  
No speech arrived; no answer came.  
The fridge still wore its humming name.  
I wrote "cheese" beneath the grocery list.  
Then took the message from my missed list.  

## Final chorus
I made a sandwich for the walk to town.  
The bread held firm; the kitchen knew my way.  
I'll leave one light for another day.  
Two pieces of bread, a folded note.  
The morning breeze around my coat.  
I called you back beside the store.  
You laughed and asked me to buy more.  

## Ending
The bag swung low along the street.  
The cheese was warm, the day was sweet.  
At home, the fridge light shone.  
I set the groceries down, not alone.  

*Performance note: Keep the guitar close and quiet; leave a small breath before each ordinary kitchen detail.*
