# Trip-Hop at the Moonlit Laundrette

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: trip-hop's smoky breakbeats and producers who turn routine loops into endless nocturnal atmosphere*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Dusty break, low bass, hushed vocal; let the laundrette's real spin cycle become the track's stubborn loop.*

**Verse 1**
The last bus leaves the corner; the laundrette stays awake.  
A lemur loads one blanket in the washer by mistake.  
The drum begins a slow breakbeat, a steady silver roll.  
The lights make every puddle look like midnight on a pole.  
A macaque at the dryer hums a low and smoky line.  
The timer says one minute; the bass says, "Take your time."  

**Chorus**
Low light, slow spin, the night won't let it go;  
Round and round the woolen waves, the machines move slow.  
One beat, two beat, the rinse begins again;  
The blanket holds the moonlight like a pocket full of rain.  

**Verse 2**
The drummer samples dryer doors, then loops the double click.  
The coin slot takes his last brass piece and makes the rhythm stick.  
The blanket tumbles past the glass, a cloudy little moon.  
The singer whispers, "Almost home," before the rinse is through.  
The owner checks the label; it's not her blanket's name.  
The washer's borrowed from the shop; the tags are not the same.  

**Chorus**
Low light, slow spin, the night won't let it go;  
Round and round the woolen waves, the machines move slow.  
One beat, two beat, the rinse begins again;  
The blanket holds the moonlight like a pocket full of rain.  

**Bridge**
They read the stitched initials, find the proper owner near.  
The blanket leaves the machine; the drum falls quiet here.  
The singer takes the loop away, the spin cycle is done.  
The laundrette lights come up; the night has lost its hum.  

**Ending**
They fold the blanket carefully, its corners set in place.  
The moon slips off the glass and leaves a pale blue trace.  
The beat stops, the door swings shut; the last machine goes still.  
The singer hums the dryer once, then walks down the hill.
