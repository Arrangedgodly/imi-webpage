# The Harvest Ballad Sings to the Crows

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rustic harvest ballads and singers who assume every attentive audience appreciates the music rather than the crops*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Steady acoustic guitar and group refrain; crows answer from the fence without moving away from the grain.*

[Verse 1]
I sang about the harvest at the farm beside the lane,  
The field held rows of golden stalks that bent beneath the grain.  
Three crows sat on the fence posts while I raised the opening tune,  
They listened with their little heads beneath the afternoon.  
I told the farmer that the birds had joined my little show,  
He said they watched the grain spill where the cart was moving slow.  
I sang a hopeful second verse about the generous earth,  
The crows hopped to the spilled grain with a different view of worth.  

[Refrain]
Sing, little field, sing beneath the sun,  
The crows have found a free meal while the opening is begun.  
Sing, little field, let the golden lines grow,  
The farmer counts the grain sacks, and the crows count what falls below.  
Three black heads, one warm tune, let the fence hold its place,  
The audience has an appetite that music can't replace.  

[Verse 2]
The farmer closed the sack seam that was spilling on the track,  
He swept the loose grain to a bin and put the whole load back.  
The crows flew to a farther field beyond the little stream,  
My audience had gone away before the harvest dream.  
The workers came at lunchtime with their plates beside the wall,  
I played the harvest song for them where everyone could call.  
They sang the little chorus with a louder voice than three,  
The farmer shared his lunch beneath the old oak tree.  

[Refrain]
Sing, little field, sing beneath the sun,  
The crows have found a free meal while the opening is begun.  
Sing, little field, let the golden lines grow,  
The farmer counts the grain sacks, and the crows count what falls below.  
Three black heads, one warm tune, let the fence hold its place,  
The audience has an appetite that music can't replace.  

[Bridge]
I wrote the crows into the song as guests who came and went,  
The farmer said the sack repair had helped the day's event.  
The workers took the chorus to the barn when rain began,  
The crows stayed in the farther field and followed their own plan.  

[Final Refrain]
Sing, little field, let the golden lines grow,  
The sacks have got a proper seam with no grain falling below.  
Three black heads, one warm tune, let the fence hold its place,  
The workers buy the recording; the crows give it no praise.  
Sing, little field, sing beneath the sun,  
The farmer asks if ticket sales can pay for one more bun.
