# The Sports Anthem Loses the Starting Pistol

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sports-event anthems and ceremonies that grow so long that the actual race cannot begin*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Punchy drums, mass voices and a stadium announcer; a raised flag supplies the practical starting signal.*

[Verse 1]
We sang before the village race beside the chalked white line,  
The runners waited patiently beneath the starting sign.  
The starter reached inside his bag to find the pistol grip,  
He pulled out sandwiches instead and searched another zip.  
The blank-firing starter pistol lay at home upon his desk,  
The anthem band kept playing when he told us what was next.  
The runners asked if we could stop the song and start the race,  
The starter held a little flag and showed the marshal its place.  

[Chorus]
Run to the future, run to the light,  
The opening ceremony wants to get each flourish right.  
Run to the future, hear the crowd,  
The absent starting pistol would have made the moment loud.  
Stand at the line and watch the red flag fall,  
The runners need a signal, not another chorus call.  

[Verse 2]
The marshal told the runners that the flag would drop at three,  
The drummer stopped his rolling beat so everyone could see.  
The starter raised the red flag in the morning's open air,  
He counted out the numbers with the runners waiting there.  
At three the flag came down and all the runners left the mark,  
The band struck up a shorter tune beside the village park.  
We played until the last runner had crossed the finishing line,  
The starter wrote the finish times beneath the starting sign.  

[Chorus]
Run to the future, run to the light,  
The opening ceremony wants to get each flourish right.  
Run to the future, hear the crowd,  
The absent starting pistol would have made the moment loud.  
Stand at the line and watch the red flag fall,  
The runners need a signal, not another chorus call.  

[Bridge]
The winner got a ribbon and a sandwich from the bag,  
The starter bought another one before he packed the flag.  
He said the next race might begin without the lengthy song,  
The drummer promised just four bars to help the start along.  

[Final Chorus]
Run to the future, run to the light,  
The flag has done the job and kept the timing quite all right.  
Stand at the line and watch the red flag fall,  
The band has one short opening and a much shorter call.  
Run to the future, hear the crowd,  
The starter eats his sandwich; it requires no starting sound.
