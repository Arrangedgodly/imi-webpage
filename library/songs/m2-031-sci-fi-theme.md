# Signal from the Tin Can Moon

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: science-fiction television theme conventions and weekly cliffhangers*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: A bold brass opening, theremin glide, and an announcer who nearly spoils the mystery.*

## Verse 1
The crew of the Tin Can drifted past the moon.  
Their captain was a capuchin who whistled out of tune.  
A blinking light appeared inside the cargo bay.  
The engineer said, "It has been there all day."  
The screen cut close to one glowing red eye.  
The theme song climbed as every star swept by.  
The narrator said, "Will they discover what it means?"  
The captain pressed a button marked "routine cleaning."  

## Chorus
Signal from the tin can, calling through the night.  
We follow every flicker, every half-seen light.  
Signal from the tin can, strange and far away.  
We'll solve it after next week's break in the display.  

## Verse 2
The blinking light was just a loose alarm.  
A crate of pears had tripped the sensor's arm.  
The engineer reset the panel with a key.  
The captain said, "The cosmos still surprises me."  
A new pulse came from somewhere past the rim.  
The crew leaned close; the monitor went dim.  
A shadow crossed the view screen, then the ads came on.  
The narrator teased, "Our hero's hair is gone."  

## Bridge
The crew debated whether to press the switch.  
The captain said, "I wonder if it's rich."  
They opened the hatch with helmets strapped on tight.  
The glow belonged to a lost satellite.  

## Chorus
Signal from the tin can, calling through the night.  
We follow every flicker, every half-seen light.  
Signal from the tin can, strange and far away.  
We'll solve it after next week's break in the display.  

## Ending
The crew brought home the satellite's old song.  
The narrator promised more; the credits rolled along.
