# The Funk Bassist Borrows a Belt

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: swaggering funk bass showcases whose physical confidence depends on a small wardrobe detail*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Syncopated bass, clipped guitar and answering horns; every trouser hitch briefly interrupts the bassist's supposedly effortless groove.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a pair of velvet pants to match my shining bass,  
They looked a little roomy but they had tremendous class.  
I stepped into the spotlight with my feet spread very wide,  
The first slap shook the waistband and the trousers tried to slide.  
I caught them with my left hand while the right maintained a note,  
The singer said my solo had a very special float.  
I tried to reach the fretboard while I held the velvet tight,  
The keyboard filled the silence; I could not release my grip that night.  

[Chorus]
Hold that groove, keep it low,  
The bass is hanging steady but the pants would like to go.  
Slap that string, make it pop,  
There is something in the waistband that I really need to stop.  
Hold that groove, let it shine,  
The coolest man on stage has got one hand working overtime.  

[Verse 2]
The singer took a trumpet break and waved toward the wing,  
The stagehand brought a little belt around a coil of string.  
I fitted it behind the amp while the drummer took a turn,  
The brass held out a riff that gave me seconds I could earn.  
I tightened up the buckle and returned with both hands free,  
The bass came back much busier; the singer grinned at me.  
The crowd called for a solo like the first one that I'd done,  
I held a note deliberately and let the keyboard run.  

[Chorus]
Hold that groove, keep it low,  
The bass is hanging steady but the pants would like to go.  
Slap that string, make it pop,  
There is something in the waistband that I really need to stop.  
Hold that groove, let it shine,  
The coolest man on stage has got one hand working overtime.  

[Bridge]
The stagehand took his belt back after all the gear was packed,  
I caught my velvet trousers with a very quick attack.  
He lent it for the journey home and put me on his list,  
Tomorrow's soundcheck starts with belts before we test the kick.  

[Final Chorus]
Hold that groove, keep it low,  
The bass has got a fine new strap; the pants have one to show.  
Slap that string, make it pop,  
The stagehand checks my buckle before any microphones go up.  
Hold that groove, let it shine,  
My first great solo had one note; my second had a waistline.
