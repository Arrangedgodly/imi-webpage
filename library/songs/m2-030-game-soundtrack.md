# Checkpoint at Cinder Pass

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: video game soundtrack loops, level design, and checkpoint frustration*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Chiptune arpeggios, a dramatic boss cue, and a tiny victory sound for the bridge.*

## Verse 1
The player reached Cinder Pass with one heart left.  
A pixel monkey jumped the gap, then leapt too deft.  
The screen went dark; the game returned to start.  
He set the controller down to cool his heart.  
The soundtrack looped the same four bright bars.  
The lava blinked like tiny orange stars.  
He noticed one loose tile beside the gate.  
The next attempt would need a slower wait.  

## Chorus
Save at the checkpoint, breathe, begin.  
The level learns the shape of every spin.  
Save at the checkpoint, watch the ground.  
A small safe step can turn the game around.  

## Verse 2
He waited out the falling rocks this time.  
The bass drum marked their pattern like a chime.  
He crossed the ledge and found a hidden room.  
A nonplayer turtle swept away the gloom.  
It gave him one more heart and asked no fee.  
The boss appeared beneath a pixel tree.  
He dodged left twice, then jumped on three.  
The victory sting rang out; he laughed with glee.  

## Bridge
The game had asked for patience, not a perfect hand.  
The player wrote the trick upon a sticky band.  
His sister tried the level after school.  
She found another path by breaking every rule.  

## Chorus
Save at the checkpoint, breathe, begin.  
The level learns the shape of every spin.  
Save at the checkpoint, watch the ground.  
A small safe step can turn the game around.  

## Ending
The screen displayed a dawn above the pass.  
They shared the controller, trading turns at last.
