# The Surf Band Waits for a Puddle

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: swaggering surf-rock beach anthems performed by a band with no access to a beach*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Tremolo guitar, spring reverb and rolling drums; the puddle's tiny splash interrupts every promised enormous wave.*

[Verse 1]
We wore our brightest shirts to shoot a video at dawn,  
The beach was closed for nesting, so we filmed upon the lawn.  
Our drummer found a puddle by the gutter of the shed,  
The singer called it ocean and the camera nodded yes.  
We set a little surfboard on a patch of flattened grass,  
The bass man waved a watering can behind a sheet of glass.  
The singer crouched upon the board and scanned the distant view,  
A sparrow drank our biggest wave before the chorus grew.  

[Chorus]
Ride, ride, ride that swell,  
Hear the tiny ocean by the garden watering well.  
Hey, hey, silver foam,  
We are twenty miles inland and a minute from my home.  
Turn, turn, catch the blue,  
The puddle has a leaf aboard; it sails ahead of you.  
Ride, ride, let it roll,  
The tide is nearly ankle deep beside the garden pole.  

[Verse 2]
The drummer hit the floor tom and the singer leaned to steer,  
The board remained precisely where we'd left it sitting here.  
The bassist swung the watering can to build a stormy sea,  
He drenched the singer's sunglasses and watered half a tree.  
Our camera caught the leaf go past like something bound for Spain,  
The singer cried, "Another wave!" The drummer checked for rain.  
The clouds were bright and empty, and the puddle grew quite small,  
We played our fiercest solo while the sun erased it all.  

[Chorus]
Ride, ride, ride that swell,  
Hear the tiny ocean by the garden watering well.  
Hey, hey, silver foam,  
We are twenty miles inland and a minute from my home.  
Turn, turn, catch the blue,  
The puddle has a leaf aboard; it sails ahead of you.  
Ride, ride, let it roll,  
The tide is nearly ankle deep beside the garden pole.  

[Bridge]
We wrapped the shoot with photographs of shirts and dusty feet,  
The camera editor enlarged the leaf upon his screen.  
It looked just like a schooner crossing golden sunset light,  
We gave the leaf top billing when the video went live.  

[Final Chorus]
Ride, ride, ride that swell,  
The leaf has got a fan club and our record doesn't sell.  
Hey, hey, silver foam,  
The leaf is touring gardens; we are packing up at home.  
Turn, turn, catch the blue,  
It wants a larger puddle for the sequel due in June.  
Ride, ride, let it roll,  
We have signed a little leaf to play the leading surfer role.
