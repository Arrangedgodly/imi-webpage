# The Skipping-Rope Counting Song

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: children's counting songs with cumulative repetition and an ever-longer sequence*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a simple bouncing melody and add one number each time; let the rope's tangles interrupt the perfect count.*

[Verse 1]
One little monkey held the rope,  
Two little monkeys tried to cope.  
Three little feet went skip, skip, skip,  
Four little elbows clipped the grip.  
Five little counts were loud and clear,  
Six little turns brought the rope too near.  
Seven little monkeys lined in place,  
Eight little tails made a looping lace.  

[Chorus]
Count it up and swing it round,  
Keep the skipping off the ground.  
One, two, three, then four, then five,  
Everyone is counting to stay alive.  
Six, seven, eight, the rope goes free,  
The ninth one joins the jubilee.  

[Verse 2]
The teacher said, "Begin again," and drew a line of chalk,  
The monkeys made a longer row and tried a steady walk.  
The rope went left, the rope went right, the count began anew,  
The drummer tapped a skipping beat and missed his count of two.  
The smallest monkey jumped too soon and tangled up the loop,  
The biggest monkey spun the rope around the whole wide group.  
They counted every tail and shoe, then counted one more friend,  
The sequence grew so long it had no number at the end.  

[Chorus]
Count it up and swing it round,  
Keep the skipping off the ground.  
One, two, three, then four, then five,  
Everyone is counting to stay alive.  
Six, seven, eight, the rope goes free,  
The ninth one joins the jubilee.  

[Bridge]
The teacher stopped the turning and asked them all to rest,  
The rope fell in a circle, the monkeys stood impressed.  
They counted one by one and found a place to start,  
Then tied the rope in loops and gave the class a chart.  

[Final Chorus]
Count it slow and set it down,  
The rope is safe upon the ground.  
One, two, three, then four, then five,  
No one needs to jump to prove they're alive.  
Six, seven, eight, the count can wait,  
The rope is neatly stored beside the gate.  

[Ending]
The teacher sang the last number; the monkeys had gone for snacks.
