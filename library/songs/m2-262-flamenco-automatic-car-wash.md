# Flamenco at the Automatic Car Wash

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: flamenco's sharp heelwork, dramatic pauses, and call-and-response clapping*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use crisp palmas, guitar rasgueado and heel taps; each wash cycle interrupts the dancers' dramatic pose.*

[Verse 1]
A gibbon brought a dusty cart to the automatic wash,  
A capuchin began to strum with a fierce and fiery flourish.  
The dancers clicked their heels in time beside the ticket booth,  
The wash sign flashed "please neutral," and they answered with a truth.  
The brushes rolled like red silk fans, the water jets took flight,  
The lead singer held a pose beneath the blinking light.  
A macaque clapped the rhythm; the soap began to fall,  
The chorus cried "Ay, caramba!" as foam covered them all.  

[Chorus]
Clap, heel, turn, hold the line,  
The water's dancing in a row of shine.  
Clap, heel, pause, then let it flow,  
The car is turning under curtains of foam.  
Clap, heel, cry out with pride,  
The wash is doing palmas on the other side.  

[Verse 2]
The driver stayed in neutral as the brushes made their sweep,  
The guitarist played a tremolo, the clappers counted deep.  
The dryer roared like thunder, the singer raised his chin,  
The car rolled out still sudsy with a wet and soapy grin.  
The wash attendant offered one last rinse beneath the arch,  
The dancers took a sideways step and turned it to a march.  
A crow landed on the mirror and pecked the soap away,  
The singer bowed to the bird, who stole his castanet.  

[Chorus]
Clap, heel, turn, hold the line,  
The water's dancing in a row of shine.  
Clap, heel, pause, then let it flow,  
The car is turning under curtains of foam.  
Clap, heel, cry out with pride,  
The wash is doing palmas on the other side.  

[Bridge]
The attendant read the ticket: "Wax, then rinse, then dry."  
The dancer made a sweeping arm and pointed to the sky.  
The wash completed every step; the mirrors shone like glass,  
The singer waved the crow farewell as all the brushes passed.  

[Final Chorus]
Clap, heel, turn, the car is clean,  
The dancers exit from the drying machine.  
Clap, heel, pause, then let it flow,  
The crow has taken the castanet home.  
Clap, heel, cry out with pride,  
The soap has left; the song will ride.  

[Ending]
The crow clicked the castanet; the dancers bowed to the wash.
