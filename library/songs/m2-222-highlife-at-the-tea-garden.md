# Highlife at the Tea Garden

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: highlife guitar interplay, buoyant horns, and a tea garden where the hostess keeps serving the band before the guests*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the interlocking guitar bright; give each horn a short reply after the call.*

[Verse 1]
A gibbon brought his guitar to the garden after noon,
The highlife band was warming up beneath a mango moon.
The hostess carried tea cups on a tray beside the stage,
She served the horns before the guests and turned another page.

[Chorus]
Tea for the drummer, tea for the bass,
Tea for the guitarist keeping time and place.
Play on, play on, let the bright strings ring,
The hostess pours a cup to match the tune they bring.

[Verse 2]
The trumpet got a saucer; the saxophone a spoon,
The keyboard had a sugar cube and stirred it with a tune.
A capuchin played guitar; the guests began to sway,
The tea cart joined the rhythm and rolled out through the gate.

[Chorus]
Tea for the drummer, tea for the bass,
Tea for the guitarist keeping time and place.
Play on, play on, let the bright strings ring,
The hostess pours a cup to match the tune they bring.

[Bridge]
The hostess called a break; the band played through the rest,
The drummer held his teacup while the guests all faced the west.
They poured one last cup for the leader of the band,
Who took it to the garden and waved his free hand.

[Final Chorus]
Tea for the drummer, tea for the bass,
Tea for the guitarist keeping time and place.
Play on, play on, let the bright strings ring,
The hostess now is dancing; let the tea cups sing.

[Ending]
The tea went cold; the band kept warm with one more tune.
