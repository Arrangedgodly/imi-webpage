# The Moonlight Sonata at the Loading Dock

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic nocturnes that make ordinary late-night work sound solemn and fated*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a slow piano arpeggio and whispered verses, then let the forklift's reversing beep puncture the mood.*

[Verse 1]
A night-shift macaque watched the moon above the freight-yard gate,  
The loading dock was quiet, and the hour was getting late.  
A cello held a tender note, the piano traced the rain,  
A gibbon moved a pallet with a long and mournful strain.  
The boxes wore their labels like the names of old regrets,  
The scanner's green light flickered where the warehouse window sweats.  
The singer gazed at one small crate and sighed into the air,  
"It holds a garden hose, but destiny is there."  

[Chorus]
Under the moon, roll softly by,  
The pallet jack is passing where the shadows lie.  
A distant beep, a velvet sigh,  
The loading dock is learning how to dream tonight.  
Under the moon, don't break the spell,  
The forklift's warning light is glowing very well.  

[Verse 2]
The driver turned the corner with a quiet little hum,  
The backup alarm began a measured, mournful drum.  
The cello followed every beep with melancholy grace,  
The piano added tremolo and gave the crate a face.  
The scanner said "wrong aisle," the supervisor held a key,  
The night crew formed a chorus near the stack of number three.  
They rolled the crate one meter, then the label came to light:  
"Garden hose, return to storage, not the moonlit loading site."  

[Chorus]
Under the moon, roll softly by,  
The pallet jack is passing where the shadows lie.  
A distant beep, a velvet sigh,  
The loading dock is learning how to dream tonight.  
Under the moon, don't break the spell,  
The forklift's warning light is glowing very well.  

[Bridge]
The night shift took a breath and moved the crate back in,  
The cello played a cadence as the scanner gave a ping.  
The moon stayed in the window, the rain kept tapping time,  
The driver found the right aisle and parked behind the line.  

[Final Chorus]
Under the moon, roll softly by,  
The pallet jack is passing where the shadows lie.  
A distant beep, a velvet sigh,  
The loading dock is learning how to dream tonight.  
Under the moon, the shift is through,  
The hose is stored; the moon can watch the crew.  

[Ending]
The piano closed on one soft note; the forklift beeped in answer.
