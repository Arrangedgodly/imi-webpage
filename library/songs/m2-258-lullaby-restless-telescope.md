# The Lullaby for a Restless Telescope

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: nursery lullabies with soothing repetition and a gently descending melody*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Sing softly over a rocking three-beat pulse; the telescope should interrupt each verse with a small mechanical squeak.*

[Verse 1]
A young macaque set up a scope beneath the evening sky,  
A gibbon turned the focus knob and asked the stars to lie  
Still for just a moment while the lenses found the moon,  
The telescope kept swiveling to follow every tune.  
A breeze moved through the treetops; the tripod shifted one,  
The finder pointed at the porch light, not the setting sun.  
The young one rubbed his sleepy eyes and whispered, "Can we see?"  
The scope squeaked twice and turned around to look at a key.  

[Chorus]
Sleep, little telescope, settle your glass,  
Let the bright planets quietly pass.  
Rock in the night, keep your lenses in line,  
The sky will be waiting at closing time.  
Sleep, little telescope, hush your gears,  
The moon is above us, the porch light is near.  

[Verse 2]
They tightened all the tripod feet and leveled every leg,  
The scope found Saturn's ring, then drifted to a hedge.  
A cricket chirped in rhythm, the telescope replied,  
Its motor hummed a lullaby and turned toward the side.  
A capuchin placed a blanket gently on the stand,  
The eyepiece grew a mitten from the blanket's trailing hand.  
The young one set the finder still, then pointed at the sky,  
The scope held on the moon at last; the family sighed.  

[Chorus]
Sleep, little telescope, settle your glass,  
Let the bright planets quietly pass.  
Rock in the night, keep your lenses in line,  
The sky will be waiting at closing time.  
Sleep, little telescope, hush your gears,  
The moon is above us, the porch light is near.  

[Bridge]
The telescope went silent; the moon came sharp and white,  
The stargazers grew quiet under velvet night.  
A squirrel crossed the eyepiece view, its tail a comet's trail,  
The telescope found sleep at last; the squirrel tipped its tail.  

[Final Chorus]
Sleep, little telescope, settle your glass,  
Let the bright planets quietly pass.  
Rock in the night, keep your lenses in line,  
The stars will be waiting another time.  
Sleep, little telescope, fold up your stand,  
The sky can be watched with a map in your hand.  

[Ending]
The scope stayed still all night; the squirrel kept watch.
