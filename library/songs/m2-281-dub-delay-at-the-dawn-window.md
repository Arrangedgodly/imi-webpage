# Dub Delay at the Dawn Window

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dub reggae echo effects and the producer habit of repeating the last word until it becomes the whole arrangement*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Relaxed one-drop groove; every echo should arrive late enough to interrupt the next task.*

**Verse 1**
Mako checks the sunrise clock and opens up the blind.  
A pigeon taps the glass in time, then taps it from behind.  
The kettle clicks; the kettle's echo clicks a bar too late.  
The toast pops up, the echo waits, and lands upon the plate.  
He calls, "Come in, the dawn is here," the hallway answers, "Here."  
The hallway's echo takes so long it misses breakfast's year.  
His neighbor waves from three doors down and asks him for a light.  
Mako says, "Good morning," and the echo answers, "Night."  

**Chorus**
Dawn in the window, late in the room,  
Echo comes back with a coat and a broom.  
Pass me the cup, pass me the cup,  
The echo says "cup" when the tea's been drunk up.  
One drop, one drop, then the echo drops two;  
Mako keeps time till the morning comes through.  

**Verse 2**
He sweeps the step; the doubled broom makes dust return in waves.  
He shuts the door; the echo knocks, and all the hallway behaves.  
He rings the bell to tell the postman, "Package for the flat."  
The echo rings again at noon; the postman brings it back.  
Mako sets the dial to dry, the speaker sends a mist.  
He turns the echo off at last; it says, "You will be missed."  

**Chorus**
Dawn in the window, late in the room,  
Echo comes back with a coat and a broom.  
Pass me the cup, pass me the cup,  
The echo says "cup" when the tea's been drunk up.  
One drop, one drop, then the echo drops two;  
Mako keeps time till the morning comes through.  

**Bridge**
He unplugs the pedal, gentle; the room goes still and plain.  
A final echo from the kettle says, "Plug me in again."  
The pigeon taps the window once; the echo stays inside.  
Mako pours his tea in peace and lets the record ride.  

**Ending**
The sun clears roofs; the speakers rest; the postman finds the door.  
Mako hangs a sign that reads: "No echoes after four."
