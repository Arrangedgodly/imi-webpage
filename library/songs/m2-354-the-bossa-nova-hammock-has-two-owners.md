# The Bossa Nova Hammock Has Two Owners

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: breezy bossa nova duets that make a cramped beach resort sound effortlessly luxurious*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Nylon-string guitar, brushed percussion and two singers politely disputing each other's relaxation.*

[Verse 1: Singer A]
I found a little hammock underneath a gentle tree,  
The room attendant said its swaying peace belonged to me.  
I settled with a magazine and listened to the bay,  
You came with your room key and said the hammock was your stay.  
We checked our little vouchers and they bore the very same,  
The hammock had two owners with a single printed claim.  

[Chorus: Both]
Sway to the left, sway to the right,  
One of us is horizontal, one is upright.  
Sip a little sunshine, smile beside the sea,  
Your exclusive hammock is exclusive also to me.  
The breeze is free, the shade is fine,  
Could you move your elbow from the paragraph that's mine?  

[Verse 2: Singer B]
I tried to join you gently with my sandals in my hand,  
The hammock tipped a little and your book fell in the sand.  
You took the left half firmly, I occupied the right,  
We faced in opposite directions with our ankles knotted tight.  
The room attendant offered us a cushion for our peace,  
We asked for two; he said our comfort had a shared lease.  

[Chorus: Both]
Sway to the left, sway to the right,  
One of us is horizontal, one is upright.  
Sip a little sunshine, smile beside the sea,  
Your exclusive hammock is exclusive also to me.  
The breeze is free, the shade is fine,  
Could you move your elbow from the paragraph that's mine?  

[Bridge]
We climbed out of the hammock and we sat upon the sand,  
The shade reached both our towels and the sea remained unplanned.  
The room attendant brought two drinks and balanced on the rope,  
He fell into the hammock with a sigh of private hope.  

[Final Chorus: Both]
Sway to the left, sway to the right,  
Both of us are horizontal; he's in the hammock tonight.  
Sip a little sunshine, smile beside the sea,  
The sand belongs to everyone and it has room for me.  
The breeze is free, the shade is fine,  
The attendant keeps the hammock; the sand is yours and mine.
