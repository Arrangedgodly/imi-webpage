# Lounge Song for the Airport Plant

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: lounge singers who turn background music into an intimate personal performance for an audience trying to travel*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Slow cocktail piano and brushed snare; singer keeps addressing the terminal fern as passengers try to board.*

**Verse 1**
The lounge singer smooths his jacket by the boarding gate.  
A fern in a brass pot is his only audience of late.  
He croons, "You look so lovely under fluorescent light."  
The travelers check their tickets; the flight is called tonight.  
A porter rolls a suitcase through the soft piano phrase.  
The singer drops his voice and sings the fern's praise.  

**Chorus**
Easy now, leaf, let the runway lights glow;  
Take my hand, little fern, and sway very slow.  
The gate says final boarding, but the piano says stay;  
The singer wants a close-up before we fly away.  

**Verse 2**
A child turns the pot around to find the gate number.  
The singer calls it jealousy and sings a little lower.  
The fern is tagged for watering; the porter checks its card.  
The singer asks the plant to stay and holds one tender chord.  
A gust from the jet bridge lifts a frond across the keys.  
He sings, "Your hair is moving in the terminal breeze."  

**Chorus**
Easy now, leaf, let the runway lights glow;  
Take my hand, little fern, and sway very slow.  
The gate says final boarding, but the piano says stay;  
The singer wants a close-up before we fly away.  

**Bridge**
The porter lifts the fern and wheels it to the greenhouse cart.  
The singer follows one short step, then stops before the start.  
He gives the travelers room to pass; the gate swings open wide.  
The band plays just a quiet phrase as passengers climb inside.  

**Ending**
The fern is safe; the travelers board; the singer packs his tie.  
He bows toward the empty gate and lets the last chord die.  
The flight lifts through the window; the pot goes down the hall.  
The lounge returns to quiet, and the fern outlives them all.
