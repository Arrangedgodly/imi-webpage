# Quiet Riot at the Library

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: punk garage-band anthems, shouted choruses, and deliberately rough production*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Scratchy guitar and stomping drums, but every chorus drops to a whisper when the librarian raises one finger.*

Verse 1
We plug the amps in backwards, we tape the cables down,  
The drummer hits a paperback and shakes the reading town.  
The singer shouts, "We won't be quiet!" The librarian appears,  
She points toward the silent room and hands us ear protectors.  
We scowl beside the checkout desk, we kick a plastic chair,  
It squeaks so loud the whole band stops and gives the squeak a stare.  

Chorus
Read! Read! Raise the page!  
Keep the chorus off the stage.  
No more feedback, no more roar,  
The library has a second floor.  
Read! Read! Shake the shelf!  
The librarian has a book herself.  
Turn it up? We'll turn it down;  
We are the loudest band in town.  

Verse 2
We write a protest anthem on the back of overdue,  
The librarian checks the date and says it's overdue too.  
Our guitarist strums an open chord; the shelves begin to ring,  
A toddler bangs a tambourine and joins us on the swing.  
The band starts sounding polished, which is punk's worst fate,  
We knock the mic stand over, but it lands beside the crate.  

Bridge
She gives us one small room where the walls can take the sound,  
We test a single power chord; the windows rattle round.  
The librarian nods along, then stamps each borrowed book,  
We finish with a whispered scream and one approving look.  

Chorus
Read! Read! Raise the page!  
Keep the chorus off the stage.  
No more feedback, no more roar,  
The library has a second floor.  
Read! Read! Shake the shelf!  
The librarian has a book herself.  
Turn it up? We'll turn it down;  
We are the loudest band in town.  

Ending
We pack the amps and leave the room as quiet as before,  
The librarian keeps our setlist, filed under noise next door.
