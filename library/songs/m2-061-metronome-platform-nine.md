# Metronome at Platform Nine

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: classical concerto virtuosity and the orchestra's reverence for exact tempo*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Solo violin over a formal string ensemble; the metronome clicks steadily while the station board changes tempo without warning.*

Verse 1
The violinist arrives at dawn with rosin on her sleeve,  
She opens up her case and hears the station whistle grieve.  
The metronome is set to sixty, formal, calm, and grand,  
The platform clock ticks eighty-five and runs ahead of band.  
A macaque porter moves the chairs in half-time down the row,  
The soloist adjusts her bow and asks the train to slow.  

Chorus
Click, click, bow and breathe,  
Count the beats beneath your feet.  
Hold the note, don't race the train,  
Tempo's lost but found again.  
Click, click, measure four,  
The conductor's at the door.  
If the schedule changes key,  
Play the platform's melody.  

Verse 2
The first train rolls in silent; the second sounds a horn,  
The conductor says the timetable was printed upside down.  
The soloist plays a rising scale to match the engine's hum,  
The porter moves a bass drum where the luggage carts should come.  
The train departs at adagio, the board declares it fast,  
The violinist holds a trill until the doors are past.  

Bridge
She turns the metronome around; the pendulum clicks true,  
The station clock keeps racing, so she takes it to the crew.  
They set the hands to local time, the platform breathes in sync,  
The conductor bows to every beat and asks her what to think.  

Chorus
Click, click, bow and breathe,  
Count the beats beneath your feet.  
Hold the note, don't race the train,  
Tempo's lost but found again.  
Click, click, measure four,  
The conductor's at the door.  
If the schedule changes key,  
Play the platform's melody.  

Ending
The soloist packs her violin; the board is nearly right,  
One train is marked "very slowly," and arrives tonight.
