# The Picnic March of the Lost Spoon

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bagpipe marches, parade formations, and a picnic procession thrown off course by one missing spoon*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the pipes boldly marching; let the spoon's clatter answer the drone.*

[Verse 1]
The pipe band marched to picnic ground in uniform and rows,
A capuchin played the melody while every bagpipe blows.
They set the table carefully, laid out bread and cheese,
Then found the serving spoon had slipped between the trees.

[Chorus]
March, march, lift the spoon,
Find it underneath the moon.
March, march, pipes ring clear,
The picnic waits; the spoon is near.
March, march, lift the spoon,
Find it underneath the moon.
March, march, pipes ring clear,
The picnic waits; the spoon is near.

[Verse 2]
The drummers searched the blanket; the pipers checked the bag,
The bass player inspected every fold and every rag.
A squirrel dragged a silver thing across the grass away,
The band marched after it and played a search parade.

[Chorus]
March, march, lift the spoon,
Find it underneath the moon.
March, march, pipes ring clear,
The picnic waits; the spoon is near.
March, march, lift the spoon,
Find it underneath the moon.
March, march, pipes ring clear,
The picnic waits; the spoon is near.

[Bridge]
The squirrel dropped the spoon beside a hollow log,
The band played one loud note and woke a sleepy dog.
The dog returned the spoon, but took the cheese instead;
The pipers changed the march to follow where he led.

[Final Chorus]
March, march, lift the spoon,
Find it underneath the moon.
March, march, pipes ring clear,
The picnic's moving over here.
March, march, lift the spoon,
Find it underneath the moon.
March, march, pipes ring clear,
The picnic's moving over here.

[Ending]
They found a fork in the grass and called it a parade.
