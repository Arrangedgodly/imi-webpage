# Disco at the Deep Freeze

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: disco conventions, mirror-ball glamour, and the diva's demand for a spotlight*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor bass, bright strings, and handclaps; the freezer alarm sings a high counter-melody.*

Verse 1
The macaque DJ struck a pose beside the bags of peas,
A mirror ball threw silver spots across the frozen cheese.
The freezer door went open, shut, then open with the beat,
The warning bell hit one high note and stole the whole front seat.

Chorus
Flash those lights and let them ring,
Every little alarm can sing.
Raise your hands and spin around,
The coldest diva owns this sound.

Verse 2
The DJ leaned toward the alarm: "Your note is sharp and clear."
He gave it silk and satin tape and called it star of year.
The door began its shrill refrain; the dancers cried, "Encore!"
They stamped the rhythm harder till the chiller shook the floor.

Chorus
Flash those lights and let them ring,
Every little alarm can sing.
Raise your hands and spin around,
The coldest diva owns this sound.

Bridge
The gibbon manager arrived with badges marked VIP,
And sold the thawing peas as souvenirs for three bananas each.
"Limited melt!" the DJ cried. "A warm collectible!"
The shoppers wore the peas as pins; the line grew irresistible.

Verse 3
The alarm sang one more warning note; the DJ bowed and grinned,
The crowd demanded one encore, then bought the chorus in.
The peas went home in paper cups, the freezer kept its chill,
The store's new disco single topped the nighttime shopping bill.

Chorus
Flash those lights and let them ring,
Every little alarm can sing.
Raise your hands and spin around,
The coldest diva owns this sound.

Ending
The alarm got billed as headliner and signed a seven-show deal,
While the DJ's name went underneath: "Additional spinning reel."
