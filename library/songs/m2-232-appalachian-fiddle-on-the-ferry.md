# Fiddle on the Ferry Deck

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Appalachian old-time fiddle breaks, foot-stomping dance, and a ferry crossing that takes longer because the crew keeps joining the tune*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the fiddle quick and raw; let the deck bell call the band to change verses.*

[Verse 1]
A gibbon brought his fiddle to the ferry in the rain,
The deckhand rang the bell to mark the crossing of the lane.
The passengers all stamped their feet; the boards began to shake,
The captain missed the channel turn while dancing by the brake.

[Chorus]
Fiddle on the ferry, bow it fast and bright,
Stomp upon the deckboards, cross the water right.
Fiddle on the ferry, let the old tune roll,
The boat is taking circles while we reach the other shoal.

[Verse 2]
A macaque held the compass while the fiddler took a break,
The needle spun in circles from the dancing and the wake.
The captain found the shoreline and the band changed to a reel,
The ferry turned around again to keep the dancers' heel.

[Chorus]
Fiddle on the ferry, bow it fast and bright,
Stomp upon the deckboards, cross the water right.
Fiddle on the ferry, let the old tune roll,
The boat is taking circles while we reach the other shoal.

[Bridge]
The deckhand rang the bell three times; the players stopped the song,
The captain lined the ferry up and steered the boat along.
They reached the pier at sunset with the fiddle in a case,
The passengers all stepped ashore still tapping out the pace.

[Final Chorus]
Fiddle on the ferry, bow it fast and bright,
Stomp upon the deckboards, tie the ropes up tight.
Fiddle on the ferry, let the old tune roll,
The ferry made one crossing and the band reached its goal.

[Ending]
The captain took the fiddle; the band went home by road.
