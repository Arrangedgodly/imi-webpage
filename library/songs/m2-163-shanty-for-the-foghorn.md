# Shanty for the Foghorn

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sea shanty work-song call-and-response, hearty hauling choruses, and a crew that turns a fog signal into a soloist*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Stomp and handclap pulse with a strong call-and-response; the foghorn answers every chorus.*

Verse 1
The lemurs hauled the harbor rope before the fog came down,
A capuchin called "Heave together!" from the quay.
The boat was tied and safe, but no one saw the town,
The foghorn boomed a note that sent the gulls away.

Chorus
Heave, ho, the line runs low,
Blow, foghorn, blow.
Pull the rope and mind the stone,
Bring the harbor safely home.

Verse 2
The horn kept sounding after every ship was tied,
The crew sang back to count the distance from the pier.
A gibbon marked the echoes on a board beside the tide,
The horn replied so low the chalk slid from his ear.

Chorus
Heave, ho, the line runs low,
Blow, foghorn, blow.
Pull the rope and mind the stone,
Bring the harbor safely home.

Bridge
The harbor master ran to check the horn's control,
A crab had crawled inside the bell and blocked the valve.
They lifted up the panel; no one lost a sole,
The crab kept tapping time as if to lead the salve.

Verse 3
They guided every boat by lanterns through the haze,
Then shut the horn until the crab had reached the sand.
It clicked its claws in rhythm; sailors joined the phrase,
The harbor crew gave one last pull and left the line by hand.

Chorus
Heave, ho, the line runs low,
Blow, foghorn, blow.
Pull the rope and mind the stone,
Bring the harbor safely home.

Ending
The crab became the harbor band's percussion star,
The foghorn saved its breath for boats beyond the bar.
