# Afrobeat in the Garden Row

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Afrobeat ensemble grooves, interlocking guitar and horn patterns, and a gardener who tries to count every dancer instead of every sprout*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Interlocking guitars, horns, bass, and layered percussion; let the groove build evenly rather than relying on a single drop.*

Verse 1
The gardener counted seedlings in a neat and narrow row,
The band began a steady groove that made the branches glow.
A horn line crossed the guitar; the bass walked through the soil,
The gardener lost her number when the dancers joined the toil.

Chorus
Grow in the groove, roots in the ground,
Hands keep the rhythm, feet turn around.
Count every sprout and let each one rise,
Garden and music share the skies.

Verse 2
A macaque tapped the watering can; the drummer took the beat,
A gibbon turned the hose around and watered every seat.
The gardener called, "Not there! The beans are by the fence!"
The brass replied with four bright notes and kept the groove immense.

Chorus
Grow in the groove, roots in the ground,
Hands keep the rhythm, feet turn around.
Count every sprout and let each one rise,
Garden and music share the skies.

Bridge
They moved the dancers to the path and left the rows alone,
The horns kept weaving patterns while the seedlings held their own.
The gardener found her tally; every sprout was still in place,
The band gave one more chorus and the leaves began to sway.

Verse 3
They shared the garden harvest when the season turned to gold,
The band played as the baskets filled and stories all were told.
The gardener counted vegetables, then counted all the crew,
The dancers held still long enough to make the final two.

Chorus
Grow in the groove, roots in the ground,
Hands keep the rhythm, feet turn around.
Count every sprout and let each one rise,
Garden and music share the skies.

Ending
The groove went down the garden path; the seedlings stayed in line,
The gardener finished counting with the bass on time.
