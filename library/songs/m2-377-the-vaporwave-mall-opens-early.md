# The Vaporwave Mall Opens Early

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: vaporwave nostalgia for empty malls disrupted by ordinary shops opening for business*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Slowed commercial synths, washed-out drums and wistful vocals; the shutter motors interrupt the dreamy pauses.*

[Verse 1]
I wandered through the shopping hall beneath the pinkish glow,  
The fountain made a lonely sound, the music moved so slow.  
I filmed the empty escalator lifting empty space,  
The neon held a memory of a vanished modern place.  
I sang about forgotten dreams beside a shuttered store,  
The manager arrived with keys and opened up the door.  

[Chorus]
Fade away, beautiful dream,  
Float along the silent shopping stream.  
Nothing here but neon and the past,  
The empty little world was never built to last.  
Fade away, softly and low,  
The shoe-shop manager would like to know where I will go.  

[Verse 2]
The bakery began to warm the corridor with bread,  
The café rolled its tables out beside my dreaming head.  
I found a lonely corner for a melancholy shot,  
The florist tagged the bench I'd claimed for my melancholy shot.  
The fountain switched to full flow and the cleaning crew went by,  
My slowed-down love for emptiness could barely keep up time.  

[Chorus]
Fade away, beautiful dream,  
Float along the silent shopping stream.  
Nothing here but neon and the past,  
The empty little world was never built to last.  
Fade away, softly and low,  
The shoe-shop manager would like to know where I will go.  

[Bridge]
A child asked why my camera made the morning look so old,  
I showed him pink reflections and the future that they hold.  
He ran toward the bakery and waved a paper bag,  
I sped the music up; the bakery had changed the track.  

[Final Chorus]
Fade away, beautiful dream,  
Float along the noisy shopping stream.  
Everything is neon and the past,  
The new bread smells as if the morning might just last.  
Fade away, softly and low,  
I buy a bun and film the crumbs where empty spaces grow.
