# Heavy Metal at the Science Fair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: heavy metal's thunderous riffs, heroic vocal delivery, and dramatic stage theatrics*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a distorted guitar and double-kick drums; let the singer treat a modest static-electricity demo like a cosmic battle.*

[Verse 1]
A baboon built a static rig from balloons and wool,  
The fair was quiet, the gymnasium's tables full.  
A metal band arrived with amps and leather vests,  
Their lead singer declared the science would be put to test.  
The balloon rubbed against the sweater; sparks began to leap,  
The guitarist struck a power chord and woke the judges from their sleep.  
A gibbon raised her clipboard, her hair began to rise,  
The singer cried, "The charge is building; witness the skies!"  

[Chorus]
Charge the air, ignite the wire,  
Raise the hands and feed the fire.  
Rub the wool and make it spark,  
A tiny bit of static lights the dark.  
Charge the air, let thunder roll,  
The balloon has taken full control.  

[Verse 2]
The drummer hit a double kick; the paper bits took flight,  
They clung to every jacket sleeve beneath the stage lights.  
The judges leaned away as the balloons began to cling,  
The singer raised a fist and made the whole gymnasium sing.  
The student explained electrons; the amp drowned out the word,  
The band then played a solo that the science judge preferred.  
The balloon touched the metal rod; the charge was safely gone,  
The singer held a final note and kept the helmet on.  

[Chorus]
Charge the air, ignite the wire,  
Raise the hands and feed the fire.  
Rub the wool and make it spark,  
A tiny bit of static lights the dark.  
Charge the air, let thunder roll,  
The balloon has taken full control.  

[Bridge]
The judges gave a ribbon to the careful little test,  
The metal band received a nod for wearing every vest.  
The guitarist touched the balloon and felt one tiny zap,  
He leapt across the stage and landed in the snack wrap.  

[Final Chorus]
Charge the air, the demo's done,  
The science fair has been won.  
Rub the wool, then let it be,  
The balloon is calm; the singer's free.  
Charge the air, the drums still roll,  
The static has a bigger solo.  

[Ending]
The balloon got first place; the band got a static cling.
