# Glam Rock at the Costume Rack

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: glam-rock spectacle, glittering costume changes, and a singer whose boots keep getting caught on the stage curtain*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Big guitars, glittering handclaps, and theatrical stops; let the costume changes interrupt the guitar solo.*

Verse 1
The singer stepped onstage in a jacket bright as chrome,
The band struck up a glam-rock chord that shook the dressing room.
Her platform boots were shining; the curtain caught a lace,
She pulled it free with one grand pose and faced the crowd with grace.

Chorus
Shine it loud, shine it bright,
Glitter walks into the night.
Raise the chord and strike a pose,
Mind the curtain as it closes.

Verse 2
She changed from silver jacket to a cape of violet thread,
The guitarist held a solo while the stagehand moved the bed.
The cape was twice as long as her, it swept across the floor,
The drummer kicked the fabric clear and opened up the door.

Chorus
Shine it loud, shine it bright,
Glitter walks into the night.
Raise the chord and strike a pose,
Mind the curtain as it closes.

Bridge
The final costume waited on a rack beside the light,
The singer reached to grab it, but the rack rolled out of sight.
The stagehand held the casters down; the guitarist hit a break,
The singer put on both her boots and wore the cape as a cape.

Verse 3
She sang the last big chorus as the glitter filled the air,
The audience raised their hands; the curtain caught her hair.
She bowed without a tug, the stagehand freed the lace,
The band held one last guitar chord while she reclaimed her place.

Chorus
Shine it loud, shine it bright,
Glitter walks into the night.
Raise the chord and strike a pose,
Mind the curtain as it closes.

Ending
The boots were tied, the rack was braked, the costumes put away,
The singer left in ordinary shoes before the break of day.
