# Six Weeks to the Comeback

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: K-pop comeback cycles, teaser calendars, and synchronized choreography*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Bright synths, immaculate group formations, and a manager counting down between sections.*

## Verse 1
The group called Moonlit Macaques had six weeks to return.  
Their teaser photos dropped at midnight, each with something to learn.  
One member held a teacup, one stared past the lens.  
The fandom mapped the wallpaper and guessed who were their friends.  
The choreographer marked the floor with tape in neon blue.  
Eight lemurs learned the turn, then taught the turn anew.  
Their leader lost a shoe but kept the camera smile.  
The stylist pinned it back and said, "We have a while."  

## Chorus
Count it in, lights on, sharpen every line.  
Six weeks of practice for a three-minute shine.  
Count it in, chin up, make the last pose hold.  
We came back with a story, not a jacket made of gold.  

## Verse 2
The music video set was built inside a laundromat.  
A dryer turned behind them like a moonlit habitat.  
The director asked for longing, so they stared at a sock.  
The youngest member laughed and nearly broke the shot.  
They shot the bridge at dawn, the dance at half past three.  
A fan sent hand warmers for the whole choreography.  
Release day came with banners wrapped around the square.  
The members bowed to every name they saw out there.  

## Bridge
The chart may rise or stall; the lights will still go out.  
They share one quiet meal and trade the day's best shout.  
The leader saves the handwritten note beside her bed.  
It says, "Your chorus got me through a week," in blue ink red.  

## Chorus
Count it in, lights on, sharpen every line.  
Six weeks of practice for a three-minute shine.  
Count it in, chin up, make the last pose hold.  
We came back with a story, not a jacket made of gold.  

## Ending
The teaser clock reset; the group went home to sleep.  
Their fans kept humming softly, promises to keep.
