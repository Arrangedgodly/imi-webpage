# The Grand Finale Singer Finds the Coat Check

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical-theater finales that keep enlarging their emotional claims after the audience has moved on to practical concerns*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Expanding Broadway orchestration with rising key changes; coat-check numbers answer the grand vows in clipped ensemble voices.*

[Verse 1]
I stood beneath the spotlight for the final song of all,  
I promised we would change the world beyond the theater wall.  
The cast came down the staircase with their arms spread to the sky,  
We held the closing chord so long the ushers slipped outside.  
The audience rose cheering, then began to leave their rows,  
I called for one more chorus of the destiny we chose.  
The curtain came between us, but my microphone stayed live,  
I followed them into the foyer with our dreams still amplified.  

[Chorus]
Take my hand, claim the dawn,  
We have reached the final curtain but our journey carries on.  
Lift your hearts, leave your fears,  
There is someone in the foyer asking whose umbrella's here.  
Take my hand, make a start,  
I am offering forever; you are searching for a scarf.  
Rise again, stand with me,  
The coat-check line is moving to the rhythm of our dream.  

[Verse 2]
I climbed upon a little step beside the coat-check hatch,  
And sang that every ending had a future to dispatch.  
The attendant called for forty-two; a man held up his tag,  
I took his raised hand as a pledge and offered him my flag.  
He said he'd only like his coat; the girl behind him too,  
The attendant handed hangers out and called for fifty-two.  
The cast arrived to help me with an overwhelming swell,  
We blocked the ticket counter and the cloakroom rang its bell.  

[Chorus]
Take my hand, claim the dawn,  
We have reached the final curtain but our journey carries on.  
Lift your hearts, leave your fears,  
There is someone in the foyer asking whose umbrella's here.  
Take my hand, make a start,  
I am offering forever; you are searching for a scarf.  
Rise again, stand with me,  
The coat-check line is moving to the rhythm of our dream.  

[Bridge]
The attendant gave me hangers and a pile of folded coats,  
She said, "Your voice can carry numbers better than my throat."  
I sang out sixty-seven with the brass behind my back,  
The audience applauded when its owner got his hat.  

[Final Chorus]
Take my hand, claim the dawn,  
We have given out the last coat and the audience has gone.  
Lift your hearts, leave your fears,  
There is one forgotten mitten that has stayed to hear us here.  
Rise again, stand with me,  
The attendant books me back for next week's paid-up matinee.  
Take my hand, make a start,  
I have changed the world of coat check with the power of my art.
