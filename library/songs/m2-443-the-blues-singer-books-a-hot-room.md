# The Blues Singer Books a Hot Room

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: travel-weary blues songs and hotel descriptions whose promise of warmth becomes an unwelcome reality*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Slow blues guitar and a weary vocal, with doubled opening lines; a radiator's hiss answers the singer's complaints.*

[Verse 1]
I booked a cozy hotel after driving through the snow,  
I booked a cozy hotel after driving through the snow.  
They put me by the boiler where the warmest travelers go.  
I hung my coat upon a chair and settled into bed,  
I hung my coat upon a chair and settled into bed.  
The radiator hissed a tune that filled my sleepy head.  

[Refrain]
Warm room, long night, let the little valve turn,  
I came inside from winter; now the blankets have to learn.  
Warm room, long night, hear the hot pipes sigh,  
I have bought a cozy evening that won't let the cold pass by.  

[Verse 2]
I took the blanket off and set it on the empty chair,  
I took the blanket off and set it on the empty chair.  
The radiator kept the warmth and didn't want to share.  
I called the desk at midnight and described the room's fine glow,  
I called the desk at midnight and described the room's fine glow.  
They said the valve was stuck; another room had quieter flow.  

[Refrain]
Warm room, long night, let the little valve turn,  
I came inside from winter; now the blankets have to learn.  
Warm room, long night, hear the hot pipes sigh,  
I have bought a cozy evening that won't let the cold pass by.  

[Verse 3]
They moved my little suitcase to a room across the hall,  
They moved my little suitcase to a room across the hall.  
The radiator stayed quiet there; I slept through it all.  
At breakfast I described the night in one slow little rhyme,  
At breakfast I described the night in one slow little rhyme.  
The desk clerk brought a repair note and apologized in time.  

[Final Refrain]
Warm room, long night, now the little valve can turn,  
The radiator's fixed at last; the next guest needn't learn.  
Warm room, long night, let the winter pass by,  
The clerk asks for a better song about the hotel's pie.
