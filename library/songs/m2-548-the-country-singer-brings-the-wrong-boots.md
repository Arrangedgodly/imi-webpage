# The Country Singer Brings the Wrong Boots

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country concert swagger and singers discovering their packed footwear belongs to the gardening job they left behind*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Easy country beat, twangy guitar and a warm solo voice; rubber boots squeak during the instrumental break.*

[Verse 1]
I packed my boots before the gig and left the garden gate,  
The bag was on the back seat when I reached the hall quite late.  
I meant my fancy leather pair with stars upon the toe,  
Two muddy rubber gardening boots were waiting for the show.  
The band said country songs should have some honest soil within,  
The caretaker said not on the clean floor where we'd begin.  
I washed the rubber boots outside and dried them with a rag,  
The fancy pair stayed home beside the other empty bag.  

[Chorus]
Dusty road, muddy field, let the little guitar cry,  
My boots have brought the garden where the stage should have gone by.  
Dusty road, shining star, let the cowboy heart stay,  
The rubber pair has got no heels to tap the usual way.  
Big hat, clean boots, hold the country sound,  
The caretaker has checked that there's no garden left around.  

[Verse 2]
I stepped up in the rubber boots and played the opening tune,  
The soles squeaked on the stage edge halfway through the afternoon.  
The drummer caught the squeak and gave the beat a little reply,  
The dancers found a new step with the boots that wouldn't lie.  
I sang about the garden and the packing that I'd done,  
The audience clapped the muddy pair for making something fun.  
The caretaker took a photograph with every floorboard dry,  
He said he'd book the garden boots if they arrived as clean next time.  

[Chorus]
Dusty road, muddy field, let the little guitar cry,  
My boots have brought the garden where the stage should have gone by.  
Dusty road, shining star, let the cowboy heart stay,  
The rubber pair has got no heels to tap the usual way.  
Big hat, clean boots, hold the country sound,  
The caretaker has checked that there's no garden left around.  

[Bridge]
I bought two tags to label all the bags beside the door,  
One read "For the Garden," one read "For the Dancing Floor."  
The next gig had the leather pair with stars upon each toe,  
The audience asked if all the honest squeaks were in the show.  

[Final Chorus]
Dusty road, shining star, let the cowboy heart stay,  
The drummer plays the squeaking rhythm in the usual way.  
Big hat, clean boots, hold the country sound,  
The rubber pair has got its own fan club in town.  
Dusty road, muddy field, let the little guitar cry,  
I sell clean gardening boots beside the albums people buy.
