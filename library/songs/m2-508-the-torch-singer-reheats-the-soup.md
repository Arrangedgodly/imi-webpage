# The Torch Singer Reheats the Soup

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: late-night torch songs and singers interpreting a cooling dinner as another tragic departure*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Smoky piano and close melancholy vocal; the kitchen timer cuts through the final aching phrase.*

[Verse 1]
You said you'd meet me after work beside the little flame,  
I set the table carefully and wrote a verse with your name.  
The soup was in two little bowls when you phoned from the train,  
You said the line had stopped outside and you'd be late again.  
I sang about an empty chair beneath the kitchen light,  
The soup lost all its steam before the ending of the night.  
You called to say the train was moving and you'd reach the gate,  
I checked the cooling bowls and found a rather colder fate.  

[Refrain]
Come back, little warmth, come back to me,  
The soup has left its glowing place beside the kettle's sea.  
Come back, little warmth, let the evening start,  
The pot can do a thing that tears cannot do for my heart.  
Slow flame, small spoon, hold the little tune,  
My tragic empty chair may get its person very soon.  

[Verse 2]
I poured the soup back in the pot and stirred it on the hob,  
The phone rang; then the doorbell rang, and I paused my singing job.  
You came inside with wet shoes and a loaf beneath your arm,  
You asked if all the tragic music meant the soup had come to harm.  
I warmed the bowls and served it with the bread cut into two,  
You took the empty chair and heard the song I'd sung for you.  
The kitchen timer rang to tell me when to stop the heat,  
The soup had reached its final warmth; we both began to eat.  

[Refrain]
Come back, little warmth, come back to me,  
The soup has left its glowing place beside the kettle's sea.  
Come back, little warmth, let the evening start,  
The pot can do a thing that tears cannot do for my heart.  
Slow flame, small spoon, hold the little tune,  
My tragic empty chair may get its person very soon.  

[Bridge]
You said the song was lovely, though the soup was better still,  
I said the train had helped me write a very sorrowful trill.  
We kept the extra bread for all the lunches of the week,  
After dinner your damp coat took the chair, too tired to speak.  

[Final Refrain]
Come back, little warmth, come back to me,  
The pot is clean and waiting for tomorrow's little sea.  
Slow flame, small spoon, hold the little tune,  
The train app has a timetable for our next evening soon.  
Come back, little warmth, let the evening start,  
You ask if I can hum a song while serving the second part.
