# The Bluegrass Pickers Meet the Leaf Blower

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: brisk bluegrass porch sessions and instrumental competitions with a neighbor's much louder machinery*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Fast banjo, mandolin and fiddle; a leaf blower supplies an intrusive drone until its operator joins the arrangement.*

[Verse 1]
We gathered on the porch to play a little mountain tune,  
The banjo caught the fiddle in the heat of afternoon.  
Our neighbor started clearing leaves beside the garden gate,  
His blower held a single note we couldn't imitate.  
The mandolin went higher and the fiddle took a run,  
The blower stayed much louder than the fastest picking done.  
We waved across the garden; he was wearing padded ears,  
He smiled and cleared the driveway without hearing us from here.  

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, bow it bright,  
The garden has a drone that is winning every fight.  
Run those strings, tap that floor,  
The leaves have got a backing band that nobody asked for.  
Pick it quick, make it swing,  
The neighbor's little motor has a very stubborn string.  

[Verse 2]
We stopped between two numbers and I walked across the grass,  
He turned the blower off at last to hear what I would ask.  
I said we had a porch show and the audience was near,  
He said his guests were coming and the leaves must disappear.  
We took our rakes beside him and we cleared the final strip,  
The fiddle kept its case closed; the banjo saved its picks.  
The neighbor brought a lemonade and settled on the rail,  
Then asked if he could play a part; he'd sung in bands as well.  

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, bow it bright,  
The garden has a drone that is winning every fight.  
Run those strings, tap that floor,  
The leaves have got a backing band that nobody asked for.  
Pick it quick, make it swing,  
The neighbor's little motor has a very stubborn string.  

[Bridge]
He sang a low and steady bass beneath our flying tune,  
It sounded like the blower had acquired a warmer room.  
We gave him every chorus and a place beside the stand,  
The blower stayed inside the shed; the neighbor joined the band.  

[Final Chorus]
Pick it quick, bow it bright,  
The garden has a singer who can hold a note all night.  
Run those strings, tap that floor,  
His guests have brought their folding chairs and asked us all for more.  
Pick it quick, make it swing,  
We hire him for the drone and let the blower sleep till spring.
