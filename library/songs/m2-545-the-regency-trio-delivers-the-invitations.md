# The Regency Trio Delivers the Invitations

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: regency musical manners and singers whose exquisite verbal invitations never include a usable address*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Light piano and three alternating voices; invitation addresses are sung plainly after the elaborate flourishes.*

[Voice One]
Your presence is the pleasure that my little heart requires.  
[Voice Two]
Your company illuminates the hall with gentle fires.  
[Voice Three]
But which hall shall I travel to upon the chosen night?  
[Voice One]
The hall where every candle makes your lovely face look bright.  

[Verse]
We printed invitations for a dance beside the square,  
A lovely verse invited all the friends we'd like to share.  
It promised fine refreshments and a night of elegant grace,  
The printer asked which date should go beside the dancing place.  
We sang another flourish about friendship through the years,  
He held the little proof and pointed at the blank space near.  
The dance needed a date and time and address of its own,  
The poetry had taken all the room those things should've known.  

[Trio Refrain]
Come, dear friends, let the evening shine,  
The invitation wants a date beneath the lovely line.  
Come, dear friends, let the candle glow,  
A street address can tell you where the candle has to go.  
Three voices, one card, hold the graceful phrase,  
The printer needs the facts before the verse can earn its praise.  

[Voice One]
Saturday, seven, Juniper Hall.  
[Voice Two]
Twelve Market Lane, beside the wall.  
[Voice Three]
Now I can find it by the square.  
[All]
We leave the lovely verse below to say that we would like you there.  

[Verse]
The printer set the details in the card's clear upper space,  
The poet's verse went underneath with all its gentle grace.  
Our friends arrived on Saturday with everyone on time,  
They found the hall with no assistance from the fancy rhyme.  
We sang the opening welcome as the little candles shone,  
The street address stayed on the cards the guests could carry home.  
The printer came to dance and kept a card beside his chair,  
He said he'd leave the poetry, now facts were also there.  

[Final Trio Refrain]
Come, dear friends, let the evening shine,  
The date and time are printed just above the lovely line.  
Three voices, one card, hold the graceful phrase,  
The printer asks for payment before we sing his praise.  
Come, dear friends, let the candles show,  
The printer gets his proper fee; the guests arrive with grace.
