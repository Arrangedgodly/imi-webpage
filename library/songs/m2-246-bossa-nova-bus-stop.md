# Bossa Nova at the Bus Stop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bossa nova's soft syncopation and polished patience, applied to a bus that never arrives*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a gently syncopated guitar and brushed snare; keep the singer cool while the bus display grows absurd.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque at the bus stop held a coffee in the shade,  
The route sign promised "every ten," the timetable was faded.  
A gibbon strummed a nylon string with breezy, careful grace,  
While every passenger kept one eye upon the empty place.  
The screen displayed "approaching" in friendly blue,  
Then switched to "nearby" though no engine came in view.  
The singer said, "No hurry, let the morning drift along,"  
The bus app added forty minutes to the song.  

[Chorus]
Sway by the sign, let the sidewalk flow,  
The bus is on its way, though we don't know where it'll go.  
Sip your coffee, tap your shoe,  
The schedule has a rhythm and a long delay too.  
Sway by the sign, breathe out, relax,  
The route is coming through the app's abstract facts.  

[Verse 2]
A capuchin held a parasol and offered room beneath,  
The bassist played a gentle line, the drummer brushed his teeth.  
The screen said "one minute," then "arriving any day,"  
The guitarist made a little chord and let it float away.  
The passengers made small talk, then a small conga line,  
The stop announced an update: "Your bus is doing fine."  

[Chorus]
Sway by the sign, let the sidewalk flow,  
The bus is on its way, though we don't know where it'll go.  
Sip your coffee, tap your shoe,  
The schedule has a rhythm and a long delay too.  
Sway by the sign, breathe out, relax,  
The route is coming through the app's abstract facts.  

[Bridge]
The actual bus went past them on the far side of the street,  
The band kept playing softly with a syncopated beat.  
They crossed at the light together, still calm and unafraid,  
The driver on the far side waved as they all waved.  

[Final Chorus]
Sway by the sign, the ride is here,  
The driver has arrived in a very different gear.  
Lift your bags and step aboard,  
The app can update while we move toward the door.  
Sway by the sign, the band plays through,  
The bus has found its passengers; the screen is still "near you."  

[Ending]
The guitar kept its cool; the bus stop changed to "service resumed."
