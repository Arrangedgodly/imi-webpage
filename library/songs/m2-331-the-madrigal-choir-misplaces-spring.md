# The Madrigal Choir Misplaces Spring

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: pastoral madrigals whose elaborate seasonal imagery survives a mistaken calendar*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Five interweaving voices; every fa-la phrase ends with one singer shivering.*

[Verse 1]
Come see the laughing meadow where the tender blossoms wake,  
The alto lifts a frozen foot above a frozen lake.  
The tenor praises swallows circling softly overhead,  
A crow lands on the music stand and eats our daily bread.  
The bass unfolds the calendar our leader packed last night,  
The page says May in fancy gold, the year is not quite right.  
Our leader shakes his garland till the little bells all ring,  
He tells us to ignore the frost and concentrate on spring.  

[Refrain]
Fa la, the roses bloom,  
Fa la, beside the boiler room.  
Fa la, the sweet birds call,  
That crow has stolen lunch from all.  
Sing soft, sing clear, sing green,  
The meadow is the whitest green our little choir has seen.  

[Verse 2]
The soprano lifts her basket for the petals drifting down,  
A clump of snow falls from a branch and knocks away her crown.  
The alto sings of gentle rain and points toward the stream,  
The tenor skates across it while composing summer's dream.  
Our leader wants a shepherd with a flute among the trees,  
The shepherd left in autumn with his sheep and unpaid fees.  
The bass produces mittens from his tunic's secret fold,  
We harmonize in woolen gloves and call the weather gold.  

[Refrain]
Fa la, the roses bloom,  
Fa la, beside the boiler room.  
Fa la, the sweet birds call,  
That crow has stolen lunch from all.  
Sing soft, sing clear, sing green,  
The meadow is the whitest green our little choir has seen.  

[Bridge]
The gardener comes trudging with a shovel and a sack,  
He takes the garland off our chief and hangs it on his back.  
He leads us to the greenhouse where the seedlings make a row,  
We crowd beside one daffodil and sing until we glow.  

[Final Refrain]
Fa la, the roses bloom,  
Fa la, inside this heated room.  
Fa la, the sweet birds call,  
The crow has followed us inside the hall.  
Sing soft, sing clear, sing green,  
Our springtime concert costs twelve coins to heat the little scene.
