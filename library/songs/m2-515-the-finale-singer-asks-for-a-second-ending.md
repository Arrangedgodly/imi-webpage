# The Finale Singer Asks for a Second Ending

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical theater finale songs and stars refusing to let a neatly closed plot end the performance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Bright stage piano and ensemble harmony; the stage manager speaks from the wings during each attempted extension.*

[Verse 1]
The lovers were together and the villain had gone home,  
The garden had its flowers and the hero had his throne.  
The orchestra played the final chord and everybody bowed,  
I asked them for another verse to thank the lovely crowd.  
The stage manager signaled that the hall was closing soon,  
I sang about the endless joy beneath the painted moon.  
The orchestra had packed its music underneath the chairs,  
My endless joy went wandering with no melody to share.  

[Chorus]
One more ending, one more line,  
Every little story has another place to shine.  
One more ending, let the curtains stay,  
The villain's gone to get his coat and cannot have a say.  
Bright moon, happy hearts, hold the last small chord,  
The orchestra would like the ending written on the board.  

[Verse 2]
The pianist stayed to give me one short phrase upon the keys,  
The manager asked me gently if I'd finish now, please.  
I thanked the cast and crew by name and sang a four-bar close,  
The curtain came between my face and everybody's rows.  
The villain came back from the coats to help us clear the scene,  
The lovers folded up the flowers painted bright and green.  
I put the thank-you verse on paper for tomorrow's final bow,  
The manager marked four bars and asked if I could count them now.  

[Chorus]
One more ending, one more line,  
Every little story has another place to shine.  
One more ending, let the curtains stay,  
The villain's gone to get his coat and cannot have a say.  
Bright moon, happy hearts, hold the last small chord,  
The orchestra would like the ending written on the board.  

[Bridge]
Tomorrow all the orchestra played the same four bars,  
The curtain closed on schedule under all the painted stars.  
The crowd applauded after it and no one asked for more,  
The manager held open the ordinary backstage door.  

[Final Chorus]
One short ending, one last line,  
The four-bar thanks has got a proper place to shine.  
Bright moon, happy hearts, hold the last small chord,  
The orchestra has got the written ending on its board.  
One short ending, let the curtains fall,  
I sing my second encore to the coat hooks in the hall.
