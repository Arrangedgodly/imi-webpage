# The Meter's Polka

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: novelty polka bounce, comic couplets, and a parking meter that keeps joining the dance instead of counting time*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Use accordion and brisk oom-pah; add a coin jingle on each refrain.*

[Verse 1]
A lemur fed a nickel to the meter by the square,
The meter made a polka sound and spun around its chair.
The band struck up a two-step; the meter swung its arm,
It stamped a little ticket that said, "Dance, but do no harm."

[Chorus]
Oom-pah, jingle, turn around, give the meter room,
It counts the coins in quarter time and dances to the tune.
Oom-pah, jingle, heel and toe, don't block the curb today,
The meter wants a partner more than it wants your pay.

[Verse 2]
The driver fed another coin; the meter bowed so low,
The ticket curled like curling ribbon and began to tap its toe.
The band played faster; every car began to rock;
The meter called a parking spot and led the lamppost polka.

[Chorus]
Oom-pah, jingle, turn around, give the meter room,
It counts the coins in quarter time and dances to the tune.
Oom-pah, jingle, heel and toe, don't block the curb today,
The meter wants a partner more than it wants your pay.

[Bridge]
The city clerk came marching with a regulation book;
The meter spun him gently round before he got a look.
He tore out one blank page and waved it like a flag;
The meter stopped its dancing when the band packed up its bag.

[Final Chorus]
Oom-pah, jingle, turn around, give the meter room,
It counts the coins in quarter time and dances to the tune.
Oom-pah, jingle, heel and toe, let every driver stay,
The meter wants a dance break; it can count the rest of day.

[Ending]
The clerk put one coin in. The meter danced him home.
