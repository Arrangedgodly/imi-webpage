# The Villain Song Orders a Nameplate

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: lavish animated villain songs whose declarations of power become a surprisingly fussy stationery order*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Brass stabs, sinister strings and a gleeful theatrical voice; the sign maker answers with cheerful shop-counter professionalism.*

[Verse 1]
I built a tower on the cliff and dressed in royal black,  
The servants had a greeting for the day that I came back.  
I needed one brass nameplate for the entrance to my lair,  
So I marched into the sign shop with a very dreadful glare.  
I sang "Supreme Controller of the Lands Beneath the Moon,"  
The maker said the plate I'd picked could hold a shorter tune.  
I said my title carried fear to every living soul,  
He said a wider plate would cost me twice as much as gold.  

[Chorus]
Bow to my name, tremble at the sign,  
The letter count is frightening and the shop charges by the line.  
Bow to my reign, hear the thunder grow,  
The maker wants a deposit before any letters show.  
Dark cape, bright brass, let the whole world see,  
There is no room for "Supreme" upon the plate he showed to me.  

[Verse 2]
I shortened it to "Dark Lord" with a formidable grin,  
The maker asked which font would let the daylight travel in.  
I chose the sharpest letters, like the teeth of hungry beasts,  
He said they might be hard to read from halfway down the street.  
I settled on a plain bold face and paid the printed bill,  
The maker promised Friday if the workshop's stock held still.  
I waited in my tower with an empty entrance door,  
The servants called me "Pending" till the sign arrived at four.  

[Chorus]
Bow to my name, tremble at the sign,  
The letter count is frightening and the shop charges by the line.  
Bow to my reign, hear the thunder grow,  
The maker wants a deposit before any letters show.  
Dark cape, bright brass, let the whole world see,  
There is no room for "Supreme" upon the plate he showed to me.  

[Bridge]
He fixed the finished nameplate to the entrance of the tower,  
The servants read it clearly and admired its shining power.  
He asked if I would recommend the work to friends I knew,  
I said my friends were enemies; he said he'd serve them too.  

[Final Chorus]
Bow to my name, tremble at the sign,  
The entrance has two words upon a very tidy line.  
Dark cape, bright brass, let the whole world see,  
The maker left his little shop sticker underneath me.  
Bow to my reign, hear the thunder grow,  
The neighboring hero orders one; the maker quotes him less.
