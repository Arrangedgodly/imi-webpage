# The Wave Ate the Set List

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: surf-rock instrumentals, reverb-drenched beach bravado, and a set list written too close to the tide*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Use reverb and a bright guitar riff; let the tide interrupt the solo twice.*

[Verse 1]
The band set up at sunrise where the breakers met the sand,
A gibbon tuned his guitar with a shell held in his hand.
Their set list lay beside him, weighted with a stone;
The tide crept up the paper while he played a rolling tone.

[Chorus]
Ride the wave, ride the wave, the guitar goes wah,
Keep the amp above the foam and the set list where you saw.
Ride the wave, ride the wave, the drum goes boom,
The ocean took the second verse and left the chorus room.

[Verse 2]
The first wave took the paper; the second took the stone.
The drummer chased the pages as they surfed the shallows on.
The singer raised his board and called the swell a fan;
A crab tapped out the backbeat on the guitarist's can.

[Chorus]
Ride the wave, ride the wave, the guitar goes wah,
Keep the amp above the foam and the set list where you saw.
Ride the wave, ride the wave, the drum goes boom,
The ocean took the second verse and left the chorus room.

[Bridge]
They moved the amps uphill, but the reverb filled the bay;
A lifeguard asked for quiet; the echo sang, "Okay."
The band played one last riff while the crab reclaimed the can;
The wave rolled out with half the song and most of their sand.

[Final Chorus]
Ride the wave, ride the wave, the guitar goes wah,
Keep the amp above the foam and the set list where you saw.
Ride the wave, ride the wave, the drum goes boom,
The ocean took the second verse and left the chorus room.

[Ending]
At low tide, the missing set list came back with a review in shells.
