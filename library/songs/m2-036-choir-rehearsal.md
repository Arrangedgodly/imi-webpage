# Tenors on the Fire Escape

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: community choir rehearsal conventions and neighborhood acoustics*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Four-part harmony begins indoors, then follows the tenor out to the fire escape.*

## Verse 1
The choir met each Thursday in a room above the store.  
The tenor missed the entrance, then apologized once more.  
The director raised a hand and held the chord in place.  
A soprano smiled and gave him time to find his space.  
Outside, the fire escape was narrow, painted green.  
A tenor stepped out to cool his face between.  
The others sang the phrase to reach him through the glass.  
The harmony slid down the alley as the buses passed.  

## Chorus
Hold the note, hold the door, let a voice return.  
A choir learns by listening while the old pipes burn.  
Hold the note, breathe in, answer when you can.  
No one has to carry every part alone again.  

## Verse 2
A neighbor opened up a window on the second floor.  
She knew the song from church, though she'd not sung before.  
She took the low line, steady as the street.  
A passing cyclist matched her rhythm with his feet.  
The tenor found his entrance from the stair.  
He sang the high note, clear against the air.  
The director's hand came down; the chord resolved.  
The neighbor clapped once, and the alley's hush dissolved.  

## Bridge
They brought a chair outside the next week to rehearse.  
The tenor stayed inside and sang the lower verse.  
The neighbor came again with tea for all.  
The bus went by, and carried on their call.  

## Chorus
Hold the note, hold the door, let a voice return.  
A choir learns by listening while the old pipes burn.  
Hold the note, breathe in, answer when you can.  
No one has to carry every part alone again.  

## Ending
The store below turned off its sign at ten.  
The choir went home, then met the next Thursday again.
