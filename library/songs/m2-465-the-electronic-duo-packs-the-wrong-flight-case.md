# The Electronic Duo Packs the Wrong Flight Case

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: electronic live shows whose sleek minimal appearance depends on an enormous and easily confused equipment case*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Dry electronic pulse and two deadpan voices; the case latches click open to reveal a very different sort of performance equipment.*

[Verse 1]
We brought the silver flight case to the club before the show,  
Its handles held the future of the beats we'd come to grow.  
We opened up the latches of our case of polished chrome,  
The caterer's bright champagne flutes were nested in the foam.  
The case belonged to catering from yesterday's affair,  
Our silver cases matched; we grabbed the wrong one there.  
The club owner looked closely and he asked about our act,  
We said the instruments had taken a somewhat different track.  

[Chorus]
Clean lines, deep pulse, minimal display,  
The equipment is a glassware set that cannot play the way.  
Bright case, soft foam, let the latches shine,  
The future has twelve champagne flutes and not one MIDI line.  
Clean lines, deep pulse, hear the silent night,  
We packed the case by color and forgot to read it right.  

[Verse 2]
We called the caterer at once; she had our keyboard case,  
She'd opened it for serving and had found an unexpected place.  
The keyboards couldn't hold her drinks but kept their foam quite clean,  
She put them back and drove to us with all the gear between.  
We swapped the silver cases at the loading door downstairs,  
She took the glasses to her van and checked that each was there.  
We carried up the keyboards and we wired the little stand,  
The club owner poured two waters and requested we read the brand.  

[Chorus]
Clean lines, deep pulse, minimal display,  
The equipment is a glassware set that cannot play the way.  
Bright case, soft foam, let the latches shine,  
The future has twelve champagne flutes and not one MIDI line.  
Clean lines, deep pulse, hear the silent night,  
We packed the case by color and forgot to read it right.  

[Bridge]
We played a shortened opening and the room began to dance,  
The caterer came back later when she had a little chance.  
She said our keyboards had no labels visible from the hall,  
We wrote our name on both sides in the biggest letters of all.  

[Final Chorus]
Clean lines, deep pulse, minimal display,  
The equipment is a keyboard set that plays the proper way.  
Bright case, soft foam, let the latches shine,  
The caterer has her glassware and we have our MIDI line.  
Clean lines, deep pulse, hear the little phrase,  
The club owner marks our water cups so we don't pack them away.
