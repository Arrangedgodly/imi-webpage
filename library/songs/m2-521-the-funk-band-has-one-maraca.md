# The Funk Band Has One Maraca

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: funk bands boasting of irresistible synchronized motion while their percussion arrives one shaker short*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Syncopated bass, dry drums and a clipped group hook; the percussionist alternates one maraca between hands.*

[Verse 1]
The funk band had a groove that put a bounce inside the hall,  
The bassist smiled at everyone and called them to the floor.  
The percussion case was open with one maraca inside,  
The other lay at home where all the packing plans had died.  
Our shaker player lifted both his hands above his head,  
One rattled out a lively beat; the other danced instead.  
The singer watched the empty hand and asked about the style,  
He called it silent percussion and he wore a serious smile.  

[Chorus]
Shake right, move left, put the pocket in the sound,  
One maraca has a voice; the other hand goes round.  
Shake right, move left, let the bass line go,  
The empty hand has choreography that only it can show.  
Low beat, bright shake, keep the rhythm tight,  
Half the shaker pair is working very hard tonight.  

[Verse 2]
He swapped the shaker hand when all the instruments took a break,  
The new hand found the rhythm with a much more rested shake.  
The singer called the left side and the dancers raised their arms,  
The silent right hand kept its grace with empty little palms.  
The drummer added hi-hat notes where both hands used to meet,  
The single shaker kept its place beside the brighter beat.  
The crowd began to copy all the empty-handed moves,  
The percussion player had a dance beside his single groove.  

[Chorus]
Shake right, move left, put the pocket in the sound,  
One maraca has a voice; the other hand goes round.  
Shake right, move left, let the bass line go,  
The empty hand has choreography that only it can show.  
Low beat, bright shake, keep the rhythm tight,  
Half the shaker pair is working very hard tonight.  

[Bridge]
Next night he brought both shakers and the dancer crowd complained,  
They wanted one free hand to show the movement they'd obtained.  
He put one in the case again and raised the empty side,  
The extra shaker stayed at hand, prepared to stand beside.  

[Final Chorus]
Shake right, move left, let the bass line go,  
The silent hand has merchandise on a little poster show.  
Low beat, bright shake, keep the rhythm tight,  
The spare maraca earns no fee for waiting out of sight.  
Shake right, move left, let the whole room sway,  
The singer books the empty hand for a dancing class next day.
