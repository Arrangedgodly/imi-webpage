# Neon Heart in Triplicate

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: polished boy-band harmonies and choreographed sincerity*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
We met you by the vending machine at noon.  
Five matching langurs entered out of tune.  
I held the gaze; my knees held choreography.  
The others formed a heart around the coffee.  
I said your name in four-part harmony.  
My shoe came loose; the move was still on beat.  
Our manager yelled, "Emotion, but stay neat."  
We sang your favorite snack was something sweet.  

## Chorus
You make my neon heart go click, click, glow.  
We practiced every feeling in a row.  
I'm yours in every carefully styled way.  
The fifth voice takes the high note anyway.  

## Verse 2
We bought one scarf and passed it down the line.  
Each member wore it for a bar of nine.  
The fan club waved with synchronized delight.  
Our left-side member missed the turn to right.  
He spun into a stack of folding chairs.  
We kept the chorus floating up the stairs.  
Your note said, "Could you all please stop at four?"  
The group rehearsed a softer exit door.  

## Chorus
You make my neon heart go click, click, glow.  
We practiced every feeling in a row.  
I'm yours in every carefully styled way.  
The fifth voice takes the high note anyway.  

## Bridge
I asked the boys to let me talk alone.  
They formed a primate wall beside my phone.  
I took a breath; they breathed in major thirds.  
I said, "I like you," without backup words.  
The silence lasted nearly half a beat.  
One member shuffled backwards with his feet.  
You smiled and asked if I could dance.  
The whole group waited for our chance.  

## Final chorus
You make my neon heart go click, click, glow.  
We'll leave the matching jackets in a row.  
I'm yours in every un-rehearsed way.  
The fifth voice takes the high note anyway.  
You make my neon heart go click, click, bright.  
No scarf, no formation, no manager in sight.  
If I miss the step, just laugh with me.  
The group can hum from down the street.  

## Ending
We bowed in sequence, then went home.  
I left my jacket by the phone.  
The others practiced in the hall.  
I met you there, and missed the call.  

*Performance note: Use crisp group vowels and one conspicuously late dancer; the confession should sound almost private.*
