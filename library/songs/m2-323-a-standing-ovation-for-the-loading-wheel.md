# A Standing Ovation for the Loading Wheel

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: arena power ballads that treat a stalled presentation as an emotional triumph*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Piano opening, enormous drum entrance, and a choir sustaining the word loading.*

[Verse 1]
The curtain rose upon a screen, the room was velvet dark,  
Our baboon stepped into the light to show his life's great work.  
He pressed the silver button and a wheel began to turn,  
A thousand people waited for the lesson they would learn.  
His opening slide was somewhere in a distant server rack,  
He told the room to trust the journey, leaning further back.  
The drummer saw him lift his arms and thundered on the snare,  
A spinning circle glowed behind the proudly empty air.  

[Chorus]
Turn for me, little wheel, turn for all we've been,  
Every revolution brings the future to the screen.  
We will raise our lighters till the final file arrives,  
You have made a waiting room of all our hopeful lives.  
Hold that note, hold that light, hold the spacebar down,  
There's a standing ovation for the loading wheel in town.  

[Verse 2]
The lighting monkey painted blue across the cheering rows,  
The singer held a microphone beneath his flaring nose.  
He sang of every mountain that his data had to climb,  
The wheel maintained a steady beat and very little time.  
A lemur in the balcony called out to try refresh,  
He said she couldn't hurry all the wisdom in this mesh.  
The choir wore their graduation robes and sang in perfect thirds,  
The only text upon the screen was two unchanging words.  

[Chorus]
Turn for me, little wheel, turn for all we've been,  
Every revolution brings the future to the screen.  
We will raise our lighters till the final file arrives,  
You have made a waiting room of all our hopeful lives.  
Hold that note, hold that light, hold the spacebar down,  
There's a standing ovation for the loading wheel in town.  

[Bridge]
The cleaner found the network lead unplugged behind a chair,  
She pushed it in; the wheel stopped dead; the crowd inhaled the air.  
The slide appeared: THANK YOU FOR YOUR PATIENCE, NOTHING MORE.  
He bowed as if the message was the thing they'd waited for.  

[Final Chorus]
Turn for me, little wheel, turn for all we've been,  
Every revolution brings the future to the screen.  
We have raised our lighters till the final file arrived,  
You have made a waiting room of all our hopeful lives.  
Hold that note, hold that light, give the band its crown,  
He clicks the encore file and brings the loading wheel back round.
