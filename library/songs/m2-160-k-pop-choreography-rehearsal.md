# K-Pop at the Choreography Rehearsal

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: K-pop's synchronized formations, camera-aware gestures, and rehearsal perfectionism*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Bright synth-pop beat with crisp counts; the camera operator keeps changing the marks.*

Verse 1
Five lemurs lined up shoulder to shoulder in the studio light,
The choreographer called the count and spun them left then right.
A camera slid along the floor to catch their perfect turn,
The tape marks shifted underneath; the dancers had to learn.

Chorus
Point, pose, sparkle, freeze,
Hit the count with perfect ease.
Step in line and face the light,
Turn together, keep it tight.

Verse 2
The center singer held her smile; the others formed a V,
A macaque on the camera rig said, "Make the V face me."
They shifted into two neat rows; the rig moved down the track,
The whole formation followed it and left the stage out back.

Chorus
Point, pose, sparkle, freeze,
Hit the count with perfect ease.
Step in line and face the light,
Turn together, keep it tight.

Bridge
The choreographer yelled, "Reset!"; the crew moved every mark,
The dancers made a heart shape in the doorway to the dark.
The camera spun around them; they hit each pose on cue,
The assistant filmed the empty stage and called it take two.

Verse 3
They found the right position when the studio clock struck four,
The singer pressed the playback; the camera filmed the door.
The fans saw perfect empty space and called the timing fine,
The crew sold tape-mark merchandise: "Get yours and join the line."

Chorus
Point, pose, sparkle, freeze,
Hit the count with perfect ease.
Step in line and face the light,
Turn together, keep it tight.

Ending
The video went viral, though the stars were out of frame;
The crew released a dance tutorial using tape marks with their name.
