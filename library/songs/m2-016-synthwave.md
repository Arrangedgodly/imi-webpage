# Arcade at the Last Exit

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: synthwave nostalgia, neon highway imagery, and analog-digital contrast*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Use a gated snare, warm analog pads, and a coin drop before the final refrain.*

## Verse 1
The arcade at the last exit stayed open past the snow.  
A lemur fed one token to a cabinet's red glow.  
The screen drew purple mountains, grids that stretched away.  
His jacket caught the neon like a late freeway.  
A cassette deck spun beside the prize machine.  
The soundtrack made the parking lot look cinematic and clean.  
His sister kept the score on paper, not the cloud.  
She circled every high score, small but proud.  

## Chorus
Drive through the midnight, pixels in the glass.  
The old machine remembers every race you didn't pass.  
Drive through the midnight, hold that signal bright.  
The exit sign is glowing like a promise for the night.  

## Verse 2
The owner said the cabinet would be gone by spring.  
The sister said, "Then let's record its opening ring."  
They filmed the buttons, scuffs, the coin return's click.  
The lemur beat the record with a lucky trick.  
The screen flashed winner, then its colors went to black.  
The room stayed full of music after power came back.  
They took the cassette and left the score behind.  
A kid found it next day and tried to top the line.  

## Bridge
Some things keep their glow when nobody can play.  
Some things need a witness to remember yesterday.  
The sister kept the tape beside her driving map.  
The lemur kept one token tucked inside his cap.  

## Chorus
Drive through the midnight, pixels in the glass.  
The old machine remembers every race you didn't pass.  
Drive through the midnight, hold that signal bright.  
The exit sign is glowing like a promise for the night.  

## Ending
The arcade shut in April; a bakery moved inside.  
They played one last cassette on the road outside.
