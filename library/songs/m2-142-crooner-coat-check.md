# Crooner at the Coat Check

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: crooner standards, velvet phrasing, and a singer whose coat-check ticket keeps changing hands during the romantic number*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Smooth crooner vocal over brushed drums and piano; stretch the final phrase while the coat ticket changes owners.*

Verse 1
The singer took the microphone beneath a soft gold light,
He sang of longing in a room that glittered through the night.
He checked his coat before the song and tucked away the tag,
A capuchin took the ticket with a very formal bow.

Chorus
Hold my coat, hold my place,
Keep the ticket close and safe.
When the final note is through,
I will come and find you.

Verse 2
The singer crooned a tender line; the band played soft and slow,
The capuchin passed the ticket to a gibbon by the door.
The gibbon held it near the stage and swayed to every rhyme,
The singer finished one refrain, then asked if this was time.

Chorus
Hold my coat, hold my place,
Keep the ticket close and safe.
When the final note is through,
I will come and find you.

Bridge
The singer asked the coat clerk; the clerk said, "Tag is blue."
The capuchin had a red one; the gibbon had tag two.
They checked the coat rack slowly; one sleeve hung on the rail,
The coat had gone to wardrobe for a missing button repair.

Verse 3
The band began a second song while the clerk found the coat,
The singer held a velvet note and buttoned up his throat.
The capuchin reclaimed the tag; the gibbon took a bow,
The singer wore his coat again and sang the final vow.

Chorus
Hold my coat, hold my place,
Keep the ticket close and safe.
When the final note is through,
I will come and find you.

Ending
The coat was found, the band was done, the singer left the room,
The coat-check clerk kept one last note and hummed it through the gloom.
