# The Ceremonial Choir Salutes the Sale Flag

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ceremonial pageantry and choirs finding that an enormous fluttering flag is an advertisement rather than their emblem*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Bright brass, snare and dignified choir; the shop owner supplies a spoken price during the refrain.*

[Verse 1]
The choir stood beside the square beneath the rising pole,  
We sang about the town's bright future with our hearts made whole.  
A flag unfurled above us with a stripe of golden sun,  
We raised our voices proudly as the opening was begun.  
The flag read "Sale this Saturday" in letters bold and high,  
The shop had rented that same pole to tell the passersby.  
The choir had arrived beside the wrong ceremonial place,  
The town emblem was across the square above a smaller space.  

[Chorus]
Raise the flag, let the morning glow,  
The choir has a stirring tune for sofas priced quite low.  
Raise the flag, let the voices shine,  
The golden stripe has got a sale until a quarter after nine.  
Brass bright, hearts high, let the little square hear,  
The shop owner asks if we'd announce the offer clear.  

[Verse 2]
The leader stopped the opening and we crossed the square in pairs,  
The proper emblem waited by the mayor's little chairs.  
We sang the town's bright future to the gathered morning crowd,  
The shop flag waved across from us with letters still quite loud.  
The mayor gave the opening and the fair began its day,  
The shop owner asked the leader if we'd sing his sale away.  
She said a short commercial tune would need a separate fee,  
He booked a little jingle for the following week to be.  

[Chorus]
Raise the flag, let the morning glow,  
The choir has a stirring tune for sofas priced quite low.  
Raise the flag, let the voices shine,  
The golden stripe has got a sale until a quarter after nine.  
Brass bright, hearts high, let the little square hear,  
The shop owner asks if we'd announce the offer clear.  

[Bridge]
We wrote a tune about the shop with every price quite plain,  
The owner played it in the door beside the windowpane.  
The mayor bought a folding chair to use at next year's fair,  
The choir used the fee to buy a stand for music there.  

[Final Chorus]
Raise the flag, let the voices shine,  
The town's own emblem has a different pole from the discount sign.  
Brass bright, hearts high, let the little square hear,  
The shop gets an advert with no ceremony near.  
Raise the flag, let the morning glow,  
The mayor folds his bargain chair and asks the choir to go.
