# The Pub Chorus Cannot Finish the Toast

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: exuberant pub singalongs whose endless toasts keep the singers from ever drinking the drinks they raise*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Fiddle, stomping feet and a hearty group chorus; every new dedication restarts the raised-glass pose.*

[Verse 1]
We gathered at the corner table after closing time,  
The landlord poured the final round and asked us for a rhyme.  
We sang a toast to everyone who kept the village bright,  
The baker and the butcher and the farmer on our right.  
The fiddler raised his little glass and led a mighty cheer,  
The singer named the postman before anyone could drink their beer.  
We added in the teacher and the cobbler by the stair,  
Our arms remained uplifted while our drinks warmed in the air.  

[Chorus]
Raise it high, keep it there,  
We have got another toast for every soul upon the square.  
Cheers to all, sing it loud,  
There is half a village waiting to be honored by the crowd.  
Raise it high, do not sip,  
The singer has a list of names that hasn't left his lip.  

[Verse 2]
The landlord said the drinks were getting warmer by the bar,  
We sang a toast to cold beer and the man who'd carried ours.  
The baker asked about his wife; we'd named his shop alone,  
We sang a little verse for her and every loaf she'd thrown.  
The fiddler put his glass down while he played a little break,  
The singer saw him lower it and cried, "For goodness' sake!"  
We toasted healthy shoulders and the strength to lift a cup,  
The landlord took a chalkboard and he wrote the names all up.  

[Chorus]
Raise it high, keep it there,  
We have got another toast for every soul upon the square.  
Cheers to all, sing it loud,  
There is half a village waiting to be honored by the crowd.  
Raise it high, do not sip,  
The singer has a list of names that hasn't left his lip.  

[Bridge]
The landlord said, "To all of them," and drank his little glass,  
We followed him in grateful silence; finally it passed.  
The singer took a breath to praise the landlord's clever hand,  
The fiddler stuffed a sandwich in his mouth before he sang.  

[Final Chorus]
Raise it high, keep it there,  
We salute the sandwich that has spared our tired arms the air.  
Cheers to all, let it end,  
The village gets one final wave from every seated friend.  
Raise it high, now put it down,  
The singer's chewing slowly; we can finally leave the pub.
