# Cumbia in the Crust

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cumbia dance groove, repeating percussion, and a bakery shift that keeps turning every tray into a dance partner*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the percussion steady; let the oven timer punctuate each chorus.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque baker set the dough beside the warming stone,
The cumbia band began to play, and nobody stood alone.
The trays began to shuffle as the dancers found their feet,
The oven timer answered with a bright and steady beat.

[Chorus]
Turn that tray, turn that tray, let the warm rolls rise,
Step to the left, step to the right, flour in our eyes.
Turn that tray, turn that tray, the rhythm's in the bread,
The oven says the batch is done; the whole floor turns its head.

[Verse 2]
A capuchin took one hot roll and balanced it in air,
The drummer hit a double beat; the roll went everywhere.
It landed in a basket and the dancers formed a line,
They passed it hand to hand and kept the cumbia time.

[Chorus]
Turn that tray, turn that tray, let the warm rolls rise,
Step to the left, step to the right, flour in our eyes.
Turn that tray, turn that tray, the rhythm's in the bread,
The oven says the batch is done; the whole floor turns its head.

[Bridge]
The baker called for cooling; the dancers stopped in place,
The rolls kept sliding forward in a soft and saucy race.
They caught them with a napkin and set them on the rack,
The band resumed the groove, and the oven called them back.

[Final Chorus]
Turn that tray, turn that tray, let the warm rolls rise,
Step to the left, step to the right, no flour in our eyes.
Turn that tray, turn that tray, the rhythm's in the bread,
The baker sells the batch; the dancers turn instead.

[Ending]
The last roll got a ribbon and sat beside the band.
