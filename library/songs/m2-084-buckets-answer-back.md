# The Buckets Answer Back

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: call-and-response work songs for a water crew, stubborn bucket echoes, and a foreman answered by the well*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Work-song pulse with claps and bucket taps; crew and foreman trade calls, then the well joins.*

Verse 1
The crew lined up at sunrise with a bucket in each hand,
A young gibbon raised the water rope and called across the land: "Lift!"
The capuchins replied, "We lift!" and pulled the creaking line,
The well replied with one wet plunk and kept its own time.

Chorus
Pull together, pass it down,
Carry water through the town.
Tap the rim and keep the beat,
Every bucket finds its feet.

Verse 2
The foreman called, "Who carries two?" The macaques answered, "We!"
The well called back, "Who carries three?" then splashed the foreman's knee.
He called, "The rope is getting short!" The crew called, "Tie it tight!"
The well replied with dripping notes that echoed through the night.

Chorus
Pull together, pass it down,
Carry water through the town.
Tap the rim and keep the beat,
Every bucket finds its feet.

Bridge
One bucket had a hole in it; the crew called, "Set it by!"
The well replied with one more plunk and splashed them from below.
They patched the rim with woven grass and set it on the track,
The bucket made a hollow drum and called the chorus back.

Verse 3
The line moved down the path at last; the garden drank its fill,
The crew set down their buckets as the sun slid past the hill.
The foreman called, "The work is done!" The well called, "Not quite!"
A final drop hit one small drum and made the last beat right.

Chorus
Pull together, pass it down,
Carry water through the town.
Tap the rim and keep the beat,
Every bucket finds its feet.

Ending
They left the well a bucket drum, then went back home to eat,
The well replied with one last plunk, then rested in the beat.
