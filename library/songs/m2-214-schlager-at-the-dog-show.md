# A Ribbon for My Beagle

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: schlager pop hooks, cheerful rhymes, and a dog-show contestant whose animal prefers the snack table to the runway*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the melody bright and direct; let the dog bark on the final beat of every chorus.*

[Verse 1]
I, Miri the macaque, brushed my beagle's ears until they gleamed like bows,
I pinned a yellow ribbon where his little collar goes.
The judges called us forward and the music filled the hall,
My beagle sniffed the snack table and ignored the call.

[Chorus]
A ribbon for my beagle, a ribbon bright and blue,
He walked the runway sideways with a biscuit in his view.
A ribbon for my beagle, the crowd began to cheer,
He took the ribbon in his teeth and paraded out of here.

[Verse 2]
I led him round the markers; he stopped beside the judge,
He offered her a biscuit and refused to walk or budge.
The band began its chorus; he barked the harmony,
The judge awarded "best in snacks" and handed him a tea.

[Chorus]
A ribbon for my beagle, a ribbon bright and blue,
He walked the runway sideways with a biscuit in his view.
A ribbon for my beagle, the crowd began to cheer,
He took the ribbon in his teeth and paraded out of here.

[Bridge]
The beagle took the ribbon to the waiting snack display,
He tied it to the biscuit tin and guarded it all day.
I tried to take him home, but he sat down on the floor,
The judges named him "best in show" for loving snacks galore.

[Final Chorus]
A ribbon for my beagle, a ribbon bright and blue,
He walked the runway sideways with a biscuit in his view.
A ribbon for my beagle, the crowd began to cheer,
He carried home the snack tin and we left the ribbon here.

[Ending]
The beagle slept on the prize; I wore his ribbon home.
