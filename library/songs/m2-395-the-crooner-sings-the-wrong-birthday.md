# The Crooner Sings the Wrong Birthday

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: suave lounge crooners who treat dedication errors as opportunities for even more confident improvisation*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Warm brass and brushed drums behind an unhurried croon; increasingly specific birthday corrections come from the table nearest the piano.*

[Verse 1]
I held the microphone like velvet in my dinner-coated hand,  
The waiter passed a folded note beside the baby grand.  
I sang for lovely Nora and her twenty-first bright year,  
A woman at the corner table said, "I'm sixty, dear."  
I smiled and changed my lyric to a wisdom-rich refrain,  
The man beside her said, "It's my birthday. You've got the name wrong again."  
The pianist kept the chords afloat while I unfolded twice,  
The note said Norman, sixty; I had read it in the light.  

[Chorus]
Here's to you, here's to tonight,  
To the name upon this paper that I didn't read quite right.  
Raise a glass, let the candles glow,  
There are more than I expected, but the band can take it slow.  
Here's to love, here's to the years,  
To the patient birthday table that is helping me from here.  
Smile for me, let the whole room sway,  
I shall land upon your birthday if the brass can hold the way.  

[Verse 2]
I sang for Norman's sixty years and toasted his good wife,  
The waiter brought the cake toward the center of the light.  
I asked about their meeting for a sweet romantic line,  
She said, "He's my brother," and the pianist swallowed wine.  
I changed romance to family with a dignified small cough,  
The drummer gave a brush sweep as I took the marriage off.  
The waiter put the cake down and I counted candles there,  
There were six beside a zero; I at last had got us square.  

[Chorus]
Here's to you, here's to tonight,  
To the name upon this paper that I didn't read quite right.  
Raise a glass, let the candles glow,  
There are more than I expected, but the band can take it slow.  
Here's to love, here's to the years,  
To the patient birthday table that is helping me from here.  
Smile for me, let the whole room sway,  
I shall land upon your birthday if the brass can hold the way.  

[Bridge]
Old Norman stood beside me and he read the next request,  
He named the person clearly and the band could do the rest.  
I gave him half my spotlight and a slice of birthday cake,  
The waiter passed him every note before I made mistakes.  

[Final Chorus]
Here's to you, here's to tonight,  
To the birthday boy whose reading keeps the dedications right.  
Raise a glass, let the candles glow,  
He has found a second career presenting my whole show.  
Smile for me, let the whole room sway,  
His sister books his next appearance for the very next day.  
Here's to love, here's to the years,  
The crooner gets the music; Norman tells us who is here.
