# The Square Dance Has Corners

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country square-dance calls, impossible partner swaps, and a caller who discovers the floor is not square*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Lively fiddle and handclaps; the caller sounds confident while dancers redraw the floor.*

Verse 1
A fiddler in the orchard tuned a string beneath the moon,
The caller shouted, "Find your pair; the dance will start at noon!"
The capuchins made circles, while two langurs formed a line,
A squirrel monkey asked the caller if the corner was a sign.

Chorus
Turn to the left, then mind the tree,
Swing your partner carefully.
Step to the edge, come back around,
The square keeps moving on the ground.

Verse 2
The caller cried, "Now promenade, go past the apple crate!"
The dancers reached the fence and found the gate was shut with twine.
They turned and met the fiddler, who was walking through the set,
He played so fast the dancers spun but never quite got wet.

Chorus
Turn to the left, then mind the tree,
Swing your partner carefully.
Step to the edge, come back around,
The square keeps moving on the ground.

Bridge
"Allemande and dosado, then promenade once more!"
The dancers circled round the tree and wound up at the door.
The caller checked the chalk lines, then admitted with a frown,
The orchard grew a corner while the fiddler faced the town.

Verse 3
They shifted every marker and made a wider square,
The branch became the center post; they danced around its air.
The caller called a final turn, the couples spun in time,
The tree wore one small ribbon as the lead in the design.

Chorus
Turn to the left, then mind the tree,
Swing your partner carefully.
Step to the edge, come back around,
The square keeps moving on the ground.

Ending
The fiddle stopped, the dancers bowed, the caller checked the chart,
The floor was still rectangular; the dance had done its part.
