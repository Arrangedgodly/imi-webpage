# The Wedding Waltz Loses the Cake Knife

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sentimental wedding waltzes whose sweeping promise of togetherness encounters the practical problem of serving dessert*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Lilting piano and brushed cymbals; the singer keeps the waltz going while the couple search the table.*

[Verse 1]
We danced beneath the lanterns as the band began to play,  
The little three-tier wedding cake had waited there all day.  
The singer sang of unity and taking hands for life,  
We reached the cake together but we couldn't find the knife.  
The table held a silver fork, two plates and folded lace,  
The photographer was kneeling with a lens beside his face.  
He asked us both to hold a pose and cut a little slice,  
We held the pose without a tool and smiled at him twice.  

[Chorus]
One step together, two steps to the side,  
Three tiers of sponge for the groom and the bride.  
One hand forever, one plate to share,  
The knife was on the table and it isn't sitting there.  
Turn in the lanterns, let the sweet notes stay,  
We cannot serve forever in the usual way.  

[Verse 2]
The caterer remembered setting cutlery by the flowers,  
The singer filled the search with all the love that could be ours.  
The photographer stood up to move his bulky canvas bag,  
A handle caught the tablecloth and pulled a little drag.  
The knife lay underneath the cloth beside the table's end,  
The caterer uncovered it and passed it to my friend.  
We held the handle jointly and we cut the waiting cake,  
The singer reached the second verse and had a breath to take.  

[Chorus]
One step together, two steps to the side,  
Three tiers of sponge for the groom and the bride.  
One hand forever, one plate to share,  
The knife was on the table and it isn't sitting there.  
Turn in the lanterns, let the sweet notes stay,  
We cannot serve forever in the usual way.  

[Bridge]
The photographer got his picture of the knife within our hands,  
The caterer served the portions to the guests beside the band.  
We danced again much later with the cake put safely by,  
The singer had a slice and sang a crumb into his tie.  

[Final Chorus]
One step together, two steps to the side,  
One crumb of sponge on the singer's silk tie.  
One hand forever, one plate to share,  
The knife goes back to catering; we know exactly where.  
Turn in the lanterns, let the sweet notes stay,  
The singer wants a napkin before promising the day.
