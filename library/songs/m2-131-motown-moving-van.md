# Motown at the Moving Van

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Motown girl-group harmonies, handclap breaks, and a moving crew that keeps lifting the couch on the wrong beat*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Tight group harmonies, tambourine, and handclaps; the couch lift should land on the chorus beat.*

Verse 1
The movers brought a couch down from the second floor,
A trio formed a harmony beside the open door.
The bass line walked, the tambourine shook bright,
The crew lifted on the wrong beat and bumped the doorframe twice.

Chorus
Hold that couch, count to four,
Move together, clear the door.
Clap on two and step on three,
Bring the furniture with harmony.

Verse 2
The singers gave a handclap cue; the movers tried again,
They raised the couch together but turned toward the den.
The label said "front room," but the hallway split in two,
The couch went down beside the stairs and blocked the rescue crew.

Chorus
Hold that couch, count to four,
Move together, clear the door.
Clap on two and step on three,
Bring the furniture with harmony.

Bridge
The lead singer held a note; the mover held his end,
The backing voices counted while they asked the door to bend.
They turned the couch on its side and cleared the narrow frame,
The tambourine made one bright shake to celebrate the game.

Verse 3
The couch reached the front room and settled by the wall,
The movers brought the table next, aligned and safe for all.
The singers stacked their harmonies; the crew packed up the van,
They clapped the final rhythm as the couch became a fan.

Chorus
Hold that couch, count to four,
Move together, clear the door.
Clap on two and step on three,
Bring the furniture with harmony.

Ending
The movers left the couch in place, the trio left the tune,
The landlord sat down carefully and joined the afternoon.
