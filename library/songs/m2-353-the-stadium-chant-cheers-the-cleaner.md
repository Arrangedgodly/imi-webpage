# The Stadium Chant Cheers the Cleaner

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: stadium chants that turn every ordinary action into a decisive sporting victory*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Stomping crowd and a giant bass drum; the cleaner's broom supplies the rhythm between chants.*

[Verse 1]
We bought our scarves and found our seats beneath the stadium clock,  
The team was in the changing room, the gate was on the lock.  
A cleaner crossed the center line to brush away a leaf,  
Our captain in the front row called the movement a relief.  
The crowd applauded every stroke as if it earned a goal,  
He swept the leaf across the grass and dropped it in a bowl.  

[Chorus]
Sweep it left, sweep it right,  
Take that leaf out of the fight.  
One more stroke and one more cheer,  
The cleanest club in town is here.  
Hold the line and clear the ground,  
Our cleaner wears the loudest little crown around.  

[Verse 2]
The cleaner reached the corner flag to empty out a bin,  
We sang about his bravery and urged the fellow in.  
He put a liner in the bin and checked its little rim,  
The band performed a trumpet blast especially for him.  
He wheeled his cart toward the stand; our scarves became a sea,  
The team emerged behind him but the crowd could barely see.  

[Chorus]
Sweep it left, sweep it right,  
Take that leaf out of the fight.  
One more stroke and one more cheer,  
The cleanest club in town is here.  
Hold the line and clear the ground,  
Our cleaner wears the loudest little crown around.  

[Bridge]
The referee requested that we let the players start,  
The cleaner parked his trolley and put both hands on his heart.  
We gave him one last thunderous roar; he bowed beside the wall,  
The striker took the opening kick and missed the little ball.  

[Final Chorus]
Sweep it left, sweep it right,  
Take that leaf out of the fight.  
One more stroke and one more cheer,  
The cleanest club in town is here.  
Hold the line and clear the ground,  
We want the cleaner back; the striker's boots have tracked mud round.
