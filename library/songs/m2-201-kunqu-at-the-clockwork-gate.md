# The Clockwork Gate's Long Goodbye

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Kunqu opera conventions, measured poetic phrasing, symbolic props, and an exit that takes an entire scene*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the vocal lines graceful; let the hand drum punctuate each slow turn.*

[Verse 1]
Capuchin Mei held a paper key against the painted moon,
A lantern glowed behind her; dawn would visit soon.
The gate was just a garden door, its hinge a little stiff,
Yet every farewell needed time; the exit was her gift.

[Chorus]
Turn, slow gate, turn, let the painted flowers part,
One step for the lantern, one step for the heart.
Turn, slow gate, turn, though the path is clear,
Let the drum say goodbye before the dawn draws near.

[Verse 2]
Mei lifted one silk sleeve and pointed to the lane,
Her friend had left a watering can beside the windowpane.
The gate moved half a handspan; the gardeners stood still,
The actor held the pose until a bird sat on the sill.

[Chorus]
Turn, slow gate, turn, let the painted flowers part,
One step for the lantern, one step for the heart.
Turn, slow gate, turn, though the path is clear,
Let the drum say goodbye before the dawn draws near.

[Bridge]
A sparrow stole the paper key and flew beyond the wall;
The hand drum gave a thunder roll; the gate did not move at all.
Mei broke the pose to chase the bird, then bowed to hide the run;
The audience applauded as the key came back at one.

[Final Chorus]
Turn, slow gate, turn, let the painted flowers part,
One step for the lantern, one step for the heart.
Turn, slow gate, turn, now the morning's here,
Let the drum say goodbye; the gate is still not clear.

[Ending]
The sparrow opened the gate. Mei took the watering can instead.
