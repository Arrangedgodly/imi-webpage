# The Soul Singer Discovers Hold Music

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soulful breakup ballads and dramatic telephone conversations derailed by a customer-service queue*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Warm organ, restrained drums and rich lead vocals; the telephone loop becomes the backing choir before the caller reaches an agent.*

[Verse 1]
I dialed to cancel cable after years of paying more,  
I had a speech prepared about the way things were before.  
The voice said I was valued and my patience meant so much,  
Then offered seven numbers that my trembling hand could touch.  
I pressed the one for leaving and I waited for the end,  
A little keyboard melody began to play again.  
It circled like a memory that refused to let me go,  
I sang about the package I'd been paying for alone.  

[Chorus]
Let me go, hear my plea,  
I have paid for fifty channels that have never comforted me.  
Stay on line, hold on tight,  
Your little tune is calling me through every minute of the night.  
Let me go, set me free,  
I have come to end the contract; you have put a choir in me.  
Soft chords, long wait, hear me cry,  
Every time the music fades I think you finally said goodbye.  

[Verse 2]
An agent came at minute twelve and asked about my plan,  
I sang the account number; she transcribed it as it ran.  
She offered me a sports pack and a month of premium shows,  
I said my heart had left the bundle many bills ago.  
She asked me to wait briefly while she checked the closing fee,  
The music came back brighter in a new and hopeful key.  
I wrote a second verse about the way we used to watch,  
The organ in the phone line gave the ballad all it got.  

[Chorus]
Let me go, hear my plea,  
I have paid for fifty channels that have never comforted me.  
Stay on line, hold on tight,  
Your little tune is calling me through every minute of the night.  
Let me go, set me free,  
I have come to end the contract; you have put a choir in me.  
Soft chords, long wait, hear me cry,  
Every time the music fades I think you finally said goodbye.  

[Bridge]
She canceled the subscription and she sent the closing mail,  
The line went quiet suddenly; my chorus lost its sail.  
I asked her who composed the tune that kept me on the line,  
She read the little music credit; I looked it up online.  

[Final Chorus]
Let me go, hear my plea,  
I have canceled cable but the melody came home with me.  
Stay on line, hold on tight,  
I hired the keyboard player for my show on Friday night.  
Soft chords, long wait, hear me cry,  
The crowd has bought a ticket just to hear the last goodbye.  
Let me go, set me free,  
Our encore is the little tune that kept me in that queue.
