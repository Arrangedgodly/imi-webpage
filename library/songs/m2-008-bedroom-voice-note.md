# Voice Memo at 2:14

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: indie bedroom confessional songs and intimate lo-fi production*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: One guitar, a close microphone, and the radiator left audible between lines.*

## Verse 1
I recorded this at 2:14 beside my bed.  
The radiator made a second voice instead.  
My room is half a laundry pile, half a nest.  
The window faces brick; it does its very best.  
I meant to write about the moon above the bay.  
A delivery truck kept backing up all day.  
So here's the beep, the kettle, and my socks.  
My feelings fit between the coat and music box.  

## Chorus
Leave the little hiss; it knows where I have been.  
The song can start before I tidy up the scene.  
Leave the little hiss, the radiator's reply.  
A small honest room is large enough tonight.  

## Verse 2
My friend texted, "You sound sad," at half past three.  
I wrote, "I'm mostly tired," and let the message be.  
A moth sat on the lampshade like a patient fan.  
I gave it half the room and made a plan.  
The chorus cracked where I had pushed too high.  
I kept the rough take, heard myself ask why.  
No name for every ache, no lesson at the end.  
Just one note held steady, then sent to a friend.  

## Bridge
Tomorrow I'll wash cups and air the blanket out.  
Tonight the song can leave a little space for doubt.  
The cheap mic catches every chair squeak on the floor.  
I used to cut those sounds; I don't anymore.  

## Chorus
Leave the little hiss; it knows where I have been.  
The song can start before I tidy up the scene.  
Leave the little hiss, the radiator's reply.  
A small honest room is large enough tonight.  

## Ending
I named the file "Tuesday, maybe, final two."  
The radiator kept the beat until the morning blue.
