# Telenovela Ballad for the Missing Cape

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: telenovela cliffhangers, dramatic reveals, and orchestral stings over small misunderstandings*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Sweeping strings and expressive spoken interjections; freeze after each revelation, even when it is plainly a costume mix-up.*

**Verse 1**
At dawn, the hero enters dressed in someone else's cape.  
The heroine gasps, "You wore that cloak to the masquerade!"  
The strings rise up, the camera turns, the hall falls into gloom.  
A cousin clutches both her pearls and faints beside a broom.  
The hero swears the cloak is borrowed from the wardrobe rack.  
A servant says the tag is gone; the music answers back.  

**Chorus**
Ay, what secret hides beneath the velvet fold?  
Ay, what promise was the tailor never told?  
Hold that look, let the strings ascend;  
The cape belongs to someone they already call a friend.  

**Verse 2**
The cousin rises, points at him, and cries, "It has my name!"  
The tailor brings the label; every cape is cut the same.  
The heroine turns away; the violins all sigh.  
The hero holds the garment up; a feather falls nearby.  
The bird keeper comes in, says, "That belongs to my bird."  
The bird is wearing matching cloth; no one says a word.  

**Chorus**
Ay, what secret hides beneath the velvet fold?  
Ay, what promise was the tailor never told?  
Hold that look, let the strings ascend;  
The cape belongs to someone they already call a friend.  

**Bridge**
The tailor reads the laundry tag: "Costume room, rack four."  
The cousin finds her own blue cape behind the wardrobe door.  
The bird keeper takes the feather; the strings begin to swell.  
The heroine gasps, "We found it," and the lighting turns to gold.  

**Ending**
The hero folds the borrowed cape and puts it on the shelf.  
The cousin says, "I thought you stole it." He answers, "I was myself."  
The camera pulls away as the bird flies through the door.  
The strings strike one last chord; a new cape hits the floor.
