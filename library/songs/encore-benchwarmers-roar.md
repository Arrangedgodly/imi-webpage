# Benchwarmers Roar

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Stadium chants and arena rock sports anthems*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Stomp-clap rhythm, cheap megaphone, a tiny pep band trying to keep up.*

[Chant]  
Who's got the towels? We got the towels!  
Who's got the water? We got it colder!  
Who gets to play? Maybe next quarter!  
Bench crew! Bench crew! Louder and louder!  

[Verse 1]  
Our jerseys have no numbers; they have names in marker pen,  
We clap so hard the mascot asks us to do it again.  
A rookie monkey waves a towel like a victory flag,  
Then catches it in midair before it hits the bag.  

[Chorus]  
Bench crew! Stand up! Shake the rafters!  
We bring the thunder, then we bring the gaffer.  
No minutes on the clock, but our spirit's in the game.  
Bench crew! Bench crew! Remember every name!  

[Verse 2]  
The coach says, "Stay prepared," so we lace our shoes anew.  
We practice high-fives with a very steady queue.  
A player needs a headband; we produce a whole supply.  
One monkey tosses three, and two go sailing by.  
The scoreboard says we're losing. The scoreboard doesn't know  
The bench has formed a wave that circles row to row.  
The camera finds our section, all our tails are in the air.  
We do the team's best dance, a shuffle with a chair.  

[Chorus]  
Bench crew! Stand up! Shake the rafters!  
We bring the thunder, then we bring the gaffer.  
No minutes on the clock, but our spirit's in the game.  
Bench crew! Bench crew! Remember every name!  

[Bridge]  
The final buzzer sounds. The coach turns, holds the door.  
"You kept us loud," he says, "and ready for one more."  
We march out with the towels, every water bottle dry.  
The rookie gets the game ball and tries not to cry.  

[Final Chorus]  
Bench crew! Stand up! Shake the rafters!  
We bring the thunder, then we bring the gaffer.  
No minutes on the clock, but our spirit's in the game.  
Bench crew! Bench crew! Remember every name!  

[Outro]  
We stack the chairs in silence, then chant for the bus.  
The bus is for the starters. We walk, and make a fuss.
