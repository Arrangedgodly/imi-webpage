# Heel Click at the Backstage Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: flamenco guitar and heelwork, dramatic hand fans, and a dancer who keeps interrupting the music to retrieve a dropped shoe*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use sharp heel clicks and guitar rasgueado; let the door's squeak answer the dancer's fan.*

[Verse 1]
A lemur took the stage in red, the guitar began to race,
Her heel struck out a warning beat that echoed through the place.
She raised her fan, the lights went low, the dancers formed a line,
Then one shoe slid beneath the door and vanished on the time.

[Chorus]
Heel, heel, click and turn,
Guitar, guitar, let the strings burn.
Fan, fan, open wide,
The shoe is waiting on the other side.
Heel, heel, click and turn,
Guitar, guitar, let the strings burn.
Fan, fan, open wide,
The shoe is waiting on the other side.

[Verse 2]
She stamped a sharp complaint; the door replied with squeak,
The rhythm got so fierce the stagehand found the key.
The dancers reached the corridor but all wore matching shoes,
They tapped along the passageway to find the missing blues.

[Chorus]
Heel, heel, click and turn,
Guitar, guitar, let the strings burn.
Fan, fan, open wide,
The shoe is waiting on the other side.
Heel, heel, click and turn,
Guitar, guitar, let the strings burn.
Fan, fan, open wide,
The shoe is waiting on the other side.

[Bridge]
A capuchin played guitar and kept the tempo neat,
The lemur found the shoe and tapped it with her feet.
She danced back through the doorway, wearing both shoes now,
The audience all rose to give the pair a bow.

[Final Chorus]
Heel, heel, click and turn,
Guitar, guitar, let the strings burn.
Fan, fan, open wide,
The shoe is back upon the proper side.
Heel, heel, click and turn,
Guitar, guitar, let the strings burn.
Fan, fan, open wide,
The door now swings with us side by side.

[Ending]
They oiled the door; the guitarist lost the beat.
