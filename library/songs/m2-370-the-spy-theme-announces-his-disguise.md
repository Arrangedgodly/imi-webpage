# The Spy Theme Announces His Disguise

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: spy-film title songs that reveal the hero's secrets with breathless dramatic confidence*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Smoky contralto, ominous guitar and lush strings; the spy whispers increasingly frantic corrections.*

[Verse 1]
He walks across the harbor with a shadow for a name,  
He carries seven secrets and a match without a flame.  
He wears the gardener's jacket with a badge upon his chest,  
The singer names his cover while he tries to look his best.  
The guard turns up the radio and listens to the tune,  
The spy attempts a silent wave beneath the rising moon.  

[Chorus]
Nobody knows him, nobody sees,  
He's hiding by the gate among the little orange trees.  
Nobody names him, nobody can,  
The tall one in the gardener's coat is our mysterious man.  
He has the world inside his hand,  
The briefcase code is something that the chorus understands.  

[Verse 2]
He whispers to the singer that her lyrics are too clear,  
She sings his whispered sentence for the guards nearby to hear.  
He changes to a waiter with a folded linen cloth,  
The singer changes verse and tells the room his jacket's off.  
He crosses through the kitchen in a borrowed paper hat,  
The strings describe the saucepan where he left his secret map.  

[Chorus]
Nobody knows him, nobody sees,  
He's hiding by the gate among the little orange trees.  
Nobody names him, nobody can,  
The tall one in the gardener's coat is our mysterious man.  
He has the world inside his hand,  
The briefcase code is something that the chorus understands.  

[Bridge]
He picks the singer's radio up and slips it in his case,  
The guard comes looking for the tune, not for the hidden face.  
He takes the case and holds it by his ear beside the pier,  
The spy walks out in silence while the high notes disappear.  

[Final Chorus]
Nobody knows him, nobody sees,  
He walks beyond the gate beneath the little orange trees.  
Nobody names him, nobody can,  
The singer names the spy again; he has already left the sand.  
He has the world inside his hand,  
He leaves beside the harbor dressed as an honest man.
