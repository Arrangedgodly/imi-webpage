# The Gospel Choir Finds the Coat Check

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel concert call-and-response and audiences responding to a question intended for the cloakroom attendant*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Uplifting piano and handclaps; solo leader calls, full choir responds, then the coat attendant speaks rhythmically.*

[Solo]
Who has seen my long brown coat?  
[Choir]
We have heard your mighty note!  
[Solo]
Who can bring it back to me?  
[Choir]
Let the whole room rise and see!  

[Verse 1]
The concert ended warmly at the hall beside the square,  
I went to find my winter coat and found no hanger there.  
The cloakroom used a ticket book; my pocket held a three,  
The attendant took a moment for a cup of cooling tea.  
I called across the room to ask which rack could hold my coat,  
The choir heard my question and responded to the note.  
The audience rose halfway from their little folding chairs,  
They thought a new encore had started underneath the stairs.  

[Chorus]
Bring it back, let it come,  
We have not yet finished what this evening has begun.  
Bring it back, let it be,  
There is one brown coat waiting with a little ticket three.  
Clap your hands, raise the roof,  
The pocket holds a receipt that supplies the final proof.  

[Verse 2]
The attendant came back through the crowd and took my little slip,  
She checked the number on the hook and gave the coat a flip.  
The choir held a chord while she pulled it from the rail,  
A scarf came down beside it like a long and woolen tail.  
I put the coat upon my back and told the room goodnight,  
The pianist found a closing chord beneath the cloakroom light.  
The audience sat down again then rose to fetch their own,  
The attendant opened up the rack where fifty coats were shown.  

[Chorus]
Bring it back, let it come,  
We have not yet finished what this evening has begun.  
Bring it back, let it be,  
There is one brown coat waiting with a little ticket three.  
Clap your hands, raise the roof,  
The pocket holds a receipt that supplies the final proof.  

[Closing Call]
Who has seen my long brown coat?  
[Choir]
You are wearing it, we note!  
[Attendant]
Who has number twenty-two?  
[Whole Room]
Seven voices shout, "I do!"
