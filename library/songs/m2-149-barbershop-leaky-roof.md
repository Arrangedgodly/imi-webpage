# Barbershop Quartet of the Leaky Roof

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: barbershop close harmony, comic miscommunication, and a perfectly held final chord*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Four-part close harmony; roof drips join the bass line while the quartet holds poses.*

Verse 1
Four macaques met in a hall to sing a chord so pure,
A drop fell through the ceiling tile and landed on the score.
The tenor sang, "The roof is leaking," bass heard "the room is speaking,"
The lead replied, "Then sing to it," and kept the chord from creaking.

Chorus
Hold that note and let it ring,
Every drip can join the thing.
Turn the bucket, mind the seam,
Make a quartet of the stream.

Verse 2
The baritone brought buckets in and set them in a row,
The tenor thought they'd joined the act and gave each one a bow.
The lead began a love song to the ceiling's little crack,
The bass sang "roof repair," and made the chorus answer back.

Chorus
Hold that note and let it ring,
Every drip can join the thing.
Turn the bucket, mind the seam,
Make a quartet of the stream.

Bridge
The roofer climbed above the hall and shouted, "Move the chairs!"
The quartet heard "improve the air," and sang through falling squares.
A tile slid down; the tenor caught it with a graceful spin,
The bass kept holding "home," while rain came pouring in.

Verse 3
They shifted all the buckets till they formed a shining ring,
The drips struck different pitches; every leak became a string.
The roofer patched the roof at last and asked them for a tune,
They gave him one final chord that made the ladder swoon.

Chorus
Hold that note and let it ring,
Every drip can join the thing.
Turn the bucket, mind the seam,
Make a quartet of the stream.

Ending
The audience stood, the quartet bowed, the roofer took a bow;
A fresh drip hit the soloist and started encore now.
