# Minuet for a Tea Tray

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: courtly minuet conventions, formal steps, and a tea server trying to keep a tray level through paired dance figures*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Graceful triple meter with harpsichord and strings; keep the tea tray perfectly level through each minuet figure.*

Verse 1
The macaques set a tea tray on a table by the door,
The fiddler played a minuet; the dancers took the floor.
A gibbon bowed to greet a guest, then stepped around the cup,
The tea server turned in triple time and nearly tipped it up.

Chorus
Step and bow, then turn with care,
Keep the tea cups in the air.
One-two-three, the dancers glide,
Leave the saucer on your side.

Verse 2
The partners crossed in measured lines, their sleeves arranged just so,
The tray moved with the server as the violins played low.
A lemur changed direction; the server changed it too,
The cups began to circle like a little silver crew.

Chorus
Step and bow, then turn with care,
Keep the tea cups in the air.
One-two-three, the dancers glide,
Leave the saucer on your side.

Bridge
The harpsichord struck a sharper note; the couples spun around,
The tray stayed flat, the cups stayed still, the sugar made no sound.
The server took a final bow; one spoon slid from its place,
It landed in a teacup with a very stately grace.

Verse 3
They stopped the dance and poured the tea, then sat in orderly rows,
The fiddler played the minuet while steam began to rose.
The server brought the tray back out and joined the final turn,
The cups remained in balance as the candles softly burned.

Chorus
Step and bow, then turn with care,
Keep the tea cups in the air.
One-two-three, the dancers glide,
Leave the saucer on your side.

Ending
The guests drank tea, the dancers bowed, the tray was safely set,
The spoon performed one tiny turn before the tea grew cold.
