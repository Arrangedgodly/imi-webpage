# The Suspense Cue Is a Spoon

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: film-score suspense conventions, tremolo strings, and a kitchen scene where the audience expects danger from a rolling spoon*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Low strings, pizzicato plucks, and one sudden brass swell; keep the suspense enormous while the prop stays tiny.*

Verse 1
The strings began to tremble in a kitchen after dark,
A spoon lay on the counter with a polished silver spark.
The kettle gave one whistle; the cellos held a tone,
A capuchin tiptoed in to claim a snack alone.

Chorus
Wait for the sound, let the silence grow,
One small shadow crosses below.
Tremble, strings, and let horns bloom,
A spoon is rolling through the room.

Verse 2
The spoon began to wobble as the counter tilted down,
The violins grew urgent; the drummer dropped his frown.
A macaque watched the drawer handle rise against the light,
The spoon slid toward the edge; the brass announced its flight.

Chorus
Wait for the sound, let the silence grow,
One small shadow crosses below.
Tremble, strings, and let horns bloom,
A spoon is rolling through the room.

Bridge
The spoon fell from the counter and hit a bowl below,
The orchestra struck a chord that made the audience go.
The capuchin grabbed the spoon; the cello held the note,
The kitchen clock ticked loudly as a napkin caught its coat.

Verse 3
The danger was a snack spoon, the shadow just a plum,
The drawer was opened gently; the horns went softly numb.
The capuchin ate one cracker, then put the spoon away,
The strings resolved the mystery before the break of day.

Chorus
Wait for the sound, let the silence grow,
One small shadow crosses below.
Tremble, strings, and let horns bloom,
A spoon is rolling through the room.

Ending
The film's great threat was cutlery, the score returned to rest,
The spoon went in the drawer beside the very ordinary chest.
