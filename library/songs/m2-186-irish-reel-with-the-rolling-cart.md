# The Reel of the Runaway Cart

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Irish reel conventions, fast repeated refrains, and a dance that accelerates beyond its musicians*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Begin with fiddle and handclaps; let each chorus speed up until the cart forces a stop.*

[Verse 1]
At the market gate the fiddler tuned a note of lively green,
While Mags the macaque set her cart where every dancer's seen.
She packed it full of apples, tied a ribbon round the load,
Then tapped the wheel and said, "It knows the measure of the road."

[Chorus]
Roll, little cart, roll, rattle and roam,
Turn at the fiddle and head for home.
Step, little feet, skip over the stone,
Catch that red wheel before it is gone.


[Verse 2]
The reel began politely, with a heel and a small clap,
But Mags's cart took off and pulled the fiddler from his lap.
The dancers spun to catch it; every spin increased its pace,
A pear became the caller and rolled first across the place.

[Chorus]
Roll, little cart, roll, rattle and roam,
Turn at the fiddle and head for home.
Step, little feet, skip over the stone,
Catch that red wheel before it is gone.


[Bridge]
The fiddler changed to triple time; the cart changed lanes instead.
A basket joined the dancers, wearing cabbage leaves for thread.
Mags grabbed the handle, planted both her feet against the ground;
The cart kept doing circles till the reel went winding round.

[Verse 3]
The wheel came loose at last and rolled straight through the final bar;
The crowd made room and clapped as it went past the pickle jar.
Mags caught it with her tail, tied it back, and rang the bell.
The fiddler played one slower note; the whole cart stayed as well.

[Final Chorus]
Roll, little cart, roll, rattle and roam,
Turn at the fiddle and head for home.
Step, little feet, skip over the stone,
Catch that red wheel before it is gone.


[Ending]
They danced the cart back to its stall, where Mags removed the load.
The apples stayed; the dancers bought the wheel and called it "Reel Road."
