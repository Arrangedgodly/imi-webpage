# The Comet Has a Lunchbox

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: psychedelic rock, echoing vocal effects, cosmic light projections, and an astronomer who mistakes a snack for a celestial body*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use echoing guitars and slow light shifts; let the reveal land on a dry spoken line.*

[Verse 1]
A capuchin at the planetarium tuned his guitar to blue,
The ceiling showed a spiral sky that slowly changed its hue.
The audience leaned backward as the constellations spun,
Then one bright shape appeared and blocked the little sun.

[Chorus]
Comet in the night, trail of silver light,
Round the moon and past the star, stay in sight.
Comet in the night, drift across the dome,
Tell the sleepy universe we're almost home.
Comet in the night, trail of silver light,
Round the moon and past the star, stay in sight.
Comet in the night, drift across the dome,
Tell the sleepy universe we're almost home.

[Verse 2]
The astronomer explained the glowing streak above,
The guitarist played a wah-wah chord and called it cosmic love.
A macaque in the back row pointed to the tail:
The comet was a lunchbox swinging from a rail.

[Chorus]
Comet in the night, trail of silver light,
Round the moon and past the star, stay in sight.
Comet in the night, drift across the dome,
Tell the sleepy universe we're almost home.
Comet in the night, trail of silver light,
Round the moon and past the star, stay in sight.
Comet in the night, drift across the dome,
Tell the sleepy universe we're almost home.

[Bridge]
The light crew moved the projector; the lunchbox crossed the sky,
The audience all gasped as it rose up high.
The capuchin stopped the music, reached up for his bread,
His elbow pulled the power cord; the startled light crew fled.

[Final Chorus]
Comet in the night, trail of silver light,
Round the moon and past the star, stay in sight.
Comet in the night, drift across the dome,
The lunchbox made its orbit; now we take it home.
Comet in the night, trail of silver light,
Round the moon and past the star, stay in sight.
Comet in the night, drift across the dome,
The lunchbox made its orbit; now we take it home.

[Ending]
The guitar kept on echoing after the lights came on.
