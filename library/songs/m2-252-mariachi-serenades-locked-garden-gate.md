# The Mariachi Serenades a Locked Garden Gate

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: mariachi serenades with trumpet flourishes, romantic declarations, and a chorus of eager witnesses*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Let trumpets answer each verse with bold flourishes; the singer should be sincerely romantic while the gate remains firmly latched.*

[Verse 1]
At midnight by the garden stood a gibbon with a rose,  
The iron gate was locked up and the moonlight struck his nose.  
He gathered six musicians with guitars and shining brass,  
And sang about his longing through the diamond-pattern glass.  
The gardener was sleeping in a shed beside the beans,  
The singer sang of moonlit paths and fountains evergreen.  
A trumpeter played high enough to wake the sleepy birds,  
The singer's velvet serenade was lost beneath the chords.  

[Chorus]
Open, open, garden gate,  
My heart has come to serenade.  
The roses hear; the peppers know,  
The sleeping gardener will wake up slow.  
Open, open, metal friend,  
This love song cannot turn the bend.  

[Verse 2]
A macaque found the latch was hooked behind a painted sign,  
The sign said "Open from the garden side, not from the line."  
The chorus sang a harmony, the violins replied,  
The gardener called out, "Use the handle on your side!"  
The singer tried a higher note to prove his love was true,  
The trumpet blared a counterpoint that shook the morning dew.  
A capuchin reached through the bars and lifted up the latch,  
The gate swung wide; the whole band fell into compost patch.  

[Chorus]
Open, open, garden gate,  
My heart has come to serenade.  
The roses hear; the peppers know,  
The sleeping gardener will wake up slow.  
Open, open, metal friend,  
This love song cannot turn the bend.  

[Bridge]
The gardener came in slippers with a rake held like a staff,  
The band began a love reprise; he asked them all to laugh.  
The singer offered him the rose and said, "It is for you,"  
The gardener said, "Plant it by the gate; the soil is good and new."  

[Final Chorus]
Open, open, garden gate,  
The dawn has come; it is not late.  
The roses heard; the peppers grew,  
The gardener has a job for you.  
Open, open, let us go,  
The band can serenade the rows.  

[Ending]
They planted the rose; the gate got a small padlock for the night.
