# Samba Through the Stacks

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: samba parade rhythms, call-and-response percussion, and a library procession that must keep its noise below the reading-room limit*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the samba rolling softly; let the librarian's stamp become a featured percussion hit.*

[Verse 1]
A samba band came marching through the library at noon,
The drummer used a bookmark as a shaker for the tune.
The librarian stood waiting with a finger to her lip,
The dancers formed a quiet line and gave a careful skip.

[Chorus]
Samba, samba, whisper low,
Turn the page and let it flow.
Samba, samba, tap your feet,
Keep the shakers soft and sweet.
Samba, samba, whisper low,
Turn the page and let it flow.
Samba, samba, tap your feet,
Keep the shakers soft and sweet.

[Verse 2]
A capuchin played the counter with a pencil and a card,
The librarian stamped the due dates like a snare that hit too hard.
The band began a chorus, and the shelves replied in tone;
A history book fell open to a drumbeat of its own.

[Chorus]
Samba, samba, whisper low,
Turn the page and let it flow.
Samba, samba, tap your feet,
Keep the shakers soft and sweet.
Samba, samba, whisper low,
Turn the page and let it flow.
Samba, samba, tap your feet,
Keep the shakers soft and sweet.

[Bridge]
The librarian joined the line and stamped upon the beat;
The dancers turned the checkout desks into a moving street.
They marched around the stacks but never raised a shout;
The quietest parade the neighborhood had read about.

[Final Chorus]
Samba, samba, whisper low,
Turn the page and let it flow.
Samba, samba, tap your feet,
Keep the shakers soft and sweet.
Samba, samba, whisper low,
Turn the page and let it flow.
Samba, samba, tap your feet,
Keep the shakers soft and sweet.

[Ending]
The band returned its books; the librarian stamped "encore."
