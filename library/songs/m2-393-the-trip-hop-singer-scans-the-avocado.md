# The Trip-Hop Singer Scans the Avocado

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: brooding trip-hop songs that give ordinary hesitation the gravity of an unresolved urban mystery*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Slow breakbeat, deep bass and whispered vocals; a self-checkout beep cuts through the haze at regular intervals.*

[Verse 1]
The fluorescent rain fell softly on the supermarket floor,  
I carried one avocado toward the exit by the door.  
The checkout held a question on its pale electric face,  
A picture of a pepper occupied the fruit's old place.  
I touched the screen with caution like a stranger at a gate,  
The weighing tray was waiting for a name and for a weight.  
A voice said, "Place your purchase," with a patience made of wire,  
I set it down and watched a little warning blink like fire.  

[Chorus]
Low light, slow beat, tell me what you know,  
There is fruit upon the scales and nowhere left to go.  
Blue screen, white bag, whisper in my ear,  
The unexpected item is the only item here.  
Low light, slow beat, hold me in the queue,  
I have chosen produce but the screen has chosen you.  
Deep bass, soft beep, let the shadows pass,  
The avocado has a secret price behind the glass.  

[Verse 2]
The customer behind me had a trolley full of bread,  
I searched beneath the sticker for the number that it said.  
The code had four small digits; I had entered only three,  
The checkout thought a radish was the thing in front of me.  
An assistant crossed the aisle with a key upon her wrist,  
She canceled my mistaken code and pointed to the list.  
The fruit appeared in color and the price came into view,  
The trolley owner sighed as though she'd solved a crime for two.  

[Chorus]
Low light, slow beat, tell me what you know,  
There is fruit upon the scales and nowhere left to go.  
Blue screen, white bag, whisper in my ear,  
The unexpected item is the only item here.  
Low light, slow beat, hold me in the queue,  
I have chosen produce but the screen has chosen you.  
Deep bass, soft beep, let the shadows pass,  
The avocado has a secret price behind the glass.  

[Bridge]
I paid and put the fruit inside my small reusable sack,  
The assistant handed me the slip; I tucked it in the sack.  
The trolley owner looked at me and asked if I was done,  
I said, "The mystery is over." She said, "Mine has just begun."  

[Final Chorus]
Low light, slow beat, tell me what you know,  
The bread has lost its barcode and the next red warnings glow.  
Blue screen, white bag, whisper in my ear,  
I have made it through the doorway but the music keeps me here.  
Deep bass, soft beep, let the shadows pass,  
I carry home the avocado like a prize of cut green glass.  
Low light, slow beat, close the city gate,  
I cut it open after all that waiting; it is not ripe yet.
