# The Chamber Quartet Fights a Squeaky Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: refined chamber music concerts and the tiny mechanical noises that become the most memorable solo*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: String quartet in a stately dance; a dry chair squeak answers the cello until the seat is changed.*

[Verse 1]
We tuned beside the portraits in the library's little hall,  
Four music stands, four velvet seats, a clock upon the wall.  
The cello gave the opening and I leaned toward the score,  
My chair supplied a piercing note the composer hadn't called for.  
I sat quite straight and held my bow as still as I could stay,  
The squeak returned the moment that I turned a page away.  
The second violinist frowned across her polished stand,  
The audience looked interested and folded every hand.  

[Refrain]
Four strings in a measured dance,  
One chair waits to take its chance.  
Bow the phrase and breathe the air,  
Somebody has commissioned the chair.  
Softly now, let the melody meet,  
The fifth musician lives beneath my seat.  

[Verse 2]
I reached the long slow passage and I tried to hold my pose,  
The chair began its little song when my left elbow rose.  
The cello carried on while I stood up behind my stand,  
My part sounded quite ordinary played upon the land.  
The usher saw the empty chair and carried it away,  
She brought a wooden kitchen stool that had no tune to play.  
I sat upon the quiet stool and found the proper bar,  
The squeaking soloist retired beneath the coats afar.  

[Refrain]
Four strings in a measured dance,  
One chair waits to take its chance.  
Bow the phrase and breathe the air,  
Somebody has commissioned the chair.  
Softly now, let the melody meet,  
The fifth musician lives beneath my seat.  

[Bridge]
We finished all four movements and the audience applauded,  
The usher took a little bow for service she'd afforded.  
A patron asked which composer wrote the startling early trill,  
We pointed at the empty chair beside the windowsill.  

[Final Refrain]
Four strings in a measured dance,  
The chair has lost its second chance.  
Bow the phrase and breathe the air,  
The usher oils the joints with care.  
Softly now, let the melody meet,  
The patron bought the chair and booked a separate seat.
