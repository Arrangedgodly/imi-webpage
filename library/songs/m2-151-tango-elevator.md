# Tango in the Elevator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tango's jealous duet, sharp pauses, and dramatic turns in a confined dance floor*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Bandoneon and clipped strings; the elevator chime interrupts each embrace.*

Verse 1
A tamarin in a crimson tie stepped in on floor three,
A capuchin with roses said, "That lift was meant for me."
The doors slid shut between them with a dangerously soft chime,
The bandoneon sighed low and started counting time.

Chorus
Step, turn, pause by the brass,
Let the elevator pass.
Hold that glare, then change your side,
Two proud dancers, one short ride.

Verse 2
She claimed the corner mirror; he said it showed his good side,
They lunged across the handrail as the lift began to glide.
At floor four they froze in pose; the doors opened on a clerk,
Who entered with a mop and watched the tango go to work.

Chorus
Step, turn, pause by the brass,
Let the elevator pass.
Hold that glare, then change your side,
Two proud dancers, one short ride.

Bridge
The singer breathed, "My rose is yours," and offered up the stem,
She tucked it in her hair, then used its thorns to steer him.
The lift stopped at the basement; both blamed the other's shoe,
The clerk had pressed the service bell and joined the dance in blue.

Verse 3
The mop made one slow circle; both dancers followed through,
The doors slid open on the lobby to a crowd in evening shoes.
They bowed as if the ride had been a grand premiere,
Then saw the floor indicator had not moved all year.

Chorus
Step, turn, pause by the brass,
Let the elevator pass.
Hold that glare, then change your side,
Two proud dancers, one short ride.

Ending
The building had one working lift, stuck between floors two and three;
They sold tickets for the tango and charged the mop a fee.
