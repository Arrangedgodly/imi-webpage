# The Long Way to the Corral

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country-western story ballad, dusty guitar, and a heroic journey whose lead refuses to hurry*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Tell the verses plainly; let the chorus swell like a frontier legend.*

[Verse 1]
A turtle named June set out at dawn to cross the ranching ground,
The herd was waiting by the gate; the supper bell had sounded.
She carried one small saddlebag and moved at turtle pace;
The ranch hands watched the sunset travel twice across her face.

[Chorus]
Take the long road, June, take the long road home,
The dust may rise, the stars may set, but still your boots will roam.
Take the long road, June, let the fiddle tell it true,
The corral gate is standing wide and supper waits for you.

[Verse 2]
A coyote offered shortcuts; June asked where they began.
He pointed down a washout trail and vanished in the sand.
She took the level path instead, passed every thirsty steer;
The cowboys packed their lunch again and made a camp right here.

[Chorus]
Take the long road, June, take the long road home,
The dust may rise, the stars may set, but still your boots will roam.
Take the long road, June, let the fiddle tell it true,
The corral gate is standing wide and supper waits for you.

[Bridge]
They rode ahead, built a fire, and played a country tune;
June reached the final fence post underneath a silver moon.
The herd had gone to sleep, but left the gate unlatched;
She nudged it with her shell; the old latch clicked back.

[Final Chorus]
Take the long road, June, take the long road home,
The dust may rise, the stars may set, but still your boots will roam.
Take the long road, June, the corral gate is through,
The legend got there slowly, but the legend followed through.

[Ending]
The ranch hands warmed the supper twice. June arrived right on time.
