# The Disco Ball Is a Planet

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: disco floor-filling repetition, mirror-ball glamour, and dance-floor certainty*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor bass with bright strings; the lyric's cosmic confidence should collapse into a very small repair request.*

Verse 1
The lights go down, the mirror ball becomes a silver moon,  
A troop of lemurs takes the floor and finds the perfect tune.  
The singer says the night is ours, the galaxy is near,  
A maintenance clerk walks through the beam and says, "The ceiling's here."  
We point our fingers to the stars, we slide across the tile,  
The ball rotates one quarter turn and shines upon a pile.  

Chorus
Disco, orbit, round we go,  
Shine your shoes and catch the glow.  
Spin that planet, turn that light,  
We own the whole dance floor tonight.  
Disco, orbit, hands up high,  
Please don't touch the sprinkler pipe.  
Spin that planet, make it gleam,  
The floor is waxed; don't split the seam.  

Verse 2
The mirror ball begins to wobble with a tiny clicking sound,  
The DJ raises both his hands and keeps the beat profound.  
A squirrel with a step stool climbs to check the spinning hook,  
The crowd makes room in time with every turn and look.  
The clerk says, "One screw's loose," and hands the DJ tape,  
The DJ wraps the cable while the dancers hold their shape.  

Bridge
We pause the bass, the room stays lit, the crew secures the chain,  
The singer holds one high note through the silence and the strain.  
The screw is tight, the ball spins true, the silver planets gleam,  
The maintenance clerk accepts a dance and joins the beam.  

Chorus
Disco, orbit, round we go,  
Shine your shoes and catch the glow.  
Spin that planet, turn that light,  
We own the whole dance floor tonight.  
Disco, orbit, hands up high,  
Please don't touch the sprinkler pipe.  
Spin that planet, make it gleam,  
The floor is waxed; don't split the seam.  

Ending
The club closes at eleven; the clerk turns off the light,  
Our tiny planet disappears, then asks for overtime tonight.
