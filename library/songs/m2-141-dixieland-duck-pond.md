# Dixieland at the Duck Pond

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Dixieland jazz, collective horn improvisation, and a clarinetist whose solos keep being answered by a duck*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Clarinet, cornet, trombone, and banjo trade overlapping phrases; the duck's quack lands as an unscheduled horn reply.*

Verse 1
The band set up beside the pond and tuned beneath the tree,
A clarinet began to play; the cornet charged no fee.
The trombone slid a low note in; the banjo strummed along,
A duck replied with one loud quack and joined the opening song.

Chorus
Play it together, pass the phrase,
Let the pond reflect the blaze.
Clarinet and cornet trade,
Duck has made a guest parade.

Verse 2
The clarinet took off alone, the cornet chased its run,
The duck kept quacking in the gaps as if the set were done.
The trombone called a lower line; the duck replied in kind,
The duck demanded solo pay; the band said, "Get in line."

Chorus
Play it together, pass the phrase,
Let the pond reflect the blaze.
Clarinet and cornet trade,
Duck has made a guest parade.

Bridge
The leader raised a feather to direct the solo break,
The duck mistook the gesture for a cue to cross the lake.
It paddled to the far shore, then came back to the band,
And dropped a soggy booking form into the leader's hand.

Verse 3
They played a softer chorus as the duck returned to shore,
The cornet held a quiet note; the clarinet played four.
The duck gave one last quack and waddled to the reeds,
The leader signed its contract for a sack of premium seeds.

Chorus
Play it together, pass the phrase,
Let the pond reflect the blaze.
Clarinet and cornet trade,
Duck has made a guest parade.

Ending
The band packed up its instruments; the duck collected pay,
Its new manager, a goose, booked them all for Saturday.
