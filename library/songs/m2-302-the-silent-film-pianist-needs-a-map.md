# The Silent-Film Pianist Needs a Map

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: silent-film accompaniment that improvises melodrama from gestures but has no map of the plot*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Live piano cues switch from chase to romance to disaster at every title card; let the pianist overreact to a simple prop.*

**Verse 1**
Mina plays the picture show beneath a flickering screen.  
The hero drops a handkerchief; the piano turns to grief.  
The heroine picks it up; a trumpet seems to wail.  
The card says, "Meet me by the gate," the piano starts a chase.  
The hero walks across the park; the pianist adds a storm.  
The usher whispers, "That's a bench," while Mina plays alarm.  

**Chorus**
Strike for the chase, strike for the fall,  
Strike for the hero who misreads it all.  
A rag, a hat, a gate, a key;  
The piano writes the plot before the film can see.  

**Verse 2**
A dog runs off with the hat; the chords turn dark and deep.  
The hero follows at a jog; the piano makes him weep.  
The dog returns the hat and sits beside the gate.  
The music swells to wedding bells; the title card says, "Wait."  
The hero ties his shoe, then smiles at the camera.  
Mina plays a cannon boom; the audience asks, "For what?"  

**Chorus**
Strike for the chase, strike for the fall,  
Strike for the hero who misreads it all.  
A rag, a hat, a gate, a key;  
The piano writes the plot before the film can see.  

**Bridge**
The projectionist hands Mina the scene order on a card.  
She changes one chord as the hero crosses the yard.  
The dog retrieves the handkerchief; the gate swings open wide.  
The pianist saves the wedding bells for when they step inside.  

**Ending**
The dog tugs the hero's hat from the bench and trots off as the screen says, "The End." The piano lands on one plain note.  
Mina folds the map she made and tucks it in her coat.  
The hero leaves the hat behind; the dog takes it away.  
The usher finds the hat and puts it in lost-and-found today.
