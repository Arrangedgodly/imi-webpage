# Chanson for the Laundry Queue

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: French chanson storytelling, intimate vocals, and finding poetry in ordinary city life*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Sing close to the microphone over accordion and brushed guitar; tell the story with affectionate detail instead of a grand chorus.*

[Verse 1]
At the laundromat on Willow Street, the evening light was thin,  
A gibbon held a basket and a sock fell rolling in.  
The washer doors were round as moons, the dryers warm as tea,  
A macaque watched the numbers turn and sighed beside the key.  
The singer found a melody between the hum and spin,  
The lost sock crossed the tiled floor and brushed a stranger's shin.  
A capuchin picked it up and read the tag aloud,  
It matched the sock upon his foot; they smiled above the crowd.  

[Chorus]
Oh, little sock, where have you been?  
Through the turning drum and the soap suds green.  
The city hums a quiet tune,  
The laundry waits beneath the moon.  
Oh, little sock, come home at last,  
The rinse is done; the spin has passed.  

[Verse 2]
The washer gave a final beep, the singer closed her eyes,  
A dryer door swung open and released a warm surprise.  
The sock had found a basket, but the pair was not complete,  
The capuchin checked every fold and searched beneath the seat.  
The gibbon looked inside a sleeve and found the missing twin,  
The singer played an accordion and let the chorus in.  
The strangers shared a detergent sheet and folded both with care,  
Their small reunion filled the room with lavender-scented air.  

[Chorus]
Oh, little sock, where have you been?  
Through the turning drum and the soap suds green.  
The city hums a quiet tune,  
The laundry waits beneath the moon.  
Oh, little sock, come home at last,  
The rinse is done; the spin has passed.  

[Bridge]
The singer watched the street outside, the buses blinking gold,  
A little story in a sock, a small return from cold.  
The line moved forward one place as the washers slowly stilled,  
The pair sat side by side, their quiet purpose fulfilled.  

[Final Chorus]
Oh, little socks, you both are here,  
The city hums; the night is clear.  
The laundry turns its gentle tune,  
The folded stack is leaving soon.  
Oh, little socks, go home at last,  
The rinse is done; the spin has passed.  

[Ending]
The singer left with one unmatched sock; the laundry had no duet.
