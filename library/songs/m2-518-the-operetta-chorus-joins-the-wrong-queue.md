# The Operetta Chorus Joins the Wrong Queue

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operetta ensembles and chorus members who make a crowded line sound more purposeful than it is*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Quick piano and buoyant ensemble patter; the box-office clerk answers the chorus with a deliberately slow sentence.*

[Patter Verse]
We joined the line at half past nine  
With every singer feeling fine.  
The theater door was just ahead,  
The leader had the notice read.  
A queue could mean a grand audition,  
A queue could mean a new position.  
We sang our names in rising order,  
A guard came down to check the border.  

[Chorus]
Queue, queue, what shall we do?  
The line has got a purpose that we haven't looked into.  
Queue, queue, let the patter fly,  
The person at the front knows what the rest have come to buy.  
Soprano, tenor, alto, bass,  
The line has got a box-office window at its face.  

[Verse]
The clerk was selling tickets for a matinee of dance,  
The chorus had an evening call with no audition chance.  
The leader checked her message and she found the proper time,  
Seven at the stage door, not the line beside the sign.  
We left the line in pairs before the people lost their place,  
The guard pointed out the stage door with a very patient face.  
One singer bought a ticket to the dance before we went,  
He said a queue should give him something for the time he'd spent.  

[Chorus]
Queue, queue, what shall we do?  
The line has got a purpose that we haven't looked into.  
Queue, queue, let the patter fly,  
The person at the front knows what the rest have come to buy.  
Soprano, tenor, alto, bass,  
The line has got a box-office window at its face.  

[Evening Patter]
At seven at the proper door,  
We sang the show we'd sung before.  
The ticket buyer knew the dance,  
He gave our entrance one new glance.  
The leader raised a strict objection:  
Our show is not that dance selection.  
He stopped the step and sang his part,  
The dance stayed quietly in his heart.  

[Final Chorus]
Queue, queue, now we know,  
The box office has tickets; the stage door has a show.  
Soprano, tenor, alto, bass,  
The leader puts the correct door in the message's first place.  
Queue, queue, let the patter fly,  
The ticket buyer wants to know what tomorrow's line will buy.
