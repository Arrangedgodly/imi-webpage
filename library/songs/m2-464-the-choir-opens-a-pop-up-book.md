# The Choir Opens a Pop-Up Book

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: elaborate choral storytelling concerts whose reverent presentation of the text meets an unexpectedly three-dimensional picture book*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Warm choir harmony with simple piano; paper castles pop upright with an audible rustle whenever the narrator reaches another page.*

[Verse 1]
The choir had a story to perform beside the school,  
The narrator brought a book with pictures bright and cool.  
He opened up the cover and a paper castle rose,  
The basses lost their opening note behind its little towers.  
The narrator sang of kingdoms with a very solemn face,  
The castle blocked the conductor's hands and occupied their place.  
The altos watched the turrets for a cue to start their line,  
The pianist kept repeating while the towers took their time.  

[Chorus]
Rise, paper kingdom, rise into the light,  
The choir needs the conductor but the castle has his sight.  
Turn, little story, let the next page show,  
The paper forest comes between the singers and the notes.  
Rise, paper kingdom, let the little walls stay,  
The story has a landscape that is getting in the way.  

[Verse 2]
The narrator turned the second page and trees sprang to the sky,  
The conductor moved his little stand and raised his hands quite high.  
The choir found the opening and sang the forest verse,  
A paper dragon rose on cue and hid the words they rehearsed.  
The narrator lowered the book to show the children in the front,  
The choir could see the conductor and finish what they had begun.  
The dragon folded quietly when the story reached its end,  
The paper castle slept inside the book again, my friend.  

[Chorus]
Rise, paper kingdom, rise into the light,  
The choir needs the conductor but the castle has his sight.  
Turn, little story, let the next page show,  
The paper forest comes between the singers and the notes.  
Rise, paper kingdom, let the little walls stay,  
The story has a landscape that is getting in the way.  

[Bridge]
The children asked to see the book after the singers bowed,  
The narrator let each page unfold away from all the crowd.  
The bass sang softly to the castle while the children watched it rise,  
The conductor took a chair and kept his hands beside his thighs.  

[Final Chorus]
Rise, paper kingdom, rise into the light,  
The story gets its second show without a conductor tonight.  
Turn, little story, let the next page show,  
The children lead the chorus now; they know where dragons go.  
Rise, paper kingdom, let the little walls stay,  
The book gets its own lectern at the next performance day.
