# Afrobeat at the Crosswalk

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Afrobeat's interlocking guitar ostinatos, layered percussion, and extended groove*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Keep the guitar pattern steady while drums and horns add interlocking parts; the lead singer should remain calm as the crossing signal cycles.*

[Verse 1]
The band arrived at sunrise at the junction by the mill,  
A gibbon played a guitar line that rolled along the hill.  
The drummer found a steady pulse on buckets, tins, and cans,  
The horns replied in little calls that moved between the stands.  
A macaque waited patiently beside the painted stripe,  
The crossing sign said "WAIT" in letters bold and bright.  
The groove went on for several bars; the traffic rolled along,  
The singer made the waiting light the subject of the song.  

[Chorus]
Keep that groove, let the rhythm grow,  
The green hand's coming, moving slow.  
Guitar keeps rolling, drums reply,  
The crossing light is blinking in the sky.  
Keep that groove, don't break the line,  
We cross together when the signal shines.  

[Verse 2]
A capuchin began a dance step, careful on the curb,  
The horns played a short response, the bass made one word.  
The signal changed to walking; the band began to move,  
But the drummer's bucket tipped and rolled into the groove.  
The band kept playing softly while the bucket crossed ahead,  
A tortoise followed after it, with helmet on its head.  
The crossing light went flashing; the whole ensemble paused,  
The singer sang one extra verse about the traffic laws.  

[Chorus]
Keep that groove, let the rhythm grow,  
The green hand's coming, moving slow.  
Guitar keeps rolling, drums reply,  
The crossing light is blinking in the sky.  
Keep that groove, don't break the line,  
We cross together when the signal shines.  

[Bridge]
The driver saw the tortoise and held the bus in place,  
The band kept interlocking as it crossed at its own pace.  
The bucket reached the sidewalk, the drummer took it back,  
The groove continued seamlessly, no one lost the track.  

[Final Chorus]
Keep that groove, the road is clear,  
The last small step is drawing near.  
Guitar keeps rolling, horns reply,  
The band has crossed beneath the morning sky.  
Keep that groove, the signal's done,  
The tortoise takes the final run.  

[Ending]
The groove went on; the tortoise reached the garden before the band.
