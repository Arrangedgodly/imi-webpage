# The Doom-Metal Singer Needs Reading Glasses

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: slow doom-metal invocations of ancient texts, interrupted by the singer's inability to read the tiny print*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Extremely slow distorted riffs and solemn growls; long instrumental spaces give the singer time to find the right glasses.*

[Verse 1]
I opened up the ancient book beneath a crimson lamp,  
The cover promised hidden doom; the print was rather cramped.  
I held it at arm's length to read the first forbidden line,  
The bassist played a low note that would hold for quite some time.  
I said, "Beware the weary…" then I lost the smaller text,  
The drummer gave a patient roll to let me find what came next.  
My spectacles were backstage in a jacket by the door,  
The bassist held his low note as I searched across the floor.  

[Chorus]
Deep, deep, let the darkness stay,  
The ancient text is waiting while I find the proper way.  
Slow, slow, let the low strings moan,  
The smallest type has conquered me before the doom was known.  
Deep, deep, hold the final gate,  
My glasses are a vital part of what the darkness states.  

[Verse 2]
The stagehand found my jacket and he brought the glasses out,  
I put them on and read the words that I had tried to shout.  
The line said "Beware the weary hinge upon the garden shed,"  
I'd brought the house inspection notes instead of doom and dread.  
The bassist let the low note fall; the drummer stayed quite still,  
The stagehand found the actual lyric book beside the bill.  
I set it on the stand and saw the words in larger black,  
The doom resumed quite smoothly with my glasses on at last.  

[Chorus]
Deep, deep, let the darkness stay,  
The ancient text is waiting while I find the proper way.  
Slow, slow, let the low strings moan,  
The smallest type has conquered me before the doom was known.  
Deep, deep, hold the final gate,  
My glasses are a vital part of what the darkness states.  

[Bridge]
The crowd requested something from the garden-shed refrain,  
They said the weary hinge described their ordinary pain.  
We played the full inspection list with one enormous chord,  
The landlord came backstage and said the roof report was more.  

[Final Chorus]
Deep, deep, let the darkness stay,  
The landlord's inspection notes have found their dreadful day.  
Slow, slow, let the low strings moan,  
The damp patch in the cellar has a kingdom of its own.  
Deep, deep, hold the final gate,  
I can read the doom in large print while the hinges await.
