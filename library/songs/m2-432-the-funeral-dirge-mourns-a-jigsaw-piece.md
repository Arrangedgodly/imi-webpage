# The Funeral Dirge Mourns a Jigsaw Piece

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ceremonial funeral dirges whose grave language and measured procession exceed the scale of the loss*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Slow brass, muffled drum and solemn group singing; the missing piece is represented by a tiny empty cushion.*

[Verse 1]
We finished up the jigsaw on a rainy Sunday night,  
The castle had one missing stone beneath the tower light.  
We searched beneath the tabletop and looked around the chair,  
The thousand-piece enclosure had nine hundred ninety-nine there.  
My brother built a little stand and laid a cushion down,  
My sister fetched a muffled drum; I wore a solemn gown.  
We sang about the little piece that left an empty sky,  
The cat watched from the window as the tiny march went by.  

[Chorus]
Farewell, small stone, lost to the floor,  
The castle has a place for you that waits forevermore.  
Slow steps, low brass, let the long note stay,  
We cannot finish heaven till the piece has found its way.  
Farewell, small stone, leave a little light,  
The cushion bears your absence on this very rainy night.  

[Verse 2]
We carried out the empty stand toward the kitchen door,  
My brother felt a clicking in the shoe he'd worn before.  
He lifted up his slipper and he shook it by the mat,  
The little tower piece fell out; the solemn drum fell flat.  
We set it in the puzzle and the castle grew complete,  
My sister changed her muffled march to quite a happy beat.  
The brother said he'd felt it when we started up the song,  
He thought that grief was uncomfortable and kept on going along.  

[Chorus]
Farewell, small stone, lost to the floor,  
The castle has a place for you that waits forevermore.  
Slow steps, low brass, let the long note stay,  
We cannot finish heaven till the piece has found its way.  
Farewell, small stone, leave a little light,  
The cushion bears your absence on this very rainy night.  

[Bridge]
We took a finished photograph and put the cushion back,  
The cat jumped on the tabletop and gave the tower a tap.  
We slid the puzzle on a board and shut the cupboard door,  
The dirge was far too long to sing for any piece once more.  

[Final Chorus]
Welcome, small stone, home from the floor,  
The castle has its tower and we march no more.  
Slow steps, low brass, let the last note stay,  
The brother has an empty shoe and walks in comfort today.  
Welcome, small stone, shine a little light,  
The cushion carries the cat now; he sleeps through the night.
