# The Dramatic Duet Cannot Share a Microphone

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic stage duets in which technical positioning prevents the singers from addressing each other*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Two theatrical voices with sweeping piano; move the microphone audibly between singers on every response.*

[Verse 1: Singer A]
I waited by the painted bridge to tell you what I feel,  
The microphone was in the middle, bolted to a wheel.  
You stood across the river cloth, too far away to hear,  
I moved the little stand toward you and whispered very near.  
You answered in a soaring note that never reached the wire,  
I pulled the stand back toward myself to sing my own desire.  

[Chorus: Both]
Come to me, speak to me,  
Say the tender thing you want to say to me.  
Stand right here, stand right there,  
The microphone can only hold one portion of our air.  
Sing my heart across the stream,  
We need another cable to complete the lovers' dream.  

[Verse 2: Singer B]
I stepped beside the stand to sing the words I'd saved so long,  
You leaned across my shoulder and you took the louder song.  
The sound technician signaled that the cable would not stretch,  
We rolled the stand a little farther, heard a sudden screech.  
The piano played a flourish while we found the broken lead,  
Our lips kept moving for the crowd; no one could hear us plead.  

[Chorus: Both]
Come to me, speak to me,  
Say the tender thing you want to say to me.  
Stand right here, stand right there,  
The microphone can only hold one portion of our air.  
Sing my heart across the stream,  
We need another cable to complete the lovers' dream.  

[Bridge]
We stood beside each other and we sang without the stand,  
The room was small enough to hear; we finally held hands.  
The technician fixed the cable just before our ending kiss,  
His voice came through the speaker asking, "Can you both hear this?"  

[Final Chorus: Both]
Come to me, speak to me,  
Say the tender thing you want to say to me.  
Stand right here, stand right there,  
The room can hold the portions of our unamplified air.  
Sing my heart across the stream,  
The sound check gets the last note of the lovers' little dream.
