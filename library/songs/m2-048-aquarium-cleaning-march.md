# The March of the Aquarium Custodians

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: patriotic military marches and their polished spectacle applied to routine cleaning*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Bright brass-and-snare march; use synchronized mop strokes as percussion, stopping cleanly for the fish-feeding bell.*

Verse 1
We polish glass at seven sharp, we march beside the tank,  
The sergeant is a pelican who checks each mop and rank.  
A capuchin holds buckets high, a lemur marks the floor,  
The marching line advances toward the algae by the door.  
We shine the glass till every ray reflects a perfect fin,  
Then wave to every fish who stares as if we might swim in.  

Chorus
Left, right, rinse, and shine,  
Keep your boots outside the brine.  
March in time, don't tap the tank,  
The fish don't care about your rank.  
Lift that pail and clear the lane,  
The penguins have a lunch campaign.  
Left, right, rinse, and shine,  
Mop the floor and hold the line.  

Verse 2
The brass band starts a victory tune beside the jellyfish,  
The bubbles rise in perfect rows, the octopus makes a wish.  
Our captain points the feathered baton toward the dolphin pool,  
The dolphins splash our polished boots and break parade-school rule.  
We pivot in a single file, retreat behind the rail,  
The trombone player loses grip and slides into a pail.  

Bridge
The feeding bell rings twice; we stop and clear the track,  
The fish are given breakfast while the band steps back.  
A turtle checks the water, then approves our shining pane,  
We march away in silence till the next scheduled stain.  

Chorus
Left, right, rinse, and shine,  
Keep your boots outside the brine.  
March in time, don't tap the tank,  
The fish don't care about your rank.  
Lift that pail and clear the lane,  
The penguins have a lunch campaign.  
Left, right, rinse, and shine,  
Mop the floor and hold the line.  

Ending
The pelican salutes the mop, the crew returns the gear,  
The fish all swim behind the glass and leave us standing here.
