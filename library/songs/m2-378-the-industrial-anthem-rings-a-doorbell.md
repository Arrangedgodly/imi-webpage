# The Industrial Anthem Rings a Doorbell

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: industrial music that turns routine machinery into a menacing factory soundscape*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Harsh machine percussion and distorted bass; a cheerful two-note doorbell cuts through every threatening refrain.*

[Verse 1]
We sampled all the workshop sounds and built a mighty beat,  
The lathe became a monster and the press supplied its feet.  
The singer wore a metal coat and raised a welded fist,  
The engineer applied distortion till the shadows hissed.  
A courier stood outside the room with cables in a box,  
He rang the tiny doorbell and it chimed between our knocks.  

[Chorus]
Steel and steam, grind and roar,  
Who keeps ringing at the door?  
Iron night and iron day,  
There's a very cheerful chime in our machinery's way.  
Steel and steam, strike the chain,  
We cannot call the end of worlds until it stops again.  

[Verse 2]
We made the doorbell lower and we stretched its little tone,  
The courier rang it twice more while we worked inside alone.  
The singer asked for silence, raised his fist to start anew,  
The cheerful chime cut through the mix before the first beat flew.  
The engineer unplugged the bell; the courier knocked instead,  
We sampled that, distorted it, and called it industrial dread.  

[Chorus]
Steel and steam, grind and roar,  
Who keeps ringing at the door?  
Iron night and iron day,  
There's a very cheerful chime in our machinery's way.  
Steel and steam, strike the chain,  
We cannot call the end of worlds until it stops again.  

[Bridge]
The courier left his parcel and a note upon the mat,  
The singer found the cables; the engineer found the note.  
We needed all those cables for the final roaring track,  
The note said PHONE FOR SIGNATURE; we had to call him back.  

[Final Chorus]
Steel and steam, grind and roar,  
We have opened up the door.  
Iron night and iron day,  
The courier sings the cheerful chime and makes us pay.  
Steel and steam, strike the chain,  
Our heaviest hit is DING DONG stamped beside his name.
