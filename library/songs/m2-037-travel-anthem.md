# Platform Four Is Somewhere

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: travel-song tropes, station announcements, and delayed departures*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Sweeping strings under a station announcer; keep the traveler's feet on the platform.*

## Verse 1
The station board kept changing every city name.  
A lemur held a paper ticket, corners curled with rain.  
The announcer promised mountains, coast, and fields of gold.  
The train remained delayed, the platform growing cold.  
A violinist played beside the vending machine.  
She made a quiet map from every note between.  
The traveler checked the clock, then checked the track.  
A pigeon crossed the yellow line and waddled back.  

## Chorus
Platform four, take me somewhere in a song.  
The destination keeps moving; the wait is just as long.  
Platform four, keep the door in sight.  
We may leave at noon or spend the whole night.  

## Verse 2
The violinist said she'd missed three trains today.  
The traveler asked if she was headed far away.  
She pointed to a town two stops beyond the bay.  
Her aunt was waiting there with soup and room to stay.  
The board announced a platform change, then blinked.  
They followed signs and found a fountain with a sink.  
At last the doors unlocked with a tired sigh.  
They boarded with their maps and watched the station fly.  

## Bridge
The mountains stayed a picture on the window glass.  
The train moved slowly past the fields of grass.  
The traveler lent an ear to the violin refrain.  
The journey took its time and didn't need a name.  

## Chorus
Platform four, take me somewhere in a song.  
The destination keeps moving; the wait is just as long.  
Platform four, keep the door in sight.  
We may leave at noon or spend the whole night.  

## Ending
At stop two, the violinist stepped away.  
The traveler stayed aboard and watched the evening gray.
