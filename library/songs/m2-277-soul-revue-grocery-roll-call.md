# The Soul Revue's Grocery Roll Call

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soul revue call-and-response, brass stabs, and a backing chorus that answers every lead*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use warm brass and a strong lead vocal; the backing singers should enthusiastically answer an increasingly ordinary grocery list.*

[Verse 1]
The band arrived at noon inside the neighborhood store,  
A baboon held the shopping list and read it by the door.  
The singer wore a shining suit and raised a velvet hand,  
The drummer hit a pocket groove; the horns took their stand.  
He called out "Celery!" and the chorus cried "Celery!"  
He called out "Two ripe pears!" and they answered "Two ripe pears!"  
A gibbon pushing a cart began to sway in time,  
The produce clerk announced the price as if it were a rhyme.  

[Chorus]
Call it out, we answer back,  
Put the groceries in the sack.  
Lead says "milk," the choir says "milk,"  
The cart rolls smooth as polished silk.  
Call it out, the horns reply,  
A soul revue beneath fluorescent sky.  

[Verse 2]
The singer called for carrots; the chorus sang them high,  
The cart rolled past the checkout with a shopping bag nearby.  
A capuchin picked up the list and read the last small line,  
It said "one paper towel," the horns began to shine.  
The drummer played a fill; the clerk scanned every tin,  
The backing singers harmonized each beep that came within.  
The scanner hit a double beep; the chorus hit it too,  
The bandleader bowed to the machine as if it sang the blue.  

[Chorus]
Call it out, we answer back,  
Put the groceries in the sack.  
Lead says "milk," the choir says "milk,"  
The cart rolls smooth as polished silk.  
Call it out, the horns reply,  
A soul revue beneath fluorescent sky.  

[Bridge]
The cashier gave the total; the singer gave a shout,  
The chorus answered "Change!" when coins came tumbling out.  
The drummer packed his sticks away, the horns went in their case,  
The grocery clerk kept scanning at a very steady pace.  

[Final Chorus]
Call it out, the show is done,  
The groceries are packed; the day's begun.  
Lead says "thanks," the choir says "thanks,"  
The cart is parked beside the tanks.  
Call it out, the horns reply,  
A soul revue has said goodbye.  

[Ending]
The list was complete; the chorus stayed to bag the pears.
