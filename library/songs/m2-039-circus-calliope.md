# The Tightrope Accountant

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: circus calliope fanfare and elaborate big-top announcements*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Calliope bursts announce each act; give the accountant a dry, spoken-sung delivery.*

## Verse 1
The circus rolled into town with wagons painted red.  
A macaque accountant counted bolts before the show began.  
The ringmaster announced a tiger who preferred his bed.  
The calliope raced ahead of every plan.  
The tightrope walker tested each knot by hand.  
The clown forgot his pie and used a rubber band.  
The accountant checked the ticket rolls and chalked the sum.  
A pony stepped in time beside the kettle drum.  

## Chorus
Roll up, count up, keep the tent lines tight.  
A show can sparkle when the books are right.  
Roll up, count up, let the brass parade.  
The best grand entrance is the one we paid.  

## Verse 2
A storm blew through and lifted half the tent.  
The ringmaster asked where every spare tarp went.  
The accountant found them stacked beside the feed.  
The crew tied down the poles with careful speed.  
The tightrope act was moved inside the hall.  
The calliope player squeezed between the wall.  
The tiger woke and watched from one small crate.  
The crowd came in, though every seat was late.  

## Bridge
The clown brought towels, the pony pulled a cart.  
The accountant let the ringmaster have her chart.  
They shared the numbers, ropes, and jobs around.  
The tent stood firm against the thunder's sound.  

## Chorus
Roll up, count up, keep the tent lines tight.  
A show can sparkle when the books are right.  
Roll up, count up, let the brass parade.  
The best grand entrance is the one we paid.  

## Ending
The circus packed at dawn without a loss.  
The tiger slept through every final call across.
