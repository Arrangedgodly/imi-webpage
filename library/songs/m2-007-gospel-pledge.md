# The Pledge Choir

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: gospel choir conventions and fundraising telethons*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Organ swells warmly; the soloist interrupts the pledge counter with practical questions.*

## Verse 1
The chapel had a leaky roof above the second pew.  
A troop of vervets set out a bucket, bright and blue.  
The host spoke slowly, voice as velvet as the night.  
The choir stood behind him, robes in matching white.  
He asked for one small gift to raise the ceiling high.  
A drummer gave one roll that shook the rafters dry.  
A baboon in the back held up a jar of screws.  
He said, "I fixed the gutter; tell me what we choose."  

## Chorus
Lift up the roof, but check the beam below.  
Raise up the chorus, let the workmen know.  
Lift up the roof, let every pledge be clear.  
We sing with open hands, and keep the figures near.  

## Verse 2
The soloist called Aunt Mabel, keeper of the books.  
She read the estimate and gave the host a look.  
The choir hummed a chord while volunteers passed round.  
The envelope came back with a measured sum announced.  
They bought the timber locally, paid the crew on Friday.  
The choir sang the invoice total, gospel in a minor key.  
The old roof came down beneath a summer sky.  
The congregation held the tarps while bats went flying by.  

## Bridge
A pledge should have a purpose that the givers understand.  
The host put down his cards and lent a willing hand.  
The drummer carried shingles; the singers mixed the lime.  
Their harmonies grew dusty, but they kept them right in time.  

## Chorus
Lift up the roof, but check the beam below.  
Raise up the chorus, let the workmen know.  
Lift up the roof, let every pledge be clear.  
We sing with open hands, and keep the figures near.  

## Ending
The final hymn rang out beneath a roof that held.  
The bucket stayed backstage, its old leak finally quelled.
