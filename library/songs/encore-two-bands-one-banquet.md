# Two Bands, One Banquet

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Wedding reception band medleys and rival musicians competing for the dance floor*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Brass-led wedding swing that breaks into a tambourine duel.*

[Verse 1]  
The wedding hall is dressed in vines, the moon hangs like a pear.  
Two bands arrive at half past six and claim the same small stair.  
The groom says, "Play us something sweet, the aunties want to dance."  
Both bandleaders bow at once. Their eyebrows start advance.  
One says, "We know the family, we played the cousin's brunch."  
The other says, "We know the floor. We brought a second lunch."  

[Chorus]  
Two bands, one banquet, one very narrow stage.  
The bride is tapping time while they negotiate the wage.  
Give the horns a bar, give the drums a turn.  
If you fight over the spotlight, let the dance floor burn.  

[Verse 2]  
The Velvet Bananas play a waltz. The Tailspinners add a beat.  
The flower girls start marching down the aisle on tiny feet.  
An uncle swings his jacket; Grandma does a slide.  
The bands forget their feud and play the same refrain in stride.  
Then both demand the final note, each holding one long chord.  
The groom's small monkey nephew grabs a triangle from the board.  
He rings it once, the hall goes quiet, then he rings it twice.  
The bands fall in behind him, and the room erupts in rice.  

[Chorus]  
Two bands, one banquet, one very narrow stage.  
The bride is tapping time while they negotiate the wage.  
Give the horns a bar, give the drums a turn.  
If you fight over the spotlight, let the dance floor burn.  

[Bridge]  
They split the set in half, then share the cake and tips.  
The bride requests a quiet song. Both bands play alike.  

[Final Chorus]  
Two bands, one banquet, one very narrow stage.  
The bride is tapping time while they negotiate the wage.  
Give the horns a bar, give the drums a turn.  
If you fight over the spotlight, let the dance floor burn.  

[Outro]  
The lights come up at midnight. The bands pack up as friends.  
The monkey nephew keeps the triangle. The party never ends.
