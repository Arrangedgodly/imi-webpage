# Vaudeville's Missing Hat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: vaudeville patter songs, prop-based comic timing, and relentless double acts*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Quick piano patter with a snare brush; pass a battered hat between singers on every rhyme.*

Verse 1
I bought a hat for opening night, a hat with proper brim,  
My partner says it suits me, though the hat is wearing him.  
We pass it left, we pass it right, we pass it through a chair,  
It lands upon the stagehand's head and stays politely there.  
The audience expects a joke; we bow before the gag,  
The stagehand takes the hat away and fills it with the flag.  

Chorus
Where is the hat? It's over there!  
No, that's the wig upon the chair.  
A tip, a trick, a tap, a toss,  
We count the hat among the props.  
Where is the hat? We heard it fall!  
It rolled behind the painted wall.  
The show must go on, the band must play,  
The hat is billing us by day.  

Verse 2
My partner sings a tragic tune of velvet, rain, and loss,  
I interrupt to ask the hat if it has paid the boss.  
The hat rolls down the orchestra pit and stops upon a drum,  
The drummer keeps the rhythm while the hat becomes the plum.  
We reach the final couplet, every curtain starts to close,  
The hat pops up on my own head; it steals my final pose.  

Bridge
The stage manager counts to three; the hat rolls through the door,  
It stops beside the ticket booth and blocks the exit floor.  
The usher lifts it gently, finds a program and a pear,  
The audience applauds the fruit; the hat receives their stare.  

Chorus
Where is the hat? It's over there!  
No, that's the wig upon the chair.  
A tip, a trick, a tap, a toss,  
We count the hat among the props.  
Where is the hat? We heard it fall!  
It rolled behind the painted wall.  
The show must go on, the band must play,  
The hat is billing us by day.  

Ending
We take our bows; the hat takes one, its brim turned to the crowd,  
The stagehand asks for half our pay; we laugh too long and loud.
