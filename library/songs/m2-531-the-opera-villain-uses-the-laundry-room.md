# The Opera Villain Uses the Laundry Room

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operatic villain boasts about absolute power applied to a shared building laundry schedule*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Grand minor-key piano and bass-baritone voice; the neighbor responds in a plain high register.*

[Recitative]
I claim the night! I rule the tower!  
The washing machine begins my hour!  
My robes shall whirl beneath the moon!  
NEIGHBOR: My booking starts at noon.  
The villain reads the little sheet.  
His name is missing, incomplete.  
He raises both his hands to reign.  
The neighbor points him to a pen.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Spin, dark robes, turn inside the night,  
The laundry room has bookings that ignore my mighty right.  
Spin, dark robes, let the water rise,  
The neighbor has a basket that is twice my laundry's size.  
Black cape, cold heart, hear the thunder grow,  
The machine will wash at half past one if I reserve it so.  

[Verse]
I write my name below hers in the space at half past one,  
She starts her ordinary wash beneath the little sun.  
I wait beside the notice with my cape upon my knee,  
The caretaker asks if I can move so others can pass me.  
At half past one the neighbor takes her laundry from the drum,  
I put my dark robes in its place with all my doom to come.  
I choose a gentle cycle as the label on them says,  
The mighty robes turn softly while I sing my darkest days.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Spin, dark robes, turn inside the night,  
The laundry room has bookings that ignore my mighty right.  
Spin, dark robes, let the water rise,  
The neighbor has a basket that is twice my laundry's size.  
Black cape, cold heart, hear the thunder grow,  
The machine will wash at half past one if I reserve it so.  

[Recitative]
My empire has a drying rack!  
I carry all my dark robes back!  
The neighbor hangs her sheets nearby,  
My shadow does not make them dry.  
I write a new decree of power:  
Reserve the rack for one more hour.  
The caretaker returns the pen,  
The drying rack needs no booking then.  

[Final Aria / Refrain]
Spin, dark robes, let the water rise,  
The laundry label knows the cloth beneath my mighty guise.  
Black cape, cold heart, hear the thunder grow,  
The next great villain needs the booking sheet before his show.  
Spin, dark robes, turn inside the night,  
I conquer one empty basket and concede the neighbor's right.
