# Calypso Under the Grocery Awning

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: calypso call-and-response, steel-pan bright hooks, and a rainstorm that turns a grocery queue into a dance line*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Steel pan, hand drum, and jaunty bass; let the rain answer each sung call from the awning.*

Verse 1
The shoppers waited under the awning for the rain to cease,
The steel pan rang a sunny tune and brought a little peace.
A lemur called, "Which fruit is sweet?" the vendor sang, "Try plum!"
The rain replied against the roof with a bright tinny drum.

Chorus
Call for mango, answer lime,
Dance beneath the roof in time.
Rain taps out the backbeat sound,
Calypso turns the queue around.

Verse 2
A macaque held a shopping list and tried to read it dry,
The raindrops blurred the letters as the pan went passing by.
The vendor called the price aloud; the chorus called it back,
The shoppers made a rhythm line and filled each paper sack.

Chorus
Call for mango, answer lime,
Dance beneath the roof in time.
Rain taps out the backbeat sound,
Calypso turns the queue around.

Bridge
The thunder gave a rumble; the umbrellas rose in flight,
The gibbon caught a runaway and spun it left to right.
It opened like a parasol and covered all the pears,
The band kept playing while the vendor moved the crates upstairs.

Verse 3
The storm grew soft; the shoppers left in pairs,
The pan player boxed the drum and folded up the chairs.
The vendor gave a lime to each who sang along,
They walked home through the puddles with the rain inside the song.

Chorus
Call for mango, answer lime,
Dance beneath the roof in time.
Rain taps out the backbeat sound,
Calypso turns the queue around.

Ending
The awning dripped at closing, the last shoppers were away,
The rain kept time alone until the start of day.
