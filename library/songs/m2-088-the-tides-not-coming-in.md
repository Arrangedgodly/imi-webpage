# The Tide's Not Coming In

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: surf-rock guitar conventions, beach-party countdowns, and a landlocked macaque waiting for a tide at the reservoir*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Reverb guitar and handclaps; let the wave sound fade into the calm reservoir at the end.*

Verse 1
A macaque brought his surfboard to the reservoir at noon,
He waited by the water for the tide to bring the moon.
His band plugged in the amplifier and struck a rolling chord,
A duck paddled by the shoreline and ignored the board.

Chorus
Ride the wave, ride the sound,
Wait for water to turn around.
Shoreline calls, guitar rings,
No one knows what the reservoir brings.

Verse 2
The singer sang of breakers and a far-off salty spray,
The drummer counted sixteen beats; the water stayed away.
The surfboard slid across the grass and bumped into a chair,
The guitarist leaned for drama and found dry air.

Chorus
Ride the wave, ride the sound,
Wait for water to turn around.
Shoreline calls, guitar rings,
No one knows what the reservoir brings.

Bridge
The band announced high tide, then announced it once again,
A goose walked through the chorus and returned at half past ten.
The macaque checked a chart and found the nearest coast was miles,
The duck had nested by his board and settled in awhile.

Verse 3
They moved the show beside the bay where actual waves rolled in,
The first small breaker reached the sand and soaked the singer's shin.
The board stood straight, the guitar rang, the drummer dropped a stick,
The duck arrived by ferry with a shell tucked in its beak.

Chorus
Ride the wave, ride the sound,
Wait for water to turn around.
Shoreline calls, guitar rings,
Now the reservoir knows what the ocean brings.

Ending
The band kept one dry chorus for their inland friends to play,
The tide came in at sunset and took the duck away.
