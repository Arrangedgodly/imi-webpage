# The Hold Music Choir Promises an Answer

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel-style advertising choruses that promise salvation through a customer-service call*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Organ and exuberant choir; a solo caller answers with steadily dwindling optimism.*

[Verse 1]
I called about a parcel that had never reached my door,  
The operator told me I was caller twenty-four.  
A choir rose behind her in a brilliant major chord,  
They said my call was precious and would never be ignored.  
I laid the phone upon the table, cooked a pot of rice,  
The choir sang the promise once and then repeated twice.  

[Chorus]
Somebody will answer, somebody will hear,  
Keep your little handset and your weary heart near.  
Your call is our treasure, your patience is gold,  
The choir has another verse to sing while you're on hold.  
Lift up your number, let the voices soar,  
You have advanced from twenty-four to twenty-four.  

[Verse 2]
The tenors took a higher part, the basses thundered low,  
The rice was cold upon my plate, the sun had lost its glow.  
A voice asked if I'd like a call when someone could reply,  
I pressed the number offered and the choir reached the sky.  
The phone went silent; minutes passed; it rang beside my chair,  
I answered to the same old choir proclaiming they were there.  

[Chorus]
Somebody will answer, somebody will hear,  
Keep your little handset and your weary heart near.  
Your call is our treasure, your patience is gold,  
The choir has another verse to sing while you're on hold.  
Lift up your number, let the voices soar,  
You have advanced from twenty-four to twenty-four.  

[Bridge]
A human finally answered and requested what I'd need,  
I said I'd lost a parcel; she pulled its tracking feed.  
It waited at the depot and had been there since the dawn,  
She asked about the service; I began to sing her song.  

[Final Chorus]
Somebody has answered, somebody has heard,  
I took my little parcel and I sang them every word.  
My call was their treasure, my patience was gold,  
I put their survey caller on a musical hold.  
Lift up your number, let the voices soar,  
I sing until she asks if we can end at twenty-four.
