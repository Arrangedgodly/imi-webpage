# The Gospel Choir Finds the Lost-and-Found

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel call-and-response arrangements lending their full conviction to a coat check*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Organ, handclaps and a lead answered by the whole choir; the attendant sings only when checking an actual item.*

[Verse 1]
We finished Sunday singing with our voices lifted high,  
The choir took its heavy coats beneath a clearing sky.  
Our lead put on a velvet hat and headed for the door,  
A voice behind the counter said, "That hat is not yours."  
He turned with all the dignity a final solo brings,  
The feather on the borrowed hat kept brushing against things.  
His own hat had a number pinned beneath its modest brim,  
The attendant held the numbered slip and read it out to him.  

[Response Refrain]
Lead: Have you seen my traveling hat?  
Choir: We have seen a hat like that.  
Lead: Does it have a feather tall?  
Choir: Yours has no feather at all.  
Lead: Bring my head its proper crown.  
Choir: Take the borrowed feather down.  
All: Let the number tell the story at the counter by the door,  
The coat check has a hat for you; the fancy one is four.  

[Verse 2]
He handed back the feather with a flourish and a bow,  
The attendant put it on its hook and asked, "Your number now?"  
He searched his pockets slowly while the choir kept the beat,  
He found a mint, a bus pass and a folded music sheet.  
The bass produced the little slip he'd kept beside his score,  
He'd held it for the soloist before we sang once more.  
The attendant matched the number to a plain felt hat of gray,  
Our lead put on his modest hat and blessed the coming day.  

[Response Refrain]
Lead: Have you seen my traveling hat?  
Choir: We have seen a hat like that.  
Lead: Does it have a feather tall?  
Choir: Yours has no feather at all.  
Lead: Bring my head its proper crown.  
Choir: Take the borrowed feather down.  
All: Let the number tell the story at the counter by the door,  
The coat check has a hat for you; the fancy one is four.  

[Bridge]
The owner of the feather came and thanked us for its care,  
She heard our little chorus and she asked to join us there.  
We left the feather on its hook until the singing ceased,  
The coat-check queue moved forward to a brisker final beat.  

[Final Response Refrain]
Lead: Have you seen my traveling hat?  
Choir: You are wearing it; there's that.  
Lead: Bring my head its proper crown.  
Choir: Keep the gray one on your crown.  
All: Let the number tell the story, let the counter clear its queue,  
The attendant sings, "Your mittens," and we answer, "Those are blue."
