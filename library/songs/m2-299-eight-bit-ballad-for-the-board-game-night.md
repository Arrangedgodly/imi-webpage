# Eight-Bit Ballad for Board-Game Night

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: retro game soundtrack loops, pixel-era power-ups, and sound effects that reward the wrong move*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Chiptune pulse with square-wave lead; each board-game mistake triggers a triumphant sound that the players misread.*

**Verse 1**
The club turns down the lights and sets the checkerboard in place.  
A tiny speaker loops one tune at a deliberate pace.  
A macaque moves a rook; the chip goes, "Power gained!"  
The rook was on a pawn's square; the player feels acclaimed.  
A knight jumps over three pieces; the speaker plays a cheer.  
The rulebook says the move is wrong, but no one wants to hear.  

**Chorus**
One more life, one more move, the pixels cheer us on;  
A bright little beep means the old mistake is gone.  
Round the board, roll the dice, the screen is in command;  
The game keeps giving bonus points for pieces off the land.  

**Verse 2**
The dice roll off the table and the speaker plays a win.  
The club awards a golden star to whoever picks them in.  
The smallest player claims a crown; the tune begins to climb.  
The rules chair tries to intervene, but can't get in on time.  
The board is almost empty; the score display says ten.  
They restart the match; the same tune begins again.  

**Chorus**
One more life, one more move, the pixels cheer us on;  
A bright little beep means the old mistake is gone.  
Round the board, roll the dice, the screen is in command;  
The game keeps giving bonus points for pieces off the land.  

**Bridge**
They unplug the speaker, read the rules, and start a slower round.  
The knight moves once, the rook stays put, no victory noise resounds.  
The players pass the dice around and count each move by hand.  
The quiet makes the smallest win feel properly well-planned.  

**Ending**
The board is cleared; the pieces sleep inside a wooden case.  
The speaker gives one final beep, then powers down in place.  
The club goes home with no high score, no flashing neon light.  
The rules chair wins the evening by one legal knight.
