# The Dinner Musical Orders an Encore

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ensemble musical finales and singers treating a restaurant dessert order as another opportunity for a grand reprise*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Large ensemble voices with jaunty piano; the waiter speaks a concise dessert menu over the opening flourish.*

[Verse 1]
We finished up our musical and went to dine next door,  
The singer thanked the waiter for the meals we'd waited for.  
The plates were cleared; the waiter asked if dessert would be desired,  
The cast sang back an encore with the energy required.  
The waiter asked which dessert we meant, and held the printed card,  
The singer said the greatest one, with harmony quite large.  
The menu had an apple tart, ice cream and lemon cake,  
The waiter needed choices that his kitchen could then make.  

[Chorus]
One more course, one more song,  
The dinner has a final act before the night is gone.  
One more course, let the warm notes stay,  
The menu has three endings that the cast must choose today.  
Big bow, small plate, let the last lights glow,  
The waiter wants the order before any food can go.  

[Verse 2]
The soprano chose the apple tart, the tenor chose ice cream,  
The bass asked for the lemon cake to close his dinner dream.  
The others gave their choices in a plain and shorter phrase,  
The waiter wrote the order down without a singer's praise.  
The desserts arrived together and the cast put music by,  
The soprano took a forkful with no note left to try.  
The waiter brought the bill after the last small plate was clear,  
The bass said, "Check the lemon cake; I see it entered here."  

[Chorus]
One more course, one more song,  
The dinner has a final act before the night is gone.  
One more course, let the warm notes stay,  
The menu has three endings that the cast must choose today.  
Big bow, small plate, let the last lights glow,  
The waiter wants the order before any food can go.  

[Bridge]
We paid the bill together and we thanked him in a chord,  
He said the kitchen liked a quiet written order on the board.  
We left a generous tip beside the folded menu page,  
The waiter kept the card as if it were a little stage.  

[Final Chorus]
One last course, let the last lights glow,  
The next cast has a printed list of choices it can show.  
Big bow, small plate, let the warm notes stay,  
The waiter has our thank-you chord to hum the plates away.  
One last course, one last song,  
The chef asks for a reprise; the kitchen has been listening all along.
