# Carol of the Cold Sap

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: winter-carol harmonies, seasonal imagery, and a choir whose sap bucket freezes into a percussion instrument*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Gentle four-part carol with handbells and a frozen bucket tapped softly as a fifth voice.*

Verse 1
The frost lay on the branches and the moon was shining clear,
A lemur led the winter choir with a bell beside her ear.
They sang about the rising sap and how it sleeps below,
The bucket by the maple made a ringing note of snow.

Chorus
Warm the branch and hush the breeze,
Let the cold rest in the trees.
Ring the bell and share the light,
Sap will stir when days grow bright.

Verse 2
The choir sang in four-part harmony beneath the frozen limb,
The tenor missed his entry; the bucket answered him.
The bell rang sharp, the bucket rang, the singers found the chord,
A capuchin brought mittens and a scarf to keep the score.

Chorus
Warm the branch and hush the breeze,
Let the cold rest in the trees.
Ring the bell and share the light,
Sap will stir when days grow bright.

Bridge
They carried wood to keep the small fire glowing by the stand,
The choir held one soft note as frost drew lace on every hand.
The bucket cracked a little, then it chimed a lower tone,
The singers marked the new note down and gave it one bell of its own.

Verse 3
By morning, thaw had reached the bark; the bucket lost its ice,
The choir sang the closing verse and shared warm tea and rice.
The sap began to travel, and the bell rang clear and slow,
The frozen bucket joined the shelf, no longer made of snow.

Chorus
Warm the branch and hush the breeze,
Let the cold rest in the trees.
Ring the bell and share the light,
Sap will stir when days grow bright.

Ending
The choir left the branch in peace; the thaw moved through the night,
The bucket kept one crystal ring, then let the morning light.
