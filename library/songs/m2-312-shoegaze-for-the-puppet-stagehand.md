# Shoegaze for the Puppet Stagehand

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: shoegaze's wall of guitar and downcast stage presence that obscures the show cues*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Hazy guitars and blurred harmonies; the band keeps looking at its pedals while the puppets wait for their cue.*

**Verse 1**
The puppet stage is ready; the dragon waits on string.  
The guitarist watches pedals more than anything.  
A wall of sound rolls over every whispered cue.  
The dragon opens up its jaw; the stagehand can't get through.  
The singer looks at silver shoes and sings a drifting line.  
The puppets wave in silence, waiting for their time.  

**Chorus**
Look down, look down, the light is getting low;  
Turn up, turn up, the strings begin to glow.  
The dragon waits, the knight waits, the curtain waits in blue;  
The song is getting louder, but the cue is overdue.  

**Verse 2**
The drummer taps the riser; the puppet knight falls flat.  
The guitar swells so slowly that the stagehand misses that.  
He tugs the dragon's cable; one wing flaps with the chord.  
The audience applauds, believing it was scored.  
The stage manager raises one small red cue card high.  
The guitarist sees a pedal light and bends to ask it why.  

**Chorus**
Look down, look down, the light is getting low;  
Turn up, turn up, the strings begin to glow.  
The dragon waits, the knight waits, the curtain waits in blue;  
The song is getting louder, but the cue is overdue.  

**Bridge**
The band turns down the amps and faces up at last.  
The stagehand gives the signal; the dragon rises fast.  
The knight is lifted upright; the curtain moves on cue.  
A clear guitar note cuts the haze and guides the chorus through.  

**Ending**
The puppets take their bows; the band packs pedals slow.  
The stagehand finds the cue card where the cables go.  
The dragon folds its wings; the knight returns backstage.  
The guitarist looks up once and sees the empty stage.
