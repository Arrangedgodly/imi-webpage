# Loop the Loop, Then Let It Go

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: live vocal-looping performance conventions, layered self-harmonies, and a singer who forgets to stop recording the first layer*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Build a live vocal loop one layer at a time; mute the first track for the ending so the singer can leave the room.*

Verse 1
A gibbon set a pedal down and tested every wire,
She sang a low note into it; the speaker made it higher.
She looped a second harmony and tapped a steady beat,
The first note stayed behind her, repeating at her feet.

Chorus
Loop it once and let it ride,
Stack the voices side by side.
Find the switch and let it go,
Leave the quiet room to grow.

Verse 2
She added claps and a breathy line, then snapped her fingers twice,
The loop repeated every sound and made the room sound nice.
A capuchin joined the chorus, then stepped away to eat,
His part kept singing on its own and followed down the street.

Chorus
Loop it once and let it ride,
Stack the voices side by side.
Find the switch and let it go,
Leave the quiet room to grow.

Bridge
The singer tried to stop the track; the pedal wore her shoe,
She hummed a lower note to find the switch and tried it too.
The layers faded one by one; the room grew still at last,
The first note kept on looping from a cable in the past.

Verse 3
A stagehand found the hidden pedal under one small chair,
He pressed it with a broomstick; silence filled the air.
The singer gave one live note, unrecorded and alone,
The audience heard it clearly, then let it find its home.

Chorus
Loop it once and let it ride,
Stack the voices side by side.
Find the switch and let it go,
Leave the quiet room to grow.

Ending
The pedal was unplugged; the singer walked away,
One faint loop stayed in the hallway till the break of day.
