# Round the Choir Around the Pears

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: choral rounds, overlapping entrances, and a fruit choir that cannot agree on its starting pitch*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Each section enters two bars later; let the voices tangle, then resolve in a clean final chord.*

[Verse 1]
The choir met at harvest to sing beside the tree,
A capuchin began the tune in bright D major three.
The altos came in early; the tenors came in late,
The pears fell one by one and rolled to form a gate.

[Round]
Sing when I sing, but start a little later,
Hold the note and pass it to your neighbor.
Sing when I sing, but keep the pear in sight,
The chorus circles left and lands on right.

[Verse 2]
The basses sang the orchard; the sopranos sang the sun,
The altos sang the basket where the golden pears were spun.
The gibbon choir director raised a hand to stop the sound;
The pears rolled round his feet and turned his cue around.

[Round]
Sing when I sing, but start a little later,
Hold the note and pass it to your neighbor.
Sing when I sing, but keep the pear in sight,
The chorus circles left and lands on right.

[Bridge]
A macaque picked up one pear and used it as the pitch,
The choir matched its hollow note; the director lost his switch.
He joined the round a measure late, then entered twice again;
The pear rolled down the front row and became the choir's refrain.

[Final Round]
Sing when I sing, but start a little later,
Hold the note and pass it to your neighbor.
Sing when I sing, but keep the pear in sight,
The chorus circles left and lands on right.

[Ending]
They finished on one chord; the pear rolled out in time.
