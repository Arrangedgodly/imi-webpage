# The Barbershop Four Outvotes the Barber

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: barbershop harmony groups whose consensus on every chord overwhelms a barber's simple haircut instructions*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Four unaccompanied voices with rich held chords; the barber speaks short practical lines between elaborate harmonized replies.*

[Verse 1]
Our tenor booked a haircut for a photograph at three,  
The other singers joined him for some four-part company.  
The barber asked how short he'd like the hair around his ear,  
The tenor said a little trim; the bass replied, "Much clearer."  
The lead proposed a side part and the alto wanted height,  
We sang our four opinions in a chord that sounded right.  
The barber held his scissors still and looked across the chair,  
He said he needed one request before he touched the hair.  

[Chorus]
Four voices, one head, hold the harmony,  
There is only one appointment and the rest are here for free.  
Short sides, tall top, let the answer ring,  
The barber needs a haircut plan; we give him notes to sing.  
Four voices, one head, settle on a line,  
The tenor has the actual hair; the bass thinks it is mine.  

[Verse 2]
The barber sent us to the bench and faced the tenor alone,  
He asked him for a picture on his little mobile phone.  
The tenor chose a tidy cut and nodded at the screen,  
The barber started clipping while we watched the mirror gleam.  
We hummed a soft agreement till the bass began to doubt,  
The barber raised one finger and our quiet chord went out.  
The haircut finished neatly; the tenor rose quite pleased,  
We sang a grand approval with the bass an octave deep.  

[Chorus]
Four voices, one head, hold the harmony,  
There is only one appointment and the rest are here for free.  
Short sides, tall top, let the answer ring,  
The barber needs a haircut plan; we give him notes to sing.  
Four voices, one head, settle on a line,  
The tenor has the actual hair; the bass thinks it is mine.  

[Bridge]
The barber brought a mirror out to show the tidy back,  
The bass said he would like the same; the others answered back.  
The barber took our next three names and booked them separately,  
Each haircut had a soloist; the bench became the gallery.  

[Final Chorus]
Four voices, four heads, hold the harmony,  
The barber has four bookings and the waiting bench is free.  
Short sides, tall top, let the answer ring,  
We look exactly like a quartet trying hard to sing.  
Four voices, four heads, pay for every line,  
The barber takes a photograph and calls us Closing Time.
