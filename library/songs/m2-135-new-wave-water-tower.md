# New Wave at the Water Tower

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: new-wave synth-pop conventions, clipped drum-machine patterns, and a water-tower light that keeps blinking the chorus cue*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Bright synth arpeggio, clipped drums, and cool vocals; treat the utility light as an ominous cue until its crew interrupts the mystique.*

Verse 1
The new band met at midnight by the water tower gate,
A singer watched one signal blink and whispered, "This is fate."
The drum machine kept ticking; the synths ran cold and blue,
The light blinked twice above the pump. "The night has spoken through."

Chorus
Blink once, blink twice, command the night,
Synths run blue beneath the light.
Hold the pose, receive the sign,
The tower sends a coded line.

Verse 2
The singer turned to face the crowd and whispered, "Water sleeps,"
The bassist hummed the secrets that a silent cistern keeps.
The light blinked red; the drummer froze and dropped his final beat,
The singer said, "The world is warning us to leave the street."

Chorus
Blink once, blink twice, command the night,
Synths run blue beneath the light.
Hold the pose, receive the sign,
The tower sends a coded line.

Bridge
The utility crew came in with ladders from the shed,
"The tank is low," the foreman said. "That blink you saw was red."
"So crimson means desire?" he asked. "A dark ancestral sign?"
"It means your speaker's in our way. Please move behind the line."

Verse 3
The singer turned the user guide into his latest hit,
The crowd all sang "Inspect the valve" and loved the grit of it.
The crew collected royalties for every numbered tank,
The singer bowed, but cash went to the waterworks' own bank.

Chorus
Blink once, blink twice, command the night,
Synths run blue beneath the light.
Hold the pose, receive the sign,
The tower sends a coded line.

Ending
The band announced "Mysteries of Flow," a sold-out synth-pop show,
The foreman brought a bucket: "Take your fog machine and go."
