# The Disco Band Orders a Mirrorball

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: disco revival bands and promoters who purchase the smallest possible version of a glamorous stage accessory*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor drums, bright bass and unison voices; the tiny mirrorball is presented on a fingertip.*

[Verse 1]
We promised all the dancers there would be a silver moon,  
A spinning ball of glitter to illuminate the room.  
The parcel reached the drummer and he tore the paper wide,  
A little Christmas ornament was nestled there inside.  
The advert showed a ballroom with the picture blown up big,  
The measurements were printed in the corner of the rig.  
The drummer read "three centimeters" slowly to the band,  
The moon that we'd been waiting for could fit inside his hand.  

[Chorus]
Turn, silver moon, turn around,  
Throw your little sparks across the pounding disco sound.  
Shine, silver moon, let the room ignite,  
One dancer gets the benefit of all your tiny light.  
Move, everybody, step in to see,  
The entire disco universe is smaller than my knee.  

[Verse 2]
We asked the lighting operator what the ball could do,  
She hung it on a little stand and aimed a beam right through.  
A patch of tiny fragments danced across the drummer's hat,  
The audience applauded while he tried to follow that.  
She switched the large colored lights to sweep across the floor,  
The crowd could dance beneath them and the show needed no more.  
The drummer kept the little ball beside his sparkling snare,  
Its silver patch kept circling through the newly colored air.  

[Chorus]
Turn, silver moon, turn around,  
Throw your little sparks across the pounding disco sound.  
Shine, silver moon, let the room ignite,  
One dancer gets the benefit of all your tiny light.  
Move, everybody, step in to see,  
The entire disco universe is smaller than my knee.  

[Bridge]
A child asked at the interval if we could sell the moon,  
The drummer said he'd bought it for a very different tune.  
She pointed to its little hook and mentioned Christmas day,  
He sold it for its cost and let her carry it away.  

[Final Chorus]
Turn, colored lights, turn around,  
The drummer has recovered what he spent upon the sound.  
Move, everybody, step in to see,  
The moon will have a better job upon a Christmas tree.  
Shine, silver moon, let the small lights sway,  
We ask for all the measurements before the next display.
