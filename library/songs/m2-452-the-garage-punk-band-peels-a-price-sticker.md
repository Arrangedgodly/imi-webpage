# The Garage-Punk Band Peels a Price Sticker

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: furious garage-punk attacks on commercial labels, confronted by a particularly stubborn price sticker on a new guitar case*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Raw guitar, fast drums and rough group shouting; the adhesive's quiet ripping sound supplies the tiniest possible breakdown.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a secondhand guitar and got a case thrown in,  
The price was on a sticker stuck beside the little pin.  
I shouted that no label could define the things we play,  
Then picked the sticker's corner with my thumbnail all the day.  
The paper came in fragments but the glue remained quite bright,  
The bassist said the paper won the opening fight.  
I wrote a furious chorus while I rubbed the sticky patch,  
The drummer brought a cloth along and watched my temper hatch.  

[Chorus]
Tear that label, make it go,  
We have got a little price tag putting on a stubborn show.  
Break that hold, shout it clear,  
The sticker's lost its numbers but the glue is staying here.  
Tear that label, take your stand,  
The label has no drummer and it's still beating the band.  

[Verse 2]
We asked the shop what cleaned the case without erasing paint,  
The owner showed the cloth he used and wiped it where it stained.  
He cleared the patch in seconds and returned the tidy case,  
The bassist said the second verse had lost its angry place.  
I said we'd sing about the first defeat we'd suffered there,  
The drummer put a paper price tag gently in my hair.  
It read "One stubborn frontman, extra patience with each sale,"  
I tore it off quite easily; the paper had no tail.  

[Chorus]
Tear that label, make it go,  
We have got a little price tag putting on a stubborn show.  
Break that hold, shout it clear,  
The sticker's lost its numbers but the glue is staying here.  
Tear that label, take your stand,  
The label has no drummer and it's still beating the band.  

[Bridge]
At the gig I told the audience about the sticky fight,  
The shopkeeper was at the back and held his cloth up high.  
The crowd applauded his quick hands; the drummer gave a roll,  
My most rebellious enemy now kept the case quite whole.  

[Final Chorus]
Tear that label, make it go,  
The case is clean and ready for the next garage show.  
Break that hold, shout it clear,  
The shopkeeper gets our thanks and leaves another flyer here.  
Tear that label, take your stand,  
His little shop logo now decorates our van.
