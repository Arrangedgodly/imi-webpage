# Rain Loop in the Treehouse

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: lo-fi study beats, vinyl crackle, repeated rain samples, and a student who loops the same page without reading it*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the beat soft and repetitive; let the rain loop skip at the moment the student notices.*

[Verse 1]
A young lemur opened up a book inside the tree,
The rain tapped on the roof in soft 4/4 harmony.
A little tape deck hummed beside a mug of tea,
The same two pages passed her by repeatedly.

[Chorus]
Rain loop, page turn, let the quiet stay,
Rain loop, page turn, make the evening gray.
Rain loop, page turn, one more line to do,
The tape keeps playing; the page keeps looking new.
Rain loop, page turn, let the quiet stay,
Rain loop, page turn, make the evening gray.
Rain loop, page turn, one more line to do,
The tape keeps playing; the page keeps looking new.

[Verse 2]
She underlined a sentence, then forgot what it had said,
The pencil rolled behind her and the notes fell from the bed.
A macaque tutor knocked and asked if she was through,
She said, "I'm on the first page," though the clock read two.

[Chorus]
Rain loop, page turn, let the quiet stay,
Rain loop, page turn, make the evening gray.
Rain loop, page turn, one more line to do,
The tape keeps playing; the page keeps looking new.
Rain loop, page turn, let the quiet stay,
Rain loop, page turn, make the evening gray.
Rain loop, page turn, one more line to do,
The tape keeps playing; the page keeps looking new.

[Bridge]
The tape began to wobble; the rain sample skipped,
The student heard the same word every time the playback slipped.
She paused the loop, read one whole page, and put the pencil down,
Then packed her book and walked outside to watch the rain fall down.

[Final Chorus]
Rain loop, page turn, let the quiet stay,
Rain loop, page turn, the book can wait till day.
Rain loop, page turn, now the night is through,
The tape is on repeat; the student isn't too.

[Ending]
The rain kept on outside; the cassette clicked to silence.
