# Dish Pit Dub

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: ska revival, kitchen-shift camaraderie, and offbeat dishroom rhythm*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Offbeat guitar, bright horns, and a sink full of pans used as the percussion section.*

## Verse 1
At closing time the diner sent its plates downstairs.  
A capuchin dish porter tied a towel around his ears.  
The sink ran hot; the radio ran thin.  
A ska band played from a speaker near the bin.  
The cook brought pans in stacks that reached the light.  
The porter bounced each one into the rinse just right.  
A busboy tapped the cutlery against a tray.  
The horn section answered from a jukebox far away.  

## Chorus
Skank through the dish pit, keep the water clear.  
The shift gets lighter when the whole crew's here.  
Skank through the dish pit, stack the silver straight.  
The last plate's coming, and we won't be late.  

## Verse 2
The new kid dropped a ladle in the foam.  
The porter fished it out and showed him how to comb.  
They sorted forks by length and cups by size.  
The cook swept up the crumbs and joined their bright surprise.  
The dishwasher kept time with a steady rattling drum.  
The manager came through and let the crew finish one.  
They dried the last pan, set it on the rack.  
The radio played a brass break, then doubled back.  

## Bridge
The work was still the work, the sink still held the grime.  
But hands could find a rhythm through the end of time.  
They signed the closing sheet and took their coats.  
The cook packed soup in jars for all the throats.  

## Chorus
Skank through the dish pit, keep the water clear.  
The shift gets lighter when the whole crew's here.  
Skank through the dish pit, stack the silver straight.  
The last plate's coming, and we won't be late.  

## Ending
Outside, the night bus hissed beside the curb.  
The kitchen crew walked out still humming the last chord.
