# Little Lantern on the Shelf

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: lullaby conventions, nursery reassurance, and nighttime household sounds*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Gentle rocking pulse, low strings, and a chorus soft enough for a sleeping room.*

## Verse 1
Little lantern on the shelf, your paper sides are gold.  
The night has put its coat on; the hallway's getting cold.  
A baby macaque blinks beneath a quilt of blue.  
The caregiver hums a tune she learned when she was two.  
The rain ticks on the gutter, slow as tapping feet.  
The dog has found a blanket and curled up neat.  
A truck rolls down the street, then fades beyond the wall.  
The lantern keeps its little circle through it all.  

## Chorus
Rest now, little one, the roof is holding tight.  
The stars can do their watching till the morning light.  
Rest now, little one, your hands can let things go.  
The house is breathing softly, moving gentle and slow.  

## Verse 2
A nightlight paints a ribbon by the bedroom door.  
The caregiver sets a glass of water on the floor.  
She checks the window latch and smooths the blanket seam.  
The baby's eyelids close halfway into a dream.  
Outside, the rain has shifted toward the park.  
A moth taps once against the lantern's paper mark.  
The lullaby grows quieter, nearly just a breath.  
The room feels wide enough for sleep to settle in.  

## Bridge
Tomorrow has its questions, shoes and cereal bowls.  
Tonight the little body gets to rest its rolls.  
The caregiver will stay until the breathing finds its pace.  
The lantern throws a soft leaf shadow on the wall's face.  

## Chorus
Rest now, little one, the roof is holding tight.  
The stars can do their watching till the morning light.  
Rest now, little one, your hands can let things go.  
The house is breathing softly, moving gentle and slow.  

## Ending
The lantern waits on the shelf, still and small.  
The rain moves east; no one wakes at all.
