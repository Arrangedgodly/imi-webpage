# The Sea Shanty Crew Paddles a Pedal Boat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rugged sea shanties whose grand voyages exceed the size and capabilities of the vessel*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Unaccompanied work-song chorus, stamping feet and a creaking pedal crank; emphasize each very small nautical achievement.*

[Verse 1]
We signed aboard the Swan at noon beside the boating lake,  
The captain wore a paper hat the rental clerk had made.  
He spoke of storm and distant ports and ancient charts to read,  
The clerk said, "Thirty minutes, and remember: use your feet."  
We climbed into its molded seats with biscuit crumbs and pride,  
The Swan had two small pedals and a handle at its side.  
The captain called for canvas, but the mate could find no sail,  
We pushed away from landing to a very gentle hail.  

[Chorus]
Heave, lads, heave, let the paddles go,  
We have crossed beyond the reeds where the little ducklings row.  
Pull, crew, pull, there's a distant shore,  
It is twenty yards behind us and we've seen it twice before.  
Heave, lads, heave, hear the captain cry,  
The rental clock is ticking and the biscuit tin is dry.  
Roll, Swan, roll, carry us away,  
We are mighty lake explorers till the end of half an hour's pay.  

[Verse 2]
Our mate grew tired of pedaling and leaned across the rail,  
The captain took the other side; we traced a perfect trail.  
It curled around a lily bed and ended where we'd been,  
The mate called it a whirlpool and the captain called it wind.  
The rental clerk waved gently from the landing with a sign,  
It said to share the pedaling and keep the bow in line.  
We worked the crank together till the Swan moved straight ahead,  
The captain called it teamwork; then he asked who'd brought the bread.  

[Chorus]
Heave, lads, heave, let the paddles go,  
We have crossed beyond the reeds where the little ducklings row.  
Pull, crew, pull, there's a distant shore,  
It is twenty yards behind us and we've seen it twice before.  
Heave, lads, heave, hear the captain cry,  
The rental clock is ticking and the biscuit tin is dry.  
Roll, Swan, roll, carry us away,  
We are mighty lake explorers till the end of half an hour's pay.  

[Bridge]
The captain saw the minute hand and steered us toward the dock,  
We landed with one minute left according to the clock.  
The clerk returned our deposit and unrolled the waiting line,  
Another crew boarded the Swan and left us standing dry.  

[Final Chorus]
Heave, lads, heave, let the paddles go,  
We have earned our little shore leave where the lemonade trees grow.  
Pull, crew, pull, there is work ashore,  
The clerk has twenty lifejackets to hang beside the door.  
Heave, lads, heave, hear the captain cry,  
He has rented us a rowboat; now our hands can have a try.  
Roll, Swan, roll, carry them away,  
Our feet have signed a mutiny and asked for double pay.
