# The Lullaby Wakes the Dreaming Dog

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soothing bedtime songs and singers whose assurances of perfect quiet are repeatedly answered by a dreaming household pet*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Gentle fingerpicked guitar and close quiet singing; the dog yips in sleep until the singer finally stops.*

[Verse 1]
Sleep beneath the little quilt and let the evening fold,  
The lamp is dim, the window shut, the stars outside are cold.  
I sit beside your pillow with a softly strummed guitar,  
The dog lies at the bedroom door and dreams of running far.  
He makes a little yipping sound and kicks his paws in sleep,  
You raise your head to ask me what the dog could want to keep.  
I sing about a quiet world where nothing makes a sound,  
He answers with a sleepy bark and scratches at the ground.  

[Refrain]
Hush, little room, hold the night,  
The dog is chasing something in the absence of the light.  
Hush, little room, settle down,  
The dream has got a rabbit on the other side of town.  
Rest, little heart, drift along,  
The dog has taken over all the answering in the song.  

[Verse 2]
I stop the soft guitar and let the little strings lie still,  
The dog wakes at the silence and looks up beside the sill.  
He stretches in the doorway, then he walks toward your bed,  
He settles on the little rug beneath your sleepy head.  
You reach a hand to stroke him and he lets a long sigh go,  
You put your head back on the pillow, breathing soft and slow.  
I place the quiet guitar inside its case beside the wall,  
The bedtime song has finished without any ending at all.  

[Refrain, whispered]
Hush, little room, hold the night,  
The dog is chasing something in the absence of the light.  
Hush, little room, settle down,  
The dream has got a rabbit on the other side of town.  
Rest, little heart, drift along,  
The dog has taken over all the answering in the song.  

[Coda]
I leave the door a little wide and dim the hall's last lamp,  
The dog lies very quietly upon his small rug camp.  
I hum a note beneath my breath while going down the stair,  
A sleepy little yip returns from someone dreaming there.  
I stop beside the bottom step and listen for a while,  
The dog is dreaming quietly; I answer with a smile.  
The song will wait till morning when the house is wide awake,  
The rabbit gets the whole night for whatever route it takes.
