# Hush Now, the Elevator Sleeps

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soft music-box lullabies and overprotective bedtime rituals*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Gentle celesta and brushed guitar; keep the final refrain quiet while the elevator insists on announcing every floor.*

Verse 1
Close your eyes, the hallway dims, the moon is on the stair,  
A small macaque has brushed her teeth and folded every chair.  
Her grandpa sings a lullaby beside the elevator door,  
It dings at every passing floor and asks if they want more.  
The night guard whispers, "Please be still," the lift replies, "Level three,"  
The sleepy family counts the floors and sings in harmony.  

Chorus
Hush now, little lift, don't ring,  
Dream of cables, dream of spring.  
Carry all the dreams upstairs,  
Leave the dings outside the lair.  
Softly hum and close your eyes,  
The lobby's full of lullabies.  
Hush now, little lift, be kind,  
Let the buttons rest and unwind.  

Verse 2
The baby hears the doorbell chime and sits up in the bed,  
Grandpa sings the lowest note and pats the quilt instead.  
The lift announces "Basement level," though no one pressed a key,  
A sleepy mouse has leaned against the panel at one-thirty.  
They walk downstairs to find him there, asleep against the rail,  
He hugs the broom like teddy bear and snores a tiny scale.  

Bridge
The guard brings tea, the mouse wakes up and says he missed his floor,  
They guide him to the proper lift and close the lobby door.  
The elevator holds its breath; one lamp begins to glow,  
It carries him to level four and whispers, "Here you go."  

Chorus
Hush now, little lift, don't ring,  
Dream of cables, dream of spring.  
Carry all the dreams upstairs,  
Leave the dings outside the lair.  
Softly hum and close your eyes,  
The lobby's full of lullabies.  
Hush now, little lift, be kind,  
Let the buttons rest and unwind.  

Ending
The house is quiet, lights are low, the guard has stopped the tune,  
The lift says, "Good night, everyone," and dings again too soon.
