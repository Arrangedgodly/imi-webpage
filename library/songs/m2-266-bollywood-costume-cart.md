# The Bollywood Costume Cart

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Bollywood playback numbers with rapid costume changes, ensemble choreography, and dramatic location jumps*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Start in a quiet backstage rhythm, then launch into bright strings and ensemble vocals; use one wheeled costume rack for every transition.*

[Verse 1]
The star of the school show stood behind a painted wall,  
A macaque fixed her collar as the curtain gave a call.  
The song began in moonlight, then the set became a train,  
The chorus changed their jackets while they danced across the lane.  
A gibbon rolled the costume rack behind a painted tree,  
A capuchin pulled one bright scarf and turned it into three.  
The hero crossed the footlights with a glittering salute,  
The rack rolled right behind him like a loyal dancing suit.  

[Chorus]
Change the scene, change the sleeve,  
Turn around and make them believe.  
One bright scarf and a quick little spin,  
The chorus is ready; let the next dance begin.  
Change the scene, let the colors go,  
The costume cart is in the show.  

[Verse 2]
The first quick change was perfect; the second lost a shoe,  
The rack went past the backdrop and the chorus followed through.  
The train became a palace, the palace became a pier,  
The drummer kept the rhythm as the props all disappeared.  
The star reached for her crown; it was still on the rack,  
A dancer wheeled it forward while the band brought the beat back.  
The chorus made a circle and the rack rolled in between,  
The final dance was mostly costumes, with one singer in the scene.  

[Chorus]
Change the scene, change the sleeve,  
Turn around and make them believe.  
One bright scarf and a quick little spin,  
The chorus is ready; let the next dance begin.  
Change the scene, let the colors go,  
The costume cart is in the show.  

[Bridge]
The curtain fell too early; the rack was left outside,  
The cast danced through the doorway and the audience replied.  
The stagehands rolled the cart back in on one last turn,  
The star took off the crown and let the costume rack return.  

[Final Chorus]
Change the scene, change the sleeve,  
Keep the cart where the dancers can leave.  
One bright scarf and a quick little spin,  
The show is complete; let the credits begin.  
Change the scene, let the colors go,  
The costume cart gets its own bow.  

[Ending]
The cast took one last bow; the rack rolled in for another.
