# The Dream-Pop Singer Blinks in Every Photo

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dreamy album-cover photography and performers whose carefully ethereal gaze keeps disappearing at the shutter click*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Washed-out guitars, soft synthesizers and airy vocals; the camera shutter clicks between lines like a tiny percussion part.*

[Verse 1]
The photographer made fog beneath a gentle violet light,  
I wore a silver jacket and I looked beyond the night.  
She asked me for a far-off gaze as if I heard the sea,  
I closed my eyes just when she clicked; the photograph missed me.  
She tried a little countdown and I nodded at the sound,  
At three I blinked quite beautifully; my gaze could not be found.  
The fog remained convincing and the jacket shone quite bright,  
My eyelids had a perfect pose that lasted through the light.  

[Chorus]
Soft eyes, pale sky, drift beyond the frame,  
The camera has my eyelids and a lovely printed name.  
Slow light, deep dream, let the picture stay,  
I have vanished from the looking but my jacket leads the way.  
Soft eyes, pale sky, let the shutter speak,  
The dream has got a sleeper where the dreamer ought to be.  

[Verse 2]
She told me not to count this time and made a quiet burst,  
The first shot caught my open eyes; the following four were worse.  
She picked the first and showed me how my gaze had finally held,  
The fog had drifted from the frame; the dream had partly failed.  
I liked the plain expression and the jacket in the light,  
She set the cover title in a narrow band of white.  
We kept the closed-eye pictures as the back cover's quiet row,  
The label said it looked like I was sleeping through the show.  

[Chorus]
Soft eyes, pale sky, drift beyond the frame,  
The camera has my eyelids and a lovely printed name.  
Slow light, deep dream, let the picture stay,  
I have vanished from the looking but my jacket leads the way.  
Soft eyes, pale sky, let the shutter speak,  
The dream has got a sleeper where the dreamer ought to be.  

[Bridge]
The fans posed with their eyes shut when the record came to town,  
They said the back cover conveyed a peace they hadn't found.  
The photographer took group shots with no countdown to give,  
The open eyes looked ordinary; the closed ones made the sleeve.  

[Final Chorus]
Soft eyes, pale sky, drift beyond the frame,  
The fan club has my eyelids and a jacket much the same.  
Slow light, deep dream, let the picture stay,  
The camera captures everyone relaxing in their way.  
Soft eyes, pale sky, let the shutter speak,  
I blinked upon the autograph; the pen drew on my knee.
