# The Battle Rapper Joins a Pottery Class

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: battle rap bravado, elaborate opponent insults and victory claims transplanted into a beginner pottery class*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Dry drum loop and forceful rhythmic verses; the pottery teacher answers without leaving the beat.*

[Verse 1]
I came into the studio with clay upon my sleeve,  
My rival took the next wheel; he had something to achieve.  
I told him that his vase was weak, his handle looked like glue,  
He said I hadn't thrown a pot; I said my moment's due.  
I slapped a lump upon the wheel and pressed the pedal down,  
The clay began to wobble like a king without a crown.  
The teacher said to center it; I said I own the floor,  
The spinning lump threw half itself against the cupboard door.  

[Chorus]
Spin that clay, claim that throne,  
Everybody's watching while I try to make a bowl.  
Hands up, talk tough, pull it high,  
My vase is leaning sideways but my confidence can fly.  
Spin that clay, own that room,  
The rival has a teapot and I have a little mound.  
Big bars, wet palms, heavy crown,  
The pottery is winning every time I put it down.  

[Verse 2]
I told him his new teapot had a very timid spout,  
He lifted up the lid and let my little insults out.  
The teacher gave me fresh clay and a quiet demonstration,  
She said to slow the pedal for a stronger foundation.  
I tried a careful cylinder and pulled its walls too thin,  
The rim curled like a microphone; my rival grinned again.  
I called it modern sculpture and demanded he concede,  
The teacher asked what use it had; I said, "It holds my lead."  

[Chorus]
Spin that clay, claim that throne,  
Everybody's watching while I try to make a bowl.  
Hands up, talk tough, pull it high,  
My vase is leaning sideways but my confidence can fly.  
Spin that clay, own that room,  
The rival has a teapot and I have a little mound.  
Big bars, wet palms, heavy crown,  
The pottery is winning every time I put it down.  

[Bridge]
She fired my twisted pencil pot beside his tidy set,  
The rival bought my sculpture for his drawing desk instead.  
He said it held his pencils with a very useful lean,  
I signed the base in marker: "Battle Champion, Number Three."  

[Final Chorus]
Spin that clay, claim that throne,  
Everybody's ordered me a pencil pot of their own.  
Hands up, talk tough, pull it high,  
My rival pours the tea while I negotiate supply.  
Spin that clay, own that room,  
My accidental sculpture has a waiting list till June.  
Big bars, wet palms, heavy crown,  
The teacher takes the credit for slowing my wheel down.
