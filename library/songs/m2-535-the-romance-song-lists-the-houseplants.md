# The Romance Song Lists the Houseplants

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic pop promises of devotion and partners using them as an increasingly specific plant-watering agreement*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Warm pop guitar, two alternating voices and a joint refrain; plant names interrupt the soaring declarations.*

[Voice A]
I promise I will cherish everything you leave with me.  
[Voice B]
The fern likes damp soil lightly, and the cactus less, you see.  
[Voice A]
I'll keep the light of all your love alive while you're away.  
[Voice B]
The orchid needs the window but no direct sun all day.  

[Verse]
You packed to see your sister for a weekend by the shore,  
I stood beside the houseplants with a promise and much more.  
You wrote a little list for every pot upon the shelf,  
I sang a tune about devotion, very proud of myself.  
The fern had got a label and the cactus had one too,  
The orchid had a separate note about the light it knew.  
I put the list beside the sink instead of in the song,  
The plants preferred a little care to promises all week long.  

[Refrain]
Grow, little green, let the warm light stay,  
The promise has a watering can to carry it today.  
Grow, little green, hold the lovely room,  
The cactus needs a dry pause and the orchid keeps its bloom.  
Two hearts, five pots, let the bright leaves shine,  
The list has got five entries and a different time for mine.  

[Verse]
I followed all the little notes beside the kitchen light,  
The fern stayed green, the cactus dry, the orchid stood upright.  
You came back Sunday evening with a photograph of sea,  
You checked the pots before you placed your suitcase beside me.  
I showed the little list with every proper action ticked,  
You said the weekend's promises had rather neatly clicked.  
The fern became the subject of the melody I'd made,  
The cactus got a short rest where the watering was delayed.  

[Refrain]
Grow, little green, let the warm light stay,  
The promise has a watering can to carry it today.  
Grow, little green, hold the lovely room,  
The cactus needs a dry pause and the orchid keeps its bloom.  
Two hearts, five pots, let the bright leaves shine,  
The list has got five entries and a different time for mine.  

[Voice B]
I liked the song you wrote for all the green things in the room.  
[Voice A]
I kept the little list as well, to help the orchid bloom.  
[Voice B]
Next month I visit longer; will you keep the promise true?  
[Voice A]
I ask the fern to harmonize and keep the list you drew.
