# Rumba for the Rolling Suitcase

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rumba guitar and handclap conventions, romanticized travel, and a suitcase whose broken wheel keeps dancing the wrong direction*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Rumba guitar, handclaps, and heel taps; let the suitcase wheel answer the rhythm, then resolve its direction.*

Verse 1
A macaque packed a suitcase for a journey by the sea,
The rumba band began to play and swayed beneath the tree.
The case had one loose wheel that turned whenever it was pushed,
It rolled away from the doorway as the traveler softly hushed.

Chorus
Roll, suitcase, roll with me,
Find the road that leads to sea.
Turn your wheel and choose a lane,
Don't return me home again.

Verse 2
The traveler caught the handle and heeled along the floor,
The guitar strummed a steady line; the case went toward the door.
A gibbon joined the handclaps and the wheel began to spin,
The suitcase circled through the band and rolled back in again.

Chorus
Roll, suitcase, roll with me,
Find the road that leads to sea.
Turn your wheel and choose a lane,
Don't return me home again.

Bridge
They laid the case on its side and tightened up the screw,
The wheel now pointed forward and the band began anew.
The traveler checked the map, then set the handle straight,
The suitcase rolled beside him and arrived before too late.

Verse 3
They reached the station calmly, the guitar still in the air,
The suitcase held its course and did not loop around the square.
The traveler boarded safely; the band played by the wall,
The loose wheel was repaired before the next trip call.

Chorus
Roll, suitcase, roll with me,
Find the road that leads to sea.
Turn your wheel and choose a lane,
Don't return me home again.

Ending
The rumba reached the harbor; the suitcase came to rest,
The traveler set it down and unpacked his dancing vest.
