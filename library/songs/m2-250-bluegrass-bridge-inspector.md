# The Bluegrass Bridge Inspector

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bluegrass breakdowns, lightning-fast picking, and storytelling about a local bridge*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Drive a brisk banjo and fiddle rhythm; slow the band whenever the inspector raises a hand.*

[Verse 1]
Down by the creek, where the cedar planks crossed,  
A banjo player counted each nail that was lost.  
The fiddle played a run like a wheel going fast,  
The inspector came walking with a level and a cast.  
A raccoon monkey watched him from a sycamore tree,  
The inspector checked the rail and said, "Let me see."  
The band tried a breakdown while he measured the span,  
He tapped each board twice with the end of a pan.  

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, but don't cross yet,  
The bridge has a note we have to get.  
Fiddle on the left, banjo on the right,  
The planks need checking before tonight.  
Pick it quick, but hold that chord,  
The river is moving; the bridge is the board.  

[Verse 2]
The guitarist played a river tune and the crew clapped time,  
The inspector raised a palm and stopped the whole rhyme.  
One plank gave a creak, the band gave a cheer,  
He said, "That is a warning, not a note for your ear."  
They tightened one bolt and replaced one rail,  
The banjo held a tremolo, the fiddle told the tale.  
The monkeys moved the picnic basket back from the edge,  
The band played a chorus from the safe side of the ledge.  

[Chorus]
Pick it quick, but don't cross yet,  
The bridge has a note we have to get.  
Fiddle on the left, banjo on the right,  
The planks need checking before tonight.  
Pick it quick, but hold that chord,  
The river is moving; the bridge is the board.  

[Bridge]
The inspector walked across and tested every seam,  
The bluegrass band kept quiet, then burst into a scream.  
He waved them over slowly; the bridge held firm and straight,  
The banjo player crossed at a bluegrass walking rate.  

[Final Chorus]
Pick it quick, now cross with care,  
The bridge is repaired and the crew is there.  
Fiddle on the left, banjo on the right,  
The river's a ribbon in the morning light.  
Pick it quick, but let notes soar,  
The bridge holds the band and the creek below.  

[Ending]
The inspector took the banjo; it had one loose string.
