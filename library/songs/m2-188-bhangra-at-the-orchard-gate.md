# Baskets in the Bhangra Beat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bhangra dance-pop build, celebratory dhol breaks, and a harvest party that turns into an overactive sorting line*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Drive the dhol harder each chorus; stop cleanly when the baskets begin to roll.*

[Verse 1]
At the orchard gate, Nita tied her scarf and checked the crates,
The dhol player struck a beat that shook the sleepy dates.
A macaque picked a basket up and bounced it to the song,
The fruit began to shuffle, and the line rolled right along.

[Chorus]
Bhangra at the gate, let the baskets celebrate,
Lift them to the left, then turn before they tip.
Bhangra in the sun, every apple joins the fun,
Clap above your head, keep the mangoes in the crate.


[Verse 2]
The dancers made a circle; the sorting table rocked.
A peach rolled through the middle and the dhol player's foot got blocked.
He played a drum fill backwards, so the crates turned in reverse;
A melon rolled uphill and joined the chorus for a verse.

[Chorus]
Bhangra at the gate, let the baskets celebrate,
Lift them to the left, then turn before they tip.
Bhangra in the sun, every apple joins the fun,
Clap above your head, keep the mangoes in the crate.


[Bridge]
Nita called a harvest pause; the dancers froze in place.
The fruit kept moving anyway, with rhythm and with grace.
She marked the rolling crates with chalk and gave the band a cue;
The drummers played a gentler beat; the peaches formed a queue.

[Verse 3]
They sorted every apple by its dance and by its size,
The bruised ones got a jam pot and the firm ones earned a prize.
The melon reached the gate at last, still rolling on the beat;
It bumped the sign that read "Come In" and made the harvest complete.

[Final Chorus]
Bhangra at the gate, let the baskets celebrate,
Lift them to the left, then turn before they tip.
Bhangra in the sun, every apple joins the fun,
Clap above your head, keep the mangoes in the crate.


[Ending]
Nita sold the melon a ticket for tomorrow's dance. It rolled inside.
