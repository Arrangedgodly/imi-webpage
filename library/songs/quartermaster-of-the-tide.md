# Quartermaster of the Tide

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: sea shanty work songs and exaggerated pirate hardship*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
The tide came in; the laundry went out.  
We chased three shirts and one small trout.  
The captain stood upon the pier.  
He wore a hat that blocked one ear.  
"Heave on the hamper, haul on the line!"  
The socks came back in a crooked sign.  
The quartermaster counted every pair.  
He found a crab asleep in there.  

## Chorus
Yo, roll the basket, turn the foam.  
The cleanest shirt shall find its home.  
We'll scrub by moon and wring by star.  
The tide can't take our trousers far.  

## Verse 2
The cabin boy had lost his left shoe.  
He swore a gull had borrowed it too.  
We raised a sail from a bedsheet wide.  
The captain steered with soap as guide.  
A wave tossed powder across the deck.  
The cook made bubbles to save his neck.  
The first mate yelled, "The colors run!"  
We chased blue socks beneath the sun.  

## Chorus
Yo, roll the basket, turn the foam.  
The cleanest shirt shall find its home.  
We'll scrub by moon and wring by star.  
The tide can't take our trousers far.  

## Bridge
A squall of towels swept over the rail.  
The captain grabbed a red striped sail.  
We tied the hamper to the mast.  
The crab rode proudly, holding fast.  
"One hand for ship, one hand for soap!"  
We scrubbed the deck and clung to hope.  
The moon came out, the wind grew mild.  
The crab looked pleased, the captain smiled.  

## Final chorus
Yo, roll the basket, bring it home.  
The last wet shirt is ours to own.  
We'll fold by moon and stack by star.  
The tide can keep that borrowed jar.  
Yo, sound the horn for every sock.  
The crab has claimed the captain's smock.  
We'll scrub again when storms return.  
For now the deck is ours to turn.  

## Ending
At dawn we docked beside the shed.  
The shirts were hung; the crab was fed.  
The captain steered his boots ashore.  
The laundry sailed no more.  

*Performance note: Put the chorus in a stomping group rhythm, with the crew tugging one long rope together.*
