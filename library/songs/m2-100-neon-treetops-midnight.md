# Neon Treetops at Midnight

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: synthwave arpeggios, artificial night-drive nostalgia, and a macaque pilot circling the same illuminated canopy sign*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Pulsing analog synth, gated snare, and a sustained final chord; evoke a midnight drive without adding a road.*

Verse 1
A macaque sat at midnight by a glowing canopy sign,
The synth began to shimmer in a carefully looping line.
He drove a little scooter round the plaza once again,
The neon letters flickered and spelled out "Open When." 

Chorus
Neon treetops, hold the night,
Loop the road in violet light.
Past the sign and back once more,
Midnight waits beside the door.

Verse 2
The bass came in like thunder from a distant summer storm,
The scooter circled slowly, keeping every turn in form.
A capuchin on the corner asked, "Are you lost or late?"
The macaque said, "I'm headed home; the sign keeps changing state."

Chorus
Neon treetops, hold the night,
Loop the road in violet light.
Past the sign and back once more,
Midnight waits beside the door.

Bridge
The sign said "Open Soon," then "Open Now," then "Maybe,"
The drummer kept a steady beat; the plaza looked like navy.
The scooter's battery blinked, and the looping synth went still,
The macaque walked the last short block beneath the quiet hill.

Verse 3
He reached his door and looked behind; the sign was dark at last,
The neon glow had finished its nostalgic little cast.
He parked the scooter, took his keys, and watched the moon rise,
The synth returned as an alarm with very bright surprise.

Chorus
Neon treetops, hold the night,
Loop the road in violet light.
Past the sign and back once more,
Midnight waits beside the door.

Ending
The scooter slept until the dawn; the sign stayed off till noon,
The macaque got home on foot beneath the synthwave moon.
