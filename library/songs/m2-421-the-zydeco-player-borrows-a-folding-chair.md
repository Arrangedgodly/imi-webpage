# The Zydeco Player Borrows a Folding Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: exuberant zydeco dance tunes and performers who discover that their supposedly sturdy stage furniture has its own rhythm*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Accordion, rubboard and an eager dance beat; the folding chair squeaks on every seated bounce.*

[Verse 1]
I carried my accordion to the little dance-house door,  
The rubboard player waited with his boots upon the floor.  
My standing stool was missing, so the host brought out a chair,  
Its metal legs unfolded with a squeak that split the air.  
I sat and drew the bellows wide to give the dancers sound,  
The chair replied beneath me every time I bounced around.  
The rubboard caught its rhythm and the drummer nodded twice,  
The host said, "It's a folding chair. It comes at no extra price."  

[Chorus]
Pull it wide, push it back,  
The chair has got a little solo hidden underneath the track.  
Rub it bright, step it strong,  
The furniture is answering the bottom of my song.  
Pull it wide, hear it squeal,  
The dancers think the squeaking is a special kind of reel.  

[Verse 2]
I leaned into the second verse and lifted up one heel,  
The chair slid just a little; I restored it with my knee.  
The host brought out a steady stool between the final chords,  
I changed my seat and played again; the chair was set by the door.  
The dancers missed the little squeak that cut across the bass,  
They asked the rubboard player if he'd lost a vital phrase.  
He pressed upon the empty chair and found its crying joint,  
The drummer gave it four beats and the crowd approved the point.  

[Chorus]
Pull it wide, push it back,  
The chair has got a little solo hidden underneath the track.  
Rub it bright, step it strong,  
The furniture is answering the bottom of my song.  
Pull it wide, hear it squeal,  
The dancers think the squeaking is a special kind of reel.  

[Bridge]
The host oiled every hinge that night before he closed the hall,  
The chair was beautifully silent when we tried it in the fall.  
The rubboard man supplied the missing squeak upon his board,  
The chair stayed by the ticket desk and never played an encore.  

[Final Chorus]
Pull it wide, push it back,  
The rubboard has the little solo folded underneath the track.  
Rub it bright, step it strong,  
The furniture is resting after one tremendous song.  
Pull it wide, hear it squeal,  
The chair has got a pension: tickets tucked beneath its seat.
