# Love Me Like a Lawnmower

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: flirtatious novelty pop and exaggerated romantic metaphor*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
You rolled into the yard with a tank half full.  
Your handlebar ribbon looked wonderfully cool.  
I wore my best gloves, you wore a grin.  
The neighbors leaned out to take us in.  
You circled the daisies with careful speed.  
I fell for your clean, even weed.  
Your engine hummed a tune in D.  
I wondered if you'd mow with me.  
I'm a capuchin gardener, shy but keen.  
My tail held the hose, neat and green.  

## Chorus
Love me like a lawnmower, steady and slow.  
Trim every worry wherever we go.  
Turn at the mailbox, spare the rose.  
Bring me home with grass on your clothes.  

## Verse 2
We met by the shed when the moon came up.  
You brought two lemonades in a dented cup.  
I offered a wrench; you offered a seat.  
The blade was off, but the mood was sweet.  
We named every gopher hole on the lawn.  
The biggest one was "Steve" by dawn.  
You said romance meant knowing the yard.  
I said my heart was already scarred.  
The troop swung over the garden gate.  
They cheered our date, then stole a crate.  

## Chorus
Love me like a lawnmower, steady and slow.  
Trim every worry wherever we go.  
Turn at the mailbox, spare the rose.  
Bring me home with grass on your clothes.  

## Bridge
The next day clouds came rolling in.  
The grass grew tall, we lost our grin.  
Your old mower coughed and quit.  
We pushed it home in careful bits.  
You said we could leave some clover.  
The bees came by and took us over.  
We sat and watched the wildflowers rise.  
You looked more lovely than the prize.  

## Final chorus
Love me like a lawnmower, patient and kind.  
Leave a little green for the bees to find.  
Turn past the mailbox, spare the rose.  
Bring me home with grass on your clothes.  
Love me when the engine won't start.  
Bring your whole and unpolished heart.  
The weeds can wait; the sky is wide.  
We'll push the mower side by side.  

## Ending
The mower rested beneath the shed.  
We watched the evening turn rose red.  
The clover leaned toward the lane.  
We kissed, then pushed through grass again.  

*Performance note: Play the metaphor straight and sweet, then give the broken mower a tiny, dignified solo.*
