# The Barbershop Quartet Counts the Steps

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: barbershop quartet close harmony, comic wordplay, and overconfident cadence tags*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use bright four-part harmony and a ringing final chord; let each singer confidently count the staircase differently.*

[Verse 1]
Four monkeys climbed a staircase to the rooftop garden gate,  
The tenor said, "There are twelve steps," the bass said, "There are eight."  
The lead held up a clipboard, the baritone a pie,  
They sang a close-position chord and let the numbers fly.  
A gibbon took the first step, then the tenor took the third,  
The bass began a walking line and didn't say a word.  
They harmonized on "seven" while a capuchin went by,  
Who counted all the risers with a quick and level eye.  

[Chorus]
Step by step, we climb in tune,  
The rooftop's waiting underneath the moon.  
Count it loud, then count again,  
Four-part harmony can add a step or ten.  
Step by step, let the chord ring true,  
The last stair's waiting for the whole crew.  

[Verse 2]
The lead said, "We are halfway," the bass said, "Not quite yet,"  
The tenor found a landing with a small welcome mat.  
The baritone sang "one more," the chorus answered "two,"  
The clipboard's page blew down the stairs and took the count with it too.  
They chased it in a canon, each singer round the bend,  
The pie stayed on the landing as the cadence met its end.  
They reached the roof at sundown with the gate still in their sight,  
The capuchin said, "There were twenty," and turned on the garden light.  

[Chorus]
Step by step, we climb in tune,  
The rooftop's waiting underneath the moon.  
Count it loud, then count again,  
Four-part harmony can add a step or ten.  
Step by step, let the chord ring true,  
The last stair's waiting for the whole crew.  

[Bridge]
They tried a ringing tag on "twenty," held the vowel clear,  
The gate latch clicked in harmony and swung open near.  
The pie slid off the landing, rolled beneath the rail,  
The bass caught it mid-cadence and saved the custard trail.  

[Final Chorus]
Step by step, the count is done,  
The roof is here, the garden's won.  
Count it loud, but check the stair,  
The number lives beneath your feet, not in the air.  
Step by step, let the chord ring true,  
The pie is safe and the whole crew's through.  

[Ending]
The quartet sang one final tag; the stairs declined to harmonize.
