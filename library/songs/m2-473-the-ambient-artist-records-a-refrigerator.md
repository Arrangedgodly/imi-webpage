# The Ambient Artist Records a Refrigerator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: luxurious ambient listening releases and musicians who discover their expensive sonic texture has a household source*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Low synth pad, refrigerator hum and small door chimes; changes in the appliance interrupt the supposedly endless drone.*

[Verse 1]
I wanted one long sound to fill a very quiet space,  
A tone without a story, with a slow and endless face.  
I placed my little recorder on the kitchen counter top,  
The refrigerator hummed beneath a clock that wouldn't stop.  
I captured twenty minutes of the smooth and settled drone,  
I called it "Arctic Landscape" when I played it on my phone.  
My flatmate came to listen with a spoon and empty bowl,  
She said she'd heard that landscape while she'd searched for casserole.  

[Refrain]
Still, cold, hold the air,  
The Arctic has a salad on its middle shelf somewhere.  
Still, cold, let time release,  
The motor stops and leaves us with an unexpected peace.  
Listen to the hollow space where little echoes roam,  
The polar region has a plug beside the fridge at home.  

[Verse 2]
She opened up the freezer and the cooling motor changed,  
My perfectly continuous world was suddenly rearranged.  
I asked if she could wait until the recording reached its end,  
She asked if twenty minutes made a hungry person's friend.  
I recorded while she took her meal and shut the door again,  
The hum returned a little later like a faraway refrain.  
I kept the door alarm that sounded when she took too long,  
Its tiny repeated warning was the only tune in the song.  

[Refrain]
Still, cold, hold the air,  
The Arctic has a salad on its middle shelf somewhere.  
Still, cold, let time release,  
The motor stops and leaves us with an unexpected peace.  
Listen to the hollow space where little echoes roam,  
The polar region has a plug beside the fridge at home.  

[Bridge]
I credited the freezer and the flatmate on the sleeve,  
She named her spoon a guest performer before agreeing to leave.  
The gallery played the recording in a white and empty room,  
A visitor asked where the milk was in the gentle afternoon.  

[Final Refrain]
Still, cold, hold the air,  
The gallery has a speaker and no middle shelf to spare.  
Still, cold, let time release,  
The door alarm reminds the crowd to interrupt their peace.  
Listen to the hollow space where little echoes roam,  
My flatmate wants her royalties in groceries brought home.
