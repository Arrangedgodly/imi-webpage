# The Metal Frontman Needs Gardening Gloves

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: heavy-metal boasts about invulnerability, enormous gauntlets and conquest, tested by ordinary rose pruning*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Low distorted guitars, thunderous drums and theatrical growls; switch to a tender clean vocal for the glove-size discussion.*

[Verse 1]
I wore my mighty stage gauntlets to prune the garden rose,  
Their shining metal fingers had a very warrior pose.  
I lifted up the secateurs but couldn't grip them tight,  
The gauntlets clanged together and the blades refused to bite.  
My neighbor had a canvas pair she used to trim her hedge,  
She said to leave the armor by the little garden shed.  
I cried that I was lord of thorns and master of the night,  
She said, "Then try the medium. The large will not fit right."  

[Chorus]
Thorns of doom, heed my roar,  
I am taking off my gauntlets by the garden shed door.  
Steel and fire, bow to me,  
The neighbor has a canvas pair in floral pink and green.  
Thorns of doom, feel the blade,  
My hands are dressed in daisies while the mighty riff is played.  

[Verse 2]
I put the little gloves on and began to shape the bush,  
The secateurs cut neatly with a very modest push.  
The band arrived for practice and they saw my flowered hands,  
The bassist said the roses had a much improved stance.  
The drummer asked to borrow gloves to clear his patch of weeds,  
The guitarist wanted smaller ones for planting little seeds.  
We worked the garden quietly before rehearsing loud,  
The neighbor brought a watering can and bowed before the crowd.  

[Chorus]
Thorns of doom, heed my roar,  
I am taking off my gauntlets by the garden shed door.  
Steel and fire, bow to me,  
The neighbor has a canvas pair in floral pink and green.  
Thorns of doom, feel the blade,  
My hands are dressed in daisies while the mighty riff is played.  

[Bridge]
We played the new song Friday with the gloves beside the drum,  
The fans requested flower prints for every hand to come.  
The neighbor took the merchandise and sold her spare supplies,  
My mighty metal gauntlets made a planter by her drive.  

[Final Chorus]
Thorns of doom, heed my roar,  
Our tour has planted roses by a dozen backstage doors.  
Steel and fire, wait and see,  
The neighbor sells her gloves to fans who lift their hands with glee.  
Thorns of doom, feel the blade,  
The gauntlets grow geraniums; the floral gloves get paid.
