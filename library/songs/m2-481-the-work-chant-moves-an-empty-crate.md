# The Work Chant Moves an Empty Crate

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: traditional coordinated work chants applied to a task that has ceased to need the entire crew*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Call and response with stomped beats; six workers surround a small crate. Solo caller and group answers alternate.*

[Caller]
Set your feet and take the weight!  
[Workers]
Six strong hands around the crate!  
[Caller]
Bend your knees and count to three!  
[Workers]
One, two, three, lift up with me!  

[Verse]
We gathered at the loading door to move the market's stock,  
The caller wore a woolen cap and watched the harbor clock.  
The first five crates were heavy with potatoes from the field,  
We carried them together with the strength six workers yield.  
The sixth crate sat upon the dock, its wooden lid unsealed,  
We gave it all our effort with no knowledge of its yield.  
It flew above our shoulders when we tried to take its weight,  
The only thing we'd lifted was a small and empty crate.  

[Caller]
Set your feet and take the weight!  
[Workers]
Six strong hands around the crate!  
[Caller]
Bend your knees and count to three!  
[Workers]
One, two, three, lift up with me!  

[Verse]
The caller set it gently down and looked inside the wood,  
One little packing label lay where all the stock once stood.  
The merchant said the potatoes had already reached the shop,  
He needed that one empty crate returned beside the dock.  
One worker picked it up and took it down the level path,  
The other five sang loudly while the merchant had a laugh.  
The worker set it by the door and checked the label's name,  
He came back for his wages just as everybody came.  

[Caller]
Set your feet and take the weight!  
[Workers]
One spare hand around the crate!  
[Caller]
Keep the time and count to three!  
[Workers]
Five paid singers, one employee!  

[Final Verse]
The merchant counted six names on the order for the day,  
The caller said the chorus had improved the working way.  
He paid all six as promised, though he made a little note,  
"Ask what is inside it before hiring the whole boat."  
The worker who had carried it requested equal song,  
We gave him all six voices for the walk to lunch along.  
The dock was clear, the crates were stacked, the market had its stock,  
We sang another work chant to a sandwich and a clock.
