# The Sound System's Library Dancehall

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dancehall sound-system culture, selector callouts, and booming bass*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Keep the bass warm but restrained; make every selector callout collide with a library whisper sign.*

[Verse 1]
The library held dance night among the atlas shelves,  
A baboon set the speakers up and warned them, "Mind yourselves."  
The selector shouted, "Big tune!" and turned the bass to ten,  
The reference books began to walk right off the table then.  
A gibbon raised a finger and pointed to the sign,  
It read "QUIET PLEASE" in letters that were large and bright.  
The DJ lowered one notch; the shelves shook just the same,  
A capuchin whispered, "Can the bass use a quieter name?"  

[Chorus]
Drop that bass, but keep it low,  
Let the paperbacks enjoy the flow.  
Selector, call the rhythm soft,  
The books are trying not to lift aloft.  
Drop that bass, let shelves stay still,  
A library dance can keep a gentle thrill.  

[Verse 2]
The DJ gave a callout: "Readers, wave your hands!"  
The dancers waved in silence through the biographical stands.  
The bass drum made a thump; the history section swayed,  
One dictionary opened up and found the word "parade."  
The librarian brought earplugs and a velvet rope,  
The selector said, "Respect," and gave the crowd some hope.  
He set the volume lower, the dancers found the beat,  
The shelves began to settle and the readers tapped their feet.  

[Chorus]
Drop that bass, but keep it low,  
Let the paperbacks enjoy the flow.  
Selector, call the rhythm soft,  
The books are trying not to lift aloft.  
Drop that bass, let shelves stay still,  
A library dance can keep a gentle thrill.  

[Bridge]
The DJ asked the crowd to make a quiet little sound,  
A thousand pages fluttered as they shuffled all around.  
The selector whispered "Forward," the bassline barely spoke,  
The whole room bounced in silence while the floorboards gently broke.  

[Final Chorus]
Drop that bass, now fade it slow,  
The readers have a beat they know.  
Selector, call the night complete,  
Return the books and clear the beat.  
Drop that bass, let shelves stay still,  
The library dance has paid its bill.  

[Ending]
The DJ checked out the speaker; its due date was next week.
