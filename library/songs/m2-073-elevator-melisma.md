# Melisma in the Elevator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: R&B vocal runs, ad-libbed high notes, and the habit of extending a simple phrase indefinitely*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Smooth keyboard chords and warm bass; stretch "going up" into an elaborate run while the elevator waits politely.*

Verse 1
A macaque singer steps inside, the lobby's nearly bare,  
He presses six and tests a note that curls around the air.  
The doors begin to close; he sings, "I'm going up," then holds,  
The elevator waits for him as every vowel rolls.  
He bends the note, he flips the note, he adds a tiny trill,  
The floor display says level two; the singer's on it still.  

Chorus
Ooh, I'm going up, up, up,  
Hold that note and don't interrupt.  
Ooh, I'm going up, up, up,  
The doors are open; please step out.  
Run it high, then run it low,  
The elevator has to go.  
Ooh, I'm going up, up, up,  
We missed the floor; let's press it once.  

Verse 2
A gibbon with a laundry cart says, "Could you let me pass?"  
The singer adds her request into a soulful, breathy phrase.  
The doors stay open, music blooms, the lobby starts to sway,  
The cart rolls in on beat four and blocks the buttons' way.  
The singer sings the floor numbers, from one to twenty-three,  
The clerk says, "You pressed six," and hands him the key.  

Bridge
The run resolves at last; the chime sounds perfectly on time,  
The singer steps to level six and finishes the line.  
The gibbon rolls her laundry out, the doors begin to shut,  
He reaches for one final note; the doors say, "That's enough."  

Chorus
Ooh, I'm going up, up, up,  
Hold that note and don't interrupt.  
Ooh, I'm going up, up, up,  
The doors are open; please step out.  
Run it high, then run it low,  
The elevator has to go.  
Ooh, I'm going up, up, up,  
We missed the floor; let's press it once.  

Ending
The singer takes the stairs back down to practice one more run,  
The elevator hums the hook and beats him there for fun.
