# The Chase Across the Wash Line

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cartoon chase-song conventions, rapid tempo changes, and a capuchin whose laundry keeps escaping the clothesline*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Piano starts nimble, accelerates through the chase, then stops dead when the last sock lands.*

Verse 1
A capuchin hung his laundry on a line above the yard,
A gust unpinned his striped socks and sent them running hard.
He chased the first one past the shed, the second past the gate,
The wash line snapped like a cymbal; the shirt took off in flight.

Chorus
Run, run, catch the sleeve,
Grab the sock before it leaves.
Duck the sheet and mind the pin,
Laundry's on the move again.

Verse 2
He leapt across a watering can and slid beneath a chair,
A pair of shorts went sailing by and landed in his hair.
The piano played a faster run, the drummer rolled a fill,
The capuchin ran circles round the laundry on the hill.

Chorus
Run, run, catch the sleeve,
Grab the sock before it leaves.
Duck the sheet and mind the pin,
Laundry's on the move again.

Bridge
He caught the shirt, the shirt caught him, the sheet wrapped round them both,
They rolled into a basket with a comic little oath.
The wind grew tired and dropped the socks beside a tree,
The capuchin stood up at last, dressed in laundry's spree.

Verse 3
He brought the line down lower and tied each pin in place,
The socks were paired by color and the shirt was dried with grace.
The breeze returned and lifted one small handkerchief in flight,
He let it wave above the yard and called the chase goodnight.

Chorus
Run, run, catch the sleeve,
Grab the sock before it leaves.
Duck the sheet and mind the pin,
Laundry's on the move again.

Ending
The piano stopped; the last sock landed in the basket,
The capuchin went indoors before the wind could ask it.
