# The Flamenco Dancer Tries New Shoes

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: intense flamenco performance in which every deliberate heel strike meets an embarrassing accidental accompaniment*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Guitar, handclaps and proud sung phrases; a small rubber squeak follows the right shoe at precisely the wrong dramatic moments.*

[Verse 1]
I entered in a dark red dress with flowers in my hair,  
The guitarist struck his opening chord and left a little air.  
I raised one hand above my head and struck the wooden floor,  
A squeak escaped my brand-new shoe; the audience asked for more.  
The left heel gave a sharp report, the right replied quite high,  
The guitarist looked toward the wings; I looked him in the eye.  
I stamped again with double force to bury that small cry,  
It sang between the handclaps like a bird that wouldn't fly.  

[Chorus]
Strike, heel, strike, answer to the string,  
I have come to dance with fire; my right shoe came to sing.  
Hands high, eyes fierce, never break the pose,  
There's a little rubber solo rising underneath my toes.  
Strike, heel, strike, let the proud foot fall,  
The left shoe knows the rhythm and the right interrupts it all.  
Turn, dress, turn, sweep the evening wide,  
My passionate declaration has a whistle at its side.  

[Verse 2]
We reached the quiet passage and I held a solemn stare,  
The guitarist slowed his fingers; every breath hung in the air.  
I took one little sliding step to cross the center light,  
The sole announced my movement with a cheerful squeak of fright.  
The clappers caught its rhythm and began to leave a space,  
The guitarist gave the squeak a note and kept a solemn face.  
I tried a longer heel run and the shoe replied in kind,  
The audience leaned forward for the sound they'd come to find.  

[Chorus]
Strike, heel, strike, answer to the string,  
I have come to dance with fire; my right shoe came to sing.  
Hands high, eyes fierce, never break the pose,  
There's a little rubber solo rising underneath my toes.  
Strike, heel, strike, let the proud foot fall,  
The left shoe knows the rhythm and the right interrupts it all.  
Turn, dress, turn, sweep the evening wide,  
My passionate declaration has a whistle at its side.  

[Bridge]
I changed my shoes at interval for older, quieter leather,  
The second half ran beautifully; our beats held tight together.  
The audience applauded, then a voice came from the back,  
"We liked the first-half singer. Could you please bring that one back?"  

[Final Chorus]
Strike, heel, strike, answer to the string,  
The new right shoe is waiting in the wings for us to bring.  
Hands high, eyes fierce, never break the pose,  
The guitarist wrote an encore for a solo rubber sole.  
Turn, dress, turn, sweep the evening wide,  
I dance in quiet leather with the noisy shoe beside.  
Strike, heel, strike, now the crowd can see,  
The shoe gets its own bow when I squeeze it on my knee.
