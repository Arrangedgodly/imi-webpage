# The Drum Major's Glove

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: military-style drum major pageantry, crisp commands, and ceremonial glove choreography*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: March with a clipped snare pattern; make each glove movement more elaborate than the musical cue requires.*

[Verse 1]
The drum major stood with a glove of white,  
He raised it toward the brass and made the banner catch the light.  
A baboon played snare; trumpets faced the gate,  
The glove turned left, the cymbals turned, the band began to wait.  
He gave a little flick for time; the flute section began,  
Then held the glove aloft again to signal "hold the plan."  
A gibbon in the tuba line raised one eyebrow high,  
The drum major drew a circle that could cover half the sky.  

[Chorus]
Glove up, glove down, command the beat,  
The band stands at attention on its feet.  
Flick once, turn twice, the brass will know,  
A spotless glove can make a whole parade go.  
Glove up, glove down, the snare rolls through,  
The drum major's hand has a solo.  

[Verse 2]
The major spun the baton; the glove flew from his hand,  
It landed on a music stand and pointed at the band.  
The players read the gesture as a cue to start the tune,  
They marched in perfect time beneath the afternoon.  
The major tried to whistle, but the tuba drowned his call,  
The glove slid off the stand and softly touched the wall.  
He fetched it with a flourish, brushed a speck away,  
Then gave a grand salute that made the trumpets play.  

[Chorus]
Glove up, glove down, command the beat,  
The band stands at attention on its feet.  
Flick once, turn twice, the brass will know,  
A spotless glove can make a whole parade go.  
Glove up, glove down, the snare rolls through,  
The drum major's hand has a solo.  

[Bridge]
The band reached the plaza, where the route was marked in chalk,  
The major raised his glove to start a ceremonial walk.  
The glove's white finger pointed toward the snack stand by mistake,  
The whole parade went marching there for lemonade and cake.  

[Final Chorus]
Glove up, glove down, the route is clear,  
The plaza has the band and the refreshments near.  
Flick once, turn twice, then point ahead,  
The glove has led the march where the map had said.  
Glove up, glove down, the snare rolls through,  
The drum major's hand gets a lemonade too.  

[Ending]
The band saluted the glove; the major carried it home.
