# Steel Pan on the Lighthouse Steps

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: calypso storytelling, steel-pan replies, and lighthouse keepers whose lunch break becomes an unplanned harbor concert*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the steel pan bright; let the warning bell interrupt the story at the exact wrong moment.*

[Verse 1]
A capuchin keeper took a lunch tin up the light,
The steel-pan player followed to rehearse before the night.
He told a tale of sailors who mistook a beam for dawn,
The lighthouse bell rang loudly and the keeper carried on.

[Chorus]
Pan on the steps, tell the harbor tale,
Light sweeps the water, wind fills the sail.
Pan on the steps, ring the bell in time,
The lunch is getting cold but the story's still in rhyme.

[Verse 2]
A macaque brought a mango; a gibbon brought a cup,
The keeper tried to eat while the brass notes bubbled up.
A ship sent back a signal; the pan replied in tune,
The captain thought it meant the lighthouse would arrive by noon.

[Chorus]
Pan on the steps, tell the harbor tale,
Light sweeps the water, wind fills the sail.
Pan on the steps, ring the bell in time,
The lunch is getting cold but the story's still in rhyme.

[Bridge]
The keeper rang the warning bell; the band held every note,
A gull had landed on the lens and nearly missed the boat.
They cleared the glass together, then the beam went round again,
The steel pan played the ending for the gull and all its friends.

[Final Chorus]
Pan on the steps, tell the harbor tale,
Light sweeps the water, wind fills the sail.
Pan on the steps, let the lunch be done,
The keeper's back on watch before the setting sun.

[Ending]
The band took half the mango; the keeper took the peel.
