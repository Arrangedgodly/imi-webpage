# The Love Song Runs Out of Rhymes

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: formulaic romantic pop songs that sacrifice sense to predictable rhyming words*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Soft guitar and earnest harmony; the second singer offers increasingly unsuitable rhyme suggestions.*

[Verse 1]
I said your eyes were bright as stars, you answered that was fine,  
I said your hand belonged in mine, you drew a little line.  
I said our hearts would never part, you counted on your thumb,  
Three rhymes already filled the verse; you asked what else would come.  
I searched my little notebook for a feeling I could name,  
The next page offered flame and game and shame and much the same.  

[Chorus]
Love above, heart apart,  
We have reached the most predictable part.  
Stay away, night light,  
Every word falls neatly into sight.  
Kiss this, dear near,  
The feeling is somewhere but the rhyme is very clear.  

[Verse 2]
You said you liked the way I brought your soup when you were ill,  
I tried to rhyme the soup with loop; you looked toward the sill.  
You said you liked my kindness to the neighbor's elderly cat,  
I found a rhyme for cat and told you you should wear a hat.  
You mentioned how I'd waited while your broken bike was fixed,  
I sang about six bricks because the syllables were six.  

[Chorus]
Love above, heart apart,  
We have reached the most predictable part.  
Stay away, night light,  
Every word falls neatly into sight.  
Kiss this, dear near,  
The feeling is somewhere but the rhyme is very clear.  

[Bridge]
You took my little notebook and you set it on the floor,  
You asked me what I liked about you, nothing less or more.  
I said you made me laugh when all the trains were running late,  
You smiled; I stopped before I found a rhyme for railway gate.  

[Final Chorus]
Love above, heart apart,  
We have reached the most predictable part.  
Stay away, night light,  
I tell you that your laugh makes waiting feel all right.  
Kiss this, dear near,  
You kiss me and I whisper, "That will fit right here."
