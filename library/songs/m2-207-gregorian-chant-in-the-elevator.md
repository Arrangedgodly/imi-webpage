# Office of the Holy Lift

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Gregorian chant, reverberant unison, and a monastery elevator that stops on every floor*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Sing in measured unison with long echoes; let the elevator bell interrupt each held note.*

[Verse 1]
The gibbon choir climbed the tower, robes folded at the knee,
The elevator moved so slow, they sang a minor key.
One bell for the ground floor, two bells for the hall,
They held a single vowel as the lift ignored them all.

[Chant]
O lift, ascend, O lift, be kind,
We have a hymn and stairs behind.
O lift, descend, O lift, be slow,
We know the way the echoes go.

[Verse 2]
The doors opened on the pantry; a novice carried bread,
The choir filed out in order, then realized they'd missed the chapel.
They turned as one and re-entered, still singing the same note,
The elevator doors shut softly on the bread inside their coat.

[Chant]
O lift, ascend, O lift, be kind,
We have a hymn and stairs behind.
O lift, descend, O lift, be slow,
We know the way the echoes go.

[Bridge]
A maintenance monk arrived and pressed the button twice,
The lift began to shudder and the choir gave advice.
The bell rang for the chapel, but the car went to the roof;
The hymn's long final note became a test of soundproof.

[Verse 3]
They took the stairs at last, but kept the measured pace,
The chant went through the stairwell and filled the empty space.
The lift arrived at chapel and opened on its own,
It echoed one last chord and claimed it as its tone.

[Final Chant]
O lift, ascend, O lift, be kind,
The chapel waits; the stairs behind.
O lift, descend, the roof is high,
We sing the last note to the sky.

[Ending]
The doors opened at the chapel, and the bell took a bow.
