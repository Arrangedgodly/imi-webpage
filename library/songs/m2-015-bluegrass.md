# Three Pickers and a Goat

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: bluegrass virtuosity, fast picking, and front-porch storytelling*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Banjo and fiddle trade hot licks; the goat's bell must land with the bass.*

## Verse 1
Three pickers took the porch at Old Crow Bend.  
A goat named Tuesday wandered in as friend.  
The banjo monkey wore a straw hat tied with twine.  
The fiddle played a run that curled around the pine.  
A mandolin clicked like hail on a tin roof.  
The goat chewed the set list, which felt like proof.  
The fiddler read the notes from memory instead.  
Tuesday rang her bell and shook her shaggy head.  

## Chorus
Pick it fast, let the porch boards shake.  
Play it clean, though the strings might break.  
Pick it fast, let the neighbors know.  
The goat's on the beat, so the whole band goes.  

## Verse 2
A farmer crossed the field to ask them for a tune.  
He'd lost a gate hinge underneath the moon.  
The pickers played a reel about a rusted pin.  
Tuesday found the hinge and came clanking in.  
The farmer fixed the gate before the song was done.  
The banjo player tipped his hat toward the sun.  
A crowd gathered with jars of cider and jam.  
The goat inspected each and chose the green bean stand.  

## Bridge
They raced through a breakdown, hands a blur of wood.  
The porch held up, though nobody thought it would.  
The fiddle broke a string but never left the line.  
The mandolin took over till the last bright chime.  

## Chorus
Pick it fast, let the porch boards shake.  
Play it clean, though the strings might break.  
Pick it fast, let the neighbors know.  
The goat's on the beat, so the whole band goes.  

## Ending
At dusk, the pickers packed the case and bowed.  
Tuesday led the way home, bell ringing proud.
