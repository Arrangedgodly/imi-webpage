# The Yodel That Found the Ranger

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: alpine yodeling, echoing mountain refrains, and heroic rescue-call conventions*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Use a bright accordion and alternating chest/head-voice yodels; the echo should answer with a practical correction.*

Verse 1
A lemur climbs the lookout trail to find a missing pack,  
The map says take the ridge ahead, the goat says, "Turn it back."  
He cups his hands and yodels high across the alpine snow,  
The mountain sends the answer back: "Your pack is down below!"  
He calls again to test the range, his note comes round the bend,  
It echoes twice and adds a verse: "You dropped it near your friend."  

Chorus
Yodel-ay, yodel-o,  
Which way did the lost pack go?  
High note, low note, hear the sound,  
The ranger's at the lower ground.  
Yodel-hee, yodel-ho,  
Follow where the echoes go.  
Call out clear and wait your turn,  
The mountain echoes what you learn.  

Verse 2
The ranger hears the yodel and replies across the pass,  
The echo sends the message back with one word changed to "grass."  
The lemur searches every ledge, the goat just chews a sign,  
The pack sits by the trailhead where they stopped to drink at nine.  
He yodels that the bag is found; the ranger answers, "Great!"  
The echo says, "Bring it down, and please don't block the gate."  

Bridge
They meet below the ridge at noon, the pack is safe and dry,  
The ranger asks if yodeling can summon help nearby.  
The lemur tries a silent note, the goat begins to bleat,  
The whole small valley answers back with four-part mountain beat.  

Chorus
Yodel-ay, yodel-o,  
Which way did the lost pack go?  
High note, low note, hear the sound,  
The ranger's at the lower ground.  
Yodel-hee, yodel-ho,  
Follow where the echoes go.  
Call out clear and wait your turn,  
The mountain echoes what you learn.  

Ending
They leave the trail in quiet, with the goat in front to lead,  
It yodels once at lunch time; the echo asks for feed.
