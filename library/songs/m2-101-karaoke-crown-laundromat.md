# Karaoke Crown at the Laundromat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: karaoke contest power ballads, audience voting, and a washing machine that keeps choosing its own finalist*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Key-change pop ballad with audience claps; the washer's spin cycle supplies the surprise vote.*

Verse 1
The laundromat held karaoke after closing time,
A macaque chose a love song and prepared to hit the climb.
The first verse reached the washer; the machine began to spin,
The crowd mistook the rumble for a harmony within.

Chorus
Sing it high, sing it low,
Let the laundry steal the show.
Hold the note, turn around,
Find the crown beside the sound.

Verse 2
A gibbon sang a ballad to a sock that lost its mate,
The dryer hummed a counterline and made the ending late.
A capuchin chose a dance tune; the audience clapped along,
The washer flashed its buttons like a judge who knew the song.

Chorus
Sing it high, sing it low,
Let the laundry steal the show.
Hold the note, turn around,
Find the crown beside the sound.

Bridge
The host announced the winner; the machine began to beep,
It paused the cycle halfway through and held the finalists in suspense.
They sang a final chorus as the drum began to turn,
The timer reached its number and the winner light would burn.

Verse 3
The washer chose the ballad, the dryer chose the sock,
The judges tied the scores at ten and checked the laundry clock.
They gave the crown to both acts, a plastic hanger prize,
The washer spun a victory while the crowd gave it a rise.

Chorus
Sing it high, sing it low,
Let the laundry steal the show.
Hold the note, turn around,
Find the crown beside the sound.

Ending
The crowd went home in clean clothes, the winner wore the crown,
The washer sang one final beep before they shut it down.
