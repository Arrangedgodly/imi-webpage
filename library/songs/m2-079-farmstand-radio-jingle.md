# Farmstand Radio Jingle

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: local radio jingles, overly specific advertising hooks, and the tiny word count of a catchy slogan*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Bright three-chord acoustic jingle with a handbell tag; the station manager keeps shortening the lyric.*

Verse 1
A lemur runs the farmstand with a sign nailed to a crate,  
The radio host asks for a tune that makes the shoppers wait.  
He writes a verse on cabbage, cheese, and jars of honeydew,  
The station says the ad is long; the time slot's only two.  
He cuts the verse to half a line, the station says it's still too much,  
The host adds three more syllables and loses all the crunch.  

Chorus
Come on by, buy a pie,  
Take a turn and wave goodbye.  
Fresh from here, sold by noon,  
The farmstand closes pretty soon.  
Come on by, here's the line,  
The bus stop's just beyond the sign.  
Buy a pie, sing it twice,  
The station charged us extra price.  

Verse 2
A capuchin plays harmonica beside the apple crates,  
The jingle loops through every ad and reaches all the gates.  
The host says "buy one, get one," but the sign says "two for one,"  
The shoppers ask the clerk to check before the day's begun.  
The clerk reads out the numbers; they agree with what's on sale,  
The radio says "limited time," the customers check the scale.  

Bridge
The station edits one more word, the jingle's nearly gone,  
The host keeps only "farmstand," then hums the rest at dawn.  
The buyers find the stand by sound and shop along the lane,  
The radio charges for a chorus though it played one refrain.  

Chorus
Come on by, buy a pie,  
Take a turn and wave goodbye.  
Fresh from here, sold by noon,  
The farmstand closes pretty soon.  
Come on by, here's the line,  
The bus stop's just beyond the sign.  
Buy a pie, sing it twice,  
The station charged us extra price.  

Ending
The stand sells out, the host says thanks, the station plays the tune,  
The sign remains, "Open today," though it is closed by noon.
