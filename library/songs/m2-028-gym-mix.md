# Mirror, Timer, Go

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: gym motivational mixes and machine-countdown vocals*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Start with a coach-like call, but keep the encouragement kind and the pauses real.*

## Verse 1
The gym clock blinked at six above the mats.  
A tamarin laced her shoes and checked the flats.  
The mirror showed a face still half asleep.  
The trainer said, "Pick a pace you'll want to keep."  
The beat began with claps, then drums came in.  
A row of macaques stretched knees and grinned.  
The timer counted down from one to eight.  
No one was late; they started when they felt it straight.  

## Chorus
Breathe in, stand tall, begin again.  
Strong is showing up, then learning when.  
Breathe out, step back, let the muscles rest.  
The body gives its answers, and it knows them best.  

## Verse 2
The heavy lifter lowered every plate.  
The runner chose a walk beside the gate.  
The coach removed the volume from the track.  
A beginner asked how long to hold her back.  
They practiced form beside the window's light.  
The beat dropped low, but every move stayed right.  
A water break became the chorus cue.  
The class returned with enough breath to finish through.  

## Bridge
A workout isn't proven by a shout.  
The smartest set can end before you burn out.  
The trainer turned the timer face away.  
They stretched in silence at the end of day.  

## Chorus
Breathe in, stand tall, begin again.  
Strong is showing up, then learning when.  
Breathe out, step back, let the muscles rest.  
The body gives its answers, and it knows them best.  

## Ending
The mirror held a room of tired smiles.  
They walked back home by several different miles.
