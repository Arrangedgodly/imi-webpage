# The Jazz Trio Orders One Coffee

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jazz standards that turn a simple café order into an elaborate exchange of solos*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Brushed snare, upright bass and languid piano; the server's short responses land on the beat.*

[Verse 1]
The pianist asked for coffee with a passing minor chord,  
The bassist stretched the order till the server looked quite bored.  
The drummer said, "A little milk," with brushes on his knee,  
We hadn't named the size yet, but we'd reached a change of key.  
The server held her pencil over one small empty square,  
We gave her eight-bar answers and a thoughtful, smoky stare.  
She asked if we meant three cups; we replied in close harmony,  
"One coffee, three performers, and a tiny saucer, please."  

[Chorus]
One cup, three spoons, take your time,  
We are splitting up the bill and subdividing every dime.  
Sweet swing, slow steam, let it flow,  
We will tell you how we like it when the bassist lets us know.  
One cup, three spoons, make it last,  
The breakfast crowd has finished while our opening theme has passed.  
Blue notes, white foam, little plate,  
We have ordered half a beverage at a most expressive rate.  

[Verse 2]
She brought a cup of coffee and a jug of milk to pour,  
The pianist claimed the handle; he had played here once before.  
The bassist took the teaspoon and began a tapping break,  
The drummer heard the saucer ring and joined it with a shake.  
The server moved the milk jug to protect her steaming tray,  
We took it as a signal for a softer middle eight.  
The coffee cooled between us while our solos filled the room,  
The server brought the printed bill and swept away the spoons.  

[Chorus]
One cup, three spoons, take your time,  
We are splitting up the bill and subdividing every dime.  
Sweet swing, slow steam, let it flow,  
We will tell you how we like it when the bassist lets us know.  
One cup, three spoons, make it last,  
The breakfast crowd has finished while our opening theme has passed.  
Blue notes, white foam, little plate,  
We have ordered half a beverage at a most expressive rate.  

[Bridge]
She said, "The table's needed," so we packed our little score,  
The pianist drank the coffee as the others reached the door.  
The bassist called that selfish; he replied without a beat,  
"You each got twenty minutes. I have finally had a drink."  

[Final Chorus]
One cup, three spoons, time to pay,  
The server gives the change back in a very brisk display.  
Sweet swing, slow steam, let it flow,  
She has served the next ten tables while we struggle with our coats.  
One cup, three spoons, make it last,  
Our café set was longer than the lunch and breakfast rush.  
Blue notes, white foam, little plate,  
The pianist asks for seconds; the server points them to the gate.
