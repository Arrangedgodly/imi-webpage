# The Tango Partner Is a Shopping Basket

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: passionate tango lyrics lavishing jealous romantic attention on an uncooperative shopping basket*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Bandoneon and sharp string accents; the basket's broken wheel squeaks between phrases.*

[Verse 1]
I saw you by the doorway with your handle shining red,  
I reached for you in silence while the ceiling music bled.  
We turned beside the oranges, your little wheels were light,  
But one wheel pulled you sideways into someone else's sight.  
He caught your slender handle and he smiled a knowing smile,  
I took you back and led you down the coffee-scented aisle.  

[Chorus]
Turn for me, return to me,  
Don't drift beside the bags of tea.  
I pull you close, you pull away,  
You want the chocolate on display.  
Our steps are sharp, our wheels are worn,  
You have belonged to everyone since this reluctant morn.  

[Verse 2]
I filled you with red apples for the color of my heart,  
You tilted toward the freezer and you tried to play your part.  
The cashier watched our struggle with her eyebrows lifted high,  
I leaned across your handle and you gave a rusty cry.  
Another shopper offered me a basket that ran straight,  
I said I'd chosen this one and I wouldn't change my fate.  

[Chorus]
Turn for me, return to me,  
Don't drift beside the bags of tea.  
I pull you close, you pull away,  
You want the chocolate on display.  
Our steps are sharp, our wheels are worn,  
You have belonged to everyone since this reluctant morn.  

[Bridge]
The clerk crouched down beside us with a tiny can of oil,  
She freed the wheel and wiped away its years of secret toil.  
You rolled beneath my fingers with no jealousy or fight,  
I swept you to the checkout in a flourish of delight.  

[Final Chorus]
Turn for me, return to me,  
Don't drift beside the bags of tea.  
I pull you close, you roll away,  
The clerk needs you for someone else's day.  
Our steps are sharp, our wheels are worn,  
She stacks you with your lovers and I walk home quite forlorn.
