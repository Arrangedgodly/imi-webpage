# The Holiday Choir Sings the Storage Label

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: holiday compilation choruses and singers confusing a box label with the title of a seasonal anthem*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Warm bells, piano and broad choir harmony; the conductor reads the label in a rhythm intended for a grand refrain.*

[Verse 1]
The choir found the winter music in the cellar by the hall,  
The boxes held the stars and streamers packed away from fall.  
A label read "Keep Dry, This Way Up, Fragile, Handle Slow,"  
The conductor thought a brand-new song had reached us down below.  
He set the words to bright chords and we practiced every line,  
The altos gave the "Fragile" part a very hopeful shine.  
The caretaker came downstairs to fetch the lanterns for the night,  
She asked why we were singing all the packing instructions right.  

[Chorus]
Keep dry, this way up, let the season stay,  
Fragile hearts need careful hands to carry them today.  
Handle slow, winter glow, keep the little lights,  
The box has got a chorus that will fill the hall tonight.  
Keep dry, this way up, let the bells come through,  
The caretaker would like us to unpack the lanterns too.  

[Verse 2]
We lifted all the lanterns and the streamers from the box,  
The caretaker hung the lights beside the hall's old clock.  
The actual winter songs lay in a folder underneath,  
We practiced those for all the guests who'd come to share the feast.  
The conductor kept the box song for a small surprise encore,  
The caretaker heard her packing rules in harmony once more.  
The audience clapped gently as the words went round the hall,  
They held the paper lanterns like they understood it all.  

[Chorus]
Keep dry, this way up, let the season stay,  
Fragile hearts need careful hands to carry them today.  
Handle slow, winter glow, keep the little lights,  
The box has got a chorus that will fill the hall tonight.  
Keep dry, this way up, let the bells come through,  
The caretaker would like us to unpack the lanterns too.  

[Bridge]
We put the lanterns back inside when all the guests had gone,  
The caretaker wrote the label with a longer second song.  
"Do not stack above this line" was added near the top,  
The conductor tried a melody before she asked him to stop.  

[Final Chorus]
Keep dry, this way up, let the season stay,  
The choir sells a winter song to help the hall next day.  
Handle slow, winter glow, keep the little lights,  
The caretaker stores the chorus with the lanterns overnight.  
Keep dry, this way up, let the bells come through,  
The songbook holds the winter songs; the box label stays true.
