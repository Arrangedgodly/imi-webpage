# Deputy of the Feed Store

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: outlaw country mythology and dusty small-town authority*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: A dry snare, battered acoustic guitar, and fiddle that never sentimentalizes the law.*

## Verse 1
The feed store sheriff wore a tin star bent in two.  
A capuchin deputy checked the town's hay queue.  
His badge was made of bottle caps and wire.  
His horse was a handcart with one squeaking tire.  
A goat had crossed the chalk line at the grain.  
The clerk called it a crime against the main.  
The deputy looked down at hoofprints by the door.  
He said, "That goat has been here once before."  

## Chorus
Ride slow, deputy, the dust will settle down.  
A little law can fit inside a little town.  
Ride slow, deputy, don't draw on the feed.  
Sometimes justice is a bucket and a lead.  

## Verse 2
They found the goat beside the sack of oats.  
It wore a paper price tag like a marshal's coat.  
The owner came from pasture with a length of twine.  
The deputy said, "Your animal has crossed the line."  
He offered to replace the chewed-up ledger page.  
The goat looked bored, too wise for any cage.  
They walked it home past trucks and a painted sign.  
The fiddle played a loping tune in four-four time.  

## Bridge
The mayor wanted wanted posters on the square.  
The deputy said, "That seems a bit unfair."  
He pinned a sketch of the goat beside the store.  
It said, "Please shut the gate." They didn't ask for more.  

## Chorus
Ride slow, deputy, the dust will settle down.  
A little law can fit inside a little town.  
Ride slow, deputy, don't draw on the feed.  
Sometimes justice is a bucket and a lead.  

## Ending
The badge stayed on the counter by the twine.  
The deputy went home, his record mostly fine.
