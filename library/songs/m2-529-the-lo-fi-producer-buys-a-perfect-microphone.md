# The Lo-Fi Producer Buys a Perfect Microphone

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: lo-fi producers paying for pristine equipment and then carefully adding the noises it was designed to remove*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Soft beat, dusty piano loop and a close vocal; hiss is muted for one bar to reveal the expensive clear recording.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a very fine microphone to catch the smallest sound,  
The shopkeeper described the cleanest signal he had found.  
I set it in the studio and sang a little line,  
It captured every syllable with a clarity quite fine.  
I wanted something cozy with a crackle and a hiss,  
I added little noises till the clear words went amiss.  
The shopkeeper came to listen when I played the final track,  
He asked if I had sent the fine microphone straight back.  

[Hook / Chorus]
Soft crackle, warm dust, let the little record turn,  
The cleanest little microphone has something old to learn.  
Soft crackle, warm dust, let the evening stay,  
I paid to take the noise out and have put it back today.  
Low light, slow beat, let the tiny speakers glow,  
The signal's clear beneath the extra noises in the show.  

[Verse 2]
I muted all the added hiss to let the clean take play,  
The shopkeeper heard every word and gave a little "Okay."  
He asked why I had paid so much to hide the steady sound,  
I said I liked to choose exactly where the dust was found.  
I made the words more audible and left a little crackle,  
The drums kept their soft shuffle; no hiss was needed to rattle.  
He heard the changed mix through the phones beside the little chair,  
He said the song was rather nice and that the microphone was there.  

[Hook / Chorus]
Soft crackle, warm dust, let the little record turn,  
The cleanest little microphone has something old to learn.  
Soft crackle, warm dust, let the evening stay,  
I paid to take the noise out and have put it back today.  
Low light, slow beat, let the tiny speakers glow,  
The signal's clear beneath the extra noises in the show.  

[Bridge]
I sent the shop two versions with the hiss turned off and on,  
They used the clean one in the catalog online.  
The dusty one played after it to show a chosen style,  
The shopkeeper sold headphones to the listeners for a while.  

[Final Hook / Chorus]
Soft crackle, warm dust, let the evening stay,  
The shop has got both versions and can switch them either way.  
Low light, slow beat, let the tiny speakers glow,  
I keep the microphone beside the noise effects I know.  
Soft crackle, warm dust, let the little record turn,  
The shopkeeper sells me silence on a cable I can return.
