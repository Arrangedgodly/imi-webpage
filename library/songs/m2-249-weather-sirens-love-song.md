# The Weather Siren's Love Song

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dramatic storm-warning ballads that turn public alerts into romantic destiny*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Start with a distant brass-like drone, then let the singer's earnest vibrato compete with the actual siren test.*

[Verse 1]
The town hall posted Tuesday for the siren test at noon,  
A macaque checked the notice underneath a paper moon.  
A gibbon brought a trumpet and a lyric book of blue,  
He said, "When warning systems sound, I'll think of only you."  
The clouds were clear, the forecast mild,  
The singer watched the vane and practiced being wild.  
The siren gave a rising wail that circled through the square,  
The trumpet joined the warning like a lover's public prayer.  

[Chorus]
Sound for me, oh siren, climb into the sky,  
Call the rooftops, call the trees, call the pigeons passing by.  
When your long note finds me, I will answer in the square,  
With a brass heart full of longing and a weatherproofed affair.  
Sound for me, oh siren, give the noon its cue,  
The town is testing speakers; I am testing you.  

[Verse 2]
The siren stopped at twelve; he kept on singing there,  
The crew began a second test; his hat flew in the air.  

[Chorus]
Sound for me, oh siren, climb into the sky,  
Call the rooftops, call the trees, call the pigeons passing by.  
When your long note finds me, I will answer in the square,  
With a brass heart full of longing and a weatherproofed affair.  
Sound for me, oh siren, give the noon its cue,  
The town is testing speakers; I am testing you.  

[Bridge]
The crew replaced the cover and the test was signed complete,  
The singer took his lyric book and bowed beside the street.  
The siren stayed quite silent as the pigeons circled wide,  
The trumpet played a closing phrase; the crew waved from inside.  

[Final Chorus]
Rest for now, oh siren, the sky is clear and blue,  
No storm is on the schedule; there is nothing left to do.  
When next year's test is called, I will bring my trumpet there,  
And sing my yearly love song to a speaker in the square.  
Rest for now, oh siren, I have sung my part,  
The town has filed the test, but you have kept my heart.  

[Ending]
A flock of pigeons answered; the singer took the solo as applause.
