# The Holiday Choir Keeps the Tinsel Up

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jubilant holiday jingles and choirs whose seasonal enthusiasm refuses to obey the calendar*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bright bells, warm group harmony and an insistent festive bounce; birdsong gradually replaces the winter wind.*

[Verse 1]
We sang beside the tinsel tree when winter filled the square,  
The mayor liked our cheerful tune and left the lights up there.  
In January we returned to sing our closing song,  
The choir said the tree looked nice; the mayor went along.  
In March the flower market put its stalls beneath the lights,  
We sang about a snowy eve on several warmer nights.  
The florist asked about the bells; she sold a tray of thyme,  
The choir said that cheerfulness could outlast wintertime.  

[Chorus]
Ring, little bells, let the bright lights glow,  
The calendar has hurried but the choir likes it slow.  
Shine, little tree, keep the season here,  
We have got a winter chorus that can last the whole bright year.  
Ring, little bells, let the whole square sing,  
The tinsel is still silver though the market smells of spring.  

[Verse 2]
By June the square had deck chairs and a stand that sold cold tea,  
The tree was wearing sunglasses someone put beneath its leaves.  
We sang the snowy chorus while the children ate their ice,  
The mayor said a summer tune might sound a little nice.  
The choir changed the words to sunny days and breezes light,  
The bells became a bicycle that passed us on the right.  
We packed the tinsel carefully and put the tree away,  
The choir sang a garden song for that year's longest day.  

[Chorus]
Ring, little bells, let the bright lights glow,  
The calendar has hurried but the choir likes it slow.  
Shine, little tree, keep the season here,  
We have got a winter chorus that can last the whole bright year.  
Ring, little bells, let the whole square sing,  
The tinsel is still silver though the market smells of spring.  

[Bridge]
In autumn we were singing of the seaside and the sun,  
The mayor pointed to the leaves; another season had begun.  
We changed the words to harvest days and coats against the rain,  
Then winter reached the village and the tree came out again.  

[Final Chorus]
Ring, little bells, let the bright lights glow,  
The calendar has caught the choir moving rather slow.  
Shine, little tree, keep the season here,  
The sunglasses are still hanging from your ear.  
Ring, little bells, let the whole square sing,  
We sing of autumn apples while the winter lights swing.
