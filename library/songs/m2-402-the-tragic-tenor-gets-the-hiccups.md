# The Tragic Tenor Gets the Hiccups

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tragic operatic death arias whose immense emotional control fails at a tiny bodily interruption*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Grand orchestral accompaniment and a ringing tenor voice; hiccups puncture sustained notes without quoting a real aria.*

[Verse 1]
I lay upon a velvet couch beneath a painted moon,  
My character had lost his love and would expire soon.  
The orchestra grew gentle as I drew my final breath,  
A hiccup cut the silence at the solemn edge of death.  
The soprano bent above me with a rose against her chest,  
She waited for my dying line; I hiccuped through the rest.  
The conductor stretched a measure while I swallowed twice in vain,  
The oboe held its sorrow; I broke into life again.  

[Chorus]
Farewell, my love, the night is near,  
A tiny little hiccup is the last thing you will hear.  
Farewell, bright moon, let darkness start,  
The tragedy is waiting for the jumping in my heart.  
Weep, strings, weep, keep the sorrow deep,  
My character is dying but my diaphragm won't sleep.  

[Verse 2]
The soprano brought a goblet as a mercy for my throat,  
Its water calmed me briefly; I resumed the dying note.  
The audience grew quieter; the couch supported all,  
I raised one trembling hand and hiccuped at the painted wall.  
She sang a line about my ghost returning from afar,  
The conductor put a tiny rest where every hiccup was.  
I held my breath behind the rose and let the strings go on,  
At last the spasms faded and I finished with the dawn.  

[Chorus]
Farewell, my love, the night is near,  
A tiny little hiccup is the last thing you will hear.  
Farewell, bright moon, let darkness start,  
The tragedy is waiting for the jumping in my heart.  
Weep, strings, weep, keep the sorrow deep,  
My character is dying but my diaphragm won't sleep.  

[Bridge]
The curtain fell upon my death; the house applauded loud,  
The soprano kept the goblet as we bowed before the crowd.  
The critic called my breathing an ingenious comic strain,  
The director booked an encore; I could not do it again.  

[Final Chorus]
Farewell, my love, the night is near,  
The director wants the hiccup but the symptom isn't here.  
Farewell, bright moon, let darkness start,  
The oboe plays a little jump to satisfy his heart.  
Weep, strings, weep, keep the sorrow deep,  
The orchestra has learned the part my diaphragm can't keep.
