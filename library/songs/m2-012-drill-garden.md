# Raised Beds, Low Bass

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: UK drill production tropes and neighborhood garden rivalry*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Sliding bass under terse verses; let garden shears click on the backbeat.*

## Verse 1
Raised beds on Briar Street, cedar boards in line.  
The young baboon gardener measured every vine.  
His crew wore gloves, their tools were neatly stacked.  
They argued over whose tomatoes had the best tact.  
A rival plot across the alley grew too tall.  
The nettles crossed the fence and started it all.  
A bass note slid beneath the watering hose.  
The lead said, "Name your weeds, we'll see how this goes."  

## Chorus
Keep it low, let the seed rows speak.  
Hands stay clean, though the compost reeks.  
Keep it low, bring the gloves at ten.  
We settle this with spades, then we plant again.  

## Verse 2
The rival crew arrived in matching rainproof coats.  
Their leader held a ledger full of gardening quotes.  
They mapped the fence line with a string and chalk.  
Both teams took turns and let the elders talk.  
Auntie Marmoset named the boundary by the drain.  
She'd planted there since before the paved lane.  
They moved the beds, widened one shared path.  
Then played a beatbox round to settle the last laugh.  

## Bridge
The music video showed no threats, just gloves and soil.  
The director asked for menace; the crew refused the spoil.  
They filmed a wheelbarrow rolling through the gate.  
The toughest look they gave was checking if seeds were late.  

## Chorus
Keep it low, let the seed rows speak.  
Hands stay clean, though the compost reeks.  
Keep it low, bring the gloves at ten.  
We settle this with spades, then we plant again.  

## Ending
The tomatoes ripened on each separate side.  
Both crews shared the harvest, and the bass rolled wide.
