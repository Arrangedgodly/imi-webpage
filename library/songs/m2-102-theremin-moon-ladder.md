# Theremin on the Moon Ladder

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: theremin science-fiction ballads, eerie glissandos, and a ladder operator mistaking the instrument for a visitor from space*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Theremin glides over a slow electronic pulse; the performer should sound earnest while the ladder creaks answer.*

Verse 1
A langur played a theremin beneath the planetarium dome,
The notes went drifting up and down like signals seeking home.
A ladder stood beside the stage to reach the hanging moon,
The stagehand heard the instrument and answered with a broom.

Chorus
Waver, silver signal, rise,
Travel through the velvet skies.
Reach the moon and circle back,
Follow every airy track.

Verse 2
A capuchin on the ladder raised one hand toward the light,
The theremin slid to a note that sounded out of sight.
The audience leaned forward; the star map began to sway,
The stagehand thought an alien had come to take the display.

Chorus
Waver, silver signal, rise,
Travel through the velvet skies.
Reach the moon and circle back,
Follow every airy track.

Bridge
The ladder creaked a message, though no one touched its side,
The player made a wavering note; the stagehand ducked to hide.
The monkey climbed down laughing and explained the sound,
The ladder wasn't speaking; one loose bolt was spinning round.

Verse 3
They tightened up the ladder, then played the song again,
The theremin's long glissando floated over every friend.
The moon stayed painted overhead; the stars remained in place,
The stagehand waved the broom in time and joined the outer space.

Chorus
Waver, silver signal, rise,
Travel through the velvet skies.
Reach the moon and circle back,
Follow every airy track.

Ending
The show was done, the ladder still, the theremin was packed,
One final note went drifting out and found its way right back.
