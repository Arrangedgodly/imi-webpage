# The Drum Circle Drops the Triangle

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: inclusive percussion-circle songs and players discovering a quiet instrument has the loudest accidental interruption*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Hand drums and sung group responses; the triangle first rings out of time when it falls onto a hard floor.*

[Verse 1]
The drum circle met on Saturday beneath the hall's bright beam,  
We held a steady little pulse to make the afternoon's theme.  
One newcomer brought a triangle and a little metal stick,  
She waited for an opening to add her gentle click.  
The leader said that everyone could enter with their sound,  
The triangle slipped from her loop and landed on the ground.  
It rang across the wooden floor beyond the drum's small beat,  
The circle paused to hear its bright arrival by her feet.  

[Response Refrain]
Everyone has a rhythm, everyone has a place,  
The triangle has announced itself in its own ringing space.  
Everyone has a rhythm, everyone can join,  
A metal shape has found the floor instead of any chosen point.  
Hands steady, ears open, let the next sound come,  
The triangle has made more noise than any little drum.  

[Verse 2]
She picked it up and tied its loop more firmly round her hand,  
The leader gave a quiet count to bring it to the band.  
She struck a single note where all the other drums held still,  
The triangle's clear ringing filled the space she'd meant to fill.  
We played the round together with a gap each eight-bar phrase,  
Her little note could answer all the steady drumming days.  
The circle finished gently and she set the triangle down,  
The floor got no second solo at the ending of the sound.  

[Response Refrain]
Everyone has a rhythm, everyone has a place,  
The triangle has announced itself in its own ringing space.  
Everyone has a rhythm, everyone can join,  
A metal shape has found the floor instead of any chosen point.  
Hands steady, ears open, let the next sound come,  
The triangle has made more noise than any little drum.  

[Bridge]
She brought a padded mat next week to rest the triangle on,  
The leader asked about the loud arrival now long gone.  
The newcomer said the floor had made a much more brilliant tone,  
She preferred to let her hand choose when the sound was shown.  

[Final Response]
Everyone has a rhythm, everyone can join,  
The triangle has got a rest and a well-chosen starting point.  
Hands steady, ears open, let the next sound come,  
The floor has been retired from the circle's little drum.  
Everyone has a rhythm, everyone has a place,  
The leader adds the wooden floor to the group's emergency bass.
