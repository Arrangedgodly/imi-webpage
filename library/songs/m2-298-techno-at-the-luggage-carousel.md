# Techno at the Luggage Carousel

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: techno's relentless four-on-the-floor pulse and airport announcements that repeat without resolving the wait*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor kick and looping synth; treat each carousel rotation as a new build, even when the same suitcase returns.*

**Verse 1**
At Gate 12, the baggage belt begins its steady turn.  
A gibbon DJ samples beeps from every bag return.  
The kick drum hits on four; the conveyor hits on two.  
A green suitcase circles past, then circles into view.  
The screen says BAGS ARRIVING; the speaker says, "Please wait."  
The DJ builds a rising line; the belt maintains its pace.  

**Chorus**
Round again, round again, the luggage never lands;  
Bass below, lights above, the belt obeys commands.  
One bag, two bag, the same blue case in flight;  
The beat keeps turning circles through the airport night.  

**Verse 2**
A traveler points at one red case, then watches it go by.  
The DJ samples every wheel and adds a laser sigh.  
The crowd begins to step in time, each traveler in a row.  
The suitcases make a second line and join the after-show.  
A label tears; the case spins past without a name.  
The agent tries to stop the belt; the DJ calls it a game.  

**Chorus**
Round again, round again, the luggage never lands;  
Bass below, lights above, the belt obeys commands.  
One bag, two bag, the same blue case in flight;  
The beat keeps turning circles through the airport night.  

**Bridge**
The agent finds the matching tag beneath the red case's handle.  
She stops the belt; the kick drum pauses; still the lights all twinkle.  
The owner lifts the suitcase off, the missing label is clear.  
The DJ drops the loop and plays a softer track to hear.  

**Ending**
The belt goes quiet; travelers leave with bags in hand.  
The DJ packs the sampler and folds up the stand.  
The green case circles once more, then stops at gate twelve.  
The agent claims it for lost property and wheels it to the shelf.
