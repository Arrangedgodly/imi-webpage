# The Folk Duet Mails the Moon

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: earnest acoustic love duets whose promise to give someone the moon becomes a very modest postal gesture*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Two alternating voices over fingerpicked guitar; postal counter questions interrupt the generous romantic declarations.*

[Verse 1]
I promised you the moon one night beside the river bend,  
You said you'd like it posted to the room you rent, my friend.  
I found a lovely postcard with the moon across its face,  
And wrote a little love note in the empty lower space.  
The clerk asked for an address as I held it to the light,  
I told him it was going to the one who loved the night.  
He said that wasn't helpful to the people sorting mail,  
I wrote your street and number with a less romantic tale.  

[Chorus]
Take the moon, take the stars,  
They are printed on a postcard traveling toward where you are.  
Take my heart, let it stay,  
The sorting office promises a rather ordinary day.  
Take the moon, hold it near,  
The stamp is on the corner and the whole night fits in here.  

[Verse 2]
You found it in your letterbox beside a water bill,  
The moon was rather small but quite a lovely silver still.  
You put it on the mantelpiece and wrote a card for me,  
It pictured summer sunlight and a very gentle sea.  
The clerk asked where it went and you supplied the proper street,  
Your little morning reached me while I sat down there to eat.  
I put it by the window and I called to sing a line,  
You said the moon and morning were arriving quite on time.  

[Chorus]
Take the moon, take the stars,  
They are printed on a postcard traveling toward where you are.  
Take my heart, let it stay,  
The sorting office promises a rather ordinary day.  
Take the moon, hold it near,  
The stamp is on the corner and the whole night fits in here.  

[Bridge]
We met beside the river with a stack of cards we'd saved,  
The clerk was walking home and saw the moon we'd sent away.  
He said our little cosmos had arrived there right on time,  
Then handed us a card marked "My Soulmate," not mine.  

[Final Chorus]
Take the moon, take the stars,  
The clerk would like a postcode for the next heart where you are.  
Take my heart, let it stay,  
We write our street quite clearly now beneath the Milky Way.  
Take the moon, hold it near,  
The universe is generous; the sorting bins are here.
