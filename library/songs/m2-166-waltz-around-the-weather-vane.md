# Waltz Around the Weather Vane

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Viennese waltz, sweeping turns, formal invitations, and couples who keep circling the same small room*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Three-quarter strings and a rising turn; let the wind vane's creak mark every third beat.*

Verse 1
The gibbons dressed in Sunday coats and gathered on the roof,
The weather vane was loose again, the forecast lacked its proof.
A macaque offered one gloved hand to lead a graceful turn,
They whirled around the chimney while the clouds began to burn.

Chorus
One-two-three, the vane goes round,
One-two-three, the wind is sound.
Turn beneath the copper sky,
Waltz until the gusts pass by.

Verse 2
The couples crossed the rooftop in a circle wide and grand,
A loose hat took the lead and danced across the land.
They chased it through the weather, still keeping time and grace,
The vane spun faster than the guests could keep their place.

Chorus
One-two-three, the vane goes round,
One-two-three, the wind is sound.
Turn beneath the copper sky,
Waltz until the gusts pass by.

Bridge
The roofer climbed the ladder with a wrench inside his coat,
He asked them all to step aside before he fixed the bolt.
The dancers made a ballroom lane and swept him to the side,
He turned the nut between each beat and kept the vane from flight.

Verse 3
The weather vane stood steady as the wind began to fade,
The couples found their partners and returned the hat they'd chased.
The roofer took a bow; the band resumed the song,
The roof became a ballroom till the sunset carried on.

Chorus
One-two-three, the vane goes round,
One-two-three, the wind is sound.
Turn beneath the copper sky,
Waltz until the gusts pass by.

Ending
The roof was clear, the vane secure, the evening breeze was mild,
The dancers waltzed around the wrench and left the roofer smiled.
