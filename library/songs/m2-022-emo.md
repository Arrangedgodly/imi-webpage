# Black Hoodie Yearbook

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: mid-2000s emo confessionals and dramatic school memories*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Begin with thin guitar and restrained drums; let the singer crack the long vowel.*

## Verse 1
The yearbook photo caught me blinking at the flash.  
My black hoodie hid a cafeteria mash.  
The drummer wrote our band name on his sleeve.  
We thought the gym's bad acoustics meant the crowd believed.  
The lockers rattled every time we played.  
My notebook held the lines I never said.  
A friend drew stars beside the margins, small and crooked.  
I said I wasn't hurt; she knew I faked it.  

## Chorus
If I sound too loud, forgive the room.  
The gym keeps every echo after June.  
If I miss the note, let it ring.  
We were young and sure of everything.  

## Verse 2
At graduation, our set got cut to one.  
The principal announced the raffle had begun.  
We played through numbers, names, and folding chairs.  
The singer's voice went thin above the stairs.  
Then every friend we knew began to sing.  
Their off-key chorus shook the rafters' spring.  
The yearbook closed; the hallway lost its sound.  
I kept that scribbled star inside the pocket I found.  

## Bridge
Years later, the hoodie hangs too small and worn.  
The cuff has frayed where restless fingers mourned.  
I saw my friend last week outside the store.  
She drew a star in air, then we were fourteen once more.  

## Chorus
If I sound too loud, forgive the room.  
The gym keeps every echo after June.  
If I miss the note, let it ring.  
We were young and sure of everything.  

## Ending
The band plays one reunion every fall.  
We still forget the bridge, but know it all.
