# Choir of the Hollow Branch

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Gregorian chant, solemn monastic harmonies, and echoes joining the wrong verse*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Slow modal chant for a small monkey choir; let the cave answer each phrase one beat late.*

Verse 1
In a hollow branch at dawn the choir arranged its knees,
A lemur held the candle while the capuchin found keys.
The leader raised one finger for the ancient opening tone,
The cave returned that finger's echo, then a second of its own.

Chorus
O hollow, hold the note,
Let the echo find its coat.
O hollow, keep it round,
Bring the wandering sound around.

Verse 2
They sang of patience, stillness, and a seed beneath the ground,
The cave sang back "beneath the ground" and added "who is found?"
The tenor turned to answer; the bass forgot his part,
The echo copied both of them and made a brand-new start.

Chorus
O hollow, hold the note,
Let the echo find its coat.
O hollow, keep it round,
Bring the wandering sound around.

Bridge
They tried a whisper so the walls would not reply,
The cave returned it louder with a very solemn sigh.
The candle-bearing lemur took a step beyond the stone,
And found the source of every echo was the choir's own drum.

Verse 3
They carried out the drum and sang beneath the open sky,
The sound went straight to morning; no reflection wandered by.
The echo stayed inside the branch, where quiet kept its seat,
The choir found a softer room and sang in even beat.

Chorus
O hollow, hold the note,
Let the echo find its coat.
O hollow, keep it round,
Bring the wandering sound around.

Ending
The choir bowed to open air, the candle made no fuss,
The cave kept one last solemn hum, addressed to none of us.
