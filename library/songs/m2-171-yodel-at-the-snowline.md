# Yodel at the Snowline

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Alpine yodeling, long-distance echoing, and a singer who mistakes every returned call for a new duet partner*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Alternate low chest notes and high yodel breaks; the mountain echo should answer one beat late.*

Verse 1
A gibbon climbed the lookout hill to call the morning clear,
He yodeled "Yo-de-lay!" and heard it reappear.
He thought a second singer stood beyond the snowy line,
A capuchin was packing gear and singing at the sign.

Chorus
Yo-de-lay, yo-de-lo,
Send it up and let it go.
Yo-de-lay, yo-de-lee,
Who is singing back to me?

Verse 2
He called across the valley, "Can you join my tune?"
The echo came back softly, ending on a spoon.
He searched among the fir trees, climbed a rocky stair,
The capuchin was eating lunch and humming in the air.

Chorus
Yo-de-lay, yo-de-lo,
Send it up and let it go.
Yo-de-lay, yo-de-lee,
Who is singing back to me?

Bridge
The capuchin said, "That was you, returning from the stone,"
The gibbon said, "I sang it twice; the second was my own."
They tested every hillside with a note and then a clap,
The echo came back louder from the picnic shelter's flap.

Verse 3
They moved the food beneath a roof to keep it from the breeze,
The shelter sent their yodels back between the wooden beams.
They sang a final chorus, then packed their lunch to go,
The echo kept on singing as they traveled down below.

Chorus
Yo-de-lay, yo-de-lo,
Send it up and let it go.
Yo-de-lay, yo-de-lee,
Who is singing back to me?

Ending
The capuchin was first to leave; the gibbon called, "Goodbye!"
The echo answered, "Buy a spoon!" and no one knows just why.
