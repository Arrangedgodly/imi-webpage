# Key Change at the Car Wash

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: arena-rock power ballads, dramatic key changes, and sustained notes over ordinary stakes*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Big electric guitars and a rising key change; the high note should coincide with the rinse cycle shutting off.*

Verse 1
I drove my muddy cart beneath the fluorescent lights,  
The wash attendant looked at me and said, "We close at five."  
I sang about the road that left its footprints on my frame,  
The guitar roared behind me as the soap began to rain.  
A chorus of my cousins held their hands up to the sky,  
The clerk said, "Please keep both your arms inside the ride."  

Chorus
I will rise, I will shine,  
I will rinse this wheel in time.  
Turn it up, let the water fall,  
I will clean this cart for all.  
Higher now, the foam is bright,  
We have one more pass tonight.  
I will rise, I will shine,  
Till the dryer hits the line.  

Verse 2
The first rinse misses one back wheel, the singer takes a breath,  
The drummer rolls a thunder fill like fate has tapped the deck.  
The attendant points to vacuum bays and hands me numbered tags,  
I belt a note so long and high it shakes the hanging rags.  
My cart emerges spotless; the guitar begins to wail,  
The clerk says, "That's the exit," and points beside the pail.  

Bridge
The key change lifts the roof; a pigeon joins the sound,  
The pressure washer changes pitch and spins the hose around.  
We grab it in a harmony, we set the nozzle down,  
The biggest note of all arrives when nothing hits the ground.  

Chorus
I will rise, I will shine,  
I will rinse this wheel in time.  
Turn it up, let the water fall,  
I will clean this cart for all.  
Higher now, the foam is bright,  
We have one more pass tonight.  
I will rise, I will shine,  
Till the dryer hits the line.  

Ending
The band unloads its amplifiers, the cart rolls clean away,  
The clerk sings through a squeegee, "Please come back another day."
