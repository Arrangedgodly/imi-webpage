# The Celtic Reel Dancer Ties a Long Lace

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: brisk reel performances and dancers whose proud footwork meets a shoe lace much longer than the choreography allows*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Fiddle, flute and a fast dance pulse; the final knot gets a deliberately extravagant drum accent.*

[Verse 1]
I broke a lace while dressing for the dancing at the fair,  
My brother found a spare one in the cupboard by the stair.  
It stretched across the table like a rope for tying boats,  
I threaded it and tied a bow beneath my little coat.  
The ends lay on the boards below my brightly polished shoe,  
I looped them through another knot and thought the task was through.  
I stepped into the opening reel and lifted up my feet,  
The knot brushed at my ankle with a little extra beat.  

[Chorus]
Step it high, turn it quick,  
The lace has got a figure that is longer than the reel.  
Heel and toe, round we go,  
There is half a mile of bow beneath the dancer's coat.  
Step it high, hold the line,  
My brother lent a boating rope and said the shoe would be fine.  

[Verse 2]
I left between two numbers and sat down beside the band,  
The fiddler kept the others dancing while I worked by hand.  
I tucked the extra lace beneath the loops across the shoe,  
The drummer held a little torch to see what I would do.  
I tied a smaller final bow and tucked its ends away,  
Then danced the next reel neatly with no rope in the way.  
My brother saw the tidy shoe and asked about his lace,  
He said he'd need it for the garden where it marked a growing place.  

[Chorus]
Step it high, turn it quick,  
The lace has got a figure that is longer than the reel.  
Heel and toe, round we go,  
There is half a mile of bow beneath the dancer's coat.  
Step it high, hold the line,  
My brother lent a boating rope and said the shoe would be fine.  

[Bridge]
At home I took the long lace out and returned it to his hand,  
He used it as a garden line beside the seedling stand.  
I bought a pair of short laces before the dancing day,  
The long one holds a row of peas; it keeps their growing straight.  

[Final Chorus]
Step it high, turn it quick,  
The lace has got a garden job beyond the final reel.  
Heel and toe, round we go,  
The peas are standing neatly where the dancing rope now goes.  
Step it high, hold the line,  
My brother says the garden has the straighter feet this time.
