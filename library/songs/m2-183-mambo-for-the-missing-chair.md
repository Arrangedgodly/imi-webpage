# Mambo for the Missing Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: mambo brass breaks, sharp accents, and a bandleader who turns the search for one chair into a dance number*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Punchy brass accents, conga and piano; each "where's the chair?" cue stops the dance cold.*

Verse 1
The band began a mambo in the hall beside the stairs,
The singer looked around and asked, "Has anyone seen chairs?"
The dancers found the answer when the trumpet hit the break,
They turned around in perfect time and stepped around the cake.

Chorus
Mambo, mambo, stop and spin,
Find the chair and pull it in.
Mambo, mambo, hit the floor,
One more seat and one encore.

Verse 2
A gibbon took a chair outside to help the drummer stand,
The drummer took it to the stage and passed it to the band.
The singer stopped the music, asked the crowd to take a seat,
The crowd kept dancing anyway, the chair moved to the beat.

Chorus
Mambo, mambo, stop and spin,
Find the chair and pull it in.
Mambo, mambo, hit the floor,
One more seat and one encore.

Bridge
They found the missing chair beneath a coat and tambourine,
The seat was at the sound desk, where the bass had never been.
The bandleader called "Freeze!" and every dancer froze,
The chair rolled to the center, wearing someone's borrowed clothes.

Verse 3
The singer sat for one brief bar, then leapt back to the floor,
The chair was now a throne for every trumpet encore.
They passed it round the circle, each musician took a turn,
The audience kept dancing till the velvet cushion burned.

Chorus
Mambo, mambo, stop and spin,
Find the chair and pull it in.
Mambo, mambo, hit the floor,
One more seat and one encore.

Ending
The chair was put away before the band went home,
The mambo kept on spinning in the empty hall alone.
