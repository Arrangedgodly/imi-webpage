# The Waltz of the Wobbly Ladder

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Viennese waltz elegance and ballroom etiquette enforced during an inconvenient repair*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Start with a graceful three-quarter-time piano and strings; let the ladder squeaks become part of the dance rhythm.*

Verse 1
In the museum's marble hall, a ceiling bulb has gone,  
The curator bows to every guest and says, "The waltz goes on."  
A gibbon climbs a ladder while the orchestra begins,  
The first two steps are lovely; then the third one gently spins.  
The dancers circle round the legs, they never break the line,  
The ladder turns like royalty and bows at every time.  

Chorus
One-two-three, keep the chandelier in view,  
One-two-three, the ladder's dancing too.  
Mind your elbows, mind your hem, don't step upon the paint,  
A waltz can be a repair job if the floor is not too faint.  
One-two-three, the bulb is almost bright,  
One-two-three, we've ballroomed through the night.  

Verse 2
The cellist tunes a careful A; the ladder answers squeak,  
The curator calls it counterpoint and asks it not to peak.  
A mandrill in a satin coat attempts a grand dip,  
He dips beneath the ladder rung and loses one shoe tip.  
The electrician climbs one step, then asks for more room,  
The guests all glide in measured time and clear a perfect boom.  

Bridge
The ladder reaches overhead; the bulb shines like a star,  
The crew applauds in ballroom pose, the repairman from afar.  
The waltz concludes on polished wood, a final sweeping turn,  
The curator hands out exit slips; the chandelier must cool and burn.  

Chorus
One-two-three, keep the chandelier in view,  
One-two-three, the ladder's dancing too.  
Mind your elbows, mind your hem, don't step upon the paint,  
A waltz can be a repair job if the floor is not too faint.  
One-two-three, the bulb is almost bright,  
One-two-three, we've ballroomed through the night.  

Ending
The dancers leave in perfect step, the crew packs every tool,  
The ladder takes a final bow and rests beside the stool.
