# The Whisper-Pop Singer Records a Crisp

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: breathy intimate pop production and close microphones that give a snack more dramatic presence than the singer*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Minimal bass, soft finger snaps and whispered melody; the crisp's crunch erupts at full microphone proximity.*

[Verse 1]
I sang beside the microphone as softly as a dream,  
The engineer turned up the gain to catch the little stream.  
I told the room a secret on a barely moving breath,  
The silence held my fragile voice and nothing sounded left.  
I took a crisp between two takes and held it to my mouth,  
Its crunch became a thunderstorm that shook the monitors loud.  
The engineer pulled down the gain and stared across at me,  
The secret had a snack break and the snack was all we'd hear.  

[Chorus]
Soft voice, small room, lean into the sound,  
The tiny little secret has a crunch that shakes the ground.  
Slow breath, sweet hush, let the quiet stay,  
The crisp has told the whole room what my voice could never say.  
Soft voice, small room, hear the silence bloom,  
There is a very noisy potato in the middle of the tune.  

[Verse 2]
We saved the crunchy sample as a single sharp drum hit,  
The engineer suggested we could make a beat of it.  
I sang the little secret over crisps in perfect time,  
The snack had found a rhythm underneath the whispered line.  
I finished off the packet well away from all the gear,  
Then came back to the microphone and made the ending clear.  
The producer heard the chorus and demanded one more take,  
He said the crisp should get a solo when the little whispers break.  

[Chorus]
Soft voice, small room, lean into the sound,  
The tiny little secret has a crunch that shakes the ground.  
Slow breath, sweet hush, let the quiet stay,  
The crisp has told the whole room what my voice could never say.  
Soft voice, small room, hear the silence bloom,  
There is a very noisy potato in the middle of the tune.  

[Bridge]
I sang it live on Friday with the sample on a pad,  
The audience cheered the little crunch as if the snack were back.  
The engineer stood in the wings and ate a silent pear,  
The microphone was nowhere near; the whisper had its air.  

[Final Chorus]
Soft voice, small room, lean into the sound,  
The potato has a following but never comes around.  
Slow breath, sweet hush, let the quiet stay,  
The sample does the chewing and the singer sings away.  
Soft voice, small room, hear the silence bloom,  
The producer wants a biscuit for the follow-up tune.
