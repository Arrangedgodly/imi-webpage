# The Marching Band Turns at the Bakery

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: military-style marching-band precision meeting a route sheet damaged by breakfast*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Brass march and snare cadence; one drum major calls directions while the band answers in shouted unison.*

[Verse 1]
The mayor asked the marching band to lead the summer fair,  
The route sheet lay beside a bun upon the drummer's chair.  
A little drop of sticky jam obscured the printed turn,  
The drum major read what he could and said we'd live and learn.  
We took the main street smartly with our uniforms in line,  
The trumpets raised the morning as the sun began to shine.  
The turn should take us to the square beside the flower beds,  
We marched into the bakery behind our drum major instead.  

[Chant]
Left, right, hold the line!  
Bread is rising right on time!  
Left, right, lift your knees!  
The baker wants a dozen loaves, not twenty-two trombones, please!  
Left, right, keep the beat!  
The parade has found the flour at the end of every street!  

[Verse 2]
The baker stopped us at the door and pointed to the map,  
The band stood in the little yard to let the morning traffic pass.  
He wiped the jam off carefully and read the missing word,  
The drum major had taken "right" where "left" should have been heard.  
We turned the band in pairs until we faced the street again,  
The drummer tapped a quiet beat to bring us through the lane.  
We reached the square a little late; the mayor had his speech,  
The baker brought the marching band a little bread roll each.  

[Chant]
Left, right, hold the line!  
Bread is rising right on time!  
Left, right, lift your knees!  
The baker wants a dozen loaves, not twenty-two trombones, please!  
Left, right, keep the beat!  
The parade has found the flour at the end of every street!  

[Bridge]
The mayor thanked the baker for the breakfast on the square,  
The drum major kept the map away from everything he'd share.  
The baker watched the afternoon parade from his own door,  
He held a sign that told us where to turn a little more.  

[Final Chant]
Left, right, hold the line!  
The bakery has posted its own closing time!  
Left, right, keep the beat!  
The drum major has a clean map of the actual street!  
Left, right, lift your knees!  
The baker's sign says, "Bread inside. Parade to the left, please!"
