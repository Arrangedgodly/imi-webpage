# The Bass Drop Needs a Permit

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: festival dance anthems whose enormous drops collide with a venue volume restriction*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor drums, rising synths and a bass drop played on one muted triangle.*

[Verse 1]
We built a tower of speakers beside the municipal lake,  
The mayor brought his dancing shoes and half a birthday cake.  
Our gibbon at the mixing desk raised both arms to the sky,  
A ranger put a clipboard up and caught his battle cry.  
She pointed to the limit printed clearly on the gate,  
He said the drop was coming and the forest ought to wait.  
She lent him one small triangle and a little rubber stick,  
He promised forty thousand fans the thunder would be quick.  

[Chorus]
Get your hands up for the authorized sound,  
Shake the paperwork and pass it around.  
Three, two, one, let the whole world rock,  
Someone very gently tap the block.  
Feel that bass in the palms of your hands,  
Read the conditions where the loudspeaker stands.  
Raise your voice to a reasonable height,  
We're the quietest earthquake in the park tonight.  

[Verse 2]
He stretched the rising synth until the ducks began to stare,  
The ranger held her meter like a precious silver pear.  
A capuchin in glitter boots demanded something deep,  
The drummer struck a cushion and the ducks went back to sleep.  
The mayor yelled for fireworks; the ranger shook her head,  
We turned the giant lighting rig to tasteful amber instead.  
The gibbon climbed the speaker with his tiny rubber wand,  
And forty thousand fingers made a ripple round the pond.  

[Chorus]
Get your hands up for the authorized sound,  
Shake the paperwork and pass it around.  
Three, two, one, let the whole world rock,  
Someone very gently tap the block.  
Feel that bass in the palms of your hands,  
Read the conditions where the loudspeaker stands.  
Raise your voice to a reasonable height,  
We're the quietest earthquake in the park tonight.  

[Bridge]
He leaned into the microphone, the crowd leaned in as well,  
The ranger gave permission for a single little bell.  
He tapped it; in the silence every dancer heard it ring,  
Then someone dropped the birthday plate and wrecked the whole darn thing.  

[Final Chorus]
Get your hands up for the authorized sound,  
Shake the paperwork and pass it around.  
Three, two, one, let the whole world rock,  
Someone very gently tap the block.  
Feel that bass in the palms of your hands,  
Read the conditions where the loudspeaker stands.  
Our encore is a spoon against a plate,  
The ranger stamps the cake and closes up the gate.
