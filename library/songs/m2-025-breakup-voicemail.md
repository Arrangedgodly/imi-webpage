# Voicemail After the Ferry

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: breakup voicemail songs and withheld-message melodrama*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Close vocal over piano; let the outgoing-message beep start the final verse.*

## Verse 1
The ferry left at seven; I was standing by the pier.  
Your last voice message ended, "I'll be back next year."  
The gulls took turns inspecting every empty bench.  
I practiced what to say, then smelled the engine's wrench.  
My phone kept your old greeting, bright and clear.  
I called to hear your voice, then hung up near.  
A dockhand swept the tickets from the planks.  
I gave him mine and thanked him for the chance.  

## Chorus
Beep, then breathe, then leave it there.  
A silence can be honest, if you let it have its air.  
Beep, then breathe, don't make the line pretend.  
Some goodbyes can stop before the final bend.  

## Verse 2
The ferry crossed the harbor, smaller than a thought.  
I heard the words I'd almost sent, then tied them in a knot.  
You liked the northern light, I liked the southern train.  
We chose the same small town and still missed the lane.  
I saved the message, not to play it every night.  
Just proof we tried to say the thing outright.  
The dock bell rang; the crew pulled in the rope.  
I turned away and carried home my coat.  

## Bridge
I won't erase the number, won't keep calling after two.  
The choice sits on the screen, and I can let it through.  
The outgoing beep is not a magic door.  
It only means the line can hold no more.  

## Chorus
Beep, then breathe, then leave it there.  
A silence can be honest, if you let it have its air.  
Beep, then breathe, don't make the line pretend.  
Some goodbyes can stop before the final bend.  

## Ending
The saved message rests with the ferry times.  
I hear the water now, not your old lines.
