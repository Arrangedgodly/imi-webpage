# Pear of the High C

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: grand opera, a diva's climactic high note, and an absurdly prolonged search for a snack*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Give the diva a huge orchestral entrance; let the chorus answer every note with fruit clues.*

[Verse 1]
Madame Luma, soprano, swept across the stage,
Her golden pear had vanished from the velvet snack cage.
The orchestra struck thunder; she sang, "Where can it be?"
The chorus held a note so long the usher found a key.

[Chorus]
Pear, pear, answer my aria,
Hide in the wings or behind the tiara.
Pear, pear, hear the strings declare,
A diva can wait, but she cannot sing bare.

[Verse 2]
The bass searched every pocket; the tenor checked the throne.
A stagehand found a peach and claimed the pear had changed its tone.
The conductor cued a storm; the curtains shook with fright;
The pear stayed on the music stand, concealed behind a light.

[Chorus]
Pear, pear, answer my aria,
Hide in the wings or behind the tiara.
Pear, pear, hear the strings declare,
A diva can wait, but she cannot sing bare.

[Bridge]
Luma raised her chin and aimed a high note at the fruit;
The pear vibrated off the stand and rolled beneath her boot.
She stopped the grand crescendo, bent, and picked it up with care;
The orchestra held its breath; she split the pear to share.

[Final Chorus]
Pear, pear, now the aria's done,
The chorus gets a slice and the bass gets one.
Pear, pear, the strings can rest,
The snack has reached the cast; the diva sings her best.

[Ending]
She sang the encore to the rind; it could not call her flat.
The chorus took the peaches home, concealed inside each hat.
