# Cumbia with the Moving Boxes

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cumbia rhythm, accordion and guacharaca patterns, and a moving crew that keeps dancing the boxes to the wrong room*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Cumbia accordion and guacharaca with a steady walking pulse; box moves should follow the rhythm but reach the correct room.*

Verse 1
The moving crew arrived at noon with boxes by the door,
A capuchin began a cumbia on the kitchen floor.
The label said "bedroom," but the dancers turned around,
They carried every box upstairs and set it on the ground.

Chorus
Slide to the left, carry it right,
Cumbia keeps the boxes light.
Follow the label, follow the tune,
Don't put the kitchen in the bedroom room.

Verse 2
The accordion kept playing as the crew moved through the hall,
A macaque carried pillows and a lamp against the wall.
The guacharaca scratched a pattern, the dancers took a turn,
They placed the couch beside the stove and gave the couch a burn.

Chorus
Slide to the left, carry it right,
Cumbia keeps the boxes light.
Follow the label, follow the tune,
Don't put the kitchen in the bedroom room.

Bridge
The leader read the labels out and formed a clear parade,
The bedroom boxes went upstairs; the kitchen things were laid.
They stopped the band before the last crate rolled away,
The stove was safe, the couch was dry, the lamps could gently sway.

Verse 3
They danced the final boxes to their proper room at last,
The mattress reached the bedroom and the kitchen table passed.
The mover signed the list, the band played one refrain,
The owner opened every box and found the plates unstrained.

Chorus
Slide to the left, carry it right,
Cumbia keeps the boxes light.
Follow the label, follow the tune,
Don't put the kitchen in the bedroom room.

Ending
The accordion closed its bellows; every box was right,
The movers danced outside the door and rolled away at night.
