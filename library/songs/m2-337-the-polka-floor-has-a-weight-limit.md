# The Polka Floor Has a Weight Limit

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: unstoppable polka party songs meeting the limits of a tiny hired dance floor*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Accordion, tuba and brisk two-step drums; reduce the band one instrument at a time.*

[Verse 1]
The village hired a dancing floor and placed it in the square,  
The sign said twenty dancers and the band was counted there.  
Our tuba took the corner and our drummer took the back,  
The accordionist spread his arms and filled the central crack.  
The mayor brought his cousins, thirty cousins came to dance,  
The floor attendant held a rope and gave them half a chance.  

[Chorus]
Step together, step apart, step outside the line,  
There's a place beside the tuba if your cousin will resign.  
Round we go and round we come, make the rafters ring,  
Wait beside the ticket desk until we call you in.  
Heel and toe and heel and toe, squeeze another tune,  
The floor is full of music and the dancers can't get room.  

[Verse 2]
We sent the drummer off the floor; two couples took his space,  
The tuba moved beside the bins and played with noble grace.  
Our accordionist kept the count while standing on a crate,  
The dancers filled the wooden square and shouted it was great.  
The floor attendant weighed the crate and shook his little book,  
The mayor said the crate was free; the attendant took a look.  

[Chorus]
Step together, step apart, step outside the line,  
There's a place beside the tuba if your cousin will resign.  
Round we go and round we come, make the rafters ring,  
Wait beside the ticket desk until we call you in.  
Heel and toe and heel and toe, squeeze another tune,  
The floor is full of music and the dancers can't get room.  

[Bridge]
The drummer started dancing on the empty cobbled street,  
The tuba player followed with his music and his feet.  
The cousins left the hired floor and formed a giant ring,  
The attendant bounced upon the boards to hear them spring.  

[Final Chorus]
Step together, step apart, step outside the line,  
Everybody's on the street and everybody's fine.  
Round we go and round we come, let the tuba sing,  
The mayor dances past the rope and takes the band in swing.  
Heel and toe and heel and toe, squeeze another tune,  
The attendant charges twenty coins to store our coats till noon.
