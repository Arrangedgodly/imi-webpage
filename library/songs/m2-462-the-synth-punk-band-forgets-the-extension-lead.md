# The Synth-Punk Band Forgets the Extension Lead

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: synth-punk manifestos about independence and performers whose entire musical rebellion depends on reaching an electrical socket*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Sharp synthesizer riff, frantic drums and shouted vocals; begin unaccompanied until the extension lead reaches the stage.*

[Verse 1]
We stood upon a warehouse stage and shouted, "We are free!"  
The keyboard had no power but the crowd could not yet see.  
The socket on the far wall lay ten meters from our stand,  
We'd left the long extension lead inside the drummer's van.  
The drummer had arrived by bus; his van was back at home,  
Our manifesto rang across the warehouse all alone.  
The singer clapped an angry beat and called it what we'd planned,  
The owner saw the unplugged lead and raised a helpful hand.  

[Chorus]
No strings, no chains, no master to obey,  
There is one small plug that must reach ten meters away.  
No strings, no chains, shout the whole thing true,  
The independent keyboard needs a little help from you.  
Free sound, bright spark, give the night a name,  
Our revolution has a socket somewhere off the stage.  

[Verse 2]
The owner brought a tested lead from underneath the bar,  
The cable reached the keyboard without moving it too far.  
He routed it beside the wall and plugged the instrument in,  
The little screen lit up at last and showed a hopeful grin.  
We played the opening riff beneath the singer's final shout,  
The crowd approved the music once the waiting had run out.  
The owner held the spare lead's box against his working chest,  
The bassist yelled his thank-you in the middle of the rest.  

[Chorus]
No strings, no chains, no master to obey,  
There is one small plug that must reach ten meters away.  
No strings, no chains, shout the whole thing true,  
The independent keyboard needs a little help from you.  
Free sound, bright spark, give the night a name,  
Our revolution has a socket somewhere off the stage.  

[Bridge]
We packed the borrowed cable with a note to give it back,  
The drummer added our own lead to tomorrow's packing stack.  
The singer wrote a second verse admitting what we'd seen,  
The owner said that honesty could make a decent scene.  

[Final Chorus]
No strings, no chains, no master to obey,  
We have brought the proper cable and the owner gets his spare.  
Free sound, bright spark, give the night a name,  
We check the plug before we claim our stage.  
No strings, no chains, shout the whole thing true,  
The owner gets a guest list spot; he brings an adapter too.
