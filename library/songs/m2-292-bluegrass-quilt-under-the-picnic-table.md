# Bluegrass Quilt Under the Picnic Table

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bluegrass breakneck picking, instrumental trading, and the assumption that every household object can become a fiddle*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Fast banjo and fiddle trade breaks; the quilt guild keeps interrupting to rescue the stitching from the jam.*

**Verse 1**
The quilt club sets its squares beneath a maple's shade.  
A bluegrass band arrives and asks if anyone can play.  
Aunt Lila taps the thimble; the banjo answers back.  
The fiddle takes a turn and sends the needle down the track.  
The seam runs crooked through the checkered cloth and past the pie.  
The picker calls it syncopation; Lila says, "That's why."  

**Chorus**
Pick it quick, stitch it straight, don't let the pattern run;  
Five-string, four-square, the quilting's not yet done.  
The bow goes high, the thread goes low, the needle skips the beat;  
The fastest song in county fair has squares beneath its feet.  

**Verse 2**
The banjo player trades a break with one old washboard hand.  
The thimble taps a counter-rhythm that no one else has planned.  
A patchwork star gets loose and sails above the pie.  
The bass player catches it before it reaches sky.  
The fiddle scratches one long note; the quilt begins to shake.  
The club pins down the corners, saying, "Mind the backing, Jake."  

**Chorus**
Pick it quick, stitch it straight, don't let the pattern run;  
Five-string, four-square, the quilting's not yet done.  
The bow goes high, the thread goes low, the needle skips the beat;  
The fastest song in county fair has squares beneath its feet.  

**Bridge**
The band drops to a walk; the quilter ties the seam.  
The star returns to center; the cloth lies flat and clean.  
The banjo gives one plucked note; the needle follows through.  
The quilt can hold the picnic while the band picks something new.  

**Ending**
They fold the finished quilt and lay it on the table.  
The band plays soft enough to keep the stitches stable.  
The star sits square; the pie stays put; the thimble takes a bow.  
The fastest break is over, and the quilt is sleeping now.
