# The Electro-Swing Baker Spins the Cake

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: electro-swing cooking videos whose flashy tempo and vintage flourishes outrun the actual decorating technique*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Swinging sampled-style horns over a modern beat, using original melodies; the cake turntable spins faster each time the beat returns.*

[Verse 1]
I put a cake on a turntable and I raised the piping bag,  
The video needed vintage charm and quite a little swag.  
I spun the stand in gentle time and made a frosting ring,  
The beat came in much harder; I began to overswing.  
The cake rotated quickly while my hand stayed in one place,  
The icing made a spiral that ran clear across its face.  
My sister held the camera and she asked if that was planned,  
I said it was a modern rose beneath a vintage hand.  

[Chorus]
Spin that cake, swing that cream,  
The frosting has a melody beyond the baker's dream.  
Snap that beat, pipe that line,  
I have put three dozen roses where the name should stand and shine.  
Spin that cake, hold it still,  
The birthday guest would like to read the name beneath the frill.  

[Verse 2]
I turned the music quieter and stopped the spinning tray,  
My sister said the cake looked like a very foamy bay.  
I scraped the extra frosting off and left a tidy top,  
The stand stood still beneath my hands; the horns had finally stopped.  
I piped the guest's short first name in a little careful arc,  
My sister filmed the lettering without a moving part.  
The camera caught the contrast and we kept both takes in view,  
The birthday guest said, "Good. The final one is meant for you."  

[Chorus]
Spin that cake, swing that cream,  
The frosting has a melody beyond the baker's dream.  
Snap that beat, pipe that line,  
I have put three dozen roses where the name should stand and shine.  
Spin that cake, hold it still,  
The birthday guest would like to read the name beneath the frill.  

[Bridge]
We served the cake that evening with the first take on the screen,  
The guests laughed at the spiral and enjoyed the tidy cream.  
The video gained a title saying Rhythm Cannot Bake,  
My sister ate the scrapings while I handed out the cake.  

[Final Chorus]
Spin that cake, swing that cream,  
The cake is cut in slices and the turntable is clean.  
Snap that beat, pipe that line,  
The guest has found his name intact upon a slice this time.  
Spin that cake, hold it still,  
The next video is dancing; there is no dessert to spill.
