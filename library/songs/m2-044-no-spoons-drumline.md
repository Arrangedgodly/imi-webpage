# No Spoons in the Drumline

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: marching-band halftime pomp and precision formations under chaotic field conditions*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Snare-led marching groove with brass stabs; let the formation gradually turn into a kitchen parade.*

Verse 1
The band captain calls the count; we lift the shining brass,  
A troop of macaques marches on the dew-beaded grass.  
Our cymbal player spins too wide and clips the lemonade,  
The drum major signals left; the tuba turns to shade.  
We make a perfect arrow, then a rectangle, then a pan,  
The judges mark our footwork while the ants inspect the stand.  

Chorus
Left, right, left, keep the silver line,  
Step, step, step, make the corners fine.  
Hold that note and mind the sideline,  
No spoons in the drumline, no soup on the twine.  
March through the puddle, march through the steam,  
The halftime show is not a canteen.  
Left, right, left, keep the silver line,  
No spoons in the drumline, no soup on the twine.  

Verse 2
At break the cooks bring stew for us in buckets painted gold,  
Our snare drums sound like lids that someone forgot to hold.  
The drum major shouts, "Maintain the ranks!" The soup begins to slosh,  
A bass drum catches one full ladle with a gentle squash.  
The line now smells like pepper while we spell the school mascot,  
The letters read "Please stir the pot" instead of what we thought.  

Bridge
We turn the brass toward the kitchen, march a measured route,  
The cooks inspect our marching form and send the ladle out.  
We return the bowl untouched, in perfect parade array,  
The band is fed at halftime, but the drums have gone away.  

Chorus
Left, right, left, keep the silver line,  
Step, step, step, make the corners fine.  
Hold that note and mind the sideline,  
No spoons in the drumline, no soup on the twine.  
March through the puddle, march through the steam,  
The halftime show is not a canteen.  
Left, right, left, keep the silver line,  
No spoons in the drumline, no soup on the twine.  

Ending
We make our final formation, a ladle shaped like brass,  
The captain takes a bow; the cooks bring bowls to pass.
