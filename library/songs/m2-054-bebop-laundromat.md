# The Bebop Laundromat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bebop scat improvisation and virtuoso solos that outrun the room's practical rhythm*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Upright bass, brushed kit, and nimble piano; scat syllables should follow the spinning washers, then stop at the end-of-cycle chime.*

Verse 1
The washer turns in fours and fives, the dryer swings in two,  
A lemur snaps a rhythm on a basket painted blue.  
The trumpet takes a slippery run that circles round the room,  
The socks all tumble countertime and march into the gloom.  
A capuchin begins to scat, "Bop-biddy, spin that sleeve,"  
The owner says, "That's not my sock," and checks it at the seam.  

Chorus
Bap-a-doo, spin it slow,  
Zip-a-zap, where'd the pair go?  
Ting-a-ling, the cycle's done,  
Fold your clothes before the sun.  
Bop-bop-biddy, trade that tone,  
Don't take somebody's underclothes.  
Bap-a-doo, make the dryer swing,  
The lint trap wants a solo thing.  

Verse 2
The pianist plays a perfect chord; the dryer answers loud,  
The trumpet improvises over every spinning cloud.  
The manager arrives and asks us all to clear the floor,  
A sock has found the ceiling fan and landed by the door.  
We trace its path through soap and lint, a jazz detective crew,  
The sock points to machine number nine; its pocket holds a shoe.  

Bridge
The timer chimes, the band cuts off, the room becomes serene,  
A pair emerges from the drum, the finest note we've seen.  
We fold in double-time, the bassist plucks a final thread,  
The manager says, "The show is done; the wash is all dry, red."  

Chorus
Bap-a-doo, spin it slow,  
Zip-a-zap, where'd the pair go?  
Ting-a-ling, the cycle's done,  
Fold your clothes before the sun.  
Bop-bop-biddy, trade that tone,  
Don't take somebody's underclothes.  
Bap-a-doo, make the dryer swing,  
The lint trap wants a solo thing.  

Ending
The band packs up its brushes; the manager checks the load,  
The trumpet plays one soft note and waits beside the code.
