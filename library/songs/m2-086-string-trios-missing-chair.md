# The String Trio's Missing Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: chamber-music elegance, hushed recital manners, and a cellist who keeps adding chairs to improve acoustics*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Delicate string trio with precise rests; squeaking chairs should become the comic counter-rhythm.*

Verse 1
Three monkeys set their music on the stage beneath a lamp,
A violinist tuned her strings beside a quiet camp. 
The cellist found one chair and said, "The room sounds rather bare,"
He dragged another from the wings and placed it by the stair.

Chorus
Hold the bow and mind the beat,
Let the quiet frame the feet.
One soft note can fill the room,
If the chairs don't join the tune.

Verse 2
The first chair gave a little squeak beneath the cellist's weight,
The violist heard a perfect pitch and marked it on the slate.
They played a phrase of silver notes; the chair replied in C,
The cellist added two more seats to build a harmony.

Chorus
Hold the bow and mind the beat,
Let the quiet frame the feet.
One soft note can fill the room,
If the chairs don't join the tune.

Bridge
The audience sat so still that no one dared to cough,
The chairs began to answer as the players shifted off.
The cellist moved a stool again; the stage grew crowded fast,
The violinist played one note and the back row joined at last.

Verse 3
They lined the chairs in one long row and bowed to every squeak,
The trio played a final chord, restrained and mild and meek.
The cellist left the chairs in place; they creaked without a hand,
The quietest recital in the grove became a one-chair band.

Chorus
Hold the bow and mind the beat,
Let the quiet frame the feet.
One soft note can fill the room,
If the chairs don't join the tune.

Ending
The musicians packed their bows and left the seats in neat array,
The chairs played one last creaking phrase after they walked away.
