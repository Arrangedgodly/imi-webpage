# The Calypso Singer Saves an Umbrella Seat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sunny calypso party songs interrupted by a beach chair reservation made for a person who never arrives*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bright percussion and bouncing call-and-response; the backing singers repeatedly ask who the reserved chair is for.*

[Verse 1]
I put a towel on a chair beneath the biggest shade,  
My cousin said he'd join me when his little ferry came.  
The beach began to fill with people looking for a seat,  
I told them it was taken by a cousin they would meet.  
At ten his message said the boat was late leaving the pier,  
At twelve he sent a photograph of lunch he ate back there.  
The chair remained quite empty as the afternoon went by,  
A child put her sand bucket there; I shook my head and sighed.  

[Chorus]
Who sits there? My cousin does,  
Though he hasn't reached the beach or caught the ferry across.  
Who sits there? Save the shade,  
There is a towel having quite a lovely holiday.  
Sun rolls high, drum rolls low,  
The towel has enjoyed the chair much longer than I know.  

[Verse 2]
The cousin called at half past two; his ferry had been missed,  
He said he'd take the evening boat and join us with his gifts.  
The family with the sand bucket asked to use the empty place,  
I moved the towel beside me and I gave them all the shade.  
Their child built a little fort around the umbrella pole,  
The mother shared some mango and the father brought a bowl.  
My cousin sent another note; he'd chosen home instead,  
I sent a picture of the chair with strangers there in red.  

[Chorus]
Who sits there? My cousin does,  
Though he hasn't reached the beach or caught the ferry across.  
Who sits there? Save the shade,  
There is a towel having quite a lovely holiday.  
Sun rolls high, drum rolls low,  
The towel has enjoyed the chair much longer than I know.  

[Bridge]
The family stayed for music as the sun began to set,  
The little fort survived the tide as far as it could get.  
My cousin asked if I would save the chair another day,  
I said the towel was booked already; he'd have to find his way.  

[Final Chorus]
Who sits there? A family does,  
They have brought a bowl of mango and a little sandy rug.  
Who sits there? Anyone who came,  
The towel is resting by my feet and doesn't need a claim.  
Sun rolls high, drum rolls low,  
My cousin has a ferry ticket framed at home to show.
