# The Operatic Detective Questions the Coat Stand

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operatic interrogation scenes and a detective who confuses a silhouette with a witness*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Low strings and interrogatory bass aria; the stage manager answers in a brisk recitative before the final refrain.*

[Verse 1]
I entered in the lamplight with a notebook in my hand,  
A figure wore a long dark coat beside the witness stand.  
I asked him where the diamond went before the clock struck nine,  
He offered not a syllable in answer to my line.  
I circled him with solemn steps and sang my accusation,  
His silence seemed a perfect sign of practiced calculation.  
The stage manager passed behind and moved his wooden feet,  
My witness was a coat stand dressed to fill the seat.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Tell me where the jewel has gone,  
Speak before the house lights dawn.  
Who was standing in the hall?  
Who saw shadows on the wall?  
Tell me, figure wrapped in gray,  
Why you keep your words away.  
Every question fills the air,  
This witness only holds what other people choose to wear.  

[Verse 2]
The manager removed the coat and hung it in the wings,  
She found the little diamond sewn inside its lining seams.  
It was a painted glass effect the wardrobe worker kept,  
She'd stitched it in the pocket where the actor's fingers swept.  
I wrote the finding carefully and asked who owned the coat,  
The wardrobe worker showed the cast list with a little note.  
The actual witness entered and prepared to sing his part,  
I asked about the jewel again with all my detective's art.  

[Aria / Refrain]
Tell me where the jewel has gone,  
Speak before the house lights dawn.  
Who was standing in the hall?  
Who saw shadows on the wall?  
Tell me, figure wrapped in gray,  
Why you keep your words away.  
Every question fills the air,  
This witness only holds what other people choose to wear.  

[Bridge]
The witness sang his answer in a bright and lengthy phrase,  
He'd seen the thief at seven in the setting's painted haze.  
I made a note of seven and dismissed him with a bow,  
The manager brought back the stand to hold my coat for now.  

[Final Aria / Refrain]
Tell me where the jewel has gone,  
Wardrobe keeps it until dawn.  
Who was standing in the hall?  
Just the coat stand by the wall.  
Every answer has its place,  
The proper witness has a face.  
I hang my coat and find a pen,  
The manager says, "Please don't take its evidence again."
