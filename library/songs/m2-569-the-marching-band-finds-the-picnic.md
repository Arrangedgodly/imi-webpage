# The Marching Band Finds the Picnic

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: marching-band ceremonial music and musicians arriving at a gathering that requires no march*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Brass melody begins on the move; snare stops when the band reaches the tables, with soft stationary playing thereafter.*

[Verse 1]
The marching band arrived beneath a banner for the park,  
We'd been engaged to play at noon and leave before the dark.  
Our captain wore his tallest plume and brought the polished mace,  
He lined us up beside the gate and asked us to keep pace.  
The picnic host came running with a basket in her hand,  
She'd booked a little brass set, not a moving marching band.  
The tuba took a step toward chairs around a serving tray,  
The host held up the basket and she waved the step away.  

[Chorus]
Left, right, hold the line,  
The tables have the sandwiches and do not need a sign.  
Left, right, stop your feet,  
The picnic has no parade route between its things to eat.  
Brass up, snare down, keep the tune in view,  
The host has put a shaded place beside the tables too.  

[Verse 2]
We stopped upon the grass before the tuba met a chair,  
The captain checked the booking and found "stationary" there.  
The host showed us the shaded spot beside the garden wall,  
We set the cases on the ground and brought the volume small.  
The drummer used his brushes while the horns took gentler breath,  
The guests could hear each other as the chairs around us met.  
The captain kept his plume but laid the mace beside his seat,  
He used his hand to give the cue and kept his resting feet.  

[Chorus]
Left, right, hold the line,  
The tables have the sandwiches and do not need a sign.  
Left, right, stop your feet,  
The picnic has no parade route between its things to eat.  
Brass up, snare down, keep the tune in view,  
The host has put a shaded place beside the tables too.  

[Bridge]
The children asked the captain for a march across the lawn,  
The host checked out a clear patch where the tables had not gone.  
We played one short procession after lunch was cleared away,  
The captain led a little loop and stopped beside the tray.  

[Final Chorus]
Left, right, let the little horns meet,  
The lawn has got a loop that doesn't cross the place to eat.  
Brass up, snare down, keep the tune in view,  
The picnic host has saved a plate for every player too.  
Left, right, end the line,  
The captain lifts his plume to fit beneath the serving sign.
