# The Bolero Singer Shares a Leaking Umbrella

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tender bolero duets whose promise of complete shelter meets a romantic prop with a small hole*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Soft guitar, rounded percussion and intimate alternating vocals; one falling drop punctuates each extravagant promise.*

[Verse 1]
I met you by the fountain as the evening rain began,  
I opened my umbrella with the confidence of a man.  
I said I'd keep the whole world from disturbing your repose,  
A single drop fell through the cloth and landed on your nose.  
You looked up at the little hole above the handle's line,  
I moved the canopy so that the raindrop became mine.  
I sang of endless shelter as it dripped behind my ear,  
You smiled and took my elbow; you could see the whole thing clear.  

[Chorus]
Stay with me, hear the rain,  
I can offer half an umbrella and a gentle little refrain.  
Stay with me, keep your hand,  
The weather has a narrow route through what I thought I'd planned.  
Soft night, warm voice, let the little drops fall,  
I am promising the heavens with a roof that's rather small.  

[Verse 2]
You took the handle from me and you turned the cloth around,  
The hole lay over neither head but dripped onto the ground.  
We walked together quietly beneath the steadier shade,  
I sang a less ambitious line about the thing we'd made.  
We reached a little café with a window by the street,  
The owner took the dripping umbrella as we chose a seat.  
He hung it open by the door; we watched the hole in the light,  
My endless shelter had a patch to find another night.  

[Chorus]
Stay with me, hear the rain,  
I can offer half an umbrella and a gentle little refrain.  
Stay with me, keep your hand,  
The weather has a narrow route through what I thought I'd planned.  
Soft night, warm voice, let the little drops fall,  
I am promising the heavens with a roof that's rather small.  

[Bridge]
You patched it with a little heart before we met again,  
Its seam survived a second walk beneath a softer rain.  
I asked if it meant love and you replied without a pause,  
"It means the cloth is mended. The hand-holding is yours."  

[Final Chorus]
Stay with me, hear the rain,  
The little heart above our heads has stopped the dripping refrain.  
Soft night, warm voice, let the little drops fall,  
The patched cloth does the roofing; you are smiling through it all.  
Stay with me, keep your hand,  
I have a smaller promise now that I can understand.
