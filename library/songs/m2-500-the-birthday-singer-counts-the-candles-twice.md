# The Birthday Singer Counts the Candles Twice

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: birthday performance traditions and singers who turn a simple candle count into an unwanted public announcement of age*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Cheery piano and group vocals; the candle count is sung as an exaggerated roll call rather than any familiar birthday melody.*

[Verse 1]
We brought a cake for Grandma at the family afternoon,  
Her grandson played a little tune upon a wooden spoon.  
The candles had been chosen from a box beside the sink,  
We set them out around the icing's carefully written ink.  
I counted every little flame to make the chorus true,  
I reached twenty-seven and I didn't know what I should do.  
Grandma said she'd picked a handful, not a number for her age,  
The cake was not a record book displayed upon a stage.  

[Chorus]
One bright flame for a lovely day,  
Two bright flames for the things we'll say.  
Three, four, five, let the counting be,  
The candles have a number that tells no history.  
Lift your voice, let the wishes grow,  
Grandma keeps her birthday and the candles keep their glow.  

[Verse 2]
I stopped the count at twenty-seven while the little spoon played,  
Grandma blew the candles out before the final phrase was made.  
We took them out together and we cut the waiting cake,  
She gave the grandson a big slice for the music he could make.  
He asked what number should be written in his little birthday book,  
She said the date was plenty for the record that he took.  
He wrote the date and drew the cake with every flame in view,  
The picture had twenty-seven, though he told her none were new.  

[Chorus]
One bright flame for a lovely day,  
Two bright flames for the things we'll say.  
Three, four, five, let the counting be,  
The candles have a number that tells no history.  
Lift your voice, let the wishes grow,  
Grandma keeps her birthday and the candles keep their glow.  

[Bridge]
She put the little drawing on the kitchen's sunny wall,  
The grandson signed his name beneath the cake and candles all.  
She asked him if he'd draw the cake for every future year,  
He promised one big candle so the picture would be clear.  

[Final Chorus]
One bright flame for a lovely day,  
One big candle and a spoon to play.  
Lift your voice, let the wishes grow,  
Grandma keeps her birthday and the cake's number isn't known.  
Three, four, five, let the counting be,  
The grandson counts the slices left and keeps one more for me.
