# The Chamber Singer Reads the Vacuum Manual

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: art-song recitals transforming every kind of printed text into profound lyrical poetry*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Delicate piano and poised solo voice; the manual's warnings and model number are delivered with great emotional weight.*

[Verse 1]
I chose a found text for the recital at the little hall,  
A booklet by the vacuum box had answered poetry's call.  
It spoke of filters, empty bins and keeping channels clear,  
I set it to a melody about the passing year.  
The pianist played the opening as I raised the booklet high,  
I sang "Remove the filter" with a tender little sigh.  
The audience leaned forward for the heartache in the phrase,  
The cleaner in the back row recognized the printed page.  

[Refrain]
Empty what is full,  
Replace what has been worn.  
Keep the little pathway clear,  
Begin again each morn.  
Disconnect before you clean,  
The deepest line the singer's seen.  

[Verse 2]
The second verse explained the way the hose should meet the head,  
The piano made an aching turn beneath the words I read.  
I sang the model number with a long and yearning note,  
The cleaner checked her booklet for the number that I wrote.  
Her vacuum had the same one; she had lost the middle page,  
She waited till the interval then asked beside the stage.  
I lent her my source booklet and she copied what she lacked,  
The piano waited quietly until the text came back.  

[Refrain]
Empty what is full,  
Replace what has been worn.  
Keep the little pathway clear,  
Begin again each morn.  
Disconnect before you clean,  
The deepest line the singer's seen.  

[Bridge]
We sang the final warning about storage in a dry place,  
The audience applauded all the sorrow in my face.  
The cleaner had the copy and a new bag in her hand,  
She said the warning helped her get the filter back as planned.  

[Final Refrain]
Empty what is full,  
Replace what has been worn.  
Keep the little pathway clear,  
Begin again each morn.  
Disconnect before you clean,  
The booklet has a use beyond the arts-and-music scene.  

[Coda]
The cleaner heard the concert and remembered every line,  
She hummed the filter warning while she worked at half past nine.  
I brought the booklet home and found a new source by the door,  
The kettle's little leaflet has a chorus and much more.
