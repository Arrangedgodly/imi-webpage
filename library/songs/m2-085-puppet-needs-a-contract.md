# The Puppet Needs a Contract

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: novelty-song theater, puppet duets, and a hand puppet demanding equal billing from its monkey performer*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Vaudeville piano and quick duet; the puppet interrupts from inside a canvas bag.*

Verse 1
A macaque brought a puppet to the talent-show stage,
The puppet wore a paper crown and asked to see the page.
The host announced a solo act; the puppet said, "Not fair!
My voice is on the program, but my name is nowhere there."

Chorus
Put my name above the fold,
Give me half the story told.
I can bow and I can sing,
I can pull my own small string.

Verse 2
The monkey made it dance a jig; the puppet stopped mid-hop,
It asked for a dressing room and one more snack on top.
The stagehand offered one banana; the puppet took a bite,
Then blamed the monkey's left hand for the missing candlelight.

Chorus
Put my name above the fold,
Give me half the story told.
I can bow and I can sing,
I can pull my own small string.

Bridge
The singer dropped the puppet; it landed in a hat,
The puppet popped back and said, "I planned the fall like that!"
The audience began to clap; the puppet took a bow,
Then asked the band to hold the chord because it made a vow.

Verse 3
They shared the billing evenly, the monkey and the sock,
The puppet took the final verse and sang about the clock.
The monkey worked the strings in time; the puppet led the tune,
The judge awarded them a dressing-room balloon.

Chorus
Put my name above the fold,
Give me half the story told.
I can bow and I can sing,
I can pull my own small string.

Ending
The curtain closed; the puppet stayed to negotiate its pay,
The monkey left with half the song and all the strings to pay.
