# Limerick at the Lunch Table

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: pub-song singalong conventions, limerick verses, and a table of monkeys that cannot agree on the final rhyme*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Pub piano and group claps; each verse invites a new rhyme, but the lunch table should resolve the dispute.*

Verse 1
A capuchin sat down with a pie,
And asked the whole table, "But why?"
The macaque said, "Wait,
We'll rhyme after eight,"
As the pie made a run for the sky.

Chorus
Pass the plate, keep the beat,
Find a rhyme and save your seat.
Sing the line and let it land,
Limericks go hand in hand.

Verse 2
A gibbon brought pickles to share,
But couldn't decide where to pair.
The baboon said, "The jar,"
The lemur said, "Far,"
And the pickles rolled under a chair.

Chorus
Pass the plate, keep the beat,
Find a rhyme and save your seat.
Sing the line and let it land,
Limericks go hand in hand.

Bridge
They argued about chair and pear,
The waiter said, "Neither is there."
A spoon hit the floor,
The pickles rolled more,
And a rhyme arrived under a chair.

Verse 3
They found all the pickles at last,
The pie was already well past.
The rhyme fit the plate,
The lunch ran till eight,
And the table sang loudly and fast.


Verse 4
The waiter cut the pie in four, the plates went round the room,
The pickles came from under chairs and filled a little spoon.
The rhyme was not a masterpiece, but lunch was served on time,
The table kept the clapping while the forks supplied the chime.

Chorus
Pass the plate, keep the beat,
Find a rhyme and save your seat.
Sing the line and let it land,
Limericks go hand in hand.

Ending
They sang through the meal till the dishes were done,
The last rhyme was done, and the table said, "Fun!"
