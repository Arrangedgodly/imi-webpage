# Honky-Tonk on the Piano Stool

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: honky-tonk piano runs, saloon singalong, and a piano stool that spins faster than the player can finish a verse*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Bright honky-tonk piano with a two-step shuffle; stool spins on each turnaround.*

Verse 1
A macaque took the piano stool and planted both his feet,
He played a rolling melody that bounced along the street.
The stool began to spin beneath his striped and checked-up vest,
He played a run of notes while facing east and west.

Chorus
Roll the keys and turn around,
Keep that stool from leaving town.
Two-step left, then face the bar,
Spin your song but stay where you are.

Verse 2
The gibbon at the counter called for one more tune,
The player hit a high note, then the stool spun toward the moon.
The melody went sideways, the bass went out of line,
The bartender kept on pouring to the piano's time.

Chorus
Roll the keys and turn around,
Keep that stool from leaving town.
Two-step left, then face the bar,
Spin your song but stay where you are.

Bridge
A capuchin reached to steady it, but got spun as well,
The two of them kept turning while the keys began to swell.
The player changed the rhythm to a steady little march,
The stool rolled to a stop beside the doorway arch.

Verse 3
They tightened up the stool and set the piano straight,
The player finished all the notes he'd saved until too late.
The crowd stood up and two-stepped till the morning light,
The stool stayed fixed; the piano played the final right.

Chorus
Roll the keys and turn around,
Keep that stool from leaving town.
Two-step left, then face the bar,
Spin your song but stay where you are.

Ending
The stool was bolted to the floor; the pianist played one more,
The bar spun round, the patrons stayed, and danced beside the door.
