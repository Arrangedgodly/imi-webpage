# The Torch Singer Sings to a Desk Lamp

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: torch-song lighting and heartbreak grand enough to demand a spotlight in a small rented room*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Slow piano and close voice; the final refrain grows warmer without increasing the lamp brightness.*

[Verse 1]
I booked a little room to sing a ballad of goodbye,  
The room came with a table and a lamp with light quite high.  
I dimmed the overhead and turned the desk lamp toward my face,  
It lit one eye with golden fire and left the rest in space.  
The piano player asked me if I needed her to start,  
I said the light must reach the depths of my abandoned heart.  
She moved the lamp a little so its shade stood in the way,  
The golden beam fell on the score; my heart was left in gray.  

[Refrain]
Hold the light, hold the night,  
Let the lonely window give my empty room its sight.  
Hold the light, let it glow,  
I have got a sorrow that the lampshade doesn't know.  
One heart, one chair, one song,  
The bulb has got a limit but the longing can be long.  

[Verse 2]
We put the lamp back on the desk to light the piano page,  
I stood beside the window and I called the small room stage.  
The player gave the opening; I sang the first goodbye,  
A listener near the back could see my face beneath the sky.  
The room had space for twenty, and the twenty took their seats,  
The piano held the melody; the rain supplied the beats.  
I sang the final lonely line without a spotlight cue,  
The little lamp stayed on the score and saw the player through.  

[Refrain]
Hold the light, hold the night,  
Let the lonely window give my empty room its sight.  
Hold the light, let it glow,  
I have got a sorrow that the lampshade doesn't know.  
One heart, one chair, one song,  
The bulb has got a limit but the longing can be long.  

[Bridge]
The owner asked if we would sing another evening there,  
I said the room was right for us and brought my favorite chair.  
The player packed her pages with a small clip for the rain,  
We booked the hour before the dark to use the light again.  

[Final Refrain]
Hold the light, let it glow,  
The lamp can light the music while I sing the words I know.  
One heart, one chair, one song,  
The window gives an evening that the crowd can sing along.  
Hold the night, let it stay,  
The owner turns the overhead back on when we go away.
