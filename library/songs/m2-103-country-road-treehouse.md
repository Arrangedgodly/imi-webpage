# Country Road to the Treehouse

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country ballad conventions, homesick road imagery, and a traveler who keeps passing the same treehouse turnoff*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Acoustic guitar, pedal steel, and a gentle train-like brush; the chorus should sound heartfelt even as the route repeats.*

Verse 1
I packed my little suitcase and I left the grove at dawn,
I followed every dusty road till all the signs were gone.
I passed a bent old mailbox and a crooked willow tree,
Then found the same old treehouse looking down at me.

Chorus
That road keeps winding home again,
Past the branch and through the glen.
I can ride a mile or two,
The treehouse waits in every view.

Verse 2
I asked a traveling langur if the highway ran out west,
He pointed to the orchard and said, "That route is best."
I rode past three old signposts, then a creek that crossed the track,
The treehouse sat beside the road with my forgotten hat.

Chorus
That road keeps winding home again,
Past the branch and through the glen.
I can ride a mile or two,
The treehouse waits in every view.

Bridge
The map was folded upside down; the compass needle spun,
A squirrel had a peanut stored beneath the northern one.
I turned the map the proper way; the road became quite clear,
The path I'd called a lonely trip had circled home from here.

Verse 3
I parked beside the old treehouse and hung my coat to dry,
The mailbox held a note from home: "Your supper's on the side."
The road was still a road, but it no longer felt unknown,
I walked the final dozen steps and found my way back home.

Chorus
That road keeps winding home again,
Past the branch and through the glen.
I can ride a mile or two,
The treehouse waits in every view.

Ending
I left my suitcase by the door, the compass on the shelf,
The squirrel took the peanut and the road went on itself.
