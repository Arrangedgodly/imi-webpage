# Second-Line Stumble at the Street Corner

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: New Orleans second-line parade traditions and sideline dancers who join the procession without knowing its route*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Brass-band swing and tambourine; keep the lead procession steady while the second line keeps arriving from the wrong streets.*

**Verse 1**
The brass band rounds the corner with a banner held up high.  
A monkey named Jojo shakes a tambourine nearby.  
The first line walks in measured steps, the second line swings wide.  
A pair of tap shoes joins them from the laundromat outside.  
They follow every trumpet turn, then reach a four-way street.  
The leader points toward the square; the dancers miss the beat.  

**Chorus**
Hey now, step light, let the brass roll through;  
The band knows the route, the dancers do too.  
Hey now, shake bright, keep the banner in sight;  
The second line grows at every corner tonight.  

**Verse 2**
A baker joins with flour shoes; a mail bird takes the lead.  
A roller-skate quartet rolls past, pursuing someone else's beat.  
Jojo waves the banner left; the trumpet points to right.  
The whole parade divides itself and circles out of sight.  
The drum major stops the tune and asks for one clear sign.  
Jojo raises both his hands; the dancers form a line.  

**Chorus**
Hey now, step light, let the brass roll through;  
The band knows the route, the dancers do too.  
Hey now, shake bright, keep the banner in sight;  
The second line grows at every corner tonight.  

**Bridge**
The band turns back toward the square; the extra feet fall in.  
The baker wipes his shoes; the mail bird folds a wing.  
The skaters slow to walking; the tap shoes find the two.  
Jojo calls, "Follow the banner, not the trumpet's shoe."  

**Ending**
They reach the square together; the brass cuts off on time.  
The second line keeps dancing, making one last crooked line.  
The leader bows; Jojo's tambourine rolls through the gate.  
The band lets it ring once more, then calls the parade complete.
