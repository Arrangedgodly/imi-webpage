# Holiday Retail Roundup

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Holiday retail carols and relentless seasonal advertising*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Jingly bells over a mall loudspeaker; let the exhausted cashier keep perfect pitch.*

[Verse 1]  
The lights went up in August, the snow machine is on,  
The mall's brass monkey quartet has sung since half past dawn.  
A wreath is on the checkout, a bow is on the till,  
The manager has asked us to look festive, still and still.  
They ask if one will do. The cashier points above:  
"The deal is shaped like a wreath. You must complete the loop of love."  

[Chorus]  
Ring the bell, scan the sale, let the checkout chime.  
Wrap a gift, wrap a fee, wrap it just in time.  
Joy is on the schedule; joy is on the shelf.  
Take a number, take a bow, then gift-wrap yourself.  

[Verse 2]  
The store's toy drum is playing though nobody is near,  
A speaker says, "Your perfect season starts right here."  
The monkeys stack their purchases in a wobbly tower,  
The tower asks for premium tape and same-day shipping power.  
A little one brings mittens to the customer return,  
The sign says, "Gently used, but seasonal. No return."  
The supervisor whispers, "Keep the holiday alive."  
The cashier adds a candy cane and clocks out at five-five.  

[Chorus]  
Ring the bell, scan the sale, let the checkout chime.  
Wrap a gift, wrap a fee, wrap it just in time.  
Joy is on the schedule; joy is on the shelf.  
Take a number, take a bow, then gift-wrap yourself.  

[Bridge]  
Outside, the snow is cardboard, taped to every pane.  
The delivery cart is sliding through a puddle in the lane.  
The store-bought wreath has shed its plastic leaves.  
The monkeys make a real one out of fallen sleeves.  

[Final Chorus]  
Ring the bell, scan the sale, now the store is closed.  
Set the label gun aside; let your shoulders decompress.  
Joy is off the schedule; joy can take a seat.  
Share one scarf between you. Keep your own two feet.  

[Outro]  
The brass band packs its instruments. The snow machine is done.  
The cashier splits a cinnamon bun with everyone.
