# The Sunshine Pop Band Performs in the Fog

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sunshine pop harmonies and unshakably bright performers who cannot see their own audience*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Bright guitar, tambourine and layered harmony; a harbor foghorn adds a low note to each sunny refrain.*

[Verse 1]
We booked the harbor stage to sing about a perfect day,  
The posters showed the sunshine on the silver waterway.  
A bank of fog came drifting in and covered every seat,  
We saw the stage and little else beneath our cheerful feet.  
The singer called a bright hello to everybody there,  
A foghorn gave a long response from out beyond the square.  
The drummer thought the crowd had found a very low new cheer,  
The harbor master told us that a ship was drawing near.  

[Chorus]
Sunny, sunny, let the bright day show,  
The fog has put a curtain where the audience ought to go.  
Sunny, sunny, let the warm notes fly,  
The harbor has a bass voice underneath the hidden sky.  
Clap your hands, show your smile,  
We cannot see a single face beyond the first small aisle.  

[Verse 2]
The harbor master moved the concert to the covered hall,  
The audience followed ushers past the fog along the wall.  
We carried all the instruments and set them on the floor,  
The band played bright harmonies beside the open door.  
The ship horn sounded faintly as the vessel left the bay,  
The audience gave a warmer cheer to set it on its way.  
We sang about the sunlight from a room with cloudy panes,  
The drummer kept the chorus bright despite the weather's gains.  

[Chorus]
Sunny, sunny, let the bright day show,  
The hall has put a roof above the foggy harbor flow.  
Sunny, sunny, let the warm notes fly,  
The horn is fading softly as the ship goes drifting by.  
Clap your hands, show your smile,  
We can see the front row from the little stage-side aisle.  

[Bridge]
The show finished indoors as the fog began to thin,  
We saw the posters outside and the sunny world within.  
The harbor master asked us for a song about the haze,  
We wrote a bright new melody for less than perfect days.  

[Final Chorus]
Sunny, sunny, let the warm notes fly,  
The hall has got a roof beneath the clouded harbor sky.  
Clap your hands, show your smile,  
The ushers have a shorter route than the foggy harbor mile.  
Sunny, sunny, let the bright day show,  
The ship horn gets a guest part where our lowest singers go.
