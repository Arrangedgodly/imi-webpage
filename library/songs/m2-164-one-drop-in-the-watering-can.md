# One Drop in the Watering Can

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: reggae one-drop rhythm, relaxed repetition, and a gardener whose calm refrain cannot stop the hose from dancing*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Warm bass and a one-drop drum on beat three; let the hose pulse between each line.*

Verse 1
A tamarin sat easy by the garden wall,
A watering can was empty in the sun.
He turned the hose to fill it, calm and small,
The pressure made it wiggle, then it spun.

Chorus
One drop, one drop, let it fall,
Keep the roots cool by the wall.
One drop, one drop, take it slow,
Even loose hoses have a flow.

Verse 2
The hose began to circle round the beans,
The gardener moved his chair but would not rush.
A gibbon kept the hose between the greens,
A macaque caught the spray beneath a bush.

Chorus
One drop, one drop, let it fall,
Keep the roots cool by the wall.
One drop, one drop, take it slow,
Even loose hoses have a flow.

Bridge
The water can was filling as the chorus rolled,
The hose made a small wave against the gate.
The singer said, "We'll let the garden hold,"
But the beans leaned over from the weight.

Verse 3
They looped the hose around a wooden stake,
Then turned the tap to half its former stream.
The can was full; the plants got what they'd take,
The garden stayed afloat inside the dream.

Chorus
One drop, one drop, let it fall,
Keep the roots cool by the wall.
One drop, one drop, take it slow,
Even loose hoses have a flow.

Ending
The gardener sat down with the can beside his knee,
The hose stayed tied; the beans began to dance in time.
