# Forecast for Two

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: smooth jazz lounge standards and weather as romantic shorthand*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
The lounge singer watched the barometer fall.  
He wore a blue suit and no socks at all.  
Outside, the rain leaned hard on the glass.  
Inside, two strangers waited for it to pass.  
She held an umbrella with a broken rib.  
He offered his coat and a ginger soda fizz.  
The piano laid back in a minor key.  
The forecast said clouds through half past three.  
The singer was a lemur, smooth and sly.  
His tail kept time when the roof leaked sky.  

## Chorus
There's a chance of weather by the door.  
There's a chance we'll ask for more.  
Keep the kettle low, the lights down blue.  
The night may clear for me and you.  

## Verse 2
The waitress brought a lemon in a bowl.  
The singer told a story without a goal.  
The umbrella opened, hit the ceiling fan.  
It spun three times, then chose a plan.  
The stranger laughed and caught it by the handle.  
The candle shook; the wax began to dangle.  
They shared a napkin, wrote the storm's path.  
The map looked more like a math class graph.  
The lemur crooned above their table edge.  
One drooping ear brushed someone's wedge.  

## Chorus
There's a chance of weather by the door.  
There's a chance we'll ask for more.  
Keep the kettle low, the lights down blue.  
The night may clear for me and you.  

## Bridge
A gust arrived and rattled every pane.  
The piano player stopped to check the drain.  
The singer slid a stool across the floor.  
The strangers moved a bucket by the door.  
They caught the leak and saved the set.  
The waitress brought the check, still wet.  
The rain eased off, then changed its mind.  
They left together, umbrellas intertwined.  

## Final chorus
There's a chance of weather; let it pour.  
There's a chance we've found a door.  
Keep the kettle low, the lights soft blue.  
The storm can walk with me and you.  
There's a chance the clouds will split.  
There's a chance we'll laugh at it.  
The street is wet, the night is new.  
I'll share the umbrella, if you do.  

## Ending
The singer finished with a breath.  
The rain applauded from the steps.  
Two shadows crossed beneath one hood.  
The weather, for once, was good.  

*Performance note: Keep the band relaxed and unhurried, with room for the umbrella gag to interrupt the mood.*
