# The Power-Pop Band Adopts a Stage Cat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sparkling power-pop love songs and bands that mistake a venue cat's ordinary demands for enthusiastic fandom*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bright guitars, brisk drums and layered harmonies; a cat's impatient meow answers each declaration of devotion.*

[Verse 1]
She sat upon the amplifier when we came to play,  
A little ginger venue cat who'd slept there through the day.  
The singer said she'd chosen him and touched his hand to heart,  
The cat looked past his shoulder toward a bag beside the bar.  
He sang the opening number with a special verse for her,  
She didn't move a whisker and she didn't even purr.  
The bartender shook a food bag and she leaped down from the stack,  
The singer called it dancing and invited her right back.  

[Chorus]
Stay with me, ginger girl,  
You're the only little listener who can brighten up my world.  
Sing with me, make it true,  
Every time you meow I think the chorus reaches you.  
Stay with me, keep your place,  
The bartender has kibble and I'm losing every race.  

[Verse 2]
She finished up her supper and returned beside the stand,  
The singer stretched a softer note and slowly raised his hand.  
She rubbed against his trouser leg and looked toward the door,  
He said her love was growing and he sang another four.  
The bartender came over and unlatched the little gate,  
The cat went to the courtyard and refused to hear the break.  
He dedicated one more song to love that wanders free,  
She came back when the bartender poured water by his knee.  

[Chorus]
Stay with me, ginger girl,  
You're the only little listener who can brighten up my world.  
Sing with me, make it true,  
Every time you meow I think the chorus reaches you.  
Stay with me, keep your place,  
The bartender has kibble and I'm losing every race.  

[Bridge]
The band packed up at midnight, but the singer stayed behind,  
He asked if she would travel; she was sleeping by the wine.  
The bartender said, "She lives here. You can visit when you play."  
He booked a monthly residency and called it their first date.  

[Final Chorus]
Stay with me, ginger girl,  
The venue has a monthly band who thinks it is your world.  
Sing with me, make it true,  
The poster has your photograph and half the crowd comes for you.  
Stay with me, keep your place,  
You sleep through every chorus with your paw across your face.
