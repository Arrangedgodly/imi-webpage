# Twelve-Tone Tea Cups

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: twelve-tone modern classical music, strict note rows, and a composer who refuses to let a kettle resolve*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Use a twelve-note row with no repeated pitch before the full sequence; kettle whistles join as percussion.*

Verse 1
The lemur set the teacups in a grid upon the tray,
Each cup received a number so the notes would not repeat.
The capuchin composer wrote a row and bowed away,
Then tapped the spoon against the saucer on the beat.

Chorus
One through twelve, then start anew,
Every note must travel through.
No one pitch can lead the line,
Even kettle steam keeps time.

Verse 2
The kettle gave a whistle; the composer said, "Too neat."
He lowered every burner, then reheated it in time.
The twelve cups answered with a careful, clinking beat,
A gibbon set the sugar bowl to ring a thirteenth chime.

Chorus
One through twelve, then start anew,
Every note must travel through.
No one pitch can lead the line,
Even kettle steam keeps time.

Bridge
The tea was ready; guests arrived and asked for something warm,
The composer said, "The sequence has not reached the end."
The kettle clicked and stopped; the kitchen lost its charm,
The cups stayed full of tea, each waiting for a friend.

Verse 3
A macaque drank the first cup, then another, then the rest,
The composer chased the sequence as the guests began to sip.
The cups all disappeared; the row was put to test,
He wrote the drinking sounds down on the edge of every lip.

Chorus
One through twelve, then start anew,
Every note must travel through.
No one pitch can lead the line,
Even kettle steam keeps time.

Ending
The guests went home refreshed; the score still had one page,
The kettle made one final note and joined the tea-room rage.
