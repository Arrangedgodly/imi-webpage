# The Ballad of the Borrowed Bell

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: folk ballad conventions, narrative verses, and a village bell that changes hands with every messenger*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Acoustic guitar and fiddle with a story-led vocal; each verse passes the bell to a new keeper.*

Verse 1
A macaque borrowed the village bell to call the market home,
He rang it from the orchard so the farmers would not roam.
A gibbon heard the ringing and came running down the hill,
The bell was handed to him while the market stood still.

Chorus
Ring, borrowed bell, tell the tale,
Pass it down the forest trail.
Every hand will lend an ear,
Bring the waiting neighbors near.

Verse 2
The gibbon carried water when he heard a distant call,
He passed the bell to a young baboon who had to reach the hall.
The baboon rang it once to ask who needed bread,
A lemur took the bell and walked where hungry neighbors fed.

Chorus
Ring, borrowed bell, tell the tale,
Pass it down the forest trail.
Every hand will lend an ear,
Bring the waiting neighbors near.

Bridge
The bell went up the hillside and across the creek,
It rang for every helper, but its clapper turned too weak.
The bell was brought to the blacksmith, who repaired its little pin,
He gave it back to the village so its voice could ring again.

Verse 3
The market closed at sunset; the bell came home at last,
The keeper placed it by the gate and tied it to the post.
No messenger was needed; every neighbor knew the way,
They met beside the gate and shared the bread that day.

Chorus
Ring, borrowed bell, tell the tale,
Pass it down the forest trail.
Every hand will lend an ear,
Bring the waiting neighbors near.

Ending
The bell hung still at evening, its journey gently done,
The village heard the quiet and went home beneath the sun.
