# The Festival Drop Is Still Loading

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: EDM festival builds that promise a massive drop after endless risers and countdowns*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Layer synth risers and kick pulses through each verse, then cut to a tiny comic sound at the promised drop.*

[Verse 1]
The DJ raised one hand above the festival crowd,  
A macaque leaned toward speakers; the bass began to pound.  
The screens displayed a countdown from a hundred down to one,  
The lights all flashed in patterns like a second rising sun.  
The synth climbed higher, higher, through the lasers and the haze,  
The singer cried, "Get ready!" through the fog and purple rays.  
At zero came a silence, then a phone began to ring,  
The DJ said, "One moment," and restarted everything.  

[Chorus]
Build it up, let the riser climb,  
The drop is coming any time.  
Count it down, then count once more,  
The bass is knocking at the door.  
Build it up, the lights ignite,  
We may drop before the end of night.  

[Verse 2]
The drummer hit a kick roll, the synth went up again,  
The crowd held both their hands up, then lowered them at ten.  
The DJ shouted "Ready?" and the screen reset to five,  
A gibbon found the loading bar still barely half alive.  
The bassline pulsed in fragments, the lights began to blink,  
A capuchin said, "Is this the drop?" and took a drink.  
The DJ spun a filter, sent the siren higher still,  
The stagehand held a note that said, "The cable needs a plug."  

[Chorus]
Build it up, let the riser climb,  
The drop is coming any time.  
Count it down, then count once more,  
The bass is knocking at the door.  
Build it up, the lights ignite,  
We may drop before the end of night.  

[Bridge]
The technician found the power strip and pressed it with a toe,  
The giant wall of synths went quiet; the crowd began to go.  
The DJ tapped the microphone; it made a single pop,  
The audience yelled "Drop!" and made the ceiling hop.  

[Final Chorus]
Build it down, the set is through,  
The DJ's laptop needs a reboot too.  
Count it down from four to one,  
The tiny pop was still the loudest one.  
Build it down, let speakers rest,  
The crowd can make the drop the best.  

[Ending]
The loading bar reached ninety-nine; the festival closed at ten.
