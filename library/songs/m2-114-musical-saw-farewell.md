# The Musical Saw's Farewell

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical-saw novelty performances, wavering glissando, and a repair crew trying to tune a saw without cutting the melody short*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Musical saw and soft piano; the saw's sliding pitch should sound wistful until the repair crew interrupts.*

Verse 1
A langur held a saw beneath the lamp's warm glow,
She bent the blade and drew the bow; a wavering note would flow.
The audience leaned forward as the melody climbed high,
A carpenter appeared and asked if she would cut a pie.

Chorus
Sing, silver saw, across the room,
Let the sliding notes make room.
Bend the blade and let it glide,
Keep the carpenter outside.

Verse 2
The carpenter brought lumber; the saw sang out a tune,
He thought the note was warning him that rain would come at noon.
He checked the roof, then checked the door, then checked the garden shed,
The saw slid down a minor third and he inspected the bed.

Chorus
Sing, silver saw, across the room,
Let the sliding notes make room.
Bend the blade and let it glide,
Keep the carpenter outside.

Bridge
The performer played a high note; the carpenter took a bow,
He thought the wavering sound meant tools were needed now.
She said the saw was singing, not asking for a cut,
He set the lumber down and listened with his mouth shut.

Verse 3
The song climbed up the scale and settled on a sigh,
The carpenter returned the bow and waved a soft goodbye.
The saw went in its case; the piano closed the tune,
The carpenter wrote "music" on the box and left at noon.

Chorus
Sing, silver saw, across the room,
Let the sliding notes make room.
Bend the blade and let it glide,
Keep the carpenter outside.

Ending
The saw played one last wavering note, then rested in the case,
The carpenter heard it in the shed and came back at a pace.
