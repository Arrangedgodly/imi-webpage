# A Canon for the Closing Library

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Renaissance vocal canon, interlocking entrances, and a librarian trying to close while every singer re-enters the refrain*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Four voices enter one by one in a canon; use quiet footsteps and page turns as countersubjects.*

Verse 1
The library bell rang softly at the ending of the day,
A macaque sang the closing verse; the first voice led away.
A gibbon entered on the line, then capuchin joined in,
The librarian began to close, but heard the song begin.

Chorus
Turn the page and pass it on,
One voice starts as one is gone.
Close the book and hum the tune,
Library doors will shut at noon.

Verse 2
The bass began the subject as the tenor took the door,
The alto sang a rising line and crossed the reading floor.
The librarian said, "Closing time," the canon answered, "Time,"
The chorus folded every warning into one more rhyme.

Chorus
Turn the page and pass it on,
One voice starts as one is gone.
Close the book and hum the tune,
Library doors will shut at noon.

Bridge
The last reader stacked the volumes, the singers held a note,
The librarian placed each book inside its proper coat.
She closed the window, dimmed the lamps, and joined the final part,
The chorus wound down neatly, like a clock within the heart.

Verse 3
The final voice descended and the doors were locked at last,
The books were safe upon their shelves, the closing hour had passed.
The librarian held the key and hummed the subject low,
The canon stayed behind the door and did not follow home.

Chorus
Turn the page and pass it on,
One voice starts as one is gone.
Close the book and hum the tune,
Library doors will shut at noon.

Ending
The singers left in order, each with one last quiet line,
The librarian turned the sign to closed and finished just in time.
