# Marina After Hours

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: yacht rock polish and soft-focus marina romance*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Silky electric piano and close harmonies, with a gull interrupting the bridge.*

## Verse 1
At Blue Heron Marina, dusk reflected in the chrome.  
A slow loris in white linen said he felt at home.  
His boat was called The Maybe, docked beside a skiff.  
He poured a glass of ginger ale and watched the tide shift.  
A gull stood on the bow, assessing his attire.  
The radio played a Rhodes chord smooth enough to hire.  
A neighboring sailor tuned a six-string by the rail.  
Their voices slipped together like a polished sail.  

## Chorus
Sail past the breakwater, easy and slow.  
The harbor lights are putting on a show.  
Sail past the breakwater, no grand design.  
The best part of the evening is the unhurried line.  

## Verse 2
His date arrived on foot, with sneakers in her hand.  
She'd missed the bus and walked the shoreline sand.  
He offered her the wheel; she laughed and took the rope.  
The boat nudged gently sideways, then they found their scope.  
The gull stole a napkin, made a sharp retreat.  
The guitar player changed the key to match its feet.  
They tied up before midnight, never left the bay.  
The moon put silver buttons on the water's jacket gray.  

## Bridge
No mansion on the hillside, no champagne in the hold.  
Just two warm cans of soda and a tale retold.  
She said, "Your boat is slow." He said, "It suits my knees."  
They let the dock rope creak along with the breeze.  

## Chorus
Sail past the breakwater, easy and slow.  
The harbor lights are putting on a show.  
Sail past the breakwater, no grand design.  
The best part of the evening is the unhurried line.  

## Ending
The gull stayed on the bow when both walked home.  
It looked like it had claimed the Maybe as its own.
