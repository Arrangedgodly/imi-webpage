# The Doo-Wop Trio Meets the Satellite Delay

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: close-harmony doo-wop groups attempting a live duet across a connection that refuses to keep their timing together*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Three close voices, finger snaps and alternating remote responses; the returning voice enters a beat late until the arrangement becomes call-and-response.*

[Verse 1]
We sang in three-part harmony for years beside the square,  
Our tenor moved across the sea and wanted to stay there.  
His island used a satellite to send our old refrain,  
The baritone gave an opening and waited for his name.  
The tenor's first reply arrived a beat behind our line,  
We left a beat of silence so his answer fit the line.  
We started all three voices but the ending wouldn't meet,  
The distant tenor heard the snaps behind our steady beat.  

[Hook / Chorus]
Doo, little distance, hold the blue,  
The song has got a waiting place between our voice and you.  
Wop, little wire, let the echo stay,  
The tenor has a tiny trip to make along the way.  
Snap, snap, wait a beat,  
Three hearts have a chorus that the timing cannot meet.  

[Verse 2]
We changed the old arrangement to a question and reply,  
Two voices gave the question and the tenor answered high.  
We waited for his whole line before beginning ours,  
The chorus had a little space as wide as foreign stars.  
The singer in the local hall gave the crowd a little cue,  
They waited with us quietly for the tenor's line to come through.  
We finished in our separate turns and bowed at either end,  
The audience waved to the screen to thank our distant friend.  

[Hook / Chorus]
Doo, little distance, hold the blue,  
The song has got a waiting place between our voice and you.  
Wop, little wire, let the echo stay,  
The tenor has a tiny trip to make along the way.  
Snap, snap, wait a beat,  
Three hearts have a chorus that the timing cannot meet.  

[Bridge]
We recorded all three parts later, separate and in time,  
The baritone lined them up into the old familiar rhyme.  
The live arrangement stayed a question answered far away,  
The studio version held the voices close as any day.  

[Final Hook / Chorus]
Doo, little distance, let the echo stay,  
The tenor gets his own whole line before we have our say.  
Snap, snap, wait a beat,  
The audience enjoys the little space between our feet.  
Wop, little wire, let the blue return,  
His kettle takes a solo while we wait our singing turn.
