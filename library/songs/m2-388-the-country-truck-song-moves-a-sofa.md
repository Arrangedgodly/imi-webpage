# The Country Truck Song Moves a Sofa

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country songs about freedom, pickup trucks and open roads, interrupted by an ordinary furniture favor*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Steady acoustic strumming and pedal steel; give the driver's grand claims a weary backing vocal from the sofa owner.*

[Verse 1]
I polished up my pickup for a weekend on the road,  
The tank was full of freedom and the radio played low.  
My cousin called at seven with a favor, small and sweet,  
Could I carry one old sofa to her third-floor rented suite?  
I pictured just a loveseat and a short drive into town,  
She met me with a sectional that spread for miles around.  
We strapped its pieces to the bed and rolled toward the sun,  
My open-road adventure had a furniture store run.  

[Chorus]
Roll on, wheels, roll on slow,  
There's a sofa where my camping gear was meant to go.  
Wide sky, long road, country air,  
I can see it past the cushion of a rather bulky chair.  
Roll on, wheels, hear me sing,  
The freedom of the highway has a three-seat floral spring.  
Big dreams, small door, heavy load,  
I'm taking someone's living room along the open road.  

[Verse 2]
We reached her block at lunchtime and we found the lift was small,  
The longest sofa section couldn't fit inside at all.  
We took it up the stairwell with a neighbor at the end,  
At every turn my cousin cried, "Just tilt it round the bend."  
The fabric brushed my hat off and the springs complained in key,  
The neighbor said the lyrics were becoming clear to see.  
We reached the third-floor landing and we set it by her door,  
She said, "The old one needs a ride to the secondhand furniture store."  

[Chorus]
Roll on, wheels, roll on slow,  
There's a sofa where my camping gear was meant to go.  
Wide sky, long road, country air,  
I can see it past the cushion of a rather bulky chair.  
Roll on, wheels, hear me sing,  
The freedom of the highway has a three-seat floral spring.  
Big dreams, small door, heavy load,  
I'm taking someone's living room along the open road.  

[Bridge]
Her old one fit the elevator, so we rolled it down with ease,  
She gave me cold refreshments and a box of spare house keys.  
I sat upon the truck bed while the evening turned to gold,  
The sofa made a fine front seat for watching traffic roll.  

[Final Chorus]
Roll on, wheels, roll on slow,  
The neighbor wants a dining set delivered down the road.  
Wide sky, long road, country air,  
My cousin's telling everybody I can get things anywhere.  
Roll on, wheels, hear me sing,  
I bought a truck for freedom and became the moving king.  
Big dreams, small door, heavy load,  
My first tour has ten couches and a very short postcode.
