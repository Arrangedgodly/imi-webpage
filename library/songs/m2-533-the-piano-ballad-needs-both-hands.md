# The Piano Ballad Needs Both Hands

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: confessional piano ballads and singers attempting to drink their stage tea without giving up the big chord*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Slow piano and earnest vocal; the right hand leaves the keyboard whenever the singer attempts a sip.*

[Verse 1]
I wrote a piano ballad for the little evening stage,  
Two hands held all the heartache that I'd written on the page.  
A cup of tea stood waiting on a table by my knee,  
I reached to lift it gently while the left hand played for me.  
The right-hand part went missing from the swelling little tune,  
The left hand held a low note in the very empty room.  
I put the cup back on the table with the sip my fingers earned,  
The audience heard me pause to sip before the chord returned.  

[Refrain]
Hold the chord, hold the night,  
The cup requires a hand that has a melody to write.  
Hold the chord, let the warmth stay,  
The tea is getting colder while the right hand has to play.  
Low note, high note, keep the heartache true,  
There are two hands at the keyboard and one thing too much to do.  

[Verse 2]
I reached the quiet middle where the chords could take a rest,  
I lifted up the teacup at the point the score thought best.  
I took one small sip while the strings inside the piano rang,  
Then set it down and played the final verse before I sang.  
The melody rose warmly with the chords all in their place,  
The cup stayed on its table and the warmth stayed in my face.  
The audience applauded at the final little sound,  
I took a bow before I drank the tea that waited round.  

[Refrain]
Hold the chord, hold the night,  
The cup requires a hand that has a melody to write.  
Hold the chord, let the warmth stay,  
The tea is getting colder while the right hand has to play.  
Low note, high note, keep the heartache true,  
There are two hands at the keyboard and one thing too much to do.  

[Bridge]
I marked the quiet sipping point upon tomorrow's page,  
The host put a small bottle on the table by the stage.  
Its lid still needed fingers that were occupied below,  
I kept the teacup waiting for the same rest in the show.  

[Final Refrain]
Hold the chord, let the warmth stay,  
The score has got a rest for all the thirst that comes my way.  
Low note, high note, keep the heartache true,  
The audience now claps the sip as if the cup were in the crew.  
Hold the chord, hold the night,  
I give the cup a bow; it needs no hands to stand upright.
