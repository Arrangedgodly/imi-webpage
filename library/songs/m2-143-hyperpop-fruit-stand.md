# Hyperpop at the Fruit Stand

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: hyperpop's clipped edits, pitch-shifted hooks, and tempo jumps, applied to a fruit seller's ever-shortening price announcement*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Bright distorted synths, quick cuts, and elastic pitch shifts; keep the price hook understandable despite the jump edits.*

Verse 1
The fruit stand opened at the corner with a sign that read "One pear,"
The singer chopped the phrase in half and sent it through the air.
The beat snapped fast, the pitch shot up, the vendor called out "Two,"
The hook returned in tiny clips before the singer knew.

Chorus
Pear on sale, sale, sale,
Fresh and sweet, no need to fail.
Price goes up and down the scale,
Hear the hook and fill the pail.

Verse 2
The vendor changed the sign to "Three pears for a seed,"
The edits cut the number out, the shoppers couldn't read.
The singer pitched the chorus low and stretched the count to four,
The shoppers took one pear each and gathered by the door.

Chorus
Pear on sale, sale, sale,
Fresh and sweet, no need to fail.
Price goes up and down the scale,
Hear the hook and fill the pail.

Bridge
The producer froze the beat and let the seller speak,
The price was plain and easy, so the edit changed the week.
The shoppers filled their baskets; the sign stayed on the stand,
The singer kept the stutter hook but cut the number clean.

Verse 3
The fruit stand sold its pears by noon; the price was understood,
The singer made a new refrain that sounded clear and good.
The tempo jumped, the synths went bright, the hook returned once more,
The vendor packed the empty crates and closed the market door.

Chorus
Pear on sale, sale, sale,
Fresh and sweet, no need to fail.
Price goes up and down the scale,
Hear the hook and fill the pail.

Ending
The track went quiet, signs came down, the vendor swept the floor,
The singer saved the price in words, then made the beat roar.
