# Calypso for the Leaky Roof

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: calypso rhythms, steel-pan replies, and a cheerful roof-repair crew tracking rain through a house*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Steel pans, guitar, and light hand percussion; raindrop taps answer each sung line.*

Verse 1
The rain came down on Tavi's roof and tapped a lively beat,
A capuchin brought a bucket and a ladder from the street.
The steel-pan player heard the drip and joined the rainy song,
The roofer called, "That leak is small; it won't be here for long."

Chorus
Drip in the bucket, tap on the tin,
Patch up the roof before rain gets in.
Pan plays bright while the clouds roll by,
Keep the beat and keep things dry.

Verse 2
A drip appeared beside the stove, another by the chair,
The roofer moved the bucket; two new drops arrived elsewhere.
The singer pointed to the ceiling; the roofer climbed the stair,
The ladder squeaked a little tune and shook a drop of rain.

Chorus
Drip in the bucket, tap on the tin,
Patch up the roof before rain gets in.
Pan plays bright while the clouds roll by,
Keep the beat and keep things dry.

Bridge
They found a cracked leaf gutter where the roof met the tree,
The monkey sealed the seam with sap, then climbed down carefully.
The drummer moved each bucket back; the roof held through the shower,
The steel pans played a victory while the clouds lost power.

Verse 3
The sun came out, the buckets dried, the chair returned to place,
The roofer checked the rafters with a satisfied face.
The band played for the neighbors who had gathered in the lane,
The roof replied with one last drip, then kept out all the rain.

Chorus
Drip in the bucket, tap on the tin,
Patch up the roof before rain gets in.
Pan plays bright while the clouds roll by,
Keep the beat and keep things dry.

Ending
The buckets became drums for a dance beneath the sky,
The roof stayed quiet, and the rain went rolling by.
