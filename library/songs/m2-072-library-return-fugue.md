# Fugue for the Library Return Slot

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Baroque fugues, interweaving motifs, and the return of a musical subject at inconvenient moments*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Harpsichord and recorder trade the same short motif; each entrance should coincide with a different book being returned.*

Verse 1
A macaque brings a library book and drops it through the slot,  
The harpsichord begins a tune that sounds like what he sought.  
The recorder takes the subject, then the cello answers low,  
The title page comes spinning back: the book is overdue.  
He pays the fine, the clerk says, "Good," the subject enters twice,  
The violins repeat the word as if it were advice.  

Chorus
Return, return, the theme comes round,  
A book goes in, a tune is found.  
One voice starts and two reply,  
The library has harmony.  
Return, return, don't lose your place,  
The fugue is running at the desk.  
The last chord lands, the stamp comes down,  
The overdue book leaves the town.  

Verse 2
He tries to leave, the subject follows through the stacks,  
The flute says "return," the bass says "bring it back."  
He finds a second copy hidden under one small chair,  
The desk clerk checks the due-date and says, "That one isn't there."  
The motif climbs the staircase, then descends beside the door,  
The same refrain returns again, then enters once more.  

Bridge
The librarian stamps each page in time and keeps the voices neat,  
The monkey holds the returned book like sheet music in the beat.  
The canon reaches final cadence, silence fills the room,  
Then someone drops a travel guide and starts the fugue anew.  

Chorus
Return, return, the theme comes round,  
A book goes in, a tune is found.  
One voice starts and two reply,  
The library has harmony.  
Return, return, don't lose your place,  
The fugue is running at the desk.  
The last chord lands, the stamp comes down,  
The overdue book leaves the town.  

Ending
He steps outside with quiet shoes, the tune stays at the desk,  
The clerk returns the music stand and stamps his library vest.
