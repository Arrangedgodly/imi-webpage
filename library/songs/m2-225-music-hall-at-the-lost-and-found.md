# The Lost-and-Found Delight

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: British music-hall patter, comic audience asides, and a lost-and-found clerk who sings every item into its proper home*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use jaunty piano and brisk patter; pause for the audience response after each misplaced object.*

[Verse 1]
At the station desk, a lemur clerk arranged the things,
A glove, a lunch tin, two umbrellas, and three rubber rings.
He called them out in rhythm, then he looked into the crowd,
A capuchin said "Mine!" before the clerk had said it loud.

[Chorus]
Lost it, found it, bring it to the stage,
Every little object has a place to turn the page.
Lost it, found it, hand it to the right,
The clerk will sing your mitten into sight.

[Verse 2]
A macaque claimed the umbrella; it was not hers to take,
The clerk asked for its handle; she described a garden rake.
The audience all giggled as the clerk raised one eyebrow,
The umbrella had a rubber duck attached to it somehow.

[Chorus]
Lost it, found it, bring it to the stage,
Every little object has a place to turn the page.
Lost it, found it, hand it to the right,
The clerk will sing your mitten into sight.

[Bridge]
He found a little lunchbox and a hat that fit too wide,
A gibbon claimed them both, then found the hat was on his side.
The clerk sang out a final list, the crowd called each one true,
The last unclaimed umbrella opened up and rained on the crew.

[Final Chorus]
Lost it, found it, bring it to the stage,
Every little object has a place to turn the page.
Lost it, found it, hand it to the right,
The clerk will sing your mitten home tonight.

[Ending]
The clerk left with the three rings still rolling after him.
