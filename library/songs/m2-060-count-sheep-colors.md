# Count Your Sheep by Color

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: children's counting songs, cumulative verses, and cheerful instructional repetition*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Bright classroom piano with handbells; add one new animal each verse, then let the counting get delightfully out of control.*

Verse 1
One white sheep is by the gate, it wants to learn to count,  
It taps its hoof and looks at me as if I am the mount.  
I show it one and show it two, it nods with woolly pride,  
Then wanders past the chalkboard and brings three friends inside.  

Chorus
Count them up and count them down,  
One more sheep has come to town.  
Red, blue, yellow, green,  
Keep the chalk marks neat and clean.  
If the number grows too tall,  
Make a longer counting wall.  
Count them up and count them down,  
Now the sheep are all around.  

Verse 2
Two lambs arrive in raincoats, each carrying a bell,  
They ring on every number, and the teacher cannot tell.  
A goat joins in the lesson, says the answer should be four,  
The sheep all count the goat twice, then start the song once more.  

Bridge
The chalk runs out at twenty, the class begins to cheer,  
The teacher uses windowsills and counts the ones from here.  
The sheep line up by color, the goat sits by the door,  
The principal walks in and asks, "Why are there so many more?"  

Chorus
Count them up and count them down,  
One more sheep has come to town.  
Red, blue, yellow, green,  
Keep the chalk marks neat and clean.  
If the number grows too tall,  
Make a longer counting wall.  
Count them up and count them down,  
Now the sheep are all around.  

Ending
At bedtime every sheep is gone, the classroom quiet and clear,  
The teacher counts the chalkboard, then finds one goat still here.
