# The New-Wave Robot Wears a Cardigan

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: icy new-wave robot personas whose immaculate futuristic image conflicts with the venue's chilly backstage room*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Stiff synthesizer rhythm, clipped bass and cool deadpan vocals; soften the delivery whenever the cardigan is mentioned.*

[Verse 1]
I wore a silver suit to sing of circuits in the night,  
My movements were mechanical; my face reflected light.  
The dressing room was freezing and my silver sleeves were thin,  
The drummer brought a cardigan and helped me pull it on.  
It had two little pockets and a row of wooden hearts,  
I wore it to the soundcheck while I practiced all my parts.  
The photographer found me there and raised a little grin,  
The robot looked quite comfortable with wool around its skin.  

[Chorus]
Cold steel, warm wool, calculate the beat,  
The future has a cardigan to keep the circuits sweet.  
Bright eyes, soft sleeves, hear the steady clock,  
There is tea inside the pocket of this very gentle robot.  
Cold steel, warm wool, let the pattern run,  
My electronic heart has found a knitted little sun.  

[Verse 2]
I took it off before the show and faced the open floor,  
The wind came through the exit; I felt cold again once more.  
I reached behind the amplifier and brought the knitwear back,  
The audience saw the wooden hearts beside the silver track.  
I sang of distant planets with a pocket full of tea,  
The crowd began to sway instead of copying my rigid feet.  
The drummer said the robot had a very human sound,  
I told him warmth had given me a deeper kind of sound.  

[Chorus]
Cold steel, warm wool, calculate the beat,  
The future has a cardigan to keep the circuits sweet.  
Bright eyes, soft sleeves, hear the steady clock,  
There is tea inside the pocket of this very gentle robot.  
Cold steel, warm wool, let the pattern run,  
My electronic heart has found a knitted little sun.  

[Bridge]
The photographer used the wool shot for our sleeve next week,  
The fans wore knitted robots at the club along the creek.  
My aunt sent matching scarves for all the other band to wear,  
The future had a winter wardrobe waiting by the stair.  

[Final Chorus]
Cold steel, warm wool, calculate the beat,  
The synthesizer's operator has a blanket for his feet.  
Bright eyes, soft sleeves, hear the steady clock,  
The whole band takes its tea break; the robot warms its hands.  
Cold steel, warm wool, let the pattern run,  
The drummer wants a space suit; my aunt has just begun.
