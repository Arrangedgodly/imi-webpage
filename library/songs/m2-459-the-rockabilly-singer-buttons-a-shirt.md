# The Rockabilly Singer Buttons a Shirt

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: fast rockabilly swagger and wardrobe rituals undertaken at the same frantic pace as the music*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Slap bass, twangy guitar and a bouncing lead vocal; the final shirt-button correction takes one conspicuously slow bar.*

[Verse 1]
I put my checkered shirt on for the dance at half past eight,  
The band was in the van and I was running rather late.  
I buttoned from the middle while the record player spun,  
My right cuff met my elbow and the collar came undone.  
I looked into the mirror with my quiff arranged quite high,  
The shirt looked like a diagonal road beneath my shining tie.  
The bassist leaned inside the door and asked about the cut,  
I said it was the latest look; he pointed to my gut.  

[Chorus]
Button it up, rock it down,  
The collar has a slant that is the strangest one in town.  
Shake that comb, shine that shoe,  
The buttons have a rhythm that my fingers never knew.  
Button it up, give a little grin,  
I missed one buttonhole and pulled the whole shirt out of line.  

[Verse 2]
He said my lowest button had no matching place to go,  
I said the shirt was custom; he said, "Undo and start below."  
I took it all apart again and matched each little pair,  
The collar grew quite ordinary underneath my lofty hair.  
We reached the dance with minutes left to play our opening tune,  
The bassist said the tidy shirt had saved the whole costume.  
I sang about the crooked style I'd nearly brought that night,  
The dancers moved diagonally beneath the colored light.  

[Chorus]
Button it up, rock it down,  
The collar has a slant that is the strangest one in town.  
Shake that comb, shine that shoe,  
The buttons have a rhythm that my fingers never knew.  
Button it up, give a little grin,  
I missed one buttonhole and pulled the whole shirt out of line.  

[Bridge]
The photographer caught the dancers leaning left and right,  
My perfectly straight shirt looked out of place amid the sight.  
The bassist said the new dance was an accidental hit,  
I asked him for a zipper next time so the clothes would fit.  

[Final Chorus]
Button it up, rock it down,  
The dancers wear a crooked pose that is the latest in town.  
Shake that comb, shine that shoe,  
The bassist has a wardrobe checklist and it starts with button one.  
Button it up, give a little grin,  
I wear a plain white T-shirt now; the quiff still lets me in.
