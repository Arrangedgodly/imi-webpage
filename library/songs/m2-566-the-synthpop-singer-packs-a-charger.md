# The Synthpop Singer Packs a Charger

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: synthpop escape songs in which a glowing city calls more loudly than the battery warning*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Bright sequenced pulse; a low-battery tone interrupts only the first verse, and the live drummer carries the last chorus.*

[Verse 1]
I packed my silver jacket for a journey through the night,  
My little music player held a thousand songs of light.  
I left the charging cable on the table by the bed,  
The battery was nearly full; that filled my hopeful head.  
I sang across the station to a bright electronic beat,  
The player flashed a warning just as I approached my seat.  
The train pulled out; the warning gave a final little tone,  
The track stopped in the middle and I sang the rest alone.  

[Chorus]
Glowing city, call me through the glass,  
Every little signal has a flicker as we pass.  
Glowing city, hold the shining line,  
The songs are in the player but the power isn't mine.  
Silver jacket, midnight train,  
The drummer in my memory can keep the beat again.  

[Verse 2]
A drummer sitting opposite tapped softly on his knee,  
He'd heard the missing rhythm and he offered it to me.  
We sang a quiet chorus so the sleeping seats stayed still,  
He kept the little pulse alive by fingers and by will.  
At the city venue I asked to borrow a matching lead,  
The sound tech checked the player and supplied the kind I'd need.  
We charged it in the dressing room while testing drums and voice,  
The drummer joined the stage that night; the live beat was our choice.  

[Chorus]
Glowing city, call me through the glass,  
Every little signal has a flicker as we pass.  
Glowing city, hold the shining line,  
The songs are in the player but the power isn't mine.  
Silver jacket, midnight train,  
The drummer in my memory can keep the beat again.  

[Bridge]
I bought the proper cable for the journey home next day,  
I packed it in the player case before we went away.  
The drummer asked what happened if the train had no spare plug,  
I showed the full charge on the screen; he gave a little shrug.  

[Final Chorus]
Glowing city, hold the shining line,  
The player has its power and the drummer has his time.  
Silver jacket, morning train,  
We carry all our cables and we hear the track again.  
Glowing city, let the live beat stay,  
The drummer taps the table while the player takes the day.
