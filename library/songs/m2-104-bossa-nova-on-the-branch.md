# Bossa Nova on the Branch

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bossa nova, understated guitar syncopation, and a sleepy cafe that keeps mistaking rehearsal for closing time*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Soft guitar, brushed percussion, and a relaxed vocal; let the cafe's chair scrape answer the syncopation.*

Verse 1
The cafe in the treetops opened just before the rain,
A gibbon tuned a nylon string and played a gentle strain.
The waiter brought a cup of tea and set it on the ledge,
The chair scraped softly in the room, as if it crossed a bridge.

Chorus
Easy now, the leaves can sway,
Let the small notes drift away.
Sip the tea and tap your toe,
Branch cafe moves soft and slow.

Verse 2
A capuchin came in to practice for the evening show,
He asked the sleepy waiter if the band could play it low.
The waiter dimmed the lanterns and brought a blanket near,
The singer asked if closing time had already appeared.

Chorus
Easy now, the leaves can sway,
Let the small notes drift away.
Sip the tea and tap your toe,
Branch cafe moves soft and slow.

Bridge
The guitar played a tiny phrase; the spoon replied with chime,
The waiter leaned against the wall and nodded into time.
The band kept playing gently as the cafe drifted shut,
The door was open just enough for one last customer.

Verse 3
A squirrel arrived for tea and found the lights gone dim,
The waiter poured a cup and let the guitar welcome him.
The cafe woke from closing; the chairs scraped one by one,
The band played through the evening till the rain was done.

Chorus
Easy now, the leaves can sway,
Let the small notes drift away.
Sip the tea and tap your toe,
Branch cafe moves soft and slow.

Ending
They opened up the windows; the rain had rolled along,
The waiter served the last warm cup and hummed the gentle song.
