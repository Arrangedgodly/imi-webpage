# Spoken Word at the Bus Stop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: spoken-word poetry performance, deliberate pauses, and commuters who keep supplying practical footnotes to a metaphor*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Bare hand drum and close mic; give the pauses room, then let commuter replies cut through the image.*

Verse 1
I speak of a road as a ribbon of possibility,
A line that pulls the wandering heart toward its destiny.
A macaque checks the schedule and says the bus is late,
The metaphor keeps walking while we stand beside the gate.

Chorus
Hold the pause, let the word arrive,
Give the image room to breathe and thrive.
If the bus comes through the line,
We can ride and still be fine.

Verse 2
I call the stop a harbor where the tired ships can rest,
A gibbon with a lunch pail says the bench is on the west.
I call the rain a silver thread that stitches up the day,
A capuchin pulls out an umbrella and sends the thread away.

Chorus
Hold the pause, let the word arrive,
Give the image room to breathe and thrive.
If the bus comes through the line,
We can ride and still be fine.

Bridge
The poet makes a silence wide enough to hold the town,
The driver checks the mirror and asks who is getting down.
The crowd steps on; the ticket box begins its little song,
The spoken word continues as the road rolls them along.

Verse 3
I speak of windows turning every face into a star,
The macaque says, "That window's stuck; please move away from the bar."
The bus pulls from the curb; the rain moves down the glass,
The metaphor sits quietly and lets the people pass.

Chorus
Hold the pause, let the word arrive,
Give the image room to breathe and thrive.
If the bus comes through the line,
We can ride and still be fine.

Ending
The poem ends in motion, with the drum a gentle beat,
The driver calls the next stop; the line returns to street.
