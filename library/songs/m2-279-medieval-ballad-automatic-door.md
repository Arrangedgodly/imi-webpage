# The Medieval Ballad of the Automatic Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: medieval troubadour ballads with long narrative verses and a refrain at every turning point*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use a lute-like plucked pattern and a formal storytelling delivery; let the sensor door answer each refrain by opening too early.*

[Verse 1]
A traveler came at dawn unto the market's heavy gate,  
He carried one small basket and a letter from his mate.  
The gate was made of glass, with a sensor on its crown,  
It opened when the traveler was still a furlong down.  
The guard cried, "Hold, good traveler, the gate has swung too soon!"  
The lute played three small notes beneath the pale crescent moon.  
The traveler stepped forward, but the doors slid closed again,  
He bowed and tried to enter, then was turned away by rain.  

[Chorus]
Open, door, and let him through,  
The traveler has things to do.  
Close, door, then open wide,  
The sensor cannot choose a side.  
Open, door, now heed the song,  
The market gate has held him long.  

[Verse 2]
A squire approached behind him and the gate sprang open wide,  
The traveler stayed outside while the squire passed inside.  
The guard waved both his arms and triggered every sensor beam,  
The doors began to flutter like the wings within a dream.  
The traveler lifted up his basket and advanced with care,  
The doors slid shut before his head had passed the threshold there.  
A gibbon from the market found the manual in a chest,  
It said, "Clean the sensor window," and the guard began the test.  

[Chorus]
Open, door, and let him through,  
The traveler has things to do.  
Close, door, then open wide,  
The sensor cannot choose a side.  
Open, door, now heed the song,  
The market gate has held him long.  

[Bridge]
The guard wiped off the dust; the sensor saw the man,  
The traveler stepped ahead and crossed as all doors can.  
The basket made it through; the letter stayed secure,  
The market bell rang clearly for a passage now made sure.  

[Final Chorus]
Open, door, the way is clear,  
The traveler has arrived here.  
Close, door, when people pass,  
Don't open for the waving grass.  
Open, door, the song is through,  
The guard has found the cleaning cue.  

[Ending]
The troubadour went out; the door opened for the lute.
