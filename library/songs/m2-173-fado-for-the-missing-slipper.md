# Fado for the Missing Slipper

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: fado's mournful guitar, longing vocals, and destiny-heavy lament over a lost shoe that is merely on the next tram*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Portuguese guitar and restrained percussion; sing the lament with grand longing while the slipper rides nearby.*

Verse 1
The lemur stood beside the stop beneath the evening rain,
One slipper on her foot, one slipper missing again.
She sang of empty doorways, of roads that twist and turn,
The tram rolled past with one small shoe beneath the seat in front.

Chorus
Oh, slipper, where have you gone?
My lonely foot waits on.
Oh, slipper, answer me,
Return across the sea.

Verse 2
A capuchin played guitar and gave the night a sigh,
The chorus mourned the footwear as the tram rolled by.
The driver heard the singing and stopped along the square,
He found the missing slipper and held it in the air.

Chorus
Oh, slipper, where have you gone?
My lonely foot waits on.
Oh, slipper, answer me,
Return across the sea.

Bridge
The singer took the slipper and cradled it with care,
Then noticed it was left and the right one was still there.
The tram's next stop was closer than the stage of her despair,
She put the slipper on and walked home from the square.

Verse 3
She sang a softer chorus as the rain began to cease,
The guitar found a warmer chord, the tram rolled off in peace.
The missing shoe had traveled one block from its old place,
The singer bowed to destiny and thanked the driver's face.

Chorus
Oh, slipper, where have you gone?
My lonely foot waits on.
Oh, slipper, answer me,
Return across the sea.

Ending
The driver kept the fare; the singer kept the shoe,
The fado told a tragedy that ended with a "thank you."
