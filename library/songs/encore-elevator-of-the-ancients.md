# Elevator of the Ancients

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Progressive rock concept suites with shifting time signatures and mythic narration*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Begin in seven, add a flute, and let the elevator bell become the grand finale.*

[Movement I: The Call]  
The display shows a symbol shaped like a pear in flight.  
Our intern reads the manual by the emergency light.  

[Movement II: The Ascent]  
We climb one floor, then slide two down, the motor hums in seven.  
A voice says, "Please enjoy your journey to floor eleven."  
We have no eleventh floor. We have a basement and a roof.  
The cart contains the quarterly snacks, our final monkey proof.  

[Refrain]  
Rise, old elevator, carry us through the night.  
Open up at ground level and make the numbers right.  
We bring the fruit, we bring the files, we bring the courage to depart.  
The doors part wide, the bell sounds bright, we make a brand-new start.  

[Movement III: The Solo]  
A great brass key appears, delivered by a tail.  
Facilities says, "That key is for the archive, not the rail."  
Our drummer taps the handrail. Our singer takes a breath.  
The lift replies in three short dings, a prophecy of death.  

[Refrain]  
Rise, old elevator, carry us through the night.  
Open up at ground level and make the numbers right.  
We bring the fruit, we bring the files, we bring the courage to depart.  
The doors part wide, the bell sounds bright, we make a brand-new start.  

[Movement IV: Resolution]  
The intern finds the reset switch behind a faded sign.  
The cart rolls out. The doors unlock. The clock says half past nine.  
We leave one banana for the lift, a peace offering in gold.  
It drops one floor, returns the fruit, and asks to be consoled.  

[Final Refrain]  
Rise, old elevator, carry us through the night.  
Open up at ground level and make the numbers right.  
We bring the fruit, we bring the files, we bring the courage to depart.  
The doors part wide, the bell sounds bright, we make a brand-new start.  

[Outro]  
The lift waits by the lobby, its brass bell clean and clear.  
We take the stairs tomorrow. The lift can stay right here.
