# Vault Full of Acorns

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: trap rap conventions, heavy 808s, ad-lib boasts, and a capuchin flexing a winter stash like a luxury empire*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use a heavy kick, crisp hats, and playful ad-libs; let the brag collapse when the vault door sticks.*

[Verse 1]
Capuchin in the vault, with the acorns stacked like a throne,
Got a tail full of plans and a room of my own.
Fresh shell, clean shine, got the winter on lock,
When I turn that wheel, hear the whole hillside knock.

[Ad-lib]
(That's a lot of nuts!) (Who counted?) (Not me!)

[Chorus]
Vault full of acorns, I am rich till the snow,
Big stash, tall stack, got a warm place to go.
Vault full of acorns, let the whole forest know,
When the door sticks shut, where the harvest gonna go?


[Verse 2]
I flex on the branch with a walnut so rare,
Put a gold little latch on my premium lair.
Then the hinge went click and the handle went slack,
My whole winter fortune sitting inside, way in back.

[Ad-lib]
(You need a key?) (I got a song!) (That is not a key!)

[Chorus]
Vault full of acorns, I am rich till the snow,
Big stash, tall stack, got a warm place to go.
Vault full of acorns, let the whole forest know,
When the door sticks shut, where the harvest gonna go?


[Bridge]
The crew brought a crowbar; I said, "No, no, no."
We tapped out a rhythm till the hinges said go.
The acorns rolled out in a beat down the hill,
And every proud monkey got a pocket to fill.

[Final Chorus]
Vault full of acorns, I am rich till the snow,
Big stash, tall stack, now the neighbors all know.
Vault full of acorns, we can share as we go,
When the door sticks shut, call the crew with a crowbar.


[Ending]
The vault stays open; I charge rent to the monkeys.
