# The Research Ship Shanty

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sea shanties, call-and-response work songs, and romanticized deck labor*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Stomp-and-clap shanty with a mixed crew; the chorus should sound hearty until the paperwork response arrives.*

Verse 1
The research ship leaves harbor when the gulls are half asleep,  
We haul the nets for water samples and the charts we mean to keep.  
The captain calls, "Heave ho, me hearties," from the upper deck,  
The biologist calls back, "First, please rinse the sample check."  
A gibbon pulls the cable while a lemur guards the jars,  
The tide takes our clipboard and returns it to the stars.  

Chorus
Heave away, haul the line,  
Keep the bottles in a row.  
Heave away, mark the time,  
Write the water level low.  
One hand for ship and one for sea,  
One hand to sign the safety sheet.  
Heave away, haul the line,  
Then wash your boots before you eat.  

Verse 2
A wave sweeps all our charts away, the captain cries, "A test!"  
The mate says, "No, the table slid; let's tie it down the rest."  
We rope the papers to a bucket, tie the bucket to the mast,  
The bucket rolls in circles while our data holds at last.  
The shanty grows a verse about the knots we should have tied,  
The captain joins the chorus but is seasick on the side.  

Bridge
The samples reach the lab intact, the ship returns at dawn,  
The crew all sings the victory while the captain's boots are on.  
He asks us for a heroic tale of tempests, waves, and foam,  
We tell him how we labeled every jar before heading home.  

Chorus
Heave away, haul the line,  
Keep the bottles in a row.  
Heave away, mark the time,  
Write the water level low.  
One hand for ship and one for sea,  
One hand to sign the safety sheet.  
Heave away, haul the line,  
Then wash your boots before you eat.  

Ending
The captain gets a medal; the clerk gets every form,  
The crew sings soft while scrubbing decks and keeping samples warm.
