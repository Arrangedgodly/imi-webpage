# Power Metal at the Paint Shed

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: power-metal quest anthems, galloping drums, and an oversized ceremonial shield too heavy for the hero to lift*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Distorted guitars, galloping double-kick drums, and a huge chorus; make the shield lift feel heroic, then reveal a practical solution.*

Verse 1
A macaque wore a shining cape and held a wooden blade,
He sang of distant mountains and a prophecy mislaid.
The town's great shield was waiting in the paint shed by the wall,
He tried to lift it for the parade and could not move it at all.

Chorus
Raise the shield, sound the flame,
Climb the hill and shout your name.
Steel and thunder, bright and strong,
Lift together, sing along.

Verse 2
The drummer rolled like thunder; the guitars began to cry,
The singer leaned into the note and pointed at the sky.
A gibbon tried to lift the shield, a capuchin joined too,
The shield stayed planted in the shed and split their cape in two.

Chorus
Raise the shield, sound the flame,
Climb the hill and shout your name.
Steel and thunder, bright and strong,
Lift together, sing along.

Bridge
The blacksmith brought a cart with wheels and slid it underneath,
The hero held the handle while the crew pushed from beneath.
They rolled the shield to parade grounds, the band struck one big chord,
The hero stood before it as the loudest in the ward.

Verse 3
The shield was painted gold and strapped safely to the cart,
The parade moved down the lane with every monkey's heart.
The hero rode beside it, sword raised to the sun,
The crowd sang out the chorus when the long parade was done.

Chorus
Raise the shield, sound the flame,
Climb the hill and shout your name.
Steel and thunder, bright and strong,
Lift together, sing along.

Ending
The drums went quiet, shield stayed safe, the cart rolled back inside,
The hero kept the cape and wore a smaller shield with pride.
