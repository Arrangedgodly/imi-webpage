# The Dubstep Drop Jams the Printer

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bass-heavy dubstep drops that turn an ordinary office breakdown into the climax of a stadium performance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Rising synthesizer tension, chopped vocals and heavy bass; use the printer's paper rollers as the build and leave silence for its error beep.*

[Verse 1]
I sent the final flyer to the printer by the wall,  
The rollers started humming like the opening of it all.  
I heard a little clicking and I counted down from eight,  
The office gathered round me with their coffees on a tray.  
I turned the bass up louder for the sheets to tumble free,  
The printer swallowed paper and began to blink at me.  
My finger held the fader as the warning light turned red,  
I shouted, "Here it comes now!" and the rollers stopped instead.  

[Chorus]
Drop, drop, bring the paper down,  
Everybody's waiting for the biggest sheet in town.  
Build, build, let the office shake,  
The printer has a paper jam instead of a bass break.  
Drop, drop, give the crowd a sign,  
The error beep is louder than the masterpiece of mine.  

[Verse 2]
The receptionist unplugged it and she opened up the side,  
A folded sheet of flyers sat in rollers, packed quite tight.  
She pulled the paper gently while I sampled every sound,  
The tearing made a rhythm; I began to loop it round.  
She said to stop performing and collect the scattered pile,  
I gathered every flyer with a very humbled smile.  
She closed the tray and plugged it in; the motor started clean,  
I watched the sheets emerge without a bass drop in between.  

[Chorus]
Drop, drop, bring the paper down,  
Everybody's waiting for the biggest sheet in town.  
Build, build, let the office shake,  
The printer has a paper jam instead of a bass break.  
Drop, drop, give the crowd a sign,  
The error beep is louder than the masterpiece of mine.  

[Bridge]
That night I played the sample at a club beneath the stairs,  
The crowd went wild at every beep and threw their hands in air.  
The receptionist heard the track and asked about her fee,  
I paid her for the paper jam; she owns the drop with me.  

[Final Chorus]
Drop, drop, bring the paper down,  
The printer's touring digitally in every club in town.  
Build, build, let the office shake,  
The receptionist gets royalties for clearing my mistake.  
Drop, drop, let the rollers shine,  
It still prints every invoice with its backstage rider fine.
