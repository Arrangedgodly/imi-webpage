# Overtone at the Campfire

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: overtone throat-singing demonstrations, resonant low tones, and a campfire chorus that mistakes echoes for extra singers*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Low sustained vocal drones with clear overtone flutes; stage the echo as the campfire rocks answering back.*

Verse 1
The lemur sang a low note by the campfire's glow,
A high bright overtone appeared above the low.
A capuchin looked round to find the singer in the trees,
The only other sound was wind moving through the leaves.

Chorus
Low note, high note, carry through the night,
One voice rising into light.
Low note, high note, echo by the flame,
One breath, one singer, two tones have the same.

Verse 2
The gibbon joined the hum and matched the lower drone,
The capuchin sang higher, thinking she was alone.
Their tones began to weave around a kettle on the stone,
The kettle's metal rim rang back a third note of its own.

Chorus
Low note, high note, carry through the night,
One voice rising into light.
Low note, high note, echo by the flame,
One breath, one singer, two tones have the same.

Bridge
A macaque took the kettle off and set it in the grass,
The new note disappeared; the singers let it pass.
They tried to make it return by tapping on the pan,
But the kettle rang so loudly none could hear the plan.

Verse 3
They cooled the kettle slowly; the hum came back again,
The campfire made a soft reply against the mountain rain.
The singers held one low note; the bright tone danced above,
The echo was the kettle, not a ghost they had to love.

Chorus
Low note, high note, carry through the night,
One voice rising into light.
Low note, high note, echo by the flame,
One breath, one singer, two tones have the same.

Ending
They packed the pot at sunrise, the overtone remained,
The kettle hummed along until the campsite drained.
