# Trailer Score for the Lost-and-Found Umbrella

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cinematic trailer music that turns a small retrieval into an apocalyptic race against time*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Start with low strings and a distant pulse; build to huge brass hits, then reveal the entire mission is one umbrella at the front desk.*

**Verse 1**
A storm rolls down the station roof; the thunder shakes the sign.  
A lone black umbrella waits beside the lost-and-found line.  
The trailer voice says, "One small object. One last chance."  
The strings begin to tremble; the camera makes it dance.  
A clerk reaches for the tag; the brass begins to climb.  
A visitor runs across the lobby, racing against the time.  

**Chorus**
Boom, boom, the sky is falling;  
Rise, rise, the drums are calling.  
Save the handle, guard the tip;  
The umbrella must not miss the trip.  

**Verse 2**
A train is leaving in a minute; the owner turns around.  
The clerk reads off the number; it gets lost beneath the sound.  
The percussion starts a heartbeat, the strings repeat the rain.  
The owner shows the matching tag; the clerk checks it again.  
The umbrella opens in the lobby; water falls in sheets.  
The soundtrack calls it destiny; the janitor calls for mops.  

**Chorus**
Boom, boom, the sky is falling;  
Rise, rise, the drums are calling.  
Save the handle, guard the tip;  
The umbrella must not miss the trip.  

**Bridge**
The owner folds it carefully; the clerk hands back the strap.  
The brass stops on a single note; the rain fades from the map.  
The janitor wrings out the mat; the train is still in view.  
The hero boards with dry sleeves, and the clerk waves him through.  

**Ending**
The lobby lights return; the trailer voice signs off.  
The umbrella rests at home, its thunder folded soft.  
The clerk pins up a small note: "Objects claimed today."  
The giant final drum rolls once, then quietly walks away.
