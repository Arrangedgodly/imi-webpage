# The Sea Ballad Loses Its Bottle Message

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: wistful sea ballads about messages crossing oceans, sung by someone who cannot persuade a bottle to leave the harbor*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Gentle guitar, distant low harmonies and a swaying refrain; tiny harbor ripples answer every imagined mighty wave.*

[Verse 1]
I wrote a long confession on a little sheet of blue,  
And put it in a bottle with a cork I'd pressed right through.  
I set it on the water by the harbor's sheltered wall,  
The tide moved gently sideways; it did not go far at all.  
I sang about the ocean and the far-off waiting shore,  
The bottle bumped my own small pier and came back once more.  
A fisherman was passing with a basket on his back,  
He said the harbor eddy would keep bringing my note back.  

[Chorus]
Sail, little letter, sail across the blue,  
There is someone in the distance who should know the words of you.  
Drift, little bottle, find the open sea,  
The harbor has a habit of returning you to me.  
Sail, little letter, carry what I know,  
You have crossed a little puddle where the big ships never go.  

[Verse 2]
I watched it make a circle for a quarter of an hour,  
The fisherman came back again and asked about its power.  
I said it held a message for a friend across the bay,  
He said the ferry left at noon and went there every day.  
I lifted out the bottle and I dried the little note,  
The ferry took me over with the letter in my coat.  
My friend met me at landing and she read the words quite near,  
She said the bottle would make a vase for flowers waiting here.  

[Chorus]
Sail, little letter, sail across the blue,  
There is someone in the distance who should know the words of you.  
Drift, little bottle, find the open sea,  
The harbor has a habit of returning you to me.  
Sail, little letter, carry what I know,  
You have crossed a little puddle where the big ships never go.  

[Bridge]
We put the bottle on her table with a stem of lavender,  
The ferry horn announced my trip; I said I'd visit her.  
She wrote her answer on the back and handed it to me,  
I took it home by ferry; the bottle stayed away from the sea.  

[Final Chorus]
Sail, little letter, sail across the blue,  
The ferry has a timetable that carries me to you.  
Drift, little bottle, rest beside the pane,  
The flowers have a message that can reach her just the same.  
Sail, little letter, carry what I know,  
The harbor knows my singing now and waves whenever I go.
