# Scat Cat at the Planetarium

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: jazz scat solos and late-night planetarium atmosphere*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Brushes whisper, the bass walks, and leave room for the monkey's nonsense syllables.*

## Verse 1
The planetarium closed to school groups at six.  
A night guard let a mandrill test the sticks.  
The house band set up beneath Saturn's ring.  
The drummer brushed a moon into the evening.  
The sax began a question, soft and blue.  
The mandrill answered, "Bada-bop, the sky is new."  
He scatted through Orion, past the silver dome.  
The stars looked pleased to have a guest at home.  

## Chorus
Shoo-bee, starlight, swing around the room.  
Doo-bah, night sky, make the dark bloom.  
Shoo-bee, starlight, give the bass a lane.  
We don't need words to find the tune again.  

## Verse 2
A janitor pushed his cart in measured time.  
Its squeaking wheel supplied the fourth-part rhyme.  
The bassist nodded, followed every creak.  
The guard tried scat, but only squeaked.  
A child had left one paper star behind.  
The mandrill stuck it on his jacket, kind.  
He sang a solo for that tiny paper sun.  
The band held back until the note was done.  

## Bridge
The dome rolled open, showing real night air.  
No painted planets could compete with what was there.  
The sax went quiet; the brushes tapped the floor.  
The mandrill hummed a tune they'd never heard before.  

## Chorus
Shoo-bee, starlight, swing around the room.  
Doo-bah, night sky, make the dark bloom.  
Shoo-bee, starlight, give the bass a lane.  
We don't need words to find the tune again.  

## Ending
At dawn the band packed up beneath the sun.  
The guard kept scatting softly as he pushed his cart along.
