# The Trolley's Second Chorus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Dixieland jazz ensemble breaks, hot improvisation, and a street trolley whose bell keeps stealing the melody*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Trade short brass breaks; use the trolley bell as a recurring response, not a tempo cue.*

[Verse 1]
A trumpet led the band aboard the trolley at the square,
The clarinet took up the line and bounced it through the air.
The driver rang the bell once; the band replied in kind,
The trolley rolled one block and left the drummer two beats behind.

[Chorus]
Ring that bell, play that horn,
Ride the rails from dusk to morn.
Ring that bell, slide that tune,
The trolley's taking every solo way too soon.

[Verse 2]
The trombone made a comic sound; the passengers all grinned,
A macaque drummer tapped the handrail while the route map slowly spun.
The bell rang at the crossing, then the band began to chase,
The trolley took a left turn and the solo changed its place.

[Chorus]
Ring that bell, play that horn,
Ride the rails from dusk to morn.
Ring that bell, slide that tune,
The trolley's taking every solo way too soon.

[Bridge]
The driver called the stop name, but the brass played over all;
The clarinet held one long note and echoed down the hall.
A passenger stood up to leave; the door was on the side,
The band swung round the handrail as the trolley took them for a ride.

[Final Chorus]
Ring that bell, play that horn,
Ride the rails from dusk to morn.
Ring that bell, end the tune,
The trolley's at the depot, and the band is still in June.

[Ending]
They reached the depot; the bell rang once for the encore.
