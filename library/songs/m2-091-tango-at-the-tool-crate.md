# Tango at the Tool Crate

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tango conventions, dramatic partner turns, and a mechanic who cannot keep the wrench from taking a dance lead*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Slow tango pulse with bandoneon and sharp heel taps; the wrench gets one metallic response.*

Verse 1
The mechanic macaque polished up a wrench beside the door,
A tango band began to play across the garage floor.
Her partner bowed with careful grace and offered her his hand,
The wrench rolled off the tool crate and joined the dance command.

Chorus
Step and turn, don't lose the beat,
Mind the wrench beneath your feet.
Hold the pose, then sweep it wide,
Let the tools become your guide.

Verse 2
They crossed the floor in measured steps, their elbows held up high,
The wrench slid through the partner line and made the dancers fly.
The drummer struck a sudden stop; the wrench spun on its side,
The mechanic caught it in a dip and kept it as her guide.

Chorus
Step and turn, don't lose the beat,
Mind the wrench beneath your feet.
Hold the pose, then sweep it wide,
Let the tools become your guide.

Bridge
The bandoneon sighed low; the mechanic took a pose,
Her partner reached to catch the wrench; it slipped beneath his toes.
They pivoted around the crate, then bowed without a sound,
The wrench lay still, then rolled again and made one final round.

Verse 3
They hung the wrench upon the wall, a medal for the night,
The couple fixed the squeaky gate before the morning light.
The band played one last tango; the tools stayed in their tray,
The wrench gave one small rattle as the dancers walked away.

Chorus
Step and turn, don't lose the beat,
Mind the wrench beneath your feet.
Hold the pose, then sweep it wide,
Let the tools become your guide.

Ending
The garage door swung open; the couple took their bow,
The wrench stayed on its hook, in time with music somehow.
