# Mariachi at the Mango Gate

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: mariachi serenade conventions, trumpet calls, and a singer whose song is repeatedly redirected by courtyard gates*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Trumpet, vihuela, and guitarron; let the singer's romantic phrases collide with each gate latch.*

Verse 1
The singer brought his trumpet to the mango garden gate,
He practiced one serenade while the neighbors stayed awake.
The first gate swung behind him, the second blocked the way,
The trumpet played a flourish as the latch began to sway.

Chorus
Sing beneath the mango tree,
Let the trumpet set you free.
Open gates and turn around,
Love can travel through this town.

Verse 2
A macaque heard the music from a balcony above,
She leaned across the railing and mistook the song for love.
The singer bowed, the gate swung shut, the trumpet lost its view,
The guitarron kept strumming as the singer faced the zoo.

Chorus
Sing beneath the mango tree,
Let the trumpet set you free.
Open gates and turn around,
Love can travel through this town.

Bridge
He tried the side entrance; it opened to a shed,
A gardener rolled a wheelbarrow past the singer's head.
The trumpet called across the yard; the macaque heard the tune,
She met him by the main gate when it opened to the moon.

Verse 3
They shared a mango under stars, the band played soft and bright,
The gardener tied each gate back so none would close that night.
The singer sang a final verse; the neighbors joined the tune,
The trumpet thanked the hinges with a gentle afternoon.

Chorus
Sing beneath the mango tree,
Let the trumpet set you free.
Open gates and turn around,
Love can travel through this town.

Ending
The couple walked through every gate, then closed them one by one,
The band played from the courtyard till the serenade was done.
