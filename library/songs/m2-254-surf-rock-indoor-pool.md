# Surf Rock in the Indoor Pool

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: surf-rock guitar reverb, beach imagery, and fearless wave-riding poses*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use springy reverb guitar and a rolling drum fill; let the lifeguard interrupt each heroic surf pose with pool rules.*

[Verse 1]
The indoor pool opened on Tuesday night,  
A capuchin brought a polished board.  
The lifeguard said, "No riding; this is lane number three,"  
The guitarist played a wave chord; it splashed against the knee.  
A macaque wore a flower shirt and shades against the glare,  
The drummer used a kick drum as an umbrella there.  
The board slid from the tiles and floated by the wall,  
The singer called it "ocean," while the lifeguard called, "Don't fall!"  

[Chorus]
Ride the wave, ride the reverb,  
The water is calm but the guitar will swerve.  
Ride the wave, let the spring tank roll,  
No diving off the shallow end, that's the pool's control.  
Ride the wave, catch the foam,  
The surf is indoors and the lifeguard is home.  

[Verse 2]
They tried a curling turn beside the floating kickboard rack,  
The board spun in a circle and brought the singer back.  
The reverb made a cavern of the tiled and echoing room,  
The lifeguard blew one whistle and the guitar lost its boom.  
A gibbon paddled gently with a noodle at her side,  
The band made a surf break from the bubbles as they glided.  
The drummer shook a tambourine; it slipped into the pool,  
The lifeguard said, "No metal toys," and handed back the rule.  

[Chorus]
Ride the wave, ride the reverb,  
The water is calm but the guitar will swerve.  
Ride the wave, let the spring tank roll,  
No diving off the shallow end, that's the pool's control.  
Ride the wave, catch the foam,  
The surf is indoors and the lifeguard is home.  

[Bridge]
They put the board away and played a wave with sound,  
The guitar rose and fell while everyone stayed on the ground.  
The lifeguard let the solo pass, then closed the lane at nine,  
The band packed up its beach gear and stood behind the line.  

[Final Chorus]
Ride the wave, let the echo roll,  
The pool has drained the reverb from the rock-and-roll.  
Ride the wave, then towel off,  
The lifeguard has the final riff and calls the show off.  
Ride the wave, the band goes home,  
The surf was indoors; now the tiles are chrome.  

[Ending]
The last guitar chord bounced twice; the lifeguard signed the set list.
