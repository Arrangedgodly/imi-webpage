# The Lift Has a Syncopation

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ragtime piano syncopation, jaunty character entrances, and a service elevator that keeps arriving between beats*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Let the piano skip around a firm left hand; use the lift bell as an offbeat accent.*

[Verse 1]
A lemur pushed a piano to the service lift at noon,
The operator tapped a rag upon a wooden spoon.
The lift arrived between the beats and opened with a ding,
The piano's casters rolled ahead and joined the tune.

[Chorus]
Lift goes up, lift goes down, skip a little beat,
Piano rolls behind the band, bumping past their feet.
Lift goes up, lift goes down, ding against the key,
The elevator takes the long way up to level three.

[Verse 2]
A macaque held the door; the piano caught upon the seam,
The operator played a fill and missed the moving team.
The lift went up without the load; the band kept playing on,
The lemur tapped the call button on offbeats till the dawn.

[Chorus]
Lift goes up, lift goes down, skip a little beat,
Piano rolls behind the band, bumping past their feet.
Lift goes up, lift goes down, ding against the key,
The elevator takes the long way up to level three.

[Bridge]
They tuned the lift's old bell to C and set the tempo right,
The piano came aboard at last and caught the morning light.
The operator bowed to all the keys along the way,
The lift stopped every half a floor to hear the music play.

[Final Chorus]
Lift goes up, lift goes down, skip a little beat,
We ride up beside the keys and tap our tired feet.
Lift goes up, lift goes down, ding against the key,
The elevator made it up to level three by three.

[Ending]
They played their gig on level three; the bell rang through the bow.
The operator held his spoon: "The lift wants an encore now."
