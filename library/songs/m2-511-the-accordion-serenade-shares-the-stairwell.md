# The Accordion Serenade Shares the Stairwell

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic accordion serenades and apartment musicians discovering that a resonant stairwell belongs to everyone*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Accordion waltz and alternating voices; residents interrupt from three different landings.*

[Verse 1]
I stood beneath your landing with a red accordion,  
I sang about the evening and the things that could be done.  
The stairwell made my little song sound beautifully wide,  
A neighbor came down carrying a bag against his side.  
He asked if he could pass me and I moved beside the rail,  
My left hand kept the rhythm while my coat caught on his mail.  
You opened up your little door and heard the chorus start,  
The neighbor freed my coat before he left with half my heart.  

[Chorus]
Love climbs stairs, music climbs too,  
The stairwell has a letterbox between my song and you.  
Love climbs stairs, let the warm notes rise,  
Three other flats can hear the tune with very different eyes.  
Open the door, hear the melody fall,  
The landing holds my longing and belongs to people all.  

[Verse 2]
A second neighbor carried laundry through the little space,  
I paused the song and let her pass beside my hopeful face.  
A third called from the upper floor to ask if I could stop,  
His baby had been sleeping until love arrived with a hop.  
You invited me inside before I reached the second verse,  
I folded the accordion and lifted up my little purse.  
We sang the ending softly in your room beside the tea,  
The stairwell had its quiet for the other flats to be.  

[Chorus]
Love climbs stairs, music climbs too,  
The stairwell has a letterbox between my song and you.  
Love climbs stairs, let the warm notes rise,  
Three other flats can hear the tune with very different eyes.  
Open the door, hear the melody fall,  
The landing holds my longing and belongs to people all.  

[Bridge]
The neighbor with the laundry heard our softer little line,  
She left a friendly message: "This one's quite a pleasant time."  
The baby slept upstairs and the first neighbor got his mail,  
You asked if our next serenade could skip the stairwell tale.  

[Final Chorus]
Love climbs stairs, let the warm notes rise,  
The instrument stays folded till we find our own small skies.  
Open the door, hear the melody fall,  
The room has got two chairs for us; the stairwell has the hall.  
Love climbs stairs, music climbs too,  
The accordion gets a lift, with the bellows shut, to you.
