# The Theme Park Anthem Praises the Queue

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: theme-park promotional anthems selling prolonged waiting as a magical attraction*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Huge orchestral fanfare and an ecstatic choir; a turnstile click marks every chorus entrance.*

[Verse 1]
We crossed beneath the golden arch and heard a mighty choir,  
The castle roofs were glittering, the flags were flying higher.  
The sign said sixty minutes till the dragon train would start,  
The music made the waiting seem a journey of the heart.  
We walked between the velvet ropes in brightly painted rows,  
The song described adventures while the little number froze.  

[Chorus]
Step into wonder, take your place,  
Follow the rope at a magical pace.  
Every corner holds a dream,  
And another twenty people join the team.  
Lift your ticket to the sky,  
The finest queue in all the land is where your morning flies.  

[Verse 2]
The first bend held a statue and a very costly snack,  
The second held a photograph of where we'd just been back.  
The third bend had a mirror that made all our faces long,  
The fourth bend had a speaker with the first bend's little song.  
The family just behind us bought a photograph of four,  
They posed beside the ropes because they hadn't seen much more.  

[Chorus]
Step into wonder, take your place,  
Follow the rope at a magical pace.  
Every corner holds a dream,  
And another twenty people join the team.  
Lift your ticket to the sky,  
The finest queue in all the land is where your morning flies.  

[Bridge]
We reached the dragon train at last and climbed upon its back,  
It rolled a little circle round a tiny painted track.  
We stepped out at the exit in a momentary glow,  
The choir sang the queue's great song and told us where to go.  

[Final Chorus]
Step into wonder, take your place,  
Follow the rope at a magical pace.  
Every corner holds a dream,  
And another twenty people join the team.  
Lift your ticket to the sky,  
The gift shop has a queue whose little song explains why.
