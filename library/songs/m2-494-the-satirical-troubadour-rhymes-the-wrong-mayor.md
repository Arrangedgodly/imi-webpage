# The Satirical Troubadour Rhymes the Wrong Mayor

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: topical troubadour songs and performers using yesterday's rhyme for a politician with an entirely different name*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Solo lute-like guitar and a pointed storyteller voice; the town clerk corrects the name between verses.*

[Verse 1]
I wrote a song about the mayor who delayed the market gate,  
I rhymed his name with every word connected to the wait.  
Mayor Flake had promised us an opening before July,  
I sang about his flaky plans beneath the summer sky.  
The town clerk came to listen and she held a little card,  
She said Mayor Flake had moved away and working now was hard.  
The new mayor's name was Brindle and the gate was open wide,  
My chorus had a problem that my rhyme could never hide.  

[Chorus]
Flake, Flake, what a mistake,  
The gate stands wide open, but the queue will not break.  
Flake, Flake, how long will it take,  
The town has had a winter for a summer opening's sake.  
Sing the rhyme and stamp the beat,  
The new mayor's name refuses all the rhymes upon my sheet.  

[Verse 2]
The clerk showed me the dates when Mayor Brindle took the chair,  
He'd opened up the market and put benches in the square.  
I asked him for a short name that could fit my old refrain,  
He said he'd rather I should write about the latest train.  
The train was late on Fridays and the passengers agreed,  
Its timetable gave plenty of the subjects I could need.  
I put the old mayor's verse into a folder marked "Last Year,"  
I sang about the railway with the current facts quite clear.  

[Chorus]
Late, late, down by the gate,  
The Friday train is running late; the passengers all wait.  
Late, late, how long will we wait,  
The station clock has passed the promised posted date.  
Sing the rhyme and stamp the beat,  
The new mayor buys a ticket to the show beside the street.  

[Bridge]
He asked me not to flatter him for buying his own seat,  
I said the rhyme for Brindle was surprisingly incomplete.  
He offered me "rekindle" and "a candle with a spindle,"  
The clerk said neither phrase described the mayor or his window.  

[Final Chorus]
Late, late, down by the gate,  
The Friday train has got a queue that promises can't break.  
Sing the rhyme and stamp the beat,  
The clerk keeps all the dates beside the lyrics on my sheet.  
Late, late, how long will we wait,  
The train driver buys a ticket but arrives a chorus late.
