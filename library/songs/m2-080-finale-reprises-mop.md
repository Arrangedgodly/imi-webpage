# The Finale Reprises the Mop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical-theater finales, reprise callbacks, and finales that gather every character for one last chorus*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Full cast show tune that reprises earlier themes as stagehands finish cleaning; build to a grand final chord, then leave one mop squeak.*

Verse 1
The curtain rises one last time, the cast returns in rows,  
A macaque actor brings the clock, the chorus brings the bows.  
The baker has a tray of rolls, the clerk a stack of forms,  
The stagehand brings the mop bucket through the final storms.  
The orchestra recalls each tune, the bass begins to swell,  
The director calls for one more entrance; everyone rings a bell.  

Chorus
All together, lift the lights,  
Bring the stories home tonight.  
Sing the hook and take your place,  
One last bow across the space.  
Raise the banner, clear the floor,  
Sweep the confetti by the door.  
All together, let it ring,  
The finale fixes everything.  

Verse 2
The chorus finds the missing hat, the singer finds the key,  
The dancer finds her left shoe and rejoins the company.  
The stagehand asks the actors to stop blocking the aisle,  
They make a grand formation that extends another mile.  
The director wants a lift; the crew brings up a crate,  
The lead steps on, the crate rolls off, the cast adjusts the gate.  

Bridge
They reprise the first refrain, then every bridge and tag,  
The orchestra grows louder while the mop removes a flag.  
The audience stands and sings along, the cast bows in a chain,  
The stagehand points toward the exit; they sing that line again.  

Chorus
All together, lift the lights,  
Bring the stories home tonight.  
Sing the hook and take your place,  
One last bow across the space.  
Raise the banner, clear the floor,  
Sweep the confetti by the door.  
All together, let it ring,  
The finale fixes everything.  

Ending
The final chord is grand and long, the mop squeaks on the beat,  
The cast comes back to bow once more, then takes the bucket out to the street.
