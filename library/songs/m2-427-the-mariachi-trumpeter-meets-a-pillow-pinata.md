# The Mariachi Trumpeter Meets a Pillow Piñata

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jubilant mariachi party arrangements whose ceremonial excitement survives the discovery of the wrong party prop*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bright trumpet, strummed strings and a proud lead vocal; soft pillow thuds interrupt the expected shower of sweets.*

[Verse 1]
We came to play a birthday in a yard beside the square,  
A brightly painted donkey hung above a little chair.  
The host brought out a wooden stick and handed it around,  
The children took their turns while we supplied a festive sound.  
The donkey took a dozen hits without releasing treats,  
The trumpeter held one long note above the bouncing feet.  
The host looked at its stitching and discovered with a sigh,  
She'd bought a donkey cushion and had hung it rather high.  

[Chorus]
Play it bright, let it ring,  
The donkey has a soft side but it doesn't hold a thing.  
Raise the tune, sound the cheer,  
The birthday has a pillow where the sweets should all appear.  
Play it bright, lift the day,  
The donkey can survive the party longer than we play.  

[Verse 2]
The host took down the cushion and she placed it on the chair,  
Its little painted donkey wore a very patient stare.  
She brought a bowl of sweets from where she'd hidden them indoors,  
The children got a handful each and came back asking for more.  
The trumpeter announced the cushion's noble victory,  
The singer sang a verse about its perfect dignity.  
The smallest child sat on it as the birthday candles glowed,  
She said the donkey helped her see the cake upon the board.  

[Chorus]
Play it bright, let it ring,  
The donkey has a soft side but it doesn't hold a thing.  
Raise the tune, sound the cheer,  
The birthday has a pillow where the sweets should all appear.  
Play it bright, lift the day,  
The donkey can survive the party longer than we play.  

[Bridge]
We played the closing number and we packed our cases tight,  
The child took home the cushion as a souvenir that night.  
The host put down next year's order with a very careful eye,  
It said "Contains no stuffing; fill with sweets before you tie."  

[Final Chorus]
Play it bright, let it ring,  
The donkey goes to bedtime without losing anything.  
Raise the tune, sound the cheer,  
The birthday has a new friend who will last throughout the year.  
Play it bright, lift the day,  
The wooden stick gets sweeping duty; nobody else must sway.
