# The New Age Singer Forgets the Water

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: new age relaxation recordings and singers using elaborate waterfall descriptions to disguise an empty sound-effects tank*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Airy flute and soft vocal; a dry pump buzz stops, then the refilled tabletop fountain supplies softly trickling water.*

[Verse 1]
I promised flowing water in the meditation room,  
A little tabletop cascade would sing beneath the tune.  
The bowls were lined with polished stones and bamboo standing tall,  
I pressed the pump's small switch and heard no waterfall at all.  
A dry and little buzzing sound came from the hidden base,  
I sang of falling silver streams to fill the empty place.  
The session host switched off the pump and looked beneath the stone,  
The tank was empty; all the pump could offer was a drone.  

[Refrain]
Flow, little water, flow to the sea,  
The fountain has a tank that is as empty as can be.  
Flow, little water, let the heart release,  
The host would like the buzz to give the whole room peace.  
Stone, reed, soft voice, hold the gentle dream,  
A jug will help considerably with the promised stream.  

[Verse 2]
The host fetched clean water and she filled the little tank,  
She put the stones back carefully along the polished bank.  
The pump began to circulate a small and steady fall,  
The people heard the water moving softly by the wall.  
I sang my opening once again above the proper sound,  
The host sat down with everyone as quiet settled round.  
We finished with the fountain singing after every voice,  
The room could hear its little stream without a singer's choice.  

[Refrain]
Flow, little water, flow to the sea,  
The fountain has a tank that is as empty as can be.  
Flow, little water, let the heart release,  
The host would like the buzz to give the whole room peace.  
Stone, reed, soft voice, hold the gentle dream,  
A jug will help considerably with the promised stream.  

[Bridge]
The recording had a minute of the water by itself,  
The host put my reminder by the fountain on the shelf.  
I wrote a small instruction in a softly curling hand,  
"Fill the tank before announcing rivers through the land."  

[Final Refrain]
Flow, little water, flow to the sea,  
The host has brought a jug for the next session after me.  
Stone, reed, soft voice, hold the gentle dream,  
The little fountain gets a credit for supplying all the stream.  
Flow, little water, let the heart release,  
The host plays the minute with no singer for her peace.
