# The Ensemble Sings the Wrong Party Date

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: exuberant musical-theater preparation numbers whose elaborate coordinated activity outruns the group's knowledge of the actual event*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Brisk theater ensemble with alternating task calls; the final calendar correction stops the band for one long beat.*

[Verse 1]
We hung a hundred ribbons in the village meeting hall,  
The pianist held a rhythm while we decorated all.  
The chorus brought the plates out and the dancers moved the chairs,  
The singer spread the tablecloth with quite a little flair.  
We sang "Tonight's the party!" while the baker baked a cake,  
The caretaker came through the door and stopped beside the stage.  
He held the booking calendar and turned its final page,  
The party was next Saturday; we were early by seven days.  

[Chorus]
Hang it high, bring it here,  
The hall has got a party that is waiting to appear.  
Set the plates, sound the cheer,  
The guests have got another week before they all come here.  
Hang it high, make it grand,  
The date is on the invitation nobody had in hand.  

[Verse 2]
The singer found the invitation in her folded coat,  
It gave next week's date clearly in a very tidy note.  
The caretaker said the hall was free until the end of day,  
The pianist said the cake was done; we shouldn't throw it away.  
We sat around the long table and enjoyed our own small feast,  
The dancers had the nicest seats; the baker took his piece.  
We packed the ribbons into boxes and we labeled every lid,  
The singer put the invitation where the calendar had been.  

[Chorus]
Hang it high, bring it here,  
The hall has got a party that is waiting to appear.  
Set the plates, sound the cheer,  
The guests have got another week before they all come here.  
Hang it high, make it grand,  
The date is on the invitation nobody had in hand.  

[Bridge]
Next week we came together with the boxes and the date,  
The caretaker had checked it; we were neither early nor late.  
The baker made a second cake; the guests arrived quite pleased,  
We sang the preparation song while setting out their tea.  

[Final Chorus]
Hang it high, bring it here,  
The guests are at the party and the right day has appeared.  
Set the plates, sound the cheer,  
The choir will sing the party twice, one week apart this year.  
Hang it high, make it grand,  
The caretaker keeps the calendar where every voice can stand.
