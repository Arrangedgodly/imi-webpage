# Lo-Fi Beat for the Rainy Window

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: lo-fi hip-hop conventions, warm vinyl hiss, and a student whose study beat keeps syncing to a leaky window*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Soft sampled piano, relaxed drums, and gentle vinyl hiss; the rain leak should become the beat without overpowering it.*

Verse 1
A macaque set a notebook by the rainy window pane,
The lo-fi beat played softly while a droplet tapped the frame.
She tried to write a sentence, but the rhythm changed her line,
The water tapped a steady note and kept the time just fine.

Chorus
Let the rain fall soft and slow,
Let the little record glow.
Write one line, then let it rest,
The drip can keep the beat its best.

Verse 2
The pencil moved in circles; the page became a map,
The droplet hit the windowsill and matched the snare's soft clap.
A gibbon brought a bucket and set it by the sill,
The bucket caught the water with a hollow little fill.

Chorus
Let the rain fall soft and slow,
Let the little record glow.
Write one line, then let it rest,
The drip can keep the beat its best.

Bridge
The student found a towel and traced the leak above,
The frame had slipped a little; they would fix it when it dried.
The study beat kept humming, the rain moved off the roof,
The bucket held one final drop, a quiet bit of proof.

Verse 3
She finished her assignment as the clouds began to part,
The vinyl hiss became the sound of pencil on the chart.
The window stayed shut tight, the bucket went away,
The lo-fi beat kept one last drip and carried it to day.

Chorus
Let the rain fall soft and slow,
Let the little record glow.
Write one line, then let it rest,
The drip can keep the beat its best.

Ending
The leak was fixed, the notebook closed, the room was warm and dry,
The record spun in gentle hiss beneath a clearing sky.
