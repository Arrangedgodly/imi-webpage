# The Heavy Metal Band Borrows a Quiet Room

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: heavy metal sound checks and the discovery that a rehearsal booking includes neighboring occupants*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-015*
*Performance note: Muted guitar opens; the chorus expands only after the band moves into the soundproof rehearsal room.*

[Verse 1]
We booked a room called Thunder at the music school downtown,  
We carried in the speaker stacks and set the cases down.  
The drummer raised his sticks above a gleaming copper ride,  
A notice on the little door said, "Tutoring inside."  
The tutor had a student at a desk beside the wall,  
We asked about our booking; she had never heard our call.  
The clerk checked both the room names on the page beneath his thumb,  
Our room was Thunder Basement; this was Thunder Seminar One.  

[Chorus]
Thunder, thunder, let the walls reply,  
First we need the room where all the sound can safely fly.  
Thunder, thunder, raise the curtain high,  
The student has a spelling test and needs us to pass by.  
Roll the cases, hold the roar,  
Our name is on the booking for the other Thunder door.  

[Verse 2]
We hauled the heavy speaker stacks back out into the hall,  
The bassist found the basement on a map beside the wall.  
The clerk unlocked the proper room and showed the padded door,  
The drummer set the copper ride upon the carpeted floor.  
We checked the room was clear before the guitar gave a shout,  
The clerk stood in the corridor and heard no sound come out.  
The tutor met him upstairs with a finished spelling sheet,  
Her student wrote the word for thunder clearly and complete.  

[Chorus]
Thunder, thunder, let the walls reply,  
First we need the room where all the sound can safely fly.  
Thunder, thunder, raise the curtain high,  
The student has a spelling test and needs us to pass by.  
Roll the cases, hold the roar,  
Our name is on the booking for the other Thunder door.  

[Bridge]
The tutor asked us later for a spelling song with drums,  
We wrote a quiet demo where the alphabet just hums.  
She used it in the lesson with the volume turned down low,  
The student spelled the band's name on a ticket for our show.  

[Final Chorus]
Thunder, thunder, let the walls reply,  
The basement holds our speakers and the tutor passes by.  
Thunder, thunder, raise the curtain high,  
The student has a ticket with our name spelled properly.  
Roll the cases, earn the roar,  
The band checks every room name on the poster by the door.
