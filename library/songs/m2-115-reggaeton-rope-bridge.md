# Reggaeton at the Rope Bridge

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: reggaeton dembow rhythm, dance-floor callouts, and a crew trying to repair a rope bridge without losing the beat*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Dembow pulse, bright synth, and dry handclaps; the crew answers the beat with knots and careful steps.*

Verse 1
The rope bridge swayed above the stream and blocked the market trail,
A macaque brought a coil of rope and tied a sturdy rail.
The speaker played a dembow pulse that traveled through the trees,
The crew began to knot in time beneath the swaying leaves.

Chorus
Tie it tight, step it slow,
Keep the steady rhythm low.
Bridge will hold, feet will cross,
Beat can help but not get lost.

Verse 2
A gibbon pulled the left-hand rope; a capuchin checked the span,
The drummer called a count while the repair crew made a plan.
The bridge bounced on the downbeat; the toolbox slid away,
The crew tied it to a post and brought it back to play.

Chorus
Tie it tight, step it slow,
Keep the steady rhythm low.
Bridge will hold, feet will cross,
Beat can help but not get lost.

Bridge
The bass grew loud, the bridge swung wide, the foreman raised a palm,
They paused the music, checked each knot, and found the crossing calm.
The band resumed at half the speed, the ropes stayed in their place,
The crew walked single file across with careful measured pace.

Verse 3
The market opened on the far side; baskets crossed the stream,
The band played by the trailhead, keeping steady with the team.
The last crate rolled across; the bridge held firm and straight,
The crew untied the toolbox and went home before too late.

Chorus
Tie it tight, step it slow,
Keep the steady rhythm low.
Bridge will hold, feet will cross,
Beat can help but not get lost.

Ending
The speaker played one final beat; the ropes went still and slack,
The bridge stayed open through the dusk and brought the crew right back.
