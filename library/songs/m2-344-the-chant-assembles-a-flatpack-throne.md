# The Chant Assembles a Flatpack Throne

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: solemn monastic chants lending cosmic importance to confusing furniture assembly*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Unaccompanied low voices in a reverberant stone room; the hex key clinks against the floor on every refrain.*

[Verse 1]
We carried in the sacred box beneath the eastern arch,  
The abbot brought the diagram; the brothers made a march.  
We laid the panels on the stones in reverential light,  
The left side had three holes where there were four upon the right.  
The youngest brother held a peg and waited for a sign,  
The abbot chanted page fourteen and asked us all to bind.  

[Refrain]
O panel A, O panel B,  
Come together in humility.  
O little peg, O hidden screw,  
We have reserved a place for you.  
Let the seat descend and let the joints align,  
Our knees are tired of worshiping the underside of pine.  

[Verse 2]
We raised the back toward heaven and the seat toward the door,  
The diagram instructed us to turn it on the floor.  
We turned it with a mighty chord; the little pegs all fell,  
The youngest brother counted them in echoes of a bell.  
The abbot found a second bag beneath his flowing gown,  
It held the missing brackets that would keep the back from down.  

[Refrain]
O panel A, O panel B,  
Come together in humility.  
O little peg, O hidden screw,  
We have reserved a place for you.  
Let the seat descend and let the joints align,  
Our knees are tired of worshiping the underside of pine.  

[Bridge]
We fitted every bracket and we tightened every joint,  
The abbot blessed the little key and made a final point.  
The throne stood straight before us and the brothers stepped aside,  
He sat upon the cushion with a very solemn pride.  

[Final Refrain]
O panel A, O panel B,  
Come together in humility.  
O little peg, O hidden screw,  
One piece remains we never knew.  
The abbot holds it high and bids the chanting stop,  
It is the spare beneath the seat; he places it on top.
