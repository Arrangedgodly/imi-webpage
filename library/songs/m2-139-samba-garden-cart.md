# Samba for the Garden Cart

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: samba school procession rhythms, surdo and tamborim patterns, and gardeners who keep marching the harvest away from the weighing table*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Layered samba percussion, whistles, and bright cavaquinho; the cart should roll in a slow procession, not race.*

Verse 1
The gardeners loaded pumpkins on a cart beside the gate,
The samba school assembled with their drums and feathered plates.
The surdo set a steady pulse; the tamborim ran light,
The cart began to roll away before the crop was weighed.

Chorus
Roll in rhythm, slow and neat,
Keep the harvest on its feet.
Drum it forward, not too fast,
Bring the baskets in at last.

Verse 2
The farmers followed dancing; the cart went round the bend,
A macaque held the steering bar; a gibbon walked behind.
The whistle called a turn; the drums responded clear,
The pumpkins rocked from side to side and rolled toward the rear.

Chorus
Roll in rhythm, slow and neat,
Keep the harvest on its feet.
Drum it forward, not too fast,
Bring the baskets in at last.

Bridge
They stopped the cart beside the scale and counted every crate,
The bass drum kept a softer pulse; the farmers checked the weight.
They found one pumpkin missing; it was tucked beneath a hat,
The drummer wore it like a crown and danced around the mat.

Verse 3
The harvest reached the market; each basket had a place,
The samba line moved slowly through the garden at a pace.
The cart was parked beside the scale; the crops were weighed and sold,
The drummer took the pumpkin crown and set it in the cold.

Chorus
Roll in rhythm, slow and neat,
Keep the harvest on its feet.
Drum it forward, not too fast,
Bring the baskets in at last.

Ending
The band played down the garden path, the cart stayed by the gate,
The pumpkins made it to the scale, one crown arrived too late.
