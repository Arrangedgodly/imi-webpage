# The Speed Metal Band Waits for a Download

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: speed metal boasts about unstoppable velocity and musicians helpless before a slowly arriving software file*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Rapid double-kick drums and chugging guitar; the band stops to read the motionless progress bar.*

[Verse 1]
We named our album Velocity and booked the listening night,  
The guitars were tuned to fury and the drums were set quite right.  
The backing file was on a drive we'd left at home that day,  
Our bassist sent a link so we could get it another way.  
The bar moved to one percent and stopped beneath the screen,  
We sang about a lightning world with nothing in between.  
The drummer played a fierce roll while the download settled still,  
The speediest album in the town was waiting on a hill.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Faster than thunder, faster than time,  
The file has got a progress bar that doesn't want to climb.  
Faster than fire, faster than light,  
One percent has settled in to stay with us all night.  
Double kick, racing pick, let the engine roar,  
The router has one little lamp and nothing more in store.  

[Verse 2]
The host said all the listening phones were using up the line,  
He paused the guest connection so the file could have its time.  
The bar began to move again beneath our heavy stare,  
The drummer played a slower beat to match its pace through the air.  
The file reached one hundred and we saved it on the drive,  
The host restored the guest connection as the music came alive.  
We played the opening fast enough to fill the little room,  
The listener's phones could share the show beneath the pounding boom.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Faster than thunder, faster than time,  
The file has got a progress bar that doesn't want to climb.  
Faster than fire, faster than light,  
One percent has settled in to stay with us all night.  
Double kick, racing pick, let the engine roar,  
The router has one little lamp and nothing more in store.  

[Bridge]
We kept the file on two drives for the tour beyond the town,  
The bassist wrote the packing list and checked the drives were found.  
The drummer wrote a slow song for the bar that wouldn't run,  
Its title took four measures and its ending weighed a ton.  

[Final Hook]
Faster than thunder, faster than time,  
The two drives have the whole file; there is no bar left to climb.  
Double kick, racing pick, let the engine roar,  
The router gets no solo at the opening anymore.  
Faster than fire, faster than light,  
The slow song gets a standing cheer that outlasts all our might.
