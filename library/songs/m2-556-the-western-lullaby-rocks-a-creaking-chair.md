# The Western Lullaby Rocks a Creaking Chair

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: western lullabies promising perfect stillness while their own accompaniment creates the loudest noise*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Slow acoustic guitar and a gentle voice; the rocking chair creaks between every pair of lines.*

[Verse 1]
Sleep, little rider, the long trail is through,  
The lamp has a small golden circle for you.  
I rock in the chair while the evening goes by,  
The chair makes a creak that has no wish to die.  
You open one eye and you ask what it means,  
I tell you the prairie has many small things.  
The chair gives another long answer to mine,  
You ask if the prairie can whisper this time.  

[Refrain]
Rest, little rider, let the stars take the trail,  
The chair has a song that is louder than the tale.  
Rest, little rider, let the old day go,  
The prairie in the bedroom has a hinge that sings quite low.  
Soft strings, slow breath, hold the gentle air,  
The rider cannot sleep beside the singing of the chair.  

[Verse 2]
I stop all the rocking and hold the chair still,  
The quiet returns from the window and sill.  
I sing the next verse with the guitar in my lap,  
You close both your eyes and you loosen your cap.  
The chair stays quite steady till your breathing goes slow,  
I put down the guitar and I get up to go.  
The chair makes one last small departing reply,  
I wait in the doorway; you sleep as I sigh.  

[Refrain, whispered]
Rest, little rider, let the stars take the trail,  
The chair has a song that is louder than the tale.  
Rest, little rider, let the old day go,  
The prairie in the bedroom has a hinge that sings quite low.  
Soft strings, slow breath, hold the gentle air,  
The rider cannot sleep beside the singing of the chair.  

[Morning Verse]
The next day I oil the chair's loose little joint,  
The quiet new rocking has made its own point.  
You sit in the chair with a horse on your knee,  
And ask for the old chair's great prairie melody.  
I hum you its creak as the horse goes around,  
You laugh at the singer who must make that sound.  
The nighttime has silence, the morning has play,  
The chair gets a singing impersonator today.  

[Closing Refrain]
Rest, little rider, let the old day go,  
The chair has got a quiet joint and nothing more to show.  
Soft strings, slow breath, hold the gentle air,  
Tomorrow you will pay me one pretend coin for the chair.
