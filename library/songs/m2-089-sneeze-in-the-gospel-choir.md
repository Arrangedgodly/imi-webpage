# The Sneeze in the Gospel Choir

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gospel choir conventions, handclap crescendos, and a soprano whose sneeze keeps changing the key*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Warm organ, layered choir, and claps; make each sneeze interrupt the cadence before the group finds its harmony.*

Verse 1
The choir gathered Sunday in the bright community hall,
A gibbon led the opening note and filled the wooden wall.
A langur soprano breathed in dust beside the organ stand,
The choir held a silver chord and offered her a hand.

Chorus
Lift the note and let it rise,
Carry harmony to the skies.
Clap together, breathe in time,
Find the chord and make it shine.

Verse 2
They sang about a garden where the branches meet the sun,
The soprano prepared her high note; the cadence had begun.
She sneezed across the measure, and the organ shifted key,
The choir turned the stumble into unexpected harmony.

Chorus
Lift the note and let it rise,
Carry harmony to the skies.
Clap together, breathe in time,
Find the chord and make it shine.

Bridge
The leader passed a handkerchief; the soprano took a breath,
The choir held one long chord while the organ tested depth.
She sneezed again, a softer sound, the tenor caught the note,
The bass went low, the clappers swayed, the hymn began to float.

Verse 3
They wrote the new chord in the book and marked it "Blessed Surprise,"
The soprano sang the ending while a feather crossed her eyes.
The choir kept the tempo slow; the organ found its place,
Then everyone leaned back to give her breathing space.

Chorus
Lift the note and let it rise,
Carry harmony to the skies.
Clap together, breathe in time,
Find the chord and make it shine.

Ending
The service closed in harmony; the handkerchief was passed,
The choir left the hall in tune, and held its breath at last.
