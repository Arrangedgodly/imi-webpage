# The Gothic Organist Finds a Dust Sheet

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gothic organ music and ominous apparitions that turn out to be practical preparations for maintenance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Grave pipe-organ chords and whispered verses; the white apparition rustles with every ventilation draft.*

[Verse 1]
I came to practice midnight music in the little chapel hall,  
A white shape stood beside the pipes against the northern wall.  
Its shoulders reached the rafters and its face was hidden deep,  
I played a minor chord to ask what brought it from its sleep.  
It fluttered in the air current that came beneath the door,  
I played another darker chord and saw it move once more.  
The caretaker arrived with keys and asked about the sound,  
I pointed at the pale shape where the pipes were wrapped around.  

[Refrain]
White shape, cold breath, linger in the gloom,  
Something stands beside the organ in the echoing room.  
Low chord, long night, hear the shadows sing,  
The caretaker has a bucket and a rather earthly thing.  
White shape, cold breath, what has brought you here?  
The painting of the window frame is scheduled for this year.  

[Verse 2]
She lifted up the corner of the dust sheet on the pipes,  
The white shape was a wooden ladder underneath its stripes.  
She'd covered it to keep the dust from settling in the space,  
The draft made all the sheet folds drift across its hidden face.  
I played a little major chord and watched her move the frame,  
She folded up the dust sheet with no interest in its name.  
Next morning at the window she used it through the day,  
My very gloomy apparition had a useful part to play.  

[Refrain]
White shape, cold breath, linger in the gloom,  
Something stands beside the organ in the echoing room.  
Low chord, long night, hear the shadows sing,  
The caretaker has a bucket and a rather earthly thing.  
White shape, cold breath, what has brought you here?  
The painting of the window frame is scheduled for this year.  

[Bridge]
I wrote a suite about the sheet with grave and mournful notes,  
The caretaker wore paint marks on the sleeves of all her coats.  
She heard the organ practice and she asked a small request,  
"Can you play the bright bit while I finish up the rest?"  

[Final Refrain]
White shape, fresh paint, windows in the light,  
The apparition's folded up and put away tonight.  
Low chord, long night, hear the shadows sing,  
The caretaker has borrowed my recording for next spring.  
White shape, cold breath, let the last notes ring,  
She labels it "For Painting" with no ghostly anything.
