# Tango at the Lost Umbrella Desk

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tango's dramatic push-pull, jealous glances, and close turns*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Play a sharp bandoneon figure with deliberate pauses; the umbrella should be treated like a jealous dance partner.*

[Verse 1]
At the station's lost-property desk, the tango started late,  
A gibbon brought one umbrella and declared it her fate.  
A capuchin stepped closer with a hat of polished blue,  
The umbrella leaned between them as if choosing who was who.  
The dancers stepped and circled side to side.  
A macaque spun the handle; its wet tip drew a line,  
The clerk said, "Please keep dancing out of the filing sign."  

[Chorus]
Step, pause, turn, don't let it go,  
The umbrella has a mind, and it moves too slow.  
Lean in, lean out, let the red fringe sway,  
The lost desk keeps it till the owners agree.  
Step, pause, turn, let the bandoneon plead,  
One umbrella, two dancers, and a very long receipt.  

[Verse 2]
The gibbon showed a claim stub with a date from Tuesday noon,  
The capuchin had a photograph of rain beneath the moon.  
They each described the silver hook, the tassel and the seam,  
The clerk kept asking questions like a judge inside a dream.  
The umbrella tipped toward the capuchin; the gibbon turned away,  
The bandoneon made a sigh and brought them back to play.  
They traded places, traded stares, then stepped behind the chair,  
The umbrella opened suddenly and captured all their air.  

[Chorus]
Step, pause, turn, don't let it go,  
The umbrella has a mind, and it moves too slow.  
Lean in, lean out, let the red fringe sway,  
The lost desk keeps it till the owners agree.  
Step, pause, turn, let the bandoneon plead,  
One umbrella, two dancers, and a very long receipt.  

[Bridge]
The clerk unhooked a tag that read "Property of the floor,"  
A gust from the swinging station door swept it out once more.  
They chased it with a sweeping turn, their footwork neatly crossed,  
The tag landed on the umbrella, now the owner was lost.  

[Final Chorus]
Step, pause, turn, don't let it go,  
The umbrella has a mind, and it moves too slow.  
Lean in, lean out, let the red fringe sway,  
The lost desk keeps it till the owners agree.  
Step, pause, turn, the clerk has decreed,  
The desk will keep the umbrella; the dancers are freed.  

[Ending]
They left in opposite directions beneath one shared umbrella.
