# The Industrial Band Sorts the Scrap Metal

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: industrial percussion concerts and musicians finding that some spectacular noise comes from items they were meant to recycle*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Scrap-metal percussion and shouted vocal; separate tones identify items as the performers sort them into bins.*

[Verse 1]
We built a percussion concert from the workshop's little heap,  
Old pans and metal pipes made all the promises we'd keep.  
The drummer found a length of brass that gave a ringing tone,  
The workshop owner heard it and said, "Leave that one alone."  
The brass was for recycling with the copper by the door,  
The steel could join the concert; she'd told us that before.  
We'd mixed the useful spare parts with the things she meant to sell,  
The glorious industrial beat had hidden it quite well.  

[Chorus]
Clang, little steel, roar in the light,  
The brass has got a buyer and a different job tonight.  
Clang, little steel, carry the beat,  
The owner has three bins beneath the workshop's little seat.  
Loud hands, bright sparks of sound, let the rhythm go,  
The metal has a price beyond the noise inside the show.  

[Verse 2]
We stopped the opening rehearsal and we sorted every part,  
The owner helped us tell the metals carefully apart.  
The brass went in its labeled bin, the copper joined its own,  
The spare steel pans could stay to make the ringing we had shown.  
The drummer chose a hanging plate to take the bright brass line,  
He struck it with his padded beater and the new sound worked quite fine.  
The owner sold the sorted scrap before the evening set,  
The concert had a steel refrain with all its parts quite met.  

[Chorus]
Clang, little steel, roar in the light,  
The brass has got a buyer and a different job tonight.  
Clang, little steel, carry the beat,  
The owner has three bins beneath the workshop's little seat.  
Loud hands, bright sparks of sound, let the rhythm go,  
The metal has a price beyond the noise inside the show.  

[Bridge]
The crowd enjoyed the steel plate with its deep and ringing voice,  
The owner kept the little sale receipt beside her choice.  
We asked if she would sell some brass for our next recording day,  
She quoted us a price and said the buyer took it all away.  

[Final Chorus]
Clang, little steel, carry the beat,  
The owner's got a price list that the band would like to meet.  
Loud hands, bright sparks of sound, let the rhythm go,  
The steel plate gets a solo in the middle of the show.  
Clang, little steel, roar in the light,  
The band sells labeled offcuts as small souvenirs tonight.
