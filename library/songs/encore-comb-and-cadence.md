# Comb and Cadence

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Barbershop quartet harmony and old-fashioned grooming-shop patter*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Four-part close harmony, tiny barber capes, one hand mirror passed like a sacred relic.*

[Verse 1, all]  
Welcome to the grooming nook, where every whisker sings.  
We comb the crown, we brush the cheek, we polish monkey things.  
The mirror says your sideburns lean a little to the east.  
The barber says, "We'll even them, then trim around the feast."  

[Verse 2, lead]  
I came to get my muzzle tidied, just a modest little trim.  
They brought a chart of cheek designs and asked me to choose him.  
A spiral? Stripe? A crescent moon? A tiny company crest?  
I chose a clean and simple line. They called that "premium basic" best.  

[Chorus, all]  
Comb and cadence, clip and shine.  
Four fine voices, one straight line.  
Turn your chin and close your eyes.  
We'll sing your whiskers to a size.  

[Verse 3, bass]  
The left side is a little short. The right side wants to stay.  
The barber says, "We'll balance it," then takes the whole half away.  
The tenor hums a soothing chord; the baritone counts each hair.  
The alto holds the looking glass above the barber chair.  

[Verse 4, lead]  
My tail got tangled in the cape. I cannot turn around.  
The quartet sings a perfect chord and gently sets me down.  
The youngest barber monkey brings a comb of polished wood.  
He frees my tail and says, "Your whiskers now look very good."  

[Chorus, all]  
Comb and cadence, clip and shine.  
Four fine voices, one straight line.  
Turn your chin and close your eyes.  
We'll sing your whiskers to a size.  

[Bridge, tenor and bass]  
The cape comes off, the mirror gleams.  
The cut is neat, the bill is long.  
The barber says, "Your tail trim's extra."  
We answer him in four-part song.  

[Final Chorus, all]  
Comb and cadence, clip and shine.  
Four fine voices, one straight line.  
Turn your chin and close your eyes.  
We'll sing your whiskers to a size.  

[Outro]  
The quartet sweeps the fur away. The monkeys leave in tune.  
The comb is booked for next week. The barber hums the moon.
