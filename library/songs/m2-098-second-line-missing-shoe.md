# Second Line for a Missing Shoe

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: New Orleans second-line parade conventions, brass-band procession, and an orangutan trying to dance while searching for one shoe*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Snare, tuba, and bright brass; keep the parade moving as the missing shoe changes feet.*

Verse 1
The tuba called the neighborhood to gather by the square,
A baboon led the second line with feathers in her hair.
An orangutan joined the march with one shoe on his foot,
The other shoe was missing, though he searched beneath the root.

Chorus
Step out, turn round, follow the brass,
One shoe forward, one shoe at last.
Shake the bell and swing the cane,
Parade will find it in the lane.

Verse 2
The snare rolled down the avenue, the trumpets called the beat,
The dancer's single shoe kept tapping as he shuffled down the street.
They asked beside the bakery; the baker shook his head,
A shoe sat on the pastry tray, mistaken for the bread.

Chorus
Step out, turn round, follow the brass,
One shoe forward, one shoe at last.
Shake the bell and swing the cane,
Parade will find it in the lane.

Bridge
The baker passed it to the band; the tuba held it high,
The shoe rode on the music stand while brass went marching by.
The dancer reached to claim it, but the parade turned around,
He followed all the way back home, still tapping on the ground.

Verse 3
They stopped before his doorway; the band struck one more phrase,
He put the missing shoe back on and joined the final blaze.
The baker brought a pastry; the tuba played a call,
The second line went dancing till the moon rose over all.

Chorus
Step out, turn round, follow the brass,
One shoe forward, one shoe at last.
Shake the bell and swing the cane,
Parade will find it in the lane.

Ending
The shoes were both secured with lace; the parade rolled on,
The baker kept one pastry drum to tap the morning song.
