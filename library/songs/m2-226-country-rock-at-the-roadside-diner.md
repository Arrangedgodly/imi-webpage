# Chrome on the Diner Sign

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country-rock road anthems, guitar solos at roadside stops, and a diner sign that flickers like a farewell*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Blend a steady country rhythm with a bright electric solo; let the sign's flicker set the final beat.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque band rolled west along a two-lane county road,
They stopped beside a diner with a chrome and neon glow.
The sign said "Open" once, then blinked a little red,
The drummer took it for a message from the highway up ahead.

[Chorus]
Ride on, ride on, through the evening light,
Guitar at the window, stars above the night.
Ride on, ride on, let the tires hum,
The diner sign is blinking; tell us where we're from.
Ride on, ride on, through the evening light,
Guitar at the window, stars above the night.
Ride on, ride on, let the tires hum,
The diner sign is blinking; tell us where we're from.

[Verse 2]
The cook served cherry pie; the bassist tuned a low E,
The guitarist found a little spark and played a melody.
The sign blinked "O-en" while the letter P went dark,
The band played one more chorus for the missing neon mark.

[Chorus]
Ride on, ride on, through the evening light,
Guitar at the window, stars above the night.
Ride on, ride on, let the tires hum,
The diner sign is blinking; tell us where we're from.
Ride on, ride on, through the evening light,
Guitar at the window, stars above the night.
Ride on, ride on, let the tires hum,
The diner sign is blinking; tell us where we're from.

[Bridge]
A gibbon fixed the wiring with a fork and piece of tape,
The sign lit up "Closed" and changed the diners' escape.
The band packed up its cases, but the cook said, "Stay,"
The road can wait till breakfast; we have pie to pay.

[Final Chorus]
Ride on, ride on, through the morning light,
Guitar by the window, the sign is shining bright.
Ride on, ride on, let the tires hum,
The diner says "Open"; now we know where we're from.

[Ending]
They left at noon, with pie crumbs in the van.
