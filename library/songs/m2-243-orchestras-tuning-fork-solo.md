# The Orchestra's Tuning Fork Has a Solo

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: concert-hall classical overtures with a long, ceremonial tuning ritual*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Begin with a mock-grand orchestral swell, then let a small tuning fork interrupt the ensemble with precise comic pings.*

[Verse 1]
The conductor raised a baton over the stage,  
The oboe gave an A-note, clear as polished glass,  
The strings began to answer while the ushers let folks pass.  
A macaque found a tuning fork tucked behind a chair,  
It rang a perfect little ping that traveled everywhere.  
The conductor froze in profile, the cymbalist held breath,  
The fork had called the orchestra to silence like a chief.  

[Chorus]
Ping, little fork, tell us when to begin,  
The brass has a question, the strings want to come in.  
We tuned for an hour, we polished the stage,  
Now one tiny note has taken the page.  
Ping, little fork, keep time with your ring,  
The orchestra waits while the smallest thing sings.  

[Verse 2]
The horn section tried a warm-up, the fork replied in kind,  
The bassoon held a lower tone and left the ping behind.  
The cellist bowed a long note, the fork made two short sounds,  
The drummer marked the echo as it bounced around.  
A gibbon moved the music stand; the fork slid to the floor,  
It pinged against the marble and rolled beneath the door.  
The usher brought it back with velvet-gloved display,  
The conductor bowed to it before the first downbeat's play.  

[Chorus]
Ping, little fork, tell us when to begin,  
The brass has a question, the strings want to come in.  
We tuned for an hour, we polished the stage,  
Now one tiny note has taken the page.  
Ping, little fork, keep time with your ring,  
The orchestra waits while the smallest thing sings.  

[Bridge]
The concert finally started with a grand and rising theme,  
The fork rang from the conductor's pocket, bright against the scene.  
Each section followed that small pitch, the hall turned crystal clear,  
The audience leaned forward to hear the fork appear.  

[Final Chorus]
Ping, little fork, tell us when to begin,  
The brass has a question, the strings want to come in.  
We tuned for an hour, we polished the stage,  
Now one tiny note has taken the page.  
Ping, little fork, you can take your bow,  
The orchestra is ready to play for you now.  

[Ending]
The soloist was a fork; the program listed "metal, uncredited."
