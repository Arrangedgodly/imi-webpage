# Electro-Swing at the Clock Tower

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: electro-swing conventions, vintage brass over electronic beats, and a town clock that drops its hands whenever the bass hits*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Swing brass over a crisp electronic kick; make the clock's hand-drop a syncopated effect, then stop the bass for the repair.*

Verse 1
The clock tower struck at midnight as the DJ spun a disc,
The trumpet played a vintage line above the synth's brisk risk.
The bass came in and shook the gear; the minute hand slid down,
A macaque checked the clock face from the edge of town.

Chorus
Swing it up and let it tick,
Brass and bass can move it quick.
Drop the beat, hold the chime,
Set the hands and save the time.

Verse 2
The dancers filled the market square and spun beneath the clock,
The hand fell down another notch and landed on a rock.
The DJ cut the low end; the clock began to move,
The trumpet held a steady tone and found a slower groove.

Chorus
Swing it up and let it tick,
Brass and bass can move it quick.
Drop the beat, hold the chime,
Set the hands and save the time.

Bridge
The clockwright climbed the tower and tightened every wheel,
The band played brass without the bass to keep the gears from peel.
The minute hand returned to noon; the dancers stopped in place,
The clock struck once, then waited for the bass to leave its space.

Verse 3
They set the speakers farther back and softened every thump,
The tower ticked in steady time; the square resumed its jump.
The DJ mixed the old and new, the clock kept up the swing,
The clockwright joined the dancers for the final brass-string.

Chorus
Swing it up and let it tick,
Brass and bass can move it quick.
Drop the beat, hold the chime,
Set the hands and save the time.

Ending
The clock struck twelve, the music stopped, the hands stayed where they should,
The DJ packed the bass away and called the tower good.
