# The Bragging Rapper Fills a Loyalty Card

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: luxury rap boasts inflated around tiny rewards from an everyday coffee shop*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Confident beat, clipped verses and a sung hook; stamp sounds punctuate each boast.*

[Verse 1]
I roll into the café with a wallet full of power,  
Seven little circles and I'm coming for the hour.  
The barista knows my order; she can hear it from the door,  
One small coffee, no additions, same as all the days before.  
She stamps the eighth small circle with the logo of the place,  
I hold it like a title deed beside my serious face.  
Two more cups to greatness, two more visits to the throne,  
My entourage is one mate who has bought a drink of his own.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Stamp it, stamp it, watch the empire rise,  
There is one free small coffee at the ending of the prize.  
Stamp it, stamp it, hold the paper high,  
The kingdom has ten circles and a very narrow sky.  
Big plans, small cup, let the counter shine,  
The barista checks the date before approving what is mine.  

[Verse 2]
I buy the ninth on Friday and the tenth on Saturday,  
I enter Sunday morning with my finished card on display.  
I ask for extra syrup and a triple-sized command,  
She says the free one is the small cup stated in my hand.  
I choose the small black coffee and she takes the card away,  
She hands me one new blank one for the next ten cups I pay.  
I sip the drink quite slowly while my mate unfolds the news,  
He asks if all my kingdoms come with plastic little spoons.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Stamp it, stamp it, watch the empire rise,  
There is one free small coffee at the ending of the prize.  
Stamp it, stamp it, hold the paper high,  
The kingdom has ten circles and a very narrow sky.  
Big plans, small cup, let the counter shine,  
The barista checks the date before approving what is mine.  

[Bridge]
I write a verse about the crown beside the takeaway shelf,  
My mate says he has saved more by making his own himself.  
I offer him my free coffee; he says he likes its taste,  
The barista clears the empty cups without the slightest haste.  

[Final Hook]
Stamp it, stamp it, watch the empire rise,  
The new blank card has space for the next ten little tries.  
Big plans, small cup, let the counter shine,  
My mate has brought a flask to keep his kingdom quite divine.  
Stamp it, stamp it, hold the paper high,  
The barista says the next cup costs the normal price to buy.
