# The Accordion Serenade Finds the Wrong Balcony

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic accordion serenades whose confidence outlasts the singer's inability to find the right window*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Swaying accordion and tender vocals; the baker's dough-slapping noises answer the romantic refrain.*

[Verse 1]
I stood beneath a balcony with roses by my shoes,  
My accordion breathed gently and I sang the evening news.  
I called my sweetheart's lovely name and lifted up my chin,  
The window opened quietly; a baker stood within.  
He asked if I was bringing flour or wanted yesterday's bread,  
I answered with a love song and he shook his floury head.  

[Chorus]
Come to the window, come hear my heart,  
Let the little night be where our feelings start.  
Lean from the balcony, smile down to me,  
Somebody up there is kneading instead of agreeing.  
Come to the window, let the roses bloom,  
The baker has a dozen rolls and no romantic room.  

[Verse 2]
I checked the little address on a note beside my chest,  
It named the next-door balcony; I moved to do my best.  
The window opened higher and a tailor raised a sleeve,  
He asked if I had come to pay before he had to leave.  
My sweetheart leaned from higher up and waved a little light,  
I'd sung to two wrong windows; she was on the upper right.  

[Chorus]
Come to the window, come hear my heart,  
Let the little night be where our feelings start.  
Lean from the balcony, smile down to me,  
Somebody up there is kneading instead of agreeing.  
Come to the window, let the roses bloom,  
The baker has a dozen rolls and no romantic room.  

[Bridge]
She came downstairs to meet me while the baker brought a tray,  
The tailor joined us at the door and put his sleeve away.  
We shared the bread beneath the moon and sang a little song,  
My sweetheart took my flowers as the bakery sang along.  

[Final Chorus]
Come to the window, come hear my heart,  
Let the little night be where our feelings start.  
Lean from the balcony, smile down to me,  
My sweetheart's on the pavement where she wanted me to be.  
Come to the window, let the roses bloom,  
The baker hires my serenade to advertise his rolls.
