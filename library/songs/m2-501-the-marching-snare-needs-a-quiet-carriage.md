# The Marching Snare Needs a Quiet Carriage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: percussion showcase songs and instrumentalists trying to demonstrate their art on public transport*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Sung percussion syllables over tapped fingers; the actual snare remains silent until the rehearsal room.*

[Verse 1]
I took my snare by train to reach the morning marching band,  
Its padded case sat by my feet, my ticket in my hand.  
The conductor checked my carriage and she pointed to the sign,  
The quiet zone had all the seats beside this part of the line.  
I tapped the rhythm on my knee before the next big stop,  
A reader raised an eyebrow; quiet asked the taps to stop.  
I stilled my hands and counted all the measures in my head,  
The drummer in the quiet zone had silent bars instead.  

[Rhythm Refrain, whispered]
Ta-ka, ta-ka, hold the roll,  
The rhythm has to wait inside a very quiet soul.  
Ta-ka, ta-ka, tuck the beat,  
The drum is in a padded case beneath my little seat.  
One, two, three, four, let the silent numbers climb,  
The reader turns a page and keeps her own quiet time.  

[Verse 2]
I reached the next small station and I carried out the case,  
The rehearsal room was waiting with its ordinary space.  
I set the drum upon its stand and warmed the wooden sticks,  
The band could hear the rhythm with its little marching kicks.  
The leader asked me what I'd practiced on my morning way,  
I showed the count upon my fingers, making no noise as I played.  
She said a silent counting trick could help us keep the line,  
The band held one bar silently and entered quite on time.  

[Rhythm Refrain]
Ta-ka, ta-ka, hold the roll,  
The rhythm has to wait inside a very quiet soul.  
Ta-ka, ta-ka, tuck the beat,  
The drum is in a padded case beneath my little seat.  
One, two, three, four, let the silent numbers climb,  
The reader turns a page and keeps her own quiet time.  

[Bridge]
The concert had a silent bar before the final tune,  
The crowd heard everybody stop beneath the bright balloon.  
A reader from the carriage recognized my little face,  
She thanked the band for giving quiet such a useful place.  

[Final Refrain]
Ta-ka, ta-ka, let the drum go,  
The concert has a stage where the rhythm has a show.  
One, two, three, four, let the silent numbers climb,  
The band has one quiet bar and a much louder ending time.  
Ta-ka, ta-ka, tuck the beat,  
Tomorrow's train has got the same reader in my seat.
