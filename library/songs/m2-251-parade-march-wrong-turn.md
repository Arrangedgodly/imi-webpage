# The Parade March Took a Wrong Turn

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ceremonial marching-band marches with rigid formations and a grand route map*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Keep a strict snare cadence; let the trumpets enthusiastically announce each increasingly incorrect street.*

[Verse 1]
The band lined up at sunrise with a drum major in white,  
A macaque checked the route map and held it to the light.  
Trumpets faced the courthouse, tubas faced the store,  
The snare marched them past the door.  
The map said "Main Street, turn at the library and stop,"  
The drum major spun his baton, the band began to hop.  
A gibbon read the street sign upside down beside the lane,  
The band made a perfect left and marched back through the rain.  

[Chorus]
Left, right, left, the brass will lead,  
We march in time with patriotic speed.  
Turn at the corner, bow at the square,  
The route is on the map if we can find it there.  
Left, right, left, the snare rolls on,  
The parade is marching where the street has gone.  

[Verse 2]
The sousaphone went first, then the flute section came,  
The cymbals made a crash beside a bakery's name.  
They passed the same flower shop for the third time around,  
The drum major called it "a ceremonial loop downtown."  
A capuchin waved from a balcony and shouted, "You're back!"  
The trombones played a fanfare and the snare kept track.  
They marched around a fountain, the route began to bend,  
The banner caught a lamppost and bowed before the band.  

[Chorus]
Left, right, left, the brass will lead,  
We march in time with patriotic speed.  
Turn at the corner, bow at the square,  
The route is on the map if we can find it there.  
Left, right, left, the snare rolls on,  
The parade is marching where the street has gone.  

[Bridge]
The drum major checked the map and saw the page was torn,  
The missing half had been beneath the sousaphone since morn.  
It showed the planned procession ending by the park,  
The band was two blocks off, but no one missed the mark.  

[Final Chorus]
Right, left, right, the brass will steer,  
The park is just ahead; the finish line is near.  
Turn at the corner, bow by the tree,  
The band has found the route and the crowd is there to see.  
Right, left, right, the snare rolls on,  
The parade ends where the street has gone.  

[Ending]
The band reached the park in time; the drum major saluted the map.
