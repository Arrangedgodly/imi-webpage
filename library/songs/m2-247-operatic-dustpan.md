# The Operatic Dustpan

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: grand opera's high-stakes arias and tragic prop business*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Give the lead a broad operatic vibrato and let the orchestra swell whenever a speck of dust moves.*

[Verse 1]
The broom was lost at closing; the hall was clean,  
A soprano macaque stood beside the dusty scene.  
She lifted up a dustpan and sang a mournful plea,  
"Who shall gather this small crumb that has escaped from me?"  
A gibbon held a mop like a spear,  
He cried, "The crumb is harmless, yet it fills us all with fear."  
The janitors took a breath and cried,  
"The dustpan has a handle, and the dust is on this side!"  

[Chorus]
O crumb, depart, the night is drawing near,  
The broom's great shadow falls; the floorboards shake with fear.  
O pan, hold fast, our final sweep begins,  
The orchestra is ready for the smallest of our sins.  
O crumb, be gone, we sing beneath the light,  
A tragic little speck has met its fate tonight.  

[Verse 2]
The soprano swept it leftward; the tenor swept it right,  
The chorus split in factions and debated through the night.  
The crumb flew to the curtains, then landed on a shoe,  
The soprano sang a high C; the janitor said, "Shoe!"  
The crumb sat on the shoelace, too embarrassed to fly.  

[Chorus]
O crumb, depart, the night is drawing near,  
The broom's great shadow falls; the floorboards shake with fear.  
O pan, hold fast, our final sweep begins,  
The orchestra is ready for the smallest of our sins.  
O crumb, be gone, we sing beneath the light,  
A tragic little speck has met its fate tonight.  

[Bridge]
The vacuum found its switch again and hummed a lower note,  
The crumb slipped off the laces and landed in the throat  
Of the dustpan, not the singer; the audience could breathe,  
The conductor gave a downbeat; the broom made one last heave.  

[Final Chorus]
O crumb, depart, your reign is drawing near its end,  
The pan has caught you safely; the broom can be your friend.  
O dust, be still, the janitors have won,  
The mighty aria is over; the sweeping is now done.  
O crumb, good night, we bow beneath the light,  
The floor is clean; the mop will sing tomorrow night.  

[Ending]
The curtain fell on one speck; the crew swept it away.
