# The Salsa Singer Stirs the Rice

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: salsa bandleaders who turn every instruction into a call-and-response, even while following a rice recipe*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Piano montuno, lively percussion and answering backing vocals; the cook tries to shorten instructions while the singer expands them.*

[Verse 1]
The cook invited me to help prepare the band's big meal,  
I brought a spoon and all the rhythm anyone could feel.  
She said to rinse the rice twice in the bowl beside the sink,  
I called the words toward the band and waited for their link.  
They answered "Rinse it! Rinse it!" while I swayed from side to side,  
The cook had rinsed it twice herself before our chorus died.  
She said to put the water in and cover up the pan,  
I called for one more answer; she put in the water then.  

[Chorus]
Stir it up, sing it back,  
Every little kitchen order gets a chorus from the pack.  
One more call, one reply,  
The cook has finished half the meal while I lift my spoon up high.  
Stir it up, make it swing,  
The recipe has eight lines but the band adds everything.  

[Verse 2]
She set the rice to simmer and she told me leave it closed,  
I sang "Leave it closed" so warmly that the drummer nearly rose.  
He tried to lift the lid to give the answer more of a beat,  
The cook held out her oven mitt and kept him from the heat.  
She asked me just to set the table while the water steamed,  
I gave the spoons to backing singers waiting for a theme.  
We laid the plates in perfect time and folded napkins neat,  
The cook announced the rice was done; we finally took our seats.  

[Chorus]
Stir it up, sing it back,  
Every little kitchen order gets a chorus from the pack.  
One more call, one reply,  
The cook has finished half the meal while I lift my spoon up high.  
Stir it up, make it swing,  
The recipe has eight lines but the band adds everything.  

[Bridge]
She put a full plate before me and said, "Now simply eat."  
I opened up my mouth to call; she pointed to the feast.  
The band went quiet for a minute; every spoon was put to use,  
Then someone called for seconds and the chorus broke loose.  

[Final Chorus]
Dish it up, sing it back,  
The cook is bringing seconds to the grateful hungry pack.  
One more call, one reply,  
There is rice enough for everyone and plenty waiting by.  
Dish it up, make it swing,  
We sing the cook a thank-you; she says, "Good. Now wash everything."
