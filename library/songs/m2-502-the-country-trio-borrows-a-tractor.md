# The Country Trio Borrows a Tractor

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country road songs and touring bands who romanticize rural transport until they must fit instruments inside it*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Easy country guitar, close trio harmony and a slow engine pulse; the engine stops during the chorus.*

[Verse 1]
Our van had stopped beside the farm five miles before the hall,  
The farmer heard us knocking and came out to take the call.  
He offered us his tractor for the trip beside the lane,  
We thought of all the country songs about a life that felt quite plain.  
One seat sat in the cabin with no room to fit us three,  
The farmer asked which person ought to drive it carefully.  
Our bassist stayed beside the van to meet the roadside crew,  
Our guitarist rode with his guitar, its case in clear view.  

[Chorus]
Down the lane, slow wheels turning,  
The little tractor keeps its engine burning.  
Down the lane, evening light,  
One seat does not take a whole band into the night.  
Country road, wide open sky,  
The other two need something with more seats to come by.  

[Verse 2]
The singer called the hall to ask for help along the road,  
The caretaker brought a car with room enough to hold our load.  
She took the singer and the drum beside the tractor's track,  
The bassist joined us later when the van was brought back.  
The tractor reached the hall and parked beside the little gate,  
The farmer took a bus to fetch it before getting late.  
We gave him tickets to the show and tea beside the stage,  
The tractor joined the set list in a verse upon the page.  

[Chorus]
Down the lane, slow wheels turning,  
The little tractor keeps its engine burning.  
Down the lane, evening light,  
One seat does not take a whole band into the night.  
Country road, wide open sky,  
The other two need something with more seats to come by.  

[Bridge]
We sang about the farmer and the tractor at the gate,  
He listened to the slow verse with his teacup on a plate.  
The garage fixed the van before the show was fully done,  
We drove it home with three seats and three instruments in one.  

[Final Chorus]
Down the lane, slow wheels turning,  
The farmer takes his tractor home with its engine burning.  
Country road, wide open sky,  
Our van has seats for all the band and cases piled quite high.  
Down the lane, evening light,  
The farmer asks us to stop naming his tractor "Flight of the Night."
