# The Festival DJ Counts the Silent Headphones

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: festival DJ hype and performers who forget that a silent-disco audience must select the same transmission channel*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Dance beat and exuberant spoken calls; the first half of the audience dances to a completely different feed.*

[Verse 1]
I climbed the platform at the fair with headphones on my head,  
I told the crowd to raise their hands and follow where I led.  
Half of them jumped with every beat and half went slowly round,  
The headphones had two channels with a different kind of sound.  
My channel played a racing beat; the other played a waltz,  
The people heard my shouted call only in the first result.  
I watched the slow revolving pairs and thought they'd missed the night,  
The host held up a red-lit pair and told me which was right.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Hands high, jump to the beat,  
The blue-lit headphones have the bass that moves my feet.  
Hands high, turn to the sound,  
The red-lit headphones take a three-step journey round.  
Two colors, two tunes, let the festival glow,  
The crowd has got a choice that the DJ ought to know.  

[Verse 2]
The host showed me the channel switch beneath the little ear,  
I listened to the waltz until the difference came clear.  
I held up blue headphones to show the people where I played,  
The dancers who wanted my beat switched over from the red.  
The other pairs kept waltzing on the far side of the floor,  
The host left them a little space beside the garden door.  
I gave the jumping half the calls that suited what they heard,  
The waltzing pairs heard a singer on their own melodic word.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Hands high, jump to the beat,  
The blue-lit headphones have the bass that moves my feet.  
Hands high, turn to the sound,  
The red-lit headphones take a three-step journey round.  
Two colors, two tunes, let the festival glow,  
The crowd has got a choice that the DJ ought to know.  

[Bridge]
I changed my opening announcement for the next day's little show,  
"Blue has got the racing beat; red lets the waltz go slow."  
The host put up a board to show the colors by the gate,  
The headphone desk explained the switch to anyone who came late.  

[Final Hook]
Hands high, jump to the beat,  
The blue-lit headphones have the bass that moves my feet.  
Two colors, two tunes, let the festival glow,  
I ask how many hear me and the blue-lit people show.  
Hands high, turn to the sound,  
The red-lit dancers wave at me once every three steps round.
