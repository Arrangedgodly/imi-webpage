# The Bossa Nova Singer Runs Out of Ice

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: effortless lounge music and hosts working very hard to preserve an atmosphere of effortless leisure*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Soft guitar syncopation and a relaxed vocal; bare ice trays provide dry percussion at the refrain.*

[Verse 1]
I sang beside the patio about a lazy afternoon,  
The host set out the glasses with a little silver spoon.  
The first six guests got icy drinks with lemons bright and thin,  
The seventh got a glass as warm as what she'd poured within.  
The freezer held two empty trays beside a loaf of bread,  
The host said everything was lovely with a careful nod of head.  
I sang of cool horizons while she filled the trays again,  
The clock gave all the water time to think about its plan.  

[Chorus]
Easy, easy, let the warm day sway,  
The water in the freezer has a very slow delay.  
Easy, easy, let the evening bend,  
The ice will reach the party somewhere nearer to its end.  
Soft sun, little glass, let the gentle music roam,  
The host has asked her neighbor if he has some ice at home.  

[Verse 2]
The neighbor brought a cooler with a bag of cubes inside,  
The host filled every waiting glass and set the bag aside.  
She brought him out a chair beneath the jasmine by the gate,  
He drank the coldest lemonade and said the party sounded great.  
I changed my verse to mention how the shade moved on the wall,  
The host sat down beside him after answering every call.  
The empty trays stayed freezing in the kitchen out of sight,  
Their little cubes grew ready when the sunset lost its light.  

[Chorus]
Easy, easy, let the warm day sway,  
The water in the freezer has a very slow delay.  
Easy, easy, let the evening bend,  
The ice will reach the party somewhere nearer to its end.  
Soft sun, little glass, let the gentle music roam,  
The host has asked her neighbor if he has some ice at home.  

[Bridge]
The guests had gone when we discovered all the new-made ice,  
The host poured out two drinks and said the quiet felt quite nice.  
We sat beside the empty chairs beneath the darkened sky,  
The cubes made tiny cracking sounds as we let the time go by.  

[Final Chorus]
Easy, easy, let the warm day sway,  
The ice arrived for people who had stayed to put things away.  
Soft sun, little glass, let the gentle music roam,  
Tomorrow we return the neighbor's cooler to his home.  
Easy, easy, let the evening bend,  
The song has finished working; let the singer drink at the end.
