# The Waltz for a Three-Legged Table

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Viennese waltz's sweeping three-beat turns and elegant ballroom poise*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Keep a lilting one-two-three; let the table's wobble interrupt the dancers' smooth rotations.*

[Verse 1]
A three-legged table stood beside the ballroom wall,  
A gibbon set a vase upon it for the springtime ball.  
The orchestra began a waltz, the dancers took the floor,  
The table leaned on beat one and slid across the door.  
A macaque caught the vase before it met the ground,  
The violins held one long note; the dancers circled round.  
The table rocked in triple time, its legs kept missing three,  
The host announced, "A graceful turn!" and asked it to agree.  

[Chorus]
One, two, three, let the ballroom sway,  
The table keeps on dancing in its own way.  
One, two, three, don't spill the wine,  
The vase is doing waltz steps out of line.  
One, two, three, the violins rise,  
The table has a lovely, uneven surprise.  

[Verse 2]
The carpenter arrived with a wedge of polished wood,  
He slid it underneath the leg and said, "It should be good."  
The table stood as upright as a duchess at the ball,  
The guests resumed their turning, gliding past the wall.  
The host set down a tray of cups and raised a crystal glass,  
The wedge slipped out, the table swayed, the dancers let it pass.  
A capuchin caught the teapot; a baboon caught the tray,  
The orchestra played through the spill and never lost the sway.  

[Chorus]
One, two, three, let the ballroom sway,  
The table keeps on dancing in its own way.  
One, two, three, don't spill the wine,  
The vase is doing waltz steps out of line.  
One, two, three, the violins rise,  
The table has a lovely, uneven surprise.  

[Bridge]
They moved the table gently to a level corner spot,  
The vase returned to center; the cups remained in thought.  
The dancers took a sweeping turn and circled it with care,  
The table stayed in place and watched them in the air.  

[Final Chorus]
One, two, three, the room is clear,  
The table keeps its three legs here.  
One, two, three, the vase stands fine,  
The wedge stays close in case it leaves the line.  
One, two, three, the violins rise,  
The table bows with one last uneven surprise.  

[Ending]
The table held the final pose; the dancers applauded its feet.
