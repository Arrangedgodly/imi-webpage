# The Diss Track Cites Its Sources

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rap rivalries undermined by scholarly footnotes and qualified claims*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Heavy boom-bap beat, precise clipped verses and a librarian responding to the hook.*

[Verse 1]
You say you run the jungle, that's a very broad assertion,  
I checked the little census and revised the first version.  
You live above the bakery, the lease is in your name,  
That makes you king of upstairs, not the whole terrain.  
You said you broke the record at the mountain swimming pool,  
The lifeguard kept the stopwatch and the judge applied the rule.  
Your forty-second victory was forty seconds late,  
See appendix B, my friend; the timestamp holds the date.  

[Chorus]
Read the note beneath the flame,  
Check the year beside the name.  
I came here with a beat and a bibliographic stack,  
You can borrow all my sources, but you have to bring them back.  
Hands up, index down,  
Who holds the verified crown?  

[Verse 2]
You called my bars a rumor, so I brought the shopping slip,  
You bought your golden necklace in the costume shop's back strip.  
The clerk supplied a statement, but requested to be masked,  
I've shaded out his monkey face exactly as he asked.  
Your rival ate your lunch, you said, and left you just the plate,  
The footage shows you ate it while you told him he was late.  
The camera clock was wrong, though, and the angle wasn't clear,  
I've struck that line completely; I won't use it in your ear.  

[Chorus]

[Bridge]
You grab the mic and claim my shoes are rented for the show,  
I hold the purchase invoice up, the crowd begins to glow.  
You point out it's a photocopy; I dig into my bag,  
The DJ loops a drumbeat while I look beneath the tag.  

[Final Chorus]
