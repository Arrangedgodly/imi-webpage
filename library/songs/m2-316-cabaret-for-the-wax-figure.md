# Cabaret for the Wax Figure

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cabaret's intimate asides and museum performers who turn a no-touch rule into an audience participation number*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Piano-led cabaret with sly spoken asides; the singer addresses the motionless figure as if it were her toughest critic.*

**Verse 1**
The gallery closes; one spotlight stays on the stage.  
A singer in a velvet coat steps out to turn the page.  
A wax explorer stands nearby with one raised wooden hand.  
She says, "You look so thrilled to see the evening show begin."  
The visitors have all gone home; the guard is at the door.  
The figure's glass case catches light and looks like one guest more.  

**Chorus**
Come closer, darling, but please stay behind the line;  
I sing for you at nine o'clock, you never miss a rhyme.  
Wink once if you like the tune, wink twice if you don't;  
The wax man keeps his steady face and naturally he won't.  

**Verse 2**
The singer pours a paper drink and sets it on the ledge.  
The guard taps on the glass to ask her not to edge.  
She takes a long dramatic pause; the figure does not blink.  
She asks him if the silence means he'd rather have a drink.  
A mop bucket squeaks behind the curtain, answering the song.  
The singer turns and bows to it; the guard says, "That's too long."  

**Chorus**
Come closer, darling, but please stay behind the line;  
I sing for you at nine o'clock, you never miss a rhyme.  
Wink once if you like the tune, wink twice if you don't;  
The wax man keeps his steady face and naturally he won't.  

**Bridge**
The guard rolls up the rope and checks the display case.  
The singer sings one final note across the empty space.  
She takes her paper cup and leaves the figure's side.  
The wax explorer stays behind with all his pride.  

**Ending**
The spotlight clicks off; the room is still; the mop returns at ten.  
The figure keeps the best seat, waiting for the show again.  
The singer leaves a paper rose outside the rope.  
The guard puts it in the lost box with the evening's hope.
