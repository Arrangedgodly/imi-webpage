# The Pop Idol Autographs a Grocery Receipt

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: pop-idol meet-and-greet sentiment and signatures applied to the wrong piece of paper*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Glossy pop chords and a bright solo voice; the cashier reads the item total during the emotional hook.*

[Verse 1]
A fan approached the singer at the supermarket gate,  
She held a folded piece of paper and apologized for being late.  
The singer signed a flowing name with stars beside the stroke,  
She added "All my love to you" and made a little joke.  
The fan unfolded all the paper and the cashier gave a sigh,  
It was the grocery receipt for goods she'd meant to take nearby.  
The star had signed across the total and the little barcode there,  
The fan needed it for a return, not a souvenir to wear.  

[Hook / Chorus]
All my love, every shining day,  
The cashier needs the total that my signature took away.  
All my love, every little star,  
The fan would like her faulty kettle changed before she goes far.  
Bright ink, warm smile, let the meeting shine,  
The receipt has got a purpose that is larger than my line.  

[Verse 2]
The cashier found the transaction from the card that paid the bill,  
She printed out a clean receipt upon the counter till.  
The fan exchanged the kettle with the clean receipt in hand,  
She kept the signed old copy with the star's bright little brand.  
The singer asked if she had wanted any signature at all,  
The fan said yes, but on a leaflet from the singer's concert hall.  
She held the proper leaflet and the star signed once again,  
The cashier checked the kettle and sent her home again.  

[Hook / Chorus]
All my love, every shining day,  
The cashier needs the total that my signature took away.  
All my love, every little star,  
The fan would like her faulty kettle changed before she goes far.  
Bright ink, warm smile, let the meeting shine,  
The receipt has got a purpose that is larger than my line.  

[Bridge]
The fan displayed the leaflet and the receipt upon her wall,  
The kettles had no place beside the picture from the hall.  
The singer wrote a short song about the stars she'd left that day,  
The cashier heard it on the radio while giving change away.  

[Final Hook / Chorus]
All my love, every little star,  
The leaflet has the signature; the kettle doesn't go far.  
Bright ink, warm smile, let the meeting shine,  
The cashier keeps the clean receipt inside the shop's own line.  
All my love, every shining day,  
The fan makes tea while both signed papers smile from their display.
