# The March of the Mislaid Boots

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: military parade marches, rigid formations, drum-major commands, and a marching band that cannot agree on left and right*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Snare and brass march; change direction only on the drum-major's call, then let the boots drift off-beat.*

Verse 1
The macaques lined up for the town's parade,
Their polished boots shone bright upon the square.
The drum-major raised his baton and they obeyed,
Then one boot rolled away into the air.

Chorus
Left, right, step in line,
Brass and drum will sound just fine.
Left, right, hold your ground,
Find the boot before the round.

Verse 2
The band marched left; the boot rolled to the right,
The trumpets turned around to follow it.
A gibbon drummer kept the tempo tight,
The snare drum chased the boot into a pit.

Chorus
Left, right, step in line,
Brass and drum will sound just fine.
Left, right, hold your ground,
Find the boot before the round.

Bridge
The drum-major shouted, "Halt!" but only half could hear,
The other half kept marching to the bass.
The boot came back and rolled between the rear,
It stopped beneath the mayor's waiting place.

Verse 3
The mayor lifted up the boot and smiled,
It fit his foot, but not the matching pair.
The band re-formed and marched around the child,
Who'd lost the other boot beneath a chair.

Chorus
Left, right, step in line,
Brass and drum will sound just fine.
Left, right, hold your ground,
Find the boot before the round.

Ending
The mayor gave both boots to the child and waved the band along,
The drum-major changed the route to make the marching song.
The boots marched home in pairs, with every heel in line.
