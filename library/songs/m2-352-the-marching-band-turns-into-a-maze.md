# The Marching Band Turns into a Maze

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: marching-band anthems whose elaborate formations become more important than the parade route*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Brass fanfare and rigid snare rhythm; the drum major calls letters that the chorus interprets literally.*

[Verse 1]
We started down the avenue in rows of shining brass,  
The mayor waved beside the gate as all our boots went past.  
The drum major requested we form letters for the crowd,  
We spelled the town's short name and played our welcome anthem loud.  
He called another letter with a flourish of his cane,  
We folded round the fountain and began to march again.  

[Chorus]
Left, right, letter A,  
Find the road and march away.  
Forward, backward, letter B,  
The trumpet row has circled me.  
Play it proudly, play it strong,  
We're spelling out the reason why the parade is taking long.  

[Verse 2]
He asked us for a spiral at the corner by the school,  
The bass drum met the cymbals and the trumpets formed a pool.  
The tubas filled the center, they could hardly turn their bells,  
The flutes played out toward freedom like a flight of tiny shells.  
The major climbed the fountain to admire the whole display,  
He called it perfect symmetry; we asked which way was away.  

[Chorus]
Left, right, letter A,  
Find the road and march away.  
Forward, backward, letter B,  
The trumpet row has circled me.  
Play it proudly, play it strong,  
We're spelling out the reason why the parade is taking long.  

[Bridge]
A schoolchild chalked an arrow from the spiral to the street,  
The flutes followed its direction and the rest regained their feet.  
We marched in one long line and let the final fanfare grow,  
The major left his fountain and ran after us below.  

[Final Chorus]
Left, right, letter A,  
Find the road and march away.  
Forward, backward, letter B,  
There is a space for you beside me.  
Play it proudly, play it strong,  
The major brings a spelling book; the street is not that long.
