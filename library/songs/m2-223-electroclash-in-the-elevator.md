# The Lift Is Listening

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: electroclash deadpan vocals, stark synth beats, and a self-aware elevator that announces every floor like a nightclub*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use a dry vocal over a rigid synth pulse; make each floor chime the only melodic change.*

[Verse 1]
I entered the elevator wearing silver on my sleeve,
The buttons glowed like statements I was not prepared to believe.
A macaque pressed the seventh floor, the lift began to rise,
A synth beat clicked in silence like a look behind the eyes.

[Chorus]
The lift is listening, the lift is awake,
It knows which floor we pressed and what time we take.
Doors open, doors close, the lights remain blue,
The lift has gone one floor; it has noticed you.

[Verse 2]
The gibbon pushed the lobby; the doors refused to part,
The panel played a bass line like a message to the heart.
A capuchin asked for music; the lift replied with "ding,"
The floor number flashed in rhythm and the cables seemed to sing.

[Chorus]
The lift is listening, the lift is awake,
It knows which floor we pressed and what time we take.
Doors open, doors close, the lights remain blue,
The lift has gone one floor; it has noticed you.

[Bridge]
The maintenance light blinked; the elevator stalled,
The occupants stood motionless and waited to be called.
A tech arrived and opened doors; the lift gave one more chime,
It reached the lobby thirty seconds after closing time.

[Final Chorus]
The lift is listening, the lift is awake,
It knows which floor we pressed and what time we take.
Doors open, doors close, we step into the blue,
The lift has gone nowhere; its shift is through.

[Ending]
The lobby button blinked alone until the morning crew.
