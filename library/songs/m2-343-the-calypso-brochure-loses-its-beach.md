# The Calypso Brochure Loses Its Beach

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sunny tourism songs whose promises exceed the attractions visible from the resort*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Steel pans, buoyant bass and a cheerful guide; the visitors answer each promise with a practical question.*

[Verse 1]
The brochure showed a golden shore, a palm and azure bay,  
Our guide picked up his little drum and danced us all that way.  
He led us through the laundry yard and past a painted wall,  
The shore was on a mural that was eighteen inches tall.  
He pointed to the palm tree in a pot beside the door,  
And told us that the ocean was an easy mile or more.  

[Chorus]
Welcome, welcome, sunshine in your hand,  
Our brochure is a passport to the loveliest of sand.  
Turn the page and feel the breeze,  
The laundry fans are free for guests who please.  
Welcome, welcome, swim and stay,  
The picture is included but the beach is far away.  

[Verse 2]
We asked about the waterfall beside the royal suite,  
He took us to a gutter where the roof and paving meet.  
We asked about the sunset from the panoramic deck,  
He moved a drying towel and asked us kindly not to check.  
He sang about the island's world-renowned tropical fruit,  
The cook brought out one apple in a tiny sailor suit.  

[Chorus]
Welcome, welcome, sunshine in your hand,  
Our brochure is a passport to the loveliest of sand.  
Turn the page and feel the breeze,  
The laundry fans are free for guests who please.  
Welcome, welcome, swim and stay,  
The picture is included but the beach is far away.  

[Bridge]
We found a public bus and took our towels to the shore,  
The guide came running after us; he'd never been before.  
He took his shoes off gingerly and held his little drum,  
The waves supplied a rhythm and the visitors began to hum.  

[Final Chorus]
Welcome, welcome, sunshine in your hand,  
Our bus ticket's a passport to the loveliest of sand.  
Turn the page and feel the breeze,  
No one needs a laundry fan beneath these trees.  
Welcome, welcome, swim and stay,  
The guide folds up his brochure and asks where to spend the day.
