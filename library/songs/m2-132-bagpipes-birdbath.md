# Bagpipes at the Birdbath

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Scottish bagpipe march conventions, drone notes, and a flock of birds that mistakes the practice tune for a water schedule*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Bagpipe drone and measured snare; keep the march dignified while the birds create their own timetable.*

Verse 1
The bagpiper marched at sunrise to the garden's little square,
He played beside the birdbath with a feather in his hair.
The drone note rolled across the lawn; the snare began to beat,
The sparrows lined the basin up as if they had a seat.

Chorus
March on, drone on, keep the line,
Birds will gather right on time.
Step in measure, play it clear,
The water is not marching here.

Verse 2
The pigeons came in pairs and formed a circle by the stone,
The piper took a leftward turn; the pigeons marched alone.
A macaque filled the basin and the birds began to cheer,
The piper stopped his march to ask if water would appear.

Chorus
March on, drone on, keep the line,
Birds will gather right on time.
Step in measure, play it clear,
The water is not marching here.

Bridge
The flock mistook the drumbeat for a signal to depart,
They flew to the roof in formation, then returned to start.
The piper lowered both his pipes; the birdbath stood quite still,
The birds came down for water on their own and drank their fill.

Verse 3
The piper played a gentle tune with no parade in sight,
The birds stayed by the basin till the morning turned to light.
The macaque moved the snare drum far beyond the garden gate,
The piper marched a smaller loop and let the birds be late.

Chorus
March on, drone on, keep the line,
Birds will gather right on time.
Step in measure, play it clear,
The water is not marching here.

Ending
The bagpipes closed, the birds took flight, the basin held the sky,
The drum stayed by the gate where it could not order birds to fly.
