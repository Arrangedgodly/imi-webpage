# Surf Rock at Low Tide

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: surf-rock guitar reverb, beach-party swagger, and the heroic ride of a tiny wave*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Reverb-heavy guitar and snare; wave sounds swell bigger than the actual water.*

Verse 1
A young macaque waxed his board beneath a blazing sun,
The beach band hit a reverb chord and cried, "Here comes the run!"
He paddled out with fearless eyes; the tide was ankle deep,
A crab climbed up the board and claimed the front seat.

Chorus
Ride that curl and shake the sand,
Surf the world's most shallow land.
Guitar rings and seagulls cry,
Tiny waves can touch the sky.

Verse 2
The drummer rolled a thunder fill; the singer roared, "A swell!"
A ripple nudged the board along, then broke beside a shell.
The crowd went wild; the guitarist bent the note so wide,
The lifeguard raised a warning flag for one inch of tide.

Chorus
Ride that curl and shake the sand,
Surf the world's most shallow land.
Guitar rings and seagulls cry,
Tiny waves can touch the sky.

Bridge
The crab began to tap its claws; the band mistook the beat,
They built a surf anthem for the creature's retreat.
The macaque posed on dry sand as if he'd crossed the sea,
The photographer sold postcards of his brave humility.

Verse 3
The tide came back at sunset with a ripple to his knee,
The band declared a comeback tour and booked a second sea.
He rode the foam three yards and landed near the snack shack,
The crab took his board home and never gave it back.

Chorus
Ride that curl and shake the sand,
Surf the world's most shallow land.
Guitar rings and seagulls cry,
Tiny waves can touch the sky.

Ending
The band played on the beach while the board was out of sight;
The crab sold surf lessons for a shell each, every night.
