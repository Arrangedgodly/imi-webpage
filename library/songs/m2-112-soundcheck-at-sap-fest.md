# Soundcheck at the Sap Fest

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: festival soundchecks, monitor-mix complaints, and a drummer who can only hear himself through the bass amp*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Festival rock band builds from a spoken mic check to a full chorus; make feedback a short comic squeal, never a sustained tone.*

Verse 1
The festival stage was ready by the old sap tree,
A gibbon checked the microphone: "Can you hear me? One, two, three."
The drummer heard his cymbals; the bassist heard a hum,
The singer heard the vendors shouting, "Fresh sap, here we come!"

Chorus
Check the left, check the right,
Find the sound and set it right.
One, two, three, the band is near,
Now the audience can hear.

Verse 2
The guitar was loud onstage, but the monitor was low,
The drummer asked for more of him; the stagehand answered, "No."
The bassist asked for less of bass; the singer wanted drums,
The mixer moved one slider and the feedback squealed like gums.

Chorus
Check the left, check the right,
Find the sound and set it right.
One, two, three, the band is near,
Now the audience can hear.

Bridge
They muted every channel, then the crowd began to clap,
The band could hear the rhythm through the soles of every tap.
The mixer set the levels low; the instruments fell in,
The drummer counted four clean beats and started up again.

Verse 3
The first song rolled across the grove; the sap cups stayed upright,
The singer's voice was clear at last, the bass sat warm and light.
The drummer heard the whole band and grinned beneath the lights,
The soundcheck ended; now the music filled the night.

Chorus
Check the left, check the right,
Find the sound and set it right.
One, two, three, the band is near,
Now the audience can hear.

Ending
The band played on, the mixer smiled, the feedback disappeared,
The only squeal was from a squirrel who had the monitor ear.
