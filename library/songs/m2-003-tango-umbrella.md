# Umbrella Tango at Closing

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: tango orchestras and staged romantic tension*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: A bandoneon leads, with deliberate pauses and one umbrella passing between partners.*

## Verse 1
Rain sealed the doors at the Plaza del Sol.  
Two spider monkeys stood by the brass coat pole.  
He held a black umbrella like a sealed decree.  
She wore red boots and said, "May I borrow three?"  
The bandoneon sighed as the café lights grew dim.  
He offered one dry corner, then she offered him a grin.  
Their shoes drew careful commas on the tile.  
The clock forgot its business for a while.  

## Chorus
Step close, step back, let the dark rain fall.  
A little room can hold a city's wall.  
Step close, step back, then turn beneath the light.  
We borrowed one umbrella and prolonged the night.  

## Verse 2
A waiter stacked the saucers in a tower by the door.  
The owner locked the register and swept across the floor.  
Still they moved in measured arcs around the tables' edge.  
He bowed as if the sidewalk were a cliff's sharp ledge.  
She took the umbrella handle, led him toward the street.  
The rain kept time in silver taps beneath their feet.  
A taxi slowed; the driver watched them turn.  
Then drove on slowly, with his meter left to burn.  

## Bridge
"I live three blocks away," she said, "the road is clear."  
He said, "My bus leaves now," then did not disappear.  
The band packed up; one violin stayed behind.  
It played a final note that neither tried to bind.  

## Chorus
Step close, step back, let the dark rain fall.  
A little room can hold a city's wall.  
Step close, step back, then turn beneath the light.  
We borrowed one umbrella and prolonged the night.  

## Ending
At dawn they returned it to the café stand.  
The handle bore two names in careful charcoal hand.
