# The Farewell Folk Singer Misses the Tip Jar

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: folk club farewell songs and singers whose prolonged emotional leave-taking allows the collection jar to leave first*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Gentle acoustic strumming and unison refrain; a volunteer carries the tip jar toward the office during the last extra verse.*

[Verse 1]
I sang a closing ballad at the folk club by the lane,  
I thanked the lovely listeners and promised back again.  
The volunteer took round the jar before the final chord,  
The people put their little coins and notes beside the board.  
I sang about a farewell that could last beyond the rain,  
The volunteer went to the office counting what remained.  
I reached for the collection at the end of verse thirteen,  
The jar had left the room before my farewell could be seen.  

[Refrain]
Fare thee well, hold the last small tune,  
The jar has reached the office underneath the rising moon.  
Fare thee well, let the warm notes stay,  
The volunteer has counted what the crowd has given away.  
Homeward road, gentle voice, let the evening bend,  
The club would like the farewell to arrive at its own end.  

[Verse 2]
The host asked for the closing chord before the chairs went back,  
I gave a four-bar thank-you and I put the guitar in its pack.  
The volunteer came out again with an envelope in hand,  
She said the tips were counted, with my name upon the band.  
I took the little envelope and helped to stack the chairs,  
The host locked up the folk club after checking all the stairs.  
We walked out to the rainy road beneath the club's small light,  
The volunteer said thirteen verses made a very long goodnight.  

[Refrain]
Fare thee well, hold the last small tune,  
The jar has reached the office underneath the rising moon.  
Fare thee well, let the warm notes stay,  
The volunteer has counted what the crowd has given away.  
Homeward road, gentle voice, let the evening bend,  
The club would like the farewell to arrive at its own end.  

[Bridge]
I wrote a shorter ballad for the booking the next week,  
The host heard it at sound check and had nothing else to speak.  
The volunteer marked down the time the closing chord would fall,  
She set the jar beside me at the back of the little hall.  

[Final Refrain]
Fare thee well, hold the last small tune,  
The shorter song has given us an earlier rising moon.  
Homeward road, gentle voice, let the evening bend,  
The jar stays for the final chord and leaves me at the end.  
Fare thee well, let the warm notes stay,  
The tips have bought my umbrella for the homeward rainy way.
