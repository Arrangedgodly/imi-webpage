# Punk Protest at the Lawn Sale

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: punk protest anthems, shouted slogans, and the DIY band that turns every object into a cause*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Fast distorted guitar and shouted gang vocals; the auctioneer keeps interrupting the chorus.*

Verse 1
Three macaques formed a punk band at a neighbor's lawn sale,
They saw a chair marked "Five bananas" and declared the price a jail.
The singer grabbed a megaphone and yelled, "Resist the seat!"
The auctioneer rang his bell and stomped the offbeat.

Chorus
No more prices! No more bids!
Give the chair back to the kids!
Kick the dust and shout it loud,
We are punk and we are proud!

Verse 2
The drummer found a saucepan; the guitarist found a rake,
They made a wall of feedback that could rattle every plate.
A lemur tried to join the chant but bought a lamp instead,
The band accused the lamp of being corporate in its shade.

Chorus
No more prices! No more bids!
Give the chair back to the kids!
Kick the dust and shout it loud,
We are punk and we are proud!

Bridge
The seller said, "It's free if you can carry it away."
The singer froze mid-slogan; the band had nothing left to say.
They staged a sit-in on the chair and claimed the lawn as land,
The buyer picked it up and walked off with the whole band.

Verse 3
They played from the sidewalk, shouting for the chair,
The seller gave them one old stool and said, "Please share."
They called it a victory, then passed it through the crowd,
A hundred fans sat down at once and sang the chorus loud.

Chorus
No more prices! No more bids!
Give the chair back to the kids!
Kick the dust and shout it loud,
We are punk and we are proud!

Ending
The band sold tickets to the sit-in and charged the seller rent;
The stool became their tour bus, by unanimous consent.
