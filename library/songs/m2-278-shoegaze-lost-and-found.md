# The Shoegaze Song at the Lost-and-Found

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: shoegaze walls of guitar effects, buried vocals, and dreamy indistinct lyrics*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Layer hazy guitar pedals and soft vocals until the singer's words disappear; make the lost shoe the only clear object onstage.*

[Verse 1]
A singer came to lost-and-found in foggy purple light,  
His guitar had seven pedals and a cable looped up tight.  
He set a wall of sound across the counter's little space,  
Then sang a quiet lyric with his chin toward his lace.  
A clerk held up a sneaker; the singer nodded slow,  
The guitar made a soft distortion like a far-off radio.  
The clerk asked, "Is this yours?" but all the vowels were drowned,  
The shoe sat on a velvet box while notes rolled round.  

[Chorus]
Fade away, let the guitars bloom,  
A shoe is waiting in the haze of the room.  
Mumble the line, let the pedals shine,  
The lost-and-found has perfect time.  
Fade away, no words need show,  
The sneaker is the clearest thing we know.  

[Verse 2]
The singer turned the chorus knob; the lights became a blur,  
The clerk repeated every question with a courteous slur.  
A capuchin brought a second shoe and placed it by the first,  
The singer struck a tremolo; the chorus almost burst.  
The pedals hummed like distant rain; the singer barely moved,  
The pair of shoes sat side by side, their ownership improved.  
He checked the label inside one and found his name in ink,  
The singer nodded slowly as the guitar began to sink.  

[Chorus]
Fade away, let the guitars bloom,  
A shoe is waiting in the haze of the room.  
Mumble the line, let the pedals shine,  
The lost-and-found has perfect time.  
Fade away, no words need show,  
The sneaker is the clearest thing we know.  

[Bridge]
The clerk unplugged one pedal; the words came into view,  
The singer said, "Those are mine," and took the shoes in two.  
The band played one bare chord without a layer wide,  
The clerk heard him say "thank you" from the counter side.  

[Final Chorus]
Fade away, the gig is through,  
The shoes are found and laced up too.  
Mumble no more, the pedals sleep,  
The clerk has records they will keep.  
Fade away, then walk on slow,  
The sneaker is the clearest thing we know.  

[Ending]
The singer left in both his shoes; one pedal kept the echo.
