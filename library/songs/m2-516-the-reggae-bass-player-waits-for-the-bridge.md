# The Reggae Bass Player Waits for the Bridge

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: laid-back reggae grooves and performers who interpret a literal raised bridge as another musical section*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Offbeat guitar, warm bass and relaxed vocal; the bridge keeper supplies a plain answer over the held groove.*

[Verse 1]
We crossed the harbor road to play the summer afternoon,  
The bassist walked beside us humming all the opening tune.  
The footbridge lifted slowly for a boat to leave the bay,  
We stood beside the keeper while the water took its way.  
The singer said the bridge was where the melody could grow,  
The keeper said this one went up to let the vessels go.  
The bassist played a little line upon his unplugged strings,  
The singer gave the waiting time some very musical things.  

[Refrain]
Wait for the bridge, let the low notes roll,  
The bridge has got a boat beneath its very iron soul.  
Wait for the bridge, let the warm chords stay,  
The keeper has a timetable that gets us on our way.  
Easy feet, steady beat, hold the harbor air,  
The musical bridge has nothing but a chorus after there.  

[Verse 2]
The boat passed through the narrow gap and turned toward the sea,  
The keeper lowered the bridge again and gave the path to me.  
We carried all the instruments across the wooden span,  
The bassist put his little line into the waiting band's plan.  
We reached the hall with time enough to set the speakers right,  
The bridge became a middle verse before the summer night.  
The keeper came to listen when his afternoon was done,  
He said he'd crossed two bridges and the musical one was fun.  

[Refrain]
Wait for the bridge, let the low notes roll,  
The bridge has got a boat beneath its very iron soul.  
Wait for the bridge, let the warm chords stay,  
The keeper has a timetable that gets us on our way.  
Easy feet, steady beat, hold the harbor air,  
The musical bridge has nothing but a chorus after there.  

[Bridge]
We held the little bass line as the singer took a breath,  
The keeper counted measures with the pocket watch he'd kept.  
The singer asked if this bridge ought to rise or ought to fall,  
The keeper said the boat had left; the music could do it all.  

[Final Refrain]
Wait for the bridge, let the warm chords stay,  
The keeper takes the harbor road at closing time today.  
Easy feet, steady beat, hold the harbor air,  
The boat is far beyond the bay; the wooden span is there.  
Wait for the bridge, let the low notes roll,  
The keeper names his next boat after our middle eight's small soul.
