# The Synthpop Star Keeps His Floppy Disk

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: nostalgic synthpop songs treating obsolete storage media as tokens of eternal love*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Bright analog synths and gated drums; the disk drive chatter replaces the percussion in the bridge.*

[Verse 1]
We met beneath the neon in a shop of little screens,  
You saved our first duet beside a folder labeled dreams.  
I kept the little disk inside the pocket of my coat,  
The label held your handwriting and half a tender note.  
The city changed its colors and the shops changed every year,  
I carried all our music but I couldn't make it clear.  

[Chorus]
Spin for me, blue plastic heart,  
Keep the song that knew us from the start.  
Read my love from track to track,  
Give that little melody back.  
The world has moved to clouds above,  
I've got one point four megabytes of love.  

[Verse 2]
I brought you to the studio to play our song at last,  
The engineer had sockets from a very different past.  
He found a box of adapters in a drawer below the desk,  
The disk sat in my fingers like a small defeated quest.  
You said you could remember how the chorus used to go,  
I said the disk would tell us if the little light would glow.  

[Chorus]
Spin for me, blue plastic heart,  
Keep the song that knew us from the start.  
Read my love from track to track,  
Give that little melody back.  
The world has moved to clouds above,  
I've got one point four megabytes of love.  

[Bridge]
The engineer unearthed a drive and clicked the disk inside,  
It chattered like a tiny train on which our memories ride.  
The file appeared upon the screen; we leaned to hear the track,  
It held a shopping list for milk and paint we never took back.  

[Final Chorus]
Spin for me, blue plastic heart,  
Keep the song that knew us from the start.  
Read my love from track to track,  
Give that little melody back.  
The world has moved to clouds above,  
You sing the tune while I buy the milk, my love.
