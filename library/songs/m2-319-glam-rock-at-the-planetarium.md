# Glam Rock at the Planetarium

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: glam-rock spectacle and planetarium presenters who prioritize star personas over the actual constellations*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Glittering guitar, theatrical pose and stomping beat; the astronomer keeps pointing at the stars while the band points at itself.*

**Verse 1**
The planetarium darkens; the projector paints the sky.  
A glam-rock band arrives with silver boots and eyeliner high.  
The astronomer says, "There is Orion on the screen."  
The singer strikes a pose and blocks the constellation clean.  
The drummer counts a giant fill; the stars all disappear.  
The guide asks for the audience to turn their heads from here.  

**Chorus**
Shine on, shine on, glitter through the night;  
The star chart's in the corner, but the band is taking light.  
Point up, pose down, let the planets roll;  
The cosmic rock show has eclipsed the telescope.  

**Verse 2**
The singer names a zodiac and asks the crowd to cheer.  
The astronomer corrects the date; the band can't hear.  
A spotlight sweeps the dome and makes a comet look like flame.  
The guide says, "That is Saturn," then corrects the claim.  
The guitarist leans into a solo, boots against the rail.  
The planetarium operator dims the stage; the solo fails.  

**Chorus**
Shine on, shine on, glitter through the night;  
The star chart's in the corner, but the band is taking light.  
Point up, pose down, let the planets roll;  
The cosmic rock show has eclipsed the telescope.  

**Bridge**
They lower the amps; the planet projector turns.  
The audience sees the moon and every crater burns.  
The band removes its glitter, takes a seat in the back.  
The astronomer points out the stars along their track.  

**Ending**
The show ends with a quiet chord; the stars remain in view.  
The band takes one small bow; the guide says, "Thank you."  
The silver boots leave the dome, the lights come on at last.  
The astronomer resets the sky; the guitar case is latched.
