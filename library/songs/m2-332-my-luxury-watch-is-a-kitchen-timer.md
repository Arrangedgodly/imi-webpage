# My Luxury Watch Is a Kitchen Timer

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: luxury-brag rap in which every coveted possession has an embarrassingly practical origin*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Sparse trap beat, deep bass and a timer beep replacing every boast's last syllable.*

[Verse 1]
I arrive at the gala with a circle on my wrist,  
Everybody wants to know which jeweler made it this.  
I say the finest craftsmen put the numbers round the face,  
The strap is from an apron but I wear it with some grace.  
I flash it at the cameras while my velvet jacket gleams,  
The editor requests the brand that sponsors all my dreams.  
I tell him it's exclusive, only kitchens keep it now,  
He writes that down as fashion and the crowd begins to bow.  

[Chorus]
Tick, tick, look at my shine,  
Every single minute is entirely mine.  
Big face, steel case, luxury time,  
The bell on my wrist says the pasta's fine.  
Tick, tick, gather round,  
My status makes a very recognizable sound.  

[Verse 2]
The dealer in the corner has a watch worth half a street,  
He asks about the movement and its power-saving feat.  
I show the little winding knob, he calls it clean design,  
I turn it back to twenty and pretend the choice is mine.  
The photographer adjusts the light to catch the polished rim,  
My watch begins to ring and all the cameras turn to him.  
I say it's my assistant calling down from a private suite,  
The caterer comes over with a pan of something sweet.  

[Chorus]
Tick, tick, look at my shine,  
Every single minute is entirely mine.  
Big face, steel case, luxury time,  
The bell on my wrist says the pasta's fine.  
Tick, tick, gather round,  
My status makes a very recognizable sound.  

[Bridge]
He asks me for his timer back; the party hears the claim,  
The dealer tries to buy it with a personalized name.  
I point them to the kitchen shop; they rush out in a line,  
I give the cook his timer and he gives me something fine.  

[Final Chorus]
Tick, tick, look at my shine,  
Every single minute is entirely mine.  
Big plate, hot cake, luxury dine,  
The bell on his counter says dessert is fine.  
Tick, tick, gather round,  
The cook pipes me a cake crown; I wear it upside down.
