# One Note Until the Bus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: minimalist piano repetition, gradual variation, and dramatic pauses mistaken for transit updates*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Keep the piano spare; let each added sound alter the pattern, then leave a clean final rest.*

[Verse 1]
At the stop, a macaque named Sol placed one finger on a key,
A single note, repeated slow, beside the route sign three.
The timetable said the bus was late, the bench was cold and wide;
He played the note again and watched a pigeon change its side.

[Refrain]
Wait, one note, wait, one note, let the quiet hold its place.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, let the bus arrive with grace.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, hear the small delay expand.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, keep your ticket in your hand.


[Verse 2]
A second key came in at noon, a semitone away;
Sol said, "That is the bus approaching," though it was the wind at play.
The crossing light began to click; he wrote it in the score.
A brake hiss from the avenue became a moving door.

[Refrain]
Wait, one note, wait, one note, let the quiet hold its place.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, let the bus arrive with grace.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, hear the small delay expand.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, keep your ticket in your hand.


[Bridge]
The bus rolled by, not theirs, but Sol put in a rest.
The passengers admired how silence sounded best.
He lifted both his hands; the crowd leaned in to hear.
The driver braked and opened doors: the bus was finally here.

[Verse 3]
Sol played the arrival as a chord of keys and coins;
The doors shut on the final note, but not on Sol's rejoins.
He tapped the rail; the driver smiled and let the piano ride.
The bench stayed at the stop alone, with one note left inside.

[Final Refrain]
Wait, one note, wait, one note, now the quiet has its place.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, roll the piano at bus pace.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, let the little rests expand.
Wait, one note, wait, one note, Sol is playing with one hand.


[Ending]
The driver asked for silence. Sol played the ticket punch instead.
