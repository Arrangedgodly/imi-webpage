# Tape Hiss Summer

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: nostalgic synth pop and wistful memories of old media*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
The cassette clicked inside the orange deck.  
The tape had warbled round the mountain bend.  
Your voice said, "Meet me where the bridge begins."  
Then static swallowed half the sentence's end.  
We wore our headphones like a pair of wings.  
The sidewalk shimmered under summer heat.  
The batteries were low, the sky was wide.  
We walked until the town forgot the beat.  
My lemur friend kept rhythm with his tail.  
The tape hiss answered, thin and frail.  

## Chorus
Rewind the hiss, let the old lights glow.  
Press play and hear the years move slow.  
The song was crooked, but we knew the tune.  
We held our summer in a plastic moon.  

## Verse 2
The bridge was closed for repairs that week.  
We sat beside a chain link sign.  
You brought a snack wrapped in a map.  
I brought a mixtape labeled "Maybe Mine."  
The pencil turned the spool by hand.  
One sleeve was cracked along its seam.  
You laughed when the chorus ran backward.  
I called the glitch a secret dream.  
The troop rolled skateboards down the hill.  
We watched the old tape, holding still.  

## Chorus
Rewind the hiss, let the old lights glow.  
Press play and hear the years move slow.  
The song was crooked, but we knew the tune.  
We held our summer in a plastic moon.  

## Bridge
The tape unspooled across our knees.  
We wound it round a borrowed pen.  
The sun dropped low behind the mill.  
The radio found its signal again.  
You said the bridge would open soon.  
I said I'd bring a better deck.  
The streetlights came on one by one.  
We packed the snacks and checked the deck.  

## Final chorus
Rewind the hiss, let the old lights glow.  
Press play and hear the years move slow.  
The tape still catches, the wires still hum.  
We'll cross that bridge when the crews are done.  
Rewind the hiss, but leave the song.  
Some little flaws can carry us along.  
The road was dusty, the night was blue.  
The mixtape played, and I walked with you.  

## Ending
The orange deck lives in a drawer.  
Its buttons stick; it asks for more.  
The tape still hums when nights run long.  
We know the words; we sing along.
