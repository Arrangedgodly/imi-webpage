# The Electronic Ballad Saves the Wrong File

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: emotional electronic ballads and composers confusing an elaborate working project with a listenable exported song*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Soft synth chords and a close vocal; dry computer notifications interrupt the attempted listening party.*

[Verse 1]
I wrote a song about the night you left the little town,  
A hundred tracks of memories with all the faders down.  
I sent the file to everyone to hear the final tune,  
Its name said "Last Goodbye" beside a picture of the moon.  
My friend clicked on the attachment and her screen asked for a key,  
The file was just the project and it couldn't play for free.  
She didn't have my music software or the sounds I'd bought,  
My great goodbye had sent her all the equipment that it sought.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Open my heart, open the file,  
The project needs a hundred sounds before it has a smile.  
Hear my last goodbye, let the little speakers glow,  
The attachment has a session where the song ought to go.  
Warm chord, cold screen, hold the final line,  
My friend would like a version that can play at half past nine.  

[Verse 2]
I opened up the session and I checked the export range,  
I brought the faders up to where the final mix could change.  
I made a simple audio file with all the parts inside,  
I played it on my phone to hear the song from either side.  
I sent the new attachment with the same small moon above,  
My friend could hear the melody and all its little love.  
She wrote a warm reply about the places in the song,  
The software stayed inside my room where all its sounds belong.  

[Chorus / Hook]
Open my heart, open the file,  
The exported song has found a way to make you smile.  
Hear my last goodbye, let the little speakers glow,  
The rendered track is ready; now the feeling gets to go.  
Warm chord, clear sound, hold the final line,  
My friend can hear the whole thing right at half past nine.  

[Bridge]
I saved the little audio file beneath the name "To Send,"  
The session stayed in "Working" where my plans could never end.  
My friend played it for the person who had left the little town,  
He said the song was clear; no plug-in dragged it down.  

[Final Chorus]
Open my heart, open the file,  
The audio has every sound to carry it a mile.  
Warm chord, cold screen, hold the final line,  
The song has reached its listener with no software left to find.  
Hear my last goodbye, let the little speakers glow,  
He asks me for the instrumental; I know which file should go.
