# The Glam Rock Star Borrows a Full-Length Mirror

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: glam rock dressing-room rituals and stars unable to inspect an enormous costume in a tiny mirror*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Stomping glam-rock beat, glittering guitar and a theatrical vocal; the hotel porter speaks during the bridge.*

[Verse 1]
I wore my silver platform boots and cape of purple rain,  
A star-shaped collar reached beyond the bedroom windowpane.  
The dressing room had one small mirror set above the sink,  
I saw one cheek and half my hair and had no room to think.  
I stepped back to see the boots and hit the little door,  
The collar filled the mirror and the boots stayed on the floor.  
I called the hotel porter for a bigger piece of glass,  
He said the lobby mirror let the entire person pass.  

[Chorus]
Shine on, silver star, let the whole world see,  
The little mirror holds a very tiny piece of me.  
Shine on, purple cape, spread the brilliant night,  
The boots would like a moment in the same reflected light.  
High heel, bright hair, give the room a glow,  
The costume needs a lobby if the whole thing's going to show.  

[Verse 2]
I walked down to the lobby with my cape above my feet,  
The porter held the lift door for the collar and its sweep.  
The mirror showed my silver boots, my hair and all my face,  
It also showed the cape had caught a towel from the place.  
The porter took the towel off and hung it by the desk,  
I thanked him for the practical addition to my dress.  
A guest asked for a photograph with me beside the glass,  
The towel got a little corner in the picture as she passed.  

[Chorus]
Shine on, silver star, let the whole world see,  
The little mirror holds a very tiny piece of me.  
Shine on, purple cape, spread the brilliant night,  
The boots would like a moment in the same reflected light.  
High heel, bright hair, give the room a glow,  
The costume needs a lobby if the whole thing's going to show.  

[Bridge]
The concert had a mirrorball and lights above the band,  
I spread the cape quite carefully with nothing in my hand.  
The porter watched the picture reach the hotel's little page,  
The towel had more comments than my entrance on the stage.  

[Final Chorus]
Shine on, silver star, spread the brilliant night,  
The hotel sells a towel with my picture printed bright.  
High heel, bright hair, give the room a glow,  
I ask the porter for a cut of what the towels show.  
Shine on, purple cape, let the whole world see,  
The porter offers one clean towel as my royalty.
