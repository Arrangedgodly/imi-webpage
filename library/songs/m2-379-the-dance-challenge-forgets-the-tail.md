# The Dance Challenge Forgets the Tail

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: viral dance singles designed for a tiny camera frame that crops away half the choreography*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Bright dance-pop, handclaps and a chant hook; the tail's tapping adds an accidental extra beat.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque made a dance beside a phone upon a stand,  
He snapped his fingers twice and drew a circle with his hand.  
His tail performed a brilliant sweep and landed on a drum,  
The phone showed just his shoulders and the smile that said to come.  
The viewers learned the fingers and the little shoulder roll,  
The tail supplied a hidden beat that nobody could know.  

[Chorus]
Snap it twice and roll it round,  
Bring your favorite dance to town.  
Hands up high and shoulders low,  
There's a little extra part the camera doesn't show.  
Snap it twice and count to three,  
The rhythm comes from somewhere that the viewers cannot see.  

[Verse 2]
The gibbons tried his shoulder move without the tapping tail,  
The drummer watched the copied moves and wondered why they failed.  
A capuchin behind the camera saw the missing sweep,  
She moved the phone much farther back to show what it would keep.  
The wide shot caught the little drum and every tapping beat,  
The viewers copied all the moves but stood on different feet.  

[Chorus]
Snap it twice and roll it round,  
Bring your favorite dance to town.  
Hands up high and shoulders low,  
There's a little extra part the camera doesn't show.  
Snap it twice and count to three,  
The rhythm comes from somewhere that the viewers cannot see.  

[Bridge]
The gibbons found a wooden spoon to tap upon the floor,  
The lemurs used a cushion and requested something more.  
The macaque watched their versions and approved each little sound,  
He took the phone off portrait mode and filmed the whole dance round.  

[Final Chorus]
Snap it twice and roll it round,  
Bring your favorite dance to town.  
Hands up high and shoulders low,  
The camera shows the little part we needed all to know.  
Snap it twice and count to three,  
The whole dance goes viral, tail and drum for all to see.
