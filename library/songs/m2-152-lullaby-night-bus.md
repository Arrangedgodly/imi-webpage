# Lullaby for the Night Bus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bedtime lullabies and soothing repetition stretched across an unreliable night route*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Gentle rocking meter with soft bells; each missed stop adds another sleepy harmony.*

Verse 1
The night bus rolled through sleepy streets beneath a velvet moon,
A lemur driver hummed the route's most comforting tune.
The passengers tucked scarves beneath their heads to rest,
The sign above the windshield read, "The shortest route is best."

Chorus
Hush now, wheels, and hum along,
Every stop can be a song.
Close your eyes, the lights are low,
We'll be where we're meant to go.

Verse 2
The bus slid past the station as the chorus drifted by,
A macaque woke to ask the driver, "Was that my stop nearby?"
The driver sang, "Perhaps it was," and rocked the steering wheel,
The passengers all nodded off and missed the next one still.

Chorus
Hush now, wheels, and hum along,
Every stop can be a song.
Close your eyes, the lights are low,
We'll be where we're meant to go.

Bridge
The route map had a lullaby printed over every street,
The bass line made the turn signal sound too soft to beat.
A gibbon at the back stood up and pulled the stop-request,
The bell rang like a tiny harp; the driver sang the rest.

Verse 3
They stopped beside a bakery at dawn's first golden light,
The baker opened up and found a bus full of the night.
They'd circled town for hours; each rider had slept well,
The driver got a standing ovation, then missed the depot bell.

Chorus
Hush now, wheels, and hum along,
Every stop can be a song.
Close your eyes, the lights are low,
We'll be where we're meant to go.

Ending
They reached the final station when the bakery closed at ten;
The driver sang the lullaby and drove them round again.
