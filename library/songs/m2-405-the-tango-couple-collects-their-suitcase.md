# The Tango Couple Collects Their Suitcase

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: intense tango duets whose dramatic pursuit and close choreography fit an airport baggage carousel too well*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Bandoneon, clipped piano and alternating passionate vocals; the carousel's steady rotation controls every lunge.*

[Verse 1]
We landed with our costumes for a show across the bay,  
The baggage belt began to turn beneath a board of gray.  
My partner saw our red case on the distant moving bend,  
She took my hand and stepped toward it as if the dance began.  
I pivoted around her while she reached across the rail,  
The case went past our fingertips; our first embrace had failed.  
We followed down the carousel in one dramatic stride,  
A stroller blocked the passage and the case went round the side.  

[Chorus]
Turn with me, hold me near,  
The suitcase has a ticket but it cannot volunteer.  
Step with me, make your claim,  
The belt has brought our costumes round to tease us once again.  
Turn with me, catch the red,  
The travelers are watching every solemn step we've tread.  

[Verse 2]
We chose an empty section and we waited by the rail,  
My partner draped her shawl aside; I watched the red case sail.  
A second red case passed us and I gripped its little strap,  
She checked the tag and stopped me; someone's trousers traveled back.  
Our case arrived behind it with the ribbon we had tied,  
We lifted it together in a very graceful slide.  
The waiting crowd applauded as we set it by our feet,  
My partner struck the ending pose; I bowed beside the wheels.  

[Chorus]
Turn with me, hold me near,  
The suitcase has a ticket but it cannot volunteer.  
Step with me, make your claim,  
The belt has brought our costumes round to tease us once again.  
Turn with me, catch the red,  
The travelers are watching every solemn step we've tread.  

[Bridge]
We rolled it toward the exit for the taxi to the show,  
Its handle caught my coat tail and would not let me go.  
My partner freed the fabric with a sharp and practiced turn,  
The crowd demanded one more dance; we had a fee to earn.  

[Final Chorus]
Turn with me, hold me near,  
The suitcase rolls between us like a third performer here.  
Step with me, make your claim,  
Tonight we give the red case its own credit and a name.  
Turn with me, bow your head,  
The airport books our baggage dance; the carousel gets paid.
