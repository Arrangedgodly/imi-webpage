# Reel by the Root Cellar

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Irish reel conventions, fast fiddle ornamentation, and a dancing macaque whose shoes lag behind the tune*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Fiddle and bodhran accelerate through each reel; heel taps should fall just behind the strings.*

Verse 1
The fiddler started reeling at the root cellar door,
A macaque tied her dancing shoes and asked to hear one more.
The bodhran tapped a quick pulse; the fiddle took to flight,
Her left shoe reached the beat, her right arrived a stride behind.

Chorus
Reel, reel, turn around,
Let the fiddle lead the sound.
Tap the heel and catch the air,
Keep the rhythm in your pair.

Verse 2
She spun past crates of potatoes and a basket full of peas,
The fiddler played an ornament that rustled all the leaves.
The left shoe kept a steady step; the right one lost its lace,
It bounced into the cellar and went rolling out of place.

Chorus
Reel, reel, turn around,
Let the fiddle lead the sound.
Tap the heel and catch the air,
Keep the rhythm in your pair.

Bridge
The drummer held the reel in time while everyone looked down,
A gibbon found the missing lace beside the cider cask.
The macaque tied it in a bow; the strings returned to speed,
The right shoe caught the final turn and joined the dancer's lead.

Verse 3
They danced around the cellar till the lanterns glowed like gold,
The fiddle slowed to walking pace; the story had been told.
The macaque bowed, the shoes stayed tied, the drums gave one last roll,
The fiddler closed the cellar door and put away the bowl.

Chorus
Reel, reel, turn around,
Let the fiddle lead the sound.
Tap the heel and catch the air,
Keep the rhythm in your pair.

Ending
The shoes went home together, though one toe tapped once more,
The fiddle played a quiet reel beside the cellar door.
