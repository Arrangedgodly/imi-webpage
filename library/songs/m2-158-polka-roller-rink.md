# Polka at the Roller Rink

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: polka's bouncing oom-pah pulse and the circular momentum of a crowded skating floor*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Accordion and tuba with quick turns; each chorus adds another lap and another skater.*

Verse 1
A capuchin put on his skates and rolled into the rink,
The band struck up a polka and he didn't stop to think.
He circled past the ticket booth and waved to every face,
Then saw his own reflection and raced it round the place.

Chorus
Oom-pah, round the painted line,
Turn and glide and feel just fine.
Oom-pah, take another lap,
Mind the tuba, mind the gap.

Verse 2
A gibbon joined the circle with a tray of lemonade,
She skated backward proudly, sure the cups would never fade.
The tuba hit a low note; every skater leaned,
The lemonade made tiny waves that matched the polka's beat.

Chorus
Oom-pah, round the painted line,
Turn and glide and feel just fine.
Oom-pah, take another lap,
Mind the tuba, mind the gap.

Bridge
The rink announced, "Last lap," but no one found the door,
The circle crossed the entry and came back for one more.
The ticket clerk put on skates and tried to catch the band,
He rolled past the cash register with tickets in his hand.

Verse 3
The dancers passed the exit sign and waved as they went by,
The band was much too busy for a final little sigh.
They circled till the moon was up and morning filled the glass,
The gibbon sold the drinks by cup and never spilled a glass.

Chorus
Oom-pah, round the painted line,
Turn and glide and feel just fine.
Oom-pah, take another lap,
Mind the tuba, mind the gap.

Ending
The clerk switched off the lights; the skaters formed a chain,
The band played one last oom-pah and rolled around again.
