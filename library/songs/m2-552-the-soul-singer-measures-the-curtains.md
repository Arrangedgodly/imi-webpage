# The Soul Singer Measures the Curtains

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: soul ballads about shutting out sorrow and singers who must measure actual windows before hanging the symbolic curtains*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Warm organ, slow drums and a rich lead voice; a measuring tape snaps back on the offbeat.*

[Verse 1]
I sang about the curtains that could close my sorrow out,  
The room had bare wide windows where the daylight moved about.  
I bought a velvet pair that looked as rich as any night,  
I held them up and found a strip of ordinary light.  
The fabric was too narrow for the window's little span,  
My sweeping song of darkness had not measured what it planned.  
The neighbor brought a tape measure and she checked the wooden frame,  
She said the velvet needed width to do the work I'd named.  

[Chorus]
Close the night, hold the heart,  
The curtains need another foot before the shadows start.  
Close the night, let the sorrow stay,  
The neighbor has a number that the song forgot to say.  
Soft cloth, wide frame, let the warm chords shine,  
The velvet needs the measurements before the room is mine.  

[Verse 2]
I took the narrow curtains back and bought a wider pair,  
The shopkeeper read the numbers that the neighbor wrote with care.  
I hung the proper curtains and I drew them to the middle,  
The strip of light disappeared without a singer's fiddle.  
The room held soft and quiet shade beside the evening chair,  
I played the new recording with the neighbor sitting there.  
She said the song was rather good and asked what I'd next sing,  
I said the door had got a draft that needed sealing in.  

[Chorus]
Close the night, hold the heart,  
The curtains need another foot before the shadows start.  
Close the night, let the sorrow stay,  
The neighbor has a number that the song forgot to say.  
Soft cloth, wide frame, let the warm chords shine,  
The velvet needs the measurements before the room is mine.  

[Bridge]
She measured out the doorway while I wrote a second verse,  
The draft was quite a little thing, not all the lonely universe.  
I bought the proper seal and fitted it beside the frame,  
The room held all its quiet warmth while I sang my little name.  

[Final Chorus]
Close the night, let the sorrow stay,  
The door seal has a measurement that keeps the draft away.  
Soft cloth, wide frame, let the warm chords shine,  
The neighbor has the tape measure and the lyrical grief is mine.  
Close the night, hold the heart,  
The shopkeeper asks if I can write a jingle for his curtain department's art.
