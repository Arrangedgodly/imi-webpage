# The Tragic Waltz Meets a Revolving Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: melancholy farewell waltzes undermined by the departing lover circling back into view*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Three-quarter-time strings and tremulous voices; time each return through the door to the first beat.*

[Verse 1]
You told me we must part beneath the hotel chandelier,  
I held a wilted flower and pretended not to hear.  
You took your little suitcase toward the glass revolving door,  
I whispered one last sorrow as you vanished once before.  
The door continued turning and you came back into sight,  
I offered you the flower and restarted my goodnight.  

[Chorus]
One, two, three, goodbye to thee,  
The turning door has brought you back to me.  
One, two, three, depart once more,  
Our love goes round the hinges of the door.  
Farewell is such a tender art,  
You need to step outside before you break my heart.  

[Verse 2]
You tried a second journey with your suitcase held behind,  
The handle caught the center post and changed your state of mind.  
You circled toward the lobby where I stood in fresh despair,  
The porter heard our chorus and approached with careful care.  
He held the suitcase upright, showed you where to leave the glass,  
The violinist added tears to every time you'd pass.  

[Chorus]
One, two, three, goodbye to thee,  
The turning door has brought you back to me.  
One, two, three, depart once more,  
Our love goes round the hinges of the door.  
Farewell is such a tender art,  
You need to step outside before you break my heart.  

[Bridge]
The porter used a side door and escorted you outside,  
I saw you through the window and my final teardrop dried.  
I lifted up the flower for a last regretted kiss,  
You tapped upon the window: 'I forgot the ticket.'  

[Final Chorus]
One, two, three, goodbye to thee,  
The turning door has brought you back to me.  
One, two, three, depart once more,  
You left your railway ticket on the lobby floor.  
Farewell is such a tender art,  
I fetch the ticket while you wait to break my heart.
