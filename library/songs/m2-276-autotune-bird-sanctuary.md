# Autotune at the Bird Sanctuary

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: pop auto-tune production that corrects every pitch into the same glossy line*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Start with a natural croon, then let the pitch correction quantize every animal call until the whole sanctuary sings in one key.*

[Verse 1]
A gibbon brought a microphone to sing beside the reeds,  
The producer set a laptop down and plugged in all the leads.  
The singer tried a soft low note; the software pulled it high,  
A heron gave a crooked squawk and joined the chorus nigh.  
The producer drew a grid and snapped each sound in place,  
The frogs began to croak on beat with glossy, perfect grace.  
A macaque sang one wobbly line about the setting sun,  
The program fixed the vowels till they all became one.  

[Chorus]
Tune it up, tune it clean,  
Smooth the edges, keep the sheen.  
Every note must land in line,  
The frogs and birds will all sound fine.  
Tune it up, remove surprise,  
A perfect chorus fills the skies.  

[Verse 2]
The duck quacked off the grid; the pitch tool gave a beep,  
It stretched the sound so long the duck fell fast asleep.  
The heron tried a trill; the software made it flat,  
The frogs became a choir in identical hats.  
The producer smiled at every waveform lined in blue,  
The natural calls outside were more interesting and true.  
The singer unplugged the laptop; all the birds resumed,  
A rough and lovely chorus filled the reeds with bloom.  

[Chorus]
Tune it up, tune it clean,  
Smooth the edges, keep the sheen.  
Every note must land in line,  
The frogs and birds will all sound fine.  
Tune it up, remove surprise,  
A perfect chorus fills the skies.  

[Bridge]
The producer set the pitch tool to "nature mode" at last,  
It quantized the raindrops and made every gust hold fast.  
The heron gave one honest cry, the laptop hummed along,  
The singer shut it down and let the marsh keep its own song.  

[Final Chorus]
Leave it loose, leave it true,  
Let the reeds and rain come through.  
Not every note must land in line,  
The birds can sing without a sign.  
Leave it loose, no glossy skies,  
A living chorus has its own surprise.  

[Ending]
The pitch tool got a bird call right; the bird promptly changed its key.
