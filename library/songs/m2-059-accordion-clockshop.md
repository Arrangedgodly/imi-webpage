# Accordion at the Clock Repair Shop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musette waltz nostalgia, café accordion flourishes, and wistful urban romance*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Light accordion in lilting waltz time; the clock chimes should interrupt the romantic rubato on schedule.*

Verse 1
I met you at the counter where the wall clocks all ran late,  
You held a pocket watch and I held half a dinner plate.  
The accordion was sighing by the rain-damp window seat,  
The watchmaker said, "It's five past four," and marked the beat.  
We spoke of old Parisian streets we'd never walked before,  
Then every clock began to chime and shook us to the floor.  

Chorus
Accordion, turn the hands,  
Let the evening take its chance.  
Tick-tock, sway around the room,  
Mind the gears and leave them room.  
One-two-three, the clocks agree,  
That dinner's late by half-past three.  
Accordion, don't play too slow,  
The shop must close; we both must go.  

Verse 2
The watchmaker opened up the case and showed a tiny spring,  
The spring leapt out, the accordionist began to sing.  
It bounced into a teacup, then rolled behind the stand,  
We searched beneath the velvet stool and found it in his hand.  
The clock began to tick again, the couple missed their train,  
They shared a bowl of soup outside and watched the passing rain.  

Bridge
The watchmaker sets the time; the singer sets the key,  
A chime falls on the downbeat and sounds like destiny.  
But when the door is locked, the pair discover with a frown,  
The clock shop has a back door that leads directly downtown.  

Chorus
Accordion, turn the hands,  
Let the evening take its chance.  
Tick-tock, sway around the room,  
Mind the gears and leave them room.  
One-two-three, the clocks agree,  
That dinner's late by half-past three.  
Accordion, don't play too slow,  
The shop must close; we both must go.  

Ending
They wave goodbye at closing, the watch now keeping time,  
The accordion plays one last note and charges by the chime.
