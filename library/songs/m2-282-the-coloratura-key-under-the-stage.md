# The Coloratura Key Under the Stage

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operatic coloratura display and productions that demand a high note before anyone checks the set*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Begin with a formal recitative, then let the soprano's agility outpace the stage crew's increasingly desperate cues.*

**Recitative**
At curtain rise, the queen must find the key to the northern gate.  
The chorus stands in velvet rows; the locksmith comes in late.  
Pip the soprano scans the floor and lifts a golden tray.  
The orchestra strikes a mighty chord; the key rolls far away.  

**Aria**
High, higher, hold that note, the key is under there!  
A silver run of scales climbs up and circles through the air.  
The queen can open any lock, can summon any storm,  
But no one checked the painted floor before they changed the form.  
She trills above the footlights while the carpenter crawls low,  
The chorus sings of destiny; the key will not show.  

**Chorus**
La, la, lock it, la, la, lift the painted stone;  
The queen can sing in seven keys, but cannot lift alone.  
La, la, locksmith, tell the pit to wait;  
The high note holds the kingdom while we find the gate.  

**Verse**
A page turns every velvet chair, a guard inspects the throne.  
The props are stacked by number; none are marked "unknown."  
Pip sustains a shining C while stagehands move a tree.  
The key appears beneath its roots, then rolls toward the sea.  
The sea is just a painted cloth; the tide is held by string.  
The chorus pulls the ocean back; the key is still the thing.  

**Chorus**
La, la, lock it, la, la, lift the painted stone;  
The queen can sing in seven keys, but cannot lift alone.  
La, la, locksmith, tell the pit to wait;  
The high note holds the kingdom while we find the gate.  

**Bridge**
The bass calls from the trapdoor, "It fell beside my shoe."  
The soprano lowers one bright note; the key comes into view.  
She picks it up; the gate swings wide; the kingdom cheers on cue.  
The orchestra keeps playing as the locksmith bows to you.  

**Ending**
At final bow, the queen sings one note, clear and low.  
The stagehand holds the key this time, and lets the curtain go.
