# The Dramatic Duet Negotiates a Window Seat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: romantic musical duets in which two lovers declare eternal devotion while arguing over a very small travel preference*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Two alternating solo voices with warm piano; both voices join only when the seating plan is finally agreed.*

[Voice A]
I'd cross the ocean just to be beside your beating heart.  
[Voice B]
I'd take a thousand journeys so that we need never part.  
[Voice A]
I've booked two seats upon a train that runs beside the sea.  
[Voice B]
The window seat, my dearest love, should clearly go to me.  

[Voice A]
I booked it for the sunrise and the cliffs beside the shore.  
[Voice B]
I packed binoculars to see the boats a little more.  
[Voice A]
Your view can be my shoulder and my tender little smile.  
[Voice B]
Your shoulder blocks the harbor for a very lengthy mile.  

[Duet Refrain]
Beside you, beside you, let the distance melt away,  
There is one little window and a very large bouquet.  
Beside you, beside you, let the journey show,  
The flowers get the best view while we argue where to go.  
Love beyond the mountains, love beyond the sky,  
The ticket has a seat number that neither can deny.  

[Voice A]
The seat beside the window has my number on the slip.  
[Voice B]
You bought them both together at the booking of the trip.  
[Voice A]
Then take the seaside section and I'll take the hills inland.  
[Voice B]
We reach the halfway station where the train will briefly stand.  

[Voice A]
We put the flowers on the rack and take our little seats.  
[Voice B]
The sea unfolds beside us while the train gives steady beats.  
[Voice A]
At halfway we exchange them and the hills come into view.  
[Voice B]
The window serves us both in turns; we trade when halfway through.  

[Duet Refrain]
Beside you, beside you, let the distance melt away,  
There is one little window and a very large bouquet.  
Beside you, beside you, let the journey show,  
The flowers get the best view while we argue where to go.  
Love beyond the mountains, love beyond the sky,  
The ticket has a seat number that neither can deny.  

[Coda, both]
We reached the last small station with the flowers safe above,  
We took them down and walked toward the harbor arm in arm.  
Tomorrow's booking has two seats across the little aisle,  
Two windows and a place where we can still exchange a smile.  
The clerk has charged to ship the bouquet ahead,  
So flowers see the window while we sit and share the bread.
