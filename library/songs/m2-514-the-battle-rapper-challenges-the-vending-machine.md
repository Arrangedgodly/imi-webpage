# The Battle Rapper Challenges the Vending Machine

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: battle rap bravado directed at an opponent whose responses consist entirely of product delivery*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Dry drum beat and aggressive lead; machine beeps form the response without a second rapper.*

[Verse 1]
I faced the station vending machine with coins inside my palm,  
Its glass held rows of little snacks with an infuriating calm.  
I chose a packet marked B4 and dropped my payment in,  
The coil turned but the packet stayed suspended by a pin.  
I launched a verse about its steel and lack of human grace,  
The screen displayed "Select Again" beneath my angry face.  
A traveler watched my battle from the bench beside the clock,  
She asked if all my rhymes could make the little packet drop.  

[Hook]
B4, before, hear the champion call,  
The packet waits above the tray and doesn't care at all.  
B4, before, take the sharp reply,  
The machine has got no tongue and lets the insults pass it by.  
Big words, bright screen, let the clash begin,  
The helpline on the panel has a number I can ring.  

[Verse 2]
I rang the little helpline and explained the packet's place,  
The operator heard the echo of the station's little space.  
I pressed return as she instructed: coin back, fair and square,  
The traveler chose B4 and pressed its button standing there,  
Her packet struck my trapped one loose; both dropped inside the tray.  
She kept her fresh one, passed me mine, and paused upon her way.  
I thanked her with a shorter verse and saved the returned coin,  
The machine went back to selling with no battle left to join.  

[Hook]
B4, before, hear the champion call,  
The packet waits above the tray and doesn't care at all.  
B4, before, take the sharp reply,  
The machine has got no tongue and lets the insults pass it by.  
Big words, bright screen, let the clash begin,  
The helpline on the panel has a number I can ring.  

[Bridge]
I told the operator that the packet had come free,  
She said to keep the refund for the wait she'd heard from me.  
I split the snack with the traveler before her train arrived,  
The battle had a chorus and two people quite alive.  

[Final Hook]
B4, before, let the little packet fall,  
The traveler did the winning without rhyming once at all.  
Big words, bright screen, let the station sing,  
The operator ends the call; I thank her for the thing.  
B4, before, hear the champion cry,  
The next machine has sandwiches I choose to walk on by.
