# Tap Shoes in the Tool Shed

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Broadway showstoppers, sudden dance breaks, and songs that solve practical problems mid-plot*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Piano-led show tune that opens as a quiet confession and erupts into a tap break on paint cans.*

Verse 1
I came to mend the garden gate, I brought a hammer, nail,  
The foreman gave a speech about the hinges and the rail.  
I sighed beside the tool shed, then the piano struck a chord,  
A chorus of mechanics came from somewhere near the board.  
I sang, "This gate is crooked, and my heart is just as bent,"  
The crew began to tap their boots and asked where all the paint went.  

Chorus
Tap, tap, hammer, swing,  
Fix the latch and let it ring.  
Paint the post and mark the line,  
The gate will close by half past nine.  
Tap, tap, sawdust flies,  
Hold the plank and watch your eyes.  
When the work is done at last,  
We'll sing the chorus walking past.  

Verse 2
The heroine arrives in gloves, she carries four brass keys,  
She tries each one against the lock; the crew all bends its knees.  
The foreman says, "The problem is a hinge that needs a screw,"  
She takes a tiny screwdriver and sings a high note through.  
The dance line forms a zigzag round the buckets on the ground,  
One dancer kicks a paint can; the paint makes perfect sound.  

Bridge
The gate swings wide, the garden blooms, the chorus starts to soar,  
The foreman checks the invoice and says, "Who swept the floor?"  
We tap our way to broom storage, the orchestra stays grand,  
The final dance is sweeping dust in synchronized command.  

Chorus
Tap, tap, hammer, swing,  
Fix the latch and let it ring.  
Paint the post and mark the line,  
The gate will close by half past nine.  
Tap, tap, sawdust flies,  
Hold the plank and watch your eyes.  
When the work is done at last,  
We'll sing the chorus walking past.  

Ending
The curtain falls on hinges, the crew takes one last bow,  
The gate is fixed; the audience can leave the garden now.
