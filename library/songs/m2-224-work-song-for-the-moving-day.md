# Lift Together, Set It Down

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: work-song call-and-response, hauling rhythms, and a move crew that carries the sofa through the wrong doorway*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep a steady hauling beat; let the call stop on every turn so the crew can reset its grip.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque called the moving crew to lift the sofa high,
A gibbon took the front end, a lemur took the side.
They counted on the beat and raised it from the floor,
Then found the sofa wider than the doorway by an inch or more.

[Call and Response]
LEAD: Lift together!
CREW: Set it down!
LEAD: Turn the cushion!
CREW: Don't scratch the town!
LEAD: Push it gently!
CREW: Watch the frame!
LEAD: Which way's forward?
CREW: We forgot the name!

[Verse 2]
They turned it on its side; the sofa touched the ceiling,
The crew kept singing through the wobble and the feeling.
A capuchin pulled the corner; the tail was caught below,
The chair at the landing joined the chorus with a squeak in tow.

[Call and Response]
LEAD: Lift together!
CREW: Set it down!
LEAD: Turn the cushion!
CREW: Don't scratch the town!
LEAD: Push it gently!
CREW: Watch the frame!
LEAD: Which way's forward?
CREW: We forgot the name!

[Bridge]
They measured the doorway; the answer was a tilt,
They rolled the sofa forward; its wooden frame began to split.
The couch cleared with a shuffle, the crew let out a cheer,
The work-song found its ending at the room they meant to clear.

[Final Call and Response]
LEAD: Lift together!
CREW: Set it down!
LEAD: Turn the cushion!
CREW: Don't scratch the town!
LEAD: Push it gently!
CREW: Watch the frame!
LEAD: Which way's forward?
CREW: We remember the name!

[Ending]
They set it in the bedroom, facing the wall again.
