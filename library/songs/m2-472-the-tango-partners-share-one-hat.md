# The Tango Partners Share One Hat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dramatic tango duets and dancers whose matching costumes arrive with one crucial accessory missing*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Sharp piano accents, accordion and two opposing voices; hat passes happen on the accented beat.*

[Verse 1]
We met behind the curtain in our matching suits of black,  
My partner opened up the box the tailor sent us back.  
Two jackets and two waistcoats lay as neat as they could be,  
One hat lay in the middle, very far from symmetry.  
He said the hat belonged to him because he'd placed the call,  
I said I'd paid the invoice for the tailoring and all.  
The pianist began to play before we'd reached a truce,  
We took the stage together with one hat and no excuse.  

[Refrain]
Your turn, my turn, let the brim go round,  
Two proud hearts compete for one small piece of ground.  
Your turn, my turn, let the music bite,  
Whoever has the hat must wear it only for the night.  
Step close, turn sharp, let the shadows fall,  
The tailor sent us half the hats but billed us for them all.  

[Verse 2]
He tipped the hat toward the crowd and passed it into my hand,  
I turned and set it on my head according to the band.  
We passed it at the corner of each eight-bar little phrase,  
The audience watched the traveling brim with fascinated gaze.  
He dipped me at the ending while the hat stayed on my head,  
I put it on his head again before the final tread.  
We bowed with hands upon the hat between our shoulders high,  
The crowd applauded both of us as if we'd planned the try.  

[Refrain]
Your turn, my turn, let the brim go round,  
Two proud hearts compete for one small piece of ground.  
Your turn, my turn, let the music bite,  
Whoever has the hat must wear it only for the night.  
Step close, turn sharp, let the shadows fall,  
The tailor sent us half the hats but billed us for them all.  

[Bridge]
The tailor sent the second hat the morning after that,  
He'd left it on his packing bench behind a sleeping cat.  
We practiced with two hats until the passing disappeared,  
The dance looked quite conventional and not the thing they'd cheered.  

[Final Refrain]
Your turn, my turn, let the brim go round,  
The second hat stays backstage where it can't disturb the sound.  
Step close, turn sharp, let the shadows fall,  
We have two hats in inventory and only use one after all.  
Your turn, my turn, let the music bite,  
The tailor buys two tickets and we pass them left and right.
