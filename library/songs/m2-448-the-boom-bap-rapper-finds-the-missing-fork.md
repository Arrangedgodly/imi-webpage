# The Boom-Bap Rapper Finds the Missing Fork

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: gritty boom-bap detective narratives and rappers who investigate a household inconvenience as a vast conspiracy*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Dry kick and snare, scratched-style original vocal fragments and dense spoken rhymes; a utensil drawer supplies the repeated clack.*

[Verse 1]
I opened up the drawer and found the final fork was gone,  
The lunch was on the table, but the mystery had begun.  
I questioned every housemate with a notebook in my hand,  
The first said check the dishwasher; I needed a bigger plan.  
I mapped the kitchen carefully and marked each suspect seat,  
The second said the washer held the things we used to eat.  
I said the clues were buried in a complicated scheme,  
The lunch grew cold beside me while I built a darker theme.  

[Chorus]
Who took the fork, who left the trace,  
There is evidence in every ordinary place.  
Who shut the drawer, who knows the score,  
The dishwasher has answers that I'm trying to ignore.  
Deep beat, sharp clue, let the long truth talk,  
The whole case has four tines and a very short walk.  

[Verse 2]
I checked behind the cereal and I questioned every mug,  
The third housemate took the notebook and swept crumbs off the rug.  
She pointed to the dishwasher and opened up its door,  
The clean forks stood in their basket; there were seven, maybe more.  
I took one for my lunch and put the others in the drawer,  
The first housemate said, "Great work. The city can sleep once more."  
I asked if they had staged it to expose my hungry pride,  
They said we'd run the dishwasher and let the dishes dry.  

[Chorus]
Who took the fork, who left the trace,  
There is evidence in every ordinary place.  
Who shut the drawer, who knows the score,  
The dishwasher has answers that I'm trying to ignore.  
Deep beat, sharp clue, let the long truth talk,  
The whole case has four tines and a very short walk.  

[Bridge]
I wrote the case in verses and I played the track at night,  
The housemates joined the chorus, which repeated they were right.  
I kept the notebook for my next investigation day,  
The third wrote "Check the obvious" upon its cover page.  

[Final Chorus]
Who took the fork, who left the trace,  
The basket had the answer and the answer had a place.  
Deep beat, sharp clue, let the long truth talk,  
The whole case has four tines and a very short walk.  
Who knows the score, who shuts the drawer,  
The spoon is on the table; I shall not investigate more.
