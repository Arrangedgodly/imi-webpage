# Spaghetti-Western at Sundown

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: spaghetti-western soundtrack cues, twangy guitar and dramatic whistles, and a sheriff whose showdown is with a runaway tumbleweed*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Twangy electric guitar, whistle, and sparse percussion; build a huge showdown for the tumbleweed, then end with a tiny tumble.*

Verse 1
The sheriff stood at sundown by the water trough alone,
A whistle crossed the canyon and a guitar twanged its tone.
The townsfolk hid behind the saloon; the dust began to rise,
A tumbleweed rolled down the street beneath the orange skies.

Chorus
Twang the string and hold your breath,
Watch the dust approach its death.
Stand your ground beneath the trees,
One dry outlaw rides the breeze.

Verse 2
The sheriff drew his lasso as the drummer tapped a boot,
The tumbleweed rolled past him and bumped against a root.
The whistle sounded danger; the guitar bent the note,
The sheriff spun to follow as the weed rolled by his coat.

Chorus
Twang the string and hold your breath,
Watch the dust approach its death.
Stand your ground beneath the trees,
One dry outlaw rides the breeze.

Bridge
The town emerged in silence; the sheriff raised his hand,
The wind pushed the tumbleweed across the border sand.
He looped the weed with one soft toss and lifted it with care,
"He's wanted for square dancing!" cried the gibbon from the square.

Verse 3
They tied him to a hitching post and charged him by the yard,
"Unlicensed rolling after dark; you haven't got a card."
A gust tore off his wanted sign; the weed escaped its string,
The posse asked the whistle man to make the sirens sing.

Chorus
Twang the string and hold your breath,
Watch the dust approach its death.
Stand your ground beneath the trees,
One dry outlaw rides the breeze.

Ending
The guitar struck one final chord; the sheriff tipped his hat,
The tumbleweed rolled one inch more; the posse sprang at that.
