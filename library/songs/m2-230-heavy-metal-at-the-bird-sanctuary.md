# Feathered Thunder

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: heavy metal power riffs, thunderous drums, and a band that must play quietly while nesting birds sleep*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use a heavy guitar tone at low volume; let the drumsticks brush instead of crash.*

[Verse 1]
A gibbon metal band arrived beside the nesting grove,
They carried amps like thunder but were told to play in low.
The sanctuary guide raised one feather like a sign,
The singer growled a whisper and the drummer kept the time.

[Chorus]
Feathered thunder, rumble low,
Let the heavy riff not show.
Feathered thunder, soft but strong,
Wake no bird while we play this song.
Feathered thunder, rumble low,
Let the heavy riff not show.
Feathered thunder, soft but strong,
Wake no bird while we play this song.

[Verse 2]
The guitarist found a muted string; the bassist held the note,
The drummer used a folded towel around the snare he wrote.
A capuchin fan was headbanging without a sound,
The birds began to bob their heads from branches all around.

[Chorus]
Feathered thunder, rumble low,
Let the heavy riff not show.
Feathered thunder, soft but strong,
Wake no bird while we play this song.
Feathered thunder, rumble low,
Let the heavy riff not show.
Feathered thunder, soft but strong,
Wake no bird while we play this song.

[Bridge]
One fledgling chirped a high note; the singer answered back,
The whole band changed the riff and followed its small track.
The guide said, "That is the encore; now let the nestlings sleep,"
The drummer laid his sticks down and counted one soft beat.

[Final Chorus]
Feathered thunder, rumble low,
Let the heavy riff not show.
Feathered thunder, soft but strong,
The birds are wide awake; we have to end the song.
Feathered thunder, rumble low,
Let the heavy riff not show.
Feathered thunder, soft but strong,
The birds are wide awake; we have to end the song.

[Ending]
The band unplugged its amps; one bird sang the final chord.
