# Keep Your Hands Off My Scone

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: brassy ska-punk protest choruses and skanking urgency*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Verse 1
The tea room opened at a quarter to ten.  
The scone tray vanished, then appeared again.  
My cousin took the largest from the plate.  
I raised a spoon and called a meeting date.  
The horns went off like an alarm in town.  
The bassline bounced; the teacups rattled down.  
The jam was pooled on every table edge.  
We marched in circles by the hedge.  
I'm a baboon with a righteous claim.  
My cousin's troop all shouted my name.  

## Chorus
Keep your hands off my scone!  
There is plenty, leave one alone!  
Pass the cream and mind the jam!  
Share the plate, that's who I am!  

## Verse 2
The baker came out with flour on her sleeve.  
She said the batch was made for all to eat.  
My cousin held his scone like stolen gold.  
The horn player told him, "Don't be bold."  
The drummer knocked a teapot with his stick.  
The whole room skanked, surprisingly quick.  
The tea went cold, the kettle boiled again.  
We drew a sharing chart with a pen.  
My auntie swung in from the balcony.  
She stole the chart and took the tea.  

## Chorus
Keep your hands off my scone!  
There is plenty, leave one alone!  
Pass the cream and mind the jam!  
Share the plate, that's who I am!  

## Bridge
Then little Kit reached for the last one.  
She had been waiting since the tea began.  
My cousin looked at crumbs upon his cuff.  
He broke his scone and said, "Enough."  
The band dropped out to hear the crack.  
We split the halves and passed them back.  
The baker brought a tray out fresh.  
The horns came in above the mesh.  

## Final chorus
Keep your hands off my scone!  
There are plenty, save some alone!  
Pass the cream and mind the jam!  
Make a place for every fam!  
Keep your hands off my scone!  
Then pass a half and share your own!  
The tea is hot, the band is loud.  
We eat together, feed the crowd!  

## Ending
The tray was empty by noon.  
The tea room hummed a ska tune.  
My cousin washed the plates with Kit.  
We saved a crumb and laughed at it.  

*Performance note: Let the horns punch the first line of the chorus; keep the crowd response playful, never hostile.*
