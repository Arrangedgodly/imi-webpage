# Ragtime at the Rain Barrel

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ragtime syncopation, nimble piano breaks, and a rain collector that adds its own unpredictable rhythm*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Bright ragtime piano with banjo accents; barrel drops should land a little off the beat.*

Verse 1
A pianist set his keyboard by the old rain barrel,
A capuchin brought a banjo and a handbell for the chorus.
The rain began to tap the lid in patterns of its own,
The pianist took the syncopation and made it his alone.

Chorus
Tick on two and drip on four,
Rain can keep the barrel score.
Skip the beat and catch the flow,
Ragtime makes the raindrops go.

Verse 2
The first drops hit the gutter; the downspout gave a plunk,
The barrel filled a little while the drummer struck a thunk.
A macaque danced around the rim and splashed her dancing shoes,
The banjo picked a skipping line; the barrel changed the blues.

Chorus
Tick on two and drip on four,
Rain can keep the barrel score.
Skip the beat and catch the flow,
Ragtime makes the raindrops go.

Bridge
The water reached the little spout and poured into a pail,
The pail rolled down the walkway with a wobble and a wail.
The band kept playing ragtime as the pail went out of sight,
It bumped the fence and came back home to join the chorus right.

Verse 3
They used the rain to water seeds along the orchard row,
The barrel kept its steady drip, the pianist played it slow.
The capuchin swept the puddles and the dancer found her shoe,
The pail sat by the garden gate, still tapping out the two.

Chorus
Tick on two and drip on four,
Rain can keep the barrel score.
Skip the beat and catch the flow,
Ragtime makes the raindrops go.

Ending
The rain let up, the piano closed, the barrel held the tune,
One final drop went plink-plink-plink beneath the afternoon.
