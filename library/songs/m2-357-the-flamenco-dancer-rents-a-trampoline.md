# The Flamenco Dancer Rents a Trampoline

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dramatic flamenco performance presented on a venue's unsuitable springy stage*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Nylon guitar, palmas and a forceful singer; replace the dancer's heel clicks with trampoline squeaks.*

[Verse 1]
She came with all the thunder in her heels beneath her dress,  
The venue gave a platform that would not withstand distress.  
They'd hired it for children in the garden yesterday,  
The dancer took one mighty step and slowly bounced away.  
The guitarist held a tremolo to match her flashing eyes,  
The singer raised a sorrowful cry to follow her surprise.  

[Chorus]
Strike the earth and strike the night,  
Bring your heel down hard and right.  
Clap the storm and clap the flame,  
Try to land where you began.  
The heart is fierce, the floor is spring,  
Count every heel before it rings.  

[Verse 2]
She stamped a second furious beat and rose above the band,  
The singer stretched his vowel till her shoes returned to land.  
The clappers watched the upstroke and the guitarist watched the down,  
Her shawl spread like a sail above the startled little town.  
The venue man assured her that the bouncing brought appeal,  
She stamped beside his clipboard and he fled behind the wheel.  

[Chorus]
Strike the earth and strike the night,  
Bring your heel down hard and right.  
Clap the storm and clap the flame,  
Try to land where you began.  
The heart is fierce, the floor is spring,  
Count every heel before it rings.  

[Bridge]
She stepped off to the paving and she planted both her feet,  
The heel clicks rang like coins against the unforgiving street.  
The band turned all their chairs around and followed every sound,  
The venue man climbed on the springs to take the platform down.  

[Final Chorus]
Strike the earth and strike the night,  
Bring your heel down hard and right.  
Clap the storm and clap the flame,  
The paving stays exactly where it came.  
The heart is fierce, the floor is spring,  
The venue man bounces through our bow like a very minor king.
