# The Rock Opera Logs into the Guest Account

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rock operas about identity and power set against a computer account with very modest permissions*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Large guitar chords, choir responses and a declamatory solo voice; the screen prompts are spoken by the ensemble.*

[Verse 1]
I named myself the ruler of the digital domain,  
I sang about the systems that could never hold my reign.  
The theater had a computer for the backing to our show,  
The guest account was open with a simple desktop glow.  
I tried to change the hall's logo to my emblem of the night,  
The screen asked for the owner's password to approve the right.  
The choir sang about my power; the prompt stayed in its place,  
My guest account could play a song but couldn't change the face.  

[Chorus]
Rule the night, command the screen,  
The guest account can hear the songs but won't remake the scene.  
Rule the night, claim the name,  
The hall logo has an owner who decides its little frame.  
Big chord, small prompt, let the choir rise,  
The password box has waited through our emperor's disguise.  

[Verse 2]
The hall manager arrived to hear the opening rehearsal,  
She asked if changing logos was the show or just dispersal.  
I said the rock opera could use my emblem in its art,  
She made a separate projection slide to hold that little part.  
The guest account played the backing and the slide showed our own sign,  
The hall's own logo stayed intact beneath its booking line.  
We sang our grand rebellion with a proper separate scene,  
The choir watched the emperor command a much smaller screen.  

[Chorus]
Rule the night, command the screen,  
The guest account can hear the songs but won't remake the scene.  
Rule the night, claim the name,  
The hall logo has an owner who decides its little frame.  
Big chord, small prompt, let the choir rise,  
The password box has waited through our emperor's disguise.  

[Bridge]
The manager removed the projection slide after the bow,  
I asked if my great emblem might remain forever now.  
She said the next production had a different little name,  
The emperor packed the artwork and accepted her same claim.  

[Final Chorus]
Rule the night, claim the name,  
The next town gets our artwork in a separate little frame.  
Big chord, small prompt, let the choir rise,  
The guest account has finished with the emperor's disguise.  
Rule the night, command the screen,  
The next show's baking queen has booked the whole projection scene.
