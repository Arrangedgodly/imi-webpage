# The Power Ballad Cannot Fold the Road Map

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: grand power ballads about holding on, finding a way home and overcoming impossible separation*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Piano opening, enormous guitar entrance and impassioned vocals; rustling map folds build into the final drum fill.*

[Verse 1]
We stopped beside a service road beneath a fading sky,  
My partner held the folding map and traced the route with pride.  
We found the turn for home at last; I took the map to close,  
Its panels spread across the seat in twenty stubborn rows.  
I folded north upon the west and tucked the south inside,  
The east rose like a paper sail and covered half my eyes.  
My partner said the crease should guide the corners back to rest,  
I held the whole world in my arms and gathered it against my chest.  

[Chorus]
Hold on, paper, hold on tight,  
We will find the way together through this complicated night.  
Come home, corners, join as one,  
There are twenty-seven valleys where a single fold belongs.  
Hold on, paper, let me try,  
The world is much too large to fit inside the pocket at my side.  

[Verse 2]
I spread the map upon the hood and flattened every crease,  
My partner held the northern edge against a little breeze.  
We folded it in stages with the coastlines facing in,  
I reached the final panel and the first came free again.  
The service station owner brought a rubber band outside,  
He said he'd seen this drama many drivers' nights besides.  
We folded it as best we could and bound it with the band,  
The map no longer fit its sleeve but stayed within my hands.  

[Chorus]
Hold on, paper, hold on tight,  
We will find the way together through this complicated night.  
Come home, corners, join as one,  
There are twenty-seven valleys where a single fold belongs.  
Hold on, paper, let me try,  
The world is much too large to fit inside the pocket at my side.  

[Bridge]
We drove back home in silence with the bound map on my knees,  
My partner framed it later for the wall above the keys.  
She said the roads looked beautiful when nobody must fold,  
I keep a tiny atlas now; its binding holds the world.  

[Final Chorus]
Hold on, paper, hold on tight,  
You have found a place above the keys beneath the hallway light.  
Come home, corners, join as one,  
The service station's rubber band has finally been undone.  
Hold on, paper, never fly,  
The frame has conquered continents I couldn't fit inside.
