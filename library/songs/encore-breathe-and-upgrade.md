# Breathe and Upgrade

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Meditation app ambient chants and monetized wellness subscriptions*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Soft bowls, slow breath, a notification chime that interrupts at the worst moment.*

[Opening chant]  
Inhale the quiet, exhale the ping.  
Let the small bell sound like a spring.  
Feet on the floor, tail in the chair.  
Notice the meeting. Leave it there.  

[Verse 1]  
The app says, "Find a still place." We sit beside the vent.  
A monkey's tail is twitching at the quarterly event.  
The guide says, "Let thoughts pass like clouds across the sky."  
A cloud of calendar alerts goes drifting through the eye.  
Our breathing starts to settle. The room begins to slow.  
Then comes a voice that tells us where our premium thoughts can go.  
It asks us to subscribe to silence, billed at every dawn.  
The trial lasts a single breath. We breathe, and it is gone.  

[Chant]  
Inhale the quiet, exhale the ping.  
Let the small bell sound like a spring.  
Feet on the floor, tail in the chair.  
Notice the meeting. Leave it there.  

[Verse 2]  
The monkey at the window finds a patch of afternoon.  
No app, no guided voice, no inspirational balloon.  
She watches dust drift slowly through a band of golden light.  
Her phone asks if she wants to share her calmness overnight.  
She turns the phone face down. The screen goes dark and still.  
A coworker asks for breathing tips beside the windowsill.  
She says, "Breathe in the air, then let your shoulders drop."  
The app asks for a referral code. She tells the app to stop.  

[Chant]  
Inhale the quiet, exhale the ping.  
Let the small bell sound like a spring.  
Feet on the floor, tail in the chair.  
Notice the meeting. Leave it there.  

[Bridge]  
The bowl rings once, a gentle note.  
The phone lights up, we let it float.  
No streak to save, no badge to win.  
We hear the room. We breathe back in.  

[Outro]  
The session ends. The office hums. The monkey stays at ease.  
The app renews at midnight. She deletes it before she leaves.
