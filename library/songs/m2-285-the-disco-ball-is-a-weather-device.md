# The Disco Ball Is a Weather Device

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: disco's mirror-ball spectacle and DJs who announce every light cue like a forecast*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Four-on-the-floor groove with bright strings; let the DJ's forecast grow more confident as the mirror ball spins out of control.*

**Verse 1**
The club is in a basement, but the ceiling's dressed in stars.  
DJ Lolo checks the weather on a stack of speaker bars.  
He spins the shining mirror ball; the dots sweep through the room.  
He calls, "A shower in the east," and guests all grab a broom.  
The ballroom's just a gymnasium, the clouds are painted tin.  
The dancers whirl like satellite dishes searching for a signal in.  

**Chorus**
Spin, spin, silver sky, forecast on the floor.  
Clouds of glitter drifting by the fire-exit door.  
Step to the left, the moon says two; step right, the sun says three.  
The disco ball predicts a storm of glitter over me.  

**Verse 2**
The mirror ball turns faster when the bass line drops below.  
Its spots move like a thunderhead across the dancers' row.  
DJ Lolo warns of heavy winds and points his flashlight north.  
The beam hits on a mirrored tile and sends the forecast forth.  
The club's old fan begins to hum; a streamer lifts and sails.  
The dancers duck beneath the stream, then form a conga trail.  

**Chorus**
Spin, spin, silver sky, forecast on the floor.  
Clouds of glitter drifting by the fire-exit door.  
Step to the left, the moon says two; step right, the sun says three.  
The disco ball predicts a storm of glitter over me.  

**Bridge**
The custodian says, "Weather's fine; the fan is on too high."  
He lowers just one switch; the silver storm subsides.  
Lolo checks his forecast chart and circles "clear by nine."  
The crowd keeps dancing, sweeping glitter in a line.  

**Ending**
At closing time the ball slows down; the ceiling stars all sleep.  
The DJ calls a fog advisory; the mop begins to sweep.  
He files his chart beside the lights, his forecast duly spun.  
Tomorrow brings a clear blue sky, indoors, when work is done.
