# The Choral Round Goes Through the Wrong Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: cumulative singing rounds and choirs mistaking an endlessly repeated route for sensible rehearsal directions*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: A round with voices entering two bars apart; break the round for a spoken caretaker interruption.*

[Voice A, alone]
Through the blue door, round the hall,  
Past the picture on the wall,  
Through the blue door, round the hall,  
Find the room where voices call.  

[Voice B enters; both repeat]
Through the blue door, round the hall,  
Past the picture on the wall,  
Through the blue door, round the hall,  
Find the room where voices call.  

[Verse]
The choir had a practice room behind the school at night,  
The leader sent a little rhyme to get the route quite right.  
The school had painted both the doors the same bright shade of blue,  
The singers took the nearer one and found a corridor through.  
They circled past the picture and returned to where they'd been,  
The round described a perfect loop and welcomed them back in.  
The caretaker heard footsteps going past his little chair,  
He asked why fourteen singers were rehearsing on the stair.  

[Full Round Refrain]
Through the blue door, round the hall,  
Past the picture on the wall,  
Through the blue door, round the hall,  
Find the room where voices call.  

[Caretaker, spoken]
There are two blue doors. You want the far one.  
Fourteen people stop.  
Fourteen people turn.  
One leader reads the room number.  
That is something we can learn.  

[Verse]
He led them through the far door to the room marked Twenty-Three,  
The chairs stood in a semicircle where the singers ought to be.  
They practiced all the proper songs with voices in their parts,  
The leader saved the little round for warming up their hearts.  
She added the room number to the message for next time,  
The school caretaker got a credit in the newly written rhyme.  
The choir left by that same door when all their work was done,  
The near blue door stayed quietly where the circling had begun.  

[New Round, all entries fully written]
Far blue door, Twenty-Three,  
Find the room where you should be,  
Far blue door, Twenty-Three,  
No more rounds around with me.  

Far blue door, Twenty-Three,  
Find the room where you should be,  
Far blue door, Twenty-Three,  
No more rounds around with me.  

[Coda]
The caretaker heard the new round as the singers passed his chair,  
He said the lyric was quite accurate, and pointed to the stair.  
Next week's practice moved upstairs when painters claimed the room,  
The leader sent a numbered map with no repeated tune.
