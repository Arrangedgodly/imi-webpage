# Classical Quartet at the Bird Blind

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: chamber-music etiquette and musicians who treat every pause as a dramatic fermata*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: A string quartet plays a stately movement beside a bird-watching hide; sustain rests until the birds become impatient.*

**Verse 1**
Four gibbons set their music stands inside the birding blind.  
The violins tune carefully; the score is neatly lined.  
They play a quiet minuet, a delicate refrain.  
The birds pause in the reeds, then leave and come again.  
The first violin lifts one bow and holds the note in air.  
A heron steps upon the score and nests its shadow there.  

**Chorus**
Rest, rest, let the measure wait;  
Hold the bow and hold the gate.  
One bird, two birds, the silence sings;  
The quartet plays softly while the birdwatcher brings the wings.  

**Verse 2**
The cellist turns one page; the paper makes a snap.  
A kingfisher dives below; the bow rests in the lap.  
The violist lifts a finger; the heron holds its pose.  
The birder whispers, "There it is," the quartet cannot hear.  
They play the passage once again, with every rest prolonged.  
The birds arrange themselves behind the stand, then move along.  

**Chorus**
Rest, rest, let the measure wait;  
Hold the bow and hold the gate.  
One bird, two birds, the silence sings;  
The quartet plays softly while the birdwatcher brings the wings.  

**Bridge**
The guide points through the window; the musicians close the score.  
The heron takes one bow hair, then steps toward the shore.  
They follow with their instruments, no longer blocking view.  
The quartet plays a single chord as the birds fly through.  

**Ending**
The music ends; the blind is clear; the birds return to rest.  
The heron keeps the page it took, its feather marks the best.  
The quartet bows to empty seats, then packs its stands away.  
A single birdcall fills the pause and closes out the day.
