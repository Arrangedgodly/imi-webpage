# Glitter in the Loading Dock

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: glam rock, theatrical costume reveals, arena-scale guitar solos, and a star who cannot leave without one final shine*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Use a huge guitar entrance and shimmering percussion; let the work lights expose the practical backstage crew.*

[Verse 1]
A gibbon star came in with a feather cape of red,
A lightning stripe of glitter cut across my helmeted head.
The band hit one big chord, the loading dock turned gold,
The forklift blinked its hazard lights; the audience took hold.

[Chorus]
Glitter, glitter, light the night,
Raise the cape and strike the light.
Glitter, glitter, hold the pose,
The work crew's moving cases while the guitar explodes.
Glitter, glitter, light the night,
Raise the cape and strike the light.
Glitter, glitter, hold the pose,
The work crew's moving cases while the guitar explodes.

[Verse 2]
I climbed a crate to make my grand and glittering debut,
The crate was full of cables marked "Please do not climb through."
A stagehand pulled the curtain back; the forklift joined the band,
It played a warning beep in time and stole the spotlight's hand.

[Chorus]
Glitter, glitter, light the night,
Raise the cape and strike the light.
Glitter, glitter, hold the pose,
The work crew's moving cases while the guitar explodes.
Glitter, glitter, light the night,
Raise the cape and strike the light.
Glitter, glitter, hold the pose,
The work crew's moving cases while the guitar explodes.

[Bridge]
The crew had swept the glitter up and packed it in a pail,
I asked them for a final flash; they showed me the disposal scale.
The work lights snapped on bright; my cape looked tired and gray,
I helped the crew load cases, then rocked the night away.

[Final Chorus]
Glitter, glitter, light the night,
Raise the cape and strike the light.
Glitter, glitter, hold the pose,
The forklift's got a solo and the loading crew now knows.
Glitter, glitter, light the night,
Raise the cape and strike the light.
Glitter, glitter, hold the pose,
The forklift's got a solo and the loading crew now knows.

[Ending]
The star left last, still sparkling from the broom's last swipe.
