# The Waltzing Cook Stirs in Three-Four Time

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: elegant cooking-show waltzes that prioritize choreography over keeping ingredients in the pan*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Viennese strings and lightly brushed drums; every third beat includes a spoon striking the pot.*

[Verse 1]
The host put on a velvet apron, bowed before the stove,  
The pianist played a waltz while little clouds of steam arose.  
He stirred upon the first beat and he twirled upon the third,  
The sous-chef caught the flying peas without another word.  
The camera swept the kitchen as the host described the sauce,  
He turned the pan to face the lens; the peas escaped the toss.  

[Chorus]
One, two, stir, one, two, turn,  
Let the little sauce politely learn.  
One, two, bow, one, two, glide,  
The vegetables are dancing on the other side.  
Lift that spoon and make it sing,  
The sous-chef catches everything the third beat likes to fling.  

[Verse 2]
He called for just a sprinkle from a tin of fragrant spice,  
The spinning tin sent spices raining over all the rice.  
The sous-chef moved the platter as he spun beside the sink,  
The host waltzed through his lesson without stopping once to think.  
He reached the final garnish in a perfect ballroom frame,  
The garnish landed on the lens and left a leafy stain.  

[Chorus]
One, two, stir, one, two, turn,  
Let the little sauce politely learn.  
One, two, bow, one, two, glide,  
The vegetables are dancing on the other side.  
Lift that spoon and make it sing,  
The sous-chef catches everything the third beat likes to fling.  

[Bridge]
The pianist stopped playing as the sous-chef took the pan,  
She served all of the portions with a steady helping hand.  
The host stood still beside her and described the careful taste,  
She held his fluttering apron clear of all the food they'd placed.  

[Final Chorus]
One, two, stir, one, two, turn,  
Let the little sauce politely learn.  
One, two, bow, one, two, glide,  
The vegetables are finally upon the plates beside.  
Lift that spoon and make it sing,  
The host takes one last bow and dips his sleeve in everything.
