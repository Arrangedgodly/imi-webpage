# The Space Opera Anthem Misses the Elevator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: grand science-fiction opening anthems and heroes whose ceremonial entrance depends on a very ordinary building lift*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Huge brass, choir and snare drums; the captain declaims while a lift chime cuts through the fanfare.*

[Verse 1]
The captain wore a silver cape and stood beside the door,  
The launch hall waited for him on the building's seventh floor.  
He sang about the distant stars and worlds beyond the sun,  
The elevator opened but his opening wasn't done.  
He raised a fist against the sky and held a final note,  
The doors slid shut behind his cape and almost caught his coat.  
He pushed the call button again and gazed into the light,  
The choir sang the second verse about his coming flight.  

[Chorus]
Rise, commander, rise to the stars,  
Our destination lies beyond the painted planet Mars.  
Rise, commander, claim the unknown,  
The lift has gone to seven; we are still here alone.  
Silver cape, golden dawn,  
The countdown in the launch hall carries on and on.  

[Verse 2]
The building's cleaner joined him with a bucket and a broom,  
She asked what floor he needed and made a little room.  
The elevator came back down; she held its open door,  
The captain stepped inside before they closed the waiting door.  
She pressed the seven button and the lift began to climb,  
The choir took the stairway with a slightly slower time.  
The captain reached the launch hall where the countdown reached its end,  
He opened the exhibition with the cleaner as his friend.  

[Chorus]
Rise, commander, rise to the stars,  
Our destination lies beyond the painted planet Mars.  
Rise, commander, claim the unknown,  
The lift has gone to seven; we are still here alone.  
Silver cape, golden dawn,  
The countdown in the launch hall carries on and on.  

[Bridge]
The starship was a model in a case beside the stage,  
The cleaner dusted planets as the choir turned its page.  
The captain signed the guest book with a name of vast renown,  
The cleaner pressed the button for the journey back down.  

[Final Chorus]
Rise, commander, rise to the stars,  
The seventh floor has biscuits and a little model Mars.  
Silver cape, golden dawn,  
The choir needs a minute with the stairs that it came on.  
Rise, commander, claim the unknown,  
The cleaner holds the lift while we bring all our trumpets home.
