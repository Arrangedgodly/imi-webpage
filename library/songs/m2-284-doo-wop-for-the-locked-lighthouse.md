# Doo-Wop at the Locked Lighthouse

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: doo-wop vocal groups, handclap harmonies, and romantic lyrics that turn a practical problem into a serenade*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Four-part close harmony; the bass sings the instructions while the lead keeps trying to woo the lighthouse keeper.*

**Verse 1**
The keeper lost the brass key at the top of Beacon Hill.  
Four macaques arrive in satin coats to help her find it still.  
The lead sings, "Baby, you're the beam that makes the darkness bright."  
The bass replies, "The door is locked; we cannot climb tonight."  
They snap their fingers, clap in time, and circle round the rail.  
The keeper points toward the service hatch beneath the pail.  

**Chorus**
Doo-wah, door won't turn; sha-na, key's not here.  
Doo-wah, sing it close; the rocks are very near.  
Bop-bop, check the hatch; don't serenade the stairs.  
The lighthouse needs a working key, not four matching flares.  

**Verse 2**
The tenor finds a shiny ring and holds it to the moon.  
The keeper says, "That's from your coat. We need the key here soon."  
The group sings "Only you" in tight and velvet blend.  
The door says nothing back to them; it has no heart to mend.  
The bass gets down upon one knee to look beneath the sill.  
He sees a glint beside the mop, behind a coil of twine.  

**Chorus**
Doo-wah, door won't turn; sha-na, key's not here.  
Doo-wah, sing it close; the rocks are very near.  
Bop-bop, check the hatch; don't serenade the stairs.  
The lighthouse needs a working key, not four matching flares.  

**Bridge**
The key fits; the heavy door swings open with a groan.  
The keeper turns the lamp on high; the beam sweeps out alone.  
The lead begins a love-song reprise, the tenor joins the rhyme.  
The bass says, "That light is for the boats; let's clear the dock on time."  

**Ending**
They harmonize while coiling rope and stacking up the pails.  
The keeper thanks the quartet; the lead forgets his tales.  
They walk down off the lighthouse hill in measured, matching shoes.  
The door stays unlocked until the keeper leaves her post.
