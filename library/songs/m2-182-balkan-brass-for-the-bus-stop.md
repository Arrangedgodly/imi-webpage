# Balkan Brass for the Bus Stop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Balkan brass band crescendos, rapid irregular meters, and a waiting crowd that starts dancing before the bus arrives*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*

*Performance note: Tuba, trumpet, and snare in a lurching uneven meter; let the bus signal interrupt every third phrase.*

Verse 1
The brass band parked beside the stop and tuned beneath the sign,
The trumpet took a soaring run; the tuba drew a line.
The bus was due at quarter past, the crowd began to sway,
The snare drum changed the meter and the timetable blew away.

Chorus
Turn, step, wait, then go,
Brass can make the bus move slow.
Turn, step, count to five,
Keep the stop line clear and wide.

Verse 2
A gibbon raised the trumpet like a signal to the road,
The driver saw the shining bell and thought the bus was owed.
He stopped at every measure, then he started up again,
The dancers made a pathway through the shelter and the rain.

Chorus
Turn, step, wait, then go,
Brass can make the bus move slow.
Turn, step, count to five,
Keep the stop line clear and wide.

Bridge
The bus conductor called, "The band can board at the next door!"
The tuba blocked the aisle; the drummer found the floor.
They packed the brass in cases and let the riders through,
The trumpeter kept playing from the sidewalk by the loo.

Verse 3
The bus pulled off at last, the band stayed at the stop,
The crowd stepped to the rhythm while the rain began to drop.
A second bus arrived; the conductor waved them in,
The brass played one more crooked bar and let the trip begin.

Chorus
Turn, step, wait, then go,
Brass can make the bus move slow.
Turn, step, count to five,
Keep the stop line clear and wide.

Ending
The band rode in the back with horns wrapped safe and tight,
The bus reached town on schedule, to the passengers' delight.
