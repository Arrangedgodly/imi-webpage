# Find It on the One

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Afrobeat interlocking grooves, horn riffs, and a lost-and-found desk that sorts objects by dance pattern*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-005*
*Performance note: Layer guitar, horns, and hand percussion; build each verse around a different found object.*

[Verse 1]
The station lost-and-found was full by half past three,
A macaque clerk named Biko said, "Bring every claim to me."
A hat came in on a rolling beat; a shoe arrived off time;
The band found all their rhythm in the objects' little rhyme.

[Chorus]
Find it on the one, find it on the two,
Horn says left, the guitar says through.
Find it in the box, let the hand drum call,
Every lost thing gets a dance before we find them all.

[Verse 2]
A red umbrella opened as the horns began to play;
A spoon struck time against a cup and pulled the line away.
Biko matched the owner by the rhythm of their feet;
The hat went to a drummer who had lost his walking beat.

[Chorus]
Find it on the one, find it on the two,
Horn says left, the guitar says through.
Find it in the box, let the hand drum call,
Every lost thing gets a dance before we find them all.

[Bridge]
A suitcase joined the pattern with a rolling, rattling sound;
The clerk asked for its owner, and a whole brass band was found.
They'd left their instruments on a platform down the line;
Biko sent the case along and kept the groove in time.

[Final Chorus]
Find it on the one, find it on the two,
Horn says left, the guitar says through.
Find it in the box, let the hand drum call,
Every lost thing gets a dance before we find them all.

[Ending]
At closing, Biko found his watch; it ticked along in two-four time.
