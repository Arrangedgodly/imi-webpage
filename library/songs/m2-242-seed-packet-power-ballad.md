# The Seed Packet Has a Power Ballad

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: arena-rock power ballads that turn a small promise into a huge key change and guitar solo*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Start with one clean guitar and build toward a full-band key change; the seed packet should get a heroic spotlight.*

[Verse 1]
A gardener named Fara found a packet in a drawer,  
It promised purple asters by the garden's wooden door.  
She read the planting depth, then she looked toward the sky,  
As thunder rolled dramatically and clouds went drifting by.  
Her neighbor raised a shovel, a capuchin raised a rake,  
The drummer hit one thunder sheet to underline the stake.  
The packet showed one flower in a pot,  
The band made it a destiny, a world-shaking plot.  

[Chorus]
Rise up, little seed, take the light,  
Push through the dark, make the morning bright.  
Grow where the rain and the garden winds lead,  
The whole band roots for one seed.  
Rise up, rise up, don't fear the ground,  
A six-piece chorus will carry you round.  

[Verse 2]
They dug a tidy furrow, but the bassist dug a trench,  
The singer faced the sunrise from a plywood garden bench.  
The packet said "one quarter inch," the guitarist played a chord,  
The crew built a paper mountain with a banner reading "ROAR."  
Fara set the seed down gently; the drummer rolled a tom,  
The neighbors hummed a harmony that shook the watering can.  
A gust blew off the lyric sheet and caught it on a tree,  
The seed remained beneath the dirt, politely out of key.  

[Chorus]
Rise up, little seed, take the light,  
Push through the dark, make the morning bright.  
Grow where the rain and the garden winds lead,  
The whole band roots for one seed.  
Rise up, rise up, don't fear the ground,  
A six-piece chorus will carry you round.  

[Bridge]
The forecast said a gentle shower, the singer cried "At last!"  
The cymbals crashed like thunder as the clouds went rumbling past.  
The hose came on at half a turn; the amp began to hiss,  
The band held one heroic note while Fara fixed the twist.  

[Final Chorus]
Rise up, little seed, take the light,  
Push through the dark, make the morning bright.  
Grow where the rain and the garden winds lead,  
The whole band roots for one seed.  
Rise up, rise up, the sprout has been found,  
It is three inches tall and the chorus kneels down.  

[Ending]
The seedling grew by morning; the guitarist signed its pot.
