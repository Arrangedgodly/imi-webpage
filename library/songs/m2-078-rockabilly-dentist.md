# Rockabilly at the Dentist

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rockabilly revival, slap-bass swagger, and pompadour theatrics*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Slap bass, twang guitar, and a fast snare shuffle; the waiting-room chair squeaks on the backbeat.*

Verse 1
A gibbon combs his pompadour beneath the clinic light,  
The receptionist calls his name; he says, "I'm feelin' right."  
The bass player thumps a rhythm on a metal waiting chair,  
The dentist lifts a mirror and says, "Open up with care."  
The guitar bends a string so high the gum display vibrates,  
The singer answers with a grin and asks if that is fate.  

Chorus
Shake, rattle, roll your sleeve,  
Hold the bib and don't you leave.  
Twang that string and tap your shoe,  
The dentist has a song for you.  
Shake, rattle, count to four,  
The suction tube is by the door.  
Shake, rattle, keep the beat,  
But don't tap-dance in the street.  

Verse 2
The hygienist says "rinse," the singer hears "rock and roll,"  
He grabs the paper cup and starts a solo by the bowl.  
The water swirls in perfect time, the bass begins to slap,  
A toothbrush spins across the tray and lands inside his cap.  
He tries to keep his hair just right while leaning in the chair,  
The dentist says, "Your pompadour has toothpaste in its hair."  

Bridge
The appointment ends, the band resumes outside the door,  
The dentist gives the singer one small bag of floss and more.  
He struts across the lobby, combing through his glossy wave,  
The automatic door shuts gently; he bows as if it's rave.  

Chorus
Shake, rattle, roll your sleeve,  
Hold the bib and don't you leave.  
Twang that string and tap your shoe,  
The dentist has a song for you.  
Shake, rattle, count to four,  
The suction tube is by the door.  
Shake, rattle, keep the beat,  
But don't tap-dance in the street.  

Ending
He leaves with polished teeth and hair, the band plays one last chord,  
The dentist keeps the waiting chair and joins them on the snare.
