# Bluegrass on the Library Card

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bluegrass picking circles, breakneck solos, and friendly rivalry over who takes the next break*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Banjo, fiddle, and upright bass; solos get faster whenever the due date is mentioned.*

Verse 1
A banjo-playing gibbon came to check out books at noon,
The clerk stamped every card in time to a brisk fiddle tune.
A macaque held a field guide like a prize against his chest,
The band began a picking duel to see who read it best.

Chorus
Pick it quick and pass it round,
Let the pages set the sound.
Read that line and take your break,
Keep the rhythm, mind the date.

Verse 2
The fiddle took a solo on the chapter about bees,
The banjo answered every fact with buzzing harmonies.
The clerk said, "Quiet, please," but plucked the desk in time,
The whole reference section joined the chorus line by line.

Chorus
Pick it quick and pass it round,
Let the pages set the sound.
Read that line and take your break,
Keep the rhythm, mind the date.

Bridge
The card machine beeped once; the bassist took his turn,
The due date showed on screen; the fiddle made it burn.
They played so fast the pages fluttered off the table in a sheet,
A tortoise checked the book out and outpaced the whole quartet.

Verse 3
The tortoise reached the exit with the book held safely tight,
The band chased after him in a bluegrass blur of light.
He turned the book in early; the clerk gave him a grin,
The group asked for a rematch; he checked a cookbook in.

Chorus
Pick it quick and pass it round,
Let the pages set the sound.
Read that line and take your break,
Keep the rhythm, mind the date.

Ending
The library stayed quiet till the tortoise chose a seat;
Then every banjo player softly picked beneath his feet.
