# The Cowboy Ballad Follows a Satnav

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: western trail ballads that imagine a navigation device as an all-knowing frontier guide*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Acoustic guitar and a loping rhythm; a politely spoken navigation voice interrupts each grand landscape.*

[Verse 1]
I rode across the prairie with the sunset on my back,  
My horse could read the river but I trusted something black.  
A little glassy box told me to turn before the hill,  
I followed past a windmill to a fence beside a mill.  
It said to take the next left at the very nearest bend,  
My horse looked at the cattle gate and waited for the end.  

[Chorus]
Ride on, faithful traveler, ride until the dawn,  
The box says take a right turn where the old oak used to stand.  
Ride on, follow starlight, follow every star,  
Recalculating cattle paths will tell us where we are.  
The west is wide, the trail is free,  
The horse knows where we're going and the box disagrees.  

[Verse 2]
It took me down a driveway to a stranger's chicken pen,  
It told me I'd arrived although I couldn't name the hen.  
The farmer read the little screen and laughed beneath his hat,  
I'd typed the nearest ranch's name without the final bit.  
He gave me plain directions drawn upon a flour sack,  
I thanked him for his kindness and I set the box to back.  

[Chorus]
Ride on, faithful traveler, ride until the dawn,  
The box says take a right turn where the old oak used to stand.  
Ride on, follow starlight, follow every star,  
Recalculating cattle paths will tell us where we are.  
The west is wide, the trail is free,  
The horse knows where we're going and the box disagrees.  

[Bridge]
I pocketed the little box and let my old horse lead,  
He followed to a water trough and drank at quite a speed.  
I read the farmer's paper and we found the proper gate,  
I stepped down at the ranch gate; the box declared me late.  

[Final Chorus]
Ride on, faithful traveler, ride until the dawn,  
The box says take a right turn where the old oak used to stand.  
Ride on, follow starlight, follow every star,  
My horse has brought us here; the porchlight shows just where we are.  
The west is wide, the trail is free,  
The box calls out ARRIVED as my horse walks home without me.
