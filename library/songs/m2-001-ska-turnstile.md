# The Turnstile Skank

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: ska revival horn breaks and offbeat guitar*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Brass snaps on every offbeat while the singer argues with a station gate.*

## Verse 1
At Kingfisher Station, dawn wore a paper hat.  
A brass band warmed up beside a sleepy cat.  
Our macaque held a ticket stamped for yesterday.  
The gate said, "Please proceed," then kept its bars in place.  
He bounced left, bounced right, his briefcase keeping time.  
The trumpet answered every beep with one rude climb.  
A pigeon in a conductor's cap inspected the queue.  
It pecked his ticket twice and waved the whole band through.  

## Chorus
Skank at the turnstile, shoulder to the sun.  
The gate can count our feet, but it can't stop anyone.  
Skank at the turnstile, brass above the rail.  
We all go dancing when the timetable goes pale.  

## Verse 2
The platform clock was running fast by half a day.  
A baboon called the inspector; he had lost his way.  
He measured every shuffle with a yellow ruler.  
Then joined the brass section as their newest tuba player.  
The drummer used a lunch pail, tapped a crisp parade.  
A lemur sold earplugs, then danced in the shade.  
The train rolled in with windows open wide.  
Its driver wore tap shoes and skanked from side to side.  

## Bridge
The station chief appeared with a whistle and a frown.  
He ordered all this rhythm to sit politely down.  
His whistle hit the backbeat, then his knees began to swing.  
He stamped his form approved and let the whole place sing.  

## Chorus
Skank at the turnstile, shoulder to the sun.  
The gate can count our feet, but it can't stop anyone.  
Skank at the turnstile, brass above the rail.  
We all go dancing when the timetable goes pale.  

## Ending
The gate stayed open after everyone had gone.  
It clicked along in rhythm till the morning shift came on.
