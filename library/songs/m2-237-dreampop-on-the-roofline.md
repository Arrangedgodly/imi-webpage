# The Moon Moves When I Blink

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: dream-pop haze, soft-focus harmonies, and a rooftop singer who blames the moon for drifting out of frame*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Layer airy harmonies over slow guitar; let the wind shift the microphone between phrases.*

[Verse 1]
A lemur tuned a silver guitar beneath the rooftop sign,
The moon hung over chimney tops like a paper lantern fine.
She looked away to check a cord; the moon had moved a mile,
She turned back to the empty sky and tried to keep the smile.

[Chorus]
Moonlight, move softly, stay inside the frame,
I turn my head and find you changed your name.
Moonlight, move softly, drift above the street,
The wind keeps moving cables while I try to hold the beat.
Moonlight, move softly, stay inside the frame,
I turn my head and find you changed your name.
Moonlight, move softly, drift above the street,
The wind keeps moving cables while I try to hold the beat.

[Verse 2]
A macaque held the microphone; the stand leaned to the right,
The lamp made every rooftop shine like water in the night.
The chorus floated over town, a gentle silver cloud,
The moon appeared behind the sign and hid itself from view.

[Chorus]
Moonlight, move softly, stay inside the frame,
I turn my head and find you changed your name.
Moonlight, move softly, drift above the street,
The wind keeps moving cables while I try to hold the beat.
Moonlight, move softly, stay inside the frame,
I turn my head and find you changed your name.
Moonlight, move softly, drift above the street,
The wind keeps moving cables while I try to hold the beat.

[Bridge]
They moved the sign aside and saw a round bright glow,
It was a laundry lamp reflecting off the snow.
The lemur changed the lyric, left the moon out of the verse,
The audience hummed along and watched the cloud disperse.

[Final Chorus]
Moonlight, drift softly, you can leave the frame,
The lamp and cloud can share the sky without a name.
Moonlight, drift softly, the rooftop cables sway,
The singer faced the crowd and let the night drift away.

[Ending]
The moon stayed where it was; the sign moved with the breeze.
