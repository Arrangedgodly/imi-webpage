# The Country Duet Misreads the Fridge Note

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: country breakup duets that interpret a partner's smallest household message as the end of a lifelong romance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Acoustic guitar, gentle fiddle and alternating male and female voices; read the torn note with increasingly dramatic conviction.*

[Verse 1]
I found her little note beside the handle of the fridge,  
It said "I'm leaving" on the top; my heart slipped from its bridge.  
The bottom half was folded underneath a smiling magnet,  
I took the first two words to mean the end of everything we'd managed.  
I called her mobile sadly and I sang about our years,  
She heard my tender chorus over shopping-trolley squeals.  
She asked what I was talking of; I said I knew the score,  
She said to lift the magnet and to read a little more.  

[Chorus]
Don't leave me, don't go far,  
I have found two words of sadness where the frozen vegetables are.  
Don't leave me, hear my song,  
The note has got a second half that I have read quite wrong.  
Soft tears, cold door, tell me where you've been,  
I'm crying by the ketchup while you're buying milk again.  

[Verse 2]
I lifted up the magnet and unfolded what she'd penned,  
It said "I'm leaving for the shop; please feed the cat, my friend."  
She said the trolley had a broken wheel that pulled against her hand,  
My tragic duet chorus wasn't helping her to stand.  
I fed the cat his supper and I cleared the kitchen side,  
Then went to meet her at the shop with my embarrassment inside.  
We brought the shopping home and put the milk where it belongs,  
She wrote the full note on the fridge beside my written song.  

[Chorus]
Don't leave me, don't go far,  
I have found two words of sadness where the frozen vegetables are.  
Don't leave me, hear my song,  
The note has got a second half that I have read quite wrong.  
Soft tears, cold door, tell me where you've been,  
I'm crying by the ketchup while you're buying milk again.  

[Bridge]
She sang her own reply about the shopping that she'd done,  
The cat supplied a backing meow; he wanted supper number two.  
I told him he had eaten and she checked the bowl with care,  
We left the fridge note open so its whole truth stayed there.  

[Final Chorus]
Don't leave me, don't go far,  
We are singing in the kitchen and the cat knows where we are.  
Soft tears, cold door, leave the doubts behind,  
The whole note has a shopping list beneath its loving line.  
Don't leave me, hear her say,  
"I'm going to the garden. Read this whole one while I'm away."
