# The Lobby Crooner

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: lounge standards and hotel-lobby sophistication*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Brushed drums and a velvet croon, with the elevator bell joining the melody.*

## Verse 1
The lobby piano stood beneath a painted palm.  
A silverback sang softly, keeping every evening calm.  
He knew the guests by luggage, never by their names.  
He played the same old standard while the elevator came.  
A suitcase rolled too quickly past the velvet rope.  
The porter caught its handle with a quiet, practiced hope.  
The singer changed one chord to match the wheels.  
The hallway hummed the tune like polished brass that feels.  

## Chorus
Stay for one more song before you go.  
The lobby lights are kind and moving slow.  
Stay for one more song, then take the lift.  
The evening has a little room to drift.  

## Verse 2
A weary tamarin checked out at quarter after ten.  
Her flight had been delayed, then delayed again.  
The crooner played a tune her mother used to hum.  
She stopped beside the piano, let the memory come.  
He offered her the bench; she sang a line.  
Her voice was thin but clear, and right on time.  
The porter set her case beside the door.  
The elevator opened, but she asked for one song more.  

## Bridge
No grand romance was waiting in the room upstairs.  
Just clean white sheets and a night of small repairs.  
The singer poured water, she unfolded her map.  
They marked the airport road and took a quiet lap.  

## Chorus
Stay for one more song before you go.  
The lobby lights are kind and moving slow.  
Stay for one more song, then take the lift.  
The evening has a little room to drift.  

## Ending
The elevator hummed its bell against the keys.  
The crooner bowed; the guest went out with ease.
