# Stone Steps, Steady Breath

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: work and walking chant traditions without sacred imitation*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Use a respectful, original walking refrain, with no borrowed ceremonial melody or text.*

## Verse 1
At dawn the macaques climbed the hill to move a fallen stone.  
It blocked the path that led to every neighbor's home.  
They set their feet in careful rows, palms open to the ground.  
A caller gave a simple beat; the others answered round.  
No borrowed prayer, no elder's words were used.  
They made a work song for the task they'd all just chosen.  
The stone shifted a finger's width, then settled back in place.  
They stopped and checked each other's hands and face.  

## Chorus
Step and breathe, then ease the weight.  
Hold the line, but don't tempt fate.  
Step and breathe, call when we're ready.  
One shared pace can keep us steady.  

## Verse 2
A neighbor brought a timber beam and wedges from his shed.  
A young one found a safer angle near the ledge.  
The caller changed the rhythm when the leverage shifted true.  
The answer came from every throat, each worker listening through.  
At last the stone rolled sideways, resting by the trees.  
The path lay clear; the crowd relaxed their knees.  
They marked the turn with paint and set a sign upright.  
The caller thanked the crew and walked them down at night.  

## Bridge
A song can help a heavy task when every voice can hear.  
The pace must leave a space for doubt and honest fear.  
They put the beam away, then shared a pot of tea.  
No one claimed the hill; the path belonged to all who'd see.  

## Chorus
Step and breathe, then ease the weight.  
Hold the line, but don't tempt fate.  
Step and breathe, call when we're ready.  
One shared pace can keep us steady.  

## Ending
The next dawn, strangers used the open way.  
The crew heard footsteps, and began their ordinary day.
