# Ska on the Sidewalk

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ska upstroke guitar, brisk horn stabs, and a sidewalk crew trying to cross while the rhythm keeps pulling them back*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Bright upstroke guitar, bass, and short horn stabs; keep the verse bouncing without rushing the crossing.*

Verse 1
The band set up beside the crosswalk when the signal turned to red,
A macaque heard the upstroke and began to nod his head.
The horn section made a warning; the crowd mistook it for a cue,
They stepped into the crosswalk, then the traffic light said "Wait!" 

Chorus
Skank on the sidewalk, stop at the line,
Wait for the walking hand, take your time.
Horn says go, but the signal says stay,
Dance on the curb till it's safe to play.

Verse 2
The guitarist played the offbeat as the bus rolled to a stop,
A gibbon did a little jump and nearly dropped his mop.
The crossing guard raised both arms, the brass began to blast,
The dancers held a frozen pose until the cars went past.

Chorus
Skank on the sidewalk, stop at the line,
Wait for the walking hand, take your time.
Horn says go, but the signal says stay,
Dance on the curb till it's safe to play.

Bridge
The drummer tried to count them in; the signal blinked to green,
The band kept to the sidewalk, the safest ska they'd seen.
They crossed in pairs, still bouncing, with the horn held soft and low,
The final couple made it through just as the lights said "No."

Verse 3
They reached the other curb and played a little victory tune,
The crossing guard joined in and tapped a bell in time with noon.
The bus rolled on, the street stayed clear, the mop remained upright,
The band returned to practice for the next safe crossing light.

Chorus
Skank on the sidewalk, stop at the line,
Wait for the walking hand, take your time.
Horn says go, but the signal says stay,
Dance on the curb till it's safe to play.

Ending
The light went red, the band stayed put, the horns gave one short call,
The monkeys danced in place and did not cross the road at all.
