# Ringtone for the Mayor

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: novelty mobile ringtones and public embarrassment*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Perform every alert as a tiny melodic hook, then let the full brass band answer.*

## Verse 1
The mayor's phone went off during the pond debate.  
It chirped a yodel loud enough to wake the gate.  
The ducks went still, the microphones went thin.  
A cricket in the gallery joined the din.  
He fumbled for the screen, his cuffs caught on the chair.  
The ringtone climbed a scale and bounced around the square.  
A clerk recognized the tune from last year's fair.  
She hummed the second phrase and shook her head with care.  

## Chorus
Ring, little phone, make your tiny claim.  
The meeting pauses; everybody knows your name.  
Ring, little phone, descend with some decorum.  
The ducks have heard the hook and started forming a quorum.  

## Verse 2
The mayor apologized and silenced it at last.  
The duck committee quacked a rhythm just as fast.  
A teen had sampled raindrops from the fountain's lip.  
He mixed them with the yodel for a novelty clip.  
By afternoon the market sang the melody.  
The fishmonger whistled it while weighing celery.  
The mayor asked the clerk to change the tune.  
She chose a softer kettle sound and left it there till noon.  

## Bridge
A public office needs a quiet call tone.  
The ducks deserve some peace beside their stone.  
Still, when the old tune leaked from someone's coat,  
The clerk smiled first, then tried to clear her throat.  

## Chorus
Ring, little phone, make your tiny claim.  
The meeting pauses; everybody knows your name.  
Ring, little phone, descend with some decorum.  
The ducks have heard the hook and started forming a quorum.  

## Ending
The mayor kept the kettle tone for years.  
The ducks kept yodeling whenever he appeared.
