# Please Clap After the Kazoo

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: televised talent-show auditions, judging panels, and staged revelations*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Start with shaky kazoo, let the judges interrupt, and keep the contestant's own tempo.*

## Verse 1
The talent show had three red chairs and one bright floor.  
A young tamarin held a kazoo behind the door.  
Her act was humming with a paper cup.  
The floor manager told her, "Smile when you step up."  
The judges watched as every camera rolled.  
One held a card marked "Surprise story to be told."  
She played her tune, a melody she'd made.  
The first judge leaned back; the second waved.  

## Chorus
Let the small song finish what it starts.  
A quiet note can still reach hearts.  
Let the small song find its own way through.  
The stage is big, but the breath is you.  

## Verse 2
The buzzer sounded early, cutting off the phrase.  
The audience stayed quiet for two awkward beats.  
The third judge asked her where she'd found the cup.  
She said, "My brother made it; he can't come up."  
He had taught her rhythm while he healed at home.  
She'd practiced on the bus and near the foam.  
The judges dropped their cards and let her play.  
The floor manager forgot to cue the close-up that day.  

## Bridge
No tearful montage showed her brother's room.  
She asked the crew to send the song, not zoom.  
The audience clapped when she was done.  
She took the stairs back down, still humming one.  

## Chorus
Let the small song finish what it starts.  
A quiet note can still reach hearts.  
Let the small song find its own way through.  
The stage is big, but the breath is you.  

## Ending
The episode aired with her cup in view.  
At home her brother hummed the tune right through.
