# The Stadium Chant Finds the Wrong Entrance

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: football terrace chants and traveling supporters whose confidence vastly exceeds their ability to read a ticket*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Stomps and claps with a large group vocal; instructions from a calm attendant interrupt the shouted claims.*

[Verse 1]
We came across the city with our scarves above our heads,  
We sang about our winning team and painted faces red.  
The entrance had a velvet rope and posters on the wall,  
We shouted for the home side as we gathered in the hall.  
The attendant held a program for a flower-arranging show,  
She asked if we had come to watch the spring begonias grow.  
Our tickets named the stadium two streets around the bend,  
The entrance we had chosen had a rather different end.  

[Chorus]
Here we come, loud and proud,  
No one else can beat our crowd.  
Here we come, raise the sound,  
We have found a potting bench instead of football ground.  
Red scarves high, let the colors flow,  
The roses in the lobby are preparing for their show.  

[Verse 2]
The attendant pointed out the road and gave us all a map,  
We thanked her with a quiet voice between each little clap.  
One supporter bought a plant to give his mother after play,  
He carried it above his scarf along the proper way.  
We reached the stadium entrance where the gate read on our pass,  
The steward found a shelf to keep the plant above the grass.  
We sang the song from proper seats and watched the home side score,  
The plant stayed in the entrance till we took it out once more.  

[Chorus]
Here we come, loud and proud,  
No one else can beat our crowd.  
Here we come, raise the sound,  
We have found a potting bench instead of football ground.  
Red scarves high, let the colors flow,  
The roses in the lobby are preparing for their show.  

[Bridge]
We stopped beside the flower show while going home at night,  
The attendant asked the score and saw the plant was quite all right.  
Our captain gave her one long chant about the winning goal,  
She said the rose committee had a quieter, shorter role.  

[Final Chorus]
Here we come, loud and proud,  
The plant has made a journey with the whole supporting crowd.  
Red scarves high, let the colors flow,  
Tomorrow we have tickets for the flower-arranging show.  
Here we come, raise the sound,  
We clap for the begonias on their own familiar ground.
