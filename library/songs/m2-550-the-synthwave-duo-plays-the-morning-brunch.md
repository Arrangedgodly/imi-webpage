# The Synthwave Duo Plays the Morning Brunch

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: retro nocturnal synthwave shows and musicians bringing midnight atmosphere to a bright breakfast event*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Retro synthesizers, electronic drums and two deadpan voices; daylight and cutlery sounds intrude on the nightclub mood.*

[Verse 1]
We built a show of neon streets beneath a midnight rain,  
Two silver jackets, dark glasses, a very distant train.  
The venue booked us Sunday for its little dining stage,  
We read the time as nine at night upon the booking page.  
The manager called Saturday to check the morning plan,  
The show was nine in the morning, when the breakfast service ran.  
We loaded up the synthesizers as the dawn began to rise,  
Our midnight city met a brunch beneath the sunny skies.  

[Hook / Chorus]
Neon night, electric blue,  
The table has a poached egg for the person next to you.  
Neon night, rain on the street,  
The waitress has a little tray of toast for us to eat.  
Dark glass, bright keys, let the skyline glow,  
The windows have the real sun at the start of every show.  

[Verse 2]
We played the opening pulse beside the breakfast counter light,  
The audience sipped their coffee while we sang of endless night.  
The drummer changed the busy beat to something soft and slow,  
The manager could hear the orders through the smaller show.  
We kept the neon melody above the gentle groove,  
The diners nodded to it with no need to stand and move.  
At ten we finished all the songs and had our own small plate,  
The midnight show had ended at a very early date.  

[Hook / Chorus]
Neon night, electric blue,  
The table has a poached egg for the person next to you.  
Neon night, rain on the street,  
The waitress has a little tray of toast for us to eat.  
Dark glass, bright keys, let the skyline glow,  
The windows have the real sun at the start of every show.  

[Bridge]
We wrote a brunch arrangement for the venue's next event,  
The manager wrote "9 AM" in ink, so no one misread what was meant.  
Our poster showed a neon city with a cup of coffee near,  
The diner asked if all the distant trains would still appear.  

[Final Hook / Chorus]
Neon night, let the skyline glow,  
The nine-in-the-morning version has a gentler beat below.  
Dark glass, bright keys, let the breakfast stay,  
We pack the city in the car and have the whole rest of the day.  
Neon night, electric blue,  
The waitress gets an encore while she brings the bill to you.
