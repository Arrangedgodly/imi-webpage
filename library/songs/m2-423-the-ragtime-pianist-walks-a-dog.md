# The Ragtime Pianist Walks a Dog

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: jaunty ragtime songs about a charming stroll, complicated by a dog whose preferred rhythm is stopping at every post*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Syncopated piano, lively sung verses and brief held chords whenever the dog stops to investigate.*

[Verse 1]
I took the little terrier out for exercise at eight,  
I hummed a brisk piano tune beyond the garden gate.  
My feet kept perfect walking time for twenty pleasant yards,  
The terrier discovered something written on a post.  
He sniffed with great attention while my melody went on,  
I reached the ending of the tune before his reading was done.  
We started down the pavement with a brand-new little beat,  
He found the next important post and settled at its feet.  

[Chorus]
Step, step, stop, wait a little while,  
The dog has got a newspaper at every quarter mile.  
Skip, skip, hold, let the piano stay,  
My jaunty little walking song is taking all the day.  
Step, step, stop, read the local news,  
The terrier has information I could never hope to use.  

[Verse 2]
I lengthened every little rest to suit his careful pace,  
He read the gate of number ten and studied number eight.  
A neighbor with a spaniel stopped beside the corner tree,  
The dogs exchanged a quiet sniff; the owners drank some tea.  
The neighbor said this daily walk was mostly standing still,  
Her spaniel checked the corner twice before he left the hill.  
Our terrier resumed his route with dignity and pride,  
I hummed the second verse and matched him stride for stride.  

[Chorus]
Step, step, stop, wait a little while,  
The dog has got a newspaper at every quarter mile.  
Skip, skip, hold, let the piano stay,  
My jaunty little walking song is taking all the day.  
Step, step, stop, read the local news,  
The terrier has information I could never hope to use.  

[Bridge]
We reached our garden finally; I checked the time and sighed,  
The terrier lay down content with all the news inside.  
I played my tune upon the keys with long rests newly planned,  
He woke at every pause as though a post was close at hand.  

[Final Chorus]
Step, step, stop, wait a little while,  
The dog has made a walking song of pauses and a smile.  
Skip, skip, hold, let the piano stay,  
The terrier is sleeping after reading half the day.  
Step, step, stop, now the song is through,  
He dreams of little messages on posts he never knew.
