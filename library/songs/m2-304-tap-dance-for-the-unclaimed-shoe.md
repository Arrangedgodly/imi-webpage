# Tap Dance for the Unclaimed Shoe

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: tap-dance showstoppers where virtuoso footwork continues after the plot has ended*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Percussive tap break grows from a missing shoe's single click into an overlong company finale.*

**Verse 1**
The stagehand finds a tap shoe waiting by the stairs.  
He holds it up; the band stops short and everyone stares.  
A dancer tests one heel against the wooden floor.  
It clicks a little answer, so the drummer asks for more.  
The chorus forms a kick line; the shoe is passed along.  
Each dancer adds a shuffle step and folds it in the song.  

**Chorus**
Tap it once, tap it twice, send the rhythm down;  
Heel to toe, toe to heel, wake the quiet town.  
The shoe says click, the floor says clack, the band says, "One more round."  
The owner left the theater, but the beat is still around.  

**Verse 2**
A porter checks the dressing room; the owner isn't there.  
The shoe gets its own close-up beneath the footlights' glare.  
The dancers tap around it; no one steps inside.  
The singer gives a solemn speech about the shoe's long ride.  
The show has reached its ending; the curtain starts to fall.  
The drummer adds a final fill and brings back one and all.  

**Chorus**
Tap it once, tap it twice, send the rhythm down;  
Heel to toe, toe to heel, wake the quiet town.  
The shoe says click, the floor says clack, the band says, "One more round."  
The owner left the theater, but the beat is still around.  

**Bridge**
A dancer spots the name tag tucked inside the heel.  
The porter takes it to the desk; the owner comes to claim.  
She tries it on; it fits her foot; she taps one simple phrase.  
The company echoes her four beats and lets them fade.  

**Ending**
The curtain falls for real this time; the stage goes dark and still.  
The owner leaves with both her shoes, the dancers leave the hill.  
The porter sweeps one lonely tap from underneath the chair.  
The floor gives one last click, then silence fills the air.
