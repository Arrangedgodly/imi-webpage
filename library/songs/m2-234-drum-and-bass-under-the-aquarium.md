# Bassline Beneath the Blue

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: drum-and-bass breakbeats, fast bass drops, and an aquarium tunnel where the fish seem to judge the dancers*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the breakbeat fast but controlled; drop to silence whenever a fish passes overhead.*

[Verse 1]
A capuchin DJ set the decks beneath the blue,
The aquarium glass reflected every move he knew.
The drum break ran at double time; the bassline rolled below,
A stingray passed above the booth and made the whole crowd slow.

[Chorus]
Bassline beneath the blue, breakbeat through the night,
Fish above the dance floor drifting out of sight.
Bassline beneath the blue, drop it, let it breathe,
The aquarium is watching from the water overhead.

[Verse 2]
A macaque danced in circles; a gibbon spun around,
The bass made ripples in the tank without a single sound.
A turtle floated upside down and watched the crowd below,
The DJ cut the music; all the dancers held a pose.

[Chorus]
Bassline beneath the blue, breakbeat through the night,
Fish above the dance floor drifting out of sight.
Bassline beneath the blue, drop it, let it breathe,
The aquarium is watching from the water overhead.

[Bridge]
The DJ brought the beat back in; the turtle turned upright,
The stingray made a pass above and vanished out of sight.
The crowd began to shuffle in a rhythm soft and neat,
The fish swam to the viewing glass and followed with the beat.

[Final Chorus]
Bassline beneath the blue, breakbeat through the night,
Fish above the dance floor drifting out of sight.
Bassline beneath the blue, the last drop rolls below,
The dancers leave the tunnel while the sea lights softly glow.

[Ending]
The DJ packed the cables; a fish kept swimming to the beat.
