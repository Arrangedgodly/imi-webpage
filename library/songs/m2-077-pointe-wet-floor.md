# Pointe Shoes on the Wet Floor

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ballet rehearsal, strict pointe technique, and the graceful denial of discomfort*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: A graceful waltz with a firm rehearsal-piano pulse; each dancer's careful balance should contrast with the wet mop marks.*

Verse 1
The macaque ballerina ties her ribbons in a bow,  
The piano plays a waltz, her pointed toes begin to go.  
She rises on her pointe shoes, then she pauses in the pose,  
A mop bucket rolls behind her and leaves a shining rose.  
The stage manager says, "The floor is wet beside the wing,"  
The dancer says, "I know," and keeps on balancing.  

Chorus
Turn, lift, hold the line,  
Keep your ankles safe and fine.  
Glide, pause, mark the place,  
Don't do arabesques on space.  
One-two-three, the music's grand,  
There's a wet floor sign beside your hand.  
Turn, lift, wait your cue,  
The mop crew has a dance move too.  

Verse 2
The corps de ballet enters, all in matching tulle,  
They glide around the caution sign and make a perfect wheel.  
The choreographer calls for snow; the stagehand shakes a sheet,  
The powder lands on dampened boards and sticks beneath their feet.  
The dancers stop in unison; the pianist holds one chord,  
The mop crew dries the stage and earns a small reward.  

Bridge
The floor is dry, the sign is gone, the music starts anew,  
The ballerinas cross the stage in lines of careful blue.  
The mop crew bows, the dancers bow, the curtain starts to fall,  
The stage manager asks them both to clear the hall.  

Chorus
Turn, lift, hold the line,  
Keep your ankles safe and fine.  
Glide, pause, mark the place,  
Don't do arabesques on space.  
One-two-three, the music's grand,  
There's a wet floor sign beside your hand.  
Turn, lift, wait your cue,  
The mop crew has a dance move too.  

Ending
The dancer takes her slippers off; the mop crew takes a bow,  
The curtain closes softly, then the floor is wet again somehow.
