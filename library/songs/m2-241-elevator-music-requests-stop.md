# The Elevator Music Requests a Stop

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: smooth lounge elevator music that promises effortless calm while the lift stalls between floors*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Use brushed drums and velvet piano; let the singer's composure crack whenever the lift chimes.*

[Verse 1]
At the hotel, the brass doors closed with a sigh,  
A macaque in a velvet vest watched floor numbers fly.  
The pianist's soft chords made the ride feel like silk,  
While a baboon balanced a paper cup of milk.  
The lift gave a shudder, then paused in the air,  
The music kept smiling as if nothing were there.  
A capuchin asked softly, "Is this part of the tune?"  
The singer said, "Certainly, we pause at the moon."  

[Chorus]
Take it easy, take it slow,  
The lift is deciding where to go.  
A little more piano, a little less fright,  
We may reach the lobby before tonight.  
Take it easy, stay composed,  
The doors are politely still closed.  

[Verse 2]
The concierge called up through a brass speaking tube,  
"Your floor is unavailable; please enjoy the smooth groove."  
The pianist played strings like a lake in the sun,  
The elevator's ceiling fan clicked one by one.  
A gibbon tried humming along with the bass,  
The lift moved one inch, then returned to its place.  
The singer leaned close with a lounge-lizard grin,  
"That downward excursion was not on the spin."  

[Chorus]
Take it easy, take it slow,  
The lift is deciding where to go.  
A little more piano, a little less fright,  
We may reach the lobby before tonight.  
Take it easy, stay composed,  
The doors are politely still closed.  

[Bridge]
The bell gave one ding for a floor we had passed,  
The cup made a circle, the milk held fast.  
The passengers swayed to the brushed-snare beat,  
Like a cocktail party with nowhere to meet.  
The pianist used the pause as a grand solo break,  
The singer kept smiling with a tremor to fake.  

[Final Chorus]
Take it easy, take it slow,  
The lift is deciding where to go.  
A little more piano, a little less fright,  
We may reach the lobby before tonight.  
Take it easy, take a bow,  
We've reached the thirteenth floor somehow.  

[Ending]
The band played the lobby theme; the singer thanked the stuck lift for its time.
