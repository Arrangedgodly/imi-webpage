# The Pirate Chorus Advertises Office Jobs

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: pirate adventure choruses and ships recruiting more paperwork workers than swaggering sword fighters*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Stomping nautical chorus with concertina; the quartermaster reads job listings in strict rhythm.*

[Verse 1]
The captain sang of daring souls to join his mighty crew,  
He promised golden sunsets and a world beyond the blue.  
A queue formed by the gangplank with their hats and wooden swords,  
The quartermaster held a list of duties and rewards.  
He needed one accounts clerk and a cook for every day,  
A person who could mend the sails, a navigator for the bay.  
The daring souls asked where the sword-fighting role would be,  
The captain said the harbor rules required no swords at sea.  

[Chorus]
Ho, bright crew, let the mast rise high,  
The accounts clerk has a ledger for the treasure we might buy.  
Ho, bright crew, let the wide sea roll,  
The cook has got a shopping list that feeds each daring soul.  
Sail, little ship, chase the golden foam,  
The application has a place for references from home.  

[Verse 2]
A baker joined the cooking crew, a tailor joined the sails,  
A bookkeeper could count the coins and read the harbor mails.  
One hopeful with a compass took the navigator's test,  
The captain checked her route against the map and liked it best.  
The crew sailed out at morning with a cargo full of grain,  
They sold it at the next port and brought oranges back again.  
The captain sang of treasure as the bookkeeper wrote the sum,  
The cook served orange pudding with no sword required to come.  

[Chorus]
Ho, bright crew, let the mast rise high,  
The accounts clerk has a ledger for the treasure we might buy.  
Ho, bright crew, let the wide sea roll,  
The cook has got a shopping list that feeds each daring soul.  
Sail, little ship, chase the golden foam,  
The application has a place for references from home.  

[Bridge]
The sword owners stayed ashore to make a pageant for the fair,  
Their painted weapons clattered on the platform in the square.  
The captain watched the pageant when his ship came back to town,  
The bookkeeper took tickets and wrote every payment down.  

[Final Chorus]
Ho, bright crew, let the wide sea roll,  
The fair has paid the bookkeeper to count its little gold.  
Sail, little ship, chase the golden foam,  
The tailor makes the pageant hats with the spare cloth brought home.  
Ho, bright crew, let the mast rise high,  
The captain needs an office clerk to manage all the jobs nearby.
