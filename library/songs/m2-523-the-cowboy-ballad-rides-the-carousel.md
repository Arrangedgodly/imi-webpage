# The Cowboy Ballad Rides the Carousel

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: western ballads of wandering riders set at a shopping-center carousel with very limited frontiers*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Steady acoustic guitar and a mournful solo vocal; carousel organ enters behind the refrain.*

[Verse 1]
I mounted up my painted horse beside the shopping mall,  
I tipped my hat to distant lands beyond the marble wall.  
The carousel began to move beneath its colored roof,  
I sang about the open range and looked down at the hoof.  
Its feet were screwed upon a pole that carried every stride,  
A child on a blue horse asked how far I'd like to ride.  
I said the mountains called me from the west beyond the plain,  
We passed the same shoe shop that we'd already passed again.  

[Refrain]
Ride, painted horse, take the lonely road,  
The saddle has a metal pole to carry all its load.  
Ride, painted horse, chase the fading sun,  
The frontier is a shopping mall; the journey's just begun.  
Dustless trail, golden mane, let the rider roam,  
Three minutes brings the whole herd very neatly back home.  

[Verse 2]
We passed the little fountain and the kiosk selling tea,  
The blue horse kept beside me as we crossed a paper sea.  
The child asked if the mountains lay behind the bakery,  
I said a circular frontier was a tricky one to see.  
The carousel slowed gently and the operator rang a bell,  
I dismounted on the marble with my frontier left to tell.  
The child rode one more journey while I walked to buy a bun,  
The same blue horse went past me on its next trip round the sun.  

[Refrain]
Ride, painted horse, take the lonely road,  
The saddle has a metal pole to carry all its load.  
Ride, painted horse, chase the fading sun,  
The frontier is a shopping mall; the journey's just begun.  
Dustless trail, golden mane, let the rider roam,  
Three minutes brings the whole herd very neatly back home.  

[Bridge]
I wrote a song about the ride beside my cup of tea,  
The operator heard it and he nodded back to me.  
He asked if I could play it for the children at the fair,  
I said the wandering cowboy would be happy to be there.  

[Final Refrain]
Ride, painted horse, chase the fading sun,  
The operator offers me a ticket and a bun.  
Dustless trail, golden mane, let the rider roam,  
The children know the chorus as their blue horse brings them home.  
Ride, painted horse, take the lonely road,  
The cowboy gets a loyalty stamp for every three-minute load.
