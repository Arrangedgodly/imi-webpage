# The Madrigal for the Mismatched Socks

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Renaissance madrigal harmonies, ornate word painting, and a laundry line whose socks keep staging a dramatic reunion*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Four-part Renaissance-style harmony; each voice should imitate the laundry line swaying in a different direction.*

Verse 1
A gibbon found a scarlet sock and held it to the sun,
A lemur found its yellow mate; the search was nearly done.
The quartet gathered by the line, their sleeves arranged with care,
They sang of cloth and wandering feet and socks that traveled where.

Chorus
Turn, turn, the laundry turns,
One sock waits and one sock yearns.
Fold, fold, the pairs align,
Bring the missing heel to mine.

Verse 2
The tenor sang of silk and lace, the alto sang of wool,
The bass described a basket where the socks were nearly full.
The soprano saw a striped sock waving from the tree,
It had been caught upon a branch and fluttered merrily.

Chorus
Turn, turn, the laundry turns,
One sock waits and one sock yearns.
Fold, fold, the pairs align,
Bring the missing heel to mine.

Bridge
They sang a rising phrase to coax the striped sock down,
The branch replied in harmony and shook the sock around.
The quartet held a final chord; the sock dropped in the bin,
The gibbon matched it with its mate and let the folding begin.

Verse 3
The chorus sorted every pair and placed them by the door,
The laundry line swayed gently, asking nothing more.
A tiny sock remained alone, too small to find its twin,
The lemur made it into a puppet and invited it to sing.

Chorus
Turn, turn, the laundry turns,
One sock waits and one sock yearns.
Fold, fold, the pairs align,
Bring the missing heel to mine.

Ending
The quartet bowed in close accord, the basket stood in line,
The smallest sock took center stage and sang the final rhyme.
