# The Chiptune Hero Fights the Automatic Tap

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: triumphant chiptune game themes and heroic boss battles translated into an automatic sink sensor's erratic behavior*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Square-wave melody, pulsing arpeggios and bright sung hooks; the water cuts out whenever the hero's hands retreat.*

[Verse 1]
I reached the station washroom with my groceries in a tote,  
I left them by the lockers, then I washed my hands with soap.  
The tap released a tiny stream when I approached its eye,  
I shifted just a little left; the precious stream went dry.  
I waved my hands like magic spells before a glowing gate,  
The sensor answered one brief burst and left me there to wait.  
The stranger at the next sink had his water flowing free,  
He held his hands quite still and looked across the room at me.  

[Chorus]
Level up, water flow,  
I have reached the final sensor but I cannot make it go.  
One more life, one more try,  
The soap is on my fingers and the tap is running dry.  
Level up, hold your ground,  
The hero needs a steady pair of hands to win this round.  

[Verse 2]
I tried the legendary gesture I had learned in level two,  
A sweeping arc above the basin made a drop or two come through.  
The stranger pointed quietly to the sensor's little square,  
He said to hold my fingers underneath and keep them there.  
I reached into the little stream and rinsed the soap away,  
The tap kept flowing evenly; I let my hands both stay.  
I dried them on a paper towel and gave a winning grin,  
The stranger pointed to the bin; I dropped the paper in.  

[Chorus]
Level up, water flow,  
I have reached the final sensor but I cannot make it go.  
One more life, one more try,  
The soap is on my fingers and the tap is running dry.  
Level up, hold your ground,  
The hero needs a steady pair of hands to win this round.  

[Bridge]
The automatic door ahead refused to open wide,  
I stood too near its sensor and then took one step aside.  
It slid apart quite smoothly; I escaped the station hall,  
My final boss was finding all my bags beside the wall.  

[Final Chorus]
Level up, water flow,  
I found my bags beside the locker where I left them by the door.  
One more life, one more try,  
The train doors shut without me; my recovered bags are dry.  
Level up, hear that sound,  
The next train is an hour away; I have one more waiting round.
