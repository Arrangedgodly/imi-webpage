# The Farewell Tour Adds Another Date

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rock farewell tours that turn every last performance into an invitation to another last performance*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Emotional stadium rock with a crowd singalong; the promoter interrupts the final chord with new dates.*

[Verse 1]
We called it our last concert in a town beside the sea,  
The singer thanked the road crew and the fans of ninety-three.  
The drummer held his sticks up as the lights began to fade,  
The bassist wiped his eyes upon the banner we had made.  
The singer said the final song was all we had to give,  
The promoter handed him a note about a place to live.  

[Chorus]
Goodbye, goodbye, remember us this way,  
This is the last time we will ever come to play.  
Keep our little melody and hold it in your heart,  
Buy the final souvenir before the curtains part.  
Goodbye, goodbye, the road has reached its end,  
Except a little extra road around another bend.  

[Verse 2]
We played the extra concert for the people who'd been late,  
The promoter found another town with one available date.  
We changed the farewell banner with a roll of silver tape,  
The drummer packed his sticks again and tried a small escape.  
The bassist booked a cottage where he'd garden for the year,  
The promoter booked the field beside it for our final cheer.  

[Chorus]
Goodbye, goodbye, remember us this way,  
This is the last time we will ever come to play.  
Keep our little melody and hold it in your heart,  
Buy the final souvenir before the curtains part.  
Goodbye, goodbye, the road has reached its end,  
Except a little extra road around another bend.  

[Bridge]
The band locked up the trailer and put all the keys away,  
We sat beneath the cottage trees and swore we wouldn't play.  
The neighbors asked for just one tune to bless the new address,  
The drummer tapped his gardening fork and watched the crowd grow less.  

[Final Chorus]
Goodbye, goodbye, remember us this way,  
This is the last time we will ever come to play.  
Keep our little melody and hold it in your heart,  
The singer sells a flower pot before the curtains part.  
Goodbye, goodbye, the road has reached its end,  
The promoter books our potting shed; the farewell starts again.
