# The Villain Song Loses Its Spotlight

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: theatrical villain songs whose menace depends entirely on lighting cues*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Sinister cabaret piano and swelling brass; a work-light hum replaces the orchestra at the villain's reveal.*

[Verse 1]
I waited in the castle with a cape of deepest black,  
The orchestra inhaled as I prepared my grand attack.  
I lifted up one finger to command the moon to die,  
The spotlight caught the gardener who was merely walking by.  
I hissed across the darkness that the world would know my name,  
The audience watched the gardener and applauded when he came.  

[Chorus]
Look at me, behold my power,  
I shall rule before the hour.  
Every shadow bends to me,  
Except the one where you can see.  
Fear my smile and fear my stare,  
Could someone put a little light where I am standing there?  

[Verse 2]
The light swept to the staircase and revealed my laundry bin,  
My spare silk cape hung limply with a sock tucked deep within.  
I sang about my enemies and all I'd make them pay,  
The operator lit my shopping list for market day.  
I stepped into the brilliant circle with my arms held wide,  
It moved beside the curtain; I moved after it in pride.  

[Chorus]
Look at me, behold my power,  
I shall rule before the hour.  
Every shadow bends to me,  
Except the one where you can see.  
Fear my smile and fear my stare,  
Could someone put a little light where I am standing there?  

[Bridge]
The gardener switched the work lights on to finish up his chore,  
My cardboard castle glittered as it hadn't done before.  
My thunder sheet was hanging from a bent and rusty stand,  
I covered up the price tag with my world-commanding hand.  

[Final Chorus]
Look at me, behold my power,  
I shall rule before the hour.  
Every shadow bends to me,  
Except the ones you clearly see.  
Fear my smile and fear my stare,  
The gardener bows beside me and takes my villain chair.
