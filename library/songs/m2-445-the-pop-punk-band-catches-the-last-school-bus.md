# The Pop-Punk Band Catches the Last School Bus

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: pop-punk songs about youthful rebellion, impatient escape and bands whose glamorous tour transport remains a school bus*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Fast bright guitars and group shout vocals; the driver delivers the practical schedule in a bored voice beneath the last chorus.*

[Verse 1]
We played the lunchtime talent show and packed the little amps,  
The singer said the world was ours beyond the schoolyard lamps.  
We headed for the front gate with our jackets flapping wide,  
The drummer saw the school bus and the driver waiting inside.  
Our rebel anthem promised we would leave this place for good,  
The bassist checked the route board; it was going to our neighborhood.  
We ran across the pavement with the cases in our hands,  
The driver said to hurry; he had other stops to plan.  

[Chorus]
Get out, go fast, leave the town behind,  
The school bus has a schedule and the driver wants us inside.  
Big dreams, loud chords, never settle down,  
We are taking all our rebellion toward the houses where we're bound.  
Get out, go fast, let the bright day run,  
The drummer's mom is waiting at the final stop at one.  

[Verse 2]
We stacked the little cases underneath the empty seat,  
The driver said the aisle must stay as clear as it could be.  
The singer wrote a travel song about the open road,  
The bus stopped at the crossing and the bassist said, "That's home."  
He got off with his bass case and a sandwich from his bag,  
The drummer left at Maple Street with sticks beside his tag.  
The singer stayed till the terminus and stepped out with the amp,  
His dad was there to carry it and asked about the band.  

[Chorus]
Get out, go fast, leave the town behind,  
The school bus has a schedule and the driver wants us inside.  
Big dreams, loud chords, never settle down,  
We are taking all our rebellion toward the houses where we're bound.  
Get out, go fast, let the bright day run,  
The drummer's mom is waiting at the final stop at one.  

[Bridge]
At practice in the garage we wrote a tour report,  
Five miles, four stops, one crossing, and a very early start.  
We called the bus our first tour van and drew it on the sleeve,  
The driver asked for royalties; we gave him tickets for free.  

[Final Chorus]
Get out, go fast, let the bright day run,  
Our driver brings the next band home when school is finally done.  
Big dreams, loud chords, keep the chorus free,  
His route has got more sold-out seats than any gig we've seen.  
Get out, go fast, shout it to the town,  
Tomorrow we board his bus again, with school still to be found.
