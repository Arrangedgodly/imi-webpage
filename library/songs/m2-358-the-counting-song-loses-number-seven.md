# The Counting Song Loses Number Seven

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: children's counting songs disrupted by the cast's literal interpretation of number roles*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Bright ukulele and handclaps; leave one conspicuous beat empty whenever seven should be sung.*

[Verse 1]
We lined ten little monkeys up to teach the numbers through,  
The first wore one, the next wore two, each knew what they should do.  
We sang the ones who stood in line and pointed as we went,  
But seven went to get a drink and left a little dent.  
The teacher held her pointing stick above an empty place,  
The number eight stepped forward with a most suspicious face.  

[Chorus]
One and two and three and four,  
Five and six and... who went through the door?  
Eight and nine and ten stand near,  
We have ten little numbers but only nine are here.  
Clap once more and count again,  
There's a thirsty little space between six and eight, my friend.  

[Verse 2]
The teacher moved the number eight to stand beside the six,  
He said his name was eight and wouldn't join her little tricks.  
The nine announced that moving down would not erase his nine,  
The ten put both his feet apart to occupy the line.  
The teacher drew a seven on a spare piece of the board,  
The monkeys bowed politely but the cardboard looked quite bored.  

[Chorus]
One and two and three and four,  
Five and six and... who went through the door?  
Eight and nine and ten stand near,  
We have ten little numbers but only nine are here.  
Clap once more and count again,  
There's a thirsty little space between six and eight, my friend.  

[Bridge]
The seven came back smiling with a cup held in his hand,  
He took his place and sang his name beside the little band.  
The teacher raised her pointing stick to finish up the rhyme,  
The number three asked for a drink and left the second time.  

[Final Chorus]
One and two and... and four,  
Five and six and seven, three went through the door.  
Eight and nine and ten stand near,  
Nine little numbers drink; number three is not here.  
Clap once more and count again,  
The teacher gives them water and waits to sing till then.
