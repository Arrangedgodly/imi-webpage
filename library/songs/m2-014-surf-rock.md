# Boardwalk Amp Crash

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: surf rock guitar twang and beach-town bravado*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Reverb-drenched guitar, snare like a breaking wave, and no actual surfing indoors.*

## Verse 1
The boardwalk band rehearsed beside the bait shop sign.  
A squirrel monkey tuned his guitar to salt and pine.  
His amp had one loose knob, his shoes were full of sand.  
The drummer kept a towel tucked beneath his hand.  
A wave broke hard against the old sea wall.  
The band hit one bright chord and startled all.  
A lifeguard checked the tide, then checked their set.  
He said, "Play something that the kids won't forget."  

## Chorus
Ride that twang, let the white spray fly.  
Pick one fast wave, then let it roll by.  
Ride that twang till the sun slips low.  
The boardwalk has a current of its own to show.  

## Verse 2
The amp gave out halfway through the big refrain.  
The guitarist played unplugged above the hiss of rain.  
The crowd came closer, forming half a ring.  
A child used two flip-flops as a cymbal thing.  
The lifeguard kept the beat by tapping on his chair.  
A vendor sold hot corn and danced beside the stair.  
They finished on the sand, the strings still warm.  
Then carried amps inside ahead of the storm.  

## Bridge
No one rode a barrel, no one claimed a crown.  
The only wave they conquered was a sound.  
The guitarist thanked the child for keeping time.  
The kid asked for a pick and took one home to shine.  

## Chorus
Ride that twang, let the white spray fly.  
Pick one fast wave, then let it roll by.  
Ride that twang till the sun slips low.  
The boardwalk has a current of its own to show.  

## Ending
Next week the boardwalk held a calmer tide.  
The amp had a new knob, the band played outside.
