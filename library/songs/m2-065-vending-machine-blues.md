# Twelve Bars for the Vending Machine

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: twelve-bar blues complaints, bent-note sorrow, and the ritual of losing one coin to a machine*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-002*

*Performance note: Slow electric blues with brushed snare; let the singer's optimism rise when the snack finally drops, then fall at the refund window.*

Verse 1
A squirrel watches as I put my coin inside the box; the little light turned red,  
I pressed the button twice and heard a clunk above my head.  
That machine took my nickel, now my nickel's feeling blue,  
I leaned to see the snack shelf, and my cap fell in there too.  

Chorus
Oh, that vending machine is holding out on me,  
It has my cap and pretzel and my last dime's memory.  
I shake it gently left and right; the clerk says, "Don't do that,"  
Now the machine has got my sleeve, my nickel, and my hat.  

Verse 2
A squirrel brings a toolbox, says the hinge is feeling tight,  
The janitor says, "Unplug it," and switches off the light.  
We open up the service door and find the hidden track,  
The pretzel's wedged behind a coil, the cap is in the back.  

Bridge
The clerk hands me a claim form, it asks for date and code,  
I write down "Tuesday, lunchtime," and sketch the snack machine mode.  
The manager returns my nickel, cap, and pretzel too,  
The machine keeps one small crumb and says it's owed its due.  

Chorus
Oh, that vending machine is holding out on me,  
It has my cap and pretzel and my last dime's memory.  
I shake it gently left and right; the clerk says, "Don't do that,"  
Now the machine has got one crumb, my nickel, and my hat.  

Ending
I walk away with cap and snack, the blues have turned to gray,  
The vending box drops one more bag; it wants me back to pay.
