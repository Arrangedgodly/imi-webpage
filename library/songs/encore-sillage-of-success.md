# Sillage of Success

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Luxury perfume advertising set to sleek electro-pop*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: Cool synth pulse, breathy ad voice, then a chorus too grand for a breakroom.*

[Verse 1]  
At dawn, in the glass tower, Mara lifts the cap,  
A scent of polished mahogany escapes the sample trap.  
The bottle has a tiny gold key and a velvet little rope,  
It guards the fragrance like a guarded annual bonus hope.  
A monkey from the mailroom walks past and sniffs the air,  
Then turns around and asks if lunch is hiding somewhere there.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The label says "Executive No. 9, distilled from alpine rain."  
The invoice says "vanilla, citrus, and a hint of copier stain."  
The brand team says the top note is ambition, sharp and clean.  
The janitor says it smells like someone microwaved a nectarine.  

[Chorus]  
Wear the meeting, wear the room, wear the look of knowing why.  
Leave a trail of quiet power every time you pass the pie.  
One bright mist, and all the interns straighten up their ties.  
Sillage of Success, now with a quarterly refill size.  

[Verse 2]  
They shut the sample in a drawer; the drawer begins to glow.  
A tiny card slides out that says, "Your personal brand can grow."  
Mara sprays it on the conference chair to claim the seat.  
The chair now smells like polished wood and very costly feet.  
The office monkey takes one sniff, then gives a solemn nod:  
"It smells like a promotion that was promised by a god."  

[Bridge]  
The refill plan renews itself whenever someone says "ambition."  
The bottle has a chatbot that keeps asking for permission.  
We canceled it at settings, page eleven, under "Aroma."  
The cap still sends us push alerts from somewhere in the aroma.  

[Final Chorus]  
Wear the meeting, wear the room, wear the look of knowing why.  
Leave a trail of quiet power every time you pass the pie.  
One bright mist, and all the interns straighten up their ties.  
Sillage of Success, now with a quarterly refill size.  

[Outro]  
By five the scent has left the room, the invoice has not gone.  
The sample's locked behind reception. The janitor wears cologne.
