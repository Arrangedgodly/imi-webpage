# Flamenco at the Umbrella Stand

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: flamenco's dramatic footwork, handclaps, and proud declarations of heartbreak*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Guitar rasgueado and sharp heelwork; rain begins exactly when the singer makes a grand claim.*

Verse 1
A mandrill wore a scarlet sash and strode into the square,
He raised one empty umbrella high and swore the sky was fair.
"I bought this for my former flame," he cried, "and now I'm free!"
A raindrop struck the painted silk and tapped a minor key.

Chorus
Clap the hands and stamp the floor,
Love has left; the clouds want more.
Turn the handle, lift it high,
Let the thunder judge my lie.

Verse 2
His heels went hard against the tiles; the puddles clapped along,
A capuchin guitar player made the drip a driving song.
He spun the umbrella, posed, then let it turn inside out,
The crowd applauded every gust and shouted, "Rain it out!"

Chorus
Clap the hands and stamp the floor,
Love has left; the clouds want more.
Turn the handle, lift it high,
Let the thunder judge my lie.

Bridge
The singer cried, "I'll never dance for anyone again!"
His ex came back to fetch a scarf and joined the claps in rain.
He offered her the umbrella; its ribs had split in two,
She used it as a castanet and struck the chorus through.

Verse 3
They faced each other, heels in time, their argument on beat,
He blamed the wind; she blamed his pose; the puddles filled the street.
The guitar climbed, the thunder rolled, the crowd called for encore,
They danced beneath the broken shade and asked the rain for more.

Chorus
Clap the hands and stamp the floor,
Love has left; the clouds want more.
Turn the handle, lift it high,
Let the thunder judge my lie.

Ending
He kept the sash, she kept the handle, both declared they'd won;
The rain took every grand farewell and made it a duet begun.
