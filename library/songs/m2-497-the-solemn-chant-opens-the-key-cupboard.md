# The Solemn Chant Opens the Key Cupboard

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sonorous ceremonial chants and groups disguising a mundane search for a spare key as a moment of revelation*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Unison low voices and a small hand drum; names of cupboard hooks are elongated into grave musical phrases.*

[Invocation]
O little door that will not move,  
O turning lock that waits to prove,  
We bring our hands, we bring our plea,  
The caretaker would like the key.  

[Verse 1]
The hall was closed for cleaning as the ceremony began,  
The singers stood outside its door and sang what they had planned.  
The caretaker had left the key inside a cupboard down the lane,  
She asked the choir's youngest voice to fetch it back again.  
He found three labeled metal hooks beneath a little light,  
One for the shed, one for the hall, one office key.  
He sang the names in solemn time to keep the sound profound,  
The hall key waited quietly while all the names went round.  

[Response]
Shed, hall, office, let the labels stay,  
The key to revelation is the middle one today.  
Shed, hall, office, turn the little sound,  
The cupboard has three hooks and there are three keys hanging round.  
O little door that will not move,  
The caretaker has something ordinary left to prove.  

[Verse 2]
He took the middle key and walked it back along the street,  
The singers gave a rising chord to welcome both his feet.  
The caretaker unlocked the hall and opened up the door,  
The choir sang their planned first chant upon the polished floor.  
They finished all the ceremony with a graceful final beat,  
The caretaker swept the hall before the evening came complete.  
The youngest voice returned the key beneath its printed name,  
The cupboard had three keys once more; the hooks stayed just the same.  

[Response]
Shed, hall, office, let the labels stay,  
The key to revelation is the middle one today.  
Shed, hall, office, turn the little sound,  
The cupboard has three hooks and there are three keys hanging round.  
O little door that will not move,  
The caretaker has something ordinary left to prove.  

[Closing Invocation]
O little door now closed again,  
O key returned beside the pen,  
We leave the labels where they be,  
The next event will need the key.  

[Spoken Tag]
The caretaker books the choir for another winter night.  
She sends a photograph of the hooks beneath the light.  
The youngest voice replies with one long, very solemn note,  
She prints it as his answer and puts "Yes" beneath the quote.
