# Salsa on the Sap Cart

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: salsa dance-band conventions, hot brass breaks, and a sap seller whose cart joins the dance*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Bright brass, clave, and rolling hand percussion; make the cart's squeak a rhythmic answer.*

Verse 1
The sap cart rolled at sunset to the plaza by the tree,
A macaque set the bottles out and shouted, "Two for three!"
The band began a salsa, and the trumpet called the crowd,
The cart wheel squeaked a sharp reply, surprisingly loud.

Chorus
Roll, little cart, roll in time,
Shake your wheel and catch the chime.
Step, little feet, cross the square,
Sap is sweet and music's there.

Verse 2
The dancers formed a circle; the cart rolled through the middle,
The drummer hit a rolling break; the bottles gave a riddle.
A gibbon spun beside it while the vendor grabbed the rail,
The cart performed a turn so neat it nearly won the sale.

Chorus
Roll, little cart, roll in time,
Shake your wheel and catch the chime.
Step, little feet, cross the square,
Sap is sweet and music's there.

Bridge
The trumpet took a solo; the crowd called back "Olé!"
The cart rolled toward the fountain; the vendor yelled, "No way!"
A capuchin caught the handle and redirected its glide,
The wheel squeaked on the offbeat as the whole plaza took a ride.

Verse 3
They parked it by the bandstand, where the dancers made a lane,
The vendor served the sap in cups and joined the brass refrain.
The cart kept one small squeak for every turn and slide,
The crowd bought every bottle, then danced home satisfied.

Chorus
Roll, little cart, roll in time,
Shake your wheel and catch the chime.
Step, little feet, cross the square,
Sap is sweet and music's there.

Ending
The band played one last flourish; the cart rolled out of sight,
Its wheel squeaked one more salsa step beneath the lantern light.
