# Breakdance on the Pier

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: breakdance battles, freeze poses, and crowd circles that keep shifting underfoot*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Start with breakbeat drums and scratch accents; let each freeze pose be interrupted by the pier's slow sway.*

[Verse 1]
The dancers made a circle on the boardwalk by the sea,  
A capuchin dropped low and said, "The floor belongs to me."  
The DJ cut the record; the snare came sharp and bright,  
A macaque spun on one hand and froze beneath the light.  
The pier moved with the water, a gentle wooden roll,  
The dancer held his balance with a very focused soul.  
A gibbon leaned in close to judge the finish of the pose,  
The whole crowd leaned together as the ocean breeze arose.  

[Chorus]
Freeze, spin, drop, come back around,  
The breakbeat shakes the harbor ground.  
Make a circle, give some space,  
The pier is shifting under place.  
Freeze, spin, don't let it sway,  
The tide is dancing in the break today.  

[Verse 2]
The rival wore bright sneakers and a jacket full of shine,  
He started a windmill move and crossed the painted line.  
The boardwalk's wind sock spun above the snack stand roof,  
The dancer froze beneath it, holding still through every gust.  
The DJ scratched a siren; the seagulls took the cue,  
They circled low above the crowd and scattered hats like confetti flew.  
The capuchin came upright, the macaque found her feet,  
They battled with a footwork step that matched the tide's retreat.  

[Chorus]
Freeze, spin, drop, come back around,  
The breakbeat shakes the harbor ground.  
Make a circle, give some space,  
The pier is shifting under place.  
Freeze, spin, don't let it sway,  
The tide is dancing in the break today.  

[Bridge]
The pier staff asked the dancers not to spin near the rail,  
They moved the circle inland, still keeping every tale.  
The DJ slowed the record; the boards stopped seeming wide,  
The final pose faced shoreward as the tide went out with pride.  

[Final Chorus]
Freeze, spin, then clear the floor,  
The crowd has made a wider door.  
Make a circle, give some space,  
The pier has found a steadier place.  
Freeze, spin, let the crowd decide,  
The seagulls take the trophy for the best windmill ride.  

[Ending]
The DJ stopped the track; one hat kept spinning by the sea.
