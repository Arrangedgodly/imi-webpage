# The Bluegrass Fiddler Shares a Broken Bow

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bluegrass instrumental races and performers discovering that speed is less useful than having a working bow*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Quick banjo and steady bass; the fiddle waits for the guitarist to retrieve a spare bow from the case.*

[Verse 1]
We gathered for the harvest dance with boots upon the floor,  
The banjo gave the opening and the dancers asked for more.  
I drew my bow across the strings to chase his flying tune,  
The hair came loose in one pale cloud beneath the harvest moon.  
The banjo kept on racing as I held the useless stick,  
The bass held down the dancers while the guitar kept the tick.  
I looked inside my fiddle case; the spare was back at home,  
My solo waited silently while other notes could roam.  

[Chorus]
Run, little fiddle, run along the track,  
The banjo's gone around the bend and won't come racing back.  
Run, little fiddle, take the dusty road,  
The bow has left its hair behind and cannot bear the load.  
Hold the beat, keep the dancers turning round,  
The fastest player in the band is making no sound.  

[Verse 2]
The guitarist remembered bringing his old fiddle too,  
Its case lay in the dressing room with a bow that we could use.  
He stopped his strumming for a verse and fetched it from the chair,  
He handed me the working bow and saw the scattered hair.  
I picked up at the chorus where the banjo met the bass,  
The dancers never stopped their steps to watch our little race.  
We finished with a closing chord beneath the lights above,  
The borrowed bow played every note as if it knew the love.  

[Chorus]
Run, little fiddle, run along the track,  
The banjo's gone around the bend and won't come racing back.  
Run, little fiddle, take the dusty road,  
The bow has left its hair behind and cannot bear the load.  
Hold the beat, keep the dancers turning round,  
The fastest player in the band is making no sound.  

[Bridge]
I gave the guitarist his bow before we packed the car,  
He played one little fiddle tune beside his own guitar.  
The banjo player listened and he nodded through the night,  
A spare bow had a solo in the dressing room's small light.  

[Final Chorus]
Run, little fiddle, run along the track,  
Tomorrow I've a new bow and a spare one in the pack.  
Hold the beat, keep the dancers turning round,  
The guitarist gets the first tune when we reach the next town.  
Run, little fiddle, take the dusty road,  
The fastest player buys the strings the borrowed player is owed.
