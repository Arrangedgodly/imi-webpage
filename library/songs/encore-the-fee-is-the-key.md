# The Fee Is the Key

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Broadway-style villain showstoppers and predatory ticket surcharges*
*By: IMI Records Encore Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

*Performance note: A velvet-voiced villain sings beside a ticket kiosk that prints faster than it listens.*

[Verse 1]  
Come closer, little monkey, choose your seat and date.  
The ticket says ten dollars. That's the charming little bait.  
The service fee is seven, the delivery fee is nine.  
The velvet-rope protection plan is extra, by design.  
The checkout page is smiling. The total climbs with glee.  
Now click the box that says, "I love the fees expressed."  

[Chorus]  
The fee is the key, the key is the gate.  
The gate is the button that adds one more rate.  
I'll take your small coin, then your medium and large.  
My kingdom is the checkout. My throne is the charge.  

[Verse 2]  
My cape is made of receipts. My crown is made of QR.  
I glide across the ballroom in a borrowed golf cart car.  
The usher asks for payment just to point toward the door.  
The ticket kiosk says the pointing plan costs more.  
A little monkey whispers, "Could you show me the base?"  
I say, "That is a secret kept in a passworded place."  
He finds the tiny link, the one in gray on gray.  
He clicks it and the screen says, "Try again another day."  

[Chorus]  
The fee is the key, the key is the gate.  
The gate is the button that adds one more rate.  
I'll take your small coin, then your medium and large.  
My kingdom is the checkout. My throne is the charge.  

[Bridge]  
But then the crowd unplugs the kiosk from the wall.  
They pass one paper ticket hand to hand through the hall.  
The villain loses power when the screen goes dark.  
The monkey at the door lets every guest embark.  

[Final Chorus]  
The fee is the key, the key is the gate.  
The gate is now open, and the price is the rate.  
Keep your small coin, your medium and large.  
My kingdom is empty. I have lost the charge.  

[Outro]  
The villain takes the cape and exits through the side.  
The band plays one free note. The audience goes inside.
