# The Vaudeville Cane Keeps Changing Hands

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: vaudeville prop comedy, fast patter, and performers who keep handing off the same prop before its gag lands*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Piano patter with a cane-tap break; make every handoff crisp until the performers lose track of whose cane it is.*

**Verse 1**
The vaudeville team arrives with one black cane between the three.  
The host says, "Watch it vanish," then forgets to count to three.  
A tumbler takes the cane and uses it to balance high.  
The juggler catches it below; the singer asks him why.  
The cane becomes a microphone, a pointer, then a spear.  
The stagehand tries to claim it, but the dancers pull it near.  

**Chorus**
Pass it left, pass it right, make the old cane fly;  
Tap it twice, bow your head, wave it to the sky.  
One prop, three acts, the gag is never done;  
The cane changes hands before the laugh has begun.  

**Verse 2**
The singer starts a comic verse about a missing stick.  
The juggler cuts across the rhyme with a quick and dizzy trick.  
The tumbler slips, the cane rolls down the polished stage.  
The stagehand stops it with his shoe and checks its serial page.  
The host says, "That is not our cane; ours has a silver tip."  
They look at every actor's hand; the silver tip has slipped.  

**Chorus**
Pass it left, pass it right, make the old cane fly;  
Tap it twice, bow your head, wave it to the sky.  
One prop, three acts, the gag is never done;  
The cane changes hands before the laugh has begun.  

**Bridge**
The stagehand reads the label and gives the cane back home.  
The cast agrees to one last gag: they all refuse to hold.  
It stands against the wing; it falls upon the floor.  
The host picks it up, then passes it once more.  

**Ending**
The curtain drops; the cane remains backstage at last.  
The stagehand hangs it on its hook and walks away fast.  
The curtain swings and strikes the hook; the cane rolls to the lip.  
The whole cast reaches for it, then stops the final bit.
