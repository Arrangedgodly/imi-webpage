# The Trap Producer Samples Breakfast

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: trap production tutorials and luxury boasts built from household sounds that reveal a very ordinary morning*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Rapid hi-hats, deep bass and clipped rap vocals; cereal rattles, spoon clicks and a kettle whistle supply the original samples.*

[Verse 1]
I filmed a studio tutorial beside the kitchen sink,  
My headphones looked expensive and my robe was salmon pink.  
I said the sound was custom, every texture was elite,  
Then shook the cereal packet for a crisp repeating beat.  
I pitched the kettle lower till it growled beneath the track,  
The spoon became a snare hit with a little metal crack.  
My housemate opened up the door and asked about the noise,  
I said the bass was cooking; he said, "Breakfast first, big boy."  

[Chorus]
Shake the box, make it shine,  
The finest little percussion has a breakfast label line.  
Drop the spoon, let it snap,  
There is milk beneath the keyboard and a raisin in my lap.  
Deep bass, fast hats, luxury at dawn,  
I have sampled half the kitchen for the track we're working on.  

[Verse 2]
The rapper came at half past eight to lay his vocals down,  
He claimed a golden breakfast in the finest part of town.  
The cereal bag split open when I shook it once again,  
The rapper ducked a cornflake and forgot his golden chain.  
My housemate took the kettle off and poured us each a cup,  
The sound recorder captured every spoon he lifted up.  
The rapper heard the boiling fade and changed his boastful line,  
"My housemate brings the finest tea; he gets a producer sign."  

[Chorus]
Shake the box, make it shine,  
The finest little percussion has a breakfast label line.  
Drop the spoon, let it snap,  
There is milk beneath the keyboard and a raisin in my lap.

[Bridge]
We wiped the counter carefully and finished off the tea,  
The housemate gave permission for his spoon to join the beat.  
The record label called us for the secret of the bass,  
We sent a shopping list instead of any studio specs.  

[Final Chorus]
Shake the box, make it shine,  
The cereal wants a credit on the back of every sign.  
Drop the spoon, let it snap,  
Our luxury is breakfast and a clean recording lap.  
Deep bass, fast hats, raise the kettle high,  
The housemate sells the remix with a toast rack on the side.
