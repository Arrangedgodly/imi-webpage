# The Narrow Hallway Polka

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: polka's bouncing oom-pah pulse and increasingly rapid partner turns*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-007*
*Performance note: Start with a modest oom-pah and accelerate each chorus; keep the dancers in their marked narrow lane.*

[Verse 1]
The community hall was booked for polka night at eight,  
But chairs were stacked along the wall and narrowed every gate.  
A baboon brought accordion, a capuchin brought shoes,  
A macaque drew a dancing line and said, "We cannot lose."  
The dancers paired up carefully, their elbows tucked inside,  
The bass went oom, the snare went pah, the partners took a stride.  
The first turn cleared the coat rack, the second missed a broom,  
The accordion kept bouncing in the narrow hallway room.  

[Chorus]
Oom-pah, step small, turn with care,  
The polka needs a little extra air.  
Oom-pah, spin, but mind the wall,  
There is not room for a grand ballroom at all.  
Oom-pah, step, keep moving through,  
The hallway's dancing right along with you.  

[Verse 2]
The host called for a faster tune to raise the dancing cheer,  
The pairs went round in tiny loops and bumped the chandelier.  
A gibbon turned too quickly and got tangled in a coat,  
The accordion played a rescue phrase and pulled him by the note.  
The dancers made a conga line and wound around the stairs,  
The band got squeezed beside a pile of folding chairs.  
The drummer's sticks went left and right, the bass slid down the floor,  
The polka train emerged again through the kitchen door.  

[Chorus]
Oom-pah, step small, turn with care,  
The polka needs a little extra air.  
Oom-pah, spin, but mind the wall,  
There is not room for a grand ballroom at all.  
Oom-pah, step, keep moving through,  
The hallway's dancing right along with you.  

[Bridge]
The manager moved one chair, the hallway gained a foot,  
The dancers made a perfect circle, then found the circle stuck.  
They slowed the beat and changed the turn to a careful sway,  
The accordion made a tiny bow and let the guests have space.  

[Final Chorus]
Oom-pah, step small, turn around,  
The room is narrow but the beat is sound.  
Oom-pah, spin, then clear the way,  
The chairs come back tomorrow for the matinee.  
Oom-pah, step, the band plays through,  
The hallway's dancing right along with you.  

[Ending]
They finished in one neat line, still holding their partners' coats.
