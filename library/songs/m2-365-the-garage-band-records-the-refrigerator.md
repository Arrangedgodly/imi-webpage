# The Garage Band Records the Refrigerator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: garage-rock bands who confuse a gritty recording with uncontrolled household noise*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Fuzzy guitar and a raw drum sound; keep the refrigerator compressor audibly below the hook.*

[Verse 1]
We booked my uncle's garage for our first authentic track,  
The drummer found a paint tin and the bassist found a jack.  
The fridge stood in the corner full of food for Sunday lunch,  
The guitarist kicked his pedal and produced a mighty crunch.  
The singer yelled the opening line, the fridge began to hum,  
The engineer announced that we had found our second drum.  

[Chorus]
Keep it raw, keep it rough,  
The garage knows when we've had enough.  
Turn it up and let it shake,  
The little fridge has more low end than all the notes we make.  
Play it loud and play it free,  
There's a freezer full of solos in the corner behind me.  

[Verse 2]
We moved the microphone closer to the battered speaker grille,  
The compressor kept on buzzing with a most impressive skill.  
The singer asked for silence so he'd try the middle eight,  
The ice tray dropped a cube before he reached his angry state.  
The engineer cut all the vocals, said the cube was very clean,  
He looped it through the chorus with the motor in between.  

[Chorus]
Keep it raw, keep it rough,  
The garage knows when we've had enough.  
Turn it up and let it shake,  
The little fridge has more low end than all the notes we make.  
Play it loud and play it free,  
There's a freezer full of solos in the corner behind me.  

[Bridge]
My uncle pulled the plug to stop the noise and serve the food,  
The band stood in the silence with a most authentic mood.  
We tried the chorus one more time; it sounded rather thin,  
The singer wheeled the fridge toward us and plugged it back again.  

[Final Chorus]
Keep it raw, keep it rough,  
The garage knows when we've had enough.  
Turn it up and let it shake,  
Our little fridge has more low end than all the notes we make.  
Play it loud and play it free,  
The label signs the fridge and asks the four of us to leave.
