# The Samba Parade Counts the Umbrellas

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: samba parade spectacle and costume coordinators trying to make a rainstorm look like planned choreography*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Fast percussion and group vocals; umbrellas open on the beat and briefly hide all the matching hats.*

[Verse 1]
We made our feathered hats for the parade beside the bay,  
The weather card had promised us a bright and shining day.  
A shower came across the street before the second bend,  
The costume keeper had umbrellas ready for the end.  
She passed them down the line in pairs before the hats got wet,  
The dancers kept their rhythm while the feathers left the set.  
A row of black umbrellas hid the colors from the crowd,  
The drummer played them forward as the rain became quite loud.  

[Chorus]
Turn in the rain, let the umbrellas rise,  
The feathers need a little roof beneath the cloudy skies.  
Turn in the rain, let the black tops sway,  
The color of the carnival has briefly gone away.  
Shake the beat, move the feet, keep the line in time,  
The costume keeper counts each roof as it goes down the line.  

[Verse 2]
The rain stopped by the harbor and the sunlight found the street,  
The keeper gave a closing cue upon a steady beat.  
The umbrellas folded down; the colored feathers sprang to view,  
The crowd applauded loudly at the picture turning new.  
She stored the folded umbrellas in the trolley at the back,  
The dancers showed their costumes with no little roofs of black.  
The parade reached its ending with the hats as dry as before,  
The keeper checked the trolley and the tally of twenty-four.  

[Chorus]
Turn in the rain, let the umbrellas rise,  
The feathers need a little roof beneath the cloudy skies.  
Turn in the rain, let the black tops sway,  
The color of the carnival has briefly gone away.  
Shake the beat, move the feet, keep the line in time,  
The costume keeper counts each roof as it goes down the line.  

[Bridge]
The local paper praised the way our colors had appeared,  
The keeper had a word about the cloud that she had feared.  
She filed the rain cue in the show for next year's afternoon,  
The drummer wrote a short new break beneath the opening tune.  

[Final Chorus]
Turn in the rain, let the umbrellas rise,  
The next parade has roofs prepared for cloudy summer skies.  
Shake the beat, move the feet, keep the line in time,  
The keeper has a weather card with no promise quite so fine.  
Turn in the rain, let the black tops sway,  
The crowd asks for the colored hats on a completely sunny day.
