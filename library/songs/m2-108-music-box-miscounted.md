# The Music Box Miscounted

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: music-box waltz conventions, clockwork repetition, and a dancer who keeps winding the box instead of letting it end*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Delicate music-box waltz with a wind-up key; slow slightly each time the melody repeats.*

Verse 1
A capuchin found a music box beneath the theater stair,
It played a tiny waltz whenever someone wound the air.
A gibbon dancer heard the tune and stepped into a turn,
The box played through the chorus while the key began to burn.

Chorus
Turn once, turn twice, turn softly round,
Let the little melody circle the sound.
When the last bright note has gone, let the music rest,
Wind it once, not seven times, and the ending sounds best.

Verse 2
The dancer finished one slow turn; the music did not stop,
She lifted up the little lid and found the gears would hop.
The stagehand wound it once again to keep the tune alive,
The dancer made a second turn and missed the curtain dive.

Chorus
Turn once, turn twice, turn softly round,
Let the little melody circle the sound.
When the last bright note has gone, let the music rest,
Wind it once, not seven times, and the ending sounds best.

Bridge
The clockwork played the first bar, then the first bar once again,
The dancer kept repeating till she wore a tiny grin.
She closed the lid, the gears slowed down, the melody grew thin,
The stagehand wound it one last time and started up again.

Verse 3
They marked the winding key with chalk: one turn and then release,
The dancer found the final beat and stopped with perfect ease.
The audience applauded; the little box went still,
The stagehand put the key away and climbed the theater hill.

Chorus
Turn once, turn twice, turn softly round,
Let the little melody circle the sound.
When the last bright note has gone, let the music rest,
Wind it once, not seven times, and the ending sounds best.

Ending
The box played one last music phrase, then settled in its nest,
The dancer bowed; the stagehand sat and gave the gears a rest.
