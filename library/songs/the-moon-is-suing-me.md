# The Moon Is Suing Me

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: operatic tragedy and grand legal melodrama*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M1*

## Recitative
O moon above the courthouse, pale and round!  
You claim my telescope has marred your ground.  
I only pointed upward from my yard.  
You sent a writ, embossed and very hard.  
The bailiff arrived at supper's end.  
He asked if I had witnesses to send.  
My cat took the stand and coughed once.  
The judge declared the cough a legal stunt.  

## Aria
The moon is suing me, behold my grief.  
I face the stars and ask for brief relief.  
My counsel brings a ladder and a chart.  
The moon replies by pulling at my heart.  

## Scene two
The prosecutor wore a silver cape.  
He said my lens had altered moon's shape.  
I showed the court my little garden chair.  
I'd watched the sky from sitting there.  
The court reporter blinked at every crater.  
The bailiff served the constellations later.  
The judge asked who could pay the fee.  
I offered one old telescope key.  

## Aria
The moon is suing me, behold my grief.  
I face the stars and ask for brief relief.  
My counsel brings a ladder and a chart.  
The moon replies by pulling at my heart.  

## Trio
Then clouds rolled in and hid the plaintiff's face.  
The judge declared a recess in the case.  
The cat climbed up and sat upon my brief.  
My counsel sighed and asked for some relief.  
I heard a tapping on the courthouse glass.  
The moon sent down a note upon the grass.  
"I only want you to stop shining there."  
I promised to turn off the backyard glare.  

## Finale
The moon withdrew its claim before the dawn.  
The silver cape and bailiff both were gone.  
I moved my lamp and faced the east.  
The cat received a tuna feast.  
The judge adjourned with solemn hand.  
The stars returned to their nightly band.  
I shut the blinds, then raised them high.  
We watched the quiet, ordinary sky.
