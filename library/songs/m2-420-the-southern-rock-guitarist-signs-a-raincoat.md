# The Southern Rock Guitarist Signs a Raincoat

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: sprawling southern-rock solos and superstar autograph rituals that consume an entire soundcheck*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Twin guitars, steady drums and a rough lead vocal; short marker squeaks punctuate an excessively elaborate autograph.*

[Verse 1]
A fan came to the outdoor gig beneath the afternoon,  
She held her yellow raincoat and she asked for my name too.  
I took a pen and promised her a signature with style,  
I wrote an opening flourish that extended half a mile.  
The drummer called for soundcheck but my first initial grew,  
It crossed the coat's left shoulder and its sleeve came into view.  
The fan held out the fabric while I reached the second name,  
The wind took up the empty sleeve and made it wave again.  

[Chorus]
Sign it long, let it ride,  
The fan has got my signature along her raincoat side.  
Curve that line, make it shine,  
The name is nearly finished but the soundcheck's out of time.  
Sign it long, never stop,  
I have drawn an encore flourish where the first coat pocket drops.  

[Verse 2]
The pen began to fade before I'd reached the final stroke,  
The bassist lent a second one and called it quite a joke.  
I added in the date beneath a looping underlined sky,  
The drummer held his sticks aloft and asked if we could try.  
The fan put on the raincoat and the shoulder folded down,  
My name became a little knot that nobody could sound.  
She thanked me very warmly and she walked into the crowd,  
The band plugged in without me; their first chord rang quite loud.  

[Chorus]
Sign it long, let it ride,  
The fan has got my signature along her raincoat side.  
Curve that line, make it shine,  
The name is nearly finished but the soundcheck's out of time.  
Sign it long, never stop,  
I have drawn an encore flourish where the first coat pocket drops.  

[Bridge]
It rained halfway through evening and her coat kept dry and bright,  
The little crowd behind her used my loops to catch the light.  
She raised an arm and signaled when my longest solo came,  
My signature looked longer than the solo I was playing.  

[Final Chorus]
Sign it long, let it ride,  
The fan has brought the raincoat back and wants the drummer's side.  
Curve that line, make it shine,  
He puts his short initials in a space beneath mine.  
Sign it long, hear him say,  
"My solo's half a minute, and my autograph's the same."
