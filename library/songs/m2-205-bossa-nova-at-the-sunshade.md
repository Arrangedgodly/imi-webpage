# The Sunshade Bossa

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: bossa nova's relaxed syncopation, beachside lounge arrangements, and a shade umbrella that keeps stealing the singer's spotlight*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the guitar soft and syncopated; let the umbrella snap shut on the backbeat.*

[Verse 1]
At the quiet cafe, the guitar moved low,
A capuchin sang softly while the warm breezes blow.
The sunshade stood behind him, tilted toward the bay,
It leaned into the spotlight and stole the singer's day.

[Chorus]
Softly, shade, drift over the sand,
Fold with the rhythm, open by hand.
Softly, shade, let the blue sky show,
The singer is waiting for your shadow to go.

[Verse 2]
The singer shifted left; the shade slid to the right,
The guitarist kept the groove while they traded the light.
A waiter carried lemonade and ducked beneath the pole,
The shade followed his tray as if it had a goal.

[Chorus]
Softly, shade, drift over the sand,
Fold with the rhythm, open by hand.
Softly, shade, let the blue sky show,
The singer is waiting for your shadow to go.

[Bridge]
A gust snapped the umbrella; the crowd leaned back in time,
The metal ribs made percussion to the mellow little rhyme.
The singer held one note; the shade spun like a fan,
The waiter caught its handle and bowed like a bandleader man.

[Final Chorus]
Softly, shade, drift over the sand,
Fold with the rhythm, open by hand.
Softly, shade, let the blue sky show,
The singer has a shadow and the shade has the show.

[Verse 3]
They tied its handle to a post beside a wicker chair,
It bowed toward passing sailboats as if the shade were there.

[Ending]
They moved the table one inch; the umbrella moved again.
