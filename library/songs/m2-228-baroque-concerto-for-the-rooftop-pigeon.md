# The Pigeon Takes the Cadenza

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: Baroque concerto form, ornate counterpoint, and a soloist interrupted by a pigeon that improvises its own cadenza*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Keep the strings crisp and ornate; let the bird's coo enter as an unplanned solo break.*

[Verse 1]
The capuchin violinist took the rooftop stage,
He played a measured phrase from an old and gilded page.
The harpsichord replied; the strings began to climb,
A pigeon landed on the score and cooed between the time.

[Chorus]
Play the line, answer the line, keep the counterpoint,
One voice turns and another joins at every point.
Play the line, answer the line, let the high notes fly,
The pigeon takes the cadenza and the violins ask why.

[Verse 2]
The cellist tried to shoo it with a bow held very low,
The pigeon took the bow and made a little show.
The violinist held a trill; the bird replied in kind,
The rooftop audience clapped along and lost the downbeat line.

[Chorus]
Play the line, answer the line, keep the counterpoint,
One voice turns and another joins at every point.
Play the line, answer the line, let the high notes fly,
The pigeon takes the cadenza and the violins ask why.

[Bridge]
The conductor dropped her baton; it rolled beneath a chair,
The pigeon hopped to fetch it and returned it through the air.
The orchestra resumed the piece, its notes in careful rows,
The pigeon cooed a final phrase and bowed before it goes.

[Final Chorus]
Play the line, answer the line, keep the counterpoint,
One voice turns and another joins at every point.
Play the line, answer the line, let the high notes fly,
The pigeon takes the cadenza; the strings reply goodbye.

[Ending]
The program listed "soloist" and drew a feather mark.
