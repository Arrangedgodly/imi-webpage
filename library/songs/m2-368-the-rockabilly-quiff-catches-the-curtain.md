# The Rockabilly Quiff Catches the Curtain

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rockabilly songs celebrating perfect hair and stage swagger despite obvious practical trouble*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Slap upright bass, twanging guitar and hiccup vocals; let the curtain rings rattle over the drum fill.*

[Verse 1]
I greased my hair before the show and raised it like a sail,  
My comb could reach the ceiling and my mirror told the tale.  
I buttoned up my leopard coat and stepped into the light,  
The curtain tassel caught my quiff and held me rather tight.  
The band began the opening lick, I tried a little sway,  
The tassel moved along with me but wouldn't move away.  

[Chorus]
Rock it back and roll it right,  
Keep that big hair standing through the night.  
Shake your shoulders, swing your shoes,  
The curtain has a grip on all my rhythm and my blues.  
Baby, baby, look at me,  
I'm the finest stationary rebel you will ever see.  

[Verse 2]
I called the bass player closer for a stylish little turn,  
He saw the tassel in my hair and looked a bit concerned.  
The guitarist played a climbing lick to fill my climbing fear,  
The drummer rattled all his toms as I pulled the curtain near.  
The audience called for movement, so I leaned toward the crowd,  
The curtain came halfway across; they cheered the trick out loud.  

[Chorus]
Rock it back and roll it right,  
Keep that big hair standing through the night.  
Shake your shoulders, swing your shoes,  
The curtain has a grip on all my rhythm and my blues.  
Baby, baby, look at me,  
I'm the finest stationary rebel you will ever see.  

[Bridge]
The stagehand brought a comb and freed the tassel from the grease,  
My quiff fell flat against my brow, the band regained its peace.  
I sang the final verse while dancing wide across the floor,  
The audience demanded that I do the curtain trick once more.  

[Final Chorus]
Rock it back and roll it right,  
Keep that big hair standing through the night.  
Shake your shoulders, swing your shoes,  
The curtain has released my rhythm and my blues.  
Baby, baby, look at me,  
The stagehand combs the tassel up to headline after me.
