# The Polka Band Shares a Table with the Chess Club

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: boisterous polka club nights and two groups booking the same small room for incompatible rhythms*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Accordion, tuba and quick dance beat; chess pieces rattle whenever the polka dancers approach the shared table.*

[Verse 1]
The polka band booked Friday at the hall beside the lane,  
The chess club booked the little room and found the time the same.  
One table stood between us with the chessboard at its end,  
The accordion opened up before the players could pretend.  
The dancers tapped their quick steps and the pawns began to sway,  
The chess captain asked the band to take the feet another way.  
The tuba gave a low reply beneath the opening beat,  
The caretaker checked the calendar and looked for separate seats.  

[Refrain]
One, two, turn the dance,  
The knight has moved without the chess player's chance.  
One, two, let the bright tune ring,  
The table has a polka where it ought to hold a king.  
Dance floor, chessboard, give the little room its time,  
The caretaker has two bookings with the very same line.  

[Verse 2]
The caretaker found a quiet side room free that Friday night,  
The chess club carried in the board and set the pieces right.  
The band kept the large room with the dancers on the floor,  
The side-room door shut gently on the polka's distant roar.  
The chess captain finished his game and came to hear the sound,  
The dancers gave him room to try a slow turn round and round.  
The band played one short number at a gentler opening pace,  
The chess captain kept his footing with the board in another place.  

[Refrain]
One, two, turn the dance,  
The knight has moved without the chess player's chance.  
One, two, let the bright tune ring,  
The table has a polka where it ought to hold a king.  
Dance floor, chessboard, give the little room its time,  
The caretaker has two bookings with the very same line.  

[Bridge]
The calendar gained separate rooms for Friday after that,  
The chess club set its board upon a green felt playing mat.  
The polka leader asked the captain which move earned his win,  
He said it was the move to somewhere quiet from the din.  

[Final Refrain]
One, two, let the bright tune ring,  
The quiet room has got a chessboard with a steady little king.  
Dance floor, chessboard, give the little room its time,  
The calendar has two rooms on its Friday booking line.  
One, two, turn the dance,  
The captain names the new opening "The Polka Room Advance."
