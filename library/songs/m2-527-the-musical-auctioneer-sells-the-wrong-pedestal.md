# The Musical Auctioneer Sells the Wrong Pedestal

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical auction patter and showy sellers too busy singing to describe what is actually included in a lot*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Fast piano patter with call-and-response bids; the clerk reads the lot description slowly.*

[Patter]
A golden vase, a graceful shape,  
A masterpiece beyond escape!  
Who gives me ten, who gives me twenty?  
The hall has coins and coins aplenty!  
The clerk holds up a printed sheet,  
The seller taps a dancing beat.  
The vase is not the lot today,  
The stand beneath it goes away.  

[Chorus]
Bid it high, bid it low,  
Let the little numbers go.  
Golden vase above the stand,  
The stand is all that passes hand to hand.  
Read the lot, hear the tune,  
The vase belongs to the display room.  

[Verse]
A buyer raised a hand because the vase could hold her flowers,  
The singer praised its color through the hall's long waiting hours.  
The clerk stopped the little bidding and he read the printed line,  
"Wooden pedestal, vase excluded," was the current lot's design.  
The buyer lowered her hand and asked to start the bids again,  
A sculptor offered five for it; another offered ten.  
The singer sold the wooden stand with all its corners shown,  
The golden vase stayed in the hall where its owner kept its throne.  

[Chorus]
Bid it high, bid it low,  
Let the little numbers go.  
Golden vase above the stand,  
The stand is all that passes hand to hand.  
Read the lot, hear the tune,  
The vase belongs to the display room.  

[Clerk's Solo]
A chair is next, a simple chair,  
The velvet cushion isn't there.  
A desk is next, four legs of pine,  
The lamp above it is not mine.  
The auctioneer would like a rhyme,  
I read what sells each separate time.  
The bids go up, the lots go through,  
The buyers know what they can do.  

[Final Chorus]
Bid it high, bid it low,  
The singer has the printed lots before the numbers go.  
Read the lot, hear the tune,  
The golden vase remains upon a shelf inside the room.  
Bid it high, let the hands rise,  
The clerk gets an encore for identifying what each buyer buys.
