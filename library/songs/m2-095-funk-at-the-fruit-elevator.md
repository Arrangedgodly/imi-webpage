# Funk at the Fruit Elevator

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: funk bass grooves, call-and-response vocals, and an elevator operator who turns each floor stop into a bass break*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Deep syncopated bass, clipped guitar, and spoken callouts; each door chime answers the groove.*

Verse 1
The fruit elevator rumbled up from cellar to the sun,
A langur ran the buttons; the bass player rode for fun.
The doors opened on the first floor; a mango rolled inside,
The operator hit a downbeat and the fruit began to slide.

Chorus
Keep that fruit in the groove,
Let the elevator move.
Floor by floor, don't let it fall,
Funk can hold the basket all.

Verse 2
At floor three, a macaque stepped in with pears beneath her arm,
The bass went low and smooth, then gave the button panel charm.
The pears bounced to the rhythm; one rolled toward the door,
The operator caught it on the beat and asked the band for more.

Chorus
Keep that fruit in the groove,
Let the elevator move.
Floor by floor, don't let it fall,
Funk can hold the basket all.

Bridge
The elevator stopped between the floors; the bass held one long note,
The passengers leaned left and right to keep the pears afloat.
The langur called, "Stay steady!" The chorus answered, "We!"
The lift resumed its journey with a basket harmony.

Verse 3
They reached the rooftop market where the fruit was weighed and sold,
The mango took the center spot; the pears were lined in gold.
The band played one more break as every shopper passed,
The doors closed on the downbeat and the elevator got the last.

Chorus
Keep that fruit in the groove,
Let the elevator move.
Floor by floor, don't let it fall,
Funk can hold the basket all.

Ending
The lift went down in silence; the bass stayed in the hall,
One pear rolled from the basket and funked against the wall.
