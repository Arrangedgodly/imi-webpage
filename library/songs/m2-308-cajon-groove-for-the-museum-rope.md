# Cajon Groove for the Museum Rope

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: flamenco cajon showcases and museum rules that turn a simple rhythm into a prohibited performance*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-008*

*Performance note: Box-drum pulse starts as a quiet demonstration, then grows as visitors mistake each tap for an invitation to dance.*

**Verse 1**
The museum's quiet gallery has one velvet rope.  
A capuchin brings a cajon and says, "I'll play one note."  
The guard says, "Please do not perform beside the art."  
The drummer taps the box once; a dancer takes a start.  
The tapping echoes through the hall; the visitors turn round.  
A flamenco heel replies, and soon there is a sound.  

**Chorus**
Tap on the box, stamp on the floor;  
The guard says, "Please don't do that anymore."  
Tap on the side, clap on the two;  
The velvet rope is dancing with the whole review.  

**Verse 2**
The group moves to the sculpture room, still following the beat.  
The guard lifts up the rope; it swings around his feet.  
He taps the post to call the crew; the cajon answers back.  
The drummer hears a rhythm and adds a heel-toe track.  
The museum guide recites the dates of every carved relief.  
The dancers count in seven; the guard requests a brief.  

**Chorus**
Tap on the box, stamp on the floor;  
The guard says, "Please don't do that anymore."  
Tap on the side, clap on the two;  
The velvet rope is dancing with the whole review.  

**Bridge**
The drummer sets the box down; the guard moves it away.  
The dancers stop on cue; the gallery returns to gray.  
A visitor asks the guard to show the proper beat.  
He taps the rope post once and points them to the street.  

**Ending**
Outside, the band plays on; the sculptures rest in peace.  
The guard shuts both the doors; the footfalls gently cease.  
The cajon waits in its case; the rope goes back in line.  
The guard taps one last time and calls the sound divine.
