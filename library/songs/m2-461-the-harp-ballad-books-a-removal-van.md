# The Harp Ballad Books a Removal Van

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ethereal harp ballads whose weightless atmosphere conceals the very heavy business of transporting the instrument*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Flowing harp arpeggios and a soft high voice; the removal crew answers romantic imagery with measurements and trolley wheels.*

[Verse 1]
I sang of floating clouds beside a harp of polished gold,  
The audience heard a weightless world the strings could gently hold.  
The concert finished softly and the house lights came alive,  
The removal crew stood waiting with a trolley by my side.  
I said my music traveled on a quiet evening breeze,  
They asked about the loading ramp and how to find the keys.  
I stroked a last small chord before we wrapped the shining frame,  
The weightless world needed two people just to lift its name.  

[Chorus]
Float, little harp, drift across the sky,  
The trolley has a strap to keep the golden frame upright.  
Fly, little song, leave the ground behind,  
The van has got a tail lift and a very heavy kind of mind.  
Float, little harp, carry what I dream,  
The crew would like a clear route through the furniture in between.  

[Verse 2]
The lift was much too narrow for the frame to fit inside,  
We took the service doorway with the trolley at its side.  
The crew measured the corridor and moved a coat stand clear,  
I sang a soft encouragement that nobody could hear.  
The trolley wheels were louder than my breathy little voice,  
The harp reached the waiting van by an ordinary choice.  
They strapped it to the padded rail and shut the heavy door,  
The floating clouds were packed away beneath a blanket once more.  

[Chorus]
Float, little harp, drift across the sky,  
The trolley has a strap to keep the golden frame upright.  
Fly, little song, leave the ground behind,  
The van has got a tail lift and a very heavy kind of mind.  
Float, little harp, carry what I dream,  
The crew would like a clear route through the furniture in between.  

[Bridge]
At home they rolled the harp back to its place beside the pane,  
The golden strings reflected stars and sounded light again.  
The crew left me their invoice and a photograph of the van,  
I wrote a ballad thanking them for making clouds land.  

[Final Chorus]
Float, little harp, drift across the sky,  
The next hall has a loading ramp the crew can use quite wide.  
Fly, little song, leave the ground behind,  
The weightless world has wheels and a pickup time of nine.  
Float, little harp, carry what I dream,  
The van gets a performance credit after every theme.
