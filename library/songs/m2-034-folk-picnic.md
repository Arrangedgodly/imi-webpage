# Maple Shade Mixtape

*Format: Song parody*  
*Parody target: picnic folk-pop conventions and handmade community compilations*  
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*  
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS*

*Performance note: Acoustic guitar, hand claps from the picnic table, and a cassette changing hands.*

## Verse 1
The community picnic spread beneath the maple trees.  
A young monkey brought a tape with handwritten tracklists.  
The songs were made by neighbors from the block.  
One had a banjo; one had a kitchen clock.  
A folk singer tuned beside the cooler's hum.  
Her father passed the lemonade around to everyone.  
The cassette caught one gull cry in the breeze.  
She left it in; it sounded like the keys.  

## Chorus
Pass the tape, pass the plate, let the small songs play.  
A little bit of home can travel far away.  
Pass the tape, pass the tune, let the old tree hear.  
The best-made chorus brings the neighbors near.  

## Verse 2
A child recorded claps against a plastic cup.  
The bass player made a loop and picked it up.  
Two aunties sang a verse about their garden row.  
A granddad added harmonica soft and low.  
They wrote the names in marker on the case.  
The tape got passed around from place to place.  
By sunset, every guest had left a sound.  
The singer played the mix while packing blankets down.  

## Bridge
No label asked for masters or a polished mix.  
The song had someone's laugh between the clicks.  
They made ten copies for the block to keep.  
The tree stood over them, patient, broad, and deep.  

## Chorus
Pass the tape, pass the plate, let the small songs play.  
A little bit of home can travel far away.  
Pass the tape, pass the tune, let the old tree hear.  
The best-made chorus brings the neighbors near.  

## Ending
Years on, the tape still turned with one worn side.  
The picnic songs stayed close, wherever folks might ride.
