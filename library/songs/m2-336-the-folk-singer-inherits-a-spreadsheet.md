# The Folk Singer Inherits a Spreadsheet

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: rustic folk ballads about family inheritance recast as a complicated shared spreadsheet*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Fingerpicked acoustic guitar; the family chorus counts columns while the singer draws out cell names.*

[Verse 1]
My granddad left no silver and no cabin on the hill,  
He left a shared spreadsheet with a most peculiar will.  
The orchard lived in column A, the chickens lived in B,  
He wrote the gate's combination in a cell I couldn't see.  
My cousins gathered round the screen with cider in their hands,  
We sang about the old man while dividing up his lands.  

[Chorus]
Oh, carry me to column C,  
Where all my family wishes to be.  
The apples ripen in a row,  
The formula tells us where they go.  
The roots run deep, the tabs run wide,  
And cousin Nell has locked the whole west side.  

[Verse 2]
My aunt inserted one new row to mark the orchard fence,  
The chickens jumped to column C and nobody made sense.  
My brother sorted names by age, the farmhouse floated north,  
The youngest cousin clicked undo and brought the farmhouse forth.  
We tried to read the harvest plan, the numbers all were red,  
Somebody typed a sunset where the yearly rent was read.  

[Chorus]
Oh, carry me to column C,  
Where all my family wishes to be.  
The apples ripen in a row,  
The formula tells us where they go.  
The roots run deep, the tabs run wide,  
And cousin Nell has locked the whole west side.  

[Bridge]
We found a faded photograph beneath the laptop tray,  
It showed us where the old gate stood and where to stack the hay.  
We walked out to the orchard while the little screen went dim,  
And Nell unlocked the western tab to add a note for him.  

[Final Chorus]
Oh, carry me to column C,  
Where all my family wishes to be.  
The apples ripen in a row,  
The formula tells us where they go.  
The roots run deep, the tabs run wide,  
Granddad's final cell says PLEASE GO LOOK OUTSIDE.
