# The Prog-Rock Suite Reaches the Merch Table

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: progressive-rock concept suites with mythic narration, multiple movements and an ending so long that the practical gig is over before the quest concludes*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-011*
*Performance note: Move from spacious keyboards through odd-meter guitar, a gentle acoustic passage and a huge final chorus. The venue cleaner supplies the final spoken beat.*

[Movement I: The Gate]
Beyond the violet mountains stands a gate of silver flame,  
Its keeper knows the secret that the stars will never name.  
Our drummer raises thunder from a cavern made of sound,  
The guitarist counts eleven as the keeper turns around.  
We sing of seven travelers who seek the hidden sea,  
The venue clock says ten o'clock; we have finally found the key.  

[Movement II: The Hidden Sea]
The sea is made of memory, the ship is made of light,  
The traveler cannot cross it till he understands the night.  
The bassist plays a long descent beneath the answering moon,  
The keyboard changes costumes and begins a warmer tune.  
The venue staff stack empty cups along the waiting bar,  
The hidden sea still waits for us beneath the evening stars.  

[Refrain]
Carry us beyond the gate,  
The silver sea is calling and the hidden dawn awaits.  
Carry us beyond the shore,  
The journey has a meaning that requires one movement more.  

[Movement III: The Keeper's Question]
The keeper asks the traveler why he wishes to be free,  
The traveler gives an answer in a changing count of three.  
The answer takes eight minutes and includes a flute reprise,  
The cleaner wheels his bucket past the keeper's painted eyes.  
He waits beside the fire door with a mop against his knee,  
The traveler is explaining why the sea can never be.  

[Movement IV: The Memory of Home]
We lay the heavy guitars down and sing an acoustic line,  
The traveler recalls a little house beneath the pine.  
His mother had a simple wish: return before the dawn,  
The venue staff request the same; the audience has gone.  
We see three faithful listeners beside the merchandise,  
The traveler begins to learn the cost of sacrifice.  

[Refrain]
Carry us beyond the gate,  
The silver sea is calling and the hidden dawn awaits.  
Carry us beyond the shore,  
The journey has a meaning that requires one movement more.  

[Movement V: The Return]
The keeper lets the traveler leave the gate of silver flame,  
He walks back through the mountains with a simpler kind of name.  
We strike the final mighty chord; the last three fans applaud,  
The cleaner puts the house lights up and points toward the cord.  
We pack the ship of memory in a long equipment case,  
The traveler finds his wallet and must pay for overtime space.  

[Final Refrain]
Carry us beyond the gate,  
The silver sea is packed away; the cleaning crew awaits.  
Carry us beyond the shore,  
The merchandise has three small sales; the venue bill has more.  
Carry us beyond the light,  
The traveler gets home at last, the band gets paid next Friday night.  

[Spoken Coda]
The cleaner says the quest was grand,  
Then asks who left a flute beneath the merchandise stand.  
The flautist takes it from the floor; the cleaner locks the door.
