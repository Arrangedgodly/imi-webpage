# Ragtime at the Birdbath

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: ragtime syncopation, jaunty piano rolls, and the escalating precision of a public fountain routine*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-004*

*Performance note: Bright stride piano with crisp syncopation; water splashes answer the treble runs.*

Verse 1
A capuchin pianist set up by the garden birdbath rim,
He played a jaunty ragtime tune; the ducks began to swim.
A lemur tapped a measuring cup to catch the splash in time,
The mayor called it civic art and asked for a measured rhyme.

Chorus
Tick the cup and splash the stone,
Play the water's stepping tone.
Skip one beat and dip one toe,
Ragtime makes the fountain flow.

Verse 2
The pianist shifted left-hand bass; the jets leapt twice as high,
The ducks kept perfect syncopation, quacking at the sky.
The mayor set out numbered cups and charged admission at the gate,
The crowd bought paper programs titled "Splash in E-flat."

Chorus
Tick the cup and splash the stone,
Play the water's stepping tone.
Skip one beat and dip one toe,
Ragtime makes the fountain flow.

Bridge
A groundskeeper came with a wrench and asked to shut it down,
The mayor waved him off; the audience would boo the town.
The ducks marched in a chorus line; the wrench began to squeak,
The pianist sampled that new sound and sold it as "Duck Chic."

Verse 3
The groundskeeper repaired the jet during the final strain,
The fountain gave a single burst and soaked the mayor again.
The ducks took all the ticket coins and waddled through the gate,
The pianist played the closing bars to an empty garden plate.

Chorus
Tick the cup and splash the stone,
Play the water's stepping tone.
Skip one beat and dip one toe,
Ragtime makes the fountain flow.

Ending
The mayor banned the cups at dawn; the ducks bought them instead,
They opened up a bathhouse band and charged the mayor to tread.
