# The Luxury Rapper Buys a Snail Chain

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: luxury rap boasts and music videos that give a tiny pet the same extravagant status symbols as the performer*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-012*
*Performance note: Heavy bass, crisp percussion and grand rhythmic boasts; the snail gets a very slow sung response to the rapper's impatient orders.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a golden pendant for the snail upon my desk,  
The video director said a pet would suit the set.  
The pendant was much bigger than the shell that it would frame,  
I put it down beside the snail and called it a famous name.  
I said my little partner had the finest gold in town,  
The snail began to cross the pendant, making its own round.  
The director kept the camera close; the lights were shining bright,  
The snail took half an hour to leave the chain's first link behind.  

[Chorus]
Big gold, slow roll, show the tiny star,  
The chain has twenty links and he has not got very far.  
Bright lights, fresh shine, let the cameras see,  
My partner has a pace that cannot hurry up for me.  
Big gold, slow roll, keep the legend near,  
The rapper wants the shot today; the snail has all the year.  

[Verse 2]
We laid a lettuce leaf beside the far end of the chain,  
The snail turned very slowly toward the food instead of fame.  
The camera filmed its journey on a quiet close-up view,  
I finished three whole verses while it crossed another two.  
The director used a time-lapse and the snail appeared quite quick,  
Its little trail shone silver where it crossed the golden links.  
I said the trail was custom polish from a special private brand,  
The director handed me a cloth and pointed to my hand.  

[Chorus]
Big gold, slow roll, show the tiny star,  
The chain has twenty links and he has not got very far.  
Bright lights, fresh shine, let the cameras see,  
My partner has a pace that cannot hurry up for me.  
Big gold, slow roll, keep the legend near,  
The rapper wants the shot today; the snail has all the year.  

[Bridge]
I cleaned the chain and gave the snail its lettuce at the desk,  
The video went out that night; the snail had gone to rest.  
The fans asked for more footage of the slow and shining guest,  
I tried to book a second shoot; it stayed beneath a leaf.  

[Final Chorus]
Big gold, slow roll, show the tiny star,  
The chain is back around my neck; the snail stays where it was.  
Bright lights, fresh shine, let the cameras see,  
The snail has got a fan base that is larger than me.  
Big gold, slow roll, keep the legend near,  
Its next appearance is tomorrow, or a different day next year.
