# The Tango Singer Keeps the Receipt

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: passionate tango songs and singers preparing for eternal heartbreak while organizing ordinary purchase returns*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-014*
*Performance note: Sharp piano and accordion with a dramatic lead; receipt folds click on the accented beats.*

[Verse 1]
I bought a red silk scarf to wear the night you went away,  
I sang about the color of the things that couldn't stay.  
The scarf was much too long for me; it caught beneath my heel,  
My turning step became a trip with little grace to feel.  
The shop had given me a slip with every price quite clear,  
I kept it in my pocket with the proof of price quite near.  
You called the very next morning; your journey was delayed,  
I held the red scarf in my hands and thought of what I'd paid.  

[Refrain]
Red silk, lost love, turn the night around,  
The scarf has got a meter more than dancing feet have found.  
Red silk, lost love, let the sharp chords meet,  
The shop will take it back if I have kept the little receipt.  
One turn, two tears, let the longing stay,  
The return must happen thirty days from the purchase day.  

[Verse 2]
I took the scarf back promptly while the label still was sewn,  
The shopkeeper read the receipt and found the sale she'd known.  
I chose a shorter scarf to keep the color by my neck,  
The turning step could happen with no fabric left to wreck.  
You came to see the tango after canceling your trip,  
I danced the verse of vanished love with my receipt upon my hip.  
You asked why every chorus said that you had gone away,  
I said the song was written before the change in your next day.  

[Refrain]
Red silk, lost love, turn the night around,  
The scarf has got a meter more than dancing feet have found.  
Red silk, lost love, let the sharp chords meet,  
The shop will take it back if I have kept the little receipt.  
One turn, two tears, let the longing stay,  
The return must happen thirty days from the purchase day.  

[Bridge]
We danced one quiet tango with the short scarf in its place,  
The shopkeeper came to see it and she recognized my face.  
She asked if I would sing a jingle for her winter sale,  
I said the scarves could have a much less sorrowful tale.  

[Final Refrain]
Red silk, warm room, let the sharp chords meet,  
The short scarf has a dance and a very small receipt.  
One turn, two smiles, let the color stay,  
Your journey has been canceled but the tango still can play.  
Red silk, lost love, turn the night around,  
The shopkeeper sells three short scarves before the final sound.
