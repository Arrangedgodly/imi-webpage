# The Reggae Band Grows a Mustache

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: easygoing reggae anthems about patience, paired with a publicity deadline that requires immediate hair growth*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Relaxed offbeat guitar and rounded bass; the photographer's impatient countdown cuts against the leisurely refrain.*

[Verse 1]
The photographer arrived at ten to shoot our record sleeve,  
He said a fine mustache would give the singer more prestige.  
Our singer had a smooth lip and a patient little smile,  
He said he'd grow one gladly if we'd wait a little while.  
The drummer took a comfortable chair beneath the studio tree,  
The bassist put the kettle on; we let the morning be.  
The photographer checked the clock and asked about the plan,  
The singer said, "I'm growing it as quickly as I can."  

[Chorus]
Grow, grow, take your time,  
The little hairs have not agreed to meet the sleeve deadline.  
Slow, slow, easy today,  
The bass can hurry forward but the mustache has its way.  
Grow, grow, see the sun,  
The photograph is waiting for the work that isn't done.  
Soft breeze, warm tea, quiet little grin,  
We have booked a morning shoot and left biology to win.  

[Verse 2]
At noon he brought a costume box with whiskers in a row,  
The singer chose a curly pair and asked how they would grow.  
The drummer found some stage adhesive underneath the chair,  
The bassist held a mirror while we fitted artificial hair.  
The photographer arranged us with the singer in the front,  
The whiskers drooped on one side when he sang the first warm note.  
We stopped to fix the curling ends; the camera flashed too soon,  
The mustache pointed sideways like a very tiny moon.  

[Chorus]
Grow, grow, take your time,  
The little hairs have not agreed to meet the sleeve deadline.  
Slow, slow, easy today,  
The bass can hurry forward but the mustache has its way.  
Grow, grow, see the sun,  
The photograph is waiting for the work that isn't done.  
Soft breeze, warm tea, quiet little grin,  
We have booked a morning shoot and left biology to win.  

[Bridge]
We kept the crooked photograph; the record sold quite well,  
The fans wore sideways whiskers when they came to hear us play.  
Six weeks later, on the tour, the singer grew his own,  
The photographer said, "Excellent. Now beards are what we want."  

[Final Chorus]
Grow, grow, take your time,  
The photograph has changed its mind about the sleeve design.  
Slow, slow, easy today,  
The singer keeps his mustache and the fans enjoy the sway.  
Soft breeze, warm tea, quiet little grin,  
The borrowed whiskers tour the shops while real ones settle in.  
Grow, grow, let it be,  
The photographer wants a beard before we finish tea.
