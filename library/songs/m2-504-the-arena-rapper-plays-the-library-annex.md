# The Arena Rapper Plays the Library Annex

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: arena-scale rap introductions and artists announcing enormous crowds in rooms designed for small literary events*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-013*
*Performance note: Forceful beat at a modest volume, large lead voice and dry replies from a library host.*

[Intro]
How many people came to see me?  
HOST: Twelve, including staff.  
Where are all my thousands?  
HOST: The booking holds this half.  
Make some noise for greatness!  
HOST: The reading room's next door.  
The rapper lowers his microphone  
And asks what the annex is for.  

[Verse 1]
I wrote a giant opening to shake the whole arena,  
The library had booked a talk on poetry, much cleaner.  
Twelve little chairs faced a desk beneath a map of the town,  
I stepped up to the microphone and turned the volume down.  
I asked if they could raise their hands above the colored sign,  
Eleven people did so; one was writing down a line.  
The host asked for the story of the rhyme I chose to use,  
I told them how the verbs could put a rhythm in the news.  

[Hook]
Hands up, let the little room rise,  
There are twelve different questions underneath twelve pairs of eyes.  
Hands up, let the gentle beat roll,  
The host wants the meaning and the meter of my soul.  
Big words, small room, give the rhyme its place,  
The map behind the desk has got more people than this space.  

[Verse 2]
A reader asked why every chorus spoke about a crown,  
I said it fit the bass line and the words that came around.  
She asked if being ruler meant I ever paid the rent,  
I wrote a verse about the bills and where my wages went.  
The twelve gave me a steady clap to test the little rhyme,  
The reader with the notebook raised her hand the second time.  
She wanted one last copy of the verse about the bills,  
The library printed twelve and asked who had the printing skills.

[Outro]
I left with twelve new listeners who knew my songs by name,  
The notebook reader asked if she could rap her rent the same.  
I booked another little room and wrote a smaller shout,  
The host wrote down my booking; next week's flyers all went out.
