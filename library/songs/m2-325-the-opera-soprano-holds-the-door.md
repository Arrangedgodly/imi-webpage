# The Opera Soprano Holds the Door

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: operatic farewell scenes prolonged by every character's refusal to exit*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-009*
*Performance note: Grand soprano aria, tremulous strings and a bass singer interrupting with delivery instructions.*

[Verse 1]
Farewell, my love, the carriage waits below the painted moon,  
The horses know the overture and wish to leave quite soon.  
I stand upon the staircase in a gown of scarlet lace,  
You reach the open doorway with a teardrop on your face.  
I sing about the distance that will tear our lives apart,  
You stop beneath the lintel with your luggage and your heart.  
The porter tries to pass you with a parcel for the hall,  
You sing another farewell and lean heavily on the wall.  

[Chorus]
Go, my love, depart, depart,  
Take the west road and my heart.  
Go, my love, the world is wide,  
Move your suitcase to the side.  
Farewell echoes through the floor,  
Farewell cannot get through the door.  

[Verse 2]
The chorus gathers on the steps to witness our despair,  
The porter holds his parcel like a sacrifice of air.  
You place one foot outside and sing how far away you'll roam,  
I call you back for just one note before you leave our home.  
The driver ties his horses up and comes to join the bass,  
The porter starts a counterpoint requesting room to pass.  
Your farewell lasts a quarter hour, my answer lasts for two,  
The driver rents the carriage to a stranger passing through.  

[Chorus]
Go, my love, depart, depart,  
Take the west road and my heart.  
Go, my love, the world is wide,  
Move your suitcase to the side.  
Farewell echoes through the floor,  
Farewell cannot get through the door.  

[Bridge]
The porter wheels your suitcase out; you follow, quite surprised,  
The chorus parts like curtains and my final note subsides.  
You turn to sing a last goodbye, I lift my hand once more,  
The porter puts the parcel down and quietly shuts the door.  

[Final Chorus]
Go, my love, depart, depart,  
Take the west road and my heart.  
Go, my love, the world is wide,  
There's no carriage parked outside.  
Farewell echoes through the floor,  
You ring the bell; we open for an encore.
