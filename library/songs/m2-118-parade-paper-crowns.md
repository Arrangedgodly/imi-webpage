# Parade of the Paper Crowns

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: theme-park parade songs, mascot entrances, and a paper crown that keeps blowing from one performer to another*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-003*

*Performance note: Bright parade brass, snare rolls, and a simple walking beat; the paper crown should transfer on each gust.*

Verse 1
The park parade began at noon with banners in a row,
A capuchin wore a paper crown and walked the route below.
The brass band played a welcome march; a sudden breeze came through,
The crown lifted off his head and found a drummer's shoe.

Chorus
Wave the flags and walk the line,
Paper crowns will travel fine.
Step by step and drum by drum,
Every branch can join the fun.

Verse 2
The drummer shook the crown loose; it flew to a float,
A lemur in a painted sun caught it by her coat.
The float turned past the ticket gate; the crown flew once again,
It landed on the parade horse, who wore it like a hen.

Chorus
Wave the flags and walk the line,
Paper crowns will travel fine.
Step by step and drum by drum,
Every branch can join the fun.

Bridge
The band marched through the fountain mist and turned toward the stage,
The crown sailed to the baton hand and perched above the page.
The conductor kept the rhythm; the crowd began to cheer,
The crown flew into the audience and landed on an ear.

Verse 3
They finished at the garden gate with every float in place,
The paper crown was passed along and rested on a vase.
The capuchin removed it gently, then set it on a stand,
The breeze gave one last flutter as the band played through the land.

Chorus
Wave the flags and walk the line,
Paper crowns will travel fine.
Step by step and drum by drum,
Every branch can join the fun.

Ending
The parade went home in time; the crown stayed in the park,
It waited for tomorrow's route beneath the sign at dark.
