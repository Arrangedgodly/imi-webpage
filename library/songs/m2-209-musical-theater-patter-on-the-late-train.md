# The Conductor's Quickest Verse

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: musical-theater patter, rapid-fire wordplay, and a train conductor who announces every small delay as a grand number*
*By: IMI Records Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-006*
*Performance note: Deliver the verses at increasing speed; hold the last word whenever the train brakes.*

[Verse 1]
A macaque conductor came aboard with a bell and a list,
He sang every station with a flourish of his wrist.
The train was running late by a minute and a half,
He added three encores and demanded one more laugh.

[Chorus]
All aboard, all aboard, the platform's moving fast,
The conductor has a verse for every train that passed.
All aboard, all aboard, let the rolling rhythm run,
We will reach the station after everyone is done.

[Verse 2]
He named the seats by number and the windows by their view,
He sang the lunch cart's menu and the route the trolley knew.
A macaque asked for silence; he composed a quiet theme,
It used six dozen syllables to whisper "let me dream."

[Chorus]
All aboard, all aboard, the platform's moving fast,
The conductor has a verse for every train that passed.
All aboard, all aboard, let the rolling rhythm run,
We will reach the station after everyone is done.

[Bridge]
The wheels began to slow; he sang a brake report,
The porter chimed in softly from the luggage port.
At last the train stopped; he paused to draw a breath,
The station sign said "Welcome"; he sang its name to death.

[Final Chorus]
All aboard, all aboard, the platform's moving fast,
The conductor has a verse for every train that passed.
All aboard, all aboard, the final stop is here,
We can leave the train now; the conductor's still in gear.

[Ending]
He sang a farewell; the train had left without him.
