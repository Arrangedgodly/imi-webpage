# The Beatboxer Books a Dentist

*Format: Song parody*
*Parody target: competitive beatboxing bravado defeated by a routine dental appointment*
*By: IMI Records Executive Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SONGS-010*
*Performance note: Vocal percussion under a rapped lead; the suction tube becomes the backing bass in verse two.*

[Verse 1]
I booked a little checkup and arrived with perfect time,  
The waiting room was silent, so I gave the chairs a rhyme.  
My lips produced a kick drum and my teeth supplied the hats,  
The receptionist tapped slowly while she filed the little stats.  
The dentist called my number and I finished with a snare,  
She asked me for my coat and pointed calmly to the chair.  

[Chorus]
Boots and cats, boots and cats,  
Keep that rhythm under all the hats.  
Open wide and take a breath,  
Please stop making drum sounds while she checks your teeth.  
Boots and cats, bass and snare,  
The world's best little drum kit needs a filling and repair.  

[Verse 2]
I lay beneath the little lamp and launched a mighty bass,  
The mirror in my open mouth reflected in her glass.  
She said to hold my tongue still while she checked the upper right,  
I gave a quick percussion fill and bit the mirror tight.  
She moved the suction tube beside the corner of my grin,  
It made a low and lovely note; I wanted it again.  

[Chorus]
Boots and cats, boots and cats,  
Keep that rhythm under all the hats.  
Open wide and take a breath,  
Please stop making drum sounds while she checks your teeth.  
Boots and cats, bass and snare,  
The world's best little drum kit needs a filling and repair.  

[Bridge]
She paused the suction gently and she asked me to be still,  
I counted silent measures while she finished up the fill.  
She held a little hand mirror up, showing polished white,  
I nodded thanks and saved my solo for the street outside.  

[Final Chorus]
Boots and cats, boots and cats,  
Keep that rhythm under all the hats.  
Open wide and take a breath,  
The drum kit has a clean new corner underneath.  
Boots and cats, bass and snare,  
I book the suction tube to guest-star at the county fair.
