# The Escape Room Hint Has a Hint

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: escape rooms that ration clues through an elaborate hint system while the clock runs out*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Game Master Yari, a macaque; players Nel, a gibbon, and Suri, a capuchin; and Owner Bex, a gorilla.

INT. ESCAPE ROOM - DAY

A wooden chest sits beneath a wall clock. The players study a mural of five birds. Yari watches through a small window.

YARI: You have forty minutes. Ask for a hint if you need one.

NEL: The mural has five birds and a number lock with four wheels.

SURI: The plaque says, "Count what flies west at dusk."

NEL: Four birds face west.

[THEY TURN THE LOCK TO 0-4-0-0. IT DOES NOT OPEN.]

SURI: We have four west-facing birds.

YARI: Would you like a hint?

NEL: Yes.

[YARI SLIDES A CARD UNDER THE DOOR. IT READS: "LOOK AT THE MURAL."]

SURI: We are looking at it.

YARI: Would you like a hint about how to use the hint?

NEL: No.

INT. ESCAPE ROOM - LATER

The clock has lost twelve minutes. Bex enters to replace a flickering lamp.

BEX: You have a free hint available.

SURI: We have used two.

BEX: Those were guidance cards. The free hint is spoken.

NEL: Tell us what the lock wants.

YARI: I can tell you the category.

SURI: The category is numbers.

YARI: Correct.

BEX: The key is under the clock.

[YARI GIVES BEX A LOOK.]

BEX: That was not a hint. It was maintenance.

[NEL LIFTS THE CLOCK. A BRASS KEY IS TAPED UNDER IT.]

NEL: The key opens the chest?

YARI: It opens the chest.

SURI: Could you have said that twelve minutes ago?

YARI: That would have been a spoiler.

BEX: It would have been a clue.

INT. ESCAPE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The chest opens. Inside is a card with a large arrow pointing to the exit.

NEL: The arrow points to the door we came through.

YARI: You solved the room.

SURI: We found a key taped under a clock.

YARI: The room's theme is navigation.

BEX: The room's exit has a handle.

[THE PLAYERS OPEN THE EXIT DOOR. YARI HANDS THEM A SATISFACTION FORM.]

SURI: We still have six minutes.

YARI: The clock includes the survey period.

NEL: Can you give us a hint for the first question?

YARI: The answer is on the form.

[THE FORM HAS NO WRITING. YARI TURNS IT OVER. THE BACK SAYS "WRITE HERE." NEL TAKES A PEN.]

NEL: We escaped the room and now we are trapped by paperwork.

BEX: You can leave the paper blank.

YARI: That counts as a response.

SURI: Is there a clue?

YARI: If you look at the bottom, the form says it is optional.

[SURI TURNS THE FORM OVER. THE WORD "OPTIONAL" IS IN SMALL PRINT UNDER YARI'S THUMB.]

SURI: We have found the exit again.

YARI: Please rate the hint system.

NEL: Is there a hint for that?

[BEX HOLDS UP THE FORM. THE ONLY BOXES ARE "MYSTERIOUS" AND "VERY MYSTERIOUS." SURI CHECKS BOTH.]
