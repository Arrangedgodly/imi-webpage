# The Western Duel Waits for the Prop Receipt

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: western showdowns whose dramatic weapons have to be returned to the same theatre cupboard*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: GUNSLINGER KORA, a baboon; RIVAL PIM, a macaque; PROP KEEPER NEL, an orangutan; STAGE MANAGER RUD, a gibbon.

[A painted western street. Kora and Pim stand ten paces apart wearing empty holsters. Nel has a tray holding two bright blue toy water pistols. Rud holds the rehearsal script.]

KORA: This town is too small for both of us.

PIM: It is painted on plywood. We can stand closer.

KORA: Draw.

[Both reach into their empty holsters and produce nothing. Nel clears her throat from beside the scenery.]

NEL: You need to sign the toy checkout sheet.

ACT ONE

[Kora walks to Nel with his eyes still fixed on Pim. He extends a hand above the tray. Nel puts a pen in it.]

KORA: You have chosen your weapon.

NEL: You have chosen the wrong hand gesture for a form.

[Pim signs first. Nel gives him the toy labeled B. Kora reads the checkout line: RETURN EMPTY.]

KORA: I never leave a confrontation empty-handed.

NEL: It means no water. These are dry props for the scene.

RUD: We are recording the duel as a wordless stare. No one fires anything.

PIM: Then perhaps our eyes could sign the form.

[Nel waits. Kora finally signs and receives toy A. He tries to put it in his holster; the bright handle sticks out at an absurd angle.]

KORA: The town has changed since my last visit.

NEL: These are the toys listed on the script. They fit the loose belt loops, not those holsters.

[Rud asks both actors to use the toys at their sides, visible as toys, while the audience understands the western convention. Kora and Pim return to their starting marks.]

RUD: Begin the stare. Hold it until I call the end.

[They stare. Kora's hat slowly slips over one eyebrow. Pim resists laughing. Nel looks at the clock without moving the tray.]

KORA: The dust is in my eyes.

PIM: There is no dust effect today.

KORA: The imagined dust is very fine.

[Rud lets the stare last two more seconds, then calls the end. Kora catches his hat before it falls. Pim exhales loudly.]

ACT TWO

[Nel holds out the tray. Pim returns toy B immediately. Kora turns it over, searching for a dramatic way to surrender it.]

KORA: In this town, a rival keeps his iron.

NEL: In this theatre, toy A goes in this outline.

[She points to the outline drawn on the tray. Kora lowers the toy into it. Nel checks both labels against the sheet and marks them returned.]

PIM: Does that settle the duel?

RUD: The stare is rehearsed. We can add the music at the next run.

KORA: Then my dignity survives.

[Nel gives Kora his return slip. Pim has already tucked his own into his pocket. Kora raises the paper as though it were a wanted poster.]

KORA: A town remembers its debts.

NEL: That says you owe nothing.

[Kora folds it and puts it in the empty holster, where it fits better than the toy. Rud points both actors toward the costume room.]

PIM: Same time tomorrow?

KORA: High noon.

RUD: Ten fifteen. The dance class needs the room at noon.

FINAL IMAGE: Kora leaves the painted street with a prop-return receipt protruding from his holster.
