# The Laundromat Sings Its Spin Cycle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grand opera, musical criticism, and ordinary appliances promoted as virtuoso performers*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: OPERA CRITIC, a gibbon; ATTENDANT, a macaque; SINGER, an orangutan; CUSTOMER, a capuchin; REPAIRER, a baboon.

[A laundromat. A washer begins a low spin. The singer steps forward with a scarf as a cape.] 

SINGER: The machine has entered its second act.

ATTENDANT: It is on the rinse cycle.

CRITIC: I hear a bold mechanical tenor.

[The washer rattles. The customer moves a basket away from it.]

CUSTOMER: That sound is new.

SINGER: A daring modulation.

REPAIRER: It is a loose panel. Please stop the cycle.

[The attendant presses stop. The drum slows to a halt. The repairer checks the panel screws, tightening one by hand after unplugging the machine.] 

CRITIC: The music has ended abruptly.

REPAIRER: The machine is being repaired.

SINGER: I can sing the missing passage.

[The singer holds a note while the repairer secures the panel. The critic raises a card marked "BRAVO," then listens to the machine restart. It spins quietly.] 

CRITIC: The tenor has become a whisper.

ATTENDANT: It has become a properly balanced washer.

CUSTOMER: My clothes are still inside.

[The attendant starts the machine. It runs smoothly. The singer tries to harmonize with the hum, but the hum is too soft to hear over the dryer across the room.] 

SINGER: I have lost the orchestra.

REPAIRER: That is a dryer vent. I can check it after this load.

CRITIC: Can the washer perform an encore?

ATTENDANT: It has one rinse remaining.

[The washer completes its cycle and unlocks. The customer collects the clothes. The singer takes a bow; the critic gives the machine a review card reading "ONE QUIET SPIN." ]

CUSTOMER: The machine did its job.

SINGER: And I sustained one note.

CRITIC: A compact evening.

[The attendant removes the review card from the washer door before starting another load.] 

[The customer checks the washer's display. One minute remains. The singer begins another held note, then stops when the display changes to zero.]

ATTENDANT: The cycle is complete.

CUSTOMER: The note lasted longer than the spin.

SINGER: I was matching the machine.

REPAIRER: The machine was not performing.

CRITIC: It was still an effective duet.

[The attendant opens the washer only after the drum stops. The customer removes a balanced load of towels. The repairer checks the panel once more without restarting the machine.]

REPAIRER: The screw stayed tight.

ATTENDANT: That is the encore we needed.

[The critic writes a short review and pins it to the bulletin board, not to the appliance. The singer bows to the customers waiting with laundry baskets.]

[The singer checks the critic's card, which says "ONE QUIET SPIN." The singer adds a small note beneath it: "NO ENCORE REQUESTED." The attendant turns the card toward the waiting customers.]

FINAL IMAGE: The repaired washer turns quietly as the singer bows to an empty laundry basket.
