# The Gardening Show's Dirt Reveal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: gardening competitions that prize surprise reveals over the ordinary work that keeps plants alive*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Host Vella, a macaque; Gardener Odi, a gibbon; Judge Kess, a capuchin; and Crew Chief Baro, a baboon.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - DAY

A garden bed sits beneath a cloth. Vella holds a microphone. Odi wears muddy gloves. Kess studies a scorecard.

VELLA: In five minutes, we reveal the transformation of this neglected patch.

ODI: I have been weeding it for three weeks.

KESS: We are scoring the reveal, not the weeks.

BARO: The cloth is snagged on the watering can.

VELLA: Can we hide that until the camera sees the finished bed?

ODI: The watering can is how the bed stays alive.

[BARO PULLS THE CLOTH. IT SNAGS, LIFTING ONE CORNER AND SHOWING A PLAIN ROW OF BEANS.]

VELLA: The audience has had a preview.

KESS: The beans look healthy.

ODI: They have been watered every morning.

KESS: Did the beans agree to be on camera?

ODI: They do not have a release form.

VELLA: We can film the leaves from behind.

KESS: Was that in the montage?

ODI: No. The montage showed me turning toward the sun.

VELLA: That shot took eight tries.

ODI: The sun moved while we were filming.

KESS: The beans kept growing.

BARO: The crew kept moving the cloth.

[THE CLOTH SNAGS ON A BEAN STAKE AGAIN. ODI UNHOOKS IT AND WATERS THE ROW.]

ODI: This is the shot that matters.

INT. GARDEN SHED - MOMENTS LATER

Vella reviews a shot list. Odi pours water into a small can. Kess points to a pot of dry soil.

KESS: The pot must look dramatic before the reveal.

ODI: It is dry because nobody watered it.

VELLA: Could you water it after the camera finds it?

ODI: Plants do not need a cue.

BARO: The camera is ready.

[ODI WATERS THE POT. A LOOSE LABEL FALLS OFF AND LANDS ON THE FLOOR.]

VELLA: That is a nice reveal of the label.

ODI: The label says what the plant is.

KESS: We should judge the garden as it stands.

VELLA: The show is called Dirt Reveal.

ODI: Then reveal the dirt. It's where the roots are.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - LATER

The cloth is removed properly. Rows of beans, herbs, and flowers appear. The watering can is visible in the center.

KESS: The garden gets the prize for care.

VELLA: I thought you judged the reveal.

KESS: The reveal was that someone did the work.

[BARO WATERS A WILTED CORNER. THE PLANTS LIFT SLIGHTLY.]

VELLA: Can we get a slow-motion shot?

ODI: You can. I have another bed to water.

KESS: I will judge that one tomorrow.

ODI: The plants will not wait for the camera.

[ODI WALKS OFF WITH THE CAN. THE CAMERA FOLLOWS THE WATERING CAN, LEAVING THE PRIZE RIBBON HANGING FROM A BEAN STAKE.]
