# The Gift Wrap Has Its Own Gift

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury gift wrapping and packaging rituals that overwhelm the thing being given*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Nemi, a gibbon gift wrapper; Sol, a macaque customer; Rafi, a capuchin shop manager; Una, a lemur recipient.

INT. GIFT-WRAPPING COUNTER - AFTERNOON

[SOL SETS A SMALL WOODEN WHISTLE ON THE COUNTER. NEMI UNROLLS A SHEET OF GOLD PAPER BIG ENOUGH TO COVER THE TABLE.]

SOL: Could you wrap this, please?

NEMI: We offer three levels. Plain, ribbon, and unveiling.

SOL: Plain.

Rafi steps from behind a display of bows.

RAFI: The whistle is a meaningful gift. It deserves the unveiling.

SOL: It's for my niece. She likes whistles.

NEMI: We can give it a mountain reveal.

Sol looks at the gold paper, the tissue, and the miniature paper mountain already on the counter.

SOL: I just need it wrapped.

Nemi lays the whistle in a box. It rolls into one corner. She adds a paper insert to keep it centered. The insert has a printed note that says OPEN HERE.

NEMI: The note tells Una where to begin.

SOL: The box opens at the top.

Rafi produces a ribbon with a tiny brass bell. He ties it around the box. The bell rings whenever the box moves.

RAFI: The sound builds anticipation.

SOL: It sounds like the whistle.

Nemi lifts the box. The bell rings, and the wrapped whistle inside rolls to the other corner. The paper insert slides over it.

NEMI: It has shifted.

RAFI: A journey before the journey.

Sol unties the ribbon. The bell stops. He asks for plain paper. Nemi reaches for brown paper, but Rafi lays a smaller gold box on the counter.

RAFI: We can place this box inside the larger box. Then the recipient discovers the gift in stages.

SOL: It is one whistle.

RAFI: Three stages.

Nemi puts the small box inside the large one. The small box is too wide. She removes the paper insert and turns it diagonally. It fits, but the larger box will not close.

NEMI: I can fold the corners.

SOL: Please do.

She folds one corner. The other pops open. Rafi pins it down with an enormous bow. The bow covers the box lid.

RAFI: The package is complete.

SOL: The gift isn't visible.

NEMI: The bow is part of the package.

Sol takes the whistle out and hands it directly to Una, who has entered the shop. She blows it once. The tiny sound is clear.

UNA: I love it.

Rafi looks at the unopened box. Nemi sets the bow on top. It slides off and lands over the brass bell.

RAFI: The unveiling is ready.

UNA: What unveiling?

Sol hands her the whistle. Rafi unties the gold box. Inside is only the paper insert marked OPEN HERE.

NEMI: The gift has already been given.

Rafi picks up the insert and folds it into a little whistle-shaped sleeve. Una slips the actual whistle inside the sleeve.

UNA: Now it's wrapped.

[THE BRASS BELL RINGS AS RAFI LIFTS THE EMPTY GOLD BOX. EVERYONE TURNS. UNA BLOWS THE WHISTLE, AND THE SHOP'S DISPLAY OF BOWS TIPS OVER IN A SOFT CASCADE.]
