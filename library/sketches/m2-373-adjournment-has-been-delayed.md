# Adjournment Has Been Delayed

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: committee meetings where procedural votes about ending the meeting create another meeting*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Chair Nori, a macaque; Clerk Fen, a gibbon; Member Ota, a capuchin; Member Venn, a lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - 4:58 P.M.

[A WALL CLOCK READS 4:58. A SMALL GROUP SITS AROUND A TABLE. FEN HAS ONE PAGE LEFT ON THE AGENDA.]

NORI: We have two minutes before closing time.

OTA: Can we adjourn?

FEN: The rules require a motion.

Venn raises a hand. Fen writes it down.

FEN: Motion to adjourn.

NORI: Is there a second?

OTA: I second it.

NORI: We need discussion.

OTA: Discussion about leaving?

NORI: The rules say all motions get discussion.

Venn asks whether the motion can be amended to adjourn sooner. Nori says an amendment requires a second. Fen writes "amendment to adjourn sooner" beneath the first motion.

FEN: We must vote on the amendment first.

NORI: All in favor?

Everyone raises a hand. Fen counts twice.

FEN: The amendment passes. We will discuss adjournment sooner.

OTA: Then let's adjourn.

NORI: We haven't discussed the amended motion.

Venn says they should schedule a special meeting to discuss whether the committee needs meetings. Fen checks the wall calendar. Every date is filled with meetings about the calendar.

FEN: We could use the next meeting to remove a meeting.

NORI: That requires notice.

Ota points at the clock. It reads 4:59. Nori taps the clock, and the minute hand jumps backward to 4:58.

OTA: Did the clock just add time?

FEN: The facilities notice says it is one minute slow.

Venn asks whether the meeting can adjourn according to the wall clock or the actual time. Fen reads the rules. They say "until the hour," without defining whose hour.

NORI: We should interpret the rule.

OTA: At the next meeting?

NORI: Yes.

Ota stands. Fen reminds him that members must remain seated until adjournment. He sits back down. The chair squeaks. The clock advances to 4:59 again.

Venn suggests a unanimous consent motion. Nori asks if anyone objects to adjourning. No one does. Fen pauses with the pen over the minutes.

FEN: Does silence count as consent?

NORI: We need a ruling.

OTA: Can we rule on it now?

NORI: The rules require a motion.

Ota raises his hand. Fen writes the motion. The wall clock moves to 5:00. Fen closes the notebook before Nori can ask for discussion.

NORI: Meeting adjourned.

Venn picks up the calendar. A note on the back reads "Facilities check: clock adjusted to actual time."

FEN: It was slow by one minute.

OTA: So we adjourned on time.

NORI: We adjourned one minute ago.

Fen stamps the minutes with the current time. The stamp reads 4:59.

[EVERYONE STARES AT THE CLOCK. NORI SAYS THEY WILL DISCUSS THE DISCREPANCY AT THE NEXT MEETING. OTA HAS ALREADY LEFT. FEN WRITES "MEMBER ABSENT" BESIDE HIS CHAIR.]
