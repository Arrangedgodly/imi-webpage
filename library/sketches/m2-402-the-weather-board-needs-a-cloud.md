# The Weather Board Needs a Cloud

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: live weather broadcasts that manufacture a dramatic forecast from ordinary conditions*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Presenter Vex, a macaque; camera operator Mara, a gibbon; intern Pip, a capuchin; gardener Nari, a lemur.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

[A portable weather board stands beside an empty sky. Pip holds a cutout cloud on a stick. Vex speaks into a microphone.]

VEX: We are live from the garden, where a cloud may develop.

MARA: There isn't a cloud.

VEX: We are reporting the possibility.

PIP: This is the only cloud in storage.

[Nari pushes a wheelbarrow through the shot. It carries compost.]

NARI: Move the board. I need the path.

VEX: You are walking into a weather alert.

NARI: I'm walking to the beans.

[Vex points to a blank space on the board labeled SEVERE. Pip raises the cardboard cloud. A breeze folds it over.]

VEX: The cloud has collapsed.

MARA: It's cardboard.

PIP: We could tape the edge.

[Vex refuses tape because the forecast board must look official. He asks Nari to hold the cloud. Nari pins it with a bean vine.]

NARI: There. It is rooted.

VEX: We cannot claim a cloud is rooted.

NARI: It is now.

[Mara films the board. Vex announces a chance of rain. A watering can drips from a hole in its handle onto the word RAIN.]

VEX: Rainfall has begun.

NARI: That's my can.

PIP: It is precipitation from one container.

[Vex moves the board to keep the camera dry. The wheelbarrow follows the garden path and blocks the lens. Mara shifts left; the barrow blocks her again.]

MARA: We need a clear shot.

NARI: You need a clear path.

[They negotiate. Nari parks the barrow beside the board. Vex adds a sun icon to the forecast. The cutout cloud casts a shadow over it.]

VEX: Sudden darkness.

PIP: The cloud is back.

MARA: That's its shadow.

[Vex turns the board around to show the cloud icon to camera. The blank back faces Nari, who uses it to prop up her bean tray. Pip's cardboard cloud slips off its stick and lands in the barrow.]

NARI: The storm is in the compost.

VEX: We have confirmed a localized cell.

MARA: It is a paper shape.

[Nari tips the barrow to unload compost. The cloud blows out and lodges over Vex's microphone. His voice becomes muffled.]

VEX: The forecast is changing.

PIP: We can hear you less.

[Vex lifts the cloud away. Nari takes it, tears a small corner to use as a plant marker, and writes BEANS on it.]

VEX: That's evidence.

NARI: That's a label.

[Nari opens the garden tap. A sprinkler starts behind the weather board and sweeps a narrow arc across the ground. Vex backs into it while keeping his microphone aimed at the sky.]

VEX: Heavy rain, moving east.

MARA: The rain is moving in a circle.

PIP: It is watering the forecast.

[Vex sidesteps. The sprinkler follows its fixed arc and soaks his shoes. Nari shuts off the tap.]

NARI: The weather has finished its shift.

FINAL BEAT: Mara frames Nari's new marker. The camera catches a real cloud drifting overhead, but Vex lowers the lens to keep the forecast board in shot. Pip pins the cardboard remnant under the word SUNNY.
