# The Wedding Photographer's Drone Has Opinions

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wedding photography packages that prioritize dramatic shots over the actual ceremony*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Photographer Reka, a capuchin; couple Luma and Davi, two macaques; officiant Odo, a baboon; and assistant Piri, a gibbon.

EXT. GARDEN CEREMONY - DAY

A small drone hovers above a wedding arch. Reka holds a tablet; Piri carries a reflector.

REKA: We need the grand aerial reveal before the vows.

LUMA: We planned a quiet ceremony.

DAVI: No aerial reveal was on the schedule.

PIRI: The drone's camera is already recording.

[The drone lowers until it blocks Odo's view of the couple.]

ODO: I can see the machine better than the people getting married.

REKA: It creates an intimate frame.

LUMA: It is the size of a dinner plate.

DAVI: And it is humming the wedding march.

[The drone's navigation chime plays three notes.]

EXT. GARDEN - MOMENTS LATER

Piri holds the reflector. Reka asks Luma and Davi to walk toward each other again for a second take.

LUMA: We are already together.

REKA: The camera missed the first approach.

DAVI: The camera arrived before we did.

[The drone flies backward to capture the approach. It bumps the flower arch; Piri catches a hanging ribbon.]

PIRI: I have the decoration.

ODO: I have the ceremony.

REKA: Can we repeat the entrance without the arch?

LUMA: We can start the vows.

[Odo begins. The drone pivots to face him and records the officiant's forehead in extreme close-up.]

ODO: Do you take this partner-

REKA: The drone needs a wider shot.

ODO: It can move back.

[The drone retreats and lands in the ceremonial flower basket.]

EXT. GARDEN - LATER

Reka places the drone on a chair and switches to a handheld camera. Piri lowers the reflector.

REKA: We can do the group portrait without a flyover.

DAVI: The guests are right there.

[The drone auto-rotates toward the flower arch. Its propellers blow the seating chart off the table.]

PIRI: I will catch the cards.

LUMA: The guests can find their names on the chairs.

[The guests shift seats to rescue the cards, accidentally arranging themselves in a neat semicircle around the couple. Reka takes the handheld shot.]

REKA: We will work with what happens once.

LUMA: Good.

DAVI: The vows are not a rehearsal.

[The couple exchange rings. Odo speaks clearly. Piri quietly adjusts a ribbon before it falls. The camera stays still.]

REKA: That was beautiful.

ODO: It was also the actual ceremony.

[The drone wakes itself, rises from the basket, and gently follows the couple as they walk away. Its camera captures the two guests carrying the flower arch and Odo carrying the entire basket.]

PIRI: It found the candid shot.

REKA: The picture is mostly flowers.

LUMA: That's the part everyone remembers.

[The drone prints a frame on its tiny onboard display: Odo with a flower basket over his head.]

ODO: Please send that one to the couple.
