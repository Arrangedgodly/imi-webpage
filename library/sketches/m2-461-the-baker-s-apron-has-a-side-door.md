# The Baker's Apron Has a Side Door

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive baking shows, secret ingredients, and apron pockets treated like plot twists*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: BAKER, a macaque; JUDGE, a baboon; HOST, a gibbon; ASSISTANT, a capuchin; APRON VOICE, an orangutan voice from a speaker in the costume prop.

[A baking tent. The baker wears a large apron with two pockets and one loose seam.]

HOST: Your challenge is to make one tart from the ingredients on this table.

BAKER: I have flour, fruit, and butter.

JUDGE: And an apron.

APRON VOICE: I am part of the kitchen team.

HOST: The apron has spoken.

[The assistant checks the apron pockets. A wooden spoon falls out and lands on the table.] 

ASSISTANT: The apron has extra equipment.

BAKER: The spoon is mine. The pocket also has a cherry from the table.

JUDGE: We cannot judge a tart made with ingredients that left the table.

[The baker reaches into the pocket to retrieve a cloth. The loose seam opens and one cherry rolls toward the oven. The assistant catches it in a measuring cup.] 

BAKER: The apron has a side door.

APRON VOICE: It is a vent.

[The judge inspects the cherry in the measuring cup. The assistant transfers it to the cherry bowl, away from the oven.] 

JUDGE: It is still clean.

HOST: We have a twist.

BAKER: We have a seam to mend.

[The baker takes off the apron and hands it to the assistant. The assistant uses a clip to close the seam. The baker begins the tart in a plain apron with empty pockets.] 

JUDGE: Now there are no hidden ingredients.

HOST: But where is the story?

BAKER: In the fruit.

[The baker slices apples and arranges them in a spiral. The host tries to make the process dramatic by moving the camera closer. The judge pulls it back so the cutting board stays in frame.] 

JUDGE: The knife is working. Let it work.

[The assistant sets the clipped apron on a hook. The cherry remains in its bowl on the table.] 

ASSISTANT: One escape attempt, contained.

APRON VOICE: The pocket is empty now.

[The baker places the tart in the oven. The timer rings. The tart is golden and the apples stay in place. The judge tastes a slice and nods.] 

JUDGE: The crust is crisp. The fruit is cooked. The apron did not season it.

HOST: The winner is the tart.

BAKER: The tart does not need a pocket.

[The assistant leaves the clipped apron on its hook, away from the food table. The cherry bowl is set in the center.] 

[The judge asks the baker to name the tart ingredients: flour, apples, butter, and lemon. The cherry stays in its bowl beside the table. The host leans toward the apron as if it might confess.]

APRON VOICE: I have been cleared of carrying fruit.

HOST: The apron has made one statement, and the tart is finished.

FINAL IMAGE: The finished tart sits beside the empty apron while the cherry bowl stays safely on the table.
