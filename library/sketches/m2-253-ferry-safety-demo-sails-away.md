# The Ferry Safety Demonstration Sails Away

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public safety demonstrations that turn a simple drill into an overproduced event*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Demonstration leader Odo, a baboon; ferry captain Nara, a gibbon; passenger Kito, a macaque; and announcer Seli, a capuchin.

EXT. DOCKED FERRY - DAY

A small ferry is tied to the dock. Seli holds a microphone. Odo stands beside a life ring and a large demonstration board.

SELI: Welcome to the safety demonstration. Please remain calm and follow the crew.

ODO: I will show you where the life rings are.

KITO: There is one beside you.

ODO: Yes. That is the first step.

NARA: The ferry is tied up and not moving.

SELI: Imagine a gentle emergency.

[Odo raises the life ring. Its rope uncoils and loops around the microphone stand.]

SELI: We have our first practical example.

ODO: The ring stays on the boat until someone needs it.

KITO: The rope has chosen the announcer.

INT. FERRY CABIN - MOMENTS LATER

Nara demonstrates the exit route. Odo follows with a pointer. Seli walks backward, narrating.

NARA: In an emergency, use this marked exit.

SELI: The bright green door leads outside.

[Kito opens it. The door leads to a broom closet. A mop tips into his arms.]

KITO: I have found the exit supplies.

NARA: That is the closet. The exit is two steps to the left.

ODO: The sign is pointed the wrong way.

SELI: The arrow has wandered.

[Nara turns the sign. Seli continues to face the closet and gestures at it.]

SELI: And here is our well-stocked emergency closet.

NARA: It is a mop.

EXT. DECK - LATER

Odo assembles passengers for a demonstration of the muster point. Seli places a cone in the center of the deck.

ODO: Everyone gather at the cone.

KITO: It is on the ramp.

NARA: The ramp is going up.

[The ferry rocks gently. The cone rolls down the ramp and stops against the dock line. Seli follows it, still speaking into the microphone.]

SELI: The muster point is mobile.

ODO: Please do not chase the cone.

KITO: I am retrieving it before it reaches the water.

[Nara catches the cone with her foot and sets it back on deck.]

NARA: A muster point should be marked where it can stay.

ODO: We can use the painted square by the rail.

SELI: The square is already labeled MUSTER.

KITO: That seems easier.

EXT. DECK - FINAL DEMONSTRATION

The group stands on the painted square. Odo holds up the life ring without pulling its rope. Seli points to the correct door.

SELI: In a real emergency, listen to the crew, use the marked route, and gather here.

KITO: I can see the sign from here.

NARA: And the ferry remains tied to the dock.

[The dock line loosens as Nara unties it for the scheduled departure. The ferry eases away. Everyone stays in the marked square. Seli's microphone cable stretches; Odo unplugs it before it follows the ferry.]

SELI: Demonstration complete.

ODO: The boat has left without the equipment.

KITO: That is the safest part so far.
