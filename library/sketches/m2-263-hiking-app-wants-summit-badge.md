# The Hiking App Wants a Summit Badge

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fitness apps that turn a simple walk into a competition for badges and recorded achievements*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Hiker Kori, a macaque; app designer Vela, a gibbon; ranger Tern, a baboon; and hiker Miri, a capuchin.

EXT. TRAILHEAD - MORNING

Kori opens a hiking app. A screen reads: SUMMIT BADGE: 0.0 MILES. Miri adjusts a water pack. Ranger Tern points to the marked trail.

KORI: The app says the summit is four miles away.

TERN: The trail is four miles round trip.

VELA: The badge needs a summit photo and continuous tracking.

MIRI: We are going for a walk.

VELA: A walk with a measurable achievement.

[The app chimes and begins recording before they leave the trailhead.]

EXT. TRAIL - LATER

Kori walks carefully over a root. The app chirps.

KORI: It says I took a detour.

TERN: You stepped around a tree.

VELA: The algorithm values straight progress.

MIRI: The trail curves around the hill.

[The app map draws a straight line from Kori through the hill. The badge bar leaps forward, then reverses when the GPS catches up.]

KORI: I have lost half a summit.

TERN: You are still on the path.

VELA: We can improve the signal by walking to the lookout.

MIRI: The lookout is on the route.

[They reach a bench. Vela poses Kori beside the view. A squirrel crosses behind him; the app's camera recognizes the squirrel as the summit marker.]

APP VOICE: Peak achieved.

KORI: That is a squirrel.

VELA: It has an impressive silhouette.

EXT. TRAIL FORK - AFTERNOON

The party reaches a fork with a clear sign: LAKE LOOP LEFT, SUMMIT PATH RIGHT. The app points straight into a patch of brush.

TERN: Follow the trail sign.

VELA: The app has a better data model.

MIRI: The app is drawing a line through a bush.

[Kori steps toward the brush. The phone loses signal and announces a record.]

APP VOICE: New personal best.

KORI: I have not moved.

TERN: It thinks the map has moved.

[They take the marked summit path. At the top, Kori stands beside the trail marker; Miri takes a photo of the view without the phone.]

KORI: Can the app award the badge now?

VELA: It requires the device to be visible in the summit image.

MIRI: Then the photo is of the device.

[She takes one photo of the landscape, then one of Kori holding the phone beside the marker.]

EXT. TRAILHEAD - LATE AFTERNOON

Back at the trailhead, the app displays three badges: DETOUR, SQUIRREL PEAK, and SUMMIT.

KORI: It counted the walk.

TERN: It counted several other things too.

VELA: The app has recognized the entire hike.

MIRI: The best part was when we stopped checking it.

[The phone chimes: SCREEN-TIME BREAK ACHIEVED. Kori turns it off and puts it in the pack.]

KORI: That one can stay unclaimed.
