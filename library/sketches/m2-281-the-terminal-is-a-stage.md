# The Terminal Is a Stage

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport gate announcements, boarding groups, and cheerful travel updates that conceal a very small scheduling problem*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Gate agent Liri, a capuchin; passenger Bok, a macaque; passenger Tavi, a gibbon; and announcement voice Omi, an orangutan.

INT. SMALL AIRPORT GATE - MORNING

A departure board reads BOARDING SOON. Liri checks a clipboard. Bok stands by the counter with one bag. Tavi has a folded paper map and a travel pillow around his neck.

LIRI: We are boarding by group. Group one may approach the line.

BOK: How many groups are there?

LIRI: Two.

TAVI: I am group two.

LIRI: The aircraft is still on the ground across the runway.

Omi's voice comes over the speaker.

OMI: We are pleased to announce that boarding will begin shortly. Please remain near the gate, except passengers who are already standing at the gate.

BOK: That is all of us.

LIRI: Then everyone is doing very well.

The board changes to BOARDING NOW. No one moves. Liri takes the microphone.

LIRI: We are ready to board. The plane is not here yet.

TAVI: Should we form a line?

LIRI: We could form a line that ends at the window.

Bok starts a line. Tavi joins behind him. Liri follows with her clipboard. The line circles the seating area and returns to the gate counter.

BOK: I am first in line again.

LIRI: That is efficient.

Omi announces a gate change to the gate beside them. The passengers turn around. There is only one gate and one counter.

TAVI: The announcement said gate two.

LIRI: This is gate two.

BOK: We were already at gate two.

LIRI: The message is accurate.

The plane arrives at the window. A ground crew member waves from the distant tarmac. Omi announces, "Passengers may now board in the order they have been standing." Everyone studies the circular line.

TAVI: Who is at the end?

BOK: I am at the beginning.

LIRI: We will use the original order. Group one: Bok. Group two: Tavi. Gate agent: me.

BOK: Are you going too?

LIRI: I will be here, keeping the gate open for the next announcement.

She opens the rope barrier. Bok and Tavi step through. The ramp is not attached yet.

OMI: Boarding is temporarily paused while the aircraft connects to the ramp.

BOK: We have moved three steps.

TAVI: I am still group two.

LIRI: Please remain where you are. That is now the boarding area.

The ramp locks into place. Bok and Tavi move to its entrance while Liri holds the barrier open.

OMI: We thank passengers for their patience and their circular line formation.

LIRI: Next time we will board by seat row.

BOK: What if the seats are in a circle?

LIRI: Then you will already be seated.

Bok and Tavi board. Liri closes the barrier. The gate light switches from BOARDING NOW to DEPARTED, and she checks the board.

LIRI: The flight has left on time.

She picks up the circular rope barrier, which has become a neat coil around her clipboard.
