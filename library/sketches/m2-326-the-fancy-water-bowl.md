# The Fancy Water Bowl

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury product unboxing videos that make basic household items sound exclusive*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Reviewer Leni, a gibbon; sponsor voice Tavi, a parrot; shopper Odo, a baboon; maker Nara, a capuchin.

INT. PRODUCT STUDIO - DAY

[A small clay bowl sits inside a box larger than the table. Leni faces a camera. Tavi perches above a card marked LIVE.]

LENI: Today we unveil the exclusive Vessel One.

ODO: I ordered a water bowl.

TAVI: We prefer not to restrict the owner's imagination.

[Nara opens the outer box, removes padding, then opens a smaller box. Leni reaches for scissors.]

NARA: The next layer is a tea towel.

LENI: Shall we cut it?

NARA: Shall we unfold it?

[Leni unfolds the towel very slowly. Odo reaches toward the exposed bowl. Tavi taps his paw with a presentation card.]

TAVI: Wait for the rim reveal.

ODO: I can see the rim.

LENI: You can see it before the audience. Please look surprised later.

[Nara rotates the bowl beneath the lights. Odo rotates his head with it.]

LENI: Notice the quiet confidence of the base.

ODO: It is not moving.

TAVI: That stability is included in the subscription.

NARA: It is included in the bottom.

[Odo lifts the bowl. Leni gasps at a tiny chip underneath.]

LENI: An individual maker's mark.

NARA: I chipped it while packing. I took a nut off the price.

TAVI: Please do not disclose discounts during the exclusivity segment.

[He unfurls a branded cloth. It covers the bowl, the box and Odo's knees.]

ODO: I have lost the product again.

LENI: This is its premium protective environment.

[Odo folds the cloth into a mat. Nara pours water into the bowl.]

LENI: Observe the liquid capacity.

[Odo drinks. Tavi flaps both wings.]

TAVI: That was the review sample! We still need the first-sip reaction.

ODO: I can remember it. I was thirsty.

LENI: Could you be thirsty again for the close-up?

[Nara sets a second bowl beside it, decorated with a gold sticker.]

TAVI: Introducing Vessel One Plus. For customers seeking more.

ODO: More water?

[Nara fills both bowls from identical cups. The gold sticker lifts and floats.]

NARA: More sticker.

LENI: A detachable personalization option.

[Odo catches the sticker and puts it on the plain bowl.]

ODO: Have I upgraded?

TAVI: The upgrade must be activated through your account.

[Nara points to a shelf holding six plain bowls.]

NARA: Those are four nuts each. Take any one.

LENI: They have no packaging.

ODO: I was going to throw that away.

TAVI: The packaging contains the ownership certificate.

[Nara tears a scrap off the enormous box and writes PAID on it. Odo gives her four nuts.]

NARA: Certified.

[Leni swivels the camera toward the empty box, blocking Odo's departure.]

LENI: Join us next week for an exclusive unboxing of our compact travel cup.

[Odo fills the studio's ordinary cup at the tap.]

ODO: It is already open.

[Tavi places the gold sticker on the cup. Leni applauds. Odo peels it off, sticks it on Tavi's certificate card and walks out drinking.]

TAVI: The certificate now qualifies for premium status.

NARA: Does it hold water?
