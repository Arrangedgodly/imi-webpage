# The Orchestra Tunes the Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: classical concert etiquette that treats every incidental noise as a threat to artistic purity*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Conductor Venn, a gorilla; Etta, a macaque cellist; Flo, a gibbon usher; and Dax, a capuchin custodian.

INT. CONCERT HALL - EVENING

The orchestra sits in silence. Venn raises a tuning fork. Dax sweeps dust from the aisle. One grain lands on a chair.

VENN: Who made that sound?

DAX: The broom touched the floor.

VENN: The floor should be silent until the downbeat.

ETTA: We are supposed to tune now.

VENN: Only after the hall is ready to receive the note.

FLO: The audience is waiting outside.

VENN: Then they can wait in tune.

[Etta draws a soft note from her cello. A water pipe knocks once behind the stage. Venn freezes.]

VENN: We have a competing percussion section.

DAX: It is the radiator.

VENN: Is it in the program?

ETTA: The cello can match it.

VENN: We do not match plumbing.

INT. CONCERT HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Flo ushers in three monkeys. They sit. One coat rustles. Venn lowers the baton.

VENN: The coat has entered before the music.

FLO: It is a coat. It came with the guest.

DAX: I can put it on the coat rack.

VENN: Quietly.

[DAX OPENS THE RACK. THE HINGE SQUEAKS A PERFECT FOURTH ABOVE ETTA'S OPEN STRING.]

ETTA: That is nearly in tune.

VENN: It is a door.

FLO: The audience has settled.

[A GUEST'S STOMACH RUMBLES. VENN POINTS TO THE CEILING.]

VENN: A low note from the balcony.

GUEST: I skipped dinner to arrive early.

DAX: The lobby has crackers.

VENN: We cannot pause for refreshments.

ETTA: We can pause for the tuning note you keep postponing.

INT. CONCERT HALL - LATER

Venn finally raises the baton. Everyone inhales. Dax's broom handle rolls off the stage and lands against the radiator. It knocks the same rhythm as before.

VENN: Stop. The room is making music again.

ETTA: The room has joined the orchestra.

FLO: The audience applauded the radiator.

VENN: They are applauding the silence between its notes.

[DAX TAPS THE RADIATOR WITH THE BROOM. A WARM, LOW NOTE SOUNDS. ETTA ANSWERS ON CELLO. FLO SQUEAKS THE RACK DOOR IN RHYTHM.]

VENN: This is not the printed program.

ETTA: It is the first thing we have played together tonight.

FLO: The audience is clapping in time.

[THE AUDIENCE CLAPS ON THE RADIATOR'S KNOCK. ONE GUEST BEGINS A SLOW, FORMAL APPLAUSE.]

VENN: That guest is a beat behind.

ETTA: No, the guest has started the encore.

DAX: Should I bring out the broom again?

VENN: Only if it is tuned.

[DAX ROLLS THE BROOM HANDLE AGAINST THE RADIATOR. IT PLAYS A SHORT, WARM RUMBLE.]

DAX: The instrument is ready.

[Venn lowers the baton. The room's accidental ensemble gives a neat final chord.]

VENN: Intermission. The radiator has the solo.
