# The Expert Panel on Mud

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: prestige roundtables that turn obvious observations into competing theories*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: host Leni, a gibbon; expert Pava, a capuchin; expert Kito, a macaque; field worker Odo, a baboon.

INT. STUDIO - DAY

A tray of mud sits on a table. Leni introduces the panel.

LENI: Is this mud wet?

PAVA: It depends on one's definition of wet.

KITO: It is damp.

ODO: I brought it from beside the pond.

Pava says the mud's sheen suggests a recent relationship with water. Kito says it is still holding its shape. Odo presses one finger into it; the print stays.

LENI: We have a live test.

Pava asks whether the finger was dry. Odo says it was. Kito says that could change the surface. Leni asks if the panel can agree that the tray contains mud. Pava says the material is mud-like.

Odo brings a second sample from farther up the path. It is dry and crumbly. Pava says both samples may have been mud at different times. Kito says one is wet and one is dry. The host asks what the mud means for the village.

Pava says it could affect walking. Odo says the pond-side path is slippery. Kito recommends going around it until the sun dries it. Leni asks whether this is consensus. Pava says consensus would require a shared vocabulary.

Odo sets a boot beside the tray. The sole has mud on it. He steps on a sheet of paper and leaves a print.

KITO: That is the same mud.

PAVA: It is now a recorded sample.

Leni asks whether they should protect the paper. Odo lifts it before the mud smears. Pava says the print has moved the discussion from texture to evidence. Kito says the evidence supports a detour. The panel turns to camera and points toward the dry path.

Odo asks the panel to compare the dry sample with the damp one. Pava says the dry sample contains less shine but may still have moisture inside. Kito pinches a crumb; it breaks apart. Odo pinches the pond sample; it sticks to his glove. The tests agree with the observations. Pava says the glove may have changed the sample. Odo tries the test with a clean stick instead. The mud clings to it. Leni, offstage, asks if the program can finish before the mud dries. Odo says the discussion is nearly complete. Pava asks for one final comparison, and Kito places both samples beside one another.

Odo records the test result in the broadcast notes.

Leni asks whether the samples can be returned to the path. Odo says they can.

The debate is adjourned.

TAG

All three experts look at the boot.

ODO: It is on my shoe.
