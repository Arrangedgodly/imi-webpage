# The Queue Has a Queue

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public-service queue systems that require a ticket before a person can join a line*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Clerk Nima, a gibbon; visitor Odo, a baboon; visitor Sera, a macaque; ticket machine, a squeaky box operated by Nima.

INT. COMMUNITY OFFICE - MORNING

[Two rope lanes face a counter. Both are empty. Odo reaches Nima before she can switch on her ticket machine.]

ODO: I need a form.

NIMA: Take a number.

ODO: From you?

NIMA: From the machine. I am qualified to recognize numbers, not issue them.

[Odo presses the button. A ticket emerges: PLEASE JOIN THE QUEUE FOR TICKETS.]

ODO: It has given me a sentence.

NIMA: That is your provisional number. Stand in lane A until you receive your actual number.

[Odo stands in lane A. Sera enters. She presses the button and gets NUMBER TWO.]

SERA: Who is number one?

ODO: I am a sentence.

NIMA: The system is still assessing his request.

[The machine prints QUEUE A. Odo collects it.]

ODO: This is where I already am.

NIMA: Excellent. Your position has been confirmed.

SERA: May I join him?

NIMA: No. Two is a number, so you use lane B.

[Sera steps into lane B. It ends at the back of Odo's lane.]

SERA: I have joined him.

NIMA: Through the correct procedure.

[Odo offers his two slips. Nima brings out a pink form.]

NIMA: You require a queue discrepancy form.

ODO: Finally. I wanted a form.

NIMA: You wanted a different form. This form decides which queue can issue that form.

[She taps a box marked CURRENT NUMBER. Odo writes PLEASE JOIN THE QUEUE FOR TICKETS. His sentence extends over three boxes.]

NIMA: Please abbreviate.

ODO: PJTQFT.

NIMA: That looks like an unauthorized department.

[Sera presses the button once more. It sticks. A ribbon of tickets unrolls across both lanes.]

SERA: I wanted one number. I appear to have booked the afternoon.

[The display jumps to fourteen. Odo picks up ticket fourteen and its attached ticket fifteen.]

NIMA: Fourteen!

ODO: Here.

NIMA: What service do you require?

ODO: Void fifteen.

[Nima stamps fifteen VOID. The display changes to fifteen.]

NIMA: Fifteen!

[Odo offers the stamped ticket. Nima's stamp hovers above it.]

SERA: You served him by refusing to serve him.

NIMA: We have completed one transaction.

[The ribbon tightens around the rope posts. Odo and Sera duck as the posts lean toward the machine. Nima unplugs it before it can pull the counter forward.]

NIMA: We will use arrival order.

ODO: I arrived first.

SERA: I arrived second. I have unusually good evidence.

[She holds up NUMBER TWO. Nima hands Odo a blank form.]

ODO: This is the form I came for.

NIMA: Keep your place. I must find the pen.

[The unpowered machine squeaks as Odo's trailing ticket catches beneath his shoe. Nima lifts it. It reads NOW SERVING: QUEUE.]

NIMA: The queue has requested a service.

ODO: Let it fill out its own form.
