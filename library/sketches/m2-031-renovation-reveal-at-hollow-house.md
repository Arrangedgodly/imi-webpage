# Renovation Reveal at Hollow House

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Home renovation reveal shows and dramatic before-and-after reveals*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Luma, a lemur; homeowner Bako, a gorilla; designer Sivi, a macaque; carpenter Tern, a capuchin; camera ape Rolo, a chimpanzee.

[STAGE: A SMALL TREE HOLLOW. A CAMERA CREW HIDES BEHIND A LEAF. A CURTAIN COVERS THE SAME ROOM.]

LUMA: Welcome back to Hollow Makeover, where we transform spaces and ask no structural questions.
BAKO: I asked for a wider doorway.
SIVI: We opened the doorway emotionally.
BAKO: I still have to turn sideways.
TERN: The door now has a beautiful curve.
ROLO: Camera rolling. Can we get a reaction shot?
LUMA: Bako, close your eyes. Imagine your dream home.
BAKO: I live in it.
LUMA: Imagine it with more suspense.
SIVI: We selected a calm, natural palette.
BAKO: The wall is brown.
SIVI: It is called Grounded Bark.
TERN: The old wall was also brown.
SIVI: The new brown has confidence.
LUMA: And this is your surprise feature.
BAKO: Is that a window?
TERN: It is an opening toward the garden.
BAKO: It looks into the back of a wardrobe.
SIVI: We made storage feel spacious.
ROLO: Give us a gasp, Bako.
BAKO: I can see my coat.
LUMA: A wardrobe with a view.
SIVI: The room now flows from the bed to the pantry.
BAKO: The pantry is over there.
TERN: We removed the wall in between.
BAKO: Now a banana can roll into my bed.
LUMA: That is indoor-outdoor living.
BAKO: I did not ask for fruit delivery.
SIVI: We added a custom branch shelf.
BAKO: It is crooked.
TERN: It follows the natural grain.
BAKO: It follows gravity.
ROLO: Hold that look. That's honest emotion.
LUMA: Now for the reveal. Bako, open your eyes.
BAKO: I never closed them.
LUMA: We will count down anyway. Three, two, one!
[THE CURTAIN DROPS. A SECOND CURTAIN IS STILL COVERING THE ROOM.]
ROLO: Great reveal. The fabric has a strong silhouette.
BAKO: Is there another reveal?
SIVI: Yes. We have layered the surprise.
TERN: I will pull it.
[TERN PULLS THE SECOND CURTAIN. IT IS TIED TO THE WARDROBE DOOR, WHICH SWINGS OPEN.]
BAKO: My coat fell into the pantry.
LUMA: Your space has become connected.
BAKO: My coat is covered in flour.
TERN: The pantry was open concept.
SIVI: The final touch is a welcome mat.
BAKO: It is inside the door.
SIVI: It welcomes you as you leave the bedroom.
LUMA: We have changed the way this home feels.
BAKO: It feels windy.
ROLO: Could you say that after the music swell?
BAKO: It feels windy.
LUMA: Perfect. A home transformed by air and intention.
TERN: The door is still narrow.
SIVI: But the curve is now famous.
BAKO: I will keep the old room.
LUMA: It is the new room now.
BAKO: Then I will keep the old coat.

[THE COAT SLIDES FROM THE WARDROBE INTO THE PANTRY AS THE CAMERA PANS TO THE CROOKED SHELF.]
