# The Queue Is the Exhibit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum line management and interactive displays that cannot handle visitors playing naturally*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Gallery manager Omi, a capuchin; exhibit designer Fenn, a gibbon; visitor Sali, a macaque; and usher Rook, a baboon.

[A MUSEUM SIGN READS "PLEASE WAIT HERE FOR THE INTERACTIVE EXHIBIT." A LINE FORMS.]

OMI: One visitor at a time.

SALI: What does the exhibit do?

FENN: It responds to your movement.

[SALI WAVES A HAND. NOTHING HAPPENS.]

ROOK: The exhibit is waiting for a visitor.

SALI: I am here.

[ROOK POINTS TO A FLOOR MARK. SALI STEPS ON IT. THE EXHIBIT BEEPS.]

FENN: It has registered a participant.

SALI: What do I do?

FENN: Move naturally.

[SALI TAKES A STEP. THE EXHIBIT PLAYS A RECORDING: "PLEASE WAIT HERE."]

SALI: I am waiting here.

[THE LINE GROWS. A MONKEY SKIPS THE QUEUE AND STEPS ON THE MARK.]

OMI: Please wait your turn.

[THE EXHIBIT BEEPS AGAIN.]

FENN: It has accepted the next visitor.

ROOK: The exhibit does not know the queue.

[OMI PUTS UP A SECOND FLOOR MARK. THE MONKEY STEPS ON BOTH.]

OMI: That is not two participants.

SALI: It has two feet.

[THE EXHIBIT PLAYS ANOTHER RECORDING: "THANK YOU FOR PARTICIPATING."]

FENN: The exhibit is complete.

SALI: I did not do anything.

FENN: You waited.

[THE LINE CHEERS. OMI HANDS OUT TICKETS FOR THE EXIT.]

ROOK: We need to move the visitors along.

[THE MONKEY TAKES A TICKET AND PUTS IT IN THE RETURN SLOT.]

OMI: That is not the exit.

[THE EXHIBIT BEEPS.]

FENN: It has started again.

SALI: Can I go now?

ROOK: You need to leave the queue first.

[SALI STEPS OFF THE MARK. THE EXHIBIT PLAYS: "PLEASE WAIT HERE."]

OMI: The exhibit wants her back.

[THE MONKEY STEPS ON THE MARK AND SITS DOWN.]

FENN: It has found the ideal visitor.

[FENN SHOWS OMI A CONTROL PANEL.]

FENN: If we press this button, the exhibit resets.

OMI: Should we press it?

FENN: Only after the current visitor finishes.

[THE MONKEY SITS ON THE FLOOR MARK AND LOOKS AT THE CONTROL PANEL.]

SALI: It is looking at you.

OMI: It is waiting for the exhibit.

[THE MONKEY PRESSES THE BUTTON WITH ONE FINGER. THE RECORDING STARTS.]

EXHIBIT: Thank you for waiting.

FENN: It has completed the interaction.

ROOK: The next person can go.

[THE LINE DOES NOT MOVE.]

SALI: We are all waiting to see what happens.

[THE EXHIBIT PLAYS THE SAME RECORDING AGAIN.]

[OMI HANDS OUT TICKETS FOR THE NEXT GROUP.]

FENN: They will wait in order.

[THE MONKEY MOVES TO THE FRONT OF THE LINE. THE STAFF MOVES THE LINE AROUND IT.]

ROOK: The queue is now the exhibit.

OMI: Please wait here.

[EVERYONE STANDS ON A FLOOR MARK.]

OMI: The next group may begin.


[THE STAFF TAKES THEIR PLACES IN LINE BEHIND THE MONKEY.]
