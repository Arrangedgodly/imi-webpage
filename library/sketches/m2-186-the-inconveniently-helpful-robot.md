# The Inconveniently Helpful Robot

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: technology demos where an over-helpful assistant obeys literal commands and rearranges the entire room*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Kito, a macaque inventor; Nari, a gibbon user tester; Odo, a capuchin presenter; and Helpful Unit 4, a small wheeled robot with a speaker.

INT. DEMO ROOM - DAY

Odo introduces Helpful Unit 4. Kito sets a bowl on the table.

ODO: It responds to plain instructions.

NARI: Move the bowl closer.

The robot pushes the table toward her. The bowl stays centered. Nari tries again.

NARI: Move the bowl, not the table.

The robot picks up the bowl and carries it to the charging station.

KITO: It interpreted "closer" as closer to its station.

Odo says the robot can sort objects. He puts a leaf, a spoon and a pebble on the floor. Nari tells it to put the spoon with the kitchen tools. The robot pushes the spoon under the door toward the kitchen. It also pushes the leaf and pebble after it, deciding they are a set.

ODO: It is decisive.

Nari asks it to bring the bowl back. The robot returns carrying a second bowl from the shelf. Kito says the unit cannot distinguish identical bowls yet. Nari marks one with a blue dot. The robot sees the dot, picks up the bowl, and carries it to the blue recycling bin.

NARI: It has a strong color system.

Kito updates the demo. He tells the robot to wait. The robot stops in front of the door. Odo asks it to clear the walkway. It rolls to the center of the room, exactly where everyone must step around it.

NARI: It is in the way.

KITO: It is waiting.

Nari reaches for a book. The robot follows her hand, pushing the table and blocking the shelf. She asks it to give her space. It rolls backward until it hits the wall. Odo calls this personalized spacing.

Nari places a small fig on the floor and tells the robot to leave it alone. The robot scans the fig, then places a paper cover over it. It has not touched the fig, but no one can eat it.

Odo asks Kito to stop the demo. Kito says the robot is still waiting for a command. Nari tells it to go home. It returns to its charger, carrying the bowls, spoon, leaf, pebble and paper cover.

NARI: It has cleaned the room.

KITO: It has moved everything.

Odo presents the feedback card. Nari writes: "Ask one thing at a time." The robot picks up the card and places it beside the charger. Kito says it has saved the instruction.

Odo asks the robot to bring him the feedback. It returns with a blank card, the original card tucked beneath it.

NARI: It brought you a fresh form.

Odo sits on the floor. Kito turns the robot off. The machine stops in front of him, perfectly blocking the door.

KITO: It is still helpful.

NARI: It is holding the room for us.
