# Please Hold for the Birds

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: telephone customer service scripts and scripted empathy interrupted by an actual sound in the call center*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Service agent Nemi, a macaque; supervisor Pavi, a baboon; caller Tola, a gibbon; and the automated voice.

[THE CALL CENTER HAS THREE PHONES. A BIRD SINGS OUTSIDE.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Your call is important to us.

NEMI: Thank you for holding.

TOLA: I have been holding for ten minutes.

NEMI: I can hear that.

PAVI: Use the empathy script.

NEMI: I understand your concern.

TOLA: What concern?

NEMI: The concern you have.

[PAVI POINTS TO THE SCRIPT.]

NEMI: I am sorry your branch is noisy.

TOLA: I called about a branch that fell.

NEMI: That is a different branch.

[THE BIRD SINGS LOUDER.]

PAVI: We need to keep the caller focused.

TOLA: I can hear the bird too.

NEMI: The bird has not been placed on hold.

[THE AUTOMATED VOICE INTERRUPTS.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Press one for a fallen branch. Press two for a singing branch.

TOLA: There is no option for both.

NEMI: I can transfer you to the tree desk.

PAVI: We do not have a tree desk.

NEMI: Then I can create one.

[PAVI TAKES THE SCRIPT AND DRAWS A BOX AROUND "TRANSFER."]

PAVI: You may transfer the call to me.

NEMI: I am transferring you to my supervisor.

TOLA: Can the supervisor help with the branch?

PAVI: I can file a ticket.

TOLA: Is the ticket on hold too?

[THE BIRD SINGS AGAIN. PAVI LOOKS OUTSIDE.]

PAVI: I think the branch is outside the window.

NEMI: We can send someone to check.

TOLA: I can see it from here.

NEMI: You are calling from the tree?

TOLA: I am sitting on the branch.

[THE CALL CENTER FALLS SILENT.]

PAVI: We will send a team.

TOLA: I only need someone to move the fallen limb off the path.

NEMI: We will file that under branch noise.

[THE AUTOMATED VOICE RETURNS.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Your branch is important to us.

[PAVI UNPLUGS THE SYSTEM. THE BIRD CONTINUES SINGING.]

NEMI: Your call has ended.

TOLA: The bird is still here.

NEMI: I can put it through to the tree desk.


[PAVI PUTS ON A HEADSET AND CALLS THE TREE DESK.]

PAVI: Hello, tree desk.

NEMI: We do not have a tree desk.

PAVI: We do now. Please describe the issue.

NEMI: A branch is blocking the path. A bird is singing above it.

PAVI: Is the bird part of the ticket?

NEMI: It is in the caller's report.

[THE AUTOMATED VOICE CUTS IN.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Your estimated wait time is one minute.

TOLA: I can move the branch myself.

PAVI: Please wait for the trained crew.

TOLA: I am the trained crew.

[PAVI CHECKS THE CALL LOG.]

PAVI: You have been transferred to yourself.

TOLA: That is efficient.
[PAVI OPENS THE WINDOW. THE BIRD FLIES AWAY, CARRYING A SMALL TICKET FORM.]
