# The Lunchbox Dress Code

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school uniform rules applied to lunch containers and the food inside them*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Deena, a gibbon lunch monitor; Savi, a capuchin student; Olo, a macaque principal; Pimm, a lemur student.

INT. SCHOOL CAFETERIA - NOON

[A POSTER READS: LUNCHBOXES MUST BE NEAT, CLOSED, AND IN APPROVED COLORS. DEENA CHECKS A SAMPLE CASE.]

SAVI: Mine is green.

DEENA: Green is approved. Is it neat?

SAVI: It's a box.

DEENA: The lid is crooked.

Savi presses the lid down. It pops up on the other side. Pimm has a yellow lunchbox with a sticker on it.

DEENA: Stickers aren't part of the uniform.

PIMM: It's a picture of a pear.

DEENA: Pears are allowed. Pictures of pears require approval.

Olo enters carrying a metal lunchbox. It clangs loudly as he walks.

OLO: We have a problem with disruptive containers.

Savi closes his box. The lid squeaks.

OLO: That is also disruptive.

SAVI: What can I bring?

Deena shows him the approved sample, a silent white box with no handle. The sample is empty.

SAVI: Can it hold food?

DEENA: Food goes inside.

SAVI: How do I open it?

DEENA: The lid is sealed for demonstration.

Olo reads the policy. It says containers must be easy to identify from across the room. Deena asks every student to hold up a lunchbox. The approved white box disappears among the white tables.

Pimm's pear sticker is visible at once.

OLO: The sticker helps.

DEENA: It is not approved.

Olo peels off the sticker. Pimm's box becomes hard to distinguish from the tables. He places it on a table and immediately loses it.

PIMM: My lunch is under your elbow.

OLO: I was testing visibility.

Savi opens his green box. Inside is a banana, a sandwich, and a small bag of crackers. Deena checks each item for dress code.

DEENA: The banana is uniform. The sandwich has a loose corner.

SAVI: It is wrapped.

DEENA: The wrapper is folded unevenly.

Pimm's apple rolls out of his box and stops against Olo's shoe. Olo holds it up.

OLO: Is this authorized?

PIMM: It's an apple.

Deena looks at the poster. Fruit is allowed. Fruit pictures require approval. She checks the apple from both sides, then places it beside the pear sticker.

DEENA: Real fruit is compliant.

Savi asks whether he can eat. Olo says the lunchboxes must be closed during the inspection. Deena closes them. The lunch period bell rings. No one has eaten.

Pimm taps his empty box. It makes a hollow sound. Olo looks at the line of approved white containers.

OLO: We need to identify them.

Deena peels the pear sticker off the rule poster and places it on Pimm's box.

DEENA: Approved identifier.

[EVERYONE OPENS A LUNCHBOX. THE BELL RINGS AGAIN. OLO SITS WITH THE SAMPLE CASE, WHICH IS STILL SEALED, WHILE THE STUDENTS EAT.]
