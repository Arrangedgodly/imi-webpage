# The Treadmill Has a Scenic Route

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: gym fitness infomercials, dramatic before-and-after claims, and equipment with more modes than anyone can name*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Rell, a gibbon; trainer Palla, a ring-tailed lemur; demonstrator Kori, a macaque; and technician Odo, a capuchin.

INT. GYM - MORNING

Rell stands beside a treadmill with a screen showing a mountain path, a beach, and a forest.

RELL: This is the TrailMaster Three Thousand. It takes you anywhere without leaving the gym.

KORI: I would like to go to the beach.

PALLA: That mode is currently set to uphill forest.

Kori steps on. The belt begins slowly. The screen shows a mountain trail. His tail trails behind and presses the speed button.

KORI: I am climbing too fast.

ODO: Your tail selected sprint mode.

Rell faces the camera.

RELL: The machine adapts to your natural movement.

KORI: My natural movement is standing.

Odo resets the controls. Palla asks Kori to walk on the beach setting. The screen displays a sunset, but the treadmill tilts uphill.

PALLA: The beach has a steep dune.

KORI: The beach is flat.

RELL: It is a virtual beach.

The machine beeps and announces an automatic cool-down. The belt stops suddenly. Kori walks forward and lands in a pile of gym mats. The presenter calls it the "unexpected terrain response."

Odo checks the manual. The incline sensor is stuck. He opens the side panel. A sock is wrapped around the hinge.

ODO: This is a sock, not a sensor.

RELL: The sock has been part of the system.

Palla removes it with a towel. The treadmill starts again, now set to low speed. The screen shows a forest and makes bird sounds. Kori walks steadily. His tail taps the safety key. The belt slows, then stops.

KORI: I have finished the forest.

PALLA: You have walked for six seconds.

KORI: It was a short forest.

Rell tries the treadmill himself. His long gibbon arms swing, and his foot hits the incline button. The belt tilts downhill; Rell slides backward into the mat pile.

RELL: It takes you anywhere.

ODO: It takes you to the mats.

Odo removes the sock, resets the incline, and labels the controls. Kori picks the beach setting and walks at a comfortable speed. The scenery moves slowly; no one falls. Rell records a new demonstration.

RELL: You can choose the path, adjust the pace, and stop when you want.

KORI: And if your tail presses a button?

ODO: The safety key stops the belt.

Kori steps off. His tail pulls the safety key free. The treadmill stops. The screen keeps showing a sunny beach.

PALLA: The beach is still there.

KORI: I am done walking to it.

Rell turns the scenery to a waterfall. The treadmill's speaker plays a rushing sound. Odo finds that the volume control is the same button as the emergency stop. He leaves the sound off and labels the switch.

RELL: The next demonstration will include fewer buttons.
