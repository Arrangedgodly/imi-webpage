# The Local History Reenactment

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: historical reenactments that elevate a minor tool mishap into a founding event*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Doma, a gibbon narrator; Seli, a macaque reenactor; Tavi, a capuchin museum guide; and Pavo, a lemur sound operator.

EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - DAY

A sign reads "The Great Acorn Cart Incident: 1998." Doma narrates beside a small cart.

DOMA: On this day, one acorn rolled from the cart and changed the settlement forever.

Tavi whispers that the settlement was already there. Doma says the reenactment needs stakes. Seli plays the cart handler. She pushes the cart. One acorn rolls out and stops beside a boot.

PAVO: I have a rolling sound.

He shakes a box of pebbles into a microphone. It sounds like a storm. Doma asks for a more restrained effect. Pavo uses one pebble. It makes a tiny click.

DOMA: History turned on that click.

Tavi reads from an old record. The cart was carrying acorns to a seedling project. The acorn rolled into the soil and sprouted. Doma asks Seli to reenact the sprouting. She holds a paper leaf above the dirt.

SELI: The acorn grew.

DOMA: Over many seasons.

Seli raises the paper leaf slowly. Pavo adds an orchestral chord. The leaf falls over. Tavi picks it up and puts it in the soil. Doma calls that the moment the community learned persistence.

TAVI: The acorn was already planted.

Doma asks for a second take. Seli pushes the cart again. This time the acorn rolls the other direction. Pavo plays the same storm effect. Tavi stops him.

TAVI: It rolled six inches.

PAVO: The sound can be six miles.

The crowd arrives. Doma tells them the story of the acorn. He points to a tiny sprout in a pot. Seli places the original cart beside it. The wheel wobbles, and another acorn rolls out. It lands beside the same pot.

Doma begins his grand speech, but Tavi catches the acorn. He hands it to a child, who places it in the soil. The crowd applauds. Doma says the reenactment has created a sequel.

Seli secures the cart wheel with a wooden wedge. Pavo tests a quiet sound effect. It is a soft tap. Doma says it will do.

DOMA: From this humble moment came a tradition.

TAVI: The seedling project is still going.

A child asks if the new seed will get its own reenactment. Doma starts to answer, but Seli gently rolls the cart toward the garden shed. Its wheel wobbles again. Tavi reaches down and tightens the nut before another acorn can fall. Pavo records the tiny sound without adding the storm effect. The crowd looks at the sprout, then at the cart.

The group waters the pot. Doma invites everyone to take a small acorn home and plant it. Pavo shakes the sound box. One pebble remains. It clicks against the microphone.

PAVO: The sequel is ready.

Doma turns off the speaker. The crowd continues planting without the soundtrack.
