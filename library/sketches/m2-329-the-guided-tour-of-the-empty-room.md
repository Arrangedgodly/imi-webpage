# The Guided Tour of the Empty Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: immersive guided tours that insist an empty space has hidden significance*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Guide Odo, a baboon; visitor Nima, a gibbon; curator Pava, a capuchin; sound operator Leni, a macaque; maintenance worker Boro, a lemur.

INT. EMPTY ROOM - DAY

Odo stands in the middle of a blank room with one small sign.

ODO: Welcome to the Room of Unspoken Possibility.

NIMA: Is there anything in it?

PAVA: There is space.

Odo asks Leni to play a soft ambient tone. It sounds like a refrigerator. Pava asks her to turn it off. The room becomes quiet.

ODO: Now we can hear the silence.

Nima asks how long the tour lasts. Odo says the room should be experienced without a fixed schedule. Pava checks the booking sheet; the next group arrives in five minutes.

Odo invites Nima to walk clockwise. She does. He asks her to pause at the north wall. It is a plain wall with a light switch. Nima asks if the switch is part of the exhibit. Pava says it turns on the lights.

Leni moves a small stool into the room. Odo calls it an interpretive anchor. Pava says it is for visitors who need to sit. Nima sits. The stool squeaks.

ODO: The room has responded.

NIMA: The stool has responded.

Odo asks Nima what she feels. She says her legs are less tired. Pava says that is a valid interpretation. Leni tries the ambient tone again, but the refrigerator cycles off and the room gets quieter. Odo wonders if the room is changing.

Pava checks the thermostat; it has not changed. The door opens. Boro enters with a ladder.

BORO: Is the room free?

ODO: The tour is still in progress.

BORO: I need to change a bulb.

Odo moves aside so Boro can reach the light.

The visitors step aside. The worker climbs the ladder, replaces the bulb, and descends. The light is brighter.

NIMA: The room changed.

PAVA: A bulb was replaced.

Odo says the experience has concluded. Nima asks what the room was supposed to mean. Pava says it has no assigned meaning; the guide is there to help visitors notice what they notice. Nima says she noticed that sitting was comfortable and the bulb was dim. Odo writes this on the visitor card.

The next group arrives. Odo starts the tour again.

ODO: Welcome to the Room of Unspoken Possibility.

NIMA: The bulb is new.

Pava asks Odo to make room for the next group. He moves the stool against the wall, leaving the room as empty as before. Nima says the tour has changed the space by showing them how to use it.

The next visitors enter.

The guide waits by the door.

TAG

The maintenance worker returns with the old bulb.

ODO: The room has released a relic.
