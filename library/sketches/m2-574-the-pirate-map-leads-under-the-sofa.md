# The Pirate Map Leads Under the Sofa

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: pirate treasure hunts that confer maritime significance on a lost living-room remote*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: CAPTAIN VELA, an orangutan; MATE PIP, a capuchin; INNKEEPER NARA, a macaque; CABIN BOY RIK, a gibbon.

[An inn lounge. A map on the coffee table shows a rectangle, a crooked line and a large X. Vela wears a tricorn hat. Pip holds a wooden spoon like a cutlass. Nara points at the television, which shows a silent weather forecast.]

VELA: The treasure lies beyond the crooked river.

NARA: The cable runs along the wall. Do not pull it.

PIP: There is a great rectangular island.

NARA: The sofa.

RIK: I drew the map after I dropped the remote behind it.

ACT ONE

[Vela orders Pip to approach from the west. Pip walks around the sofa's right side, then hesitates. There are no compass marks on the carpet.]

PIP: Which west?

RIK: The side near the lamp. I drew the lamp here.

[Vela studies the small circle in the map's corner. He lowers his voice.]

VELA: A watchtower.

NARA: A reading light, which stays on its table.

[Pip kneels beside the sofa and peers under it. He finds a slipper and brings it out on the spoon.]

PIP: The first relic.

NARA: My slipper. Thank you.

[She puts it on, balancing against the chair rather than the lamp. Rik fetches a small flashlight from the reception drawer and lights the gap behind the sofa.]

RIK: I can see the remote near the back leg.

VELA: At last, the chest.

NARA: It is plastic and has no chest inside it.

[Vela begins a speech about the perils of the expedition. Pip remains on his knees, waiting with the spoon. Rik's flashlight rests firmly in both hands.]

PIP: Captain, the treasure is getting farther away.

VELA: It is not moving.

PIP: My arm is getting shorter.

[Nara asks Vela to move the coffee table slightly forward so Pip can lie comfortably beside the sofa. Vela moves the table, keeping the map and cups flat on top. Pip reaches with the spoon and nudges the remote toward the open side. Rik follows it with the beam.]

RIK: A little more toward me.

[Pip slides it within reach. Nara picks it up and checks its buttons. She turns up the weather forecast just enough to hear the announcer.]

ACT TWO

VELA: Divide the spoils.

NARA: We can all watch the forecast.

PIP: I would rather watch the boat race.

RIK: It starts after the weather.

[Nara puts the remote in the bowl on the side table, where it belongs. Vela asks whether the bowl has a lid.]

NARA: We do not bury it again.

[Vela folds the map carefully. Rik writes FOUND across the X, and Pip returns the wooden spoon to the inn's utensil pot after Nara wipes it. They sit together on the sofa.]

VELA: A fine campaign. The crew should have a monument.

[Nara puts her recovered slipper on the coffee table for a moment while adjusting its loose strap. Vela looks at it with new suspicion.]

PIP: That belongs to the innkeeper. We found it earlier.

VELA: I shall remember its provenance.

FINAL IMAGE: Vela keeps the crossed-out treasure map on his knee while the remote rests openly in its bowl.
