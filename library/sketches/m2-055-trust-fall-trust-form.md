# Trust Fall, Trust Form

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate team-building retreats and leadership workshops*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: COACH VEL, a macaque facilitator; NIM, a cautious langur employee; BRICK, a baboon manager; and a CAMERA OPERATOR.

[A retreat lawn. A padded mat lies beside a broad tree. A banner reads TRUST IS A VERB.]

COACH VEL: Today's exercise is the trust fall. Brick, you will stand behind Nim and catch them.

NIM: Has Brick agreed?

BRICK: I read the form.

COACH VEL: Wonderful. Stand with your back to your teammate.

[Nim turns. Brick takes a step away to check his phone.]

COACH VEL: Brick, you need to face Nim.

BRICK: I am facing the group chat.

NIM: I don't want to fall toward a phone.

[The camera operator moves closer.]

COACH VEL: This is a safe space. We are filming the trust moment.

NIM: That makes it less safe.

COACH VEL: We'll do a smaller fall.

[Nim leans one inch. Brick catches them by the shoulder.]

BRICK: I caught you.

NIM: I was standing.

COACH VEL: Progress. Now let's add the blindfold.

[Nim reads the blindfold label: NOT A BLINDFOLD, A VISUAL RESET.]

NIM: Why is this called a visual reset?

COACH VEL: It's more positive.

BRICK: Can I opt out?

COACH VEL: The form says participation is encouraged.

BRICK: Does it say required?

[Coach scans the page.]

COACH VEL: The footnote says no.

NIM: Then the team-building exercise has built a team capable of reading footnotes.

[Brick removes the mat from the grass and discovers a hole underneath.]

BRICK: That would have been a bad landing.

COACH VEL: We should pause and inspect the ground.

CAMERA OPERATOR: We are losing the light.

NIM: The light is not part of the exercise.

[They move the mat under the tree. Nim ties a rope between two low branches to mark the edge of the hole.]

COACH VEL: Now the group has solved a problem together.

BRICK: We did it without falling.

CAMERA OPERATOR: Can we stage the catch?

NIM: No.

COACH VEL: We will close with a trust circle.

[Everyone stands around the mat. The tree sheds a leaf onto the banner.]

BRICK: The tree trusts us to clean up.

NIM: It has not signed the form.

COACH VEL: The workshop guide says we should end with a shared commitment.

NIM: I commit to not being pushed backward into a hole.

BRICK: I commit to looking where I step.

COACH VEL: Those are specific.

CAMERA OPERATOR: Can we get a group photo around the mat?

NIM: The mat is beside the hole.

[The crew moves the camera to the other side. The banner slides down and covers the mat.]

BRICK: Is the banner our new safety equipment?

COACH VEL: It says trust.

NIM: It does not say load-bearing.

[They remove it before anyone leans on it.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: That is the shot.

COACH VEL: We will call the session a success.

NIM: Call it a meeting that ended on time.

[They roll up the banner and carry it away. The leaf stays.]

[BLACKOUT.]
