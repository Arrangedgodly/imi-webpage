# The Umbrella Repair Hotline

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: automated customer-service menus that route a simple repair through endless diagnostic branches*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Rella, a lemur caller; Goss, a gibbon repair clerk; Nima, a macaque technician; Pipet, a capuchin trainee.

INT. REPAIR SHOP - DAY

[GOSS WEARS A HEADSET. A BROKEN UMBRELLA LIES ON THE COUNTER. ITS HANDLE IS DETACHED.]

GOSS: Thank you for calling Umbrella Care. Please describe the weather.

RELLA: It is sunny.

GOSS: Please confirm your umbrella is wet.

RELLA: It is dry. The handle came off.

Goss presses a key. A recording plays through his headset and the counter speaker. Rella holds the shop's customer handset.

AUTOMATED VOICE: To report a dry umbrella, press one. To report a wet umbrella, press two.

Rella presses one. The voice asks whether the canopy is open. She says it is closed. Goss presses another key.

AUTOMATED VOICE: To close an open canopy, press three.

RELLA: I didn't say it was open.

GOSS: The system is trying to help.

Nima enters with a screwdriver. Pipet follows, carrying a mug.

NIMA: Which part is broken?

RELLA: The handle.

Nima screws it onto the shaft. It catches halfway, then slips off.

NIMA: The threads are stripped.

Goss's headset chirps.

AUTOMATED VOICE: To report stripped threads, press four.

Goss presses four. A new prompt asks whether the umbrella has a canopy.

RELLA: It has a canopy.

PIPET: Is it attached?

Rella opens the umbrella. One corner is caught in the shaft. The canopy balloons open inside the shop and lifts the mug from Pipet's hand.

PIPET: My tea is under warranty.

Nima pulls the release. The umbrella collapses, taking the headset wire with it. Goss is dragged halfway across the counter.

GOSS: The diagnostic tree is still running.

Rella pulls the wire free. The headset records a loud click and routes them to "tree damage." Goss tries to hang up. Every button gives the same menu.

NIMA: Just cut the wire.

GOSS: I can't. It is the only way to reach a person.

PIPET: You're a person.

GOSS: Not to the system.

Nima takes a pair of scissors and cuts the tangled headset wire. Goss's earpiece goes quiet. While Goss rubs his ear, Nima fits a fresh threaded collar over the umbrella shaft and screws the handle onto it. Rella picks up the umbrella. The handle stays on this time.

RELLA: Is it fixed?

NIMA: For this weather.

Rella looks outside. A cloud passes over the sun. She opens the umbrella, and the canopy holds. Pipet tests the handle. It stays attached.

PIPET: The repair worked.

Goss removes the headset. The customer handset rings on the counter.

GOSS: The hotline has called us back.

Nima lifts the customer handset. A recorded voice asks them to rate the service.

NIMA: Five.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Please explain your rating.

NIMA: The umbrella is fixed.

AUTOMATED VOICE: To confirm the umbrella is wet, press two.

Nima tapes a handwritten repair note over the speaker, but the recording continues beneath it.

[Rella opens the door and walks out under the dry umbrella. The system asks questions to the empty customer stool. Goss hangs a CLOSED sign on the stool; the voice asks whether the sign is wet.]
