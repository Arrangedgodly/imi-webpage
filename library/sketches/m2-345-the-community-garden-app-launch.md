# The Community Garden App Launch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: technology launches that promise to replace simple in-person coordination*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: founder Tavi, a macaque; gardener Nara, a gibbon; investor Odo, a baboon; tester Piri, a capuchin.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - DAY

Tavi unveils an app called SproutSync.

TAVI: It helps neighbors coordinate watering.

NARA: We already use a chalkboard.

TAVI: The app has notifications.

Odo asks if it can water the plants. Tavi says it can remind people to water. Piri downloads it and receives a prompt: "Confirm that you want to confirm your garden."

PIRI: I want to water the tomatoes.

The app asks her to select a garden. There is only one. She selects it. It asks her to choose a plant. Piri picks tomatoes. It asks which tomato bed. Nara says there is one bed. Tavi says the app supports future growth.

Odo tries to assign Nara a watering task. The app sends Nara a notification. She taps it; the app asks her to sign in. She has no account. She creates one, but the confirmation email goes to spam.

Nara takes the watering can and waters the plants. Odo asks the app to record the task. It asks for a photo. Piri takes one. The app says it cannot detect water because the image is too bright.

Tavi says SproutSync uses machine learning. Nara says the plants are wet. Odo points to the chalkboard, where the schedule is written. Tavi says the board cannot send reminders. Piri says it does not need charging.

The app battery warning appears. Tavi plugs the phone into a solar battery. The charger cable reaches only the bench, so she sits there. Nara passes her the watering can. Tavi gets up and waters the plants herself.

PIRI: Did the app schedule that?

TAVI: No. I was already here.

Odo asks if the app can coordinate a harvest. Tavi says that is in the premium version. Nara says the tomatoes are ready now. Everyone picks them and puts them in a basket.

Piri asks Tavi to show the app's best feature. Tavi opens the calendar and taps "water tomatoes." It displays a reminder for next Tuesday, although the tomatoes are already wet. Nara says the schedule changes when it rains. Tavi tries to edit the event, but must confirm the garden, plant, bed, time, and account. She taps through each screen while Odo reads the chalkboard. The board says the next watering is only needed if the soil dries. Piri checks the soil with one finger. It is damp. Tavi cancels the reminder. The app asks her to confirm cancellation, then asks if she wants to rate the notification.

Nara puts the phone away.

TAG

Tavi edits the app's description.

TAVI: "For people who don't have a chalkboard."

NARA: There are three of them.
