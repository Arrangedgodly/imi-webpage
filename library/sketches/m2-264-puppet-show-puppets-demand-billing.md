# The Puppet Show's Puppets Demand Billing

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: show-business contract negotiations applied to a low-budget puppet production*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Producer Daro, a macaque; puppeteer Seli, a gibbon; puppet Captain Clack, a sock puppet; and stage manager Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - AFTERNOON

Seli checks a puppet stage. Daro holds a contract. Tavi watches the curtain.

DARO: The puppet show starts in ten minutes. We have one performer and two puppets.

SELI: Captain Clack has a speaking role.

DARO: The contract lists the puppeteer.

[Captain Clack pops above the stage and points at the contract.]

CLACK: I request a separate dressing area.

TAVI: The dressing area is the sock drawer.

CLACK: That is not a dressing area. That is a storage location.

DARO: We are not paying a sock.

CLACK: I am made of cotton. I know what a percentage is.

INT. BACKSTAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Daro offers a revised contract. Clack rejects it with a tiny paper hand. Seli moves the puppet stage closer.

DARO: You get one extra bow and a clean drawer.

CLACK: What about transportation?

TAVI: You are already on Seli's hand.

CLACK: That is a ride, not transportation credit.

[Captain Clack exits the stage by falling into a laundry basket. Tavi lifts the basket.]

TAVI: Your car is ready.

CLACK: I cannot drive a basket.

DARO: We will call it a company vehicle.

INT. BACKSTAGE - BEFORE SHOWTIME

Daro adds a line: "Puppets may not negotiate during the performance." Clack reads it from the stage.

CLACK: I request an exception for applause.

DARO: Applause is not billable.

CLACK: Then I will hold the ending until the audience claps.

SELI: The audience has not arrived yet.

CLACK: I am protecting their future rights.

INT. THEATER - SHOWTIME

The audience settles. Seli begins the story. Captain Clack refuses to appear.

SELI: The captain has arrived at the harbor.

[The stage stays empty.]

DARO: Clack, your cue.

CLACK (OFFSTAGE): I am waiting for my call time.

TAVI: Your call time was ten minutes ago.

CLACK: That was the puppeteer's call time.

[Daro reads the contract. The final clause says all cast members appear when called. He points to the line.]

DARO: It says all cast.

CLACK: I have not signed.

SELI: You can sign with the pen in your mouth.

[Clack appears holding a pen between his felt fingers and signs a crooked X.]

INT. THEATER - FINAL SCENE

The show resumes. Clack delivers the ending, takes a bow, and exits into the laundry basket. Daro presents a small paper certificate.

DARO: One extra bow and a clean drawer.

CLACK: Also, I retain the rights to my own voice.

SELI: It is my voice.

CLACK: We have agreed to disagree.

[Tavi places Clack in the sock drawer. The drawer is labeled CAPTAIN'S DRESSING ROOM.]

CLACK: Finally, a room with my name on it.

[Daro closes the drawer. It immediately opens again because the puppet's nose is caught in the track.]

TAVI: Your dressing room has a bad door.

CLACK: Add it to the contract.
