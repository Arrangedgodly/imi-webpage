# The Coupon Is the Prize

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grocery-store coupon contests, shopping challenge countdowns, and hosts who score savings without checking what anyone needs*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Omi, a gibbon; shopper Vex, a macaque; shopper Tali, a capuchin; judge Nari, a ring-tailed lemur; clerk Palla, an orangutan.

INT. GROCERY STORE - DAY

[Omi stands between two carts, holding an enormous coupon. A board reads HIGHEST VERIFIED SAVINGS WINS. Palla waits at the register.]

OMI: Five minutes. Fill your cart with savings!

TALI: I need oats and soap.

VEX: I need to win.

[Nari starts the timer. Vex races toward a cardboard sign marked HALF PRICE. Tali walks to the oats.]

PALLA: The soap coupon expired yesterday.

TALI: Then I will pay for the soap. The oats still save two nuts.

[Vex returns with six lampshades stacked around his head.]

VEX: Twelve nuts saved on each. Seventy-two total!

NARI: Where are the lamps?

VEX: I can win those later.

[His tail catches a cereal coupon on Omi's clipboard. It sticks to the nearest lampshade. Palla scans it. The register beeps.]

PALLA: One nut off cereal.

VEX: Add that to seventy-two.

PALLA: It does not apply to a lampshade.

OMI: An innovative combination purchase.

[Palla looks for a barcode. She lifts the shade from Vex's head, revealing a label: DISPLAY PROP.]

PALLA: These aren't stock. They belong to the promotional display.

[Vex points toward the HALF PRICE sign. Its bottom half remains behind a cereal box. Nari moves the box. The sign now reads HALF PRICE CEREAL.]

VEX: The discount had a hidden condition.

TALI: The condition was cereal.

[Omi studies the cartful of shades, then the giant coupon in his hands.]

OMI: We have impressive height. Could height count toward the score?

NARI: The board says verified savings. Vex has zero so far.

[Vex grabs a cereal box and pushes it beneath the coupon. Palla scans both.]

PALLA: One nut saved.

VEX: I have entered the contest.

[He takes a second box. Palla points to the coupon's ONE PER CUSTOMER line.]

VEX: I could become a second customer.

TALI: You still owe for the first box.

[The timer rings. Vex puts down the second box. Palla prints their receipts. Nari compares them.]

NARI: Tali, two nuts saved. Vex, one. Tali wins.

[Omi offers Tali the giant coupon. It says ONE FREE BAG, VALID NEXT WEEK.]

TALI: How do I carry my groceries today?

PALLA: Use the ordinary bag beside it. That is the actual prize. The giant coupon is another display prop.

[Tali packs her oats and soap. Vex lifts the nearest lampshade toward the abandoned giant coupon.]

VEX: Could I return all the props for a refund?

PALLA: You have not paid for them.

VEX: Then I would save the whole refund.

[Nari writes ZERO beside his name. Omi raises the cardboard coupon to cover the score.]

OMI: A moment for our sponsor.

[The coupon's cutout handles make it look like an enormous bag. Vex pours the lampshades through its open middle. They fall straight onto his shoes.]

PALLA: That bag also has a hidden condition.

TALI: No bottom.

[She leaves with her groceries while Vex tries to carry the display back intact.]
