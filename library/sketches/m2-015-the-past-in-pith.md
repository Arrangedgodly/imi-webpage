# The Past in Pith

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Historical reenactments and authoritative documentary narration*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Narrator Crumb, a baboon; reenactor Pella, a langur; reenactor Joss, a macaque; historian Aunt Dree, an orangutan; director Fenn, a squirrel monkey.

[STAGE: A HISTORY SET WITH CARDBOARD PALM TREES AND A VERY MODERN WATER BOTTLE.] 

CRUMB: In the year 142, the great troop faced a shortage of shade.

DREE: We have no record of a shade shortage.

FENN: It gives the story stakes.

DREE: The records say the troop moved the fruit baskets.

CRUMB: The baskets were sacred.

DREE: They were wicker.

PELLA: I am playing the chief.

JOSS: I am the basket carrier.

FENN: The chief must look troubled.

PELLA: I am troubled by the cardboard palm.

CRUMB: The chief gazes upon the valley.

PELLA: I cannot see the valley. The palm is in my face.

FENN: Move the palm.

JOSS: The palm is attached to the wall.

CRUMB: This was an age of great engineering.

DREE: That is a refrigerator hinge.

FENN: We borrowed it from the museum.

DREE: The museum opened last year.

CRUMB: The past was built with whatever stood nearby.

JOSS: I have found a water bottle.

FENN: That is a prop.

JOSS: It says "Best Intern."

CRUMB: Perhaps the chief honored an intern.

DREE: There were no interns in the records.

CRUMB: We cannot let facts ruin a good moment.

DREE: Facts are the reason we are here.

[A PAUSE. THE CARDBOARD PALM LEANS OVER.] 

PELLA: The chief will now make a speech.

JOSS: About the baskets?

PELLA: About the shade.

DREE: The chief asked the troop to move the baskets under a tree.

CRUMB: That's too plain.

DREE: It is what happened.

FENN: Can we make it sound grander?

PELLA: "Carry the fruit to the cool place."

JOSS: That sounds practical.

CRUMB: Add thunder.

[A THUNDER SHEET RATTLES. THE MODERN WATER BOTTLE ROLLS.] 

JOSS: The bottle is rolling toward the basket.

PELLA: The chief catches it.

DREE: There is no bottle in the archive.

FENN: There is now.

CRUMB: The chief protects the future.

DREE: The intern's water bottle is not history.

JOSS: It is evidence of rehearsal.

FENN: We should keep it in the behind-the-scenes segment.

CRUMB: The narrator will not mention the bottle.

PELLA: I will mention the shade.

JOSS: I will carry the baskets.

DREE: That is what the record supports.

CRUMB: The troop moved together, and the fruit stayed cool.

FENN: We have our ending.

CRUMB: In the next scene, the chief discovers the wheel.

DREE: The wheel was invented much later.

FENN: Then the chief discovers the round basket.

JOSS: It was round the whole time.

CRUMB: History is full of surprises.

DREE: The recording has also captured a modern water bottle.

FENN: We can call it a future artifact.

DREE: It is from today.

[THE WATER BOTTLE IS PUT IN A MODERN BACKPACK. LIGHTS FADE.]
