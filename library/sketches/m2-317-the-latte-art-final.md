# The Latte Art Final

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: coffee competitions, slow-motion drink reveals, and judges who score foam patterns before tasting the coffee*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Barista Sola, a capuchin; rival Rell, a macaque; judge Nari, a gibbon; and customer Odo, an orangutan.

INT. COFFEE SHOP - MORNING

Two baristas stand behind the counter. A bell marks the start of the latte-art final.

NARI: You have one minute to pour a design and explain its meaning.

SOLA: I will make a leaf.

RELL: I will make a swan.

ODO: I would like coffee.

NARI: The customer has requested a beverage.

Sola pours milk into espresso. Her tail curls around the pitcher handle for balance. The foam forms a leaf, then a stem. Rell tilts his cup, creating a swan with a long neck. The judges lean in.

NARI: The swan has great posture.

ODO: Is it ready?

SOLA: I have one more leaf.

ODO: I only need one coffee.

Rell presents his cup. A bubble pops, turning the swan's neck into a question mark.

RELL: The swan is asking about the origin of foam.

NARI: A challenging concept.

Odo tastes Sola's coffee.

ODO: This is good.

NARI: We have not scored taste yet.

ODO: It is coffee.

The bell rings. The baristas must explain their designs. Sola says her leaf represents a tree. Rell says his swan represents a lake. Nari asks why the swan is inside a cup.

RELL: It is visiting.

Sola's leaf begins to disappear as the foam settles. Rell's swan remains, but its neck slumps. Nari awards points for shape, story, and staying visible.

ODO: Can I have another cup without a bird?

Sola prepares a plain coffee. Rell tries to copy the shape on Odo's cup but spills a little milk on the counter. He wipes it with a cloth, which creates a foam puddle shaped like a duck.

NARI: The duck is spontaneous.

RELL: I did not enter it.

SOLA: It is a free-range duck.

Odo takes the plain coffee and leaves a tip. Nari declares a tie between the leaf and the swan. The judges taste both drinks and agree that each is coffee.

RELL: We have both won.

NARI: We have both made drinks.

Sola and Rell receive matching ribbons. Odo asks whether he can keep the cups. They say yes. He carries them to the table, where he arranges them into a line.

NARI: That is an installation.

ODO: That is where I sit.

The shop fills with customers. The baristas make ordinary coffee. One foam leaf survives long enough for a customer to see it. Rell bows as if the cup has won a prize.

Nari orders a plain cup. Sola serves it without a design. Nari tastes it and awards the drink the highest score of the day. Rell begins to draw a swan on the receipt, but Odo takes the receipt to pay.
