# The Taxidermist's Model Wants to Move

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: natural-history exhibits and documentary reconstructions that demand impossible stillness from a live pose model*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: TAXIDERMIST, a gibbon; MODEL, a macaque; MUSEUM CURATOR, an orangutan; PHOTOGRAPHER, a baboon; ASSISTANT, a capuchin.

[A museum workshop. A life-sized display of a monkey in a tree is being assembled. The model sits on a painted branch beside it. The photographer adjusts the light.]

CURATOR: We need a realistic photograph for the exhibit card.

MODEL: I can hold a pose for a while.

TAXIDERMIST: The specimen is supposed to look alert.

MODEL: So am I.

[The assistant brushes dust from the display. The model blinks. The photographer clicks the shutter.]

PHOTOGRAPHER: The live one moved.

ACT ONE

[The curator asks the model to match the display's pose: one arm raised, head turned, tail curled around the branch. The model tries. The branch wobbles.]

MODEL: Is the branch attached?

ASSISTANT: It is foam.

MODEL: It feels like foam.

[The taxidermist checks the exhibit's base and tightens a bolt. The model holds still again. A small fan blows the leaves. The display's artificial fur remains still while the model's ears flutter.]

PHOTOGRAPHER: The living one is too expressive.

CURATOR: We need viewers to see the difference between reconstruction and observation.

TAXIDERMIST: Then take both in the same frame.

[The photographer moves back. The curator stands beside the display and begins a serious explanation. The model slowly lowers the raised arm to scratch their nose.]

CURATOR: Keep the arm up.

MODEL: I have an itch.

TAXIDERMIST: It is a common behavior.

[The curator asks for a close-up. The camera lens points at the display's glass case, reflecting the photographer instead. The assistant draws the curtain behind them to remove glare. The model sneezes; several artificial leaves shake loose from the branch.]

PHOTOGRAPHER: The exhibit has moved.

TAXIDERMIST: It was the sneeze.

[The assistant retrieves the leaves and tapes them back. The curator decides the photo should include the model holding a label that says LIVE OBSERVATION. The model holds the sign. The taxidermist holds another sign: RECONSTRUCTION. The two signs overlap in the photograph.]

CURATOR: We have mislabeled the entire exhibit.

MODEL: I can switch sides.

[The model climbs down carefully and swaps places with the display. The photographer frames them side by side, one still and one yawning. The curator approves.]

ACT TWO

[They take the final photo. The display stays rigid. The model stretches after the shutter. The curator writes separate captions for the preserved specimen and the living pose model, then thanks the model.]

MODEL: Do I get to move now?

TAXIDERMIST: Yes. The exhibit is closed for lunch.

[The curator checks the photo card and labels the two images separately. The still specimen gets a date and location. The model gets a name and a note describing the stretch and yawn. The assistant removes the prop branch from the room before lunch.]

CURATOR: The exhibit has both evidence and movement.

MODEL: Put the movement in the caption.

TAXIDERMIST: I will stay still for that.

FINAL IMAGE: The living model leaves the frame while the glass display keeps holding its pose.
