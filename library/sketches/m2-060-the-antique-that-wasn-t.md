# The Antique That Wasn't

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised antiques appraisals and auction estimates*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: APPRAISER ADA, a macaque; OWNER MOSS, a sloth; HOST VERA, a gibbon; and a RUNNER, a capuchin.

[A studio table holds a crooked stick under a glass case. A camera light blinks.]

VERA: Welcome to What's It Worth? Moss brought an object found in his family tree.

MOSS: It has been in the branches as long as I can remember.

ADA: Is it a tool?

MOSS: It may be a royal staff.

VERA: We have a specialist in forest regalia.

ADA: I am the specialist. This is a stick.

MOSS: It has a groove.

ADA: A branch rubbed against the fork where it grew.

VERA: Could it be from a lost civilization?

ADA: It could be from yesterday.

[The runner places a velvet pad under the case. The stick rolls off and lands in Ada's hand.]

ADA: It is light, dry, and cracked. It is not safe to swing.

MOSS: My grandfather used it to reach fruit.

VERA: That sounds like a family story.

MOSS: He used it once. Then it broke.

ADA: The story is more valuable than the stick.

VERA: Our auction estimate is-

ADA: Don't estimate it. It is not an antique.

[The camera light flashes. A graphic appears: POSSIBLE ROYAL STAFF: 8,000 COINS.]

ADA: Who made that graphic?

RUNNER: The producer.

MOSS: Eight thousand?

ADA: No. The graphic is wrong.

VERA: We cannot take it off the screen without a correction segment.

ADA: Then do a correction segment.

[SFX: GRAPHIC BEEPS.]

VERA: We have a correction. The object is a stick with sentimental value and no auction price.

MOSS: Can I keep it?

ADA: Yes. Store it somewhere dry. Don't lean on it.

[The runner brings a small foam cradle.]

MOSS: That's a better place than the case.

VERA: What should viewers bring next week?

ADA: Bring an object you know the history of. We can discuss its age without inventing a kingdom.

MOSS: I have a fruit bowl from my aunt.

VERA: Any estimate?

ADA: It holds fruit.

MOSS: That is a good estimate.

[The camera zooms on the bowl. The graphics crew adds: POSSIBLY ROYAL.]

ADA: Take that off.

[The runner removes the graphic. A real twig remains on the table.]

[The next guest, a squirrel, arrives carrying a small carved cup.]

VERA: Could this be royal?

ADA: It is a cup.

SQUIRREL: My grandmother carved it for acorns.

VERA: The camera graphic says "possible ancient vessel."

ADA: Remove the graphic. We have a maker and a use. That's already more than we know about the stick.

MOSS: Could the stick support the cup?

ADA: It is cracked. Use a table.

[The squirrel sets the cup on the table. The runner brings a foam ring to keep it from rolling.]

VERA: Does the foam ring add value?

ADA: It adds stability.

MOSS: I have a second stick.

ADA: Keep it in the tree.

[The camera starts to zoom. The crew's graphic flashes "POSSIBLY SECOND ROYAL STAFF."]

ADA: We are ending the appraisal.

VERA: The show has a second object.

ADA: The show has a branch and a cup. The family story is enough.

[The graphic disappears. The squirrel takes the cup home.]

[BLACKOUT.]
