# The Crossing Button Is a Doorbell

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: pedestrian signals that add confusing buttons and instructions to an ordinary street crossing*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Pedestrian Runi, a macaque; traffic engineer Odo, a baboon; crossing guard Pava, a gibbon; cyclist Tavi, a capuchin; and shopkeeper Jori, a lemur.

EXT. MARKET CROSSING - DAY

Runi presses a large button on the signal pole. A sign reads "WAIT FOR WALK." A small bell rings inside a nearby shop.

RUNI: Did I press the crossing button?

ODO: Yes.

PAVA: The shop doorbell rang too.

TAVI: It is wired to both.

[THE SHOPKEEPER OPENS THE DOOR, EXPECTING A CUSTOMER.]

JORI: Who is it?

RUNI: I am waiting to cross.

EXT. MARKET CROSSING - MOMENTS LATER

Odo opens the signal cabinet. Pava holds traffic. Tavi points at a second button lower on the pole.

TAVI: That one is labeled for the crossing.

RUNI: It is behind the bench.

ODO: The larger button opens the shop door.

PAVA: The sign above it says "WAIT FOR WALK."

ODO: The sign belongs to the signal.

[RUNI PRESSES THE SMALL BUTTON. A TONE SOUNDS, AND THE WALK SYMBOL APPEARS.]

TAVI: The signal heard you.

RUNI: The shop did not.

EXT. MARKET CROSSING - LATER

Odo adds a small arrow to the sign. Pava demonstrates the button. Tavi waits with his bicycle.

ODO: Press the small button for the crossing. The larger button is the shop bell.

RUNI: We should label them clearly.

PAVA: People can read the sign while waiting.

[THE WALK SIGNAL TURNS ON. EVERYONE CROSSES. THE SHOP DOORBELL RINGS AGAIN AS THE DOOR SWINGS OPEN IN THE BREEZE.]

JORI: Another customer?

TAVI: Just the wind.

EXT. MARKET CROSSING - AFTERNOON

Odo installs a new plate. It reads "CROSSING BUTTON BELOW. SHOP BELL ABOVE." Runi tests it once.

[THE WALK SYMBOL APPEARS. THE SHOP STAYS CLOSED.]

RUNI: That works.

PAVA: No bell.

[THE SHOPKEEPER PRESSES THE CROSSING BUTTON FROM INSIDE. THE WALK SYMBOL APPEARS; NO ONE IS THERE.]

ODO: We may need a cover for the inside.

INT. SIGNAL CABINET - MOMENTS LATER

Odo opens the control box. Two wires share one loose terminal. Jori watches from the shop doorway, holding the small bell.

JORI: This rings every time someone wants to cross?

ODO: The wires were joined when the shop moved its door.

RUNI: So the sign sent walkers to your door.

JORI: I have been greeting the traffic light.

[PAVA HOLDS A CARDBOARD COVER OVER THE SHOP BUTTON WHILE ODO SEPARATES THE WIRES.]

TAVI: That button is still easy to hit by mistake.

PAVA: Then the cover should make it harder without hiding the actual crossing control.

ODO: I can add a raised rim and move the shop bell to its own switch.

JORI: Put the bell on my door. That is where customers are.

[ODO FINISHES THE WIRING. RUNI PRESSES THE SMALL BUTTON. THE WALK SYMBOL APPEARS. JORI PRESSES THE SHOP SWITCH. THE BELL RINGS ONCE.]

RUNI: We have two separate signals.

JORI: One for crossing, one for shopping.

[THE WALK LIGHT CHANGES. EVERYONE CROSSES. JORI STANDS READY AT HIS DOOR; THE BELL REMAINS SILENT.]

TAVI: No accidental customer.

JORI: I will wait for the ones who mean it.
