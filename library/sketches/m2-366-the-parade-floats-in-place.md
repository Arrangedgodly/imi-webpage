# The Parade Floats in Place

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: civic parades where every committee adds a stopping point and the procession never reaches the finish*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Juno, a macaque parade marshal; Nel, a gibbon float builder; Tavi, a capuchin drummer; Bo, a lemur mayor.

EXT. MAIN STREET - MORNING

[A BANNER READS WELCOME TO THE ANNUAL PARADE. THE ROUTE MAP SHOWS A START LINE, A FINISH LINE, AND SIX COMMITTEE STOPS. JUNO HOLDS A WHISTLE.]

JUNO: The parade moves continuously from here to the park.

BO: We have a ribbon-cutting at the bakery.

JUNO: That's stop one.

NEL: The float can't stop. It has a wheel that only turns downhill.

TAVI: The drum corps can stop. We have six different ending beats.

JUNO: Why six?

TAVI: The committees each asked for a finale.

Bo cuts the starting ribbon. The parade rolls a few yards, then halts at a rope. A sign says OFFICIAL WAVE TO THE LIBRARY.

JUNO: We haven't reached the library.

BO: The library committee wanted a wave while we were still in view.

Nel waves from the float. The rope drops. The group moves again, reaching a painted line labeled BAKERY RIBBON. There is no bakery.

BO: The bakery is three blocks away.

JUNO: Then why is the line here?

BO: The ribbon committee wanted an easy photo.

Tavi plays an ending beat. Everyone assumes the parade is over and begins to leave.

JUNO: That's stop two, not the end.

TAVI: It was the bakery finale.

NEL: The wheel is rolling. We should keep moving.

The float rolls forward, pulling the banner with it. Juno runs beside the wheel. The next marker reads FLOAT PHOTO WITH BANNER. Bo asks them to hold still.

NEL: The banner is moving.

BO: It says "hold still."

JUNO: The sign is tied to the float.

Tavi plays the second ending beat. The drummer at the back takes it as a cue and starts stacking the drums. The front drummer continues walking, dragging the rest of the line.

TAVI: We have two endings and no finish.

JUNO: We are at the route's midpoint.

Nel leans over the float, points to the map, and spots the finish line printed under a sticker. She peels the sticker off. It reads PARADE FINISH: PARK GATE.

BO: The park gate is closed for maintenance.

JUNO: Can we end at the gate?

BO: We can end in front of it.

The float reaches the gate. The wheel turns downhill and carries the whole parade through the open side entrance. Bo cuts the finish ribbon as the floats pass.

BO: The parade has arrived at the park.

JUNO: It has. Please don't add a photo stop.

Bo looks at a camera crew setting up. He raises his hand to wave. Juno points at the continuous-movement notice. Bo keeps the hand raised while he walks.

TAVI: Is this a finale?

JUNO: It's a wave.

TAVI: I have a beat for that.

Juno blows the whistle. Tavi taps a short rhythm that keeps pace with the rolling float. The banner flaps; the parade continues into the park without stopping.

[BO WAVES WHILE WALKING. THE SIGN ON THE FLOAT TURNS AROUND TO READ: THANK YOU FOR ATTENDING. ITS BACK SAYS: PLEASE KEEP MOVING.]
