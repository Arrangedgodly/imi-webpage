# The Aquarium Gift Shop Sells the Ocean

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: aquarium gift shops that attach a souvenir pitch to every living creature visitors admire*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Sella, a macaque aquarium guide; Tuck, a capuchin shop clerk; Maru, a gibbon visitor; and Ebb, a sea lion who can hear the intercom.

INT. AQUARIUM TUNNEL - DAY

Maru stands beneath a tank. A ray glides overhead. Sella points up. Tuck rolls in a cart of plush rays.

SELLA: This ray swims by using the current. It has no need to hurry.

TUCK: You can take one home for twelve shells.

MARU: The real ray has a whole ocean.

TUCK: The plush one has a removable tag.

SELLA: Please let people watch the animal before you sell them its face.

TUCK: I am supporting conservation.

SELLA: You put a barcode over the conservation sign.

[TUCK PEELS THE BARCODE OFF. IT STICKS TO HIS THUMB.]

TUCK: This one is now a limited edition.

INT. TOUCH POOL - LATER

Sella leads Maru past the pool. Tuck has arranged starfish-shaped keychains beside the handwashing sink.

SELLA: The animals are resting. We observe without touching.

TUCK: For hands that need to touch, we have these soft toys.

MARU: That one is shaped like a sea cucumber.

TUCK: It is our most popular pencil case.

[THE INTERCOM CRACKLES. EBB'S VOICE COMES FROM THE SEA LION HABITAT.]

EBB: Who keeps leaving little plastic things near the sink?

TUCK: The shop has a new customer-feedback line.

SELLA: That is Ebb. He can hear the public-address speaker.

EBB: I can hear you too. The pencil case is not food.

TUCK: It is not advertised as food.

EBB: Someone asked if it came with snacks.

MARU: Did you answer?

EBB: I waved at them until they understood.

INT. SHOP COUNTER - AFTERNOON

Tuck places a model submarine, a mug, and a snow globe on the counter. Sella directs Maru toward the exit.

TUCK: Final stop. For the complete ocean experience, choose the globe. It contains a tiny ray in glitter.

SELLA: The exhibit is right behind you.

TUCK: The exhibit does not fit in a bag.

[EBB ENTERS THROUGH THE STAFF GATE WITH A WET TOWEL. HE LIFTS THE SNOW GLOBE, SHAKES IT, AND WATCHES THE GLITTER SWIRL.] 

EBB: This is not a ray. It is a storm in a jar.

TUCK: It is an artistic interpretation.

EBB: The ray swims in a real current. This one is trapped in a paper forecast.

[EBB TURNS THE GLOBE SO ITS PRICE STICKER FACES THE WALL.]

SELLA: The animals are still here. The souvenirs are optional.

MARU: I'll take a postcard of the actual ray.

TUCK: We have one.

[HE REACHES FOR THE POSTCARDS. EBB WATCHES HIM CLOSELY.]

EBB: No barcode on the ray's eye.

TUCK: Fine. The barcode goes on the back.

MARU: Can I take a photo of the real ray?

SELLA: Yes. No flash.

[MARU LIFTS A CAMERA. THE RAY GLIDES OUT OF FRAME.]

TUCK: That is the rare disappearing-ray postcard moment.

EBB: It disappeared because it is swimming.

TUCK: The postcard is still twelve shells.

SELLA: The ray is still free to look at.
