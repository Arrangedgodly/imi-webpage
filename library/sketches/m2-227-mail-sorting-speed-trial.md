# The Mail Sorting Speed Trial

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace speed trials that turn routine sorting into a contest with rules that contradict the actual job*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Supervisor Kiri, a macaque; Sorter Ralo, a gibbon; Auditor Dema, a capuchin; and Courier Tavi, a baboon.

INT. POST ROOM - DAY

Three baskets sit on a table: NORTH, SOUTH, and RETURN. Kiri starts a stopwatch. Ralo wears gloves. Dema holds a clipboard.

KIRI: Sort these letters as fast as possible.

RALO: By address?

DEMA: By destination, with no errors.

KIRI: And keep your hands inside the marked area.

TAVI: The baskets are outside the marked area.

KIRI: The baskets were moved for the camera.

RALO: Then I cannot put letters in them.

[THE TIMER STARTS. RALO SORTS QUICKLY INTO THREE NEAT PILES ON THE TABLE.]

DEMA: The letters are not in baskets.

RALO: The basket is six inches away.

KIRI: The contest measures hand speed, not distance.

DEMA: The timer shows twelve seconds.

TAVI: The route van leaves in ten.

KIRI: The trial only lasts one minute.

TAVI: The letters still have to make the trip.

[TAVI ROLLS THE VAN OUT. KIRI HOLDS UP A HAND TO STOP HIM UNTIL THE ROUND ENDS.]

RALO: I can load the baskets while the clock runs.

KIRI: The stopwatch only stops when the last letter is sorted.

TAVI: I am not allowed to deliver a stopwatch.

DEMA: The rule says the sort is complete at the table.

RALO: The route begins at the door.

KIRI: The door is outside the trial.

INT. POST ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Dema adds a fourth basket labeled "UNSURE." Tavi rolls in a mail sack.

TAVI: The north and south routes share one road today.

KIRI: Keep sorting by destination.

RALO: This envelope has no town name.

DEMA: Put it in UNSURE.

RALO: It has a return address.

TAVI: That address is smudged.

[THEY TURN THE ENVELOPE OVER. A CLEAR ADDRESS IS STAMPED ON THE BACK.]

KIRI: That counts as a successful find.

DEMA: The clock is running.

RALO: The clock is not delivering these.

TAVI: I can take the sorted bags when they are ready.

KIRI: The finish line is the table edge.

TAVI: I need the bags at the van.

INT. POST ROOM - FINAL ROUND

The timer begins. Ralo sorts the last letters by route and drops each stack into the correct basket. Tavi picks up the baskets and carries them toward the exit.

KIRI: Stop the clock. The table is clear.

DEMA: Two letters remain.

[RALO POINTS TO TWO LETTERS ON THE FLOOR. THEY SLIPPED OFF THE TABLE.]

RALO: I will pick them up.

KIRI: That ends the trial.

TAVI: It completes the delivery.

[THEY SORT THE LETTERS AND HAND THEM TO TAVI. KIRI LOOKS AT THE SCOREBOARD: RALO IS LAST BY TIME, FIRST BY ACCURACY.]

DEMA: The winner is the route.

KIRI: The route was not entered.
