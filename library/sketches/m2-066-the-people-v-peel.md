# The People v. Peel

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: courtroom television and sensationalized civil trials*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: JUDGE ADA, a macaque; PROSECUTOR TOLL, a gibbon; DEFENSE LAWYER MOSS, a capuchin; and the DEFENDANT, a banana peel.

[A courtroom. The peel sits on a tiny evidence stand. A jury of squirrels watches.]

ADA: The court will hear the case of The People versus one banana peel.

TOLL: The defendant is accused of causing a slip near the market.

MOSS: The defendant has no legs and cannot move.

TOLL: It was found in the path.

ADA: Where?

TOLL: Directly beside a fruit stall.

MOSS: It was beside a fruit stall because that is where bananas are sold.

[The prosecutor displays a large photograph of a paw print beside the peel.]

TOLL: A witness approached the scene.

MOSS: That is the defendant's own footprint.

ADA: The defendant is a peel.

TOLL: Then whose footprint is it?

MOSS: The market keeper's. She removed the peel from the path.

TOLL: Objection. That is not dramatic.

ADA: Sustained. The objection is too dramatic.

[The jury leans forward.]

TOLL: We will call the witness.

[The market keeper enters carrying a broom.]

KEEPER: I swept the path. Nobody slipped.

TOLL: But the peel was there.

KEEPER: For two minutes. I picked it up and put it in the compost.

[The peel is gone from the stand. Moss holds an empty evidence tray.]

ADA: Where is the defendant?

TOLL: The jury has eaten the evidence.

[The jury members look at each other. One squirrel wipes a crumb from its whiskers.]

MOSS: The defendant may have been disposed of properly.

TOLL: I demand a mistrial.

ADA: On what grounds?

TOLL: The jury had snacks.

ADA: The snacks were not evidence.

MOSS: Can we dismiss the case?

ADA: We can. The market keeper has confirmed the path was clear, and the peel is in compost.

TOLL: But the trial was scheduled for an hour.

ADA: Then adjourn early.

[The gavel falls. The jury rises and walks to the compost bin.]

TOLL: The court will be back after a brief recess.

ADA: There is no case after recess.

[The gavel becomes a compost lid.]


TOLL: The jury has eaten the defendant.

ADA: The defendant was already in compost.

MOSS: Can we call the compost manager?

[The camera follows a squirrel carrying the empty evidence tray toward the bin.]

TOLL: The public deserves an explanation.

ADA: The public heard one. A keeper found the peel and moved it.

TOLL: But the courtroom graphic says "slip case."

MOSS: The only slip is the graphic.

[The jury returns. One squirrel holds a crumb.]

ADA: Have you reached a verdict?

JURY, off: We find the peel not guilty of being a snack.

TOLL: That is not a legal verdict.

ADA: It is the final ruling. The keeper will place a compost bin by the stall. The court will place a sign by the bin.

[The sign reads: PLEASE DROP PEELS HERE. The camera zooms in as if it were a confession.]

MOSS: Case closed.

TOLL: Until the next peel.

ADA: The court will not try produce again.

[BLACKOUT.]
