# The Survival Host Packs a Serving Tray

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wilderness survival shows, sponsored gear demonstrations, and hosts who try to make every inconvenience look like an expedition*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: HOST, a baboon; CAMERAPERSON, a macaque; GUIDE, an orangutan; SPONSOR, a gibbon; PARK RANGER, a capuchin.

[A marked picnic shelter in a city park. The host kneels beside a backpack and speaks to camera. A paved path and a water fountain are visible nearby.]

HOST: We have reached the wilderness, several yards from the parking lot.

GUIDE: It is a public park.

HOST: There are no visible snack shops.

[The cameraperon pans to a vending machine beyond the trees. The host blocks it with a branch.]

SPONSOR: This segment is brought to you by our emergency serving tray.

[The host pulls a silver tray from the backpack. It is wrapped in tissue paper.]

HOST: It can protect us from the elements.

GUIDE: It is aluminum. It can hold sandwiches.

[The host turns the tray over and uses it as a mirror. A squirrel appears in the reflection and startles the cameraperon.]

HOST: We have detected wildlife.

PARK RANGER: That is a squirrel.

ACT ONE

[The host arranges three napkins on the tray and sets it on the grass. A breeze lifts one napkin. The host pins it down with a compass.]

HOST: The wind is trying to steal our map.

GUIDE: That is a napkin. The map is in the pocket.

[The host checks the backpack and finds a folded menu instead of the map.]

HOST: The menu shows the route to lunch.

SPONSOR: Our tray is a navigation aid.

GUIDE: The shelter is behind us.

[The host turns around. The marked shelter is clearly visible. The park ranger points to a trail sign.]

PARK RANGER: The exit is that way. Stay on the path.

[The cameraperon asks the host to walk toward the sign. The host steps off the path and sinks ankle-deep into soft mulch.]

HOST: The terrain has changed.

GUIDE: That is the flower bed.

[The guide offers a hand. The host steps back onto the path, leaving a neat boot print in the mulch. The ranger places a small marker beside the bed.]

PARK RANGER: Please stay on the path for the rest of the shoot.

[The sponsor asks for a product close-up. The host puts the serving tray over the backpack like a shield. A leaf falls onto it and makes a loud metallic ping.]

HOST: A warning signal.

GUIDE: A leaf hit the tray.

ACT TWO

[The host picks up the napkins, removes the compass from the tray, and uses it to carry lunch to the picnic table. The ranger points to a recycling bin for the tissue paper.]

HOST: We have secured the food supply.

GUIDE: It was a sandwich.

SPONSOR: The tray performed well.

[The host sits at the table. The squirrel leaps onto the bench and takes a crumb.]

HOST: The wildlife has breached camp.

PARK RANGER: It has taken a crumb, not the sandwich.

[The host slides the tray under the sandwich and carries both to the other end of the table.]

FINAL IMAGE: The host eats lunch from the emergency serving tray while the squirrel watches from the marked path.
