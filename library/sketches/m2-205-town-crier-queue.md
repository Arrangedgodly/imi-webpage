# The Town Crier's Queue

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: ceremonial public announcements that require a queue, a seal, and a proclamation for ordinary civic updates*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Town Crier Aro, a macaque; Clerk Nemi, a capuchin; Sefu, a gibbon resident; and Brack, a baboon bell keeper.

EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - MORNING

Aro stands on a wooden box with a scroll. Four residents wait in a straight line. Brack holds a handbell. Nemi carries a small notice that reads: "FOOTBRIDGE OPEN AGAIN."

ARO: Hear ye, hear ye. The footbridge is open again.

NEMI: That is the whole notice.

ARO: We need the ceremonial seal.

NEMI: The seal is in the archive.

BRACK: The archive opens after the proclamation.

SEFU: I need to cross the bridge before the market closes.

ARO: You must wait for the announcement to be entered in the public record.

SEFU: It says the bridge is open.

NEMI: It says it on this paper.

ARO: A paper is not a proclamation until read from the box.

[ARO UNROLLS THE SCROLL. A LONGER DOCUMENT UNFURLS AND DROPS OFF THE BACK OF THE BOX.]

NEMI: That is the old proclamation about repairing the bridge.

ARO: It mentions today's opening.

SEFU: On page seven.

INT. VILLAGE ARCHIVE - LATER

Nemi unlocks a cabinet. Aro holds the scroll flat. Brack rings the bell to mark each page.

BRACK: Page one.

ARO: The bridge was inspected.

BRACK: Page two.

ARO: The inspector crossed the bridge.

BRACK: Page three.

ARO: The inspector returned.

SEFU: The bridge is open. Could I cross now?

NEMI: We need the seal to authenticate the scroll.

[THE SEAL IS STUCK TO THE CABINET DOOR. NEMI PEELS IT OFF. THE IMPRESSION READS "ARCHIVE COPY."]

ARO: It has authority.

NEMI: It has adhesive on the back.

BRACK: The bell is stuck to it too.

[BRACK TRIES TO LIFT THE BELL. THE SEAL PEELS AWAY FROM THE CABINET WITH A LOUD RIP.]

ARO: That is the sound of official business.

NEMI: That is the sound of the archive invoice.

SEFU: Is there a bridge on the other side of the building?

NEMI: There is the same bridge. It is still open.

EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - AFTERNOON

The queue is now one resident longer. Sefu waits with a basket of vegetables. Aro lifts the sealed scroll.

ARO: Hear ye. The footbridge has been open since this morning.

SEFU: And now the market is closed.

NEMI: We can issue a second notice that the market is closed.

BRACK: Should I ring first?

[SEFU WALKS OFF WITH THE BASKET. ARO STEPS DOWN FROM THE BOX, STILL READING.]

ARO: Hear ye. I am going to the market.

NEMI: The market is closed.

ARO: Then this announcement will be timely tomorrow.

[BRACK RINGS THE BELL. EVERYONE IN LINE LEAVES EXCEPT HIM.]

BRACK: Is the bridge open?

NEMI: Yes.

[BRACK CROSSES IT. THE BELL FADES ON THE OTHER SIDE.]
