# The One-Page Strategic Plan

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: management retreats that expand a straightforward task into overlapping mission statements*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Rell, a gibbon manager; Miri, a capuchin facilitator; Odo, a macaque note-taker; and Pava, a lemur employee.

INT. RETREAT ROOM - MORNING

Miri hands everyone a sheet with one blank box.

MIRI: Write one practical goal for the grove.

RELL: We should water the young trees.

Odo writes: "Water trees." Rell looks at the paper and says it lacks vision. Pava says the trees need water today. Miri asks them to make the goal measurable. Odo writes: "Water five trees by noon." Rell adds that the goal should inspire.

RELL: We will nourish the future.

PAVA: With water?

The group divides into subcommittees: vision, measurement and watering. Pava tries to leave for the orchard. Rell asks her to remain for alignment. Miri draws three circles on the board. They overlap over the word "water."

ODO: The circles agree.

RELL: We need a shared objective.

PAVA: The trees need a bucket.

Miri brings in a watering can. Odo asks who owns the can. Rell names a can steward. Pava fills it and walks toward the garden. Everyone follows, carrying the strategic plan.

At the first tree, Pava pours water. Rell asks whether the watering should be tracked. Odo makes a tally mark. Miri asks if the plan should include soil moisture. They inspect the soil and find it dry. Pava waters the next tree while they discuss.

The group reaches the fifth tree before noon. Odo completes the tally. Rell says they have delivered on the goal. Pava sees a sixth dry sapling. She starts to water it. Rell stops her: the plan said five. Miri points out they can write an addendum. Odo turns over the paper. The back is blank.

MIRI: We have room for a second objective.

PAVA: Or we can use the same can.

They water the sixth sapling. Rell asks if the plan should be amended. Odo writes "and any dry ones we see." Miri underlines it. Pava picks up the empty can and heads back to refill it.

Rell pins the plan on the wall. It falls because the paper is wet. The ink has run into a blue blur. Odo reads the remaining words.

ODO: Water trees.

RELL: That is concise.

Pava returns with the full can. Everyone takes a turn carrying it outside. Miri erases the circles from the board, leaving only the word WATER.

MIRI: The strategy is ready.

Rell fills in a title for the plan: An Integrated Watering Vision. Pava takes the page, crosses out the title, and writes the time each tree was watered. Odo pins it beside the refilled can, where the next volunteer can find both.

Miri takes the watering can outside and starts with the next sapling.

PAVA: The trees are too.
