# The Neighborhood Watch for a Shadow

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: overzealous security briefings that treat normal shadows as threats*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: captain Pava, a gibbon; watcher Odo, a baboon; neighbor Leni, a capuchin; lighting volunteer Kito, a macaque.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - EVENING

Odo points at a large shadow on the shed wall.

ODO: Something is watching the gate.

PAVA: It is the watering can.

Leni moves the can. The shadow moves too.

ODO: It is following us.

Kito turns on a lantern. The shadow disappears. Pava says the watch group should remain alert.

The crew checks the garden path. Odo sees another shadow under the tree. It is a branch moving in the breeze. Leni holds the branch still; the shadow stops.

PAVA: We have secured the perimeter.

KITO: There is no perimeter.

Pava has printed a map of the garden and drawn a red line around the path. Odo says they need patrol rotations. Leni asks who will watch the map. Pava assigns Kito, but Kito is holding the lantern.

The lantern shifts. A shadow appears behind Odo. He turns and sees his own outline.

ODO: I have become the watcher.

Leni asks if they can lower the lantern. Kito sets it on the ground. The light now casts a giant shadow of the empty watering can. Pava asks if it could be a prowler. Odo says it has no feet.

The wind knocks over a leaf basket. The sound makes everyone turn. The basket casts a new shadow, but it is smaller than the watering can. Odo says the threat may be shrinking.

Pava instructs the group to identify each object before calling it suspicious. Leni points out the watering can, the branch, and the basket. Kito moves the lantern so the shadows fall away from the shed. The wall is blank again.

Kito takes the watering can and places it beside the shed. The group walks past the wall one at a time while the lantern stays low. Each person sees a different outline, but the objects are all visible in the garden. Odo asks if they should keep a log. Pava says they can note where shadows fall so the night gardeners know what to expect. Leni writes down "can beside shed, branch above path, basket on ground." The list is ordinary and useful. Odo suggests a patrol route that does not cross the beds. They walk it once and find the gate latched.

The shadows stay on the wall.

Pava thanks everyone for checking the garden before closing.

Odo adds the route to the garden map.

Kito checks that the gate is latched, then puts the lantern back on the shelf.

Everyone heads home.

Leni closes the garden gate behind the group.

TAG

A cloud passes over the moon. The garden goes dark.

ODO: Should we report the sky?

PAVA: No.
