# The Group Photo Has a Seating Chart

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: organized group photos that assign people positions until nobody can pose naturally*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Photographer Seli, a capuchin; organizer Nara, a macaque; guest Tavi, a gibbon; and guest Odo, a baboon.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - DAY

Nara unfolds a seating chart beside a small backdrop. Seli holds a camera. Tavi and Odo wait with the other guests.

NARA: Tall people in back, short people in front, everyone angled toward the banner.

TAVI: The banner is behind the camera.

SELI: We can rotate the group.

[Everyone turns ninety degrees. The whole group now faces the compost bin.]

ODO: The compost has joined the photo.

NARA: We need a triangle, not a line.

TAVI: We are already standing in a row.

[Nara marks spots with tape. Each spot has a different number and arrow. Guests shuffle to their assigned marks, rotating in opposite directions until they form a circle.]

INT. GARDEN PHOTO AREA - MOMENTS LATER

Seli looks through the viewfinder. Odo blocks the banner; Tavi blocks Odo; a guest's hat fills the foreground.

SELI: The front row is too far forward.

NARA: The chart says front row.

TAVI: The chart is on the floor.

[The guests bend to read the chart. Seli takes a photo of everyone looking down at their feet.]

SELI: That is a very unified pose.

ODO: We are trying to read the arrows.

NARA: We need one person to call the pose.

TAVI: Let the photographer do it.

SELI: I need both hands on the camera.

INT. GARDEN PHOTO AREA - BEFORE THE FINAL SHOT

Seli sets a timer and runs into the group. The camera flashes before she reaches her mark. She runs back to adjust it.

SELI: The timer is too short.

NARA: The chart gives you a spot.

SELI: It gives me spot number twelve, behind Odo.

[The timer starts again. Seli dashes to spot twelve and crouches behind Odo. The camera captures one eye and half her ear.]

TAVI: You are in the photo.

SELI: I am represented by a forehead.

ODO: It is a good forehead.

INT. PHOTO AREA - LATER

Nara replaces the chart with a simple instruction: STAND TOGETHER AND LOOK HERE. She points to the lens.

NARA: No positions. Just come in close.

[The group squeezes together. Odo's hat tips forward, covering his eyes. Tavi holds it up; Seli counts down.]

SELI: Three, two, one.

[At the shutter click, everyone looks at Odo's hat instead of the lens.]

ODO: Did we get it?

SELI: We got everyone looking at the same thing.

NARA: That is more together than the chart.

[She hangs the photo beside the seating chart. The group appears as a neat semicircle focused on the crooked hat.]

TAVI: The hat is centered.

ODO: I would like to see the picture.

SELI: You are in it.

ODO: Then I will trust the hat.
