# The Reunion Name Tags Are Wrong

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: class reunions and nostalgia built around unreliable memory*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: ORGANIZER SEDA, a macaque; GUEST RELL, a gibbon; GUEST NIM, a capuchin; and a box of name tags.

[A community hall. Seda arranges name tags on a table. Rell and Nim enter.] 

SEDA: Welcome to the branch school reunion. Please wear your name tag so everyone can remember you.

RELL: Mine says "Rell, Class of Last Dry Season."

NIM: Mine says "Nim, Class of Two Years Ago."

SEDA: The labels came from the old class list.

NIM: I started last dry season.

RELL: I was here two years ago.

SEDA: The list may be out of date.

[RELL TAKES A TAG FROM THE BOX. IT READS "NEW FRIEND."] 

RELL: This one is accurate.

NIM: We were in the same class.

RELL: I remember you. You sat by the window.

NIM: That was the water dish.

SEDA: The class photo is ready. Stand in your original places.

[THEY GATHER AROUND A BLANK FRAME.] 

NIM: I was behind the teacher.

RELL: The teacher was in front of the tree.

SEDA: The tree has grown since then.

NIM: We can stand where the picture would have been.

[THEY MOVE. RELL STANDS BEHIND A BRANCH.] 

RELL: I cannot see the camera.

SEDA: You are exactly where you were in the photo.

RELL: I have grown too.

NIM: We could take a new picture instead of recreating the old one.

SEDA: The reunion program says "Remember the past."

NIM: It does not say we have to stand behind the same branch.

[SEDA GIVES THEM NEW TAGS. THEY WRITE THEIR NAMES THEMSELVES.] 

SEDA: The new tags have no class year.

RELL: We can write the year on the back.

NIM: Or leave it blank. We know when we are here.

SEDA: The old class list will need an update.

RELL: It can say we both attended the school.

NIM: And that we came back today.

NIM: I think your tag says my name.

RELL: Mine says Nim.

NIM: I am holding Rell.

SEDA: The tags got mixed when I stacked them.

RELL: I can wear your name for the photo.

NIM: We will look like each other.

SEDA: You already stood in each other's places.

[THEY SWITCH TAGS. THE CLASS PHOTO FRAME FALLS OVER.] 

RELL: The photographer is behind the frame.

NIM: We can take a new photo without it.

SEDA: I was hoping for the old frame.

RELL: The old frame is cracked.

SEDA: It is part of the reunion display.

NIM: It is falling apart during the reunion.

[THEY PROP THE FRAME AGAINST THE WALL. IT HOLDS, CROOKED.] 

SEDA: That is how I remember it.

RELL: Crooked?

SEDA: The frame. The school was fine.

NIM: We can take the new photo in front of the tree.

SEDA: Should I fix the tags first?

RELL: No. The wrong names are making people talk.

NIM: I have remembered you now.

SEDA: I will print a fresh class list tomorrow.

[THEY TAKE A NEW PHOTO. A BRANCH SHADOW CROSSES THE FRAME.] 

SEDA: The shadow is in the picture.

RELL: The tree was here when we were.

NIM: It can be in the photo again.

SEDA: Welcome back, both of you.

[THEY TRADE NAME TAGS BY ACCIDENT, THEN KEEP TALKING.]
