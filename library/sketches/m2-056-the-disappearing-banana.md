# The Disappearing Banana

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: science demonstration television shows and staged reveal segments*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: DR. VEX, a macaque science host; PEA, a marmoset camera operator; MURU, a capuchin assistant; and an AUDIENCE.

[A bright studio. A banana sits under a glass dome. A sign reads DO NOT BLINK.]

VEX: Today we test whether an object can disappear without moving. Muru, place the banana under the dome.

[Muru places the banana. Pea adjusts the camera. The banana is gone.]

AUDIENCE: Ooooh!

VEX: A shocking result.

MURU: It is in my cheek.

VEX: The cheek pouch cannot be seen from this angle.

PEA: The camera is pointed at the dome.

VEX: The dome is the control group.

PEA: The control group has nothing in it.

VEX: Precisely. We need to repeat the demonstration.

[Muru removes the banana from his cheek and replaces it. He covers the dome with a cloth.]

VEX: The cloth will prevent visual bias.

PEA: It will prevent the camera from seeing.

VEX: Science requires patience.

[The cloth lifts. Banana gone again.]

AUDIENCE: Ooooh!

VEX: Two successful trials.

MURU: I ate it.

VEX: Did you observe the outcome?

MURU: I can taste it.

PEA: We need a new banana for the control.

VEX: The audience deserves a bigger reveal.

[Pea brings a large bunch of bananas. The audience applauds. Muru takes one, then another, filling both cheek pouches.]

VEX: That is not part of the protocol.

MURU: It is the same demonstration with more data.

PEA: The camera can't see the bunch anymore.

VEX: We can use a thermal camera.

[They point a toy scanner at Muru. It beeps.]

VEX: We have detected warm fruit.

MURU: That is me.

VEX: A promising result.

PEA: The test question was whether the banana could disappear without moving.

MURU: It moved to my cheek.

VEX: We must define "disappear."

[The audience laughs. Vex takes the dome and inspects it.]

VEX: The dome is empty, the banana is gone, and our assistant is chewing.

MURU: The experiment is repeatable.

VEX: Next week, we test whether an empty snack bowl is truly empty.

[The audience reaches for the remaining fruit. Vex covers the bunch with the dome. The dome is too small.]

VEX: We should show that this result can be repeated by anyone.

MURU: You need another banana.

PEA: We have one left.

VEX: The control group must remain untouched.

[Muru takes the banana. The audience gasps.]

VEX: We have lost the control group.

MURU: It was lunch.

PEA: The camera is still recording.

VEX: Then let it record our revised hypothesis. When a macaque has a cheek pouch and sees a banana, the banana may move.

MURU: That is not a hypothesis. That is a warning.

[The audience begins checking its own pockets.]

PEA: They think the fruit is in their pouches.

VEX: This has become a participatory experiment.

MURU: They can participate by leaving the last banana alone.

[The audience leaves it alone. Vex puts the dome over it, then moves the dome so everyone can see the fruit.]

[BLACKOUT.]
