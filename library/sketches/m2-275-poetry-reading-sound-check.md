# The Poetry Reading Has a Sound Check

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: literary readings that add so much production polish that quiet poems become sound events*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Poet Nara, a gibbon; sound operator Odo, a baboon; host Rali, a macaque; and audience member Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. BOOKSHOP - EVENING

A stool and microphone stand on a small stage. Odo checks the sound board. Nara holds a notebook.

RALI: We welcome Nara for a quiet poem about rain.

ODO: We have rain ambience, echo, and a soft thunder cue.

NARA: The poem is just me reading.

[Odo plays rain. It sounds like a heavy shower on a metal roof.]

TAVI: Is the roof leaking?

RALI: It is atmosphere.

NARA: The rain in the poem is outside.

INT. BOOKSHOP - MOMENTS LATER

Nara begins. Odo adds a gentle echo after each line. The echo repeats the final word.

NARA: A drop waits on the windowsill.

[The speakers say "windowsill" again. Nara stops.]

ODO: That was the literary echo.

NARA: I have already said the word.

RALI: The room is reflecting it.

TAVI: The word has returned.

[Nara tries another line. Odo's rain sound grows louder. The audience leans in, then leans closer as the speakers rumble.]

INT. SOUND TABLE - LATER

Rali asks for a more intimate reading. Odo turns the speakers down but leaves the thunder cue armed.

NARA: I will read without effects.

[She reads softly. The bookshop is silent. At the line about a quiet street, Odo's thunder cue crashes.]

TAVI: The street has become a storm.

ODO: I pressed the wrong button.

RALI: Can we make it sound intentional?

NARA: The poem is about waiting.

[They wait. The thunder machine continues to rumble, fading very slowly.]

TAVI: We are waiting for the storm to finish.

INT. BOOKSHOP - BEFORE FINAL READING

Odo tests the echo one last time. He claps once; the speakers repeat it three times.

ODO: The setting is still active.

RALI: Can you stop the echo?

[Odo searches the board. One button says ECHO, another says ECHO AGAIN, and a third says FINAL ECHO.]

TAVI: Which one turns it off?

ODO: They all add more echo.

[Nara points to the power switch. Odo turns it off. The speakers go quiet.]

NARA: That is a clear ending.
INT. BOOKSHOP - FINAL READING

Odo disconnects the sound board. Rali steps back from the microphone. Nara reads in her natural voice; the audience listens.

NARA: A drop waits on the windowsill. The street shines after the rain.

[The silence after the poem lasts a beat. Then the audience applauds. Odo reaches for the sound board but it is unplugged.]

RALI: That was the whole sound design.

ODO: I have been replaced by a quiet room.

TAVI: It did not repeat the last word.

[Nara closes her notebook. The shop's front door opens, and a real drop of rain falls from the awning into a bucket.]

NARA: The rain has found its own ending.

[Odo lifts the bucket away from the microphone.]
