# The Lifeguard Whistle Starts a Talent Show

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised talent competitions and pool rules colliding over one whistle cue*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: LIFEGUARD, a baboon; HOST, a gibbon; SWIMMER, a macaque; JUDGE, an orangutan; SOUND TECH, a capuchin.

[A community pool. The host stands beside a judging table. The lifeguard sits high above the water, holding a whistle. A banner reads SPLASH TALENT NIGHT.]

HOST: First act, a synchronized swim.

LIFEGUARD: No running on the deck.

[The swimmer takes three steps, slips slightly, and catches the rail. The lifeguard whistles. The sound tech triggers a drumroll.]

JUDGE: Was that the performance?

HOST: It was the opening signal.

ACT ONE

[The swimmer begins a routine, making careful circles in the shallow end. The host narrates. The lifeguard whistles each time the swimmer goes underwater.]

HOST: A dramatic descent.

JUDGE: It lasts two seconds.

LIFEGUARD: I am checking breath holds.

SWIMMER: I am checking choreography.

[The sound tech lowers the drumroll, but a whistle still triggers the show's applause track. The audience applauds every time the lifeguard signals a safety warning.]

LIFEGUARD: Clear the diving board.

[Whistle. Applause.]

HOST: The audience loves a clean deck.

JUDGE: They love being told when to clap.

[The next act is a rubber duck race. The ducks are placed in separate lanes. The lifeguard blows one short whistle to start. The sound tech plays a victory sting. The ducks have not moved.]

SWIMMER: That sounded like the finish.

HOST: The race has finished before beginning.

[The judge asks the sound tech to disconnect the applause track. The lifeguard asks for a clear visual signal. The host raises a blue card for "start" and a red card for "stop." The swimmer tests them from the water; the first blue card is hidden behind the host's banner.]

JUDGE: We need the audience to see the signal.

[The host moves the banner. The blue card is visible. The duck race starts again. The lifeguard watches the water, the host holds the cards, and the sound tech keeps both hands off the effects panel.]

[The swimmer asks to perform the routine again, this time with the sound tech controlling the music. The lifeguard agrees to watch the lane instead of the board. The host raises the blue card. The swimmer completes the circles, reaches the edge, and climbs out without anyone mistaking a whistle for a drumroll.]

JUDGE: That was synchronized.

SOUND TECH: And the applause was ours.

ACT TWO

[The ducks drift to the finish. One gets caught at the lane divider. The lifeguard uses a long pool skimmer to free it without blowing the whistle. The duck crosses, and the judge awards it a ribbon.]

HOST: The show has a winner.

LIFEGUARD: The pool is still safe.

SWIMMER: Can my routine have music next time?

SOUND TECH: If the whistle stays out of the soundboard.

FINAL IMAGE: The host raises the winner's ribbon while the lifeguard holds up a quiet red card that says NO APPLAUSE NEEDED.
