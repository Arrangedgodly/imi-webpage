# The Green Belt Test

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Martial arts grading ceremonies and disciplined training montages*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Master Raku, a baboon; student Timo, a capuchin; student Lei, a macaque; examiner Boro, a mandrill.

[STAGE: DOJO WITH A BELL, TWO MATS, AND A ROW OF COLORED BELTS.] 

RAKU: The green belt test begins now. Show control, balance, and respect.

TIMO: I have practiced the bow.

LEI: You bowed to the wall.

TIMO: The wall was watching.

BORO: First test, stand on one foot.

TIMO: Which foot?

BORO: The one you are not standing on.

TIMO: I am standing on both.

RAKU: That's a good start.

[TIMO LIFTS ONE FOOT. HIS TAIL CATCHES THE MAT.] 

LEI: Your tail is touching the floor.

TIMO: It is helping me balance.

BORO: The test is about balance, not rules.

RAKU: Second test, break the board.

TIMO: Is it a real board?

BORO: It is foam.

TIMO: Then I will break it gently.

LEI: The goal is to strike the center.

TIMO: I will strike the center gently.

[TIMO TAPS THE BOARD. IT BOUNCES OFF HIS HAND.] 

TIMO: The board has defended itself.

RAKU: Use your palm, not your fingers.

BORO: We are grading control.

TIMO: I am in control of the board.

LEI: The board is under the bench.

RAKU: The board has moved to the next test.

BORO: Third test, retrieve the practice bell without making it ring.

TIMO: It is hanging above the mat.

LEI: It is attached to a string.

TIMO: I can climb the post.

RAKU: Use a careful step.

TIMO: My feet are careful.

[TIMO CLIMBS THE POST. THE BELL SWAYS.] 

BORO: Do not touch the bell.

TIMO: I am holding the string.

LEI: The string is attached to the bell.

TIMO: I understand the problem now.

[THE BELL RINGS ONCE.] 

TIMO: The bell has finished its test.

RAKU: You remained calm.

BORO: You showed balance, restraint, and attention.

LEI: He also hit the foam board.

TIMO: The board is still under the bench.

RAKU: The belt test is not about defeating props.

BORO: It is about practice.

TIMO: Did I pass?

BORO: You earned the green belt.

TIMO: Can I tie it myself?

RAKU: Yes. Make a square knot.

TIMO: I have made a bow.

LEI: To the wall?

TIMO: The wall is still watching.

RAKU: The ceremony is complete.

BORO: The foam board may be retrieved.

LEI: It is under the bench.

TIMO: It is training there.

LEI: The next test is balance. Stand on one foot.
TIMO: Which foot?
LEI: The one that is not holding your snack.
TIMO: Both feet are holding snacks.
LEI: Then balance the snacks.
TIMO: They are balanced on my head.
LEI: That is advanced.
TIMO: I learned by carrying lunch through a swinging vine.
LEI: The final test is patience.
TIMO: I have been waiting for you to say that.
LEI: You pass.
TIMO: Do I get a belt?
LEI: You get this leaf.

[THE BELL RINGS AS EVERYONE BOWS TO THE WALL.]
