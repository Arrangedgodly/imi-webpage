# Please Do Not Feed the Audio Guide

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio tours that turn every object into a solemn revelation*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Mara, a macaque guide; Tilo, a capuchin technician; Nell, a lemur visitor; Bongo, a sleepy gibbon.

INT. ORDINARY OBJECTS GALLERY - DAY

[A SIGN SAYS PLEASE TOUCH THE BUTTON, NOT THE EXHIBITS. NELL HOLDS THE MUSEUM'S NEW AUDIO GUIDE. BONGO SLEEPS UNDER A BENCH. MARA STANDS BESIDE A SMOOTH STONE IN A GLASS CASE.]

MARA: Welcome to our collection. This stone was found beside the west path.

NELL: Why is it behind glass?

MARA: It had a complicated journey here.

TILO: It was in my shoe.

MARA: That isn't the approved origin story.

[NELL PRESSES THE AUDIO BUTTON. A DEEP RECORDED VOICE FILLS THE GALLERY.]

GUIDE: This stone asks what it means to be carried.

NELL: It means Tilo had a pebble in his shoe.

MARA: Please save your interpretation for the question period.

TILO: I did put it in the case. It kept rolling off the desk.

MARA: A case gives it dignity.

[NELL PRESSES THE BUTTON AGAIN. THE RECORDING SKIPS TO AN EMPTY FRAME HANGING ON THE NEXT WALL.]

GUIDE: Before you is an absence. It is a portrait of the viewer.

NELL: The picture fell off.

TILO: I haven't found it yet.

MARA: That makes the frame even more moving.

[BONGO SNORES. THE RECEIVER'S MICROPHONE ACTIVATES.]

GUIDE: Listen closely. The ancient floor speaks.

Nell crouches to hear. Bongo snores again.

NELL: The floor sounds tired.

MARA: It has held generations of visitors.

TILO: It has held one napper for forty minutes.

Mara reaches for the guide. The button sits under a tight plastic cover. Nell turns the receiver sideways and lands on a new track.

GUIDE: You have reached the museum's most mysterious object, the locked supply cupboard.

MARA: That track is for staff orientation.

NELL: Why is it locked?

TILO: Because the guide keeps narrating while I'm trying to work.

GUIDE: Behind this door waits a collection of brushes, tape, and one missing portrait. Imagine what they might become.

MARA: It is supposed to end after the empty frame.

TILO: You asked for an all-access recording.

MARA: For visitors, not for the cupboard.

[THE GUIDE CONTINUES. MARA PULLS THE COVER OFF, BUT THE BUTTON SPRINGS OUT AND ROLLS UNDER THE BENCH.]

GUIDE: You are now entering the threshold between order and maintenance.

NELL: That's a very narrow threshold.

TILO: Don't step on it. That's my only spare button.

Bongo wakes and reaches under the bench. He finds the loose button, pushes it back into the receiver, and presses it. The guide chirps.

GUIDE: Thank you for completing the tour. Please return your device to the desk.

Bongo walks to the front desk and puts it down. The guide stops. Tilo unlocks the cupboard, but its spring door keeps closing against his arm. He opens the glass case, takes the stone and wedges the cupboard door with it. The missing portrait leans against a mop.

MARA: We should put the stone back.

TILO: Let it finish the tour first.

NELL: It's the only exhibit that has helped us.

[MARA LIFTS THE STONE. THE CUPBOARD DOOR SWINGS SHUT ON TILO'S MOP HANDLE. AT THE DESK, BONGO PRESSES THE REPAIRED BUTTON AGAIN.]

GUIDE: Please do not feed the audio guide.

NELL: Nobody did.

[BONGO OFFERS THE RECEIVER A LEAF. THE GUIDE BEEPS. MARA OPENS THE CUPBOARD TO FREE THE MOP, THEN SETS THE STONE BACK IN ITS CASE. TILO REPLACES ITS LABEL: ON LOAN TO MAINTENANCE. MARA TURNS THE LABEL FACE DOWN; NELL TURNS IT BACK.]
