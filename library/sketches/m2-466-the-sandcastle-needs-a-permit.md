# The Sandcastle Needs a Permit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: building competitions, construction inspections, and beach projects measured against a moving shoreline*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: BUILDER, a macaque; INSPECTOR, a baboon; HOST, a gibbon; ENGINEER, an orangutan; CHILD, a capuchin.

[A beach. The builder pats a tall sandcastle beside a small sign reading "SITE PLAN."]

HOST: The grand opening is moments away.

INSPECTOR: I need to see the foundation.

BUILDER: It is under the castle.

ENGINEER: We need to check the sand's moisture before adding the tower.

CHILD: It is wet at the bottom and dry at the top.

[The inspector circles the castle with a clipboard. A wave approaches, then retreats.] 

INSPECTOR: The shoreline has moved.

HOST: The lot has expanded.

ENGINEER: It has not. The tide is coming in.

[The builder packs a small trench around the base. The host places a flag on the tower. The flag tips it over.] 

BUILDER: That was not in the plan.

HOST: The flag was a ceremonial finish.

INSPECTOR: The tower is unstable.

[The child removes the flag and hands over a flat shell.] 

CHILD: This is lighter.

ENGINEER: It is also not a roof.

[The builder makes a wider base, then presses a shell into the wall as a window. The inspector checks it with a small level. The level shows a slight tilt.]

INSPECTOR: The window is crooked.

BUILDER: It is a shell.

INSPECTOR: The shell is crooked.

[Another wave advances. The host lifts the clipboard, the builder lifts the castle's loose tower, and the engineer moves the site plan up the beach.] 

HOST: Are we evacuating the site?

ENGINEER: We are moving above the tide line.

INSPECTOR: That is a new location. Does it need a new permit?

BUILDER: It is a sandcastle, not a hotel.

CHILD: The tide does not read permits.

[The group rebuilds a small castle farther from the wet sand. The engineer checks the base; the inspector checks that the shell window stays in place. The flag is left out.] 

HOST: We have a safe structure.

INSPECTOR: We have a structure that can wash away.

BUILDER: That is the material.

[The host asks the builder to add a second tower for the finale. The builder refuses and places a shell on top instead. The tower remains steady.] 

CHILD: It is finished.

INSPECTOR: It passes until the water reaches it.

HOST: And when the water reaches it?

ENGINEER: We let the beach take it back.

[They watch the next wave approach from a distance.] 

[The child presses the shell beside the castle's base. The engineer watches the damp line along the sand, then nudges the shell slightly farther uphill.]

ENGINEER: This marks the last wave's reach. It does not predict the next one.

INSPECTOR: Is that a boundary marker?

CHILD: It is a shell. The tide can move it.

HOST: Can we open before the next inspection?

INSPECTOR: The structure is stable for now. I am inspecting the flag instead.

[The host lifts the flag and tries to plant it away from the tower. A gust pulls the flag sideways; the host catches it with both hands.]

HOST: The flag requires a permit for wind.

BUILDER: The flag requires a shorter pole.

[The host folds the flag and tucks it into the clipboard. The child pats the shell roof once; the castle holds. The group waits as a small wave stops short of the shell marker.]

CHILD: It did not reach us.

INSPECTOR: That is today's inspection result.

FINAL IMAGE: The small castle stands above the tide line with one shell on its roof and no flag to knock it over.
