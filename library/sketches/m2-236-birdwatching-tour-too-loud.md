# The Birdwatching Tour Is Too Loud

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: guided nature tours that promise quiet observation while guides narrate every sighting*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Guide Nemi, a macaque; Naturalist Boro, a gibbon; Visitor Sela, a capuchin; and Photographer Dax, a baboon.

EXT. FOREST PATH - MORNING

The group walks quietly. Boro carries binoculars. Nemi holds a sign that reads "PLEASE SPEAK SOFTLY." Dax's camera strap jingles.

NEMI: We may see a green heron near the creek.

BORO: It is better to stop and listen.

NEMI: Our next scheduled stop is in two minutes.

SELA: I hear a bird now.

DAX: I hear the camera strap.

[NEMI TIES A CLOTH AROUND THE STRAP. IT STOPS JINGLING.]

BORO: Good. Now the path is quiet.

NEMI: Welcome to the quiet stretch. Please remain silent.

[HE WHISPERS INTO A MEGAPHONE. THE GROUP WINCES.]

INT. FOREST HIDE - MOMENTS LATER

Everyone sits behind a wooden screen. Boro points to a bird across the creek. Nemi opens a guidebook.

BORO: It is a kingfisher.

NEMI: The guide says green heron.

BORO: The guide is wrong.

NEMI: I will announce the correction.

SELA: Could we watch first?

NEMI: I have a fact about the bird.

BORO: You can tell us after it leaves.

NEMI: It may leave if we stay quiet.

DAX: I turned the camera sound off.

[NEMI OPENS THE GUIDEBOOK TO THE PAGE ABOUT THE HERON. A FOLDED MAP FALLS OUT AND FLUTTERS ACROSS THE HIDE.]

SELA: That sounded like a bird.

BORO: It sounded like paper.

NEMI: I can read the bird's range from the guide.

SELA: We can walk to the range.

DAX: The camera is ready.

NEMI: I will save the announcement for later.

BORO: The bird is still on the branch.

NEMI: I can tell everyone which way to look.

SELA: I can see it without a direction.

[NEMI LOWERS THE GUIDEBOOK. THE BIRD STAYS IN VIEW.]

SELA: Please don't announce the correction yet.

DAX: I can get a photo without flash.

[THE CAMERA MAKES A LOUD SHUTTER SOUND. THE BIRD FLIES OFF.]

DAX: The camera was on sound mode.

NEMI: The bird has departed.

BORO: We can listen for another one.

[NEMI TURNS A PAGE. THE PAPER RUSTLES LIKE A WING.]

SELA: That one is the guidebook.

EXT. CREEK PATH - LATER

The group stops by the water. A small bird lands on a branch. Nemi raises the megaphone, then lowers it.

NEMI: I will say nothing.

BORO: Good.

[DAX RAISES THE CAMERA. NEMI TAPS THE SHUTTER BUTTON OFF. Dax nods and takes a silent photo.]

SELA: I can hear the bird's call.

BORO: What does the guidebook say?

NEMI: Nothing. I left it in the van.

[THE BIRD CALLS AGAIN. THE GROUP LISTENS.]

NEMI: That is the green heron.

BORO: It is the kingfisher.

SELA: It is the bird.

[THE TOUR GROUP QUIETLY FOLLOWS THE STREAM, WHILE NEMI CARRIES THE MEGAPHONE UPSIDE DOWN.]
