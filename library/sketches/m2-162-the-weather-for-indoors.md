# The Weather for Indoors

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: local television weather forecasts that treat a leaky roof and a desk fan like regional climate events*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Miri, a capuchin weather presenter; Odo, a macaque technician; Fenn, a gibbon field reporter; and studio intern Pava, a slow loris.

INT. LOCAL NEWS STUDIO - MORNING

Miri stands beside a map of the studio. A blue cloud has been drawn over the supply closet.

MIRI: A strong chance of drips after lunch, moving west toward the desk.

Odo enters with a bucket. A drop lands in it with a plink.

ODO: The ceiling has confirmed the forecast.

Miri points to the map. A paper arrow labeled BREEZE swings toward the camera. Pava holds a desk fan pointed at the graphic.

MIRI: Winds will reach three leaves per hour.

PAVA: The fan has two settings.

MIRI: We expect the wind to strengthen.

Fenn reports from the hallway beneath the ceiling leak. He wears a rain cape and stands next to a small puddle.

FENN: Conditions here are damp. The puddle is expanding toward the east wall.

Odo moves the bucket six inches. The drip misses it.

ODO: East has shifted.

Miri asks Pava to rotate the map. Pava slowly turns it. The cloud now covers the studio kitchen.

MIRI: The storm is moving indoors.

Fenn crosses the hall holding a ruler above the puddle.

FENN: I have measured the flood. It is one ruler wide and half a ruler deep.

ODO: The ruler is standing upright.

Fenn adjusts it. The puddle is shallow enough for him to step across. He demonstrates, then lands in the water with one foot.

FENN: The crossing is more difficult than expected.

Miri changes the graphic to show a sun over the dry side of the room. The desk fan blows the map. The paper sun falls onto the bucket, covering the drip.

MIRI: Conditions are clearing.

A drop lands on the back of her collar. She turns to look up. The camera follows and shows a small stain on the ceiling.

ODO: There is a second weather system.

Pava carries in a towel and dries the map. The fan turns the towel into a small sail, pushing it across the floor. Fenn catches it with his ruler.

FENN: We have winds moving furniture.

Miri announces a severe towel advisory. Everyone moves the chairs against the wall. Odo catches the bucket and shifts it beneath the leak. The room settles.

MIRI: Tonight, clear skies, with a chance the landlord checks the roof.

A drip hits the paper map exactly on the sun symbol.

PAVA: The forecast has changed.

Odo lifts the bucket and checks the ceiling. The drip has stopped, but the bucket is half full. He empties it into a plant pot. The studio map now shows the cloud, the sun, and a small plant icon beneath them.

MIRI: No. The sun is sweating.
