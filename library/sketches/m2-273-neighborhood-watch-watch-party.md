# The Neighborhood Watch Has a Watch Party

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: neighborhood watch groups that confuse vigilance with constant updates and dramatic alerts*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Coordinator Kito, a macaque; neighbor Pava, a baboon; neighbor Runi, a gibbon; and mail carrier Seli, a capuchin.

EXT. APARTMENT COURTYARD - EVENING

Kito holds a clipboard. Pava and Runi sit beside a row of apartment mailboxes. A motion light clicks on.

KITO: The watch is active. Report anything unusual.

PAVA: The light turned on.

RUNI: That is what it does.

KITO: I will log it.

[He writes "LIGHT: ON" on the clipboard.]

SELI: I am delivering mail.

KITO: Please identify yourself.

SELI: I am wearing the uniform and carrying the letters.

PAVA: We have confirmed the mail carrier.

KITO: I will log a confirmed mail carrier.

INT. COURTYARD - MOMENTS LATER

A cat crosses the courtyard. Runi points. Kito rings a small desk bell.

KITO: Possible visitor, moving north.

PAVA: It is the building's cat.

RUNI: We have seen it every night.

KITO: Familiar movement can still be unusual.

[The cat returns with a leaf in its mouth. Kito writes "LEAF CARGO." ]

SELI: It is carrying a leaf.

KITO: Do you know where the leaf came from?

SELI: The planter.

[Pava checks the planter. It is missing one leaf. Kito starts a report.]

INT. COURTYARD - LATER

The wind blows a paper bag across the pavement. Kito stands, alarmed. Pava takes a photo. Runi uses a broom to stop it.

KITO: Object moving without an escort.

RUNI: The wind is escorting it.

PAVA: I have a blurry photo.

SELI: The bag is empty.

KITO: We should preserve the evidence.

[He puts the bag in a clear plastic envelope. The wind catches the envelope and lifts it into the air.]

PAVA: The evidence has escaped.

RUNI: The broom can get it.

[She reaches it down, then hands the envelope to Kito.]

INT. COURTYARD - BEFORE NIGHT

Kito tests a phone alert system. It sends a notification to the neighbors: UNUSUAL ACTIVITY: COORDINATOR MOVING.

PAVA: It has reported you.

KITO: I was checking the system.

RUNI: The system noticed.

[Every phone chimes at once. Seli's delivery scanner also chimes; the cat bolts under the stairs.]

SELI: We have alerted the entire courtyard.

PAVA: And the cat.

[Kito switches off the alerts. Everyone's phone chimes once more to confirm they are off.]
EXT. COURTYARD - NIGHT

Kito reads his report aloud. It has seven pages, including the cat, leaf, light, mail carrier and bag.

KITO: No incidents occurred.

PAVA: We have watched everything.

SELI: I delivered the mail during the whole watch.

RUNI: And the cat returned the leaf to the planter.

[The cat drops the leaf in the soil and walks away. Kito writes "CASE CLOSED." The motion light switches off.]

KITO: The courtyard is secure.

[The light turns on again as Kito moves. Everyone turns to look at him.]

PAVA: Possible visitor.

KITO: It is me.

SELI: We have confirmed the coordinator.
