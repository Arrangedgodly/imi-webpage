# The Committee for Smaller Committees

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate efficiency panels and meetings that multiply whenever someone proposes a shorter meeting*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: CHAIR, a baboon; MINT, a macaque; NOTE-TAKER, a gibbon; INTERN, a capuchin; TIMER, an orangutan.

[A meeting room. A sign reads "COMMITTEE FOR SMALLER COMMITTEES." Five chairs are arranged in a circle.]

CHAIR: We are here to reduce the number of committees.

MINT: How many committees are there now?

NOTE-TAKER: The committee has asked me not to read the number until we agree how to define a committee.

INTERN: Is this one?

CHAIR: It is the parent committee.

TIMER: I was assigned to time the parent committee.

MINT: Who assigned you?

TIMER: The committee for shorter meetings.

CHAIR: That committee is a subcommittee of this one.

NOTE-TAKER: I have written "we will review the subcommittee structure."

[The chair places a small bell in the center of the table.]

CHAIR: Each person may speak for thirty seconds.

[The bell rings every time anyone inhales.]

INTERN: It is ringing at me.

TIMER: The timer is sensitive to pauses.

MINT: We need a committee to decide whether the bell counts as a person.

CHAIR: That would increase the committee count.

[The note-taker adds a chair to the circle for the bell.]

NOTE-TAKER: It has voting rights now.

CHAIR: We cannot let an object vote on committee reduction.

MINT: Then remove the bell.

[The timer takes the bell away. The empty space in the circle looks like an open seat.]

INTERN: We have created a vacancy.

CHAIR: The committee for vacancies can handle that.

MINT: How many vacancies?

NOTE-TAKER: One bell-shaped vacancy and two unfilled subcommittee chairs.

[The timer starts a thirty-second countdown. It rings at zero. Everyone stops speaking.]

CHAIR: We have used the whole meeting and reached no decision.

MINT: We did decide to stop when the timer rang.

INTERN: We could keep that decision and cancel the subcommittee meeting.

CHAIR: The subcommittee meets after this meeting.

MINT: Then we should cancel this meeting and have the subcommittee decide.

NOTE-TAKER: I can write that down.

CHAIR: No. A decision must be made in the room.

[The intern stands and carries the five chairs out one at a time. The chair tries to continue the meeting, but has no chair.]

CHAIR: We still have a quorum.

MINT: We have one person standing in a room.

TIMER: The meeting is over.

[The timer removes the committee sign from the door and turns it around. The back says "NO MEETING."]

NOTE-TAKER: Is that an official sign?

TIMER: It is a sign with no committee attached.

CHAIR: Then we have reduced the count by one.

MINT: Two. The subcommittee is canceled too.

[The note-taker rips up the agenda. The paper falls into the recycling box.]

FINAL IMAGE: The empty room has no chairs, no bell, and one sign reading "NO MEETING."
