# The Welcome Basket Has a Welcome Basket

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate onboarding kits and overproduced first-day orientation*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: ORIENTATION LEAD TESS, a macaque; NEW HIRE MIKA, a gibbon; OFFICE GUIDE PIP, a capuchin; and an HR VIDEO VOICE.

[A lobby. A large welcome basket sits inside a smaller welcome basket. Tess wears a badge reading ASK ME ANYTHING.] 

TESS: Welcome to Fern Works. Your first task is to open your welcome basket.

MIKA: Which one?

TESS: Start with the outer basket.

[MIKA LIFTS THE LID. A SECOND BASKET IS INSIDE.] 

PIP: That is the inner welcome basket. It contains the welcome card.

MIKA: The card says to open the welcome basket.

TESS: The card is in the inner basket.

MIKA: I have opened the inner basket.

TESS: Then you're ready to begin.

[THE VIDEO SCREEN TURNS ON.] 

VOICE: Welcome to the welcome experience. Please watch this four-minute video before asking questions.

MIKA: I have a question about the baskets.

TESS: Save it for the question period.

VOICE: The question period follows the orientation.

MIKA: What is the orientation?

PIP: The video.

[THE VIDEO RESTARTS FROM THE BEGINNING.] 

VOICE: Welcome to the welcome experience.

MIKA: It is repeating.

TESS: That means it has not finished welcoming you.

PIP: The office map is beneath the baskets.

MIKA: Can I move them?

TESS: You may move the basket as long as you keep the welcome experience intact.

[MIKA LIFTS BOTH BASKETS. A ROLL OF TAPE AND A PENCIL FALL OUT.] 

PIP: The office supplies were placed in the welcome basket.

MIKA: I can use the pencil to write my question.

TESS: We have a question form for questions.

MIKA: Is it in a basket?

TESS: It is in the question basket.

MIKA: Is that inside the welcome basket?

PIP: It is under the desk.

[MIKA FINDS THE FORM. A STICKER ON IT SAYS WELCOME.] 

MIKA: The form asks what I need to do my job.

TESS: What do you need?

MIKA: A place to put my things and directions to the workroom.

PIP: The shelf is there. The workroom is down that hall.

PIP: There is also a tiny envelope beneath the inner basket.

MIKA: It says "For your first task."

TESS: That must be the form.

MIKA: It is blank.

TESS: We wanted you to write down what would make you feel welcome.

MIKA: Clear directions would make me feel welcome.

PIP: Then we should give you the map first.

TESS: The map is also in a basket.

MIKA: Does it have a basket inside?

PIP: No. It has a ribbon around it.

MIKA: I can untie the ribbon.

[MIKA PULLS THE RIBBON. IT UNWINDS AROUND THE OUTER BASKET.] 

TESS: That ribbon was supposed to make the basket look festive.

MIKA: Now I can carry the map without carrying the basket.

PIP: That's a good first-day skill.

VOICE: Please complete the welcome video before leaving the lobby.

MIKA: Is the workroom beyond this door?

PIP: Yes.

MIKA: Then I can watch the rest of the video after I find my desk.

TESS: That is allowed.

[MIKA OPENS THE DOOR. THE VIDEO VOICE CONTINUES WELCOMING THE EMPTY LOBBY.]

TESS: You have completed orientation.

MIKA: I thought I needed to finish the video.

TESS: The video will finish when it has no audience.

[MIKA WALKS OUT. THE VIDEO CONTINUES WELCOMING THE EMPTY ROOM.]
