# The Fire Marshal's Ribbon Is Too Long

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grand opening ceremonies, ribbon-cutting speeches, and safety officials who must keep the ribbon from blocking an exit*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: FIRE MARSHAL, a baboon; MAYOR, an orangutan; EVENT PLANNER, a gibbon; SCISSOR BEARER, a capuchin; REPORTER, a macaque.

[A new community hall. A red ribbon stretches from the front door across the lobby and out an emergency exit. The mayor holds a long speech. The fire marshal checks the doorway.]

FIRE MARSHAL: The ribbon blocks the exit.

EVENT PLANNER: It is a ceremonial line.

FIRE MARSHAL: It is across the door.

MAYOR: We can cut it after the speech.

REPORTER: How long is the speech?

[The mayor unfolds several pages. The ribbon droops across the reporter's microphone.]

ACT ONE

[The scissor bearer approaches with large scissors. The ribbon is tied to a stanchion, the door handle, and a potted plant.]

SCISSOR BEARER: Which end do I cut?

EVENT PLANNER: The symbolic end.

FIRE MARSHAL: The safe end.

[The reporter asks the mayor to summarize. The mayor begins a speech about community, then the ribbon slips off the handle and rolls across the lobby. The marshal lifts it over a wheelchair ramp.]

FIRE MARSHAL: Keep the route clear.

EVENT PLANNER: We need the whole ribbon for photographs.

[The planner loops the ribbon around the stanchion to shorten it. The stanchion topples, and the ribbon makes a neat red circle around the mayor.]

MAYOR: I am now the opening.

[The fire marshal checks the emergency plan. The exit door must open outward. The ribbon is tied to the inside handle, so pulling it tight would stop the door. The marshal asks the planner to move the stanchion and run the ribbon only across the clear wall.]

EVENT PLANNER: That leaves less ribbon in the photograph.

REPORTER: It leaves more exit in the building.

[The planner relocates the ribbon to the wall, but the scissors are now at the other end. The scissor bearer walks along it, carefully avoiding the ramp. The mayor takes one page from the speech and folds it into a shorter version.]

MAYOR: We welcome everyone to the hall.

FIRE MARSHAL: That is enough.

[The marshal opens the emergency exit fully. It clears the ribbon, ramp, and stanchion. The scissor bearer cuts the wall-mounted ribbon at the marked spot. The mayor holds a short piece for the photograph.]

ACT TWO

[The reporter takes a picture with the mayor, marshal, and planner standing beside the open exit. The remaining ribbon is rolled up and placed in a box. The event planner labels it for the next opening.]

MAYOR: Now we can begin the event.

FIRE MARSHAL: The doors are open.

[The mayor asks the reporter to take one more picture. The fire marshal opens the door wide, checks the path, and then steps out of frame. The planner gathers the spare ribbon into a coil. No one is asked to stand in front of the exit.]

REPORTER: Ready?

MAYOR: Short speech, open doors.

FIRE MARSHAL: That is a safe opening.

FINAL IMAGE: The mayor cuts the final small ribbon while the emergency exit swings freely behind him.
