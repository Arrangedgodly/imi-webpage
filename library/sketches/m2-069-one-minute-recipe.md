# One-Minute Recipe

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: short-form cooking tutorials and impossible time-saving claims*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: HOST JAX, a macaque; CHEF ELA, an orangutan; EDITOR NIB, a capuchin; and a TIMER.

[A kitchen counter. Ingredients are placed in clear bowls. A phone is mounted vertically.]

JAX: Today we make a three-minute breakfast in sixty seconds.

ELA: The oats need to soak overnight.

JAX: We can speed that up in editing.

NIB: The phone only records one minute.

JAX: Then we need a faster breakfast.

[Ela peels a banana and slices it with a spoon.]

ELA: Banana, oats, and warm milk. Stir and wait for the oats to soften.

JAX: Can we say "instant"?

ELA: The oats are not instant.

JAX: "Instant-ish"?

NIB: That's not a food word.

[Jax starts the timer. He tries to stir and talk at the same time.]

JAX: Add oats, add milk, add banana, and-

[The bowl slides. Ela steadies it.]

ELA: Hold the bowl.

JAX: The video has lost two seconds.

NIB: It has gained a counter that isn't covered in breakfast.

[Jax tastes the mix.]

JAX: It needs another minute.

ELA: The oats need to soften. You can eat it now if you like the texture.

JAX: The clip promises a finished dish.

ELA: It is finished. It is just chewy.

[The timer rings. Jax looks at the bowl.]

JAX: We have made breakfast in one minute.

ELA: We have prepared breakfast in one minute. It still needs to sit.

NIB: The caption can say "rest for five minutes."

JAX: That ruins the title.

ELA: Then change the title.

[Jax tries to add a bright fruit garnish. The banana slice sticks to the lens.]

NIB: We have a close-up.

JAX: We have a lens with breakfast on it.

[Ela removes the slice and wipes the lens.]

ELA: The bowl is ready now.

JAX: Can we film the taste test?

ELA: Only if the phone is clean.

[They taste. Jax nods, then reaches for the bowl. Nib moves the phone aside.]

JAX: Wait, the clip ends.

NIB: It ends with the meal being eaten.

ELA: Finally, a realistic cooking video.

[The timer rings again. The phone shuts off.]


JAX: We need a thumbnail.

NIB: The bowl is on the phone.

ELA: Move the phone away from the food.

[Jax tries to hold the phone, stir the oats, and keep the timer visible. The spoon falls.]

JAX: We have lost the spoon.

ELA: It is on the counter.

NIB: The camera captured it.

JAX: That's our first blooper.

ELA: It is also the only moment that happened at full speed.

[The editor shows the clip. It contains a title card: "BREAKFAST IN 60 SECONDS." The rest is sped up.]

JAX: It looks like I peeled the banana with my elbow.

ELA: You peeled it with your hands.

NIB: We can show the original footage.

JAX: Then viewers will see breakfast takes five minutes.

ELA: It takes five minutes to soften, and that time belongs to the oats.

[The phone timer ends. The bowl is ready.]

JAX: We have a more honest thumbnail.

NIB: What is it?

JAX: The bowl.

ELA: And the spoon.

[They put both on screen. The caption reads: "Wait for your oats."]

JAX: That is not catchy.

ELA: It is accurate.

[BLACKOUT.]
