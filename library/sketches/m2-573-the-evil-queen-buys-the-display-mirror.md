# The Evil Queen Buys the Display Mirror

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fairy-tale vanity scenes in which a mirror's flattering message is attached to the showroom display*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: QUEEN KORA, a baboon; SALES CLERK PIM, a macaque; ASSISTANT NEL, an orangutan; PORTER RUD, a gibbon.

[A furnishings showroom. Kora, wearing a small crown, stands before an ornate mirror. A gold sticker at its corner says YOU ARE THE FAIREST. Nel carries the queen's shopping list. Pim watches from the counter.]

KORA: It recognizes me.

PIM: That is our spring promotion.

KORA: The mirror has spoken the truth.

NEL: The truth appears on a sticker.

[Kora tilts her head, giving the mirror a different profile. The sticker stays where it is.]

KORA: Unwavering loyalty.

ACT ONE

[Pim offers Kora the price sheet. She looks only at its reflected image, where the numbers are backward.]

KORA: A curious code. Does it conceal the names of my rivals?

PIM: Turn the paper around and read it directly.

[Nel reads the actual price from beside Kora. Kora asks whether the mirror can be bought without anyone else looking into it. Pim gestures toward the empty transport crate behind the counter.]

PIM: Once it is packed, it will be covered until your palace staff unpack it.

KORA: Then no one can steal my title on the journey.

NEL: No one can read the sticker in the crate.

[Rud brings a padded trolley. He asks Kora to move aside while he and Pim lift the display mirror onto it. Kora steps to the smaller unlabelled mirror next to its empty stand.]

KORA: This one refuses to answer.

PIM: That one has no promotional sticker.

KORA: Perhaps it is loyal to another household.

[Nel looks into the smaller mirror. Kora moves sharply beside her, watching for a contest.]

NEL: I can see that my collar is folded.

[She straightens it. Kora watches the collar straighten in the mirror as well.]

KORA: It follows your every move.

PIM: That is what we sell mirrors for.

[Kora returns to the display mirror on the trolley. She notices the sticker lifts at one corner. Pim reaches to remove it before packing, but Kora raises a hand.]

KORA: The pledge must travel with the glass.

PIM: The adhesive may leave a mark. Would you like the sticker on a card instead?

[Kora considers. Nel takes a plain card from the counter and offers it to Pim. Pim gently peels the promotion from the corner and places it on the card. The reflected room is now uninterrupted.]

ACT TWO

[Kora looks into the clear mirror while Nel holds the card beside it.]

KORA: The title has followed you.

NEL: I am holding it. You can hold it instead.

[Nel gives Kora the card. Pim completes the sale and gives Nel the delivery receipt. Rud covers the mirror in its padding and secures it in the crate.]

KORA: Shall the palace receive word of my triumph?

NEL: It will receive the crate at three.

[Kora places the sticker card inside her crown so it will remain close. One gold corner protrudes above her ear. Pim points it out gently.]

KORA: Let them see the declaration.

PIM: They will read it backward if you stand before the next mirror.

FINAL IMAGE: Kora practices turning her crowned head so the loose gold card faces outward while Rud wheels away the silent mirror.
