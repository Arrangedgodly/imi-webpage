# Audition for the Tree

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Musical-theater auditions and demanding casting panels*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Director Mora, a langur; accompanist Pock, a macaque; actor Jemi, a capuchin; actor Tuff, a gorilla; reader Nell, a tarsier.

[STAGE: A PIANO, A MARKED AUDITION LINE, AND A FAKE TREE WITH ONE BRANCH.] 

MORA: We are casting the role of the tree in our new musical.

JEMI: The tree has a solo?

MORA: It has a long silence.

POCK: The music is one chord.

MORA: It is a meaningful chord.

JEMI: I prepared a song.

MORA: The tree does not sing.

JEMI: It can rustle.

MORA: Show us the rustle.

[JEMI SHAKES BOTH ARMS. THE FAKE LEAVES RATTLE.] 

POCK: That is a convincing windstorm.

MORA: We need a quiet tree.

JEMI: I can rustle quietly.

NELL: I will read opposite you as the squirrel.

MORA: There is no squirrel in the musical.

NELL: Then who talks to the tree?

MORA: The audience imagines it.

NELL: I can imagine loudly.

TUFF: I have auditioned for the tree.

MORA: What did you bring?

TUFF: A branch.

MORA: That is part of the set.

TUFF: It is my branch.

MORA: You cannot bring your own tree.

TUFF: It is a very small branch.

POCK: It has roots.

TUFF: I brought the roots too.

MORA: The song is about two friends learning to share shade.

JEMI: I can play the whole tree.

TUFF: I can play the shade.

NELL: I'll play a squirrel who needs shade.

MORA: There is no squirrel.

NELL: The squirrel is an audience perspective.

MORA: We do not have a squirrel perspective.

NELL: Then you have a casting problem.

[POCK PLAYS THE ONE-CHORD SONG. EVERYONE HOLDS A DIFFERENT POSE.] 

MORA: Jemi, stay still through the chord.

JEMI: I am still.

MORA: Your leaves are shaking.

JEMI: My tail is nervous.

TUFF: My branch is blocking the exit.

MORA: Trees do not block exits.

TUFF: This one does.

POCK: The chord is over.

NELL: The shade is gone.

JEMI: I can move to follow the sun.

MORA: The tree moves?

JEMI: No. The light does.

TUFF: My branch can turn toward it.

MORA: Trees do not turn.

TUFF: My branch does.

POCK: I can play the wind.

MORA: With the piano?

POCK: With a fan.

JEMI: The wind is now in the audition.

NELL: So is the audience.

MORA: The audience is still not in the cast.

NELL: They have heard the whole song.

MORA: It was one chord.

NELL: They heard it twice.

NELL: The squirrel likes this tree.

MORA: You are not cast as a squirrel.

NELL: I'm cast as the audience.

MORA: The audience is not in the cast.

POCK: It is if we sell tickets.

MORA: Fine. Jemi is the tree, Tuff is the moving shade, Nell is the audience squirrel.

JEMI: What do I sing?

MORA: Nothing.

JEMI: Can I rustle?

MORA: Once, on the chord.

[ONE CHORD. LEAVES RUSTLE ONCE. THE FAKE TREE FALLS OVER GENTLY.]
