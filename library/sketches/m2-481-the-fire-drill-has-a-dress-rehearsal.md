# The Fire Drill Has a Dress Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: emergency drills staged like theater rehearsals, with a director demanding better timing from the alarm*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: FLOOR WARDEN, a baboon; STAGE DIRECTOR, a gibbon; ACTOR, a macaque; SOUND TECH, an orangutan; USHER, a capuchin.

[A community theater. A red alarm box is mounted by the exit. The stage director holds a clipboard. The floor warden checks the clear aisle.] 

DIRECTOR: We rehearse the scene, then we rehearse the drill.

WARDEN: The drill is not a scene.

ACTOR: I am playing the person who calmly exits.

WARDEN: You are a person exiting. Please use the marked route.

[The sound tech plays an alarm cue from a speaker. The actor takes a bow instead of leaving.] 

USHER: The alarm was not applause.

ACTOR: It had a strong opening.

[The floor warden raises a hand. The tech stops the cue.] 

WARDEN: That was a rehearsal sound. We will begin the actual drill when I give the signal.

[They return to their starting marks. The actor faces the exit. The usher checks that the aisle has no props.] 

DIRECTOR: Can we make the actor's exit more urgent?

WARDEN: No running. Walk promptly.

ACTOR: Promptly, but not dramatically?

WARDEN: Correct.

[The warden explains the route and points to the meeting area outside. The stage director tries to move a prop bench into the aisle for a better camera angle. The usher stops him and moves it behind the seats.] 

USHER: The path must stay clear the whole time.

DIRECTOR: Even during the close-up?

USHER: Especially during the close-up.

[The sound tech checks the real alarm panel and confirms it is ready. The actor stands at the marked position. The warden takes the clipboard from the director and holds the route sheet.] 

WARDEN: This is the drill. Please exit by the nearest marked door and meet at the courtyard sign.

[The warden activates the real alarm. The actor and usher walk out. The director follows the marked route instead of filming. The sound tech checks the stage is clear, then joins the group in the courtyard. The warden follows them outside with the clipboard.]

DIRECTOR: We made it outside in one take.

WARDEN: You followed the route. The take does not matter.

[The warden counts everyone at the courtyard sign. The actor, usher, director, and sound tech answer their names. The warden adds their own name and records five present on the clipboard.]

WARDEN: All five accounted for. The drill is complete.

ACTOR: Do we reset?

WARDEN: No. We completed the drill and accounted for everyone.

[The group waits by the courtyard sign while the warden returns to the lobby panel, silences the alarm, and comes back outside to give the all-clear. The group walks back inside together. The warden returns the clipboard to the director only after they reach the lobby.]

DIRECTOR: I will put the courtyard sign on the call sheet.

[The sound tech leaves the theatrical alarm cue muted. The director sets the clipboard on a chair, away from the clear exit.]
FINAL IMAGE: The marked exit stays clear as the director finally sets the clipboard down.
