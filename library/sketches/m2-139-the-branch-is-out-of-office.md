# The Branch Is Out of Office

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: office auto-reply messages and leave policies during a communal tree maintenance day*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Manager Pella, a macaque; employee Jori, a gibbon; assistant Tavi, a capuchin; and automated voice.

[A DESK HAS A SIGN: "JOURNEY TO BRANCH 4." JORI PUTS ON A LEAF HAT.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Jori is out of office until the branch is repaired.

PELLA: The branch is repaired.

JORI: I am not.

TAVI: The auto-reply says you are.

[PELLA OPENS THE LAPTOP.]

PELLA: We need to update your status.

JORI: I am sitting at my desk.

TAVI: Your desk is under the branch.

[THE BRANCH CREAKS.]

JORI: I am temporarily at another desk.

[THE AUTO-REPLY SENDS A MESSAGE: "Jori is working remotely from the lower limb."]

PELLA: That is accurate.

TAVI: The lower limb has no keyboard.

[JORI SETS A LAPTOP ON THE LIMB. IT SLIDES.]

JORI: I need a stable workspace.

PELLA: The branch is stable.

[THE BRANCH SWAYS.]

TAVI: The branch is working flex hours.

[PELLA BRINGS A SMALL STOOL.]

PELLA: We can move the desk.

JORI: The desk is bolted down.

TAVI: We can move the bolt.

[A MONKEY UNSCREWS IT AND TAKES THE BOLT.]

PELLA: The desk is now unbolted.

JORI: The branch is still there.

[THE MONKEY CARRIES THE BOLT UP THE TREE.]

TAVI: The bolt has gone out of office.

[PELLA DRAFTS A NEW STATUS.]

PELLA: "Jori is available by email."

[THE LAPTOP BATTERY DIES.]

JORI: I am unavailable by email.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Jori is out of office and cannot respond.

TAVI: That sounds like a leave day.

[JORI PICKS A BETTER BRANCH AND SETTLES IN WITH A NOTEBOOK.]

PELLA: Are you back at work?

JORI: I am on a branch break.

[THE AUTO-REPLY PINGS: "Jori will respond when the branch is stable."]

TAVI: That may take all season.


[THE TEAM TRIES A NEW WORKSTATION NEAR THE TRUNK.]

TAVI: This desk is closer to the network cable.

JORI: It is also under the branch.

[THE MONKEY CLIMBS ABOVE THEM. EVERYONE LOOKS UP.]

PELLA: We can add a hard hat to the office supply list.

JORI: I do not think a hard hat is the answer.

TAVI: The auto-reply has sent another message.

[THE SCREEN READS: "Jori is available for urgent branch matters."]

JORI: What counts as urgent?

[THE BRANCH DROPS A LEAF ON THE KEYBOARD. THE LAPTOP SENDS A MESSAGE FULL OF RANDOM LETTERS.]

TAVI: The branch has replied.
[PELLA CLOSES THE LAPTOP. THE TREE STOPS SWAYING.]

JORI: I think I am back online.

TAVI: You have been offline all day.


[JORI WRITES A NOTE ON PAPER AND LEAVES IT BESIDE THE KEYBOARD.]


TAVI: The desk is clear.


[THE STATUS IS SET TO "AVAILABLE."]


[THE TREE STOPS SWAYING. THE AUTO-REPLY SENDS: "Jori is back online." JORI HAS FALLEN ASLEEP.]
