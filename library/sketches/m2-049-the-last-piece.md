# The Last Piece

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking competition shows and manufactured rivalries*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: PIPER, an excitable cooking-show host; RUMI, a macaque contestant; MOSS, a langur judge; and a FLOOR MANAGER.

[A cooking set. One ripe banana sits under a glass dome. Two plates wait beneath spotlights.]

PIPER: Chefs, tonight's challenge is to make a dish inspired by the last piece of fruit. You have one banana, two plates, and three minutes.

RUMI: Who gets the other plate?

PIPER: The judges will score presentation.

MOSS: I judge taste, texture, and whether the cook left enough for anyone else.

PIPER: That is not on the scorecard.

MOSS: Then the scorecard is missing a category.

[The floor manager starts a dramatic timer. Rumi peels the banana and divides it in two.]

PIPER: A bold choice. Rumi has split the hero ingredient.

RUMI: It was one banana. I made two servings.

PIPER: The camera needs a close-up of the better half.

RUMI: They are the same size.

PIPER: One looks more confident.

[Moss examines the plates. One half has a bruise.]

MOSS: This one is softer. It will mash well.

RUMI: I can make a mash and leave the other half plain.

PIPER: Or you could create a rivalry between the halves.

RUMI: They are not competing.

PIPER: The show needs a winner.

[The timer rings. Rumi places each half on a small leaf and passes one plate to Moss.]

MOSS: I can taste both.

PIPER: The second plate is for the camera.

RUMI: Then the camera can have a leaf.

[The floor manager places a toy crown on the uncut banana stem.]

PIPER: Our judges are ready to crown a champion.

MOSS: The fruit is gone.

PIPER: The stem still has dramatic potential.

[Moss tastes the soft half, then the firm one.]

MOSS: One is sweet and one is sweeter. The cook noticed the difference and used both.

PIPER: Can I announce a tie?

MOSS: There was no race.

[The crew looks at the empty plates. The floor manager removes the crown from the stem.]

RUMI: Is the challenge over?

PIPER: The challenge is over. The camera still wants a winner.

MOSS: Then give the camera the crown.

[The crown slides off the stem and lands on the lens hood.]

PIPER: The camera wins.

[SFX: TIMER BEEPS AGAIN.]

FLOOR MANAGER: That was the next segment.

PIPER: What is the ingredient?

FLOOR MANAGER: There is no ingredient. We used the last piece.

[Everyone looks at the empty plate.]

PIPER: We still need to announce a winner.

MOSS: The meal is finished.

FLOOR MANAGER: The camera needs a close-up for the results.

RUMI: There is nothing on the plate.

PIPER: That's a dramatic reveal.

MOSS: It is an empty plate.

[The floor manager takes the scorecard. It has categories for crunch, height, and camera appeal.]

FLOOR MANAGER: There is no score for sharing.

PIPER: Add one.

FLOOR MANAGER: That changes the results.

MOSS: The results were a banana split between two plates.

RUMI: I can make another dish next week. With two bananas.

PIPER: That is over the ingredient limit.

RUMI: Then the challenge can be to make one banana last.

[The timer rings. Everyone turns toward the empty plate.]

FLOOR MANAGER: I think the clock wants a bite.

PIPER: We will announce its score after the break.

[BLACKOUT.]
