# The Apology Carousel

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate apology videos and reputation-management language*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: CEO BRAM, a mandrill; COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTOR TAVI, a vervet; EMPLOYEE NIA, a macaque; and a live camera.

[A conference room. A potted sapling leans against a stack of branded mugs. Tavi adjusts Bram's chair until he looks remorseful but comfortable.]

TAVI: The camera is live. Begin with accountability.

BRAM: We take full responsibility for the incident.

NIA: Which incident?

TAVI: The chairs.

NIA: The chairs were sent to the wrong grove.

BRAM: We take full responsibility for the chairs being sent to the wrong grove.

TAVI: Good. Now acknowledge impact.

BRAM: The impact has been acknowledged.

NIA: By whom?

BRAM: By me, just now.

[Tavi holds up a card: LISTEN. Bram leans toward the lens.] 

BRAM: We hear you.

NIA: I haven't said anything yet.

BRAM: We are listening in advance.

[Tavi lowers the card and points at the mugs.] 

TAVI: No products in the apology shot.

BRAM: These mugs are not products. They are gifts to employees.

NIA: They say "We Listen" on one side and have a lid on the other.

BRAM: The lid is for listening privately.

[Tavi moves the mugs out of frame. The sapling tips over. Nia catches it.] 

NIA: This is from the grove where the chairs were delivered. They put it in a pot because your boxes blocked the shade.

BRAM: That is not in the approved statement.

TAVI: It may be useful to mention the affected community.

BRAM: The affected community is the tree?

NIA: The troop, the tree, and the person who ordered chairs for the other grove.

BRAM: We regret the confusion.

NIA: You sent twelve chairs to a place with no meeting room.

BRAM: We regret the chairs.

TAVI: Don't regret the chairs. Regret the outcome.

BRAM: I regret the outcome of the chairs.

NIA: Then move them back and put the sapling in the shade.

[The camera operator shifts to follow as Nia wheels the chairs toward the door. Bram remains in the chair Tavi positioned.]

TAVI: We should keep you in frame.

BRAM: I am delivering accountability from here.

NIA: You are sitting in the one chair that got sent to the right grove.

[Bram stands. The chair rolls backward and bumps the branded mugs. They clatter into a box marked RETURN TO SENDER.] 

TAVI: That's a strong visual, but we cannot use it without context.

NIA: The context is on the box.

[They carry the chairs and sapling outside. Bram tries the apology again in the grove, without backdrop or logo.] 

BRAM: We sent chairs where they were not needed. We will bring them here, ask what is useful, and stop making mugs about listening.

NIA: Better.

TAVI: Can we post that?

BRAM: After we do it.

[They lift a chair into the shade. Bram faces the camera.] 

BRAM: We will update you when the chairs arrive.

NIA: They're arriving now.

[The live camera pans to the troop sitting on the chairs, while Bram stands beside the sapling.]

TAVI: That's our first apology with no executive in the center.

BRAM: Is that good?

NIA: It's a start.
