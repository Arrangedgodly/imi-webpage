# The Forecasting Committee

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: planning meetings that predict every possible outcome but postpone the simple task on the agenda*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Odo, a gibbon committee chair; Miri, a macaque planner; Vela, a capuchin clerk; and Pavo, a squirrel monkey gardener.

INT. MEETING HUT - MORNING

Odo writes the agenda: "Move the seed basket under cover before rain." Miri adds a risk matrix. Vela brings a weather chart. Pavo waits by the door with the basket.

ODO: We should forecast every possibility before moving it.

PAVO: The rain is starting.

Miri lists drizzle, downpour, wind and unexpected sunshine. Vela draws arrows. The arrows point to every corner of the chart. Odo asks which corner has the safest shelter. Pavo points to the roof outside.

The committee begins a vote on whether the basket should be moved before or after a test. Pavo carries it to the doorway. Odo calls a recess to confirm the vote. A drop lands on the basket. Miri records it as an early data point.

PAVO: The forecast is here.

Vela opens the hut door. Wind blows the chart away. Everyone follows the chart as it slides across the clearing. Pavo keeps the basket beneath his arm. Odo catches the chart against a tree and asks whether the arrow now points to the shed.

MIRI: It points to the east.

PAVO: The shed is east.

They carry the basket to the shed. Odo asks them to wait at the threshold while the committee checks the floor for moisture. Pavo looks inside. It is dry. Miri tests the roof with a stick. No leaks. Vela checks the door latch. It works.

Odo proposes a formal motion to move the basket. Pavo walks it in. The rain begins in earnest. The seed basket is dry under the shed roof. The group stands in the doorway, still discussing the safest corner.

Vela takes the chart and draws a square around the basket's location. Miri asks if the forecast should include wind direction. Odo says yes, next meeting. Pavo puts the basket on a shelf and shuts the door.

ODO: Motion approved.

PAVO: The seeds are safe.

A gust blows the agenda off the table. It lands on the shed roof. The rain soaks the paper, dissolving the ink. Odo tries to read the agenda through the wet sheet.

MIRI: The meeting is adjourned.

The committee tests the back shelf, which is dry and level. Pavo transfers the basket there while Miri steadies it. Vela closes the vent against the gust. Odo checks the latch and nods.

Pavo carries the seed basket deeper into the shed, away from the wall. The committee follows, carrying the chart and the dripping agenda. The roof holds. Odo crosses "move basket" off the list and writes "check roof before rain" on the back of the chart.
