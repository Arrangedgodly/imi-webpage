# The Tiny Parade of Tools

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: home repair shows that introduce every household tool as a celebrity guest and keep delaying the repair*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Piri, a capuchin host; Odo, a macaque carpenter; Nima, a gibbon homeowner; and the tool cart, which rolls when unlocked.

INT. NIMA'S TREEHOUSE - DAY

Piri faces the camera beside a loose shelf.

PIRI: Today we repair one wobbly shelf with a single screw.

Odo lifts a screwdriver. Piri asks him to introduce it. Odo says it turns screws. Piri calls it a versatile veteran of the toolbox.

NIMA: Can we fix the shelf?

Piri rolls out a red carpet for the screwdriver. The tool cart begins moving downhill. Odo catches it. Piri introduces a level, then a pencil, then a small stool. Each tool gets a short entrance. Nima asks why the stool needs an entrance. Piri says it will support the repair team.

Odo checks the shelf. The screw is loose, and the bracket is tilted. He tightens the screw. The shelf straightens. Piri asks if he can demonstrate the other tools. Odo says the repair is complete.

Piri says the episode needs a reveal. Nima places a cup on the shelf. It stays level. Piri asks if the cup can be a guest star. Nima removes it and uses it to hold screws.

The tool cart is still unlocked. It rolls past the shelf and bumps the wall. A small hammer falls from the top tray. Odo catches it by the handle. Piri turns to camera.

PIRI: An unexpected tool entrance.

NIMA: Please lock the cart.

Odo locks the wheels. The cart stops. Piri asks if they can shoot a before-and-after. Nima points to the shelf's before photo, but it shows the room upside down. Piri says it was artistic. Odo flips the photo over.

Nima tests the shelf with a book, then a small pot. Both remain in place. Piri wants to put the tools in a line for the closing shot. Odo returns each tool to the cart. Piri objects that the screwdriver is hidden. Odo says it is stored safely.

The crew prepares the final shot. The camera frames the shelf, the level and the locked cart. Piri asks Odo to hold the screwdriver like a trophy. Odo sets it down and hands Nima the screw box.

ODO: Keep the spare screws in one place.

Nima labels the box and places it on the shelf. The shelf holds it. Piri calls this an encore.

NIMA: It is storage.

Piri thanks the tools by name. The cart rolls one inch, then stops at the lock. Odo raises the camera's frame to show the repair.

PIRI: Next week, we install a hook.

NIMA: How many tools?

Piri opens the tool cart. A tape measure unspools across the floor.

PIRI: We have a guest list.
