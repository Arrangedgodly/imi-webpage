# Campfire Safety Demonstration

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: outdoor survival television demonstrations where theatrical host tools are less useful than animal knowledge*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Host Rema, a gibbon; scout Piri, a macaque; safety officer Dolo, a baboon; and trainee Kip, a capuchin.

[A CAMPSITE HAS A FIRE RING, A WATER BUCKET, AND A BOX OF MATCHES.]

REMA: Tonight we demonstrate a safe campfire.

DOLO: The fire is not lit.

REMA: We will begin with the dramatic lighting.

[PIRI POINTS TO A NO-FIRE SIGN.]

PIRI: The ground is dry. We should use the lantern.

REMA: A lantern does not create atmosphere.

DOLO: It creates light.

[KIP TAKES A MATCH. DOLO TAKES IT BACK.]

DOLO: Matches stay in the box until the fire site is approved.

REMA: We are on camera.

DOLO: The ground is still dry.

[REMA PICKS UP A FLINT TOOL.]

REMA: We can make a spark.

PIRI: There is a wind warning.

[THE WIND BLOWS THE HOST'S SCRIPT INTO THE FIRE RING.]

REMA: My notes are in danger.

[KIP CATCHES THE PAPER AND PUTS IT IN THE WATER BUCKET.]

DOLO: Good response.

REMA: The demonstration has already begun.

[PIRI CHECKS THE WEATHER. THE WIND IS STRONGER.]

PIRI: We should move camp to the sheltered side.

REMA: That changes the shot.

DOLO: It improves the conditions.

[THE CREW CARRIES THE GEAR BEHIND A ROCK. REMA HOLDS UP THE LANTERN.]

REMA: We can show how to position a light safely.

KIP: I can hang it from the hook.

[KIP CLIMBS A LOW BRANCH AND HANGS THE LANTERN. IT LIGHTS THE CAMP.]

REMA: A beautifully placed flame.

DOLO: It is an LED lantern.

REMA: A beautifully placed light.

[PIRI HEARS A RUSTLE.]

PIRI: Something is near the food bag.

[THE GROUP LOOKS. A RACCOON HAS TAKEN A BANANA.]

DOLO: Secure the food.

[KIP CLOSES THE BAG AND HANGS IT FROM THE BRANCH.]

REMA: We have learned three safety rules without lighting anything.

DOLO: Four. Keep matches dry.

[REMA OPENS THE MATCH BOX. IT IS EMPTY.]

REMA: The matches are missing.

[PIRI LOOKS IN THE HOST'S POCKET. THE BOX IS THERE.]

PIRI: You put them away.

REMA: I was demonstrating storage.

DOLO: Then you can demonstrate the lantern again.

[THE CREW SITS UNDER THE ROCK SHELTER. THE LANTERN HANGS ABOVE THEM. REMA STARTS A CAMPFIRE STORY.]

REMA: There once was a host who tried to light a fire during a wind warning.

DOLO: The end.


[DOLO CHECKS THE LANTERN HOOK.]

DOLO: It is secure.

PIRI: The food is hung.

KIP: The matches are dry.

REMA: The fire is not lit.

DOLO: That is the safest demonstration we have done.

REMA: It still had a beginning and an ending.

[THE RACCOON RETURNS, SEES THE CLOSED BAG, AND LEAVES.]
KIP: I will pack the lantern first.


[EVERYONE LAUGHS. REMA USES THE WET SCRIPT TO FAN THE LANTERN.]
