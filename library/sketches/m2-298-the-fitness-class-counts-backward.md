# The Fitness Class Counts Backward

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: group exercise classes, motivational instructors, and routines that assume everyone can follow the same count*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Instructor Kett, a gibbon; participant Tula, a capuchin; participant Vex, a macaque; and music operator Omi, an orangutan.

INT. COMMUNITY GYM - MORNING

Kett stands in front of a row of exercise mats. A large screen counts down from ten.

KETT: We begin with ten easy stretches. Reach up, reach down, and feel the energy.

TULA: My tail is already stretched.

KETT: Then reach with your arms.

Omi starts the music. Vex moves one beat late. Tula moves two beats early. Kett counts, "Ten, nine, eight." The screen counts upward: one, two, three.

VEX: The screen and instructor disagree.

KETT: Your body knows the rhythm.

VEX: My body is waiting for the screen.

Kett changes the routine to a simpler side step. Tula's tail taps the next mat, which slides across the floor. Vex steps on the mat and glides toward the wall.

KETT: That is an advanced lateral move.

VEX: I did not choose it.

Omi switches the music to a slower track. Kett asks everyone to squat. Vex squats. Tula lowers herself but her tail holds her upright. The screen shows a demonstration silhouette that has no tail.

TULA: The model cannot balance.

KETT: The model is a graphic.

TULA: The model is falling.

Omi pauses the music. Kett lets the class choose the tempo. They start at a comfortable pace, using the instructor's hand signals instead of the screen. Vex follows the gestures. Tula moves her mat so her tail has room.

KETT: We will do five slow steps, then stop.

OMI: The music has a chorus.

KETT: The chorus can wait.

They step five times. Omi starts the chorus anyway. Kett pauses. The music continues without the class.

VEX: It is exercising by itself.

Omi stops the track and rewinds. The class continues without music. Kett counts backward. Everyone stops together at zero.

TULA: That was the first time the screen was right.

KETT: The screen was always counting.

VEX: It was counting something else.

Kett changes the display to show large arrows. The arrows point left. Vex, Tula, and Kett all turn left. Omi is on the other side of the room facing them and turns right.

OMI: The arrows are from my side.

The class rotates the screen. Now the arrows point the same way for everyone. Kett asks the group to end with a cool-down. Vex sits on his mat. Tula wraps her tail around it and pulls it closer.

KETT: Breathe in, breathe out.

VEX: We did that without a timer.

TULA: I can do it again.

The music starts at full volume. Everyone jumps. Omi quickly turns it down.

KETT: That is the final stretch.

VEX: The final stretch was a surprise.

They fold their mats. The screen keeps counting down. It reaches zero after the class leaves.
