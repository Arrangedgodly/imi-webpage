# The Spy Receives the Sandwich Password

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: spy thrillers in which a dead drop happens to be an ordinary lunch collection*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: AGENT KORA, an orangutan; COUNTER CLERK PIM, a capuchin; COURIER NEL, a macaque; SUPERVISOR RUD, a gibbon, voice on phone.

[A small sandwich shop. Kora wears a coat with its collar up. Pim stands behind the counter. Nel checks a collection list beside a delivery bag.]

KORA: The pigeon flies only when the fountain sleeps.

PIM: Is that the name on the order?

KORA: It is the recognition phrase.

PIM: I have Kora, Nel and a group order for Fountain Office.

[Kora looks at the chalkboard: TODAY'S SPECIAL - PIGEON PEA SANDWICH. He lowers his voice further.]

KORA: You have put the operation on the board.

PIM: It has a price so people can buy it.

ACT ONE

[Kora's phone vibrates. He answers without taking his eyes from the counter.]

RUD: Have you collected the packet?

KORA: The contact denies the phrase.

RUD: Which phrase?

KORA: The pigeon and the sleeping fountain.

RUD: That was the training exercise. Today's instructions are on the next page.

[Kora opens the folded sheet inside his coat. Its second page reads: COLLECT TEAM LUNCH. ASK FOR KORA.]

KORA: This appears to be a cover story.

RUD: It is a sandwich story. Our actual briefing is at one.

[Pim points to a brown bag marked KORA. Nel reaches for the larger Fountain Office order, leaving Kora's bag on the counter.]

KORA: The courier is extracting the target.

NEL: I am taking twelve lunches to the fountain office. Yours has your name on it.

[Kora asks Pim to open the bag where he can see it. Pim folds back its top, showing a wrapped sandwich and a napkin.]

PIM: Two items, both expected.

KORA: Could there be a message inside the wrapper?

PIM: There is the order number printed on the label.

[Kora inspects it. The label says NO MUSTARD. He calls Rud again, still holding the bag open.]

KORA: The message warns against mustard.

RUD: You requested no mustard. The briefing room has napkins if yours blows away.

[Kora ends the call. Nel lifts the group order into her delivery bag and zips it shut. Kora tries to exchange a meaningful glance. Nel checks the clock instead.]

NEL: The fountain office eats at twelve thirty. I should leave now.

ACT TWO

[Nel walks out with the correct order. Pim folds Kora's bag closed again and places it directly in his hands.]

PIM: Collected. Would you like a drink?

KORA: Something that cannot be traced.

PIM: The water jug has no till entry.

[Kora takes a paper cup, fills it from the jug and sets it on a side table while he removes his coat. With the collar lowered, he sees a tiny ticket pinned to its back: TRAINING DAY.]

KORA: Was that visible when I came in?

PIM: It helped me guess you weren't asking about pigeons.

[Kora unpins the ticket and slips it into the training sheet. His phone receives a message from Rud: BRING RECEIPT. He returns to the counter, now speaking normally.]

KORA: Could I have the receipt, please?

PIM: Certainly. The name is Kora?

KORA: Today it is.

FINAL IMAGE: Kora staples the ordinary lunch receipt to his classified training sheet before leaving with the sandwich.
