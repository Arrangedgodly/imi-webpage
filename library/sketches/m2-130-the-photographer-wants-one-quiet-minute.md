# The Photographer Wants One Quiet Minute

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wedding photography shoots that promise candid moments while staging every subject*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Photographer Miri, a macaque; couple Tavi and Sela, gibbons; assistant Olo, a capuchin; and family members.

[A GARDEN GAZEBO. MIRI SETS A CAMERA ON A TRIPOD.]

MIRI: We will capture a candid family moment. Nobody look at the camera.

[EVERYONE LOOKS AT THE CAMERA.]

OLO: They are waiting for direction.

MIRI: Then I will give no direction.

[TAVI AND SELA HOLD HANDS. A LITTLE MONKEY CLIMBS INTO THE FRAME.]

MIRI: The child is not part of the portrait.

SELA: He is my nephew.

MIRI: He can stand just outside.

[THE MONKEY MOVES CLOSER.]

TAVI: He is still outside.

[MIRI ADJUSTS THE LENS. THE FAMILY STARTS TALKING.]

MIRI: Stop. That was natural. Can you do it again?

SELA: We were talking about the cake.

MIRI: Talk about the cake again, naturally.

TAVI: It is melting.

[EVERYONE LOOKS AT THE CAKE. OLO RUNS TO MOVE IT INTO THE SHADE.]

MIRI: That was a candid moment.

[THE CAMERA'S TIMER BEEPS.]

MIRI: I was not ready.

OLO: The camera was.

[MIRI SETS A NEW TIMER. THE COUPLE HOLDS STILL.]

MIRI: Now, please relax.

TAVI: I am relaxed.

SELA: Your hand is gripping mine.

TAVI: It is the pose.

[MIRI CHANGES THE BACKDROP. A TREE BRANCH LEANS INTO THE FRAME.]

MIRI: We need to move the branch.

OLO: The branch is attached to the tree.

[THE LITTLE MONKEY CLIMBS IT AND SITS ABOVE THE COUPLE.]

MIRI: The monkey can stay if he looks away.

[THE MONKEY STARES DIRECTLY INTO THE LENS.]

SELA: He is the only one who knows where the camera is.

[MIRI TAKES A PHOTO. EVERYONE IS LAUGHING EXCEPT MIRI, WHO IS CHECKING THE SETTINGS.]

MIRI: That is the best one.

TAVI: It looks like us.

OLO: And the cake is still in the shade.

[MIRI PRINTS THE PHOTO. THE MONKEY TAKES IT, CLIMBS DOWN, AND HANDS IT TO THE COUPLE.]

MIRI: The subject has approved the candid shot.

TAVI: We approve it too.


[OLO FINDS A PHOTO OF THE COUPLE'S FIRST MEETING.]

OLO: Should we include this?

MIRI: That would make a nice posed moment.

TAVI: We met under this tree.

SELA: The branch was lower then.

[MIRI ASKS THEM TO STAND UNDER IT. THE BRANCH DROPS A LEAF ON THE CAMERA.]

MIRI: The tree has contributed.

OLO: I will wipe the lens.

[THE COUPLE LAUGHS. MIRI TAKES ANOTHER PHOTO BEFORE THE LEAF IS REMOVED.]
[MIRI HANDS THE COUPLE THEIR PRINT. THEY PLACE IT BESIDE THE WEDDING CAKE.]


OLO: The next photo can be entirely unplanned.


MIRI: That is usually when the best moment happens.


[THE FAMILY GATHERS AROUND THE PRINT AND POINTS OUT THE DETAILS.]


[TAVI AND SELA FRAME IT. MIRI ASKS EVERYONE TO LINE UP FOR A FORMAL PHOTO. THE MONKEY TAKES THE CAMERA REMOTE AND PRESSES THE BUTTON BEFORE THEY ARE READY.]
