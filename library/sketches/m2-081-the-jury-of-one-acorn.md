# The Jury of One Acorn

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: courtroom dramas and dramatic evidence presentation*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: JUDGE OBI, a macaque; ADVOCATE VEL, a gibbon; WITNESS NIM, a capuchin; and COURT CLERK PAX, a tamarin.

[A courtroom. A single acorn sits beneath a glass. The jury box is empty except for a cushion.] 

PAX: All rise for the case of the missing acorn.

OBI: Sit. The jury is one cushion and it has not reached a verdict.

VEL: Your honor, the acorn was found beneath the west bench.

NIM: It was not stolen. I put it there while opening a jar.

VEL: Why is the jar not here?

NIM: It is on the shelf. I was looking for its lid.

OBI: The court has one acorn, one empty jury seat, and a jar that is not evidence. Continue.

VEL: Did you intend to leave the acorn under the bench?

NIM: No. I forgot it when I found the jar empty.

VEL: Do you know where the lid is?

NIM: Not yet.

VEL: Was the acorn marked?

NIM: I marked it with my tooth so I would know it was mine.

VEL: That could be a signature.

NIM: It is an acorn mark. I do not have a rival.

[VEL LIFTS THE GLASS TO SHOW THE ACORN. IT ROLLS OFF THE TABLE, BOUNCES ON A CUSHION, AND STOPS IN OBI'S LAP.] 

PAX: Evidence has moved into the judge's seat.

OBI: It is a dry acorn with a small tooth mark.

VEL: The court should consider the chain of custody.

OBI: The chain is my hand. Nim says it is his. The mark agrees.

VEL: Then we must establish why it was under the bench.

NIM: I set it down while I searched for the jar lid. I found three seeds instead.

PAX: The jar contains three seeds?

NIM: Yes. The lid is still missing.

OBI: That explains the acorn's location but not the lid.

[THE CUSHION SQUEAKS AS OBI LEANS FORWARD.] 

PAX: The jury has signaled.

OBI: The cushion has squeaked. Do not interpret it until we know why.

[OBI LIFTS THE CUSHION. THE JAR LID IS UNDER IT.] 

NIM: There it is.

VEL: The jury was sitting on the lid.

OBI: The jury is a cushion. The lid was under it, not on trial.

PAX: The court has established the acorn's owner, the reason it was beneath the bench, and the lid's location.

VEL: I move that the acorn be returned to Nim.

OBI: Granted. Also return the lid to the jar before another case begins.

[PAX FETCHES THE JAR FROM THE SHELF. NIM PUTS THE LID ON IT, THEN PICKS UP THE ACORN.]

NIM: I can put the acorn beside the jar so I remember it.

VEL: The case is closed.

OBI: The case has not been opened. Clerk, why was the acorn on the evidence table?

PAX: The lost-property basket was full.

OBI: Then move the basket, not the evidence table.

[NIM USES THE ACORN TO HOLD THE COURTROOM DOOR OPEN WHILE PAX CARRIES THE BASKET OUT.] 

VEL: Your honor, the defendant is blocking the exit.

OBI: The door is free to leave.
