# Headphones for the Quiet Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: library equipment desks with lending rules that prevent anyone from using the equipment*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Sella, a macaque librarian; Rook, a capuchin visitor; Nemi, a gibbon clerk; Pavo, a lemur technician.

INT. LIBRARY QUIET ROOM - DAY

[A SIGN READS QUIET ROOM. A SECOND SIGN SAYS HEADPHONES AVAILABLE AT THE DESK. ROOK APPROACHES SELLA.]

ROOK: May I borrow headphones?

SELLA: Yes. Please whisper your request.

Rook whispers. Sella points to the desk across the room. He walks over. A sign says "Return headphones to the quiet room."

NEMI: You need a reservation.

ROOK: I just need to listen to this recording.

NEMI: We have noise-canceling, sound-isolating, and silent display models.

Rook asks for the silent ones. Nemi gives him a pair with no wire. He puts them on. Nothing happens.

ROOK: Are these working?

PAVO: They are for display.

NEMI: To borrow the working set, you need a deposit.

Rook has no coins. Sella offers a library card. Nemi swipes it. The reader beeps loudly.

SELLA: That was a loud beep.

NEMI: The reader isn't in the quiet room.

Pavo brings a noise-canceling headset. It blocks the beep, but also blocks Sella's whispered explanation. Rook asks how to turn it on. She gestures. He cannot hear her.

PAVO: The controls are on the left ear.

Rook touches the right ear. The headset plays a test tone. Everyone winces.

NEMI: Please keep the volume down.

Rook presses the control. The tone stops, and the headset cancels all sound. He smiles and presses play on his recording player, although it has not been connected to the headset. Sella asks him to return it. He can't hear her. She taps the desk. He sees her waving and removes one earcup.

ROOK: Am I in trouble?

SELLA: We need the headset back.

Rook hands it over. Sella tries it on to confirm it is quiet. She cannot hear Nemi asking for the deposit receipt. Nemi holds up a paper. Sella nods without seeing it.

Pavo checks the recording player. Its cable hangs off the desk. He takes the real headset from Sella, plugs it into the player, and sets it on the counter. The play indicator lights.

ROOK: Can I finish listening now?

NEMI: Not until the loan is entered.

PAVO: They need the quiet room to test them.

Sella asks Pavo to test the recording aloud. The room speakers crackle, and the quiet-room sign falls off its stand. Nemi lowers the volume. Rook picks up the now-connected headset, presses play, hears the closing announcement, and nods.

ROOK: It is just the library's closing announcement.

SELLA: Then we can all hear it without headphones.

NEMI: Not in the quiet room.

Sella places the working headset in the quiet room. Nemi whispers the instructions from the doorway. No one hears them.

[ROOK TAKES THE HEADSET FROM THE QUIET-ROOM TABLE AND USES ITS EARCUPS TO COVER THE CARD READER SPEAKER. THE ROOM IS FINALLY QUIET, AND NEMI STAMPS HIS LOAN SLIP.]
