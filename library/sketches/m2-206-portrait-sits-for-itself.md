# The Portrait Sits for Itself

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: formal portrait commissions whose clients keep adding symbolic objects until the sitter disappears*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Painter Oru, a capuchin; Mayor Talla, a macaque; Archivist Wen, a gibbon; and Momo, a young lemur apprentice.

INT. TOWN HALL STUDIO - DAY

Mayor Talla sits on a tall chair. A blank canvas stands before Oru. Wen holds a list of requested details.

TALLA: I asked for a portrait of me at the bridge opening.

WEN: The commission also requests civic warmth, leadership, and a visible symbol of public trust.

ORU: You can hold the bridge key.

TALLA: It is a key, not a scepter.

WEN: We have a ceremonial sash.

TALLA: The bridge opening is outdoors. I wore a jacket.

ORU: I can paint the jacket.

WEN: The sash is more legible from across the square.

MOMO: The square is outside this building.

TALLA: That will cover the jacket.

[MOMO PUTS A SMALL POTTED FERN NEXT TO THE CHAIR.]

MOMO: The office asked for a natural element.

ORU: The bridge has river reeds. We could include those.

WEN: The list says indoor plant.

TALLA: The portrait is of me outside.

ORU: I can paint the fern in the corner.

WEN: The corner is reserved for the seal.

INT. TOWN HALL STUDIO - LATER

The canvas now has a sash, key, fern, seal, and a miniature bridge. Talla is almost hidden behind the props.

TALLA: Can I see the sketch?

ORU: The eye line is good.

TALLA: I cannot see my eyes.

WEN: The portrait must represent the office, not merely the person.

TALLA: The office is not sitting in this chair.

[MOMO MOVES THE FERN. IT BLOCKS THE BRIDGE MODEL. WEN MOVES THE MODEL. IT KNOCKS THE KEY OFF THE TABLE.]

ORU: The key fell.

WEN: The key symbolizes access.

TALLA: It symbolizes that I need to pick it up.

[TALLA LEANS FORWARD TO RETRIEVE IT. ORU PAINTS A QUICK STUDY OF HER REACHING DOWN.]

ORU: There. That is a real pose.

WEN: The mayor is not supposed to be reaching under a table.

TALLA: I am doing more work in this pose than in the sash.

INT. TOWN HALL LOBBY - EVENING

The finished canvas is unveiled. Talla is seated naturally, holding the key. Behind her are the fern and a small bridge sketch. The sash hangs from the chair back.

WEN: The committee approved the symbols.

TALLA: And the portrait?

ORU: I painted the person who arrived, not the office that never sits down.

[MOMO HANGS THE CEREMONIAL SASH OVER THE FRAME. IT COVERS THE PAINTED MAYOR.]

TALLA: Move the sash.

WEN: It is part of the ceremony.

TALLA: Then the ceremony can stand behind the portrait.

[WEN MOVES THE SASH. THE MAYOR'S PAINTED FACE REAPPEARS.]

MOMO: It looks like you.

TALLA: Good. I had nearly forgotten what I was wearing.
