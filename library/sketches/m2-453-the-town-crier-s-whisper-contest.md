# The Town Crier's Whisper Contest

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public announcements and ceremonial town criers who compete to sound grand even when no one can hear them*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: CRIER ONE, a baboon; CRIER TWO, a macaque; MAYOR, an orangutan; SOUND TECH, a gibbon; MARKET SELLER, a capuchin.

[A town square. Two criers stand beside a bell. A sign reads "WHISPER ANNOUNCEMENT CHALLENGE."]

MAYOR: The challenge is to announce the market closing time quietly.

CRIER ONE: At what time?

MAYOR: Six.

CRIER TWO: The market closes at six.

MARKET SELLER: I heard "six."

CRIER ONE: That is because you are standing beside me.

SOUND TECH: The audience microphone is on the fountain side.

[The mayor points to the microphone. Crier One leans toward it and whispers. The speaker plays a loud whisper across the square.]

CRIER ONE: The market closes at six.

MARKET SELLER: It sounds like a shout wearing slippers.

CRIER TWO: I can whisper with less breath.

[He inhales deeply, cheeks puffing. His cheeks make a soft squeak.] 

SOUND TECH: The microphone heard your cheeks.

CRIER TWO: They were preparing the announcement.

[The mayor rings the bell to signal the next round. The bell is too loud. Everyone jumps.]

MAYOR: The bell was not part of the contest.

CRIER ONE: It has announced itself.

MARKET SELLER: I have closed my stall early.

[The sound tech lowers the speaker. Now the criers whisper but no one hears. The seller continues arranging fruit.]

CRIER TWO: We have achieved quiet.

CRIER ONE: We have not achieved an announcement.

[The mayor asks them to use plain voices. They both speak at once, creating a small chorus of "market closes at six." The seller hears the message and begins packing.] 

MAYOR: One at a time.

CRIER ONE: The market closes at six.

MARKET SELLER: Thank you. I can finish counting.

CRIER TWO: I will announce the rain delay next.

MAYOR: There is no rain delay.

[The crier points to the blue awning, which has collected a drip. The sound tech checks the sky; a sprinkler is watering the square from above.] 

SOUND TECH: The sprinkler timer is on.

MARKET SELLER: It is not rain. I will keep the fruit out.

CRIER ONE: The market remains open until six, unless the sprinkler makes it wet.

MAYOR: That is a different announcement.

[The mayor turns off the sprinkler. The criers lower their voices, this time without the mic. The seller repeats the closing time to a customer.]


[The mayor asks each crier to repeat the closing time once, without a bell or speaker.]

CRIER ONE: The market closes at six.

CRIER TWO: The market closes at six.

The seller hears both and looks up at the clock. It is five minutes to six. She starts folding her awning, not because of the volume but because she heard the words. The mayor names Crier Two winner for clarity. Crier One asks if the contest had a volume score.

MAYOR: It was a whisper contest.

CRIER ONE: Then I won the quietest.

SOUND TECH: The square heard Crier Two.

FINAL IMAGE: Both criers stand quietly beside the silent bell while the seller closes the stall at six.
