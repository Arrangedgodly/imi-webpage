# The Reenactors Need a Historically Accurate Exit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: historical reenactments that value period detail over practical stage logistics*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Director Venn, a baboon; actor Seli, a gibbon; actor Daro, a macaque; and stagehand Odo, a capuchin.

EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - AFTERNOON

A small reenactment set shows a medieval market. Venn holds a script. Seli wears a long cape; Daro carries a wooden cart.

VENN: At the end, the villagers leave through the north gate.

ODO: The north gate is the portable toilet.

VENN: Then they leave through the east gate.

DARO: The east gate is a hedge.

SELI: Historically, the town had a wall.

ODO: Historically, the hedge was not here.

[Odo sets a painted cardboard doorway beside the hedge.]

VENN: That is not authentic.

ODO: Neither is the portable toilet.

EXT. MARKET SET - MOMENTS LATER

Venn stages a dramatic arrival. Daro pushes the cart. Its wooden wheel catches on a modern cable ramp.

DARO: The cart refuses the century.

VENN: The wheel is supposed to break at the bell.

SELI: We have not reached the bell.

[The wheel comes loose early and rolls toward the audience. Odo catches it with a mop.]

ODO: The wheel has reached the audience.

VENN: Can we make the mop look period?

ODO: It is already behind the tavern.

[He hides it behind a barrel painted to resemble a tavern.]

EXT. MARKET SET - LATER

Seli rehearses a speech from the balcony. Her cape catches on the pulley used to raise the town banner.

SELI: Citizens, we must defend the gate!

[The banner rises, pulling Seli gently upward until she hangs a foot above the stage.]

DARO: The citizens are losing their speaker.

VENN: Keep the speech going. It is a powerful ascent.

SELI: I am not ascending by choice.

ODO: The pulley is from the fairground stage.

VENN: Can we call it a siege engine?

ODO: Only if the siege engine has a release latch.

[Odo lowers Seli. Venn adds a line to the script: THE TOWN'S BANNER LIFTS THE BRAVEST CITIZEN.]

EXT. MARKET SET - PERFORMANCE

The audience watches. Daro rolls the cart to the mark; Odo has secured the wheel. Seli gives her speech and exits through the cardboard doorway.

VENN: The scene works.

DARO: The gate is cardboard.

VENN: It is the least dangerous gate.

[The banner rises on cue. It lifts the town sign, which has been tied to the pulley. The sign reads VILLAGE GREEN PARKING THIS WAY and points directly at the audience.]

SELI: The townspeople have found the car park.

VENN: That is not in the chronicle.

ODO: It is in the event map.

[The audience follows the arrow toward the car park. Venn remains beside his authentic market cart, now blocking the exit.]

VENN: The reenactment is over.

DARO: The traffic has begun.

VENN: Historically, it is a procession.
