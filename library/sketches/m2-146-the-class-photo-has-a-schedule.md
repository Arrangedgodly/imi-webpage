# The Class Photo Has a Schedule

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school picture day procedures and posed perfection disrupted by genuine monkey behavior*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Photographer Lumo, a gibbon; teacher Pavi, a macaque; student Tavi, a capuchin; and class.

[A BACKDROP HAS A PAINTED SCHOOLHOUSE. STUDENTS LINE UP.]

LUMO: First row sits. Second row stands.

PAVI: Please keep your hands in your lap.

[TAVI HOLDS A BANANA.]

PAVI: Put the snack away.

TAVI: It is not a snack. It is a prop.

LUMO: We do not have props.

[TAVI SETS IT ON THE BACKDROP. IT ROLLS DOWN.]

LUMO: The backdrop is not level.

PAVI: Neither is the class.

[THE PHOTOGRAPHER ADJUSTS THE LIGHT. A STUDENT SQUINTS.]

LUMO: Open your eyes.

STUDENT: The light is bright.

PAVI: Use the window light.

LUMO: That changes the setup.

[THE CLASS MOVES TO THE WINDOW. THE STUDENTS ARRANGE THEMSELVES.]

LUMO: Everyone look at the lens.

[TAVI LOOKS AT THE BANANA. THE CLASS TURNS TO SEE IT.]

LUMO: Keep looking forward.

PAVI: The banana is rolling again.

[TAVI CATCHES IT WITH HIS FOOT.]

LUMO: That was a good pose.

[THE CAMERA TAKES A PHOTO. EVERYONE IS LOOKING AT THE BANANA EXCEPT LUMO.]

PAVI: That one is honest.

LUMO: The school wants a traditional photo.

TAVI: This is our class.

[THE PHOTOGRAPHER SETS A TIMER. HE RUNS INTO THE GROUP. THE STUDENTS MAKE ROOM FOR HIM.]

LUMO: I am in the picture.

PAVI: You are part of the class today.

[THE PHOTO PRINTS. THE PHOTOGRAPHER HANDS IT TO THE TEACHER.]

LUMO: I have cut off half my head.

TAVI: We can take another.

[THE STUDENTS SCOOT TO FIT HIM IN. THE BANANA ROLLS INTO THE FRONT ROW.]

PAVI: Ready?

[THE CLASS LOOKS AT THE CAMERA. LUMO LOOKS AT THE BANANA.]

TAVI: You are part of the class today.


[PAVI CHECKS THE ROSTER.]

PAVI: We are missing one student.

[THE CLASS LOOKS AROUND. THE STUDENT IS BEHIND THE BACKDROP.]

LUMO: I can add them to the end.

TAVI: There is no end. We are in a row.

[THE BACKDROP FALLS FORWARD. THE HIDDEN STUDENT CATCHES IT.]

PAVI: Please hold it steady.

STUDENT: I am holding the whole schoolhouse.

LUMO: That is a strong pose.

[THE PHOTOGRAPHER ADJUSTS THE BACKDROP. THE GROUP REFORMS WITH THE STUDENT IN PLACE.]

PAVI: We can take the picture now.

[TAVI SETS THE BANANA ON THE FLOOR. THE CLASS CAREFULLY LEAVES A SPACE AROUND IT.]

LUMO: Why is there a gap?

TAVI: The banana is in the class.

[THE PHOTOGRAPHER LOOKS AT THE FRAME, THEN MOVES THE CAMERA LOWER.]

LUMO: We can fit everyone.
[THE TEACHER SIGNS THE PRINT ORDER.]

TAVI: We should send one to every family.

LUMO: Even the banana?

PAVI: Especially the banana. The whole class.

LUMO: The schoolhouse fits after all.

[THE CAMERA FLASHES. THE PRINTED PHOTO IS SENT HOME WITH THE STUDENTS.]
