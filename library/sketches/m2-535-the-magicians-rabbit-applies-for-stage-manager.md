# The Magician's Rabbit Applies for Stage Manager

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: backstage job interviews, magic-show secrecy, and assistants who know where every prop is hidden*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: MAGICIAN, a baboon; RABBIT, a capuchin; PRODUCER, a gibbon; STAGEHAND, an orangutan; AUDITIONER, a macaque.

[A backstage interview table. A top hat sits between the magician and producer. The rabbit sits in a small chair with a resume.]

PRODUCER: Why do you want to stage-manage?

RABBIT: I know every entrance, cue, and hidden compartment.

MAGICIAN: The rabbit is part of the act.

RABBIT: I have been part of the act for six years.

[A stagehand wheels in a box. The magician asks the rabbit to jump inside. The rabbit opens the box first and removes a clipboard.]

ACT ONE

AUDITIONER: What would you do if a prop vanished?

RABBIT: Check the magician's left sleeve.

[The magician pulls a bouquet from his right sleeve.]

RABBIT: The other left.

[He switches hands, revealing the bouquet from his left sleeve. The producer takes notes.]

PRODUCER: What if the curtain cue is late?

RABBIT: I would hold the reveal until the crew is ready.

MAGICIAN: That is not how the trick works.

RABBIT: The audience can wait six seconds.

[The stagehand sets a marked box beside the table. The rabbit checks the label and turns it toward the back wall.]

STAGEHAND: Why did you turn the box?

RABBIT: The label says FRAGILE. The magician keeps sitting on it.

[The magician starts to protest, then looks at the dent in the lid.]

MAGICIAN: It is part of the illusion.

RABBIT: The illusion is that the stool holds your weight.

[The producer asks for a sample cue. The rabbit taps the table twice. The magician pulls out a dove, then reaches for the wrong hat. The rabbit moves the correct hat into place without standing.]

PRODUCER: How did you know?

RABBIT: He does the trick in the same order every night.

[The auditioning macaque is asked to demonstrate a stage entrance. They walk on during the blackout and collide gently with the magician's cape. The rabbit calls a halt, checks the floor, and asks the stagehand to move a cable.]

RABBIT: The entrance is clear now.

MAGICIAN: I was going to do that.

RABBIT: You were standing on the cable.

ACT TWO

[The producer offers the rabbit the job. The magician asks who will now sit inside the hat. The rabbit hands him the clipboard and points to a new line on the schedule: MAGICIAN MOVES OWN PROPS.]

MAGICIAN: That is a difficult cue.

RABBIT: I will call it slowly.

[The producer announces the next show. The magician tries to disappear into the hat, but the rabbit checks the lid and removes the clipboard first.]

FINAL IMAGE: The rabbit stands at the stage manager's desk while the magician waits beside the correctly labeled box.
