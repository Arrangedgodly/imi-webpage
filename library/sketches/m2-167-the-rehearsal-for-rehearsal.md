# The Rehearsal for Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: theater rehearsals that schedule preparation for the preparation until the show is already underway*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Lali, a gibbon director; Pavo, a macaque stage manager; Fenn, a capuchin actor; and Nara, a baboon who plays a tree.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - AFTERNOON

Pavo claps twice. Fenn enters, holding a script.

PAVO: This is the rehearsal before the rehearsal.

FENN: Should I perform?

LALI: No. We are practicing getting ready to perform.

Nara stands in a cardboard tree costume. She asks if she should be in the room. Lali says the cast should rehearse entering the room. Everyone exits, then returns. Nara's cardboard branches catch in the doorway.

NARA: The forest is stuck.

PAVO: We can rehearse the release.

Lali tells Fenn to read the first line as if he has never seen it. Fenn opens the script and reads, "The gate is open." Lali asks for more uncertainty. Fenn looks at the door, which is closed.

FENN: The gate may be open.

Lali asks Pavo to note the emotional shift. Pavo writes "door confusion." Nara turns sideways to get free. The cardboard branches scrape the frame and make a sound like applause.

LALI: Good. We have a response from the audience.

PAVO: That was the wall.

Lali schedules a rehearsal to practice receiving applause. Fenn bows. Nara cannot bow because the costume blocks her. Pavo suggests she nod instead. The cardboard branches sway and hit Fenn on the head. He bows again, this time to avoid the branch.

LALI: That is the most honest bow we have.

A real audience enters. They have arrived for the performance. Pavo checks the schedule. The rehearsal for the rehearsal has run into the curtain time.

PAVO: We should start the show.

LALI: We have not rehearsed the transition.

FENN: The gate is open.

Nara backs through the doorway. The costume catches again, but this time Fenn holds the door while Pavo lifts the cardboard branch. She enters the stage. The audience applauds the practical teamwork.

LALI: That applause is early.

PAVO: It is still applause.

Lali takes her position. Fenn delivers his line: "The gate is open." Nara steps forward and the cardboard forest blocks the stage entrance. The actors rotate her gently until the trunk faces front.

FENN: The forest has arrived.

NARA: It was here for the rehearsal.

The audience laughs. Lali whispers to Pavo that they should rehearse the laugh. Pavo points to the clock. The show is live. Lali takes a bow. Nara's branches clap together and accidentally provide the finale.

PAVO: Do we rehearse the curtain call?

Pavo checks the original schedule. The audience is still seated, and the cast is still on stage. He crosses out the rehearsal slot and writes performance in progress. Lali asks whether that needs a rehearsal. Pavo puts the clipboard away.

LALI: We have performed it.
