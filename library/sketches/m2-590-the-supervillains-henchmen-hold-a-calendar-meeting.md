# The Supervillain's Henchmen Hold a Calendar Meeting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: villainous organizations that turn world domination into recurring meeting invites*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: BOSS VELA, a baboon; HENCHMAN PIP, a capuchin; HENCHMAN RIKO, a macaque; ASSISTANT NARA, an orangutan.

[A lair with a dark map, a dramatic chair and a whiteboard calendar. Vela stands at the head of the table. Pip and Riko sit with notebooks.]

VELA: Tomorrow, we conquer the city.

NARA: The room is booked for the fire drill.

ACT ONE

[Vela points to the calendar. The plan is written on three overlapping sticky notes. Pip cannot tell which date is current.]

PIP: Is the conquest Tuesday or the rehearsal Tuesday?

RIKO: There are two Tuesdays.

[Vela asks Nara to cancel the fire drill. Nara checks the building schedule. The lair is in the basement of city hall; the drill is mandatory. Vela says they will reschedule the conquest. Nara opens the calendar app. It creates a meeting called RESCHEDULE CONQUEST about rescheduling the conquest.]

VELA: Decline the meeting.

NARA: You are the organizer.

[She passes Vela the tablet. Vela presses DECLINE. The meeting disappears, then reappears as a weekly series.]

ACT TWO

[Pip suggests a paper calendar. Riko writes "Conquer City" on Wednesday. Nara says Wednesday is the parade. Vela changes it to Thursday. Pip's dentist appointment is there; he asks if the conquest can start after lunch.]

VELA: We are not planning around dental care.

PIP: I have the keys to the tunnel.

[They move the event to Friday. Riko points out that he is assigned to guard the device on Friday. Vela asks why the guard schedule overlaps the conquest. Nara says both events use the same three people and the same tunnel.]

[The villain draws a new plan with arrows. The arrows cross every calendar block. Nara rotates the whiteboard to show another month. It is already full of "meeting to discuss plans." Riko removes one sticky note. A secret passage appears on the map.]

RIKO: We can use this route.

VELA: That passage is for the fire marshal.

NARA: The drill includes it.

[The fire drill alarm chirps once. Everyone stands. Vela stays seated, then sees the others heading to the stairs.]

ACT THREE

[Nara starts the official drill timer. Vela appoints Pip as the meeting organizer and Riko as the route lead. They walk the tunnel to the marked exit, carrying the calendar tablet. At the exit, Nara checks the attendance list. All three henchmen are present. The villain asks if the city is conquered. Nara says the drill is complete.]

VELA: Then our plan worked.

RIKO: We reached the exit on schedule.

[Pip opens the tablet. It displays a notification: CONQUEST MEETING STARTS NOW. Vela taps JOIN. Nara asks if the city is part of the meeting. Vela looks at the invitation. It is only the four of them.]

NARA: At least the room is available.

[They sit at the curb and open the calendar. Vela schedules a new planning meeting for next week, after the drill. Pip adds a fifteen-minute limit. Riko adds a task to bring the tunnel map. Nara checks whether anyone else is invited. Vela writes "city" in the goals field, not the attendee list. The invite sends. All four devices chime at once.]

PIP: We have been summoned.

VELA: By me.

[She accepts the invite. Nara closes the tablet before the meeting begins.]

FINAL IMAGE: The henchmen march toward the lair with an approved calendar invite, while the city remains unconquered behind them.
