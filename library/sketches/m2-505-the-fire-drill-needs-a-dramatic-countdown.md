# The Fire Drill Needs a Dramatic Countdown

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: emergency drills, action-movie countdowns, and safety presenters who turn a routine exit into a spectacle*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: SAFETY OFFICER, a baboon; TEACHER, an orangutan; STUDENT, a gibbon; ANNOUNCER, a capuchin; CUSTODIAN, a macaque.

[A classroom. The safety officer holds a clipboard beside a harmless practice alarm. The teacher opens the door.]

SAFETY OFFICER: When the bell rings, walk calmly to the marked exit.

ANNOUNCER: We are ready for the drill.

[The student checks a small stopwatch.]

STUDENT: Is there a countdown?

SAFETY OFFICER: The bell is the signal. We leave when it rings.

STUDENT: A countdown builds tension.

TEACHER: We are practicing an exit, not launching a rocket.

[The custodian tests the alarm. A short bell rings. The students stand and walk to the door. The safety officer checks the aisle.]

SAFETY OFFICER: Good pace. Keep the path clear.

ANNOUNCER: We have reached the hallway.

STUDENT: That was quick.

ACT ONE

[The announcer holds up a red card that says DRAMATIC REPLAY. The safety officer lowers it.]

SAFETY OFFICER: We do not replay the alarm for entertainment.

ANNOUNCER: The audience did not see the exit.

TEACHER: They are in the hallway.

[The student walks back into the room to retrieve the stopwatch. The safety officer points to the exit route.]

SAFETY OFFICER: The drill is complete. Please stay with the group.

STUDENT: My stopwatch is still in there.

[The teacher guides the student to wait while the custodian retrieves it from the desk. The student peers through the door.]

STUDENT: The tension is enormous.

CUSTODIAN: It is on the desk next to a pencil.

[The custodian returns with the stopwatch. The student stops it. The display reads 00:00.]

STUDENT: The drill was too fast for timing.

SAFETY OFFICER: That means everyone followed the route.

[The announcer asks for a second take, this time with a smoke machine. The safety officer covers the machine with a towel and checks the alarm switch.]

SAFETY OFFICER: No smoke. We use a clear path and a real signal.

ANNOUNCER: Could there be dramatic music?

TEACHER: The alarm is the music.

[The student offers to count down while the officer tests the bell. The officer lets the student hold the clipboard, then sets the safety route sign at eye level.]

STUDENT: Five, four, three-

SAFETY OFFICER: Stop. We are not waiting for zero.

[The student stops counting. The teacher asks everyone to stand away from the door. The custodian presses the bell once.]

ACT TWO

[The class walks in a line to the hallway. The student carries the stopwatch and keeps it in hand. The teacher follows last. The safety officer checks the room, then joins them.]

SAFETY OFFICER: Exit clear. Group accounted for.

ANNOUNCER: That is the whole drill?

SAFETY OFFICER: That is the successful drill.

[The student checks the stopwatch. It shows 00:18. The announcer looks disappointed, then uses the timer to schedule a three-minute recap.]

ANNOUNCER: The recap will be longer than the evacuation.

FINAL IMAGE: The students stand calmly by the exit while the announcer queues dramatic music for the recap and nobody listens.
