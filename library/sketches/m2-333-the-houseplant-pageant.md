# The Houseplant Pageant

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: beauty pageants that force contestants to answer impossible questions about their talents*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Host Miri, a gibbon; judge Piri, a capuchin; contestant Nara, a macaque; contestant Odo, a baboon.

INT. GREENHOUSE - DAY

Two potted plants sit beneath a banner: MISS GREENHOUSE. Miri holds a microphone.

MIRI: Contestants, tell us what makes you unique.

NARA: I lean toward the light.

ODO: I have three new leaves.

Piri studies the scorecard.

PIRI: We are judging confidence, presentation, and a question about community service.

Miri asks the fern what it would do to improve the village. Nara answers for it: it would provide shade. Odo says his plant would improve the village by standing near a window. Piri awards both a point for optimism.

The talent round begins. Nara's plant has a broad leaf. Odo's has a sturdy stem. Miri asks them to demonstrate a special skill. Nara rotates the pot so the leaves face the sun. Odo's plant drops a dry leaf.

MIRI: A daring shed.

PIRI: It was a dead leaf.

Odo says the plant is making room for growth. Nara says hers has been growing all week. Miri asks them to perform a walk across the stage. Nara's pot is heavy. Odo's pot slides when the fan turns on. Piri tells the contestants not to move the plants without permission.

The final question asks what beauty means. Nara says healthy roots. Odo says a good drainage hole. Piri says the answer is practical but lacks sparkle. Miri lifts a small watering can like a trophy.

A loud fan rattles. The plants lean. Nara moves the pots away from the draft. Odo asks if that counts as community service. Piri says they have protected each other from the fan.

Piri asks each plant to receive one point for a helpful trait. Nara says her plant's wide leaves catch light. Odo says his plant's roots hold the soil. Piri asks how the contestants serve the community. Odo's plant is on a low shelf where smaller visitors can see it. Nara's plant sits near a sunny window and brightens the room. Miri awards equal points. The fan blows again and Odo's dry leaf drops onto the stage. Miri asks if it is part of the talent. Odo picks it up and puts it in the compost bowl. Nara turns her pot so the new leaves face the light. The judges agree that both plants are doing useful things without competing.

The pageant ends with both plants beside the window, where they can get light.

Miri returns the crown to the greenhouse shelf.

Odo waters the plant carefully.

The leaves face the sun.

Miri claps once.
