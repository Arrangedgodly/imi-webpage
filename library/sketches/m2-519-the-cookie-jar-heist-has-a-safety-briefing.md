# The Cookie Jar Heist Has a Safety Briefing

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: elaborate heist films, crew role assignments, and tense plans for an absurdly low-stakes target*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: PLANNER, a baboon; LOOKOUT, a gibbon; LOCKPICK, a macaque; OWNER, an orangutan; NIGHT CLEANER, a capuchin.

[A break room after hours. A cookie jar sits on a counter beneath a sign: COOKIES FOR TOMORROW. The planner opens a notebook.]

PLANNER: We enter, take one cookie, and leave the room unchanged.

LOOKOUT: The room is empty.

LOCKPICK: The jar has a lid.

PLANNER: That is why we have a specialist.

[The lockpick examines the jar. It has no lock, just a loose glass lid.]

LOCKPICK: The security system is a lid.

PLANNER: Do not underestimate it.

[The lookout peers through the door window. The night cleaner pushes a cart past the hall and pauses.]

LOOKOUT: We have movement.

NIGHT CLEANER: I am mopping.

[The planner signals for everyone to freeze. The cleaner continues down the hall.]

ACT ONE

[The lockpick lifts the lid. It makes a small clink. The lookout flinches. The planner catches the lid before it falls.]

PLANNER: No sound.

LOCKPICK: It is a glass jar.

[The planner takes out a cloth and places it under the lid. The lockpick lifts again. The lid is quiet. Inside, the cookies are wrapped individually.]

LOOKOUT: They are bagged.

PLANNER: We adapt.

[The planner uses a small hook to pull one wrapped cookie toward the rim. It catches on the jar's paper label.]

LOCKPICK: The label is blocking the exit.

[The lookout keeps watch. The cleaner's cart returns, and a mop bucket squeaks at each wheel turn. The planner mistakes the sound for an alarm and drops the hook. It lands inside the jar.]

PLANNER: We are compromised.

NIGHT CLEANER: You are in the break room.

[The cleaner enters, turns on the lights, and reads the sign. The planner puts both hands up.]

NIGHT CLEANER: Those cookies are for tomorrow's meeting.

LOOKOUT: We planned to take one.

OWNER: That is still taking one.

[The owner enters holding a lunchbox. The jar belongs to them. They lift the lid and take out one cookie.]

OWNER: You can have one if you ask.

[The lockpick puts the hook away. The planner closes the notebook.]

[The owner asks each crew member to choose one cookie. The planner counts the wrapped pieces aloud, then stops at four.]

PLANNER: There are five of us.

OWNER: The fifth is the one I kept.

[The lookout nods and turns the notebook over, where a page says "one cookie for everyone who asks." The owner hands the crew one cookie each. The cleaner wipes the counter where the jar sat.]

ACT TWO

[The owner hands one cookie to each of the crew. The cleaner wipes the counter where the jar sat. The planner checks the room and returns the jar to its original spot.]

PLANNER: The plan is revised. Ask before entering.

LOOKOUT: That is a shorter plan.

[Everyone takes one cookie. The owner closes the jar, which makes the same clink. The crew jumps in unison.]

FINAL IMAGE: The cookie jar sits untouched on the counter while five monkeys stand around it chewing.
