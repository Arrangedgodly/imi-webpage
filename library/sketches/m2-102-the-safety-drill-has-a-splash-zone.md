# The Safety Drill Has a Splash Zone

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: safety demonstrations and competitive emergency training*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: TRAINER VEE, a gibbon; DEMONSTRATOR OBI, a macaque; TRAINEE LUX, a capuchin; and a small water basin.

[A training mat surrounds a basin. Vee holds a whistle and a laminated checklist. A scoreboard reads RESCUES: VEE 0, OBI 0, LUX 0. A gold badge hangs above it.]

VEE: Today's drill covers what to do if someone slips near the water dish. Most rescues wins the gold badge. No points for merely preventing an accident.

OBI: First, check that the path is clear. Then offer a hand or get an adult helper.

LUX: What if the helper slips too?

VEE: There is a second helper.

LUX: And if the second helper slips?

VEE: Each rescued helper earns another point. Unfortunately the drill does not have an unlimited cast.

[OBI WALKS TOWARD THE BASIN. HIS FOOT SKIDS ON A WET LEAF.] 

OBI: I am pretending to slip.

VEE: Good. Trainee, respond.

LUX: I will pick up the leaf.

OBI: Before checking whether I am hurt?

LUX: You said the path should be clear.

VEE: Both are important. Ask if the person is okay before moving them.

LUX: Are you okay?

OBI: I am fine.

LUX: Then I can move the leaf.

[THE LEAF IS LIFTED. WATER DRIPS FROM IT.] 

VEE: Why is the leaf wet?

OBI: I dipped it in the basin for the demonstration.

VEE: That was not in the plan. You were supposed to fall before adding realism.

OBI: The wet leaf made the hazard visible. I saw your zero on the scoreboard and wanted to give you a chance.

LUX: Now the floor is wet too.

VEE: We need to dry the mat before repeating the drill.

LUX: I can fetch a cloth.

OBI: I will stand by the basin and keep it from tipping.

VEE: That is teamwork. I will supervise it from beside the scoreboard.

[THEY DRY THE MAT. THE BASIN WOBBLES.] 

LUX: The basin is not stable.

VEE: Secure it on a flat surface.

OBI: We can move it to the low table.

LUX: Then the path is clear and the water stays contained.

VEE: The drill is complete.

LUX: Do I get a badge?

VEE: You get a dry mat. You eliminated the only rescue opportunity without rescuing anyone.

OBI: That is safer.

[THE WHISTLE SLIPS FROM VEE'S HAND AND LANDS IN THE BASIN.]

LUX: I can bring the scoop.

VEE: The drill should show a safe way to retrieve an object from water. My whistle has entered the water voluntarily. It counts as a swimmer.

OBI: We can set the scoop beside the basin before we start.

LUX: What if the water is too deep to reach?

VEE: Don't lean over or climb into it. Get an adult and use a proper tool.

OBI: The basin is shallow and stable. I can use the scoop from the floor.

[THE SCOOP LIFTS THE WHISTLE. WATER DRIPS INTO THE BASIN.] 

LUX: The whistle is out, and the floor is dry.

VEE: Good. Now put the scoop away so nobody trips on it.

LUX: Should I blow the whistle?

VEE: Only if we need a signal.

[LUX BLOWS IT. THE SOUND STARTLES OBI, WHO JUMPS BACK.] 

OBI: The drill has created a new hazard. Does that give me a point?

LUX: You jumped over a dry mat. The whistle was the only thing I rescued.

VEE: Equipment cannot be a swimmer when someone else rescues it. I award myself the badge for supervising the rescue.

OBI: Then I award myself a point for rescuing you from a zero score.

LUX: I will put the badge somewhere neither of you can reach while arguing.

VEE: Hand it over. This is an awards ceremony, not a practical demonstration.

[VEE AND OBI REACH FOR THE BADGE. LUX SETS IT ON THE BASIN'S RIM. THE BADGE DROPS INTO THE WATER. BOTH DEMONSTRATORS FREEZE.]

LUX: Don't lean over. Get a proper tool.

[LUX LIFTS THE BADGE WITH THE SCOOP AND PINS IT ON.]

LUX: That's two rescues. Unless awards cannot be swimmers either.

[VEE ERASES THE SCOREBOARD. OBI BLOWS THE WET WHISTLE; IT MAKES A SMALL BUBBLE.]
