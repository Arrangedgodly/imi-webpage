# The Cooking Show Has Two More Minutes

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking competitions, timed recipe reveals, and television hosts who insist every dish is nearly ready*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: HOST, a macaque; CHEF, a baboon; JUDGE, an orangutan; CAMERA OPERATOR, a gibbon; TIMER, a capuchin.

[A bright cooking set. A pan sizzles. A countdown clock reads 00:02:00. The host faces the camera with a wooden spoon.] 

HOST: Two minutes remain. Our chef has chosen a classic: toast.

CHEF: It is in the toaster.

HOST: Can you plate it with a flourish?

CHEF: I can wait for it to pop.

[The camera operator rolls in for a close-up of the toaster. The judge sniffs the air.] 

JUDGE: I detect toast, anticipation, and a burnt corner from yesterday.

CHEF: Yesterday's corner is not part of today's dish.

TIMER: The clock is at one minute fifty-eight.

HOST: We need a twist.

CHEF: The twist is that the toaster is functioning.

[The host opens a cabinet and pulls out a jar of sprinkles. The chef takes it away.] 

CHEF: This is toast, not a birthday cake.

HOST: A sprinkle could lift the dish.

JUDGE: A sprinkle could fall off the plate.

ACT ONE

[The toast pops up. The camera operator zooms in. The chef reaches for a plate, but the host blocks the shot with a decorative herb.] 

HOST: Can we get a slow-motion reveal?

CHEF: It is bread with heat on it.

[The chef places the toast on a plate. One corner is slightly darker than the rest. The judge leans close.] 

JUDGE: The dark edge has a bold finish.

CHEF: It is burnt.

HOST: We call that a smoky crust.

[The timer flips to 00:45. The host gestures toward a bowl of jam.] 

HOST: Our chef can still add a sauce.

CHEF: Jam goes on the toast. That is the recipe.

HOST: Then make the application theatrical.

[The chef dips the spoon into jam and draws one straight stripe. The host asks for a spiral. The chef turns the plate once; the stripe curls around the toast and lands on the table.] 

CAMERA OPERATOR: The table is now plated.

JUDGE: I will not taste the table.

[The host wipes the jam with a napkin, making a red smear across the spotless counter.] 

HOST: A rustic flourish.

CHEF: A napkin emergency.

ACT TWO

[The timer reaches zero. The host holds up the toast, now cleanly plated with a small cup of jam beside it.] 

HOST: Our time is up. Judge?

JUDGE: Crisp center, respectable jam, and one dark edge that tells the truth.

CHEF: It is toast.

JUDGE: It is very good toast.

[The host sets the dish down. The timer beeps again because it has been set for the next round.] 

TIMER: Two minutes.

HOST: What is the next dish?

CHEF: Another slice of toast.

[The host turns to camera and whispers.] 

HOST: We have a trilogy.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera operator frames the jam cup like a grand prize while the chef loads one more slice into the toaster.
