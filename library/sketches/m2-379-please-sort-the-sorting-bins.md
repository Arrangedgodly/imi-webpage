# Please Sort the Sorting Bins

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: recycling systems with overlapping labels and color rules that make disposal harder than the packaging*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Ivo, a gibbon custodian; Petra, a lemur visitor; Zed, a macaque facilities lead; Fip, a capuchin volunteer.

INT. COMMUNITY CENTER - DAY

[FOUR BINS STAND IN A ROW. THEIR LABELS READ PAPER, PAPER WITH FOOD, CLEAN PAPER, AND OTHER PAPER.]

PETRA: Which bin takes this paper cup?

IVO: Is it clean?

PETRA: It held tea.

IVO: Then it is paper with food.

ZED: Cups go in the lined bin.

FIP: Which one is lined?

Zed opens each bin. They all have a liner. The paper-with-food bin's liner is green; the others are clear.

ZED: The green liner is for food.

IVO: The cup has no food in it.

PETRA: It has tea stains.

FIP: Tea is food.

ZED: The sign says "paper with food." It does not say beverages.

Ivo points to a fifth bin tucked behind the others. Its sign reads BEVERAGE PAPER.

PETRA: How long has that been there?

IVO: Since we installed the other four bins.

Zed pulls the fifth bin forward. It is full of clean paper cups. The labels now face away from the room.

FIP: We need to rotate them.

Zed turns the bins so every label faces the doorway. The doorway is narrow. The bins block it.

PETRA: We could move the labels to the front.

Ivo peels off one label. It sticks to his glove. He reaches for the clean-paper bin, and the label transfers to it.

IVO: This bin is now paper with food.

ZED: The bin is empty.

IVO: The label says otherwise.

Fip takes the cup from Petra and tries the beverage-paper bin. A flap covers the opening. The cup will not fit.

FIP: The slot is too small.

ZED: It is designed for flat paper.

PETRA: Cups aren't flat until you crush them.

Fip squeezes the cup. It pops open and sprays a few drops of tea into the clean bin. Everyone looks at the label.

ZED: Now it is paper with food.

Ivo finds a waste bag under the sink. He opens it beside the bins. Petra drops the cup inside.

IVO: That's general waste.

PETRA: The sign is easy to understand.

Zed looks at the four complicated bins and takes off their labels. He stacks them by the door. The signs still stick together in a thick bundle.

FIP: Can we recycle the labels?

IVO: They are clean paper.

ZED: Which clean-paper bin?

Ivo opens the stacked bins. The lids fall into a neat pile. He throws the sticky labels into the waste bag and puts the cup's empty sleeve in the bag too.

[PETRA USES THE CLEAR SPACE TO WALK THROUGH THE DOOR. THE FIVE BINS REMAIN STACKED BEHIND THE SIGN: PLEASE SORT THE SORTING BINS.]
