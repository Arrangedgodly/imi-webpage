# The Catwalk Has a Speed Limit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fashion runway shows, dramatic designer commentary, and models asked to walk slowly enough for judges to inspect a sleeve*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Designer Oda, a capuchin; model Rell, a gibbon; judge Palla, a macaque; stage manager Vex, an orangutan; and tailor Neri, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL RUNWAY - NIGHT

A narrow catwalk runs between two rows of chairs. Oda adjusts Rell's jacket. The sleeve is pinned with a clothespin.

ODA: The look is called "Wind at the Orchard Gate."

RELL: The sleeve is called "Not Finished Yet."

NERI: I can stitch it in a minute.

VEX: The show starts in thirty seconds.

PALLA: The judges need to see the garment in motion.

Oda signals the music. Rell steps onto the runway and walks slowly. The clothespin catches on the curtain. The jacket stops while Rell continues forward, leaving one sleeve behind.

ODA: That was not the reveal.

RELL: I have a second sleeve.

Neri runs to the stage with a needle and thread. Vex asks Rell to return to the start. Rell backs up, but the music plays in reverse because the DJ has pressed the wrong button.

PALLA: A bold choice.

ODA: It is not a choice.

The second model walks. Her oversized hat blocks the judges' view. She turns sideways, but the hat sweeps a row of scorecards onto the floor. Palla tries to catch them; her tail holds three cards, all showing different scores.

PALLA: The outfit has earned an unpredictable mark.

Neri stitches Rell's sleeve while Rell holds the jacket with both hands. Oda asks the judge to look at the seam.

PALLA: I can only judge what reaches the runway.

RELL: I can carry the seam down the runway.

Rell walks again, one arm tucked into the jacket. The sleeve stays on. The hat model returns, this time holding the brim with both hands. She cannot wave.

VEX: The audience needs a final pose.

ODA: Pose at the end.

RELL: How long is the end?

PALLA: Three seconds.

Rell turns, the jacket catches the airflow, and the loose thread forms a tiny flag. The audience applauds. The judges confer. Oda wins for design, Neri wins for repair, and Rell receives a prize for keeping both sleeves attached.

PALLA: The catwalk has a speed limit. It is the speed at which clothes remain with the model.

VEX: The next show has a wider stage.

NERI: And finished hems?

ODA: We can reveal those after the show.

Rell returns the clothespin to the tailor's kit. The runway lights stay on while the audience leaves.

As the audience leaves, Neri finds the sleeve's loose thread caught on the curtain. She pulls it free and sews it in place. The hat model finally lowers her arms, but the brim stays upright like a second pair of shoulders.

PALLA: The final pose is still happening.

VEX: The show is over.

ODA: Then it is a very successful final pose.
