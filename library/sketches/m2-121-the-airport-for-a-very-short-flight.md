# The Airport for a Very Short Flight

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport announcements and security procedures applied to a one-branch hop across a clearing*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Gate agent Lolo, a macaque; traveler Venn, a gibbon; security officer Pika, a capuchin; and boarding announcer.

[AN AIRPORT GATE HAS A SIGN: "FLIGHT 1: ACROSS THE CLEARING." A SMALL PLANE EXISTS ONLY AS A DRAWING ON A BOARD.]

ANNOUNCER: Passengers for the three-minute flight may now board.

VENN: Is there a shorter line?

LOLO: The line is one person.

VENN: I prefer priority boarding.

PIKA: You are the only passenger.

VENN: Then I have priority.

[PIKA POINTS TO A SECURITY TRAY. VENN SETS A BANANA AND A CUP ON IT.]

PIKA: Any liquids?

VENN: One sip of water.

PIKA: The cup is not empty.

VENN: It is the liquid container.

PIKA: Drink it, then.

[VENN DRINKS. PIKA INSPECTS THE CUP AND THE BANANA.]

PIKA: Both are cleared.

LOLO: Your gate is across the clearing. Your pass is written on your wrist.

[VENN CHECKS. HIS WRIST SAYS "GO."]

VENN: This pass has no seat number.

LOLO: There is one seat.

[THE PLANE DRAWING FLAPS SIDEWAYS IN THE WIND.]

ANNOUNCER: Flight 1 is delayed due to a diagram issue.

VENN: The clearing is still there.

LOLO: Please remain at the gate.

PIKA: The rope is open.

LOLO: The rope is the gate.

[PIKA MOVES THE ROPE. VENN WALKS ACROSS THE CLEARING WHILE LOLO AND PIKA FOLLOW WITH A CLIPBOARD.]

VENN: I have arrived.

LOLO: Your luggage is still at security.

[PIKA HANDS HIM THE BANANA.]

VENN: It has been screened.

PIKA: It was a banana.

[THE ANNOUNCER CHECKS THE BOARD.]

ANNOUNCER: Flight 1 is now boarding for the return trip.

VENN: I am ready.

[VENN LOOKS AT HIS WRIST. THE INK HAS SMUDGED.]

VENN: My pass is gone.

LOLO: The flight is canceled.

VENN: Because the ink ran?

LOLO: The board cannot verify you.

PIKA: I watched him walk across.

LOLO: That verifies the outward trip.

VENN: I can show you my boarding picture.

[HE OPENS HIS PHONE. THE PHOTO IS A CLOSE-UP OF HIS WRIST, NOW BLURRED.]

PIKA: It is a picture of a wrist.

LOLO: We need a replacement pass.

[LOLO TAKES A FRESH CARD AND WRITES "RETURN: WALK BACK." THE STAMP LANDS ON THE BANANA. SHE PRESSES THE INKED PEEL AGAINST THE CARD.]

VENN: The pass has a fruit mark.

PIKA: It has been screened.

[VENN HOLDS THE CARD UP. IT SAYS "RETURN: WALK BACK."]

ANNOUNCER: Flight 1 is ready to depart.

VENN: I will walk.

[VENN CROSSES BACK THROUGH THE ROPE. LOLO STAMPS HIS CARD AT THE OTHER SIDE.]

LOLO: Welcome back to the terminal.

VENN: Does the banana need a return pass?

PIKA: Its screening is valid in both directions.

[THE ANNOUNCER ERASES THE PLANE DRAWING AND WRITES "WALK BOTH WAYS." VENN EATS THE BANANA WHILE THE CREW FOLDS THE ROPE.]
