# The Survival Expert Forgot the Water

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: outdoor survival programs and overconfident gear demonstrations*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: EXPERT VEX, a gibbon; CAMERAPERSON LUX, a macaque; TRAINEE NIM, a capuchin; and a backpack.

[A campsite. Vex stands beside a backpack full of gear.] 

VEX: Welcome to Stay Ready. Today I will show you how to survive a night in the woods with one compact bag.

LUX: Did you pack water?

VEX: I packed a filter.

NIM: Is there a stream nearby?

VEX: I assumed there would be.

LUX: The map shows one half a mile away.

VEX: We can demonstrate the filter there.

[THEY WALK TO THE STREAM. VEX OPENS THE BAG AND TAKES OUT A METAL CUP.] 

VEX: First, collect water.

NIM: The cup has a hole in it.

VEX: It is a lightweight cup.

LUX: It will not hold water.

VEX: The filter can make a small amount safe.

NIM: We still need a container.

[VEX TURNS THE CUP OVER. A DRY LEAF IS STUCK TO THE BOTTOM.] 

VEX: This leaf can be folded into a vessel.

NIM: It is brittle.

LUX: Do you have another bottle?

VEX: I have this one.

[HE PULLS OUT A PLASTIC BOTTLE. IT IS EMPTY AND CRACKED.] 

NIM: This leaks too.

VEX: The gear was tested before the crack.

LUX: When did you notice it?

VEX: On the walk in.

NIM: Then we should not use it for drinking water.

VEX: We have the filter.

NIM: The filter needs a container. We can return to the trailhead and get an intact bottle.

VEX: The program is about self-reliance.

NIM: It is also about knowing when you are missing a basic item.

LUX: I am turning off the camera until we have safe equipment.

[THE CAMERA LIGHT CLICKS OFF.] 

VEX: Can we film a close-up of the crack first?

LUX: The show can note what failed after we are back.

NIM: There is one more issue. Your pack strap is fraying.

VEX: The buckle is secure.

LUX: The strap is splitting beside it.

VEX: I have cord.

[HE OPENS THE PACK. CORD IS TANGLED AROUND THE FILTER.] 

VEX: The cord is in the same pocket as the filter.

NIM: We can sit on the dry rock and untangle it. Then carry the pack by the good strap.

[THEY UNTANGLE THE CORD AND SECURE THE FRAYED STRAP. VEX MARKS THE CRACKED BOTTLE WITH A BRIGHT LEAF.] 

LUX: The bottle is clearly marked for repair. The strap is secured. We still have no drinking water.

VEX: We will return to the trailhead, fill a sound bottle, and come back another day.

NIM: With more water than we think we need.

VEX: The camera can record the return trip once it is recharged.

LUX: We can also walk without filming.

[VEX LIFTS THE PACK BY THE REPAIRED STRAP. IT HOLDS.] 

NIM: That equipment passed its test.

VEX: One out of three is not the episode I planned.

LUX: It is the episode we have.
