# The Tape Measure's Big Break

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: backstage theater work, celebrity-style prop introductions, and crews who make a tool wait for its cue*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: STAGE MANAGER, a gibbon; CARPENTER, a baboon; ACTOR, a macaque; TAPE MEASURE, an orangutan; INTERN, a capuchin.

[A theater stage. The carpenter needs to measure a small door frame. The tape measure sits in a velvet box.] 

STAGE MANAGER: We need the width before the actor enters.

CARPENTER: I need the tape measure.

INTERN: It is in the spotlight.

TAPE MEASURE: I have been waiting for my close-up.

ACTOR: The door is still unfinished.

[The stage manager rolls out the tape. It snaps back into its case before the carpenter can read the number.] 

CARPENTER: It has returned to its trailer.

TAPE MEASURE: I am not a measuring tool until the cue.

STAGE MANAGER: The cue is now.

[The carpenter extends the tape to the frame. The actor stands in the doorway, blocking the far edge.] 

CARPENTER: Move one step left.

ACTOR: I am rehearsing my entrance.

CARPENTER: You are standing where the door should be.

[The actor moves. The tape reaches the frame, but the hook slips off the corner.] 

INTERN: The width is changing.

CARPENTER: The hook slipped. The width did not move.

[The carpenter hooks the tape on the edge and measures again. The stage manager calls "hold" as the actor begins his entrance.] 

ACTOR: I have a line.

STAGE MANAGER: You have a door frame under construction.

[Tape measure locks in place. The carpenter reads the number and marks the wood. The intern hands over a pencil.] 

CARPENTER: We need two inches trimmed.

TAPE MEASURE: I have delivered the measurement.

STAGE MANAGER: You can return to your case.

TAPE MEASURE: After the applause.

[The actor tries to step through the unfinished frame, catches a sleeve on a rough edge, and stops. The carpenter sands the edge and removes the measured two inches. The actor tests the doorway with an open hand before walking through.] 

ACTOR: The entrance works.

CARPENTER: The measurement worked.

[The stage manager closes the velvet box and moves it out of the spotlight. The tape measure is placed on the tool shelf, not in a dressing room.] 

[The carpenter calls for the tape again. The stage manager lifts the case from the spotlight and places it on a workbench. The tape measure extends across the full doorway without retracting. The carpenter marks both ends with pencil.]

CARPENTER: We need the frame square, not just wide.

INTERN: The top is a quarter inch lower on the right.

The carpenter checks the level and adds a thin shim under the frame. The tape measure stays locked while they test it. The actor waits offstage, holding the door open by its handle.

ACTOR: I have not entered yet.

STAGE MANAGER: You will enter when the frame is safe.

Once the carpenter nails the trim, the tape retracts slowly into its case. The intern keeps fingers away from the spring.

FINAL IMAGE: The actor exits through the finished doorway while the tape measure rests in its labeled tool case.
