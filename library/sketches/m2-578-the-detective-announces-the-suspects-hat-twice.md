# The Detective Announces the Suspect's Hat Twice

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: noir detectives who keep mistaking dramatic wardrobe details for evidence*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: DETECTIVE ODO, a gibbon; CLERK LUMA, a macaque; WITNESS PAVA, a lemur; SUSPECT RIKO, an orangutan.

[A rainless alley set. Odo stands beneath a streetlamp holding a damp notebook. Riko sits at a table wearing a brown hat. Pava waits nearby with an umbrella folded shut.]

ODO: The night was dark.

LUMA: The studio lights are on.

ODO: The night was selectively dark.

ACT ONE

[Odo circles Riko, staring at the hat brim.]

ODO: You were seen in a hat.

RIKO: I am wearing one.

ODO: The witness saw a brown hat.

PAVA: It was gray.

[Riko removes the hat. Its inside lining is gray; the outside is brown. Odo places it on the table and walks around it.]

ODO: Two colors. Two possible hats.

LUMA: It is one hat.

[Odo draws two columns on the evidence board: BROWN HAT and GRAY HAT. He pins the same sketch in both columns. The paper curls away from the board.]

ACT TWO

[Pava explains that she saw Riko carry a parcel at noon. Odo lowers his voice.]

ODO: The hat was also there.

PAVA: The parcel was a hatbox.

[Odo adds a third column: HATBOX. Riko says the box belonged to the theater costume shop. Luma checks the inventory sheet. The box was signed out by Odo himself.]

LUMA: You borrowed it.

ODO: For the investigation.

RIKO: You were investigating hats?

ODO: I had a feeling.

[Odo places the hatbox on the table. It is empty. He opens the lid and finds a card reading RETURN TO COSTUMES. He turns it over. The reverse says RETURNED BY DETECTIVE ODO, but the signature line is blank.]

PAVA: It was gray when I saw it because the light was behind him.

ODO: A backlit hat.

LUMA: That still makes it one hat.

[Odo changes the evidence board to one column. He writes BROWN OUTSIDE, GRAY LINING. The suspect leans over to read it. Odo blocks him with his notebook.]

ODO: The case is not closed.

RIKO: What is left to solve?

[Odo looks at the hat, the box and his own reflection in the dark window.]

ODO: Why did I borrow the hatbox?

LUMA: To carry the hat.

[Odo sits. He opens the notebook to the first page and reads his own note: "Borrow costume hatbox to collect detective coat." He checks the chair and finds his coat folded beneath it.]

ACT THREE

[Odo returns the coat to Riko, who is the costume-room attendant for the evening show. Pava says she saw Riko carrying the box toward the theater, not away from it. Odo crosses out the suspect's name and writes COSTUME RETURN.]

ODO: The hat was innocent.

RIKO: The hat does not have a record.

LUMA: The hat has a return card.

[Odo pins the return card on the evidence board. It falls off again. He tries to pin it to the hat's brim; the point catches the lining. Riko takes the pin and places the card on the table where it can be read.]

ODO: Case solved.

PAVA: What about the rain?

[Odo looks at her folded umbrella.]

ODO: A weather matter. Not my department.

[Luma points to the dry studio floor. Odo hangs his damp notebook beside the umbrella, which is still closed.]

FINAL IMAGE: The detective's board displays one hat, one return card and no suspects; Odo's coat hangs safely beside the unused umbrella.
