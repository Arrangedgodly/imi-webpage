# The Camp Counselor Counts the Marshmallows

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wilderness survival lessons and campfire stories built around an increasingly disputed snack inventory*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: COUNSELOR, a gibbon; CAMPER ONE, a macaque; CAMPER TWO, a baboon; COOK, an orangutan; RANGER, a capuchin.

[A campsite. The counselor places a bag of marshmallows on a log. A card reads SURVIVAL SKILLS. The cook holds a metal pot.]

COUNSELOR: We have twenty marshmallows.

CAMPER ONE: I counted nineteen.

CAMPER TWO: I ate one.

COUNSELOR: Then we have nineteen.

[The ranger arrives with a bag of acorns.]

RANGER: Those are for the trail.

CAMPER TWO: Are acorns marshmallows?

RANGER: Absolutely not.

ACT ONE

[The counselor teaches how to build a safe fire. The cook places a pot over the flames. The campers begin skewering marshmallows, but the counselor asks them to wait until the lesson ends.]

COUNSELOR: First, learn where the wind is going.

[The smoke blows toward the sleeping tents. Everyone moves their stools. The cook rotates the fire ring, but the pot slides toward the edge.]

COOK: We have a second problem.

CAMPER ONE: The marshmallow count is nineteen.

[The ranger checks the bag and finds a hole. Several marshmallows are stuck to the counselor's sleeve.]

COUNSELOR: They are not gone.

CAMPER TWO: They have joined your uniform.

[The counselor removes the marshmallows. One falls into the dirt; the cook puts it aside. The other five are clean and returned to the bag. The count is now eighteen usable treats. The campers ask whether they can roast the acorns.]

RANGER: Acorns need different preparation.

COUNSELOR: That is tomorrow's lesson.

[The wind shifts again. The cook moves the pot to a stable hook. The campers practice tying a knot around the marshmallow bag. Camper Two ties it too tightly and cannot open it.]

CAMPER TWO: The bag is secure.

CAMPER ONE: We cannot reach dinner.

[The ranger shows a quick-release knot. The counselor lets the campers try. The bag opens, the marshmallows stay inside, and everyone counts them together.]

[The ranger counts the food one last time and writes down eighteen usable marshmallows. The counselor checks the fire ring and pours water around its edge. The campers put the sealed bag in the hanging food sack, well above the ground.]

COOK: No snack stays beside the fire overnight.

CAMPER ONE: The count will survive until breakfast.

ACT TWO

[The fire is stable and the pot is off the edge. The counselor distributes one marshmallow per camper. There are enough for the group, with one extra that the counselor puts away for breakfast. The ranger reminds them to keep food sealed overnight.]

COUNSELOR: Survival skill one: count what you have.

CAMPER TWO: Survival skill two: do not wear your lunch.

FINAL IMAGE: The counselor hangs the sealed bag from a branch while the campers roast acorns beside tomorrow's lesson card.
