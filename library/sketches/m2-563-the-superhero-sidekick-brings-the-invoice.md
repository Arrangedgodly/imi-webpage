# The Superhero Sidekick Brings the Invoice

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: superhero debriefings that give the hero credit while assigning every material cost to the sidekick*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: HERO SOL, an orangutan; SIDEKICK PAX, a macaque; ACCOUNTANT NELL, a gibbon; BUILDING CARETAKER ODO, a capuchin; non-speaking staff photographer.

[A headquarters meeting room. Sol stands beneath a poster showing him lifting a cardboard asteroid. Pax puts a folder beside Nell's calculator. Odo carries a bent door handle.]

SOL: Another city saved.

PAX: Another equipment invoice.

SOL: We do not put a price on courage.

NELL: We put a price on replacement hinges.

[Odo sets the door handle on a cloth. Sol raises one hand as though saluting the wreckage.]

ODO: You pulled the headquarters door toward you. It opens away from you.

SOL: I was answering the emergency call.

PAX: The emergency call was the printer asking for paper.

ACT ONE

[Nell opens the folder. The first invoice is for smoke fluid used in Sol's dramatic rooftop entrance. The second is for Pax's bus fare.]

SOL: The smoke concealed our arrival.

PAX: It concealed the sign saying the lift was out of service. We took the stairs.

NELL: Did the bus fare carry both of you?

PAX: Only me. He flew to the roof. I carried the spare cape from the stop.

SOL: A vital supporting role.

PAX: The cape has its own supporting invoice.

[Pax takes a cleaning receipt from the folder. Odo looks at the photograph on the wall: Sol is wearing the cape while standing in a puddle beside a leaking plant pot.]

ODO: I fixed that pot before you reached the roof.

SOL: I was containing the flood.

PAX: The photograph was the second take. You asked for the puddle to remain.

[Nell writes "publicity" next to the cleaning charge. Sol sits at the table for the first time.]

SOL: You make the victory sound very small.

NELL: You announced the city was saved. This folder says a door, a pot and a photo call.

PAX: The fundraiser paid for a new roof garden. That part was useful.

[Sol brightens. Pax shows a donation total printed beneath the event photograph. Nell compares it to the cash receipt and nods.]

NELL: The garden fund received the full proceeds. Headquarters still owes these costs.

SOL: Then deduct the door from my appearance fee.

ODO: And let the carpenter replace it before you pose with it.

ACT TWO

[Sol signs the payment authorization. Nell stamps it approved and gives Odo the maintenance copy. Pax collects his bus reimbursement from the petty-cash envelope, signing the receipt.]

PAX: Thank you. Tomorrow I can arrive without financing the entrance.

SOL: We should make a poster showing the whole team.

ODO: I would prefer the door sign.

[Sol picks up a marker and writes PUSH on a card. He checks with Odo before attaching it to the intact door outside the room. Odo points to the correct side.]

SOL: Every headquarters needs a symbol.

PAX: It now has four letters and an arrow.

[Nell asks Sol to stand beside Pax for a new photograph. Pax keeps the folder under one arm. Odo holds the PUSH sign in the background.]

SOL: Shall I look into the distance?

NELL: Look at the invoice so the photographer can see both of you.

[Sol looks down. Pax turns the folder outward.]

FINAL IMAGE: The new team photograph shows Sol reading the costs while Pax smiles beside the reimbursed bus ticket.
