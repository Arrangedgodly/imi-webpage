# The Meeting Runs on Acorn Time

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate meeting facilitation and timeboxing in a troop with a strong preference for foraging breaks*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Facilitator Sela, a gibbon; director Varo, a baboon; recorder Timi, a macaque; and participants.

[THE MEETING ROOM HAS A TIMER SET FOR FIFTEEN MINUTES. A BOWL OF ACORNS SITS BESIDE IT.]

SELA: We have one agenda item: choosing a new path marker.

VARO: We can decide quickly.

[THE TIMER STARTS. A PARTICIPANT TAKES AN ACORN.]

TIMI: I will note that the snack has begun.

VARO: The snack is not on the agenda.

SELA: We can keep talking while we eat.

[VARO OPENS A SLIDE SHOW. THE FIRST SLIDE READS "MARKER OPTIONS."]

VARO: Option one is a painted stone.

PARTICIPANT: It looks like a fruit.

VARO: That is a feature.

TIMI: Option two is a hanging ribbon.

PARTICIPANT: Wind moves it.

SELA: That is also a feature.

[THE TIMER BEEPS. EVERYONE STOPS.]

VARO: We have used fifteen minutes.

SELA: We have not made a choice.

TIMI: We have chosen to take a break.

VARO: That was not a vote.

[THE PARTICIPANTS LEAVE FOR A SNACK. VARO FOLLOWS WITH THE SLIDES.]

SELA: The break is five minutes.

VARO: I can present the options outside.

[THE GROUP SITS UNDER A TREE. A MONKEY TAKES THE PAINTED STONE AND USES IT TO CRACK AN ACORN.]

PARTICIPANT: The stone is useful.

VARO: It is now a tool.

SELA: Does it mark the path?

[THE MONKEY CRACKS ANOTHER ACORN. THE STONE ROLLS TO THE SIDE.]

TIMI: The marker moved.

VARO: We can attach a sign.

SELA: The meeting is still on the agenda.

[THE GROUP RETURNS TO THE ROOM. THE TIMER IS STILL BEEPING.]

PARTICIPANT: I vote for the ribbon.

VARO: I vote for the stone.

SELA: The stone has one vote, and the ribbon has one vote.

TIMI: The monkey has used the stone twice.

VARO: That is not a vote.

[THE MONKEY ENTERS CARRYING THE RIBBON AND TIES IT AROUND THE STONE.]

SELA: The options have combined.

[THEY PLACE THE STONE AT THE PATH. THE TIMER STOPS WHEN THE MONKEY TAKES ITS BATTERY.]

VARO: The meeting has ended.

TIMI: We did not adjourn.


[SELA DRAWS A MAP OF THE TWO PATHS ON THE WHITEBOARD.]

SELA: The marker should be visible from both directions.

VARO: We can make the stone taller.

PARTICIPANT: Then it will look like a small wall.

TImI: I will record the concern.

VARO: What if we paint a stripe on it?

[THE MONKEY RETURNS WITH THE STONE, NOW BEARING A CRACKED ACORN SHELL.]

SELA: The stone has already been used.

PARTICIPANT: It marks where the snack break happened.

VARO: That is not the path.

[THE PARTICIPANTS WALK TO THE PATH AND TEST THE STONE. IT IS EASY TO SEE, BUT THE RIBBON FLAPS IN THEIR FACES.]

TImI: We can shorten the ribbon.

VARO: The marker now has a measurable adjustment.
[THE GROUP FOLLOWS THE MONKEY TO THE ACORNS.]
