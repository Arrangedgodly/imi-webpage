# The Golden Leaf Awards

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised awards ceremonies and acceptance-speech politics*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: HOST SURI, a macaque; NOMINEE KETT, a gibbon; PRESENTer RO, a capuchin; and STAGE MANAGER BEX, a baboon.

[A stage. A golden leaf trophy sits beside a microphone. A sign says SPEECHES: 30 SECONDS.]

SURI: Welcome to the Golden Leaf Awards, honoring excellence in gardening, nest repair, and branch navigation.

RO: The nominees for Best Route are the north path, the river trail, and the shortcut behind the bakery.

KETT: The shortcut should win.

BEX: You are presenting the award.

KETT: I can present it to myself.

SURI: Please don't.

[Ro opens the envelope.]

RO: The winner is... the shortcut behind the bakery.

KETT: I accept.

BEX: The envelope says the river trail.

RO: I misread the handwriting.

KETT: I would like to thank the bakery for creating the shortcut.

SURI: You have thirty seconds.

KETT: I also thank the river trail for being a finalist.

BEX: The river trail is not here.

KETT: I can thank it later.

[He reaches for the trophy. Bex moves it away.]

SURI: We have an appeal from the route committee.

RO: The shortcut is closed for repairs.

KETT: It was open this morning.

BEX: It was open because the sign fell down.

[The camera zooms on the sign, which lies on the stage.]

SURI: The award must be reassigned.

RO: The river trail wins.

KETT: Can I keep my speech?

BEX: You can shorten it.

KETT: I thank the river.

SURI: We have another award, for Best Nest Repair.

[The winners line up. Every nominee has brought the same repaired nest, because they all helped.]

BEX: How do we choose?

RO: The nest is holding.

SURI: Then the team wins.

[The trophy is too heavy for the small branch table. It tips.]

KETT: The branch navigation award should go to whoever catches it.

[The golden leaf falls into a flower pot. Everyone reaches at once.]

BEX: The pot wins.

SURI: It has excellent placement.

[The trophy is awarded to the pot. The stage manager begins the closing music before any speech can start.]


SURI: Our final award is Best Acceptance Speech.

BEX: There were no speeches under thirty seconds.

RO: Then we have no nominees.

KETT: I have a short one.

SURI: The award is not for presenting.

KETT: I thank the river.

RO: The river trail was the winner.

KETT: I have not used it yet.

BEX: The trophy has fallen into a flower pot.

SURI: The pot has won Best Placement. It can accept the award.

[The pot tips. Kett catches it before the trophy falls.]

SURI: Kett, you have won Best Catch.

KETT: I accept on behalf of the pot.

RO: The pot is not a member.

BEX: The pot is carrying the award.

[The stage manager attaches the golden leaf to a twig beside the pot. The twig bows under its weight.]

SURI: The closing ceremony will now move to the floor.

KETT: The floor is not a nominee.

SURI: It has supported every winner.

[BLACKOUT.]
