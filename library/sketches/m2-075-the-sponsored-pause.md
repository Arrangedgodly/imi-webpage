# The Sponsored Pause

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: influencer product placements and authenticity disclaimers*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: CREATOR LEX, a macaque; BRAND REP IVY, a gibbon; MODERATOR PIP, a capuchin; and an AUDIENCE.

[A bright set. A small water bottle sits on a stump. A leafy backdrop covers the sponsor logo.]

LEX: Today's video is a quiet moment in nature.

IVY: Please hold the bottle toward the camera.

LEX: The quiet moment is sponsored by ClearSip.

PIP: Can we label the sponsorship before the bottle appears?

IVY: It appears in the first three seconds.

LEX: We are seven seconds in.

[Lex moves the bottle. The cap catches a vine and pulls down the backdrop.]

AUDIENCE: Oh!

IVY: That is a behind-the-scenes moment. Do not include it.

PIP: We are live.

LEX: This bottle is reusable, light, and does not leak.

IVY: Mention the color options.

LEX: Green and blue.

IVY: The blue one is not ready.

LEX: Then it comes in green.

[Lex takes a drink. The bottle makes a loud slurp.]

IVY: Can we do another take?

LEX: The audience heard me drink water.

IVY: The slurp is off-brand.

PIP: There is no brand of slurp.

[The bottle rolls off the stump. Lex catches it with the tail.]

LEX: It stayed closed.

IVY: Good demonstration.

PIP: It was an accident.

IVY: Accidents can be authentic.

[The backdrop falls the rest of the way, exposing a painted forest and a stack of boxes.]

AUDIENCE: Ooooh!

LEX: This is where the bottles are stored.

IVY: Please don't show the warehouse.

LEX: It is the only nature behind us.

PIP: We can take a real outdoor shot.

IVY: The light is changing.

LEX: Nature does that.

[They go outside. Wind moves the leaves and rolls the bottle away. Lex catches it again.]

IVY: The product has passed two tests.

LEX: We didn't test it. It rolled on a level stump.

PIP: Then say what happened.

LEX: The bottle stayed closed when I caught it. I still need to test it properly.

IVY: That is not a strong tagline.

LEX: It's a true one.

[The audience claps. The sponsor rep checks whether the bottle is in frame.]


IVY: We need a final shot with the logo visible.

LEX: The logo is under the fallen backdrop.

PIP: We can put the bottle on a plain rock.

IVY: The rock is not branded.

LEX: That's the point.

[The audience begins leaving. Ivy raises the bottle to stop them.]

IVY: Wait. We have a giveaway.

PIP: The bottle is reusable, not unlimited.

IVY: We have three samples.

LEX: Give them to the people who helped hold the backdrop.

[The audience takes the bottles. One person checks the cap and asks for the product details.]

LEX: The bottle stayed closed when it rolled. We have not tested it in a river or on a long hike.

IVY: That is all we can claim?

LEX: That's what happened.

PIP: We can film another segment after proper testing.

IVY: Can we call it authentic?

LEX: You can call it an unfinished review.

[The brand representative takes down the painted forest and folds it into a box.]

[BLACKOUT.]
