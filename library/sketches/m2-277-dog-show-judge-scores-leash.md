# The Dog Show Judge Scores the Leash

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: pet competitions that judge handlers' presentation more than the animals*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Judge Vela, a macaque; handler Kito, a capuchin; announcer Nara, a gibbon; and dog Pip, a friendly mixed-breed dog.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - DAY

Nara introduces the neighborhood dog show. Kito and Pip enter the ring. Vela holds a scorecard.

NARA: The first category is best entrance.

KITO: Pip, walk with me.

[Pip trots in, sniffs the ring marker and sits.]

VELA: The handler's entrance was strong. The dog has chosen a stationary position.

KITO: He is comfortable.

NARA: That is the sit category.

INT. RING - MOMENTS LATER

Kito guides Pip through a short path of cones. Pip walks around the cones, then pauses to greet a child.

VELA: The pattern was not completed.

KITO: Pip noticed the audience.

NARA: The audience likes Pip.

[The child offers a hand. Pip gently sniffs it, then returns to Kito.]

VELA: We should score the handler's route separately.

KITO: We are a team.

VELA: The form has separate boxes.

NARA: There is no box for friendly greeting.

[Vela adds a new box to the card. The pencil breaks.]

INT. HALL - FINAL CATEGORY

Nara announces the dramatic finish. Kito leads Pip toward a ribbon. Pip stops before the ribbon and lies down beside it.

NARA: The finish is on the other side.

KITO: Pip is taking a break.

VELA: The handler has failed to deliver the dog to the finish line.

[Pip rolls onto his side. The ribbon falls over his back like a sash.]

NARA: The dog has crossed the finish without moving.

VELA: That is not how the rules define a finish.

[The children clap. Pip gets up and wags his tail.]

KITO: He is ready to leave.

INT. RING - BETWEEN CATEGORIES

Vela asks Kito to demonstrate a formal turn. Pip turns once, then sits beside the water bowl.

VELA: The turn was incomplete.

KITO: He found the water.

NARA: The water was in the ring for the dogs.

[Pip drinks, then nudges the bowl toward another dog waiting outside the ring.]

VELA: The second dog has not entered the competition.

KITO: Pip is sharing the water.

[The second dog drinks. The audience applauds. Vela adds a note to her scorecard.]

VELA: I am creating a new category: useful kindness.
INT. AWARDS TABLE - LATER

Vela totals the scores. Pip rests beside Kito while Nara reads the results.

NARA: The winner is Pip, for best friendly greeting and most relaxed finish.

VELA: The judging card did not have those categories.

NARA: You added them.

[Vela checks the card. Her handwritten boxes are the only completed ones.]

VELA: I have awarded him my categories.

[She places a ribbon on Pip's harness. Pip stands, and the ribbon slides to the floor.]

KITO: He does not need the ribbon.

NARA: The audience can applaud him anyway.

[Pip returns to his favorite spot by the child. The judges move the award stand beside him.]
