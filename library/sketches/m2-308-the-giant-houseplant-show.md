# The Giant Houseplant Show

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: garden competitions, expert judging, and contestants who use increasingly theatrical names for common plants*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Palla, an orangutan; entrant Rell, a macaque; entrant Vex, a gibbon; and judge Nari, a capuchin.

INT. COMMUNITY GREENHOUSE - AFTERNOON

Two large houseplants stand on rolling carts. Palla introduces the competition.

PALLA: Welcome to the Giant Houseplant Show. Judges will score height, health, and presentation.

RELL: Mine is called The Tower of Ferns.

VEX: Mine is called The Green Horizon.

NARI: What kind of plants are they?

RELL: Ferns.

VEX: A pothos.

Palla asks the entrants to reveal their plants. Rell pulls away a cloth, but the plant's fronds catch on it. Vex's vine reaches across the aisle and wraps around the fern cart.

NARI: The plants are competing with each other.

RELL: They are sharing space.

VEX: My vine has taken the lead.

Palla measures the fern. It is tall but leaning. She measures the pothos. It is shorter, with leaves spread widely across a frame.

NARI: The fern has height. The pothos has coverage.

PALLA: Which one is healthier?

Both entrants water their plants at once. Rell pours too much; water drains into the tray. Vex pours a small amount and checks the soil with a finger.

NARI: The pothos needs water.

VEX: It has been dry.

Rell tries to improve presentation by putting a tiny crown on the fern. The crown slides down a frond. Palla says the crown is not part of the judging. Rell removes it, and the fern springs upright.

RELL: The crown was weighing it down.

Vex's vine has grown around the greenhouse label. It now says "Green Horizfern." Nari asks them to separate the plants. They rotate the carts in opposite directions. The vine tightens. Palla places a trellis between them and guides the vine through it.

NARI: The plant can climb without leaning on another plant.

VEX: That is a good design.

PALLA: The score is tied. Both entries need care.

Rell asks who wins the giant plant prize. Nari awards a watering can to both competitors. Rell accepts it, then gives it to Vex because his plant is dry. Vex waters the pothos and hands the can back.

RELL: We can share the prize.

VEX: We can also give our plants regular names.

PALLA: What will you call yours?

RELL: Fern.

VEX: Pothos.

Nari writes the labels. The show announcer announces one last category: best dramatic introduction. Palla and Rell give the plants elaborate speeches; Vex simply lifts a leaf.

NARI: The leaf has won.

PALLA: It did not enter.

NARI: It was the only part of the plant that stayed still.

The greenhouse fan turns on. The leaves sway, and the crown rolls off the table into the watering tray.

Nari checks the soil once more. The fern is still upright, and the pothos has room to climb. She writes "healthy" on both scorecards, then moves the fan so it does not blow the labels away.
