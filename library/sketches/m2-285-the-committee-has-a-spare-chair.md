# The Committee Has a Spare Chair

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: civic committees, procedural meeting rules, and agenda items that multiply every time someone asks where to sit*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Chairperson Tera, a macaque; clerk Palla, a ring-tailed lemur; members Odo, a gibbon, and Neri, a capuchin; and custodian Vex, an orangutan.

INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - EVENING

Four committee members sit around a table with five chairs. A sign says PUBLIC MEETING: WHERE TO STORE THE EXTRA CHAIR.

TERA: We will begin with the attendance roll.

PALLA: Tera, present. Odo, present. Neri, present. Palla, present.

ODO: There are four of us.

PALLA: The chairperson is also chair.

TERA: That is not a title on the roster.

VEX: The extra chair belongs in the closet.

NERI: The closet has no room.

TERA: That is the agenda item. We will hear public comment.

Vex explains that the closet contains folding tables, a coat rack, two lamps, and a box marked "chairs." The committee asks whether the chair should be placed beside the box.

ODO: Then it is not stored.

NERI: It is with the other chairs.

PALLA: The box is labeled chairs, but it contains one stool.

TERA: We need a motion to inspect the closet.

Neri moves. Odo seconds. They all stand and go to the closet, carrying the spare chair. The closet door opens only halfway because the chair blocks it.

VEX: Turn it sideways.

They rotate the chair. The door opens fully, revealing there is room if the box of lamps is moved. Vex shifts the lamps. A lamp turns on when plugged in, lighting the closet.

PALLA: We have discovered storage and illumination.

TERA: Please do not add illumination to the agenda.

They return to the table. The spare chair is now beside the closet, which everyone agrees is a temporary solution. Tera asks for a vote. Neri votes to store it. Odo votes to move the box. Palla abstains because she recorded both motions.

TERA: The vote is tied.

VEX: There are only three voting members.

TERA: I am the chair. I do not vote unless there is a tie.

ODO: That is why we need to decide whether the spare chair is a member.

PALLA: The chair has no voting card.

Neri offers it a pen. It rolls off the seat and under the table. They all bend to retrieve it, and Tera's tail catches the chair, pulling it out of the room.

TERA: The spare chair has left the meeting.

VEX: I moved it to the closet.

Neri proposes labeling the closet shelf. Odo moves to approve. Palla records the motion. Tera calls the vote. All three agree. Vex writes "extra chair shelf" on a strip of tape and sticks it above the box.

TERA: The matter is resolved.

PALLA: The minutes should include the motion, the inspection, the lamp, and the chair's departure.

ODO: How long will that take?

PALLA: Longer than the meeting.

Tera calls the meeting adjourned. Everyone stands. The spare chair rolls back into the room on a loose wheel.

VEX: It was never in the closet.

TERA: We can add that to next month's agenda.

Palla places the chair beside the labeled shelf. It fits exactly.
