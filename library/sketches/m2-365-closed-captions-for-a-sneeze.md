# Closed Captions for a Sneeze

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: live captioning systems that turn tiny audio glitches into official dialogue*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Piri, a lemur caption operator; Davi, a macaque presenter; Omi, a capuchin technician; Kettle, a gibbon audience member.

INT. COMMUNITY LECTURE HALL - DAY

[A SCREEN READS: TODAY'S TALK, HOW TO KEEP A GARDEN QUIET. PIRI SITS AT A CAPTION KEYBOARD. DAVI APPROACHES THE PODIUM. KETTLE SITS IN THE FRONT ROW.]

DAVI: Welcome. Please silence your devices.

[THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: PLEASE SILENCE YOUR DEVICES.]

DAVI: Today we will discuss compost, leaf mulch, and how to avoid disturbing nesting birds.

[THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: TODAY WE WILL DISCUSS COMPOST, LEAF MULCH, AND HOW TO AVOID DISTURBING NESTING BIRDS.]

Kettle sneezes. The screen displays: I OBJECT TO THE MULCH.

KETTLE: I sneezed.

PIRI: The system heard a full sentence.

OMI: The microphone is aimed at the audience.

DAVI: Could you aim it at me?

Omi pivots the microphone. It squeals, and the captions read: THE GARDEN IS SCREAMING.

DAVI: Let's proceed without the microphone.

[THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: LET'S PROCEED WITHOUT THE MICROPHONE.]

PIRI: It is still listening.

DAVI: Piri, turn it off.

[THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: PIRI, TURN IT OFF.]

Piri presses a key. The screen reads: I REFUSE.

PIRI: That's the key labeled "pause."

OMI: It seems to have paused your instructions.

Davi turns to the audience and speaks slowly. Piri types every word. Kettle coughs. Captions appear: THE COMPOST REQUESTS A GLASS OF WATER.

KETTLE: I have a dry throat.

DAVI: We'll take questions.

[THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: THE COMPOST WILL TAKE QUESTIONS.]

A squirrel in the back asks where the garden tools are. The screen reads: WHERE ARE THE GARDEN FOOLS?

DAVI: Tools. T-O-O-L-S.

PIRI: The spelling correction just changed it to "fools" again.

Omi unplugs the caption display. It goes black. Davi continues the talk. The screen flickers back on with: SILENCE IS A FORM OF MULCH.

DAVI: That is not in my lecture.

PIRI: It's from the auto-suggestion list.

Omi finds a small switch beneath the desk and lowers the microphone sensitivity. Kettle clears his throat. Nothing appears. Davi resumes.

DAVI: The best way to keep a garden quiet is to choose plants that do not need constant pruning.

[THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: THE BEST WAY TO KEEP A GARDEN QUIET IS TO CHOOSE PLANTS THAT DO NOT NEED CONSTANT PROOF.]

Davi stops. The audience looks at the screen.

DAVI: That one is almost useful.

KETTLE: It sounds like advice from a plant.

Piri removes the suggestion list. The captions now display only Davi's words. He finishes the talk without another sneeze. The crowd applauds. The screen says: [APPLAUSE].

Kettle sneezes again. The captions read: THANK YOU FOR YOUR REVIEW.

KETTLE: That's fair.

[PIRI SAVES THE TRANSCRIPT. OMI UNPLUGS THE AUDIENCE MICROPHONE. THE SCREEN DISPLAYS: GOODBYE, COMPOST.]
