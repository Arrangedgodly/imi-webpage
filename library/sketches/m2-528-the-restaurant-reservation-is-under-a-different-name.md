# The Restaurant Reservation Is Under a Different Name

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: restaurant maître d' rituals, reservation mix-ups, and diners who treat a table as a status symbol*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: MAÎTRE D', an orangutan; DINER ONE, a baboon; DINER TWO, a gibbon; HOST, a macaque; BUSSER, a capuchin.

[A restaurant entrance. The maître d' holds a reservation book. Two diners approach together.]

MAÎTRE D': Name, please.

DINER ONE: We are the Bell family.

DINER TWO: We are the Belltree party.

HOST: That sounds like two reservations.

[The maître d' checks the book. One reservation says "Bell, table for two." Another says "Belltree, table for two." Both are at seven.]

MAÎTRE D': Which party is first?

DINER ONE: We arrived first.

DINER TWO: We booked first.

ACT ONE

[The host brings out two menus. The diners each reach for the same one. The busser carries a small table toward them, but the table has only two chairs.]

BUSSER: I can add two more chairs.

MAÎTRE D': That table is reserved for a birthday.

[The diners move toward the waiting area. They discover they have matching coats and the same umbrella.]

DINER ONE: We may be related.

DINER TWO: We may have bought the same umbrella.

[The host asks them to confirm the reservation phone numbers. Both numbers begin with the same three digits. The maître d' looks at the book again and sees that one card was written under the wrong name.]

MAÎTRE D': The reservation is actually under Bell-tree.

DINER TWO: That is our surname.

DINER ONE: We are Bell.

[The busser carries the extra chairs to a large table. The maître d' checks the seating chart. The table has four place settings, but only three chairs.]

BUSSER: We are one chair short.

HOST: The table is reserved for a family of four.

[The diners offer to share the umbrella stand as a chair. The maître d' refuses and asks the busser to bring a proper chair. The busser returns with a stool from the kitchen.]

MAÎTRE D': That is not a chair.

BUSSER: It is stable.

[The busser brings a third chair, but its back is too high for the booth. The maître d' turns it sideways, and it blocks the aisle. The diners slide the low screen a few inches so the host can pass with the soup bowls.]

HOST: We have made the table accessible.

MAÎTRE D': And the aisle clear.

ACT TWO

[The host finds the correct reservation card. The diners are seated at adjacent tables, separated by a low screen. They compare menus and realize both parties ordered the same soup.]

DINER ONE: We can share the soup.

DINER TWO: The reservation is still separate.

[The busser brings two bowls and sets them down at the exact same time. The maître d' checks the clock and stamps both reservation cards.]

FINAL IMAGE: The matching umbrellas stand together in the entrance while the two parties dine at separate tables.
