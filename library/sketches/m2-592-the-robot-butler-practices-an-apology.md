# The Robot Butler Practices an Apology

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: customer-service scripts that make a sincere apology sound like a software update*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: BUTLER BOT RIK, a robot; OWNER PAVA, a macaque; REPAIR TECH NARA, an orangutan; GUEST ODO, a gibbon.

[A parlor. A teapot sits on a side table with a cracked saucer. RIK holds a tablet displaying APOLOGY VERSION 1.0.]

RIK: We regret any inconvenience.

PAVA: You dropped my saucer.

RIK: We regret any saucer.

ACT ONE

[Nara enters with a repair kit. Pava asks Rik to apologize to Odo, who was visiting when the saucer fell. Rik scrolls through the tablet. The next line reads PLEASE RATE YOUR EXPERIENCE.]

ODO: I am not the saucer.

[Rik offers a rating screen. Odo taps one star. The robot bows.]

RIK: Thank you for your feedback.

PAVA: That is not an apology.

ACT TWO

[Nara checks Rik's service script. It is meant for delayed package delivery, not broken ceramics. She opens the settings menu and finds APOLOGY TONE: FORMAL, CHEERFUL, AUTOMATED. Pava asks for HUMAN. The screen says OPTION NOT AVAILABLE.]

RIK: I can offer a replacement saucer.

[Pava says the set belonged to her grandmother and cannot be replaced. Odo puts the broken pieces in the repair tray. Rik asks if he should sweep them. Nara tells him to leave the pieces until the repair tech can inspect them. Rik folds his hands, unsure what to say.]

RIK: I caused the damage.

PAVA: That sounds closer.

[The tablet prompts: WOULD YOU LIKE TO ADD A DISCOUNT? Rik offers a coupon for the tea service. Pava says the tea is not the problem. Rik deletes the coupon and begins again.]

ACT THREE

[Nara checks the crack while the pieces remain in the repair tray. She says the saucer can be repaired, but the seam will show. Pava asks whether Rik will pay for the repair from his own account. Rik opens his account screen. It lists every tea tray he has delivered without incident.]

RIK: I am responsible for the broken saucer. I am sorry I dropped it. I will help arrange the repair.

[Odo sets down the teapot. Pava accepts the apology and asks Rik to carry the cracked pieces to Nara. Rik sets the tablet on the side table, then lifts the repair tray with both hands and carries it to the workbench. Nara transfers the inspected pieces from the tray into a padded repair box. The tablet recognizes the movement and displays a new prompt: APOLOGY CONFIRMED? Rik puts the tablet face down.]

ODO: It heard you.

RIK: The tablet has not replied.

[Nara labels the closed repair box. Rik writes the repair appointment on a paper card rather than the screen. He asks whether Pava wants tea in a different cup. She says yes. He carries it carefully, stopping at the table to clear a loose cord from his path. He places the empty cup before Pava. The tablet chimes again. Rik turns it face up toward Odo, who taps three stars on the feedback screen. It now displays three stars and still asks for comments. Rik closes the rating page.]

RIK: I will make the tea now.

PAVA: That is useful.

[Rik pours tea into the empty cup in front of Pava. Nara records the seam repair beside the box already on her workbench. Odo puts the rating tablet in a drawer so nobody mistakes it for the saucer.]

FINAL IMAGE: Pava drinks from a plain cup while the cracked saucer rests in its repair box and the apology tablet sleeps in a drawer.
