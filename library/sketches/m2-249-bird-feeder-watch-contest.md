# The Bird Feeder Watch Contest

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wildlife-viewing contests that score sightings while their equipment scares away the animals*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Contest host Nara, a gibbon; watcher Kito, a macaque; watcher Seli, a capuchin; and groundskeeper Venn, a baboon.

EXT. PARK FEEDER - MORNING

A bird feeder hangs from a branch. Nara holds a scorecard. Kito and Seli sit behind a blind with binoculars.

NARA: First person to identify three species wins the feeder trophy.

KITO: Do we have to name them out loud?

NARA: The judge must hear the answer.

SELI: The birds can hear us too.

[NARA WHISPERS THROUGH A MEGAPHONE. A SPARROW FLIES AWAY.]

NARA: One species has departed.

EXT. PARK FEEDER - LATER

Venn carries a tripod. Kito looks through binoculars. Seli holds a field guide.

KITO: I see a finch.

NARA: Say it clearly.

KITO: A finch.

[THE FINCH FLIES OFF.]

SELI: The call was clear.

NARA: The bird is now outside the viewing area.

VENN: The feeder is empty.

NARA: We can refill it.

[Venn opens the seed bag. The plastic crinkles loudly. The birds in the trees scatter.]

VENN: It is full now.

KITO: No birds are here.

INT. PARK BLIND - AFTERNOON

Seli draws a tally chart. Nara rearranges the scorecards. Kito waits with binoculars lowered.

NARA: We need a winner by the end of the hour.

SELI: We have one sparrow, one finch, and three empty branches.

KITO: The trophy is shaped like a bird.

NARA: It is a generic bird.

[THE TROPHY'S METAL WING CATCHES THE SUN. A BIRD LANDS ON THE FEEDER.]

SELI: There is a bird.

NARA: Identify it.

KITO: Let it eat first.

[THE BIRD EATS A SEED AND FLIES OFF.]

NARA: Did anyone see the markings?

SELI: I saw it eat.

INT. PARK BLIND - LATE AFTERNOON

Nara checks the tally sheet. Kito lowers the binoculars. Venn reaches for the trophy.

NARA: We can run one final silent round.

KITO: If we do not speak, the birds may return.

SELI: We can watch without announcing them.

[NARA PUTS DOWN THE MEGAPHONE. VENN SETS THE TROPHY ON THE FLOOR.]

KITO: The birds are back in the tree.

NARA: Do not call the species yet.

SELI: One is carrying a twig.

KITO: That is a nest-building behavior.

NARA: Does that count as a point?

SELI: It counts as a bird.

[NARA CROSSES OUT THE EMPTY BRANCHES. THE BIRDS STAY IN THE TREE.]

VENN: The contest is ending.

KITO: The watch can continue.

EXT. PARK FEEDER - CLOSING

Venn removes the tripod. Kito and Seli pack the binoculars. Nara fills in the scorecard.

NARA: The winner is the feeder.

KITO: It did not enter.

SELI: It hosted the birds.

[Venn hangs the generic trophy beside the feeder. The metal wing flashes; the birds move to a nearby tree.]

NARA: The trophy has made a sighting.

KITO: The tree has made an exit.
