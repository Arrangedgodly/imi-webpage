# The Botanical Garden Renames the Fern

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: botanical institutions that turn a clear plant label into an academic naming dispute*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Curator Tavi, a gibbon; Label Writer Nera, a macaque; Gardener Olo, a baboon; and Visitor Runi, a capuchin.

INT. BOTANICAL GARDEN GREENHOUSE - DAY

A fern sits beneath a sign reading "FERN." Nera holds three replacement labels. Tavi studies them. Olo trims a dead frond.

TAVI: The label needs more context.

NERA: It needs the plant's name.

TAVI: We can call it "Ancestral Frond Display."

OLO: It is a fern.

RUNI: I came to see the fern.

TAVI: The sign is too plain for a botanical garden.

NERA: The plant is plain too. It is green and growing.

TAVI: It has a history.

OLO: It has been here for two years.

[TAVI TURNS THE SIGN AROUND. THE BACK SAYS "PLEASE DO NOT WATER." OLO CROSSES THAT OUT.]

INT. BOTANICAL GARDEN GREENHOUSE - LATER

Nera arranges the labels on a table. One says "Fern, Common." Another says "Fern, Select Specimen." The third says "Living Frond Collection."

RUNI: Which one tells me what it is?

NERA: The first one.

TAVI: The second one sounds rare.

OLO: It is a common fern from the local nursery.

TAVI: We could note its journey from nursery to greenhouse.

OLO: It arrived in a pot on my cart.

NERA: We could add a label about the cart.

RUNI: I can see a cart in the next room.

TAVI: Does that cart have a name?

OLO: It has a squeaky wheel.

[OLO PUSHES THE CART. THE WHEEL SQUEAKS THREE TIMES.]

RUNI: I know that sound.

NERA: The fern is easier to identify.

TAVI: We could call the cart "mobile horticultural support."

OLO: I call it the cart.

RUNI: I found the fern without a label.

TAVI: We should not diminish its contribution.

OLO: It shades the bench.

[OLO MOVES THE POT. A PATCH OF SUNLIGHT LANDS ON THE SIGN.]

RUNI: I can read it now.

TAVI: The sign has better lighting.

NERA: We can put the simple label at eye level.

TAVI: Then the academic label can go below it.

OLO: Nobody reads below the bench.

INT. BOTANICAL GARDEN ENTRY - AFTERNOON

Tavi presents the new sign. It reads "FERN: A PLANT WITH FEATHERY LEAVES." The old label is framed nearby.

RUNE: That answers my question.

TAVI: The title is less grand than I hoped.

NERA: It is more accurate than we had.

[OLO SPRAYS THE FERN. WATER SPLASHES THE FRAME. THE INK ON THE OLD LABEL RUNS, REVEALING THE WORD "FERN" UNDERNEATH.]

TAVI: The old name has returned.

OLO: It was never gone.

[RUNI POINTS TO A SECOND POT.]

RUNI: What is that one?

TAVI: A fern.

NERA: We are learning.

TAVI: Could we frame that as a discovery?

RUNI: We just named the plant.

OLO: It is still a fern.
