# The Talent Show Has No Sound

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised talent contests, dramatic judging pauses, and competitions built around a deliberately silent performance*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: HOST, a gibbon; DANCER, a macaque; JUDGE, an orangutan; SOUND TECH, a capuchin; STAGEHAND, a baboon.

[A talent-show stage. A sign reads "SILENT ACT." The sound tech wears earmuffs.]

HOST: Our next act will perform without sound.

DANCER: I will do a mime about a squeaky wheel.

JUDGE: How will we know it is squeaky?

DANCER: You will see me react.

[The dancer pushes an imaginary cart. Her face twists with each silent wheel turn. The sound tech plays a squeak anyway.] 

SOUND TECH: Sorry. The cue fired automatically.

HOST: The act now has one sound.

DANCER: It is not the wheel I performed.

[The stagehand disconnects the cue box. The judge asks the dancer to start again. She moves the imaginary cart across the stage, stops, and points at an invisible wheel.] 

JUDGE: I can see the problem.

DANCER: Thank you.

JUDGE: I cannot hear it.

DANCER: That is the challenge.

[The host leans toward a microphone to announce the score. The microphone makes a feedback squeal. Everyone freezes.]

HOST: We have accidentally added a sound.

SOUND TECH: The microphone is live.

[The stagehand unplugs it. The host continues by holding up a score card. It reads "9." The sound tech gives a thumbs-up.] 

DANCER: Is that a score or a temperature?

JUDGE: It is a score. The act was clear without the wheel sound.

[The host holds up a second card that says "APPLAUSE." The audience claps. The stagehand gestures for them to stop so the silent act can continue.] 

STAGEHAND: The audience is part of the act now.

DANCER: I am trying to finish the cart journey.

[The dancer pushes the cart to an imaginary repair shop, takes out a wrench, and tightens the invisible wheel. She rolls it again. This time she smiles and gives a thumbs-up.] 

JUDGE: It is fixed.

HOST: We have our first silent repair.

[The sound tech puts a small card over the cue box: DO NOT AUTO-PLAY. The host gestures to the next act, but the next performer has not entered. The stagehand silently points to the empty wing.] 

HOST: We have a pause.

JUDGE: It is not an act.

DANCER: It is a transition.

[The next performer enters without sound and waves. The audience understands the show is continuing.] 

[The judge holds up two cards: one reads "ACT COMPLETE" and the other reads "ENCORE." The dancer points at the encore card, then at the cart, asking whether another silent repair is permitted.]

JUDGE: One more pass, but no new sounds.

[The sound tech removes the cue cable from the box and shows both ends to the stagehand. The stagehand covers the microphone with a padded cap. The dancer pushes the imaginary cart again, pauses at a crack, and silently lifts one wheel over it. The mime ends with a grand bow.]

HOST: The wheel crossed the crack without a single squeak.

SOUND TECH: I can confirm the cue box stayed disconnected.

JUDGE: The encore is also silent.

[The audience claps. The stagehand flips the score card face down until the clapping stops, then raises the 9 again. The dancer bows once more, then exits with the imaginary cart.]

FINAL IMAGE: The score card reads 9 while the unplugged microphone and covered cue box stay silent.
