# The Suitcase Carousel Is a Runway

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport baggage claims, fashion runway reveals, and travelers who recognize their luggage by tiny details*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: TRAVELER, a capuchin; BAGGAGE AGENT, an orangutan; ANNOUNCER, a baboon; DESIGNER, a gibbon; PORTER, a macaque.

[An airport baggage carousel turns. A red suitcase passes. The designer stands beside it with a fabric swatch.]

ANNOUNCER: Passengers may collect their luggage from the moving belt.

DESIGNER: The red case has a strong silhouette.

TRAVELER: That is mine. It has a strip of blue tape on the handle.

[The suitcase rotates into view. The tape is on the underside. The traveler bends to inspect but it moves away.]

TRAVELER: I will recognize it on the next lap.

BAGGAGE AGENT: Please do not chase the belt.

[The suitcase passes again, wearing a scarf that was not there before.]

DESIGNER: It has acquired an accessory.

TRAVELER: That is my scarf. It was tied to the handle.

PORTER: I tied it there when the case came off the plane. It was dragging.

[The traveler reaches, misses, and catches the scarf's loose end. The suitcase keeps moving; the scarf stretches like a sash.]

ANNOUNCER: Please release the fabric.

TRAVELER: It is attached to my luggage.

DESIGNER: It is now part of the collection.

ACT ONE

[The baggage agent stops the carousel. The suitcase stops just beyond the traveler. The agent uses a hooked pole to bring it closer.]

BAGGAGE AGENT: Check the tag before opening it.

[The traveler checks the tag. It shows the wrong flight number.]

TRAVELER: This is not mine.

DESIGNER: But it has your scarf.

PORTER: The scarf caught on the handle of the next case as it passed.

[The agent reads the tag. It belongs to the designer, who lifts an eyebrow.]

DESIGNER: I was wondering where my case went.

TRAVELER: My blue tape is on the next one.

[The agent restarts the belt. Two black suitcases pass together. The traveler points to one with blue tape. It has a sticker of a tiny banana.]

TRAVELER: That is mine. I put the sticker on upside down.

[The suitcase turns. The sticker is upright.]

TRAVELER: Or I put it right side up.

[The traveler waits for another lap to confirm. The porter notes a dent on the side.]

PORTER: Is there a dent shaped like a comma?

TRAVELER: Yes, from a suitcase with a dramatic handle.

[The agent checks the tag. It matches the traveler.]

ACT TWO

[The agent stops the belt again. The traveler lifts the correct suitcase and removes the scarf from the wrong one. The designer holds a garment bag open.]

DESIGNER: I can take the scarf. It matches the case.

TRAVELER: It matches my coat.

[The traveler wraps the scarf around their neck and rolls the suitcase away. The designer collects the red case.]

ANNOUNCER: Claim area is clear. Please enjoy your belongings.

[The carousel begins again with no bags. It revolves slowly like a display platform.]

FINAL IMAGE: The designer gives the empty belt a runway wave while the traveler exits with the correctly dented suitcase.
