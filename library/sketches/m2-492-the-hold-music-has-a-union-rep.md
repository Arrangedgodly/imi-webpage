# The Hold Music Has a Union Rep

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: customer-service hold loops, automated menus, and calls that become more complicated as you wait*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: CALLER, a capuchin; RECORDED MENU, an orangutan; MUSICIAN, a gibbon; SUPERVISOR, a macaque; TECHNICIAN, a baboon.

[A customer-service booth. A caller holds a receiver. On a small stage, a musician plays a three-note tune. A sign reads YOUR ESTIMATED WAIT IS TWO MINUTES.]

RECORDED MENU: Press one for delivery, two for returns, or three to hear the same options again.

CALLER: I pressed two. I am returning a lamp that arrived as a lampshade.

[The caller presses two. The hold music begins. The musician plays three notes, pauses, and plays them again.]

CALLER: How long has that been the song?

MUSICIAN: Since the company replaced its orchestra with a chair and one triangle.

CALLER: The triangle sounds tired.

MUSICIAN: It has been holding for eleven years. It gets breaks by playing the high note.

[The caller taps the receiver. The musician stops. A tiny bell rings from the booth.] 

RECORDED MENU: We noticed the music stopped. Press one if you heard it. Press two if you did not.

CALLER: I am pressing zero to speak to a person.

RECORDED MENU: I did not understand. I will transfer you to the department that explains zero.

[A light changes from green to yellow. The musician flips a card from BREAK to UNION MEETING.]

MUSICIAN: We are requesting a fourth note and a chair with a back.

CALLER: Can you file my return while we negotiate?

MUSICIAN: I can play the return theme.

ACT ONE

[The supervisor steps out with a clipboard. The caller stands and approaches the desk, still holding the receiver.]

SUPERVISOR: Please stay on the line.

CALLER: I am physically here.

SUPERVISOR: The instruction is automated. I cannot override it until the call reaches me.

CALLER: You are holding the phone.

[The supervisor checks the clipboard. It has a music note sticker on the caller's ticket.] 

SUPERVISOR: The system says your item is a lamp.

CALLER: It is a shade. There is no lamp.

SUPERVISOR: Have you tried turning the shade off and on?

CALLER: It has no switch.

[The musician plays the three notes in reverse order. The caller turns toward the stage.]

CALLER: That sounds like my complaint going backward.

MUSICIAN: The system marked your case resolved when I played the last note.

SUPERVISOR: That is a serious problem. Technician!

[The technician crawls under the desk and finds the music machine plugged into the case tracker.] 

TECHNICIAN: Every time the tune ends, it closes the oldest case.

CALLER: How many cases did it close?

TECHNICIAN: It says zero. Then it plays the tune again.

ACT TWO

[The technician unplugs the music machine. The caller's case reappears on the monitor. The supervisor prints a return label and hands it over.] 

SUPERVISOR: Your shade is approved for return. I will mark it manually.

MUSICIAN: May I have a chair with a back?

SUPERVISOR: Yes. And a fourth note.

[The musician adds a low fourth note. The caller sticks the label on the lampshade, which is resting upside down on the desk.]

CALLER: There. The shade is going back.

RECORDED MENU: Press one if your issue is resolved.

[The caller holds up one finger. The musician waits, then plays the new four-note ending.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The caller exits with an empty receiver while the musician sits in a chair that finally has a back.
