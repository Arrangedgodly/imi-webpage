# The Stapler Wins the Lifetime Award

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: celebrity award ceremonies, sentimental retrospectives, and speeches interrupted by stage production*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: HOST, a baboon; STAPLER, a capuchin; PRESENTER, an orangutan; STAGEHAND, a macaque; REPORTER, a gibbon.

[A banquet stage. A stapler rests on a velvet cushion beneath a spotlight. The host holds a card.]

HOST: Tonight we honor a machine that held our pages together through deadlines, drafts, and one very long menu.

PRESENTER: The stapler has served this office for nine years.

[The stapler clicks once as the stagehand adjusts the cushion.]

REPORTER: It has accepted the award.

HOST: We have not called its name yet.

PRESENTER: The envelope says "Lifetime Achievement in Fastening."

[The host opens the envelope. It contains three loose staples.]

HOST: The winner is the stapler.

[The audience claps. The stapler's top arm springs open.]

ACT ONE

[The presenter reaches for the trophy. The stagehand moves it closer. The stapler closes around the award ribbon.]

STAGEHAND: It has attached itself to the ceremony.

PRESENTER: We should let go slowly.

[The host tries to pull the ribbon free. The stapler keeps it. The reporter moves in for a close-up.]

REPORTER: The winner is holding the trophy.

HOST: It is holding the ribbon.

[The presenter asks the stagehand to open the stapler. The stagehand lifts the top arm, but the ribbon remains caught between two staples.]

PRESENTER: We need a speech.

HOST: The winner is shy.

[The reporter places a microphone beside the stapler. The stapler clicks. The presenter interprets.]

PRESENTER: It says it thanks the paper tray.

STAGEHAND: That is not what it said.

[The host nods solemnly. The reporter asks whether the stapler will retire. The stagehand checks the base, which has a fresh sticker reading OUT OF STAPLES.]

HOST: A difficult announcement.

[The presenter looks under the cushion and finds a full strip of staples. The host gestures for the audience to wait while it is loaded.]

[The reporter asks for a final close-up of the award. The host places the microphone near the stapler. The stapler closes on the corner of the host's cue card, pinning it to the program.]

HOST: It has accepted my notes.

PRESENTER: It has improved your filing.

[The stagehand frees the card and checks the stapler's base. The host gives the audience a fresh cue card with no loose ribbons. The reporter frames the cushion without leaning close.]

ACT TWO

[The stagehand opens the stapler, removes the ribbon, and refills it. The presenter sets the trophy beside it, outside its reach. The host reads a prepared tribute while the reporter holds a sheet of paper flat.]

HOST: For every packet held, every corner aligned, and every hand that found it when the tape ran out.

[The stapler clicks once into the paper. The reporter inspects it. The tribute is now fastened to the program.]

REPORTER: It has signed the speech.

PRESENTER: It has filed it.

[The host places the trophy on the table. The stapler remains on the cushion, ready for the next page.]

FINAL IMAGE: The audience applauds as the stapler closes over the printed award certificate.
