# The Trophy Is for the Doorway

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: award ceremonies whose oversized trophies create a second competition after the winner is announced*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Host Luma, a macaque; winner Bex, a capuchin; stage manager Orri, a gibbon; presenter Vani, a lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

[A HUGE GOLD TROPHY STANDS BESIDE A NARROW DOOR. A SIGN READS AWARD CEREMONY EXIT. ORRI MEASURES THE DOOR WITH A RULER.]

LUMA: Our award for best neighborhood garden goes to Bex.

VANI: Please accept this year's trophy.

Bex walks forward. The trophy is wider than the table. Vani tilts it toward Bex. It catches on the curtain and pulls the backdrop sideways.

ORRI: Keep the trophy upright.

BEX: I can't reach it while it's upright.

LUMA: We'll bring it down.

Vani lowers the trophy. Its base lands on Orri's foot. He lifts his clipboard to hide his grimace.

ORRI: The winner may carry it through the exit.

Bex turns toward the door. The trophy's handles catch on the door frame. Bex backs up, and the trophy drags the curtain another foot.

VANI: We could remove the handles.

LUMA: The handles are part of the award.

ORRI: So is the door.

Bex tries to angle the trophy. It wedges between the frame and the wall. The audience applauds, mistaking the struggle for a photo pose.

LUMA: Hold that position for the official picture.

BEX: I'm stuck.

VANI: The trophy has chosen its winner.

Orri asks everyone to hold the curtain while he clears the door. The curtain rod slips, and the backdrop falls across the trophy. The gold cup becomes a tent. Bex's voice comes from underneath.

BEX: Can I take it home like this?

LUMA: We can't present the award while it's covered.

VANI: We have announced it.

Orri suggests carrying the trophy out through the wide loading door. It is on the opposite side of the hall, past the seats. The stage manager asks the audience to remain seated while they move the award. The trophy cannot pass between the rows.

BEX: Could I receive a smaller plaque?

LUMA: The plaque says "runner-up."

VANI: We can make a new plaque.

Orri checks the schedule. The ceremony is already ten minutes over. Bex removes the trophy's detachable cup and carries it through the narrow door. The base stays inside.

ORRI: We have separated the award.

LUMA: The cup goes to Bex. The base stays with the ceremony.

VANI: Who won the base?

Everyone looks at the giant pedestal. The audience applauds again.

Bex returns with the cup in his hands and places it on the empty base. The two parts line up, but the trophy blocks the doorway once more.

[ORRI HANDS BEX A RIBBON WITH A SMALL DRAWING OF THE TROPHY. BEX WALKS OUT WITH IT. THE REAL TROPHY REMAINS ONSTAGE, AND LUMA ANNOUNCES IT AS THIS YEAR'S WINNER OF BEST DOORWAY.]
