# Motivate This

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: High-energy motivational seminars and personal breakthrough speeches*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Coach Brava, a baboon speaker; Limi, a nervous tarsier attendee; Obo, an orangutan stagehand; Sela, a capuchin instructor.

[STAGE: A SEMINAR PLATFORM. A BANNER SAYS "LEAP INTO YOUR BEST SELF." A TINY STEP LADDER SITS BESIDE IT.] 

BRAVA: Welcome, achievers. Today we will leap over every obstacle.

LIMI: The platform is too high for me.

BRAVA: Excellent. You have identified an obstacle.

SELA: The ladder has one step missing.

OBO: I used it to prop the projector.

BRAVA: Every challenge contains a lesson.

LIMI: The lesson is not to stand under the projector.

BRAVA: Write that down.

SELA: The projector is sliding.

OBO: I can catch it.

BRAVA: Use your growth mindset.

OBO: I am using both hands.

[PROJECTOR SLIDES. OBO CATCHES IT WITH HIS FOOT.] 

BRAVA: A triumph of agility.

OBO: A triumph of gravity.

BRAVA: Who here wants to rise above the crowd?

LIMI: I would rather see over it.

SELA: We can put the chairs in a half circle.

BRAVA: That sounds less dramatic.

LIMI: It sounds more useful.

BRAVA: We are here to break through fear.

LIMI: I am afraid of slipping.

BRAVA: Visualize a successful leap.

LIMI: I visualize a rail.

OBO: I can build a rail from the spare banner poles.

BRAVA: Do not let logistics interrupt inspiration.

SELA: It is holding the projector up.

BRAVA: Then logistics may continue.

[OBO SETS THE POLES DOWN. THEY ROLL AWAY.] 

LIMI: We need a wider base.

OBO: Two poles and a crossbar.

SELA: I'll hold the joint.

BRAVA: You are creating a support structure.

LIMI: We are making a step I can use.

BRAVA: Can I call it a breakthrough?

LIMI: You can call it a step.

OBO: The rail is steady.

SELA: The projector is safe.

BRAVA: Then it's time for the seminar's signature exercise. Everyone leap in place.

LIMI: I can't leap in place. My feet need room.

SELA: We can move the front row back.

OBO: And keep the poles tied together.

BRAVA: All right. We will take one step forward instead.

LIMI: That I can do.

BRAVA: One step forward, together.

SELA: The whole group moved.

OBO: The projector stayed still.

BRAVA: I feel transformed.

LIMI: You moved one step.

BRAVA: Transformation is a journey.

SELA: It is also a new railing.

BRAVA: My next seminar is "Build Your Own Step."

OBO: Bring a crossbar.

LIMI: Bring a friend to hold the other end.

BRAVA: Before we finish, write one goal on the cards under your chair.

SELA: There are no cards under the chairs.

BRAVA: Then write the goal in your hand.

LIMI: I want to reach the fruit shelf without jumping.

OBO: I'll build a lower rack.

BRAVA: My goal is to stop moving the ladder during the speech.

SELA: That's a useful goal.

BRAVA: I feel a breakthrough.

LIMI: The ladder has both steps now.

OBO: One step is still holding the projector.

BRAVA: Then we have a second goal.

SELA: Put the projector on the table.

BRAVA: The table is full of goal cards.

LIMI: Those are coasters.

[THE BANNER FALLS. IT LANDS ON THE CHAIR, NOT THE FLOOR.]
