# The Security Briefing for a Picnic

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate risk briefings that assign elaborate controls to a blanket and a basket*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Odo, a baboon safety lead; Tavi, a capuchin picnic host; Miri, a gibbon guest; and Piri, a macaque note-taker.

EXT. PICNIC FIELD - DAY

Odo gives a briefing beside a checked blanket.

ODO: We have identified four hazards: crumbs, ants, wind and sitting.

TAVI: Sitting?

ODO: It can cause prolonged sitting.

Piri writes every risk on a clipboard. Miri asks where the food is. Tavi points to a basket. Odo says it must remain closed until the basket is inspected. He asks Piri to record the latch condition. Piri writes "latched."

Odo opens the basket and removes a pear. He asks if the pear has been washed. Tavi says yes. Odo asks if the washing station has been inspected. Piri writes "unknown." Tavi wipes the pear on a clean cloth. Odo adds "cloth contact" to the list.

Miri spreads the blanket. Odo asks if the ground has been cleared. Tavi picks up a twig. Piri notes that removing a twig may disturb the soil. The twig is placed in a marked area just outside the blanket.

A breeze lifts one corner of the blanket. Odo orders the group to anchor it. Miri sits on the corner. Piri checks that no one has used an unapproved weight. Odo places the basket on the other corner. The pear rolls toward the edge. Tavi catches it.

ODO: We have contained the rolling fruit.

MIRI: We could eat it.

Odo asks for a food-handling approval. Piri hands him the clipboard. Odo checks the box marked "consume." It is not there. Piri adds one. Miri takes a bite. Odo records the first bite and asks if she is experiencing symptoms.

MIRI: It tastes like a pear.

Odo says the taste is within expected range. Tavi offers the rest to Piri. She asks if sharing is approved. Odo says it is a new risk. The group pauses. The wind blows the clipboard off the blanket. Piri catches it before it lands in the grass.

PIRI: Paper retrieval successful.

Odo adds "flying clipboard" to the hazards. Tavi says it is time to eat. Odo looks over the list. He notices they have handled the wind, moved the twig, secured the blanket and checked the pear. He signs the briefing complete.

ODO: The picnic is ready.

Miri has already eaten the pear. Tavi opens the basket and takes out another. Odo reaches for the clipboard, then lets it go.

ODO: I will document this after lunch.

Miri folds the clipboard under the corner of the blanket to keep it from lifting. Odo marks the final hazard as contained. Piri passes the last pear slice to Tavi, and they continue the picnic.

Piri sits down. The blanket stays still. The group eats while the risk list flutters harmlessly beside them.
