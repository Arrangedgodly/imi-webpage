# The Record Attempt That Needs a Ramp

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: record-breaking stunts and organizers ignoring accessibility*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: HOST TAVI, a gibbon; ORGANIZER PEL, a macaque; ATTEMPT LEADER SAI, a capuchin; and PARTICIPANTS.

[A field has a sign reading: MOST MONKEYS TO CROSS A LOG IN ONE MINUTE. A narrow log rests between two platforms.] 

TAVI: Today the troop attempts a new record: the most monkeys across a log in one minute.

PEL: We have a timer, two witnesses, and a log that passed inspection.

SAI: It rolls when someone steps on it.

PEL: It is a natural log. Some movement is expected.

SAI: Expected movement is not the same as safe movement.

[SAI TESTS THE LOG WITH ONE FOOT. IT SHIFTS.] 

TAVI: The log has moved before the attempt.

PEL: We can wedge it in place.

SAI: The ramp beside it is already stable. We could include both lanes.

PEL: The record is for a log crossing.

SAI: Then the log can stay one route. The ramp makes the course accessible to everyone.

PEL: The rules say participants must cross the marked course.

TAVI: We can mark the log and ramp as two lanes in one course.

PEL: The official may reject it.

[THE OFFICIAL INSPECTS THE LOG, RAMP, AND WEDGES.] 

OFFICIAL: Both lanes are safe. The rules allow multiple routes if the start and finish are the same.

PEL: We need to reposition the finish marker.

SAI: I can move it six paces forward so both routes join there.

[THE MARKER IS MOVED. THE LOG IS WEDGED. SAI CHECKS THE RAMP.] 

TAVI: The course is set. Spectators stand outside the marked lanes. The finish line is visible from both routes. Are all participants ready?

PEL: The timer is ready.

SAI: One participant needs the ramp. The log is clear for anyone who prefers it.

PEL: The painted line is hidden behind the log.

SAI: I can move it to the common finish. The ramp and log will meet there.

[SAI MOVES THE LINE. BOTH LANES NOW LEAD TO THE SAME MARKER.] 

OFFICIAL: I will count every crossing at the finish marker, from either lane.

TAVI: Starting in three, two, one.

[SFX: TIMER BEEPS. THE PARTICIPANTS CROSS IN A STEADY LINE, SOME ON THE LOG, SOME ON THE RAMP.] 

PEL: Fourteen, fifteen, sixteen.

TAVI: No one is waiting at the start. The lanes are moving evenly.

SAI: The last participant is on the ramp.

[THE TIMER BEEPS AGAIN. THE LAST PARTICIPANT REACHES THE MARKER A MOMENT LATER.] 

OFFICIAL: Twenty-three crossings before the signal. One after.

PEL: We have a new record of twenty-three.

SAI: And everyone had a safe route.

TAVI: The record board can list two lanes.

PEL: I will update the sign before the next attempt. The log stays, and so does the ramp.
