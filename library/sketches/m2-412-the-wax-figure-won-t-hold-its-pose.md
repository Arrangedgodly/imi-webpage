# The Wax Figure Won't Hold Its Pose

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum portrait sessions where the artist wants stillness and the subject keeps reacting*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Sculptor Vex, an orangutan; model Pip, a capuchin; curator Mara, a macaque; guard Nari, a lemur.

INT. MUSEUM STUDIO - DAY

[A clay pedestal waits beneath a sign: HISTORIC FIGURE, DO NOT TOUCH. Pip sits on it in a silver cape. Vex holds a sculpting tool.]

VEX: Hold the noble pose.

PIP: Which one?

VEX: The one you had a moment ago.

PIP: I was scratching my elbow.

[Mara enters with a tray of clay.]

MARA: The museum needs the figure by opening.

VEX: We need a heroic chin.

NARI: It has a chin.

[Vex pinches clay onto Pip's jaw. Pip opens his mouth to object. The clay stretches into a beak.]

PIP: Is this the hero?

VEX: It is a work in progress.

[Nari rings the gallery bell. Pip startles and turns. Vex rotates the pedestal to face him. The cape catches on the tool table and drags it across the floor.]

MARA: The figure has moved.

VEX: The pedestal moved.

NARI: The cape pulled it.

[Vex asks Pip to hold a heroic profile. Pip sits upright. Vex smooths the cape. Pip's hand drifts toward a bowl of grapes on the side table.]

VEX: Do not reach for the fruit.

PIP: The pose is hungry.

[The guests behind the rope lean in. Pip's reaching hand casts a long shadow on the wall.]

MARA: That is the exhibit they can see from outside.

VEX: It needs a title.

PIP: "A Monkey Reaching for Lunch."

VEX: Too honest.

[Vex places a sign beside Pip: HISTORIC FIGURE. Visitors enter. Pip stays very still. A child waves. Pip waves back automatically.]

MARA: The figure is waving.

VEX: It is welcoming guests.

PIP: I thought it was a parade.

[Vex begins adding a hand gesture to the sculpture. Pip's tail brushes the clay bowl. A lump lands on the cape and sticks like a medal.]

VEX: Excellent. A battle honor.

NARI: It is wet clay.

[The clay medal slides down the cape. Pip tries to catch it without moving his arms. His elbow rises, and Vex molds the motion as a salute.]

VEX: There. A statesman.

MARA: The museum label says explorer.

VEX: Explorers salute too.

[The visitors applaud. Pip holds the salute while Vex steps back. Nari looks at the sculpture. It is now a mix of beak, salute, and clay medal.]

NARI: Is this the final pose?

VEX: It is the pose history remembers.

PIP: History remembers me waving at a child.

[Mara changes the label to "The Welcoming Explorer." A guest tries to hand Pip a flower. Pip remains still. The flower lands on his knee.]

FINAL BEAT: The doors close. Vex asks Pip to come down. Pip stands, and the clay statue behind him keeps the salute. Vex puts a small visitor map in its raised hand.
