# Family Field Day

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: family competition reality television and challenge reveals*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: HOST ROXY, a macaque; siblings BIBI and TOTO, both capuchins; GRANDMA LEN, a langur; and a CAMERA OPERATOR.

[A grassy field. Three stations are arranged beneath a banner: FAMILY FIELD DAY. A wheel labeled SURPRISE CHALLENGE spins.]

ROXY: Families will compete for the golden acorn. First, the relay race.

BIBI: We have different stride lengths.

TOTO: I can carry the baton in my cheek.

GRANDMA LEN: You cannot pass it that way.

[The race begins. Bibi hops through the cones. Toto stops to inspect a loose one.]

ROXY: The clock is running.

TOTO: The cone is in the wrong place.

GRANDMA LEN: Move it, then run.

[Toto sets it upright. Bibi loops back to meet him.]

ROXY: That is not the course.

BIBI: We are using the course.

[They finish last. Roxy spins the wheel.]

ROXY: The surprise challenge is building a nest from provided materials.

GRANDMA LEN: These are paper napkins and pipe cleaners.

ROXY: It is a creative challenge.

TOTO: A nest needs twigs.

ROXY: Twigs are not permitted.

[Toto removes one twig from his own fur.]

ROXY: You brought an outside item into the competition.

TOTO: It was already on me.

[Bibi uses a napkin to cushion the broken cone. Grandma turns a branch-shaped pipe cleaner into a small arch.]

ROXY: The family has built a nest.

BIBI: No, we made the cone stop wobbling.

ROXY: The judges have awarded one point for innovation.

GRANDMA LEN: I would rather have two points for fixing the cone.

[The camera operator zooms on the golden acorn trophy. A real squirrel grabs it and runs.]

ROXY: We have a surprise guest.

TOTO: The squirrel won.

BIBI: It knew the challenge was about gathering supplies.

ROXY: We must recover the trophy.

GRANDMA LEN: Let it go. We have enough acorns at home.

[The squirrel climbs the tree and sits with the trophy. The family shares the napkin nest.]

ROXY: The final score is tied.

BIBI: We came last in the race.

ROXY: The scoring system includes spirit.

TOTO: Does it include the squirrel?

ROXY: The squirrel is disqualified for being a professional.

[The squirrel drops one acorn. It lands on the score sheet and makes the family winners.]

GRANDMA LEN: We will take the acorn. The trophy can stay in the tree.

ROXY: The family has won without the prize.

TOTO: Can we keep the cone?

ROXY: We need a family interview after the challenge. Bibi, how did you feel when the squirrel took the trophy?

BIBI: It climbed faster than I could.

TOTO: It had no obstacle course.

GRANDMA LEN: It also had a tree.

ROXY: Can you say you were heartbroken?

BIBI: I was worried it would drop the acorn.

ROXY: That's not as dramatic.

GRANDMA LEN: It is more accurate.

[The camera follows the squirrel. It puts the trophy beside its nest and leaves the acorn on the ground.]

TOTO: The prize has been returned.

ROXY: The family has won again.

BIBI: We did not win. We got our acorn back.

ROXY: We can call it a redemption arc.

GRANDMA LEN: Call it a squirrel taking a break.

[The family picks up the acorn and leaves the trophy in the branches.]

[BLACKOUT.]
