# The Mascot Has the Mic

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports mascot appearances that take over the broadcast while the actual game waits for the costume to stop interfering*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Kiri, a macaque play-by-play host; Piri, a capuchin mascot performer inside a giant fruit suit; Doma, a gibbon referee; and Seli, a lemur scorekeeper.

INT. SMALL ARENA - NIGHT

Kiri stands at the sideline. Piri wears a giant orange costume with a microphone taped inside the peel.

KIRI: We are ready for the opening serve.

Piri waves. The costume blocks the court. Doma whistles for the mascot to move. Piri backs up and bumps the scoreboard. The score changes from zero to eight.

SELI: That is not the score.

Kiri asks Piri to remove the microphone. Piri cannot reach it inside the costume. The audience hears muffled commentary.

PIRI (MUFFLED): The orange is in play.

Doma blows the whistle again. Piri turns, and the oversized peel spins. The ball bounces off the costume and rolls under the bench. Kiri describes it as an unexpected defensive move. Doma says it is not allowed.

Seli retrieves the ball. The teams line up for the serve. Piri tries to stand still, but the costume's bottom rolls forward, slowly crossing the sideline. Kiri keeps the broadcast mic pointed at the players, but the crowd is watching the orange.

Doma instructs Piri to stand behind the advertising board. The costume is too wide and covers the sponsor logo. Piri sidesteps. The peel swings back and reveals the logo for a moment, then hides it again.

KIRI: Excellent rotation.

SELI: The mascot is not on the roster.

Piri's internal mic turns on. The mascot's voice booms through the arena. He tries to say "Good luck," but the costume muffles it into a long, dramatic growl. The crowd cheers. One player applauds. Doma asks everyone to resume positions.

The serve begins. The ball arcs toward Piri. He tries to duck, but the costume's top catches the ball and carries it along. The audience gasps. Piri carefully crouches. The ball falls into his arms. Doma calls a pause.

KIRI: The mascot has possession.

DOMA: The mascot is not playing.

Piri hands the ball to Seli and unclips the microphone. Kiri announces the game can continue. The mascot exits through the narrow tunnel, turning sideways and scraping the door frame. The costume's peel catches for a moment. Piri waves from inside it, then pops free.

The team serves again. The orange costume rolls back onto the court, empty. Doma whistles. Seli stops it with one foot and updates the score.

SELI: The mascot is still on the court.

Kiri asks the audience to help push it to the bench. They roll it away together. The game resumes. Piri, now out of costume, sits down and takes the microphone.

PIRI: The players are back in control.

The ball flies toward him. He ducks.
