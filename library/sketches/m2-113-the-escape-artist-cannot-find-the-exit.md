# The Escape Artist Cannot Find the Exit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: stage magic and escape acts that treat ordinary access as suspense*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: MAGICIAN VEX, a gibbon; ASSISTANT LOMA, a macaque; STAGEHAND PIP, a capuchin; and an audience.

[A wooden box sits center stage. A sign reads: THE IMPOSSIBLE ESCAPE.] 

VEX: I will enter this box, lock it from inside, and escape before the bell rings.

PIP: How will you lock it from inside?

VEX: That is part of the mystery.

LOMA: The latch is on the outside.

VEX: Then I will ask you to lock it after I enter.

PIP: That changes the act.

VEX: It improves the suspense.

[THEY OPEN THE BOX. VEX CLIMBS INSIDE. THE BOX IS TOO SMALL.] 

VEX: I am in.

LOMA: Your tail is out.

VEX: The audience should imagine it inside.

PIP: The audience can see it.

[VEX PULLS THE TAIL IN. THE LID WILL NOT CLOSE.] 

LOMA: The prop is not wide enough.

VEX: Add a curtain.

[PIP RAISES A CURTAIN. VEX'S FEET STICK OUT BELOW IT.] 

VEX: The escape has begun.

LOMA: You are still in the box.

VEX: I am creating a false ending.

[THE BELL RINGS. VEX TRIES TO OPEN THE LID, WHICH IS NOT LATCHED.] 

VEX: It is stuck.

PIP: It is open on the other side.

VEX: I cannot see the other side.

LOMA: Lift the lid.

[VEX PUSHES. THE BOX TIPS OVER. HE ROLLS OUT ONTO A MAT.] 

VEX: The escape is complete.

PIP: You fell out of an open box.

VEX: A classic misdirection.

LOMA: The audience saw the whole thing.

VEX: Then I will do a second trick. I will disappear behind this curtain.

[HE STEPS BEHIND THE CURTAIN. HIS FEET REMAIN VISIBLE.] 

PIP: We can see you.

VEX: I am partially disappeared.

LOMA: We can move the curtain stand.

PIP: The stand is on wheels.

[THEY ROLL THE CURTAIN. VEX IS REVEALED STILL STANDING.] 

VEX: Now the curtain has disappeared.

LOMA: The curtain moved.

PIP: We should end with the box.

VEX: The next act is a rope escape. Tie me to this post and start the timer.

LOMA: The rope is already loose.

VEX: The audience should not know.

PIP: The knot is visible.

VEX: Then stand in front of it.

LOMA: The post is on wheels.

VEX: That is an advantage.

[LOMA TIES THE ROPE. THE POST ROLLS TOWARD THE CURTAIN.] 

PIP: Your escape has moved the post.

VEX: I am drawing the audience's attention away from myself.

LOMA: The audience can see you.

VEX: Can you stop the post?

[PIP SETS A WEDGE AGAINST THE WHEEL. IT STOPS.] 

PIP: The prop is secured.

VEX: The wedge is now part of the trick.

LOMA: It is a piece of wood keeping a wheel still.

VEX: I will escape on the bell.

[THE BELL RINGS. VEX SLIPS THE LOOSE ROPE OFF HIS WRISTS.] 

VEX: Behold. I am free.

PIP: The post is still tied to the rope.

[THE POST ROLLS AGAINST THE WEDGE AND STAYS.] 

VEX: The post is also free, emotionally.

LOMA: We need to reset it before the next show.

VEX: The audience has seen the secret.

PIP: The secret is that the knot was loose and the post had wheels.

VEX: Those are excellent secrets.

LOMA: We can close with the curtain moving, as before.

VEX: Let the curtain take its bow.

[VEX SITS BESIDE IT. THE BOX SLIDES AWAY ON ITS WHEELS.] 

VEX: It escaped.

LOMA: That is the first thing tonight that did.
