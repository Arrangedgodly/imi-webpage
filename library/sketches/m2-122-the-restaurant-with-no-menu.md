# The Restaurant With No Menu

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: overwrought restaurant reviews and tasting menus at a family fruit stand*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Critic Poro, a capuchin; server Mina, a macaque; chef Dabu, a baboon; and diner Lili, a gibbon.

[THE TABLE HAS ONE FIG ON A WHITE PLATE.]

PORO: I am ready to review the tasting menu.

MINA: We have figs.

PORO: What is the chef's concept?

DABU: Ripe fruit.

PORO: And the story?

DABU: It grew on a tree.

[MINA SETS DOWN A SECOND FIG.]

PORO: A paired course.

LILI: I ordered one fig.

MINA: We have a half portion.

[MINA CUTS A FIG IN HALF. THE JUICE RUNS ONTO THE PLATE.]

PORO: A dramatic reduction.

DABU: It is juice.

PORO: Does it pair with the plate?

DABU: The plate is clean.

[PORO SNIFFS THE FIG.]

PORO: Notes of sunlight, soil, and someone else's lunch.

LILI: It is good.

PORO: The diner has skipped the review and eaten.

[MINA BRINGS A BOWL.]

PORO: Is this the next course?

MINA: It is where we put the peels.

PORO: A rustic vessel.

DABU: A bowl.

[THE CRITIC TAKES A BITE. A SMALL SEED STICKS TO HIS NOSE.]

PORO: The texture is challenging.

LILI: The seed is on your nose.

PORO: A garnish with ambition.

[MINA HANDS HIM A NAPKIN.]

PORO: The service is attentive.

MINA: The napkin is reusable.

PORO: An ethical flourish.

DABU: It is a cloth.

[PORO WRITES IN A NOTEBOOK.]

PORO: The meal tells a story of restraint. One fruit, no menu, no choice.

LILI: You can have another fig.

PORO: That changes the concept.

DABU: We have plenty.

[THE CRITIC TAKES A SECOND FIG.]

PORO: The sequel is bolder.

[MINA TAKES THE EMPTY PLATE. PORO STOPS HER.]

PORO: Leave it. I have not reviewed the plate.

[MINA RETURNS THE PLATE.]

PORO: The ceramic is smooth, cool, and entirely unrelated to dinner.

DABU: It holds dinner.

LILI: I am full.

[PORO LOOKS AT HIS NOTES.]

PORO: I give the restaurant four stars.

MINA: Out of how many?

PORO: Four figs.

[DABU PLACES FOUR FIGS ON THE TABLE. PORO TAKES ONE.]

PORO: The staff has misunderstood the award.


[THE CRITIC ASKS TO MEET THE CHEF.]

PORO: Chef, what is your signature technique?

DABU: I pick the ripe figs.

PORO: And your plating style?

DABU: I put them on a plate.

[MINA BRINGS ANOTHER FIG. IT IS SLIGHTLY SMALLER.]

PORO: A miniature version.

DABU: It grew smaller.

LILI: It tastes the same.

[PORO TAKES A BITE.]

PORO: The flavor is consistent.

DABU: The tree made them.

PORO: The tree is part of the kitchen.

MINA: It is outside.

[THE CRITIC WRITES THAT DOWN.]

PORO: Outdoor dining, then.

[HE ADDS A FIFTH STAR BY MISTAKE, THEN CROSSES IT OUT.]
DABU: The tree does not know the star rating.


[THE OTHER THREE FIGS ARE EATEN BY THE WAITING STAFF.]
