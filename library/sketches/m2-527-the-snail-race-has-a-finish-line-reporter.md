# The Snail Race Has a Finish-Line Reporter

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: live sports broadcasts, photo finishes, and commentators trying to manufacture suspense in a very slow race*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: COMMENTATOR, a baboon; REFEREE, an orangutan; TRAINER, a gibbon; REPORTER, a macaque; SNAIL, a capuchin performer.

[A garden path with two chalk lanes. A tiny finish line sits three feet away. The commentator speaks into a microphone.]

COMMENTATOR: The competitors are at the starting leaf.

TRAINER: One snail is ready.

REFEREE: The other is still in the tray.

[The reporter moves the second snail onto the track. The snail stops at the chalk line.]

COMMENTATOR: A cautious opening.

TRAINER: It is looking at the finish.

ACT ONE

[The referee drops a leaf as a start signal. The snail moves forward. The commentator walks beside it, narrating each inch.]

COMMENTATOR: The lead has shifted. We may have a pass on the inside.

REPORTER: The snail is in the same place.

COMMENTATOR: It is considering the move.

[The trainer places a lettuce leaf beyond the finish line. The snail turns toward it. The referee checks the rules.]

REFEREE: The finish is straight ahead.

TRAINER: The lettuce is also straight ahead.

[The snail inches toward the lettuce, crossing the finish line by a tiny amount. The commentator throws both arms up.]

COMMENTATOR: A dramatic finish!

[The reporter measures the shell. The referee checks the time card, which has not been started.]

REFEREE: The clock was never started.

COMMENTATOR: Then we have a winner without a time.

[The second snail begins moving. The trainer moves the lettuce to the center lane. Both snails turn toward the leaf, leaving the finish line behind.]

REPORTER: The race has become lunch.

TRAINER: The lettuce is for after the race.

[The referee places two separate leaves at the finish line. The snails move forward again, each one taking a different lane. The commentator tries to keep both in frame.]

[The referee sets a small timer and asks the commentator to announce the start only after both snails cross the line. The trainer lines them up again. The commentator starts early, then stops when the referee raises a hand.]

COMMENTATOR: We are waiting for the start.

REPORTER: That is the most accurate update.

[The referee drops the leaf. The snails begin moving. The timer runs this time. The commentator avoids walking beside them and instead holds a magnifying glass over the finish stripe.]

ACT TWO

[The first snail reaches the finish. The reporter starts the timer too late and records an estimated time. The second snail reaches the line moments later.]

REFEREE: Both crossed. We have a tie.

COMMENTATOR: A shared victory after a patient contest.

TRAINER: They were both moving toward food.

[The reporter offers each snail a leaf. The referee stamps the scorecard, then checks that the finish tape has not stuck to either shell.]

FINAL IMAGE: The commentator raises a tiny trophy while the two snails eat their way past the podium.
