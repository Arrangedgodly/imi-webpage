# The Science Fair Judges the Poster

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school science fairs that reward decorative displays over the experiment being tested*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Student Rima, a gibbon; Judge Kori, a macaque; Parent Venn, a capuchin; and Science Teacher Odo, a baboon.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

Rima stands beside a simple experiment: two cups of ice, one wrapped in cloth. A large glittering poster shows a mountain.

KORI: What did you test?

RIMA: Whether cloth slows the melting of ice.

VENN: The poster took all night.

ODO: The poster is impressive.

KORI: The fair scores presentation, research, and result.

RIMA: The wrapped ice melted slower.

KORI: Where is the conclusion on the poster?

[RIMA POINTS TO A SMALL NOTE UNDER A LARGE PAPER MOUNTAIN.]

KORI: I can barely see it.

RIMA: The sentence says cloth slows melting.

ODO: The test was repeated three times.

VENN: I can add a larger conclusion box.

[VENN PULLS A STAR STICKER OFF THE POSTER. IT TAKES PART OF THE PAPER WITH IT.]

VENN: The star was holding the corner down.

RIMA: The data is on the back too.

KORI: Why is the data on the back?

RIMA: The poster ran out of room.

ODO: The measurements are written in the notebook.

KORI: Can you compare both cups?

RIMA: The wrapped cup retained more ice each time.

VENN: I can fold the poster around the notebook.

RIMA: Then no one will see the data.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - LATER

The judges move between displays. Venn adds glitter stars to the poster. Odo watches the ice cup.

KORI: This display has a volcano, a rocket, and a movable sun.

RIMA: My experiment has two cups.

VENN: We can add a movable cloth flap.

ODO: The test should be finished before the judging ends.

[THE CLOTH-WRAPPED CUP HAS LESS WATER IN THE BOTTOM. THE OPEN CUP HAS A PUDDLE AROUND IT.]

RIMA: The result is visible.

KORI: Can you explain the method?

RIMA: I used equal ice cubes and checked every ten minutes.

VENN: Can we put that in a speech bubble?

RIMA: The poster has no room.

KORI: There is a blank corner.

[Venn adds the method to the blank corner. The poster's paper mountain slides down and covers the cups.]

INT. SCHOOL GYM - AWARDS

Odo lifts the mountain off the table. Kori holds two ribbons.

KORI: The prize for display goes to the volcano.

RIMA: And the experiment?

KORI: The result is clear. Your method was repeatable.

ODO: She gets the science ribbon.

VENN: The poster has a silver star.

[THE PAPER MOUNTAIN FALLS OFF THE POSTER. UNDER IT, THE TWO CUPS AND RIMA'S NOTES ARE STILL IN PLACE.]

KORI: The mountain was not part of the experiment.

RIMA: No. It was part of the display.

[THE JUDGES MOVE ON. THE OPEN CUP'S PUDDLE REACHES THE VOLCANO POSTER AND MAKES ITS PAPER LAVA RUN DOWN THE PAGE.]
