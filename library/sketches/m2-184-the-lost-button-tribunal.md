# The Lost Button Tribunal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: small-claims courtroom programs that turn a missing button into a televised cross-examination*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Judge Vela, a macaque; claimant Omi, a capuchin; respondent Nara, a gibbon; and bailiff Pito, a baboon.

INT. COMMUNITY COURT - DAY

Pito announces the case: Omi versus Nara, concerning one missing coat button. Judge Vela examines the coat on the table.

VELA: When was the button last seen?

OMI: Before the coat went in the laundry.

NARA: After I took it out.

Vela asks for evidence. Omi presents a photograph of the coat. The button is hidden behind a fold. Pito zooms in with a magnifying glass, which enlarges the fold but not the button.

PITO: The evidence remains obscure.

Nara says the button may have been loose. Omi says it was firmly attached. Vela asks how they know. They tug on a second button. It pops off and lands in the judge's water cup.

VELA: The court has produced another case.

Pito retrieves the button and places it in an evidence envelope. The envelope is too small, so he gets a larger envelope. The larger envelope is marked "miscellaneous." Omi says the missing button was blue. Nara says the coat had two blue buttons, both still present.

Judge Vela asks why Omi filed the case. Omi says Nara borrowed the coat and returned it missing a button. Nara says she did not notice it had fallen off. Vela asks if she will replace it. Nara offers a spare button from her sewing kit. Omi says it does not match.

Pito brings in a color chart. The court compares the buttons under three lights. Under the first they look blue, under the second gray, under the third brown. Vela orders the lights turned off. They look blue again.

VELA: The court finds the color variable.

Nara offers to sew the spare button on. Omi says he wants a public apology. Vela asks what words. Omi says, "I am sorry the button fell off." Nara repeats it. Omi asks her to say it with more feeling. Nara says she is sorry the button fell off, and that the court has found the spare button is brownish blue.

Pito asks if the case is settled. Vela says the coat must be repaired before the court can close. Nara threads a needle. Omi holds the coat. Vela supervises. Pito reads the sewing instructions aloud as if announcing a verdict. The button is secured. Nara ties a knot and clips the thread.

Omi tries the coat on. The button holds. He says it is acceptable. Vela dismisses the case. Pito places the spare button in the evidence envelope and returns it to Nara.

PITO: The court has returned the button.

NARA: That is the replacement.

Vela stamps the file CLOSED. The stamp makes a blue mark on the coat. Omi takes it off carefully, then asks who will remove the stamp.

VELA: That is a separate matter.
