# The Mascot Has a Contract

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports mascots and sponsor clauses that turn an natural animal behavior into a licensing dispute*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Mascot supervisor Piri, a gibbon; sponsor representative Vex, a capuchin; player Noma, a macaque; and mascot Tuck, a baboon.

[THE COURT IS SET UP BESIDE A BASKETBALL HOOP. TUCK WEARS A LEAF COSTUME.]

PIRI: The mascot contract allows waving, dancing, and pointing to sponsors.

VEX: It also requires the mascot to smile at the camera.

TUCK: I do not smile on command.

VEX: It is in section four.

[TUCK TURNS HIS BACK AND GROOMS HIS SHOULDER.]

PIRI: That is not a smile.

VEX: It is a brand engagement.

[NOMA THROWS A BALL. IT BOUNCES OFF THE HOOP AND ROLLS TOWARD TUCK.]

TUCK: The game is in my area.

PIRI: The mascot should avoid the ball.

VEX: Unless the ball features our logo.

[TUCK CATCHES THE BALL AND HOLDS IT. THE SPONSOR LOGO IS FACING THE CROWD.]

VEX: Excellent placement.

NOMA: Can I have the ball back?

VEX: The contract says the mascot may hold it for up to twelve seconds.

PIRI: Why?

VEX: Visibility.

[TUCK TAPS THE BALL ON THE GROUND. THE LOGO SPINS.]

VEX: Stop. We need the logo still.

TUCK: The ball is round.

[VEX OPENS THE CONTRACT TO A NEW PAGE.]

VEX: There is an addendum. The mascot may not groom during sponsored appearances.

[TUCK HAS A LEAF STUCK TO HIS COSTUME. HE PICKS IT OFF.]

PIRI: Was that grooming?

VEX: It was maintenance.

NOMA: The game is paused.

[PIRI TAKES THE CONTRACT, PULLS A LEAF FROM THE COSTUME, AND USES IT TO WIPE A DUSTY SPONSOR LOGO.]

PIRI: The logo is visible now.

VEX: That is approved.

[TUCK POINTS AT THE SCOREBOARD. IT READS 0-0.]

TUCK: Can I point to the score?

VEX: Is the scoreboard a sponsor?

NOMA: The crowd is.

[VEX CHECKS THE CONTRACT.]

VEX: The crowd is not listed.

PIRI: Then we can point at them.

[TUCK WAVES TO THE CROWD. THE CROWD WAVES BACK. VEX TRIES TO STOP HIM, BUT THE BALL BOUNCES AWAY.]

NOMA: The ball is loose again.

[TUCK CHASES IT, LEAF COSTUME FLAPPING. HE SLIDES INTO THE BALL, STOPS IT, THEN TURNS THE LOGO TOWARD VEX.]

TUCK: Is this good placement?

VEX: It is a little too close.


[VEX POINTS TO THE NEW CLAUSE.]

VEX: The mascot still cannot leave the sponsored area.

TUCK: The ball left the area.

VEX: Then the ball is in violation.

[NOMA ROLLS IT BACK UNDER THE HOOP.]

PIRI: That is where it belongs.
[THE REFEREE WHISTLES. TUCK WAVES AT THE CROWD.]


PIRI: The contract now has a practical exception.


TUCK: I can still point at the crowd.


[TUCK ROLLS THE BALL AWAY. NOMA RESUMES THE GAME. PIRI ADDS A NEW CONTRACT CLAUSE: "MASCOT MAY PLAY IF BALL ROLLS INTO THEIR ZONE."]
