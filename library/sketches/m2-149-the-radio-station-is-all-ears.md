# The Radio Station Is All Ears

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: radio station management and soundproofing gone wrong when the quiet room contains a real troop*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Host Kiri, a gibbon; engineer Rolo, a macaque; manager Tavi, a baboon; and callers.

[A RADIO BOOTH HAS A SIGN: "ON AIR. QUIET." KIRI SPEAKS INTO THE MICROPHONE.]

KIRI: Good morning. You are listening to Canopy FM.

[THE SOUND METER JUMPS.]

ROLO: There is noise in the quiet room.

TAVI: The quiet room is soundproof.

[THE ROOM DOOR OPENS. A TROOP SITS INSIDE.]

KIRI: We have an audience.

TAVI: They are not on the schedule.

[A MONKEY GROOMS. THE MICROPHONE PICKS UP A SOFT SCRATCHING SOUND.]

ROLO: It sounds like rain.

KIRI: We can use it as ambience.

TAVI: No unscheduled sounds.

[THE HOST INTRODUCES A TRAFFIC REPORT.]

KIRI: The path is clear from the east tree to the water bowl.

[THE TROOP RUSTLES.]

ROLO: The path is no longer clear.

[THE MONKEYS LEAVE THE QUIET ROOM, WALK THROUGH THE BOOTH, AND EXIT.]

TAVI: We have lost our soundproofing.

KIRI: The room is empty.

ROLO: The sound meter is still high.

[THE DOOR SWINGS. IT CREAKS.]

TAVI: That is the noise.

[ROLO OILS THE HINGE. THE CREAK STOPS.]

KIRI: Our listeners have heard a live demonstration of maintenance.

[THE PHONE RINGS.]

CALLER: I heard the monkeys.

KIRI: They were in the studio.

CALLER: Can I hear them again?

TAVI: We do not replay unscheduled sounds.

[THE TROOP RETURNS, CARRYING A METAL BOWL. IT CLANGS ON THE FLOOR.]

ROLO: The room has a new sound source.

KIRI: It is our lunch break.

[TAVI TAKES THE BOWL. A MONKEY TAKES IT BACK.]

TAVI: We need to keep the broadcast area clear.

KIRI: We need to keep the bowl in the quiet room.

[THEY MOVE THE MICROPHONE. THE SOUND METER FALLS.]

ROLO: Silence.

[THE TROOP BEGINS GROOMING AGAIN.]

KIRI: Not silence.

ROLO: Better sound.

[ROLO PUTS HEADPHONES ON THE TROOP.]

ROLO: We need to check how much sound is coming through.

[THE MONKEYS REMOVE THE HEADPHONES AND GROOM ONE ANOTHER.]

KIRI: They have rejected the test.

TAVI: The room is not soundproof if they can open the door.

ROLO: We can add a latch.

[ROLO CLOSES THE DOOR. THE TROOP IS QUIET.]

KIRI: We have silence.

[THE SOUND METER STAYS HIGH.]

ROLO: It is picking up the air conditioner.

TAVI: Turn it off.

[ROLO SWITCHES IT OFF. KIRI'S PAPER SCRIPT FLUTTERS FROM THE VENT.]

KIRI: The script is making its own sound.

[THE HOST HOLDS IT STILL. THE TROOP INSIDE STARTS TAPPING THE GLASS.]

ROLO: New source.

TAVI: We will put the quiet room back on the schedule.

[THE HEADPHONES ARE PUT BACK ON THE RACK.]

[THE HOST SIGNS OFF.]

KIRI: Thanks for listening. Please leave the room as you found it.

KIRI: The signal is clear.


[THE MONKEYS LEAVE THE BOWL IN THE BOOTH.]
