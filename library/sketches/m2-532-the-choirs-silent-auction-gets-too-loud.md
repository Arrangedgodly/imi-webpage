# The Choir's Silent Auction Gets Too Loud

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: charity auctions, bidding-war melodrama, and groups who cannot keep a silent auction silent*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: AUCTIONEER, a gibbon; CHOIR DIRECTOR, a baboon; ALTO, an orangutan; BIDDER, a macaque; VOLUNTEER, a capuchin.

[A church hall. Tables display donated objects and bid sheets. A sign reads SILENT AUCTION. The choir sits behind a rope with pencils. The auctioneer speaks in a whisper.]

AUCTIONEER: Please write your bid. Do not call it out.

[The alto raises a hand and sings a soft note.]

CHOIR DIRECTOR: We are not performing yet.

ALTO: I was testing the room.

VOLUNTEER: The room heard you.

[The bidder writes a number on a clipboard. The choir director squints at it.]

CHOIR DIRECTOR: Is that a bid or a lyric?

BIDDER: It is the price I am willing to pay.

ACT ONE

[The auctioneer points at a ceramic teapot. The alto harmonizes with the auctioneer's gesture. Three choir members join in a low chord.]

AUCTIONEER: The teapot is still silent.

CHOIR DIRECTOR: They are reading the bid sheet.

ALTO: The numbers have a natural minor quality.

[The volunteer takes the choir's pencils, but the singers start tapping their knees in rhythm. Every new bid makes the rhythm faster. The bidder writes a higher number; the choir swells.]

BIDDER: I am not bidding against the choir.

CHOIR DIRECTOR: They are not bidding. They are building suspense.

VOLUNTEER: The auction closes in five minutes.

[The auctioneer tries to signal with a raised card. The choir interprets it as a cue and sings one short, dramatic chord. A child at another table writes a bid in crayon. The volunteer checks the sheet.]

VOLUNTEER: The child has offered three drawings and a button.

AUCTIONEER: Those are acceptable donations.

[The director tells the choir to hold their breath. They do. The hall becomes quiet enough to hear the teapot's lid rattle as the air vent starts.]

BIDDER: The teapot is responding.

ALTO: It wants a solo.

[The volunteer places a felt pad under the lid. The rattle stops. The auctioneer points to the clock: there are two minutes left before the bids are counted.]

[The auctioneer asks the choir to bid silently with number cards. The alto raises a card on the beat; the bidder thinks it is a price and raises his own card. The volunteer checks the sheet and finds the teapot has received a bid of three hundred and two. The bidder insists his card showed a two. The auctioneer separates the choir behind the rope and puts the price cards on the table.]

AUCTIONEER: Now the numbers are the only voices.

ALTO: I can hum internally.

ACT TWO

[The choir director asks the auctioneer to announce the results in writing. The volunteer posts a card beside each item. The choir hums silently with closed mouths, cheeks puffing.]

AUCTIONEER: The teapot is sold.

[The child carries it carefully to the collection table. The bidder asks for the drawings instead. The volunteer trades one for a spare button.]

CHOIR DIRECTOR: No encore.

ALTO: I can hum it later.

FINAL IMAGE: The choir leaves in silence, still carrying the teapot's winning bid sheet like a sheet of music.
