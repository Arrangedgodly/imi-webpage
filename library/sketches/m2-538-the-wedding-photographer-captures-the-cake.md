# The Wedding Photographer Captures the Cake

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wedding photo direction and photographers who stage a candid moment until it stops being candid*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: PHOTOGRAPHER, a macaque; BRIDE, an orangutan; GROOM, a gibbon; BAKER, a capuchin; AUNTIE, a baboon.

[A reception hall. The photographer arranges the couple beside a tiered cake. The baker holds a tray of extra flowers. Auntie is already taking photos on her phone.]

PHOTOGRAPHER: Look at each other like no one is watching.

BRIDE: You are watching.

PHOTOGRAPHER: I am looking away.

[The photographer turns around, raises the camera behind their back, and clicks. The groom blinks.]

GROOM: That was not candid.

AUNTIE: I caught it too.

ACT ONE

[The photographer moves the couple toward the cake for a romantic close-up. The baker asks them to stay clear of the table while he adjusts the bottom tier.]

BAKER: The cake is leaning.

PHOTOGRAPHER: Hold that look.

BRIDE: The cake is leaning.

[The couple braces the table. The photographer photographs their hands, then asks them to repeat the moment with softer expressions. Auntie copies the pose. The cake wobbles again.]

GROOM: Every time we look romantic, the cake gets nervous.

[The baker slides a support under the board. The photographer asks the couple to cut the cake. The knife is on the other side of the room. Auntie reaches for it, but the photographer directs her to hold the bouquet instead.]

AUNTIE: I am not in the wedding party.

PHOTOGRAPHER: You are in the frame.

[The bride picks up the knife herself. The photographer says to wait for the perfect light. The hall lights shift as the band begins a dance number. The cake's shadow stretches across the couple's faces.]

PHOTOGRAPHER: The mood is cinematic.

BAKER: The top tier is slowly sliding.

[The groom catches the cake board. The photographer clicks. The baker steadies the stand. The bride cuts one slice and offers it to the groom. The photographer steps closer, blocking the auntie's phone.]

AUNTIE: I have the wide shot.

PHOTOGRAPHER: Please lower your phone.

AUNTIE: It is the only camera showing the cake upright.

[The photographer looks at the display and sees that all the formal shots have the tilted cake in the background. The couple asks to use the photograph of them holding it steady.]

ACT TWO

[The photographer moves the cake to a stable counter and takes one real photograph of the couple laughing with flour on their sleeves. Auntie lowers her phone. The baker serves the first slice. The photographer checks the image and nods.]

PHOTOGRAPHER: That one is candid.

BRIDE: We were holding the cake.

GROOM: And thinking about dessert.

[The photographer turns the camera screen toward the couple. The cake is upright, the light is good, and the couple's sleeves are dusted with flour. The auntie asks whether she should move out of frame. The photographer says she can stay, but she must stop posing the cake like a rival.]

AUNTIE: The cake has better posture.

BAKER: It has more support.

FINAL IMAGE: The wedding album opens to the couple's laughing photo; in the background, Auntie poses the cake for its own portrait.
