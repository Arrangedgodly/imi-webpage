# The Seed Pod Launch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Startup pitch contests, investor panels, and tech-launch demos*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Founder Jori, a capuchin; investor Mako, a gorilla; investor Biri, a macaque; host Sena, a lemur; engineer Tabb, a baboon.

[STAGE: A PITCH ROOM WITH A PROJECTOR, A POD, AND A SLIDE TITLED DISRUPTING THE TREE.]

SENA: Welcome to Seed Tank. Founders have three minutes to change the forest.
JORI: Our product is PodPal, a seed pod that tells you when it is ready to open.
MAKO: How does it tell you?
JORI: It opens.
BIRI: Could it tell me before it opens?
JORI: Then it would be a different product.
TABB: We have a prototype.
[THE PROJECTOR SHOWS A DRAWING OF A POD WITH A FACE.]
MAKO: Is the face part of the technology?
JORI: It increases trust by 12 percent.
BIRI: How did you measure trust?
JORI: The pod looked happier.
SENA: Please explain your market.
JORI: Every tree has seeds. Every seed has a future. Our service helps the future happen on schedule.
MAKO: What is the price?
JORI: One fruit per pod.
BIRI: What if I own a tree?
JORI: You are our enterprise customer.
TABB: The pod connects by vine.
MAKO: Is the vine included?
JORI: The basic plan includes a short vine.
BIRI: How short?
JORI: It depends on your branch.
SENA: Investors, questions.
MAKO: What happens if the pod opens too early?
JORI: We call that a successful launch.
BIRI: What if it does not open?
JORI: We call that a premium waitlist.
MAKO: That sounds like a pod doing nothing.
JORI: It is conserving energy.
TABB: I will start the demo.
[HE TUGS THE VINE. THE POD BOUNCES OFF THE PROJECTOR AND LANDS ON THE TABLE.]
BIRI: It opened.
JORI: The technology works.
MAKO: It opened on the investor slide.
JORI: A strong early adopter.
SENA: Do you have customer feedback?
TABB: A squirrel used one as a hat.
BIRI: Did the squirrel pay?
JORI: In exposure.
MAKO: Can exposure buy fruit?
JORI: Not yet.
SENA: How much equity are you offering?
JORI: Ten percent for a basket of fruit.
MAKO: I will invest one plum.
BIRI: I will invest a basket, but I want two pods.
JORI: One pod per branch.
BIRI: I have two branches.
TABB: That is not how branches work.
MAKO: I invest the vine.
JORI: We already own the vine.
MAKO: Then I invest in your confidence.
SENA: Is confidence convertible?
JORI: After the next round.
TABB: The pod has rolled under the table.
BIRI: The product is leaving the pitch.
JORI: It is pursuing a new market.
MAKO: I will invest if it comes back.
[THE POD ROLLS BACK INTO THE ROOM.]
SENA: A remarkable return.
JORI: We accept all offers in fruit.
MAKO: Here is one plum.
JORI: We are now profitable.

[THE INVESTORS HOLD THE PLUM WHILE THE POD ROLLS PAST THE EXIT WITH ITS LITTLE PAPER FACE.]
