# The Museum Audio Guide Is a Person

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio guides and the pressure to sound authoritative*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: GUIDE ARI, a lemur; VISITOR BOK, a macaque; CURATOR NEM, a capuchin; and an audio guide voice.

[A GALLERY CONTAINS A SMALL STONE ON A LOW PEDESTAL. BOK HOLDS A HANDHELD AUDIO DEVICE.] 

VOICE: Exhibit one: an unidentified stone from the east path. Please listen while facing the display.

BOK: Is it old?

ARI: It was found this morning.

NEM: The audio guide says "unidentified artifact."

ARI: We do not know who moved it here.

BOK: I can tell you. I did.

NEM: You brought the stone into the museum?

BOK: I was carrying it to the path marker. I set it down to tie my shoe.

NEM: This is a gallery. We need to preserve the exhibit.

ARI: It is a stone from outside. We can take it back.

VOICE: Exhibit two: the empty pedestal, symbolizing absence.

BOK: That is the same pedestal.

NEM: The guide has advanced to the next track.

ARI: Can we pause it?

[BOK PRESSES THE DEVICE. IT BEEPS AND STARTS TRACK THREE.] 

VOICE: Exhibit three: the visitor's hand approaching the controls.

BOK: It is describing me.

NEM: The sensor is pointed at the pedestal.

ARI: The sensor is pointed at the entrance. Anyone who walks in triggers the track.

VOICE: Please step back to appreciate the approach.

BOK: I am stepping back.

[THE DEVICE BEEPS. TRACK THREE REPEATS.] 

NEM: We should unplug the sensor until we can aim it properly.

ARI: Then visitors can hear the description without being followed by it.

NEM: We also need to clarify the stone's label.

BOK: Write: "Found on the east path. Being returned there."

VOICE: Track four: the return of the stone.

ARI: The guide has made the return into a major exhibit.

BOK: It is a short walk.

NEM: We can take it ourselves and reset the pedestal.

BOK: What is track five?

VOICE: Exhibit five: the visitor requesting another track.

ARI: The device is recording questions instead of describing the stone.

NEM: I can switch it to the fixed description.

[BUTTON PRESSED. A TONE SOUNDS, THEN THE GUIDE RESTARTS.] 

VOICE: Exhibit one: an unidentified stone from the east path.

BOK: I can answer that.

NEM: The device cannot take answers.

BOK: Then it is not a guide. It is a recording.

ARI: We can leave the device on a stand and have a person explain the exhibit.

NEM: That costs more than a loop of audio.

ARI: It also lets the visitor ask why the stone matters.

BOK: It matters because I left it there and should take it back.

NEM: What if another visitor asks about the stone tomorrow?

BOK: Tell them the same thing.

ARI: We can put a card beside the pedestal: "This stone was left here by mistake. It will be returned to the path."

NEM: That is less mysterious than the guide.

VOICE: Thank you for completing the exhibit.

BOK: We have not returned the stone yet.

ARI: Then the guide can stop congratulating us.

[ARI UNPLUGS THE DEVICE. THE GALLERY IS QUIET.]

[THEY CARRY THE STONE OUT. THE AUDIO DEVICE CONTINUES FROM THE GALLERY.] 

VOICE: Thank you for completing the full exhibit sequence.

ARI: We didn't.

NEM: The device thinks we did.

BOK: Leave it on repeat. Maybe it will discover another track.
