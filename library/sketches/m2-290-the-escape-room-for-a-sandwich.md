# The Escape Room for a Sandwich

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: escape-room games, cryptic clue chains, theatrical countdowns, and teams that overlook the obvious door*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Game master Rala, a macaque; player Vex, a gibbon; player Neri, a capuchin; player Palla, a ring-tailed lemur; and builder Omi, an orangutan.

INT. ESCAPE ROOM - DAY

A sandwich sits under a glass dome. A sign reads: SOLVE THE ROOM TO EARN LUNCH. The door is unlocked, but a timer is counting down from fifteen minutes.

RALA: You must find the key hidden in the room, unlock the box, and retrieve the sandwich.

VEX: What is the first clue?

RALA: The first clue is on the wall.

The wall displays a picture of a loaf, a clock, and a tree. Neri studies it.

NERI: Bread, time, branch.

PALLA: Break time and branch?

VEX: The room is telling us to break a branch at the right time.

Omi watches through the window. Rala starts dramatic music. Vex searches behind a framed poster. Palla looks under the table with her tail. Neri discovers a loose floor tile.

NERI: I found a key.

RALA: That is not the key to the lunch box.

VEX: It is a decoy key.

The timer reaches ten minutes. They find a locked drawer. The clue on the drawer says, "Turn the key where the meal begins." Vex tries the key in the sandwich dome. It does not fit. Palla notices the door has a keyhole on the outside.

PALLA: We could leave.

RALA: That would not solve the room.

NERI: It would solve our hunger.

They continue. Neri uses the loose tile as a mirror to read a clue written backward under the table: "The bread is under the bell." The room has a small bell on a shelf. Vex rings it. A panel opens, dropping a second key into a bowl.

VEX: The bell was the answer.

PALLA: The bell was above the answer.

The second key opens the drawer. Inside is a note: "Look beneath the place where you sit." They lift the chair and find the box key taped underneath. The countdown reaches thirty seconds. Palla uses her tail to hold the chair while Neri unlocks the box.

RALA: Ten seconds!

VEX: We have the key.

RALA: Nine!

The box opens. The sandwich remains under the glass dome. Omi enters with a key and lifts the dome.

OMI: The room is complete.

NERI: We solved every clue and the sandwich was still locked.

OMI: The dome is for hygiene. The door is the only thing that locks.

The players stare at the open exit.

PALLA: We could have left at the beginning.

RALA: But then you would have missed the clues.

VEX: We could have asked for lunch.

RALA: That was not a clue.

Omi divides the sandwich into four pieces. Rala stops the timer at zero.

RALA: The room has been escaped.

NERI: We did not escape.

OMI: You are outside it now.

They eat at the table while the exit remains open. The next group peers through the window.
