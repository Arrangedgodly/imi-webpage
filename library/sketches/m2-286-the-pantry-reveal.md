# The Pantry Reveal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: home makeover competition shows, dramatic reveal curtains, and designers who renovate around a camera angle*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Designer Lomi, a gibbon; homeowner Vek, a macaque; carpenter Bex, a capuchin; judge Runi, an orangutan; and camera operator Tali, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. SMALL KITCHEN - DAY

A camera crew stands around a pantry. Lomi measures the doorway with a gold tape measure.

LOMI: This pantry needs a transformation.

VEK: It needs a shelf for cups.

LOMI: We can create a reveal wall, a snack island, and a dramatic hidden drawer.

BEX: The wall is load-bearing.

LOMI: Then we will reveal the wall without moving it.

Tali asks Vek to cover his eyes. She hangs a curtain over the pantry. When it drops, the curtain falls onto the camera. The crew resets. Lomi brings in a mirror, but it reflects the mess outside the kitchen.

LOMI: The space feels larger.

VEK: It feels like the mess is closer.

The judges arrive. Runi asks to see the storage plan. Lomi points to a drawing with two shelves and a plant. Bex has already installed two sturdy shelves, but the bottom one blocks the door when it opens.

BEX: The door swings into the pantry.

LOMI: Can we make that an intentional feature?

VEK: It keeps knocking over the cups.

Bex removes the door and hangs it to slide along the outer wall. Vek tests it. The pantry opens cleanly.

LOMI: The room has a hidden doorway.

VEK: It has a door that no longer hits things.

The camera operator tries a wide shot. The mirror shows Vek's tail pulling a chair through the kitchen. She moves the mirror. The pantry looks smaller again, but the shelves are visible.

Runi asks Vek what he wants to store. Vek places cups on the top shelf and bowls on the lower shelf. His tail reaches the latch and slides the door shut.

RUNI: The reveal works.

LOMI: We can add a brass label.

VEK: We can add a label that says cups.

Bex makes one from a scrap of wood. Runi holds up score cards. The judges award points for function, access, and a door that does not hit anyone.

LOMI: The camera found the perfect angle.

VEK: I can reach the cups from any angle.

The crew drops the reveal curtain. It catches on the new sliding door, then hangs neatly from its track.

TALI: We accidentally made the curtain useful.

LOMI: That is an unexpected design moment.

Vek pours tea for the crew. No cups fall. The camera holds as everyone drinks.

Runi checks the pantry door three times, then gives up and simply uses it. The camera operator tries to frame the new shelf behind him, but Vek's tail reaches around the lens and offers her a cup.

RUNI: This is the first reveal where the homeowner used the room before the credits.

LOMI: Next week we renovate a chair.

BEX: The chair is fine.

LOMI: Then we will reveal that.
