# The Queue Ticket Has a Queue

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: service counters that add ticketing systems to manage a line that already has a line*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Counter clerk Miri, a gibbon; customer Runi, a macaque; customer Kito, a capuchin; and floor manager Pava, a baboon.

INT. TOWN SERVICE HALL - MORNING

Runi enters an empty hall. A ticket machine sits beside a sign: TAKE A NUMBER. Runi pulls ticket 8. A second dispenser drops a ticket marked LINE A.

RUNI: Which number do I wait for?

MIRI: The number tells you where to wait.

RUNI: I am the only person here.

MIRI: That is why the line is moving quickly.

[Kito enters and takes a number. The display calls A-1. Kito looks around; no line is marked A.]

KITO: Where is A?

MIRI: Behind the blue rope.

[The blue rope makes a circle around the ticket machine. Kito stands inside it.]

INT. SERVICE HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Pava arrives with a clipboard and announces the new procedure.

PAVA: To join the queue, take a number, join a letter, and wait for your color.

RUNI: My ticket has a number and a letter.

PAVA: The color is printed on the back.

[Runi turns the ticket over. A stripe of ink appears on her fingers.]

RUNI: It is blue.

MIRI: The printer is low on toner. We are using the paper color.

KITO: My ticket is white.

PAVA: You are in line B.

RUNI: Is there a line A?

MIRI: Only when there are enough customers to form one.

KITO: There are three of us now.

MIRI: Then A has become a crowd.

KITO: The sign says blue rope.

PAVA: Line B uses the white chair.

[There is one white chair, placed inside the blue rope circle.]

INT. SERVICE HALL - LATER

The display flashes A-1, B-1, then A-1 again. Miri leans out from the counter.

MIRI: A-1, please.

RUNI: That is me.

KITO: I am B-1.

PAVA: You both have the same turn number.

RUNI: We could walk to the counter together.

PAVA: The system does not permit group service.

[The two customers step forward at exactly the same time. The ticket machine prints a third number and drops it between them.]

MIRI: C-1.

PAVA: The system has opened another queue.

KITO: For whom?

[The automatic door opens. A draft blows the ticket into the empty corridor.]

RUNI: C has left the building.

INT. SERVICE HALL - CLOSING

Pava removes the rope and turns the display off. Miri places a simple sign on the counter: NEXT PERSON, PLEASE.

MIRI: Runi, I can help you now.

RUNI: Do I still need a number?

MIRI: Not while there are two people.

[Kito steps to the counter. Pava reaches for the ticket machine, then sees the display is unplugged.]

PAVA: Who is next?

MIRI AND RUNI: Kito.

[The printer quietly spits out a ticket marked A-1. No one takes it.]
