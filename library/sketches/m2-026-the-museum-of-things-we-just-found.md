# The Museum of Things We Just Found

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Reverent museum audio tours and modern-art interpretation*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Curator Pella, a capuchin; guide Miro, a macaque; visitor Tavi, a gibbon; guard Rusk, a baboon; artist Nunu, an orangutan.

[STAGE: A GALLERY WITH VELVET ROPES AROUND A SINGLE SOCK, A PINECONE, AND AN EMPTY PEDESTAL. A SOFT CHIME SOUNDS.]

PELLA: Welcome to the Museum of Things We Just Found. Please keep a respectful distance from the discoveries.
TAVI: Is that a sock?
MIRO: The official title is Left Foot, Afternoon.
RUSK: It was found behind the coat stand.
PELLA: Discovery can happen anywhere.
TAVI: Is the pinecone also art?
NUNU: It is an invitation to consider a tree's unfinished thought.
TAVI: It looks like a pinecone.
NUNU: You have accepted the invitation.
MIRO: The audio guide will now pause for contemplation.
TAVI: It stopped working.
MIRO: Contemplation has no battery.
RUSK: Please do not touch the empty pedestal.
TAVI: What is on it?
PELLA: The space where certainty used to be.
TAVI: I can see the dust ring.
NUNU: Dust is the museum's most honest critic.
RUSK: Dust is why we have a cleaner.
PELLA: The cleaner is currently on break, which deepens the piece.
MIRO: At the next marker, turn left toward an ordinary door.
TAVI: Why is the door roped off?
PELLA: It opens to the storage closet.
NUNU: A threshold between public meaning and private mops.
RUSK: It also opens into the wall if you pull hard.
TAVI: Is that part of the exhibit?
RUSK: It is part of the repair bill.
PELLA: Please admire the label, not the hinge.
MIRO: Visitors often ask whether the sock has a partner.
TAVI: Does it?
PELLA: The other sock declined exhibition.
NUNU: A powerful statement about absence.
RUSK: It is in my locker.
PELLA: Rusk, we discussed this.
RUSK: I was cold.
TAVI: So the artist borrowed your laundry?
NUNU: I found it. The distinction is curated.
MIRO: The gift shop sells a postcard of the empty pedestal.
TAVI: A blank card?
PELLA: It is our best seller. People write their own exhibit on it.
RUSK: Some write complaints.
PELLA: Those are displayed in the staff room.
TAVI: Can I make a discovery?
NUNU: Certainly. What have you found?
TAVI: The velvet rope is tied to a chair.
RUSK: That chair is holding the west gallery together.
PELLA: A structural installation.
TAVI: The chair is wobbling.
RUSK: Then the installation is interactive.
MIRO: Please follow the exit arrows.
TAVI: They point to the pedestal.
PELLA: We have achieved a complete circuit of attention.
NUNU: The audience has become the frame.
RUSK: And the chair has become a hazard.
TAVI: I will write that on my postcard.
PELLA: Excellent. The show has acquired a review.

[THE CHAIR SQUEAKS, THE VELVET ROPE DROPS, AND EVERYONE STANDS VERY STILL BESIDE THE EMPTY PEDESTAL.]
