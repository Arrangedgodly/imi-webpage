# The Adoption Interview

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hiring panels that make applicants demonstrate impossible qualifications*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Interviewer Piri, a gibbon; applicant Odo, a capuchin; panelist Nara, a macaque; shelter volunteer Tavi, a baboon; Kit, a young capuchin who does not speak.

INT. ANIMAL SHELTER INTERVIEW ROOM - DAY

[Piri and Nara sit behind a table. Odo sits opposite them. Kit climbs across Tavi's lap toward a bowl of fruit.]

PIRI: Thank you for applying to adopt Kit. Describe your experience managing a small team.

ODO: I have a warm tree, a low hammock and room for two.

NARA: We asked about leadership.

ODO: My tree is not a company.

[Tavi gives Odo a chart marked COMPETENCIES. Kit grabs its bottom corner. Tavi slides it out of reach.]

PIRI: Tell us about a time you handled conflict.

ODO: Two neighbors wanted my blanket. I gave them the spare.

NARA: Did you delegate?

ODO: I handed it over.

NARA: Please use the word delegate in your second answer.

[Odo studies the chart. Kit climbs onto Piri's chair and peers over her shoulder.]

ODO: I can delegate a banana. Does that count?

TAVI: Rewards may influence the assessment.

[Kit takes Piri's pencil. She makes a mark on the clipboard, following Piri's earlier movements.]

PIRI: The candidate must demonstrate patience under pressure.

ODO: I am doing that now.

[Piri takes the pencil back. Kit reaches for Odo. Tavi sets a small stool between them.]

TAVI: Direct interaction follows the interview.

ODO: She is interviewing me with her arms.

NARA: First, a weakness.

ODO: I sometimes eat before finishing a task.

[He points to his empty fruit plate. Kit points to her own empty bowl.]

PIRI: You have completed the catering assessment.

[Odo takes a toy from his bag and sets it on the stool. Kit climbs down to collect it, then climbs onto his knee. Tavi lifts the chart so he can finish reading it.]

TAVI: Describe an unexpected situation and your response.

[Kit pulls Odo's ear. He gently moves her hand onto the toy's soft ear. Kit squeezes it; it squeaks.]

ODO: That one has a better sound.

NARA: Strong improvisation. Can you repeat it for our notes?

ODO: I am not asking her to pull my ear again.

[Piri writes DOES NOT FOLLOW REPEAT INSTRUCTION. Kit reaches over Odo's arm and takes the chart.]

TAVI: We also need two references.

ODO: Their names are on the back. They have seen the tree.

[Piri turns the page. Kit stamps her palm onto the empty bowl, then extends it toward Piri.]

NARA: She appears to be requesting further evidence.

ODO: She appears to be requesting fruit.

[Odo puts a piece in the bowl. Kit carries it to Piri, takes her pencil and draws a circle around APPROVED on the form.]

PIRI: We have not completed the interview.

[Kit hands Piri the toy. Its ear squeaks when Piri takes it.]

TAVI: Now she is assessing your response.

NARA: Remember to say delegate.

[Piri looks at the pencil in Kit's hand, then at the fruit Kit has placed in front of her.]

PIRI: Have I been hired?

ODO: You have passed the catering assessment.

[Kit climbs back onto Odo's shoulder. Piri stamps APPROVED, then keeps her own hands folded until Kit returns the pencil.]
