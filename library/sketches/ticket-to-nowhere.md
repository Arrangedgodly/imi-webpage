# Ticket to Nowhere

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Automated tech support chatbots and endless troubleshooting trees*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[OFFICE TECH SUPPORT BOOTH. A blinking screen. TECH RUFUS, an orangutan, wears a headset and holds a banana with one foot. EMPLOYEE MIMI, a capuchin, approaches with a laptop tucked under her tail.]

MIMI: The meeting room screen won't show my slides.

RUFUS: Is your meeting certified for indoor use?

MIMI: The room?

RUFUS: It's what the chatbot asks. I'm a primate escalation.

MIMI: Can you help?

RUFUS: First, please describe the problem in one sentence.

MIMI: The cable is plugged in, but the screen says no signal.

RUFUS: Thank you. I understand that you're having trouble connecting your device to the meeting room display. Is that correct?

MIMI: Yes.

RUFUS: Great. Is the cable plugged in?

MIMI: I just said it is.

RUFUS: The system asks me to confirm.

MIMI: Confirmed.

RUFUS: Have you tried a different cable?

MIMI: There isn't another cable.

RUFUS: I can submit a request for another cable.

MIMI: Will that fix this meeting?

RUFUS: The expected delivery window is three to five business days.

MIMI: The meeting starts in two minutes.

RUFUS: Then choose "urgent." That moves your request to the top of the queue.

MIMI: How long does urgent take?

RUFUS: Four to six business days.

[SCREEN CHIMES. A CHATBOT VOICE EMERGES FROM THE LAPTOP.]

BOT: Hello, Mimi. I can help. Please select the device you are connecting.

MIMI: The room screen.

BOT: I found three devices called Room Screen, Screen Room, and Screen in Room. Please select the correct device.

MIMI: The one in the room.

BOT: I'm sorry, I didn't understand. Would you like to schedule a session about room naming?

MIMI: No.

BOT: I can connect you to a live agent.

MIMI: That would be Rufus.

BOT: I can connect you to a live agent.

RUFUS: I'm right here.

BOT: I can connect you to a live agent.

RUFUS: It's very committed to the idea.

MIMI: Can I just borrow your laptop?

RUFUS: I can lend you the test laptop. It has no battery.

MIMI: Is there a charger?

RUFUS: Yes. It only fits the conference room clock.

[THE SCREEN CHANGES TO "PLEASE WAIT FOR SUPPORT." RUFUS REACHES BEHIND IT, UNPLUGS THE DISPLAY, AND PLUGS IT BACK IN. THE SCREEN LIGHTS UP.] 

MIMI: You fixed it.

RUFUS: The ticket must remain open for verification.

MIMI: It's showing the slides.

RUFUS: Please confirm that it is not showing the slides.

MIMI: It is showing them.

RUFUS: I need the exact phrase "issue resolved" for the form.

MIMI: Issue resolved.

BOT: Thank you. Was this interaction helpful?

MIMI: Yes.

BOT: Please rate your experience from one to five.

MIMI: Five.

BOT: You selected five. Please explain what we could improve.

MIMI: Nothing. It works.

BOT: I can connect you to a live agent.

RUFUS: Don't.

BOT: Connecting you now.

[HOLD MUSIC. A METRONOME TICKS. MIMI'S MEETING STARTS IN THE BACKGROUND.] 

BOT: Your issue has been closed for inactivity.

MIMI: I was presenting!

RUFUS: That's okay. The system marked you helpful.

MIMI: Did it keep the slides?

RUFUS: Yes. It attached them to the ticket and sent them to everybody in the company.

MIMI: So the support system distributed my presentation.

RUFUS: The room screen is also in the distribution list.

[SCREEN DISPLAYS "THANK YOU FOR YOUR FEEDBACK." END.]
