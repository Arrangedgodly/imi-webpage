# The Measuring Tape Has a Dress Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: costume fittings where production notes and last-minute alterations overtake the actor*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Pella, a lemur tailor; Nori, a gibbon actor; Sefu, a macaque director; Tavi, a capuchin assistant.

INT. COSTUME ROOM - AFTERNOON

[PELLA HAS A MEASURING TAPE AROUND NORI'S WAIST. SEFU HOLDS A SCRIPT. TAVI CARRIES A COSTUME WITH A VERY LONG TAIL.]

SEFU: The actor needs to fit the coat before rehearsal.

PELLA: I am measuring the coat.

NORI: Which one of us is supposed to stand still?

Pella marks a line on the coat. Nori turns to read the mirror. The measuring tape twists around the sleeve.

PELLA: Don't move your arm.

NORI: I wasn't. The coat moved.

TAVI: I carried it over.

Pella takes the coat and finds two labels: "HERO" and "HERO, FINAL." The final version has one extra inch in the shoulder.

SEFU: We need the final version for the rehearsal.

PELLA: Which final?

Sefu checks the script. It has a note: "The hero enters wearing the coat that fits." Nori tries the first coat. It buttons at the chest but pulls at the shoulder.

NORI: I can raise one arm.

SEFU: The role needs two arms.

Pella measures the shoulder. Tavi holds the costume. The long tail drags across a chalk mark on the floor.

PELLA: Stand over the line.

Nori steps on the line. The coat swings behind him and knocks over the mirror. Tavi catches it, but the measuring tape wraps around the mirror's frame.

TAVI: The tape is measuring the mirror now.

PELLA: It is measuring the coat's reflection.

Sefu asks for one more fitting. Pella pins the shoulder seam. Nori turns to face the mirror again, and the pin catches the curtain. The curtain opens to reveal the rehearsal stage and the waiting cast.

SEFU: That's our entrance.

NORI: I haven't put the coat on.

Pella hangs the coat beside the curtain and walks to the stage. The coat is still backstage. The cast watches her enter without it. Sefu checks the script.

SEFU: The hero enters.

TAVI: The actor hasn't entered.

Pella pulls the measuring tape from the mirror. The loose end catches the coat hanger and drags it through the curtain onto the stage.

NORI: There it is.

Pella calls for a pause and tightens one seam. The coat fits the hanger perfectly. Nori tries it again. He can raise both arms, but the long tail loops around his feet.

NORI: I can move my arms.

SEFU: Can you walk?

Nori steps forward, the tail catches the measuring tape, and the tape snaps back. The coat turns him in a full circle.

Tavi catches the loose tape and calls out a measurement. Pella writes it on the tag. Sefu asks if the actor can repeat the spin on cue. Nori tries, but the tail catches the curtain again. This time the curtain reveals the wardrobe rack, and three coats swing onto the stage behind him.

NORI: Do I have a larger cast now?

PELLA: You have a wider costume.

[SEFU CALLS IT A SPINNING ENTRANCE. PELLA WRITES "TURNING RADIUS" ON THE COSTUME LABEL. NORI ENTERS SIDEWAYS, STILL WEARING THE COAT.]
