# The Train Station Announces the Wrong Train

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public-address systems and transit announcements pretending certainty*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Station manager Sela, a macaque; announcer Vex, an orangutan; traveler Nari, a lemur; porter Pip, a capuchin.

INT. TRAIN STATION - DAY

[Vex stands at the microphone. A board reads TRACK 2: TRAIN TO THE COAST. Nari studies a paper ticket. Pip pushes a luggage cart with Nari's small suitcase on it.]

VEX: The train on Track Two is now arriving on Track Two.

NARI: That seems likely.

SELA: Don't improvise. The coast train is delayed. Track Two is empty.

VEX: I was building confidence.

PIP: I heard the train.

[From somewhere below comes a metallic squeal as Pip turns the empty cart. The wheels judder.]

NARI: That was the cart.

PIP: It sounds like it has a destination.

[Vex grabs the microphone.]

VEX: Correction. The train arriving on Track Two is a luggage cart with ambition.

SELA: You cannot announce a cart.

VEX: People deserve to know what is moving.

[Nari walks to the platform edge. The board flips by itself to TRACK 4. Sela hurries to reset it.]

SELA: Track Four is closed for repairs.

VEX: The board says it is open.

SELA: The board is stuck.

PIP: I can push it.

[He gives the board a small nudge. It flips to TRACK 1: TRAIN TO THE COAST. Nari starts toward Track One.]

NARI: Is it there now?

SELA: No. The train is still delayed.

VEX: We could say it is expected.

SELA: Expected where?

VEX: By the people waiting.

[A far-off horn sounds. Everyone looks toward Track Two. The cart has rolled down the slight platform slope and stops precisely at the yellow line. Pip jogs after it.]


[Vex points to the timetable.]

VEX: If we announce the delay at the exact minute, it sounds official.

SELA: It is official without a flourish.

NARI: My ticket says Track Two.

SELA: The ticket is correct. The timetable is not a promise.

PIP: The cart has no timetable.

VEX: It is running without delays.

NARI: My train is still delayed?

SELA: Three minutes.

PIP: The cart has a flexible schedule.

VEX: The cart to the coast is now arriving at Track Two.

NARI: Does it have tickets?

PIP: It has a suitcase on it.

[The suitcase belongs to Nari. She opens it and removes a scarf. A gust from the tunnel catches the scarf, which streams toward the station doors. Pip catches the end; the cart rolls backward.]

SELA: Don't let it depart!

[They hold the cart and scarf. Vex announces over the speaker.]

VEX: Passengers for the scarf service, please remain behind the yellow line.

NARI: My train is still delayed?

SELA: Three minutes.

PIP: The scarf is on time.

[The board finally flips to TRACK 2: DELAYED. Sela sighs in relief. The cart squeals as Pip parks it beside the board.]

VEX: The next departure is the luggage cart.

SELA: It is not departing.

PIP: It has a platform pass.

[The cart's wheel squeaks once. Vex raises the microphone.]

VEX: Final boarding call for one very determined cart.

[Nari takes her suitcase and waits beside it.]

NARI: I'll travel with the scarf.
