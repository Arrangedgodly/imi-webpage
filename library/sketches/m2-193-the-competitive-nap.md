# The Competitive Nap

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports commentary that turns a nap into a scored event with judges, stats and a dramatic replay*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Pavo, a gibbon commentator; Seli, a macaque athlete; Odo, a capuchin judge; and Miri, a lemur statistics keeper.

INT. REST HALL - AFTERNOON

Seli lies on a mat. Pavo speaks into a microphone.

PAVO: The regional nap final begins now.

Odo holds a score card for comfort, stillness and duration. Miri starts a stopwatch. Seli closes her eyes. Pavo whispers that she has settled into a strong opening posture.

MIRI: One minute, no movement.

A small monkey drops a leaf nearby. Seli does not stir. Odo gives her a high score for composure. Pavo asks if the leaf could affect the result. Miri measures the distance from the leaf to the mat and records it as an environmental variable.

The second contestant, Rell, enters carrying a pillow. Odo says props are allowed if declared. Rell adds the pillow to his inventory. He lies down and immediately adjusts it. Miri restarts the clock. Pavo calls it a tactical reset.

Seli twitches one ear. Odo lowers her stillness score. Pavo asks for a replay. Miri plays back the moment using a hand mirror. The ear moved, but the rest of Seli remained still.

PAVO: A controlled movement.

ODO: It is still movement.

Rell snores. Odo gives him points for depth. Miri asks if snoring counts as activity. Odo checks the rules. They define stillness as no visible movement, but the snore is audible. Pavo calls for an audio review. Rell wakes while they are replaying it.

RELL: Was that my best snore?

The judges confer. Seli remains asleep. A draft lifts the corner of her mat. Odo marks it as an equipment issue. Miri places a small pebble on the mat. It rolls toward Seli. Seli catches it without opening her eyes and sets it beside her.

PAVO: Exceptional reflexes in the nap zone.

ODO: That may be disqualifying.

Seli opens one eye. Odo asks if she is awake. She closes it again. The judges debate whether an eye counts as a full wake. Miri checks the timer and finds the competition has exceeded the scheduled break.

Pavo asks if he should keep commentating quietly. Miri motions him to stop. Rell adjusts his pillow once, then holds still. Odo tries to sit upright for the judging notes but his eyes close between sentences. Miri catches the clipboard before it slides from his lap.

MIRI: Everyone should be resting.

Pavo lowers the microphone. Rell returns to his pillow. Odo sits down and closes his eyes. Miri sets the stopwatch aside. Seli rolls onto her side. The hall becomes quiet.

A bell sounds, signaling the end of the nap. Odo wakes and reads the scores. Seli wins by one point. Pavo announces the victory at full volume. Seli puts the pebble over one ear and goes back to sleep.
