# The Compliment Department

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace praise programs that measure and rank every spontaneous kind word*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Dena, a macaque praise manager; Suni, a gibbon employee; Pito, a capuchin intern; and Varo, a baboon auditor.

INT. OFFICE - DAY

Dena installs a bell on the desk. A poster reads: COMPLIMENTS COUNT.

DENA: Every kind comment must be logged.

Suni tells Pito his filing was neat. Pito rings the bell. Dena writes "neat" on a chart and asks who said it, when, and whether it was specific.

SUNI: It was neat.

VARO: Could be more measurable.

Pito compliments Dena's handwriting. She rings the bell herself and awards herself one point. Varo says the point must be verified by a witness. Suni confirms the handwriting is legible. Dena adds two points to the tally.

A delivery monkey enters with a package. Suni holds the door. The courier says "thanks." Dena rushes over with a clipboard.

DENA: Please repeat the compliment for the record.

COURIER: I said thanks.

VARO: That is gratitude, not praise.

Dena asks the courier to compliment Suni's door holding. He says the door is heavy. Suni nods. Dena rings the bell and writes "strong door" on the chart.

Pito tries to give a compliment without ringing the bell. He whispers that Suni's map is clear. Dena hears and asks him to fill in a form. Pito says he meant it casually. Dena says casual praise is still praise.

The chart fills with words: neat, legible, heavy, clear. Varo audits the list and rejects "heavy" because it describes the door. Dena removes the point. Suni compliments Varo's attention to detail. Varo pauses, then asks if this is a compliment or an audit result.

Suni: It can be both.

Varo rings the bell. Dena asks him to log it. He writes "good question" and gives himself a point.

The office gets noisy with bell rings. Pito moves the bell to a shelf. A breeze rings it once. Dena turns to record who complimented the breeze. Suni unplugs the bell.

DENA: We cannot count that.

SUNI: Then it was just a nice sound.

Varo reviews the chart. Everyone has earned points, but the total is higher than the number of compliments. He asks if anyone can explain.

PITO: We complimented the same map twice.

Dena looks at the map. It has two copies on opposite sides. She removes one from the wall.

DENA: The second compliment was redundant.

Suni says Dena handled the problem well. Dena lifts the pen to log it, then puts it down.

DENA: Thank you.

The bell on the shelf rings from the breeze. Varo begins to write, but the chart is full. He writes the compliment on the back of the poster.

PITO: That is a good use of space.

Dena reaches for the bell. The cord has fallen off.
