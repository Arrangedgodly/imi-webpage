# Eau de Eucalyptus

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Luxury perfume advertisements and sensual product narration*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Vale, a slow loris perfumer; model Zizi, a capuchin; Director Sorn, a peacock; sound mixer Pock, a macaque.

[STAGE: BLACK SILK DRAPES. A SINGLE FERN. A GLASS BOTTLE ON A PEDESTAL. SOFT WIND.] 

VALE: Eau de Eucalyptus. A fragrance for the monkey who has everything except an unoccupied branch.

SORN: More mystery. Let the bottle appear through the fog.

POCK: The fog is from the humidifier.

SORN: Then let it be mysterious humidity.

ZIZI: The bottle smells like leaves.

VALE: Notes of rain, bark, and distant confidence.

ZIZI: It smells like the grove after a storm.

SORN: Say it with longing.

ZIZI: I miss the grove after a storm.

SORN: Better. Walk toward the bottle.

ZIZI: The bottle is on the floor.

SORN: The camera is low.

POCK: So is the pedestal.

[BOTTLE LID CLICKS. A SPRAY HISS.] 

VALE: One mist, and the world remembers your presence.

ZIZI: The world is coughing.

POCK: That's the fog machine.

SORN: I have lost the fern.

VALE: The fern is behind the curtain.

SORN: The fern has more screen time than the model.

ZIZI: It's a good fern.

VALE: The fragrance is made from fallen eucalyptus leaves. No tree is cut for our bottle.

SORN: Can we say "harvested by moonlight"?

VALE: They were collected at noon.

SORN: Can we dim the lights?

VALE: We can. We cannot change noon.

ZIZI: What is the bottle made of?

VALE: Recycled glass, molded to resemble a sleeping lemur.

ZIZI: It resembles a rock.

POCK: The nose is the cap.

ZIZI: I thought it was a dispenser.

SORN: The dispenser is the tail.

ZIZI: The tail is glued shut.

VALE: The product team is still testing it.

SORN: The launch is tomorrow.

POCK: The bottle can't spray.

SORN: We can pour it dramatically.

[ZIZI TILTS THE BOTTLE. PERFUME DRIPS ON THE PEDESTAL.] 

ZIZI: It is sticky.

VALE: That is the syrup base.

ZIZI: I smell eucalyptus and breakfast.

POCK: The sponsor left a jar of syrup beside the fern.

SORN: Then the ad is a success. The fragrance tells a story.

ZIZI: It tells me the fern wants pancakes.

VALE: The fern is not included.

ZIZI: Can I have the bottle?

SORN: Wait. Give the perfume one final close-up.

POCK: The lens is fogged.

SORN: Then this is an intimate close-up.

ZIZI: I can wipe it with a leaf.

VALE: Use a clean leaf.

POCK: The fern has dropped another one.

SORN: The fern is becoming our spokesperson.

ZIZI: It has not agreed to the campaign.

VALE: Then we will ask before filming it.

VALE: Take it. I will put the scent in a plain atomizer.

SORN: The atomizer has no story.

ZIZI: It sprays.

POCK: We can film the fern smelling it.

SORN: We cannot ask a fern to act.

VALE: It has already done more than the bottle.

[THE FERN DROPS A LEAF INTO THE PERFUME. THE CAMERA CLICKS.]
