# The Moon Landing Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: elaborate theatrical rehearsals that turn a simple prop reset into a full mission-control procedure*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Director Omi, a gibbon; Actor Lata, a macaque; Stage Manager Bex, a capuchin; and Prop Maker Venn, a gorilla.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - EVENING

A gray foam rock sits on a blue stage. Lata wears a cardboard space helmet. Omi studies a drawing labeled "MOON SURFACE." 

OMI: We need the lunar walk to feel weightless.

LATA: The helmet is heavy.

VENN: It is a mixing bowl with silver paint.

BEX: The cue is the landing light, then three steps, then the flag.

OMI: The flag must go into the moon at a historic angle.

LATA: It has a flat cardboard base.

VENN: We can add a dowel.

BEX: We can add a hole in the foam rock.

OMI: No visible hole. The moon is pristine.

[VENN HIDES THE HOLE UNDER A SMALL PIECE OF GRAY FELT.]

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - LATER

The landing light flashes. Lata steps down from a chair and sinks into a pile of foam dust.

LATA: I am on the moon.

OMI: You are on the dust cloud.

BEX: The landing light came on too early.

VENN: The rock moved when the chair touched it.

OMI: The moon should not move.

LATA: It is foam. It moved.

BEX: We can secure it with tape.

OMI: No tape on the moon.

[Venn uses two clothespins. The rock stays still.]

LATA: My first step is blocked by the rock.

OMI: The astronaut must overcome the terrain.

LATA: I can step around it.

OMI: The script says forward.

BEX: The prop says sideways.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - FINAL REHEARSAL

The stage lights dim. Lata reaches the foam rock and raises the flag. The dowel slides through the felt-covered hole and the flag leans sharply toward Omi.

OMI: The flag is falling.

LATA: It is planted.

BEX: The audience can see the flag.

OMI: The audience should believe the flag is stable.

VENN: I can wedge a moon rock under the base.

[VENN SLIDES A FOAM ROCK UNDER THE FLAG. THE ROCK ROLLS DOWN THE SLIGHTLY SLOPED STAGE.]

LATA: Lunar gravity is one-sixth of ours.

VENN: The stage is tilted.

OMI: Could we tilt the stage the other way?

BEX: Then the rock will roll toward the audience.

BEX: The flag is planted at a dramatic angle.

VENN: The felt is sliding.

[THE FLAG TIPS OVER. IT LANDS ON THE BLUE STAGE, POINTING STRAIGHT AT THE EXIT.] 

OMI: Can we reset for one more take?

LATA: The flag has chosen a direction.

BEX: It points to the exit, which is where the astronaut goes after the moon walk.

OMI: That is the end of the scene.

[Venn turns the foam rock so its hidden hole faces the audience.]

VENN: The moon is ready for the next crew.

LATA: The next crew can bring a lighter helmet.
