# The Silent Disco Has a Noise Complaint

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: silent-disco events that promise quiet dancing while multiplying rules for headphones and channels*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: DJ Ketta, a macaque; Venue Manager Rul, a gibbon; Dancer Pavo, a capuchin; and Neighbor Sela, a lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

Guests wear glowing headphones. Ketta stands behind two turntables. A sign reads "SILENT DANCE, THREE CHANNELS." Rul checks a sound meter.

KETTA: Channel one is disco. Channel two is bird calls. Channel three is spoken directions.

PAVO: Why is channel three a man saying "left foot"?

KETTA: He is the movement coach.

RUL: The venue must remain quiet.

[THE CROWD DANCES. THEIR HEADPHONES GLOW IN DIFFERENT COLORS. PAVO DANCES TO THE COACH'S DIRECTIONS, TURNING IN PLACE.]

SELA: I can hear the feet on the floor.

RUL: The event is silent.

SELA: The crowd is stomping in three different rhythms.

PAVO: I am on the bird channel.

KETTA: That channel has no beat.

PAVO: The birds are arguing about it.

[TWO DANCERS BOB TO A BIRD CALL. A THIRD TRIES TO FOLLOW THE COACH AND WALKS INTO A PILE OF FOLDED CHAIRS.]

RUL: We need a traffic pattern.

KETTA: The headphones have three colors.

SELA: No one is looking at the colors.

KETTA: They are hearing three different songs.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - LATER

Rul hands out earplugs to the neighbors. Sela enters wearing a pair of headphones from the lost-property table.

SELA: Which channel is quiet?

KETTA: Channel two has the birds.

SELA: The birds are very loud.

PAVO: Channel one is calmer.

[THE DISCO TRACK CHANGES. EVERYONE JUMPS. THE SOUND METER NEEDLE SWINGS, EVEN THOUGH IT MEASURES ROOM NOISE.]

RUL: The meter says we have a spike.

KETTA: No one can hear the spike.

RUL: The meter is supposed to measure sound.

KETTA: It measures the speakers on the headphones too.

SELA: Then it is measuring the music people cannot hear.

[RUL TURNS THE METER AROUND. THE NEEDLE FALLS. HE TURNS IT BACK. THE NEEDLE JUMPS AGAIN.]

RUL: The room is quiet when I face away.

KETTA: That is not how meters work.

RUL: I can see it.

SELA: I can hear you all arguing.

[KETTA CUTS THE MUSIC. THE CROWD STOPS. A SINGLE HEADPHONE BEEPS, AND EVERY DANCER TURNS TO LOOK AT PAVO.]

PAVO: Sorry. My battery is low.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - CLOSING

Ketta removes the headphones from the guests. The room quiets. Sela lifts one ear cup.

RUL: The sound meter is back to zero.

KETTA: The floor is still vibrating.

PAVO: That is my feet.

SELA: The quietest channel was no channel.

KETTA: We can add it next time.

RUL: A fourth channel?

KETTA: Silence. No lights, no music, no instructions.

[EVERYONE TAKES OFF THE HEADPHONES. THE CROWD STANDS STILL. THEN THE FLOOR SQUEAKS AS THEY ALL STEP TOWARD THE EXIT AT ONCE.]

RUL: The quiet exit is now a conga line.
