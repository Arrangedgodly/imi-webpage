# The Improv Exercise with Rules

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: improv comedy workshops that overstructure spontaneity*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: TEACHER MIKA, a lemur; STUDENT PEL, a gibbon; STUDENT FEN, a macaque; and STUDENT NIB, a capuchin.

[A rehearsal room. A whiteboard reads: YES, AND. Underneath are six rules.] 

MIKA: Today's exercise is free improvisation. Say yes to whatever your partner offers.

PEL: I am a lighthouse.

FEN: Yes, and I am a boat.

NIB: Yes, and I am the ocean.

MIKA: Good. Now follow the rule about not using props.

[NIB PICKS UP A BLUE CLOTH.] 

MIKA: That is a prop.

NIB: It is the ocean.

MIKA: It is a cloth.

PEL: Yes, and it is now both.

MIKA: The scene must have a clear objective.

FEN: The boat is trying to reach shore.

MIKA: Why?

FEN: To bring the lighthouse a sandwich.

MIKA: Is that in the premise?

PEL: It is now.

MIKA: You need a conflict.

NIB: The ocean does not want the sandwich to get wet.

MIKA: The ocean is a cloth.

NIB: It can still have feelings.

[THE CLOTH SLIDES OFF THE TABLE.] 

FEN: The boat has capsized.

MIKA: No physical danger in the exercise.

FEN: The boat is a cushion. It is safe.

PEL: The lighthouse can turn on a light.

MIKA: There is no actual light.

PEL: I can make a sound.

[PEL MAKES A LOW HUM. THE OTHERS TURN TOWARD HIM.] 

MIKA: Now the scene has a sound cue. Keep it consistent.

NIB: The ocean is calm.

FEN: The boat reaches shore.

PEL: The lighthouse receives the sandwich.

MIKA: What is the ending?

FEN: Everyone shares the sandwich.

MIKA: That resolves the conflict.

NIB: The ocean is still wet.

MIKA: The cloth is dry.

PEL: We have returned to the first rule. Say yes.

MIKA: Yes, and I will erase the other six rules.

MIKA: We have one more scene before the room is reset. The lighthouse needs a keeper.

PEL: I will be the keeper.

FEN: The boat is still beside shore.

NIB: The ocean cloth is folded.

MIKA: The keeper should give the boat a warning.

PEL: There is a storm coming.

MIKA: Do we need to create a storm sound?

FEN: We can use the cloth.

[THE CLOTH UNFOLDS. IT SWISHES LOUDLY.] 

MIKA: That is a large storm.

NIB: It is one cloth.

PEL: The boat has heard the warning and turns back.

FEN: The boat is now a cushion again.

MIKA: Props are not allowed.

FEN: It was already a cushion before we began.

MIKA: The exercise is to accept the offer.

NIB: Yes, and the cushion is now a boat.

MIKA: You have satisfied the rule.

PEL: The keeper has closed the lighthouse for the night.

MIKA: Why?

PEL: The storm is over and the boat is safe.

FEN: That is a complete ending.

MIKA: We also need to return the room to its original state.

[THEY FOLD THE CLOTH AND PUT THE CUSHION BACK.] 

NIB: That is another scene.

MIKA: It is the scene after the scene.

PEL: Can we say yes to going home?

MIKA: Yes, and I will erase the whiteboard after class.

[MIKA ERASES THE WHITEBOARD. ONLY YES, AND REMAINS.] 

FEN: The class is over.

MIKA: Yes, and we can improvise a way to put the chairs back.
