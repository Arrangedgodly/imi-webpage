# The Investor Pitch for a Better Nest

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: startup pitch competitions and inflated promises about a simple nest accessory*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: FOUNDER NARA, a macaque; INVESTOR VEX, a gibbon; ENGINEER PIP, a capuchin; JUDGE LUX, a lemur; and BUILDER, a tamarin repairing the nest.

[A demo table holds a woven wall pocket attached to a nest panel. Three twigs sit inside.] 

NARA: We are seeking a pilot investment for NestPocket, a place to keep spare twigs during repairs.

VEX: How do you monetize it?

NARA: We weave it once. Builders can use it as long as they need it.

VEX: That is not recurring revenue. Charge one twig each month to access the pocket. Send reminders at dawn so they cannot forget.

PIP: A twig is already what goes in the pocket.

VEX: Then charge a twig per twig stored.

LUX: The customer would pay to put their own materials into a pocket attached to their own nest?

VEX: They pay for convenience.

NARA: The pocket has no latch or lock.

VEX: We can add one. The first twig is free. After that, they subscribe.

[THE BUILDER APPROACHES AND TAKES A TWIG FROM THE POCKET.] 

VEX: That twig is part of the demo inventory.

BUILDER: I need it to tighten the roof. It came from this tree.

VEX: You have not paid the monthly access fee.

BUILDER: I am repairing the nest the pocket is attached to.

PIP: If the builder cannot reach the twig, the roof stays loose.

NARA: Take it. We can weave another pocket when the repair is done.

VEX: That is unlicensed access.

[THE BUILDER USES THE TWIG TO SECURE THE ROOF. THE PANEL STOPS SAGGING.] 

LUX: The product has improved the nest without a subscription.

VEX: We can bill the nest for structural improvement.

PIP: The nest is not a company.

VEX: We could register it as a business.

NARA: I will not charge the builders to use what they made.

VEX: Then I need an investor share. One percent of every twig that passes through.

[THE BUILDER REMOVES THE POCKET AND WEAVES IT INTO THE ROOF EDGE.] 

PIP: The pocket is no longer a product.

VEX: It is now part of the nest. I will take one percent of the nest.

LUX: You do not own the nest.

VEX: My deal was not signed.

NARA: It was not even written down.

[THE BUILDER HANDS VEX A TWIG.] 

BUILDER: Here. You can own this one.

VEX: It is a single twig.

BUILDER: You asked for a share.

[VEX HOLDS THE TWIG WHILE THE OTHERS FINISH THE ROOF.] 

LUX: The pilot has concluded. The investor is now holding inventory.

NARA: We will weave another pocket when we need it.

VEX: I will need a recurring storage fee.

VEX: I will still need a subscription agreement.

NARA: Use the paper you brought.

[PIP FOLDS THE AGREEMENT INTO A POCKET.]

PIP: You can put your twig in your own pocket.
