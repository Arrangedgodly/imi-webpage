# The Science Fair Has a Volcano

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school science fairs, explosive demonstrations, and presenters who call a small result groundbreaking*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Student Rell, a macaque; student Tavi, a capuchin; judge Omi, an orangutan; and teacher Nari, a gibbon.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

A cardboard volcano stands on a table. Rell wears safety goggles. Tavi holds a poster labeled VOLCANIC ERUPTION.

OMI: What is your hypothesis?

RELL: If we add vinegar, the volcano will foam.

TAVI: We already tested it.

NARI: This is the demonstration.

Rell pours vinegar into the model. Foam rises slowly and leaks down the cardboard. The judges lean forward.

RELL: The eruption is beginning.

TAVI: It began three minutes ago.

OMI: The data indicates gradual activity.

A rival student unveils a paper airplane launcher. It shoots one plane across the gym, then jams.

Rell's volcano leaks onto the poster. The word "ERUPTION" turns into "ERUPTI." Tavi draws an arrow to the puddle.

TAVI: The liquid is moving downhill.

OMI: A significant observation.

Rell says the volcano is a model, not a prediction. Omi asks what the project shows. Tavi explains that the reaction makes gas bubbles, and the foam rises because the gas needs space. Omi asks how they controlled the amount of vinegar.

Rell holds up a measuring spoon. Tavi holds up a cup. They used different measures in each trial.

NARI: That is not a controlled test.

RELL: We can repeat it with the same spoon.

Tavi begins measuring. The cup tips over. The volcano releases another slow foam stream, this time onto the measurement notes.

TAVI: The results are wet.

Omi asks whether the students can keep the foam away from the power strip. Nari moves the volcano to a tray. The judges gather around. Rell uses a spoon to clear the foam and reveal the cardboard mountain underneath.

OMI: The project has survived its own eruption.

TAVI: It is cardboard.

OMI: It is still standing.

The students explain that they will redo the trials with equal amounts and keep the model over a tray. They label the project "Gas and Foam" instead of "Volcano." Omi says the judges will score it after the other demonstrations.

RELL: We did not win best eruption.

OMI: You won best explanation.

TAVI: That is less dramatic.

NARI: It is more accurate.

The judges ask the students to clean the table. Rell wipes the poster while Tavi pours the remaining solution into a labeled container. A second volcano at the next table erupts much louder. Everyone turns toward it.

OMI: That demonstration has a larger reaction.

RELL: Their model is made of plastic.

NARI: They used more vinegar.

TAVI: We have a controlled comparison now.

Omi awards Rell and Tavi one ribbon for explaining the reaction. The other team receives a ribbon for a taller foam rise. The students exchange measuring tips, then pack the tray. A final bubble rises in the bottle.

RELL: The experiment is done.

TAVI: The bottle is still bubbling.

NARI: Then leave it in the tray until it stops.

[THE STUDENTS CARRY THE TRAY TO THE CLEANUP TABLE. THE PAPER AIRPLANE LAUNCHER WAITS NEXT DOOR.]
