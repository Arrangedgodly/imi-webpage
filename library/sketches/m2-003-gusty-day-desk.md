# Gusty Day Desk

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Television weather alerts and overdramatic storm coverage*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Anchor Maru, a baboon; field reporter Jett, a squirrel monkey; meteorologist Nola, a mandrill; cameraman Finch, a gibbon.

[STAGE: A WEATHER MAP SHOWS ONE TREE, A SUN, AND A LARGE RED ARROW.] 

MARU: We interrupt the afternoon with an urgent gust advisory.

NOLA: The wind is seven miles per hour.

MARU: That's miles per hour in the exposed branch zone.

NOLA: There is no exposed branch zone.

MARU: We made one on the map.

JETT: I'm live from the park, where leaves are moving in a concerning direction.

MARU: Which direction?

JETT: Sideways.

NOLA: That's what wind does.

JETT: We have a warning for loose hats.

FINCH: Your hat is still on.

JETT: It is holding on by confidence.

[A FAN TURNS ON. JETT'S HAT FLIES OFF.] 

JETT: We have lost the hat.

MARU: This is exactly what our map predicted.

NOLA: The map predicted a hat icon at the studio.

MARU: The hat moved to the park.

JETT: I am following it now.

FINCH: Stay behind the safety rope.

JETT: The safety rope is tied to a paper tree.

NOLA: The paper tree is not load-bearing.

MARU: Tell us about the storm surge.

JETT: I see a large wave approaching.

NOLA: That is a gardener watering the flower bed.

JETT: The water is reaching my shoes.

MARU: Viewers should move to higher ground.

NOLA: The grass is higher than the path.

JETT: I am on the grass.

FINCH: The hat is stuck to the hose.

JETT: The hose is pulling the hat toward the roses.

MARU: We have a developing situation.

NOLA: We have a sprinkler on a timer.

[SFX: SPRINKLER TICKS, THEN SPRAYS.] 

JETT: I am taking shelter behind a bench.

FINCH: The bench is dry.

JETT: Not anymore.

MARU: Can you retrieve the hat?

JETT: I can reach it with my tail.

NOLA: The hose will pull back when the timer stops.

JETT: I have the hat.

FINCH: You have a rose bush.

JETT: I have both.

MARU: This is a dramatic rescue.

NOLA: The gust has passed. The spray is off.

JETT: I have the hat and three roses.

FINCH: The roses belong to the park.

JETT: I will return them to the bush.

MARU: The warning is lifted. Residents may resume normal hat behavior.

NOLA: What is normal hat behavior?

MARU: Keeping it indoors when the studio fan is on.

JETT: I am back at the desk. My hat has dried. The park is fine.

MARU: Our map will remain on screen for one more hour.

NOLA: It shows a sun, one tree, and a hat in a rose bush.

MARU: Accuracy matters.

JETT: The hat has now blown free.

[THE MAP'S HAT ICON BLOWS OFF THE SCREEN. MUSIC STING.]
