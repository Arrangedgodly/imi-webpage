# The Moving Company Lost a Box

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: moving-day reality television and inventory control in a home where monkeys carry objects instinctively*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Crew lead Sela, a baboon; client Lumo, a gibbon; mover Nari, a macaque; and camera operator Pavi, a capuchin.

[A STACK OF BOXES IS LABELED "KITCHEN," "BEDROOM," AND "IMPORTANT."]

SELA: We need every item checked off before loading.

LUMO: My favorite branch is not in a box.

NARI: We can label it "fragile."

[THE BRANCH IS STILL ATTACHED TO THE TREE.]

SELA: We cannot move the tree.

LUMO: The new place has a tree.

PAVI: This is a relocation story.

[THE CREW LIFTS A BOX. SOMETHING INSIDE RATTLES.]

SELA: What is in this one?

NARI: It says "important."

LUMO: It is the bowl.

[THEY OPEN IT. THE BOWL IS EMPTY.]

LUMO: The acorns are missing.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON THE LABEL.]

SELA: We need to find them before the truck leaves.

[NARI CHECKS THE BOXES. A MONKEY SITS ON THE "BEDROOM" BOX.]

NARI: That one is occupied.

PAVI: The inventory says nothing about passengers.

[THE MONKEY LEAVES. THE BOX HAS A HOLE IN IT.]

SELA: Something escaped.

[THEY FOLLOW ACORN SHELLS TO A LOW BRANCH. THE MONKEY HAS MOVED THE ACORNS.]

LUMO: It is sorting them.

NARI: Into what categories?

LUMO: Ripe and not ripe.

SELA: The move is delayed.

[THE DRIVER HONKS.]

PAVI: We need a shot of the deadline.

[THE CREW CHECKS THE INVENTORY SHEET.]

SELA: Two boxes of kitchen tools, one lamp, one bowl.

NARI: The bowl is missing.

LUMO: It is inside the truck.

[PAVI LOOKS THROUGH THE VAN WINDOW. THE BOWL IS ON THE SEAT.]

PAVI: The bowl is not a box.

SELA: We need a separate inventory line.

NARI: The monkey is also not a box.

[THE MONKEY CLIMBS INTO A BOX MARKED "BEDROOM."]

SELA: It is now cargo.

LUMO: It is a passenger.

[THE MONKEY CLIMBS OUT AND CARRIES A SMALL PILLOW TO THE VAN.]

PAVI: The pillow is on the list.

NARI: The monkey is carrying the pillow.

SELA: We need a line for carried items.

[THEY ADD "PILLOW, IN TRANSIT." THE MONKEY TAKES THE PAPER.]

LUMO: The form is now in transit.

SELA: I will keep the master copy.

PAVI: The form has caught up with the move.

[THE DRIVER HONKS AGAIN. THEY CLOSE THE VAN DOORS.]

SELA: We can track the pillow by the form.

[THE MONKEY PUTS THE ACORNS BACK IN THE BOWL.]

LUMO: The inventory is complete.

SELA: We need to pack it.

[NARI WRAPS THE BOWL IN PAPER. THE MONKEY UNWRAPS IT.]

PAVI: The subject refuses packing.

LUMO: The bowl is coming with me.

[THEY CARRY IT BY HAND. THE TRUCK PULLS AWAY.]

SELA: We missed the truck.

NARI: We can take the bowl in the van.

[THE CAMERA FOLLOWS THE BOWL INTO THE VAN, THEN THE MONKEY CLIMBS IN BESIDE IT.]
