# Campfire Story Consultant

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: horror campfire stories and escalating requests for scarier endings*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: STORYTELLER MOSS, a gibbon; CAMPER BEE, a macaque; CAMPER RILL, a capuchin; and the wind.

[Three campers sit by a small fire. Moss holds a notebook titled TRUE TALES.] 

MOSS: I will tell you the story of the shadow that followed me across the ridge.

BEE: Was it a real shadow?

MOSS: It was attached to me.

RILL: That's how shadows work.

MOSS: I know. But it moved when I moved, stopped when I stopped, and lengthened when the sun went down.

BEE: That sounds ordinary.

MOSS: The shadow had a second shadow.

[THE WIND BLOWS. THE FLAME FLICKERS.] 

RILL: That is just the other side of you.

MOSS: The second shadow reached out with a long, dark arm.

BEE: You were holding a branch.

MOSS: I was. It became part of the story.

RILL: Was there anything behind you?

MOSS: A tree.

BEE: A tree with a branch?

MOSS: Yes.

RILL: So you were walking past a tree at sunset.

MOSS: The audience is supposed to imagine it.

BEE: I am imagining it. It is a tree.

MOSS: Fine. I have a new story. A whisper came from inside the camp stove.

RILL: Was the stove on?

MOSS: No.

BEE: Was someone inside it?

MOSS: No.

RILL: Then why was it whispering?

MOSS: The wind passed through a loose vent.

BEE: That is a maintenance issue.

MOSS: You are ruining the suspense.

[THE STOVE VENT WHISTLES.] 

RILL: There. The sound has returned.

BEE: We can tighten the vent tomorrow.

MOSS: I could tell a story about the vent tightening by itself.

RILL: You can. We will still tighten it tomorrow.

MOSS: You both want an explanation before a scare.

BEE: We want to know whether the stove is safe.

MOSS: The stove is cold. The vent is loose. The shadow was mine. I can still make those things sound strange.

RILL: Then tell the story as it happened.

MOSS: The vent sang in the dark. We heard it, checked it, and sat closer to the fire.

BEE: That is a good campfire story.

MOSS: It is not frightening.

BEE: If the stove is cold, we can move it away from the tent before we sleep.

MOSS: The vent whistles when it is still.

RILL: The stove is not running. We can carry it by the handles.

MOSS: I want to check the vent first.

[THEY LISTEN. THE VENT WHISTLES ONCE.] 

BEE: It is louder when the wind comes from the west.

RILL: The wind is coming from the west now.

MOSS: The second shadow has returned.

BEE: That is the stove casting a shadow on the tent.

MOSS: The moon moved behind a cloud.

RILL: The shadow is longer now.

MOSS: There is a mystery after all.

BEE: There is a loose vent and a moving light source.

MOSS: Can I tell it that way?

RILL: Yes, but first move the stove. We can fix the vent in daylight.

[THEY LIFT THE COLD STOVE AND SET IT ON A CLEAR PATCH OF GROUND.] 

MOSS: The shadow has disappeared.

BEE: The stove is no longer between us and the moon.

MOSS: It was a good shadow while it lasted.

RILL: You can put it in your story.

MOSS: The shadow followed us until we moved the stove.

BEE: That is true.

MOSS: And tomorrow the vent will be repaired.

RILL: That is the sequel.

MOSS: It will be shorter.

[THE FIRE CRACKLES. A BRANCH SNAPS IN THE WOODS.] 

RILL: That one was real.

MOSS: I did not write it down.

BEE: You can wait and see what happens next.

[MOSS CLOSES THE NOTEBOOK. THE WIND QUIETS.]
