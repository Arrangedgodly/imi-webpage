# The Community Pool's Deep End

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury resort pool rules that market ordinary amenities as exclusive experiences*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Lifeguard Nemi, a macaque; Resort Host Pavo, a gibbon; Guest Sela, a capuchin; and Maintenance Worker Oru, a baboon.

INT. RESORT POOL - DAY

Pavo stands beside a small pool with a microphone. A sign reads "INFINITY WATER EXPERIENCE." Sela looks over the edge.

PAVO: Welcome to the deep end, where the water meets the horizon.

SELA: The pool ends at the wall.

NEMI: It is three feet deep across the whole basin.

PAVO: The horizon is a state of mind.

ORU: The horizon is the fence.

[Oru tests the water with a pole. It taps the tile.]

SELA: Is there a deeper section?

NEMI: No. This is the wading pool.

PAVO: Our brochure calls it "a vertical immersion retreat."

SELA: I am standing in it.

PAVO: Exactly. Fully immersed in the experience.

INT. POOL DECK - LATER

Pavo unveils a gold rope across one corner of the pool. A small sign reads "PRIVATE COVE." Sela steps toward it.

NEMI: That section is reserved for guests with the blue wristband.

SELA: I have a blue wristband.

PAVO: That is the towel wristband. The cove wristband is slightly darker blue.

ORU: They are the same color.

PAVO: One has a tiny wave printed on it.

[Sela compares wristbands. Both have a tiny wave.] 

SELA: Which wave is the premium one?

PAVO: The wave on the right.

NEMI: The pool is all one water.

PAVO: The experience is sectioned.

[Oru moves the rope. It floats directly to the other side of the pool.]

ORU: The cove has migrated.

PAVO: We call that a floating upgrade.

INT. POOL DECK - AFTERNOON

Nemi blows a whistle. Sela is seated on the pool's edge, feet in the water. Pavo holds a tray of sparkling water.

NEMI: The pool is closing for cleaning.

PAVO: Our closing ritual includes a complimentary splash.

SELA: I have not gone swimming yet.

NEMI: The water is below your knees.

PAVO: Your stay includes a towel.

ORU: The pool is clean. We can open it again.

[Oru removes the rope. The tiny private cove vanishes. Pavo hands Sela a towel.]

SELA: Was that the infinity experience?

PAVO: The infinity experience has ended.

NEMI: The pool is open for anyone now.

PAVO: We should rename the private cove.

SELA: It is the left corner.

ORU: The right corner is the same depth.

PAVO: Then the upgrade could be a choice of corner.

NEMI: There are four corners.

SELA: That's a lot of tiers for three feet of water.

ORU: The water level is the same across the pool.

PAVO: We could sell a premium waterline.

NEMI: The waterline moves when someone steps in.

[Sela steps into the pool. The water barely covers her feet.]

SELA: I can see the horizon from here.
