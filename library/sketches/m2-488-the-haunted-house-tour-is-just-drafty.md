# The Haunted House Tour Is Just Drafty

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: haunted-house tours, spooky sound effects, and guides who mistake building maintenance for supernatural activity*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: TOUR GUIDE, a gibbon; SKEPTIC, a macaque; SOUND TECH, an orangutan; CARETAKER, a baboon; VISITOR, a capuchin.

[A historic house at night. A curtain lifts and falls near a cracked window. A low moan echoes from the hall.]

GUIDE: The ghost has returned.

SKEPTIC: The window is open.

SOUND TECH: The moan is coming from the speaker.

[The visitor clutches a brochure. The caretaker carries a small tool kit.]

VISITOR: Is the ghost part of the ticket?

GUIDE: It is included in the night tour.

CARETAKER: The window latch is broken. I can secure it after the group leaves.

[The guide leads the visitors into the next room, but the curtain keeps moving. The skeptic closes the door between rooms; the moan grows louder.]

SKEPTIC: The speaker is behind this door.

[The sound tech opens the booth and finds the spooky track looping at maximum volume. A control is stuck.]

ACT ONE

INT. HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

The caretaker checks the window from the safe interior side. The latch is loose but the frame is stable. The sound tech turns down the speaker and switches off the ghost track. The house becomes quiet except for the curtain flutter.

GUIDE: The haunting has stopped.

VISITOR: The curtain is still moving.

SKEPTIC: The window is still open.

[The guide asks the sound tech to restart the moan so the tour has a climax. The caretaker says the group should leave that room while the latch is repaired. The visitors follow the marked route.]

CARETAKER: Please keep the doorway clear.

GUIDE: Could the latch be the ghost's weakness?

SKEPTIC: It is the latch's weakness.

[The sound tech tapes a note over the control: DO NOT USE. The guide asks whether they can use a different effect. The tech offers a quiet wind track at low volume, but the skeptic says the real draft is already audible.]

ACT TWO

INT. FRONT PARLOR - LATER

The caretaker tightens the latch with a replacement screw. The guide rehearses a story about an invisible hand that opens the window. The skeptic tries the latch from inside; it holds. The curtain stays still. The visitor checks the brochure, which labels the room as the "whispering parlor."

VISITOR: Does the room still whisper?

SOUND TECH: Only if I turn on the sound file.

GUIDE: The visitors expect a spooky moment.

CARETAKER: We can tell the true story: a draft made the curtain move.

[The guide tries the story without an effect. The group listens to the quiet room. A branch taps the outside sill once. Everyone jumps; the caretaker opens the curtain slightly and shows a tree branch rubbing the wall.]

SKEPTIC: There is the sound.

GUIDE: The house has a tree.

[The caretaker clips the branch away from the wall. The visitor applauds the tiny repair. The guide thanks them and moves to the next room.]

ACT THREE

INT. ENTRY HALL - NIGHT

The tour concludes. The guide offers a refund to anyone who came only for the ghost track. The visitor says the window story was more interesting. The sound tech labels the old effect file "ARCHIVE - DO NOT LOOP." The caretaker checks the front door and the repaired window latch once.

GUIDE: The ghost has left the house.

SKEPTIC: The speaker is switched off.

CARETAKER: The window is latched.

[The guide asks the group to leave by the marked exit. The visitor turns off a small flashlight, and the room stays still. The tech takes the speaker remote away from the guide.]

VISITOR: Will the next tour have the real draft?

CARETAKER: Not if the latch holds.

[The group exits. The curtain remains still behind the glass.]

FINAL IMAGE: The silent speaker sits beside the labeled sound file as the window stays shut.
