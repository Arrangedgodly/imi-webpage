# The Very Important Envelope

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: office dramas that treat routine paperwork as a high-stakes secret*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Courier Odo, a baboon; director Nara, a macaque; assistant Piri, a gibbon.

INT. OFFICE - DAY

[Odo enters with a padded envelope held flat on both palms. A red stamp says DIRECTOR ONLY. Nara reaches for it. Odo backs away.]

ODO: Signature first.

NARA: I am the director.

PIRI: Do we have evidence of that?

[Nara points to the nameplate on her desk. Piri compares it with the envelope.]

PIRI: The envelope says THE DIRECTOR. Your plate says NARA.

NARA: My office door says DIRECTOR.

[Odo carries the envelope to the door. He presents his clipboard to its handle.]

ODO: I have an authorized location, but not an authorized paw.

[Nara steps between him and the door.]

NARA: Give me the pen.

[Piri blocks the clipboard with a letter opener.]

PIRI: Does the signature accept responsibility for the contents?

ODO: It confirms receipt of one envelope.

PIRI: What if it contains two envelopes?

ODO: My training film stopped before that chapter.

[Nara signs. Odo places the envelope on her desk with exaggerated care. The desk phone is absent from its charging base.]

NARA: Who sent it?

ODO: The sender requested confidentiality.

[Piri turns the envelope over. Its return address is THEIR OFFICE.]

PIRI: We are remarkably private about our own location.

[Nara uses her mobile to call the printed sender number. A ring comes from inside the envelope. Odo stiffens.]

ODO: Please do not activate the contents before inspection.

NARA: We have been inspecting the outside for five minutes.

[The ringing continues. Piri lowers the letter opener.]

PIRI: It may be trying to authorize itself.

[Nara opens the envelope. Inside are the missing cordless desk handset and a card: MAILROOM ROUTING TEST. CONFIRM RECEIPT OF ONE ENVELOPE.]

NARA: You delivered our telephone from the outbox to this desk.

ODO: Under a confidential instruction.

PIRI: I put it there while testing the new dispatch labels.

[She points to a label printer. It has printed DIRECTOR ONLY on a roll of kitchen tissue.]

NARA: The printer appears to have developed a specialty.

[Odo offers another form. Nara takes the handset off her mobile call, ending the ring.]

ODO: The confirmation must return to the sender.

NARA: The sender is standing here.

ODO: The instruction requires sealed return delivery.

[Piri writes RECEIVED on the card. Nara puts it back in the envelope without the telephone. Odo seals it and takes one step toward the outbox.]

PIRI: Wait. That is where the routing test began.

ODO: Then it is an established route.

[He puts the envelope in the outbox. The printer immediately spits out a new DIRECTOR ONLY label. Odo sticks it on the envelope and returns to Nara.]

ODO: One confidential delivery. Signature first.

[Nara picks up the handset and calls the courier desk. Odo's belt radio rings. He answers, still holding the envelope.]

NARA: I would like to cancel a delivery.

ODO: Certainly. Can you send the request in writing?

[Piri quietly puts the pen in the outbox. Nara stares at it.]

PIRI: We may have to wait for authorized return delivery.
