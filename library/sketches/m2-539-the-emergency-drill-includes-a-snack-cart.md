# The Emergency Drill Includes a Snack Cart

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate evacuation drills and training videos that cannot decide whether realism or catering comes first*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: SAFETY OFFICER, a baboon; OFFICE MANAGER, a gibbon; INTERN, a capuchin; TRAINER, an orangutan; SNACK VENDOR, a macaque.

[An office break room. A snack cart blocks part of the marked exit. The trainer holds a clipboard. A poster reads PRACTICE THE ROUTE.]

TRAINER: When the alarm sounds, leave your belongings and walk to the assembly point.

OFFICE MANAGER: Does that include the cart?

SNACK VENDOR: The cart belongs to me.

SAFETY OFFICER: It is blocking the exit.

INTERN: The crackers are in the way too.

ACT ONE

[The trainer sets off a practice chime. Everyone stands. The snack vendor begins pushing the cart toward the exit.]

SAFETY OFFICER: Leave the cart.

SNACK VENDOR: It is my livelihood.

TRAINER: In a real evacuation, move people first.

[The office manager leads everyone toward the marked exit. The cart's handle catches the door bar. The vendor releases it and moves to the side. The cart rolls downhill along the corridor.]

INTERN: The snacks are evacuating.

[The safety officer catches the cart before it reaches the stairs. The trainer stops the drill and asks where the backup route is. The manager points to a second exit, but it leads to a supply closet.]

OFFICE MANAGER: That route is on the map.

TRAINER: The map is upside down.

[The intern rotates it. The backup route is a hallway that ends at a locked storage door. The safety officer tries the handle; a stack of paper boxes presses against it from the other side.]

SAFETY OFFICER: The exit is clear on paper.

SNACK VENDOR: I can move the boxes.

TRAINER: After the drill.

[The manager assigns the vendor to move the cart into a marked parking spot. The intern fetches a doorstop so the route stays open. The safety officer removes the boxes from the emergency exit while the trainer updates the map. They rehearse the route again without the cart.]

ACT TWO

[The chime sounds a second time. Everyone follows the corrected route to the assembly point. The vendor leaves the cart behind and brings only the till pouch. The safety officer checks the headcount; everyone is present. The trainer marks the drill complete.]

INTERN: Can we practice returning?

TRAINER: Not until the all-clear.

[The office manager looks toward the snack cart.]

OFFICE MANAGER: I think the crackers passed the drill.

[At the assembly point the trainer asks each person to name one thing they learned. The manager says to use the second exit. The intern says to rotate the map. The vendor says to park the cart. The safety officer points out that the crackers are still on the cart and everyone turns toward the office.]

SAFETY OFFICER: The drill is over.

INTERN: My appetite did not get the message.

FINAL IMAGE: The group waits at the assembly point while the snack cart remains parked beside a sign reading NOT AN EXIT.
