# The Waiting Room Is Ready

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: medical waiting rooms, appointment updates, and office staff who announce delays with increasingly specific estimates*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Receptionist Neri, a ring-tailed lemur; patients Odo, an orangutan, and Vela, a gibbon; nurse Tali, a macaque; and the clinician, a capuchin.

INT. CLINIC WAITING ROOM - MORNING

Odo and Vela sit in the waiting room. Neri sits behind the desk.

NERI: The clinician will see you shortly.

ODO: How shortly?

NERI: Soon after the next patient.

Vela asks whether she can fill out a form. Neri gives her a clipboard. The form asks her to list her favorite waiting-room chair.

VELA: I have only sat in one.

NERI: Then you can compare it to the others.

Vela moves to another chair. It squeaks. Odo watches.

ODO: The first chair was quieter.

Neri announces that the clinician is running ten minutes behind. The clock reads 9:00. She updates the estimate to fifteen minutes. The clock still reads 9:00.

TALI: The clock stopped.

NERI: That explains the delay.

Tali checks the clock. It works when tapped. Vela puts the clipboard on the counter. The pen is tethered to the clipboard with a cord that is too short, so she cannot reach the form from the chair.

Vela pulls the clipboard. The pen rolls to Neri's side. Neri gives her another pen, which is also tethered.

ODO: The waiting room has secured its pens.

NERI: We had a pen shortage.

Tali returns with a tray and asks Odo to sit in the exam room. Odo stands, but his coat is caught under the chair. The chair comes with him, still attached to his coat.

ODO: I have brought the quiet chair.

Neri helps free him. Vela fills out the form while standing. Neri asks for her preferred chair, and Vela writes "none."

Tali says the clinician is ready. She calls Odo, but he has gone to retrieve his coat from the chair. Vela goes to the exam room instead, though Odo had the earlier appointment.

NERI: We need to update the order.

TALI: The clinician can see both of them.

Neri changes the waiting-room board from Odo to Vela, then back to Odo. The marker squeaks. The door opens.

CLINICIAN (offstage): I can see the next patient now.

Odo and Vela both stand.

NERI: You are both next.

ODO: That is not a number.

TALI: It is a room with two chairs.

They enter together. Neri updates the board to say "seen." The pen runs out of ink. She borrows the tethered one from Vela and writes "waiting room clear."

The chair squeaks once after everyone leaves.

Neri marks the chair as repaired on the maintenance list. The list has a separate field for the time it was fixed, and she writes "after the next patient."

The clinician returns the clipboard. It has a box for whether the room was busy. Neri checks every box.
