# The Most Efficient Picnic

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: productivity influencers who optimize leisure until it becomes work*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: organizer Tavi, a macaque; guest Pava, a gibbon; guest Odo, a baboon; recorder Miri, a capuchin.

EXT. PARK - DAY

Tavi unfolds a chart titled "PICNIC FLOW." Pava sets down a basket.

TAVI: We can reduce setup time by 30 percent.

ODO: We could sit down.

TAVI: First we assign tasks.

Pava is assigned blanket placement. Odo is assigned snack inventory. Miri records each step. Tavi opens a timer.

Pava spreads the blanket. It is upside down. Tavi says the corners should align with the sun. Pava rotates it. Odo opens the basket and counts apples. The chart says each apple should be placed at equal intervals.

ODO: They are round.

MIRI: The timer is still running.

Tavi tells everyone to stack cups by size. There are two cups, one large and one small. Odo places the large cup over the small one. Tavi calls it an efficiency gain. Pava asks if they can eat now. Tavi says the meal begins after the setup metrics are entered.

Miri records "blanket placed in 47 seconds." Tavi says they can improve. She asks Pava to fold and unfold it to rehearse. The blanket catches on a corner of the basket. Odo lifts the basket to free it. Two apples roll away.

TAVI: We need a recovery protocol.

Pava picks up the apples. One has a small bruise. Odo says it is still edible. Tavi adds a "bruise assessment" step to the chart. Miri stops the timer.

MIRI: We have been preparing for lunch longer than lunch takes.

Tavi reads the chart: "Set blanket. Open basket. Eat." She crosses out the rest. Everyone sits. Odo shares the bruised apple first. Pava pours water. Tavi starts to explain the productivity lesson, then takes a bite instead.

Tavi proposes timing how long it takes to eat one apple. Odo says he is hungry and begins. Miri records the time, then asks whether chewing speed is an efficiency measure. Pava says the point of a picnic is to enjoy it. Tavi checks the chart and sees that they have not included conversation. She adds "social exchange" as a task. Odo tells a short story about a squirrel stealing an apple. The group laughs, and the chart has to be revised because the laughter added time. Tavi asks whether they should schedule the next laugh. Pava suggests they leave the chart blank and let the afternoon happen. Tavi folds it and places it under the basket so the wind will not carry it away. The picnic now has one useful purpose for the chart.

Tavi leaves the timer in the basket.

The picnic continues.

TAG

Miri checks the timer. It has stopped.

TAVI: That is our best metric.
