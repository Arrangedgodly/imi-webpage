# The Award for Most Natural Pause

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: performance awards that honor unscripted moments by rehearsing them*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Host Piri, a gibbon; actor Odo, a baboon; director Nara, a macaque; stage manager Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. THEATER - NIGHT

[Piri stands beside a clock-shaped trophy. Odo waits at a taped mark. Nara holds a script; Tavi holds a stopwatch.]

PIRI: You are nominated for the season's most natural pause.

ODO: I forgot my line.

NARA: You looked thoughtful.

TAVI: For five and a half seconds.

ODO: Then I looked at you.

PIRI: A powerful search for connection.

[Tavi hands Odo a card: RECREATE THE ORIGINAL MOMENT. Odo reads it and takes his position.]

NARA: Your line is, "The train leaves at dawn."

ODO: The train leaves at dawn.

PIRI: You forgot the pause.

ODO: I remembered the line.

TAVI: Try to remember forgetting it.

[Odo starts again. Tavi raises the stopwatch. Odo waits, watching Tavi's thumb.]

ODO: Has it been five and a half?

PIRI: You cannot ask during the pause. That makes it a conversation.

[Tavi lowers the watch. Nara puts a rehearsal chair on the taped mark.]

NARA: Perhaps sit. The original pause happened while seated.

[Odo sits. A chair leg squeaks. Tavi starts the watch at the squeak. Piri signals her to stop.]

PIRI: That was the chair's pause.

ODO: Does the chair get nominated?

NARA: It does not have an agent.

[Piri asks Tavi to play the original recording. Odo's recorded voice begins the sentence, then stops. An audience cough fills the gap.]

RECORDING OF ODO: I have lost the sentence.

[Recorded laughter. Piri nods reverently.]

PIRI: That is what we need. The authentic cough.

TAVI: I can provide a cough.

[She coughs on cue. Odo flinches and immediately says the entire line.]

ODO: The train leaves at dawn.

PIRI: Too immediate.

NARA: Can we remove the stopwatch?

[Tavi pockets it, then looks at her wrist. Odo starts the scene again. Piri raises her hand halfway through his first word.]

PIRI: Wait. The trophy inscription needs the length of the winning pause.

[Tavi retrieves her watch. Nara brings the inscription card. Neither writes. Odo remains seated, waiting for permission to finish.]

ODO: Is this still my turn?

PIRI: Please remain in the moment.

[Odo watches her compare the card with the trophy. Someone in the audience coughs. He looks toward them, opens his mouth, then closes it.]

ODO: I have lost the permission.

[The audience laughs. Piri suddenly puts the card down.]

PIRI: That is the pause! Entirely unforced.

ODO: You forced me to wait for your card.

NARA: Give him the trophy before he remembers the line.

[Piri hands it over. Odo studies its stationary hands.]

ODO: How long did it last?

TAVI: I forgot to start the watch.

[Piri takes a breath, unable to answer. Tavi raises the watch toward her.]

ODO: Careful. She could win next year.

[Odo exits with the trophy while Piri is still holding her breath. Nara gently lowers Tavi's watch.]
