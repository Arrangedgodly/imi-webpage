# The Dockmaster Demands a Receipt from the Pirate Captain

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: pirate adventures that turn a harbor landing into an argument over marina paperwork*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: CAPTAIN VELA, a macaque; FIRST MATE PIP, a capuchin; DOCKMASTER NARA, a lemur; CLERK RIK, an orangutan.

[A pier at dawn. A small dinghy is tied at the visitors cleat; it has brought Vela and Pip from their larger ship offshore. Vela strides up its short gangplank with a rolled chart; Pip follows with a coil of rope. Nara stands beside the dock office.]

VELA: We have crossed the storm to claim this harbor.

NARA: Did you reserve a berth?

PIP: We claimed the tide.

RIK: The tide has no berth assignment.

ACT ONE

[Vela unrolls her chart over the dockmaster's clipboard. It shows a skull, an X and an entire coastline, but no harbor name.]

VELA: This inlet is ours by right of discovery.

NARA: It is the town marina.

VELA: Then we discover it again.

[Nara points to the sign: GUEST BOATS - CHECK IN AT DESK. Vela draws her sword. Pip offers her a pen instead.]

PIP: The desk is not guarded.

RIK: It does have a form.

[Vela sheathes her sword before approaching the dock office with theatrical caution. Rik slides a visitor form through the window. It asks for vessel name, arrival time, departure time and emergency contact.]

VELA: Put down "The Unpaid Tide."

PIP: That is the vessel. The tide is paid.

RIK: The fee is for the dock, not the sea.

[Vela signs with a looping flourish. Ink runs off the line and onto the chart. Nara gives Pip a paper towel. He mops the spot and uncovers a compass rose beneath the ink.]

PIP: The map has a north.

VELA: A useful secret.

NARA: It's printed on every map.

ACT TWO

[Rik collects the three-coin dock fee, gives Vela a receipt, stamps the form and hands her a numbered berth token. She holds it up as though it were a royal seal. Nara points to berth four. Vela looks down the pier and selects berth six, which is occupied by a rowboat.]

NARA: Four is empty.

VELA: Six has better light.

PIP: Six has a boat.

[Vela turns the token around. It reads BERTH FOUR. Pip carries the rope to the open cleat. Vela orders the crew to secure their ship, then realizes their vessel is still offshore. The gangplank leads to a dinghy tied to the pier.]

RIK: Your arrival form says one small craft.

VELA: A landing party.

NARA: Your dinghy is tied at the visitors cleat. Berth four is open.

[Pip unties the dinghy from the visitors cleat and walks it along the short pier to berth four. He secures the new line while Vela waits on the dock. She hands Nara the token, and Nara clips it to the dinghy handle.]

VELA: We claim this berth until sunset.

NARA: Your permit says noon.

[Vela checks. She wrote the time as 12:00, not 18:00. Pip begins to loosen the line, then stops when Nara offers a renewal form. Vela fills it out carefully, using the line this time. Pip reads the emergency contact aloud: "First mate, reachable by gull." Rik asks for a phone number. Pip enters the harbor office's number. Vela pays the additional fee. Nara takes back the noon token and exchanges it for one valid through sunset. Pip clips the replacement token to the dinghy and leaves it secured at berth four.]

VELA: The crew will return at noon.

PIP: The crew is here.

VELA: Then our victory lasts until sunset.

NARA: Your paid berth does.

TAG

[Nara checks the renewed token on its clip. Vela straightens it upright like a tiny flag. Pip rolls the inked chart and tucks the stamped permit inside.]

FINAL IMAGE: The tiny pirate dinghy sits in berth four beneath its official numbered token, while the captain salutes the harbor office's open window.
