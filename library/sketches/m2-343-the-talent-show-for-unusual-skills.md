# The Talent Show for Unusual Skills

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised talent competitions that demand every act have a dramatic backstory*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Host Miri, a baboon; judge Nara, a macaque; contestant Odo, a gibbon; contestant Piri, a capuchin; stage manager Tavi, a squirrel monkey.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

[A banner reads: EVERY SKILL HAS A STORY. Odo stands behind a table of mixed stones. Piri holds a tray of leaves. Miri faces the camera; Nara grips a scorecard. Tavi waits beside a cue light and a box of sound effects.]

MIRI: Odo, what is your talent?

ODO: I sort stones by size.

MIRI: And what inspired this journey?

ODO: They were mixed together.

[Tavi cues a swelling string chord. Odo looks at the speaker.]

NARA: Can we get a more emotional answer?

ODO: The small stones kept rolling under the table.

[Miri nods solemnly. Tavi cues thunder. A stone rolls off the table and lands in the sound-effects box. A crash booms through the hall.]

MIRI: A turning point.

ODO: A stone fell.

[Tavi retrieves it from the box. Odo begins sorting: small, medium, large. Miri steps in front of the table to ask about his childhood. Odo waits with a stone in each hand.]

MIRI: Did you always dream of this moment?

ODO: I was sorting before you stood there.

[Nara peers over the scorecard.]

NARA: The audience needs an obstacle.

[The fan turns on. Piri's leaf tray slides toward the edge. Odo catches it with one elbow while keeping the stones sorted. Piri lifts the tray back.]

PIRI: That was my obstacle.

MIRI: Excellent. Tell us how you overcame it.

PIRI: I picked up the tray.

[Tavi cues a triumphant horn. The fan blows two leaves into Odo's small-stone pile. Odo plucks them out and puts them back on Piri's tray.]

ODO: The categories are drifting.

MIRI: Now we have a crossover episode.

[Nara asks Piri to demonstrate her skill. Piri sorts leaves by color, then aligns their stems. Tavi cues a ticking clock. Piri keeps sorting at the same pace. Miri leans in.]

MIRI: Is there a deadline?

PIRI: The leaves are not going anywhere.

[Nara turns over the scorecard. Its categories read DRAMA, SURPRISE, and REVEAL.]

NARA: How do I score the actual sorting?

MIRI: Give it a reveal.

[Tavi drops a curtain between the tables. It catches on the fan and wraps around Miri's microphone stand. Nara switches off the fan. Odo and Piri each free one corner; the curtain falls over the neatly sorted work.]

MIRI: We have lost the results.

PIRI: The leaves are still in color order.

ODO: The stones are still in size order.

[Nara lifts the cloth. Both tables are intact. She crosses out DRAMA, SURPRISE, and REVEAL and writes USEFUL.]

NARA: The winners are the skills that kept working during the show.

MIRI: Can we repeat that for a stronger close?

ODO: No. We have finished sorting.

[Piri places the leaves in a jar of water. Odo returns the stones to a tray marked SMALL, MEDIUM, LARGE. Tavi switches off the thunder cue.]

TAG

Miri takes one stone from each pile and mixes them together.

ODO: You have created a new season.

[Tavi reaches for the sound board. Nara unplugs it.]
