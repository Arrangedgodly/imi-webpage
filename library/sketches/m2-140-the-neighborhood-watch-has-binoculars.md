# The Neighborhood Watch Has Binoculars

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: community safety meetings that treat ordinary animal movement as a coordinated security threat*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Watch captain Mina, a macaque; neighbor Odo, a baboon; lookout Liri, a gibbon; and passerby Pavi, a capuchin.

[A COMMUNITY NOTICE BOARD READS "NEIGHBORHOOD WATCH, 7 PM." ODO LOOKS THROUGH BINOCULARS.]

ODO: Movement in the west tree.

MINA: What kind?

ODO: A monkey.

LIRI: I am a monkey.

ODO: Not you. A different monkey.

[MINA TAKES THE BINOCULARS. THE MONKEY IS GROOMING.]

MINA: It appears calm.

ODO: It may be gathering information.

PAVI: I am looking for my water bowl.

[ODO POINTS AT THE TREE.]

ODO: The suspect is carrying a bowl.

PAVI: That is my bowl.

MINA: Do you recognize the carrier?

PAVI: It is my cousin.

[THE COUSIN CARRIES THE BOWL TO A LOW BRANCH AND SETS IT DOWN.]

LIRI: The bowl has been placed in public view.

ODO: We should record the location.

MINA: The location is Pavi's porch.

[THE COUSIN DRINKS FROM THE BOWL.]

PAVI: I left it there.

ODO: Why did you not report it?

PAVI: I knew where it was.

[MINA WRITES "NO INCIDENT" ON THE FORM.]

LIRI: We should check the east path.

[THE GROUP WALKS AROUND THE BLOCK. A LEAF FALLS.]

ODO: Something dropped from the canopy.

MINA: It is a leaf.

ODO: It could be a signal.

PAVI: The tree is losing leaves.

[THE WATCH MEMBERS LOOK UP. THREE LEAVES FALL IN A ROW.]

LIRI: It is a coordinated event.

MINA: It is autumn.

[ODO ADDS "LEAF ACTIVITY" TO THE REPORT.]

PAVI: You have written down the weather.

ODO: The meeting form has a weather section.

[THE COUSIN RETURNS THE BOWL.]

MINA: The bowl is recovered.

PAVI: It was never missing.

[MINA CROSSES OUT "RECOVERED." THE FORM TEARS.]

LIRI: We need a new incident report.

[ODO HANDS HER THE BINOCULARS.]

LIRI: I can see the new report from here.


[THE WATCH MEMBERS TRY TO WRITE A SAFETY NOTICE.]

MINA: "Please leave personal bowls on your own porch."

PAVI: My porch is next to the tree.

ODO: We can add a map.

[ODO DRAWS THE TREE, PORCH, AND WATER BOWL. THE MAP IS UPSIDE DOWN.]

LIRI: The north arrow points to the ground.

ODO: That is where the roots are.

MINA: The notice should be simple.

[PAVI PUTS THE EMPTY BOWL ON THE PORCH. A LEAF FALLS INTO IT.]

ODO: Another item has appeared.

PAVI: It is a leaf.
[LIRI TAKES THE BINOCULARS AND LOOKS THROUGH THEM.]

LIRI: I see no danger, only a branch.

ODO: The branch is still under observation.


MINA: Let us close the report.


[THE WATCH MEMBERS PUT AWAY THE BINOCULARS.]


LIRI: Nothing was lost.


[THE GROUP SITS ON THE PORCH. A LEAF LANDS IN THE EMPTY BOWL.]

ODO: I will keep watch.

PAVI: Please do not report the leaf.
