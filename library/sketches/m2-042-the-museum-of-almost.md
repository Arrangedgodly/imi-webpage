# The Museum of Almost

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Art-house museum audio tours and curator jargon*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: ADA, a macaque docent; LEN, a visitor; DEX, the director; and THE LABEL, a recorded voice.

[A white room. An empty plinth sits behind a rope. A handwritten card reads PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH THE SPACE.]

ADA: Welcome to the Museum of Almost. Please stay behind the rope. The exhibit is a stone that looked like fruit yesterday.

LEN: Where is it now?

ADA: It has moved into a more active relationship with the floor.

DEX: The stone is in the cleaning closet.

ADA: We don't say closet. We say the transitional gallery.

THE LABEL: Untitled. Possibly stone. Possibly fruit. The artist may be known to us.

LEN: Who made it?

DEX: The question assumes one maker.

LEN: It is a rock.

DEX: Your certainty is part of the experience.

[Len steps to the left. The plinth is empty from there too. ADA quietly shifts to block the view.]

ADA: This angle invites contemplation.

LEN: It invites me to see the broom closet.

[The LABEL plays again, louder.]

THE LABEL: Please consider what an empty plinth asks of you.

LEN: It asks where the exhibit went.

DEX: That is an excellent response. Would you like to record it for the donor wall?

LEN: I would like to see the stone.

[Dex opens a small viewing window in the closet door. A stone sits atop a mop bucket. ADA moves the rope to cover the window.]

ADA: The piece is not ready for public access.

LEN: It looks ready to fall.

[The stone rolls off the bucket. ADA darts through the door, catches it, and returns holding it with both hands.]

DEX: You have touched the artwork.

ADA: I saved it from the floor.

DEX: You have become part of the installation.

LEN: Does that mean I can touch the rope?

THE LABEL: Rope, attributed to the visitor, date unknown.

[Len reaches for the actual stone. ADA hands it over, then lets go too late. It rolls across the gallery and stops against the exit door.]

DEX: We need to preserve the work's journey.

LEN: The journey ends at the gift shop.

[The LABEL clicks on.]

THE LABEL: The gift shop is temporarily unavailable. It has entered a more active relationship with lunch.

[The exit door will not open against the stone. ADA moves the rope aside. Everyone lifts the plinth, but DEX insists they carry it through the transitional gallery.]

DEX: This is a major change in the work's relationship to the public.

LEN: It's a major change in our route to the parking lot.

ADA: The stone rolls faster if we leave the plinth behind.

[They lift the stone. THE LABEL speaks from the gallery speaker.]

THE LABEL: The absence of a plinth may be interpreted as a new exhibit.

DEX: Don't interpret it. Help us with the door.

[ADA opens the door. LEN carries the stone outside and places it on the ground.]

THE LABEL: Untitled. Outdoors. Artist unknown.

LEN: That one can stay outside.

[BLACKOUT.]
