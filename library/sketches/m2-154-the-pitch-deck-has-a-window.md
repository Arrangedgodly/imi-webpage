# The Pitch Deck Has a Window

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: startup demo days and investor language applied to an ordinary canopy window*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Founder Vex, a capuchin; investor Pavi, a baboon; builder Liri, a gibbon; and advisor Mino, a macaque.

[ON A SMALL STAGE, VEX STANDS BESIDE A SLIDE: "WINDOWS AS A SERVICE."]

VEX: We are disrupting the window.

PAVI: What does it do?

VEX: It opens.

LIRI: It also lets air in.

VEX: That is our premium tier.

[THE INVESTORS LOOK AT THE SLIDE.]

PAVI: What is your revenue model?

VEX: Monthly access to daylight.

MINO: The window is already in the wall.

VEX: Our customer is the wall.

[LIRI OPENS THE WINDOW. A BREEZE BLOWS THROUGH.]

PAVI: Is that included?

VEX: The breeze is a limited-time free trial.

LIRI: I need to shut it. The rain is coming.

VEX: Closing requires the paid latch.

[LIRI PULLS THE WINDOW SHUT. IT HAS A SIMPLE HOOK.]

VEX: You have bypassed the subscription.

PAVI: Can the company prevent that?

VEX: We can put the latch behind a login.

MINO: The login needs internet.

[THE ROOM HAS NO INTERNET.]

VEX: Then the window will remain open until connectivity returns.

LIRI: The room is getting wet.

[PAVI LEANS OUT AND PULLS THE HOOK.]

PAVI: You have a working latch.

VEX: That was the prototype.

PAVI: Who owns the window?

LIRI: The village does.

VEX: Our terms assign all future daylight to the company.

MINO: Does that include moonlight?

VEX: We have not priced moonlight.

[THE INVESTOR SLIDE ADVANCES TO "SCALABLE HORIZONS."]

LIRI: I built it to open for air and close for rain.

PAVI: That is enough.

VEX: We could offer a lifetime plan.

PAVI: The window outlasts the plan.

[LIRI SHOWS THE INVESTORS THE WOODEN FRAME.]

LIRI: The frame is made from fallen branches.

PAVI: It is sturdy.

VEX: Could we license the frame?

LIRI: The wood was already on the ground.

MINO: The wall has asked for a free trial.

[THE WINDOW OPENS AGAIN. A BIRD LANDS ON THE SILL.]

PAVI: Is the bird a user?

VEX: We can count it as a monthly active user.

[THE BIRD FLIES OFF. VEX ADDS A LINE TO THE SLIDE.]

[A RESIDENT ENTERS, CARRYING A WET COAT.]

RESIDENT: Can I close the window?

VEX: You will need a subscription.

LIRI: The hook is right there.

[THE RESIDENT CLOSES THE WINDOW AND HANGS THE COAT.]

PAVI: The customer has used the product without the plan.

VEX: We can offer a free trial.

[THE RESIDENT LEAVES. THE WINDOW OPENS AGAIN IN THE BREEZE.]

MINO: The trial has renewed itself without payment.

PAVI: The wall does not need a subscription.

[THE INVESTORS LEAVE. VEX STAYS BY THE WINDOW, TRYING TO COLLECT A MONTHLY FEE FROM THE BREEZE.]

MINO: The breeze has left.

VEX: I will invoice the wall.

[THE WALL DOES NOT RESPOND.]
