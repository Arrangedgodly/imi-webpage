# The Toaster Demonstration Needs Bread

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: product launches where the demo equipment is missing the thing the product is meant to handle*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Tavi, a macaque presenter; Sela, a capuchin engineer; Rono, a gibbon investor; Yori, a lemur assistant.

INT. PRODUCT SHOWROOM - DAY

[A NEW TOASTER SITS ON A PEDESTAL. A BANNER ON A LIGHT STAND READS TOASTING, REIMAGINED. THE STAND WOBBLES WHEN THE COUNTER IS BUMPED. THERE IS NO BREAD.]

TAVI: Today we unveil the first toaster that learns your preferred crispness.

RONO: Will it toast bread?

SELA: Yes. We have a demonstration.

Yori looks around the table. There is a plate, a butter knife, and a jar of jam. No bread.

YORI: We forgot the bread.

TAVI: We can demonstrate the interface first.

Sela presses the toast button. The display asks her to select a bread type. The choices are white, whole grain, or artisan.

RONO: What if someone has no bread?

SELA: Then the toaster waits.

Tavi taps artisan. The toaster asks how crisp, from one to ten. Sela selects eight. The machine starts, then stops.

SELA: It detected no bread.

TAVI: A safety feature.

RONO: How does it know?

SELA: The sensors are under the slots.

Yori lays a paper napkin across the top. The toaster beeps and displays "BREAD TOO FLAT."

TAVI: It is already adapting.

Rono asks if it can toast a bagel. Sela has one in her lunchbox, but it is still frozen. She puts it in. The toaster lowers the halves and immediately raises them.

SELA: It needs to defrost first.

TAVI: The toaster understands its limits.

Rono looks at the jam jar. Tavi holds it up as though it is the product.

TAVI: The complete breakfast experience.

The toaster's timer counts down. Nothing is inside. It pops up with a cheerful ding. The display says "READY."

RONO: Ready for what?

SELA: The next item.

Yori brings a slice of bread from the staff table. Sela inserts it. The toaster asks whether it is a sample, a meal, or a calibration object.

TAVI: Choose sample.

Sela chooses sample. The toaster lowers the bread, counts down, and pops it up. The toast is pale on one side and dark on the other.

RONO: I prefer the dark side.

SELA: We can adjust the settings.

She turns the bread around and runs it again. The toaster recognizes it as a second item and charges another cycle. The machine prints a small receipt.

YORI: It charged us twice.

TAVI: It learned that you like toast.

Rono tastes one corner. He says the flavor is good, but asks if the toaster can produce another slice for the room. Yori gives everyone a slice of bread. While she loads the slices, her elbow catches the light stand. The banner tips forward; Sela catches its pole and props it against the toaster, clear of the slots. The machine processes each slice and prints a separate receipt.

[THE PRESENTERS PICK UP THE RECEIPTS. SELA LETS GO OF THE POLE. THE BANNER SLIDES DOWN ACROSS THE TOASTER, WHICH BEEPS AND DISPLAYS "ITEM TOO LARGE." ITS EDGE EMERGES PERFECTLY TOASTED.]
