# The Birding Club Spots a Decoy

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive birdwatchers who prize rare sightings, official counts, and field-guide certainty*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Loro, a gibbon club leader; Mina, a macaque birder; Tess, a capuchin recorder; Pavo, a lemur park keeper.

EXT. PARK POND - EARLY MORNING

[THE BIRDING CLUB STANDS BEHIND A LOW HEDGE. MINA LOOKS THROUGH BINOCULARS. TESS HOLDS A CHECKLIST.]

LORO: Today we record the rare green crested marsh bird.

MINA: I see one on the post.

TESS: Marked.

PAVO: That's the park's decoy.

Loro lowers his binoculars.

LORO: The sign says birds may not be fed.

PAVO: The decoy keeps ducks from nesting in the fountain.

Mina follows the bird with her binoculars. It does not move. The wind turns it slowly on its post.

MINA: It has excellent patience.

TESS: How do I list it?

LORO: We need evidence of a live bird.

Pavo shows them a small wooden bird with a metal base. Mina asks if they can examine the legs. The base is painted green.

TESS: It has two legs.

LORO: They are fixed.

Pavo explains that the decoy rotates to show visitors how the real bird looks from every angle. Loro says that is useful, but the club needs a field sighting, not an exhibit.

Mina notices a live duck behind the decoy. She tracks it, but Tess cannot identify it because the duck keeps swimming behind the wooden bird.

TESS: I have one body and two heads.

PAVO: The duck is real. The other is a decoy.

Loro asks everyone to stay quiet. A squirrel runs across the path carrying a sunflower seed. The decoy's metal base squeaks as it turns.

MINA: The bird called.

PAVO: The post squeaked.

Tess writes "post, possibly vocal."

Loro says no one should enter the restricted marsh to get a better angle. Mina takes a photo through a gap in the hedge. The image shows the decoy's painted eye and the live duck's tail.

TESS: The photo has the bird and the real bird.

LORO: Which one is the rare one?

Mina points to the duck. It quacks and flaps toward the pond. The wooden decoy turns to face the club.

TESS: That's a strong look.

Pavo takes the decoy down for cleaning. Beneath it, a live green bird hops onto the post. Everyone freezes. Loro signals Tess to record it. Tess's pencil breaks.

TESS: I saw it.

LORO: We need an exact count.

The bird flies off.

Mina asks Pavo to leave the post empty for the count. Pavo says visitors expect the wooden decoy there. He sets the decoy back, and its base squeaks again. Loro raises one finger for the recorder. Tess writes "one bird, one squeak." Mina asks if the live bird counts as a second sighting if it appeared during the same minute.

LORO: Only if it is a different bird.

PAVO: It was the same bird.

TESS: Then the decoy may be more reliable.

[THE CLUB WRITES ONE SIGHTING ON THE CHECKLIST. PAVO PUTS THE DECOY BACK. THE WIND TURNS IT SO IT FACES AWAY, AND LORO MARKS IT AS A SECOND OBSERVATION.]
