# The Menu Interpreter

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: restaurant menus translated into elaborate descriptions of ordinary food*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Server Pava, a gibbon; diner Odo, a baboon; chef Nara, a macaque.

INT. CAFE - DAY

[Pava gives Odo a menu with gold lettering. Nara sets a pot of soup on the kitchen hatch.]

PAVA: Today's special is a broth-forward root experience.

ODO: Soup?

PAVA: With a warm grain accompaniment.

ODO: Bread?

PAVA: You seem familiar with our concept.

[Odo points to the price. Pava turns the menu toward the light.]

PAVA: That is the introductory experience. We recommend the expanded journey.

NARA: A bigger bowl.

ODO: I want the introductory one. I have twenty minutes.

[Pava writes SAVORY IMMERSION on her pad. Nara reads it upside down from the hatch.]

NARA: Soup, bread, table four.

PAVA: Please don't say the kitchen names in the dining area.

[Nara serves a bowl. Odo tastes it.]

ODO: This is good. What's in it?

NARA: Carrots, lentils and salt.

PAVA: Roots coaxed from soil, legumes given time, and a mineral finish.

ODO: Did you coax the carrots?

NARA: I dug them up. One resisted.

[Odo dips his bread into the soup. Pava raises a spoon with a small flourish.]

PAVA: We offer this precision immersion instrument.

ODO: My accompaniment is already immersed.

PAVA: Would you prefer a deconstructed presentation?

ODO: I prefer lunch.

[Pava has already placed a tiny empty plate beside each ingredient name on the menu. She removes Odo's bowl and holds it toward Nara.]

PAVA: One deconstruction.

NARA: He has started eating.

PAVA: Then one retrospective deconstruction.

[Nara sets the bowl back before Pava can ladle anything onto the tiny plates. Odo pulls his bread to his side of the table.]

ODO: If you separate this lunch, I will have to order dinner to assemble it.

[Pava turns her pad over. On its back is the kitchen list: SOUP, BREAD, TEA. Odo taps it.]

ODO: This menu has translated the first menu.

PAVA: Those are only working titles.

NARA: They have worked since we opened.

ODO: Tea, please. Under its working title.

[Pava brings a cup. She takes a breath.]

PAVA: A leaf infusion in a heat-retaining vessel.

ODO: And a napkin?

PAVA: A linen moment.

[Nara opens the drawer and drops a folded napkin onto Odo's table.]

NARA: We have eight moments left. Laundry comes tomorrow.

[Odo wipes a drop from his chin. Pava offers the bill, embossed with three elaborate descriptions.]

ODO: How much is soup, bread and tea?

PAVA: Nine nuts.

[Odo puts nine nuts on the table. Pava circles a blank box marked GRATUITY EXPERIENCE.]

PAVA: Would you like to acknowledge your culinary guide?

[Odo puts a tenth nut beside it.]

ODO: A spherical woodland thank-you.

[Pava writes the phrase down. Nara looks over her shoulder.]

NARA: Don't add that to the menu.

PAVA: I can't. The squirrel next door has trademarked it.

[Odo takes the kitchen list as he leaves. Pava catches him at the door.]

PAVA: That is our emergency translation guide.

ODO: Then put it where hungry people can reach it.

[He clips it over the gold menu. Nara hands Pava the tip nut. Pava studies it with a magnifying glass.]

PAVA: The woodland thank-you appears sincere.
