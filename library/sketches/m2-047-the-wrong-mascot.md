# The Wrong Mascot

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports mascot auditions and halftime spectacle*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: COACH BRISTLE, a macaque team director; MIMI, a capuchin mascot hopeful; JO, a tortoise stagehand; and the TEAM, heard from the gym.

[A gym. A foam eagle head sits on a chair. A banner reads SHOW US YOUR SPIRIT.]

COACH: The team needs a mascot who can lead a chant, dance at halftime, and never block the scoreboard.

MIMI: I can climb the scoreboard.

COACH: You cannot climb the scoreboard.

MIMI: Then I can point at it from here.

[She points. Her foam head faces backward.]

JO: The costume is turned around.

MIMI: I can see the audience this way.

COACH: The audience should see the eagle.

[Mimi turns the head. Her tail sticks through the back.]

COACH: That is not on the uniform diagram.

MIMI: The diagram has a blank space where my tail goes.

[The team practices a chant. Their voices are drowned out by the squeaky eagle feet.]

COACH: The mascot must pump up the crowd.

MIMI: I can clap.

[She claps. The foam hands stick together and won't separate.]

JO: The sponsor glued them for the photo.

COACH: We need a big gesture.

MIMI: We need the players to hear the whistle.

[The coach blows a whistle. Nobody hears it. The mascot's oversized head blocks the team's view of the clock.]

COACH: The eagle has to be more visible.

MIMI: The team can't see the clock.

[She removes the head. The team immediately notices the whistle and lines up.]

COACH: The crowd needs a show.

MIMI: The players need the court.

[At halftime, Mimi enters without the head. She leads one short call-and-response, then moves to the sideline before play resumes. The crowd chants back; the players can still hear the referee.]

COACH: That was shorter than the sponsor package.

JO: It also left time for the game.

[Mimi picks up the foam head and sets it in the front row.]

MIMI: Let the eagle watch.

[The head tips forward and blocks the scoreboard again.]

COACH: Move it.

JO: The eagle has been benched.

[The team wins by one point. Coach raises the foam head to celebrate. It squeaks loudly.]

MIMI: The mascot has a victory sound.

COACH: And a seat with a clear view.

[The foam beak points at the scoreboard.]

COACH: We have to get a second mascot for the final.

JO: The eagle head is already on the floor.

COACH: The sponsor wants a pair.

MIMI: Then the pair can be the mascot and its shadow.

[She moves the head under the scoreboard light. Its shadow stretches across the court.]

TEAM, off: Go shadow! Go shadow!

COACH: The crowd has chosen.

JO: The shadow won't block anyone's view.

COACH: Does it have a name?

MIMI: It depends where you stand.

[The players shift. The shadow changes shape and seems to wave.]

TEAM, off: Shadow! Shadow!

COACH: We'll give it a jersey.

MIMI: The jersey can stay on the chair.

[The shadow covers the scoreboard again. Mimi moves the head two inches; the score is visible.]

COACH: That's our final pose.

[BLACKOUT.]
