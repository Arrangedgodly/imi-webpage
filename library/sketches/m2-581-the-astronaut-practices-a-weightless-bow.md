# The Astronaut Practices a Weightless Bow

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: space mission briefings that turn every ordinary gesture into a ceremonial maneuver*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: COMMANDER ROKO, an orangutan; PILOT SELA, a lemur; TRAINER PIP, a macaque; MISSION TECH VELA, a capuchin.

[A cabin mockup on a studio floor. A strap holds a pen to the instrument panel. Roko stands at a taped mark.]

PIP: Your first public bow should be graceful.

ROKO: In orbit, I will be floating.

SELA: Then bow toward the audience.

ACT ONE

[Pip shows the crew a formal bow. His clipboard floats nowhere; it drops to the floor.]

PIP: Imagine the ground has released you.

[Vela clips the clipboard to her belt. Sela pushes gently off the wall and drifts into a padded storage net.]

SELA: I have reached the audience.

[Roko tries the bow at the taped mark. He bends, loses balance and grabs the microphone stand.]

ROKO: That is a strong opening.

PIP: You grabbed the stand.

ROKO: It seemed moved by my performance.

[The mission patch on his sleeve catches on the microphone cable. Vela unhooks it. Pip asks them to practice without equipment. Roko bows toward Sela, who is facing away and reading a checklist.]

ACT TWO

[Vela tapes a paper audience to the opposite wall. She labels it FRONT ROW. Sela rotates the paper strip so the faces point toward Roko. Pip tells him to push off gently and let his body stop before speaking.]

ROKO: I can salute instead.

PIP: A salute is still a gesture.

[He demonstrates a slow two-finger salute. His hand taps the paper audience, turning it upside down.]

SELA: They have fainted.

[Vela pins the paper audience to the panel with two clips. Pip marks the microphone position with a fabric loop. Roko practices a small bow with his feet anchored. It works, but the tether pulls his collar sideways.]

ROKO: My uniform is orbiting.

[Vela adjusts the clip so the tether attaches at the waist. The collar settles. Sela reads the mission protocol: secure the mic, check orientation, then greet the crew. Pip says the bow can happen after the greeting, if the commander still wants it.]

ACT THREE

[The crew stages the arrival briefing. Vela clips the mic tether to Roko's waist. Sela calls "cabin stable." Pip releases the practice restraint but stays ready beside the net. Roko pushes off lightly. He rotates half a turn, sees the paper audience facing him, and gives a tiny bow with one hand on the rail.]

SELA: A controlled greeting.

ROKO: A controlled greeting to the entire front row.

[The fabric microphone loop catches the paper audience's clip. The whole strip swings away from the wall like a slow, silent curtain. Roko catches its edge before it covers the control panel. Sela pins it back with the spare clip. Pip checks the mic cable and finds it still secured at Roko's waist.]

PIP: The bow worked.

ROKO: The audience has moved.

[Vela turns the paper strip right side up. Roko practices the greeting once more from the wall rail, making no grand gesture. The paper crowd stays attached. Sela records the sequence for the briefing, then saves the file as CABIN GREETING, not CEREMONY. Pip signs off the exercise.]

FINAL IMAGE: Roko holds the rail in a neat bow while the clipped paper audience floats nowhere and faces the right way.
