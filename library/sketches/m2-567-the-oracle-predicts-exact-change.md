# The Oracle Predicts Exact Change

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fantasy prophecies that treat a purchase counter as the meeting place of destiny*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: ORACLE VELA, an orangutan; TRAVELER PIP, a capuchin; ATTENDANT NARA, a macaque; APPRENTICE RIK, a gibbon.

[A fortune-telling tent at a fair. Vela sits behind a crystal bowl. A discreet price board says SHORT READING: THREE COINS. Pip places one large silver token on the counter.]

VELA: A great exchange lies before you.

NARA: It certainly does. That is a ten-coin token.

PIP: I want the short reading.

VELA: Your path will divide into seven shining fragments.

NARA: Seven coins in change.

[Rik opens the coin box. There are two coins and a folded receipt. He shows it to Nara below the counter, keeping it away from Vela's bowl.]

RIK: We cannot make seven fragments yet.

VELA: Fate has withheld a portion.

ACT ONE

[Nara asks Pip whether he has three small coins. Pip checks a pouch and finds a button, a feather and the ten-coin token already on the counter.]

PIP: No. Can the reading wait while I change the token?

VELA: Time bends around those who seek knowledge.

NARA: Yes. We will hold the token untouched while you visit the neighboring stall, or you may take it with you.

[Rik points out the refreshment stall on the public map. Pip takes the token and leaves for that stall. Rik removes the bowl's blue cloth to dust the stand. Vela lifts one hand dramatically.]

VELA: The traveler shall return carrying water.

RIK: The only place to break the token is the lemonade stall.

VELA: Do not spoil the revelation.

[Nara puts a small sign on the counter: CORRECT CHANGE APPRECIATED. Rik asks whether the sign should be visible. Vela says knowledge must sometimes wait for the worthy. Nara places the sign beside the price board anyway.]

[Pip returns with a lemonade and several coins. He counts three onto the counter, setting the drink on a side stool. Nara takes the payment and writes a receipt.]

VELA: You carry water touched with the sun.

PIP: It is lemonade. The apprentice told me which stall changed tokens.

[Vela glances at Rik. Rik points to the handwritten stall map behind Pip's chair.]

RIK: It was public knowledge.

[Vela begins the reading. She studies the bowl, then looks toward the feather still tucked in Pip's pouch.]

VELA: A creature of the air has brushed your journey.

PIP: I bought the feather at the costume stall.

VELA: A golden face watches over your shoulder.

PIP: The lemonade seller has a sun painted on his sign.

ACT TWO

[Vela lowers her hands. Pip leans forward, finally interested.]

PIP: Can you predict something I cannot already see?

VELA: I can predict you will ask that.

NARA: After he has asked it?

[Rik places a small empty card before Vela.]

RIK: Write the prediction now. We can read it after the traveler chooses what to do next.

[Vela writes privately, folds the card and leaves it on the counter. Pip considers leaving, then decides to stay for the fair's puppet show. Nara unfolds the card. It says: HE WILL REMAIN UNTIL THE SHOW.]

PIP: You got one right.

VELA: Destiny speaks at last.

RIK: He asked me for the puppet timetable when he came back.

[Pip laughs and takes the receipt. Nara adds his coins to the box. There are now five, still too few to change another ten-token reading.]

FINAL IMAGE: Vela waits in a magnificent pose beside Nara's very visible correct-change sign.
