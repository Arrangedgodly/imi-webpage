# Live from the Limb Shop

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Live home-shopping television and breathless product demonstrations*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Vana, a lemur; product expert Grit, a macaque; caller Moss, a gibbon; floor assistant Pip, a tarsier.

[STAGE: BRIGHT SET. A COUNTER HOLDS A TAIL COMB, A MIRROR, AND AN UNWILLING MANNEQUIN.] 

VANA: Welcome to Live from the Limb Shop. Today's offer is the Tail-Tidy 4000, the comb that reaches every tangle without leaving your chair.

GRIT: It works on tails, manes, and small rugs.

PIP: The comb is stuck in the mannequin.

VANA: That proves it has a strong grip.

GRIT: The teeth are flexible. Watch.

[GRIT PULLS. THE MANNEQUIN TIPS FORWARD.] 

VANA: It is standing on its own.

PIP: It is leaning on my foot.

VANA: You can use it without help.

GRIT: Unless you have a very thick tail.

VANA: Then ask a friend to hold the mirror.

PIP: I can hold the mirror.

GRIT: Not by the glass.

PIP: I am holding the handle.

VANA: Our next demonstration is the one-stroke shine.

GRIT: One stroke from root to tip.

MOSS, CALLER: My tail has knots from climbing through a thorn hedge.

VANA: The comb is gentle enough for that.

GRIT: Start at the ends and work upward.

VANA: That is not one stroke.

GRIT: It is one responsible stroke at a time.

VANA: Our offer is still very exciting.

MOSS: Can you demonstrate on the mannequin?

PIP: It has no tail.

GRIT: The scarf is tied to the stand.

VANA: The scarf will model our product.

[GRIT RUNS THE COMB THROUGH THE SCARF. THE KNOT TIGHTENS.] 

VANA: That is a very secure scarf.

GRIT: I will reverse the comb.

PIP: The comb is caught on the tassel.

VANA: You can remove the tassel.

MOSS: I use the tassel to find the end of my scarf.

GRIT: Then do not remove it.

VANA: The Tail-Tidy comes with a travel sleeve.

PIP: The sleeve is too small for the comb.

GRIT: It stretches.

VANA: We are demonstrating that now.

[THE SLEEVE SNAPS OVER THE COMB AND FLIES INTO THE CAMERA.] 

VANA: A close-up of our studio.

MOSS: I have a wide-tooth comb at home. It works if I take my time.

GRIT: The wide teeth are better for large knots.

VANA: Then why are we selling this one?

GRIT: Because it's easier to pack.

PIP: Your comb is bigger than the sleeve.

VANA: We have heard you. The Tail-Tidy is a small comb with a very large promise.

MOSS: I will keep my old comb.

GRIT: I will use this one on the mannequin.

PIP: It has no tail.

GRIT: The scarf is still tangled.

VANA: That is our next demonstration.

MOSS: Can the comb remove burrs?

GRIT: It can loosen them if you start at the ends.

VANA: The card says it makes one stroke.

GRIT: The card has never met a knot.

PIP: We should print that on the back.

[THE PRODUCT CARD SPINS. THE BACK READS: ASK A FRIEND, USE GENTLE STROKES.]
