# The Reenactment of the Missing Spoon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: historical reenactment programs that inflate mundane kitchen mysteries*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: NARRATOR LUX, a gibbon; HISTORIAN ODO, a macaque; REENACTOR PEA, a capuchin; and ARCHIVIST RIM, a lemur.

[A kitchen table is roped off. One spoon rests beside a bowl.] 

LUX: In the year of the long rain, a spoon vanished from the communal kitchen. Its disappearance altered the course of breakfast.

ODO: No, it did not. We used another spoon.

RIM: The archive shows the utensil drawer was opened at dawn.

PEA: I was looking for a spoon.

LUX: Reenact the moment.

[PEA OPENS THE DRAWER. SPOONS CLINK.] 

LUX: The drawer groans under the weight of the evidence.

ODO: The drawer is old.

RIM: The spoon was not in its slot.

PEA: It was beside the bowl. I left it there after stirring.

LUX: A clue hidden in plain sight.

ODO: It was beside the bowl.

LUX: History often leaves objects where they were placed.

RIM: We found a note in the archive: "Please return the spoon after use."

LUX: A message from the past.

PEA: It is from yesterday.

RIM: The note has no date.

PEA: I wrote it.

LUX: The mystery deepens.

ODO: It is a reminder.

[PEA PICKS UP THE SPOON AND PUTS IT IN THE DRAWER.] 

LUX: The reenactment reaches its turning point. The spoon returns to its home.

RIM: It belongs in the drawer.

ODO: The bowl still needs stirring.

PEA: I can use the spoon again and return it after.

LUX: Will the spoon disappear once more?

PEA: No. I will wash it and put it away.

RIM: The archive can record the new location.

ODO: The drawer.

RIM: We should record the spoon's return to the drawer for future researchers.

ODO: It is an ordinary spoon.

LUX: The archive needs a closing image.

PEA: I can set it beside the bowl again.

ODO: Then someone may think it is missing.

PEA: I can set it in the drawer, where it belongs.

[THE DRAWER OPENS. SPOONS CLINK AS PEA PLACES IT IN AN EMPTY SLOT.] 

LUX: The utensil has returned to its designated position.

RIM: We have preserved the sound of the drawer.

ODO: And the bowl is still waiting.

PEA: I can stir the porridge now.

[THE SPOON STIRS. THE PORRIDGE MAKES A SOFT SWIRL.] 

LUX: A final piece of evidence.

ODO: That is breakfast.

RIM: Should we write down the time?

PEA: It is morning.

LUX: The reenactment is complete. The spoon was never stolen. It was used, set down, and returned.

ODO: Next time, we can begin with the drawer.

RIM: Then it will be a much shorter program.

LUX: History does not always need a chase scene.

PEA: Especially when the object is in the kitchen.

LUX: The story ends with order restored.

PEA: The porridge is cold now.

ODO: That is the real consequence of the investigation.

LUX: Next week, we examine the cup that was on the other shelf.

RIM: It is still on the other shelf.

[THE SPOON TAPS THE BOWL. EVERYONE TURNS TOWARD IT.]
