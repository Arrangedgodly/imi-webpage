# The Windmill Tour Is a Rotation

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: heritage demonstrations that narrate a working machine so long the operator cannot run it*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Guide Odo, a baboon; miller Kira, a macaque; visitor Pavi, a gibbon; and technician Doma, a capuchin.

EXT. OLD WINDMILL - DAY

Odo stands beneath the sails with a placard. Kira waits by the control lever. Pavi holds a small bag of grain.

ODO: The windmill has turned for more than a century.

KIRA: It was repaired last month.

ODO: The wheel's original shape remains.

DOMA: The current sails are new.

PAVI: Will it grind the grain?

KIRA: If we open the gate and the wind holds.

ODO: Before that, I will explain the history of the cap.

[THE WIND PICKS UP. THE SAILS TURN SLOWLY.]

INT. WINDMILL FLOOR - LATER

The visitors enter. Odo points to the gears. Kira watches the sails through a small window.

ODO: This gear transfers motion to the millstone.

PAVI: Is it moving?

KIRA: Not yet. The brake is still on.

PAVI: What happens when the brake is off?

ODO: The sails turn the shaft, the shaft turns the gear, and the gear moves the stone.

KIRA: That is the whole process.

ODO: We have not explained the grain gate.

DOMA: The grain gate feeds kernels between the stones.

PAVI: Does the sign have a diagram?

[ODO UNFOLDS A LARGE DIAGRAM. IT COVERS THE WINDOW, BLOCKING THE VIEW OF THE SAILS.]

KIRA: The sails are turning now.

PAVI: I can hear them.

ODO: The diagram is more precise.

DOMA: The brake keeps the stones from turning during the tour.

ODO: It is a safety feature from a later restoration.

KIRA: The wind is right now.

[ODO KEEPS TALKING. THE WINDMILL TURNS, BUT THE BRAKE HOLDS.]

PAVI: The wind is doing its part.

KIRA: The guide is doing his.

DOMA: We can release the brake when the group is clear.

EXT. WINDMILL - AFTERNOON

The group steps outside. Kira opens the gate. The sails begin to turn faster. Odo begins another explanation.

ODO: We are now approaching the demonstration of rotational transfer.

KIRA: The demonstration is happening.

[THE MILLSTONE STARTS TO TURN. GRAIN GOES IN. FLOUR FALLS INTO A BIN.]

PAVI: It works.

ODO: The stone has completed one full rotation.

KIRA: It has ground a bag of grain.

DOMA: The bin is almost full.

ODO: The next part of the tour is the flour.

PAVI: I can see the flour.

[KIRA SCOOPS SOME INTO A PAPER SACK. ODO'S PLACARD BLOWS AGAINST THE MILL, MAKING A SMALL TAPPING SOUND WITH EACH ROTATION.]

KIRA: The mill is making its own tour cue.

ODO: That is the historic rhythm.

PAVI: It is your sign tapping the wall.

ODO: I can narrate without it.

KIRA: Let the mill turn.

PAVI: I can hear the grain moving.

[ODO TAKES THE SIGN DOWN. THE MILL CONTINUES TURNING.]
