# The Museum Guard Tries to Whisper

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hushed museum tours, audio-guide narration, and guards who try to keep visitors quiet around noisy exhibits*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: GUARD, an orangutan; GUIDE, a gibbon; VISITOR, a capuchin; TECH, a macaque; SCULPTURE, a baboon performer.

[A museum gallery. A sign says PLEASE KEEP YOUR VOICE DOWN. A sculpture of a seated monkey wears an audio-guide headset.]

GUIDE: This sculpture was carved from one piece of stone.

VISITOR: Is it listening?

GUARD: Please whisper.

[The visitor leans close to the sculpture. The headset plays a loud recorded voice.]

SCULPTURE: I have been listening for four hundred years.

GUIDE: That is not the official audio guide.

[The tech opens the headset. A tiny speaker is wedged inside the sculpture's ear.]

TECH: Someone connected the tour channel to the statue.

GUARD: Can you turn it down?

TECH: The volume knob is on the other side of the room.

ACT ONE

[The guard walks quietly toward a wall panel. The sculpture turns its head to follow him.]

SCULPTURE: You are walking very loudly.

GUARD: I am wearing soft shoes.

VISITOR: The statue has a point.

[The guide tries a new audio-guide channel. It plays the sound of a door slamming. Everyone flinches.]

GUIDE: That is the sound exhibit next door.

[The tech checks the channel labels. Two wires are crossed. The guard reaches for the volume knob, but it is behind a rope.]

GUARD: I cannot cross the barrier.

SCULPTURE: I can reach it.

[The sculpture stretches one stone-looking arm through the rope and turns the knob. The sound lowers, but every headset starts whispering at once.]

VISITOR: Is the statue whispering to all of us?

TECH: The channel is open.

[The tech points to two controls: the gallery transmitter carries audio to visitor headsets; a separate display player controls the sculpture's small speaker and caption panel. The guide switches off the transmitter, and the tech pauses the display audio while leaving captions active. All headsets go quiet. The sculpture's mouth moves, but no sound comes out. Its caption panel lights up: I HAVE BEEN MUTED.]

GUARD: The room is finally whispering.

SCULPTURE: [caption only] I object in print.

[The visitor selects the artist stop on the headset, but the disabled transmitter leaves it silent. The guide reads the artist's approved quotation card aloud beside the visitor. The sculpture does not echo it.]

GUIDE: [as the artist, reading the quotation] I wanted the figure to look as if it had just heard something it could not repeat.

VISITOR: That is exactly what happened.

[The sculpture's display player remains paused. The guide returns the quotation card to its stand. The guard asks the tech to restart only the display feed, not the gallery transmitter. The tech checks the two switches aloud before pressing the display button. The caption clears; the sculpture's speaker plays one recorded phrase, then stops.]

TECH: Display feed is live. Headset feed stays off.

SCULPTURE: Thank you for keeping the room quiet.

[The tech switches the gallery transmitter back on, with the official tour channel isolated from the display. The guide restarts the official headset tour. One headset receives the guide's narration; the sculpture's display continues its own short loop without repeating the artist quotation.]

ACT TWO

[The tech labels the two controls HEADSETS and DISPLAY. The guide stands beside the visitor and demonstrates each channel separately. The sculpture remains still as the visitor listens.]

GUIDE: The artist intended the figure to appear thoughtful.

VISITOR: I thought it was grumpy.

[The guard asks the visitors to lower their headsets before leaving. The sculpture's recording plays one soft phrase: "Thank you for keeping the room quiet."]

FINAL IMAGE: The guard stands beside the rope as the sculpture faces forward, wearing its headset correctly.
