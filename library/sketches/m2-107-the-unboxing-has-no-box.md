# The Unboxing Has No Box

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: internet unboxing videos and escalating attempts to create suspense around a useful tool*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: HOST DEX, a gibbon; CAMERA OPERATOR LUMA, a macaque; REVIEWER NIB, a capuchin; and a woven basket.

[A table. Nib sits before an empty woven basket. Luma adjusts a ring light.] 

DEX: Welcome back to Unwrap with Us. Today we will reveal something exciting from this basket.

NIB: It is empty.

DEX: The anticipation is part of the experience.

LUMA: Can you rustle the tissue paper?

NIB: There is no tissue paper.

DEX: What did the supplier send?

LUMA: A short length of twine.

NIB: That is useful.

DEX: Do not reveal it yet.

[NIB LIFTS THE TWINE. IT IS TIED AROUND THE BASKET HANDLE.] 

LUMA: The object was holding the package closed.

DEX: A hidden feature.

NIB: It's a tie.

DEX: Tell the audience what you see.

NIB: Twine. Brown. Short. A knot at one end.

DEX: What do you feel?

NIB: It feels rough.

LUMA: That's helpful.

DEX: Can you demonstrate how it works?

NIB: I can use it to tie the basket handle to the shelf.

[HE TIES THE BASKET TO A LOW SHELF. IT STOPS WOBBLING.] 

LUMA: It has stabilized the basket.

DEX: We need a stronger reveal.

NIB: The reveal is that we had an empty basket and now it is secure.

DEX: Could you pretend the twine was difficult to find?

NIB: It was tied to the handle.

LUMA: The camera is still recording.

DEX: Then we can review the footage.

NIB: We have already reviewed it. The basket was empty.

DEX: We have received a question from a viewer. What was inside the basket before we started?

NIB: Nothing.

DEX: Could something be hidden beneath the cloth?

NIB: There is no cloth.

LUMA: We can check the basket together.

[THEY TURN IT OVER AND TAP THE BOTTOM. IT IS SOLID.] 

DEX: There may be a secret drawer.

NIB: There is not.

LUMA: The basket is woven all the way through.

DEX: This unboxing is becoming a product inspection.

NIB: That's because there is no product.

DEX: The twine is a product.

NIB: The twine came from the basket handle.

LUMA: Can we test its strength?

NIB: It is holding the basket to the shelf. Do not pull it.

DEX: The live audience wants a dramatic reveal.

NIB: The reveal is that we tied the basket down so it would stop wobbling.

[THEY PLACE A SMALL STACK OF LEAVES INSIDE. THE BASKET STAYS STILL.] 

LUMA: That is a successful test.

DEX: The leaves are not an unboxing item.

NIB: They are now in the basket.

DEX: We can review the result honestly.

LUMA: The basket is empty, the twine is attached, and the leaves are packed away.

DEX: Our next video will contain an object before we announce the box.

[DEX TURNS THE BASKET UPSIDE DOWN. A LEAF FALLS OUT.] 

DEX: Surprise accessory.

NIB: That is the leaf we put in before filming.

LUMA: It has been here the whole time.

DEX: The basket has a hidden compartment.

NIB: It does not.

[HE PUTS THE LEAF BACK AND TURNS THE BASKET UPRIGHT.] 

DEX: Today's review: the twine works, the basket is stable, and the leaf was not included.
