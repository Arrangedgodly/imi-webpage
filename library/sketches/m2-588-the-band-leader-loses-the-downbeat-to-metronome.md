# The Band Leader Loses the Downbeat to a Metronome

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: concert perfectionism that trusts a click track more than the musicians playing together*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: BAND LEADER NIMA, a lemur; DRUMMER PIP, a macaque; BASSIST RALO, an orangutan; SOUND TECH VELA, a gibbon.

[A rehearsal stage. A metronome clicks on a stool. Nima raises a baton.]

NIMA: We begin on my downbeat.

PIP: The click track already began.

ACT ONE

[Nima lowers the baton. The metronome keeps clicking. Vela turns the tempo dial down; the click speeds up.]

VELA: The dial is labeled LOUDNESS.

[Nima asks Pip to start with a count. Pip taps four beats. The metronome clicks five. Ralo enters on the fourth, and Nima stops him.]

NIMA: You are early.

RALO: I followed the count.

[The metronome continues. Vela looks beneath the stool and finds a second metronome, ticking at a different speed. Nima says the click track has a backup.]

ACT TWO

[Vela unplugs both devices. One continues clicking from a battery. Pip removes the battery. Silence.]

NIMA: Now we can rehearse.

[Nima raises the baton. Pip counts in, Ralo plays a note, and Nima brings the baton down too soon. The drummer misses the beat.]

PIP: You were early.

NIMA: I was conducting.

RALO: We were following you.

[Nima repeats the gesture. It arrives too late. The trio plays a lurching phrase that starts with a bass note, then drum, then baton. Vela records it. It sounds like a small parade losing a wheel.]

ACT THREE

[Nima sets the baton on the stool. The band stands in a circle, watching each other's hands. Pip taps a steady four-count on his knee. Ralo joins on the third beat. Nima nods on the next measure and lifts the baton only after the two are together. They play the opening phrase in time.]

[Vela asks if the metronome should be included in the concert. Nima says only if it can follow the band. Pip puts the battery back. The click begins at a different tempo. Ralo turns the device around so they cannot see its swinging arm. The band continues without it. The click gradually falls into time by accident, then speeds up again.]

NIMA: It had one good bar.

[Pip takes the battery out and puts the metronome in its box. Nima leads the phrase again with a smaller gesture. Ralo and Pip follow each other's rhythm instead of staring at the baton. Vela plays back the earlier recording, which begins with an isolated click and ends with the band. Nima asks to keep the end, where they found the pulse together. She strikes the stool with her baton as if calling the next take, but the band looks to Pip's hand. He gives the count. Nima smiles and joins on the downbeat.]

FINAL IMAGE: The silent metronome sits in its box while the musicians finish a shared four-beat phrase around the empty stool.
