# The Public Bench Auction

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: auctioneers who create bidding frenzy around ordinary public property*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Auctioneer Miri, a macaque; clerk Odo, a baboon; bidder Pava, a gibbon; groundskeeper Nara, a capuchin; park visitor Bex, a lemur; bird, a silent performer or sound effect.

EXT. PUBLIC PARK - DAY

[Miri stands beside a bench enclosed by a rope. Odo holds a ledger. Pava approaches with two tokens. Bex is already sitting on the bench, reading.]

MIRI: We open the bidding at one token.

PAVA: For the bench?

NARA: The bench belongs to the park.

ODO: We are selling the right to choose its location for one week. Proceeds go to the park fund.

PAVA: One token. Put it in the shade.

MIRI: One for shade! Do I hear two?

[A bird chirps. Miri points her gavel toward its tree.]

MIRI: Two from the balcony!

ODO: The bird is not registered.

MIRI: It has been attending every auction.

NARA: It lives there.

[Pava lifts both tokens.]

PAVA: Two, if the bench faces the path.

NARA: It must leave room for the mower.

ODO: Your chosen location requires a direction. North, south, east or west?

PAVA: Under the tree.

ODO: That is not a direction.

[Bex turns a page. Miri points at him.]

MIRI: A silent counteroffer!

BEX: I am on page forty.

MIRI: Forty tokens!

[Bex lowers the book. Odo firmly crosses out the new figure.]

ODO: Reading is not bidding.

BEX: That is a relief. This book has three hundred pages.

[Nara rolls up the rope to clear the public path. Its loose end catches Miri's gavel. Miri draws it toward her and almost pulls the rope post onto the ledger.]

MIRI: A move toward the auctioneer. Do I have a new offer?

NARA: You have a knot.

[She frees the gavel. Pava walks around the tree, studying the patch of shade. Bex stays seated.]

PAVA: East. There is space beside that root.

NARA: That clears the mower route.

ODO: Two tokens for the east side. Any further bids from registered participants?

[Miri waits. The bird chirps. Odo closes his eyes.]

MIRI: Going once. Going twice. Sold to Pava!

[Pava drops both tokens into the park donation box. Nara and Odo take the ends of the bench.]

NARA: Bex, stand up for ten seconds.

BEX: Does the auction remove my right to sit?

PAVA: It moves the seat. Your book can come too.

[Bex stands. They carry the bench beneath the tree and put it down clear of the root. Bex immediately sits again.]

PAVA: Perfect. I would like to try the shade.

[Bex moves along, leaving her room. Miri follows them with the gavel raised.]

MIRI: Shall we auction the naming rights next?

PAVA: I name it Bench.

ODO: That name is already in use.

BEX: By this bench. It has seniority.

[The bird lands on Miri's gavel and pecks it. Miri holds very still.]

MIRI: An opening bid on the auction equipment.

NARA: Is the bird registered now?

ODO: It has taken possession. We may have to bid to get it back.
