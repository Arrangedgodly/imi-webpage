# The Magician's Assistant Labels Every Trapdoor

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: stage magic that depends on mystery until the crew labels every secret*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: MAGICIAN VELA, a gibbon; ASSISTANT NARA, an orangutan; STAGEHAND PIP, a capuchin; REVIEWER ODO, a macaque.

[A small theater. Nara tapes labels on the stage floor: HATCH A, HATCH B, FALSE WALL. Vela enters in a cape.]

VELA: The audience must believe nothing is marked.

NARA: They can read the back of the set.

ACT ONE

[Vela opens a cabinet. A card inside says BOTTOMLESS PIT - DO NOT STORE MOPS. He shuts it.]

VELA: This is a secret compartment.

PIP: It stores mops.

[Odo sits in the first row with a notebook. Vela performs a hand flourish and points to the trapdoor. Nara has labeled it TRAPDOOR - OPENS DOWN. Odo reads it aloud.]

ODO: Very clear.

VELA: Too clear.

[Nara peels the label off. The adhesive pulls up a corner of the floor cloth. Pip catches it before it curls. Vela steps over the flap and pretends it was part of the illusion.]

ACT TWO

[Nara asks where to put the prop rabbit. Vela gestures toward a box. Pip opens it; the rabbit is already inside, wearing a tag marked RABBIT - EXIT THROUGH LEFT HATCH. It hops out through the right hatch.]

NARA: The arrow is wrong.

[Vela tells Pip to swap the label. Pip carries it to the right hatch, where he finds another label: RIGHT HATCH - NOT THE LEFT HATCH. He sticks both labels side by side. Odo takes notes.]

ODO: This is the most accurate show I've reviewed.

[Vela prepares a disappearing scarf. Nara has pinned a note to the cape: SCARF IN LEFT POCKET. Vela searches the right pocket, then pretends to discover the scarf behind Odo's ear. Odo checks his ear; a label says AUDIENCE PARTICIPATION AREA.]

VELA: You have spoiled the surprise.

ODO: I was informed where to look.

[Nara offers a blank label. Vela writes MYSTERY on it and sticks it over the back curtain. The label covers the pull cord. Pip cannot open the curtain. He reaches around and tugs the cord from the other side; the curtain falls, revealing the storage rack and the broom.]

ACT THREE

[Vela announces one final trick: making the stage labels disappear. Nara removes them in order and hands each to Pip. He places them in a box. Odo closes his notebook and waits. Vela taps the box with his wand. Nothing happens.]

VELA: The labels are gone.

ODO: They are in the box.

[Nara turns the box around. Its own label says LABELS. Pip begins to peel it off, but Vela stops him. He asks Nara to let the audience discover one thing on their own. She removes the label and sets it face down. Pip carries the box away without opening it.]

[Vela lifts a small cloth from the center of the stage. Beneath it is the rabbit, who has somehow returned and is chewing the corner of the "MYSTERY" label. Nara tries to intervene, then sees the audience watching. She lets the rabbit finish. Odo writes something in his notebook.]

ODO: That was the only unannounced trick.

VELA: It was planned.

NARA: By the rabbit?

[Vela bows. The rabbit hops toward the left hatch, sees the remaining directional arrows and chooses the center exit. Pip follows with the broom. Nara leaves the stage blank for the first time. Vela starts a grand flourish, but Odo turns the page of his notebook, missing it completely.]

FINAL IMAGE: The stage is spotless and unmarked except for one small paper scrap stuck to the rabbit's paw.
