# Please Hold the Applause

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: audience etiquette films and self-important experimental theater*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: VEE, a macaque usher; NIB, a baboon audience member; RO, a gibbon stage manager; BABY, a young gibbon; PERFORMER, an orangutan.

[A theater. A sign reads PLEASE HOLD APPLAUSE UNTIL THE END. The PERFORMER places a pebble on a table and stares at it. NIB claps once.]

VEE: Sir. We haven't reached the end.

NIB: He put it down. I thought that was good.

VEE: This is a ninety-minute examination of weight.

NIB: Mine or the pebble's?

[RO rushes from the wings, wearing a headset with no wire.]

RO: Who applauded the opening placement? You've spent the audience response allocated to the final lift.

NIB: I can clap again.

RO: Then it will look rehearsed.

[The PERFORMER lifts the pebble an inch. BABY claps.]

VEE: Children under two must hold applause until they understand subtext.

NIB: He understands up.

RO: We don't say up. We say resistance to down.

[The PERFORMER lowers the pebble. BABY laughs. The PERFORMER glares.]

RO: That's our bleakest moment.

NIB: Maybe move it lower.

VEE: Please don't direct from the audience. Premium tickets include a feedback questionnaire.

[NIB reads his program. It contains a questionnaire headed DID YOU DESERVE TO BE HERE?]

NIB: Where do I put yes?

VEE: There isn't space for every interpretation.

[The PERFORMER raises the pebble again. NIB holds his hands apart. His tail starts tapping the seat.]

RO: We have unauthorized percussion.

NIB: It's involuntary.

VEE: Voluntary control of all appendages is in the booking terms.

[VEE hands NIB a tiny pair of tail mittens. He puts them on his hands.]

NIB: Now I can't applaud.

RO: Perfect. The work is provoking restraint.

[The PERFORMER bows. Nobody claps. RO looks delighted, then worried.]

RO: That was a bow within the piece. You're meant to recognize the false ending.

NIB: I did. I didn't clap.

RO: But you should acknowledge that you recognized it.

VEE: One discreet nod is permitted.

[NIB nods. BABY nods. VEE nods. Their seats squeak in rhythm. The PERFORMER begins keeping time with the pebble.]

RO: Stop! The pebble doesn't develop a groove until act four.

NIB: How many acts?

RO: Six. All one minute long. The pauses supply the other eighty-four.

[The pebble rolls off the table. Everyone reaches for it. The PERFORMER catches it, slips on a program, and lands seated beside NIB. Thunderous applause erupts from BABY.]

VEE: That wasn't the scheduled ending.

NIB: Best one so far.

PERFORMER: Thank you.

RO: Don't encourage them! You're still investigating gravity!

[The PERFORMER gives BABY the pebble. BABY puts it on the floor.]

NIB: He's solved it.

[VEE turns the sign over. It reads PLEASE CONTINUE APPLAUDING UNTIL VALIDATED. NIB stops clapping.]

VEE: Sir, now you've stopped early.

NIB: I'm taking a pause. It supplies the other eighty-four minutes.

[The PERFORMER settles into the next seat. VEE and RO stand waiting for applause. BABY puts a finger to their lips. BLACKOUT.]
