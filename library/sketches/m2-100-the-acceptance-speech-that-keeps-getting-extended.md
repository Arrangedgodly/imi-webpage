# The Acceptance Speech That Keeps Getting Extended

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: awards ceremonies, speeches, and winners thanking increasingly broad groups*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: WINNER SORA, a macaque; HOST PELL, a gibbon; STAGE MANAGER TAVI, a capuchin; and the orchestra.

[An awards stage. Sora holds a small wooden trophy. The orchestra waits with bows raised.] 

PELL: Congratulations on winning the Branch Builder award. You have thirty seconds for your speech.

SORA: I thank the troop who helped weave the roof.

TAVI: Twenty seconds.

SORA: I thank the neighbors who carried the twigs.

TAVI: Fifteen.

SORA: I thank the tree that held the branches.

PELL: That is generous.

TAVI: Ten seconds.

SORA: I thank the rain for showing us where the roof leaked.

[THE ORCHESTRA PLAYS A SOFT EXIT TUNE.] 

SORA: I also thank the rain for stopping.

PELL: Please wrap up.

SORA: I thank the nestlings for not waking while we worked.

TAVI: Five seconds.

SORA: And I thank the person who made the trophy.

PELL: That was you.

SORA: Then I thank myself for not making it heavier.

[APPLAUSE. SORA TRIES TO LEAVE, BUT THE TROPHY CATCHES ON THE PODIUM.] 

TAVI: The speech has concluded.

SORA: The trophy is still here.

PELL: Leave it. We will bring it backstage.

SORA: It is made from the leftover branch. I should return it to the workshop.

PELL: We can put it in the display case.

SORA: It will take up space and no one can use it.

TAVI: The award is supposed to be displayed.

SORA: The award is made of wood. We can turn it into a repair wedge for the roof.

PELL: That may be the first award to return to work.

TAVI: The orchestra is waiting for the next line.

SORA: I have already thanked everyone who built the roof.

PELL: You can thank the people who will repair it.

SORA: We do not know who they are yet.

TAVI: The next award is for the best speech. Your speech is still happening.

PELL: We can nominate it while you are on stage.

SORA: I would like to thank the judges for not making me thank the judges.

[THE ORCHESTRA PLAYS ANOTHER WALK-OFF.] 

TAVI: That is the cue.

SORA: It sounds like the cue to stop.

PELL: The band has learned to speak for the stage manager.

SORA: I can leave now.

[THE TROPHY CATCHES ON THE PODIUM AGAIN. SORA LIFTS IT FREE.] 

TAVI: The award is clear.

PELL: The podium is clear.

SORA: The stage is almost clear.

TAVI: We should take the trophy to the workshop, then.

PELL: Can it really become a repair wedge?

SORA: The branch is dry and solid. We can cut it to fit.

PELL: Then the award will support the roof it recognized.

TAVI: That is a better display than a case.

[SORA AND TAVI CARRY THE TROPHY TOWARD THE WORKSHOP EXIT. PELL RETURNS TO THE PODIUM. THE ORCHESTRA STOPS PLAYING.]

PELL: The program is finally on schedule.

[SORA WAVES FROM THE EXIT. PELL TAKES THE MICROPHONE.]

PELL: I would like to thank the judges, the builders, the orchestra, and

[THE ORCHESTRA PLAYS THE WALK-OFF TUNE. PELL TRIES TO SPEAK OVER IT.] 

PELL: And the orchestra, for playing through my thank-you.

TAVI: That is your cue to leave.

[PELL FOLLOWS SORA OFFSTAGE, STILL HOLDING THE MICROPHONE.]
