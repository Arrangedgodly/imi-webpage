# The Travel Guide Tours the Bus Shelter

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury travel programs, destination marketing, and guides who promise spectacular sights in a very ordinary town*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: GUIDE, a gibbon; TOURIST, a baboon; CAMERA OPERATOR, an orangutan; LOCAL, a macaque; ANNOUNCER, a capuchin.

[A bus shelter beside a quiet road. The guide stands beside a sign that reads WELCOME TO LITTLE CROSSING. The camera operator frames the scene.]

GUIDE: Here we are at the town's central transport pavilion.

TOURIST: It is a bus shelter.

GUIDE: A covered place to wait for possibility.

[The local arrives carrying a paper timetable.]

LOCAL: The next bus is in twenty minutes.

GUIDE: Excellent. We begin the tour now.

TOURIST: Is there a famous view?

[The guide points to a field across the road. A cow chews slowly.]

GUIDE: Our pastoral overlook.

CAMERA OPERATOR: The cow is facing away.

GUIDE: It is preserving the reveal.

ACT ONE

[The guide leads the tourist around the shelter. A bench and a timetable case are the only features. The tourist studies a map.]

TOURIST: The brochure promised a historic clock.

LOCAL: That clock is in the grocery store. It is five minutes fast.

[The announcer appears with a portable sign and props it against the shelter. It reads SUNSET EXPERIENCE.]

ANNOUNCER: We have a premium stop at sundown.

TOURIST: Can I see it now?

GUIDE: The sunset is scheduled later.

[The camera operator asks the guide to point out the local art. The guide gestures to a small mosaic on the shelter wall. It depicts a bus.]

GUIDE: A tribute to reliable transportation.

LOCAL: The bus is usually late.

[The tourist checks the schedule. It says "approximately." The guide covers the word with a finger.]

GUIDE: The town prefers flexible time.

[The cow turns and looks toward the group. The camera operator zooms in.]

TOURIST: That is the best view so far.

GUIDE: You have discovered the secret attraction.

LOCAL: That is a cow.

[The bus arrives early. The local waves it down. The driver opens the door. The tourist looks from the bus to the rest of the town.]

[The tourist asks to see the historic clock. The local walks with them to the grocery store. The clock above the till reads 4:20 while the tourist's phone reads 4:15. The local taps the clock face. It loses another minute.]

TOURIST: Is this the famous timepiece?

LOCAL: It is the only one.

[The guide takes a photo of the two clocks side by side. The camera operator asks them to stand still. The minute hand moves backward one tick.]

GUIDE: The town offers flexible time in both directions.

ACT TWO

GUIDE: Our tour must pause for transport.

TOURIST: Is there a return route?

LOCAL: Same bus, same stop, another twenty minutes.

[The tourist boards with the local. The guide hands over the brochure and points to a blank square labeled SUGGESTED ACTIVITIES.]

GUIDE: You can write your own highlights.

[The camera operator remains at the shelter, filming the empty bench. The announcer places the sunset sign back in the bag.]

FINAL IMAGE: The bus pulls away as the cow continues chewing beside the town's premium overlook.
