# The Ranger Hotline

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: park-service call centers and overproduced outdoor advice segments*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: RANGER LEE, a macaque; CALLER MOSS, a baboon; DISPATCHER FIN, a gibbon; and a SQUIRREL on an open line.

[A park office. Three phones ring. A map has been turned sideways.]

LEE: Ranger hotline. What is your location?

MOSS: Beside the big tree.

LEE: Which big tree?

MOSS: The one with the low branch.

FIN: That's every tree here.

[Fin rotates the map. The river is at the top.]

LEE: Can you see a trail marker?

MOSS: I see a red sign that says CLOSED.

LEE: Stay on the open path and tell me the marker number.

MOSS: The sign has a number. Is that the trail number?

LEE: It might be the date.

[The squirrel line chirps.]

SQUIRREL: I have a complaint about the closed trail.

LEE: Are you lost?

SQUIRREL: No. I live here. The sign is blocking my hole.

FIN: We can dispatch someone to move the sign.

MOSS: Is the ranger coming for me?

LEE: I am trying to establish where you are.

MOSS: Under the tree.

LEE: Could you look at the marker?

MOSS: I can see a number.

LEE: Read it.

MOSS: Four.

FIN: There are only three marked trails.

[Lee takes the map and turns it upright. The red sign on the map is the ranger station's own exit.]

LEE: Moss, do you see a building?

MOSS: I see a building.

LEE: Then you're at the ranger station.

MOSS: I thought the forest was larger.

SQUIRREL: Can someone move the sign from my hole?

FIN: That squirrel is at the station too.

[The squirrel pokes its head through a vent.]

LEE: We'll move the sign and walk you back to the marked path.

MOSS: Can the sign say where the big tree is?

LEE: The forest has many big trees.

MOSS: Then the sign should say that.

[Fin pins the map to the wall. The squirrel climbs it and sits on the exit symbol.]

LEE: We have updated the map.

FIN: The squirrel is now the "you are here" dot.

SQUIRREL: I was here first.

[The phone rings again. Everyone looks at the map.]


LEE: Ranger hotline, you're on the air. Can you see a marker?

CALLER, off: I can see the ranger station.

FIN: That's where we are.

CALLER: The map says I'm at marker four.

LEE: The map was upside down.

CALLER: Am I lost?

LEE: No. We were.

[Fin rotates the map properly. The red exit symbol points toward the door.]

FIN: The map has a small "you are here" label.

LEE: It was under the squirrel.

SQUIRREL: I was sitting in my home.

[Lee hangs up the phone, then calls the number back.]

LEE: The ranger station has been restored to its correct location.

FIN: Should we update the public map?

LEE: First, let's move the squirrel off it.

[The squirrel moves one paw. The map shifts.]

[BLACKOUT.]
