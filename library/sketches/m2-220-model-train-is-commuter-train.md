# The Model Train Is the Commuter Train

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hobby rail clubs that insist on realism while the real railway runs on a delayed schedule*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Club President Olan, a gibbon; Engineer Miri, a macaque; Commuter Fenn, a capuchin; and Club Member Tavo, a baboon.

INT. TRAIN CLUB HALL - MORNING

A detailed model railway loops around a table. Miri holds a tiny station sign. Olan checks a timetable. Fenn waits at the door with a travel mug.

OLAN: The model express departs at nine sharp.

MIRI: The real commuter train is delayed by twenty minutes.

TAVO: The model is not affected by track maintenance.

FENN: I need to catch the real train.

OLAN: We are demonstrating accurate service.

[FENN WATCHES THE MODEL. IT PASSES THE STATION TWICE WITHOUT STOPPING.]

MIRI: The express is still on schedule.

FENN: It has not stopped once.

OLAN: It is an express.

MIRI: The next train is a local.

[TAVO ADDS A TINY PASSENGER CAR. IT FALLS OFF THE TRACK AND LANDS IN A CUP OF TEA.]

FENN: That is more realistic than the express.

INT. TRAIN CLUB HALL - LATER

Miri fixes the model track. Olan holds up a tiny sign: "DELAYED." Tavo has a clipboard.

OLAN: We should add a delay announcement.

MIRI: The model has no speakers.

TAVO: I can make one.

TAVO: Attention passengers, the service is delayed because the tiny track is being repaired by a very large hand.

FENN: That's accurate.

OLAN: The hand must not appear in the scene.

[MIRI USES A SMALL TOOL TO REPAIR THE TRACK. THE MODEL TRAIN WAITS.]

MIRI: The train is stopped until the track is clear.

OLAN: The delay should be less than five minutes.

TAVO: The hand is still on the tracks.

OLAN: It is the operator's hand.

FENN: The passengers can see it.

MIRI: The train is waiting for the hand to clear.

[OLAN LIFTS HIS HAND. THE TRAIN MOVES FORWARD, THEN STOPS AT A TINY SIGNAL.]

OLAN: The signal is red.

MIRI: The signal is a sticker. I can turn it green.

MIRI: It will be six.

OLAN: The model cannot be late.

FENN: It is already late.

INT. TRAIN CLUB HALL - FINAL RUN

The model local rolls toward the station and stops. Miri lifts a miniature barrier. Fenn looks at the clock.

MIRI: Track clear. Local service is moving.

OLAN: The express is next.

FENN: I am taking the local.

[HE PUTS DOWN HIS MUG AND BOARDs THE TINY TRAIN WITH ONE FOOT, STRADDLING THE MODEL TRACK.]

TAVO: That is not to scale.

FENN: Neither is the delay.

[MIRI STARTS THE TRAIN. FENN WALKS ALONGSIDE IT, ARRIVING AT THE STATION AT THE SAME TIME.]

OLAN: The model has arrived on time.

FENN: So did I.

[THE REAL TRAIN HORN SOUNDS OUTSIDE. FENN RUNS FOR THE DOOR. THE MODEL TRAIN CONTINUES ITS LOOP, EMPTY AND PERFECTLY PUNCTUAL.]
