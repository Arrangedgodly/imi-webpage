# The Carry-On Inspection

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport security training videos and overdesigned luggage rules*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: AGENT TOLL, a macaque security trainer; PAX, a langur passenger; DOT, a capuchin inspector; and ANNOUNCER, a training-film voice.

[A checkpoint. A taped line leads to a tray table. A sign reads EMPTY YOUR POCKETS, EXCEPT THE ONES YOU NEED.]

ANNOUNCER: Welcome to the carry-on inspection. Remove all objects from your bag and place them in the tray.

PAX: All objects?

AGENT TOLL: The tray is small. We recommend removing the largest objects first.

[Pax lifts a broad leaf from the bag and places it in the tray. It covers the entire table.]

DOT: That is not a tray anymore. It is a canopy.

ANNOUNCER: Please use tray two.

[Tray two is smaller. Pax lowers a fruit pouch into it.]

AGENT TOLL: Any liquids?

PAX: One bottle of water.

AGENT TOLL: Empty it.

PAX: The sign says empty pockets.

AGENT TOLL: The sign is not about bottles.

PAX: The sign is doing too much.

[Dot opens the bag and finds a twig, a comb, and a small banana.]

DOT: We need to identify this object.

PAX: It is a banana.

ANNOUNCER: The inspection protocol requires a second opinion.

AGENT TOLL: I confirm it is a banana.

DOT: I need to scan it.

[She rotates the scanner around the banana. The scanner beeps at Pax's tail.]

ANNOUNCER: We have detected an elongated personal item.

PAX: That is my tail.

AGENT TOLL: The scanner cannot tell.

PAX: Can the agent tell?

[Everyone looks at Toll.]

AGENT TOLL: I can. But the scanner has a checklist.

[The training film voice says "Please remain patient." A slow-moving tray arrives with Pax's tail still in it.]

DOT: We have a tail in the tray.

PAX: The tray is moving.

AGENT TOLL: Retrieve the tail only when the tray stops.

[The tray stops. Pax lifts the tail and puts it over his shoulder.]

ANNOUNCER: Thank you for your cooperation. Please collect all items and proceed.

PAX: Which tray has my leaf?

DOT: The tray with the banana, comb, twig, and tail.

PAX: Those are in four trays.

[The line behind him has become a single queue of confused trays.]

AGENT TOLL: We will return the items in order.

ANNOUNCER: The training film will now demonstrate a fast checkpoint.

[Dot flips the sign from FAST to CLOSED.]

DOT: We are not demonstrating while people are waiting.

AGENT TOLL: Fair. We will inspect the sign next.

[The sign falls over. Everyone places it in a tray.]
[The training film rewinds to the start of the checkpoint.]

ANNOUNCER: Please place all personal items in the tray.

PAX: Which tray?

DOT: The one without the other passenger's tail.

AGENT TOLL: We need a better sign.

[Dot writes: ONE PERSON, ONE TRAY. THE LEAF HAS ITS OWN.]

ANNOUNCER: That is not the approved language.

PAX: It is clear.

[The training-film voice offers a slow-motion replay. The line of trays begins moving again.]

AGENT TOLL: We have an unscheduled demonstration.

DOT: No. We have people trying to get through.

[She removes the projector. The queue moves at normal speed.]

[BLACKOUT.]
