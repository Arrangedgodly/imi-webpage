# The Method Actor Becomes the Wrong Tree

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: method-acting interviews that confuse intense character research with the role actually cast*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: ACTOR VELA, an orangutan; DIRECTOR PIP, a macaque; WARDROBE NARA, a capuchin; RUNNER RIK, a gibbon.

[A rehearsal room. A painted forest flat leans against the rear wall. Vela stands motionless in a brown coat, arms stretched upward. Pip holds a script. Nara carries a folded costume; Rik has a watering can for a real potted fern.]

PIP: Ready for your entrance?

VELA: I have been ready for three seasons.

PIP: The production has run for six days.

VELA: I am measuring as a tree.

[Nara unfolds the costume: a long blue river cape.]

NARA: You are playing the river.

ACT ONE

[Vela slowly lowers one branchlike arm to read the script Pip offers. The cast line says RIVER VOICE.]

VELA: I was told to embody the landscape.

PIP: We said the river runs through the landscape.

VELA: I spent a week observing bark.

RIK: You can still bring that to the scene. There are trees beside the river.

PIP: Please do not encourage another tree.

[Rik waters the fern. Vela turns toward the can, moving so slowly that Rik finishes before he reaches it.]

VELA: Water is my first connection to the new role.

RIK: This fern needs it more urgently.

[Nara hangs the blue cape on a free rail, then asks Vela to take off the brown coat. He studies the coat's sleeve.]

VELA: Removing bark is a profound act.

NARA: It is a rental, and we need it for the gardener in the next scene.

[Vela removes the coat. Nara hangs it on the rail and helps him fasten the river cape over his ordinary rehearsal clothes. He lifts both arms again. The cape makes a very narrow blue trunk.]

PIP: Rivers move horizontally. Try crossing from this mark to that one while you speak.

VELA: I have no roots to guide me.

PIP: You have two taped marks.

[Vela walks across, speaking the river's line: "I carry the rain beyond the village." He finishes beside the fern and drapes the cape toward it.]

RIK: Please keep the cloth out of the pot. The soil is wet.

VELA: A river must meet its banks.

NARA: This river must return a clean costume.

ACT TWO

[Pip asks for another crossing, this time keeping the cape just above the floor. Vela moves faster and lets the cloth ripple behind him. Rik shifts the fern to the window, out of the acting path.]

PIP: That is the movement. Keep it for the scene.

VELA: I can feel the current.

NARA: And I can see both of your shoes.

[Vela reads the next line, realizing it belongs to the village gardener. He starts it anyway in a deep wooden voice. Pip holds up the cast list.]

PIP: The gardener speaks after the river. Different actor.

VELA: My former life is calling to me.

RIK: Your former coat is waiting on the rail.

[Nara folds the gardener's sleeve inward so it will not drag. Vela ends his river line at the correct mark and bows. Pip marks the crossing in the script without adding any forest research.]

VELA: Shall I research the ocean for the final scene?

PIP: The final scene is in a village kitchen. You are offstage.

FINAL IMAGE: Vela studies the empty kitchen entrance with tremendous seriousness while Rik quietly moves the fern farther away.
