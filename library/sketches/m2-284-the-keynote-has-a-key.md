# The Keynote Has a Key

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: motivational conference keynotes, audience participation slogans, and speakers who turn one practical tool into a personal breakthrough*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Speaker Omi, a gibbon; event host Rala, a macaque; audience member Pip, a capuchin; technician Nox, a ring-tailed lemur; and stage manager Vela, an orangutan.

INT. CONFERENCE HALL - DAY

Omi stands beneath a banner that says UNLOCK YOUR POTENTIAL. A brass key sits on a velvet pillow. Rala speaks to the audience.

RALA: Our keynote speaker will teach us how to unlock our potential using one simple object.

OMI: A key.

The audience nods. A giant screen shows a key rotating slowly.

OMI: This key opens the supply closet.

PIP: Why is it on a velvet pillow?

RALA: It represents access.

OMI: It also opens the supply closet.

Omi begins a story about being locked out of the conference room. The door was closed because the janitor was mopping. He waited, then used the key when the floor dried. The audience waits for the dramatic lesson.

OMI: The lesson is that sometimes you have to wait for the floor to dry.

RALA: Can we phrase it as "unlock your next level"?

OMI: You can phrase it however you want.

Vela whispers to Nox: the keynote slides are stuck. Nox taps the screen, which advances three slides at once. A diagram shows a key, a ladder, and a mountain.

OMI: I did not climb a mountain.

RALA: That slide is from the next speaker.

Pip raises a hand.

PIP: Can I borrow the key? I need a pen from the closet.

RALA: Please wait until the keynote exercise.

PIP: The exercise is about access.

Omi hands him the key. Pip unlocks the closet and returns with a pen, a box of name tags, and a mop.

OMI: We have now accessed three useful things.

RALA: Everyone, write down the door you need to open.

The audience writes. One attendee writes "closet." Another writes "bathroom." A third writes "my calendar." Omi tells them the calendar has no lock. Rala urges everyone to share one word that describes their breakthrough.

AUDIENCE: Access. Growth. Courage. Closet.

Vela discovers the supply closet door does not latch when shut. The key is unnecessary to open it from the outside. Omi tests the door, and it swings open.

OMI: The key is not the problem. The latch is.

RALA: We can make that the takeaway.

PIP: Could the keynote be about fixing the latch?

Nox retrieves a screwdriver. Omi holds the door while Vela tightens the latch. The audience watches. It takes two minutes. The door now closes properly and opens with the key.

RALA: That was a very effective session.

OMI: It was maintenance.

PIP: I have my pen.

Vela returns the key to its ring. Rala puts the velvet pillow away.

RALA: The next speaker is about a ladder.

OMI: Does the ladder reach the screen?

RALA: It represents ambition.

OMI: It can also fix the projector.

Nox wheels the ladder toward the screen. The audience stands to help move chairs out of the way. The ladder reaches the projector, but the keynote's giant spinning key graphic is still on the screen.

OMI: We have unlocked the next level.

PIP: The projector is frozen.

[Nox turns the projector off. The key disappears, and the supply closet door clicks shut behind them.]

RALA: The practical session is over.

OMI: Finally, a keynote with a clear exit.
