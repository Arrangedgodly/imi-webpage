# The Public Speaking Contest

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public speaking competitions where confidence is scored more highly than clear communication*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Host Dena, a gibbon; judge Odo, a baboon; contestant Veli, a macaque; contestant Piri, a capuchin; and timekeeper.

[A SMALL STAGE HAS A PODIUM, A TIMER, AND A SIGN: "SPEAK WITH CONFIDENCE."]

DENA: Contestants have one minute to persuade us to take a safer path.

VELI: The left path has dry stones.

[THE TIMER STARTS.]

ODO: Good content. Low projection.

PIRI: The right path has shade and a fallen branch.

[THE TIMER BUZZES.]

ODO: Strong confidence. No recommendation.

PIRI: I wanted to show the branch.

[THE JUDGE OPENS A SCORECARD.]

ODO: We score posture, volume, and eye contact.

VELI: Do we score safety?

ODO: Safety is assumed.

[THE TIMEKEEPER HANDS VELI A MICROPHONE. VELI SPEAKS.]

VELI: Please use the left path. It is dry.

[THE MICROPHONE FEEDS BACK.]

DENA: The feedback is strong.

ODO: It is also loud.

[PIRI STEPS TO THE PODIUM.]

PIRI: I recommend the left path, and I can show you the stones.

[THE CROWD STARTS FOLLOWING HER OFFSTAGE.]

DENA: The speech is still happening.

PIRI: The path is outside.

[THE JUDGE CHASES AFTER THE CROWD WITH HIS SCORECARD.]

ODO: I have not assigned points.

[THE TIMEKEEPER CARRIES THE PODIUM TO THE DOOR.]

DENA: We need the podium for the next speaker.

VELI: The next speaker is on the path.

[THE JUDGE RETURNS, OUT OF BREATH.]

ODO: Piri gets full marks for confidence.

PIRI: I was not confident. I was showing the route.

ODO: Then we need a new category.

DENA: Clear direction.

[THE TIMER BUZZES. VELI POINTS TO THE EXIT.]

VELI: That is the way out.

[THE CROWD LEAVES. THE JUDGE WRITES "CLEAR DIRECTION" ON HIS SCORECARD.]

ODO: The contest has concluded.

[THE JUDGE ASKS THE CONTESTANTS TO REPEAT THEIR SPEECHES WITH THE NEW CATEGORY.]

ODO: Veli, speak to the whole room.

VELI: The left path is dry. Please use it.

[THE CROWD MOVES LEFT.]

ODO: That was clear.

DENA: Piri, your turn.

PIRI: The right path is shaded, but the branch is down. Wait until it is moved.

[THE CROWD STOPS.]

ODO: She has introduced a condition.

PIRI: The condition matters.

[THE TIMEKEEPER STARTS THE CLOCK. IT BUZZES BEFORE PIRI FINISHES.]

DENA: The clock cut off the useful part.

ODO: We should give contestants time to finish.

[THE JUDGE CROSSES OUT THE ONE-MINUTE LIMIT.]

DENA: The contest now measures whether the audience understands the direction.

ODO: We can ask them to point.

[THE CROWD POINTS LEFT. THE JUDGE MARKS THE SCORECARD.]



VELI: That is the first clear instruction we have heard.

[PIRI HANDS THE JUDGE THE SCORECARD.]

PIRI: The audience followed the directions.

ODO: Then the audience understands the point.

DENA: We can give the award to the clear instruction.

[THE PODIUM IS LEFT EMPTY. THE MICROPHONE CONTINUES AMPLIFYING THE TIMER.]
