# The Costume Contest Needs a Ruler

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: judging panels that turn a playful costume contest into technical scoring*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Sava, a gibbon judge; Niko, a macaque contestant; Jori, a lemur judge; Bim, a capuchin contestant.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

[A SMALL STAGE HOLDS A SIGN: COSTUME CONTEST. SAVA HAS A RULER, JORI A SCORECARD. NIKO WEARS A CUCUMBER COSTUME. BIM WEARS A BOX.]

SAVA: We will judge fit, finish, and originality.

BIM: I'm a moving box.

JORI: The box has a shipping label.

BIM: That's my name tag.

Niko steps up. His cucumber costume is long and green, with a felt leaf at the top. Sava measures the leaf. Jori asks whether the costume represents a cucumber or a whole plant.

NIKO: I am a cucumber.

SAVA: The scorecard asks for botanical accuracy.

Jori peers at the costume. The leaf is sewn to the hood. Niko turns, and the leaf flops over his eyes.

JORI: The leaf is functional.

SAVA: It obscures the contestant.

Bim enters the stage. The box costume is too wide to fit between the curtains. Bim turns sideways, and the box catches on the edge.

BIM: I can't get through the judging area.

JORI: The box is oversized.

BIM: It's a shipping box.

Sava measures the opening. The box is one inch wider. She deducts a point. Jori says a shipping box should fit through a doorway. Bim points to the box's label, which says FRAGILE.

SAVA: That is not a dimension.

Bim removes the label and folds one side of the box inward. It now fits. Jori awards him an originality point for adapting his costume.

Niko tries to keep the leaf from his eyes. He pins it back with a safety pin from Bim's costume box. The pin holds, but the green felt leaf points straight up.

JORI: Better visibility.

SAVA: Less botanical accuracy.

NIKO: I traced the pattern from a cucumber.

JORI: You have the original?

Niko unfolds his green paper pattern. Sava tastes its edge before he can explain. She leaves a small bite in the template.

SAVA: It has no flavor.

NIKO: It's a costume.

Bim says the box can be recycled after the contest. Jori asks if that counts as a costume or a promise. Sava awards a point for sustainability.

Niko and Bim stand side by side for the final photo. The side lamp throws Niko's cucumber outline over the broad shadow of Bim's box, making one enormous cucumber silhouette. The judges look at it.

JORI: The shadow has strong composition.

SAVA: It also fits the stage.

Jori points out that the shadow has no seam and no shipping label. Sava checks the scorecard. The shadow has scored highest in fit, finish, and originality without being entered. Bim nudges the lamp aside. His box blocks it and changes the shape.

BIM: It needs a better angle.

NIKO: I can hold the leaf still.

Bim steps away from the lamp; the enormous silhouette becomes a skinny cucumber. Niko lowers the leaf and Bim returns beside him. Their shadows merge again.

[THE JUDGES AWARD THE PRIZE TO THE SHADOW. JORI TRIES TO PIN A RIBBON TO THE WALL. IT FALLS THROUGH THE SILHOUETTE. BIM CATCHES IT IN HIS BOX WHILE NIKO KEEPS THE LEAF STILL FOR THE WINNER'S PHOTOGRAPH.]
