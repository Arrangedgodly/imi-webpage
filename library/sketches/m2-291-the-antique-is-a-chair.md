# The Antique Is a Chair

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: antique appraisal programs, dramatic provenance reveals, and experts who value an object more after hearing a story about it*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Appraiser Lela, a macaque; owner Vex, a gibbon; host Omi, an orangutan; and producer Nari, a capuchin.

INT. APPRAISAL STUDIO - DAY

A wooden chair sits under a cloth. Omi turns to camera.

OMI: Today, we appraise a chair with a mysterious past.

VEX: It has been in my family for three days.

LELA: What do you know about it?

VEX: My aunt gave it to me. She said it belonged to a famous explorer.

NARI: We will reveal the value after hearing the story.

Lela inspects the chair. One leg is shorter. The seat has a notch. The wood is ordinary.

LELA: It is a sturdy chair, likely made last year.

OMI: But perhaps it was built on an expedition.

VEX: My aunt said the explorer sat in it.

LELA: Did the explorer own it?

VEX: She did not say.

Nari plays dramatic music. Omi asks Vex for a family history. Vex describes his aunt's kitchen, where the chair stood beside a cupboard. He remembers sitting on it to reach a jar of crackers.

OMI: A childhood memory.

VEX: I was an adult.

Lela discovers the notch fits against the cupboard handle. It was cut so the chair could slide under the counter. The producer asks whether this makes it more valuable.

LELA: It makes it more useful.

OMI: What is the appraisal?

LELA: Fifteen coins if sold as a chair. Twenty if the buyer needs a shorter leg repaired.

VEX: Can I keep it?

LELA: That is worth more to you.

Omi removes the cloth again, preparing a second reveal. The camera zooms in. Vex sits down. The chair wobbles.

VEX: It is shorter on the other side.

Lela adjusts the foot with a folded piece of felt. The chair stops wobbling.

NARI: The value has increased.

LELA: No. The chair is still the same value. It just works better.

OMI: We have a surprising conclusion.

VEX: I will take it home. It fits under my counter.

Lela gives him a small receipt. Omi asks whether the explorer's name should be included.

VEX: My aunt never knew it.

LELA: Then we can call it your aunt's kitchen chair.

The host turns to camera for the closing. Nari removes the dramatic cloth, revealing that it was covering a second chair borrowed from the waiting room.

OMI: This one belonged to our producer.

NARI: It is the chair I was sitting on.

Lela appraises it: "Currently in use." The crew moves the chair back to the waiting area. Vex lifts his chair and carries it carefully, making sure all four legs clear the door.

Omi starts a slow-motion replay of Vex carrying the chair. The screen labels it "A Return to Its Origins." Vex sets it down beside the tea table and sits. The chair works immediately, ending the historical mystery.
