# Science Fair Judges

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: School science fairs and overconfident judging panels*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Judge Pelt, a baboon; judge Liri, a lemur; student Toma, a capuchin; student Ren, an orangutan; announcer Suki, a marmoset.

[STAGE: TWO POSTER BOARDS, A TABLE, A SMALL PENDULUM MADE FROM A PEAR.] 

SUKI: Welcome to the Canopy Science Fair. Please do not touch the experiments or the snacks, which are experiments.

PELT: Toma, describe your project.

TOMA: I tested whether monkeys can tell a fresh branch from a dry one by sound.

LIRI: What did you find?

TOMA: Fresh wood makes a lower tap. Dry wood makes a sharper tap.

PELT: Demonstrate.

TOMA: This is the fresh branch.

[TAP. A LOW WOODEN NOTE.] 

TOMA: This is the dry branch.

[TAP. A BRIGHT CLICK.] 

LIRI: I can hear the difference.

PELT: Could the difference be the table?

TOMA: I tested five branches on three tables.

PELT: Could the difference be your hand?

TOMA: I asked Ren to tap them too.

REN: I tap branches with my knuckle.

LIRI: How many times?

REN: Until they answer.

PELT: Ren, what is your project?

REN: I built a pulley to lift the fair's display canopy.

PELT: Can you show us?

REN: The canopy is already up.

LIRI: That is helpful.

REN: I wanted to lower it for the demonstration.

SUKI: Please keep the canopy up. The snack table is underneath.

REN: Then I will demonstrate with the small cover.

[A ROPE TIGHTENS. THE PEAR PENDULUM SWINGS.] 

TOMA: That's my experiment.

REN: I used it to test the pulley.

PELT: You have combined two projects.

LIRI: It appears the pear swings at a different rate depending on the rope length.

TOMA: That is a second experiment.

REN: I can write it down.

PELT: Do you have enough measurements?

REN: I counted six swings.

LIRI: The rope was moving.

REN: I can count again.

SUKI: The pear is heading toward the snack table.

TOMA: Catch it!

[REN SNAGS THE PEAR WITH THE PULLEY ROPE.] 

REN: It is stable.

PELT: The pulley works.

LIRI: The canopy is still up.

SUKI: The snack table is safe.

PELT: I award the ribbon to Toma for careful comparison.

LIRI: I award Ren for making a useful pulley.

SUKI: The judges have tied.

TOMA: Do I get a ribbon?

PELT: You can share it.

REN: The ribbon is too short for two projects.

SUKI: Then we will make a second ribbon from the poster string.

LIRI: What did the students learn?

TOMA: Listen before you guess.

REN: Measure before you claim.

PELT: And keep the canopy over the snacks.

SUKI: The science fair is closed. The pear is back in the fruit bowl, and no one has eaten the control group.

MIRI: The volcano has cooled.
KITO: It is waiting for the judges.
JUDGE ONE: We are ready.
MIRI: It has become shy.
KITO: I can demonstrate the eruption again.
JUDGE TWO: Please do not shake the table.
KITO: The table is part of my experiment.
JUDGE ONE: We have learned something about tables.

[A JUDGE TAPS THE PEAR. IT MAKES A SHARP, JUICY SOUND.]
