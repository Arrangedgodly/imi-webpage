# The Silent Film Needs an Interpreter

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: live arts interpretation that explains every pause and visual gesture until the audience acts on it*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Arlo, a lemur interpreter; Mina, a capuchin pianist; Vex, a macaque film presenter; Suri, a gibbon audience member.

INT. TOWN CINEMA - NIGHT

[A BLACK-AND-WHITE FILM PLAYS ON A SMALL SCREEN. A CARD READS "THE LOST HAT." ARLO STANDS BESIDE THE SCREEN WITH A MICROPHONE.]

VEX: Tonight's film has no dialogue. Arlo will interpret its visual language.

ARLO: The character looks left. This may mean he expects someone, or perhaps he dislikes the right side of the room.

SURI: He dropped his hat.

ARLO: The hat has separated from its owner.

Mina plays three gentle piano notes. On screen, the character picks up the hat, puts it on, then loses it again when a gust blows it away.

ARLO: The recurring hat suggests a cycle of attachment.

SURI: It suggests the wind is strong.

Vex asks Mina to hold the music while Arlo explains the gust. Mina stops playing. The film continues in silence. Arlo whispers into the microphone.

ARLO: The silence is itself a sound.

SURI: It is very quiet.

The character on screen follows the hat down a hill. Arlo narrates each step. Suri follows his gestures instead of the screen and begins moving down the aisle.

VEX: Please remain seated.

SURI: The hat is at the door.

Arlo says the character's journey represents ambition. Suri reaches the exit and opens it. A real draft lifts the program from Vex's hand.

VEX: We have a second hat.

Suri catches the program. Mina resumes the piano, playing a quick chase rhythm. The on-screen character catches the hat, then steps on it. Suri folds the program into a hat and puts it on Vex's head.

ARLO: The audience has entered the image.

VEX: I am not in the film.

SURI: You are now wearing the hat.

Arlo asks Mina to pause the film so he can clarify whether the character's expression is regret or relief. Mina cannot pause the projector, so she plays the same two notes repeatedly. The audience begins to sway with the loop.

ARLO: We are in a moment of suspended meaning.

SURI: We're waiting for the next scene.

The film reaches its final card: "THE END." Arlo starts explaining that the ending may be ironic. Vex switches on the house lights.

VEX: The film is over.

ARLO: We have not interpreted the card.

Mina stops playing. Suri takes the paper hat off Vex's head and places it on a seat.

SURI: It means the show is over.

Vex asks the audience to applaud the pianist. Everyone claps. Arlo tries to interpret the applause, but his microphone cuts out.

[THE SCREEN GOES DARK. A PAPER HAT BLOWS OFF THE EMPTY SEAT AND LANDS ON ARLO. MINA PLAYS THREE FINAL NOTES; ARLO TAKES A BOW WITHOUT EXPLAINING IT.]
