# The Safety Video Is Too Safe

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace safety training videos, cheerful hazard demonstrations, and presenters who add warnings until nobody can move*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Trainer Rell, a macaque; presenter Omi, an orangutan; worker Tavi, a capuchin; and camera operator Luma, a gibbon.

INT. TRAINING ROOM - DAY

Omi stands beside a ladder. A poster reads SAFETY FIRST, SECOND, AND THIRD.

OMI: In today's safety video, we will show how to use a ladder safely.

RELL: First, inspect the ladder.

TAVI: It has four feet.

RELL: Check that the feet are on a level surface.

Tavi lifts the ladder and places it on the floor. One foot lands on a cable. The ladder wobbles.

RELL: That cable is a trip hazard.

LUMA: The camera cable.

RELL: Move the camera cable.

Luma moves it. The microphone cord now crosses the ladder's path.

OMI: Should we stop filming?

RELL: The video must demonstrate every hazard.

Tavi steps around the cord, places the ladder, and checks the locks. Omi climbs one rung. Rell calls out another warning.

RELL: Do not climb while carrying objects.

Omi hands down a clipboard. Rell tells Tavi not to stand under the ladder. Tavi moves to the side, where she stands directly behind the camera.

LUMA: You are in the shot.

TAVI: I am out of the ladder zone.

RELL: Keep three points of contact.

Omi grips both rails and puts one foot on a rung.

OMI: I have three points.

RELL: Good. Do not overreach.

Omi reaches for a marker on the table. She leans too far. Tavi steadies the ladder. Rell stops the demonstration.

RELL: The ladder should be repositioned, not leaned from.

They move it. A safety cone is placed beside the ladder, but it blocks the door. Rell moves the cone, then warns everyone not to carry a cone while climbing.

Luma asks whether the video can be finished with the simple instruction to ask for help. Rell says the instruction needs a demonstration. Omi climbs down and asks Tavi to hold the ladder. Tavi does. Luma turns off the camera to move the microphone stand.

RELL: You cannot turn off the camera during the training.

LUMA: I am moving the trip hazard.

[Luma secures the microphone stand, switches the camera back on and nods to Rell.] Rell turns to the lens. He delivers a summary: inspect the ladder, secure its feet, keep the path clear, and ask someone to steady it. He tries to add that viewers should not use ladders without watching the video.

TAVI: We are watching the video while using the ladder.

OMI: Then the video is already working.

The crew folds the ladder and clears the cables. Rell says the training is complete. The safety poster falls off the wall; everyone steps away and waits for it to land.

RELL: That was safe.

LUMA: It was also the last take.

The ladder stands neatly in storage. The camera finally captures an empty room.

RELL: Add a warning before this shot.

OMI: Against what?

RELL: Entering the room before watching the video.

LUMA: Then we can leave the room empty forever.

[Rell points at the vacant presenter mark, ready to deliver another warning. Luma switches off the camera.]
