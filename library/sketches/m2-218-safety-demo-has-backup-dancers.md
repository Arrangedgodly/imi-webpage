# The Safety Demonstration Has Backup Dancers

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airline safety demonstrations that bury clear instructions under polished choreography*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Cabin Lead Sima, a gibbon; Choreographer Runi, a macaque; Performer Dax, a capuchin; and Passenger Kori, a lemur.

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN - DAY

The plane is parked. Sima stands in the aisle with a seat belt. Runi gives a silent count. Dax waits beside the overhead bin.

SIMA: We need to show how the belt opens.

RUNI: The music begins on the buckle click.

DAX: The buckle is behind the dancer.

RUNI: Then we need a turn.

KORI: I just need to see how it works.

SIMA: Press the metal tab and lift the clasp.

[RUNI CUES MUSIC. DAX SPINS, THE BELT WHIPS AROUND, AND THE CLASP LANDS IN HIS HAND.]

DAX: There. It opened.

KORI: I saw the spin.

RUNI: The spin represents release.

SIMA: The belt is here for everyone to see.

DAX: I can hold it still.

RUNI: That may be too still for the music.

KORI: The music can be quiet for a moment.

[RUNI STOPS THE MUSIC. THE BUCKLE CLICKS OPEN CLEARLY.]

RUNI: That click is a good cue.

SIMA: The tab releases the belt. The spin is optional.

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN - MOMENTS LATER

Runi places a life vest on the seat. Dax stands on a small platform. Sima points to the pull tab.

SIMA: Pull this tab to inflate the vest after leaving the aircraft.

RUNI: The vest reveal needs a lift.

DAX: Should I inflate it on the ground?

SIMA: No. It can make it harder to move through the aisle.

RUNI: Could the dancers mime an inflation?

[KORI BLOWS A WHISTLE. THE MUSIC STOPS.]

KORI: We are trying to see the tab.

DAX: The tab is right here.

[HE POINTS. RUNI STEPS IN FRONT OF HIM TO HIT A POSE.]

SIMA: Runi, you are blocking the instruction.

RUNI: I'm framing it.

SIMA: Frame the vest, not yourself.

RUNI: I can stand behind the seat.

KORI: Then I can see the vest.

SIMA: Everyone can see now.

INT. AIRPLANE CABIN - FINAL RUN

Sima holds up the belt and vest. Dax points to each part. Runi keeps the music low and stands against the bulkhead.

SIMA: Press the tab to open the belt. The vest inflates when you pull the red tabs outside.

DAX: The whistle means look at the crew.

KORI: That was clear.

RUNI: Could we add one step at the end?

SIMA: One step, after the instruction.

[DAX MAKES A SMALL, CAREFUL TURN. THE BELT CLASPS CLICK IN RHYTHM. Runi taps the beat on the seat back.]

KORI: That dance helped me remember the buckle.

SIMA: Good. Now everyone can sit down.

[RUNI TAKES A SEAT. DAX STARTS TO DANCE AGAIN. THE SEAT BELT CLICKS CLOSED AROUND HIM MID-POSE.]

DAX: The show is secured.
