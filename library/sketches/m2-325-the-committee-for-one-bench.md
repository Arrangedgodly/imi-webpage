# The Committee for One Bench

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: neighborhood committees that hold repeated meetings to avoid a simple shared decision*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Chair Piri, a baboon; clerk Miri, a gibbon; resident Kito, a macaque; gardener Odo, a capuchin.

EXT. PARK - DAY

A bench sits beside a tree. Piri opens a folder.

PIRI: We are here to decide whether the bench should stay by the tree.

KITO: It is already there.

MIRI: We need a motion.

Odo sits. The bench creaks once.

ODO: I move that we test it.

Piri asks for a second. Kito sits beside Odo. The bench creaks again.

PIRI: The motion is supported.

Miri takes minutes. She asks whether the creak counts as public comment. Piri says only if the bench is registered. Kito says the bench has been in the park for years. Piri opens the folder; there is no bench registration.

The committee considers moving the bench to the sunny side. Odo says the tree gives shade. Kito says some people like sun. Miri draws two maps. The bench appears in both locations, which Piri says may confuse the public record.

PIRI: We should form a subcommittee to study the maps.

KITO: Can't we sit in both places and decide?

Piri calls a five-minute recess. Everyone stays seated. Odo notices that the bench is level under the tree. Miri sits on the grass and says she would prefer a backrest. The bench has one. She moves to it.

Piri asks for a vote. Kito votes to keep it. Odo votes to keep it. Miri votes to keep it where it is, as long as it remains available. Piri says the chair should not vote unless there is a tie.

PIRI: There is no tie.

MIRI: The minutes say the chair is undecided.

Piri looks around. He wants to sit down but has avoided the bench because he is chairing the meeting. Kito offers him a spot. Piri sits. The bench creaks once more.

PIRI: The bench has submitted an objection.

ODO: It has held all of us.

Piri admits the bench is useful where it is. He asks Miri to record the decision. Miri says the meeting can close only after the minutes are signed. The pen is in the folder, under the map of the sunny side.

They pass the folder around without standing. Kito signs on his knee. Odo signs on the back of the map. Miri signs beneath them. Piri signs last, then asks whether they should schedule a follow-up meeting to confirm the decision.

Everyone gets up at once. The bench stays still.

Miri reads the signed minutes back to the group. Piri tries to add a footnote about future seating. Odo says they can discuss that when another bench arrives.

The bench creaks.

PIRI: We will put that in the minutes.
