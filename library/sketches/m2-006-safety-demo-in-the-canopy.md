# Safety Demo in the Canopy

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Airline safety demonstrations and overly cheerful travel briefings*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Flight attendant Koa, a macaque; pilot Fern, a gibbon; passenger Miri, a capuchin; safety trainer, a mandrill.

[STAGE: THE CABIN OF A VINE TRAM. FOUR SEATS, A WINDOW, AND A DEMONSTRATION BRANCH.] 

KOA: Welcome aboard the Canopy Flyer. Please stow your tail inside the seat belt.

MIRI: Mine is longer than the seat belt.

KOA: Then please fasten the belt around your waist and keep your tail clear of the aisle.

TRAINER: Demonstrate the brace position.

KOA: Place your hands on the seat in front of you.

MIRI: There is no seat in front of me.

KOA: Place your hands on the branch in front of you.

MIRI: That's a passenger's head.

TRAINER: We will need another volunteer.

FERN: I can sit in the front row.

KOA: The pilot should not be in the demonstration.

FERN: I have finished pre-flight checks.

KOA: Then please remain in the cockpit.

FERN: It is behind the curtain.

MIRI: The curtain is a shower curtain.

KOA: It separates the cockpit from the cabin.

MIRI: It has ducks on it.

TRAINER: Ducks are calming.

[KOA PULLS DOWN A LEAF-SHAPED OXYGEN MASK.] 

KOA: If the cabin loses pressure, masks will drop from the overhead vines.

MIRI: How do I put it on?

KOA: Place it over your nose and mouth.

MIRI: It smells like leaf mold.

KOA: That is the oxygen scent.

TRAINER: There is no oxygen scent.

KOA: Then that is the branch scent.

MIRI: Do I put it on before helping my hatchling?

KOA: Yes. Secure yours first, then assist the small passenger.

FERN: The tram has no hatchlings aboard.

KOA: A passenger may be carrying a small passenger.

MIRI: My backpack has a sleepy gecko.

TRAINER: The gecko is not a passenger.

MIRI: It has a ticket.

KOA: Please help the gecko after your own mask is secure.

[A BELL DINGS. THE TRAM JERKS FORWARD A FEW INCHES.] 

MIRI: We are moving.

FERN: We have not left the platform.

KOA: That was a practice jolt.

TRAINER: We did not schedule a practice jolt.

FERN: The brake is loose.

KOA: Everyone stay seated.

MIRI: My tail is in the aisle.

KOA: Fold it under your seat.

MIRI: It is holding the emergency lever.

TRAINER: Do not move the lever.

MIRI: I am not. The tram is.

FERN: I can secure the brake with this spare vine.

KOA: Is it rated for tram use?

FERN: It is rated for my laundry.

TRAINER: Use the safety rope from the kit.

FERN: Good. The tram is secure.

MIRI: The gecko is awake.

KOA: Please remain seated until the platform crew opens the doors.

TRAINER: The safety demonstration is complete.

KOA: We will repeat the brace position after the branch is inspected.

MIRI: I feel safer already.

KOA: That is the branch scent.

[THE DUCK CURTAIN FALLS. A REAL DUCK QUACKS OUTSIDE.]
