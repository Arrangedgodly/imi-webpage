# The Guide Audio Is a Tour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio guides, numbered stops, hushed narration, and visitors who follow the recording past the actual exhibit*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Visitor Miri, a macaque; guide voice Nala, a gibbon; curator Omi, an orangutan; and guard Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. SMALL MUSEUM - DAY

Miri picks up an audio guide. Its screen shows STOP 1. Omi explains that the museum has moved the displays while the floor is repaired.

OMI: The guide may not match the new layout.

MIRI: It says stop one is a carved bowl.

OMI: The bowl is now by the window.

NALA, THE GUIDE: Stop one. The bowl rests in the center of the gallery.

Miri looks at an empty pedestal.

MIRI: The guide says it is here.

TAVI: The bowl is by the window.

NALA: Please proceed to the next gallery.

Miri walks the wrong direction and enters the coat room. The audio guide beeps.

NALA: Stop two. The painting depicts a long journey.

MIRI: I am looking at three coats.

TAVI: One of them has been on a long journey.

Omi rushes over and directs her back to the gallery. The guide's map rotates with Miri, but it has not been updated for the move.

NALA: To your left, observe the carved bowl.

MIRI: My left or the recording's left?

Omi takes a walkie-talkie and calls the staff desk. They decide to use the guide as a treasure hunt. Tavi moves labels to match the recording while Omi watches visitors approach.

Miri reaches the window. The bowl is there, but the guide still says it is in the center.

NALA: Notice the bowl's curved rim.

MIRI: I notice the window behind it.

TAVI: The window was not part of the exhibit.

Omi asks Miri to help place a temporary sign. She writes "Bowl moved to window." The guide announces an update.

NALA: Stop one has moved to the window.

MIRI: How did it know?

OMI: Tavi recorded the new line.

TAVI: I stood next to the bowl and read the sign aloud.

Miri continues to stop two. The painting has been moved behind a folding screen. The guide tells her to turn left, but she bumps the screen. The painting wobbles.

TAVI: Please do not follow the guide through the screen.

NALA: Stop two is now behind the screen.

OMI: That is less helpful.

The museum staff move the painting to a clear wall and update the guide. Miri returns the device. The guide says, "Thank you for completing the tour." A second visitor picks it up and hears the old first instruction.

MIRI: The museum needs a new recording.

OMI: We will record after the displays stay put.

TAVI: That could take a while.

NALA: Please wait for the next update.

The second visitor walks toward the coat room. Omi follows with the map upside down, while Tavi turns it around without stopping.
