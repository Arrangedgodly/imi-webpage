# The HOA Will Not Approve That Branch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: homeowners association meetings and restrictive covenants applied to a living canopy*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Chair Varo, a baboon; resident Kiri, a macaque; arborist Lumo, a gibbon; and neighbor Fenn, a capuchin.

[A COMMUNITY MEETING HAS A MODEL TREE ON THE TABLE. KIRI HOLDS A SMALL LADDER.]

VARO: We are here to review Kiri's proposed branch extension.

KIRI: It is a branch that already grew.

VARO: It extends over the property line.

LUMO: The tree is shared.

VARO: The covenant defines shared as visible from the path.

FENN: I can see it.

VARO: That makes it visible.

[KIRI SETS THE LADDER AGAINST THE TREE OUTSIDE THE WINDOW.]

VARO: The ladder is not approved.

KIRI: I need to pick fruit.

VARO: Please submit a ladder application.

FENN: How long does that take?

VARO: Ten business days.

LUMO: The fruit will ripen in two days.

VARO: Then it may be a temporary fruit.

[KIRI CLIMBS THE LADDER. VARO BLOWS A WHISTLE.]

VARO: Work has begun without approval.

KIRI: The branch is already here.

LUMO: It is healthy. We can trim the part over the path.

VARO: There is no approved pruning schedule.

FENN: There is a branch hanging over the meeting table.

[THE MODEL TREE'S BRANCH DROOPS AND KNOCKS OVER THE MINUTES.]

VARO: We will add a pruning discussion to next month's agenda.

[KIRI PICKS UP THE MINUTES. A FIG HAS LANDED ON THEM.]

KIRI: The tree has submitted a comment.

VARO: Written comments must be filed in triplicate.

[LUMO CUTS THE OVERHANGING TWIG. VARO MEASURES IT.]

VARO: The cut is within the allowed length.

KIRI: Can I pick the fruit now?

VARO: The fruit is not covered by the ladder clause.

[FENN TAKES A FIG FROM THE BRANCH AND SETS IT ON THE TABLE.]

FENN: Then I will apply for the fruit.

VARO: There is no fruit form.

KIRI: Great. The fruit is approved.

[VARO SIGNS THE MINUTES. THE DROOPING BRANCH TAPS HIS PEN.]

VARO: I am recording this as an incidental contact.

LUMO: The tree has voted to adjourn.

[LUMO PUTS A PAPER TAG ON THE BRANCH.]

LUMO: This identifies the limb we will trim.

VARO: It needs an application number.

KIRI: The tree has not applied.

FENN: It grows without paperwork.

[VARO CHECKS THE COVENANT.]

VARO: The tree is listed as a shared structure.

LUMO: Then the shared structure may be maintained.

VARO: Maintenance requests need two signatures.

[KIRI AND FENN SIGN. VARO ADDS HIS NAME AS WITNESS.]

VARO: The branch can be trimmed.

[KIRI CUTS A SMALL TWIG. IT FALLS ON THE FORM.]

FENN: The application has been completed.

VARO: The twig is evidence of work.

KIRI: The twig is also a handle for the fruit basket.

[VARO STARTS TO WRITE A NEW FORM.]

LUMO: No form is needed for a twig.

VARO: I will make a note.

[THE TWIG IS CARRIED TO THE TREE. THE FIGS ARE PICKED.]

[EVERYONE LEAVES. VARO STAYS TO FILL OUT A FORM FOR THE TWIG.]
