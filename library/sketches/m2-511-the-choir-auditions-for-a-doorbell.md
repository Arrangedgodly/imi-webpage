# The Choir Auditions for a Doorbell

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive choir auditions, perfect pitch, and performers who treat household sounds as concert repertoire*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: CHOIR DIRECTOR, a gibbon; SINGER, a baboon; PIANIST, an orangutan; HOST, a macaque; NEIGHBOR, a capuchin.

[A small stage with a doorbell on a stand. The host holds a clipboard. A pianist waits at an upright piano.]

HOST: Welcome to the neighborhood's first doorbell choir audition.

CHOIR DIRECTOR: We are looking for tone, timing, and a clear signal visitors can hear.

SINGER: I have prepared a five-minute medley.

CHOIR DIRECTOR: The doorbell has two notes.

[The singer gives an operatic trill into a handheld microphone. The unconnected bell stays still on the stand.]

PIANIST: The bell has not joined in.

SINGER: It appreciates my range.

HOST: Please audition the doorbell, not your own voice.

[The host reaches toward the button, then pauses for the official audition.]

NEIGHBOR (from the porch): I can hear the warm-up from out here.

ACT ONE

[The choir director auditions a second bell. The host presses its button. It rings three times, then gives a tired buzz.]

HOST: It has personality.

CHOIR DIRECTOR: It has a loose battery.

[The pianist plays along with the buzz. The singer attempts to conduct and points the pianist toward the ceiling.]

SINGER: We are building to a modulation.

PIANIST: The bell is still buzzing in the same key.

[The neighbor enters carrying a box. A small bell rings inside.]

NEIGHBOR: This is an old handbell. It rattles in the box whenever I carry it.

[The host marks a score. The choir director asks for a clean take. The neighbor sets the box down. Silence.]

HOST: We have lost our soloist.

NEIGHBOR: It only rings when I carry it.

[The pianist rocks the box gently. The handbell rattles. The singer harmonizes, then the host catches the box before its loose wheel carries it off the table.]

CHOIR DIRECTOR: Stop the movement.

[The neighbor checks the loose wheel and removes it.]

NEIGHBOR: The box needs its wheel repaired before anyone rolls it again.

HOST: We will keep it off the stage.

[The neighbor carries the old handbell box back to the porch.]

ACT TWO

[The host plugs the original button bell into its power supply and presses it once. It plays its clean pair of notes. The pianist follows with the same two notes, then stops. The director writes the result on the audition card.]

CHOIR DIRECTOR: The connected button bell wins. Two notes, one pause, and no extra solo.

[The neighbor nods from the porch. The host leaves the button bell on its stand.]

NEIGHBOR: I heard the winner from outside.

SINGER: I could add a coda.

CHOIR DIRECTOR: The result is final.

[The host signs the card and places it beside the winning bell. The pianist closes the music book without adding a coda.]
FINAL IMAGE: The two-note button bell stands alone in the spotlight as the singer lowers the microphone.
