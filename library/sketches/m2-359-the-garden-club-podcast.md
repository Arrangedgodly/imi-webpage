# The Garden Club Podcast

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: niche podcasts that create elaborate segments for an ordinary shared activity*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: host Nara, a gibbon; cohost Odo, a baboon; guest Piri, a capuchin; sound editor Kito, a macaque.

INT. SHED STUDIO - DAY

Nara speaks into a microphone.

NARA: Welcome to The Garden Club Podcast. Today we discuss watering.

ODO: Our first segment is "The Pour."

Piri says she waters the seedlings when the soil feels dry. Odo asks if she checks the moisture by finger or by instrument. She says she uses a finger. Kito plays a suspenseful sound.

PIRI: It is damp.

Nara asks Piri to describe the sensation. Piri says the top layer is cool. Odo asks whether the moisture extends deeper. Piri checks; it does.

Kito asks if they should add a sound effect for roots. Nara says roots do not make noise. Kito plays a low hum anyway. Piri says it sounds like a refrigerator. Odo calls it "the hidden life beneath the bed."

Piri lifts a seedling tray. The soil is damp under the first row and dry under the far corner. She waters only the dry corner. Odo asks if this can be a recurring segment. Piri says she will check again tomorrow.

Nara asks what listeners should remember. Piri says not to water on a schedule if the soil is already wet. Odo says that is practical. Kito says it is difficult to make a dramatic sting for practical advice. Nara offers to play a single drop sound.

Piri waters the dry corner. One drop falls from the can onto the soil. Kito records it.

Nara asks Piri to explain the watering decision for a short clip. Piri points to the dry corner, touches the soil, and pours only where needed. Odo asks if the podcast can name the moisture test. Piri says it is "checking the soil." Kito wants a sound cue for the result. Nara suggests a bell when water is needed, but Piri says they can see the leaves. Odo looks at the tray; one plant has drooped slightly. Piri adds a little water around its roots. The plant lifts after a moment. Odo asks if the episode can include a before-and-after photo. Kito takes one and sets the camera aside. The guest has finished watering, and the hosts have learned to stop recording.

Nara closes the episode with the microphone off. The plants remain in their tray, and the hosts put the watering can away.

Odo asks if the next episode can cover picking tomatoes. Piri says only when they are ripe. Kito removes the extra sound effects from the edit.

The garden stays quiet.

Kito smiles.
