# The Bicycle Bell Has a Personal Assistant

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: premium product launches that hire a presenter to explain a basic feature*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Presenter Vex, a macaque; mechanic Nari, a lemur; rider Pip, a capuchin; guest Mara, a gibbon.

INT. BIKE SHOP - DAY

[A bicycle stands on a small stage. A microphone hangs beside its handlebars. Vex gestures toward the bell.]

VEX: This is the new companion bell.

MARA: It rings.

VEX: It communicates.

NARI: It rings.

[Pip squeezes the bell. It gives a bright ding.]

VEX: A greeting.

[Pip squeezes twice.]

VEX: A follow-up.

MARA: Someone is walking into the display.

[Nari moves Mara aside. Vex continues.]

VEX: The companion bell comes with a trained assistant.

PIP: What does the assistant do?

VEX: Explains the bell.

NARI: So, you.

[Vex puts on a headset. The bell rings once by itself as the handlebar shifts. He bows.]

VEX: It has chosen its moment.

[Nari checks the mount. The spring is loose. She tightens it. The bell rings again. Vex bows again.]

MARA: Does the assistant bow every time?

VEX: Only for premium members.

[Pip tries to ride the bicycle through the shop. Each bump in the floor makes the bell ring. Vex follows, bowing at every sound.]

PIP: It's ringing at the doorway.

VEX: It is introducing you to the street.

[Outside, a pigeon startles and flies onto Vex's headset. Vex stops. The bell rings as the wheel passes over a crack.]

VEX: A welcome from our feathered partners.

NARI: The bird landed on you.

[Pip rides back inside. The bell rings with each turn. Vex spins after him, greeting the shelves. Mara taps the bell's clapper so it rings continuously. Vex continues bowing until he is dizzy and sits on a display tire.]

VEX: The feature has reached the end of its cycle.

NARI: I can fix the spring.

[Vex removes his headset. Nari loosens the mount one turn. The bell stops.]

MARA: It is quiet now.

PIP: I miss the assistant.

[Vex puts his headset back on. Nari nudges the bicycle. The bell rings once. Vex bows to it.]

MARA: Does the assistant come with the bicycle?

VEX: For the first ride.

PIP: What about the second ride?

VEX: You subscribe.

[Nari hands Mara a receipt. It lists one bicycle, one bell, and three assistant greetings.]

MARA: I got four greetings.

VEX: The bell rang four times.

NARI: One was the test.

[Pip rides the bicycle through the doorway. The bell rings as he crosses the threshold. Vex rushes after him, bowing to the sidewalk. A dog barks. Vex bows to the dog too.]

PIP: Does the assistant greet dogs?

VEX: Only if they have a membership.

[The bicycle rolls back. The bell rings again. Vex bows, then notices the headset has come unplugged.]

VEX: The bell did that without me.

NARI: It has learned to work alone.

FINAL BEAT: Mara rings the bell with one finger. Vex stands and bows. Pip asks if the bike is for sale. Nari hands him the receipt, which includes a separate line for "assistant greetings."
