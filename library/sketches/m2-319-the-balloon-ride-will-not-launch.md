# The Balloon Ride Will Not Launch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: adventure-tour ads, balloon launch ceremonies, and instructors who describe waiting for safe wind as a scenic feature*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Pilot Rell, a gibbon; guide Palla, an orangutan; guests Tavi, a macaque, and Neri, a capuchin; and ground crew Vex, a ring-tailed lemur.

EXT. BALLOON FIELD - DAWN

A colorful hot-air balloon lies on the grass. Palla speaks into a camera.

PALLA: Today, our guests will float above the valley at sunrise.

VEX: The wind is moving toward the trees.

RELL: We will wait for it to turn.

TAVI: How long will that take?

RELL: The forecast says the breeze may shift after sunrise.

Neri looks at the balloon basket. A brochure says LIFT OFF AT DAWN.

NERI: Dawn is happening now.

PALLA: The experience begins with preparation.

The crew inflates the balloon. It lifts slightly, then tips sideways. Vex holds the guide rope. Rell asks everyone to step back. The balloon settles again.

RELL: The balloon is testing the ground.

VEX: The wind is testing the balloon.

Palla tries to film a dramatic countdown. She reaches "three," then pauses as the fabric leans toward the trees.

PALLA: We will resume the countdown when conditions are safe.

TAVI: Is the basket part of the ride?

RELL: Yes.

TAVI: Does it move?

RELL: When the balloon moves.

Neri asks whether they can still see the valley from the ground. Palla says they can. The guests climb a small hill and look at the sunrise. The balloon remains in place.

VEX: We should not launch in this wind.

PALLA: The tour is called Sunrise Flight.

RELL: It is safer to cancel today's flight.

PALLA: We can offer a refund or reschedule.

TAVI: I would like the refund.

NERI: I would like to come back on a calm day.

Palla turns to the camera and says the ride is postponed for safety. The wind catches the brochure and sends it across the field. Neri catches it. The launch crew deflates the balloon and folds the fabric.

PALLA: Our guests have enjoyed an aerial view.

TAVI: We stayed on the hill.

PALLA: The hill is elevated.

Rell offers to show them the basket and explain the controls. The guests climb in while the balloon remains tethered to the ground. Vex holds the rope. The basket rises an inch as a gust passes.

TAVI: We are flying.

RELL: We are floating.

NERI: We are still tied down.

The balloon settles. Everyone steps out. The crew returns the brochure to the stand and updates the launch time to "when conditions allow."

Rell shows the guests how to check the wind sock and inspect the basket before a flight. The sock points toward the trees. Tavi says she is glad they waited. Palla folds the brochure so the new launch time is visible.

The wind sock stays pointed away from the balloon.
