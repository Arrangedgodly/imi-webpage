# The Announcement Is for Everyone

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public announcement systems and escalating specificity about simple shared spaces*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: ANNOUNCER PEL, a lemur; FACILITIES LEAD VEE, a macaque; VISITOR RO, a gibbon; and a public-address speaker.

[A hall with a small sign reading: PLEASE KEEP THE PATH CLEAR. Pel stands at a microphone.] 

PEL: Attention, visitors. Please keep the path clear.

VEE: Which path?

PEL: The main path.

RO: Is the side path included?

PEL: Please keep both paths clear.

VEE: The announcement should specify where to put the baskets.

PEL: Please keep the paths clear by putting baskets on the shelf.

RO: The shelf is full.

VEE: Then we need to clear the shelf.

[THE SPEAKER SQUEALS. EVERYONE COVERS THEIR EARS.] 

PEL: The announcement was too loud.

VEE: We can lower the volume.

[PEL TURNS THE KNOB. THE SPEAKER BECOMES A WHISPER.] 

PEL: Please keep the paths clear.

RO: I cannot hear you.

PEL: That is because the speaker is quiet.

VEE: We should use a sign instead.

RO: The sign is behind the baskets.

[VEE MOVES ONE BASKET. A SECOND SIGN IS UNDER IT.] 

VEE: This one says "Use the shelf."

PEL: The shelf is full.

VEE: We can put the baskets along the wall until the shelf is clear.

RO: That blocks the wall path.

PEL: Then we need another path.

VEE: No. We need fewer baskets in the hall.

RO: These are empty. I can carry them to storage.

[RO STACKS THREE BASKETS. THE TOP ONE WOBBLES.] 

PEL: Please carry no more than you can hold safely.

RO: I can carry two.

[RO SETS ONE BASKET DOWN. IT ROLLS INTO THE WALL AND STOPS.] 

VEE: The path is clear now.

PEL: I will announce that.

RO: No need. We can see it.

PEL: The announcement system needs a closing statement.

VEE: Turn it off.

PEL: The visitor at the door cannot hear the speaker. They can hear us directly.

RO: We can just tell them the path is clear.

VEE: If we tell one person, the announcement system has no record.

PEL: The announcement system is turned down too low.

VEE: I can write a note and put it beside the door.

[PEL WRITES A NOTE. THE PAPER SLIPS OFF THE WALL.] 

RO: The tape is old.

VEE: We can use a tack.

PEL: The tack has fallen out.

VEE: The wall is soft.

RO: We could place the note on the shelf.

VEE: The shelf has baskets.

PEL: The baskets are in storage now.

[THE NOTE IS SET ON THE EMPTY SHELF.] 

RO: Now people can read it without a speaker.

VEE: It says, "Path clear. Use the shelf for baskets."

PEL: We should specify that the shelf has a limited capacity.

RO: The shelf is right there. People can see whether it is full.

VEE: The announcement system is silent.

PEL: I will announce that the system is off.

RO: Do not turn it back on to tell us.

[PEL REACHES FOR THE SWITCH, THEN STOPS.] 

PEL: I can tell you quietly.

VEE: That is how people have shared information for a long time.

[PEL SWITCHES OFF THE SPEAKER. THE HALL IS QUIET.] 

RO: That is the clearest announcement yet.

PEL: The microphone is still on.

VEE: Then turn that off too.
