# The Emergency Exit Is in the Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: theater productions that schedule elaborate safety rehearsals and mistake the performance for the drill*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Della, a gibbon stage manager; Puck, a capuchin actor; Mora, a macaque safety officer; Tesh, a lemur usher.

INT. SMALL THEATER - EVENING

[DELLA HOLDS A CLIPBOARD. A SIGN ABOVE THE DOOR SAYS EXIT. ANOTHER SIGN BESIDE IT SAYS REHEARSAL EXIT, NOT PERFORMANCE EXIT.]

DELLA: This is the emergency exit rehearsal. We will walk calmly to the door when Mora gives the signal.

PUCK: Do I leave the stage?

MORA: Only during the drill.

PUCK: My character is waiting for his cue.

DELLA: Your cue is after the drill.

Tesh stands at the door holding a stopwatch. Mora gives a whistle. Puck runs toward the exit, shouting his line.

PUCK: I have discovered the door!

MORA: That is not the signal.

DELLA: Stop. The rehearsal has become the show.

Puck freezes in a dramatic pose. The audience seats are empty, but Tesh claps anyway.

TESH: The door discovery was convincing.

MORA: We are measuring evacuation time, not acting.

Puck tries again. Mora points toward the exit. He walks at a measured pace, turns sideways, then bows to an imaginary crowd.

DELLA: Why did you bow?

PUCK: The exit sign is above the door. It looked like a balcony.

Mora resets the stopwatch. Tesh tries to open the door, but a prop moon has been leaned against it. Della moves the moon. It rolls across the stage and blocks the aisle.

MORA: This is why we practice.

PUCK: The moon has blocked the route.

DELLA: The moon is not in the performance tonight.

PUCK: It has a speaking role now.

Tesh rolls the prop moon away. The door opens. A gust catches the rehearsal schedule and pins it over the exit sign.

TESH: The sign is covered.

MORA: We need another exit marker.

Della takes a bright red sash from Puck's costume and ties it around the door handle. Puck sees it and starts a scene about a trapped prince.

PUCK: At last, my signal.

MORA: That's a sash.

PUCK: My character doesn't know that.

Mora tells Puck to exit without a speech. Puck takes one step and waits. Della checks the clock. Tesh quietly removes the sash and lifts the schedule. The EXIT sign is visible again.

DELLA: We can finish the drill with a plain walk.

Puck walks straight through the door. Tesh stops the watch. Mora checks the time.

MORA: Four seconds.

PUCK: Did I make my entrance?

DELLA: You made your exit.

Puck peers back into the theater. The audience remains empty. He comes back in through the door.

MORA: You don't need to re-enter.

PUCK: My character has unfinished business.

Tesh gives him the clipboard. Puck takes it, reads the rehearsal notes, and walks back out without speaking.

[DELLA CHECKS THE EXIT SIGN, THE CLEAR AISLE, AND THE EMPTY DOORWAY. MORA STOPS THE WATCH. PUCK'S VOICE COMES FROM OUTSIDE.]

PUCK: Is this where I wait for my cue?

DELLA: Only if the play begins out there.
