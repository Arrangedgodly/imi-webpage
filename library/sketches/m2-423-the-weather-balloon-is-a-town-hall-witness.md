# The Weather Balloon Is a Town Hall Witness

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public hearings that summon evidence without checking where it is attached*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Chair Nari, a lemur; balloon keeper Pip, a capuchin; councilor Mara, a macaque; resident Vex, a gibbon.

INT. TOWN HALL - EVENING

[A weather balloon bobs outside a high window. Its tether runs through the open door and is tied to a table leg. Nari taps a gavel.]

NARI: We are hearing complaints about the wind.

MARA: My garden chairs blew into the street.

VEX: The wind took my laundry.

PIP: The balloon measured a gust at noon.

NARI: Can it testify?

PIP: It is outside.

[Mara calls for the balloon to be brought in. Pip unties the rope from the table. The balloon rises. Its tether snaps taut around the gavel.]

NARI: The witness is pulling the chair.

VEX: The wind is in the room.

[The balloon drags the table a foot. Nari grips the gavel. Mara grabs the tablecloth. Pip holds the rope.]

PIP: I can bring it down.

NARI: Do that before the minutes leave.

[Vex opens the window. The balloon bobs against the frame, pulling the tablecloth through the doorway. A stack of papers slides toward the exit. Mara catches it.]

MARA: It has no statement.

VEX: It is making a point.

[Pip loosens the tether. The balloon settles against the ceiling. The knot around the table leg catches on a chair. Nari tries to resume the hearing.]

NARI: The next speaker is the gardener.

[The balloon drifts over the microphone and blocks it. Pip moves the chair; the tether pulls the microphone stand over.]

PIP: The witness is obstructing the hearing.

MARA: It is a balloon.

VEX: It may be an expert witness.

[Nari orders Pip to close the window. Pip pulls the sash down, but the tether is caught in it. The window closes halfway and holds the balloon outside like a giant bobbing flag.]

NARI: The weather report is adjourned.

[Mara moves her chairs back from the door. Vex retrieves a shirt from the balloon's string.]

VEX: My laundry has returned.

[Nari tries to read the proposed wind ordinance. The tether drifts across the page, lifting one corner. Pip holds it down with the microphone base.]

NARI: We need a vote before the balloon moves again.

MARA: The garden chairs are still outside.

VEX: The ordinance says "secure all loose items."

[Vex ties his shirt to the table leg. The balloon tugs the tether and drags the shirt along the floor.]

PIP: Your laundry is voting against it.

[Nari crosses out "secure" and writes "bring indoors." Mara picks up her chair.]

[The balloon nudges the window. Pip catches the microphone as it tips toward the floor.]

PIP: The balloon has evidence.

FINAL BEAT: Nari writes "wind, confirmed" in the minutes. The balloon taps the window once, and Pip marks it "seconded."
