# The Puppet Show Runs Out of Strings

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: children's theater that layers moral lessons onto a simple puppet story until the puppets revolt*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Writer Mela, a macaque; Director Olan, a gibbon; Puppeteer Bex, a capuchin; and Pip the puppet, a squirrel monkey puppet.

INT. CHILDREN'S THEATER - DAY

A cardboard forest sits behind a small stage. Bex holds Pip above the curtain. Mela reads from a script. Olan watches the rehearsal.

MELA: Pip finds a lost acorn and returns it to the oak tree.

OLAN: What does Pip learn?

MELA: That returning a lost acorn is kind.

OLAN: Could Pip also learn teamwork, patience, and how to ask for help?

BEX: Pip is holding the acorn.

MELA: We can give the badger a line.

OLAN: The badger should ask before helping.

BEX: The badger has no arms.

MELA: It has paws.

PIP: The acorn is still in my hand.

[BEX MOVES THE ACORN FROM PIP TO THE BADGER. THE STRING TANGLES AROUND THE CARDBOARD TREE.]

BEX: Now the acorn is in the forest.

OLAN: Give Pip a friend.

[MELA ADDS A SECOND PUPPET, A BADGER, TO THE SCRIPT.]

PIP: I found an acorn.

BADGER: I have a map.

PIP: The tree is behind us.

INT. CHILDREN'S THEATER - LATER

The script now has a narrator, a song, and a cardboard bridge. Bex's arms are tired.

OLAN: The bridge should represent cooperation.

MELA: The bridge is a plank.

BEX: The plank is slipping.

PIP: I need a break.

OLAN: Pip needs to learn perseverance.

BEX: Bex needs to put Pip down.

[THE PUPPETEER LOWERS PIP. THE PUPPET'S HAND GRABS THE SCRIPT.]

PIP: This story has too many pages.

MELA: That line is not in the script.

BEX: Pip grabbed the script.

OLAN: Could we call that an unscripted moment?

PIP: I grabbed it because my hand was free.

MELA: You have a line about the acorn.

PIP: The acorn is under the stage.

[BEX REACHES UNDER THE STAGE. THE PUPPET'S STRING PULLS THE BADGER DOWN TOO.]

OLAN: The story now has a rescue.

OLAN: It could be the lesson about voice.

PIP: I am the voice.

INT. CHILDREN'S THEATER - PERFORMANCE

A group of children sits before the stage. Mela and Olan watch from the side. Bex lifts Pip and the Badger.

PIP: I found an acorn. I will take it back to the tree.

BADGER: I can walk with you.

[THE CARDBOARD BRIDGE FALLS. PIP POINTS TO A CLEAR PATH AROUND IT.]

PIP: We can go around.

OLAN: That is not the lesson.

BEX: It is what the puppet did.

[THE CHILDREN LAUGH. Bex's strings tangle. Pip drops onto the stage and stands upright against the fallen bridge.]

PIP: The story is finished.

MELA: It is.

OLAN: The audience wants the song.

[THE CHILDREN START THE SONG THEMSELVES. BEX HOLDS THE PUPPETS STILL.]

PIP: They know it already.

[THE CHILDREN SING. OLAN CLOSES THE SCRIPT AND LISTENS.]
