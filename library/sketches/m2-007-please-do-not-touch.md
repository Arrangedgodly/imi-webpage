# Please Do Not Touch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Self-guided museum audio tours and reverent exhibit narration*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Guide Voice, a refined langur; visitor Pavi, a young capuchin; guard Eko, an orangutan; curator Bex, a tamarin.

[STAGE: A GALLERY WITH A ROPE LINE. THREE ROCKS SIT UNDER A SPOTLIGHT. A HEADPHONE SET HANGS BY THE DOOR.] 

GUIDE VOICE: Exhibit one, "Three Stones in Quiet Conversation." Please stand at a respectful distance.

PAVI: How far is respectful?

EKO: Behind the rope.

PAVI: The rope is on the floor.

EKO: It is a very short rope.

GUIDE VOICE: Imagine these stones as three generations speaking across time.

PAVI: They are in a row.

BEX: The artist placed them that way.

PAVI: Did they speak?

BEX: They are stones.

GUIDE VOICE: The second stone leans toward the first, seeking a reply.

PAVI: It is round. It rolls.

EKO: Please do not test that.

PAVI: I am only listening.

GUIDE VOICE: To your left, a small bronze ladder commemorates the museum's first curator.

PAVI: It has a loose rung.

BEX: That is how the artist made it.

PAVI: The rung moves when I breathe on it.

EKO: Please breathe elsewhere.

GUIDE VOICE: The ladder asks us to climb in memory, not in fact.

PAVI: The rope is low enough to step over.

EKO: Please do not step over the rope.

PAVI: I'm still behind it.

EKO: Your foot is not.

[A SMALL BELL DINGS. THE AUDIO GUIDE SKIPS A TRACK.] 

GUIDE VOICE: Exhibit four, "The Visitor's Foot."

PAVI: That's my foot.

BEX: The motion sensor started the next recording.

EKO: You are part of the exhibit now.

PAVI: Do I need to stand still?

GUIDE VOICE: The foot represents a journey interrupted by a boundary.

PAVI: My journey was to the snack room.

BEX: That is also behind a boundary.

EKO: We can move the rope so visitors can see the ladder without stepping into the stones.

PAVI: Can you move it two feet left?

EKO: I can move it one foot. The other foot belongs to me.

BEX: The artist asked for a low boundary, not a trip hazard.

GUIDE VOICE: The rope invites us to consider distance.

PAVI: It currently invites me to fall.

BEX: We'll replace it with a waist-high cord.

EKO: And I will stand by the stones until it arrives.

PAVI: Can I ask the stones a question?

GUIDE VOICE: The stones are silent.

PAVI: Then I will ask the curator.

BEX: Ask away.

PAVI: Why is the bronze ladder on the floor?

BEX: The artist wanted it at eye level for mice.

PAVI: We could put it on a low plinth.

EKO: Then the loose rung would be easier to see.

GUIDE VOICE: The gallery has reached a new interpretation.

BEX: We have reached a practical change.

PAVI: I can see all three stones now.

EKO: And the snack room?

PAVI: I can hear it.

GUIDE VOICE: The tour ends at the doorway, where the visitor may choose a path.

PAVI: I choose lunch.

EKO: Please keep your hands off the exhibit.

PAVI: My hands are holding the headphones.

[THE ROPE IS LIFTED. THE AUDIO GUIDE PLAYS A TINY CHIME.]
