# The Training Video Needs a Moderator

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace training videos that pause for audience participation at the least useful moments*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Kiri, a gibbon trainer; Odo, a capuchin trainee; Mel, a macaque video operator; Zuna, a lemur supervisor.

INT. TRAINING ROOM - MORNING

[A PROJECTOR SHOWS A VIDEO TITLE: HOW TO CARRY A BOX. KIRI SITS BESIDE THE SCREEN WITH A PAUSE REMOTE.]

KIRI: Please watch the video and answer when it asks a question.

The video shows a monkey holding a small box. It asks, "What should the worker do next?"

ODO: Put it down?

Kiri pauses the video. Mel says the answer should be "check the path."

KIRI: The path is on the screen.

ODO: The box is blocking it.

Kiri unpauses. The video worker walks forward and trips over a cone. The screen asks, "What caused the fall?"

Odo says the cone. Kiri pauses again.

KIRI: We should discuss the surrounding conditions.

Zuna enters carrying a stack of boxes.

ZUNA: The video is taking longer than the actual job.

KIRI: It has three knowledge checks.

The next scene shows the worker lifting a box. The video asks, "Is the load balanced?" Odo says no. The box on screen tips. The video freezes before it falls.

MEL: The next section is only available after a correct answer.

ODO: How can I answer if it won't show me what happens?

Kiri tells him to say "yes." The video plays. The box falls onto the worker's foot. The screen asks, "Was the load balanced?"

ODO: It said yes.

MEL: The answer key says "no."

KIRI: We should restart the section.

Zuna puts the real boxes on the floor. The video asks what the worker should do with a box that is too heavy. Odo watches the screen. Zuna lifts the box and takes two steps.

ODO: Put it down.

ZUNA: That's what I did.

Kiri pauses the video. The screen says, "Do not pause during the practical example." Mel tries the remote. It is stuck, and each press advances the scene instead. The worker in the video now carries three boxes.

KIRI: That's unsafe.

ODO: We are only watching.

The video asks, "Which item should the worker use to clear the path?" Three choices appear: broom, shovel, and another box. Odo chooses the broom. The video shows the worker using a shovel.

ODO: It marked my answer wrong.

MEL: It is the new version.

Kiri checks the manual. It says the video is interactive but the answers are not updated.

Zuna places a broom beside the real boxes and sweeps the path clear. Odo picks up the smallest box and carries it across.

KIRI: Correct. The path is clear, the load is light, and the route is open.

The video reaches a final quiz. The worker walks past the cone and asks, "What have you learned?"

ODO: We should stop watching and move the cone.

[ZUNA MOVES THE CONE. THE VIDEO CONTINUES TO ASK QUESTIONS TO AN EMPTY TRAINING ROOM.]
