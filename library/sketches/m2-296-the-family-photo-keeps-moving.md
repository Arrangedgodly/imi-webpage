# The Family Photo Keeps Moving

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: family portrait studios, endless retakes, matching outfits, and photographers who ask everyone to look naturally coordinated*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Photographer Sola, a gibbon; family members Vex, a macaque, and Miri, a capuchin; stylist Nari, an orangutan; and assistant Tuli, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. PORTRAIT STUDIO - DAY

A gray backdrop stands behind a bench. Sola adjusts a light. The family wears matching blue sweaters.

SOLA: Everyone smile naturally.

VEX: We were already smiling.

MIRI: I was chewing.

NARI: The sweater is hiding her collar.

TULI: The collar is hiding her tail.

Nari tries to tuck Miri's tail behind the bench. It springs around the other side and knocks over a flower vase. Tuli catches the vase, but water spills on the bench.

SOLA: Hold that pose.

VEX: We are wet.

SOLA: The camera captures feeling.

She takes a picture. The flash startles everyone. Vex's ears go up, Miri's eyes close, and Tuli's tail forms a question mark.

SOLA: Great. We have surprise.

NARI: We need calm. Let's make the sweaters match better.

She brings three scarves, all different shades of blue. Vex puts his on backwards. Miri's tail loops through the scarf. Tuli lays the third scarf over the bench as a seat cover.

SOLA: Now look at each other.

They turn. Vex looks at Miri. Miri looks at Tuli. Tuli looks at the flower vase.

SOLA: Look at the camera.

The family turns. Tuli's tail pulls the bench a few inches. Everyone slides to one side but stays seated.

VEX: We have formed a slope.

SOLA: Don't move. That is a relaxed pose.

Miri laughs. The camera clicks. This time the picture captures a real smile, Vex leaning to catch Miri, and Tuli holding the vase.

NARI: It is not symmetrical.

TULI: It is balanced.

Sola shows the family the photo. They like it, but Nari asks for one formal version. The group sits again. Tuli wraps her tail around the bench leg. Miri sets the vase on the floor. Vex holds both hands still.

SOLA: On three, smile.

MIRI: One, two, three.

The flash goes off too early. Everyone remains still, waiting for the count. Sola takes another picture by accident. That one shows them patiently waiting.

VEX: That is how we look before the picture.

NARI: It is very honest.

They choose the candid image with the slight tilt. Nari prints it, trims the edges, and offers a blue frame. The family selects an unmatching yellow frame instead.

SOLA: The portrait is ready.

TULI: So are we.

Vex lifts the bench to move it. The flowers are safely on the floor. Sola asks for one last shot of the empty set. Her flash captures only the mismatched frames.

NARI: That might be the family portrait.

SOLA: It is a portrait of the studio.

Miri takes the framed picture and leaves. The family walks out together, their scarves still tangled in one tail.
