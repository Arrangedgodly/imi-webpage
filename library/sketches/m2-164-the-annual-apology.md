# The Annual Apology

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate apology videos that prioritize framing and sincerity lighting over fixing the problem*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Bex, a mandrill executive; Miri, a gibbon communications coach; Pavo, a capuchin camera operator; and Jori, a macaque employee.

INT. TRAINING STUDIO - DAY

Bex sits before a gray backdrop. Miri holds a card that says WE HEAR YOU.

MIRI: Look into the lens and speak plainly.

BEX: We regret the inconvenience caused by the new door.

Jori enters carrying the door's broken handle.

JORI: It fell off when I opened it.

Miri asks Pavo to stop filming. Bex moves the handle out of frame.

MIRI: We need a sincere setting.

PAVO: This is the studio.

MIRI: The backdrop feels cold.

They move to the break room. Bex sits beside a potted plant. The plant's leaf blocks one eye. Pavo shifts the camera. The plant then blocks the WE HEAR YOU card.

BEX: We hear you.

JORI: The door still will not close.

Miri gives Bex a second card: ACTIONS SPEAK. Bex nods and asks Jori to demonstrate the door. Jori pushes it; it swings open and stays open. Bex reaches over and holds it shut with one hand.

BEX: We are taking immediate steps.

MIRI: That looks like a fix.

BEX: It is a temporary arm.

Pavo asks if they can film the actual repair. The maintenance worker arrives with a screwdriver and replaces the loose screws. The door closes. Bex lets go and sits back down beside the plant.

BEX: We have heard your concerns.

JORI: You heard the door hit the wall.

MIRI: We should avoid assigning blame.

BEX: The handle was installed without enough screws.

Jori shows the camera the repaired hinge. Miri tries to keep the shot on Bex's face, but Pavo pans to the closing door.

MIRI: The message is the apology.

PAVO: The door is the result.

Bex reads the closing card: WE WILL DO BETTER.

BEX: We will do better at installing screws.

Miri pauses, then gives a thumbs up. Jori pulls the door shut. It latches. Everyone hears the click.

BEX: Is that on camera?

PAVO: It is the whole video.

Miri removes the cards. Bex holds the door open for Jori to leave. Jori exits, then pushes it closed from the hall. It latches again.

BEX: We should issue an apology for the cards.

MIRI: The cards are reusable.

Miri reads the instruction, then removes a wedge holding the break-room door open. The door stays shut on its own. Bex looks toward the camera for a final statement, but Jori has already taken the repaired handle back to maintenance. Pavo switches off the light. Miri folds the cards into the plant's empty saucer.

Pavo records the quiet latch once more for the closing frame. She turns one over. The back reads: DO NOT USE AS A DOORSTOP.
