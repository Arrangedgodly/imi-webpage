# The Gardening Show Meets a Very Persistent Weed

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: calm gardening programs, expert demonstrations, and hosts who frame every plant as a design choice*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: GARDEN HOST, a baboon; BOTANIST, an orangutan; ASSISTANT, a macaque; CAMERA OPERATOR, a gibbon; NEIGHBOR, a capuchin.

[A garden set. A small weed grows through a crack in a stone path. The host speaks softly to camera.]

GARDEN HOST: Today we are considering an uninvited sprout.

BOTANIST: It is a dandelion.

GARDEN HOST: We prefer "sunburst volunteer."

BOTANIST: You can call it what you like. It is still growing in the path.

[The assistant places a pot beside the crack. The host leans down to admire the weed.] 

GARDEN HOST: It brings a touch of yellow to the walkway.

NEIGHBOR: It is blocking my wheelbarrow.

[The neighbor nudges the wheelbarrow. The weed bends and springs upright again.]

GARDEN HOST: Resilient.

BOTANIST: Persistent. We should move it before it spreads.

ACT ONE

[The camera operator sets a close-up lens. The host puts on gardening gloves and grips the weed near the soil.]

GARDEN HOST: We will relocate our guest to a container.

[The host pulls. The stem comes away, but the root stays in the crack.]

ASSISTANT: The top has moved. The bottom has not.

BOTANIST: The root is deeper. Use the hand fork.

[The botanist passes a fork. The host digs around the crack carefully. The assistant holds the pot. The camera operator follows the tool and loses the host's face.]

GARDEN HOST: A quiet moment with the earth.

CAMERA OPERATOR: I have a close-up of dirt.

[The root comes free. A small clump of soil falls into the pot. The weed flops over the rim.]

GARDEN HOST: Our sunburst has a new home.

[The neighbor rolls the wheelbarrow down the path. Its wheel hits the empty crack and drops into it.] 

NEIGHBOR: Now the path needs repair.

BOTANIST: The weed was not holding the stones together.

[The host uses a small brush to clear the soil from the crack. The assistant sets the pot on a shelf, but the weed springs upright and tips the label.]

ASSISTANT: It has been marked "sunburst volunteer."

BOTANIST: Label it dandelion. That is what it is.

[The host changes the label. A gust blows it off the pot and into the wheelbarrow.]

ACT TWO

[The botanist sets a paver over the repaired crack. The neighbor tests the wheelbarrow over the smooth path. The host turns the potted weed toward the light.]

GARDEN HOST: We have an open path and one relocated plant.

BOTANIST: It may need to be trimmed before it seeds.

[The assistant checks the pot. One white puff has appeared on the flower.]

ASSISTANT: It is ready to seed now.

[The host tries to catch the puff in a paper bag. The camera operator follows it as it floats across the set and lands on the host's hat.]

FINAL IMAGE: The host stands still under the softbox while a dandelion seed rides the brim of the gardening hat.
