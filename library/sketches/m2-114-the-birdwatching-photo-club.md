# The Birdwatching Photo Club

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wildlife photography competitions and intrusive demands on animals*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: CLUB LEADER PEL, a gibbon; PHOTOGRAPHER RIN, a macaque; MEMBER SAI, a capuchin; and a small bird.

[A clearing. Rin aims a camera at a branch. Pel consults a judging sheet.] 

PEL: Today's challenge is to capture a bird looking directly at the camera.

RIN: The bird is facing away.

SAI: It is eating.

PEL: We need eye contact for the portrait category.

RIN: I can wait until it turns.

PEL: The light will change.

SAI: The bird will finish eating when it finishes eating.

PEL: We have only ten minutes.

RIN: I can use the time to adjust focus.

[THE CAMERA CLICKS. THE BIRD FLIES TO ANOTHER BRANCH.] 

PEL: It has moved out of frame.

RIN: The frame can move.

SAI: Let's keep a respectful distance.

PEL: The rules say the image should fill the frame.

RIN: We can use the long lens.

[HE ADJUSTS THE LENS. THE BIRD IS NOW VISIBLE WITHOUT ANYONE MOVING CLOSER.] 

PEL: The bird's eye is still turned away.

SAI: It is watching the path.

RIN: Something may be moving there.

[THE MEMBERS LOOK. A SECOND BIRD HOPS ALONG THE GROUND.] 

PEL: We could follow that one.

SAI: It is looking for seeds. Don't step into its way.

RIN: I can photograph from here.

[THE CAMERA CLICKS. THE SECOND BIRD PICKS UP A SEED AND FLIES OFF.] 

PEL: Did you get the shot?

RIN: I got the bird with the seed.

PEL: It is not looking at the camera.

RIN: It is looking where it is going.

SAI: That is a better portrait.

PEL: The judge has asked for the original photo.

RIN: I have two versions. One is close enough to fill the frame, but I took it before I saw the bird's seed.

SAI: The other shows the bird carrying food to its nest.

PEL: The close frame scored higher on sharpness.

RIN: But it cuts off where the bird is going.

SAI: The wider frame includes the branch and the path below.

PEL: The rule says the bird should fill half the image.

RIN: The bird fills one third. The rest is its route.

PEL: Can we crop the path and keep the bird?

RIN: Then it looks like the seed appeared in its beak.

SAI: The photo should not make the bird look like a prop.

[JUDGE PEL LOOKS THROUGH THE TWO PRINTS.] 

PEL: The wider frame tells me what the bird is doing. I will score it higher.

RIN: That changes the contest rule.

PEL: I am the club leader. I can update next year's rules.

SAI: Add one: do not move closer just to fill the frame.

PEL: And one: the bird chooses where to look.

RIN: I can print the wider image and leave the branch visible.

[THE BIRD RETURNS WITH A SECOND SEED. RIN LOWERS THE CAMERA.] 

PEL: You are not taking the photo?

RIN: I already have one. This one can stay with the bird.

PEL: We need a winner.

RIN: Choose the photo that shows what the bird was doing.

PEL: The entry form asks for a title.

RIN: "Seed for the Road."

SAI: That tells the story.

PEL: The judges accept it. Next week's challenge is a bird on a nest.

SAI: Only from a distance.

PEL: We will add that to the rules.

[THE FIRST BIRD CALLS FROM THE BRANCH. THE CAMERA STAYS DOWN.]
