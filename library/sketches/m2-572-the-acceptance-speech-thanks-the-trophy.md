# The Acceptance Speech Thanks the Trophy

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: awards ceremonies that expand an acceptance speech until the awarded object seems to deserve its own credit*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: WINNER KORA, an orangutan; PRESENTER PIM, a macaque; STAGE MANAGER NEL, a gibbon; TROPHY MAKER RUD, a capuchin.

[An awards stage. Pim presents Kora with a small golden monkey trophy. Nel waits in the wing beside a countdown clock. Rud, invited as the maker, sits in the front row.]

KORA: I could not have done this alone.

PIM: Congratulations.

KORA: I thank my cast, my crew and my patient family.

[Nel shows a card reading THIRTY SECONDS. Kora turns the trophy toward the microphone.]

KORA: And most of all, this little fellow.

ACT ONE

[Kora holds the trophy close to her face. Its tiny painted eyes point toward the audience.]

KORA: He never doubted me.

RUD: I made him last Thursday.

KORA: Yet in that short time, he has stood by me.

PIM: He was in the presentation box until two minutes ago.

[Nel holds up a card reading TWENTY SECONDS. Kora turns the trophy toward the wing as if inviting it to read.]

KORA: I would let him speak, but he has always preferred action.

RUD: The jaw is fixed.

KORA: A reserved character.

[Pim points to the programme listing Kora's actual winning performance. Kora nods and starts to thank her director. Then she notices the little trophy's coat is painted the same green as hers.]

KORA: We dressed alike without consulting each other.

RUD: I was given the production photograph.

PIM: That explains the resemblance.

KORA: He understood the role before meeting me.

[Nel gives the ten-second card to Pim, who holds it level with the trophy. Kora moves the trophy sideways to see his whole card.]

KORA: He has spent his time so generously.

PIM: You have spent your time. We have the next award to present.

[Kora looks at the audience, realizes the applause music is ready, and ends her speech with a plain thank-you. Nel starts the short exit cue. Kora bows and walks toward the wing with the trophy held securely in both hands.]

ACT TWO

[Rud leaves his seat by the side aisle and meets Kora behind the curtain, after Pim has begun announcing the next award. Nel stays at her cue desk.]

RUD: I hope you like the trophy.

KORA: I owe him an enormous debt.

RUD: The organizers paid the invoice.

[Kora notices the maker's name stamped beneath the base. She turns it gently to read it, keeping her fingers clear of the small painted ears.]

KORA: I should have thanked you.

RUD: The name under the base is enough. It is nice to see someone look.

[Kora asks Nel whether she may go back for one sentence. Nel points to the stage, where the next nominee's name is already being read.]

NEL: You can send a note after the show.

[Kora takes a small thank-you card from her bag and writes Rud's name. She gives it to him without a microphone. He reads it, smiles and returns through the side aisle to his seat.]

KORA: He was very quiet too.

NEL: He had a sentence when you gave him room for one.

FINAL IMAGE: Kora sets the trophy beside her bag while Rud places the handwritten card inside his programme.
