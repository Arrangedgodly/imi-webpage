# The Red Carpet Is a Rug

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: award-show arrivals, celebrity interviews, and red-carpet fashion questions asked of people carrying folding chairs*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Mira, a macaque; guest Vex, a gibbon; producer Palla, a capuchin; and stagehand Omi, an orangutan.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

A red rug leads from the entrance to a stage. Palla checks a clipboard. Mira stands beside a microphone.

MIRA: Welcome to the annual neighborhood awards. Our first guest is arriving now.

Vex enters carrying two folding chairs.

MIRA: Who are you wearing?

VEX: A vest.

MIRA: And who designed it?

VEX: I did. It has pockets for chair bolts.

Mira asks Vex to pose. He sets down the chairs and strikes a dramatic stance. The rug bunches under his heel. Omi flattens it with a broom.

MIRA: The carpet has made a statement.

OMI: It is a rug.

Palla whispers that the event's real award, "Most Helpful Neighbor," has gone missing. Mira turns the interview into a mystery segment. Vex says he saw the award near the folding chairs. Omi says the chairs were moved from the stage. Palla says she polished the trophy but did not take it.

The host asks Vex to describe his outfit again. He demonstrates the vest pocket. A bolt falls out and rolls down the red rug. Everyone follows it, and it stops beneath the stage.

VEX: The trophy may be down there.

Omi reaches under the stage and finds the award beside a toolbox. It had been placed there so the table could be repaired. Mira asks whether the award is ready for its close-up.

PALLA: It is a small brass bell.

MIRA: What does it represent?

PALLA: Helpful neighbors.

Vex holds the bell. It rings. The audience claps. Mira asks who will receive it. Palla says the neighbors voted for Omi, who fixed the chairs, and Vex, who carried them.

MIRA: Is that a tie?

PALLA: It is one award for two people.

Omi and Vex share it. Mira asks them to walk the carpet together. They pick up the folding chairs and carry them to the stage. The rug bunches again. This time they roll it up and carry it too.

MIRA: The carpet has left the event.

OMI: It was blocking the aisle.

Palla presents the bell on the stage. The neighbors stand to applaud. Mira asks each person what they are wearing. They all answer with one piece of clothing and a practical reason for it.

MIRA: The evening's best look is a jacket with a pocket for screws.

VEX: That is useful.

Omi rings the bell once. The chairs are set out for the audience, and the rug rests rolled neatly by the wall.

Mira turns to the camera for the closing. The microphone is still on the stage, so Omi rings the bell again. The audience applauds the sound operator.
