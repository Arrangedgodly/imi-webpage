# Tea Service for the Entire Table

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: formal tea ceremonies where every movement must be performed in a prescribed order*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Yara, a macaque tea host; Em, a gibbon guest; Silo, a capuchin server; Pira, a lemur instructor.

INT. TEA ROOM - AFTERNOON

[A LOW TABLE HOLDS ONE TEAPOT, FOUR CUPS, AND A PAPER CARD OF INSTRUCTIONS. PIRA STANDS BEHIND YARA.]

PIRA: First bow to the pot.

Yara bows. Em bows to Yara. Silo bows to the tray, which shifts.

PIRA: The tray has not been introduced.

SILO: I thought it was the host.

YARA: The host is me.

Em lifts the pot. Pira stops her.

PIRA: The handle faces the guest.

EM: Which guest?

There are three guests and one handle. Pira asks them to agree on a receiving order. Yara turns the handle toward Em. Em passes it to Silo, who is serving, not receiving.

SILO: I can carry it.

PIRA: You may carry the tray after the pour.

Yara pours tea into the first cup. The pour is a little short. Pira asks her to complete the circle with the pot. Yara rotates it; the spout drips onto the instruction card.

PIRA: The card is now wet.

EM: Is that part of the ceremony?

PIRA: It is a deviation.

Silo replaces the card with a towel. The towel covers the remaining instructions. Pira reaches to lift it, but Yara asks if they can drink now. Pira says the final bow comes before the first sip.

Yara lifts her cup and bows. Tea sloshes over the rim. Em catches the drop with a saucer. Silo sets down the tray to help. The tray rests on a corner of the towel, which pulls the wet instruction card underneath it.

PIRA: Stop. The sequence has shifted.

YARA: What comes next?

PIRA: We must restore the card.

Silo slides the card from beneath the tray. It is soaked and stuck to the table. Em peels it up. One line remains legible: "Serve the tea while warm."

Yara tests the pot. It is cool. Pira says they should start the ceremony again with fresh water. Silo points at the full cups. The guests have already begun drinking, so there is no tea left to pour. Pira watches them swallow and quietly turns the instruction card over.

PIRA: We can call that the tasting phase.

EM: This is the first useful instruction.

PIRA: The next instruction says to admire the steam.

Yara's tea has stopped steaming. She asks Silo to refill the cups. He pours the rest of the pot evenly. There is enough for everyone, and the cups line up around the table.

Silo sets the pot down with the handle facing the host. Pira tries to stop him, then notices the pot is empty.

PIRA: The ceremony is complete.

EM: We haven't had tea.

Everyone drinks. Yara relaxes. Pira reaches for her cup and bows to it. The cup slides away on the wet card.

[SILO MOVES THE CARD TO A DRY CORNER. THE TEA STAYS ON THE TABLE, AND PIRA BOWS TO THE TOWEL.]
