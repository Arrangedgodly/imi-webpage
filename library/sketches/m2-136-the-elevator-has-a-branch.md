# The Elevator Has a Branch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: office elevator etiquette transplanted to a tree-hollow building with vertical residents*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Lift operator Sumi, a gibbon; commuter Pavo, a macaque; inspector Nori, a baboon; and a small troop.

[AN ELEVATOR DOOR OPENS INSIDE A HOLLOW TREE.]

SUMI: Please let passengers exit before boarding.

[PAVO TRIES TO ENTER. THREE MONKEYS EXIT WITH ACORNS.]

PAVO: I am going up.

SUMI: The elevator goes to the middle branch.

PAVO: I need the top floor.

SUMI: There is no top floor.

[NORI INSPECTS THE BUTTON PANEL. IT HAS BUTTONS FOR ROOT, TRUNK, BRANCH.]

NORI: Who installed a root button?

SUMI: People with heavy bags.

[PAVO PRESSES BRANCH. THE ELEVATOR MOVES ONE FOOT.]

PAVO: Is that the floor?

SUMI: That is the branch.

[THE DOOR OPENS TO A NARROW LIMB.]

NORI: It is not wide enough for a crowd.

[THE MONKEYS ENTER, CARRYING A LONG STICK.]

SUMI: Please keep the stick inside the car.

[THE STICK EXTENDS OUT THE DOOR.]

PAVO: It does not fit.

SUMI: We can take it diagonally.

[THE STICK BLOCKS THE DOOR. THE ELEVATOR BEEPS.]

NORI: Remove the obstruction.

[THE MONKEYS TURN THE STICK. IT FITS, BUT NOW NO ONE CAN TURN AROUND.]

PAVO: We are all facing the same direction.

SUMI: That is the elevator policy.

[THE LIFT MOVES. THE STICK TAPS EACH FLOOR BUTTON.]

NORI: The stick is selecting every stop.

[THE DOOR OPENS AT ROOT. THE MONKEYS EXIT, LEAVING PAVO ON BOARD.]

PAVO: I wanted the branch.

SUMI: We are returning to branch.

[THE ELEVATOR BEEPS. PAVO HOLDS THE BUTTON.]

NORI: Please do not hold the door.

PAVO: I am holding the elevator.


[THE ELEVATOR DOOR CLOSES ON THE LONG STICK. IT BOUNCES BACK OPEN.]

SUMI: The door sensor detected an obstruction.

PAVO: The stick is fully inside.

NORI: Its end is in the doorway.

[THE MONKEYS MOVE THE STICK BACK. THE DOOR CLOSES.]

SUMI: We can proceed.

[THE ELEVATOR RISES. PAVO PRESSES BRANCH AGAIN.]

SUMI: We are already at branch.

PAVO: This is the middle branch. I need the high branch.

NORI: The panel needs another button.

[SUMI ADDS A STICKER ABOVE THE BRANCH BUTTON: "HIGH." THE MONKEY PRESSES IT. THE ELEVATOR RISES ANOTHER FOOT.]

PAVO: There.

SUMI: Please exit one at a time.

[THE MONKEYS WALK UP THE TREE. PAVO STAYS IN THE CAR.]

PAVO: I am on the high branch.

SUMI: You are in the elevator.

[NORI ADDS A SIGN TO THE PANEL: "HIGH BRANCH."]

NORI: The label is temporary.

SUMI: The sticker is also temporary.

[THE LIFT BEEPS. PAVO POINTS TO THE DOOR.]

PAVO: Is this my stop?

SUMI: It is the branch.

[THE DOOR OPENS. PAVO STEPS OUT. THE ELEVATOR STAYS.]
PAVO: I will take the stairs next time.


NORI: The elevator has reached its highest stop.


[THE DOOR CLOSES. THE MONKEYS CLIMB THE STICK TO THE MIDDLE BRANCH.]
