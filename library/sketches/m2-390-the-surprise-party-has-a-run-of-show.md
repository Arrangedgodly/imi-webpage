# The Surprise Party Has a Run of Show

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: over-planned surprise parties where the schedule and staging give away the surprise*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Bera, a macaque planner; Noll, a capuchin guest of honor; Tiko, a lemur friend; Suni, a gibbon decorator.

INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - EVENING

[A BANNER READING "SURPRISE" IS HIDDEN BEHIND A CURTAIN. BERA HOLDS A DETAILED RUN OF SHOW. TIKO AND SUNI CROUCH BEHIND A TABLE.]

BERA: Noll arrives at seven. We say surprise at seven oh five.

TIKO: Why five minutes later?

BERA: To let him hang up his coat.

Suni sets a party hat on the table. It rolls toward the door. She catches it and places it inside a box labeled HAT CUE.

BERA: The hat comes after the first clap.

TIKO: I thought the first clap was our cue to hide.

Bera checks the run of show. It says "hide at six fifty." Tiko looks at the clock. It is six fifty-two. He jumps behind the curtain, taking the banner with him.

Suni grabs one end of the banner. The word SURPRISE appears around the curtain edge.

SUNI: We are exposed.

BERA: That is the banner, not us.

Noll knocks. Everyone ducks. Bera opens the side door to ask who it is. Noll is standing there.

NOLL: It is me. I brought the cake.

BERA: You're early.

NOLL: It is seven.

Bera checks the schedule. The arrival time is written as 7:00, but the surprise time is 7:05. She asks Noll to wait outside while they prepare.

NOLL: Should I come back at seven oh five?

BERA: That would be ideal.

Noll leaves. Suni rushes to hide the cake. The cake is too wide for the box. Tiko offers to cover it with the banner, but the banner says SURPRISE and the cake has a frosting message: "HAPPY BIRTHDAY NOLL."

TIKO: Can we turn it around?

Suni rotates the cake. The message now faces the wall. Bera says that is the correct position until the reveal.

Noll knocks again. Bera checks the clock. It is seven oh five. She signals the group to clap. Tiko claps too soon; Suni is still holding the cake. They clap in staggered rhythm. Bera tries to restart the cue.

NOLL: Is this the surprise?

BERA: Not until the next clap.

NOLL: I heard you say surprise through the wall.

Bera drops the run of show. Noll enters. Suni brings out the cake, but the frosting message is upside down. Tiko takes off the hat box lid, and the hat is stuck inside.

NOLL: You planned every minute.

BERA: We planned the surprise.

NOLL: You also invited me to bring the cake.

TIKO: You were the only one who knew where the bakery was open.

[THE GROUP SINGS. NOLL PUTS ON THE HAT, WHICH STILL HAS THE BOX LID ATTACHED. BERA ADDS "GUEST OF HONOR KNOWS" TO THE RUN OF SHOW.]
