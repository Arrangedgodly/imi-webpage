# The Shuttle Stop Shuffle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: transit announcements and route maps that fail to account for monkeys using branches as an alternate network*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Driver Rolo, a baboon; rider Miri, a capuchin; route planner Sela, a macaque; and announcer.

[A SMALL SHUTTLE WAITS AT A STOP. A SIGN READS "NEXT BUS: 12 MINUTES."]

ANNOUNCER: Please stand behind the line.

[MIRI STANDS ON THE LINE.]

ROLO: Behind it.

[MIRI STEPS BACK. A MONKEY LEAPS OVER THE BUS AND LANDS ON A BRANCH.]

SELA: The route map does not show that connection.

MIRI: It is faster than the shuttle.

ROLO: It does not stop at the marked locations.

[THE MONKEY LEAPS TO THE NEXT TREE.]

ANNOUNCER: Route 2 is now approaching.

[THE SHUTTLE PULLS UP. THE DOOR OPENS. EVERYONE WAITS.]

ROLO: Please board.

MIRI: I need to know if this goes to the fruit stand.

ROLO: It goes to the central path.

SELA: The fruit stand is two stops away.

MIRI: The branch route is one leap.

ROLO: The branch route is not insured.

[THE MONKEY RETURNS, CARRYING A FIG.]

SELA: It has reached the stand.

ROLO: It has no fare.

[THE MONKEY DROPS THE FIG INTO THE FARE BOX.]

ROLO: That is not legal tender.

MIRI: It is a fare.

[THE BUS BEEPS. THE FIG ROLLS UNDER THE DRIVER'S SEAT.]

ANNOUNCER: The fare has been accepted.

ROLO: The fare has been misplaced.

[ROLO OPENS THE DOOR. MIRI BOARDS. THE MONKEY CLIMBS TO THE ROOF.]

ROLO: Passengers must remain inside.

SELA: The roof is part of the vehicle.

ROLO: It is not listed on the route permit.

[THE BUS STARTS. THE MONKEY JUMPS OFF AT THE NEXT TREE.]

ANNOUNCER: Stop requested.

MIRI: I did not request a stop.

SELA: The monkey did.

[ROLO STOPS. MIRI GETS OFF AT THE FRUIT STAND.]

MIRI: That was quick.

ROLO: You took the shuttle.

SELA: And paid with a fig.

[ROLO CHECKS THE FARE BOX. IT IS EMPTY.]

ROLO: We need a new fare system.


[SELA UNFOLDS THE ROUTE MAP ON THE BUS STOP BENCH.]

SELA: We could add a marked branch connection.

ROLO: Branches are not scheduled.

MIRI: The troop uses them every day.

ROLO: How often does the branch arrive?

[THE MONKEY JUMPS BACK TO THE STOP AND HOLDS UP ANOTHER FIG.]

SELA: It has returned for another fare.

ROLO: We cannot take fruit as payment.

MIRI: We can accept it as a snack.

[ROLO TAKES THE FIG AND PUTS IT IN HIS LUNCH BAG.]

ROLO: The shuttle is now carrying a passenger contribution.

[ROLO PUTS THE FIG IN THE LUNCH BAG. THE MONKEY CLIMBS OFF THE ROOF.]

ROLO: The service is back on schedule.

SELA: The branch route is still faster.

[THE BUS PULLS AWAY. THE MONKEY LEAPS AHEAD OF IT.]
[THE MONKEY WAVES FROM A BRANCH. THE BUS ANNOUNCER SAYS "NEXT STOP: THE NEXT BRANCH."]
