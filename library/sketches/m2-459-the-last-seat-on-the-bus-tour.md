# The Last Seat on the Bus Tour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sightseeing bus tours, premium seating, and guides who narrate views blocked by the bus itself*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: GUIDE, a gibbon; DRIVER, an orangutan; TOURIST, a macaque; SEAT, a baboon; TICKET CLERK, a capuchin.

[A parked tour bus. The last seat has a velvet rope around it and a sign: "BEST VIEW."]

GUIDE: The final seat offers an uninterrupted view of the valley.

TOURIST: The bus is parked in a garage.

DRIVER: We leave in two minutes.

SEAT: I have been facing the wall for three hours.

TICKET CLERK: The seat is reserved for the panoramic package.

[The tourist buys a panoramic ticket and climbs aboard. The last seat is behind a tall speaker.] 

TOURIST: I cannot see the valley.

GUIDE: The speaker is part of the tour experience.

DRIVER: It announces the turns.

[The guide turns on the speaker. It says "TURN LEFT" at full volume. The bus is still parked.] 

GUIDE: We are approaching the valley.

DRIVER: We are in the garage.

[The ticket clerk removes the velvet rope. The seat can swivel, but only toward the speaker. The tourist rotates it; the seat faces backward.] 

SEAT: I can now see where we have been.

TOURIST: We have been in a garage.

[The driver moves the bus outside. The view through the windows is blocked by a route map taped over the glass.] 

GUIDE: The mountain is just beyond this map.

TOURIST: Could we move the map?

TICKET CLERK: It is the route display.

DRIVER: I can put it above the windshield.

[The driver removes the map and clips it on the dashboard. The tourist takes the last seat. The speaker still blocks the valley. The guide tries to narrate around it.] 

GUIDE: On your right, a spectacular cliff.

TOURIST: That is the speaker.

GUIDE: The cliff is next to it.

[The bus drives to an overlook and stops. The driver opens the door. Everyone gets out. The last seat is empty, facing the open valley through the parked bus window.] 

SEAT: I can see it now.

TOURIST: So can I, from outside.

GUIDE: The premium view includes the seat.

DRIVER: The free view includes standing.

[The clerk removes the premium sign and turns it over. The other side says "PLEASE TAKE A SEAT." The tourist sits on a bench at the overlook.] 

TICKET CLERK: The last seat is available.

SEAT: I would like to stay here.


The guide takes a portable speaker off the last seat and clips it above the aisle. The driver tests the turn signal before moving. The tourist stays at the overlook and points out a cloud shaped like the speaker.

TOURIST: That is the first view the guide did not name for me.

GUIDE: It is a cloud.

DRIVER: It will not be on the route map.

The ticket clerk checks that the seat is folded forward and no one is standing in the doorway. The driver waits until the group is seated before closing the door. The guide gives the next stop without calling any seat premium.

FINAL IMAGE: The bus door stays open beside the overlook while the once-premium seat faces the actual valley.
