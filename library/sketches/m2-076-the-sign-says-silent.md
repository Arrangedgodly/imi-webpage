# The Sign Says Silent

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio-guide etiquette and self-guided exhibits*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: GUIDE ADA, a ring-tailed lemur; VISITOR BOK, a macaque; CURATOR NELL, a tamarin; and two TOURISTS.

[A gallery displays one fallen palm frond beneath a velvet rope. A sign reads PLEASE ENJOY QUIETLY. Ada wears an audio-guide headset and holds a tiny bell.]

ADA: Welcome to the Museum of Quiet Objects. Please keep your voices low and your hands to yourself.

BOK: Is this a real leaf?

NELL: It is a replica of a leaf that was real.

BOK: Where is the original?

NELL: It is under the replica. We could not move it without changing its relationship to the floor.

[Ada points to a headset.] 

ADA: Press one for the history of silence.

BOK: I pressed two.

ADA: Two is the sound of a gallery being admired.

[The headset emits a long, dignified hush. Bok nods. One tourist copies the nod. The second tourist nods at a blank wall.]

TOURIST ONE: I think I understand the wall.

NELL: It is a wall.

ADA: Please don't explain the wall. The wall has its own audio track.

BOK: What does it say?

[He taps the headset. A recording begins: the slow scrape of a chair.] 

ADA: That is track three, the room remembering a visitor.

NELL: The recording is from yesterday. The visitor was me, looking for the light switch.

BOK: How long is the track?

NELL: Four minutes.

BOK: What happens at the end?

NELL: I find the switch.

[The tourists sit on a bench. Ada whispers that sitting is permitted if done thoughtfully. The bench creaks loudly.] 

ADA: Please admire the bench's response in silence.

BOK: It sounds like a tiny door.

NELL: The bench is part of the exhibit now.

[She adds a label: BENCH, TEMPORARY SOUND PIECE. Bok tries to read it without making noise. His lips move.]

ADA: There is a silent-reading area.

BOK: Where?

[They look around. Every corner has a small speaker.]

NELL: We did not want to privilege one kind of quiet.

BOK: I can offer another kind.

[He sits on the floor and uses his tail to sweep the fallen frond into a neat circle. The movement is soft and careful. The tourists lean in, then follow suit, sweeping dust into little rings with their tails.] 

ADA: Is this a tour activity?

NELL: It is now. Call it track four: visitors making room.

BOK: Do we need to explain it?

NELL: No. They can listen.

[The curator removes the rope so the tourists can finish their circles. Ada approaches the sign and turns it around. The back says PLEASE ENJOY QUIETLY, WITH YOUR WHOLE TAIL.

The group continues sweeping. The audio guide plays a recording of a chair scrape, then stops.]

ADA: The museum is closing.

BOK: Is that track five?

NELL: No. That's the actual door.

[The tourists quietly leave. Nell adds the door's squeak to the collection, then places a velvet rope around the audio-guide sign.]
