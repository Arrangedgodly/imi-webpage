# The Film Crew Is in the Frame

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: behind-the-scenes movie sets, perfect-shot retakes, and directors who demand realism while rearranging reality*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Director Luma, a gibbon; actor Rell, a macaque; camera operator Vex, a capuchin; and script supervisor Omi, an orangutan.

INT. STUDIO SET - DAY

A cardboard forest stands under bright studio lights. Rell waits in costume. Luma peers through a viewfinder.

LUMA: This is the quiet moment before the hero discovers the path.

RELL: There is a camera operator in the path.

VEX: I am outside the frame.

RELL: Your tail is in the frame.

VEX pulls the tail back. It catches a paper tree, which falls behind the hero.

LUMA: We will reset the forest.

Omi checks the script. The scene says the hero enters alone, notices a leaf, and realizes the forest is alive.

RELL: I have noticed the leaf.

LUMA: The audience needs to feel the discovery.

They reset. Rell enters. The stagehand drops a real leaf from above. It lands on his head.

LUMA: Cut. The leaf is too direct.

OMI: The script says he notices a leaf.

LUMA: He should notice it emotionally.

Rell tries again, moving slowly. The camera tracks him. Vex backs into the lighting stand. The lights turn, casting a giant shadow across the cardboard forest.

LUMA: That shadow is excellent. Keep it.

VEX: It is my shadow.

LUMA: It feels authentic.

Omi asks for the next take. Rell delivers the line: "This forest is alive." At the same moment, a cardboard tree tips over. The crew catches it before it falls.

LUMA: The tree nearly fell too early.

RELL: The forest is alive and unstable.

Omi marks the line in the script. Luma asks the crew to clear the set so Rell can perform the scene alone. The crew steps away. The camera remains, still recording.

RELL: The path is clear.

The cardboard tree falls on its own. The crew watches from off-camera. Rell catches it and props it against a rock.

LUMA: That was the best take.

VEX: You can see the crew in the reflection.

The camera shows a shiny prop pond, and every crew member appears in it.

OMI: We can cover the pond.

RELL: Then it will not reflect the forest.

LUMA: We can keep the reflection. It tells us how the scene was made.

Omi gives the line to Rell. He repeats it, now looking at the crew and the cardboard props.

RELL: This forest is alive because we are holding it up.

The crew laughs. Luma says, "Cut," but no one moves. The director checks the shot and accepts it.

VEX: Are we in the scene now?

LUMA: We were in it the whole time.

The cardboard tree stays upright. The crew takes a group photo beside the reflected pond.

Omi writes the final scene number on the slate. Vex has already packed the camera, so they hold the slate beside the tree in the last frame of the footage.
