# The Very Official Ribbon Cutting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grand civic opening ceremonies where no one knows what is being opened*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Mayor Bolo, a baboon; Seli, a capuchin ceremony planner; Kito, a macaque facilities worker; and Miri, a gibbon guest.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - DAY

A red ribbon stretches between two stakes. Mayor Bolo holds oversized scissors. Seli checks the program.

SELI: Welcome to the opening of the new gate.

KITO: The gate is already open.

The gate swings in the breeze. Bolo lowers the scissors and asks whether they should close it first. Kito closes it. The latch catches, but the ribbon is tied across the gate. The mayor cannot reach the handle without crossing the ribbon.

MIRI: What are we opening?

SELI: The gate.

MIRI: It has a path on both sides.

Seli announces a ceremonial countdown. Bolo asks the crowd to count slowly. They do. At zero, the mayor cuts the ribbon. Both halves fall neatly to the ground. The gate remains closed.

Bolo tries the handle. It is locked. Kito reaches into his pocket for a key ring, but finds a seed instead. He eats it while thinking. Seli suggests the spare key is in the garden shed. The crowd waits as Kito walks away.

Bolo begins a speech about access. The gate opens behind him as the wind lifts it. The audience applauds. Bolo turns and sees the path.

BOLO: The gate has opened itself.

KITO (O.S.): I have the key.

Kito returns with the key and unlocks the gate. It swings open wider, bumping the ceremonial podium. The podium rolls down a slight slope. Miri catches it with her foot.

SELI: We have also opened the podium.

Miri hands it back. Bolo asks for another ribbon so they can cut one for the shed. Seli says the shed is already open. The mayor points the scissors toward a compost bin. Kito quickly closes it.

KITO: That one is better closed.

Seli checks the program. It says the ceremony includes a ribbon cutting, a speech and a group photo. Bolo cuts the remaining ribbon into small pieces and gives them to the guests. They tie them to their bags as keepsakes.

Miri walks through the open gate. The crowd follows. Bolo remains beside the podium.

BOLO: Where is everyone going?

MIRI: Through the gate.

Bolo follows, carrying the scissors. Kito hangs a sign on the gate: PLEASE CLOSE AFTER ENTERING. It swings shut behind the last guest. Seli reaches for the handle, but the gate opens easily from the inside.

Bolo asks Seli to amend the program. She crosses out opening and writes access demonstration. Kito tests the latch from both sides while Miri holds the gate. The path stays clear, and the crowd can enter without stepping over ribbon scraps.

SELI: We should have tested that before the ceremony.

KITO: We did. The gate was open.
