# The Firework Has to Attend a Sound Check

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: concert sound checks that turn a short test into a full production*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Stage manager Nari, a lemur; drummer Vex, an orangutan; pyrotechnic Pip, a capuchin; announcer Mara, a macaque; ECHO, the hillside return.

EXT. FESTIVAL STAGE - DUSK

[A row of paper fireworks stands behind a rope. Pip tests a small stage flash. Vex taps a snare drum.]

NARI: One sound check. Then the audience comes in.

VEX: I need to hear the full show.

PIP: The fireworks are not lit yet.

MARA: The program says opening burst at eight.

[Nari points to the clock. It is six.]

NARI: We are checking the cues, not starting the show.

[Vex plays a snare roll. The paper rockets wobble on their stands.]

VEX: The sky is responding.

PIP: It's a drum vibration.

[Mara speaks into the microphone. It echoes from the empty bleachers.]

MARA: Welcome to the festival.

[Her echo returns, slower.]

ECHO: Festival.

VEX: The crowd is rehearsing.

NARI: That's the hill.

[Pip places a paper firework on the floor and taps its cardboard tube. It makes a hollow thump. Vex matches the beat. Mara announces each thump as a launch.]

MARA: Three, two, one.

PIP: There is no one.

[Mara raises the mic. The echo arrives after Vex's next drumbeat, making the countdown continue twice as long. Nari tries to stop them, but the band follows its own echo.]

NARI: Stop at three.

VEX: Three.

ECHO: Three.

VEX: Two.

ECHO: Two.

PIP: We have reached one.

[He holds up a paper rocket. It has a tiny label: SOUND CHECK ONLY.]

MARA: The show has started.

NARI: No. The rehearsal has become louder.

[The drum roll rattles the paper rockets. One falls onto Vex's snare. He hits the drum. The rocket springs up and lands in Mara's hat.]

MARA: I have a firework.

PIP: You have a hat.

[Nari removes the rocket and places it back on its stand. She lowers the drum volume. The stage flash pops once, softly. The echo rolls down the hill and returns as a delayed pop.]

VEX: We have the finale.

NARI: We have one echo.

[They wait. The echo fades. Pip marks the cue on his card.]

VEX: We should test the applause cue.

NARI: There is no applause cue.

[Vex taps the snare once. The echo returns as a long roll. Mara claps. The echo claps back after she has lowered her hands.]

MARA: The hill applauds late.

PIP: We can put it at the end of the program.

[Nari rewrites the cue card. She shifts "finale" to the bottom. The paper rockets are stacked and tied shut. Vex reads the last line.]

VEX: Applause follows the sound check.

NARI: The applause is the echo.

[Mara lowers the microphone. Vex holds his drumsticks still. The hillside gives one final, faint pop.]

FINAL BEAT: Mara announces "Sound check complete." The empty hill repeats "complete" after the crew has packed the paper fireworks into a box.
