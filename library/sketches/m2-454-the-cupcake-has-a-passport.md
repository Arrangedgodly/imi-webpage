# The Cupcake Has a Passport

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking competitions that assign elaborate travel stories to ordinary desserts*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: JUDGE, a gibbon; BAKER, a macaque; HOST, an orangutan; RUNNER, a capuchin; FOOD SAFETY LEAD, a baboon.

[A baking show table. A cupcake sits on a plate beside a tiny paper passport.]

HOST: Tonight, one cupcake travels the world.

BAKER: It was baked ten minutes ago.

JUDGE: It has not left the plate.

[The host opens the paper passport. It is stamped with four cities.]

HOST: It has visited Rome, Cairo, Lima, and the moon.

FOOD SAFETY LEAD: The moon is not a city.

RUNNER: The stamp is on the frosting.

[The runner takes the passport away. The judge tastes a crumb.] 

JUDGE: Vanilla cake, lemon icing, toasted seed on top.

HOST: A culinary itinerary.

BAKER: It is one recipe.

[The host spins the plate like a globe. The cupcake slides toward the edge. The baker catches it by the wrapper.] 

BAKER: Please keep the dessert on the table.

HOST: The journey has turbulence.

FOOD SAFETY LEAD: The plate is wobbling because the table has a short leg.

[The runner folds a napkin and slips it beneath the table leg. The plate steadies.] 

JUDGE: Now the cupcake is stationary.

HOST: It has arrived.

[The baker cuts the cupcake in half. A pocket of icing shifts to one side.] 

JUDGE: The lemon is stronger in this half.

HOST: A regional variation.

BAKER: The icing moved when you spun it.

[The host turns the plate again, slowly. The runner holds it steady. The judge tastes the second half.] 

JUDGE: The crumb is even. The toasted seed gives it a crisp finish.

FOOD SAFETY LEAD: The passport should not touch the food.

HOST: It has been removed from the itinerary.

[The host reaches for a flag to mark the finish line. The food safety lead stops him.] 

FOOD SAFETY LEAD: No paper flag in the frosting.

HOST: What about a clean spoon?

JUDGE: We already have a plate.

[The host puts the spoon down. The baker serves the cupcake halves on separate clean plates. The judge checks the garnish and nods.] 

JUDGE: This dessert is complete. It does not need to travel.

BAKER: It has been served.

HOST: We have a local winner.

[The runner retrieves the passport. It has one smear of lemon icing, but no stamps have transferred to the plate.] 

RUNNER: The cupcake's only trip was from oven to table.

HOST: And the moon?

FOOD SAFETY LEAD: Still not a city.

The judge asks the baker how the recipe should be stored. The baker says the cupcake can stay covered at room temperature until serving, but the icing should not sit under a hot lamp. The host moves the prop globe away from the plate; its metal base has warmed in the spotlight. The runner checks that the passport ink has not touched the frosting.

JUDGE: A good dessert does not need a destination stamp.

HOST: It has arrived at the table.

FINAL IMAGE: The cupcake plates sit steady on the leveled table while the paper passport rests safely beside the prop globe.
