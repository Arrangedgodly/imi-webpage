# The Pottery Class Glazes the Sign-up Sheet

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: community art classes, overconfident craft demos, and registration forms mistaken for materials*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: INSTRUCTOR, an orangutan; STUDENT, a baboon; ASSISTANT, a capuchin; RECEPTIONIST, a gibbon; JANITOR, a macaque.

[A pottery studio. A sign-up sheet lies beside bowls of glaze. The instructor demonstrates a pinch pot. The student holds a clay bowl.]

INSTRUCTOR: Glaze goes on the clay after it dries.

STUDENT: Which bowl is the glaze?

[The assistant points at a jar. The student dips a brush and paints the sign-up sheet.]

RECEPTIONIST: You have glazed the attendance list.

STUDENT: It needed color.

ACT ONE

[The instructor asks the class not to touch the paper. The student tries to lift the sheet; it sticks to the table. The assistant slides a plastic scraper underneath. The paper tears into two pieces, separating the names from the checkboxes.]

ASSISTANT: We have names without attendance.

RECEPTIONIST: That describes half the class.

[The janitor brings a tray to dry the sheet. The instructor says glaze must be fired, but the paper cannot go in the kiln. The student asks if the names can be copied. The receptionist has a backup list in a folder, but it is covered in clay fingerprints.]

RECEPTIONIST: This one is also an art object.

[The instructor demonstrates how to apply glaze with a clean brush. The student paints the clay bowl carefully, then reaches for the wrong cup again. The assistant has moved the glaze jars to a high shelf and labeled them. The student reads "GLAZE" and "WATER" aloud before choosing.]

STUDENT: The blue jar is water.

INSTRUCTOR: Correct.

[The janitor brings a damp cloth to clean the table. The glaze sheet peels loose, leaving a blue rectangle on the wood. The receptionist copies the names from the backup. The class checks who is present. One name appears twice because the student signed again after painting.]

STUDENT: I thought the first one was practice.

RECEPTIONIST: There is no practice attendance.

[The instructor places a paper sign-up sheet inside a clear sleeve. The student helps wipe the table and places the finished bowl on the drying rack.]

[A late student arrives and asks to sign up. The receptionist slides over the dry form in its sleeve. The student signs once and places the pen back in its cup. The instructor checks the list, then points to the glaze shelf and its blue label.]

RECEPTIONIST: We have a clean record.

STUDENT: I have a clean bowl.

ACT TWO

[The class cleans its tools. The assistant sets out new forms in a marked folder, far from the glaze. The student signs once, then puts the pen down. The receptionist confirms the class list. The janitor turns the blue-stained paper into a label for the glaze shelf.]

INSTRUCTOR: It can still be useful.

STUDENT: My name is on it.

FINAL IMAGE: The glaze jar sits beneath the stained label while the unglazed sign-up sheet remains dry in its sleeve.
