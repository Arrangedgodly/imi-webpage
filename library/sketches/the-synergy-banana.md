# The Synergy Banana

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Corporate all-hands meetings and business jargon*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[STAGE: A conference room. A bowl of bananas sits under a sign reading "Q4 ALIGNMENT." A projector displays a single yellow circle. MARA, a baboon, grooms her lapel. KITO, a macaque, and LELA, a capuchin, hang by their tails from the seating rail. DEX, a gibbon, has both feet on the calculator.]

CEO MARA: Thank you for joining our all-hands. Today we will align around the banana.

EMPLOYEE KITO: The fruit?

MARA: The opportunity. The fruit is a vehicle for the opportunity.

EMPLOYEE LELA: I brought a banana because the invite said "bring a banana."

MARA: Excellent. You have already engaged with the deliverable.

[The projector clicks. A slide appears: BANANA: BEFORE / BANANA: AFTER.]

MARA: Our consultants have identified a peel gap. In the past, a monkey peeled a banana and ate it. Going forward, we will create a scalable peel journey.

KITO: Does that mean we all peel one?

MARA: It means we form a cross-functional peel pod.

LELA: I can peel. I'm on snacks.

MARA: Snacks will advise. Operations will own the peel. Finance will measure peel return on investment.

FINANCE MONKEY DEX: What does a peel return?

MARA: The question is not what the peel returns. The question is what the peel enables.

DEX: A banana without a peel?

MARA: That's a phase-two outcome.

[LELA PEELS HER BANANA. EVERYONE WATCHES.]

MARA: Let's pause there. Lela, what did you feel as you peeled?

LELA: Hungry.

MARA: Good. An authentic user insight. Can you repeat the action, but with more ownership?

LELA: I own this banana.

MARA: Strong. Put that in the deck.

KITO: Why does the slide say "banana transformation office"?

MARA: We need a central team to coordinate the pods.

KITO: How many people?

MARA: Four, initially.

DEX: And what will they do?

MARA: Coordinate the pods.

[THE PROJECTOR DISPLAYS A FLOWCHART. ARROWS LOOP FROM "BANANA" TO "ALIGNMENT" AND BACK.]

KITO: The diagram says the banana goes to alignment, then alignment goes to banana.

MARA: That's the circular model. It reduces waste.

LELA: What do we do with the peel?

MARA: Log it as an emerging asset.

DEX: Is the banana still edible?

MARA: We should not move ahead of the framework.

[LELA TAKES A BITE.]

MARA: Lela, please stop. You're consuming a deliverable before the steering committee reviews it.

LELA: I can save you a review. It's good.

KITO: We could give everyone a banana and stop here.

MARA: Kito, that's a very interesting simplification. Let's workshop what stopping looks like.

DEX: It looks like closing the meeting.

MARA: Let's put that in the parking lot.

[DEX WALKS TO THE WALL, FINDS NO PARKING LOT, AND SETS DOWN A BANANA PEEL.]

MARA: Not there. Facilities just waxed that floor.

LELA: So the peel is both an asset and a hazard?

MARA: That's the nuance. We need a risk owner.

KITO: I nominate the person holding it.

MARA: Great. Lela, you own the peel until we align.

LELA: Can I throw it away?

MARA: Let's circle back after lunch.

[LELA EATS THE BANANA, PUTS THE PEEL IN THE COMPOST BIN, AND EXITS.]

MARA: She has taken the first-mover advantage. We'll close with one takeaway. Nobody knows what the banana strategy is, but the bananas are gone, the room is booked for another hour, and the compost has a clear owner.

DEX: The compost?

MARA: You. Meeting adjourned.

[PROJECTOR CLICKS OFF.]
