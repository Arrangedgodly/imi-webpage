# The Forecast Is Fashion

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fashion runway shows, seasonal trends, and makeover commentary*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: HOST MIRA, a macaque; STYLIST OLA, an orangutan; MODEL PAX, a gibbon; and WEATHER REPORTER REN, a capuchin.

[A runway runs from one sturdy branch to another. A spotlight shines on a rack of scarves.]

MIRA: Tonight's collection is called "Prepared for Anything." Ola, what's the first look?

OLA: A lightweight shawl for mild wind, with a fastening that does not catch in branches.

REN: The forecast calls for rain.

OLA: Then we need the waterproof layer.

MIRA: Can it be dramatic?

OLA: It has a hood.

[Pax walks out wearing a clear poncho. A leaf sticks to the front.]

MIRA: Unexpected texture.

PAX: It is wet.

REN: The rain has started.

[Mira turns to the audience.]

MIRA: The designer planned for a chance of rain.

REN: I said rain.

OLA: The runway is too narrow for an umbrella. Pax, take the covered path.

MIRA: The covered path is off camera.

PAX: The uncovered path is slippery.

[Ola walks out carrying a broad leaf over Pax's head. The camera tries to follow.]

MIRA: We have an unplanned accessory.

OLA: It is a leaf. It also works.

[Ren checks the forecast sheet.]

REN: Wind shifts in two minutes. The upper branch will be exposed.

MIRA: We have a finale look on that branch.

PAX: I can wear it on the ground.

MIRA: The runway is the point.

OLA: The runway is wet wood.

[The spotlight swings as wind moves the branch. A scarf flies off the rack and lands on the weather sheet.]

REN: The temperature is now partly scarf.

MIRA: We need a rescue for the finale.

OLA: We need to move the audience under cover.

[The models carry the clothes to the covered path. Guests help carry the rack.]

MIRA: This is not how the show was blocked.

PAX: It is how the clothes stay dry.

[The runway clears. Mira tries to announce the next look, but the microphone is wrapped in a scarf.]

MIRA: Is the scarf part of the collection?

OLA: It is part of the microphone now.

[The crowd applauds from under the shelter.]

REN: Tomorrow's forecast is clear.

PAX: Then the clothes can stay on the rack.

MIRA: The collection has adapted to the weather.

OLA: The collection has been moved indoors.

[The rain falls on the empty runway.]
MIRA: We have a final sponsor message.

ANNOUNCER, off: This look is suitable for all seasons.

REN: It is raining.

ANNOUNCER, off: All seasons except rain.

OLA: Then it is not for all seasons.

PAX: The rain is stopping.

[Mira turns the scarf rack. A dry side appears.]

MIRA: The collection has a second side.

OLA: That's the side we were trying to show.

REN: The forecast did not change. We moved the rack.

MIRA: Can we call that a reveal?

PAX: You can call it a rack.

[The audience claps as the models carry the rack to a dry branch. The fabric stays on the hangers.]

ANNOUNCER, off: The collection is available in three colors.

REN: What are they?

ANNOUNCER, off: Dry, damp, and still on the rack.

[The models bow under the shelter..]

[BLACKOUT.]
