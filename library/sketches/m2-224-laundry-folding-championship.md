# The Laundry Folding Championship

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports championships that add timing, rankings, and dramatic review to ordinary household tasks*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Announcer Piri, a macaque; Competitor Sanu, a gibbon; Judge Toma, a capuchin; and Volunteer Odi, a gorilla.

INT. COMMUNITY GYM - DAY

A table holds clean towels. Piri stands at a microphone. A stopwatch sits beside Toma's score cards.

PIRI: Welcome to the inaugural towel-folding championship. Competitors will be judged on speed and corner alignment.

SANU: I have folded towels at home for years.

TOMA: Home results do not count.

ODI: The towels came from the laundry room.

PIRI: The first round is the hand towel sprint.

[THE TIMER STARTS. SANU FOLDS ONE TOWEL IN THREE SECONDS. TOMA INSPECTS IT WITH A RULER.]

TOMA: Two corners are uneven by a finger width.

SANU: It fits on the shelf.

PIRI: The judges disagree.

INT. COMMUNITY GYM - LATER

Odi brings in a stack of bath towels. Piri has added a slow-motion replay screen. Sanu examines a towel with a loose thread.

SANU: This one is fraying.

TOMA: The competition towel must be folded as provided.

ODI: I can replace it.

PIRI: That would interrupt the round.

SANU: I can tuck the thread inside.

[HE FOLDS THE TOWEL. THE THREAD POKES OUT. THE REPLAY SHOWS IT IN SLOW MOTION.]

PIRI: A controversial protrusion.

TOMA: The towel remains functional.

SANU: Thank you.

PIRI: We will review that fold on the replay screen.

[THE SCREEN SHOWS A CLOSE-UP OF THE TOWEL'S HEM.]

SANU: It is a towel.

TOMA: The thread is tucked inside now.

ODI: I brought a sewing kit if it comes loose.

PIRI: That may be an unauthorized repair.

ODI: It is an authorized laundry room.

PIRI: The audience is split.

[THE AUDIENCE IS TWO PEOPLE. ONE NODS. THE OTHER IS ALREADY USING A TOWEL AS A SEAT.]

TOMA: That guest has selected a practical use.

SANU: The towel is doing two jobs.

ODI: It is also clean.

SANU: It can dry the cups after the awards.

PIRI: We have cups?

TOMA: They are in the supply closet.

ODI: I folded those towels too.

INT. COMMUNITY GYM - FINALS

Sanu and Odi face a mountain of towels. The timer begins. Odi folds quickly but creates a pile that slides off the table.

ODI: The stack is unstable.

TOMA: The score is based on folds per minute.

SANU: A stack that falls is not ready for the shelf.

PIRI: Sanu is slowing down.

[SANU BUILDS A SMALL STABLE STACK. Odi folds the last towel and wedges it beneath the table leg. The table stops wobbling.]

TOMA: That towel is not in the stack.

ODI: It leveled the table.

SANU: That is a useful fold.

[PIRI LOOKS AT THE SCOREBOARD. IT READS "TIED." TOMA LOOKS AT THE STABLE TABLE.]

TOMA: Award both competitors. One for neatness, one for structural support.

PIRI: The table wins the overall title.
