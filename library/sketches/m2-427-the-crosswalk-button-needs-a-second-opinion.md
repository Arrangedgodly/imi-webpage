# The Crosswalk Button Needs a Second Opinion

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: city planning hearings about a pedestrian button that takes longer to hear than to press*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Planner Vex, an orangutan; engineer Mara, a gibbon; resident Pip, a capuchin; clerk Nari, a lemur.

EXT. CORNER INTERSECTION - DAY

[A crosswalk button stands on a post. A small sign reads PRESS ONCE. Vex addresses a folding table of residents.]

VEX: We are here to discuss the button's responsiveness.

PIP: I pressed it ten minutes ago.

MARA: It has registered the request.

[The button's light blinks. No signal changes.]

PIP: Is it thinking?

NARI: The walk cycle is ninety seconds.

VEX: The button has asked for a second opinion.

[Mara checks the wiring. Pip presses again. The button squeaks.]

MARA: Please press once.

PIP: I did.

VEX: The record shows twelve presses.

PIP: That was the meeting before this one.

[Nari reads the minutes. Vex asks whether the button's delay affects seniors. Pip, a young capuchin, steps to the curb and mimics a slow elderly walk. He reaches the middle of the street before the signal changes.]

VEX: A compelling demonstration.

MARA: He is still on the pavement.

PIP: I have not crossed.

[A pigeon walks across the road. The walk signal changes just as it reaches the far curb.]

VEX: The button has served a constituent.

NARI: The pigeon did not press it.

[Mara removes the button cover. A small spring is wedged under the switch. Pip pokes it with a feather. The button lights.]

PIP: It works for birds.

MARA: It works when the spring isn't stuck.

[Vex asks whether they should hold a second meeting. Nari checks the agenda. It has a line for "public comment on spring." Pip drops the feather. The button returns to its position and the light goes out.]

VEX: The device is withdrawing its statement.

[Mara unwedges the spring with a screwdriver. The walk sign lights. Nari and Pip wait at the curb while Vex finishes his question beside the table.]

NARI: The meeting has the right of way.

VEX: One last comment.

[Pip points to the walk sign. Vex gathers his papers and hurries after the group.]

[Mara turns the button cover toward the audience. A tiny note reads "NEXT CYCLE." Vex takes a photograph from the sidewalk to catch the framing.]

VEX: The button has provided a written statement.

MARA: That note came with the switch.

NARI: It is still official.

[Vex steps off the curb while looking at the photo. Pip grabs his sleeve and pulls him back onto the sidewalk.]

PIP: The button is not the crosswalk.

VEX: I was following the minutes.

[Mara pockets the screwdriver. The group crosses together on the one green signal.]

FINAL BEAT: They reach the far curb as the signal changes back. Nari records "button worked; meeting delayed" and hands Vex the screwdriver for the next public comment.
