# The Press Conference About the Puddle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: political crisis press conferences and overmanaged answers*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: MAYOR RELL, a gibbon; SPOKESMONKEY PIP, a macaque; REPORTER NIM, a capuchin; and CITY WORKER ORO, a lemur.

[A podium stands beside a small puddle. A sign reads: PRESS CONFERENCE. PIP holds a folder marked RESPONSE.] 

PIP: The mayor will address the puddle at this time and take no questions about unrelated water.

RELL: We are aware of the puddle.

NIM: It is on the main path.

RELL: We are aware of the location.

NIM: It is blocking the ramp.

PIP: The ramp remains open in principle.

ORO: The puddle is two inches deep and can be stepped around, but the ramp is slippery.

PIP: Please stay on message.

RELL: Our message is that we are aware.

NIM: Are you going to drain it?

RELL: We are considering the water's options.

ORO: I have a bucket.

PIP: We cannot say the bucket is approved until we review its capacity.

ORO: I have used it for puddles before.

RELL: The city values experience.

NIM: Is the ramp safe right now?

ORO: It is not. I put a cone beside it and can sweep the water toward the drain.

PIP: The cone is not in the photo plan.

[ORO PLACES THE CONE. THE REPORTER STEPS BACK.] 

NIM: That is a useful warning.

RELL: We are evaluating the warning.

ORO: I am sweeping the water.

[BRUSH SWISHES. WATER RUNS INTO A DRAIN.] 

PIP: Can you pause until the remarks are done?

ORO: The ramp is getting drier.

RELL: We appreciate the worker's initiative.

NIM: Will the city clear the drain so this does not happen again?

ORO: I will check the grate after the path dries.

PIP: We are not prepared to promise a grate inspection.

RELL: We can promise to tell the crew what we saw.

NIM: The crew is right here.

ORO: I heard you.

RELL: Then our statement is complete.

PIP: We have one final message: the puddle has been addressed.

PIP: The city needs a final statement about the cause.

RELL: The cause was rain.

ORO: The drain grate was covered in leaves.

RELL: We are aware of the grate.

NIM: You can see it from the podium.

PIP: The podium is not part of the response plan.

ORO: I can lift the grate after the path dries.

NIM: Why wait?

ORO: The water is still running. If I lift it now, leaves may wash into the drain.

PIP: The statement should say the worker will inspect the grate when the flow slows.

RELL: That is specific.

PIP: The folder says to avoid specifics.

RELL: The folder is not standing beside the drain.

[THE WATER FLOW LESSENS. ORO LIFTS THE GRATE AND PULLS OUT A CLUMP OF LEAVES.] 

ORO: The grate is clear.

NIM: That is the cause and the fix.

RELL: We have now addressed both.

PIP: The press conference can end.

RELL: The mayor will now take questions.

NIM: Is the podium dry?

ORO: Not yet.

[THE PODIUM LEG SINKS INTO THE LAST PATCH OF WATER.] 

NIM: The podium is now in the puddle.

ORO: I'll move it before anyone trips.

RELL: The city remains aware.
