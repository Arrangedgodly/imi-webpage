# The Playground Zoning Hearing

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: planning hearings that regulate spaces before anyone uses them*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Chair Leni, a gibbon; planner Miri, a macaque; resident Odo, a baboon; child Kito, a capuchin.

EXT. EMPTY PLAYGROUND - DAY

Miri presents a map. The playground has one slide and a patch of grass.

MIRI: We need to designate the slide as a play zone.

KITO: It already is one.

LENI: We need public comment.

Odo asks whether children may run on the grass. Miri says running requires a permit after 3 p.m. Kito asks if he can climb the slide ladder. Leni says only in the direction marked by the arrows.

Kito climbs. The arrows point up. He reaches the top and asks where he should go. Miri says down the slide, but the map labels the landing area "temporary gathering space."

ODO: Is a landing temporary?

MIRI: Only until the next use.

Leni calls for comments. Kito says the grass is soft and he wants to play tag. Odo asks if tag counts as running. Miri checks the manual. It says running in circles is a track activity. Kito says they can run in a straight line. Leni asks whether that would cross the slide zone.

Miri places cones to mark the boundaries. The cones block the path to the slide. Odo moves one cone. Miri says he has altered the site plan.

Odo sits on the grass. It is comfortable. Kito sits beside him. Leni asks if sitting requires a permit. Miri says the plan only covers movement.

The children start a game of follow-the-leader without running. They walk around the cones, then through the slide zone. Miri tries to stop them, but the map's boundaries are drawn on paper, not on the ground. Leni asks if the playground has been approved for play.

Miri asks the group to test the site plan before voting. Kito walks from the path to the slide. One cone blocks the shortest route. Odo moves the cone to the grass, where it marks a corner for tag. Miri says the zone has shifted. Leni asks Kito to climb and slide under the rules. He does; the landing zone stays clear. Kito asks if the game can include the grass and the slide. The planner checks the map and finds the grass labeled "open use," with no restrictions. She admits the running permit applies only during maintenance. Odo says the sign should say that. Miri writes a new sign with plain language. The residents move the cones away from the slide and leave one beside the path to warn people about the slope.

Kito tests the slide once more, then joins the game.

TAG

Kito slides down.

MIRI: The landing zone was unapproved.

LENI: It has now been used.
