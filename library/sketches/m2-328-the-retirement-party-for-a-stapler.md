# The Retirement Party for a Stapler

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sentimental workplace retirement ceremonies for replaceable office equipment*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Host Miri, a baboon; office manager Nara, a macaque; assistant Toma, a gibbon; office stapler nicknamed Seli, a metal tool.

INT. OFFICE BREAK ROOM - AFTERNOON

A stapler sits on a cushion. Miri holds a microphone.

MIRI: Today we say goodbye to Seli, who has served this office for many years.

NARA: It jammed once this morning.

TOMA: We unjammed it.

Miri asks Seli to share a few words. The stapler stays silent until Toma presses its handle; it staples one page.

MIRI: A final statement.

Nara says the stapler can keep working. Miri says the ceremony is scheduled, and the retirement banner has been printed. The banner says "Seli's Long Staple Journey." Toma says there are still staples in the tray.

Miri reads a list of achievements: joining two forms, securing an agenda, and fastening the snack sign. Nara says the stapler also stapled a sleeve once. Miri calls it an unplanned collaboration.

Toma brings out a gold box for the stapler. It is too narrow. Seli will not fit inside. Nara asks if they can give the box to the office supply cabinet. Miri says the gift is for Seli.

Toma removes the box lid and sets the stapler on top. Miri asks whether the farewell speech can begin. Nara wants to test the stapler before they decide. She feeds it two sheets. It jams again. Toma gently clears a bent staple. It works.

MIRI: The machine has overcome adversity.

NARA: It needs a refill.

Toma opens the supply drawer. There are staples. Miri says the office is not ready to let go. Nara asks if the stapler can be retired from the desk and kept in the cabinet as a backup. Miri agrees that this is a graceful transition.

Seli is moved from the cushion to the supply shelf. Toma places a newer stapler on the desk. It has a label that says "temporary replacement." Miri asks if the new stapler has a name. Nara writes "Seli II" on a tag.

MIRI: The legacy continues.

NARA: It is the same model.

Toma tries to staple the farewell certificate. The new stapler jams. Everyone looks at old Seli. Miri reaches to get it, but Nara points to the backup shelf.

Nara asks if Seli can staple one page before she goes into storage. The old stapler works after the jam is cleared. Miri says the farewell can include one last task, but no one calls it a miracle.

Seli tests one more staple.

Seli sets it on the shelf.

The next meeting starts soon.

The stapler remains on the shelf.

TAG

Miri gives the new stapler its own small welcome speech.

TOMA: It has been here four minutes.

MIRI: The program is flexible.
