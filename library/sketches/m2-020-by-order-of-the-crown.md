# By Order of the Crown

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Formal royal announcements and ceremonial public addresses*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Royal announcer Boro, a mandrill; Queen Tessa, a macaque; herald Venn, a baboon; citizen Lali, a gibbon; gardener Omi, a capuchin.

[STAGE: A BALCONY, A RED CURTAIN, A SCROLL TIED WITH GOLD CORD. TRUMPETS SOUND.] 

BORO: Hear now the words of Queen Tessa, keeper of the royal fig tree and protector of the north stair.

TESSA: We have reviewed the matter of the stair.

VENN: The Queen has reviewed the stair.

TESSA: The stair needs a railing.

BORO: A solemn decree.

LALI: It has needed a railing for months.

VENN: The citizen speaks out of turn.

TESSA: She speaks at the right time.

BORO: The announcement will continue.

TESSA: Omi will build the railing tomorrow.

OMI: I can build it today.

VENN: The royal schedule says tomorrow.

OMI: The wood is here now.

TESSA: Then the royal schedule is too slow.

BORO: This decree shall be read in every square.

LALI: The square has no railing either.

TESSA: How many stairs are there?

LALI: Twelve by the well. Seven by the market.

VENN: The scroll names one stair.

TESSA: Add the other stairs.

BORO: The scroll has no room.

TESSA: Unroll it.

[THE SCROLL UNFURLS DOWN THE STAIRS, KNOCKING OVER A TRUMPET STAND.] 

BORO: The decree has reached the ground.

VENN: And the trumpet is under it.

TESSA: We will read the announcement while Omi works.

OMI: I need the scroll for measuring.

BORO: The royal document cannot be cut.

OMI: I need a straight edge.

TESSA: Use it. We can write another decree.

VENN: That is not how decrees are made.

TESSA: It is how railings are built.

LALI: The first post is straight.

OMI: Hold it steady.

BORO: The Queen's hand is on the post.

TESSA: It is a heavy post.

VENN: The herald will assist.

BORO: I am wearing ceremony sleeves.

LALI: Roll them up.

BORO: They are embroidered with the royal crest.

TESSA: The crest can help hold the wood.

OMI: The railing is secure.

LALI: I can use it.

VENN: The public may now ascend safely.

BORO: By order of the crown.

TESSA: By order of the stairs.

OMI: The other railings will be finished tomorrow.

VENN: Shall we announce them?

TESSA: Tell people where the work is happening. They will see it.

BORO: The formal address is over.

LALI: The trumpet is still under the scroll.

BORO: That will be the next announcement.

TESSA: Announce that the stair is open.

VENN: All the stairs?

TESSA: The one with the railing.

BORO: The public may use one stair safely.

LALI: We will use the other route until tomorrow.

OMI: I can start the next railing now.

TESSA: Take the wood to the market steps.

VENN: The formal schedule says after lunch.

TESSA: Then lunch can wait until the tools arrive.

BORO: By order of the crown?

TESSA: By order of the carpenter.

[THE TRUMPET PLAYS A MUFFLED NOTE. THE NEW RAILING HOLDS.]
