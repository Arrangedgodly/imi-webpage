# The Secret Handshake Seminar

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: spy thriller conventions and mandatory team-building exercises*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: INSTRUCTOR VEX, a gibbon; AGENT LOMA, a macaque; AGENT RUE, a capuchin; and RECEPTIONIST KIP, a lemur.

[A training room. A poster reads: TRUST IS A CODE. Vex demonstrates a complicated handshake.] 

VEX: Operatives, this greeting has twelve steps and one concealed signal.

LOMA: Which step is concealed?

VEX: The one you do not explain.

RUE: Then how do we know whether we are doing it right?

VEX: You will feel the trust.

[THEY TRY THE HANDSHAKE. LOMA'S TAIL GETS CAUGHT BETWEEN ELBOWS.] 

LOMA: I feel a pinch.

VEX: That is step seven.

KIP: The reception desk has a simpler greeting. Say your name and why you are here.

VEX: That is too easy to imitate.

KIP: Anyone can imitate a handshake too.

VEX: But not the concealed signal.

RUE: You said we should not explain it.

VEX: Correct.

LOMA: Then how will the new team know which signal to use?

VEX: They will be briefed.

KIP: The briefing room is locked.

VEX: What is the access code?

KIP: The code is on the sign by the door.

VEX: That is a security flaw.

KIP: The sign says "Room 3. Push door." It is a direction.

[THEY PUSH THE DOOR. IT OPENS.]

RUE: We have breached the briefing room.

VEX: No. We have entered as authorized staff.

LOMA: The handshake is still tangled.

[LOMA UNWINDS THE GROUP'S ARMS. THEY ALL RELEASE AT ONCE AND THE DOOR CLOSES.] 

VEX: We need a protocol for disengagement.

KIP: Try saying "excuse me."

VEX: That is not discreet.

KIP: It is quieter than the door.

VEX: We can practice the greeting without the full sequence. One hand, one nod, and a turn toward the door.

LOMA: That sounds like a normal greeting.

VEX: The turn is the concealed signal.

RUE: Which direction?

VEX: You must infer it from your partner.

[THEY ALL TURN DIFFERENT WAYS. THE DOOR OPENS AND SHUTS.] 

KIP: You are pointing at the exit.

VEX: That is part of the exercise.

KIP: Someone needs to deliver the lunch tray.

LOMA: We can take it.

VEX: The tray is unmarked.

RUE: It has lunch on it.

KIP: The kitchen sent it here because this room was on the schedule.

[THE TRAY IS PASSED ALONG. EACH OPERATIVE TRIES TO HIDE IT BEHIND THEIR BACK.] 

VEX: The operation has a package.

LOMA: It is a lunch.

RUE: If we pass it openly, no one has to wonder what we're hiding.

VEX: We would lose the element of surprise.

KIP: The soup is getting cold.

VEX: Then the mission has a deadline.

[THE GROUP SETS THE TRAY ON THE TABLE. THEY SIT.] 

LOMA: The handshake can wait until after we eat.

VEX: A real operative adapts to changing conditions.

RUE: Is that in the handbook?

VEX: It will be in the next edition.

[THEY FORM A LINE. EACH SAYS THEIR NAME AND PURPOSE.] 

RUE: Rue. I am here to learn the route.

LOMA: Loma. I am here to carry the message.

VEX: Vex. I am here to teach the signal.

KIP: Kip. I am here to open the door.

[THE DOOR OPENS WITHOUT A SOUND.] 

VEX: The mission is complete.

LOMA: We did not use the handshake.

VEX: We can practice it after lunch.

KIP: Lunch is in the cafeteria. The sign is on the wall.

VEX: I will follow the sign.

RUE: A secret route?

VEX: A route with directions.
