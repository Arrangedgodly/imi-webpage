# The Very Serious Talent Search

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Reality talent competitions, judging panels, and emotional backstories*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Vela, a mandrill; judge Omi, a macaque; judge Tallo, a baboon; contestant Nix, a gibbon; stage manager Pru, a capuchin.

[STAGE: A TALENT SHOW SET WITH A GOLDEN BANANA BUTTON. A SPOTLIGHT CIRCLES THE EMPTY FLOOR.]

VELA: Welcome to The Very Serious Talent Search, where dreams meet a panel with clipboards.
OMI: I have a clipboard and a very serious face.
TALLO: I have two clipboards.
VELA: Tell us your name and your journey.
NIX: I'm Nix. I learned to juggle while waiting for my aunt to finish grooming me.
OMI: How long did you wait?
NIX: Long enough to juggle the same three seed pods all afternoon.
TALLO: A powerful origin story.
PRU: The pods are from the prop basket.
NIX: They are family to me now.
VELA: What will you perform tonight?
NIX: I will juggle while hanging from one arm.
OMI: That sounds dangerous.
NIX: The pods are soft.
TALLO: What are the stakes?
NIX: If I drop one, I have to pick it up.
VELA: Music, please.
[UPBEAT MUSIC STARTS. NIX SWINGS FROM A LOW BAR AND TOSSES THREE PODS.]
OMI: The first pod has landed in my lap.
TALLO: That is audience participation.
PRU: The second pod is in the light rig.
VELA: Our contestant is reaching new heights.
NIX: I cannot reach the pod.
OMI: We should keep the performance going.
TALLO: The suspense is excellent.
NIX: I have stopped juggling.
VELA: A bold pause.
NIX: I am climbing down.
OMI: The pause has become a descent.
[THE MUSIC SWELLS AS NIX CLIMBS DOWN.]
TALLO: I have never seen gravity used so confidently.
VELA: Judges, your comments.
OMI: Your focus was impressive.
NIX: I was looking for the pod.
OMI: Exactly. Commitment.
TALLO: Your story moved me.
NIX: I am still telling it.
TALLO: Even better.
VELA: Now the golden banana button. Press it to send Nix to the finale.
OMI: The button is sticky.
PRU: It is not a button. It is a banana.
VELA: That's why it's golden.
TALLO: I will press it.
[HE PRESSES THE BANANA. IT SQUISHES.]
TALLO: The finale is moist.
OMI: I vote yes.
VELA: I vote yes.
NIX: Do I get to perform again?
VELA: The finale will be tomorrow.
NIX: Will the pods be back?
PRU: We have one in the light rig.
TALLO: Then your journey continues.
OMI: I would like to hear your story again.
NIX: My aunt has finished grooming.
VELA: Can she come on the show?
NIX: She can judge how I juggle.
TALLO: We need a fourth clipboard.
OMI: I will share mine.
PRU: That is not how clipboards work.
VELA: The panel is learning, too.
NIX: Should I take the golden banana?
TALLO: It is part of the set.
NIX: Then I will leave it here.
OMI: A gracious exit.
VELA: A contestant who knows when to stop.
PRU: The pod is still in the light.
TALLO: Then there is one more act.

[THE SPOTLIGHT STOPS ON THE STRANDED POD WHILE THE JUDGES RAISE THEIR CLIPBOARDS.]
