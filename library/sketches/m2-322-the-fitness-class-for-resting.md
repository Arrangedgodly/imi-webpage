# The Fitness Class for Resting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wellness classes that turn relaxation into a competitive achievement*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Instructor Pava, a capuchin; students Miri, a gibbon; Odo, a baboon; and Leni, a macaque.

INT. QUIET ROOM - DAY

Pava lowers the lights. A sign says REST CLASS: NO MOVEMENT REQUIRED. Miri lies on a mat. Odo sits cross-legged. Leni checks the class chart.

PAVA: Today's goal is to relax more deeply than the person next to you.

MIRI: I thought this was resting.

PAVA: It is. But we track progress.

Pava gives everyone a small bell. Students are told to ring it when they achieve a calm thought. Odo rings immediately.

PAVA: What was your calm thought?

ODO: I was calm before the bell.

Miri asks whether ringing a bell is restful. Pava says the bell measures success. Leni lies down and tries to clear her mind. Pava walks between the mats with a clipboard.

PAVA: How is your breathing?

LENI: Now that you asked, louder.

Pava asks Miri to visualize a quiet pond. Miri says she is thinking about the water bowl outside. Pava marks her "externally focused." Odo rings his bell again because he has noticed Miri's score.

MIRI: You are competing at resting.

ODO: I am winning.

Pava announces a new exercise: remain still while hearing a pleasant sound. Leni says the bell is not pleasant. Pava replaces it with a soft chime. Odo asks if the chime counts as a point. Pava says no. He stops moving, but keeps one paw raised to ring the bell.

Miri rolls onto her side and falls asleep. Pava whispers that she may be demonstrating advanced rest. Odo rings his bell quietly. The sound wakes Miri.

MIRI: I was doing it.

PAVA: You achieved a deep state.

MIRI: I lost it.

Leni turns the scoring chart face down. Odo asks if that is allowed. Pava says the class has no written rule against hiding the chart. Leni asks the students to put their bells on the floor and rest without recording anything.

The room quiets. Odo keeps looking at the bell, waiting for a score. Pava takes the clipboard and leaves it outside. Miri closes her eyes. Leni's shoulders drop.

Odo finally lets his paw lower. He starts to breathe slowly. Pava sits too, then reaches for the clipboard out of habit. Leni points at the empty wall where it used to hang.

Leni asks whether the bells can be returned to the box. Odo gathers them, but they ring against one another. Pava winces at the noise and admits the measuring tools have kept everyone alert. She writes "no score today" on the chart and sets it outside. Odo says he feels better now that he is not winning.

TAG

Pava whispers, "How relaxed are we?"

All three students open one eye.

PAVA: I have to stop measuring.
