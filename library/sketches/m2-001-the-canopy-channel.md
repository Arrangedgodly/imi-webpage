# The Canopy Channel

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Grandly narrated wildlife documentaries*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Narrator Nix, a hushed-voiced gibbon; Pallo, a capuchin subject; Kesh, a camera macaque; Dr. Venn, a field biologist.

[STAGE: A FAKE FOREST SET. A PAPER MOON HANGS FROM A STRING. NIGHT SOUNDS PLAY.]

NIX: At first light, Pallo emerges from the sleeping tree.

PALLO: I have been awake for an hour.

NIX: His hunger is visible in the quickness of his glance.

PALLO: I am looking for my glasses.

KESH: We are rolling.

PALLO: Then roll the glasses over here.

NIX: The troop gathers at the water bowl, each animal waiting for its turn.

PALLO: The water bowl is empty.

DR. VENN: It is a flowerpot saucer. The bowl is behind you.

NIX: Pallo has found a new source of water.

PALLO: I found my glasses. The bowl is still empty.

[PAPER BIRDS FLUTTER. A STAGEHAND POURS WATER.]

NIX: Now the troop drinks.

PALLO: Please let the smaller ones go first.

NIX: A remarkable act of generosity.

PALLO: They are blocking the camera.

KESH: Pallo, can you move one branch to the left?

PALLO: My left or the camera's left?

KESH: The camera's.

PALLO: It has no left. It has a lens.

NIX: A sound observation from the canopy.

DR. VENN: Pallo, what are you doing with those leaves?

PALLO: Making a shade roof. The sun is hot.

NIX: The male builds a shelter to protect the troop.

PALLO: I am protecting the camera from glare.

KESH: I appreciate that.

NIX: His behavior may reveal an ancient instinct.

PALLO: It reveals that your lamp is in my eyes.

[A SMALL FAN STARTS. THE PAPER MOON SWINGS WILDLY.] 

NIX: The wind changes without warning.

PALLO: That's the fan. Someone has it pointed at the moon.

KESH: The moon is supposed to rise slowly.

PALLO: Tie it down, then.

NIX: Pallo approaches the strange object with cautious curiosity.

PALLO: It's a prop. I moved it here ten minutes ago.

DR. VENN: He is right. There are stage marks on it.

NIX: The natural world leaves traces even when we cannot see them.

PALLO: Those are tape marks. The natural world did not bring tape.

KESH: Can we get a clean shot of Pallo looking thoughtful?

PALLO: I can look annoyed.

NIX: The distinction is subtle.

PALLO: It is not subtle to me.

[PAPER MOON DROPS INTO THE WATER BOWL.] 

NIX: The moon has entered the pool.

PALLO: The moon is wet paper.

DR. VENN: We should dry it before the next take.

PALLO: Or film the water bowl. It's the only thing behaving naturally.

NIX: The Canopy Channel will return after the break, when Pallo meets the mysterious creature holding the boom microphone.

PALLO: That's Kesh. He has lunch in his pocket.

KESH: You can tell?

PALLO: I have a good nose.

[LIGHTS SHIFT. THE FALSE FOREST FALLS OVER, EXCEPT THE WATER BOWL.]
