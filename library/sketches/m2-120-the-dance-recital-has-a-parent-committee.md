# The Dance Recital Has a Parent Committee

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: children's dance recitals and adult expectations imposed on performers*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: CHOREOGRAPHER MIRA, a macaque; PARENT PEL, a gibbon; DANCER NIM, a capuchin; USHER VEE, a lemur; and an ensemble of dancers and parents.

[A small stage. One leaf is taped to the floor as a marker. Pel holds a program.] 

MIRA: The young dancers will perform their leaf dance. They may move wherever feels comfortable.

PEL: The program says they will form a perfect circle.

MIRA: The circle is a suggestion.

NIM: I want to follow the leaf.

PEL: The leaf is taped to the wrong side of the stage.

NIM: I put it there because the light is warmer.

VEE: The parent committee has a seating chart.

PEL: It says parents should sit in the front row.

VEE: The front row is in the dancers' way.

PEL: We can move the dancers back.

MIRA: The stage is small. They need room.

[THE MUSIC STARTS. THE CHILDREN MOVE IN A LOOSE GROUP. NIM CIRCLES THE LEAF.] 

PEL: They are not in formation.

MIRA: They are keeping time.

PEL: The music has a count.

NIM: It has a bird call too.

[A BIRD CALL SOUNDS IN THE RECORDING. THE DANCERS TURN TOWARD THE WINDOW.] 

PEL: They have all turned away from the audience.

VEE: The audience can hear the bird too.

[THE PARENTS TURN TO LOOK OUT THE WINDOW. THE DANCERS CONTINUE, WITH MORE SPACE.] 

MIRA: The group has found a new shape.

PEL: The program says a circle.

MIRA: The program is in your hand. The children are on the stage.

[NIM STEPS AROUND THE LEAF AND INVITES ANOTHER DANCER TO JOIN. THE GROUP MOVES IN A SLOW CURVE.] 

PEL: Should I ask them to repeat it for a photograph?

VEE: They are halfway through the dance.

MIRA: Let them finish first.

[THE BIRD CALLS AGAIN. THE DANCERS COMPLETE THE CURVE AND BOW TOWARD THE WINDOW.] 

PEL: Should they bow to the audience?

MIRA: They can bow wherever they want.

NIM: I want to bow to my parent.

[THE PARENTS STAND. THEY BOW BACK. PEL LIFTS HIS CAMERA.] 

PEL: The program says the group photo happens after the dance.

VEE: The parents are already in the aisle.

MIRA: Take the photo there. No one needs to go back onstage.

[THE DANCERS GATHER AROUND THE LEAF MARKER, WHICH VEE HAS CARRIED OFF THE STAGE.] 

NIM: The leaf can be in the photo too.

PEL: It is a floor marker.

MIRA: Tonight it was their partner.

PEL: The program says everyone should fit in the frame.

VEE: The frame is too small for the parents and dancers together.

MIRA: Step back a pace. The leaf can stay in the center.

[THE GROUP SHIFTS. ONE PARENT BLOCKS THE CAMERA WITH A FLOWER.] 

PEL: The flower is in the frame.

PARENT: It is for the dancer.

NIM: Put it beside the leaf.

[PEL TAKES THE PHOTO. THE BIRD CALLS FROM THE WINDOW, AND THE DANCERS TURN TOWARD IT AGAIN.]
