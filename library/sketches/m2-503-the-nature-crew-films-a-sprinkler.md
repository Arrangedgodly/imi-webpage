# The Nature Crew Films a Sprinkler

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: serious wildlife documentaries, suspenseful narration, and camera crews assigning epic behavior to ordinary garden equipment*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: NARRATOR, a gibbon; CAMERAPERSON, an orangutan; FIELD PRODUCER, a baboon; GARDENER, a macaque; NEIGHBOR, a capuchin.

[A suburban lawn at dawn. A sprinkler ticks back and forth. The camera person crouches behind a hedge. The narrator whispers into a microphone.]

NARRATOR: At first light, the metal creature surveys its territory.

GARDENER: It waters the lawn.

FIELD PRODUCER: Keep the description dramatic. We need a three-minute cold open.

[The sprinkler sweeps left and sprays the camera person's boot.]

CAMERAPERSON: It has found me.

NARRATOR: The creature marks its prey with a fine mist.

GARDENER: It is watering your shoe.

[The camera person backs away and slips on the damp grass. The producer catches the tripod.]

FIELD PRODUCER: We have an unexpected predator encounter.

GARDENER: It is a sprinkler on a hose.

ACT ONE

[The crew resets behind a wheelbarrow. The neighbor approaches with a paper bag of seed. The narrator lowers their voice further.]

NARRATOR: Another animal enters the clearing, carrying food.

NEIGHBOR: These are sunflower seeds. I am taking them to the garden.

NARRATOR: The seed carrier approaches the territorial machine.

NEIGHBOR: It is off.

[The sprinkler rotates once and sprays the neighbor's sleeve. The field producer signals the camera person to zoom in.]

FIELD PRODUCER: Get the sleeve. The water has made contact.

GARDENER: I turned it off at the tap.

[The gardener points to the hose. The spray comes from a second sprinkler hidden behind the planter. The neighbor hops backward, keeping the seed bag dry.]

NEIGHBOR: There are two of them.

NARRATOR: A rival has joined the first creature.

CAMERAPERSON: They are not rivals. They share a hose.

[The producer checks the footage monitor. It shows only the camera person's wet boot. The producer asks for a second take.]

FIELD PRODUCER: Can we get the mist in slow motion?

GARDENER: You can get it in real time if you stand somewhere dry.

[The camera person moves to the sidewalk. The neighbor sets the seed bag on the wheelbarrow. The producer holds the bag as if it were evidence.]

NARRATOR: The seeds wait at the edge of the water.

NEIGHBOR: I can plant them now.

FIELD PRODUCER: Don't move. The shot needs a reveal.

ACT TWO

[The gardener closes the tap. Both sprinklers stop. The crew waits. The neighbor takes the bag and pours seeds into a prepared garden row.]

NARRATOR: The mechanical beast has retreated.

GARDENER: I shut off the water.

CAMERAPERSON: We have a clean shot of the garden.

FIELD PRODUCER: Can you make the seed dramatic?

[The neighbor drops a seed. It rolls into a shallow groove and stops. The narrator pauses, then leans toward the microphone.]

NARRATOR: A single seed has chosen its ground.

NEIGHBOR: I put it there.

[The camera person frames the seed and the idle sprinkler in one shot. The gardener turns the tap handle to confirm it is closed.]

FINAL IMAGE: The crew silently films a seed while the sprinkler waits, dry and motionless, beside the hose.
