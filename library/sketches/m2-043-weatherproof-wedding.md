# Weatherproof Wedding

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Luxury wedding makeover television*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: MARA, the host; FINN, a macaque groom; SABLE, a gibbon planner; and PIP, a parrot officiant.

[An outdoor wedding set. White cloth is stretched between two branches. Sable wheels in a leaf-shaped seating chart.]

MARA: Today we turn a damp clearing into the most intimate celebration this canopy has ever seen. Sable has planned every detail.

SABLE: The guest list has twelve names and twenty-one assigned leaves.

FINN: Why twenty-one?

SABLE: Some relatives prefer to sit near an exit.

FINN: There are no walls.

SABLE: That's why I made the chart.

[Wind lifts the cloth like a sail. Sable clips it down with a basket of stones.]

MARA: Look at that reveal.

SABLE: It's ballast.

FINN: I asked for something simple.

SABLE: Simple means fewer emergency branches.

[Pip arrives carrying a twig.]

PIP: Repeat after me. I promise to share the shade and return this branch.

FINN: I promise to share the shade and return this branch.

MARA: A personal touch.

SABLE: That branch was listed as a floral accent.

PIP: It is a branch.

[Rain begins. Sable opens a case of miniature umbrellas. They are all attached to the seating chart.]

SABLE: Plan B.

FINN: The umbrellas are tied to the chart.

SABLE: That is how we keep the seating dry.

[The chart tips into the mud. Leaves slide into a puddle. Mara gestures at the camera crew.]

MARA: Stay on the wide shot. We want the audience to see the full transformation.

FINN: We haven't transformed anything. The chairs are sliding downhill.

[Sable wedges a stone beneath a chair. It stops. Finn and the guests pull the cloth down under the broad fig leaf. The ceremony is now covered by the one thing not on the chart.]

PIP: Are you ready to make your vows?

FINN: I promise to share the shade, the fruit, and the useful sticks.

PIP: That's three things. The form only has room for one.

SABLE: I can add a supplement.

FINN: You can hold the cloth.

[The cloth catches the wind again. Sable drops the clipboard to tie it down. Mara's camera operator slips on a wet leaf, but Finn catches the camera before it falls.]

MARA: That was a beautiful unscripted moment.

FINN: He was holding the camera with one hand and the lighting stand with the other. Give him the umbrella.

[Sable hands the operator her own umbrella. The seating chart floats away.]

SABLE: We can rebuild the chart.

FINN: We know where everybody is.

PIP: Then you are married.

MARA: The couple chose the natural setting.

SABLE: They chose it after my chart sank.

[The camera zooms in on the wet clipboard.]

MARA: We need one last shot for the reveal.

FINN: The reveal is us standing under a leaf with the guests.

SABLE: The shot list says the couple should walk through the aisle.

FINN: The aisle is a puddle.

PIP: I can bless the puddle.

SABLE: Please don't. The microphones are plugged in.

[The camera operator backs away from the puddle. The cloth finally tears free and floats down over the chair chart.]

MARA: That can be the closing image.

FINN: No. Take a picture of the guests helping each other.

[Sable looks at the muddy chart and the stones. She puts the clipboard away.]

SABLE: I'll send the invoice without charging for the umbrella arch.

FINN: That is the first thing today that sounds romantic.

[BLACKOUT.]
