# The Colony's First Podcast

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: amateur podcasting that mistakes microphone equipment and a long intro for compelling conversation*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Dena, a gibbon host; Piri, a macaque sound editor; Tavi, a squirrel monkey guest; and Odo, a capuchin co-host.

INT. RECORDING NOOK - AFTERNOON

Dena sits before a microphone. Odo wears headphones. Piri holds up a card that says INTRO.

DENA: Welcome to our new podcast about the grove.

ODO: We have not chosen a name.

DENA: Welcome to the podcast formerly known as the grove.

Piri starts recording. Odo taps the microphone to test it. The sound booms through the headphones. Tavi enters carrying a basket.

TAVI: Is this where I leave the figs?

DENA: You are our first guest.

Piri asks him to move closer to the microphone. Tavi places the basket directly in front of it. The microphone picks up the figs rolling together.

ODO: The basket has a strong voice.

They record an introduction. Dena reads a paragraph about the grove. Odo asks if they can begin the topic. Piri says they need a theme sound. Tavi taps a hollow log. It makes a clear knock. Odo taps it too, producing a dull thud. They argue about which sound is better.

Piri records both and calls them the intro and outro. Dena asks Tavi about carrying fruit. He describes the path, the slope and how he balances the basket. Odo interrupts to ask whether the basket has a name. Tavi says no.

ODO: What if it did?

TAVI: Then I would carry it the same way.

Piri gestures that this is good audio. Dena asks Tavi to repeat it more slowly. He does, and the words sound like a formal interview. The natural version was funnier.

The recording timer runs out. Piri says they have captured forty minutes. Dena asks how much can be used. Piri says two minutes after edits. Odo tries to add a sponsor message for the community water bowl. He reads it in a dramatic whisper. The bowl is audible in the room as someone drinks.

Tavi picks up the basket. The figs shift again, making a soft rhythm. Piri asks him to leave it by the microphone. Dena wants to record another take. Tavi says he has to deliver the fruit.

DENA: Can you come back next week?

TAVI: The path will still be here.

He leaves. The door closes with a loud thump, the best sound of the session. Piri marks it for the outro. Odo asks what the episode is about.

DENA: Carrying fruit.

ODO: We did not say that once.

Piri plays the short clip of Tavi's natural answer. Everyone listens. It lasts eight seconds. Dena names the episode "The Basket Has a Strong Voice." The basket remains in the recording nook, holding the microphone cable in place.
