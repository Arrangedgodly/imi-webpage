# The Hotel Concierge Books a Room for the Luggage

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury hotel service conventions displacing guests for their belongings*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Concierge Sela, a macaque; guest Nari, a lemur; porter Pip, a capuchin; manager Vex, an orangutan.

INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

[Pip pushes a luggage cart with one small suitcase. Nari approaches the desk. Sela wears a gold concierge sash.]

NARI: I have a reservation under Nari.

SELA: Of course. And your suitcase?

NARI: It is coming with me.

SELA: We have a separate arrival experience for luggage.

PIP: It arrived before she did.

VEX: A loyal guest.

[Nari reaches for the suitcase. Sela steps between her and it.]

SELA: We can offer the suitcase a room with a view.

NARI: I need the room.

VEX: The room is for you. The view is for the case.

[Nari follows them to a brass luggage scale. Pip places the case on it. The needle climbs, then sticks.]

PIP: The suitcase is over the weight limit.

NARI: It has one shirt.

SELA: It may be carrying emotional baggage.

[Vex checks the scale. The cart's front wheel is resting on the platform, adding its weight. Pip lifts the wheel away. The needle settles below the limit.]


[Vex points to a placard on the cart: SUITE SERVICE.]

NARI: That says suite.

SELA: A word chosen for its premium feeling.

PIP: It is a cart.

VEX: The cart is a mobile room for luggage.

[Nari opens her case. Sela places a folded turndown card on the shirt.]

SELA: We left a note for your case.

NARI: It cannot read.

PIP: I can read it to the case.

VEX: That is the level of service we promise.

NARI: I am still the guest.

PIP: The case fits the scale now.

SELA: We will upgrade it.

[Nari takes the suitcase and heads for the elevator. Vex hands her a room key. Sela hands Pip another key.]

NARI: Why does the suitcase have a key?

SELA: Your case has been granted a quieter floor.

PIP: I can take it up.

NARI: It is staying with me.

[Vex looks at the key.]

VEX: We cannot have you carrying unregistered luggage to a registered room.

NARI: You gave me the registration.

[Sela opens the velvet rope to a tiny alcove marked "Luggage Lounge." Pip parks the cart there. The suitcase tilts; its handle catches the velvet rope and draws it around the alcove like curtains.]

PIP: It has privacy.

NARI: It has my pajamas.

[The elevator dings. Sela offers Nari a luggage tag. She ties it to Nari's wrist.]

SELA: This confirms you are traveling with the case.

NARI: I am the traveler.

VEX: The system lists the suitcase as primary guest.

[Nari takes the tag, ties it to the suitcase handle, and leads it toward the elevator. The rope pulls free and trails behind the cart.]

PIP: The room is on the third floor.

SELA: The case is on the third floor. The guest may accompany it.

[Nari enters the elevator with the suitcase. The doors begin to close.]

NARI: I would like turndown service.

SELA: For you or the case?

NARI: Whichever gets the bed.

[The doors close on the suitcase standing upright beside her.]

VEX: Put down a second towel.
