# The Dragon's Dating Profile Needs a Motive

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: dating profiles that turn ordinary preferences into overconfident heroic claims*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: DRAGON VERA, a dragon; PROFILE COACH NILO, a macaque; MATCHMAKER PELI, a lemur; VISITOR DARA, an orangutan.

[A small castle office. Vera sits before a laptop. Nilo holds up profile cards. Peli has placed a potted cactus between two chairs.]

NILO: What are you looking for?

VERA: Someone who does not run away when I sneeze.

PELI: Honest and specific.

NILO: We could phrase it as "seeking a brave knight."

VERA: I am seeking a handkerchief.

ACT ONE

[Nilo types: MYSTERIOUS FIRE-BREATHER SEEKS FEARLESS COMPANION. Vera leans toward the screen. A little smoke curls from one nostril and sets off the laptop fan.]

VERA: Is there a way to list my interests?

NILO: Hoarding, flying, mountain naps?

VERA: Baking. I keep burning the outside.

[Peli suggests a class. Nilo adds "I enjoy candlelit dinners." Vera points at the phrase.]

VERA: I cannot sit near candles.

NILO: We can say you bring the atmosphere.

[Vera huffs. The cactus bends away from her breath. Peli rotates the pot.]

ACT TWO

[Dara arrives for a profile consultation. Nilo shows them Vera's page. Dara reads it, then looks at Vera across the desk.]

DARA: Do you actually want a fearless knight?

VERA: No. I want someone who will say when the soup is too hot.

[Dara asks what Vera cooks. She describes a vegetable stew heated by a warm stone, not flame. Nilo deletes "fire-breather" and types "mountain cook." Peli asks Vera to describe a good evening. She says a quiet flight at dusk, then tea when the stone cools.]

NILO: That sounds less exciting.

DARA: It sounds like a date.

[Nilo scrolls to the profile photo. It shows Vera roaring over a pile of gold. Dara asks if the gold is her retirement plan. Vera says it is mostly old spoons. Peli suggests a photo with the stew pot. Nilo props the laptop on a chair. Vera tries to smile; the smoke alarm chirps. Dara reaches to silence it, but the device is only a low-battery warning. Nilo adds "comfortable with occasional kitchen beeps" to the profile.]

VERA: That seems more alarming than the sneeze.

[Dara offers to take the photo outside. Vera opens the castle window. Her wings unfold, knocking Nilo's stack of profile cards into the air. Peli catches one. It reads "Must love caves." Vera says she does not require a cave; she likes the mountain overlook. Nilo edits the line.]

ACT THREE

[Nilo reads the revised profile aloud: "Dragon who enjoys cooking, evening flights and honest feedback seeks a companion for tea." Vera approves. Dara asks if the first meeting can be at the overlook. Vera says yes, if they bring a thermos. Nilo begins to add "scenic peril." Peli takes the keyboard from him.]

PELI: The view is enough.

[Dara asks whether they should bring a handkerchief. Vera says that would be thoughtful. Nilo looks at the smoke alarm and asks if it is safe to use the kettle. Vera points to the warm stone on the table. Dara asks how long it takes to heat. Vera says long enough to finish the profile without exaggerating.]

[Nilo reaches for the laptop to upload it. The machine fan starts again. Vera moves the stew pot away from the vent. The fan stops. Dara laughs; Vera does too, gently, and nothing smokes. Peli saves the profile. Nilo reaches for the old "fearless knight" card, but it has landed in the cactus. He uses tongs to retrieve it.]

FINAL IMAGE: Vera and Dara leave with a thermos for their overlook tea, while the deleted heroic profile card hangs from the cactus like a warning label.
