# The Monologue That Wouldn't End

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: late-night talk-show monologues and celebrity desk bits*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: HOST ZAZA, a macaque; WRITER BO, a capuchin; BAND LEADER KES, a gibbon; and an AUDIENCE.

[A late-night desk. Zaza holds a stack of cue cards. The band plays an intro that keeps going.]

ZAZA: Good evening. The town has started a new recycling program. Everyone is excited, except the bin, which has been left at the curb.

[The band plays three more bars.]

ZAZA: We had a joke about the bin.

BO: You used it last week.

ZAZA: I said it was full of old material.

AUDIENCE: Groans.

ZAZA: The council says each household gets one blue bin.

BO: That's the setup.

ZAZA: I am waiting for the punchline.

BO: It is the bin.

ZAZA: A joke should have a turn.

KES: We can add a cymbal crash.

ZAZA: That is a turn for the band.

[The audience laughs at Kes's cymbal.]

ZAZA: The council will also publish pickup times online.

BO: Great. You can miss your bin at home and online.

ZAZA: That works.

[The audience laughs. Zaza looks at the cue cards.]

ZAZA: We have a guest tonight, the recycling coordinator.

BO: She is waiting backstage.

ZAZA: Can we do a joke about her?

BO: She wrote the schedule.

ZAZA: So the bin has a schedule and I don't.

KES: The band has a schedule.

ZAZA: You're all getting stronger material.

[He shuffles the cards. A card sticks to his palm. He tries to shake it off. The audience laughs.]

ZAZA: This card says "wave to camera."

[He waves. The card sticks to his hand and blocks his face.]

BO: That's the best bit.

ZAZA: I am not doing the bit. The card is doing the bit.

[He pulls the card free. It tears in half.]

KES: We can put that in the monologue.

ZAZA: We can put it in recycling.

[He drops it in the blue bin beside the desk. The bin tips over, spilling the rest of the cards.]

BO: We have a callback.

ZAZA: We have a mess.

[Zaza picks up the cards one by one. The audience helps pass them forward.]

ZAZA: Thank you. Our next guest is the person who wrote the pickup schedule. She will be here as soon as we sort the jokes.

[The band plays a short sting. This time, Zaza signals them to stop.]

ZAZA: That's enough music.

[The bin rolls offstage with the cards.]
[A buzzer sounds from backstage.]

ZAZA: The recycling coordinator is here.

BO: She has brought a bin.

ZAZA: Does it have a punchline?

BO: It has two sections.

[The coordinator rolls the bin onto the stage. The blue side is labeled PAPER; the green side is labeled PEELS.]

ZAZA: That's clear.

COORDINATOR, off: Please don't put cue cards in the peel side.

[Zaza looks at the cards scattered on the floor.]

ZAZA: I was going to recycle them.

BO: Which side?

ZAZA: I need another joke.

KES: The audience is waiting.

ZAZA: The bin has more categories than my monologue.

[The audience laughs. The coordinator wheels the bin away.]

ZAZA: Good night. Put your jokes in the right place.

[The bin rolls back onto the stage by itself..]

[BLACKOUT.]
