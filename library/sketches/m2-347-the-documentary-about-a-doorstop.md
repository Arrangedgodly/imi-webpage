# The Documentary About a Doorstop

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: nature documentaries that narrate an object's ordinary purpose as a dramatic survival story*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: narrator Leni, a gibbon; camera operator Miri, a capuchin; caretaker Odo, a baboon; wooden doorstop, a silent prop.

INT. HALLWAY - DAY

A door slowly closes. Odo wedges a wooden doorstop beneath it.

LENI (V.O.): In a narrow corridor, one small wedge stands against the force of a heavy door.

MIRI: The door needed to stay open.

ODO: I am moving a cart through.

Leni narrates the wedge's struggle. Odo pushes the cart through. A gust from the open window makes the door press harder against the stop.

LENI: The pressure increases.

Miri zooms in on the wedge. It slides one inch. Odo stops the cart and pushes the door wider. He adjusts the wedge and tests it with his foot.

ODO: It is stable now.

Leni asks Odo to step away for a close-up. The door begins closing. Miri keeps filming. The wedge holds. Leni calls it a contest of patience.

Odo returns and picks up the wedge. The door closes quietly.

LENI: The creature has disappeared.

ODO: I put it in my pocket.

Miri asks Leni if they should track it. Odo opens his pocket and sets the doorstop on the floor. The door is held open again. Leni says it has returned to its territory.

A second door begins to close down the hall. Odo carries the wedge over. Miri follows with the camera. Leni narrates a migration across a landscape of carpet.

The wedge fits beneath the second door. Odo lets go. It stays open. Miri asks if there will be a sequel.

A cart arrives with gardening tools. Odo asks Miri to film the route through the doorway. The door swings shut behind the cart, but the stop holds it open. Leni narrates the object's service as if it has defended the corridor from a closing threat. Odo says the stop has performed exactly as expected. Miri asks if it should be tested with a heavier door. Odo says one door is enough. The stop shifts under pressure, so he moves it closer to the hinge and checks again. It holds. Leni asks whether this marks the end of the documentary. Odo says the job is complete. He removes the stop and puts it in the supply drawer, where several other doorstops wait. The camera pans across the drawer as Leni describes a community of wedges.

The caretaker closes the drawer and takes the cart down the hall.

The door stays open until the cart is through.

Miri thanks Odo for the demonstration.

Odo slides the doorstop back into the drawer.

TAG

Leni whispers, "The doorstop has found a new home."

Odo takes it back to the first door.

ODO: It belongs in the supply drawer.
