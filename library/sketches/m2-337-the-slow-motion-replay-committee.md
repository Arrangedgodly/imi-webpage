# The Slow-Motion Replay Committee

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports review panels that analyze tiny moments until nobody remembers the original play*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: analyst Miri, a gibbon; referee Kito, a macaque; player Odo, a baboon; camera operator Leni, a capuchin.

INT. REVIEW ROOM - DAY

A screen shows Odo kicking a ball. It rolls between two stones.

MIRI: We are reviewing whether the ball crossed the line.

KITO: It went through.

ODO: I saw it go through.

Leni slows the footage. The ball stops just before the line, then rolls across.

MIRI: At normal speed it is clear.

KITO: The panel must review the whole sequence.

The footage is slowed further. Odo's foot appears to hover above the grass for several seconds. Kito asks Leni to freeze at contact. She pauses one frame before the ball moves.

ODO: I haven't kicked it yet.

Miri says the frame may show intent. Kito says they are reviewing the line, not the intention. Leni advances one frame. The ball barely shifts.

MIRI: Did it move?

KITO: It did.

The panel watches the ball cross. Miri asks if the line is part of the field. Kito says yes. Odo asks whether the goal counts.

KITO: I will call it after we see the other angle.

[Miri overlays a grid on the frozen image. Its sponsor logo covers the field line.]

MIRI: Can we measure from the edge of the logo?

KITO: The logo is not on the field.

[Leni switches the graphic off. Odo points to the actual painted line beneath it.]

Miri asks for another angle. Leni shows the same play from the other side. A bird flies across the camera and obscures the ball. The panel pauses on the bird.

MIRI: Was the bird in play?

KITO: No.

ODO: It had a better view than I did.

Leni zooms in until the screen shows grass, stone and one blurry feather. Kito says the replay has lost the ball. Miri says they should reset to normal speed. They do. The ball rolls through the stones again, plainly across the line.

KITO: Point awarded.

MIRI: Can we review the review?

KITO: We can, but the next game starts now.

Leni selects the live feed of the next match. The bird is perched behind its goal. Miri asks whether it needs a ruling before play can start. Kito points to the signed decision; the bird is a spectator. Odo asks whether spectators need names. Leni writes "bird" in the attendance margin while Miri requests a close-up of its credentials.

Miri reaches for the replay controls again. Kito turns the monitor toward the next match schedule. The review room lights shut off automatically. Leni rolls the next ball into the equipment basket; its label reads TRAINING ONLY. Miri reaches for the remote, but Kito covers the buttons with the signed ruling. Odo carries the real ball back to the field while Miri and Leni sign the result without watching it again.

TAG

The players return to the field. Odo kicks the ball again. It stops on the line.

MIRI: We may need another committee.
