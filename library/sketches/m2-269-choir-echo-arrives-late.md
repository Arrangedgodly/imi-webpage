# The Choir's Echo Arrives a Measure Late

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grand choral performances that rely on elaborate acoustics and precise echo timing*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Conductor Miri, a gibbon; singer Odo, a baboon; singer Kessa, a macaque; and sound technician Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

A choir stands before a small echo chamber built from polished panels. Tavi tests the sound board. Miri raises her baton.

MIRI: We will sing one note and listen for the echo.

ODO: The room is very small.

TAVI: The delay unit creates a cathedral effect.

KESSA: Is the cathedral effect on?

[Tavi presses a button. Odo sings one note. The echo returns late, after the choir has already stopped.]

ODO: That was my note.

TAVI: It was the room's note.

MIRI: The room is behind.

INT. HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Miri asks the choir to hold a chord. Tavi adjusts the delay. The first echo overlaps the next chord.

KESSA: We have two endings.

ODO: One of them is still holding the first note.

[Tavi turns a dial. The echo becomes louder than the singers.]

MIRI: The hall is leading us.

TAVI: The setting says "grand."

KESSA: It sounds like the hall has a solo.

[The echo returns as the choir takes a breath. Everyone inhales at once; the echo mimics them a beat later, creating a second synchronized gasp.]

ODO: The room is also breathing.

MIRI: We will sing through it.

INT. SOUND BOOTH - LATER

Tavi moves the microphone away from the polished panels. The echo returns sooner but now repeats the conductor's count.

MIRI: One, two, three.

[The speakers say "one, two, three" after her. The choir begins on the echo's count, not hers.]

KESSA: We have two conductors.

ODO: One has a better memory.

MIRI: Turn off the delay.

TAVI: Then we lose the cathedral.

MIRI: We have a community hall.

[Tavi switches off the effect. The singers test a chord. It fills the room naturally.]

INT. SOUND BOOTH - FINAL TEST

Tavi asks the choir to sing a scale. The old echo unit is unplugged, but a second small speaker begins repeating the notes.

TAVI: There is another delay channel.

MIRI: Where?

[Tavi follows the cable to a portable speaker under the conductor stand. Odo lifts it; the cable pulls a music folder off the stand.]

ODO: The room has a backup singer.

KESSA: Does it know the words?

[The speaker repeats only the last note. Miri closes the folder and sets it on the speaker, muffling the sound.]

MIRI: It can sing from under the music.

INT. HALL - PERFORMANCE

The choir sings a short piece. The sound is clear, with a modest natural echo. Miri lowers the baton; the singers stop together.

[One last artificial echo leaks from a speaker: "Together." The singers hold still. Odo bows to the sound booth.]

ODO: Thank you, second conductor.

TAVI: I thought I turned it off.

[He unplugs the delay unit. The echo ends.]

KESSA: Now the room is listening.

MIRI: It can applaud with the rest of us.

[The audience claps. The polished panels send the applause back once, at the right time.]
