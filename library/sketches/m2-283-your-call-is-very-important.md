# Your Call Is Very Important

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: customer-support phone trees, sentimental hold music, and menus that ask callers to identify a problem before they know what it is*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Caller Tula, a macaque; automated voice BEX, a gibbon; support agent Neri, a capuchin; quality monitor Voss, an orangutan; library manager Rumi, a lemur.

INT. SUPPORT ROOM - AFTERNOON

Tula calls the public library help line. The phone plays soft flute music.

BEX: Your call is important. For opening hours, press one. For a book that is late, press two. For a book that is early, please remain on the line.

TULA: Books do not arrive early.

BEX: Your call is important. To hear our menu again, press nine.

Tula presses nine by mistake. The menu restarts in a lower flute key.

BEX: For a missing page, press three. For a page that is present but difficult to find, press four.

TULA: I need to report a wet book.

BEX: For a book damaged by rain, press one. For a book damaged by tea, press two. For a book damaged by an unknown liquid, press three.

Tula presses three. The flute music changes to a suspenseful plucked string. A light flashes in the support room. Neri looks at her queue screen.

NERI: That caller has selected unknown liquid. We need the advanced support chair.

VOSS: It is the same chair.

NERI: It swivels.

She answers.

NERI: Thank you for calling the library. What happened to the book?

TULA: I set it beside the sink. The sink dripped.

NERI: Was it a slow drip or a fast drip?

TULA: It was a sink drip.

NERI: I can record that as slow.

VOSS: Ask whether she wants to replace the book or dry it.

NERI: Would you like to replace the book, dry the book, or speak to a supervisor about the concept of a book?

TULA: I would like to return it and pay the drying fee.

NERI: There is no drying fee.

TULA: Then why did the menu ask about liquid?

NERI: The menu was designed to route wet-book cases.

VOSS: It routes them in circles.

Neri looks at the policy screen. It recommends that a damp book be placed flat with absorbent paper. Tula can bring it to the desk. Neri creates an appointment, but the calendar has no category for wet books.

NERI: I will book you under "other." The appointment is at 3:00.

TULA: It is 3:10.

NERI: Then I will book you for 3:00 yesterday.

VOSS: That may cause an error.

NERI: It did. The calendar says you are already here.

TULA: I can walk to the library now.

NERI: That is the fastest option.

BEX: Your call is very important. To end your call, press one. To continue holding, press two.

Tula hangs up. Tula arrives carrying the damp book. At the desk, Neri lays absorbent paper between the wet pages. The library manager notices the phone tree still playing flute music in the support room.

MANAGER: Did we resolve the caller's issue?

NERI: Yes. We told her to bring the book.

MANAGER: Then you can remove the nine-step menu.

BEX: Your menu is important. To hear the shorter menu, press one.

Voss unplugs the phone. The flute stops. Neri picks up a ringing desk bell.

NERI: For a wet book, come to the desk.

BEX: Your call is no longer important.

NERI: It is still important. It just doesn't need flute music.
