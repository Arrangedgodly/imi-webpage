# The Food Stall Has a Tasting Flight

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: food festivals that dress up a single snack with tasting notes and ceremony*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Vendor Suri, a macaque; Festival Critic Dema, a gibbon; Customer Olo, a capuchin; and Organizer Venn, a baboon.

EXT. FOOD FESTIVAL - DAY

Suri serves roasted corn from a small stall. Dema carries a tasting notebook. Venn checks a row of tiny plates.

DEMA: Please describe the corn's opening aroma.

SURI: It smells roasted.

DEMA: Any notes of wood, smoke, or late summer?

SURI: It smells like the grill.

OLO: Can I have one?

VENN: First, choose a tasting flight of three kernels.

OLO: I want an ear of corn.

VENN: The flight is the festival format.

SURI: I can serve a full ear after the flight.

DEMA: We need to preserve the tasting sequence.

OLO: I can preserve the corn.

[SURI WRAPS AN EAR IN PAPER. THE PAPER IS STAMPED "THREE-COURSE SAMPLE."]

VENN: That is a packaging violation.

SURI: It is a napkin.

DEMA: Is the paper toasted?

SURI: No. The corn is.

OLO: Can I eat the sample?

VENN: You have to describe it first.

[SURI SERVES THREE KERNELS ON A SPOON. OLO EATS THEM.]

OLO: Good. Can I have the corn now?

INT. FOOD FESTIVAL TENT - LATER

Dema writes notes while Suri turns corn on the grill. Venn places a small bell at the counter.

DEMA: The texture is firm, with a bright finish.

SURI: It has salt and lime.

DEMA: The lime is bright.

OLO: I can taste the salt.

VENN: Please ring the bell before requesting a second sample.

[OLO RINGS THE BELL. IT DOES NOT SOUND. VENN POLISHES IT WITH A CLOTH.]

VENN: Try again.

OLO: I would like a second sample.

SURI: Here you go.

[THE BELL FINALLY RINGS, AFTER THE CORN HAS BEEN SERVED.]

DEMA: That sound pairs well with the lime.

EXT. FOOD FESTIVAL - CLOSING

Dema presents a review card. Suri hands Olo a full ear of corn. Venn removes the tiny plates.

DEMA: I award the stall four stars for depth and a fifth for the bell.

SURI: The bell is not food.

OLO: The corn is.

DEMA: We should assess the corn at serving temperature.

SURI: It is warm.

VENN: The tasting plates are cold.

OLO: The corn cools while we write.

[SURI HANDS OLO A FRESH EAR. DEMA LEANS IN TO TAKE NOTES BEFORE THE FIRST BITE.]

DEMA: The aroma is rising.

OLO: The steam is rising.

SURI: It is hot. Hold it by the husk.

OLO: Thank you for the warning.

[OLO BITES THE CORN. A KERNEL POPS LOOSE AND LANDS IN DEMA'S NOTEBOOK.]

DEMA: A surprise texture.

SURI: A kernel.

VENN: Does it count as a sample?

OLO: It counts as lunch.

DEMA: I will write that in the review.

[DEMA CLOSES THE NOTEBOOK. THE KERNEL FALLS OUT AND LANDS IN THE TASTING SPOON.]
