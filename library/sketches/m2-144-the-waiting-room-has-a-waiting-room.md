# The Waiting Room Has a Waiting Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: medical appointment administration and recursive waiting systems in a community clinic*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Receptionist Tavi, a macaque; patient Loma, a gibbon; nurse Fenn, a capuchin; and line coordinator Odo, a baboon.

[A CLINIC SIGN READS "CHECK IN HERE." A SECOND SIGN BEHIND THE COUNTER READS "WAIT FOR CHECK-IN."]

LOMA: I have an appointment.

TAVI: Please wait in the waiting room.

LOMA: I am in it.

TAVI: This is the check-in room.

[LOMA STEPS THROUGH A DOOR. A SMALLER ROOM HAS A BENCH AND A SIGN: "WAIT FOR CHECK-IN."]

LOMA: This room is also a waiting room.

ODO: It is the waiting room for the check-in room.

LOMA: Do I need an appointment to wait?

TAVI: You need your appointment number.

[LOMA SHOWS A CARD.]

TAVI: That is the number for the appointment line.

ODO: You need a waiting room number.

[FENN HANDS HER A TICKET.]

LOMA: It says B.

TAVI: B is the room.

[THEY WALK TO A THIRD DOOR MARKED "B." INSIDE IS A BENCH AND AN EMPTY CLIPBOARD.]

LOMA: Is this the appointment room?

FENN: It is the waiting room for the nurse.

[THE NURSE CALLS A NAME.]

FENN: Loma?

LOMA: Yes.

TAVI: You have to answer from the check-in room.

[LOMA WALKS BACK THROUGH TWO DOORS.]

LOMA: I am here.

FENN: You were called.

LOMA: I was in room B.

ODO: Room B is not where you wait to be called.

[ODO POINTS TO A SIGN: "PLEASE REMAIN WHERE YOU ARE." THE SIGN IS IN THE HALLWAY.]

LOMA: Where should I remain?

TAVI: In the waiting room.

[THE DOORS ALL SWING OPEN. EVERYONE CAN SEE ONE ANOTHER.]

FENN: I can call patients from here.

TAVI: That changes the process.

ODO: It saves the walk.

[LOMA SITS ON THE BENCH. A MONKEY ENTERS, SEES THE OPEN DOORS, AND SITS BESIDE HER.]

TAVI: Do you have an appointment?

[THE MONKEY HANDS OVER A BANANA.]

ODO: It may be a check-in item.

FENN: It is a snack.

[THE NURSE LOOKS AT THE CARD.]

FENN: Loma, we are ready now.

LOMA: I have been ready for three rooms.

TAVI: We can check Loma in from this counter.

ODO: That removes two doors.

LOMA: And one number?

TAVI: We can keep the appointment number and skip the room letter.

[ODO TAKES DOWN THE EXTRA ROOM SIGN. THE DOOR BEHIND IT IS THE EXAM ROOM.]

FENN: That will make the call easier.

LOMA: I can hear you from the bench.

[THEY OPEN THE EXAM ROOM DOOR. IT LEADS BACK TO THE FIRST WAITING ROOM.]

LOMA: I have returned.

FENN: The exam room is the next door.

[THE MONKEY OPENS THE CORRECT DOOR AND WALKS IN.]

TAVI: The patient has checked in.

ODO: The line has been bypassed.

[FENN INVITES LOMA THROUGH THE OPEN DOOR. THE SIGNS FALL OFF THE WALL.]
