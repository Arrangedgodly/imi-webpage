# The Ribbon Must Be Cut Before the Fountain Flows

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: civic dedication ceremonies that keep a public amenity unusable until every official speech is complete*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Pera, a gibbon organizer; Domi, a lemur engineer; Yali, a macaque mayor; Nox, a capuchin child.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - NOON

[A SMALL DRINKING FOUNTAIN IS COVERED BY A CLOTH. A RIBBON BLOCKS THE BASIN. YALI HOLDS SCISSORS.]

YALI: We are here to dedicate the fountain.

NOX: Can I drink from it?

PERA: Not until after the dedication.

DOMI: The fountain is ready.

Pera checks the ceremony plan. First comes the welcome, then the history, then the ribbon cut, then the first drink. The plan says the first drink must be taken by the mayor.

YALI: I will take the first sip.

Nox looks at the fountain. A drop leaks from beneath the cloth and lands in the basin.

Nox reaches for it. Pera blocks him.

PERA: It is not officially open.

DOMI: The water is on.

Pera says the ribbon must be cut first. The scissors are tied to the ribbon with a decorative bow. Yali pulls the bow loose with his free hand. The scissors slip from the loosened loop and fall into the basin. Domi fishes them out with a long-handled wrench.

YALI: Can we continue?

Pera checks the schedule. The history speech is next. She begins describing the empty square before the fountain. A breeze blows the cloth over her face.

PERA: The square was once a place where people gathered.

Nox pulls the cloth free. It lands on the grass. The fountain is visible. Pera tries to cover it again, but Domi says the water will splash the fabric.

DOMI: The pump is running.

YALI: Then the dedication is working.

Pera suggests a ribbon cut. Yali positions the scissors, but the ribbon has sagged into the basin. Domi lifts it with the wrench. The water splashes onto Yali's sleeve.

YALI: The fountain has touched its first official.

Nox asks if the drink is allowed now. Pera says the mayor must drink first. Yali leans over; his enormous ceremonial hat hits the spout before his mouth reaches the water. Domi suggests the child test the spout. Pera pencils "honored guest" over "mayor" on the plan, then consults her guest list.

Nox is not on the guest list.

Yali gives him the scissors and asks him to cut the ribbon. Nox snips it in one motion. Domi catches both ends with the wrench and puts them aside. Nox presses the drinking button; the spout makes a clear arc above the basin.

Nox drinks and releases the button. Pera checks the plan again, rubbing off her own pencil amendment.

PERA: It says the mayor should go first.

YALI: He was the guest of honor.

Nox wipes his mouth and hands the scissors back. A stray drip falls from the spout.

Domi holds the button to test the stream. Nox takes a paper cup from the supply table and sets it beneath the arc for the mayor. Pera pulls the soaked ceremony plan away from the splash and tries to read the last item. The ink has run, so the remaining instruction is just an arrow pointing toward the fountain.

[PERA UNFOLDS THE CEREMONY PLAN TO BLOCK THE SPLASH. YALI TAKES HIS FIRST SIP FROM THE CUP. PERA TRIES TO RECORD THE EXACT TIME ON THE SOAKED PLAN; THE DRIPPING PAPER WRITES A BLUE LINE DOWN HER SHOE.]
