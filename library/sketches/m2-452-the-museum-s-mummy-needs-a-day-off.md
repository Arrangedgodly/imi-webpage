# The Museum's Mummy Needs a Day Off

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum tours, preservation rules, and visitors who assume every exhibit is available for a dramatic reveal*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: GUIDE, a gibbon; MUMMY PERFORMER, a macaque; CONSERVATOR, an orangutan; VISITOR, a baboon; INTERN, a capuchin.

[A museum gallery. A mummy costume stands behind a rope. The guide begins a tour.]

GUIDE: This exhibit represents a fictional ancient ruler.

VISITOR: Can it wave?

CONSERVATOR: It is a costume on a stand. It cannot wave.

[The mummy shifts slightly. The intern looks at it.]

INTERN: The exhibit moved.

MUMMY PERFORMER: My left foot is asleep.

GUIDE: The fictional ruler is taking a break.

VISITOR: I thought it was preserved.

CONSERVATOR: The performer inside is not.

[The conservator opens the back seam. The performer steps out, leaving the costume supported by the stand.]

MUMMY PERFORMER: I need water and a chair. The bandage wrap is tight around my elbows.

GUIDE: The tour has reached the rest period.

[The visitor points to the display label.]

VISITOR: The label says "eternal ruler."

CONSERVATOR: The costume is eternal. The actor has a shift ending at four.

MUMMY PERFORMER: It is four now.

[The intern places a chair behind the rope, then realizes it is on the visitor side. The conservator asks visitors to stay behind the line. The performer cannot reach the chair.] 

GUIDE: The ruler cannot cross the rope.

MUMMY PERFORMER: I can step out of the costume and cross the rope.

CONSERVATOR: The exhibit is on break. The actor is not an exhibit.

[The guide moves the chair to the staff area. The performer drinks water. The visitor tries to take a photo of the empty costume. A flash goes off; the conservator covers the label.] 

CONSERVATOR: No flash. The fabric is sensitive.

VISITOR: Does the ruler mind?

MUMMY PERFORMER: I mind. I am right here.

[The visitor lowers the camera. The guide offers to explain the costume's materials instead. The conservator demonstrates a loose linen strip with a replica sample, not the costume.] 

GUIDE: The wrap is layered for the stage. It is not an authentic burial object.

MUMMY PERFORMER: And I can move when I am wearing it.

INTERN: Does that mean the ruler is alive?

MUMMY PERFORMER: It means the actor has a body.

[The conservator helps the performer loosen one wrap, then hangs the costume back on its stand. The guide turns the exhibit light down for the break.]

VISITOR: Will it be back after the break?

MUMMY PERFORMER: I will. The costume is staying here.

GUIDE: The gallery remains open, but the character is off duty.

[The intern places a small card on the rope: "MUMMY AT REST UNTIL 4:15."]

The guide tells the next group the costume is a stage replica and the performer has taken a break. The visitor asks whether the exhibit changes after four fifteen. The conservator says the same costume returns, but the person inside may be different on the next tour. The intern checks the cup, fills it from the staff fountain, and sets it outside the rope.

MUMMY PERFORMER: Thank you. I will be back after water.

GUIDE: The gallery can wait.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty costume rests on its stand while the performer sits behind the rope with a cup of water.
