# The Retreat Has No Agenda

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate retreats and the demand to optimize unstructured bonding*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: FACILITATOR ORA, a gibbon; MANAGER PELL, a macaque; STAFFERS DEX and MIRI, capuchins.

[A clearing. A flip chart reads: UNSCHEDULED CONNECTION, 9:00-9:15.] 

ORA: Welcome to the retreat. We have fifteen minutes of unstructured bonding.

DEX: What should we do?

ORA: Whatever comes naturally.

MIRI: Is there a prompt?

ORA: The prompt is to be present.

PELL: Should we record our insights?

ORA: Not during unstructured time.

PELL: When do we record them?

ORA: In the reflection session.

DEX: When is that?

ORA: After the bonding activity.

MIRI: Is the bonding activity the unstructured time?

ORA: Exactly.

[THE GROUP STANDS QUIETLY. A BIRD CALLS.] 

DEX: I like that bird.

MIRI: It is sitting on the flip chart.

PELL: Should we write down the bird's contribution?

ORA: We are not documenting this moment.

PELL: I can remember it.

ORA: Please remember it without trying.

[THE WIND BLOWS THE FLIP CHART OVER.] 

MIRI: The schedule is on the ground.

ORA: Let the agenda go.

PELL: We need the agenda for the next activity.

DEX: I can pick it up.

ORA: That would be a useful act of connection.

MIRI: I can hold the chart while you do.

ORA: There are five minutes left in the unscheduled bonding block.

PELL: Should we use them to reflect on what happened?

ORA: Reflection has its own time.

DEX: We can sit on the ground and let the bird decide whether to come back.

MIRI: That is still unstructured.

PELL: I feel uneasy without a prompt.

ORA: Notice the feeling.

PELL: I have noticed it.

ORA: You do not need to write it down.

[THE BIRD RETURNS AND PECKS AT THE STONE HOLDING THE PAPER.] 

DEX: The bird is looking under the chart.

MIRI: Maybe it wants to see the schedule.

PELL: It cannot read.

ORA: We have not asked.

[THE BIRD TUGS A CORNER. THE STONE SLIDES, BUT THE PAPER STAYS DOWN.] 

DEX: The stone needs to be larger.

MIRI: We could move it to the center.

[THEY MOVE THE STONE TO THE MIDDLE OF THE FLIP CHART.] 

PELL: The schedule is now pinned and the bird has left.

ORA: How do you feel?

PELL: I feel that the paper will not blow away.

DEX: That is a good result.

ORA: We have one minute. We can spend it in silence.

MIRI: The chart is still readable.

PELL: We should leave the stone there.

[THE GROUP SITS. THE WIND BLOWS. THE CHART STAYS IN PLACE.]

[THEY HOLD THE CHART TOGETHER. THE BIRD FLIES OFF.] 

PELL: We have bonded through shared paper management.

ORA: It happened naturally.

DEX: We should keep the paper from blowing away.

MIRI: There is a stone beside the path.

ORA: Are you proposing a team exercise?

MIRI: No. I am putting a stone on the chart.

[STONE SETS DOWN. THE PAPER STAYS PUT.] 

PELL: The next session is called "Discover Your Collaboration Style."

DEX: We have already discovered it.

ORA: What is it?

MIRI: We hold down the paper before the wind takes it.

ORA: That is not on the worksheet.

PELL: Then the worksheet can stay blank.
