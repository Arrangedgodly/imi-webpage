# The Pet Parade Requires Identification

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: parade registration rules that demand documentation from animals who cannot complete the forms*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Fara, a gibbon registrar; Niko, a macaque handler; Pippa, a capuchin judge; Muru, a lemur dog.

EXT. TOWN PARADE CHECK-IN - MORNING

[A TABLE HOLDS ENTRY FORMS. A SIGN READS PET PARADE, PROOF OF IDENTITY REQUIRED. NIKO STANDS WITH MURU ON A LEASH.]

FARA: Your pet needs an entry number.

NIKO: His name is Muru.

FARA: Does he have a number?

NIKO: He has a collar.

Fara checks the form. It asks for the pet's signature. Niko gives her a paw-print card. Fara says the print must be witnessed.

PIPPA: I can witness it.

FARA: You are judging the parade.

PIPPA: I have seen the paw.

Muru sniffs the clipboard and noses it onto the ground. The forms scatter. Niko gathers them. The page asking for proof of identity has stuck to the leash.

NIKO: This is his leash.

FARA: It says "handler signature."

Niko signs. Muru tugs the leash, pulling Niko toward the parade route.

PIPPA: The pet is ready.

FARA: We haven't confirmed the costume.

Muru wears no costume. Fara says every entry needs a theme. Niko suggests "dog." Pippa says the theme needs to be visible from the reviewing stand.

Fara pins a paper badge to the collar. Muru shakes. The badge flies off and lands on a bicycle in the next lane.

FARA: We need to retrieve his number.

Niko leads Muru to the bicycle. Muru sits beside it. The bicycle's owner asks if Muru is an entry in the bicycle parade.

PIPPA: There is no bicycle parade.

The owner says the badge is stuck to the seat. Niko peels it off. The paper tears in half. Fara assigns a new number. Muru eats the corner of the form while Niko signs again.

FARA: The form is not a snack.

NIKO: He is verifying it.

The judges line up the entries. Muru sits at the start line and will not move. Pippa asks Niko to encourage him. Niko shows a treat. Muru walks forward, towing the registration table by its cloth.

FARA: The table is not in the parade.

PIPPA: It is following the entry.

Muru reaches the reviewing stand. The table catches the paper banner and pulls it down. The crowd applauds. Pippa awards Muru the prize for best themed entry.

FARA: The theme is "dog"?

PIPPA: The entry brought the whole registration process with him.

Fara tries to recover the table. Muru sits on its cloth and will not move. Niko offers the leash, then the torn paper badge, then a treat. Muru takes the treat and lets the table roll forward. Niko picks up the badge and holds it low; Muru carries it gently in his mouth toward the painted finish line.

NIKO: He is bringing the finish line to the judges.

FARA: The finish line is painted on the street.

PIPPA: Then we have two finish lines.

[AT THE FINISH LINE, NIKO TAKES THE BADGE FROM MURU'S MOUTH AND HANDS IT TO FARA. SHE STAMPS IT "IDENTIFIED BY ACTION."]
