# The Orchard's Scarecrow Gets the Harvest Award

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: rural award ceremonies, judging panels, and ceremonial winners who cannot be removed from their display*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: JUDGE, a macaque; FARMER, an orangutan; SCARECROW, a capuchin; HOST, a baboon; HELPER, a gibbon.

[A harvest fair. A scarecrow stands beside the apple table wearing a blue ribbon. The host holds a microphone. The judge checks a clipboard.]

HOST: The award for best orchard helper goes to the scarecrow.

FARMER: It scared off the birds.

JUDGE: It also scared three judges.

SCARECROW: I am available for interviews.

[The helper turns the scarecrow to face the audience. Its hat falls off. The host places the award ribbon on the hat instead.]

ACT ONE

[The judge asks the scarecrow how it improved the harvest. The farmer answers for it. The scarecrow's sleeve flaps in the breeze and knocks an apple from the display.]

HOST: A demonstration of its technique.

JUDGE: The apple is bruised.

FARMER: The birds are still afraid.

[The host tries to move the scarecrow toward the stage. Its post is stuck in the dirt. The helper loosens the base, but the scarecrow leans forward and bows too deeply.]

SCARECROW: I thank the committee.

JUDGE: You are not speaking for the committee.

[The judge begins a second category: best dressed. The scarecrow wins again because it wears the largest hat. The farmer accepts the ribbon, but the scarecrow asks to receive it personally. The helper clips it to the coat, causing the scarecrow to tilt sideways.]

HOST: The winner is modest.

SCARECROW: I am off balance.

[The farmer props the post with a crate. The judge inspects the scarecrow's sleeves and discovers a crow has built a nest inside one. The scarecrow turns slightly. A twig falls into the judge's hat.]

JUDGE: Is the nest part of the entry?

FARMER: It is an occupancy award.

[The host asks the crowd to applaud. The scarecrow's loose sleeve flaps again. The apples roll toward the stage. The helper catches the crate, but the ribbon drops into the harvest basket.]

ACT TWO

[The judge changes the award to "Best Shared Habitat." The farmer places the scarecrow on a wider base and leaves the sleeve undisturbed. The host reads the new title. The crow peeks from the sleeve.]

SCARECROW: We accept.

JUDGE: The bird has not been entered.

FARMER: It is the co-winner.

[The volunteer reads the judging notes aloud. The scarecrow receives points for protecting the orchard and loses one point for dropping an apple. The crow receives a special ribbon for nest construction. The judge asks whether the co-winners want to share the microphone. The scarecrow's sleeve flaps, and the crow answers with a short caw.]

HOST: We will count that as a joint speech.

JUDGE: It was more concise than mine.

FINAL IMAGE: The blue ribbon hangs from the scarecrow's post while the crow wears the best-dressed ribbon in its nest.
