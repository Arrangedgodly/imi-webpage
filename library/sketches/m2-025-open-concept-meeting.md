# Open Concept Meeting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Corporate brainstorm workshops and sticky-note facilitation*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Facilitator Pella, a gibbon; manager Dorr, a baboon; analyst Lio, a capuchin; note-taker Sumi, a macaque.

[STAGE: A WALL OF STICKY NOTES. A TIMER, A CIRCLE OF CHAIRS, AND A SINGLE MARKER.] 

PELLA: Welcome to our open-concept meeting. The goal is to solve one problem in twenty minutes.

DORR: The problem is that meetings take too long.

LIO: We are in a meeting about that.

SUMI: I will write that on a note.

PELLA: Please use one idea per note.

DORR: My idea is a shorter meeting.

SUMI: That's one note.

LIO: My idea is to decide what we need before we meet.

SUMI: That's two notes.

DORR: My idea needs a committee.

PELLA: What will the committee do?

DORR: Decide what the committee will do.

SUMI: That's one note or two?

PELLA: We have ten minutes. Let's group similar ideas.

LIO: The short meeting and deciding first go together.

DORR: The committee goes with everything.

PELLA: It cannot go with everything.

DORR: It can if we draw enough arrows.

[SUMI DRAWS ARROWS. THE WALL OF NOTES BENDS.] 

SUMI: The wall is sagging.

LIO: We have too many notes.

DORR: We need a note about the wall.

PELLA: The wall is the meeting.

SUMI: I will write that down.

LIO: Don't. It will make the wall heavier.

DORR: We can move the notes to the other wall.

PELLA: That wall is a window.

DORR: Then we have an open concept.

LIO: We have an open window.

[A DRAFT LIFTS SEVERAL NOTES. SUMI CATCHES ONE WITH HER TAIL.] 

SUMI: The idea about shorter meetings is leaving.

PELLA: Catch it.

LIO: We can decide now. Invite only people who need to make the decision.

DORR: What about everyone who wants to listen?

PELLA: Send them the notes after.

SUMI: How many notes?

LIO: Three.

DORR: Can we keep the committee idea?

PELLA: No.

DORR: Can we keep one arrow?

LIO: One arrow points to the decision.

DORR: Fine.

SUMI: I will write "Short meeting, needed people, one decision."

PELLA: That's the outcome.

DORR: I thought an outcome was a slide.

LIO: The outcome is what happens next.

DORR: What happens next?

PELLA: We leave.

DORR: Is that the decision?

LIO: Yes.

SUMI: I have written it on a note.

DORR: We have completed the meeting in nineteen minutes.

PELLA: The wall is still standing.

SUMI: The window is still open.

DORR: Then we should have another meeting about the draft.

LIO: Close the window.

DORR: We need a quiet room for focus.
LIO: We removed every wall.
DORR: Now the focus can leave in any direction.
LIO: We have a focus bell.
DORR: What does it do?
LIO: It rings whenever somebody says focus.
DORR: Focus.
LIO: There.
DORR: Everyone turned toward the bell.
LIO: The room has brought us together.
DORR: Around a noise.
LIO: That is where most meetings start.

[THE NOTES STICK TO THE WINDOW AND STOP THE DRAFT.]
