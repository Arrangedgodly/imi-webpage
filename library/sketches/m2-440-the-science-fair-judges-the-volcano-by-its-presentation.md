# The Science Fair Judges the Volcano by Its Presentation

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school science fairs rewarding spectacle and display over experiment*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Student Pip, a capuchin; student Nari, a lemur; judge Vex, an orangutan; teacher Mara, a macaque.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

[Two projects share a table. Pip's small model volcano has a clear label: "DOES DOOR AIRFLOW CHANGE ERUPTION HEIGHT?" Beside it, Nari has a paper vane balanced loosely on a pin, free to spin in the draft. Vex wears a judge sash. The gym door stands open.]

VEX: Your volcano needs drama.

PIP: It needs a measurement.

VEX: Can it have both?

MARA: Judges, please evaluate the experiment, not the decorations.

[Vex peers at the volcano.]

VEX: What is the hypothesis?

PIP: With the same mixture, an open door will push the foam higher than a closed door.

VEX: And what is the emotional arc?

PIP: It starts low and goes up.

NARI: That is also the data.

[Vex turns to Nari's vane. It spins slowly in the gym's draft.]

VEX: Where is the volcano?

NARI: I am measuring airflow from the open doors.

VEX: Could you add a mountain?

NARI: The wind doesn't need a mountain.

[The open gym door sends a gust across the room, turning Nari's vane quickly and blowing Vex's scorecards onto Pip's table. One card lands over the volcano.]

PIP: I haven't started.

VEX: The presentation has suspense.

MARA: Remove the cards, then run the test.

[Pip measures one level spoon of baking soda and a marked cup containing 50 milliliters of vinegar for the first trial. He sets out an identical spoonful and an identical 50-milliliter cup for the second. Vex offers him a red paper pennant.]

VEX: Put this on top. It will show the height.

PIP: The ruler shows the height.

VEX: The pennant shows the audience where to look.

[Pip accepts it and places it beside the measuring stick. He pours the marked cup of vinegar into the measured spoonful of baking soda. Foam rises, nudges the ruler, and tips the pennant. The open-door draft catches the pennant and leans it toward Nari's vane.]

NARI: Your eruption has found the wind.

[The vane spins; the pennant flutters. Vex claps.]

VEX: A cross-disciplinary finale.

PIP: It is one test. The foam went 6 centimeters.

MARA: Record that before it dries.

[Vex reaches for a glitter star to place beside the score. Mara moves the star away. Pip writes his measurement. Nari notes that the air was moving.]

NARI: Record the open door beside that number.

PIP: Then I should repeat it with the door closed.

MARA: Good control.

[Vex closes the door. The gym goes quiet. Pip uses the second level spoon of baking soda and second 50-milliliter cup of vinegar, then repeats the test. The foam rises to 4 centimeters.]

PIP: Different result. Does that prove the airflow mattered?

VEX: But the second one was less exciting.

MARA: It gives us a question. We need more trials, and a ruler that does not move.

VEX: The first one answered with a flag.

[Nari places her vane beside the two measurements. It does not move. Pip writes "door open" and "door closed" under the figures.]

PIP: Now the display says what happened.

VEX: Can I judge the flag?

MARA: You can judge the experiment.

[Vex studies the chart, then awards Pip a ribbon. After recording the closed-door result, Mara opens the gym door to let the next judge in. A fresh gust crosses the table and spins Nari's free paper vane; the ribbon lifts straight up like a pennant.]

NARI: The ribbon went up without any baking soda.

PIP: I am not putting a ribbon in my foam chart.

VEX: That is the winner.
