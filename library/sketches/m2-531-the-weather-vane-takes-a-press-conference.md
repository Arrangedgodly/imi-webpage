# The Weather Vane Takes a Press Conference

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: breaking-news press conferences and spokespeople forced to explain a minor equipment failure*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: REPORTER, a macaque; MAYOR, a baboon; VANE, an orangutan; PRESS AIDE, a gibbon; ROOFER, a capuchin.

[A town hall rooftop. A weather vane points west while its arrow spins in the faint breeze. The reporter holds a microphone. The mayor stands before a banner reading WIND UPDATE.]

REPORTER: Is the vane pointing toward the storm?

MAYOR: It is pointing toward the roof.

VANE: I am trying to answer.

PRESS AIDE: We will take three questions, then the mayor must attend a ribbon cutting.

ROOFER: The ribbon is tied to the weather vane.

[The roofer carefully unties it. The vane swings south.]

ACT ONE

REPORTER: The vane has changed direction.

MAYOR: Our position is unchanged.

VANE: I turned because he untied the ribbon.

PRESS AIDE: Please let the mayor finish the sentence.

[The reporter asks whether the town is prepared for rain. The mayor says the town has a plan, then opens a folder full of sun-umbrella plans. The aide discreetly closes it.]

REPORTER: Are those the plan?

MAYOR: Those are the public plan.

ROOFER: The storm is still two counties away.

VANE: I can see the clouds from here.

[The vane leans around to look at the horizon. The mayor tries to turn it back toward the press. The roofer catches the stand before it tips.]

PRESS AIDE: We have a live camera. No one should move the equipment.

[The reporter asks the vane directly for its forecast. The aide reaches for the microphone, but the reporter keeps hold of it. After a pause, the reporter turns and offers it to the vane.]

VANE: Wind from the east, rain after lunch.

REPORTER: That is useful.

MAYOR: We have a second statement. The town will issue an umbrella if it rains.

ROOFER: The supply cabinet contains one umbrella. This is it.

[He holds up the single blue umbrella. The aide's folder contains only printed umbrella plans and a photograph of that same umbrella, not additional stock.]

MAYOR: Then we will issue it in shifts.

[The aide looks into the folder, finds a maintenance photo, and reads the log. The vane was installed backwards. The roofer loosens its base and turns the mounting plate, not the arrow.]

ROOFER: The compass mark faces north. Now the arrow can point where the wind comes from.

VANE: Finally, I can answer without turning my whole body.

ACT TWO

[The roofer secures the bolts and tests the plate with a handheld fan. The vane points east, then settles. The reporter asks the same question again.]

VANE: East wind. Rain after lunch.

MAYOR: We will distribute our one umbrella at noon.

PRESS AIDE: There are six thousand residents.

MAYOR: We will shorten each shift.

[The reporter turns to leave. A tiny breeze rotates the vane toward the mayor, whose loose campaign banner flaps across his face.]

FINAL IMAGE: The weather vane points east while the mayor holds the banner still and waits for his umbrella turn.
