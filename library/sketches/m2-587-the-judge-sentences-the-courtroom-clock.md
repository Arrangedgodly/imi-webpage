# The Judge Sentences the Courtroom Clock

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: courtroom dramas that treat a slow clock as a witness to conspiracy*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: JUDGE RIKO, a macaque; CLERK PAVA, a lemur; ATTORNEY VELA, a gibbon; BAILIFF ODO, an orangutan.

[A courtroom. A large clock ticks loudly. Riko enters wearing a robe and carrying a small gavel.]

RIKO: The court is now in session.

PAVA: The clock says we are early.

VELA: The clock is five minutes slow.

ACT ONE

[Riko asks the clerk to swear the clock in. Pava sets a small book on the witness stand. The clock keeps ticking.]

RIKO: Do you promise to tell the correct time?

[The clock ticks.]

ODO: It has declined to answer.

[Vela says the clock has kept the court waiting all week. Odo says he adjusted it yesterday. Pava checks the maintenance log; the clock has lost five minutes since yesterday. Riko asks whether this is a civil or criminal case.]

VELA: It is a maintenance case.

ACT TWO

[Riko orders the clock to explain itself. Pava climbs a short step stool and inspects its battery cover. It is shut. She checks the minute hand; it is dragging against the folded corner of a paper schedule someone taped to the face.]

PAVA: The clock has been obeying the schedule.

[Odo lifts the schedule edge, which says COURT BEGINS AT NINE. A torn corner has slipped beneath the minute hand, so he leaves it for removal after the clock comes down. Riko asks the clock whether it moved the paper. The clock keeps ticking over the judge's question.]

VELA: It is nervous.

[Odo checks the time on his wristwatch. It is five minutes ahead of the clock. Pava calls the building office, which confirms the wall clock is wrong. Riko asks the bailiff to record the statement.]

ODO: My wristwatch is not an official time source.

RIKO: Then who is?

[Pava consults the court computer. It shows the correct time. Riko asks whether the computer can testify. Pava says it has not been called.]

ACT THREE

[Odo unhooks the clock from the wall and sets it on the clerk's table. It keeps ticking while Pava removes the paper and trapped corner, setting the schedule on the witness stand. The hand moves freely again. Pava sets the minute hand to match the court computer, then checks both displays. No battery change is needed.]

RIKO: The court sentences this clock to one hour of accurate timekeeping.

VELA: Is that consecutive?

[The clock ticks. Pava writes the correction in the maintenance log and removes the witness book from the stand. Odo lifts the clock back onto its wall hook and checks that the minute hand remains clear.]

ODO: The case is dismissed.

[The clock strikes once. The sound is exactly on the hour. Riko lifts the gavel, but waits until the sound finishes.]

RIKO: The court acknowledges the time.

[The clock begins ticking steadily. Vela asks whether the court will hear the next case. Riko says it will, now that everyone knows what time it is. Odo returns the step stool to the closet. Pava tapes the correct start time beside the clock, not over its face. Vela removes the "conspiracy" label from her folder and replaces it with "maintenance." The judge asks the clerk to call the next case. The clock ticks once as she does.]

FINAL IMAGE: The wall clock keeps correct time above the empty witness stand, where the removed paper schedule lies face down.
