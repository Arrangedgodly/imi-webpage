# The Language Lesson for One Word

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: language-learning apps that stretch a tiny vocabulary lesson into a streak-keeping ordeal*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Niko, a gibbon learner; Fara, a macaque tutor; Joss, a capuchin app designer; and Bim, a baboon neighbor.

INT. CLASSROOM NOOK - MORNING

A tablet displays one enormous word: "DOOR." Niko holds a cup of tea. Fara stands by an actual door.

FARA: Today's lesson is one useful word. Door.

NIKO: I know that word.

JOSS: The app says you have a ninety-seven-day streak. We cannot waste it on something you know.

FARA: We can learn how to use it in a sentence.

JOSS: The lesson plan has six stages, three badges, and a surprise review.

NIKO: I only need to ask Bim to close the door.

JOSS: First, tap the speaker icon.

[THE TABLET SAYS "DOOR" IN A GRAND VOICE.]

FARA: Now say it.

NIKO: Door.

JOSS: You were supposed to repeat after the bird.

NIKO: I repeated after the tablet.

JOSS: The bird comes in stage two.

[FARA OPENS THE DOOR. BIM WALKS IN, CARRYING A BOWL OF PAINT.]

BIM: Why is the room so loud?

NIKO: I was learning a word.

BIM: You learned it. The door is open.

INT. CLASSROOM NOOK - MOMENTS LATER

Joss puts a tiny bird icon on the screen. It chirps. Niko looks at the tablet.

JOSS: Choose the phrase that means "please close the door."

NIKO: Please close the door.

JOSS: You have to choose from these tiles.

[THE TILES READ: "THE DOOR IS A DOOR," "A DOOR PLEASES ME," AND "CLOSE PLEASE THE."]

FARA: The last one is in the wrong order.

JOSS: It is a challenge round.

BIM: I can close it.

[JOSS SLIDES A NEW SCREEN BETWEEN BIM AND THE DOOR. IT ASKS HIM TO VERIFY HE IS HUMAN. BIM PRESSES THE PICTURE OF A BANANA.]

JOSS: That is a plantain.

BIM: It is the fruit with the peel. I am pressing it.

NIKO: The door is still open.

JOSS: We need to keep the streak alive.

NIKO: If I stop the lesson, the room stays open.

FARA: Then the lesson is teaching you the wrong thing.

[JOSS SCRAMBLES THE WORD TILES AGAIN. NIKO WALKS TO THE DOOR AND CLOSES IT. A QUIET CLICK.]

NIKO: Door closed.

BIM: Now I can paint without the hallway draft.

JOSS: The app says you skipped the final quiz.

NIKO: Give me one word that means I can leave.

FARA: Goodbye.

JOSS: There is a two-word phrase for that, but you need a premium course.

BIM: The door is free to use.

JOSS: The app has a free trial for doors.

FARA: It ends when you close one.

[THE TABLET COUNTS DOWN A TRIAL PERIOD. THE DOOR STAYS SHUT.]

[NIKO WALKS OUT. THE APP PLAYS ITS STREAK ALARM INTO AN EMPTY ROOM. BIM TURNS THE TABLET FACE DOWN.]
