# The Unboxing of the Empty Box

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: influencer unboxing videos that manufacture suspense around packaging while the contents have already been removed*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Vela, a macaque unboxer; Kito, a capuchin camera operator; Pavo, a gibbon sponsor representative; and Seli, a lemur delivery helper.

INT. VIDEO STUDIO - DAY

Vela sits at a table with a taped box. Kito adjusts the camera. Pavo reads a card that says BUILD ANTICIPATION.

VELA: Today we open a package.

KITO: What is inside?

Vela studies the shipping label. It says "Empty display carton." Seli says the product was delivered separately.

PAVO: Do not reveal that yet.

Vela runs a hand over the cardboard. She describes its texture, color and corners. Kito zooms in. The tape is already cut. Pavo asks them to use the other side, where the seam is intact. Seli turns the box around.

Vela peels the tape slowly. Kito plays a suspenseful drumbeat on the table. The lid opens. Inside is crumpled paper and a smaller empty box.

VELA: A box within a box.

PAVO: This is the reveal.

Seli checks the delivery note. The product, a water bottle, is on the counter behind them. Vela asks if they should show it. Pavo says the video is about unboxing, not the bottle.

They open the smaller box. It contains a paper card with a picture of the bottle. Kito focuses on the card. Pavo asks for a reaction. Vela gasps. Seli takes the actual bottle from the counter and places it beside the box.

VELA: The packaging was protecting the product.

Seli points out there was no product inside. Pavo says the empty carton protects the idea of the bottle. Vela asks if she can drink from it. Pavo says it has not been unboxed on camera. He places it back on the counter.

Kito asks for a closing shot. Vela says the unboxing is complete. Pavo asks her to summarize the experience without mentioning the missing item. Vela holds the empty carton.

VELA: It was light, spacious and easy to recycle.

Seli folds the box flat. Pavo asks her not to recycle it until the sponsor has approved. The cardboard springs open again. Kito films the pop.

KITO: Surprise feature.

The team tries to fold it. The flaps catch, making it look like the box is closing itself. Vela narrates the final transformation. Seli folds it correctly and sets the bottle on top.

PAVO: Can we do another take with a fuller reaction?

VELA: The box is still empty.

Seli drinks from the bottle. Vela asks if that counts as an unboxing. Kito points the camera at her. She shrugs and takes another sip. Pavo sees the bottle is working and ends the shoot.

The folded box goes into recycling. The bottle stays on the table, opened by the person who needed it.
