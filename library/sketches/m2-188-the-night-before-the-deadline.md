# The Night Before the Deadline

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: creative-writing workshops that polish a title and font while a manuscript remains unwritten*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Toma, a gibbon workshop leader; Seli, a macaque writer; Pavi, a capuchin peer; and Nara, a lemur librarian.

INT. LIBRARY MEETING ROOM - EVENING

Toma asks everyone to read one sentence from a story. Seli lays down a blank page with a carefully lettered title: "The Long Road Home."

TOMA: Where is the story?

SELI: I am still deciding what the title means.

Pavi suggests beginning with a character. Seli draws a monkey beside the title. Toma says the monkey needs a desire. Seli gives it a tiny backpack. Nara checks the clock; the library closes in fifteen minutes.

TOMA: What does the monkey want?

SELI: To go home.

PAVI: Where is home?

Seli points to the blank page. The others wait. Toma suggests a first line. Seli writes: "The monkey set out." She reads it aloud. Pavi asks what happens next.

SELI: I was going to choose the font.

Toma says story structure matters. He draws a curve on the board: beginning, middle, ending. Seli asks if the curve can be a road. Toma says yes. She draws a winding road that takes up half the board. Pavi adds a tree. Nara notes that the story now has a setting.

Seli writes: "The monkey walked along a road and saw a tree." Toma asks for a conflict. Pavi says the road might be blocked. Seli adds a fallen branch. Nara asks how the monkey gets home. Seli says it goes around the branch.

TOMA: That is a complete story.

Seli looks at the page. The sentence is still the only one. She asks if she can keep writing tomorrow. Nara says the library closes in ten minutes, but the page can be checked out. Seli places it in a folder. The folder has a cover page, contents page and acknowledgments, all blank.

Pavi reads the sentence again. Toma asks whether the story needs a dramatic title. Seli crosses out "The Long Road Home" and writes "A Monkey Goes Around a Branch." Everyone agrees it is clear.

Nara stamps the folder due next week. The stamp lands on the blank acknowledgments page. Seli says the librarian has become part of the story. Nara asks to be credited only if she has a speaking role. Toma gives her one: "The library is closing." 

Nara says the line, then turns off the lights. Seli picks up the page. The road, tree and branch are visible beneath the title. She adds one final sentence: "The monkey reached home before the library closed."

TOMA: It has an ending.

PAVI: And a deadline.

Seli puts the story in the return slot. Nara stamps it once more, this time on the title. The words "due next week" become the last line.
