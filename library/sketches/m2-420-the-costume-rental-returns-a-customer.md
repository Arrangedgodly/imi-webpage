# The Costume Rental Returns a Customer

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: costume rental shops that focus on inventory codes instead of the person still wearing the outfit*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Clerk Nari, a lemur; renter Vex, a macaque; manager Mara, a gibbon; mascot Pip, a capuchin inside a bear costume.

INT. COSTUME RENTAL SHOP - EVENING

[Pip stands in a large bear suit beside the counter. Vex points at him.]

VEX: I would like to return the bear.

NARI: Is it damaged?

VEX: It has been dancing all day.

[Pip waves one plush paw. Nari scans the tag on the costume.]

NARI: The bear was due at noon.

VEX: It is six.

NARI: Late fee is four coins an hour.

VEX: I am returning it now.

MARA: The renter is inside.

[Vex opens the costume's front flap. Pip's face appears.]

PIP: I am still wearing it.

VEX: I thought you were the bear.

NARI: The bear is the costume.

[Pip tries to remove the head. The foam muzzle catches on his ears. Vex holds the costume while Mara pulls the head upward. The bear's body lifts off Pip's feet, leaving him hanging inside the suit.]

MARA: Stop pulling. The tag is caught.

[She turns the suit. The tag is sewn into the back seam. Nari reads it.]

NARI: Customer responsible for cleaning.

PIP: I have been sweating in here.

VEX: The bear needs a bath.

NARI: The renter needs water.

[Mara puts a bucket under the costume. Pip's arm slips through the sleeve and knocks over a row of hats. Vex catches one. Nari catches another. The bear head falls onto the counter, facing Vex.]

VEX: It is staring at me.

PIP: It has no eyes in there.

[Vex places the bear head on himself. The costume tag swings around and catches his nose.]

NARI: Now you are the late renter.

VEX: I am holding the inventory.

[The shop's return bell rings. Pip finally pulls free. He stands in his undershirt, holding one plush paw.]

MARA: Costume returned.

NARI: Renter returned.

[Pip places the paw in the costume. Nari scans it separately; the reader prints a charge.]

NARI: One missing accessory.

[Pip puts the paw on his own hand.]

PIP: It is not missing.

[Nari scans the bear suit one last time. The reader prints a late fee. Pip taps the bear's belly; a small bell rings.]

NARI: The costume is still checked out.

PIP: I am back in my own clothes.

VEX: The character is still on the rental form.

[Mara searches the clipboard. She finds a line for "bear performer" with no name. Vex points at Pip.]

PIP: I was the bear.

NARI: We have found the customer.

[She charges the fee to Pip. He hands over one coin. The reader prints a second tag and sticks it to the plush paw.]

PIP: That is my paw now.

MARA: You can keep the tag.

FINAL BEAT: Mara hangs the bear suit up. Vex reaches for his coat. Nari scans him and says, "Customer still in costume." Vex discovers the bear head is still on his shoulders.
