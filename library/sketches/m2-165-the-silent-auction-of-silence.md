# The Silent Auction of Silence

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fundraising auctions where hosts sell increasingly abstract experiences and guests compete to misunderstand the lot*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Omi, a macaque auctioneer; Tavi, a squirrel monkey donor; Sela, a gibbon lot manager; and Rell, a baboon bidder.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

Omi stands beside a table with a bell. Sela displays Lot One: one hour of quiet in the grove.

OMI: Bidding starts at one fig.

TAVI: Does the hour include travel?

SELA: It begins when you reach the grove.

Rell raises a paddle.

RELL: Two figs, and I want the quiet delivered here.

Omi asks Sela if quiet can be delivered. She places a folded blanket on the table.

SELA: This represents the grove.

Tavi bids three figs for the blanket. Rell bids four for the hour. Omi rings the bell. The sound interrupts the silence lot.

OMI: Sold.

TAVI: The quiet is over.

Sela announces Lot Two: an uninterrupted story told by a local elder. Rell asks whether the story has a beginning and ending. Omi says it does. Tavi asks if the bidder can choose the ending. Sela explains that the elder chooses it.

RELL: I bid five figs for a story without an ending.

OMI: That is not the lot.

Rell raises his paddle higher. Omi mistakes it for a bid and calls seven figs. Tavi bids eight to stop him. The elder, waiting in the front row, begins telling a story about a paddle that keeps rising.

The audience listens. Omi tries to continue the auction but every word interrupts the story. He lowers the gavel. The elder continues, now describing a monkey who learns to listen.

TAVI: Is the story part of the auction?

SELA: It is now.

Rell offers the five figs anyway. The elder accepts one fig and gives the other four back. Omi presents Lot Three: a personal dedication on the community notice board. Tavi bids one fig to write "Thank you." Rell bids two to write his name larger.

Sela places a small board on the table. The bids keep rising until the board tips over. Everyone catches it. The elder takes a marker and writes the winning dedication: "Please put the chairs back." The bidders look at their scattered seats.

Omi rings the bell and calls the auction complete. Tavi begins stacking chairs. Rell joins him. The elder carries the blanket back to the grove.

RELL: What did we raise?

SELA: Enough for one quiet hour.

Omi takes a fig from the donation bowl. The bell rings again as it settles in. Everyone stops moving to preserve the silence, then Omi's stomach growls.

Tavi raises his paddle for one fig. Rell bids two, then sees the elder has already split the snack with the chair-stacking crew. Omi lowers the bell and marks the lot as shared. Sela adds the final fig to the donation bowl, where it rests without ringing.

OMI: The next lot is a snack.
