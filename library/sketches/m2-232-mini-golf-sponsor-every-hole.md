# Mini Golf Has a Sponsor on Every Hole

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: branded entertainment that interrupts a simple game with product placement and promotional challenges*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Course Manager Venn, a gibbon; Player Seli, a macaque; Promoter Ralo, a capuchin; and Groundskeeper Miri, a baboon.

EXT. MINI GOLF COURSE - DAY

A small windmill sits beside the first hole. Ralo holds a sign for a fizzy drink. Seli lines up a putt.

RALO: Before you putt, say the sponsor phrase.

SELI: I am trying to hit the ball.

VENN: The sponsor bought the windmill.

MIRI: The windmill is blocking the hole.

RALO: It is part of the brand experience.

[THE WINDMILL TURNS. SELI'S BALL ROLLS BACK TO THE TEE.]

SELI: Is that a penalty?

VENN: It is a windmill.

RALO: It is a branded windmill.

VENN: The first hole is supposed to be a straight putt.

RALO: The windmill adds engagement.

MIRI: The ball has returned to the tee.

SELI: I have not moved it.

[RALO HANDS SELI A SMALL FLAG WITH THE DRINK LOGO. IT COVERS THE SCORECARD.]

RALO: Hold that near the hole for the photo.

SELI: I need to see the hole.

VENN: We need to see the score.

EXT. MINI GOLF COURSE - HOLE FOUR

A tiny bridge crosses a dry channel. Ralo has placed a drink cooler beside the putting lane.

RALO: Take a refreshing break before the shot.

SELI: I am not thirsty.

RALO: The cooler is part of the route.

MIRI: The cooler is on the green.

VENN: Move it off the course.

[RALO MOVES IT. THE COOLER'S WHEELS ROLL IT BACK INTO THE LANE.]

SELI: I could putt around it.

[THE BALL HITS THE COOLER, BOUNCES OVER THE BRIDGE, AND LANDS IN THE CUP.]

RALO: A sponsor-assisted hole in one.

SELI: I hit the cooler.

MIRI: The cooler is now in the cup.

EXT. MINI GOLF COURSE - HOLE SEVEN

Venn places a small sponsor flag beside the cup. Miri clears leaves from the lane.

RALO: This hole is sponsored by the fizzy drink. Aim for the logo.

SELI: The logo is behind the hole.

MIRI: The flag is behind the hole.

[SELI PUTTS. THE BALL HITS THE FLAG, BOUNCES BACK, AND ROLLS INTO THE CUP.]

RALO: Another brand assist.

VENN: The flag is a hazard.

SELI: The scorecard says one stroke.

INT. MINI GOLF CLUBHOUSE - LATER

Venn checks the scorecard. Ralo displays a prize cooler. Seli holds the ball.

VENN: Seli won with six strokes.

RALO: The sponsor prize goes to the player who says the phrase.

SELI: I didn't say it.

RALO: Then the prize goes to the cooler.

[MIRI OPENS THE COOLER. IT IS EMPTY.]

MIRI: The cooler has won a cooler.

VENN: The course record still belongs to Seli.

RALO: Can I get a quote?

SELI: I hit the cooler.

[RALO WRITES IT ON THE SIGN. MIRI ROLLS THE EMPTY COOLER BACK OUTSIDE. IT COASTS THROUGH THE WINDMILL AND DROPS A BALL INTO THE FIRST CUP.]
