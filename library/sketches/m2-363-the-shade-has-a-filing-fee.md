# The Shade Has a Filing Fee

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: small-claims court and legal billing applied to a moving patch of shade*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Judge Pelt, a slow loris; Ina, a macaque clerk; Vero, a capuchin claimant; Bex, a gibbon respondent.

EXT. COURTROOM BENEATH A TREE - DAY

[A SHORT BENCH SITS HALF IN SUN, HALF IN SHADE. INA HAS DRAWN A CHALK BORDER AROUND THE COOL PATCH.]

INA: Court is in session for Vero versus Bex, regarding possession of the shaded end of this bench.

VERO: I was sitting there first.

BEX: You were on the sunny end. The shade moved over me.

VERO: The bench is one piece.

BEX: So is the shade.

[PELT ENTERS WITH A FAN AND THREE FORMS.]

JUDGE PELT: Clerk, place the disputed property on the evidence table.

INA: The property is sunlight's absence.

JUDGE PELT: Then place the absence on the table.

Ina and Bex slide the bench into court. The shade remains beneath the tree.

BEX: The bench moved. The shade did not.

VERO: That proves it was attached to my side.

Ina checks the form. A box asks for the object's current address. Vero writes "under the tree." Bex objects that the tree stands on public land. Pelt orders a temporary marker around the shade. Ina moves the chalk ring. A breeze shifts the leaves, and the cool patch slides outside it.

JUDGE PELT: We have lost the property.

BEX: It relocated.

VERO: I request an injunction.

INA: That form costs two leaves.

VERO: I have one.

BEX: I can lend you a leaf. I don't admit ownership.

Pelt asks both parties to describe the shade's shape. Vero says rectangle. Bex says it has a tail. Ina places a grid on the ground. The patch changes shape before she can mark it.

INA: The court cannot measure a moving boundary with a still diagram.

JUDGE PELT: Then the diagram is in contempt.

Bex shifts the bench to make room. Vero grabs the other end. They carry it, but the patch of shade remains beneath the tree. Both sit in the sun and glare upward.

VERO: The court should order the tree to hold still.

JUDGE PELT: The tree is not a party to this case.

INA: It has been named in three filings.

[A LEAF FALLS ON THE FORM. VERO CLAIMS IT AS EVIDENCE. BEX SAYS THE TREE OWNS IT. PELT STAMPS THE LEAF. IT STICKS TO THE INK PAD.]

JUDGE PELT: Exhibit A is now part of the court.

BEX: Can I appeal the leaf?

INA: You may file an appeal. The filing fee is one leaf.

Bex looks at the stuck exhibit. Vero reaches for it, but Pelt raises the ink pad out of reach.

JUDGE PELT: I find the shade is public and subject to movement. The bench is shared. Either party may sit in the shade when it reaches their end.

VERO: What if it reaches both ends?

JUDGE PELT: Then you may both sit.

BEX: What if it reaches neither?

Pelt looks at the bright ground around them.

JUDGE PELT: Then the filing fee was unnecessary.

Vero and Bex carry the bench under a broader branch, each holding one end. The shadow covers the entire seat. Ina draws a new border, and it moves again before she finishes.

INA: Shared bench, shared shade. Case closed.

Pelt slides his chair two inches. A thin beam lands on his papers.

VERO: Judge, you're in my shade.

JUDGE PELT: File a motion.

[INA HANDS VERO THE FORM. ITS TITLE READS MOTION TO MOVE THE SUN. VERO TAKES BEX'S BORROWED LEAF AND STICKS IT TO THE COURTROOM WINDOW, WHERE IT MAKES A PERFECTLY STILL PATCH OF SHADE.]
