# The Customer Service Maze

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: phone support systems that require callers to navigate menus about the problem they are trying to solve*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Nara, a gibbon customer; Kito, a macaque support agent; Vela, a capuchin menu voice; and Omi, a lemur supervisor.

INT. SERVICE DESK - DAY

Nara holds a phone. A menu voice asks her to press one for account help. She presses one.

VELA (RECORDED): For help with pressing one, press one.

Nara presses one again. The menu says her selection is important. A bell sounds, then asks her to confirm the bell.

Nara: I need to change my mailing address.

VELA (RECORDED): For mail, press three. For an address, press four.

Nara presses three, then four. The phone plays a map sound and transfers her to the lost property menu. Kito answers.

KITO: Have you lost your address?

NARA: No, I moved.

Kito asks if she is moving a package, a letter or an account. Nara says account. Kito transfers her to Vela, who asks whether the account is moving. Nara says yes. Vela asks for the old address. Nara gives it. Vela says it is not recognized because it is no longer current.

NARA: That is why I am changing it.

Omi listens in and says they need a supervisor override. Kito presses a button. The phone asks him to rate his call. He chooses five stars. It asks if he would recommend himself.

KITO: I have not met myself.

Nara asks to speak to a person. Vela offers options for automated support, live support and human support. Nara chooses human. The system says live support is human-adjacent. Kito assures her that he is a person, though his desk phone is still navigating menus.

Omi gives Kito an address form. He asks Nara to read each line aloud so he can enter it. She reads the new address. Kito types it into the system. A dropdown asks whether the new location is home, work, seasonal or other. Nara says home. The system asks if home is seasonal.

NARA: It is home all year.

Kito selects "other" and writes "home." Omi approves the form. The system sends a confirmation code to the old address. Nara asks how she can get it. Kito says she can change the address once the code arrives.

Omi pauses the system. He walks to the mail shelf and finds a blank envelope. Kito writes Nara's new address on it and drops it into the outgoing slot. The system flashes a green check.

VELA (RECORDED): Your address has been updated.

NARA: That was easy once someone stopped using the phone.

Nara checks the copy before leaving. The new address is correct, and the old one is crossed out in ink. Kito places the form in a folder labeled completed.

Kito removes the receiver. It keeps playing a menu. Omi unplugs the base. The recording stops. Nara leaves with a copy of the form, addressed to her new home.
