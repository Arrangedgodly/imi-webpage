# The One-Day Museum Membership

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum memberships that grant access to every room except the room visitors came to see*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Vela, a macaque membership agent; Omi, a gibbon visitor; Piri, a capuchin gallery attendant; and Rell, a baboon security guard.

INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - DAY

Vela offers Omi a one-day membership card. It includes all galleries, the roof, the gift shop and a quarterly newsletter. Omi asks to see the seed collection.

VELA: That gallery is closed today.

OMI: Does the card include it?

VELA: It includes access to all open galleries.

Piri points toward the closed door. A sign says "Seed Collection: Please Return Tomorrow." Omi looks at the card. It says "All Access."

Omi tries the card at the entrance. The scanner beeps and opens the gift shop door. Vela says the system has recognized his membership. Omi walks through and emerges carrying a postcard of seeds.

OMI: I can see them here.

Piri says the postcard is printed at life size. Omi holds it beside the closed gallery. The picture shows a seed in a jar. Rell asks him not to press it against the glass.

Vela offers an upgrade for behind-the-scenes access. It allows visitors to stand in the hallway behind the gallery. Omi asks if he can see the collection from there. Vela says the door remains closed, but the hallway has a framed photograph.

Omi buys the upgrade. The card opens a door to a hallway. Piri leads him to the photograph. It is the same postcard, now framed. Omi asks if the seeds are actually in the gallery. Piri says yes, but the lights are off for cleaning.

Rell arrives with a cart and unlocks the door. He is moving a small jar to the storage room. Omi gets a brief look at the seed collection. The seeds are all labeled. One label faces backward. Rell turns it around.

OMI: Is that the rare seed?

PIRI: It is a lentil.

Vela asks if Omi would like to renew tomorrow. He asks if the gallery will be open. She checks the calendar. It is scheduled to close for a staff meeting.

OMI: Can I see the lentil today?

Rell places the jar in a display case near the entrance. The membership card opens the case's outer panel. Omi can view the jar without entering the gallery. Vela says the card has delivered access.

Omi reads the label. It says "lentil, teaching specimen." He nods and returns the card. Piri gives him the postcard as a souvenir. Omi leaves through the gift shop.

VELA: The membership includes a discount.

Omi checks the postcard price. It is the same as the card. He returns the card too. Piri replaces the sign with a note showing the gallery's actual opening hours.

Rell closes the case and turns the lentil toward the next visitor.
