# The Trail Sign Points Both Ways

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: outdoor wayfinding signs that promise a simple route while adding contradictory arrows and distances*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Ranger Kori, a macaque; hiker Sela, a gibbon; volunteer Omi, a capuchin; and mapmaker Ralo, a baboon.

EXT. FOREST TRAILHEAD - MORNING

A signpost shows three arrows. One says "LOOKOUT, 1 MILE." Another points the same way and says "LOOKOUT, 2 MILES." Sela reads them. Kori carries a map.

SELA: Which distance is right?

KORI: The lookout is one mile from here.

OMI: The second arrow measures the loop.

RALO: The sign does not say that.

[OMI TURNS THE SIGN. THE BACK SAYS "DISTANCES APPROXIMATE."]

SELA: One or two miles?

KORI: One mile to the lookout, two miles around the loop.

RALO: We can add a label.

EXT. TRAIL FORK - LATER

A smaller sign points left to "PINE VIEW" and right to "PINE VIEW." Sela looks at both paths.

SELA: These go to the same place?

KORI: They meet at the ridge.

OMI: The left path is shaded. The right path climbs more gradually.

RALO: I used one label to save paint.

[SELA TAKES THE LEFT PATH. KORI TAKES THE RIGHT PATH. THEY MEET AT THE SAME MARKER A FEW STEPS LATER.]

SELA: We arrived together.

KORI: The sign made two options out of one destination.

RALO: That is useful for choice.

OMI: It made us walk in opposite directions for ten seconds.

EXT. RIDGE - AFTERNOON

The group reaches a final sign. The arrow points back toward the trailhead, but the distance reads "0.0 miles." 

SELA: Are we already at the trailhead?

KORI: We are at the ridge.

RALO: The sign points to the trailhead from the trailhead.

OMI: I installed it facing the wrong way.

[OMI TURNS THE SIGN AROUND. THE ARROW NOW POINTS DOWN THE PATH.]

SELA: It is clear now.

RALO: The distance still says zero.

KORI: The trailhead is one mile away.

[OMI PEELS OFF THE ZERO LABEL. UNDER IT IS A LARGE NUMBER ONE.]

OMI: The old marker was stuck on top.

RALO: The sign has been corrected.

SELA: Can we follow it back?

KORI: Yes. The arrow points the right way.

[THEY START DOWNHILL. LATER, THEY RETURN TO THE TRAILHEAD. RALO MOUNTS A CLEAN SIGN.]

RALO: The lookout is one mile. The loop is two miles.

KORI: Put that on separate lines.

OMI: Left path and right path meet at the ridge.

SELA: A sign should tell us where we are going.

[RALO SCREWS THE NEW SIGN IN PLACE. THE ARROWS POINT TO THREE PLACES.]

OMI: The sign is upside down.

[RALO TURNS IT AROUND. THE ARROWS NOW POINT TO THE CORRECTLY LABELED TRAILS.]

KORI: That is clear from both directions.

OMI: We should add a map at the trail fork.

RALO: I will make sure it faces the path.

SELA: And points to the trail.
