# The Storm Drill Musical

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: municipal emergency-preparedness musicals and public-service jingles*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: MAYOR MOSS, a baboon; JUNE, a macaque weather officer; LEO, a gibbon singer; and a CHORUS of residents.

[A town hall. A weather map is pinned crookedly. A piano sits beside a stack of sandbags.]

MAYOR MOSS: We need a song to teach the storm route. Keep it brief and calm.

LEO: I wrote a chorus with a key change and a fog machine.

JUNE: The storm is tomorrow. We need people to know which road is closed.

LEO: The fog helps them imagine the rain.

[Moss pulls the fog machine plug.]

MAYOR MOSS: No fog. One map, one route, one verse.

LEO: The chorus is called "Run for the Ridge."

JUNE: Don't tell people to run. The trail is narrow.

LEO: "Walk to the Ridge" doesn't scan.

JUNE: Then change the meter.

[Leo begins playing.]

LEO: If the river rises high, climb the ridge and reach the sky-

JUNE: The ridge road is closed.

LEO: Which road is open?

JUNE: The school path. The low bridge is unsafe. The library is the meeting point.

MAYOR MOSS: Put those facts in the song.

[The chorus begins. Residents sing too fast, tripping over the route names.]

CHORUS: School path, library, not the low bridge!

LEO: They need a beat.

JUNE: They need the address.

[Moss turns the crooked map. The correct road is on the back.]

MAYOR MOSS: We have been singing to the wrong side.

LEO: That is why the song felt unresolved.

JUNE: It felt unresolved because the road was missing.

[Leo changes the lyric.]

LEO: When the river rises, take the school path east. Avoid the low bridge. Meet at the library.

CHORUS: When the river rises, school path east. Not the bridge. Library.

MAYOR MOSS: Better. Add the time of the drill.

JUNE: Tomorrow at ten. No siren. We will announce it on the radio.

LEO: Can I hold the final note?

JUNE: Not if the announcement starts.

[The chorus sings once more. The residents follow the map with their fingers. One resident points to the low bridge.]

CHORUS MEMBER: This one is closed?

JUNE: Yes. We will put a barrier there today.

LEO: Should the song include a bridge sound?

MAYOR MOSS: It can include silence where the bridge would be.

[They all sing the route correctly. The piano player reaches for a dramatic final chord; Moss taps the clock.]

MAYOR MOSS: We are done. The drill is at ten.

LEO: That was a short song.

JUNE: It got everyone to the right place.

[The chorus carries the sandbags to the school path. Leo follows, humming softly.]
[The chorus tries to make the final line rhyme.]

LEO: Take the school path east, where the library waits-

JUNE: The library isn't waiting. It is a building.

LEO: Take the school path east and meet at the library.

CHORUS: That works.

MAYOR MOSS: Can we sing it without the piano?

[The piano player closes the lid. Everyone sings the route in an ordinary speaking rhythm.]

JUNE: Better. People can hear the directions.

LEO: I will call it a song without a key change.

MAYOR MOSS: You can call it what you like after the drill.

[The chorus points to the open school path. The fog machine stays unplugged.]

[BLACKOUT.]
