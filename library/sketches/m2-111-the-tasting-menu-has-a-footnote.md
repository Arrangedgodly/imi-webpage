# The Tasting Menu Has a Footnote

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fine dining tasting menus, invented ingredients, and elaborate server narration*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: SERVER PEL, a gibbon; CHEF RIN, a macaque; DINER LUX, a capuchin; and HOST VEE, a lemur.

[A small table holds a leaf plate with one berry. Pel sets down a folded menu and waits for applause.] 

PEL: The first course is a single berry, presented on a leaf that once shaded the orchard.

LUX: Is the leaf clean?

RIN: Yes. I rinsed it after it fell.

PEL: The menu describes it as gathered at dawn by the chef's own hand.

RIN: I picked it up after breakfast.

VEE: The menu has created a timeline problem.

PEL: I can amend it to say, "berry, picked after breakfast, served on a washed leaf."

LUX: That tells me what I am eating.

[PEL WRITES THE NEW DESCRIPTION. THE INK SMEARS ACROSS THE PAGE.] 

PEL: The menu is now a visual interpretation.

RIN: It is wet ink.

LUX: I can eat the berry while it dries.

[LUX TAKES THE BERRY. IT ROLLS OFF THE LEAF. LUX CATCHES IT.] 

PEL: A surprise second presentation.

RIN: It is the same berry.

VEE: The diner has interrupted the service sequence.

LUX: I am ready for the next course.

[THE BERRY IS EATEN. RIN SETS DOWN A CUP OF WATER.] 

PEL: A mineral infusion, served at room temperature.

LUX: Is it from the spring?

RIN: Yes.

LUX: Can I drink it?

PEL: You may sip and consider the finish.

[LUX DRINKS.] 

LUX: It tastes like water.

PEL: The palate has reached the source.

VEE: The second course is complete.

RIN: We can serve the figs now. They are ripe.

PEL: The menu describes a procession of fruit.

VEE: How many is a procession?

RIN: Three.

[THREE FIGS SET DOWN. LUX TAKES ONE BEFORE PEL CAN SPEAK.] 

PEL: The diner has taken control of the pacing.

LUX: I am hungry.

RIN: The fruit is fresh. Eat it before it softens.

VEE: Should the final course be the folded note with the recipe?

LUX: What recipe?

RIN: It says where we got the water and how to pick a ripe fig.

LUX: I can see where you got the water. And the figs are right here.

PEL: Then the menu has become unnecessary.

VEE: We can keep it as a record of what was served.

[PEL FOLDS THE MENU. IT STICKS TO THE TABLE.] 

RIN: The ink is still wet.

LUX: Let it dry while I finish lunch.

PEL: The tasting menu is complete.

VEE: I will write the review on a clean card. What was the review?

LUX: The berry was good, the water was water, and I ate the figs before the speech.

PEL: A concise and memorable account.

RIN: Please write it on the dry side.
