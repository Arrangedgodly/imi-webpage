# The Food Critic Reviews the Empty Table

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: restaurant reviews that judge the room's buzz before a single diner arrives*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: CRITIC PAVA, a lemur; HOST ODO, a macaque; CHEF NARA, an orangutan; PRODUCER VELA, a capuchin; SERVER RIK, a gibbon.

[A dining room before opening. Every table is empty. Vela places a bouquet on the nearest one. Odo faces the camera beside a chalkboard reading FULL HOUSE.]

ODO: The room is alive with conversation.

RIK: I can hear the refrigerator.

ACT ONE

[Pava enters with a notebook and sits. Vela places a menu in front of her. It lists a roast that Chef Nara has not prepared.]

PAVA: Is this available?

NARA: Not tonight. The menu is from last week's rehearsal.

[Odo whispers that the broadcast needs a lively restaurant. Vela asks Rik to stack chairs behind the camera so the room looks full. Rik stacks two chairs; they scrape loudly across the floor.]

RIK: That is a sound effect.

[The refrigerator clicks off. Silence lands. Odo smiles and nods as if hearing applause.]

ACT TWO

[Nara brings Pava a bowl of today's soup. She tastes it and writes a note. Vela asks Odo to describe the aroma. He inhales the bouquet.]

PAVA: The soup has not arrived at your nose yet.

[Odo slides the flowers away. Rik serves the bowl. Pava asks what is in it. Nara lists the ingredients. The camera zooms in on the steam. It dissipates while Vela waits for the shot.]

VELA: Can we warm it again?

NARA: It is food, not a prop.

[Pava tastes the soup. She notes that it is good, but the serving is smaller than the menu picture. Nara says the picture was taken with a wide bowl; tonight's bowls are narrow. Odo asks if the review can call the portion generous. Pava holds the bowl up next to the photograph. It looks like a cup beside a swimming pool.]

[Rik seats a real early diner near the window. Vela wants to reshoot the greeting. Rik starts over, but the diner says hello to Pava before Odo can introduce them. Odo asks whether the diner is a regular. They say it is their first visit. Pava asks what they ordered. They point to the soup and ask whether it comes with bread. Nara nods and brings a slice.]

ACT THREE

[Vela moves the camera to include the occupied table, leaving two empty ones visible. Odo starts to say "packed." Pava closes her notebook.]

PAVA: There is one diner. Let's review the meal.

[She describes the soup honestly and praises the warm bread. Nara serves the first dinner order. The refrigerator clicks back on, humming through Odo's closing line. He tries to speak over it, but Pava points at the sound. Rik opens the kitchen door, and the hum stops. Vela whispers for Odo to call the room bustling. Pava turns the camera toward the empty tables. Odo abandons the word.]

ODO: Quiet, then.

[The diner finishes the soup and asks for another bowl. Nara writes the order. Rik removes the chalkboard that says FULL HOUSE. Vela asks whether the camera can stay on the dining room without hiding anything. Pava says yes. Odo checks the actual menu against the slate. Nara writes the current dishes in chalk. The diner points to the bread and requests another slice; Rik carries it to the table while the camera follows. The review is about dinner now, not the room's imaginary crowd.]

FINAL IMAGE: Pava's notebook rests beside the single occupied table, while the chalkboard reads ONE TABLE SERVED and the refrigerator hums in the background.
