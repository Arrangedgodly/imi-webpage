# The Mobile Library Takes the Hill

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: bookmobile tours that promise an orderly catalogue from a vehicle on a slope*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Librarian Nari, a lemur; driver Vex, a macaque; assistant Pip, a capuchin; reader Mara, a gibbon.

EXT. HILL STREET - AFTERNOON

[A hand-pulled book cart has a bell, an awning, and neat shelves. Vex brakes it at the top of a hill.]

VEX: The mobile library is open.

NARI: The brake is on.

PIP: The books are in alphabetical order.

[Mara walks up with a card that says "M".]

MARA: Do you have a mystery?

[Nari reaches for a book. The cart rolls a few inches downhill. Vex leans into the handle.]

NARI: Not yet.

VEX: We have a mystery about a missing brake.

[Pip finds the wheel lock and clicks it on. The cart stops. Nari hands Mara a mystery novel. Mara opens it; a card falls out.]

MARA: This says "Return to the mobile library." I just found it here.

PIP: The book is on loan to itself.

[Nari scans it. The scanner beeps and prints a fine notice.]

NARI: The cart owes six days.

VEX: It has not moved in six days.

NARI: That's why it's overdue.

[A gust catches the awning. It opens like a sail. The cart pulls Vex downhill. Pip grabs the rear handle. Nari holds the shelf. Books slide forward and line up at the front edge.]

VEX: Stop the library!

MARA: I have a book.

PIP: Put it down and help steer.

[Mara wedges the mystery under a wheel. The cart stops. Vex releases the handle and the awning folds shut. The books slide back.]

NARI: We need to take the hill off the route.

VEX: The hill is where the readers live.

[They push the cart up a few steps. The brake squeaks. A stack of travel guides drops open to the page "How to Walk Downhill Safely." Vex reads the heading.]

VEX: We have found the manual.

PIP: It is about walking.

[Mara asks to renew her mystery. Nari reaches for the scanner, but the cart rolls downhill again. Vex braces it with the travel guide.]

NARI: That guide is overdue.

VEX: It is holding the library in place.

MARA: Then give it a new due date.

[Pip stamps the cover. The stamp reads DUE TODAY. The book slides out and the cart rolls a few inches. Vex catches it by the handle.]

PIP: The library has renewed itself.

[Nari uses a bookmark to hold the brake lever in place. Mara returns the mystery. The cart rolls forward when she lets go. The book presses against the lever, stopping it.]

MARA: It has checked itself out again.

NARI: That is a very short loan.

FINAL BEAT: Vex rings the bell to close the library. The cart rolls downhill at walking speed, carrying every book home. Pip and Mara follow it with the overdue stamp.
