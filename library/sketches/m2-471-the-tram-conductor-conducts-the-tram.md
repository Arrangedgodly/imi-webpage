# The Tram Conductor Conducts the Tram

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: symphony rehearsals and public transit announcements that treat every stop like a premiere*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: CONDUCTOR, a gibbon; MAESTRO, an orangutan; DRIVER, a macaque; PASSENGER, a capuchin; INSPECTOR, a baboon.

[A parked tram at the depot. The conductor holds a baton. The driver waits at the controls; the tram is secured and its doors are open.]

CONDUCTOR: Today's service will depart on the downbeat.

DRIVER: Today's service will depart when the signal is green.

MAESTRO: We can align those events.

INSPECTOR: The baton is not a signal device.

[The maestro taps a music stand. The conductor lifts the baton. The passenger enters and sits, keeping a hand on the rail as the tram remains still.]

PASSENGER: Is this the express to the conservatory?

CONDUCTOR: It is the local. We stop at every musical phrase.

DRIVER: We stop at every marked platform.

[The conductor gives a dramatic four count. The driver points to the still-red depot lamp.]

DRIVER: Not cleared to move.

MAESTRO: Then we rehearse the announcement while stationary.

CONDUCTOR: Dear passengers, we are now approaching the idea of a station.

PASSENGER: Is there a real station after the idea?

INSPECTOR: Two stops away, according to the route.

[The maestro begins a grand brass flourish. A door chime interrupts. The conductor bows to it.]

CONDUCTOR: Our guest soloist has arrived.

DRIVER: That's the door warning. Please keep the doorway clear.

[The passenger steps fully inside. The doors remain open; no one is in their path. The inspector checks the platform indicator.]

INSPECTOR: Green. The tram may move when the driver says so.

MAESTRO: Can the downbeat be the driver's signal?

DRIVER: It can be the announcement. I will operate the tram.

[The driver closes the doors after the passenger is seated and the inspector signals clear. The tram rolls one meter inside the depot. The conductor conducts silently so as not to distract the driver.]

PASSENGER: We moved on the beat.

DRIVER: We moved on clearance.

MAESTRO: A successful collaboration.

[The tram stops at a marked rehearsal point. The driver opens the doors after the tram is stationary. The passenger remains seated until the inspector confirms the stop is only a rehearsal.]

CONDUCTOR: Rehearsal stop complete. Garden Street is the second public stop for the conservatory.

INSPECTOR: And nobody mistook the baton for the brake.

[The passenger asks which stop comes first. The conductor opens the route card, but the maestro points to the musical score instead.]

MAESTRO: First movement, then second movement.

PASSENGER: I need Garden Street, by the botanical garden entrance to the conservatory.

DRIVER: Two marked stops after departure. The route card says Garden Street.

CONDUCTOR: I will announce it without a fanfare.

[The inspector holds up the green clearance paddle. The conductor rehearses the announcement at speaking volume; the passenger repeats "Garden Street for the conservatory" back correctly. The maestro lowers the music stand so it does not block the route card.]

MAESTRO: The score can wait until the terminus.

DRIVER: The route cannot.

[The rehearsal is over. The passenger steps clear of the doorway; the driver checks the platform, signals the inspector, then closes the doors for the actual departure. The conductor gives one small downbeat to the announcement, not the controls.]

FINAL IMAGE: The conductor lowers the baton as the driver checks the route card, and the passenger reads the real stop names.
