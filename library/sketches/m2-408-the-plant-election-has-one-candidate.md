# The Plant Election Has One Candidate

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: local campaign debates where an unopposed candidate still gets a full campaign machine*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Chair Mara, a gibbon; candidate Pip, a capuchin; campaign manager Vex, a macaque; voter Nari, a lemur.

INT. GARDEN CLUB HALL - EVENING

[A potted fern sits behind a tiny podium. A sign reads ELECTION FOR WINDOW PLANT. Vex hands out leaf-shaped ballots.]

MARA: We have one candidate.

VEX: One candidate can still earn a mandate.

NARI: Can the plant speak?

PIP: I am running on its behalf.

VEX: The fern's platform includes shade, regular misting, and stronger window representation.

MARA: Those are its current conditions.

[Vex unveils a campaign banner. It blocks the window. The fern droops.]

PIP: The candidate is concerned about the lighting.

VEX: We can make a statement.

[He points a desk lamp at the plant. Mara moves the lamp away before the leaves heat. Nari places a ballot in the box.]

MARA: Did you vote for Pip or the fern?

NARI: I voted for the window.

[Vex reads the ballot.]

VEX: A write-in candidate. The window now has one vote.

PIP: The window doesn't have a platform.

MARA: It has a view.

[Vex rolls in a campaign podium with a wheel. It bumps the plant stand. The fern tilts. Pip reaches to steady it, but Vex blocks him so he won't look like he is favoring the candidate.]

PIP: It is falling.

VEX: We must let the voters decide.

[Nari catches the pot. Soil spills onto the ballot box. Mara lifts the lid. The paper ballots are stuck together.]

MARA: We cannot count these.

VEX: We can call them a landslide.

PIP: The fern has soil on its roots.

[He gently settles it back into the pot. The plant's lowest frond brushes the ballot box and drops one leaf-shaped card inside.]

NARI: The candidate voted for itself.

VEX: A historic first.

MARA: It is one leaf, not a ballot.

[Vex's banner slips. The fern is revealed in the sunny window. Its leaves lift. Pip takes down the banner.]

PIP: It has sunlight now.

NARI: That is its first policy passed.

[Mara invites the candidate to give a closing statement. Pip rotates the pot toward the audience. The fern's leaves point at the empty watering can.]

VEX: The candidate calls for investment.

MARA: It calls for water.

[Pip lifts the can. It is empty. Nari pours her glass into it. Pip waters the fern.]

VEX: A donor has funded the platform.

NARI: I was drinking water.

[The campaign sash slips from Pip's shoulder and loops around the plant pot.]

[Vex holds up the single clean ballot Nari placed aside. It reads "window." He calls for a recount. Mara takes the ballot box away.]

FINAL BEAT: Mara opens the curtains. Nari moves the fern into the light. Vex hands Pip the campaign sash, which becomes a plant tie when the fern leans over the sill.
