# The Petting Zoo Has a Waiver for the Goats

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: overlitigious attraction waivers that ignore the actual animal behavior*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Keeper Mara, a macaque; visitor Pip, a capuchin; lawyer Vex, an orangutan; goat handler Nari, a lemur.

EXT. PETTING ZOO - DAY

[A sign reads "GENTLE GOATS: PLEASE READ ALL TERMS." Vex holds a long waiver. Mara opens a small gate. Nari carries a bucket.]

VEX: Before entering, please initial every risk.

PIP: The page is longer than the fence.

MARA: The goats are gentle.

VEX: The form mentions chewing, nudging, startling, and "unexpected interest in documents."

NARI: That's because they eat paper.

[Behind them, a goat noses the waiver. Vex lifts it high.]

VEX: No signatures until the terms are understood.

PIP: I don't understand why a goat needs a lawyer.

VEX: The goat has not retained me. The zoo has.

[Mara leads Pip inside. The goat follows Nari's bucket. Vex remains outside the gate, reading aloud.]

VEX: Section four: visitors agree not to offer snacks from their pockets.

PIP: I have no snacks.

[The goat pats Pip's jacket pocket and finds a folded napkin.]

PIP: It's a napkin.

NARI: The goat considers it an appetizer wrapper.

[The goat tugs the napkin. Pip holds one end; the animal steps backward. The napkin unfolds, revealing Pip's lunch wrapped inside.]

PIP: I did have a snack.

VEX: You failed to disclose an edible item.

MARA: The goat disclosed it for him.

[Vex tries to hand Pip the waiver. The goat grabs its lower corner. Vex and the goat pull opposite ends, stretching the page.]

VEX: Release the contract.


[Vex opens a folder to a diagram showing a visitor and goat smiling at each other.]

PIP: Does the goat sign?

VEX: The visitor acknowledges the goat's right to decline.

NARI: She declined your pen.

MARA: She accepted the bucket.

[Vex demonstrates a gentle approach. The goat walks past him and leans against Pip.]

VEX: The diagram does not show favoritism.

PIP: She likes the lunch.

VEX: I did not bring lunch.

NARI: That is the difference.

[The waiver tears free and lands beside the bucket. Vex stumbles backward into the open gate. Another goat steps up and examines his tie.]

VEX: I am not an authorized snack.

MARA: The tie is a little leafy.

[Pip offers his lunch on the flat of his hand. The goat takes one crumb, then turns to the waiver. Nari puts the bucket on the ground; both goats move to it.]

NARI: The bucket is more persuasive.

VEX: The waiver says feeding is prohibited.

MARA: The sign says petting zoo.

PIP: Can I pet them?

[He reaches slowly. One goat presses its head against his palm. Vex rushes to document the contact, but the second goat sniffs his pen and pulls it away.]

VEX: That's a breach.

NARI: The pen wasn't in the waiver.

[The goat drops the pen into the bucket. Vex retrieves it, now covered in hay.]

VEX: We need an addendum.

MARA: We need another pen.

PIP: And a shorter form.

[The goats chew the waiver's bottom edge. Vex studies the remaining pages.]

VEX: Good news. They have waived section four.
