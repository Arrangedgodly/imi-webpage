# The Museum Gift Shop Sells the Exhibit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum gift shops turning education into branded souvenirs*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Curator Nari, a lemur; clerk Vex, an orangutan; school visitor Pip, a capuchin; guard Mara, a macaque.

INT. MUSEUM GIFT SHOP - DAY

[A velvet rope separates a stone wheel from a shelf of miniature stone wheels. Nari points to the exhibit beyond the shop window. Vex stands behind the register wearing a pin shaped like a wheel.]

NARI: The wheel is a thousand years old. It was found beside the river.

VEX: And this one is available in three convenient sizes.

PIP: Is that the real wheel?

NARI: The real one is in Gallery Two.

VEX: This one is a faithful replica, except it's lighter, brighter, and has a barcode.

[Mara walks in with a clipboard and checks the rope.]

MARA: Why is the gift shop roped off?

VEX: We are protecting the merchandise from the exhibit.

NARI: The merchandise is an exhibit now?

VEX: The display card says "Objects That Changed the World."

PIP: Did this wheel change the world?

NARI: It changed how people moved heavy things.

VEX: Our keychain changed how people find their keys.

[Pip picks up a keychain wheel. The tiny wheel spins freely and rolls off the counter. It runs under the rope and circles the real wheel's display.]

PIP: It found the old wheel.

VEX: A customer journey with historic context.

[Mara stops the keychain with one foot. Nari retrieves it, then notices the rope now encloses the miniature display and leaves the gallery entrance open.]

NARI: The rope is backwards.

VEX: The replicas have greater foot traffic.

MARA: Visitors can get close to the replica. The original needs space.

[Vex moves the rope around the gallery window. The wheel keychains are now outside the protected area. Pip steps toward one; Vex gasps.]

VEX: Careful! That is an artifact of commerce.

PIP: It costs two acorns.

VEX: History is priceless. This is two acorns.

[Nari places a label beside the real wheel: "Please do not touch." Vex sets a similar label beside the keychain display: "Please do touch; turning demonstrates value." Pip turns one. All the keychains rotate in a clattering chain reaction, rolling off the shelf into Mara's open evidence box.]

MARA: I was collecting lost property.

VEX: You have collected our entire wheel line.


[Vex lifts a replica from the display. It spins across the counter and bumps the register.]

VEX: A customer journey with historic context.

NARI: The river bends on the map. The real wheel helped move stone from that bank.

PIP: Does this one move stone?

VEX: It moves a shopping bag.

PIP: Can I test it?

[Vex gives him the keychain. Pip turns it once; the wheel rolls away and Mara stops it with her shoe.]

MARA: The replica has left the shop.

NARI: That is not how the original worked.

PIP: They traveled.

NARI: The original changed the world. The replicas changed departments.

[Mara wheels away the box. Vex puts a tiny "Gallery Two" sign on the empty shelf and faces it toward the checkout.]

VEX: And now the shop has an exhibit.

PIP: Does it come in a smaller size?

VEX: Two acorns.
