# The Costume Has a Union Card

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: mascot appearances that schedule a character's every wave while ignoring the performer's physical limits*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Piri, a capuchin performer inside a giant heron costume; Manager Gell, a macaque; Event Host Odo, a gibbon; and Stagehand Mina, a lemur.

INT. FESTIVAL BACKSTAGE - DAY

Piri sits inside a large heron suit. Only the beak moves. Gell studies a schedule with tiny time slots.

GELL: You wave at 1:02, point at the sponsor board at 1:03, then lead the children's parade at 1:04.

PIRI: The beak blocks my view.

GELL: The audience sees the character, not the view.

MINA: The head weighs more than the costume rack.

GELL: The heron is majestic.

PIRI: The heron needs a chair.

ODO: The crowd is ready for a surprise entrance.

GELL: Piri, wait for the drumroll, then turn left.

PIRI: I cannot see left.

MINA: I can guide you by the elbow.

GELL: The character should move independently.

ODO: The character is about to move into the prop table.

GELL: The table is outside the marked performance zone.

MINA: The beak is longer than the marked performance zone.

PIRI: I cannot see the tape from inside the head.

ODO: We could move the table.

GELL: Then the character would have to turn.

PIRI: The character has a neck.

GELL: The character has a schedule.

MINA: The performer has a neck too.

[Mina gently steers the heron around the table. The beak catches a ribbon and pulls a banner down.]

INT. FESTIVAL MAIN STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

Odo announces the sponsor while Piri stands beside a fan. The fan blows the heron's neck feathers backward.

ODO: Welcome our majestic mascot!

GELL: Wave now.

[Piri lifts one wing. The wing knocks the sponsor board over.]

GELL: That was the wrong wing.

PIRI: Both wings are attached to me.

MINA: The costume has a side zipper. She can breathe if we open it.

GELL: Keep the head on until the photo.

[Piri sits down. The oversized heron head bobs forward and lands on the stage, beak pointed at the sponsor logo.]

ODO: The bird is bowing.

PIRI: I am sitting.

ODO: The audience loves the humility.

PIRI: The audience can love a chair too.

INT. FESTIVAL MAIN STAGE - LATER

Mina wheels out a sturdy chair and places it behind the heron. Gell checks the schedule.

GELL: We have a rest break scheduled at 1:05.

PIRI: It is 1:04.

MINA: The performer has been standing since noon.

GELL: Move the break to 1:04.

GELL: Then take it now.

ODO: The mascot is taking a seat before the parade.

PIRI: The mascot is drinking water.

[THE BEAK TILTS. A STRAW APPEARS THROUGH A SMALL FLAP. THE CROWD APPLAUDS.]

GELL: Add hydration to the appearance plan.

MINA: The union card says it was already in the plan.
