# Emergency Contact Tree

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Corporate emergency drill videos and compliance flowcharts*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: DEX, a macaque safety trainer; BELL, a squirrel trainee; JO, a lemur; and NARRATOR, the training-film voice.

[An office training room. A diagram shows a tree with arrows connecting the branches to other trees.]

NARRATOR: In an emergency, identify the nearest responsible adult.

BELL: What if the adult is on the branch that fell?

DEX: Identify a second adult.

JO: What if the second adult is the person holding the ladder?

DEX: Refer to page twelve.

[Dex flips a binder. Page twelve is a flowchart that points back to page one.]

BELL: The diagram has put us in a loop.

NARRATOR: Loops build confidence.

[A thump outside. Bell runs to the window.]

BELL: Branch down!

DEX: Do not approach the branch. Alert the nearest responsible adult.

[Jo raises a hand.]

JO: I am a responsible adult.

DEX: You are the nearest responsible adult.

JO: Then who do I alert?

DEX: The next nearest responsible adult.

[They all stare at the diagram.]

NARRATOR: Correct. Continue until the nearest responsible adult is identified.

[The office phone rings. Bell answers.]

BELL: The branch is blocking the path.

VOICE ON PHONE: Please submit a branch obstruction form.

BELL: The form is on the other side of the branch.

DEX: This is a known access issue. Use the mobile form.

[The mobile form requires a photo of the blocked path. Bell points the phone at the window. The camera opens the selfie lens.]

BELL: It wants a picture of me.

DEX: It needs to confirm you are near the hazard.

JO: I am near the hazard. I am also in this room.

NARRATOR: Keep calm. A calm employee is easier to categorize.

[Dex pulls a card from the binder. It reads DO NOT MOVE THE BRANCH. A smaller note says UNLESS IT IS BLOCKING THE DOOR.]

DEX: We have an exception.

BELL: The path is blocked, not the door.

JO: Then we call the grounds crew and use the south path.

DEX: That isn't in the form.

[The branch rolls slightly down the slope. Everyone moves back.]

NARRATOR: Never improvise.

[The binder slides off the table and wedges the office door open.]

JO: Now the door is blocked by the training materials.

BELL: Which form do we use?

DEX: The form for training materials.

[They carry the binder outside and call the grounds crew. The branch stays where it is. NARRATOR's voice continues from the dropped laptop.]

NARRATOR: Congratulations. You have successfully completed the drill and may now report the drill.

[Dex turns off the laptop. It says YOUR SESSION HAS TIMED OUT.]

BELL: The form is gone.

DEX: The hazard is still there.

JO: The grounds crew has arrived.

[SFX: CART WHEELS, A BRANCH LIFTED CLEAR.]

NARRATOR: The path is open. Submit your completed drill report by the end of the day.

BELL: Where do we submit it?

DEX: In the binder.

[He looks at the binder outside, on the cart.]

JO: The binder is now on the other side of the hazard.

DEX: Then the report can wait until tomorrow.

NARRATOR: You have completed the drill.

[BLACKOUT.]
