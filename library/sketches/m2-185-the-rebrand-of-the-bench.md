# The Rebrand of the Bench

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: brand consultants that rename ordinary public furniture without improving how anyone uses it*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Rell, a gibbon brand consultant; Doma, a macaque parks worker; Piri, a capuchin designer; and Vela, a lemur resident.

EXT. PARK PATH - DAY

Rell unveils a sign beside a wooden bench: "Resting Platform: The Experience." Vela reads it and sits down.

VELA: It is a bench.

RELL: We are moving beyond benches.

Piri presents a logo: a leaf hovering above a horizontal line. Doma says the bench needs one loose board fixed. Rell asks if they can call it a modular seating refresh. Doma gets a screwdriver.

Piri offers Vela three seating modes: pause, reflect and recharge. Vela asks where to put her bag. Rell says the bag can use the accessory zone. It is a hook on the side, still attached with a single loose screw.

Doma tightens the screw. The hook holds a small bag. Rell calls it a secure carry solution. A squirrel monkey tries to hang a heavy basket and the hook bends. Doma removes it before it falls.

Doma: It is for a light bag.

Rell: We can add that to the brand language.

Vela asks if the bench will still be there tomorrow. Rell says the experience is evergreen. Doma points out the bench is due to be moved for path repairs. Rell says relocation creates an opportunity for a mobile experience. Piri wheels over a small cart. The bench does not fit on it.

They try to lift the bench. One leg catches in the grass. Rell suggests calling it a grounded platform. Doma frees the leg, and the group moves the bench to a dry spot. The new spot faces a tree, not the path. Vela sits again. She likes the shade but cannot see the trail.

Piri rotates the bench. Rell asks whether the logo should rotate too. Doma finishes positioning the bench and plants the original sign back into the soil.

Vela: Thanks for fixing the board.

Doma: It was loose.

Rell opens a survey. It asks which brand word best describes the bench: calm, active or vibrant. Vela writes "dry." Piri says that is not on the list. Vela points to a puddle beneath the old spot. The new location keeps the seat dry.

Rell asks Doma to pose beside the bench for a launch photo. A small monkey sits down first. Piri photographs the monkey, who immediately leaves. Rell calls it authentic engagement.

Doma takes down the new sign and writes "BENCH MOVED FOR REPAIR" on its back. Rell examines the sign.

RELL: Clear, direct, and available in one size.

Piri turns it around. The brand logo covers the word BENCH. Doma removes the logo and sits beside Vela. Doma sets the screwdriver beside the bench for the next repair. Rell adds "dry" to his brand deck.
