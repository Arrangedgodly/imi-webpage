# Emergency Meeting of the Weather Committee

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Disaster-planning committee dramas and overconfident emergency briefings*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Chair Lesso, a baboon; forecaster Mina, a lemur; treasurer Vup, a macaque; runner Kett, a squirrel monkey; observer Doro, an orangutan.

[STAGE: A MEETING ROOM WITH A MAP, A TINY FAN, THREE UMBRELLAS, AND A BOWL OF DRY LEAVES.]

LESSO: This emergency committee is now in session.
MINA: The forecast is for a brief shower.
LESSO: How brief?
MINA: Until the cloud passes.
VUP: What is the budget for the cloud?
MINA: Clouds are free.
VUP: Then why are we meeting?
LESSO: To prepare.
KETT: I brought the emergency kit.
DORO: It is an empty basket.
KETT: It is lightweight and easy to carry.
LESSO: Excellent. What emergencies can it handle?
KETT: We can place small emergencies inside.
MINA: The rain will come from the west.
LESSO: Mark the west.
VUP: Which west?
MINA: The one where the sun sets.
VUP: I thought that was the other side of the room.
DORO: The map is upside down.
LESSO: Turn it around.
[THE MAP IS TURNED. A DRAWING OF A BANANA IS REVEALED.]
KETT: That is the old emergency plan.
LESSO: What was the emergency?
KETT: A banana was rolling downhill.
VUP: Did the plan work?
KETT: The banana stopped in the bowl.
MINA: The forecast says light wind.
LESSO: We have a fan.
DORO: The fan is unplugged.
VUP: The plug is in the emergency kit.
KETT: I packed it with the basket.
LESSO: Plug in the fan.
[THE FAN BLOWS THE DRY LEAVES ACROSS THE TABLE.]
MINA: That is now a leaf advisory.
VUP: The leaves are in my notes.
LESSO: The notes are part of the plan.
DORO: I have caught one leaf.
KETT: You are the first responder.
DORO: It is stuck to my hand.
LESSO: We need an evacuation route.
MINA: The door is behind you.
VUP: The map shows a river there.
DORO: The map shows a banana.
LESSO: We will take the route with fewer fruit.
KETT: The shower has started.
[ONE DROP FALLS INTO THE EMPTY BASKET.]
MINA: The basket has collected the rain.
LESSO: The plan is working.
VUP: We should allocate a second basket.
DORO: It is still one drop.
LESSO: We are ready for one drop.
KETT: What about the wind?
[THE FAN STOPS.]
MINA: The wind has passed.
LESSO: Committee adjourned.
VUP: Who will carry the basket?
KETT: I am the runner.
DORO: I caught the leaf.
LESSO: Then the team has covered every emergency.
MINA: Except the banana.
[THE BANANA ROLLS OFF THE MAP.]
VUP: Emergency resumed.

VUP: The basket has a hole.
KETT: We can use the leaf as a lid.
DORO: The leaf is still on my hand.
LESSO: Your hand is now part of the equipment.
MINA: The cloud has moved east.
VUP: Mark east on the map.
DORO: The map still shows a banana.
LESSO: Then the banana is our east.
KETT: It is rolling west.
MINA: The forecast is changing.

[THE COMMITTEE CHASES THE BANANA IN A PERFECTLY ORDERLY LINE.]
