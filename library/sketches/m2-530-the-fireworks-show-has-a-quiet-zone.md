# The Fireworks Show Has a Quiet Zone

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public fireworks finales, live announcers, and event crews trying to honor a quiet zone*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: EVENT HOST, a baboon; PYRO TECH, an orangutan; QUIET-ZONE MARSHAL, a gibbon; CHILD, a macaque; DRUMMER, a capuchin.

[A park field. A rope separates the fireworks launch area from a quiet zone with blankets. The host holds a microphone.]

HOST: The finale begins at nine. Please stay behind the rope.

MARSHAL: This side is quiet. Keep the drumline near the stage.

DRUMMER: I can play softly.

[The drummer taps once. The quiet-zone sign rattles.]

CHILD: The sign heard it.

PYRO TECH: The fireworks will be silent fountains tonight.

HOST: The crowd expects a grand finale.

ACT ONE

[The host practices the announcement. Each time they say "finale," the drummer adds a roll.]

HOST: We will begin the finale-

[The drum roll starts.]

MARSHAL: The quiet zone is still occupied.

HOST: I am only speaking.

[The child places earmuffs over the quiet-zone sign. The marshal removes them.]

MARSHAL: The sign does not need earmuffs.

CHILD: It looked nervous.

[The pyrotechnician checks the launch board. One fountain is set to a louder burst. They remove it and replace it with a low spark pattern.]

PYRO TECH: The final cue is safe and quiet.

DRUMMER: Can I play one note?

MARSHAL: After the show.

[The host cues the fireworks. The first fountain rises quietly. The crowd watches. The announcer whispers play-by-play into the microphone.]

HOST: A small sparkle at the center of the field.

[The microphone amplifies the whisper into a loud echo.]

MARSHAL: The microphone is not quiet.

[The host lowers the microphone and speaks without amplification. The crowd can still hear from the field.]

[Before the finale, the pyrotechnician demonstrates the control board. Each button has a color and a matching label. The host reads the names aloud without using the microphone. The marshal moves the quiet-zone rope farther from the launch line.]

PYRO TECH: Green is the low fountain. Red is the stop switch.

HOST: I can remember that.

[The drummer repeats the instruction in a whispered rhythm. The child gives the quiet-zone sign a thumbs-up. The host checks the stop switch one more time without touching it.]

ACT TWO

[The final fountains start. The pyrotechnician watches the panel while the marshal keeps the rope line clear. The drummer holds both sticks still. The child watches the lights and covers their ears only when the crowd cheers.]

HOST: That is the finale.

DRUMMER: Now may I play?

MARSHAL: The quiet zone is still occupied.

[The child points to a space beyond the rope. The drummer walks there and taps a single soft note. The crowd applauds.]

FINAL IMAGE: The last spark fades above the silent sign while the drummer carries the sticks away.
