# The Weather Balloon Has Stage Fright

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: live weather broadcasts that stage dramatic outdoor shots regardless of what the weather actually does*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Reporter Nala, a macaque; Camera Op Venn, a capuchin; Meteorologist Boro, a gorilla; and Intern Sef, a gibbon.

EXT. STATION ROOFTOP - MORNING

A small red weather balloon rests in a net beside Nala. The sky is clear. Venn checks a camera. Boro holds a forecast card.

NALA: We are live above the city. The balloon will show how calm the air is today.

VENN: It is not moving.

BORO: There is no wind on the roof.

NALA: We could say it is gathering suspense.

SEF: The balloon is tied to the railing.

[SEF UNTIES THE BALLOON. IT BOBS ONCE AND DROPS GENTLY BACK INTO THE NET.]

NALA: The conditions remain calm.

VENN: The producer asked for urgency.

BORO: The forecast is sunny, with a light breeze after noon.

NALA: Is the light breeze here now?

BORO: No.

INT. ROOFTOP STUDIO - MINUTES LATER

Venn points a small fan at the balloon. It moves, then pulls the net across the roof. Sef grabs the net before it reaches the stairs.

NALA: We are seeing a sudden gust.

BORO: We are seeing a fan.

VENN: The remote audience can't see the fan from this angle.

SEF: They can hear it.

[THE FAN WHINES LOUDLY. THE BALLOON SPINS IN PLACE.]

NALA: A circular wind pattern has formed.

BORO: The fan is pointed at a knot.

NALA: That is where the knot is.

VENN: Can we get a cloud shot?

BORO: There are no clouds.

VENN: A dramatic cloud would help.

SEF: I have a gray umbrella.

[SEF OPENS IT. THE UMBRELLA COVERS THE CAMERA LENS.]

NALA: Conditions have darkened.

BORO: Only for us.

EXT. ROOFTOP - LATER

The fan is off. The net lies flat. The red balloon has drifted a foot and stopped. Nala stands beside it for the final report.

NALA: The weather has held steady. The balloon traveled one foot.

BORO: The forecast has not changed.

VENN: But the shot has a beginning and an end.

VENN: Can we call that a trend?

BORO: It is a measurement.

SEF: One foot toward the door.

VENN: The producer wants a second measurement.

BORO: The balloon is tied to the railing again.

[SEF UNCLIPS THE STRING. THE BALLOON FLOATS UP, THEN SETTLES AGAINST THE ROOF VENT.]

NALA: The balloon has found a new weather station.

SEF: The vent is blowing warm air.

BORO: That is the building, not the forecast.

VENN: We could call it a microclimate.

BORO: The microclimate is the vent.

NALA: The balloon has traveled again.

[NALA WALKS THE BALLOON TO THE DOOR. IT GLIDES BEHIND HER LIKE A VERY SMALL PARADE FLOAT.]

NALA: The breeze is arriving early.

BORO: That is you walking.

VENN: Keep the shot. The balloon is finally moving.

NALA: Reporting live from the edge of a slow parade.
