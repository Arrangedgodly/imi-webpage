# The Committee for a Quiet Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: meeting culture that uses so much discussion to plan quiet that nobody experiences it*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: chair Tavi, a macaque; member Pava, a gibbon; member Odo, a baboon; facilitator Miri, a capuchin.

INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - DAY

A sign reads QUIET ROOM. Tavi opens a meeting folder.

TAVI: We are here to decide how quiet the room should be.

PAVA: Can we be quiet now?

MIRI: We need to approve the agenda first.

Odo passes out a form asking members to rate silence from one to ten. Pava marks the paper without speaking. Tavi asks her to explain her answer. Pava remains quiet.

Tavi says the meeting needs discussion. Miri proposes a talking stick. Odo asks whether the stick is supposed to make people quiet. Tavi says it helps organize who speaks.

Pava takes the stick and holds it out to Odo. He begins describing the benefits of a talking stick. The explanation lasts three minutes. Pava raises one finger. Tavi says questions require a separate agenda item.

Miri asks if the group can test the room. They all sit silently. Odo hears the radiator click. Tavi writes "possible noise." Pava points to the radiator. Odo says the click is part of the building, not the meeting.

They close the door. The hallway sound stops. Miri switches off the light; Tavi turns it back on for note-taking. The quiet test starts again. Odo's stomach growls. He says the room is too quiet to hide it.

Pava puts the agenda away. She places the talking stick on the floor and closes her eyes. One by one, the others follow. Tavi tries to keep minutes without making the pen scratch. Miri folds the form.

Miri asks the members to share what interrupted their quiet test. Odo says the radiator clicked, Pava says the hallway door shut, and Tavi says the pen scratched. Each comment creates more noise as they compare sounds. Pava asks them to stop listing interruptions and notice the room as it is. Odo moves the agenda away. Tavi sets the talking stick beside the wall. The radiator clicks again. Nobody writes it down. Miri closes the blinds, and daylight remains. The group sits without trying to score the silence. After a minute, Tavi asks if anyone wants to continue. Pava nods toward the door; they can finish the meeting outside. They leave the room quiet.

Tavi returns the folder to the shelf and leaves the room without a final vote. The quiet room remains available.

Miri says the room should be reserved for people who need a break. Pava writes that on the door. The sign fits without needing a committee vote.

No one objects.

TAG

The room stays quiet until the meeting alarm rings.

TAVI: We have reached consensus.

PAVA: We did not vote.
