# The School Bell Has a Dramatic Pause

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school assemblies that stretch routine announcements into grand ceremonies*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Principal Omi, a macaque; announcer Pava, a baboon; student Reka, a gibbon; and custodian Tern, a capuchin.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - MORNING

Students sit on folding chairs. A microphone stands at center court. Omi checks a clipboard; Pava waits beside a large velvet curtain.

OMI: We have three announcements and then class.

PAVA: The bell cue gives each announcement its own moment.

REKA: The bell is for class change.

PAVA: Today it will punctuate the program.

[Tern tests the bell switch. A deep clang echoes through the gym. Everyone flinches.]

TERN: It is very loud.

PAVA: It sounds important.

INT. GYM - MOMENTS LATER

Pava approaches the microphone.

PAVA: First announcement: the library will close early today.

[She pauses. The bell clangs.]

REKA: Is that the end?

PAVA: The bell means reflect.

OMI: It means class change.

PAVA: We can honor both meanings.

[Omi reaches for the switch. Pava steps between him and the bell.]

PAVA: Second announcement: the lost-and-found table is by the gym door.

[The bell clangs again. The curtain drops, revealing the lost-and-found table. A row of mismatched mittens spills onto the stage.]

REKA: The mittens have been announced.

INT. GYM - LATER

Omi asks Reka to deliver the final announcement: the field trip bus leaves at ten. Pava positions the bell rope.

REKA: The bus leaves at ten. Please be outside by nine fifty.

PAVA: Give them a moment to absorb the time.

[The bell clangs. The gym doors swing open. A bus horn sounds outside. Students stand and start toward the exit.]

OMI: The bus is here early.

PAVA: The bell called it.

REKA: The driver heard the announcement.

[Tern tries to stop the bell from swinging. It clangs a second time. The students turn around and return to their seats.]

REKA: Now they think class has started again.

OMI: We have two minutes to settle this.

INT. GYM - FINAL ANNOUNCEMENT

Omi takes the microphone. Pava holds the bell rope with both hands. Reka stands at the door.

OMI: The announcements are over. The bus leaves at ten. Students, board now.

[Pava holds the rope still. The students move. The bell remains quiet. Tern unplugs its switch.]

PAVA: We should close with a ceremonial ring.

OMI: We should let them reach the bus.

[The bus begins to pull away. Reka waves from the doorway. Pava releases the rope by accident; the bell rings once, and the bus brakes. The driver opens the door.]

REKA: The bell has delayed the bus.

PAVA: It has extended the moment.

OMI: It has bought us one announcement: please stop ringing the bell.

[Tern removes the bell clapper and puts it in the lost-and-found basket.]

REKA: I think that clapper belongs to the school.

TERN: It is waiting for its number.
