# The Improv Class Cannot Say Yes

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: improv workshops that turn a simple "yes, and" exercise into a rigid test with rules about spontaneity*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Teacher Vela, a macaque; Student Omi, a gibbon; Student Tavi, a capuchin; and Stage Manager Rell, a baboon.

INT. IMPROV CLASSROOM - EVENING

Vela stands before two students. A chair sits in the center of the room. Rell holds a bell.

VELA: The exercise is simple. Accept the offer and add something.

OMI: This chair is a boat.

TAVI: Yes, and we are sailing.

VELA: Good. Keep going.

OMI: We need a captain.

TAVI: No, we need a map.

[RELL RINGS THE BELL.]

RELL: That was a no.

TAVI: The map is useful.

VELA: The rule is to accept the offer first.

OMI: Then the map is in the boat.

VELA: Good. Tavi, give him a complication.

TAVI: The map is upside down.

OMI: Yes, and north is now south.

VELA: Don't change the world. Accept it.

TAVI: The map is upside down, and we are holding it upside down.

RELL: That is a correction, not a complication.

OMI: Can I turn it around?

VELA: Only if Tavi offers that action.

VELA: Better.

INT. IMPROV CLASSROOM - LATER

Vela adds a second chair. Rell draws a line on the floor to mark the imaginary water. Tavi stands on the wrong side.

VELA: Omi, give Tavi a new fact.

OMI: The boat has sprung a leak.

TAVI: Yes, and we should leave the boat.

[RELL RINGS THE BELL.]

RELL: That ends the scene.

TAVI: The boat is sinking.

VELA: Accept the problem before solving it.

TAVI: Yes, the boat is sinking, and we are calmly rearranging the chairs.

OMI: The chairs are not life jackets.

VELA: That is another no.

RELL: I am running out of bell.

VELA: We can finish with a silent scene.

OMI: The boat is approaching a dock.

TAVI: Yes, and the dock is made of chairs.

RELL: Don't move the chairs. We just stacked them.

TAVI: The dock is already built.

OMI: The scene can end.

[THE BELL FALLS OFF ITS HANDLE. RELL LOOKS AT IT.]

INT. IMPROV CLASSROOM - FINAL EXERCISE

Vela stands beside the chair boat. Tavi and Omi wait. Rell holds the broken bell in two pieces.

VELA: The bell is broken.

TAVI: Yes, and now we can hear each other.

OMI: Yes, and we can use the chair as a boat again.

VELA: The scene has found a new direction.

RELL: The class ends in five minutes.

TAVI: Yes, and we need to stack the chairs.

[THEY STACK THE CHAIRS. THE TOP ONE WOBBLES.]

OMI: The boat is listing.

VELA: Yes, and we should steady it.

[THEY ADJUST THE STACK. IT STANDS STRAIGHT.]

RELL: That was practical.

VELA: It was also accepted.

[THEY EXIT. THE BROKEN BELL SITS ON THE TOP CHAIR LIKE A TINY CAPTAIN.]
