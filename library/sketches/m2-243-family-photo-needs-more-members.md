# The Family Photo Needs More Members

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: formal group portraits that keep rearranging everyone to meet a photographer's ideal composition*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Photographer Neri, a gibbon; organizer Vela, a macaque; family member Tavi, a capuchin; and grandparent Sori, a baboon.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

A family gathers before a plain backdrop. Neri adjusts a tripod. Vela holds a seating chart.

NERI: We need three rows, everyone angled toward the center.

TAVI: The center is the chair.

SORI: I am sitting in it.

VELA: The chart says the oldest family member sits in the middle.

SORI: The chart is right.

[NERI MOVES THE CHAIR. SORI MOVES WITH IT.]

NERI: We need the group tighter.

TAVI: We are already shoulder to shoulder.

VELA: There are still two gaps.

SORI: Those are the aisles.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - LATER

Neri asks everyone to lean inward. Tavi holds a small child. Vela brings in a potted plant.

NERI: The plant fills the left side of the frame.

TAVI: It is not part of the family.

VELA: It was a gift from Sori's garden.

SORI: The plant is visiting.

[NERI PHOTOGRAPHS THE GROUP. THE FLASH REFLECTS OFF A MIRROR.]

NERI: We have a glare.

VELA: The mirror is in the hall.

TAVI: We can move two feet to the right.

NERI: Then the frame will be unbalanced.

SORI: The mirror is not in the family either.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - STAGING AREA

Vela checks the seating chart. Neri moves a stool into the frame. Tavi shifts the plant away from the wall.

VELA: The back row needs to be taller.

SORI: I am sitting in the tallest chair.

NERI: The stool is for the shortest members.

TAVI: The stool is for the plant.

[THE PLANT SITS ON THE STOOL. ITS LEAVES COVER THE TOP OF THE BACKDROP.]

NERI: The plant is now the tallest member.

SORI: The plant did not bring a chair.

VELA: Could everyone hold still while we adjust?

[THE FAMILY HOLDS POSES. NERI MOVES THE STOOL. THE PLANT WOBBLES, AND TAVI CATCHES IT.]

TAVI: The plant needs a lower seat.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - FINAL PHOTO

Neri moves the tripod back. Vela stops rearranging the chairs. Everyone settles in their own way. Sori keeps the plant beside the chair.

NERI: Look at the camera. Ready?

VELA: The seating chart has one more name.

TAVI: Who is missing?

[THE DOOR OPENS. A COURIER DELIVERS A PRINTED COPY OF THE SEATING CHART.]

COURIER: I was told to bring this to the family photo.

SORI: We are the family.

NERI: There is a blank space beside the plant.

[TAVI MAKES ROOM. THE COURIER HOLDS THE CHART. NERI TAKES THE PICTURE.]

VELA: Everyone is in the frame.

SORI: Even the chart.

[THE PLANT LEANS INTO THE COURIER'S SHOULDER.]

NERI: One more, with the plant included.

TAVI: It is already included.

NERI: The plant gets one print too.
