# The Very Small Book Club

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: literary discussion shows that inflate an ordinary book into a serious debate about interpretation*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Host Oda, a capuchin; critic Veli, a gibbon; reader Sumi, a macaque; and librarian Rook, a baboon.

[A TABLE HOLDS A SHORT BOOK WITH THREE PAGES.]

ODA: Welcome to The Very Small Book Club. Tonight's book is "The Seed."

VELI: It has only three pages.

SUMI: It is a picture book.

ROOK: Please keep the cover clean.

[VELI OPENS THE BOOK.]

VELI: The seed on page one is alone.

SUMI: It is in a field.

VELI: Alone in the field.

ODA: A bold interpretation.

[ROOK TURNS THE PAGE. A SPROUT APPEARS.]

ROOK: It grows.

VELI: The sprout rejects the seed's past.

SUMI: It came from the seed.

ODA: A complicated inheritance.

[THEY TURN TO PAGE THREE. A TREE IS DRAWN.]

VELI: The final page is a triumph of verticality.

SUMI: It is a tree.

ROOK: The book is due back tomorrow.

ODA: What did the author intend?

ROOK: The author is the librarian who drew it.

SUMI: Rook, did you intend a triumph of verticality?

ROOK: I intended to teach children where seeds grow.

[VELI TAKES NOTES.]

VELI: That may be one layer.

[A LITTLE MONKEY CLIMBS ONTO THE TABLE AND TURNS THE PAGES BACKWARD.]

ODA: The monkey is revisiting the text.

SUMI: The monkey wants the bookmark.

[THE MONKEY TAKES A LEAF BOOKMARK AND RUNS.]

ROOK: Return the bookmark.

[THE MONKEY HIDES IT BEHIND THE BOOK.]

VELI: The text has consumed its own paratext.

ROOK: The bookmark is in the book.

[SUMI FINDS IT AND RETURNS IT.]

ODA: Our conclusion?

SUMI: The seed grows into a tree.

VELI: The ending remains open.

ROOK: It is not open. There are no more pages.

[THE MONKEY TURNS THE BOOK OVER AND USES IT AS A SEAT.]

ODA: A new reading position.

ROOK: Please do not bend the cover.


[ODA HOLDS UP THE THREE-PAGE BOOK FOR THE CAMERA.]

ODA: Readers, what does the empty sky on page two mean?

SUMI: There is a cloud in the corner.

VELI: A cloud that refuses to dominate the frame.

ROOK: I left room for children to draw their own weather.

ODA: The audience may send in a cloud.

[THE MONKEY TAKES A CRAYON AND DRAWS ON A SCRAP PAPER INSERT.]

ROOK: Not on the book.

SUMI: It has drawn a small branch.

VELI: The monkey contributes a counter-reading.

ROOK: Put the drawing beside the book, please.

[SUMI PLACES THE DRAWING NEXT TO THE COVER.]
[ROOK SLIDES THE BOOK BACK INTO ITS LIBRARY SLEEVE.]


SUMI: The book is safe.


ROOK: There are no pages after three.


[THE MONKEY SITS ON THE BOOK AGAIN. ROOK PLACES A CUSHION UNDER IT.]


[THE HOST CLOSES THE BOOK. THE MONKEY SITS ON TOP OF IT.]

VELI: The book has become a platform.

SUMI: It is still due tomorrow.

ROOK: And the bookmark is due with it.
