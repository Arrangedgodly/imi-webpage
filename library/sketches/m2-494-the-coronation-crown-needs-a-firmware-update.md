# The Coronation Crown Needs a Firmware Update

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: royal pageantry, ceremonial protocol, and product launches that interrupt a formal event*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: REGENT, a mandrill; HERALD, an orangutan; TECHNICIAN, a macaque; PAGE, a capuchin; CHOIR LEADER, a gibbon.

[A palace hall. The regent sits on a cushion. A jeweled crown rests on a velvet pillow beside a small charging cable. The herald holds a scroll.] 

HERALD: We begin the coronation with the ancient words, the silver bell, and the crown.

REGENT: Is the crown ready?

TECHNICIAN: It needs to finish updating.

HERALD: The crown is older than the palace.

TECHNICIAN: The jewel lights are new. They need version four.

[The choir leader raises a hand. The choir hums. The crown emits a bright blue ring and a soft chime.] 

PAGE: It has announced itself.

REGENT: Does it know who I am?

TECHNICIAN: It knows you are near a ceremony.

HERALD: The crown has recognized the occasion.

TECHNICIAN: It has recognized the charging cable.

[The page reaches for the crown. The technician stops them.]

TECHNICIAN: Please do not unplug it during the update.

REGENT: I am about to wear my own crown.

TECHNICIAN: After the restart.

ACT ONE

[The herald unrolls the scroll. It is printed with the ceremony schedule and a small QR code.] 

HERALD: At the bell, the crown shall pass from the pillow to the regent's head.

TECHNICIAN: The screen says it will restart in nine minutes.

REGENT: The bell rings in one minute.

[The choir leader speeds up the hum. The crown's lights pulse with the tune, then begin pulsing faster.] 

CHOIR LEADER: The music is encouraging it.

TECHNICIAN: The music is changing the brightness setting.

PAGE: It is glowing brighter than the regent.

REGENT: I have been outshone by a circle.

[The technician turns the crown face down. The lights continue to glow against the pillow. The page moves the pillow, and the crown rolls toward the edge.] 

PAGE: It is leaving the ceremony.

[The regent catches the crown before it falls. The choir stops.]

HERALD: A noble catch.

TECHNICIAN: The update is paused because the crown was moved.

REGENT: Then resume it while I hold it.

TECHNICIAN: It needs to be still.

[The regent holds perfectly still. The choir leader whispers a count. The herald slowly turns the scroll to hide the QR code.] 

ACT TWO

[The crown chimes. The technician checks the screen.] 

TECHNICIAN: Update complete. It is ready to pair.

REGENT: Pair it with the throne.

TECHNICIAN: It pairs with a nearby phone.

[The page's phone chimes. The crown's lights switch from blue to pink.] 

PAGE: It has paired to me.

REGENT: The crown has chosen the page.

HERALD: By ceremony, the page must place the crown on the regent.

[The page unpairs the phone. The crown goes dark. The technician hands it to the page, who places it carefully on the regent's head.]

REGENT: At last.

[The crown chimes once. A small screen displays LOW BATTERY.] 

FINAL IMAGE: The regent raises a hand in greeting while the page holds the charging cable like a ceremonial scepter.
