# The Puppet Court Has a Witness Stand

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: courtroom testimony, courtroom cartoons, and legal proceedings where props insist on being heard*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: JUDGE, a baboon; LAWYER, a gibbon; WITNESS, an orangutan puppet operated by the lawyer; CLERK, a macaque; BAILIFF, a capuchin.

[A tiny courtroom stage. The judge sits behind a desk. The lawyer holds an orangutan puppet beside a cardboard witness stand.]

JUDGE: Please swear in the witness.

LAWYER: Do you promise to tell the truth?

WITNESS: I do.

JUDGE: The witness has answered before you moved your mouth.

LAWYER: The puppet is eager.

[The clerk raises a hand.]

CLERK: The witness stand is too high. The puppet cannot see over it.

BAILIFF: I can lower the box.

[The bailiff lowers the cardboard stand. The puppet's head falls forward and bumps the desk.]

WITNESS: I object to the furniture.

LAWYER: You cannot object to your own witness stand.

JUDGE: The court will hear the objection.

ACT ONE

[The lawyer raises the puppet. The puppet points toward a paper banana on the evidence table.]

LAWYER: Do you recognize this item?

WITNESS: I recognize that it is paper.

JUDGE: Strong start.

[The lawyer turns the banana around. It has a label reading EXHIBIT A.]

WITNESS: That is a label.

LAWYER: Can you tell the court where the real banana went?

WITNESS: I did not see a real banana.

[The clerk flips through a file.]

CLERK: The case concerns a missing paper banana from the stage prop box.

JUDGE: Then the witness has answered accurately.

[The lawyer lowers the puppet to whisper. The puppet's mouth stays open.]

LAWYER: You are supposed to say you saw it.

WITNESS: I was not there.

LAWYER: You were on my hand.

WITNESS: I was in the green room.

[The bailiff checks the puppet's pocket and finds a small banana sticker.]

BAILIFF: The witness has a sticker.

WITNESS: I collect labels.

[The judge asks for the evidence. The clerk places the paper banana on the stand. A loose hinge folds, sending it sliding into the puppet's lap.]

WITNESS: The exhibit has come to me.

JUDGE: Do not eat the evidence.

WITNESS: It is paper.

ACT TWO

[The lawyer asks the puppet to return the exhibit. The puppet hands it to the clerk, who places it in a sealed folder. The judge calls the case concluded.]

JUDGE: The missing paper banana was found on the witness stand. There is no further mystery.

LAWYER: The witness was helpful.

WITNESS: I was available.

[The bailiff unfolds the witness stand. A second paper banana drops from the cardboard base. The clerk catches it before it reaches the floor.]

CLERK: We have another exhibit.

JUDGE: It is the back of the first banana.

[The clerk turns it over. The words EXHIBIT B are written on the reverse.]

FINAL IMAGE: The puppet sits beside the judge as the clerk files the banana under both exhibit letters.
