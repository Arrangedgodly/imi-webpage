# The Farmers Market Has a Brand Consultant

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: farmers-market branding jargon imposed on actual produce vendors*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Consultant Vex, an orangutan; farmer Nari, a lemur; shopper Pip, a capuchin; baker Sela, a macaque.

EXT. FARMERS MARKET - MORNING

[Stalls stand beneath canvas awnings. Vex unrolls a banner that reads "ROOTED IN TOMORROW." Nari arranges carrots by size.]

VEX: We need an emotional through-line.

NARI: The carrots were pulled from that field this morning.

VEX: That's the origin story. What is the carrot's personal growth?

NARI: It grew underground.

PIP: Can I buy two?

VEX: First, let's define the customer journey.

[Pip reaches toward the carrots. Vex places a card in his path.]

PIP: This says "Discover."

VEX: You're at the top of the funnel.

PIP: I'm hungry.

NARI: That is the bottom of the stomach.

[Sela arrives with a basket of rolls. Vex flips the banner to reveal its other side: "BREAD: A SOFT LAUNCH."]

SELA: My bread is already baked.

VEX: The product is ready. The concept is still rising.

SELA: It will get stale while the concept rises.

[Vex asks Nari to tell a story. Nari holds up a carrot.]

NARI: This one was crooked because it grew around a stone.

VEX: A resilience narrative. Could it be more aspirational?

NARI: It made it out of the ground.


[Vex places a blank card before Nari.]

VEX: We need a product statement.

NARI: Carrots, three acorns a bunch.

VEX: That is the price. What does it mean?

NARI: It means three acorns.

PIP: It means lunch.

[Vex writes both statements down and underlines lunch.]

VEX: A direct message. Unusual.

SELA: My rolls are cooling. Can we sell something?

VEX: Selling is the measurable outcome. The brand is what happens before.

NARI: The carrots were growing before your banner arrived.

PIP: I aspire to eat it.

[Vex pins a small paper crown on the carrot. The crown slides down its tapered end and stops over the leafy top.]

VEX: The crown signals premium.

NARI: The leaves signal fresh.

PIP: The crown signals I should pay extra?

VEX: You're learning the language.

[He sets up a sample table. The banner blows over, covering the bread. Sela pulls it free; it drapes across the rolls like a tent.]

SELA: My soft launch has become a hard cover.

[Nari wedges a crooked carrot under the banner pole. The cloth holds. Vex steps back, impressed.]

VEX: We have a visual metaphor. The farmer props up the future.

NARI: The carrot props up your sign.

PIP: Can I get the one holding it?

NARI: It's working. Take it.

[Vex tries to remove the carrot for the display, but the banner slips. Sela catches it with a bread tray. The rolls tumble into Pip's basket.]

PIP: My customer journey has reached checkout.

SELA: Those are samples.

PIP: I sampled six.

[Vex raises the banner; the crooked carrot remains under the pole. Nari hands Pip two more carrots.]

VEX: The market's first integrated campaign.

NARI: It's a carrot and a pole.

PIP: And a very clear price.
