# The Professional Apology Tour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public relations tours that send executives to apologize at the wrong locations and for the wrong things*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Rell, a macaque executive; Nima, a gibbon publicist; Piri, a capuchin driver; and Sela, a lemur resident.

EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

Rell steps from a handcart wearing a sash labeled APOLOGY. Nima hands him a card.

NIMA: We are apologizing for the broken water bowl.

RELL: Where is it?

Sela points to the intact bowl. The broken one has already been taken to repair. Nima says the stop is for visibility. Rell stands beside the bowl and reads his statement: "We regret that the bowl was broken."

Sela asks who broke it. Rell looks at Nima. She turns the card over. The next line reads "We are investigating." Piri checks the itinerary. Their next stop is the orchard, to apologize for a missing pear.

PIRI: The pear was found yesterday.

NIMA: We have a statement for that too.

They travel to the orchard. The keeper greets them with a crate of pears. Rell begins his apology, but the keeper thinks he is thanking them for the delivery. Rell adjusts the tone. Nima asks him to express accountability without naming an accountable person.

RELL: We accept responsibility for the situation.

The keeper asks which situation. Piri says the missing pear. The keeper opens the crate; the pear is inside. Rell apologizes to the pear. Nima marks the second stop completed.

Their final stop is a public bench with one loose board. Rell reads an apology for the inconvenience. Sela sits on the bench. It wobbles. Piri asks whether this is the right apology. Nima says the bench issue is on tomorrow's schedule.

Sela stands and points out the loose screw. Rell takes a small tool from the cart and tightens it. The bench stops wobbling. Nima asks if this counts as a public apology. Rell says he can apologize for not fixing it sooner. Sela says the repair is enough.

Nima wants a group photograph. Rell and Sela stand beside the bench. Piri frames the shot, but the sash reads backwards. Nima turns it around. The sash now says APOLOGY TOUR in the correct direction. Rell asks whether the tour is finished. Nima checks the card. One line remains: "Apologize for the tour." Rell looks at the garden, orchard and bench.

RELL: I am sorry we came to tell you we were sorry.

Rell checks each pear for bruises before placing it on the cart. Piri asks if the gesture needs a statement. Sela says the fruit is for the trip, not the camera. Rell carries the bag himself.

Sela hands him a bag of pears for the ride back. Nima asks if this requires another statement. Piri says they can thank her. Rell does. The handcart rolls away without the sash, which is now tied around the repaired bench as a reminder to check the screws.
