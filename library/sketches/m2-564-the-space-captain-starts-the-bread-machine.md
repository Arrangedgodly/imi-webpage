# The Space Captain Starts the Bread Machine

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: starship launch scenes whose irreversible command is a small kitchen appliance*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: CAPTAIN KORA, a gibbon; PILOT PIM, a macaque; COOK NEL, an orangutan; ENGINEER RUD, a capuchin.

[A space-drama rehearsal set. Two consoles stand side by side: a painted flight desk and a real bread machine brought for lunch. Kora enters in a silver cloak. Pim sits at the flight desk. Nel reads the appliance booklet.]

KORA: Set a course beyond the known stars.

PIM: The backdrop is being repainted. We can rehearse the launch without it.

KORA: Then initiate the jump.

[Kora presses the bread machine's START button. A kneading paddle begins turning behind its closed lid.]

NEL: You initiated lunch.

KORA: Why was it next to the command chair?

RUD: This is the rehearsal break area. The command chair is next to lunch.

ACT ONE

[Kora peers at the display. It shows three hours until the loaf is finished.]

KORA: The journey will take longer than expected.

NEL: A loaf was in the pan. I set the recipe before you arrived.

PIM: At least we will reach bread at the scheduled time.

[Kora reaches toward the lid. Nel puts the booklet on the table between them.]

NEL: Leave it shut while it runs. You can read the baking stages here.

KORA: Knead. Rise. Bake. They sound like our three-act structure.

RUD: The painted flight buttons do nothing. Use those for the scene.

[Rud moves the flight desk to its marked rehearsal position, several steps away from the bread table. Pim takes the command chair with him. Nel stays beside the running machine.]

PIM: Bridge ready.

KORA: I will launch without refreshments.

[Kora approaches the painted desk and presses its red wooden button. Nothing happens. Pim makes a soft engine noise.]

KORA: The ship refuses me.

PIM: The sound operator is on break. I am standing in for the engines.

RUD: We need the dialogue and timing today. The effects will be added at the studio.

[Kora tries the launch line again. Pim answers on cue. Rud counts four beats before turning a painted wheel representing the star field.]

KORA: We have passed the outer planet.

NEL: From over here, it looks like you have passed the coffee tin.

[Rud moves the tin from the flight desk back to the bread table. Kora holds his pose until Rud finishes.]

KORA: Our cargo is secure.

NEL: My cargo is cinnamon.

ACT TWO

[The rehearsal concludes. Rud writes the working cues on a single page: wooden button, pilot response, star wheel. Pim checks his line against it. Kora returns the silver cloak to its hanger, clear of the bread machine.]

KORA: Lunch in three hours?

NEL: Two hours and forty minutes now. The rehearsal used twenty.

PIM: We can rehearse the return journey before then.

KORA: Does that need a different button?

RUD: Same wooden one. The script tells us which direction we're going.

[Kora looks toward the bread display, then firmly toward the rehearsal desk. Nel sets a note beside the appliance: CAST LUNCH.]

NEL: I will call you when it is ready. You need no countdown in the meantime.

PIM: We can save the countdown for the launch scene.

FINAL IMAGE: Kora practices his command at the powerless wooden console while the bread machine quietly advances to its first rise.
