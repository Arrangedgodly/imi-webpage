# The Award-Winning Waiting Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: prestige architecture showcases that praise waiting rooms for making people wait elegantly*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: presenter Nara, a macaque; architect Odo, a baboon; visitor Piri, a gibbon; receptionist Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. CLINIC WAITING ROOM - DAY

Nara films a row of chairs.

NARA: This design has won a civic comfort award.

PIRI: I have been waiting for twenty minutes.

ODO: The chairs are comfortable.

Tavi says the award is for the room, not the wait. Nara asks Odo to describe the design. He points to the windows, which bring in daylight. Piri says the sunlight is in her eyes. Tavi closes a blind.

Nara says the adjustable blinds create a personalized atmosphere. Piri asks how long until her appointment. Tavi checks the list; she is next.

Odo demonstrates the chair spacing. The chairs are close enough for conversation but far enough for privacy. Piri's neighbor sneezes. The room has tissues on a side table, but the table is behind the chairs. Tavi moves it closer.

Nara says the room supports community and care. Piri asks if the award included a clock. There is no clock. Odo says clocks can increase anxiety. Piri asks if the clinic has a way to know when appointments are due.

Tavi says they use a list. She calls Piri's name. Piri stands, but the door is on the other side of the room. She walks around the chairs, passing the camera.

NARA: A seamless transition.

PIRI: I was called.

Odo moves one chair to clear a path. Nara asks if the room will still meet design standards. Tavi says it will be easier to use. Piri enters the exam room. The camera remains on the chairs.

Odo asks Piri to rate the chair after she returns. She says the chair was fine; the wait was the part she noticed. Tavi says the appointment list has moved. Nara asks whether the room can display the expected wait time. Tavi says she can write it on the board. Odo worries the board will distract from the architecture. Piri says a useful sign is part of a comfortable room. Nara places a small board by the door. Tavi writes "next appointment: Piri." The room now has a clock and a list. Odo says the award committee may object. Tavi says the patients will know when they are called. Piri returns to the chair and sits beside the window, away from the direct sun.

Tavi adds the blind's position to the room checklist. Piri says she can now see the waiting board and avoid the glare. Nara records the change as an adjustment, not an award feature.

The chair stays by the window.

Tavi smiles.

Piri sits down again and waits quietly.

TAG

Nara interviews the empty seat.

NARA: How was your experience?

TAVI: It is still waiting.
