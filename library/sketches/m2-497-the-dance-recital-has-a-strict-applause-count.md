# The Dance Recital Has a Strict Applause Count

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: recital judging, synchronized dance competitions, and audiences coached to react on cue*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: DANCE CAPTAIN, a gibbon; JUDGE, an orangutan; STAGE MANAGER, a baboon; DANCER, a macaque; APPLAUSE COACH, a capuchin.

[A recital stage. Four tape marks form a square. A sign reads CLAP AFTER THE FINAL POSE. The applause coach holds two wooden clappers.]

STAGE MANAGER: The dance ends on the music, then the audience claps.

APPLAUSE COACH: On my cue. Two beats, then a respectful rumble.

DANCE CAPTAIN: The dance has its own ending.

JUDGE: I will score the timing and the technique.

[The dancer begins a slow turn. The captain counts with tiny hand movements. The audience holds still. The applause coach silently lifts a clapper.]

DANCER: I can see the clapper in my peripheral vision.

APPLAUSE COACH: That is why I stand behind the judge.

[The dancer finishes the turn, but one foot lands outside the taped square. The captain circles the mark with a pencil.]

DANCE CAPTAIN: The foot crossed the border.

DANCER: The floor mark is drifting.

[The stage manager checks the tape. One corner has curled up.]

STAGE MANAGER: It is a loose corner, not a border.

ACT ONE

[The captain restarts the music. The dancer repeats the turn, keeping both feet inside the square. The applause coach claps twice before the pose.]

JUDGE: The applause arrived early.

APPLAUSE COACH: I saw a beautiful ankle.

DANCE CAPTAIN: The ankle was still moving.

[The coach tries again. The dancer holds a final pose. The judge raises a score card. The audience remains silent.]

APPLAUSE COACH: Now.

[The clappers snap. A small prop bird above the stage drops a paper feather onto the judge's head.]

JUDGE: Is that part of the choreography?

DANCER: It is part of the scenery.

[The stage manager retrieves the feather. The captain inspects the prop bird, which is a spring-loaded toy.]

DANCE CAPTAIN: The bird has better timing than the coach.

APPLAUSE COACH: The bird gets a cue light.

[The stage manager turns the cue light toward the audience. The coach nods too soon and claps again. The dancer has not started.]

DANCER: That was the warm-up.

JUDGE: I award the warm-up one point for surprise.

ACT TWO

[The stage manager tapes the curled corner flat and covers the cue light. The coach sets down the clappers and watches the dancer's feet.]

DANCE CAPTAIN: Music, please.

[The dancer performs the turn again, crosses the square, and freezes with both arms raised. The judge displays a score. The coach waits, then looks to the audience.]

APPLAUSE COACH: The pose is finished. Two beats.

[The audience claps. The toy bird drops one final feather, which lands in the dancer's open hand.]

DANCER: That one was on time.

JUDGE: The dancer gets the highest score. The bird gets a special mention.

FINAL IMAGE: The coach practices a silent clap while the toy bird sits under the covered cue light.
