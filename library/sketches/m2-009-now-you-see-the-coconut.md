# Now You See the Coconut

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Stage magic shows and impossible-sounding illusions*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Magician Mako, a capuchin; assistant Vela, a gibbon; critic Bram, a macaque; volunteer Dot, a tamarin.

[STAGE: A VELVET CURTAIN, TWO COCONUTS, A HAT WITH A HOLE IN IT.] 

MAKO: Ladies, gentlemen, and monkeys with excellent ears, I will make this coconut vanish.

VELA: Which coconut?

MAKO: The left one.

BRAM: From where I sit, the left one is on the right.

MAKO: The coconut nearest me.

DOT: We are on the same stage.

MAKO: That is why the trick is difficult.

VELA: Shall I dim the lights?

MAKO: No, I need to see the coconut.

BRAM: So do we.

[MAKO COVERS THE COCONUT WITH THE HAT.] 

MAKO: Watch closely. Count backward from three.

DOT: Three.

BRAM: Two.

VELA: One.

MAKO: It has vanished.

DOT: The hat is still on it.

MAKO: The coconut is hidden from view.

BRAM: That's not vanishing.

MAKO: It's the first phase.

VELA: The second phase is lifting the hat.

MAKO: The second phase is a surprise.

BRAM: I have seen the coconut.

MAKO: You saw a coconut-shaped possibility.

DOT: Can I volunteer?

MAKO: You will hold the hat while I turn away.

DOT: I am holding the hat.

MAKO: I will turn around.

[MAKO TURNS. VELA SLIDES THE COCONUT INTO THE HOLE IN THE HAT.] 

VELA: It is gone.

DOT: It is in the hat.

MAKO: It is gone from the table.

BRAM: You are redefining magic.

MAKO: I am a magician. I define the stage.

DOT: I can hear it rolling.

MAKO: Don't listen.

BRAM: A coconut cannot be silenced by instruction.

[COCONUT ROLLS OUT FROM UNDER THE CURTAIN.] 

VELA: It has escaped.

MAKO: The coconut has a mind of its own.

DOT: It is a coconut.

BRAM: It has crossed the footlights.

MAKO: That is the grand reveal.

DOT: It is heading for the orchestra.

VELA: I will catch it.

MAKO: Don't touch the coconut.

VELA: It is touching my foot.

BRAM: The audience can see the trick now.

MAKO: The audience is part of the trick.

DOT: I will stop it with my tail.

[DOT CATCHES THE COCONUT WITH HER TAIL. IT SPINS IN PLACE.] 

MAKO: The coconut is floating.

DOT: It is rolling on my tail.

BRAM: Better magic than the first act.

MAKO: For the finale, I will make the second coconut vanish.

VELA: There is only one coconut.

MAKO: Exactly.

DOT: Now you see it.

BRAM: Now we all hear it.

VELA: The coconut is under the front row.

MAKO: That is the audience participation part.

DOT: The front row is looking at me.

BRAM: They are looking at the coconut.

MAKO: I will take a bow.

VELA: The curtain is already down.

MAKO: Then I will bow to the curtain.

DOT: The curtain is not clapping.

MAKO: It has a very quiet audience.

[THE COCONUT DROPS INTO THE HAT. CURTAIN FALLS.]
