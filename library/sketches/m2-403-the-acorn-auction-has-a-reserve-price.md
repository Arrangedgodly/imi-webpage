# The Acorn Auction Has a Reserve Price

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: auction broadcasts that turn small objects into high-stakes investment drama*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Auctioneer Vex, a macaque; cataloguer Mara, a gibbon; bidder Pip, a capuchin; bidder Nari, a lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY AUCTION ROOM - EVENING

[A single acorn sits on a velvet stand. Vex faces an audience of four. Mara holds a clipboard. Pip has both hands folded in his lap. Nari keeps one cheek pouch conspicuously full.]

VEX: Lot one. An acorn with a firm shell and a rich history.

MARA: It fell from the tree this afternoon.

VEX: History begins somewhere.

[He taps his gavel.]

VEX: We open at ten coins.

[Pip raises a finger.]

PIP: One.

VEX: One what?

PIP: Coin. It is an acorn.

MARA: The reserve is fifty.

VEX: The catalog says fifty.

MARA: I wrote that number to stop you opening at ten.

[Nari slowly raises her hand. Pip turns to her. Her cheek pouch bulges.]

NARI: Fifty.

VEX: Fifty coins?

NARI: Fifty acorns. I can deliver today.

[Vex pauses for the camera that is not there. Mara points to a small phone on a tripod, recording the auction.]

VEX: We have an institutional bidder.

MARA: She is a squirrel.

NARI: I am a lemur. The squirrel is my delivery partner.

[Pip reaches for the acorn. Vex drops the gavel on the stand. It bounces; the acorn rolls toward the edge. Nari catches it in her other hand.]

VEX: The lot is active.

MARA: The lot is in her palm.

NARI: I bid fifty-one.

PIP: I bid two, and I have a bag.

[Vex looks at the camera.]

VEX: Fierce competition from a prepared collector.

MARA: Pip came to buy a snack.

[Pip opens his bag. It is empty except for a lunch napkin. Nari presents a receipt for fifty acorns from the orchard co-op. Mara checks the reserve note. It says fifty coins, not fifty acorns.]

MARA: Currency. Not volume.

NARI: Then fifty-one coins. I have those too.

VEX: Sold once to Nari. The reserve is met.

[Vex raises the acorn for the close-up. Nari's gaze follows it. Pip's gaze follows Nari's cheek pouch. The acorn's shadow makes the item look enormous on the phone screen.]

VEX: This may be the largest acorn in the room.

MARA: It's the only one.

[Pip lifts the velvet stand. A second acorn is stuck beneath it.]

PIP: Two lots.

[Vex strikes the gavel. It hits the table leg and rolls under the chair. Everyone dives to find it. Nari guards her purchased acorn.]

MARA: Who won?

NARI: I did. I have the reserve.

PIP: I won the other one. I found it.

VEX: Discovery is not a recognized bid.

[Pip takes the acorn anyway and cracks it with his teeth.]

MARA: That is the second lot.

PIP: I am not bidding on it. I am eating it.

FINAL BEAT: Mara lists the velvet stand as the next lot. Nari bids one coin. Vex turns to the camera for a triumphant sign-off, then starts the auction over.
