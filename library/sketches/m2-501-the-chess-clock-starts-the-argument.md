# The Chess Clock Starts the Argument

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: tournament chess broadcasts, grandmaster analysis, and commentators who over-read every move*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: PLAYER ONE, a baboon; PLAYER TWO, a macaque; COMMENTATOR, an orangutan; CLOCK OPERATOR, a capuchin; FLOOR JUDGE, a gibbon.

[A tournament table. The players sit opposite a chessboard. A digital clock ticks. The commentator whispers into a microphone.]

COMMENTATOR: Player One has touched the knight. That may signal an attack, a retreat, or a request for a glass of water.

PLAYER ONE: I was moving it.

PLAYER TWO: The touch rule says you must move it.

PLAYER ONE: That was the plan.

[Player One moves the knight. The clock operator taps the timer. The commentator leans toward the board.]

COMMENTATOR: The knight enters the center. The center has not agreed to this.

FLOOR JUDGE: Please keep commentary outside the playing area.

COMMENTATOR: I am whispering.

FLOOR JUDGE: Your whisper has a microphone.

[Player Two studies the board, then lifts a pawn. The floor judge raises one finger.]

FLOOR JUDGE: Touch rule.

PLAYER TWO: I know. I am thinking with my hand.

PLAYER ONE: That is an aggressive thought.

ACT ONE

[The commentator draws a diagram on a small board. The arrow points through the player's water cup.]

COMMENTATOR: If the pawn advances, the bishop line opens.

[The clock operator moves the cup to protect it. The commentator's arrow now points at the floor judge.]

COMMENTATOR: And the judge is under pressure.

FLOOR JUDGE: I am not part of the position.

[Player Two moves the pawn. Player One stares at the board for several seconds.]

COMMENTATOR: Silence. A bold defensive silence.

PLAYER ONE: I am counting.

CLOCK OPERATOR: The clock is counting too.

[The clock ticks rapidly. Player One reaches for the knight, remembers the touch rule, and folds both hands under the table.]

PLAYER ONE: I have not touched anything.

PLAYER TWO: You touched the table.

FLOOR JUDGE: The table is not a chess piece.

COMMENTATOR: It is supporting the entire match.

[The commentator places a finger on the diagram to show a variation. The board's camera follows the finger and broadcasts a close-up of the commentator's hand instead of the pieces.]

COMMENTATOR: We are looking at the hand that saw the line.

FLOOR JUDGE: We are looking at your knuckle.

ACT TWO

[Player One makes a legal move and the clock operator starts Player Two's timer. Player Two knocks over the water cup while reaching toward the clock. Water spills across the table, but the players lift the board before it reaches the pieces. The floor judge hands them a towel.]

PLAYER TWO: I resign. The board is dry, but my position is not recoverable.

FLOOR JUDGE: Resignation recorded. Player One wins this game.

[The clock operator stops the clock. The judge checks the pieces and the score sheet, then clears the wet table with the towel. The commentator disconnects the microphone before the boards are reset.]

COMMENTATOR: We will now begin a new round after the players change seats.

PLAYER ONE: Please keep the cup on the side table.

[The players reset the board only after the result has been announced and the table is dry. The clock operator loads fresh time onto both stopped timers.]

PLAYER TWO: I am ready for the next game.

[The commentator takes one step back, points the dead microphone toward the board, and whispers anyway.]
FINAL IMAGE: The clean board waits under a stopped clock while the commentator whispers into the disconnected microphone.
