# The Aquarium's Quiet Hour Gets a Megaphone

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: visitor attractions that advertise calm experiences while promoting them at full volume*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Aquarium host Toma, a gibbon; keeper Runi, a macaque; visitor Kito, a capuchin; and promoter Venn, a baboon.

INT. AQUARIUM TUNNEL - MORNING

A sign reads QUIET HOUR: PLEASE SPEAK SOFTLY. Venn stands beside a portable speaker. Toma welcomes Kito and Runi.

TOMA: The tunnel is calm and peaceful.

VENN: Today's quiet hour is sponsored by the loudest sale in town.

[The speaker booms a jingle. A school of fish darts behind a rock.]

RUNI: The fish have left the event.

VENN: They are creating a dramatic reveal.

KITO: I cannot hear the water.

TOMA: The water is silent.

VENN: Then I will narrate it.

[He speaks into the megaphone: "Gentle water flowing." The fish disappear completely.]

INT. TUNNEL - MOMENTS LATER

Runi lowers the speaker volume. Venn holds up a promotional banner. It unrolls down the glass and covers the reef.

TOMA: Visitors are here to see the exhibit.

VENN: They can see the banner.

KITO: The banner says "Be amazed by the ocean." I am amazed by the fabric.

RUNI: The turtle is behind it.

[The turtle swims past underneath the banner. Venn lifts one corner, accidentally pulling the whole banner sideways.]

VENN: A moving display.

TOMA: It is a curtain.

INT. TUNNEL - CONTINUOUS

Venn unveils a ring light shaped like a coral reef. He switches it on; blue circles ripple across the glass.

VENN: A serene selfie station will bring visitors closer to the animals.

RUNI: It will bring the light closer to the animals.

[The fish turn away from the ring light. Kito rotates the sign toward the visitors; the reflected circles now land on Venn. He poses in the pattern, smiling.]

KITO: You have become the exhibit.

TOMA: Please step back from the glass.

[Venn steps back with the power cable caught around his heel. He drags the ring light on its wheeled stand and bumps the banner, which rolls back over the window.]

INT. KEEPER STATION - LATER

Runi offers a small sign: LET THE ANIMALS REST. Venn adds a QR code and an animated flashing arrow.

RUNI: A plain reminder would do.

VENN: Visitors need a call to action.

KITO: The action is being quiet.

[The flashing arrow reflects on the glass. The fish turn toward their reflections, then swim to the dark side of the tank.]

TOMA: We should dim the signs and turn off the speaker.

VENN: But how will people know the quiet hour is happening?

RUNI: They will notice it is quiet.

[She switches off the speaker. The tunnel settles. Kito leans toward a viewing window; the fish return.]

KITO: There they are.

VENN: I can announce their return.

TOMA: You can watch it.

[The turtle surfaces near the glass. Venn opens his mouth to speak, then shuts it. The turtle stares at him until he lowers the banner.]

RUNI: The turtle has given the quiet-hour review.

VENN: What did it say?

KITO: Nothing. It was peaceful.
