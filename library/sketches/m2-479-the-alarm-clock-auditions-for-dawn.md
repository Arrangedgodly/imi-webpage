# The Alarm Clock Auditions for Dawn

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: talent auditions, theatrical monologues, and alarm clocks competing for the loudest opening cue*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: DIRECTOR, a baboon; ALARM ONE, a macaque; ALARM TWO, a gibbon; SLEEPER, an orangutan; TECH, a capuchin.

[A rehearsal room at dawn. Two alarm clocks sit on a table. The sleeper wears an eye mask on a padded chair.]

DIRECTOR: We need a gentle wake-up for the morning show.

ALARM ONE: I offer a bell at low volume.

ALARM TWO: I offer a rooster with a cymbal.

SLEEPER: I offer another ten minutes.

[The director asks each clock to audition. Alarm One rings softly. The sleeper lifts one eyelid and reaches for the off switch.] 

DIRECTOR: Subtle. Try again with more presence.

[Alarm One rings louder. The sleeper rolls away and pulls a cushion over one ear.] 

ALARM ONE: The audience is not responding.

TECH: The audience is asleep.

[Alarm Two plays its rooster-cymbal cue. The sleeper sits upright, startled, and knocks the padded chair's pillow onto the floor.] 

SLEEPER: That was not a wake-up. It was an opening night.

DIRECTOR: Excellent energy, but too much cymbal.

ALARM TWO: I can remove the rooster.

TECH: The rooster is the sound file. The cymbal is the actual alarm bell.

[The tech lowers the volume and disconnects the cymbal. The sleeper returns the pillow to the chair and lies back down. The director requests one final audition.] 

ALARM ONE: I can chime once, then wait.

ALARM TWO: I can start with one quiet chirp.

SLEEPER: I will wake when the room is bright.

DIRECTOR: The show starts before sunrise.

[The tech opens the blackout curtain a hand's width. A narrow line of morning light enters. Alarm One chimes once. The sleeper sits up without being startled.] 

SLEEPER: That works.

ALARM TWO: I could add the cymbal after the light.

SLEEPER: You could remain unplugged.

[The director awards Alarm One the cue. Alarm Two's rooster file is saved under "OPTIONAL," and the tech labels its volume knob.] 

[The director asks the sleeper to test the cue from the chair one more time. The sleeper sits with eyes closed while the tech draws the curtain to the same narrow opening.]

TECH: Light cue ready.

DIRECTOR: Single chime.

[Alarm One chimes once. The sleeper opens both eyes and sits up. Alarm Two's rooster file remains saved but does not play.]

SLEEPER: I am awake, and no one has been startled.

ALARM TWO: I could play the cymbal after the show.

DIRECTOR: The show is the quiet chime.

[The tech labels the selected clock "DAWN" and turns the unused alarm face down. The director checks that the blackout curtain is no longer covering the window.]

TECH: Morning is visible.

SLEEPER: That was the cue.

FINAL IMAGE: The sleeper faces the sunrise while the quiet alarm waits for its single cue.
