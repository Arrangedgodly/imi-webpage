# The Pottery Judge Wants a Flaw

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: craft competitions that praise imperfection only when it looks deliberately artistic*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Judge Rova, a gibbon; Potter Miri, a macaque; Host Tavi, a capuchin; and Kiln Tech Odo, a baboon.

INT. COMMUNITY POTTERY STUDIO - DAY

Three bowls sit on a table. Rova circles them with a score sheet. Tavi holds a microphone. Miri has clay on her apron.

TAVI: The theme is "beautifully imperfect."

MIRI: I made a bowl that holds water.

ROVA: It is too even.

MIRI: The rim is level.

ROVA: A little wobble would show character.

ODO: The kiln has a shelf that wobbles.

TAVI: Could we put the bowl on the shelf for the reveal?

MIRI: The shelf needs repair first.

ROVA: We are judging the work, not the shelf.

[ODO SETS THE BOWL ON THE TABLE. IT SITS LEVEL.]

ROVA: This one has a calm silhouette.

MIRI: It is a bowl.

INT. COMMUNITY POTTERY STUDIO - LATER

Tavi places a scarf over the bowls. Rova studies the score sheet. Odo carries a small crooked cup.

TAVI: We need a dramatic unveiling.

MIRI: The bowls are right there.

ROVA: The audience wants to see an accident become art.

ODO: This cup cracked when it cooled.

ROVA: That's promising.

MIRI: It leaks.

ROVA: It has a visible history.

MIRI: It cannot hold tea.

TAVI: We can call it a sculptural vessel.

[ODO POURS WATER INTO THE CUP. IT LEAKS ONTO THE TABLE.]

ODO: The water is now part of the installation.

MIRI: Please keep it away from the electric wheel.

ROVA: That practical concern is another layer.

TAVI: Can we get the crowd to gasp?

[THE AUDIENCE GIVES A POLITE "OH." ROVA NODS AND WRITES IT DOWN.]

INT. COMMUNITY POTTERY STUDIO - JUDGING TABLE

Rova lines up the pieces. Odo places a cup of water beside each.

ROVA: We should test the vessels for function.

TAVI: The show is about beauty.

MIRI: The theme says pottery.

ODO: The bowl holds water. The cup does not.

[ROVA POURS WATER INTO THE BOWL. IT HOLDS. SHE POURS INTO THE CUP. IT LEAKS AGAIN.]

ROVA: The cup has a memorable edge.

TAVI: The edge is a crack.

MIRI: It is where the water came out.

INT. COMMUNITY POTTERY STUDIO - AWARDS

Rova presents a ribbon. Miri holds her sturdy bowl. Odo wipes the table.

ROVA: The prize goes to the cup whose crack tells a story.

MIRI: What does the story say?

ROVA: That it did not survive the kiln.

TAVI: Our winner is brave.

ODO: The bowl is still holding water.

[ODO FILLS MIRI'S BOWL. IT HOLDS. HE PLACES IT BESIDE THE AWARD CUP, WHICH LEAKS INTO THE RIBBON BOX.]

ROVA: The box has a beautiful stain.

MIRI: The box is not in the competition.

TAVI: It is now the audience favorite.

[MIRI USES HER BOWL TO CARRY WATER TO THE WHEEL. THE JUDGES FOLLOW THE LEAKING CUP WITH MOPS.]
