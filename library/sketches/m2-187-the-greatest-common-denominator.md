# The Greatest Common Denominator

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school math competitions that prize speed over understanding until the timer cannot explain its own answer*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Pava, a gibbon math host; Tavi, a macaque contestant; Rell, a capuchin scorekeeper; and Omi, a baboon challenger.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - AFTERNOON

Pava announces a timed multiplication round. Tavi and Omi stand at buzzers. Rell holds the score cards.

PAVA: First question: two times three.

Tavi answers six. Omi answers "a dozen" and claims he was thinking of two fruit baskets. Rell asks for clarification. Omi says the baskets hold six each.

RELL: That is twelve.

Pava adds a bonus question: two groups of three. Tavi answers six again. Omi says the wording changed, so his answer should count twice. Rell checks the rules. The rules mention only one point per correct answer.

Omi buzzes before the next question is finished. Pava asks him to wait until the full problem is read. Omi says he heard enough. The problem is "one plus one minus one." Omi answers two. Tavi answers one. Rell draws the calculation on a board. Omi says he used a faster route.

PAVA: Which route?

OMI: The first number.

The crowd laughs. Omi says they have distracted him, and requests a quiet room. Rell moves the buzzer table to a corner. The crowd follows him to watch.

Pava switches to a visual problem using three sticks. She asks how many triangles they make. Tavi forms one triangle. Omi adds a stick and creates another. Rell asks whether adding sticks is allowed. Pava says yes. Omi adds every stick from the gym set until the shapes collapse into a pile.

OMI: Infinite triangles.

RELL: One pile.

Tavi points out one triangle remains intact beneath the sticks. Rell gives him the point. Omi says the game is biased toward stable constructions. Pava hands him a stack of foam pieces. He builds a tower, which falls before the timer sounds.

The final round is mental arithmetic. Pava asks the scorekeeper how many points each has. Rell tries to add the cards while the timer runs. The timer buzzes. Omi answers "zero" before Rell finishes. It is correct because the timer has reset the scoreboard.

Tavi asks if the round is over. Pava checks the rules: the final question is to count how many questions were asked. They review the cards. Rell counts them and arrives at seven. Omi says it was eight because of the bonus. Tavi says they are both counting the bonus differently.

Pava announces the tie. Omi asks for a tiebreaker. Rell hands both contestants the chalk and asks them to write the total. They write seven and eight. The gym clock stops.

PAVA: The timer has declined to score.

Tavi and Omi shake hands. The crowd cheers. Rell erases the board, but leaves one triangle. Omi looks at it.

OMI: That one is not timed.
