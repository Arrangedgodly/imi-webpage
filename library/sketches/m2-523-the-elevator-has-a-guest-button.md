# The Elevator Has a Guest Button

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury-building tours, concierge service, and elevators with too many personalized options*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: CONCIERGE, an orangutan; TENANT, a baboon; GUEST, a capuchin; TECHNICIAN, a macaque; ELEVATOR VOICE, a gibbon.

[A lobby. An elevator panel has buttons for lobby, roof, laundry, and GUEST. The tenant presses GUEST.]

ELEVATOR VOICE: Please state your guest's preferred destination.

TENANT: The lobby.

ELEVATOR VOICE: Do they prefer a scenic route?

TENANT: They are standing beside me.

[The guest steps into the elevator. The concierge hands them a welcome card.]

CONCIERGE: The building offers a quiet ride, a lively ride, or a ride with local history.

GUEST: I would like to go up one floor.

ELEVATOR VOICE: One floor has no history.

ACT ONE

[The elevator doors close. The tenant presses two. The elevator asks for a reason.]

ELEVATOR VOICE: Work, rest, or social call?

TENANT: Rest.

ELEVATOR VOICE: Your floor contains a gym.

GUEST: We can walk.

[The elevator opens on the lobby again. The technician checks the panel.]

TECHNICIAN: The guest button is supposed to call the concierge.

CONCIERGE: It is taking guests on a tour.

[The tenant presses GUEST again. The elevator plays a recorded welcome speech and begins rising. It stops at the laundry floor.]

ELEVATOR VOICE: Historic laundry room. Built three years ago.

GUEST: I did not ask for history.

TENANT: Can we press stop?

[The concierge reaches for the emergency stop. The technician blocks the button.]

TECHNICIAN: That stops the elevator. Use the panel reset.

[The technician opens the service panel. Inside, wires labeled "GUEST," "TOUR," and "SOUVENIR" are crossed. The guest reads a display that says TAKE A PHOTO.]

GUEST: The elevator wants a souvenir.

CONCIERGE: The building has a gift shop.

[The tenant presses the service reset. The elevator doors open on the lobby. The guest steps out and walks to the stairs.]

[The elevator stops between floors. The guest presses the help button. The concierge answers from the lobby desk.]

CONCIERGE: Are you in the elevator?

GUEST: Yes. We are not looking for history.

[The technician opens the service panel from the outside. The elevator is level with the lobby; the doors had closed on a loose welcome card. The card is caught in the sensor.]

TECHNICIAN: The souvenir was blocking the door.

TENANT: The welcome card has delayed our visit.

[The technician pulls the card free. The doors open, and everyone steps out onto the lobby floor.]

ACT TWO

[The technician disconnects the tour speaker and reconnects the guest button to the concierge desk. The tenant presses GUEST from the lobby. The desk bell rings.]

ELEVATOR VOICE: Concierge notified.

CONCIERGE: Hello. How may I help your guest?

GUEST: I would like the stairs.

[The concierge points to the stairwell. The guest walks up one floor. The tenant stays by the elevator, waiting.]

FINAL IMAGE: The elevator doors open onto an empty lobby as the guest's welcome card rides up by itself.
