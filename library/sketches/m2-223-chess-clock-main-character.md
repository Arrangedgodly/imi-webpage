# The Chess Clock Is the Main Character

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive chess commentary that assigns more drama to the clock than to the players' moves*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Commentator Hara, a gibbon; Player Niko, a macaque; Player Venn, a capuchin; and Arbiter Omi, a baboon.

INT. CHESS CLUB - EVENING

Niko and Venn sit across a chessboard. Hara leans into a microphone. Omi sets a digital clock between them.

HARA: We begin with twelve minutes each. The clock will decide who has the nerve to think.

NIKO: The board decides the move.

VENN: The clock is louder.

[OMI PRESSES START. THE CLOCK TICKS AUDIBLY.]

HARA: Niko studies the center. Venn studies the clock.

VENN: I can see the clock from here.

HARA: An aggressive opening.

OMI: No one has moved yet.

NIKO: I am choosing between two safe moves.

HARA: The clock has taken six seconds.

VENN: I choose to move the knight.

[VENN MOVES A KNIGHT. HARA LEANS TOWARD THE TIMER.]

HARA: A bold 90-degree turn.

OMI: The knight always moves that way.

NIKO: The clock has taken another six seconds while we discuss the knight.

HARA: The clock has become the leading player.

NIKO: The clock is not playing.

INT. CHESS CLUB - LATER

The board has several pieces moved. Hara points to the clock. Venn has three minutes left.

HARA: Venn's time is falling. The tension is measurable.

VENN: My bishop is pinned.

HARA: The clock does not care about the bishop.

OMI: Please keep the commentary quiet while players think.

HARA: The quiet is also tense.

[NIKO MOVES A PAWN. VENN SLAMS THE CLOCK BUTTON. IT BEEPS TWICE.]

VENN: My turn.

HARA: A decisive press.

OMI: It was a normal press.

NIKO: I can hear you narrating the button.

HARA: I will whisper.

HARA: The button receives a gentle tap.

VENN: That is somehow worse.

NIKO: I have moved my rook.

HARA: The rook travels in a straight line. The clock travels in seconds.

OMI: The rook cannot travel through the bishop.

VENN: The clock can travel while we explain that.

[THE TIMER BEEPS. HARA LOOKS UP, EXPECTING A PIECE TO HAVE MOVED.]

OMI: That is the low-time warning.

HARA: A sound cue for a piece that is still.

NIKO: It is my move.

INT. CHESS CLUB - FINAL MOVE

The players study the board. Venn's clock reaches zero. Omi stops the timer.

HARA: The clock has fallen.

VENN: I still have a legal move.

OMI: Your time has expired. Niko wins on time.

NIKO: I had a mate in two.

HARA: We have a dramatic ending before the ending.

VENN: Can we finish the game anyway?

OMI: The result is recorded.

[THEY RESET THE CLOCK FOR A FRIENDLY FINISH. NIKO MAKES THE MATE. HARA LEANS TOWARD THE BOARD.]

HARA: And the clock remains silent.

OMI: I turned it off.

VENN: That's the first good move it made all night.
