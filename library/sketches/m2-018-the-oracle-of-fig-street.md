# The Oracle of Fig Street

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Psychic hotlines and theatrical fortune telling*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Oracle Numa, a lemur; caller Lio, a macaque; caller Babs, a squirrel monkey; assistant Zed, a gibbon.

[STAGE: PURPLE CURTAIN. A CRYSTAL BALL IS CLEARLY A GLASS SALAD BOWL.] 

NUMA: Welcome to the Oracle of Fig Street. I sense a question arriving from far away.

ZED: The phone rang.

NUMA: I sensed the ring.

LIO: Oracle, will I find my lost sandal?

NUMA: I see a path, a gate, and a laundry basket.

LIO: The sandal is in my laundry basket.

NUMA: The vision is strong.

ZED: Your sandal is under the chair.

LIO: I put it there.

NUMA: A powerful memory has surfaced.

LIO: It is a sandal.

NUMA: The universe speaks in footwear.

[A SMALL BELL RINGS. ZED TURNS OVER A CARD.] 

NUMA: Caller two, I sense you are holding a cup.

BABS: I am. It is a telephone.

NUMA: The spirits are using modern tools.

BABS: I need to know whether I should take a new job.

NUMA: I see a door opening.

BABS: The office door opens every time the fan runs.

ZED: The latch is loose.

NUMA: The future has a draft.

BABS: The job is in a greenhouse. I like plants, but I worry about leaving my friends.

NUMA: I see a message from a friend.

BABS: My friend just texted me.

NUMA: The vision is immediate.

ZED: It says, "Come visit on your day off."

BABS: I can do that.

NUMA: The cards agree.

ZED: They are blank.

NUMA: They are open to interpretation.

BABS: I think I will take the job and keep my day off.

NUMA: A brave choice.

[CRYSTAL BALL RATTLES. A SPOON FALLS OUT.] 

LIO: What is the spoon?

NUMA: It is from the salad bowl.

ZED: The bowl was our crystal ball.

NUMA: Then the future has given us a utensil.

LIO: Can you predict tomorrow?

NUMA: Tomorrow, someone will ask about a missing object.

ZED: The spoon is missing.

NUMA: The prophecy has come true.

ZED: I am holding it.

NUMA: Your hands are part of the mystery.

LIO: The sandal is found, and Babs has chosen the job.

NUMA: The callers have shaped their own future.

ZED: Can I have my spoon back?

NUMA: Yes. I predict you will wash it.

ZED: I was already going to wash it.

NUMA: The spirits are rarely surprised by good hygiene.

BABS: Do I owe you for the reading?

NUMA: The sign says five coins.

ZED: The sign is upside down.

NUMA: Then today the reading is free.

LIO: The future feels affordable.

ZED: The spoon is clean now.

NUMA: I foresaw that.

LIO: The bowl still needs washing.

ORACLE: Ask one final question.
TAVI: Will my vine reach the roof?
ORACLE: It will reach the roof if you climb it.
TAVI: That sounds like a riddle.
ORACLE: It is gardening with a dramatic pause.
TAVI: Do you have a forecast?
ORACLE: The roof is above you.
TAVI: The oracle has answered clearly.

[THE SALAD BOWL IS TURNED RIGHT SIDE UP. A SPOON TINKS INSIDE.]
