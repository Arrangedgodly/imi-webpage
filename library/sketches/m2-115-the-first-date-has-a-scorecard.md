# The First Date Has a Scorecard

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: dating reality shows and the conversion of conversation into competition*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: HOST MIRI, a lemur; DATE ONE FEN, a macaque; DATE TWO VEE, a gibbon; and PRODUCER PAX, a capuchin.

[A garden table is set with two cups and a scorecard. Miri stands between the chairs.] 

MIRI: Welcome to First Branch. Two monkeys will meet, answer prompts, and see whether they earn a second date.

FEN: Do we talk to each other?

PAX: After the opening question.

VEE: What is the opening question?

MIRI: Describe your ideal afternoon.

FEN: Sitting under a tree and sharing fruit.

VEE: Walking to the spring, then sitting under a tree.

PAX: Strong compatibility. Now select a card.

[FEN DRAWS A CARD.] 

FEN: "What is your most surprising habit?"

VEE: I turn a leaf over to check whether there is an insect beneath it.

FEN: I do that too.

MIRI: The scorecard has a leaf category.

PAX: Add one point to both.

VEE: Why is there a timer?

PAX: It keeps answers concise.

FEN: I can answer without a timer.

VEE: I can listen without a score.

MIRI: The next prompt asks who will plan the second date.

FEN: We could take the walk we both mentioned.

VEE: We can decide after we finish this one.

PAX: The show needs a decision before the commercial break.

FEN: Then the decision is to keep talking.

VEE: We do not need another date to have a conversation.

PAX: The scorecard has three measures: shared interests, eye contact, and dramatic pauses.

FEN: We share two interests. Shade and fruit.

VEE: We have not made eye contact. We were looking at the prompt cards.

MIRI: The timer is almost out. Look at each other.

[FEN AND VEE TURN AT THE SAME TIME, THEN BOTH TURN AWAY TO AVOID BUMPING HEADS.] 

PAX: That counts as a synchronized movement.

VEE: Is there a point for that?

PAX: There is if the producers agree.

FEN: I would rather ask Vee what kind of fruit they like.

VEE: Figs. What do you like?

FEN: Figs.

MIRI: The scorecard has awarded a match.

PAX: The fruit category was not on the card.

MIRI: Add one.

PAX: The card is full.

VEE: Then the card can have a second page.

[THE SCORECARD FLIPS OVER. THE BACK IS BLANK.] 

PAX: I can add "fruit preference" on the back.

FEN: We can share this fruit while you write.

MIRI: The camera has caught an unscripted moment.

PAX: Is it a better moment than the card?

MIRI: The card is now under the fruit.

[FEN AND VEE MOVE THE PLATE. THE SCORECARD HAS A FIG STAIN.] 

PAX: The results are unreadable.

MIRI: We will use the conversation instead.

VEE: We can take a walk after this.

FEN: Let's go before the timer starts again.

MIRI: The scorecard has no box for that.

PAX: I can draw one.

[HE DRAWS A BOX LABELED "CONTINUE WITHOUT SCORES."] 

FEN: We can share the fruit now.

VEE: Yes. And leave the cards on the table.

MIRI: The pair has chosen a second date.

PAX: The scorecard says they have not.

MIRI: The scorecard is not dating them.

[FEN AND VEE WALK TOWARD THE TREE, TAKING THE FRUIT.] 

PAX: Should I follow with the camera?

MIRI: Let them have an afternoon.
