# The Library Fine Negotiation

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: bureaucratic debt collection applied to a book returned one minute late*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: librarian Nara, a macaque; patron Pava, a gibbon; clerk Odo, a baboon; book "Mosses of the Valley."

INT. LIBRARY DESK - DAY

Pava returns a book. Nara scans it.

NARA: One minute late.

PAVA: I am sorry.

ODO: The fine is one seed.

Pava offers a seed. Odo says the fine is now two seeds because the payment was made after the due time. Nara checks the clock; it is one minute late.

PAVA: Then I owe two seeds?

ODO: One seed, plus a late payment fee.

Nara asks if they can waive the fee. Odo says only the head librarian can authorize a waiver. Nara is the head librarian. She says she waives it. Odo asks her to submit the form.

NARA: To myself?

ODO: The form requires a supervisor signature.

Pava waits while Nara fills out the waiver request. The form asks for the reason the book was late. Pava says she was reading it. Odo says "reading" is not one of the listed options. The options are "transport difficulty," "weather," and "other."

Pava checks other and writes "reading." Odo asks for a supporting document. Pava gives him the book. Nara points out that the book is already returned.

The library computer prints a receipt. It says "Balance: one seed." Nara signs the waiver. The computer prints a second receipt, showing zero seeds due. Odo says the first receipt is now invalid but must be retained for records.

Pava offers to put the seed in the library garden. Odo says the seed is not a standard payment. Nara says it can be a donation. Pava places the seed in a small planter by the desk. Odo asks if a plant grown from a donation needs a library card.

NARA: It needs water.

Odo writes "water plant" on the calendar.

Nara checks the calendar and sees the book is already requested by another reader. Pava hands it over instead of keeping it while the paperwork continues. Odo stamps the return date. The waiver clears the account, and the next reader takes the book from the hold shelf. Nara asks Odo to cancel the second receipt. He stamps it "VOID" and files both copies.

Odo asks whether a one-minute delay deserves a fine at all. Nara says the rule applies to every book, but the waiver exists for small mistakes. Pava says the book was due before she finished the last page. Nara points out that the library is for reading and marks the exception clearly on the form.

The form is complete.

Nara closes the drawer.

TAG

Pava checks out another book.

ODO: Due in two weeks.

PAVA: I will try to finish it.

NARA: No fine for reading.
