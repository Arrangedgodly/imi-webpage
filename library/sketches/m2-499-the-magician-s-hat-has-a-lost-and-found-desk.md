# The Magician's Hat Has a Lost-and-Found Desk

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: stage magic reveals, misdirection, and performers whose props acquire their own backstage bureaucracy*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: MAGICIAN, a baboon; ASSISTANT, a gibbon; STAGEHAND, a macaque; USHER, an orangutan; AUDIENCE MEMBER, a capuchin.

[A small theater. A tall black hat sits on a table beside a sign that reads LOST ITEMS. The magician bows.]

MAGICIAN: From this empty hat, I shall produce a white rabbit.

ASSISTANT: Is the hat empty?

MAGICIAN: It is empty of rabbits.

[The magician reaches in and pulls out a scarf, two gloves, and a program.]

MAGICIAN: Those are not part of the trick.

STAGEHAND: They were all in the hat when I found it.

USHER: People keep putting their belongings there.

AUDIENCE MEMBER: I thought it was the coat check.

[The magician peers into the hat. Something thumps from inside.]

MAGICIAN: The rabbit is ready.

ASSISTANT: That sounded like a shoe.

[The magician turns the hat upside down. A small umbrella slides out and opens on the floor.]

ACT ONE

[The stagehand brings a clipboard and begins listing items. The assistant holds the hat steady. The magician tries to perform a flourish around them.]

MAGICIAN: Behold, a vanishing umbrella!

[The magician covers it with a cloth, snaps, and pulls away the cloth. The umbrella remains.]

MAGICIAN: It has vanished from my enthusiasm.

USHER: I have an umbrella ticket.

[The usher checks the ticket. It matches the umbrella's ribbon.]

USHER: This belongs to the second row.

[The audience member raises a hand.]

AUDIENCE MEMBER: That is mine. I put it there during the first trick.

MAGICIAN: You put it in the hat?

AUDIENCE MEMBER: You said it was empty.

ASSISTANT: He said empty of rabbits.

[The magician reaches into the hat again. A rabbit's ear rises above the brim. Everyone freezes.]

MAGICIAN: Now the rabbit is here.

STAGEHAND: And it has a claim ticket tied to its foot.

[The assistant gently lifts the rabbit out. The ticket reads BACKSTAGE, LEFT SIDE.]

ASSISTANT: That is the rabbit's holding tag.

MAGICIAN: It was meant to appear without paperwork.

STAGEHAND: It was meant to wait until the cue.

[The rabbit hops across the stage, noses the umbrella, and ducks beneath the table.]

ACT TWO

[The magician performs the final reveal with the rabbit resting safely in the assistant's arms. The audience member retrieves the umbrella. The stagehand closes the lost-and-found ledger.]

MAGICIAN: The hat is empty again.

[The usher checks inside and pulls out one glove.]

USHER: There is still a glove.

MAGICIAN: Then the hat is almost empty.

[The audience laughs. The magician gives the glove to the stagehand, who hangs it on the lost-items rack. The assistant carries the rabbit toward the backstage pen.]

FINAL IMAGE: The magician lifts the hat to bow, and a claim ticket flutters out onto the stage.
