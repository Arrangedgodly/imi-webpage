# The Bell Has Been Rescheduled

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: municipal meeting notices and public consultations that cannot decide when the meeting happens*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Ada, a squirrel clerk; Rufus, a macaque bell ringer; Mimi, a capuchin resident; Tarn, a tortoise meeting chair.

INT. TOWN HALL - 2:59 P.M.

[FOUR BELLS HANG FROM A FRAME. A NOTICE READS PUBLIC MEETING, TODAY, 3:00. ADA STANDS BESIDE A CLOCK. RUFUS HOLDS A MALLET.]

ADA: We begin when the meeting bell rings.

MIMI: Which one is the meeting bell?

RUFUS: The large one. Unless there's a fire, then it's the small one.

ADA: The small bell is for weather closures.

RUFUS: We added that last week.

TARN: What does the middle bell do?

ADA: It announces that a meeting has been postponed.

MIMI: What announces the new time?

ADA: The meeting bell.

RUFUS: That seems circular.

ADA: It is a circle with a mallet.

[RUFUS TAPS THE LARGE BELL. ITS NOTE ECHOES THREE TIMES.]

ADA: The notice permits one ring.

RUFUS: I struck it once.

MIMI: The town heard three meetings.

Tarn opens the agenda. The first item reads "Choose an official bell for the consultation about the bells."

TARN: We can settle this with a vote.

[Ada hands everyone a ballot. The only boxes read RING, CHIME, and OTHER. Mimi writes MEETING under other. Rufus collects the ballots.]

RUFUS: We cannot vote until we know which bell calls the vote.

ADA: The notice says 3:00. It is 3:02.

MIMI: Then we've missed it.

TARN: We could start late.

ADA: The policy says a late meeting must be rescheduled using the middle bell.

Rufus taps the middle bell. A banner drops from the ceiling: MEETING POSTPONED TO 3:15.

MIMI: It's 3:02.

RUFUS: The bell has provided us thirteen minutes to prepare.

Tarn points at the sunny windows. Rufus taps the small bell. A blue flag unfurls: WEATHER CLOSURE. Everyone looks outside.

ADA: The notice says we must vacate.

MIMI: There isn't weather.

RUFUS: The bell has declared it.

Mimi takes the sign to the door. A gust flips the paper, exposing a second notice on the back: "If closed, meeting moves to the steps."

TARN: We are on the steps now.

ADA: Then it has moved.

RUFUS: I should ring the large bell to announce the move.

MIMI: Don't. It will echo and move us again.

Rufus sets down the mallet. Mimi reads her ballot aloud. Ada and Tarn vote with raised hands. Rufus points to the large bell.

TARN: That's three votes. The bell has no vote.

RUFUS: It has been ringing since before this town had a clerk.

ADA: It still isn't on the voter list.

RUFUS: The voter list is posted inside.

The wind carries the list out the door. Rufus runs after it. The large bell swings as he passes, ringing once. Everyone stops.

MIMI: That sounded like a meeting.

ADA: It did. One ring, no echo.

TARN: The large bell is our meeting bell. We can begin.

[THE SMALL BELL TINKS IN THE WIND.]

ADA: Weather meeting postponed.

TARN: We did not schedule a weather meeting.

RUFUS: The small bell did.

[TARN OPENS THE AGENDA. THE CLOCK FLIPS TO 3:15. ADA TURNS THE NOTICE OVER AND WRITES: MEETING IN SESSION. THE LARGE BELL RINGS ONCE; EVERYONE SITS ON THE STEPS.]
