# The Cape Doesn't Fit the Sidekick

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: superhero origin films, dramatic costume reveals, and sidekicks whose outfits are designed for camera rather than movement*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: HERO, a baboon; SIDEKICK, a capuchin; DESIGNER, an orangutan; DIRECTOR, a gibbon; STAGEHAND, a macaque.

[A costume fitting room. The hero wears a fitted suit and flowing cape. The sidekick stands on a box while the designer pins a much longer cape.]

HERO: The cape should catch the wind.

SIDEKICK: I would like it to clear my feet.

DESIGNER: The camera loves a long line.

[The sidekick steps down. The cape drags across the floor and catches on the hero's boot.]

DIRECTOR: Freeze. The reveal is perfect.

SIDEKICK: My cape has captured the hero.

ACT ONE

[The designer shortens the cape by folding it under and pinning the hem. The sidekick runs across the room to test it. The cape flips over their head.]

SIDEKICK: I cannot see the villain.

HERO: The villain cannot see you either.

DIRECTOR: That is a useful advantage.

[The stagehand removes the pin. The cape falls long again. The director asks the sidekick to try a dramatic turn. The fabric wraps around the hero's arm.]

HERO: I have been recruited.

SIDEKICK: You are my partner.

DESIGNER: The cape needs a release clasp.

[The designer attaches a clasp at the shoulder. The sidekick tries to leap onto a padded platform. The clasp releases, and the cape lands on the floor while the sidekick lands safely.]

DIRECTOR: That was a great reveal.

SIDEKICK: I was not wearing the costume.

[The hero picks up the cape. The stagehand checks the clasp and finds the designer used a safety pin backward.]

STAGEHAND: It opens when pulled.

DESIGNER: That is the release feature.

HERO: It released before the action.

[The director asks for a test with the camera. The sidekick runs down the short hallway. The camera follows the cape instead of the sidekick, then loses both when the fabric catches on a door handle.]

DIRECTOR: The cape has taken the lead.

HERO: The hero is still running.

[The designer pins a bright tab on the cape hem so the camera can see the release point. The sidekick repeats the run at half speed and turns before reaching the door.]

SIDEKICK: That is how I move in the suit.

ACT TWO

[The designer replaces the pin with a proper quick-release clasp and shortens the cape to ankle height. The sidekick tests a run, a turn, and a jump. The cape stays behind without covering the face.]

DIRECTOR: Now give me the reveal.

[The hero and sidekick enter from opposite wings. The hero's cape billows. The sidekick's cape stays neat. The camera operator zooms in on the clasp.]

SIDEKICK: We are both ready.

[The stagehand gives the hero a thumbs-up and the sidekick a smaller one.]

FINAL IMAGE: The hero's cape flows behind the team while the sidekick carries the discarded extra fabric as a villain's net.
