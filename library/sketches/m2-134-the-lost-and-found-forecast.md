# The Lost-and-Found Forecast

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: morning television segments that turn routine lost property into suspenseful community announcements*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Host Omi, a gibbon; reporter Tavi, a macaque; producer Fenn, a capuchin; and owner Lila, a lemur.

[THE MORNING SET HAS A TABLE OF UNCLAIMED OBJECTS.]

OMI: Today we solve the mystery of a missing blue scarf.

FENN: It is on the table.

OMI: It is a different blue scarf.

TAVI: The owner called in.

[THE PHONE RINGS.]

LILA: I lost a blue scarf near the fountain.

OMI: Was it wool, woven, or wrapped?

LILA: It was a leaf.

OMI: We have a leaf, but it is green.

LILA: It turned blue in the rain.

[FENN HOLDS UP A LEAF WITH A BLUE STAIN.]

FENN: This one has a stripe.

LILA: That is mine.

OMI: A breakthrough.

[TAVI WALKS TO THE FOUNTAIN. THERE ARE THREE BLUE LEAVES.]

TAVI: The trail leads to the water.

OMI: Could the scarf have traveled?

LILA: It floated.

[THE LEAF DRIFTS UNDER A BENCH.]

FENN: We have lost it again.

[OMI LEANS OVER THE BENCH. A MONKEY HAS MADE A NEST OF LEAVES.]

OMI: The missing item may have been repurposed.

LILA: That is not my nest.

[TAVI POINTS TO A FOURTH BLUE LEAF ON THE BENCH.]

TAVI: Here it is.

[LILA TAKES THE LEAF. THE MONKEY TAKES IT BACK.]

OMI: A dispute has emerged.

LILA: It was mine before it was a nest.

FENN: We can return the other leaves.

[LILA HANDS THE MONKEY A DRY LEAF.]

LILA: This one can stay.

[THE MONKEY ADDS IT TO THE NEST. THE HOST LOOKS AT CAMERA.]

OMI: We have returned the lost property to its owner, and found a new owner for another leaf.

TAVI: Is this the forecast?

OMI: Expect scattered leaves through the afternoon.


[OMI OPENS THE LOST-AND-FOUND LOG.]

OMI: We have had four missing leaves this week.

TAVI: Were they all blue?

FENN: One was green, one was yellow, and one had a bite.

OMI: The report will show a pattern.

LILA: It is autumn.

[THE HOST LOOKS AT THE CAMERA, THEN AT THE LEAVES.]

OMI: We have confirmed a seasonal disappearance.

FENN: The leaves fell.

TAVI: The wind carried them.

[THE MONKEY SHAKES THE NEST. TWO LEAVES FALL OUT.]

OMI: The case is expanding.

LILA: I only called about mine.

[THE REPORTER MOVES THE CAMERA TO SHOW THE NEST.]

TAVI: The nest is not missing property.

OMI: It is found property.

FENN: The leaves were never reported lost by the tree.

LILA: Mine was lost by me.

[OMI ADDS A SECOND COLUMN TO THE LOG: "OWNER IDENTIFIED."]

OMI: Your leaf is returned, and the other leaves stay with their nest.
OMI: The morning report is complete.


LILA: I will keep the leaf on my shoulder.


[THE CAMERA SHOWS THE BLUE SCARF LEAF RESTING ON LILA'S SHOULDER.]
