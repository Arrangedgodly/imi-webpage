# The Resort Is Just a Pool

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury travel commercials, breathless destination voiceovers, and resort amenities described as remote discoveries*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Travel host Vex, a gibbon; camera operator Sola, a capuchin; resort manager Palla, a macaque; and guest Neri, a ring-tailed lemur.

EXT. TOWN REC CENTER - DAY

Vex stands beside a small public pool. Sola films from a low angle so the water appears vast.

VEX: Escape to a hidden oasis with crystal water, private shade, and a complimentary towel.

PALLA: It is the town pool.

VEX: We are describing the experience.

Neri arrives carrying a beach bag. She reads a sign: OPEN SWIM, TWO COINS.

NERI: Is the pool private?

PALLA: No. It is open to the community.

VEX: The community is our exclusive guest list.

Sola pans past two deck chairs, then zooms in until one chair fills the frame.

VEX: Relax on a spacious sun terrace.

PALLA: That is one chair.

Neri sits down. It squeaks. Vex says the resort offers soothing ambient sounds. A child splashes in the pool; the camera frames the ripples like a waterfall.

VEX: Discover the cascading water feature.

PALLA: The shallow end.

Neri asks where the complimentary towel is. Palla points to a rack of clean towels. Vex describes the towel as locally sourced and refreshed daily. Sola films a close-up of the label, which reads MUNICIPAL PROPERTY.

VEX: Each towel carries a story.

NERI: It carries water.

Palla explains the pool rules. No diving in the shallow end, no glass, and no running. Vex rephrases them as wellness guidelines.

VEX: Begin your journey with a mindful step.

Neri walks carefully toward the pool. Vex asks her to make it look more luxurious. She steps onto the wet deck and slides half a foot. Palla catches her by the elbow.

PALLA: The deck is wet. Walk slowly.

VEX: A thrilling arrival.

Neri sits in the chair while Sola films the pool without the fence. The shot looks like a secluded getaway. A janitor walks through frame carrying a mop bucket.

VEX: Our staff maintain the water all day.

PALLA: Yes. I am cleaning the deck.

The travel crew asks Palla to repeat the cleaning shot. She refuses; the pool is busy. Vex tries one final narration.

VEX: The resort offers everything you need: water, shade, towels, and a safe place to sit.

NERI: I came here for a swim.

Palla gives her a towel. Neri jumps in. The splash drenches Vex and the camera.

SOLA: We have the real experience.

VEX: We have the waterproof camera.

PALLA: We do not have a luxury resort.

VEX: We have a destination.

The pool closes for cleaning. Vex takes his wet towel and sits on the deck chair. It squeaks again.

VEX: Complimentary ambient sound.

Neri returns the towel to the rack and leaves with damp hair.
