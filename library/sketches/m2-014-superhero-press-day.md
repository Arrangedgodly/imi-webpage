# Superhero Press Day

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Superhero press conferences and franchise-universe hype*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Thunderclap, a bonobo hero; publicist Vix, a peacock; reporter Lo, a capuchin; engineer Bap, a macaque.

[STAGE: PODIUM, BACKDROP WITH A CITY SILHOUETTE, ONE CAPE ON A COAT RACK.] 

VIX: Welcome to the first press day for Thunderclap, guardian of the east district.

LO: What did you guard yesterday?

THUNDERCLAP: A sandwich from a curious pigeon.

VIX: Please ask about the larger mission.

LO: What is the larger mission?

THUNDERCLAP: Keep the bridge open and the pigeons fed somewhere else.

VIX: We have prepared a new hero emblem.

BAP: It is upside down.

VIX: It's a flexible emblem.

LO: Why is there a sequel logo behind the podium?

VIX: We are announcing the next three installments.

THUNDERCLAP: I haven't finished the first one.

VIX: The universe is expanding.

THUNDERCLAP: Is it expanding toward the bridge?

BAP: The bridge is a real place. It needs a new support cable.

VIX: We will address infrastructure during the question period.

LO: How do your thunder powers work?

THUNDERCLAP: I clap my hands, and sound waves shake loose branches.

BAP: The name is misleading. It is a loud clap.

VIX: Thunderclap is a brand.

THUNDERCLAP: I can also hear when the bridge cable is fraying.

LO: Can you hear it now?

[A SMALL METALLIC TING COMES FROM BEHIND THE STAGE.] 

THUNDERCLAP: Yes.

BAP: That's my microphone stand.

THUNDERCLAP: It's leaning on the cable.

VIX: We are not discussing cables in this franchise announcement.

LO: Is that the bridge outside?

THUNDERCLAP: The vibration is coming from the east span.

VIX: We have a photo opportunity in two minutes.

THUNDERCLAP: I have to go.

VIX: Can we get a heroic exit first?

THUNDERCLAP: I'm leaving by the door.

VIX: Use the cape.

THUNDERCLAP: It catches on the handle.

BAP: The clasp is twisted.

THUNDERCLAP: Untwist it.

LO: The press is following you.

VIX: This will be excellent bonus footage.

THUNDERCLAP: Keep back from the bridge.

BAP: The support has slipped.

THUNDERCLAP: I can hold the beam while you reset the cable.

BAP: Don't clap. The bolts are loose.

THUNDERCLAP: I will hold it with my hands.

LO: I can clear the path.

VIX: I will alert the crowd.

THUNDERCLAP: Tell them the bridge is closed for ten minutes.

VIX: Can I call it a dramatic temporary closure?

LO: Call it a closure.

[TOOLS CLINK. THE CABLE TIGHTENS. FOOTSTEPS CROSS THE BRIDGE.] 

THUNDERCLAP: It is safe again.

VIX: We got the shot.

THUNDERCLAP: Of the bridge?

VIX: Of the cape caught on the railing.

LO: That's the first installment.

THUNDERCLAP: There won't be a sequel until the cable is replaced.

VIX: The release date is flexible.

VIX: We can use that as the poster.

THUNDERCLAP: The cable is the story.

[THE UPSIDE-DOWN EMBLEM FALLS. IT LANDS RIGHT SIDE UP.]
