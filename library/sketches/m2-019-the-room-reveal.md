# The Room Reveal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Home-makeover reality reveals and emotional before-and-after reactions*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Pippa, a capuchin; designer Ruel, a mandrill; resident Toma, a squirrel monkey; camera operator Vim, a gibbon.

[STAGE: A SMALL TREEHOUSE ROOM HIDDEN BEHIND A CURTAIN. A CLOCK COUNTS DOWN.] 

PIPPA: Toma, you have not seen your room for seven days.

TOMA: I have been in the room.

PIPPA: But not since we covered it with this curtain.

TOMA: The curtain is covering my window.

RUEL: We have changed the space to better fit your life.

TOMA: My life is in there.

VIM: The camera is ready for your reaction.

PIPPA: When we lift the curtain, give us a big surprise.

TOMA: I do not know what you changed.

PIPPA: That's why it will be authentic.

RUEL: The countdown begins now.

TOMA: I need my climbing mitts.

VIM: They are in the room.

TOMA: I need my mitts to get in the room.

PIPPA: Three, two, one.

[CURTAIN PULLS. A SMALL SHELF, A LOW BRANCH, AND A WIDE OPEN FLOOR APPEAR.] 

PIPPA: What do you think?

TOMA: I can reach the shelf.

RUEL: We lowered it.

TOMA: The branch is lower too.

RUEL: We moved your sleeping perch down from the rafters.

TOMA: I slept in the rafters because the fan was loud.

VIM: We turned off the fan.

TOMA: The room is hot now.

PIPPA: Do you love the transformation?

TOMA: I need a window.

RUEL: The curtain covers it.

TOMA: Why?

RUEL: We needed a dramatic reveal wall.

PIPPA: We can open it.

VIM: The curtain rope is tied to the shelf.

TOMA: The shelf holds my bowls.

RUEL: We can move the shelf.

PIPPA: That's another reveal.

TOMA: I would like a room I can use now.

RUEL: The wide floor gives you space to swing.

TOMA: The floor is under the window.

VIM: We can move the bowls.

PIPPA: We have forty seconds left in the reveal.

TOMA: The reveal has lasted longer than my lunch break.

RUEL: What would you change?

TOMA: Put the shelf beside the branch. Move the perch away from the fan. Open the window.

RUEL: Those changes fit the room.

PIPPA: We will make them before filming the after shot.

VIM: I can roll the camera back.

TOMA: Why is the camera in the room?

PIPPA: It is part of the makeover.

TOMA: I can climb around it.

RUEL: We should move it first.

VIM: I am ready.

TOMA: Now I like the room.

PIPPA: Can you say that again for the close-up?

TOMA: I like the room when the camera is gone.

RUEL: That is a useful note.

VIM: I am backing out.

PIPPA: The room is complete.

TOMA: The room was complete when I could reach the window.

[THE CURTAIN OPENS. SUNLIGHT FILLS THE ROOM. CAMERA ROLLS OUT.]
