# The Passport Office of Nowhere

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: government paperwork counters and identity verification applied to a troop that moves between branches*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Clerk Varo, a baboon; applicant Liri, a gibbon; examiner Paki, a macaque; and line manager Fenn, a capuchin.

[A COUNTER HAS A SIGN: "PASSPORTS FOR TRAVEL OUTSIDE THE GROVE." LIRI APPROACHES WITH A FORM.]

VARO: Name?

LIRI: Liri.

VARO: Legal name?

LIRI: That is my name.

PAKI: We need proof.

[LIRI SHOWS A PHOTO OF HERSELF.]

VARO: The photo shows you in a tree.

LIRI: I live in a tree.

VARO: That is not the same as a mailing address.

[PAKI HANDS HER A FORM WITH A BOX FOR STREET NUMBER.]

LIRI: There is no street.

FENN: Write the tree number.

LIRI: The tree has no number.

VARO: Then use the nearest branch.

[LIRI WRITES "UPPER LEFT BRANCH."]

PAKI: We need a witness.

[THE LINE MANAGER CALLS OVER A MONKEY.]

FENN: Can you verify the applicant?

MONKEY: I know her.

VARO: How do you know her?

MONKEY: We groom together.

PAKI: That is a relationship.

VARO: Is it on the approved list?

[FENN READS A SIGN: "FAMILY, NEIGHBOR, OR GROOMING PARTNER."]

FENN: It is.

[VARO STAMPS THE FORM. THE INK IS DRY.]

VARO: The stamp has expired.

LIRI: How does a stamp expire?

VARO: The date wheel is stuck.

[THE MONKEY TAPS THE WHEEL. IT ROLLS FORWARD.]

PAKI: The form now says next month.

LIRI: My trip is today.

FENN: We can issue a temporary branch pass.

VARO: There is no temporary pass form.

[THE MONKEY HOLDS UP A LEAF.]

FENN: We can write the name on that.

VARO: Leaves are not official.

[THE MONKEY PRESSES THE LEAF INTO THE STAMP PAD AND MAKES A CLEAR MARK.]

VARO: That is a valid mark.

LIRI: Is the leaf my passport?

VARO: It is your temporary proof of identity.

[LIRI TAKES THE LEAF AND WALKS TO THE DOOR. THE DOOR OPENS ONTO THE GROVE.]

FENN: Where are you traveling?

LIRI: To the lower branches.

VARO: Please return by the expiration date.

[LIRI LOOKS AT THE LEAF. A BREEZE BLOWS IT AWAY.]

LIRI: My passport has left.

[THE MONKEY CHASES IT. Varo begins a report.]

VARO: Lost document, applicant still present.

FENN: The applicant is outside now.


[PAKI CHECKS THE BACK OF THE FORM.]

PAKI: It asks for an emergency contact.

LIRI: I can call the troop.

VARO: We need one name.

LIRI: The troop is many names.

[THE MONKEY TAKES THE PEN AND WRITES A ROW OF SMALL MARKS.]

FENN: The contact list is complete.

VARO: It is not legible.

PAKI: It is legible to the troop.

[THEY STAMP THE FORM. THE STAMP LANDS ON VARO'S SLEEVE.]

VARO: I am now officially a contact.
[VARO LOOKS THROUGH THE DOOR. LIRI IS GROOMING THE MONKEY WHO CAUGHT THE LEAF.]

VARO: Relationship verified.
