# Pause for Progress

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: guided meditation apps and quantified mindfulness*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: CALM, a sloth meditation guide; NIX, a macaque app designer; and BEE, a restless capuchin listener.

[A studio. A small bell, a timer, and a leaderboard glow on a screen.]

NIX: Welcome to StillNow, the meditation app that ranks your ability to stop wanting a ranking.

CALM: Settle into a comfortable pose. Let your shoulders drop.

BEE: My shoulders are down. The app says I am at the 42nd percentile.

CALM: Notice the number. Let it pass.

BEE: It just sent me a badge for noticing the number.

NIX: That is our Mindful Observer achievement.

CALM: We are here to observe, not collect badges.

NIX: There is a seven-day streak badge too.

CALM: Please turn off the streak.

[Nix taps the tablet. A fanfare plays.]

BEE: The badge has unlocked an even bigger badge.

NIX: We are A/B testing serenity.

CALM: Who decided a quiet mind should have a dashboard?

NIX: The investors wanted evidence that people were relaxing.

BEE: I was relaxing until the timer told me I had only two minutes left.

CALM: Let the timer be a sound. You do not have to race it.

[SFX: SOFT BELL. TIMER COUNTS DOWN LOUDLY.]

BEE: The countdown is louder than the bell.

NIX: We can make the premium countdown quieter.

CALM: Make the free one quieter.

[Nix lowers the volume. Bee sits. His tail continues tapping the floor in a steady rhythm.]

CALM: Notice the tail. Don't force it to stop.

NIX: The app has flagged repetitive movement.

BEE: It's keeping time for me.

CALM: Good. Follow the beat if it helps.

[Nix watches the leaderboard. Bee's score stops rising.]

NIX: Your calm score has plateaued.

BEE: That sounds peaceful.

NIX: The app says you should try the advanced breathing plan.

CALM: No. The session is over.

NIX: We still have a feedback screen.

BEE: How do I rate this session?

NIX: Choose one to five leaves.

BEE: I was asked to stop measuring myself.

CALM: Give the app one leaf for the bell and four minutes of silence.

[Nix taps the rating. The leaderboard disappears.]

NIX: The silence screen has no data.

BEE: It has a tail tapping.

[The bell rings. Everyone waits for the app to congratulate them. It stays dark.]

CALM: There. A full minute without a badge.

NIX: I feel like we should record that.

BEE: You can write it down later.

[The timer rings again. Nix unplugs it.]
[The app lights up again with a progress chart.]

NIX: Your session is complete. You are in the top ten percent of people who completed a session.

CALM: Turn off the ranking.

NIX: Then how will Bee know if it worked?

BEE: I know because my shoulders dropped.

CALM: Ask the person, not the chart.

NIX: What did you notice?

BEE: The timer was loud, the bell was fine, and my tail kept tapping even when I sat still.

NIX: That's more detail than the app captures.

BEE: Then the app can stay quiet.

[Nix closes the rating screen. The timer remains unplugged.]

CALM: We are done. There is no streak to maintain.

[BLACKOUT.]
