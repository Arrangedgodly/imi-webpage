# The Ventriloquist Loses the Voice

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: ventriloquist acts, talent-show judging, and performers trying to hide which character is speaking*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: VENTRILOQUIST, a macaque; DUMMY, a baboon puppet operated by the ventriloquist; JUDGE, an orangutan; STAGE MANAGER, a gibbon; SOUND TECH, a capuchin.

[A talent-show stage. The ventriloquist sits beside a wooden dummy. The sound tech tests a microphone.]

SOUND TECH: One, two. The microphone is live.

DUMMY: I can hear you.

VENTRILOQUIST: I did not say that.

JUDGE: The act has begun.

[The dummy looks toward the sound booth. The ventriloquist's mouth stays closed.]

DUMMY: The microphone said one, two. I said hello.

SOUND TECH: That was the dummy's voice through the floor monitor.

STAGE MANAGER: Please keep all voices onstage.

DUMMY: I am onstage. The sound is over there.

[The ventriloquist lifts the dummy and tries a fast exchange. The dummy says "Where is the hat?" while the ventriloquist silently points to a hat on the table.]

JUDGE: I cannot tell who asked the question.

VENTRILOQUIST: That is the point.

JUDGE: Then I cannot tell who answered it.

ACT ONE

[The stage manager moves the hat to the other side of the table. The sound tech lowers the monitor volume. The dummy turns its head toward the ventriloquist.]

DUMMY: You moved my hat.

VENTRILOQUIST: I am holding you.

DUMMY: That doesn't mean you own the hat.

[The audience laughs. The ventriloquist tries to make the dummy laugh back, but the sound tech's effect plays a recording of applause.]

SOUND TECH: Sorry, wrong button.

DUMMY: That was my laugh track.

VENTRILOQUIST: You have a laugh track?

DUMMY: I have a rider.

[The judge flips the score card. It reads ORIGINALITY, TIMING, AND HAT OWNERSHIP.]

JUDGE: We need a clear exchange.

[The ventriloquist sets the dummy on a stool and places the hat between them. The sound tech turns off the monitor. The dummy speaks in the ventriloquist's voice; the ventriloquist speaks in the dummy's deeper voice.]

DUMMY: You sound strange.

VENTRILOQUIST: I am trying your voice.

DUMMY: You have not earned it.

[The audience laughs louder. The stage manager checks the sound board. The dummy's mic cable is connected to the ventriloquist's belt pack.]

STAGE MANAGER: The channels are crossed.

SOUND TECH: The voices have swapped.

DUMMY: I knew that was my voice.

VENTRILOQUIST: I thought it was mine.

ACT TWO

[The sound tech switches the cables back. The ventriloquist and dummy face the audience.]

VENTRILOQUIST: Let's finish the act.

DUMMY: My line is, "Where is the hat?"

VENTRILOQUIST: It is on the table.

[The dummy looks. The hat has rolled to the floor. The dummy picks it up and puts it on the judge's head.]

JUDGE: I will allow that ending.

[The stage manager raises the curtain cue. The ventriloquist bows. The dummy bows a beat later.]

FINAL IMAGE: The judge leaves the stage wearing the hat while the dummy holds the microphone toward the silent ventriloquist.
