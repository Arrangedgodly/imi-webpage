# The Runway Needs a Handrail

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fashion runway shows and the practical demands of animal movement*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: DESIGNER PELL, a lemur; MODEL RON, a macaque; STAGE MANAGER VEE, a gibbon; and a row of judges.

[A runway has been built along a narrow branch. A spotlight shines on a dramatic cape.] 

VEE: The show begins with a straight walk from the trunk to the platform, one turn, then a pose.

RON: The branch curves.

PELL: The collection is called Vertical Confidence.

RON: I need a handrail.

PELL: A handrail would interrupt the silhouette.

VEE: The silhouette is currently holding onto the branch.

PELL: Can you hold the pose longer?

RON: I can hold it until the wind changes.

[THE WIND MOVES THE CAPE. RON'S TAIL WRAPS AROUND A STANCHION.] 

VEE: The tail has altered the garment.

PELL: It has created a second line.

RON: It has stopped me from sliding.

PELL: We'll call that functional tailoring.

VEE: We need a second look. The cape has to show movement from the back row.

PELL: The spotlight is already on it.

RON: The branch is moving under me.

PELL: The branch is supposed to move. It is part of the environment.

VEE: The branch is part of the runway. The platform support is supposed to stay still.

JUDGE: We should test the route without the cape first.

PELL: The cape is the collection.

RON: I can walk the route in the cape if the tail tie stays loose.

VEE: The tail tie is pinning the cape to the stanchion.

PELL: It creates a clean line.

RON: It creates a tether.

[RON TURNS. THE CAPE PULLS THE STANCHION HALF A STEP.] 

VEE: The tether has moved the runway marker.

PELL: That's a dramatic reveal.

JUDGE: The marker is now in the walking lane.

PELL: We can replace it after the show.

RON: Or before the next model walks.

[THEY MOVE THE MARKER TO THE WIDE BRANCH. RON TESTS THE TURN.] 

VEE: The walk is smooth now.

PELL: The cape does not catch.

RON: The handrail is clear, and I can keep my tail free.

PELL: The design still has shape.

JUDGE: It also has an exit.

VEE: That is the final look: a model reaching the end of the runway without being tied to it.

VEE: The judges are ready. Model two, proceed.

[MODEL TWO STARTS. A SEED FALLS ON THE RUNWAY. SHE STOPS TO PICK IT UP.] 

PELL: Keep walking.

MODEL TWO: Someone could slip on it.

RON: The seed is now part of the collection.

VEE: The collection is creating hazards.

PELL: It is showing a relationship between body and environment.

VEE: It is showing a seed on a narrow path.

[THE JUDGES LEAN IN. ONE JUDGE TESTS THE BRANCH WITH A FOOT.] 

JUDGE: The branch flexes more when two people stand together.

RON: We should send the models one at a time and add a support under the platform.

PELL: A support will change the design.

RON: The support will make the design stay up.

VEE: We can pause the show and adjust the route. The models can walk along the wider branch, with a rail at the turn.

PELL: The cape will catch on the rail.

RON: Then shorten the cape.

PELL: The cape is the main feature.

VEE: The main feature is that the model reaches the end safely.

[PELL USES CLOTH TO PIN THE CAPE BACK. THE MODEL WALKS THE WIDE BRANCH, TURNS, AND POSES.] 

JUDGE: That is a stronger line.

PELL: The design still moves.

RON: And I can let go of the stanchion.

[RON SHOWS THE SHORTER CAPE. THE WIND LIFTS IT CLEAR OF THE BRANCH.] 

VEE: Final look: room to move.

PELL: The collection has a new name: Vertical, With Support.

RON: I can walk in that.
