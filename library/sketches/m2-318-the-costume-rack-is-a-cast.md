# The Costume Rack Is a Cast

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: theater costume fittings, character-driven wardrobe notes, and directors who assign personalities to coats*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Director Tavi, a gibbon; actor Neri, a capuchin; wardrobe lead Vex, a macaque; and stage manager Luma, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. BACKSTAGE COSTUME ROOM - AFTERNOON

A rack of coats rolls into the room. Tavi inspects it.

TAVI: This coat is the villain.

VEX: It is a brown coat.

TAVI: It has a dark past.

NERI: It has a torn pocket.

Luma asks which costume Neri should wear for the lead role. Tavi chooses a red jacket. Vex says the jacket belongs to the stagehand playing a mail carrier. Neri tries on a gray coat instead; it is too long and covers her hands.

TAVI: The coat makes you mysterious.

NERI: I cannot open the door.

VEX: We can shorten the sleeves.

Tavi says no alterations until the character's arc is clear. Vex puts the coat on a rack and gives Neri a hat. The hat is too small and perches on her head. Her tail knocks it off when she turns.

TAVI: The hat refuses to stay.

LUMA: It has an elastic strap. It is tangled around the rack.

The rack starts rolling downhill. Coats swing like a crowd. Neri follows it, trying to catch the red jacket. Vex catches the handle. The villain coat lands on Tavi's shoulders.

TAVI: I feel a sudden urge to monologue.

NERI: That is the coat.

Tavi opens his mouth, but Vex gives him a measuring tape. The urge passes. Luma reassigns the costumes by role and marks each hanger with the actor's name. The brown coat goes to the villain, the red jacket goes to the mail carrier, and Neri gets a short blue coat with clear sleeves.

NERI: I can hold the script.

TAVI: The costume tells the story.

NERI: It lets me use my hands.

The actors try their costumes on stage. The villain coat's pocket tears wider when Tavi raises his arms. Vex sews it while Tavi delivers the villain's line.

The audience enters for a short preview. Neri walks onstage in the blue coat, opens the door, and sets the parcel down. Tavi delivers his villain line. The audience laughs when Vex emerges with the needle and tells him to hold still.

TAVI: The coat has stolen my scene.

VEX: The coat is still in wardrobe.

TAVI: You shall never open the parcel.

VEX: You need to hold still.

TAVI: The villain is angry.

VEX: The tailor is busy.

Luma calls places. The actors enter in their costumes. Neri opens a door, carries a parcel, and delivers the line. The scene works. Tavi asks whether the coat's backstory is complete.

VEX: It has a pocket, a seam, and a hook. That is enough.

Luma wheels the costume rack offstage. It rolls by itself, but this time all the hangers are locked. The cast follows in the correct outfits.
