# The Performance Art Piece Is on Break

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: gallery performance art and demands for interpretation*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: ARTIST MIRI, a gibbon; CURATOR PAX, a macaque; VISITOR SEDA, a capuchin; and a folding chair.

[A WHITE ROOM. A chair sits in the center. A card reads: "A PLACE TO WAIT." Miri stands very still beside it.] 

PAX: The artist will now begin a ninety-minute performance.

SEDA: What is she doing?

PAX: Waiting.

SEDA: For what?

PAX: We are not allowed to tell you.

MIRI: I am waiting for my tea to cool.

PAX: Please do not reveal the intention.

MIRI: I said what I am doing.

SEDA: Can I sit in the chair?

PAX: The chair is part of the piece.

MIRI: The card says a place to wait.

PAX: It is a conceptual place.

SEDA: My legs are tired.

[SEDA STARTS TO SIT. PAX CATCHES THE CHAIR.] 

PAX: The visitor is altering the work.

MIRI: The chair is for sitting.

PAX: The label invites ambiguity.

SEDA: I am not ambiguous. I need a seat.

[MIRI TAKES A SIP OF TEA. IT IS STILL HOT.] 

MIRI: I have been waiting for the tea to cool.

PAX: How long?

MIRI: Four minutes.

PAX: The performance is scheduled for ninety.

MIRI: The tea may cool sooner.

SEDA: Can I sit while you wait?

MIRI: Yes.

[SEDA SITS. THE CHAIR CREAKS. MIRI REMAINS STANDING.] 

PAX: The piece now includes an audience member.

MIRI: It includes a chair doing chair work.

SEDA: The seat is comfortable.

PAX: That is an interpretation.

SEDA: It is a fact.

PAX: The visitor has sat in the chair. Does the performance continue?

MIRI: The chair is doing its job.

PAX: The artist must stay in place for the duration.

MIRI: I am standing in place.

SEDA: You could sit too. The chair is wide enough.

PAX: Two people would change the composition.

MIRI: It is a folding chair. Its composition is metal and wood.

[PAX UNFOLDS A SECOND CHAIR. IT SQUEAKS.] 

PAX: That chair belongs to the gallery staff.

SEDA: It could be part of the piece.

MIRI: Then we would have two places to wait.

PAX: The label only describes one.

MIRI: We can update it to say "A place to wait, sometimes shared."

SEDA: I can move over.

MIRI: No, keep the seat. I have been standing all morning.

[PAX SITS IN THE SECOND CHAIR.] 

PAX: The performance now includes the curator.

MIRI: It includes someone who needed a seat.

SEDA: The tea has cooled.

MIRI: Then my waiting is finished.

PAX: And the gallery is open to sitting.

MIRI: It was open before we put up the label.

[THE TEA STEAMS LESS.] 

MIRI: It is cool enough now.

PAX: The performance has ended early.

MIRI: The waiting is over.

SEDA: What happens to the chair?

MIRI: It can stay here for the next visitor.

PAX: We will need a new label.

SEDA: Write "Sit down if you need to."
