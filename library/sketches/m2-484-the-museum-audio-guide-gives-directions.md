# The Museum Audio Guide Gives Directions

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: self-guided museum tours, overconfident audio narration, and devices that direct visitors toward exhibits that moved*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: GUIDE, a macaque; VISITOR, a gibbon; TECHNICIAN, an orangutan; GUARD, a baboon; CHILD, a capuchin.

[A museum lobby. The visitor wears headphones. A tablet map shows a bronze statue in Gallery Three. The guard points down the hall.] 

GUIDE: Walk straight ahead to find the bronze statue.

VISITOR: The sign says Gallery Three is closed.

TECHNICIAN: The statue moved for cleaning.

[The audio guide repeats, "Walk straight ahead." The visitor turns toward a supply closet.] 

GUARD: The statue is in the side gallery today.

CHILD: The guide is telling us to go the wrong way.

[The technician taps the tablet. Its map does not update.] 

TECHNICIAN: The route file is old. I can switch it to the current map.

GUIDE: Please continue straight ahead.

VISITOR: Is that a recorded voice or a command?

TECHNICIAN: It is a recorded voice. The speaker does not know where you are.

[The guard opens the side gallery. The statue is visible inside, on a padded platform. The child starts to follow the blue line on the tablet, which points back toward the closet.] 

GUARD: Follow the floor signs, not the old blue line.

[The technician mutes the tablet's route instructions and prints a temporary map. The visitor takes off the headphones and looks at the paper map.] 

VISITOR: This one points to the statue.

GUIDE: You are now facing the east gallery.

VISITOR: I am facing the side gallery.

[The technician opens the settings screen and tries to update the guide. It asks the user to select a museum wing; all three options are labeled EAST.] 

TECHNICIAN: The wing names are duplicated.

CHILD: Can we use the signs?

GUARD: Yes. The signs are correct.

[The technician tapes a note to the kiosk: AUDIO ROUTE PAUSED. The child walks to the statue by following the sign. The visitor follows, still holding the printed map. The guide continues speaking from the headphones on the bench.] 

GUIDE: You have reached the bronze statue.

VISITOR: We have reached the statue. The device is still on the bench.

[The technician retrieves the headphones and switches them off. The guard welcomes the next group and points them to the correct gallery.] 

[The visitor reads the printed map and finds the side gallery. The child walks alongside, checking each wall sign. The guard opens the next room only after confirming the route is clear.] 

CHILD: The floor arrow matches the paper map.

VISITOR: The guide could use this route.

TECHNICIAN: I will upload the new map after closing and test it before tomorrow.

[The technician labels the old route file with the date it became outdated, then removes the tablet from the public stand until the update is ready.] 

GUARD: Until then, the signs lead the tour.

FINAL IMAGE: The statue stands in the side gallery while the outdated tablet map rests facedown at the kiosk.
