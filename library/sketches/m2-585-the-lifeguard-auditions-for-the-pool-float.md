# The Lifeguard Auditions for the Pool Float

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: lifeguard training reels that turn rescue equipment into audition props*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: LIFEGUARD LUMA, a macaque; DIRECTOR ODO, a lemur; TRAINEE PAVA, an orangutan; POOL MANAGER RIK, a gibbon.

[A dry indoor pool set. A bright rescue float hangs on the wall. Odo carries a camera slate.]

ODO: We need a confident entrance.

LUMA: The pool is closed.

RIK: That is why it is dry.

ACT ONE

[Odo asks Luma to demonstrate the proper reach from the deck. She plants her feet and extends the rescue pole toward a painted target. Pava, playing a swimmer, lies on a mat beside the pool. Luma gives the safety instruction, then passes the pole.]

ODO: Can you make it more heroic?

LUMA: It is a pole.

[Pava sits up and asks whether the camera can film from the shallow end. Rik says there is no water. Odo wants the shot anyway. Luma points to the dry tiles and says the demonstration needs to stay on the deck.]

ACT TWO

[Odo hands Pava the rescue float and asks him to perform a slow-motion entrance. Pava trots across the deck as if in a dramatic chase, trips over the camera cable and catches himself on a padded bench. The float rolls away. Luma retrieves it and checks the strap.]

RIK: It is meant to be thrown to someone in the water.

ODO: Can he throw it toward the lens?

LUMA: No.

[Pava stands in the marked rescue position. Odo films him turning toward camera with the float on his shoulder. Its rope catches the slate and pulls it from Odo's hand. The slate lands in the prop box.]

ODO: That was a strong take.

LUMA: It was an untied rope.

[Rik ties the rope neatly into the storage loop. Luma demonstrates how to hold the float while staying clear of the pool edge. Pava asks if he can be the person who calls for help. Odo hands him a cue card: HELP. Pava reads it in a stage whisper. Luma tells him to use a clear voice. Pava shouts; the card flutters out of his hand and lands inside the rescue float.]

ACT THREE

[The manager puts a small mannequin at the marked point in the shallow-end set. Luma demonstrates calling for assistance, then extends the pole from a stable position on the deck. Pava holds the float and waits for the next direction rather than running into the shot. Odo asks for a heroic soundtrack. Rik leaves the room quiet so the instructions can be heard.]

[Luma shows the rope loop and where it belongs after use. Pava returns the float to the wall hook. It catches on the hook but swings back toward his face. He ducks; the float lands neatly in the storage bracket. Odo calls it an accidental perfect take.]

LUMA: The hook worked.

PAVA: I helped it.

[Rik checks the cord is looped. Odo asks for the closing line. Luma says the pool stays closed until trained staff open it and the equipment is in place. Pava tries to add a movie-trailer voice. Luma points to the dry pool. He returns to his normal voice.]

TAG

[Odo asks whether the float can be the star of the training reel. Rik puts it away.]

FINAL IMAGE: The rescue float hangs ready on its marked hook while Pava practices a calm entrance on the dry deck.
