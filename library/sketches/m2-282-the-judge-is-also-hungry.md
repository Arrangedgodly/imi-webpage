# The Judge Is Also Hungry

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive cooking shows, timed mystery ingredients, and judges who critique dishes before tasting them*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Rell, a gibbon; contestants Miri, a ring-tailed lemur, and Dex, a macaque; judge Omi, a capuchin; and kitchen runner Palla, an orangutan.

INT. COOKING STUDIO - DAY

A bell rings. Two silver cloches sit on the counter. Rell addresses the camera.

RELL: Contestants, your mystery ingredient is hidden beneath the cloche. You have thirty minutes to create a dish that celebrates the ingredient's natural character.

MIRI: What is it?

RELL: You will find out when the clock starts.

DEX: Then how do we know what to cook?

RELL: That is the challenge.

Omi leans toward the camera.

OMI: I will judge aroma, balance, texture, and whether the dish has a story.

PALLA: The ingredient is roasted squash.

RELL: You revealed it.

PALLA: I delivered it. The label fell off.

The clock starts. Miri makes a squash tart. Dex makes a squash broth and narrates each spoonful. Omi circles the counter, sniffing before the dishes are ready.

OMI: I detect a warm autumn note.

DEX: That is the oven.

OMI: The oven is part of the experience.

Rell announces the halfway point. Miri's tart crust cracks. She uses the broken pieces to make crisp layers. Dex's broth is too thick, so Palla adds water. Dex objects that the bowl will look less dramatic.

DEX: It needs height.

PALLA: Soup does not need height.

DEX: This one does.

He stacks a garnish on a spoon. It falls into the broth. The bell rings for time. Omi tastes Miri's tart first, then pauses for the camera.

OMI: The crust has a decisive edge. The filling is gentle but assertive.

MIRI: Is that good?

OMI: I need another bite to decide.

He tastes again. Dex presents the broth in a tall glass. Omi studies it from every angle.

OMI: The presentation is daring.

DEX: It is soup.

OMI: Daring soup.

He drinks. The broth is delicious. Omi's stomach growls. The microphone catches it.

RELL: Judge Omi, would you like a serving?

OMI: I am judging. I cannot eat the whole dish.

PALLA: There are two bowls.

OMI: I can judge the second bowl privately.

Rell announces a tie. Miri and Dex are both awarded a golden ladle. The contestants exchange recipes. Palla clears the counter and finds a third cloche containing a snack for the crew.

RELL: The final challenge: identify the staff meal.

OMI: I recognize that aroma.

PALLA: It is the same roasted squash.

OMI: A strong through-line.

Rell lifts the cloche. Under it is a plate of plain bread.

DEX: The mystery ingredient is bread?

RELL: It is the mystery dish.

Miri shares the tart with the crew; Dex serves the broth in regular bowls. Omi takes both, one for each hand.

OMI: I have reached my final verdict.

RELL: What is it?

OMI: I am still hungry.

The camera zooms in as Omi reaches for another piece of tart. Palla moves the plate just out of frame.
