# The Busker Auditions for Silence

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: street-performance auditions that judge musicians by their ability to attract a crowd without inconveniencing anyone*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Drummer Kesh, a macaque; Evaluator Umi, a gibbon; Shopkeeper Tov, a baboon; and Passerby Rala, a lemur.

EXT. MARKET WALKWAY - DAY

Kesh taps a practice drum. Umi holds a clipboard marked "STREET PERFORMER PERMIT." Tov stands in front of his shop with a tray of cups.

UMI: Your audition must be audible from ten paces and quiet from inside the shops.

KESH: Those are opposite directions.

TOV: I sell glass cups. I would prefer quiet.

RALA: I stopped because the drum sounded good.

UMI: You stopped at eight paces. The permit requires ten.

KESH: I can play louder.

TOV: The cups will answer.

[KEsh taps the drum softly. A cup on the tray rings in sympathy.]

UMI: That is an acoustic event.

TOV: That is my inventory.

EXT. MARKET WALKWAY - LATER

Umi places a chalk circle ten paces away. Kesh carries the drum to it. Rala stands there, listening.

UMI: Please attract three listeners without blocking foot traffic.

KESH: I will play a short rhythm.

[THE DRUMBEAT STARTS. A DOG SITS. TWO CHILDREN STOP. A CART PULLER JOINS THE BEAT AND ROLLS IN TIME.]

UMI: We have four listeners and a moving cart.

TOV: The cart is now in front of my door.

KESH: I did not ask it to dance.

RALA: The rhythm has a good walking pace.

UMI: The performance must not redirect pedestrians.

TOV: It has redirected everyone to my doorway.

[Kesh stops. The cart keeps rolling for another beat, then stops.]

UMI: Could you finish on a silent note?

KESH: A note is a sound.

UMI: Then finish on a quiet sound.

KESH: I can rest my hand on the drum.

EXT. MARKET WALKWAY - AFTERNOON

Kesh sets the drum beside Tov's shop. The audience is scattered, leaving the walkway clear. Umi watches the permit timer.

UMI: Your final audition is a two-minute set with no audience blocking.

KESH: I'll play from here, facing the wall.

TOV: The wall is fine.

[Kesh plays a light rhythm. Tov's cups hum along. Rala claps softly. The dog lies down.] 

UMI: It meets the sound limit, and nobody has stopped walking.

TOV: The cups are still singing.

UMI: Your permit is approved.

KESH: Can I play here tomorrow?

TOV: Only if I can sell tickets to the cups.

RALA: I can stand ten paces away and still hear it.

UMI: The permit allows listeners, but no designated audience zone.

KESH: Does the dog count?

TOV: The dog has left.

[THE DOG RETURNS AND SITS BESIDE THE CUP TRAY.]

RALA: The dog came back for the encore.

UMI: I can include the dog in the audience count.

TOV: Only if the dog buys a ticket.

KESH: The dog is already in rhythm.

[THE CUPS RING TOGETHER AS KESH SETS DOWN HIS DRUM. UMI WRITES "ACCOMPANIMENT: GLASSWARE" ON THE PERMIT.]
