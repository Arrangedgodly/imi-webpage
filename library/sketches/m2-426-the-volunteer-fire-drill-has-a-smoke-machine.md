# The Volunteer Fire Drill Has a Smoke Machine

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: emergency drills staged with theatrical effects that obscure the instructions*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Coordinator Mara, a gibbon; volunteer Vex, a macaque; volunteer Pip, a capuchin; announcer Nari, a lemur; passerby, a macaque.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MORNING

[A smoke machine sits beside an exit sign. Mara holds a clipboard. Nari holds a megaphone.]

MARA: This is a drill. Leave by the marked exit.

VEX: Should we look concerned?

MARA: You should leave.

[Nari switches on the smoke machine. It blows a thick cloud directly across the exit sign.]

PIP: I can't see the exit.

NARI: The effect is working.

MARA: It is working against us.

[Vex pulls a red curtain aside to reveal the exit. The curtain is part of the stage set and falls across the door.]

VEX: The exit is blocked.

PIP: We have found a second problem.

[Mara asks everyone to follow the green arrows on the floor. The smoke machine's cord lies across the arrows. Pip steps over it. Vex follows the cord instead, dragging the machine behind him.]

NARI: The smoke is moving toward the exit.

MARA: Vex is carrying it.

[Vex stops. The machine continues puffing. A cloud rises around his head.]

VEX: I cannot see the clipboard.

MARA: You don't need the clipboard.

[Vex reaches for the megaphone. It squeals. Nari turns it off. The volunteers continue through the door, but the curtain catches on Pip's belt. He pulls it with him, making the whole hall's backdrop move.]

PIP: The wall is following me.

NARI: It is scenery.

[Mara releases the curtain from Pip's belt. The smoke has cleared enough to reveal two exits, both covered by green arrows. Vex tries one; it opens into a closet. He backs out carrying a stack of folding chairs.]

VEX: I found supplies.

MARA: That's the storage room.

PIP: The drill has made a room list.

[Mara shuts off the smoke machine. A final puff comes out and sets off the hall's real smoke alarm. Everyone looks at it. Nari raises the megaphone but stops before speaking.]

NARI: Is this still a drill?

MARA: This part is not.

[The volunteers exit through the clear side door. Vex leaves the chairs in the hall, then returns to carry them out one at a time.]

[The puff drifts toward the exit sign. Mara turns the machine off at the wall. Vex returns for the clipboard, then sees the machine and carries it outside too.]

VEX: We have evacuated the equipment.

MARA: The equipment was not in the drill.

PIP: It joined late.

[Nari checks the hall. The red curtain still covers the door. She rolls it away from the exit.]

NARI: The exit is clear now.

MARA: The drill ended ten minutes ago.

[Vex sets the smoke machine on the sidewalk. It puffs at a passerby, who calmly walks around it.]

VEX: The public has completed the drill.

FINAL BEAT: Mara checks the clipboard. The drill is marked "successful evacuation." Nari adds "smoke machine evacuated last." The machine gives one tiny puff after the room is empty.
