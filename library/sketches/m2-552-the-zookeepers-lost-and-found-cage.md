# The Zookeeper's Lost-and-Found Cage

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: zoo announcements and lost-property systems that mistake animal habitat for a storage area*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: KEEPER, an orangutan; VISITOR, a macaque; PARROT, a capuchin monkey playing a parrot; MANAGER, a baboon; INTERN, a gibbon.

[A zoo information desk. The keeper wheels in a small empty cage with a sign reading LOST AND FOUND. The visitor approaches holding a hat.]

VISITOR: I lost this hat.

KEEPER: You are holding it.

VISITOR: I found it.

[The parrot repeats, "Found it!" from a nearby perch. The intern writes the words on the intake tag.]

MANAGER: That is not the item description.

ACT ONE

[The keeper explains that lost belongings go in a cabinet. The visitor asks whether the hat can be identified. The parrot says, "Blue hat, blue hat," and flaps one wing. The keeper places the hat on the counter.]

INTERN: The parrot has reported a blue hat.

MANAGER: The hat is green.

PARROT: Blue hat!

[The keeper moves the empty cage away from the desk. The parrot's perch is beside it, and the bird drops a toy ring into the cage. The intern labels it "lost ring." The parrot retrieves the ring through the bars.]

KEEPER: It belongs to the parrot.

INTERN: The cage is now occupied.

[The manager requests the day's lost-and-found log. The keeper reads entries: one lunchbox, two gloves, a sunhat, and a small plush turtle. The parrot repeats "turtle" and carries a leaf to the counter.]

VISITOR: Is that the turtle?

KEEPER: It is a leaf.

[The keeper returns the leaf to the bird's enclosure. The visitor remembers the hat has a name tag inside. They turn it over, revealing a phone number. The intern calls the number; the owner answers from the monkey house and says it belongs to a child who is waiting by the fountain.]

MANAGER: Then it is not the visitor's hat.

VISITOR: I was just holding it for the announcement.

[The keeper asks the parrot to stop repeating the report. The parrot says, "Found it!" again. The intern removes the temporary lost tag, returns the hat to the child, and writes an accurate note in the log. The manager closes the empty cage.]

[Another visitor arrives carrying a scarf and asks whether it was left near the lemur habitat. The keeper does not put the scarf in the cage. The intern checks the lost-item cabinet, finds a matching name tag, and calls the information desk. The parrot repeats the visitor's name correctly for the first time.]

INTERN: We will keep the scarf at the desk.

PARROT: At the desk!

ACT TWO

[The parrot drops the toy ring into the cage once more. This time the keeper records it as the parrot's toy and clips it to the perch. The bird retrieves it. The intern puts the cage in storage and leaves the cabinet open for actual lost items.]

KEEPER: No belongings in habitats.

PARROT: Blue hat!

MANAGER: We heard you.

FINAL IMAGE: The child wears the recovered hat while the parrot keeps the ring on its perch.
