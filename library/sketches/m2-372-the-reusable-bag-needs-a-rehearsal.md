# The Reusable Bag Needs a Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grocery checkout systems that turn carrying a reusable bag into a compliance performance*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Toma, a capuchin shopper; Biri, a gibbon cashier; Hush, a lemur bag inspector; Cal, a macaque manager.

INT. GROCERY CHECKOUT - EVENING

[TOMA SETS A CLOTH BAG ON THE COUNTER. IT HAS A LONG HANDLE AND A PATCH ON ONE SIDE.]

BIRI: Will you be using your own bag?

TOMA: Yes.

Biri scans the bag. The register beeps and displays BAG NOT FOUND.

BIRI: It wants me to add a bag.

TOMA: The bag is right here.

Biri scans again. The machine prints a label reading BAG, ONE. She tries to stick it on the cloth. It curls around the handle and sticks to itself.

HUSH: Hold on. The bag must be inspected.

TOMA: For what?

HUSH: Cleanliness, capacity, and structural confidence.

Cal approaches carrying a clipboard. Toma holds the bag open. Hush peers inside.

HUSH: I see one apple, a loaf, and a sock.

TOMA: The sock is clean.

CAL: We need to make sure the bag can carry its groceries.

Hush takes the apple, loaf and sock out and sets them on the counter for the test. Biri places the apple inside. The bag holds. She adds the loaf. It holds. Toma adds the sock. The bag sags.

HUSH: The sock is undermining the test.

TOMA: It is the bag's usual job.

Cal asks Toma to lift the bag by both handles. Toma lifts it. The label catches on the counter, and the groceries tip sideways. The apple rolls down the conveyor. Biri chases it with a handheld scanner.

BIRI: The apple is now on lane two.

HUSH: We have not approved a second lane.

The apple reaches the end of the belt. Cal catches it in the cloth bag. The label is now stuck to his sleeve.

CAL: I am the bag.

TOMA: You need to be inspected.

Hush checks his sleeve for capacity. Cal stretches his arm, and the label peels off. Biri attaches it to the bag's patch. The machine beeps again and asks whether Toma brought a bag.

BIRI: It forgot.

Toma sets the loaf and sock on the counter and takes the apple out of the bag. He turns the empty bag inside out. Biri aims her scanner into the inverted bag at the label; the machine recognizes it. A new prompt asks for proof that the bag is reusable.

TOMA: I used it yesterday.

HUSH: Can you show the groceries?

TOMA: I ate them.

Cal writes "reused once" on the clipboard. The register accepts the bag. Toma puts the apple, loaf and sock inside and lifts it. The sock slips out through a tiny hole in the corner.

HUSH: Structural confidence failed.

TOMA: It carried the apple and bread.

Biri catches the sock. Cal offers a paper bag.

TOMA: I brought this one so I wouldn't need that.

Biri reaches under the counter for a roll of clear packing tape. She patches the hole from the inside, then sticks the loose label over the repair so the register can still scan it. The bag holds when Toma lifts it again.

CAL: Reusable and repaired.

The register asks if he wants a receipt. Toma says yes. It prints a strip long enough to drag across the floor.

HUSH: Does the receipt need inspection?

BIRI: It is too long to fit in the bag.

Toma settles the repaired bag on his arm and hands the receipt to Cal. Cal pins it over the checkout screen.

[CAL LIFTS THE RECEIPT TO READ THE SCREEN: BAG ACCEPTED. HE TRIES TO SCAN HIS SLEEVE. THE MACHINE BEEPS: PLEASE USE YOUR OWN BAG.]
