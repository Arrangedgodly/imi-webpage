# Team Building on a Branch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Corporate team-building retreats and trust exercises*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Facilitator Rella, a gibbon; manager Odo, a mandrill; analyst Fern, a capuchin; new hire Jax, a macaque; groundskeeper Nemi, an orangutan.

[STAGE: A LOW BRANCH WITH A CIRCLE OF LEAVES, A ROPE, AND A WHITEBOARD READING TRUST FALL.]

RELLA: Welcome to the canopy cohesion retreat. Today we strengthen trust.
ODO: I already trust my team.
FERN: You made us come here to prove it.
RELLA: First exercise: everyone stands in a circle and shares a strength.
JAX: I can open a seed pod quietly.
FERN: That will help during meetings.
ODO: My strength is decisive delegation.
NEMI: You delegated carrying the snacks to me.
ODO: A practical demonstration.
RELLA: Now, the trust fall. One partner leans back while the other catches them.
FERN: The branch is narrow.
RELLA: Trust requires space.
NEMI: Space is over there, on the ground.
JAX: I can catch Fern.
FERN: You are smaller than my lunch.
JAX: I am not made of lunch.
ODO: Let's use the rope as a safety net.
RELLA: The rope is for the next exercise.
ODO: Then let's use the next exercise early.
RELLA: Fine. Form a human pyramid.
NEMI: We are monkeys.
RELLA: Form a primate pyramid.
JAX: Who goes on top?
ODO: The manager.
FERN: The rope is tied around your waist.
ODO: For trust.
NEMI: It is tied to the branch.
ODO: For safety.
RELLA: The goal is to communicate without words.
FERN: Odo is pointing at the snack bag.
ODO: I am communicating hunger.
JAX: I thought we were communicating trust.
NEMI: The bag is empty.
ODO: Then communicate disappointment.
RELLA: We will now pass an invisible coconut around the circle.
JAX: It is heavy.
FERN: It is invisible.
JAX: I am acting the weight.
ODO: That is an excellent performance.
RELLA: How does the team feel?
FERN: Tired.
JAX: United by the invisible coconut.
NEMI: The real coconut is under the whiteboard.
ODO: Why didn't anyone say so?
NEMI: We were communicating without words.
RELLA: Our final challenge is a group climb to the upper branch.
FERN: We can take turns.
ODO: A good team moves together.
JAX: The branch will not hold together.
NEMI: The branch is a signpost. It says ground route.
RELLA: I had the map upside down.
ODO: We built trust in the wrong direction.
FERN: We can still share the coconut.
JAX: I will open it quietly.
NEMI: That is the strength we needed.
RELLA: Retreat complete. Please fill out the teamwork survey.
ODO: The paper is blank.
RELLA: It is an invisible survey.
FERN: I give it a strong score for not asking questions.

FERN: The survey needs a comment.
JAX: Write that the coconut was real.
ODO: Write that we climbed together.
NEMI: We walked.
ODO: A ground-level climb.
RELLA: Please rate the retreat's energy.
FERN: One leaf.
JAX: I give it two.
NEMI: The snacks get three.
ODO: The team gets five.
FERN: Out of how many?
ODO: We are still building trust in numbers.

[THE GROUP WALKS DOWN TO THE GROUND TOGETHER; ODO IS STILL SAFELY TIED TO THE SIGNPOST.]
