# The Improv Team Said Yes to the Weather

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: improv games built around "yes, and," audience prompts, and performers who accept every premise until the set collapses*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: HOST, a macaque; PLAYER ONE, a gibbon; PLAYER TWO, a baboon; STAGE MANAGER, an orangutan; AUDIENCE VOICE, a capuchin.

[A small improv stage. A sign says "YES, AND." The stage manager holds a rain umbrella.]

HOST: Give us a location.

AUDIENCE VOICE: A beach during a snowstorm.

PLAYER ONE: Yes, and I am a lifeguard.

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and I am the ocean.

PLAYER ONE: The ocean is usually water.

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and today it is snow.

[The host shivers dramatically. The stage manager raises the umbrella.]

HOST: The scene is already getting wet.

STAGE MANAGER: The sprinkler is off.

PLAYER ONE: Yes, and the lifeguard is wearing a wool hat.

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and I am a wave made of scarves.

[Player Two wraps a scarf around Player One.]

PLAYER ONE: I can no longer see the shore.

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and the shore is also wearing scarves.

HOST: The beach is now a textile event.

[The stage manager tries to remove the scarf. Player Two adds a second one.]

STAGE MANAGER: We have one costume scarf. Please stop multiplying it.

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and it has cousins.

HOST: The rule has been followed.

PLAYER ONE: The lifeguard needs a whistle.

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and the whistle is frozen.

[Player One blows the whistle. It makes no sound. The host plays a whistle effect from the soundboard.]

HOST: That was the sound of winter.

STAGE MANAGER: That was the emergency cue.

[The stage manager turns off the effect. The stage is silent. The audience waits.]

PLAYER ONE: Yes, and we can stop adding things.

PLAYER TWO: The ocean is tired.

PLAYER ONE: The lifeguard will take a break and bring a blanket.

PLAYER TWO: I will move back so everyone has room.

[They unwrap the scarf and fold it over the umbrella handle.]

HOST: You have broken the game.

PLAYER ONE: We used "yes, and" to make the scene, then stopped when the props were enough.

PLAYER TWO: The ocean can wait for its next tide.

[The stage manager checks the umbrella. A drop of water falls from the tip.]

STAGE MANAGER: We did get wet.

HOST: The sprinkler was off.

STAGE MANAGER: The umbrella came in from outside.

[A stagehand offers the host a towel. The host adds it to the scene as a cape. Player One takes it back and hangs it on a hook.] 

PLAYER ONE: We have reached the shore.

[The host holds up the prompt card. It says "A SHIP IN A STORM." Player Two looks at the dry floor, then folds the card into a paper boat.]

PLAYER TWO: Yes, and we will keep it on the table.

STAGE MANAGER: The set is still dry.

FINAL IMAGE: The folded scarf hangs on the umbrella while the performers stand on a dry stage.
