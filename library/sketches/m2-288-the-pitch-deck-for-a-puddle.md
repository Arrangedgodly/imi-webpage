# The Pitch Deck for a Puddle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: startup pitch competitions, market-size slides, and founders who rename ordinary objects as disruptive platforms*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Founder Kori, a macaque; investor Vela, a gibbon; analyst Bex, a capuchin; presenter Nala, a ring-tailed lemur; and caretaker Rumi, an orangutan.

INT. STARTUP DEMO ROOM - AFTERNOON

A small puddle sits on a tarp. Kori stands beside a screen showing a logo: PUDDLE+.

KORI: We are not selling water. We are selling a responsive ground-level reflection experience.

VELA: Who is the customer?

KORI: Anyone who has ever looked down.

BEX: That is a very large market.

KORI: Exactly. Our addressable market is every pedestrian.

NALA: The puddle formed when the roof gutter overflowed.

KORI: That is our pilot acquisition funnel.

Rumi brings a mop.

RUMI: The gutter needs clearing. The puddle will go away.

KORI: We have a retention problem.

Kori flips to a slide showing a graph. The line rises when it rains and falls when the mop approaches. Vela asks how the company will scale.

KORI: We will place puddles in every district.

BEX: That sounds like leaking roofs.

KORI: We call it distributed water visibility.

Nala tests the service by stepping near the tarp. Her reflection breaks into ripples.

VELA: The product responds to movement.

KORI: It is a real-time engagement layer.

Rumi clears the gutter. Water stops dripping. The puddle shrinks. Kori rushes to the screen and changes the graph title from growth to seasonal engagement.

VELA: What is your revenue model?

KORI: Premium reflections, sponsored reflections, and a subscription tier with exclusive clouds.

BEX: Clouds are free.

KORI: The standard clouds are free.

Nala uses a squeegee to push the remaining water toward a drain. Kori announces a live demo. The reflection moves across the tarp, then disappears down the drain.

KORI: We have successfully delivered the experience to the infrastructure.

VELA: I will invest in the gutter repair, not the puddle company.

RUMI: That is the problem I came to fix.

BEX: The deck has one accurate slide. The one showing the leak.

Kori points to it. The slide is upside down. Nala rotates the laptop. The graph now makes sense: water came from the roof, crossed the floor, and reached the drain.

KORI: We have pivoted to building maintenance analytics.

RUMI: You can help me inspect the downspout.

KORI: Does it have recurring revenue?

RUMI: It has recurring rain.

Vela signs a repair estimate. Bex collects the pitch deck. Nala places a caution cone where the puddle was.

KORI: The cone is our new product.

VELA: It is a cone.

KORI: A safety cone with a premium reflection surface.

Rumi turns it so the reflective stripe faces outward. The demo room is dry, the roof is repaired, and no one invests in Puddle+.

KORI: We will relaunch after the next storm.

RUMI: There should not be a next storm inside.
