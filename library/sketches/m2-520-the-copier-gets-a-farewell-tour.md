# The Copier Gets a Farewell Tour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace retirement ceremonies, office nostalgia reels, and farewell speeches interrupted by aging equipment*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: OFFICE MANAGER, a baboon; TECHNICIAN, an orangutan; INTERN, a capuchin; SPEAKER, a macaque; COPIER VOICE, a gibbon from the machine's speaker.

[An office copier stands beside a small retirement cake. A banner reads THANK YOU, MODEL 4. The manager holds a microphone.]

OFFICE MANAGER: After twenty years, this copier has earned a quiet retirement.

COPIER VOICE: Paper tray is open.

[The technician closes the tray. The copier hums, then prints a blank page.]

INTERN: It has made a program.

TECHNICIAN: It has made a blank sheet.

SPEAKER: I prepared a farewell speech.

OFFICE MANAGER: Please begin.

[The speaker leaves the original on the scanner glass, hoping for a cleaner copy. The copier starts printing behind them.]

COPIER VOICE: Toner low.

SPEAKER: It has interrupted me before I started.

ACT ONE

[The manager asks the intern to play a slideshow. The intern connects a laptop to the copier display. The screen shows a scan of the office manager's lunch receipt.]

OFFICE MANAGER: That is not the slideshow.

INTERN: The copier remembered the last job.

TECHNICIAN: It should not remember anything. Clear the queue.

[The technician opens the print queue. One job remains, titled FINAL FINAL FINAL.]

SPEAKER: That is my farewell speech.

OFFICE MANAGER: How many copies did you send?

SPEAKER: One.

[The copier prints five pages. Each has a different margin.]

COPIER VOICE: Paper jam.

[The technician opens the side panel, frees the remaining stuck sheet, and removes a page folded into a tiny fan. The speaker takes it and reads the first line.]

SPEAKER: "Dear colleagues, this machine never let us down."

[The copier makes a grinding noise.]

TECHNICIAN: It is letting us know the page is stuck.

[The intern retrieves the real slideshow from a USB drive and plugs it into a laptop. The office manager asks everyone to gather beside the copier. The machine's status light turns green. The first photo shows the old machine beside its replacement. The speaker keeps the jammed page folded until the speech.]

ACT TWO

[The speaker unfolds the jammed copy and passes it to the manager for the opening paragraph. The intern advances through the slideshow. The manager then returns the page to the speaker, who reads the final paragraph.]

OFFICE MANAGER: We thank this machine for every signed form and crooked handout.

SPEAKER: One jam today. Twenty years of service behind it. We salute both the paper and its machine.

[The copier speaks while the technician checks the scanner glass.]

COPIER VOICE: Please remove original from glass.

[The technician lifts the single speech original from the scanner bed, places it in the speaker's hand, clears the paper queue, and powers the copier off. The intern turns off the laptop after the final photograph.]

TECHNICIAN: Queue empty. Machine is off.

[The speaker folds the jammed copy as a keepsake and files the clean original in a folder. The staff wheels the silent copier toward storage.]
FINAL IMAGE: The unplugged copier rolls toward storage as the speaker carries the folded keepsake beside it.
