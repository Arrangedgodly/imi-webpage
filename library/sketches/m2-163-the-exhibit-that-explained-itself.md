# The Exhibit That Explained Itself

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio guides that turn unfinished objects into grand historical mysteries*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Vela, a macaque guide; Nara, a gibbon curator; Pito, a capuchin visitor; and a volunteer docent, Suni, a slow loris.

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - DAY

Pito stands before a wooden frame holding three pegs and a loose string. Vela's audio guide speaks from a small speaker.

VELA (RECORDED): Before you is a profound instrument of uncertain purpose.

PITO: It looks like a coat rack.

Suni hurries over.

SUNI: Please do not guess. The mystery is central to the exhibit.

Nara enters carrying a label. It reads: "Unfinished branch sorting rack, maker unknown, found in storage."

NARA: That is the current research.

Vela asks Pito to begin the guide again. A solemn bell sounds. Suni dims the lights. Pito squints at the rack.

VELA (RECORDED): Some scholars believe the three pegs represent the three stages of longing.

NARA: I wrote that as a joke for the draft label.

Suni points out a coil of string attached to one peg. Pito pulls it gently. The string unravels and drops a small wooden tag.

PITO: It says "sort twigs." 

Suni quickly covers the tag with her clipboard.

SUNI: The tag may be later.

NARA: It matches the handwriting on the box.

Vela plays a dramatic music cue. The rack's loose string vibrates in the speaker's bass. Pito steps back. Suni asks if he has sensed a presence.

PITO: I sensed the speaker.

Nara wants to update the exhibit. Suni says the audio tour is already printed and visitors expect an ancient mystery. Vela's recording continues: "Notice the empty space where the fourth peg may once have stood." Nara points out the fourth peg in the storage box beside her.

NARA: It fell off.

Suni places the peg on the frame. It fits perfectly. The rack now looks even more like a coat rack.

PITO: Can I hang my scarf?

SUNI: The gallery is not interactive.

Pito drapes the scarf over a peg. The frame tips slightly. Nara steadies it and writes "not for hanging" on the label.

The speaker crackles. Vela's recording says, "The object's final secret remains unresolved." Nara unplugs the speaker.

NARA: Now it is resolved.

Suni turns the label around. The back says "Please return audio guide to desk." Pito returns it. The speaker immediately begins playing again from the desk.

VELA (RECORDED): Thank you for visiting the mystery.

Suni places the rack in the practical objects gallery. Nara labels it plainly. Pito leaves his scarf on his shoulders.

SUNI: The mystery has moved.

Pito stops at the door and looks back. The audio guide is still playing from the desk, now narrating the empty space where the rack stood. Suni turns it off and carries it to the practical objects gallery too.

NARA: The rack has not.
