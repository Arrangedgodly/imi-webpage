# The Tour Guide Leads the Group to the Gift Shop Again

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum tours that repeat the same scripted route even when the guide has reached the gift shop*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: GUIDE ODO, a capuchin; VISITOR PAVA, a lemur; VISITOR RIKO, an orangutan; MUSEUM CLERK NARA, a macaque.

[A museum gift shop. A group stands beside postcards and a rack of tiny dinosaur hats. Odo points to a plush model.]

ODO: On your left, the ancient fossil.

RIKO: This is a soft toy.

ACT ONE

[Odo checks his tour script. The next line describes a towering skeleton. Behind him is a display of plastic replicas. Pava asks if the tour has left the main gallery. Odo says the group is at stop four.]

NARA: Stop four is the entrance.

[Odo turns his map upside down. It has a red arrow marked YOU ARE HERE, pointing toward the shop counter. He says the map must be decorative. Nara points to the same arrow on the wall.]

ODO: We are retracing the visitor journey.

PAVA: We have not started it.

ACT TWO

[Odo leads them out of the shop and into the main gallery. He finds a fossil display and begins the correct speech. A clerk wheels in a box of gift-shop stock; it blocks the exhibit label.]

RIKO: What creature is that?

ODO: A late-period cardboard carton.

[Odo reads from the script: "The giant skeleton stood here for generations." The exhibit is a small cast of one claw. Pava asks where the rest is. Odo says it is in another hall. Nara shows them the route map; the skeleton hall is in the opposite direction.]

[Odo follows the printed red arrow again and returns to the gift shop. Nara stops him at the entrance. Pava buys a postcard, then discovers it is a map of the museum printed upside down. Riko rotates it. The route now makes sense.]

ACT THREE

[Nara gives the visitors a corrected map. Odo checks the first stop against the doorway. He walks them back to the dinosaur hall using the wall signs, not the script. The big skeleton stands in a lit case. He starts his introduction, but his script page is upside down again.]

ODO: The dinosaur was a very tall animal.

RIKO: That's true.

[Pava asks where the tiny claw fits. Odo points to the skeleton. The group compares the cast to the enormous limb in the case. Nara moves the tour party behind the marked line. Odo describes one fact from the label, then lets them look. Riko reads the next fact aloud. Pava recognizes the gift-shop model's missing tail as part of the skeleton display.]

[At the end of the visit, Odo reaches a hallway marked EXIT. He turns left, following the route printed on the old script. The gift shop appears again. Nara hands him the corrected map and points right. Odo turns around, then notices the shop door behind him has a sign reading EXIT THROUGH GALLERY.]

ODO: The museum has a loop.

NARA: The tour did.

[Odo leads the group through the proper exit. He flips the script over and writes the gallery route on the back. Pava keeps the upside-down postcard as a souvenir. Riko asks if the shop sells an accurate version. Nara hands him the corrected visitor map.]

FINAL IMAGE: Odo leaves the gift shop with the visitors on the right route, while his script's arrow points back to the dinosaur hats.
