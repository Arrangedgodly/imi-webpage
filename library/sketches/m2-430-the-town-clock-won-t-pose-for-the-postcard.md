# The Town Clock Won't Pose for the Postcard

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: tourism campaigns that need an iconic landmark to stay still for the camera*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Photographer Nari, a lemur; guide Vex, an orangutan; clock keeper Mara, a macaque; visitor Pip, a capuchin.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - NOON

[A postcard camera is set up facing the clock tower. Vex holds a small crowd in place. Mara listens to the clock.]

VEX: We need the hands at twelve.

MARA: They are at twelve.

NARI: The bell will ring in a moment.

PIP: I can hold the minute hand.

MARA: No one touches the clock.

[Nari frames the shot. The clock chimes. A flock of pigeons lifts from the roof and blocks the face.]

NARI: We missed it.

VEX: The pigeons have photobombed our heritage.

[Mara turns a brass crank to reset the display. The clock advances one minute.]

MARA: It has started again.

VEX: Can we wind it back?

MARA: It is a clock, not a camera.

[Pip climbs the stairs and opens the maintenance hatch. The pigeons return and settle on the minute hand. It bends under their weight.]

PIP: They are sitting on time.

VEX: Ask them to move.

PIP: They won't take direction.

[Nari tries to photograph the clock from below. Vex shifts the crowd so their heads form a semicircle. The pigeons' shadows make the hands look like a beak.]

VEX: The town clock has a face.

MARA: It always had a face.

NARI: It looks like a bird now.

[Pip climbs one rung higher. The pigeons all turn toward him, blocking the other side of the face.]

PIP: They are watching the photo shoot.

VEX: They are part of the landmark.

[Vex offers a crumb to clear the dial. The pigeons shift to the ledge, where they sit in a row like an audience. Nari takes another picture.]

NARI: Now the clock has spectators.

MARA: The spectators are on the clock.

[Pip reaches for the camera tripod. A pigeon hops onto its handle, tilting the lens toward the crowd.]

VEX: The town is in the frame.

NARI: The clock isn't.

[Nari turns the camera back toward the tower. The pigeon remains perched on the tripod and looks directly into the lens.]

[The clock chimes once. The minute hand moves. The pigeons fly, leaving one feather stuck across the number twelve.]

VEX: The landmark has changed its logo.

[Mara reaches the hatch and removes the feather. Pip takes a postcard from Vex and places it on the clock ledge. The card blows away.]

PIP: The picture has left.

NARI: We can take another.

[Mara says the clock will not chime again until one. Vex asks the crowd to wait. Everyone looks at the clock. It advances a minute at a time.]

VEX: The town has a long minute.

MARA: We are watching the clock.

FINAL BEAT: Nari photographs the empty square. Vex asks where the clock went. Mara points up. The photo shows only the pigeons returning to the roof.
