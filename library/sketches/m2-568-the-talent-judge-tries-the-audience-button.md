# The Talent Judge Tries the Audience Button

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised talent contests that mistake a convenient sound effect for public opinion*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: JUDGE KORA, a baboon; SINGER PIM, a macaque; SOUND OPERATOR NEL, a gibbon; FLOOR MANAGER RUD, an orangutan.

[A rehearsal stage. Pim waits by a microphone. Kora sits behind a large red button. Nel stands at a sound desk. Rud holds the rehearsal schedule. The audience seats are empty.]

KORA: I shall let the audience decide.

RUD: There is no audience until seven.

[Kora presses the red button. A recorded cheer fills the room. Pim smiles tentatively.]

PIM: Have I already passed?

NEL: That is our button test.

KORA: A very positive response.

ACT ONE

[Rud asks Pim to sing one verse for the microphone check. Pim sings a short original melody, stopping at the marked breath. Kora presses the button before the last note ends. The same cheer plays.]

KORA: They loved the finish.

NEL: They cheered through it. I need to hear that note.

PIM: I can sing it again once the cheer stops.

[Nel waits for the effect to finish and asks Kora to keep his hands flat on the desk. Pim sings the last phrase. The microphone is clear. Kora taps a finger, resisting the button.]

KORA: Silence can indicate deep feeling.

RUD: It can also indicate six hundred empty chairs.

[Nel changes the button assignment for a second test, telling Kora it now plays a single quiet tone. Kora presses it. A small beep sounds.]

KORA: The public has grown thoughtful.

NEL: The public is still outside the building.

[Pim asks whether the actual contest will use the cheer button. Rud explains that the audience microphones will carry the real response. The button is for rehearsal cues and will be removed from the judge's desk.]

KORA: How will I show my decision?

RUD: With the score card in front of you.

[Kora lifts his card. A red number eight is printed on it. He turns it around and discovers it is identical on the reverse.]

KORA: The card supports my choice from both sides.

NEL: Please choose only after the whole performance.

ACT TWO

[Rud removes the button and gives it to Nel. Nel places it beside her sound desk. Kora's desk now has the score cards, a pencil and a glass of water.]

KORA: It looks very bare.

PIM: The person singing is meant to fill the time.

[Pim rehearses a complete short verse and its last held note. Kora waits until the note ends, then raises an eight. Rud records the rehearsal score only as a cue test, not a contest result.]

RUD: That timing works. Tonight, use your actual judgment.

[Kora nods. He looks toward the empty seats and begins composing a compliment. Nel accidentally rests her schedule on the removed button. The little test beep sounds once. She lifts the page immediately.]

KORA: A lone dissenting voice.

NEL: A piece of paper.

[Pim laughs and walks to the wing for water. Kora puts down the eight, picks up the pencil and writes: WAIT FOR END. Rud checks the clock; there is still time before the doors open.]

KORA: When the audience arrives, may I thank them?

RUD: Yes. They will be able to answer without the button.

FINAL IMAGE: Kora waits beneath his handwritten reminder while Nel puts the rehearsal button in its case.
