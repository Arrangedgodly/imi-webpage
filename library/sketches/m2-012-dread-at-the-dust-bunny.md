# Dread at the Dust Bunny

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Horror trailers and ominous movie-preview narration*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Trailer Voice, a deep-voiced baboon; Tilda, a nervous marmoset; Wex, a capuchin vacuum repairer; Dust Bunny, a timid rabbit.

[LIGHTS OUT. A SINGLE CLOCK TICKS. A VACUUM MOTOR RUMBLES UNDER THE STAGE.] 

TRAILER VOICE: In a house where no one dares dust, one soft shape waits beneath the couch.

TILDA: I saw it move.

WEX: The vacuum moved.

TILDA: It was unplugged.

WEX: It was not unplugged.

TRAILER VOICE: It feeds on lint. It grows with every forgotten corner.

TILDA: I can hear it breathing.

WEX: That's the vacuum bag.

TILDA: The bag is empty.

WEX: It is full.

TRAILER VOICE: Tonight, the furniture will rise.

[A COUCH CUSHION LIFTS AND DROPS.] 

TILDA: There! It moved!

WEX: The leg is uneven.

TILDA: Can you fix it?

WEX: I need a shim.

TILDA: We are under attack, and you need a shim?

WEX: I need a shim for the couch.

TRAILER VOICE: From the deepest shadow comes a sound no creature can explain.

[A SCRATCH. A TINY SNEEZE.] 

TILDA: The monster sneezed.

WEX: Dust.

TILDA: It knows my weakness.

WEX: Dust makes everyone sneeze.

TRAILER VOICE: One brave monkey must enter the closet.

TILDA: Why the closet?

WEX: That's where the broom is.

TILDA: I am not going in alone.

WEX: I'll go. Keep the light on.

TRAILER VOICE: The light cannot save you.

TILDA: It can show the floor.

WEX: I found the broom.

TILDA: Did you find the creature?

WEX: I found a sock with a face drawn on it.

TILDA: It has eyes.

WEX: The eyes are marker.

TRAILER VOICE: Its gaze follows you.

TILDA: The eyes really do follow me.

WEX: That's how circles work.

[THE VACUUM CLICKS OFF. A FLUFFY RABBIT EMERGES FROM UNDER THE COUCH.] 

DUST BUNNY: Sorry. I was hiding from the vacuum.

TILDA: You are the dust monster?

DUST BUNNY: I am a rabbit. The dust is on me.

WEX: I can clean the floor and leave the rabbit alone.

DUST BUNNY: Please use the soft brush.

TRAILER VOICE: The creature is gentle. The vacuum is not.

TILDA: That is less scary.

WEX: The vacuum has a quiet setting.

TILDA: Does the trailer have a quiet setting?

TRAILER VOICE: It does now.

[SFX: VACUUM LOWERS TO A WHISPER. COUCH LEG STOPS WOBBLING.] 

DUST BUNNY: Can I stay under the couch?

TILDA: If you stop eating the lint.

DUST BUNNY: I thought it was nesting fluff.

WEX: We'll make you a proper nest from clean cotton.

TRAILER VOICE: This fall, the house will face its greatest fear.

TILDA: The electricity bill?

WEX: The vacuum's warranty.

TRAILER VOICE: Dread at the Dust Bunny. Rated G for grooming.

FARA: The dust bunny moved.
VELL: It is a clump of fur.
FARA: It moved toward the vacuum.
VELL: Perhaps it wants to be cleaned.
FARA: Or it is luring us.
VELL: It has no eyes.
FARA: That is how it watches.
VELL: The vacuum is unplugged.
FARA: It planned this.

[LIGHTS UP. DUST BUNNY SNEEZES, AND EVERYONE SNEEZES BACK.]
