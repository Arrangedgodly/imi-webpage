# Crate Expectations

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Influencer unboxing videos and affiliate-link enthusiasm*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Vee, a capuchin lifestyle influencer; Zuzu, her macaque camera operator; Pip, a tarsier delivery helper; Sponsor Voice, a peacock on a tiny speaker.

[STAGE: A RING LIGHT. A WOODEN CRATE SITS ON A PLINTH. A SMALL BELL DINGS.] 

VEE: Hello, leaf-lovers. Today I am opening the Canopy Crate, a surprise box delivered to my doorstep.

ZUZU: It was delivered to the studio.

VEE: The studio is where my doorstep lives now.

PIP: It is a crate of moss.

VEE: Don't spoil the reveal.

PIP: It is making the floor damp.

VEE: That's part of the experience.

ZUZU: We should put down a mat.

VEE: The mat is in the crate.

PIP: I can see the mat.

VEE: Viewers cannot. They must wait.

[CAMERA ZOOMS IN. VEE HOLDS UP A SMALL WOODEN KNIFE.] 

VEE: Our first tool is a hand-finished crate opener.

ZUZU: It is a spoon.

VEE: A versatile spoon.

PIP: The crate has a latch.

VEE: I will cut the ribbon.

PIP: There is no ribbon.

VEE: The anticipation is building.

ZUZU: The anticipation is a delivery label.

VEE: I love an organic unboxing.

SPONSOR VOICE: Add code VEE20 for twenty percent off your crate opener.

VEE: Thank you, mysterious bird.

SPONSOR VOICE: I am the sponsor voice.

PIP: The latch is open.

VEE: Let me get the first reaction.

ZUZU: The box is open.

VEE: My reaction is delighted.

PIP: You haven't looked inside.

VEE: Delighted is my default.

[LID LIFTS. A PATCH OF MOSS, A WOODEN STOOL, AND A BUNCH OF TWIGS APPEAR.] 

VEE: We have moss, a stool, and a stick bundle.

ZUZU: Those are packing materials.

VEE: They are rustic.

PIP: The real item is underneath.

VEE: A pocket mirror!

ZUZU: It's a broken lens cap.

VEE: It reflects my inner canopy.

PIP: It reflects a light bulb.

SPONSOR VOICE: The Crate is a subscription. Each month, it brings one object and three useful packing materials.

VEE: Can I cancel the subscription?

SPONSOR VOICE: Yes. You must place the crate outside by dawn.

ZUZU: The crate is already outside.

PIP: That is a stage door.

VEE: This month's surprise is a stool with a wobbly leg.

ZUZU: We can fix it with a twig.

VEE: Then the packing is useful.

PIP: I have used the moss to soak up the spill.

VEE: We have tested the crate. It keeps the floor dry, offers one seat, and contains a mirror for looking at the ring light.

ZUZU: Would you recommend it?

VEE: I would recommend buying a stool and gathering moss outside.

SPONSOR VOICE: That does not use the code.

VEE: The code is for people who prefer damp surprises.

PIP: Should I close the crate?

VEE: Leave it open. The next video is "Organizing Your Packing Material."

ZUZU: It is all going back in the box.

VEE: Exactly. Subscribe for the reveal.

[THE CRATE LID FALLS SHUT ON ITS OWN. THE SPONSOR SPEAKER DINGS.]
