# The Weather Report for One Leaf

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hyperlocal forecast segments and elaborate warnings for small changes*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: FORECASTER KET, a gibbon; FIELD REPORTER ONA, a macaque; and GRAPHICS TECH FIP, a squirrel monkey.

[A GREEN SCREEN SHOWS ONE LEAF ON A BLANK BACKDROP. Ket points to a spinning chart.] 

KET: Our afternoon forecast calls for a slight breeze, scattered shade, and one leaf moving from the west branch to the lower path.

ONA: The leaf has not moved yet.

FIP: The graphic shows it moving at high speed.

KET: That's the wind model.

ONA: The actual leaf is still on the branch.

KET: We should prepare viewers for change.

FIP: The model predicts a ninety percent chance of rustling.

ONA: I can hear it now.

[SFX: A LEAF RUSTLES ONCE.] 

KET: The forecast is confirmed.

ONA: The leaf is still on the branch.

KET: It has experienced the breeze.

FIP: Should I update the icon?

KET: Show the leaf with motion lines.

FIP: I have drawn six lines.

ONA: It moved once.

KET: The lines indicate potential.

[THE SCREEN NOW SHOWS A HURRICANE ICON OVER THE SINGLE LEAF.] 

ONA: That looks severe.

FIP: The hurricane icon was in the template.

KET: We need a calm advisory. Tell people to secure loose objects.

ONA: There are no loose objects.

FIP: The leaf is loose.

ONA: It is attached to the branch.

KET: Then advise viewers not to secure attached leaves.

ONA: The leaf just fell.

[THE LEAF FLOATS DOWN AND LANDS ON ONA'S SHOULDER.] 

FIP: It has reached the lower path.

KET: We have a significant location change.

ONA: It is still a leaf.

KET: The forecast has verified our model.

ONA: The model predicted motion. It did not predict that it would land on me.

FIP: I can add an icon for shoulder.

KET: Please don't.

[ONA SETS THE LEAF BESIDE A ROOT SO IT WILL NOT BLOW BACK INTO THE PATH.] 

KET: The leaf is now stationary.

ONA: Until the next breeze.

FIP: I have a warning graphic ready: FALLING LEAF, LOW RISK, HIGH VISIBILITY.

KET: That is too much language for one leaf.

ONA: The leaf is on the ground. We can check whether it blocks the path.

[ONA MOVES THE LEAF TO THE ROOT OF THE TREE.] 

KET: Is it still part of the forecast?

ONA: It is part of the ground now.

FIP: The graphic can show the leaf at ground level.

KET: We do not need a graphic for every change.

FIP: Then how will viewers know the leaf moved?

ONA: They can listen. It rustled when it fell.

[SFX: A LIGHT RUSTLE.] 

KET: That sound is enough.

FIP: The model has updated. It now predicts a ninety percent chance the leaf will stay on the path.

ONA: We moved it off the path.

KET: The model is behind the report.

FIP: I'll clear the screen.

[THE HURRICANE ICON DISAPPEARS. A BLANK GREEN SCREEN REMAINS.] 

KET: That is much calmer.

ONA: The tree is still there.

KET: We should forecast the breeze and let the leaf be a leaf.

FIP: I can do that without the red arrow.

KET: Please do.

KET: We will update the forecast if that happens.

FIP: I can remove the hurricane.

KET: Replace it with a leaf.

FIP: The leaf is already there.

KET: Then our graphic is accurate for once.
