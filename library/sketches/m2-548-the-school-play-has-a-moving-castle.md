# The School Play Has a Moving Castle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: theatrical stagecraft, live scenery changes, and child actors whose castle moves during the royal speech*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: DIRECTOR, a baboon; PRINCE, a macaque; QUEEN, an orangutan; STAGEHAND, a capuchin; NARRATOR, a gibbon.

[A school auditorium. A cardboard castle stands on a wheeled platform. The prince rehearses a speech. The stagehand holds the brake.]

PRINCE: Our kingdom is safe behind these walls.

[The stagehand nudges the platform. The castle rolls three feet to the left.]

QUEEN: The kingdom is moving.

DIRECTOR: Keep the speech going.

ACT ONE

[The narrator reads a cue: "The castle trembles." The stagehand shakes the platform. The castle begins rolling toward the curtain. The prince follows it, still speaking.]

PRINCE: We shall defend the gate!

STAGEHAND: Which gate?

[The queen tries to catch the platform. Her robe catches on a wheel. The director calls for a pause and asks the stagehand to lock both casters.]

DIRECTOR: Scenery must stay put.

STAGEHAND: The floor slopes.

[The stagehand wedges a rubber stop under one wheel. The castle stays still, but the prop drawbridge falls down and blocks the prince's feet.]

PRINCE: The bridge has surrendered.

NARRATOR: The bridge is a flap of cardboard.

[The director asks for a second run. The queen enters from the wrong side because the castle now blocks her entrance. The narrator walks around the platform to find the script. The prince waits at center stage.]

QUEEN: I cannot reach the throne.

PRINCE: It is on the castle.

[The stagehand moves the castle slightly to clear a path. The rear panel opens, revealing the broom and paint cans stored inside.]

NARRATOR: The kingdom contains cleaning supplies.

[The director decides the castle should not roll during the speech. The stagehand removes the wheels and sets the platform on blocks. The prince rehearses his line again. The queen enters through the clear aisle and reaches her mark.]

PRINCE: Our kingdom is safe behind these walls.

[The drawbridge falls. The queen catches it and holds it up with one hand.]

QUEEN: The kingdom requires maintenance.

[The director asks the cast to rehearse the ending. The queen closes the drawbridge, the prince delivers his line, and the guard performs the tremble. The cardboard castle stays on its blocks. The stagehand checks every wheel and puts the removed casters in a labeled box.]

NARRATOR: The kingdom has been secured.

STAGEHAND: The box is secured too.

ACT TWO

[The stagehand screws the drawbridge hinge and checks the floor blocks. The castle stays in place. The cast performs the speech once without interruption. The director lets the prince finish, then calls for the scenery crew to reset.]

DIRECTOR: Now we can rehearse the tremble.

[The stagehand gives the castle one small shake. It does not move. The actor playing the guard shakes instead.]

FINAL IMAGE: The cardboard castle stands firm while the guard trembles beside the locked wheels.
