# The Picnic Is Sponsored by a Napkin

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: branded outdoor events where sponsors claim the smallest patch of public space*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Organizer Nari, a lemur; sponsor Vex, a macaque; guest Pip, a capuchin; park keeper Mara, a gibbon.

EXT. PARK LAWN - NOON

[A picnic blanket lies on the grass. One paper napkin sits in its center with a printed logo. Vex stands beside it with a banner.]

VEX: Welcome to the FizzCo summer picnic.

PIP: Where is the food?

NARI: We are waiting for the sponsors.

MARA: The park is open to everyone.

[Vex unfolds the banner. It covers the blanket and shades the napkin.]

VEX: The brand needs a clear presence.

PIP: I can't see lunch.

VEX: Lunch is a concept.

[Nari places a basket on the blanket. Vex unrolls a tablecloth over it, also marked with the logo. The basket disappears.]

MARA: That's the guests' food.

VEX: Our placement is premium.

[Mara lifts the tablecloth. The basket handle has snagged a corner, pulling the cloth into a tent over the napkin. Pip crawls underneath.]

PIP: I found the shade.

VEX: That is the sponsor lounge.

[More guests arrive and sit on the edge of the banner. It slides across the lawn, carrying the napkin on top. Vex follows it with the microphone.]

VEX: Stay within the activation area.

NARI: It's a blanket.

MARA: It is leaving the park.

[The cloth catches on a tree root and flips. The napkin floats down, logo-side-up, onto Vex's head. The guests' sandwiches roll free. Pip catches one in his cheek pouch.]

VEX: The logo has achieved visibility.

PIP: So has lunch.

[Nari pulls the cloth back onto the grass. The sponsor banner falls over it. Mara tethers the banner to a fence with Vex's microphone cable.]

MARA: Now the wind can sponsor the fence.

[Vex steps to the center of the cloth for a sponsor photo. The banner covers his shoes and hides the logo on his shirt.]

VEX: The event needs an ambassador.

PIP: The napkin has the logo.

[Pip lifts the napkin. A mustard stain shows through the paper. Vex rotates it to hide the stain. The printed logo now faces the grass.]

NARI: Your brand is underground.

VEX: It is investing in roots.

[Mara picks up the napkin before it blows away and tucks it under the basket to stop it sliding.]

VEX: We need a photo of the event.

[Pip arranges sandwiches in a circle around the napkin. Nari sets drinks beside them. Vex stands outside the frame, holding the giant banner upright.]

NARI: The napkin is the centerpiece.

PIP: It has the only logo.

[The wind lifts the napkin. Vex drops the banner to catch it. The brand disappears, but the guests cheer as the sandwiches stay put.]

FINAL BEAT: Mara folds the napkin and hands it to Vex. He uses it to wipe mustard off the sponsor logo on his own shirt.
