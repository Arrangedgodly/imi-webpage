# The Snowperson Has a Dress Code

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: winter festivals that judge snow sculptures by accessories and rules unrelated to sculpting*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Sculptor Piri, a macaque; Judge Nala, a gibbon; Festival Host Omi, a capuchin; and Groundskeeper Kesh, a baboon.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - WINTER DAY

Three snowpeople stand behind a rope. Piri's snowperson has smooth arms. Nala carries a clipboard. Omi holds a box of approved accessories.

OMI: This year's theme is winter formal.

PIRI: I built a snowperson.

NALA: It needs a scarf, gloves, and a name card.

KESH: The gloves keep falling off.

[PIRI PUTS THE GLOVES BACK ON. THEY SLIDE DOWN THE SNOW ARMS.]

PIRI: The arms are too small for the gloves.

OMI: The dress code requires them.

NALA: The snowperson's buttons are uneven.

PIRI: They are stones.

OMI: The entry needs a name.

PIRI: It is a snowperson.

KESH: The wind is picking up.

NALA: The gloves have blown off.

[ONE GLOVE SKIDS ACROSS THE SQUARE AND CATCHES ON THE AWARD BANNER.]

OMI: The banner is part of the display.

PIRI: The glove is now part of the banner.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

Omi pins a paper sash across the snowperson. Kesh brings a bucket of slush to patch a cracked base.

NALA: The sash covers the sculpted chest.

OMI: It identifies the entry number.

PIRI: The number is on the card.

KESH: The base is melting faster on the sunny side.

NALA: Is that a structural flaw?

KESH: It is the sun.

OMI: We can move it into the shade.

[PIRI AND KESH MOVE THE SNOWPERSON. THE SASH CATCHES ON THE ROPE AND PULLS OFF A SNOW ARM.]

PIRI: The arm is gone.

NALA: We must report any alteration.

KESH: The arm fell off.

INT. FESTIVAL TENT - AWARDS

The judge studies the entries. Piri's snowperson now has one arm, no gloves, and a clean name card.

OMI: Can we replace the arm?

PIRI: I can make a new one.

NALA: The judging has begun.

KESH: It takes a minute.

NALA: We would need to pause the scoring.

[PIRI ROLLS A SNOWBALL AND ATTACHES IT AS AN ARM. IT IS LARGER THAN THE OTHER ARM.]

OMI: It is asymmetrical.

PIRI: The snowperson has a waving arm now.

[THE SNOW ARM SLUMPS AND WAVES AT THE GROUND.]

NALA: The pose is expressive.

KESH: The sun is taking the other arm.

OMI: We should announce the award before it melts.

[PIRI'S SNOWPERSON LEANS FORWARD AND ITS NAME CARD FALLS INTO THE SNOW.]

PIRI: It is bowing.

NALA: The award goes to the entry with the most natural finish.

OMI: Should we put the ribbon on the snowperson?

KESH: It will melt faster where the ribbon touches.

PIRI: Put it on the name card.

[OMI PINS THE RIBBON TO THE CARD. THE CARD TILTS FORWARD AND DISAPPEARS INTO THE SNOW.]

OMI: The card has entered the sculpture.

KESH: The snowperson has become the snow.
