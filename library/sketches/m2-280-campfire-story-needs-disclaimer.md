# The Campfire Story Needs a Disclaimer

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: risk-averse entertainment scripts that interrupt a scary story with excessive disclaimers*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Camp leader Miri, a capuchin; storyteller Odo, a baboon; camper Runi, a gibbon; and camper Kito, a macaque.

EXT. CAMPFIRE RING - NIGHT

The group sits around a small fire. Odo holds a flashlight under his chin. Miri holds a clipboard.

ODO: Deep in the woods, a shadow moved between the trees.

MIRI: Please clarify whether the shadow was a person, animal, or cloud.

ODO: It was a shadow.

RUNI: That is why it is scary.

MIRI: We need a content note before the next part.

KITO: The next part has a door.

MIRI: Is the door safe?

ODO: It is a cabin door in a story.

[Miri writes "FICTIONAL DOOR" on the clipboard.]

EXT. CAMPFIRE - MOMENTS LATER

Odo continues. A knock sounds from the dark. Everyone jumps. Kito holds up a tent peg.

KITO: What caused the knock?

ODO: In the story, no one knew.

MIRI: We need to identify the sound source.

[They all turn toward the supply tent. A loose pot lid rocks in the breeze.]

RUNI: The wind knocked the lid.

ODO: That was not the story's knock.

MIRI: Listeners may confuse them.

KITO: Now I am afraid of the pot lid.

[The lid rocks again. Runi places a stone on it; it stops.]

EXT. CAMPFIRE - BEFORE THE ENDING

Miri gives each camper a checklist. It includes the story's sound effects, the source of the fire, and the location of every tent flap.

KITO: Are we listening or inspecting?

MIRI: We can do both.

[Odo makes a soft owl hoot. Miri checks "wildlife sound." Runi points to the trees.]

RUNI: The owl is real.

[Miri adds a second note. The owl hoots again, exactly like Odo.]

ODO: It is echoing me.

KITO: We have a sound source and a copy.

MIRI: I will need another checkbox.
EXT. CAMPFIRE - LATER

Miri reads from her clipboard.

MIRI: This story contains suspense, an unidentified shadow, and one fictional door.

ODO: It also contains a forest.

MIRI: Does the forest have a clear exit?

RUNI: The path is behind us.

KITO: The story's character is still inside the cabin.

ODO: The character has not entered yet.

[The group waits. Odo lowers the flashlight. The fire pops. Everyone jumps again.]

MIRI: Was that the fire or the story?

ODO: The fire.

RUNI: This is more frightening than the story.

EXT. CAMPFIRE - ENDING

Odo gives the final line. Miri stops writing and listens.

ODO: The door creaked open. Inside was a note that said, "Please close the door behind you."

[The group turns toward the supply tent. Its flap is open. Miri walks over and closes it.]

MIRI: The disclaimer has been resolved.

KITO: The story is over.

RUNI: What is in the tent?

MIRI: Sleeping bags.

[The tent flap opens again as the wind catches it. Odo shines the flashlight at the loose tie.]

ODO: It is the wind, not the shadow.

[Miri writes "WIND". A gust flips every page on her clipboard, exposing a blank form titled UNEXPECTED DEVELOPMENTS.]

MIRI: I will need another checkbox.

[Odo switches off the flashlight. Runi begins snoring before Miri can find her pen.]
