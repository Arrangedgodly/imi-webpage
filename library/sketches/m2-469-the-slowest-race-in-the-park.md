# The Slowest Race in the Park

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports commentary and race-day ceremonies that mistake patience for a lack of competition*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: COMMENTATOR, a baboon; RUNNER ONE, a gibbon; RUNNER TWO, a macaque; MARSHAL, an orangutan; TIMER, a capuchin.

[A park path. Two runners stand at a starting line. A small bell sits beside the timer.]

COMMENTATOR: Welcome to the slowest race in the park. Contestants must reach the bench without running.

RUNNER ONE: What if I walk quickly?

MARSHAL: Walking is allowed. Running is not.

RUNNER TWO: What if I stop to smell the flowers?

MARSHAL: You may stop, but the race continues.

[The timer rings the bell. Both runners begin at a measured walk. The commentator whispers loudly into a microphone.] 

COMMENTATOR: Runner One takes an early lead.

TIMER: They are side by side.

COMMENTATOR: Runner Two is making a tactical pause.

[Runner Two stops to tie a loose shoe. Runner One stops too, then notices the flower patch.] 

RUNNER ONE: I am not required to stop.

RUNNER TWO: You are allowed to stop.

RUNNER ONE: I know.

[Runner One smells the flower. The commentator announces a dramatic change of pace. The marshal tells him to use the regular voice.] 

COMMENTATOR: The race is still underway.

MARSHAL: It is a walk.

[The timer's bell rolls off the table. The timer catches it before it hits the path.] 

TIMER: The bell has not finished.

[Both runners reach the bench at the same time. The commentator asks the marshal who crossed first. The marshal says their toes arrived together.] 

RUNNER ONE: Can we tie?

TIMER: The rules allow ties.

COMMENTATOR: We have two winners and one very patient bench.

[The runners sit. The marshal pours water into two cups. The commentator tries to give the bench a medal, but the timer says it is part of the park.] 

MARSHAL: The winners are the two who finished without running.

RUNNER TWO: We did what the race asked.

[The commentator hands each runner a small ribbon. The timer rings the bell softly; it stays on the table this time.] 

[The commentator asks the timer to verify the finish. The timer takes a small paper strip from the table and checks the two footprints pressed into the damp path. Both toes are level.]

TIMER: A tie is official. Neither runner stepped past the line.

COMMENTATOR: We need a photo finish.

MARSHAL: The finish was a bench. It did not move.

[The commentator crouches with a magnifying glass and peers at the footprints. The runners remain seated, still wearing their ribbons.]

COMMENTATOR: I can see one print.

TIMER: You are looking at the same print from two angles.

[The commentator switches places and tries again. The marshal takes away the magnifying glass before the commentator can ask the runners to repeat the race.]

MARSHAL: The result stands. The only replay is the timer reading zero.

RUNNER ONE: May we leave at our own pace?

MARSHAL: You may. There is no second lap.

[The runners stand, take one slow step toward the path exit, and stop so the commentator can finish the broadcast without chasing them.]

COMMENTATOR: Both champions are moving at precisely the speed they chose.

FINAL IMAGE: The runners sit together on the bench with matching ribbons while the bell rests safely beside the timer.
