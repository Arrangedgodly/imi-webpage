# The Hotel Bellhop Hides in a Luggage Cart

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hotel-lobby farces, mistaken room assignments, and service staff treated as part of the luggage*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: BELLHOP, a capuchin; GUEST, a baboon; CLERK, an orangutan; MANAGER, a gibbon; HOUSEKEEPER, a macaque.

[A hotel lobby. A bellhop pushes an empty luggage cart past the front desk. The clerk gives him a room key.]

CLERK: Room twelve. One bag, one guest.

GUEST: I have two bags.

CLERK: Then one bag goes on the cart.

[The bellhop loads both bags. The guest sits on the cart's lower shelf to tie a shoe. The bellhop, not seeing him, pushes the cart toward the elevator.]

BELLHOP: The cart feels unusually heavy.

GUEST: I am trying not to be trouble.

ACT ONE

[The elevator doors open. The manager steps out carrying a sign: CARTS MUST BE EMPTY.]

MANAGER: Why is that cart occupied?

GUEST: I am the guest.

MANAGER: Guests go in elevators.

BELLHOP: He came with the bags.

[The clerk checks the room list and finds the guest booked into room twelve, but the key is for room twenty-one. The guest has followed the wrong cart to the wrong floor.]

CLERK: The key is upside down.

GUEST: So was the room number on the envelope.

[The housekeeper brings a fresh key. The bellhop tries to unload the bags, but the guest stands on the lower shelf and the cart tips backward. The manager catches one bag; the clerk catches the other; the guest stays seated, still tying his shoe.]

MANAGER: We need a rule.

GUEST: I would recommend not loading the guest beneath the bags.

[The bellhop backs the cart to level ground and puts both suitcases on the floor. The guest gets off and walks to the elevator. The cart rolls after him because one wheel is not locked.]

HOUSEKEEPER: The cart wants to check in too.

[She catches it with a foot. The manager inspects the wheel and finds a luggage tag wrapped around the brake. The bellhop removes the tag, locks the wheel, and puts the key on top of the bags.]

BELLHOP: Room twelve.

GUEST: That is my number.

[The elevator dings. Everyone tries to enter with the cart. It does not fit. The clerk asks the guest to take the stairs while the bellhop uses the service lift. The guest reaches the corridor first and waits beside a door marked 21.]

ACT TWO

[The housekeeper brings the correct key and swaps it for the upside-down one. The clerk reads room twelve aloud. The guest checks the number, opens the door, and carries his own overnight bag inside. The bellhop wheels the large suitcase to the threshold and leaves the cart in the hallway.]

GUEST: Thank you. I can take it from here.

BELLHOP: The cart is not going in.

MANAGER: That is the new rule.

[The guest wheels the suitcase over the threshold. The cart's loose tag flutters and blocks the room number.]

FINAL IMAGE: The guest settles into room twelve while the bellhop hangs a DO NOT LOAD GUESTS tag on the empty cart.
