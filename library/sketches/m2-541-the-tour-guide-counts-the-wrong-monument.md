# The Tour Guide Counts the Wrong Monument

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: historical walking tours, landmark trivia, and guides who follow the loudest group instead of the map*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: GUIDE, an orangutan; TOURIST, a macaque; DRIVER, a baboon; HISTORIAN, a gibbon; VENDOR, a capuchin.

[A town square. Beside the group stands an unlabelled stone figure holding a loaf. A folding sign reads OLD CLOCK TOWER TOUR.]

GUIDE: This is our first mayor.

TOURIST: The mayor is holding bread?

GUIDE: A ceremonial loaf.

[The historian points across the square to a statue holding a scroll.]

HISTORIAN: That one is the mayor. The unlabelled loaf-holder is the clockmaker.

GUIDE: The pamphlet gave them both hats.

ACT ONE

[The group starts toward the clock tower. The driver eases the tour bus along behind the walkers at a crawl; the folding sign, tied to its luggage rack, rolls after them on its little wheels.]

TOURIST: The sign is joining the tour.

DRIVER: It's still attached to the bus. I am keeping walking speed.

[The bus pulls into a marked bay and parks. Only then does the driver unhook the sign and fold its wheels.]

[The group reaches the tower, where scaffolding hides the clock face. The historian explains the clock is being repaired. The guide points to the blank tarp.]

GUIDE: This is where the clock used to be.

TOURIST: It is still behind the tarp.

HISTORIAN: Exactly.

[The tourist takes a photo. The guide asks everyone to look toward the statue for a reenactment. The vendor brings over a paper bag for the loaf-holder statue. The group mistakes the clockmaker statue for the mayor and lines up for a staged photograph.]

GUIDE: The clockmaker will now accept the town's first sandwich.

VENDOR: That bag is my lunch.

[With the bus parked, the driver carries the sign to the first stop. The historian notices that the tour map has the landmarks numbered in reverse because it was printed double-sided.]

HISTORIAN: We started at stop four.

GUIDE: So the sandwich was the ending?

TOURIST: It was a beginning for the vendor.

[The guide rearranges the route, placing the tower after the statue. The tourist turns the map right side up. The driver unfolds the wheels and rolls the sign beside the correct stop.]

ACT TWO

[The guide gives a short explanation of both statues, naming the mayor and the clockmaker correctly. The vendor takes the sandwich back. The group takes one photo with the mayor statue and one with the clock tower tarp. The driver points at the bus clock; the tour is exactly on time.]

GUIDE: The tower will be visible when repairs are done.

TOURIST: We can come back for the face.

[The guide folds the map along its marked lines and gives the group one last direction to the bus. The tourist checks the sign before walking. The historian points out that the clockmaker designed the clock but did not build the mayor's statue.]

GUIDE: Tomorrow I will bring the corrected pamphlets.

TOURIST: And a sandwich?

VENDOR: Only if you buy it.

FINAL IMAGE: The guide rolls the corrected map closed while the sandwich vendor walks off with his recovered lunch loaf.
