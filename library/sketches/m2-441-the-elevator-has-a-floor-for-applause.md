# The Elevator Has a Floor for Applause

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury hotel service announcements and elevators that turn routine movement into an occasion*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: GUEST, a macaque; LIFT ATTENDANT, a gibbon; MANAGER, an orangutan; BELLHOP, a capuchin.

[A hotel elevator. The attendant wears white gloves and a sash. The manager rides beside them in a uniform. The bellhop holds a tiny cymbal.]

ATTENDANT: Welcome aboard the vertical lounge. Which floor?

GUEST: Three.

ATTENDANT: A classic choice. The third floor is where the hallway begins to expect you.

[The attendant presses 3. The bellhop taps the cymbal. The elevator begins to rise.]

GUEST: I can go up without a fanfare.

ATTENDANT: Of course. We have a silent package.

[The bellhop puts the cymbal behind his back. It rings when he moves.]

GUEST: Does the silent package include the cymbal?

ATTENDANT: It includes not mentioning it.

[The elevator stops between floors. A light reads 2 1/2.]

GUEST: Is this my floor?

ATTENDANT: This is our appreciation level.

BELLHOP: Please applaud the lift.

GUEST: I am holding a suitcase.

BELLHOP: One hand is enough.

[The guest does not clap. The lift attendant shakes a maraca.]

MANAGER: Our elevators are equipped to read guest enthusiasm.

GUEST: Then it can read my silence.

MANAGER: The sensor has been trained to interpret silence as anticipation.

[The attendant reaches for a plain button. A hidden speaker whispers "thank you for choosing direct service." The lift remains at the half floor. The manager opens a small panel and finds two controls marked APPLAUSE and ENCORE. The attendant disconnects the speaker wire, revealing ordinary numbered floor buttons beneath it.]

ATTENDANT: The third floor button is marked 3.

GUEST: Was it there the whole time?

MANAGER: The display was covering it. We will try the lift again.

[The manager presses the lobby reset button. The elevator drops gently to the lobby. The doors open. A small audience of hotel staff applauds.]

GUEST: I asked for the third floor.

MANAGER: We brought you to the first round of applause.

GUEST: Can I take the stairs?

ATTENDANT: Certainly. We have a stairwell with a private landing for guests who prefer not to be celebrated.

[They enter the stairwell. A door opens to a carpeted platform labeled 2 1/2. The bellhop follows with the cymbal.]

GUEST: You have a half floor in the stairs too?

ATTENDANT: We are consistent.

[The bellhop bows. The cymbal slips down the stair rail and rings on each step.]

GUEST: Is this also part of the silent package?

BELLHOP: Only on the way down.

GUEST: I would like to reach my room without a performance.

[They descend the stairs to the lobby. The attendant removes the sash and the manager points to the exposed floor controls.]
[The lobby doors open. The bellhop retrieves the cymbal from the bottom step and carries it by the handle. The manager points out the exposed floor controls.]

MANAGER: Reset at the lobby. Now the attendant can take you straight to three.

[The guest reenters the elevator. The bellhop stays in the lobby with the cymbal. The manager rides with the guest and attendant. The attendant presses the exposed 3 button. The doors close, the car rises, and they open at floor three.]

GUEST: That was one trip.

MANAGER: And no applause required.


FINAL IMAGE: The guest walks down the third-floor hall while the elevator waits, completely uncelebrated.
