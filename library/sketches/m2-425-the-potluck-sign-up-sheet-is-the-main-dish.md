# The Potluck Sign-Up Sheet Is the Main Dish

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: communal dinners where the planning spreadsheet becomes more important than the food*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Organizer Nari, a lemur; guest Pip, a capuchin; guest Vex, a macaque; guest Mara, a gibbon.

INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - EVENING

[A long table is empty except for a clipboard. Nari points to the sign-up sheet.]

NARI: Please write what you are bringing.

PIP: I brought bananas.

NARI: Write "fruit" in column C.

PIP: I have fruit in my hands.

[Vex arrives with a pot.]

VEX: I made stew.

NARI: Is it vegetarian?

VEX: It is a stew.

[He checks the sheet. The vegetarian column is full. The dessert column has no names.]

VEX: I could call it a dessert.

PIP: It is hot.

NARI: The sheet says we have enough hot dishes.

[Mara walks in with a pie. She looks at the clipboard.]

MARA: I signed up for plates.

NARI: We have no food yet.

MARA: The sheet assigned me plates.

[She sets down a stack of empty plates. Vex ladles stew onto one.]

NARI: We need the dishes before we can serve.

VEX: It says we have plates.

MARA: I brought plates, not dishes.

[Pip places bananas in a row on the table. Nari moves them to the fruit column. Pip moves them back.]

PIP: They are in the wrong column.

NARI: The bananas need a label.

[The sign-up sheet slips off the clipboard and lands on Vex's pot lid. Steam fogs the ink. Names run together.]

VEX: We have lost the menu.

MARA: We can eat the food.

NARI: We don't know who brought what.

PIP: I brought these.

[He points to the bananas. Vex lifts the pot lid; the sheet sticks to it and rises like a paper flag. Nari peels it free. One line has become a list of smudges.]

NARI: The sheet is now part of the stew.

MARA: Then it has contributed.

[Vex serves stew onto the plates. Pip offers bananas. Mara cuts the pie. Everyone eats. Nari tries to fill the sign-up sheet from memory.]

NARI: We have fruit, stew, pie, and plates.

VEX: We have eaten the list.

PIP: It was not the main dish.

[Nari writes the dishes on the clean side of a paper plate. Vex reads the list.]

VEX: We have bananas, stew, and pie.

PIP: And plates.

MARA: We have enough food for everyone.

[A guest reaches for the clipboard. Nari gives them the empty sign-up sheet. The guest tries to write "seconds" in the dessert column.]

NARI: That column is closed.

VEX: The stew is still available.

[He serves himself another plate, then offers the spoon to the guest.]

FINAL BEAT: Nari writes "bring appetite" on the clean back of the sheet. Mara pins it to the wall. Pip adds a banana beside the words, and Vex insists it goes in column C.
