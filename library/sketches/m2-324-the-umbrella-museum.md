# The Umbrella Museum

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: prestige museum audio tours that treat mundane objects as priceless relics*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Curator Nara, a capuchin; guide Odo, a baboon; visitor Leni, a gibbon; conservator Tavi, a macaque; owner Seli, a capuchin.

INT. VILLAGE MUSEUM - DAY

A single umbrella rests beneath a spotlight. Odo whispers into an audio receiver.

ODO: Before you is the umbrella that kept the mayor dry during a brief drizzle.

LENI: Can I open it?

NARA: Please do not touch the artifact.

TAVI: It is borrowed from the lost-and-found.

Odo guides Leni through the exhibit. He says the umbrella's curved handle represents resilience. Nara says it represents a handle. Tavi examines the tag; it reads "Owner: Seli. Please return."

ODO: The inscription deepens the mystery.

NARA: It gives us the owner's name.

Leni asks when the umbrella will be returned. Nara says the museum has scheduled a ceremony first. Odo invites her to imagine the rain that once touched its canopy. Tavi says it was two drops.

The museum has added a rope barrier. Leni walks around it and reads the wall text. The text describes the umbrella as a symbol of civic protection. Nara says the mayor borrowed it from a basket outside the office. Odo says an object can begin in an ordinary place and become meaningful.

Tavi lifts the barrier so the owner can collect her umbrella. Odo stops her; the audio tour is not finished. Leni asks if she can use the umbrella if it rains. Odo says the museum prohibits practical use of displayed objects.

A drop lands from a leaky ceiling onto the floor beside the exhibit.

Leni: The museum needs an umbrella.

Nara looks at the loan form. It says the umbrella is due back today. Tavi lifts the umbrella from its stand and holds it beside the entrance for Seli to collect. Odo reaches for the microphone to narrate the return. Nara asks him to stop.

NARA: It belongs to Seli. We should give it back.

Odo follows them to the entrance, continuing in a reverent voice: "The umbrella departs the gallery, returning to the community from which it came."

Tavi says it is going to the coat hook. Seli arrives, takes the umbrella, and shakes off one drop. She thanks the museum for keeping it dry. Leni asks if she can borrow it after the rain stops.

SELI: It is mine. Ask before borrowing.

Odo says the umbrella has fulfilled its journey. Seli opens it to check the ribs. It works.

Tavi checks the loan form and confirms Seli can take the umbrella now. Odo asks if the exhibit can remain open without its centerpiece. Nara says the empty stand can show the return notice.

TAG

The spotlight has been left on an empty stand.

Odo whispers, "The stand awaits its next masterpiece."

Nara places the return form on it.

NARA: The form is due tomorrow.
