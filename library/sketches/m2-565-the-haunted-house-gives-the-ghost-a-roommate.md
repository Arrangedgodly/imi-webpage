# The Haunted House Gives the Ghost a Roommate

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: haunted-house reveals and the supernatural occupant who discovers ordinary room assignments*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: GHOST VELA, a baboon in a translucent robe; GUEST PIP, a macaque; HOST NARA, a gibbon; CLEANER RIK, an orangutan.

[A guesthouse attic. A suitcase stands beside a bed. Vela rises from behind the wardrobe while Pip unfolds a room-assignment card.]

VELA: You must leave this house before dawn.

PIP: My checkout is eleven.

VELA: I have haunted this room for eighty years.

PIP: Then you know where the spare blanket is.

[Vela points reluctantly at a drawer. Pip takes the blanket, then notices a second small bed behind the wardrobe.]

PIP: Is somebody else staying here?

VELA: That is my spectral resting place.

PIP: The booking said shared attic. I assumed a normal roommate.

ACT ONE

[Nara enters with two keys on one ring. Rik follows with a linen basket.]

NARA: Has everyone found their bed?

VELA: This visitor refuses my warning.

NARA: The shared room costs less. The single room was booked already.

PIP: I have paid for the week.

VELA: I have paid with endless loneliness.

NARA: That is not on our tariff.

[Rik asks Vela to move his robe clear of the basket. Vela rises onto a low platform hidden behind his bed and spreads both arms.]

VELA: No living hand shall pass beyond this line.

RIK: I need to put your clean pillowcase on.

[Vela steps down. Rik changes the pillowcase and returns the old one to the basket. Pip checks the room card again.]

PIP: Quiet hours start at ten. Does the haunting finish then?

VELA: The chain rattles at midnight.

NARA: We moved that to nine thirty after the complaint from the room below.

VELA: The dead do not live by clocks.

RIK: Your chain is on a timer.

[Rik points to the small prop-effects box beside the wardrobe. Its display reads 21:30. Vela lowers his arms.]

PIP: Nine thirty suits me. I can finish my letter before the rattling.

VELA: You intend to write in my presence?

PIP: I can mention you if you'd like.

[Vela asks what Pip will write. Pip reads his opening aloud: "The attic has a long-standing resident who is helping me locate the blankets." Vela offers "an ancient terror" instead. Pip replaces "long-standing resident" but keeps the blanket sentence.]

ACT TWO

[Nara leaves the room's actual door key with Pip. Vela has no need of a key; he has his platform entrance behind the wardrobe. Rik puts the basket by the door and points to the missing pillow on Pip's bed.]

RIK: I will bring one from the linen cupboard.

VELA: Mine is beneath the spare blanket.

[Pip lifts the blanket she took earlier. A small cushion lies underneath. She hands it to Vela. He holds it to his robe.]

PIP: Does your spectral resting place need it?

VELA: It gives shape to eternity.

[Rik fetches Pip's proper pillow and leaves with Nara. Pip sits at the desk to finish her letter. Vela checks the effects timer, waiting for the first rattle.]

VELA: I shall make the night unforgettable.

PIP: Please make it nine thirty. I have a walking tour tomorrow.

FINAL IMAGE: The ghost practices a terrifying pose while Pip labels her envelope with the guesthouse's ordinary street address.
