# The Talk Show Has a Surprise Guest

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: daytime talk shows, surprise entrances, audience reactions, and hosts who tease a guest whose chair is already on stage*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Mira, a macaque; producer Rell, a gibbon; guest Odo, a capuchin; and stagehand Nari, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. TALK SHOW SET - DAY

Two chairs face the audience. One is empty. The other is occupied by Odo, who is holding a cup of water. Mira addresses the camera.

MIRA: Today we have a surprise guest. You will never guess who it is.

ODO: I am Odo.

MIRA: Please don't reveal the surprise.

ODO: I was introduced when I sat down.

Rell speaks into Mira's earpiece.

RELL: We have not shown his name card yet.

MIRA: Our guest has a remarkable story. We will reveal his identity after the break.

ODO: I am already here.

The audience claps. Nari rolls a curtain between the chairs. The curtain hides Odo but blocks the camera.

MIRA: The guest is behind this curtain.

ODO: I can hear you.

RELL: The curtain was for the reveal, not the interview.

Mira steps around it and asks Nari to cue the surprise music. Nari presses the button. A cheerful jingle plays. The audience claps again. Odo remains in the chair, visible behind the curtain's gap.

MIRA: The mystery guest has entered the room.

ODO: I have not moved.

MIRA: How did you first become known?

ODO: I was on this show last week. You asked me to return the cup.

NARI: He was the guest who found the set's missing cup.

RELL: We cannot use the same introduction.

MIRA: We can use a new surprise. Odo has brought something with him.

Odo lifts the cup.

MIRA: It's a cup.

ODO: It is still the same cup.

Rell tells Mira to tease a dramatic reveal. Mira asks Odo to open the cup. He turns it upside down. A note falls out: RETURN TO PROP TABLE.

NARI: That is where the cup belongs.

MIRA: Our surprise guest has solved a mystery.

ODO: I did that last week too.

The audience laughs. Rell signals another break. Mira looks toward the empty chair.

MIRA: We also have a surprise guest on the other chair.

Nari sits down to test it. The chair sinks almost to the floor.

NARI: The chair has a broken leg.

MIRA: That is a surprise.

Odo gets up and helps Nari stand. Rell calls for the repair crew. The show continues with Mira interviewing Odo about why the chairs are unsafe. Odo says he noticed the wobble when he sat down. Nari uses the prop cup to hold one chair level until the repairer arrives.

MIRA: We have revealed the broken chair, the missing cup, and Odo.

ODO: The audience knew me first.

The closing music begins. Rell puts the name card on Odo's chair. It reads GUEST: ODO.

MIRA: We will reveal that at the start next time.

ODO: That is not a surprise either.
