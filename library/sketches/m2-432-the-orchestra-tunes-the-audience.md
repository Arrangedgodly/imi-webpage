# The Orchestra Tunes the Audience

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: concert etiquette and orchestras treating listeners like instruments*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Conductor Sela, a macaque; usher Pip, a capuchin; audience member Nari, a lemur; percussionist Vex, an orangutan.

INT. CONCERT HALL - EVENING

[The orchestra tunes. Sela raises her baton. The audience rustles. Pip distributes small cards marked "A."]

SELA: We will tune the instruments, then begin.

NARI: What are the cards for?

PIP: The audience is slightly flat tonight.

NARI: I haven't made a sound.

VEX: Your coat zipper is in B minor.

[Sela listens as Vex taps a triangle. The audience shifts in its seats.]

SELA: First violins, A.

[The violins sound. Pip walks down the aisle, holding his tuning fork near Nari's shoulder.]

PIP: You're resisting the pitch.

NARI: I'm sitting still.

PIP: Excellent support. Now hum the A.

NARI: I bought a ticket to hear the orchestra.

SELA: And we want you to hear it in tune.

[The players tune again. Nari's chair squeaks. Everyone stops. Sela turns slowly.]

SELA: The chair is sharp.

PIP: We have a felt pad for that.

NARI: Can we start the concert?

SELA: We can, when the room is ready.

[Vex approaches the squeaking chair with a felt pad. He puts it under one leg. The chair no longer squeaks but rocks. Nari leans left to balance. The row follows her movement, producing a low, synchronized groan.]

VEX: The audience has found its bass note.


[Vex passes Nari a card marked PLEASE HUM A. She lowers it.]

NARI: I came to hear the orchestra, not audition for a chair.

PIP: I can hum the note.

SELA: We should be listening instead of recruiting the room.

VEX: The room is our largest instrument.

SELA: The room has doors.

[She opens one. A draft ruffles the programs. The violins tune again while the pages flap.]

NARI: Now the hall has percussion.

PIP: Programs in A.

SELA: Not in the score.

PIP: It is in the seating plan.

[Sela gives a downbeat. A cough sounds in the balcony. The orchestra stops. Pip turns and hands a card to the cougher.]

PIP: Please try again in A.

NARI: You cannot tune a cough.

VEX: Not with that attitude.

[The cougher tries to hum. The orchestra accidentally matches the note. Sela hears a clean chord and freezes with her baton raised.]

SELA: Hold it. That's beautiful.

PIP: The audience is ready.

NARI: I just want the music.

SELA: So do I. We have spent twenty minutes tuning the room to avoid its sound. Let's let it sing.

[Sela gives the downbeat. The orchestra begins. A chair squeaks in rhythm; Vex quietly removes its felt pad, and it squeaks on every beat. Sela changes the tempo to fit it. The audience begins rocking together.]

PIP: We have found the chair's key.

NARI: Is this the piece?

SELA: It is now.

[The final chord lands. The squeak completes it. Sela turns toward the audience.]

SELA: Thank you for coming in tune.

NARI: I was never in tune.

VEX: You were in the seat.
