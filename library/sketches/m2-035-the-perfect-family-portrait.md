# The Perfect Family Portrait

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Formal family portraits and sentimental photo-session conventions*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Photographer Deme, a macaque; parent Ula, a gorilla; parent Fenn, a gibbon; child Kiri, a capuchin; assistant Ollo, a lemur.

[STAGE: A PHOTO STUDIO WITH A PAINTED SUNSET, STOOL, AND A BASKET OF ARTIFICIAL FLOWERS.]

DEME: Welcome. We are going to capture your family exactly as you are.
ULA: Should we sit close?
DEME: Closer. The camera has a narrow idea of family.
FENN: Kiri is climbing the backdrop.
DEME: That is a natural pose.
OLLO: The sunset is falling over her.
KIRI: I am holding it up.
DEME: Excellent. A child supporting the evening.
ULA: Could we have one picture where we all look at the camera?
DEME: Certainly. Everyone look at the lens.
FENN: Kiri is looking at the flower basket.
KIRI: The flowers smell like paint.
DEME: That is the fragrance of memory.
OLLO: The stool is wobbling.
ULA: It is one leg shorter.
DEME: That gives the portrait movement.
FENN: We can stand instead.
DEME: Standing can look very formal.
KIRI: I am sitting on the floor.
DEME: That is an intimate floor-level composition.
ULA: I will sit too.
FENN: Now we are all on the floor.
DEME: Beautiful. The family gathered around a single point.
OLLO: The point is a loose screw.
DEME: A symbol of togetherness.
KIRI: Can I hold the camera?
DEME: Only if you point it at your family.
KIRI: I am part of the family.
DEME: That is the perfect perspective.
[THE CAMERA FLASHES FROM KIRI'S HAND.]
FENN: Did we get it?
OLLO: We got the ceiling.
DEME: The ceiling brings everyone together overhead.
ULA: Can we see a proof?
DEME: It will be ready after I develop the film.
KIRI: I thought this camera was digital.
DEME: It is. I develop the photo by making it more flattering.
FENN: Can you remove the paint on Kiri's hand?
DEME: I can call it a birthmark.
KIRI: It is paint.
DEME: A temporary birthmark.
ULA: Could you leave the mess in?
DEME: The mess tells the story.
FENN: Which story?
DEME: That this family has lived in the room.
OLLO: The room is a studio.
DEME: A studio with a lived-in feeling.
KIRI: I found a real flower behind the backdrop.
DEME: Please hold it up.
KIRI: It has a bug.
DEME: The bug can be a cousin.
ULA: Now we all look at the camera.
FENN: Even the bug.
DEME: Everyone smile on three.
KIRI: One, two-
[THE FLOWER DROOPS. KIRI AND THE ADULTS REACH TO CATCH IT.]
OLLO: The camera caught everyone moving.
DEME: That is the family, in one frame.
ULA: I like it.
FENN: We look busy.
KIRI: We are busy.
DEME: Then the portrait is perfect.
OLLO: Should I take down the painted sunset?
DEME: No. It is holding the room together.

[THE FAMILY EXAMINES THE BLURRED PHOTO WHILE THE PAINTED SUNSET SAGS GENTLY BEHIND THEM.]
