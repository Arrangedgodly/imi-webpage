# The Tiny Unboxing Has a Drumroll

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: unboxing streams that stretch a small product reveal through suspense, reaction shots, and sponsored accessories*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Streamer Piri, a macaque; camera operator Omi, a gibbon; sponsor rep Venn, a capuchin; and viewer Sanu, a baboon.

INT. STREAMING STUDIO - NIGHT

Piri holds a small cardboard box. Omi frames it with two cameras. Venn stands behind a ring light.

PIRI: Tonight we open the new key ring.

SANU (ON SCREEN): It is a key ring.

VENN: Build anticipation before you show it.

[PIRI SHAKES THE BOX. IT MAKES A TINY METAL TICK.]

PIRI: Something is inside.

OMI: The product is inside.

INT. STREAMING STUDIO - MOMENTS LATER

Piri peels one strip of tape. Venn holds up a sponsor card. Omi zooms in.

PIRI: First, we read the packing note.

VENN: This stream is supported by Durable Tape.

SANU (ON SCREEN): The box has tape on it.

[PIRI PEELS THE SECOND STRIP. THE BOX OPENS. INSIDE IS A KEY RING AND A SMALL PAPER INSERT.]

PIRI: We have reached the product.

VENN: Show the insert first.

PIRI: It says "Thank you." 

OMI: Hold it closer.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS SO CLOSE THAT THE WORDS GO OUT OF FOCUS.]

SANU (ON SCREEN): I can read it from here.

INT. STREAMING STUDIO - LATER

Piri attaches one key to the ring. Venn brings out a pouch, a display stand, and a polishing cloth.

VENN: The complete unboxing includes these recommended extras.

PIRI: The ring came by itself.

OMI: The pouch is for storage.

PIRI: My keys go in my pocket.

SANU (ON SCREEN): Does the ring hold keys?

PIRI: It holds one.

[Venn places the ring on the display stand. It slides off.]

VENN: We need a better product shot.

Omi sets the ring beside the box. It rolls into the foam insert and stops.

PIRI: The packaging has a slot for it.

INT. STREAMING STUDIO - DETAIL TABLE

Venn lines up the pouch, stand, cloth, and key ring. Omi changes to the overhead camera.

VENN: We should show the accessories as a complete experience.

PIRI: The ring is the product.

SANU (ON SCREEN): I only need the ring.

VENN: The pouch protects it.

PIRI: The ring protects the key.

[OMI PLACES THE KEY RING IN THE POUCH. IT DISAPPEARS COMPLETELY.]

OMI: The camera cannot see the product.

VENN: We can add a clear window.

PIRI: The pouch did not come with the ring.

SANU (ON SCREEN): I still only need the ring.

INT. STREAMING STUDIO - FINAL REVEAL

Piri holds up the ring. Venn reads a promotional code. Sanu posts a comment.

SANU (ON SCREEN): Is the unboxing over?

PIRI: Yes. The ring is out of the box.

VENN: There is one more sponsored accessory.

[OMI COVERS THE CAMERA WITH THE FOAM INSERT.]

OMI: The stream has ended.

TAG

Piri places the key ring on the table. It rolls back into the box.

PIRI: It has returned to its packaging.
