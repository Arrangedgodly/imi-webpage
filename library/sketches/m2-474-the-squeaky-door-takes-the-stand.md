# The Squeaky Door Takes the Stand

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: courtroom dramas, dramatic witness examinations, and legal arguments built around an audible but ordinary door*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: JUDGE, a baboon; CLERK, a gibbon; LAWYER, a macaque; WITNESS, an orangutan; DOOR TECH, a capuchin.

[A courtroom. A wooden door stands in its frame. The judge enters; the door squeaks behind the judge. Everyone looks at it.]

JUDGE: The court will come to order.

DOOR: SQUEEEEEAK.

LAWYER: The witness has interrupted the court.

CLERK: The witness is the door.

WITNESS: I am here to testify that I heard the sound.

[The lawyer approaches the door with a notepad. The clerk stops the lawyer from touching it.] 

LAWYER: Where were you at the time of the squeak?

WITNESS: In this room.

LAWYER: Can you identify the source?

WITNESS: The hinge.

JUDGE: That is not a person under oath.

[The clerk opens and closes the door carefully. It squeaks each time. The lawyer asks the door tech to explain.] 

DOOR TECH: The top hinge is dry. I have oil and a cloth.

LAWYER: Before we allow a repair, I request a dramatic reenactment.

JUDGE: The court will hear one demonstration.

[The clerk opens the door a few inches. It squeaks. The witness points at the top hinge. The lawyer asks the clerk to repeat it in slow motion; the judge declines.] 

WITNESS: The sound occurs when the hinge turns.

LAWYER: Could it be a chair?

CLERK: The chair is on the other side of the room.

[The door tech applies one drop of oil to the hinge pin, wipes the excess, then opens and closes the door. It moves quietly.] 

LAWYER: The witness has gone silent.

JUDGE: The witness's observation led to a repair. That is sufficient.

WITNESS: I can still testify.

JUDGE: You may, but the court will not ask you to squeak.

[The clerk marks the repair on the maintenance slate. The lawyer tries to object to the quiet test, but the judge raises a hand.] 

JUDGE: Objection noted. Door resolved.

[The lawyer turns to exit. The door opens without a sound; the lawyer turns back, surprised.] 

LAWYER: I was prepared for a closing argument.

DOOR TECH: You may save it for another case.

[The clerk asks the witness to point to the hinge from the witness stand. The witness points. The lawyer requests that the door repeat the sound so the jury can compare it with the testimony.]

JUDGE: The repair has already removed the sound. We will not damage the hinge for dramatic effect.

LAWYER: Could the witness describe it?

WITNESS: A high squeak as the top hinge turned.

DOOR TECH: That matches the dry hinge I oiled.

[The clerk writes "HINGE LUBRICATED" on the slate. The lawyer looks for a final objection and finds the door standing quietly open.]

LAWYER: No further questions.

JUDGE: The court finds the door repaired.

[The witness steps away from the frame. The judge closes the case folder, and the clerk turns the quiet door's sign from "IN EVIDENCE" to "IN SERVICE."]

FINAL IMAGE: The judge signs the maintenance slate as the door closes quietly behind the departing lawyer.
