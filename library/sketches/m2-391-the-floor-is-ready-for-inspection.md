# The Floor Is Ready for Inspection

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: building inspections that treat wet-floor warning signs as evidence of a hazard rather than a cure*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Jori, a gibbon custodian; Vela, a macaque inspector; Fip, a capuchin supervisor; Nara, a lemur visitor.

INT. COMMUNITY CENTER HALL - MORNING

[JORI HAS JUST MOPPED A SMALL SQUARE OF FLOOR. THREE WET-FLOOR SIGNS SURROUND IT. VELA ARRIVES WITH A CLIPBOARD.]

VELA: Is the floor wet?

JORI: I just mopped it.

VELA: Then I must mark a wet-floor finding.

FIP: The signs are already up.

VELA: The signs warn people. They don't change the finding.

Nara approaches carrying a tray of cups. The signs block every way around the wet square.

NARA: Which path is dry?

Jori points toward the far wall. Vela checks that area and marks another square. The mop has not touched it.

VELA: This section is not wet.

JORI: Then why mark it?

VELA: It is a safe route around the wet area.

Fip moves one sign to open a path. Vela puts it back so the signs form a complete triangle.

FIP: We need a clear route.

VELA: A triangle has three exits.

Nara tries to pass between two signs. The edge of her tray touches one, and it tips. The cups slide but do not spill.

JORI: Careful.

NARA: I was careful. The sign moved.

Vela photographs the fallen sign. She writes "warning device unstable." Jori wrings the mop into the bucket and uses it to dry the wet square. The floor is clean, but Vela asks him to leave a small area wet so she can verify the warning.

JORI: You want me to re-wet it?

VELA: Only for testing.

Jori dips the mop into the bucket and squeezes it. One drop falls. Vela points to it.

VELA: The hazard is active.

Fip places a towel over the drop. Vela writes "hazard obscured." Nara sets the tray down and picks up the warning sign.

NARA: Does this sign need inspection?

VELA: Yes. It must be visible and stable.

Nara places it on the dry floor. It stands straight. Vela checks the box. Jori removes the other two signs, but Vela stops him.

VELA: We need to inspect the whole route.

Fip asks the visitors to use the other hall. Nara moves the tray there. Jori mops the remaining floor. Vela follows behind, marking each damp patch on the form.

VELA: The floor is wet.

JORI: The mop is cleaning it.

VELA: The report does not say why.

Nara returns with an empty tray, still damp from the cup rings, and asks whether she can pass through. Vela points to the route on her form. It ends at the square Jori has just mopped. Nara steps around it, and one drop from the tray lands on the floor. Vela checks the hazard box again. Jori hands Nara a napkin.

NARA: Is the napkin approved?

VELA: It is dry.

JORI: For now.

Fip takes the clipboard and writes "cleaning in progress." Vela rereads the note, then checks the corrective-action box.

[JORI WRINGS THE MOP AND DRIES THE PASSAGE. VELA PUTS A WET-FLOOR SIGN ON THE CLIPBOARD TO KEEP IT FROM SLIDING. NARA CARRIES THE TRAY THROUGH THE DRY DOORWAY. VELA TRIES TO WRITE A FINAL FINDING BUT CANNOT GET HER FORM OUT FROM UNDER THE SIGN.]
