# The Stapler's Retirement Party

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace retirement ceremonies that turn office equipment into sentimental colleagues*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Teli, a lemur office manager; Brin, a capuchin assistant; Moss, a gibbon custodian; Kora, a macaque presenter.

INT. OFFICE BREAK ROOM - AFTERNOON

[A STAPLER SITS ON A CAKE PEDESTAL. A CARD READS THANK YOU FOR 20 YEARS OF SERVICE. TELI HOLDS A SPEECH.]

TELI: We are gathered to honor the stapler.

BRIN: It still works.

TELI: It has earned its rest.

Moss places a new stapler beside it. The old one has a faded label reading "Property of Mail Room." Kora says the retirement plaque must use its official name.

KORA: What was its name?

MOSS: Stapler.

Teli reads the speech. She says the stapler held the office together through quarterly reports and countless forms. Brin asks how many staples remain inside. Teli doesn't know.

BRIN: We should check before retiring it.

TELI: The ceremony is already underway.

Moss presses the stapler. It makes a hollow click. The cake shifts on the pedestal. Kora catches it and asks whether the stapler has a final staple.

Brin opens the stapler. The magazine is empty.

BRIN: It is out of staples.

Teli says that is why they are retiring it. Moss inserts a strip of staples. The old stapler now works. Kora asks if the ceremony should be paused.

TELI: It has already been thanked.

Brin offers the new stapler. It is too large for the desk drawer. Moss tries to fit it inside. The drawer catches on the handle and cannot close.

MOSS: The old one fit.

TELI: We need to make room for the future.

Kora moves the old stapler to a display shelf. The shelf is full of retired pens, an old hole punch, and a ruler with its markings worn off. There is no space.

BRIN: We can put it in the mail room.

Teli says it no longer belongs there. Moss checks the label. The office has been using the mail room's stapler all along.

KORA: The plaque should say "loaned from Mail Room."

TELI: That is less sentimental.

MOSS: It is accurate.

The mail room clerk enters to retrieve the stapler. Teli hands it over. Brin tries the new stapler on the card. It staples the plaque to the speech.

TELI: It has joined the ceremony.

Kora reads the retirement inscription from the stapler's label.

KORA: "Property of Mail Room."

Moss carries the old stapler out. The drawer closes around the new one. Teli cuts the cake, but the knife sticks to the pedestal's paper decoration.

Brin removes the old label from the drawer and puts it on the new stapler. Teli objects that the label belongs to the room. Moss has already taken the old stapler away. Brin staples the label to the speech so it can be delivered with the farewell card.

TELI: We have transferred its name.

BRIN: We have transferred its paperwork.

[BRIN USES THE NEW STAPLER TO FASTEN THE SPEECH TO THE CAKE BOX. THE RETIREMENT CARD GOES TO THE MAIL ROOM, WHERE THE OLD STAPLER IMMEDIATELY GETS A RETURN-TO-WORK FORM.]
