# The Heist Crew Buys a Museum Membership

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: heist planning scenes whose target vault contains the institution's public membership supplies*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: PLANNER VELA, a macaque; LOOKOUT PIP, a capuchin; CURATOR NARA, a gibbon; MEMBERSHIP CLERK RIK, an orangutan.

[A museum reception desk. Vela unrolls a carefully drawn plan showing a large rectangle marked VAULT. Pip wears sunglasses indoors. Rik sits beside a membership leaflet rack. Nara carries a box of postcards.]

VELA: We enter at twelve, bypass the guard and reach the treasure.

RIK: We open at ten. The gallery ticket is five coins.

PIP: A toll before the vault.

NARA: Which vault?

[Vela points discreetly to a steel cupboard behind reception. Nara looks at it, then at the postcards in her hands.]

NARA: That is where we store membership badges.

ACT ONE

[Vela checks a second page of the plan. A handwritten note reads GOLD MEMBERSHIP.]

VELA: We were told it contained gold.

RIK: The badges are gold-colored.

PIP: Colored with what?

RIK: Ink. They come from the printer across the street.

[Vela asks to see one. Rik lifts a sample from the desk: a small card featuring a golden fern. Pip tilts his sunglasses to inspect it.]

PIP: It appears light enough to transport.

RIK: It goes in a wallet.

[Nara opens the steel cupboard with the key attached to her ordinary key ring. Vela watches every turn as if learning an elaborate code. She puts the postcards on the upper shelf, leaving the packets of badges below.]

VELA: The vault has two levels.

NARA: The upper level holds postcards. The lower one holds stationery.

PIP: A decoy chamber.

NARA: A cupboard.

[Rik offers Vela the membership leaflet. It lists gallery visits, a small newsletter and one guest ticket. Vela reads the benefits to Pip.]

VELA: If one of us joins, the other enters under cover.

RIK: Under the guest ticket.

PIP: Are we allowed to remain together?

RIK: Yes. The paintings do not separate visitors.

[Vela asks whether the newsletter includes a floor plan. Nara says it includes a gallery map and a note about the fern collection. Pip takes off his sunglasses completely.]

PIP: I do like ferns.

VELA: We have never included botany in an operation.

PIP: We have never had an operation with a guest ticket.

ACT TWO

[Vela buys one membership, signing with her actual name. Rik gives her a badge and Pip the guest ticket. Nara closes the cupboard after placing the leaflet stock inside.]

VELA: Shall we still approach from the blind side?

NARA: Approach the gallery door. I will show you the new fern painting.

[Pip folds the heist plan so its blank reverse faces outward. He asks Rik for a pencil and writes the exhibition title on it. Vela studies her new badge.]

VELA: They let us leave with gold.

RIK: With gold-colored ink.

[Nara leads them toward the gallery. Pip glances back at reception and checks the newsletter delivery date. Vela holds her new badge carefully; Pip pockets his guest-ticket stub.]

PIP: We should return for the next exhibition.

VELA: Same crew, same entrance.

RIK: The membership lasts the year. You can use the entrance as often as that leaflet permits.

FINAL IMAGE: Vela carries her meticulous vault plan into the public gallery while Pip circles the fern lecture on the members' calendar.
