# The Lost Property Needs a Witness

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: bureaucratic lost-property claims and proof-of-ownership rules*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Sella, a capuchin clerk; Varo, a macaque claimant; Ibis, a gibbon witness; Mott, a slow loris supervisor.

INT. LOST PROPERTY OFFICE - AFTERNOON

[SHELVES HOLD A SCARF, A WOODEN SPOON, A SINGLE SHOE AND A VERY LARGE FEATHER. SELLA PUTS A BLUE TICKET ON THE COUNTER.]

SELLA: What have you lost?

VARO: A blue ticket.

SELLA: What does it look like?

VARO: Like that one.

Sella points to the ticket on the counter. Varo reaches for it. She covers it with a glass dome.

SELLA: You have not proved it's yours.

VARO: I just described it.

SELLA: You described a blue ticket. We have six.

Varo looks over the counter. All six are identical. Ibis enters carrying a wooden spoon.

IBIS: I found this by the fountain.

SELLA: Do you know who owns it?

IBIS: It was stirring a cup when I saw it.

SELLA: That's not a name.

MOTT: Our policy requires two witnesses for an item with no identifying mark.

IBIS: I am the finder.

MOTT: A finder cannot witness their own discovery.

IBIS: I saw myself find it.

Mott hands Ibis a form. It asks the witness to describe the finder's expression. Ibis writes "surprised."

VARO: Can we discuss my ticket?

SELLA: Yes. What is printed on the back?

VARO: Nothing.

SELLA: That's what all six say.

Varo remembers he had drawn a tiny sun on one corner. Sella turns each ticket over. One has a sun. Varo reaches for it. She covers it too.

SELLA: Anyone could have drawn that.

VARO: I drew it while waiting for the bus.

IBIS: I saw him draw it.

MOTT: Were you looking at the ticket?

IBIS: I was looking for the bus.

MOTT: Then you are a witness to the bus, not the drawing.

Ibis tries to help. He asks Varo what he did after drawing the sun.

VARO: Folded the ticket into a little boat.

Sella finds a crease on the right corner. Varo names the crease. She compares it to the ticket, but the fold is on the left.

VARO: I made the boat backwards.

SELLA: That is a detail only the owner would know.

She lifts the glass dome. Varo takes the ticket and unfolds it. It is a receipt for the lost-property office.

VARO: This says I paid a fee to search for my lost ticket.

SELLA: You still have the receipt.

VARO: I thought it was the ticket.

MOTT: Then your ticket is still missing.

Ibis puts the spoon on the counter. He claims it as evidence that he found something. Sella takes it and stamps it with an inventory tag.

SELLA: Now it belongs to the office.

IBIS: It was a spoon before you tagged it.

MOTT: Ownership changed at the counter.

Varo finds his actual blue ticket tucked behind his ear. The office's receipt was in his other hand. Sella opens the claim form, but Mott stamps it FOUND and closes the book.

VARO: Can I claim the spoon?

SELLA: Do you have a witness?

[IBIS RAISES HIS HAND. MOTT GIVES HIM A WITNESS FORM, AND SELLA PLACES THE SPOON ON A SHELF MARKED ITEMS WITH TWO OWNERS.]
