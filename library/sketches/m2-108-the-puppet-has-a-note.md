# The Puppet Has a Note

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: children puppet shows and performers losing control of a character*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: PUPPETEER LEO, a macaque; PUPPET MOSS, a sock puppet with button eyes; DIRECTOR RUE, a gibbon; and a small audience.

[A colorful puppet stage. Leo lifts Moss from behind the curtain.] 

LEO: Hello, children. Moss the squirrel has a question.

MOSS: Why is there a note pinned to my tail?

RUE: The script says you do not know about the note.

MOSS: I can feel it.

LEO: The note is part of the story.

MOSS: I would like to read it.

RUE: Not until act two.

MOSS: I have hands.

LEO: You have felt paws.

MOSS: I can point with them.

[THE PUPPET POINTS TO THE NOTE.] 

RUE: Keep the story moving. The note is a mystery.

MOSS: It says "Do not forget the acorn."

LEO: You were not supposed to know that yet.

MOSS: It is pinned to my tail.

RUE: Can the puppet ask the audience for help?

LEO: Children, have you seen the acorn?

AUDIENCE: Yes!

MOSS: Where is it?

AUDIENCE: Behind you!

[MOSS TURNS AROUND. LEO'S HAND KNOCKS A PROP ACORN OFF THE TABLE.] 

RUE: The acorn has rolled away.

MOSS: The note told me not to forget it.

LEO: I will find it after the show.

MOSS: The children saw it roll under the curtain.

RUE: The story needs an ending.

MOSS: I can ask the children to point.

[THE AUDIENCE POINTS. LEO REACHES UNDER THE CURTAIN AND FINDS THE ACORN.] 

LEO: Moss has found the acorn.

MOSS: I was holding the note.

RUE: We can say the note helped.

MOSS: The note was pinned to me. I helped.

RUE: We need one final audience question before the show ends.

LEO: Moss, ask the children where the acorn belongs.

MOSS: Where does an acorn belong?

AUDIENCE: In the tree!

MOSS: It can grow into a tree, but first it needs soil.

RUE: Can the children show us where there is soft ground?

[THE AUDIENCE POINTS TO A PROP PATCH OF SOIL.] 

LEO: Moss can place the acorn there.

MOSS: I can also keep one acorn for later.

RUE: Is that part of the script?

MOSS: It is part of being a squirrel.

[THE PUPPET HIDES THE ACORN IN A FELT POCKET.] 

LEO: We will need to find it before the next show.

MOSS: I know where it is.

RUE: You will have to remember.

MOSS: The note will remind me.

LEO: The note is on the script now.

RUE: Good. The performance has one planted acorn and one saved acorn.

MOSS: And one puppet who knows where both are.

[LEO REMOVES THE NOTE AND HANDS IT TO RUE.] 

RUE: I will pin it to the script next time.

MOSS: Then the script can remember the acorn.
