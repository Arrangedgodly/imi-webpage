# The Friendly Smash

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Professional wrestling promos and theatrical rivalries*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Ring announcer Kett, a mandrill; Brutus, a gorilla wrestler; Flip, a squirrel monkey wrestler; referee Lala, a gibbon.

[STAGE: A SMALL WRESTLING RING MADE OF POOL NOODLES. A SPOTLIGHT CIRCLES TOO FAST.] 

KETT: Tonight, two rivals enter the ring. Only one will leave with the Golden Fig Leaf.

BRUTUS: I will lift Flip over my head.

FLIP: You said you had a sore shoulder.

BRUTUS: I have healed dramatically.

LALA: No lifting above the second rope.

BRUTUS: How high is the second rope?

LALA: Lower than your head.

KETT: The audience is ready for conflict.

FLIP: The audience is my aunt.

KETT: She is ready for conflict.

BRUTUS: Flip, prepare to meet the mat.

FLIP: The mat is a pool noodle.

BRUTUS: Then prepare to meet the noodle.

[BRUTUS FLEXES. THE SPOTLIGHT STOPS ON HIS ELBOW.] 

KETT: The champion is displaying immense strength.

LALA: The spotlight is on his elbow.

BRUTUS: My elbow is ready.

FLIP: My plan is to duck.

KETT: The challenger unveils a cunning strategy.

FLIP: I am short.

BRUTUS: I have a counter move.

LALA: It is called "do not fall."

BRUTUS: I know the move.

[BELL. THE TWO WRESTLERS CIRCLE. THEIR TAILS KNOT TOGETHER.] 

FLIP: We are attached.

BRUTUS: That is my grip.

FLIP: Your tail is tied to mine.

LALA: No pulling.

KETT: The crowd gasps at the rare double-lock.

FLIP: Auntie, can you untie us?

KETT: The crowd is cheering.

LALA: The crowd is one person eating popcorn.

BRUTUS: I can lift the knot.

FLIP: Don't. My tail is in the knot.

LALA: Stay still. I will loosen it.

KETT: A dramatic pause before the next move.

LALA: It is a safety check.

[THE ROPE POPS FREE. BOTH WRESTLERS FALL INTO THE SAME CORNER.] 

BRUTUS: We are tied again.

FLIP: The ring has a loose rope.

LALA: That's a pool noodle.

BRUTUS: We need a different contest.

FLIP: Who can fold the mat?

KETT: The audience loves a surprise challenge.

LALA: The audience is asking for a refund.

BRUTUS: I can fold it with one hand.

FLIP: I can fold it with one tail.

[THEY FOLD THE NOODLE MAT INTO A NEAT SQUARE.]

KETT: We have a tie.

LALA: You have cleaned up the ring.

BRUTUS: We can split the Golden Fig Leaf.

FLIP: It is a paper leaf.

KETT: The prize is a handshake.

LALA: Please do not squeeze.

BRUTUS: I will shake gently.

FLIP: My aunt approves.

KETT: The rivals are now a tag team.

LALA: The event is over.

BRUTUS: Can we have a rematch?

FLIP: After my aunt finishes the popcorn.

KETT: The pool noodles will be available for refreshments.

LALA: They are not edible.

[THE SPOTLIGHT FINDS THE EMPTY RING. THE BELL DINGS.]
