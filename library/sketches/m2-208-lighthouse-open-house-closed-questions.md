# Lighthouse Open House, Closed to Questions

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: heritage tours that protect a landmark's mystique by refusing to explain how it works*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Guide Sena, a gibbon; Engineer Boro, a macaque; Visitor Lili, a lemur; and Curator Venn, a capuchin.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE - DAY

Visitors climb the spiral stairs. Sena waits at the top beside a lamp. A sign reads "ASK ABOUT THE HISTORY." Lili raises her hand.

LILI: How does the light turn?

SENA: It turns in a very historic way.

BORO: There is a motor under the platform.

VENN: We prefer to say the mechanism is mysterious.

BORO: It is a motor. It needs oil twice a year.

SENA: The original lens was installed in 1891.

LILI: Is this the original lens?

VENN: It is the original shape.

BORO: It is a replacement lens.

VENN: The shape carries the spirit of the original lens.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE LANTERN ROOM - LATER

Boro opens a maintenance hatch. Venn puts a rope barrier around it. Sena points to a painting of an old keeper.

SENA: This painting shows the keeper polishing the glass.

LILI: Where does the motor get power?

BORO: The cable runs down the center column.

VENN: Please do not follow the cable. It is a historic route.

LILI: Could the motor stop?

BORO: If the power goes out, the backup lamp comes on.

LILI: Is the backup lamp older than the motor?

BORO: No. It is a battery lantern.

VENN: We call it the keeper's flame.

BORO: We call it a battery lantern.

[VENN HOLDS UP THE LANTERN. A STICKER ON ITS HANDLE READS "PROPERTY OF MAINTENANCE."]

LILI: Did the keeper use a sticker?

VENN: The sticker is from the present day.

VENN: We have never had to use it during a tour.

[THE MAIN LIGHT PAUSES. THE BACKUP LAMP CLICKS ON.]

SENA: A rare display of our emergency system.

BORO: The breaker tripped. I can reset it.

VENN: Is the breaker original?

BORO: It was installed last winter.

VENN: Then reset it with reverence.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE BASE - MINUTES LATER

Boro resets the breaker. The lamp rotates again. Venn hands Lili a brochure labeled "THE MYSTERY OF THE TURNING LIGHT."

LILI: It has a diagram of the motor.

VENN: That diagram is an artist's impression.

BORO: It is a wiring diagram.

SENA: We do explain some things.

LILI: The sign said ask about the history.

VENN: The history is clear. The exact present is less marketable.

[A BELL SOUNDS. THE TOUR HAS REACHED ITS END.]

BORO: The lamp is turning, the backup is off, and the breaker is stable.

VENN: Could you say that with more mystery?

BORO: The beam completes its circle on schedule.

LILI: That is less mysterious and more useful.

[SENA POINTS TO THE EXIT. THE GROUP DESCENDS. VENN STAYS BESIDE THE MAINTENANCE HATCH, GUARDING THE VERY ORDINARY MOTOR.]
