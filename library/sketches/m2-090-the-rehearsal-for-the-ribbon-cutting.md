# The Rehearsal for the Ribbon Cutting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: civic ribbon-cutting ceremonies and ceremonial overplanning*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: MAYOR PELL, a macaque; CEREMONY DIRECTOR SIV, a gibbon; GROUNDSKEEPER NIM, a capuchin; and the ribbon.

[A SHORT GARDEN PATH ENDS AT A NEW BENCH. A RIBBON BLOCKS THE BENCH. Siv holds oversized scissors.] 

SIV: The ceremony begins when the mayor approaches from the west, pauses at the ribbon, and cuts on my signal.

PELL: What if I approach from the east?

SIV: That is not in the plan.

NIM: The east side is the path people use to reach the bench.

SIV: We will open that path after the ceremony.

NIM: The path is already open.

PELL: We can use it now.

SIV: We need a clear entrance for the photograph.

[PELL WALKS TO THE WEST. THE RIBBON IS TIED TO A LOW BRANCH.]

PELL: I cannot reach the ribbon without ducking under the branch.

SIV: The branch is not part of the ceremony.

NIM: It is part of the garden.

SIV: We can raise the ribbon.

[NIM RAISES ONE END. THE OTHER END SLIDES OFF THE BENCH AND FALLS.] 

PELL: The ribbon has opened the bench.

SIV: The ribbon must remain intact until I give the signal.

NIM: It is on the ground.

SIV: We will attach it higher.

NIM: The branch is too low. Tie it to the posts.

[THEY TIE THE RIBBON TO TWO POSTS. IT NOW BLOCKS THE WHOLE PATH.] 

PELL: That is a very official barrier.

NIM: It prevents anyone from using the bench.

PELL: The bench was built for people to sit on.

SIV: We need the photograph first.

PELL: Let's rehearse the photograph. Everyone sit on the bench.

SIV: The ribbon is in front of it.

NIM: We can cut it now.

SIV: On the signal.

PELL: Give the signal.

[SIV HOLDS UP THE SCISSORS. THEY ARE TOO LARGE TO CUT THE NARROW RIBBON.] 

SIV: They are ceremonial scissors.

NIM: The garden shears are on the shelf.

PELL: Use the shears. We can untie the ribbon after.

PELL: The photographer is ready. The mayor should stand beside the bench, scissors raised, while the director holds the ribbon ends.

SIV: I am the director, and the ribbon is tied to the posts.

NIM: It slipped loose, but it is still across the path.

SIV: We need to rehearse one clean cut.

PELL: Then give the signal and cut it once.

SIV: The photograph should show the bench, not the ribbon.

NIM: Once we cut it, people can use the bench.

PELL: That is the point of opening it.

SIV: I will tell the photographer to be ready when the ribbon falls.

[THEY CUT IT. THE PATH OPENS.] 

SIV: The ceremony has concluded.

PELL: The bench is ready.

NIM: The ribbon is on the ground again.

PELL: This time it is not blocking the path.

SIV: Can we still take a photograph?

PELL: Take one of the bench being used.

[THEY SIT. SIV STANDS BEHIND THEM HOLDING THE OVERSIZED SCISSORS.]
