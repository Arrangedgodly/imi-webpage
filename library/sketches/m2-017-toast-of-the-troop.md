# Toast of the Troop

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Wedding toasts and sentimental reception speeches*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Emcee Didi, a mandrill; bride Kiri, a bonobo; groom Ollo, a gibbon; best friend Fen, a capuchin; timer keeper Pip, a marmoset.

[STAGE: RECEPTION TABLE. A MICROPHONE, A WEDDING CAKE, AND A VISIBLE KITCHEN TIMER.] 

DIDI: Welcome, friends. Each toast gets one minute. Pip will ring the bell when time is up.

FEN: I have written a short toast.

PIP: It is six pages.

FEN: The font is large.

KIRI: We know you have stories.

OLLO: Please choose the one where I fall out of the tree.

FEN: That was a private moment.

KIRI: It happened at our first picnic.

FEN: You were both there.

OLLO: I was under the tree.

DIDI: Fen, begin when you're ready.

FEN: I first met Ollo on the north path. He was upside down, holding a pie with his feet.

OLLO: The plate had broken.

FEN: He offered me the pie.

OLLO: I had already eaten the crust.

KIRI: That sounds like him.

FEN: I knew then he was generous.

PIP: Thirty seconds.

FEN: I have not reached the story about Kiri.

DIDI: You may toast both of them.

FEN: Kiri once climbed a branch to rescue my hat.

KIRI: Your hat was on your head.

FEN: It was my other hat.

OLLO: The one you lost in the pond.

FEN: That was also a private moment.

[TIMER TICKS LOUDLY.] 

PIP: Ten seconds.

FEN: Love is like a branch.

DIDI: Careful.

FEN: It bends when two people share the shade.

KIRI: That is lovely.

OLLO: And if it breaks?

FEN: You find another branch.

PIP: Time.

FEN: I was going to say, "May you find a strong one."

DIDI: That is a toast.

OLLO: Thank you, Fen.

KIRI: I liked the part about the pie.

FEN: I can tell the other stories at dessert.

PIP: Dessert is in four minutes.

DIDI: The next speaker is Kiri's aunt.

AUNT RAVA: I have one sentence.

PIP: That is allowed.

AUNT RAVA: May you always have a place to land.

OLLO: I'll drink to that.

KIRI: Me too.

DIDI: The timer is still running.

PIP: I forgot to stop it.

FEN: Then may every speech end before the cake is cut.

OLLO: That is a useful wish.

DIDI: Raise your cups. To Kiri and Ollo, who found a branch and made room for everyone.

FEN: I would like to add one more memory.

PIP: The timer has ended.

FEN: This one is only twelve seconds.

KIRI: Go ahead.

FEN: Ollo once held the branch while I climbed down.

OLLO: It was not a branch. It was the laundry pole.

FEN: I was carrying a wet blanket.

DIDI: That is a good memory.

PIP: The timer agrees.

KIRI: We can toast to laundry poles too.

OLLO: They have held us up.

DIDI: That is the final toast.

[CUPS CLINK. THE TIMER DINGS UNDER THE APPLAUSE.]
