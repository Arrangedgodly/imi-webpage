# The Ice Cream Truck Cannot Keep a Secret

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: covert stakeouts and spycraft undermined by a vehicle whose cheerful jingle announces its position*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: AGENT, a macaque; DRIVER, a baboon; DISPATCHER, an orangutan; SUSPECT, a gibbon; CHILD, a capuchin.

[A quiet street. The agent hides behind an ice cream truck. The driver opens the service window. A cheerful melody plays.]

AGENT: We are on a stakeout.

DRIVER: I am selling cones.

DISPATCHER: Keep your position and stay unnoticed.

[The truck's jingle plays again. The suspect looks toward the truck.]

SUSPECT: I wonder if there is chocolate.

ACT ONE

[The agent asks the driver to turn off the jingle. The driver switches it off, but the truck's bell rings whenever the freezer door opens. The suspect walks closer.]

DRIVER: The bell is mechanical.

AGENT: The mission is also mechanical.

[The dispatcher whispers directions through an earpiece. The agent moves behind a tree. The truck rolls a few inches downhill and the jingle starts again.]

DISPATCHER: Why are you moving?

AGENT: I am not.

[The driver blocks the truck with a wheel chock. The suspect reaches the window and asks for a vanilla cone. The driver serves one. The agent tries to hand over a coded note, but it sticks to the cone wrapper.]

SUSPECT: Is this a receipt?

AGENT: It is not.

DRIVER: It is a napkin.

[The suspect unfolds the note and reads the code aloud. The agent asks for it back. The suspect thinks it is a flavor menu. The child arrives and asks for sprinkles. The driver serves the child. The agent has to move again to avoid being seen, but each hiding place is beside the truck's painted menu.]

CHILD: You are standing by the pistachio.

AGENT: I like pistachio.

[The dispatcher announces the suspect's location. The suspect is standing at the truck window, finishing the cone. The agent decides to give up the stakeout and asks a direct question. The suspect admits they were waiting for a delivery and gives the agent a package.]

DISPATCHER: You have secured the package.

AGENT: We have also sold two cones.

[The agent tries to make one final call to dispatch. The truck's bell rings whenever the driver opens the window to serve another customer. The dispatcher asks whether the ringing is a signal. The agent says no. The driver shuts the window, and the bell rings again as the latch settles.]

DISPATCHER: Signal received.

AGENT: That is the latch.

ACT TWO

[The driver switches off the jingle and locks the freezer. The agent takes the package to the unmarked car. The suspect pays for the cone. The child asks the agent for a secret flavor. The agent checks the package and says it is sealed.]

DRIVER: The truck can keep a secret if it stops playing.

[The bell rings as the driver closes the window.]

FINAL IMAGE: The agent hides the package under a napkin while the truck's tune follows the suspect down the block.
