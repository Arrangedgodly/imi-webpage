# The Secret Ingredient Reveal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking shows that disguise common ingredients as shocking discoveries*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: chef Odo, a baboon; host Nara, a macaque; judge Piri, a gibbon; camera operator Miri, a capuchin.

INT. KITCHEN SET - DAY

Nara presents a covered bowl.

NARA: Our chef has added one surprising ingredient.

Odo uncovers it. The bowl contains salt.

PIRI: Salt?

ODO: A pinch.

Nara says the ingredient changes everything. Odo puts a pinch into the soup. He holds up the spoon for a camera close-up; the frame catches only the salt shaker. Piri tastes it.

PIRI: It tastes like soup.

Miri zooms in on the salt shaker. Nara asks Odo to reveal why he chose it. Odo says the soup was bland. Nara asks if there was a deeper reason.

ODO: It needed salt.

Nara interviews him again with dramatic lighting. Odo repeats the answer. Piri asks if they can taste the soup before and after. Miri pours two small cups. One is unsalted; one has salt. The salted soup tastes better.

Nara calls it a transformation. Odo says the recipe card lists salt. Piri checks the card. It says "salt to taste." Nara says the card is from the previous episode and may be unreliable.

Odo adds a tiny bit more salt. The soup is too salty. He stirs in water to balance it. Nara calls this a reversal. Piri asks if they can serve it. Odo tastes again and says it is fine.

The crew asks Odo to describe the secret. He says he tasted the food and adjusted it. Nara says that is not dramatic enough. Odo says it is cooking.

Piri asks to taste the soup before and after the first pinch. She samples both cups and says the difference is real but not mysterious. Nara explains that salt brings out flavor. Odo asks if that makes it the secret. Piri says knowing when to stop is the cook's job. Miri labels the cups BEFORE and AFTER, then swaps the cards for a dramatic reveal. Odo points out that the camera now shows the salted sample under BEFORE. Miri switches them back. The soup needs no further correction. Piri writes "season gradually" on the recipe card while Odo puts the shaker on the shelf.

Nara asks Miri to retake the close-up with the salt shaker in frame. Piri says the ingredient should not be hidden. Odo moves the shaker beside the pot, then labels it "secret." Nara removes the label. Odo asks if the audience will lose interest without a reveal. Piri says they can see him cook. Nara tastes one more spoonful and confirms the broth is balanced. Odo writes the amount on the recipe card so another cook can repeat it.

TAG

Nara takes a pinch of salt.

NARA: I have discovered the ingredient.

PIRI: Put it back.
