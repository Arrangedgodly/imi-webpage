# The Train Conductor Hosts a Farewell Party

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sentimental farewell speeches and rail announcements that keep delaying a departing train*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: CONDUCTOR, a baboon; PASSENGER, an orangutan; ANNOUNCER, a gibbon; PORTER, a macaque; MUSICIAN, a capuchin.

[A station platform. A train waits with doors open. The conductor stands beside a small cake and a stack of paper cups. The passenger holds a suitcase.]

CONDUCTOR: We will say goodbye before departure.

PASSENGER: My train leaves in two minutes.

ANNOUNCER: The train is now delayed for a short farewell.

[The musician starts a slow tune. The porter rolls the cake cart toward the platform edge; the conductor stops it.]

PORTER: The cake is not ticketed.

ACT ONE

[The conductor begins a speech about the passenger's years in the town. The passenger tries to board. The conductor hands them a paper cup.]

CONDUCTOR: One toast.

PASSENGER: I would like one seat.

[The announcer checks the timetable. The train's departure is one minute away. The musician shortens the tune to four notes. The conductor says the speech still needs a final paragraph.]

PORTER: The train needs a final passenger.

[The conductor reads the paragraph while walking backwards. The porter pushes the cart after them. The passenger follows with the suitcase, but the train door begins to beep.]

ANNOUNCER: Doors are closing.

CONDUCTOR: Hold them!

[The porter presses the platform button. The doors stop. The passenger places the suitcase inside, then returns to get the cake. The musician resumes the farewell tune.]

PASSENGER: The cake can stay here.

CONDUCTOR: It has your name on it.

[The porter checks the label. It says "For the station crew." The conductor reads it as a sign to keep the cake. The passenger explains the party is for everyone who helped with the move. The conductor offers the cake to the platform staff.]

ANNOUNCER: The train is ready to depart.

[The conductor asks for one photograph. The passenger and crew line up. The musician plays the tune again, but the passenger has already boarded and leans out from the open door. The porter places the cake on a luggage cart and wheels it away from the track.]

ACT TWO

[The conductor gives the passenger the train ticket instead of another cup. The passenger waves from inside. The announcer makes one final departure call. The train leaves on the corrected timetable. The crew cuts the cake after the platform clears.]

CONDUCTOR: That was a proper farewell.

PORTER: And the cake traveled safely.

[The passenger sits by the window and holds up the paper cup. The conductor waves from the platform. The announcer waits until the last carriage passes before restarting the farewell music. The porter sets out plates; everyone receives a slice after the train clears the signal.]

MUSICIAN: We have finished the song.

CONDUCTOR: The timetable agrees.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty platform holds one paper cup while the train's last window carries the passenger's wave away.
