# The Exit Survey Is Part of the Haunt

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: haunted-house attractions that keep the audience inside to collect feedback after the scare is over*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Nara, a gibbon actor; Timo, a capuchin visitor; Vel, a macaque survey taker; Rell, a lemur manager.

INT. HAUNTED HOUSE EXIT CORRIDOR - NIGHT

[A FAKE COBWEB HANGS ABOVE THE EXIT. A TABLE HOLDS SURVEY CARDS. RELL STANDS BESIDE A SIGN: PLEASE RATE YOUR EXPERIENCE BEFORE LEAVING.]

TIMO: The show was good. Can I go?

RELL: First, tell us which scare worked best.

NARA: I was the ghost in room three.

TIMO: You were very convincing.

Nara reaches for a survey card. Her costume sleeve catches on the cobweb. She turns to untangle it, and the cobweb stretches across the exit sign.

VEL: On a scale of one to five, how startled were you?

TIMO: Four.

VEL: Could you make that a five?

TIMO: I was startled by the exit survey.

Rell says the survey is anonymous. He asks Timo to write his name at the top so the team can follow up. Timo leaves it blank. Vel asks which room he visited. Timo says all of them. Vel hands him a map to circle the rooms.

Nara stays tangled in the cobweb. She tries to pull free, but the paper survey catches on her sleeve and tears in half.

NARA: I may have added a new scare.

RELL: Please mark it on the form.

Timo circles "room three" because that is where the ghost appeared. Vel says the survey box asks for "most memorable moment." Timo circles "exit." Rell looks pleased.

RELL: The exit was the most memorable.

TIMO: I am still at the exit.

Vel gives him a pencil to rate the lighting. Timo points at the corridor. The emergency light is red. The shadow of Nara's sleeve looks like a hand reaching through the web.

TIMO: That is a good effect.

Nara waves to him from behind the web. Timo jumps. Vel asks whether the scare is worth five stars.

NARA: Please help me.

Rell tells Timo that the actors cannot break character before the exit. Timo pulls the web away from the sign. Nara steps free, and her ghost sleeve remains stuck. She holds it up like a severed arm.

TIMO: That's worth six.

VEL: The scale ends at five.

Rell asks the survey team to add a box for "extra arm." Timo uses the pencil to write it on the back of the card. The card drops into the suggestion box, which is shaped like a monster's mouth.

RELL: Thank you. Your feedback helps us improve.

TIMO: Does it help me leave?

Rell moves the survey table. The exit door is clear. Timo walks through. Nara follows, still holding the sleeve. Vel asks her to complete the staff version of the survey.

[NARA MARKS "EXIT" AS THE MOST MEMORABLE ROOM. THE MONSTER-MOUTH BOX SNAPS SHUT ON THE PENCIL.]
