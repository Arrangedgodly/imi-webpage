# The Typing Test Has a Tail

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: office productivity contests and typing benchmarks that ignore the way monkeys actually use a keyboard*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Supervisor Rena, a baboon; employee Kito, a macaque; trainer Lomi, a gibbon; and evaluator Vex, a capuchin.

[A COMPUTER SCREEN SHOWS A TYPING TEST. KITO SITS AT THE KEYBOARD.]

VEX: You have one minute to type the sentence exactly.

KITO: What is the sentence?

VEX: "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog."

KITO: We do not have foxes here.

RENA: It is a standard typing sentence.

[KITO STARTS. HIS TAIL HITS THE SPACE BAR.]

COMPUTER: "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog."

VEX: Your score is too low.

KITO: I typed it.

VEX: You typed it with extra spaces.

[LOMI WRAPS A TOWEL AROUND THE CHAIR BACK.]

LOMI: We can keep his tail away from the keys.

KITO: My tail is part of my typing posture.

RENA: Use the ergonomic chair.

[THE CHAIR HAS A SLOT FOR A TAIL. KITO SITS. HIS TAIL FITS, BUT THE CHAIR ROLLS BACK.]

VEX: The chair moved.

KITO: I did not.

[RENA LOCKS THE WHEELS. KITO TYPES AGAIN. THE TAIL HITS ENTER.]

COMPUTER: Test submitted.

VEX: You submitted before the sentence was complete.

KITO: The key was under my tail.

LOMI: We can reassign the enter key.

[LOMI MOVES THE KEYBOARD. KITO TYPES WITH TWO FINGERS. THE COMPUTER SHOWS A SLOWER SCORE.]

VEX: Your speed dropped.

KITO: My accuracy improved.

RENA: Which one does the evaluation measure?

VEX: Speed.

[THE COMPUTER PROMPTS THE FOX SENTENCE AGAIN. KITO TYPES. HIS TAIL HITS THE SHIFT KEY.]

COMPUTER: "THE QUICK BROWN FOX JUMPS OVER THE LAZY DOG."

VEX: Incorrect capitalization.

KITO: It is still the sentence.

RENA: The test should measure the job.

VEX: The test is standardized.

[THE TAIL HITS THE SPACE BAR AGAIN. THE SCREEN ADDS A DOUBLE SPACE. VEX MARKS IT WRONG.]

KITO: Can I type a sentence about a monkey?

VEX: Not in the test.

VEX: The benchmark measures consistent input.

KITO: My input is consistent. The keyboard is small.

RENA: The job includes writing reports, not typing foxes.

VEX: We need a comparable measure.

LOMI: Compare the report Kito wrote with the one the evaluator wrote.

[VEX OPENS THE REPORTS. KITO'S HAS CLEAR NOTES. VEX'S IS A PAGE OF SCORE NUMBERS.]

RENA: Which one helps the next shift?

[VEX PUTS THE TEST SHEET FACE DOWN.]

[KITO OPENS A BLANK DOCUMENT AND TYPES: "MY TAIL CAN TYPE TOO."]

LOMI: It has a period.

RENA: It is the only test that shows his actual work.

[VEX LOOKS AT THE DOCUMENT.]

VEX: It is not standardized.

KITO: It is finished.

[RENA PRINTS IT. THE PRINTER SPITS OUT THE PAGE WITH A LONG TAIL-SHAPED INK MARK.]

VEX: The printer has also failed the test.

[THEY POST THE PAGE ABOVE THE COMPUTER.]
