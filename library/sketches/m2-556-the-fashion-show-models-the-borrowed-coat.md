# The Fashion Show Models the Borrowed Coat

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: runway shows, designer interviews, and fashion critics treating a borrowed coat as a collection*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: DESIGNER, a gibbon; MODEL, a macaque; ASSISTANT, a capuchin; CRITIC, an orangutan; RUNWAY HOST, a baboon.

[A backstage dressing area. A model wears a coat with a paper tag still tied to its sleeve. The designer checks the lineup.]

DESIGNER: This coat is the centerpiece.

MODEL: Whose is it?

ASSISTANT: The coat-check attendant lent it for the rehearsal.

CRITIC: The borrowed quality gives it a history.

[The runway host announces the collection. The model walks forward. The tag trails behind like a train.]

ACT ONE

RUNWAY HOST: A study in autumn texture.

MODEL: It says coat check, number forty-two.

[The designer tells the model to turn. The tag wraps around the model's wrist. The assistant tries to free it without taking the coat off. The critic calls it a sculptural accessory.]

CRITIC: The wrist is restrained by the garment.

MODEL: I am restrained by a paper tag.

[The assistant cuts the string. The tag drops, revealing the coat-check ticket still pinned underneath. The model turns back toward the rack.]

DESIGNER: The walk is not over.

MODEL: The coat belongs to someone in the audience.

[The host asks the audience to identify the owner. A baboon in the front row raises a hand but is holding the matching ticket. The designer asks whether the coat can remain for the last look. The owner says yes, but only if it is not altered.]

CRITIC: We can style it without changing it.

[The assistant adds a belt. The owner shakes their head. The belt is removed. The designer pins a flower to the lapel. The owner shakes their head again. The flower is removed. The critic suggests the coat's natural silhouette is the statement.]

OWNER: It is a warm coat.

[The model walks the runway again, holding the ticket in one hand. Halfway down, the coat-check owner stands and reaches out. The model brings the coat back to the rack rather than continuing to pose.]

ACT TWO

[The designer asks the owner to wear the coat for the final walk. The owner refuses the runway but puts it on to leave. The host calls it an audience collaboration. The critic records the garment's features: sleeves, buttons, pockets.]

MODEL: It also has a ticket.

[The owner returns the ticket to the coat-check attendant, who hangs the coat in the correct place.]

[The host announces a final walk. The owner steps out, turns around at the end of the runway, and asks for the coat back. The model immediately gives it over. The critic says the gesture represents fashion's relationship with ownership. The assistant points out that the owner simply needs to catch the last bus. The designer removes the loan tag and puts it back on the sleeve.]

OWNER: Thank you. I will bring it back next season.

MODEL: Please bring a coat that fits the model.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty runway holds a small label reading LOANED; the model walks off wearing the assistant's spare scarf.
