# The Royal Herald Interrupts His Own Announcement

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: formal royal proclamations whose pageantry outlasts the actual news*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: HERALD PAVA, a macaque; KING ODO, an orangutan; PAGE RIK, a capuchin; TOWN CRIER LUMA, a lemur.

[A throne room. Pava unrolls a scroll. It trails across the floor, around the throne and out through the door. Rik holds the far end.]

PAVA: Hear ye, hear ye, the royal announcement-

RIK: Which page?

ACT ONE

[Pava checks the scroll. The first page is a grocery list. He turns it over; the back says ROYAL ANNOUNCEMENT. Odo sits up straight.]

ODO: Proceed with majesty.

PAVA: I shall proceed with page one.

[He reads: flour, lantern oil, three new quills. The king nods.]

ODO: The realm needs flour.

[Luma waits with a trumpet. Pava reaches the second page, which is blank. Rik says the announcement must be on the third page; the scroll was wound backward. Pava tries to reverse it. The tail wraps around the throne leg.]

ACT TWO

[Rik unhooks the scroll. It rolls up and knocks the trumpet from Luma's hands. She catches it, but a single note sounds as the mouthpiece brushes her lips. Odo announces that the decree has begun. Pava asks what decree. Odo looks to Rik.]

RIK: The announcement about tomorrow's courtyard being closed for sweeping.

ODO: Important to the realm.

[Pava finds the correct page and clears his throat. Luma raises the trumpet. He says the first sentence: "The eastern courtyard will close at noon for sweeping." Luma blows the trumpet, drowning out the time, then sets it on its floor stand. Pava starts over. This time Rik holds up a card reading NO TRUMPET UNTIL THE PERIOD.]

PAVA: The courtyard closes at noon for sweeping.

[Odo asks who is sweeping. Pava checks the scroll. It says the Royal Sweepers' Guild. Rik checks the calendar and says they have moved the work to Thursday. Pava looks at the king.]

ODO: Revise the proclamation.

[Pava adds Thursday in the margin, but the page is too narrow. He writes it on the blank reverse. Luma suggests a short notice instead of unrolling the whole scroll. Pava is horrified.]

ACT THREE

[The herald takes a separate clean notice card from the table and writes: "Courtyard closed Thursday at noon for sweeping." He reads it once. Odo asks whether the kingdom has received its announcement. Luma says the town crier can carry it through the square. Rik offers to hold the notice while the scroll is rolled.]

Pava lifts the trumpet from its floor stand. Luma raises a finger to stop him. He sets it back. Odo asks for one ceremonial flourish. Pava unrolls the long scroll, which catches a draft from the open door and wraps around his robe. The separate notice card remains on the table, clear of the scroll.

RIK: Shall I unroll it?

PAVA: Announce the announcement.

[Pava and Rik roll the scroll together from opposite ends. They meet in the middle, then discover it is now a tube. Pava gives the separate notice card to Luma. She reads it clearly, then leaves to post it at the courtyard gate.]

ODO: The proclamation is complete.

PAVA: The scroll has yet to be stored.

FINAL IMAGE: The one-sentence notice hangs neatly on the courtyard gate while the herald and page carry the royal scroll like a very long baton.
