# The Synchronized Snack Break

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: office productivity videos that insist group eating can be optimized with calendars and an announcer*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Pavi, a gibbon timekeeper; Kito, a macaque supervisor; Rell, a capuchin presenter; and Sela, a lemur worker.

INT. WORKROOM - NOON

Rell stands beside a clock and a table with fruit. Kito hands out a schedule for a synchronized snack break.

RELL: We will all take one efficient bite at precisely twelve.

SELA: Why one bite?

KITO: It is the approved serving interval.

Pavi counts down. Everyone picks up fruit. At zero, Sela bites a pear, Kito bites a banana and Rell bites an apple. The sounds do not match.

RELL: Our timing is inconsistent.

PAVI: The fruit is different.

Kito sets a metronome. The group takes another bite on each beat. Sela's pear is harder than the banana, so she needs two beats. Rell's apple rolls off the table. Pavi stops the metronome to retrieve it. Kito marks the interruption on the schedule.

KITO: We lost seven seconds.

SELA: We saved the apple.

Rell suggests everyone use the same fruit. He passes out bananas. Sela peels hers quickly. Kito is still trying to peel from the stem. Rell's banana splits in half. Pavi announces a variance. Kito asks for a peel consultant.

Sela shows him how to open it from the other end. The banana comes free. Kito writes "peel from base" on the schedule. Rell says the group should now eat together. Pavi counts down again. A small monkey reaches for the fruit bowl before the count ends.

RELL: The snack is not yet active.

The small monkey takes a banana and leaves. Sela says the monkey was hungry. Kito marks the snack schedule "unauthorized participant." Pavi checks the clock; the break is almost over.

Rell attempts a final synchronized bite. Everyone raises their banana. The timer sounds. Kito bites. Sela bites. Pavi bites. Rell pauses to announce the result. His banana slips from his hand and lands in the empty bowl.

PAVI: That was an unscheduled deposit.

Kito says the break is complete. Sela points out that Rell has not eaten yet. The timer has already started the next work interval. Rell picks up the banana and takes a bite at his desk.

KITO: You are out of compliance.

RELL: I am eating at work.

Sela turns off the timer and shares the remaining fruit. Pavi tears the schedule into napkins. Kito checks the clock, then takes a bite without asking for a count.

KITO: That was efficient.

Rell marks the schedule as retired. Kito places the clock in a drawer, but the drawer sticks. Sela offers the fruit bowl as a doorstop, then decides to eat the last piece instead.

The drawer closes at last, without a timer.

Kito closes the drawer firmly without another beep.

PAVI: It took one bite.
