# The Garden Tour's Plants Have Name Tags

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: guided garden tours that turn ordinary plants into elaborate celebrity introductions*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Tour guide Tavi, a capuchin; botanist Nara, a macaque; visitor Venn, a gibbon; and gardener Odo, a baboon.

EXT. BOTANICAL GARDEN - DAY

Tavi leads a group to a potted fern. A name tag on the pot reads "FERNANDA - LOCAL LEGEND." Nara holds a clipboard.

TAVI: Please welcome Fernanda, who has stood in this corner for three years.

VENN: Does she speak?

NARA: It is a fern.

TAVI: She communicates through fronds.

ODO: The tag is covering two of them.

[Tavi moves the tag. The fern leans slightly as Odo waters it.]

TAVI: A graceful entrance.

NARA: That was the watering can.

EXT. GARDEN PATH - LATER

The group reaches a rose bush with a tag reading "THE RED CARPET." Venn takes a photo. Tavi points to a label with a long title.

TAVI: This is the rose's full name and professional biography.

VENN: It is printed in six-point type.

NARA: The plant is easier to see than the label.

[Odo turns the label. The back says "ROSE." ]

TAVI: That is the shorter stage name.

VENN: Is there a fan club?

ODO: The bees visit regularly.

[Tavi offers the visitors a small sign-up sheet. A bee lands on it.]

VENN: It has joined.

EXT. GREENHOUSE - AFTERNOON

Nara stops at a modest cactus. A tag reads "GUEST ARTIST - ONE DAY ONLY."

TAVI: This cactus is only visiting.

NARA: It has been here since the greenhouse opened.

ODO: The tag is for the touring sculpture beside it.

[The group turns. The sculpture is shaped like a cactus. Tavi accidentally introduces the sculpture as the plant.]

TAVI: This is a rare stone specimen.

VENN: It does not need water.

ODO: Neither does the sculpture.

EXT. GREENHOUSE - BEFORE THE EXIT

Venn asks for an autograph from the celebrity rose. Tavi finds a pencil and hands it to him.

VENN: Should I ask the gardener?

NARA: The rose cannot sign.

[Tavi props the tag against a leaf. The pencil rolls down the stem and lands in Odo's watering can.]

TAVI: The autograph session is over.

ODO: The can has collected a signature.

[Venn asks Odo to sign the tag. Odo writes WATERED TODAY and pins it to the pot.]
EXT. GARDEN EXIT - CLOSING

Tavi hands out a tour survey. It asks visitors to rate each plant's charisma. Venn marks every box.

NARA: The survey should ask if the garden was enjoyable.

TAVI: That is too broad.

ODO: The fern got a five.

VENN: I liked her entrance.

[Odo collects the name tags and stacks them neatly. Tavi places the Fernanda tag on the exit sign.]

TAVI: One final appearance.

NARA: The exit is not a plant.

[The tag covers the word EXIT. Visitors follow Fernanda's name tag and circle back to the fern.]
