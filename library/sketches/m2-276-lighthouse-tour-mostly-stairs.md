# The Lighthouse Tour Is Mostly Stairs

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: attraction tours that oversell ordinary access routes as exclusive experiences*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Tour guide Runi, a gibbon; keeper Tern, a baboon; visitor Miri, a capuchin; and visitor Odo, a macaque.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE ENTRY - DAY

Runi welcomes a small group. A sign reads 118 STEPS TO THE TOP. Tern holds a box of souvenir postcards.

RUNI: Welcome to the lighthouse experience.

MIRI: Is the light on?

TERN: The bulb is being repaired.

ODO: What do we see at the top?

RUNI: A panoramic view, plus the original lantern room.

[She opens the door to the stairwell. A second sign reads STAIRS ONLY.]

INT. LIGHTHOUSE STAIRWELL - MOMENTS LATER

The group climbs. Runi speaks at each landing. Tern hands out postcards.

RUNI: Here we have the mid-level landing, which offers a mid-level experience.

MIRI: What makes it special?

TERN: This is where you can purchase a postcard of the top.

ODO: Can I see the top on the card?

TERN: Yes, the postcard has a picture of it.

[The group climbs another flight. Tern's postcard box grows heavy; he sets it down on the stairs and blocks the passage.]

RUNI: Please keep the stairs clear.

TERN: I am offering an exclusive landing sale.

MIRI: The landing is not exclusive. We are all on it.

INT. LIGHTHOUSE - UPPER LANDING

The top door is locked. Runi tries the handle. Tern searches his pockets.

RUNI: The lantern room is temporarily closed.

ODO: The tour description says it is open.

TERN: The postcard shows it open.

[He holds up a postcard. Its tiny door is indeed open.]

MIRI: The picture is more accessible than the room.

[Tern unlocks the door, but it opens only a few inches because the souvenir display is behind it.]

TERN: We have a premium peek.

RUNI: Move the display.

INT. UPPER LANDING - BEFORE THE LANTERN ROOM

Runi sets out a small telescope for visitors. Tern sets a price tag beside it.

TERN: The telescope is a premium viewing accessory.

MIRI: It is pointed at the wall.

[Odo adjusts the telescope. It points down the stairs at the souvenir box.]

ODO: Now I can see the postcards.

TAVI: The tour has become a product demonstration.

[Tern turns the telescope upward. A bird lands on it, blocking the view.]

RUNI: That is a local visitor.

TERN: Does the bird need a ticket?
INT. LANTERN ROOM - FINAL STOP

The group enters. The lighthouse lens is covered with a cloth. Through the windows, they can see the harbor. Tern places postcards on a table; they blow away in the breeze.

ODO: The view is real.

MIRI: And the postcards are leaving.

[Everyone catches cards. Runi pulls back the cloth from the lens; the room brightens.]

RUNI: The tour is complete.

TERN: The souvenir shop is back at the bottom.

MIRI: We will buy a postcard when we get there.

[The group starts down. Tern picks up the box, now empty, and uses it as a seat at the landing.]
