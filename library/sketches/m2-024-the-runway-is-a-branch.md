# The Runway Is a Branch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Fashion runway shows and breathless backstage commentary*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Commentator Seli, a langur; designer Pru, a capuchin; model Boro, a gorilla; stage manager Tavi, a squirrel monkey.

[STAGE: A NARROW RUNWAY MADE FROM TWO PLANKS. CAMERAS FLASH. A CLOTHES RACK WOBBLES.] 

SELI: Welcome to the Branch Line show, where every look is inspired by a climb.

PRU: Our first design is the "Reach for the Canopy" jacket.

BORO: It has no sleeves.

PRU: The shoulders move freely.

TAVI: The left cuff is on the floor.

PRU: It is a detachable cuff.

BORO: It is attached to my foot.

SELI: A bold new way to wear a sleeve.

TAVI: Please step onto the runway.

BORO: The runway is a branch.

TAVI: It is two planks.

BORO: The planks move.

PRU: They move with the model.

SELI: The movement gives the garment life.

BORO: The garment is tugging me left.

TAVI: The rack is leaning right.

[PLANKS CREAK. A HAT FALLS OFF THE RACK.] 

SELI: The hat has made its entrance.

PRU: It is the "Nest After Rain" piece.

BORO: It is a bowl.

PRU: A sculptural bowl.

TAVI: It has a hole in the bottom.

PRU: That's ventilation.

SELI: The collection balances function and form.

BORO: The bowl is leaking.

PRU: It is not meant to hold water.

SELI: Then it is a dry-season hat.

TAVI: The model is approaching the turn.

BORO: The turn is smaller than my shoulders.

PRU: Pivot with the branch.

BORO: I am pivoting.

TAVI: The cuff is tangled on the rack.

SELI: The design is connecting with its environment.

BORO: The design is stopping traffic.

PRU: We can shorten the cuff.

BORO: You can make it a wristband.

SELI: A miniature statement.

TAVI: The next look is a cape made from leaf fabric.

BORO: It is caught on the spotlight.

PRU: It catches the light beautifully.

SELI: The audience is leaning forward.

TAVI: The audience is holding the runway steady.

BORO: Thank you. I can finish the walk now.

PRU: Keep the cape clear of the rack.

BORO: I can wear it as a scarf.

SELI: That changes the silhouette.

BORO: It also lets me turn around.

TAVI: The model has completed the runway.

PRU: We have a new design: clothes that let you climb.

SELI: The collection is practical, dramatic, and mostly off the floor.

BORO: That is all I ask of a jacket.

SELI: New look: the emergency jacket.
BORO: Why is it tied around my waist?
SELI: The buttons are still practicing.
BORO: They have had three fittings.
SELI: They are shy in public.
BORO: The belt is also a vine.
SELI: It is a sustainable belt.
BORO: It is growing back into the jacket.
SELI: That is the collection returning to its roots.
BORO: I cannot turn around.
SELI: Hold the pose. The camera loves a trapped silhouette.
BORO: The camera can untie me.
SELI: That is our next campaign.

[THE HAT ROLLS DOWN THE RUNWAY AND STOPS IN A CAMERA BAG.]
