# The Villain Reads the Relaxation Script

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: villain monologues whose theatrical menace is softened by an instructor's breathing prompts*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: VILLAIN VELA, a baboon; HERO PIP, a macaque; DIRECTOR NARA, an orangutan; MOVEMENT COACH RIK, a gibbon.

[A rehearsal hall. A cardboard control panel suggests a villain's lair. Pip sits freely in a chair used as the captured hero's mark. Vela enters holding a script. Nara watches from the floor. Rik has left a relaxation handout on top of the dialogue stack.]

VELA: Close your eyes and release the tension in your shoulders.

PIP: Is that the threat?

VELA: Breathe slowly into the space you have created.

NARA: You picked up Rik's warm-up sheet.

[Vela looks at the page header: FIVE MINUTES BEFORE REHEARSAL.]

ACT ONE

VELA: It has a formidable opening.

RIK: It is intended to help everyone move comfortably before we begin.

PIP: My shoulders were rather tense.

NARA: We already did the warm-up. The villain enters with his plan for the city.

[Rik takes the warm-up page and gives Vela the actual dialogue. Vela reads the first sentence quietly to himself, then delivers it with a sweeping arm.]

VELA: Before dawn, the entire city will know my name.

PIP: What is your name?

[Vela pauses. The script identifies him only as VILLAIN. Nara checks her copy and realizes the omission.]

NARA: We need to add that before the final run.

[She writes LORD VELA beside the character heading and gives the name to the performers.]

RIK: You could say it without tensing your jaw.

VELA: Menace requires a certain tension.

PIP: You asked me to release mine a moment ago.

[Vela moves to the cardboard console and lays the script on top. It bends slightly beneath the paper. He looks down as if betrayed by his invention.]

VELA: My control system is failing.

NARA: The shelf is for light props. Hold the script or put it on the real table.

[Vela puts it on the rehearsal table beside Rik's warm-up sheet. Rik separates the two into marked folders: DIALOGUE and MOVEMENT. Vela takes only the dialogue back to his entrance.]

NARA: Now the name, the plan and the hero's answer.

[Vela reads from the dialogue, gives his newly agreed name, and announces his scheme. Pip stands from the chair on his cue and refuses to help. Vela holds a dramatic silence.]

RIK: Keep breathing through the pause.

VELA: The pause is where the terror lives.

PIP: I can hear it holding its breath.

ACT TWO

[Nara asks Rik to save coaching notes until after the scene. Rik nods and sits at the table. Vela rehearses the monologue again, this time letting his pauses happen without stopping his breath. Pip gives the answer at the proper moment.]

NARA: Good. The words are clear, and the pause gives Pip room to respond.

VELA: Shall I laugh triumphantly?

PIP: I have just refused your plan.

VELA: Then a postponed triumph.

[Nara marks the laugh for a later scene where the villain actually believes he has won. Rik writes his movement note on a separate card. Vela collects the dialogue folder to take home.]

VELA: I will practice until the city trembles.

RIK: Take the warm-up sheet too. Separate pocket.

[Vela accepts it. Pip offers the villain the chair for a short rest between rehearsals. Vela sits and releases his shoulders without reading aloud.]

FINAL IMAGE: The villain rests comfortably beside his cardboard lair, holding the two distinctly labeled scripts.
