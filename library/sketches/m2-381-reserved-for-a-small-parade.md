# Reserved for a Small Parade

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: event parking systems that reserve spaces for vehicles that do not exist yet*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Omi, a capuchin attendant; Rell, a macaque driver; Fena, a gibbon planner; Suri, a lemur visitor.

EXT. COMMUNITY CENTER PARKING LOT - MORNING

[A ROW OF CONES MARKS EMPTY SPACES. EACH CONE HAS A CARD: RESERVED FOR PARADE VEHICLE. OMI HOLDS A CLIPBOARD.]

RELL: The parade is on foot. Why are the spaces reserved?

FENA: The parade committee has not decided whether it needs a vehicle.

SURI: When will they decide?

OMI: After they arrive.

Rell drives toward an empty space. Omi steps in front of it.

OMI: This space is reserved.

RELL: For what vehicle?

Omi checks the card. It says "one vehicle, exact dimensions pending."

Rell asks if he can park while the committee decides. Fena says only if the vehicle is the right size. Rell measures his cart. Omi has no dimensions to compare.

SURI: Could we reserve the space for the vehicle we have?

FENA: That would be premature.

A tiny handcart rolls into the lot carrying a box of streamers. Fena points to it.

FENA: That might be the parade vehicle.

Omi measures the cart. It is narrower than the reserved space. He moves two cones inward. Rell's cart now doesn't fit.

RELL: You made the space smaller.

OMI: I made it exact.

Suri brings over a bicycle. The parade committee's note says "possibly a bicycle." Omi asks if the bicycle will carry the banner. Suri puts the banner over the handlebars. It covers the front wheel.

FENA: We need a trial run.

Suri pushes the bicycle. The banner drags on the ground. The handcart follows, snagging the banner and pulling it through the cones.

OMI: The vehicle has crossed two reserved spaces.

FENA: It's a convoy.

Rell moves his cart out of the way. Omi lifts a cone to clear the path. The committee arrives on foot, each carrying a different sign. They ask where to park their floats.

One committee member brings a folding chair. Omi stops him at the cone line and checks the reservation card. The chair has no wheels, but the card says the parade may have a vehicle later. Fena tries to fold the chair small enough for the space. Its legs catch on two cones and pull them together. Rell backs his cart away again.

RELL: The space is now occupied by the reservation.

OMI: The card is not a vehicle.

FENA: It has four corners.

RELL: So does your card. Which one is marching?

FENA: We have no floats.

A volunteer rolls in a small wagon with a speaker attached. It is exactly the size of the reserved space. Omi measures it twice and places the card beside it.

OMI: Space confirmed.

RELL: Finally, a vehicle.

A volunteer starts the speaker. It plays parade music. The volunteer pushes the wagon forward to lead the parade. Omi follows with his tape measure, trying to measure a space that keeps leaving him.

[OMI MOVES THE RESERVED CARD TO THE OPENING IN THE CONE LINE. THE PARADE MEMBERS WALK THROUGH THE SPACE, AND FENA WRITES "FOOT TRAFFIC" ON THE CARD.]
