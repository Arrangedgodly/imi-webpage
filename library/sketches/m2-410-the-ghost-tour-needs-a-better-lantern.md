# The Ghost Tour Needs a Better Lantern

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: guided ghost walks that turn ordinary night sounds into premium scares*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Guide Mara, a macaque; visitor Nari, a lemur; visitor Vex, an orangutan; night keeper Pip, a capuchin; THE MOTH, a silent performer.

EXT. OLD ORCHARD PATH - NIGHT

[Mara holds a lantern that barely glows. Vex carries a brochure. Nari holds a small notebook. Pip locks a shed behind them.]

MARA: On your right is the orchard where the ghost was last seen.

NARI: What did it look like?

MARA: It was a shadow.

VEX: A pale shadow, or a dark one?

PIP: It was night.

[The lantern flickers. A large shape crosses the path. Vex clutches the brochure.]

VEX: The orchard has noticed us.

PIP: That's my moth trap.

[The moth lands on the lantern glass. Its wings throw a huge shadow across the trees. Mara turns the lantern. The shadow grows.]

MARA: The ghost is expanding.

NARI: The lantern is facing the trees.

[Vex reads the brochure.]

VEX: Premium tour includes one unexplained whisper.

NARI: Can we hear it?

[Mara raises a hand for silence. The trees creak. Vex gasps. Pip looks up.]

PIP: The branch is rubbing the roof.

VEX: We have identified the whisper.

MARA: That was not in the brochure.

[The moth flies toward Nari's notebook. She waves it away. The shadow leaps across Vex's shirt and appears to climb his chest. He spins, dragging the brochure around himself like a shield.]

VEX: It has chosen me.

NARI: It's a moth.

VEX: Why does it keep following me?

PIP: You are standing beside the lamp.

[Mara moves the lantern low. The moth settles on the page that advertises "A FACE IN THE WINDOW." Its shadow makes a face in the shed's glass pane. Vex points.]

VEX: That is the face.

PIP: That's the face on your brochure.

[Nari turns the brochure around. The same face is printed on the back as the tour logo. The moth crawls over it, covering the eyes.]

MARA: We have lost the ghost.

VEX: We can issue a refund.

NARI: You said this tour was premium.

MARA: Premium means the lantern is new.

[Pip swaps it for a bright work lantern. The trees become ordinary. The moth flies to the brochure and leaves one dusty wing mark over the logo.]

[A late group joins the path. Mara restarts her speech. The moth rises from the brochure and circles the bright work lantern. Every visitor follows its tiny shadow instead of the guide.]

MARA: The orchard ghost has returned.

NARI: It is one moth.

VEX: It has an audience now.

[Pip lowers the lantern. The group stops following the shadow. The moth lands on the shed latch. Vex opens his brochure to point out the premium option, but the moth's wing mark covers the price.]

VEX: The price is now mysterious.

MARA: That is a free feature.

FINAL BEAT: Vex asks to buy the brochure as a souvenir. Mara charges extra for the moth's autograph. Pip opens the shed door, and the moth flies inside to clock out.
