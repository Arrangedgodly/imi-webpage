# The Orchestra Tunes the Wrong Instrument

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: formal orchestral rehearsals where tuning protocol becomes more important than finding the note*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Conductor Vela, a gibbon; oboist Kito, a macaque; cellist Runa, a capuchin; and stage manager Odo, a baboon.

INT. CONCERT HALL - AFTERNOON

The orchestra sits in rows. Vela raises her baton. Kito gives the tuning note on oboe. Runa adjusts her cello peg.

VELA: We will tune to the oboe.

KITO: A concert A.

RUNA: I have it.

[A LOW TWANG sounds. The hall lights flicker.]

ODO: The stage door is vibrating.

VELA: Is it in tune?

RUNA: The cello is. The door is not.

[Kito gives the note again. The door vibrates harder and swings half open.]

ODO: We are tuning the building.

INT. CONCERT HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Vela asks each section to match the oboe. Brass players breathe in unison; the doors rattle. Odo holds one door shut with a sandbag.

VELA: Strings first. Match the A.

RUNA: I am matching it.

KITO: Your A is lower than mine.

RUNA: The instrument is warm now.

ODO: The sandbag is singing.

[The sandbag slides; the door opens. A draft lifts the sheet music. The players catch pages in order, forming a passing line down the stage.]

VELA: Keep the pitch steady.

RUNA: I am holding a page, not playing.

KITO: The page is in tune with the floor.

VELA: We need a reference that does not move.

ODO: The piano is bolted down.

[Odo points to the piano. Its tuning fork is attached by a string to the door handle.]

INT. CONCERT HALL - LATER

Vela has the orchestra hold a single note while Odo secures the door with a wedge. The note sustains. A loose spotlight slowly rotates toward the ceiling.

ODO: The wedge is holding.

VELA: The pitch is drifting.

RUNA: The cello is stable.

KITO: The oboe is stable.

[The spotlight rotates completely around and shines on the tuning fork hanging by the door.]

ODO: The fork is not stable.

VELA: It is swinging in time.

RUNA: That is not the beat we rehearsed.

Vela lowers her baton. The orchestra stops. The fork swings twice and rings on the music stand.

KITO: That is an A.

VELA: It is the cleanest one we have heard.

INT. CONCERT HALL - PERFORMANCE

The audience takes its seats. Odo has fixed the door and placed the tuning fork on a stand. Vela starts the concert. Every instrument enters on the correct note.

[At the first rest, the fork gives a faint ring as the building settles. All players turn toward it. Vela conducts the fork's decay with a solemn downward motion.]

RUNA: It has finished tuning.

VELA: At last, a musician who stops when the note is done.

[The orchestra resumes. Odo gently holds the door; the sandbag sits in the front row as an honored guest.]
