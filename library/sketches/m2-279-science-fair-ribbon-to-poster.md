# The Science Fair Ribbon Goes to the Poster

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: science fairs where polished display boards overshadow the experiment*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Judge Omi, a baboon; student Rali, a macaque; student Seli, a gibbon; and teacher Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

Projects fill the tables. Rali displays a simple paper bridge test. Seli has a tall board with glitter lettering and a working light strip.

OMI: What question are you testing?

RALI: How much weight can folded paper hold?

OMI: Show me the results.

[Rali places a small stack of washers on the bridge. It holds.]

SELI: My project asks whether a display can attract attention.

TAVI: It has attracted the judge.

[The light strip flashes. Omi turns toward Seli's board.]

INT. GYM - LATER

Omi returns with a score sheet. Rali has added a graph. Seli has added a spinning title sign.

OMI: Rali, explain the method.

RALI: I folded three paper shapes and added washers until they bent.

OMI: Do you have the notes?

RALI: Here.

[He opens a notebook. The pages are small and clear.]

SELI: My project has a dramatic reveal.

[She presses a button. The title sign spins, catches the score sheet, and wraps it around the display.]

OMI: I cannot read the score sheet.

TAVI: The experiment has affected the judging equipment.

INT. GYM - JUDGE'S WALKTHROUGH

Omi approaches a third table displaying an ordinary paperclip, surrounded by a velvet rope and spotlights.

OMI: What is the experiment?

TAVI: That is the display stand.

[The paperclip lies on the floor beneath the table.]

SELI: The light show is working.

RALI: I will pick up the paperclip.

[As Rali bends, the spotlight follows him. The glitter lettering reflects onto the ceiling.]

OMI: The equipment is conducting the presentation.

TAVI: The paperclip is still untested.
INT. GYM - AFTERNOON

Tavi helps Rali set the bridge back up. Omi holds a stack of washers. Seli turns off the spinning sign.

RALI: The widest fold held the most weight.

OMI: That is a result.

SELI: My board held the most attention.

OMI: Also a result.

[Omi places a ribbon between the projects. It reads BEST PRESENTATION. The ribbon catches the light strip and glows.]

SELI: I won.

OMI: You won presentation. Rali won the paper test.

RALI: Can the bridge hold the ribbon?

[He places it on the bridge. The paper bends but stays upright.]

TAVI: There is your final data point.

INT. GYM - AWARD TABLE

Omi gives the ribbon to Seli and the notebook to Rali. Seli turns off her board's lights so the project can be read.

SELI: Your result is easier to see now.

RALI: Your light is still bright.

[Seli directs the light toward the table. It illuminates the bridge and ribbon together.]

OMI: We have a joint display.

[The spinning title sign starts again, rotates once, and points at the exit.]
