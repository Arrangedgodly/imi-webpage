# The Missing Pageant Sash

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: small-town pageants, ceremonial titles, and organizers who treat a missing sash like an international incident*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Organizer Tessa, a gibbon; contestant Miri, a capuchin; contestant Odo, a macaque; tailor Sola, a ring-tailed lemur; and announcer Bex, an orangutan.

INT. TOWN HALL STAGE - EVENING

The pageant begins in five minutes. A table holds a crown, two flowers, and an empty display stand.

TESSA: The sash is missing.

BEX: We can delay the pageant.

MIRI: It is a ribbon with words on it.

TESSA: It says "Harvest Ambassador."

ODO: We can announce the winner without the sash.

TESSA: The sash is part of the ceremony.

Sola checks backstage. She finds a trail of silver thread leading to a costume trunk. The trunk contains capes, scarves, and a banner from last year's festival.

SOLA: The sash could be in the trunk.

TESSA: Do not open it. That would change the order of discovery.

BEX: The audience is waiting.

Tessa interviews the contestants about where they last saw the sash. Miri says she put it on a chair. Odo says the chair was moved to make room for the judges. Bex says the chair was used to hold the microphone.

TESSA: We have three possible locations.

SOLA: We have one chair.

They search the chair. The sash is not there. The microphone cable is wrapped around one leg. When Sola lifts it, the chair rolls, dragging a curtain with it. The curtain reveals a costume trunk.

TESSA: The sash may be behind the curtain.

MIRI: The curtain was the curtain.

Odo opens the trunk. Inside is last year's banner, folded so that the words "Harvest Ambassador" appear across one corner. Everyone gasps.

BEX: The sash has been found.

SOLA: That is the banner.

She lifts the fabric. A narrow sash is sewn along its edge. Miri reaches for it, but the seam catches on a pin. Sola cuts one thread and frees the sash.

TESSA: We have recovered the ceremonial object.

MIRI: It was attached to the old banner.

ODO: The banner is also ceremonial.

The pageant begins. Bex introduces the contestants. Tessa pins the sash on the winner, Odo, but it is too short to tie. Sola loops it over one shoulder instead.

BEX: Harvest Ambassador Odo.

ODO: I will use the title to announce the pie table.

TESSA: That is a noble responsibility.

Odo takes the microphone and announces the pie table. The audience applauds more loudly than for the crown. Miri helps serve slices while Sola repairs the banner seam.

Miri helps fold the banner. Sola pins the loose seam. The organizers pack the sash in a labeled box before the next pageant.

BEX: Next year, we will keep it in the same place.

TESSA: Where is that?

SOLA: In the labeled box.

They all look at the box. It is already placed beside the microphone stand.
