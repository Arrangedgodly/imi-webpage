# The Queue for Nothing

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace training films and customer-service efficiency campaigns*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: PIP, a macaque customer; MOSS, a langur customer; CLERK, a capuchin; NARRATOR, the voice of the training film.

[A waiting room. PIP takes a ticket. MOSS is asleep standing up. The CLERK wears a sash reading ZERO UNRESOLVED COMPLAINTS.]

NARRATOR: At Infinite Monkey Industries, our service begins with you.

PIP: Good. I want to return this toaster.

CLERK: Have you taken a number?

PIP: Seven.

CLERK: Wonderful. Seven is one of our numbers.

[A cheerful chime. The CLERK puts a gold star beside CUSTOMER UNDERSTOOD NUMBERS.]

PIP: It burns everything.

CLERK: The number?

PIP: The toaster.

CLERK: Then seven may be inappropriate. Take a heat-related number.

[PIP tears another ticket. It reads 7, in red.]

NARRATOR: Color-coded service makes a world of difference.

MOSS: I've been waiting since the film was black and white.

CLERK: We call that customer continuity.

PIP: Where is the returns window?

CLERK: Behind you.

[PIP turns. A mirror is labeled RETURNS WINDOW.]

PIP: That's me.

CLERK: We empower customers to resolve their own problems.

PIP: I don't work here.

CLERK: Then you've already reduced our payroll. Excellent initiative.

[The CLERK gives PIP an employee lanyard. PIP puts the toaster on the counter.]

PIP: Fine. As an employee, I authorize my refund.

CLERK: Employees must use the staff queue.

[The CLERK swings a rope around PIP. MOSS is now first in the customer queue.]

MOSS: I want to return a toaster.

PIP: I'll help you.

CLERK: Cross-queue communication requires supervisor approval.

PIP: Who's the supervisor?

[The CLERK points at the mirror. PIP salutes himself.]

PIP: Approved.

NARRATOR: Occasionally, an employee will show too much initiative.

[The film FREEZES. Everyone holds still except PIP'S tail, which keeps unrolling tickets.]

NARRATOR: Can you spot the mistake?

MOSS: You hired the customer.

NARRATOR: The mistake is visible tail movement during a learning pause.

[The CLERK tapes PIP'S tail to the counter. The film resumes.]

PIP: I want to speak to whoever wrote this training film.

CLERK: That's the complaints department. Congratulations, you've found your role.

[The CLERK pins COMPLAINTS MANAGER over PIP'S lanyard. MOSS hands him a ticket.]

MOSS: Your service is terrible.

PIP: I know.

[An alarm. The ZERO on the CLERK'S sash flips to ONE.]

CLERK: Don't acknowledge it! Now it's unresolved!

PIP: Give him his money back.

CLERK: That would teach him to complain.

MOSS: This film already did.

[PIP plugs in the toaster. It launches smoking bread into the ceiling projector. The screen burns through the words CUSTOMER SATISFACTION.]

NARRATOR: This is a valuable opportunity to practice evacuation procedures.

PIP: Where's the exit?

CLERK: Take an evacuation number.

[PIP feeds the dispenser into the toaster. Every ticket emerges flaming. MOSS opens the door.]

MOSS: Everybody's number is up.

[They leave. The CLERK follows, clutching the sash.]

NARRATOR: Another successful day with zero unresolved complaints.

[The empty mirror falls off the wall. Behind it is a real returns window. Someone knocks from the other side.]

CLERK, off: We're closed for staff training!

[BLACKOUT.]
