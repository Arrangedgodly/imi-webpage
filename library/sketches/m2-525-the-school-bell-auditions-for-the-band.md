# The School Bell Auditions for the Band

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: marching-band tryouts, school routines, and instruments that insist on joining the wrong ensemble*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: BAND LEADER, a baboon; BELL, an orangutan; STUDENT, a capuchin; TEACHER, a macaque; CUSTODIAN, a gibbon.

[A school courtyard. A marching band tunes up. A brass school bell sits on a rolling cart.]

BAND LEADER: We need one more low note.

BELL: I can ring.

STUDENT: That bell is for class changes.

BELL: I have been ringing for years.

[The band leader gestures. The bell rings once. The band follows with a chord that is much too loud.]

TEACHER: That was the lunch bell.

CUSTODIAN: It is not lunch.

ACT ONE

[The student rolls the bell cart to the band line. The bell rings at every bump. The band leader asks it to hold a steady beat.]

BAND LEADER: One ring per bar.

BELL: I ring when moved.

STUDENT: I can push evenly.

[The student pushes the cart across the courtyard. A wheel hits a crack; the bell rings twice. The band plays two extra notes and marches into a stack of folding chairs.]

CUSTODIAN: The chairs were not in the music.

[The custodian retrieves the chairs. The teacher checks the bell's mounting bracket. It is loose.]

TEACHER: The bell is ringing because the cart is shaking.

BAND LEADER: It has enthusiasm.

[The band leader asks for a second audition. The custodian locks the cart wheels. The bell rings once anyway, as the hallway echo returns from the gym.]

BELL: I can ring without moving.

STUDENT: That was the echo.

[The teacher moves the cart into the open courtyard. The band leader gives the bell a quiet strike with a padded mallet. It makes a deep tone. The band plays along.]

[The band leader tries the bell beside a drum and a cymbal. The bell's low note masks the flute, so the flute player moves to the back row. The custodian rolls the cart onto a rubber mat to stop the hallway echo.]

BAND LEADER: One note, then silence.

BELL: I can do one note.

[The student taps the bell once. The band holds the chord. The teacher raises a hand to stop them, but the sound continues to ring in the gym.]

TEACHER: The building is still singing.

CUSTODIAN: It will stop when the note fades.

[The band waits. The bell's vibration dies away. The leader changes the arrangement so the bell plays only at the end.]

ACT TWO

[The custodian tightens the bracket and replaces the squeaky cart wheel. The student rolls it carefully to the school entrance. The bell gives one even tone when struck with the mallet.]

BAND LEADER: It can join the percussion section.

TEACHER: Only for the concert.

BELL: I will ring on cue.

[The bell rings early as the band leader lifts the baton. The band begins the lunch march.]

STUDENT: That is still the wrong cue.

FINAL IMAGE: The band marches toward the cafeteria while the custodian parks the school bell beside the door.
