# The Ribbon Cutting Is Too Long

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: grand opening ceremonies and ceremonial speeches that delay a simple community path opening*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Mayor Omi, a baboon; organizer Kiri, a gibbon; resident Pavi, a macaque; photographer Lumo, a capuchin; and the townspeople.

[A NEW PATH IS BLOCKED BY A RIBBON. A CROWD WAITS.]

OMI: We gather to open this path, built through shared effort.

KIRI: The ribbon is tied to the branch.

PAVI: Can we walk around it?

OMI: Not before the ceremony.

[LUMO POSITIONS THE CAMERA.]

LUMO: Everyone look toward the ribbon.

[THE CROWD LOOKS. A MONKEY CLIMBS THE BRANCH AND UNTIES IT.]

KIRI: The ribbon is down.

OMI: The ceremony has not reached the cutting.

PAVI: The path is open.

[OMI TAKES OUT A PAIR OF CEREMONIAL SCISSORS.]

OMI: I will now make the official cut.

[THE RIBBON IS ALREADY ON THE GROUND.]

LUMO: We can reset it.

KIRI: The branch is too high.

[THE MONKEY HANDS OMI THE RIBBON.]

OMI: I accept this honor on behalf of the town.

PAVI: It is a ribbon.

[OMI STARTS THE SPEECH AGAIN.]

OMI: This path represents our shared...

[A CROWD MEMBER WALKS PAST.]

OMI: Please wait.

CROWD MEMBER: I am going to the water bowl.

KIRI: The bowl is down the path.

[THE CROWD MEMBER RETURNS WITH A FULL BOWL.]

PAVI: The path is already serving the community.

[OMI LOOKS AT THE SCISSORS.]

OMI: I will cut the ribbon now.

[KIRI HANDS THE MAYOR A COPY OF THE SPEECH.]

KIRI: You can read this while the crowd uses the path.

OMI: It has four paragraphs.

PAVI: We can shorten it to one sentence.

OMI: The town deserves a proper opening.

[THE MONKEY MOVES THE RIBBON TO THE SIDE. TWO RESIDENTS WALK THROUGH.]

LUMO: The path is open now.

OMI: I have not finished speaking.

PAVI: The residents have finished waiting.

[OMI CLOSES THE SPEECH. LUMO RAISES THE CAMERA.]

LUMO: We can take the opening photograph now.

OMI: The ribbon is on the ground.

KIRI: Hold one end. I will hold the other. The path can stay clear.

[THEY HOLD THE RIBBON BESIDE THE PATH. OMI OPENS THE SCISSORS. RESIDENTS WALK PAST IN THE BACKGROUND.]

LUMO: Cut on three.

OMI: Before I cut, a brief word about access.

PAVI: Three.

[OMI CUTS. LUMO TAKES THE PHOTO. KIRI COLLECTS THE LONG PIECE OF RIBBON.]

OMI: Did you get everyone in the picture?

LUMO: Everyone who was using the path. They are a little blurred.

OMI: Am I blurred?

LUMO: No. You held still throughout the speech.

[THE MAYOR EXAMINES THE PHOTO. PAVI DRINKS FROM THE BOWL.]

OMI: The headline should say the town is moving forward.

KIRI: It is. The photograph shows them walking past you.

[THE MONKEY TAKES THE SHORT RIBBON AND TIES IT AROUND THE WATER BOWL.]

OMI: What is that ceremony?

PAVI: Decoration. You can drink without opening it.

[OMI STEPS TOWARD THE BOWL WITH THE SCISSORS. KIRI TAKES THEM AND PUTS THEM IN THE TOOL BAG.]

KIRI: One official cut per path.

OMI: Could we call it a waterfront development?

LUMO: The camera is packed away.

[OMI LOOKS AT THE BOWL, THEN OPENS HIS SPEECH AGAIN. PAVI AND KIRI FOLLOW THE RESIDENTS DOWN THE PATH.]

OMI: We gather to celebrate this water...

[THE MONKEY DRINKS FROM THE BOWL AND WALKS AWAY. OMI IS LEFT ADDRESSING HIS REFLECTION.]
