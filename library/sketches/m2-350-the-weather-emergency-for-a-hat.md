# The Weather Emergency for a Hat

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: emergency-response drills that overreact to minor weather and personal belongings*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: marshal Nara, a gibbon; trainer Odo, a baboon; trainee Piri, a capuchin; passerby Miri, a macaque.

EXT. PATH - DAY

A breeze lifts Piri's hat and drops it three steps away.

ODO: Emergency drill!

NARA: It is a hat.

Odo tells Piri to secure the area. Piri places a cone around the hat. Nara asks if the cone is necessary. Odo says the training card lists "loose object response."

Miri walks toward the hat. Piri warns her to stay back until the scene is assessed. Miri says it is her hat. Odo asks her to identify a unique feature. She points to a green patch. Piri checks the hat. It has a green patch.

NARA: We have an owner.

ODO: The drill isn't finished.

Odo has Piri take a photo of the hat and file an incident report. The form asks for the weather conditions. Piri writes "breeze." It asks whether the hat caused an injury. Miri says it did not. The form still requires a medical contact. Nara crosses out the field.

Miri picks up the hat and puts it on. The breeze lifts it again. This time she holds it with one hand. Odo asks if she has followed the protocol. She says she has.

Piri asks whether the drill should include a second response. Odo says yes. Nara points to the hat on Miri's head. Miri ties the strap under her chin. The hat stays in place.

The crew removes the cone. Odo says the area is secure. Piri submits the form. The printer produces a three-page report. Nara uses the pages to fan herself.

Odo reads the report aloud: one hat moved, one owner recovered it, and no one was hurt. Nara asks why the report needed three pages. Piri says the form had three copies. Miri uses the first page to shade her eyes while standing under the tree. Odo worries this counts as a second loose-object incident. Nara tells him to look at the situation before activating the drill. The breeze blows the cone over. Piri picks it up without calling an emergency. Odo starts to say "secure the area," then notices Miri has tied the hat strap. He marks the lesson complete. Nara folds the unused pages and returns them to the station. Miri asks if the drill is available for other problems. Odo says only if the form can be filled in under three pages.

Piri clips the unused form pages together.

Nara puts the form in the office drawer.

Odo checks the garden path.

Piri folds the report and takes it to the station.

TAG

Miri walks away wearing the hat.

ODO: We should monitor the wind.

NARA: We should end the drill.
