# The Apology for the Weather

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate crisis statements that apologize for things nobody controls*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: spokesperson Tavi, a parrot; manager Odo, a baboon; gardener Miri, a gibbon; reporter Kito, a macaque.

INT. PRESS ROOM - DAY

Tavi stands at a podium. Odo hands her a statement.

TAVI: We regret the rain.

MIRI: It was a cloud.

KITO: Who is apologizing?

Odo says the garden brand must address customer concern after a light shower interrupted its picnic. Miri says the picnic continued under the tree. Tavi reads the next line: "We take full responsibility for the damp grass."

MIRI: The grass is supposed to be damp.

Kito asks if the company caused the rain. Odo says it is not about cause; it is about acknowledging impact. Tavi asks whether they should apologize to the picnic blanket.

Odo gives her a revised statement: "We are listening to the sky." Miri says the sky has no feedback channel. Kito asks how many people complained. Odo says one person wrote that their sandwich got wet.

Miri says the sandwich was under the blanket. Tavi says the company can replace it. The customer arrives with the sandwich, which is dry. They say they just wanted to know if the picnic was canceled.

Odo says the picnic was not canceled. Tavi begins another statement: "We apologize for any confusion regarding the picnic, the weather, and the status of the sandwich." The customer asks if they can have another sandwich anyway.

Miri opens the picnic basket. There are extra sandwiches. She offers one. The customer accepts. Odo says the gesture is a good-faith resolution. Miri says it is lunch.

The rain stops. Tavi looks at the prepared statement and asks whether she should apologize for saying it might rain. Kito says the forecast was accurate. Odo says the company should communicate with empathy. Miri says the grass is already drying.

Kito asks if the statement can be shortened to "The picnic is still on." Tavi repeats it into the microphone. Miri says that is all the customer needed. Odo asks if they should mention the grass. Miri tells him the grass is doing fine. The customer opens the replacement sandwich and offers to share it with the crew.

Tavi folds the statement and puts it away. Odo wants to keep the podium for future announcements. Miri says they can use it to tell people where the dry blankets are. Kito asks the customer if they need a towel. The customer says no, the grass never soaked the blanket.

The crew shares the sandwich while the sun comes out.

The microphone records laughter.

The picnic continues beneath the tree.

Tavi signs off.

Miri gives Tavi the final statement to file away.

TAG

Tavi reads the statement to the empty room.

TAVI: We regret the sun.

MIRI: Stop.
