# The Lighthouse Keeper Guards a Pond

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: maritime rescue dramas and local news crews who turn a small pond patrol into a coastal emergency*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: KEEPER, a macaque; REPORTER, a gibbon; CAMERAPERSON, an orangutan; PARK RANGER, a baboon; DUCK, a capuchin.

[A park pond at dusk. A small decorative lighthouse sits beside the path. Its lamp turns slowly over the water. One duck paddles in a circle near the bank; the island is empty.] 

REPORTER: We are live at the edge of a dangerous inland sea.

RANGER: It is a pond.

KEEPER: The lighthouse helps ducks find the island after sunset.

[The cameraperson frames the water dramatically. The duck continues paddling along the bank.] 

REPORTER: A vessel is circling the harbor.

RANGER: That is one duck.

[The keeper checks the lamp panel. The beam stops turning. The reporter lowers the microphone as the keeper opens the service hatch.] 

KEEPER: The rotation motor has stalled.

REPORTER: Is the pond in danger?

KEEPER: The duck can see the island. I need to free the motor before the lamp overheats.

[The ranger holds the service hatch open while the keeper switches off the lamp. The cameraperson keeps the camera behind the path rail. The keeper removes a curled leaf from the gear, closes the hatch, and presses the latch to confirm it is secure.] 

RANGER: Clear.

[The keeper restarts the lamp. It turns steadily, sweeping the pond and the path. The duck remains near the bank, watching the moving beam.] 

REPORTER: The emergency has been resolved.

RANGER: The maintenance has been completed.

CAMERAPERSON: Can I zoom in on the duck?

KEEPER: From the path, without the dramatic fog filter.

[The cameraperson adjusts the lens from the pond rail. The duck shakes water from its feathers near the bank; a few droplets reach the camera lens. The reporter wipes it with a clean cloth. The duck then paddles to the island and climbs out.] 

REPORTER: We have been splashed by the story.

RANGER: You were standing beside a pond.

[The keeper checks the lamp timer and turns off the news crew's portable spotlight so it does not compete with the lighthouse beam. The duck settles on the island.] 

KEEPER: The lamp will stay on until the park closes.

REPORTER: And the vessel?

RANGER: The duck is staying overnight.

[The reporter signs off. The cameraperson backs down the path, keeping the pond rail between the crew and the water.] 

[The ranger checks the light's sweep from the path. It clears the trees and crosses the island without shining toward nearby homes.]

RANGER: The beam is within the park boundary.

KEEPER: The timer will turn it off at closing.

REPORTER: Does the duck need an escort?

RANGER: The island is its usual roost. We do not disturb it.

[The camera operator wipes the lens and takes one still photograph from behind the rail. The reporter reads the sign beside the path: "DECORATIVE LIGHT - POND VIEW." ]

REPORTER: That is less dramatic than "inland sea."

RANGER: It is accurate.

[The keeper checks the latched service hatch and the panel light. The lamp continues rotating. The crew packs the spotlight away before leaving the path.]

CAMERAPERSON: No fog filter?

KEEPER: No fog. No rescue vessel. Just a light and one duck.

FINAL IMAGE: The small lighthouse sweeps its steady beam across the pond as the duck tucks its head under one wing.
