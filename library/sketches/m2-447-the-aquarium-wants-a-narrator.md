# The Aquarium Wants a Narrator

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wildlife-documentary narration and aquarium tours that assign motives to every animal in the tank*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: NARRATOR, a gibbon; AQUARIUM GUIDE, a macaque; VISITOR, an orangutan; TECHNICIAN, a capuchin; TANK KEEPER, a baboon.

[An aquarium tank. A small fish drifts past a rock. A microphone hangs over the glass.]

NARRATOR: The fish approaches the rock, seeking shelter from its rivals.

GUIDE: That is a filter intake.

NARRATOR: It approaches the filter intake, seeking shelter from its rivals.

VISITOR: Does the fish have rivals?

KEEPER: It has a neighbor on the other side of the plant.

[The fish turns and swims in the opposite direction. The narrator lowers his voice.]

NARRATOR: A sudden retreat.

GUIDE: It turned around.

NARRATOR: The mystery deepens.

[The technician taps the microphone. A loud thump plays through a speaker. The fish darts behind the rock.]

VISITOR: That startled me too.

TECHNICIAN: I was checking the pickup.

NARRATOR: The disturbance has altered the migration.

KEEPER: There is no migration. It is one tank.

[The guide reads a card about the fish's habitat. The narrator speaks over her.]

NARRATOR: In this ancient reef, the fish battles the currents.

GUIDE: The tank was installed last year, and the pump is on low.

NARRATOR: The fish remembers a wilder sea.

KEEPER: We don't know what it remembers.

[A second fish enters frame, swims beside the first, then leaves.]

NARRATOR: An alliance forms, then fractures.

VISITOR: They passed each other.

GUIDE: The second fish is moving toward the feeding corner.

NARRATOR: The alliance was about food.

KEEPER: They have not been fed yet.

[The keeper lifts the feeding lid. The fish rise toward the surface. The narrator begins a dramatic sentence, but the guide holds up one finger.]

GUIDE: We feed them on schedule. Please let visitors hear the explanation.

NARRATOR: The keeper prepares to release the treasure.

KEEPER: It is measured food.

[The keeper scatters a small amount. The fish eat. One piece falls behind the filter guard. The technician retrieves the guard with a long tool.]

TECHNICIAN: The food is stuck at the intake. I will clear it after feeding.

NARRATOR: A hidden cache has been discovered.

KEEPER: It's one piece of food.

[The narrator takes off his headphones.]

NARRATOR: I can describe what we can see. The fish swims, turns, eats, and rests. The tank keeper checks the filter. I do not know whether the fish is brave.

VISITOR: That helps me watch it.

GUIDE: The exhibit label says the species and its habitat. The rest is movement.

[The fish returns to the rock and rests. The narrator starts to whisper. The guide hands him the label card.]

NARRATOR: It is resting beside the filter intake.

KEEPER: That is accurate.

FINAL IMAGE: The microphone hangs quietly above the glass while the fish drifts past the rock without a plot.
