# The Museum Guard Explains the Rope

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio and gallery guides that bury simple viewing rules under interpretive language*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Gallery guide Tavi, a capuchin; visitor Nema, a macaque; guard Odo, a baboon; and curator Sela, a gibbon.

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - DAY

A rope barrier keeps visitors a short distance from a small clay bowl. Tavi speaks into a headset. Nema leans forward.

TAVI: This rope creates a dialogue between the viewer and the object's protected space.

NEMA: Can I see the bowl?

ODO: From this side of the rope, yes.

TAVI: The rope establishes a threshold of contemplation.

NEMA: I understand. I am on this side.

[Sela enters with a clipboard and a new sign reading DO NOT CROSS.] 

SELA: Visitors keep asking what the rope means.

ODO: We could leave the sign visible.

TAVI: That might interrupt the dialogue.

INT. GALLERY - LATER

Tavi gives a tour. Nema follows, trying to look at the bowl around the large interpretive panel.

TAVI: Notice how the vessel invites an imagined journey.

NEMA: I can only see your panel.

SELA: The panel is covering the vessel.

TAVI: It frames the absence of direct access.

ODO: It is in front of the bowl.

[Odo shifts the panel aside. The bowl is visible; a visitor on the other side can now read the label.]

NEMA: It is small.

TAVI: Smallness can be a powerful scale.

NEMA: It is a bowl.

SELA: It held water. The label says so.

TAVI: The bowl's silence carries its history.

ODO: The label carries its history.

INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Sela replaces the sign with a large diagram explaining the rope, the label, the bowl, and the visitor's viewing angle. It blocks the entire display.

NEMA: Now I can see the diagram.

TAVI: It offers a complete interpretive experience.

ODO: It blocks the object again.

SELA: We need a sign that answers the question and leaves the bowl visible.

[Sela removes the diagram and writes on a small card: PLEASE LOOK; DO NOT TOUCH.] 

NEMA: That is clear.

TAVI: Is there room for an artist statement?

ODO: On the back.

[Tavi turns the card over and begins to write. The text becomes so long it folds over the bottom edge and hides the bowl.] 

TAVI: The sentence is still developing.

SELA: The card is not.

INT. GALLERY - CLOSING

Odo places the small sign beside the rope. Nema stands at the marked viewing spot. The bowl is unobstructed.

NEMA: I can see it now.

TAVI: What does it say to you?

NEMA: It says, "I am a bowl. Please do not touch me."

SELA: That is the label.

[The museum lights dim. Tavi's headset continues to glow as he faces the empty room.]

TAVI: And the rope says, "Good night."

ODO: The lights say good night.

[TAVI TAKES OFF HIS HEADSET. THE GLOW DISAPPEARS.]

TAVI: The guide has ended.
