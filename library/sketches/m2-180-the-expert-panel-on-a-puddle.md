# The Expert Panel on a Puddle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: serious roundtable news programs that treat a small weather event as a geopolitical crisis*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Vela, a macaque moderator; Odo, a gibbon hydrologist; Pito, a capuchin neighborhood correspondent; and Seli, a lemur resident.

INT. NEWS SET - EVENING

A screen shows a puddle on the studio floor. Vela sits between two guests.

VELA: Is the puddle expanding?

ODO: It is not moving.

PITO: It is near the entrance.

Seli enters with a mop. Vela asks her to wait until the panel has finished.

SELI: It is wet. I can mop it.

Odo explains that the water likely came from a dripping umbrella. Pito says the puddle is three steps from the door and one step from a boot tray. Vela asks what it means for the neighborhood.

PITO: It means someone came in from the rain.

The screen changes to a map of the studio, with the puddle marked in red. Odo points out that the map is upside down. Vela asks if the puddle's position is uncertain. Odo rotates the map. The mark now covers the host desk.

Seli sets the mop beside the puddle. Pito reports that a cleaning tool has arrived. Vela asks whether the tool changes the balance of power. Seli picks it up and wipes the floor. The puddle shrinks.

ODO: The water level is falling.

PITO: The mop is responsible.

Vela asks Odo if the puddle could return. He says another wet umbrella could create one. Pito notes the umbrella stand is empty. A guest enters carrying an umbrella and drips onto the floor. The puddle starts again.

VELA: We are seeing a renewed development.

Seli takes the guest's umbrella outside, shakes it, then returns and places it in the stand. She wipes the new drops. Odo says the situation is stable. Pito asks if stability can be confirmed by a second expert. Vela points at Seli, still holding the mop.

SELI: It is dry.

The panel nods. Vela asks the viewers to stay tuned for a continuing analysis. The camera pulls back, revealing a small towel on the floor. Pito reports a new fabric deployment. Seli picks it up and hangs it over the radiator.

ODO: The floor remains dry.

VELA: Our experts agree.

Seli carries the mop away. The umbrella stand tips slightly. Vela starts to ask about a new crisis, but Odo catches it and sets it upright.

PITO: The situation is contained.

Vela turns toward camera and promises more coverage after the next rain. Behind her, the studio sprinkler test begins. A single drop lands on the radiator beside the screen. Everyone looks up.

SELI (FROM THE DOORWAY): That is the ceiling.

The panel watches the drop hit the towel. Vela asks if they can go to commercial. Odo says the floor is still dry. The show cuts to an ad for an umbrella stand.
