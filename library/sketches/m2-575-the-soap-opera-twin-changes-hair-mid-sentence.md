# The Soap Opera Twin Changes Hair Mid-Sentence

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: soap-opera twin reveals staged by one performer who has nowhere to change between entrances*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: ACTOR KORA, a macaque; DIRECTOR PIM, an orangutan; HAIR ARTIST NEL, a capuchin; CO-STAR RUD, a gibbon.

[A rehearsal living-room set. Kora wears a short dark wig. Rud sits on the sofa with a script. Nel holds a second, curly wig on a stand. Pim marks two entrances on the page.]

KORA: You thought my brother had left forever.

RUD: I thought I was speaking to him.

KORA: That is the twist.

PIM: You are playing both brothers. At this point, we need the first brother to leave before the second arrives.

[Kora looks at the single doorway, then at the next line, which immediately introduces the second brother.]

ACT ONE

KORA: Where does my hair change?

NEL: Behind the folding screen in the wing.

RUD: I have one line to cover the change.

PIM: Make it a thoughtful line.

[Kora leaves through the door. Rud looks at the audience seats and speaks slowly.]

RUD: I wonder what became of his brother.

[From the wing comes the sound of a dropped wig stand. Nel catches the curly wig before it touches the floor, but the empty stand rolls briefly against the door. Kora returns without either wig.]

KORA: I am the second brother.

RUD: The first brother's hair has also disappeared.

NEL: We need more than one line.

[Pim adds an action: Rud will read a letter left on the table. Rud picks up the prop envelope, opens it and finds a blank sheet.]

RUD: It is a remarkably private letter.

PIM: We will write the text before the run.

[Nel resets both wigs on their stands. Kora puts on the dark one for the first brother. Pim asks him to rehearse the door movement alone. Kora exits, walks behind the screen and changes with Nel's help. Rud reads an improvised note about a delayed train, giving them time.]

[Kora returns in the curly wig. Rud stops the letter at the intended cue and turns toward him.]

KORA: My brother has been keeping a secret.

RUD: Was it the hair?

PIM: That line is not in the scene.

RUD: It is the only secret I have observed.

ACT TWO

[Pim writes an actual letter onto the prop sheet, giving Rud a fixed passage long enough for the change. Nel checks the two wig stands are secure behind the screen. Kora asks whether the brothers could meet face to face.]

PIM: In the recorded close-ups, yes. This stage version keeps them in separate entrances.

KORA: I would like to confront myself.

NEL: Confront the second wig after the first has come off.

[They perform the transition once more with the finished letter. Kora exits as the first brother. Rud reads the news of a missed train. Kora returns as the second brother on the last sentence. The change is clean.]

RUD: You arrived faster than your train.

KORA: I arrived from the hallway.

[Pim points to the script. Kora repeats the proper secret line instead. Rud answers his actual cue. Nel sits beside the empty dark-wig stand, finally able to put down her comb.]

FINAL IMAGE: The two wig stands wait behind the screen while Kora solemnly accuses his absent twin of a secret the audience already knows.
