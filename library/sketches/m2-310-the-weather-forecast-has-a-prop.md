# The Weather Forecast Has a Prop

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: television weather segments, oversized maps, dramatic pointing, and forecasters who use props that create their own weather*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Forecaster Bex, a capuchin; host Tavi, a macaque; technician Luma, a gibbon; and caller Omi, an orangutan.

INT. WEATHER STUDIO - DAY

Bex stands beside a giant map. Luma brings out a small fan and a bowl of cotton clouds.

TAVI: Today's forecast is clear skies and light wind.

BEX: To demonstrate the wind, I have a fan.

LUMA: The fan is for the studio.

BEX: It is now for the forecast.

She turns it on. The cotton clouds scatter across the floor. One lands on Tavi's head.

TAVI: A cloud has reached the host.

BEX: It is moving east.

LUMA: It is moving toward the air vent.

Omi calls in to ask whether it will rain in the west part of town. Bex points to the map, but her tail hits the fan switch. The breeze pushes a paper rain cloud across the map and over the west district.

BEX: The west is under cloud cover.

LUMA: The west is under paper.

TAVI: Should we issue an umbrella advisory?

BEX: Only if the cloud is dark.

The cloud is white. Bex places a blue cloth over it to make it look stormy. The cloth covers the map legend.

OMI: I cannot tell which area is the west.

BEX: It is beneath the cloud.

OMI: The cloud is beneath the cloth.

Luma turns off the fan and retrieves the props. Bex attempts a forecast without them. She points to the map with a long stick. The stick catches on the map, pulling it off its stand. Tavi catches it. The map folds in half, hiding the town.

TAVI: We have lost the eastern region.

BEX: The rain chance remains low.

LUMA: The map is upside down.

Bex flips it around. Omi says the weather outside is sunny. Bex confirms that the forecast is accurate. Tavi opens the studio door, and a real breeze blows in, scattering the cotton clouds again.

BEX: The front has arrived.

LUMA: That is the hallway door.

TAVI: Is it a cold front?

LUMA: It is an open door.

The crew closes the door and places the clouds in a box. Bex reads the forecast: clear skies, light wind, no rain expected. Omi asks what to do with an umbrella.

BEX: Keep it by the door in case the forecast changes.

LUMA: The forecast is on the screen.

BEX: The door is also by the screen.

Tavi ends the broadcast. The map stands upright. A cotton cloud remains stuck to the studio fan.

OMI: Is that the forecast?

BEX: That is the only cloud left.

Luma removes the last cloud from the fan and stores it with the other props. The forecast stays clear until the next segment, when the studio air vent turns on by itself.
