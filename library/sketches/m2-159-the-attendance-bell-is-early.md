# The Attendance Bell Is Early

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school attendance tracking systems that register a monkey before its classmates arrive*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Teacher Vela, a macaque; clerk Rook, a baboon; student Nimi, a gibbon; and a small monkey.

[A CLASSROOM DOOR HAS A SCANNER. THE BELL HAS NOT RUNG.]

ROOK: Please scan your student card.

NIMI: I forgot it.

VELA: You can sign the paper sheet.

[THE SMALL MONKEY PRESSES ITS HAND TO THE SCANNER. IT BEEPS.]

ROOK: We have a student.

VELA: It is not enrolled.

[THE SCANNER DISPLAYS "PRESENT."]

ROOK: The system has counted it.

NIMI: I am here too.

[THE SCANNER WILL NOT BEEP FOR NIMI.]

ROOK: It says your card is inactive.

NIMI: I am active.

[THE MONKEY CLIMBS INTO A DESK.]

VELA: It is in the classroom.

ROOK: We need a guardian signature.

[THE MONKEY PRESSES THE BUTTON AGAIN. THE SCANNER BEEPS.]

ROOK: It has checked in twice.

NIMI: Can I sit down?

VELA: You are still waiting for your attendance to register.

[THE BELL RINGS. EVERYONE SITS EXCEPT THE MONKEY, WHO RUNS ACROSS THE DESKS.]

ROOK: The system has marked it tardy.

VELA: It arrived before the bell.

ROOK: It left its assigned seat.

NIMI: It has no assigned seat.

[THE TEACHER GIVES THE MONKEY A PAPER CARD. IT CHEWS THE CORNER.]

ROOK: The card is damaged.

VELA: It is present.

[ROOK OPENS THE ATTENDANCE SETTINGS.]

ROOK: We can disable repeat scans.

VELA: That will not enroll the monkey.

ROOK: It already enrolled itself.

NIMI: Can it be in my class?

VELA: It can sit beside you while we sort this out.

[ROOK ENTERS THE MONKEY AS A VISITOR. THE SCREEN ASKS FOR A VISITOR BADGE.]

ROOK: It needs a badge.

[THE MONKEY TAKES A BLANK BADGE AND WEARS IT AS A BRACELET.]

VELA: That is not how it is worn.

NIMI: It can still be identified.

[ROOK SCANS THE BADGE. THE SYSTEM MARKS "PRESENT."]

ROOK: We have a student and a visitor.

VELA: We have one monkey and one Nimi.

[THE SYSTEM ADDS A THIRD RECORD.]

ROOK: The system has counted the teacher.

VELA: At least it recognizes me today.

[ROOK PRINTS A CLASS LIST WITH TWO NAMES AND THREE RECORDS.]

NIMI: The class is now ready.

[VELA CLOSES THE LESSON BOOK. THE MONKEY SITS BESIDE HER.]

[THE MONKEY SITS BESIDE NIMI AND LOOKS AT THE LESSON BOARD.]

ROOK: The count is still confusing.

[THE MONKEY HANDS THE CARD TO NIMI. NIMI SIGNS IT.]

ROOK: Now we need a signature.

NIMI: I am signing for myself.

[ROOK ENTERS THE ATTENDANCE BY HAND. THE SYSTEM BEEPS.]

ROOK: Both students are present.

VELA: There is one monkey.

ROOK: It has two attendance records.

[THE MONKEY SITS BESIDE NIMI. VELA STARTS THE LESSON.]

VELA: Today we are learning to count.

NIMI: How many students?

ROOK: Three.

[THE MONKEY BEEPS THE SCANNER AGAIN.]

ROOK: Four.
