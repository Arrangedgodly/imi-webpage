# The School of Dramatic Entrances

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: performing-arts classes that teach theatrical entrances until a student misses the actual scene*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Piri, a macaque drama teacher; Nara, a gibbon student; Odo, a capuchin stagehand; and Vela, a lemur director.

INT. CLASSROOM STAGE - AFTERNOON

Piri draws a doorway on the blackboard.

PIRI: Today we practice entrances.

Nara walks onto the stage. Piri stops her.

PIRI: You entered without anticipation.

NARA: I walked in.

Piri shows her how to pause behind the door, listen for a cue and step into a shaft of light. Odo holds a cardboard door. Vela calls the cue. Nara waits, hears her name and enters with a grand turn. The cardboard door falls over.

ODO: The entrance was strong.

PIRI: The architecture was weak.

Odo props the door upright. Piri asks Nara to try a mysterious entrance. She comes in quietly from the side. Vela says the audience did not see her. Nara repeats the entrance, moving slower. She is visible, but the class is now staring at the door.

Piri tells her to build suspense. Nara stands behind the door. The class waits. A long pause passes. Odo asks if she is ready. Nara says she is waiting for the cue. Vela forgot to call it.

VELA: Enter.

Nara enters, then exits to practice again. Piri says an entrance should make a promise. Nara asks what promise. Piri says the character will arrive. Nara walks through the door and says, "I am here." The class applauds.

Piri wants a surprise entrance. Odo hides behind the cardboard door. Nara enters. Odo jumps out, but the door opens the wrong way and traps his tail. Nara helps him free it. Piri calls this an unexpected reveal.

Vela checks the script. The scene begins before the entrance. The actor is already meant to be onstage. Piri says the entrance exercise has prevented the scene from starting. Nara asks if she can start from the stage this time. Piri agrees. Nara stands under the light and delivers the first line.

NARA: We have been waiting for the door.

Odo folds the cardboard door flat and sets it aside. Piri says the next lesson will cover exits. Vela asks if she can leave now. Piri says she must exit with intention. Vela walks out, then comes back in to ask where the door went. Odo carries the cardboard door after her.

PIRI: That is a strong callback.

Piri nods for the line to start. Nara sits at center stage and speaks without crossing the doorway. Odo holds the flat cardboard door out of sight. Vela gives the next cue. The scene continues, and the audience can hear every word without watching a rehearsal of the entrance.

Vela calls the cue clearly from the front row. Nara begins the scene again. This time no one waits for a door.
