# The Celebrity Chef Judges the Menu Font

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive cooking finales that review plating before anyone has made the food*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: CHEF VELA, an orangutan; JUDGE RIK, a baboon; DESIGNER PIP, a capuchin; SERVER NARA, a gibbon.

[A restaurant rehearsal. A clean plate stands under a spotlight. Rik sits at a judging desk. Pip carries two printed menu designs. Vela wears her working apron.]

RIK: The presentation is timid.

VELA: It is an empty plate. The food is in the kitchen.

RIK: An absence of ambition.

PIP: We are approving the menu today.

[Nara places both menu samples before Rik. One uses curling gold letters; the other uses plain dark type. Rik leans toward the gold version.]

RIK: That has flavor.

VELA: You have not tasted anything.

RIK: I can sense the chef's confidence.

PIP: You are sensing a font I licensed yesterday.

ACT ONE

[Rik points at the longest gold flourish, which crosses the price for soup.]

RIK: A fine reduction.

NARA: The price is eight. You are pointing at the end of the letter S.

RIK: The eight needs garnish.

PIP: It needs to be readable.

[Vela brings a covered bowl from the kitchen and sets it on the serving counter. She removes the cover; steam rises.]

VELA: We can check the description against the dish now. This is leek soup.

RIK: I expected something more ornamental.

VELA: The leek does not know about the gold letters.

[Nara offers Rik a spoon and the plain menu. He reads the description aloud, then tastes the soup.]

RIK: It has a generous finish.

PIP: Thank you. I increased the space below the paragraph.

RIK: I meant the soup.

PIP: Then please keep the spoon over the bowl, not over my sample.

[Rik moves it. A tiny drop has already landed on the margin. Pip circles the stain with a pencil instead of wiping wet paper.]

NARA: We have tested stain resistance too.

[Pip asks Nara to read the gold description from the opposite side of the counter. She mistakes the word "leek" for "luck." Vela holds the plain menu beside it. Nara reads that version correctly.]

VELA: A restaurant should be able to tell people what's in the bowl.

RIK: Luck soup would be memorable.

NARA: It would make a difficult conversation with anyone who expected a lottery ticket.

ACT TWO

[Pip clips the plain menu on the approval board. Rik takes a thick marker and raises it above the empty plate.]

PIP: That is for the paper signature.

RIK: I was about to award the plate a score.

VELA: It still has no dinner on it.

[Nara brings Vela a small lunch plate from the kitchen. Vela moves the untouched display plate aside and sits at the end of the counter. Rik watches the ordinary plate receive actual food.]

RIK: You have changed the presentation.

VELA: I have started my lunch break.

[Pip shows Rik the marked approval line. Rik signs it, adding a tiny star next to his name. Pip checks that the star does not cross any menu price, then puts the approved sample in her folder.]

NARA: Will the restaurant get stars too?

RIK: That depends on the dinner.

VELA: Excellent. Come back when we are serving it.

[Nara removes the judging microphone, which was never connected. Rik notices its cable curled beneath the desk.]

RIK: Was anybody watching?

PIP: The printer is waiting for my email.

FINAL IMAGE: Rik admires the star through Pip's open folder while Vela eats soup beneath the unused spotlight.
