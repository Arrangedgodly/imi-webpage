# Weather for Indoor Vines

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Local television weather maps, sponsor breaks, and cheerful forecasts*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Meteorologist Zuzu, a lemur; camera operator Bex, a macaque; producer Goro, a gorilla; intern Pip, a squirrel monkey; caller Marn, a capuchin.

[STAGE: A NEWS SET. A MAP OF THE STUDIO HAS A GIANT SUN DRAWN OVER THE COFFEE POT.]

ZUZU: Good morning, troop. A warm front is moving through the break room.
BEX: That's the coffee machine.
ZUZU: The map confirms it is producing a high-pressure system.
GORO: We have a sponsor message before the forecast.
PIP: Today's weather is brought to you by Umbrella Vine, which grows wherever you leave it.
ZUZU: The vine has reached my chart.
GORO: That's product placement.
ZUZU: It is blocking the seven-day outlook.
PIP: The vine outlook is seven days of vine.
BEX: Camera two, show the eastern canopy.
ZUZU: On the map, we see a patch of fog over the supply closet.
GORO: That's the humidifier.
ZUZU: A persistent fog bank, then.
PIP: Should I draw a warning triangle?
ZUZU: Draw a small umbrella.
PIP: The umbrella is already covered by the vine.
BEX: I can tilt the camera.
ZUZU: Now the whole map is sideways.
GORO: Viewers love a dramatic angle.
ZUZU: The wind is shifting from west to down.
MARN: I'm calling from the north branch. Is it safe to swing to lunch?
ZUZU: What is the local condition?
MARN: One loose leaf and a suspiciously quiet crow.
ZUZU: Stay on the lower route until the crow declares its intentions.
MARN: It has a sandwich.
ZUZU: Then it is a lunch advisory.
GORO: Can we make it a red alert?
ZUZU: Only if the sandwich is airborne.
BEX: The crow is now on the set.
PIP: It took the sponsor card.
GORO: Keep rolling. This is breaking weather.
ZUZU: We are tracking a fast-moving snack across the studio.
MARN: The crow is heading south.
ZUZU: The sandwich has created a warm, moist pocket of silence.
BEX: I lost the bird behind the green screen.
GORO: Put a sun graphic there.
PIP: The sun is over the coffee pot.
ZUZU: We now have a cloudy coffee pot and a clear green screen.
GORO: Can we say partly sponsored?
ZUZU: No. We can say the forecast is brought to you by a vine.
PIP: It has wrapped around the microphone.
BEX: The microphone is moving on its own.
ZUZU: A gentle breeze from the east.
GORO: That is the fan.
ZUZU: The fan has entered the weather map.
MARN: Should I take the lower route?
ZUZU: Yes, and bring the sandwich back if it lands.
MARN: It landed on the map.
PIP: The seven-day outlook is now lunch.
GORO: Great picture. Hold it.
ZUZU: Tomorrow: more clouds, same studio.
BEX: The map is falling.
ZUZU: And today's high is one chart below normal.

[THE MAP SLIDES DOWN, THE CROW FLIES OFF WITH THE SPONSOR CARD, AND ZUZU POINTS TO AN EMPTY WALL.]
