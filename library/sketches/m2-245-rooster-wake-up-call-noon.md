# The Rooster Wake-Up Call Is at Noon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hotel concierge services that sell a personalized wake-up experience without checking what the guest requested*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Concierge Miri, a macaque; guest Nalo, a gibbon; rooster Odi, a capuchin; and manager Venn, a baboon.

INT. HOTEL LOBBY - EVENING

Nalo checks in. Miri hands over a key and a card reading "WAKE-UP CALLS AVAILABLE."

NALO: I need a call at noon.

MIRI: Noon is available.

VENN: We also offer an authentic countryside wake-up.

NALO: I sleep lightly. Please use the phone.

[ODI WALKS THROUGH THE LOBBY WEARING A SMALL BELL.]

MIRI: The rooster is part of the package.

NALO: I did not order a rooster.

ODI: I am scheduled for dawn.

INT. HOTEL HALLWAY - MORNING

Nalo's door is closed. Odi stands outside with the bell. Miri holds the wake-up schedule.

MIRI: The schedule says dawn.

NALO: The schedule is not my request.

VENN: The rooster has arrived.

NALO: It is seven in the morning.

ODI: It is the countryside hour.

[ODI COCKS. THE BELL RINGS. NALO OPENS THE DOOR WEARING A SLEEP MASK.]

NALO: I asked for noon.

MIRI: The phone call is still scheduled.

VENN: We can make the rooster quiet.

ODI: I can whisper.

INT. HOTEL CONCIERGE DESK - MID-MORNING

Miri opens the wake-up log. Nalo stands beside the desk with the key card. Venn reads the hotel's default schedule.

NALO: The rooster woke me at seven.

MIRI: The phone call is still set for noon.

VENN: The rooster is the default countryside package.

NALO: I asked for a phone call.

MIRI: I will remove the rooster from your room.

[MIRI CROSSES OUT THE ROOSTER ICON. THE CARD UNDER IT SAYS "DAWN. UPDATE TO GUEST TIME."]

VENN: The service card was not updated.

NALO: Then update it.

MIRI: I have set the call to noon and canceled the rooster visit.

ODI: I have another appointment at dawn.

VENN: The next guest requested the full package.

NALO: Make sure they know about the rooster.

[MIRI ADDS A CHECKBOX TO THE FORM: "PHONE ONLY" OR "ROOSTER VISIT."]

INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NOON

Miri calls Nalo's room. The phone rings. Odi waits beside the desk. Venn holds a sign saying "PERSONALIZED."

NALO: Hello?

MIRI: This is your noon wake-up call.

NALO: I am awake.

MIRI: Would you like the rooster now?

NALO: No.

[ODI WHISPERS A TINY COCK-A-DOODLE-DOO.]

NALO: I can hear that from here.

VENN: The experience has reached the room.

NALO: I will take the phone call.

MIRI: The phone call is complete.

[ODI PUTS HIS BELL AWAY.]

VENN: The call is complete. The room request is closed.

TAG

Nalo leaves the hotel. Miri hands over the final bill.

NALO: Was the rooster included?

MIRI: The call was included.

[ODI READS THE UPDATED FORM BEFORE LEAVING FOR DAWN.]

ODI: Does the rooster visit include a phone call?

MIRI: The visit is in person.

ODI: Then I need the phone package.
