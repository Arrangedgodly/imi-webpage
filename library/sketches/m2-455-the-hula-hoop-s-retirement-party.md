# The Hula Hoop's Retirement Party

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fitness infomercials and product demonstrations that describe a basic exercise tool as a life-changing invention*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: TRAINER, a macaque; HOST, a gibbon; RETIRING HOOP, an orangutan; ASSISTANT, a capuchin; CUSTOMER, a baboon.

[A fitness studio. A large hula hoop leans beside a banner reading "GOODBYE, OLD FRIEND."]

HOST: Today we retire the hula hoop after twenty years of service.

TRAINER: It is a plastic ring.

RETIRING HOOP: I have gone around this room many times.

CUSTOMER: Is the hoop speaking?

ASSISTANT: It is the microphone inside the ring.

[The host presents a new smart belt with lights. The trainer turns it on. It beeps when no one moves.] 

HOST: The belt detects motion.

TRAINER: It detects the lights in the studio.

[The customer holds the hoop at waist height. The belt beeps again.]

HOST: The hoop has triggered the sensor.

RETIRING HOOP: I remain relevant.

[The trainer shows a simple waist turn. The hoop rolls to the floor and circles the trainer's feet.] 

CUSTOMER: It is exercising the room.

TRAINER: It is rolling downhill. The floor slopes toward the drain.

[The assistant stops the hoop with a shoe and checks the floor. A small rubber mat is curled at the edge.] 

ASSISTANT: This is the slope.

[The trainer flattens the mat. The host tries again, but the smart belt flashes green and starts a countdown.] 

HOST: We have begun the advanced mode.

TRAINER: No one pressed advanced mode.

ASSISTANT: The belt is reacting to the hoop's reflection.

[The hoop turns away from the light. The belt stops beeping. The trainer puts it back in its box.] 

CUSTOMER: Can I use the hoop without the belt?

TRAINER: Yes. Move gently, keep space around you, and stop if it hurts.

HOST: That is not as exciting as the retirement party.

RETIRING HOOP: I would like one last spin.

[The trainer raises the hoop and sets it on the floor, then gives it a gentle push. It circles once and slows. The assistant catches it before it reaches the drain.] 

ASSISTANT: One lap. No slope.

RETIRING HOOP: I accept the retirement.

HOST: The new belt is going to the lost-and-found.

CUSTOMER: The hoop is staying for the class?

TRAINER: Yes. It still works as a hoop.

[The host folds the retirement banner. The assistant stores the belt in a drawer marked "LIGHT SENSORS." The hoop hangs on a hook at waist height.] 

The customer takes one careful turn with the hoop. It stays at waist height for two rotations, then drops to the mat. The trainer catches it before it rolls downhill. The host asks whether that counts as a workout.

TRAINER: It counts as practice.

RETIRING HOOP: I have made one last lap and met the floor.

The assistant carries it back to the hook, keeping it away from the curled mat. The smart belt remains boxed and silent.

FINAL IMAGE: The retired hoop rests on its hook while the class begins with the trainer, not the belt, leading the movement.
