# The Haunted Elevator Tour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: haunted-house tours that assign a supernatural story to every ordinary building noise*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Tour guide Sela, a gibbon; building manager Kori, a macaque; visitor Tavi, a capuchin; and elevator technician Odo, a baboon.

INT. OLD HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

Sela leads Tavi to a small elevator. Kori holds a candle-shaped flashlight. The elevator doors squeak open.

SELA: The lift has opened by itself.

KORI: I pressed the button.

TAVI: I watched you press it.

SELA: The spirit may have sensed the signal.

[ODO STEPS OUT OF THE ELEVATOR WITH A TOOL BAG.]

ODO: The door track needs oil.

INT. ELEVATOR - MOMENTS LATER

Sela points to a brass panel. Tavi stands beside the buttons. Kori watches the floor indicator.

SELA: Legend says the elevator stops on the floor that no longer exists.

KORI: This building has four floors.

TAVI: The indicator says three.

[THE ELEVATOR DINGS. THE DOORS OPEN ONTO THE THIRD FLOOR. A HOUSEKEEPER PUSHES A CART PAST.]

SELA: The lost floor has returned.

KORI: It is the third floor.

[THE DOORS CLOSE. THEY DESCEND ONE FLOOR.]

SELA: The elevator has chosen the basement.

KORI: We chose the basement button.

INT. BASEMENT HALL - NIGHT

The group steps out. A pipe taps inside the wall. Sela whispers. Odo kneels beside the panel.

SELA: Three knocks from beyond the wall.

ODO: The heating pipe expands when it warms up.

TAVI: Can it knock again?

[THE PIPE TAPS TWICE.]

SELA: A reply.

KORI: The thermostat just clicked.

[THE HEAT TURNS ON. THE PIPE KNOCKS THREE TIMES.]

TAVI: It has a rhythm.

ODO: It has a valve.

SELA: The sound is drawing us upstairs.

KORI: The stairs are outside the elevator.

ODO: I can take the lift to the lobby.

[ODO PRESSES THE LOBBY BUTTON. THE DOORS CLOSE. THEY DO NOT OPEN.]

TAVI: Are we trapped?

ODO: The doors are stuck because the inner latch is bent.

SELA: The ghost has stopped the lift.

KORI: The latch has stopped the lift.

[ODO USES A TOOL TO RELEASE THE DOOR. IT OPENS.]

TAVI: The ghost has let us out.

SELA: The spirit is telling us to leave.

KORI: The valve is telling us to tighten it.

INT. HOTEL LOBBY - LATER

Sela guides the group back. Odo oils the elevator track. Kori tests the door. It opens smoothly.

SELA: The ghost has gone quiet.

ODO: The hinge is quiet.

TAVI: Could we take the tour again?

SELA: It is more frightening now that we know the causes.

[KORI TURNS OFF THE CANDLE FLASHLIGHT. THE LOBBY LIGHTS FLICKER ON AND STAY ON.]

KORI: The motion sensor works.

SELA: It has seen us.

[ODO CHECKS THE SENSOR.]

ODO: It saw the door open.

SELA: The hotel is full of watchers.

KORI: The sensor is pointed at the elevator.

TAVI: Then it has the best seat for the tour.

[THE SENSOR CLICKS OFF. SELA LEADS EVERYONE OUT BEFORE IT CLICKS ON AGAIN.]
