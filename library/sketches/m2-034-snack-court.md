# Snack Court

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Courtroom procedural dramas and school cafeteria disputes*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Judge Poma, a mandrill; prosecutor Ledd, a macaque; defender Viri, a lemur; witness Noko, a baboon; clerk Essi, a gibbon.

[STAGE: A COURTROOM BUILT FROM CAFETERIA TABLES. A BANANA PEEL SITS IN A CLEAR EVIDENCE BAG.]

ESSI: All rise for the honorable Judge Poma, presiding over Snack Court.
POMA: Sit down. The benches are lunch trays.
LEDD: The accused, Noko, is charged with taking the last guava slice.
NOKO: I took a slice, not the last slice.
VIRI: We plead hungry.
POMA: Hunger is not a legal argument.
VIRI: It is a physical fact.
POMA: Continue.
LEDD: The evidence is this peel.
POMA: That proves someone ate a banana.
LEDD: The guava was beside it.
POMA: Proximity is not guilt.
ESSI: The cafeteria monitor is calling the case.
LEDD: I will question the witness.
VIRI: Noko, did you take the guava?
NOKO: Yes.
VIRI: Did you know it was the last slice?
NOKO: No. It was behind the pitcher.
LEDD: You admit taking it.
NOKO: I thought it was a sample.
POMA: Was there a sign saying sample?
NOKO: There was a sign saying "Try me."
POMA: That is persuasive.
LEDD: The sign referred to the new soup.
VIRI: The sign was placed under the guava bowl.
ESSI: I have the seating chart.
POMA: Why is it sticky?
ESSI: The chart was used as a placemat.
LEDD: We call the lunch monitor.
NOKO: The lunch monitor is on break.
POMA: Court will wait.
[THE COURT WAITS. THE CLOCK TICKS.]
VIRI: The monitor has returned with soup.
LEDD: Is that the new soup?
ESSI: It is yesterday's soup.
POMA: Then it cannot testify.
LEDD: I move to dismiss the guava.
POMA: The guava cannot be dismissed. It has already been eaten.
VIRI: My client offers to replace it with two plum halves.
NOKO: I have one plum.
VIRI: We can split it.
POMA: Court accepts the settlement.
LEDD: Who gets the pit?
POMA: The pit is the court's fee.
ESSI: I have placed it in evidence.
NOKO: May I go?
POMA: Yes, but return after lunch for the banana case.
VIRI: There is no banana case.
POMA: The peel is still here.
LEDD: I rest.
POMA: You can rest after you stack the trays.
ESSI: All rise.
NOKO: Do I have to rise?
POMA: The trays need room.
VIRI: The court has reached a fair verdict.
LEDD: The verdict is that nobody knows who placed the sign.
POMA: Then the sign is sentenced to a new location.
ESSI: Where should it go?
POMA: Above the soup.
NOKO: Which soup?
POMA: The one that needs trying.

LEDD: I request a recess.
POMA: For how long?
LEDD: Until the soup cools.
ESSI: It is already cold.
POMA: Then the recess has ended.
NOKO: Can the plum be admitted?
VIRI: It is the settlement.
POMA: The plum may enter the record.
[THE PLUM ROLLS UNDER THE BENCH.]
ESSI: The record has moved.

[THE SIGN IS MOVED ABOVE THE SOUP; EVERYONE LEANS FORWARD TO READ IT, BLOCKING THE EXIT.]
