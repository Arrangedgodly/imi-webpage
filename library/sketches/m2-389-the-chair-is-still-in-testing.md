# The Chair Is Still in Testing

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: endless product testing that keeps a prototype from being used for its intended purpose*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Mara, a capuchin designer; Tess, a lemur tester; Volo, a macaque manager; Fenn, a gibbon observer.

INT. DESIGN LAB - AFTERNOON

[A PROTOTYPE CHAIR SITS INSIDE A BOXED TEST AREA. TESS HOLDS A CLIPBOARD. MARA HAS A TAPE MEASURE.]

MARA: Please sit in the new chair.

TESS: The test boundary is blocking it.

VOLO: The boundary is there to protect the prototype.

FENN: From what?

VOLO: From unapproved sitting.

Mara removes one ribbon from the boundary. Fenn puts it back, saying the perimeter must remain complete until safety has tested the perimeter.

Tess fills out the form. It asks whether the chair is comfortable.

TESS: I haven't sat in it.

MARA: We can measure the seat first.

She measures it. The tape slips under the cushion and pulls it sideways.

FENN: The cushion moved.

VOLO: That is a test result.

Mara tightens the tape. The seat springs upward and knocks the clipboard from Tess's hand. The form lands on the chair.

TESS: It is now occupied by paperwork.

Fenn takes the form. The test area rule says only an authorized person may retrieve an item. He checks his badge. It has expired.

VOLO: We need a temporary badge.

Mara makes one from a blank test card. Fenn steps over the loose ribbon and catches it with his heel. The ribbon flicks against a chair leg, and the chair rolls toward him as he reaches for the paper. The wheels were designed to lock when someone sits.

MARA: They are very sensitive.

TESS: We have not sat.

The chair rolls into the boundary ribbon and stops. The ribbon wraps around one leg.

VOLO: It has passed the first mobility test.

Fenn removes the paper from the seat and places it on the clipboard. The chair moves backward. Tess catches it.

TESS: It rolls when unoccupied.

MARA: We should test it occupied.

VOLO: The boundary must come down first.

Fenn starts untying the ribbon. Mara says they need another signature before removing a safety barrier. Volo signs, but the form is in Tess's clipboard. She holds it out; the chair rolls away again.

Mara pins the clipboard to the wall. It slides down and lands on the chair. Volo retrieves it, and the chair rolls toward the wall. Fenn stops it with one foot. The form asks whether the chair moves when no one is touching it. Tess checks yes.

TESS: The test is complete.

VOLO: We haven't tested the arms.

Tess steps over the low ribbon and sits. The chair stops. She leans back. It tips a little, then settles.

TESS: It is comfortable.

MARA: And stable?

TESS: As long as someone sits in it.

Fenn tries the chair. When he stands, it rolls toward the door. The team chases it, carrying the clipboard and ribbon. It stops beside a desk.

[VOLO SIGNS THE TEST FORM ON THE CHAIR'S SEAT. TESS SITS DOWN TO KEEP IT FROM ROLLING AWAY WHILE MARA MARKS THE DESIGN APPROVED.]
