# The Emergency Contact for Everything

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: overcautious event-planning checklists that turn ordinary tasks into cascading forms and approval loops*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Miri, a macaque coordinator; Jori, a gibbon volunteer; Odo, a capuchin safety officer; and Vela, a lemur guest.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MORNING

Miri prepares a small fruit-tasting table. Odo checks a clipboard with boxes for weather, seating, fruit and emergency contact. Jori carries a bowl of guavas.

ODO: Who is the emergency contact for the guavas?

MIRI: The orchard.

ODO: The orchard is not answering.

Jori sets the bowl on the table. Odo asks for the table's backup contact. Miri points to herself. Odo asks who will contact her if she is busy. Jori volunteers. Odo asks for Jori's backup. Vela walks in and gets assigned.

VELA: I just came to taste fruit.

ODO: You are now backup for the backup.

Miri starts the event. Odo stops her to review the evacuation path. The exit sign points toward the kitchen. Jori turns it around. The arrow now points at the wall.

JORI: The exit is on the other side.

ODO: We need a confirmation form.

Miri asks Vela to confirm the exit. Vela walks through the door and returns with a guava. Odo checks the box.

ODO: The exit is confirmed.

He asks who will supervise the fruit. Miri says the bowl is on the table. Odo asks for a fruit chaperone. Jori sits beside the guavas. Vela asks if she can try one. Odo asks for a tasting authorization. Miri hands her a napkin.

Vela takes a guava. The bowl shifts. Jori catches it before it falls. Odo calls for the incident form. Miri says nothing happened. Odo points to the bowl's new location, two inches from the original mark.

MIRI: The table is not a grid.

Odo measures the distance with a ruler and records it. Vela puts the guava back. Jori asks whether the fruit has to be signed out. Odo says yes, then realizes the pen is clipped to his clipboard with a string that will not reach the bowl.

Vela holds the clipboard while he writes. The form asks for an emergency contact for the pen. Odo writes his own name. Miri takes the form and adds "Please eat the fruit" at the bottom.

The guests approach. Odo tries to brief them all at once, but each person is already holding a guava. He scans the room and sees everyone chewing safely. He marks the event successful.

ODO: No incidents.

Jori: The bowl moved two inches.

ODO: We have documented it.

Miri points to the empty bowl. Vela has eaten the final guava and is holding out the bowl for more.

VELA: Who is the emergency contact for refills?

Odo opens the clipboard. It has a form for that. Miri takes the clipboard, tears the form off, and writes ORCHARD in large letters. Jori carries the empty bowl outside. Everyone follows.
