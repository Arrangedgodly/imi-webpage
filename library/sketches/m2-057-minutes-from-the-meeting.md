# Minutes from the Meeting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: municipal committee proceedings and public-comment broadcasts*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: CHAIR PEL, a gibbon; CLERK TAVI, a macaque; RESIDENT NIB, a capuchin; and a BRANCH, which interrupts from outside.

[A town meeting. Three microphones face one chair. A sign says COMMENTS LIMITED TO TWO MINUTES.]

PEL: I call this meeting to order. The first item is whether to move the bench ten feet to the left.

NIB: Which left?

TAVI: The left indicated on the map.

NIB: The map has north at the bottom.

PEL: That is Item Two.

TAVI: We have four proposed bench positions and one bench.

PEL: Read the motion.

TAVI: "Move the bench to a location that is more accessible, less wet, and still shaded."

NIB: That is three places.

[Outside, the branch creaks loudly.]

PEL: Is that a comment from the public?

TAVI: The branch has not signed up to speak.

NIB: It is over the current bench.

[The branch falls onto the old bench with a crack. Everyone jumps.]

PEL: We will recess while staff assesses the damage.

TAVI: Should I record the branch as an attendee?

PEL: Record it as an incident.

NIB: The branch has moved the bench.

PEL: That is not on the agenda.

[The chair and clerk look at the bench now covered in leaves.]

TAVI: The motion can be withdrawn.

PEL: I would like to hear public comment first.

NIB: The public is under the table.

[The audience ducks away from the window.]

PEL: We have one speaker. Please state your name and whether you support the motion.

NIB: I support moving the bench away from the branch.

TAVI: The motion does not say branch.

PEL: Can we amend it?

TAVI: We need a second.

[Nib raises a hand. A second hand rises from under the table.]

PEL: The amendment passes.

TAVI: We also need a location.

[Nib points to a shaded patch across the path.]

PEL: There is no bench there.

NIB: There was one here.

[The branch shifts. The old bench leg snaps.]

PEL: The branch has made a second amendment.

TAVI: We cannot accept a motion from a branch.

NIB: It has more evidence than we do.

[The chair marks the map. Staff carry the broken bench away.]

PEL: The committee will purchase one replacement bench and inspect the branches first.

TAVI: That's two actions.

PEL: Put them in separate minutes.

NIB: Can the branch attend the next meeting?

PEL: Only if it registers.


[A radio reporter arrives with a microphone.]

REPORTER: Has the branch altered the proposal?

PEL: We are still in session.

TAVI: The meeting is in recess because the bench is damaged.

REPORTER: Is this a crisis for public seating?

NIB: It is a broken bench under a branch.

REPORTER: Can I quote you?

NIB: You can quote the sign. It says keep clear.

[The reporter steps around the debris. The clerk draws a new map, with the bench, path, and tree labeled plainly.]

PEL: Now the motion makes sense.

TAVI: It took one fallen branch and two pages of minutes.

NIB: The tree made its point without public comment.

[The chair closes the ledger before another leaf can land inside.]

[SFX: CLERK STAMPS A FORM. A LEAF LANDS ON IT.]
