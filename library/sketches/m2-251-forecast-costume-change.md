# The Forecast Has a Costume Change

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: local weather segments that turn tiny forecast changes into dramatic wardrobe and graphic events*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Weather host Bibi, a macaque; producer Lolo, a gibbon; camera operator Tern, a capuchin; and gardener Pavi, a baboon.

INT. LOCAL WEATHER STUDIO - MORNING

Bibi stands beside a map with a sun graphic. Lolo holds a rack of costumes: rain cape, scarf, and a tiny umbrella hat.

BIBI: Today will be mostly clear with one small cloud over the north hill.

LOLO: We need a wardrobe shift for changing conditions.

BIBI: The cloud is the size of a hat.

LOLO: Then wear the hat.

[Tern slides a rolling clothes rack into frame. The wheels squeak across the green screen.]

TERN: The radar is behind the coats.

LOLO: The audience likes to see preparation.

BIBI: They like to see the map.

[LOLO pulls the scarf off the map, revealing the cloud over the studio logo.]

LOLO: There. A dramatic reveal.

INT. STUDIO - LATER

A red banner reads STORM WATCH. Pavi enters carrying a dry flowerpot.

PAVI: I came to ask whether to water these before lunch.

LOLO: You can demonstrate the storm precautions.

PAVI: There is no storm. The cloud missed the garden.

BIBI: The breeze may turn east.

LOLO: Put on the rain cape.

[Bibi dons the cape. The hood falls over the weather map.] 

BIBI: I cannot see the wind arrow.

TERN: The camera can see your sleeve.

LOLO: Keep rolling. It looks like conditions are worsening.

PAVI: The cape is on backwards.

[Bibi turns. The cape's clear plastic window is at the back.]

BIBI: I can now see yesterday.

INT. STUDIO - MIDDAY

Lolo replaces the map with a three-dimensional cloud model. A fan turns on. The cloud rolls off its stand and bumps the rack.

LOLO: We need a live severe-weather graphic.

BIBI: It is foam.

TERN: The wind machine is at level one.

[The wind machine sends the umbrella hat tumbling. It lands on Pavi's flowerpot.]

PAVI: The plant has the warning hat.

LOLO: It may be our most prepared guest.

BIBI: We should tell viewers the actual forecast: a brief breeze, then clear skies.

LOLO: Can we say the skies are clearing dramatically?

BIBI: Only if the cloud moves.

[Tern unhooks the foam cloud. The fan blows it across the set; it catches on Lolo's costume rack and pulls the scarf over the STORM WATCH banner.]

LOLO: The storm has changed wardrobe.

BIBI: The garden stays dry.

PAVI: Good. I will water it myself.

[LOLO hands Pavi the umbrella hat. Pavi puts it on the flowerpot and carries the plant out. Bibi points to the unobstructed map.]

BIBI: Forecast: sunny after one cloud and a very small wardrobe emergency.

LOLO: Is the rain cape available for reruns?

BIBI: Only if we schedule a cloud.
