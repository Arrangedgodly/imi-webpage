# The Volcano Fair's Last Eruption

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school science fairs, overconfident project demonstrations, and presenters negotiating with unreliable machinery*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: STUDENT, a macaque; JUDGE, an orangutan; TEACHER, a baboon; CUSTODIAN, a gibbon; ANNOUNCER, a capuchin.

[A school gym. A papier-mache volcano sits on a tray. A hand-lettered sign says ERUPTION AT 2:00. The judge checks a clipboard.]

ANNOUNCER: Science fair demonstrations begin in thirty seconds.

STUDENT: Mine erupts at two. It has been waiting all morning.

JUDGE: What causes the eruption?

STUDENT: Vinegar, baking soda, and a very small funnel.

[The student pours a little vinegar into the crater. Nothing happens.]

STUDENT: That was the preview.

TEACHER: Is the baking soda in the funnel?

[The student lifts the funnel. It is full of paper stars.]

STUDENT: The stars are decoration.

CUSTODIAN: They are blocking the opening.

[The student removes the stars one by one. The judge starts a stopwatch.]

JUDGE: Is the reaction supposed to be this slow?

STUDENT: It has a dramatic build.

ACT ONE

[The student pours baking soda into the crater. A quiet fizz rises, then stops. The announcer leans toward the microphone.]

ANNOUNCER: We have a bubbling event at station four.

STUDENT: It is not bubbling now.

ANNOUNCER: The event was brief.

[The teacher checks the project board. The written hypothesis says the model will erupt when the ingredients meet.]

TEACHER: The ingredients met. They have no further plans.

STUDENT: I need more vinegar.

[The custodian rolls over a mop bucket. The student reaches for it, and the custodian pulls it away.]

CUSTODIAN: That is mop water.

STUDENT: It could be a larger test.

CUSTODIAN: It could be a wet floor.

[The judge points to a red light on the wall. The student has plugged a tiny pump into the same power strip as the display.]

JUDGE: What does the red light mean?

CUSTODIAN: The outlet is overloaded.

[The custodian unplugs the pump. The display goes dark, then the paper stars fall from the cone into the tray.]

STUDENT: My volcano has erupted confetti.

TEACHER: That was the prop shelf above it.

ACT TWO

[The student removes the pump, clears the display, and measures a small fresh mix in a paper cup. The teacher supervises. The judge resets the timer.]

STUDENT: This time I will use the recipe as written.

[The student adds the ingredients. Foam rises gently over the rim and runs into the tray. The judge marks the board.]

JUDGE: Successful reaction. Small eruption, safe tray.

STUDENT: Can I call it a quiet volcano?

ANNOUNCER: The quiet volcano is now accepting questions.

[The custodian wipes the tray. A single paper star floats on top of the foam.]

CUSTODIAN: That one can stay until judging is over.

FINAL IMAGE: The student poses beside the foaming volcano while the judge gives the floating star its own score box.
