# The Travel Host Has No Map

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: travel television conventions performed as a live sketch with a lost host*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: HOST DARA, a macaque; LOCAL GUIDE PEL, a capuchin; CAMERA OPERATOR NIX, a gibbon; and a passing troop.

[An outdoor set. Dara holds a blank map upside down. Nix frames a large tree.] 

DARA: Welcome to Hidden Fern Hollow, the destination that guidebooks have somehow failed to mention.

PEL: It is on the sign behind you.

DARA: We do not want to spoil the reveal.

NIX: The sign says "Fern Hollow, population 42."

DARA: Cut the sign. We need a sense of discovery.

PEL: The trail begins at the sign.

DARA: I will lead.

[HE WALKS IN A CIRCLE AND RETURNS TO THE SIGN.] 

DARA: We have found an ancient marker.

PEL: That is the welcome sign.

DARA: Before we move on, tell us where the trail leads.

PEL: It loops behind the spring and returns to the entrance.

DARA: Can we call it an undiscovered path?

PEL: No. People use it every day.

NIX: The camera is still rolling. We could film the locals walking it.

DARA: Only if we ask first.

PEL: They are carrying fruit. They are going home.

DARA: Could one of them tell us what the view looks like?

PEL: The view is mostly trees.

DARA: Let's ask what they like about the path.

[THE PASSING TROOP PAUSES. A MONKEY POINTS TO A SHADED TURN.] 

TROOP MEMBER: The shade stays here longest in the afternoon.

DARA: That is useful. The path has a favorite time.

PEL: The shade does not have preferences.

TROOP MEMBER: I do. I walk here when it is warm.

NIX: The camera battery is back on for one minute.

DARA: We can film the walk without asking anyone to pretend it is a discovery.

PEL: Start at the sign, follow the trail, and stop at the spring.

DARA: That sounds like a route.

[THEY WALK. A LOW BRANCH RUSTLES AS DARA DUCKS.] 

DARA: The branch is still low.

PEL: It has not moved since your first take.

DARA: Then at least the landmark is consistent.

NIX: The battery is out again.

DARA: Perfect. We have the route and no footage of us taking credit for it.

DARA: The local culture is rich in repetition.

PEL: It is a short path. Walk this way.

[THEY WALK. A BRANCH LOWERS ACROSS THE TRAIL.] 

DARA: A natural gateway. Do visitors bow before crossing?

PEL: They duck.

DARA: A custom with deep roots.

PEL: The branch is low.

NIX: Camera battery is low too.

DARA: We will capture a sunrise shot.

PEL: It is afternoon.

DARA: Then we will call it an afternoon sunrise.

[THE TROOP GATHERS NEAR A WATER DISH.] 

DARA: The residents are preparing a feast.

PEL: They are drinking water.

DARA: A simple meal with liquid ingredients.

PEL: Would you like a cup?

DARA: I would like to try the local experience.

[PEL OFFERS A CUP. DARA TURNS TO CAMERA, SPILLS A LITTLE WATER.] 

DARA: The water is crisp, with notes of stone and cup.

PEL: It came from the spring.

DARA: Tell us what makes this place special.

PEL: The trees provide shade. The path is easy to follow. People share the spring.

DARA: Can you say that with more mystery?

PEL: No.

DARA: Then we will follow the path without pretending to discover it.

[THE GROUP WALKS TO A SHADY CLEARING. DARA TURNS THE MAP RIGHT SIDE UP.] 

DARA: It shows us here.

PEL: You have been holding it upside down.

DARA: It is a local orientation technique.

NIX: We are out of battery.

DARA: The episode has reached its natural conclusion.

PEL: The path loops back to the sign.

DARA: Then our journey ends where it began, exactly as the map predicted.

[THEY ARRIVE AT THE SIGN. DARA READS IT THIS TIME.] 

DARA: Fern Hollow. I recommend visiting without a camera crew.
