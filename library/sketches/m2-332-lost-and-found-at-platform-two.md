# Lost and Found at Platform Two

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: transit authority lost-property procedures that require an item to identify its owner*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Clerk Kito, a macaque; passenger Leni, a gibbon; inspector Odo, a baboon; passenger Bex, a lemur.

INT. STATION OFFICE - DAY

Leni reports a missing hat. Kito opens a drawer of hats.

KITO: Can you describe it?

LENI: Brown, with a green leaf pinned to the side.

Odo pulls out a hat with a green leaf. Leni says it might be hers. Kito asks whether the leaf is attached with a pin. It is held on by a twig.

ODO: We need proof of ownership.

Leni says the hat has a bite mark in the brim. Odo finds one with a bite mark. Kito asks if the bite mark is registered. Leni says no.

Kito gives her a claim form. It asks for the hat's serial number. Leni says it is woven from straw. Odo says straw hats still need numbers under transit policy.

Bex enters carrying a hat.

BEX: I found this on the train. It has a green leaf and a bite mark. Leni recognizes the twig she used to pin the leaf. The passenger says the hat was on the seat beside him.

Kito asks Leni to identify the bite mark. She says she bit it when she was holding the brim while climbing aboard. Odo asks if there were witnesses. The passenger points to the conductor. Kito calls the conductor on the station phone.

The conductor confirms Leni wore a hat when she boarded, but cannot confirm the mark. Odo says the form requires a signature from someone who saw the hat before it was lost. Leni says she saw it herself.

KITO: Claimants cannot witness their own property.

Leni takes the hat and puts it on. It fits. Odo checks the rulebook. Kito says the owner should still fill out the form.

Kito puts the hat on a scale. The transit policy says found hats are weighed and logged. It weighs less than a sandwich. Odo asks whether the weight proves anything. Kito says it confirms the hat exists. Leni looks through the drawer and finds a second brown hat with a green leaf, but its brim has no bite mark. Odo asks if she has worn both hats. Leni says she bought one at the market and borrowed the other from her cousin. The hat on her head has a twig pin, not a metal one. The passenger remembers seeing her leave with the hat. Kito compares the leaf, the twig and the bite mark. The details match Leni's account. Odo reluctantly signs the claim form, but asks her to fill in the serial number anyway. Leni writes "woven straw" in the box.

TAG

Leni writes "hat" in the description box.

KITO: That is not enough detail.

LENI: It is now on my head.
