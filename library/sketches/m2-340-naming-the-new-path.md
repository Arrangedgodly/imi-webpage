# Naming the New Path

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public naming contests that turn a practical sign into a popularity campaign*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: organizer Odo, a baboon; judge Pava, a gibbon; entrant Nara, a macaque; entrant Tavi, a capuchin.

EXT. GARDEN PATH - DAY

A signpost stands at a fork. Odo holds a ballot box.

ODO: We need a name for this path.

NARA: It goes to the orchard.

TAVI: I submitted "The Grand Passage of Fruit."

PAVA: I submitted "Path."

Odo says the public has voted. The winning name is "The Grand Passage of Fruit," by one vote. Pava asks how many ballots were cast. Odo says two.

Nara points out that the path also leads to the water pump. Tavi says that can be a subtitle. Odo writes the subtitle in small letters. It does not fit.

Pava asks if they can use a shorter sign. Odo says the naming contest is already online. Tavi reads comments from the voting page: one person thought the path led to a fruit festival; another thought it was a new kind of dessert. Nara says the path is just a route.

Odo says changing the name after the contest would disappoint voters. Pava proposes a test: ask walkers where they think the path goes. A monkey arrives and asks for the water pump. Nara points down the path. Another asks where the orchard is. Tavi points down the same path.

Odo says the sign needs two arrows. He adds one for fruit and one for water. The arrows point in opposite directions because the destinations share the same route.

PAVA: They are both right.

Odo asks whether the sign should say "The Grand Passage of Fruit and Water." It is too long. Nara suggests "Orchard and Pump." Pava likes it. Tavi asks if that honors the vote. Odo says it honors the destinations.

The contest ballot box is full of paper. Odo says he should count again. Pava says the two votes were clear. Tavi says the online poll had four votes, but one person voted twice. Nara asks whether the sign must wait for a recount. Odo walks down the path and returns carrying a bucket from the pump.

Nara writes the destinations in large letters. Tavi removes the online contest banner from the post. Odo says the voters may ask why the winning name is gone. Pava says they can explain the sign is meant to help people find the orchard and pump. A walker reads it, takes the right path, and waves. Odo marks the contest complete.

Pava carries the old sign to the shed. It can be reused on another path.

Odo pockets the ballot box.

The sign points east.

TAG

Odo points to the new sign: "ORCHARD / PUMP."

TAVI: It needs a subtitle.

PAVA: It is a path.
