# The Costume Designer's Pockets Are Full of Props

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: backstage costume dramas, continuity checks, and actors whose costumes collect props between takes*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: DESIGNER, a gibbon; ACTOR, a baboon; DIRECTOR, an orangutan; CONTINUITY CLERK, a macaque; RUNNER, a capuchin.

[A theater dressing room. The actor wears a long coat with six pockets. The designer checks a costume chart.]

DESIGNER: The left pocket holds the letter. The right pocket holds nothing.

ACTOR: What about the inside pocket?

DESIGNER: Nothing.

[The actor reaches inside and pulls out a silver spoon.]

CONTINUITY CLERK: That spoon belongs to the banquet scene.

DIRECTOR: Why is it in the coat?

ACTOR: The coat has been in every scene.

ACT ONE

[The continuity clerk checks the last take. The actor removes a folded map from the back pocket.]

CONTINUITY CLERK: That map appears in the forest scene.

ACTOR: It was in the coat then too.

DESIGNER: I did not put it there.

[The runner brings a tray of props. A small crown, a chalk stub, and a theater ticket are missing.]

RUNNER: The props are not on the tray.

[The actor checks the pockets. The crown is in the left sleeve, the chalk in a cuff, and the ticket under the collar.]

DIRECTOR: We cannot shoot a scene with the crown in your sleeve.

ACTOR: I did not know it was there.

CONTINUITY CLERK: It has a ticket to the matinee.

[The designer asks the actor to remove each item and place it on the marked table. The runner reads the list aloud. The actor takes out a scarf, two buttons, one dry leaf, and a small bell.]

DESIGNER: The bell is from the opening scene.

ACTOR: I thought it was part of the coat.

[The director calls for a clean take. The actor puts on the now-empty coat. It looks unusually flat.]

ACTOR: I feel underdressed.

DIRECTOR: You are wearing a full costume.

[The continuity clerk starts the scene. The actor reaches for the letter, but both pockets are empty. The designer hands it over.]

ACTOR: The letter is now external.

CONTINUITY CLERK: It is where the script says it is.

[The runner checks the costume rack before the next take. One coat sleeve is turned inside out, and a second bell drops from its cuff.]

RUNNER: We have another bell.

DESIGNER: That one is stitched to the lining.

ACT TWO

[The director calls cut. The runner checks the tray and finds that the spoon has gone missing again. Everyone looks at the actor. The actor holds up both empty hands.]

DESIGNER: Check the coat lining.

[The runner finds the spoon in a seam that was never a pocket. The designer pins the seam shut. The actor redoes the scene with the letter in the left pocket and no surprise objects.]

FINAL IMAGE: The coat hangs on a labeled hook while the spoon sits in a locked prop tray.
