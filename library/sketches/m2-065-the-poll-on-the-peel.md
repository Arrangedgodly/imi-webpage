# The Poll on the Peel

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: political debate broadcasts, instant polls, and campaign messaging*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: MODERATOR VALE, a macaque; CANDIDATE PIP, a gibbon; CANDIDATE RUMI, a capuchin; and POLLSTER NIB, a lemur.

[A debate stage. A large banana peel sits under a spotlight. Two podiums face each other.]

VALE: Tonight's question: should the town compost fruit peels in the green bin or the brown bin?

PIP: Green. It matches the sign.

RUMI: Brown. The compost pile is brown.

NIB: Our instant poll says eighty percent prefer whichever bin is closest.

VALE: That is not one of the options.

NIB: The viewers have spoken.

PIP: I support a clear label.

RUMI: I support a bin that is not behind the locked gate.

VALE: Please answer the question.

RUMI: The peel can go in the bin people can reach.

[The audience applauds. Nib checks the poll screen.]

NIB: New data says ninety percent agree with Rumi.

PIP: We just moved the gate.

VALE: The poll has changed.

NIB: The poll is live.

PIP: Can we ask how many people have composted a peel?

NIB: That isn't a preference question.

VALE: We are here to compare policies, not peel habits.

[The debate stage's audience seating is moved so everyone can see the bins. The peel rolls off its platform and stops between the two.]

RUMI: It has chosen neither.

PIP: It rolled downhill.

VALE: We need a neutral expert.

NIB: The poll says sixty percent want the peel to answer.

RUMI: The peel does not speak.

PIP: I can explain the town's compost guide.

VALE: You may speak for thirty seconds.

PIP: The guide says fruit scraps go in the green bin. The brown bin is for dry leaves. The sign is backwards.

[The audience turns to look at the sign. It is indeed backwards.]

NIB: New poll: should we flip the sign?

VALE: We don't need a poll for that.

[Rumi flips the sign. The bins are labeled correctly.]

PIP: We agree.

VALE: The debate is over.

NIB: Our poll says the debate should continue.

RUMI: The peel is in the green bin.

[The poll screen goes dark.]
NIB: The audience poll has closed.

VALE: Did it answer the question?

NIB: It says the sign should face the path.

RUMI: That is useful.

PIP: The sign was backwards.

VALE: We have reached a decision without a winner.

NIB: The poll wants a rematch.

VALE: A rematch of what?

NIB: The peel and the bins.

[The banana peel lies flat in the green bin.]

RUMI: The peel has already been sorted.

PIP: It did not need a campaign.

[The camera operator lifts the poll screen. Its battery is dead.]

NIB: The results have disappeared.

VALE: We can still read the corrected sign.

PIP: Can we go home?

VALE: The debate is over.

[The audience leaves. The peel stays in the green bin, exactly where both candidates said it belonged.]

[BLACKOUT.]
