# The Dentist's Waiting Room Has a Musical

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: backstage musicals, dramatic understudy swaps, and performers who turn an ordinary waiting room into a stage*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: RECEPTIONIST, a macaque; PATIENT, a baboon; UNDERSTUDY, a gibbon; DENTIST, an orangutan; PIANIST, a capuchin.

[A dental waiting room. A small keyboard sits beside a rack of magazines. The pianist plays a dramatic chord. The receptionist looks up from the appointment book.]

RECEPTIONIST: This is a waiting room.

PIANIST: I was asked to hold the note.

PATIENT: I have been waiting through two verses.

[The understudy enters wearing a paper crown and carrying a script.]

UNDERSTUDY: Is the lead here?

RECEPTIONIST: The dentist is in the exam room.

UNDERSTUDY: I mean the singer.

PATIENT: I am the only patient.

[The pianist plays another chord. The patient stands, holding a clipboard.]

PATIENT: I have a six o'clock appointment. I can perform until then.

ACT ONE

[The receptionist points to the waiting-room chairs. The patient steps onto one and sings a short line about the appointment book. The understudy follows with the next line, then checks the script.]

UNDERSTUDY: I have the harmony, but not the ending.

PIANIST: The ending is a chord.

RECEPTIONIST: The ending is your name being called.

[The dentist opens the door and calls the patient's name. The pianist adds a flourish. The patient pauses mid-step.]

PATIENT: Is that my cue?

DENTIST: It is your appointment.

[The understudy takes the clipboard from the patient and holds it like a prop. The pianist switches to a suspenseful melody.]

RECEPTIONIST: Please keep the clipboard at the desk.

UNDERSTUDY: I can sing the patient into the room.

DENTIST: The patient can walk in.

[The patient picks up the clipboard and heads to the exam room. The understudy begins a farewell song. The dentist closes the door gently, cutting off the final note.]

[While the patient is away, the pianist tries to resolve the chord. One key sticks. The receptionist lifts the music stand and finds a magazine coupon wedged under it. The pianist frees the key and plays a clean ending, but the understudy enters on the final note.]

UNDERSTUDY: I was waiting for my cue.

RECEPTIONIST: It arrived while you were behind the chair.

[The understudy tries a bow. The pianist plays a new chord, and the understudy freezes again.]

[The patient returns a few minutes later with an appointment card. The pianist resumes the chord where it stopped. The receptionist hands the patient a small sticker.]

PATIENT: The appointment went fine. The musical is still unresolved.

UNDERSTUDY: We have one more verse.

[The receptionist points to the next person waiting. A childlike monkey puppet sits on a chair with its mouth open. The pianist plays the same chord.]

RECEPTIONIST: It is not a singer. It is waiting for its owner.

[The owner, a capuchin, picks up the puppet and takes it toward the door. The understudy bows to the empty chair.]

FINAL IMAGE: The pianist finally resolves the chord as the waiting room empties and the receptionist turns off the stage lamp.
