# The Lost-and-Found Fashion Show

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: runway shows that transform lost property into couture while the owners wait to reclaim their items*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Nara, a macaque event host; Piri, a gibbon stylist; Tavi, a capuchin model; and Odo, a baboon lost-property clerk.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

A rack holds lost scarves, hats and one sock. Nara announces the first annual Lost-and-Found Fashion Show. Odo stands beside a sign: CLAIM BEFORE ALTERING.

NARA: Tonight, forgotten items become unforgettable.

ODO: They are still someone's items.

Piri puts a striped scarf around Tavi's shoulders. Tavi walks the runway. The scarf trails behind him and catches on the rack. The rack rolls along after him.

PIRI: A mobile collection.

Odo stops the rack. He asks the audience if anyone recognizes the scarf. A small monkey raises a hand. Piri tries to remove it, but Tavi is still wearing it.

TAVI: The owner is in the front row.

Nara switches to a knitted hat. It covers Tavi's eyes. He walks by touch and bumps into Odo's claim desk. Odo stamps the hat with a claim ticket.

NARA: A bold statement about identity.

ODO: A bold statement is not a claim form.

The next look is a single sock pinned as a shoulder cape. Piri says asymmetry is on trend. Odo recognizes the sock from his own laundry and asks for it back. Piri reluctantly returns it. Odo wears it over one hand like a glove.

Tavi models a small umbrella. It opens indoors and catches a paper program. The program lands in the donation box. Nara calls it an interactive look. Odo retrieves the program and finds a name written inside. He calls the owner forward.

The show becomes a series of claims. A resident identifies a blue scarf. Another claims a tiny cap. Piri complains the runway is emptying. Nara turns the rack around and shows the empty hangers as the finale.

NARA: The collection has been reunited.

PIRI: We have nothing left to wear.

Odo produces a plain lost-property vest with pockets for claim tickets. Piri tries it on, discovers two forgotten pencils and returns them to the writing desk. The audience applauds the practical outfit.

Tavi comes back wearing his own sweater. Nara asks him to walk once more. He turns to Odo.

TAVI: Is this mine?

ODO: It is if you can describe the missing button.

Tavi points to the cuff. Odo checks the claim list and stamps it. Tavi exits with the sweater. Piri takes the last hanger and wears it as a necklace.

Odo checks the rack one more time. A small mitten has been tucked behind the sign. He places it on the claim desk, where the owner can see it from the door. Piri removes the hanger from his neck and returns it to the rack.

ODO: That is not lost property.

PIRI: It is now a look.

Nara adds the hanger to the return rack.
