# The Lost-and-Found Orchestra

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Prestige orchestra auditions and solemn classical concert etiquette*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Conductor Maela, a chimpanzee; auditioning flutist Dori, a lemur; percussionist Kesh, a baboon; usher Nib, a squirrel monkey; patron Varo, a gorilla.

[STAGE: A CONCERT HALL. A MUSIC STAND HOLDS A GLOVE, A SPOON, AND A SINGLE SHOE. THE AUDIENCE COUGHS QUIETLY.]

MAELA: Welcome to the audition for the Lost-and-Found Orchestra. Play your instrument with dignity.
DORI: I brought my flute.
KESH: I brought the things nobody claimed.
VARO: Is that my glove?
NIB: Please wait until the interval to claim it.
MAELA: First, a scale.
DORI: Which scale?
MAELA: The musical one.
KESH: I have a bathroom scale.
MAELA: Put it down.
KESH: It makes a deep note.
DORI: It makes a number.
MAELA: Both are useful in modern music.
NIB: Please silence your belongings.
VARO: My shoe is making a sound.
KESH: It is a fine percussion shoe.
VARO: It is my only shoe.
MAELA: Then it has a solo.
DORI: May I begin?
MAELA: Begin when the hall is ready.
NIB: The hall is ready.
VARO: I am not.
MAELA: The hall has begun without you.
DORI: [PLAYS THREE CLEAR NOTES.] Was that enough?
MAELA: It was a promising opening.
KESH: [TAPS THE SPOON ON THE STAND.] I can accompany her.
MAELA: You are playing the stand.
KESH: The stand has been waiting all evening.
NIB: The glove is in tune.
VARO: How can a glove be in tune?
NIB: It fits the note.
MAELA: Next, a dramatic pause.
DORI: [WAITS.] Is this the pause?
MAELA: It is a little short.
KESH: [WAITS LONGER.] My spoon is losing interest.
VARO: I have been sitting in this pause since the doors opened.
NIB: That was the prelude.
MAELA: We will now hear the finale.
DORI: I thought I was auditioning.
MAELA: Everyone is auditioning. Even the silence.
KESH: [TAPS THE SHOE.] The shoe has a strong ending.
VARO: The shoe is leaving.
NIB: It is walking toward lost-and-found.
MAELA: Follow the tempo.
DORI: The tempo is limping.
KESH: That is the shoe's signature rhythm.
VARO: I want my glove back.
NIB: It is holding the program.
VARO: The program is upside down.
MAELA: That is the second movement.
DORI: Should I play one more note?
MAELA: Only if it resolves the shoe.
DORI: [PLAYS A LOW NOTE.] The shoe stops.
KESH: The orchestra has tuned it.
VARO: I can finally go home.
NIB: Please return your other shoe at the exit.
VARO: I have both.
NIB: The orchestra has one.
MAELA: A complete performance. We have found our missing principal instrument.
KESH: It has a heel and excellent projection.
DORI: Will there be another audition?
MAELA: After the next concert, when we lose the program.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS QUIETLY; THE SHOE SLIDES BACK ONTO THE MUSIC STAND.]
