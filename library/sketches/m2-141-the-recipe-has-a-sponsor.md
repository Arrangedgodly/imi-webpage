# The Recipe Has a Sponsor

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking demonstrations where product placement competes with the actual meal*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Chef Nemi, a capuchin; producer Vex, a baboon; assistant Loma, a macaque; taste tester Jori, a gibbon; a visiting monkey; and the camera operator.

[A COUNTER HAS FRUIT, A BOWL, AND A LARGE SPONSOR BOTTLE.]

VEX: Every shot needs the bottle label facing camera.

NEMI: I am making a fruit salad.

VEX: Use the sponsored syrup.

NEMI: The fruit is sweet.

[LOMA TURNS THE BOTTLE LABEL FORWARD.]

LOMA: The bottle is ready.

[NEMI CUTS A FIG. JUICE DRIPS ON THE LABEL.]

VEX: The label is covered.

NEMI: The fig is cut.

[VEX WIPES THE BOTTLE. THE SYRUP STICKS TO HIS HAND.]

VEX: We need a clean shot.

[JORI TAKES A PIECE OF FRUIT.]

VEX: Do not eat before the taste segment.

JORI: I am the taste tester.

VEX: We have not introduced you.

NEMI: The fruit is ready.

[THE CAMERA STARTS. NEMI ADDS THE FRUIT TO A BOWL. VEX MOVES THE BOTTLE INTO FRAME.]

VEX: Mention the syrup by name.

NEMI: The fruit is in the bowl.

VEX: The syrup is the hero.

[LOMA POURS THE SYRUP. TOO MUCH COMES OUT.]

LOMA: The bottle is very enthusiastic.

NEMI: That is not a salad. That is a puddle.

[JORI TASTES IT.]

JORI: It is sticky.

VEX: Say delicious.

JORI: It is deliciously sticky.

[VEX NODS. THE CAMERA ZOOMS ON THE BOTTLE. A MONKEY REACHES IN AND TAKES IT.]

VEX: We have lost the hero.

[THE MONKEY CLIMBS THE SHELF WITH THE BOTTLE.]

NEMI: The syrup is leaving the set.

LOMA: The label is still facing the camera.

[THE MONKEY OPENS THE BOTTLE AND LICKS THE CAP.]

VEX: Stop recording.

[JORI EATS ANOTHER FIG.]

NEMI: The food is still here.

VEX: We will reshoot with the bottle.

[THE MONKEY RETURNS THE EMPTY BOTTLE.]

LOMA: The sponsor has been consumed.

VEX: We can call it a product demonstration.


[NEMI SETS THE SYRUP PUDDLE ASIDE. LOMA BRINGS A CLEAN BOWL OF CUT FRUIT.]

NEMI: We will let this fruit speak for itself.

VEX: Can it speak with the label visible?

JORI: If it starts talking, I refuse to eat it.

[VEX HOLDS THE EMPTY BOTTLE IN FRONT OF THE FRESH BOWL. THE LABEL BLOCKS THE FIGS.]

LOMA: We cannot see the recipe.

VEX: The recipe is behind the product. That is the arrangement.

[NEMI MOVES THE BOWL LEFT. VEX MOVES THE BOTTLE LEFT. JORI TAKES A FIG FROM THE OTHER SIDE.]

JORI: Excellent. A moving privacy screen for lunch.

[VEX OPENS THE SPONSOR CONTRACT.]

VEX: The bottle is not allowed to leave the set.

[THE MONKEY TAKES THE EMPTY BOTTLE BACK TO THE SHELF AND SITS BESIDE IT.]

LOMA: It hasn't left. The bottle and its manager are both on set.

NEMI: Give the manager a fruit salad and we might get our close-up.

[LOMA OFFERS A FIG. THE MONKEY HANDS OVER THE BOTTLE CAP IN RETURN. VEX PLACES IT BESIDE THE BOWL.]

VEX: A miniature product shot. We can call it a serving suggestion.

[THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN. JORI PUTS A FIG ON THE CAP AND EATS IT.]

JORI: It tastes like fruit.

VEX: Keep rolling. At last, the sponsor has become a plate.
