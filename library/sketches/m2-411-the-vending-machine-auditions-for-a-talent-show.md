# The Vending Machine Auditions for a Talent Show

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: talent competitions that insist every contestant needs a memorable origin story*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; judge Vex, a gibbon; stagehand Pip, a capuchin; machine operator Nari, a lemur.

INT. TALENT SHOW STAGE - NIGHT

[A vending machine stands under a spotlight. A microphone is taped to its side. Nari has placed a paper number on its glass.]

MARA: Contestant seven is a vending machine.

VEX: What is its story?

NARI: It sells crackers.

VEX: Everyone has a story.

[The machine hums. A bag drops into the slot. Pip retrieves it.]

PIP: That is a strong opening.

MARA: Does it have a talent?

NARI: It can return exact change.

[Vex presses C3. The machine dispenses two crackers and a coin.]

VEX: It has range.

[The machine's compressor buzzes in a steady rhythm. Mara taps the microphone. The hum changes pitch.]

MARA: Can you sing?

NARI: It is refrigerated.

VEX: Many singers are under pressure.

[Pip rolls the machine toward the judging table. Its wheels squeak in time with the compressor. Vex leans toward the mic.]

VEX: Tell us what made you choose this path.

[Nari feeds a coin. The machine hums, then dispenses a packet of peanuts.]

PIP: It says, "I was built this way."

MARA: That's not in the lyric sheet.

VEX: What did you overcome to get here?

NARI: A delivery truck.

[She points to a dent in the side. The machine's coin return rattles.]

VEX: A difficult childhood.

NARI: It was dropped off a pallet.

[The judges exchange serious looks. Pip adjusts the microphone.]

PIP: Should it say something about its dreams?

NARI: It dreams of exact change.

[The compressor stops. A small light blinks. Nari opens the maintenance panel. It is hot inside.]

NARI: It needs a break.

VEX: The judges need a backstory.

[Pip offers the machine a paper cup of water. Nari takes it away before it can spill into the coin slot. She fans the panel with a program. The humming starts again, now slightly lower.]

MARA: The machine has changed its key.

VEX: It has matured.

[The audience claps. The vending machine lights up. Coins begin falling into the return tray, one at a time. Pip collects them in a bowl.]

NARI: It has jammed.

VEX: It is giving back to the community.

[Nari pulls a stuck candy bar from the spiral. The machine stops clanking. The judges sit up.]

MARA: A dramatic silence.

VEX: Contestant seven, we need to hear your final note.

[Nari presses the button again. The machine hums and releases one last cracker. It lands on Vex's scorecard.]

VEX: Perfect pitch.

PIP: Perfect snack.

[Mara pins a star sticker to the glass. Nari unlocks the wheels so it can roll offstage. The machine refuses to move; the brake is still set.]

FINAL BEAT: Vex gives it a standing ovation. The audience rises. The vending machine dispenses a "THANK YOU" chocolate bar, which falls directly into its own empty delivery slot.
