# The Jury Cannot Agree on the Donut

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: courtroom deliberation dramas, dramatic evidence reveals, and jurors treating a small case like a national crisis*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: FOREPERSON, a baboon; JUROR TWO, a macaque; JUROR THREE, an orangutan; BAILIFF, a gibbon; CLERK, a capuchin.

[A jury room. A half-eaten donut sits in a clear evidence bag. The foreperson holds a note that reads WHO TOOK THE LAST DONUT?]

FOREPERSON: We are here to reach a verdict.

JUROR TWO: We are here because one donut is missing.

JUROR THREE: It is not missing. Half of it is right there.

[The foreperson turns the bag. A bite mark faces the jury. The clerk places a magnifying glass on the table.]

CLERK: The court has authorized one magnifying glass.

JUROR TWO: The bite is too neat for the bailiff.

BAILIFF: I have an ordinary bite.

FOREPERSON: Can you demonstrate?

BAILIFF: No. I am not biting evidence.

[The jury leans in. The bag crinkles and the donut slides to the bottom.]

JUROR THREE: It is moving.

FOREPERSON: The evidence has shifted.

JUROR TWO: Maybe the table is tilted.

[The bailiff places a folded napkin under one table leg. The evidence bag stops sliding.]

ACT ONE

[The foreperson writes POSSIBLE TILT on the whiteboard. Juror Two studies the napkin and finds a smear of frosting.]

JUROR TWO: The napkin was in the break room.

BAILIFF: The break room is across the hall.

JUROR THREE: That is a long walk for frosting.

CLERK: The court will allow one witness.

[The bailiff opens the door. A vending machine sits in the corridor with an empty donut spiral.]

BAILIFF: The machine has no witness statement.

FOREPERSON: It has motive. It sells snacks.

JUROR TWO: A machine cannot chew.

[The clerk turns the bag over. A paper receipt is stuck beneath it. The receipt lists two donuts, but the handwritten note says one was eaten before the hearing.]

CLERK: The receipt came from the clerk's desk.

JUROR THREE: Did you eat the other donut?

CLERK: I ate a bagel. The crumbs are from lunch.

FOREPERSON: There are no bagel crumbs in evidence.

[The bailiff points to a blue crumb on the table. Everyone watches the crumb.]

BAILIFF: That might be a crumb.

JUROR TWO: It is a button from your uniform.

[The bailiff checks his sleeve. A button is missing. The crumb is indeed blue plastic.]

ACT TWO

[The foreperson asks each juror to vote. Juror Two votes for "unknown." Juror Three votes for "someone with frosting." The foreperson looks at the half donut.]

FOREPERSON: We cannot prove who ate the other half. We can decide the remaining piece is evidence no longer.

CLERK: The court says it must be returned to its owner.

[The clerk flips the receipt. On the back, the break-room sign-up sheet lists Juror Three's name beside "one donut, please."]

JUROR THREE: I ordered it. I took one bite before court, then put it down.

FOREPERSON: You were both the owner and the witness.

JUROR THREE: I am also hungry.

[The foreperson marks the case dismissed. The bailiff removes the plastic bag and gives the donut half back to Juror Three, who puts it on a napkin.]

FINAL IMAGE: The jury exits in a line while the foreperson stays behind to inspect the napkin under the tilted table leg.
