# The Wedding Planner Rehearses the Wrong Vows

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wedding rehearsals that treat every cue as sacred even when the cue cards belong to another couple*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: PLANNER RIKO, a macaque; PARTNER VELA, a lemur; PARTNER PIP, a capuchin; OFFICIANT NARA, an orangutan; USHER TAVI, a gibbon.

[A rehearsal room with two chairs, a paper arch and a basket of cue cards. Riko checks a clipboard. Pip and Vela stand together.]

RIKO: When I nod, begin your vows.

NARA: I haven't begun the ceremony.

ACT ONE

[Riko says he is rehearsing the schedule, not the words. Tavi hands Nara a stack of cards labeled CEREMONY. Nara opens one. It begins: "Dear friends, please take your seats."]

NARA: These are ushers' instructions.

TAVI: I was told to hand out the vows.

[Pip takes a card from Riko. It says "You may now be seated." Vela reads hers: "Please rise." They sit and stand at the same time.]

RIKO: Excellent timing.

VELA: We haven't made a promise.

[Pip asks for their real vows. Riko says the ring exchange should be tested first. Tavi presents a ring box; it contains a seating chart rolled into a circle.]

ACT TWO

[Nara looks in the box and finds the actual rings in a side pocket. Riko asks Tavi to mark which ring goes to whom. Tavi labels both cards PIP. Vela switches the cards. Now both say VELA.]

PIP: The rings are right.

VELA: The names have merged.

[Nara gives each ring back to the partner who brought it. They place them on the small table. Riko calls for the music cue; the speaker plays an instrumental version of the seating announcement. Everyone looks toward the door, expecting guests.]

TAVI: The guests are still at home.

[Riko crosses out the cue and asks for a quiet practice. Nara reads the opening line, and Pip and Vela walk forward together. The paper arch collapses. Tavi holds it upright while Riko checks whether the aisle is wide enough for two people. Vela says they want to walk side by side. Riko widens the tape marks.]

ACT THREE

[Nara asks Pip and Vela what they want the ceremony to sound like. They want to speak their vows themselves without the planner supplying lines. Riko sets the clipboard down. Pip and Vela take their places, then exchange the rings after Nara's cue. The rings fit. Tavi removes the wrong cue cards from the table and replaces them with a simple order: welcome, vows, rings, recessional.]

RIKO: What happens after the recessional?

VELA: We go eat.

PIP: The cake is at the other end of the hall.

[The speaker finally plays the correct short processional. Riko nods too early, but Nara keeps reading until the music reaches its proper cue. Pip and Vela begin their walk together. Tavi lifts the paper arch to keep it from falling. Riko reaches to help, then remembers to stay out of their path. They arrive at the front with the arch still standing.]

[Nara asks whether they want to practice their own words once. Pip says he will promise to share the last piece of cake. Vela promises to save him the corner slice. Tavi checks the seating chart; it remains a ring in the box. He flattens it and places it under the arch as a brace. The arch holds.]

FINAL IMAGE: Pip and Vela walk through the paper arch side by side as the planner finally stops checking the clipboard.
