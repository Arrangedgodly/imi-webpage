# The Recipe Video Starts With a Two-Hour Story

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: recipe videos that delay cooking with long personal introductions and unnecessary cinematic detours*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Food host Nima, a capuchin; editor Ralo, a gibbon; viewer Kessa, a macaque; and kitchen assistant Tavi, a baboon.

INT. SMALL KITCHEN STUDIO - DAY

Nima faces the camera beside a bowl of oats. Ralo holds a slate. Tavi waits with a measuring cup.

NIMA: Today we are making a quick bowl of oatmeal.

RALO: Start with your childhood memory of a mountain morning.

NIMA: I grew up in a city.

RALO: We can use stock footage.

[The monitor shows a snowy mountain. Nima's bowl sits on the counter beside a window with traffic outside.]

KESSA: I came for the recipe.

RALO: First, a slow pan across the ingredients.

[The camera pans so slowly that Tavi's arm goes numb holding the oats. A single oat rolls out of the bowl and across the counter.]

TAVI: We lost one.

INT. KITCHEN STUDIO - LATER

Nima begins again. Ralo cues a montage of chopping, stirring, and steaming.

NIMA: Put half a cup of oats in a pot.

RALO: Can we get a close-up of the emotional journey?

KESSA: It is a cup.

[The close-up shows Tavi pouring oats. The camera zooms in until the frame contains only one oat.]

TAVI: This one has a strong backstory.

NIMA: Add one cup of water and simmer.

RALO: We need a dramatic reveal.

[He throws a sheet over the pot, then lifts it. Steam fogs the lens completely.]

KESSA: I cannot see the instructions.

NIMA: There are only two.

INT. KITCHEN STUDIO - AFTERNOON

Ralo checks the edit timeline. It is two hours long. The oatmeal is ready, but the camera is still recording Nima's introduction.

NIMA: The oats are soft. Add a little salt.

RALO: We need a sponsor break before the topping.

KESSA: I have been waiting since the mountain.

TAVI: The oatmeal is cooling.

[The monitor shows an animated mountain sunrise again. The studio light changes to orange.]

RALO: It is morning somewhere.

KESSA: Not in this kitchen.

NIMA: We can cut to the finished bowl.

RALO: The reveal needs a final shot.

[Nima places the bowl down. Ralo rotates it toward the camera. The spoon spins around and lands handle-first in the oatmeal.]

TAVI: That is the garnish.

INT. KITCHEN STUDIO - FINAL TAKE

Nima faces the camera without music or montage.

NIMA: Combine oats and water in a pot. Simmer until soft. Add salt and any topping you like.

KESSA: Finally.

RALO: This is just thirty seconds.

NIMA: The food is ready in ten minutes. The video should be shorter than breakfast.

[The camera widens. Tavi has eaten the stray oat, the bowl, and the sponsor's decorative mountain pinecone is sitting in the oatmeal.]

TAVI: I thought it was a topping.

KESSA: At least the recipe arrived before the landscape.
