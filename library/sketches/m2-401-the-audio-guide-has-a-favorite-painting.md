# The Audio Guide Has a Favorite Painting

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio guides that turn a short visit into a mandatory journey*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Curator Mara, a macaque; guide Pip, a capuchin; visitor Nari, a lemur; audio guide, a box worn by Vex, an orangutan.

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - AFTERNOON

[A row of paintings hangs on the wall. Pip hands Nari a headset. Vex wears a wooden audio-guide box with a speaker grille. The box's long cable runs through a safety loop clipped to Mara's curator belt, then trails across the floor toward Nari.]

PIP: The tour is twelve minutes.

NARI: Which painting should I look at?

PIP: The one in front of you.

[The speaker crackles.]

AUDIO GUIDE: Welcome. Before proceeding, please admire the frame.

NARI: I came for the painting.

AUDIO GUIDE: The frame was carved by an artisan whose name is not on the label. Please imagine the paperwork.

[Mara starts walking to the next painting. Vex follows her with the box.]

AUDIO GUIDE: Do not leave the frame.

VEX: The guide wants you to stay.

MARA: The guide is attached to you.

[The cable snags under a low floor cleat beside the bench. Vex stops; the taut cable pulls the belt loop and tugs Mara backward. She bumps the frame, and the painting swings on its hook.]

AUDIO GUIDE: Observe the motion. This work captures instability.

NARI: It is crooked now.

MARA: We should straighten it.

AUDIO GUIDE: No touching the art.

[Mara holds up both hands. Pip moves the bench, lifting the snagged cable clear of the floor cleat. With the line slack, Mara unclips the loop from her belt. Pip disconnects the cable at the guide box and coils it; Vex holds the box steady.]

PIP: The tour is at minute nine.

NARI: We have seen one painting.

AUDIO GUIDE: We have discussed its frame, wall, and emotional distance.

[Vex turns to leave. The box speaker faces the next gallery.]

AUDIO GUIDE: We will now retrace our route to locate the beginning.

VEX: I didn't press anything.

MARA: The guide has a favorite painting.

NARI: Which one?

MARA: The first one. We keep going back to it.

[The speaker plays a wireless chime. The tether remains disconnected and coiled; Vex keeps the box balanced with his hands.]

AUDIO GUIDE: Your tour is complete. Please return the device where you found it.

PIP: It found Vex.

[Vex steps toward the checkout desk. With the tether disconnected, the box stays in his hands and nothing pulls on Mara. The headset gives one more wireless chime, making him turn toward the painting.]

VEX: It wants one last look.

NARI: It wants to stay here.

[The painting swings slightly. Mara reaches to fix it, then stops before touching the frame. Pip rotates the label instead so its text is level.]

AUDIO GUIDE: Thank you for choosing the western wall.

MARA: We didn't.

[Nari tries the headset again beside the bench. She pivots in place to face the painting. Vex swivels the box speaker toward her, like a very attentive docent.]

NARI: Is the tour always this personal?

VEX: The device has a strong attachment.

[The guide falls silent. Nari removes the headset. Vex takes off the box and sets it on the empty bench.]

NARI: Can I see the painting now?

MARA: We have eight seconds before the next tour.

FINAL BEAT: The speaker wakes on the bench and whispers, "Please admire the bench." Vex turns it toward the painting. The bench squeaks as if it objects.
