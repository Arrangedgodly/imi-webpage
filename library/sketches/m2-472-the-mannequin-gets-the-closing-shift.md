# The Mannequin Gets the Closing Shift

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: retail makeover shows and fashion mannequins treated as demanding celebrity clients*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: STYLIST, a macaque; MANAGER, a baboon; MANNEQUIN, a gibbon; STOCK CLERK, an orangutan; CUSTOMER, a capuchin.

[A clothing shop after closing. A mannequin wears a coat and a tiny paper crown. The stylist circles it with a measuring tape.]

STYLIST: The client wants a more relaxed silhouette.

MANAGER: The client is a mannequin.

STYLIST: It has not approved the shoulders.

STOCK CLERK: It has not moved since Tuesday.

[The stylist rotates the mannequin's arm. It tips forward; the stock clerk catches the stand before it falls.]

STYLIST: It is giving a strong editorial lean.

MANAGER: It is giving a loose bolt.

[The customer knocks at the locked door. The manager opens it a crack.]

CUSTOMER: I left a scarf inside.

MANAGER: We are closed.

CUSTOMER: It is wrapped around the mannequin's elbow.

[The stylist peers at the mannequin. The scarf is indeed looped around its elbow, holding the raised arm in place.]

STYLIST: That is the most useful accessory in the collection.

MANAGER: Retrieve it, then secure the arm.

[The stock clerk steadies the base while the stylist unwraps the scarf and returns it to the customer through the door. Without it, the mannequin's arm drops to its side.]

CUSTOMER: Thank you. It is mine.

STYLIST: It had completed the look.

MANAGER: It had completed the customer's scarf.

[The stylist selects a proper display pin and fastens the coat's lapel. The stock clerk tightens the base bolt with a short wrench. The mannequin now stands upright without the scarf.]

STOCK CLERK: Stable.

STYLIST: The client is ready for the window.

MANAGER: The window is locked until opening.

STYLIST: We can do a reveal inside.

[The manager turns on the display lights. The customer watches from outside as the stylist slowly pulls a curtain across the empty window.]

CUSTOMER: I cannot see the outfit.

STYLIST: That is the before shot.

MANAGER: Open the curtain.

[The curtain moves aside. The mannequin's new look is a coat with its sleeves even and crown removed. The crown had been pinning the coat's tag to its head.]

CUSTOMER: The tag says the coat is on sale.

MANAGER: The mannequin is not.

[The stylist sets the crown on a shelf and labels it "PROP." The customer takes the scarf home.]

[The stylist wheels the mannequin toward the window display. The manager asks for a final look from the shop entrance. The stock clerk checks that the base bolt remains tight.]

MANAGER: Does the coat read from the sidewalk?

STYLIST: It reads as a coat.

STOCK CLERK: It reads as being safely attached to a stable form.

[The customer walks by outside and sees the scarf-free mannequin. They wave through the glass. The mannequin's arm stays down; it no longer appears to be reaching for the customer's scarf.]

CUSTOMER: That looks better.

STYLIST: The client approves.

MANAGER: The client did not move.

STYLIST: The customer approved.

[The manager turns the price card so it faces the sidewalk. The stylist tries to add the paper crown back, but the manager points to the shelf labeled PROP. The stylist places it there.]

FINAL IMAGE: The mannequin stands securely in its coat beneath the open curtain, while the reclaimed scarf disappears down the sidewalk.
