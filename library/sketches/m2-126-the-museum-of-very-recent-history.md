# The Museum of Very Recent History

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum docents and grand historical framing applied to ordinary objects from yesterday*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Docent Luma, a macaque; curator Fen, a baboon; visitor Pella, a gibbon; and guard Simo, a capuchin.

[THE GALLERY HAS A ROPE AROUND A SINGLE MUDDY BOOT.]

LUMA: This boot changed the course of village life.

PELLA: I wore it yesterday.

FEN: It crossed the wet path before the bridge was repaired.

SIMO: Please do not step past the rope.

[PELLA LOOKS AT THE BOOT.]

PELLA: It is still muddy.

LUMA: The mud is part of its context.

FEN: The context is that the path was muddy.

LUMA: We have prepared a dramatic audio guide.

[THE AUDIO GUIDE PLAYS A THUNDERCLAP.]

PELLA: It rained.

LUMA: The storm was a turning point.

SIMO: The speaker is too loud.

[FEN TURNS IT DOWN. A SECOND EXHIBIT SHOWS A PAPER RECEIPT.]

LUMA: This receipt records the purchase of a new bridge plank.

PELLA: I bought the plank.

FEN: You did not. The village used the shared fund.

PELLA: I carried it.

[THE DOCENT POINTS TO A MAP WITH A LINE THROUGH THE PATH.]

LUMA: This route was closed for two hours.

PELLA: I took the other route.

LUMA: The archive contains no record of that route.

SIMO: There is a muddy track behind the display.

[THEY LOOK. A FRESH TRACK LEADS FROM THE DOOR TO THE BOOT.]

FEN: The visitor has added an exhibit.

PELLA: I came through the garden.

LUMA: We should label the track.

SIMO: It will disappear when it dries.

LUMA: Then the label should be temporary.

[FEN WRITES ON A CARD: "MUD FROM TODAY."]

PELLA: That is not history yet.

LUMA: It will be by tomorrow.

[A LITTLE MONKEY TAKES THE PAPER RECEIPT AND FOLDS IT INTO A BOAT.]

SIMO: The artifact is moving.

FEN: It is a copy.

[THE MONKEY FLOATS IT IN A WATER BOWL.]

LUMA: We have discovered the receipt's second life.

PELLA: It is going to the other route.


[THE VISITORS MOVE TO THE NEXT DISPLAY. IT IS AN EMPTY FRAME.]

LUMA: This is the space where tomorrow's history will appear.

PELLA: It is empty.

FEN: The display was cleaned this morning.

SIMO: We should put up a sign.

[THE MONKEY RETURNS THE PAPER BOAT, NOW DRY, AND SETS IT INSIDE THE FRAME.]

LUMA: We have a new artifact.

PELLA: It is yesterday's receipt.

FEN: Its history has doubled.
SIMO: We will label it "temporary."

LUMA: Until tomorrow.


[SIMO MOVES THE ROPE BACK AROUND THE FRAME.]


LUMA: It will be part of the gallery until someone cleans it.


PELLA: The receipt will be part of the story.


[SIMO SWEEPS THE MUD FROM THE FLOOR. LUMA ADDS ONE MORE TEMPORARY LABEL.]


SIMO: The mud is gone.

[THE BOAT SINKS. LUMA ADDS A NEW LABEL: "UNSUCCESSFUL NAVIGATION, 4:02 PM."]
