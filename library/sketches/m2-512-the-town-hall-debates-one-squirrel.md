# The Town Hall Debates One Squirrel

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: contentious municipal town halls, public comment rules, and local disputes given outsized gravitas*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: MAYOR, an orangutan; CLERK, a macaque; RESIDENT ONE, a baboon; RESIDENT TWO, a capuchin; PARK WORKER, a gibbon.

[A community hall. A projector shows one squirrel on a bench. The mayor taps a wooden gavel.]

MAYOR: This meeting concerns the squirrel at Maple Park.

RESIDENT ONE: It stole one mitten.

RESIDENT TWO: It returned the mitten.

CLERK: The mitten was found under the bench.

PARK WORKER: The squirrel was on the bench.

[The clerk opens a thick folder. A single photo falls out.]

CLERK: There are fourteen pages of comments.

MAYOR: We will hear each speaker for two minutes.

RESIDENT ONE: I request extra time to describe the mitten.

MAYOR: Denied.

[The projector changes to a close-up of the squirrel holding a nut.]

RESIDENT TWO: That is not the mitten.

PARK WORKER: It is a different day.

ACT ONE

[The clerk reads the first comment card. It says "SQUIRRELS SHOULD USE THE PATH." The mayor asks the park worker to explain.]

PARK WORKER: The path is paved. The squirrel climbs trees.

RESIDENT ONE: Can it be directed to the path?

PARK WORKER: We have tried speaking to it.

RESIDENT TWO: What did it say?

PARK WORKER: It left.

[The mayor calls for a vote on a sign. The clerk holds up two mockups. One reads PLEASE WALK. The other reads PLEASE SQUIRREL.]

MAYOR: Which sign addresses the issue?

[The squirrel appears outside the window, climbs the sill, and sits above the meeting room. Everyone looks up.]

RESIDENT ONE: It is attending remotely.

CLERK: It did not sign in.

RESIDENT TWO: It is on the public tree.

[The squirrel drops an acorn. It lands on the projector keyboard and changes the slide to a map of the whole city.]

MAYOR: The squirrel has expanded the jurisdiction.

PARK WORKER: The keyboard is wireless.

[The clerk moves the acorn to the evidence table. The mayor asks the residents to identify what they want the city to do. Resident One holds up the mitten. Resident Two points to a small hole in it.]

RESIDENT ONE: I want the mitten returned.

RESIDENT TWO: It is returned. It has a new hole.

MAYOR: The city does not repair mittens.

ACT TWO

[The park worker sets a small box of spare mittens by the door. The clerk drafts a note to label the lost-and-found bin. The mayor closes the discussion without a new squirrel ordinance.]

CLERK: The minutes will record no policy change.

RESIDENT ONE: What about the sign?

MAYOR: We will put a sign at the lost-and-found, not the tree.

[The squirrel taps the window. The park worker opens it a crack and places the acorn outside. The squirrel takes it and leaves.]

FINAL IMAGE: The mayor hangs the mitten beside the meeting agenda while the clerk labels both "found items."
