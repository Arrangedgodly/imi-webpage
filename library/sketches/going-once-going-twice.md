# Going Once, Going Twice

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Charity auctions and high-pressure auctioneers*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[STAGE: A hotel ballroom full of primates in rented evening wear. AUCTIONEER VEE, a gibbon, stands beside a velvet-covered table holding a used office stapler. Bidders may raise a hand or a foot. A sign says "ALL PROCEEDS SUPPORT THE SNACK FUND."]

VEE: Welcome, bidders. Our first item is a stapler used by the chief executive during one quarterly report. It comes with a certificate of authenticity and three staples that may or may not be original.

BIDDER JOJO: Does it work?

VEE: It has been tested on a single sheet. We don't know how it feels about a stack.

JOJO: Five dollars.

VEE: I have five. Do I hear ten?

BIDDER PIP: Ten, if the certificate is a separate item.

VEE: It is attached to the stapler by a ribbon. The ribbon is not included.

PIP: Fifteen.

VEE: Fifteen from Pip. Do I hear twenty?

[THE ROOM IS SILENT. VEE TAPS THE GAVEL.]

VEE: Sold to Pip for fifteen. Please leave the ribbon where you found it.

[AN INTERN SLIDES A BOWL OF BANANAS ONTO THE AUCTION TABLE.] 

VEE: Our next lot is one premium office banana, donated by the executive fruit bowl. It is slightly bruised, which the donor calls "character."

JOJO: I'll bid three dollars.

VEE: Three dollars. The reserve is five.

JOJO: Then I bid five.

VEE: Five. Do I hear ten?

PIP: I bid ten and a half.

VEE: Bids must use whole dollars.

PIP: Then eleven.

VEE: Eleven dollars for a banana that will be gone in under a minute. A sound investment in your own afternoon.

JOJO: Twelve.

VEE: Twelve from Jojo. The snack fund is watching. The snack fund has asked me to mention that it is currently empty.

[THE CFO WHISPERS TO VEE.] 

VEE: I have a note from Finance. The banana cannot be sold unless the auction covers the accounting cost of selling the banana.

PIP: How much is that?

VEE: The estimate is fourteen dollars.

JOJO: Then the minimum bid is fourteen?

VEE: The minimum bid is five. The accounting cost is fourteen. These are separate numbers and we cannot discuss how they relate.

PIP: Fifteen.

JOJO: Sixteen.

VEE: Sixteen dollars. Do I hear seventeen?

[FROM THE BACK, A JANITOR RAISES A HAND.] 

JANITOR: I'll trade a clean lunch container.

VEE: We accept cash, card, and approved barter. Is the container labeled?

JANITOR: It says "not tuna."

VEE: That is not a label of contents. It is a warning.

PIP: Seventeen dollars.

JOJO: Eighteen.

VEE: Eighteen dollars. Going once. Going twice.

[AN EXECUTIVE ENTERS AND TAKES THE BANANA.] 

EXECUTIVE: Oh, is this the snack for the board meeting?

VEE: It is the final lot.

EXECUTIVE: Great. I'll take it.

JOJO: I just bid eighteen dollars.

EXECUTIVE: Put it on the company card.

VEE: The highest bidder is Jojo at eighteen. Executive, do you wish to bid nineteen?

EXECUTIVE: No, I wish to eat it.

[EXECUTIVE PEELS BANANA. EVERYONE GASPS.] 

VEE: The item is now consumed. The auction terms say the sale is final at the sound of the gavel.

JOJO: You haven't used the gavel.

VEE: I was saving it for the closing remarks.

[THUNK.]

VEE: Sold to the executive for zero dollars. Pip, collect your stapler. The ribbon is extra.

PIP: Does the stapler work?

VEE: We can auction a test sheet after the break.

[THE SNACK FUND JAR PASSES THROUGH THE ROOM. EVERYONE DROPS IN A COIN.]
