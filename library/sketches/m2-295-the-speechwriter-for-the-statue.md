# The Speechwriter for the Statue

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: civic dedication ceremonies, grand speeches for ordinary public works, and speechwriters who keep adding emotional backstory*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Speechwriter Neri, a capuchin; mayor Oda, a macaque; groundskeeper Vex, an orangutan; sculptor Palla, a gibbon; and reporter Tuli, a ring-tailed lemur.

EXT. TOWN PARK - MORNING

A new drinking fountain is covered by a cloth. Mayor Oda prepares a dedication speech. Neri holds three versions.

NERI: Version one is brief: "Here is the fountain. Please use it."

ODA: Too brief.

NERI: Version two describes the history of water in the town.

ODA: Better.

NERI: It is twelve pages.

VEX: The fountain took two days to install.

PALLA: The stone is local granite.

ODA: We need to honor its journey.

Tuli starts recording. Neri asks Palla for the fountain's inspiration. Palla says the old tap dripped on the path and people slipped. The new fountain drains into a gravel bed.

NERI: Can we call it a story of renewal?

VEX: Call it a dry path.

The ceremony begins. Oda reads the speech. After six minutes, she reaches the paragraph about the first water wheel. A visitor fills a cup from the fountain. Another washes paint from their hands. The gravel bed drains the spill.

TULI: The fountain is already being used.

ODA: Please wait until the unveiling.

A child approaches. Oda keeps reading, but Neri skips to the last page. The speech concludes with a promise to serve every visitor. The mayor pulls the cloth. It catches on the faucet and refuses to fall.

VEX: The cloth is tied to the drain line.

PALLA: I tied it so the wind wouldn't carry it away.

NERI: I can cut the ribbon.

ODA: It is not a ribbon.

Neri loosens the knot. The cloth drops, revealing the fountain. The crowd applauds. The child drinks. Oda puts down the speech.

ODA: The fountain is open. The path is dry. Thank you to the crew.

The crowd applauds louder than before. Oda asks Neri to shorten version three to one sentence. Neri crosses out ten pages.

NERI: "Here is the fountain; please use it."

ODA: That was version one.

VEX: It was also true.

Palla demonstrates the basin's drain. Tuli interviews the person who installed it. The mayor gives the twelve-page speech to Vex, who uses it to wipe a water drop off the stone.

ODA: That speech has served the town.

VEX: It has absorbed one drop.

Neri asks the mayor to give a short interview. Oda starts with the twelve-page version, then notices the visitors still using the fountain. She closes the speech and speaks without it.

ODA: We built this so people could get a drink without slipping. Please enjoy it.

Tuli asks whether the town plans another dedication. Oda points to the dry path and says there is no ceremony scheduled for it. Vex places the speech on a bench. A visitor sits on it, flattening the pages into a cushion.

NERI: That was the final version.

VEX: It has served the town twice.
