# The Retreat Has No Retreat

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate team-building retreats, trust exercises, and facilitators who turn every activity into a lesson about collaboration*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Facilitator Kori, a capuchin; manager Vex, a macaque; team member Palla, a gibbon; and team member Odo, an orangutan.

INT. CONFERENCE ROOM - DAY

A sign reads TEAM RETREAT. The room has one exit and a row of chairs. Kori stands beside a box of blindfolds.

KORI: Our first exercise is a trust fall.

VEX: Into what?

KORI: Your colleagues.

ODO: How many?

KORI: All of them.

Palla and Odo stand behind Vex. He leans back one inch, then catches himself.

KORI: Let go completely.

VEX: I am managing the fall.

PALLA: That is not trust. It is falling with a plan.

Kori pairs the team. Odo will guide Palla through a simple obstacle course while she wears a blindfold. The course is a row of chairs. Odo gives instructions: left, step, turn, pause. Palla follows, reaches the exit, and turns the door handle.

KORI: You have left the exercise area.

PALLA: The obstacle course led to the exit.

KORI: The exit is not the goal.

ODO: It is a door.

Kori brings everyone back and gives them a rope to hold as a team. They must cross an imaginary river without letting go. The river is two feet wide. Vex suggests stepping over it. Kori says the exercise requires all participants to move together.

VEX: We can all step at the same time.

They step together. The rope slackens. Kori calls this a communication challenge. Palla and Odo move the chairs to create a bridge. Vex helps steady the bridge. They cross in one step.

KORI: You have completed the activity.

VEX: We used the chairs.

KORI: You used teamwork.

ODO: We used the chairs together.

Kori asks everyone to share one insight. Vex says the exit was easy to find. Palla says the blindfold made a simple path confusing. Odo says a rope is useful if everyone agrees where it goes.

KORI: My insight is that every activity is a metaphor.

PALLA: This room has one exit.

KORI: That represents opportunity.

VEX: It represents the exit.

The group sits around the table. They decide to cancel the next trust fall and use the time to repair a loose chair. They ask Kori for tools. Kori opens the team-building box and finds a tiny screwdriver.

ODO: That is enough for the chair.

They fix the chair together. The retreat ends early. Kori asks whether the group wants to write a shared vision statement.

PALLA: We want chairs that do not wobble.

VEX: And a room with two exits.

KORI: I will write that down as a metaphor.

The facilitator hands out a survey. It asks whether the chair exercise improved team alignment. Vex writes "The chair is fixed." Palla circles "yes" and adds that the next retreat should include a screwdriver.

The team walks out through the single exit, carrying the repaired chair with them.
