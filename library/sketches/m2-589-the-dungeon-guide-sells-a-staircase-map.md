# The Dungeon Guide Sells a Staircase Map

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fantasy quest guides who sell a map for corridors that are mostly stairs*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: GUIDE ODO, a gibbon; KNIGHT PAVA, a macaque; CARTOGRAPHER NARA, an orangutan; GATEKEEPER PIP, a capuchin.

[A stone dungeon entrance. Odo holds a map with an elaborate dragon border. Pava wears armor and carries a lantern. Nara sits beside a small map stand.]

ODO: For three coins, I can guide you to the treasure.

PAVA: Is the map included?

NARA: The map is three coins.

ACT ONE

[Odo unfurls a map. It shows a winding line, a skull and a large blank space labeled STAIRS. Pava asks where the treasure is. Odo points to the blank.]

ODO: Somewhere below.

PIP: That is the stairwell.

[Nara says the map is a new edition. Odo has drawn the entrance, the stairs, and a question mark. Pava asks what happened to the rest. Odo says the old map was eaten by a dragon. Nara says the dragon was a paper shredder in the guild office.]

ACT TWO

[Pava buys the map and starts down the first staircase. Odo tells her to follow the winding line. She turns left, then finds the line leads back to the entrance.]

PAVA: This map is circular.

NARA: You turned it sideways.

[She rotates it. The line now leads down the stairs. Odo points to the next mark, a tiny sword. Pava follows it and reaches a landing with four identical doors. Pip says only one leads to the treasure room. Odo consults a separate key card.]

ODO: The sword means stairs. The cup means a door.

PAVA: What does the skull mean?

PIP: We ran out of cups.

[Odo sells her a compass add-on. It points to Nara, who is standing beside the cartographer table. Nara checks it and says the compass is decorative; there is a metal hinge in the map stand. Odo offers a premium replacement. Pava sets the compass down and listens. Behind one door, she hears coins clinking. Odo says the sound could be a bucket.]

ACT THREE

[Nara unfolds a clean sheet and asks Pava to help draw a real route. They mark the entrance, each landing, the four doors and the coin sound. Pip checks the route by walking it once. It leads to a broom closet, where a bucket of loose coins sits on a shelf.]

PAVA: The treasure is a cleaning fund.

PIP: It pays for the dungeon.

[Odo says the actual treasure chamber is another level down. Pava asks if there is a route on the map. He points to another blank. Nara takes the sheet and adds one more staircase. Pip checks the lower landing and finds a locked door. Odo says the key is in his pocket. He finds a key labeled MAP STAND. It opens the map stand, not the door.]

Nara hears the door latch click when the airflow shifts. Pava tests it and discovers it opens inward. Beyond it is a small chest holding copper coins and a plaque: VISITOR GUIDE FEES. Odo reaches for the chest, then stops when Pip points to the posted sign: GUIDE FEES SUPPORT STAIR REPAIRS. The group agrees to leave it.

[Nara marks the true route to the public viewing alcove and labels the locked door clearly. Odo offers a new map for three coins. Pava says she has already drawn one. Pip asks if Odo will buy a copy. Odo studies the accurate sheet and pays her two coins. Pava pockets one and gives the other to the dungeon stair fund.]

FINAL IMAGE: Pava and Odo descend with the hand-drawn route while the decorative dragon map points back toward the entrance.
