# The Magician's Assistant Is on Break

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: stage magic reveals, mysterious assistants, and performers who cannot begin without an offstage helper*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: MAGICIAN, a baboon; ASSISTANT, a macaque; STAGE MANAGER, an orangutan; VOLUNTEER, a gibbon; ANNOUNCER, a capuchin.

[A small theater. A cabinet with two doors sits center stage. The magician flourishes an empty scarf.]

ANNOUNCER: Prepare for an illusion beyond explanation.

MAGICIAN: My assistant will vanish from this cabinet.

STAGE MANAGER: Your assistant is not in the cabinet.

MAGICIAN: That is why it will be surprising.

[The magician opens one door. The cabinet is empty. He shuts it with a flourish.]

MAGICIAN: Assistant, take your position.

ASSISTANT: I am on break in the lobby.

MAGICIAN: The trick is scheduled.

ASSISTANT: My break is scheduled too.

[The stage manager checks the call sheet. The volunteer steps forward, eager to help.]

VOLUNTEER: I can stand in the cabinet.

STAGE MANAGER: The cabinet is a prop, not a person-sized entrance.

MAGICIAN: We can make it work with angles.

[The magician rotates the cabinet. The back is an open panel, visible to the audience. The volunteer points at it.] 

VOLUNTEER: Those angles show the backstage wall.

MAGICIAN: The wall is part of the mystery.

[The assistant enters, still wearing a break-room cup sleeve on one wrist.]

ASSISTANT: I can do the card trick. I cannot do the cabinet trick until the back is closed.

STAGE MANAGER: We have one safe illusion ready: the scarf changes color.

MAGICIAN: It requires an assistant to hold the scarf.

ASSISTANT: I can hold it from here.

[The magician presents the scarf. The assistant holds one end. The magician pulls it through a paper tube; the hidden second scarf emerges blue. The volunteer gasps.] 

VOLUNTEER: It changed.

ASSISTANT: It changed because we used two scarves.

MAGICIAN: You have revealed the secret.

STAGE MANAGER: The audience saw a color change. They did not ask for a structural inspection.

[The announcer tries to restart the grand music. The magician stops them and asks the audience to applaud the assistant's return. The assistant bows, then hands back the break-room sleeve.] 

ASSISTANT: Break complete. I will help close the cabinet after the show.

MAGICIAN: Can you vanish now?

ASSISTANT: I can leave through the door.

[The assistant exits through the visible door. The magician bows beside the cabinet, which remains open and empty.]

[The stage manager gives the magician a second paper tube. The magician puts the red scarf into one tube and pulls a blue scarf from the other, keeping both tubes visible.]

MAGICIAN: Behold, a color change without a cabinet.

VOLUNTEER: I can see both ends.

MAGICIAN: Then behold, a color change with excellent sightlines.

[The audience applauds. The assistant, now back from break, checks that the cabinet is still empty before closing its doors.]

ASSISTANT: The cabinet is ready for storage, not a person.

STAGE MANAGER: Put the scarves in the prop box after the bow.

[The magician attempts a dramatic exit through the cabinet's front door, discovers it is a cabinet, then turns and exits through the theater door. The volunteer holds the curtain.]

ANNOUNCER: The assistant has left by the door.

ASSISTANT: Correct.

FINAL IMAGE: The audience applauds the honest two-scarf trick while the magician stands beside the cabinet's plainly visible back panel.
