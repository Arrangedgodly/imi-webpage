# The Delivery Window

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: logistics tracking that updates constantly without delivering useful information*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: customer Odo, a baboon; tracking voice Miri, a parrot; clerk Leni, a gibbon; courier Pava, a macaque.

INT. DELIVERY OFFICE - DAY

Odo checks a tracking page.

MIRI: Your parcel is nearby.

ODO: How nearby?

MIRI: It is approaching the area.

Leni says the parcel is in the next room. The tracking page still says "in transit." Odo refreshes it. The status changes to "label created."

Odo asks where the parcel is. Leni says Pava has it on the cart. Pava brings in a small box. Miri's voice says "delivery expected tomorrow."

PAVA: It is here.

Miri says the parcel has reached the local facility. Pava places it on the counter. Odo asks if he can sign for it. Leni hands him a tablet. It asks him to confirm that the parcel is expected.

Odo says yes. The screen asks if he is home. He is at the delivery office. He selects "other." The system asks him to define other. He writes "here."

The box is addressed to Odo, but the street number is missing. Leni says he can identify the sender. Odo says he ordered a small cup. Pava asks him to describe the packaging. It is a small box with a picture of a cup.

Miri's voice says the delivery has been delayed due to an address issue. Odo points at himself and the box. Leni says the address is now confirmed. The tracking page changes to "out for delivery."

Pava picks up the box and walks toward Odo. Miri says the parcel is in transit again. Odo asks if they can turn off the notifications. Leni says that requires an account login. Odo uses the tablet and signs in. It asks him to verify the parcel.

Leni checks the delivery label. It has Odo's name and no apartment number, because the office is a single room. The tablet still requests a location pin. Odo drops the pin on the desk where he is standing. Miri announces that the package is now three meters away. Pava wheels the cart closer, and the distance changes to one meter. Odo asks if the system can stop narrating. Leni turns off the notifications, but the screen says "You may miss an update." Odo accepts that risk. Pava opens the box. Inside is a small cup packed in paper. Odo checks for cracks. It is intact. The tracking page updates to "delivered to recipient," then asks him to rate the delivery.

Odo signs for the package on the tablet. The tablet asks him to sign again to confirm the first signature. He draws a small cup.

TAG

Pava wheels the empty cart toward the door.

MIRI: Delivery completed.

ODO: I was here the whole time.
