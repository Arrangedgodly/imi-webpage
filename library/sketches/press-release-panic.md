# Press Release Panic

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Corporate crisis press conferences and evasive executive statements*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[STAGE: A podium with the IMI logo. CEO MARA, a baboon, faces a troop of reporters with microphones strapped to their tails. Behind her, a banner reads "WE HEAR YOUR CONCERNS." A banana peel sits under the lectern.] 

MARA: Thank you for coming. At 9:02 this morning, our office banana supply ran out. We understand that this caused confusion, frustration, and one highly visible trip in the hallway.

REPORTER LEO: Why did the company announce "unlimited bananas" if there were only twelve?

MARA: We define unlimited as a mindset.

REPORTER KIKI: Was the supply limited?

MARA: The supply was in a period of transition.

LEO: The empty bowl says "out of bananas."

MARA: We are reviewing that wording.

KIKI: Who took the last banana?

MARA: We don't want to speculate about individual fruit decisions.

LEO: The camera shows you carrying it into this room.

MARA: I was protecting it from a volatile market.

[THE REPORTERS LOOK AT THE PEEL UNDER THE PODIUM.] 

KIKI: Is that the peel?

MARA: I can't comment on active peel matters.

LEO: The banana has already been eaten.

MARA: We are aware of reports that a banana may have been consumed.

KIKI: You have the peel in your shoe.

[MARA LOOKS DOWN, DISCOVERS THE PEEL STUCK TO HER SOLE, AND KICKS IT UNDER THE PODIUM.] 

MARA: This is exactly why we launched our transparency initiative. We will post a photograph of the bowl after Facilities refills it.

LEO: When will that happen?

MARA: We are working toward a future in which photographs and fruit are aligned.

KIKI: Did you eat the last banana?

MARA: I had a banana adjacent experience.

LEO: You ate it.

MARA: I took a bite. The bite was consumed.

KIKI: By whom?

MARA: I don't have that information in front of me.

[AN AIDE WHISPERS TO MARA.] 

MARA: We can now confirm that I ate the banana. I thought it was a sample. It was labeled "final banana," but I interpreted final as a brand name.

LEO: What will you do to prevent this?

MARA: We have assigned the bowl a dedicated owner.

KIKI: Who?

MARA: The bowl.

KIKI: The bowl owns the bananas?

MARA: The bowl owns the restocking process. We have also created a cross-department group to study equitable distribution.

LEO: How many people are on it?

MARA: Eight, including the bowl.

KIKI: Can staff eat bananas while the group studies distribution?

MARA: Staff may eat bananas purchased personally, brought from home, or received through approved sharing. Company bananas will resume after the group issues guidance.

LEO: When will that be?

MARA: After the group meets.

KIKI: When does it meet?

MARA: We are waiting for the bowl's calendar access.

[A REPORTER RAISES A HAND.] 

REPORTER: I brought a bunch. Want one?

MARA: We should not accept gifts during an active review.

[THE BOWL ROLLS OFF THE TABLE, TOPPLES, AND REVEALS A DOZEN BANANAS HIDDEN INSIDE.] 

KIKI: There were bananas in the bowl the whole time.

MARA: Those are the reserve bananas. I am pleased to announce the system worked.

LEO: You hid them.

MARA: The reserve protected our supply from demand.

[THE STAFF IN THE BACK TAKE A BANANA EACH. MARA SIGHS.] 

MARA: We will have no further comment until lunch.
