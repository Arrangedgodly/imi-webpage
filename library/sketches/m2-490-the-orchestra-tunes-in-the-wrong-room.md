# The Orchestra Tunes in the Wrong Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: formal orchestra rehearsals, tuning rituals, and musicians who follow a pitch to the wrong rehearsal room*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: CONDUCTOR, an orangutan; OBOIST, a gibbon; VIOLINIST, a macaque; CUSTODIAN, a baboon; PIANIST, a capuchin.

[A school music wing. The oboist gives a tuning note in Room A. The conductor raises a baton. The violinist enters Room B with a music stand.]

CONDUCTOR: Everyone tune to the oboe.

VIOLINIST: I can hear it through the wall.

PIANIST: It sounds like it is coming from the hallway.

[The conductor gives the downbeat. The violinist and pianist play a chord. The oboist's note continues from Room A, where a different ensemble is warming up.]

CUSTODIAN: This is Room B. Your booking is in C.

[The conductor lowers the baton.]

ACT ONE

INT. ROOM B - CONTINUOUS

The custodian checks the rehearsal calendar on the door. Room B is booked for a student choir. Room C is empty until the orchestra's time. The conductor asks whether the group can stay in B for one movement. The custodian says the choir arrives in five minutes.

CONDUCTOR: We only need to tune.

CUSTODIAN: The choir is also tuning.

[The orchestra carries stands into the corridor. The violinist's tuner still points toward Room A. The oboist opens the door and explains that their original group has a louder tuning pitch.]

OBOIST: You followed my note through the wall.

VIOLINIST: It was very clear.

PIANIST: We thought the room was resonating.

[The conductor checks the booking sheet and discovers it was printed backward. Room C appears in the mirror as Room B. The custodian rotates the paper.]

CUSTODIAN: The reflection caused the mistake.

CONDUCTOR: The paper is not a mirror.

[The pianist sets the stand down and follows the custodian to Room C. The violinist retunes to the oboe's note at the correct doorway.]

ACT TWO

INT. ROOM C - LATER

The orchestra assembles in Room C. The oboist gives the tuning note. The strings tune, the pianist checks the keyboard, and the conductor listens. A ventilation fan hums at a slightly different pitch. The violinist follows the fan instead of the oboe.

VIOLINIST: My string is sharp.

OBOIST: I have not changed the note.

[The custodian turns off the fan for the rehearsal. The hum stops; the violinist adjusts the string and checks the tuner.]

PIANIST: That is the right pitch.

CONDUCTOR: We can begin.

[The conductor raises the baton. The pianist plays the first chord. A wall clock chimes. The violinist turns to the clock; the conductor holds the beat.]

CONDUCTOR: Stay with the score.

VIOLINIST: I thought that was the next note.

OBOIST: It is the school clock.

[The custodian silences the clock chime by closing the classroom door. The orchestra resets from the first bar.]

ACT THREE

INT. ROOM C - AFTERNOON

The ensemble completes the opening passage. The conductor lowers the baton, satisfied. The student choir in Room B begins tuning through the wall, but the orchestra stays on its own score. The violinist looks at the tuner, then at the conductor, and keeps playing the written line.

CONDUCTOR: We have found our room and our pitch.

OBOIST: The note next door is still good.

CUSTODIAN: It belongs to the choir's rehearsal.

[The custodian returns the backward calendar printout to the office tray. The pianist packs the keyboard cover. The violinist labels the music stand ROOM C.]

VIOLINIST: I will follow the sign next time.

CONDUCTOR: Follow the score first. The sign gets you to it.

[The orchestra exits Room C together, carrying their stands. The custodian checks the room booking sheet, now oriented correctly.]

FINAL IMAGE: The choir's tuning note sounds from Room B as the orchestra leaves Room C in silence.
