# The Cap and Gown Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school graduation pageants and ceremony logistics*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: PRINCIPAL LUMA, a lemur; GRADUATE PIP, a capuchin; STAGE MANAGER VEL, a macaque; and FAMILY MEMBERS.

[A school stage. Caps and gowns hang from a branch rack. A banner says CONGRATULATIONS, GRADUATES.]

LUMA: We will rehearse the ceremony in order. Walk up, take the certificate, turn to the audience, and hold still for the photograph.

PIP: My cap keeps sliding off.

VEL: The tassel is heavier than the cap.

PIP: It points me toward the fruit table.

LUMA: That is not the direction of the stage.

[Pip's cap falls. He catches it with his tail.]

VEL: No tail assistance in the official photo.

PIP: Then give me a bigger cap.

[Vel brings a box labeled LARGE. It contains tiny caps.]

VEL: The labels are reversed.

LUMA: We can swap the labels after the ceremony.

PIP: The ceremony is in ten minutes.

[The families enter, each carrying a different bouquet. One bouquet has a banana tucked in.]

LUMA: Please take your seats. The graduates will enter by class.

VEL: We have one class.

LUMA: Then enter by row.

[The graduates line up. Pip climbs the stage branch instead of using the stairs.]

VEL: That is not the entrance.

PIP: The stairs are blocked by the camera.

LUMA: The camera is not supposed to block the stairs.

[The camera operator moves. The stage mic squeals.]

VEL: We need the microphone for names.

PIP: My name is Pip.

[The microphone picks up only a squeak.]

LUMA: We will read the names without amplification.

VEL: The families at the back won't hear.

[Pip carries the microphone to the back row. The cord pulls the banner down.]

LUMA: Stop. We cannot hand out certificates under a falling banner.

[The families help pin it back up. The ceremony begins, without the microphone. Pip receives a certificate and looks at it.]

PIP: This is a blank page.

VEL: The printer ran out of ink.

LUMA: It is a certificate of completion.

PIP: It says nothing.

LUMA: We can write your name by hand.

[She writes. Pip's cap slides down over his eyes.]

PIP: I have graduated into darkness.

[The families laugh and clap. Vel places the banana from the bouquet on the certificate to hold it flat.]

VEL: The class picture is ready.

[Everyone looks at the banana.]


VEL: We have a revised ceremony order.

LUMA: There is only one graduate.

VEL: The microphone needs to be passed between the family rows.

PIP: It is still squealing.

[The stage manager gives up and hands Pip the certificate to read.]

PIP: "Pip has completed the climbing and snack-sharing course."

LUMA: That is the correct certificate.

PIP: I was expecting a blank page again.

VEL: The printer is fixed.

[The printer starts up and produces twenty copies.]

LUMA: We need one.

VEL: It has printed one for every family member.

[The family takes turns holding certificates. Pip's cap slips over his eyes once more.]

PIP: I can still hear the applause.

LUMA: That is the important part.

[The graduates line up for a photo. The banner droops, but Vel catches it before it falls.]

VEL: We have learned to secure the decorations.

PIP: And label the caps.

[The camera flashes. The only clear item in the photo is the banana holding the certificate down.]

[BLACKOUT.]
