# The Common Area Is a Common Area

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: homeowners association meetings, property-line disputes, and rules that multiply around a shared bench*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Board chair Vex, a gibbon; resident Luma, a macaque; resident Bex, a capuchin; and groundskeeper Odo, an orangutan.

EXT. APARTMENT COURTYARD - DAY

A bench sits under a tree. Vex holds a map with colored borders. Luma and Bex stand on either side of the bench.

VEX: The bench is in the common area.

LUMA: I put it there.

BEX: I painted it.

ODO: I fixed the loose leg.

VEX: The board must decide who owns it.

LUMA: Everyone uses it.

VEX: That is why we need a committee.

He lays the map on the bench. The wind flips it shut. Vex opens it again and places a rock on each corner. The bench now has no room to sit.

BEX: The map is sitting on the bench.

VEX: The map is not a resident.

Luma asks whether the bench can be moved away from the tree. Odo says the shade is the point. Bex says she painted it green because the tree casts a green shadow. Vex looks at the rules.

VEX: The rules say common furniture must be beige.

LUMA: The bench is brown.

BEX: The paint is green.

ODO: The wood is beige underneath.

Vex measures the bench. His tail curls around one leg and pulls it crooked. Odo straightens it. Vex asks whether the bench is now in the same position as before.

ODO: It is in the same place, but the leg is straighter.

VEX: That may require an amendment.

Bex brings a sign that says PLEASE SHARE THE BENCH. Vex objects that the sign might be attached to common property without approval. Luma points out the sign is leaning against the bench, not attached.

VEX: Then it is a temporary sign.

LUMA: How long can it be temporary?

VEX: Until the next meeting.

Odo puts the sign on the tree. The tree is also common property. Vex checks the rulebook and discovers there is no rule about signs on trees.

VEX: We will table the question.

BEX: The table is inside.

VEX: The question.

Luma sits on the bench. Bex sits beside her. Odo sits at the other end. Vex remains standing because he is still holding the map.

VEX: We need to vote on bench access.

LUMA: We are using it.

BEX: All in favor of sharing the bench?

Everyone raises a hand except Vex, who is holding the map. His tail raises the corner.

VEX: My tail is not a vote.

ODO: It is still in favor.

Vex folds the map and sits. The bench holds four primates. The board approves shared use, the green paint, the repaired leg, and one temporary sign.

VEX: We will review the sign next month.

LUMA: It will still say the same thing.

The tree drops a leaf onto the map. Vex adds it to the agenda.
