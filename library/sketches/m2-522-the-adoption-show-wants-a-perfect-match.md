# The Adoption Show Wants a Perfect Match

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: reality-dating eliminations, pet-adoption interviews, and producers who mistake an animal's preferences for a dramatic twist*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: HOST, a gibbon; ADOPTER, a macaque; PRODUCER, a baboon; SHELTER WORKER, an orangutan; PUPPY, a capuchin.

[A shelter meeting room. The host stands beside two chairs and a bowl of toys. The producer holds a card reading FINAL MATCH.]

HOST: Tonight, one adopter meets one very particular puppy.

SHELTER WORKER: The puppy is looking for a quiet home.

PRODUCER: Can we make it choose dramatically?

[The puppy walks past the adopter and curls up in the toy basket.]

ADOPTER: It chose the basket.

HOST: We have a twist.

SHELTER WORKER: The basket is not an adopter.

ACT ONE

[The producer asks the adopter to hold a squeaky toy. The adopter squeezes it. The puppy hides under the chair.]

PRODUCER: It is nervous.

SHELTER WORKER: It does not like that sound.

ADOPTER: I can put the toy away.

[The adopter sets the toy on a high shelf. The puppy comes out and sniffs their hand.]

HOST: The connection is growing.

PRODUCER: Can we add a slow-motion close-up?

[The camera operator walks backward and bumps into the toy bowl. Balls roll across the floor. The puppy chases one, the adopter crawls after another, and the host tries to keep the cards from sliding.]

HOST: The puppy has chosen the chase.

SHELTER WORKER: The puppy is playing.

[The producer reads the card.]

PRODUCER: The puppy must choose between two homes.

SHELTER WORKER: No. We are checking if the adopter can meet its needs.

[The host takes the card and flips it over. The back lists: quiet space, short walks, patient introductions.]

ADOPTER: I have a quiet room and a yard. I work from home.

SHELTER WORKER: You will need to visit a few times before taking it home.

PRODUCER: That has no elimination.

HOST: It has a process.

[The puppy trots to the adopter and drops the ball at their feet. The producer signals the camera for a close-up. The puppy carries the ball to the shelter worker instead.]

PRODUCER: The puppy is changing its mind.

SHELTER WORKER: The worker has been here longer.

ACT TWO

[The producer puts down the dramatic card. The shelter worker hands the adopter a visit schedule. The adopter kneels and waits. The puppy sits nearby, then rests its head on the adopter's shoe.]

HOST: A match is not a rose ceremony.

PRODUCER: Can we call it a first date?

SHELTER WORKER: You can call it a visit.

[The adopter signs the visit form. The puppy goes back to its basket and sleeps. The host stops the cameras and asks everyone to leave the puppy alone.]

FINAL IMAGE: The host places the unused FINAL MATCH card beside the toy basket as the puppy snores.
