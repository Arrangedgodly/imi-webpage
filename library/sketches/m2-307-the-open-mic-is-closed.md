# The Open Mic Is Closed

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: open-mic comedy nights, signup sheets, and performers who use their time explaining why they need more time*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Host Neri, a capuchin; performer Tavi, a gibbon; performer Omi, a macaque; and sound tech Luma, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. SMALL COMEDY CLUB - NIGHT

A handwritten sign reads OPEN MIC: THREE MINUTES EACH. Neri has a clipboard with twelve names.

NERI: We begin with Tavi. Your time starts when you speak.

TAVI: I have a joke about clocks.

NERI: Your time has started.

TAVI: That's the joke. The clock is already running.

Luma taps the timer. Tavi tells a long story about a clock that stops whenever someone looks at it. The timer reaches zero.

NERI: Thank you. Please leave the stage.

TAVI: I have the punchline.

NERI: Save it for your next set.

Omi walks up with a notebook. He unfolds one page, then another. The pages reach the floor.

OMI: My act is about the length of a list.

NERI: You have three minutes.

OMI: I need three minutes to find the beginning.

Luma offers to hold the pages. Omi gives her the first half, then reads from the second half. The notes are upside down. He turns the notebook around, making it difficult to see.

OMI: I have forgotten where I was.

The audience laughs. Omi looks at the timer.

OMI: That was the first joke.

Neri asks for the next performer. Tavi comes back and says he has the punchline. Neri tells him he has already used his time.

TAVI: The clock stopped.

LUMA: It did not.

TAVI: Then the joke has changed.

Neri introduces the next performer, who is absent. The audience looks toward the empty stage. Neri checks the signup list; the name is written on the back of the clipboard, where no one can see it.

NERI: We have an unscheduled open slot.

Omi raises his hand. He has finished finding the beginning but lost the ending. Tavi offers to perform the punchline. They each step toward the microphone at once.

LUMA: There is one mic.

NERI: You can share one minute.

TAVI: The punchline is: the clock stops when you look at it.

OMI: And the list is upside down.

The audience laughs. Neri rings the closing bell. The sound tech turns off the microphone, but the speakers continue playing a test tone.

NERI: The show is closed.

LUMA: The mic is off.

TAVI: Is the test tone the last performer?

Neri puts the clipboard down. It falls open to a blank signup sheet.

OMI: I will write my name on the front this time.

TAVI: I will save my punchline for next week.

NERI: Next week, three minutes each. The timer will start when you speak.

Luma switches off the speaker. The silence lasts one beat, and the audience laughs again.
