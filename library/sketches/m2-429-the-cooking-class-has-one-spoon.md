# The Cooking Class Has One Spoon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking classes where the instructor insists on a shared tool for every task*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Instructor Mara, a gibbon; students Vex, an orangutan, and Pip, a capuchin; assistant Nari, a lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - EVENING

[A counter holds bowls of flour, sugar, and berries. One wooden spoon lies on a towel. Mara faces the students.]

MARA: Tonight we make one batch of muffins.

VEX: There is one spoon.

MARA: We will share.

PIP: How?

MARA: Pass it.

[Nari pours flour into a bowl. Pip reaches for the spoon. Vex reaches at the same time. Their hands meet.]

VEX: I am measuring.

PIP: I am mixing.

MARA: First measure, then mix.

[Vex takes the spoon and taps flour into a measuring cup. Pip watches the time. The flour sticks to the spoon. Vex scrapes it with his sleeve.]

PIP: That is not food safe.

VEX: It is a sleeve.

[Nari hands him a clean towel. Vex wipes the spoon and passes it to Pip. Pip stirs the sugar, but the spoon sticks upright in the thick mixture.]

PIP: It is too much sugar.

MARA: It is a sturdy recipe.

[The class passes the spoon to Nari. She uses it to fold berries into the batter. One berry sticks to the bowl's rim. She reaches for the spoon again, but Vex has claimed it to taste the mixture.]

NARI: The spoon is for mixing.

VEX: I am testing.

MARA: Use a clean spoon.

[They all look at the towel. It is empty. Pip offers a finger, then remembers the rule.]

PIP: We have one spoon.

[Nari removes her apron pocket and finds a small measuring spoon. Mara checks it.]

MARA: That is a teaspoon.

NARI: It is a spoon.

VEX: It is a different department.

[Mara uses the big spoon to portion batter. Vex holds each muffin cup while Pip adds berries with the teaspoon. They finish the tray. The oven door is already open, but no one has turned it on.]

PIP: We have used the whole class.

MARA: Preheat next time.

[Mara reaches for the oven dial. Nari has hung the recipe card over it, and the card is now spotted with batter.]

NARI: Which temperature did you want?

MARA: The one on the recipe.

[Nari lifts the card. The dial reads OFF. Vex looks for the cord.]

VEX: It is plugged into the lights.

PIP: That explains the bright muffins.

[Nari unplugs the string lights. The kitchen goes dark. Mara closes the oven door without turning it on.]

MARA: We'll eat the batter.

VEX: Is there a spoon?

[Vex reaches for the spoon to scrape the bowl. Nari takes it first. She licks the batter off, then puts it down.]

NARI: Clean.

FINAL BEAT: Mara lifts the tray of uncooked cups. Vex plants the spoon in one muffin's batter so it will stand upright. Pip writes "one spoon included" on the recipe card.
