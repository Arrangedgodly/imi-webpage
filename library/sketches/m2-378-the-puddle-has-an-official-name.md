# The Puddle Has an Official Name

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: municipal naming commissions that add ceremony and paperwork to ordinary places*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Senn, a macaque clerk; Doro, a capuchin resident; Hale, a gibbon historian; Rika, a lemur sign painter.

EXT. TOWN PATH - MORNING

[A SMALL PUDDLE SITS IN THE CENTER OF THE WALKWAY. A TEMPORARY SIGN READS "PUDDLE NAMING CONSULTATION."]

SENN: We need a name before the maintenance crew fills it.

DORO: Why does it need a name?

HALE: So we can refer to it accurately in the drainage record.

RIKA: The sign needs a shorter name. It only has one line.

Senn reads the suggestions. "North Path Water Feature," "Small Reflection," and "Puddle Number Seven."

DORO: It's the only puddle.

HALE: There were six last spring.

Rika starts painting "Puddle Number Seven." A drop of paint falls into the puddle. The water turns blue.

SENN: We need to update the description.

DORO: It is still water with a bit of paint.

Hale consults a binder. A historical note says the path used to run four feet farther east. Senn moves the proposed sign. Now it stands beside a dry patch.

RIKA: The sign should point at the puddle.

SENN: The puddle moved?

DORO: No. The sign moved.

Hale says they can name it by the nearest landmark. A squirrel runs past carrying a nut. The puddle reflects it. Senn writes "Nut Crossing."

DORO: The squirrel crossed the path, not the puddle.

RIKA: The name needs a verb.

The maintenance crew arrives with gravel and a shovel. Senn stops them until the consultation is complete. The crew waits beside the puddle. The sun comes out, and the water begins to shrink.

HALE: We may be naming a temporary feature.

SENN: The form asks whether the landmark is permanent.

DORO: It isn't.

Rika paints a smaller sign: "WET SPOT." Senn says it lacks historic context. Hale places the binder beside it. The binder falls open to a page describing an old stone drain underneath the path.

DORO: That's the name. Stone drain.

SENN: Is there a stone drain?

The crew lifts a grate nearby. Water trickles down. The puddle shrinks as the drain clears.

RIKA: The sign can say "Drain inlet."

HALE: That is accurate.

SENN: Does the commission need to vote?

Doro points to the puddle, now only a damp outline. The crew sweeps the remaining water toward the grate.

Hale holds the binder over the damp outline to keep the pages dry. Senn asks whether the binder has become part of the landmark. Hale lifts it away, leaving a clean rectangle where the water did not reach.

SENN: The feature has disappeared before we named it.

HALE: We can name the site.

Rika flips the sign over and writes "FORMER PUDDLE." Senn stamps the consultation form, then puts the stamp away.

[THE MAINTENANCE CREW CARRIES OFF THE GRATE. A NEW SMALL PUDDLE FORMS UNDER THE SIGN. RIKA ADDS A SUBTITLE: "SUBJECT TO CHANGE."]
