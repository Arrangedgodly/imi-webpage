# The Premium Waiting Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: subscription services that invent tiers for access to ordinary benches and basic information*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Omi, a macaque receptionist; Tavi, a squirrel monkey customer; Piri, a gibbon salesperson; and a slow loris waiting-room attendant, Sela.

INT. SERVICE OFFICE - DAY

Tavi enters and asks where to wait. Omi points to a bench. Piri steps between them with a chart showing three membership levels.

PIRI: Basic includes standing nearby. Plus includes sitting. Premium includes knowing when your turn is.

TAVI: I only need to renew a library card.

PIRI: Premium moves your name to the top of the list.

Sela carries a clipboard. She tells Tavi the list is alphabetical. Piri turns the chart around and calls alphabetical order a legacy feature.

Omi asks Tavi to fill out a form. The form has a box labeled CURRENT PLAN. Tavi writes "none." Piri circles it and says the first month is free. Tavi asks what happens after the month. Piri says the bench becomes visible only to members.

Tavi sits. Piri places a small card under him that says PREMIUM SEAT. Omi looks at the card and says it is the office's only bench. Piri adds a paper crown to the seat and calls it priority seating.

A second customer arrives. Piri says the bench is reserved. Sela asks who reserved it. Piri points to Tavi's card. Tavi stands to let the customer sit. Piri says standing is not included in the Premium plan.

TAVI: Then I will cancel.

PIRI: Cancellation requires Plus.

Omi flips the form and finds the cancellation box on the back. It is blank. He gives Tavi a pencil. Piri tries to cover the box with the benefits chart. The chart slides off and lands on the bench.

Sela calls Tavi's name. Piri says the call is included with Premium. Sela shows him the clipboard: she called every name in order.

PIRI: That is a valuable benefit.

Tavi renews his card at the counter. Omi stamps it and hands it back. Piri adds an optional membership sticker. Tavi declines. Piri sticks it to the chair instead.

Sela sits on the chair. The sticker says PREMIUM ATTENDANT. Piri tells her that she now has access to the deluxe clipboard. Sela opens the clipboard; it is empty.

SELA: Where is the list?

OMI: On the basic clipboard.

Piri looks at the chart. He crosses out the three plans and writes one line: WAIT HERE.

TAVI: Is that free?

PIRI: It is included with arriving.

Sela calls the next customer. Piri sits on the bench and waits his turn. Omi hands him the same form he was selling.

PIRI: Which plan do I need?

Tavi takes the pencil and signs the cancellation line. Piri offers to upgrade the pencil to a writing instrument with a wooden grip. Omi places it back in the shared cup.

OMI: A pencil.
