# The Watercolor Class Paints a Dry River

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: art workshops that turn a simple landscape into a competitive masterclass*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Instructor Mara, a gibbon; student Pip, a capuchin; student Vex, an orangutan; groundskeeper Nari, a lemur.

EXT. PARK PAVILION - MORNING

[Three easels face a dry stone channel. Mara holds up a watercolor board.]

MARA: Today, we paint a river.

PIP: There isn't one.

VEX: That's the challenge.

[Nari walks past with a watering can.]

NARI: The irrigation is off. The river is dry.

MARA: We are painting its memory.

[Vex dips his brush into a cup. He uses the whole brush. Water runs down the paper.]

VEX: The current is strong.

PIP: The river is going through your easel.

[Mara shows a narrow blue wash. Pip copies it. Vex paints a large blue spiral and labels it "BEND TWO." Nari checks the park map.]

NARI: The river has one bend.

VEX: The second one is artistic.

[Pip squints at his paper.]

PIP: Mine looks like a puddle.

MARA: Let it dry before you add the bank.

[Vex adds brown paint immediately. It blends into a gray cloud.]

VEX: The river has flooded.

NARI: It is still on the paper.

[The class compares paintings. Vex's oversized river covers his easel. Pip's thin blue line stops halfway down the sheet. Mara's is neat but has no water.]

MARA: I painted the channel before it filled.

VEX: You have painted the absence of a river.

MARA: Correct.

[Nari tips her watering can. One drop falls into the stone channel, then trickles toward the students. Pip picks up his painting to protect it. The paper bends into a curve.]

PIP: My river has a bridge.

[The trickle stops at a leaf. Vex sweeps the leaf aside with his giant painting. The water follows the paper's blue spiral and circles the easel leg.]

VEX: The second bend works.

NARI: You have redirected the irrigation.

[The students lift their easels. The trickle continues through the channel. Mara sets her dry landscape on the ground. The wet path runs beside it, exactly matching the blank curve she painted.]

MARA: The river found the picture.

VEX: I want to paint the next turn.

PIP: Wait for it to dry.

[Vex unfolds his sheet to reveal the title "RIVER AT DAWN." He has painted over the corner where the instructor was meant to sign.]

VEX: The judge will understand.

MARA: The judge is me.

VEX: Then you will understand twice.

[Nari sets her watering can in front of Vex's painting. Its reflection makes a narrow strip of blue across the page. Vex shifts the can; the reflection disappears.]

VEX: The river has left again.

NARI: It was in the can.

[Pip holds his paper beside the channel. His curved sheet catches a tiny trickle and drips onto the title. He flips it over.]

PIP: My puddle is now on the back.

[Vex holds his paper above the channel. The gray wash drips onto the ground and makes a new little stream.]

FINAL BEAT: Nari turns the watering can upright. Everyone lifts their paintings. Vex names the puddle "Bend Three" and asks Mara to grade the landscape.
