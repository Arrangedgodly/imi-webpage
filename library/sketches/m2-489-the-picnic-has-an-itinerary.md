# The Picnic Has an Itinerary

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: over-planned outdoor events, schedule-driven picnic hosts, and friends who just want to sit on the blanket*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: PLANNER, a baboon; FRIEND ONE, a macaque; FRIEND TWO, a gibbon; PARK ATTENDANT, an orangutan; VENDOR, a capuchin.

[A park lawn. A picnic blanket lies folded beside a laminated schedule. The planner unfolds a map and sets a timer.]

PLANNER: We have four minutes for arrival, six for greetings, and eight for sandwiches.

FRIEND ONE: Can we start with sitting?

PLANNER: Sitting begins at 12:14.

FRIEND TWO: What happens before that?

[The planner points to a card labeled SCENIC WALK. The path runs twenty feet around a tree and ends at the blanket.]

FRIEND ONE: We walked here from the entrance.

PARK ATTENDANT: The path is not a loop. It leads to the pond.

[The planner flips the map. The path is indeed a straight line. The timer rings for greeting time.]

ACT ONE

EXT. PARK LAWN - CONTINUOUS

Friend Two reports clouds; Friend One notes the blanket is still folded. The planner schedules blanket deployment. The attendant warns that this lawn stays clear for a cart.

PLANNER: We can move the blanket two feet.

PARK ATTENDANT: The cart needs the path clear, not just two feet.

[They move to the marked area. The planner restarts; Friend One sits on the basket.]

FRIEND ONE: I have completed the sitting event.

PLANNER: Sitting is at 12:14.

[The attendant points to the clock. It is 12:14. The planner checks the card; the time is correct.]

FRIEND TWO: The schedule has succeeded.

[The planner lays out plates in color order. The vendor arrives with a cart and asks if they want lemonade. The planner says the drink order is scheduled after the sandwich segment.]

VENDOR: The lemonade is cold now.

ACT TWO

EXT. PICNIC AREA - LATER

The friends begin eating. The planner asks them to wait until the food photograph is taken. Friend One lifts a sandwich; Friend Two's napkin catches the wind and lands on the laminated schedule. The planner tries to pull it away without bending the paper. The schedule gets stuck to the napkin by a dab of mustard.

PLANNER: We have a schedule emergency.

FRIEND TWO: The sandwich is still here.

[The attendant points to the napkin bin. The planner peels the schedule from the napkin; mustard blurs one corner.]

PLANNER: The afternoon plan is smudged.

FRIEND ONE: We can choose what to do next.

PLANNER: There is no card for that.

[Friend Two offers lemonade. The vendor pours into three cups. The planner looks at the clock, then at the cups. The friends wait. The planner crosses out the next timed event and writes DRINK LEMONADE UNTIL DONE.]

PARK ATTENDANT: The cart can pass now. The path is clear.

[The group moves their basket off the edge of the path. Friend One folds the stained schedule and puts it inside the basket so it cannot blow away.]

ACT THREE

EXT. PICNIC AREA - AFTERNOON

The planner has stopped the timer. The friends finish the food at their own pace. The park attendant checks the walkway and gives a thumbs-up. The vendor packs the cart and asks whether the group needs anything else. The planner looks at the schedule, which has mustard on the exact hour block.

PLANNER: We missed dessert by six minutes.

FRIEND TWO: We had lemonade instead.

FRIEND ONE: And we sat down.

[The planner crosses out the final event. The three friends fold the blanket together, shake off crumbs, and pack the basket. They leave the park area clear for the cart.]

PARK ATTENDANT: Thanks for moving when asked.

PLANNER: I can put that on next week's itinerary.

FRIEND TWO: Or we can meet without one.

[The planner folds the schedule into a small square and tucks it into a pocket, no longer using it as a timer.]

FINAL IMAGE: The empty blanket rests in the basket while the picnic group walks toward the park exit together.
