# The Interview with the Chair

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: job interview panels that evaluate candidates using irrelevant corporate rituals and a chair that answers nothing*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Sela, a macaque hiring manager; Pavo, a capuchin candidate; Nari, a gibbon panelist; and Rell, a baboon office coordinator.

INT. INTERVIEW ROOM - DAY

A chair sits in the center of the room. Sela welcomes Pavo and asks him to sit. Pavo sits. Nari studies the chair's empty cushion.

NARI: Tell us about a time you worked as a chair.

PAVO: I have never been a chair.

Rell hands Pavo a laminated card: THINK LIKE FURNITURE.

Sela asks Pavo to describe his strengths. He says he can carry messages, organize fruit and learn routes. Nari asks him to explain how those skills create a seating culture. Pavo looks at the chair.

PAVO: It is comfortable.

Nari writes that down. Sela asks him to share a weakness. Pavo says he is not comfortable when people ask him to be furniture. Rell notes "resistant to workplace metaphors."

Sela presents a group exercise: the panel and candidate must move the chair without touching it. Pavo asks if they can just lift it. Nari says the task tests influence. They all stare at the chair. Nothing happens. Rell gives the chair a second card, which reads "OWN YOUR SPACE."

Pavo stands and asks if the interview can continue. The panel all stand too. The chair remains in place. Sela calls this a successful alignment exercise.

Nari asks Pavo to solve a hypothetical problem: the chair has missed a meeting. Pavo says chairs do not attend meetings. Rell says the chair was invited. Pavo suggests they send a note to the person who moved it. Sela asks how he would build accountability into the chair.

PAVO: I would ask who has the chair.

NARI: Direct communication.

Sela places a blank evaluation form on the chair. It slides to the floor. Pavo picks it up and hands it back to her.

PAVO: The chair has declined to participate.

Rell enters carrying a second chair. He says the first one belongs in the waiting room. Everyone helps him carry it out. The panel sits in the spare seats around the table. Pavo stays standing.

SELA: Do you have any questions for us?

PAVO: Who is the job for?

Nari looks at the role description. It says "Message runner, part time." Sela turns the card around and reads it aloud.

SELA: We have been interviewing for the wrong position.

Pavo receives the job. Rell gives him a map of the office and a message to deliver. Pavo exits through the door. The panel turns to the empty chair.

NARI: Would you like the position?

Rell returns to the room and asks whether the chair has been hired. Sela says it declined. He writes the chair's name under references and leaves the line blank.

The chair remains silent. Sela marks "no response" on the form.
