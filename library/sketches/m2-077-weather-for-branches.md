# Weather for Branches

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: local television weather segments and forecast graphics*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: FORECASTER PEB, a squirrel monkey; CAMERAPERSON LO, a gibbon; FIELD REPORTER MOSS, a capuchin; and the WEATHER WALL, a painted sheet.

[Studio. Peb stands beside a hand-painted map. A paper sun is pinned over the troop's fig grove.]

PEB: Good evening. Tomorrow brings bright skies, light breezes, and a sixty percent chance of somebody saying, "It feels like rain." That somebody is usually me.

LO: The map is upside down.

PEB: It is a regional view from the sky.

LO: The river is at the top.

PEB: The river is moving upward in spirit.

[Lo rotates the map. The paper sun falls off and lands on Peb's head.] 

PEB: The sun has reached the forecast desk early.

MOSS: I'm live at the fig grove, where the wind has removed several leaves but left every fruit exactly where it was.

PEB: That's a strong gust.

MOSS: It is a mild breeze. I am holding a branch for scale.

[He lifts a twig. It barely moves. A gust blows his notes away. He chases them, still reporting.] 

MOSS: Conditions are changing quickly for my paperwork.

LO: Our radar shows a green patch approaching from the west.

PEB: Is that rain?

LO: No. It's Moss's jacket on the map.

[Lo removes a green sweater from behind the weather wall. The map shows a huge storm in exactly the same shape.] 

PEB: We will continue to monitor this developing wardrobe.

MOSS: Back here, the troop has started gathering nesting leaves. They seem unconcerned by the forecast.

PEB: Nature knows more than we do.

MOSS: The troop is building under the broad canopy.

PEB: Which our map says will be sunny.

LO: The map is a sheet of paper.

PEB: Paper can be accurate.

[The studio lights flicker. A small fan behind the map starts, sending the forecast cards into the air.] 

PEB: We have a wind advisory indoors.

LO: Turn off the fan.

PEB: If we turn it off, the leaves won't move.

MOSS: They are not leaves. They're the forecast cards.

[One card sticks to Peb's forehead: TOMORROW: SOME CLOUDS.] 

PEB: I can confirm cloud cover over the upper brow.

[Lo opens the studio door. Outside, the actual sky is clear. The troop's canopy shakes gently. Moss looks at it, then at the map.] 

MOSS: The grove has shade and fruit. We can call that a good forecast.

PEB: It doesn't fit our graphics package.

LO: It fits the weather.

[Peb removes the paper sun from her head and pins it to the map's canopy. The forecast now shows sun over the grove and a storm over the sweater pile.] 

PEB: Update: the weather is pleasant. The sweater region remains unsettled.

MOSS: Back to you.

PEB: I am you.

MOSS: Then back to the map.

[The fan switches on again. The sweater flaps like a storm cloud. Peb points gravely.] 

PEB: Tomorrow's outlook: dress in layers, especially if your forecast has sleeves.
