# The Emergency Whistle Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: disaster drills that become more confusing than the emergencies they prepare for*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Trainer Tavi, a capuchin; marshal Nara, a gibbon; trainee Odo, a baboon; observer Piri, a macaque.

EXT. CLEARING - DAY

Tavi holds a whistle and a laminated emergency card.

TAVI: When you hear one whistle, walk to the safe tree.

Odo asks what happens if they hear two whistles. Tavi says two means stay where you are. Nara asks what a long whistle means. Tavi checks the card.

TAVI: We haven't assigned one.

Piri writes "long whistle: unknown."

Tavi blows one short note. Odo walks to the safe tree. Nara stays where she is because she is the marshal. Piri looks at the card.

PIRI: The card says everyone moves.

NARA: Then I move too.

They all reach the tree. Tavi checks attendance. Odo asks if the drill is over. Tavi says they must practice the all-clear. She blows two notes. Odo stays at the tree; Nara and Piri return to the start.

ODO: I thought two meant stay.

TAVI: Two notes were the all-clear.

Piri checks the card. It says two whistles mean stay where you are. Tavi says the card is from last year.

NARA: Then we should use the card we have.

Tavi blows one long note to signal a pause. Odo lies down, thinking that means rest. Nara asks him to stand. He says no one assigned the long note.

The group starts again. Tavi blows one short note. Everyone moves to the tree. She blows two short notes. Everyone freezes. She waves her arms to signal all-clear, but Odo thinks she is directing them to another tree.

Piri suggests one whistle for move, no whistle for stay, and a spoken "all clear" at the end. Nara agrees. Tavi writes it on the card. Odo asks if the card should include what to do if the whistle breaks. Tavi says then the marshal will speak loudly.

Tavi gives the new signal. One note; all move. Then she says "All clear." Everyone returns. The drill works. Odo asks what to do if they cannot hear her. Nara says stay with the group and follow the marshal.

Nara shows the trainees where the safe tree is from each side of the clearing. Odo walks the route once at normal pace. Piri checks that the card matches the agreed signals. Tavi asks if anyone has a question. Odo asks whether the drill is finished. Nara says yes, and everyone returns to their work.

Piri folds the card and puts it away.

Nara checks the whistle twice.

The group nods and disperses.

The card stays on the table.

TAG

Piri blows the whistle by accident. Everyone starts walking.

TAVI: We need to add "accidental whistle."

NARA: Or put it away.
