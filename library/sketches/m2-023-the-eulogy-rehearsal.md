# The Eulogy Rehearsal

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Funeral eulogies and overly polished memorial speeches*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Speech coach Miri, a gibbon; speaker Dolo, a mandrill; cousin Fenn, a capuchin; memorial subject Gramps, an elderly orangutan, present in a recording.

[STAGE: A QUIET HALL. A LECTERN, A PHOTO OF GRAMPS, AND A TAPE RECORDER.] 

MIRI: Dolo, your eulogy is sincere. It runs seventeen minutes.

DOLO: Gramps lived ninety-two years.

MIRI: The service has a time limit.

FENN: Gramps would have liked a short speech.

DOLO: He liked long naps.

MIRI: That is a good memory.

DOLO: He napped through the first half of every picnic.

FENN: Then woke when the pie came out.

MIRI: Keep that. It gives the room a place to breathe.

DOLO: I wrote that he was "a towering presence."

FENN: He was short.

DOLO: He had tall feelings.

MIRI: Say what he did, not what the phrase does.

DOLO: He always saved me the end piece of pie.

FENN: He ate the crust.

DOLO: He said the crust was the best part.

MIRI: Was it?

DOLO: No. He was trying to make me feel generous.

FENN: He was trying to keep the pie.

[THE RECORDER CLICKS ON. GRAMPS' VOICE PLAYS.] 

GRAMPS, RECORDING: If anyone is talking about me, say the pie was good.

DOLO: He planned this.

FENN: He recorded that at the last picnic.

MIRI: The speech is becoming more honest.

DOLO: I also wrote that he was "a light in the darkness."

FENN: He left the porch lamp on all night.

DOLO: He did. We found every moth in the village there.

MIRI: That's a story.

DOLO: He used to sit outside and name them.

FENN: He named one "Peanut."

DOLO: It was a moth.

FENN: He liked peanuts.

MIRI: This is the voice the family remembers.

DOLO: Should I keep the part about his last words?

FENN: What were they?

DOLO: "Save me the end piece."

MIRI: That's a good ending.

DOLO: It might make everyone laugh.

FENN: They may laugh and cry.

DOLO: I don't want to tell it wrong.

MIRI: Speak simply. You don't have to control what everyone feels.

DOLO: Gramps loved pie, long naps, and porch moths. He saved me the end piece, even when he wanted it himself.

FENN: That's him.

[PAUSE. THE RECORDER SPINS.] 

DOLO: I can say that in three minutes.

MIRI: Two, if you don't describe each pie.

DOLO: There were many pies.

FENN: We can eat one after.

DOLO: I will save you the end piece.

FENN: You can have the crust.

DOLO: Gramps would want that.

MIRI: Then the speech is ready.

GRAMPS, RECORDING: Is there pie?

FENN: There will be.

DOLO: We will save you a piece.

FENN: Should we rehearse the pause after the name?
DOLO: I have been pausing since breakfast.
FENN: That is grief.
DOLO: That is a very slow pear.
FENN: We can honor both.
DOLO: Gramps would have liked that.
FENN: He liked a speech with a clear snack break.
DOLO: And a chair facing the pie.

[THE RECORDER CLICKS OFF. A PIE KNIFE TAPS THE PLATE.]
