# The Aquarium Map Is Missing One Tentacle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: aquarium tours that treat animal enrichment as a carefully scheduled demonstration*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Guide Mara, a macaque; keeper Pip, a capuchin; visitor Vex, an orangutan; visitor Nari, a lemur; OLLIE, an octopus behind the glass.

INT. AQUARIUM TUNNEL - DAY

[A laminated map shows a smiling octopus with eight numbered arms. Pip holds the map. Mara leads a small tour. Behind thick glass, Ollie the octopus untwists a jar lid.]

MARA: On your left, Ollie will demonstrate problem-solving at two.

VEX: It's two now.

PIP: The clock is slow.

NARI: The octopus is opening a jar.

[Ollie lifts the lid, reaches inside, then closes it again. Pip checks the schedule.]

PIP: The demonstration starts when the lid is shut.

MARA: Then we are early.

[Vex steps to the map stand. One of the printed tentacles is folded underneath the laminate.]

VEX: The map has only seven arms.

MARA: The map has eight.

NARI: I see seven.

[Pip flattens the corner. A laminated arm springs out and covers the "YOU ARE HERE" dot.]

PIP: The map has moved us.

MARA: We are still in the tunnel.

[Ollie presses a suction cup against the glass. A white tour badge clings to the other side. Pip pats his shirt.]

PIP: Where is my badge?

VEX: The octopus has joined the staff.

[Ollie peels the badge from the glass and carries it toward the jar. Mara signals to keeper Pip. He taps a feeding bucket against the railing. Ollie releases the badge and turns toward the sound.]

MARA: Thank you. The map is missing a badge now too.

[Pip retrieves it. Vex holds up the map to compare. Ollie reaches through a small keeper's hatch and pulls the map off its stand.]

VEX: The animal has taken the route.

NARI: It wants the map.

[Ollie pushes the map against the glass. A damp arm covers the arrow pointing to the exit. Nari traces the route with her finger on the outside of the pane. Ollie follows it from the other side, arm by arm, then stops at the aquarium's staff door.]

PIP: We are following the tour guide.

MARA: The guide is following the octopus.

[Vex unclips the map. Its last folded tentacle hides the staff door. He flips it open. The map now shows an arrow straight through the glass.]

VEX: The exit is through the tank.

MARA: No. The exit is behind you.

[Ollie turns the map around, presses it flat, and releases it. The suction cups hold it to the glass, arrow facing the tank.]

NARI: The octopus has its own tour.

PIP: Where does it go?

[Mara checks the map. The first stop is the jar. Ollie has reopened it and is eating a mussel.]

FINAL BEAT: Vex steps into line behind the glass, waiting for the tour. Ollie points one tentacle at the label "Please do not tap." Vex puts his knuckle away and follows the map to the mussel exhibit.
