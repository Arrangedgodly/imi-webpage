# The Seating Chart Is a Family Tree

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wedding seating charts, delicate family diplomacy, and planners who treat every chair as a statement*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Planner Sola, a macaque; couple Tavi, a gibbon, and Neri, a capuchin; aunt Omi, an orangutan; and usher Vex, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. WEDDING HALL - MORNING

Sola studies a chart with round tables and names. Tavi and Neri stand beside her.

SOLA: We have one table for family, one for friends, and one for guests who prefer quiet.

NERI: Aunt Omi is family and prefers quiet.

TAVI: She also wants to sit by the window.

SOLA: The window seat is at the friends' table.

VEX: The seating chart has become a maze.

He carries a roll of name cards. His tail catches one and places it on the wrong table.

SOLA: That is the card for the officiant.

VEX: The officiant is now with the friends.

The guests arrive. Omi enters and asks where she should sit. Sola points to the family table. Omi says her cousin is at the window. Sola moves Omi's card. The cousin wants to sit near the music. Tavi moves the cousin's card. The band asks for more space.

NERI: Can we put the family where there is space?

SOLA: That would make the chart inaccurate.

TAVI: The chart is about chairs.

Sola redraws the seating plan. Each time she moves one name, another card shifts. Vex's tail gathers three cards into a neat stack. He gives them to Omi.

OMI: These are all my name cards.

SOLA: We have three Omi cards.

OMI: One is me. One is my sister. One is the table name.

VEX: Which table is named Omi?

Omi points to a table labeled "Omi's side."

SOLA: That is not a person.

Tavi suggests assigning seats at the door. Sola worries guests will choose the wrong chairs. Neri says guests can sit next to people they know. The first guests do this and naturally fill the family and friends tables.

The quiet table remains empty. Omi sits there by the window. Her sister joins her. The officiant sits with Tavi's friends and starts telling a story. The band moves one chair away from the speaker.

SOLA: This arrangement was not on the chart.

NERI: Is everyone seated?

VEX: Everyone has a chair.

Sola folds the seating chart and puts it away. The couple walks to the front. Omi's side table has three Omi cards, but everyone knows who is sitting where.

TAVI: Do you approve?

SOLA: I approve of the chairs.

Neri reaches for Tavi's hand. The officiant stands, but his chair is caught on a table leg. Vex frees it with one tug of his tail.

SOLA: Now the chart is accurate.

VEX: We did not change the chart.

SOLA: The room changed around it.

The ceremony begins, with the band playing softly enough for Omi to hear.
