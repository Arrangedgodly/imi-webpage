# The Blank Canvas Requests an Artist Statement

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: gallery openings and art criticism that demand a grand explanation before anyone has painted anything*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: ARTIST, a gibbon; CURATOR, an orangutan; CRITIC, a baboon; INSTALLER, a macaque; VISITOR, a capuchin.

[An art gallery. A large blank canvas hangs on the wall. The artist holds a brush. A card beneath the work reads UNTITLED.] 

CURATOR: Your artist statement is due before the opening.

ARTIST: The canvas is blank.

CRITIC: Is the blankness an intentional void?

ARTIST: It is awaiting paint.

[The installer checks the wall hook and steps away. The critic leans in close to the canvas.] 

CRITIC: The edge has a remarkable restraint.

VISITOR: There is no edge treatment.

CRITIC: That is what I mean.

[The curator hands the artist a clipboard with an empty statement form. The artist dips the brush in blue paint and makes one small dot in the center.] 

ARTIST: Now it has a mark.

CRITIC: A concentrated horizon.

VISITOR: It is a dot.

[The artist steps back. The curator asks for a longer statement. The visitor reads the label aloud.] 

VISITOR: "One blue dot. Paint on canvas."

CURATOR: That is factually complete.

[The critic proposes a paragraph about memory. The artist adds a second dot beside the first, then wipes the brush clean.] 

ARTIST: I was making a pair.

CRITIC: A conversation between two points.

ARTIST: They are dots.

[The gallery doors open. The first visitors enter. The curator asks the artist to stand beside the canvas for photographs. The artist tries to move the label so the text is readable, but the critic shifts it back to center.] 

INSTALLER: The label belongs beside the work, not over it.

[The installer moves it to the correct rail. The visitor takes a photograph with the two dots in frame. The critic asks whether the work is finished; the artist puts down the brush.] 

ARTIST: It is finished for tonight.

CURATOR: And the statement?

ARTIST: Two blue dots, made before the opening. I stopped painting when I was done.

[The critic reads the short statement and nods. The visitor asks if the dots are for sale. The artist says the gallery can list them as one canvas, not as separate points.] 

CRITIC: The title could be "Conversation."

ARTIST: It is still "Untitled."

[The curator updates the sale card but keeps the original label. The artist hangs the cleaned brush on a peg.] 

[A second visitor enters and asks if the dots are a map. The artist says they are not. The critic tries to describe the blank space between them as a route; the artist steps aside and lets the visitor look without narration.] 

VISITOR: I see two dots and a lot of white.

ARTIST: That is what is on the canvas.

CURATOR: The label will stay short.

[The installer checks the canvas level. It hangs straight. The critic lowers the clipboard and stands beside the visitor instead of in front of the work.] 

CRITIC: I can write my response after the opening.

FINAL IMAGE: Two blue dots sit on a white canvas beneath the plain label, "Untitled."
