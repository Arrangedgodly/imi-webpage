# The Mail Carrier Has Too Many Parcels

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: delivery service conventions and the mismatch between addresses and actual homes*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: CARRIER RIN, a macaque; ROUTE SUPERVISOR PAX, a gibbon; RECIPIENT ODO, a capuchin; and three parcels.

[A trail divides beneath a tree. Rin holds three parcels. Pax studies a route sheet.] 

PAX: The parcels are for the west hollow, the east hollow, and the hollow marked "near the big tree."

RIN: Every hollow is near a big tree.

ODO: Mine is the one with the red root.

PAX: The route sheet says address, not root color.

RIN: The red root is visible from the path.

PAX: The system does not have a field for root color.

ODO: It can have a note.

PAX: Notes are on page two.

RIN: Page two is wet.

[THE ROUTE SHEET DRIPS. INK RUNS.] 

PAX: The addresses have merged.

RIN: I can ask each person where they live.

PAX: We must verify the parcel number first.

ODO: This one has my name.

RIN: The label says Odo, west hollow.

ODO: I live in the east hollow, near the red root.

PAX: The sender wrote west.

RIN: Maybe the sender used the old path.

ODO: I can show you my door.

PAX: We cannot deviate from the route.

RIN: The route has no address that matches the recipient.

PAX: We can mark it undeliverable.

ODO: It is deliverable. I am here.

[ODO POINTS. A NEST WITH A RED ROOT MARKER IS VISIBLE.] 

PAX: The system has no proof that is your door.

RIN: You know Odo. I know Odo. We are at Odo's door.

PAX: I can take a photo.

RIN: The camera is out of battery.

ODO: You can ask my neighbor.

PAX: The neighbor is not on the route sheet.

RIN: I can check the remaining parcel labels before we take the route.

PAX: They say west hollow, east hollow, and "near the big tree."

RIN: The west hollow has the blue stone. The east hollow has the red root.

PAX: The route sheet has no color key.

ODO: The parcels have names. We can ask the recipients to confirm the color.

PAX: They are not on the schedule.

RIN: They are at home.

[ODO CALLS TOWARD THE PATH. TWO MONKEYS ANSWER FROM DIFFERENT TREES.] 

RECIPIENT ONE: Blue stone, west hollow.

RECIPIENT TWO: Red root, east hollow.

PAX: The route has now been verified by witnesses.

RIN: We can deliver the parcels in order.

PAX: The first parcel is for the blue stone. It has no color on the label.

ODO: I can set that parcel aside for the blue-stone recipient.

PAX: Then the next one must be east.

RIN: It says "near the big tree."

PAX: There are two big trees.

ODO: One has a platform. One has a spring.

PAX: Which is the recipient's?

ODO: Ask them. They are here.

[THE RECIPIENTS POINT TO THEIR HOMES AT THE SAME TIME.] 

PAX: The route sheet is upside down.

RIN: Turn it over.

PAX: The addresses are written on the back.

ODO: The route was there the whole time.

PAX: It was on the wrong side.

RIN: That is why we check both sides of a parcel.

[THE NEIGHBOR CALLS FROM INSIDE THE NEST.] 

NEIGHBOR: That parcel is Odo's.

PAX: I will add a witness note.

RIN: On page two?

PAX: On the dry corner.

[ODO TAKES THE PARCEL. THE OTHER TWO LABELS ARE TURNED AROUND.] 

RIN: The west hollow is up the path. The east hollow is by the red root.

PAX: I will revise the route for tomorrow.

ODO: Start with the people. The map can catch up.

PAX: The map is required.

RIN: Then carry it with us.
