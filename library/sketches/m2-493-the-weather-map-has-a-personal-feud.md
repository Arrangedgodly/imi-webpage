# The Weather Map Has a Personal Feud

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: local weather broadcasts, dramatic forecast graphics, and meteorologists who take tiny weather personally*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: METEOROLOGIST, a baboon; CAMERAPERSON, a gibbon; INTERN, a macaque; WEATHER MAP, a capuchin behind a panel; CALLER, an orangutan.

[A small studio. The meteorologist faces a weather map showing one gray cloud over a neighborhood. The intern holds a tiny umbrella.]

METEOROLOGIST: Good evening. A single cloud is moving east at a speed best described as stubborn.

WEATHER MAP: I am moving at a normal pace.

METEOROLOGIST: The cloud has refused to enter the county line.

WEATHER MAP: That is a municipal border. I do not cross it without permission.

[The cameraperon widens the shot. The gray cloud covers only the corner of a park.] 

INTERN: The sun is out everywhere else.

METEOROLOGIST: We will not let facts interrupt the radar.

[The meteorologist draws a red arrow toward the cloud. The arrow sticks to the screen and points back at the meteorologist.]

METEOROLOGIST: It is targeting me.

WEATHER MAP: I am a cloud symbol. I do not have hands.

METEOROLOGIST: Your shadow is over my desk.

[The caller appears on a monitor, holding a dry paper bag.] 

CALLER: I'm under that cloud. It rained for twelve seconds, then stopped while I was opening my umbrella.

METEOROLOGIST: A localized shower. Did the cloud apologize?

CALLER: It moved away before I could ask.

ACT ONE

[The intern changes the forecast graphic to SUNBREAK. The single cloud icon stays in place and gains sunglasses.]

INTERN: The update tool gave it eyewear.

WEATHER MAP: I look good in this forecast.

METEOROLOGIST: This is a weather report, not a portrait sitting.

[The cameraperon tries to focus on the map. The meteorologist steps in front of the cloud and gets illuminated by the graphic's fake lightning.] 

METEOROLOGIST: I have been struck by studio lightning.

INTERN: That is the warning animation.

METEOROLOGIST: Warn me before I become a graphic.

[The meteorologist points to a temperature reading. The map rotates its panel and hides the number behind a cartoon umbrella.] 

WEATHER MAP: The umbrella is in the rain zone.

CALLER: My paper bag is still dry.

METEOROLOGIST: The cloud is making a fool of my forecast.

CALLER: It rained on one bench. The bench was already wet from someone spilling a drink.

[The intern zooms out to show the whole county, then taps the tiny cloud. A small gray icon pops free and lands in the intern's umbrella.] 

INTERN: It appears to be a sticker.

WEATHER MAP: I was a sticker this whole time?

METEOROLOGIST: I spent six minutes arguing with adhesive.

ACT TWO

[The meteorologist peels the cloud sticker from the umbrella and places it over the exact park bench on the printed map. The caller checks the sky.] 

CALLER: The sun is still out.

METEOROLOGIST: The forecast is clear for most of the county. A brief shower may affect one bench, if the beverage spill returns.

[The cameraperon lowers the studio lights. The intern replaces the lightning animation with a plain map.]

WEATHER MAP: I prefer the portrait shot.

METEOROLOGIST: You can have it after the broadcast.

[The caller folds the paper bag. The cloud sticker peels off the map and lands on the meteorologist's sleeve.]

FINAL IMAGE: The meteorologist exits with a cloud on one shoulder as the map reports clear skies.
