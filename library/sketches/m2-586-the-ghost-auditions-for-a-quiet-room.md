# The Ghost Auditions for a Quiet Room

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: theatrical hauntings that mistake louder effects for scarier atmosphere*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: GHOST VELA, a gibbon; SOUND DESIGNER PIP, a capuchin; TOUR GUIDE NARA, a lemur; HOUSEKEEPER ODO, an orangutan.

[A mock haunted mansion. Vela stands in a white sheet beside a chair. Pip operates a soundboard. Odo dusts the mantel.]

PIP: When you moan, I will add wind.

VELA: I would rather have quiet.

NARA: This is a haunting audition.

ACT ONE

[Vela drifts behind the chair. Pip plays a thunder crash. The chair tips over. Odo catches it before it hits the floor.]

ODO: The house is not haunted by furniture.

PIP: It sounded good.

VELA: It drowned out my entrance.

[She tries again. Pip adds chains, a wolf howl and a door slam. Nara gives the tour narration over the effects. No one hears Vela say "boo." She lowers the sheet.]

VELA: I am a ghost, not a sound cue.

ACT TWO

[Nara asks Vela what kind of haunting she wants. Vela says a quiet room with a draft under the door and one creaking floorboard. Odo opens the door slightly. A draft lifts the sheet. Pip adds a wind machine; the sheet balloons over Vela's head.]

PIP: Spectacular.

[The ghost bumps into the mantel. Odo steadies a candle and gives Pip the plug to the wind machine. Nara explains that visitors come to hear the house, not a stack of sound effects. Pip switches off the board. The room goes silent except for the one floorboard creaking under Odo.]

ODO: That is my foot.

[Vela crosses the room without touching the floor. The board creaks again. Odo looks down and sees his other foot on it. He steps away. The board stops.]

ACT THREE

[Vela auditions the quiet version. Nara welcomes a small group of imaginary visitors. Odo opens the door and closes it once; the latch makes a soft click. Vela appears in the doorway without a sound. Pip reaches toward the thunder button, then puts both hands in his pockets.]

VELA: Good evening.

[The tour group stares. One visitor asks if she is part of the tour. Nara says she is the house's resident ghost. Vela glides past the visitors, and a draft moves one curtain. The curtain brushes Pip's soundboard. He jumps, then tries to claim it was a planned effect.]

PIP: A soft atmospheric fabric event.

NARA: A curtain.

[Vela asks if the quiet room can be hers after closing. Odo shows her the guest room, where the door has a loose latch. She closes it, then opens it a finger's width. The latch gives one gentle click. Vela smiles. Pip moves the thunder machine into a cupboard and closes it. The cupboard creaks loudly. Everyone turns toward it.]

ODO: That is the cupboard.

VELA: It may audition next.

TAG

[Nara revises the tour card: ONE RESIDENT GHOST, NO THUNDER CUE. Pip writes the same note on the soundboard.]

FINAL IMAGE: Vela glides into the quiet room as the cupboard door swings open and shuts with one very loud creak.
