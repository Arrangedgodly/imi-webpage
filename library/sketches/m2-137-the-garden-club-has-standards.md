# The Garden Club Has Standards

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: competitive gardening clubs where judges reward presentation over actual plant health*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Judge Rell, a mandrill; gardener Fia, a capuchin; gardener Toma, a macaque; and announcer.

[TWO POTTED PLANTS SIT ON A JUDGING TABLE. ONE IS HEALTHY, THE OTHER HAS A RIBBON.]

ANNOUNCER: Welcome to the village garden show.

RELL: I will judge leaf shape, color, and arrangement.

FIA: My plant has three new leaves.

TOMA: Mine has a ribbon.

[RELL EXAMINES THE RIBBON.]

RELL: The ribbon is blue.

TOMA: It matches the pot.

FIA: The pot has no drainage hole.

[TOMA TILTS THE POT. WATER POURS OVER THE RIM ONTO RELL'S SCORE NOTEBOOK.]

RELL: The arrangement has a dramatic cascade.

[THE ANNOUNCER HANDS FIA A TROPHY FOR A PREVIEW PHOTOGRAPH, BEFORE THE SCORES ARE ANNOUNCED.]

FIA: My plant is wilting.

RELL: We judge what is presented.

[FIA MOVES THE PLANT INTO SHADE.]

RELL: That changes the display.

FIA: It needs shade.

[TOMA ADDS A DECORATIVE ROCK. THE PLANT LEANS OVER.]

TOMA: It has a natural curve.

FIA: It is falling.

[TOMA ADDS ANOTHER ROCK. THE POT TIPS.]

RELL: The composition is unstable.

ANNOUNCER: A surprise score change.

[FIA POKES A DRAINAGE HOLE IN THE POT.]

RELL: You cannot modify an entry after judging.

FIA: I am saving the plant.

[THE HEALTHY PLANT STANDS UPRIGHT. THE RIBBON PLANT SAGS.]

TOMA: Can I have a hole too?

RELL: You may have a drainage hole, but it will not count.

[TOMA POKES A HOLE. WATER DRAINS. THE PLANT STRAIGHTENS.]

ANNOUNCER: Both plants have improved.

RELL: The show cannot award two first places.

FIA: Then award the plant a second chance.

[THE JUDGE SETS THE TROPHY BESIDE THE DRAINAGE TOOLS.]

RELL: This is the new category: Best Recovery.


[A VISITOR ASKS WHICH PLANT WON.]

RELL: The official winner is the plant with the best presentation.

FIA: Which one is that?

RELL: We have not decided.

[TOMA TURNS THE RIBBON PLANT SO THE BLUE BOW FACES THE CROWD.]

ANNOUNCER: The ribbon is now visible from all sides.

RELL: That may change the score.

FIA: The leaves have not moved.

TOMA: The presentation has.

[THE JUDGE LOOKS AT THE DRAINAGE HOLE, THEN THE BOW.]

RELL: I will award points for health and display.

FIA: How many for a hole?

RELL: One.

TOMA: And the bow?

RELL: Also one.

[RELL TURNS THE BLUE RIBBON SO THE BOW FACES THE CROWD.]

FIA: The ribbon now matches the watering can.

TOMA: The plant is still in the shade.

RELL: The score must reflect both health and display.

ANNOUNCER: The watering can wins.

[THE TWO PLANTS TIE. THE TROPHY REMAINS BESIDE THE WATERING CAN.]

RELL: The plants still need water.

[FIA POURS A SMALL CUP INTO EACH POT.]

TOMA: The ribbon is thirsty too.

[FIA REMOVES THE BOW AND SETS IT ON THE TROPHY. TOMA TIES THE BLUE RIBBON AROUND THE WATERING CAN.]

ANNOUNCER: The watering can has won best dressed.

RELL: The show can end beside it.
