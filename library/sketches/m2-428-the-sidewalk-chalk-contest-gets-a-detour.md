# The Sidewalk Chalk Contest Gets a Detour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: street-art contests judged by judges who keep changing the available canvas*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Driver, a macaque; Judge Nari, a lemur; artist Vex, a macaque; artist Pip, a capuchin; groundskeeper Mara, a gibbon.

EXT. TOWN PLAZA - MORNING

[A chalk contest begins on a wide sidewalk. Vex sketches a grand castle. Pip draws a banana. Mara sets out cones to keep pedestrians clear.]

NARI: You have one hour. Keep your work inside the chalk line.

VEX: Mine needs a moat.

MARA: The chalk line is the contest border.

PIP: My banana fits.

[Vex draws a moat. It crosses the line and reaches a manhole. Mara shifts a cone.]

MARA: Keep the drain clear.

VEX: The moat is part of the design.

[The street sweeper arrives. Its driver points to the sidewalk.]

DRIVER: We need this lane.

NARI: The contest closes at noon.

DRIVER: The street closes now.

[Mara moves the cones to make a detour. The painters must continue on a new patch of sidewalk. Pip's banana is cut in half by a crack. Vex tries to extend his castle across the detour.]

PIP: My banana has a border.

VEX: My castle has lost its moat.

[The sweeper rolls past. The breeze from it blows chalk dust over Vex's castle. He coughs, then waves the cloud away. It settles into a mountain range.]

VEX: The kingdom has expanded.

NARI: You have a mountain now.

[Pip rotates his drawing around the crack. The two halves make a banana boat. Mara uses a cone as a perspective marker.]

MARA: The detour goes through the river.

PIP: The banana is sailing.

[Vex asks to redraw the castle on the new path. Nari says the judge will score what fits the chalk border. Vex erases the moat with his sleeve. It smears into a cloud. The castle now looks like a small shed behind the mountain.]

VEX: The castle has become a visitor center.

NARI: It has become a drawing.

[The sweeper returns and waits while groundskeeper Mara collects the traffic cones, revealing the true sidewalk edge. Both artists adjust their pavement drawings to fit. Pip's banana boat now points toward Vex's mountain.]

VEX: The line has changed.

NARI: The sidewalk was here all morning.

[Vex measures the edge with his brush handle. It is longer than his painting. Pip follows the crack in the pavement to extend the banana boat.]

PIP: The river has a second bank.

MARA: That is the curb.

[Nari examines the two drawings side by side. The banana boat appears to sail toward the castle's visitor center.]

VEX: We should enter as one piece.

PIP: We didn't plan it.

NARI: The detour did.

[A passerby steps around the chalk. Vex shifts to make room; his sleeve smears the castle title. He turns to the pavement and follows the smear, making it into a directional street map.]

VEX: This piece has a new direction.

FINAL BEAT: Nari awards the prize to the detour. Mara waits beside the street sweeper with the collected cones. Pip steps through the still-wet chalk river; blue chalk coats his soles. He walks across the pavement, leaving little blue prints that carry the drawing down the sidewalk.
