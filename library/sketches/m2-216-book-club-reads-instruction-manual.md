# The Book Club Reads the Instruction Manual

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: book clubs that overanalyze an assigned book while ignoring the straightforward instructions for their own meeting space*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Club Chair Runi, a macaque; Reader Toma, a capuchin; Librarian Esi, a gibbon; and Custodian Korr, a baboon.

INT. LIBRARY MEETING ROOM - EVENING

Four chairs surround a table. Runi holds a novel. A folded sign on the table reads "ROOM USE INSTRUCTIONS." Korr waits by the door.

RUNI: Tonight we discuss chapter six, where the character refuses to open the door.

TOMA: I think the door is a symbol of the author's fear.

ESI: The door is also locked. The key is in the room instructions.

RUNI: We should not jump ahead to the practical answer.

KORR: The meeting room is reserved until seven.

TOMA: The novel's ending may be a comment on ownership.

KORR: The next group is standing outside.

RUNI: They can wait while we interpret the door.

[KORR TAPS THE SIGN. IT FALLS OPEN. A SMALL KEY SLIDES OUT.]

INT. LIBRARY MEETING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Runi reads aloud from the instructions. The key fits the supply cupboard, not the door.

RUNI: The text says, "Use the key for the cabinet."

TOMA: A key inside a locked cabinet is a circle of authority.

ESI: There is another line. "The door does not lock. Pull the handle." 

[ESI PULLS THE DOOR. IT OPENS.]

KORR: That was the exit.

RUNI: It was not in the assigned reading.

TOMA: We have encountered a literal door.

RUNI: We can discuss whether it is literal.

KORR: The next group has knocked twice.

[THE ROOM DOOR OPENS. THREE WAITING MONKEYS PEEK IN.]

WAITING MONKEY: Are you finished?

RUNI: We have not reached the final interpretation.

ESI: The book has a back cover.

KORR: And the door has a handle.

TOMA: The back cover is a threshold.

INT. LIBRARY HALL - NIGHT

The book club files out carrying chairs. Korr points to the room sign. Runi finally reads its other side.

KORR: The chairs go against the wall.

RUNI: Is that an instruction or a metaphor?

KORR: It is a chair instruction.

TOMA: The wall could represent the limit of our discussion.

ESI: The wall is where the chairs belong.

[TOMA STACKS A CHAIR. IT WOBBLES. KORR SETS THE FEET STRAIGHT.]

KORR: Stable now.

RUNI: That applies to the book too.

TOMA: The book is still open.

ESI: It is time to close it.

RUNI: "Please stack chairs after use."

TOMA: The chairs are arranged like the characters in chapter six.

ESI: They are arranged around a table.

[THE BOOK CLUB STACKS THE CHAIRS. A STACK TOPPLES; THE NOVEL LANDS OPEN ON A SENTENCE ABOUT LEAVING.]

RUNI: We should stop here.

TOMA: Is that the author speaking?

KORR: It is the library closing.

[ESI TURNS OFF THE LIGHT. THE CLUB EXITS THROUGH THE DOOR THEY SPENT AN HOUR INTERPRETING.]
