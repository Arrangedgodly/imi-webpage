# The Public Fountain Needs a Password

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: smart-city systems that put app gates between residents and basic public amenities*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: City engineer Runi, a macaque; resident Daro, a gibbon; visitor Pava, a capuchin; and clerk Tern, a baboon.

EXT. PLAZA FOUNTAIN - DAY

Daro approaches a public drinking fountain. A screen reads: SCAN TO BEGIN. Pava holds a cup. Runi watches the new control panel.

DARO: I would like a drink of water.

RUNIT: The fountain uses a smart activation system.

PAVA: Does it use water?

RUNIT: Yes. First, open the city app.

[Daro opens the app. It asks for a password.]

DARO: I do not have a city account.

RUNIT: You can create one.

PAVA: I am thirsty now.

INT. PLAZA KIOSK - MOMENTS LATER

Tern sits at a kiosk. Daro types his email. The screen sends a confirmation code to the fountain itself.

DARO: The code is in the fountain?

TERN: It may appear on the display.

[The display changes to "WELCOME, USER 004." The water remains off.]

PAVA: We have been welcomed.

RUNIT: Now select the fountain.

[Daro taps a map. A pin appears across the plaza on a decorative sculpture.]

DARO: The map thinks the fountain is that statue.

TERN: The statue has better signal.

EXT. PLAZA FOUNTAIN - LATER

Runi opens the control panel. Tern reads the manual. Pava cups her hands under the spout.

RUNIT: There is a physical button beneath the smart panel.

PAVA: Can I press it?

RUNIT: We need to register your hand first.

[Pava presses. The fountain starts, spraying water onto the ground behind her.]

PAVA: I have activated the plaza.

DARO: The water is on the other side.

[Tern turns the spout. It now points at the kiosk, and the screen flickers.]

TERN: We may need the app to reposition the nozzle.

RUNIT: The nozzle is a handle.

[Pava turns it by hand. The stream reaches the cup.]

EXT. PLAZA - FINAL TEST

Runi removes the tablet control and installs a clearly marked push button beside the fountain. Daro presses it; water flows into his cup. Pava fills hers. Tern closes the kiosk.

RUNIT: The app can still report maintenance issues.

DARO: It can report that I drank water.

TERN: That is not a maintenance issue.

[The fountain screen flashes: THANK YOU FOR YOUR FEEDBACK. Daro steps away; the sensor turns the fountain on again and soaks the city map.]

PAVA: It is still listening.

RUNIT: It is supposed to stop when nobody is there.

[The fountain keeps running. Tern places the soaking map over the sensor; the water shuts off.]

TERN: The paper system is working.

PAVA: It does not even need a password.

DARO: Please add that to the report.

[Tern looks at the screen. It asks him to log in. He puts the wet map on top of the tablet.]
