# The Airport Announcer Practices the Wrong Gate

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport gate announcements that use polished language to conceal constant operational confusion*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Announcer Sela, a gibbon; gate agent Odo, a baboon; traveler Miri, a capuchin; and traveler Kori, a macaque.

INT. SMALL AIRPORT GATE - DAY

A display reads BOARDING GROUP ONE. Odo stands behind the counter. Sela holds a microphone and a stack of cards.

SELA: We are now boarding group one for flight 14.

MIRI: My ticket says flight 41.

ODO: That flight uses this gate later.

KORI: The display says 14.

ODO: The display is practicing.

[Sela flips her card. It reads "WELCOME ABOARD." She reads it aloud.]

SELA: Welcome aboard flight 41.

ODO: Not yet.

INT. GATE AREA - MOMENTS LATER

Odo changes the display. It now reads FLIGHT 14 DELAYED. Sela announces the update.

SELA: Flight 14 is delayed by a short operational pause.

KORI: How short?

ODO: We are waiting for the aircraft.

MIRI: Is the aircraft at this airport?

ODO: It is expected to arrive.

[The screen changes to GATE CHANGE. The new gate is the same gate.]

SELA: Please proceed to gate 14.

KORI: We are at gate 14.

SELA: Then remain where you are and proceed emotionally.

ODO: That is not on the card.

INT. GATE AREA - LATER

A plane appears through the window. Odo checks the paperwork. Sela takes out a new card.

ODO: That is the flight 41 aircraft.

MIRI: The display still says flight 14.

KORI: Should we board?

ODO: We need the airline name to match.

[Sela turns the display around. The back reads FLIGHT 41. The front is still 14.]

SELA: Our sign has two destinations.

ODO: I will change it.

[He rotates the sign. The letters fall off and spell "GHT 4."]

INT. GATE AREA - BEFORE FINAL ANNOUNCEMENT

The display flashes BOARDING COMPLETE, though nobody has boarded. Odo checks the scanner while Sela prepares another card.

MIRI: The plane is still outside.

ODO: The scanner counted the luggage cart.

KORI: Does the cart have a seat assignment?

[Odo scans the cart. The display changes to BOARDING GROUP TWO. A suitcase rolls toward the aircraft.]

SELA: We are now boarding one piece of luggage.

ODO: It is ahead of the passengers.

MIRI: At least the suitcase knows where to go.

KORI: It is boarding before the announcement ends.
INT. GATE COUNTER - FINAL ANNOUNCEMENT

Odo holds the broken display while Sela speaks clearly without a card.

SELA: Passengers for flight 41 may board at this gate. Flight 14 remains delayed.

MIRI: That is clear.

KORI: No emotional proceeding?

SELA: Not unless the gate moves.

[The boarding scanner beeps. It accepts Miri's ticket, then rejects Odo's clipboard.]

ODO: I am not a passenger.

SELA: The machine is practicing too.

[Odo turns the display to its blank side. Sela points the waiting travelers toward the aircraft.]
