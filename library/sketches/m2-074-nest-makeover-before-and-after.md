# Nest Makeover: Before and After

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: home makeover television and decluttering transformations*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: DESIGNER RIN, a macaque; HOST VELA, a gibbon; HOMEOWNER TOMA, a capuchin; and CAMERA OPERATOR.

[A branch nest is covered in leaves, fibers, and carefully hidden snacks. A sign reads BEFORE.]

VELA: Toma's nest has storage problems. Rin will transform it in one afternoon.

TOMA: I know where everything is.

RIN: The floor is hidden.

TOMA: The floor is under the things.

VELA: We will reveal a calm, open retreat.

[Rin removes a woven mat. A cache of seeds spills out.]

TOMA: That's my emergency snack.

RIN: It is in the doorway.

TOMA: It was near the doorway.

[The camera operator zooms on a small pile of twigs.]

VELA: What does that pile represent?

TOMA: The corner where I put twigs.

RIN: We can make a vertical storage solution.

[She installs a rack on the branch. The rack tilts immediately.]

TOMA: The branch slopes.

RIN: We can add a level platform.

VELA: The budget has one platform.

TOMA: I need the branch to climb.

[The crew bolts a board across the nest entrance. Toma tries to pass and gets stuck.]

VELA: The new layout is blocking the natural flow.

RIN: We can widen the opening.

TOMA: Or remove the board.

[They remove it. The rack falls, spilling every snack back into the nest.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: That's the reveal.

VELA: The room is now open concept.

TOMA: The seeds are everywhere.

RIN: We can use matching baskets.

TOMA: I do not want my seeds to match.

[The host holds up two baskets, one for snacks and one for twigs.]

VELA: We have created zones.

TOMA: This basket is on the climbing route.

[Rin moves it. It fits neatly beneath the nest ledge.]

RIN: Better?

TOMA: I can climb past it. I can reach the food. I can still see the branch.

VELA: The after shot is ready.

[The camera flips the BEFORE and AFTER signs. The camera captures the same nest from the other side.]

TOMA: Which one is after?

RIN: The one where you know where the basket is.

[The camera operator tries to place a decorative pillow. Toma uses it to cover the snack bowl.]

VELA: That's not the design.

TOMA: It is the design now.

[]


VELA: We are ready for the after shot.

TOMA: The after shot is from where I sleep.

RIN: The camera cannot fit there.

TOMA: Then put the camera outside.

[The camera operator backs out of the entrance. The lens catches only the storage baskets.]

VELA: We need to show the whole transformation.

TOMA: You can show the baskets. I am using the branch.

[Rin replaces the decorative pillow with a plain mat. Toma climbs up, turns twice, then settles.]

RIN: The room works.

VELA: The transformation is subtle.

TOMA: I can find my food without moving the mat.

RIN: That is the reveal.

[The host tries to place a small vase on the ledge. Toma takes it down and uses it to hold two twigs.]

VELA: That was the final accessory.

TOMA: It is a twig holder now.

[The camera operator films the empty ledge. The picture looks exactly like the before shot.]

RIN: We should put the basket in frame.

TOMA: Leave it where it is. I know where it is.

[BLACKOUT.]
