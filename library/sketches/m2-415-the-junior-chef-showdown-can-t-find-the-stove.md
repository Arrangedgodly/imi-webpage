# The Junior Chef Showdown Can't Find the Stove

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking competitions that add suspense before anyone has turned on the equipment*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Host Mara, a macaque; contestant Pip, a capuchin; judge Vex, an orangutan; stage manager Nari, a lemur.

INT. STUDIO KITCHEN - DAY

[A studio timer displays 10:00 and begins counting down. Three spotless counters hold bowls of ingredients. No stove is visible. Mara speaks to camera.]

MARA: Today's challenge is a hot lunch.

PIP: Where is the heat?

NARI: The stoves are arriving.

VEX: The clock has already started.

[The audience gasps. Pip holds up a tomato.]

PIP: Can I make a salad?

VEX: Is it hot?

PIP: It was near the lights.

[Vex taps the bowl.]

VEX: A warm memory of lunch.

PIP: It is a tomato.

MARA: The judges will evaluate temperature, flavor, and presentation.

PIP: I can do two of those.

[He slices the tomato into careful wedges. Vex leans in, waiting for steam. None appears.]

VEX: The suspense is excellent.

[Mara asks Nari to fetch the portable burner. Nari opens a cabinet and removes a hot plate labeled DISPLAY ONLY.]

NARI: This one is for the set.

VEX: Use it.

NARI: It has no cord.

[Pip fans the plate with a recipe card. Vex tastes the warm air above it.]

VEX: A subtle first course.

[The timer reaches eight minutes. Mara calls for a close-up. Pip tosses chopped vegetables in a bowl. One carrot slice lands in Vex's jacket pocket.]

VEX: I have been plated.

[Nari wheels in a small camping stove. It has one burner. Mara announces a dramatic twist: both contestants must cook at once. Pip is the only contestant.]

PIP: Who is the other one?

[Mara points to Vex. He takes off his judge's badge and puts on an apron.]

VEX: I will demonstrate restraint.

[He lights the burner. Pip sets a pan of tomato wedges over it. Vex reaches for the same pan. Their hands meet on the handle. The stove slides across the counter. Nari catches its base and steadies it; Pip keeps the pan over the flame until the wedges soften and sizzle, then lifts the hot pan clear.]

NARI: The equipment is moving.

MARA: The stakes are rising.

[The display reaches 05:00 and gives its halfway chime, exactly halfway through the ten-minute challenge. Vex has not cooked anything. Pip sets the pan on a heatproof pad, stirs the softened wedges, and adds a pinch of salt.]

VEX: Too much seasoning.

PIP: It is one pinch.

VEX: A bold choice under pressure.

[Pip serves a small warm tomato slice. Vex bites the carrot from his pocket.]

MARA: Two dishes. One plate.

VEX: The contestant has made a meal of the challenge.

[Nari finally brings in the full stove. It is unplugged. She searches for the cord while Vex starts the stopwatch again.]

PIP: The tomato is ready.

VEX: It has not had time to develop character.

MARA: The challenge was lunch.

VEX: The challenge was tension.

[Pip lays a hot tomato slice on the trophy base. Its juice dampens the paper ribbon, which slumps against the warm wood.]

NARI: The award is soggy.

FINAL BEAT: Pip finishes his tomato slice while it is still warm. The timer beeps. Vex awards the trophy to the stove for arriving before the next challenge.
