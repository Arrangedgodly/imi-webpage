# One Stop for the Entire Tour

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: guided sightseeing tours that compress an entire region into one carefully branded viewpoint*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Fara, a macaque tour guide; Vim, a slow loris driver; Jex, a capuchin tourist; Lolli, a lemur photographer.

EXT. OVERLOOK PARKING AREA - MORNING

[A TOUR VAN SITS BESIDE A SIGN: THE COMPLETE VALLEY IN SIX MINUTES. FARA HOLDS A FLAG. VIM SITS BEHIND THE WHEEL.]

FARA: Welcome. We will visit the river, the orchard, the old bridge and the mountain.

JEX: Are those separate stops?

FARA: They are all visible from this overlook on a clear day.

Lolli takes a photo of the river. The van's side mirror blocks it.

LOLLI: I can only see the mirror.

VIM: It is the most local part of the view.

Fara unfolds a panorama on a long paper strip. It includes all four sites. She stands at one end while Jex and Lolli hold the other. The paper crosses the road.

FARA: Here is the river. Here is the orchard.

JEX: Which one is in front of us?

FARA: The paper is oriented north.

JEX: I am facing the van.

Fara rotates the strip. The road now points toward the river. A breeze lifts the paper and wraps it around the van's antenna.

VIM: The mountain has taken the map.

Fara grabs the paper. The van's antenna bends. Vim starts the engine to straighten it, but the antenna twirls the map like a flag.

LOLLI: I got a photo of the whole valley.

JEX: Is it on the map?

LOLLI: It's on the antenna.

Fara tells everyone to board. She narrates a tour while the van is parked.

FARA: To our left is the old bridge.

JEX: We're facing the other way.

FARA: The bridge is still to your left if you turn your head.

VIM: If we move, I can show it.

Fara checks the tour schedule. Each landmark has one minute. The first minute is already gone. She points to a patch of orchard visible between two buildings.

FARA: The orchard is just beyond the red roof.

JEX: That's the bakery.

FARA: The orchard is behind the bakery.

LOLLI: Can we stop there?

VIM: We are at the overlook. If I drive to every view, we'll miss the overlook.

Jex asks for the mountain. Fara tilts the map vertically. The strip covers the windshield, showing the mountain at the top and the road at the bottom.

VIM: I cannot drive through a map.

FARA: It's a visual aid.

VIM: It is blocking the actual view.

Lolli frees the antenna and folds the panorama into a small square. The map now shows only the stop where they are standing.

JEX: Is this the whole tour?

FARA: This is the part we can see.

Jex looks past the van and spots the river shining below. Lolli takes a photo. Vim starts the engine and pulls to the curb. The mirror no longer blocks the view.

FARA: We have a new stop.

VIM: We have moved six feet.

LOLLI: The river looks different from here.

[THE TOUR GROUP TAKES A PHOTO. FARA FOLDS THE MAP. IT OPENS AGAIN, SHOWING ONLY THE VAN. VIM PUTS THE TOUR FLAG IN THE WINDOW AND DRIVES TO THE NEXT PARKING SPACE.]
