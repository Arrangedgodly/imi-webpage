# The Busker's Hat Gets a Booking Agent

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: talent agents who negotiate for the performer standing closest to the microphone*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Busker Pip, a capuchin; agent Vex, a macaque; passerby Mara, a gibbon; musician Nari, a lemur; festival runner, a gibbon with a schedule.

EXT. MARKET STREET - AFTERNOON

[Pip plays a small drum beside a hat on the pavement. One coin sits inside it. Vex approaches with a contract.]

VEX: I represent the hat.

PIP: I play for the hat.

VEX: It is getting more attention than you.

[Mara stops and drops a coin into the hat. The coin bounces out and rolls to Nari.]

NARI: The hat rejects payment.

VEX: It wants a guarantee.

[Vex places the contract on the hat. The paper covers the coin. A breeze lifts it. Pip's drumbeat speeds up to keep the page in place.]

PIP: Is this a booking?

VEX: I can get the hat a five-minute slot at the festival.

MARA: What does it do?

VEX: It listens.

[Nari places her fiddle case beside the hat. Vex opens it, thinking it belongs to the hat.]

NARI: That's mine.

VEX: The act has a band.

[Pip taps the hat brim. It makes a hollow thup. Vex listens closely.]

VEX: Percussion.

[Pip taps again. Mara claps. Nari plays a short note. Vex writes "hat-led trio" on the contract.]

PIP: The hat is not playing.

VEX: It is setting the tone.

[Mara puts a coin into the hat. Vex takes it as an advance. The hat tips over when he lifts the contract; the coin rolls back to Mara.]

VEX: The hat has refused my fee.

NARI: It belongs to Pip.

VEX: Then I represent Pip.

PIP: I didn't hire you.

[Vex moves beside Pip and introduces him to a passing crowd. Pip starts drumming. The hat sits farther away, empty. No one stops. Vex repositions Pip beside it. Mara and Nari stop, and both drop coins into the hat.]

VEX: See? The hat is the draw.

PIP: The drum brought them over.

[A festival runner arrives with a schedule.]

RUNNER: We need the hat onstage at four.

PIP: I'm the act.

VEX: The contract says hat.

[The runner picks up the hat. Pip keeps drumming. The crowd follows Pip; the runner carries the empty hat off in the opposite direction.]

MARA: Who has the audience?

NARI: The drummer.

VEX: Who has the booking?

PIP: The hat.

[Vex takes back the contract and scratches out "hat solo." He writes "Pip and hat, shared billing." The hat's brim catches the pencil and flips the paper over.]

VEX: It wants top billing.

[Vex hands Pip the contract. It is folded into a paper hat. Pip puts it over his head.]

NARI: You are wearing your booking.

PIP: Now it works for me.

FINAL BEAT: Vex asks for his commission. Pip points to the hat, now on his own head. Vex drops a coin into it, and Pip plays a short victory roll.
