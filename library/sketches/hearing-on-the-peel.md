# Hearing on the Peel

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Legislative hearings and bureaucratic testimony*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[STAGE: A hearing table with tail slots in every chair. CHAIR MONA, a mandrill, taps a spoon on a water glass. A single banana peel rests in a clear evidence bag. TAVI, a capuchin, waits beside the committee's climbing rail.]

CHAIR MONA: The committee will come to order. We are here to determine how one banana peel came to be in the executive elevator and whether this requires a new policy.

CLERK: The peel is marked Exhibit A.

CHAIR MONA: Is Exhibit A the peel?

CLERK: It may be the peel. The label says "banana-related material."

CHAIR MONA: I ask unanimous consent to call it a peel.

MEMBER GUS: Objection. It may be plantain.

CHAIR MONA: Is there a motion to examine the peel?

MEMBER GUS: I move to examine Exhibit A after we define examine.

CHAIR MONA: The motion is pending a definition. Witness, state your name.

WITNESS TAVI, FACILITIES: Tavi. I clean the elevators.

CHAIR MONA: Did you see the banana peel?

TAVI: Yes. It was on the floor.

CHAIR MONA: How did it get there?

TAVI: Someone dropped it.

MEMBER GUS: Do you have evidence of a drop?

TAVI: The peel was on the floor and someone's shoe had a banana-shaped smear.

GUS: Could the smear have arrived independently?

TAVI: It was stuck to the shoe.

GUS: Shoes move. The peel may have chosen the elevator.

CHAIR MONA: The committee will avoid assigning motive to the peel.

[THE CHAIR'S ASSISTANT WHISPERS.]

CHAIR MONA: I have been informed that the executive elevator has a waste bin. Why was the peel not in it?

TAVI: The bin was full of printouts of the new peel policy.

GUS: How many pages?

TAVI: Eight hundred and twelve.

CHAIR MONA: The policy is eighty pages.

TAVI: It says to print ten copies.

GUS: That is 800, plus the email thread.

CHAIR MONA: We are investigating the email thread separately.

MEMBER LUZ: I would like to ask the witness about the proposed peel disposal workflow. Does the worker scan the peel, photograph it, or enter its estimated weight?

TAVI: I pick it up with a paper towel and put it in compost.

LUZ: How do you verify that the compost accepted it?

TAVI: The compost bin is right there.

LUZ: Does it provide a receipt?

TAVI: No.

LUZ: Then I cannot support the process.

CHAIR MONA: The committee has five minutes left. We will now hear from the peel itself.

[THE CLERK LIFTS THE BAG. SILENCE.] 

CHAIR MONA: Does Exhibit A wish to make a statement?

GUS: It has not been sworn in.

CHAIR MONA: Clerk, administer the oath.

CLERK: Do you swear to tell the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth?

[MORE SILENCE.] 

CHAIR MONA: The record will show the peel declined to comment.

TAVI: Can I take it to compost now?

CHAIR MONA: Only if the committee authorizes transfer.

LUZ: I move to establish a subcommittee on peel transfer.

GUS: Second.

CHAIR MONA: So moved. The subcommittee will meet next month, after the facility audit. Tavi, please leave the peel on the table.

[TAVI QUIETLY REMOVES THE PEEL AND DROPS IT INTO THE COMPOST BIN.] 

CHAIR MONA: The committee did not authorize that.

TAVI: The committee was still talking.

CHAIR MONA: Then we will investigate the disappearance of Exhibit A at our next hearing.

[THE SPOON TAPS THE GLASS.]
