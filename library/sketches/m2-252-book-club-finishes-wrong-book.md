# The Book Club Finishes the Wrong Book

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: book clubs that perform confidence about a title no one has finished*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Moderator Kessa, a gibbon; member Daro, a macaque; member Venn, a baboon; and librarian Miri, a capuchin.

INT. LIBRARY MEETING ROOM - EVENING

Four chairs form a circle. One very thick novel sits on a stand. Kessa has arranged color-coded tabs along its edge.

KESSA: Welcome. Tonight we discuss chapter thirty-two through the ending.

DARO: I have read chapter one.

VENN: I have read the ending.

MIRI: I checked it out for both of you.

KESSA: Excellent. We have the beginning and the end. Let us discuss the middle.

DARO: The hero has a strong arc.

MIRI: Which hero?

DARO: The one from the beginning.

VENN: My ending has a different hero.

[Kessa flips to the final page. The book's middle falls open and a bookmark slips to the floor.]

KESSA: The story seems to have changed direction.

MIRI: That is the plot.

INT. MEETING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Kessa draws a chart on a whiteboard: BEGINNING, MIDDLE, END. Daro puts his one chapter under beginning; Venn puts the last page under end.

KESSA: We can reconstruct the middle through themes.

VENN: The ending has a boat.

DARO: The opening has a hat.

MIRI: The middle has the chapter where the hat falls into the boat.

[Kessa draws a line between the two cards. It loops around the board and tangles with the marker cord.]

KESSA: A circular structure.

MIRI: It is a cord.

VENN: I expected the hat to return.

DARO: It did, as a boat accessory.

KESSA: That is a satisfying interpretation.

MIRI: It is written in the book.

INT. MEETING ROOM - LATER

Kessa reveals a discussion question card: WHAT SURPRISED YOU MOST?

KESSA: Daro?

DARO: The middle.

KESSA: What about it?

DARO: I did not know there was so much of it.

VENN: I was surprised the boat was not a hat.

MIRI: Neither of those is a surprise in the actual novel.

[Kessa opens the book to the middle. A paper map unfolds across the table, covering everyone's notes.]

KESSA: The author has given us a map.

MIRI: That is the foldout map from chapter seventeen.

VENN: I thought the meeting was about the ending.

KESSA: It is. We are finding out how we got there.

[She traces the map. Daro and Venn follow with their fingers until both point at the same library entrance.]

DARO: The characters came back here?

MIRI: The characters came to a harbor.

KESSA: But the discussion has reached a destination.

INT. LIBRARY MEETING ROOM - CLOSING

Miri places two checkout slips beside the book.

MIRI: You can borrow it again and read the middle.

DARO: Will there be a meeting before then?

KESSA: We could discuss the cover.

VENN: I have not read it.

KESSA: Perfect. We will all bring our own interpretation.

[Miri closes the book. Its heavy cover knocks the color tabs loose; they land in a neat row from beginning to end.]

MIRI: You have already made a reading schedule.

KESSA: We are prepared for the next book.
