# The Future Is in the Seed Jar

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fortune-telling performances and their dependence on vague predictions*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: SEER NARA, a lemur; CLIENT PIP, a macaque; ASSISTANT REX, a gibbon; and a jar of seeds.

[A dim tent. A jar of mixed seeds sits on a velvet cloth. Nara peers into it.] 

NARA: I see a turning point in your future.

PIP: Which direction?

NARA: The one you choose.

REX: A powerful reading.

PIP: I came to ask whether I should move my nest to the lower branch.

NARA: The seeds reveal a change in height.

PIP: Is the lower branch safe?

NARA: The future is not a building inspection.

PIP: I can check it.

REX: Our service includes one follow-up question.

PIP: Can I try the branch before moving my nest?

NARA: I see a trial period.

PIP: I can sit there this afternoon.

NARA: The seeds are unusually clear.

[REX SHAKES THE JAR. A SEED STICKS TO THE CLOTH.] 

REX: A sign has appeared.

PIP: The jar has a crack.

NARA: The seed is asking for freedom.

PIP: It is stuck in the velvet.

[HE LIFTS THE SEED. A SMALL HOLE IS VISIBLE IN THE JAR.] 

REX: The future is leaking.

NARA: We should repair the jar before another seed escapes.

PIP: I can take it to the tool shelf.

REX: The client is now part of the reading.

PIP: I am part of the repair.

[THEY WRAP THE JAR IN A CLOTH AND CARRY IT TO THE SHELF.] 

NARA: The stars suggest you will need a strip of bark.

PIP: The tool shelf has twine.

NARA: The stars are not familiar with the shelf.

REX: We can offer a practical reading. Check the branch, try it while awake, and move your nest only if it holds.

NARA: That is less mystical.

PIP: It is more useful.

NARA: The seeds agree.

REX: The seeds are in a jar.

NARA: And the jar is now repaired.

PIP: I tried the lower branch. It is wide and the support is firm.

NARA: Did the seeds predict that?

PIP: I checked it in daylight.

REX: We should record the prediction before hearing the result.

NARA: I predicted a change in height.

PIP: That could mean anything.

NARA: I can be more precise. You will test the lower branch before moving your nest.

PIP: I already did.

REX: The prediction has been confirmed.

PIP: It was my plan, not a prophecy.

NARA: The jar is repaired, the branch is safe, and the client has made a choice.

REX: We could charge for a second reading.

NARA: No. The follow-up question is included.

PIP: I will move my nest tomorrow. I will keep the old one until the new spot feels right.

NARA: That is a sensible transition.

REX: The reading has given the client a schedule.

PIP: The reading asked questions. I checked the branch.

NARA: Perhaps the clearest fortune is the one you investigate yourself.

REX: That is a good line for the sign.

PIP: Put it next to the jar, not over the entrance.

[REX MOVES THE SIGN ASIDE. THE TENT DOOR OPENS FREELY.]

NARA: Your future is in your hands.

PIP: So is the jar.
