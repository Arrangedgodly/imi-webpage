# The Very Last Slice

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: restaurant tasting menus that turn one piece of fruit into a chain of solemnly narrated courses*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Chef Miri, a lemur; server Pito, a capuchin; critic Vela, a macaque; and Kito, a baboon kitchen porter.

INT. TINY RESTAURANT - NIGHT

Pito brings a single fig on a large plate. Vela leans forward. Miri watches from the kitchen window.

PITO: Course one: the anticipation.

VELA: Is there food?

PITO: The fig arrives in three movements.

He turns the plate slightly. The fig stays still. Vela waits. Kito brings a spoon the size of a leaf and sets it beside the plate.

MIRI: The spoon is larger than the course.

PITO: It is a palate guide.

Vela takes a bite. Pito announces the fig's notes: sweetness, shade, and a hint of yesterday's rain. Vela asks what the next course is. Pito returns to the kitchen, comes back with an empty plate and says it is the memory of the fig.

VELA: I remember the fig.

PITO: Then the course has succeeded.

Miri sends out a small bowl of water. Pito calls it a cleansing course. Kito points out it is the kitchen's rinse bowl. Pito takes it back. Vela says she would like another bite. Miri divides the remaining fig into two pieces with a careful knife.

PITO: Course three is the division.

Vela eats one piece. Kito carries the second to the sink by mistake, thinking it is a scrap. Pito rushes after him. They return the piece to the plate, now wet on one side.

MIRI: It has gained a river.

Pito presents it as a variation. Vela takes one bite and asks for the bill. The bill lists six courses, including anticipation, memory and division. Vela asks where the actual dinner is.

MIRI: We have one more course: the conclusion.

Kito enters with a banana. Pito blocks him.

PITO: That is for staff.

KITO: I brought it from the kitchen.

Vela looks at the banana. Miri says the tasting is complete. Kito peels the banana and offers Vela half. Pito begins to narrate the gesture as a communal finale. Vela eats the half before he can finish.

VELA: That was a meal.

Pito sets down the remaining half. The bill still includes the last course. Miri crosses out "conclusion" and writes "banana." Vela pays for the fruit. Kito takes the tasting menu and folds it into a napkin holder.

Miri checks the plate and confirms there is no more fruit. Pito offers Vela a printed receipt for the memory course. She folds it around the last napkin and carries it out.

PITO: The menu has found a new purpose.

MIRI: It is holding napkins.

The critic leaves satisfied. Pito turns to the next table and announces the menu. Kito brings another banana and hides it behind the kitchen door.
