# The Fountain Opening Has No Water

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: civic unveilings that schedule a public attraction before the practical work is finished*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Mayor Oda, a gibbon; Technician Fenn, a macaque; Host Miri, a capuchin; and Resident Laru, a baboon.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

A fountain is covered with a cloth. Miri holds scissors. Oda stands at a lectern. Fenn checks a hose behind the fountain.

MIRI: We are opening the new fountain at eleven.

FENN: The water valve is still closed.

ODA: The ceremony is at eleven.

LARU: Is the fountain ready?

MIRI: We are ready to unveil it.

[FENN TURNS THE VALVE. A PIPE KNOCKS, BUT NO WATER FLOWS.]

FENN: There is air in the line.

ODA: Is that part of the show?

FENN: It is part of the pipe.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

Miri hands Oda a speech about the fountain's future. Fenn kneels by a drain cover. Laru watches.

ODA: This fountain will bring people together.

LARU: It has brought us together around a dry basin.

MIRI: The unveiling will happen after the speech.

FENN: I need to clear the drain.

ODA: Can you wait until after the ribbon?

MIRI: The ribbon is tied to the lectern.

LARU: The lectern is in front of the fountain.

FENN: I need the drain cover open.

ODA: Can we move the lectern?

[THEY LIFT THE LECTERN. ITS SPEECH CARD SLIDES OFF AND LANDS IN THE DRY BASIN.]

MIRI: The speech has reached the fountain.

LARU: The fountain remains dry.

FENN: The ribbon is across the drain.

[ODO LIFTS THE RIBBON. IT SNAGS ON THE DRAIN COVER.]

MIRI: The ribbon has opened the fountain early.

FENN: The ribbon has blocked the drain.

[LARU PULLS IT FREE. A SMALL SPLASH OF OLD WATER SPRAYS THE LECTERN.]

ODA: That was the first water.

FENN: That was water from the pipe.

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - OPENING

Fenn clears the drain and opens the valve. The fountain starts with a weak trickle, then a strong jet shoots up, soaking the cloth and lectern.

MIRI: The fountain is open.

ODA: The speech is wet.

LARU: It says the fountain will bring people together.

[THE JET TURNS TOWARD THE CROWD. EVERYONE TAKES ONE STEP BACK IN UNISON.]

FENN: The nozzle is aimed too high.

MIRI: Can we adjust it after the photograph?

FENN: We can adjust it now.

[HE TURNS THE NOZZLE. THE WATER FALLS INTO THE BASIN. THE CROWD APPLAUDS.]

ODA: That is the opening shot.

LARU: And the first useful thing we have seen.

MIRI: The cloth can be dried for the photograph.

ODA: The speech card is still in the basin.

[FENN FISHES IT OUT WITH A LONG-HANDLED NET.]

FENN: The speech has been rescued.

ODA: Keep it away from the water.

[MIRI WRINGS OUT THE RIBBON. IT DRIPS INTO THE FOUNTAIN, WHICH CONTINUES RUNNING AT A SENSIBLE HEIGHT.]
