# The Launch of the New Spoon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: product launch events that build hype around a minor redesign and a single useful feature*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Miri, a macaque presenter; Pavo, a capuchin product engineer; Toma, a gibbon executive; and a demo volunteer, Seli, a lemur.

INT. SHOWROOM - DAY

A covered pedestal stands beneath a spotlight. Miri holds a microphone.

MIRI: Today we reveal a tool that changes how we stir.

Toma unveils a spoon with a longer handle. Pavo explains the handle keeps fingers farther from hot soup. The audience applauds. A volunteer asks if it can scoop soup. Pavo says yes, as can the previous spoon.

TOMA: But this one has a softer curve.

Miri shows a chart comparing the new spoon to the old spoon. Both have a bowl and a handle. The new one has a tiny blue stripe.

MIRI: The stripe indicates the stirring direction.

Seli tries the spoon in a cup. She turns it clockwise. The stripe disappears beneath the soup.

SELI: I cannot see the direction.

Pavo says the stripe is visible when the spoon is dry. Toma calls this a breakthrough in pre-stirring awareness. Miri presents a carrying case. The case is larger than the spoon and has a latch requiring two hands.

A customer asks if the spoon is available now. Toma says the first batch ships in six weeks. Pavo asks if they can use the prototype. Toma says it is not approved for unscheduled soup.

Seli uses the spoon to stir the demo cup. It works. The old spoon sits beside it and also works. Miri asks the audience which feels more innovative. Someone says the long handle is helpful. Toma writes that down.

Pavo pours more soup into the cup. The spoon's blue stripe floats off. Seli catches it with the old spoon.

PAVO: The feature has separated from the product.

Toma calls it an optional accessory. Miri asks whether there will be a launch event for the stripe. Toma says the stripe deserves its own stage.

Seli hands the wet spoon to Toma. He tries to stir, but the long handle hits the edge of the cup and splashes soup onto the display chart. The audience leans back. Pavo notices the cup is too small for the new handle. He switches to a bowl. It fits.

PAVO: The spoon works best in a bowl.

MIRI: We have discovered a use case.

Toma asks if anyone has a bowl. The audience raises several. Seli borrows one and stirs the soup. The spoon works well. Miri removes the cover from the old spoon and places it beside the new one.

MIRI: Two tools, one kitchen.

Toma asks the crowd to pre-order. No one does. They are eating soup from the bowls instead. Pavo turns off the spotlight and carries the spare spoons back to the kitchen.
