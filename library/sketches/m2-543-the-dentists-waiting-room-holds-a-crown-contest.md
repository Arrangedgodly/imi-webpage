# The Dentist's Waiting Room Holds a Crown Contest

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: pageant competitions and dental waiting rooms where every patient receives a different kind of crown*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: DENTIST, an orangutan; RECEPTIONIST, a macaque; PATIENT, a baboon; PAGEANT HOST, a gibbon; ASSISTANT, a capuchin.

[A dental waiting room. A portable stage has been set up beside the fish tank. A banner reads CROWN NIGHT. The patient sits in the chair with a paper bib.]

PATIENT: Is this a pageant?

RECEPTIONIST: The community center canceled its room.

PAGEANT HOST: We need one final contestant.

DENTIST: The patient needs a dental crown.

[The host places a sparkly sash around the patient's shoulders. The assistant brings a tray with a metal tooth crown.]

PATIENT: That crown is smaller than the sash.

ACT ONE

[The pageant host asks the patient to describe a talent. The patient taps the paper bib like a drum. The fish tank bubbles on the beat.]

HOST: A dramatic performance.

DENTIST: The bubbles mean the filter is working.

[The dentist checks the patient's X-ray. The crown must be fitted after the tooth is prepared. The host announces a swimsuit round. The receptionist points to a sign saying PLEASE KEEP SHOES ON.]

RECEPTIONIST: We are not changing outfits in the waiting room.

[The pageant host offers the patient a glittering paper crown. The patient tries it, but it rests on the dental bib and slides down over their nose.]

PATIENT: I cannot see the dentist.

DENTIST: I cannot see your tooth.

[The assistant removes the paper crown and places it on the fish tank. The fish swim through its open points. The host calls for judges. The dentist, assistant, and receptionist raise scorecards, but the dentist's cards are labeled LEFT and RIGHT.]

HOST: Those are not numbers.

DENTIST: I use them for teeth.

[The patient begins to panic about the metal crown. The dentist explains each step plainly and shows it on a model tooth. The pageant host whispers that a calm smile is a good talent. The patient relaxes. The assistant checks the fit on the model, not the patient's mouth.]

DENTIST: We will fit the crown after numbing the tooth.

PATIENT: And the contest?

HOST: You win for staying in the chair.

[The dentist notices the host's sash is caught on the rolling instrument cart. The host backs up; the cart rolls forward. The assistant stops it with a foot. The pageant stage is moved away from the treatment area.]

ACT TWO

[The dentist fits the metal crown, checks the bite, and gives the patient a care card. The patient returns to the waiting room. The host gives them a ribbon reading MOST COURAGEOUS. The patient asks for a mirror.]

PATIENT: I can smile now.

DENTIST: Carefully until the numbness fades.

[The host announces the winner. The receptionist applauds with one hand while holding the appointment book with the other.]

FINAL IMAGE: The paper crown floats in the fish tank as the patient carries the care card home.
