# The Weather Balloon Has a Press Agent

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public science events that turn a simple launch into a media spectacle*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Science leader Nima, a capuchin; publicist Vela, a gibbon; student Kori, a macaque; and technician Odo, a baboon.

EXT. SCHOOL FIELD - MORNING

A weather balloon rests on a tarp. Kori checks a small sensor package. Vela positions a banner and microphone.

NIMA: We will launch the balloon, collect temperature and pressure data, then recover the sensor.

VELA: First, the announcement.

KORI: The balloon is ready.

VELA: Wait for the camera crew. We need a countdown and a dramatic reveal.

ODO: The wind is changing.

[The balloon tugs at its tether. Vela pulls the banner into position; the banner catches the same gust and wraps around her.]

VELA: The reveal is happening to me.

EXT. FIELD - MOMENTS LATER

Nima frees Vela. Kori reads the sensor. Odo holds the tether.

NIMA: We have a short launch window.

VELA: We need the sponsor's logo visible in the launch shot.

KORI: The logo is on the back of the balloon.

VELA: Then rotate it toward the camera.

[They turn the balloon. The sensor package swings and bumps the tripod.]

ODO: We should stop moving it.

NIMA: The readings have begun.

VELA: Can we restart the data after the press photo?

KORI: The air will not wait for the photo.

[The publicist sets up a second shot. A gust lifts the banner again, this time covering the sensor screen.]

EXT. FIELD - LAUNCH

Nima signals. Odo releases the tether. The balloon rises cleanly. Vela steps into the camera frame holding the banner; its lower edge catches a fence post.

VELA: Keep filming.

[The balloon floats upward while the banner remains snagged. Vela is gently pulled forward until Kori unhooks it.]

KORI: The balloon has launched.

NIMA: The data is recording.

ODO: The banner is grounded.

VELA: We got the launch shot.

KORI: The shot has no sensor.

NIMA: We can show the actual data after the flight.

EXT. FIELD - RECOVERY

The balloon returns by parachute and lands safely in the grass. Kori retrieves the sensor. The data screen shows a clear graph. Vela arranges the banner behind it.

VELA: This will make a perfect announcement.

[The banner flips in the wind and covers the graph. Vela holds it down. The wind then lifts her press badge and pins it over the sponsor logo.]

NIMA: The graph is readable now.

KORI: It shows the temperature changed during the launch.

ODO: And the wind moved the banner every time we needed it still.

VELA: The event had strong visual movement.

[She turns the banner around. The plain back reads WEATHER BALLOON LAUNCH. Kori holds up the graph beside it.]

KORI: Science and the announcement, in one frame.

[The banner blows flat over the camera lens.]

VELA: That is a strong closing image.
