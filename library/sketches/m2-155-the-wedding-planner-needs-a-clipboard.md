# The Wedding Planner Needs a Clipboard

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: event planning television where timelines matter more than the people expected to use them*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Planner Tali, a gibbon; couple Miri and Odo, macaques; musician Fenn, a capuchin; and helper Pavi, a baboon.

[A WEDDING ARCH STANDS BESIDE A TREE. TALI HOLDS A DETAILED SCHEDULE.]

TALI: Ceremony at four, vows at four-oh-five, photo at four-ten.

MIRI: We want to start when our families arrive.

TALI: They are scheduled for four.

[THE FAMILIES ARRIVE AT THREE-FIFTY.]

ODO: They are here now.

TALI: We can hold them in the waiting area.

[THE WAITING AREA IS UNDER THE TREE. MONKEYS BEGIN GROOMING.]

TALI: Please keep the guests in a single line.

PAVI: They are greeting one another.

TALI: That is not on the schedule.

[FENN PLAYS A TUNE. THE GUESTS MOVE TOWARD THE ARCH.]

TALI: Music is scheduled after the vows.

FENN: They are already dancing.

[MIRI AND ODO JOIN THEM.]

TALI: We are twelve minutes ahead.

MIRI: We are together.

[TALI LOOKS AT THE CLIPBOARD.]

TALI: The photo has not been taken.

PAVI: The photographer is here.

[THE PHOTOGRAPHER ASKS EVERYONE TO HOLD STILL. A SMALL MONKEY CLIMBS THE ARCH.]

TALI: The arch is not rated for climbing.

ODO: It is a tree branch.

[THE MONKEY SETTLES ABOVE THE COUPLE.]

TALI: We cannot take the photo with a monkey on the arch.

MIRI: He is part of the family.

[TALI SCRATCHES OUT THE SCHEDULE.]

TALI: We will take it when everyone is ready.

[EVERYONE LOOKS AT THE CAMERA. THE MONKEY LOOKS AWAY.]

FENN: He is not ready.

[TALI WAITS. THE MONKEY TURNS, AND THE CAMERA FLASHES.]

TALI: We are now on schedule.

MIRI: What time is it?

TALI: The time the photo happened.

[THE COUPLE TAKES THE CLIPBOARD AND USES IT TO FAN THEMSELVES.]

ODO: That is the first useful schedule today.

[TALI TRIES TO PUT THE FAMILIES BACK INTO THEIR PLACES.]

TALI: The relatives should stand behind the couple.

MIRI: My grandmother should be beside me.

ODO: My brother is holding the rings.

PAVI: Then the photo should show who is here.

[TALI LOOKS AT THE SCHEDULE, THEN AT THE GUESTS.]

TALI: We can change the arrangement.

FENN: I will play while you move.

[THE GUESTS SHIFT AROUND THE COUPLE. THE ARCH CREAKS.]

TALI: One at a time.

[TALI TRIES TO PUT THE FAMILIES BACK INTO THEIR PLACES.]

TALI: The relatives should stand behind the couple.

MIRI: My grandmother should be beside me.

ODO: My brother is holding the rings.

PAVI: Then the photo should show who is here.

[TALI LOOKS AT THE SCHEDULE, THEN AT THE GUESTS.]

TALI: We can change the arrangement.

FENN: I will play while you move.

[THE GUESTS SHIFT AROUND THE COUPLE. THE ARCH CREAKS.]

TALI: One at a time.

[TALI JOINS THE DANCE. SHE STILL HOLDS THE PEN.]
