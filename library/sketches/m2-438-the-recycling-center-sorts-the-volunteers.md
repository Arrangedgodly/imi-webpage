# The Recycling Center Sorts the Volunteers

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: municipal recycling campaigns using humans as props for sorting demonstrations*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Coordinator Nari, a lemur; volunteer Pip, a capuchin; inspector Mara, a macaque; host Vex, an orangutan.

INT. RECYCLING CENTER - DAY

[Three bins are labeled PAPER, METAL, and PEOPLE WHO ASK QUESTIONS. Vex stands beside a demonstration table.]

VEX: Today we sort responsibly.

MARA: Why is there a people bin?

NARI: It was labeled "plastic," but the sign fell off.

PIP: I asked a question. Where do I go?

VEX: Please stand beside the table until we establish your material.

[Pip steps onto a cardboard box. It collapses. He lands sitting among flattened cartons.]

PIP: Mostly cardboard, apparently.

MARA: That's not how the bins work.

VEX: We are demonstrating what happens when labels are unclear.

NARI: The labels are clear.

[Vex points to the bins. A paper cup is in METAL; a metal can is in PAPER.]

MARA: These are reversed.

VEX: The materials are exploring new categories.

[Nari takes the cup from the metal bin and places it in paper. Pip hands her a can. The can rolls across the floor and knocks the PEOPLE sign flat.]


[Mara taps the cup, then the can.]

MARA: One is paper. One is metal.

VEX: And where do volunteers go?

NARI: They choose a task.

PIP: I choose to flatten boxes.

[Pip folds a carton into a small hat and places it on the table.]

PIP: Reuse.

VEX: Is this a new role?

MARA: It is a temporary container.

[Vex tries to straighten the hat; it folds into a box around his ears.]

PIP: The sign has sorted itself.

[The can rolls under Vex's presentation table. Vex crawls after it and emerges with a cardboard crown stuck on his head.]

VEX: I found a mixed-material item.

MARA: That's a crown from last week's parade.

NARI: Cardboard goes in paper.

VEX: The crown is on my head.

PIP: Then it belongs in "people who ask questions."

[The volunteers laugh. Mara begins checking bins while Vex tries to remove the crown without bending it. It catches on his ears.]

VEX: Please do not classify the host during the presentation.

NARI: We need one example for each bin.

[Pip lifts the paper sign and turns it over. The back reads "REUSE." He props it over the metal bin.]

PIP: This one can be a sign again.

MARA: That's reuse, not recycling.

VEX: We can demonstrate both. The campaign has two segments.

[The can rolls back out from under the table. Nari stops it with her shoe and places it in METAL. Pip flattens the cardboard crown, then lays it in PAPER. Vex finally frees his head.]

VEX: I have been successfully separated from my packaging.

MARA: Now we can sort the volunteers.

NARI: Into what?

[She hands Pip the tongs. Pip hands Vex the label maker.]

PIP: Useful tools go with useful people.

VEX: And where do I go?

MARA: Back behind the table.

[The PEOPLE sign falls again and lands face up in the empty bin.]

PIP: That one is for questions.

VEX: Put me in it.
