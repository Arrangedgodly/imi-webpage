# The Display Sandwich Is Not for Sale

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: retail display rules that confuse sample merchandise with the real product*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Yuni, a capuchin deli clerk; Brok, a gibbon display manager; Palla, a macaque customer; Teri, a lemur trainee.

INT. DELI COUNTER - LUNCHTIME

[A GLASS CASE HOLDS A PERFECT SANDWICH UNDER A SIGN: TODAY'S SPECIAL. A SMALL CARD BESIDE IT SAYS DISPLAY ONLY.]

PALLA: I'd like the sandwich.

YUNI: The one in the case?

PALLA: The one on the sign.

Brok emerges holding a clipboard.

BROK: That's a display sandwich. It shows the proportions.

PALLA: I would like those proportions for lunch.

Teri looks at the display. The sandwich has a toothpick flag and a slice of tomato that is brighter than any tomato in the shop.

TERI: We made it with cardboard.

PALLA: The whole thing?

BROK: Bread, lettuce, tomato. The meat is painted felt.

Palla looks at the price on the sign. It matches the real sandwich.

PALLA: Can I buy the one you make?

Yuni says the kitchen is waiting for the display to be approved. Brok measures the display with a ruler. The bottom slice is one centimeter thinner than the top.

BROK: We need a consistent profile.

Teri asks how she can make a real sandwich match the display. Brok places a real slice of bread on the case and compares it. The bread sags under the glass sign.

BROK: The bread is bending.

YUNI: It is bread.

Palla asks if she can take the display home. Brok refuses because it is part of the counter layout. She asks whether the little flag is included.

BROK: The flag is not edible.

PALLA: It is paper. Neither is the sign.

Teri takes a fresh sandwich from the kitchen. The tomatoes are small and the bread is irregular. Brok asks her to rotate it until it looks like the display. Teri turns it. The lettuce slides out.

YUNI: We can serve it as is.

BROK: The menu photo shows it stacked.

Palla catches the lettuce and puts it back into the sandwich. Brok checks the sign and sees "Today's special." The special ends at noon, and it is 12:01.

PALLA: Then what is it now?

BROK: Yesterday's special.

Palla asks Yuni to charge her for the sign price. Yuni scans the display card. The register prints "display item, zero ingredients." Brok says that is correct. Teri tries scanning the real sandwich, but the register still shows yesterday's price. Palla asks for a discount because she arrived one minute late.

YUNI: The special is still good.

BROK: The sign is no longer current.

PALLA: The sandwich is.

Yuni pulls down the sign. The display sandwich is revealed to have been holding up the card. Without it, the card falls into the case. Teri lifts the sign, and the display remains upright on a toothpick.

TERI: It can still be sold.

BROK: It is not food.

Palla orders the real sandwich. Yuni wraps it. Brok sets his clipboard on the open menu cupboard while he helps Teri take the display sandwich to the back room. The cardboard sandwich is used to prop the cupboard door open; when Teri removes it, the door swings shut on Brok's clipboard.

[THE MENU CUPBOARD CLOSES. BROK'S CLIPBOARD GETS CAUGHT INSIDE. HE ORDERS A DISPLAY CLIPBOARD TO KEEP THE REAL ONE SAFE.]
