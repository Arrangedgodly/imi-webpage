# The Community Theater Has One Door

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: community theater run-throughs and stage managers who manage entrances as if the building were a character*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Director Pavi, a gibbon; stage manager Miri, a macaque; actor Odo, a baboon; and usher Tali, a capuchin.

[THE STAGE HAS A DOORWAY MARKED "ENTRANCE." THE AUDIENCE SITS IN A SMALL ROW.]

MIRI: Odo, enter on the cue.

ODO: Which cue?

PAVI: The line is "I have returned."

ODO: I have returned.

MIRI: You are still backstage.

ODO: I am speaking from backstage.

PAVI: That is not an entrance.

[ODO WALKS THROUGH THE DOOR. THE DOOR STICKS.]

ODO: I have returned.

MIRI: The door is not opening.

[ODO PUSHES. THE DOOR OPENS ALL THE WAY AND HITS A PROP TREE.]

PAVI: The tree is in the way.

TALI: It is supposed to be a forest.

MIRI: The actor cannot enter through the forest.

[THEY MOVE THE TREE. ODO RETURNS BACKSTAGE.]

PAVI: We will try again.

MIRI: On the cue.

[TALI RINGS A BELL. ODO ENTERS. HE HOLDS THE DOOR FOR THE NEXT ACTOR.]

ODO: I have returned.

PAVI: That was good. Let go of the door.

TALI: It is trying to stay in the play.

[THE DOOR CLOSES ON THE NEXT ACTOR'S TAIL.]

TALI: The tail has entered before the actor.

MIRI: We need a door holder.

[ODO TAKES A POSITION BESIDE THE DOOR.]

PAVI: You are still in the scene.

ODO: I have returned.

MIRI: You are holding the door.

ODO: The line is working.

[PAVI POINTS TO THE SCRIPT.]

PAVI: The next character enters from the other side.

[TALI OPENS A SECOND DOOR. IT LEADS TO THE COAT ROOM.]

TALI: This door is not on the set.

MIRI: It can be.

[PAVI LOOKS AT THE AUDIENCE.]

PAVI: We are changing the blocking.

[THE ACTORS TRY THE SCENE WITH BOTH DOORS. ODO ENTERS THROUGH ONE AND EXITS THROUGH THE OTHER.]

ODO: I have returned.

PAVI: You have left again.

MIRI RINGS THE BELL. THE DOOR OPENS THE WRONG WAY AND PUSHES ODO BACKSTAGE.

ODO: I have returned.

PAVI: You have not left.

TALI: The door is an actor now.

MIRI: It has two entrances and no lines.

[THEY REVERSE THE DOOR SWING. ODO OPENS IT WITH HIS ELBOW WHILE HOLDING THE PROP TREE.]

PAVI: Better.

ODO: I have returned.

[THE PROP TREE FALLS OVER. ODO CATCHES IT AGAIN.]

[THE AUDIENCE LAUGHS. MIRI MAKES A NOTE.]

MIRI: We can keep both doors.

TALI: The coat room is open to the audience.

[AN AUDIENCE MEMBER WALKS THROUGH WITH A COAT.]

PAVI: That is not part of the play.

ODO: They have returned.

[MIRI RINGS THE BELL. THE ACTORS ALL ENTER AT ONCE THROUGH BOTH DOORS.]

PAVI: We have an ending.

TALI: And no coats.

MIRI: The door is now part of the blocking.

[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS. ODO HOLDS THE ORIGINAL DOOR OPEN FOR EVERYONE TO EXIT.]
