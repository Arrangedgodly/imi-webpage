# The Trophy Has Two Handles

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Awards ceremonies, acceptance speeches, and prestige television pacing*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Presenter Lolli, a lemur; nominee Pavi, a macaque; nominee Greb, a gorilla; stagehand Tuma, a capuchin; announcer Venn, a baboon.

[STAGE: AN AWARDS STAGE WITH A SHINY TROPHY, TWO PODIUMS, AND AN ENVELOPE SEALED WITH A LEAF.]

VENN: Welcome to the Golden Branch Awards. Please keep speeches shorter than the ceremony.
LOLLI: Our first award is for Best Shared Nest.
PAVI: I am nominated.
GREB: So am I.
LOLLI: There is one trophy.
TUMA: It has two handles.
LOLLI: Convenient. And the winner is-
[THE ENVELOPE WILL NOT OPEN.]
VENN: A suspenseful pause.
PAVI: It is stuck to the leaf.
GREB: The leaf is part of the seal.
LOLLI: I will open it dramatically.
[LOLLI PULLS. THE TROPHY TILTS.]
TUMA: Careful. The trophy is on a loose platform.
LOLLI: That is an award-winning platform.
VENN: The winner is Greb.
GREB: I would like to thank my nestmates.
PAVI: I helped build the nest.
GREB: Then I thank you too.
LOLLI: You may share the trophy.
TUMA: It has two handles.
PAVI: We can each hold one.
GREB: What if we disagree?
TUMA: The trophy stays between you.
VENN: Our next award is for Best Dramatic Exit.
LOLLI: The nominees are Pavi, Greb, and the envelope.
PAVI: The envelope cannot exit.
TUMA: It has already stuck to the podium.
LOLLI: Winner: the envelope.
VENN: Please come to the stage.
[THE ENVELOPE REMAINS STUCK.]
GREB: A moving performance.
PAVI: It is not moving.
LOLLI: It is holding the moment.
TUMA: I can free it with a spoon.
VENN: Please wait for the commercial break.
PAVI: There is no commercial break.
LOLLI: Then we will have a ceremonial pause.
[EVERYONE HOLDS STILL.]
GREB: How long is the pause?
LOLLI: Until the envelope releases.
TUMA: I have the spoon.
PAVI: Is the spoon nominated?
LOLLI: For Best Supporting Utensil.
VENN: The winner is the spoon.
TUMA: I accept on behalf of the kitchen.
GREB: Can the spoon help the envelope?
TUMA: That is outside my category.
PAVI: I will pull the corner.
[THE ENVELOPE PEELS FREE AND THE AWARD CARD FALLS OUT.]
LOLLI: It says we have one more category.
VENN: Best Use of Two Handles.
GREB: We should both win.
PAVI: We already share the trophy.
TUMA: Then the trophy is the winner.
LOLLI: An unprecedented result.
GREB: Should it give a speech?
PAVI: It has two handles. It can hold the microphone.
VENN: The microphone is still with Tuma.
TUMA: I am accepting for all objects on stage.

LOLLI: We have reached the final award.
VENN: Best Envelope Recovery.
PAVI: The envelope is under the podium.
TUMA: It has won by remaining present.
GREB: Should we retrieve it?
LOLLI: It is already a winner.
PAVI: The ceremony is over.
VENN: The closing speech begins now.
GREB: How long?
LOLLI: Until the trophy is returned.
TUMA: It belongs to both nominees.
GREB: Then we can carry it out together.

[THE TROPHY SITS BETWEEN PAVI AND GREB AS THE ENVELOPE FLUTTERS DOWN BESIDE THE SPOON.]
