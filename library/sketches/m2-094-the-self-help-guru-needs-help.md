# The Self-Help Guru Needs Help

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: motivational seminars and the gap between polished advice and actual needs*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: GURU ZEN, a macaque; ASSISTANT LIO, a lemur; ATTENDEE TAVI, a gibbon; and ATTENDEE NIM, a capuchin.

[A stage has a lectern and a banner reading: UNLOCK YOUR INNER CANOPY. Zen stands on a box.] 

ZEN: Welcome. Today we will learn to reach our highest branch.

TAVI: How do we know which branch is highest?

ZEN: The one you believe in.

NIM: What if the branch is too thin?

ZEN: Believe harder.

LIO: We should not encourage anyone to climb an unsafe branch.

ZEN: The metaphorical branch.

TAVI: What if the metaphor is confusing?

ZEN: Confusion is the first step toward clarity.

NIM: I am confused.

ZEN: You are making progress.

[ZEN POINTS TO A SLIDE. IT READS: STEP ONE: SET A GOAL.] 

ZEN: Write down one goal.

TAVI: I want a quiet place to rest.

NIM: I want to carry the water dish without spilling.

ZEN: Those are small goals. Think bigger.

LIO: They are specific goals.

ZEN: The seminar promised transformation.

LIO: We can transform the path to the water dish by moving the loose stones.

NIM: That would help.

TAVI: We could also add a leaf roof over the resting spot.

ZEN: You are turning my seminar into a work party.

LIO: The attendees are asking for practical changes.

TAVI: The leaf roof is too low at the left edge.

NIM: I can lift that side while you tie it.

[THEY RAISE THE FRAME. THE LEAF ROOF SHIFTS.] 

ZEN: This is a powerful visual metaphor.

LIO: It is shade.

ZEN: We could call it the canopy of commitment.

TAVI: The knot is slipping.

ZEN: Then we need to commit to a stronger knot.

NIM: We need to use a shorter strip of vine.

[THE VINE TIGHTENS. THE ROOF HOLDS.] 

TAVI: Now it stays in place.

ZEN: How did you know what to do?

NIM: I held one side while Tavi tied the other.

ZEN: That is collaboration.

LIO: It is also a clear instruction.

ZEN: I will add that to my seminar. First, name the task. Second, ask who can help. Third, check the support.

TAVI: That is much better than believing harder.

ZEN: I am learning to make the metaphor useful.

NIM: Can the banner come down now? It is shading the wrong place.

[ZEN TAKES THE BANNER DOWN. LIGHT FALLS ON THE NEW RESTING SPOT.] 

ZEN: Our inner canopy has become an actual canopy.

LIO: And the path is clear.

ZEN: The seminar is officially complete.

TAVI: We did the work before the worksheet.

[THE AUDIENCE STARTS MOVING STONES AND TYING LEAVES TO A SHADE FRAME.] 

ZEN: I should be at the lectern.

TAVI: The lectern is in the way.

ZEN: Then I will carry it.

[HE LIFTS THE LECTERN. THE BANNER FALLS AND COVERS THE SLOGAN.] 

NIM: Now we can see the path.

ZEN: The banner was motivating us.

LIO: It was blocking the entrance.

ZEN: I have learned something today.

TAVI: What?

ZEN: My highest branch is the one that has a stable support.

LIO: That is useful advice.

ZEN: Can I put it on the banner?

NIM: After we finish fixing the shade frame.
