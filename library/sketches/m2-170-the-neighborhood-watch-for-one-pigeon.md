# The Neighborhood Watch for One Pigeon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: paranoid neighborhood-watch meetings that build an intelligence network around an ordinary bird*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Dena, a baboon chair; Kito, a macaque note-taker; Luma, a lemur resident; and Pavi, a capuchin volunteer.

INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - NIGHT

Dena points to a sketch of a pigeon on a whiteboard. Kito has written "unusual bird activity" beneath it.

DENA: The pigeon landed on the fence at 4:10.

LUMA: Pigeons land on fences.

PAVI: This one was facing north.

Kito draws an arrow. Dena says they need a watch schedule. Luma volunteers for the morning. Pavi takes the afternoon. Dena assigns herself the night shift, even though the pigeon has not been seen after sunset.

KITO: We need a code word.

LUMA: For a pigeon?

DENA: If we say pigeon, it may hear us.

They agree to call it "the visitor." Kito records the code word in large letters. Pavi asks if the bird might be looking for food. Dena says that is exactly what an investigator would ask.

The group tests the watch plan. Luma looks out the window and reports a shadow. Everyone ducks. It is a leaf moving across the path. Pavi takes a photo. Kito labels it "possible wing." Dena asks him to preserve the original image.

A real pigeon lands on the sill. It pecks at a crumb. Dena whispers the code word. The pigeon continues eating.

PAVI: It has not reacted.

DENA: A trained visitor would not.

Luma opens the window and moves the crumb to the ground outside. The pigeon hops down and eats there. Dena asks if that was a breach of protocol. Luma says it was a crumb.

Kito wants to interview the pigeon. He places a chair outside and waits. The pigeon flies to the roof. Pavi watches through binoculars, which are actually two paper tubes.

PAVI: It is looking at the street.

DENA: Or monitoring the street.

The residents hear a rustle in the hall and form a line. It is the janitor carrying a broom. He asks why everyone is crouching. Dena tells him the visitor has changed location. The janitor says he moved the crumb so the floor would stay clean.

The pigeon returns to the window, lands on the sill and coos. Kito writes down the sound.

KITO: It said coo.

LUMA: That is a pigeon sound.

Dena erases the threat map and draws a small feeding area beside the fence. Pavi asks what to call the new plan. Dena writes "crumb placement schedule." The pigeon eats the crumb and flies away.

PAVI: The visitor has departed.

Before closing the meeting, Kito checks the window latch. It is open, and the pigeon has already left. Luma shuts it gently so no bird flies into the room. Dena adds a final note to the schedule: watch from outside. Pavi folds the paper binoculars and puts them in the supply box.

DENA: We will remain vigilant.

Luma packs the binoculars. The paper tubes collapse. Kito adds a final note: "Equipment also requires supervision."
