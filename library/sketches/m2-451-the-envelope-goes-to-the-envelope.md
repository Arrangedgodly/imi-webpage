# The Envelope Goes to the Envelope

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: awards ceremonies, suspenseful nominee pauses, and presenters who value the envelope more than the winner*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: PRESENTER, a macaque; ENVELOPE HANDLER, a gibbon; NOMINEE, an orangutan; STAGE MANAGER, a capuchin; TROPHY BEARER, a baboon.

[A small awards stage. The handler carries a gold envelope on a velvet cushion. The nominee sits on a side bench beside an empty mark where the prop chair will be placed.]

PRESENTER: The award for Best Supporting Seat goes to...

[The presenter pauses. The envelope handler takes a step toward the microphone.]

STAGE MANAGER: The presenter is reading.

HANDLER: I thought the pause was for me.

NOMINEE: I am still sitting down.

[The presenter opens the envelope. The result card slips to the floor. The envelope remains in their hand.]

PRESENTER: The envelope is empty.

[The handler picks up the card, reads it, and hands it to the presenter.]

HANDLER: Best Supporting Seat: the chair that held the ladder through the second act.

PRESENTER: The chair is the winner.

TROPHY BEARER: Shall I take the envelope?

PRESENTER: The gold one deserves its own close-up.

HANDLER: I have been carrying it all evening.

STAGE MANAGER: The award is for a prop, not the person carrying it.

[The handler puts the envelope on the podium. The stage manager brings out a trophy shaped like a small chair.]

NOMINEE: That was my prop.

PRESENTER: The envelope received the most dramatic close-up.

NOMINEE: The chair appeared in three scenes and held a ladder.

TROPHY BEARER: The chair was nominated for Best Supporting Seat.

[The presenter looks at the card. It reads "Best Supporting Seat: the chair that held the ladder."]

PRESENTER: The card was inside the envelope. The envelope is not the winner.

HANDLER: I would like to thank the envelope for holding the result.

STAGE MANAGER: Please do not thank an envelope.

[The handler begins to fold the envelope into a paper hat. The nominee reaches for it.]

NOMINEE: It is part of the ceremony set.

HANDLER: It is empty now.

NOMINEE: It can still be used again.

PRESENTER: We have a tie between the envelope and the chair.

STAGE MANAGER: There is no tie. The chair won.

[The trophy bearer wheels the real chair prop onto the stage, followed by the miniature chair-shaped trophy. The nominee receives the small trophy and stands beside the real chair.]

NOMINEE: This is the award?

TROPHY BEARER: It is a miniature representation.

NOMINEE: I thought it was a doorstop.

HANDLER: The envelope also thought it was a hat.

[The presenter hands the correctly read result card to the nominee.]

NOMINEE: Thank you. It held the ladder and the scene together.

[The trophy bearer places the miniature trophy on the real chair. It squeaks when the nominee sits, then holds steady. The handler unfolds the hat, smooths the envelope flat, and offers it to a waiting silent presenter and gives them a new card; this is a new category, not another winner announcement.]

PRESENTER: Next category: Best Quiet Entrance.

[The next presenter takes both the envelope and card in one handoff, then waits to read it until the following ceremony beat.]

FINAL IMAGE: The nominee stands beside the award-winning chair while the next presenter holds one envelope and its new category card.
