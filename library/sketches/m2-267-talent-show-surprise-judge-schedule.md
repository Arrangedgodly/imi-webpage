# The Talent Show's Surprise Judge Knows the Schedule

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised talent competitions that advertise spontaneous judging while rehearsing every reveal*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Host Nara, a macaque; judge Odo, a baboon; performer Kito, a capuchin; and stage manager Seli, a gibbon.

INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM - NIGHT

Nara stands under a spotlight. Seli holds cue cards. Odo sits at the judges' table.

NARA: Our surprise judge is Odo, who has judged every show this season.

ODO: I am surprised to be introduced as a surprise.

SELI: The cue card says, "Pretend you did not know."

ODO: I did not know the card would be this large.

[Kito enters with a small xylophone. The spotlight follows him late, illuminating an empty stool.]

NARA: Please welcome Kito and his original percussion piece.

KITO: I will play the sound of a rainy roof.

SELI: The rain cue is in three, two-

[A bucket of water pours onto the stage before Kito begins. Seli checks the card.]

SELI: We are early.

INT. AUDITORIUM - MOMENTS LATER

Kito starts playing. The rhythm is gentle. Nara mouths the countdown from the wings. Odo waits to press a gold buzzer.

KITO: The last note is the drip from the gutter.

[He taps one final note. The buzzer sounds before he lifts the mallet.]

ODO: I liked it.

NARA: The buzzer is supposed to mark the end.

ODO: I liked it before it ended.

SELI: The card says hold for dramatic pause.

[Kito waits. The audience waits. The buzzer's light slowly fades.]

INT. BACKSTAGE - LATER

Nara gives Kito a second chance to perform the ending. Seli resets the rain bucket. Odo holds the buzzer plug.

NARA: This time we will let the act finish.

KITO: I can play it again.

ODO: I can wait.

[The bucket drips into the xylophone. A single note rings.]

KITO: That is the ending.

ODO: I did not press the buzzer.

NARA: The machine pressed it.

SELI: The buzzer has its own cue.

INT. BACKSTAGE - BEFORE THE FINAL REVEAL

Seli cues the spotlight. It moves to the judges instead of Kito. Nara switches it back, but a mirror ball redirects it onto the empty stage.

NARA: The spotlight should find the performer.

SELI: It is following the cue card.

ODO: That card has been in charge all night.

[Kito plays a soft note. The spotlight swings to him, then the judges all look at the xylophone.]

KITO: I can perform under the light.

NARA: It may be the only part of the show that is surprised.

INT. AUDITORIUM - FINAL REVEAL

Nara returns to the stage. Seli hands her a sealed envelope. Odo's name is printed on the outside.

NARA: The winner is selected by a surprise vote.

ODO: Who voted?

SELI: The audience, the judges, and the cue cards.

[The envelope contains another cue card: EVERYONE WINS. Odo looks at his gold buzzer.]

ODO: I will award this to the xylophone.

[Kito accepts the buzzer. It sounds when he touches it.]

KITO: It likes the ending.

NARA: The buzzer has selected a winner.

SELI: That was the planned reveal.

ODO: Then let us pretend to be surprised.

[All four gasp. The xylophone rings one final note.]
