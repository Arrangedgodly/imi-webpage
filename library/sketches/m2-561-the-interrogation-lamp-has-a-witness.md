# The Interrogation Lamp Has a Witness

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: hard-boiled interrogations whose lighting threatens to expose the wrong suspect*
*By: IMI Executive Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-016*

Cast: DETECTIVE KORA, a macaque; SUSPECT PIM, a capuchin; ELECTRICIAN NEL, an orangutan; OFFICER RUD, a gibbon.

[A bare interview room. Pim sits across from Kora. A desk lamp between them points straight into Kora's eyes. Rud holds a notebook.]

KORA: Where were you at nine?

PIM: At the office. Where were you?

KORA: Asking questions.

PIM: With your eyes closed?

[Kora swivels the lamp toward Pim. Its stiff cable pulls it back toward Kora.]

KORA: That is a very long silence.

PIM: I answered. You were trying to see.

RUD: I wrote "office." Then I wrote "lamp." Should those be separate lines?

KORA: The lamp is not evidence.

[Nel knocks and enters carrying a folded work order. She puts it on the desk, away from the hot shade.]

NEL: You reported a light that wouldn't stay pointed.

KORA: We are in the middle of an interrogation.

NEL: Your report said urgent.

PIM: It has been quite urgent for her eyes.

ACT ONE

[Nel asks everyone to leave the lamp alone. She switches it off and unplugs it. The ordinary ceiling light remains on. Pim's chair casts no dramatic shadow.]

KORA: Now there is no atmosphere.

PIM: There is the question about nine.

RUD: I still have it on the first line.

[Nel follows the cable under the desk. It is wrapped around Kora's chair leg. She lifts the loose loop off the leg and gives the cord slack.]

NEL: The lamp returns to you because your chair pulls it.

KORA: A suspect might have arranged that.

RUD: You moved the chair closer before Pim came in.

KORA: Do you have to write everything?

RUD: You told me to write everything.

PIM: Could you write that I had a colleague at the office?

[Kora nods. Rud writes the colleague's name. Nel places the cable along the rear of the desk, clear of the chairs, then plugs in the lamp and switches it on. It stays pointed at the tabletop.]

NEL: Fixed. Please sign the work order.

KORA: I am not signing anything during an interrogation.

NEL: Then the repair stays listed as awaiting confirmation.

[Kora studies the form. Pim watches her, hands folded.]

PIM: Where were you when the lamp was unplugged?

KORA: Right here.

PIM: Is there a witness?

RUD: Three, counting me.

ACT TWO

[Kora signs the work order. Nel adds the time and picks it up. Rud calls the colleague on the office phone, with Kora's permission, and confirms Pim was helping close the office at nine. Kora checks that information against the time on her case note.]

KORA: The missing parcel was reported at eight forty. You are free to go while we check the delivery records.

PIM: May I take my umbrella?

RUD: It is by the door. It was not written as evidence.

[Pim collects it and leaves. Nel follows with the signed form. Kora sits in the quiet room and turns the lamp toward her notebook. It stays there.]

KORA: At last, something is cooperating.

RUD: Should I write that?

[Kora opens her mouth, considers the notebook, then reaches for her own pen.]

FINAL IMAGE: Rud writes "lamp repaired" on a separate line while Kora covers her first unfinished question with the signed case note.
