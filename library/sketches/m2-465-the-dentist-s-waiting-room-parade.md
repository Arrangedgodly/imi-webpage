# The Dentist's Waiting Room Parade

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: waiting-room announcements, overly cheerful appointment reminders, and office mascots who lead patients in a parade*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: RECEPTIONIST, a macaque; DENTIST, a gibbon; PATIENT, an orangutan; HYGIENIST, a capuchin; MASCOT, a baboon.

[A dental waiting room. A small tooth mascot stands beside a sign reading "PLEASE WAIT."]

RECEPTIONIST: The dentist will see the next patient in a moment.

MASCOT: I can lead a parade while we wait.

PATIENT: I came for a checkup, not a parade.

HYGIENIST: We have a short hallway and a long list of names.

[The mascot marches in place. The receptionist's bell rings each time the mascot's foot lands.] 

RECEPTIONIST: That bell is for the desk.

MASCOT: It is keeping time.

DENTIST: The next patient is ready.

[The mascot turns toward the hallway. The patient stands, but the hygienist holds up a hand.] 

HYGIENIST: We will call your name, not follow the mascot.

MASCOT: I have a sash.

PATIENT: I have an appointment.

[The receptionist looks at the list and misreads the name. The mascot marches toward the wrong patient. The patient stays seated.] 

RECEPTIONIST: I called the wrong name. Please remain where you are.

MASCOT: The parade has a detour.

DENTIST: The mascot can wait with me.

[The dentist checks the correct chart and calls the patient's name. The patient walks to the treatment-room door. The mascot tries to follow, but the hygienist points to a line on the floor.] 

HYGIENIST: The parade stops here. The treatment room is private.

MASCOT: I can wave from the waiting room.

PATIENT: That is enough.

[The mascot waves. The patient enters; the dentist closes the door. The hygienist hands the mascot a small toothbrush prop and asks it to demonstrate gentle circular brushing on a model.] 

MASCOT: This is a parade for the toothbrush.

RECEPTIONIST: The toothbrush is not a patient.

[The mascot rotates the brush on the model. The brush head falls off. The hygienist picks it up and replaces it.] 

HYGIENIST: We demonstrate with a complete brush.

MASCOT: I will stop marching.

[The patient returns from the room, smiling. The dentist says the checkup is done and gives them a reminder card. The mascot offers a sticker shaped like a tooth.] 

PATIENT: I will take the sticker. I will leave the parade here.

DENTIST: Fair.

[The receptionist resets the bell. The mascot stands beside the sign without moving its feet.] 

[The receptionist calls the next appointment from the list and waits for the patient to answer. The mascot stays beside the sign instead of marching.]

MASCOT: I can hold the place in line.

RECEPTIONIST: That is helpful.

The hygienist sets the toothbrush model back on its stand. The mascot gives the arriving patient a sticker but does not lead them down the hallway. The dentist opens the door and calls the next name directly. The room continues with ordinary appointments, not a parade.

FINAL IMAGE: The patient leaves with a reminder card while the tooth mascot holds its place beside "PLEASE WAIT."
