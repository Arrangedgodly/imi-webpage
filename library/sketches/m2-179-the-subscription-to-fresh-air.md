# The Subscription to Fresh Air

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wellness subscriptions that monetize outdoor access through tiers, bracelets and branded breathing*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Nima, a baboon sales lead; Tavi, a capuchin customer; Piri, a gibbon breathing guide; and Sola, a macaque park attendant.

EXT. GROVE ENTRY - DAY

Nima stands beside a gate and a sign reading FRESH AIR ACCESS. Tavi approaches. Piri gives him a colored bracelet.

PIRI: Basic lets you breathe near the entrance.

TAVI: What does the red bracelet do?

NIMA: It gives you a longer exhale.

Sola opens the gate. The air is the same on both sides. Tavi steps through and takes a breath.

TAVI: Is this included?

Piri checks his bracelet. It is blue.

PIRI: That breath was a preview.

Nima offers a monthly plan with four grove visits and a bonus inhale. Tavi asks if he can simply walk through the public gate. Sola points to a free path around the side. Nima says the path is for members who prefer unstructured access.

Tavi tries to follow it. Piri walks with him, narrating each step as a mindfulness exercise. They reach a tree. Piri asks him to notice the bark. Tavi notices a fig on a low branch and eats it.

NIMA: Fruit is a premium benefit.

SOLA: The fig fell from a public tree.

Nima hands Tavi a sticker for his water bottle. It says BREATHE PLUS. Tavi sticks it on the gate. The gate sticks slightly when it closes. Sola removes the sticker and uses it to mark the monthly sign-up clipboard.

Piri teaches a special exhale for members. Tavi exhales normally. Nima marks it as an unlicensed breath. Sola asks if he has a permit for the air. Nima checks the brochure and finds no permit category.

Tavi asks if any plan includes the walk home. Piri says the gold tier includes a guided return. Sola gestures to the path, which leads back to town. Tavi takes it without paying. Nima and Piri follow, trying to upsell the scenery.

NIMA: The sunset is available in premium.

TAVI: It is happening anyway.

Piri asks Tavi to inhale through the nose and exhale toward the brochure. The breeze flips the brochure shut. Nima calls it a demonstration of ambient value. Sola picks it up and places it in recycling.

The trio reaches the grove's other entrance. Sola closes the gate and removes the FRESH AIR ACCESS sign. Tavi takes another breath. Nima asks which tier he is on.

TAVI: Outside.

Piri looks at his bracelet and takes it off. The color has rubbed onto his wrist. Nima offers a wipe as an add-on. Sola opens the gate and tells them both exits are free. Tavi walks away breathing at no charge.

NIMA: We should create an unlimited plan.

SOLA: Everyone already has one.
