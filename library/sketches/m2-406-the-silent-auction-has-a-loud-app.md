# The Silent Auction Has a Loud App

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fundraising auctions where a bidding app announces every private choice to the room*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Auction host Mara, a macaque; donor Vex, an orangutan; bidder Pip, a capuchin; volunteer Nari, a lemur; APP, the auction phone voice.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

[A sign reads SILENT AUCTION. Four baskets sit on tables. Nari passes out phones.]

NARI: The app lets you bid without interrupting the speeches.

VEX: Excellent. I prefer privacy.

[He taps a phone. A loud bell rings.]

APP: Vex has bid twelve coins on a basket of pears.

VEX: It said silent.

MARA: It meant the auction. Not the app.

[Pip taps his phone.]

APP: Pip has outbid Vex by one coin.

VEX: I was buying those for the mayor.

PIP: I was buying them because I'm hungry.

[The app rings again.]

APP: Pip has been outbid by Nari.

NARI: I need the basket for the school bake sale.

[She holds up a phone with the screen dark.]

MARA: How did you bid?

NARI: I typed it in the pledge box.

[The app rings.]

APP: Nari has pledged to bake a pear cake.

VEX: I would bid on that.

MARA: We have no cake lot.

[Vex taps the screen. The app reads his fingerprint as a bid.]

APP: Vex has bid forty coins on one volunteer's apron.

NARI: I need that apron.

VEX: I thought it was a painting.

[Pip lifts the apron from a chair. It has a flour handprint across the front. The app's camera scans his face.]

APP: Pip has been recognized as a pear.

PIP: I have eaten one pear.

MARA: The app is not judging your snack.

[She turns off the microphone. The app keeps announcing. Vex tries to hide his phone under the table. The camera reads the tablecloth as a bidder and assigns it a number.]

APP: Bidder seven has offered the tablecloth.

NARI: That is the hall's tablecloth.

VEX: I can outbid it.

MARA: You cannot buy the furniture you're sitting under.

[Vex taps the app.]

APP: Vex has bid forty-one coins on bidder seven.

VEX: What is bidder seven?

MARA: The tablecloth.

VEX: I have outbid the table.

[The app rings.]

APP: Bidder seven has accepted Vex's offer and requests delivery.

[Vex stands. The tablecloth lifts with him, pulling the baskets together. Nari grabs the pear basket before it falls.]

NARI: The table has accepted your bid.

VEX: I don't want it anymore.

MARA: The app says the sale is final.

[Pip peels the app phone from Vex's hand. Nari pulls the battery cover free. The announcements stop. Everyone checks their screens in silence.]

NARI: I won the pears.

PIP: I won the apron.

VEX: I won a tablecloth.

MARA: You won the app's attention.

FINAL BEAT: The room applauds. A phone left on the podium announces, "Mara has bid on applause." She raises her hand to stop it, and the app declares her the auction winner.
