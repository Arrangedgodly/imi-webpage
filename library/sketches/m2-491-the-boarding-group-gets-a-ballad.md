# The Boarding Group Gets a Ballad

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport boarding announcements and prestige musical numbers interrupting routine travel*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: GATE AGENT, a macaque; SINGER, a gibbon; PASSENGER, a baboon; RUNNER, a capuchin; PILOT, an orangutan.

[A small airport gate. The boarding display says GROUP THREE. The agent has a fixed desk microphone and a separate handheld announcement mic. The singer stretches beside a carry-on bag.]

GATE AGENT: Group Three may board now. Keep your pass ready and your instrument case shut.

SINGER: The moment deserves a note.

GATE AGENT: It deserves a line that moves.

[The singer takes the handheld microphone and holds a grand, endless amplified note. The passenger steps up, but the note keeps going.]

PASSENGER: Is the microphone broken?

GATE AGENT: No. I am waiting for the vowel to end.

SINGER: I am sustaining the suspense.

PASSENGER: My seat is in row eight. I have reached the chorus emotionally and physically.

[The agent presses the scanner's manual test button. It chirps once, then the agent scans the passenger's pass.]

GATE AGENT: Your pass is valid. Your boarding group is still Three.

SINGER: Then let Three rise!

[The singer climbs onto a luggage scale. It dips. The pilot steps from the jet bridge and leans through the doorway.]

PILOT: Is this the flight where everyone sings before sitting down?

GATE AGENT: Only one person. The rest are trying to board.

ACT ONE

INT. GATE AREA - CONTINUOUS

[The agent mutes the microphone. The amplified note stops, though the singer is still humming. The crowd moves six steps.]

SINGER: The silence lacks an ending.

PASSENGER: It had a boarding pass.

[The agent unmutes the announcement channel and uses the desk microphone to announce a bag left beside the seats. The singer instantly resumes the same note. The announcement disappears beneath it.]

GATE AGENT: Unattended blue suitcase, please come to the desk.

SINGER: The suitcase has a solo.

RUNNER: It has wheels. Roll it over.

[The runner pushes the blue suitcase to the desk. A small travel pillow slips out and lands on the agent's shoe.]

PILOT: That bag belongs to me. I left it while checking the jet bridge.

GATE AGENT: Please keep it beside you. We are boarding.

PILOT: I know. I am trying to get to my cockpit before the singer takes over the cabin.

[The pilot offers the singer a paper cup of water. The singer drinks, inhales, and starts another note. The agent keeps the microphone muted.] 

GATE AGENT: That sound is not an announcement.

SINGER: It could be, with training.

ACT TWO

[The agent hands the singer a printed boarding pass and points to the scanner.]

GATE AGENT: Scan this, then take your seat. No more microphone.

[The singer scans the pass. The reader gives a green beep. The singer tries one soft note into the muted microphone. Nothing is amplified.]

SINGER: It prefers paper.

PASSENGER: Paper has always been good at being paper.

[The remaining passengers file aboard. The singer boards behind them and takes a cabin seat. The pilot returns to the cockpit. The agent calls the last passenger at the door.]

GATE AGENT: Final passenger, last call.

PASSENGER: I am right here.

[The passenger crosses the threshold. The agent closes the gate only after the singer and every passenger have boarded. The pilot stays aboard, and the microphone stays muted.]

FINAL IMAGE: The singer buckles into a cabin seat, holding the boarding pass flat and silent.
