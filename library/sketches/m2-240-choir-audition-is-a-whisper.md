# The Choir's Audition Is a Whisper

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: audition panels that ask singers to demonstrate confidence while interrupting them with technical demands*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Choir Director Neri, a gibbon; Singer Omi, a macaque; Pianist Tavi, a capuchin; and Stagehand Ralo, a baboon.

INT. COMMUNITY CHOIR ROOM - EVENING

Omi stands before a panel. Neri holds a clipboard. Tavi sits at a piano. Ralo adjusts a microphone.

NERI: Please sing one verse at a comfortable volume.

OMI: Should I use the microphone?

RALO: It is not plugged in yet.

TAVI: I can play the starting note.

[OMI SINGS A CLEAR, QUIET VERSE. THE PANEL LEANS IN TO HEAR.]

NERI: Good pitch. Can you project?

OMI: I was asked for a comfortable volume.

NERI: The room is large.

RALO: The microphone is still unplugged.

TAVI: I can play from farther away.

OMI: Then I cannot see the accompanist.

NERI: The panel needs to see your confidence.

OMI: I was looking at the piano.

[NERI MOVES THE PANEL'S CHAIRS. THE CHAIR LEGS SCRAPE SO LOUDLY THAT OMI STOPS SINGING.]

NERI: That was a distraction.

RALO: The floor is louder than the singer.

INT. CHOIR ROOM - LATER

Ralo plugs the microphone in. It squeals. Omi stops singing. Tavi checks the piano.

NERI: The singer must continue through a small technical distraction.

OMI: That was not small.

TAVI: I can play again.

[RALO TURNS THE MICROPHONE DOWN. THE SQUEAL STOPS.]

NERI: Please sing the same verse at half volume.

OMI: The first verse was already quiet.

NERI: We need to hear control.

[OMI SINGS SOFTLY. THE PANEL CANNOT HEAR THE WORDS. TAVI PLAYS LOUDER TO COMPENSATE.]

OMI: I cannot hear myself.

TAVI: I cannot hear the piano.

RALO: The microphone is pointed at the piano.

NERI: That explains the balance.

TAVI: We can move the piano two feet.

NERI: The piano is on wheels.

[THE PIANO ROLLS AWAY FROM TAVI. HE BRACES A WHEEL WITH HIS FOOT, STILL HOLDING DOWN THE STARTING KEY.]

OMI: The starting note is still sounding.

RALO: The audition has a continuous tone.

INT. CHOIR ROOM - FINAL TRY

Ralo points the microphone toward Omi. Tavi plays a soft note. Neri lowers the clipboard.

NERI: Sing the first line once more.

OMI: At what volume?

NERI: The volume you started with.

[OMI SINGS. TAVI JOINS ON PIANO. THE MICROPHONE PICKS UP BOTH CLEARLY.]

NERI: That is the sound we needed.

OMI: I sang it the same way the first time.

RALO: The microphone was facing the piano.

NERI: We will offer you a place in the alto section.

OMI: Thank you.

TAVI: We can start rehearsal now.

NERI: First, one more test. Please sing while the panel turns its chairs around.

[THE PANEL TURNS. RALO UNPLUGS THE MICROPHONE. OMI SINGS WITHOUT IT. EVERYONE HEARS HER.]

NERI: Omi, you are hired. Chairs, come back tomorrow for a quieter audition.

[THE PANEL TURNS BACK. THE CHAIRS SCRAPE. NERI CROSSES THEIR NAMES OFF THE CLIPBOARD WHILE TAVI PLAYS OMI INTO REHEARSAL.]
