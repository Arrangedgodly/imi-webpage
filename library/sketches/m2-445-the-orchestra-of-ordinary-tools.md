# The Orchestra of Ordinary Tools

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: prestige orchestras and arts programming that frame routine maintenance as a premiere performance*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: CONDUCTOR, a baboon; VIOLINIST, a gibbon; CUSTODIAN, a macaque; CRITIC, an orangutan; STAGEHAND, a capuchin.

[A concert hall. A broom, wrench, and dustpan sit beneath a velvet cloth. The orchestra tunes.]

CONDUCTOR: Tonight, we premiere "Maintenance in Four Movements."

CRITIC: Is it written for a full orchestra?

CUSTODIAN: It is written for the floor.

[The custodian lifts the cloth. The stagehand strikes the wrench with a padded mallet.]

[SFX: A CLEAR METAL TING.]

VIOLINIST: That was in tune.

CONDUCTOR: First movement: the loose bolt.

[The custodian tightens a bolt on a chair. The wrench squeaks. The conductor raises both hands.]

CONDUCTOR: Hold the fermata.

CUSTODIAN: I am holding the chair.

CRITIC: The audience is moved.

CUSTODIAN: The chair is stable now.

[The violinist plays a soft note. The broom falls over. Everyone turns.]

CONDUCTOR: Second movement: gravity.

STAGEHAND: I can put it back.

CONDUCTOR: Not yet. Let the sound settle.

[The custodian sweeps dust into a pan. The bristles make a steady shh-shh rhythm. The orchestra tries to follow. The violinist speeds up.]

CUSTODIAN: Please keep the tempo where the dust is.

VIOLINIST: The dust has no conductor.

CUSTODIAN: It moves when I do.

[The critic writes notes, then drops the pencil. It rolls under the stage. The custodian stops sweeping and reaches for it.]

CONDUCTOR: Do not interrupt the sweep.

CUSTODIAN: The critic has lost a pencil.

CRITIC: It is a small soloist.

[The stagehand uses the broom to retrieve the pencil. The pencil rolls farther. The custodian takes the broom and draws it back.]

STAGEHAND: The broom has a long reach.

CONDUCTOR: Third movement: retrieval.

[The orchestra begins a dramatic chord. The pencil stops at the custodian's foot. She picks it up and hands it to the critic.]

CUSTODIAN: There. No encore.

CRITIC: I was prepared to applaud the pencil.

[The conductor asks the orchestra to follow the custodian's broom rhythm. The violinist enters early; the custodian stops sweeping, and the music stops too.]

CONDUCTOR: The broom has taken the tempo.

CUSTODIAN: I stopped because you were in my way.

CRITIC: A startling silence.

The stagehand offers a music stand. The custodian props the broom against it, then points to a strip of dust beneath the stand. The conductor tries to sweep it with his baton; the dust stays put. The custodian clears it in one pass. The orchestra waits for her nod.

VIOLINIST: We should tune to the floor.

CUSTODIAN: The floor is clean now.

[The conductor cues the final movement. The custodian checks the stage for dust, returns the wrench to its case, and the violinist plays a clean note.]

CONDUCTOR: The hall is ready.

CUSTODIAN: It was ready before the premiere.

CRITIC: Then what did we hear?

CUSTODIAN: Work getting done.

[The audience applauds. The custodian bows, then picks up the broom.]




FINAL IMAGE: The orchestra holds its last chord while the custodian quietly sweeps the final patch of dust.
