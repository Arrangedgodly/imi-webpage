# The Profile Picture Is on a Branch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: dating apps, profile filters, and matching algorithms that turn one shared hobby into a perfect-life claim*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: User Mira, a capuchin; match Vex, a macaque; friend Nari, a gibbon; and app voice Omi, an orangutan.

INT. COMMUNITY CLIMBING GYM - DAY

Mira sits beside a phone, editing a profile. Nari looks over her shoulder.

MIRA: I like climbing trees and reading maps.

NARI: Say what you mean.

MIRA: I like climbing trees and reading maps.

Omi's voice reads an auto-generated bio: "Adventurous explorer who scales every summit and never gets lost."

MIRA: I got lost yesterday.

NARI: Put that in.

Mira posts a photo. The filter adds a mountain, a wind-blown scarf, and a dramatic sunset. Her tail is cropped out.

MIRA: I do not own a scarf.

OMI: Your profile has been improved.

A match arrives: Vex. His profile says he likes maps and trees. They meet at the climbing gym. Vex carries a paper map and wears a scarf.

VEX: Your photo made you look taller.

MIRA: The filter added a mountain behind me.

VEX: The mountain looked like part of you.

Nari asks if they would like to start over with unfiltered profiles. Vex shows his picture. A filter gave him a backpack and a summit flag. He actually likes mapping neighborhood paths and planting saplings.

MIRA: I climb trees to prune branches.

VEX: I draw maps of the paths around them.

They walk to a young tree near the gym. Mira shows how she balances on a low branch; Vex uses his map to find where roots are lifting the sidewalk. Nari takes an unfiltered photo. Mira's tail appears across the lens, making it look like a separate branch.

Vex marks the uneven paving on his map. Mira finds a safer route around the tree for the climbing class. Nari takes another photo, this time with all three of them in frame and no mountain filter. The background shows the gym's plain brick wall.

MIRA: That is what the place looks like.

VEX: And the path really does go around the tree.

OMI: Your match has been boosted.

MIRA: We are right here.

The app asks each person to rate the meeting. Mira chooses "good conversation." Vex chooses "similar interests." The algorithm offers a compatibility score of 99 percent.

NARI: What is the last percent?

OMI: The filter is still active.

Mira disables it. Their compatibility score drops to 72 percent. Vex is relieved.

VEX: That seems more accurate.

MIRA: We can still meet again.

VEX: I would like that.

Omi suggests a premium subscription for more accurate tree matches. Mira closes the app and hands Vex the paper map. They walk together toward the garden path.

NARI: Did you match?

MIRA: We found the same sidewalk.

The app sends a final notification: "Your match is nearby." Mira looks around. Vex is beside her. Omi's voice says, "Would you like to say hello?" Nari shuts off the phone.
