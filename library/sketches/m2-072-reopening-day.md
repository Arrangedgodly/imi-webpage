# Reopening Day

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: retail grand-opening broadcasts and staged customer excitement*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: MANAGER VEX, a macaque; CASHIER LUZ, a capuchin; CUSTOMER PEL, a langur; and ANNOUNCER, the store's prerecorded voice.

[A seed shop entrance. A ribbon stretches across the door. A line of customers waits.]

ANNOUNCER: Welcome to the grand reopening. The doors are open, the deals are fresh, and the excitement is real.

VEX: The doors are not open yet.

LUZ: The ribbon is tied to both handles.

VEX: That's part of the reveal.

PEL: I have been standing here since the doors were open.

ANNOUNCER: Our first customer is about to enter.

VEX: Pel, walk through the ribbon when I count down.

PEL: Do I get the discount?

VEX: The discount starts after the doors open.

PEL: Then why am I entering?

[The announcer plays applause. The ribbon is still intact.]

VEX: Three, two, one!

[The ribbon drops. The doors remain shut.]

LUZ: The key is on the other side.

VEX: Can we call this a soft opening?

PEL: It's a closed opening.

[The manager climbs through a low side window and unlocks the door. The prerecorded voice keeps applauding.]

ANNOUNCER: A historic moment.

LUZ: We opened this shop last year.

VEX: It has been repainted.

PEL: The seed bins are empty.

VEX: The stock truck is late.

ANNOUNCER: Customers are enjoying the selection.

[Pel holds up an empty basket.]

PEL: I am selecting the basket.

LUZ: We have one bag of sunflower seeds in the back.

VEX: Place it beneath the spotlight.

[They put the bag on a pedestal. The announcer plays triumphant music.]

ANNOUNCER: A premium product display.

PEL: Can I buy it?

VEX: After the ribbon cutting.

PEL: We already cut the ribbon.

VEX: The ceremonial ribbon.

[Pel snips a loose thread from the bag. The bag opens, seeds spill across the floor, and the entire line begins sweeping.]

LUZ: We have a reopening special: one seed for every customer who helps clean.

PEL: That's a real deal.

[The announcer starts applause again. Vex unplugs it.]

VEX: We'll announce the next deal in person.

PEL: Is the store open?

LUZ: It is now.

[The line enters, carrying brooms.]


PEL: I need a bag of seeds.

LUZ: We have one.

VEX: It is reserved for the reopening display.

PEL: The opening is over.

ANNOUNCER: Customers are invited to admire the remaining stock.

PEL: I want to buy it.

VEX: The display is not for sale.

LUZ: It's the only bag.

[The manager looks at the empty shelves.]

VEX: We can sell it after the ribbon-cutting photo.

PEL: The ribbon is on the floor.

[Pel picks up the bag, places it on the counter, and pays.]

LUZ: First sale of reopening day.

ANNOUNCER: A historic transaction.

VEX: Please say "historic" only once.

[The cash register opens. No receipt comes out.]

PEL: Do I need to return the bag?

LUZ: No. Keep it.

[The prerecorded applause plays. Pel walks out with the seeds.]

[BLACKOUT.]
