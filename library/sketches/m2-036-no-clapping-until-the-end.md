# No Clapping Until the End

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Experimental theater, post-show talkbacks, and strict audience etiquette*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Director Sorn, an orangutan; performer Yela, a lemur; usher Bim, a capuchin; critic Oru, a mandrill; audience member Tavi, a gibbon.

[STAGE: A BLACK BOX THEATER. ONE BRANCH, ONE STOOL, AND A CARD THAT READS NO CLAPPING UNTIL THE END.]

BIM: Please silence your phones and your instincts.
TAVI: My hands are quiet.
BIM: Keep them that way.
SORN: Tonight's play explores the meaning of waiting.
ORU: How long does it run?
SORN: That depends on your relationship with time.
YELA: I will now stand beside the stool.
[YELA STANDS BESIDE THE STOOL. A LONG PAUSE.]
TAVI: Is something happening?
SORN: The audience is part of the stillness.
ORU: The audience is also wondering about the stool.
YELA: I will move the stool one handspan.
[YELA MOVES THE STOOL. ANOTHER LONG PAUSE.]
TAVI: That was a large handspan.
SORN: The movement has consequences.
BIM: Please hold your questions until the talkback.
ORU: I am writing that down.
YELA: I will sit on the stool.
[THE STOOL CREAKS.]
TAVI: The stool made a sound.
SORN: The stool has spoken.
ORU: Does it have a speaking role?
SORN: It has a listening role.
YELA: I will rise from the stool.
TAVI: I think it is ending.
BIM: Do not clap.
TAVI: I was only moving my hands apart.
BIM: That is how clapping begins.
ORU: I have a question about the branch.
SORN: Save it.
ORU: The branch is shedding bark.
SORN: It is letting go of its past.
YELA: I will pick up the bark.
BIM: That may be applause.
YELA: It is bark.
SORN: Both can be true.
TAVI: How will we know when the play ends?
SORN: When the performer knows.
YELA: I know.
[SHE REMAINS BESIDE THE STOOL.]
TAVI: The performer seems to be waiting.
SORN: That is the final scene.
ORU: Are we at the talkback?
SORN: We are at the feeling before the talkback.
BIM: Please do not leave.
TAVI: I was going to stretch.
BIM: Stretch silently.
YELA: I will now turn toward the audience.
[YELA TURNS. THE STOOL TIPS OVER.]
ORU: The stool has made its final statement.
SORN: We are finished.
TAVI: Now can we clap?
BIM: Wait for the lights.
[THE LIGHTS DIM.]
TAVI: Now?
BIM: Wait for the program note.
ORU: The program note says "Thank you."
SORN: That is the end.
[THE AUDIENCE APPLAUDS.]
YELA: I will take the stool with me.
TAVI: It deserves a curtain call.
BIM: The stool has already fallen.

ORU: I have one final note.
SORN: Is it about the play?
ORU: It is about the stool.
TAVI: The stool is already outside.
BIM: It is waiting for the talkback.
YELA: I will retrieve it.
SORN: That is a sequel.
ORU: I would attend.
BIM: The program says the talkback is optional.
TAVI: Is leaving optional too?
BIM: Only after the lights.

[THE STOOL IS CARRIED OFF TO A SMALL, CAREFUL OVATION.]
