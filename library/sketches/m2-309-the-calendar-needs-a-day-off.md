# The Calendar Needs a Day Off

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: scheduling apps, shared calendars, color-coded productivity systems, and reminders that duplicate the task they are meant to prevent*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Scheduler Tali, a ring-tailed lemur; organizer Kori, a macaque; assistant Odo, a capuchin; and calendar voice Rell, a gibbon.

INT. SHARED OFFICE - MORNING

Kori opens a wall calendar. Every day is covered in colored notes.

KORI: We need to schedule one meeting about cleaning the break room.

TALI: I have made a calendar invite.

RELL: Reminder: break-room cleaning meeting in five minutes.

ODO: The room is already clean.

KORI: Then we can cancel the meeting.

RELL: Reminder: cancel break-room cleaning meeting in five minutes.

Tali taps the calendar. The invite appears on every day for the next month.

TALI: I selected recurring.

KORI: We need one cleanup.

TALI: I will remove the recurrence.

Rell announces three alerts: one for the meeting, one for the cancellation, and one for the meeting about canceling. Odo opens the break-room door. It is clean except for a cup on the counter.

ODO: I can put away the cup.

KORI: We should document the task.

TALI: Should it be a task or an event?

KORI: A task.

RELL: Reminder: task to decide whether the cup is a task or an event.

Tali creates a color code. Red means urgent, blue means scheduled, green means completed. The cup is marked red, the meeting blue, and the clean room green. Rell reminds them that the calendar is color-blind.

TALI: The colors are for us.

RELL: Reminder: you are using colors.

Odo puts the cup in the cupboard. The app sends a notification that the task is complete. Tali checks it off. A duplicate task remains on next week's calendar.

KORI: Why is the cup still scheduled?

TALI: It is a recurring cup.

ODO: Cups recur.

KORI: We do not need to schedule the same cup.

Rell offers to create a reminder to stop scheduling cups. Tali refuses. She deletes the repeated meeting. A pop-up asks if she wants to delete the series, this event, or every instance of the event.

TALI: Which one is the single meeting?

KORI: The one we have already canceled.

They delete every instance. The reminder disappears. The calendar is clear for one afternoon.

RELL: Reminder: you have free time.

The team stares at the screen.

ODO: Can free time be recurring?

KORI: No.

Tali closes the app. Rell's voice continues from the wall speaker.

RELL: Reminder: you have closed the app.

Odo unplugs the speaker. The room is quiet. Kori writes "cup away" on a sticky note and puts it directly on the cupboard. Tali sets a single calendar alert for next month's office meeting.

RELL: Reminder: there is no reminder today.

TALI: Thank you.

The calendar displays a blank square. They sit beside it without filling it in.
