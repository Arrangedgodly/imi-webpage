# Birdhouse Build-Off

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: reality construction competitions and judging for style over function*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: HOST RELL, a macaque; BUILDER PIP, a capuchin; JUDGE NARA, a gibbon; and INSPECTOR VEE, a lemur.

[A WORKBENCH. Two birdhouses sit side by side, one decorated with sequins, the other with plain slats.] 

RELL: Welcome to the Birdhouse Build-Off. Each builder has one hour to make a home that impresses our judges.

NARA: I will judge on charm, silhouette, and entrance appeal.

VEE: I will inspect whether a bird can actually use it.

PIP: My birdhouse has a wide roof and a small entry hole.

NARA: It is plain.

PIP: It keeps rain off the nest.

NARA: Can you add a decorative arch?

PIP: The bird needs space to land.

RELL: Our other contestant has built a glittering tower.

[THE TOWER WOBBLES IN THE BREEZE.] 

NARA: It has drama.

VEE: It has no floor.

RELL: The builder says birds can imagine the floor.

VEE: Birds need somewhere to stand.

NARA: Pip, your entrance hole is small.

PIP: It is wide enough for the bird that uses this tree.

NARA: The judging card says grand entrance.

VEE: The bird is not attending a ceremony.

[A BIRD APPROACHES THE WORKBENCH AND LANDS ON PIP'S ROOF.] 

RELL: We have an unscheduled guest.

NARA: Is the bird part of the competition?

VEE: It is testing the roof.

PIP: The roof is fixed with twine and supported on both sides.

[THE BIRD PECKS AT THE GLITTER TOWER. A SEQUIN FALLS.] 

VEE: That material could be swallowed.

NARA: The sparkle is part of the design.

PIP: It is also loose.

RELL: We will pause the competition for a safety inspection.

VEE: The plain house passes. The tower needs a floor and safer materials.

NARA: Can the plain house have one decorative leaf?

PIP: It can have a leaf that does not block the entrance.

[HE TIES ONE LEAF BESIDE THE DOOR. THE BIRD ENTERS.] 

RELL: The judge's decision is in.

NARA: The plain house has no name.

PIP: The birdhouse.

RELL: The tower has a name: The Jewel of the Grove.

VEE: It also has a loose roof panel.

NARA: We could add a leaf trim to Pip's house.

PIP: The bird may pull at it.

NARA: Then perhaps a small painted line?

PIP: Paint can smell strong while it dries.

VEE: The house is ready without decoration.

[A BIRD CARRIES A DRY GRASS STEM TO THE ENTRY.] 

RELL: The guest has brought a building material.

PIP: It can add the lining it needs.

NARA: The bird is decorating the inside.

VEE: It is nesting.

NARA: I will award the house a point for charm.

VEE: You said charm was your category.

NARA: The bird's work is more charming than sequins.

RELL: Does the builder get a point?

NARA: Pip gets the ribbon, the bird gets the house, and the tower gets a repair list.

PIP: That is a fair distribution.

VEE: The judges agree for different reasons.

RELL: The competition has found a winner and a contractor.

NARA: The bird has chosen the house.

VEE: That is not a score.

NARA: It is a review.
