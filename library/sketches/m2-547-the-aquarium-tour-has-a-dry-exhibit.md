# The Aquarium Tour Has a Dry Exhibit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: immersive aquarium tours and presenters whose centerpiece exhibit contains no water*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: GUIDE, a gibbon; KEEPER, an orangutan; VISITOR, a baboon; CHILD, a capuchin; TECH, a macaque.

[Aquarium corridor. A large tank holds rocks, a toy ship, and no water. The guide stands beside it with a pointer.]

GUIDE: This is our deep-sea wreck exhibit.

VISITOR: It is dry.

KEEPER: The tank is being serviced.

[The child presses a button. A recorded whale song plays. The empty tank echoes loudly.]

CHILD: The whale sounds thirsty.

ACT ONE

[The guide begins a speech about ocean pressure. The tech rolls in a blue light projector. The rocks glow under simulated waves.]

GUIDE: Imagine a world beneath the sea.

VISITOR: I do not have to imagine the sand.

[The keeper checks the maintenance board. The tank is scheduled to be refilled after the seal dries. The guide says the exhibit is open, but the sign says CLOSED FOR SERVICE. The tech turns the sign around; the other side reads PLEASE ENJOY THE OCEAN.]

KEEPER: The sign has two opinions.

[The child asks whether the toy ship can sail. The guide says it is a model. The child uses a hand fan to make the projected waves move faster. The toy ship rolls across the dry floor of the tank and hits a rock.]

TECH: The current is too strong.

[The visitor points to a puddle under the tank. The keeper checks the valve and finds it is not a leak; a volunteer spilled a water bottle while cleaning. The keeper wipes it, then tapes down the power cable for the projector.]

KEEPER: We have a dry floor now.

GUIDE: And a simulated tide.

[The child moves the ship back to its starting place. The guide asks the group to watch a feeding demonstration. The keeper opens a small hatch and drops food into the neighboring, filled tank. A fish swims by. Everyone turns away from the empty shipwreck.]

VISITOR: The live exhibit is next door.

GUIDE: This one is for the imagination.

[The tech turns off the projector. The empty tank is just a box of rocks again.]

[A second tour group arrives at the tank, expecting the promised wreck. The guide points to the service notice before anyone enters. The keeper demonstrates the empty tank's seal by placing a dry paper towel under the valve. It remains dry. The child asks whether that means the tank has passed its test.]

KEEPER: It means we can safely fill it later.

VISITOR: That is a real result.

ACT TWO

[The keeper hangs a clear maintenance notice on the tank and moves the group to the live exhibit. The guide gives a short explanation of the fish. The child asks if the shipwreck will be wet later.]

KEEPER: After the seal cures.

CHILD: Can the ship wait in the dry dock?

[The guide looks at the model ship, now parked beside the sign.]

FINAL IMAGE: The empty tank stays dark while the toy ship sits on a towel labeled DRY DOCK.
