# The Aquarium's Fish Count

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public attractions that turn a simple animal count into an interactive app challenge*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Guide Lero, a macaque; App Designer Tavi, a capuchin; Visitor Mina, a gibbon; and Keeper Odo, a baboon.

INT. AQUARIUM VIEWING ROOM - DAY

A school of silver fish moves through a tank. Lero hands Mina a tablet. Tavi watches an analytics screen. Odo carries a bucket.

LERO: There are about twenty fish in this tank.

TAVI: The app asks visitors to count exactly.

MINA: They keep moving.

TAVI: That is the challenge.

LERO: The tank has a curved glass panel.

TAVI: We can add a bonus for spotting the curved view.

MINA: I see two fish that might be one reflection.

ODO: There is one fish and one reflection.

[TAVI ADDS A QUESTION MARK TO THE COUNT. THE COUNTER ADDS TWO FISH.]

TAVI: The app rewards curiosity.

LERO: It rewards tapping.

ODO: We do not have an exact count. The school changes during the day.

TAVI: We can update the number each hour.

LERO: The fish do not hold still for hourly updates.

INT. AQUARIUM VIEWING ROOM - LATER

Mina taps the tablet. A counter jumps from 18 to 23. Tavi points to a fish-shaped button.

TAVI: Tap when you see a fish.

MINA: I see all of them.

TAVI: Tap each one as it crosses the center.

[MINA TAPS RAPIDLY. THE APP COUNTS 46. THE FISH KEEP SWIMMING.]

LERO: It counted some fish twice.

TAVI: We can add a streak bonus.

ODO: The fish do not care about a streak.

MINA: The app says I have found a rare fish.

LERO: That is a reflection of your sleeve.

[TAVI LOOKS AT THE TANK. A BRIGHT RED SLEEVE APPEARS IN THE GLASS.]

TAVI: The app can classify reflections as discoveries.

ODO: That is not a fish.

MINA: The counter is at 46 now.

LERO: There are about twenty fish.

TAVI: The remaining twenty-six could be reflections.

ODO: Or repeated taps.

[TAVI TAPS THE RESET BUTTON. THE COUNTER RETURNS TO ZERO, THEN IMMEDIATELY COUNTS THE RED EXIT SIGN AS A FISH.]

TAVI: It can recognize red.

MINA: The fish are silver.

INT. AQUARIUM EXIT - AFTERNOON

A sign invites visitors to submit their counts. Mina hands back the tablet. Tavi studies the data.

TAVI: Visitors reported between 12 and 87 fish.

LERO: The school has about twenty.

MINA: I counted the same fish every time it crossed the center.

ODO: That makes sense. They loop around.

TAVI: We could turn the counter into a swimming pattern game.

LERO: Or let people watch the fish.

[TAVI TURNS OFF THE COUNTER. THE SCHOOL SWIMS PAST THE VIEWING WINDOW. MINA WATCHES WITHOUT TAPPING.]

MINA: There are twenty.

ODO: About twenty.

[THE FISH TURN TOGETHER. THE APP SCREEN, STILL ON THE TABLET, COUNTS THEM AS ONE LARGE FISH.]
