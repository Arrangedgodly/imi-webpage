# The Fire Drill Has a Tour Guide

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum audio tours that narrate emergencies as if they were curated exhibits*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Luma, a macaque museum guide; Bex, a capuchin fire marshal; Orin, a gibbon visitor; and Pip, a lemur audio technician.

INT. NATURAL HISTORY MUSEUM - DAY

A small alarm chirps. Luma holds out numbered headsets. Orin is looking at a fossil. Bex enters carrying a red alarm panel.

BEX: That is the drill signal. We leave by the west doors.

LUMA: Please collect headset number four. This is our new evacuation tour.

BEX: It is not a tour. It is an evacuation.

PIP: The headsets already have the route loaded.

ORIN: I came to see the ancient turtle.

LUMA: You can see it on the way to the exit. It has been here a long time.

BEX: Everyone, west doors. Walk calmly.

LUMA: Stop at the orange floor marker. Exhibit one, the painted footprint of a fire extinguisher.

BEX: We do not stop at exhibits.

[THE GROUP STOPS. PIP'S AUDIO TRACK PLAYS A DISTANT ROAR.]

PIP: Sorry. I have the dinosaur sound channel open.

ORIN: Is the dinosaur part of the drill?

BEX: It is part of the recording.

LUMA: Please keep moving. Our next feature is the west hallway, where the ceiling is a little low.

INT. WEST HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

Bex reaches a door with a bright EXIT sign. Luma steps in front of it.

LUMA: Before we exit, a brief note on the door's brass hinge.

BEX: No note. Open it.

LUMA: The hinge was installed during the 1982 renovation.

BEX: The fire marshal is now installing it into the open position.

[BEX PUSHES THE DOOR. IT SWINGS WIDE. PIP'S TOUR RECORDING SAYS "PLEASE REMAIN IN THE GALLERY."]

PIP: That is the old track.

ORIN: The headset just told me to stay inside.

BEX: Take it off. We are leaving.

LUMA: We have reached the outdoor courtyard. Please look back at the elegant exit we used.

EXT. MUSEUM COURTYARD - DAY

Everyone steps outside. Bex counts heads. Luma counts headsets. Pip holds a coil of cables.

BEX: All four accounted for.

LUMA: Three visitors and one marshal.

BEX: Four people.

PIP: Five headsets. One is on the fossil turtle.

[THEY LOOK THROUGH THE WINDOW. A HEADSET RESTS ON THE FOSSIL'S SHELL.]

ORIN: It found the quietest listener.

BEX: The drill is over. We can go back in when the all-clear sounds.

PIP: Should the audio track start over at the beginning?

BEX: No. That would put the visitors back inside.

LUMA: The recording can wait outside with us.

PIP: I can delete the tour track after the all-clear.

[THE ALL-CLEAR BEEPS. BEX OPENS THE DOOR. LUMA GOES INSIDE AND REMOVES THE HEADSET FROM THE TURTLE.]

LUMA: The drill is over. Our final exhibit is the west door, which is now simply a door.

BEX: And the tour is canceled.

ORIN: I saw the turtle on the way out.

LUMA: Then today's tour has one stop and no return route.
