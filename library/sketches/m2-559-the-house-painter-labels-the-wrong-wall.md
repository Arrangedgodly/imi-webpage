# The House Painter Labels the Wrong Wall

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: home renovation shows, confident color reveals, and painters who label the wall after painting it*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: PAINTER, a baboon; HOMEOWNER, an orangutan; DESIGNER, a gibbon; CAMERA OPERATOR, a macaque; HELPER, a capuchin.

[A living room set for a renovation show. Three walls are covered in sample squares. The painter holds a roller. The designer holds paint cards.]

DESIGNER: The blue goes on the west wall.

PAINTER: Which wall is west?

HOMEOWNER: The one with the window.

[The camera operator pans to the opposite wall. The helper places a label marked WEST beside the fireplace.]

ACT ONE

[The painter rolls blue paint onto the fireplace wall. The designer notices but assumes the camera angle is reversed. The homeowner asks for a reveal. The camera operator counts down.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: Three, two, one.

[The painter pulls away a paper covering. The wall is gray.]

HOMEOWNER: That is not blue.

PAINTER: That is the primer.

[The helper lifts another covering. The wall underneath is blue, but it is behind the curtain. The designer checks the plan and sees the labels were swapped.]

DESIGNER: The west label is on the east wall.

HOMEOWNER: The sun sets through my window.

[The painter stops rolling. The camera operator asks for a reaction shot. The homeowner looks at the wall, then at the paint can. The designer asks the helper to move the labels, but the adhesive sticks to the fresh paint. The label tears, leaving half the word on each surface.]

HELPER: We have two partial directions.

[The painter brings a compass. It points north but wobbles near the metal fireplace. The homeowner uses the window and the actual afternoon light to identify west. The painter starts touching up the correct wall. The camera operator moves the light, and the blue looks purple.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: It changed on camera.

DESIGNER: It is the same paint.

[They turn off the colored production light. The blue appears blue again. The helper writes the direction on a removable card and holds it beside the window.]

ACT TWO

[The painter finishes the west wall. The homeowner approves the color. The camera operator records a final reveal with the daylight unchanged. The designer marks the paint can and the removable card; nobody sticks labels to the wall.]

HOMEOWNER: We know which wall now.

PAINTER: The paint knew all along.

[The camera operator replays the reveal on the monitor. The wrong wall appears purple only because of the light, then blue once daylight returns. The homeowner asks whether the show can keep the original take. The designer says it can, as long as the label is removed from the painting and the room number is spoken aloud.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: We can use the wide shot.

PAINTER: The wide shot knows west.

FINAL IMAGE: The removable WEST card hangs from the window latch while the painter carries the roller away.
