# Last Call for Talent

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Television talent competitions and dramatic audition judging*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Dori, a mandrill; judge Riff, a peacock; judge Sula, a squirrel monkey; contestant Nib, a macaque; stage manager Oren, a gibbon.

[STAGE: A SPOTLIGHT, A RED BUTTON, THREE JUDGES AT A DESK. A SIGN READS "ONE MINUTE TO IMPRESS."] 

DORI: Welcome to Last Call for Talent. Contestants, you have sixty seconds to show us something unforgettable.

NIB: I can balance a pebble on my head.

RIFF: Can you do it with three?

NIB: I have one pebble.

SULA: Let's see the pebble.

OREN: We have fifty seconds.

NIB: The pebble is backstage.

DORI: Is it a very large pebble?

NIB: It is a very forgettable pebble.

RIFF: That could be an artistic choice.

SULA: Or a missing prop.

[OREN RUNS OFF. A DRUMROLL STARTS.] 

DORI: While we wait, tell us why you auditioned.

NIB: I heard there was a talent competition.

RIFF: And you brought no talent?

NIB: I brought the pebble.

SULA: We are making progress.

OREN: I found the pebble in the judge's water glass.

RIFF: I thought the glass was unusually heavy.

NIB: That is the trick. I can move a pebble without touching it.

DORI: Can you demonstrate?

NIB: I can ask someone else to move it.

SULA: The pebble just moved.

OREN: I removed it from the glass.

RIFF: The audience is impressed.

SULA: The audience is waiting for the act.

[A TIMER BEEPS. THE SPOTLIGHT SWINGS TO THE JUDGES.] 

DORI: Nib, your time begins now.

NIB: I will perform a three-part balance.

RIFF: We only have one pebble.

NIB: I will balance it on my head, then on one finger, then on the floor.

SULA: The floor is not a body part.

NIB: It is part of the stage.

OREN: That is technically accurate.

NIB: First, the head.

[PEBBLE DROPS ON NIB'S HEAD, THEN ROLLS INTO HIS EAR.] 

NIB: I can hear the pebble thinking.

RIFF: What is it thinking?

NIB: "This is not a safe place."

SULA: I agree with the pebble.

OREN: It has come out the other ear.

NIB: That's the second balance.

DORI: You have twelve seconds.

NIB: For the finale, I will balance it on the floor.

RIFF: It is already on the floor.

NIB: The floor has been holding it all along.

SULA: That is annoyingly true.

RIFF: I press yes.

SULA: I press yes for the floor.

DORI: The audience votes for a group act. We have Nib, a pebble, and one floor.

OREN: The next contestant is a drummer.

NIB: Can I keep the pebble?

RIFF: It belongs to the set.

SULA: Then the set has talent.

DORI: The floor and pebble advance to the finals. Nib may return as their agent.

NIB: I will negotiate a short contract.

[THE RED BUTTON LIGHTS UP BY ITSELF. THE JUDGES LOOK UNDER THE DESK.]
