# The Escape Room Hint System Escapes

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: escape-room puzzles that hide clues behind a staff member's overly theatrical instructions*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Game master Mara, a gibbon; players Vex, a macaque, and Pip, a capuchin; floor attendant Nari, a lemur.

INT. ESCAPE ROOM - NIGHT

[A door has a four-digit lock. A speaker crackles. Mara watches from behind a one-way window.]

MARA: Your first hint is under the table.

[Vex and Pip crawl under the table. A card reads "LOOK UP." They look up. A paper key hangs from the ceiling.]

PIP: We have found the second hint.

MARA: That was the first hint.

VEX: What is the second?

MARA: Your time is running out.

[Vex shakes the table. The paper key swings, striking a bell. Nari enters through the staff door carrying a mop.]

NARI: Why are you on the floor?

PIP: We are escaping.

NARI: This room is booked until nine.

[Mara speaks through the speaker.]

MARA: Please do not let the hint system leave the room.

[Nari looks at the speaker. A long wire trails under the door and out into the hall.]

NARI: The hint system is in the hallway.

[Vex tugs the wire. The speaker rattles. A second card slides through the doorway: "THE KEY IS A METAPHOR." Pip grabs it.]

PIP: The key is paper.

VEX: The door key is metaphorical.

[They inspect the lock. It needs four digits. Nari wipes the floor. The mop bumps a loose tile; a number is painted underneath.]

NARI: Four.

MARA: That is a staff note.

PIP: It is a number.

[Vex enters 4-4-4-4. The lock clicks. The door opens into the supply closet. A stack of hint cards spills out.]

MARA: That's where we keep the hints.

NARI: The hint system escaped.

[Vex picks up a card. It reads "ASK THE GAME MASTER." He turns toward the one-way window. Mara ducks. Pip knocks on the glass.]

PIP: Can we have another hint?

[Mara speaks through the hallway speaker. Her voice echoes from the open supply closet.]

MARA: You have solved the staff door.

NARI: The game door is still locked.

[Pip finds a number scratched on the mop handle. He enters it into the game lock. The bolt clicks but does not move.]

PIP: The room has given us another number.

VEX: The lock has given us another problem.

[Nari lifts the mop. A loose bristle catches on the paper key hanging from the ceiling. It drops into Pip's hand.]

PIP: We have the key.

MARA: The key is a metaphor.

VEX: We already escaped the staff door.

[Vex closes the supply closet. The game door remains shut as the timer reaches zero.]

FINAL BEAT: Nari opens the game door from the hall. The players hand her the mop and ask for their next hint. She says, "Look under the table," then leaves through the exit.
