# The Traffic Cone Gets Promoted

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace promotion ceremonies that mistake visibility for responsibility*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Supervisor Nari, a lemur; worker Vex, a macaque; inspector Mara, a gibbon; cone, a silent performer; courier, a macaque.

EXT. ROAD WORK SITE - MORNING

[A traffic cone stands beside a shallow trench. Nari pins a paper badge to it: ACTING SITE MANAGER. Vex holds a clipboard.]

NARI: The cone has been here longest.

VEX: It has not filled out an application.

MARA: It is visible from the street.

[The cone is nudged by a breeze and points toward the trench.]

NARI: It has identified a hazard.

VEX: That's where it was already standing.

[Mara asks the new manager to direct traffic. Nari gives the cone a small flag. Vex places it on top. The flag flutters, and drivers slow down.]

MARA: Effective leadership.

VEX: It hasn't said anything.

NARI: Neither has the director.

[The road crew carries a sign around the cone. The cone's point catches the sign's handle. It pivots, pointing traffic away from the site and toward Vex's wheelbarrow.]

VEX: The manager has rerouted the public.

NARI: The manager has moved the sign.

[Mara steps back to inspect. A cyclist follows the cone's new direction and rolls straight toward the wheelbarrow. Vex pulls it aside. The cyclist passes safely.]

MARA: Good judgment under pressure.

VEX: The wheelbarrow was in the way.

[Nari pins another badge to the cone: TEMPORARY TRAFFIC DIRECTOR. The extra paper makes it top-heavy. The cone tips over and rolls down the slight slope. Everyone chases it.]

NARI: Stop the manager!

VEX: It has left the site.

[Mara catches the cone before it reaches the road. The badges have wrapped around its base, hiding the orange stripes. She peels them off.]

MARA: We promoted the cone until no one could see it.

[Vex returns the cone to the trench. A truck approaches. The driver sees the cone and stops. Nari removes the badges. The orange stripes are visible again.]

NARI: It works better without its title.

VEX: Should we give it a raise?

MARA: Put it in the shade. It has been on shift since dawn.

[Nari rests the cone under the tree. The crew moves the wheelbarrow back to the proper path.]

[A delivery monkey approaches and sees the cone resting. He stops, then backs up to the start of the street.]

MARA: The manager has sent him around the block.

VEX: The cone is on break.

[Nari lifts the cone. The delivery monkey walks forward. Vex puts the clipboard beside the trench. The monkey stops again and waits for a signature.]

NARI: It needs a clear route.

MARA: It needs us to move the clipboard.

[Vex moves the clipboard. The monkey crosses, carrying the traffic report for the cone to sign.]

FINAL BEAT: Vex pins the manager badge to the empty clipboard instead. The clipboard falls over, and Mara parks the cone beside it to warn people away.
