# The Elevator Pitch Has No Exit

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: startup pitch contests, elevator pitches, and investors trapped in a literal elevator with presenters*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: FOUNDER, a capuchin; INVESTOR, a baboon; TECHNICIAN, an orangutan; PASSENGER, a macaque; ANNOUNCER, a gibbon.

[An elevator stops between floors. A founder holds a small prototype shaped like a soap dish. An investor checks a watch.]

ANNOUNCER: Please remain calm. A technician is on the way.

FOUNDER: Perfect. You are trapped with my idea for a subscription service that delivers a fresh soap dish every week.

INVESTOR: Why would anyone need a weekly soap dish?

FOUNDER: The old one becomes emotionally stale.

PASSENGER: Mine has been fine since Tuesday.

[The founder presses the emergency button. The elevator speaker clicks.]

ANNOUNCER: That button reaches building staff.

FOUNDER: Could it also reach a seed investor?

INVESTOR: It reached me. I am asking questions.

[The founder points at a chart on a phone. The display says 12 customers, 11 soap dishes, 1 return.]

INVESTOR: Your returns are almost your customers.

FOUNDER: One customer returned twice.

PASSENGER: That is one customer.

ACT ONE

[The elevator lights flicker. The technician's voice comes through the speaker.]

TECHNICIAN: We are working on the door. Please do not press every floor button.

[The founder presses all floor buttons.]

TECHNICIAN: I heard that.

FOUNDER: I was demonstrating user engagement.

INVESTOR: Your business depends on a stopped elevator?

FOUNDER: The captive audience is a low-cost acquisition channel.

PASSENGER: We are not captive. We are stuck.

[The investor takes the prototype and turns it over. A label reads SAMPLE, NOT SOAP.]

INVESTOR: You cannot wash with this.

FOUNDER: It is a concept dish.

INVESTOR: It has a hole in the bottom.

FOUNDER: That is the drain feature.

[The investor sets the dish on the elevator floor. It spins slowly around the hole.]

PASSENGER: At least it is entertaining.

FOUNDER: We can add entertainment to the premium tier.

[The elevator jolts. The founder's phone drops into the dish and slides to the center.]

FOUNDER: The product has captured my phone.

ACT TWO

[The technician opens the doors a few inches and asks everyone to stay back. The founder tries to hand over the chart, but the paper catches in the gap.]

TECHNICIAN: Give me your hand, not your pitch deck.

[The technician guides the passengers safely to the floor. The investor exits first, then turns back.]

INVESTOR: Send me a prototype that holds soap.

FOUNDER: Do you want a weekly delivery?

INVESTOR: I want one that works on the first day.

[The founder retrieves the phone from the sample dish and pockets both. The passenger steps out with a full-size bar of soap from a tote bag.]

PASSENGER: I have brought the market test.

FINAL IMAGE: The founder rides the stairs beside the investor, trying to time a pitch to each landing.
