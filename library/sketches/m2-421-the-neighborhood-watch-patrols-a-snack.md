# The Neighborhood Watch Patrols a Snack

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: security patrols that turn small neighborhood noises into scheduled incidents*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Watch captain Mara, a macaque; patrol Pip, a capuchin; resident Vex, a gibbon; recorder Nari, a lemur.

EXT. APARTMENT COURTYARD - NIGHT

[A folding table holds a flashlight, a clipboard, and a plate of banana chips. Mara points to a map.]

MARA: Patrol the west path every ten minutes.

PIP: What are we watching for?

MARA: Anything unusual.

[Vex steps out of the building carrying a plate.]

VEX: Is a midnight snack unusual?

NARI: It is if we record it.

[Mara writes "suspicious plate." Vex covers the chips with a napkin.]

VEX: It is a regular plate now.

[Pip lifts the flashlight. The beam hits a pair of glowing eyes under a bench.]

PIP: Intruder!

[A cat walks out, unimpressed.]

MARA: That's Mrs. Vex's cat.

VEX: The cat is visiting.

NARI: At a suspicious hour.

[Vex offers the cat a chip. Mara tells him not to feed a witness. The cat takes the chip and jumps onto the clipboard.]

PIP: It has taken the report.

[The cat sits on the map. Nari tries to slide the clipboard free. The cat stretches, keeping one paw on the page.]

NARI: We need a record of its route.

VEX: It is sitting still.

MARA: Then record its position.

[Pip moves the plate of chips to the other end of the table. The cat leaves the map and follows the scent.]

PIP: It has changed position.

NARI: We have a new incident.

[Mara moves the flashlight beam. The cat follows it across the courtyard and stops at the snack plate.]

VEX: The suspect is following evidence.

MARA: The suspect is following light.

[Vex waves the clipboard to block the beam. The cat jumps onto his shoulder, then down to the plate. Pip tries to record the route while keeping his flashlight pointed at the ground.]

PIP: It has circled the table.

NARI: So have we.

[Pip draws a circle around the cat. The cat stands and walks away, leaving one paw print inside the circle.]

PIP: It has escaped the perimeter.

[The team follows it. The cat leads them to the west path and stops beside a dripping gutter. A banana chip floats in a puddle. Vex recognizes it.]

VEX: One of my snacks is missing.

MARA: The cat took it.

VEX: That's a theft.

NARI: The snack was on the patrol table.

[Pip examines the wet chip with a flashlight. It reflects the beam.]

PIP: The evidence is shining.

MARA: It is wet.

[Vex retrieves the plate and counts the chips. One is gone. He writes a complaint against the cat, then looks at its empty dish.]

VEX: It needs a snack bowl.

MARA: We are not expanding patrol to animal catering.

[The cat rubs against Pip's leg. Pip gives it the plate.]

PIP: The bowl was in my hand.

FINAL BEAT: Nari changes the report to "patrol snack redistributed." The cat carries the clipboard back to the table, leaving its paw print beside the signature line.
