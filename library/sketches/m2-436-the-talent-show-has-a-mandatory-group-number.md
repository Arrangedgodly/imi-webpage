# The Talent Show Has a Mandatory Group Number

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised talent competitions forcing unrelated acts into group finale*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Producer Vex, an orangutan; juggler Pip, a capuchin; singer Nari, a lemur; judge Mara, a macaque.

INT. COMMUNITY THEATER - NIGHT

[A banner reads "ONE STAGE, ONE DREAM." Pip juggles three seed pods. Nari warms up quietly. Vex holds a clipboard.]

VEX: Excellent acts. Now we need the finale to feel unified.

PIP: My act is juggling.

NARI: Mine is a quiet song.

VEX: Together, they become a loud juggling song.

MARA: The show is in five minutes.

VEX: Plenty of time to add a theme.

[He gives Pip a tambourine and Nari a pair of seed pods.]

NARI: I don't juggle.

PIP: I don't sing.

VEX: That's the magic of collaboration.

[Pip tries to juggle the tambourine. It rings each time he catches it. Nari begins her song; the ringing interrupts every other phrase.]

NARI: The river moves so slow-

[Tambourine rings.]

PIP: Sorry.

NARI: -beneath the winter-

[Ring.]

NARI: Is that part of the arrangement?

VEX: It is a recurring musical motif.

[Mara watches from the judges' table, then clicks her pen in time. Pip hears it and matches the tambourine rhythm. Nari pauses, confused.]

MARA: I didn't mean to join.

VEX: You're the judge. Your participation raises the stakes.

MARA: It raises my hand.

[She waves the pen to stop them. Pip mistakes it for a cue and tosses a seed pod. It lands neatly in Nari's open palm. Nari uses it as a shaker, keeping the beat while singing.]

NARI: The river moves so slow, but it carries what we throw.

PIP: That's a good line.

NARI: It is the song.

VEX: We have a story now. The juggler throws, the singer catches, the judge decides.

MARA: I am not catching anything.

[The final rehearsal begins. Pip tosses a pod too high; it lands in Vex's clipboard clip. Vex's pages fan open, revealing a printed schedule that says "GROUP FINALE: EVERYONE."]

PIP: Does everyone include the audience?

VEX: If they can clap.

MARA: I can stop this.

[The stage manager's bell rings. Nari walks to center stage, holding the pod. Pip stands beside her, hands empty. Vex gestures enthusiastically.]

NARI: I will sing my song. Pip can juggle if he wants.

PIP: I want to.

[He juggles the pods silently while she sings. The audience claps at the end. Mara does not. Vex looks at her expectantly. She taps her pen once; Pip catches it in the air and adds it to the juggling pattern.]

MARA: I was keeping score.

PIP: Now you're in the act.

VEX: Our finale has a judge.

MARA: Our judge is leaving.

[She exits, but the audience claps in time with her footsteps.]

NARI: The show has a beat.

VEX: And a departure.
