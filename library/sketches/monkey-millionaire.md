# Monkey Millionaire

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: High-stakes television quiz shows and suspenseful lifelines*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[GAME SHOW MUSIC. A spotlight lands on contestant PIP, a nervous monkey at a podium. HOST VERA smiles with perfect teeth.]

VERA: Welcome to Monkey Millionaire, where one correct answer can win you a golden banana and one wrong answer can send you back to your regular job. Pip, ready for question one?

PIP: I was ready before the fog machine started.

VERA: For one hundred points. Which department approves office snacks?

PIP: Snacks.

VERA: Correct. We asked Snacks. They said they assumed it was Facilities.

PIP: That's our process.

VERA: For five hundred points. The meeting invite says "optional." What does that mean?

PIP: You can choose whether to attend.

VERA: Incorrect. It means the organizer will notice if you don't. You may use a lifeline.

PIP: I'll call a coworker.

[SFX: PHONE RINGS.]

COWORKER LULU: Hello?

PIP: What does "optional" mean?

LULU: Are you asking about the meeting I sent you?

PIP: Maybe.

LULU: Then it means attend. I need someone to take notes.

PIP: That sounds like a job.

LULU: It is optional for you to be happy about it.

VERA: A confident answer. Is that your final?

PIP: It's my only answer.

VERA: Correct. The audience groans because they know. For two thousand points, what is the correct response to "Can you hop on a quick call?"

PIP: Ask how long.

VERA: Incorrect. You must first say "Sure," then spend twenty minutes finding out the call is a screen share. Would you like to ask the audience?

PIP: Yes.

[APPLAUSE SIGN FLASHES. AUDIENCE MONKEYS HOLD UP CARDS: "NO," "MAYBE," AND ONE BANANA.] 

VERA: The audience is split. The banana is not a registered vote.

PIP: It should be. It knows what's at stake.

VERA: Your next lifeline is Ask the Expert. We have invited a senior manager.

MANAGER: I can't answer. I have a quick call.

VERA: Question: What does our company do?

MANAGER: We empower teams to unlock the future of work.

PIP: Is that correct?

VERA: It is the approved answer. It is not the accurate answer. For ten thousand points, name our product.

PIP: It's called BananaBase.

VERA: What does it do?

PIP: It stores bananas.

VERA: Correct. The product team spent three months debating whether to call it PeelCloud, FruitStack, or BaaS. The first customer uses a basket.

PIP: Can I have the golden banana now?

VERA: You must answer the final question. What is the golden banana made of?

PIP: Gold?

VERA: It's a banana-shaped trophy coated in gold-colored paint. The real gold banana is a separate prize, available through our premium tier.

PIP: I'll take the trophy.

VERA: Excellent. Please sign this release, photo consent, and seventeen-page waiver.

PIP: Can I use my lifeline?

VERA: You have one left. Phone a lawyer.

PIP: I thought I could phone a friend.

VERA: Our lawyer is also your friend. He brought a pen.

[LAWYER ENTERS, HANDS OVER THE PEN, AND EATS THE REAL BANANA ON THE STAND.] 

PIP: That's the prize.

LAWYER: It was in the terms.

VERA: You win the trophy, Pip. Your regular job awaits backstage.

PIP: At least the trophy is shiny.

VERA: The paint is still wet.

[THE THEME MUSIC PLAYS. PIP CAREFULLY HOLDS THE TROPHY BY ITS UNPAINTED BASE.]
