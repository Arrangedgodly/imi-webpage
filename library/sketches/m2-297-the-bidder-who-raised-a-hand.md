# The Bidder Who Raised a Hand

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: live charity auctions, rapid-fire bidding, and auctioneers who mistake ordinary gestures for enormous pledges*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Auctioneer Rell, a macaque; bidder Vela, a gibbon; bidder Odo, a capuchin; and clerk Palla, a ring-tailed lemur.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

Rell stands beside a painting of a small pond. Palla holds a clipboard.

RELL: We begin at ten coins. Do I hear twelve?

Vela raises her hand to scratch her ear.

RELL: Twelve from the back!

VELA: I was scratching.

RELL: Fourteen?

Odo raises his hand to wave at a friend.

RELL: Fourteen from the front!

ODO: I am greeting my aunt.

PALLA: We have two bids and two unrelated gestures.

RELL: The auction is moving quickly.

He points to Vela. She raises both hands to fix her hair.

RELL: Sixteen!

VELA: My hands are busy.

PALLA: Should I record that as a bid or a hairstyle?

Rell pauses. He asks everyone to use numbered paddles. Palla hands each bidder one. Vela holds hers in her left hand. Odo holds his in his right. Rell resumes.

RELL: Ten coins. Do I hear twelve?

Vela raises paddle two.

RELL: Twelve.

Odo raises paddle four.

RELL: Fourteen.

Vela lowers her paddle to drink water. Rell mistakes the movement for a withdrawal.

RELL: Going once to Odo.

Palla taps the clipboard.

PALLA: She lowered the paddle to reach her cup. The bid stands.

RELL: The paddle is a very important object.

Vela places her cup under the chair. Odo starts to raise his paddle but sneezes. The paddle flips into the air and lands on his head, pointing straight up.

RELL: Sixteen!

ODO: The paddle bid for me.

PALLA: It did not. Your last clear bid was fourteen.

The auctioneer asks both bidders to place paddles on the table between bids. He restarts at sixteen. Odo raises his paddle; Vela answers at eighteen. Odo checks the price card, looks at the pond painting, and lowers his paddle.

ODO: I will help clean the pond instead.

RELL: Going once at eighteen, going twice, sold to Vela.

The painting sells to Vela for eighteen coins. Rell announces the winner. Vela asks whether the proceeds will pay for the community pond cleanup.

PALLA: Yes. The pond needs a new footpath and a bench.

Rell gestures toward the painting. Odo raises his paddle to scratch his nose.

RELL: We are not accepting bids on the bench yet.

Palla removes the numbered paddles. She gives each bidder a small bell to ring for the next item.

RELL: Do I hear one ring?

Vela's bell rings as she sets it down.

RELL: One ring from Vela.

VELA: I am not bidding.

PALLA: We will keep the bell on the clerk's table.

Odo wins the next item, a basket of garden tools, with a clear paddle raise. He carries it to the cleanup crew. Rell ends the auction with both hands on the podium.

RELL: Sold. No additional bids from my elbows.

Palla records the total. Vela and Odo help roll up the pond painting. It shows the exact spot where the new bench will stand.
