# The Receipt Has a Long Story

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: prestige documentaries that turn ordinary purchases into sprawling investigative sagas*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Leni, a lemur cashier; Oss, a capuchin customer; Mako, a gibbon documentarian; Rilla, a macaque producer.

INT. CORNER SHOP - DAY

[MAKO SETS A CAMERA ON THE COUNTER. RILLA UNROLLS A RECEIPT THAT REACHES THE FLOOR.]

RILLA: We need the receipt's full story.

LENI: It records two apples and a paper bag.

MAKO: We will let it speak for itself.

OSS: I paid for it.

RILLA: You may appear in the film as "the buyer."

Oss tries to take his apples. Mako asks him to hold still while the camera tracks the receipt. It curls under the counter and disappears behind the cash drawer.

MAKO: Cut. The document has gone off the record.

LENI: It is under the register.

RILLA: Can you reconstruct the last time you saw it?

Leni remembers printing it. She presses the register key. The machine prints a duplicate receipt, which curls toward the first one.

MAKO: Two receipts. A pattern.

OSS: No. The first is under the drawer, and the second is on the counter.

Rilla places the duplicate inside a clear sleeve labeled SUSPECT TWO. Mako sets up a light. The glare makes all the numbers vanish.

MAKO: The evidence resists being seen.

LENI: The plastic sleeve is shiny.

Rilla takes it off. The receipt's ink appears.

OSS: It says I bought two apples.

MAKO: We haven't established motive.

Oss looks at the apples. One has a bruise.

OSS: I wanted fruit.

Rilla pulls out a paper timeline. It begins at the opening of the shop and tracks every register sound. Leni presses the drawer key by accident. It rings. The camera swings toward the register.

MAKO: The receipt is signaling.

LENI: That's the cash drawer.

Rilla asks Mako to narrate the discovery. He speaks gravely while Leni reaches under the counter and retrieves the original receipt.

MAKO: At 10:06, the buyer approached the counter. At 10:07, the paper emerged. By 10:08, it had vanished.

OSS: It's 10:09.

Mako turns to the camera. Leni folds the receipt and puts it in Oss's bag.

RILLA: We need a final interview.

OSS: My interview is that I bought apples.

MAKO: Can you say it again with more uncertainty?

Oss bites one apple. It crunches loudly. Mako lowers the camera.

MAKO: That's useful audio.

Rilla opens the paper timeline to add the bite. The paper's weight pins down the loose receipt under the counter. Leni slides the register drawer shut.

LENI: The mystery is solved.

RILLA: We don't have the original ending.

Mako films the register display, which reads TOTAL: $2.00.

MAKO: It ends with a number.

Oss leaves with the apples and the original receipt. Mako asks him to return for the epilogue.

[THE CAMERA HOLDS ON THE DUPLICATE RECEIPT IN ITS SLEEVE. A BREEZE FROM THE OPEN DOOR LIFTS IT. RILLA CATCHES IT, THEN LABELS THE SLEEVE "WIND."]
