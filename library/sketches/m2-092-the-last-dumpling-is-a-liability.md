# The Last Dumpling Is a Liability

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking competition shows and overdramatic judging of one shared snack*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: HOST MIRA, a lemur; CHEF BOK, a macaque; JUDGE VEE, a gibbon; and ASSISTANT NIM, a capuchin.

[A kitchen counter holds one dumpling on a plate. A clock counts down from ten minutes.] 

MIRA: The challenge is to create a memorable meal from one dumpling.

BOK: One dumpling is a meal for one.

VEE: The judges expect an elevated concept.

NIM: I can bring a leaf.

VEE: Is it edible?

NIM: It is clean.

MIRA: The challenge begins now.

[BOK CUTS THE DUMPLING IN HALF.] 

VEE: You have reduced the central ingredient.

BOK: I have made two portions.

MIRA: What will you do with them?

BOK: Put one on each side of the plate.

VEE: That is symmetry, not cooking.

BOK: I can warm them.

NIM: The stove is off.

BOK: Then I can serve them at room temperature.

MIRA: The clock is running.

VEE: Do you have a sauce?

BOK: I have water.

VEE: Water is not a sauce.

BOK: It is if it is in a cup.

[NIM SETS DOWN A CUP. VEE TAKES A SIP.] 

VEE: That is just water.

BOK: It is palate-cleansing water.

MIRA: The judges are intrigued.

VEE: We have one minute. What is the story behind the dish?

BOK: The dumpling was made yesterday. I saved half for lunch.

NIM: The other half is already gone.

BOK: Then this is the last dumpling.

VEE: That raises the stakes.

MIRA: How will you plate it?

BOK: Put the halves back together.

[THE PIECES ARE REUNITED. THEY FIT.] 

VEE: The dumpling has returned to its original form.

BOK: It is still one dumpling.

MIRA: The judges are discussing presentation.

VEE: There is no garnish, no sauce, and no dramatic flame.

BOK: The dumpling was already cooked.

VEE: A cooking competition usually includes cooking.

BOK: The challenge was one dumpling. I made two portions and kept them warm.

NIM: There is a question from the audience. Could the halves have been plated separately?

BOK: They can be. One half is already on Judge Vee's plate.

VEE: I did not know the dish was being served.

MIRA: The plate is here. The remaining half is here.

VEE: I have tasted one half. The other half looks identical.

BOK: It is from the same dumpling.

VEE: Then the second bite confirms the first.

[HE TAKES THE SECOND HALF. THE PLATE IS EMPTY.] 

NIM: The judge has eaten the evidence.

VEE: I can still describe it. The filling was warm and the wrapper held together.

MIRA: What would you change?

VEE: I would make more than one.

BOK: That would require another dumpling.

VEE: The competition has revealed a supply problem.

MIRA: The challenge was one dumpling. It has been shared by the cook and judge.

NIM: I am still hungry.

BOK: We can make a second batch after the cameras leave.

MIRA: Time. Judges, taste.

[VEE TAKES A BITE. BOK TAKES THE OTHER HALF.] 

VEE: The filling is good.

BOK: The wrapper is soft.

NIM: There is none left for me.

MIRA: The competition has delivered one clear verdict: make enough for everyone.

VEE: That was not in the challenge.

BOK: It is in the kitchen.
