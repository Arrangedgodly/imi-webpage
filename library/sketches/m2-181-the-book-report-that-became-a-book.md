# The Book Report That Became a Book

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school assignments that reward formal presentation more than reading, until the student reads the report aloud*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Pava, a gibbon student; Doma, a macaque teacher; Rell, a capuchin classmate; and Nari, a lemur librarian.

INT. CLASSROOM - MORNING

Doma announces book reports. Pava places one sheet on her desk. Rell carries a decorated folder with tabs, drawings and a small bell.

DOMA: The report is about the book, not the folder.

RELL: The folder has a title page.

Pava begins. Her report says the book has a beginning, a middle and an ending. Doma asks what happens in it. Pava says a monkey searches for a lost key and finds it in a basket.

RELL: My report has a map of the basket.

Doma says Rell may present after Pava. He rings his folder bell to mark the start. Nari, visiting from the library, asks if the class has returned the borrowed book. Rell says he needed it to decorate his report. The book is beneath the folder.

Doma asks him to take the book out. The folder collapses. A paper map falls to the floor and covers the real book.

PAVA: My report fits on one page.

RELL: It has no illustrations.

Pava reads a sentence from the book: "The key was in the basket." She explains that the character had looked under a chair and behind a curtain first. Doma asks why the monkey did not check the basket sooner. Pava says the story needed to last more than one page.

Rell presents his report. He has a timeline of the key's journey, but all the events are labeled "before basket" and "after basket." He asks the class to ring the bell at each transition. Doma rings it. Nari points out that the timeline is upside down. Rell turns it over; the key drawing falls out.

Nari picks up the key drawing and notices the borrowed book's library card is still inside. She checks the due date. It is today.

DOMA: You can return the book after the report.

NARI: The library is open now.

Rell looks at the clock. He asks if returning the book counts as the ending. Pava says it is what happens after the ending. Doma declares the return trip an educational outing. Nari leads the class to the library. Pava carries the book; Rell carries his collapsed folder.

At the library desk, Nari stamps the card. Rell asks if the stamp can go on his report. Nari says it belongs in the book. He takes the last page from his folder and reads the ending aloud. Doma leads the class back; Rell follows, reading his list of acknowledgments.

DOMA: That was a good report.

RELL: Should I add the bell?

PAVA: The story already ended.

Rell puts the bell away and folds his empty folder into his pack. At the library, Nari slides the borrowed book onto the shelf. Back in the classroom, the bell rings inside Rell's bag.

DOMA: Have you started the next report?

RELL: That was my bibliography.
