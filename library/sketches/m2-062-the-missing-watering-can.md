# The Missing Watering Can

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: true-crime reenactment documentaries and overdramatized evidence boards*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: NARRATOR, a deep-voiced macaque; DETECTIVE PELL, a lemur; WITNESS DOT, a squirrel monkey; and GARDENER MOSS, a baboon.

[A shed. An empty hook casts a long shadow. A red string connects a photo of a watering can to a map of the garden.]

NARRATOR: At dawn, one watering can vanished. The garden would never be the same.

PELL: The garden was watered last night.

NARRATOR: The suspect knew the plants would be thirsty.

MOSS: I put the can by the tap.

DOT: I saw it by the compost.

PELL: Those locations are six steps apart.

NARRATOR: A changing alibi.

[The detective examines a damp trail.]

PELL: This water goes toward the shed.

NARRATOR: Evidence of a hurried escape?

PELL: It is the path from the tap.

[The narrator adds a red string anyway.]

NARRATOR: The case grows more tangled.

DOT: The can has a dent shaped like a leaf.

MOSS: It is shaped like a watering can.

[The camera shows a printout of the shed with the word CRIME SCENE taped across it.]

PELL: Please remove that tape. The shed door needs to open.

NARRATOR: The suspect may still be inside.

[The detective opens the shed. The can is on the floor under a bag of soil.]

MOSS: I put it there so it wouldn't blow over.

DOT: Then who watered the plants?

MOSS: The rain.

NARRATOR: A second mystery.

PELL: There is a weather log. It rained at midnight.

NARRATOR: The investigation now points to the sky.

PELL: The sky does not have a motive.

[The narrator pins a cloud photo to the board.]

NARRATOR: The storm had access, opportunity, and no witnesses.

MOSS: The storm also left a puddle.

PELL: That's ordinary weather.

NARRATOR: The most ordinary suspect of all.

[The detective removes the red string. It tangles around the can's handle.]

PELL: Now the evidence is attached to the object.

DOT: Should I call the gardener?

MOSS: I am here.

NARRATOR: The case is closed, but the garden is still damp.

PELL: That's how rain works.

[The narrator turns the board around. On the back is a shopping list: HOSE, NEW HOOK, MORE STRING.]

NARRATOR: Next week, the hook goes missing.

MOSS: I took it off to fix the shed.

PELL: We need to review the evidence board before closing.

NARRATOR: The red strings reveal a web of suspicion.

PELL: They are tying the watering can to the door handle.

DOT: The can was found in the shed, under soil.

MOSS: I put it there.

PELL: The label should say that.

[The narrator removes the photo of the empty hook. A second photo underneath shows the can beside the tap.]

NARRATOR: Another image from the night of the disappearance.

MOSS: That was before I moved it.

PELL: When?

MOSS: After I watered the seedlings.

DOT: So nobody stole it.

NARRATOR: The garden will recover.

PELL: It is fine. The storm watered it.

[The camera operator sets up a dark reenactment of Moss moving the can.]

MOSS: I don't move that slowly.

NARRATOR: Can we do it again, but with more fear?

PELL: No. The can is back on its hook.

[The narrator turns the evidence board around. It is a shed door.]

[BLACKOUT.]
