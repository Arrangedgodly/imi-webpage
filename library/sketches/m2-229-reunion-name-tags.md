# The Reunion Name Tags

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: reunion organizers who replace familiar recognition with elaborate icebreakers and identity games*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Host Sori, a gibbon; Guest Pala, a macaque; Organizer Miri, a capuchin; and Guest Daro, a baboon.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

A reunion table holds blank name tags, markers, and a bowl of tiny question cards. Pala walks in and sees Daro.

PALA: Daro! It has been years.

DARO: Pala! You still wear that blue scarf.

MIRI: Please pause. The welcome activity begins with a memory prompt.

SORI: Draw a card and describe a childhood object without naming it.

PALA: I know who Daro is.

MIRI: The activity helps everyone connect.

SORI: We will use the prompt cards to find familiar people.

PALA: We already found each other.

DARO: The card has a drawing of a school bell.

PALA: We both remember that bell.

MIRI: The instructions say not to name the object.

DARO: The bell is ringing now.

[THE KITCHEN BELL RINGS. EVERYONE LOOKS TOWARD THE CAKE TABLE.]

[DARO DRAWS A CARD. IT ASKS HIM TO DESCRIBE A SCARF.]

DARO: It is blue, soft, and around a neck.

PALA: That's my scarf.

SORI: Excellent. You have made a connection.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - LATER

Miri hands out name tags with numbers instead of names. Sori points to a seating chart.

MIRI: For privacy, we use numbers until after the icebreaker.

PALA: I am number twelve.

DARO: I am number eight.

SORI: Find the person whose number matches your first memory of the school.

DARO: I remember Pala, not a number.

PALA: We used to share a desk.

MIRI: The activity card asks for a shared object, not a shared memory.

DARO: We shared the desk.

SORI: Does the desk have a number?

[MIRI LOOKS AT THE CHAIR LABELS. THEY ARE NUMBERED. THE DESK IS NOT.]

MIRI: We will need another card.

MIRI: Please follow the chart.

[THE CHART PLACES PALA AND DARO AT OPPOSITE ENDS OF THE ROOM. THEY WAVE TO EACH OTHER OVER A TABLE OF CAKE.]

PALA: We can see each other.

SORI: The activity requires a partner.

DARO: We are partners.

MIRI: Your numbers do not match.

PALA: Our names do.

[THEY TRADE NAME TAGS. THE TAGS SAY "PALA" AND "DARO" IN LARGE LETTERS.]

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - CLOSING

The reunion guests gather near the exit. The question cards have been stacked away. Sori holds a bowl of blank tags.

SORI: We have one final activity. Write your name as you want to be remembered.

PALA: I want to be remembered as Pala.

DARO: I want to be remembered as the one who saved the cake.

[MIRI LIFTS A TRAY. ONE SLICE REMAINS.]

MIRI: The cake is still here.

DARO: Then the memory is accurate.

[THE GUESTS WRITE THEIR NAMES ON THE TAGS. SORI PUTS THE QUESTION CARDS IN THE RECYCLING BIN.]
