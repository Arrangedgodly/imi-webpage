# The Bake Sale Charges a Transfer Fee

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: fundraising sales where every small purchase triggers another payment step*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Treasurer Mara, a macaque; baker Pip, a capuchin; cashier Vex, a gibbon; customer Nari, a lemur.

INT. SCHOOL HALL - SATURDAY

[A table holds banana bread, muffins, and a card reader. Vex has taped a price list to the table.]

VEX: Muffins are two coins. Banana bread is four.

NARI: I will take one muffin.

[Vex taps the card reader. It displays SERVICE FEE: ONE COIN.]

NARI: The muffin costs two.

VEX: The reader costs one.

MARA: The bake sale doesn't charge a reader fee.

VEX: The reader is fundraising too.

[Pip sets a tray of muffins down. One rolls to the edge. Nari catches it.]

PIP: That's the sample.

VEX: Samples are free.

[The card reader beeps.]

VEX: Sample deposit: one coin.

MARA: No.

[Vex changes the sign to "Cash only." Nari hands him a coin. He puts it into a jar labeled TABLE RENTAL.]

NARI: I paid for the muffin.

VEX: The table took it.

[Mara lifts the jar. A hole in the bottom lets the coins fall into a tin beneath the table.]

MARA: The table is charging rent to the jar.

[Pip moves the jar onto the chair. The chair squeaks.]

VEX: Seat fee.

NARI: I am standing.

VEX: The chair is reserved.

[Mara removes the chair. Vex puts the muffin on a paper plate. The reader beeps again.]

VEX: Plate surcharge.

PIP: The plate is part of the muffin.

VEX: Then we need a receipt.

[He prints a long slip. It wraps around Nari's wrist like a bracelet. The paper lists muffin, reader fee, table rent, seat fee, plate surcharge, and receipt charge.]

MARA: Which charge did you invent last?

VEX: The receipt charge.

[Nari returns the muffin. Vex holds the card reader out for a refund. The device asks for a service fee to process it. Mara unplugs the reader.]

MARA: Cash sale. Two coins. One muffin.

[Pip hands Nari the muffin and gives her two coins from the jar.]

VEX: We should document the refund.

MARA: We should sell the muffins.

[Nari takes the muffin to a table at the other end of the hall. Pip slides the remaining tray toward her. Vex tries to follow, but Mara gives him the long receipt to hold.]

VEX: This is very long.

PIP: It lists every fee.

VEX: It lists the fee for the list.

[Mara places a cash box on the table. A student buys the remaining muffins with exact coins. Vex tears off the receipt and hands it over.]

NARI: The sale is complete.

VEX: We should have a transfer receipt.

MARA: You can write one on the back.

[Vex turns the paper over. The reverse is blank. He starts to write.]

FINAL BEAT: Vex adds a sign that says "No hidden fees." Mara turns it around; the back reads "Sign fee, waived." Vex faces the clean side toward the customers.
