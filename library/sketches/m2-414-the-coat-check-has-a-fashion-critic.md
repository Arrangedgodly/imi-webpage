# The Coat Check Has a Fashion Critic

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: celebrity fashion coverage that judges an outfit before its wearer arrives*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Attendant Pip, a capuchin; critic Vex, a gibbon; guest Mara, a macaque; guest Nari, a lemur.

INT. THEATER FOYER - EVENING

[A coat-check rack is nearly empty. Pip hangs a bright yellow raincoat on a numbered hook. Vex inspects it through a jeweled lorgnette.]

VEX: The silhouette is bold.

PIP: It's wet.

VEX: The sheen is intentional.

[Mara enters in a plain blue coat.]

MARA: Number twelve, please.

[Vex circles her, studying the coat.]

VEX: A daring refusal to participate.

MARA: I am going to a play.

[Vex whispers into a handheld microphone.]

VEX: We have a minimalist in the foyer.

[Pip hands her a ticket. A second coat, a large green cape, slides off the rack and covers Vex's head.]

PIP: Number eleven.

VEX: I have been styled.

[Nari arrives with a striped scarf. Vex rates it before she removes her jacket.]

VEX: A line that implies motion.

NARI: It implies a scarf.

[She hands it to Pip. He ties it around the rack's base to keep it from wobbling. Vex watches, fascinated.]

VEX: Structural accessories. Very current.

[Mara returns for her coat. Vex tries to stop her.]

VEX: The blue reads as quiet confidence.

MARA: It reads as mine.

[Vex asks Pip for a close-up. Pip lifts the coat by its hanger. The sleeve is turned inside out and a mitten falls out.]

VEX: Hidden winter detail.

MARA: That's my mitten.

[Pip retrieves the mitten. A second mitten falls from the yellow raincoat. The rack tips toward Vex. Nari grabs the base, but her scarf is tied to it and pulls her forward.]

NARI: My scarf has become the foundation.

PIP: It was holding the rack.

[Vex removes the green cape from his head. The rack straightens. Mara puts on her coat. Vex turns to camera.]

VEX: The evening's strongest look is leaving.

MARA: The evening's only look is late.

[She exits. Vex attempts to follow, but Pip hands him a numbered ticket.]

PIP: Your coat is still checked.

VEX: I am wearing it.

PIP: That is number eleven.

[Vex looks down. The green cape is still over his shoulders. He tries to hand it to Pip, but Pip points to the rack.]

VEX: I am wearing this.

PIP: That's the coat you checked.

VEX: It is also the cape I borrowed.

[Nari takes her scarf from the rack. The rack leans without its support and catches Vex's cape again.]

NARI: The fashion is holding the furniture up.

[Vex attempts to sit on the rack's lower bar. It rolls toward the check-in desk. Pip catches the hanger while Mara catches Vex.]

PIP: Number eleven is unstable.

FINAL BEAT: Vex gives back the green cape. Pip hangs his microphone on the hook. Nari adjusts the striped scarf to keep the rack upright, and Pip issues her a coat-check ticket for the furniture.
