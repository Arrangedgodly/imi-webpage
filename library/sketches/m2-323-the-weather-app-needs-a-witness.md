# The Weather App Needs a Witness

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: phone apps that demand excessive confirmations for simple observations*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: User Kito, a macaque; app voice, a parrot; friend Seli, a gibbon.

EXT. GARDEN PATH - DAY

Kito opens a weather app. The sky is sunny. The app asks, "Are you sure you want to view today's weather?" Kito taps yes.

APP: Confirm that you are outdoors.

KITO: I am.

APP: Please provide a witness.

Seli walks up carrying a watering can.

SELI: I saw him.

APP: Witness must verify the sky.

Seli looks up.

SELI: Blue. A few clouds.

APP: Was this observation made in the current location?

Kito taps "yes." The app asks him to rotate the phone toward the horizon. He turns it. The app displays a spinning map and says "Finding your area." It places them in the pond.

KITO: We are beside the garden.

APP: Confirm you are not boating.

Seli says they are on land. The app asks if she is an authorized weather witness. Seli taps "not sure." The screen requests a backup witness. A tortoise crawls by.

KITO: He was here first.

APP: Please obtain a signature.

Kito holds out the phone. The tortoise crawls over the screen. The app recognizes a fingerprint and unlocks a premium forecast.

APP: Upgrade to see whether it will rain.

Kito says the sky is clear. The app asks if he wants to report the sky as inaccurate. Seli taps "report an issue." The app asks them to take a photo. Kito photographs the sky. The app uploads it, then asks if it is an image of the sky.

SELI: It is blue with clouds.

APP: Please describe the weather.

KITO: Sunny.

APP: Did you mean "sunny" or "mostly sunny"?

Kito chooses "sunny." The app says that answer may affect future recommendations. It asks permission to access the camera, microphone, and pond.

SELI: Why does it need the pond?

APP: To improve local rain prediction.

A cloud passes in front of the sun. The app announces "partly cloudy." Kito says that is now correct.

APP: Would you like to save this location?

KITO: Yes.

APP: Name this location.

Kito types "Garden." The app says the name is already in use. It offers "Garden 2." Kito accepts. The app generates a detailed forecast for Garden 2, located at the pond.

Seli's watering can tips and spills a little water on Kito's foot.

APP: Rain detected.

KITO: That came from the can.

APP: Would you like to report a local shower?

Seli taps "no." The app asks for a witness. Kito points at her.

Kito locks the forecast on Garden 2. The app requests one final review. Seli declines and sets the phone on the low garden bench. The sun is still visible above the real garden.

TAG

The tortoise has crawled away with the phone balanced on its shell.

APP: Location updated: moving slowly.

SELI: That is the most accurate thing it has said.
