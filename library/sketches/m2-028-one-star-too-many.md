# One Star Too Many

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Online restaurant reviews, influencer dining, and fussy tasting menus*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Chef Sada, a macaque; reviewer Brindle, a baboon; food influencer Kiko, a capuchin; server Obo, a gibbon; diner Yara, an orangutan.

[STAGE: A TINY RESTAURANT TABLE. A BELL, A PHONE ON A TRIPOD, AND ONE VERY LARGE MENU.]

OBO: Welcome to Branch Table. Our menu changes when the chef remembers it.
SADA: Tonight's tasting begins with a leaf of welcome.
BRINDLE: Is it local?
SADA: It grew under the table.
KIKO: Hold on. I need a shot of the leaf arriving.
YARA: It has been here since we sat down.
KIKO: Then I'll photograph the anticipation.
OBO: The leaf is served on a warm pebble.
BRINDLE: The pebble is cold.
SADA: It cooled while traveling from the kitchen.
BRINDLE: How far is the kitchen?
OBO: Three steps and a curtain.
SADA: The next course is a deconstructed fruit.
YARA: It is an apple with a bite in it.
SADA: The bite is the deconstruction.
BRINDLE: Who took the bite?
KIKO: I did, for the video.
BRINDLE: Then it is a partially reviewed apple.
OBO: Shall I bring the second course?
YARA: Yes, please.
OBO: The second course is a broth of possibilities.
BRINDLE: This is an empty cup.
SADA: The broth is being imagined.
KIKO: My followers love an immersive meal.
YARA: I am imagining soup with noodles.
BRINDLE: I am imagining a refund.
SADA: A refund is available as a side dish.
OBO: It comes in a small bowl.
BRINDLE: How much?
OBO: The same as the broth.
KIKO: Let me ask my audience.
YARA: Your audience cannot taste this.
KIKO: They can vote on the garnish.
SADA: I have a garnish of one crisp leaf.
BRINDLE: Is it the welcome leaf?
SADA: It has advanced in its career.
OBO: The chef's special is a quiet moment between courses.
YARA: That sounds pleasant.
BRINDLE: How long is the moment?
SADA: Until someone asks for the check.
KIKO: I have a sponsorship code for checks.
OBO: We don't take codes. We take shells.
KIKO: Can I pay in exposure?
SADA: Only if exposure is edible.
BRINDLE: My review says the service is slow.
OBO: I brought three courses in one trip.
BRINDLE: The trip was short.
YARA: The quiet moment was nice.
SADA: Thank you. It is our most consistent dish.
KIKO: My video is getting comments.
OBO: What do they say?
KIKO: One asks whether the pebble is gluten-free.
BRINDLE: And the rating?
YARA: Four stars for the quiet, one for the soup I invented.
SADA: That is five stars.
BRINDLE: I gave the restaurant one.
SADA: There are six stars now.
OBO: We have exceeded the menu.
KIKO: I will post the total.
YARA: Post the apple too.
KIKO: I ate it.
BRINDLE: Then we have a strong conclusion.
SADA: Dessert is the photograph of dessert.
OBO: I'll bring the phone.

[THE PHONE FLASHES. THE TABLE IS EMPTY EXCEPT FOR A WARMING PEBBLE AND FIVE HANDS REACHING FOR IT.]
