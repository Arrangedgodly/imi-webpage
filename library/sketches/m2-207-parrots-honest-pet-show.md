# The Parrot's Honest Pet Show

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: pet-show pageants that coach animals to perform polished personalities for judges*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Host Pella, a macaque; Handler Ron, a gibbon; Tiki, a parrot; Judge Nera, a capuchin; and Sound Tech Dax, a baboon.

INT. COMMUNITY SHOW HALL - AFTERNOON

A banner reads "MOST CHARMING COMPANION." Ron stands beside Tiki's perch. Pella holds a cue card.

PELLA: The judges want a friendly greeting, a clever phrase, and a graceful wave.

RON: Tiki knows one greeting.

PELLA: Which one?

TIKI: Where is my cracker?

NERA: Very direct.

RON: He learned that from asking for a cracker.

PELLA: We can work with it. Give him a cue.

RON: Tiki, say hello.

PELLA: Could he say it toward the judges?

RON: He can turn around.

PELLA: Please turn slowly, for the cameras.

[RON ROTATES THE PERCH. TIKI TURNS HIS HEAD BACK TOWARD THE DOOR.]

TIKI: Where is my cracker?

NERA: He has a clear sense of direction.

TIKI: Your pocket is loud.

DAX: That was a clean entrance.

PELLA: Let's try the clever phrase.

RON: Tiki, what do you think of the judges?

TIKI: They are wearing hats indoors.

[Nera removes her hat. It is the pageant's small crown.]

NERA: The hat is part of my judging uniform.

TIKI: You have a bird on your head.

INT. COMMUNITY SHOW HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Pella arranges a tiny silk flag beside the perch. Ron holds a sunflower seed as a reward.

PELLA: When the music plays, Tiki waves the flag. Then we present the charm ribbon.

RON: He doesn't like flags.

PELLA: Does he like music?

TIKI: Turn it down.

[DAX PLAYS A BRIGHT MARCH. TIKI LEANS AWAY FROM THE SPEAKER.]

NERA: He has a strong opinion about volume.

PELLA: We can edit that into a charming head tilt.

TIKI: Where is my cracker?

RON: The cracker is in my pocket.

TIKI: Your pocket is full of crumbs.

[Pella quietly trades the flag for a small perch bell. Tiki taps it once.]

DAX: We have a wave.

NERA: We have a tap.

PELLA: The tap can symbolize a wave.

TIKI: That is not what it means.

INT. COMMUNITY SHOW HALL - AWARDS

Nera holds up a ribbon. Pella smiles at the audience.

NERA: The award for best stage presence goes to Tiki.

RON: He is looking at the exit.

TIKI: Where is my cracker?

[Pella offers a cracker. Tiki takes it, then drops it into the ribbon bowl.]

TIKI: For the bird on your head.

[Nera looks at the tiny crown perched on her hair. The audience laughs.]

PELLA: That is an original gesture.

TIKI: I want to go home.

RON: That part is true.

[RON CARRIES THE PERCH OUT. PELLA TRIES TO PIN THE RIBBON TO THE EMPTY STAND. IT FALLS OFF.]

DAX: The winner has left with his own handler.
