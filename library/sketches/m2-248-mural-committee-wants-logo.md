# The Mural Committee Wants a Logo

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public-art committees that judge a mural by sponsor visibility instead of the artist's design*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Artist Miri, a capuchin; committee chair Ralo, a gibbon; sponsor rep Tavi, a macaque; and resident Odo, a baboon.

EXT. COMMUNITY WALL - DAY

Miri paints a grove of trees across a wall. Ralo holds a clipboard. Tavi carries a large sponsor stencil.

RALO: The committee likes the branches.

TAVI: The sponsor logo needs to be in the center.

MIRI: The center is the tree canopy.

TAVI: The logo can be the sun.

ODO: The sun is already painted.

[TAVI HOLDS THE STENCIL OVER THE SUN. THE LOGO COVERS HALF THE CANOPY.]

RALO: It is visible.

MIRI: It blocks the birds.

EXT. COMMUNITY WALL - LATER

The committee reviews the wall. Tavi has added a small logo beside each tree. Odo steps back.

ODO: There are four logos.

TAVI: One for each sponsor contribution.

MIRI: The wall is about the grove.

RALO: We can keep the sponsors in a border.

TAVI: The border is not in the contract.

[ODO MEASURES THE WALL. THE LOGOS TAKE UP MORE SPACE THAN THE PAINTED PATH.]

ODO: The sponsor area is larger than the grove.

TAVI: It is a high-visibility wall.

MIRI: The highest visibility is the empty space around the logo.

EXT. COMMUNITY WALL - AFTERNOON

Tavi asks Miri to paint a blank square for a QR code. Ralo holds up a sign.

RALO: The code lets visitors learn about the project.

MIRI: It points to a sponsor page.

TAVI: The page has the project information.

ODO: The link says "Coming Soon."

[TAVI SCANS THE CODE. HIS PHONE OPENS A BLANK PAGE.]

TAVI: The page is loading.

MIRI: The paint is dry.

[RALO TAKES THE SPONSOR STENCIL AND SETS IT ASIDE.]

RALO: We can put the project story on a small plaque.

TAVI: The logo still needs to appear.

MIRI: It can go on the plaque.

ODO: The plaque is smaller than the mural.

[TAVI LOOKS AT THE PAINTED GROVE.]

TAVI: The mural is the project.

MIRI: That is what I painted.

EXT. COMMUNITY WALL - EVENING

A resident stops to look at the painted trees. Ralo gives them the project plaque.

RESIDENT: Where is the grove?

MIRI: The wall shows the walking path and the old trees.

RESIDENT: I can see the bench I use.

TAVI: The plaque lists the sponsor names.

ODO: It is covering the painted bench.

[RALO MOVES THE PLAQUE TO THE EDGE. THE LOGO IS NOW SMALLER THAN THE BENCH IN THE MURAL.]

TAVI: The sponsors are still visible.

MIRI: The residents are too.

RESIDENT: I came here to see the wall, not scan it.

[THE COMMITTEE APPROVES THE WALL. TAVI HOLDS THE PLAQUE IN FRONT OF THE LOGO, WHICH COVERS ITS NAME.]

TAVI: The plaque needs its own plaque.

MIRI: Paint it on the back.

[RALO TURNS THE PLAQUE AROUND. THE SMALL LOGO FACES THE WALL.]
