# The Toy That Does Everything

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: toy commercials promising impossible all-in-one play features*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: PITCHER VEE, a macaque; INVENTOR TOLL, a gibbon; TESTER RO, a capuchin; and a small wooden toy.

[A bright mat. Vee holds up a wooden cube with one painted side.] 

VEE: Introducing the All-Play Cube. It is a block, a platform, a hiding place, and a friend.

TOLL: It is a cube.

VEE: It can become anything.

RO: Can I stack it?

VEE: Absolutely.

[RO STACKS A SECOND CUBE ON TOP. IT FALLS.] 

TOLL: It cannot support another cube.

VEE: It supports imagination.

RO: Can it roll?

TOLL: Cubes do not roll smoothly.

VEE: Watch this.

[VEE PUSHES THE CUBE. IT TUMBLES ONTO ONE SIDE AND STOPS.] 

VEE: A rolling surprise.

RO: It did not go anywhere.

TOLL: The cube is made of wood. It can be a building block, but not a wheel.

VEE: We can add a wheel attachment.

TOLL: Then it will not be a cube.

RO: Can it hide a seed?

[VEE TURNS THE CUBE OVER. THE BOTTOM IS SOLID.] 

VEE: It can pretend to hide a seed.

RO: The seed is sitting on top.

TOLL: We can make a slot, but then we need to round the edges and check it does not split.

VEE: That sounds like product development.

RO: I like the plain cube. I can make a little wall.

[RO BUILDS A LOW WALL. THE CUBE HOLDS.] 

VEE: The All-Play Cube is now a wall.

TOLL: The wall has one cube in it.

RO: It can keep the marble from rolling under the bench.

[RO ROLLS A MARBLE. IT STOPS AT THE CUBE.] 

VEE: That is a real feature.

TOLL: The cube is useful in the way it is built.

VEE: Can we film an ad with the marble?

RO: Only if I get to keep playing.

[THE MARBLE ROLLS AGAIN. RO MOVES THE CUBE TO CATCH IT.] 

VEE: The cube adapts to changing needs.

TOLL: Ro moved it.

RO: Can I use the cube as a step to reach the shelf?

TOLL: No. It is not stable enough to stand on.

VEE: We can add a warning on the box.

RO: The box says the toy is for stacking.

TOLL: Stacking toys is different from climbing toys.

VEE: We should show what it can do safely.

[RO BUILDS A LOW WALL, THEN LEAVES A GAP FOR THE MARBLE.] 

RO: The marble can pass through here and stop at the cube on the other side.

TOLL: That is a game with one cube, one marble, and room to change the path.

VEE: We could call it the All-Play Maze.

RO: It is just a wall with a gap.

VEE: The cube can also be a door.

TOLL: Only if the wall has a doorway.

[RO MOVES THE CUBE. THE MARBLE ROLLS THROUGH.] 

RO: Now it is an open door.

VEE: The toy keeps changing.

TOLL: The child keeps changing where the cube goes.

RO: I want another cube so I can make a longer wall.

VEE: That is a useful product request.

TOLL: We can make another one after checking the wood and edges.

VEE: Our ad can say: one sturdy cube, many ways to arrange it.

RO: And don't stand on it.

VEE: That can go on the package.

VEE: The All-Play Cube. One cube, one job at a time.

RO: And one marble that stays out from under the bench.
