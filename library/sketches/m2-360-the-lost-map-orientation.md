# The Lost Map Orientation

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate onboarding that gives new hires elaborate maps for a one-room workplace*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Trainer Pava, a gibbon; new hire Odo, a baboon; guide Leni, a macaque; receptionist Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. ONE-ROOM COMMUNITY OFFICE - DAY

[Pava places a folded map in Odo's paws. He stands beside the desk to which he has been assigned.]

PAVA: Welcome. This is your route to your station.

ODO: I am touching my station.

LENI: You have arrived before completing orientation.

[Odo removes his hand from the desk. Pava unfolds the map across it.]

PAVA: The blue square is reception. The red square is your station.

ODO: They are the same desk.

TAVI: We share. She has drawn my side larger.

[Leni points toward the kettle, four steps away.]

LENI: Follow route A to refreshments.

[Odo walks to the kettle. Leni hurries after him with the map.]

LENI: You crossed a departmental boundary.

ODO: I crossed the rug.

PAVA: Route A goes around the chairs, past the motivational poster and through the copier area.

[Odo returns to the desk. He follows the longer route, turning sideways between two chairs. Pava ticks a box.]

PAVA: Successful navigation.

ODO: May I make tea?

TAVI: Sign for receipt of the map first.

[Odo signs. He sets his cup on the paper while closing the pen. A damp ring forms beside reception.]

LENI: The map is contaminated.

PAVA: We will need a revised edition.

ODO: Call it a pond. The blue square already needs water.

[Leni goes to the copier. Odo follows without looking at his map.]

PAVA: You have found an unapproved shortcut.

ODO: I followed Leni.

LENI: I am approved.

[She prints six clean copies. Pava spreads them across the floor to check their orientation. Tavi turns her chair; a wheel rests on a red square.]

TAVI: Am I occupying his station?

PAVA: On that copy, yes. Move north.

[Tavi moves her chair. It rolls over another copy.]

ODO: She has entered the pond.

[Leni kneels to retrieve the pages. Odo reaches over the desk and switches off the copier before another stack emerges.]

LENI: I requested six. It says sixty.

PAVA: We must maintain enough maps for the new intake.

ODO: There is one new hire.

[Tavi collects the pages. Pava checks the exit arrow. The copies are now piled directly in front of the door.]

PAVA: Our exit has moved.

ODO: The paper has moved.

[He lifts the stack onto his desk, leaving the door clear. He takes one map, folds it into a small tent and stands it beside the kettle.]

ODO: Refreshments. I have marked the destination.

[Pava studies the tent from the doorway. Leni follows the rug, passes the poster and reaches it. Tavi reaches it in four steps.]

TAVI: My route was faster.

LENI: It omitted the poster.

[Odo places the remaining maps beneath the copier's paper tray. Pava gives him a badge marked CARTOGRAPHY SUPPORT.]

ODO: I applied for reception.

PAVA: You demonstrated a specialist skill.

[The copier beeps. Its screen reads PAPER TRAY EMPTY. Pava turns to Odo with a fresh sheet and a pencil.]

PAVA: Where is the supply tray on the map?

[Odo points to the stack directly beneath the tray. Pava draws a new boundary around it.]

PAVA: Excellent. A district for supplies.
