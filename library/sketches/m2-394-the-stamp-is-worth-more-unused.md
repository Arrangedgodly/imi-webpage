# The Stamp Is Worth More Unused

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: collectibles grading shows that inflate tiny differences into high-stakes appraisals*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Hedi, a macaque appraiser; Loro, a capuchin collector; Fena, a gibbon host; Tavi, a lemur assistant.

INT. COLLECTIBLES SHOW TABLE - AFTERNOON

[A SMALL POSTAGE STAMP RESTS IN A CLEAR SLEEVE. HEDI LEANS OVER IT WITH A MAGNIFYING GLASS.]

FENA: Loro believes this stamp may be rare.

LORO: It has a tiny green mark in the corner.

HEDI: Is the mark part of the design?

Loro says he does not know. Hedi turns the sleeve without removing the stamp. Tavi brings a catalog.

TAVI: The catalog shows this stamp without the mark.

FENA: Could it be an error?

HEDI: It could be lint.

Loro pulls the sleeve closer. Hedi stops him.

HEDI: Any movement could reduce the value.

LORO: It's in plastic.

HEDI: The plastic has a seam.

The seam catches on the table. Tavi lifts the sleeve. The stamp slides toward the edge. Everyone dives to catch it. It lands on Fena's cue cards.

FENA: The stamp is safe.

HEDI: We should not disturb it.

Tavi uses tweezers to move the stamp back into the sleeve. The tweezers leave a tiny crease in the paper behind it, not on the stamp.

LORO: Did the value change?

HEDI: The display changed.

Fena announces an appraisal. Hedi says the stamp may be worth three leaves if the mark is a printing error, or one leaf if it is dust. Loro offers to pay three leaves now. Hedi says she cannot sell it.

TAVI: It belongs to Loro.

HEDI: I meant I cannot sell the appraisal.

Loro asks if he should clean the mark. Hedi says no. Tavi says the stamp could be checked under stronger light. The show lights click off for a scheduled demonstration. A small desk lamp turns on. The green mark is revealed to be a fleck of dried paint on the sleeve.

LORO: Can we remove the sleeve?

HEDI: We can, if you accept that the stamp will remain ordinary.

Loro takes the stamp out carefully. The green fleck stays on the sleeve. Without it, the stamp matches the catalog.

FENA: Its value?

HEDI: One leaf, perhaps.

LORO: I paid two for it.

TAVI: Then you lost a leaf.

Loro presses the stamp onto a postcard and writes a note to a friend. He puts the card in the outgoing mail tray.

Hedi watches the postal clerk take the tray. She asks Loro if he kept a record of the stamp's original condition. Loro points to his receipt. It lists "one common stamp, used." He had bought it from the same shop, where Tavi's cousin sold it for a leaf.

TAVI: The receipt is an appraisal.

FENA: It is also the only document with a value.

FENA: You used the collectible.

LORO: I wanted to send something.

[HEDI PUTS THE EMPTY SLEEVE IN THE APPRAISAL FRAME. SHE SAYS IT IS AN EXCEPTIONAL EXAMPLE OF A STAMP THAT WAS NEVER THERE.]
