# The Debate Clock Has a Second Opinion

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: televised panel debates where time limits apply to answers but never to the moderator*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Moderator Nari, a lemur; panelists Vex, a gibbon, and Mara, a macaque; timekeeper Pip, a capuchin; stagehand, a macaque.

INT. TOWN HALL STUDIO - NIGHT

[Two podiums stand beneath a large countdown clock. Pip tests the buzzer. Nari adjusts her microphone. Vex and Mara each hold a card marked OPENING STATEMENT.]

NARI: You each have thirty seconds. I will ask one question, then the bell ends the answer.

VEX: What happens if the bell rings during your question?

NARI: The question is not timed.

MARA: Can my answer be one sentence?

PIP: The clock counts seconds, not sentences.

[Nari turns toward camera.]

NARI: First question. What should the town do about the park bench?

[Vex launches into his statement. The clock ticks.]

VEX: The bench is central to our civic identity, a place where generations-

[The buzzer sounds. Pip lifts one finger.]

VEX: I have not reached the bench.

PIP: Your time reached it.

[Mara steps to the microphone.]

MARA: Move the bench two feet left so people can pass.

[The clock stops at twenty-seven seconds.]

MARA: Did I go too long?

PIP: No. It started late.

[Nari smiles to camera.]

NARI: A sharp contrast in style.

MARA: You asked about a bench.

NARI: Follow-up. How will you pay for it?

[Vex raises his card. It is a detailed map of the park. Mara points at the small bench sketch.]

VEX: There are several funding streams.

[The timer sounds at thirty. Nari continues talking over it.]

NARI: We will now hear why the bench matters to our shared future, and then-

[Pip resets the clock to zero. The buzzer sounds again.]

PIP: That was your thirty seconds.

NARI: I was introducing the next question.

MARA: Your question has its own podium.

[Vex holds his card over the clock. The digits vanish.]

VEX: We should pause the debate until the clock is repaired.

PIP: The clock is working. I can see the reflection in your card.

[He pulls the card away. It has absorbed the timer display's red glow and now reads 00:30 across Vex's proposal.]

NARI: Can the panel agree on anything?

MARA: Yes. Move the bench.

VEX: I agree to a subcommittee to study the two-foot relocation.

[The buzzer rings while Vex is speaking. Pip looks at the clock. It is unplugged.]

PIP: I did not press it.

[The buzzer is tied to the same extension cord as the studio lights. A stagehand pulls the cord to clear it from the walkway. The lights go dark.]

NARI: We are still on air.

MARA: The bench is outside.

[They all turn toward the dark studio door. Vex picks up his map and starts walking, still talking. The others follow him out.]

FINAL BEAT: Pip checks the stopwatch. All four have left the studio in under thirty seconds. Nari's mic remains on the empty podium, asking, "And why is the bench important?"
