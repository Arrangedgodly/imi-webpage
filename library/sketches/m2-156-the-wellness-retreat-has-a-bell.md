# The Wellness Retreat Has a Bell

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate wellness retreats and mandatory relaxation for a troop that would rather move naturally*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Retreat leader Sela, a macaque; participant Nori, a baboon; facilitator Tavi, a gibbon; and assistant Piri, a capuchin.

[A CIRCLE OF CUSHIONS SURROUNDS A SMALL BELL.]

SELA: We will practice stillness. When the bell rings, breathe in.

[TAVI RINGS THE BELL.]

NORI: I was already breathing.

SELA: Notice the breath.

[NORI SNIFFS THE AIR.]

TAVI: Is that part of the exercise?

NORI: There is fruit nearby.

[PIRI BRINGS A BOWL OF FIGS. SELA MOVES IT OUT OF THE CIRCLE.]

SELA: We will eat after the session.

NORI: I am calm when I eat.

SELA: First, we sit quietly for ten minutes.

[THE BELL RINGS. A MONKEY STARTLES AND CLIMBS A TREE.]

TAVI: The sound was abrupt.

SELA: We can ring it more gently.

[SELA TAPS THE BELL SOFTLY. IT DOES NOT RING.]

PIRI: It needs a stronger tap.

[NORI TAKES THE BELL AND HITS IT WITH A STICK. THE SOUND IS LOUD.]

SELA: That is too strong.

NORI: I have found the middle.

[TAVI LIES ON A CUSHION. Nori sits on the cushion's other end.]

TAVI: I am trying to relax.

NORI: You are in my spot.

[THEY BOTH MOVE. THE CUSHIONS SHIFT INTO A LINE.]

SELA: The circle has become a path.

PIRI: The troop is walking on it.

[THE MONKEYS WALK AROUND THE CUSHIONS, GROOMING EACH OTHER AS THEY GO.]

TAVI: They seem relaxed.

SELA: They are moving.

NORI: I am moving too.

[THE FACILITATOR RINGS THE BELL. NO ONE STOPS.]

SELA: We have not followed the cue.

PIRI: The cue is not helping.

[SELA PUTS THE BELL BESIDE THE FRUIT BOWL.]

SELA: We can eat and listen to the birds.

NORI: That is a retreat.

[THE MONKEY TAKES THE BELL AND USES IT AS A BOWL.]

TAVI: The bell has found a quieter purpose.

[SELA TAKES A BREATH AND SITS ON A CUSHION.]

TAVI: Are you practicing stillness?

SELA: I am deciding whether to keep the bell.

NORI: It can be a fruit bowl.

[PIRI PLACES THREE FIGS INSIDE IT. THE MONKEY TAKES ONE.]

SELA: That is a useful use.

TAVI: We could have offered a quiet session without a bell.

NORI: Or a moving session without a timer.

[THE RETREAT LEADER PUTS THE TIMER AWAY.]

[SELA TAKES A BREATH AND SITS ON A CUSHION.]

TAVI: Are you practicing stillness?

SELA: I am deciding whether to keep the bell.

NORI: It can be a fruit bowl.

[PIRI PLACES THREE FIGS INSIDE IT. THE MONKEY TAKES ONE.]

SELA: That is a useful use.

TAVI: We could have offered a quiet session without a bell.

NORI: Or a moving session without a timer.

[THE RETREAT LEADER PUTS THE TIMER AWAY.]

[THE GROUP SHARES FIGS. THE BELL IS NEVER RUNG AGAIN.]
