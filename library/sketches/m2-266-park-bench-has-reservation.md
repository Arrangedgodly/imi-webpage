# The Park Bench Has a Reservation

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: restaurant-style booking systems applied to public spaces that anyone can use*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Park attendant Pava, a baboon; walker Tavi, a gibbon; gardener Miri, a capuchin; and jogger Ralo, a macaque.

EXT. CITY PARK - MORNING

A bench sits beneath a tree. Pava stands beside it with a tablet. Tavi approaches with a book.

PAVA: Do you have a reservation?

TAVI: For the bench?

PAVA: The booking system keeps the benches available.

TAVI: It looks available.

[Pava scans the bench's small QR code. The tablet displays RESERVED, 9:00 TO 9:15.]

PAVA: Someone has booked this one.

TAVI: There is nobody here.

PAVA: They may be on their way.

[Ralo jogs up, holding a phone.]

RALO: I booked the bench beside the fountain.

PAVA: That bench is occupied.

RALO: My app says I have priority.

MIRI: The fountain bench is a planter now.

[Ralo points to a bench-shaped planter. A shrub fills the seat.]

EXT. PARK - MOMENTS LATER

Pava consults the tablet. Tavi reads. Ralo tries to stretch beside the bench without touching it.

TAVI: Could we remove the reservation system?

PAVA: Then people might sit wherever they like.

MIRI: It is a park.

PAVA: The system allocates time fairly.

[The tablet chirps. It shows Tavi's reservation on a bench two blocks away.]

TAVI: I did not book anything.

PAVA: The app may have booked for you based on your walking pattern.

RALO: It knows where I run?

MIRI: It knows you run around the same path.

[Ralo jogs away. The tablet updates his location and reserves each bench along the route.]

PAVA: You have booked the next six benches.

RALO: I cannot sit while running.

TAVI: The benches are moving faster than the people.

EXT. PARK - AFTER THE APP UPDATE

Pava refreshes the tablet. It now requires a table number for each bench. Ralo tries to sit on a low stone wall.

PAVA: That is not a registered seat.

RALO: It is stone.

PAVA: The app calls it a landscape feature.

MIRI: It was a seat before the update.

[The tablet flashes a warning: UNAUTHORIZED SITTING. Pava taps dismiss, but the warning returns with a photo of Ralo on the wall.]

RALO: It has taken my picture.

TAVI: It has booked the wall too.

EXT. PARK - LATER

Miri removes the QR labels from two benches. Pava objects as Tavi sits down with his book.

PAVA: We need an open slot.

TAVI: This is the slot.

MIRI: The bench is public. People can take turns by noticing one another.

[Ralo returns at a walk. He sees Tavi reading and waits nearby.]

RALO: Is there room when you finish?

TAVI: Yes.

[Pava checks the tablet. It displays a waitlist of forty-seven names for the bench they are sharing.]

PAVA: The app says the wait is three hours.

MIRI: The real wait is one chapter.

[Tavi closes the book. Ralo sits. Pava turns the tablet face down and takes the other end.]

PAVA: Do I need to reserve this seat?

TAVI: You are already sitting in it.

[The tablet buzzes. Pava lets it slide under the bench.]
