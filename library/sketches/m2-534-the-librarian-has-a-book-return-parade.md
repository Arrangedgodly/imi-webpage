# The Librarian Has a Book Return Parade

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: ceremonial parades and library due-date notices treated with far too much pageantry*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: LIBRARIAN, an orangutan; PARADE MARSHAL, a baboon; STUDENT, a macaque; DRUMMER, a gibbon; VOLUNTEER, a capuchin.

[A library entrance. A return slot is decorated with ribbons. The marshal carries a small baton. The drummer waits with a snare. The librarian holds a stack of books.]

LIBRARIAN: We are marking the end of overdue week.

STUDENT: I returned my book yesterday.

PARADE MARSHAL: Then you are eligible to lead.

STUDENT: I do not know how to march.

DRUMMER: Neither do the books.

[The volunteer places a cart of returned books behind the student. The marshal raises the baton. The drummer plays a roll. The books rattle against one another.]

ACT ONE

[The parade starts down the short sidewalk. The cart wheels squeak on every beat. The student tries to walk in step, but one shoe has a loose sole and claps twice per pace.]

PARADE MARSHAL: Keep the formation.

LIBRARIAN: The books are sliding.

[The volunteer wedges a dictionary between two book stacks. It falls open and catches a streamer.]

VOLUNTEER: The dictionary has snagged the parade.

[The drummer switches to a quiet beat. The librarian hands each marcher a book to carry. The student gets a picture book with a paper crown taped to the cover.]

STUDENT: Is this my prize?

LIBRARIAN: It is a library book. Please do not wear it.

[The marshal leads the parade toward the return slot, but the cart is too wide to pass through the decorated arch. The volunteers remove the ribbons. A second banner reads RETURN YOUR BOOKS ON TIME, but the wind wraps it around the marshal's baton.]

PARADE MARSHAL: I cannot direct anyone.

DRUMMER: I can keep the beat.

[The drummer taps a single slow beat. The student follows it, and the cart rolls smoothly. The librarian opens the slot and feeds in the overdue books one at a time. The audience applauds each return as if it were a finish.]

LIBRARIAN: That one was twelve days late.

[The applause gets louder.]

STUDENT: Should we clap for the book?

VOLUNTEER: It has paid its fine.

[The last book in the cart is a cookbook with a receipt sticking out. The librarian pulls the receipt and sees the book was checked out by the parade marshal.]

PARADE MARSHAL: I was practicing the route.

ACT TWO

[The marshal places the cookbook in the slot. The library computer beeps and prints a clean receipt. The librarian gives the student a bookmark instead of the paper-crown book. The drummer rolls the cart back to storage at an ordinary pace.]

PARADE MARSHAL: Formation dismissed.

[The student steps aside to let a reader enter. The reader returns one book without ceremony.]

FINAL IMAGE: The librarian pins the printed receipt to the parade banner, which now reads ALL BOOKS HOME.
