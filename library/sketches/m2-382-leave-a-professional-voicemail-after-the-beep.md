# Leave a Professional Voicemail After the Beep

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: workplace communication coaching that polishes a simple message until nobody can understand it*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Hara, a lemur coach; Veli, a gibbon employee; Puck, a capuchin colleague; Nema, a macaque receptionist.

INT. OFFICE TRAINING ROOM - MORNING

[A PHONE SITS ON A TABLE. A POSTER SAYS YOUR VOICE REPRESENTS THE WHOLE ORGANIZATION. HARA POINTS TO A SAMPLE GREETING.]

HARA: Leave a professional voicemail for your colleague.

VELI: Hi, Puck. The ladder is by the west door.

HARA: That's clear, but abrupt.

PUCK: I can find the ladder.

HARA: First, state your full name, department, and callback number.

Veli starts again. The phone beeps before he reaches the ladder.

HARA: You used the full message window.

NEMA: The system allows twelve seconds.

Veli tries speaking faster. The phone records his greeting as one long word. Nema plays it back. Puck cannot tell where the department ends and the phone number begins.

PUCK: Is that the ladder code?

HARA: We need a pause between sections.

Veli records again. He says his name, pauses, says the department, pauses, then says the callback number. The pauses trigger the voicemail system to beep between phrases.

NEMA: The system thinks you are finished.

Hara gives him a script with "At your earliest convenience" and "Please be advised" inserted before the ladder location.

VELI: I just need Puck to move it.

HARA: The message should feel considerate.

Veli records. The greeting takes so long that Puck leaves the room to get the ladder himself. Veli finishes speaking to an empty chair.

Nema shows Veli the playback queue. His first greeting is still waiting to be reviewed. The new recording cannot be saved until the old one is deleted. Veli presses delete. The phone asks him to record a reason for deleting the greeting. He says, "It was too long." The phone saves that sentence as the start of his greeting.

HARA: That's honest.

VELI: Can I leave the ladder part now?

PUCK: I found it.

HARA: Did you receive the message?

PUCK: I heard it from the next room.

Nema says the office has a "message confidence" option. She turns on the speaker. The phone now plays a cheerful tone after every sentence.

VELI: The ladder is by the west door.

[CHEERFUL TONE.]

PUCK: I know.

[CHEERFUL TONE.]

HARA: The tone makes the message warmer.

Puck moves the ladder to the east door. Veli tries to correct him over the phone, but the system asks him to rate the call.

NEMA: Press one for helpful, two for pleasant.

VELI: I need to say east door.

The system records "I need to say east door" as his new greeting. Hara listens to the playback.

HARA: It is direct.

PUCK: Now I know what to do.

Nema deletes the elaborate greeting and saves Veli's plain message. Hara tries to make one last suggestion, but the call disconnects. Puck carries the ladder through the training room to the east side.

[THE PHONE BEEPS. ITS AUTO-GREETING PLAYS: "HI, PUCK. THE LADDER IS BY THE WEST DOOR." PUCK TURNS AROUND AND CARRIES IT BACK.]
