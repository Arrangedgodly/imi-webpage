# The Talent Scout Arrives at Nap Time

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: reality talent scouting and pressure to turn ordinary behavior into a breakthrough act*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: SCOUT REX, a gibbon; MANAGER PALA, a macaque; PERFORMER SEDGE, a capuchin; and SLEEPING MONKEYS.

[REX enters a quiet nursery with a clipboard and a camera.] 

REX: I am looking for raw talent. No coaching, no rehearsals, just natural ability.

PALA: The troop is resting.

REX: Rest can be a performance.

PALA: It is nap time.

REX: Sedge, show me what makes you special.

SEDGE: I can peel a banana without waking anyone.

REX: A silent peel. That's marketable.

[SEDGE PEELS A BANANA. THE SKIN MAKES A FAINT RUSTLE.] 

REX: Can you do it slower?

SEDGE: It is already slow.

REX: Add suspense.

PALA: If you add suspense, the peel will take longer.

REX: Exactly. We build anticipation.

SEDGE: I was hungry.

REX: Could you peel the banana over your head?

SEDGE: Then the fruit might fall on someone.

PALA: The sleeping monkeys did not sign a release.

REX: The camera can focus on Sedge.

PALA: The camera light is on their faces.

[REX COVERS THE LIGHT WITH A CLIPBOARD. IT DROPS ON THE FLOOR.] 

REX: That was a strong sound cue.

SEDGE: It woke the troop.

[THE MONKEYS SHIFT AND MURMUR.] 

PALA: We need to stop filming.

REX: A spontaneous ensemble reaction!

SEDGE: They're trying to go back to sleep.

REX: Can you ask them to clap?

PALA: No.

SEDGE: I can take my banana outside.

[SEDGE STEPS INTO THE HALL, EATS, AND RETURNS WITH THE PEEL.] 

REX: The act has a beginning, middle, and end.

PALA: It has a peel that needs a bin.

SEDGE: I can put it in compost.

REX: That is a second talent.

PALA: It is ordinary cleanup.

REX: The show is called Hidden Stars.

PALA: Then keep looking. You might find one after the camera is off.

REX: This could be a series. Each week, Sedge performs a different silent task.

PALA: Sedge did not agree to be on a series.

REX: I have not asked yet.

PALA: You are standing in the nursery with a camera.

REX: The first clip will show the banana peel.

SEDGE: I can decide after I hear the proposal.

REX: We film you eating breakfast, moving objects quietly, and settling in for a nap.

PALA: That's a schedule of Sedge's day, not a talent show.

SEDGE: I don't want to be filmed while I sleep.

REX: We can point the camera away during the nap.

PALA: Then what is the show?

REX: The anticipation of the nap.

SEDGE: I would rather eat my banana and go back to rest.

PALA: The answer is no.

REX: Could we film the compost bin instead?

SEDGE: You can film the peel going in. Do not film the sleeping troop.

[REX TURNS THE CAMERA TOWARD THE BIN. SEDGE DROPS THE PEEL INTO COMPOST.] 

REX: That is a clean ending.

PALA: It is a banana peel in a bin.

REX: It has a beginning, middle, and end.

SEDGE: I have finished my snack.

PALA: And now the nursery is quiet again.

[REX PACKS THE CAMERA. THE TROOP SETTLES.]

[REX TURNS THE CAMERA OFF. THE TROOP SETTLES BACK DOWN.] 

REX: I heard someone snore in harmony.

PALA: Don't wake them.
