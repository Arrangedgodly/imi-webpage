# The Perfectly Ordinary Parade

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: municipal parades that add ceremonial roles and choreography to a simple neighborhood walk*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Tavi, a macaque parade marshal; Luma, a gibbon banner carrier; Piri, a capuchin drummer; and Odo, a baboon mayor.

EXT. MAIN STREET - DAY

Tavi stands before a line of residents carrying a banner that reads WALK TO THE GARDEN. Odo checks a program listing grand marshal, ribbon bearer, honorary ribbon bearer, and ribbon observer.

TAVI: We are walking to the garden.

ODO: In procession.

Piri taps a drum. The line moves three steps and stops so the mayor can wave. Luma asks who carries the banner. Odo says she does. He then asks who carries the ceremonial ribbon. Luma hands it to Tavi.

TAVI: Why do we need a ribbon?

ODO: To mark the parade's beginning.

PIRI: We are already walking.

Odo asks Piri to play the opening drumroll. Piri rolls the drum down the street by accident. It bumps a curb and stops at the garden gate. Everyone follows it. Tavi calls this an efficient route. Odo says they have skipped the grandstand.

The mayor's chair is set up beside the road. No one is sitting there. Odo asks the parade to pause for the official viewing. Residents stand facing the empty chair. Luma adjusts the banner so it can be seen from the seat.

LUMA: The chair has a good view.

TAVI: The chair is not attending.

Odo takes the seat. The parade moves again. The chair scrapes along with him, attached to the bottom of his robe. Piri plays a slow march to match the chair's pace. Tavi stops and frees the chair. Odo stands, and the parade starts walking too fast.

TAVI: The chair is not in the parade.

ODO: It has been promoted to float.

At the garden, Piri starts the closing drumroll. The residents stop. Odo looks at the program and says the ribbon must be cut. Tavi holds the ribbon across the entrance. Odo takes the scissors. Luma points out the garden gate is already open. The ribbon falls from her hands and lands around the mayor's waist.

PIRI: The parade has arrived.

ODO: The parade has opened the garden.

Tavi unrolls the banner, which now reads "GARDEN WALK COMPLETE." Odo declares a brief ceremony. Everyone looks at the empty chair, which is still by the road.

LUMA: We can carry it back.

The residents carry the chair together. Piri taps the drum once. Odo begins waving from the seat as they lift it.

TAVI: The parade has a passenger now.

Seli retrieves the ribbon pieces and ties them to the chair legs so they do not blow across the garden. Piri places the drum back in its case. The residents continue planting while the mayor practices waving from a chair that is no longer moving.

ODO: An honored passenger.

They set the chair by the garden gate and begin their ordinary work. The banner is left hanging over the chair, advertising the next parade: a walk back home.
