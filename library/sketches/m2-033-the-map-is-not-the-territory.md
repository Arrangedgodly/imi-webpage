# The Map Is Not the Territory

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Survival challenge shows, dramatic maps, and wilderness narration*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Field host Garo, a baboon; contestant Sela, a gibbon; contestant Moti, a macaque; guide Ivi, a capuchin; camera operator Rell, a chimpanzee.

[STAGE: A FOREST CLEARING WITH A LARGE MAP, TWO BACKPACKS, AND A CLEAR PATH MARKED START TO FINISH.]

GARO: Welcome to The Last Route. Two contestants, one forest, and a map printed upside down.
SELA: Is the map upside down?
IVI: It says the waterfall is above us.
MOTI: Water does go down.
GARO: The challenge is to reach the finish without help.
IVI: I am the guide.
GARO: You may help by being present.
RELL: Camera ready. Add tension.
[RELL RUSTLES A LEAF BESIDE THE MICROPHONE.]
GARO: We hear the forest stirring.
SELA: That was the camera.
MOTI: The camera is part of the forest now.
GARO: Contestants, choose a route.
SELA: I will take the line marked river.
MOTI: It runs through a picture of a mountain.
SELA: The map says river.
IVI: That is a blue crayon mark from the previous challenge.
GARO: We cannot reveal that yet.
MOTI: I will follow the dotted line.
IVI: That is the trail we are standing on.
MOTI: I have found it.
GARO: A strong early breakthrough.
SELA: The river route ends at the snack tent.
GARO: A hidden water source.
IVI: It is a pitcher.
SELA: It is full of water.
MOTI: The map was right about one thing.
GARO: Contestants may take one survival item.
SELA: I choose the compass.
IVI: It points at the camera.
RELL: I am holding a snack.
MOTI: I choose the rope.
IVI: It is tied to the tent.
MOTI: Then we can bring the tent.
GARO: A clever strategy.
SELA: The finish is just past that stump.
GARO: We should not tell the contestants.
MOTI: I can see the finish flag.
GARO: That is a very similar flag.
IVI: It is attached to the same stump.
SELA: We have reached the goal.
GARO: There is still the final test.
MOTI: What test?
GARO: Read the map correctly.
IVI: Turn it around.
[SELA AND MOTI TURN THE MAP. THE FINISH IS MARKED WHERE THEY STAND.]
SELA: We finished before the challenge began.
GARO: You finished emotionally.
MOTI: Is there a prize?
GARO: The prize is a real map.
IVI: That one is real.
GARO: It is a map of the parking area.
SELA: We walked here.
MOTI: We can walk back.
RELL: Should I film a dramatic departure?
GARO: Yes. Make it feel like a long journey.
[RELL ZOOMS IN ON THE STUMP.]
SELA: We are still here.
GARO: The journey is internal.
MOTI: My stomach is external.
IVI: The snack tent is behind you.
GARO: Do not reveal the water source.
SELA: We already drank it.
GARO: Then the challenge is complete.
MOTI: Finally, an ending we can locate.

[THE CONTESTANTS WALK TO THE SNACK TENT WHILE GARO HOLDS THE UPSIDE-DOWN MAP TO CAMERA.]
