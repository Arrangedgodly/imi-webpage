# The Talent Scout Is Lost

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: talent scouting shows and exaggerated discovery moments when a monkey simply wants directions*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Scout Luma, a lemur; performer Kito, a macaque; camera operator Fenn, a gibbon; and passerby Nemi, a capuchin.

[A TALENT SCOUT STANDS IN A CLEARING WITH A CAMERA CREW.]

LUMA: We are searching for raw talent.

[KITO WALKS INTO FRAME.]

LUMA: What is your special skill?

KITO: Finding the path to the water bowl.

LUMA: Can you demonstrate?

[KITO LOOKS AROUND, THEN POINTS LEFT.]

FENN: What did you see?

KITO: The bowl.

[THE CREW FOLLOWS. THEY ARRIVE AT A SMALL WATER BOWL.]

LUMA: An instinctive sense of direction.

KITO: I have been here before.

LUMA: Can you make it more dramatic?

[KITO WALKS BACK TO THE CLEARING AND POINTS LEFT AGAIN.]

LUMA: Incredible consistency.

[NEMI APPROACHES.]

NEMI: Excuse me, where is the orchard?

LUMA: We are filming.

KITO: Follow me.

[THEY WALK TO A FORK. KITO PAUSES.]

LUMA: The suspense builds.

KITO: I am checking the smell.

[FENN SNIFFS.]

FENN: Figs to the right.

LUMA: This is a second talent.

NEMI: It is the orchard.

[KITO LEADS THEM TO A TREE. LUMA HANDS HIM A CONTRACT.]

LUMA: We can represent you.

KITO: Do you know where the water bowl is?

LUMA: It is in the clearing.

KITO: Then I do not need a representative.

[THE CAMERA CREW TRIES TO FOLLOW KITO BACK. THEY TAKE THE WRONG TURN.]

FENN: We are lost.

LUMA: Kito, can you lead the crew home?

KITO: That is the same skill.

[HE LEADS THEM TO THE CLEARING. LUMA OPENS THE CONTRACT.]

LUMA: We should add travel consulting.

KITO: I want a drink.

[THE CREW REACHES THE BOWL. KITO DRINKS. THE CAMERA KEEPS FILMING.]

LUMA: A powerful ending.

KITO: It is water.


[LUMA SETS UP A MOCK AUDITION IN THE CLEARING.]

LUMA: Show us the moment you find the water bowl.

KITO: I can simply walk there.

LUMA: Can you add a pause before you point?

[KITO PAUSES TO SNIFF THE AIR.]

FENN: That is a natural pause.

LUMA: We need a close-up.

[KITO POINTS. THE CAMERA ZOOMS IN ON HIS FINGER, WHICH POINTS BEHIND IT.]

LUMA: We have lost the location.

KITO: The bowl is still there.

[NEMI RETURNS WITH TWO MORE LOST VISITORS.]

NEMI: They need the orchard.

KITO: Follow the fig smell.

[LUMA STARTS A SECOND CAMERA. THE CREW FOLLOWS THE VISITORS INSTEAD OF KITO AND GETS LOST AGAIN.]

FENN: We need a guide.

KITO: I need a drink.

[THE VISITORS FIND THE ORCHARD. KITO POINTS THEM BACK TO THE BOWL.]

LUMA: We have located both destinations.

KITO: I have found my drink.

[FENN TURNS OFF BOTH CAMERAS.]
LUMA: The crew needs a map.


[THE VISITORS FOLLOW THE CREW BACK TO THE BOWL.]


[FENN TURNS OFF THE CAMERA. THE SCREEN READS "TALENT FOUND: CREW."]
