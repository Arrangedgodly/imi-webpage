# One Minute of Fame

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: celebrity red-carpet interviews and publicity cycles*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: GLINT, a macaque entertainment host; KIKO, a climbing film star; PEA, a marmoset reporter; and a stagehand.

[A red carpet has been laid over a sloping patch of ground. KIKO climbs the backdrop pole to avoid the crowd.]

GLINT: Kiko, what are you wearing tonight?

KIKO: A jacket. It has a pocket for fruit and a pocket that is stitched shut so I can't eat the prop.

PEA: Who designed the jacket?

KIKO: The tailor. I asked for a strap that would not catch on branches.

GLINT: How does it feel to be nominated for the film that changed cinema?

KIKO: The camera was lower than usual.

PEA: Did you change your acting style?

KIKO: I stopped standing in front of the camera.

[The stagehand tugs the carpet flat. It slides downhill like a red river.]

GLINT: A daring visual choice.

KIKO: It's leaving.

[The carpet pulls a camera tripod with it. Kiko swings down, catches the tripod, and hooks the carpet to the pole with his tail.]

PEA: You've just saved the broadcast.

GLINT: We need a quote about the moment.

KIKO: I caught the tripod.

GLINT: Can you say it felt like a metaphor?

KIKO: It felt like a tripod.

[Flashbulbs pop. The crowd calls questions over one another.]

PEA: Your film is about friendship. Are you and your co-star friends off camera?

KIKO: We share fruit when there is enough.

GLINT: That is a beautiful answer. We can use it in the profile.

KIKO: She said I can have the last mango if I get the camera cable out of the way.

[He unhooks the cable from the carpet. The stagehand raises a hand.]

STAGEHAND: Please don't swing from the lighting rig.

KIKO: I won't. The cable was on the floor.

PEA: Your fans want to know if you have a message for young actors.

KIKO: Learn where the exits are. The carpet is not attached to the ground.

[The wind lifts the carpet again. Glint grabs one end; Pea grabs the other.]

GLINT: Kiko, could you give us a final quote while we hold this?

KIKO: The interview is over.

[He steps off the carpet, helps tie it to the pole, then leaves. The interview clock rings.]

PEA: That was exactly one minute.

GLINT: We have a full evening of coverage and no one has said "iconic" yet.

[The carpet stays tied. The stagehand hands Glint a card.]

STAGEHAND: The network wants a quote for the morning recap.

GLINT: "Kiko saves the premiere."

KIKO: I saved the tripod.

PEA: "Kiko Saves a Tripod" sounds like a different movie.

GLINT: Can we say the carpet was an adversary?

KIKO: It was cloth on a hill.

[The camera pushes in on Glint, who is holding the carpet down with both feet.]

PEA: Your jacket is caught under the carpet.

GLINT: Is this live?

KIKO: It is now.

[The stagehand frees the jacket. Glint checks the clock.]

GLINT: We have used our whole minute.

PEA: We asked one question.

KIKO: You asked it six ways.

[BLACKOUT.]
