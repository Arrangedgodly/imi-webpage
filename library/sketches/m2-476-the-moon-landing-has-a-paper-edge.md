# The Moon Landing Has a Paper Edge

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: prestige historical reenactments, documentary retakes, and productions that mistake a visible set seam for a plot twist*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: DIRECTOR, a gibbon; ASTRONAUT, a macaque; HISTORIAN, an orangutan; CAMERA OPERATOR, a capuchin; SET BUILDER, a baboon.

[A studio moon set. The astronaut stands beside a paper-mache crater. A bright seam shows along the moon backdrop.] 

DIRECTOR: Hold the heroic pose. Camera ready?

CAMERA OPERATOR: Ready. The seam is in frame.

DIRECTOR: The seam is part of the frontier.

HISTORIAN: The frontier is a set wall.

[The set builder presses the loose seam flat with a broad brush. It springs up again. The director asks for a solemn close-up.] 

ASTRONAUT: I have found a ridge.

HISTORIAN: You have found tape.

[The camera operator shifts the lens to hide the edge. The director watches the monitor.] 

DIRECTOR: Now the landing feels authentic.

CAMERA OPERATOR: The lunar horizon is also cropped out.

DIRECTOR: Add the horizon back.

[The camera operator widens the shot. The seam reappears. The set builder places a gray rock prop in front of it; the rock is labeled "EARTH." ]

HISTORIAN: That is the Earth prop.

SET BUILDER: It covers the seam.

ASTRONAUT: I can plant the flag near it.

DIRECTOR: No flag until the establishing shot is locked.

[The astronaut steps onto a foam footprint. It compresses too far and swallows one boot. The set builder offers a flat board beneath the surface, but the director says the footprint must remain visible.] 

ASTRONAUT: The moon is soft today.

HISTORIAN: The foam is soft every day.

[They replace the footprint with a shallow molded mark. The astronaut tests it gently; the boot stays above the surface. The set builder tapes the backdrop seam from behind, out of the camera frame.] 

CAMERA OPERATOR: The seam is hidden, and the horizon is back.

DIRECTOR: Roll the scene.

ASTRONAUT: One small step onto a very well-secured stage.

DIRECTOR: Cut. Too much honesty.

HISTORIAN: It is the first accurate line today.

[The director keeps the take and asks the astronaut to plant the flag beside the molded footprint. The camera holds the repaired horizon.] 

[The historian reviews the take on the monitor. The camera operator points out that the astronaut's shadow falls toward the studio door.]

HISTORIAN: The key light is on the wrong side.

DIRECTOR: Move it without crossing the set seam.

[The set builder wheels the light around the outside of the backdrop and stops at its taped mark. The astronaut returns to the molded footprint. The camera operator checks the frame: horizon, flag, no seam, clear shadow.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: The shadow now points away from the light.

ASTRONAUT: Can we keep the take?

HISTORIAN: Yes. The set is still a studio, but the scene holds together.

[The director calls action. The astronaut plants the flag. The set builder keeps one hand on the secured backdrop from behind, outside the shot.]

FINAL IMAGE: The flag stands on the foam moon while the crew watches the monitor, where no paper edge is visible.
