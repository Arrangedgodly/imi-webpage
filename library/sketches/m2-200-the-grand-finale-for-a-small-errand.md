# The Grand Finale for a Small Errand

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: variety-show finales that save every surprise for a low-stakes task and accidentally reveal the crew's ordinary work*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Nara, a gibbon host; Tavi, a macaque assistant; Piri, a capuchin magician; and Odo, a baboon stage manager.

INT. SMALL STUDIO - NIGHT

Nara stands beneath a glittering curtain. Odo whispers that the finale must top the entire show. Piri holds a covered tray. Tavi carries a note from the control room.

NARA: Tonight, one mystery remains.

She opens the note. It says "Please return the spare microphone to the shelf." Odo says this is a good final challenge. Piri suggests a magic reveal. He covers the tray and pulls away the cloth. It reveals a microphone.

TAVI: That is the one we need to return.

Nara announces that the microphone has been missing for three acts. Odo says it was on the shelf backstage. Piri proposes a dramatic search. They look behind the curtain, under the table and inside the prop box. Each place contains another microphone.

NARA: We have discovered a surplus.

Odo counts them. There are four microphones. The note asks for one. Tavi says to take one to the shelf. Piri suggests a grand procession. The crew forms a line. Nara carries the microphone at the front; Odo follows with a spotlight; Piri plays a drumroll. Tavi walks beside them, holding the note.

They reach the shelf. It is already full. Odo moves a small trophy to make space. The trophy falls over and reveals a fifth microphone behind it.

PIRI: The plot thickens.

TAVI: The shelf is crowded.

Nara asks the audience to choose which microphone belongs on the shelf. They point to the one she is holding. She places it in the empty space. It fits. Odo turns off the spotlight. The drumroll stops. The errand is complete.

The crew returns to center stage for the finale bow. Nara bows. Piri bows. Odo bows. Tavi remains by the shelf, reading the rest of the note: "Also lower the curtain after the show." He pulls the cord. The curtain descends halfway, then stops on a hook.

NARA: The final obstacle.

Tavi frees the hook. The curtain drops. It covers the stage lights but leaves the crew visible below the waist. Odo says the show is over. Piri's hat rolls beneath the curtain. He reaches for it and disappears. The hat pops out the other side.

Nara announces the last reveal. Tavi reaches behind the curtain and retrieves Piri. They bow together. The audience applauds. Odo turns off the house lights, but the stage lights remain on because they are plugged into a separate switch.

Tavi finds the switch. The lights go dark. In the darkness, Nara says the microphone is back on the shelf. Its red power light glows, illuminating a label: "RETURN SWITCHED OFF."

NARA: Is it off?

TAVI: Not this one.

The camera zooms out. Odo walks over and unplugs the spare microphone. The red light goes out.
