# The Award for Best Backup Plan

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: awards ceremonies that recognize preparation more than the event itself*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: Host Odo, a baboon; planner Piri, a gibbon; presenter Nara, a macaque; stagehand Tavi, a capuchin.

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NIGHT

[Odo stands at a working microphone. Nara holds a trophy engraved TO THE PLAN THAT SAVED THE EVENING. Tavi carries envelopes marked PLAN A and PLAN B.]

ODO: The award for best backup plan goes to the backup plan.

PIRI: Which one? I wrote eight.

NARA: We have only one trophy.

TAVI: I prepared an alternate trophy. It is in the van.

[Piri reaches for PLAN A. Odo takes PLAN B. They stop with crossed arms.]

PIRI: A says use the handheld microphone if this microphone fails.

ODO: B says speak loudly if the handheld fails.

NARA: Both microphones work.

ODO: Then how do we decide which plan saved the evening?

[A drop falls from Piri's water glass. Tavi points at it with both envelopes.]

TAVI: Liquid event, stage left!

[Piri wipes it with her sleeve before Nara can move the trophy.]

ODO: Successful activation of the cleanup response.

PIRI: I had an arm.

TAVI: The sleeve is covered in appendix four.

[Nara places the trophy on a small table. Its short leg makes it tilt. Tavi starts unfolding a seven-page checklist.]

NARA: The table needs a shim.

PIRI: This program is thick enough.

[She folds it beneath the short leg. The trophy steadies. Odo searches for his next cue.]

ODO: My script has become structural support.

TAVI: I have a duplicate.

[She hands him another program. Piri reaches for the trophy. Tavi produces a sheet marked EMERGENCY ACCEPTANCE SPEECH.]

PIRI: I can thank everyone myself.

TAVI: This version covers loss of voice, loss of notes and unexpected sincerity.

[Odo taps the microphone. Tavi unplugs it. His next word is inaudible. He looks at her.]

TAVI: A documented failure makes the award defensible.

[Piri takes the handheld microphone and switches it on. It squeals because she is standing in front of the speaker. Nara guides her one step sideways. The squeal stops.]

PIRI: Thank you. I would like to recognize Nara's one-step solution.

ODO: That solution is not in the envelope.

[Tavi opens PLAN B and holds up a handbell.]

TAVI: We anticipated this. The bell communicates acceptance without electronic equipment.

NARA: But the handheld works now.

[Odo takes the bell. Its clapper falls onto the table. They all look at the two separate pieces.]

ODO: Is there a plan for the bell?

[Tavi reaches for the program under the table. Piri stops her hand.]

PIRI: That is currently preventing the trophy from winning the floor.

[Nara lifts the trophy, leaving Piri free to retrieve the program. She opens it to a page titled WHEN EVERY PLAN FAILS.]

NARA: It says take a short intermission.

[Odo announces the intermission through the working handheld microphone. The audience applauds and heads toward refreshments.]

PIRI: The plan has saved the evening.

[She takes the trophy from Nara and follows the audience. Tavi offers Odo the emergency speech.]

ODO: Keep it. We may need an emergency ending.

[Tavi files it in the envelope marked PLAN B. The loose bell clapper drops into the same envelope with a tiny victorious ding.]
