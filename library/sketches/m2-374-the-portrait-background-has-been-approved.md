# The Portrait Background Has Been Approved

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate portrait sessions where the backdrop and brand rules matter more than the person being photographed*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Photographer Yana, a gibbon; Poro, a lemur employee; Brand lead Kett, a macaque; Assistant Miri, a capuchin.

INT. OFFICE PHOTO STUDIO - DAY

[A BLUE BACKDROP STANDS BEHIND A STOOL. KETT HOLDS A SWATCH BOOK. PORO SITS ON THE STOOL.]

KETT: The approved backdrop is the light blue.

YANA: That is the backdrop behind Poro.

KETT: It looks darker on the camera monitor.

MIRI: The monitor is showing the blue backdrop.

KETT: Then we need to approve the monitor.

Poro stands. The blue background looks lighter from the side.

PORO: Can we take the picture?

YANA: Yes. Sit and look toward the lens.

Kett places a small brand card beside the camera. It covers one corner of the lens.

KETT: The logo should appear in the frame.

YANA: This is a portrait, not a product photo.

KETT: Poro is representing the company.

Poro adjusts the stool. It squeaks. Miri hands him a cushion. The cushion raises him so high that the logo card covers his face.

MIRI: We need a lower cushion.

KETT: The cushion is from the approved furniture palette.

YANA: The palette doesn't sit in the picture.

Kett picks up the card. Poro's face returns to frame, but the card leaves a blue rectangle afterimage on the monitor.

KETT: The logo is still there.

YANA: That's a screen reflection.

Poro blinks. Yana takes a test shot. It captures his eyes closed.

PORO: I blinked.

KETT: The expression communicates calm.

YANA: He was blinking.

Miri asks Poro to hold his eyes open. He does. His ears tilt backward, and his smile stiffens.

PORO: Is this the approved expression?

KETT: It is close to "confidently available."

Yana takes another photo. Poro's shadow forms a dark stripe across the backdrop. Kett declares the shadow off-brand and asks Miri to move the lamp.

Miri shifts it. The shadow moves across Kett's swatch book and darkens the light blue sample.

KETT: Now the color has changed.

YANA: The paint has not changed. The light moved.

Poro steps off the stool and holds the backdrop upright while Miri adjusts the lamp. The blue cloth slips from its stand and wraps around Kett. Only his arm and the swatch book remain visible.

PORO: This is the clearest company portrait so far.

Kett frees himself and puts the swatch book on the stool. Yana takes a quick photo of the empty backdrop with Kett's samples neatly arranged.

YANA: The background is approved.

KETT: And Poro?

Poro steps into the frame beside the swatch book. He gives his usual lopsided grin.

MIRI: That looks like him.

Kett checks the monitor. The logo card is still in the way, covering one ear.

KETT: Can we crop out the card?

YANA: Then we'll crop out the logo too.

[PORO PICKS UP THE CARD AND HOLDS IT BESIDE HIS FACE. YANA TAKES THE PHOTO. KETT APPROVES THE BACKGROUND, THE LOGO, AND THE EAR, IN THAT ORDER.]
