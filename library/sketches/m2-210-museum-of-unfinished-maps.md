# The Museum of Unfinished Maps

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: map exhibitions that treat missing information as a curatorial triumph rather than a problem to solve*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Curator Dela, a macaque; Cartographer Piri, a gibbon; Visitor Omi, a capuchin; and Guard Tov, a baboon.

INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - DAY

A map hangs beneath a sign: "THE WORLD, AS FAR AS WE KNOW." A blank corner covers half the coast. Omi studies it.

OMI: Is the map unfinished?

DELA: It captures the moment before completion.

PIRI: I drew that corner. The ink ran out.

DELA: The empty space invites imagination.

PIRI: It invited me to get another pen.

[TOV SETS OUT A CUP OF PENS. A VISITOR TAKES ONE.]

DELA: Please do not mark the map.

OMI: You said the blank part invites imagination.

DELA: Quiet imagination.

INT. MAP GALLERY - LATER

Piri rolls out a newer map with a clear coastline and printed labels. Dela quickly covers it with a velvet cloth.

PIRI: This is the corrected version.

DELA: It removes the mystery.

PIRI: It adds the river mouth, the harbor, and the north road.

OMI: Those are useful things to know.

DELA: We could describe the coast as "suggested by the available evidence."

PIRI: The coast is on the survey sheet.

OMI: And the north road is marked clearly.

DELA: It has a dotted line.

PIRI: The dotted line means it is unpaved.

DELA: I have been calling it an invitation.

TOV: The visitors took it as a road.

DELA: We are exhibiting uncertainty.

PIRI: We could label the uncertain areas.

DELA: A label would make them certain.

TOV: The visitors keep asking where the restroom is.

DELA: That is in the other wing.

TOV: The map says the other wing is an uncharted sea.

DELA: It was drawn before the restroom was built.

PIRI: Then let's update it.

INT. MAP GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Piri lays a transparent sheet over the old map and draws the missing coast in washable ink. Dela watches as visitors lean closer.

DELA: They are reading the map instead of contemplating it.

OMI: I found the harbor.

TOV: And the restroom.

DELA: The title says "as far as we know." We now know more.

PIRI: A map can change without losing its history.

[DELA WRITES "UPDATED TODAY" ON A SMALL CARD. SHE PLACES IT BESIDE THE BLANK MAP.]

DELA: We can preserve the original beside the new one.

OMI: So the blank part is still here?

PIRI: It is on the old sheet. The coast is on this one.

[TOV MOVES THE RESTROOM ARROW TO THE CORRECT HALL. A QUEUE OF VISITORS IMMEDIATELY FORMS IN FRONT OF THE ORIGINAL MAP, WHICH COVERS THE DOORWAY.]

DELA: The map has become a barrier.

PIRI: It has always been one. We just finally marked it.

DELA: The exhibit will stay open until closing.

OMI: The original map still blocks the doorway.

PIRI: I will lift it onto the empty wall hook.

[PIRI AND TOV MOVE THE OLD MAP. THE EXIT DOOR IS CLEAR.]

TOV: Follow the updated arrow.

OMI: It points to the restroom.

DELA: The exit is behind you.

OMI: At last, a map that admits where I am.

[OMI LEAVES THROUGH THE CLEAR DOOR. DELA TURNS THE OLD MAP SO ITS BLANK CORNER FACES THE WALL.]
