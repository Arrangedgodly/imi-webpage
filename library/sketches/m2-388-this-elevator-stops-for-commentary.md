# This Elevator Stops for Commentary

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: guided building tours that insist on narration while the tour is physically stuck in transit*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Dena, a gibbon guide; Olo, a macaque visitor; Finn, a capuchin operator; Maru, a lemur architect.

INT. OFFICE BUILDING ELEVATOR - DAY

[FOUR PEOPLE STAND IN A SMALL ELEVATOR. DENA HOLDS A TOUR FLAG. A PLACARD SAYS ARCHITECTURAL TOUR, FLOORS ONE THROUGH SIX.]

DENA: On your right is the building's original elevator.

OLO: We're inside it.

DENA: The tour includes the lift.

Finn presses the button for floor three. The elevator moves one foot and stops.

FINN: The doors are still closed.

MARU: This is an intentional pause.

OLO: Did you design the pause?

MARU: I designed the lobby. The elevator is from before my time.

Dena starts narrating the polished brass panel. The elevator gives a small lurch. The tour flag points at the ceiling.

DENA: Above you, a subtle lighting choice.

OLO: The light just flickered.

FINN: I can call maintenance.

DENA: Please wait until after the ceiling description.

Finn speaks into the intercom. It echoes in the car, making everyone turn toward him.

INTERCOM: Maintenance is on the way.

DENA: That is a recorded message about the building's service culture.

The doors open an inch. Olo tries to look through the gap. Dena blocks him with the tour flag.

DENA: Please remain with the group.

OLO: I am still with the group.

The elevator begins to move again, but the floor display shows 2.5. Maru asks Finn whether the display always has halves.

FINN: Only when the elevator is undecided.

Dena says the decimal is a metaphor for transition. Olo counts the floor buttons. There are six, no half floors.

OLO: Maybe we're between floors.

MARU: That's where every building is between floors, eventually.

The elevator stops. Finn presses the door-open button. Nothing happens. Dena suggests they admire the handrail. Olo tries to lean on it, and the rail moves down slightly.

MARU: That's not original.

DENA: It is a responsive feature.

Finn calls maintenance again. The intercom says the technician has arrived at the lobby. Everyone waits.

OLO: Can the tour continue in the lobby?

DENA: We haven't reached the floor.

The doors open fully. They are still on floor one. A technician stands outside holding a screwdriver.

TECHNICIAN: The elevator is back in service.

Dena lowers the tour flag and steps out. She invites everyone to continue on foot. Maru points to the stairs.

The technician asks them to confirm the elevator reached the requested floor. Finn points to the display. It still reads 2.5. Dena says the tour's first stop was the lobby, so everyone has completed half the route. Olo asks what happens to the other half.

MARU: We take the stairs.

DENA: That is an optional extension.

MARU: The stairwell has the original handrail.

Olo starts climbing. The tour flag catches in the elevator doors.

[FINN HOLDS THE DOOR. DENA PULLS THE FLAG FREE, THEN ANNOUNCES THAT THE GROUP HAS ARRIVED AT THE FIRST FLOOR.]
