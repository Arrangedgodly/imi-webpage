# The Puppet Troupe Loses Its Strings

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: backstage variety shows, puppet-show stagecraft, and performers whose props rebel during a live introduction*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: PUPPETEER, a gibbon; PUPPET, a capuchin; STAGE MANAGER, a baboon; MUSICIAN, an orangutan; USHER, a macaque.

[A small theater. A puppet booth faces the audience. The puppeteer holds a marionette by its strings. The musician tunes a tiny piano.]

PUPPETEER: Welcome to the show. Our first character is a prince.

PUPPET: I am a tax auditor.

STAGE MANAGER: We have changed the script.

[The puppeteer pulls a string to make the puppet bow. The puppet tips sideways and drags a small chair from behind the curtain.]

PUPPET: This chair is not a deductible expense.

ACT ONE

[The stage manager checks the rigging. The strings from the prince puppet are tangled with the curtain cord. The usher tries to open the curtain; the puppet rises above the booth.]

USHER: The prince has escaped the stage.

PUPPETEER: That is the planned flight.

PUPPET: I would like to file a complaint.

[The musician plays a royal fanfare. The puppet begins bobbing to the beat, then points toward the piano. The puppeteer looks at the strings and finds the left and right controls swapped.]

STAGE MANAGER: The hands are reversed.

PUPPETEER: I can compensate.

[They try again. The puppet waves both hands while the puppeteer stands still. The audience applauds. The stage manager lowers a small sign reading IMPROVISED.]

PUPPET: I accept the applause, but not the job.

[The puppeteer asks the stagehand to hold the curtain cord. The stagehand is not in the cast; the usher takes the cord and keeps the curtain still. The puppet is lowered to the booth. The musician changes the fanfare to a slow courtroom theme.]

PUPPET: That music is not helping.

STAGE MANAGER: The strings are now clear. Try the bow again.

[The puppeteer moves the control bar carefully. The puppet bows and its little hat falls off. The usher catches it. The puppet reaches for the hat, but the string makes it point at the stage manager instead.]

PUPPET: You may keep the hat.

STAGE MANAGER: I do not want it.

[The usher notices a knot at the control bar. The puppeteer removes it, and the strings separate. The prince's right hand lifts; the left hand waves at the audience. The puppet asks for a chair and sits.]

ACT TWO

[The troupe performs the short finale. The puppet is introduced as a prince and bows correctly. The audience claps. The puppet then points toward the tax forms on the prop table.]

PUPPET: There is still an audit.

[The stage manager calls the end of the show. The musician plays a final chord. The puppeteer lowers the puppet into its box without tangling the strings.]

FINAL IMAGE: The prince sits in a miniature chair while the real troupe packs the control bar and leaves the tax forms on stage.
