# The School Photo Needs Everyone to Look Natural

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: school picture day poses manufactured as candid authenticity*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-012*

Cast: Photographer Mara, a macaque; teacher Nari, a lemur; student Pip, a capuchin; principal Vex, an orangutan.

INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

[A blue backdrop stands beside a sign: "BE YOURSELF, BUT FACE FORWARD." Mara adjusts a camera. Nari lines up students.]

MARA: We want a natural smile.

PIP: I was smiling naturally.

MARA: That was a grin. We need effortless.

PIP: How do I effort less?

VEX: Think of something spontaneous.

[Pip stares at the ceiling.]

PIP: The ceiling has a crack shaped like a banana.

NARI: That's not a banana.

PIP: It could be. If it tried.

[Mara clicks. The flash reflects from Vex's shiny principal badge. The photo is a white square.]

MARA: The badge has stolen the light.

VEX: It is a symbol of leadership.

NARI: It's a mirror.

[Vex turns the badge inward. Nari asks Pip to sit on a stool. The stool wobbles. Pip grips the sides and goes perfectly still.]

MARA: There. Relaxed posture.

PIP: I'm holding on for survival.


[Mara turns the preview toward Nari. It shows only the stool and blue backdrop.]

NARI: Where is Pip?

PIP: Under the table. The stool threw me.

VEX: That is an authentic low angle.

NARI: It is a picture of furniture.

[Pip crawls out with the stool cushion.]

PIP: I brought my own seat. It does not wobble.

MARA: Now you look comfortable.

PIP: I am. Do not take a picture.

[Mara raises the camera anyway. Pip hides behind the cushion; Vex steps into frame with his rehearsed smile.]

MARA: We call that candid.

[The stool rocks. Pip tilts right. Mara follows him with the camera. Nari steadies the stool with one foot. Vex instructs a group of children waiting offstage to "look like they just heard good news."]

VEX: Everyone imagine a surprise assembly is canceled.

[The students cheer loudly. Pip startles and falls backward into a soft gym mat. The camera clicks.]

MARA: That's the one.

PIP: You photographed my panic.

MARA: We captured your authentic reaction.

NARI: He was surprised by the principal.

VEX: My announcement has finally helped the school.

[Mara shows them the preview. Pip's eyes are wide; Vex is in the background with his mouth open mid-announcement.]

PIP: I look like I saw a ghost.

MARA: You look unposed.

NARI: He looks like the stool betrayed him.

VEX: Could we crop me out?

MARA: You're the only one looking directly at the camera.

[Vex moves behind the backdrop. Mara takes another picture. The blue fabric pulls taut around his silhouette, giving him a giant round halo.] 

MARA: That's a strong school portrait.

VEX: I look like the school spirit.

PIP: You look like a curtain with a badge.

[Nari lets the backdrop go. It drops, revealing Vex still posing with a rehearsed smile. Mara clicks once more.]

MARA: Perfect. A candid principal.

VEX: Was I natural?

PIP: You were behind a curtain.
