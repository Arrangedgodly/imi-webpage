# Terms and Conditions Apply

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Overcomplicated product advertising and tiny-print disclaimers*
*By: IMI Broadcast Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

[SFX: CHEERFUL JINGLE. A PRODUCT REP stands beside a clear jar labeled "ONE FREE BANANA." A CUSTOMER monkey approaches.]

REP: Try the new PeelSure banana. One banana, no surprises.

CUSTOMER NIA: Is it free?

REP: Completely free, after the usual conditions.

NIA: What conditions?

REP: You must accept the banana in a state of reasonable banana readiness. You may not use it as a hammer, a hat, or proof that you own a banana.

NIA: I wasn't planning to.

REP: Great. Please initial here, there, and on the banana.

NIA: You want me to write on the fruit?

REP: Just the peel. The peel remains company property until consumed.

NIA: If I eat it, do I own the inside?

REP: You receive a temporary eating license. Ownership depends on whether you chew at home or in a shared area.

NIA: This is a snack.

REP: And we're excited to keep it simple. That's why there's a fourteen-page guide.

[REP HANDS OVER A ROLL OF PAPER THAT UNFURLS ACROSS THE FLOOR.]

NIA: This says the banana may be substituted for a banana-like item.

REP: Only in markets where bananas are unavailable.

NIA: We're standing next to a bowl of bananas.

REP: They're for display. This jar contains the free offer.

NIA: The jar is locked.

REP: The key is available with our premium access package.

NIA: How much?

REP: The key is free. The package that lets you request the key is a monthly fee.

NIA: I'll just buy a banana.

REP: You're free to do that. The free banana includes no fruit.

[PAUSE. THE JINGLE PLAYS ONE NOTE.] 

NIA: Then what's in the jar?

REP: The opportunity to claim a banana if you meet the criteria.

NIA: Which are?

REP: You must be a monkey, which you are. You must have a valid work badge, which we can verify. You must not have received a banana in the last thirty days.

NIA: I got one this morning.

REP: Then you're not eligible for the free banana.

NIA: I brought it from home.

REP: The system doesn't distinguish home fruit from office fruit.

NIA: Can I appeal?

REP: Yes. Tell us why you deserve fruit in under five hundred characters.

NIA: "I'm hungry."

REP: That's forty characters. We need at least two hundred for the form to submit.

NIA: I'm hungry, and your form is hungry for words.

REP: Strong. Please wait while I check with my supervisor.

[REP WALKS TO THE END OF THE ROLL AND WHISPERS INTO A PAPER CUP.] 

REP: The supervisor says we can offer you half a banana.

NIA: Is it free?

REP: Yes, if you pay the handling fee.

NIA: And if I don't?

REP: We retain the banana and charge you for storage.

NIA: I have a better idea. I'll take this one from the display bowl.

[NAILS CLIP. SHE PEELS AND EATS.] 

REP: That's inventory!

NIA: I'm a monkey. I found a banana. This is the whole consumer journey.

REP: You have violated paragraph nine.

NIA: I can't hear you over the potassium.

[REP LOOKS AT THE FORM.]

REP: There's an exception. If the customer has already eaten the product, the claim is considered settled.

NIA: Put that in the ad.

REP: Legal won't approve it.

NIA: Then legal can have the jar.

[THE JINGLE RETURNS, NOW SUNG BY NIA WITH HER MOUTH FULL.]
