# The Star Chart Has Too Many Labels

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: stargazing apps that cover the night sky with alerts, labels, and social features*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Astronomer Kiri, a macaque; App Rep Daro, a gibbon; Visitor Mela, a capuchin; and Caretaker Olo, a baboon.

EXT. HILLTOP OBSERVATORY - NIGHT

Mela looks through a telescope. Kiri adjusts its focus. Daro holds a tablet that glows brightly.

MELA: I can see the moon.

KIRI: Keep both eyes open. Let the view settle.

DARO: The app can identify the moon, show its phase, and add a location tag.

OLO: Please dim the screen. The visitors came to see the dark sky.

DARO: I have a brightness slider.

[HE SLIDES IT. THE SCREEN FLASHES WHITE, THEN DIM. MELA LOSES THE TELESCOPE VIEW.]

MELA: I was looking at the moon.

DARO: The app can restore your last view.

KIRI: The moon is still there.

EXT. HILLTOP OBSERVATORY - LATER

Daro opens the app's constellation layer. Names and lines cover the sky display.

DARO: Here is the bear, the spoon, the ancient sailor, and the sponsored constellation of the month.

MELA: I see a cluster of stars.

KIRI: Start with the brightest one.

DARO: You can tap any star for a fact card.

MELA: I don't want a card. I want to find the bright cluster.

KIRI: Look just above the ridge.

DARO: The app has a guided tour with audio.

OLO: The audio will disturb the night walk.

DARO: It uses earbuds.

[THE TABLET LOSES THE MAP WHEN THE SCREEN LOCKS. DARO TAPS IT, AND THE BRIGHTNESS RETURNS TO FULL.]

MELA: I can see the screen better than the sky.

KIRI: Turn it off for a minute.

[THE TABLET BEEPS. A NOTIFICATION COVERS THE STAR MAP.]

DARO: Someone has liked your moon view.

OLO: No one has posted a moon view.

DARO: The app did it automatically.

MELA: Can I turn off the labels?

DARO: That is an advanced feature.

KIRI: You can close the app.

[DARO TRIES TO CLOSE IT. A POP-UP ASKS HIM TO RATE THE CONSTELLATION.]

DARO: It wants feedback.

OLO: Give it one star.

DARO: That will lower its rating.

KIRI: One star is a rating.

MELA: The real cluster does not need a score.

[DARO TRIES TO TAKE A PHOTO. THE TABLET FLASH TURNS ON AND ILLUMINATES EVERYONE'S FACES.]

OLO: The sky has not changed.

KIRI: The camera settings have.

EXT. HILLTOP OBSERVATORY - END OF NIGHT

Mela looks through the telescope again. Kiri points toward a small cluster.

KIRI: That is the Pleiades. You can see them without the screen.

MELA: I see them.

[DARO TURNS OFF THE TABLET. ITS BRIGHT SCREEN REFLECTS THE CLUSTER FOR A MOMENT, THEN GOES DARK.]

DARO: The app is offline.

OLO: The stars are still online.

MELA: The view is better without the comments.

[KIRI LETS MELA ADJUST THE FOCUS. Daro stands quietly beside them, tablet tucked under his arm.]
