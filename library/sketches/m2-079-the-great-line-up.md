# The Great Line-Up

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport queue systems and priority boarding announcements*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: GATE AGENT SURI, a macaque; PASSENGER DEX, a capuchin; PASSENGER OLLA, a lemur; and a flock of travelers.

[A small airport gate is marked with ropes, signs, and one cardboard doorway. The boarding sign reads GROUP A, GROUP B, GROUP C, and GROUP THAT HAS QUESTIONS.]

SURI: Welcome. Please board by group. Group A first.

DEX: I am Group A.

OLLA: Your ticket says Group C.

DEX: I am emotionally Group A.

SURI: Emotions must remain behind the line.

[Dex steps toward the doorway. The rope swings shut in front of him.] 

SURI: Group A, proceed.

[Olla calmly walks through. Dex tries to follow but the rope catches his tail.] 

DEX: My tail is Group A.

SURI: Tail status is determined by the ticket.

OLLA: This is Group C. We were told to wait for the special call.

SURI: Group C, please prepare to be ready to prepare.

[She holds up a sign. The flock reads it and rearranges into a perfect circle.] 

SURI: You have formed a queue with no front.

OLLA: Everyone can see the doorway.

SURI: The doorway is not the process.

[Dex takes the sign and turns it around. On the back is a diagram of arrows that points in a loop.] 

DEX: The process appears to board itself.

SURI: That is the new self-service lane.

[Passengers begin walking in circles. One traveler passes the door four times and becomes increasingly confident.] 

TRAVELER: I think I have priority now.

SURI: You have completed the loop.

OLLA: Can we board by seat rows?

SURI: Rows are not groups.

DEX: Can we board by who has been waiting longest?

SURI: That would be unfair to those who arrived later with a strong case.

[Olla's tail reaches the doorway and curls around the handle. The door opens a crack.] 

SURI: Please do not use your tail to alter the boarding infrastructure.

OLLA: I was standing still.

DEX: The line has finally reached the plane.

[Everyone looks. The cardboard doorway rolls away on tiny wheels, pulled by the rope. Suri races after it. The queue follows, maintaining its shape.] 

SURI: Please stay in order while we relocate the gate.

[The doorway stops beneath a tree. The flock forms a neat line. Dex is now first because the rope's loop has flipped.]

SURI: Group A may board.

DEX: I knew I was Group A.

SURI: You are Group C. The line moved you.

OLLA: So the system is flexible.

SURI: The system is mobile.

[The travelers pass through the door, each tapping the sign as if it were a scanner. Suri follows last, carrying the rope.] 

SURI: Thank you for flying with us. Please collect your luggage at the end of the process.

DEX: Which end?

[The flock turns around together. The line now has a clear end, right beside a pile of bags.]

SURI: You have reached baggage claim.

OLLA: We haven't taken off.

SURI: You completed the journey efficiently.
