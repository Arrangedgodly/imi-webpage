# The Compass Points to the Gift Shop

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: museum demonstrations, overconfident navigation advice, and retail directions mistaken for scientific discovery*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: GUIDE, a gibbon; NAVIGATOR, a macaque; CURATOR, an orangutan; VISITOR, a capuchin; SHOPKEEPER, a baboon.

[A museum exhibit about navigation. A compass sits beside a large metal gift-shop sign.]

GUIDE: This compass points north.

NAVIGATOR: It points toward the sign.

CURATOR: The sign has a metal frame.

VISITOR: Is the gift shop north?

SHOPKEEPER: It is east of the gallery.

[The guide turns the compass. The needle turns too, still pointing toward the sign.]

GUIDE: It is following me.

NAVIGATOR: It is following the metal.

[The curator moves the compass several feet away. The needle steadies and points north.] 

CURATOR: A compass needs distance from magnetic objects.

VISITOR: The exhibit sign is magnetic?

CURATOR: The frame contains steel. The nearby speaker also has a magnet.

[The navigator removes the speaker from the table. The needle turns again, now toward a metal tray of souvenir pins.] 

SHOPKEEPER: The pins are made of tin.

CURATOR: Their display tray is steel.

SHOPKEEPER: I can move the tray.

[The shopkeeper lifts it. The compass settles. The guide turns it so north lines up with the gallery map.] 

GUIDE: Now it points north.

NAVIGATOR: Now we can use it.

[The visitor asks where the exit is. The guide points north. The compass needle turns east when she walks near the display case.] 

GUIDE: The exit has moved.

CURATOR: You have moved beside the case.

[The guide steps back. The needle points north again. The visitor follows the map, then notices a sign to the gift shop.] 

VISITOR: Is the shop still east?

SHOPKEEPER: Yes. I moved the tray, not the shop.

NAVIGATOR: The compass is not a shopping assistant.

[The shopkeeper hands the visitor a paper map. The visitor lays it over the compass. The needle disappears under the map.] 

VISITOR: It has stopped pointing.

CURATOR: It is under paper.

GUIDE: The compass points north when you keep it away from metal and let the needle move.

VISITOR: And the gift shop?

SHOPKEEPER: Follow the signs. The compass will only send you toward my tray.

[The navigator places a small wooden marker at the correct north point. The compass remains on the center of the exhibit table, away from the speaker and pin tray.] 

[The navigator asks the visitor to hold the compass at arm's length, away from the steel tray. The needle settles. The visitor turns in place, and the needle keeps pointing north.]

VISITOR: The compass turned, but north stayed put.

GUIDE: Exactly. The room moved around it.

The shopkeeper rolls the pin tray to the far counter. The compass no longer swings toward the display. The visitor follows the map's north arrow to the exit instead of the souvenir shelf.

SHOPKEEPER: The gift shop is still here if you want it.

VISITOR: I know where to find it.

FINAL IMAGE: The compass needle points north while the gift-shop sign points east, each in its own place.
