# The Quiet Car Is a Jungle

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Commuter etiquette announcements and train-car rules*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: ANNOUNCER, a slow loris; DOT, a macaque commuter; GUS, a gibbon carrying a leaf blower; and RHEA, a capuchin with a thermos.

[A train car. A sign says QUIET CARRIAGE. Another says NO FOOD, NO CALLS, NO TAILS IN AISLES.]

ANNOUNCER: Welcome to the quiet car. Please keep voices low and all limbs within your seat.

DOT: My tail is longer than the seat.

ANNOUNCER: Please fold it into the approved tail area.

DOT: There is no approved tail area.

ANNOUNCER: We are adding one to the suggestion box.

[Rhea opens a thermos. The lid squeaks loudly.]

ANNOUNCER: Please silence beverages.

RHEA: It is a lid.

ANNOUNCER: The lid has interrupted three passengers.

GUS: Can I use my leaf blower to move it?

ANNOUNCER: No leaf blowers.

GUS: Mine is battery powered.

ANNOUNCER: The rule does not mention batteries.

GUS: Then I'm technically allowed.

[He starts it. Everyone's fur streams backward. A loose leaf flies into Dot's tea.]

DOT: My drink is now a salad.

[The train halts. The lights go out. A recorded voice says, "Please remain calm and keep all complaints quiet."]

ANNOUNCER: We are experiencing a brief delay. Please do not ask how brief. The schedule is under review.

GUS: Can I clear the track?

DOT: With the blower?

GUS: It can clear leaves.

[He switches it on. The leaf in Dot's cup sticks to the ceiling. A second leaf drops into the aisle.]

ANNOUNCER: We have created a leaf event.

RHEA: My thermos can make a warm drink for the conductor.

ANNOUNCER: Please do not speak to the conductor.

RHEA: I can leave it at the door.

[She pours. The squeaky lid rings like a tiny bell. A door opens, and the conductor takes the thermos.]

CONDUCTOR, off: Thank you.

ANNOUNCER: The delay is over.

GUS: Because of the tea?

ANNOUNCER: Because the conductor found a loose branch on the track. The tea is not in the official report.

[The train moves. The blower rolls under a seat.]

GUS: I am leaving it at home next time.

DOT: And your leaf?

GUS: I am keeping it. It has been promoted to passenger.

ANNOUNCER: Please keep promoted passengers off the seats. This has been the quiet car. We will now announce every rule in a whisper. 

[The train reaches the next stop. The automated voice whispers the stop name so softly no one hears it. Everyone misses their stop.]

DOT: Was that our stop?

ANNOUNCER: I cannot repeat it. Repetition may disturb the quiet.

GUS: We can pull the emergency cord.

ANNOUNCER: That is for emergencies, not missed announcements.

RHEA: Then what do we do?

ANNOUNCER: Wait for the next stop. It will be whispered too.

[The train goes by the station, then returns on the same track.]

DOT: We are back where we started.

ANNOUNCER: Thank you for keeping your concerns inside the carriage.

[BLACKOUT.]
