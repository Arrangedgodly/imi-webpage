# The Queue That Would Not End

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: airport security queues that inspect the line itself as passengers await screening*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Tavi, a squirrel monkey traveler; Rell, a gibbon queue marshal; Nimo, a capuchin inspector; and passengers in line.

INT. TERMINAL CHECKPOINT - DAY

A line of passengers snakes around the inspection area. Nimo holds a measuring tape. Rell directs Tavi to stand on a floor mark.

NIMO: The line has exceeded its approved length.

TAVI: I have not reached the front.

NIMO: You are part of the length.

Rell asks everyone to step back so the line can be measured. The line steps back and bends around a post, adding another three feet.

RELL: The queue has grown during inspection.

Nimo clips a numbered tag to the rope barrier. A passenger asks if the tag means they are next. Nimo says it identifies the rope, not the passenger. The rope has no boarding pass.

Tavi checks the flight board. His gate is open for boarding. Rell says the queue must be checked before anyone leaves it. Tavi asks how he can exit the line. Rell produces an EXIT LINE form and asks him to fill it out without stepping outside the queue.

TAVI: The form is on the table.

RELL: The table is outside the line.

The passengers shuffle toward the table. The line now stretches through the doorway. Nimo asks Rell to measure the new length. Rell walks alongside it, but the line moves as he goes. He reaches the end just as it curls behind him.

RELL: I have found the back.

NIMO: The back has moved.

Rell places a floor cone at the end. The line immediately advances. The cone is now in the middle. Nimo declares it an obstruction and asks the passengers to go around it. The line forms a loop around the cone and grows longer again.

Tavi tries to leave through a gap. Nimo holds up a sign: PLEASE REMAIN IN QUEUE WHILE QUEUE IS UNDER REVIEW.

TAVI: My gate closes in five minutes.

NIMO: The queue is not scheduled to close.

A passenger asks whether the inspection covers the line's contents. Nimo says the contents are people and each person must remain in order. The first passenger turns around to see how long the line is. Everyone else follows, forming a second queue facing backward.

RELL: We now have two lines.

NIMO: Are they one queue or two?

They measure the gap between them. Rell steps across it, and the line follows, briefly becoming a single row again. The passengers applaud. Nimo records the applause as a queue event.

Tavi asks Rell if he can walk beside the line to reach the front. Rell tries it. Because Tavi is not in the queue, Nimo directs him to the end. Tavi walks around the rope, arriving at the end without ever entering. Rell measures the loop and announces it is a perfect circle.

NIMO: A queue with no end.

TAVI: It has a front. I can see it.

Rell takes the rope barrier's hooks off their posts and opens a straight lane. The line becomes a short row. Nimo checks the tape: within limits. Tavi reaches the checkpoint and passes through. Rell removes the final rope and folds it.

NIMO: Queue cleared.

The passengers head toward their gates. Nimo begins measuring the empty floor. Rell looks at him.

RELL: What are you inspecting now?

NIMO: The space where the line was.

Rell unrolls the rope again. Everyone turns around and gets back in line.
