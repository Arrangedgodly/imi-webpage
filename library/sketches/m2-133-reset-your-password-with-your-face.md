# Reset Your Password With Your Face

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cybersecurity identity checks and login recovery flows for a troop whose faces change with grooming*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Support agent Nalo, a macaque; user Piri, a gibbon; technician Sumo, a baboon; and automated voice.

[A LAPTOP DISPLAYS "PASSWORD EXPIRED." PIRI LEANS IN.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Please verify your face.

PIRI: My face is here.

[THE CAMERA DOES NOT RECOGNIZE HER.]

NALO: Try facing the light.

[PIRI TURNS. THE CAMERA STILL FAILS.]

NALO: Can you remove your bangs?

PIRI: They are fur.

SUMO: Try the backup question.

AUTOMATED VOICE: What was the name of your first branch?

PIRI: The branch I was born on.

AUTOMATED VOICE: Incorrect.

PIRI: I was very young.

NALO: We can send a code to your phone.

PIRI: It is in my other hand.

[THE PHONE BUZZES. PIRI READS THE CODE.]

PIRI: Four, seven, two, nine.

NALO: The code expired.

PIRI: It arrived three seconds ago.

SUMO: The clock may be fast.

[A LITTLE MONKEY TAPS THE SCREEN. A NEW PROMPT APPEARS: "SELECT ALL IMAGES WITH A MONKEY."]

PIRI: All of them have monkeys.

NALO: Select the ones that are you.

[PIRI CHOOSES THREE PHOTOS. THE SYSTEM ACCEPTS TWO.]

PIRI: That is my cousin.

NALO: The system thinks you are similar.

SUMO: Try the recovery email.

PIRI: Which one?

NALO: The one you registered.

PIRI: I registered from the branch.

[THE LAPTOP ASKS FOR A RECOVERY KEY. PIRI PRODUCES A SMALL KEY.]

NALO: That opens the nest box.

PIRI: It is my recovery key.

[THE KEY DOES NOT FIT. THE MONKEY TAKES IT AND OPENS THE NEST BOX.]

SUMO: We can reset the password once you log in.

PIRI: I cannot log in.

NALO: Then we can verify you another way.

[THE MONKEY GROOMS PIRI'S EAR. THE CAMERA RECOGNIZES HER.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Face verified.

PIRI: I changed nothing.

SUMO: Your cousin did.

[THE PASSWORD RESETS. PIRI WRITES IT ON A LEAF.]

NALO: Do not store passwords on a leaf.

PIRI: It is a private leaf.


[SUMO OPENS THE ACCOUNT RECOVERY PAGE.]

SUMO: It will ask for a second factor.

PIRI: I have a second face.

NALO: The system means a second device.

[PIRI HOLDS UP A TABLET. THE SCREEN SHOWS HER WITH A LEAF ON HER HEAD.]

PIRI: It says I am not me.

SUMO: The leaf changes your outline.

PIRI: The leaf is part of the branch.

[THE MONKEY TAKES THE LEAF OFF HER HEAD. THE TABLET RECOGNIZES HER.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Identity confirmed.

PIRI: The system prefers me without shade.

NALO: We can adjust the account photo.

[PIRI SETS A NEW PHOTO WITH THE LEAF INCLUDED. THE SYSTEM FLAGS IT AGAIN.]

SUMO: It is a stubborn security feature.

[NALO CLOSES THE ACCOUNT PAGE AND HANDS PIRI A PAPER RECEIPT.]

PIRI: This one recognizes me.
[THE MONKEY TAKES THE LEAF AND EATS IT.]

AUTOMATED VOICE: Password successfully stored.

PIRI: That is not what happened.
