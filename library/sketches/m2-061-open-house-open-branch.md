# Open House, Open Branch

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: home-buying reality shows and staged property reveals*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: HOST MILO, a macaque; BUYER SERA, a gibbon; AGENT FERN, a capuchin; and a CAMERA OPERATOR.

[A listing sign hangs crookedly from a branch. A vine frames a small hollow.]

MILO: Today we show Sera a rare canopy home with natural light, rustic texture, and a move-in-ready nest.

SERA: Who lives here now?

FERN: Nobody. The previous owner has moved to the upper canopy.

[An owl pokes its head out of the hollow.]

MILO: The listing says vacant.

FERN: It was vacant when I took the photos.

SERA: When was that?

FERN: Before the branch grew leaves.

MILO: Let's see the open-plan living area.

[They crawl inside. The camera operator cannot fit.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: I can film from the entrance.

MILO: That's not the angle we planned.

SERA: I need to see the floor.

[She looks down. The nest is on a slant.]

SERA: The floor is moving.

FERN: It's a natural sway.

SERA: The branch is cracked.

MILO: We can call that a flexible foundation.

SERA: Can you call a carpenter?

FERN: That's not on the tour.

[The owl spreads both wings and bumps the camera.]

MILO: We have an unexpected resident.

SERA: Is the owl included in the price?

FERN: The home is listed as "wildlife-adjacent."

SERA: That's not the same as included.

[The camera operator backs away. The branch bends. Everyone quickly climbs to a stronger limb.]

MILO: Our crew has discovered a second bedroom.

SERA: It is another branch.

FERN: With panoramic views.

SERA: And no cracked floor.

MILO: The tour is going well.

SERA: The tour is over. I'll make an offer on the sturdy branch, contingent on inspection and the owl keeping its home.

FERN: The seller wanted a quick close.

SERA: The seller is an owl. It can wait.

[The agent folds the listing sign. The camera pans to the empty hollow.]

MILO: The home is available again.

[The owl closes the door with one wing.]
SERA: We should also check the branch above the path.

FERN: It is not part of the property.

MILO: It is in the listing photo.

SERA: Then it is part of the disclosure.

[The owl pushes the sign down. The word VACANT folds in half.]

FERN: The seller wants to know if you are still interested.

SERA: Yes, in the sturdy limb. No, in the hollow. And I want a written note that the owl keeps its home.

MILO: Can we call this a pre-approved offer?

SERA: You can call it a conditional offer.

FERN: I will need a new photo.

[The owl sits on the sign. Only the word AVAILABLE shows.]

MILO: The listing now says "owl, available."

SERA: The owl is not available.

FERN: The branch?

SERA: Inspected first.

[The camera frames the listing agent holding the sign under a tree. The owl taps it until it falls flat.]

[BLACKOUT.]
