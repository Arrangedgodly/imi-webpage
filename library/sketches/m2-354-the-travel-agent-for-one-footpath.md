# The Travel Agent for One Footpath

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury travel planners who sell complicated itineraries for ordinary outings*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-010*

Cast: agent Leni, a gibbon; client Odo, a baboon; guide Miri, a macaque; porter Piri, a capuchin.

INT. TRAVEL OFFICE - DAY

Leni spreads a map across the desk.

LENI: Your journey to the orchard includes a scenic walk, a rest stop, and a return route.

ODO: It is one path.

Leni offers three packages: standard, premium, and immersive. The premium package includes a guided pause beside a tree.

Miri says the tree is on the path. Piri asks whether the route has a snack stop. Leni says the orchard is the snack stop. Odo chooses the standard package.

Leni gives him a luggage tag. It says "Destination: Orchard." Odo has no luggage. Piri says he can carry the map. Miri says the map is already printed on the signpost.

The group sets out. Leni narrates the route: first, they leave the office; second, they follow the path; third, they reach the orchard. Odo asks if there is a faster option. Miri points ahead. They are already walking.

Piri has packed a small bag in case the group needs provisions. It contains one apple. Odo asks if the apple is included in the premium package. Leni says it is an optional upgrade.

At the tree, Leni stops for the guided pause. The group rests. Odo asks if they can continue. Miri says yes. They reach the orchard, where the fruit is growing. Leni presents a certificate of arrival.

ODO: I have arrived before the certificate.

Leni stamps it anyway. Piri offers the apple back to Odo. He says he would rather pick fruit at the destination. Miri reminds them not to take any fruit without permission. The orchard keeper waves them in.

Leni asks Miri to lead the group without using the map. Miri follows the path signs. Piri carries the apple bag, though it contains only one apple. Odo asks if it should be divided as a trail snack. Leni says the apple is not included in the package. Miri reaches the tree and pauses; Leni calls this a premium moment, even though Odo bought standard. Piri sits on a root and eats the apple herself. Leni checks the certificate and finds the arrival time written before the group arrived. She says it is a pre-arrival document. Odo asks if it can be corrected. Leni crosses out the time and writes the real one. The orchard keeper confirms their entry.

Piri ties the empty apple bag to the side of her pack. Leni says it can be recycled at the orchard shed. Odo writes that down as the return itinerary.

TAG

Leni asks if they want to book the return trip.

ODO: We can walk back.

LENI: That is a separate itinerary.
