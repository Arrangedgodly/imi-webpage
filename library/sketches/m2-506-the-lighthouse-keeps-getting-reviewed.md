# The Lighthouse Keeps Getting Reviewed

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: travel review shows, lighthouse tours, and hosts who rate practical buildings like luxury resorts*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: HOST, a macaque; LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER, an orangutan; CAMERA OPERATOR, a gibbon; TOURIST, a baboon; SEAGULL, a capuchin.

[A lighthouse landing. The host stands by a spiral stair with a tiny rating card. The camera operator struggles to fit the stairs in frame.]

HOST: We are reviewing the coast's most vertical accommodation.

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: It is a lighthouse. Nobody sleeps here.

HOST: No room service?

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: There is a lamp room and a supply shelf.

[The host climbs three steps, then looks down.]

HOST: The lobby has an impressive upward view.

CAMERA OPERATOR: We are only one flight up.

[The tourist arrives carrying a thermos and a folded map.]

TOURIST: Is the beacon working?

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: Yes. I am about to switch it on for the fog signal test.

HOST: What is the complimentary welcome drink?

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: Salt air.

[The seagull flies in and steals the host's rating card. It lands on the rail above the stairs.]

HOST: The staff has taken my review.

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: That is a bird.

ACT ONE

[The keeper starts the lamp motor. The beacon turns slowly. The camera operator circles the room to capture the light.]

CAMERA OPERATOR: It keeps moving out of frame.

HOST: A rotating feature.

[The host raises a score card. The light sweeps across the paper and makes it glow.]

HOST: The ambience is dramatic.

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: The lamp is for ships, not reviewers.

[The tourist unfolds the map. A gust catches it and carries it toward the open hatch. The keeper catches one corner and pins it under a toolbox.]

TOURIST: My map is now part of the equipment.

HOST: The service is hands-on.

[The seagull returns with the rating card and drops it into the lamp's light path. The card circles around the room on a draft.]

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: Please keep paper clear of the lamp.

HOST: The review has entered a feedback loop.

[The keeper stops the motor, retrieves the card, and checks that the lamp is clear. The tourist holds the map under a heavy thermos.]

ACT TWO

[The fog signal test sounds once. The host jumps and drops the rating pencil. It rolls down one stair, then stops against the keeper's boot.]

HOST: Soundproofing needs work.

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: That is a foghorn.

TOURIST: It kept the boats off the rocks.

[The host writes five stars on the rating card, then crosses out the words "luxury stay" and writes "working lighthouse." The keeper restarts the lamp.]

HOST: I recommend visiting during daylight.

LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: I recommend staying off the stairs during the signal test.

[The camera operator packs the camera. The seagull grabs the pencil and flies to the window.]

FINAL IMAGE: The beam turns across the water while the seagull holds the review pencil above the host's final score.
