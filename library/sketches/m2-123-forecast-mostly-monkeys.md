# Forecast: Mostly Monkeys

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: weather broadcasts with graphics, storm warnings and a forecast built around a troop moving through the studio*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-005*

Cast: Meteorologist Tavi, a gibbon; field reporter Reni, a macaque; camera operator Bolo, a capuchin; and a troop of young monkeys.

[WEATHER MAP. A LARGE GREEN AREA COVERS THE FOREST.]

TAVI: Good evening. Expect scattered fruit, followed by a brief period of grooming.

[THE MAP HAS A RED ARROW MOVING TOWARD THE STUDIO.]

TAVI: A troop is approaching from the west.

RENI (ON SCREEN): I am at the edge of the clearing. The troop appears to be following a fig scent.

[TAVI POINTS TO A SATELLITE IMAGE.]

TAVI: The projected route passes through our broadcast area.

[THE STUDIO DOOR OPENS. THREE MONKEYS ENTER.]

TAVI: The forecast is arriving early.

[THE MONKEYS CLIMB THE WEATHER DESK.]

TAVI: We have a high-pressure system on the desk.

BOLO: One is sitting on the map.

TAVI: That is the southern region.

[RENI RUNS INTO THE STUDIO.]

RENI: The troop is not approaching. They were already here.

TAVI: Then why did you report from outside?

RENI: The camera was outside.

[ONE MONKEY TAKES THE RED POINTER.]

TAVI: The red pointer indicates a warm front.

[THE MONKEY POINTS IT AT THE CAMERA.]

BOLO: Now the camera is the warm front.

[THE MONKEYS MOVE THE POINTER ACROSS THE MAP. TAVI TRIES TO RECLAIM IT.]

TAVI: We are seeing a sharp change in direction.

RENI: It is going toward the fruit bowl.

[THE TROOP CLUSTERS AROUND A BOWL OF FIGS.]

TAVI: A dense band of monkeys is expected around the snacks.

RENI: Visibility is falling.

BOLO: The lens is covered by a tail.

[TAVI HOLDS UP A HAND-DRAWN MAP.]

TAVI: The rest of tonight's forecast is calm.

[A MONKEY PULLS THE MAP DOWN.]

RENI: The forecast has been revised.

TAVI: We will have a clear patch once the fruit is gone.

[THE TROOP FINISHES THE FIGS AND MOVES OFF THE DESK.]

TAVI: The clearing is visible again.

RENI: Are we safe to resume?

TAVI: Yes. Expect a quiet night, unless another fig appears.


[THE CREW PUTS A SECOND BOWL OF FIGS UNDER THE DESK.]

TAVI: We will keep the fruit off the map.

RENI: The troop may find it.

BOLO: Then the forecast will hold.

[THE MONKEYS RETURN. THEY TAKE THE BOWL AND CARRY IT TO THE DOOR.]

TAVI: The weather is moving east.

RENI: That is the troop.

TAVI: They are carrying the fruit.

[THE MAP SHOWS A CLEAR PATCH WHERE THE BOWL WAS.]

BOLO: Visibility is improving.

[TAVI POINTS TO THE EMPTY SPOT.]

TAVI: The clearing is now sunny with a chance of leftovers.

[THE CREW MOVES THE WEATHER MAP BACK INTO VIEW.]
RENI: We will report the chance of more monkeys.


[BOLO HOLDS UP ONE FIG. THE MONKEYS REENTER.]

TAVI: We have a new watch.
