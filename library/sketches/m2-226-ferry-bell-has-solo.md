# The Ferry Bell Has a Solo

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: ceremonial transit launches that give the ribbon and speech more attention than the actual departure*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-007*

Cast: Captain Sela, a macaque; Ceremonies Officer Odo, a gibbon; Deckhand Venn, a capuchin; and Passenger Miri, a gorilla.

EXT. FERRY PIER - MORNING

A small ferry waits at the dock. Odo holds a ribbon across the gangway. Sela checks the tide. Miri stands with a suitcase.

ODO: We will cut the ribbon, hear the bell, and then begin boarding.

MIRI: The ferry leaves at ten.

SELA: The tide is right now.

ODO: The speech begins at nine fifty.

VENN: The bell is already ringing.

[THE BELL RINGS ONCE. ODO RAISES A HAND FOR SILENCE. IT RINGS AGAIN.]

ODO: We have a bell cue.

SELA: That is the departure bell.

MIRI: Should I board?

ODO: Please wait for the ribbon.

INT. FERRY DECK - LATER

The ribbon has been tied to two posts. Venn holds scissors. Odo reads a long speech from a card.

ODO: This crossing links the eastern shore to the western shore and honors generations of water travel.

SELA: It is a four-minute ride.

MIRI: I can see the other dock.

ODO: We have a ceremonial journey ahead.

[THE TIDE PULLS THE RIBBON LOOSE. IT DRIFTS ONTO THE DECK.]

VENN: The ribbon has crossed first.

ODO: Please retrieve it. It is part of the ceremony.

MIRI: I can see the dock from the deck.

SELA: We can board now.

ODO: The welcome line has not been read.

MIRI: The welcome line is on the ticket.

[ODO CHECKS MIRI'S TICKET. THE WELCOME LINE IS PRINTED ON THE BACK.]

ODO: It says "Welcome aboard."

VENN: The passenger has read the line.

SELA: Then we are ready.

ODO: I will shorten the speech.

[ODO FOLDS THE SPEECH CARD IN HALF. THE LAST SENTENCE REMAINS VISIBLE.]

MIRI: I can board in the time it takes to read that line.

SELA: The ferry is leaving in two minutes.

ODO: The ribbon has not been cut.

MIRI: It has been released.

EXT. FERRY PIER - DEPARTURE

Sela starts the engine. Odo holds one end of the ribbon. Venn holds the other. Miri boards.

ODO: We will cut on three.

SELA: The tide will not wait for three speeches.

ODO: One, two-

[THE DEPARTURE BELL RINGS. VENN SNIPS THE RIBBON. IT FALLS INTO THE WATER AND FLOATS TOWARD THE OTHER DOCK.]

MIRI: The ferry is underway.

SELA: We are on schedule.

MIRI: The seats are open.

ODO: The ribbon is also underway.

SELA: That is the route.

[THE FERRY PULLS AWAY. THE RIBBON DRIFTS ALONGSIDE IT FOR A FEW FEET, THEN TURNS TOWARD THE DOCK.]

ODO: The ribbon has returned to the pier.

VENN: The ferry has not.

MIRI: Good. I have a ticket.

[THE BELL RINGS ONCE MORE. THIS TIME, EVERYONE BOARDS WITHOUT WAITING FOR A SPEECH.]
