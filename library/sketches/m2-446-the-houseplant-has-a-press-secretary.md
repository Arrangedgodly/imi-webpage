# The Houseplant Has a Press Secretary

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: corporate crisis communications and press briefings that assign a spokesperson before anyone knows what happened*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: SPOKESMONKEY, a baboon; GARDENER, a macaque; REPORTER, a gibbon; EXECUTIVE, an orangutan; INTERN, a capuchin.

[A corporate lobby. A potted fern sits beside a sign reading "NO COMMENT." The gardener holds a watering can.]

REPORTER: We are here to ask why the fern has been moved.

SPOKESMONKEY: The plant has issued no statement.

GARDENER: I moved it away from the vent. The leaves were drying.

EXECUTIVE: We should announce a strategic relocation.

REPORTER: It is four feet from its old position.

SPOKESMONKEY: The fern will answer questions through me.

[The fern drops a dry leaf.]

REPORTER: Is that a resignation?

SPOKESMONKEY: It is an unsolicited document.

GARDENER: It is a dead leaf.

INTERN: I can compost it after the briefing.

[The spokesperson places a tiny microphone in front of the fern. The fern does not move.]

SPOKESMONKEY: The plant is taking time to consider its response.

REPORTER: Does it have an opinion about the vent?

SPOKESMONKEY: It has not confirmed the vent's existence.

GARDENER: You can feel the hot air from here.

[The executive moves behind the fern, then steps back when its leaves brush his tie.]

EXECUTIVE: We may need a second plant to corroborate.

REPORTER: You have not asked the gardener.

SPOKESMONKEY: The gardener is not authorized to interpret leaf movement.

GARDENER: I water it every day.

[The spokesperson leans close to the fern. A leaf bends toward the watering can.]

SPOKESMONKEY: The plant appears to request comment from the can.

GARDENER: It needs water.

[The gardener waters the fern. A little soil spills over the rim. The executive hands him a towel.]

EXECUTIVE: Please avoid describing this as a spill.

REPORTER: It is visible on the table.

SPOKESMONKEY: We can confirm a small amount of soil has entered the public area.

GARDENER: I will wipe it up.

[The gardener lifts the pot and sees a damp patch beneath it. The fern's stand has a crack.]

GARDENER: The pot is leaking through the base. I can put it in a tray.

EXECUTIVE: Is the tray approved?

GARDENER: It is a saucer.

[He places the fern in the saucer. Water stops spreading. The reporter points the microphone toward the leaves.]

REPORTER: Does the fern have a final statement?

SPOKESMONKEY: The plant has expressed itself through improved posture.

GARDENER: It is upright because I moved it.

[The spokesperson removes the tiny microphone. The executive takes down the NO COMMENT sign and turns it around; the other side reads "PLEASE WATER."]

REPORTER: Is that the official statement?

INTERN: I will refill the can after the camera leaves.

SPOKESMONKEY: It is the gardener's instruction.

FINAL IMAGE: The fern rests in a saucer beside the open vent while the press microphone points at an empty chair.
