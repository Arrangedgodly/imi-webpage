# A Very Small Parade

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Grand civic parades, marching bands, and ceremonial grand marshals*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Parade marshal Tillo, a mandrill; drummer Senn, a macaque; flag bearer Rumi, a gibbon; announcer Pava, a capuchin; resident Grit, a gorilla.

[STAGE: A SHORT STREET WITH A ROPE BARRIER, THREE PAPER FLAGS, AND ONE DRUM.]

PAVA: Welcome to the annual Canopy Day parade, traveling from this lamp to that stump.
GRIT: That is six steps.
TILLO: We have a very distinguished route.
SENN: I brought the drum.
RUMI: I brought the flag.
PAVA: And our grand marshal is the oldest fig in the grove.
GRIT: Where is the fig?
TILLO: It is on the float.
[THE FLOAT IS A LOW CART CARRYING ONE FIG.]
GRIT: The cart is smaller than the rope barrier.
TILLO: The parade is intimate this year.
SENN: Shall I start the marching beat?
TILLO: Give us a ceremonial roll.
[SENN TAPS THE DRUM TWICE.]
PAVA: The procession begins.
RUMI: The flag is caught on the barrier.
TILLO: Pause for pageantry.
GRIT: The flag is tied to the rope.
RUMI: It is our tallest flag.
PAVA: The crowd is waving.
GRIT: I am holding the rope.
TILLO: Your support is appreciated.
SENN: The drum has rolled under the cart.
PAVA: The music is coming from beneath the grand marshal.
TILLO: A powerful sound system.
GRIT: It's just the drum.
SENN: I can get it.
[HE CRAWLS UNDER THE CART. THE FIG WOBBLES.]
TILLO: Protect the grand marshal.
RUMI: The grand marshal is a fig.
TILLO: It has served the community.
GRIT: The fig is rolling toward the finish line.
PAVA: We have a surprise sprint.
SENN: The band is ready.
[SENN TAPS A QUICK RHYTHM. THE CART ROLLS TWO FEET.]
TILLO: The parade has picked up speed.
RUMI: The flag is still tied.
GRIT: The rope is moving with us.
PAVA: The crowd is now part of the procession.
GRIT: There is no crowd.
PAVA: There is one very attentive resident.
TILLO: The route is complete.
GRIT: We moved the start line.
TILLO: We moved it forward.
SENN: The drum is free.
RUMI: The flag is loose.
PAVA: The grand marshal is at the finish.
[THE FIG FALLS INTO GRIT'S HANDS.]
GRIT: I caught it.
TILLO: A civic act of courage.
GRIT: I was holding the rope.
PAVA: The parade has honored teamwork.
SENN: May I eat the fig?
TILLO: After the closing ceremony.
GRIT: How long is that?
TILLO: Two taps.
[SENN TAPS TWICE.]
PAVA: The ceremony is complete.
GRIT: Excellent. I am hungry.
RUMI: The flag is still tied to the rope.
GRIT: Then the parade is still happening.

PAVA: We have a message from the reviewing stand.
GRIT: There is no reviewing stand.
TILLO: The lamp is reviewing us.
SENN: The lamp has a fine view.
RUMI: The flag is now wrapped around the drum.
SENN: It improves the sound.
PAVA: A patriotic muffling.
GRIT: The drum is quiet.
TILLO: The parade has entered its silent phase.
SENN: I can still tap with my elbow.

[THE LITTLE CART ROLLS BACK TO THE START WHILE THE FLAG AND ROPE FOLLOW BEHIND.]
