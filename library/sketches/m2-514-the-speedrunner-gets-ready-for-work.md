# The Speedrunner Gets Ready for Work

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: video game speedrun commentary, record attempts, and performers breaking ordinary routines into optimized moves*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: RUNNER, a gibbon; COMMENTATOR, a baboon; TIMER, a capuchin; ROOMMATE, an orangutan; REFEREE, a macaque.

[A bedroom. A backpack, one sock, and a lunchbox sit on the floor. The runner stands at a start line made of tape.]

COMMENTATOR: The task is simple: get ready for work and leave the room.

RUNNER: I have practiced the route.

TIMER: Timer set for sixty seconds.

[The referee raises a hand. The timer starts. The runner grabs the backpack, but one strap catches on the bedpost.]

COMMENTATOR: Early obstacle. The bedpost has a strong defensive position.

RUNNER: I can roll the bag off.

[The runner crouches. The roommate steps in and picks up the strap.]

ROOMMATE: You can lift it.

RUNNER: That is a slower route.

ACT ONE

[The runner puts on one sock while hopping toward the closet. The referee calls a pause.]

REFEREE: The sock is inside out.

RUNNER: It still counts as worn.

COMMENTATOR: A disputed technique.

[The runner flips the sock off, turns it right side out, and puts it back on. The timer rings. The runner reaches for the lunchbox, which is still on the floor.]

RUNNER: I lost time to the sock.

ROOMMATE: You have another foot.

[The runner grabs the lunchbox with one hand and opens the door. The backpack slides off the shoulder and lands inside the room.]

COMMENTATOR: The door has taken the backpack out of play.

RUNNER: It is still in bounds.

[The runner leans through the doorway and pulls the strap. The lunchbox slips from the other hand. The roommate catches it and places it in the bag.]

ROOMMATE: Inventory managed.

TIMER: Forty seconds.

[The referee checks the route card. It lists keys, lunch, coat, and exit. The runner has no keys.]

RUNNER: I can use the backup key.

[The roommate points to a hook by the door. The runner reaches for it, but the coat swings down and covers the keys.]

COMMENTATOR: The coat has hidden the objective.

[The runner lifts the coat, grabs keys, puts the coat on, and turns toward the door. The bag strap is tangled in the coat sleeve.]

ACT TWO

[The runner stops, untangles the strap, and places the bag on both shoulders. The referee confirms keys, lunch, coat, and both socks. The timer shows twelve seconds.]

RUNNER: Ready.

[The runner walks through the doorway. The commentator starts to announce a record, but the timer stops only when the runner closes the door.]

TIMER: One minute, eleven seconds.

RUNNER: That was not the practice time.

ROOMMATE: You forgot to start the timer on the last run.

RUNNER: Next attempt, I will put the keys in my pocket first.

ROOMMATE: And start the timer.

FINAL IMAGE: The runner checks the route card again while the roommate stands outside holding the starting tape.
