# The Umbrella Lending Library

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: public libraries, checkout rituals, and item-lending systems that cannot decide whether an umbrella is a book*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: LIBRARIAN, a baboon; BORROWER, a macaque; PAGE, a gibbon; WEATHER READER, an orangutan; CHILD, a capuchin.

[A library lobby. A rack holds six umbrellas. A sign says "PLEASE RETURN DRY."]

BORROWER: I need an umbrella. It is raining.

LIBRARIAN: Are you returning one?

BORROWER: I am borrowing one.

LIBRARIAN: That is less complicated.

[The borrower scans a small card attached to an umbrella. The scanner beeps twice.]

PAGE: It thinks the umbrella is a large-print novel.

LIBRARIAN: The system says "The Long Rain."

BORROWER: That is the weather.

WEATHER READER: Rain is expected through the afternoon.

LIBRARIAN: Then the title is accurate.

[The librarian opens the umbrella to check it. It is wet. Water splashes into the return bin.]

LIBRARIAN: This one is still checked out.

BORROWER: It is in the rack.

PAGE: The rack is outside the return line.

CHILD: The sign says return dry. How do I dry an umbrella in the rain?

[The staff consider this. The weather reader holds a towel over the bin; the towel immediately becomes wet.]

WEATHER READER: We need an indoor drying place.

LIBRARIAN: The book dryer is in the basement.

BORROWER: Do not put an umbrella in the book dryer.

[The page opens a closet. It contains one standing fan and a stack of dictionaries.]

PAGE: We can use the fan.

[They open the umbrella indoors. It catches the fan's breeze and turns inside out. The borrower holds the handle; the librarian steadies the frame.]

BORROWER: I came here to borrow it, not test it.

LIBRARIAN: The library does both.

[The page closes the umbrella carefully, then sets it in a drip tray. The scanner beeps once.]

PAGE: It has checked itself in.

LIBRARIAN: The system recognizes the tray as a return slot.

CHILD: Can I borrow one for my walk home?

LIBRARIAN: Yes. Choose a dry one from the top rack.

[The child takes an umbrella. The borrower tries to leave with another, but the scanner beeps.]

SCANNER: ITEM ALREADY CHECKED OUT.

BORROWER: I have not checked it out yet.

LIBRARIAN: The first one is still on your card.

[The borrower shows the first umbrella. It is folded under the arm, still dripping. The page hands over a paper towel and a tray.] 

BORROWER: Do I return it before leaving?

LIBRARIAN: It is not a book. Take it home and bring it back when it is dry.

WEATHER READER: The rain is stopping.

[Everyone looks through the window. Rain continues.] 

WEATHER READER: The rain is pausing in that one corner.

[The borrower signs the umbrella card with a damp pencil. The librarian gives them a towel for the handle, not the whole umbrella.]

BORROWER: I will bring it back dry.

LIBRARIAN: Or at least in the tray.

FINAL IMAGE: Two wet umbrellas rest in the drip tray while the child carries the dry one past the "RETURN DRY" sign.
