# The Circus Has a Serious Safety Briefing

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: circus spectacle and pre-show safety talks that compete with performers eager to demonstrate every stunt*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-013*

Cast: RINGMASTER, a baboon; ACROBAT, a gibbon; CLOWN, a macaque; RIGGER, an orangutan; USHER, a capuchin.

[A circus ring. The rigger stands beside a ladder and a low balance beam. The audience is seated.] 

RINGMASTER: Before the show, please keep clear of the rope line.

CLOWN: I can demonstrate where the rope line is.

RIGGER: Please remain behind it.

[The clown steps backward and bumps into the ladder. The rigger catches it.] 

RINGMASTER: That is why we have the briefing.

ACROBAT: The ladder was not part of my act.

CLOWN: It was part of mine.

[The usher walks the audience along the marked path. One child tries to run toward the ring. The clown points to the rope line and gestures for them to stop.] 

CLOWN: The line is your stop sign.

RINGMASTER: Excellent. That was safe and funny.

[The acrobat approaches the balance beam. The clown tosses a foam ball. It bounces toward the rope line. The usher catches it in a bucket.] 

USHER: Props stay inside the ring.

CLOWN: The ball was exploring.

RIGGER: It can explore after I secure the net.

[The rigger checks each knot. The ringmaster begins announcing the performance while the acrobat waits.] 

RINGMASTER: Prepare to witness a leap of astonishing height.

RIGGER: It is a low beam. The landing mat is in place.

ACROBAT: My trick is to step over a hoop without touching it.

CLOWN: That sounds achievable.

[The clown stretches a hoop across the beam. It rolls to the side. The acrobat does not begin.] 

ACROBAT: I will wait until the prop stops.

RINGMASTER: The suspense is extraordinary.

[The hoop stops against a mat. The rigger checks the floor and signals ready. The acrobat steps over the hoop, lands on the mat, and bows. The audience applauds.] 

CLOWN: I can do that.

RIGGER: You may do it with the hoop on the floor and no running.

[The clown steps over it, then trips on the foam ball he tossed earlier. The usher catches him by the sleeve before he falls.] 

CLOWN: I have learned the shape of the ring.

RINGMASTER: We have learned not to leave the ball near the path.

[The rigger returns the ball to a prop bin and folds the ladder. The usher keeps the rope line clear. The acrobat offers the clown a hand to the mat.] 

ACROBAT: One safe step at a time.

CLOWN: Can I make that sound more daring?

RIGGER: You can say it louder.


The ringmaster asks the clown to repeat the safety briefing for the late arrivals. The clown points to the rope line, shows where props are stored, and asks the audience not to enter the ring without a performer. The usher checks that the aisle is clear before the acrobat starts again.

CLOWN: The safest trick is the one you can explain.

RINGMASTER: And the funniest?

CLOWN: The hoop that stays on the floor.

The audience laughs. The rigger nods and keeps the ladder folded until the act needs it.

FINAL IMAGE: The rope line stays clear as the clown takes one careful step toward the ring.
