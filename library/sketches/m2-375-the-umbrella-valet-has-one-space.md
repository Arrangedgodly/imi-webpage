# The Umbrella Valet Has One Space

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury valet services that claim ownership of ordinary public space*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-011*

Cast: Tavi, a macaque valet; Reni, a capuchin guest; Bolla, a gibbon supervisor; Fenn, a lemur doorman.

EXT. HOTEL AWNING - RAINY DAY

[A SIGN READS UMBRELLA VALET. ONE SMALL STAND SITS UNDER THE AWNING. TAVI HOLDS A NUMBERED TAG.]

RENI: Could you hold my umbrella while I go inside?

TAVI: Certainly. Do you prefer covered storage or open display?

RENI: Just hold it.

TAVI: We have options.

Reni hands it over. Tavi closes the umbrella and tries to place it in the stand. It does not fit. The stand holds one umbrella, and another already occupies it.

TAVI: We are at capacity.

RENI: Then put them together.

TAVI: That would mix the reservations.

Bolla steps outside with a clipboard.

BOLLA: We can offer overflow storage.

RENI: Where?

Bolla points to the awning. Fenn holds the door open, and wind blows rain under the cover. Tavi proposes a premium stand with wider slots. It is still outside.

RENI: I only need to walk through the door.

TAVI: We can escort the umbrella.

Tavi walks toward the door carrying both umbrellas. The one he is holding opens in the gust. It catches the handle of Reni's umbrella and spins him around. Fenn lets go of the door to help. The door swings shut.

FENN: We need the door open.

BOLLA: The door service is separate from the umbrella service.

Fenn reopens it. Tavi tries to fold the umbrella. It snaps shut, trapping his tag between the ribs.

TAVI: It has checked in.

Reni takes the tag. It reads UMBRELLA 04, but the stand contains only one umbrella.

RENI: Whose is this?

Bolla checks the clipboard. The stand's umbrella has no owner.

BOLLA: It arrived before the valet opened.

FENN: That means it belongs to the hotel.

RENI: I think it's mine. I left it at the door yesterday.

Bolla asks Reni to identify a mark. Reni points to a strip of green thread wrapped around the handle. Tavi checks the other umbrella. It has a red ribbon.

TAVI: They are distinguishable.

BOLLA: The system calls both "dark umbrella."

Reni removes the green thread and ties it around the numbered tag. Fenn puts the tag on the correct handle. Tavi hands Reni the umbrella.

RENI: Thank you. Can I leave the other one here?

BOLLA: Yes. We have storage for found umbrellas.

RENI: What does that cost?

BOLLA: We charge only when someone retrieves it.

Fenn carries the unclaimed umbrella into the lobby. The wind catches it and opens it above his head, making a dry little tent.

TAVI: We should charge for indoor shade.

BOLLA: That's a different service.

[RENI WALKS THROUGH THE DOOR WITH THE UMBRELLA CLOSED. THE RAIN HAS STOPPED. FENN LOOKS UP AT THE OPEN UMBRELLA OVER HIS HEAD AND TAKES A NUMBER.]
