# The Cooking Contest's Timer Has a Sponsor

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: cooking competitions that turn timing and branded equipment into the main event*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Host Daro, a gibbon; contestant Miri, a capuchin; contestant Tern, a baboon; and judge Kessa, a macaque.

INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - DAY

Two contestants stand at separate counters. Daro holds a sponsor-branded timer. Kessa sits behind a judging table.

DARO: You have ten minutes to prepare a simple salad.

MIRI: What is the timer counting?

DARO: The sponsor's ten-minute freshness window.

TERN: The lettuce is already fresh.

[The timer starts. Its screen displays the sponsor logo instead of the numbers.]

KESSA: How much time remains?

DARO: The logo is still fresh.

INT. KITCHEN - MOMENTS LATER

Miri chops vegetables. Tern washes lettuce. Daro moves the timer between counters to keep it in frame.

MIRI: Please put the timer back.

DARO: The product needs visibility.

TERN: So does the cutting board.

[The timer blocks Tern's board. Tern moves it; the timer starts a sponsored alert.]

TIMER: Freshness update.

KESSA: Is that the time?

DARO: It is an update.

MIRI: The tomatoes are ready.

[The timer plays a jingle. Both contestants pause to look at it; Miri's cucumber rolls off the board.]

INT. KITCHEN - LATER

Tern assembles a salad. Miri reaches for dressing. The timer flashes a button: PRESS FOR BONUS ROUND.

KESSA: Is there a bonus round?

DARO: The timer says so.

MIRI: We are making salad, not racing the timer.

[The timer chirps again. Daro presses the button; a tiny flag rises from the timer and knocks over the dressing.]

TERN: We have a bonus spill.

KESSA: The dressing is on the floor.

DARO: Can we call it a dramatic drizzle?

INT. KITCHEN - FINAL MINUTE

Miri and Tern both ignore the timer and finish their salads. Daro tries to set it between them for a final product shot.

MIRI: The timer can sit by the plates.

TERN: It cannot sit on the plates.

[Daro props it on a lettuce leaf. The leaf bends and the timer tips into the dressing spill.]

TIMER: Freshness update.

KESSA: The timer is now dressed.

[Daro lifts it. A smear of dressing covers the sponsor logo.]

DARO: The brand is still visible.

MIRI: Only from the back.
INT. JUDGING TABLE - FINAL TASTE

Kessa samples both salads. The timer sits between the plates, still showing the logo.

KESSA: Miri's has cucumber, greens and tomato. Tern's has lettuce and radish.

DARO: Which contestant won the freshness window?

KESSA: The lettuce is fresh. The timer is not food.

[The timer announces the end of its sponsored period. A paper receipt emerges with a score printed on it: 10/10.]

MIRI: It gave itself a perfect score.

TERN: It was the only thing with a brand.

[Kessa turns the timer so its blank back faces the camera and takes another bite.]

KESSA: The salad wins.

DARO: Can the timer get a tasting shot?

[The machine keeps flashing its logo. Kessa places a lettuce leaf over the screen.]
