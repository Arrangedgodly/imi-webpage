# The Wildlife Reintroduction Ribbon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: wildlife rescue documentaries and feel-good release ceremonies*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-004*

Cast: RANGER ELLA, a macaque; DOCUMENTARIAN VEX, a gibbon; RESCUED SQUIRREL PIP, a squirrel monkey; and VOLUNTEER RO.

[A clearing. A small gate opens into a quiet grove. Vex positions a camera beside a ribbon.] 

VEX: We need Pip to emerge into the wild at the emotional peak.

ELLA: Pip has lived in the grove for three weeks.

PIP: I can leave whenever the gate is open.

RO: The gate is open.

VEX: Wait for my signal. We need the sunrise behind you.

PIP: The sun is behind the tree.

VEX: That is a symbolic sunrise.

ELLA: The release is not a performance. Pip can go when ready.

VEX: The audience wants a moment of transformation.

PIP: I have already transformed the feeding dish into a bed.

RO: The dish is still in the shed.

PIP: It was in the shed. I moved it.

[THE DISH CLINKS IN A LOW BRANCH.] 

VEX: Can we film you leaving the dish behind?

PIP: I use it for water.

ELLA: We can bring the dish to the grove until Pip knows where the spring is.

VEX: That is not a symbolic moment.

RO: It is a useful one.

[RO SETS THE DISH BY THE SPRING. PIP SIPS, THEN TURNS TOWARD THE TREES.] 

VEX: Wait. The ribbon is across the path.

ELLA: It is a hazard. Take it down.

VEX: It marks the release point.

PIP: I can step over it, but the small ones might not see it.

RO: I'll move it to the camera stand.

[RO UNTIES THE RIBBON AND TIES IT TO THE TRIPOD.] 

VEX: The camera is now the release point.

ELLA: The release point is the open grove.

PIP: I am going now.

[HE LEAPS TO A BRANCH, PAUSES, THEN LEAPS AGAIN.]

VEX: That was the moment. Did we record it?

RO: I recorded the dish moving and Pip finding the spring.

ELLA: Good. That is what happened.

VEX: We need a close-up of the gate opening.

ELLA: The gate is open already.

VEX: Can you close it and open it again?

PIP: If you close it, I will be on the grove side.

VEX: That's an even better shot. We can show the two sides of release.

ELLA: We are not moving Pip backward for a shot.

RO: We can film the gate from here while it stays open.

VEX: The audience will not know it is a gate.

PIP: It has a latch.

[HE TAPS THE LATCH. IT CLICKS.] 

RO: There is your sound cue.

VEX: I wanted the ribbon to snap.

ELLA: The ribbon was never part of the release. It was tied across the path for the camera.

PIP: It is still tied to the tripod.

VEX: The tripod is not wildlife.

ELLA: Exactly.

[RO UNTIES THE RIBBON. THE TRIPOD STANDS WITHOUT IT.] 

VEX: I can use the shot of the gate, the water dish, and the grove.

RO: And Pip choosing when to go.

VEX: That is the part the camera almost missed.

PIP: I didn't miss it.

ELLA: Nobody did.

VEX: Could we get one more take?

PIP: I am already in the grove.

VEX: The grove is small. Could you come back to the ribbon?

PIP: No.

ELLA: The release is complete.

[THE CAMERA TURNS OFF. A BIRD CALLS FROM THE GROVE.]
