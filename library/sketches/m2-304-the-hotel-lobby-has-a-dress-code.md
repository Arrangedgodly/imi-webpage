# The Hotel Lobby Has a Dress Code

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury hotel concierge service, scripted hospitality, and staff who treat every ordinary request as a bespoke experience*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-009*

Cast: Concierge Loma, a gibbon; guest Rell, a macaque; bellhop Tavi, a capuchin; and manager Odo, an orangutan.

INT. HOTEL LOBBY - EVENING

Rell enters carrying a damp umbrella. Loma greets him beside a sign: PLEASE ENJOY OUR WELCOMING AMBIENCE.

LOMA: Welcome. May I offer you a custom arrival?

RELL: I would like my room key.

LOMA: Of course. Would you prefer the brass key, the silver key, or the key presented on a velvet pillow?

RELL: The one that opens room twelve.

Tavi brings a tray with three keys. The brass key is for the pantry; the silver key is for the luggage room; the velvet pillow holds the room key.

TAVI: The pillow is very soft.

RELL: I will take the key.

Loma asks whether Rell would like a welcome beverage. He asks for water. Odo appears and describes the lobby fountain as a complimentary hydration feature. Rell drinks from the fountain.

ODO: We usually serve it in a glass.

RELL: The fountain was closer.

Tavi offers to carry the umbrella. It drips onto the carpet. Loma presents an umbrella stand that is already full.

LOMA: Our umbrella stand is at capacity.

RELL: Could we move one umbrella?

Tavi removes a closed umbrella. It opens, filling the lobby. Everyone ducks. Rell's wet umbrella rolls across the floor and stops beside the concierge desk.

TAVI: We have a spontaneous umbrella display.

ODO: We have a wet floor.

The manager brings a towel and wipes the carpet. Loma asks Rell whether he would like the towel folded into a swan. Rell uses it to dry the umbrella handle.

RELL: That works.

Loma checks the room key. It opens room twelve. Tavi offers to escort Rell upstairs. Rell says he can follow the signs. The signs point in two directions: one says GUEST ROOMS, another says GUEST ROOMS - SCENIC ROUTE.

RELL: Which route is shorter?

TAVI: The scenic route has paintings.

RELL: I would like the short route.

Loma turns the sign. Both arrows now point to the elevator. The elevator is waiting, with a luggage cart inside. Tavi pushes it out. Rell enters.

LOMA: Would you like us to send a personalized message to your room?

RELL: No.

LOMA: We will leave your preference blank.

The elevator doors close. Odo picks up the wet towel and folds it into a swan. It immediately droops.

TAVI: The swan needs a room key.

Odo uses the brass pantry key to prop the towel upright. Loma places it on the concierge desk, where it welcomes the next guest without speaking.

The next guest asks where the elevator is. Tavi points to the sign. The towel swan tips over, and Loma catches it before it hits the desk.

LOMA: Would you like a personalized direction?

GUEST: I would like the elevator.
