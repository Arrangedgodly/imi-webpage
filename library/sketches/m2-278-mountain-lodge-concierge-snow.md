# The Mountain Lodge Has a Concierge for Snow

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: luxury hospitality that packages ordinary weather as personalized service*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-008*

Cast: Concierge Seli, a gibbon; guest Odo, a baboon; guest Tavi, a macaque; and groundskeeper Kori, a capuchin.

INT. MOUNTAIN LODGE LOBBY - MORNING

Seli welcomes Odo and Tavi beside a window. Outside, snow falls. Kori stands near a shovel.

SELI: We offer a curated snow experience.

ODO: It is snowing.

SELI: We have three tiers: observe, stroll, and premium flurry.

TAVI: What is premium flurry?

SELI: A blanket, hot drink, and a reserved window.

[Odo points to the window. A guest is already standing there.]

ODO: The view is occupied.

SELI: I can reserve the next snowfall.

INT. LODGE - MOMENTS LATER

Seli gives Tavi a schedule: 10:00 observe snow, 10:15 stroll, 10:30 return indoors. Kori clears the path with a shovel.

TAVI: The snow is still falling.

SELI: Each flurry has its own itinerary.

KORI: The path is clear now.

[Snow slides off the roof and covers the path. Kori begins shoveling again.]

ODO: The weather has changed the schedule.

SELI: I will notify the snow desk.

TAVI: Who is at the snow desk?

SELI: The snow desk is me.

INT. LODGE - AFTERNOON

Seli sets up a premium flurry tray at the window. A draft blows the napkin across the room. Odo follows it; the reserved window is now clear.

SELI: Your view is ready.

[Odo sits. A snowplow passes and piles snow against the window, blocking the view.]

ODO: The view has closed.

SELI: I can offer a partial snowbank view.

KORI: That is the parking lot.

TAVI: It is still snow.

INT. LODGE HALLWAY - BEFORE FINAL SERVICE

Seli delivers the schedule to Odo. The paper has separate times for viewing, strolling, and sipping.

ODO: What if we do all three at once?

SELI: The tiers are designed to prevent overlap.

TAVI: The snow is falling during every time slot.

[Kori opens the door to clear the path. A gust blows snow into the lobby and dusts the schedule.]

SELI: The itinerary now includes indoor snow.

ODO: That is the premium tier.

[Kori brushes the schedule into a snow shovel.]

KORI: The shovel has reservations now.

SELI: It is booked for the next snowfall.
INT. LODGE LOBBY - FINAL SERVICE

Kori places the shovel by the door. Seli removes the schedule and sets mugs on a table. Odo and Tavi stand by the window, watching the flakes.

SELI: The snow is available to everyone.

ODO: I did not need a tier.

TAVI: The hot drink is nice.

[The roof releases another small load of snow onto the path. Kori picks up the shovel. Seli hands him a mug.]

KORI: Is this a premium shovel experience?

SELI: It is a complimentary reset.

[The guests help clear the path. The snow falls again, neatly filling the tracks.]
