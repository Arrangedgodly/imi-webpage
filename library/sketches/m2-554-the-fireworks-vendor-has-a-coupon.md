# The Fireworks Vendor Has a Coupon

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: infomercial sales pitches and event vendors trying to attach a discount to the wrong product*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-015*

Cast: VENDOR, a macaque; EVENT PLANNER, a gibbon; PYROTECHNICIAN, an orangutan; HOST, a baboon; CUSTOMER, a capuchin.

[A fairground booth. A banner advertises a fireworks show. The vendor holds a coupon reading TWO FOR ONE. The event planner checks a safety checklist.]

VENDOR: Buy one ticket, get one sparkler free.

PYROTECHNICIAN: That coupon is for bottled water.

VENDOR: It has the same blue logo.

HOST: It says "two bottles for one."

ACT ONE

[The customer buys a ticket and asks for the free sparkler. The vendor points to a case of water. The customer accepts the water, then asks if the fireworks are included.]

VENDOR: The show is included.

CUSTOMER: So the coupon is for drinks?

VENDOR: The coupon is for the feeling of a deal.

[The event planner asks the vendor to take down the fireworks banner from the water table. The vendor moves it, but it covers the exit arrow. The pyrotechnician carries the sign to a clear wall.]

PYROTECHNICIAN: Keep the route visible.

[The host rehearses a promotional announcement. The vendor feeds the coupon into a megaphone slot, thinking it is a microphone. The megaphone prints the coupon through a receipt opening.]

HOST: The microphone has issued a discount.

[The customer asks whether two tickets qualify for two bottles. The vendor reads the fine print: one coupon per purchase, water only. The event planner checks the inventory and sees only one bottle left. The vendor looks at the coupon, then at the case.]

VENDOR: We are out of the item the coupon covers.

CUSTOMER: Can I have the coupon anyway?

[The vendor hands it over. The customer offers it to the pyrotechnician, who uses the paper to mark the water table's location on a map. The host asks the event planner whether there is a second table. There is not.]

EVENT PLANNER: We have one table and one bottle.

[The vendor puts a new sign on the booth: TICKET INCLUDES SHOW. WATER SOLD SEPARATELY. The customer reads it and buys a bottle at the regular price.]

[The host checks the first-aid station and confirms the crowd is behind the viewing line. The vendor changes the sign to show the actual water price. The customer offers to buy a bottle for the pyrotechnician, who refuses until after the equipment is shut down.]

PYROTECHNICIAN: Water after the final check.

VENDOR: The coupon will still be invalid.

ACT TWO

[The fireworks show begins at the scheduled time. The vendor watches from behind the line. The customer shares the bottle with the host. The vendor tries to hand out the coupon again, but the pyrotechnician points to the safety line. The vendor folds it and puts it away.]

VENDOR: No free sparks?

PYROTECHNICIAN: Only the scheduled ones.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty water bottle sits beside the used coupon while the fireworks flash beyond the marked line.
