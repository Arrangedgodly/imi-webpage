# The Moving Finish Line

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: marathon broadcasts that treat every neighborhood obstacle as a record-breaking course adjustment*
*By: IMI Sketch Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-006*

Cast: Piri, a capuchin race announcer; Doma, a macaque course marshal; Miri, a gibbon runner; and Niko, a squirrel monkey volunteer.

EXT. GROVE RACE COURSE - MORNING

Piri holds a microphone at the starting line. A painted sign reads ONE LAP. Doma checks the route map. Niko rolls out a measuring tape.

PIRI: The runners face a simple loop around the grove.

DOMA: The tape says the loop is short by one tree.

PIRI: Then the race will gain a tree.

Niko moves the finish ribbon to the next trunk. Runners gather. Miri ties her bib around her waist, but it slips down to her knee.

MIRI: The bib is running faster than I am.

Doma secures it with a leaf pin. Piri starts the race. The runners make it to the first turn, where a gardener has placed a watering can on the path.

PIRI: An unexpected hydration station!

DOMA: It is a watering can.

Niko lifts it. A thin stream pours onto the track. Doma marks a dry detour around the puddle. Piri announces a new technical segment. Miri takes the detour and passes the leader, who is trying to run through the water without splashing.

The course reaches a low branch. Doma asks everyone to duck. Piri calls it the under-canopy tunnel. Miri ducks; the taller runners stop and wait for a course marshal to hold the branch. Niko raises the branch, and all runners pass safely.

PIRI: The field is bunching at the tunnel.

DOMA: The branch is not part of the race.

They approach the original finish tree. Piri notices the moved ribbon is several steps farther away. He asks Doma why.

DOMA: You said we needed another tree.

PIRI: I meant for the course map.

The runners continue to the ribbon. Miri crosses first, but the ribbon slips from Niko's hand and lands behind her. She pauses to help him retie it.

MIRI: Does the finish move if the ribbon moves?

Doma checks the painted line on the ground. Miri has already crossed it.

PIRI: Our winner has stopped to help the finish crew.

Miri waits while Niko ties the ribbon between two posts. The other runners reach the line. Doma declares the race complete. Piri conducts a finish interview.

PIRI: What did you think of the changing course?

MIRI: It changed when things needed moving.

PIRI: A daring strategy.

MIRI: I was following Doma.

Doma rolls up the tape. Piri measures the distance to the original starting line and discovers that the new finish is farther away than the full lap. He announces a bonus event: walking back to the start.

Miri and the runners return together. Piri sets the microphone down to join them. It rolls down the hill and crosses the painted finish.

NIKO: New course record.

Doma picks it up.

DOMA: The microphone is not registered.
