# The Laundromat Has a Play-by-Play Booth

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: sports commentary, championship broadcasts, and commentators assigning athletic stakes to routine laundry*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-014*

Cast: COMMENTATOR, a baboon; LAUNDRY OWNER, a gibbon; CUSTOMER, a macaque; RIVAL CUSTOMER, an orangutan; SCOREKEEPER, a capuchin.

[A laundromat. Two washers churn. A commentator sits at a folding table with a microphone. A small scoreboard reads SPIN CYCLE.]

COMMENTATOR: We are live for the final rinse. Customer One has selected warm water.

CUSTOMER: I pressed cold.

[The customer checks the machine. The dial has moved to warm.]

LAUNDRY OWNER: The dial is loose. I will fix it after this load.

COMMENTATOR: A late change in strategy.

[Rival Customer pushes a basket toward the next washer. One sock slips out and lands on the floor.]

RIVAL CUSTOMER: I lost a player.

SCOREKEEPER: It is a sock.

COMMENTATOR: The referee has not confirmed that.

[The laundry owner picks up the sock and returns it. The rival customer places it on top of the basket.]

ACT ONE

[The washers reach spin. They shake gently. The commentator rises and describes the motion.]

COMMENTATOR: Machine A moves to the left. Machine B counters with a heavy vibration.

LAUNDRY OWNER: It is balancing the load.

[Customer One adds a single towel to the washer. The machine stops, then resumes more quietly.]

COMMENTATOR: A substitution at the buzzer.

RIVAL CUSTOMER: That was the towel.

[The scorekeeper writes one point beside Customer One. The laundry owner erases it.]

LAUNDRY OWNER: Points are not awarded for towels.

[The dryers finish. Both customers reach for their loads at once. The baskets tangle handles. The commentator stands between them like a referee.]

COMMENTATOR: We have a contested basket.

CUSTOMER: Mine is the one with green stripes.

RIVAL CUSTOMER: That is mine.

[The scorekeeper checks the dryer labels. One basket has a green-striped towel; the other has green-striped socks.]

SCOREKEEPER: You both have green stripes.

[The laundry owner lifts the baskets apart. A red sock has caught on both handles, connecting them.]

LAUNDRY OWNER: The sock is the only thing on both sides.

COMMENTATOR: A neutral athlete.

[The commentator requests a replay. The owner refuses and points to the clear folding tables. The rival customer shakes out a pair of socks and catches one by the toe before it falls. The commentator calls it a clean catch, but the scorekeeper writes "ordinary laundry" on the board.]

ACT TWO

[The customers unhook the sock and sort their laundry on separate tables. The commentator asks for a final score. The scorekeeper turns over the board; the reverse says LOAD COMPLETE.]

SCOREKEEPER: The cycle finished. Nobody won.

COMMENTATOR: The machines performed evenly.

LAUNDRY OWNER: One dial needs repair. I will tag it out before the next load.

[The owner places a note on the loose dial. The commentator folds the microphone stand and moves it away from the washers.]

FINAL IMAGE: The scoreboard reads LOAD COMPLETE while the loose dial wears a tiny paper flag.
