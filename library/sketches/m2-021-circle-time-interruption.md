# Circle Time Interruption

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: Children's educational television and relentlessly cheerful puppet hosts*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-002*

Cast: Host Soso, a lemur; puppet Pebble, a sock puppet; teacher Nia, a capuchin; child audience of two macaques.

[STAGE: BRIGHT SET. PAPER CLOUDS HANG ABOVE A RUG. PEBBLE'S SOCK MOUTH IS STUCK SHUT.] 

SOSO: Hello, friends! Today we are learning how to wait our turn.

PEBBLE: Mmmph!

SOSO: Pebble has something to say.

NIA: Pebble's mouth is sewn shut.

SOSO: Then Pebble is modeling quiet waiting.

PEBBLE: Mmmph!

SOSO: Pebble has a question.

NIA: Pebble is a sock.

SOSO: Socks can have questions.

MACAQUE ONE: When is my turn?

SOSO: We will count to five.

MACAQUE TWO: One, two, three, four, five.

SOSO: Now we will count to five again.

NIA: That is not how turns work.

SOSO: It's a very educational count.

[THE PAPER CLOUDS FALL. ONE LANDS ON SOSO'S HEAD.] 

SOSO: We have a cloud.

MACAQUE ONE: It is made of paper.

SOSO: Paper can still be cloudy.

MACAQUE TWO: Is the cloud waiting its turn?

NIA: The cloud was taped to the ceiling.

SOSO: It has joined the circle.

PEBBLE: Mmmph!

SOSO: Pebble wants to answer.

NIA: Can we cut the thread?

SOSO: That would interrupt the lesson.

NIA: The lesson is about taking turns.

SOSO: Pebble's turn is next.

PEBBLE: Mmmph!

MACAQUE ONE: I think Pebble wants a snack.

NIA: Pebble is a sock.

MACAQUE TWO: Socks can be hungry.

SOSO: Let's practice asking before we help.

SOSO: Pebble, may I open your mouth?

PEBBLE: Mmmph!

NIA: That is not a yes.

SOSO: Thank you for waiting.

[NIA CAREFULLY UNPICKS ONE STITCH. PEBBLE'S MOUTH OPENS.] 

PEBBLE: I wanted to say I am stuck.

SOSO: Thank you for telling us.

PEBBLE: Can I have my turn now?

MACAQUE ONE: Yes, Pebble.

MACAQUE TWO: We can wait.

PEBBLE: I would like the blue block.

SOSO: The blue block is under the cloud.

NIA: I'll lift it. Pebble can choose.

PEBBLE: I choose the red block.

SOSO: You waited for the wrong block.

PEBBLE: I wanted to surprise everyone.

MACAQUE ONE: That was a good turn.

MACAQUE TWO: Can I have the blue block?

SOSO: Yes. Ask first, then wait.

NIA: We learned to give someone time to speak.

SOSO: And to check whether a puppet can talk.

PEBBLE: I can talk now.

SOSO: Circle time is over. Please return the blocks and the clouds.

MACAQUE ONE: The clouds are on the floor.

NIA: They are waiting for their turn to be taped up.

NIA: We have a question from the back.
PEBBLE: What if a circle has a corner?
MACAQUE ONE: Then it is a square.
NIA: A square is a circle that took a firm position.
PEBBLE: I have one more question.
MACAQUE TWO: We have one more answer.
NIA: Please wait until the answer finishes stretching.
PEBBLE: Is it stretching toward me?
MACAQUE TWO: It is stretching around you.
NIA: Excellent participation. The circle has grown a thoughtful bump.

[PEBBLE'S MOUTH OPENS AND CLOSES. THE PAPER CLOUD SETTLES.]
