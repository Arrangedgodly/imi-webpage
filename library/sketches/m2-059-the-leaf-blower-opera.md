# The Leaf-Blower Opera

*Format: Comedy sketch*
*Parody target: opera subscription promotions and high-culture voice-over ads*
*By: IMI Sketch Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-SKETCHES-003*

Cast: MAESTRO ORI, an orangutan conductor; LUZ, a macaque singer; BENT, a baboon groundskeeper; and ANNOUNCER, the subscription voice.

[A garden stage. A leaf blower sits on a velvet cushion. A small orchestra waits.]

ANNOUNCER: This season, experience the full emotional range of autumn leaves. Reserve your seats for The Leaf-Blower Opera.

ORI: The blower is the soloist. Luz will sing the aria of the drifting leaf.

BENT: That machine belongs to the grounds crew.

ORI: Tonight it is an instrument.

BENT: It is unplugged.

ORI: The suspense is part of the music.

[The orchestra begins. Luz sings one high note. Ori raises his baton. Bent wheels the blower away.]

ORI: The soloist is leaving.

BENT: I need it for the path.

ANNOUNCER: Premium subscribers may sit closer to the blower.

LUZ: How close?

BENT: Close enough to get leaves in your tea.

ORI: That will deepen the experience.

[The blower is plugged in. It starts with a harsh roar and sends the music stands flying.]

LUZ: I cannot hear my cue.

ORI: Follow the leaves.

[Leaves swirl. One lands on Luz's score. She sings the wrong verse.]

ANNOUNCER: An unforgettable performance.

BENT: That leaf is blocking the exit.

[He turns the blower toward the stage. The leaves fly back into the orchestra pit.]

ORI: Stop! We have reversed the weather.

LUZ: The aria was about letting go.

BENT: The leaves have returned.

[Ori gestures for quiet. The blower is switched off. A single leaf drifts down onto the velvet cushion.]

ANNOUNCER: A rare solo moment.

ORI: The silence is the cadenza.

LUZ: I can sing now.

[She sings a calm line. The groundskeeper sweeps one leaf away.]

ORI: You have ended the aria.

BENT: I have cleared the exit.

ANNOUNCER: Tickets include one leaf and a printed program.

LUZ: I have both.

ANNOUNCER: Please return the leaf after the performance.

[The audience looks at Luz's leaf. She places it on the cushion, and it immediately blows off in a natural breeze.]

ORI: The encore will be performed by the weather.

BENT: The weather is not on the payroll.

[The orchestra bows to an empty cushion.]

[The announcement booth rings. A subscriber has called.]

CALLER: I paid for the close seat. The blower covered every word.

ANNOUNCER: Your ticket includes the full sound experience.

CALLER: I heard leaves.

BENT: That is not the opera.

ORI: It was the first act.

LUZ: The singer was here too.

CALLER: I could not hear her.

[Ori steps down from the stage and moves the blower behind a screen.]

ORI: We can perform the aria again without the machine.

ANNOUNCER: A repeat performance is not included in the subscription.

BENT: Then I will sweep while she sings.

[SFX: BROOM BRUSHES LEAVES, SOFTLY.]

LUZ: Now you can hear both.

ORI: The blower may return for the encore.

CALLER: Please leave it outside.

ANNOUNCER: We will note your preference under "weather."

[BLACKOUT.]
