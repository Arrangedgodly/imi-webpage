# The Delivery to the Edge of the Map

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: epic courier adventures, perilous journeys, cryptic directions, and the package that must arrive before sunset*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
COURIER MIRI, a macaque
MAPMAKER VEX, a gibbon
POSTMASTER SULA, a ring-tailed lemur
FERRY PILOT ODO, an orangutan
RECIPIENT PIP, a capuchin

SCENE 1. INT. POST OFFICE - DAWN

A small parcel sits on a sorting table beside a map with a blank corner. Courier Miri signs for it. The address reads: "Pip, beyond the last bridge, near the tall tree, before sunset." Postmaster Sula says the tree was cut down years ago.

MIRI: Then I will find what replaced it.

SULA: A greenhouse.

Miri folds the map and puts it in her satchel. Vex, the mapmaker, offers to guide her. His map contains a legend, four north arrows, and a note that says the river moves seasonally.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIVER CROSSING - MORNING

Miri and Vex reach the ferry. Odo says the crossing is closed until he repairs the ramp. Vex points to a dotted line on his map and calls it a land route. Odo says the dotted line is the ferry route.

Vex insists they stop to inspect a marker stone shaped like an arrow. He turns it three times, then realizes it is a bench with a chipped arm. Miri checks the package seal and asks whether the detour changed anything. Vex says it has confirmed the stone is not a marker. They resume walking, but Vex keeps a rubbing of the bench's scratches as evidence.

They carry the parcel along the riverbank to a footbridge. Vex reads an old sign that says "Bridge Closed for Repairs." It is a notice from last year, folded so the word CLOSED is hidden. Miri checks the boards and crosses only after testing each plank. Vex follows, narrating the journey as if the bridge were collapsing. One board squeaks but remains firm.

SCENE 3. EXT. ORCHARD PATH - AFTERNOON

A delivery cart rolls toward them from the upper path, carrying three unmarked parcels. Miri catches the cart and checks each label. One parcel is addressed to the post office, another to the greenhouse, and the third to a bakery. Vex wants to follow the cart's track as a clue. Miri sends the bakery parcel back with the cart owner and keeps her own delivery sealed. Vex updates his map with a note: 'A rolling cart is not necessarily a guide.'

They find a path splitting into three. Vex consults the map. One route ends at a hill, one at a gate, and one at a cluster of trees. Miri asks a gardener tending the orchard. The gardener says Pip lives near the greenhouse at the end of the paved path. Vex says there should be a more heroic clue.

MIRI: The person who lives here knows where the house is.

The gardener points to a small sign: COMMUNITY GREENHOUSE. Vex studies it as if it were a cipher, then asks whether the sign has always been there. The gardener says it was painted last spring. Miri thanks them and turns toward the door, while Vex writes down the paint color for his map notes. Miri walks toward it. Vex stops to compare the sign with the map. He finds a tiny mark showing the greenhouse, drawn upside down at the edge of the page. He turns the map around and follows.

SCENE 4. EXT. COMMUNITY GREENHOUSE - LATE AFTERNOON

Pip is repairing a watering can. Miri hands over the parcel. It contains a new nozzle ordered from the hardware co-op. Pip is relieved; the old one has been spraying water onto the path. He installs the replacement, and the hose sends a neat arc into the vegetable beds.

Vex asks whether the parcel contained a secret. Pip says the secret was the correct part number. Miri gets Pip to sign the delivery pad. The sunset is still an hour away. Vex proposes that they take the scenic route back. Miri says the parcel is delivered and she still has a route sheet to return.

SCENE 5. INT. POST OFFICE - EVENING

Sula stamps the receipt and updates the map. The blank corner now has a small greenhouse symbol, a current bridge note, and one north arrow. Vex asks to preserve the old map as an epic artifact. Miri says they can keep it in the drawer marked OLD MAPS.

Pip arrives with a basket of vegetables for the post office. The new nozzle worked. Sula places the vegetables on the counter, but one tomato rolls toward the open door. Miri catches it with her delivery satchel.

FINAL IMAGE: The parcel's old address label hangs beside the updated map, while a single clear route joins the post office to the greenhouse.
