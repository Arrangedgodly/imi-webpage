# The Great Aviary Blackout

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster blockbusters, countdowns, impossible control-room maps, and the reluctant hero who knows the building's actual switches*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
MARA, a capuchin electrician
PIP, a young macaque intern
DIRECTOR KETT, a gibbon administrator
AUNTIE SULA, a ring-tailed lemur caretaker

SCENE 1. INT. AVIARY CONTROL ROOM - NIGHT

A thunderstorm rattles the glass roof. The aviary's lights flash, then go out. An emergency screen turns red and displays a dramatic countdown: 00:59:59. Director Kett reaches for a microphone while Mara opens a cabinet of fuses.

KETT: Attention, residents. We are experiencing a historic darkness.

MARA: The grid is out. The backup switch is behind that panel.

KETT: If I announce a historic darkness, everyone stays in the lobby.

PIP: The lobby is dark too.

The emergency speakers play a trumpet sting. The screen changes to 00:59:58. Kett points at it as if the number has personally challenged him.

SCENE 2. INT. AVIARY HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Sula leads visitors toward the ground-floor shelter with glow sticks. A flock of parrots repeats the countdown at the wrong speed. Pip carries a box of flashlights but keeps turning each one toward his face to check if it works.

SULA: Keep the birds away from the glass dome until the backup lights come on.

PIP: The birds are going where they usually go.

SULA: That's why I brought the glow sticks.

The parrots seize the sticks and arrange themselves into an arrow pointing at the snack kiosk. Visitors follow. Kett rushes after them, announcing an orderly evacuation while walking directly into a hanging rope perch.

KETT: This is a controlled retreat.

SULA: You are retreating into the souvenir net.

SCENE 3. INT. GENERATOR ROOM - NIGHT

Mara finds water dripping onto the generator. She closes the inlet valve, but the stiff wheel turns only halfway. Pip braces his feet against the floor and pulls. The valve squeals; a sleeping owl drops from a beam and lands on the instruction manual.

MARA: Don't pull harder. Push the wheel back, then lift the pin.

PIP: The dramatic countdown says we have fifty-two minutes.

MARA: The countdown began at an hour and loses one second every second. It is behaving correctly.

Pip lifts the pin. The wheel turns. The generator coughs, spins, and dies again. Kett arrives with a paper map showing a red arrow marked EMERGENCY HERO ROUTE.

KETT: Take the route. It leads to the main breaker.

MARA: That's a mural of the route. The breaker is painted on the wall.

KETT: It looked more useful in the planning meeting.

SCENE 4. INT. SERVICE CORRIDOR - LATER

Mara leads Pip and Sula through a narrow corridor to a manual battery bank. Kett follows, trying to keep his microphone working. A group of parrots follows him, repeating his words back with increasingly severe echoes.

KETT: We remain calm.

PARROTS: We remain calm. We remain calm. We remain CALM.

MARA: The battery bank can power the ventilation fans or the stage lights, not both.

SULA: The birds need air. The stage can be dark.

KETT: The emergency broadcast needs a visual.

PIP: We can use the flashlight.

Kett resists, then hands Pip the microphone and takes a flashlight. When Pip announces the fan switch, the parrots repeat the directions perfectly. The residents move to the safe hallway in a single, quiet line.

SCENE 5. INT. GENERATOR ROOM - NIGHT

Mara notices that the generator's intake is blocked by a banner from the aviary's annual night gala. Pip and Sula pull it clear while Kett holds a lantern. The fabric slides free, revealing a nest of twigs packed into the grate.

SULA: The finches have been improving the ventilation.

MARA: Their nest has covered the air intake.

PIP: Can we move the nest?

SULA: The parents are at the shelter. We'll carry it with them.

They transfer the nest into a padded carrier. Mara resets the breaker. The generator starts and the ventilation fans hum back to life. The screen reaches 00:00:01, pauses, then changes to 00:00:00 with a faint beep.

KETT: We have survived the final second.

MARA: We fixed the intake.

KETT: You may say that in the technical cut.

SCENE 6. INT. AVIARY HALL - DAWN

The lights return one section at a time. The parrots have arranged glow sticks around Kett's abandoned map, making the painted emergency route shine like a runway. Visitors applaud as Mara restores the fans and Sula returns the finches' nest to a quiet perch.

KETT: This was a team effort. I held the lantern.

PIP: You held it facing the wall.

KETT: The wall needed reassurance.

Mara flips off the dramatic countdown monitor. It powers down with a trumpet fanfare, then displays a smiling sun. The flock tries to repeat the fanfare but can only manage the last two notes. Kett asks if the system can keep the countdown screen for future emergencies. Mara removes its fuse.

FINAL IMAGE: One parrot switches on a tiny reading lamp, squints at the control panel, and says, "Where is the off switch?"
