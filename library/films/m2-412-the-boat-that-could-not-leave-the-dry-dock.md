# The Boat That Could Not Leave the Dry Dock

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: launch-day adventure films, ceremonial countdowns, and vessels delayed by last-minute inspections*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
CAPTAIN LUMA, a gibbon
PIP, a capuchin builder
VEX, a macaque sponsor
NARI, a lemur harbor master
THE BOAT, a silent performer

SCENE 1. EXT. DRY DOCK - MORNING

A new boat rests on wooden supports. A banner reads FIRST VOYAGE AT NOON. Captain Luma stands at the bow. Pip checks the ropes.

VEX: We begin the countdown at eleven fifty-five.

NARI: The boat is still on the blocks.

PIP: We need to remove them.

Vex says the launch ceremony should begin before the boat moves. Nari asks which crew will move it. Vex points to the builders.

Luma asks Pip to check the stern. He knocks on the hull. It answers with a hollow thunk. Vex begins the sponsor speech. The boat shifts slightly on its blocks, and Vex steps backward off the platform.

VEX: It is ready.

NARI: It moved.

PIP: It is settling.

Vex asks for a photo beside the bow. The banner blocks the boat's name. Luma pulls the banner aside. It reveals "THE WAITING ROOM," the name Vex chose for the vessel.

LUMA: We are naming it after the dock?

VEX: It has been waiting here.

SCENE 2. INT. DRY DOCK OFFICE - LATER

Pip reads the inspection sheet. Every line is checked except "hull meets water." Nari says it cannot be checked until the boat is launched.

VEX: We cannot launch until it's checked.

LUMA: Then we cannot leave the dock.

Pip brings a bucket of water. He pours it onto the hull. The water runs down the side and collects in a drain.

PIP: It has met water.

Nari says the rule means the hull must float. Vex asks if they can float the boat in the office. Luma points to the door, which is too narrow for the boat.

Nari measures the door and the hull. The boat is wider by three feet. Vex asks whether they can turn it sideways. Pip rotates the miniature model on the desk. It still does not fit.

LUMA: The model fits.

PIP: The boat does not.

Vex asks Nari to certify the model. Nari writes "model fits" on a separate line. Vex tries to place the signature beside the hull test. Nari moves the form away.

NARI: A signature cannot change the measurement.

PIP: We can lower it into the dock.

VEX: The dock is the place where it is.

Luma says the supports can be moved when the tide rises. Pip demonstrates by pulling a short wooden block from beneath a miniature boat. It tips onto its side.

VEX: That is not the demonstration.

PIP: It is what happens when we remove one support.

Nari orders him to replace the block. The miniature boat rights itself.

SCENE 3. EXT. DRY DOCK - NOON

The crew prepares to lower the supports. Vex starts the countdown.

VEX: Ten, nine, eight.

Pip removes a wedge. The boat shifts, but the other blocks hold.

VEX: Seven, six.

Nari checks the tide. The water is low. The boat cannot reach the channel.

NARI: The tide comes in at three.

LUMA: We can wait.

VEX: The banner says noon.

Pip places a chalk mark beside the waterline. The boat's shadow reaches the mark before the water does. Vex asks if they can launch on the shadow.

NARI: The shadow will not float.

VEX: It has the same shape.

Luma asks him to hold the rope while the crew waits. Vex wraps it around his waist for the official photo. Pip pulls the stern rope to straighten the boat, turning Vex toward the harbor.

PIP: You are facing the correct direction now.

Nari asks them to change the banner. Vex says the sponsors have already printed programs. Pip turns the banner around. The back is blank.

PIP: We can write "at high tide."

SCENE 4. EXT. DRY DOCK - AFTERNOON

The tide rises. The boat floats in the cradle. Nari checks the hull. Water reaches the marked line. She signs the inspection sheet.

VEX: We can launch.

PIP: The cradle is still attached.

Nari unhooks it. The boat glides forward. The banner catches on the mast and tears. Vex grabs it before it falls into the water.

LUMA: The boat has left the dock.

VEX: The ceremony is incomplete.

PIP: The boat is complete.

Vex asks for one more photograph. The torn banner hangs from the mast like a sail. A puff of wind carries the boat farther from the cradle. Nari reaches for a mooring rope, but Luma lifts the oar and steers.

LUMA: We can take the picture from the pier.

VEX: The sponsor logo is backwards.

PIP: The boat is moving forward.

SCENE 5. EXT. HARBOR - SUNSET

The crew brings the boat alongside the pier. Pip loops the rope over a cleat, and Vex reads the sponsor names from the torn banner. The boat rocks gently. Luma asks where they will sail first.

NARI: The boat needs a safety check.

PIP: It has one.

Nari points to the paperwork. It still says "first voyage at noon." She crosses out the time and writes "first voyage at high tide."

Luma unties the rope. Pip takes the oars. Vex holds the torn banner so it can serve as a small sail. Nari signs the departure line while the boat is already moving.

FINAL IMAGE: The boat sets out as the sun lowers. Vex waves from the dock with half the banner, while the other half floats behind the boat like a small sail.
