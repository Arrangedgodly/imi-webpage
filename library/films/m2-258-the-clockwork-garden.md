# The Clockwork Garden

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: steampunk adventure films, where an elaborate mechanical irrigation clock is built to water one flower bed*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: An inventor races to unveil a clockwork irrigation system before sunset, while the gardeners keep asking it to do the one thing it was designed for.

Cast: Inventor Sava, gibbon; gardener Kito, macaque; apprentice Piri, capuchin; presenter Voss, baboon.

SCENE 1. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, AFTERNOON

A brass clock with gears and pipes stands beside a dry flower bed. Voss faces a camera.

VOSS: Today, innovation will bring water to every flower.

KITO: The hose is right there.

SAVA: The hose is not automated.

PIRI: It reaches the whole bed.

Sava winds the clock. Gears turn. A small bell rings, but no water comes out.

[SFX: CLOCKWORK, BELL.]

VOSS: The machine has awakened.

KITO: The water has not.

Sava adjusts a lever. A valve opens, sending a thin stream onto the path.

PIRI: It is watering the stone.

VOSS: The stone is part of the garden.

KITO: It is the path.

SCENE 2. EXT. GARDEN BED, CONTINUOUS

Piri follows the pipe and finds a kink under a flowerpot. She lifts the pot. The pipe springs free and sprays Sava's sleeve.

[SFX: WATER SPRAY.]

SAVA: The system is primed.

KITO: Your sleeve is wet.

VOSS: The inventor has made the first sacrifice.

Sava turns a wheel to redirect the pipe. The gears click, and water sprays the opposite side of the bed. Kito moves the flowerpots into the stream.

KITO: Now the flowers are watered.

SAVA: The clock did it.

PIRI: We moved the flowers.

Voss asks for a close-up of the rotating gears. One gear catches a leaf and stops.

SAVA: The mechanism has encountered an obstacle.

KITO: The obstacle is a leaf.

SCENE 3. EXT. TOOL SHED, LATER

Sava replaces the gear with a wider one. Piri reads the instruction plate. It says "turn slowly after filling reservoir."

PIRI: The reservoir is empty.

SAVA: We have been turning the clock without water.

KITO: It is a clock.

PIRI: It is also an irrigation system.

Voss wants a dramatic reveal. Kito fills the reservoir with a watering can. The clockwork system begins ticking. A small arm swings toward the flower bed.

[SFX: TICKING, METAL ARM.]

VOSS: The garden is about to receive its future.

The arm stops halfway.

Sava checks the pipe connections before starting the system. One joint leaks onto the path, so Piri tightens the collar with a wrench. The clock stops ticking while Sava works.

VOSS: The machine has paused for repairs.

KITO: The machine has a leak.

PIRI: I can hear the water.

Sava catches the drip in a cup and pours it onto the flower bed. The plants get a little water before the main cycle begins.

SAVA: Now the system can run.

VOSS: The garden has survived the interruption.

SAVA: The counterweight is too light.

KITO: We can tie a stone to it.

SAVA: That would be a field adjustment.

KITO: The flower bed is a field.

They attach a smooth stone. The arm swings fully and opens the valve. Water flows onto the bed. The bell rings again.

VOSS: We have achieved automated watering.

PIRI: For this one bed.

Before the final cycle, Piri notices water pooling near one plant. Kito shifts the pipe and clears a blocked hole. The stream moves evenly across the row.

PIRI: The system was watering one flower too much.

SAVA: I can add another gear.

KITO: The hole was blocked by soil.

SAVA: Then the design includes maintenance.

VOSS: A dramatic vulnerability.

Kito wipes the soil from the pipe. Sava resets the timer to one minute and watches each flower receive water.

SCENE 4. EXT. FLOWER BED, SUNSET

The clock ticks as the bed receives water. Voss narrates the first cycle. Sava watches the gears. Kito checks the soil with a finger.

KITO: The soil is damp.

SAVA: The timer will stop in three minutes.

KITO: The plants need water, not three minutes.

SAVA: The clock needs three minutes.

Piri turns the valve off early. The bell rings.

VOSS: The system has completed its cycle.

PIRI: I stopped it.

SAVA: The cycle is adjustable.

KITO: Then adjust it for the bed.

Sava changes the stop pin. The clock now runs for one minute. The water reaches the roots without flooding the path.

SCENE 5. EXT. GARDEN, DUSK

Voss announces the unveiling. The flowers stand upright. The clock ticks softly.

VOSS: A triumph of gears, valves, and vision.

KITO: And a hose.

SAVA: The hose filled the reservoir.

PIRI: The stone held the counterweight.

VOSS: Then a triumph of collaboration.

The bell rings once. Water reaches each root and stops at the garden edge. Voss steps onto the path for a heroic close-up; the counterweight lowers gently and rings the bell above him.

VOSS: The garden has applauded.

KITO: It has finished watering.

SAVA: The timer did its job.

Piri hangs a small sign on the hose: PLEASE MOVE SOIL FROM THE OUTLET. Voss turns it around so the warning faces the audience.

VOSS: The machine has a sequel.

[SFX: CLOCK TICK, WATER STOPS.]
