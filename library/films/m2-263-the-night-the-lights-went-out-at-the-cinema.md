# The Night the Lights Went Out at the Cinema

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble disaster films, where a power outage interrupts the premiere and every department insists its job is essential*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: When a cinema loses power before the premiere, the staff race to save the screening while arguing over which emergency plan takes priority.

Cast: Manager Kito, macaque; projectionist Sava, gibbon; usher Piri, capuchin; electrician Odo, baboon.

SCENE 1. INT. CINEMA LOBBY, NIGHT

Guests gather under the marquee. The lights go out. The lobby falls dark except for a glow from the exit signs.

[SFX: POWER CLICKS OFF, CROWD MURMUR.]

KITO: Stay calm. We are experiencing a brief interruption.

PIRI: The film starts in five minutes.

SAVA: The projector needs power.

ODO: So does the lobby.

KITO: The emergency lights are on.

PIRI: The popcorn machine is off.

KITO: That is not an emergency.

PIRI: The popcorn is part of the event.

Sava says the projector's backup battery can run for ten minutes. Odo says the building circuit tripped, likely because the lobby warmer and popcorn machine were on together.

KITO: Can you fix it?

ODO: I can reset the breaker.

SAVA: Do not reset it until we know what caused the overload.

SCENE 2. INT. ELECTRICAL ROOM, CONTINUOUS

Odo opens the panel. Kito follows, holding a flashlight. Piri remains in the lobby to keep guests calm.

ODO: The circuit is overloaded.

KITO: Reset it.

ODO: The load is still connected.

KITO: Turn off the popcorn machine.

ODO: It is already off.

KITO: Then the warmer.

Odo disconnects the warmer. The breaker still will not reset. He finds a portable heater plugged into the same strip.

ODO: Someone added a heater.

KITO: It is cold in the lobby.

ODO: It is warming the posters.

Sava arrives with the projector's battery. She asks if they can start the film without lobby lights.

KITO: The guests need to find their seats.

PIRI (OVER RADIO): They are using phone lights. The audience is seated.

KITO: The heater can wait.

SCENE 3. INT. LOBBY, MOMENTS LATER

Odo resets the breaker. The lobby lights return. The marquee flickers, then goes dark again.

[SFX: LIGHTS HUM, THEN FAIL.]

KITO: The power is back.

ODO: Only in this circuit. The marquee is separate.

PIRI: The guests think the show is canceled.

KITO: Tell them the screening is beginning.

SAVA: The projector has four minutes of battery left.

Odo checks the exterior panel. A loose cable to the marquee has disconnected. He asks Kito to hold the flashlight while he reconnects it.

KITO: I am the manager.

ODO: You are holding the flashlight.

KITO: I am managing the light.

Piri goes outside and turns the marquee switch off to prevent a short. Odo secures the cable. The sign lights up.

SCENE 4. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, NIGHT

Sava starts the film on battery power. The title card appears. The projector fan slows.

SAVA: We have three minutes.

KITO: How long is the opening?

SAVA: Four minutes.

KITO: Can we skip the first minute?

SAVA: It introduces the characters.

PIRI: The audience knows the actors.

KITO: We cannot skip the story.

The power circuit comes back. The projector's battery is still connected, so Sava switches it over. The film continues without a pause.

[SFX: PROJECTOR HUM RETURNS.]

PIRI: The audience is watching.

KITO: The premiere is saved.

SAVA: The film is only at the opening credits.

KITO: That is where premieres begin.

Before the guests leave, Piri asks whether the emergency lights should remain on. Odo says the backup battery is nearly empty. Sava suggests turning off the marquee so the lobby circuit can cool down.

KITO: The sign is part of the cinema.

ODO: The sign is outside.

PIRI: The guests can find the door without it.

Kito switches the sign off. The lobby lights stay on.

SAVA: The building is stable.

KITO: The premiere is stable.

ODO: The heater is still unplugged.

KITO: The lobby can be cold.

SCENE 5. INT. CINEMA LOBBY, LATER

The film ends. The guests leave. Odo has unplugged the heater and popcorn warmer. Kito turns them on one at a time. The popcorn machine works; the heater trips the circuit.

ODO: The heater is faulty.

PIRI: The popcorn survived.

KITO: The lobby is warm enough.

SAVA: It is still cold.

Kito hands out blankets from the lost-and-found. Guests wrap themselves as they leave.

KITO: The emergency plan worked.

ODO: We followed it.

KITO: And the popcorn?

PIRI: It is warm.

SAVA: The audience is warm.

Kito checks the breaker. The lights remain on. The marquee glows over the empty entrance.

[SFX: SIGN BUZZES STEADILY.]

KITO: Next premiere, no heater.

PIRI: Next premiere, two popcorn machines.

ODO: One circuit.

PIRI: One machine, then.

[SFX: CINEMA DOORS CLOSE, PROJECTOR FAN FADES.]
