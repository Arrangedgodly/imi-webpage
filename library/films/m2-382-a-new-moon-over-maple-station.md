# A New Moon Over Maple Station

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: lunar thrillers, eerie station announcements, mysterious shadows, and space crews who confuse routine maintenance with a cosmic warning*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
ENGINEER LUMA, a lemur
COMMANDER NARI, an orangutan
SIGNAL TECH PIP, a capuchin
VISITOR VEX, a macaque
STATION ROBOT ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. MAPLE ORBITAL STATION - NIGHT

A new moon rises outside the station window. A warning tone sounds. The lights dim. Engineer Luma checks the power panel and sees a routine maintenance cycle. Commander Nari asks the crew to stay at their stations. Signal tech Pip hears a voice through the speaker.

ANNOUNCEMENT: Please clear the west corridor.

PIP: It knows we're here.

Odo the station robot rolls past carrying a mop and a caution sign.

SCENE 2. INT. WEST CORRIDOR - LATER

The crew follows Odo. A long shadow moves along the wall. Vex appears at the far end in a silver suit, carrying a suitcase. Luma says the shadow is from the station's rotating solar panels. Vex has arrived for a visit but was delayed by the airlock.

NARI: Why is the corridor clear?

ODO: Cleaning.

Vex asks whether he has missed a crisis. Pip says the station is running a maintenance cycle. Vex points to the dim lighting. Luma shows him the panel; it lowers the lights to save power during the orbit's dark side.

SCENE 2A. INT. STATION GALLEY - NIGHT

Vex unpacks a meal while Pip checks the station's audio log. The repeated announcement begins again. The crew follows it to the corridor, where Odo has placed three caution signs. One sign faces the wrong direction, so it appears to warn people away from the control room. Luma turns it to face the wet floor.

NARI: The message is about a mop.

VEX: It sounded urgent.

ODO: It is important not to slip.

Pip checks the station clock and sees that the announcement repeats every time the cleaning cart reaches the west corridor. The cart sensor is triggering the recording. Odo asks if the system can announce "cleaning in progress" instead. Luma writes the new wording on a note for the control room; the current cleaning cycle is still active.

SCENE 3. INT. CONTROL ROOM - MIDNIGHT

The current announcement repeats once more. Pip isolates the audio channel and confirms the cart sensor is triggering the recording. The west corridor is blocked because Odo is polishing the floor. A timer counts down to the cycle's end. Vex asks if they should override it. Nari says the crew should not walk on a wet floor. When the cycle ends, Luma installs the new wording, "cleaning in progress," and tests it with the cart. No warning tone plays.

The station's rotation brings the moon into view. Its surface glows blue. Pip says the moon is broadcasting. Luma checks the telescope and traces the blue edge to the station's own beacon, still aimed along the old orbit path. Nari orders one correction, toward the docking lane. Luma turns it once; the glow leaves the moon and the docking computer records the new angle.

SCENE 3A. INT. CONTROL ROOM - MIDNIGHT

Vex asks for a second adjustment to make the glow return for his camera. Luma refuses to point the beacon back at the window. Pip shows the navigation log: the corrected beam marks the station for passing craft and stays clear of the visitor's flight path. The moon remains dark while the station rotates. Vex tilts his camera toward the window, then lowers it when the empty blue lens flare appears only on his monitor.

NARI: We have one cause and one correction.

VEX: The audience will want a reveal.

ODO: The floor is dry. That can be the reveal.

Pip schedules the next beacon check on the maintenance board instead of repeating the announcement. Vex films the before-and-after monitor: blue reflection, then plain moon. He labels the first shot "our own beacon" before Nari lets him keep it.

SCENE 4. INT. WEST CORRIDOR - LATER

The floor dries. Odo removes the caution sign. Vex walks through carrying his suitcase. He slips slightly on the clean surface but catches himself on the handrail. He asks whether the station can add more lighting. Luma changes the power schedule so the corridor brightens when someone enters.

Pip checks the signal monitor. The voice has stopped. The station computer confirms the cleaning cycle is complete. Nari writes a note to the crew: dim lights are normal during orbit night, but corridor alerts should name their source.

SCENE 5. INT. OBSERVATION WINDOW - DAWN

Vex watches the moon set behind the station and records the corrected beacon angle in his travel log. Pip files the maintenance note beside the route chart. Odo rolls the mop back to its closet. The cleaning cart passes the west corridor without triggering another urgent announcement.

FINAL IMAGE: The station lights brighten as the sun rises, and the moon slips out of view without sending another announcement.
