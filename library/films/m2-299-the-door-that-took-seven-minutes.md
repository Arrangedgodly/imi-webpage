# The Door That Took Seven Minutes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: slow art-house dramas, long static takes, cryptic silences, and filmmakers who treat an ordinary door as an unsolved metaphor*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A quiet film crew spends an entire afternoon observing a door that will not close, until the building's primate residents turn its seven-minute struggle into a practical solution.

Cast
- DIRECTOR OMI, a capuchin who shoots long unbroken takes.
- SOUND ARTIST VEX, a macaque who records silence with a boom mic.
- CARETAKER LUMA, a gibbon who can reach the top hinge.
- TENANT PIP, a ring-tailed lemur whose tail keeps the door from slamming.
- VISITOR NARA, an orangutan carrying a parcel.

## Scene 1: The Door

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

A door stands ajar. The camera holds on it. A clock ticks. No one enters. After a long pause, the door drifts open another inch.

OMI: We are filming the space between arrival and departure.

LUMA: The closer is loose.

VEX: Please do not diagnose the image.

PIP: I have been holding it with my tail for two minutes.

OMI: Your tail is part of the composition.

PIP: My arm is getting tired.

The door moves. Pip's tail tightens, slowing it. Nara enters with a parcel and waits for someone to hold the door.

## Scene 2: The Take

Omi resets the scene. Nara walks in again. Vex follows the door with the boom microphone, recording each creak. Luma asks them to stop filming long enough to tighten the hinge.

OMI: We have not captured its true movement.

LUMA: It has been moving the same way all afternoon.

VEX: The sound changes when the room is empty.

PIP: The sound changes because you move the microphone.

The door swings slowly toward its frame, then stops against a wedge. Omi tells Nara to enter again, this time from the opposite side. She passes through the doorway and knocks on the same door from the hall.

NARA: I am outside.

OMI: We can hear you, but the camera cannot see you.

VEX: The voice arrives before the image.

PIP: It is because the camera is facing the room.

Omi repositions the camera. The door blocks the lens entirely. The crew waits for it to settle; the wedge rolls away and the door closes with a bang. The sound echoes through the hall.

LUMA: That is the closer hitting too hard.

OMI: It may be our most dramatic take.

Omi asks Nara to leave and re-enter. She does, carrying the parcel. The door swings open, then closes on the edge of her coat. Nara tugs free. The crew holds the take, refusing to cut.

## Scene 3: The Meaning

The film crew interviews each resident about the door. Pip says it means the building needs a new closer. Vex says the creak resembles a low note. Luma explains the hinge screws have loosened. Omi asks whether the door represents a choice.

NARA: It represents getting my parcel inside.

The parcel contains a new door closer, purchased by the community hall. Luma asks the crew to move their equipment so she can install it. Omi wants to film the process without interruption.

LUMA: If you stand here, you are in the way.

OMI: That could be the film's central conflict.

LUMA: It is my central obstacle.

## Scene 4: The Seven-Minute Repair

Luma removes the old closer. The film continues in one shot. Pip holds the door with her tail, Nara holds the parcel, and Vex records the sound of the screwdriver turning. Omi circles the crew slowly, making a quiet orbit around the repair.

The new closer is attached. Luma adjusts the speed. The door closes too fast, nearly catching Pip's tail. She pulls it free. Vex records the final click.

VEX: The door has completed its journey.

PIP: The door is closed.

NARA: The parcel is inside.

OMI: We have three endings.

LUMA: We have one repair.

## Scene 5: The Screening

The crew screens the film at the community hall. It is seven minutes of a door, a tail, a parcel, and a screwdriver. The audience waits through the long take. At the end, the door closes quietly and stays shut.

OMI: What did the audience feel?

PIP: I felt that my tail was tired.

NARA: I felt that my parcel was heavy.

LUMA: I felt the door needed a new closer.

The residents vote to keep the film as a maintenance record. Vex adds the sound of the door before and after the repair to the building's archive.

## Scene 6: A New Shot

Omi returns to the hall the next day. The door opens smoothly and closes gently. He sets up the camera, then thinks better of it. The building is busy with residents entering and leaving.

OMI: Should we film it again?

LUMA: You can use it.

Omi carries the camera inside without recording.

## Final Image

The door swings closed behind him. The latch clicks once. The hall continues with people moving through it, no one waiting for the door to mean anything else.
