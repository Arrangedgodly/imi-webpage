# The Grandmother Clock Takes Lunch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-travel thrillers, paradox rules, and exposition delivered beside glowing machinery*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: Two clock repairers trigger a one-hour time loop in the station lobby and keep trying to use it to prevent a small scheduling mistake.

Cast: Sela, an orangutan horologist; Dash, a macaque apprentice; Mimo, a pigeon station clerk; and Inspector Rye, a tortoise safety officer.

SCENE 1. INT. OLD STATION LOBBY, MORNING

Sela winds the giant clock while Dash holds the ladder. A brass gear slips and strikes the bell. The lobby clock rolls backward to 8:00. Mimo walks through the doors, drops a bundle of timetables, and repeats the same greeting.

MIMO: Good morning. The 8:10 is delayed.

Dash looks at the clock. He remembers hearing this. Mimo drops the bundle again, in exactly the same pattern. Dash catches it. A timetable is now upside down.

SCENE 2. INT. CLOCKWORK PLATFORM, LATER

Sela opens the service panel. The bell spring is over-wound and keeps resetting the hands. Dash proposes stopping the clock. Sela says the station depends on it. Inspector Rye arrives and asks them to isolate the mechanism before touching it. Dash starts explaining time paradoxes. Rye gives him a lockout tag and asks for the model number instead.

They inspect the gear train. Each hour, the clock repeats the morning's announcements. Mimo has begun using the loop to practice a more dramatic delay notice. He tries a solemn voice, then a whisper, then a shout. The passengers continue reading their newspapers.

DASH: If we change anything, will it erase the future?

RYE: If you touch the live gear, it will erase your glove.

Sela finds the spring tension is increasing with every reset. She asks Dash to fetch the correct wrench. The loop resets before he can reach the tool cabinet. On the next pass he remembers the route and arrives holding the wrench. He also brings a lunch tin because he forgot to eat in the previous cycle.

Inspector Rye tests what survives the reset. A paper ticket returns to its original place, but the bent tooth keeps its new shape. Sela explains that the clock resets the lobby's hour, not the metal inside the casing. Dash wants to write a rule on the wall. Rye says to write it on the service record, where the next repairer can find it. Dash adds a note to his slate: "Do not test paradoxes with loose hardware."

Mimo uses the repeated minute to rehearse a speech about punctuality. Every version blames passengers for arriving late, though the train has not departed. One passenger points out that the clerk's clock is an hour behind. Mimo checks it and adjusts the minute hand. The bell rings and resets him again, but this time he has copied the corrected schedule onto the slate.

SCENE 3. INT. STATION LOBBY, REPEATING MORNING

Dash puts chalk marks beside the clock's minute hand. They vanish at the reset. Sela records the gear position on a slate. The slate remains, because it is outside the clock's vibration plate. Inspector Rye makes them write down the shutdown procedure, step by step. Mimo keeps interrupting to announce the delayed train with a different flourish.

On the next reset, Dash anticipates Mimo's bundle and catches it again. The clerk is impressed. Dash begins to boast that he has mastered causality. The bundle contains a schedule showing the train was never due at 8:10; Mimo read the wrong line. The delay announcement caused the crowd to gather at the wrong platform.

MIMO: I have been apologizing for a train that arrived on time.

Sela says the loop is still dangerous. The spring's metal tooth is bent. They can stop the clock safely if Rye holds the brake while Sela releases the spring and Dash removes the jammed tooth. The trio rehearse once with the power isolated. Mimo practices reading the correct schedule.

Dash asks whether they should announce the time loop to the passengers. Rye tells him to explain the immediate condition and the safe route. They mark a path around the service panel with stanchions. Mimo reads the notice at normal speaking volume. A child asks whether the train will really arrive. Mimo checks the platform board and says yes, on Platform Two. The child follows the arrows without asking for a theory of time.

SCENE 4. INT. CLOCKWORK PLATFORM, NEXT RESET

They perform the repair. Rye locks the brake. Sela releases tension. Dash removes the bent tooth and places it in a parts tray instead of his pocket. The clock stops at 8:17. The next bell does not reset the room. Mimo checks the timetable and makes a plain announcement: the train is boarding at Platform Two.

The passengers move to Platform Two. One asks whether the clock is fixed. Sela says it is repaired but needs a full inspection before service. Dash starts to describe their time loop. Rye points to the inspection form. Dash writes down the event, then crosses out "temporal anomaly" and writes "over-tensioned spring."

Mimo asks if he should make the correction sound more exciting. Sela says accuracy is enough. He delivers the announcement without theatrical echo, and nobody applauds. The passengers board anyway.

FINAL IMAGE: The station clock advances to 8:18. Dash opens his lunch tin. The sandwich is one hour fresher than he expected, and he eats it before anyone can announce a paradox.
