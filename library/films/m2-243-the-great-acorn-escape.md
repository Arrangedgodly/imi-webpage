# The Great Acorn Escape

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prison-break thrillers, where inmates devise an elaborate escape from a pantry shelf*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A crew of pantry regulars plots a daring escape from a shelf, but the cupboard door opens every morning and their greatest obstacle is deciding where to go.

Cast: Brik, a macaque planner; Noll, a capuchin climber; Sava, a gibbon lookout; and Keeper Rumi, a lemur who stocks the pantry.

SCENE 1. INT. PANTRY, NIGHT

Acorns sit in a glass jar on the middle shelf. Brik draws a map in spilled flour. Noll watches the cupboard seam.

BRIK: At dawn, the door opens. We cross the shelf, descend the towel, then reach the table.

NOLL: Why not go tonight?

SAVA: The door is closed.

NOLL: It opens from the inside.

BRIK: We have not confirmed that.

Sava puts one hand on the cupboard door. It swings inward.

SAVA: Confirmed.

BRIK: Then the plan is to wait until dawn.

NOLL: That is not an escape. That is breakfast.

Brik points to a tiny gap behind the jars. They could climb through it, but there is no exit on the other side.

NOLL: The gap leads behind the flour tin.

BRIK: A hidden passage.

SAVA: It leads to the wall.

BRIK: A dead end in the map. We will adapt.

A spoon shifts in the drawer below. Everyone freezes.

[SFX: SPOON CLINK.]

SAVA: The guard is near.

NOLL: The guard is a spoon.

BRIK: It may have allies.

SCENE 2. INT. PANTRY, LATER

They tie a strip of cloth to the shelf. Noll lowers herself to the counter, then looks back.

NOLL: The towel does not reach the floor.

They test an alternate route across stacked bowls. Brik places a seed on each rim; the seed rolls toward the table edge.

BRIK: The bowls are not level.

NOLL: The table is tilted.

SAVA: The whole kitchen is sloping.

BRIK: The map must account for terrain.

They shift one bowl. It makes a hollow ringing sound. The spoon in the drawer answers with a clink.

[SFX: BOWL RING, SPOON CLINK.]

NOLL: The guard heard us.

BRIK: The spoon is listening.

SAVA: Or the bowl is loud.

The crew abandons the bowl route. They check the jar lid instead; it twists easily, and Noll pops it open.

NOLL: We could leave through the jar.

BRIK: It is not an exit. It is a container.

NOLL: This place has too many rules.

BRIK: That is why we must escape carefully.

NOLL: The cloth reaches the table, but the bowls block the landing.

NOLL: The table is covered in bowls.

Sava peers through the cupboard seam. The kitchen is dark.

SAVA: The route is clear.

A cat-like shadow passes across the door, but it is only Rumi's tail moving outside. Brik whispers that the guard has left.

RUMI (OFF): I can hear you.

The crew freezes.

RUMI (OFF): If you are trying to get out, push the door.

NOLL: That is a trick.

RUMI (OFF): The trick is keeping the acorns in the jar.

Brik tells Noll to test the cloth rope. It slips off the shelf. Noll catches the end before it falls.

NOLL: The rope failed.

BRIK: The knot was temporary.

SAVA: The whole operation is temporary.

Rumi opens the door. Morning light floods the pantry.

SCENE 3. INT. KITCHEN, CONTINUOUS

Rumi places the jar on the table and opens it. Brik, Noll, and Sava jump down.

RUMI: You could have asked.

BRIK: We were under observation.

RUMI: The cupboard door has been open for ten minutes.

NOLL: We thought it was a trap.

SAVA: It was a very bright trap.

Rumi offers a bowl of acorns. The crew refuses; they want to choose their own destination. Brik points toward the window.

BRIK: We need open territory.

RUMI: The table is not territory?

NOLL: It has bowls.

Rumi clears a path through the bowls. The three climb down from the table and cross to the window ledge. A potted plant blocks the way.

SAVA: New obstacle.

BRIK: We need a diversion.

Rumi moves the plant.

NOLL: The guard has cleared the way.

RUMI: I am the keeper, not the guard.

BRIK: That is what a guard would say.

SCENE 4. EXT. WINDOW LEDGE, MORNING

The crew reaches the ledge. Beyond it is a safe branch. Sava tests the jump. Noll makes it across. Brik hesitates.

BRIK: The plan was to escape together.

NOLL: We are waiting.

RUMI: If you stay, I will close the cupboard again.

Brik jumps. They land on the branch and settle into a fork where acorns have gathered.

SAVA: We are free.

NOLL: We are on a branch.

BRIK: A branch has more exits.

Rumi puts the empty jar back in the pantry. The crew watches through the window as she closes the door.

RUMI: The cupboard is secure.

BRIK (OFF): That is what the guard thinks.

They look out over the yard. The branch sways gently. Brik starts drawing another map in the dust.

NOLL: Where does this one go?

BRIK: Somewhere with no cupboards.

SAVA: And no spoons.

[SFX: BRANCH CREAK, MORNING BIRDS.]
