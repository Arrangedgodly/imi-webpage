# Ropes Over the Rapids

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: river-rafting adventure films, heroic slow-motion paddling, danger montages, and a guide who insists the water is only mildly enthusiastic*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
KARA, a capuchin river guide
BO, a macaque camera operator
NIMI, a gibbon cook
FENN, a ring-tailed lemur medic

SCENE 1. EXT. RIVER LANDING - MORNING

A wide river rushes past. Kara, a capuchin guide, studies a hand-drawn route map. Bo attaches a camera to the raft. Nimi packs lunch into a waterproof barrel.

KARA: The rapids are moderate.

BO: The river looks dramatic.

KARA: The river has a publicist.

Fenn checks everyone's life vests. Bo fastens his backward, covering his face with the buckle. The crew boards. Nimi secures the lunch barrel with a rope and tests the knot by tugging it with his tail.

SCENE 2. EXT. UPPER RAPIDS - LATER

The raft glides into the first fast water. Bo turns the camera toward the crew and narrates a danger montage while the river is calm.

BO: The team faces the unknown.

KARA: We are facing downstream.

Nimi opens the lunch barrel to check the sandwiches. A wave splashes inside. He closes it quickly, but the lid is now stuck. The raft spins in an eddy. Kara points to a rock, and everyone paddles on the same side, turning the raft away from it.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROCK GARDEN - NOON

The river narrows into a maze of rocks. Kara calls instructions. Bo films a close-up of a paddle and misses the actual obstacle. The camera shows a heroic slow-motion splash as Fenn's hat flies into the water.

FENN: My hat is downstream.

KARA: Your head is still upstream.

Nimi reaches with the lunch rope and catches the hat. The rope pulls the barrel from the raft; it floats beside them. The crew must choose between rescuing lunch and steering through the rocks.

NIMI: The lunch has a life vest.

SCENE 4. EXT. SMALL WATERFALL - AFTERNOON

The raft approaches a low waterfall. Kara tells everyone to lean forward. Bo leans backward for a better camera angle. The raft tips, and Bo lands in the shallow water with the camera still recording.

BO: The water is colder than it looks.

KARA: It looks cold.

Fenn throws him a rope. Bo catches it, then pulls the raft sideways rather than himself aboard. Nimi grabs the rope and uses it to guide the raft toward the bank. The crew steps out safely, drenched but intact.

SCENE 5. EXT. RIVERBANK - LATER

They inspect the raft. The lunch barrel is still tied on. Nimi finally opens it; every sandwich has become a single, flat sandwich cake. Bo's camera contains an entire minute of sky and one close-up of Fenn's hat.

FENN: The hat is the star.

BO: It had the best angle.

Kara shows them a calm side channel that reaches the landing. The crew paddles there, using the rope to keep the raft aligned. The route is slower but avoids the last rapids.

SCENE 6. EXT. RIVER LANDING - EVENING

The crew returns. Bo shows the footage to waiting friends. They cheer for the hat, the lunch barrel, and the accidental view of the sky. Kara updates the route map with a note: "side channel for tired crews."

SCENE 7. EXT. RIVER LANDING - NEXT MORNING

A film crew arrives to capture a slow-motion replay of the waterfall. Bo sets the camera on a tripod. Kara explains the safe side channel to the crew. The camera operator asks them to repeat the fall for a better shot.

KARA: The fall was not planned.

BO: Neither was the camera angle.

The camera tips toward the river. Fenn catches it with the rescued hat. Nimi uses the lunch rope to tie down the tripod. The film crew tries to stage a heroic paddle pose, but the raft slowly drifts away from the shore.

KARA: You need a rope.

BO: We need a second take.

Nimi throws the rope, and the raft stops. The crew gets its shot of everyone safely on land, but the camera records only the bottom of the hat. Bo approves the image as an honest ending.

SCENE 8. EXT. SHALLOW FORD - DAY

The crew tests the side channel with an empty raft. A low branch catches the rope, and the raft turns broadside. Kara stops the test and has everyone unclip before pulling. Bo's camera keeps filming the river while he helps Nimi free the line.

FENN: The water is still enthusiastic.

KARA: It is also shallow here.

They move the rope to a higher branch and guide the empty raft through. It clears the ford cleanly. Nimi places the rescued hat on the camera tripod as a marker for the safe landing spot. The hat slides off and floats away; Fenn wades after it and brings it back, now wetter than before.

FINAL IMAGE: The flattened sandwich cake rests on a picnic plate while the rescued hat sits beside it like a decorated captain.
