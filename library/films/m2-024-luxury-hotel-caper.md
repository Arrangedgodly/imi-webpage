# The Suite Escape

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: luxury hotel capers, elaborate disguises, and incompetent high-end security*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A capuchin bellhop and a team of hotel workers try to retrieve a famous diamond from a penthouse suite, but the security system keeps mistaking their disguises for the hotel furniture.

Cast: Gio, a capuchin bellhop; Madame Palm, a hotel manager; Lute, a langur elevator operator; Inspector Brass, a rhinoceros security chief; and the Diamond, a glass exhibit with a very realistic shine.

SCENE 1. INT. GRAND PALM HOTEL, LOBBY, NIGHT

The hotel hosts a gala. The diamond sits in a glass case on the top floor. Gio watches the security guards. Madame Palm says the jewel must be moved because the floor is being renovated.

GIO: We can use the service lift.

BRASS: No unauthorized staff.

PALM: We are authorized.

BRASS: Not by me.

Lute arrives dressed as a potted plant. Brass scans him and says the plant has suspicious shoes.

SCENE 2. INT. SERVICE ELEVATOR, LATER

The crew rides upstairs disguised as housekeeping staff. The elevator stops at every floor because Gio presses all the buttons with his tail. Lute tries to blend into the corner. A guest waters him.

Gio asks for a towel. Brass appears in the mirror. The team freezes. Brass asks why the mop bucket has a hotel badge. Gio says it is a staff member.

SCENE 3. INT. PENTHOUSE HALLWAY, NIGHT

The case is open, but the diamond is gone. Madame Palm finds a note: "Moved for polishing." Brass suspects the staff. Gio follows a trail of polishing cloths down the hall. They lead to the laundry room.

The crew opens the washer. Inside are twenty white towels and the diamond, stuck to a sock. Gio retrieves it with tongs. Brass asks why it was placed in laundry. Madame Palm says the polishing team uses the same cart.

SCENE 4. INT. LAUNDRY ROOM, CONTINUOUS

The alarm triggers. The sprinkler system activates. Towels tumble across the floor. Gio slips, catches the cart, and keeps the diamond from rolling into a drain. Lute uses the elevator emergency phone to call for help, but it connects to room service.

Brass arrives carrying a mop. He sees the workers saving the jewel and joins them. They place it in a clear plastic bag. The alarm stops. The bag is labeled "lost sock."

SCENE 5. INT. GRAND PALM LOBBY, DAWN

The crew presents the diamond to the museum courier. Brass apologizes for accusing the plant. Lute steps out of the potting soil. The renovation is delayed because the service elevator is full of wet towels.

SCENE 5A. INT. SECURITY OFFICE, NIGHT

Brass reviews the camera feeds with Gio and Madame Palm.

BRASS: Identify the suspect.

GIO: The potted plant.

PALM: That was Lute.

BRASS: The mop?

GIO: That was me.

PALM: The laundry cart?

BRASS: That was all of us.

GIO: The diamond moved.

BRASS: The camera saw it.

PALM: The camera saw towels.

BRASS: It was a secure system.

GIO: It has one angle.

BRASS: Two angles.

PALM: The other shows a wall.

BRASS: A secure wall.

GIO: The case was open.

BRASS: It was being polished.

PALM: In the laundry?

BRASS: The polishing room was booked.

GIO: By whom?

BRASS: The gala.

PALM: The gala is downstairs.

BRASS: It booked everything.

GIO: Even the elevator?

BRASS: Especially the elevator.

LUTE: It stopped on every floor.

BRASS: That is the schedule.

PALM: Why?

BRASS: Full service.

GIO: It was full of towels.

BRASS: Also service.

PALM: We'll revise the rules.

BRASS: No plants?

GIO: Let plants use the lift.

PALM: No polishing in laundry.

BRASS: Fine.

LUTE: No disguises?

BRASS: No suspicious shoes.

GIO: Those were my shoes.

BRASS: I suspected.

Madame Palm pins the updated security memo to the wall. It falls immediately. Brass catches it and adds "secure pin" to the revision list.

SCENE 5B. INT. GRAND PALM LOBBY, MORNING

The hotel reviews the gala incident. Brass has brought a clipboard. Madame Palm has brought coffee. Gio carries a basket of clean towels.

PALM: The diamond is safe.

BRASS: The case was open.

GIO: The sock was clean.

BRASS: The sock was not listed.

PALM: Neither was the plant.

LUTE: I was the plant.

BRASS: Your shoes gave you away.

LUTE: I changed them.

GIO: He wore slippers.

BRASS: Suspicious slippers.

PALM: Can we stop suspecting footwear?

BRASS: The shoes moved.

GIO: We were working.

BRASS: That's a motive.

PALM: The gala paid for security.

BRASS: It also booked the elevator.

LUTE: I stopped at every floor.

PALM: Why?

LUTE: The buttons were all pressed.

GIO: By my tail.

BRASS: That's unauthorized.

GIO: My tail works here.

PALM: We'll add it to the staff list.

BRASS: Tails?

PALM: Authorized tails.

LUTE: What about the potted plant?

BRASS: It may ride.

GIO: Without shoes?

BRASS: We'll discuss that.

The staff carry towels upstairs. Brass scans the basket, then apologizes to it.

FINAL IMAGE: The diamond sits safely in its case. Brass installs a new sign: "DO NOT LAUNDER." Gio reads it, then turns it around. The other side says "DO NOT POLISH WITH SOCKS."
