# The Cabin That Only Haunted Its Own Door

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-house comedies that turn every creak into supernatural evidence while ignoring obvious mechanical causes*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Three monkeys rent a supposedly haunted cabin for a weekend, but the ghost's most terrifying trick is a badly hung screen door.

Cast: Mara, a macaque skeptic; Puck, a capuchin ghost hunter; Seli, a gibbon filmmaker; host Boro, a lemur caretaker; a porch swing.

SCENE 1 - EXT. WOODSIDE CABIN - DUSK

Mara and Puck arrive with luggage. Seli carries a camera and a flashlight. A porch swing creaks in the wind. The screen door slams shut behind them.

PUCK: The ghost has sealed the exit.

MARA: The wind closed the door.

SELI: I caught it on camera. We have our opening scare.

Boro approaches from the shed carrying a toolbox.

BORO: The latch sticks. Lift the handle when you close it.

Puck sweeps a handheld detector beside the door. It gives no reading. A sharp chirp comes from the ceiling.

PUCK: The detector is quiet, but something just called from above.

BORO: That is the smoke alarm. Its battery is low.

Before unpacking, Seli finds a guest book on the counter. Several entries report that the cabin door "moved on its own." Puck turns to the camera and lowers his voice.

PUCK: The witnesses have returned.

MARA: They probably used the screen door.

Seli reads another entry. The guest says the kettle whistled at midnight. A caretaker has written beneath it: "Please fill only to the line."

PUCK: A warning from the other side.

BORO: A warning from the kettle. It boils over if you fill it to the lid.

Seli flips the guest book over. A printed note lists the door latch, loose cupboard hinge and alarm battery as routine repairs.

SELI: The hauntings have a maintenance checklist.

MARA: Check the items before you film them.

SCENE 2 - INT. CABIN KITCHEN - NIGHT

The group sits at the table. A cupboard opens by itself. Seli raises the camera. Puck whispers a question to the darkness.

PUCK: Spirit, are you here?

MARA: That hinge is loose.

She closes the cupboard. It opens again. Mara wedges a folded towel under it. The door stays shut.

SELI: The ghost is quiet now.

BORO: I can tighten the hinge tomorrow. Don't put the kettle under it.

A kettle whistles. Puck jumps and spills his tea.

PUCK: A second spirit!

MARA: The kettle is boiling.

SCENE 3 - INT. CABIN HALLWAY - LATER

The smoke alarm chirps. Puck shines his flashlight at the ceiling. Seli pans the camera dramatically.

PUCK: The ghost is warning us.

BORO: The battery needs replacing. I left one on the shelf.

Mara takes the battery and replaces it. The alarm stops. The porch swing creaks outside, slow and regular.

SELI: The spirit is walking around the cabin.

MARA: The swing is moving in the wind.

Seli opens the curtain. The swing moves, stops, and moves again.

PUCK: It knows we are watching.

MARA: It is tied to the porch roof with rope.

SCENE 3A - INT. CABIN LIVING ROOM - LATE NIGHT

Puck and Seli review the footage. On playback, the camera catches a shadow moving across the wall. Puck pauses the frame.

PUCK: There. A figure.

MARA: That's Seli's coat hanging on the chair.

SELI: I set it there when the door slammed.

Puck zooms in. The coat sleeve lifts as air moves through a cracked window.

PUCK: It reached toward us.

MARA: Close the window.

Seli shuts it. The sleeve falls still. Puck looks disappointed, then checks the rental guide for the window latch.

PUCK: The ghost appears to be a draft.

MARA: The draft is also on the checklist.

SCENE 4 - EXT. PORCH - MORNING

Boro checks the swing rope. One knot has loosened. He reties it. The screen door slams again when a gust catches it.

BORO: I will adjust the door closer. The latch is not catching.

Puck insists on filming the repair as an exorcism. Seli holds the flashlight under Boro's chin.

BORO: Please hold the light on the screws.

MARA: We can show the actual fix. The story is funnier if the cabin is ordinary.

PUCK: Then I am the only monster here?

MARA: You spilled tea on the rug.

SCENE 5 - INT. CABIN LIVING ROOM - AFTERNOON

Boro removes the towel wedge from the cupboard and tightens its hinge screws with a screwdriver. The door now stays closed. He checks the new battery Mara installed in the smoke alarm, then adjusts the screen-door closer. The camera records Puck stepping outside and reentering. The latch catches without a slam.

SELI: No ghost, no slam.

PUCK: I will leave a note for the next guests. The cabin is haunted by maintenance.

BORO: The note should say how to lift the handle.

Mara writes instructions on a card. Puck adds a sketch of a ghost holding a screwdriver.

FINAL IMAGE: The instruction card sits beside the door, and the porch swing moves once in the breeze before settling without a sound.
