# The Chess Club's Last Move

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: noir mysteries, secret societies, ominous chess metaphors, and detectives who treat every game as a crime scene*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
DETECTIVE NARI, an orangutan
CLUB CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque
MIRA, a lemur chess player
PIP, a capuchin janitor
ODO, a gibbon librarian

SCENE 1. INT. LIBRARY CHESS ROOM - NIGHT

A chessboard sits under a single lamp. One black knight is missing. Detective Nari enters as rain taps the window. Vex says the club's final tournament cannot continue without the piece. Mira points to the empty square. Pip carries a broom and a dustpan.

NARI: Who saw the knight last?

VEX: It made a move before the game began.

Nari studies the board. A paper cup sits beside the king. Odo, the librarian, says the cup is not part of the set.

SCENE 2. INT. LIBRARY STACKS - LATER

Nari interviews the players. Mira says she moved the knight to clean the board, then placed it on the side table. Vex says he was studying a strategy. Odo says he shelved the chess manuals. Pip heard a clatter from the return slot but assumed a book had fallen.

A thunderclap punctuates each answer. Nari looks at the ceiling; a rain gutter is loose. The dramatic sound comes from water striking a metal bin. She moves the bin beneath the leak. Vex insists the storm is part of the mystery.

SCENE 2A. INT. CHESS ROOM - NIGHT

Nari reconstructs the last game. Mira demonstrates how she lifted the knight to wipe a water ring from the table. Vex moves the pieces around, claiming the empty square reveals an attack. Nari asks him to repeat the move with the pieces in their recorded positions. He cannot; he had already changed the board while making his speech.

ODO: The game was not recorded.

VEX: It was remembered.

Mira points to the score sheet. The notation shows the knight moved to the side table, not captured. Nari checks the side table and finds a damp ring beneath the paper cup. The knight's base left a dry circle beside it. Pip remembers sweeping a small object toward the book cart after the clatter. He did not know it was a chess piece.

SCENE 3. INT. LIBRARY RETURN DESK - NIGHT

Pip checks the book cart. One chess manual is upside down and heavier than the others. Nari opens it; the missing knight is tucked inside the pages. A note says "hold this place" in Mira's handwriting. The book was placed in the return slot with the piece inside, and Odo shelved it without noticing.

VEX: The knight has been captured.

MIRA: I was saving it from the wet table.

Nari asks why nobody mentioned the book. Pip says they asked who saw the piece, not who moved a book. Vex wants to call it a theft. Odo says the piece is library property and has now been found in a library book.

SCENE 3A. INT. LIBRARY STACKS - NIGHT

Odo returns the manual to its shelf with the call number facing out. Vex stands over his shoulder, trying to turn the ordinary shelving into a dramatic reconstruction. Pip rolls the cart toward the desk; its uneven wheel makes the books tremble. Nari catches the chess manual before it slides free again.

NARI: The clue is still loose.

PIP: The cart wheel is loose.

Odo wedges a folded scrap of cardboard under the wheel. The cart stops rocking. Mira checks her note against the score sheet and confirms that she placed the knight in the manual to keep it off the damp table. Nari records that the piece was stored, not stolen. Vex asks if a librarian can stamp the case file. Odo stamps the return slip instead.

SCENE 4. INT. CHESS ROOM - LATER

The club resumes its tournament. Nari places the recovered knight back on the board. Mira makes a move and Vex responds. Pip sweeps the floor, careful not to cross the game line. A draft from the window lifts the score sheet; Nari closes the loose latch, and the rain quiets. The game ends in a draw. Vex asks for a rematch. Mira says the library closes in five minutes. Odo announces the club can book the room next week. Nari records the result and notes that the knight was temporarily stored inside the manual.

SCENE 5. INT. LIBRARY EXIT - NIGHT

The club packs the board away. Vex counts the pieces into their fitted slots while Mira checks the score sheet. Every piece is present. Vex turns the box upside down to prove it; the pieces remain in their compartments, but the lid falls open and the knight slides into his palm.

VEX: It has escaped again.

NARI: You opened the box upside down.

Vex rights the box. Odo locks the chess room, while Pip returns the empty manual cart to its proper place. Nari closes the case file with the library stamp, not the police seal Vex has brought from his desk.

FINAL IMAGE: The lamp clicks off above the empty chessboard, leaving the squares alternating between rainlight and shadow.
