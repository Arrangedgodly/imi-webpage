# The Green Screen Has a Weather Report

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: green-screen spectacle, postproduction previews, and actors reacting to imaginary weather before the effects exist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR VEX, a macaque
VISUAL EFFECTS LEAD LUMA, a lemur
CAMERA OPERATOR ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - MORNING

A green wall fills the stage. Pip wears a windbreaker and clutches a paper map. Vex stands on a square of painted foam. Director Nari surveys the empty stage.

NARI: You are on a cliff above a stormy sea.

PIP: I can see the wall.

VEX: The wall is the cliff.

LUMA: The sea arrives in postproduction.

Odo checks the camera monitor. It shows Pip standing beside a green void. Nari asks for the waves. Luma says the first preview will be ready after they film the scene. Vex shakes his coat to suggest wind. It barely moves. Odo turns on a fan; the map flies offstage and lands in a bucket.

PIP: The sea has taken our map.

NARI: We do not have a sea yet.

SCENE 2. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - LATER

The actors rehearse. Pip is supposed to peer over the cliff. Vex grips his shoulder. Nari asks for terror, not a weather report. Pip points to the foam edge.

PIP: I am worried the cliff ends here.

NARI: It continues for two hundred feet.

LUMA: In the composite, yes.

Odo lays blue cloth across the floor to give the actors a horizon line. The cloth wrinkles. Nari asks for rolling waves. Luma requests a clean plate without actors, so the visual effects team can build water under their feet. Pip tries the scene again, but he looks at the blue cloth instead of the imaginary sea.

VEX: The storm is approaching from your left.

PIP: My left or the camera's left?

Odo points. Pip turns toward the camera. Nari asks him to stop looking at the equipment.

SCENE 3. INT. EDIT BAY - AFTERNOON

The first composite appears on the monitor. A gray sea boils behind the actors. Pip's boots are replaced by digital waves; his toes disappear and reappear with each crest. Vex watches himself cling to an invisible shoulder.

VEX: The storm is magnificent.

PIP: I am standing in water.

LUMA: You are standing on foam. The water is in the computer.

Nari pauses the video. She asks why the waves cover Pip's feet. Luma says the tracking markers on his boots were hidden by the rain machine. The markers are small green dots. Nari asks them to film again, but the machine is not scheduled until tomorrow. Vex offers to make the rain by shaking a spray bottle. He mists the lens.

ODO: We have rain on the camera and none in the movie.

The editor wipes the lens. Luma adds a temporary rain pass. The scene now has sideways rain, a sunset, lightning, and an enormous wave. Pip's map remains perfectly dry.

PIP: Can the map get wet?

LUMA: It can, but the file is already approved.

SCENE 4. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - NEXT MORNING

They return to film the clean plate. Pip and Vex leave the stage. Luma asks Odo to roll the blue cloth smooth. Odo's shadow crosses the green wall. The camera records him pulling a rope; in the preview, the rope appears to stretch out of the storm cloud. Nari considers using it as a lightning effect.

NARI: We can make the storm strike before the wave.

PIP: The map should be wet first.

LUMA: Wet maps are hard to track.

Pip folds the damp map along its real creases. Vex asks whether the folds can move like waves. Odo holds it open for the camera; a breeze flattens it against the lens hood.

Vex puts the map in the fan's path. It flies toward Odo, who catches it with one paw. Nari sees a physical effect that works without computer water. She asks them to repeat the map toss with the camera rolling. Odo catches it again, but this time the fan pushes the map into the lens hood.

ODO: The sea has taken the camera.

The camera is fine. Luma adds a simple dark horizon and wind sound. She leaves Pip's shoes visible and the map slightly crumpled.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - EVENING

The crew watches the finished shot. The waves look calm, the storm remains far off, and Pip holds a damp map. Vex wants the giant wave back. Nari says the scene is about finding the route, not surviving a wall of water. Pip studies the map and finds the path marked in pencil.

PIP: That is where we are.

VEX: The sea has no map.

PIP: This one does.

FINAL IMAGE: The preview freezes on Pip's wet paper map, while the green wall behind the screen waits for its next imaginary disaster.
