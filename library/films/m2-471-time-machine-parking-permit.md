# The Time Machine Arrives Before Its Parking Permit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-travel adventures that turn a simple schedule error into a paradox and insist every arrival changes history*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A time machine arrives at the science fair one day early, and its crew tries to prevent the resulting paradox by changing a sign that was never wrong.

Cast: Inventor Pavi, a macaque; future visitor Loma, a gibbon; fair judge Tavi, a capuchin; student Rell, a lemur; Rell's silent classmate, a young macaque; custodian Oru, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. SCHOOL GYM - AFTERNOON

A brass time machine stands beside a poster reading SCIENCE FAIR, FRIDAY. Pavi checks a small clock. Loma steps out wearing a silver suit and carrying a parking permit.

LOMA: We have arrived yesterday.

PAVI: The fair is tomorrow.

TAVI: It is Thursday. The fair is Friday.

Loma looks at the poster. The machine sparks, then prints a ticket that reads ARRIVAL: THURSDAY 3:00 PM.

RELL: The machine has the correct date.

PAVI: But we are too early.

SCENE 2 - INT. GYM - CONTINUOUS

Pavi draws a timeline on the poster. Loma warns that any change may split reality. Tavi checks the school's event calendar.

TAVI: The gym is reserved for setup today. The fair starts tomorrow at nine.

PAVI: If we leave now, we might arrive before we left.

LOMA: That is a temporal loop.

Oru rolls a cart past them. It carries tables and a stack of chairs.

ORU: We are setting up the fair. You can help move a table.

Pavi and Loma stare at the cart, unsettled by the ordinary explanation.

SCENE 2A - INT. GYM - CONTINUOUS

Loma insists they must return to the exact second they departed. Pavi presses the machine's blue button. A small panel opens. Separate windows read ARRIVAL DISPLAY: THURSDAY 3:00 PM and PERMIT DATE: FRIDAY. A handwritten checklist below says: check both dates, lock wheels, check permit.

PAVI: The arrival display and permit date are separate. The arrival display says Thursday.

LOMA: We have no idea what that means.

TAVI: The arrival display is already right. The permit wheel is a different control.

Pavi leaves both settings alone. The machine makes a chime and releases a puff of steam. The gym lights blink. Rell checks the wall clock. It has advanced by one minute.

RELL: We have changed the present by one minute.

LOMA: The paradox is growing.

ORU: The clock has been running the entire time.

Loma steps outside the gym and reenters. She asks what day it is. Pavi says Thursday. The date has not changed.

LOMA: We have avoided a time loop.

TAVI: You walked through a door.

Loma writes the result in her log: "A door does not count as time travel." Pavi tapes the log beside the date dial so the next crew checks the clock before traveling.

SCENE 2B - INT. SCHOOL GYM - CONTINUOUS

Tavi places the machine on a square marked JUDGING AREA. Pavi pulls the lever. It flashes and prints a parking ticket instead of moving anyone through time.

TAVI: Your demonstration has produced parking.

LOMA: In my era, a ticket can alter a dynasty.

Oru's table catches the ticket and drags it toward the gym doors. Loma lunges after it and grabs the table. Rell rescues the ticket from beneath a chair. It reads PERMIT DATE: FRIDAY.

RELL: It printed tomorrow's permit. We still need to check that separate wheel.

Pavi checks the separate lever, labeled DEMO / PRINT. Loma had selected PRINT, expecting a demonstration. He switches to DEMO. A timeline projects onto the wall. Oru rolls a table through the image, splitting Thursday from Friday.

LOMA: The future has been divided.

ORU: I am setting up the fair.

Pavi raises the projector. The full timeline now hangs above the crew as they work beneath it.

SCENE 3 - INT. GYM STORAGE ROOM - LATER

Pavi tries to reprogram the machine. Loma holds the parking permit. It is stamped for Friday, although it is Thursday and they need to leave the machine parked today.

LOMA: We have crossed time without permission.

TAVI: The school parking lot is open today. The fair parking permit is for tomorrow.

Pavi studies the panel and finds the separate permit-date wheel set to Friday. He turns only that wheel back one day to Thursday. The machine lights blink and a second permit prints, valid Thursday only.

RELL: We have a permit for the day we are already in.

PAVI: We can park the machine in the storage bay.

SCENE 4 - INT. GYM - EVENING

The fair is nearly ready. Pavi and Loma help Oru carry tables. The time machine is parked in the storage bay, plugged into a wall outlet. Tavi places a sign beside it: DEMONSTRATION TOMORROW, DO NOT TOUCH.

Rell's classmate approaches and reaches for the lever. Rell stops him and explains that it is a time machine. The classmate holds up a worksheet stamped SPELLING TEST NEXT WEEK and points hopefully at the machine.

LOMA: We do not alter tests.

PAVI: We also do not need to travel. The machine is already here.

SCENE 5 - INT. GYM - FRIDAY MORNING

The science fair opens. Judge Tavi asks for a demonstration. Pavi leaves the arrival display alone, advances the permit-date wheel to Friday for today's fair, and flips the separate lever from PRINT to DEMO. The machine projects a timeline from Thursday to Friday. Loma checks the display and nods.

LOMA: The arrival display still says Thursday. The fair is today.

RELL: We were here yesterday.

LOMA: The projection shows the two days of our visit. We have not travelled again.

Oru wheels the last table into place. The time machine's parking permit is taped to its side. Pavi begins the presentation, explaining how one arrival display and one permit-date wheel caused confusion without changing history.

FINAL IMAGE: The machine stands beside the fair sign, its two parking permits clipped together in date order while the clock shows Friday at nine.
