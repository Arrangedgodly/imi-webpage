# The Capsule at the Gym Wall

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: nostalgia dramas, time-capsule reveals, and montage-driven reunions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A school reunion opens a time capsule to reveal the future students imagined, but the dramatic predictions have aged poorly and one practical note turns out to be useful.

Cast: Tavi, a macaque archivist; Pella, a lemur teacher; Mott, a gorilla alumnus; June, a capuchin former student; and the current school's drama club.

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL GYM, AFTERNOON

A reunion banner hangs crookedly. Tavi places a metal capsule on a table. Mott says the class buried it twenty-five years ago and expected it to be opened today. Pella asks if anyone remembers the combination. June remembers a code written in the yearbook. They open the book; the code is a phone number for a payphone that no longer exists.

MOTT: The past has called.

JUNE: It has been disconnected.

The drama club gathers around the capsule. Tavi says no one should force it until they check the school archive.

SCENE 2. INT. SCHOOL ARCHIVE, LATER

The class looks through the yearbook for the missing key. Mott finds a photo of himself holding a key ring and pointing at a wall. June says that is the gym closet key. Pella checks the caption: Future engineer demonstrates a new locking system. The lock in question is a coat hook. The group laughs, then notices the capsule inventory sheet was folded inside the yearbook cover. It says the key was placed in the art-room drawer.

Pella finds the capsule entry. It lists a key, a class photo, a letter, and one anonymous item. The key is missing from the envelope. Mott recalls that he kept it on his key ring. He checks; it opens the gym storage closet but not the capsule. June finds an old receipt showing the capsule was borrowed from the art department, which means the lid may open with a standard tool.

SCENE 3. INT. GYM, EVENING

Before opening the capsule, Tavi asks each alumnus what they remember putting inside. Mott remembers the moon prediction. June remembers a drawing of a singing tree. Pella remembers nothing and worries she was absent that day. The class photo shows her in the front row holding the marker used to write the label. She remembers drawing the tree and forgot because the marker stained her sleeve.

The group tries a small screwdriver. The capsule lid turns. Inside are drawings, a class photo, a note predicting everyone would live on the moon, and a paper bag of seeds. Mott reads the note aloud in a dramatic voice. The drama club adds a spotlight and slow-motion reactions.

JUNE: We do not live on the moon.

MOTT: Not yet.

Pella asks whose seeds they are. Tavi finds a label: sunflower seeds for the school garden. The bag is intact. The class photo shows the group at the gym wall, but the image is stuck to the capsule with old tape. Tavi carefully lifts the corner; the photo tears slightly. Everyone gasps.

SCENE 4. EXT. SCHOOL GARDEN, MORNING

The drama club films a montage of the alumni digging. Mott is given a toy helmet for his astronaut dream. He takes it off and uses it to carry soil. June teaches the students the melody she wrote for the singing tree. The class plants the sunflower seeds in a straight row, then adds stakes marked with the opening year. Pella notices the irrigation hose is cracked and asks Tavi to repair it before watering.

While the students finish tamping the soil, Mott reads a letter predicting he would be an astronaut. He is now a bus mechanic and says he prefers the ground. June reads a wish to become a famous singer; she is a music teacher. She starts a school chorus with the drama club, who perform the tree melody while working. Pella asks if anyone predicted a broken irrigation hose. No one did. Tavi finds the crack before watering begins and replaces the connector. The students take turns opening the tap, then mark the date on the garden map. Mott is asked to pose in his toy helmet; he sets it on the hose reel so it will not blow into the seedlings. The camera catches him checking the connector again, and he tells the drama club that a reliable future is mostly remembering which fitting leaks.

SCENE 5. INT. SCHOOL GYM, AFTERNOON

The alumni sit for the new group photo. The drama club asks everyone to pose as their predicted future selves. Mott wears the toy helmet; June holds a microphone; Pella holds the garden map; Tavi holds the repaired hose connector. The photographer asks them to look toward the moon. They look through the gym skylight at the garden.

The reunion adds new items to a replacement capsule: a current class photo, a garden map, and a list of practical repairs. The drama club wants to add a speech about destiny. June suggests they include the actual seed packet so future students know what grew.

The new capsule is entered into the archive with a clear key location.

FINAL IMAGE: The capsule is sealed and labeled OPEN IN 25 YEARS. Tavi adds the opening tool to the archive box, taped beside the key, with a note saying "Check the book before calling the past."
