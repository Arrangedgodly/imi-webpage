# The Scales Come Off at Lunch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: creature films that ignore make-up continuity, transformation timing, and the performer trapped under a heroic prosthetic*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A creature actor's scales keep changing between takes while the crew tries to film a transformation scene before the lunch break.

Cast: Roka, a gorilla creature performer; Nelli, a lemur makeup artist; Fenn, a macaque director; and Puck, a capuchin continuity assistant.

SCENE 1. INT. CREATURE MAKEUP TRAILER, DAWN

Nelli applies blue scales along Roka's cheek. A photo board shows yesterday's look: nine scales on the left side, eight on the right. Puck counts them. Fenn arrives and asks for a more frightening design.

FENN: Can we add a ridge?

NELLI: Then we need a new continuity photo.

PUCK: The script says the creature has one ridge.

Nelli adds one raised strip. Roka tries to turn his head, but the prosthetic pulls at his ear. He waits while Nelli loosens it. Puck photographs the new position, then notices the scale count is different in the mirror.

PUCK: Yesterday was nine and eight.

NELLI: The mirror reverses the sides.

Puck takes another photo and writes "mirror version" on the board. Fenn asks if the ridge can be longer. Nelli says the longer version takes an extra hour to apply. Fenn asks whether they can film the transformation backward and reverse the shot. Puck says the scales would slide the wrong way.

ROKA: I would still have to sit here.

Nelli offers him a small fan. Roka holds it under the prosthetic while she adds the ridge. The fan blows the scale chart off the wall.

SCENE 2. INT. CAVE SET, MORNING

Roka stands before a painted cave wall. The scene shows him discovering his reflection and realizing he is changing. Fenn asks for a slow turn. Roka turns left; the ridge catches the light. Puck checks the photo and calls cut because one scale is missing.

Nelli searches the floor. The loose scale has stuck to Roka's glove. He holds it out. Fenn asks if the creature should be afraid of losing a scale. Roka cannot speak through the mouth prosthetic.

NELLI: He can point.

Roka points at the scale. Fenn calls it a powerful moment and asks for another take. Nelli reattaches the scale and needs ten minutes to reset the adhesive. Puck updates the schedule. Fenn says they can film the cave entrance while they wait. Roka removes one glove and flexes his hand. A blue scale remains stuck to his palm. Puck photographs it for the continuity record. Fenn asks whether the hand can be a separate shot.

FENN: It would look like the creature is changing faster.

NELLI: It would look like the glove came off.

Puck tapes the scale to the chart so it will not migrate to another surface.

SCENE 3. INT. CAVE SET, LATER

Roka returns with the scale restored. Fenn asks him to face the camera, then turn toward the painted pool. Puck notices that the makeup photo shows the ridge on the opposite side. The crew has rotated the camera, not Roka. They turn the light stand instead.

Roka steps to the pool. The reflection shows a smear of blue makeup on his jaw. Nelli brings a cloth. Fenn asks whether the smudge can become part of the transformation. Nelli says it is powder from the prosthetic.

FENN: The audience will read it as shedding.

NELLI: The audience will read it as makeup.

Roka lifts the cloth and wipes his jaw. Puck checks the photo. The scales match again. Fenn asks for the turn, but the lunch bell sounds. The crew pauses. Roka sits down carefully, keeping the ridge away from the cave wall. Fenn asks him to hold the frightened expression through lunch so the next shot can match. Nelli says facial muscles need to rest too. Roka drops the expression and smiles.

FENN: That is a very different creature.

ROKA: That is my face.

SCENE 4. INT. MAKEUP TRAILER, AFTERNOON

Nelli removes the prosthetic in sections. Roka drinks water through a straw and rolls his jaw. Fenn asks if he can stay in the scales for the afternoon scene. Nelli says the skin needs a break. Puck changes the order so they film the unmade-up close-up next.

Roka asks for a copy of the continuity photo. Puck gives him the one marked "mirror version." Roka sets it beside the lunch menu. Nelli asks if the actor wants the makeup removed all the way or just loosened around the ears. He chooses the ears first. Nelli releases the adhesive and Roka rolls his neck without the ridge catching. Roka holds it beside the actual photo and points out that the scale pattern is symmetrical only by accident.

FENN: It is a fantasy creature.

PUCK: It still has two cheeks.

FINAL IMAGE: The next shot begins with Roka's natural face. Nelli's scale chart is pinned beside the call sheet, not over the lunch menu.
