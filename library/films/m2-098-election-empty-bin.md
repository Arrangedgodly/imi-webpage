# The Election of the Empty Bin

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: campaign biopics, triumphant speeches, and elections that treat every small service change as a historic crusade*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: Two school-cafeteria candidates campaign for a new recycling bin while their dramatic consultants turn a practical request into a sweeping ideological battle.

Cast: Nella, a macaque cafeteria monitor; Grom, a gorilla student council candidate; Yari, a lemur campaign strategist; and Pita, a capuchin custodian.

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL CAFETERIA, MORNING

The cafeteria has one trash bin and no recycling bin. Nella stacks clean cartons beside it. Grom announces he is running for student council on a promise to install a blue bin. Yari unfurls a banner: A NEW ERA OF SORTING.

GROM: This is our moment.

PITA: The cartons need a different bin.

Yari asks Pita to repeat the line with more conviction. Pita points to the vendor label showing cartons are recyclable. Nella asks who will empty the bin. No one has asked the custodian. Grom says he can empty it himself. Pita asks whether he will be available during lessons and after lunch. Grom checks his campaign calendar, which contains three speeches and a photo session. Yari suggests a rotating volunteer schedule. Nella asks whether volunteers know which materials are accepted. Pita says the vendor has a one-page guide. Yari wants a dramatic reveal of the guide; Nella says it can be posted beside the bin.

SCENE 2. INT. AUDITORIUM, AFTERNOON

Grom debates his opponent. Yari writes a speech about changing the future. Nella hands Grom a list of practical needs: bin, signs, pickup schedule, and training for lunch monitors. He says the speech should focus on values. Pita asks if the values include keeping food out of the paper bin.

At the debate, Grom promises a blue bin in every hallway. Pita raises a card asking who will collect them. Grom hesitates. Yari whispers that the answer should be "the people." Nella says the answer should be "the custodian on the existing collection route." The audience nods. Grom's opponent asks what happens if the bin fills before pickup. Pita explains the custodian's route and asks for a backup bag in the cafeteria closet. Nella adds that cartons should be rinsed, not left dripping. Grom repeats the details accurately, then asks the moderator whether practical answers count as a platform. The moderator says they count as answers. Yari lowers the banner that reads A NEW ERA OF SORTING.

SCENE 3. INT. CAFETERIA, NEXT DAY

Grom wins the election. He arrives with a ceremonial shovel and asks where to install the bins. Pita shows him the service map. The hallway bins would block doors and create an extra collection route. Nella asks whether the cafeteria can test one bin near the dish return. Pita says yes, if they label it clearly and empty it before the afternoon pickup. Grom asks if the camera should capture the ribbon cutting. Pita hands him the label maker. The first sign comes out as "RECYLE." Nella peels it off and prints another. Grom holds the new sign while she sticks it at eye level. A student places a carton in the right bin, then asks where the straw goes. Nella shows the trash slot and updates the sign with a small picture.

Yari wants a ribbon-cutting ceremony. Grom asks the lunch monitors to help make signs. They label the bins: CLEAN CARTONS and FOOD WASTE. One student tries to put a banana peel in the carton bin. Nella redirects it before the pickup. Pita weighs the sorted bag and records the amount.

SCENE 4. INT. STUDENT COUNCIL ROOM, WEEK LATER

The pilot bin works. Grom presents the collection numbers and asks the council to approve two more bins in the cafeteria. Yari proposes a historic declaration. Pita asks for a larger bag and a weekly check of labels. The council approves the limited plan. A council member asks what they will do if recycling rises. Pita says they will review the weekly weights before buying more bins. Grom points to the actual numbers rather than his campaign chart. Yari proposes a celebratory march, but the bell rings for lunch. The council members carry the bins back to the cafeteria and set them beside the dish return.

Grom gives his victory speech, but it is now a short thank-you to the lunch monitors and custodian. Pita asks him to help carry the replacement bags. Grom removes the campaign sash so it does not drag on the floor. Nella posts the pickup schedule beside the bins.

FINAL IMAGE: The blue bin fills at a normal pace. The campaign banner is folded into a storage box while Grom reads the collection checklist before the next council meeting.
