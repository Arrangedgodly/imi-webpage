# The Army Behind the Hill

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: historical epics that imply thousands of soldiers with tiny extras, repeated costumes, and sweeping camera moves*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
CAPTAIN ODO, a baboon
SCOUT PIP, a capuchin
EXTRA VEX, a macaque
COSTUME RUNNER LUMA, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. MEADOW - MORNING

A painted ridge divides a meadow. Director Nari studies a battle plan. Captain Odo stands with three extras in identical helmets. Scout Pip carries a flag.

NARI: The enemy army fills the valley.

PIP: The valley is empty.

NARI: It is behind the hill.

VEX: I am the enemy army.

Nari asks Vex to walk past the camera several times while changing helmets. Luma hands him blue, green and red cloth. Vex puts the same helmet back on each time.

ODO: They all have the same face.

VEX: That is how you know the army is unified.

Odo asks if they can film from farther away. Nari points to a field with one hill, one tree and a water trough. She says the camera can hide the trough.

SCENE 2. EXT. MEADOW - LATER

Pip runs up the ridge with the flag. He is meant to report that an army approaches. He looks down and sees Vex changing his sash behind a rock.

PIP: The enemy is changing sides.

VEX: They are changing uniforms.

Nari calls for a cavalry charge. Odo and Pip ride two hobby horses. Luma wheels a camera fan behind them to blow dust. The fan blows the flag over Pip's face. He rides in a circle and returns to the same place.

NARI: Bigger arc.

ODO: The meadow ends there.

NARI: The frame does not.

Luma moves the camera left. The background now includes the trough. She covers it with a painted shield. Vex steps out from behind the hill, puts on a blue sash, and marches across frame. Luma ducks, changes him to a red sash and sends him back. The camera records five armies in six minutes.

SCENE 3. EXT. MEADOW - NOON

The extras gather for the battle speech. Odo stands before the three actors and tries to address the invisible thousands.

ODO: Hold the line!

VEX: Which line?

Pip points to a chalk mark in the grass. Vex takes it for a boundary and steps over it. Nari asks the crew to scatter more helmets through the meadow. Luma sets the helmets on fence posts. Wind blows one onto the water trough.

PIP: The left flank has fallen in.

Nari sends Odo to rescue the helmet. Odo asks if this is part of the battle. Nari says every object in frame belongs to the war. Odo uses a branch to retrieve the helmet, which makes the shot look like a spear maneuver.

VEX: I surrender to the branch.

Nari asks for smoke. Luma turns on the fan behind a cloth. The cloth balloons, then drops over Vex and the camera. Pip keeps the flag upright and helps lift the cloth. Odo returns with the wet helmet.

SCENE 4. EXT. MEADOW - AFTERNOON

Nari reviews the wide shot. It shows three extras, two hobby horses, six helmets, and one red cloth moving from left to right.

NARI: It looks like an army.

PIP: It looks like Vex.

VEX: I am a committed company.

Odo suggests using the sound of marching feet. Luma records the four performers walking at different speeds. The sound becomes a rolling rhythm, then drifts out of sync with the picture. Nari asks them to match steps. The hobby horses squeak in alternating time. Vex's helmet falls off on the downbeat.

Nari watches again. She keeps the close shots of the flag, boots, horses and helmets, then cuts to the empty hill. The marching sound continues as the camera pans across grass. Odo says the enemy can remain unseen.

Odo records the sound with Pip and Vex walking past the microphone at different speeds. Their steps start together and drift apart. Nari asks them to try again without marching in costume. Vex takes off his helmet; it rings against Pip's flagpole. The metallic clang sounds like a sword strike. Nari keeps it. Luma lowers the helmet gently beside the trough, where it makes one final hollow note.

SCENE 5. EXT. MEADOW - SUNSET

They perform the final charge. Odo raises the flag. Pip leads the horses. Vex changes from blue to red behind the ridge. Luma films low so the trough and spare helmets stay out of view. Nari calls the shot. The sound of four sets of steps fills the valley.

FINAL IMAGE: The flag rises above the ridge while Vex, wearing all three sashes, tries to return the spare helmets without stepping into the shot.
