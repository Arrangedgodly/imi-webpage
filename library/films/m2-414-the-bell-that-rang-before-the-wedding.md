# The Bell That Rang Before the Wedding

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic comedies, public misunderstandings, and a bell that insists on announcing the wrong couple*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a macaque wedding planner
PIP, a capuchin bell ringer
VEX, an orangutan florist
NARI, a lemur baker
ODO, a gibbon musician
WEDDING COUPLE, two macaques
PROPOSING PARTNERS, two capuchins

SCENE 1. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - MORNING

A flower arch leans over the square. Mara checks two ceremony cards. Pip polishes a hand bell.

MARA: At noon, ring when the couple reaches the arch.

PIP: Which couple?

MARA: The couple getting married.

PIP: There are two couples.

Vex wheels in a cart of flowers. A second arch is being built beside the first for a surprise engagement photo. Its ribbon says "YES."

VEX: We only have one bell.

MARA: Ring it for the wedding. The photographer can tap a spoon for the proposal.

Odo tests a trumpet note. The bell answers by vibrating on the table.

PIP: It has opinions.

Mara ties a red ribbon around the bell handle. Vex ties an identical ribbon to the engagement arch so the photographer knows where to stand. The square's footpath divides between both arches.

SCENE 2. INT. BAKERY - LATER

Nari decorates a wedding cake with two sugar monkeys. Vex carries in a bouquet. He has put the bride's boutonniere on a rolling cake stand.

NARI: That is the cake.

VEX: I thought the white frosting meant formal.

He lifts the flower. The icing pulls into a long white ribbon. Nari repairs it with a sugar leaf.

Mara enters with the ceremony schedule. She explains the couple will walk from the bakery to the square, while the proposing pair will arrive from the river path.

MARA: Keep the flower cart on the bakery side. The wedding party needs the clear path.

VEX: I can make the aisle romantic.

He rolls flower petals down the path. They stick to the cake stand wheels, which begins drifting downhill. Pip catches it with the bell. The bell rings once.

NARI: Was that the ceremony?

PIP: No. That was cake rescue.

Nari looks at the schedule. The bell's sound has brought the wedding couple to the bakery door early. Vex turns the flower cart sideways to hide the unfinished cake.

SCENE 3. EXT. SQUARE - NOON

Guests sit before the two arches. Odo plays a soft tune. The wedding couple approaches from the bakery. On the river path, the other pair carries a picnic blanket and a ring box.

Mara signals Pip to wait until the wedding couple reaches the red ribbon. The identical ribbons flutter in the breeze. The bell rope is looped around a post between the arches.

VEX: I tied it tight.

MARA: You tied the rope to both arches.

A gust pulls the engagement arch's ribbon. The rope tugs the bell. It rings while the wedding couple is still twenty steps away.

Guests stand. Odo switches to a triumphant march. The proposal pair kneels by the picnic blanket.

PIP: Which event did I announce?

MARA: Both, apparently.

The wedding couple arrives under the proposal arch. The kneeling partner opens the ring box. The wedding couple looks at the ring. The proposing pair realizes the mistake, then joins the crowd to watch.

VEX: This is a very efficient ceremony.

MARA: It is not a ceremony until the couple is in place.

Pip tries to silence the bell with a bouquet. The flowers get caught in the clapper. Odo stops playing, and the sudden quiet makes the bell's tiny trapped rattle sound louder.

SCENE 4. INT. BAKERY - MOMENTS LATER

The wedding party retreats inside to reset. Nari rolls out the cake, now patched with a sugar leaf shaped like a tiny flag. The proposal pair follows, still holding the ring box.

NARI: The cake is repaired. The bell is not.

PIP: I can remove the clapper.

MARA: We need it to ring.

PIP: It can ring after I remove it. I will keep it in my pocket and shake it.

Vex gives him a spoon. Pip taps it against the bell. The tone is clear and gentle.

MARA: That is the proposal cue.

PIP: It sounds like dessert.

The couples exchange glances. The wedding pair decides to proceed first, in the bakery, beside the cake. The proposal pair agrees to wait outside until the vows are done. Vex moves the flower cart from the aisle, but its wheel rolls across the sugar leaf flag. It stops with the flag pointing at the door.

SCENE 5. INT. BAKERY - CONTINUOUS

Mara begins the wedding vows. Odo plays the gentle tune. Pip raises the spoon and waits until both partners face each other.

MARA: Do you promise to share the last piece?

NARI: That is not the usual vow.

MARA: This is the bakery version.

The couple says yes. Pip taps the bell with the spoon. The sound is small, so every guest leans in. Outside, the proposal pair hears it and enters, mistaking the intimate silence for their cue. One partner kneels. The wedding couple raises their joined hands, forming an arch over the ring box.

VEX: We made two arches after all.

The proposal is accepted. Mara adds the second couple's names to the schedule, then crosses out "separate events."

FINAL IMAGE: Pip hangs the bell between the couples. It rings only when both pairs walk through the bakery door together.
