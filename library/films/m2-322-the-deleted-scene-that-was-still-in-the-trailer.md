# The Deleted Scene That Was Still in the Trailer

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: trailers assembled before final edits, advertising footage that no longer exists in the released film*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
EDITOR LUMA, a lemur
TRAILER CUTTER ODO, a baboon
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
MARKETING EXECUTIVE VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING SUITE - MORNING

Trailer cutter Odo assembles a teaser from the latest footage. A dramatic shot shows Pip on a rope bridge, reaching for a silver key. Editor Luma scrubs the timeline and finds that the bridge sequence was removed from the feature after the set flooded.

LUMA: This scene is not in the film.

ODO: It is the best shot.

VEX: Then it is the best trailer shot.

Director Nari enters holding the final cut. The story now has Pip cross a creek on stepping stones and discover the key on the opposite bank. Vex asks why they did not keep the bridge.

NARI: It fell down.

VEX: In the story?

NARI: In the studio.

Pip watches his rope-bridge image. He says it was filmed before the crew changed the ending. Vex asks him to look more surprised in the trailer. Pip looks at the footage as if he has never seen a bridge.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING SUITE - LATER

Odo adds a line from the deleted scene: "The bridge is our only way across." In the new film, Pip's character says, "Those stones look safe." The trailer now claims both paths are the only path.

LUMA: Which route does the hero take?

VEX: The trailer route.

NARI: There is no trailer route.

Vex asks for a title card: ONE BRIDGE. ONE KEY. ONE CHOICE. Luma puts the card over the deleted shot. Odo adds the stepping stones beneath it, so the bridge appears to hover over water. Vex calls it a visual metaphor. Nari asks what the metaphor means. Vex says they can test it at the preview.

Pip records a voiceover saying, "I have to choose." In the feature, he chooses the stepping stones immediately. Odo cuts in a shot of Pip hesitating on the abandoned bridge. The trailer makes it seem like the hero delays for half an hour.

SCENE 3. INT. PREVIEW THEATER - AFTERNOON

The trailer plays before the feature. The audience sees Pip choose the bridge, then sees him choose stepping stones. A viewer whispers, "He changed his mind." Another asks when the bridge collapsed. Vex takes notes under the heading ENGAGEMENT.

The feature begins. Pip crosses the stones and finds the key. After the credits, Vex asks the audience whether they liked the bridge. A guest says she liked the bridge in the trailer but was confused when it vanished. Vex writes "strong bridge recall." Nari asks what the guests thought of the actual film. They liked the creek, the key and Pip's careful crossing.

VEX: Nobody mentioned the bridge in the ending.

LUMA: There is no bridge in the ending.

VEX: That may be why.

SCENE 4. INT. EDITING SUITE - EVENING

Luma recuts the trailer with footage from the final film: the stones, the key, the creek and Pip testing each rock with his staff. Odo says the new cut has less danger. Nari suggests keeping suspense through sound: the creek rushes, Pip's staff slips, and he catches himself before moving forward.

They view the cut. The footage builds tension without the missing bridge. Vex asks to use one half-second of the bridge anyway. Odo places it before Pip reaches the bank, framing it as a dream flash. Nari says the feature has no dream. Luma proposes using it as a rejected shot in the making-of extra, clearly labeled. Vex agrees, then asks whether the extra can advertise the film.

PIP: It can show us taking the bridge down.

VEX: That sounds like a spoiler.

NARI: The bridge is already down.

Vex watches the accurate trailer. It ends as Pip lifts the key from the mud. The title appears, and the creek sound stops on a crisp cut. Vex seems unsettled by the ending's lack of a bridge. He asks for the title card to read ONE CHOICE. Luma approves it.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY - NIGHT

The final trailer plays at the premiere. It shows only scenes present in the feature. The audience enters expecting a creek crossing and gets exactly that. Vex checks the lobby poster, where a still of the bridge remains printed. A guest points at it and asks if that scene is included.

VEX: It is in the bonus materials.

LUMA: After the feature.

The guest buys a ticket. Odo packages the discarded trailer shot into a separate making-of clip, with a caption: BRIDGE SCENE, REMOVED AFTER SET DAMAGE. Nari sees that the description is accurate and lets the upload proceed. Vex asks if they can call the clip a surprise preview.

FINAL IMAGE: On the lobby screen, the real hero steps across stones while the printed bridge poster curls away from the wall.
