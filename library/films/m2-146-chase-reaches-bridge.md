# The Chase Never Reaches the Bridge

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action chases that ignore road geography, traffic signals, and the fact that every turn should change where the vehicles are*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A director films a city chase by repeating the same corner until the location manager points out that the hero has been circling a bakery.

Cast: Vek, a macaque director; Juno, a gorilla driver; Seli, a lemur location manager; and Puck, a capuchin camera operator.

SCENE 1. EXT. MARKET STREET, MORNING

Juno sits in a parked car on a closed street. Vek explains that the hero is racing to reach the river bridge before the villain. Seli checks the route map. The bridge is six blocks east. Puck mounts the camera on a small tracking cart.

VEK: Take the first left, then the bridge.

SELI: The first left is a dead end.

VEK: It looks fast on camera.

Juno drives around the block. The camera passes a bakery twice. Puck asks if the second pass should be faster. Seli says the route is the same. Vek calls for another take and asks Juno to turn left at the next corner. The street is blocked by a delivery truck.

JUNO: The bridge is still east.

Vek asks if she can make the truck look like an obstacle in the chase. Seli says it is unloading flour. The driver pushes a dolly of flour bags down the ramp. Vek asks for the camera to tilt as if the car is trapped. Juno waits while the delivery is completed. A bag rolls toward the curb. She catches it before it hits the tire.

JUNO: Is that part of the chase?

SELI: It is part of the street.

SCENE 2. EXT. MARKET STREET, LATER

The crew moves to a side street. Puck puts a red arrow on the windshield to show the viewer the direction of travel. The arrow points left. Juno turns right to avoid a parked cart. Vek asks her to reverse and take the marked route.

JUNO: The cart is in the lane.

SELI: The street is open to deliveries.

Puck films the car passing the cart. Vek asks for a close-up of the speedometer. The car is parked. Juno points out that the needle cannot move. Puck asks her to shake the dashboard. The camera vibrates, but the speed remains zero.

Seli draws a route on a paper map. Vek overlays a second route in bright marker, crossing through two buildings. Puck frames the map so the lines look dramatic. Juno traces the actual route with her finger and points out where the car must turn. The route reaches the bridge in three turns. Vek says the audience expects more turns. Seli asks if he wants the hero to get lost. Vek says he wants the chase to feel longer.

JUNO: We can hold on the driving.

Puck plays the footage. The car passes the same bakery, then the same mural, then the bakery again. Seli circles both passes in red pencil.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVER ROAD, AFTERNOON

The car starts at the correct end of the street. Juno drives toward the bridge at a safe speed. Puck tracks alongside on the cart. Vek asks for a near miss. Seli holds up a sign marked "ROAD CLOSED" where the bridge approach is under repair.

Vek says the hero has to reach the bridge. Seli says no vehicles can enter it. Juno follows the posted detour, which leads to a small overlook. The camera shows the bridge in the distance.

JUNO: The car cannot cross.

VEK: Can you make it feel like we almost did?

Juno parks at the overlook. Puck zooms in on the bridge. Vek adds a sound of screeching tires. Seli says the car is stopped and the wheels are straight. Vek asks Puck to add a second vehicle behind them. There is only one parked van at the overlook. Puck films its headlights. Juno turns off the ignition, and the lights go out. The crew borrows a battery lantern to make the shot readable.

SCENE 4. INT. LOCATION OFFICE, EVENING

Seli lays out the route map. The crew films a clear sequence: the car leaves the market, follows the detour, and arrives at the overlook. Vek watches the cut. The chase lasts forty seconds and reaches the bridge once.

Puck asks whether they can repeat the bakery pass as a callback. Seli asks why the hero would return to the same street. Juno says the bakery sells good bread, but the hero is late.

Vek removes the repeated pass. The sequence now shows the route and the closure sign. Vek asks if the closure will disappoint viewers. Seli says it is the reason the driver takes the detour. Puck adds the map to the end of the scene. Juno points to the road they actually drove.

FINAL IMAGE: The car is parked beside the overlook. A paper map rests on the passenger seat with one route marked in pencil.
