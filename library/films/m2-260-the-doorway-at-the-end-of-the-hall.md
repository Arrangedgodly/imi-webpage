# The Doorway at the End of the Hall

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-house horror films, where a creaking doorway becomes the center of an escalating investigation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A family hears a door creak every night and prepares for a haunting, only to discover the house is settling around a stubborn hinge.

Cast: Mara, a macaque; Teo, a capuchin; caretaker Odo, a baboon; and investigator Seli, a gibbon.

SCENE 1. INT. HOUSE HALLWAY, NIGHT

A door at the end of the hall slowly opens.

[SFX: LONG WOODEN CREAK.]

MARA: It moved again.

TEO: The wind is coming from the window.

MARA: The window is closed.

ODO: The house is old.

SELI: We should document the sound before we decide what it is.

The door creaks again, but does not open fully. Seli shines a lantern down the hall. The light throws a long shadow from the coat rack.

TEO: Something is standing behind the door.

ODO: That is my coat rack.

MARA: Why is it facing us?

ODO: It is in the hall.

Seli asks them to close the door. Teo does so. It stops two inches from the frame.

SCENE 2. INT. KITCHEN, LATER

They review the sound on a small recorder. The creak grows louder through the headphones.

SELI: There is a second sound underneath.

MARA: A whisper?

ODO: The hinge.

TEO: It sounds like a whisper.

ODO: It is a hinge that needs oil.

Mara refuses to sleep in the bedroom near the door. Teo offers to switch rooms. The hallway light flickers.

[SFX: LIGHT SWITCH, BUZZ.]

SELI: The power is unstable.

MARA: The house is sending a warning.

ODO: The bulb is old.

Seli asks Odo when the hinge was last repaired. He says it was repaired once, but only the top hinge. The bottom hinge is loose.

SELI: Then the door is uneven.

MARA: Uneven doors do not open by themselves.

ODO: They do if they are leaning.

SCENE 3. INT. HALLWAY, MIDNIGHT

Odo places a wedge under the door. The creak stops. Everyone exhales.

MARA: It is quiet.

TEO: The house has stopped.

SELI: The door has stopped.

A gust moves through the vent. The wedge slips. The door opens again.

[SFX: WOOD CREAK.]

MARA: It has escaped.

ODO: The wedge was too small.

Seli examines the floor. One tile is lifted, making the door's path slope toward the hallway. She asks Teo to stand near the hinge while she tests it.

TEO: I can feel the floor move.

ODO: The house is settling.

MARA: We need to leave.

SELI: We need to find the source of the tilt.

Teo lifts the tile and finds a small pebble underneath. The pebble is wedged against the door frame.

TEO: The house has a tooth.

Odo asks Mara to hold the flashlight while he works. The beam makes the coat rack's shadow stretch across the door. Mara lowers the light; the shadow shrinks to a coat and two hats.

MARA: The visitor has left.

TEO: It was never a visitor.

SELI: The camera would have made it a very convincing visitor.

She records the hinge, tile, and vent on one page. Odo checks the pebble, then sets it on the windowsill instead of putting it back beneath the tile.

ODO: Evidence stored.

MARA: It is a pebble.

ODO: Evidence can be small.


ODO: It is a pebble.

MARA: It was holding the door open.

SELI: It was holding the floor tile up.

Odo removes the pebble. The tile settles flat. The door swings closed without creaking.

SCENE 4. INT. HALLWAY, MOMENTS LATER

Odo oils the hinge while Teo holds the door. Seli places the pebble back beside the loose tile, then removes it and lets the tile sit flat. They test the door once. It closes quietly and stays closed.

MARA: The haunting is over.

ODO: The tile was holding the door at an angle.

TEO: And the hinge was dry.

SELI: A two-part case with no ghost.

Mara asks whether the house is safe. Odo presses the tile with his heel; it does not rock. Seli records one last opening and closing. The door makes no sound, and no draft pushes it.

[SFX: DOOR OPENS, DOOR CLOSES SOFTLY.]

MARA: I am staying in my room.

TEO: I never left mine.

SCENE 5. INT. HALLWAY, DAWN

The family hangs the coat inside the closet instead of on the rack. Sunlight fills the hall. Teo closes the door gently; it latches on the first try.

SELI: Case closed.

ODO: Door closed.

MARA: House quiet.

A floorboard creaks under Seli's foot. Everyone turns toward her.

SELI: That one is me.

She steps onto the rug. The creak vanishes. Odo rolls the oil can away before it squeaks again.

[SFX: ONE FLOORBOARD CREAK, THEN QUIET.]

MARA: The hallway has one last comment.

SELI: It can file it in the morning.
