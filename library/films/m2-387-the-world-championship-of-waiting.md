# The World Championship of Waiting

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports championships, ceremonial qualifiers, intense training montages, and final races won by the competitor who respects the line*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
COACH NARI, an orangutan
PIP, a capuchin competitor
VEX, a macaque rival
JUDGE LUMA, a lemur
ANNOUNCER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN HALL - MORNING

A banner announces the World Championship of Waiting. Contestants must remain in a marked line until called. Pip stretches beside a chair. Vex runs laps around the hall. Coach Nari reviews the rules: no cutting, no saving places, no leaving the line.

VEX: This is a test of speed.

LUMA: It is a test of waiting.

Odo announces the opening ceremony and calls everyone to their marks.

SCENE 2. INT. HALLWAY - LATER

The contestants line up at a counter to receive numbered cards. Vex tries to stand near the front by walking very slowly past the queue. Luma asks him to join at the end. Pip offers to hold his place while he ties his shoe, but the rules forbid place saving. Pip ties the shoe himself, then returns to his spot.

Nari leads the training montage. Pip practices standing still, reading a book, and breathing calmly. Vex practices shifting his weight without moving his feet. Odo conducts a loud commentary that distracts both of them.

SCENE 2A. INT. HALLWAY - MIDDAY

Nari supervises the qualifying round. Contestants must wait for a number to be called while standing behind a taped line. Vex practices not looking at the clock. He stares at the exit sign instead, then mistakes it for his number and steps forward. Luma asks him to return to his mark. Pip gives him a quiet reminder that the sign is not a number.

PIP: It is the way out.

VEX: I was checking the route.

Odo announces each number slowly. One contestant leaves to get water and forfeits the place. Nari adds a water station beside the line so contestants do not have to leave. Pip stays in place for thirty minutes, reading the posted rules. Vex sits down, then realizes there is no chair inside the marked area. He stands up without stepping over the tape.

SCENE 3. INT. REGISTRATION HALL - AFTERNOON

The final round begins. Each contestant waits for a number to be called. Vex's number is 14; Pip's is 15. The clerk calls 14, and Vex steps forward. He reaches the desk, but his ticket is upside down. He returns to his place, and the clerk calls 15. Pip presents his card correctly.

VEX: That was a false start.

LUMA: It was a misread card.

Pip receives a ribbon for following the rules. Vex asks if there is a rematch. Luma says there is another round after the judges finish their lunch. Vex takes his place in the line again. He checks his ticket carefully.

SCENE 3A. INT. REGISTRATION HALL - AFTERNOON

Vex asks the clerk if his ticket can be reprinted with a larger number. The clerk agrees, but the printer jams. Pip helps clear the paper. The ticket emerges with a smudge across the middle. Luma verifies the number by checking the registration list, not by guessing from the mark. Vex accepts a clean card and returns to his place at the end of the line.

The room's wall clock stops. Contestants begin asking whether waiting time still counts. Nari checks a phone and confirms the clock is the only thing that stopped. Odo announces the actual time and restarts the wall clock. Pip volunteers to hold the time card, but Vex says the judges should do it. Luma agrees and puts the card where every contestant can see.

SCENE 4. INT. HALLWAY - EVENING

The judges return. Odo announces the final result: Pip wins because he waited without changing the line. Vex receives a special mention for staying in place after the wrong call. Nari shakes both competitors' hands. Pip asks when they can leave. The clerk says the exit door is at the end of the hall.

VEX: We have to wait for the awards photo.

PIP: I can do that.

The photographer takes several shots because Vex keeps looking at the clock. Odo calls out that the championship has no time limit for blinking. Vex holds still so carefully that he keeps his eyes open through three flashes. The final photo shows Pip facing the camera and Vex looking patiently forward.

SCENE 5. EXT. TOWN HALL - NIGHT

The contestants exit in order. The ribbon is too long and drags along the floor. Pip holds it up for Vex, who folds it carefully. Nari posts next year's rules beside the entrance. The championship will have numbered cards and a clear calling system.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty line remains marked on the floor as the hall lights switch off one by one.
