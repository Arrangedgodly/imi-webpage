# The Action Scene Needs a Ramp

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action blockbusters, escalating stunt notes, and directors who confuse bigger movement with clearer stakes*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR VEX, a macaque
STUNT COORDINATOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR LUMA, a lemur
PROP MASTER ODO, a baboon
CAMERA OPERATOR TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. EMPTY WAREHOUSE - MORNING

Director Vex stands in front of two cardboard crates. Actor Pip holds a key. Actor Luma plays the guard. Stunt coordinator Nari checks the clear floor and soft crash mats.

VEX: The hero runs, vaults the crate, slides beneath the gate and escapes.

NARI: The gate is waist high and open.

VEX: It closes during the shot.

NARI: It is cardboard.

Odo tests the gate. It tips over before anyone touches it. Tavi films the collapse, but Vex says the camera has missed the danger. Nari props up the gate and marks a safe route with tape.

PIP: I can walk through the opening.

VEX: You can, but then the audience knows the gate is open.

LUMA: It is open.

SCENE 2. INT. WAREHOUSE - LATER

Pip runs toward the crate. Nari calls the movement slow and checks his landing. Vex asks for a higher vault. Nari refuses; the crate is hollow and the floor is not built for a jump. Odo brings a low foam platform. Vex says it looks like a cake.

ODO: It is safer than the crate.

VEX: Can we paint it like the crate?

TAVI: The camera will still see the cake edge.

They try a sliding move on a mat. Pip stops several feet before the open gate. Vex asks him to slide farther. Nari measures the mat's travel and adds a second pad beyond it. Pip repeats the move, stopping with his hands clear of the frame. The camera track rolls into the edge of the mat, so Nari locks it down too. Vex asks for a camera tilt that makes the slide look faster. Tavi tilts; the floor goes diagonal, and Luma reaches for the gate to steady herself. She is not part of the stunt, so Nari moves her mark clear. Vex watches the monitor and agrees that the safer angle still reads as motion.

LUMA: I was guarding the other side.

VEX: You need to chase him.

LUMA: I thought I was a guard.

VEX: The plot has changed.

SCENE 3. INT. WAREHOUSE - AFTERNOON

Vex asks Odo for a ramp. Odo brings a rolling platform designed for loading boxes. Nari locks the wheels and checks the height. Vex wants Pip to jump from it into a stack of crates. Nari demonstrates that the stack wobbles when touched. Odo removes the crates and replaces them with padded blocks. Vex says the shot has lost its scale.

Pip practices stepping off the locked ramp onto a mat. Luma waits with a foam baton. She taps Pip's shoulder when he reaches the mark. Tavi films from a low angle. In the monitor, the small platform looks like a tower; the foam baton looks like a steel bar.

VEX: There. A massive leap.

NARI: A careful step.

PIP: I stepped down.

VEX: The camera believes you did.

Nari allows one take with the locked ramp, floor mats and a clear path. Luma holds the baton away from Pip's body. Pip steps down, turns, and passes her the key. She does not swing at him. Vex calls cut because the handoff is too quiet.

SCENE 4. INT. WAREHOUSE - LATE AFTERNOON

Vex asks for explosions. Tavi suggests a flash of light reflected off a metal sheet. Nari checks the sheet and has Odo hold it behind a safety barrier. The flash makes the open gate look as if it is slamming. Pip runs the same route once, then stops at the exit.

LUMA: The key opens the door.

PIP: Then we can leave.

VEX: The chase is over?

NARI: The objective is met.

Vex watches the monitor. The reflections create a burst of sparks at the gate. Luma's calm handoff is visible. He asks for one bigger move, then sees Nari's taped route and says nothing. Odo adjusts the sheet by a few inches, producing a bright flash exactly as Pip turns the key. Vex asks if the flash can be called an explosion. Nari says it can be called reflected light. Luma points to the doorway and repeats the objective: key, open gate, exit. Vex nods and asks Tavi to hold on the wide shot long enough for the audience to see all three steps.

SCENE 5. INT. WAREHOUSE - EVENING

The crew films the finished action beat. Pip takes the key, steps off the ramp, and reaches the exit. Luma follows and closes the cardboard gate behind them. It lands gently against the mat. Vex leans toward the monitor.

VEX: It looked enormous.

NARI: It was enough.

Pip checks the key against the gate once more. It fits the lock. Luma returns the key to Odo, who places it in the prop tray instead of a hidden pocket. Vex asks if that is the final shot. Tavi lowers the camera only after Nari calls cut.

FINAL IMAGE: Odo unlocks the same wheeled loading ramp, rolls it to the warehouse rack, and locks its wheels again. Vex holds the tiny key up to the camera as if it weighs a ton.
