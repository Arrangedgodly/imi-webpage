# The Mountain Hotel Checkout

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: alpine travel films where checkout becomes a farewell to the mountain itself*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
GUEST VEX, an orangutan
MANAGER MARA, a macaque
BELLHOP PIP, a capuchin
COOK NARI, a lemur
CLERK ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. MOUNTAIN HOTEL LOBBY - MORNING

Vex stands beside a suitcase and gazes through the window at the mountain.

VEX: We must leave before the peak forgets us.

MARA: Checkout is at eleven. The peak will still be there.

ODO: You still have the room key. The bill is paid except for breakfast.

PIP: The luggage cart is ready by the door.

NARI: Breakfast ends in twenty minutes. I can pack a roll for the trail.

VEX: The journey ends in this lobby.

MARA: Your stay ends. The mountain does not.

Vex opens the suitcase and removes a small decorative stone he packed from the room's nightstand. Mara asks him to put it back; hotel items and landscape rocks are not souvenirs.

VEX: It has witnessed my dreams.

PIP: It is a room decoration. Please leave it on the shelf.

SCENE 2. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Odo checks the room key against the ledger. Vex asks for a ceremonial key return, with a speech and a farewell bell. Mara points to the brass key tray.

MARA: Put the key here. We will mark the room vacant.

VEX: Should the bell toll once for each night?

ODO: You stayed three nights. The bell is for the front desk, not for guests.

NARI: The trail shuttle leaves at ten-thirty. Your booking includes a packed lunch; breakfast is the remaining charge.

Vex begins an emotional speech about the staircase, the rug and the mountain-facing window. Pip places the suitcase on the cart, but Vex stops him to finish describing the curtains.

PIP: The cart is ready when you are.

VEX: The curtains have seen the first light.

MARA: They will see it tomorrow too.

Vex puts the decorative stone back on the shelf. He keeps the key in his hand and turns toward the lobby door instead of the tray.

SCENE 2A. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Before leaving for the breakfast room, Vex asks Mara to stamp his travel journal as proof that he stayed at the mountain. Mara offers a receipt instead. Vex wants the stamp to include a silhouette of the peak.

MARA: We have a date stamp and the hotel name. That confirms your stay.

VEX: The mountain should sign the record.

ODO: The mountain is not listed as a staff member.

PIP: I can watch your suitcase while you finish breakfast. It is already on the cart and will stay by the lobby desk.

NARI: I'll keep the packed roll at the breakfast counter. You can take it when you're done.

Vex gives the journal to Mara. She stamps it once. He asks if the ink will fade; she explains that it is the same stamp on every guest receipt.

VEX: A standardized memory.

MARA: A hotel record.

He leaves the journal closed on the counter and keeps the room key in his hand as he heads to breakfast, delaying checkout.

SCENE 3. INT. HOTEL BREAKFAST ROOM - LATER

Nari hands Vex a wrapped roll. The room is nearly empty. Pip waits with the cart in the hall; Odo brings the breakfast receipt and a reminder that the key tray is back at reception.

NARI: Here is your trail lunch. Eat the fruit now or keep it in the side pocket.

VEX: The final meal must be remembered.

NARI: It is a roll and a pear.

Vex pays the breakfast charge to Odo, who marks the receipt. Vex takes a bite of the roll, then pauses to make a toast to the mountain. The toast lasts long enough for Pip to push the waiting cart up to the breakfast-room doorway.

PIP: We have five minutes before the shuttle.

VEX: The mountain has asked me to stay.

Mara comes from reception to remind Vex of the time.

MARA: The mountain is outside. It cannot change your shuttle ticket.

Odo returns to reception with the receipt. Vex realizes he has not returned the key. He walks back to the lobby once, collects his stamped journal and puts the key on the tray. Odo marks the room vacant.

ODO: Key returned. Checkout complete.

VEX: The room is released from my care.

MARA: The room is ready for the next guest.

SCENE 4. EXT. MOUNTAIN HOTEL - MORNING

Vex stands by the shuttle, holding the wrapped roll and his suitcase. Pip secures the luggage. Nari waves from the doorway, and Mara checks the departure list.

VEX: I carry the mountain in my heart.

MARA: You carry lunch in your hand. The mountain is behind you.

PIP: Bags secured. Shuttle is ready.

ODO: Receipt is filed. The key is back on its tray.

Vex waves at the peak through the shuttle window while the driver waits to close the door. The hotel remains open; the next guests step into the lobby.

TAG: Before the door closes, Vex asks the driver whether the shuttle can circle the mountain once before the station. From the curb, Mara points to the printed route; it already goes around the mountain. The door closes and the shuttle departs.
