# The Last Three Minutes of the Film

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: experimental cinema, prolonged silences, abrupt jump cuts, and endings that arrive before the plot does*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
EDITOR VEX, a macaque
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
PROJECTIONIST LUMA, a lemur
AUDIENCE MEMBER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING ROOM - NIGHT

Nari watches a cut of her new film. The first two hours show Pip crossing an empty room. The last three minutes contain the entire story: Pip finds a key, opens a door, and meets his old friend. Vex says the ending is too clear. Nari says it is the only part with dialogue.

PIP: I have been walking for forty minutes.

VEX: The audience feels the walk.

NARI: The audience has seats.

SCENE 2. INT. CINEMA - EVENING

Luma prepares the film for its premiere. She checks the projector and finds two reels labeled ENDING. One is a close-up of a key; the other is a close-up of the door. Vex says he will decide their order during the screening. Odo enters carrying a cushion, hoping to sit comfortably through the long walk.

The lights dim. The film begins. Pip walks across a white room, turns, and walks back. A clock on screen advances by one minute in each shot. The audience grows restless. Odo adjusts his cushion. Luma watches the projector timer, which estimates another hour.

SCENE 2A. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - EVENING

Luma inspects the reel labels with Nari. One reel has no title and is marked "middle." Vex says the middle should remain missing because the audience can imagine it. Nari asks what happens to the character during the missing hour. Vex describes several possibilities, none of which appear on film. Luma threads the reel into the projector. It contains footage of Pip walking to a chair and sitting down.

ODO: That is something.

VEX: It is almost nothing.

NARI: It is the middle.

The group discusses whether the chair matters. Pip says he sat down because the room had no door. Nari asks the editor to keep the shot where he notices the key under the chair. Vex says the image is too direct. Luma marks the reel so it cannot be swapped by mistake.

SCENE 3. INT. CINEMA - LATER

Pip reaches the door. Vex has cut the shot just before he opens it. The screen goes black for thirty seconds. Odo asks whether the projector has stopped. Luma checks the machine; it is running. Nari listens to the audience shift and decides to show the next reel. Vex reaches for the key close-up, but the door reel is loaded first.

The film cuts to the door handle, then back to Pip standing across the room. The key is never shown. Odo recognizes that Pip already has it in his hand. He points at the screen, and the audience laughs. Vex looks startled to hear laughter during the silent section.

SCENE 3A. INT. CINEMA - CONTINUOUS

The screening continues. Pip reaches the door and tries the key. It does not fit. Odo, in the audience, recognizes the key as belonging to the small cabinet beneath the screen. Nari asks Luma to pause the projector, not the audience. Luma holds the frame while Vex swaps the two ending reels in the booth. They resume with Pip finding the spare key and opening the door once.

The friend waits in the lobby. The final cut lets Pip cross the threshold, then uses one jump cut that briefly places the friend on both sides of the door. The audience laughs. Nari accepts the odd edit because the meeting is clear and the splice is visible.

SCENE 4. INT. CINEMA LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER

The audience steps out to stretch. Odo says the last three minutes were the clearest part. Vex says the walk mattered too. Pip, still in costume, joins them at the lobby door and finally meets the actor playing his friend.

PIP: We have been waiting in separate hallways.

VEX: That is what the film was about.

NARI: The audience followed you both to the same door.

SCENE 5. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - NIGHT

Luma labels the corrected reels "KEY, DOOR, MEETING" and locks them in order. Vex asks whether they should preserve the earlier cut. Nari says yes, in the archive, under "long walk version." Odo returns his cushion to its seat and notices the projector still shows the door's last frame. Luma turns off the lamp; the frame disappears.

Vex reaches for the label maker to rename the folder "Unresolved Door Study." Luma turns the label maker around; its tape has run out. He writes the title on the archive card by hand, then leaves enough room for the running time. Nari adds "three minutes of ending" beneath it, and Odo checks that the card fits the drawer before closing it.

FINAL IMAGE: The projector goes dark, leaving the door's rectangle of light on the empty screen for one quiet beat.
