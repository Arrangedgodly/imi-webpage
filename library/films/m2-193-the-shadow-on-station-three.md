# The Shadow on Station Three

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: film noir mysteries, hard-boiled narration, venetian-blind lighting, and clues that turn out to be ordinary objects*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
MARLOW, a capuchin private investigator
ADA, a ring-tailed lemur night clerk
MR. FINCH, a gibbon station manager
THE SHADOW, a figure seen through window blinds

SCENE 1. INT. RIVER STATION - NIGHT

Rain stripes the windows. A desk lamp cuts the office into bright bars and dark bars. Marlow enters, hat low, coat collar high. He narrates to the camera.

MARLOW (V.O.): The case arrived at midnight, wrapped in a timetable and carrying a shadow.

Ada slides a note across the counter: "The platform clock is five minutes ahead."

MARLOW: Who gains from a fast clock?

ADA: Anyone trying to catch the last train.

Marlow studies the clock. It ticks loudly, then skips a beat.

SCENE 2. INT. PLATFORM THREE - NIGHT

A shadow moves across the platform wall. Marlow follows it past a column. No one is there. The shadow reappears beside a suitcase.

MARLOW (V.O.): The shadow was always one step ahead. It knew the station better than I did.

Ada points to a passing train outside. Its headlamp sweeps across the window blinds, throwing a moving silhouette onto the wall.

MARLOW: The train has an alibi. It was outside.

ADA: The train makes the shadow.

A station announcement interrupts them, naming a delayed train. The platform clock advances two minutes at once.

SCENE 2A. INT. TICKET HALL - LATER

Marlow questions Ada about the note. It has a smear of blue ink and a crescent-shaped tear. She recalls that a clerk carried a timetable in the rain and used the note as a bookmark.

MARLOW: A clue left by the weather.

ADA: A wet paper edge.

Marlow examines the light stripes on the floor. They shift as a train passes outside. The shadow falls across the platform clock, then disappears when the train leaves.

MARLOW: The station's suspect has wheels.

ADA: The train is on the schedule.

MARLOW: Most suspects are.

SCENE 3. INT. CLOCK ROOM - LATER

Mr. Finch maintains the clock. He removes the back panel and finds a moth caught in the gears. The moth flutters, slowing the mechanism. When it escapes, the hands jump forward.

FINCH: The clock was not running fast. It was catching, then overcorrecting.

MARLOW: A mechanical witness.

ADA: It's a moth.

The clock resumes steady time. Marlow asks why the station schedule disagrees. Finch shows him the timetable, which was printed from last week's clock setting. The station is operating correctly; the paper schedule is out of date.

SCENE 3A. INT. CLOCK ROOM - NIGHT

Marlow asks Finch to set the clock back five minutes. Finch uses a hand crank. The clock moves, then stops. The moth has left a thread caught in the gear.

FINCH: If we force it, the clock will lose another tooth.

MARLOW: Then let it run at the pace it can keep.

Ada clears the thread with a soft brush. The clock resumes. They compare it with the station's master clock. The platform clock is now correct, but the printed schedule is still old.

ADA: The mystery has two parts: a clock that caught, and a timetable that wasn't replaced.

MARLOW: Two witnesses, neither of them lying.

The station manager checks the wall map. The passenger's train will arrive on time at the east platform. Finch posts the updated schedule and circles the platform number so no one has to guess.

SCENE 4. EXT. PLATFORM THREE - PRE-DAWN

A passenger arrives, worried she has missed the first train. Marlow checks the wall clock, the updated timetable, and the light at the eastern horizon. The train is still ten minutes away.

MARLOW: The shadow told us nothing. The clock told us too much. The timetable told us yesterday.

ADA: And the train will arrive in ten minutes.

Marlow investigates the briefcase near the bench. It belongs to a station cleaner, who is sleeping in the break room. Inside are polish rags and a sandwich. The mysterious note came from him; he wrote it to remind himself to reset the clock after maintenance.

SCENE 5. INT. STATION OFFICE - MORNING

Finch updates the clock. Ada prints a new timetable. Marlow returns the note to the cleaner and leaves the sandwich untouched.

MARLOW (V.O.): The city woke. The rain quit. The shadow stayed where the light put it.

A train arrives exactly on the minute. The passenger boards. The cleaner wakes, checks the clock, and takes his briefcase.

FINAL IMAGE: Sunlight passes through the venetian blinds. The stripes fall across Marlow's face. He steps sideways, and the shadow follows him in the simplest way possible.
