# The Planet with One Door

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: science-fiction invasion films that turn first contact into a military countdown before anyone tries to communicate*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: An alien capsule lands outside a monkey weather station, where the crew prepares for an invasion before discovering its passenger is looking for the station's only open door.

Cast: astronomer Yali, a lemur; station chief Omo, an orangutan; guard Peki, a macaque; visitor Zuzu, a small extraterrestrial puppet operated by a visible gloved hand; engineer Sela, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. WEATHER STATION - NIGHT

A silver pod settles in the snow. Yali watches through binoculars. Omo sounds the alarm. Peki rolls out a wheeled barricade and parks it across the entrance.

PEKI: Nobody comes through.

SELA: The station door is behind the barricade.

Omo orders the lights dark. The crew crouches behind sandbags. A small hatch on the pod opens. A paper flag emerges, marked with a drawing of a door.

YALI: It may be a declaration.

SELA: It may be a picture of a door.

SCENE 2 - INT. WEATHER STATION ENTRY - CONTINUOUS

The crew argues over a red button labeled EMERGENCY BROADCAST. Omo wants to announce an invasion. Sela says the pod has not moved since landing. Peki points a flashlight through the window. The beam reflects off a handwritten note on the pod: PLEASE OPEN.

OMO: That could be a threat.

YALI: It has a question mark.

The note is folded into a paper airplane and slides under the door. It reads: "Cold outside. One door?" Sela looks at the wheeled barricade blocking their only entrance. Peki tries to roll it aside, but its brake is on.

SCENE 3 - EXT. WEATHER STATION - LATER

The crew cautiously opens the door. Zuzu stands beside the pod, wrapped in a silver blanket. The visible puppeteer's gloved hand holds a small suitcase. Zuzu points at the station door, then at the blanket.

ZUZU: Door. Warm?

YALI: You want to come inside?

Zuzu nods. Omo asks for identification. Zuzu produces a star chart, which unfolds across the snowy ground and covers Peki's boots. Peki steps carefully off it.

PEKI: Their map is larger than our station.

SELA: It is a star chart.

Omo moves the barricade. The brake snaps loose and it rolls down the slight slope, stopping against the pod. Everyone watches for an attack. The pod door closes with a soft chime.

Omo requests a formal statement from the visitor. Yali places a notepad on the table and asks simple questions. Zuzu answers by drawing pictures: a snow cloud, a damaged panel, a small heater. Peki takes each sketch as a tactical diagram and positions cups around the room to mark defensive points. Sela collects the cups before anyone spills them.

SELA: They are asking for a repair and a warm place to wait.

PEKI: This cup was covering the western approach.

Yali asks Zuzu to point to the damaged part. Zuzu lifts the pod's loose panel; a small screw rolls under the table. Omo reaches for it, but Peki pins it with a boot, thinking it is a device. Sela retrieves it with a magnet from the tool drawer. The crew all exhale at once.

SCENE 4 - INT. WEATHER STATION COMMON ROOM - NIGHT

Zuzu warms up near a heater. Omo sets a long interrogation lamp beside the chair. Sela switches it off and offers a cup of warm water. Zuzu draws a new symbol on the fogged window: a circle, a line, and a door.

YALI: Is that your planet?

Zuzu draws a second circle and points to the station. The crew realizes Zuzu was seeking shelter during a storm, not claiming territory. Peki asks whether the pod can fly again. Zuzu taps the roof. A warning light blinks.

SELA: The landing damaged a panel.

Omo looks at the emergency broadcast button. It has been pressed. A loudspeaker outside repeats, "Possible invasion, possible invasion," while Zuzu waves from the window. The emergency announcement then repeats, "Possible blanket, possible blanket."

SCENE 5 - EXT. WEATHER STATION - DAWN

The crew repairs the pod's loose panel with a bracket from the supply shed. They attach a sign beside the station entrance: OPEN DOOR, WARM ROOM, ASK FIRST. Omo cancels the broadcast, but the loudspeaker sputters once more before going silent.

OMO: We should send a correction.

YALI: We can say our visitor asked for shelter.

PEKI: And our barricade attacked the pod.

Sela rolls the barricade back beside the entrance and leaves its brake off. Zuzu waves, boards the capsule and lifts into the pale morning sky.

FINAL IMAGE: The station's welcome sign swings on one hinge as the empty barricade rolls gently in a circle beneath it.
