# For Your Consideration, One Missing Reel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: awards-season campaign films that treat every conversation as a chance to lobby voters and every small detail as a legacy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A small film crew prepares a screening for awards voters, but their campaign manager keeps interrupting the movie to explain why it deserves to win.

Cast: Director Sela, a macaque; campaign manager Voro, a gibbon; editor Nima, a capuchin; projectionist Tavi, a lemur; guest Oru, a baboon.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA - NIGHT

A poster reads FOR YOUR CONSIDERATION: THE LAST PAPER BOAT. Voro places gold stars around it. Sela and Nima carry a film can to the booth.

VORO: The voters arrive in five minutes. We should remind them the film is about resilience.

SELA: It is seven minutes long. The boat folds in a puddle and the child makes another one.

VORO: A profound meditation on impermanence.

NIMA: It is a paper boat.

TAVI: The projector is ready. I need the film can and the cue sheet.

Voro hands him a campaign pamphlet instead. Tavi tries to load it into the projector gate.

SCENE 2 - INT. CINEMA LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER

Oru arrives. Voro walks beside him, pointing at every gold star.

VORO: The director used natural light, folded all boats by hand and never compromised the vision.

ORU: I have not seen the film yet.

VORO: The short runtime is a statement about scarcity.

SELA: We had one roll of film.

Oru sits. Voro places a ballot card on the seat. The card covers the screen's first row.

TAVI: The image is blocked.

VORO: The card is a reminder to consider the film.

TAVI: It is in front of the lens.

SCENE 2A - INT. CINEMA LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Voro unrolls a red carpet across the lobby. It stops short of the auditorium door. He stretches it with both hands until it covers the campaign table. The ballot box tips sideways.

ORU: The film has no premiere carpet.

VORO: It has a premiere. The carpet is an invitation to take it seriously.

SELA: The screening is the invitation.

Nima rights the ballot box. One card slips out and lands under the table. Tavi crawls to retrieve it.

TAVI: I found the ballot. I also found a spool of tape and three pamphlets.

VORO: I prepared a different pamphlet for each voter.

ORU: There is one voter here.

VORO: We can still personalize the message.

SELA: Please use the time to find the film can.

Voro folds the carpet back into a narrow runner, leaving the box upright and the auditorium entrance clear.

SCENE 3 - INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - CONTINUOUS

Nima discovers the reel is missing. The film can is empty. Voro has used it to hold the campaign pamphlets.

NIMA: Where is the reel?

VORO: It was in this can.

SELA: It was on the editing table. We packed the can at the end of the day.

TAVI: The show starts in two minutes.

Voro searches the pamphlet stack. The reel is not there. Nima checks the lobby table. The poster has fallen over, revealing the film reel propping up one corner.

SCENE 4 - INT. CINEMA LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER

Sela retrieves the reel. She places it in the can and hands it to Tavi. Voro begins a speech about the near-disaster.

VORO: This is a story of adversity, a campaign that overcame the odds.

SELA: We put the reel under the poster. We should stop doing that.

TAVI: Ready to screen.

Oru takes the ballot card off the seat and lays it face down on his lap.

ORU: I would like to watch the film now.

Voro takes a breath, then sits. The lights dim. The movie plays: a child folds a paper boat, sets it in a puddle, watches it soften, and folds another from a clean sheet.

SCENE 5 - INT. CINEMA - LATER

The lights come up. Oru looks at the campaign card.

ORU: That was a quiet, complete film.

VORO: Did you notice the symbolism of the water?

ORU: I noticed the boat sank.

SELA: It was paper. The child had more.

VORO: The ballot asks for one category. Best Short, Best Editing, Best Campaign.

NIMA: The campaign did not make the film.

Voro holds up the pamphlet, which has a small crease from the projector gate. He smooths it with the side of his paw.

VORO: I can revise the card to say "Please watch before voting."

TAVI: That is all it needed to say.

Oru drops his ballot in the box. Voro watches but does not ask how he voted.

FINAL IMAGE: The unlit screen reflects the poster, now standing on its own, while the empty film can rests safely on the shelf beside the exit.
