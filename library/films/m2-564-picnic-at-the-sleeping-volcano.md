# Picnic at the Sleeping Volcano

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster adventures that stage a catastrophe around a perfectly safe picnic hill*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
PICNIC HOST NARI, a lemur
GEOLOGIST MARA, a macaque
ANNOUNCER VEX, an orangutan
COOK PIP, a capuchin
RANGER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. VOLCANO OVERLOOK - DAY

A grassy hill rises behind a picnic table. A sign reads DORMANT VOLCANO: TRAIL OPEN. Vex points toward a thin wisp of steam from a kettle.

VEX: The volcano is waking.

MARA: That's steam from Pip's kettle. The volcanic vent is on the other side of the marked path.

PIP: The kettle is on the camp stove, and the flame is low.

ODO: The ranger report says no activity. The trail is open.

NARI: Let's place the food on the table and keep the walkway clear.

SCENE 2. EXT. PICNIC TABLE - CONTINUOUS

Vex holds a paper map upside down. Mara rotates it to the right direction.

VEX: The red line points to the crater.

MARA: The red line marks the picnic boundary. The crater trail is closed today for maintenance.

ODO: There is a barrier at the junction and a sign.

VEX: I saw no barrier.

PIP: You walked past it. It's behind the map.

A gust lifts a napkin. Nari catches it and weighs the corners with clean stones.

NARI: Wind is picking up. We'll pack the light plates before they blow away.

VEX: Is the volcano causing the wind?

MARA: The forecast says a valley gust. No volcanic activity.

SCENE 2A. EXT. PICNIC OVERLOOK - DAY

Mara unfolds the official trail map. Odo points to the picnic boundary and the closed crater spur. Pip sets the kettle on the designated camp pad.

ODO: The overlook is open. The crater spur is closed for maintenance until tomorrow.

VEX: The red line is a prophecy.

MARA: It's the boundary on the map. We stay on this side of it.

NARI: The table is level. I'll place the plates at the center so the wind can't take them.

PIP: The stove is on the pad, away from the grass. Kettle is heating.

Vex picks up a napkin and waves it toward the distant hill. It flutters in the valley breeze.

VEX: The mountain is exhaling.

MARA: That's wind through the saddle. The ranger report says no activity.

ODO: We can hear the road below. The sound came from the maintenance truck.

PIP: The water is boiling. I'll turn off the stove and pour it into the thermos.

NARI: The flame is off. The kettle is on the pad, cooling.

VEX: We have survived the eruption.

MARA: We have prepared tea. Do not touch the kettle until it cools.

Pip places the thermos on the table and caps it. Odo folds the map and keeps the closed-trail notice visible.

SCENE 2B. EXT. PICNIC TABLE - DAY

Nari notices that a sauce jar is too close to the table edge. She slides it toward the center while Pip pours tea.

NARI: The jar is stable. I'll keep the lid on until lunch is served.

VEX: A tremor has moved the condiments.

MARA: Nari moved it. The table leg is level.

ODO: The trail status remains open to the overlook and closed to the crater spur. No change on the ranger channel.

VEX: The volcano is holding its breath.

MARA: You are hearing the kettle cool. Please stop narrating equipment as geology.

Pip caps the thermos and wraps a cloth around the warm lid. Mara checks that the stove is off and the fuel valve is closed.

PIP: Fuel valve shut. Stove is cool enough to pack.

NARI: We'll eat here, then carry out the dishes and food scraps.

VEX: The final meal before the mountain erupts.

ODO: The forecast says clear through evening.

SCENE 3. EXT. OVERLOOK - LATER

A distant rumble rolls through the valley. Everyone pauses. Odo checks the ranger radio.

ODO: That was a maintenance truck on the lower road.

VEX: The mountain has spoken.

MARA: It was a truck. We stay on the open trail and don't approach the closed junction.

PIP: The kettle is off. I moved it to the table after it cooled.

NARI: The picnic is nearly finished. Let's put out the food and carry the reusable dishes back.

SCENE 4. EXT. TRAILHEAD - AFTERNOON

The group reaches the trailhead. The volcano remains quiet. Odo posts a note about the closed crater spur.

VEX: Our escape is complete.

MARA: We had lunch and walked the marked trail.

PIP: The kettle is in the equipment bag, cool and dry.

NARI: The table is clean, and no litter remains.

TAG: Vex hears another rumble. Odo points to the ranger's empty stomach; it was Vex asking for the last sandwich.
