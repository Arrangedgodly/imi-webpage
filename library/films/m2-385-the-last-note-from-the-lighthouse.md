# The Last Note from the Lighthouse

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic mystery dramas, coded messages, lighthouse signals, and lovers who mistake maintenance logs for declarations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
KEEPER MIRI, a lemur
SINGER VEX, a macaque
ENGINEER ODO, a gibbon
ARCHIVIST NARI, an orangutan
COURIER PIP, a capuchin

SCENE 1. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE - DUSK

A sheet of music appears under the lighthouse door. It contains four notes and a handwritten message: "Play this at the final light." Keeper Miri assumes it is a love song. Singer Vex arrives carrying a violin. Engineer Odo checks the lamp; one lens is loose.

MIRI: The notes may be a message.

ODO: The lens is definitely loose.

Pip arrives with a sealed package for the lighthouse archive.

SCENE 2. INT. LIGHTHOUSE - NIGHT

Miri compares the music with the signal log. The four notes match the pattern used by a nearby harbor buoy. Vex says the tune is too beautiful to be a maintenance signal. Nari opens the archive package and finds a drawing of the lighthouse lens. It was sent by a former keeper as a repair guide.

Vex plays the notes. The lamp flickers in time because the loose lens catches the beam. He calls it a duet. Odo secures the lens and the flickering stops. Vex is disappointed. Miri asks him to play again; the notes now sound ordinary.

SCENE 2A. INT. LIGHTHOUSE ARCHIVE - NIGHT

Nari compares the music sheet with a stack of old signal books. The page is a copy of a harbor code used when fog obscures the buoy. The first two notes mean slow down; the last two indicate the safe channel. Vex plays them as written, but the rhythm is too fast. Miri taps the pattern on the railing. Odo recognizes it as the timing used for the lamp's flash sequence.

ODO: The tune is a signal when the fog is thick.

VEX: It can still be music.

Pip checks the current weather report. Fog is moving toward the harbor. Miri asks Odo to inspect the lamp while Vex practices the slower pattern. Nari writes the notes beside the printed signal chart so they can be used by both the keeper and the harbor crew.

SCENE 3. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE BALCONY - LATER

Pip retrieves the buoy's signal chart from the harbor office. The notes indicate fog conditions and the safe channel. Before fog arrives, Miri asks Vex to rehearse the sequence from the balcony while Odo watches the lamp. Vex plays too quickly; Miri taps the slower rhythm on the railing. The beam now matches the harbor code.

A test horn answers from the dock. Nari confirms the signal was received. Vex finishes the phrase and asks Miri whether the song means something to her. She says it means the harbor is clear. Odo writes the tested timing beside the lens repair note.

SCENE 3A. EXT. HARBOR - NIGHT

The fog reaches the water. A small boat sounds its horn once, then waits. Miri turns the lamp to the safe channel and signals the fog pattern. Vex plays the notes slowly from the balcony. Odo watches for the boat's response. A second horn sounds from the channel. Nari checks the chart and confirms the boat is turning away from the rocks.

PIP: The signal worked.

MIRI: The crew heard it.

The boat enters the harbor and ties up at the lower dock. The passengers are safe. Vex lowers his violin and asks whether the last note should be held. Miri says it should be held only until the boat is in the channel. Odo secures the lamp housing and checks that it does not flicker.

SCENE 4. INT. LIGHTHOUSE ARCHIVE - DAWN

Nari files the old repair drawing with the current lens record. Miri adds the buoy notes to the signal chart. Vex copies the tune into his music book. He titles it "Harbor at Dusk." Odo checks the lens one last time. It is secure. Pip returns the package wrapper to the recycling bin.

MIRI: The final note got the boat home.

VEX: It may also be a song.

MIRI: Both can be true.

SCENE 5. EXT. HARBOR - MORNING

The boat docks. The crew waves from the lighthouse balcony. Vex asks if Miri will hear the song again. She says yes, at the next signal test. He agrees to play it then. Nari records the test time on the calendar. She also adds the fog signal pattern to the keeper's copy of the harbor chart. Odo writes the next inspection beside the music notation, so no one mistakes the tune for an unscheduled warning again.

FINAL IMAGE: The lighthouse beam holds steady over the water as the violin's last note fades behind the buoy bell.
