# The Quiet Planet Charge

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-opera blockbusters, ominous prophecy, bridge-wide command shots, and battles paused for a ceremonial speech*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
CAPTAIN NYX, a macaque pilot
LIEUTENANT ORI, a gibbon navigator
ENGINEER PALI, a capuchin
THE ORACLE, a ring-tailed lemur

SCENE 1. INT. STARSHIP HORIZON - BRIDGE

Stars stretch across the main screen. Captain Nyx stands before a huge chair designed for someone with much longer legs. A red warning flashes: "THE PLANET IS QUIET."

ORI: We have entered the orbit of Vellum, the silent world.

NYX: Why is it silent?

ORI: No transmissions, no engines, no weather. Only a beacon at the northern pole.

PALI: The beacon is transmitting a low battery warning.

NYX: A warning from the ancients.

PALI: It says, "Replace battery."

The Oracle enters through the bridge doors, trailing a ceremonial cape. It catches on the door handle. The Oracle continues walking, leaving the cape suspended behind.

ORACLE: The prophecy has begun. When the silent world calls, the chosen crew must answer.

PALI: The beacon calls every six minutes. It's a timer.

ORACLE: The prophecy repeats.

SCENE 2. EXT. VELLUM - NORTHERN POLE

The crew descends in a silver shuttle. An enormous stone monument bears three carved symbols: a cup, a hand, and a small square. The beacon blinks beneath it.

NYX: This must be the key to the planet's fate.

ORI: The inscription says "cup, hand, square."

PALI: Or it's a set of instructions for the battery cover.

The Oracle studies the symbols and raises both hands. The monument does nothing. Nyx places a cup under the blinking beacon. Still nothing. Ori uses a hand to point toward the square symbol.

A panel clicks open. Behind it is a dusty battery compartment.

ORACLE: We have awakened the machine.

PALI: We found the latch.

SCENE 3. INT. BEACON CHAMBER - CONTINUOUS

The old battery is stuck. Pali tries to lift it. Nyx tells the crew to assume defensive positions. Ori scans for energy signatures. The Oracle begins a solemn chant.

NYX: What is the source of the tremor?

ORI: The beacon sends a vibration through the pole every time the timer cycles.

PALI: I can remove the battery, but I need the panel to stay open. Someone hold the hinge.

Nyx braces the panel with her boot. The Oracle holds the ceremonial cape out of the dust. Ori watches the timer and calls each pulse before it arrives. At the next blink, Pali loosens the corroded edge with a plastic tool.

ORACLE: The ancient device has accepted our offering.

PALI: I have not offered anything. I'm using a nonconductive pry tool.

NYX: Keep the panel steady. We will wait for the next cycle.

The beacon blinks. The panel shifts open a fraction. Pali catches the old battery before it falls. A green crust clings to the contact. She wipes it with a dry cloth and inspects the chamber for a leak.

ORI: No fuel, no heat, no hidden signal. The device is a small emergency beacon.

NYX: Then the prophecy can wait until after the repair.

NYX: We face an ancient force.

PALI: We face corrosion.

The crew coats the battery seam with warm cloth, then waits. The panel's timer continues blinking. At six minutes, the beacon sends the low-battery signal again. The sound reverberates around the chamber. The Oracle flinches and calls it the voice of the planet.

Pali removes the old battery. The beacon stops blinking. The entire planet remains quiet.

ORI: We have ended the transmission.

NYX: And silenced the world.

PALI: It was one device.

A tremor rolls through the chamber. Dust falls from the monument. A deep rumble begins.

ORACLE: The world stirs!

PALI: The shuttle's engine is idling outside. It's echoing through the rock.

SCENE 4. EXT. VELLUM - NORTHERN POLE

The crew boards the shuttle. The engine vibrates the monument. The square panel swings open and closes with the rhythm of the motor, producing a booming drumbeat.

NYX: The planet is answering us.

ORI: It's the panel.

NYX: Then we will answer in rhythm.

The crew taps a beat against the shuttle hull. The panel swings in time. Their improvised rhythm grows into a grand anthem. The Oracle conducts with both hands. A local species of tiny burrowing primates emerges from the rocks, curious but unafraid.

SCENE 5. INT. STARSHIP HORIZON - BRIDGE

Back in orbit, the captain delivers a report to the fleet. She says Vellum is quiet, inhabited, and capable of percussion when a loose panel is struck by engine vibration. The fleet's admirals request a ceremonial victory speech.

NYX: We came to conquer the silence, but the planet needed a replacement battery and a tighter hinge.

PALI: And a sign explaining the symbols.

ORI: The tiny primates have already made one. It shows a cup, a hand, and a wrench.

ORACLE: The prophecy is fulfilled.

NYX: The prophecy needs a maintenance appendix.

FINAL IMAGE: The repaired beacon flashes once, then settles into steady light. Below, the burrowing primates use the shuttle's discarded packing foam as a stage, tapping the old battery cover like a drum as the Horizon drifts into the stars.
