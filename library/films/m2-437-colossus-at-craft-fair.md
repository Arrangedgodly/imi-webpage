# The Colossus at the Craft Fair

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: giant-monster disaster movies that give a creature a city-sized rampage over a small misunderstanding*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A giant gorilla approaches a town craft fair to retrieve its giant knitted scarf, while local officials mistake the visit for a city-ending attack.

Cast: Goma, a giant gorilla; mayor Luki, a macaque; field reporter Tena, a lemur; marshal Pavo, a baboon; knitter Dori, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - EXT. TOWN CRAFT FAIR - DAY

Stalls line the town green. Dori displays a huge knitted scarf on a mannequin. A low rumble shakes the lemonade cups. Beyond the hill, Goma rises above the rooftops, pointing toward the scarf.

TENA: A colossal figure has appeared at the edge of town.

PAVO: Sound the warning.

DORI: That is my scarf. Goma lent me the wool and came back to collect it.

PAVO: Why is it taller than the clock tower?

DORI: Because Goma is very large.

The mayor arrives in a helmet. She peers through binoculars, which are still capped.

LUKI: We must evacuate the fair. Every stall, every pie, every bunting rope.

DORI: Goma is not angry. She is waiting.

SCENE 2 - EXT. TOWN GREEN - MOMENTS LATER

Pavo directs people toward the community hall. The loudspeaker blares an evacuation message, then automatically switches to the craft fair program announcing a jam contest.

TENA: The town is receiving mixed instructions.

PAVO: Pause the speaker.

The mayor pulls a lever. The speaker plays a recording of a marching band. Goma tilts her head, listening. A row of paper lanterns sways.

LUKI: She has caused the lights to flicker.

DORI: Those lanterns are tied to a loose branch.

Goma reaches toward the scarf, but Pavo raises a foam barricade. Goma pauses and lowers her hand.

SCENE 2A - INT. TOWN HALL RADIO ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The mayor pins a map to the wall. Pavo marks a red circle around the fair, then a wider circle around the clock tower. Tena watches the live camera feed, where Goma is still standing at the hill.

TENA: She has not moved for six minutes.

LUKI: Perhaps she is charging up.

PAVO: The alert asks people to shelter in place. The shelter is the fair tent.

DORI: That tent is made of canvas. Goma is not going to attack it. She is waiting for the scarf.

LUKI: How do you know?

DORI: She left a note with me when she lent the wool. It says, "Back by noon for the scarf."

Tena films the note. The mayor reads it, then removes her helmet.

LUKI: I wish someone had shown me that before I called the emergency meeting.

PAVO: We can lower the alert and tell people to return to the stalls.

Luki reaches for the microphone but the town-hall speaker gives another marching-band burst. Everyone waits for the music to stop.

SCENE 3 - EXT. FAIR ENTRANCE - CONTINUOUS

Dori approaches the giant gorilla carrying a small sewing kit. The marshal follows at a distance with a megaphone.

PAVO: Do not approach the creature.

DORI: Goma knows me. I will ask her to wait while I untie the scarf.

TENA: This is the most dangerous moment in town history.

DORI: This is the knot I used to keep the scarf from blowing away.

Goma kneels. Dori climbs a short ladder and frees the yarn. Goma's hand is large enough to lift the entire mannequin, but she carefully pinches only the scarf.

LUKI: She has taken the town's largest object.

DORI: The mannequin is still here.

PAVO: The fair is still open?

DORI: If people stop running, yes.

SCENE 4 - EXT. TOWN GREEN - LATER

The mayor has closed the emergency alert. Tena's camera is now focused on Dori and Goma. Children hold up knitted squares and wave them. Goma wraps the scarf around her neck. It drags through the grass and gathers a string of fair bunting.

DORI: Lift the end, Goma. The bunting is attached.

Goma raises the scarf. The bunting comes with it, hanging like a ribboned collar. The crowd laughs. Pavo lowers the foam barricade and helps detach the remaining rope.

PAVO: The warning system says all clear.

LUKI: Did it ever say what the threat was?

PAVO: It announced the jam contest twice.

SCENE 5 - EXT. CRAFT FAIR STAGE - SUNSET

Dori gives Goma a knitted square made by the townspeople. Goma accepts it gently and places it in her scarf fold. The mayor offers a microphone but it is too small. Tena asks Dori for an interview instead.

TENA: What did the giant creature want?

DORI: Her scarf.

LUKI: The city is safe, the stalls are intact, and the jam contest begins in ten minutes.

PAVO: I will test the loudspeaker before the next announcement.

Goma waves and walks back toward the hill. One end of the long scarf trails behind her. Dori collects the loose end and makes a neat loop so it does not drag.

FINAL IMAGE: The town clock stands behind an empty mannequin, while Goma's enormous scarf glows in the sunset like a banner leaving the fair.
