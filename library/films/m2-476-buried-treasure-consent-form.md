# The Buried Treasure Consent Form

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: treasure-hunt adventures that treat every old map as a license to trespass and every warning as a dare*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A crew follows a dramatic map to an island treasure, only to discover the X marks a community garden plot whose caretaker expects them to ask first.

Cast: mapmaker Deka, a macaque; captain Pilo, a capuchin; historian Senn, a lemur; gardener Ora, an orangutan; deckhand Vix, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - EXT. HARBOR PIER - DAWN

Pilo unfurls a weathered map across a crate. It shows an island, a cliff, and a large red X. Deka holds down its corners as the wind lifts them.

PILO: At sunrise, we claim the hoard.

SENN: The map says, "Ask at the blue gate."

PILO: A riddle.

VIX: It might be directions.

A gull lands on the map and pecks the X. Pilo shoos it away. The bird flies to a blue gate at the end of the pier, where a sign reads ISLAND GARDEN FERRY.

SCENE 2 - EXT. ISLAND GARDEN - MORNING

The crew steps off the ferry with shovels. Ora meets them beside a neat plot of beans and marigolds.

ORA: Are you the volunteer group?

PILO: We are treasure hunters.

ORA: Then please keep the shovels on the path.

Deka compares the map to the garden. The X is exactly over the bean plot. Senn reads the map's faded border: "For the spring plot auction, lot X."

PILO: The treasure is underground.

ORA: Potatoes are underground. Those are not yours either.

Pilo starts a speech about ancient claims. Ora asks him to hold the ferry crate steady while she unloads watering cans. He does, and the speech collapses under the weight.

SCENE 3 - EXT. GARDEN SHED - LATER

Ora invites them to inspect an old chest stored in the shed. It is painted blue and bears the same X. Pilo pries at the lid with a shovel. It does not move. Vix spots a latch and opens it. Inside are garden tools, seed packets and a ledger.

PILO: The hoard has been reduced to seeds.

ORA: This is the garden's supply chest.

SENN: The mapmaker used the same mark for a storage lot.

Pilo finds a brass key in the ledger pocket and holds it up. A dramatic gust blows loose papers around the shed. One page sticks to the key and reads TOOL SHED SPARE. Deka pulls the paper free and places it back in the ledger.

DEKA: The key belongs here.

Pilo tries to turn the moment into a discovery speech. Ora asks him to sort the seed packets by planting month. He reads each label in a grand voice while Vix stacks them. The performance slows them down; Ora hands him one packet at a time until he stops improvising and finishes the task.

Pilo tries to pose with the shovel for a commemorative portrait. The children arrange him beside the trellis, but the handle sinks into the soft soil and tilts the shovel across his helmet. Ora removes it and sets it with the tools. Pilo asks whether this counts as a trial. Ora points to the watering can he is holding.

ORA: The next trial is keeping the seedlings damp.

He waters the row too enthusiastically. Vix turns the can's rose head upward, and the spray makes a rainbow over the garden. The children cheer; Pilo calls it a celestial omen. Ora hands him the empty can to refill.

SCENE 4 - EXT. ISLAND GARDEN - NOON

A local children's group arrives for planting day. The crew is asked to dig one marked hole for a bean trellis. Pilo declares that the shovel will uncover the island's secret. He digs carefully and finds a small tin. Everyone freezes. Senn opens it. Inside are handwritten notes left by previous gardeners, plus a map of the water line.

PILO: A record of those who came before.

ORA: And the irrigation plan. Please don't lose it.

The group unrolls the water map. Pilo positions it against the old treasure map, which accidentally aligns with the garden rows. Deka finds the X corresponds to the trellis anchor. Vix holds the post while Pilo fills the hole. The children plant beans around it.

SCENE 5 - EXT. FERRY LANDING - AFTERNOON

The crew prepares to leave. Pilo asks Ora to sign the map as a witness to their expedition. She adds: "Permission granted to help plant. No digging elsewhere." Pilo looks at the note, then signs beneath it. Deka rolls the map carefully instead of folding it through the ink.

VIX: Is there treasure on the next island?

PILO: We will ask at the gate.

Ora gives them a tin of seeds for their own dockside planter. Pilo carries it aboard with both hands, protecting it from the wind.

FINAL IMAGE: The red X on the map is pinned above the new trellis, where the bean vines begin to curl around it.
