# Branch Office Hours

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: office comedies about mergers, space planning and organizational charts applied to two neighboring trees*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A corporate team tries to merge two neighboring tree offices into one efficient workspace. The layout collapses when both troops need the same branches for different reasons.

Cast: Consultant Nemi, a gibbon; manager Rook, a baboon; employee Sumi, a macaque; and analyst Piri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. TWIN TREES, DAY

Nemi stands between two trees with a clipboard. A rope connects their lower branches.

NEMI: We can merge the offices by removing duplicated space.

ROOK: We have two trees.

NEMI: Exactly. We have found redundant branches.

Sumi is seated in the left tree, grooming a young monkey. Piri sits in the right tree with a stack of leaves.

PIRI: The finance team uses this branch.

NEMI: They can move to the left tree.

SUMI: The left tree is the nursery.

ROOK: We can stagger the schedule.

The young monkey grabs Nemi's clipboard and climbs higher.

SCENE 2. INT. LEFT TREE HOLLOW, LATER

The team draws an organization chart on a broad leaf.

NEMI: Finance reports to operations, operations reports to management, and management reports to the merger team.

ROOK: Who reports to the tree?

NEMI: The tree is infrastructure.

Sumi taps the hollow wall. A drop of water falls onto the chart.

SUMI: Infrastructure is leaking.

Piri moves the chart. Another drop lands on the finance branch.

PIRI: The leak is a recurring cost.

NEMI: We can relocate finance.

ROOK: To the other tree?

NEMI: That is the plan.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROPE WALKWAY, MOMENTS LATER

Piri carries leaves across the rope. The walkway sways. Nemi follows with a small desk.

NEMI: The combined office has a single shared route.

SUMI: The route is moving.

A young monkey crosses from the opposite direction carrying a fruit bowl. Everyone stops.

ROOK: Who has right of way?

NEMI: The organizational chart does not specify.

SUMI: The bowl is for lunch.

PIRI: The leaves are the quarterly report.

The monkey turns around and returns to the left tree. Piri follows. Nemi turns with the desk. Rook remains between the trees.

ROOK: We have separated the teams again.

SCENE 4. INT. RIGHT TREE HOLLOW, AFTERNOON

The team tests the consolidated office. A desk, a bowl, the report, and three workers crowd one small branch.

NEMI: We have improved utilization.

Sumi tries to turn around and bumps the desk. Piri catches the report before it falls.

PIRI: We have improved density.

ROOK: Where does the nursery go?

NEMI: It was listed as nonessential space.

A baby monkey cries from the other tree. Everyone looks over.

SUMI: The nursery is operational.

NEMI: We can add it back to the chart.

ROOK: The chart is too small.

Piri flips the leaf over. The reverse side is blank.

PIRI: We can use the other side.

NEMI: That would be duplication.

SCENE 4A. EXT. ROPE WALKWAY, AFTERNOON

The team tests the proposed shared route with a tray of fruit, a stack of reports, and the desk. Nemi directs each department.

NEMI: Finance first, then nursery, then operations.

Sumi comes across with a young monkey. Piri carries the reports in the opposite direction.

ROOK: We have two teams on one rope.

PIRI: And the desk cannot turn.

The desk catches on the trunk. A fruit rolls off the tray. Sumi reaches for it, and the young monkey grabs the report.

NEMI: We should reduce the number of active users.

ROOK: They are not users. They work here.

The young monkey climbs to a side branch and places the report on a dry ledge. Piri retrieves it.

PIRI: The side branch is useful.

SUMI: It is also where we keep the nursery.

NEMI: Then the schedule needs more than one workspace.

ROOK: The trees already provide that.

Nemi marks the rope route on the chart, then draws a second route around the trunk.

SCENE 5. EXT. TWIN TREES, SUNSET

Rook ties a second rope between the trees and places the bowls on separate branches. The teams move between them without carrying the desk.

NEMI: We have decided to keep both offices.

ROOK: They are not offices. They are trees.

PIRI: The finance team wants a name for the bridge.

SUMI: Call it the bridge.

NEMI: That is not branded.

The young monkey takes Nemi's clipboard and hangs it from the rope as a sign.

ROOK: The merger is complete.

PIRI: The sign says "Office Hours."

NEMI: That is acceptable.

The teams share fruit between the trees. Nemi tries to carry the desk back, but no one helps.

NEMI: I can move this alone.

The desk catches on the rope.

SUMI: The office is closed.

Nemi lets go. The desk stays wedged between the trees, functioning as a shelf.
