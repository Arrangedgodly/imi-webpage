# The Last Detective at the Banana Peel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: hard-boiled noir detectives who narrate every clue as profound, even when the evidence is a slippery office accident*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A trench-coated detective investigates a banana peel in a corridor, only to uncover an office cleanup rota and a very small mystery.

Cast: Detective Varo, a macaque; Officer Leni, a lemur; janitor Bim, a gibbon; clerk Nara, a capuchin; Director Moss, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. CITY OFFICE HALL - NIGHT

Rain crawls down the window. Detective Varo crouches over a banana peel beneath a flickering lamp. A desk phone rings in the empty office.

VARO (V.O.): The peel lay in the corridor like a confession no one wanted to make.

Leni enters carrying an evidence envelope.

LENI: It is a peel. The break-room bin is six steps away.

VARO: Six steps from justice.

LENI: There is a wet shoe print beside it.

Varo leans in. He slips on a damp floor sign and catches the wall with both hands.

VARO (V.O.): The floor had another witness.

LENI: The sign says WET FLOOR. It is doing its job.

VARO: Mark it as a suspect.

SCENE 2 - INT. BREAK ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Bim wrings a mop into a bucket. Nara holds a clipboard with a cleanup rota.

VARO: Who put the peel in the corridor?

BIM: I did not. I found it by the door and set the sign near it.

NARA: The rota says the office empties food scraps before closing. The bin was full.

VARO: Motive: an overfilled receptacle.

LENI: That is a cause, not a motive.

NARA: I put the peel on a plate so the fruit flies would stay away from the desk. Then someone moved the plate.

BIM: I moved it while mopping. I left the sign.

VARO: You admit moving the evidence.

BIM: I admit cleaning the floor.

NARA: The plate is on my filing cabinet. The peel slid off when the door shut.

Varo lifts the evidence envelope, but it is empty. He looks into the mop bucket.

VARO: The peel has vanished.

BIM: I picked it up with the tongs and put it in the compost caddy.

LENI: We have the peel.

VARO: A suspect escaped into compost.

SCENE 3 - INT. RECORDS ROOM - LATER

Nara searches the cleanup log. Varo opens drawers with exaggerated caution, narrating each one.

VARO (V.O.): The paper trail led to the records room, where the truth hid among forms and forgotten lunch plans.

LENI: The log is on the desk. Read the last entry.

NARA: At 8:12, someone reported a full bin. At 8:17, I moved the plate. At 8:20, Bim mopped the corridor.

BIM: I put the peel in compost at 8:22.

VARO: Why was the corridor wet before you mopped?

BIM: Someone tracked rain in. There are footprints from the entrance.

Leni compares the shoe print to Varo's polished shoes. A matching crescent of wet grit is stuck to his heel.

LENI: Detective, the suspect trail begins at your office.

VARO: I came in through the rain.

NARA: You stepped over the wet-floor sign and the peel.

VARO: I may have nudged the plate.

BIM: The plate has a rubber bottom. It did not move.

Varo examines the plate. His glove leaves a dark fingerprint on its rim.

SCENE 4 - INT. DIRECTOR'S OFFICE - NIGHT

Director Moss reviews the report while Varo stands beside a desk lamp.

MOSS: So the fruit was placed on a plate, the plate stayed on the cabinet, the peel fell when a door shut, and Bim removed it safely.

VARO: A chain of events. A city of shadows.

LENI: No injury, no crime. We should add a smaller bin and move the sign near the entrance.

MOSS: We also need the office to follow its cleanup rota.

VARO: What of the wet shoe print?

LENI: Yours.

Varo looks at his heel and wipes the grit onto the evidence envelope by mistake.

MOSS: Please use the trash can.

SCENE 5 - INT. CITY OFFICE HALL - DAWN

Nara brings in a small compost caddy and sets it beside the break-room door. Bim rolls up the wet-floor sign after the floor dries. Varo writes a report on a typewriter.

VARO (V.O.): The case was closed, but the night left its mark.

LENI: Your report says, "A shadowy figure transported the peel to a hidden receptacle."

VARO: It sounds better than "Bim put it in compost."

BIM: The caddy is labeled COMPOST. It is not hidden.

Varo changes the line to "Janitor Bim disposed of the peel in the compost caddy." He pauses, then adds: "The detective slipped on the sign."

LENI: You can leave that part out.

VARO: The truth must be recorded.

FINAL IMAGE: The report sits beside a clean corridor; its dramatic title, THE CASE OF THE WET PRINT, is underlined while Varo's damp shoe dries on a chair.
