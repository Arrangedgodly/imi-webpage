# The Puppet General Plans a Retreat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: war epics where the command tent is a school puppet theater*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
GENERAL VEX, an orangutan
CAPTAIN MARA, a macaque
SCOUT PIP, a capuchin
MAPKEEPER ODO, a gibbon
PUPPETEER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL GYM - DAY

A cardboard map covers a table. Vex wears a paper helmet. Behind him, Nari holds a puppet army on strings.

VEX: The enemy approaches the north hill.

PIP: The north hill is the equipment closet.

MARA: This is a puppet show rehearsal. The audience sits on the other side of the gym.

ODO: The map's upside down. The stage is south.

VEX: Then the army has marched in the wrong direction.

NARI: The puppets are still on the table. I haven't moved them.

SCENE 2. INT. GYM - CONTINUOUS

Vex moves a paper flag across the map. The paper tears at the fold.

MARA: Stop. The map is tearing.

ODO: The fold is weak. We can use the laminated practice map instead.

VEX: A retreat demands a dramatic tear.

PIP: We can draw a dotted route. Nobody needs to rip anything.

NARI: The puppet strings are tangled. I need everyone to hold still while I free them.

VEX: The cavalry is entangled.

MARA: The strings are tangled. Give Nari room.

SCENE 2A. INT. GYM - DAY

Mara unfolds the laminated map and lays it flat. Odo places the puppet booth at the south end. Pip reads the show plan.

PIP: The audience enters at the east door, sits behind the tape, and leaves through the west door.

VEX: The opposing army is at the north hill.

ODO: That's the equipment closet. We can label it "hill" for the play, but it remains a closet door.

NARI: The strings are free now. I'll test one puppet at a time before we start.

MARA: Keep the audience aisle clear. No props cross the tape.

VEX: I need a battle map with a dramatic arrow.

PIP: Use the dotted route on the laminated page. It shows where the puppets can move without tangling the strings.

Vex draws a bold arrow with a dry erase marker. The marker squeaks. Nari tests the soldier puppet; its arm lifts smoothly.

NARI: The string is clear. The soldier can wave, but the spear is loose.

MARA: Remove the spear until it is secured. Nobody waves it near the audience.

VEX: The army will fight without weapons.

ODO: It's a puppet show about moving out of the gym so the floor can be cleaned.

VEX: The retreat is now the plot.

SCENE 2B. INT. GYM - LATER

A custodian rolls a floor-cleaning machine into the hall. Mara asks the group to clear the route. Vex positions the puppets across the floor.

MARA: The machine needs the center aisle. Move every puppet to the booth.

NARI: I'll gather the strings and keep the puppets upright.

PIP: I'll carry the map and prop box. The cardboard soldiers are in the tray.

VEX: This is a strategic withdrawal under pressure.

ODO: The custodian has asked for the floor. We are simply making room.

Vex takes the map off the table and folds it along its existing crease. The repaired tape holds. He follows Nari to the booth, walking around the cleaning machine rather than across its path.

SCENE 2C. INT. GYM - CONTINUOUS

The custodian's machine passes across the center aisle. Vex's paper army waits in the booth.

VEX: The battle is delayed.

MARA: The floor is being cleaned. We'll rehearse the final entrance after it dries.

NARI: The puppets are in order, strings untangled, spear stored separately.

PIP: The map is flat and the door route is clear.

ODO: I have logged the prop inventory. No one needs to fight over the equipment closet.

VEX: The retreat is complete.

MARA: The rehearsal is ready to resume.

SCENE 3. INT. GYM - LATER

The map is repaired with clear tape. The puppets stand in a row. Nari moves one across the tabletop.

NARI: The unit retreats to the safe side of the stage. No puppet crosses the audience aisle.

VEX: We have lost the hill.

PIP: We have moved the puppet away from the gym door so people can enter.

ODO: The audience line is clear.

SCENE 4. INT. GYM - AFTERNOON

The show begins. Vex announces the retreat. Nari moves the puppets toward the puppet booth. One soldier remains attached to the map.

NARI: This one is still tied to the table.

MARA: We can pause while you unhook it.

VEX: A last stand.

NARI: A knot. Hold the paper while I loosen it.

TAG: The final puppet reaches the booth. Vex salutes; the cardboard map stays taped together.
