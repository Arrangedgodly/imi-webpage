# The Mountain of Unsorted Mail

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mountain-climbing survival epics, where an office stack of mail becomes a treacherous summit*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A pair of clerks climb a towering mail pile to find one missing invitation, while their manager keeps adding envelopes to the slope.

Cast: Clerk Luma, lemur; messenger Piri, capuchin; manager Odo, baboon; sorter Tavi, macaque.

SCENE 1. INT. POST ROOM, MORNING

A waist-high pile of envelopes covers the sorting table. Odo looks up at a tower of letters stacked nearly to the ceiling.

ODO: We have a backlog.

PIRI: It has a summit.

LUMA: The invitation for the garden opening is somewhere inside.

TAVI: The mail should be sorted by route.

ODO: It is sorted by height.

Luma ties a cord around her waist and hands the other end to Piri.

LUMA: I will climb the north face.

PIRI: The north face is the outgoing-mail tray.

LUMA: Then keep it clear.

Tavi points to a crate labeled "urgent." It is empty.

TAVI: We can start with the newest mail.

ODO: The newest mail is on top.

PIRI: That seems reasonable.

ODO: The older mail is underneath and also urgent.

Luma climbs the stack. An envelope slides beneath her hand.

[SFX: PAPER SLIDE.]

LUMA: The surface is unstable.

PIRI: Take the binder clip.

She clips the envelope to the stack. Odo brings a new bundle and adds it to the top.

LUMA: You are changing the mountain.

ODO: The mail keeps arriving.

TAVI: Stop adding to the stack while she is on it.

Odo sets the bundle down. The pile leans. Piri pulls the safety cord; Luma slides a few inches but catches herself on a cardboard box.

LUMA: I have found a ledge.

PIRI: That is the outgoing-mail tray.

LUMA: The ledge is important.

SCENE 2. INT. POST ROOM, LATER

Tavi suggests sorting the letters into baskets. Odo says that would destroy the mountain's structure.

ODO: We need the pile intact to locate the invitation.

TAVI: The invitation is a flat rectangle. The pile is many flat rectangles.

Piri finds an envelope with a blue flower stamped on it. Luma reaches down, but the stack shifts. She removes two envelopes from one side; the pile tilts toward Odo.

ODO: The west face is moving.

TAVI: There is no west face.

PIRI: There is now.

They brace the stack with empty boxes. Luma descends to the table and opens the blue-flower envelope. It is a seed catalog.

LUMA: False summit.

TAVI: We have three mail routes. Let's sort as we go.

Odo hands each of them a basket. Piri separates north, south, and local. The mountain quickly shrinks.

ODO: You are dismantling the feature.

LUMA: We are finding the letter.

During the sort, a gust from the open window lifts a stack of envelopes. Piri catches them against her chest.

PIRI: The mountain is shedding.

LUMA: Close the window.

Odo shuts it, but the letter baskets block the handle. Tavi slides the baskets aside, revealing another envelope trapped underneath.

TAVI: We found a lower layer.

ODO: Is it urgent?

LUMA: It is addressed to the garden opening.

PIRI: We have found a second invitation.

Odo opens it. It is a thank-you note from last year's event.

ODO: The mountain has preserved a message from the past.

TAVI: It is three weeks old.

SCENE 4. INT. POST ROOM, AFTERNOON

The stack is knee-high. Luma finds the garden invitation, addressed to the wrong route. It was beneath the urgent crate.

PIRI: The summit was not the highest point.

TAVI: It was the bottom.

ODO: That is not how mountains work.

LUMA: It is how mail works when you stack it.

Odo admits he put unsorted letters on top because he could not decide where they went. Tavi gives him the local-route basket.

TAVI: Choose a route before the pile forms.

Odo takes the basket. One envelope is too large. He places it on top of the stack.

LUMA: Don't start again.

Odo slides it into the oversized-mail slot.

SCENE 5. EXT. GARDEN PATH, EVENING

Piri carries the invitation to the garden. Luma follows with a small sorted bundle. The mailroom is clear except for one box of envelopes.

PIRI: We found the invitation and delivered the rest.

LUMA: We did not climb.

PIRI: We crossed a mountain of mail.

LUMA: We moved letters into baskets.

Piri opens the invitation. It is an invitation to a mail-sorting party.

PIRI: The garden opening is for the post room's new letter garden.

LUMA: There is a letter garden?

PIRI: It is where they plant seeds from the catalogs.

Luma looks back toward the post room.

LUMA: We should bring the mail.

[SFX: ENVELOPES SHIFT IN BASKET, EVENING BIRDS.]
