# The Magician's Empty Hat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: stage-magic mysteries, secret compartments, planted volunteers, and magicians who conceal the obvious prop in plain sight*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
MAGICIAN MIRA, a lemur
ASSISTANT VEX, a macaque
STAGEHAND PIP, a capuchin
INSPECTOR ODO, an orangutan
AUDIENCE MEMBER NARI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN THEATER - NIGHT

Magician Mira presents a black hat. She reaches inside and pulls out a bouquet. The audience applauds. Inspector Odo watches closely. A volunteer, Nari, checks the hat and finds it empty. Vex bows as if the trick worked anyway.

ODO: The flowers were in your sleeve.

MIRA: That was the first trick.

The theater's silver key disappears during the act. It opens the locked prop cabinet. Odo suspects a theft. Vex says the key was never in the cabinet; it was part of the show.

SCENE 2. INT. BACKSTAGE HALL - LATER

Pip searches the costume rack. He finds three identical black hats and a paper rabbit. The rabbit has a tag reading "Property of the children's matinee." Mira says the key was placed under a tablecloth for the next trick. The cloth is gone. Vex says it vanished mysteriously. Pip says it was taken to laundry.

Odo asks who had access to the key. The stagehands, magician, and audience volunteer all handled props. Nari says she saw Vex put something in his pocket, but it was a handkerchief. Vex pulls out the handkerchief, which contains a small coin, a ticket stub, and a bread crumb.

SCENE 2A. INT. THEATER STAGE - NIGHT

Mira repeats the bouquet trick slowly. She shows Odo the false bottom in the table, the cloth loop, and the sleeve pocket. Vex practices the reveal. He pulls out a paper rabbit too early, then bows to the empty hat. Nari asks him to stop looking at the audience and watch the prop table. He catches the cloth with his cuff, pulling the whole tablecloth sideways.

PIP: The flower is still on the table.

MIRA: That is why we use a table.

Odo finds a small strip of glitter tape under the table edge. It matches the tape on the key, but it could also be from any costume. He collects it in an evidence envelope and asks the crew to avoid moving props until they finish checking the setup.

SCENE 3. INT. PROP CABINET - NIGHT

The cabinet remains locked. Mira performs a second trick to distract the audience while Odo checks the hinges. A false panel slides open, revealing a second hat. It is not the key. It is a hat-shaped cushion used to protect a glass orb. Pip retrieves the orb and places it on the shelf.

Nari finds the silver key taped beneath the magician's table. The tape has the same glitter as Vex's cuff. Odo asks if Vex hid it. Vex says the tape came from the prop repair kit. He was trying to keep the tablecloth from sliding and forgot the key was attached.

SCENE 3A. INT. BACKSTAGE HALL - NIGHT

Nari walks through the sequence from the audience's view. The key is visible on the table before the cloth covers it. When Vex pulls the cloth, the key stays stuck underneath. The trick is not a disappearance, just a missed view. Odo asks whether the key could be moved to the labeled hook before the next act. Mira agrees and repeats the routine using a fake key made of painted wood.

Vex asks if the audience will notice the fake. Mira says they should not be asked to notice the key at all. She places it under the cloth and completes the trick without a second explanation. The theater crew laughs when the handkerchief comes out with another bread crumb.

SCENE 4. INT. THEATER STAGE - LATER

The key is now on its labeled hook. Odo checks that the cabinet opens with the spare key. Vex asks Mira to perform one final illusion, but she uses the harmless paper rabbit instead of the cabinet key. She shows the empty hat, then pulls the rabbit from her sleeve.

ODO: The equipment is accounted for.

MIRA: And the audience still gets a trick.

SCENE 5. INT. THEATER LOBBY - NIGHT

The audience leaves discussing the magic. Nari says the best trick was the one where everyone looked at the hat and missed the table. Odo files a note to attach the key hook to the wall. Pip returns the rabbit to the children's prop box. Mira signs the equipment sheet.

Vex asks whether they can perform the key trick next week. Odo says only if they use a spare key. Mira offers the bread crumb from Vex's pocket as a replacement. He declines.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty hat sits beneath the key hook, while the silver key remains visible above it.
