# The Spare Room Has Two Kings

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal succession dramas, ceremonial room assignments, and rulers who treat furniture as territory*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
KING VEX, a macaque
QUEEN NARI, a lemur
PRINCE PIP, a capuchin
MARA, a gibbon chamberlain
THE MATTRESS, a silent performer

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE HALL - MORNING

A palace notice announces that the spare room has been assigned to the visiting king. King Vex and Queen Nari arrive at the same time, each carrying a key.

VEX: My room.

NARI: My room.

Mara checks the room ledger. Both keys open the same door. Inside stands one large bed with two headboards.

PIP: It has two kings.

MARA: It has two ends.

Vex says the room was promised to him during the treaty. Nari says the same room was offered as a diplomatic courtesy. Mara reads both invitations. One says "spare room, east wing." The other says "spare room, east-facing."

SCENE 2. INT. SPARE ROOM - LATER

The two rulers debate who gets the bed. Vex claims the west headboard. Nari claims the east. Pip lies across the mattress to test it. The bed sags in the middle, rolling both rulers toward each other.

VEX: The bed is choosing a side.

NARI: It is choosing the middle.

Mara asks the two rulers to test each end. Vex lies on the west side and Nari sits on the east. The mattress dips toward the middle, carrying Vex's crown to the floor. Pip retrieves it and sets it on the empty pillow.

VEX: The crown has changed rooms.

NARI: It has rolled six inches.

Mara marks the bed's center with chalk. The line disappears beneath the sagging mattress. She asks them to sit upright. They do. The bed becomes level.

MARA: You can share it.

VEX: A king does not share a headboard.

NARI: A queen does not share a pillow.

Pip stretches a measuring cord across the mattress to divide their territory. The cord sinks into the dip. Vex and Nari each pull an end, and both slide toward the center again.

PIP: The room has a valley.

Mara suggests turning the mattress around. Vex and Nari lift it. One end hits the ceiling, and a small rain of dust falls onto both crowns.

VEX: We are being attacked.

MARA: It is dust.

NARI: The room has more residents than we do.

Mara suggests dividing the room with a curtain. Vex agrees, but the curtain rod is too short. Nari asks for a longer one. Pip finds a pole in the wardrobe. It is a ceremonial flagstaff.

MARA: The room was prepared for a parade.

PIP: The bed has the flag.

Vex hangs a curtain from the staff. It falls across the bed. Nari says they have created a wall, not a room. The flagstaff bends. The curtain drops on Vex's crown.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE ARCHIVE - AFTERNOON

Mara searches the treaty records. The paper is old and brittle. A map shows a shared guest wing, but the room numbers have been crossed out. The east wing has one room, while the east-facing rooms are in the west wing.

NARI: Which invitation did the clerk mean?

MARA: Both.

Pip finds a second page. It lists two beds, one facing east and one in the east wing. The beds were combined for a furniture demonstration.

VEX: We have been arguing over a demonstration.

PIP: The bed has never had a guest.

SCENE 4. INT. SPARE ROOM - EVENING

The rulers return. Mara asks them to separate the two halves of the bed. The mattress is joined by a strip of cloth. Pip pulls the strip. The bed separates into two narrow cots.

VEX: I will take the east-wing one.

NARI: I will take the east-facing one.

The cots face opposite directions. Vex turns his around. The room becomes too narrow to walk between them. Nari moves hers against the wall. Vex follows. The cots touch again.

MARA: You have reunited the beds.

Pip rolls one cot into the hallway. It fits through the doorway, but its legs catch on the rug. He lifts the front end. The cot rolls out of the room with him holding it upright.

SCENE 5. INT. EAST WING CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Mara gives each ruler a separate room. Vex's is in the east wing. Nari's faces east from the west wing. Both receive their intended rooms.

PIP: The spare room is empty.

MARA: It is now a spare room.

Vex asks if the separated cots can be used by the guards. Nari asks for the mattress to be returned to its original shape. Pip says the beds are already in use: one guard has taken each cot.

FINAL IMAGE: Mara closes the spare-room door. Two crowns remain on the shelf above the empty bed frame.
