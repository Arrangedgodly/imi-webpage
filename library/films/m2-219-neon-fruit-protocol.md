# Neon Fruit Protocol

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cyberpunk thrillers, glowing code walls, rooftop pursuits, and hackers who trust a terminal more than a witness*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
KEX, a capuchin street coder
VANA, a macaque courier
BYTE, a gibbon market technician
THE ORACLE, a talking vending machine

SCENE 1. EXT. NEON NIGHT MARKET - NIGHT

Rain glitters on the signs. Kex, a capuchin coder, watches a wall of green code scroll across his cracked tablet. Vana, a macaque courier with a satchel of peaches, skids into the alley.

VANA: The market lights are changing every price to one credit.

KEX: That is either a system breach or a very generous fruit season.

A row of neon signs flashes: SALE, SALE, SALE. Every stall drops its price to one credit. The crowd surges. Kex recognizes a block of code copied from an old game and opens a terminal.

SCENE 2. INT. UNDERGROUND ARCADE - NIGHT

Kex connects his tablet to an arcade cabinet. The screen fills with green symbols. He types quickly, then announces he has located the source: a vending machine called the Oracle on top of the central tower.

VANA: How does a snack machine control the signs?

KEX: It has the fastest processor in the district.

VANA: It also ate my coin.

The cabinet freezes on a pixelated banana. Byte, a gibbon technician, enters carrying a toolkit and an extension cord.

BYTE: You connected to the arcade.

KEX: It had a network port.

BYTE: It has a high score board. You have now put your name on it.

A neon triangle appears on Kex's screen. The code points toward the tower, but the elevator is out.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROOFTOP WALKWAY - NIGHT

The three cross rooftops toward the tower. Vana swings across a laundry line, landing on a water tank. Kex follows more slowly, clutching the tablet. Byte takes the stairs and arrives first.

A drone patrol circles above. Kex hides behind a billboard. The sign changes to a glowing image of a peach, illuminating his hiding place.

VANA: Your disguise is delicious.

KEX: I didn't program the billboard.

BYTE: You connected us to it.

The drone pauses to scan the giant peach. Vana throws one real peach toward an empty roof. The drone follows it, leaving the path clear. Byte opens the maintenance door with a screwdriver.

SCENE 4. INT. CENTRAL TOWER - TOP FLOOR

The Oracle vending machine glows beneath a glass roof. It dispenses a can whenever anyone asks a question.

ORACLE: State your query.

KEX: Who changed the market prices?

ORACLE: You did. Your old game code included a test command: SET EVERY PRICE TO ONE.

KEX: I did that when I was twelve.

BYTE: Did you leave it connected to a city network?

KEX: I was twelve.

The Oracle dispenses a can of sparkling water. Byte finds the control cable running through the roof's sign relay. Vana spots a loose connection; her tail steadies the ladder while Byte reaches the panel.

SCENE 5. INT. CENTRAL TOWER - CONTROL PLATFORM

Kex begins to undo the command. A countdown appears on every sign: ten seconds until all market lights shut down. He tries typing, but the tablet's screen is cracked. The green code reflects in his glasses, making him look like a villain.

VANA: Use the switch on the panel.

KEX: The terminal has the safer interface.

BYTE: It is attached to a vending machine.

Vana pulls the physical switch. The lights flicker. Kex enters the reset sequence with two fingers while Byte holds the loose cable in place. The signs stop changing. The Oracle dispenses one last can, marked "REFUND."

SCENE 6. EXT. NEON NIGHT MARKET - DAWN

The stalls reopen at their proper prices. The crowd returns the fruit they bought for one credit, except for the peaches, which Vana gave away during the chase. Kex apologizes to the vendors and offers to repair their lights. Byte disconnects the Oracle from the city's main network and leaves it with a small local battery.

ORACLE: Would you like to connect to a wider system?

BYTE: No.

The machine dispenses a can anyway.

SCENE 7. INT. ORACLE ALCOVE - MORNING

Byte labels the machine's local battery "LOCAL ONLY." The Oracle asks what a local system is. Kex explains that it can serve the market without controlling the whole district. The machine dispenses a can marked "THANK YOU," then rejects Byte's offer to update its software.

ORACLE: I am content with this version.

VANA: The vending machine has made a decision.

KEX: It has excellent security instincts.

The vendors use the repaired signs to display their own prices. One stall's screen reads "PEACHES: THREE CREDITS." Vana points to it, and the Oracle's display changes to "PEACHES: VERY GOOD."

FINAL IMAGE: Kex walks past a sign glowing "WELCOME"; the Oracle, visible through its tower window, flashes "YOU ARE WELCOME" one letter at a time.
