# The Mountaintop Mailbox

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests and chosen-one prophecies applied to a mail carrier seeking a safe route*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A mail carrier must deliver a single letter to a mountaintop mailbox before a storm. A prophecy, a glowing key and a fellowship of excitable companions keep pulling her toward dramatic but unsafe shortcuts.

Cast: Courier Miri, a macaque; guide Tavi, a gibbon; bard Odo, a baboon; and apprentice Piri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN BASE, DAWN

Miri holds a sealed letter. A small stone glows beside the trail.

ODO: The prophecy has chosen you.

MIRI: I was assigned the delivery route.

TAVI: The weather report says the ridge is clear.

PIRI: The glowing stone points upward.

Miri looks at the marked trail, which points around the mountain.

SCENE 2. EXT. LOWER TRAIL, MORNING

Odo sings about a hidden passage. Piri finds a key-shaped branch.

PIRI: The key fits the gate.

TAVI: The gate is open.

Odo tries the key anyway. It breaks in the lock.

MIRI: We have delayed the letter.

ODO: We have awakened the gate.

TAVI: It was already open.

They pass through the gate.

SCENE 3. EXT. SWITCHBACK, MIDDAY

The group reaches a fork. One trail is steep and narrow; the other follows a safer switchback.

PIRI: The stone points to the steep path.

MIRI: The route map points left.

ODO: The chosen one must climb.

TAVI: The chosen one must deliver the letter.

Miri ties a ribbon to the correct turn. Odo moves it to the steep path for a dramatic reveal.

MIRI: Put the marker back.

Odo returns the ribbon. The group follows the switchback.

SCENE 4. EXT. SHELTER, AFTERNOON

Rain begins. Piri opens the letter case and checks the seal.

PIRI: It is dry.

MIRI: Good.

Odo removes his cloak and puts it over the case.

ODO: My sacrifice will be remembered.

TAVI: You can wear it again.

The group waits out the storm. The glowing stone stops glowing.

PIRI: The prophecy has gone dark.

MIRI: The sky is cloudy.

SCENE 4A. EXT. MOUNTAIN SHELTER, NIGHT

The group waits under a stone roof. Miri keeps the letter case dry. Piri studies the trail map by lantern light.

PIRI: The upper path reaches the mailbox sooner.

TAVI: It crosses exposed rock.

MIRI: We take the marked route.

Odo begins a song about courage. Rain taps the shelter roof.

ODO: The storm will not stop the chosen one.

MIRI: The storm has stopped us.

TAVI: That is why the shelter is here.

Piri places the glowing stone beside the map. Its light falls across the route line.

PIRI: It shines on the safe trail.

MIRI: The lantern is shining on the map.

Odo stops singing and checks the letter case. The seal is dry.

ODO: The mail is safe.

Miri hands out fruit from her pack. They eat while the rain passes.

SCENE 4B. EXT. FINAL SWITCHBACK, EVENING

The group reaches the last turn. The upper trail is steep, while the marked switchback is longer.

PIRI: The mailbox should be above us.

Miri checks the letter seal again.

MIRI: The letter will get there safely.

Odo points up the steep trail.

ODO: It is the final test.

TAVI: It is also loose rock.

Piri secures the map and follows Miri onto the switchback. Odo pauses, then joins them.

ODO: The fellowship chooses the safe road.

MIRI: The fellowship chooses the marked road.

They continue uphill at an even pace, keeping the letter dry beneath Miri's cloak.

SCENE 5. EXT. MOUNTAINTOP, EVENING

They reach the mailbox. It is mounted on a post beside a large bell.

MIRI: The delivery is complete.

Odo rings the bell. Nothing happens.

ODO: The summit has rejected our signal.

Tavi notices a small sign: "PULL ROPE GENTLY."

Miri pulls the rope. The bell rings.

PIRI: We have awakened the mountain.

TAVI: We have read the sign.

Miri places the letter in the box. The lid sticks. She clears a leaf from the hinge and closes it.

The group pauses where the trail divides. Miri checks the ribbon tied to the lower branch, while Tavi compares it with the note in the mailbag. Odo nearly chooses the steeper path because it looks more legendary. Miri points out the clear footprints and the broad, dry stones. They take the safe route together.

SCENE 6. EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAIL, SUNSET

The group begins the safe route back. Odo carries the broken key branch.

ODO: We have fulfilled the prophecy.

MIRI: We delivered the mail.

PIRI: The branch can be a walking stick.

TAVI: It is too short.

Odo snaps off another twig. A small bird takes the piece.

ODO: The fellowship has lost a relic.

The group follows the ribbon markers downhill. The storm clears, and the glowing stone lights again, pointing toward the trail.

MIRI: It was reflecting the sun.

PIRI: It still points the right way.

They continue home together.
