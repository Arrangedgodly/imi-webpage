# The Prophecy on the Back of a Ticket

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests that treat a mundane travel ticket as an ancient prophecy and ignore its printed directions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A fellowship follows a cryptic prophecy across a mountain pass, not noticing that the message is printed on their bus ticket.

Cast: quester Miri, a lemur; warrior Tolo, a macaque; guide Senn, a gibbon; oracle Nema, an orangutan; driver Kavi, a capuchin; gate guard, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - EXT. MOUNTAIN BUS STOP - DAWN

Miri unfolds a paper ticket. The back reads: LAST STOP, NORTH GATE. She holds it toward the rising sun.

MIRI: The light reveals our destination.

TOLO: The ink says north.

Senn studies a nearby sign that points to the North Gate. Miri says the sign is too plain to be meaningful. The bus arrives. Kavi opens the door and asks for tickets. Miri passes hers over but keeps the prophecy side facing outward.

SCENE 2 - INT. BUS - MORNING

The fellowship sits near the back. Miri reads a line aloud: "Remain seated until the gate." Tolo says it sounds like an instruction. Miri insists it is a warning about a trial. The bus passes the East Gate. Senn asks if they should get off. Miri says the prophecy has not spoken.

Kavi calls the next stop, North Gate. The fellowship steps off. The bus leaves. The ticket remains in Kavi's hand.

SCENE 3 - EXT. NORTH GATE - CONTINUOUS

The group finds a gate with a sign: NORTH GATE. A guard asks for their tickets. Tolo points to the sign. The guard says tickets are required for admission. Miri studies the ticket and discovers a number on the front. It matches the guard's entry ledger.

MIRI: The prophecy gave us a number.

GUARD: It is seat 14.

The guard opens the gate. Inside is a bus terminal, not a palace. The oracle Nema waits by a bench with a clipboard. She is conducting an appointment survey for the town transit office.

SCENE 4 - INT. TERMINAL - LATER

Nema asks the fellowship to explain why they travelled to the North Gate. Miri presents the ticket as a sacred text. Nema turns it over and points to the route map printed on the back. The words Miri called prophecy are the bus schedule and transfer notice.

NEMA: The ticket told you exactly where to go.

TOLO: We followed it after adding a mountain quest.

The driver returns with Miri's ticket. She has left the return stub on the bus. Kavi gives it back, and Nema shows them the next return departure. The group hurries to the platform, interpreting the countdown as the final trial.

SCENE 4A - INT. TERMINAL - CONTINUOUS

The group asks Nema to interpret another line: "Keep this half until the journey's end." She points out that it refers to the ticket stub. Miri holds it toward the window, expecting a hidden constellation. The perforation makes three holes of uneven size.

MIRI: The stars are out of alignment.

KAVI: The ticket tore unevenly.

Tolo asks Nema whether the oracle can foresee the bus's next stop. She reads the route display and tells him. The bus announces the same stop a moment later. Miri records it as a prophecy confirmed by public address. The driver announces the next stop, and Senn checks it against the printed schedule before anyone stands. Nema gives her a folded timetable, and Miri begins to unfold it like an ancient scroll. Kavi points to the route number before closing the door.

Miri asks the fellowship to read the return timetable before they board. Tolo checks the stop names and finds the same North Gate printed in both directions. Senn notices the return bus uses a different bay. The group walks to the correct one. Miri calls the bay number a final sign, but Kavi simply points to the display. They board together and keep their tickets ready instead of staging a ritual.

Nema explains that the ticket's route map is designed to be folded along its printed line. Miri folds it into a small square and pockets it. Tolo checks the bus number on the sign. It matches the ticket, so the group boards without asking the oracle to confirm a second time.

SCENE 5 - EXT. BUS TERMINAL - AFTERNOON

The bus is ready to leave. Miri asks whether the North Gate is the realm's threshold. Nema says it is the terminal entrance. Tolo boards first and takes seat 14. Miri follows, folding the ticket so the schedule remains visible. Senn asks for the nearest stop to the palace. Kavi checks the route map and answers plainly.

FINAL IMAGE: The fellowship rides home with the ticket pinned to their travel cloak, the words NORTH GATE visible beneath the marked return time.
