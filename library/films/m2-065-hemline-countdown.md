# The Hemline Countdown

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fashion-runway dramas, impossible makeover deadlines, and judges who treat hems as destiny*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A macaque tailor is given one night to repair a runway collection, while the fashion show's judges demand more drama than the garments can safely support.

Cast: Iva, a macaque tailor; Lume, a lemur designer; Baron Thread, a peacock judge; Cora, a capuchin runway model; and Pipette, a young marmoset assistant.

SCENE 1. INT. BACKSTAGE SEWING ROOM, NIGHT

The runway doors open in six hours. Lume shows Iva a collection of capes, each with a train that reaches the floor.

IVA: These will catch on the stairs.

LUME: The drama is in the length.

Cora tries on one cape and trips over the hem. Iva pins it up. Baron Thread enters, sees the shorter fabric, and declares the silhouette ruined. Cora takes three safe steps and turns without falling.

BARON: The audience expects danger.

PIPETTE: The audience expects seats.

SCENE 2. INT. RUNWAY, LATER

The judges inspect the garments. Baron wants each model to reveal a secret about the clothing. Lume says the capes are made from recycled curtains. Baron calls the confession courageous. Iva notices a loose button and starts sewing it back on. The camera follows the needle as if it is a thriller weapon.

CORA: Is the show really in six hours?

IVA: Five and a half.

BARON: A deadline creates beauty.

IVA: A deadline creates a queue for the iron.

SCENE 3. INT. WARDROBE HALL, MIDNIGHT

Baron Thread orders a sudden makeover for Cora. He swaps her cape for a sequined jacket with sleeves longer than her arms. Cora waves and disappears inside the cuffs. Iva rolls the fabric up and secures it with a hidden stitch. Baron says the result lacks transformation. Cora lifts both arms; the sleeves fall again.

CORA: I have become a curtain.

PIPETTE: A curtain with pockets.

The jacket pockets contain backstage passes from last year's show. Iva asks who forgot them. The assistant costume manager raises a hand. Baron calls the discovery an emotional reveal. Iva puts the passes in the lost-property box.

The zipper on the final coat breaks. Lume wants to staple it. Iva refuses. She asks Pipette to find matching thread. Pipette returns with a spool labeled EMERGENCY GOLD. Iva uses it to sew a reinforced seam. Baron demands a dramatic montage; the assistants instead sort buttons, label outfits, and clear the walkway. The work is efficient but not cinematic.

SCENE 4. INT. RUNWAY, PRE-DAWN

A model's shoe strap snaps as the music begins. Iva crawls forward with needle and thread. Baron urges her to wait until the end of the walk for a dramatic rescue. Cora blocks the runway so the model can sit. The repair takes thirty seconds. The crowd claps; Baron tries to turn the interruption into a planned pause.

The show begins. Cora walks out in the repaired cape, turns at the marked spot, and returns without stepping on the fabric. The judges wait for a near-disaster. The next model's sleeve catches on the backdrop; Pipette holds the cloth while Iva frees it. The audience applauds the repair.

BARON: It lacked a fall.

IVA: It lacked an injury.

Lume tells the audience the collection is made from old curtains. A gust from the stage fan lifts one cape, revealing a small curtain hook still sewn in the hem. Iva removes it with a safety pin. Baron calls it a surprise reveal.

SCENE 5. INT. BACKSTAGE, MORNING

Cora asks Iva to check the remaining seams before the closing walk. Baron tries to announce each repair as a reveal. Pipette hands him a sewing basket and asks him to help sort buttons by size. He does, then calls the task a transformation of his perspective.

The judges compare scores. Baron gives extra points for the longest train. Another judge awards Iva points for safe hems. The score ties. Lume asks which matters more: a dramatic silhouette or a walkable garment. Cora demonstrates that the repaired cape can turn, bow, and sit without catching. The judges award the collection for wearability. Baron asks if that category has a red carpet.

The show ends. Iva returns each garment to its rack and writes care instructions. Baron awards the collection a trophy for dramatic construction. Lume asks whether it can be worn again. Iva says yes, after the hook is removed from the last hem. Cora asks if she can keep the cape. Lume gives it to her, with the safety pins included.

FINAL IMAGE: Baron poses with the trophy, his train caught under a chair leg. Iva quietly frees it; he thanks her without turning the moment into a speech.
