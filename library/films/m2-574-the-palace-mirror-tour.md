# The Palace Mirror Tour

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal biopics where a mirror's reflection gets mistaken for a rival heir*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
GUIDE NARI, a lemur
PRINCE VEX, an orangutan
CURATOR MARA, a macaque
ATTENDANT PIP, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE GALLERY - DAY

A long mirror stands between two portraits. Vex enters and stops before it.

VEX: Another heir has arrived.

NARI: That's your reflection. The mirror is part of the palace tour.

MARA: Visitors should stand behind the floor marker. The frame is fragile.

ODO: The portrait on the left is Queen Sela. The right one is her cousin, not a twin.

VEX: The resemblance is uncanny.

PIP: They have different collars and different dates on the labels.

SCENE 2. INT. GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

Vex moves left; the reflected figure moves left. He steps back; the figure follows.

VEX: It copies me.

NARI: Mirrors reflect people. That's how they work.

MARA: Please don't touch the glass. The frame is being conserved.

ODO: The tour begins at the door. I'll read the dates so visitors can compare the portraits.

SCENE 2A. INT. PALACE ARCHIVE - DAY

Odo rolls out the family tree. Nari reads the portrait labels while Mara keeps Vex behind the rope.

ODO: Queen Sela had one child, who died young. Her cousin inherited the estate but never claimed the throne.

VEX: The mirror heir could still be hiding in the portrait.

PIP: The portraits were painted three years apart. The same tailor made both collars.

MARA: That explains the matching clothes. It doesn't make the cousins the same person.

NARI: We can use the portrait dates in the tour. Visitors often confuse the two names.

Odo pins a corrected card beside the family tree. It lists each cousin separately.

VEX: The court has been divided by a tailor.

ODO: It has been clarified by a record.

Mara checks the mirror frame. One corner has a loose felt pad.

MARA: The frame is slightly tilted. I'll ask the conservator to reset it before the afternoon tour.

PIP: Could the reflection make the portraits appear to switch places?

NARI: Only if visitors stand at the far end. We'll move the floor marker closer to the labels.

Vex stands on the new marker. His reflection lines up between the portraits.

VEX: I appear to be their heir.

MARA: You appear to be a visitor standing between two portraits.

SCENE 2B. INT. PALACE GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

Pip and Odo move the floor marker closer to the labels. Mara checks that it does not overlap the rope around the mirror.

PIP: Visitors can compare the portrait dates from here without getting near the frame.

ODO: I'll add a note that the cousins had a shared tailor. That explains their similar collars.

NARI: The tour group is waiting at the entrance. I can lead them in one at a time.

VEX: I will greet them as the third heir.

MARA: You are the guide's assistant. Use your name, not a royal title.

Vex practices bowing. His reflection repeats every movement exactly.

VEX: This rival has no independent plan.

NARI: The mirror reflects you. The portrait labels explain the two people from history.

Mara secures the frame's loose felt pad with a temporary support. The mirror sits level. Pip wipes a fingerprint from the lower edge using the approved cloth.

MARA: The gallery is ready. No one touches the mirror or the portraits.

SCENE 2C. INT. PALACE GALLERY - LATER

Before the visitors arrive, Nari checks the route once from the doorway. The marker sits far enough from the mirror for people to see both portrait labels. Pip holds the tour card while Mara tightens the temporary felt support at the frame's lower corner.

MARA: Frame is steady. The conservator will replace the pad after today's tour.

PIP: I'll keep the group on the marked side and leave the rope in place.

VEX: Should I confront my reflected rival when the guests come in?

NARI: You should introduce the portraits and let people read their dates.

ODO: Archive card is in its sleeve, with the corrected family note. No one needs to bring the tree into the gallery.

Vex steps onto the marker and tries a solemn pose. His reflection copies him. He changes pose; it copies that too.

VEX: My rival has mastered mimicry.

MARA: That is the mirror doing its job.

Nari opens the gallery door for the waiting group. Vex steps to the side, leaving the view unobstructed.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE ARCHIVE - AFTER THE TOUR

Odo files the corrected card in the archive after the tour group enters the gallery. Mara checks the date in the register; Vex remains by the mirror, still searching for a rival.

ODO: Queen Sela ruled first. Her cousin inherited the east estate, not the throne.

PIP: The archive shows they shared a tailor, which explains the similar collars.

VEX: A conspiracy of wardrobe.

MARA: A shared tailor is not a conspiracy.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

The single tour ends. Nari guides the visitors toward the exit while Mara checks that the mirror rope and floor marker are still in place. Vex stays behind the marker.

NARI: The gallery is closed for the next group.

VEX: The mirror rival departs.

MARA: Your reflection stays until you leave.

TAG: Vex bows to the mirror. The reflection bows too; he bows again.
