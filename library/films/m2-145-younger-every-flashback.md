# Younger in Every Flashback

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: digital de-aging effects that smooth faces while leaving voices, posture, and props stubbornly the same age*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A biopic's hero appears young in flashbacks, but the digital effect forgets to change his reading glasses and his very practiced knees.

Cast: Boro, a gorilla actor; Nima, a lemur visual-effects artist; Rena, a macaque director; and Puck, a capuchin editor.

SCENE 1. INT. SOUNDSTAGE, MORNING

Boro plays an inventor at three ages. Rena films the present-day scene first. Boro sits at a workbench, removes his reading glasses, and examines a drawing. Nima opens the effects preview. The software smooths his face but leaves the glasses on.

RENA: Can we take those out?

NIMA: They are on top of the face layer.

BORO: I need them to read the drawing.

Rena asks for a younger posture. Boro stands quickly, then catches his knee on the bench. Puck adds a flashback card: "Thirty years earlier." Boro looks at it.

BORO: I would have used a taller bench.

Nima adjusts the height of the bench in the preview. The effect stretches the wood but leaves the tools floating at the old level.

SCENE 2. INT. EFFECTS SUITE, LATER

Nima applies the de-aging pass. Boro's face becomes smooth. His voice remains low and gravelly. Rena asks for a younger voice. Boro reads the same line at a higher pitch. The effect makes his mouth move too quickly. Nima turns off the face tracking and tries a younger jaw shape. Boro's mouth stays still while the voice continues. Puck re-syncs the audio. The line lands at the right time but comes from a face that looks surprised.

BORO: I am talking about a lens.

RENA: Can you be less astonished by it?

Nima adjusts the brows. Boro's face settles, but the glasses sit crooked.

RENA: Can you speak naturally?

BORO: This is my natural voice.

Puck slows the dialogue, but the sound now arrives after Boro's lips close. Nima adjusts the face timing. The glasses reappear because they were tracked as part of the eyes. Puck paints them out. Boro can no longer see the drawing.

Boro asks for a large print. The prop team brings one. The drawing is upside down. Nima rotates it, but the tools in the digital frame rotate with it. Rena asks to keep the face young and the set unchanged.

SCENE 3. INT. WORKSHOP SET, AFTERNOON

Boro films the flashback at the bench. He bends over the drawing and braces one hand on the table. Rena asks him to move more quickly. Boro stands, reaches for a tool, then pauses to stretch his back. Puck says the audience will know it is a flashback from the title card.

RENA: We also need the body to feel younger.

BORO: Then give me a chair.

Nima adds a chair in the effects preview. It appears in front of the bench, blocking Boro's legs. She removes it. Boro takes the glasses off and squints at the drawing. Nima restores the glasses, then paints out their reflection. The frames vanish, but so does one eyebrow. Nima restores the eyebrow by copying it from the present-day shot. It blinks half a second after the other one. Puck marks the shot for another pass. Rena asks if the audience will notice. Boro raises the glasses and reads the drawing without digital correction.

BORO: I can see the next line.

The crew laughs. Nima saves the unaltered take.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The crew watches two versions. In one, Boro's face is youthful but his knees creak as he stands. In the other, the face and body look smooth while his original voice remains. Rena asks which version feels honest. Boro says he can play the younger scene with the glasses and keep the line simple.

BORO: I am learning to build a better lens.

The line matches the drawing and his voice. Nima removes the de-aging pass. Rena keeps the flashback card and the original performance. Puck asks whether the title card should say "Thirty years ago" or "Thirty years earlier." The script has both versions in adjacent notes. Rena chooses one and deletes the other. Boro adds his glasses to the prop tray. Nima labels them correctly this time. Puck photographs the repaired drawing and attaches it to the continuity board beside both age cards.

FINAL IMAGE: Boro sits at the workbench with his glasses on. A new drawing shows a chair with stronger legs. The team runs the final frame with the lights at normal brightness. Boro checks the drawing again and sets the clock by the wall reference. Nima records the monitor settings for the next scene.
