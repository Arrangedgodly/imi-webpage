# The Lighthouse Keeper's Shadow

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mystery dramas where a long rail-post shadow is mistaken for an approaching ship*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
KEEPER MARA, a macaque
WATCHER VEX, an orangutan
TECHNICIAN ODO, a gibbon
LOOKOUT PIP, a capuchin
HARBOR CALLER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE DECK - DUSK

The lighthouse lamp is already on its timed sweep. A rail post blocks the beam for one second, throwing a long dark bar across the deck. Vex points beyond the rail.

VEX: A ship is approaching without lights.

MARA: The bar is the rail post's shadow. The beam is passing behind it.

PIP: It appears every twelve seconds with the lens rotation.

ODO, from the doorway: Motor speed is steady. Harbor radar shows no vessel near the rocks.

NARI, over radio: Visibility is clear. No distress call.

VEX: The ship is hiding in the dark.

MARA: We will check the deck once from the sheltered side, then watch from indoors.

SCENE 2. INT. LIGHTHOUSE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the maintenance log on the desk. The prior entry records a twelve-second rotation and a clear west rail. Odo reads the current meter without leaving the room.

ODO: Rotation remains twelve seconds. The lamp is operating normally.

VEX: The shadow looks like a mast.

MARA: A mast would move with a vessel. This dark bar repeats at the same spot every sweep.

PIP: I can see the post line up with the beam through the window.

NARI, over radio: Harbor remains clear. No incoming vessel in the west channel.

VEX: A mystery should have a clue.

MARA: The clue is the fixed post and the timed beam.

SCENE 2A. INT. LIGHTHOUSE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Odo places the rotation chart beside the window. A narrow strip marks the rail post's position; a second strip marks the beam's twelve-second cycle.

ODO: The post is fixed. The beam crosses it at the same count on every rotation.

VEX: I can mark the shadow as an incoming vessel on the harbor map.

MARA: The map is for vessels. Keep the shadow in the maintenance note, where the next watch can use it.

NARI, over radio: To confirm, harbor has no target on radar and no caller requesting aid.

PIP: The dark bar also ends exactly at the deck post. A mast would continue above the rail.

VEX: Unless the ship is very small.

MARA: Then it would not cast a shadow across the whole deck from this angle. We have enough evidence to call it a post shadow.

Odo adjusts the chart so the two strips line up. The mark matches the beam pattern seen through the window.

ODO: Angle and rotation match. No moving object indicated.

VEX: Can we name it?

MARA: Call it "rail-post shadow at west sweep." That description will help the next watch.

Vex writes the phrase on a sticky note, then places the note beside the log, not on the harbor chart.

SCENE 3. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE DECK - MOMENTS LATER

Mara and Pip step onto the sheltered deck once. Odo stays inside at the meter; Vex remains behind the window with Nari on the radio. Mara checks the rail post and its base while Pip keeps the inspection path clear.

MARA: Post is fixed. No loose bolts, no rope caught on it.

PIP: The deck is dry at the threshold. The rope coil is in its bin, not on the rail.

MARA: Good. We are going back inside. No reason to approach the water.

They return indoors together. Mara closes the deck door and secures the latch. Neither returns to the deck.

SCENE 4. INT. LIGHTHOUSE ROOM - NIGHT

Through the window, a gull lands on the rail beyond the post. Its small shadow crosses the lower edge of the sweeping beam. Vex leans toward the glass but does not open the door.

VEX: The ship has sent a scout.

PIP: It's a gull. It is on the rail outside.

NARI, over radio: Harbor radar is clear. The gull is not a vessel.

MARA: We can watch from here. The deck door stays latched.

The gull lifts off. The shadow disappears. Odo enters the single completed inspection in the maintenance log: lamp steady, post secure, deck clear, no vessel detected. He closes the log once.

ODO: Watch entry complete. The next sweep is on schedule.

VEX: The phantom has departed.

MARA: The gull flew away. The lighthouse keeps working.

TAG: Vex salutes the now-empty rail through the window. Mara turns down the room lamp; the beam continues its ordinary sweep outside. Vex writes "no ship" on his suspense board, then adds "gull, briefly" beneath it. The next watch will inherit a mystery with two disappointing suspects.

Vex considers adding the name of a legendary sea monster to the board. Pip hands him the real observation sheet, with its empty vessel column.

PIP: Save the monster for a story after the watch.

VEX: Then I will give it better lighting.

He puts the pencil down as the next ordinary beam crosses the window.
