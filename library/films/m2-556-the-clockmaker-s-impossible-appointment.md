# The Clockmaker's Impossible Appointment

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-loop comedies that mistake a double booking for destiny*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
CLOCKMAKER PIP, a capuchin
VISITOR NARI, a lemur
SHOPKEEPER MARA, a macaque
DELIVERY VEX, an orangutan
TOWN CLERK ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. CLOCK SHOP - MORNING

Pip opens the appointment book. Two names are written in the same time slot: Nari, clock repair; Vex, delivery of a tower clock.

PIP: The schedule has two appointments at ten.

MARA: Nari is here now. Vex can wait until she leaves.

NARI: I have a clock that loses twelve minutes each day. It belonged to my grandmother.

VEX: I have a tower clock that must be installed before noon.

PIP: The tower clock is outside in a crate. It can wait while I inspect Nari's mechanism.

VEX: The town square cannot tell time without it.

ODO: The square has a digital clock. It is five minutes fast.

MARA: That is still better than installing a clock before the bracket is measured.

SCENE 2. INT. CLOCK SHOP - LATER

Pip opens the small clock. Nari holds the case steady. Vex circles the table with a measuring tape.

VEX: The hour hand moves, but the minute hand hesitates.

PIP: The spring is dirty. I'll clean it and check the escapement.

NARI: Can you finish today?

PIP: If the spring is intact, yes. If not, I need to order a part.

VEX: We could install the tower clock while we wait.

MARA: The bracket hasn't been measured. Don't open the crate.

Vex looks at the tape and starts marking the floor instead of the wall.

ODO: That measurement is the length of the shop.

SCENE 2A. INT. CLOCK SHOP - DAY

Pip removes the clock's face and sets each piece on a clean cloth. Nari waits beside the workbench. Vex checks his watch against the shop clock.

VEX: Your shop clock is two minutes slow.

PIP: It is not a reference clock. Odo can compare it with the town signal at the hour.

NARI: The clock I brought loses twelve minutes a day. Can you tell whether the spring is damaged?

PIP: The spring looks intact. The escapement is dirty. I'll clean it, then run it against the time signal for an hour before I promise it is fixed.

MARA: We should not rush a repair just because another delivery is waiting.

VEX: The tower clock will make the square look unfinished.

ODO: It is a new installation. The bracket has to support its weight. I'll measure the timber frame and write down the width.

Vex follows Odo outside with the tape. He measures from the wrong edge, including the trim.

ODO: The bracket's mounting surface begins here. Don't include the trim.

VEX: The trim looked important.

ODO: It is decorative. It cannot bear the clock.

Vex remeasures. The number changes. He writes it on the crate label instead of the work sheet.

MARA: Use the installation sheet, please. The crate label tells us what's inside.

Pip fits a temporary battery tester to Nari's clock. The minute hand advances without hesitation.

PIP: It moves now, but we still need the hour test. I'll keep it on the bench while I prepare the tower support.

NARI: I can come back after lunch.

MARA: We'll keep the clock here until the test is complete. No one needs to wait in the shop.

SCENE 2B. INT. CLOCK SHOP - LATE MORNING

Odo returns from the square with a mounting diagram. Mara reads the measurements while Pip finishes the hour test.

ODO: The support needs two bolts in the timber, not the trim. The repair shed has the correct bracket.

PIP: Nari's clock has kept time for an hour. The minute hand has not hesitated.

NARI: Thank you. I'll collect it after lunch and compare it with the town signal.

VEX: The appointments are finally separated.

MARA: They were never a paradox. We just had two jobs scheduled at the same time.

SCENE 3. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - DAY

The tower clock crate is opened. The wall bracket is too narrow by two inches. Vex holds the clock face; Mara measures the bracket.

MARA: We need a wider support. The square has a timber frame in the repair shed.

VEX: The appointment book said ten.

ODO: It said delivery at ten, not installation complete at ten.

PIP: Nari's clock is repaired. The minute hand moves evenly now.

NARI: It keeps time.

VEX: So does the digital clock.

MARA: Five minutes fast.

SCENE 4. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

The support is installed and checked. The tower clock is mounted. Pip sets it to match the public time signal.

VEX: The display says twelve.

ODO: It is eleven fifty-eight. That display is five minutes fast; the town signal will chime at noon.

VEX: Destiny is late.

NARI: The clock is accurate. The appointment is complete.

TAG: At noon, the tower clock chimes with the town signal. The digital display reads 12:05. Pip writes "installation finished 11:58" in the book.
