# The Last Seat in the Canopy

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic dramas that treat a shared resting spot as a fated meeting while friends keep rearranging the seating*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: Two monkeys keep meeting at the same branch seat, but the grove's practical residents need it for shade, lookout duty and carrying laundry.

Cast: Ema, a gibbon poet; Rafi, a macaque mapmaker; Vela, a capuchin gardener; and Tiko, a young langur who patrols the canopy.

SCENE 1. EXT. FIG TREE, MORNING

Ema reaches a broad branch overlooking the grove. A folded leaf rests on it. She reads the note: "For the one who sees the sunrise." Rafi arrives from the other side carrying a map tube.

EMA: Did you leave this?

RAFI: I left a map. It blew away.

A breeze lifts the leaf and settles it between them.

EMA: Perhaps it has chosen its reader.

RAFI: Or the wind has chosen the branch.

They sit apart. A gardener calls from below that the branch shades the seed trays. Ema and Rafi move to make room.

SCENE 2. EXT. FIG TREE, AFTERNOON

Rafi returns with a new map, pinned by a pebble. Ema is tying a ribbon to mark the best path to the water. Their hands meet over the same branch.

RAFI: You have marked my route.

EMA: The route to the water belongs to everyone.

Tiko climbs up and announces that the branch is needed for the afternoon lookout. Ema and Rafi move aside. Tiko scans the grove and spots a loose basket rolling down a path. He calls below; Vela catches it.

TIKO: I have prevented a catastrophe.

VELA: It was an empty basket.

TIKO: A catastrophe with room to grow.

SCENE 3. EXT. FIG TREE, LATE AFTERNOON

Ema and Rafi try to meet again. Vela has hung a line of damp cloth from the branch to dry. They duck beneath it. A leaf drips onto Rafi's map, blurring a river line.

RAFI: The river has moved.

EMA: Only on paper.

Vela apologizes and lowers the line. Ema helps fold the cloth. Rafi uses the dry corner of the map to make a paper boat for a child. Their meeting becomes a shared task, and the child sails the boat in a water bowl.

SCENE 4. EXT. FIG TREE, SUNSET

The branch is empty at last. Ema and Rafi return carrying two small cushions. They arrange them side by side. Tiko calls from below that the lookout is needed again because clouds are moving in.

TIKO: The sunset may bring rain.

RAFI: I can update the map.

EMA: I can bring the cushions inside.

They work together, tying each cushion to a strap. A gust blows the ribbon away. Rafi's map catches it against the trunk. He retrieves it and marks the direction of the wind. Ema notices he has drawn a tiny sunrise in the map margin.

SCENE 4A. EXT. FIG TREE, DUSK

The first raindrops fall before Ema and Rafi can untie the cushions. Vela asks them to lower the drying line so the cloth will not blow into the path. Tiko climbs to the lookout and sees a branch shifting in the wind. He calls for everyone to move away from the tree. Ema and Rafi carry the cushions to the shelter, while Vela secures the line. The branch drops a cluster of leaves onto the empty seat.

SCENE 4B. INT. GROVE SHELTER, NIGHT

Rafi unrolls his damp map. The river line is blurred but the trail markers remain visible. Ema lays the two cushions near the dry wall, leaving space for the others. Tiko enters and reports the path is clear. Vela brings the folded cloth inside and hangs it away from the map.

RAFI: The branch will still be there tomorrow.

EMA: If the wind lets it rest.

TIKO: The morning lookout will check.

Rafi adds a note to the map: "Seat closed during wind." Ema laughs, and the group settles in for the storm.

Vela checks the knots on the cloth line while Tiko watches the rain from the doorway. He reports that the branch has stopped swaying. Ema folds the map along its dry edge, and Rafi marks the shelter as a place to wait when the weather turns. The cushions stay stacked until morning.

SCENE 5. INT. GROVE SHELTER, NIGHT

Rain begins. Ema and Rafi sit on opposite sides of the shelter, sorting damp leaves from dry ones. Their cushions are beside them. Vela enters with the clothesline, now empty.

VELA: The branch is free tomorrow.

Ema and Rafi smile, then hear Tiko calling that the morning lookout begins at dawn.

RAFI: We can take the first watch.

EMA: Together?

TIKO (O.S.): In shifts. One of you needs to sleep.

Ema and Rafi set the cushions down and share the early shift schedule. Outside, the rain taps the roof. Rafi draws two tiny figures on the map, both facing the sunrise, then labels them "lookout crew."
