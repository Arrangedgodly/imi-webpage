# A Tail on Each Side

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: body-swap comedies, identity confusion, exaggerated mirror reactions, and characters who learn each other's routines by living them*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
SENA, a capuchin botanist
KARO, a macaque rock climber
DR. FERN, a ring-tailed lemur physicist

SCENE 1. INT. CITY GREENHOUSE - MORNING

Sena measures a vine's growth. Karo climbs a trellis to fix a roof vent. Dr. Fern warns them not to touch the experimental pollen sample, which reacts to warmth and vibration.

KARO: I won't touch it.

SENA: You're climbing above it.

Karo slips. Sena catches him. The pollen spills into the air. Both sneeze. The screen flashes white.

SCENE 2. INT. GREENHOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Sena wakes in Karo's body; Karo wakes in Sena's. They run to separate mirrors and scream at the same time. The camera cuts between their reflections, each making larger gestures than the last.

SENA: I have your shoulders.

KARO: I have your hands.

SENA: Those are my hands.

KARO: They're attached to me.

Dr. Fern tests their voices. They sound unchanged, but their bodies have traded places. The pollen has bonded to their skin. They must stay calm until it fades.

SCENE 2A. INT. GREENHOUSE - LATER

Dr. Fern makes them perform simple tasks to confirm the exchange. Sena tries to hold a seed tray in Karo's broad hands. The tray bends. Karo tries to reach a high shelf in Sena's body and cannot get enough height.

DR. FERN: The pollen appears to have exchanged motor habits along with the bodies.

SENA: My balance is different.

KARO: Your fingers are more precise than I expected.

Sena demonstrates pruning. Karo watches how she tests a stem before cutting. Karo demonstrates a climbing knot. Sena ties it slowly, then checks every loop.

DR. FERN: The effect should fade when the pollen cools. Avoid high places and sharp tools until then.

KARO: I have a climbing trial in two hours.

SENA: You can skip one trial.

KARO: I have never skipped one.

SCENE 3. INT. GREENHOUSE - DAY

Sena tries to prune a vine using Karo's climbing grip. She pulls down the trellis instead. Karo attempts to water seedlings, but his usual climbing instincts make him leap from bench to bench, sending watering cans everywhere.

SENA: My plants need a gentle hand.

KARO: Your hands are very strong.

SENA: That is your strength.

KARO: It is difficult to be careful with a body that wants to climb.

Sena shows Karo how to read the leaves before watering. Karo teaches Sena to move along the trellis without stepping on the roots. They start to work together.

SCENE 3A. INT. GREENHOUSE - LATER

The climbing team arrives, expecting Karo. Sena explains the swap but no one believes her until she demonstrates Karo's careful knot using his hands. A teammate asks her to climb the practice wall. She studies the harness, checks each buckle, and refuses to go higher than the first ledge.

SENA: I don't know how this body handles a fall.

The team respects her decision and lowers a practice hold for her. She completes the route slowly, naming every grip aloud. Meanwhile, Karo's first watering pass floods one seed tray. He adjusts the spout and moves the seedlings to drain. A small plant leans toward the window, and he sets it upright without breaking a stem.

DR. FERN: You are both learning what the body already knew.

KARO: I thought climbing was speed.

SENA: I thought pruning was only precision.

They trade tasks again, this time asking before reaching.

SCENE 4. EXT. ROCK WALL - AFTERNOON

Karo's climbing team expects him at a competition. Sena, in Karo's body, arrives. She studies the wall, checks the knots twice, and refuses to rush. The judge assumes Karo has become nervous.

At the greenhouse, Karo, in Sena's body, discovers the main vine has grown through the roof vent. He climbs carefully and supports the vine while Dr. Fern guides it back into the greenhouse.

SCENE 5. INT. GREENHOUSE - SUNSET

The pollen effect fades after the sample is cooled. Sena and Karo return to their bodies. The climbing judge asks why Karo changed his pace. He says he finally noticed how much the wall shifts in the wind. Sena says she learned to trust a hold before reaching for the next one.

They visit the greenhouse together. The vine now runs along the trellis instead of the vent. Dr. Fern logs the experiment as "identity exchange caused by pollen; repeat only with safety harnesses."

FINAL IMAGE: Sena reaches up to test a leaf. Karo offers the ladder. They trade places on the ground voluntarily, both smiling at the fact that their hands are their own again.
