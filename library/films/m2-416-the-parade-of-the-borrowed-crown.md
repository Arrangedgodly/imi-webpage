# The Parade of the Borrowed Crown

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal pageants, ceremonial processions, and the crown that has more obligations than its wearer*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a macaque costume keeper
VEX, an orangutan parade director
PIP, a capuchin page
NARI, a lemur heir
ODO, a gibbon town crier
COUNCILOR, a macaque town representative

SCENE 1. INT. COSTUME ROOM - MORNING

Nari stands in a velvet robe. Mara opens a padded case. Inside is a jeweled crown with a tag tied to its rim: "RETURN BY FOUR."

NARI: I thought the crown belonged to the palace.

MARA: It belongs to the theater company. The palace borrowed it for the parade.

VEX: It is a royal heirloom for the day.

PIP: What happens after four?

MARA: The theater company has a matinee.

Vex pins a schedule to the wall and paints RETURN BY FOUR on the back of the parade banner as a reminder for Pip. Nari must wave from the balcony, lead the procession, greet the village council, and return the crown before the matinee.

ODO: Where is the crowning?

VEX: It is a walk with a crown.

Odo practices a proclamation. The crown's tag flaps in his face. Mara tucks it inside the case, but Vex pulls it back out so the audience can see the return deadline.

SCENE 2. EXT. PALACE COURTYARD - LATE MORNING

Pip carries the crown on a cushion. Nari walks beside it without wearing it. Vex stops the procession.

VEX: The heir must wear the symbol.

MARA: The heir must not scratch the rental.

Nari tries the crown. It is too large and slides over one eye. Pip steadies it with a ribbon. The ribbon matches the parade banner, so Vex uses the banner cord as a chin strap.

NARI: I can see the village.

PIP: The crown can see the village.

The procession begins. Odo announces Nari's titles. Each title takes another step, and the group stops before the gate.

ODO: Heir of the eastern orchard, keeper of the river stones, defender of the public steps-

VEX: Keep moving.

ODO: I am listing her legal obligations.

A gust pulls the long banner around the crown. The banner's end knots on a jewel. Nari turns her head. The whole banner turns with her, wrapping Odo like a curtain.

SCENE 3. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - NOON

The crowd waits. Nari arrives with Odo inside the banner. Pip holds the crown steady. Vex signals the balcony wave, but there is no balcony in the square.

VEX: Wave from the platform.

PIP: The platform is a crate.

Nari steps onto it. The crown tips forward. She uses the banner to hold it up, and the crowd cheers at what looks like a royal salute.

NARI: They think this is intentional.

MARA: Then keep doing it until we reach the council.

Nari tries to walk while holding the crown, banner, and one sleeve of Odo's costume. The procession moves sideways. The town crier must announce every turn as a change of direction in royal policy.

ODO: The heir now faces the bakery.

VEX: Say something noble.

NARI: I can see the bakery.

The crowd applauds. Vex writes the line down for the next parade.

SCENE 4. INT. COUNCIL HALL - AFTERNOON

Mara frees Odo from the banner before the group enters. He keeps one loose ribbon as a sash.

The council sits in a row. A table is covered with petitions. Nari removes the crown and sets it on a pillow. It rolls slowly toward a stack of papers. Mara catches it just before it touches the ink.

COUNCILOR: We have a request for a bridge.

VEX: The royal response is in the script.

NARI: I do not have the script.

Pip unfolds the banner. The words "Return by Four" are printed across the back. Vex calls it a helpful prompt. Nari reads the petition and says the bridge should be built near the orchard, not the palace gate.

COUNCILOR: That is sensible.

VEX: It is not in the script.

NARI: Put it in the next one.

The council claps. The crown is already packed in its case. Vex hears a clock chime and checks the schedule. It is three-thirty. They must cross town before the theater company closes the prop room.

SCENE 5. EXT. THEATER LOADING DOOR - 3:55 P.M.

The procession reaches the loading door. Odo is free of the banner but still wears one ribbon as a sash. Mara returns the crown in its case. The theater manager checks the tag and signs the receipt.

VEX: The parade is complete.

MARA: The council got its answer.

NARI: The crown got home.

Odo turns to proclaim the heir's new title. He unfolds the schedule, but the paper is blank on the back.

ODO: Keeper of the crown until four.

The manager closes the case and wheels it toward the stage.

FINAL IMAGE: Nari waves goodbye to the crown through the loading door, while the crown's tag swings like a tiny royal flag.
