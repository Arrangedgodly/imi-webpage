# The Vault That Opened for Maintenance

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: heist films, laser grids, master keys, elaborate crew roles, and vault doors opened by reading the service manual*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
PLANNER VEX, a macaque
LOCKSMITH NARI, an orangutan
LOOKOUT PIP, a capuchin
MUSEUM CURATOR LUMA, a lemur
NIGHT GUARD ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM BASEMENT - NIGHT

A vault door stands at the end of a hallway. Vex spreads a blueprint across a crate. His team wears dark clothes and carries tiny flashlights. Curator Luma approaches with a maintenance clipboard. Vex hides behind the crate.

PIP: She has the key.

NARI: She has a clipboard.

The vault alarm blinks. A sign beside it reads SERVICE DUE.

SCENE 2. INT. BASEMENT HALL - LATER

Vex reviews the plan: Nari will pick the lock, Pip will watch the hallway, and Vex will carry the artifact. Luma asks why they are gathered by the vault. Vex says they are conducting a security drill. Luma hands him the maintenance clipboard and asks him to sign the visitor log.

Nari examines the door. The lock is sealed, but a panel displays a code. Pip tries to cover the security camera with a hat. The camera is angled toward the ceiling because the museum installed it for a pipe inspection. Odo arrives and asks if the crew has seen a screwdriver. Luma says it is in the maintenance drawer.

SCENE 2A. INT. BASEMENT HALL - LATER

Nari draws a diagram of the vault panel on the back of Vex's blueprint. She explains the blinking light is a maintenance reminder, not an alarm. Vex claims the light could still be a decoy. Pip tests it by opening the service manual. The manual lists the same pattern: one blink for inspection, two for battery replacement. The panel blinks twice.

VEX: That is exactly what the vault wants us to believe.

LUMA: It is a service notice.

Odo gives Pip the screwdriver and asks him not to touch the panel until the work order is signed. Vex tries to make a silent hand signal to continue the plan. The gesture is so elaborate that Luma thinks he is asking for a ladder. She brings one from the storage room.

SCENE 3. INT. VAULT ROOM - NIGHT

The alarm chirps. Nari checks the service panel and finds a loose battery. The vault is not locked; it is waiting for a maintenance reset. Vex insists the battery may be a trap. Luma reads the manual and confirms the sequence: press reset, enter the supervisor code, and wait for the green light.

Pip stands lookout. He hears footsteps and whispers that the guard is coming. Odo enters carrying a screwdriver and a thermos. He asks if the crew needs help. Vex says they are in the middle of a heist. Odo looks at the clipboard and says the work order is overdue.

SCENE 3A. INT. VAULT ROOM - NIGHT

Nari opens the manual to the battery diagram. The old cell is visibly corroded. She removes it with insulated gloves and places it in a disposal pouch. Pip checks the security log; the door has not been opened since the museum closed. Vex shines a flashlight across the empty room, searching for a hidden laser grid. The beam lands on a strip of dust and reveals only footprints from the installation crew.

VEX: Could the footprints be a clue?

ODO: The installation crew walked here.

Luma reads the inventory card aloud: the sculpture is packed for tomorrow's move. Vex asks whether the artifact is valuable. Nari says it is a museum object and has a record. They can move it with permission, not by sneaking it out.

SCENE 4. INT. VAULT HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Luma enters the code. The vault door unlocks with a soft click. Inside are display stands, empty crates, and a small sculpture wrapped for transport. The museum is preparing to move the sculpture to a new gallery. There is no treasure. Nari checks the inventory and confirms the sculpture is scheduled for tomorrow's installation.

Vex insists the crew should still carry out the object. Luma gives him a padded cart and asks him to follow the transport form. He signs the form and wheels the sculpture toward the elevator. Pip keeps watch, but Odo holds the elevator door open.

SCENE 5. INT. NEW GALLERY - DAWN

The team installs the sculpture in its new location. Vex is listed as transport lead, Nari as locksmith, and Pip as corridor spotter. Luma signs the maintenance log. Odo returns the screwdriver to its drawer. The vault alarm stays quiet.

PIP: We opened a vault.

NARI: We replaced a battery.

VEX: We moved an artifact under supervision.

FINAL IMAGE: The vault door closes smoothly behind the empty padded cart, and the service sign flips from DUE to COMPLETE.
