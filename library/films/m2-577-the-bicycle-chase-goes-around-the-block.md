# The Bicycle Chase Goes Around the Block

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action films that turn a simple delivery ride into a looping pursuit*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
RIDER PIP, a capuchin
DIRECTOR VEX, an orangutan
MECHANIC MARA, a macaque
MAPKEEPER ODO, a gibbon
NEIGHBOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. STREET - MORNING

Pip rides a bicycle with a basket of library books. Vex follows on foot, filming a chase.

VEX: The courier is pursued through the city.

PIP: I'm delivering books to the next block.

MARA: Your rear brake is rubbing. Stop at the curb; I can see the pad is touching the rim.

ODO: The route map shows a straight street, no alley turns.

VEX: We need a shortcut.

NARI: The alley is closed for paving.

SCENE 2. EXT. CORNER - CONTINUOUS

Pip dismounts. Mara looks at the rear wheel without turning it. The brake pad is visibly pressed against the rim.

MARA: The pad shifted. We need to lift the bike and center it before you ride.

PIP: I'll hold the frame steady while you work.

VEX: The pursuer is gaining.

ODO: There is no pursuer. Vex is standing at the corner.

Mara tightens the bolt. Pip spins the wheel; it turns freely.

SCENE 2A. EXT. CORNER - CONTINUOUS

Mara flips the bicycle upside down on its padded saddle and handlebars. Pip holds the frame steady while she adjusts the brake pad.

MARA: Don't squeeze the lever until I tell you. The wheel needs to spin freely first.

PIP: Understood. The brake rubbed because the pad shifted toward the rim.

VEX: A sabotage attempt.

ODO: The repair tag says the bike was checked last month. The bolt may have loosened during the ride.

MARA: The bolt is secure now. Pip, spin the wheel.

Pip turns it. The wheel completes two rotations without scraping.

PIP: Clear. I'll test the brake while the bike is still held.

Mara keeps one hand on the frame. Pip squeezes the lever; the wheel stops.

MARA: Brake works. I will clip the completed inspection tag to the handlebar. Set the bike upright and try a slow roll within the curb lane.

Pip rides a few feet, then stops at the painted line. The basket remains level; the books do not shift.

NARI: The route to the library is open. The paving crew is in the alley, so avoid it.

PIP: I'll use the straight street and signal at the corner.

VEX: A chase needs a pursuer.

MARA: The delivery has a route. You can film from the sidewalk as long as you don't block anyone.

Vex begins walking backward, then nearly steps into a planter. Nari taps his shoulder and directs him onto the clear side.

NARI: Cameras face forward. Watch where your feet go.

VEX: The director has narrowly avoided a shrub.

SCENE 2B. EXT. STREET - CONTINUOUS

Pip mounts the bicycle and rides at a slow pace toward the library. Vex jogs on the sidewalk behind, trying to keep both rider and route in frame.

VEX: The courier is pursued by an unseen force.

NARI: The force is your own legs. Keep the camera on the sidewalk.

PIP: I'm stopping at the next corner to signal. The cross street has traffic.

MARA: Brake test worked. Use the marked crossing and wait for the walk signal.

Pip stops before the curb. The signal is red for pedestrians. Vex catches up, still narrating.

VEX: The chase has reached the intersection.

ODO: The delivery route has a red light.

The signal changes. Pip crosses carefully, dismounting for the narrow curb ramp. Vex walks the bicycle past the ramp without blocking a wheelchair user approaching from the opposite side.

NARI: Keep right. Let the other rider pass first.

VEX: The pursuit has an obstacle.

MARA: It's a shared path. Give people space.

Pip returns to the saddle after the crossing and pedals to the library. Vex takes the shorter direct sidewalk and arrives first, panting.

VEX: I have beaten the bicycle.

PIP: You walked a straight line. I carried the books safely.

SCENE 2C. EXT. LIBRARY - CONTINUOUS

Pip locks the bicycle to the rack and carries the book basket inside. Vex checks the street behind them for a pursuer.

VEX: The block is clear.

MARA: It was clear when we started.

PIP: I'll hand the books to the librarian and sign the delivery sheet.

NARI: Leave the bicycle locked until the repair tag is removed.

SCENE 3. INT. LIBRARY - CONTINUOUS

Pip carries the basket to the service desk. The librarian checks the titles against the delivery sheet and signs it.

PIP: Six books delivered. The basket is empty.

VEX: The chase ends with a signature.

NARI: The bicycle is still locked outside, and the repair tag is on the handlebar.

MARA: I'll sign the repair tag now that the brake test passed. Keep it locked until the tag is removed.

SCENE 4. EXT. LIBRARY - DAY

Mara removes the completed tag and places it in her repair folder. Pip checks the lock once and leaves the bicycle at the rack.

VEX: The courier is free to ride.

PIP: The delivery is done. You're out of breath.

TAG: Vex tries to film a dramatic getaway, but the bicycle remains locked to the rack.
