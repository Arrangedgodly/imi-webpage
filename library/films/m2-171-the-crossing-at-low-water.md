# The Crossing at Low Water

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: epic war films that treat a carefully negotiated animal crossing like a final campaign*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: Two neighboring troops plan a dramatic crossing to reach ripe figs, but the creek's changing depth and competing commanders turn the march into a farce.

Cast: Sera, a langur scout; Doma, a macaque commander; Runi, a capuchin quartermaster; and Tavi, a young gibbon carrying the ceremonial flag.

SCENE 1. EXT. CREEK BANK, MORNING

Doma surveys the shallow creek. Across it hangs a fig branch. Sera tests the water with a stick. Tavi plants a small flag in the sand.

DOMA: At the sound of my call, we cross in a single line.

RUNI: The creek is ankle-deep.

DOMA: Every crossing begins ankle-deep.

SERA: The stones are stable. The left edge is muddy.

Doma draws a battle arrow in the sand. A wavelet erases it.

DOMA: The river has challenged the plan.

TAVI: It erased the arrow.

Runi distributes smooth stones as markers. Tavi puts the flag down to help. Wind catches it and carries it toward the creek. Tavi darts after it, stops before the muddy edge, and calls for Sera.

SCENE 2. EXT. CREEK, MIDMORNING

Sera lays stones in a safe line across the shallow water. The troops wait. Doma insists the first step must be ceremonial. Runi offers a dry leaf as a banner.

DOMA: We cannot cross without a standard.

RUNI: We can cross without one. We need something to mark the return route.

Doma accepts the leaf. Tavi holds it, but a gust sends it spinning into the stream. It lodges against a stone. The troop waits while Sera retrieves it with a branch.

SERA: The route is still safe. The marker is wet.

TAVI: It is a river flag now.

Doma gives the signal. Everyone takes one careful step. A young macaque leaps two stones ahead, causing the others to bunch up. Runi orders them to pause and spread out. Doma whispers a heroic speech to the line.

DOMA: We brave the current, together.

RUNI: One at a time, please.

SCENE 3. EXT. MIDSTREAM ROCK, NOON

The troop reaches a broad dry rock. The water has risen slightly from upstream rain. Sera checks each stone again. The path back remains clear, but the last two stones are now slick.

SERA: We can continue to the fig branch or return by the upper bank.

DOMA: The mission is the fig.

RUNI: The mission is getting everybody there and back.

Doma wants the group to press onward for the dramatic image. He asks Tavi to wave the leaf banner. Tavi keeps it low so it will not catch another gust. The troop waits while Sera tests the next stones with her stick. A small fish flicks the water beside her.

DOMA: The enemy has sent a swimmer.

SERA: A fish has passed through the creek.

Tavi sees the fig branch bend toward the bank as the wind changes. Several figs drop on their own and land on the near side of the stream.

TAVI: The harvest has come to us.

Doma studies the fruit, then the crossing route.

DOMA: We will not risk the line for fruit that crossed first.

SCENE 4. EXT. UPPER BANK, AFTERNOON

The troop returns one at a time along the tested stones. Runi counts everyone onto dry ground. Tavi plants the soaked leaf flag in the sand. It droops, then sticks upright.

RUNI: All accounted for. No injuries. Three figs.

DOMA: A victory beyond measure.

SERA: A safe return before the water rises.

Doma gives Tavi a small pebble as a medal. Tavi hangs it on the flag stem. The group shares the figs, including one with a passing bird. Doma begins a speech about courage, but the troop is already walking toward the grove.

Before they leave the bank, Runi checks the path for anyone still on the far side. Sera watches the water for another minute, then folds the route map only after the stones are visible. Doma puts away his campaign arrow. Tavi carries the damp leaf carefully so it will not brush the ground.

SCENE 5. EXT. GROVE PATH, EVENING

Sera folds the map. Runi carries the remaining stones in a pouch. Doma marches behind them, repeating the campaign story for an audience of two sleepy monkeys.

DOMA: The creek roared. The line held. The standard rose above the current.

TAVI: It was a wet leaf.

DOMA: A standard need not be dry to be noble.

The wind takes the leaf flag from the pebble and drops it at Doma's feet. Everyone pauses. He picks it up and tucks it into his belt.

DOMA: The river returns our banner.

SERA: The wind returned it.

DOMA: The river has allies.
