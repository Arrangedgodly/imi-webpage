# The Inn with the Moving Mountain View

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: travel mysteries, hidden-room reveals, and hotel brochures that promise a view from every window*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a gibbon innkeeper
VEX, a macaque travel writer
PIP, a capuchin porter
NARI, a lemur surveyor
THE MOUNTAIN, a silent performer

SCENE 1. INT. MOUNTAIN INN - MORNING

Vex opens a brochure. It shows a mountain framed in the inn's front window.

VEX: I booked the room with a mountain view.

MARA: Every room has a mountain view.

PIP: At different times.

Vex looks out. The mountain is gone. A wooden wall blocks the window.

MARA: The inn rotates.

VEX: The whole inn?

PIP: It turns one degree every hour.

Nari checks the floor compass. The inn is on a round stone foundation. She says the mountain is still in the same place; the building is turning away from it.

Vex moves the curtains aside and checks the windows. One shows a pine tree, one the valley, and one the inn's own roof.

VEX: Which window did the brochure photograph?

MARA: The east one.

Vex turns the compass. The east window now faces a ridge. Pip marks the direction on a paper map. The inn shifts another degree, and the needle moves.

PIP: The compass has changed its mind.

NARI: The compass is fixed. The building is moving.

Mara asks Vex to stay in one room for an hour and watch the view. He sets his travel case down. The mountain slides behind the wardrobe while he writes. He moves the desk to follow it, then the chair, then the open case.

MARA: You are rearranging the room.

VEX: I am preserving the view.

SCENE 2. INT. ROOM FOUR - LATER

Vex moves a chair to follow the view. The room rotates slowly. The mountain appears in the bathroom mirror, then behind the wardrobe. Pip slides the wardrobe aside. A narrow panel opens onto the hallway.

VEX: A secret passage.

MARA: A storage gap.

Nari checks the inn's turning schedule. The building rotates to balance weight in the rooms. Vex asks if the view can be held in place. Mara says the inn must keep turning or the foundation settles unevenly.

Pip places a glass of water on the floor and watches the surface. It stays level while the furniture shifts around it. Nari sets a compass beside the glass. The needle swings as the building turns.

VEX: Could we stop the foundation for the night?

MARA: Then the west rooms would bear all the weight.

VEX: Could the guests sleep in the west rooms?

MARA: They already do.

Vex tries to reserve the room with the best view. Mara shows him the occupancy board. Every room has a different view slot. Guests have written their names beside the hour when the mountain appears.

PIP: Room three gets the mountain at breakfast.

NARI: Room five gets it at bedtime.

Vex asks to move to room three. Pip turns the occupancy board; room three is already booked by a family carrying a large telescope.

SCENE 3. INT. LOBBY - AFTERNOON

Guests gather around the central compass. Each asks when the mountain will appear in their window. Mara gives them a schedule. Vex says the brochure did not mention the rotation.

MARA: It says "panoramic view."

VEX: It shows one mountain.

NARI: The view includes the valley and the mountain.

A bell rings. The inn rotates. Chairs slide toward the outer wall. Pip braces the desk. Vex holds the brochure open, and the paper spins like a compass needle.

VEX: We need a stable view for the guests.

MARA: We have a stable inn. The guests can take turns with the view.

Two guests wheel their telescopes toward opposite windows. Their tripod legs lock together, and both chairs roll toward the compass. Pip releases the latch while Vex grabs the brochure to fan them. It catches on a barrel and folds over its mountain picture.

PIP: The furniture has brought everyone together.

VEX: The view has been removed from the brochure.

MARA: It is still outside.

Vex sees the mountain framed in the lobby's round service hatch as the inn turns. Nari refuses to promise it will stay.

SCENE 4. INT. ROOM FOUR - EVENING

The mountain returns to the window. Vex sets up his writing desk. The room continues turning, slowly moving the view away. He tries to hold the desk against the wall. The desk rolls on small wheels and follows him.

VEX: The room is leaving the mountain.

MARA: The mountain is staying.

Nari measures the rotation. She says the inn will face the mountain again in a day. Vex asks for a room that remains still. Mara offers the pantry, which is built into the foundation.

Pip opens the pantry. It has no window. The mountain is visible through a small vent, framed by a jar of pickles.

Vex tries to squeeze into the pantry with his travel case. He knocks the pickle jar; Nari catches it. The mountain remains centered through the vent.

VEX: I can write from here.

PIP: You can sit outside the door.

Vex takes the stool into the hallway. A guest sees the tiny view above his shoulder and photographs it.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY - NIGHT

Vex finishes his travel article. He calls the inn "the only hotel where the view moves." Mara asks if the review is positive. Vex says the beds are comfortable and the mountain returns on schedule.

A bell rings. The inn turns. The guests look up from their books and watch the windows.

Vex pins his article beside the brochure. It is titled "A View That Knows When to Leave." Guests read it aloud, rotating the page as the inn turns. Pip turns the brochure; its mountain faces the lobby wall.

MARA: Which picture will you keep?

VEX: The one that moves.

NARI: The picture cannot move.

Vex points to guests at the pantry vent. The inn turns; they shuffle to keep the view in sight.

FINAL IMAGE: The mountain appears in the pantry vent. Pip places the pickle jar beside it like a tiny monument.
