# The Orbital Garden Club

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: futuristic colony dramas, inspirational speeches, and technology presented as a substitute for routine maintenance*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: On a space station, a crew's first garden fails because the automated watering system is programmed for a planet with gravity.

Cast: Omi, a lemur botanist; Kesh, a gorilla commander; Rillo, a macaque engineer; and Tavi, a capuchin communications officer.

SCENE 1. INT. ORBITAL GARDEN MODULE, MORNING

The crew gathers for the first sprout ceremony. Commander Kesh gives a speech about humanity's future among the stars. A water bubble floats past his face and lands on the control screen. Omi catches it with a cloth.

OMI: The pump ran too long.

RILLO: The schedule says ten seconds.

OMI: It is ten seconds of continuous flow.

The seedlings drift inside their trays. Tavi tries to pin the station flag to the wall. The magnet does not hold. Kesh calls the floating tools a test of discipline. Rillo says it is a test of the missing straps.

SCENE 2. INT. ENGINEERING BAY, LATER

Rillo examines the pump controller. It was programmed for a ground garden, where water falls downward. In zero gravity, the water forms beads and coats the filters. Kesh asks if a dramatic manual override is possible. Rillo says a slow pulse is safer.

Kesh requests a live link to mission control for the sprout ceremony. Tavi opens the channel, but the camera floats away from the planter. He secures it with a strap and frames the small tray. The ground crew asks whether the garden is already producing food. Omi says one seed has not germinated yet. Kesh says it is still a milestone. Omi says it is a seed.

Rillo finds the pump's calibration label hidden behind the control box. It says "Earth gravity setting." He asks why they did not test the system with an empty tray before loading the seeds. Kesh begins an answer about ambitious exploration, then stops and says they were excited. Omi asks to add a preflight check to the station manual.

Omi asks the crew to secure the trays with straps. Tavi uses the flag magnets as temporary clips, and the flag drifts away. Kesh catches it, then asks whether the moment should be recorded for morale. Tavi says the camera is pointed at the water filter.

The crew rewrites the watering sequence: short pulses, a pause, then a pressure check. They test one tray. The first pulse wets the soil. The second bead floats away. Omi adjusts the wick and anchors it. Kesh wants to announce the success, but Rillo asks them to test the remaining trays first.

One tray's strap catches on a cabinet latch. Tavi secures the latch with a tie and labels it. The crew checks the other trays before starting the next pulse. A bubble travels along the wick and returns to the reservoir. Omi explains that the roots need moisture, not a floating orb for the camera. Tavi tilts the camera down to show the soil. The broadcast audience sees a plain, useful test and asks where the updated procedure will be posted.

SCENE 3. INT. ORBITAL GARDEN MODULE, AFTERNOON

The second test begins. Tavi reads each step aloud while Rillo watches the pump. The crew catches two leaks before water reaches the electronics. Kesh holds the tray straps, discovering that a ceremonial speech can be delivered while keeping both hands busy.

Omi inspects the roots. One seed has sprouted. The crew cheers, but the sound triggers the module's noise monitor. Tavi reads the alert: loud activity is permitted during scheduled exercise only. Kesh asks whether joy counts as exercise. Rillo says the monitor counts decibels.

The crew records a maintenance procedure for the next shift. Omi labels the filter, the pump, and the emergency shutoff. Kesh wants to call the garden a frontier. Omi says it is a garden that needs a daily check. He writes that on the sign.

Kesh reviews the shift handoff aloud. He lists the watering interval, filter check, strap inspection, and next observation time. Rillo adds that the pump must be turned off before the filter is opened. Tavi sends the note to the incoming crew and receives a read receipt. Kesh says the station can still celebrate. Omi gives the sprout a measured portion of light and logs the time.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNICATIONS BAY, EVENING

Tavi sends a report to the ground team. The first version describes a heroic struggle against hostile water. Omi asks for the actual cause. Tavi revises it: the irrigation timing assumed gravity. Rillo includes the new pulse schedule and asks for extra wicks.

Ground Control replies with a message congratulating the crew on the first sprout and requesting the revised instructions. Kesh reads it aloud without adding a speech. The crew secures the trays, checks the moisture, and logs the next watering time.

FINAL IMAGE: The small sprout stays rooted as the station turns above the planet. Beside it, a label reads "WATER IN SHORT PULSES; CHECK THE FILTER," and Kesh's inspirational quote has been written on the back of the maintenance clipboard.
