# The Space Station's Missing Spoon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space survival films where a utensil shortage threatens an entire mission*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
COMMANDER NARI, a lemur
ENGINEER MARA, a macaque
SCIENTIST PIP, a capuchin
MISSION VOICE VEX, an orangutan
TECHNICIAN ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. SPACE STATION GALLEY - MORNING

A meal pouch floats against a cabinet. Pip reaches for a spoon, but the utensil drawer is open and empty.

VEX: We have lost the only spoon.

NARI: We have four forks and two clean spoons in the storage sleeve.

PIP: The drawer is empty because I moved everything to the sleeve while cleaning.

MARA: Close the drawer before the loose utensils drift out.

ODO: The pouch is tethered. No food is leaking.

VEX: Our mission depends on the spoon.

SCENE 2. INT. GALLEY - CONTINUOUS

Mara closes the drawer. A spoon floats past the camera. Vex lunges but bumps a padded rail.

NARI: Don't push off the wall. Use the handholds.

VEX: The spoon is escaping.

PIP: I'll catch it with the soft bag, not your hands near the fan.

Pip positions the bag. The spoon drifts inside.

MARA: Bag closed. Odo, inspect it for the missing spoon label.

ODO: This is the spare spoon. The one in the sleeve is still there.

SCENE 2A. INT. SPACE STATION GALLEY - MORNING

Pip opens the storage sleeve. One spoon is tethered to a meal pouch. The spare is secured under the clear flap.

PIP: The inventory matches. We have two spoons, four forks, and all meal clips.

VEX: Why did the drawer appear empty?

MARA: Because Pip moved the utensils while cleaning. They were stored here, where they cannot float into the vent.

ODO: Drawer latch is closed now. The fan intake is unobstructed.

NARI: We'll check the floor tether points before meal service. No loose utensil should drift near a panel.

Vex reaches toward the spare spoon. Mara holds up a finger.

MARA: Use the tethered spoon for the meal. The spare stays in its sleeve until needed.

VEX: The spoon has a security detail.

PIP: The spoon has a storage tab.

SCENE 2B. INT. STATION - CONTINUOUS

The pouch drifts toward the ceiling. Odo catches its tether and secures it to the galley rail.

ODO: Pouch is anchored. Seal is intact.

VEX: The food is trying to leave.

NARI: It was not tied down. Now it is.

MARA: Check the pouch seal before opening it.

Pip inspects the seal and gives a thumbs-up. She opens the pouch, removes the utensil and eats one spoonful.

PIP: Meal is ready. No leak, no missing spoon.

ODO: The tethered spoon is back in its sleeve after use. Spare count remains one.

VEX: The mission log will note our survival.

NARI: It will note that the galley drawer was reorganized.

SCENE 2C. INT. SPACE STATION GALLEY - CONTINUOUS

Vex finishes the meal and wipes the pouch surface with the cleaning cloth. Pip stores the cloth in its labeled pocket.

PIP: Pouch empty. Utensil cleaned and returned to the tether point.

MARA: Check that the drawer is latched before we leave the galley.

ODO: Drawer closed. Storage sleeve clipped. No loose items near the vent.

VEX: The spoon is home.

NARI: The spoon is stored. The mission continues.

Vex tries to float dramatically across the room but catches the handrail and turns himself around.

MARA: Keep one hand on the rail near the galley. The corridor is narrow.

VEX: I will make a slow and safe exit.

SCENE 2D. INT. SPACE STATION - CONTINUOUS

Odo opens the inventory panel and checks the food pouches for the next meal.

ODO: Four meals remain, all sealed. The spare spoon is in the labeled sleeve.

PIP: I'll update the board so the next shift knows the drawer was reorganized.

NARI: Note the location, not a missing-item alert.

VEX: The spoon report will be filed.

MARA: File the meal count, too. The next crew needs to know what is available.

Odo clips the report to the station log. Pip returns the pen to its holder.

ODO: Galley secure. Nothing floating loose.

SCENE 3. INT. STATION - LATER

The crew inventory the drawer. Two forks, one spoon, sealed food clips, and a cleaning cloth.

PIP: The original spoon is attached to the pouch by its loop.

VEX: There are two spoons.

NARI: We had two before. One is a spare, and one is attached.

MARA: Repack the loose one in the sleeve and close it with the tab.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLEY - DAY

Pip eats from the pouch with the tethered spoon. Vex holds up a fork.

VEX: Mission saved.

PIP: It was a meal, not a mission emergency.

ODO: Inventory complete. Drawer latched.

TAG: The spoon floats away only as far as its tether allows. Vex watches it, relieved.
