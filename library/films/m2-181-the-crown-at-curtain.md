# The Crown at Curtain

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent film slapstick, intertitle cards, exaggerated gestures, and escalating physical misunderstandings*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
MIRI, a capuchin stage usher
BOK, a gibbon lift mechanic
TAVI, a macaque prop maker
THE MAYOR, a mandrill
THE CONDUCTOR, a proboscis monkey with an unused baton

SCENE 1. INT. GRAND KETTLE THEATER - EVENING

An orchestra conductor raises a baton. The pit is empty. A title card appears: "TONIGHT: THE CITY CROWN IS PRESENTED."

MIRI, in a spotless usher uniform, reveals the ceremonial crown: a brass hoop with a feather plume nearly as tall as she is. She places it on a velvet pillow. The pillow collapses under the crown's weight. Miri catches it with her tail, pins it to a chair, then bows to the chair.

A second card: "THE CROWN MUST REACH THE STAGE BEFORE THE MAYOR."

TAVI rolls in a handcart. The crown is wider than the theater door. Tavi turns it sideways. The plume catches a chandelier. The chandelier swings like a pendulum. Tavi freezes beneath it, eyes crossed, holding the crown aloft as if she supports the building.

SCENE 2. INT. THEATER LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Miri ushers an elderly pair toward their seats while the crown follows behind her on the cart. Each time she points left, Tavi rolls right. Each time she points right, the cart rolls left. They circle the lobby, never leaving the audience's sightline.

The Mayor arrives, carrying a tiny presentation box. He opens it: a miniature gold star. He looks at the empty stage, then his watch, then the crown rolling in circles. Miri flashes a reassuring smile so broad it nearly hides Tavi.

The Mayor produces a card: "I WILL PRESENT THE STAR AT EIGHT." He points to the clock. Seven fifty-five.

SCENE 3. INT. SERVICE ELEVATOR - MOMENTS LATER

Bok, a gibbon mechanic, opens the lift doors. The crown is too tall to fit upright and too wide to fit sideways. Tavi turns it diagonally. It fits, but the feather plume pokes through the ceiling hatch.

Bok holds the doors. Miri pushes. The elevator descends one inch and stops. The crown's plume has snagged a ceiling fan in the shaft. The fan begins turning; the crown spins like a lopsided carousel.

A title card: "THE LIFT WILL NOT MOVE WHILE THE FAN IS TURNING."

Bok pulls the emergency stop. The fan halts. The crown continues to spin from its own momentum. Miri's uniform skirt catches the wind and inflates like a parachute. She rises onto her toes, still holding the cart handle. Tavi grabs Miri's belt. Bok grabs Tavi's apron. All three form a human chain suspended above the elevator floor.

The Mayor peeks in. Seeing the chain, he salutes it as a ceremonial formation. He takes out the miniature star to decorate them. The three rotate past him before he can choose a recipient. He pockets the star and salutes each time Bok passes.

SCENE 4. INT. SERVICE ELEVATOR - LATER

Bok climbs onto the elevator roof and releases the plume from the fan. He unhooks it gently. The crown settles. The cart wheels, however, now point in different directions. Bok rotates the axle. The cart rolls forward, backward, and sideways in a tight figure eight, carrying the crown inches toward the shaft opening.

Miri, Tavi, and Bok move around the cart, passing the crown between them without letting it touch the floor. Their gestures become an elaborate dance: Miri bows, Tavi pivots, Bok catches the plume just before it clips a light. The Mayor applauds silently. The theater orchestra conductor, who has followed the commotion, gives them tempo.

The crown finally clears the elevator and enters the stage wing. The Mayor's watch reaches eight. He steps forward for the ceremony. The crown is nowhere to be seen.

SCENE 5. INT. THEATER STAGE - NIGHT

A curtain rises. The audience sees the Mayor at a bare lectern. He lifts the miniature star. A title card: "THE HONOR IS SMALL, BUT THE MOMENT IS LARGE."

The crown rolls in from stage left. Tavi follows, pushing with one hand and catching a bouncing wheel with the other. Miri and Bok guide it like a float in a parade. The Mayor places the star on the plume. The crown tips forward, carrying him gently onto the velvet pillow.

The Mayor sits inside the crown's hoop. The plume arches over him like a royal umbrella. The audience laughs and applauds. Instead of lifting the crown, Miri offers the Mayor the pillow. He steps out, takes the crown, and presents it to the three workers.

A final title card: "THE CROWN BELONGS TO THE ONES WHO BROUGHT IT."

The Mayor, Miri, Tavi, and Bok bow together. The crown's oversized plume falls forward and covers all four faces. They bow again, unseen. The audience gives them a standing ovation.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty crown rests on the velvet pillow. A feather slowly rotates above it as the theater fan turns one careful notch.
