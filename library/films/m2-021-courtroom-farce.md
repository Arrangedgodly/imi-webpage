# The People Versus the Pie

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: courtroom farce timing, legal jargon, and chaotic witness testimony*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A squirrel judge presides over a case about a stolen pie, but the courthouse staff keep eating the evidence and the witnesses remember the crust differently.

Cast: Judge Acorn, a squirrel; Ada, an orangutan attorney; Prosecutor Quill, a parrot; Bailiff Moss, a tortoise; and three witnesses: Baker Wren, Monkey Lin, and a raccoon delivery driver.

SCENE 1. INT. COURTROOM, MORNING

A pie sits under a glass dome. Judge Acorn calls the trial to order. The dome is empty. Bailiff Moss says he placed it there. Prosecutor Quill says the pie was a key witness.

QUILL: I object to the missing dessert.

ADA: You cannot object to evidence that isn't present.

The judge orders the pie found before opening statements.

SCENE 2. INT. COURTHOUSE HALLWAY, LATER

The staff search the building. A trail of crumbs leads to the clerk's room. The clerk says he ate only one corner. A second trail leads to the jury room. The jurors insist the pie was already missing when they arrived.

Baker Wren identifies the crust by its lattice pattern. Monkey Lin says it had a braided edge. The delivery driver says it was in a blue box. Everyone describes a different pie.

SCENE 3. INT. COURTROOM, AFTERNOON

The attorneys give opening statements without the evidence. Quill describes a dramatic theft. Ada asks whether anyone saw the pie leave the bakery. Three witnesses raise their hands, then point at one another.

The judge calls for order. The gavel is missing. Bailiff Moss says he used it to hold a door open. He retrieves it, but the door closes on his shell. The trial pauses while the bailiff is freed.

SCENE 4. INT. JURY ROOM, EVENING

The jurors deliberate. They find a recipe card under the table. It says "practice pie, for courtroom demonstration." The actual pie is still in the bakery oven. The delivery driver brought the empty display box.

The clerk has eaten the practice pie's corner, but not the evidence. The jury debates whether this is theft, poor labeling, or an invitation.

SCENE 5. INT. COURTROOM, NEXT MORNING

The real pie arrives. Judge Acorn rules that the case is dismissed because no pie was stolen. He orders the courthouse to label all demonstration food. Quill asks whether the pie can be admitted as lunch. The judge says only after court.

SCENE 5A. INT. COURTROOM, CONTINUOUS

Judge Acorn asks each witness to identify the pie. The answers do not match.

ACORN: Describe the crust.

WREN: Fluted edge.

LIN: Braided edge.

DRIVER: Blue box.

QUILL: Is blue a crust?

ADA: Not in this court.

ACORN: Where was it last seen?

WREN: My bakery.

LIN: The evidence table.

DRIVER: My cart.

QUILL: The jury room.

ADA: These are four locations.

ACORN: Was it one pie?

WREN: One recipe.

LIN: Several slices.

DRIVER: One delivery.

QUILL: One allegation.

ACORN: Did anyone see theft?

WREN: I saw crumbs.

LIN: I saw a fork.

DRIVER: I saw the clerk.

CLERK: I saw nothing.

ADA: You have frosting.

CLERK: It is unrelated.

ACORN: To the pie?

CLERK: To the case.

QUILL: I request a recess.

ACORN: For what reason?

QUILL: The court is hungry.

ADA: That's not a reason.

ACORN: It is a familiar condition.

MOSS: I found the gavel.

ACORN: Where was it?

MOSS: Holding the door.

QUILL: Any pie?

MOSS: No pie.

ACORN: Then continue.

WREN: May I check my oven?

ACORN: You may.

LIN: May we check the jury room?

ACORN: You may.

DRIVER: May I check my cart?

ACORN: You may.

CLERK: May I check the break room?

ACORN: You may not.

CLERK: I object.

ACORN: Denied.

The witnesses leave in four directions. They return carrying four different pies. The judge asks which one is evidence. Nobody knows.

SCENE 5B. INT. COURTROOM, LATER

Judge Acorn questions the clerk about the frosting.

ACORN: Did you eat the pie?

CLERK: Which pie?

QUILL: There is more than one?

ADA: We found four.

ACORN: Why four?

WREN: One was practice.

LIN: One was lunch.

DRIVER: One was in my cart.

MOSS: One was under the bench.

ACORN: Which one was evidence?

QUILL: The one we didn't eat.

ADA: That narrows it.

ACORN: Clerk, explain the frosting.

CLERK: I tested it.

ACORN: For what?

CLERK: Quality.

WREN: I baked it.

CLERK: Then it's good.

ACORN: That is not a test.

QUILL: It tasted persuasive.

ADA: Did the clerk remove evidence?

CLERK: I removed a slice.

ACORN: From which pie?

CLERK: The evidence pie.

QUILL: I object.

ACORN: On what ground?

QUILL: The answer is inconvenient.

ADA: Sustained.

ACORN: I cannot sustain that.

MOSS: The gavel is back.

ACORN: Where was it?

MOSS: The clerk's desk.

CLERK: I needed a doorstop.

ACORN: This courthouse needs better storage.

ADA: And labels.

QUILL: And lunch.

ACORN: Court is adjourned.

WREN: May I take the pie?

ACORN: Which pie?

WREN: Exactly.

The witnesses leave carrying the correctly labeled pies. The clerk stays behind and writes "not evidence" on the empty plate.

FINAL IMAGE: The courthouse staff gather for a group photo beside the pie. The timer clicks. When the photo prints, the pie is gone again. The clerk is holding a fork and looking surprised.
