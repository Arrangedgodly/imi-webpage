# The Shot That Could Not Cut

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: one-take action thrillers, continuous camera moves, escalating corridor chases, and heroes forced to solve problems without an edit*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A gibbon courier must carry a town's only power key through a supposedly unbroken action sequence, while the camera crew's refusal to cut turns routine obstacles into spectacular delays.

Cast
- RELL, a gibbon courier with long arms and a careful walking pace.
- VIKA, a macaque camera operator who never wants to lose a shot.
- DEX, a capuchin lighting technician rolling a battery cart.
- SAVA, a mandrill security guard at the civic building.
- MAYOR TUL, a ring-tailed lemur who needs the key before the power test.

## Scene 1: The Briefing

INT. MUNICIPAL HALL - DAY

A digital timer counts down to the evening power test. Rell holds a brass key. Mayor Tul tells him to bring it to the control room across the building.

MAYOR TUL: Take the north corridor. Give the key to Sava. Do not lose it.

VIKA: We will follow him in one continuous shot.

RELL: Why?

VIKA: The audience needs to feel every step.

DEX: I need to bring the battery cart. It cannot fit through the narrow door.

VIKA: We will follow from behind.

SAVA: There are two doors. One is a closet.

VIKA: We will reveal that in the shot.

The camera begins. Rell walks down the corridor at a steady pace. The crew follows, cart squeaking.

## Scene 2: The Corridor

INT. NORTH HALL - CONTINUOUS

The north corridor ends at a locked gate. Sava points to a side door. Rell tries the key; it does not fit.

SAVA: That is the storage room.

VIKA: Stay in the shot. We can have the key try another lock.

RELL: The key is for the control room.

DEX: The battery cart is stuck.

He pulls. The cart rolls backward, nearly hitting Vika. Rell holds the key aloft and sidesteps the cart. The camera circles him, showing the same closed gate from three angles.

RELL: We need the west stairs.

SAVA: That requires a cut through the lobby.

VIKA: No cuts.

SAVA: I meant a shortcut.

## Scene 3: The Lobby Chase

INT. LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

They cross the lobby. A cleaning robot blocks the path. Dex taps it with the battery cart. The robot turns and follows them. Vika backs away, keeping Rell centered while the robot chases the cart.

DEX: It thinks the battery belongs to it.

RELL: It can have the cart after the power test.

VIKA: Can we make this more urgent?

MAYOR TUL (over speaker): The power test starts in six minutes.

VIKA: Excellent.

Rell darts around the reception desk. The robot follows the light on Vika's camera. The group reaches the west stairs, but the door is held open by a fire extinguisher.

SAVA: That is against safety rules.

RELL: Remove it.

SAVA: If I do, the door closes on us.

Rell places the key on his shoulder, frees the door, then retrieves the key before crossing. The camera follows the whole movement in a close-up and a wide shot without cutting.

## Scene 4: The Stairs

INT. WEST STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS

They climb. The battery cart cannot climb the steps. Dex suggests carrying it. Vika rejects the idea because the cart wheels will leave the frame. They lift it together, moving up one stair at a time. The robot remains below, scanning.

RELL: The key is still in my hand.

VIKA: We have seen it. Keep moving.

SAVA: There is a landing door at the top.

RELL: Is it the control room?

SAVA: It is a door.

At the landing, Rell turns the handle. The door opens onto a supply closet filled with folded chairs. The camera continues to roll as everyone stares at the chairs.

DEX: At least the cart fits.

VIKA: We are not stopping in the closet.

RELL: Then why did we open the door?

No one answers. The cleaning robot arrives at the bottom of the stairs, its light blinking.

## Scene 5: The Key Transfer

They find the correct corridor by following Sava's posted evacuation map. It leads directly to the control room. Mayor Tul waits inside beside the main switch.

MAYOR TUL: You made it.

RELL: The north corridor was closed.

MAYOR TUL: I forgot the gate was locked for the floor repair.

VIKA: We have the entire journey in one shot.

DEX: And the cart is still full.

Rell hands the key to the mayor. Tul unlocks the control panel and starts the power test. The lights dim, then return. The robot's charging station lights up in the lobby.

SAVA: The building stayed powered.

RELL: The key fit the control room lock.

VIKA: That is the climax.

## Final Image

The camera keeps rolling as the crew walks back down the stairs. Vika refuses to stop filming until the battery dies. Rell places the brass key in a labeled hook by the control panel and watches the red recording light finally go dark.
