# The House That Moved

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: home renovation reality programs that promise transformation before inspecting the tree or its inhabitants*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A renovation crew promises to turn an old tree hollow into a modern shared home. They discover the tree already houses a troop whose daily use conflicts with the planned open floor plan.

Cast: Builder Rena, a macaque; designer Varo, a baboon; resident Seli, a gibbon; and camera host Piri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. OLD TREE, DAY

Piri stands before the tree.

PIRI: Today we create a dream home.

Seli steps out of a hollow carrying a water bowl.

SELI: It is already my home.

VARO: We will modernize it.

Rena taps the trunk. A branch shakes above them.

RENA: The structure is alive.

PIRI: That is a feature.

SCENE 2. INT. TREE HOLLOW, CONTINUOUS

Varo sketches an open floor plan on a leaf.

VARO: We can remove this wall and connect the sleeping nook to the dining area.

SELI: That is the trunk.

RENA: We cannot remove it.

PIRI: We can make a reveal of it.

A small monkey swings through the hollow and takes Varo's pencil.

VARO: The pencil is part of the design kit.

SELI: It is now part of the troop.

Rena measures the opening. It is too narrow for the proposed sofa.

RENA: The sofa will not fit.

PIRI: Can we angle it?

They try. The sofa wedges in the entrance.

SCENE 3. EXT. TREE, LATER

Rena and Seli pull the sofa. Varo pushes from inside. The small monkey sits on the sofa and refuses to move.

PIRI: The homeowners have rejected the feature.

SELI: We do not need a sofa.

VARO: The render includes one.

RENA: The render is not the tree.

They remove the sofa. The entrance is clear again. The troop carries a woven mat into the hollow.

PIRI: That is not the reveal.

SELI: It is what fits.

SCENE 4. INT. TREE HOLLOW, AFTERNOON

The crew paints a wall panel. The paint runs into a knot hole. A stream of sap emerges.

VARO: The wall is sweating.

RENA: The tree is sealing the cut.

SELI: It does that after rain.

Piri asks Seli to repeat walking through the entrance for a camera shot.

SELI: I live here. I do not make an entrance.

The small monkey comes in backwards, carrying the pencil.

SCENE 4A. EXT. TREE, LATE AFTERNOON

Rena shows Seli the renovation plan. It includes a painted doorway, a decorative shelf, and a wide step.

RENA: The step will keep mud outside.

SELI: We enter from the branch.

Varo points to the shelf.

VARO: We can display the fruit there.

The young monkey takes the fruit from the bowl and climbs away.

PIRI: The homeowner has rejected the shelf.

SELI: We keep fruit in the bowl until it is eaten.

Rena studies the tree's root and branch paths. She sketches a new layout with the existing entrance, a cleared step, and no added furniture.

RENA: This is the plan the tree allows.

VARO: It looks like the old tree.

SELI: Because it is the old tree.

Piri turns the camera toward the troop. The young monkey returns and walks through the hollow without getting stuck.

PIRI: That is a real reveal.

Rena removes the paint cans. Varo folds the original rendering.

SCENE 4B. INT. TREE HOLLOW, CONTINUOUS

Varo asks the camera crew to film the old hollow before it is repaired.

VARO: We need a before image.

Seli sits on the woven mat. The young monkey moves through the entrance, carries a fruit, and climbs to the upper branch.

PIRI: The shot has already changed.

VARO: Can everyone stay still?

SELI: We are not a showroom.

Rena measures the loose step. It shifts when the monkey lands on the branch.

RENA: I will wedge a stone under this edge.

She tries a flat stone, then a smaller one. The step settles. Seli tests it with one foot.

SELI: That holds.

VARO: The change is not visible.

PIRI: It is visible to the person using the step.

Varo looks into the lens, then puts the camera down and tests it himself.

SCENE 5. EXT. TREE, SUNSET

The crew's design board shows the tree with a new sign, "OPEN PLAN." Rena removes it.

RENA: We can repair the loose step and clear the entrance.

VARO: That is a smaller transformation.

SELI: It is a better one.

The crew clears branches from the path and stabilizes the step with a flat stone. The troop enters and exits easily. Varo takes down the design board.

PIRI: The home is complete.

SELI: The home was complete before you arrived.

Piri looks at the camera, then at the tree.

PIRI: The renovation is complete.

The small monkey carries the sign away and places it on the compost pile.
