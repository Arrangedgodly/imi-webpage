# The Map Beneath the Museum

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: treasure-adventure films, cryptic maps, traps, and competitive explorers*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: Two monkey cousins search for a lost observatory beneath a museum, but the clues were written by a curator who loved puns and hated people touching the exhibits.

Cast: Nessa, an orangutan archivist; Deke, a capuchin climber; Curator Vale, an elderly baboon; Pip, a mouse guide; and a flock of pigeons who nest in the museum's clock tower.

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM ARCHIVE, NIGHT

Nessa compares an old map to a painting of a hill. Deke arrives wearing a climbing harness and carrying a rope. He asks where the treasure is.

NESSA: Probably under something labeled "do not dig."

They find a note behind the frame: "Begin where time keeps its face." Deke runs to the clock. Nessa notices the painting's sun points toward the museum's sundial.

SCENE 2. EXT. MUSEUM COURTYARD, MORNING

The sundial's shadow points to a bench. Deke lifts the bench. Nothing. He digs under the bench with a spoon. Nessa stops him and reads the inscription: "The seat of learning." They inspect the library chair. A drawer beneath it contains a brass key and a second note.

Deke asks whether the key opens a door. Nessa tries it on a display case. An alarm rings. Curator Vale appears, holding a broom. He recognizes the key as belonging to the old planetarium basement.

SCENE 2A. INT. MUSEUM GIFT SHOP, EVENING

Deke buys a toy shovel and a hat with a built-in compass. The compass spins whenever he turns his head. Nessa tests it beside the brass model and learns the toy points to the museum's metal security gate.

Lord Feathers arrives wearing a headlamp. He says treasure hunters must move quickly. He steps on the display mat and triggers an alarm. The museum lights flash. He freezes in a heroic pose while the alarm plays a cheerful melody.

Curator Vale asks them all to leave the shop. Deke wants to bring the toy shovel. Nessa makes him return it. The cashier says the shovel has already been scanned. Vale refunds the money in exact coins, which Deke drops. He spends the next minute retrieving them from under a brochure rack.

SCENE 2B. INT. STORAGE HALL, NIGHT

The note's next clue says, "Where the stars sleep, turn the key." Deke checks the planetarium again. Nessa realizes the old star projector is stored in a closet. They locate it beside a broken mannequin and three unclaimed umbrellas.

The brass key opens the projector's battery compartment, not the observatory. Inside is a smaller note, folded around a dead battery. The note says, "Bring new batteries." Vale admits the curator who made the clues had a fondness for practical instructions.

Deke asks if this counts as a trap. Nessa says it counts as maintenance. They borrow fresh batteries from the gift shop and power the projector.

SCENE 3. INT. PLANETARIUM, AFTERNOON

The cousins descend into a sealed room. Painted stars cover the ceiling. The second note reads: "Find the bear that never sleeps." Deke looks for a bear statue. Nessa sees the constellation Ursa Major projected above the old telescope.

They aim the telescope at the ceiling. A small compartment opens. Inside is a brass model of the first observatory. No jewels. Deke looks disappointed. Vale explains that the model contains the original star chart.

SCENE 3A. EXT. ROOFTOP OBSERVATORY, NIGHT

The projector lights the old star chart across the dome. Nessa aligns the brass model with the projected constellations. One panel opens, revealing the deed and a logbook of public observing nights.

Lord Feathers tries to take the deed, but the page is so brittle that it tears at the corner. He gasps as if the treasure has been destroyed. Nessa repairs it with archival tissue and asks him not to touch anything else.

The logbook contains hundreds of names, sketches, and questions from local children. Deke reads one child's note asking whether the moon has a back. He looks up at it and says he hopes so.

SCENE 4. INT. ARCHIVE, LATER

A rival treasure hunter, a peacock named Lord Feathers, enters wearing a cape and carrying a metal detector. He claims the chart belongs to him. The detector beeps at every brass door hinge. He follows the sound in circles.

Nessa unrolls the chart. It shows an unmarked patch of sky and a note: "The treasure is where the sky can be shared." Vale explains the old observatory was open to the public. The real prize is a deed preserving it as a community science room.

SCENE 5. EXT. ROOFTOP OBSERVATORY, NIGHT

The cousins restore the telescope. Children line up to look at the moon. Lord Feathers tries to plant a flag, but the flag sticks in the telescope's wheel and slowly spins him around. Deke catches him. The peacock pretends the spin was part of his entrance.

SCENE 6. INT. ROOFTOP OBSERVATORY, NIGHT

Feathers asks whether he may take one souvenir. Nessa points to the gift shop below. He asks if the stars are for sale. Vale says no. Deke offers him the brass model to inspect while Nessa closes the display case.

A pigeon lands on the model. Feathers asks if it is guarding the treasure. The bird pecks the flag on his cape and flies off with it. Feathers starts to chase, then notices children waiting for the telescope. He stays and helps them take turns. The next child points out a constellation shaped like a spoon. Vale says it is not an official constellation. Deke says it is now.

FINAL IMAGE: Nessa places the brass model beside the telescope. A pigeon lands on the model and sits exactly where the observatory dome should be. Deke asks if that is a clue. Nessa says it's a pigeon. The pigeon coos, and the children all look up.
