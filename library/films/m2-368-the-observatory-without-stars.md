# The Observatory Without Stars

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: astronomical mysteries, ominous observatory alarms, star maps, and explorers who look for the universe before turning off the lights*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
ASTRONOMER NARI, an orangutan
TECHNICIAN PIP, a capuchin
DIRECTOR VEX, a macaque
VISITOR LUMA, a lemur
NIGHT GUARD ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. OBSERVATORY - NIGHT

The dome opens, but the telescope sees no stars. Director Vex announces that the sky has vanished. Astronomer Nari checks the weather; the sky is clear. Technician Pip finds the dome lights are still on. Night guard Odo points to the wall switch.

VEX: The cosmos is dark.

ODO: The room is bright.

Nari turns off the lights. A row of stars appears on the screen. The telescope remains pointed at the floor.

SCENE 2. INT. TELESCOPE ROOM - LATER

Pip adjusts the telescope. The stars move out of view. Vex says they are fleeing. Nari explains the telescope mount was locked. She unlocks it, aligns the instrument, and asks Luma to read the coordinates. Luma reads them from a printed chart. The chart is upside down.

PIP: North is at the bottom.

LUMA: The chart is at the bottom.

They rotate the chart and input the correct coordinates. The telescope finds a bright planet. Vex asks whether it is a new world. Nari says it is Venus, visible all season.

SCENE 2A. INT. OBSERVATORY LOBBY - EVENING

A school group arrives for a star viewing. Vex promises them a rare event. Nari checks the schedule and sees no unusual alignment. Pip explains the lights need to be dimmed before the telescope can see faint objects. A student asks why the dome is dark. Odo turns on one small lamp and immediately dims it again when the telescope loses its view.

Luma reads the chart and points out a bright planet. Vex tells the group it could be a new world. Nari asks him to use the catalog name and explain how to find it. The students take turns at the eyepiece. Each sees the same bright point. One student says it looks like a tiny lantern. Pip adjusts the focus so everyone can see clearly.

SCENE 3. INT. CONTROL ROOM - MIDNIGHT

A warning signal sounds. The telescope has lost its tracking lock. Pip checks the motor and finds a loose cable. Vex asks for the emergency override. Nari says the telescope can be reset manually. Odo goes to the dome and finds a ladder blocking the rotation track. He moves it. The dome turns again.

The screen shows a streak of light. Vex calls it a comet. Nari checks the schedule and finds it is a satellite crossing the frame. Pip slows the tracking speed. The satellite moves off screen, and the stars stay visible. Vex asks if they can name the object. Nari says its catalog name is already printed.

SCENE 3A. INT. TELESCOPE ROOM - LATER

A cloud passes across the field. Vex tells the crew the stars have vanished a second time. Nari checks the weather screen and confirms a thin cloud layer. Pip asks the dome to close, then stops it when the telescope is still tracking. Odo clears a chair from the rotation path. The cloud moves away on its own. The telescope resumes its track without anyone resetting the sky.

The crew compares the live image with the recording. A bright point appears in a different place because the Earth has turned. Luma notes the time and coordinates. Vex asks whether they have found a new signal. Nari points to the rotation chart and shows that the object is expected.

SCENE 4. EXT. OBSERVATORY DECK - DAWN

The crew watches the sky brighten. Luma asks whether the stars have disappeared. Nari says they are still there; daylight makes them difficult to see. Vex asks for one last shot of the empty sky. Pip points the camera toward the telescope display, which shows the star field they recorded overnight.

ODO: We found them when we turned the lights off.

VEX: The story has a clear beginning and end.

NARI: The sky had both before we arrived.

SCENE 5. INT. OBSERVATORY LOBBY - MORNING

The team updates the instruction card: turn off the dome lights, unlock the mount, enter the coordinates, then track the sky. Vex adds a dramatic title but Nari removes it. The observatory doors open for the daytime school group. Pip leaves the chart right side up and tapes the light switch label beside it. He puts the chairs back beneath the window and stores the charts flat.

FINAL IMAGE: The dome closes over the telescope as the sun rises, and the star map remains pinned beside the light switch.
