# The House That Whispered Later

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-house horror, ominous hallway shots, cursed objects, and characters who ignore practical explanations until the finale*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
NORI, a capuchin caretaker
VALE, a ring-tailed lemur archivist
MR. THISTLE, a gibbon groundskeeper
THE HOUSE, creaking doors, pipes, and one unreliable bell

SCENE 1. EXT. OLD WILLOW HOUSE - NIGHT

Rain lashes a crooked mansion at the edge of town. Nori and Vale arrive with one flashlight between them. A plaque reads "WILLOW HOUSE: CLOSED UNTIL REPAIRS." The front door creaks open by itself.

VALE: That's an invitation.

NORI: That's a loose hinge.

The camera peers into a dark hallway. A bell rings from somewhere upstairs. Vale clutches the flashlight. Nori checks the front door. Its hinge is indeed loose.

SCENE 2. INT. WILLOW HOUSE - HALLWAY

They enter. The front door slams behind them. Nori tries the knob. It sticks. Vale finds an old coat on a hook. As she walks past, the coat sleeve lifts and points toward the stairs.

VALE: It wants us to go up.

NORI: It's caught in the draft.

The coat points again. Nori removes it from the hook. The sleeve drops. They continue upstairs.

A long corridor stretches before them. Every door is open except one. Behind it, a whisper: "Later."

VALE: The house has spoken.

NORI: A pipe is vibrating.

VALE: The pipe said "later."

NORI: It could be a water knock.

The door handle turns by itself. Nori grabs it. It stops. When Vale approaches, it turns again. She backs away; it stops. She steps forward; it spins. They repeat the move like a reluctant dance.

SCENE 3. INT. ATTIC - NIGHT

The attic is filled with chairs. Each chair faces a blank wall. The bell rings. A rocking chair begins to move. Vale gasps. Nori notices a rope running from the chair's rocker through a hole in the floor.

They follow the rope. It is tied to a water pipe, which shudders every time the rain hits the roof. The rope pulls the chair. The vibration makes the bell ring.

VALE: The ghost has a very complicated arrangement.

NORI: The attic has a very old plumbing system.

A sheet lifts from a stack and floats down the stairs. They run after it. The sheet catches on the banister and hangs like a figure. Nori reaches to remove it. The house lights flicker. The figure appears to move closer.

VALE: Don't touch it!

NORI: I'm going to untangle it.

The sheet drops over Nori. Vale screams, then runs into a chair. The chair rolls downhill and bumps the sheet. Nori slides down the hall, wrapped in linen, crashing gently into a laundry basket.

A door at the end of the hall opens by itself. A shadow stretches across the wall, tall and horned. Vale freezes. Nori takes the flashlight, but the beam only makes the shadow larger.

VALE: It has found us.

NORI: It is blocking the lamp.

The shadow lifts one arm. Nori moves left; it moves right. Vale steps toward the door; the shadow grows. Nori pulls the door wider. The shadow shrinks, revealing a coat rack with two hats and a moth fluttering in the lamp's beam.

VALE: The house has a moth.

NORI: The house has a coat rack.

The moth lands on the light. The bulb flickers, and the horned shadow returns. Vale covers the lamp with a glass jar. The moth flies free; the shadow disappears. The bell rings again from the attic.

VALE: That one wasn't a moth.

NORI: No. That one is still a loose pipe.

SCENE 4. INT. KITCHEN - LATER

Mr. Thistle, the groundskeeper, arrives with a toolbox. He hears the whispering pipe and nods.

THISTLE: That pipe has been loose for years. I meant to fix it after the roof.

VALE: The house says "later."

THISTLE: The valve is labeled "later." It is the old control for the upstairs radiator.

He tightens a bracket. The whisper stops. He ties the bell rope away from the pipe. The attic chair stops rocking.

NORI: So the house isn't haunted.

THISTLE: The door still sticks. And the roof still leaks.

A loud creak comes from upstairs. Everyone looks up. A chair rolls slowly into view at the top of the stairwell.

They freeze. The chair stops. A cat emerges from beneath it, batting the loose bell rope.

VALE: The cat was the ghost.

THISTLE: The cat lives here.

SCENE 5. EXT. WILLOW HOUSE - DAWN

The rain ends. Nori repairs the hinge. Vale writes a new plaque: "OPEN BY APPOINTMENT. CAT ON PREMISES." Thistle carries the rocking chair outside and places it beneath the porch roof.

VALE: I still heard something upstairs.

NORI: The radiator is cooling.

VALE: It whispered "later."

NORI: It might have been the valve.

The bell rings once. The cat has found the rope again. Thistle removes it and gives the cat a ball of twine.

FINAL IMAGE: The house sits quiet in the dawn light. Inside, a pipe cools with one small click. The cat bats the twine, and the repaired door swings shut without a sound.
