# The Masked Ball's Name Tag

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: masked-ball romance films that rely on a single lost identity clue while the cast ignores obvious introductions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: At a town masquerade, two monkeys search for the mysterious guest behind a silver mask, though each has already introduced themselves by name.

Cast: Elia, a macaque florist; Rumo, a capuchin tailor; host Sava, a gibbon; announcer Piri, a lemur; guard Oda, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. TOWN HALL - EVENING

Guests arrive in masks and name tags. Elia wears a silver fox mask. Rumo wears a blue heron mask. They meet by the coat rack as both reach for the same empty hook.

ELIA: I'm Elia. The fox mask is borrowed.

RUMO: I'm Rumo. I made the heron mask myself.

Piri, holding a microphone, announces their arrival.

PIRI: A mysterious silver guest and a mysterious blue guest have entered the hall.

ELIA: We just said our names.

PIRI: The announcement needs mystery.

SCENE 2 - INT. BALLROOM - LATER

Elia and Rumo dance. Piri moves through the crowd asking guests to guess who they are. Sava hands out a bowl of blank name tags.

SAVA: The tags fell off when the coat rack tipped. Please write your name again.

ELIA: Mine is in my pocket.

RUMO: Mine is pinned under my collar.

Piri ignores them and asks a guest in a green mask for a clue. The guest points at her tag, which reads LUMA.

PIRI: The identity is revealed!

LUMA: It has been written on my chest all evening.

The orchestra starts a waltz. Elia and Rumo move to the center. Their name tags catch on each other's sleeves and swap places.

SCENE 3 - INT. BALLROOM - CONTINUOUS

Rumo looks down and sees Elia's name. Elia reads Rumo's tag. Both look up, confused.

RUMO: I thought you were the silver guest.

ELIA: I am Elia. You just told me you were Rumo.

PIRI: Could one of you be wearing the other's identity?

SAVA: They are wearing the other's tag. The pins came loose.

Piri asks the orchestra for a dramatic sting. The musicians hit a soft chord. It is not dramatic enough, so Piri cues it again. The musicians stop playing.

ORCHESTRA LEADER: We are not repeating the sting until the dance is over.

SCENE 3A - INT. BALLROOM - CONTINUOUS

Piri lays out a series of clue cards. The first says THE SILVER GUEST LIKES FLOWERS. Elia raises her hand. The second says THE BLUE GUEST KNOWS HOW TO SEW. Rumo raises his hand.

PIRI: Our audience is getting close.

ELIA: I am a florist. My name is on the tag.

RUMO: I am a tailor. My name is also on the tag.

Piri turns the cards over. Their answers are printed on the backs.

SAVA: Those are the guest registration forms. You copied the information from the sign-in table.

PIRI: We can still make it a mystery.

Oda brings over a tray of replacement pins. He places it beside the cards. A guest picks up a pin and fixes her own tag without removing her mask.

ELIA: That is the simplest reveal of the night.

RUMO: We should fix the rack before everyone returns to the coats.

They move the rack away from the ballroom entrance and set it beside the wall. Piri steps aside, allowing the next guest to pass without another announcement.

SCENE 4 - INT. COAT ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The host searches the coat room for a spare pin. The rack is full of coats, masks and tag strings. Elia finds a small basket labeled SPARE NAME TAGS. Rumo finds two safety pins.

ELIA: Here is mine. The pin was loose.

RUMO: I can fix yours while you fix mine.

They trade tags back and secure them with safety pins. Elia notices that Rumo has a needle and thread in his pocket.

ELIA: You made the heron mask?

RUMO: I did. I sew costumes at the theater.

PIRI: The mysterious tailor has been revealed.

RUMO: I told you my name and job when we met.

SCENE 5 - INT. BALLROOM - LATER

The guests return to the dance. Piri puts down the microphone. Elia and Rumo dance without masks. Sava introduces them to the room by their actual names. The orchestra plays a waltz without an extra sting.

SAVA: Elia, this is Rumo. Rumo, this is Elia.

ELIA: We have met.

RUMO: And our tags are in the right places.

Piri tears up a cue card that says UNMASKED REVEAL. Oda checks the coat rack and stands it against the wall so it cannot tip again.

FINAL IMAGE: Two name tags sit correctly pinned beneath two masks on the coat rack, while Elia and Rumo dance in the open ballroom with no one guessing who they are.
