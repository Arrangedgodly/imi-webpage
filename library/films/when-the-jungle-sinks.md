# When the Jungle Sinks

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: Big-budget disaster blockbusters*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Logline: When a luxury cruise ship built over a migrating whale's route springs a leak, a skeptical capuchin weather reader must persuade a city of tourists to evacuate before the floating casino becomes a very expensive reef.

Cast: Dr. Lolo, a capuchin meteorologist who reads cloud shapes and ferry schedules; Captain Bragg, a gorilla in a white uniform; Nia, a macaque lifeguard; Mayor Tusk, an elephant who hates being wrong in public; Vinnie, a raccoon casino manager; and Old Blue, a humpback whale with a habit of surfacing under speeches.

SCENE 1. EXT. SUNSPLASH BAY, DAY

A glittering cruise ship, THE PALM PALACE, glides into harbor. A banner promises the world's largest wave pool. It has been built over a protected whale corridor because the developer described the corridor as "mostly water."

LOLO crouches on the pier with a pocket notebook. The clouds form a bent spoon. She listens to the ferry horn, which sounds lower than yesterday.

LOLO: Something big is pushing water toward the harbor.

Mayor Tusk pats her shoulder. He is posing for a photograph beside the ship's mascot, a plastic banana in a captain's hat.

TUSK: Weather is just the sky being dramatic.

The harbor lifts six inches. The mayor's shoes do not.

SCENE 2. INT. PALM PALACE, CASINO FLOOR, AFTERNOON

Vinnie the raccoon spins a roulette wheel. Nia races through the room carrying an unconscious guest over one shoulder. Every machine flashes JACKPOT. The ship tilts, and the coins roll toward the snack bar.

Bragg orders everyone to remain calm. He grips a brass pole as the ship rolls farther.

BRAGG: The ship is designed to flex.

LOLO: The ship is flexing into the whale route.

Through a window, Old Blue's back rises beside the hull. Another whale follows. They have turned to avoid the ship and shoved the tide toward the bay. A seam tears. Salt water pours through the lowest deck.

Bragg tells the crew to seal the bulkhead. The bulkhead has a casino carpet pattern and an enormous golden palm painted on it. No one can find the handle.

SCENE 3. INT. ENGINE ROOM, CONTINUOUS

Nia leads a line of passengers down a ladder. She is a macaque and has no trouble climbing against the tilt, though she scolds a guest for trying to bring a slot machine.

NIA: It doesn't have a life jacket.

GUEST: It has my savings.

LOLO and Bragg reach the engine controls. A floor grate buckles. Water hisses up around their ankles. The emergency pumps are locked behind a panel. Bragg hits it with his shoulder. Nothing. Lolo sees a row of plastic palm fronds in the wall and pulls the wrong one. A tiny speaker plays steel drums.

LOLO: The ship is themed.

She tracks the power cable behind the fronds. The right switch bears the logo of the casino's wave pool. She throws it. The pumps start, sucking water from the engine room and, alarmingly, the wave pool.

SCENE 4. EXT. PALM PALACE DECK, DUSK

The storm hits. A wall of water rolls over the ship. Guests crowd the top deck. The lifeboats are on the wrong side, beneath the listing hull.

Lolo finds the ship's horn. She asks Bragg to sound it in a pattern used by the local ferry crews. The first blast is weak. Old Blue answers from the dark. The whale and her pod turn toward the sound, away from the harbor mouth.

LOLO: They can make us a channel. We have to get everyone to the stern.

TUSK: The stern is underwater.

NIA: Then we go up.

The macaque lifeguard climbs a decorative mast and swings down a line. Bragg anchors it around the main mast. Passengers cross above the flooded deck while Nia calls out footing. Vinnie fills a pool ring with casino chips, then realizes it will sink and swaps them for actual life jackets.

SCENE 5. EXT. HARBOR, NIGHT

Old Blue's pod parts the waves, holding a calmer lane between the reef and the ship. The Palm Palace drifts through the opening. Lolo sounds the horn in short bursts. The whales answer, guiding the vessel toward a sandbar where it settles without capsizing.

The storm passes. Mayor Tusk stands knee-deep in water, soaked and quiet. The ship's wave pool drains into the sea. A plastic palm floats past wearing the mascot hat.

TUSK: I was wrong about the water.

LOLO: That's an excellent place to start.

He tells the harbor to close the cruise route until the whale corridor is marked and protected. Vinnie gives every rescued guest a refund and a free towel. He gives the casino its slot machine back, but only after Nia removes the cash box.

SCENE 6. EXT. SAND BAR, MORNING

The Palm Palace rests on the sand. A crew paints a large blue line around the protected corridor. Lolo checks her notebook. The clouds look like a banana peel.

NIA: What does that mean?

LOLO: It means the weather is improving. Or someone dropped breakfast.

Old Blue rises offshore. The passengers wave. The whale turns one eye toward them, then dives. The mayor plants a sign that reads KEEP THE CHANNEL CLEAR. A wave reaches his knees and knocks the sign over.

Everyone laughs. He picks it up and plants it farther inland.

The sun breaks through as the battered ship releases one last puff of steam, a perfect imitation of a whale's breath.

Cut to black.
