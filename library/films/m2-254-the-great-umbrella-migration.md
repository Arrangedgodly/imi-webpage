# The Great Umbrella Migration

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: wildlife migration documentaries, where a flock of umbrellas is tracked across a village during a rainstorm*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A documentary crew follows a line of umbrellas that keeps changing owners as rain drives everyone toward the same dry awning.

Cast: Narrator Seli, capuchin; field guide Tavi, macaque; camera operator Odo, baboon; commuter Nara, gibbon.

SCENE 1. EXT. VILLAGE PATH, RAIN

A red umbrella moves down the path. Seli narrates from beneath a yellow one.

SELI: At the start of the wet season, the red umbrella begins its migration.

TAVI: It belongs to Nara.

NARA: I am carrying it.

ODO: The camera cannot see the migration if you hold it so low.

NARA: It is raining.

Seli says they will track the umbrella's route. Nara hands it to Tavi while adjusting a bag. Tavi walks two steps, then realizes he has the red umbrella and Nara has the yellow one.

TAVI: The red has changed hosts.

SELI: A rare mid-route transfer.

NARA: We traded because mine has a hole.

Odo zooms in on the hole. Rain drips through onto his camera.

ODO: The camera needs cover.

SELI: Follow the umbrella, not the camera.

SCENE 2. EXT. MARKET AWNING, CONTINUOUS

The group reaches a narrow awning. Three other villagers stand beneath it, each holding a closed umbrella. Nara opens the red one; it bumps the awning.

NARA: There is no room.

TAVI: The flock has reached shelter.

SELI: The flock is three umbrellas and four primates.

Odo asks Nara to open the umbrella again for a close-up. She does. The umbrella catches a gust and turns inside out.

[SFX: FABRIC POP.]

SELI: The red umbrella has changed its shape to conserve energy.

NARA: It is broken.

Tavi turns the spokes back. One is bent. He asks Odo for tape. Odo offers a microphone cable tie, but Nara refuses to fasten a wet umbrella with camera equipment.

SCENE 3. INT. MARKET STALL, MOMENTS LATER

A vendor lets them stand inside while the umbrella dries. Seli continues the narration through the open doorway.

SELI: The migration pauses at a sheltered feeding ground.

TAVI: It is a market.

NARA: I need to get across the square before the rain gets heavier.

A second umbrella, blue and striped, rolls past the doorway. Odo turns the camera.

ODO: A new individual has entered the group.

TAVI: It is rolling down the hill.

Nara catches it with her foot. The owner appears, thanks her, and takes it. Seli asks if the blue umbrella will join the red one.

OWNER: We are going to different streets.

SELI: The flock has split.

Before the rain stops, Nara suggests they use the blue umbrella to cover Odo's camera. Odo refuses because the camera is already wet. Tavi points out that the lens needs a dry cloth, not an umbrella.

TAVI: The camera does not migrate.

SELI: It has traveled with us.

NARA: You are standing still under the awning.

Odo wipes the lens. The red umbrella remains open beside the door, drying. The blue one is already gone with its owner.

SELI: The two species have chosen different routes.

TAVI: They are owned by different people.

SCENE 4. EXT. SQUARE, LATER

The rain stops. The red umbrella is still drying. Tavi says the migration is over. Seli says they need a final shot. Nara opens the red umbrella; a drop falls onto the camera lens.

ODO: The lens is wet.

SELI: The umbrella has marked its territory.

NARA: It marked your lens.

Odo wipes it. The group stands under the awning as the sun comes out. Everyone closes their umbrellas.

SELI: The flock has folded its wings.

TAVI: They are umbrellas.

NARA: They are also dry now.

Tavi hands the red umbrella back to Nara. She checks the bent spoke and ties it with a strip of cloth. It opens, but its shape is slightly lopsided.

SELI: The red one has adapted to its environment.

NARA: It has been repaired.

SCENE 5. EXT. PATH, SUNSET

The camera follows Nara as she walks away. Seli narrates a final passage about the species' return to ordinary life.

SELI: As the sky clears, the umbrellas scatter, each to its own home.

TAVI: Nara is taking hers back to her porch.

SELI: A solitary journey.

NARA: I can hear you.

Odo pans to the yellow umbrella, which has been left beside the market stall.

TAVI: One has remained behind.

SELI: The abandoned juvenile-

The vendor picks it up and hangs it by the door.

NARA: That one is the vendor's.

SELI: The migration has reached a stable resting place.

[SFX: UMBRELLA SHAKES DRY, FADE.]
