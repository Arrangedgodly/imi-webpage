# The Summit Is Closed Today

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mountaineering epics, summit fever, heroic rope teams, and climbers who mistake a posted closure for a personal challenge*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
GUIDE NARI, an orangutan
CLIMBER VEX, a macaque
CLIMBER PIP, a capuchin
RANGER LUMA, a lemur
PORTER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAILHEAD - DAWN

A sign says SUMMIT TRAIL CLOSED: LOOSE ROCK. Vex studies the peak through binoculars and declares the mountain has challenged them. Guide Nari checks the weather and says the closure is real. Pip asks if there is another route. Ranger Luma points to a lower ridge that remains open.

VEX: We came to reach the summit.

NARI: We came to climb safely.

Odo unpacks three ropes, two thermoses, and a banner reading I MADE IT.

SCENE 2. EXT. LOWER RIDGE - MORNING

The group climbs the open trail. Vex keeps looking up at the summit, while Nari points out safe footing. Pip stops to tie his boot. Odo hands him a thermos and waits. Vex urges them onward, but the sign at the next marker says the summit route remains closed. He searches for a smaller sign that might allow the climb.

Luma arrives with a radio. A recent rockfall has blocked the upper path. She asks the team to report any loose debris and keep away from the barrier. Vex says he can climb around it. Nari refuses and takes the rope from his pack.

SCENE 2A. EXT. TRAIL MARKER - MORNING

Nari asks each climber to read the posted trail conditions aloud. Pip reads the closure notice, including the date and the inspection update. Vex skips to the map legend and claims the red line marks the fastest route. Luma explains it marks the closed trail. Odo tries to tie the banner to a signpost, but the wind turns it into a sail and drags him several steps. Pip catches the loose edge and they fold it into a smaller windbreak.

VEX: The banner was meant for the summit.

NARI: The banner is useful here.

A hiker arrives from the opposite direction and confirms the lower path is clear. Vex asks whether the hiker saw anyone on the closed route. The hiker says only the ranger crew. Luma checks her radio before the group continues.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIDGE SHELTER - MIDDAY

Rain begins. The team shelters under a rock overhang. Odo unfolds the banner to cover their packs. The letters I MADE IT face upside down. Pip turns it into a windbreak. Vex studies the route map, but the paper is wet and the summit icon has blurred. Nari uses a compass and landmarks to identify the lower trail back to the shelter.

PIP: We can still see the summit.

VEX: It looks closer now.

NARI: It is the same distance. The clouds moved.

The team hears a crack from above. Rocks tumble down the closed route. One lands near the barrier. Luma radios the ranger station and confirms no climbers are on that trail. Vex looks at the open path and finally accepts the closure.

SCENE 3A. EXT. RIDGE SHELTER - MIDDAY

The crew uses the map to plan the return. Rainwater has blurred one mark, so Nari compares the ridge shape with the compass. Odo knots the rope to a pack and demonstrates how to keep it from slipping. Vex insists they should use it to climb toward the summit. Luma says it is for keeping the packs together while they walk the open trail. Vex ties his own bootlace to the rope by mistake and has to untangle it before moving.

PIP: The equipment list is also in the pack.

VEX: I thought the list was the map.

NARI: They are separate for a reason.

SCENE 4. EXT. LOWER RIDGE - AFTERNOON

The rain stops. The group reaches a viewpoint below the summit. They can see the entire peak, the closed trail, and the rockfall. Odo takes a photo of the team beside the trail marker. Vex asks for the summit banner. Pip gives him the other side, where the words are still upside down. They hold it correctly this time.

Nari explains that the route will reopen after inspectors secure the slope. Vex asks if they can return another day. Luma says the ranger station posts updates. He writes down the information instead of circling a date on the mountain.

SCENE 5. EXT. TRAILHEAD - EVENING

The climbers return to the parking area. Vex unfolds the banner and tries to hang it behind the closure sign for a victory photograph. The wind wraps it around the signpost, hiding the word CLOSED. Nari pulls it free and asks him to hold the banner at the lower ridge marker instead.

VEX: It says I made it.

NARI: You made it to the open trail's viewpoint.

PIP: That is where we are.

Odo takes the thermoses back to the equipment shed. Pip signs the route log, noting that the lower ridge is open and the summit route is closed. Nari checks every climber's gear before they leave.

VEX: The mountain did not defeat us.

NARI: It did not compete with us.

FINAL IMAGE: The summit stands behind the closure sign as the clouds lift, revealing a clear trail marker below it.
