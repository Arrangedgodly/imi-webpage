# The Crown That Passed Its Own Inspection

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal succession fantasies, ceremonial trials, inherited crowns, and rulers who discover the throne needs maintenance*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
PRINCE PIP, a capuchin
QUEEN NARI, an orangutan
BLACKSMITH LUMA, a lemur
HERALD VEX, a macaque
CASTLE KEEPER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. CASTLE HALL - MORNING

Prince Pip prepares for his coronation. The crown is too loose and keeps sliding over his eyes. Queen Nari says the ceremony can wait until the blacksmith checks the band. Herald Vex announces that the crown is a symbol of destiny. Odo points to a wobbly step on the throne dais.

PIP: Is the crown the only thing that needs checking?

NARI: No.

SCENE 2. INT. BLACKSMITH SHOP - MIDDAY

Luma measures the crown. It is an old family piece with a cracked hinge and a missing pin. Pip asks if she can repair it before the ceremony. Luma says she needs a matching pin. Vex proposes using a gold ribbon. Luma says ribbon will not hold the crown in place.

Odo checks the dais and finds one leg shorter than the others. He wedges a wood shim beneath it. Vex asks if the throne can be made taller for the procession. Odo says it is already tall enough. Pip tries the crown; it slides onto his eyebrows again.

SCENE 2A. INT. THRONE ROOM - AFTERNOON

Vex rehearses the coronation proclamation while Pip wears the crown. The crown tips forward. Odo tries to steady the throne; the shim slips on the polished floor and the dais rocks again. The court members shift to keep the crown from falling. Nari pauses the rehearsal and asks everyone to remain still while Luma measures the hinge.

VEX: The ceremony requires a moment of suspense.

LUMA: The hinge requires a pin.

The blacksmith checks the crown against an old portrait. The portrait's crown is a different shape; it belonged to a previous ruler. Vex wants the newer design because it looks grander. Pip asks if the current crown is safe to wear. Luma tests the band and shows the crack. Nari postpones the rehearsal until the repair is complete.

SCENE 3. EXT. CASTLE COURTYARD - AFTERNOON

A messenger delivers a box of spare pins from the royal workshop. The pin is brass, not gold. Vex says the ceremony requires gold. Nari asks whether the crown should stay on Pip's head or look expensive. Pip chooses the brass pin. Luma repairs the hinge and adds a small strap so the crown fits securely.

Luma fits the brass pin into the cracked hinge and tests it once. It opens and closes without catching. Vex asks for a gold coating. Luma says that would hide the repair and make the crown harder to inspect.

Odo returns the shim to the dais, this time placing it on a strip of grip cloth. He presses down on each corner; the wood stays level. The runner still curls at one edge, so Luma tacks it down before the herald can step on it again. Vex requests a dramatic drumroll. The drummer starts before he finishes speaking, then cannot stop because the drumstick has wedged under the rim. Pip pulls it free, and the drum gives one final thump. Vex attempts a stately turn for the portrait painter; his sash catches on the workbench and spins him back toward the level dais. Nari asks Pip to climb the steps once before the ceremony. He does, wearing his own shoes and no crown. Vex says the rehearsal lacks grandeur. Nari says it has gained stability.

SCENE 4. INT. CASTLE HALL - EVENING

The coronation proceeds. Pip walks up the secured steps, wears the crown without it slipping, and takes a seat on the level throne. Nari asks him to read the town's maintenance requests before signing any royal proclamation. The first request is for the bridge railing; the second is for the fountain pump.

PIP: I thought I was supposed to inherit a kingdom.

NARI: You did. It has repairs.

Luma gives Pip a small tool kit. Vex asks if it is part of the pageant. She says it is part of being responsible. The guests applaud. The crown stays in place. Pip checks the fit one last time.

SCENE 5. INT. CASTLE WORKROOM - NIGHT

Pip and Odo review the maintenance list. Nari teaches him how to approve a repair and schedule an inspection. Vex drafts a proclamation about safe bridges and level steps. Luma puts the repaired crown in its case. The family crest reflects in the glass but is not brighter than the brass pin.

FINAL IMAGE: The crown rests on a level shelf beside Pip's first signed work order, the hinge closed and the missing pin replaced.
