# The Puppet King Loses His Strings

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy epics where a ruler's fate hinges on prophecy, even when the ruler is visibly operated by stagehands*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A touring puppet troupe loses control of its king during the climactic prophecy, and the stagehands' visible corrections become the kingdom's most dramatic political crisis.

Cast: performer Daro, a macaque; puppet king Orvo; string handler Teki, a gibbon; narrator Nala, a lemur; theater manager Boro, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. VILLAGE THEATER - AFTERNOON

Daro paints the puppet king's crown backstage. Teki tests the strings from a wooden bridge above the stage. Nala rehearses the prophecy, while Boro checks the curtain rope.

NALA: When the moon touches the tower, the king will choose a successor.

DARO: The moon is a lantern on a pulley.

TEKI: The pulley is sticking.

The king puppet rises, then spins sideways. Teki catches the string before it tangles around the crown. Boro enters and says the audience is already lining up. Daro asks for five minutes to repair the rig. Boro says the theater has exactly five minutes before the doors open.

SCENE 2 - INT. THEATER STAGE - EVENING

The curtain rises. Nala speaks the prophecy. Teki lowers the moon lantern. The king's right arm rises, but the left stays down. Daro performs the king's voice from behind the screen.

DARO (AS KING): My people, I hold the future in both hands.

The right arm waves. The left hand remains limp. Nala tries to cover it with a line about divided rule. The audience murmurs. Teki pulls the left string; the king's head bows so low his crown falls into the orchestra pit.

SCENE 3 - INT. ORCHESTRA PIT - CONTINUOUS

Boro climbs down to retrieve the crown. The musicians stop playing as he crosses their stands. Daro improvises a royal decree ordering the court to remain calm. Nala calls for the curtain, but the rope catches. The king puppet's body lifts out of view while his head stays onstage.

NALA: The king has ascended.

DARO: The king has lost his body.

Boro hands the crown to Teki. Teki reconnects it, but the king now hangs backward. Daro speaks in a strained voice while Teki tries to turn the puppet upright. The king's face repeatedly points at the floor.

DARO (AS KING): I have seen the kingdom from another angle.

The audience laughs. Nala pivots the prophecy into an announcement that the ruler must hear counsel from below. She invites the audience to call out advice. A child shouts, "Pull the left string!" Teki tries it. The king's hand raises and salutes the balcony.

The audience begins chanting for the king to speak again. Daro returns behind the screen. Teki tests each labeled loop, but pulling COURT makes the king's left arm wave while pulling ROYALTY turns the crown. Nala chooses to improvise a new prophecy: the king will appoint the audience as temporary ministers. A child calls for a snack break. Boro checks the clock and says intermission is scheduled in ten minutes. The crowd applauds the child as if the throne has changed hands. Daro gives the puppet a stiff bow, and Teki moves the handle into the light, revealing the strings instead of hiding them.

SCENE 4 - INT. THEATER STAGE - MOMENTS LATER

The scene is nearly over. Teki's sleeve catches on the string bridge. He cannot move the king without lifting his own arm. Daro sees the tangle and stops the speech. Boro lowers the curtain halfway to hide the repair, but the king's crown appears beneath it, bobbing like a second show.

NALA: The court has discovered the truth behind the curtain.

DARO: The court has discovered the knot.

Boro asks the audience to hold for a royal consultation. Teki untangles his sleeve. Daro steps out from behind the screen and asks the puppet king what he wants. Teki releases the strings. The king droops onto a stool.

DARO: The king wishes to retire.

The audience applauds. Daro picks up the puppet and bows with it. Teki holds the strings loose, letting the puppet's hand move with each bow.

SCENE 5 - INT. THEATER LOBBY - NIGHT

Boro posts a new show time for the next day: THE KING AND HIS STAGEHANDS. Nala rewrites the prophecy to include a visible puppet bridge. Teki knots the strings into labeled loops. Daro signs programs as both actor and royal adviser.

NALA: The moon will touch the tower again tomorrow.

TEKI: The moon is in the repair box.

FINAL IMAGE: The puppet king sits upright in the lobby with his crown safely pinned on, while his labeled strings lead openly to a wooden handle held by the smiling stagehand.
