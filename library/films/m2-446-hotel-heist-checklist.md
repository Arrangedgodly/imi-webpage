# The Hotel Heist Checklist

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: luxury-hotel caper films that rely on layered disguises and timed distractions while ignoring basic guest services*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A crew plans to steal a hotel chandelier's crystal centerpiece during a gala, but their elaborate disguises keep sending them to the front desk for help.

Cast: Planner Niva, a macaque; locksmith Soro, a capuchin; lookout Peli, a gibbon; concierge Varo, a lemur; housekeeper Dena, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. HOTEL SERVICE CORRIDOR - EVENING

Niva unrolls a floor plan on a laundry cart. Soro wears a bellhop uniform several sizes too large. Peli has a fake mustache and carries a bouquet.

NIVA: At nine, the orchestra starts. At nine-oh-five, we cross the ballroom. At nine-ten, we take the crystal centerpiece and exit through the service lift.

SORO: The service lift is out of order.

NIVA: The plan says it works.

DENA: The sign says it has been out of order since Tuesday.

Peli turns the sign around to hide it. The lift doors open an inch, then stop.

PELI: The sign is correct.

SCENE 2 - INT. HOTEL LOBBY - MOMENTS LATER

Soro tries to enter the ballroom with a tray. The concierge stops him.

VARO: Catering staff use the south entrance.

SORO: I am a guest with a tray.

VARO: Then you can use the front entrance.

Niva walks past wearing a feathered hat and carrying a room key. Varo greets her by name.

VARO: Welcome back, Ms. Niva. Your room is ready.

NIVA: I have never been here.

VARO: Your reservation says otherwise.

The plan requires a distraction. Peli drops the bouquet. The flowers scatter across the marble. Dena brings a broom. Guests help pick them up, creating a neat line toward the ballroom.

SCENE 2A - INT. HOTEL LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

Niva checks the clock. It is eight fifty-nine. She tries to lead the crew toward a side door, but the feathered hat catches on a hanging plant.

NIVA: The disguise is compromised.

VARO: It is a very wide hat. The ballroom has a coat room.

Niva removes it and hands it over. The concierge tags it and gives her a number.

NIVA: Now we have a receipt.

SORO: That is better than the plan.

Peli walks toward the south entrance, still holding the bouquet. The flowers hide the door sign. Dena gently turns the bouquet so Peli can read the label.

DENA: That entrance is for staff and deliveries. Guests use the main hall.

PELI: We are disguised as staff.

DENA: Your mustache is on the floor.

Peli looks down. The mustache is indeed missing. She places the bouquet on the lobby table and retrieves the mustache. The clock strikes nine.

SCENE 3 - INT. BALLROOM - NINE O'CLOCK

The orchestra begins. Niva, Soro and Peli enter under their disguises. A waiter asks Soro to deliver the tray to table six. Soro loses track of the chandelier and follows the tray instead.

NIVA: The centerpiece is above the dance floor.

SORO: This tray has soup.

PELI: The distraction is working.

A guest asks Peli to hold a coat. Peli accepts it, then becomes the coat-check attendant by default. Niva climbs onto a chair to reach the chandelier. The chair is part of a table setting and slides sideways.

DENA: Please step down. That chair is reserved.

SCENE 4 - INT. BALLROOM SERVICE ALCOVE - LATER

The crew hides behind a curtain. Niva checks the plan and discovers that the centerpiece is not a crystal. It is an acrylic ornament installed for the gala. The real crystal pieces are stored in a locked display cabinet.

NIVA: We planned a theft of a copy.

SORO: The copy is lighter.

PELI: The timing is still good.

DENA: You can ask the concierge to see the display. It is part of the hotel tour.

NIVA: That would reveal our disguises.

VARO: I can see your fake mustache on the floor.

Peli picks it up. Varo offers them a regular guest tour badge and directs them to the display cabinet.

SCENE 5 - INT. HOTEL DISPLAY HALL - NIGHT

The crew joins the tour. Varo explains the crystal's history. Soro studies the cabinet lock, then notices that the key is hanging behind the desk with a large tag: TOUR GUIDE.

SORO: We could still take it.

NIVA: We could also leave it where it belongs.

The gala ends. Dena returns the borrowed tray to the kitchen and checks the ballroom chairs. Peli returns the guest badge to the concierge. Niva removes the feathered hat and places it in the costume rental box.

VARO: Would you like to reserve a room?

NIVA: No. We came for the chandelier.

VARO: The chandelier is part of the building.

FINAL IMAGE: The crystal centerpiece glitters safely above the empty ballroom while the crew leaves through the front doors carrying one bouquet and a tray ticket for soup.
