# The Whiteout at Beargrass Ridge

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: alpine disaster epics, heroic rope teams, aerial weather maps, and a summit rescue delayed by a misplaced thermos*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
KITO, a macaque trail guide
SHEA, a capuchin climber
PON, a gibbon drone operator
MARA, a ring-tailed lemur cook

SCENE 1. EXT. BEARGRASS RIDGE BASE CAMP - DAWN

A mountain fills the frame. A forecast map predicts clear skies, then immediately covers the ridge with a red cloud. Kito checks the guide rope. Shea tightens her climbing harness. Pon launches a drone with a tiny camera.

PON: The summit is twelve minutes away by drone.

KITO: And four hours away by feet.

MARA: The thermos is missing.

The climbers freeze. The soundtrack swells as if the thermos contains the rescue beacon. Mara points to the cooking tent, where a second thermos sits under a pile of mittens.

SCENE 2. EXT. LOWER SLOPE - LATER

The team climbs in formation. Kito leads, Shea follows, Pon tracks the route from a tablet, and Mara carries lunch. The wind begins to blow sideways. The drone's map turns upside down, making the ridge appear to descend into the sky.

PON: The route is beneath us.

KITO: The ground is beneath us.

PON: Not on this screen.

Shea reaches a rock ledge. A gust blows her hat toward the summit. Mara catches it with a ladle from the lunch pack.

SHEA: You brought a ladle?

MARA: We may need to stir the weather.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIDGE SHELTER - NOON

The group ducks into a small shelter. The whiteout obscures the trail. Pon's drone lands on the roof, guided by its own return signal. The team tries to use the aerial camera, but the snow fills the lens. Kito ties the rope around a fixed post. Shea counts the visible trail markers.

KITO: We wait until the markers return.

SHEA: The weather report said the cloud would clear by noon.

MARA: The weather has not read the report.

A loud clank comes from outside. The team assumes a rockfall. It is the drone bumping the shelter's metal roof.

SCENE 4. EXT. RIDGE - AFTERNOON

A gust lifts the shelter's door and sends the lunch pack sliding downhill. Shea clips onto the rope and braces it. Kito secures the pack. The thermos rolls out and stops at the next marker. Pon crawls after it, moving slowly enough to stay clipped in.

PON: The drone can retrieve it.

KITO: The drone is on the roof.

PON: I can retrieve the drone first.

Mara extends the ladle and hooks the thermos handle. Pon clips the thermos to the rope, and the team hauls it back hand over hand. Inside is hot soup; the lid has stayed closed.

SCENE 5. EXT. RIDGE SHELTER - LATE AFTERNOON

The cloud thins. Kito studies the actual ridge, not the tablet. The summit is closer than the drone's upside-down map suggested, but the team has lost daylight. Shea proposes turning back; Pon says the drone can reach the top. Kito decides the group will descend together and try again another day.

PON: The summit will still be there.

KITO: That is the first useful thing the map has said.

They follow the rope down, stopping at each marker. Mara carries the thermos, now tied to her pack. The drone follows on foot in Pon's hands.

SCENE 6. EXT. BASE CAMP - EVENING

The team returns safely. Mara pours the soup into cups. Shea pins her rescued hat to the camp notice board as a "summit flag." Pon repairs the drone camera and finally turns the map right side up. Kito marks the route by hand, adding the shelter and its loud roof.

SCENE 7. INT. BASE CAMP TENT - NIGHT

Pon reviews the drone footage. Most of it is white snow, one glove, and an upside-down image of the ridge. The team identifies the useful frames by the thermos rolling past a marker. Kito redraws the trail from the visible points; Shea adds the place where the hat flew away.

MARA: Do we need the thermos marked on the map?

KITO: It was the best landmark we had.

PON: The drone made it look like the summit.

Shea pins the hat beside the route. The wind eases outside, and the team hears the shelter roof stop clanging. Mara packs the thermos for tomorrow's lower trail.

SCENE 8. EXT. LOWER RIDGE TRAIL - NEXT MORNING

Kito leads the group on a short practice walk below the whiteout line. They check each marker in daylight and place a bright ribbon on the shelter roof so the drone can identify it. Pon launches the repaired drone, which immediately photographs its own shadow.

SHEA: Is the ridge in frame?

PON: The ridge is behind the shadow.

MARA: Then we know where the sun is.

The team returns to camp with every rope coiled and the thermos still in Mara's pack. Kito folds the corrected route into the map case. Shea writes "bring a spare hat" beside the weather symbol.

FINAL IMAGE: The forecast screen clears to a perfect blue sky, and Mara places the empty thermos directly over the summit icon.
