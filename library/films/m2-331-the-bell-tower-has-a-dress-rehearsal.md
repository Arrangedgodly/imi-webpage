# The Bell Tower Has a Dress Rehearsal

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sweeping historical romances, mistaken identities, balcony confessions, and letters that arrive at the worst possible moment*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
LINA, a ring-tailed lemur clock repairer
MILO, a capuchin theater usher
DUCHESS PIP, a macaque civic patron
CAPTAIN ORRO, an orangutan ferry pilot
SABLE, a gibbon costume keeper

SCENE 1. EXT. BELL TOWER COURTYARD - SUNSET

The old bell tower overlooks a river ferry terminal. Lina climbs a ladder to inspect the clock. Milo waits below holding a sealed letter that has been sent to the wrong person twice. He has written three names on the envelope and crossed out each one with a neat usher's pencil. A brass bell gives one small, apologetic clang.

MILO: The letter says it is urgent.

LINA: The clock says it is early.

MILO: They may both be right.

Milo looks up, sees Lina, and forgets his speech. He offers the letter. A breeze lifts it from his hand and catches it on the ladder rung. Lina retrieves it, but a smear of grease covers the address.

SCENE 2. INT. TOWER MECHANISM ROOM - EVENING

Lina points to a festival ribbon tangled in the gears.

LINA: Someone has decorated the escapement. It stopped the clock.
 Milo tries to help, but he is dressed for his usher shift in a dramatic velvet coat. He holds a lantern while Lina extracts the ribbon. The mechanism moves; the clock chimes twelve times at half past five.

MILO: It has made a decision.

LINA: It has made a noise.

Duchess Pip enters with Sable, seeking the letter's intended recipient. Lina hands Pip the letter. Pip opens it and reads the ferry closure notice. The name Captain Orro has run into a theatrical loop. Pip mistakes Milo's uniform for an officer's coat and assumes the message is for him.

DUCHESS PIP: You must be the captain.

MILO: I guide people to their seats.

DUCHESS PIP: A captain guides people somewhere.

Sable drapes a captain's sash over Milo's usher coat.

SABLE: The promenade needs one more lead.

PIP: Deliver your letter at the festival. Bring the lady from the balcony.

LINA: This is a ladder. I have a wrench.


SCENE 3. EXT. FESTIVAL PROMENADE - NIGHT

Milo and Lina cross the courtyard while the orchestra plays a grand love theme. The music keeps pausing whenever a vendor cart blocks their path. MILO: Do you believe in fate?

LINA: Loose bolts. I believe in tightening them. A cart rolls downhill; Lina catches it with her foot before it reaches the river stairs.

The letter slips into a basket of costume accessories. Sable finds it and reads the first line aloud: "Captain, your route will be suspended." PIP: Your heart will be suspended! Orchestra, give us longing!

SABLE: I said route.

The orchestra swells anyway. Milo takes the letter back and explains it is a ferry notice.

MILO: We should give it to the actual captain.

LINA: Before the ferry closes.

They run toward the dock. The orchestra follows on a small wagon; its wheels squeak in the rhythm of the love theme.

SCENE 4. EXT. FERRY DOCK - LATER

Captain Orro is loading repair tools. Milo hands him the letter. Orro confirms the crossing will pause for one day while workers replace a damaged ramp. He has already arranged a footbridge for festival visitors. Pip arrives and insists the letter must contain a secret declaration because everyone has crossed town to deliver it.

ORRO: It is a schedule.

PIP: Schedules can change lives.

Lina shows Orro the clock ribbon. It was printed with the ferry route, and its loose end has tangled the tower gears. The same ribbon came from a dockside bunting display. Orro says the crew can remove the rest of it before morning. Milo admits he came to the tower every evening because the clock chime signals his ferry home.

MILO: I thought the sound meant the day was ending.

LINA: It means the clock needs repair.

He asks if she will join him on the new footbridge after her shift. She says yes, but only after he learns to hold a lantern steady.

SCENE 5. INT. TOWER MECHANISM ROOM - DAWN

Lina and Milo finish the repair. The clock strikes exactly six, on time. Pip arrives with the orchestra, ready for a final confession. Instead she hears the correct chime and smiles. Sable removes the borrowed captain's sash from Milo's usher coat.

The ferry stays closed for the scheduled ramp repair. Orro waves from the work deck. Lina and Milo walk across the footbridge, carrying the loose ribbon to the recycling bin. Pip signals the orchestra to follow. Their wagon cannot pass the bridge posts. The love theme stops halfway through its opening chord.

MILO: Do we wait for them?

LINA: We have been waiting all night.

They continue across. Sable takes one violin from the stranded wagon and follows on foot, playing the rest of the phrase.

FINAL IMAGE: The tower clock moves to the next minute, while Lina and Milo's shadows meet on the bridge before they do.
