# The Cable Car Escape That Stayed in the Station

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action films where the hero treats a safety inspection like a jailbreak*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
OPERATOR MARA, a macaque
PASSENGER VEX, an orangutan
MECHANIC PIP, a capuchin
DISPATCHER ODO, a gibbon
INSPECTOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. CABLE CAR STATION - DAY

A cable car sits at the platform with doors open. Vex looks at the locked control panel.

VEX: They have sealed the escape route.

MARA: The car is waiting for its scheduled inspection.

PIP: The door sensor is working. It won't close while anyone stands in the threshold.

NARI: The inspection takes three minutes. Please wait behind the yellow mark.

VEX: The clock is ticking.

ODO: The clock tells us when the next car departs.

SCENE 2. INT. STATION - CONTINUOUS

Pip tests the door sensor with a padded pole. The doors stop and reopen.

VEX: The prison has detected our plan.

MARA: The sensor has detected an obstacle. Nobody is trapped.

NARI: The platform is clear now. Run the door test again.

Pip clears the threshold. Doors close, then open on command.

PIP: Test passed. Control panel stays locked during passenger boarding.

SCENE 2A. INT. CABLE CAR STATION - DAY

Nari places an inspection tag on the control panel. Mara reads the checklist aloud while Pip checks each item.

MARA: Door sensor. Emergency brake. Communications button. All tests happen while the car is stationary.

VEX: The station is performing a ritual.

NARI: It is a safety inspection. The tag shows the date and inspector.

ODO: Dispatch confirms the line is clear. The next car remains at the platform until the inspection ends.

PIP: Door sensor test passed. Emergency brake indicator is green. Call button reaches the operator.

VEX: We could escape through the maintenance ladder.

MARA: The ladder is locked because no repair is underway. Stay inside the passenger area.

Vex steps toward the yellow line. Mara gestures back to the waiting mark. He returns and sits on the bench.

SCENE 2B. INT. CABLE CAR STATION - CONTINUOUS

A child drops a mitten onto the platform. Pip picks it up and hands it to Nari, who returns it to the family.

VEX: A coded message.

NARI: A mitten. The family is still here.

ODO: All passengers are accounted for. Platform is clear again.

MARA: The doors can close now. We remain at the station until dispatch signals departure.

The signal lamp turns green. Mara checks the panel, then gives Pip a nod.

MARA: Ready to depart.

PIP: Doors closed. Sensor clear.

Vex watches the station without touching the controls.

VEX: The escape may proceed.

MARA: The scheduled ride may proceed.

SCENE 2C. INT. CABLE CAR STATION - CONTINUOUS

The inspection tag is signed. Nari gives the control panel a final look before allowing boarding.

NARI: Tag is complete. Car number matches the dispatch sheet.

ODO: Dispatch authorizes one trip to the summit station. Weather at the top is clear.

PIP: Doors open. Please board in order and keep bags inside the line.

VEX: The car accepts passengers.

MARA: The car is an elevator on a cable. Sit down and enjoy the ride.

The doors close. Vex grabs the seat, then notices that the car is still stationary.

VEX: Have we escaped?

ODO: Departure signal has not arrived.

A green lamp lights. Mara starts the car at its normal speed. The station slides away beneath them.

MARA: Now we are moving.

SCENE 2D. INT. CABLE CAR - CONTINUOUS

The car moves along the cable. Pip watches the door indicator while Vex watches the station drop away.

PIP: Door sensor is green. Car is on the line.

VEX: The ground has retreated.

MARA: The car is traveling uphill. Keep your feet inside the marked area.

ODO, over speaker: Summit station reports clear platform and light wind.

NARI: We can unload when the car is docked and the doors are open.

VEX: No midair escape?

MARA: No midair anything. Stay seated.

Vex sits back. The car reaches the summit at its posted speed.

SCENE 3. INT. CABLE CAR - LATER

Passengers board in order. Vex presses the call button, which chimes at the operator station.

MARA: That button calls the operator. It does not release the doors.

VEX: A cry for help.

ODO: I hear you. The doors are open and the car is stationary.

VEX: Can we depart before the inspection ends?

NARI: Inspection is complete. Car is cleared.

SCENE 4. INT. CABLE CAR - DAY

The car moves up the line at normal speed. Vex peers through the window.

VEX: We have escaped the station.

PIP: We left on the scheduled car.

MARA: Please remain seated until the platform.

TAG: Vex sees the station below and waves as if fleeing a fortress. The operator waves back from the marked platform.
