# The Pigeon Protocol

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: spy thrillers, gadget briefings, and implausibly elaborate covert plans*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A capuchin courier is recruited to recover a stolen rooftop weather vane, but the agency's gadgets create more public attention than the theft.

Cast: Vee, a capuchin messenger; Director Bristle, a mandrill spymaster; Dot, a macaque engineer; Agent Plume, a pigeon operative; and Mr. Swoop, a crow fence.

SCENE 1. INT. AGENCY LAUNDRY ROOM, NIGHT

BRISTLE: Can you identify the suspect?

VEE: The one with a beak?

BRISTLE: Too obvious.

DOT: It is literally a crow.

Director Bristle summons Vee to a room full of dryers. A red laser traces a map across a hanging sheet. It is Dot's laser pointer, aimed by a cat outside. Bristle calls it the agency's projection system. The mission: retrieve the brass weather vane from Mr. Swoop before dawn. The vane points toward wind; Bristle says it points toward betrayal.

BRISTLE: The thief left no fingerprints.

VEE: It's a bird statue.

BRISTLE: Exactly. A professional.

Dot issues Vee a briefcase. It contains a periscope, a listening cup, and a banana-shaped transmitter that only works when peeled. Vee asks why a spy transmitter needs peeling. Dot says the antenna is under the skin. Bristle insists on a code name: Soft Landing. Vee is assigned the hard-shell briefcase.

SCENE 2. EXT. ROOFTOP MARKET, NIGHT

VEE: The periscope is backward.

PLUME: No, the target is facing the wrong way.

VEE: Targets do not face directions.

PLUME: Mine do.

Vee and Plume survey the target roof. Plume wears sunglasses though it is dark. The crow's crew unloads the weather vane into a delivery cart. Vee uses the periscope; it is mounted backward, so he sees Dot waving from the agency roof. He waves back. The crow notices and waves too, beginning an unplanned chain of greetings across the market.

PLUME: We have been made.

VEE: We have been polite.

Dot activates the banana transmitter. It emits a loud peel-shaped ringtone. Every pigeon on the block descends toward Vee's briefcase. Plume argues this is excellent cover; the flock forms a moving wall that also blocks the alley. Vee offers the birds a crumb from his pocket. They follow him instead of the crow.

SCENE 3. INT. CROW'S STORAGE SHED, LATER

SWOOP: Are you here to inspect the roof?

VEE: Yes.

SWOOP: Then why are you wearing gloves?

VEE: It is a very sensitive roof.

Vee slips into the shed wearing a delivery cap. He presents a clipboard that says ROUTINE INSPECTION. Mr. Swoop asks what is being inspected. Vee looks at the ceiling and says the roof. The crow gestures toward three obvious holes. Vee is handed a ladder.

While Vee patches one hole, Plume distracts Swoop with a fake intelligence briefing. Plume whispers that the agency knows about the crow's secret collection of shiny bottle caps. Swoop denies it while standing beside an entire wall of bottle caps. He is offended that Plume called them a collection instead of an archive.

The weather vane stands on a crate. Dot's gadget, a magnetic glove, sticks Vee's hand to the crate. He cannot lift the vane, but he can drag the crate with him. It scrapes across the shed floor, waking the crew. Vee claims the noise is a ventilation test. Everyone looks up at the holes.

SCENE 4. EXT. ROOFTOP MARKET, PRE-DAWN

PLUME: The plan is working.

VEE: The plan is airborne.

PLUME: That is a kind of working.

The chase is staged across awnings. Swoop escapes with the vane while Vee, Plume, and the pigeon flock pursue him. Dot's pocket smoke capsule releases a puff so small that it resembles a sneeze. Vee sneezes for real; the flock takes off and lifts the lightweight vane by its ribbon. It sails toward the town hall.

BRISTLE (over radio): Retrieve the objective with discretion.

VEE: The objective is flying over a bakery.

The mayor sees the vane, mistakes it for a ceremonial parade float, and sends a marching band after it. Swoop tries to blend into the band, but his bottle-cap buttons jingle in time. Plume identifies him by rhythm. Vee catches the vane as it lands on the band leader's hat.

SCENE 5. INT. AGENCY LAUNDRY ROOM, MORNING

Dot demonstrates the transmitter on the table. Its alarm goes off when the banana is peeled, so Bristle orders everyone to stand at attention. Vee asks whether this test is meant to detect spies or fruit. Dot says she will revise the design after breakfast.

Vee returns the vane. Bristle declares the operation a success and asks for the debrief. Vee reports the transmitter attracted the flock, the glove trapped him to a crate, and the smoke capsule barely concealed a sneeze. Dot writes each failure on a board labeled FIELD TEST RESULTS. Bristle turns the board around; the reverse says CLASSIFIED SUCCESS.

Swoop is offered a deal: he can keep the bottle caps if he returns the stolen property. He agrees, but asks the agency to stop calling his archive a collection. Bristle marks the request classified.

Bristle tries to award Vee a medal, but the pin fastens his lapel to the mission map. Vee frees him with the magnetic glove, which sticks the medal to a filing cabinet.

DOT: Should I remove the sign?

VEE: Leave it.

BRISTLE: It is a security advisory.

FINAL IMAGE: The repaired weather vane is mounted above the agency. It spins wildly in a light breeze. Bristle calls it a security alarm; Vee hangs a sign beneath it reading WIND, NOT TREASON.
