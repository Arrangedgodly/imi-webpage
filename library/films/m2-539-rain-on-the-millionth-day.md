# Rain on the Millionth Day

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster melodramas about a town facing its first rain in years, with an overprepared mayor and a very ordinary gutter*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: MAYOR DELL, a baboon; ARI, a macaque farmer; BEE, a gibbon weather watcher; SOL, an orangutan roofer; NIMI, a capuchin child; PRESS HOST, a mandrill.

EXT. DRY TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

A painted line on a wall marks the last flood. It is dated one million days ago. The town is dry and dusty. Bee watches a cloud through a brass telescope.

BEE: Rain in ten minutes.

MAYOR DELL: We have prepared for this.

ARI: We have a bucket.

DELL: We have six buckets.

The press host arrives with a camera. They ask Dell to stand beneath the flood line. A gust blows the banner away. Nimi catches it.

NIMI: The rain is not here yet.

PRESS HOST: Can you catch it again for the report?

ACT ONE

EXT. TOWN ROOFTOPS - LATER

The first drops fall. Ari runs to cover a row of seedlings with a tarp. Sol climbs onto the town hall roof to clear the gutter. The mayor directs residents to put buckets beneath the downspouts.

DELL: Every bucket must be centered.

The gutter overflows at one corner. Water pours onto the town clock. Bee checks the drain and finds a bird nest blocking the downspout.

SOL: The nest is empty.

BEE: The birds moved before the rain.

The mayor orders a second bucket. It lands on a table and begins to fill. Press Host films the water running off the table and calls it a river.

PRESS HOST: The town is flooding.

ARI: It is a table.

Ari moves the bucket under the eave. The water now runs into it. Sol clears the drain with a long hook. A rush of water spills down the pipe and splashes the mayor.

DELL: The rain has breached the square.

NIMI: It reached your hat.

The rain keeps falling. The mayor asks the residents to move the six buckets to the roof gutters. Ari and Sol set up a barrel under the largest downspout. The first barrel fills too quickly and begins to tip.

SOL: The gutter is feeding it faster than we can drain it.

ARI: We need the overflow hose.

The town has one hose, used to water the dry garden. Nimi runs to the shed and returns with it. They connect the hose to a second barrel. Water flows into the storage tank instead of across the square. Bee reads the rain gauge. The storm is steady, not a flash flood, but the creek is rising.

BEE: We have enough time to move the seedlings.

The press host asks Mayor Dell whether the town is under emergency order. Dell points to the clear evacuation route and says the school hill remains open. The camera operator steps into the gutter stream and gets soaked.

PRESS HOST: The storm is moving in.

NIMI: It is moving down the pipe.

They carry the seedlings uphill while Sol checks the roof drain. The first barrel stops tipping. The town has a working overflow route now.

ACT TWO

The downpour grows heavier. The town's small creek starts to rise. Bee leads residents toward the school hill, not because the square is flooded but because the town has no measurements for a heavy storm. Ari carries the seedlings in trays. Sol checks the roof and confirms the gutter is open.

At the hill, the residents watch the creek. The water rises to the old mark, then stops. The mayor asks the press host to lower the camera and help carry a bucket.

PRESS HOST: I am reporting.

DELL: You can report from the hill.

The host carries a bucket. Nimi notices water spilling from it and tips it to empty the collected rain into a barrel. Ari's seedlings are damp but upright. The townspeople cheer when the rain eases.

BEE: We have enough water for the fields.

The mayor asks Ari to open the barrel valve slowly. The first channel runs toward the dry garden. Water reaches the seedlings and darkens the soil. The residents watch the shallow stream rather than the camera. Bee records the gauge reading on the town wall.

ARI: The rain is not a flood. It is a start.

Nimi places a small marker beside the barrel so the next storm will show how full it became.

ARI: We have enough to plant again.

The mayor checks the wall, then orders the flood line updated with today's mark. Sol makes the line lower than the million-day mark but higher than the creek reached. Press Host changes the headline from FLOOD to FIRST RAIN.

DELL: We prepared six buckets for one cloud.

NIMI: You used all six.

The town stores the barrels and leaves the downspout clear. The birds return to the empty nest, now dry beneath the roof.

FINAL IMAGE: A thin stream flows through the town gutter into a barrel as the new rain mark glistens on the wall.
