# The Swamp Creature Wants a Towel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: monster-feature films that interpret a creature's awkward arrival as an attack before asking what it needs*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A giant swamp creature emerges beside the town pool, but the panicked rescue team eventually discovers it only wants a dry towel and a way back to the marsh.

Cast: Morga, a giant amphibious primate; lifeguard Leni, a macaque; reporter Varo, a gibbon; marshal Tavi, a capuchin; biologist Oru, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - EXT. TOWN POOL - MORNING

The pool is empty before opening. A tall, muddy figure rises behind the diving board. Water splashes across the deck. Leni sounds the whistle and raises a red flag.

LENI: Everyone stay back. The pool is not open.

Morga lifts both hands. A long reed is stuck to one wrist. Varo arrives with a camera crew.

VARO: A creature has emerged from the depths.

ORU: This pool is four feet deep.

VARO: The creature may be hiding its full size.

Morga points toward a towel rack. There are no towels on it.

SCENE 2 - EXT. POOL GATE - MOMENTS LATER

Tavi places cones around the deck. Morga sits on a bench and drips muddy water onto the concrete. Leni brings a hose but does not turn it on.

TAVI: We need to secure the perimeter.

LENI: The perimeter is the pool fence. The gate is already closed.

VARO: The creature may be testing the boundary.

Morga points again to the empty towel rack, then rubs both arms. Oru watches the reeds stuck to Morga's shoulder.

ORU: She came from the marsh. She may be cold.

VARO: The camera needs a stronger angle. Can she roar?

Morga opens her mouth. A small frog hops out and lands in a cone.

SCENE 2A - INT. POOL OFFICE - MOMENTS LATER

Varo reviews his footage. The water splash looks enormous when zoomed in. He tells the camera crew to add a low rumble. The crew tests a speaker; it vibrates the empty towel rack.

LENI: Turn that off. The pool is closed, not under attack.

TAVI: The public alert is already queued. It says "unknown creature approaching the town center."

ORU: The creature is sitting by the pool gate. We can describe what is happening.

Varo sees Morga point to the towel rack on the monitor. He rewinds the footage. Morga points again.

VARO: She has been asking for something this whole time.

LENI: A towel.

Tavi cancels the alert and changes the message to "Pool closed briefly for a wildlife visitor. Please use the east gate." The speaker gives one short chime.

VARO: That sounds less dramatic than a warning siren.

ORU: It also tells people where to go.

SCENE 3 - EXT. POOL DECK - CONTINUOUS

Oru approaches from the side, keeping a clear path to the gate. He holds up a towel and waits. Morga looks at it, then at him. She reaches slowly. Leni lowers the red flag.

LENI: We can give her a towel, but keep the gate clear.

Tavi moves the cones. Varo continues filming, narrating over the scene.

VARO: The beast accepts our offering.

ORU: It is a towel from the lost property bin.

Morga wraps the towel around her shoulders. The reed falls off and lands beside the diving board. She points down the road toward the marsh.

SCENE 4 - EXT. MARSH TRAIL - LATER

The group escorts Morga along the public path. Oru identifies the route. Varo tries to keep the camera on Morga's face, but a branch blocks the lens. Tavi asks a park worker to hold the branch aside. A sign reads MARSH CLOSED FOR NESTING.

ORU: The side trail is open. We can take her around the nesting area.

VARO: The long route will add suspense.

LENI: It is the only route that avoids the closed marsh.

Morga walks carefully around a puddle, then hands the towel back to Leni. The towel is muddy now. Leni holds it at arm's length.

LENI: We will wash it before returning it to lost property.

SCENE 5 - EXT. MARSH EDGE - AFTERNOON

The open side path reaches the marsh. Morga steps into shallow water and turns back once. Oru waves. Varo lowers the camera. Tavi removes the remaining cones from the pool entrance.

VARO: We have no attack, no chase and no citywide evacuation.

ORU: We have a visitor who needed a towel and a safe path home.

Leni places a fresh towel on the pool rack for opening. Morga disappears into the reeds. Varo looks at his camera and changes the title card from THE SWAMP ATTACKS to THE MARSH VISITOR.

TAVI: That is a better description.

VARO: It may not sell as many tickets.

Oru points to the pool sign, which still reads CLOSED FOR CLEANING. The muddy footprints lead only to the gate.

FINAL IMAGE: A clean towel hangs on the pool rack as a single reed floats past the marsh path, leaving a tiny ripple beside the open gate.
