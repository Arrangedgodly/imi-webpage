# Ninety Seconds Until the Ending

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: short-film contests that impose arbitrary time limits, producing rushed twists and title cards mistaken for conclusions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A filmmaking pair races to make a ninety-second film, but the timer becomes the only character with a complete arc.

Cast: Pika, a macaque director; Umi, a lemur editor; Taro, a gorilla actor; and Bex, a capuchin contest volunteer.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY MEDIA ROOM, MORNING

Bex announces the contest rule: the film must be ninety seconds or less and include a door, a blue object, and the word "tomorrow." Pika sets a kitchen timer beside the camera. Umi writes the requirements on a whiteboard. Taro stands by a door holding a blue mug.

PIKA: We need a story.

TARO: I can enter a room.

UMI: That is a location.

Pika says the film should have a twist. Taro enters and announces he is leaving. Umi asks where he is going. Taro says through the door. Pika adds a close-up of the mug. Bex starts the timer. They have seventy seconds. Pika tells Taro to enter with urgency. Taro hurries through the door, then asks whether he should stop on the other side. Umi says the camera cannot see him there. He returns, starts again, and bumps the door with the blue mug. Bex marks down "door included."

SCENE 2. INT. MEDIA ROOM, LATER

Umi edits the first version. Taro enters, says "Tomorrow," and exits. Pika asks where the twist is. Umi reverses the footage. Taro now walks backward into the room and says "Tomorrow" backward. Bex says the word is no longer clear. Pika tries to put the word on a title card. Umi reminds him that the required word must be spoken. Taro says it normally, then again while holding the mug close to the lens. The mug covers his mouth.

TARO: Tomorrow.

PIKA: We need to see your face.

TARO: Then the mug has to move.

Umi adjusts the camera. The timer keeps running while they reposition the table and chair.

PIKA: We need a reveal.

TARO: The mug is blue.

UMI: We already know that.

Pika asks Taro to discover the mug is empty. Taro looks inside. The timer beeps. Bex stops it at ninety seconds. Pika says the contest film has ended before the story begins. Umi cuts the title card, saving six seconds. The new cut ends on a black screen with the words "COMING SOON."

BEX: That is not part of the contest.

SCENE 3. INT. MEDIA ROOM, AFTERNOON

The team tries a new ending. Taro enters, finds the blue mug, and sees a note beneath it: "Tomorrow, bring the mug back." Pika asks for an emotional reaction. Taro examines the note, then looks at the empty shelf.

TARO: Someone is expecting this back.

PIKA: Can you make it feel like a loss?

TARO: It is a mug.

Umi asks whether the note should be shown before or after he enters. Bex says the rules require the blue object to appear. Pika places the mug in front of the lens so the color is unmistakable. The rest of the room disappears behind it. Taro asks who wrote the note. Umi says it is from the room's previous user.

TARO: They want their mug.

PIKA: That is a twist?

BEX: It is a request.

Pika adds dramatic music. The music takes twelve seconds to reach the chorus. Umi cuts it. Taro starts to leave with the mug, then returns it to the shelf. The timer shows nine seconds left. Pika asks for a freeze-frame. Umi freezes Taro halfway through the turn, with one hand still on the mug. Pika wants a second freeze-frame of the timer. Bex says they cannot add more footage after the limit. Umi runs the cut again at full speed; it finishes at eighty-eight seconds.

PIKA: We have two seconds to spare.

TARO: The mug has returned.

BEX: And the film is under time.

Pika looks at the timer, then leaves the cut alone.

SCENE 4. INT. CONTEST ROOM, EVENING

The audience watches the film. It lasts eighty-eight seconds. At the end, the timer beeps on screen. Several viewers laugh. A judge asks if the timer is part of the story. Pika starts to answer, but the timer beeps again from the table. The judge checks the runtime and says it was ninety seconds on the dot with the opening card. Umi points out the contest clock starts after the title. Bex takes the timer off the table before the next screening. Umi says it is part of the contest. Taro says he liked the ending where the mug was returned.

Bex announces the result. The film receives a special mention for using the door, blue object, and required word. Pika asks if they can make a longer version. Umi says the story is already complete.

FINAL IMAGE: The mug sits on the shelf beside the timer. A note says "Tomorrow: clean up."
