# The Quietest Alarm

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: heist thrillers built around silent timing, elaborate disguises and security systems that mistake routine animals for intruders*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A team sneaks into a closed museum to retrieve a seed collection, only to discover the alarm is activated by every normal movement in the building.

Cast: Nara, a macaque archivist; Pito, a capuchin locksmith; Miri, a gibbon climber; and Sal, a night-shift tortoise guard.

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM HALL, NIGHT

The museum is dark. A glass case holds a seed packet labeled "Community planting stock." Nara, Pito and Miri enter through a side window. Pito signals for silence. His tool belt jingles.

PITO: I will remove the alarm wire.

A light flashes. Sal appears from behind a display.

SAL: The wire is decorative. The floor mat is the alarm.

Nara steps off the mat. The light stops flashing.

SCENE 2. INT. MUSEUM AISLE, NIGHT

The team moves across the stone floor, avoiding the mat. Miri climbs the wall ledge to reach the case from above. A painted sign says NO CLIMBING. She stops, then lowers herself carefully onto the display's wooden frame.

NARA: We came for the seeds.

MIRI: I am standing beside them.

Pito checks the case lock. The case is unlocked but sealed with a paper inventory strip. He can lift the lid without breaking the strip if he loosens two clips.

SAL: The strip tells me if someone opened it.

PITO: What if you open it?

SAL: I write my name on the strip.

Nara examines the label and sees the seed packet belongs to a public planting day. She asks Sal why it is in the museum after hours.

SAL: The storage cabinet leaked. This case is dry.

SCENE 3. INT. STORAGE ROOM, LATER

Sal leads them to a dry cabinet. They must move a stack of boxes to reach the seeds. Each box has a fragile label. Pito lifts one, but the weight shifts and a small bell rings. A light flashes in the hall.

PITO: The museum has heard us.

SAL: It heard the bell.

Nara proposes moving one box at a time onto the bench. Miri passes them down from the shelf. The crew stacks the boxes by label, leaving a clear walkway. The seed packet is at the back, safe and dry.

SCENE 4. INT. MUSEUM HALL, BEFORE DAWN

They return the packet to the public case with a new note explaining the leak and temporary storage. Sal uses the inventory strip and writes his name across the seal. Pito watches the door for signs of morning staff. Miri is already outside, holding the door so no one steps on the alarm mat.

NARA: We have completed a quiet retrieval.

PITO: The bell made a sound.

SAL: And the floor mat worked exactly as designed.

SCENE 4A. INT. SERVICE CORRIDOR, BEFORE DAWN

A cleaning cart blocks the corridor. Sal says the staff arrives soon and the cart must stay where it is. Nara checks the side passage, but it is narrower than the seed packet case. Miri measures the space with her arm and suggests carrying the packet alone while the others move the cart only after Sal approves.

SAL: I can move the cart. I have the key.

PITO: We brought three tools.

SAL: None of them is the key.

Sal unlocks the cart's brake and rolls it aside. They wait until the wheels stop before moving the packet through.

SCENE 4B. INT. MUSEUM ENTRY, DAWN

The guard checks the log, the inventory strip and the case latch. Nothing is damaged. Nara adds a note to the public sign that the planting stock has been moved to a dry shelf during the leak. Pito wants to add a dramatic warning. Sal gives him the incident form. He writes "Watch your step" beside the floor mat and signs his name.

Nara double-checks the case latch and confirms the community planting date is still posted on the door. Miri reads the sign aloud so Sal can record it correctly. The guard notes the cart's new position and checks that the alarm mat is clear. Pito adjusts the strap on his tool belt so it will not jingle on the next visit.

SCENE 5. EXT. MUSEUM STEPS, DAWN

The crew carries the packet to the community garden. Sal locks the museum and files an incident report: "Three visitors helped move stored seeds. No alarm breach; one bell rung." Pito insists the report should call it a caper. Nara hands him a watering can.

NARA: You can write the label for the planting row.

PITO: What is the security protocol?

SAL: Do not step on the mat.

The three walk toward the garden. Pito tiptoes across the public sidewalk, just in case.
