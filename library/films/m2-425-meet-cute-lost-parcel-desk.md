# Meet Cute at the Lost Parcel Desk

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic comedies that mistake a routine mishap for fate and stretch a simple misunderstanding into a third-act separation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Two monkeys meet while retrieving mislabeled parcels, then let a romantic comedy soundtrack turn a practical correction into an unnecessary near-breakup.

Cast: Sumi, a macaque courier; Rafi, a capuchin printmaker; Goro, the parcel clerk, a gibbon; Fenn, a lemur musician; Ada, a baboon supervisor.

SCENE 1 - INT. PARCEL DEPOT - MORNING

Sumi and Rafi reach for the same padded envelope on the counter. Their paws bump. A violin swells from Fenn's phone in the waiting area.

SUMI: Sorry. I was collecting a parcel for the print shop.

RAFI: I'm returning one. It has my neighbor's name on it.

GORO: It is addressed to Sumi at the east delivery route.

SUMI: That's me. This is my ink order.

RAFI: My neighbor is also named Sumi. She ordered a kettle.

Goro compares labels. The two envelopes have been swapped at the sorting belt.

GORO: These went to the wrong bins. We can exchange them after I verify the route slips.

Sumi and Rafi hold their envelopes while Fenn raises his phone again.

FENN: The music says you should look at each other now.

SUMI: The music is from your phone.

RAFI: And we are holding envelopes.

Fenn taps stop. The swelling violin ends on an awkward electronic chirp.

SCENE 2 - INT. PARCEL DEPOT, SORTING FLOOR - LATER

Ada brings the route slips. Sumi's parcel is marked EAST SHOP, while the kettle is marked EAST COURT. Goro had placed both in EAST by mistake.

ADA: The labels are similar, but the route codes differ. We will re-sort by code.

RAFI: My neighbor has been waiting for that kettle.

SUMI: My ink is for a poster due today.

GORO: The belt is stopped. Let's check the bins by hand.

Rafi volunteers to help. Sumi gives him a pair of gloves. They inspect the east tray together. Fenn tries to cue another romantic song, but it is a jaunty spy theme.

SUMI: That is the wrong soundtrack.

FENN: My playlist says "destined meeting." It may be an old version.

ADA: Please keep the phone clear of the parcel labels.

Fenn lowers the phone. Rafi discovers the kettle envelope under a stack of posters.

RAFI: Found it. And this one is your ink shipment.

SUMI: The envelopes came together, but they are going to different addresses.

RAFI: I could walk the kettle to my neighbor after my shift.

SUMI: I can take the ink to the shop. We are headed opposite ways.

SCENE 3 - EXT. DEPOT ENTRANCE - AFTERNOON

Sumi and Rafi leave with their correct parcels. Rain begins. Rafi opens a large umbrella. Sumi stands under the awning and checks the label one final time.

RAFI: The print shop is three blocks west. I can walk you there.

SUMI: You need to deliver the kettle east.

RAFI: My neighbor can wait ten minutes.

SUMI: The poster job cannot. I have to dry the ink before the rain.

Fenn follows carrying a portable speaker. He plays a soft piano cue as Rafi offers the umbrella.

SUMI: Stop the music, Fenn.

Fenn stops it. The rain becomes audible on the umbrella fabric.

RAFI: I can take the west route tomorrow. My print studio is near your shop.

SUMI: Then we can meet tomorrow with the correct parcels already delivered.

RAFI: I would like that.

SCENE 4 - INT. PRINT SHOP - NEXT DAY

Sumi rolls out a poster proof. Rafi arrives with his neighbor's signed delivery receipt. Fenn waits by the doorway with another playlist.

FENN: I have a song called "The One Who Came Back." Should I start it?

SUMI: No. We are both here on purpose.

Rafi puts the signed receipt on the counter. The poster has the two route labels printed across its top. Sumi notices and laughs.

RAFI: Your printer turned our parcel mix-up into a design.

SUMI: The labels are backwards.

She flips the proof. The text is still reversed.

Rafi turns the sheet toward himself, then turns it back. They rotate it together until the words read correctly.

SCENE 5 - EXT. PRINT SHOP - EVENING

Sumi and Rafi step outside. Fenn starts the spy theme again by mistake. He shuts it off before the first beat.

RAFI: Would you like to get tea?

SUMI: Yes. We can take the straight route. No wrong bins, no dramatic separation.

FENN: I have a gentle tea-house melody.

SUMI: You can walk with us quietly.

Fenn pockets his phone. Sumi and Rafi carry their respective work bags, not each other's parcels.

FINAL IMAGE: Their poster hangs in the shop window, correctly oriented, while the two walk toward the tea stall beneath one umbrella held level between them.
