# The Split Screen That Separated the Wrong Twins

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: split-screen plotting that multiplies misunderstandings by keeping characters visually apart after they meet*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
EDITOR ODO, a baboon
DIRECTOR NIMA, a lemur
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR SURI, a macaque
PRODUCER VEX, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING SUITE - MORNING

Editor Odo places two shots side by side. Pip's character searches a train station for a red suitcase. Suri's character holds the red suitcase in the same station, behind a pillar. Director Nima asks Odo to cut to their meeting. Producer Vex prefers the split screen.

VEX: Keep them apart. The audience is waiting for the collision.

NIMA: They are standing ten feet apart.

ODO: On opposite sides of the frame, they look far away.

PIP: I can see Suri in the shot.

VEX: Your character cannot.

Suri turns toward the camera. Pip waves. Vex asks them not to acknowledge each other until the center divider disappears.

SCENE 2. INT. TRAIN STATION SET - LATER

The actors perform their sides separately. Pip asks a ticket clerk if anyone has seen the suitcase. Suri answers from the other half of the set, though the clerk cannot hear her. Odo edits their dialogue together.

PIP: I am looking for a red case.

SURI: It is right here.

PIP: Did you hear that?

SURI: I said it is right here.

Nima joins the set and asks the actors to stand in the same space. Vex says the split-screen design requires half-frame separation. Odo places a strip of black tape down the center floor. Pip and Suri stand on either side, facing each other. They can still touch the suitcase between them, but Vex asks them to keep their hands in their respective frames.

SCENE 3. INT. TRAIN STATION SET - AFTERNOON

The train arrival is staged as another split screen. On the left, Pip sees a train door open. On the right, Suri sees the same door open from the other angle. They both step toward it, but the edit makes it look like separate trains. Odo adds a ticking clock. Nima says the station has no clock in the scene. Vex adds it anyway, then asks for a second clock on the other half.

ODO: They are going to miss the same train twice.

VEX: It is a very punctual train.

The actors walk toward the center. Their halves meet, but the black divider remains over the suitcase handle. Pip reaches for it; Suri reaches from the other side. The handle seems to vanish between them. Odo adjusts the matte, but the suitcase now floats between two hands.

SCENE 4. INT. EDITING SUITE - EVENING

Nima asks Odo to remove the divider when the characters finally speak face to face. The split screen continues for one more beat, showing both actors reacting to the same sentence. Vex says the audience will miss the structure if it ends too early. Pip suggests they make the meeting the payoff: both characters discover they have been searching for the same suitcase because they packed matching red cases.

SURI: Mine has a yellow tag.

PIP: Mine has a blue tag.

They compare tags. The cases are identical except for the ribbons, which were hidden by the divider. Nima lets the image merge into one frame as they exchange cases. Vex asks for the split screen to return during the handshake. Odo keeps the single frame. The scene finally feels like two people sharing a station rather than two televisions.


Odo checks the audio tracks. Pip's lines are on the left channel and Suri's on the right, so the first screening preview makes each character sound as if they are speaking from a separate room. Nima asks him to center their voices as soon as the divider lifts. Vex wants the sound split to continue through the handshake. The actors try it; Pip hears only one half of Suri's sentence and shakes the wrong case. They repeat the take with both voices together.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The audience watches the search in split screen, then the frames join when Pip and Suri compare tags. The red cases are exchanged; both actors leave through the same door. Vex asks whether the sequence can have an alternate cut with more division. Nima says the feature is complete. Odo saves the split-screen setup as a project file rather than an ending.

Pip and Suri exit the theater together. Vex stands in the doorway, blocking the center. The guests split around him into two streams. He asks Odo to film it. Nima tells him to move so the audience can go home. He steps aside, and the two lines merge at the lobby.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty screen shows one frame, while Vex's two stacks of survey cards sit side by side.
