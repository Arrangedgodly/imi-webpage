# The Silent Film Star Has One Sound Effect

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent-film melodramas whose intertitle speeches and live foley turn tiny gestures into monumental events*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A silent-film star rehearses a grand farewell while the foley artist has only one coconut, one sheet of thunder metal, and a very limited budget.

Cast: star Vela, a macaque; director Odo, an orangutan; foley artist Nix, a capuchin; pianist Rulu, a gibbon; camera operator Sava, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. STUDIO SET - DAY

A painted cliff stands behind a small platform. Vela faces the camera, one hand pressed to her heart. Odo holds up an intertitle: "FAREWELL, MY BELOVED!" Nix sits beside a table of sound props: two coconuts, a paper bag, and a sheet of metal.

Odo points to the cliff. Vela steps forward, turns to the lens and raises her hand. Nix strikes the thunder sheet. It makes a soft metallic ping. Rulu plays a dramatic chord. Sava cranks the camera.

SCENE 2 - INT. STUDIO SET - MOMENTS LATER

Odo reviews the take. He wants the star's final step to sound like a collapsing mountain. Nix uses the coconuts for hoofbeats, but they sound like a horse crossing a kitchen floor. Vela performs the step again, more slowly. Nix bangs the coconuts together. One cracks.

NIX holds up the broken shell. Odo shows an intertitle: "THE MOUNTAIN TREMBLES." Nix points to the cracked coconut and then the platform.

Odo asks Vela to fall. She drops onto a padded mat. Nix shakes the paper bag for gravel. It sounds like a snack wrapper. Rulu softens the piano accompaniment. Odo looks at the bag, takes a handful of paper confetti from it, and sprinkles it around the mat.

SCENE 3 - INT. STUDIO SET - CONTINUOUS

The crew tries a close-up. Vela's eyes widen. Nix strikes the metal sheet too early, startling her. The camera keeps turning. Odo holds up another card: "THE STORM HAS STRUCK." Nix points at the lighting stand, where a fan has blown the paper bag open.

Vela gathers the loose paper and places it back in the bag. She then steps onto the platform, which creaks audibly. Nix looks at the platform, then taps the floor with one coconut. The sound is deeper than the thunder sheet. Odo signals for another take.

Odo asks for a second close-up. Vela repeats the hand-to-heart gesture. The camera operator cranks too slowly, making the gesture last a full minute on screen. Nix tries the broken coconut, but the halves do not meet. He taps the intact one against the padded floor instead. Odo shows the intertitle "A THUNDEROUS GOODBYE." Vela reads it, points to the tiny foley table and then points to the exit. The crew laughs silently. Odo changes the card to "GOODBYE." Rulu removes three extra bars from the score, and the take becomes brisk.

SCENE 4 - INT. STUDIO SET - LATER

The final take begins. Rulu plays the farewell theme. Vela slowly lifts her hand, turns toward the painted cliff and steps off the platform onto the mat. Nix taps the floor with the coconut at the exact moment her heel lands. The impact is a modest, convincing thud. Vela falls gently onto the mat, and a few paper scraps drift down like rock dust.

Odo watches the scene on the small projection screen. The shot works. He holds up the intertitle: "THE MOUNTAIN FALLS." Vela reads it and gestures toward the stable wooden platform. Odo crosses out the words and writes "SHE LEAVES." The simpler card fits in one line.

SCENE 5 - INT. STUDIO SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The crew watches the finished reel. The audience laughs at the coconut thud and the tiny paper storm. Vela bows. Nix demonstrates the sound again, tapping the floor with the unbroken coconut. The first cracked shell rolls off the foley table and lands in a metal pail with a loud clang.

Odo freezes the frame and writes a new intertitle: "THE SOUND DEPARTMENT RETURNS." Nix retrieves the shell and places it beside the other props. Rulu plays a short closing chord.

At the screening, the audience watches Vela read the short intertitle and leave the frame. Nix provides a single quiet footstep with the coconut. The projected image ends. Odo waits for the crowd to react before rolling the next title card. One spectator claps early; the rest follow, mistaking the pause for the ending. Rulu stops the piano, and Sava turns off the projector. Vela returns to the room through the side wall, bowing to the seated audience as if emerging from the cliff.

FINAL IMAGE: Vela exits the painted cliff set through its visible side wall, while the foley table displays one coconut, a paper bag, and the thunder sheet marked GENTLE USE.
