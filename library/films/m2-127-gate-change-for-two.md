# Gate Change for Two

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic comedies that use airport sprints, missed departures, and grand public declarations to replace ordinary honest conversation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: Two monkeys race through an airport to stop a departure, only to discover the confession they need to make can happen before boarding.

Cast: Mina, a macaque travel writer; Ralo, a lemur sound editor; Juno, a gorilla gate agent; and Pip, a capuchin shuttle driver.

SCENE 1. INT. AIRPORT CAFE, MORNING

Mina and Ralo sit at a small table. Mina has accepted a writing job in another city. Ralo has been editing her travel column and wants to tell her he will miss their daily walks. He begins, then the airport loudspeaker announces a gate change.

MINA: You can finish your sentence.

RALO: I should get to my gate.

MINA: Then say it while walking.

Ralo grabs his bag. Mina follows him through the cafe. They pass a wall clock. Ralo checks his ticket; his flight leaves in two hours. Mina says there is time to talk. Ralo takes out his phone to check the departure board. Mina asks him to put it away long enough to finish one thought. He does, but the board updates behind her with a flashing message. Ralo glances at it anyway.

MINA: That message is for everyone.

RALO: I thought it was our cue.

Mina points to the cafe chairs. Ralo sits for three seconds, then stands again when the airport chime sounds. The speaker announces a gate change, not a boarding call. He says a film like this would already have a sprint.

SCENE 2. INT. TERMINAL CORRIDOR, LATER

Pip drives an electric shuttle along a marked route. Ralo runs beside it, and Mina walks at a normal pace behind him. Pip slows down so they can talk. A moving walkway begins beside them.

RALO: I thought if I waited for the right moment, it would be easier.

MINA: The right moment is usually a chair and enough time.

The walkway carries Ralo forward while Mina stays behind. Pip asks if he should stop. Ralo steps off, turns around, and goes back to her. An airport announcement calls the wrong gate number. A crowd heads toward the far end of the terminal. Mina and Ralo follow the crowd, then see the gate sign has been updated.

JUNO: Gate changed to twelve. Please read the screens.

Pip points at the screen directly above them. Ralo tries to apologize for running ahead. Mina says the airport is not a test of how fast he can get to her. He asks if she wants him to stay nearby. She says she wants him to listen when she says the gate has changed.

RALO: I am listening.

MINA: You are looking at the screen.

Ralo turns toward her and misses the next update. Juno taps the display with a pen and reads it aloud. The new gate is only one row away. The crowd reverses direction. Ralo starts running again, but Mina catches the strap of his bag.

MINA: You have an hour.

RALO: The music would be building by now.

PIP: The shuttle has a slow setting.

SCENE 3. INT. GATE TWELVE, AFTERNOON

Mina and Ralo reach the gate. The plane is still boarding. Ralo asks Juno if the gate can hold departure for a declaration. Juno asks if there is a medical emergency. Ralo says no. Juno says then they should not block the line.

Mina sets her bag down. She tells Ralo the job is only for a season and she will be back to visit. Ralo says he wanted to ask her to keep editing his work when she is away. Mina says she can do that, but he has to send it before midnight instead of at dawn.

RALO: I can do that.

MINA: You said that last month.

RALO: I can do it after dinner.

She smiles. Juno scans the next passenger's boarding pass and waits while the conversation resolves. Pip asks whether anyone wants to make a grand announcement over the public address. Juno points to the sign that says "For emergency use only." Ralo reaches for the phone, then puts it back in his pocket. No one runs.

SCENE 4. INT. AIRPORT CAFE, EVENING

The flight is delayed by weather. Mina and Ralo return to the cafe. Pip parks the shuttle. Juno joins them on a break. They share a table and open the draft column. Ralo reads a sentence about a city that looks better from the window.

JUNO: You could describe the place after you land.

MINA: That would make it a travel story.

PIP: Or you could get a snack before the storm.

Ralo orders two sandwiches. The gate changes again, but Juno points to the screen and everyone checks it before moving. They walk together at a steady pace.

FINAL IMAGE: The cafe table holds four boarding passes and a shared plate of toast. The airport loudspeaker announces the correct gate, and nobody sprints.
