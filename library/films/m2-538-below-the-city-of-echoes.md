# Below the City of Echoes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: underground adventure films with collapsing tunnels, misleading echoes, and explorers who must listen before choosing a route*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: SENA, a gibbon cartographer; DUM, a baboon miner; KEL, an orangutan engineer; NEO, a macaque apprentice; ORI, a capuchin historian.

INT. OLD STORM TUNNEL - DAY

A crew enters a tunnel beneath the city. Their lanterns illuminate carved marks on the wall. A distant knock answers every footstep.

DUM: The city is under us.

ORI: It is a drainage tunnel.

SENA: The map says the old bell chamber is ahead.

KEL: The map is from before the tunnel was sealed.

The group hears three knocks from the left passage. Neo moves toward it. Sena raises a hand.

SENA: Wait for the echo to finish.

Another three knocks answer from behind them.

NEO: We are being followed.

ORI: That is the first knock returning.

ACT ONE

INT. TUNNEL FORK - LATER

The team reaches a fork. One branch slopes upward, the other descends toward water. Sena checks the map, but the ink has faded. Kel taps each wall with a small hammer. The left tunnel returns a sharp sound; the right one gives a dull thump.

KEL: The left passage is solid rock. The right has a hollow wall.

DUM: The hollow wall is the old chamber.

SENA: Or a blocked drain.

Ori reads an inscription near the fork: "Ring the bell where the air turns." The team lights a small candle. Its flame bends toward the lower passage.

NEO: The air is moving down.

KEL: The bell chamber had a vent.

They descend carefully. Water trickles over the floor. A loose stone rolls past Neo's boot and disappears into a narrow crack. Dum tries to move a fallen beam, but it shifts and drops dust from the ceiling.

SENA: Do not lift it yet.

DUM: It is between us and the chamber.

KEL: The beam is holding the ceiling. We need a support first.

The crew uses two metal braces from the tool cart. Kel wedges them beneath the beam. Sena and Neo clear stones one at a time. An echo begins to rumble from the chamber.

ORI: That is not the bell.

A crack opens across the floor. Water begins to rise through it. The crew backs toward the fork. Neo spots an iron wheel on the wall, half hidden beneath moss.

Before they reach the wheel, the lower tunnel fills with a rush of water. The crew climbs onto a narrow stone shelf. A second set of echoes comes from the wall ahead, louder than the first.

DUM: The chamber is close.

ORI: The water is closer.

Sena holds the candle near the crack. The flame bends toward the wall, then goes out. Neo finds a small air vent overhead and taps the metal cover. The sound answers from behind the blocked beam.

NEO: The chamber is on the other side.

Kel tests the wall with the hammer. One section gives a hollow sound. He does not strike again. Instead, he finds the old sluice wheel carved into the stone, with a notch marking the closed position.

SENA: The water gate is blocking the passage.

DUM: Then we open it.

KEL: The pressure is holding the gate shut. We open it too fast and the water comes through here.

Ori finds the inscription's lower line under moss: "Open the high drain before the bell." The group returns to the fork. They use the iron wheel to open the upper drain first, letting the water fall away in a narrow stream. Only then do they turn the lower gate.

ACT TWO

Kel turns the wheel. A sluice gate opens, draining the rising water into an old culvert. The echo fades. The team returns to the blocked beam and braces it more firmly. Dum and Kel lift together while Sena pulls the loose stones aside. A narrow doorway appears.

Inside is the bell chamber, small and dry. A bronze handbell hangs from a hook. Ori checks the inscription and finds a date carved beneath it.

ORI: This marked the first flood-warning system in the city.

Sena rings the bell once. The sound travels through the tunnel and comes back from the upward passage, clear and even.

NEO: We found the bell.

DUM: And the route back.

The team carries the bell to the surface in a padded case. The city archivist meets them at the storm drain entrance. Kel records the location of the sluice wheel so maintenance crews can reach it. Sena updates the map with the water gate and the solid-rock passage.

FINAL IMAGE: The bell hangs in the city museum beside a map that marks the echoing tunnel in red.
