# The Missing Third Act

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: self-important mystery films, where the editor loses the final reel and the cast has to end the story live*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A village screening stops before the mystery is solved, so the actors improvise the missing ending while the projector operator searches for the reel.

Cast: Director Nara, macaque; projectionist Odo, baboon; actor Pavi, capuchin; actor Luma, lemur.

SCENE 1. INT. VILLAGE HALL, NIGHT

The screen shows a detective at a locked garden gate. The reel runs out and the picture goes blank.

[SFX: PROJECTOR CLICKS.]

ODO: The reel has ended.

NARA: That was act two.

PAVI: The detective has not found the key.

LUMA: The gardener is still missing.

ODO: There is no third reel in the case.

NARA: The mystery cannot end here.

PAVI: It could end with everyone going home.

NARA: That is not an ending.

The audience waits. Odo turns on the hall lights. Nara asks him to search the projection booth. He says he has already checked the case twice.

A child in the front row asks whether the detective caught the gardener. Nara does not know. Pavi asks the audience to remember the clues: the damp path, the missing key, and the open shed window.

PAVI: Which clue matters?

LUMA: The garden needed water.

PAVI: That is not a clue. That is gardening.

LUMA: The film is about a garden.

NARA: We should have noticed that earlier.

Odo checks the film case once more. It is empty, but a strip of tape is stuck to the bottom. Nara sends him to search the projection booth.

SCENE 2. INT. BACKSTAGE, MOMENTS LATER

Nara tells Pavi and Luma to perform the ending live. Pavi objects that they do not know the culprit.

PAVI: I play the detective. I only know the clues.

LUMA: I play the gardener. I know where I went.

NARA: Why did you go?

LUMA: The script does not say.

NARA: Then we have found the mystery.

They return to the stage. Pavi holds a magnifying glass. He examines the locked gate, then the audience.

PAVI: The culprit is in this room.

LUMA: That is a theater.

PAVI: It could be the theater.

The audience laughs. Nara signals them to continue.

SCENE 3. INT. STAGE, CONTINUOUS

Pavi asks Luma where she was at sunset. She says she went to water the garden. He asks why the gate was locked.

LUMA: I had the key.

PAVI: Then why was the gate locked?

LUMA: I locked it.

PAVI: Why?

LUMA: The goats were getting in.

Nara whispers that the gardener is not missing. Luma says she was in the shed, fixing the latch.

PAVI: The mystery has become a repair.

NARA: Keep going.

Pavi finds a key in his pocket. It is the key prop from the first act.

PAVI: I have had the key all along.

LUMA: You borrowed it to open the evidence cupboard.

PAVI: Then I am the culprit.

LUMA: You are the detective.

PAVI: The detective has uncovered himself.

The audience applauds. Nara says the missing reel may have a different ending. Odo calls from the booth that he found a small strip of film under the projector.

ODO: It is a clue shot, not the ending.

NARA: Can you show them?

ODO: The film is upside down.

SCENE 4. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, CONTINUOUS

Odo rewinds the strip. It shows the gardener carrying a watering can toward the shed, then the image ends. Nara asks the actors to freeze on stage while he projects it.

[SFX: PROJECTOR RUNS.]

The screen shows Luma carrying the can to the shed. Then the film stops again.

LUMA: That is where I went.

PAVI: The film has supplied a clue, not an ending.

NARA: We still need the last act.

PAVI: And the audience still needs their ending.

LUMA: You are standing in front of the screen.

Pavi turns to face the audience and bows. The screen goes dark behind him.

SCENE 5. INT. VILLAGE HALL, LATER

Before the audience can leave, Odo finds the missing reel inside an empty popcorn bowl. It is intact but damp from a spilled drink.

ODO: It cannot go through the projector.

NARA: What is on it?

ODO: I can see the label. "Act Three."

NARA: We cannot project it wet.

PAVI: Then we will perform what the frames show.

Odo holds the damp reel up to the light. The final frames are legible: Pavi takes the key from the evidence cupboard, then opens the gate for Luma, the gardener, carrying her water can.

LUMA: I was watering the seedlings.

PAVI: The detective accuses the gardener, then opens the gate.

NARA: Play it.

Nara cues the actors. Pavi unlocks the gate and Luma enters with the water can. Pavi holds up his magnifying glass to the audience as she waters the stage flowers. The audience applauds the ending the reel could not show.

PAVI: The detective solved it.

LUMA: The gardener was not missing.

NARA: The audience has the ending. We will dry the reel before the next show.

Odo hangs the reel in loose loops on the drying rack, out of the projector's reach.

[SFX: BOX LID CLOSES, BLACKOUT.]
