# One Voice, Four Mouths

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: overdubbing scenes, mismatched lip movements, and voice actors asked to make every line sound profound*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: Four performers try to record one line for a scene, but every voice is paired with the wrong mouth and the director keeps asking for greater gravitas.

Cast: Nara, a lemur voice actor; Boro, a gorilla screen actor; Venn, a macaque director; and Puck, a capuchin sound editor.

SCENE 1. INT. RECORDING BOOTH, MORNING

Boro appears on a monitor, opening a box. Nara reads the line: "This is not what I expected." Her voice is soft and clear. Venn asks for a more dramatic take. Nara tries one with a whisper, one with a shout, and one as if announcing a train delay. Boro silently mouths "platform two" on the monitor. Puck records each take under the same filename, and the soundboard now shows three identical labels.

NARA: What is the character expecting?

VENN: The audience will infer it.

Puck syncs the line. Nara's voice begins before Boro's mouth moves. Boro's mouth continues after the line ends. Puck pauses the video. Venn asks Nara to record the line with a smile that cannot be heard. She asks what the character sees in the box. Venn says the box is emotionally complicated. Boro turns it upside down on the monitor; a spoon drops out.

BORO: It is a kitchen box.

VENN: The spoon can remain off camera.

NARA: Then what is the line reacting to?

Venn turns the script around, as if a note might be printed on the back. Puck marks the take "early mouth, late meaning".

SCENE 2. INT. RECORDING BOOTH, LATER

Nara tries the line at three speeds. The slow version lands after Boro closes the box. The fast version sounds like a sports announcement. Venn asks for "a quieter kind of thunder." Nara asks what that means.

Venn replays the shot. Boro opens the box, sees a cup, and blinks. Nara asks if the line could be "It's a cup." Venn says the script requires the original line. Puck checks the cut and finds the editor stretched the shot by two seconds to accommodate a sweeping camera move.

Puck removes the unnecessary pause. Nara reads the line at normal speed. It now matches Boro's mouth. Puck loops the picture and sound. The line lands; Boro's next mouth movement stays open. Venn asks Nara to provide an extra breath for the gap. Nara supplies a tiny gasp. Boro on screen is visibly chewing.

NARA: Is he eating?

PUCK: The set has crackers.

Venn requests a second take with more mystery. Nara gives the line while Puck plays a single low cello note. The note continues after the scene cuts to a slate. Venn asks her to add wonder. Nara says the character expected a letter, not a cup.

SCENE 3. INT. SOUND MIX ROOM, AFTERNOON

The crew tests the line in the full mix. The orchestra swells over Nara's voice. Puck lowers the music. A second voice appears under the line: a scratch track recorded by Venn. Nara points at the soundboard.

NARA: Is that the character or the director?

VENN: It was a guide.

Puck mutes the scratch track. The line is clear. Venn asks if the room tone can sound more like a mountain cave. Puck adds a hollow echo. Nara's voice returns from the speakers three times, each copy more solemn. Boro's small nod lands beneath the last echo like a reluctant bow.

BORO: I was just asking for the cup.

Puck removes the echo. Venn asks to keep one reflection. Nara points at the waveform, where the same sentence has become a row of identical hills. Venn asks if they can keep both voices for a layered effect. Nara says the character has one mouth. Boro appears on a video call and asks if the box is supposed to contain a cup. Venn checks the script. It says "old cup."

Boro asks where the letter went. Puck finds it on the prop table. The scene had been cut before the reveal. Venn reads the next page and realizes the line belongs after the letter, not after the cup. They move the voice recording to the correct scene.

SCENE 4. INT. RECORDING BOOTH, EVENING

Nara records the line once more. The picture shows Boro opening the box and finding the letter. His mouth moves; her voice lands in sync.

NARA: This is not what I expected.

Venn does not ask for thunder. Puck plays the scene. Boro nods, then asks if he can keep the cup. The crew returns it to props and labels the box.

FINAL IMAGE: The credits list Nara's voice and Boro's performance on separate lines. The cup sits beside the correctly labeled letter.
