# Gate Changes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: airport ensemble melodramas, coincidence chains, and last-minute reconciliations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: During a storm delay at a small airport, a reunion-bound baboon, a cake courier, and a manager with two identical suitcases keep missing one another by a gate until the baggage cart becomes the only traveler who knows where it is going.

Cast: Lolo, a langur gate agent; Fern, a baboon traveling to a reunion; Kit, a capuchin courier carrying a cake; Mr. Doss, a tortoise airport manager; Sol, a quiet macaque cart driver; and Fern's brother, heard on a crackling phone.

SCENE 1. INT. REGIONAL AIRPORT, MORNING

Rain lashes the windows. A flight board flashes GATE THREE, then GATE FIVE. Lolo speaks into the PA. Fern follows a stranger wearing a red scarf.

LOLO: Flight 18 to Cedar Bay now boards at Gate Five.

FERN: I thought Cedar Bay was at Three.

LOLO: It was. The plane has moved.

FERN: The plane moved?

LOLO: The gate moved. The plane is still outside.

The stranger turns. He is not Fern's brother. Kit arrives carrying a cake box and asks where the reunion flight boards. The PA blares over Lolo's answer. Kit places the box on a baggage cart while he checks his ticket. Sol pushes the cart away.

KIT: My cake!

SOL: I thought it was baggage.

KIT: It has frosting. Baggage doesn't have frosting.

Sol looks at the cart, now carrying six identical suitcases and one cake.

SCENE 2. INT. BAGGAGE CLAIM, LATER

Fern claims a blue-ribbon suitcase. Kit claims another. They open them. One holds sweaters; the other holds jars of jam. Mr. Doss arrives and admits both tags have his name because the printer duplicated a sheet.

FERN: Which one is yours?

DOSS: I was hoping the contents would tell me.

KIT: Is this your jam?

DOSS: I don't remember buying jam.

A jar slides inside the case. Doss catches it without looking. Lolo enters with a list and reads the destination labels aloud. Neither bag belongs to Fern or Kit. Sol rolls past with the cart.

SOL: The cart has seen both suitcases at every gate.

DOSS: That's because I've been following it.

LOLO: Then you know where your luggage is.

DOSS: I know where the cart is.

SCENE 3. INT. FOOD COURT, AFTERNOON

The storm grounds departures. Kit opens the cake box. The icing has shifted, leaving HAPPY R TIREMENT. A child adds a tiny airplane with a plastic fork. Fern calls her brother. The signal crackles.

FERN: Are you at Gate Five?

BROTHER (PHONE): I'm at the gate with the red sign.

FERN: They're all red.

Lolo points out the arrival hall has a red sign too. Fern gets up. The PA announces a gate change, and every seated passenger stands at once. Doss stays seated, protecting the jam.

DOSS: Nobody panic. I am in charge.

LOLO: Of the jam?

DOSS: At this moment, yes.

Fern recognizes the reunion town on Doss's conference badge. They compare itineraries: her brother's flight is arriving, not departing. She has been waiting at the wrong end of the airport. Lolo checks the arrivals board; the flight is due at Gate Two.

SCENE 4. INT. ARRIVAL GATE TWO, EVENING

They reach Gate Two. It is closed for cleaning. Lolo tries the door. A cleaner points to the public viewing window across the hall. Fern looks through it and sees her brother holding a red scarf and a small bouquet.

FERN: That's him.

KIT: The scarf is the same color as the warning sign.

FERN: I know his face.

LOLO: That helps more this time.

The door opens. Fern crosses the hall. Her brother sees her, takes one step forward, then is stopped by an airport employee asking to check his bag. He has brought a suitcase containing a cake stand. Kit recognizes it from the cart and retrieves his own cake before it can be loaded onto the wrong belt. Doss identifies his luggage by finding a jam jar in one pocket.

SCENE 5. INT. DEPARTURE HALL, NIGHT

The storm eases. The departure board settles, then goes blank. Lolo grabs a marker and writes each gate on a paper sign. Passengers line up to ask her where they should go. Sol parks the cart beside the information desk and takes a break.

Fern reunites with her brother. Kit delivers the cake, whose imperfect message now reads HAPPY RETIREMENT with the airplane drawing covering the missing letters. The brother says it is the most accurate airport cake he has seen. Doss returns the extra suitcase to its owner and keeps the jam as a finder fee.

FINAL IMAGE: Lolo hangs a sign reading ASK HERE. Sol turns the baggage cart toward the desk; three passengers follow it, assuming it knows the gate. This time, it does.
