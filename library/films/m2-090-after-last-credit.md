# After the Last Credit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: franchise films that attach a sequel tease to every ending, including stories that are already finished*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A projectionist tries to end a neighborhood film night, but the short's credit sequence keeps teasing crises that turn out to be ordinary follow-up tasks.

Cast: Vee, a lemur projectionist; Odo, a gorilla director; Miri, a macaque editor; Puck, a capuchin audience member; and Custodian Toma, a tortoise park custodian.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, NIGHT

Odo's film, "The Bench," ends with neighbors repairing a park bench and sitting together. The audience applauds. Vee starts the credits. A post-credit shot shows a loose screw rolling toward the sidewalk.

PUCK: Is that the sequel?

MIRI: It is a screw.

ODO: The next film will reveal who dropped it.

VEE: The hall must be cleared before closing.

ODO: One more teaser.

Odo rewinds to capture a close-up of the screw rolling. Miri says the shot is from the wrong side of the bench and shows no one dropping it. Odo wants to add a low rumble. Vee plays the sound check: the rumble is a ventilation fan. Puck asks whether the bench is still safe.

TOMA (OVER PHONE): That screw is from the old display prop, not the repaired bench. The new fasteners are in place.

ODO: The reveal belongs in the next film.

MIRI: The inventory lists the screw as a spare.

ODO: Can we hold that back from the audience?

VEE: The theater has no secrecy policy for props.

The crew returns the screw to its labeled container.

SCENE 2. INT. CINEMA LOBBY, MOMENTS LATER

The audience leaves. Odo films a second tag: the neighbors find a toolbox with a mysterious label.

MIRI: Is the label actually mysterious?

The label reads PROPERTY OF PARKS DEPARTMENT.

ODO: The next installment will uncover its owner.

Puck takes the toolbox to the parks desk.

TOMA: Did you find the screwdriver inside?

PUCK: Yes.

Toma gives it to the maintenance crew. Odo films the handoff.

ODO: The Tool Awakens.

VEE: The cinema closes in ten minutes.

Odo writes three more sequel titles on the whiteboard.

MIRI: Each depends on a task already completed.

ODO: Audiences enjoy an expanding universe.

Puck reads the titles: "Screw of Destiny," "Toolbox: Origins," and "The Map Beyond." He asks whether the films have plots. Odo says each ends with a question. Miri suggests the question should be answered in the same film. Odo objects that it would remove the suspense. Vee says suspense can come from whether the projectionist finishes before closing. She begins switching off the lobby lights.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, LATER

Miri shows Odo the edit. The first tag turns the loose screw into a threat. The second turns a labeled toolbox into a mystery. The third reveals a park map with a coffee stain. Odo proposes that the stain marks an ancient boundary. Vee says the map is upside down. Puck turns it around and finds the park entrance clearly labeled.

ODO: Which teaser do you want?

PUCK: I want the credits to finish so I can get home.

TOMA: Has anyone seen the bench?

They look outside; it is still in place and the new screw holds.

TOMA: The park staff logged the repair. We will check the bench in the morning.

ODO: Can the log be a secret clue?

MIRI: It is available to the public.

PUCK: I like a story where maintenance already knows what happened.

Odo watches the final still of the bench. It holds two people and a paper bag of snacks, none of whom are looking toward a shadow.

Miri offers a single end card listing the park maintenance contact and the next community screening.

ODO: It is not cinematic.

VEE: The audience already watched the story end.

Odo considers this, then lets Miri replace the three tags with one still image of the bench.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK, NIGHT

The crew walks to the park to check the bench. Toma tests the seat and tightens the screw.

ODO: Film that shadow. It could be a future villain.

PUCK: It is a tree branch moving in the wind.

ODO: An unresolved mystery.

MIRI: What was the film about?

ODO: Neighbors fixed a bench and used it together.

TOMA: It is ready for morning visitors.

VEE: Anything else to film?

The group looks around. The park is quiet.

Odo puts the camera away.

ODO: Close the toolbox and return the key to the parks desk.

PUCK: This is not a teaser.

ODO: It is the ending.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, LATER

Miri exports the final cut.

ODO: Should the end card hint at a sequel?

VEE: The community screening date is enough information.

Odo changes the card to include the screening time and a note thanking the park crew. Puck checks that the credits list Toma by name. Toma sees it and nods, then leaves to lock the storage shed.

FINAL IMAGE: At the next screening, the credits roll uninterrupted. The audience stays through the final card, then leaves together. In the empty theater, Vee turns off the projector and closes the doors.
