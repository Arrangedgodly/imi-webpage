# The Great Soup-Off

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cooking competition films, hostile judge theatrics, and impossible secret ingredients*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: Two rival monkey cooks enter a televised soup contest, but the judge keeps demanding secret ingredients until the kitchen runs out of ingredients and the contestants start comparing their recipes.

Cast: Chef Mina, an orangutan who cooks by smell; Chef Rollo, a capuchin who measures everything; Judge Brisket, a rhinoceros food critic; Host Pip, a squirrel; and Aunt Fen, the market vendor who supplied the vegetables.

SCENE 1. INT. TV KITCHEN, MORNING

Mina lays out vegetables. Rollo aligns measuring spoons. Host Pip introduces the contest as "the most intense soup battle in television history." One burner will not light.

PIP: Chefs, your secret ingredient is revealed in three, two. 

BRISKET: Mustard.

MINA: In soup?

ROLLO: How much?

BRISKET: Secret.

SCENE 2. INT. KITCHEN, CONTINUOUS

The chefs begin. Mina sautés onions. Rollo measures water by the milliliter. Brisket circles the stations, asking for more drama. Pip urges them to glare at one another. Mina and Rollo compare chopping techniques.

Rollo's scale breaks. Mina lends him a bowl. The host says they are forming an alliance. Brisket calls it collusion. Aunt Fen arrives with a crate of carrots, which the show had forgotten to provide.

SCENE 3. INT. PANTRY, LATER

The secret ingredients keep changing. Brisket announces cinnamon, then lemon, then "something that remembers the sea." Rollo searches the pantry. Mina asks Aunt Fen what grows near the shore. She says kelp.

They add a small strip of dried kelp to one pot. Rollo's soup tastes like seawater. Mina's is balanced. Brisket asks them to swap pots to increase suspense. They refuse.

SCENE 4. INT. TASTING STAGE, AFTERNOON

Brisket tastes both soups with a silver spoon. He makes a face and says one lacks passion. Mina asks what passion tastes like. He says it has a bold finish. Rollo asks whether the finish is salt. Brisket says the judges must remain mysterious.

Aunt Fen tastes the soup and says the carrots are old. The contestants adjust the seasoning. Their soups improve. Brisket complains that they have solved the problem too efficiently.

SCENE 4A. INT. BACK KITCHEN, CONTINUOUS

Mina and Rollo discover the television crew has placed both soup pots under the same heat lamp. The lamp has turned the top layer into a skin. Brisket insists this proves the contest is intense.

MINA: It proves the lamp is too hot.

ROLLO: I measured it.

PIP: We can reshoot the tasting.

AUNT FEN: You can't reshoot the soup.

The contestants stir the pots and add broth. The soup recovers. Brisket requests a blind taste. Pip puts numbers on the bowls, but he forgets which number belongs to which cook. Brisket tastes both and declares the first one his favorite. He has tasted the same soup twice.

Rollo recognizes his own recipe by the dill. Mina says she used dill too. The two cooks compare notes and realize both recipes came from Aunt Fen's market stall. The "rivalry" was mostly the host's dramatic music and one broken burner. Pip asks the crew to turn off the music. Without it, Brisket's tasting face looks less fierce and more confused.

SCENE 5. INT. TV KITCHEN, FINAL

The judges announce a tie. They award the trophy to the host for "best dramatic reveal." Pip says he did not cook. Brisket says television is a different kitchen.

SCENE 6. INT. TASTING STAGE, CONTINUOUS

Pip announces the judges' vote. Brisket holds up two identical cards.

PIP: We have a tie.

MINA: What is the prize?

BRISKET: A trophy.

ROLLO: What is inside?

BRISKET: Nothing.

PIP: It can hold spoons.

BRISKET: This is a serious contest.

MINA: The soup is warm.

ROLLO: It is ready.

BRISKET: Where is the rivalry?

MINA: We shared a bowl.

ROLLO: We shared a scale.

PIP: That sounds dramatic.

BRISKET: Did anyone sabotage a pot?

MINA: The burner failed.

ROLLO: It failed both of us.

BRISKET: Did anyone hide an ingredient?

AUNT FEN: The pantry was empty.

PIP: We have a twist.

AUNT FEN: The twist is vegetables.

BRISKET: Can we call them secret?

MINA: Everyone saw them.

ROLLO: I measured them.

BRISKET: What if the judges disagree?

PIP: We have one judge.

BRISKET: I can disagree with myself.

MINA: Please don't.

ROLLO: We brought extra soup.

PIP: That's generous.

BRISKET: Is generosity permitted?

AUNT FEN: It's lunch.

MINA: Serve the crew.

ROLLO: Use clean bowls.

BRISKET: I will judge the second bowl.

PIP: You already voted.

BRISKET: I can reconsider.

AUNT FEN: You can eat.

Brisket takes a second helping. He says it needs no secret ingredient. Pip asks if that is his final verdict. Brisket answers with a full mouth.

FINAL IMAGE: Mina and Rollo serve their soups to the crew. The trophy sits on the counter, filled with spoons. Brisket returns for seconds and asks whether the bowl has a secret ingredient. Aunt Fen says, "Vegetables."
