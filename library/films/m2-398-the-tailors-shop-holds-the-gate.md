# The Tailor's Shop Holds the Gate

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: siege movies, royal defenses, and battle plans that overlook the useful people repairing the uniforms*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MIRA, a gibbon tailor
CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque guard
TAVI, a capuchin apprentice
NARI, a lemur messenger
THE GENERAL, an orangutan

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN TAILOR SHOP - DAWN

A bell rings beyond the city wall. Captain Vex rushes into the tailor shop wearing a torn uniform.

VEX: The gate is under siege.

MIRA: Your sleeve is under a chair.

TAVI: I can mend it.

VEX: We need a battle plan.

Mira cuts a strip of cloth. The General enters with a map covered in arrows. He says the invaders are at the east gate. Nari arrives with a message that the gate is still closed.

GENERAL: They have surrounded the wall.

MIRA: Then they cannot be at the gate.

VEX: The wall has more than one gate.

Mira asks where the guards are. Vex says they were called to the west tower. Tavi lays the uniform flat. A missing button rolls beneath the map.

SCENE 2. INT. TAILOR SHOP - MORNING

The General spreads the map across the cutting table. It covers Mira's scissors. She asks him to move it. He says the map shows every possible attack. Mira points to a chalk line on the cloth.

MIRA: This is the seam.

GENERAL: It looks like a road.

TAVI: The map is upside down.

The General turns it around. The arrows now point toward the town. Nari says the messenger has a second message. The enemy wants to negotiate.

VEX: I thought they were attacking.

NARI: They are waiting at the wall.

The General asks why the message was not delivered first. Nari says she was stopped at the outer gate, where the guard mistook the sealed letter for a surrender notice. The General reaches for it. A loose thread from Vex's uniform on the cutting table catches on the wax seal and pulls it open.

GENERAL: We have read the negotiation.

VEX: The sleeve has negotiated.

Mira takes the letter and smooths it flat. The enemy requests a bridge key and promises to return it before sunset. The General says they should answer with a counteroffer. Mira tears a narrow strip of cloth from the repaired cuff and ties it to the letter as a marker.

MIRA: Send it back before the thread comes loose.

Mira hands Vex his repaired uniform. The sleeve is longer now, with a bright patch.

VEX: I cannot go into battle wearing this.

MIRA: You can move your arm.

Vex lifts it. The patch catches the map, pulling the whole paper onto the floor.

SCENE 3. EXT. EAST WALL - NOON

The General arranges guards along the wall. Vex carries the message. Nari points out the enemy's banner. It reads "REQUEST TO BORROW THE BRIDGE."

GENERAL: They have come to take the town.

NARI: They need to cross the river. The bridge is ours.

MIRA: If they cannot cross, why are we fighting?

The General asks for a ladder. Tavi brings a measuring stick from the shop. It is too short to reach the wall. Vex climbs the stairs instead and looks down. The enemy commander raises a white cloth.

VEX: They are waving a surrender flag.

NARI: It says "Please send the bridge key."

The General says no one will surrender the key. Mira arrives with a box of buttons. She needs the guards to hold still so she can finish sewing their uniforms.

VEX: The town is under siege.

MIRA: The button is missing.

She finds it on the wall, stuck to the enemy banner. The commander has accidentally carried it over. Nari calls down and asks for the button back. The commander tosses it. It lands in the moat.

SCENE 4. INT. GATEHOUSE - AFTERNOON

Mira takes Vex's spare button from his coat. She sews it on while he stands at the gate. The guards grow impatient. The General insists they keep formation. Tavi opens the gate a crack to retrieve the bridge key. A gust blows the map through the opening and into the moat.

GENERAL: The plan!

NARI: It was a map.

Mira finishes the repair. Vex's sleeve moves freely. The guards lower their spears. The enemy commander offers a roll of cloth in exchange for borrowing the bridge. Mira says she can make a banner from it.

SCENE 5. EXT. BRIDGE - SUNSET

The two sides meet at the bridge. The town lends the key and keeps the gate. Mira stitches a temporary banner while the General counts the guards. Vex stands at the entrance in his repaired uniform.

VEX: We held the gate.

MIRA: You held the sleeve.

Tavi attaches the banner. It reads "BRIDGE KEY RETURNED BY DUSK." The enemy crosses, then brings the key back before sunset.

FINAL IMAGE: The General hangs the soggy map on the tailor's wall as a pattern. Mira cuts a new uniform from its blank corner.
