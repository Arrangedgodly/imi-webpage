# The Three Hundred Foot Rescue

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action-hero rescue scenes, needless slow motion, and climaxes built around a single misplaced object*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A dramatic rescue team races across a recreation center to retrieve a child's paper kite, only to find the building's caretaker already has a safe ladder.

Cast: Vero, a baboon stunt coordinator; Dali, a lemur caretaker; Nima, a macaque parent; and Pip, a capuchin child.

SCENE 1. INT. RECREATION CENTER, AFTERNOON

A paper kite is caught on a high indoor beam. Vero declares a rescue. Nima tells Pip to stay behind the floor line. Dali arrives with a step ladder and measures the ceiling height. Nima asks Pip to describe how the kite got there. Pip says the tail snagged during a practice launch. Vero suggests a flashback with wind machines. Dali asks whether the fans are approved for the gym. The supervisor says no. Vero tells the crew to keep the fans off. Vero asks whether the situation has been assessed.

DALI: The kite is paper.

VERO: The beam is very high.

Pip says he can wait. Vero turns on a fan for dramatic wind. Dali turns it off before the kite sails farther into the rafters.

SCENE 2. INT. EQUIPMENT ROOM, MOMENTS LATER

Vero gathers a rescue crew. He assigns Nima to the rope, Dali to the ladder, and himself to the slow-motion entrance. Dali checks the ladder rating. Vero asks if the safety check can happen during the montage. Dali says it already did.

The crew carries the ladder into the gym. Vero walks in slow motion while the others move at normal speed. He reaches the beam long after Dali has positioned the ladder. Nima checks that the floor is dry and asks two staff members to hold the base. Dali climbs one rung and reaches toward the kite, but it is still too far away. The crew lowers the ladder instead of stretching. Vero asks if the suspense is working. Pip says it is working on the ladder. Nima holds the base while Dali checks the reach from the floor.

Pip offers the kite string. Dali asks him to keep his hands behind the line. Vero proposes throwing a rope around the beam. Dali says the rope could snag the sprinkler head. They lower the ladder and measure the reach.

VERO: We need a bigger rescue.

DALI: We need a longer reach.

NIMA: What if we ask for the pole?

Vero calls for a rope. Nima returns with an actual maintenance rope. Dali tells her to keep it coiled; it could catch on the sprinkler head. Vero asks for a helicopter shot. The recreation center has no helicopter. He accepts a wide shot from the balcony.

SCENE 3. INT. GYM, LATER

Dali asks the building supervisor for the approved extension pole. It is stored in the equipment room, where Vero had not looked because the door was not dramatic. The pole has a soft hook for retrieving decorations. Dali checks its length and confirms the floor is clear.

Nima steadies the ladder while Dali uses the pole. The kite slides toward the beam's edge. Vero orders everyone to hold their breath. Pip says the kite cannot hear them. Dali asks Pip to guide the tail gently from below. Together they lower it without tearing the paper.

The crew returns the pole and ladder to storage.

VERO: Can I do one leap for the ending?

DALI: The sign says NO JUMPING.

Vero steps down from the platform. Nima checks the kite; one corner is bent, but it is intact. Pip asks for tape. Dali gives him a small repair kit, and he smooths the corner against the table. The rescue crew packs the rope without using it.

SCENE 4. EXT. RECREATION CENTER, EVENING

Pip repairs the bent corner with tape. Nima helps him relaunch the kite outside in a clear field. Vero sets a camera for a slow-motion shot. A light breeze lifts the kite. It climbs a few feet and circles. Dali asks them to keep away from the parking lot.

The kite drifts toward a tree. Pip reels it in before it catches. Vero begins a speech about courage. Nima says Pip followed the safety line and kept control. Vero nods and records that as the final shot. Pip folds the kite carefully and asks if they can relaunch it tomorrow. Dali checks the weather notice and says the field is available after school. Vero writes the time on the call sheet. Nima asks whether slow motion is scheduled. Vero leaves the box blank.

FINAL IMAGE: On the camera monitor, the kite footage plays above the field. In the foreground, Pip holds the folded kite while Vero stays behind the marked line.
