# The Field Guide to a Fake Tree

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: nature documentaries that narrate ordinary animal behavior as epic strategy and disguise staged shots as observation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A crew filming a park's resident monkeys keeps staging "wild" moments until the troop starts directing them back.

Cast: Nali, a macaque documentary narrator; Venn, a lemur field producer; Tavi, a capuchin camera operator; and Goro, a gorilla park keeper.

SCENE 1. EXT. CITY PARK, DAWN

Venn hides a plastic bowl behind a fake tree. Tavi points the camera toward a bench. Nali stands at the microphone, reading in a hushed voice.

NALI: At first light, the troop gathers at the ancient watering hole.

GORO: That is a drinking fountain.

Venn asks him to stay out of frame. Goro steps behind the fake tree, which wobbles. Tavi braces it with a sandbag. Nali restarts the line and calls the fountain "a spring-fed basin." A jogger walks through the background, fills a bottle, and leaves.

TAVI: The basin has a visitor.

VENN: We will use a tighter lens.

A squirrel climbs into the frame and steals the plastic bowl. Venn calls it a natural surprise. Goro says the bowl belongs to the park's lost-and-found table.

SCENE 2. EXT. FAKE TREE, MORNING

Venn resets the bowl. She asks Tavi to capture a monkey discovering it. Nali watches two real monkeys arrive, sniff the bowl, and ignore it. One turns the fake tree around to inspect the zip tie.

NALI: The animals are assessing the structure.

GORO: They know it is a prop.

Venn whispers that the camera should wait for a more dramatic response. The monkey tugs the zip tie. The tree's cardboard bark peels away in a long strip. Tavi follows the action until the tree's painted back is visible. The monkey sits on the sandbag. Venn tells Nali to describe the moment as a bold inspection of human engineering. Nali tries.

NALI: The young observer tests the stability of the shelter.

GORO: The shelter is a folded sign.

The monkey leans back. The fake tree bends, and the sandbag rolls away. Tavi pans to the monkey, who steps aside before the cardboard trunk falls. Nali lowers the microphone.

NALI: The observer demonstrates excellent judgment.

Venn asks whether they can reset the trunk. Goro says the animal has moved on and the crew should too.

VENN: Do not cut. This is access.

Goro reaches in and gently retrieves the plastic bowl. The monkey follows his hand and takes the bowl, then sets it on the bench. Venn asks if she can call that a territorial claim. Nali says it looks like a bowl on a bench.

SCENE 3. EXT. PARK PATH, AFTERNOON

The crew follows the troop along the path. Nali records a line about the leader's patient search for shade. The monkeys stop beside a patch of sunlight and groom each other. Tavi frames the shot through leaves.

TAVI: Are those leaves ours?

VENN: They are foreground.

Goro points out that the camera is behind a hedge planted to keep visitors off the new grass. Venn asks Tavi to step over the low rope for a better angle. Goro says the rope is there because the grass is growing. One monkey reaches through the hedge and pulls Venn's shot list from her clipboard.

Nali starts narrating the paper theft. The monkey drops the pages in a puddle and sits on them. Venn tries to rescue the shot list without crossing the rope. She reaches with the boom pole, which taps the camera tripod. Tavi catches it before it falls.

GORO: The troop has protected the seedlings, the tripod, and your paperwork.

VENN: We need a clean establishing shot.

Goro opens the gate to the public path. Visitors walk by without entering the grass. The monkeys move to a shaded bench. Tavi gets the shot from the legal side of the rope. A visitor asks whether the crew is making a documentary about the monkeys or the grass. Venn says the film follows the troop's relationship with its habitat. Goro points to the taped boundary and the monkeys resting exactly where the shade ends.

VENN: We could use a drone for the overhead.

GORO: The park does not allow drones.

NALI: The troop is unbothered by the absence of an aerial view.

A monkey climbs the bench support and shakes loose a leaf onto Venn's clipboard. She stops writing and watches it settle. Tavi keeps recording.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK OFFICE, EVENING

The crew reviews the footage. The best sequence is the squirrel stealing the bowl, the monkey exposing the fake tree, and Goro stopping the boom pole. Nali records a final line without lowering her voice.

NALI: The park's residents reject the props, preserve their resting place, and leave the crew to carry its own equipment.

Venn considers asking for another take. Goro points at the time sheet. The shoot has ended. He gathers the sandbag and folds the fake tree.

FINAL IMAGE: The squirrel sits on the real park sign while the crew packs away the plastic bowl. The sign reads "Leave wildlife undisturbed."
