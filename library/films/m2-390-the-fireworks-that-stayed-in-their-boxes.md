# The Fireworks That Stayed in Their Boxes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: holiday disaster films, countdowns to midnight, expert teams defusing fireworks, and celebrations saved by choosing a quieter plan*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
FIREWORKS TECH LUMA, a lemur
MAYOR VEX, a macaque
EVENT PLANNER NARI, an orangutan
VOLUNTEER PIP, a capuchin
SAFETY INSPECTOR ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN FIELD - EVENING

The town prepares for a fireworks show. Safety inspector Odo discovers the launch platform is too close to dry grass. Mayor Vex checks the countdown clock. Event planner Nari asks whether the show can be moved. Fireworks tech Luma says the boxes must remain closed until the new site is inspected.

VEX: The crowd expects a display.

ODO: The field is not safe.

Pip carries a box labeled SPARKLERS. It is actually full of paper lanterns.

SCENE 2. INT. TOWN HALL - LATER

The committee searches for an alternate site. The riverside lawn is wet and safe, but the launch platform is too heavy to move before midnight. Luma checks the fireworks labels. The largest rockets need a clear radius and a permit. Odo says the permit cannot be issued until the site is measured.

Vex proposes a countdown projection and a music show instead. Nari asks the band to prepare a set. Pip unfolds the paper lanterns and finds they can be hung under the covered arcade. Odo tests the power line. It is dry and properly grounded.

SCENE 2A. EXT. RIVERSIDE LAWN - LATE EVENING

Luma examines the permit. The launch site must be a certain distance from trees, buildings, and the audience. The riverside lawn is safe for music but not for the larger fireworks. Pip offers to help measure. Vex holds the other end of the tape and keeps shifting the starting point so the distance looks longer. Odo pins the tape at the proper mark.

NARI: Measurements do not change to fit the program.

VEX: The program can change.

Luma returns to the closed launch area at the town field to watch the boxes. The rest of the committee updates the schedule. The fireworks are postponed, and the music show stays at midnight. Nari prints a new notice and posts it at the entrances. A volunteer reads the notice aloud to confirm that the message is clear.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVERSIDE LAWN - NIGHT

The crowd settles behind the rope line. Across the path at the town field, Luma guards the fireworks behind the barrier. The launch platform there is still too close to dry grass. Vex asks if they can light one small rocket. Odo says no. Nari begins the music program, and Pip hands out paper lanterns decorated with small drawings of stars. Odo checks that the exits are clear and the lantern stands are secured against the breeze.

Pip leads one countdown to midnight. The town clock strikes twelve. No rocket launches. Vex starts to announce that the celebration is over, but the band hits its first note. The lanterns glow under the string lights as the crowd dances. Luma watches the closed boxes from the town field while Odo walks the audience perimeter. On her radio she hears Vex trying to conduct the band with an unused launch flag.

SCENE 3A. EXT. RIVERSIDE LAWN - LATER

A gust lifts one lantern stand. Odo catches its base while Pip tightens the strap. The paper lantern spins like a tiny weather vane, pointing directly at Vex's event banner. Vex asks Nari whether the program can call it a sky display. She refuses; the lantern is still attached to its stand and the fireworks remain closed. He turns the banner around so it advertises the band instead. Luma radios from the field that the boxes are secure; the band plays loud enough for her to tap her foot at her post. The crew keeps the exits open until the last song ends. Vex tries to raise the unused countdown clock over the stage for a final shot; its stand tips, and Odo catches it before it reaches the lanterns. He sets it against the stage, where it displays 12:17.

VEX: Seventeen minutes late for my closing speech.

NARI: The band has packed. You have until twelve eighteen.

Vex watches the seconds change. He unfolds a six-page speech, looks at the clock again, and folds it into a paper hat.

SCENE 4. EXT. TOWN FIELD - LATER

The crew crosses the path and joins Luma to collect the fireworks. Odo seals the cases and signs the storage log. Luma notes that the display can be rescheduled after the new launch area is inspected. Vex says the town still got a celebration. Nari thanks the band as they pack their instruments. Vex wears his folded speech while helping carry a case.

Pip brings the decorative lanterns back from the riverside stands and hangs them under the field's covered arcade for tomorrow. The last spectators follow the lit path home. Vex's paper hat unfolds when he ducks under the arcade; Pip catches the dangling speech and uses its blank reverse as a lantern label.

SCENE 5. INT. STORAGE SHED - DAWN

The fireworks are stored securely. Luma labels the cases with the next inspection date. Vex signs the event report. Nari files the music program and the revised site plan. Vex writes STARS OF THE SHOW above the case numbers. Luma sticks the inventory sheet over his title.

LUMA: They did not appear.

VEX: Neither did my speech. Shall I label that too?

Pip points through the window. Under the arcade, his new lantern label reads MAYOR'S SIX-PAGE SILENCE. Odo closes the shed door on Vex's reply.

FINAL IMAGE: The closed fireworks boxes rest beside the calendar, while the paper lanterns hang above the town field in the morning light.
