# The Escape Room Has a Fire Exit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: puzzle-box adventure films that hide ordinary clues behind ominous mechanisms and treat accessibility rules as part of the riddle*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A group of monkey friends races to solve an escape-room puzzle before time runs out, while ignoring the clearly marked exit beside the first clue.

Cast: Team leader Bera, a macaque; codebreaker Sumi, a capuchin; mechanic Tolo, a gibbon; host Neri, a lemur; attendant Odo, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - AFTERNOON

A timer starts at sixty minutes. The room contains a desk, three locked boxes, a painted telescope and a large red EXIT sign.

NERI (over speaker): Find the key to the moon chest before the final bell.

BERA: The final bell is our deadline.

SUMI: The exit is right there.

TOLO: The game is to solve the puzzle.

Sumi finds a card beneath the desk. It reads: THE MOON HAS FOUR PHASES.

SCENE 2 - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - LATER

The group arranges four wooden disks to match the moon phases. The lock opens. Inside is a flashlight and another clue: FOLLOW THE RED PATH.

Bera follows a red stripe painted on the floor. It leads to the exit sign. The door opens easily.

BERA: The path leads out.

TOLO: We should keep playing. We have fifty minutes.

Sumi closes the door and points to a wall map. The red line marks the accessible route around the room.

SCENE 2A - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - LATER

Bera opens the desk drawer and finds a printed puzzle book. The clue mentions "four phases" but the wall has five painted moons, including a full moon and a crescent repeated.

BERA: The room has contradicted itself.

SUMI: The small card says four. The fifth moon is a reflection in the mirror.

Tolo tilts the painted telescope and the image shifts. The extra moon disappears. A metal plate beneath the telescope reads DO NOT FORCE.

TOLO: I was going to force it.

Sumi rotates the dial gently. A hidden compartment opens. It contains a brass key that does not fit the red door.

BERA: A false key.

SUMI: It fits the next box.

The team tries it in the box. The lock opens, revealing a folded route map. The route loops around the table and ends at the EXIT sign. Bera starts tracing the map with her finger.

BERA: The puzzle wants us to go in a circle.

TOLO: The path is marked for wheelchairs too. It is a route through the room, not a maze.

They follow the red stripe to the table and find a small chime speaker beneath it. The speaker is connected to the final button.

SUMI: The room has been giving us clues by sound as well as shape.

Bera tests the button. It clicks but does not open anything.

SCENE 2B - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The timer drops to twenty minutes. A recording plays a deep rumble. Bera rushes to the wall and searches for a hidden lever. Sumi notices the speaker is under a padded bench. Tolo crouches to listen; each time he shifts, the bench creaks in a different pitch.

TOLO: The room is asking us a question.

SUMI: It is a speaker under a bench.

Bera presses one end of the bench. The rumble changes to a squeak. Neri's voice comes through the ceiling speaker, struggling to stay ominous.

NERI: You have discovered the sound clue.

BERA: We have discovered your speaker.

NERI: It is part of the room.

Sumi traces the sequence: low rumble, two taps, then a chime. She taps the bench twice. Nothing happens. Odo opens the staff door and points at a playback button on the wall. The recording needs to run to completion; their bench tapping was not a code.

ODO: Let the clip finish.

They replay it. The chime rings at the end. Sumi presses the nearby button. A small drawer opens, revealing the final key. Bera glances at the red EXIT sign, then at the timer. They have learned the room's sequence without needing to force a prop or block the exit.

SCENE 3 - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

The team continues. Tolo uses the flashlight to look under the painted telescope. A mirror reflects a number on the ceiling. Sumi turns the telescope and finds a small dial.

SUMI: The number is 4-2-1.

Bera types it into a box. The box opens and releases a spring-loaded paper moon. It lands on Tolo's head.

TOLO: The moon has landed.

The next clue says: PRESS THE BUTTON WHEN THE BELL RINGS.

SCENE 4 - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The team waits by the button. The timer shows five minutes. Neri speaks through the speaker, but the sound is faint. Odo enters through a staff door.

ODO: Do you need a hint?

BERA: The bell has not rung.

ODO: The bell rings at the end of the game. The clue is to press the button when you hear the chime in the recording.

Sumi replays the room audio. A small chime can be heard after the words. She presses the button. The final box opens, revealing a key to the EXIT door.

SCENE 5 - INT. ESCAPE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Bera tries the key. It opens a drawer containing a certificate for completing the puzzle. The EXIT door is still available without the key. The timer stops at two minutes.

NERI: Congratulations. You solved the moon-room sequence.

SUMI: We could have left any time.

TOLO: But we found the four phases, followed the red path and heard the chime.

Bera puts the certificate in her pocket. Odo points out that the team used the accessible route even though the clue was not intended to be hidden.

BERA: Next time, we will read the room before assuming it is a trap.

FINAL IMAGE: The team exits through the open red-marked door, carrying a paper moon and a certificate while the timer resets for the next group.
