# The Duel at the Seed Counter

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: period courtship dramas, public declarations, and romantic rivals who could resolve everything by asking a direct question*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: Two garden-club rivals prepare a formal duel for the right to lead the spring planting, but the club's rules call for a short interview and a volunteer schedule.

Cast: Lady Nera, a lemur gardener; Baron Tavi, a macaque botanist; Puck, a capuchin master of ceremonies; Elder Sumi, an orangutan garden steward; and the interview panel and garden volunteers.

SCENE 1. INT. CONSERVATORY, MORNING

Nera and Tavi stand across a table of seed packets. Puck announces a contest for the spring planting lead. Nera carries a polished hand trowel. Tavi carries a bound proposal. Both assume the role is awarded through a public challenge.

NERA: I have tended the beds for six years.

TAVI: I have improved the compost schedule.

SUMI: Did either of you read the volunteer notice?

NERA: I thought seniority decided.

TAVI: I thought the compost chart decided.

SUMI: It asks for a one-page plan and a ten-minute interview.

Puck raises the clipboard like a challenge.

PUCK: Choose your weapon.

SUMI: Choose an interview time.

Puck lowers the clipboard and writes his name in the 2:30 slot. Nera places her trowel on the table. Tavi opens his compost proposal. They both lean over the sign-up sheet at once and bump heads.

SCENE 2. INT. CONSERVATORY, AFTERNOON

Nera practices a speech about legacy. Tavi practices bowing before handing over his compost chart. Puck arranges flowerpots like an audience. Sumi moves them away from the walkway and posts the interview schedule.

Puck is disappointed that the rivals are cooperating. He sets flowerpots between them as if they are barriers. Nera moves one pot so they can see the same chart.

NERA: Why do you want the lead?

TAVI: The compost plan misses the wet season.

NERA: And the beds need shade cloth.

They compare their charts. Tavi points to the wet-season gap; Nera marks where shade falls across the beds. Puck waits for an insult. Nera asks Tavi to spell the plant names correctly. He corrects one label and hands her the marker. She adds a sun symbol beside the shaded row.

Sumi asks them to decide who will present first. They consult the schedule. Nera has the 2:10 slot; Tavi has 2:20. Puck declares that the order is a sign from fate. Nera says it is a ten-minute difference.

SCENE 3. INT. GARDEN STEWARD'S OFFICE, NEXT DAY

The interview panel asks Nera and Tavi how they will coordinate volunteers. Nera proposes dividing the beds by sun exposure. Tavi presents a revised compost chart. A steward asks who will water during the holiday weekend. Neither has assigned the task.

PANEL CHAIR: Who waters during the holiday weekend?

NERA: I can cover Saturday.

TAVI: I can cover Sunday, if someone checks the compost.

A volunteer raises a hand to take that shift. Sumi asks whether the volunteer agrees to the task. They nod and write their names on the sheet. Puck asks if this is the moment for a grand declaration. Sumi says it is the moment to confirm the schedule.

The panel asks what they will do if the seedlings sprout early. Nera proposes moving the trays to the greenhouse. Tavi checks the space on the diagram and makes room for them. Puck prepares a grand declaration, but the panel asks him to hold the door while the applicants leave.

The panel appoints Nera as planting lead and Tavi as compost coordinator. Nera asks if she may credit him on the plan. Tavi says yes. The panel asks them to post the schedule before the first planting day.

SCENE 4. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, SATURDAY

Nera leads the volunteers through the bed layout. Tavi reviews the compost chart. A volunteer brings the wrong seed packet. Nera checks the label and catches the mistake before planting. Puck calls it a dramatic twist. Sumi says it is why the group reads labels.

The volunteers finish the first row and record what was planted. Nera asks Tavi to join the planning committee for summer shade. He agrees after checking his schedule. Puck offers to host a formal announcement. They tell him to post the volunteer roster instead.

Puck raises the trowel like a trophy and announces that the rivals now share the garden. Nera takes the trowel back and asks him to put it down before he chips the handle. Tavi hands Nera the corrected planting chart. They walk to the bed and check where the first row should go. Puck follows, still waiting to announce a winner.

FINAL IMAGE: The plan is pinned to the garden gate. Nera holds the trowel while Tavi checks the compost chart; Puck bows to the schedule board.
