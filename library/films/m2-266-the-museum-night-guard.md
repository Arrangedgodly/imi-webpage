# The Museum Night Guard

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: night-at-the-museum fantasy films, where exhibits appear to move after closing*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A night guard investigates moving exhibits and discovers that visitors leave props in places that change the shadows after closing.

Cast: Guard Nara, macaque; conservator Pava, gibbon; custodian Odo, baboon; night visitor Seli, capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM HALL, NIGHT

A small wooden boat is displayed in a glass case. Nara's flashlight sweeps across it. The shadow appears to move.

NARA: The boat shifted.

ODO: The case is locked.

PAVA: The boat is on its stand.

SELI: I saw it move too.

NARA: You are a visitor after closing.

SELI: I am the night tour guide.

ODO: The tour ended an hour ago.

Seli points to the boat. Its shadow has shifted toward the wall.

PAVA: The overhead light is on a timer.

NARA: It is midnight. The exhibits should be still.

ODO: Exhibits are objects.

SELI: The boat looks like it is sailing.

SCENE 2. INT. GALLERY, CONTINUOUS

Seli leads the crew to the large clock in the central hall. Its hands are set to noon, but the pendulum swings in the emergency draft.

SELI: The clock is counting down.

NARA: It is keeping time.

PAVA: The clock is not wound.

Odo checks the case and finds that the pendulum is loose. He secures it with a felt pad. The movement stops.

ODO: It is not a countdown.

NARA: We should log the clock as still.

SELI: The clock is the only exhibit that was actually moving.

PAVA: It is a mechanism.

SELI: A mechanical exhibit.

They move the flashlight away. The clock's shadow shrinks, and the hall looks quiet again.

SELI: The clock is counting down.

NARA: It is keeping time.

PAVA: The clock is not wound.

Odo checks the case and finds that the pendulum is loose. He secures it with a felt pad. The movement stops.

ODO: It is not a countdown.

NARA: We should log the clock as still.

SELI: The clock is the only exhibit that was actually moving.

PAVA: It is a mechanism.

SELI: A mechanical exhibit.

They move the flashlight away. The clock's shadow shrinks, and the hall looks quiet again.

They check the other displays. A painted bird seems to flap when the vent turns on. A stone mask appears to turn toward the door.

NARA: The entire gallery is awake.

PAVA: The vent is blowing the paper tag.

ODO: The mask is carved from stone.

SELI: The bird's wing moved.

PAVA: The paper tag is in front of it.

Odo switches off the vent. The tag stops moving. The shadow of the bird settles.

NARA: It has stopped.

SELI: The bird is asleep.

PAVA: It is painted.

Nara checks the boat case. The stand is level, but a small card has slid under one side.

PAVA: The case is tilted.

ODO: The floor is level.

NARA: The case is not.

SCENE 3. INT. STORAGE ROOM, LATER

Pava retrieves a small wedge. She lifts one corner of the case while Odo slides it underneath. The boat's shadow shifts again, now toward the door.

SELI: It is escaping.

NARA: The case is being leveled.

ODO: It is not locked open.

The light timer clicks. The overheads switch off, and the emergency lights glow. The shadows jump across the gallery.

[SFX: LIGHTS CLICK, ROOM HUM.]

SELI: The exhibits have moved.

PAVA: The lights changed.

NARA: We need to finish before the timer resets.

They reset the timer. The gallery lights return. The shadows return to their earlier positions.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY, MOMENTS LATER

Seli asks whether she can lead the night tour anyway. Nara says the museum is closed. Seli offers to do a quiet tour for the crew.

PAVA: We are here to repair the display.

SELI: The boat is still in its case.

NARA: It is not moving.

Seli walks around the case. Her flashlight crosses the glass; the boat's reflection seems to glide forward.

SELI: It moved again.

NARA: That is your reflection.

SELI: I am in the museum.

PAVA: The boat is reflecting the person looking at it.

Seli steps aside. The reflection disappears.

SELI: It has vanished.

ODO: You moved.

Nara marks the floor beside the case. The reflection returns when she stands at the same angle.

SCENE 5. INT. FRONT DESK, DAWN

The night ends. Pava signs the maintenance log: "Case leveled; vent tag secured; timer checked." Nara gives Seli the day tour schedule.

SELI: I will tell visitors the boat moves at night.

NARA: It does not.

SELI: I will tell them its shadow moves.

PAVA: The lights change.

SELI: I will tell them to stand here and look.

Odo switches the lights off to test the timer one last time. The boat's shadow lengthens across the floor.

NARA: There it goes again.

PAVA: The timer works.

SELI: The boat is sailing.

NARA: It is a shadow.

SELI: A shadow with a good route.

[SFX: LIGHTS CLICK BACK ON. MORNING DOORS OPEN.]
