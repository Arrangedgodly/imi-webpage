# The Frame Between Two Museums

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: art-thief thrillers, laser security grids, museum rivalries, and restorers who are more worried about fingerprints than treasure*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
RESTORER NARI, an orangutan
CURATOR PIP, a capuchin
GUARD VEX, a macaque
ARTIST LUMA, a lemur
MESSENGER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. CITY MUSEUM - NIGHT

A painting hangs in a glass case. A red laser grid crosses the room. Guard Vex patrols with a flashlight. Restorer Nari examines a small crack in the frame. The painting appears to be missing a corner.

VEX: The thief has arrived.

NARI: The corner is behind the mat.

Pip discovers the museum next door is missing its frame. The paintings are intact, but each frame was switched during a shared exhibit.

SCENE 2. INT. CONNECTING HALL - LATER

The two museums share a temporary gallery. Curator Pip compares the loan records. One painting belongs to the city collection; the frame belongs to the harbor museum. Artist Luma arrives with a sketch of the frame, which she designed to hold the work securely.

Vex searches for a hidden panel. He shines his light through the glass and sees his own reflection. He mistakes it for another guard and salutes. Nari asks him to turn off the flashlight so she can inspect the surface without glare.

SCENE 2A. INT. LOAN OFFICE - NIGHT

Pip searches the loan records. One form lists the painting and frame as a single item; a later amendment separates them. Odo signed the amended form while carrying several crates and put the copy in the wrong folder. Vex reads the phrase "frame transfer" and assumes a theft. Nari explains that the transfer happened in the hallway between the two museums.

LUMA: The hallway has a cart.

VEX: The hallway has an alibi.

Pip checks the cart log. The frame was moved during gallery setup, and the cart passed the security camera at 10:14. Vex identifies the time as a clue. Nari asks him to notice the frame is still on the cart in the footage. Vex says the thief could have returned it. The log shows Odo's signature and a work order.

SCENE 3. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - MIDNIGHT

Nari removes the painting from the frame. The backing board is too tight and has bowed the canvas. She lifts it carefully and finds a label beneath the mat. The label identifies the frame's owner. The apparent missing corner is a section of the mat board cut to fit the frame's curved corner.

PIP: So no one stole anything?

NARI: The frame went to the wrong building.

Vex checks the security footage. It shows Odo carrying a frame through the connecting hall. He was moving it to the exhibit room according to an outdated floor plan. Odo arrives with a signed cart log and the correct frame. Vex asks why he did not look suspicious. Odo says he was wearing a badge.

SCENE 3A. INT. RESTORATION ROOM - LATE NIGHT

Nari demonstrates how the frame's backing had pressed against the canvas. She inserts thin spacers and allows air to circulate. Vex puts on cotton gloves and offers to help. He keeps touching the glass with his knuckles. Nari assigns him to hold the light instead. Pip reads the storage instructions and notes the correct orientation on a diagram.

LUMA: The frame is a support, not a disguise.

PIP: The label will say that.

They take a photograph of the painting beside its frame and attach the image to the loan file. Vex's flashlight reflects in the glass, so he steps to the side. For the first time, the artwork is clearly visible to the camera.

SCENE 4. INT. SHARED GALLERY - DAWN

The painting is fitted into its own frame. Nari adjusts the backing so the canvas can expand and contract. Luma checks the design. Pip updates the loan records and labels both frames. Vex removes the laser grid; it was only a display effect and had never been connected to the alarm.

VEX: We have secured the art.

NARI: We have secured the records.

Odo carries the borrowed frame back through the connecting hall. This time he uses the current map. Pip watches the transfer and signs the receipt.

SCENE 5. INT. CITY MUSEUM - MORNING

The exhibit opens. Visitors view both paintings and their frames. Luma explains how she designed the curved corner. Nari stands nearby to answer questions about the canvas. Vex keeps his flashlight switched off. Pip confirms the labels are in the right places and files the final transfer receipt.

FINAL IMAGE: The glass reflects two frames facing each other across the hall, each with its own painting and a clear ownership label.
