# The Stunt Double's Last Cup of Tea

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action films that confuse practical stunt preparation with escalating spectacle and keep reshooting a safe, simple gag*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A stunt team tries to film a dramatic leap over a tea trolley, but the director keeps adding effects until the actor's simple step becomes a production crisis.

Cast: stunt performer Keli, a macaque; director Noro, a gibbon; actor Pavi, a capuchin; coordinator Dena, an orangutan; camera operator Toma, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. STUDIO SET - MORNING

A tea trolley is parked beside a low foam step. Keli checks the floor mats. Pavi rehearses a scene where his character leaps over the trolley.

DENA: We can step over the trolley. No jump is needed.

NORO: The hero must clear it in one grand movement.

PAVI: I can do a small hop on the mat.

DENA: If it is comfortable and rehearsed. We have a spotter.

TOMA: Camera is set. The trolley is empty.

Keli checks the wheels, locks them, then gives the signal. Pavi steps over the foam block and lands on the mat.

NORO: Cut. It looked too easy.

SCENE 2 - INT. STUDIO SET - LATER

Noro asks for wind. A stage fan blows napkins off the trolley. Keli catches one before it reaches the mat. Dena turns the fan away from the performers.

NORO: Add a second trolley, rolling toward the hero.

DENA: That changes the stunt. We need to reset and rehearse.

PAVI: I can dodge it.

DENA: We do not use a moving trolley. It is not part of the plan.

Noro asks for a crash sound. Toma plays a recording of a tray falling. Pavi flinches before the step.

KELI: The sound cue is early.

TOMA: It is on the same button as the slate.

SCENE 2A - INT. STUDIO SET - MOMENTS LATER

Noro adds a rolling camera move. The camera track crosses the same floor mark where Pavi is meant to land. Toma points at the track.

TOMA: The camera has the landing spot.

NORO: We can swing past him at the last moment.

DENA: No. Move the track or move the actor.

Pavi looks at the tea trolley, then at the foam step.

PAVI: I can stay on the mat while the camera moves outside the mark.

Dena has the crew lift and reposition the track. Keli checks the mat again. The tea trolley remains locked in place.

KELI: Clear landing zone.

Toma pushes the camera dolly along the new track. A wheel bumps the foam block and shifts it half an inch.

DENA: Reset the block. We do not roll until it is on the tape mark.

Noro checks his watch.

NORO: We are losing daylight.

DENA: We are in a studio. The light is overhead.

Pavi steps back to the starting mark and waits for the next safety check.

SCENE 3 - INT. CONTROL BOOTH - CONTINUOUS

Dena reviews the shot. The fan blows napkins, the sound effect plays, and Pavi steps aside from the trolley, bumping the foam block. No one is hurt, but the shot is unusable.

NORO: We can make it a near miss.

DENA: We can make it a safe step. The original choreography works.

KELI: The mat, locked wheels and clear mark are in place. The extra fan and trolley are not.

Pavi removes the cape from his costume. It had covered the floor mark.

PAVI: I could not see where to land.

NORO: The cape is part of the hero's silhouette.

DENA: It can stay above the knees and out of the landing area.

SCENE 4 - INT. STUDIO SET - AFTERNOON

The crew resets the scene. The trolley stays locked. The fan is off. Toma moves the crash cue to a separate button and waits for Dena's signal. Keli checks the mat and confirms the clear landing space.

DENA: We will film the step as rehearsed. One take, then a review.

Pavi crosses the foam block in a small, controlled hop and lands on both feet. Keli spots from the side. The shot is clean.

NORO: Can we add sparks?

TOMA: The sound booth has no sparks.

DENA: No effects. The scene is complete.

SCENE 5 - INT. CRAFT SERVICES - LATER

Pavi drinks tea. Keli rolls the mat back into storage. Noro watches the footage on a monitor. The step is clear and the trolley remains still.

NORO: It looks like a hero crossed a dangerous obstacle.

PAVI: It was a tea trolley.

KELI: The audience will understand the scene without a second cart.

Dena signs the stunt sheet and returns the locked wheels to the storage rack. Toma packs the separate sound button.

FINAL IMAGE: The tea trolley stands parked beside the foam block, its wheels locked, while the stunt mat is rolled neatly against the wall.
