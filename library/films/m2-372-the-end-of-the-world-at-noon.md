# The End of the World at Noon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: apocalyptic disaster films, countdown clocks, emergency broadcasts, and heroes who discover the catastrophe is a scheduling mistake*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
MAYOR PIP, a capuchin
METEOROLOGIST NARI, an orangutan
RADIO HOST VEX, a macaque
ENGINEER LUMA, a lemur
CLERK ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN RADIO STATION - MORNING

A red alert flashes on every screen: WORLD ENDS AT NOON. Radio host Vex interrupts the weather report. Mayor Pip arrives with a binder of emergency plans. Meteorologist Nari checks the satellite data and sees that the warning refers to a scheduled simulation drill.

VEX: The clock says three hours.

NARI: The clock is counting to the drill.

The station's dramatic music plays automatically. Engineer Luma unplugs the warning speaker, but the music continues from the lobby radio.

SCENE 2. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - LATER

The residents gather beneath a clock tower. Vex broadcasts instructions to shelter. Odo the clerk reads the drill notice aloud. The notice says: "At noon, the world simulation ends." The words have been separated across two lines. Pip announces that the world is not ending, but the crowd has already packed umbrellas, snacks, and folding chairs.

Luma checks the town siren. It is set to sound at noon as part of the drill. She tests it once; the siren echoes across the square. Everyone ducks behind the fountain. Vex calls it a successful evacuation test.

SCENE 2A. INT. TOWN HALL - LATER

Mayor Pip leads a meeting to explain the alert. The residents bring supplies they gathered after hearing the broadcast: umbrellas, blankets, sandwiches, and a large box of candles. Odo checks the drill notice and discovers the poster was printed with the last line cropped. The full line says the simulation ends at noon. Vex asks if the correction can wait until the broadcast. Pip asks him to say it now.

PIP: The warning was a drill notice.

VEX: The warning looked urgent.

NARI: It was urgent that we read the whole notice.

Luma demonstrates the siren panel. One button activates the drill, another activates an actual emergency warning. The buttons are the same color. She labels them with clear text and asks Odo to update the instruction sheet. Vex offers to record a dramatic voiceover. Luma asks him to record both labels at normal volume.

SCENE 3. INT. WEATHER OFFICE - ELEVEN FIFTY

Nari shows the satellite map to Vex. The red band on the screen is a heat map from the town's new pavement, not a burning continent. The broadcast uses a zoomed-in image that removes the map legend. Vex asks her to explain the color. She does; he repeats only the alarming part.

PIP: Can we put the legend back on the screen?

VEX: The legend is small.

LUMA: So is the red patch.

She restores the full image. Odo changes the alert to "simulation drill ends at noon." The town begins to relax, though the emergency clock still ticks down.

SCENE 3A. INT. RADIO STATION - ELEVEN FIFTY-FIVE

Vex prepares the noon broadcast. His script says "At noon, the world ends." Nari crosses out the sentence and writes the full drill explanation. Vex says the shorter line will hold listeners. Pip sets the clock where the camera can see it. The station music starts by itself again; Luma finds the timer connected to the alarm panel and disconnects it.

VEX: It is quieter now.

LUMA: The broadcast should be clearer too.

Odo reads the corrected notice aloud. Vex records it once, without adding an ominous pause. The whole town hears that the simulation will finish at noon and that normal services will continue. The update appears on the town website and station board.

SCENE 4. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - NOON

The siren sounds. The clock reaches zero. The alert screen turns green and displays DRILL COMPLETE. A pigeon lands on the screen's frame, obscuring the word COMPLETE. The crowd gasps. Luma lifts the bird gently to the fountain ledge. Pip announces that the exercise is over and the town is safe.

Vex asks Nari for an update on the world. She points to the weather map: clear skies, light wind. Odo files the drill report and notes that the warning should be displayed on one line. The town folds its chairs and returns to lunch.

SCENE 5. INT. RADIO STATION - AFTERNOON

Vex records a correction. He says the world did not end; the scheduled simulation ended. Nari checks the headline before it airs. Luma lowers the music volume. Pip removes the red alert graphic and replaces it with the standard weather report.

FINAL IMAGE: The countdown clock resets to 24 hours, displaying the time until tomorrow's noon drill in small, ordinary numbers.
