# The Dragon Under the Roof

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests, prophecy riddles, magical artifacts, and a final battle won by reading the instructions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
FENN, a capuchin apprentice
SORA, a gibbon cartographer
THE ORACLE, a ring-tailed lemur
BRAM, a macaque roofer

SCENE 1. EXT. MISTY HILL - DAY

A storm swirls around a black mountain. Fenn and Sora arrive carrying a sword, a map, and a basket of lunch. The Oracle waits beside a stone arch.

ORACLE: The dragon sleeps beneath the mountain. Only the bearer of the silver blade can wake it.

FENN: Why would we wake it?

ORACLE: The prophecy says the mountain must sing before the valley can bloom.

SORA: It's spring. The valley is already blooming.

The Oracle points to a line carved into the arch: "When the roof cracks, the dragon will wake." A deep rumble comes from above.

SCENE 1A. EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAIL - LATER

The heroes climb a narrow path. The sword catches on every branch. The lunch basket slides down the hill. Fenn catches it with the map. The Oracle continues ahead, cape dragging through mud.

ORACLE: The mountain tests our resolve.

SORA: The trail is steep. We can use the marked switchback.

The Oracle takes the direct route and arrives at the same place after slipping twice. Fenn and Sora follow the switchback, reaching the cavern with their lunch intact.

SCENE 1B. EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAIL - LATER

The heroes climb a narrow path. The sword catches on every branch. The lunch basket slides down the hill. Fenn catches it with the map. The Oracle continues ahead, cape dragging through mud.

ORACLE: The mountain tests our resolve.

SORA: The trail is steep. We can use the marked switchback.

The Oracle takes the direct route and arrives at the same place after slipping twice. Fenn and Sora follow the switchback, reaching the cavern with their lunch intact.

SCENE 2. INT. MOUNTAIN HALL - LATER

The heroes enter a cavern with a stone door. It has three slots: sword, map, and lunch basket. They insert the sword into the first slot. It sparks. The map fits the second. The basket fits the third.

FENN: This is a very specific lock.

SORA: It may be a storeroom.

The stone door opens. Inside are tools, roofing tiles, and a sleeping dragon curled around a chimney. The dragon snores, sending smoke rings through the roof.

SCENE 3. INT. MOUNTAIN HALL - CONTINUOUS

Fenn tries to remove the sword from the lock. The door closes. The dragon snorts. The roof shakes, and a tile falls onto its tail. The dragon opens one eye.

DRAGON: Who has disturbed my nap?

ORACLE: The chosen ones have come to awaken you.

DRAGON: I was awake. The roof is leaking.

Sora examines the chimney. The tiles are cracked and the smoke has nowhere to go. Bram, a roofer who followed the storm, enters with a ladder and a tool belt.

BRAM: I told the village that roof needed work.

FENN: We brought a sword.

BRAM: That is not roofing equipment.

The dragon blows a warm gust. Fenn's map flies onto the roof. Sora climbs the ladder to retrieve it. The Oracle holds the ladder, quoting the prophecy with each step.

SCENE 4. EXT. MOUNTAIN ROOF - AFTERNOON

Bram shows the group how to replace a tile without waking the dragon too much. The dragon pretends to sleep, then corrects his measurements with a puff of smoke. Fenn holds the tiles. Sora passes them up. The Oracle stands by with the sword, waiting for a battle cue.

The roof is patched. The smoke flows through the chimney and rises cleanly into the air. A cloud forms above the peak, then breaks into sunlight.

DRAGON: Thank you. I can finally breathe without coughing into the valley.

ORACLE: The mountain has sung.

BRAM: It was the chimney.

SCENE 4A. EXT. MOUNTAIN PATH - DUSK

The crew carries the unused sword down the trail. The Oracle insists on narrating their descent as a victory march. Fenn hands the sword to Bram, who uses its flat side to hold a loose tile in place while the repair sets.

BRAM: At least it has one practical use.

ORACLE: It is an honored artifact.

BRAM: It is a very wide putty knife.

SCENE 5. EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - EVENING

The heroes return with the sword unused and the lunch basket empty. The village celebrates the clearing sky. Fenn asks the Oracle what the prophecy meant.

ORACLE: It meant someone would repair the roof.

SORA: The carving could have said that.

ORACLE: Prophecies are usually shorter than repair estimates.

Bram offers Fenn a roofing apprenticeship. Fenn accepts, placing the sword in a tool rack. Sora updates the map to mark the safe trail to the mountain. The dragon's smoke curls above the roof in a shape that resembles a crown, then disperses.

FINAL IMAGE: The repaired mountain chimney sends up one neat puff of smoke. The dragon sleeps beneath it, while the Oracle uses the sword as a pointer to show a group of villagers where to find Bram's ladder.
