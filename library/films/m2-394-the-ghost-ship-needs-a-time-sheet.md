# The Ghost Ship Needs a Time Sheet

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-ship movies, ominous fog, and crews who mistake basic work records for supernatural warnings*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
CAPTAIN SERA, a macaque
MATE ODO, a gibbon
NARI, a lemur cook
PIP, a capuchin deckhand
THE GHOST (ELI), an orangutan

SCENE 1. EXT. HARBOR - NIGHT

A dark ship slips into the harbor without a sound. Fog rolls across the water. Captain Sera steps onto the deck. A bell rings by itself.

SERA: Who is on watch?

ODO: The schedule says Pip.

PIP: I was below deck.

NARI: Then who rang the bell?

The ghost appears at the bow, holding a damp paper sheet. Odo shines a lantern at it. The sheet glows.

ODO: It is a list of names.

SERA: A crew manifest.

PIP: It has times beside the names.

NARI: A time sheet.

The ghost points to a blank box beside its name. It looks angry. Sera tells the crew to prepare for a haunting. Odo fetches salt. Nari brings a ladle.

Pip counts the signatures. There are four crew members and five lines. Odo says the extra line is probably for the ghost. The ghost taps the blank box until Odo turns the sheet over. On the back are the watch instructions: "Record arrival, departure, and break."

ODO: It wants a break.

NARI: It has been standing in fog since midnight.

Sera asks the ghost when it took its last break. It points to the galley. Nari opens the door. A kettle whistles by itself, and everyone jumps.

PIP: The kettle is on.

NARI: Then the ghost has been making tea.

SCENE 2. INT. GALLEY - LATER

The ghost follows them below deck. It taps the time sheet against the wall. The blank box remains empty.

SERA: It wants something.

PIP: Maybe it wants us to leave the ship.

NARI: It could want its hours recorded.

Odo says ghosts do not punch clocks. The ghost points to his watch. Odo hides it behind his back.

Nari asks when the ghost boarded. It writes "11:40" on the sheet. The ship's clock reads 11:39. Pip says the ghost is a minute ahead.

SERA: That is not possible.

ODO: The clock may be slow.

The ghost turns the clock hands forward. They stop at 11:40. The ship's bell rings. The crew dives behind the table.

PIP: It moved time.

NARI: It corrected the clock.

The ghost holds out the sheet again. Sera notices the rest of the crew names are signed, but the last column is blank. She asks Odo to sign his watch time.

ODO: I am not signing a haunted document.

The ship jolts. Pots rattle. Nari grabs a bowl before it falls. The ghost points to a line on the page marked "hours aboard."

SCENE 3. EXT. DECK - MIDNIGHT

Sera orders the crew to inspect the deck. The fog thickens. Pip sees a lantern moving toward them. Odo calls out the ghost's name, but it does not answer. Nari recognizes the lantern as the one she left in the galley.

NARI: Someone is carrying the spare light.

PIP: The ghost took it.

The ghost raises its empty hand. The lantern swings behind them, tied to the mast by a loose rope. Wind pulls it in circles. Odo walks toward it, but the rope catches his coat and spins him around.

ODO: The ship is turning.

SERA: The ship is anchored.

Pip untangles Odo. The ghost points again to the time sheet. Sera takes it. The paper is dry now. She fills in the blank time beside the ghost's name: "11:40 to midnight."

The bell rings once. The ghost nods. The ship's ropes loosen. It begins drifting from the pier.

SERA: We are moving.

ODO: The ghost has released the ship.

NARI: The tide has turned.

Pip checks the anchor line. It is slack. He pulls it in, hand over hand, as the ship glides toward the channel.

SCENE 4. INT. CAPTAIN'S CABIN - LATER

Sera reviews the log. The ghost sits across from her, watching the pen. She adds its watch hours to the crew ledger. Odo says the ghost's name is missing.

GHOST: Eli.

They all look up. It is the ghost's first spoken word.

ODO: We have a name.

NARI: We had a name. It was on the sheet.

Sera writes ELI at the top of the page. The ghost signs the line. The cabin lamp stops flickering.

SCENE 5. EXT. HARBOR - DAWN

The ship returns to the pier. The crew ties the ropes. Eli leaves the time sheet in the captain's log and steps toward the fog.

PIP: Are you leaving?

ELI: My shift is over.

Odo checks the sheet. It says Eli's next watch begins at midnight.

ODO: You are scheduled again.

Eli fades away. The ship's bell rings. Nari sets out breakfast. Sera assigns Odo to the first morning watch.

FINAL IMAGE: Odo signs the time sheet, looks toward the fog, and writes "present" beside Eli's name before the next bell.
