# The Tournament of Quiet Machines

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: tournament action films, bracket reveals, rival schools, training montages, and champions who learn to win by changing the rules*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
MARA, a capuchin inventor
MASTER VEX, a macaque mechanic
NARI, an orangutan judge
PIP, a squirrel monkey announcer
ODO, a gibbon competitor

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN HALL - MORNING

A banner reads ANNUAL QUIET MACHINE TOURNAMENT. Inventors bring devices designed to perform a household task without making noise. Mara carries a silent hand-cranked fan. Odo brings a self-folding laundry basket. Master Vex arrives with a brass machine that has twelve gears and a loud bell.

PIP: The bracket has been drawn.

NARI: It is a list of names.

The competitors are paired by alphabetical order. Mara faces Vex. Odo faces an empty chair because one contestant canceled.

SCENE 2. INT. WORKSHOP - DAY

Mara tests her fan. It turns quietly but moves only a little air. Vex polishes his machine and rehearses a speech about precision. The gears make a deep whir. He covers them with felt; the machine still rings its bell when it finishes.

Nari reminds the competitors that the task is to fold a towel. Vex's device grabs the towel, twists it, and knots it to the gear. Mara's fan dries a small corner. Odo's laundry basket folds itself around him and rolls across the floor.

SCENE 2A. INT. WORKSHOP - LATER

Pip films a training montage for the town's public broadcast. The camera follows Mara testing three fan blades, Vex tightening each gear, and Odo folding towels by hand while his basket is repaired. The footage is set to a thundering drum track. Nari watches the monitor and points out that the program is about quiet machines.

NARI: The montage is the loudest invention so far.

PIP: Should I lower the volume?

NARI: Start by removing the drums.

Pip replaces the music with a ticking metronome. The metronome is also louder than the fan. Mara turns it off, and the footage ends on a silent frame of Odo holding a folded towel.

SCENE 3. INT. TOWN HALL - AFTERNOON

The first round begins. Pip announces each match with a dramatic drumroll. Nari asks him to stop because sound levels are being measured. Pip whispers the drumroll, which is even less effective. Vex demonstrates his machine. It folds the towel into a neat rectangle, then rings the bell. The audience covers its ears.

Mara tests her fan again. She removes the heavy base and aims it toward the wet towel. The cloth lifts, floats, and lands over Vex's bell. The sound stops. Nari declares the round a tie because both inventions solved part of the problem.

SCENE 3A. INT. TOWN HALL - AFTERNOON

Vex asks for a practice round. He feeds the towel into his machine and pulls the lever. The gears turn, the bell swings, and the towel knots itself around the axle. Mara tries her fan to cool the machine, but the breeze lifts the bracket and sends a stack of scorecards across the hall. Odo uses the basket to catch the papers before they scatter beneath the chairs.

ODO: The basket has won the paperwork event.

Nari adds a separate test for noise, function, and safety. Vex asks if he can compete in each category separately. Nari says they will test the same machine once, then score what it actually does. He removes the bell and adjusts the axle instead of asking for a new event.

SCENE 4. INT. TOWN HALL - EVENING

For the final round, Vex asks for a rematch. Nari offers each competitor the same towel and the same quietness limit. Mara folds the towel by hand, then uses her fan to dry the damp corner. Vex removes the bell from his machine. It now works silently but takes longer. Odo enters the match with his laundry basket, which he has repaired with a latch.

The judge compares the devices. Mara's fan helps with drying, Vex's tool folds evenly, and Odo's basket stores the towel without unfolding. Nari awards a shared prize for three different useful tasks. She adds a small sound meter beside each machine so the contestants can see the readings. Vex accepts but asks which competitor stands in the center of the winner's photo. Nari places the three devices in a row.

SCENE 5. INT. WORKSHOP - NIGHT

The inventors exchange design tips. Vex learns to balance the fan's base. Mara adds a folding plate to her handle. Odo installs a smaller latch. Pip turns off the announcement speaker, which had been left on since the first round.

FINAL IMAGE: The banner comes loose and drapes over the silent machines, while the empty bell hangs beneath it without ringing.
