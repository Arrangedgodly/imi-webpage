# The Ambassador's Umbrella

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: diplomatic spy thrillers with secret signals, guarded entrances, and negotiations interrupted by rain*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: AMBASSADOR LUMA, an orangutan; KITO, a macaque aide; SEREN, a gibbon courier; VALE, a baboon security officer; NIX, a capuchin interpreter; MINISTER RHO, a mandrill envoy; FALSE COURIER, a vervet; PILOT, a squirrel harbor captain.

EXT. EMBASSY GARDEN - NIGHT

Rain falls over a formal garden. Ambassador Luma stands beneath a striped umbrella while Minister Rho approaches the stone table.

RHO: We have agreed to exchange the harbor map.

LUMA: We agreed to discuss an exchange.

Kito watches the fountain. A second umbrella rests against the bench. Seren, dressed as a groundskeeper, sweeps wet leaves toward the drain.

KITO: The garden has two umbrellas.

LUMA: One is mine.

SEREN: The other belongs to nobody.

Vale checks the umbrella's curved handle and finds a tiny brass pin. Seren looks up.

SEREN: Do not pull that.

A fountain jet pops. The umbrella turns inside out, releasing a folded map into Kito's hand. A security light flashes red.

VALE: Someone has opened the umbrella signal.

LUMA: It is a weatherproof document holder.

RHO: That is how the harbor map was supposed to arrive.

ACT ONE

INT. EMBASSY RECEPTION HALL - NIGHT

Vale locks the garden doors. Kito unfolds the map on a table. It shows the harbor, the old breakwater, and a red route through a restricted channel.

NIX: This route is not in the public charts.

RHO: It marks the rescue channel. We need the route before the storm reaches the outer boats.

LUMA: Then why hide it inside an umbrella?

SEREN: Because someone searched every folder that came through the gate.

Vale examines the handle. The umbrella has two pins, brass and silver. Kito removes the silver pin; a strip reads "Use the west gate." The west gate opens onto the locked garden. A courier bell rings.

A voice beyond the gate says, "The rain is on the stone."

NIX: Wrong response. Do not open.

A shadow passes the frosted glass. Kito spots water dripping from the umbrella onto the map.

VALE: The caller knows half the phrase.

NIX: Then half is not enough to enter.

Seren says the expected pilot wears a yellow scarf and carries a chart case with a chipped clasp. The shadow has neither. It knocks three times, pauses, then once. Vale keeps the gate locked. The false courier leaves a wet footprint and retreats. Vale photographs the print through the glass.

KITO: A copied knock is still a knock.

The map's red route has begun to fade. Seren sets it on a towel. Luma remembers the breakwater, while Rho recalls the rescue channel bending behind the western marker buoy. Nix raises the radio to verify which buoy carries the striped lantern tonight.

A single caller gives the proper harbor call sign over the radio and names the striped western buoy. Nix verifies the voice with harbor control. This is the expected pilot, not the false courier. Only then does Vale release the garden gate. The pilot enters carrying the chipped chart case.

PILOT: The storm has pushed two ferries toward the outer channel. I need the marked route now.

KITO: The red line is fading. We have the breakwater, not its bend.

The pilot checks the buoy report against a dry public chart. Rho traces the remembered curve, and the pilot confirms that the striped lantern is on the western marker tonight.

RHO: The umbrella delivered a damaged clue, not a complete order.

LUMA: Then no one sails on the clue alone.

Kito lays carbon paper beneath the dry chart and draws the backup route in blue pencil, retaining a copy for the embassy. The pilot traces it, repeats it to harbor control, and gets acknowledgment from both ferries. They turn toward safe water.

PILOT: Route received. We are clear to depart.

Seren folds the wet map away from the dry chart. Vale locks the garden gate after the pilot leaves with the case. Seren puts the damaged map in a drying rack.

RHO: We can discuss the harbor boundary after the storm.

LUMA: We can discuss it at the harbor, where the map belongs.

Seren checks the umbrella. Its brass pin is bent. Luma asks Kito to repair it before it returns to the garden.

KITO: I thought it was a weatherproof holder.

LUMA: It has been a diplomatic tool for an hour. It deserves a dry place.

The rain eases. The security light returns to green. The ambassador and minister return to the chart table in the hall.

ACT TWO

Inside the hall, Rho places his official boundary chart beside Kito's rescue route. They agree the charts can be compared in daylight, when neither tide nor umbrella is dictating diplomacy. Vale labels the false courier's abandoned footprint photograph for the security file. Nix hangs up the radio after harbor control signs off. Luma and Rho leave together through the west garden path.

FINAL IMAGE: The striped umbrella dries open in the embassy hall while Kito's carbon copy of the blue rescue route rests beside the boundary chart.
