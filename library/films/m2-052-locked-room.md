# The Room With No Secret Door

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: locked-room mysteries, impossible-room clues, and detective overconfidence*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A museum curator vanishes from a locked gallery during a fundraiser, and a detective searches for hidden passages while staff wait for him to check the obvious closet.

Cast: Inspector Fenn, a lemur detective; Curator Moss, a tortoise; Jip, a capuchin guard; Aria, a macaque conservator; and three baffled donors.

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM GALLERY, NIGHT

The gallery door is locked from inside. Moss is missing. Donors describe a bell, a thump, and a squeak. Inspector Fenn examines the polished floor with a magnifying glass.

FENN: No footprints.

ARIA: The floor was cleaned.

FENN: A clue.

JIP: It's cleaning day.

Fenn taps the walls. He finds a hollow sound behind a painting and declares a secret passage. Aria removes the painting; it is the water pipe. The donors gasp when the pipe drips on Fenn's hat.

SCENE 2. INT. GALLERY, CONTINUOUS

Fenn inspects the windows. They are sealed. He checks the chandelier for a pulley. It lowers slightly, then lifts him two inches off the floor. Jip lowers it before Fenn can declare an escape route.

FENN: Someone used the ceiling.

ARIA: The ceiling is twelve feet high.

FENN: That is why they chose it.

One donor asks if the curator could have been shrunk. Fenn writes this down. Aria points to the staff closet. Fenn says closets are too ordinary to matter.

SCENE 3. INT. SECURITY OFFICE, LATER

Fenn pins the clues to a board: bell, thump, squeak. He draws red string between them until it covers the museum map. Jip points out that the string blocks the camera monitor. Fenn moves the board. The string catches on his sleeve and pulls three pins free. Aria puts the pins back in a row.

FENN: The clues are connected.

JIP: They were on the same cart.

Fenn circles the cart. A wheel squeaks in the hallway. All three donors jump. Moss calls from offscreen that it needs oil.

Jip reviews the footage. The gallery door never opened. A cart entered before the fundraiser and left without anything on it. Fenn says the thief must have flown. Jip pauses the video: the cart is pushed by Moss, who then walks to the closet.

FENN: The suspect was in the room.

JIP: The curator was in the room.

Fenn asks if they can zoom in. The monitor becomes a blur. Aria says Moss was repairing a display panel and stored the tools in the closet. A loud thump sounds from the gallery. Fenn rushes out, followed by the others.

SCENE 4. INT. MUSEUM GALLERY, NIGHT

Fenn tests every drawer before Aria reaches the closet. One contains a museum brochure, another a sandwich. He calls the sandwich a possible motive. The donor who owns it takes it back. Fenn inspects the back of the painting again and finds no opening, only a label reading PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH. He asks whether the wording is a warning. Aria says it is for visitors.

The closet door is stuck. Moss calls from inside. Fenn circles it, looking for a hidden hinge. Jip tests the handle. It does not move. Aria checks the latch and finds a bent screw. She gets a screwdriver from the cart.

FENN: It could be a trap.

MOSS (O.S.): It is a closet.

Aria repairs the latch. The door opens. Moss steps out carrying a display label and a tin of screws. He says he called for help, but the reception bell rope had been moved. The thump came from a crate falling off the cart. The squeak came from its wheel.

Fenn writes down the sequence. The donors ask if the mystery is solved. He says the room remains impossible because the door was locked. Moss demonstrates the bent latch, then closes and opens it again. It works.

SCENE 5. INT. MUSEUM FRONT DESK, LATER

Fenn prepares his report. He lists the pipe, chandelier, cart, closet, and broken latch. Aria asks him to remove "possible aerial escape." He crosses it out but adds "closet with suspiciously ordinary appearance." Moss replaces the label on the display panel. Jip tests the bell cord; it rings at the desk.

FENN: A reliable bell.

JIP: It's the reception bell.

The donors sign the guest book. One asks where the secret passage is. Aria points to the museum map. Fenn points to the closet. The donor says it is too small for a passage. Fenn says that makes it more mysterious.

Jip files the report under maintenance. Fenn tries to move it to mysteries; the drawer is locked.

FINAL IMAGE: Fenn demonstrates the repaired latch by stepping into the closet. The door closes behind him. It opens easily, but he waits for the donors to find him. They have already gone to the gift shop.
