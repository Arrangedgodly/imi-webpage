# The Forest Is Not a Prison

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prison-break dramas, elaborate escape plans, secret tunnels, and inmates who refuse the obvious gate because the plan needs a twist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A group of primates trapped overnight in a wildlife center plans a dramatic escape, unaware that the gate is unlocked and they are free to return to the forest whenever they choose.

Cast
- KATO, a macaque who designs escape routes on napkins.
- LUMA, a capuchin climber who tests every vent.
- REED, a gibbon who knows the enclosure better than the guards.
- PIP, a ring-tailed lemur who wants to retrieve a lost seed pouch.
- WARDEN SOL, a mandrill caretaker who wears a key ring on his belt.

## Scene 1: Lockdown

INT. WILDLIFE CENTER - NIGHT

A storm has knocked out the main lights. Four primates sit in the indoor habitat. The outer gate is closed, the inner gate is open, and a sign says NIGHT CHECK IN PROGRESS.

KATO: We escape at midnight.

REED: We are not prisoners.

KATO: We are behind two gates.

REED: The outer gate is closed for the storm. We can go through the inner passage.

LUMA: I will check the vents.

PIP: I need my seed pouch from the storage room.

Kato draws a map on a napkin, marking guard posts and camera angles. Reed points out the habitat's open door, but Kato says it is too obvious.

## Scene 2: The Plan

The crew makes rope from bedding, stacks branches beneath a vent, and uses a tin cup to listen for footsteps. Luma climbs the wall but finds the vent too narrow. Pip distracts a camera by rolling a seed across the floor. The camera follows the seed, which stops beneath Warden Sol's desk.

LUMA: The vent is blocked by a leaf.

KATO: A guard has sealed it.

REED: It is a leaf.

PIP: My pouch is still missing.

KATO: We'll find it during the escape.

REED: We can just ask Sol in the morning.

KATO: The plan has reached the point where asking would ruin the tension.

## Scene 3: The Guard

INT. HALLWAY - LATER

Sol walks past with a flashlight. The primates hide behind a low wall. His keys jingle. Kato signals the others to wait until the guard passes. Sol stops and drops a flashlight battery. Pip slips out, picks it up, and hands it back.

SOL: Thank you, Pip.

KATO: You revealed our position.

PIP: We were sitting in the open.

Sol checks the outer gate. The storm has ended, but a fallen branch blocks the path. He returns to get a saw. The primates watch him leave.

## Scene 4: The Tunnel

Kato decides to dig beneath a planter. The others help scrape soil with bowls. They reach a water pipe, not the outside. The pipe begins to leak. Reed shuts the small valve. Sol hears the drip and returns.

SOL: Why is there soil on the floor?

KATO: We are constructing a tunnel.

SOL: The exit is through the habitat passage.

KATO: That is exactly what a warden would say.

SOL: The gate is open.

REED: We told him that.

Kato refuses to stop. He insists on one final test of the tunnel. It collapses under a small root, filling the room with dirt. Sol hands them a broom.

## Scene 5: The Choice

Sol opens the habitat passage and leads them to the outer gate. The fallen branch is still there. The group works together to move it. Kato notices that the gate latch is unfastened; it had only been pulled shut against the wind.

KATO: The gate was never locked.

SOL: It was closed while I checked the building.

LUMA: So we could have walked out.

REED: Once the path was clear.

PIP finds the seed pouch beneath the storage bench, where she left it earlier. The team must decide whether to go to the forest now or wait until morning when the path is safer. They choose morning.

Kato asks Sol to confirm the route through the woods. Sol draws the safe path on the back of Kato's napkin, circling the section where the storm knocked down branches.

KATO: Is this the official map?

SOL: It is the one I walked just now.

REED: That makes it more useful than ours.

Luma checks the path from the doorway. She spots a small branch still resting against the gate. Sol moves it aside before anyone steps through. Pip carries the seed pouch; no one needs a decoy.

KATO: We could have planned that.

REED: We did. We asked.

## Scene 6: Dawn

At sunrise, Sol opens the gate. Kato carries the dirt-covered napkin map. Luma stretches in the trees. Pip retrieves her pouch. Reed looks back at the center.

KATO: The escape plan worked.

REED: We waited for the gate to open.

KATO: The plan included waiting.

SOL: It did not include the tunnel.

KATO: The tunnel was a decoy.

PIP: It was full of dirt.

## Final Image

The primates walk into the forest. Sol sweeps soil from the threshold. The napkin map blows through the open gate and lands beside the planter, pointing toward the tunnel.
