# The Noon Duel at the Prop Corral

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: western showdown films that make a public duel out of a minor dispute over a borrowed prop*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: Two monkey actors prepare for a dramatic noon duel on a film set, but the confrontation is really about a missing prop horse and a misplaced lunch box.

Cast: Marshal Tavi, a macaque; outlaw Poro, a gibbon; director Reka, a lemur; prop master Omi, an orangutan; actor Neli, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. WESTERN BACKLOT - MORNING

A dusty street set stands beneath a bright studio sun. Tavi wears a marshal badge. Poro wears a black hat and a toy holster. Reka reviews the scene on a clipboard.

REKA: At noon, you meet in the street, draw, then freeze before the camera cuts.

TAVI: What are we dueling over?

REKA: The script says the outlaw stole the marshal's horse.

PORO: I need a horse for the scene.

Omi arrives carrying a prop saddle. The horse is missing from the corral.

SCENE 2 - EXT. PROP CORRAL - LATER

Poro inspects the empty stall. Tavi points to a hoofprint in the dust. Neli follows the track and finds it ends at the catering tent.

TAVI: The trail leads to the lunch line.

PORO: A western mystery.

Omi checks the prop log. The horse is a wooden hobby horse, assigned to a different set. The live animal is at the stable for a rest.

OMI: The prop was checked out for a rehearsal. It has not returned.

REKA: Can the duel proceed on foot?

TAVI: The script needs a horse reveal.

SCENE 2A - EXT. CATERING TENT - LATE MORNING

Neli opens the lunch box. It contains a folded script, a napkin and a toy sheriff badge. Tavi recognizes the badge as his.

TAVI: I left that in my holster pocket.

PORO: The prop horse may be in the catering tent.

Neli checks under the table. The wooden hobby horse is not there, but its cardboard silhouette appears in the backdrop painting.

Neli takes the silhouette to Reka.

NELI: This is the horse from the scene.

REKA: That is a painted horse. It cannot be led into frame.

Tavi compares the silhouette to the prop log. The painted version has a different saddle, which belongs to another production.

TAVI: We have two horses in the wrong scenes and one horse missing from the corral.

Poro looks toward the stable. A stable hand waves from far away but keeps the live horse in its paddock.

PORO: We are not borrowing that one.

Omi checks the reservation sheet and sees the hobby horse was signed out to the rehearsal room.

SCENE 3 - EXT. MAIN STREET SET - NOON

The camera rolls. Tavi and Poro stand at opposite ends of the street. The crew holds its breath. Poro draws a toy pistol, but it fires a paper flag reading BANG. Tavi draws a lunch menu from his holster.

TAVI: I put the wrong item in the holster.

PORO: The duel has become a picnic.

The director calls cut. Omi brings a hobby horse from the prop shed. It has a cracked handle. Reka refuses to use it until it is repaired.

SCENE 3A - EXT. BACKLOT STREET - MOMENTS LATER

The crew resets. Tavi draws the paper flag again. Poro tries to look menacing, but the paper flag has stuck to the sleeve of his coat.

REKA: That is the take. Hold the pose.

TAVI: I cannot lower my arm.

Neli peels the flag away. It reads BANG in large letters. Poro tries to point the toy pistol at the ground, but the prop barrel is capped.

PORO: The duel has been disarmed.

Omi arrives with the hobby horse, then checks the cracked handle.

OMI: This one needs repair before it goes in front of camera.

Reka lowers the clipboard. The noon sunlight shifts behind the backlot clouds.

REKA: We can wait for the repair instead of pretending the prop is ready.

Tavi holsters the lunch menu correctly and steps off the street set.

SCENE 4 - INT. PROP WORKSHOP - AFTERNOON

Omi tightens the handle screw and checks the wooden horse. Poro holds the horse by its reins. Tavi retrieves the lunch menu from his belt and returns it to the catering tent.

REKA: We can shoot the scene without a gallop. The horse can be led into frame.

TAVI: That is less dramatic.

OMI: It is also safe and matches the prop.

Neli replaces the paper flag with a foam prop. The crew rehearses the draw. Poro steps forward, realizes he has the wrong lunch box, and sets it down before the take.

SCENE 5 - EXT. WESTERN BACKLOT - LATE AFTERNOON

The scene is filmed with a hobby horse led into frame. Tavi and Poro stage the duel, draw their props and freeze. The camera captures the last frame. Reka calls cut.

REKA: We have the showdown.

PORO: The horse is not stolen anymore.

TAVI: It was never stolen. It was in the wrong shed.

Omi stamps the prop log and returns the hobby horse. Neli carries the lunch box to the right table. Reka changes the script note from STOLEN HORSE to MISPLACED PROP.

FINAL IMAGE: The hobby horse stands in its marked stall while Tavi and Poro share lunch at the catering table, toy holsters safely empty.
