# The Aquarium's Empty Tank

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: underwater disaster films where a dry maintenance tank is mistaken for a missing habitat*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
KEEPER MARA, a macaque
ANNOUNCER VEX, an orangutan
TECHNICIAN ODO, a gibbon
DIVER PIP, a capuchin
CURATOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. AQUARIUM GALLERY - MORNING

A large glass tank is empty. A sign reads CLOSED FOR CLEANING. Vex peers through the glass.

VEX: The ocean has disappeared.

MARA: The tank is drained for scheduled maintenance. The fish are in the holding pool.

ODO: The pump is off and tagged. The tank floor is dry.

PIP: The holding pool has a secure cover and an air line.

NARI: The exhibit opens tomorrow after the inspection.

SCENE 2. INT. AQUARIUM SERVICE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the maintenance log. Vex follows, carrying a bucket as if it were a rescue vessel.

VEX: We must return water to the sea.

MARA: The tank isn't the sea. We need to inspect the filter before refilling.

ODO: Filter housing is open. No cracks visible.

PIP: I'll check the gasket while Mara checks the return pipe.

VEX: The pressure could be building.

ODO: The pump is disconnected. There is no pressure.

NARI: The fish need quiet while the inspection continues. Please lower your voice.

SCENE 2A. INT. AQUARIUM SERVICE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mara turns the maintenance sheet toward the crew. The pump remains disconnected; the tag hangs from the switch.

MARA: We will inspect the tank from top to bottom, then refill it to the low mark. No fish move until the filter cycle is clear.

VEX: The creatures await their return.

NARI: They are in the holding pool, where the air line is running. The cover is latched.

ODO: Gasket checked. Drain checked. No crack in the filter housing.

PIP: There is a small piece of algae in the return pipe. I'll remove it with the soft brush.

Mara holds a clean tray under the opening. Pip lifts the algae out and places it in the tray. Nothing falls into the tank.

VEX: The habitat has a wound.

MARA: It has a bit of algae in the pipe. The pipe is clear now.

NARI: We'll discard the debris in the service bin, not in the holding pool.

Odo checks the pump tag and reads the isolation number aloud. Mara compares it with the work order before authorizing reconnection.

MARA: Numbers match. Pump stays off until we close the housing.

PIP: Housing is closed. Brush is clean and back on the rack.

ODO: I will reconnect after Mara signs the inspection.

Vex takes the empty bucket back to its hook. It was never used to carry water.

VEX: The rescue vessel is decommissioned.

MARA: It's a cleaning bucket. Leave it on the hook.

SCENE 3. INT. TANK SERVICE WALKWAY - LATER

The crew completes the last checks with the pump still off. Pip inspects the filter, Odo checks the drain plug, and Mara compares the gauges with the work order before the sign-off.

PIP: Gasket is seated. No tears.

ODO: Drain plug is in place. I have not tightened it until Mara confirms the tank is clear.

MARA: Floor and walls are clean. We can refill slowly after the sign-off.

VEX: The sea returns.

NARI: The water returns to the exhibit tank. The sea is outside.

SCENE 3A. INT. AQUARIUM GALLERY - LATER

The exhibit remains closed. Vex positions himself beside the glass with a rolled blue backdrop, ready to reveal the returning "ocean." Mara stops him before he unrolls it across the service path.

MARA: The refill has not started. We still need the signed inspection and a clear route to the pump switch.

VEX: The audience has been promised a dramatic sea return.

NARI: No audience is in the gallery. The sign says closed, and the fish are resting in the covered pool.

VEX: We could announce the event to the crowd outside.

PIP: The viewing panel is still covered. If you announce it, people will press their faces to the glass while we work.

Vex rolls the backdrop back up. It catches on his paper crown and pulls it sideways.

ODO: That is why scenery stays off the service path.

VEX: Then I shall narrate the water level from behind the line.

MARA: You may read the gauge from there after the pump starts. No narration until then.

Odo signs the work order. Mara reconnects the pump only after confirming the housing is closed; Pip points to the marked low line. The holding pool remains covered and aerated.

SCENE 4. INT. AQUARIUM GALLERY - AFTERNOON

The signed inspection is complete. The pump is connected once; Pip watches the water level rise. Vex steps back as the first ripple reaches the glass.

ODO: Refill is at the low mark. Pump is operating normally.

MARA: We stop at the marked level and let the filter cycle before moving any fish.

PIP: The holding pool remains covered. Air line is running.

VEX: The ocean has returned.

MARA: The tank is refilling on schedule.

TAG: Vex presses his ear to the glass. A bubble floats up; Nari points him toward the sign that says DO NOT TAP.
