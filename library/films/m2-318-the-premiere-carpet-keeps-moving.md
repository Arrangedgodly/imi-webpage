# The Premiere Carpet Keeps Moving

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: premiere red carpets, celebrity arrival choreography, and publicity crews who measure success by camera flashes*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
EVENT DIRECTOR TAVI, a gibbon
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR SURI, a macaque
PHOTOGRAPHER LUMA, a lemur
PUBLICIST ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. EXT. CINEMA ENTRANCE - AFTERNOON

A short red carpet runs from a painted arch to the theater doors. Event director Tavi tests each corner with a boot. Publicist Odo places a velvet rope around it. Actors Pip and Suri arrive in costume for the premiere of their film about a ferry that never leaves port.

ODO: Stop at the first mark. Wave at the photographers. Stop at the second mark. Wave at the same photographers from a new angle.

PIP: Are there other photographers?

LUMA: I'm the only one, and I'm standing here.

Odo asks her to change positions so the flash will look like a crowd. Tavi points out that the carpet is only six steps long. Odo says the camera frame can make six steps feel historic. Suri asks where the entrance is. Odo points to the far end, behind them.

SCENE 2. EXT. CARPET - LATER

The actors begin their walk. Pip turns toward Luma. Suri pauses to wave. The carpet shifts beneath them; one corner slides across the wet pavement. Tavi halts the event and rolls it back into place. Odo asks for a second take with slower walking.

TAVI: It is not attached.

ODO: It needs to flow.

TAVI: It needs tape.

Odo adds a ribbon of tape under the carpet. Pip and Suri walk again. Their shoes stick at the tape seam. They perform a graceful little hop to get free. Luma gets a sharp frame of the hop and asks whether that is the film's dance number. Pip says there is no dance number. Odo says there is now a dance number, if they repeat it three times.

The actors repeat the hop. The carpet rolls forward with them, pulling the velvet rope. A stanchion tips gently into Tavi's arms. He catches it and requests that the camera keep rolling. Odo calls it an unscripted arrival moment.

SCENE 3. EXT. CARPET - SUNSET

Odo asks the actors to pose beside a step-and-repeat wall. It lists the film's title, but the logo is so large it covers half the backdrop. Luma frames a photograph; the actors' faces are hidden by the word FERRY.

SURI: We are in the movie.

ODO: You are behind the movie.

Tavi moves the backdrop a little. The carpet rolls again, tugging its weighted base. Pip and Suri end up standing in front of the theater doors, accidentally blocking the entrance. Guests queue behind them. Odo asks them to hold the pose for one more flash.

GUEST: Is this the line to get inside?

PIP: It was supposed to be the carpet.

SURI: We are waiting for the premiere too.

The guests settle into a second line. Odo sees a crowd forming and asks Luma for a wide photograph. Tavi says the crowd is waiting to enter, not to admire the actors. Odo suggests that the guests wave. One guest lifts a ticket; another points at the door.

SCENE 4. INT. CINEMA LOBBY - NIGHT

Tavi unrolls the carpet toward the lobby, anchoring each corner with sandbags. The guests step inside. Odo wants the actors to make a second pass. Pip and Suri are now holding the theater doors open, letting everyone through. Luma photographs them from inside the lobby; the shot shows the cast helping their audience instead of staring at cameras.

ODO: We need the arrival picture.

TAVI: They arrived.

ODO: The picture needs the carpet.

SURI: The carpet is under the line.

Odo asks the guests to exit and re-enter for a final shot. The first guest declines and takes a seat. The others follow. Odo looks at the shrinking queue, then at the empty carpet. Tavi brings a small runner from the lobby so the actors can walk without moving the full roll. It ends precisely at the auditorium door.

PIP: Where do we wave?

LUMA: At the people watching the film.

SCENE 5. INT. AUDITORIUM - LATER

The premiere ends. The audience applauds the ferry comedy. Odo studies Luma's pictures. The best image shows Pip and Suri holding the doors while the carpet bunches harmlessly behind them. He asks for one more shot, but now the audience has begun to leave. Tavi rolls the carpet back into its case. It winds around Odo's shoes, carrying him slowly toward the lobby.

ODO: Don't let it take me before the encore.

TAVI: It is a carpet, not a ferry.

Pip and Suri hold the case open. Odo steps free and bows to the guests. They applaud, mostly because he is standing beside the exit.

FINAL IMAGE: The red carpet is safely rolled in its case, with Odo's publicist badge caught at the very end like a tiny premiere pennant.
