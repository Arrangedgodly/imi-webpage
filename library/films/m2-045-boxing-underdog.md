# The Bell Between Rounds

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: boxing underdog movies, punishing training montages, and false comeback expectations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A small marmoset boxer enters a charity exhibition to save the local gym, but his coach's movie-inspired training plan clashes with the non-contact rules and a leaking roof.

Cast: Tiko, a marmoset novice; Coach Brass, a gorilla who loves old boxing films; Ria, a macaque referee; Dolo, Tiko's capuchin cousin and opponent; and Nana Vee, the gym owner.

SCENE 1. INT. GYM, MORNING

Brass hangs a poster reading NO PAIN, NO GLORY. Nana Vee replaces it with NO FEES, NO GYM. Tiko practices footwork around buckets catching roof leaks. Dolo arrives with two mouthguards.

COACH BRASS: The bell is your enemy.

RIA: The bell is a bell.

TIKO: What are the rules?

RIA: No contact. No dangerous moves. Keep it friendly.

Brass lowers the dramatic music. He asks if they can at least glare at each other. Tiko and Dolo glare, then laugh; they share a ride home.

SCENE 2. INT. GYM, LATER

Brass begins a training montage. Tiko runs up the stairs holding a towel. He shadowboxes beside a mirror and apologizes to his reflection. He skips rope, gets tangled, and turns the rope into a jump line for children. Brass calls for more intensity. Ria turns the music down so everyone can hear the timer.

COACH BRASS: One hundred punches!

RIA: No punching. Footwork.

COACH BRASS: One hundred steps, then.

Tiko steps around the buckets. Dolo practices blocking imaginary punches and blocks the snack cart, which rolls away without him noticing. Nana Vee returns the cart and asks them to stack towels.

SCENE 3. INT. GYM, AFTERNOON

Brass unveils a training chart with arrows, lightning bolts, and a drawing of Tiko standing on a mountain. Tiko asks where the mountain is. Brass points to the staircase. Nana Vee tells them to carry the buckets upstairs. Brass calls it a stair-climbing endurance test. Tiko says it is a leak-prevention test.

COACH BRASS: We train for the moment.

TIKO: The moment is right now.

They climb. Brass reaches the landing, out of breath, and asks if the montage can be filmed from a chair. Ria gives him the towel used to catch the drip.

Rain comes through the roof. Brass says the gym needs a dramatic victory. Nana Vee says it needs a roofer. Tiko suggests the exhibition proceeds as planned; the tickets are sold. Ria checks the ring ropes and finds one loose.

RIA: We stop until this is tied properly.

BRASS: The clock is ticking.

NANA VEE: The roof is dripping.

The cousins tie the rope with a towel. It holds. Brass announces a final training trial: run from one corner to the other without touching a bucket. Tiko succeeds. Brass adds six buckets. Tiko refuses, saying the drill now tests plumbing, not boxing. Nana Vee hands him the actual bucket list.

SCENE 4. INT. GYM, EVENING

The announcer holds the microphone near the bell. Brass asks for a tense stare-down. Dolo extends his hand; Tiko shakes it. The crowd cheers. Brass whispers that a handshake is not a comeback. Nana Vee hands him a raffle ticket roll and asks him to sell ten. He sells twelve by promising that every ticket has a chance to win a roof inspection.

The exhibition begins. The announcer introduces Tiko and Dolo as bitter rivals. They tap gloves, then whisper about whose turn it is to drive Nana Vee home. The bell rings. They circle without contact. Tiko slips on a wet patch and sits down. Dolo offers a hand. Ria pauses the match and dries the floor.

COACH BRASS: This is where the hero gets back up!

RIA: He is already up.

Tiko and Dolo demonstrate footwork, avoiding each other and the buckets. The crowd applauds. Nana Vee passes a raffle drum. Brass spins it instead of demanding a knockout. The winning ticket belongs to the roofer, who promises to inspect the building tomorrow.

SCENE 5. INT. GYM, LATER

The event reaches its fundraising goal. The roofer checks the ceiling and gives Nana Vee an estimate. Tiko and Dolo split the exhibition prize. Brass removes the old poster and hangs the gym schedule. He asks whether the event counts as a win. Ria says the gym is staying open and nobody was punched.

COACH BRASS: A very unusual victory.

NANA VEE: We can repeat it next year.

The roofer measures the ceiling while the last spectators help fold chairs. Brass tries to lead a victory chant, but everyone chants the closing-time cleaning list instead.

FINAL IMAGE: The bell rings for closing time. Tiko and Dolo both duck, then look at the roof. A drop lands in Brass's empty trophy cup. He calls it the first medal for maintenance.
