# The For Your Consideration Cut

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: awards campaigns, dramatic clip reels, and studios that turn a modest scene into a parade of prestige moments*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
EDITOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR ODO, a baboon
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
PUBLICIST LUMA, a lemur
DIRECTOR VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. EDIT ROOM - MORNING

Editor Nari scrubs through a quiet film about a community that repairs a footbridge. Actor Odo tightens one bolt while Pip holds the railing. Publicist Luma lays out a blank card.

LUMA: We need a clip that says "important performance."

VEX: The performance is important.

LUMA: The clip needs to say it.

Odo watches his close-up. He turns the wrench, pauses, and checks the bolt.

ODO: I am making sure it is tight.

LUMA: Can you make the pause more emotional?

ODO: It is a bolt.

Pip asks for the bridge shot. Luma says the campaign should show faces, not a structure. Vex asks Nari to find three moments: sacrifice, transformation and a look that could become a poster.

SCENE 2. INT. SCREENING ROOM - LATER

They build the reel. The first clip shows Odo losing his wrench in the grass. Luma calls it loss. The second shows Pip catching it. She calls it loyalty. The third shows the bolt turning. She asks for swelling music.

VEX: The bridge stands.

LUMA: Is there a tear?

PIP: The rain is starting.

Nari adds a close-up of the pair holding the railing while the crew carries boards across. The campaign cut replaces the work with a slow-motion shot of Odo looking over his shoulder. Luma loves it. Odo asks why he is looking back.

ODO: I was checking the plank.

LUMA: The audience can decide.

Vex adds a black card: "One performance. One bridge. One moment." Pip asks if that sentence belongs to the film. Luma says it belongs to the campaign.

SCENE 3. INT. EDIT ROOM - AFTERNOON

The studio asks for an even stronger reel. Luma wants a reaction shot where Odo cries. Nari reviews every frame. Odo is not crying; rain is running down his face. Luma asks for a tighter crop. The shot removes the bridge and shows only Odo's wet cheek.

PIP: He has rain in his eye.

LUMA: It reads as grief.

ODO: It stings.

Nari refuses to misrepresent the scene. She tries a different clip: Pip holds the plank while Odo tightens the bolt. Luma says it lacks a star turn. Vex asks if they can call the plank a symbol. Odo says it is a plank in the bridge. Nari keeps the real shot and removes the music.

Luma complains the reel now feels small. Nari says the movie is small. It follows a crew doing one job well. Vex asks if the awards form accepts that. Luma finds the category: "Ensemble Achievement." The scene belongs to both actors.

SCENE 3A. INT. EDIT ROOM - LATE AFTERNOON

Luma brings a second request from the awards campaign. The clip must end with an emotional close-up and a short sentence for the voting card. Nari searches the footage. The only close-up is Pip looking down at the bridge rail while Odo tightens the bolt.

LUMA: What is he feeling?

PIP: I am checking the rail.

ODO: I am checking the bolt.

Luma suggests adding a voiceover about sacrifice. Vex tries one: "We gave everything to cross." The line is too large for the quiet footage. Nari removes it. Pip points to the actual sound: the wrench turns, the bolt stops, and the crew tests the railing together. Luma asks if the sound can be louder. Odo places the wrench on the table; the clank is deafening. They lower it to the floor and keep the natural click of the bolt.

SCENE 4. INT. CAMPAIGN OFFICE - EVENING

Luma prints a card with Odo's face on it. Pip's shoulder appears at the edge. Odo asks if the campaign will credit Pip. Luma enlarges the frame, but it cuts out Odo. She tries a split screen. One side shows Odo turning the wrench; the other shows Pip holding the plank. Together, the action makes sense.

VEX: Two performances.

LUMA: One clip.

NARI: One scene.

They add a plain caption: "A bridge repair, completed by a team." Luma asks whether the word "team" sounds too ordinary. Odo hands her the wrench. It is still damp from the rain. She sets the card down and helps dry it.

SCENE 5. INT. EDIT ROOM - NIGHT

The finished campaign reel shows the bridge, the two actors, and the repair. It has no slow motion and no false tears. Luma watches it once, then sends it. Odo and Pip return to the bridge to check the railing.

FINAL IMAGE: The campaign card sits beneath the wrench, with both actors visible only when the frame is wide.
