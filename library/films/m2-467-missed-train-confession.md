# The Missed Train Confession

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic breakup dramas that save an honest conversation for the last train departure and rely on a last-second sprint*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: Two monkeys believe their last train is leaving without them, but a station closure gives them time to finish the conversation they have postponed all evening.

Cast: Lira, a macaque musician; Venn, a gibbon baker; station clerk Pomo, a capuchin; announcer Seli, a lemur; porter Daro, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. TRAIN STATION - EVENING

Lira stands beside a suitcase and a violin case. Venn holds a paper bag with two pastries. A departure board reads PLATFORM CHANGE: TRAIN DELAYED.

LIRA: I thought you were leaving at eight.

VENN: I thought you were leaving at eight.

POMO: The last train is delayed by a signal issue. It will depart from Platform Three after clearance.

Lira and Venn look toward the stairs. The departure chime sounds. Both start running.

SCENE 2 - INT. PLATFORM THREE - CONTINUOUS

They reach the platform. A maintenance gate is closed. The train is not there. Seli announces that service is temporarily halted.

SELI: Passengers should remain in the waiting area. The train has not arrived.

VENN: We ran for a train that is not here.

LIRA: The music in my head made it sound final.

Daro pushes a cart of luggage across the platform. One pastry slips from Venn's bag and rolls beneath the bench. Daro retrieves it with a long-handled grabber.

SCENE 2A - INT. PLATFORM THREE - CONTINUOUS

Lira finds a folded note tucked in the violin case. Venn recognizes the paper bag it came in, but not the note. The wind from the ventilation fan lifts it toward the tracks. Daro uses the grabber to catch it before it reaches the edge.

DARO: Please keep loose paper inside the waiting room.

LIRA: I wrote this before the station. I never gave it to you.

VENN: Is it a goodbye note?

LIRA: It is a list of what I wanted to say.

Venn begins reading, but Lira gently takes it back.

LIRA: I can say it myself.

Pomo opens the maintenance gate after checking the platform is clear. The signal board still reads HOLD.

POMO: You can use the waiting area. The train has not been cleared.

VENN: We have been treating every announcement like a final scene.

LIRA: Maybe we should wait until there is actually a train.

SCENE 3 - INT. WAITING AREA - MOMENTS LATER

Venn and Lira sit at opposite ends of a bench. Venn opens the pastry bag. One pastry is crushed. Lira opens her violin case. The bow has come loose but is unbroken.

VENN: I was leaving because I thought you wanted space.

LIRA: I wanted you to ask me directly instead of deciding for me.

VENN: I kept waiting for a dramatic moment.

LIRA: The platform is not a stage.

Pomo brings two cups of water and the updated schedule. The train will be delayed at least forty minutes.

POMO: You have time to sit. There is no need to run.

SCENE 3A - INT. WAITING AREA - CONTINUOUS

Venn offers Lira the uncrushed pastry. She shakes her head at first, then takes it and breaks it in half. The bag has a printed label from Venn's bakery.

LIRA: You baked these today?

VENN: I made them for the station kiosk. I brought two because I thought we could talk.

LIRA: Then why did you not say that?

VENN: I was trying to make the last train do the talking for me.

Lira unfolds her note and reads only the first line.

LIRA: I was angry that you accepted the tour dates for my shows without asking me first.

VENN: I thought you wanted me to stay in the city.

LIRA: I wanted to choose where I performed.

Pomo returns with the service notice and offers them seats away from the platform draft. They move to the warmer side of the room.

SCENE 4 - INT. STATION CAFE - LATER

Venn and Lira share the uncrushed pastry. Venn apologizes for leaving without saying goodbye. Lira says she will be performing across town and could use a ride for the violin case.

VENN: I can carry it to the exit. I do not need to decide what it means.

LIRA: That is a start.

Seli's voice announces a further delay. The couple laugh at the announcement, not at each other.

SCENE 5 - INT. STATION PLATFORM - NIGHT

The train arrives. Venn is not boarding; he has agreed to walk Lira to the street exit. Pomo returns the grabber and points them toward the stairs. Daro lifts the suitcase onto a luggage cart.

VENN: I am not leaving tonight.

LIRA: I am not asking you to.

They walk side by side. The train doors close behind them. Neither turns to watch.

FINAL IMAGE: The station clock advances as the platform empties, while the two walk toward the exit at an ordinary pace carrying one pastry bag and one violin case.
