# The Siege of the Shared Washroom

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: historical war epics that frame routine resource-sharing and long negotiations as a glorious siege*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Two primate departments stage a grand siege over a shared washroom schedule, until the janitor points out that both groups can use the sink.

Cast: Commander Rakka, a baboon; Captain Timo, a macaque; Scout Vela, a lemur; Quartermaster Oru, a gibbon; Janitor Kesi, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. OFFICE HALL - MORNING

A red cloth hangs over one end of the hallway. A blue cloth hangs over the other. Rakka peers around the corner with a cardboard telescope.

RAKKA: The washroom door is held by the blue division.

TIMO: We only need the sink to rinse the paint from our hands.

RAKKA: A request for the sink is how a siege begins.

TIMO: We have a sign-up sheet. Your division booked ten to ten fifteen.

RAKKA: We booked the room, not the sink.

Vela carries a stack of paper maps. They unfold and cover the floor.

VELA: The old chart places the basin on neutral ground.

ORU: That is a plumbing diagram. The room is shared by everybody.

Rakka signals a drumroll. Oru taps a lunch tray with a spoon. The echo makes both groups duck.

SCENE 2 - INT. SUPPLY CLOSET - LATER

The commanders meet over a mop bucket as if it were a treaty table. Oru draws a schedule on the wall with washable marker.

ORU: Group A uses the room from nine to nine fifteen. Group B takes the next slot. Everyone else can use it between reservations.

RAKKA: We demand a buffer zone.

TIMO: The hallway is the buffer zone.

RAKKA: We require a flag.

TIMO: The sign already says WASHROOM.

Rakka unfurls a huge banner. It covers the fire-exit symbol.

VELA: Your banner is blocking the emergency route.

RAKKA: A banner is not an army.

ORU: Neither are these departments. Please take it down.

Timo rolls the banner up. It catches on the closet latch. The door opens, spilling paper towels across the floor like a snowstorm.

SCENE 3 - INT. WASHROOM - CONTINUOUS

Kesi mops the floor. The two groups stand outside the open door, both holding their hands away from the handle.

KESI: I need to clean the room. Move back, please.

RAKKA: We will yield this ground under protest.

TIMO: We are not contesting the ground. We are waiting for the floor to dry.

Kesi sets a yellow sign by the entrance. Rakka tries to place the red cloth beside it, but the cloth drags through the wet patch.

KESI: Now I have to wash the cloth too.

RAKKA: The banner has been captured.

KESI: The banner has been mopped.

Vela lifts one end. A stream of water falls into the bucket.

VELA: It is losing its colors.

ORU: We can stop the performance and let Kesi finish.

SCENE 4 - INT. OFFICE BREAK AREA - MIDDAY

The departments gather around a table. The schedule is printed and taped beside the door. Rakka has drawn battle arrows over it in pencil.

TIMO: The room was free at eleven. We both used the sink and left it clean.

RAKKA: We did so under a truce.

ORU: You both washed paint off your hands. That was the purpose.

RAKKA: The chronicles will record our restraint.

KESI: The chronicles can record that no one put muddy boots on the floor.

Rakka sees the floor is clean and the banner lies folded on a chair.

RAKKA: I shall declare a monument.

TIMO: We have a paper towel dispenser.

RAKKA: A monument made of paper towels would be temporary.

KESI: It would also be wasteful.

SCENE 5 - INT. OFFICE HALL - AFTERNOON

The groups each take their scheduled slot. They use the sink, wash brushes and leave the room tidy. Rakka stands guard beside the schedule, still wearing a cardboard helmet.

RAKKA: The red division may enter.

TIMO: We are a design team. The colors are for our poster.

RAKKA: Your poster is the treaty?

TIMO: It is a sign that says, "Please wipe the sink." 

Rakka studies it, then makes a small salute. Kesi hangs the poster straight, replacing the tattered banner.

KESI: There. The room is ready for everyone.

Rakka removes the cardboard telescope, which has a paper towel tube taped to it.

RAKKA: I concede the basin without surrendering the epic.

ORU: You can still tell the story at lunch.

Rakka begins to drum on the tray. Kesi points to the schedule.

KESI: Quiet hours until one.

Rakka lowers the tray. Timo gives him a clean brush.

FINAL IMAGE: The new sign hangs above a spotless sink while the red and blue cloths dry side by side on the same laundry line.
