# The River Race with Every Stop

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: boat-racing adventures, dramatic shortcuts, rival crews, and finish lines moved by a tide chart*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
CAPTAIN LUMA, a lemur
ROWER PIP, a capuchin
NAVIGATOR ODO, an orangutan
RIVAL VEX, a macaque
HARBOR MASTER NARI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. RIVER DOCK - DAWN

Two crews prepare for the annual river race. The course has six checkpoints, each marked by a buoy. Captain Luma checks the tide chart. Rival Vex polishes his boat and says he will take the shortcut through the mill channel. Harbor Master Nari says the channel is closed for repairs.

VEX: Then it will be an unexpected shortcut.

NARI: It will be a closed channel.

The starting horn sounds. Odo unfolds a map that is twice as wide as the boat. Pip uses it as a sail.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIVER - MORNING

At the first buoy, Luma's crew rings the checkpoint bell and turns downstream. Vex rings it four times, hoping the sound will count as four checkpoints. Nari radios him that each marker must be passed in order. He swings around the buoy again, then heads the wrong way. His crew points to the course arrows painted on the bank. Vex corrects course and claims he was testing the current.

Luma's boat reaches the second checkpoint. The bell is tied to a low branch, and Pip must duck to ring it. Odo checks the tide chart before they turn. A floating leaf passes their boat, then Vex's, then loops back upstream in an eddy. Vex follows it, thinking it marks a current shortcut, and loses several minutes.

SCENE 2B. EXT. RIVER - MORNING

Vex emerges from the eddy downstream of Luma, still insisting the leaf was a course marker. His crew points to the bank arrows and asks him to choose a direction they can all defend. Luma's boat approaches a floating log near the second checkpoint. Pip hooks it with an oar and guides the hull clear while Odo calls the safe side.

PIP: That log was not on the map.

ODO: The river moves.

Vex shouts that he will catch up and takes the mill channel, where a barrier rope blocks the route. His crew ties up beside the sign reading CLOSED FOR REPAIRS. Vex asks if the sign is part of the challenge. Nari radios that it is a sign, then heads out in the marshal launch to direct them back to the course.

SCENE 2C. EXT. RIVERBANK - LATE MORNING

A sudden gust pushes the boats toward reeds. Luma asks the rowers to stop and use their paddles to keep the hulls parallel to shore. Vex orders his crew to row harder. The boat turns in a circle, then catches a reed stalk across its bow. His crew pulls it free. Nari reaches them in the marshal launch and asks whether they need a tow. Vex says he can continue, but accepts a line to guide the boat back into the marked channel.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVER BEND - MIDDAY

Luma's boat arrives at the third checkpoint, where a volunteer has placed the buoy on the wrong side of the bend. Odo compares the chart and signals to circle the correct marker. Vex catches up after being released from the mill channel. He accuses Luma of taking the wrong route. Nari confirms the buoy was misplaced and resets it.

The race pauses while the course is checked. The crews eat lunch on the riverbank. Vex continues rowing in place, moving his boat three feet into the mud. Pip suggests that they wait for the marshal. Vex says waiting is not racing. The marshal arrives and pulls the boat free.

SCENE 4. EXT. FINISH REACH - AFTERNOON

The race resumes. The tide has turned, and the finish buoy has moved downstream from where it was at dawn. Nari confirms its new position on the chart. Vex heads for the old finish line and rings the bell. Nobody else follows. He turns around, embarrassed, and rejoins the course.

Luma and Vex reach the final stretch side by side. Instead of sprinting, they steer around a family of ducks crossing the water. Odo calls out a slow count. The ducks cross safely. The two boats arrive at the finish together.

SCENE 5. EXT. RIVER DOCK - EVENING

Nari declares a tie. Vex asks if the finish line can be moved back for a rematch. Nari shows him the tide chart and says the line will be where the river is safe. Pip helps unload the boats. Odo folds his map and discovers it has been holding a sandwich against the bottom of the hull.

The crews share the sandwich while the boats dry. Vex admits the shortcut was closed. Luma offers to help him read the chart before next year's race.

FINAL IMAGE: The finish buoy drifts downstream with the evening current while the racers row together toward the dock.
