# The Market in One Take

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: long tracking shots, elaborate camera choreography, and directors who call ordinary obstacles part of the composition*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A director attempts a single unbroken walk through a market, but every stall has a real customer and the camera cart keeps getting stuck.

Cast: Sela, a macaque director; Koro, a gorilla camera operator; Nima, a lemur market vendor; and Puck, a capuchin actor.

SCENE 1. EXT. MARKET ENTRANCE, MORNING

Sela explains the shot: Puck enters the market, buys a pear, follows a chalk line, and exits by the river gate. Koro pushes the camera rig. Nima asks them to keep the route clear for customers.

SELA: It has to be one take.

NIMA: The market is open.

Puck starts walking. He reaches the first stall and steps around a customer. Koro pivots the rig, clips a basket, and stops before it falls. Sela calls cut. Nima gathers the basket while Koro backs the rig away.

SELA: The camera cannot stop in the middle.

KORO: The basket was in the middle.

NIMA: The basket is how I carry the goods you keep nearly filming onto the ground.

Sela asks for a second pass with the basket placed outside the composition. Nima puts it beside the route. A customer picks it up to buy eggs. Koro pans after the customer before remembering Puck is still walking.

SCENE 2. EXT. MARKET AISLE, LATER

Koro marks a wider route with tape. Nima moves one crate to make room. A customer asks if the stall is still open. Nima serves them while Puck waits on his mark.

PUCK: Do I pretend not to see them?

SELA: They are not actors.

The shot begins again. Puck buys a pear. Nima hands it to him. Koro tracks backward down the aisle. A delivery cart arrives from the far end. Koro stops; Puck keeps walking and reaches the cart first.

KORO: The cart has blocked the river gate.

SELA: Keep rolling.

Nima redirects the cart through the side lane. The camera follows it, leaving Puck alone in the frame. Sela calls cut and asks why the shot has become about the cart. Koro shows her the monitor: the camera follows the cart, Puck disappears, and a child walks through frame holding a paper bag over their head.

SELA: That is not our lead actor.

NIMA: That is my nephew protecting his lunch from rain.

The child lifts the bag. A plum rolls down the aisle. Puck picks it up and offers it to Nima. She returns it to the customer, who pays. Sela asks if they can repeat the moment. Nima says the plum is sold. Sela studies the footage again. She says the customer's wave could serve as a natural transition. Koro asks if the customer knows they are in the film. Nima points out that the customer is now asking for their change. Sela steps into frame to return the coins and becomes the only person who notices the lens.

SCENE 3. EXT. MARKET, AFTERNOON

The market quiets between deliveries. Nima suggests using the real open lane instead of the taped route. Sela checks it with Koro. The camera can pass the stall without blocking customers. Puck rehearses the route at walking speed.

Puck reaches the pear stall. Nima hands him a pear. He takes out a coin. Nima says the film is not paying for the fruit, so he should use the prop pear. Puck reaches into the prop basket. It is empty; a child has borrowed the rubber pear as a toy. Sela asks if they can use a real pear. Nima says yes, if they pay for it. Puck holds up the production's two coins; Koro needs one for the camera cart's squeaky wheel. Sela chooses the rubber pear and asks the child to return it after play.

Sela asks whether they can pause and reset. Koro says a single take can start again. Nima retrieves the rubber pear from the child with permission and wipes it clean. They mark the start and move the camera rig carefully through the open lane.

SCENE 4. EXT. MARKET RIVER GATE, EVENING

The shot runs. Puck enters, buys the prop pear, follows the route, and exits by the river gate. Koro keeps the rig level. A real customer crosses behind them and waves. Sela does not call cut. The customer is part of the market.

The crew watches the take on the camera screen. It shows the stalls working, the cart moving, and Puck leaving with the pear. Sela asks whether the crossing customer ruins the composition. Nima says the market was open. Sela keeps the shot.

FINAL IMAGE: Koro parks the camera rig outside the aisle. Puck hands the rubber pear back to the prop box before the next customer arrives.
