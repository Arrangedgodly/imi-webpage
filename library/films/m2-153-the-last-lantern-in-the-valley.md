# The Last Lantern in the Valley

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: adventure films that treat every practical delay as an epic trial on a dangerous expedition*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: Three explorers carry one lantern across a valley to light a signal tower. A storm, a narrow bridge, and one missing wick turn their heroic quest into an argument about packing lists and fuel.

Cast: Explorer Aro, a macaque; guide Luma, a gibbon; cartographer Venn, a capuchin; and caretaker Pavi, a baboon.

SCENE 1. EXT. VALLEY RIDGE, DUSK

Aro raises a brass lantern against the sunset. Luma checks the path below. Venn unfolds a map.

ARO: The signal tower is beyond the valley. We must reach it before night.

LUMA: It is one hill away.

VENN: The map says three.

LUMA: The map was drawn from above.

Aro tries to light the lantern. The wick is missing.

ARO: We have a problem.

VENN: We have a quest.

LUMA: We have forgotten the wick.

SCENE 2. EXT. STONE BRIDGE, CONTINUOUS

The explorers approach a narrow bridge. Wind moves the ropes.

VENN: No one has crossed this bridge in years.

PAVI: I cross it every morning to check the lantern tower.

ARO: You are our guide?

PAVI: I am the caretaker.

LUMA: Then you know if it is safe.

PAVI: I know the center plank is loose.

Aro steps onto the bridge. The plank shifts. He returns to the path.

ARO: The bridge has challenged us.

PAVI: The bridge needs a nail.

Venn searches his pack and finds a small hammer, no nails, and three copies of the map.

VENN: We can use the corner of a map.

LUMA: Paper is not a nail.

SCENE 3. EXT. BRIDGE, MOMENTS LATER

Pavi wedges a flat stone beneath the loose plank. Luma tests it with her foot. The bridge holds.

ARO: The path is open.

VENN: We have conquered the crossing.

PAVI: We have supported the plank.

They cross one at a time. Venn carries the maps. A gust lifts them. Luma catches one. Aro catches another with his tail.

ARO: The map nearly escaped.

VENN: It was trying to find the tower.

SCENE 4. EXT. SIGNAL TOWER, NIGHT

The group reaches the tower. Pavi unlocks the door. Inside is a shelf of spare wicks.

PAVI: We keep supplies here.

ARO: The final challenge is to choose the right wick.

LUMA: The lantern is small.

Venn chooses a thick wick. It does not fit. Aro tries a thin one. It fits, but burns low.

PAVI: Use the middle one.

ARO: The middle path.

LUMA: It is a wick.

Pavi threads it into the lantern. The flame catches. The signal tower glows.

SCENE 4A. EXT. TOWER PATH, NIGHT

Wind rises. The explorers reach a fork where the map's ink has run from the earlier rain. Aro holds the lantern while Venn tries to compare the map with the ridge.

VENN: The tower is either uphill or on the map's wet side.

LUMA: The wind is coming from the tower.

PAVI: We can hear the bell rope.

[THEY LISTEN. A DISTANT METAL CLINK SOUNDS.]

ARO: The signal is calling us.

PAVI: It is a loose latch.

Venn folds the map to cover the wet ink. The fold hides the fork.

VENN: I have made the map smaller.

LUMA: You have also hidden the path.

Pavi points out a line of stones set beside the correct trail. Aro uses the lantern to check each one. The group moves forward carefully. At the next turn, the path opens to the tower.

VENN: The stones were better directions than the map.

PAVI: They are there for nights like this.

LUMA: We should add them to the next map.

[THEY CONTINUE TO THE TOWER.]
PAVI: The tower can be checked tomorrow.

SCENE 5. EXT. VALLEY RIDGE, LATER

The explorers look back across the valley. The tower light shines steadily.

ARO: We have delivered the light.

PAVI: You have brought back the lantern.

VENN: The map can show the bridge repair.

LUMA: Mark the loose plank and the stone support.

Venn draws on the map. The paper tears along the fold.

VENN: The record has suffered.

PAVI: It still shows the path.

Aro packs the lantern. A small monkey removes the wick, leaving the lamp dark.

ARO: The light has gone out.

LUMA: We are back where we started.

PAVI: We need a spare wick.

Venn opens the map case. It contains three more maps and no wick.

VENN: The next expedition will be better prepared.

Luma hands him the spare wick from her pocket.

LUMA: This expedition can finish first.

She relights the lantern. They walk back down the hill together.
