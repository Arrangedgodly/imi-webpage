# The Snowplow and the Swan

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster road movies, stranded towns, heroic convoy shots, and a storm that delays the rescue by a single snowbank*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
RHEA, a macaque snowplow driver
KETT, a capuchin radio host
NARI, an orangutan weather observer
PIP, a squirrel monkey courier
SWAN, a large local bird

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN ROAD - DAWN

Snow covers the pass. A line of delivery carts is stuck behind one small drift. Rhea climbs down from a snowplow and tests the road with a shovel. A swan stands in the middle of the lane, guarding a nest under the guardrail.

RHEA: I can clear the road when it moves.

PIP: How long will that take?

NARI: The storm ends in twenty minutes.

Kett's radio announces that the pass is impassable. The plow's alarm beeps because the fuel tank is low. The radio cuts to dramatic music.

SCENE 2. INT. PLOW CAB - MORNING

Before leaving the drift, Nari checks the snow depth at several points. The plow can clear the road, but the shoulder is soft and cannot support the carts. Kett asks her to repeat the measurement for the broadcast. She does, using the same pole and same depth. He adds a thunder sound from his portable speaker. The speaker is swallowed by static.

KETT: The storm is not cooperating with the story.

RHEA: The storm does not know we are filming.

Pip's delivery cart has a loose axle. Rhea uses a strap from the plow to stabilize it. Pip asks if it will hold for the climb. Rhea says it will hold if he walks beside it and keeps the weight low. He moves the medicine to the lower shelf. The swan watches from its nest and hisses when Kett comes too close with the camera. Kett backs away and uses a long lens.

SCENE 2A. INT. PLOW CAB - CONTINUOUS

Rhea studies the route map. It shows a deep ravine where the road was rerouted last year. Kett wants to send the convoy through the old route because it makes a better broadcast. Nari says the old route is closed. Pip has a parcel of medicine for the village clinic and asks whether he can walk the rest of the way.

RHEA: Not while we cannot see the edge.

PIP: Then what do we do?

NARI: We wait for the snow to stop and keep the engine warm.

The radio voiceover calls this a battle against time. Kett holds his microphone to the plow window. The swan pecks the windshield wiper, and it begins moving in slow motion. Rhea turns it off before the bird scratches the glass.

SCENE 3. EXT. MOUNTAIN ROAD - LATER

The storm eases. Rhea clears the small drift, then approaches the swan on foot with a bag of grain. The bird walks to the roadside and keeps its nest out of the plow's path. Nari places two markers to show where the road narrows. Kett films the plow's headlights as if it were a rescue fleet entering enemy territory.

The first cart moves forward. Its wheels spin on the icy slope. Pip ties a rope from the cart to the plow. Rhea pulls slowly. The cart moves a few feet, then stops when its brake catches. Pip climbs aboard and releases it. The convoy starts again, one cart at a time.

SCENE 4. EXT. PASS SUMMIT - AFTERNOON

A second snowbank lies ahead. Rhea lowers the plow and clears it in two passes. Kett describes the moment as an impossible obstacle. Nari checks the road depth with a pole and says it is less than the plow's blade. The vehicles roll past without a dramatic plunge.

The swan follows along the guardrail. Pip offers it grain. It takes the bag and waddles back toward the nest. The convoy reaches the village clinic. The package is delivered before the medicine is needed. Pip gets the clinic to sign the receipt, while Kett films an exhausted handshake with the plow.

SCENE 5. EXT. VILLAGE CLINIC - EVENING

The villagers gather to thank Rhea. She says the plow did its job and the crew waited until the weather was safe. Kett asks if they can call it a daring rescue. Nari says they can call it snow removal. Pip gives the remaining grain to the swan's nest area.

Rhea checks the fuel gauge. It is low, but the plow is safely parked. She tells Kett the radio can announce that the pass is open. He makes the announcement without music. The convoy disperses to its destinations.

FINAL IMAGE: Fresh tire tracks curve around the swan's nest, and the bird steps onto the cleared road after the last cart has passed.
