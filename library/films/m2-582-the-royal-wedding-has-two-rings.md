# The Royal Wedding Has Two Rings

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: period romances that build a mistaken jewelry handoff into a succession crisis*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
BRIDE NARI, a lemur
ROYAL HOST VEX, an orangutan
JEWELER MARA, a macaque
MESSENGER PIP, a capuchin
REGISTRAR ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE PREPARATION ROOM - MORNING

Two small boxes sit on a dressing table. Vex holds a ring box over his head.

VEX: The crown jewels have been stolen.

MARA: One box holds the ceremony ring; the other holds a costume ring for the play afterward.

PIP: I carried both from the jeweler. The labels face down.

ODO: The inventory lists two rings, both present.

NARI: The ceremony begins in an hour. Please find the correct one.

SCENE 2. INT. PREPARATION ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mara turns the boxes over. One label reads CEREMONY, the other THEATER.

VEX: A double-cross.

MARA: A double box. The jewelry is not missing.

PIP: I put the boxes down in the order the jeweler handed them to me.

ODO: Inventory says the real ring has a small blue stone. We can verify with the signed description.

NARI: I don't want to see it until the ceremony.

MARA: Then Odo will check the description without opening the ceremony box. The label is enough.

SCENE 2A. INT. JEWELER'S COUNTER - DAY

Mara opens the inventory ledger but leaves both boxes closed. Odo compares the labels to the jeweler's receipt.

ODO: Ceremony ring, blue stone, one box. Costume ring, brass finish, one box. Both delivered this morning.

VEX: The kingdom's fate rests on these two labels.

MARA: The labels are on the boxes. We can keep them face up and take them to the correct rooms.

PIP: The ceremony box belongs with Odo, the registrar. I'll carry the costume box to the theater.

NARI: Please don't show me the ceremony ring before the vows. I asked for it to stay closed.

Vex lifts one box, then sets it down on the marked tray when Mara points to the handling instructions.

MARA: Keep the box level. Don't stack anything on it.

ODO: The inventory is signed. I will take the ceremony ring to the registrar's table and keep it in my custody until the ceremony begins.

PIP: I'll deliver the costume box to the stage manager and get a receipt.

SCENE 2B. INT. THEATER WING - DAY

Pip places the labeled costume box on the stage table. He asks the stage manager to sign the receipt, then returns to the hall without opening it.

VEX: The other ring has vanished from the plot.

PIP: It is exactly where the theater needs it.

MARA: The two paths are separate. No one is carrying a ring now.

SCENE 2C. INT. PALACE PREPARATION ROOM - LATER

The ceremony hall is being arranged. Mara places a plain card beside the registrar's table: CEREMONY RING STAYS WITH ODO. Vex tries to replace it with a scroll declaring a royal jewel emergency.

MARA: The guests need directions, not a crisis notice.

VEX: A wedding without peril is merely a meeting with flowers.

NARI: We have flowers. We also have a timetable. The registrar must be at the hall before the music begins.

ODO: I have the ceremony ring box. It stays closed until the vows. I will carry it directly to my table.

PIP: The costume ring is already at the theater. The stage manager signed for it.

Vex asks Pip to retrieve it so both rings can be presented together. Pip points to the separate delivery receipt and refuses to leave the theater unattended.

PIP: The play starts after the wedding. Its prop stays with its crew.

VEX: Two rings, two destinies, no convenient reunion.

MARA: Correct. That is what labels are for.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE HALL - LATER

Vex stands at the hall entrance, announcing each guest as if entering a throne room. Pip stands beside the theater doorway; the sealed costume ring remains on the stage table.

VEX: The royal ring is leaving the palace.

PIP: This is the costume ring. It goes to the stage table after the wedding.

ODO: The ceremony ring remains with the registrar.

MARA: Keep the boxes separate. The theater door is on the left; the ceremony hall is on the right.

Vex steps between them and blocks both paths.

NARI: Please move. The guests are arriving.

SCENE 4. INT. CEREMONY HALL - DAY

Odo presents the correct ring box to Nari. She opens it only when the registrar nods. The ring matches the signed description.

NARI: This is the ceremony ring.

VEX: The succession is safe.

MARA: The wedding can begin.

PIP: The costume ring is on the theater table, still in its labeled box.

TAG: Vex announces the couple. Pip quietly labels his own lunch box CEREMONY to keep him from confusing it with the catering tray.
