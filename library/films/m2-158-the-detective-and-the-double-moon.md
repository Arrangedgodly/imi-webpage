# The Detective and the Double Moon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: noir detective films whose hard-boiled narration misreads a harmless reflection as a conspiracy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A private detective investigates a second moon appearing above the village pond. The mystery grows darker with every voice-over until a young monkey tilts the water bowl.

Cast: Detective Miro, a macaque; client Pavi, a gibbon; inspector Rook, a baboon; and witness Tavi, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. POND, NIGHT

Miro stands under a tree in a raincoat. He narrates.

MIRO (V.O.): The night had two moons and one too many questions.

Pavi points to the pond. The moon reflects in the water.

PAVI: There it is.

MIRO: Who put it there?

ROOK: The sky.

MIRO: The sky has an alibi.

SCENE 2. INT. VILLAGE OFFICE, NIGHT

Miro pins a sketch of the moon to a board.

MIRO: The reflection appears every night at the same time.

ROOK: That is when the moon is overhead.

MIRO: Convenient.

Tavi enters carrying a water bowl.

TAVI: The second moon is in this bowl too.

Miro looks. A second reflection appears.

MIRO: The conspiracy is portable.

ROOK: The bowl is clean.

SCENE 3. EXT. POND EDGE, LATER

Miro examines the mud and finds small handprints.

MIRO: The culprit has been near the water.

Pavi sees a young monkey peering into the pond.

PAVI: That is my nephew.

The monkey drops a pebble. Ripples distort the reflection.

MIRO: The moon is changing shape.

ROOK: The water is moving.

Miro follows the ripples as if they were a trail.

SCENE 4. INT. Miro's office, dawn

Miro writes a report with dramatic certainty.

MIRO: "Two moons observed. One likely counterfeit."

Rook takes the report.

ROOK: You should check the source.

Miro looks through the window. The real moon is setting.

MIRO: The first moon is leaving.

Tavi raises the bowl. The second moon moves with the water.

TAVI: This one moves with me.

MIRO: The suspect has confessed.

SCENE 4A. INT. VILLAGE OFFICE, DAWN

Miro pins the water bowl beside the moon sketch. Rook places a flashlight on the table.

ROOK: We can test whether the light source moves with the bowl.

Miro moves the flashlight. The bright circle moves on the wall.

MIRO: The light is cooperating with the suspect.

TAVI: It is reflecting.

Rook fills the bowl. The light spot wobbles. Miro watches it shrink as the water settles.

MIRO: The evidence is becoming calm.

ROOK: The bowl is still.

Miro takes the bowl outside and sets it beneath the open sky. The reflection of the moon is gone, but the sky brightens.

MIRO: We can compare the report with the morning.

He reads his note aloud.

MIRO: "One moon observed in sky and water. Water moon moved with bowl."

ROOK: That is a clearer report.

MIRO: It is less dramatic.

Tavi wipes the bowl and returns it to the shelf.

SCENE 4B. EXT. POND, DAWN

Miro asks Pavi to place the bowl at different distances from the pond.

MIRO: The second moon should follow the bowl if it is a reflection.

PAVI: It is a reflection.

Miro sets the bowl under a low branch. The bright spot appears on the branch's underside.

ROOK: We have put a moon in a tree.

Tavi moves the bowl to a patch of shade. The bright spot disappears.

MIRO: The culprit has hidden the moon.

TAVI: The water is shaded.

Miro removes a leaf from the bowl. The reflection returns.

MIRO: The evidence is visible again.

Rook writes the observations on a clean page.

ROOK: This is what an investigation looks like without a conspiracy.

MIRO: It still has a witness.

PAVI: The witness is a bowl. It has been here every morning.

MIRO: We can close the file now.

SCENE 5. EXT. POND, MORNING

Miro returns to the pond. The moon is gone. He asks the nephew how he made the reflection.

The monkey tilts the bowl toward the sun. A bright spot appears on the wall.

MIRO: The moon has changed addresses.

ROOK: It is a reflection.

MIRO: That is what it wants us to think.

Pavi takes the bowl and sets it flat.

PAVI: The mystery is over.

Miro folds his raincoat over one arm.

MIRO: Every case ends in daylight.

The young monkey lifts the bowl again. The bright spot returns, landing directly on Miro's report. The ink runs.

ROOK: The evidence has dissolved.

Miro looks at the wet report.

MIRO: We will have to start over.

PAVI: No.

She wipes the report and puts it in the office.

MIRO: The case is closed.

The monkey carries the empty bowl away.
