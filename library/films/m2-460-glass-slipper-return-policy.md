# The Glass Slipper Has a Return Policy

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fairy-tale romance adaptations that use a single lost shoe as proof of identity despite a very practical fit problem*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A palace search party hunts for the wearer of a glass slipper, while the kingdom's cobbler tries to explain that several guests borrowed the same size.

Cast: Prince Rumi, a macaque; cobbler Tavi, a gibbon; Ella, a capuchin; courier Mena, a lemur; royal herald Odo, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. PALACE BALLROOM - NIGHT

The music stops at midnight. Ella leaves a glass slipper on the stair. Prince Rumi picks it up while the guests hurry outside.

RUMI: I will find the person who wore this.

TAVI: Is there a maker's mark?

RUMI: There is a tiny number inside: six.

TAVI: That is a shoe size.

RUMI: It is a clue.

The herald announces that the prince will search the kingdom. Mena carries the slipper in a velvet box.

SCENE 2 - INT. VILLAGE COBBLER SHOP - MORNING

Rumi brings the slipper to Tavi. Several residents have gathered. Tavi measures the glass shoe with a wooden ruler.

TAVI: The fit is narrow. The heel is low.

RUMI: Then the wearer will be easy to identify.

A customer, Ella's neighbor, steps forward. She wears a size six and tries the slipper. It slides off.

NEIGHBOR: It is too narrow.

RUMI: The clue was false.

TAVI: The number is real. The conclusion is not.

SCENE 3 - INT. COBBLER SHOP - LATER

Mena checks the palace guest list. Three attendees wore size six shoes. One borrowed slippers from the costume room. Tavi notes that the glass slipper has a tiny chip on the sole.

TAVI: This is not safe for walking. It could cut someone.

RUMI: It survived the ball.

TAVI: The ball ended because the slipper fell off. That is not a test of durability.

Ella enters carrying a plain leather shoe. She recognizes the glass slipper and points to the chip.

ELLA: I borrowed it from the costume room. It was a prop. I left it on the stairs because it pinched.

RUMI: You were at the ball?

ELLA: I was carrying the stage lanterns. I am a theater carpenter.

SCENE 3A - INT. COBBLER SHOP - CONTINUOUS

Mena brings the three size-six guests into a line. Rumi asks each to stand on a rug. Tavi refuses to let anyone put on the glass slipper.

RUMI: We need to confirm the fit.

TAVI: We have confirmed it is unsafe.

MENA: One guest borrowed shoes from the costume room.

The first guest shows a pair of flats. The second points to a boot. The third brings a small basket of dance shoes. None matches the glass slipper.

RUMI: The shoe cannot identify the wearer.

TAVI: It can identify the costume department.

The cobbler checks the chip on the sole. A thin strip of blue paint clings to the edge. Ella notices the same color on her work gloves.

ELLA: That paint came from the stair set. I carried the lanterns through the ballroom before the dance.

MENA: The slipper was in the prop box, then.

ELLA: I borrowed it for the display. I did not know the heel had cracked.

Tavi sets it in a padded tray. Rumi looks at the guests and apologizes for the public search.

RUMI: I turned a prop checkout into a royal investigation.

TAVI: The ledger can take us to the right room.

SCENE 4 - INT. PALACE COSTUME ROOM - AFTERNOON

Tavi returns the slipper to the prop shelf. The costume room ledger shows it was checked out to Ella for the ballroom set. Rumi reads the entry. The neighbor's name is not on the list.

RUMI: The shoe identified its borrower, not its owner.

TAVI: That is how rental records work.

Mena places a label on the box: GLASS SLIPPER PROP, NOT FOR WALKING. Ella signs it back in.

ELLA: I can build a safer stair rail for the next event.

RUMI: I thought the story ended with a wedding.

ELLA: It ends with a repaired stair and a prop in the right place.

SCENE 5 - INT. PALACE HALL - EVENING

Rumi invites Ella to the next theater performance. He does not ask her to try on another shoe. Ella accepts and asks whether he can help carry lumber. He agrees.

Tavi fits a soft lining inside the glass slipper display case. Mena closes the lid. The herald changes the announcement from "Find the princess" to "Return the prop to the costume room."

RUMI: The slipper started a search.

ELLA: The ledger finished it.

The prince and Ella walk toward the theater workshop. They carry separate tools and leave the shoe in its case.

FINAL IMAGE: The glass slipper rests behind protective glass beside its checkout ledger, while a newly built handrail runs securely along the ballroom steps.
