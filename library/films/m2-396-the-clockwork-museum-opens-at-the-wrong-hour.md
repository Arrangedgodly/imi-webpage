# The Clockwork Museum Opens at the Wrong Hour

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-travel adventures, museum grand openings, and exhibits that run on a schedule no one can change*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
KETT, a lemur curator
VEX, a macaque clockmaker
PIP, a capuchin guard
MARA, a gibbon visitor
THE TICKER, a small mechanical monkey

SCENE 1. INT. CLOCKWORK MUSEUM - DAWN

Kett prepares to open the museum at nine. The main clock reads nine, though dawn light still fills the lobby. Vex winds a brass monkey that sits in the lobby. It begins ticking loudly.

KETT: The doors open in one hour.

VEX: The mechanism is set to open at seven.

PIP: It is eight by the sun.

VEX: The clock is an hour ahead.

Kett checks the exhibit schedule. It says the clock should be wound at seven, but the museum opens at nine. The Ticker's eyes flash. It points to a brass key on the wall.

MARA: Is that part of the exhibit?

VEX: It is the master key.

Pip guards the key. The Ticker springs from its display and runs toward the front doors. Pip catches it by the tail. The clock strikes nine.

SCENE 2. INT. MAIN GALLERY - MORNING

The doors open. Mara enters. She asks to see the clockwork exhibit. Kett points to the main clock. It now reads ten. Vex says the clock is keeping museum time.

MARA: I came at nine.

KETT: It was nine when the doors opened.

The Ticker runs to a second clock and turns its hands backward. The main clock follows. Pip tries to stop it. He puts his hand over the minute hand, and the clock pushes his arm around.

PIP: Time is moving me.

VEX: The gear is running free.

Kett asks Vex to stop the mechanism. He needs the master key. Pip reaches for it, but the Ticker has taken it. It hides beneath the display platform.

SCENE 3. INT. CLOCKWORK EXHIBIT - NOON

Vex kneels beside the platform. The Ticker's ticking is heard from inside. Mara lifts the exhibit card. It reads "Do not wind during opening hours." Kett points to the winding key hole. It has been turning on its own.

VEX: The Ticker has wound itself.

PIP: It is a clockwork monkey.

MARA: It is also a visitor.

Kett says the museum cannot close because guests have just arrived. The clocks strike twelve, then eleven, then ten. The visitors begin leaving before the exhibits are open.

Kett asks Mara to help retrieve the key. She lies on the floor and reaches under the platform. The Ticker rolls out, holding the key in its teeth. It stops beside the exit.

PIP: It is trying to leave.

VEX: The key winds it.

Pip takes the key. The Ticker stops. The clocks freeze at 10:03.

SCENE 4. INT. CLOCK ROOM - AFTERNOON

Vex opens the clock panel. One gear is loose. He says the key should be used only once. Kett asks if the clock can be set to the correct hour. Vex says the gear has skipped the hour hand's stop.

Mara finds a maintenance note behind the panel. It says the clock gains time whenever the Ticker is wound. Vex says the Ticker is supposed to run only for demonstrations. Kett points to the morning log. It was wound at seven, eight, and nine.

PIP: We gave it three extra hours.

VEX: We gave it three turns.

KETT: The clock thinks each turn is an hour.

Vex marks the gear teeth with chalk. Mara holds the clock still while Pip turns the key once. The hands jump forward. Kett asks them to reverse the mechanism. The Ticker begins running backward across the floor, dragging the key's chain.

MARA: It is taking the clock with it.

PIP: We need the key back.

Vex catches the chain. The Ticker stops at the exhibit card and points to the line about opening hours.

Mara suggests setting the clock by the sun. They open the roof shutter. Light lands on the floor. Kett moves the minute hand until the shadow aligns with a painted noon mark.

KETT: It is noon.

VEX: The clock says ten.

PIP: The clock is wrong.

They remove the loose gear and set the hands to noon. The Ticker wakes when Vex winds it a quarter turn. It runs toward the lobby and stops at the front door.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY - EVENING

Kett announces that the museum will open at nine the next day. She covers the clock with a sign reading "TIME SUBJECT TO REPAIR." Mara asks if the Ticker is part of the exhibit or a staff member.

VEX: It is a clock.

The Ticker takes the brass key and returns it to the wall. Pip locks the case. The museum clock strikes one, though the sun is setting.

KETT: We'll open tomorrow.

FINAL IMAGE: The Ticker points to the door. Its sign reads "CLOSED UNTIL NINE," while the clock behind it shows three.
