# The Last Seat in the Airship

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: high-flying adventure romances, boarding races, impossible luggage, and captains who fill every seat with scenery*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
CAPTAIN ODO, a gibbon
PILOT PIP, a capuchin
TRAVELER NARI, an orangutan
MECHANIC LUMA, a lemur
RIVAL VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. EXT. AIRSHIP DOCK - MORNING

The last seat on the airship is reserved for a passenger carrying a small brass box. Traveler Nari arrives just before departure. Rival Vex is already onboard with three trunks, a hat rack, and a large portrait. Captain Odo says the luggage exceeds the limit. Vex claims each piece is essential.

PIP: There is one seat left.

LUMA: There is also one cargo hold.

The airship's boarding clock starts counting down.

SCENE 2. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - LATER

Vex's portrait blocks the aisle. Nari waits on the dock. Odo asks Vex to store the frame. He says it is fragile. Luma checks the cargo hold and finds room for one trunk. Pip moves the hat rack and discovers a spare seat hidden behind it. The airship had two seats; the inventory listed one because the second was folded.

Nari boards and secures the brass box. Vex tries to place a trunk on the seat beside him. Odo says the seat is for a passenger. Vex asks if the portrait can count as a passenger. Luma says it can ride as cargo.

SCENE 2A. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - MORNING

Luma checks the passenger manifest. The reserved seat belongs to Nari, while Vex's extra trunks are listed as cargo. Vex asks whether a portrait case can sit in the aisle for the flight. Odo says the aisle must remain clear. Pip suggests folding the seat and storing the case under it. The latch sticks. Luma lubricates it and tests it twice.

NARI: I can carry the brass box myself.

VEX: It is fragile.

NARI: So is the aisle if you block it.

Vex takes the box, checks its seal, and hands it back. The airship's boarding clock continues to count down. Odo asks everyone to put away loose items. Vex straps down his hat rack and secures the portrait case inside the cargo net.

SCENE 3. EXT. CLOUD ROUTE - AFTERNOON

The airship hits a pocket of rough air. The portrait tips forward. Pip secures it with a strap. Nari holds the brass box in her lap. The captain asks each passenger to check their belt. Vex has tied his scarf around the buckle instead. Luma shows him the proper latch.

The ship banks around a cloud. Odo asks Pip to adjust the route. Pip follows the wind chart and avoids a mountain ridge. Vex says the detour ruins his arrival time. Nari says the route is safer. The portrait case shifts against its net again. Vex reaches to hold it, then stops when Luma points to the seat belt across his lap. He buckles in and lets the strap do its job. The painting stays put.

The airship lands at the valley port with both passengers and cargo intact.

SCENE 3A. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - AFTERNOON

The crew continues the detour around the ridge. Vex asks whether they can still reach the valley before sunset. Odo estimates forty minutes. Vex starts to unbuckle so he can straighten the portrait case, but Luma points to the secured strap and he sits back. Nari keeps the brass box under her seat and checks that the aisle is clear. Pip calls out each cargo item from the manifest; Vex answers for his three trunks, hat rack and portrait. The count matches. Odo marks the route change on the flight chart while the boarding clock, long since expired, hangs uselessly above the cabin door.

SCENE 4. INT. PORT TERMINAL - EVENING

Nari delivers the brass box to the observatory. It contains a replacement lens. Vex retrieves his portrait, which now has a small crease in one corner. He asks if the airship can take him back with all his luggage. Odo says yes, if he checks the cargo limit and folds the portrait case.

Pip checks the passenger list and marks both seats occupied. Luma signs the aircraft inspection. The boarding clock stops counting down and displays the next departure time. Vex asks whether his portrait earns frequent flyer points. Odo stamps the cargo tag, not the canvas.

SCENE 5. EXT. VALLEY PORT - SUNSET

The airship lifts off again. Vex sits beside the portrait case, properly secured. Nari waves from the observatory steps. Odo asks whether the last seat was worth saving. Pip says the second seat was there all along.

FINAL IMAGE: The airship crosses the evening sky with both cabin windows lit and the folded portrait case stowed below.
