# The Train Robbery That Missed Its Train

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: heist films where an elaborate crew mistakes the timetable for a target*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
PLANNER VEX, an orangutan
CONDUCTOR MARA, a macaque
RUNNER PIP, a capuchin
STATION CLERK ODO, a gibbon
PORTER NARI, a lemur
ROUTE CLERK LUMA, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. STATION WAITING ROOM - MORNING

Vex pins a timetable to the wall. A paper plan titled THE GREAT TRAIN JOB covers half the schedule.

VEX: At 10:15 the train arrives. We take the briefcase and disappear.

MARA: The 10:15 train is a passenger service. The satchel belongs to the station office.

ODO: It contains route maps, not money.

PIP: The office is open. We can return the misplaced bag to the clerk.

VEX: This is not a robbery?

NARI: It is lost property.

SCENE 2. INT. PLATFORM - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the track display. The train is delayed four minutes. Vex peers down the track.

VEX: Our window is closing.

MARA: The platform is closed beyond the yellow line. Stand behind it.

PIP: I found the bag under the bench. The tag says Station Office.

ODO: We should not open it. I'll sign the handoff.

Vex reaches for the bag, then looks at the camera. Mara places it on the counter.

MARA: The station clerk will collect it.

SCENE 2A. INT. STATION OFFICE - DAY

Odo places the bag on the counter and reads its tag aloud. Vex stands nearby with his hand hovering over the zipper.

ODO: Station Office. Route maps, pencils, one wrapped lunch. The bag is still closed.

VEX: The plan called for us to open it on the moving train.

MARA: We are at the station. The owner is in the office, and the tag matches.

PIP: I found it beneath the waiting bench. It wasn't hidden; it had slipped behind the leg.

NARI: The bench is on the public side. The porter checks beneath it after each departure.

ODO: The route clerk is coming down the hall.

Vex squares his shoulders and pulls a blank note from his coat.

VEX: I will demand a reward for the recovered treasure.

MARA: Don't demand payment. The clerk may thank us, but the bag belongs to the station.

The clerk arrives, checks the tag, and signs the return log. Vex puts the note away.

LUMA: Thanks. The maps are for today's service, and the pencils are in the right pocket.

ODO: Return logged at 10:06. Nothing removed.

PIP: The train is on Platform One. We can board as passengers.

VEX: The robbery is now a commute.

MARA: It was a misplaced bag from the beginning.

SCENE 2B. EXT. PLATFORM - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the passenger display. The train doors open; passengers step down before boarding begins.

MARA: Keep the aisle clear. Wait until the exit line is off the platform.

VEX: The crew is changing positions.

ODO: It is a normal passenger exchange.

PIP: I will board after the last passenger exits. My ticket is for the 10:15 service.

NARI: The route clerk has the returned satchel. The office is locked while she boards.

Vex studies the timetable. The train's destination matches his ticket.

VEX: The target train is here.

MARA: The train is your ride. It is not a target.

The last passenger exits. Pip boards and stows the folded plan in the overhead rack. Vex follows and takes a window seat.

ODO: No items left on the bench. The station office has the returned bag and signed log.

VEX: The mission has concluded before it began.

MARA: The trip is beginning. Please sit down.

SCENE 2C. INT. TRAIN CAR - CONTINUOUS

The doors close after the boarding chime. Mara checks that no one is standing in the vestibule.

MARA: All passengers aboard. The aisle is clear.

VEX: The heist crew has escaped.

PIP: We have boarded a public train with valid tickets.

ODO: Station report is filed. The satchel remains secured in the office.



SCENE 3. INT. TRAIN CAR - CONTINUOUS

The train pulls away. Pip sits beside the folded plan in the overhead rack. Vex watches the station disappear through the window.

VEX: The operation has crossed into its next phase.

MARA: We boarded a passenger train with valid tickets. The satchel is returned and logged.

ODO: Destination is two stops away. Luma has the signed handoff.

PIP: No property was taken.

SCENE 4. EXT. DESTINATION PLATFORM - DAY

The train stops at the listed destination. Mara waits until the doors open before stepping down. Vex follows, still holding his empty plan.

NARI: Platform is clear. We can walk to the exit.

VEX: The train robbery is foiled.

MARA: There was no robbery. There was a misplaced satchel and an ordinary trip.

ODO: The service arrived after its short delay. The return log is already complete.

TAG: Vex folds the plan into a paper timetable. On the cover he writes one word: "Commute."
