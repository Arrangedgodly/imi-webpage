# Please Remain for the Credits

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: end credits that treat everyone as background, hide essential contributors, and add one more card after every apparent ending*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A small film's credits keep growing until the crew must decide whether to name every helper or finally let the audience go home.

Cast: Vika, a lemur editor; Ralo, a gorilla sound recordist; Mena, a macaque director; Puck, a capuchin projectionist; and the theater volunteer.

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING ROOM, MORNING

Mena screens the finished short film. It ends with the main character returning a borrowed key. Vika adds the credits. The first card lists the director, editor, and cast. Ralo asks where the sound recordist appears.

VIKA: After camera.

RALO: There was no camera operator.

Puck checks the crew sheet. It lists six people who helped move the lights, one person who brought tea, and the person who lent the key. Mena says the credits should recognize the whole team. Vika adds another card. The sequence now lasts longer than the film. Vika asks Mena whether every helper wants their full name listed. Mena says yes. Puck scrolls through the sheet and finds one person listed as "Monkey who found tape."

RALO: We know their name.

VIKA: It is written on the sign-in form.

Mena gives Puck the form. He has credited the tape but not the person who bought it.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING ROOM, LATER

Puck plays the credits. The names scroll. A card says "Special Thanks: The Key." Ralo asks whether the key has a legal name. Mena says the owner should be credited. Vika adds "Key provided by Hall Committee."

RALO: That is a group.

VIKA: It is the group on the permission form.

Puck advances to the music credit. The short film has no music, but the template includes a section. Mena asks to keep the card. Vika types "No music used." Puck says the card is blank for eight seconds.

Vika changes it to "Silence: Recorded on location." Ralo asks who recorded the silence. The room falls quiet as everyone looks at him. Ralo says he did not record silence; he recorded room tone between takes. Vika checks the audio file. It includes a chair scrape, a kettle click, and Puck turning a page.

PUCK: Should the page turn be in the credits?

MENA: It is not in the film.

Vika removes the silence card. The credits now use the same sound as the end of the story.

SCENE 3. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, AFTERNOON

The team tests the credits on the cinema screen. The first names are too small to read. Vika enlarges them. The credits move more slowly. The final card says "Thanks for staying." Puck asks whether the audience will wait for a post-credit note.

Mena says the film has no post-credit scene. Puck has already added one: a close-up of the returned key. Ralo asks why the key is back on screen. Puck says it is a callback. Vika removes the shot. The key has already been credited. Puck asks if the owner should receive a second thanks for allowing a close-up. Vika says the owner lent the key, not the scene. Mena checks the permission form and sees the owner requested only a group credit. They restore the hall committee card once and delete the second one.

A volunteer enters the booth and says the theater closes at nine. Puck asks if the film can end before then. Mena says the story already ends before the credits. Vika trims repeated cards, keeps the six-person crew list, and moves the hall committee to special thanks.

Vika exports a copy for the hall committee and tests it on a phone. The names remain legible on the small screen, and the last card stays visible long enough to read. Puck tries to add a thanks for the phone. Vika closes the export window.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, EVENING

The film screens for an audience. The story ends. The credits begin. Names appear in a readable size. Ralo's sound credit appears before the thanks. The audience stays through the final card, which says "The End."

Puck waits for the post-credit shot. Nothing appears. The audience begins leaving. Mena asks if they should add a final thank-you. Vika says the last card is enough. Ralo carries the sound recorder out of the booth. Puck watches the empty screen for a few seconds, waiting to see if the title returns. Vika switches on the aisle lights. The audience is gone, and the crew still has to return the key to the hall committee.

FINAL IMAGE: The blank screen reflects the empty seats. The crew exits together before the lights go out. Ralo locks the booth, Mena carries the crew sheet, and Vika checks the key into the envelope. Puck closes the cinema door without looking back at the screen.
