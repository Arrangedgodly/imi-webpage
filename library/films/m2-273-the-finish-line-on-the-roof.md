# The Finish Line on the Roof

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: underdog sports dramas, training montages, rival schools, and climactic races whose course changes at the worst moment*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A low-ranking capuchin runner must lead a mismatched team across the school roof race after a gust blows the finish banner onto the wrong building, while the favored squad insists the rules are more important than the course.

Cast
- JORI, a capuchin sprinter whose tail helps her balance on narrow rails.
- MOK, a macaque distance runner who counts every step aloud.
- EDA, a ring-tailed lemur hurdler with a powerful leap.
- VEX, a gibbon rival captain and careful rule-reader.
- COACH NAL, a mandrill who gives speeches before checking the map.
- OFFICIAL PEL, an orangutan referee.

## Scene 1: Last Place

EXT. SCHOOL TRACK - MORNING

A scoreboard lists the teams for the annual Roof Relay. Jori's team sits at the bottom. Their baton is a rolled-up map tied with string.

COACH NAL: We are not here to finish last. We are here to redefine last.

MOK: Does that mean we run slower?

Jori peels the map open. The posted route goes from the track up a stairwell, across the gym roof, over a covered footbridge, and down to the courtyard. The finish banner marks the east tower.

JORI: The roof gate is locked.

COACH NAL: Then we will find another way. In every sports film, the team discovers the path inside itself.

EDA: Is there a door inside the school?

Jori turns the map over. A note on the reverse says ROOF ACCESS MOVED TO WEST TOWER.

## Scene 2: The Training Montage

EXT. WEST TOWER - DAY

Music rises. Jori sprints stairs while Mok counts loudly. Eda leaps over hurdles made from fallen branches. Coach Nal blows a whistle at the wrong time, sending them back to the start.

MOK: Forty-seven, forty-eight, forty-nine steps!

JORI: You counted the landing twice.

MOK: I needed an extra step.

VEX and the rival team run precise intervals. Vex carries the official rulebook in a waist pouch.

VEX: The course markers must stay in place. The route is the same for every team.

A gust blows the finish banner loose. It catches on the railing between the towers.

EDA: The finish moved.

VEX: The finish banner is not the finish line. The official map is.

Jori looks across the gap. The route has been taped on the west tower, but the banner hangs from the east tower.

## Scene 3: The Rival's Complaint

INT. GYM - AFTERNOON

Official Pel reviews the route with both teams. The map shows the west tower finish, while the event program still says east.

VEX: We trained for the posted course.

JORI: So did we. We just noticed the map changed.

COACH NAL: Our team will prevail because of heart.

PEL: Heart does not settle a route discrepancy.

MOK: Could we race to both towers?

PEL: The course would take twice as long.

EDA: Could we move the banner?

PEL: The banner is weighted and tied above the courtyard.

The teams argue. Jori asks to inspect the tie. She climbs the tower ladder using her tail for balance and discovers the banner's rope is tangled around a roof vent. The official route itself is clear; the marker is the only confusion.

JORI: If we pull from the ground, it will tighten. We need to feed the rope back over the vent.

VEX: That is not in the rulebook.

JORI: It is in the knot.

Vex reluctantly holds the rope while Jori guides it free. The banner descends, but it is too gusty to rehang before the race.

## Scene 4: The Relay

EXT. SCHOOL ROOFS - AFTERNOON

Teams gather at the start. Pel announces that the finish is the west tower marker, not the banner. Coach Nal begins a speech about overcoming adversity. The starting whistle cuts him off.

The racers sprint uphill. Jori takes the first leg, her tail counterbalancing a narrow roof turn. Mok counts steps on the second leg, then loses count when a pigeon flaps past. Eda bounds over a maintenance hose but lands beyond the handoff mark. Their baton-map unrolls in the wind.

VEX: Stop! The baton is part of the course rules.

Jori catches the map before it blows away. She folds it into a tighter roll and passes it to Eda. No penalty: Pel confirms the handoff occurred inside the marked zone.

The rival team reaches the footbridge first. A safety gate swings shut behind them. Vex stops to reopen it for the trailing runners rather than leave it blocking the course. Jori's team gains the lead, but Mok is tiring.

MOK: I have reached two hundred and six steps.

JORI: There are only two hundred and five in this leg.

MOK: I was counting the bridge.

## Scene 5: The Last Turn

At the west tower, the banner is gone but the painted finish stripe remains. The rival runner and Jori arrive together. Eda must complete the final pass across the rooftop garden, where planters narrow the path.

VEX: Stay inside the chalk. No shortcuts.

EDA: I am staying inside.

She leaps over a fallen leaf, lands safely, and reaches the stripe. The judges consult the clock. The teams have finished within one breath of each other.

PEL: The official result is a tie. Both teams used the marked route and completed all handoffs.

COACH NAL: A tie is two victories in matching shoes.

VEX: It is one shared trophy.

JORI: We can split the practice schedule, too. Nobody owns the roof.

## Scene 6: The Awards

The teams carry the trophy downstairs together. Coach Nal starts a speech, then sees the safety crew repairing the banner rope.

COACH NAL: I will keep it short. We noticed the map, helped the gate, and finished together.

MOK: That was under ten seconds.

EDA: And it had the whole team in it.

## Final Image

The trophy stands below the west tower map. The banner hangs properly over the courtyard, its two ends held by rival captains. Jori traces the route with one finger while Mok counts only the steps that exist.
