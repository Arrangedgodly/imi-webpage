# The Orchard Under Glass

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: scientific disaster films where a sealed habitat magnifies ordinary maintenance into a crisis*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: When a crew seals an experimental glasshouse to protect its seedlings, their procedures create a bigger emergency than the weather outside.

Cast: Dr. Nemi, a lemur botanist; Kato, a macaque engineer; Vela, a capuchin communications officer; and a troop of young capuchins assigned as observers.

SCENE 1. INT. GLASSHOUSE, MORNING

Rain patters on the dome. Nemi checks a row of seedlings, each growing in a separate cup. Kato locks the main door.

KATO: Pressure is stable.

NEMI: The plants are not under pressure.

Vela speaks into the intercom, practicing a calm announcement.

VELA: We have secured the habitat. Please remain calm.

A young capuchin slips under the door before it seals and sits among the cups.

YOUNG CAPUCHIN: Am I the hazard?

KATO: You are an unlisted occupant.

NEMI: He is also holding the watering can.

The young monkey tips the can. Water splashes into one seedling cup, then another. Nemi catches the handle.

SCENE 2. INT. CONTROL BOOTH, LATER

Kato points to a blinking panel. A filter fan slows because a large leaf is caught in the intake grate.

KATO: The air system is losing flow.

VELA: I can announce that the air is losing flow.

NEMI: First remove the leaf.

Kato opens the service hatch. Air whistles through it and pulls a chart from the wall. The chart sticks to Vela's face.

VELA (muffled): I have the situation under control.

The observers giggle. Kato reaches carefully with a pole and dislodges the leaf. The fan resumes. The chart flutters onto Nemi's seedlings.

NEMI: Please save the planting schedule.

Vela lifts the chart. A wet corner tears off.

VELA: We have lost the schedule.

NEMI: We have the labels on the cups.

SCENE 3. INT. GLASSHOUSE, AFTERNOON

A gust hits the dome. The rain stops, and sunlight pours in. The temperature display climbs. Kato activates the automatic shade, which lowers a screen over the whole roof. The plants are safe, but the solar panel powering the fan is covered too.

KATO: Cooling has stopped.

VELA: The announcement should say the cooling has stopped.

NEMI: The announcement can say we are opening the side vents.

KATO: The vents are locked by the pressure seal.

The young capuchin peers through a clear maintenance tube. A strip of shade fabric has jammed a vent lever. Nemi reaches in with a long branch. Kato tries the same from outside, and both meet at the narrow opening.

NEMI: Your hand is in my way.

KATO: Your branch is in my hand's way.

Vela reads the emergency card, which directs them to evacuate through the main door. Kato cannot unlock it until the pressure equalizes.

VELA: The card says leave immediately.

NEMI: It was written before we sealed the door.

They stop tugging and use the branch to lift the fabric. The lever clicks. A vent opens. The fan starts as sun reaches the panel again.

SCENE 4. INT. GLASSHOUSE, LATE AFTERNOON

The crew takes stock. One cup has tipped, but the seedling is intact. Nemi places it upright and adds a small pebble for balance. Kato removes the shade fabric from the vent. Vela prepares the official report.

VELA: Shall I say the habitat endured a critical failure?

KATO: The vent was stuck.

NEMI: And the seedlings need more room between cups.

The observers rearrange the cups in a wider row. Air reaches each leaf. The temperature holds steady.

KATO: We should keep the door open while we work.

VELA: The door is sealed.

KATO: Then we should have designed a way to open it while we work.

Nemi points to the manual latch outside the pressure seal. Kato adds a bright tag so no one mistakes it for a power switch.

Kato checks the exterior latch with the door open, then closes it gently and tests the manual release. It works without power. Nemi arranges the cups where the shade falls evenly. Vela revises the emergency card so it tells a person which vent to open before it tells them to remain calm.

SCENE 5. EXT. GLASSHOUSE, SUNSET

The crew exits. Vela reads her prepared statement to the observers.

VELA: The habitat remained safe because everyone followed the correct procedure.

The young capuchin points to the peeled-off corner of the report, still stuck to Vela's sleeve.

YOUNG CAPUCHIN: That paper says the old procedure.

Vela looks at the page, then turns it over and writes: "Open vent before announcing emergency." Nemi smiles and carries the seedlings outside for a little fresh air.

KATO: The plants were asking for that.

VELA: Did you hear them?

A leaf brushes Kato's cheek. He sneezes.

KATO: They made a formal request.
