# The Medieval Pageant Has One Dragon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: medieval fantasy films that promise an enormous battle despite a village pageant's single cardboard creature*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A village pageant director tries to stage a sweeping dragon siege with one cardboard puppet, a short wall, and actors who keep following the posted running order.

Cast: director Marn, a macaque; knight Edda, a gibbon; dragon operator Bolo, a capuchin; herald Seli, a lemur; caretaker Pema, an orangutan; sound volunteer Nima, a squirrel monkey.

SCENE 1 - EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - AFTERNOON

Marn unfolds a painted battle map over two sawhorses. A banner reads ANNUAL PAGEANT, ONE PERFORMANCE ONLY. The cardboard dragon lies beside a low cloth wall. Bolo crawls inside its body and lifts the head; one eye is missing.

MARN: We open with the dragon over the northern ridge.

BOLO: We have one ridge. It is the flower bed.

EDDA: I enter from the south gate, which is the library steps.

Seli rings a handbell to call the cast. The dragon's paper jaw falls off. Pema retrieves it from the grass and hands it to Bolo.

PEMA: It came loose in rehearsal yesterday.

MARN: The creature has suffered its first wound.

SCENE 2 - EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - LATER

Marn blocks the entrance. Edda is to ride in on a hobby horse and rescue the mayor. The hobby horse has a long paper mane that catches on the cloth wall. Edda tries again, carefully lifting the prop over it.

EDDA: The path is blocked.

MARN: That is the siege.

HERALD SELI: The program says the dragon appears after the knight's speech.

MARN: The program is from last year.

Pema checks the cast sheet. The dragon's appearance is still printed after the speech. Marn asks her to turn the sheet over, but the reverse side is the pageant's sponsor list. Every sponsor's name has been lettered in gold.

SCENE 3 - EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - CONTINUOUS

Bolo tests the dragon's wings. The left wing rises. The right wing catches beneath a bench. Edda pulls the bench aside, revealing a stack of town notices. One reads: PLEASE KEEP THE PATH CLEAR FOR THE PARADE.

EDDA: We have found a secret warning.

PEMA: The notice is for the parade route.

Marn asks Seli to ring the bell for the attack. The clapper falls out and lands in a paper cup. The bell stays silent. Nima holds the only microphone at the stage mark and tells the cast to wait for a clear cue. Bolo advances the dragon a few inches anyway. It bumps the cloth wall and tips sideways.

MARN: The castle has fallen.

BOLO: The castle is a blanket on two stakes.

The performers pause while Pema restores the wall. Nima hands the microphone to Seli for the herald's announcement, then takes it back before the battle cue. Marn asks the cast to make the battle larger with their voices. Edda and Bolo shout from opposite ends; passing villagers stop to watch the pageant's folding chairs vibrate.

SCENE 4 - EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - EVENING

The audience arrives. Marn cuts the opening battle and begins with the herald. Seli announces that a dragon has been spotted over the northern ridge. Bolo lifts the puppet. Its missing eye points directly toward the audience.

EDDA: Stand back, citizens.

The hobby horse mane catches on the same wall. Rather than tug, Edda dismounts, unhooks the mane and leads the horse around the obstacle. Bolo's wing bumps the sponsor banner, turning it backward. Pema passes the banner to Marn, who turns it front-facing and holds it clear of the dragon. Nima hands the microphone to Seli for the sponsor names, then takes it back before the dragon moves.

MARN: Give us the prophecy!

SELI: The program says the pageant ends with the dragon retreating.

Marn signals Bolo to retreat. The dragon reverses, but its tail wraps around a tent stake. Edda unties it. The audience applauds the rescue more loudly than the scripted sword salute.

During the live pageant, the dragon blocks Seli as she reads the next line. Marn calls an unplanned extra attempt from the edge of the stage: the actors will repeat the turn now, in front of the audience. Nima passes the microphone to Seli for the line, then retrieves it before the movement. Edda signals Bolo with the silent bell; Bolo turns the dragon toward the crowd on her raised hand. The audience sees the correction happen instead of a cutaway.

SCENE 5 - EXT. VILLAGE GREEN - NIGHT

Edda lowers her wooden sword. Bolo removes the dragon head and bows from inside the body. Marn starts a speech about a kingdom saved, then sees the crowd already helping fold the cloth wall and stack chairs. He hands out the cast's gold sponsor ribbons as thank-you awards.

MARN: The realm is secure until next year's rehearsal.

PEMA: We should repair the dragon's eye first.

Pema gives the loose bell clapper to Seli, who seats it back inside the bell. Bolo takes the paper eye from the grass and attaches it to the dragon head with a separate strip of tape. Seli rings the bell; it makes a small metallic clink.

FINAL IMAGE: The patched dragon stands beside the sponsor banner, now facing the flower bed while the pageant cast rolls the castle blanket into its storage tub.
