# The Case of the Stolen Traffic Cone

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: buddy-cop action films, mismatched partners, and excessive car-chase escalation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A by-the-book owl officer and a reckless raccoon detective investigate one missing traffic cone, turning a quiet parking-lot case into a chase after a marching band.

Cast: Officer Hoot, an owl who follows procedure; Detective Rags, a raccoon who improvises; Clerk Mimi, a macaque equipment clerk; and a marching band that borrowed a traffic cone as a music stand.

SCENE 1. EXT. CITY STREET, MORNING

Hoot measures the gap between parked cars. Rags slides in beside him, blocking a hydrant. The radio reports a missing traffic cone.

RAGS: We have a runner.

HOOT: We have a cone.

Rags sees chalk marks and calls for backup. Hoot asks him to photograph them first. A marching band passes with a cone balanced on a tuba. Rags reaches for his scooter. Hoot points to his bicycle.

SCENE 2. INT. PRECINCT, DAY

Mimi checks the equipment log. The cone was signed out for roadworks; the signature is a duck doodle. Rags pins it to a map with red string connecting the depot, the snack room, and the river.

MIMI: The snack room has no outside door.

RAGS: That's why it's a perfect hiding place.

Hoot finds a cart receipt: cone, tuba stand, apples. The road crew confirms the band borrowed it to hold sheet music during rehearsal. Rags calls it a lead. Hoot calls it a return address.

SCENE 3. EXT. CITY STREETS, AFTERNOON

Mimi follows in a municipal van with the loan form. Rags waves her into a side street, but Hoot signals her to stop at the crosswalk. A flock of pigeons crosses slowly. Rags circles the block and arrives behind them.

RAGS: We lost the target.

HOOT: The target is playing a tuba.

The band turns the corner. The cone tips forward. Rags reaches out to catch it but grabs a trombone slide instead. The musician keeps playing as Rags holds the instrument extended. He releases it and apologizes. Hoot asks the band leader to pause at the hall. The leader nods without stopping the tune.

The officers follow the band. Rags takes off on his scooter. Hoot cycles at a steady pace. Rags cuts through a park and has to turn back because the path is one-way for strollers. Hoot arrives first at the rehearsal hall, locks his bicycle, and holds the door for Rags.

RAGS: You beat me.

HOOT: I followed the map.

Rags says the band is fleeing. Hoot says they are marching. The tuba player turns down a side street. Rags follows the rhythm, then joins the parade by mistake. A child hands him a tambourine. Hoot whistles for him to return to the case.

SCENE 4. INT. REHEARSAL HALL, LATER

The officers wait by the exit while the band repeats the chorus. Rags taps his foot. Mimi checks the form and says they need the cone's number. Hoot reads it from the city stamp. It matches the rack inventory. Rags asks if this proves the cone was stolen. Mimi says it proves it is a city cone. Hoot says the report will be accurate.

The cone is wedged inside a bass drum. Each strike makes a hollow clang. Rags circles the drum while Hoot asks the band leader to pause after the current measure. The band completes the whole song, then starts it again. Hoot raises one wing. The leader sees him and stops.

HOOT: The cone belongs to the city.

LEADER: We thought it was abandoned.

MIMI: The log shows it was borrowed.

RAGS: The cone has been drumming up trouble.

Mimi asks the leader to sign the return form with his name. He writes a musical note. She hands him the pencil again. He writes his name. The drummer removes the cone and gives it to Hoot. Rags reaches for it, but Hoot asks him to hold the clipboard instead.

SCENE 5. EXT. ROADWORKS DEPOT, EVENING

The officers return the cone to its marked rack. Mimi updates the log. The crew thanks the band for bringing it back. Rags writes his report: CONE ESCAPED DURING MUSICAL PURSUIT. Hoot crosses out escaped and writes borrowed for rehearsal.

RAGS: Your version has no chase.

HOOT: There was a bicycle.

Rags finds a second cone on his scooter. He claims it is a souvenir. Hoot asks for its loan form. Rags puts it back in the rack. They ride toward the precinct, side by side, at the posted speed.

Rags starts to rev the scooter. Hoot points at the bicycle rack. He sighs, parks the scooter, and takes his bike.

FINAL IMAGE: At the next intersection, Hoot stops at the red light. Rags skids to a halt beside him and says they caught the signal. Hoot waits until it changes before moving.
