# The Crossover at Table Four

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: franchise crossover films, cameo overload, and plots that make every character important to the same small problem*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: Characters from three unrelated shorts hijack a neighborhood screening to solve a projector jam in the style of their own genres.

Cast: Wren, a lemur projectionist; Captain Button, a gorilla superhero; Detective Pebble, a macaque from a mystery short; Farmer Mott, a capuchin from a farm comedy; and Sali, a tortoise technician.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, NIGHT

The projector stutters. The picture tears into a bright stripe and freezes. The audience murmurs. Wren looks through the booth window at the jammed reel.

WREN: We need two minutes.

Captain Button enters the booth in a cape. He raises his fists toward the machine.

BUTTON: Stand back. I will lift the projector and free the film.

SALI: You will not lift a projector.

Detective Pebble crouches beside the cabinet. He takes out a magnifying glass.

PEBBLE: A jam means someone wanted this screening stopped.

Farmer Mott arrives carrying a bucket of seeds.

MOTT: Maybe the projector needs watering.

Sali reaches past all three and switches off the power. The audience claps, thinking the superhero has started a stunt. Wren explains the delay over the house microphone.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, CONTINUOUS

Sali opens the service panel. A strip of film is buckled inside the gate.

SALI: The edge folded. Nobody touch it.

BUTTON: I can hold the reel while you work.

SALI: You can hold the door.

Button plants himself at the doorway in a heroic pose. The cape blocks the emergency light. Wren pulls it aside and points to the door closer. Button removes the cape and folds it onto a chair.

Pebble circles the panel, narrating his deductions.

PEBBLE: The jam occurred after the reel was loaded. The projectionist had access. The technician had access. The motive is unclear.

WREN: I loaded the film. I checked the edge. It caught when the motor started.

Pebble studies the sprocket. Sali asks him to step back. He sees a small curl on the film and asks whether it is a coded message. Sali says it is the buckle.

Mott opens the seed bucket. Sali closes it before the seeds spill near the projector.

MOTT: The crop is secure.

SALI: The floor is clean.

Sali removes the damaged section and splices the film. Wren holds the reel steady when asked. Button offers to shine a flashlight, but points it at the ceiling. Pebble asks to photograph the splice for his case file. Sali tells him to take a picture of the labeled repair log instead.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, MOMENTS LATER

The audience waits. Button slips into the aisle and announces that the screening will resume once the threat is contained. Pebble follows, asking whether anyone saw the suspect near the booth. Mott wheels his seed bucket toward the stage and says he can plant a new film if this one is lost.

Wren comes to the microphone.

WREN: The film is being repaired. Please stay seated.

BUTTON: The citizens are calm.

PEBBLE: One of them has a suspicious program.

SALI (OVER INTERCOM): The suspect is a bent strip of film.

The audience laughs. Button starts a chant. Pebble asks them to preserve the scene. Mott passes out kernels from his bucket, then discovers they are seeds, not popcorn. He apologizes and takes them back.

Sali tests the projector with a blank leader. The image runs smoothly. She tells Wren the splice is secure but asks her to check the reel tension before restarting. Wren does so. Button asks if he can announce the return as a hero's entrance. Sali says the movie is not about him. Pebble asks if the technician can at least reveal the culprit. Sali points to the repair log, which records "film edge buckled at gate." Mott asks if the screening can begin now.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, LATER

The film starts. Button sits with his cape folded under his seat. Pebble puts away the magnifying glass. Mott keeps the seed bucket closed. When the credits roll, the audience stays long enough to read the names.

Button asks if there is a post-credit scene. Sali holds up the repaired film leader.

SALI: There is a return-to-storage scene.

Wren rewinds the reel and places it in its case. Pebble signs the repair log as a witness. Mott puts his bucket under the chair, away from the cables. Button holds the booth door open without blocking the exit.

FINAL IMAGE: The three crossover guests leave through separate doors toward their own stories. Sali switches off the projector, and Wren turns out the booth light.
