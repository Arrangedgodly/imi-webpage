# The Trailer Told Them Everything

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: movie trailers that reveal every major plot turn while promising audiences a mystery*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: An editor cuts a trailer for a mystery by showing each clue, suspect, betrayal, and solution, then cannot persuade the detective to act surprised.

Cast: Pera, a macaque trailer editor; Dumo, a gorilla detective actor; Seli, a lemur producer; and Tavi, a capuchin marketing assistant.

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING ROOM, MORNING

Pera screens a trailer. It opens on a dark hallway, shows a muddy boot, a broken cup, and a key beneath a chair. A voice says, "Nothing is as it seems." The next shot shows the caretaker hiding the key.

DUMO: I have not found the key yet.

PERA: It is only a preview.

SELI: The audience needs a reason to come.

Tavi has added a card that reads "THE CARETAKER DID IT." Pera asks him to remove it. Tavi says the card tested well. He shows a chart with one bar labeled "SPOILER" and another labeled "MYSTERY." The spoiler bar is taller.

PERA: Who was surveyed?

TAVI: The marketing department.

Dumo asks if the caretaker is guilty. Seli says the trailer editor is not supposed to reveal the ending. Pera freezes the frame before the caretaker appears. Tavi adds a sound cue: a gasp. The gasp lands over an empty hallway. Dumo says his detective character has not interviewed the caretaker. Seli asks him to look more suspicious while holding the evidence board.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING ROOM, LATER

Pera tries a shorter cut. She shows the empty key hook, then the muddy footprint. Tavi adds rapid flashes of the caretaker, the witness, and Dumo's left boot. The trailer ends with the words "WHO CAN YOU TRUST?"

DUMO: The boot is mine.

TAVI: The audience can decide.

Dumo points to the boot in the final flash. It has his name written on the sole. Seli asks Pera to replace it with a dramatic silhouette. Pera cuts to black, but Tavi adds a title card that says "A DETECTIVE'S OWN FOOTPRINT."

Pera plays the cut without sound. The sequence still reveals the answer. She removes the caretaker from the trailer. Tavi puts him back in as a blurred figure. The blur walks into the same room, opens the same drawer, and retrieves the key.

SELI: Can we hold back the key?

TAVI: We can show the key from the side.

The key has a bright red tag. Pera crops the frame. The tag remains visible in a reflection. She removes the mirror. The key remains visible in the polished door handle. She dulls the handle with a cloth. The key still makes a bright spot in the caretaker's eye.

PERA: Could we use another shot?

TAVI: The key is in every shot after the reveal.

Dumo says that is why it is a reveal. Seli asks whether the audience can see one clue without the solution. Pera exports a cut that ends before the key appears.

SCENE 3. INT. STUDIO SCREENING ROOM, AFTERNOON

A test audience watches. Pera has made a trailer that shows only the empty room, the clock, and the title. The audience asks whether it is a trailer for a mystery or a clock repair film. One viewer guesses the caretaker, another guesses the detective, and a third says the key itself has the strongest screen presence. Seli asks if they want to see the full film. They ask whether it has a clock repair scene.

Dumo looks at the empty hook on screen. Seli asks them what they expect.

AUDIENCE MEMBER: Someone will find a key.

Dumo looks at the empty hook on screen. He recognizes the line of the scene and asks if he should act surprised in the feature. Pera says yes, but only after the evidence appears.

Dumo practices. He enters the room, sees the footprint, and gasps. Tavi plays the trailer on a phone behind him. Dumo sees his own boot on screen and points at it.

DUMO: That's my boot.

TAVI: Great reaction. Can we use it in the trailer?

Pera takes Tavi's phone and locks it in the drawer.

SCENE 4. INT. EDITING ROOM, EVENING

Pera cuts a new trailer: the detective entering a room, a single question, and the title card. No culprit, clue, or answer appears. Seli watches the full cut.

SELI: What will make people click?

PERA: The question.

Tavi adds a small card that reads "THE KEY IS UNDER THE CHAIR." Pera deletes it. Dumo asks whether the film will still be a mystery after the trailer. Pera says the audience can discover it with him. Tavi asks if he can add a countdown to the release date. Pera allows it, but the countdown ends before the title card. Tavi argues the date is still a clue; Seli replies that people can look at a calendar.

FINAL IMAGE: The final trailer frame shows the empty key hook. Tavi reaches toward the keyboard, sees Pera watching, and leaves the frame blank.
