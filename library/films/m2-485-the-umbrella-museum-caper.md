# The Umbrella Museum Caper

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: elaborate museum-heist films whose teams plan a precision operation around an object nobody has asked them to take*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A crew of monkey thieves sneaks into a museum after closing to steal a famous umbrella, only to learn it is a loaner the museum wants returned to the coat rack.

Cast: planner Jek, a macaque; climber Luma, a gibbon; lookout Pavi, a capuchin; curator Noma, an orangutan; guard Seli, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. ABACUS MUSEUM - NIGHT

Jek spreads blueprints across a table. The museum's prized umbrella, displayed under glass, is labeled THE FIRST WEATHERPROOF UMBRELLA, LOAN FROM TOWN ARCHIVE.

JEK: We enter through the skylight, descend the cable, and switch the case.

PAVI: The front door has a night bell.

LUMA: The skylight has a ladder.

Pavi tests the ladder. It unfolds into a short step stool. Jek calls it a tactical ladder. Luma climbs it and cannot reach the roof hatch.

SCENE 2 - EXT. MUSEUM ROOF - LATER

The crew finds a maintenance hatch secured with a simple latch. Jek uses a lockpick. It is a bent hairpin from his own costume. Luma opens the latch by lifting it with one finger. They lower a rope into the gallery. Pavi descends and lands beside a motion sensor. A small display lights up: PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH THE UMBRELLA.

PAVI: We have triggered the alarm.

LUMA: It is a reminder sign.

Jek reaches the umbrella case. The glass is not locked; a placard asks visitors to keep it closed. Jek produces a suction cup, a small mirror, and a timer. Noma, the curator, enters with Seli, the guard.

NOMA: Are you here to return the loan?

JEK: We are here to remove it.

NOMA: The archive sent a request this afternoon. The display closes tomorrow.

SCENE 3 - INT. MUSEUM GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

The team freezes. Seli shines a flashlight on them. Jek claims the group is an after-hours restoration crew. Noma checks the booking board. There is no crew listed. Jek points to his blueprints; they show the museum's loading dock. Noma asks why their plan includes a cable, a decoy umbrella, and a smoke capsule.

PAVI: We thought the umbrella was more valuable than it is.

NOMA: It is valuable. That is why it is on loan.

Noma explains that the museum needs the umbrella packed safely before morning. Luma offers to climb the case and lift it from above. Seli points to the service key hanging on the wall. Jek refuses to use it because that would end the caper. Noma takes the key and opens the case herself.

Jek attempts to salvage the operation by ordering a distraction. Pavi rings the museum's front bell. Nobody reacts; the bell is connected to the daytime entrance. Luma tries the smoke capsule, but Seli recognizes it as a stage prop from the museum shop and asks her to put it away. Jek whispers that the crew should split up. They all step in different directions and each reaches a locked staff door.

Seli opens one door with her key. Behind it is the loading room, exactly where the team needs to go. Noma asks them to carry the cart, not the glass case. They form a careful line, passing the packing materials hand to hand. The last box contains the umbrella sleeve and a printed return label. Jek studies it for a hidden code. It is an ordinary shipping address.

SCENE 4 - INT. MUSEUM LOADING ROOM - LATER

The crew helps pack the umbrella. Jek insists on lowering it on his cable. The cable is too short, leaving the case suspended a handspan above the cart. Pavi pushes the cart underneath. Luma steadies the case while Seli unties the rope. Noma adds a padded sleeve and signs the archive receipt.

JEK: The exchange is complete.

NOMA: The museum lent it. We are returning it.

Jek asks whether this counts as a successful heist. Noma says the museum's insurance form needs the recipient's signature. He signs under "delivery team." The carbon copy sticks to his glove and trails across the floor like a getaway plan.

SCENE 5 - EXT. MUSEUM FRONT STEPS - DAWN

The archive van arrives. Noma hands over the umbrella in its sleeve. Jek and the crew carry their equipment to the street. Seli holds open the front door, which has a large sign reading OPEN FOR DELIVERIES.

PAVI: We could have walked in.

JEK: We did not know that.

The team looks at the ladder leaning against the wall. It is still the small step stool. Noma asks them to return it to the gallery. Jek does so with the solemnity of a master thief replacing a priceless artifact.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty glass case stands open beside the signed loan receipt, while the crew tiptoes out through the ordinary front door.
