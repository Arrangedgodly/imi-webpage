# The Button That Saved the City

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: superhero origin stories, secret identity montages, and citywide emergencies solved by one oddly specific skill*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: TIKO, a capuchin repair apprentice; CAPTAIN CEDAR, an orangutan hero; MAYA, a macaque dispatcher; VEX, a baboon inventor; OLD BUTTON, a gibbon tailor.

EXT. CITY ROOFTOP - DUSK

Captain Cedar stands above the skyline. A bright signal flashes from the clock tower. The city lights blink out, then on again.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: The grid is unstable.

MAYA, on radio: The lights are not unstable. Someone has turned them off.

A metal hand reaches from a rooftop vent and pulls out a glowing panel. Vex appears beneath it, wearing a cape made from an emergency blanket.

VEX: I have seized the city's central switch.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: Why?

VEX: It was the largest button I could find.

At street level, Tiko runs out of a repair shop, carrying a tray of replacement buttons. One button is missing from Captain Cedar's old uniform.

TIKO: I can fix the switch.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: You are a tailor's apprentice.

TIKO: I fix buttons.

ACT ONE

INT. OLD BUTTON'S SHOP - NIGHT

Old Button checks the missing spot on the hero uniform. Tiko threads a needle. Captain Cedar sits stiffly while Maya studies a city map.

MAYA: Vex is holding the main switch above the tram junction. If he presses it, the whole district loses power.

TIKO: What happens if he releases it?

MAYA: The lights come back.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: I will confront him.

OLD BUTTON: Your cape is caught in the door.

Captain Cedar turns. The cape is indeed pinned beneath the shop door. Tiko frees it and sews on a broad replacement button.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: Is that strong enough?

TIKO: It is the strongest button in the shop.

Maya points to the old signal uniform. The missing button slot has a thread loop connected to a safety latch.

MAYA: The uniform's power clasp is missing.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: I thought that was decorative.

TIKO: It holds the emergency beacon in place.

Vex's message flashes on a radio screen: "MIDNIGHT TEST." The city's countdown begins.

EXT. CLOCK TOWER - NIGHT

Captain Cedar and Tiko climb a service ladder. Maya directs them by radio from the street. Vex stands beside a large lever and an oversized illuminated switch.

VEX: One press and the city goes dark.

TIKO: One press, then what?

VEX: I will enjoy the silence.

MAYA: The tram is still moving. Stop the switch.

Captain Cedar lunges, but Vex pulls the lever. The lights go out. The clock tower's backup lantern stays on. Tiko reaches the switch and feels a loose hinge.

TIKO: It is not connected to the power. It is connected to the alarm.

VEX: What?

TIKO: You found the big emergency button. You did not find the grid.

Vex looks at the panel. The main power switch is a small black lever behind him. Captain Cedar reaches for it, but the cape button catches on the railing.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: Tiko!

Tiko unthreads the new button from the cape, loops its thread through the safety latch, and pulls. The cape frees. Captain Cedar turns the black lever back on. The city lights return.

ACT TWO

Maya radios the crew a switch-by-switch status. Tiko reads each label from the panel, and the crew confirms that the city grid, alarm circuit, and tram line are separate. They lock the main lever behind a clear cover. Vex watches the labels go on without making a speech.

The alarm button flashes. Maya calls the city response crew and tells them the power is back. The tram stops safely at the platform. Repair crews arrive at the tower and check the main switch before letting anyone touch it.

MAYA: The grid is steady. The alarm was real.

TIKO: The big button called for help. The little lever shut off the lights.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: Now we know which one to guard.

Vex tries to slip away down the ladder, but the emergency blanket cape snags on the handrail. The repair crew keeps the service gate clear while Captain Cedar untangles the cloth.

VEX: This cape has poor mobility.

CAPTAIN CEDAR: It is a blanket.

Tiko hands Vex a pair of scissors, then holds out a spare button.

TIKO: You can leave the blanket here and keep the button.

VEX: Why would I want a button?

TIKO: It opens the service gate.

Maya unlocks the gate from below. Vex walks down, without the blanket. Captain Cedar thanks Tiko and offers him a place on the repair crew.

TIKO: I can help with the uniforms. And the switch covers.

Old Button stitches a small patch over Captain Cedar's missing clasp. The hero does not put the new button back on the cape. He pins it to Tiko's work apron.

FINAL IMAGE: The city clock rings one clear chime while Tiko tests the oversized emergency button with a gloved finger.
