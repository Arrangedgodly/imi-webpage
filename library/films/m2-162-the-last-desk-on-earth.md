# The Last Desk on Earth

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: post-apocalyptic survival films where a crew tries to preserve office furniture instead of food and water*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: After a fictional disaster, three survivors find a sealed office supply depot. The manager insists the last desk is civilization's greatest resource, while the others search for food, clean water and a way out.

Cast: Survivor Luma, a gibbon; clerk Rook, a baboon; engineer Piri, a macaque; and a small monkey.

SCENE 1. INT. OFFICE DEPOT, DUSK

A flashlight illuminates shelves of paper, chairs and desks. Rook guards a large oak desk.

ROOK: This desk will hold society together.

LUMA: The water filter is behind it.

PIRI: We can move it.

ROOK: It is the last desk.

A small monkey climbs onto the desk and finds a sealed packet of crackers.

LUMA: The desk has food.

ROOK: The desk has inventory.

SCENE 2. INT. DEPOT AISLE, NIGHT

Piri checks the water filter. It is clogged with paper dust.

PIRI: We need a clean cloth.

Rook offers a desk pad.

ROOK: This is official felt.

LUMA: It is a cloth.

Piri uses it to clean the filter. Water begins to flow.

ROOK: We have damaged a desk accessory.

LUMA: We have water.

SCENE 3. INT. LOADING BAY, NIGHT

A door is blocked by stacked desks. Luma checks the emergency exit.

LUMA: We can leave through the side door.

ROOK: That door leads outside.

PIRI: That is the point.

Rook tries to move one desk. It catches on the others.

ROOK: The stack is load-bearing.

PIRI: It is furniture.

[THE MONKEY CRAWLS THROUGH A GAP AND OPENS THE SIDE DOOR FROM OUTSIDE.]

LUMA: We have an exit.

Rook carries a chair to the doorway.

ROOK: We need to take a seat with us.

SCENE 4. EXT. DEPOT, PRE-DAWN

The survivors follow a trail toward a community shelter. Rook drags the desk pad and a chair.

LUMA: We cannot carry the desk.

ROOK: We can carry one chair.

PIRI: We need our hands free.

Rook sets the chair down, then sits on it.

ROOK: I am conserving energy.

Luma and Piri walk ahead. Rook stands and follows, leaving the chair behind.

SCENE 4A. INT. LOADING BAY, NIGHT

Piri tests the side door while Luma checks the hinges. The door opens only a few inches before hitting a filing cabinet.

PIRI: The exit is blocked too.

ROOK: We can move the cabinet.

The small monkey opens a lower drawer and pulls out a rolled floor plan.

LUMA: The plan shows a second door.

Piri unrolls it on the floor. The marked exit is behind the desk stack.

ROOK: We cannot move the desk.

LUMA: We need to move the top one first.

Rook and Piri lift a chair off the stack. Luma slides a small desk aside. The filing cabinet shifts enough to clear the door.

PIRI: The way out is open.

ROOK: The desk is now misaligned.

LUMA: We can leave it that way.

Rook looks at the furniture, then takes the water filter and follows them.

SCENE 4B. EXT. DEPOT, PRE-DAWN

The group makes one last pass through the shelves. Rook finds a stack of paper and starts to carry it.

LUMA: We cannot take all of it.

ROOK: It could be useful for records.

PIRI: The shelter needs something to write on.

Rook sets down most of the stack and keeps a single pad. The monkey takes a chair cushion and carries it toward the door.

ROOK: That is not on the list.

LUMA: It can keep someone warm.

Piri checks the path beyond the exit. There is room for the group and the supplies, but not a desk.

ROOK: We have the important things.

They lift the filter, crackers, and paper pad. The monkey carries the cushion.

SCENE 5. EXT. COMMUNITY SHELTER, MORNING

The group arrives with the water filter and crackers. The shelter has benches.

LUMA: We have what we need.

ROOK: We did not bring a desk.

PIRI: We brought clean water.

The small monkey brings the desk pad, which it used as a sled. It is frayed and covered in dirt.

ROOK: That was official felt.

LUMA: It was also useful.

Rook lays the pad on a bench. The survivors share crackers and water. Piri starts a list of supplies on the back of a paper box.

ROOK: That is not a desk.

PIRI: It is holding the list.

Rook looks at the distant depot, then at the group.

ROOK: We can build a smaller desk later.

LUMA: After we find more food.

They leave the paper box on the bench for the next group.
