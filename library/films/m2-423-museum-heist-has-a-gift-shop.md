# The Museum Heist Has a Gift Shop

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: high-tech museum heists that depend on elaborate laser choreography while ignoring ordinary opening hours and visitor procedures*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A crew attempts to steal a priceless bell from a museum after closing, only to discover the loudest obstacle is the gift shop's automated checkout.

Cast: Miri, a capuchin planner; Dex, a macaque acrobat; Rollo, a lemur locksmith; Venn, a gibbon night guard; Sada, an orangutan curator.

SCENE 1 - INT. MUSEUM BASEMENT - NIGHT

A hand-drawn plan covers a folding table. Miri points at a box labeled LASERS. Rollo holds a roll of red ribbon. Dex wears black gloves and a bicycle helmet.

MIRI: We enter through the service door, cross the hall without touching a beam, lift the brass bell and leave before the guard completes one patrol.

ROLLO: The red lines on this map are the lasers?

MIRI: Yes.

ROLLO: They are ribbon.

MIRI: The laser plan is confidential. The ribbon marks the beams.

DEX: We should practice the roll under the beams.

MIRI: There are no beams in the basement.

Dex tumbles under the table and rolls into a mop bucket.

ROLLO: We have learned the bucket is in the way.

MIRI: Adjust the route.

SCENE 2 - INT. MUSEUM SERVICE HALL - NIGHT

The trio slips through a door. A motion sensor turns on the lights. All three freeze.

DEX: It saw us.

ROLLO: It turned on the lights.

MIRI: The light is a witness. Proceed slowly.

They tiptoe toward the gallery, but each step activates a floor speaker that says, "Welcome to the history wing." Dex attempts a gymnastic flip over the mat. He lands on it, and the greeting repeats.

MIRI: Stop greeting the museum.

DEX: The mat keeps greeting me.

Rollo finds a clear acrylic case around the brass bell. No lasers. A paper label reads DO NOT LIFT; ALARMED CASE.

ROLLO: The case is alarmed.

MIRI: I prepared for a laser grid.

ROLLO: You prepared a ribbon.

MIRI: We can bypass the case alarm.

DEX: I have a gadget.

He produces a toy remote with a single large button. Rollo takes it, presses it, and the gift shop lights turn on at the end of the hall.

SCENE 3 - INT. GIFT SHOP - CONTINUOUS

A checkout terminal wakes. It announces, "Please scan your souvenir." Miri creeps past. The terminal flashes a red light.

MIRI: What did it detect?

ROLLO: The sign says after-hours inventory. It is scanning the shelves.

DEX: I will distract it.

Dex picks up a postcard and scans it. The terminal chirps. The automatic door opens, letting in the night guard Venn.

VENN: Are you three shopping?

MIRI: We are leaving.

VENN: The exit is behind you. The bell is in the gallery and it does not leave the museum.

Dex hides behind a rack of miniature statues. His helmet makes the rack wobble. A row of souvenir bells rings in sequence.

VENN: The real bell is on the stand. Those are keychains.

MIRI: We have not touched it.

VENN: Then the alarm has not sounded. Please keep it that way.

SCENE 4 - INT. HISTORY GALLERY - MOMENTS LATER

Curator Sada arrives in a robe, carrying the museum's overnight log. She turns on the gallery lamp. The brass bell is mounted on a low stand, secured with a cable and a tag.

SADA: The bell is not priceless. It is on loan from the harbor school until morning.

MIRI: The dossier said it was the Crown Bell.

SADA: The Crown Bell is three rooms over and weighs two hundred pounds.

Rollo unfolds the map. The route ends at the wrong stand.

ROLLO: The dossier had two bell drawings. We picked the smaller one.

DEX: It was a remarkably detailed keychain.

SADA: Why are you here after hours?

MIRI: We planned a heist.

SADA: The museum has a volunteer handling session tomorrow. You can help move the display with staff present and a cart.

MIRI: That destroys the escape sequence.

VENN: It also avoids the alarm.

SCENE 5 - INT. MUSEUM LOADING HALL - MORNING

The crew returns with Sada and Venn. The harbor school has sent a representative, a young macaque named Tiko, to collect the bell. Rollo secures it in a padded case while Dex pushes the cart slowly. Miri reads the loan form aloud and signs in the wrong box; Tiko points to the line marked RECEIVED BY.

TIKO: The museum keeps a copy, and our school gets the original.

MIRI: The paperwork has defeated our final escape.

SADA: It has documented a normal return.

The toy remote slips from Dex's helmet and lands on the checkout mat. The terminal chirps, opens the gift shop door and prints a receipt for one postcard.

VENN: Someone has purchased a souvenir.

DEX: It is a postcard of a bell. We can keep the caper theme alive.

MIRI: No. We should pay for it.

FINAL IMAGE: The real bell rests safely in its padded case while the crew stands in line at the gift shop checkout holding a postcard.
