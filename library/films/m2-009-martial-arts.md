# The Dojo of Many Bowls

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: martial arts tournament cinema, training montages, and ritualized rivalries*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A gentle red-shanked douc learns a complicated tournament form to defend the community dojo, but her greatest opponent is a rival school's overly theatrical entrance routine.

Cast: Lin, a calm douc student; Master Taro, a red panda instructor; Brakk, a mandrill rival captain; Sumi, a macaque referee; and the dojo's rescue turtle, Bowl.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY DOJO, MORNING

Lin practices a slow sequence with a wooden spoon. Master Taro corrects her stance. A row of ceramic bowls sits on a shelf.

MASTER TARO: Balance first. Speed later.

Lin turns, bumps the shelf, and catches every bowl before it falls. Master Taro calls it good reflexes. Lin says she was reaching for breakfast.

SCENE 2. EXT. TOURNAMENT HALL, DAY

Brakk's team arrives through a cloud of stage smoke. The smoke machine coughs and fills their warm-up room. Brakk poses anyway, holding a banner that says FEAR OUR FORM. It unfolds backward and reads MROF RUO RAEF.

Lin and her team enter quietly. The announcer calls them "unassuming." Lin bows. Brakk challenges her to a dramatic duel before the match. Referee Sumi says the bracket starts after lunch.

SCENE 2A. INT. TRAINING HALL, EVENING

Lin practices the form alone. She moves slowly, correcting each stance. Master Taro watches from the doorway. He places a bowl on the end of a staff and asks her to carry it across the room without spilling.

Lin does it. Then he adds a second bowl. She adjusts her balance and carries both. A third bowl appears. She asks how many bowls are in the lesson.

MASTER TARO: Enough to make you think about your feet.

Lin turns the corner. The rescue turtle, Bowl, blocks the path. She stops, waits, and lets Bowl pass. The bowls remain steady. Master Taro claps once. Lin says she was really trying not to get stepped on.

SCENE 2B. INT. TOURNAMENT HALL, MORNING

Brakk's team practices their entrance. A drummer beats a fast rhythm, and the team charges through a paper arch. The arch sticks to Brakk's horns. His teammates follow, forming a paper-covered train. They march around the hall while pretending this is deliberate.

Sumi helps remove the arch. Brakk asks her to score the entrance. She marks it down for unsafe paper use. Brakk appeals. Sumi asks him to demonstrate the exit. The team tries to back through the arch and becomes tangled again.

Lin offers a pair of scissors. Brakk declines, saying the team must solve the challenge with discipline. One student frees them by walking backward. Brakk thanks him and calls it a secret technique.

SCENE 2C. INT. TROPHY HALL, AFTERNOON

The finalists wait beside a display of old trophies. Lin notices the tallest trophy leans toward the wall. She tells the staff. Brakk says a champion should fear no trophy. The shelf shifts. Lin and Brakk both catch it before it falls.

Sumi inspects the shelf and orders it fixed before the final. The organizer says there is no time. Brakk volunteers his team to hold the display. Lin's team helps. The two rival groups spend ten minutes bracing the shelves with their backs while the staff secures the brackets.

When the final begins, the teams are tired. They perform more slowly and with better control. The crowd gives them a standing ovation. The judges award a special prize for "most responsible use of shelving."

SCENE 3. INT. WARM-UP ROOM, LATER

Master Taro teaches Lin a new form with steps, turns, and a controlled stop. A montage begins: Lin practices on mats, in the hallway, beside the water cooler, and while waiting for noodles. Each time she knocks a bowl, she catches it and returns it to the shelf.

Brakk watches from the doorway. He copies her moves but adds a roar to each turn. His team follows. They spin too fast and tangle their sleeves together. Brakk claims this is a secret binding technique.

SCENE 4. INT. TOURNAMENT FLOOR, AFTERNOON

Lin faces Brakk. He starts with a sweeping feint. She steps aside, holds her balance, and gently redirects him toward the mat. He keeps spinning, unable to stop, and circles the judges' table. He bows to each judge as he passes.

Sumi raises her hand. The match stops. Brakk says he meant to do that. Lin helps him untangle his sleeves. The crowd applauds both teams. The next match begins, but the smoke machine fires again. Everyone coughs. Lin opens a side door and fans the smoke out with a banner.

SCENE 5. INT. DOJO, EVENING

The dojo hosts a shared meal. Brakk asks Master Taro to teach him the slow form. Lin shows him the catch-and-return move using bowls. Brakk catches one, then another. The third bowl is full of soup. He catches it, but the soup lands on his sash.

Brakk studies the stain and says it is a badge of honor. Sumi says it's lunch.

SCENE 6. INT. DOJO, EVENING

Brakk asks Lin to teach him the bowl exercise. He lifts three bowls, then sets one down too hard. The shelf wobbles. Lin catches the next bowl. Brakk catches her sleeve. Taro steadies the shelf with one hand and asks both students to breathe before trying again.

BRAKK: I thought balance meant holding still.
LIN: It means noticing when something shifts.
SUMI: And not roaring at it.

Brakk tries the form once more. He turns slowly, keeps his tail clear, and places each bowl on the mat. Bowl the turtle crosses between them. Brakk waits. The class claps. He says he meant to include the turtle. Lin hands him the broom to sweep up the spilled rice.

FINAL IMAGE: Lin, Brakk, and their teams bow together. Behind them, Bowl the turtle slowly crosses the floor and bumps the trophy. The trophy tips. Everyone catches it in a neat, practiced sequence. Master Taro nods. Lin asks if that counts as a form. He says it counts as cleaning up.
