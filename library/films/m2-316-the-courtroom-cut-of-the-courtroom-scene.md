# The Courtroom Cut of the Courtroom Scene

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: courtroom dramas that treat every objection, reveal, and dramatic pause as a reason to restart the trial*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR SURI, a macaque
EDITOR TAVI, a gibbon
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR NIMA, a lemur
PRODUCER VEX, a baboon
CLERK ACTOR FINN, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING ROOM - MORNING

A courtroom set fills the monitor. Pip, playing a witness, raises one paw. Nima, the opposing lawyer, rises at the same instant. Director Suri pauses the footage. Producer Vex leans over the editor's shoulder.

VEX: The pause is excellent. Put in another pause before it.

TAVI: Then the witness pauses, the lawyer pauses, and the jury pauses.

VEX: Now it feels important.

PIP: I was about to answer.

SURI: You still can. The cut just hasn't reached your answer yet.

Tavi backs up the timeline. The scene's case concerns a missing brass bell from the courthouse lobby. Pip saw the clerk carry a covered box out at noon. Nima asks what was in it. Pip points to a bell-shaped outline beneath a cloth.

VEX: We need a surprise witness.

NIMA: The clerk is right there.

VEX: A surprise clerk.

Suri brings in the actor playing the clerk, who has been waiting beside the monitor. Vex asks him to enter through a dramatic side door. There is no side door on the set.

SCENE 2. INT. COURTROOM SET - LATER

The crew films a new entrance. The clerk steps through the main door holding the box. Nima objects before he reaches the witness stand. Suri calls cut.

SURI: You don't know what he is carrying yet.

NIMA: The outline is shaped like a bell.

VEX: Save the outline for the trailer. Here, it is a mystery box.

Pip is given a second line: "What is in the box?" He asks it while standing directly beside the clerk.

PIP: What is in the box?

CLERK: The bell.

VEX: Too much answer. Keep the lid on until the verdict.

Tavi films the clerk setting the box on the evidence table. It lands with a clear metallic ring. Vex asks for a gasping reaction from the jury. The jury gasps so loudly that the microphone clips. They repeat the shot, this time whispering. A juror's stomach growls; Vex calls it a second bell.

SCENE 3. INT. COURTROOM SET - AFTERNOON

Suri returns to the original footage. Pip testifies that the clerk carried the box. Nima asks if he saw the bell. Pip says no, but heard it ring. The evidence is straightforward. Vex suggests the jury deliberate overnight, then return with a verdict at sunrise.

SURI: The case happens in one afternoon.

VEX: We can make the afternoon longer.

TAVI: We would need another window.

The painted windows show the same noon sun. Vex asks the lighting crew to make it look like sunset. A warm lamp swings into frame, illuminating the evidence table like a stage spotlight. The clerk looks directly into it and raises the lid. Inside is the brass bell, resting on a folded court notice. The notice says the bell was moved to muffle an annoying lobby chime during the hearing.

NIMA: So it wasn't stolen.

CLERK: I was going to return it after court.

VEX: Could he deny it once, then confess later?

PIP: He just confessed.

Vex asks for a second take in which the clerk says nothing. The actors wait. Suri lets the silent take run until the clerk's arm trembles under the box. Pip reaches out and helps him lower it.

SCENE 4. INT. EDITING ROOM - EVENING

Tavi cuts between the witness, the ringing box, and the clerk's embarrassed face. Vex inserts a dramatic objection over the bell's sound. The objection overlaps the only proof. Tavi removes it. Vex puts it back. They play both versions. In the first, the audience can hear the bell; in the second, Nima's objection makes it sound like a gong announcing a prize.

SURI: The bell is the evidence. Let it ring.

VEX: But the objection is a convention.

TAVI: It can wait until after the ring.

Nima records one clean objection into the editing-room microphone.

SCENE 4A. INT. COURTROOM SET - NIGHT

Suri gathers the actors for the verdict pickup. The clerk returns the bell to the lobby. Pip watches him go and turns to the jury.

PIP: We can finish now.

Vex asks for a verdict scene anyway. The jury raises cards marked NOT GUILTY, NOT GUILTY, and PLEASE STOP RINGING IT. Suri chooses the last card as the comedy button. Vex requests a close-up of the judge's thoughtful brow. The judge's brow is hidden under the powdered wig.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The audience follows the bell from testimony to evidence table, hears it ring, and understands the clerk's explanation. There is one objection, after the sound. The verdict is delivered before the credits. Vex watches his watch, disappointed that the trial ended in under two hours.

VEX: Could we release a director's deliberation cut?

SURI: The audience already deliberated.

A viewer rings the theater's service bell for more popcorn. Vex points to the screen, thrilled by the sequel hook. Tavi shows him the credits: SOUND RECORDED LIVE. Vex asks if the audience bell is in the movie. Suri shakes her head and ushers everyone out before he can add a post-credit scene.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex sits alone in the courtroom set, holding up an objection card to an empty jury box.
