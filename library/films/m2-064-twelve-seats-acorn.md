# Twelve Seats for the Acorn

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: jury-room dramas, dramatic votes, and arguments inflated by one missing fact*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: Twelve jurors deliberate over who took the community garden's ceremonial acorn, until their grand theories collapse under a basic sign-out sheet.

Cast: Foreperson Tavi, a macaque; Della, a cautious lemur; Brumm, a gorilla who loves dramatic speeches; Nib, a capuchin note-taker; and nine jurors from the garden club.

SCENE 1. INT. JURY ROOM, DAY

A velvet pouch sits open on the table. The ceremonial acorn is missing. Brumm rises to address the room.

BRUMM: We must determine who took the symbol of our shared roots.

DELLA: It's a walnut-sized acorn.

TAVI: The vote is on whether anyone removed it without signing it out.

Nib writes this down in enormous letters. The board already says THE ACORN CASE.

SCENE 2. INT. JURY ROOM, LATER

The jurors examine clues: a leaf, a smudge, and a chair that faces the window. Brumm connects them with string. Della asks why the string goes to the snack cabinet. Brumm says the snack cabinet has motive.

NIB: Motive for what?

BRUMM: To be near the acorn.

Tavi asks each juror where they were after the garden meeting. One remembers watering the seedlings; another was stacking chairs. A third says they saw someone leave with a blue bag. Everyone turns toward Della, who is holding a blue bag. She opens it. It contains gloves and a sandwich.

SCENE 3. INT. JURY ROOM, AFTERNOON

The jurors take another vote. Brumm asks each person to explain their suspicion in one sentence. The answers reveal they all saw the same empty pouch, but none checked the sign-out sheet. Nib records the result. Brumm wants the vote sealed in an envelope; Tavi says they can use the meeting minutes.

The first vote is eleven to one for theft. Della votes no. Brumm demands that she explain her doubt. She asks whether the acorn could have been moved for safekeeping. The room erupts in accusations of conspiracy.

TAVI: We are allowed to check the records.

BRUMM: The records are part of the mystery.

Nib finds the garden sign-out sheet beneath the minutes. It lists the acorn as borrowed by the junior planting club to demonstrate seed depth. The return box is blank.

SCENE 4. INT. JURY ROOM, CONTINUOUS

Brumm insists the photograph could have been staged. He requests a magnifying glass. Nib gives him one from the evidence kit. Brumm holds it backward and claims the squirrel looks suspiciously small. Della turns it around. The label on the demonstration tray is clear: ACORN, RETURN AFTER CLASS.

BRUMM: Could someone have forged the label?

TAVI: The ink matches the sign-out sheet.

BRUMM: Could someone have forged both?

DELLA: We can call the person who wrote them.

The club leader answers the phone. She confirms the handwriting and offers to bring the acorn to the room. Brumm asks her to wait until the jury reaches a verdict. Tavi says the verdict depends on the evidence, not the schedule.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, EVENING

Tavi calls the junior club leader. A young squirrel answers and says the acorn is still in the demonstration tray. Brumm asks whether the child understands the importance of evidence. The squirrel says yes and sends a photograph: the acorn beside a ruler and a tiny label.

The jurors look at the photo. The leaf is from the demonstration tray. The smudge is soil. The chair faced the window because that is where the light was best. Della changes her vote to not stolen. Brumm studies the board, then pulls down the red string.

SCENE 6. INT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, EVENING

The jurors return the acorn. The junior club has planted it in a pot, labeled DEMONSTRATION ONLY. Tavi says it must go back to the pouch after the lesson. The club leader agrees and adds a sign-out card to the tray.

Brumm asks if the deliberation can be entered into the garden archive. Nib says yes, as a reminder to check the form before accusing the snack cabinet. Brumm asks whether the archive will preserve his speech. Della says it can preserve the minutes.

Before leaving, Nib gathers the string, magnifier, and evidence cards. He asks if the red string can be checked out. Della says it is office supply. Brumm asks whether that makes it less suspicious.

The club leader returns the acorn in the labeled tray and asks where the classroom is. Brumm walks her there.

FINAL IMAGE: The acorn sits in its velvet pouch beside the sign-out sheet. Brumm signs it out to inspect the label, then immediately signs it back in.
