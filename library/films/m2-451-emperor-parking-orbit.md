# The Emperor of the Parking Orbit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-opera adventures that inflate a routine docking maneuver into a destiny-bearing interstellar crisis*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A young monkey pilot is ordered to dock a royal shuttle at a crowded orbital station, but the emperor keeps turning parking instructions into a galactic prophecy.

Cast: Pilot Veya, a macaque; Emperor Orlan, a gibbon; navigator Pott, a lemur; dockmaster Hali, an orangutan; royal guard Kesh, a capuchin who recorded the shuttle ceremony voice.

SCENE 1 - INT. ROYAL SHUTTLE - SPACE

A warning light blinks above the cockpit. Veya grips the controls. Emperor Orlan stands behind her, wearing a gold cape that floats in zero gravity.

ORLAN: The stars have aligned. We must enter the station by the seventh gate.

POTT: There is no seventh gate. Docking bay seven is on the station's lower ring.

VEYA: The station map shows bays one through twelve.

ORLAN: I was told the prophecy names seven.

KESH: The prophecy is the parking permit. It says bay seven is reserved for the royal shuttle.

Veya follows the docking beacon. The shuttle turns toward bay eight.

VEYA: The autopilot says bay seven is occupied.

ORLAN: A rival vessel blocks our destiny.

SCENE 2 - INT. STATION CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Dockmaster Hali speaks through the radio. A cargo tug sits in bay seven, unloading crates.

HALI: Bay four is cleared and available. Bay seven will clear after the tug unloads.

ORLAN: The crown cannot wait.

HALI: The cargo has to be unloaded before the shuttle can use bay seven.

POTT: The tug is carrying the royal banquet supplies.

ORLAN: Then it is part of the prophecy.

Veya aligns the shuttle with bay four. Orlan's cape floats over the instrument panel, obscuring the docking display.

VEYA: Your cape is covering the distance readout.

ORLAN: It is the royal banner.

KESH: Fold it, Your Majesty.

SCENE 2A - INT. ROYAL SHUTTLE - APPROACH

Orlan presses a gold button on the console. The cabin lights dim. A fanfare plays through the shuttle speakers while Veya tries to read the docking indicators.

VEYA: That button is for the ceremony, not the flight.

ORLAN: It announces our arrival.

POTT: We have not arrived. We are still outside the ring.

The fanfare ends. RECORDED KESH (V.O.): All hail the emperor of the stars. The station control channel cuts through.

HALI: Royal shuttle, bay four is clear. Your docking light is green.

Veya taps a switch. The fanfare returns louder. Kesh pulls the gold button cover down.

KESH: Ceremony disabled until we are parked.

Veya restores the cabin lights and checks the green docking lamp.

ORLAN: The honor guard will hear about this.

VEYA: The honor guard can hear us when the engines are off.

SCENE 3 - INT. STATION DOCKING RING - MOMENTS LATER

The shuttle lands safely at bay four. Hali waves the crew through the airlock. Orlan steps onto the station floor and asks where the ceremonial audience waits.

HALI: The meeting room is on the next corridor. The banquet crates are going there too.

ORLAN: The seventh gate has opened.

POTT: We are at bay four.

Orlan looks at the floor signs. A large arrow marked MEETING ROOM points down the hall. A second sign reads BAY 7 - RESERVED.

ORLAN: The numbers are symbolic.

Veya checks the cargo list. One crate is missing.

VEYA: The fruit crate did not come off the tug.

HALI: The loader needs an authorized manifest signature. I can supply it.

SCENE 4 - INT. CARGO OFFICE - LATER

Hali signs the manifest as authorized dockmaster. The missing crate is located on the rear pallet, still aboard. Station loaders and Veya roll it onto a cart. Orlan tries to lift it with one hand for the ceremonial procession. It does not move.

ORLAN: The crate is heavier than its omen suggested.

VEYA: It is full of pears.

POTT: We can roll it down the marked corridor.

The royal cape catches on a cart handle. Kesh frees it. Orlan folds the cape and carries it under one arm.

SCENE 5 - INT. STATION MEETING ROOM - EVENING

The crew arrives with the fruit crate and the folded cape. The emperor's guests wait beside a simple table. Orlan opens the royal permit and finds a note written by the station office: BAY 7 AVAILABLE AFTER 18:00.

ORLAN: We have arrived at the appointed hour.

HALI: Bay seven is clear now. The shuttle can move after dinner.

VEYA: Or it can stay at bay four. It is a legal berth.

ORLAN: The prophecy did not specify which bay.

POTT: It specified seven. We know why.

The guests laugh. Orlan serves pears from the crate and offers one to the tug crew.

FINAL IMAGE: The royal shuttle remains parked neatly in bay four while the folded cape rests on the emperor's chair, no longer covering a single instrument.
