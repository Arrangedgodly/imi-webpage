# Emergency at the Top Floor

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action-disaster films, where a service lift stuck one floor below its destination triggers a high-stakes rescue*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A lift full of party supplies stalls between floors, and the building crew must coordinate a rescue before the cake arrives.

Cast: Engineer Pava, gibbon; caretaker Odo, baboon; courier Miri, capuchin; host Tavi, macaque.

SCENE 1. INT. SERVICE LIFT, DAY

The lift stops with a jolt. Boxes shift. Miri steadies a small cake.

MIRI: We have stopped.

TAVI: How far from the party room?

MIRI: One floor.

TAVI: How far from the ground?

MIRI: Several floors.

Odo speaks through the intercom.

ODO: Stay still. I am checking the panel.

TAVI: Can you see us?

ODO: I can hear you.

Pava arrives at the control box with a tool roll. She says the lift's safety brake engaged because a box blocked the door sensor.

PAVA: Do not force the doors.

TAVI: We are not forcing anything.

MIRI: The cake is leaning.

TAVI: Keep the cake level.

SCENE 2. INT. LIFT SHAFT, CONTINUOUS

Odo climbs the maintenance ladder to the access panel. Pava warns him to lock the lift first. He checks the brake and confirms it is engaged.

ODO: The car is secure.

PAVA: Now we can open the service hatch.

TAVI: We are losing time.

PAVA: The party has not started.

TAVI: The guests are arriving.

MIRI: The cake is still level.

Odo opens the hatch from above. The gap is too narrow for the boxes. Pava says they should remove the obstruction through the side access panel. Miri slides a small parcel toward the door, but it catches.

MIRI: The parcel is wedged.

TAVI: It contains napkins.

ODO: The napkins can wait.

TAVI: They are printed with the guest of honor's face.

PAVA: Then the guest can wait too.

SCENE 3. INT. LIFT, MOMENTS LATER

Pava speaks to the passengers through the panel. She asks them to move the boxes away from the sensor, one at a time. Tavi tries to move a large fruit crate. The crate blocks the cake.

MIRI: The cake cannot pass.

TAVI: We can move the cake first.

PAVA: Do not lift anything while the lift is unlocked.

Odo checks the brake again. The lift remains secured. Miri moves the cake onto a stable shelf. Tavi and Miri slide the fruit crate away from the door.

[SFX: BOX SLIDES, SENSOR BEEPS.]

PAVA: The obstruction is clear.

The doors do not open.

ODO: The brake is still engaged.

PAVA: The car must be level before the brake releases.

TAVI: It is one floor below.

PAVA: It is a few inches below.

SCENE 4. INT. CONTROL ROOM, LATER

Pava uses the manual lowering crank to bring the lift to the floor. Odo guides the car from above. Tavi counts the turns.

TAVI: One. Two. Three.

MIRI: Stop. The cake is sliding.

Tavi braces it. Pava turns the crank again. The lift aligns with the floor. The door sensor beeps.

[SFX: BEEP, DOORS UNLOCK.]

The doors open. The party guests are waiting outside. Tavi steps out holding the cake.

TAVI: We have arrived.

MIRI: We have moved one floor.

TAVI: It was an important floor.

Before the party begins, Miri notices one box is still in the lift. Odo rides the stairs to retrieve it, but the lift remains locked out while Pava checks the sensor.

ODO: The box contains the plates.

TAVI: We can serve cake without plates.

MIRI: We cannot carry cake slices by hand.

Pava tests the sensor with an empty crate. It clears properly. She marks the lift out of service for another inspection, then asks Odo to bring the plates by the stairwell.

ODO: The stairs are safe.

Miri asks for a head count. The guests are all present, but the guest of honor is upstairs and has not heard about the lift.

TAVI: Should we bring the cake to them?

PAVA: The room is ready. Let them use the stairs at their own pace.

TAVI: We have rescued the cake, the plates, and the schedule.

MIRI: The guest is still on the stairs.

ODO: We can wait at the landing.

TAVI: It is an important landing.

ODO: They are also several floors.

ODO: It is an important climb.

SCENE 5. INT. PARTY ROOM, MOMENTS LATER

The cake is set on a table. The napkins are still in the lift. Odo retrieves them and brings them up by the stairs.

ODO: All supplies delivered.

TAVI: We are ready.

PAVA: The lift is out of service until the sensor is checked.

TAVI: How will the guests go down?

PAVA: The stairs.

TAVI: They have already climbed one floor.

MIRI: It was an important floor.

Tavi cuts the cake. The first slice leans, but Miri catches it with a napkin. Everyone applauds the rescue.

[SFX: APPLAUSE, CAKE PLATE.]

PAVA: The lift remains closed.

ODO: The cake is safe.

TAVI: The party can proceed.

MIRI: The napkins are fine too.

[SFX: PARTY MUSIC, LIFT DOORS CLOSE.]
