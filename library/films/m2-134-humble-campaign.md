# A Humble Campaign

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: award-season campaigns that perform humility while coordinating every interview, meal, and reaction shot*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A director insists her film needs no awards while her publicist schedules a month of "casual" appearances to say exactly that.

Cast: Lomi, a macaque director; Varo, a gorilla publicist; Pera, a lemur interviewer; and Tavi, a capuchin assistant.

SCENE 1. INT. PRESS SUITE, MORNING

Lomi sits before a plain backdrop. Varo adjusts a small sign that says "NO CAMPAIGN MATERIALS." Beneath the table, Tavi has stacked brochures about the film.

VARO: We want to seem grateful, not eager.

LOMI: I am grateful.

VARO: Good. Say the film was a small personal project.

Tavi slides a schedule across the table. It lists eight interviews, two lunches, and a walk past the award display. Lomi asks if the walk is part of the film. Varo says it is part of being seen near the film.

Pera arrives to interview Lomi. Varo asks her not to mention nominations. Pera says she was planning to ask how the film was made. Varo hands her a card with approved questions. One asks how Lomi feels about being "overlooked by the awards."

PERA: Has anyone overlooked it?

VARO: That is why we should prepare.

SCENE 2. INT. HOTEL LOBBY, AFTERNOON

Lomi and Varo stage a casual conversation beside a display of trophies. Tavi photographs them from across the lobby. Lomi says she has not thought about awards. Varo nods toward the trophy shelf.

LOMI: I did not think about awards.

VARO: You looked at the awards.

LOMI: They are in front of me.

Varo moves them behind a potted plant. A hotel guest asks if the plant is part of a film promotion. Tavi takes a picture of the guest asking. Varo says it looks spontaneous. Lomi asks whether the guest has agreed to be photographed. Tavi lowers the camera.

Pera returns with a recorder. She asks Lomi what the film is about. Lomi gives a clear answer: a group of caretakers keeps a small theater open after the roof leaks. Varo says the answer needs a personal angle. Lomi says she worked in a theater as a teenager. Pera asks what she learned there.

LOMI: That a roof leak is not improved by a speech.

SCENE 3. INT. PRESS SUITE, EVENING

Varo watches the interview playback. Lomi's answer about the theater is sincere. Varo asks her to repeat it with less polish so it sounds unrehearsed. Lomi repeats it, then asks if Varo wants her to forget the story between takes.

Tavi prepares a still photograph. It shows Lomi holding a coffee cup beside the "NO CAMPAIGN MATERIALS" sign. Varo likes the frame but asks Tavi to remove the brochure stack reflected in the window. Tavi moves the stack. The reflection now shows the interview schedule.

PERA: This picture says you planned not to plan.

VARO: It says she is approachable.

LOMI: I am sitting in a hotel lobby.

Pera asks Lomi if she would make the film again. Lomi says yes, but she would fix the roof before opening night. Varo asks if that is an awards quote. Lomi says it is a practical answer. Tavi asks if they need a shot of the roof repair. Pera turns the camera toward the patched ceiling. A drip lands in Varo's coffee cup.

VARO: Is this part of the story?

LOMI: It is part of the building.

They move the cup and call the caretaker to check the ceiling. Varo cancels the planned photo by the trophy display.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY THEATER, NIGHT

The campaign crew arrives at the theater for one last photograph. The roof has been repaired. Caretakers sweep the floor and stack chairs. Varo asks them to pause so Lomi can stand by the new patch.

A caretaker hands Lomi a broom. She sweeps the stage. Tavi takes a picture, then lowers the camera when the crew starts working again. Pera asks if this is the final interview. Varo says it is a quiet moment between events.

LOMI: It is just the theater.

Varo folds the schedule. A caretaker asks whether the campaign crew can stack the chairs they moved. Tavi and Pera help. Varo looks at his schedule, then at the chairs, and joins them. Lomi keeps sweeping until the stage is clear. The awards display is still in the hotel lobby. The crew packs up and leaves the caretakers to finish. At the door, Tavi asks if the broom should appear in the press photograph. Lomi hands it back to the caretaker. Varo asks whether the picture can look unplanned. The caretaker points to the camera and says the theater closes in ten minutes.

FINAL IMAGE: A framed campaign photograph hangs in the press suite: Lomi looks humble beside the brochure stack, which is visible in the glass behind her.
