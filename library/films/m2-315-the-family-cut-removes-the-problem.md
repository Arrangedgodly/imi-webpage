# The Family Cut Removes the Problem

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: family-friendly edits that erase every conflict while leaving characters reacting to what has been removed*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
EDITOR NARI, an orangutan
DIRECTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR ODO, a baboon
DISTRIBUTOR VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING SUITE - MORNING

Editor Nari plays a scene from a small adventure film. Luma's character stands outside a garden gate. Odo holds the key on the other side. Director Pip watches the timeline. Distributor Vex carries a ratings form.

LUMA (ON SCREEN): Give me the key.

ODO (ON SCREEN): I can't. The lock is jammed.

VEX: Too much conflict.

PIP: That is the whole scene.

VEX: Could she ask more politely?

Nari trims the argument. Luma now says, "May I have the key?" Odo replies, "Certainly," but the gate stays shut.

VEX: The obstacle is still present.

NARI: The obstacle is a gate.

VEX: Can we remove the gate?

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING SUITE - LATER

Nari removes the gate shot. Luma's character looks at a blank wall and asks for the key. Odo hands it to her. She tries the key on empty air.

PIP: The scene no longer has a place.

VEX: It has a friendship.

Luma visits the editing suite and watches. She points to Odo's floating hand. Nari restores the gate shot. Vex says the gate looks dangerous because its hinge is rusty. Odo suggests repainting it. Pip asks whether the character should still need to cross.

VEX: Crossing implies separation.

LUMA: We are on opposite sides.

VEX: We can move you to the same side.

Nari drags the shot. Now Luma and Odo stand together while the locked gate blocks an empty path. Vex marks the frame APPROVED, then notices both actors are staring through the bars at the seedling they need to save.

SCENE 3. INT. GARDEN SET - NOON

They reshoot the scene. Luma and Odo stand on opposite sides of the gate. Odo holds the key; rain clouds gather beyond the painted sky. Vex asks Nari to remove the clouds. Pip objects: the characters have to carry the seedling to shelter before the rain.

VEX: Weather pressure may feel frightening.

PIP: It is a gray cloud.

VEX: Could it be a friendly gray cloud?

Odo reaches the key through the bars. It catches on the rusted hinge. Luma cannot turn it from her side. The seedling droops in its pot as the first drops strike the set.

LUMA: The key will not turn.

ODO: The hinge is jammed.

VEX: Can we call it resting?

NARI: The hinge is metal.

Luma looks at the seedling, then at the can of oil on the gardener's bench. Pip keeps rolling. Vex raises his ratings form between the lens and the action. Nari lowers it. The scene ends with the key stuck halfway and one fat drop landing in the pot.

SCENE 4. INT. GARDEN SET - AFTERNOON

Vex asks for a cut where the gate simply opens. Nari plays the footage back: the key catches, the seedling is exposed, and Luma notices the oil. Pip points out the cause and the choice. Vex asks if the oil can be a friendship gift. Odo applies one drop to the hinge. It still squeaks.

VEX: That sound says something is wrong.

LUMA: Something is wrong. We are fixing it.

Pip lets that line stay. Odo oils the hinge again. Luma turns the key, and the gate swings wide. Rainwater has gathered in the path. Odo holds a plank across the puddle; Luma carries the seedling. They cross together, keeping the roots level. The pot wobbles, and Odo steadies it with his elbow. Vex reaches for the stop button, but Pip keeps the shot running until both feet are on dry ground.

VEX: Is the seedling all right?

NARI: It is a prop.

VEX: Then it is fine.

PIP: The scene is fine too.

Nari saves the take. Vex crosses out his proposed gate removal, though he adds a note to shorten the squeak by half a second.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - EVENING

The finished cut plays for a small audience. The key catches; the rain starts; the actors oil the hinge and bridge the puddle. Vex grips his ratings form as the plank bends under the pot. The seedling tips. Luma and Odo catch it between them, perfectly level, then take one synchronized step onto dry ground.

A child in the front row laughs at the carefully saved plant. Vex looks down at the form. Its corner is wet from a leak in the ceiling. He moves his chair, but the drop follows the form's new position.

VEX: Is the room certified for family viewing?

NARI: The room is not in the film.

PIP: Yet.

Vex puts the ratings form over his head. The audience laughs again. He watches the final frame without touching the timeline.

FINAL IMAGE: Onscreen, the gate swings shut gently behind the seedling; offscreen, Vex's paper umbrella collapses into his lap.
