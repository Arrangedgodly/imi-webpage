# One More Take at the Waterfall

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: adventure filmmaking, where a director keeps demanding a more dangerous waterfall shot from a cautious crew*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A film crew tries to shoot a dramatic crossing beside a small waterfall, but the director's repeated takes keep moving the camera closer to the water.

Cast: Director Pavo, capuchin; stunt performer Nara, macaque; camera operator Odo, baboon; guide Luma, gibbon.

SCENE 1. EXT. STREAMSIDE, DAY

A shallow stream runs over flat stones. The camera is set behind a safety rope. Pavo studies the view.

PAVO: It needs more danger.

LUMA: The crossing is safe.

PAVO: The audience must feel the risk.

NARA: I can stumble on the dry bank.

PAVO: The waterfall should be in frame.

ODO: The waterfall is behind the camera.

Pavo asks Odo to move the camera closer to the stream. Luma says the bank is soft. Odo plants one tripod leg, and it sinks.

ODO: The camera is tilting.

PAVO: It looks dramatic.

LUMA: It is sinking.

Nara steps onto a flat stone and crosses carefully.

PAVO: Cut. That was too competent.

NARA: I was told to cross.

PAVO: This is an adventure.

SCENE 2. EXT. STREAM, MOMENTS LATER

Pavo asks Nara to look back while crossing. She does. The camera shifts, and Odo reaches to steady it.

[SFX: TRIPOD LEG SQUELCH.]

ODO: The frame is moving.

PAVO: Keep rolling.

LUMA: The tripod is slipping.

Pavo calls for another take. Luma moves the camera back behind the safety rope. The shot shows the stream, the rocks, and Nara crossing without risk.

PAVO: The rope is visible.

LUMA: The rope is for safety.

PAVO: It breaks the illusion.

NARA: The illusion is that I'm in danger.

Pavo gestures for another take. This time Nara pauses at the stream and tests each stone with a stick. Pavo says the pause is too long.

NARA: I am checking the footing.

LUMA: That is the right thing to do.

PAVO: The right thing is rarely cinematic.

SCENE 3. EXT. UPSTREAM POOL, LATER

Luma guides the crew to a calmer pool where they can film a close-up of the water. Pavo wants a splash. Odo drops a leaf in. It floats.

PAVO: That is not a splash.

NARA: I can step in.

LUMA: The pool is deeper at the center.

NARA: I will stay at the edge.

Pavo asks her to slip slightly. She refuses. The crew debates how to show danger without creating any. Odo suggests tilting the camera. Pavo agrees.

They take the shot. The horizon slants; the water seems to climb the screen.

PAVO: The waterfall is rising.

LUMA: The camera is crooked.

Odo levels it. Pavo says it was better crooked. Nara finds a dry branch and lays it across two stones, creating a small bridge.

NARA: We can use this.

PAVO: The branch is too simple.

LUMA: It works.

Before the crossing take, Odo asks Luma to stand where she can grab the safety line. Pavo wants the line outside the frame, but Luma says it is the only thing keeping the camera tripod from sliding into the water.

LUMA: The rope protects the camera.

PAVO: It blocks the shot.

ODO: I would like to keep the camera.

Nara suggests tying the rope to a tree behind the camera. They test the knot. The tripod stays upright; the line remains invisible from the lens.

PAVO: Now the danger is cinematic.

LUMA: Now the equipment is anchored.

SCENE 4. EXT. STREAMSIDE, AFTERNOON

The crew films the crossing with the branch in place. Nara steps over it. The branch bends but holds. Odo keeps the camera behind the rope. The water remains calm.

PAVO: Could you look more afraid?

NARA: No.

PAVO: Could you look determined?

NARA: Yes.

She crosses with a steady expression. Pavo likes the shot but asks for another take with a longer pause. Nara looks back, then continues. The shot works.

SCENE 5. EXT. TRAILHEAD, SUNSET

Pavo reviews the footage. The camera caught the crossing, the leaf, the tilted horizon, and the branch bridge.

PAVO: The danger is real in the edit.

LUMA: The water is still shallow.

ODO: The tripod is dry.

NARA: And no one slipped.

Pavo thanks Nara for the performance. She asks whether she can remove the safety rope now. Luma keeps it up until the gear is packed.

PAVO: One final take, just the waterfall.

Odo points the camera at the stream. A leaf floats past.

PAVO: Cut.

LUMA: That was a good take.

PAVO: It needs more danger.

The leaf catches on a rock and stops.

NARA: The leaf has taken a risk.

[SFX: STREAM, CAMERA SHUTTER.]
