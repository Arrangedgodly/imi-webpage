# The Case of the Missing Voiceover

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: noir detective films, smoky monologues, suspicious shadows, and private eyes who narrate clues after they have already solved the case*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
DETECTIVE VEX, a macaque
JUNE, a capuchin records clerk
LUMA, a lemur projectionist
MR. ODO, an orangutan theater owner
THE NARRATOR, Vex's own recorded voice

SCENE 1. INT. EMPTY MOVIE THEATER - NIGHT

Rain taps the roof. Detective Vex stands in the aisle, facing a blank screen. A voice speaks from the projector booth.

NARRATOR: The theater had a secret.

VEX: Who said that?

The voice repeats the sentence, then a click. Vex looks at the microphone in his coat pocket. It is recording his earlier practice line.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTOR BOOTH - NIGHT

Luma explains that the booth's speaker has been playing an old audio reel. The reel contains Vex's noir narration from last month's case. June says the theater's caption machine also lost its script and has been displaying fragments of the voiceover. Vex writes both facts in his notebook under "possible conspiracy."

ODO: It is just the projector.

VEX: That's what it wants us to think.

Luma winds the reel to find the missing sound. It plays the words "follow the paper trail" and then stops. A strip of film hangs from the projector. Vex declares it evidence.

SCENE 3. INT. TICKET OFFICE - LATER

The desk lamp throws Vex's shadow across the wall. He narrates that the missing reel has cast a shadow over the entire case. June turns on the overhead lights, revealing that the shadow is from his coat hanging on a hook. Vex takes the coat down, then notices the hook is shaped like a question mark. He writes it in the notebook anyway.

JUNE: That hook holds coats.

VEX: Tonight it holds a clue.

June shows him the log entries: Luma signed out the reel for a test, returned a different canister by mistake, and stored the soundtrack in the cabinet. The record is clear, but the handwriting is smudged where a coffee cup sat on it. Vex asks for a fingerprint. June hands him a napkin and points to the coffee ring.

SCENE 4. INT. TICKET OFFICE - CONTINUOUS

June produces the maintenance log. It shows a spool was removed during a projector repair and never returned. The paper trail leads to a storage cabinet. Vex finds a dusty footprint beside it and crouches dramatically. The footprint is his own; his shoes are dusty from the booth.

VEX: The detective leaves a trail.

JUNE: The detective should clean his shoes.

The cabinet is locked. Odo brings the key ring. Three keys look alike. Vex tries one; it opens the cabinet. Inside are old reels, a flashlight, a half-eaten pear, and a missing invoice. Luma recognizes the invoice as a record of the sound reel's replacement.

SCENE 5. INT. PROJECTOR BOOTH - NIGHT

They locate the reel under a stack of silent-film canisters. Luma plays it. Vex's recorded narration describes a different case, including a line about a cat at the service door. A cat has been waiting outside the current theater, but it is there to catch a mouse.

VEX: I knew the voice was warning me.

JUNE: The recording was from last month.

Vex asks Luma to play the reel from the beginning. The audio starts with a tap, a click, and his own voice saying, "Testing one, two." June recognizes the pattern: the projector only plays a section when the booth door is opened. The old latch vibrates against the speaker wire, triggering the reel. Luma tightens the latch and plays the recording again. The narrator is silent. Vex makes a note to label future reels by date.

Vex replays the line. It now sounds like a perfectly ordinary sentence. Odo takes the reel away before Vex can label it evidence. Luma checks the caption machine and finds the script file saved in the wrong folder. June restores the correct caption schedule.

SCENE 6. INT. EMPTY MOVIE THEATER - DAWN

The theater opens for a matinee. Vex tries to narrate his findings live, but the microphone is disconnected. He writes the conclusion in his notebook instead: old recording, misplaced spool, repaired caption file. Luma points to the speaker, now silent. Odo asks Vex to turn on the house lights. He reaches for the switch and finds it beside the exit.

June shows him the corrected maintenance log. The case is closed. Vex asks if the film can still end on a mysterious image. Luma points the camera at the blank screen. Vex's reflection appears on it, holding the notebook upside down.

FINAL IMAGE: The projector beam crosses the empty room, illuminating a single caption: "Please take your seats."
