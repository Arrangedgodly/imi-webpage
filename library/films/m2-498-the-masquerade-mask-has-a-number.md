# The Masquerade Mask Has a Number

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: costume mysteries where anonymous guests trade clues at a ball despite the organizers numbering every mask*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A detective attends a masked palace ball to identify a mysterious guest, but the coatroom's numbered tags reveal everyone's identity before the orchestra begins.

Cast: detective Osa, a macaque; host Vela, a gibbon; masked guest Piri, a lemur; coat attendant Dena, an orangutan; musician Kori, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. PALACE BALLROOM - NIGHT

Guests enter wearing decorated masks. Osa carries a notebook and studies the invitation, which says MASKS REQUIRED, NUMBERS OPTIONAL. At the coatroom, Dena pins a number tag to each mask so the guests can retrieve them later.

OSA: Anonymous guests are difficult to question.

DENA: The numbers are for lost property.

Osa receives number 12. Piri receives 13. Vela asks everyone not to announce their numbers until the unmasking at midnight.

SCENE 2 - INT. BALLROOM - LATER

Kori plays a waltz. Osa questions guests about a masked visitor who left before the unmasking. Each guest gives a different clue about the coatroom. Vela says no one has entered the archive since the ball began. Dena points to the archive key, still hanging behind her desk.

OSA: The key is accounted for. The guest is not.

VELA: We have not reached the unmasking.

Osa continues the investigation anyway. She asks Piri to dance. The masks bump. Osa's number tag falls off and lands in the punch bowl. Dena retrieves it with a serving spoon and writes the number in the ledger.

SCENE 3 - INT. COATROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Osa discovers two masks labeled 13. One is Piri's feathered mask; the other is a plain spare. Dena explains that the spare is for guests who forget theirs. Osa asks who took it. Dena checks the sign-out sheet. Vela borrowed it for the midnight unmasking rehearsal.

VELA: I needed to check the announcement.

OSA: You said no one entered the archive.

VELA: The coatroom is not the archive.

Osa finds a silver key-shaped pin in the spare mask. Piri says it is costume jewelry. Kori examines the pin and says it is not the archive key; it is the fastener holding the mask's beak closed.

Osa interviews the guests one at a time. Each says the silver key was last seen on a different surface: the piano, the coat desk, the archive shelf. Dena asks whether they mean the display key or the real key on her ring. Osa places a handkerchief over the ring as if preserving evidence. Kori plays a tense chord. The guests all turn toward the piano, convinced the music is a clue. Osa checks beneath it and finds only a dropped dance card.

Osa asks Kori to stop the waltz so she can hear the guests' footsteps. The silence makes every shoe squeak sound suspicious. Piri tiptoes toward the piano; Vela tiptoes toward the coatroom; Dena remains still behind the desk. Osa follows the loudest squeak and finds her own shoe heel loose. Kori resumes the music while she tightens it with a ribbon from her notebook. The guests applaud the detective's repair as if it were a reveal.

SCENE 4 - INT. BALLROOM - MIDNIGHT

Vela rings a bell. The guests gather for the unmasking. Osa announces that the mystery was not a theft but a combination of a misplaced key and confusing mask tags. She asks everyone to reveal their numbers. The guests remove their masks and compare them to the coatroom ledger. The two number 13s belong to Piri and the spare mask, exactly as recorded.

Piri finds the real key still on Dena's hook. She returns it to the archive. Osa writes a note that reads CASE CLOSED. The ink runs from the damp notebook, leaving only CASE CL. Vela congratulates her on the abbreviated report.

Before the guests leave, Osa checks the coatroom ledger against the masks on the rack. One tag is missing: number 12. She searches the ballroom and finds it stuck to the bottom of her notebook, where the punch spilled. Dena holds up Osa's mask; its empty hook is visible through the eye slit. Osa removes the mask and pins the tag back on it. The mystery of the missing number is solved by the detective wearing it.

SCENE 5 - INT. PALACE BALLROOM - LATER

The orchestra resumes. Guests collect their masks by number. Osa returns number 12 and receives her own plain mask. She asks Dena if she can keep it as evidence. Dena says it belongs to the palace. Osa writes its return date in the ledger.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty ballroom floor is scattered with numbered mask tags, while the silver key hangs plainly behind the coatroom desk.
