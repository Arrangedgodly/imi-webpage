# Again at the Museum Gate

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-loop comedies that repeat a day until the hero makes a grand personal transformation instead of changing one practical choice*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A monkey relives the museum's opening minute until she stops trying to win a perfect entrance and asks the guard where the group tickets are.

Cast: Lala, a macaque docent; Pev, a lemur guard; Anki, a gibbon tour leader; Koro, a capuchin visitor; the Museum Clock.

SCENE 1 - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - MORNING

A large clock clicks from 9:00 to 9:01. Lala arrives at the front gate carrying an enormous welcome banner. A gust pushes the banner over her face. She bumps into Pev, who drops a stack of tickets.

LALA: This is my first day as the docent. I planned a perfect entrance.

PEV: You have arrived one minute after opening and blocked the gate.

Lala looks at the clock. It clicks backward to 9:00. The banner snaps upright. She is back outside the gate, holding the same banner.

SCENE 2 - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - MOMENTS LATER

Lala enters more slowly, holding the banner carefully. Koro asks where the tour begins. Lala gestures grandly toward the gallery and walks backward into the ticket stand. Tickets scatter again.

KORO: Are the tickets part of the exhibit?

LALA: They are a dramatic introduction.

Pev gathers them. The clock clicks backward. Lala reappears outside.

SCENE 3 - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - THIRD TRY

Lala leaves the banner behind and enters carrying a small card. Pev nods. Anki arrives with a group of twelve. The ticket scanner beeps red.

ANKI: I booked the 9:00 tour.

LALA: I can check the list.

The list is under the banner, which lies over the ticket desk. Lala lifts it, and the banner sweeps the tickets onto the floor. The clock resets.

SCENE 3A - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - REPEATED MINUTE

The clock snaps back. Lala returns outside, now holding only the small card. She enters and takes a breath before greeting anyone. Pev puts down the ticket stack.

LALA: I am going to wait until the group is here.

KORO: We are here.

LALA: I mean the entire group.

Anki enters with eleven visitors behind him. One carries a folded umbrella that springs open in the doorway. The umbrella catches the corner of Lala's welcome banner, pulling it down across the ticket counter.

PEV: The entrance is blocked again.

LALA: I can lift the banner while you bring them in.

The group pushes forward. The umbrella turns inside out. Tickets slide under the desk. The clock clicks backward.

SCENE 3B - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - SAME MINUTE

Lala stands outside once more. The umbrella is closed. She leaves the banner rolled up and moves the map stand away from the gate. She enters and holds the door as Anki's group arrives. The group waits in a neat line. Lala checks the card and sees only the tour title, not the time.

LALA: Pev, can you tell me where the schedule is?

PEV: On the blue clipboard, under the map stand.

Lala reaches for it, but the group asks about their tickets. She turns to answer, steps onto the map stand's base and tips the clipboard to the floor. The clock clicks backward again.

SCENE 4 - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - FOURTH TRY

Lala sits on a bench before opening. She writes down each event: banner, tickets, group, list. The clock clicks forward to 9:00. She enters, but does not bring the banner. Pev starts stacking the tickets.

LALA: Before the group arrives, where is the reservation list?

PEV: On the desk, beneath the blue clipboard.

Lala picks up the clipboard. The list is there. She checks the booking. The time reads 9:15, not 9:00.

LALA: The tour is fifteen minutes later.

ANKI: We are early. The group can wait by the map.

Lala points the visitors toward a clear waiting area. No tickets fall. The clock continues to 9:01.

SCENE 5 - INT. MUSEUM ENTRY - 9:15

The group checks in. The scanner beeps green. Lala begins her introduction with a normal voice.

LALA: Welcome. We will start with the map, then visit the sculpture gallery.

KORO: Is the banner part of the tour?

LALA: It is a banner. It says welcome.

Pev moves it away from the entrance so it cannot block the line. Lala watches the clock. It advances to 9:16. Nothing resets.

ANKI: You look relieved.

LALA: I was trying to make the first minute perfect. I only had to find the right list and the right time.

Pev returns the tickets to the drawer. Koro takes a photograph of the group by the map. Lala stands beside them rather than in front of the banner.

The museum clock ticks normally. One ticket slips from the drawer and lands at Lala's feet. She picks it up and returns it to the stack without a flourish.

FINAL IMAGE: The welcome banner hangs neatly beside the map, while the museum clock's second hand moves past the minute mark without turning backward.
