# The Detective Who Investigated His Own Reflection

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: noir mysteries where every clue is a dramatic reflection*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
DETECTIVE VEX, an orangutan
PARTNER MARA, a macaque
CLERK PIP, a capuchin
CAMERA OPERATOR ODO, a gibbon
WITNESS NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. OFFICE - NIGHT

Vex stands before a dark window. A figure appears behind him. He reaches for his notebook.

VEX: Someone entered the room.

MARA: That's your reflection. The office lights are brighter than the street.

ODO: The camera shows the door stayed closed.

PIP: I was at the filing cabinet the whole time.

VEX: The culprit could be invisible.

MARA: Or the glass could be reflecting you.

SCENE 2. INT. OFFICE - CONTINUOUS

Vex places a desk lamp near the window. The reflected figure brightens. Mara moves the lamp away; it fades.

VEX: The suspect reacts to light.

NARI: So does a reflection.

PIP: The missing case file is on the cabinet. I moved it there to keep it away from the open window.

VEX: A motive.

MARA: A location. Let's check the file number.

ODO: The folder is the one from yesterday's plant theft report.

SCENE 2A. INT. DETECTIVE OFFICE - NIGHT

Mara turns on the ceiling light. Vex's reflection becomes clear in the dark window. He stands still; the reflected figure stands still too.

MARA: Raise your right hand.

Vex raises his right hand. The reflection mirrors him.

VEX: The suspect is copying my movements.

NARI: That is what a reflection does.

ODO: The camera timestamp shows the office door never opened. I can pull the last hour of footage.

PIP: The file you are looking for is the plant report. I moved it because someone set a drink on the desk.

VEX: Why was the file open?

PIP: I was adding the greenhouse return date. The plant has been back for two days.

MARA: Let's read the report before inventing another suspect.

Odo opens the folder. A pressed leaf is stuck to the last page. Vex holds it up to the window; the leaf's shadow overlaps his face in the reflection.

VEX: The clue has changed shape.

MARA: You moved the leaf. The reflection moved because you moved.

NARI: The report says the plant was mistakenly taken to the greenhouse for watering and returned the next day.

ODO: I can close the case and file the corrected location.

PIP: I'll remove the leaf and put it in the compost. It's not evidence.

VEX: It was the only physical clue.

MARA: It was a bookmark. The case has a documented answer.

Odo turns off the desk lamp and lowers the blinds. The window turns black; no second figure remains.

SCENE 2B. INT. OFFICE - NIGHT

Odo plays the camera footage. It shows Pip taking the folder from the desk, moving it to the cabinet, and returning to the desk with a watering can.

VEX: The camera has captured the suspect.

PIP: I moved the folder so water would not spill on it. The can was for the plant by the window.

MARA: The footage confirms what you told us. No one took the file out of the office.

ODO: I can add a note that the file was moved away from the drink and returned after watering.

NARI: The plant is still in the greenhouse, not in this room. Its shadow cannot be on the window now.

Vex looks at the window. His reflection remains the only silhouette.

VEX: I concede that the second figure is me.

MARA: Case closed. Put the camera copy back in the secure folder and turn off the lights before we leave.

PIP: I'll file the plant report under completed cases, not active investigations.

ODO: The folder is closed. The leaf is in compost. The room is empty except for us.

SCENE 3. EXT. OFFICE WINDOW - NIGHT

The team steps outside. Vex looks through the glass and sees no figure. Mara draws the blinds.

MARA: The reflection is gone because the interior is hidden.

VEX: The witness has vanished.

PIP: The witness is inside, closing the office.

NARI: I'll check the latch. It is secure.

SCENE 4. INT. OFFICE - LATER

Pip opens the folder. A pressed leaf falls onto the desk. Odo checks the report; the plant theft was a mistaken relocation to the greenhouse.

VEX: The clue was hidden in the case file.

MARA: It was a leaf used as a bookmark.

ODO: The plant was returned yesterday. The report is closed.

VEX: Then who was the figure in the window?

MARA: You. We have established that.

TAG: Vex turns off the desk lamp and disappears from the reflection. Odo takes his coat from the chair, where it had made a second silhouette.
