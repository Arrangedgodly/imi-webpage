# The Janitor's Grand Staircase

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent slapstick shorts that build elaborate chains of physical mishaps around ordinary cleanup*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A quiet janitor tries to mop a theater staircase before the audience arrives, while every tool in the building conspires to turn the job into a pratfall.

Cast: Pipo, a macaque janitor; usher, a lemur; two stagehands, gibbons; an empty bucket; a mop; a rolling ladder.

SCENE 1 - INT. THEATER LOBBY - MORNING

No dialogue. Pipo rolls a bucket across the lobby. One wheel squeaks. He stops, taps it, and the squeak becomes louder. He removes the wheel, examines it, then places it back on the wrong side. The bucket now rolls in a slow circle.

Pipo walks beside it, pretending this is intended. The bucket circles the ticket desk and bumps a small stand of programs. He catches every program except one. It lands face up in the mop water.

SCENE 2 - INT. GRAND STAIRCASE - CONTINUOUS

Pipo sets a WET FLOOR sign at the base of the stairs. He dips the mop and starts at the top. The mop head slips from its handle and slides down every step, leaving a perfect wet stripe. Pipo chases it with the handle, stepping around the sign as carefully as possible.

At the bottom, the mop head stops beside the bucket. Pipo retrieves it and presses it back onto the handle. He turns to admire the clean stripe. A stagehand enters from the landing carrying a stack of costume boxes. The stagehand sees the sign, steps around it and sets the boxes on the wet stripe. The bottom box slides; Pipo catches it with the mop handle. The entire stack tilts, then settles against the wall.

SCENE 3 - INT. STAIRCASE LANDING - MOMENTS LATER

Pipo moves the boxes to a dry patch. He lifts the WET FLOOR sign to put it near the stripe. The sign's hinge folds, and the sign collapses flat. Pipo props it open with a program. The program is wet and sags, lowering the warning toward the floor.

An usher arrives with a tray of tickets. Pipo points to the sign, then to the wet stripe. The usher nods, walks around the stairs and takes the long route. Pipo smiles. The sign tips over again, this time toward the mop bucket. The bucket rolls down one step, then another.

Pipo catches the handle with his foot. The bucket balances at the stair edge. He gently lowers it to the landing. The mop handle rolls the opposite direction and knocks the doorstop loose. The lobby door swings open.

SCENE 4 - INT. LOBBY - CONTINUOUS

The door catches a draft. A row of paper programs lifts from the ticket desk. Pipo drops the bucket to grab them. The mop handle slides beneath the desk and knocks a bell. The usher hears the bell, assumes the doors are open, and begins placing tickets on the counter.

Pipo gathers the programs. The wet one sticks to his glove. He shakes it once; it sails onto the usher's hat. The usher removes the hat and gently places the program in the waste bin. Pipo bows an apology. The usher offers him a clean, dry towel from the supply closet.

SCENE 5 - INT. GRAND STAIRCASE - LATER

Pipo resets the sign with a small wooden wedge. He mops one step at a time, moving the bucket to each landing before proceeding. The wet stripe disappears. He places the mop head securely on the handle and parks the bucket against the wall.

The two stagehands carry a long scenery plank toward the stairs. They stop at the sign and look at Pipo. He holds the door open for them and points to the dry path around the staircase. They take it. The usher brings a stack of programs, dry this time, and sets them on the desk, away from the mop bucket.

Pipo tests the bucket wheel. It squeaks once, then rolls straight past the entrance and stops on the marked parking square. He sets the WET FLOOR sign beside the bucket, not in the middle of the stairs. The theater doors open. The first audience member enters and walks up the dry steps.

Pipo steps aside, lifts the mop in a neat salute, and turns to leave. The mop handle catches the sign's wooden wedge. The sign begins to tilt. Pipo catches it before it falls, places it upright and checks the wedge with his toe.

FINAL IMAGE: Pipo exits through the side door, carrying the mop vertically, while the staircase remains dry, the sign stands firmly and the bucket wheel squeaks once in perfect time with the lobby bell.
