# The Second Ending Is a Weather Report

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: melodramatic alternate endings, reshoots that change nothing, and bonus scenes that undercut the climax*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A small film crew shoots four endings to a story about a picnic tarp, while the weather crew waits for them to clear the park.

Cast: Liri, a macaque director; Toma, a tortoise weather observer; Nella, a lemur actor; and Puck, a capuchin editor.

SCENE 1. EXT. PARK PICNIC AREA, MORNING

Nella holds a tarp over a picnic table as wind lifts the corners. Liri calls action. Nella ties the tarp to the table legs and saves the food from a drizzle. Puck shouts cut.

PUCK: We need a bigger ending.

TOMA: The forecast says light rain until noon.

Liri asks for a version where the tarp tears. Nella holds it up; the tarp is new and tied securely. Puck suggests an emotional close-up of the sandwiches. Toma asks them to move the camera away from the weather station.

LIRI: We need a clean frame.

TOMA: Your stand blocks the gauge.

Nella shifts the tripod. Puck tests a wind machine behind the shed. The tarp flaps over the table and lifts the sandwich basket. Toma catches it and turns off the fan. Liri says the scene should feel stormy. Toma reads the gauge: a light breeze.

SCENE 2. EXT. PARK SHELTER, LATER

They shoot an alternate ending. Nella releases the tarp and runs for cover. The wind pushes the tablecloth toward the path. Toma catches the corner before it hits a stroller.

TOMA: Please tie it down.

LIRI: That changes the scene.

TOMA: It changes the weather.

Nella ties the tarp again. Puck asks whether they can film a storm sound effect. A thunderclap from the speaker frightens a flock of pigeons. Toma unplugs the speaker. Liri marks the take as unusable. Puck asks if the pigeons can appear as an omen. Toma says they are roosting and should not be disturbed. Nella folds the tarp so its corners cannot catch the wind. Puck asks her to repeat the fold in slow motion. She does it at normal speed; the tarp stays put. Liri watches the take and asks for a close-up. Nella says the knot is visible from there.

SCENE 3. INT. PARK MAINTENANCE SHED, AFTERNOON

Puck assembles three endings: the tarp blows away, the picnic is rescued, and a surprise guest arrives after the rain. Liri asks which one resolves the story. Nella says the picnic stays dry because she tied the tarp.

Puck proposes a post-credit shot of the tarp floating over the pond. Toma checks the park rules and says loose fabric could reach the water.

TOMA: Loose fabric could reach the water.

LIRI: Then what should the ending show?

NELLA: The table dry and the tarp folded.

They shoot the closing action in one take. Nella unties each corner, folds the tarp, and puts it on a shelf. Puck requests a musical swell. Toma's weather radio interrupts with an ordinary forecast. Liri keeps the take without music. 

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK PICNIC AREA, EVENING

The crew watches the final cut on a tablet. It shows the drizzle, the tied tarp, and the picnic continuing. Puck asks if the audience will expect a twist. Toma points to the actual sky. The rain has stopped.

Liri removes the three alternate endings. Nella asks if the film needs a final line. Puck tries one about fate. Toma says the tarp held because of the knots. Nella repeats the line and checks the tie.

PUCK: What if the weather changes before the screening?

TOMA: The forecast gives a ten percent chance of rain.

LIRI: That does not require another ending.

Puck loads a thunderclap into the sound track. It lands over the shot of a quiet picnic and makes everyone jump in the screening room. Nella says the weather audio contradicts the scene.

PUCK: The storm sound makes the final shot bigger.

LIRI: The shot is about a tied tarp holding.

TOMA: Tomorrow is mostly clear.

Toma turns down the speaker and reads the live forecast. Puck replays the scene without the thunder. The edit feels quieter, so he asks Nella to hold her look at the knot for one beat longer. She does. The cut ends on the tarp and the picnic basket, with no extra effect. Liri removes the thunder effect and keeps the natural sound of rain on the tarp.

The picnic group packs its food. Puck locks the cut without adding a sequel card. The weather station remains clear, and the film ends on the recorded shot of the dry table.

FINAL IMAGE: The credits roll over the folded tarp on its shelf. The final card reads "RAIN CHANCE: 10 PERCENT," and Puck does not add a second ending.
