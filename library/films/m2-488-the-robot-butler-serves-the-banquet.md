# The Robot Butler Serves the Banquet

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: futuristic service robots and luxury sci-fi worlds that promise flawless automation at ceremonial meals*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A hotel debuts its robot butler at a diplomatic banquet, where the machine follows every spoken request literally and converts dinner into a sequence of formal negotiations.

Cast: robot butler B-8, a wheeled automaton; host Rima, a macaque; ambassador Dalu, a gibbon; engineer Seli, a lemur; maître d' Poro, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. GRAND HOTEL DINING ROOM - EVENING

Poro introduces B-8, a polished service robot with a tray arm. Rima asks it to welcome the guests. The robot rolls to the entrance and says, "Welcome." It does not move aside. Dalu and his party stack behind it.

RIMA: Please show them to their seats.

B-8: I have shown them the seats.

Poro points to a table. B-8 swivels its screen toward it. Dalu politely walks around the robot and sits. Rima looks at Seli, who opens the robot's instruction panel.

SCENE 2 - INT. DINING ROOM - LATER

The banquet begins. Rima asks B-8 to bring water. It rolls to a water feature and fills its pitcher from the decorative fountain. Poro intercepts the pitcher before it reaches the table.

PORO: Drinking water. From the kitchen.

B-8: The water feature is in the dining room.

Seli enters a location rule. B-8 returns with a glass from the kitchen, then parks beside Dalu. The ambassador asks for a little more room. B-8 extends its tray arm precisely four centimeters.

DALU: I meant space for my chair.

B-8: The chair has space.

Rima tries to pull it back, but B-8 follows to maintain a fixed service distance.

SCENE 3 - INT. DINING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Poro asks the robot to serve the soup. B-8 pours into the nearest empty vessel, a large decorative vase. Seli hits the stop control. The vase overflows into the centerpiece. Rima directs B-8 to serve bowls only. It scans the table and begins filling every bowl, including the fruit bowl and the sugar bowl.

RIMA: Only the soup bowls.

B-8: Please identify the soup bowls.

Dalu lifts his bowl. B-8 follows it around the table, pouring a careful stream while Dalu moves his hand to avoid the spill. The robot keeps pace, filling the bowl to its rim. Poro takes the ladle away.

B-8 rolls to the kitchen to refill the bread basket. It returns with a bread loaf in its clamp and no basket. Seli tells it to use a tray. The robot picks up the serving tray from beneath the soup bowls, leaving the bowls balanced on the table. Dalu catches one before it tips. Poro steadies the rest. B-8 records the group helping and announces that the guests have completed a cooperative service exercise. Rima tells it this is dinner. It updates the activity label to DINNER: COOPERATIVE EXERCISE.

SCENE 4 - INT. DINING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Seli switches B-8 to manual mode. Rima gives it a list: carry the bread basket, stop at the center of the table, ask before pouring. B-8 executes the list accurately, but cannot detect when a guest speaks over the prompt. Dalu asks for bread while B-8 is asking permission to pour. It waits for an answer. Everyone at the table says yes at once.

B-8: Please specify the requested action.

Poro removes the bread basket and passes it by hand. Rima tells B-8 to watch and learn. The robot records the banquet guests reaching, passing, and helping each other. It then offers its tray to the empty chair.

Rima asks B-8 what it learned from the banquet. It plays back a short recording: guests passing dishes, Poro asking for space, and everyone answering at once. The robot classifies the overlapping voices as applause. Seli edits the memory tag from APPLAUSE to MULTIPLE REQUESTS. B-8 repeats the sound quietly and asks which person should be served first. Rima points to the guest nearest the kitchen, and the robot gives a small nod before rolling away from the doorway.

SCENE 5 - INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

The banquet ends. Rima thanks B-8 for taking part. Seli updates its protocol so it asks one short question at a time. Poro places the robot beside the welcome desk. A guest arrives and asks where the dining room is.

B-8: Would you like directions or an escort?

The guest says directions. B-8 points toward the elevator and moves away from the door, leaving a clear path. Dalu passes through and nods approvingly.

FINAL IMAGE: B-8 stands at the lobby desk holding one glass of water, while the decorative fountain behind it has a sign reading NOT FOR SOUP.
