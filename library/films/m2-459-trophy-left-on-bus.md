# The Trophy Left on the Bus

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports victory films that build a grand championship celebration around an award no one remembered to bring*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A small monkey team wins the neighborhood relay, then discovers the trophy is still riding the city bus on its way back to the depot.

Cast: Captain Rina, a macaque; sprinter Kodo, a gibbon; anchor Tavi, a capuchin; coach Mela, a lemur; driver Oru, an orangutan; announcer Nemi, a squirrel monkey.

SCENE 1 - EXT. COMMUNITY TRACK - AFTERNOON

The relay team gathers at the finish line. Coach Mela checks a clipboard. The trophy table is empty except for a square of clean dust.

MELA: We won the relay. Where is the cup?

RINA: I left it on the bus so it would not get scratched.

KODO: The bus went back to the depot.

NEMI: We have a champion, but no trophy for the photograph.

TAVI: We can take the photo with the ribbon.

Nemi turns to the camera and announces the team as if a brass band should follow.

SCENE 2 - INT. COMMUNITY TRACK OFFICE - MOMENTS LATER

Mela calls the transit depot. Oru answers from the bus driver's seat. The trophy is on the floor behind the last row, safe but rattling in its box.

ORU: I have it. The bus has left the depot and is on the next route.

MELA: Can you bring it back?

ORU: Not until the route ends. I have passengers.

NEMI: The crowd is waiting for the victory shot.

RINA: Let them wait. We will meet the bus at the terminal.

Kodo and Tavi prepare to run. Mela tells them not to race through the streets.

SCENE 2A - EXT. COMMUNITY TRACK - CONTINUOUS

The crowd gathers around the empty trophy table. Nemi asks a volunteer to hold a blue ribbon above the team's heads. The ribbon sags in the middle.

NEMI: Can we use a cardboard cup as a stand-in?

MELA: Not if it says CITY FINALS.

KODO: We can use the relay baton.

RINA: It is still on the last runner's wrist.

Tavi holds up a baton with a strip of ribbon tied around it. The team poses. The crowd applauds, but a photographer asks them to lift the trophy.

RINA: It is on Route Six.

PHOTOGRAPHER: Is that a bus or another team?

NEMI: Both are still in motion.

Mela checks the terminal schedule and confirms the bus's next stop. The crowd begins to follow them.

MELA: We will meet it at the terminal. Please clear the track first.

Kodo asks the crowd to keep the running lane open. The photographer rolls the empty trophy table away from the finish line and locks its loose wheels.

SCENE 3 - EXT. BUS TERMINAL - LATER

The team arrives at the terminal. They line up beside the curb as if waiting for a final race. Oru parks and opens the rear door. A passenger exits carrying a bag of oranges.

ORU: The trophy is still here. It did not fall.

Rina checks the box. The lid is open, but the cup remains inside. One ribbon has caught in the seat hinge.

KODO: The trophy has been through an extra lap.

TAVI: The ribbon needs untangling.

Mela frees the ribbon and closes the box. The team thanks Oru. Nemi asks the driver to pose beside them.

SCENE 4 - EXT. COMMUNITY TRACK - SUNSET

They return with the trophy. Nemi sets it on the table. The announcer asks the team to hold a dramatic pose. Rina takes the cup, but the engraved plate is facing backward. She turns it around. The engraving reads COMMUNITY RELAY, NOT CITY FINALS.

NEMI: We have won the neighborhood event.

MELA: That is what we entered.

KODO: The crowd thought it was the championship.

TAVI: We can still be proud without upgrading the title.

The crowd applauds. The team lifts the cup. The base is loose, and it wobbles. Oru catches it before it falls.

SCENE 5 - EXT. TRACK OFFICE - EVENING

Mela secures the trophy base with a small screw. Rina fills out the transit lost-property form, just in case. Oru checks the bus for any remaining gear. Nemi redoes the announcement, this time naming the correct event.

NEMI: Neighborhood relay champions, with their trophy returned by Route Six.

RINA: And the bus driver who kept it safe.

Oru raises a hand. Kodo offers him the victory ribbon. He pins it to the driver's jacket and returns it before boarding.

FINAL IMAGE: The trophy stands on the track office shelf beside the bus route map, its ribbon looped around the base and its engraved title clearly visible in full from the finish line.
