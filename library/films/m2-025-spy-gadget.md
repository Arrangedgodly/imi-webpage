# Agent Banana Peel Protocol

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: spy gadget comedy, overengineered equipment, and mission briefing jargon*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A young monkey spy must retrieve a stolen weather balloon, but the agency's expensive gadgets fail one by one while the only reliable tool is a plain, borrowed umbrella.

Cast: Agent Pip, a young macaque operative; Q-Branch, a tortoise gadget designer; Director Slate, a baboon supervisor; Madam Finch, a cockatoo balloon thief; and Sergeant Button, a mouse who guards the equipment room.

SCENE 1. INT. AGENCY BRIEFING ROOM, DAY

Director Slate reveals the mission. A weather balloon has drifted into a botanical garden. Madam Finch is trying to claim it as a mobile greenhouse.

SLATE: Retrieve the balloon quietly.

PIP: How quiet?

Q: We have a whispering grappling hook.

It fires and clangs against a projector. Slate asks for a quieter demonstration. Q hands Pip a pair of silent shoes. They squeak.

SCENE 2. INT. EQUIPMENT ROOM, LATER

Pip tests gadgets. A wristwatch projects the wrong map. The mini-drone carries a tiny flag reading "HELLO." The self-folding parachute folds around Q.

BUTTON: The umbrella works.

Q: It's not a gadget.

PIP: It opens.

SLATE: Take the umbrella.

Q: It has no tracking chip.

SLATE: It has a handle.

SCENE 3. EXT. BOTANICAL GARDEN, AFTERNOON

Pip sneaks through the greenhouse. The silent shoes squeak on wet leaves. He switches to ordinary shoes. The watch map points to the gift shop. The mini-drone flies into a fern and returns with a leaf.

Madam Finch spots him. She asks whether he is a spy. Pip says he is lost. She offers directions to the greenhouse. He accepts and follows her.

SCENE 4. INT. GREENHOUSE, CONTINUOUS

The balloon is caught among the rafters. Finch has tied it to a watering system. Q's grappling hook fires, hits a hanging basket, and showers the team with potting soil. Pip opens the umbrella to shield the plants. The balloon catches its handle and begins to descend.

Finch says she wanted the balloon for shade. Pip explains it is full of weather data. Q tries a tracking chip. It beeps inside his own pocket.

SCENE 5. EXT. GARDEN EXIT, SUNSET

The team returns the balloon to the weather office. Finch agrees to help build a proper shade cloth for the greenhouse. Slate commends Pip for improvising. Q asks for his gadgets back. The silent shoes are stuck together with soil.

SCENE 5A. INT. AGENCY BRIEFING ROOM, NIGHT

Slate debriefs Pip and Q. The umbrella stands open beside the gadgets.

SLATE: Mission status?

PIP: Balloon returned.

Q: Greenhouse shaded.

SLATE: Gadget status?

Q: Mostly damp.

PIP: The hook is bent.

Q: It still hooks.

SLATE: The shoes?

PIP: Squeaky.

Q: They were silent.

PIP: Until used.

SLATE: The watch?

Q: It knows the gift shop.

PIP: Very well.

SLATE: The drone?

Q: It found a leaf.

PIP: A useful leaf.

SLATE: The umbrella?

PIP: It worked.

Q: It has no chip.

SLATE: It has a canopy.

Q: That's not technical.

SLATE: It's effective.

PIP: We should label it.

Q: "Portable shield."

PIP: "Umbrella."

Q: Less impressive.

SLATE: It kept the plants dry.

PIP: And caught the balloon.

Q: By accident.

SLATE: Update the manual.

Q: Which page?

PIP: The umbrella page.

Q: There isn't one.

SLATE: Add one.

Q: What features?

PIP: Opens.

SLATE: Closes.

Q: Requires a hand.

PIP: Can be borrowed.

SLATE: That's the key feature.

Q: I'll write it down.

PIP: Don't use the wristwatch.

Q: It's a pen.

PIP: It points at the gift shop.

Slate takes a pencil from the table and writes "umbrella" on the equipment list.

SCENE 5B. INT. EQUIPMENT ROOM, MORNING

Q catalogues the returned equipment. Pip sorts items into working and not working. The umbrella is in the working pile.

Q: The grappling hook is bent.

PIP: It grabbed the basket.

Q: The shoes squeak.

PIP: They stopped.

Q: The watch points wrong.

PIP: It points somewhere.

Q: The drone brought a leaf.

PIP: The leaf was useful.

Q: The umbrella is ordinary.

PIP: It saved the plants.

Q: It caught the balloon.

PIP: It did both.

Q: We can improve it.

PIP: Add a handle?

Q: It has one.

PIP: Add more handle?

Q: That's not engineering.

SLATE: It worked.

Q: We could add a tracker.

PIP: Then it won't be borrowed.

SLATE: Keep it simple.

Q: Simple is hard.

PIP: The umbrella opened.

Q: And closed.

SLATE: And covered the evidence.

PIP: It was a balloon.

Q: It covered the balloon.

SLATE: Put it on the list.

Q: The equipment list?

PIP: The umbrella list.

Q: There isn't one.

SLATE: There is now.

Q: What code?

PIP: "Rain."

SLATE: Approved.

Q: That is not a spy name.

PIP: Good.

The agency printer produces a label. It sticks to Q's sleeve. He tries to peel it off, but the adhesive is stronger than the grappling hook.

FINAL IMAGE: Pip hangs the plain umbrella beside the agency's gadget wall. A label reads "Field tested." The mini-drone tries to salute and bumps into it.
