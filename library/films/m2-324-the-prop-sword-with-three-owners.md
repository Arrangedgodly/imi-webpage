# The Prop Sword with Three Owners

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy productions where prop departments, continuity teams, and actors each treat a weapon as belonging to a different character*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR NARI, an orangutan
ARMORER ODO, a baboon
CONTINUITY SUPERVISOR VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. CASTLE SET - MORNING

A foam sword rests on a velvet cushion. Pip's character is meant to discover it in the throne room. Nari's character, the rival knight, is meant to claim it in the next scene. Armorer Odo logs the prop as SWORD 4B. Continuity supervisor Vex's slate says SWORD 4A.

VEX: The sword is in the hero's right hand.

ODO: It is on the cushion.

LUMA: The scene has not started.

VEX: I am tracking ahead.

Pip lifts the foam sword. Vex photographs it. Nari enters, takes the sword, and checks the rubber edge. Luma asks whose property it is in the story. Pip says it belongs to the missing king. Odo says it belongs to the armor rack until checked out.

SCENE 2. INT. CASTLE SET - LATER

The actors rehearse. Pip discovers the sword and calls it the king's. Nari enters and says it belongs to the knight. Vex stops them because the sword has changed sides too early. Odo checks the prop sheet; both actors signed it out.

ODO: They cannot both own it.

PIP: I found it.

NARI: I inherited it.

LUMA: You are both disputing the same foam blade.

Vex suggests splitting the sword into two halves for matching shots. Odo refuses; the prop is molded as one piece. They try shooting over each actor's shoulder, but the camera catches Pip holding one end and Nari the other. It looks like a tug-of-war.

SCENE 3. INT. ARMORY SET - AFTERNOON

Odo lays out the prop sheet, the script and a photograph from the prior scene. The sword's handle has a strip of blue tape identifying the hero's version. The blade carries a red ribbon identifying the rival's version. The crew has combined both labels on the same prop.

VEX: I marked continuity by the ribbon.

ODO: The tape is the checkout mark.

NARI: The king wrapped the ribbon around it.

PIP: My character found it after the king vanished.

Luma examines the script. The king left the sword for whoever would return it to the throne. Pip found it; Nari inherited the duty when she joined his journey. They do not own the sword. They are both responsible for returning it.

VEX: That is less dramatic than a duel.

LUMA: The duel is in the next film.

Vex wants a close-up of both hands gripping the hilt. Pip and Nari each hold one side. The foam sword bends in the center and points toward the camera like a question mark. Odo asks them to stop pulling.

SCENE 4. INT. THRONE ROOM SET - EVENING

Luma blocks a new scene. Pip picks up the sword; Nari reaches for it, then turns her hand palm-up. Pip passes it to her. She places it on the throne, where both characters bow. The sword lies flat, its ribbon and tape visible at opposite ends. Vex objects that no one has won it.

NARI: The point was to return it.

PIP: It belongs to the room.

ODO: The prop belongs in the rack after the take.

They run the scene. Pip discovers the sword; Nari recognizes the ribbon; they carry it together to the throne. Vex photographs each handoff. In every frame the blade stays whole and points safely down. He sees that the continuity problem has become the scene's emotional beat. Nari carries the hilt; Pip supports the blunt tip. They pause at the throne while the red ribbon slips loose. Odo catches it before it hits the floor, and both actors wait for the armorer to retie it before the camera rolls again. Vex misses his cue.

SCENE 5. INT. PROP ROOM - NIGHT

The finished scene plays on the monitor. The characters return the sword, and the story moves on. Vex files the stills in two folders, HERO POSSESSION and RIVAL POSSESSION. Odo asks him which one receives the final image. Vex considers, then opens a third folder: SHARED RESPONSIBILITY.

Luma says the sword must now return to its rack. Odo logs it in. Pip starts to reach for it as he leaves, then notices the checkout sheet and withdraws his hand. Nari signs the form. Vex checks the labels one last time and finds that the red ribbon is tied to the wrong end.

FINAL IMAGE: The sword rests on its rack, tagged with two names and one overdue-return notice.
