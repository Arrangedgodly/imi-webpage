# The Goalkeeper's First Practice

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports underdog dramas that recruit an untested player moments before a match and substitute pep talks for practice*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A neighborhood monkey team recruits a new goalkeeper just before a match, then learns that a moving montage cannot replace learning where the goal line is.

Cast: Coach Vela, a macaque; keeper Jori, a lemur; striker Pavo, a capuchin; captain Tumi, a gibbon; referee Nessa, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - EXT. COMMUNITY FIELD - MORNING

The team warms up. The usual goalkeeper has sprained a paw. Jori arrives carrying a water bottle and wearing a borrowed jersey.

VELA: You are our new keeper.

JORI: I have never played goalkeeper.

PAVO: You have good reflexes. Catch this.

Pavo kicks a ball toward Jori. Jori catches it against the chest and falls backward into the goal net.

TUMI: We have found our underdog.

Nessa checks the clock. The match starts in thirty minutes.

SCENE 2 - EXT. FIELD SIDELINE - LATER

Vela draws a plan on a whiteboard. It has arrows, zones and a large X over the goal.

VELA: You stand here. Watch the ball, not the crowd.

JORI: What happens if it goes past me?

VELA: Then you stop it.

The team runs a practice drill. Pavo kicks softly. Jori catches it. Tumi cheers. Vela starts a motivational speech about the team's long journey. The ball rolls behind the goal.

JORI: Is that one in?

NESSA: It is outside the line.

Jori steps out of goal to retrieve it. Vela stops the drill and points to the boundary markers.

SCENE 3 - EXT. COMMUNITY FIELD - MATCH START

The teams take their positions. A small crowd gathers. The opposing captain kicks off. Jori watches the ball, then sees a banner flap beside the goal. Jori turns to look at it. The ball rolls into the net.

NESSA: Goal.

JORI: I thought the banner was moving.

VELA: The ball is what we defend.

PAVO: You can do this.

Jori moves the banner so it no longer overlaps the goal line. The match resumes. The opposing team shoots again. Jori catches the ball cleanly and throws it to Tumi. Tumi passes to Pavo, who scores to level the match before the whistle.

SCENE 3A - EXT. FIELD SIDELINE - HALFTIME

The scoreboard shows the visitors ahead by one. Vela lines up water bottles in a row and delivers a speech about every great goalkeeper's first match.

VELA: The crowd believes in you. Your teammates believe in you.

JORI: The crowd is eating sandwiches.

PAVO: I can take the next shot if you keep the ball in play.

Jori checks the goalposts. One net corner is loose and flaps in the breeze. Nessa tightens the strap while Vela asks Jori to visualize the ball coming from the left.

JORI: Can we practice with a slower ball first?

TUMI: We have a soft practice ball in the kit.

They spend two minutes making short throws. Jori catches three in a row, then returns the ball carefully to Tumi.

VELA: That was better than the speech.

PAVO: The second half starts now.

The team returns to the field. Jori points out that the visitors' striker always looks at the near post before shooting. Vela marks the angle on the whiteboard, then wipes it away before the team goes back onto the field.

SCENE 3B - EXT. FIELD SIDELINE - HALFTIME

A parent with a camera asks Vela to stage a motivational montage for the team page. Vela lines Jori up against the goal and asks Tumi to toss a ball in slow motion. The first toss lands in Jori's water bottle. The second hits the camera's foam microphone cover. The third is caught cleanly, but Vela asks Jori to look more surprised.

JORI: I was concentrating.

VELA: The poster needs a transformation.

PAVO: Could we use halftime to practice the corner kick?

Vela starts a speech about destiny. As he speaks, the opposing team takes its corner kick early. Nessa blows the whistle, and everyone turns toward the ball except Vela, who is still facing the camera.

TUMI: Coach, the dramatic speech has started without us.

Vela drops the pose and runs to the field. Jori watches the ball roll toward the near post, calls Tumi's name, and covers the opening while Tumi clears it. The camera keeps filming. The parent lowers it.

PARENT: That was the useful part.

VELA: Keep that. Cut my speech.

Jori asks for three slow shots in a row. Pavo obliges, each from a different angle, and Jori catches two while letting the third roll out for a goal kick. Vela marks the practice drill on the board rather than writing a slogan.

SCENE 4 - EXT. FIELD - LATER

The score is tied. Vela asks the team to slow down. Pavo tries a fast shot and sends the ball into a hedge. Jori leaves the goal to fetch it, but Tumi takes the keeper position until Jori returns.

TUMI: We can cover the goal together.

JORI: I thought the underdog had to do it alone.

VELA: That is a movie idea. This is a team.

They return to their positions. The opposing team takes a corner kick. Jori calls out the angle, and Tumi clears the ball. Pavo dribbles downfield and scores. The team wins by one goal.

SCENE 5 - EXT. FIELD SIDELINE - AFTERNOON

The crowd cheers. Jori removes the borrowed jersey and returns it to the equipment bag. Vela offers another practice time.

JORI: I will play again, but I want to practice before the next match.

PAVO: We can start tomorrow.

Nessa checks the net and ties the loose corner. The flapping banner is taken down and folded.

TUMI: The game was won because we talked to each other.

JORI: And because the goal line was clear.

FINAL IMAGE: The ball rests in the center of the goal, the banner folded beside the net, while the team marks a practice time on the whiteboard.
