# The Camera Loves the Referee

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports documentary films, where a camera crew follows a referee and misses the entire game*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A documentary crew follows a referee's rule book so closely that it records every whistle and none of the match.

Cast: Referee Odo, baboon; filmmaker Luma, lemur; player Nira, macaque; commentator Pavi, capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. FIELD, DAY

Two teams prepare for a friendly match. Luma points the camera at Odo, who checks a rule book.

LUMA: We are documenting fairness.

PAVI: The teams are ready.

NIRA: The ball is in the center.

ODO: I am checking the boundary markers.

LUMA: Can you look toward the camera while you do that?

ODO: I am measuring the line.

Luma pans to Odo's whistle. Pavi commentates over the warm-up.

PAVI: The referee prepares the instrument that will shape the game.

NIRA: The game has not started.

ODO: It will start when both teams are ready.

LUMA: We need a strong opening shot.

Odo blows the whistle. The players begin. Luma keeps filming his hand signals.

SCENE 2. EXT. FIELD, MOMENTS LATER

Nira passes the ball. It rolls out of frame. Luma stays on Odo.

PAVI: The ball is moving downfield.

LUMA: I can hear it.

ODO: The ball is out.

NIRA: It was in.

ODO: It crossed the marker.

NIRA: The marker is tilted.

Odo adjusts the flag. Luma zooms in as he plants it.

LUMA: That is a clear visual.

PAVI: The ball is back in play.

Luma moves to follow the game, but Odo walks across the shot to check the other boundary.

LUMA: We lost the play.

ODO: I am checking the field.

PAVI: The match is happening over there.

ODO: I need to be in position.

SCENE 3. EXT. SIDELINE, LATER

Luma asks Nira to reenact the best pass. Nira says the game is still on. Pavi reports the score while Odo explains an offside call.

PAVI: One to zero.

LUMA: Referee, tell us why.

ODO: The player was ahead of the ball.

LUMA: Can you demonstrate?

Odo sets up the players. Nira passes; Pavi narrates. The camera records the referee's pointing arm, blocking the pass.

NIRA: We cannot see the ball.

ODO: I am showing the line.

LUMA: The line is very important.

NIRA: So is the ball.

Pavi says the audience might think the match is about hand signals. Luma says that is a compelling subject. Nira scores while they talk.

ODO: Goal.

PAVI: The score is now tied.

LUMA: I missed it.

NIRA: You were filming the referee.

LUMA: We have the whistle.

ODO: We have to restart at center.

SCENE 4. EXT. CENTER FIELD, CONTINUOUS

At halftime, Luma asks Odo to describe the game. He says both teams are playing well and the ball has remained in bounds most of the time.

PAVI: That is not a documentary.

LUMA: It is a profile of officiating.

NIRA: Then the game should be in the background.

Luma tries a wide shot, but Odo signals a substitution and walks directly in front of the lens. The camera follows him.

LUMA: He has entered the frame again.

ODO: I am responsible for the match.

NIRA: You are responsible for the match, not the camera.

Pavi suggests placing a second camera on the field. Luma says there is only one. Odo offers to stand still; the ball hits his foot and rolls out.

[SFX: BALL THUMPS, WHISTLE.]

ODO: The ball is out.

LUMA: The camera got the call.

Before the final whistle, Pavi asks Nira to describe the equalizing goal. She reenacts the pass with Odo standing as referee. Luma films Odo raising his arm again.

NIRA: The ball came from the left.

LUMA: I have the signal.

PAVI: The viewers need to see the play.

Luma turns the camera toward Nira. The ball has already been returned to the center.

LUMA: Can you do it again?

NIRA: The game is over.

ODO: A replay is not a second goal.

Luma records Nira describing the goal while Odo demonstrates the call behind her. The film finally includes both the player and the referee.

PAVI: Now we have the match.

The match ends in a draw. Luma reviews the footage. It shows the opening whistle, Odo checking lines, the referee's gestures, and the final whistle. Almost no play is visible.

PAVI: What will you call the film?

LUMA: "The Match, According to Odo."

ODO: I did not score.

NIRA: You appeared in every shot.

LUMA: The game was out of frame.

Odo blows the whistle to call the teams together. Everyone turns to the camera.

LUMA: We have the closing shot.

PAVI: Of the referee?

LUMA: Of the players looking at him.

NIRA: We are looking at the camera.

ODO: The match is over.

LUMA: The documentary is complete.

[SFX: WHISTLE, CAMERA SHUTTER.]
