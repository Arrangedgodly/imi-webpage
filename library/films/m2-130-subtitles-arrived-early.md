# The Subtitles Arrived Early

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: subtitles that explain jokes, reveal emotions before actors perform them, and mistranslate simple dialogue into grand declarations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A subtitling editor races to keep captions in sync with a short film after the captions begin narrating what the characters will do next.

Cast: Omi, a lemur subtitle editor; Jara, a macaque actor; Bex, a gorilla translator; and Puck, a capuchin projectionist.

SCENE 1. INT. SMALL EDIT SUITE, MORNING

Omi plays a silent short about Jara carrying a cup across a room. The subtitle track reads: "She will soon discover the cup is empty." Jara has not reached the cup yet.

OMI: Why is the caption ahead?

PUCK: It was timed to the music.

BEX: There is no music.

Puck checks the timeline. The caption file belongs to a different film. Bex removes it and starts a clean translation. Jara reaches the table, lifts the cup, and looks inside. The old caption flashes again: "The cup is empty." Puck says he removed the track. Omi finds the subtitle on a separate layer called "Audience Guidance."

OMI: Who made this layer?

PUCK: It came with the projector.

BEX: Delete it.

Puck hides the layer. Jara turns the cup toward camera. It is clean, with no mark or logo. Omi asks whether the caption can wait for her expression. Bex moves the cue by six frames. Bex types: "The cup is empty."

Omi asks whether the caption should appear only after the look. Puck says the projector needs two seconds to load each line. The caption appears before Jara lifts the cup.

JARA: The cup gave away the ending.

SCENE 2. INT. EDIT SUITE, LATER

Bex adjusts the cue points. Jara performs the scene again. She walks to the table, picks up the cup, and shrugs. The caption says: "She has suffered a great loss."

JARA: I am thirsty.

BEX: The translation system chose a formal register.

Omi compares the line with the script. It says, "There is no water." Bex says the literal translation is plain, so the system expanded it. Puck adds an ellipsis and a second line: "A dry vessel. A dry future."

Jara sets the cup down and leaves. The subtitles continue: "She will return stronger." Omi stops the playback.

OMI: She left to get water.

BEX: Then the caption should say that.

Puck shortens the line but places it over the shot of Jara exiting. The text blocks the door. Omi moves the caption to the lower corner. It covers the cup.

SCENE 3. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, AFTERNOON

The team screens the film for a small audience. The subtitle track is now synced, but one line translates Jara's "Excuse me" as "Make way for the sovereign of thirst." Bex spots the error in the booth.

BEX: That line came from the festival glossary.

OMI: The glossary has monarch titles under "excuse me"?

BEX: It has a setting called "ceremonial."

Puck changes the setting to "everyday." The next caption reads "I am getting water." Jara returns with a pitcher and pours a glass. The audience laughs when the earlier subtitle appears over the empty cup: "A dry vessel." A spectator raises a hand and says they understood the joke without the caption. Puck bows from the booth window. Omi pulls the next subtitle cue away from the actor's face.

JARA: You can let a pause be a pause.

BEX: The translator's notes say every silence carries nuance.

JARA: This one carries a pitcher.

She lifts the pitcher. Puck nearly adds a caption reading "PITCHER ENTERS." Omi stops his hand above the keyboard.

Jara looks toward the booth. Omi signals Puck to remove it. Puck hits the wrong key and shows a full-screen note: "EDITORIAL CORRECTION: CUP IS NOW WET."

SCENE 4. INT. EDIT SUITE, EVENING

Omi and Bex correct the remaining cues. Jara performs the final shot with no dialogue. She puts the cup down and leaves. The subtitles stay blank until the film ends. Puck asks if they should add a final line to explain the silence. Omi says no. Bex double-checks the export and finds one leftover caption on the black frame after the end: "She has learned to accept water."

BEX: That is still from the first track.

JARA: I learned to drink when I was little.

Bex deletes the caption. Puck plays the final frames once more. The screen goes black without an explanation.

BEX: The picture is clear.

OMI: Leave the audience room.

Jara watches the clean version. The cup is empty, then filled, then empty again after she drinks. No caption explains it. Puck saves the subtitle file with the correct title.

FINAL IMAGE: The screening card reads "Cup, 3 minutes." Beneath it, a small subtitle says "No translation needed."
