# The Thing in the Reservoir

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: creature-feature thrillers, grainy sightings, panicked town meetings, and monsters that appear just beyond the flashlight beam*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: When a long shape circles the town reservoir, three primates race to expose a monster before the mayor drains the water supply, discovering that the real threat is a mystery no one has bothered to measure.

Cast
- LUMA, a ring-tailed lemur lifeguard whose tail signals every suspicion.
- BOK, a capuchin boat mechanic who trusts tools more than stories.
- TESS, a macaque student filmmaker with a shaky camera.
- MAYOR VENN, a gibbon who speaks in crisis announcements.
- DR. OMI, an orangutan hydrologist.
- THE THING, an unidentified shape with a moving shadow.

## Scene 1: The Sighting

EXT. RESERVOIR DOCK - DUSK

A fishing line jerks. Luma pulls up a boot with a long ribbon of weed wrapped around it. Behind her, the water dimples in a slow circle.

LUMA: I saw it move under the dock.

BOK: That is the intake pipe. It starts when the pump kicks on.

TESS: I have footage.

Her camera shows a dark blur, then her own tail crossing the lens.

TESS: It has rings.

LUMA: That was my tail.

The water bulges. A low, hollow thump sounds beneath the dock. Bok backs away.

BOK: The pump does not thump.

The dock shifts. A long shadow moves under the surface and vanishes.

## Scene 2: The Warning

INT. TOWN HALL - NIGHT

Mayor Venn unveils a hand-painted poster: RESERVOIR MONSTER. He has covered the waterworks map with it.

MAYOR VENN: We drain the reservoir at dawn. The creature will be exposed, and the town will be safe.

DR. OMI: The reservoir supplies three neighborhoods. Draining it takes two days and disrupts the water pressure.

LUMA: What if it is still in the water?

BOK: What if it is the pump?

TESS: The film needs one more sighting before the reveal.

DR. OMI: We need a measurement before the decision.

Venn announces a midnight inspection. Everyone brings flashlights. The beams sweep across reeds and reflections. Each moving leaf becomes a fin, each ripple a back.

## Scene 3: The Search

EXT. RESERVOIR EDGE - MIDNIGHT

Luma and Tess stand on the dock. Bok checks the pump housing. Dr. Omi lowers a measuring pole. The pole hits something soft.

TESS: It touched the creature.

DR. OMI: It touched a net.

She pulls up an old fishing net tangled with reeds and a long orange float. The orange shape stretches across the water, then collapses as the net drains.

BOK: There is your monster.

A second thump sounds. The float jerks against the net. Luma's tail bristles.

LUMA: That was not the net.

BOK: The pump cycled off.

Dr. Omi watches the water. The shape is moving against the current, circling the intake.

DR. OMI: Something is pulling the net around the intake grate.

## Scene 4: The Creature Shows Itself

A wide shadow rises near the dock. Tess films. The creature's back breaks the surface: slick, dark, and ridged. The group runs. The mayor orders the evacuation bell rung.

MAYOR VENN: Everyone away from the water!

BOK: The pump is pulling the net inward. The shadow is its float.

LUMA: Then what is moving the net against the current?

A large beaver surfaces, grabs the net, and drags it toward a bank lodge. It pauses, stares at them, then disappears with one end of the mesh.

TESS: The monster has a flat tail.

DR. OMI: That is a beaver. It is carrying a net from the intake.

MAYOR VENN: We have a beaver in the reservoir?

DR. OMI: It has been building a lodge along the north bank. The net and pump made the shape look much larger.

BOK: The pump did make that thump. The loose housing made the second one.

## Scene 5: The Real Crisis

The mayor orders the pump shut off to free the net. Water pressure drops in the town. A sprinkler system turns on at the hilltop greenhouse, drawing the remaining flow. Luma climbs the access ladder and sees water running onto the public path.

LUMA: The leak is uphill. We have to close the greenhouse valve before the pipe empties.

BOK: The valve is behind the pump housing. I can reach it from the dock.

The beaver's net blocks the hatch. Bok uses a boat hook to lift it. Luma signals the route with her tail so he can avoid the moving intake. Dr. Omi turns the pump to its safe manual setting while Tess lights the hatch. The valve shuts. The water pressure steadies.

MAYOR VENN: The town is safe from the reservoir.

DR. OMI: It was never in danger from the beaver. It was in danger from the leak.

Venn removes the monster poster. Under it, the waterworks map has a red circle around the old valve.

## Scene 6: The Evidence

The town gathers at dawn. Tess screens her footage on the hall wall. The monster blur is revealed as Luma's tail, the orange float, and the beaver's wake.

TESS: The camera was frightened before the crew was.

LUMA: The camera has no eyes.

TESS: It has a lens.

BOK: We will repair the intake cage, recover the net, and keep the pump housing tight.

MAYOR VENN: Should we leave a sign warning people about the creature?

DR. OMI: Leave a sign about the deep water and the maintenance work.

## Final Image

At sunrise, the beaver swims across the quiet reservoir with a reed in its teeth. On the dock, Luma lowers her flashlight and watches the water without naming what she sees.
