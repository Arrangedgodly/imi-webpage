# The Flood of Fern Hollow

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster blockbusters where a minor drainage problem earns a citywide emergency response and a heroic press conference*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A storm warning sends a village into blockbuster panic when a puddle forms beside the main path. A practical engineer tries to clear the drain while the mayor stages a rescue operation around the puddle.

Cast: Engineer Luma, a macaque; Mayor Varo, a baboon; reporter Seli, a gibbon; and resident Pavi, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. FERN HOLLOW PATH, DAY

Clouds gather. A shallow puddle spreads beside a leaf-choked drain. Varo stands on a crate.

VARO: The storm has arrived.

LUMA: It is raining lightly.

VARO: We need a response team.

PAVI: The water is ankle-deep.

VARO: We will evacuate the bench.

Seli's camera zooms on the puddle. Dramatic music begins.

SCENE 2. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, MOMENTS LATER

Varo directs volunteers around a map. The map marks the puddle in red.

VARO: We will deploy the rescue rope.

LUMA: The drain is blocked with leaves. I need a rake.

PAVI: I have one.

Varo hands the rope to Seli for a heroic shot. It is tied around a chair.

SELI: The chair is now secured.

VARO: We have prevented a furniture disaster.

Luma takes the rake and walks to the drain.

SCENE 3. EXT. PATH, CONTINUOUS

Luma pulls leaves from the drain. Water begins to flow. Varo and Pavi hold the rescue rope behind her.

VARO: Stay back. The current is unpredictable.

The stream is only a few centimeters deep. A paper boat floats by.

PAVI: The boat is moving.

VARO: We need a second line.

LUMA: I can reach it with the rake.

She pulls the boat to the edge. It is a folded meeting agenda.

SELI: The flood has claimed official documents.

VARO: We have recovered the agenda.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, LATER

The storm stops. Varo calls a press conference. Seli lights a red beacon.

VARO: The village has survived.

LUMA: The drain is clear.

PAVI: The bench is dry.

VARO: We will distribute sandbags.

LUMA: We do not need sandbags.

Varo hands Luma one anyway. She puts it beside the drain to stop leaves from blowing back.

SCENE 4A. EXT. PATH, CONTINUOUS

The drizzle becomes steady. Varo orders volunteers to carry a canoe from the shed.

VARO: If the path floods, we will need an evacuation vessel.

PAVI: The water is not deep enough for a canoe.

The volunteers lift it anyway. It catches between two roots and blocks the path.

LUMA: Move the canoe back. I need to reach the drain.

VARO: We have committed to the rescue plan.

PAVI: The rescue plan is keeping people from walking.

Seli films Varo struggling with the bow. The canoe slips, turns sideways, and becomes a roof over the shallow puddle.

VARO: We have covered the flood.

LUMA: We have covered the drain.

She and Pavi move it to the grass. Luma clears a second pile of leaves. The puddle drains.

SELI: The water has disappeared.

VARO: It is retreating.

LUMA: It is flowing into the drain.

Pavi helps a resident cross. No one needs the rope. Varo folds the rope and tucks it under his arm.

VARO: We can keep it ready for the next storm.

PAVI: Keep the rake ready first.

SCENE 4B. EXT. PATH, MOMENTS LATER

Seli asks Luma to stand beside the puddle for a scale shot. Luma sets the rake down and holds out a boot.

SELI: Can you show the water reaching your ankle?

LUMA: It does not reach my ankle.

Varo stands on the far side of the puddle, holding the rescue rope.

VARO: The current is stronger near the edge.

Luma steps through the water. It barely covers her toes.

PAVI: The path is wet, not flooded.

A gust blows the red emergency flag from Varo's hand. It lands in the puddle. Pavi retrieves it and wrings it out.

SELI: We have a rescue image.

LUMA: You have a wet flag.

Varo folds the flag and puts it into the sandbag.

VARO: The team has secured the warning.

PAVI: The warning is about rain.

Luma rakes leaves away from the drain again. Water runs freely toward the garden.

SCENE 5. EXT. PATH, SUNSET

Residents walk past the clear drain. A new sign says "KEEP LEAVES OUT." Varo stands beside it for a photograph.

VARO: The response team stood firm.

PAVI: The rake did most of it.

LUMA: And the drain did the rest.

A gust blows leaves into the drain. The water slows.

VARO: The crisis is returning.

Luma lifts the grate and clears the leaves again.

SELI: Can we get the heroic shot?

LUMA: Film the drain.

The camera zooms in. The water flows smoothly. Varo stands behind it holding a sandbag.

VARO: I am ready.

PAVI: For what?

VARO: The next puddle.

The team folds the unused rescue rope. A single drop falls from the tree into the puddle beside Varo's boot.
