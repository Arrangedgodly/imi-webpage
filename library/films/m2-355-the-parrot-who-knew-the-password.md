# The Parrot Who Knew the Password

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: spy capers, secret passwords, disguised agents, and elaborate gadgets undermined by a parrot that repeats everything*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
AGENT LUMA, a lemur
TECHNICIAN PIP, a capuchin
DIRECTOR VEX, a macaque
PARROT MISTER CLICK, a green parrot
GUARD ODO, an orangutan

SCENE 1. INT. AGENCY GARAGE - NIGHT

Agent Luma receives a mission to retrieve a coded key from a riverside office. Director Vex gives her a phrase: "The moon is late." Technician Pip hands her a pocket watch that emits a smoke cloud when opened. Luma opens it; the garage fills with smoke.

LUMA: Does it have another function?

PIP: It tells time.

A parrot in the garage repeats the phrase. Vex orders everyone to keep the code secret. Mister Click repeats it louder.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIVERSIDE OFFICE - NIGHT

Luma disguises herself as a delivery worker and approaches the entrance. Guard Odo asks for the password. She says, "The moon is late." The parrot repeats it from inside her satchel. Odo opens the door. Luma says the bird is part of the package.

Inside, a hallway camera tracks her. Pip directs her by radio, but the signal echoes through the office speakers. Every instruction reaches the guard. Luma turns left when told to turn right and walks into a supply closet. She finds a stack of boxes labeled CODES. They contain old keypad manuals.

SCENE 2A. INT. RIVERSIDE OFFICE - NIGHT

Pip guides Luma through the office on a floor plan. The plan marks three identical doors, each labeled SECURITY. She opens one and finds a supply closet full of umbrellas. The second opens to a stairwell. The third is the actual security desk, where Odo is feeding Mister Click a cracker. Luma tries to reach the desk without alerting anyone, but the parrot calls out every step of the phrase Vex gave her.

MISTER CLICK: The moon is late. The moon is late.

Odo says the bird has learned the phrase from the agency's practice recording. Pip checks his radio and discovers the recording is playing on loop. He turns it off. The phrase stops. Luma walks toward the desk, but a motion sensor turns on the overhead lights. She freezes in a delivery pose with an empty box.

SCENE 3. INT. OFFICE HALL - LATER

Luma uses a mirror to look around a corner. The parrot sees its reflection and repeats the password to itself. Odo hears the echo and follows the sound. Luma hides behind a rolling file cabinet, which slowly rolls down the hall. She stops it with her tail before it bumps the guard.

Pip reveals the key is inside a locked desk drawer. The code is written in a notebook. Vex says that sounds too simple for a spy mission. Luma finds the notebook under the office phone. The number written inside is the desk extension, not the drawer combination.

MISTER CLICK: The moon is late.

Odo: We know that.

SCENE 3A. INT. RECORDS ROOM - NIGHT

Luma and Odo compare the key tag with the office inventory. The label reads "CABINET 4" and the coded number matches the supply log. Vex arrives over the radio and tells Luma to find the vault. She asks if the mission file says vault. He reads the header again and realizes it says "spare key cabinet." The briefing team enlarged a thumbnail image until it looked like a vault door.

Pip sends a corrected file. Vex says the operation will continue, because the key still has to be returned. Luma tells him she is already doing that. The parrot repeats "returned" in a cheerful voice and flaps from the satchel onto the filing cabinet.

SCENE 4. INT. OFFICE BREAK ROOM - NIGHT

Luma finds a wall calendar showing the drawer code as a reminder for the office manager. She opens the drawer and retrieves the coded key. The parrot has flown out of the satchel and landed beside the key. It repeats each number as Luma enters it, alerting Odo to her location.

Luma stops and offers the bird a cracker. Mister Click quiets down long enough for her to close the drawer. Odo arrives. She tells him the key is a spare for the office's supply cabinet and shows him the label. The agency misread the mission file; there is no secret vault.

SCENE 5. INT. AGENCY GARAGE - DAWN

Vex reads the corrected file. The coded key was intended for the office manager, who has been waiting for the agency to return it. Pip removes the smoke cartridge from the watch. Luma returns the key and receives a signed receipt. Mister Click is adopted by Odo's office, where he can repeat the daily access code to authorized staff.

VEX: The operation was a success.

LUMA: We delivered a key.

PIP: And tested the watch.

FINAL IMAGE: Mister Click sits beside a calendar and repeats tomorrow's appointment to himself, while the smoke watch remains safely closed.
