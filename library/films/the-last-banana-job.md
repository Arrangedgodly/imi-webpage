# The Last Banana Job

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: Glossy ensemble heist movies*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Logline: A retired capuchin locksmith reunites a crew of specialists to steal a six-foot golden banana from a museum gala, only to learn the fruit is a decoy and the real prize is the exhibit's neglected rescue fund.

Cast: Mabel, a retired capuchin who can open any latch but never a jar; Pip, a young macaque who climbs before thinking; Gloria, a langur getaway driver with excellent peripheral vision; Soot, an elderly chimpanzee sound engineer; Mr. Brindle, a spectacled museum tapir; and the Croupier, a cockatoo who narrates nobody's plans but his own.

SCENE 1. INT. CLOSED BANANA ARCADE, NIGHT

Rain ticks the skylight. MABEL sits at a counter, trying to open a jar of pickled beets. The lid wins. PIP drops through the ceiling and lands in a basket of arcade tokens.

PIP: I have a job.

MABEL: You have a bruise.

He shows her a photograph of the Golden Cavendish, a six-foot bronze banana plated in real gold. It sits in the new Museum of Natural History, under a glass dome, at tomorrow's gala. The rumor says its seed contains the last wild banana genome. Mabel reads the small print on the back. The seed is a museum gift-shop magnet.

MABEL: We're stealing a magnet?

PIP: It's a very rare magnet.

Mabel looks at the arcade's rent notice and at the old crew photograph pinned above it. She dials a number.

SCENE 2. INT. Soot's recording booth, EVENING

SOOT, a chimpanzee wearing studio headphones, edits the sound of a single door closing. Gloria waits in a van outside, parallel parking between two cones with an inch to spare. Mabel arrives with a museum map.

MABEL: No one gets hurt. We take the banana, we leave the magnet.

SOOT: I can make the alarms sound like a choir. People trust a choir.

GLORIA: People trust a parking ticket less. I found the loading bay.

Pip has brought a backpack full of bananas. He keeps eating them during the briefing. Mabel crosses each banana off the supplies list.

The museum's rear door opens at midnight for the catering crew. The dome has three locks and a pressure plate. Soot will distract the gala with a sound mix. Gloria will get them in and out. Pip will climb to the catwalk. Mabel will pick the locks. The plan fits on one napkin, mostly because Pip ate the corner.

SCENE 3. INT. MUSEUM GALA, NIGHT

Guests in formalwear crowd around a string quartet. Brindle, the tapir curator, delivers a speech beside the Golden Cavendish. He clears his nose against a decorative fern and apologizes to the fern.

SOOT crouches in the sound booth. Instead of the choir, he plays the recording of a thousand tiny monkeys discovering a locked snack cupboard. The room goes silent. Then every primate guest turns toward the sound booth. A waiter drops a tray. Brindle leaves the stage to rescue the fern.

Pip scales a column. He moves with the sure confidence of a macaque who has climbed every forbidden shelf in three apartments. At the catwalk, he meets a real security monkey, a sleepy tamarin named Nibs.

NIBS: You're not supposed to be up here.

PIP: Neither is the fern, technically.

Nibs accepts this as a compelling procedural argument and follows Pip toward the dome.

SCENE 4. INT. GOLDEN CAVENDISH HALL, CONTINUOUS

Mabel kneels at the lock. Gloria watches the door. The lock opens. A red light blinks beneath the glass.

MABEL: Weight plate.

Pip, already on the rim, freezes. He is a small monkey, but his pockets hold fourteen bananas and a wrench. The plate sinks. The dome lowers like an enormous cake cover.

Gloria drives a display cart under the edge, brakes squealing. Mabel slips beneath the gap and reaches for the fruit. The bronze shell falls away. Inside is a hollow display with a stack of letters, a bank statement, and a photograph of Brindle feeding street monkeys behind the museum.

The real prize is a transfer form. The museum board has been quietly diverting the gala's conservation gifts away from its rescue clinic. Brindle prepared the form but needs proof of the board's scheme.

MABEL: We came for a magnet.

BRINDLE: It was the only exhibit they wouldn't inventory twice.

Brindle steps out from behind the curtain. He has been waiting. He wants the letters shown to the donors before the board can shred them.

SCENE 5. INT. MUSEUM GALA, MOMENTS LATER

The crew has no time for a second plan. Soot patches into the projection system. Pip climbs the drapes with Nibs following, both carrying letters in their teeth. Gloria pushes the cart through the crowd. Mabel steps onto the stage and switches off Brindle's speech.

MABEL: If you came to see a banana, sorry. If you came to help monkeys, stay for the receipts.

Soot projects the board's emails and Brindle's rescue photographs onto the wall. Donors lift their phones. Board members try to leave; Gloria has parked the catering van across the exit, exactly within the loading markings.

The museum director signs the transfer on the spot, under a hundred camera flashes. The clinic receives its funding. The Golden Cavendish returns to the case with a fresh plaque: MAGNET NOT INCLUDED.

SCENE 6. EXT. BANANA ARCADE, DAWN

The crew splits a crate of rescued bananas on the arcade steps. Nibs has joined them, still wearing his museum badge. Mabel finally opens the beet jar. Pip takes one beet, tastes it, and gives it back.

GLORIA: What now?

MABEL: We retire again. This time someplace with jars that have pull tabs.

Above them, the museum's new banner flaps in the dawn wind. It reads: ADMISSION FREE FOR ALL PRIMATES. A tiny tamarin has already climbed to the top of the sign.

The crew watches him wave. Cut to black.
