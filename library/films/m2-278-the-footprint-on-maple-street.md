# The Footprint on Maple Street

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: giant-monster blockbusters, aerial panic montages, trembling cups, and a city saved by the creature's overlooked behavior*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A town mistakes enormous tracks for a rampaging beast and evacuates before the spring parade, until a schoolyard team discovers the monster is moving the town's own oversized float.

Cast
- DR. SENA, an orangutan zoologist who measures before she warns.
- JORI, a macaque junior ranger with sharp hearing.
- MAYOR KET, a gibbon who loves emergency maps.
- TALI, a capuchin parade builder whose tail steadies her paintbrush.
- THE CREATURE, a huge shadow that appears at the edge of town.

## Scene 1: The Footprint

EXT. MAPLE STREET - MORNING

A deep print dents the dirt. A cup on a fence rattles. The town's alarm bell rings.

MAYOR KET: A giant creature has entered Maple Street. Evacuate the square.

DR. SENA: The print is six feet long, but the edges are soft. It may have been pressed by a prop.

MAYOR KET: It is too large for a prop.

JORI: The parade's mountain float is six feet wide.

TALI: It is on the other side of the hill. We left it there during the night.

A heavy thump sounds from the woods. Every window rattles. The mayor points to the ridge.

## Scene 2: The Panic Montage

INT. TOWN HALL - DAY

Ket unrolls a glowing map. Red circles mark the school, market, and parade route. The circles overlap until the map shows no safe area.

MAYOR KET: The creature is approaching all neighborhoods at once.

DR. SENA: Those circles are the evacuation zones. The creature is only marked at the hill.

MAYOR KET: It could move.

JORI: We should listen for the thump again.

The town cups tremble. The mayor orders a helicopter survey. The helicopter pilot reports a moving shadow, but the camera shows only the ridge and a cloud.

## Scene 3: The Search

EXT. WOODLAND PATH - AFTERNOON

Sena, Jori, and Tali follow the impressions. They find a broad track, then a long drag mark leading uphill. Tali recognizes flecks of orange paint.

TALI: This is from our float. The mountain has a painted sun on its side.

JORI: Who moved it?

TALI: The wheels are locked.

Sena examines the soil. A small trail of water leads toward a maintenance ditch. She hears a soft grinding sound.

DR. SENA: The float moved when the hill drainage pipe pushed water under its base. The ground is sloped.

Another shadow passes across the trail. The group hides behind a tree. A huge shape moves across the crest, one broad leg at a time.

## Scene 4: The Monster's Approach

The creature steps into view: a towering papier-mache mountain on wheels. Its cloth legs drag along the ground. The wind has caught its sail, pushing it downhill. Each wheel hits a rut with a thunderous thump.

TALI: That is our float.

JORI: It is headed for the square.

DR. SENA: It weighs more than the parade crew can stop by hand.

They run alongside it. Jori climbs a low branch to signal the town. Tali jumps onto the rear platform, uses her tail to steady herself, and reaches the emergency brake rope. The rope is tied in a complex parade knot.

TALI: Who tied this?

JORI: You did.

TALI: I had good reason at the time.

She pulls the wrong loop. A side panel swings open, revealing painted birds.

## Scene 5: The Town Saves the Town

Mayor Ket sees Jori's signal and sends the parade crew to clear the road. Dr. Sena directs people to lower the sail by releasing the corner ropes. The town follows her instructions instead of the emergency map.

MAYOR KET: I can move the evacuation line.

DR. SENA: Move the float's route. The creature is the float.

The crew releases the sail. The wind dies. Tali finds the brake knot and loosens it. The mountain float rolls into a hay pile and stops gently. Its head panel falls forward, revealing a carved smiling face.

TALI: The monster has a grin.

JORI: It has a paint bucket on its head.

## Scene 6: The Explanation

In the square, Sena shows the residents the float tracks and the drainage slope. The thumps came from the wheels crossing the ruts. The vibrating cups were caused by the same ground tremors as the parade float moved.

MAYOR KET: I will update the map.

DR. SENA: Mark the drainage ditch and the float route.

TALI: And add a brake check before the parade.

JORI: Can we still have the spring parade?

TALI: Yes, once we anchor the float.

The townspeople help carry stakes and ropes to the square. The parade begins later than planned. The float stays in place, and the creature is not seen again.

## Final Image

A child places a small cup on the ground beside the float. It does not rattle. High above, a cloud moves across the moon-shaped painted sun.
