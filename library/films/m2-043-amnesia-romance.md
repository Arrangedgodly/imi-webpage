# The Name on the Napkin

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic amnesia melodramas, memory-recovery montages, and coincidental keepsakes*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A forgetful gibbon mistakes a grocery note for a love letter and tries to reconstruct a bus-stop conversation as a sweeping romance, while the woman involved keeps correcting the details.

Cast: Theo, a gibbon who misplaces short-term memories; Nami, a macaque who writes lists on napkins; Dr. Moss, a tortoise memory specialist; and a bus driver with no patience for flashbacks.

SCENE 1. INT. BUS, MORNING

Theo discovers a napkin in his coat pocket: MEET AT BLUE BENCH, BRING PEARS. He clutches it as if it were a clue from a lost life. The bus driver checks the note.

THEO: Is Nami the name of someone I loved?

DRIVER: It's the name on a grocery list.

THEO: How can you be sure?

DRIVER: I drove you both here yesterday. She bought pears.

A passenger asks Theo to move so they can sit down. He stares out the window, imagining rain, a violin, and a dramatic glance. The bus bumps. His imagined violin falls off an imaginary chair.

SCENE 2. EXT. BLUE BENCH, AFTERNOON

Theo waits at the blue bench with three pears. A squirrel takes one and says it is lunch. Nami arrives with a shopping bag. She recognizes Theo and asks whether he has brought the oats.

THEO: I brought pears.

NAMI: You were supposed to bring oats.

THEO: The napkin said pears.

NAMI: It says pears on the other side. Oats is on this side.

Theo flips it. The list continues. He blushes. Nami takes a pear and invites him to carry the groceries. He asks whether this is the start of something. She says it is the end of a shopping trip.

SCENE 3. INT. MEMORY CLINIC, LATER

Moss hands Theo a card and asks him to write what he remembers without looking at the napkin. Theo writes: green bench, late bus, pencil borrowed. He pauses at the name. Nami spells it for him. Moss points out that remembering her name with help is still remembering the conversation. Theo asks if they can add a thunderclap to the timeline. Moss refuses and taps the radiator, which makes a small clank. Theo says the radiator has excellent timing.

Dr. Moss displays a blank timeline. Theo fills it with a storm, a dropped book, and a violinist. Nami corrects every detail: it was sunny, he dropped a pencil, and the violinist was tuning across the street.

MOSS: A memory isn't a film trailer.

THEO: Mine has music.

NAMI: That was the bus brakes.

Moss asks Theo to describe only what he knows. Theo remembers that Nami lent him a pencil, showed him a green bench, and told him the bus was late. He wrote BLUE BENCH on the napkin because he forgot the color. Nami says the mistake made her laugh; she did not consider it a proposal.

SCENE 4. EXT. MARKET, EVENING

They pass a shop window displaying a violin. Theo tries to recreate the imagined rain scene by standing under the shop awning and looking wistful. Nami gives him the grocery bags. He looks less wistful and more overloaded. The bags swing into the window display; a toy violin plays when a sensor triggers.

THEO: The music found us.

NAMI: The bags found the sensor.

They step away, and the music stops. Theo asks if this counts as their first duet. Nami says it counts as carrying groceries safely.

Theo attempts to recreate the meeting. He drops a book. Nami picks it up, then hands it back before he can call the moment fate. A violinist tunes nearby. Theo waits for a romantic cue. The violinist asks if they have seen his missing string. Nami finds it wrapped around Theo's shopping bag.

THEO: That's a coincidence.

NAMI: That's where you put the bag.

He asks if she wants to have dinner sometime. Nami says yes, if he can remember which day. He writes the plan on a proper card and asks her to check it. She writes her name correctly under his.

SCENE 5. INT. BUS, NEXT DAY

Theo boards with the card. He reads it aloud: dinner Friday, six o'clock. The driver says the bus is on time. Theo looks disappointed; there is no dramatic delay. Nami boards at the next stop and takes the empty seat beside him.

NAMI: Still remember me?

THEO: Nami. Friday. Six. Pears are optional.

She says that is accurate enough. He offers her the napkin, now folded into a tiny grocery list. She uses it to jot down tea and bread.

Moss passes by on the bus and gives Theo a thumbs-up. Theo checks the card again before waving back.

FINAL IMAGE: The bus turns sharply. Theo's card slides off his lap. Nami catches it, adds "bring oats," and returns it. Theo checks the note twice before smiling.
