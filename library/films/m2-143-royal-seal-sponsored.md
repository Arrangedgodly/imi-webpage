# The Royal Seal Is Sponsored

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: product placement that interrupts a story to give modern products heroic close-ups*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A costume drama's royal messenger keeps stopping to display a sponsor's travel mug, until the queen asks who actually delivered the message.

Cast: Asha, a macaque director; Rul, a gorilla messenger; Nima, a lemur queen; and Puck, a capuchin placement coordinator.

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE HALL SET, MORNING

Rul enters in a velvet cloak carrying a sealed message. Asha rehearses the scene: the queen asks if the northern bridge is safe; the messenger reports a flood. Puck places a stainless travel mug on the table beside the throne.

ASHA: Why is there a mug?

PUCK: The sponsor requested a visible placement.

NIMA: What does it contain?

PUCK: Water.

NIMA: The palace has cups.

Puck asks Rul to carry the mug in his other hand. Rul tries, but the cloak catches on the handle. He drops the message. Asha asks him to keep the seal intact. Rul sets the mug down and picks up the letter. Puck places a tiny mark on the floor to show where the mug should stand. Nima asks whether the mug has a speaking part. Puck says it has a visibility requirement. Rul tries the entrance again and nearly steps on the mark. The director moves it beside the throne.

SCENE 2. INT. PALACE HALL SET, LATER

The take begins. Rul enters, bows, and delivers the message. Puck whispers that the mug should be visible. Rul turns it toward camera. The lid clicks. Nima asks if the bridge is safe. Rul says it is flooded. Puck asks for the mug to be held higher.

RUL: The bridge is flooded.

PUCK: Can the mug face the lens?

NIMA: I asked about the bridge.

Rul turns back toward the queen. Puck moves the mug beside the sealed letter. The condensation makes the royal seal damp. Nima lifts the letter, sees the ink smear, and asks for a new prop. Puck says the sponsor prefers the mug near the action.

ASHA: The action is the message.

Puck takes the mug off set. Nima delivers the line again. Rul keeps both hands free. Puck watches the monitor and says the sponsor needs a second angle. Asha moves the camera to the other side. The mug is now reflected in the polished shield on the wall. Nima turns the shield toward the hall. The reflection disappears, and the shield blocks the queen's face.

NIMA: That shield has a purpose.

PUCK: So does the mug.

ASHA: The scene has one message and one messenger.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE KITCHEN SET, AFTERNOON

The sponsor's representative arrives with a larger mug. Puck says it will read better in wide shots. The mug is taller than the table and blocks the throne. Asha asks if it can be placed on the floor. Puck says the logo must face camera. Rul tries to set it on a stool. The stool wobbles.

Nima asks the representative to sit. The mug tips toward the script pages. Rul catches it. The lid stays closed. Everyone looks at the mug.

REPRESENTATIVE: Does that count as product use?

NIMA: It counts as a spill avoided.

Asha moves the mug to the kitchen set. Nima asks for an ordinary cup of water. The prop master brings a ceramic cup. Rul carries the message to the queen and reports the bridge is closed. Nima orders a detour for the market carts. The scene plays without interruption. The representative asks if the mug could be mentioned in the dialogue. Nima asks Rul to tell the court what he saw. Rul describes the river rising over the bridge, then hands the queen the map. The representative starts to lift the mug into frame. Asha gives him the damp seal to hold while the prop master resets the table.

SCENE 4. INT. EDITING ROOM, EVENING

Puck reviews the footage. The sponsor's mug appears for three seconds in the kitchen and not at all in the palace hall. He asks Asha to insert a close-up. Nima says the only close-up needed is the wet royal seal, which shows why the mug was removed.

Puck saves the cut. The sponsor agrees to a still photograph of the mug beside the kitchen door. The crew leaves the palace set for the next day's market scene. Puck asks if the mug can travel with them as a recurring object. Nima says the queen has ordered carts to avoid the bridge. Puck checks the story pages and sees the mug belongs to no character. He leaves it on the folding table.

FINAL IMAGE: The travel mug sits on a modern folding table behind the set, its logo facing the sponsor representative instead of the camera.
