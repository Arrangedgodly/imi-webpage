# The Room Without a View

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: home-renovation competition films, dramatic reveal curtains, demolition montages, and judges who score rooms before the paint dries*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
RENO HOST VIVA, a gibbon
MICA, a capuchin carpenter
TOL, a macaque neighbor
JUDGE BRAM, a ring-tailed lemur designer

SCENE 1. INT. OLD COMMUNITY ROOM - MORNING

Viva introduces a renovation challenge: transform a windowless room into a shared garden classroom by sundown. Mica has a plan to open a skylight. Tol wants shelves for seed trays. The judge prefers a dramatic paint reveal.

VIVA: You have eight hours and one curtain.

MICA: Why is there a curtain?

VIVA: For the reveal.

SCENE 2. INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - DAY

A demolition montage begins. Mica removes old shelves carefully. Viva asks for more dust. Tol carries out a broken table, then returns with seedlings. The judge critiques the room's "lack of transformation" before anything is finished.

Mica finds a beam above the ceiling. The skylight cannot be cut there. The room's only exterior wall is shared with the library, which cannot be disturbed.

SCENE 2A. INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - LATE MORNING

Mica removes a warped cabinet. Behind it, a pipe runs along the wall. The crew stops work. Viva asks for a dramatic reveal of the hidden infrastructure. Tol turns off the water and checks the pipe for leaks.

TOL: We can build around it, but the lower shelf has to stay removable.

MICA: We'll make a hinged panel and keep the shelves shallow.

The judge objects that a hinged panel does not look finished. Mica shows him the valve and explains that the room must remain maintainable. The judge asks whether the panel can match the wall paint.

VIVA: That would ruin the before-and-after shot.

TOL: The room is for growing seedlings, not for a photograph.

The neighbors sand the reclaimed table legs. Viva films a slow-motion brush stroke. A seedling tips over, and Tol catches it before the soil spills. The crew adds a low guard rail to keep trays in place.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - AFTERNOON

The crew argues. Tol places seed trays on the floor to test the light. A narrow strip of sun moves across them from a high interior window between the room and library.

MICA: We don't need a skylight. We need the light to reach lower shelves.

They build reflective panels from safe white board and angle them toward the seed trays. Tol reuses the broken table legs to create a stand. Viva turns the construction into a montage with hammering, sawdust, and a slow-motion paint roller.

SCENE 3A. INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - LATE AFTERNOON

The reflective panels are tested one at a time. The first sends a bright beam into the hallway. The second makes a hot spot on a seed tray. Mica adjusts the angles. Tol checks the seedlings' leaves for heat stress.

TOL: This one is too warm. Move it lower.

MICA: If I angle it toward the wall, the light will spread.

The judge wants the panel centered for symmetry. Mica shifts it two inches to avoid glare. The reflection becomes even across the tables.

JUDGE BRAM: It is not symmetrical.

MICA: The seedlings are growing evenly.

Viva compares the before-and-after photos. The room looks less dramatic than a full skylight reveal, but the worktables are usable, the pipe can be reached, and the seedlings receive light.

VIVA: We can show the result without hiding the repair panel.

TOL: Leave it visible. Someone will need it later.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - SUNSET

The dramatic reveal curtain drops. It covers the new shelves. The judge pulls it aside. The panels reflect the last light into the room, brightening the seedlings and the work tables. One panel is too high; it sends a beam directly into the judge's eyes.

JUDGE BRAM: It is beautiful and blinding.

Mica lowers the panel. The room brightens evenly. Tol places the seedlings on shelves. The neighbors arrive with books and tools.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY ROOM - EVENING

The judging begins. Viva asks for a score out of ten. Bram says the room is a nine because the paint is still tacky. Tol touches the wall and gets blue paint on one hand.

TOL: It's not dry.

VIVA: The reveal is complete.

MICA: The room is usable. We can open the window to the library corridor and let the paint cure.

The neighbors begin setting up. The judge awards the project first place for making a bright room without cutting a hole in the roof. Viva asks whether there will be a sequel challenge.

FINAL IMAGE: A row of seedlings sits beneath reflected sunlight. The curtain is reused as a shade cloth, tied over the brightest shelf. The room has a view, though the window faces the library's book cart.
