# The Last Witness at the Beehive

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: detective mysteries, unreliable witnesses, and investigations that treat every sound as a clue*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
NARI, a lemur ranger
PIP, a capuchin beekeeper
VEX, a macaque detective
MARA, a gibbon clerk
THE BEE, a silent performer

SCENE 1. EXT. ORCHARD - MORNING

A honey jar is missing from the picnic table. Vex places a magnifying glass beside a ring of sticky marks.

VEX: The thief came this way.

PIP: The jar was moved to the shade.

NARI: Who moved it?

PIP: I did. The sun was warming the honey.

Vex studies the table. He finds a bee walking along the sticky ring.

VEX: Our witness has arrived.

Mara opens a notebook. The bee circles the jar lid and flies away.

MARA: It refused to testify.

SCENE 2. INT. BEEKEEPER'S SHED - LATER

Pip shows the detective the hive. Vex asks whether the bees saw anyone take the jar. Pip says they are busy. Vex holds up a drawing of a jar.

VEX: Does this look familiar?

A bee lands on the drawing. Vex writes "positive identification."

NARI: It landed on the paper.

VEX: The paper is evidence.

Pip places a small sample vial from the hive beside the drawing. The bee leaves the page and lands on the vial. Vex says it has identified the original. Nari moves the drawing. The bee stays with the sample.

MARA: The witness prefers the exhibit.

VEX: Then the exhibit is persuasive.

Pip turns the jar so the bee can reach a drop of honey on the rim. The bee feeds, then flies out of the shed. Vex follows, holding the drawing like a warrant. It catches on the doorframe and tears across the middle.

PIP: The witness has left.

VEX: The case has expanded.

Pip lifts a frame from the hive. The bees cluster around a patch of wax. Mara holds the notebook near it. Vex says the pattern is a secret message.

PIP: It's where the honey comb starts.

VEX: A map.

Nari points to a trail of sticky drops leading to the shade tree. They follow it. The trail ends at a basket of fruit. A jar lid sits beside it.

SCENE 3. EXT. SHADE TREE - AFTERNOON

Mara asks the witnesses to stand where they were at the time the jar disappeared. Pip stands by the table. Nari stands near the tree. Vex places himself between them and says he is reconstructing the crime.

A bee lands on his sleeve. He freezes.

VEX: The witness has approached me.

NARI: It is looking for pollen.

Vex holds out his notebook. The bee walks across it and stops beside a word he has written: "honey." He draws a circle around the word.

MARA: The bee has edited your report.

Pip checks the jar. It is inside the fruit basket. He lifts it out. The lid is loose, and the sticky ring on the table came from a drip.

PIP: I put it here when the honey warmed.

VEX: Who moved it from the shade?

PIP: I did.

Vex asks Pip to demonstrate how he carried the jar. Pip lifts it by the lid. The lid turns, and a thin line of honey runs across the basket handle. A bee approaches the scent, lands, and walks along the handle.

NARI: It followed the honey.

VEX: The witness followed the evidence.

Mara turns the jar so the lid sits straight. The drip stops. Vex marks the table, basket, and shade tree on his map. All three are within a few steps.

MARA: Your crime scene is smaller than your map.

SCENE 4. INT. BEEKEEPER'S SHED - LATER

Vex prepares the case report. Pip puts a cloth under the jar. Vex writes "bee participated" on the report. Nari asks what the bee did. Vex says it was present, moved, and left.

NARI: The witness has left.

VEX: It could be avoiding the question.

Mara reads the notebook. There are three lines: "jar moved," "bee seen," and "case open." Vex says the third clue must be a window. Pip points to the shed door. It is open.

MARA: The case is the investigation.

Vex turns the notebook over. The word "open" refers to the case file, which he never closed.

SCENE 5. EXT. ORCHARD - SUNSET

Pip returns the jar to the picnic table. Vex marks the case resolved. The bee lands on the jar lid and walks around its rim.

VEX: Is it here to sign the report?

PIP: It is tasting the honey.

Vex puts his pen down. Nari closes the notebook. Mara lifts the jar and moves it back into the shade.

FINAL IMAGE: The bee flies into the hive while Vex files the case under "witness unavailable."
