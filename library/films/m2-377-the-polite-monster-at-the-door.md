# The Polite Monster at the Door

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: monster movies, invasion alarms, terrified villagers, and creature reveals that turn out to be extremely courteous*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
MAYOR LUMA, a lemur
SCOUT PIP, a capuchin
ENGINEER VEX, a macaque
MONSTER ODO, a gibbon in a giant costume
NARI, an orangutan translator

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN GATE - DUSK

A huge shadow covers the town. The gate rattles. Mayor Luma calls an emergency meeting. Scout Pip reports a giant creature approaching with one enormous footstep at a time. Engineer Vex sounds the alarm. The shadow stops outside the gate.

PIP: It is waiting.

LUMA: It may be listening.

The gate opens a few inches. A large hand reaches in and places a basket of apples on the ground.

SCENE 2. INT. TOWN HALL - NIGHT

The residents gather behind a table. Nari studies a picture Pip drew of the visitor. She says the creature is holding a basket, not a weapon. Vex designs a signal flag. The flag has three red stripes and reads STAY AWAY. Luma suggests a welcome sign instead.

The monster knocks gently. The knock sounds like a quake because the door is old. Pip opens it. Odo, wearing a giant creature costume, bows and introduces himself. He has come to return the town's orchard ladder, which blew into his garden during the storm.

SCENE 2A. INT. TOWN HALL - NIGHT

Nari asks Odo to repeat his name slowly so the town can write it correctly. Vex records it as "Monster O." Odo corrects him and gives his full name. Pip fetches the visitor log. The town had invited a theater group to rehearse, but the letter was delivered to the wrong address. The invitation had a drawing of a monster beside the entrance. The theater group had been expected to rehearse at the town hall, not the gate. Odo followed the map to the gate and waited for someone to answer.

LUMA: We thought you were here to frighten us.

ODO: I was waiting for permission to come in.

The residents notice his padded gloves are too large to use a normal door handle. Odo demonstrates by tapping with one claw, then apologizes for the loud sound. Luma asks him to wait while they open the side gate. Vex draws a new map that marks the theater entrance separately from the town gate.

SCENE 3. EXT. TOWN GATE - LATER

The ladder is too large to fit through the main gate. Odo lowers it carefully and waits while Vex checks the entrance. Nari points out the wider service gate on the side wall. Luma asks the residents to clear its path before Odo tries again.

The crowd follows, expecting a dramatic confrontation. Odo waits outside while Vex checks the wider service gate. Luma asks the residents to clear the path, and Pip carries the wrench over. The gate remains closed until the ladder can pass safely.

SCENE 3A. EXT. SIDE ENTRANCE - LATER

Odo carries the ladder around the wall to the side entrance. The first opening is still narrow; Pip tries turning the ladder vertically, but it reaches above the roofline. Vex opens the wider service gate, whose hinges squeak loudly. He loosens them with a wrench while Odo waits with the ladder clear of the wall. They guide it through the wide opening once and return it to the town's storage rack. Odo then helps Luma tighten the rattling main-gate hinge, while Vex puts away the signal flag. Odo says he borrowed the ladder to repair a roof at his theater. The storm blew it off the truck on the way home. Nari suggests the town attach a tag to shared tools. Pip writes the ladder's number on the tag and adds Odo's theater as its last location.

SCENE 4. INT. TOWN HALL - MORNING

The town shares breakfast with Odo. He explains that the costume is used for a neighborhood theater show. It was designed to look frightening from far away, but the feet are padded to protect the garden paths. Nari asks if he can wear it for the children. He says yes, as long as they know it is a costume.

The gate hinge is fixed. Luma posts a sign asking visitors to knock. Vex removes the alarm bell. Pip returns the apples to the basket, then adds a note thanking Odo for bringing them.

SCENE 5. EXT. TOWN GATE - SUNSET

Odo walks home carrying the repaired ladder. The residents wave. The giant shadow passes over the wall once more, but this time everyone recognizes the costume. The children ask for a photo. Odo poses with the ladder and the welcome sign.

FINAL IMAGE: The gate swings quietly on its new hinge as the monster bows and closes it gently behind him.
