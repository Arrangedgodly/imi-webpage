# The Bakery's Midnight Launch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: product-launch films that treat a new loaf as a revolutionary technology*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
BAKER MARA, a macaque
PRESENTER VEX, an orangutan
ASSISTANT PIP, a capuchin
TIMER ODO, a gibbon
TASTER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. BAKERY - NIGHT

A loaf rests beneath a cloth. Vex stands before a blank banner. Pip checks the oven timer.

VEX: Tonight, bread changes forever.

MARA: It's a new rye loaf. The recipe has one extra seed.

ODO: Oven is off. The loaf has cooled for twenty minutes.

NARI: I can taste a slice after you cut it.

VEX: The launch should include smoke.

PIP: The oven is off, and the ventilation fan is on. No smoke.

SCENE 2. INT. BAKERY - CONTINUOUS

Mara lifts the cloth. Vex presents the loaf as Pip places it on the cutting board.

VEX: Behold: the future.

MARA: It is today's batch. Please don't touch the crust until it is sliced.

PIP: The knife is clean. I'll cut two samples.

ODO: The loaf is one kilo. The order list calls for six.

VEX: We have produced a limited release.

MARA: We have six loaves cooling in the rack. This is the sample loaf.

SCENE 2A. INT. BAKERY - NIGHT

Pip checks the loaf labels against the recipe card. Mara keeps the six finished loaves on separate cooling racks.

PIP: Batch one has rye flour, water, yeast, salt, and sunflower seeds. The card lists the same ingredients.

NARI: The seeds are toasted. They smell warm, not burnt.

ODO: Timer stopped at 11:32. Oven is off and the trays are cooling.

VEX: Could we call this the midnight breakthrough?

MARA: Call it the midnight bake. We have made rye bread before.

PIP: The new version changes the seed ratio. I will mark that on the card so the morning team can compare it with the previous batch.

Vex puts a glossy brochure beside the loaves. A corner touches one cooling rack.

MARA: Move the brochure. It isn't food-safe packaging.

VEX: It is a launch deck.

ODO: The loaf wraps are in the drawer. Use those after the bread reaches room temperature.

Pip checks the center of one loaf with a thermometer. It is still warm. Nari moves the rack away from the open window.

NARI: Airflow is good here. No crumbs will fall onto the floor.

MARA: We'll wrap the loaves after they cool, then the delivery driver can collect them at seven.

SCENE 2B. INT. BAKERY - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the oven door seal and confirms that the oven is cold. Odo carries the cooling rack to the back table while Pip keeps the ingredient card in view.

ODO: Rack two is full. The six loaves can cool on rack three with space between them.

PIP: I'll move each loaf with the bread paddle. The crust is still soft.

VEX: The first batch is ready for the launch.

NARI: It is still cooling. We should not wrap warm bread; condensation will soften the crust.

MARA: Use the recipe card to note the bake time and the seed ratio. No claims about a revolution.

Vex crosses out "the future of bread" on the banner and writes "new rye loaf, 7 a.m."

VEX: The words feel less enormous.

PIP: The loaf is the same size.

Odo sets a clean tray beside the rack for the morning delivery. The label faces outward and lists the ingredients.

ODO: Tray ready. Driver arrives at seven. I will leave the order sheet at the desk.

SCENE 2C. INT. BAKERY - CONTINUOUS

The final crumb sample is swept from the counter. Pip wipes the board and hangs the clean cloth to dry.

PIP: Kitchen is clean. The loaves remain on the rack until cool.

MARA: We will wrap the order after cooling, then the morning crew can stage pickup.

VEX: The launch concludes at seven.

ODO: Delivery pickup is seven. The launch is now.



NARI: The loaf is cooling evenly.

Pip confirms the rack is stable. Mara records the loaf count on the delivery sheet; no loaf leaves the shop before morning pickup.

SCENE 3. INT. BAKERY - LATER

Nari tastes a small slice. She chews thoughtfully.

NARI: The rye is good. The extra seed adds a little crunch.

VEX: A historic reaction.

PIP: I can label the batch with the bake time and ingredient list.

MARA: Good. Put the allergy information on the same card.

SCENE 4. INT. BAKERY - MIDNIGHT

The first loaf is wrapped for the morning delivery. The banner now reads NEW RYE LOAF, AVAILABLE AT 7 A.M.

VEX: The launch is complete.

ODO: Delivery begins at seven. We are going home now.

TAG: Vex tries to unveil the empty board. Pip hands him a broom instead.
