# The Projectionist's First Eclipse

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent-era science adventures that present a first film projection as proof of a cosmic phenomenon*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A village projectionist screens a painted eclipse for the first time, but a hole in the screen turns the moon's shadow into an accidental tour of the audience.

Cast: projectionist Jori, a lemur; inventor Sema, a macaque; schoolteacher Baku, an orangutan; child visitor Nilo, a capuchin; pianist Havi, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - INT. VILLAGE SCHOOL - EVENING

Sema wheels a hand-cranked projector into the classroom. Jori checks a white sheet stretched across the blackboard. A title card reads THE ECLIPSE: A MOTION PICTURE. Havi sits at the piano with a stack of cue cards.

SEMA: The film is six minutes long.

JORI: The reel is five minutes long.

NILO: Which minute is missing?

Sema explains that the final frame is held for a full minute. Jori dims the lamps. A painted moon begins to cross the sun. The screen has a tiny tear near the top.

SCENE 2 - INT. SCHOOL - CONTINUOUS

As the painted shadow reaches the sun, the projector beam passes through the tear and lands on the ceiling. The eclipse appears to move over a row of hats. Havi plays the cue for total darkness, but one classroom lamp remains on. The audience turns to look at it.

BAKU: We have not reached totality.

JORI: The film has shown us the roof.

Sema adjusts the projector. The image drops to the floor, where it covers the teacher's shoes. Baku lifts both feet as if floating in space. Nilo giggles. Jori turns the crank more slowly, and the moon inches across the shoes.

SCENE 3 - INT. SCHOOL - LATER

Sema patches the tear with a strip of opaque cloth. The projector runs too hot, so the film begins to curl in the gate. Jori stops the crank before it jams. Havi keeps playing the suspense cue, unaware that the image has frozen. The audience waits as the same half-eclipse remains on the screen.

NILO: Is the moon stuck?

SEMA: The film is resting.

Baku switches on the house lights. The school clock shows the screening is already ten minutes late for cleanup. Jori rewinds the strip by hand. Sema explains the projector's cooling pause, then demonstrates how to keep the shutter open only while the crank moves.

Baku places a real astronomy chart beside the projector and invites the children to compare it with the painted film. The film's moon crosses from left to right; the chart shows the shadow moving the other direction from their seats. Sema says the reel was painted for a traveling screen that faced the opposite wall. Jori rotates the projector stand. The image flips onto the side curtain, where the eclipse now moves correctly but covers the school's fire notice. Baku shifts the chart. Nilo holds a paper moon over the chart and makes his own shadow. The audience watches him instead of the projector.

SCENE 4 - INT. SCHOOL - MOMENTS LATER

They restart the reel. The painted eclipse reaches totality. The patched screen holds. The image looks crisp, and the audience grows quiet. Havi plays the final chord. The last frame stays on the screen as promised, but the projector lamp shines through the fixed image and heats the film.

Jori closes the shutter. Sema ends the projection one second early. Baku applauds. Nilo asks whether the moon will return. Sema points to the rewind handle and says the film can be shown again after the projector cools.

During the final run, the screen pinhole throws a tiny circle onto the back wall. Jori takes it for a celestial body and moves the projector to follow it. Sema holds the screen steady while Baku turns off each classroom lamp in sequence. The circle disappears when the hallway light is blocked, then returns when Nilo walks past the doorway. Havi changes the score from triumph to a quiet repeated note. The children follow the circle to the hallway and line up behind it like an orbiting procession.

Baku takes the projection crank from Jori and turns it one notch too far. The moon jumps across the sun in a single frame. Nilo gasps and holds his own paper moon up to block the hall lamp, making a second tiny eclipse on the wall. Sema asks him to keep the paper still for the final shot.

SCENE 5 - INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY - NIGHT

The audience leaves. Jori packs the projector, Sema rolls the patched screen, and Havi carries the cue cards. Nilo asks to borrow the film for the astronomy club. Baku says the reel needs its owner present. Jori writes the next show time on the screen's storage tag.

FINAL IMAGE: The patched screen leans against the classroom wall, showing a small moon-shaped pinhole of light from the hallway night lamp.
