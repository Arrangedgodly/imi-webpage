# The Macaque Protocol

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: Gadget-heavy international spy thrillers*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Logline: A spy chimp with a knack for escaping cages must stop a smooth-talking parrot from replacing every world leader with a convincingly polite puppet, using one borrowed key and a stubborn sense of smell.

Cast: Agent Koko, a chimpanzee field operative; Midge, a macaque handler whose tail can steady a moving camera; VELVET, a cockatoo mastermind; Sir Prawn, a crab diplomat; and Pogo, a capuchin bellhop who knows every service corridor in Geneva.

SCENE 1. INT. HOTEL LAKEVIEW, SERVICE CORRIDOR, NIGHT

Koko crawls beneath a banquet table, wearing a tuxedo jacket over a field harness. He is a chimpanzee, and his hands can open a latch while holding a dessert spoon. His earbud clicks.

MIDGE: The target has entered the blue room.

KOKO: I'm in the blue room.

MIDGE: Then the target has entered the other blue room.

At the far end, VELVET, a white cockatoo, delivers a speech about peaceful leadership. The delegates applaud. Koko smells a sharp scent beneath the room's lavender spray. It is banana oil, used to disguise the adhesive on a row of small mechanical masks.

SCENE 2. INT. HOTEL KITCHEN, LATER

Midge briefs Koko beside a rack of desserts. She has a camera taped to her tail, which she keeps from shaking with practiced poise.

VELVET plans to swap the diplomats' faces with polite clockwork puppets during the midnight toast. The puppets will sign a treaty giving Velvet control of every nation's seed vault. Midge has one key, stolen from a guard, and a map of the ventilation ducts.

KOKO: How many guards?

MIDGE: Four.

KOKO: How many doors?

MIDGE: Three.

KOKO: That sounds manageable.

MIDGE: One door is a freezer.

Koko takes a canapé, thinks, then puts it back. He dislikes espionage on an empty stomach, but he dislikes cold toes more.

SCENE 3. INT. VENTILATION SHAFT, NIGHT

Koko crawls through a duct. The metal bends under him. Below, VELVET explains that the puppets will make government "less emotional." A delegate asks whether this includes joy. Velvet says joy is a scheduling issue.

Koko drops into a linen closet. He grabs a sheet and creates a ghost disguise. It fools one guard, who salutes. The second guard notices Koko's feet.

GUARD: Ghosts don't have thumbs.

KOKO: This one is a very ambitious ghost.

The guard reaches for his radio. Koko swings the sheet around him, then escapes through the laundry chute. He lands in the freezer. His jacket freezes to a bag of peas. He tears free, leaving the jacket behind.

SCENE 4. INT. BLUE ROOM, MIDNIGHT

Velvet raises a glass. The puppets stand behind the delegates, their painted smiles too wide. Koko enters in a waiter's apron, carrying a tray of sparkling water. Midge crawls under the dais and reaches for the puppet control wire with her tail.

The cable is sticky with banana oil. She smells it and understands: the masks need heat to seal. She cuts the power to the room's heaters.

Koko spills water across Velvet's control panel. The cockatoo leaps onto the table, wings spread.

VELVET: You have no proof!

KOKO: Your puppet has my face.

He points. One of the mechanical masks has copied Koko's frozen, annoyed expression. The delegates turn. Velvet tries to flee through the curtains, but Koko tosses a bunch of bananas in its path. Velvet slips, lands in a chair, and is promptly surrounded by diplomats.

SCENE 5. INT. HOTEL SERVICE HALL, LATER

The treaty lies unsigned. The delegates argue over whether to destroy the puppets or donate them to a school play. Koko and Midge retrieve the seed vault keys. Pogo the capuchin bellhop appears with two towels and a ring of keys.

POGO: You left your jacket in the freezer.

KOKO: It was a tactical sacrifice.

Pogo hands it over. Midge returns the stolen key to the guard, who pretends not to recognize her. The guard asks if she can help with a stuck luggage cart. She uses her tail to lift the wheel.

SCENE 6. EXT. HOTEL BALCONY, DAWN

Koko and Midge watch the diplomats leave in a string of cars. The seed vaults remain independent. Velvet is escorted to a cage, where it complains about the lack of natural light.

MIDGE: You lost your jacket, your cover, and the key.

KOKO: We stopped a coup.

MIDGE: And stole four desserts.

Koko reveals a pastry tucked inside his sleeve. He offers half. Midge declines, then takes it. They eat quietly as the sun rises over Geneva.

A pigeon lands on the balcony rail. Koko and Midge both reach for their radios. It drops a crumb, then flies away.

KOKO: False alarm.

MIDGE: Best kind.

They head inside, walking instead of leaping. For a moment, the world is almost ordinary.
