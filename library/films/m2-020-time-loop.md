# One More Tuesday

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-loop comedy mechanics, repeated resets, and false mastery montages*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A macaque hotel clerk relives the same Tuesday until she learns the loop resets at the lobby bell, which rings whenever the manager misplaces his keys.

Cast: Ada, a macaque desk clerk; Mr. Flit, a forgetful peacock manager; Nibs, a capuchin bellhop; and Mrs. Yarrow, a tortoise guest who checks out at exactly noon.

SCENE 1. INT. HOTEL LOBBY, MORNING

The bell rings. Ada greets Mrs. Yarrow, who asks for a quiet room. Mr. Flit enters looking for his keys.

FLIT: Has anyone seen my keys?

The bell rings again. Ada wakes at the desk. It is the same morning. She says the line before Mrs. Yarrow does. The guest pauses, then asks for a quieter room.

SCENE 2. INT. HOTEL LOBBY, DAY

Ada tests the loop. She hides the keys, moves the bell, and tells Nibs to stand by the front door. Mr. Flit still rings the bell by tapping the desk with a spoon. The day resets.

Ada tries to use her foreknowledge to impress the guests. She predicts a rainstorm. The sun comes out. She predicts a package delivery. It arrives, but it is addressed to the hotel next door.

SCENE 3. INT. MANAGER'S OFFICE, AFTERNOON

Ada searches for the keys. Mr. Flit says he has not seen them. She finds three key rings in his desk drawer. He says those are decoys. She asks what they unlock. He says he does not know.

The bell rings. Ada wakes again. She starts a chart of reset triggers. Every event points to the keys, the bell, or Mr. Flit's tendency to tap metal objects.

SCENE 4. INT. LOBBY, LATE AFTERNOON

Ada asks the guests to help. Mrs. Yarrow guards the bell. Nibs guards the keys. Mr. Flit is asked to sit on his hands. He reaches for a spoon with his foot. The bell rings anyway as the spoon rolls across the counter.

Ada catches it before it strikes the bell. For the first time, the day continues. Mr. Flit remembers where his keys are: in his coat pocket. The loop breaks.

SCENE 5. INT. HOTEL LOBBY, EVENING

The guests check out. Ada celebrates with Nibs. Mrs. Yarrow says she knew something was odd because the breakfast menu kept returning to oatmeal. Mr. Flit announces he will install a key hook. He asks where his keys are.

SCENE 5A. INT. HOTEL LOBBY, RESET

The morning restarts. Ada recites the events before they happen.

ADA: The bell will ring.

FLIT: I haven't touched it.

NIBS: You have a spoon.

FLIT: It's for breakfast.

ADA: Put it down.

FLIT: Why?

ADA: The loop.

FLIT: What loop?

NIBS: The one we discussed yesterday.

FLIT: It is yesterday?

ADA: Tuesday again.

YARROW: Is my room ready?

ADA: Quiet room, third floor.

YARROW: I asked for second.

ADA: You asked for quiet.

YARROW: I changed my mind.

FLIT: Has anyone seen my keys?

ADA: Your coat pocket.

FLIT: Which coat?

NIBS: The one you're wearing.

FLIT: I don't wear this coat.

ADA: You do every Tuesday.

FLIT: This is Thursday.

YARROW: It's Tuesday.

FLIT: Who told you?

YARROW: The oatmeal.

ADA: We should label breakfast.

NIBS: The bell's swinging.

FLIT: I didn't touch it.

ADA: The spoon is still moving.

FLIT: Then it touched me.

YARROW: Can I check out?

ADA: At noon, as usual.

YARROW: I've been here three days.

NIBS: In one Tuesday.

FLIT: That makes no sense.

ADA: Welcome to Tuesday.

YARROW: Please lower my bill.

FLIT: We can discuss it tomorrow.

ADA: It'll be Tuesday.

FLIT: Then I can prepare.

The bell starts swinging. Ada catches it. Everyone freezes. Mr. Flit notices the keys in his coat and takes them out. The bell stops moving.

SCENE 5B. INT. HOTEL LOBBY, LATER

Ada shows Mr. Flit the key hook. He has already forgotten the morning and insists this is a new idea.

ADA: We tried the hook.

FLIT: Today?

NIBS: Today again.

FLIT: I remember breakfast.

YARROW: You forgot my room.

FLIT: I did not.

YARROW: You gave me a room by the elevator.

FLIT: It was quiet.

YARROW: The elevator made noise.

FLIT: Then it was convenient.

ADA: Put the keys on the hook.

FLIT: Why?

NIBS: So you find them.

FLIT: I already found them.

ADA: Tomorrow you'll forget.

FLIT: Tomorrow is Wednesday.

YARROW: It is Tuesday.

FLIT: I have a calendar.

NIBS: It's last year's.

FLIT: That explains it.

ADA: We can label it.

FLIT: Label the calendar?

ADA: Label the keys.

YARROW: Label the oatmeal.

FLIT: Why the oatmeal?

YARROW: It keeps returning.

ADA: That's breakfast.

FLIT: I knew that.

NIBS: The bell is swinging.

FLIT: I didn't touch it.

ADA: The door did.

YARROW: Draft from the lobby.

FLIT: We should fix that.

ADA: We will tomorrow.

NIBS: Tomorrow is Tuesday.

FLIT: Then we have time.

Ada pins the key ring to the hook. Mr. Flit watches it carefully. He writes "keys" on his hand, then washes his hands before lunch.

FINAL IMAGE: Ada hangs them on the hook. The bell rings as the hook swings. She looks at the camera, then places a folded towel beneath it. The next morning begins without a reset, but the oatmeal is back.
