# The Last Show in the Hollow

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: movie musicals that turn practical disagreements into showstopping songs while the crew is trying to close a community hall*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A troupe tries to stage one final musical before their hollow is repaired. The director keeps restarting the finale, while the carpenter needs the cast to clear the floor before the roof crew arrives.

Cast: Director Pella, a gibbon; carpenter Rook, a baboon; singer Miri, a macaque; and chorus leader Tavi, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HOLLOW, MORNING

Pella stands on a small stage. Rook carries a ladder past her.

PELLA: Places for the finale.

ROOK: We need the stage clear for repairs.

MIRI: Can we sing while you work?

ROOK: Not under the loose beam.

Pella points to a handwritten sign: "GRAND FINALE."

PELLA: The song will bring the house down.

ROOK: That is what I am trying to prevent.

SCENE 2. INT. HOLLOW, LATER

The chorus rehearses. Rook marks the floor with chalk.

TAVI: The dance line crosses the repair area.

PELLA: We can move the line.

MIRI: The song says "meet me by the wall."

Rook points to the wall with the loose beam.

ROOK: Not that wall.

The chorus begins to sing a new verse about moving to the other side. Pella stops them.

PELLA: That verse lacks emotional lift.

ROOK: It has safety.

SCENE 3. EXT. HOLLOW ENTRANCE, AFTERNOON

The audience waits outside. Tavi distributes programs. Pella moves the stage curtain.

Pella: We need one more rehearsal.

Rook: The roof crew is here.

A beam creaks. Everyone freezes.

Miri begins a soft note. The chorus follows, harmonizing while carrying chairs out of the hollow.

PELLA: That is not the finale.

TAVI: It is the moving chorus.

The audience helps carry chairs. The room clears.

SCENE 4. INT. HOLLOW, CONTINUOUS

Rook examines the beam. It is held by an old knot.

ROOK: We need to remove this support carefully.

Pella wants to sing over the work. Rook asks for silence.

MIRI: We can count the beats for you.

They tap a steady rhythm as Rook lowers the beam. The chorus sings the count. The beam reaches the floor without falling.

ROOK: The support is down.

PELLA: That was the strongest note.

SCENE 4A. EXT. COMMUNITY HOLLOW, SUNSET

The audience begins to gather outside. Pella rehearses the final verse with Miri and Tavi. Rook checks the repaired beam.

PELLA: When I raise my hand, we begin the chorus.

ROOK: When I lower my hand, everyone leaves the stage.

TAVI: That is not a musical cue.

ROOK: It is a safe exit.

Pella raises her hand. The chorus sings. Rook lowers his hand to signal that the beam is secure. The cast exits the stage.

PELLA: You ended the song.

ROOK: I cleared the floor.

MIRI: The verse can continue outside.

Pella listens to the song echo in the yard. She moves the final chorus to the courtyard.

PELLA: The show will start here.

Rook carries the last chair outdoors. A small monkey takes the stage bell and rings it once.

TAVI: That is the cue.

SCENE 4B. EXT. COURTYARD, CONTINUOUS

Pella tests the new position. The audience can see the stage from the courtyard.

PELLA: We will sing the finale here.

Rook sets the repaired beam behind the wall and checks it once more.

ROOK: The hall is ready for the crew.

The chorus forms a line. The small monkey sits near the stage bell.

TAVI: Will the bell ring?

Pella hands the bell to Rook.

PELLA: Only when the room is safe.

Rook rings it. The chorus begins, and the audience moves closer.

SCENE 5. INT. HOLLOW, SUNSET

The troupe performs outside, with the hollow behind them. The final song is about clearing space and rebuilding.

PELLA: Now we reach the grand finale.

The chorus sings, but the audience begins helping the roof crew carry materials.

TAVI: They have joined the number.

MIRI: The final verse is now a work song.

Rook carries a plank past the stage. Pella changes the lyric to fit.

PELLA: "Lift together, set it straight."

ROOK: That line is useful.

The crowd finishes the song while stacking boards.

SCENE 6. EXT. HOLLOW, NIGHT

The repairs are complete. The troupe gathers inside. Pella places a sign on the new beam: "STAGE THIS WAY."

PELLA: The show will go on.

ROOK: The beam will stay up.

Miri strikes a final chord on a small drum. A loose chair squeaks in rhythm.

TAVI: The chair has joined the orchestra.

Pella conducts the squeak. Rook lifts the chair to tighten its leg. The squeak stops.

PELLA: The orchestra has lost a member.

ROOK: It has found a repair.
