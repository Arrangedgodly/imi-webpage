# The Airship With a Paper Map

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sky-pirate adventures where a fragile map receives more attention than navigation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
CAPTAIN MARA, a macaque
CARTOGRAPHER VEX, an orangutan
PILOT PIP, a capuchin
ENGINEER ODO, a gibbon
MESSENGER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - DAY

A small paper map lies beneath a glass weight. The airship hums steadily. Vex points at a dotted route.

VEX: This map leads to the floating citadel.

MARA: It leads to the north landing. The citadel is a weather station.

PIP: We are above the valley, heading north at a safe speed.

ODO: Fuel and lift readings are stable. The paper is not a flight instrument.

VEX: Every great voyage begins with a secret map.

NARI: This one came from the station office. The landing is marked in ink.

A gust makes the map corner lift. Vex grabs it with both hands. The glass weight rolls toward the table edge; Mara catches it before it falls.

MARA: Tie the map to the board. Do not hold it over the control panel.

VEX: The map was trying to escape.

PIP: The map is paper. The ship is moving.

SCENE 2. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - CONTINUOUS

Odo clips the map to a board. Pip keeps one hand on the control wheel. Vex insists that the dotted line represents a hidden passage through a cloud bank.

VEX: We must follow the trail exactly.

MARA: We follow the marked bearing and the pilot's instruments. The map shows a valley, not a tunnel.

NARI: The station radio says cloud cover is thinning on the eastern side.

VEX: That is where the map has no ink. A void.

ODO: It is a blank margin.

Vex stretches the paper toward the window. Pip asks him to keep it clear of the compass. The map brushes the dial and spins its needle.

PIP: Please move the map. I need a stable heading.

VEX: The compass has reacted to its secrets.

MARA: The paper covered the compass. We can use the duplicate route card, which is clipped beside the radio.

Vex looks offended that there is a duplicate. Nari points to the station stamp on both copies.

SCENE 3. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - LATER

A narrow strip of cloud passes the windows. Pip follows the compass while Mara compares the route card with the valley landmarks. Vex kneels over the paper map, trying to hold it flat with his elbows.

VEX: The mountain should be shaped like a crown.

PIP: It is the mountain on our left. We are on course.

ODO: Lift remains steady. I can hear no change in the engine.

NARI, over radio: North landing is clear. Winds are mild.

VEX: The map's blank margin predicts danger.

MARA: The map predates the new landing tower. It cannot predict weather.

A small tear forms where Vex presses the fold. Mara places a repair strip over it, but only after Odo tells her to lower the map onto the table. The instruments remain unobstructed.

ODO: Paper repaired. No need to turn the ship.

VEX: We must protect the artifact.

PIP: We can land, then put it in an envelope.

SCENE 3A. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - LATER

The cloud clears. Through the window, Pip spots the north landing tower. Vex tries to overlay the paper map against the real horizon, turning it upside down.

PIP: The tower is ahead and to the left. I am lining up for the marked approach.

VEX: The map's mountain is below the fold.

MARA: Turn the map the way the north arrow points. Keep your hands away from the compass.

Vex rotates the paper. The route now matches the view. He looks disappointed that the mystery has become legible.

VEX: The map has betrayed its secrets.

ODO: It has a printed north arrow.

NARI: The station has confirmed the landing is still clear. No detour is needed.

Vex insists on announcing the map's revelation to the crew. Mara asks him to hold the padded folder open instead. He does, solemnly, while Odo checks the lift gauge and Pip maintains the approach.

VEX: I guard the knowledge.

MARA: You are holding an envelope.

SCENE 4. EXT. NORTH LANDING PLATFORM - DAY

The airship descends under Pip's control. Mara gives the landing signal. Odo checks the lift gauge and confirms the platform is level. Nari waits by the mooring post with a padded folder.

VEX: The floating citadel is reached.

NARI: This is the north weather station.

MARA: And the route is complete. Secure the lines first, then the map.

Pip eases the ship onto the platform. Odo shuts down the engine after the mooring lines are fastened. Mara places the folded map in the padded folder; no one carries it into the controls.

VEX: The blank margin concealed an entire landing.

PIP: The landing is printed on the other side.

TAG: Vex turns the map over and finds a printed legend. He adds "secret citadel" beside the weather station, in pencil.
