# The Map Under the Mosaic

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: treasure-hunt adventures, cryptic clues, rival expeditions, and ancient riddles that point to a community resource*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
NELA, a ring-tailed lemur archaeologist
BEX, a capuchin guide
VARN, a macaque treasure hunter
MAYOR TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. OLD TRAIN STATION - DAY

Nela lifts a mosaic tile from a station wall. Beneath it is a brass map etched with a sun, a bell, and a tree.

NELA: The treasure map.

BEX: It's a map of the town square.

Varn enters with two assistants and a velvet bag. He wants the map for his private collection. The Mayor says the mosaic belongs to the town, but the group follows the clues before the station closes for repair.

SCENE 2. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - AFTERNOON

The first clue reads: "Where noon casts no shadow, the second mark will shine." Varn searches beneath the clocktower. Nela notices the clock's shadow is blocked by the market awning. They lift the awning and find a small brass sun in the paving stones.

The second mark points to the bell tower. Varn climbs the ladder, ignores Bex's warning, and rings the bell. A flock of pigeons rises. One drops a seed on Varn's hat. Nela finds the next clue beneath the bell's counterweight.

SCENE 2A. EXT. MARKET SQUARE - LATER

The brass sun fits into a shallow slot in the paving. When Nela turns it, a small mirror catches sunlight and points toward the old bell tower. Varn insists the reflection marks a hidden vault. Bex notices it points to the tower's maintenance ladder.

They climb. Varn tries to race ahead and knocks a dust pan loose. It slides down the stairs, striking every step like a descending drumroll. The market vendors look up, expecting an announcement.

NELA: The town used sound to find the next clue.

BEX: Or the bell tower has stairs that need sweeping.

At the top, the bell's counterweight hides a second plate. The plate is not locked; it is wedged behind a wooden bracket. Varn strains to pull it free while Nela and Bex lift the weight safely and slide the plate out.

SCENE 3. INT. BELL TOWER - CONTINUOUS

The clue says: "The tree remembers what the river forgot." Bex recognizes it as the old willow beside the dry canal. Varn races ahead. Nela and Bex follow more slowly, checking the map's scale against the streets.

At the willow, they discover an iron box buried among its roots. Varn opens it with a crowbar. Inside are seed packets, handwritten planting notes, and a list of shade trees planted decades ago.

VARN: There is no gold.

NELA: These seeds will restore the riverbank orchard.

BEX: The map was never a route to treasure. It was the planting plan.

SCENE 4. EXT. OLD CANAL - EVENING

Varn accuses Nela of changing the meaning of the clues. The Mayor arrives and confirms the town lost its orchard after the canal dried. The seeds came from the first trees and were stored for replanting when the water returned.

VARN: I risked a climb for seeds.

NELA: You rang a public bell and found a public map.

BEX: The tree remembers. That was the clue.

Varn's assistants begin to leave. Varn looks at the empty canal, then at the seed packets. He chooses one and asks where to plant it.

SCENE 4A. INT. TOWN HALL - NIGHT

Nela compares the planting notes with the map. Each seed packet is marked for a different stretch of the bank. Varn wants to take the brass map to a collector who offered money for it.

NELA: The map is how the town will know where each tree belongs.

VARN: A collector could preserve it.

MAYOR TAVI: We can preserve a copy and keep the original where the planting happens.

Bex traces the sun and bell symbols. The map doubles as a calendar: the bell marks the first market day of the planting season; the sun indicates which side of the canal receives shade first.

VARN: I thought the symbols led to gold.

NELA: They led to a place that can grow something valuable.

Varn agrees to help mount the map where residents can see it. The Mayor chooses a frame that protects it from rain but leaves the seed notes accessible.

The town gathers to plant. The Mayor reads the old notes. Nela marks spacing. Bex makes a small irrigation channel. Varn digs a hole too deep, then fills it back to the right level. The first seedling is placed beside the canal.

NELA: The treasure is not buried anymore.

MAYOR TAVI: It will take years to grow.

VARN: Then we have years to check it.

The townspeople plant along the bank. Varn gives the brass map to the Mayor, who mounts it beside the mosaic where residents can read it.

FINAL IMAGE: The sunrise reflects in the brass map. The new sapling casts a thin shadow across the first etched tree, pointing toward the next planting site.
