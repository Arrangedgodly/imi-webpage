# The Mask in the Main Event

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: professional wrestling movies, rival personas, choreographed rivalries, dramatic mask reveals, and a finale decided by a missed cue*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
TALA, a capuchin wrestler known as Comet
BRUNO, a gibbon champion called the Mountain
MEL, a macaque ring announcer
COACH RIMA, a ring-tailed lemur trainer

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY ARENA - AFTERNOON

A hand-painted banner reads "THE MAIN EVENT: COMET VS. THE MOUNTAIN." Tala tightens the silver mask she wears as Comet. Bruno, a gibbon, warms up by lifting the ring steps. Coach Rima checks the cue cards.

RIMA: The match is a performance. You both know the signals. It ends only after a clean count to three. If either wrestler stops before the count, the referee calls a draw.

TALA: I know my entrance. I don't know where the ramp ends.

BRUNO: The ramp ends at the ring.

TALA: It has two sides.

Mel enters with a microphone and announces the match to an empty room. The echo returns "THE MOUNTAIN" twice. Bruno bows to the sound.

SCENE 2. INT. LOCKER ROOM - LATER

Tala discovers that her mask's elastic has snapped. Coach Rima offers a plain spare from the prop bin. It is too large, so Tala ties it with a ribbon. Bruno sees her wearing it and thinks she has adopted his persona.

BRUNO: You can't be the Mountain.

TALA: I don't want to be. I want my mask to stay on.

MEL: The audience has already bought tickets to Comet versus the Mountain.

RIMA: Then the match is still accurate.

Mel practices the introductions, but his cue cards are stuck together. He announces the winner before the match. Rima takes the cards away and gives him the roster upside down.

SCENE 3. INT. ARENA - EVENING

The crowd fills the seats. Comet enters to a burst of silver paper. The Mountain enters carrying the ring steps, then sets them down gently when the referee points to the ring. The two circle each other, each waiting for the opening cue.

BRUNO: You go first.

TALA: You're the Mountain.

BRUNO: I can wait.

The referee taps the mat. Tala ducks beneath a rope. Bruno steps over it. The crowd cheers at their careful entrances. They begin a choreographed series of feints and safe holds. Each pretends to be surprised by the other's obvious move, and the audience responds loudly.

SCENE 4. INT. RING - CONTINUOUS

Mel announces a sudden costume reveal. Coach Rima signals for the masks to come off at the final bell, not before. Mel reads the wrong card and announces the reveal early. Bruno's own mask slips over his eyes. He can't see the ropes.

TALA: Hold still.

She guides Bruno away from the ropes while the referee steadies his mask. The crowd thinks this is a dramatic save. Mel begins narrating a feud between the masks.

MEL: One face cannot contain two legends!

RIMA: It is one face and one spare mask.

SCENE 5. INT. RING - MAIN EVENT

The final sequence begins. Bruno signals the planned finish: he will fall to one knee, Comet will leap over him, and the referee will count to three. The arena's sound system cuts out. Mel's microphone goes silent just before the planned finish. Bruno drops to one knee; Comet starts the safe pin, and the referee counts one, then two. Tala sees the referee's hand pause; without the agreed third tap from the bell operator, she releases Bruno. He stands, then both give a small bow to show they have stopped the match. The referee signals a draw because neither wrestler completed the three-count.

BRUNO: I believe we have tied.

TALA: The crowd counted.

BRUNO: They counted to three.

SCENE 6. INT. ARENA - AFTER THE MATCH

The masks come off. The crowd applauds both wrestlers. Mel announces the draw, then accidentally announces tomorrow's bake sale as the new champion. Coach Rima gives Bruno the spare ribbon and helps Tala repair her mask's elastic with a stitched loop. They agree to wrestle as partners in the next show.

MEL: The Mountain and Comet, united!

RIMA: Please check the card before announcing the bake sale.

SCENE 7. INT. EMPTY ARENA - NEXT AFTERNOON

Tala and Bruno rehearse their partnership match for an empty room. They agree to let the crowd call the finish, but Mel insists on testing the microphone first. He announces the names correctly, then reads the bake sale card by mistake.

MEL: Tomorrow's champion is a lemon cake.

RIMA: At least this time it is on the right day.

Tala and Bruno practice the safe count together. The referee taps once, twice, then stops to ask whether the draw has ended. Coach Rima points to the bell, and the rehearsal finishes when it rings.

FINAL IMAGE: In the empty arena, the two wrestlers raise the ring steps together while Mel tests the microphone against a single empty seat.
