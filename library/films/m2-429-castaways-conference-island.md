# The Castaways of Conference Island

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: stranded-survival adventures that turn a planned retreat into a test of primitive ingenuity despite abundant modern supplies*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A ferry delay strands a group of monkeys at a conference lodge, where they attempt to build a wilderness camp beside a fully stocked pantry.

Cast: Pika, a macaque event planner; Ulu, a capuchin engineer; Saba, a lemur speaker; Ro, a gibbon cook; ferry captain Venn, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - EXT. CONFERENCE ISLAND DOCK - DAY

Rain blows sideways. The ferry captain ties the boat to the dock and posts a notice: RETURN SAILING DELAYED UNTIL WIND DROPS. Pika reads it, holding a schedule binder under her jacket.

PIKA: We have a four-hour delay. The lodge is thirty steps away.

SABA: The survival exercise is tomorrow. We could rehearse.

ULU: There are rooms, blankets, a kitchen and a phone.

SABA: But the program says "authentic island ordeal."

Pika opens the binder. The lodge door swings outward and reveals a lit dining room.

SCENE 2 - INT. LODGE GREAT ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Ro puts kettle water on. Ulu checks the ferry update on a wall phone. Saba stares at a display of canned soup.

SABA: The wild may test us.

RO: The soup is tomato. The pantry has crackers.

PIKA: We'll make an emergency camp before the keynote. Use the brochure as a map.

Saba spreads a brochure across the carpet. It shows a trail loop and a drawing of the lodge. Pika interprets the dotted line as a river. Ulu points to the legend: it marks the accessible path.

ULU: It leads to the west porch, which is where we already are.

SABA: The wilderness has repeated our steps.

RO: That is because you are standing in the dining room.

Before going outside, Saba draws a route on the lodge welcome map. The map already marks the nearest restrooms, dining hall and emergency phone. She circles the emergency phone twice.

SABA: We need a signal fire.

ULU: The fire alarm is connected to the lodge system. Do not light anything.

PIKA: The emergency phone connects to the mainland office. The ferry captain is on channel four.

SABA: We can signal him with a flag.

RO: We can also walk to the dock and speak to him.

Pika folds the map to hide the conference itinerary and sets it down. Ulu picks it up and turns it right side up.

ULU: The lodge has already planned a safe route to the dock. It is this dotted line.

SCENE 3 - EXT. WEST PORCH - AFTERNOON

Saba and Pika try to build a lean-to from conference banners. Ulu brings out four folding chairs and a tarp. Wind flips the banner over, revealing the sponsor logo. Pika anchors it with a stack of conference tote bags.

PIKA: We have shelter.

SABA: The island provides.

ULU: The conference printer made the banner. We brought the chairs.

The wind lifts the tarp. Ro catches it and ties it to the porch rail. The knot slips. He knots it again, using a loop around the chair frame.

RO: It will hold until the wind changes.

Pika checks the ferry notice. A new update says sailing remains delayed, but the lodge is open for guests.

SCENE 4 - INT. LODGE KITCHEN - LATER

The group enters for food. Saba insists they forage. Ro opens a refrigerator stocked with fruit, vegetables and leftovers from lunch.

SABA: Foraging would make the story stronger.

RO: The refrigerator has labels. That is safer and faster.

Pika reaches for a jar marked WILD BERRIES. Ro catches her wrist.

RO: That label means a recipe title. They are cranberries from the catering order.

ULU: The kitchen has a shelf of snacks for delayed travelers. We should ask before eating them.

Ro finds a note from the lodge manager authorizing guests to help themselves. Saba reads it aloud with solemn grandeur.

SABA: The island has granted us crackers.

PIKA: The lodge manager has.

SCENE 5 - EXT. DOCK - EVENING

The wind drops. The ferry captain signals that boarding will begin in ten minutes. The group folds the tarp and stacks the chairs. Saba carries the conference banner. Pika returns the schedule binder to her bag.

SABA: We survived the ordeal.

ULU: We waited in a warm building and ate dinner.

RO: We also found out the banner makes a decent windbreak.

Venn helps them board. The ferry leaves the dock. Through the window, they see the island lodge lights and the neatly folded camp.

PIKA: Tomorrow's survival session should include reading the ferry notices.

SABA: That sounds practical.

ULU: We can still make it dramatic. The weather changed at the last minute.

SABA: A sudden gust. A false trail. A perilous snack shelf.

RO: The tomato soup was genuinely hot.

FINAL IMAGE: The ferry pulls away as the conference banner, folded around the chairs, displays its sponsor logo like a flag of surrender.
