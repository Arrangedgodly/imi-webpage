# The Kitchen That Kept Changing Doors

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: slapstick chase comedies, impossible house layouts, doors that open into the wrong room, and escalating food fights*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
CHEF TAVI, a capuchin
MARA, a macaque kitchen manager
FENN, a lemur delivery runner
PIP, a gibbon architect
BIX, an orangutan food critic

SCENE 1. INT. RESTAURANT KITCHEN - NIGHT

Chef Tavi prepares dinner for the city's food critic. The kitchen's new swinging doors have been installed backward. Each time someone pushes through, they return to the same counter. Fenn carries in a basket of pears and bumps into Mara, who is carrying three soup pots.

MARA: The service door is over there.

FENN: I went over there.

The doors swing open again, revealing the same kitchen. Pip studies the blueprint and realizes the contractor mirrored the plan. The back hallway now loops through the pantry.

SCENE 2. INT. PANTRY - CONTINUOUS

Fenn enters the pantry to fetch flour. He opens one door and emerges behind the stove. He goes back in, tries another door, and appears in the dining room wearing a flour sack as a hat. Mara asks him to stop using the doors until Pip can map the loop.

Bix arrives early to inspect the kitchen. He gets trapped between the swinging doors and is carried back and forth like a pendulum. Tavi tries to finish the sauce while calling directions. A spoon of sauce flies through the hatch and lands on Bix's tasting card.

BIX: I haven't tasted it yet.

TAVI: It has made the first impression.

SCENE 3. INT. KITCHEN CORRIDOR - LATER

Mara tapes arrows on the floor and asks every worker to walk the route once before the critic arrives. Fenn begins at the back door and ends up in the pantry. He follows the blue arrow and appears beside the stove. Tavi walks behind him carrying the soup pot; the door swings shut between them, so he passes the pot through a serving hatch. The kitchen staff form a chain and move dinner down the line.

TAVI: I can see the dining room from here.

MARA: That is the freezer door.

Pip checks the hinges. The blueprint has been printed in reverse because someone held it against a window while tracing. The door labels were based on that copy. He marks the real direction with chalk and asks everyone to wait while the hinges are moved. A timer rings from the oven. The sauce is ready, but the chef cannot reach it from the looping corridor. Mara guides Tavi through the correct door by tapping the wall twice at each turn.

SCENE 3A. INT. BASEMENT LANDING - NIGHT

Mara and Fenn reach the basement landing through the service stairs. The bread basket is stopped inside the dumbwaiter. Pip holds the brake cord while Mara opens the lower gate; Fenn lifts the basket out without tilting it.

PIP: The stop lever was for the fan.

MARA: The brake cord is for the lift.

PIP: The labels were reversed.

Pip marks the correct controls with chalk. Mara keeps the serving hatch open while Fenn carries the bread upstairs. The crew does not send the basket through the doors again.

SCENE 4. INT. RESTAURANT KITCHEN - NIGHT

The bread basket is back upstairs. Pip finishes reversing the swinging doors and tests each one while Mara stands on the kitchen side and Fenn waits in the hall. The doors now open toward the kitchen, not into the pantry loop. Bix tastes the soup and bread. The bread is slightly warm from its trip through the basement. Tavi calls it a subterranean course. Bix says it is good, though he recommends delivering the next basket by hand.

Bix says the soup is excellent. Mara says the basement delivery was not intentional, but at least the bread stayed clean.

Tavi sends a plate of pears to the dining room through the corrected service door. Fenn opens the same door from the hall and sees Mara waiting on the kitchen side. No one loops back to the stove. Bix asks whether the kitchen has returned to its original layout.

PIP: The building never moved.

MARA: We changed the doors.

Bix signs the service log. The critic's chair fits through the doorway without turning sideways. Tavi carries the soup pot through after him, and the handles clear the frame by a hand's width.

SCENE 5. INT. RESTAURANT KITCHEN - CLOSING

The crew serves the rest of dinner without chasing through the walls. Pip labels the doors with arrows. Fenn delivers the final basket through the correct entrance. Bix writes his review: the food is good, the kitchen is confusing, and the staff never gave up on the bread.

Tavi celebrates by tossing one pear to Mara. It bounces off the corrected door and lands in the fruit bowl. The door swings shut and stays shut.

FINAL IMAGE: A little strip of colored tape peels off the pantry door and sticks to the pear, marking the fruit's route through the kitchen.
