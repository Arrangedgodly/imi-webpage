# The Moonlight Menu

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic comedies with rival vendors, repeated meet-cutes, and a grand gesture built around an event deadline*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: LENA, a macaque chef; SOL, a gibbon baker; MAGS, an orangutan night-market organizer; RUE, a baboon rival vendor; FINN, a capuchin courier.

EXT. RIVERFRONT NIGHT MARKET - EVENING

Lanterns hang over a row of food carts. Lena wheels in a soup cart. Sol arrives with a bread cart. They both try to park in the same square spot.

LENA: I booked this space.

SOL: I booked the square.

MAGS: You booked two different squares. The chalk line was moved.

They look down. One wheel sits over a chalk mark. Their carts are nose to nose.

SOL: I can move.

LENA: I have a hot pot.

SOL: I have warm bread.

The two carts roll slowly toward each other on the sloped pavement. Finn catches Lena's cart. Sol catches his own. The soup ladle falls and lands inside a basket of bread.

LENA: Your bread has a garnish.

SOL: Your ladle is now part of the menu.

Rue pulls up a bright cart with a sign: MOONLIGHT SPECIAL. A line forms immediately.

RUE: Free samples until the moon rises.

MAGS: The permit says one sample per guest.

RUE: The moon has not risen yet.

ACT ONE

INT. NIGHT-MARKET STORAGE TENT - LATER

Lena and Sol try to retrieve the ladle from the bread basket. The basket has been placed in the storage tent, behind a row of folded chairs.

SOL: I can reach it.

LENA: Your arm is trapped under the tent rope.

Sol tries to lift the rope. The tent wall shifts, and Mags calls from outside.

MAGS: Do not move the wall. The lantern wire is attached.

They hold still. Lena slides a serving spoon under the basket and nudges the ladle toward Sol. Sol gets it and passes it back.

SOL: We have completed a rescue.

LENA: The ladle is still wet.

They laugh. The tent door opens, and Mags hands them a new vendor map. The map puts them in separate spots at opposite ends of the market.

MAGS: Your carts cannot share a square. The fire lane runs between them.

Lena and Sol take their places on opposite sides of the painted lane. A crowd gathers between them. Lena calls out a soup order, and three customers turn toward Sol. Sol answers with a bread order, and a line forms at Lena's pot.

LENA: They are following the loudest voice.

SOL: I can call more loudly.

MAGS: This is a market, not a stadium.

Finn brings two order tickets and clips one to each cart. Lena writes SOUP on hers. Sol writes BREAD on his. They exchange tickets across the table without moving the carts.

FINN: The system works if customers can read.

[One guest holds both tickets upside down. Lena and Sol point the guest to the proper sides of the lane, then serve the order in turn.]

LENA: It is a lot of hand signals for dinner.

SOL: It is a very good dinner.

LENA: We do not want to share a square.

SOL: We want to share the ladle.

They look at each other, then stop.

LENA: I mean, borrow the ladle.

SOL: Of course.

EXT. NIGHT MARKET - NIGHT

Rue's line has grown long. A lantern above the special cart flickers. Finn notices a loose battery panel and tells Rue. Rue says the flicker is part of the moonlight effect.

A small spark pops. Mags shuts off the lantern string. The market goes dark for a moment. Guests raise phone lights. Lena's soup pot stays warm; Sol's bread remains covered.

RUE: My special was meant to glow.

MAGS: It is meant to be inspected.

Mags checks the panel. The wire is loose, not dangerous, but the lantern needs a new clip. Rue turns off the cart's battery.

ACT TWO

Lena sets a pot of soup under one table lamp. Sol brings bread to the same table. Mags checks the fire lane and places them on opposite sides of the marked line. Finn passes bowls from one side to the other, careful not to cross the lane with either cart.

LENA: You have the bread.

SOL: You have the soup.

LENA: We can share at this table.

Sol dips a piece of bread. The market lights come back on, one strand at a time. The moon rises over the river. Their two signs, SOUP and BREAD, hang side by side.

Rue packs away the dark lantern and asks for a bowl. Lena hands one over. Mags approves the shared table but keeps the fire lane clear.

SOL: Would you like to meet after closing?

LENA: If you bring bread.

SOL: If you bring soup.

They smile. A gust turns both signs backward. The crowd reads the blank backs and asks what is being sold.

MAGS: Please turn your signs around.

They do.

FINAL IMAGE: Lena and Sol close their carts on opposite sides of the chalk line, sharing the last bowl beneath the moon.
