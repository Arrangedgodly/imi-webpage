# The Lagoon Has a Rubber Shark

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: creature features that build suspense around an unseen predator while the prop department struggles to keep its rubber fin afloat*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A beach-town director promises a terrifying shark attack for a tourism film, but the rubber fin keeps drifting into the frame before the swimmers are ready.

Cast: director Kalo, a macaque; lifeguard Nima, an orangutan; effects swimmer Fenn, a gibbon; camera operator Turi, a lemur; producer Sela, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. BEACH LAGOON - MORNING

A yellow buoy floats near the shore. A rubber shark fin is tied to a fishing line. Kalo studies the camera monitor. Sela watches the budget clock.

KALO: We reveal the fin only at the end.

FENN: It is already visible behind your foot.

Kalo steps to the left. The fin drifts into frame on the right. Nima asks the swimmers to remain in the shallow area while the line is tested. Turi rolls the camera; the fin bumps the buoy and flips upside down.

SCENE 2 - EXT. LAGOON - LATER

Fenn swims toward the marker. The fin circles him, pulled by the line. Kalo asks for a frightened reaction. Fenn sees the line wrapped around a floating ring and waves to stop the shot. Sela thinks it is a dramatic distress signal and tells Turi to zoom in.

NIMA: Cut. Fenn is tangled in the prop line.

Nima wades in and unhooks it. The rubber fin springs free and sails toward the camera. Turi ducks. The fin lands on his shoulder and slowly slides down his back.

SCENE 3 - EXT. BEACH CABANA - CONTINUOUS

Kalo reviews the footage. The fin appears in every shot, sometimes upright, sometimes upside down. Sela says they can edit around it. Turi points out that the beach banner is visible too, advertising the town's annual sandcastle contest.

KALO: We are filming a predator, not a contest.

NIMA: The fin has spent more time attacking the camera than the swimmers.

Fenn suggests anchoring the prop with a weighted line. Kalo rejects it because the fin should seem to move on its own. Nima ties it to a buoy anyway, keeping the weight submerged. The fin now circles at a consistent distance, like a dog following its leash.

The producer asks for a second attack from the deeper water. Nima refuses to let Fenn swim where the bottom drops away. Kalo moves the camera closer to shore and frames the floating fin against the horizon. A small wave turns the fin sideways. Turi calls for a cut. Sela tells the crew to make the best of the take, but the sound recording catches her saying that the shark has a loose seam. Kalo tries a retake with the seam facing away. The fin slowly rotates back toward the camera.

FENN: It knows its mark.

KALO: It knows the current.

Sela adjusts the buoy line. The fin travels in a tight circle, then bumps the camera tripod. Nima catches the tripod before it tips. The shot remains steady; the rubber fin fills the entire frame, looming like a monster above the beach.

SCENE 4 - EXT. LAGOON - AFTERNOON

They film a final sequence. Fenn swims toward shore, notices the fin and runs from the water. The rubber shark bumps the shoreline and stops against a child's bucket. The child picks it up and asks whose toy it is. Kalo signals for a close-up. Turi films the fin beside the bucket, making the prop look enormous in the low angle.

Sela watches the monitor. The shot is unexpectedly convincing. Nima keeps the swimming area clear and tells everyone to hold position while they get the last take. Fenn gives a frightened look, then steps onto the sand before the line pulls the fin after him.

Kalo asks for one last beach shot. Nima places a bright orange float beyond the fin, marking the shallow limit. Fenn walks the buoy line toward shore while Turi films. The rubber shark follows the line, reaches the sand and rests against the float. Kalo calls it a final attack. Sela stops the camera before the prop slides into the children's play area. She asks them to reset only after everyone is safely behind the marker.

SCENE 5 - INT. COMMUNITY SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The town watches the short film. The audience gasps at the low-angle fin. Then the child lifts it from the bucket and waves. The crowd laughs. Kalo lets the shot run, and the tourism film ends with a title card inviting visitors to the sandcastle contest.

Sela whispers that the sponsor likes the ending. Nima hands the rubber fin back to the prop box and checks that the line is untangled.

FINAL IMAGE: The fin rests beside a sandcastle trophy, while the film's title card reads VISIT THE LAGOON - THE SHARK IS OFF DUTY.
