# The Elevator to the Moon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prestige space-exploration films and their devotion to emergency countdowns*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
CAPTAIN NARI, an orangutan
ENGINEER PIP, a capuchin
SCIENTIST MARA, a macaque
MISSION DIRECTOR VEX, an orangutan
MAINTENANCE ROBOT ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. LUNAR LIFT - NIGHT

A steel elevator climbs the four floors of a tower. A mural of a cratered moon is painted across the roof access wall above the upper landing. Nari grips the rail; Pip watches the floor indicator.

NARI: Mission Control says the lunar platform is ready.

VEX, over speaker: It is ready for your emotional arrival. Please avoid saying "roof" on the broadcast.

PIP: The indicator has stopped at four. That's the service floor, one level below the landing.

MARA: The lift has stopped smoothly. No power loss, no pressure alarm.

VEX: A countdown is appearing.

The display shows 09:59. A red label above it reads LIGHT CUE. A second label, OXYGEN, has been stuck over the first.

PIP: That's the ten-minute lighting timer. The labels were switched.

NARI: Mission Control, confirm we still have air.

VEX: You have air, a timer, and a dramatic situation.

ODO rolls to the panel. A small compartment contains three keys. He displays the key with a crescent mark.

ODO: Service key located. Lift is secured at floor four.

PIP: I will unlock the service door and check the landing before we step out.

SCENE 2. INT. SERVICE FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Pip turns the marked key. The door opens a few inches. Beyond it, a lit landing and a sign point to the roof stairs. Pip looks both ways, then opens the door fully.

PIP: Landing is clear. No loose tools or carts.

Vex enters from the stairwell, carrying the mission flag. He has come down from the control room, which is on the same service floor.

VEX: I joined you at the lunar airlock.

MARA: You joined us at the service landing. This is the safe place to meet.

NARI: We will climb together. Odo, remain with the lift and mark it out of service until the inspection.

ODO: Lift tagged for inspection. The service key stays with Pip.

Pip hangs the tag on the control panel and puts the key in a labeled pouch. No one re-enters the elevator.

SCENE 3. INT. STAIRWELL - MOMENTS LATER

The group climbs one flight. Vex holds the flag against his side so it cannot catch on the rail. At the landing, Mara checks through the small window before opening the roof door.

MARA: The roof is clear. The access path is dry; a wet-paint sign stands beside the far border of the mural.

NARI: We can proceed to the viewing area.

VEX: The ascent has begun.

PIP: The stairs began one floor below. We are now at the roof access.

SCENE 3A. INT. STAIRWELL LANDING - MOMENTS LATER

The group reaches the roof landing together. Vex carries the flag rolled around its pole; Pip keeps the service key in the marked pouch.

PIP: We are at the top landing. The lift remains on floor four with the inspection tag.

VEX: The mission flag has made the ascent without snagging.

MARA: Keep it rolled until we reach the viewing area. The door is narrow.

NARI: I can see the painted mural through the window. We will stay on the dry path and leave the border alone.

PIP: I'll check the door swing, then hold it open while everyone steps through.

Vex waits until Mara and Nari clear the doorway, then follows Pip through with the flag. Nobody returns to the service floor or reboards the lift.

VEX: One continuous ascent from service landing to roof.

MARA: One flight of stairs. That is the route.

SCENE 4. EXT. ROOFTOP VIEWING AREA - NIGHT

The crew steps onto the roof. A large painted moon mural covers the wall. There is no suspended moon or loose prop. The lighting timer reaches zero; a spotlight comes on and reveals the mural's painted craters.

VEX: A historic first sighting.

MARA: It's a wall painting. Please keep the flag away from the wet border.

Vex lowers the flag. Nari stands beside the mural while Pip photographs the completed lighting cue.

NARI: Mission Control, log the stop as a service stop, the elevator as tagged, and the roof mural as illuminated.

VEX: Can the report say "unexpected lunar surface"?

PIP: The report can say, "Lift stopped at floor four; crew used stairs; no injuries; inspection requested."

ODO, over radio: Confirmed. The service key is back in its pouch, and the lift remains out of use.

MARA: We have a safe route, a working light cue, and no reason to dramatize the equipment fault.

VEX: Our expedition ends beneath the moon.

NARI: Beneath a painted moon mural, on the roof.

TAG: Vex plants the mission flag beside the stairwell door. The sign above it reads: ROOF ACCESS BY STAIRS.
