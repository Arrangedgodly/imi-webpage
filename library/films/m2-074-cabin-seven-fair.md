# Cabin Seven Above the Fair

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: amusement-park disaster films, countdown rescues, and command-center spectacle*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A gondola stalls above a county fair during a dramatic storm warning, and the rescue crew discovers the riders are waiting comfortably while the announcer keeps escalating the emergency.

Cast: Tala, a macaque ride mechanic; Jun, a capuchin operator; Mayor Plink, a squirrel; Vera, a lemur weather reader; and two calm passengers in Cabin Seven.

SCENE 1. EXT. FAIRGROUNDS, AFTERNOON

The Ferris wheel stops with Cabin Seven near the top. Mayor Plink grabs a megaphone and declares a crisis. The two passengers wave from the cabin. Vera checks the weather radar; the storm is twenty miles away.

PLINK: We have six minutes before disaster.

VERA: The storm is an hour away.

TALA: The wheel's brake engaged. I'm checking the motor.

Jun offers to bring a ladder. Tala says to keep the area clear and call maintenance.

SCENE 2. INT. RIDE CONTROL BOOTH, CONTINUOUS

The control panel flashes SERVICE STOP. Plink calls it a countdown. Jun explains that the ride stops automatically when a sensor detects too much sway. The wind has moved a paper pennant. Plink asks if the pennant is an agent of the storm. Vera folds it and puts it away.

Tala calls the passengers by radio. They ask whether the snack stand is still open. Jun says yes. Plink tells them to conserve energy. They say they are sitting down.

Jun switches to the public-address channel so the ground crew can hear him. Plink grabs the microphone first and whispers as if volume creates urgency. He tells Cabin Seven to remain calm and avoid sudden movements. One passenger carefully lifts a napkin from a paper bag.

PASSENGER: Does that count as sudden?

TALA: Keep doing what you are doing. Jun, tell the crew the brake is holding.

Plink studies a laminated emergency chart. It has three colored arrows and a smiling stick figure. He turns it upside down, then announces that the red arrow indicates a vertical evacuation. Tala rotates the chart back and reads the label: RED IS FOR FIRE. There is no fire. Plink asks if they should create one for the arrows to feel useful. Vera takes away the megaphone.

Outside, fair workers begin moving the line away from the wheel. Plink tries to direct them into a camera-ready semicircle. The line snakes around him and forms a much better barrier by accident. Tala sends Jun to fetch the certified rescue platform, while she checks the brake lock twice and tags the control panel. Plink keeps announcing the minutes until the storm, although the passengers can see the blue sky behind him.

SCENE 3. EXT. FERRIS WHEEL PLATFORM, LATER

The rescue crew arrives with a platform ladder. Plink insists on a dramatic rope descent. Tala checks the ride manual; the wheel must be locked before anyone climbs. Jun turns the key. The motor clicks off. A crew member tests the platform and finds it stable.

Plink asks the crew to pause while he frames the shot. The crew refuses and begins the inspection. A loose pennant flaps against the platform rail, startling Plink into ducking under a folding chair. The crew secures it with a tie. Tala explains that the stop sensor prevented a worse sway. Plink asks if the sensor can be thanked on camera. Jun says it is a sensor and cannot sign a release.

Tala climbs only after the crew confirms the lockout. She clips in, checks the ladder brackets, and speaks directly to the passengers. They listen to the instructions, then descend at a steady pace. Plink tries to hand each passenger a commemorative medal. The first passenger uses it to keep the kettle-corn bag closed.

The two passengers climb down one at a time. The first carries a paper bag of kettle corn. The second asks if they can ride again. Tala says after inspection. Plink gives the bag to the rescue crew, saying it may be evidence. The crew eats it during the safety check.

SCENE 4. INT. FAIR ANNOUNCER BOOTH, EVENING

Plink records an emergency report. Vera corrects the time line: the wheel stopped at 2:14, the brake worked, and both passengers were safe. Plink asks if they can call it a near-miss. Tala says a sensor did exactly what it should. Jun reviews the maintenance log and finds a loose bolt on the pennant bracket, not the ride.

SCENE 5. EXT. FERRIS WHEEL, NIGHT

The wheel passes inspection and reopens. The passengers return for a ride. Plink asks for a quote about courage. One says they were mostly hungry. Vera tells the fair staff to secure the pennants before the next windy afternoon.

An evening crowd gathers as the ride starts again. Plink has prepared a ribbon-cutting speech about leadership under pressure. The ribbon is too short to reach both posts, so he holds one end while a child cuts the other. The wheel circles once without incident. A camera operator asks Plink what he learned. He says to respect the sensors, then adds that he personally discovered the loose pennant first. Tala points toward the maintenance tag with her name on it. Plink quietly hands her the microphone.

FINAL IMAGE: Cabin Seven reaches the top. The riders raise a fresh bag of kettle corn. Plink raises the megaphone, then lowers it when the ride keeps turning normally.
