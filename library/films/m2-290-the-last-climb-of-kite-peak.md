# The Last Climb of Kite Peak

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mountaineering epics, summit fever, heroic rope teams, and climbers who mistake reaching the top for completing the mission*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A gibbon climbing guide must turn a record-breaking summit attempt into a rescue mission when the team discovers its summit flag has blown into a dangerous ravine.

Cast
- GUIDE LUMA, a gibbon with a sure grip and an unhurried pace.
- CLIMBER VEX, a macaque determined to plant his name on the peak.
- DOC SAI, a ring-tailed lemur who tracks weather and ration weight.
- PHOTOGRAPHER OMI, an orangutan carrying a heavy camera.
- RANGER TUL, a capuchin who knows the lower trail.

## Scene 1: Base Camp

EXT. KITE PEAK - DAWN

The team stands beside a map. A storm line is moving in from the west. Vex points to the summit. The flag is tied to his pack.

VEX: We reach the peak before noon and set a new record.

LUMA: We turn back if the wind reaches the ridge.

VEX: The summit window is clear.

SAI: The summit is not a window.

Omi adjusts the camera, which hangs from his shoulder and pulls him sideways. Luma rebalances the pack by moving the camera to the center.

## Scene 2: The Ascent

The climbers move up the rock face. Omi films each step in slow motion. Vex leaps ahead, but his rope catches on a stone. Luma anchors the line and frees it. Sai checks the weather meter.

SAI: Wind is increasing.

VEX: It is a tailwind.

LUMA: It is a side wind. Your tail is pointing east.

Vex looks at his tail, which indeed points sideways. The team continues along a narrow shelf. Omi's camera battery dies. He panics, then discovers he has been filming with the lens cap on.

OMI: The mountain is invisible.

LUMA: The mountain is right here.

## Scene 3: The Summit

They reach the summit ridge. Vex unpacks the flag, but a gust snatches it from his hands. The flag sails into the ravine and lands on a lower ledge. Vex begins to descend after it.

LUMA: Stop. The ridge is icing over.

VEX: The flag is our proof.

SAI: The team is proof. We have photos from the approach.

VEX: My name is on the flag.

OMI: The flag is now below us.

Ranger Tul radios from base camp. A pair of climbers on the lower path need help; their rope has crossed the marked trail. The team must choose between reclaiming the flag and going down to assist.

## Scene 4: The Turnaround

Luma chooses the rescue. Vex is angry, but follows. The group anchors a line to a stable rock, then descends to the lower ledge. The flag is caught on a branch near the path. They do not risk the cliff edge.

At the lower trail, they find two climbers tangled in a loose rope loop. Sai talks them through the knot while Luma braces the line. Vex uses his quick hands to loosen the crossing without pulling the climbers off balance.

CLIMBER: We thought we could reach the ridge before the storm.

LUMA: We have all thought that.

They secure the climbers and begin descending together. The wind rises; Omi puts the camera away rather than film the rescue.

## Scene 5: The Weather

The storm reaches the ridge. The group shelters beneath a rock overhang. Vex admits he wanted his name on the summit flag more than he wanted to listen to the forecast.

VEX: If we had kept climbing, we would have been above the shelter.

SAI: The forecast was clear about the wind.

VEX: I thought I could outrun it.

LUMA: No one outruns weather. We choose where to wait.

The team uses a rope to secure the shelter tarp. Omi shares the camera's spare battery with Sai's weather meter. They wait until the gusts ease.

## Scene 6: The Return

At base camp, Ranger Tul greets them. The two rescued climbers are safe. Vex unfolds the recovered flag. It is torn, the name nearly unreadable.

VEX: We can sew it back together.

TUL: You can keep it as a trail marker at the lower ledge.

VEX: That seems better than the summit.

The team logs the weather, the rope work, and the shelter location in the ranger book. Omi's camera contains no summit pose, but it has one clear photo of everyone descending together.

Sai posts the updated wind reading at the trail board. Vex adds the location of the loose rope loop. Luma checks the group count before anyone unclips from the main line. Every climber is present, and every rope is accounted for.

## Final Image

The damaged flag flutters beside the lower trail. Above it, clouds cover Kite Peak. The team walks toward camp at an even pace, with no one looking back for a record.
