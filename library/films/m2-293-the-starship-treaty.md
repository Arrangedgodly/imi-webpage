# The Starship Treaty

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-opera diplomacy, ceremonial alien introductions, glowing control panels, and captains who treat every negotiation as a battle*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A macaque starship captain must deliver a peace treaty across a crowded galaxy, but every ceremonial protocol delays the meeting while the treaty's two signatories wait on different sides of the same docking ring.

Cast
- CAPTAIN SERA, a macaque who steers by ear as much as by screen.
- AMBASSADOR TAVI, a gibbon who speaks in long introductions.
- ENGINEER LUMA, a capuchin who has taped labels over confusing controls.
- ENVOY VEX, an orangutan diplomat from the moon Koro.
- ENVOY PEL, a ring-tailed lemur diplomat from the moon Luma.

## Scene 1: Launch

INT. STARSHIP WAYFINDER - DAY

The ship glows with blue panels and ceremonial lights. Captain Sera holds a sealed treaty tube.

SERA: We dock at Ring Station, deliver the treaty, and return before the fuel cycle.

TAVI: First I must introduce the ship, the crew, the mission, the historical context, and the ceremonial bowls.

LUMA: The bowls are in the cargo bay. They are not part of the fuel system.

TAVI: They are part of the ceremony.

SERA: We will have time for one introduction.

Tavi practices an address to the empty cabin. Each time he says "peace," a panel flashes red.

## Scene 2: The Docking Ring

The ship reaches Ring Station. Two airlocks are marked EAST and WEST. The two envoys have arrived at the same station but refuse to meet without a formal greeting from the opposite side.

VEX: I will enter after the treaty is presented.

PEL: I will enter after the other envoy is present.

SERA: You are both present. The airlocks are thirty steps apart.

TAVI: There is a protocol. We greet from our respective vessels.

LUMA: The station has one docking ring. Both ships are attached to it.

A giant map shows the vessels on either end of the same structure. The envoy crews interpret the map as a tactical formation. Sera orders a neutral docking corridor.

## Scene 3: The Protocol

Tavi performs the formal introduction. It lasts twelve minutes. The station doors close automatically between the phrases. Each time he reaches the names of the signatories, the doors reopen, then close again while he explains their titles.

LUMA: The pressure cycle is being interrupted.

SERA: Can we shorten the titles?

TAVI: The titles establish respect.

VEX: My title is Envoy of Koro.

PEL: Mine is Envoy of Luma.

TAVI: Those are the short titles.

The ship's fuel alarm blinks. Sera tells the envoys they have one hour before the docking ring must cycle. Both insist they can wait; neither can leave without signing.

## Scene 4: The Translation Error

Luma checks the treaty. The two copies are identical except one word: "orbit" has been translated as "boundary." The phrase now reads, "We will share the boundary," which each side thinks gives the other control of the ring.

LUMA: This is not a threat. It is a bad translation.

VEX: The treaty grants us the boundary.

PEL: It says we share the boundary.

TAVI: In the oldest language, "orbit" and "boundary" use the same symbol.

SERA: Then we should write the meaning they agree on.

The envoys sit on the floor and compare maps. They realize both moons depend on the same navigation lane around the ring station. They agree to keep the lane open together.

PEL: We need a time when both ships can enter the ring.

VEX: Our vessels use different clocks.

LUMA: The station's beacon marks the shared cycle. Each ship can dock on alternating passes.

SERA: That leaves the service lane clear for repairs.

VEX: And neither moon controls the beacon.

PEL: We can both maintain it.

The envoys review the route markers and add a maintenance schedule. Tavi starts to give the full history of the beacon, then folds the notes and lets the two engineers explain it in three sentences.

## Scene 5: The Signature

Sera places a blank page between the treaty copies. Each envoy writes the route in their own language. Tavi speaks a short introduction, limited to the two names and the shared promise. The airlock remains open.

VEX: I will sign if the ring stays neutral.

PEL: I will sign if the lane stays open.

SERA: Those are the same condition.

They sign. The station lights shift from red to blue. The treaty tube clicks shut. No one fires a weapon or gives a victory speech.

## Scene 6: Departure

The envoys return to their ships by the corridor. The Wayfinder's fuel alarm stops. Sera sends the signed treaty to both moons. Tavi places the ceremonial bowls back in storage, still unused.

TAVI: We could have used them for the signature.

LUMA: They were bowls.

TAVI: They were ceremonial bowls.

SERA: The ceremony was the agreement.

## Final Image

From orbit, the station's ring turns in steady silence, with two ships traveling beside it at the same speed.
