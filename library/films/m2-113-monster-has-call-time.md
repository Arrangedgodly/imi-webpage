# The Monster Has a Call Time

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: creature features that keep the monster offscreen until the finale and blame the creature for production delays*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A monster-film crew spends a night reacting to scratches and shadows, while the creature actor waits in makeup for a scene that keeps being postponed.

Cast: Sola, a lemur director; Bram, a gorilla in a creature suit; Venn, a macaque assistant director; and Puck, a capuchin sound artist.

SCENE 1. INT. SOUNDSTAGE, NIGHT

A door rattles. The heroes turn toward it. Puck plays three ominous knocks. Sola asks whether the monster is ready. Venn checks the call sheet.

VENN: The monster has a call time at eight.

BRAM (OFF): It is eight thirty.

Sola tells Bram to stay in the makeup trailer until the reveal. Bram says the suit is hot. Venn asks if the creature can appear in shadow. Sola says the audience must imagine it first. Bram asks whether imagining counts as his scene. Sola says it counts as waiting.

VENN: The monster has a call time at eight.

BRAM (OFF): It is eight thirty.

Sola asks Bram to stay in the makeup trailer until the reveal. Bram says the suit is hot. Venn asks if the creature can appear in shadow. Sola says the audience must imagine it.

SCENE 2. INT. MAKEUP TRAILER, LATER

Bram sits in the creature suit beside a small fan. Puck records claws scratching a door. The sound is actually Bram tapping a spoon against his water cup. Sola asks for a louder sound. Bram taps harder and dents the cup.

BRAM: Does the monster get a drink?

SOLA: After the take.

Venn arrives with a new schedule. The shadow scene has moved to midnight, then to the next day, then to "when the fog machine is fixed." Bram reads his call slip and asks which costume he should wear for the fog test. Venn says the monster suit. The shadow scene moves from midnight to tomorrow, then to "when the fog machine is fixed." Bram points to the call sheet. His suit is already booked for a wide shot that has no monster in it. Puck records another claw sound. It is Bram tapping a spoon on his empty water cup.

SCENE 3. INT. SOUNDSTAGE, NEXT DAY

The heroes film another reaction shot. A shadow crosses the wall. The crew looks up. Bram is not on set; his suit hangs on a rack. The shadow is cast by the lighting technician carrying a ladder. Sola asks to keep the shot. Venn says it breaks continuity because the heroes are facing the wrong direction.

Bram enters, still in the creature suit, and stands behind them. No one notices. He clears his throat. They turn and scream. Puck records the reaction. Sola asks whether the monster can hold still for a close-up. Bram's tail catches on a cable. Venn calls cut and frees it.

BRAM: Was that the reveal?

SOLA: That was the cable.

Bram removes one claw and points to the script. The creature has no dialogue, but the scene calls for it to knock over a table. There are no tables on set. Puck offers a folding chair. Sola asks whether the chair is approved to fall. Venn checks the prop label. Bram crosses the set, nudges it with his tail, and stops before the camera track.

Bram removes one claw and points to the script. The monster has no dialogue, but the scene calls for it to knock over a table. There are no tables on the set. Puck offers a folding chair. Sola says the creature should have a clear path. Venn moves the chair. Bram crosses the set, knocks it over with his tail, then stops before reaching the camera.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The cut shows the monster in one clear shot, then keeps the heroes' startled reactions. The shadow takes are gone. Bram asks whether the audience will see the suit seams. Sola says the lighting hides them. Puck plays the claw sound from the spoon tap. Bram says it sounds like a kitchen.

The crew screens the final scene. The monster knocks over the chair, the heroes flee, and the room goes dark. Venn asks if the final image should be a claw. Sola says the movie already ends. Bram asks whether he can take off the suit. Venn marks the creature reveal complete. Puck plays the claw sound from the spoon tap. Bram says it sounds like a kitchen. Sola asks him to drink water before makeup removes the mask.

FINAL IMAGE: Bram sits in the makeup chair as the crew wraps. His call sheet shows "creature reveal: completed," and he finally drinks a cup of water.
