# The Time Capsule Is Already Open

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: family adventure films that turn a school archive box into a race against their own paperwork*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
TEACHER MARA, a macaque
STUDENT PIP, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon
ANNOUNCER VEX, an orangutan
GARDENER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL BASEMENT - AFTERNOON

A sealed metal box rests on a table. A date painted across the lid reads 2040. Pip holds a key; Mara checks the school record.

PIP: The class buried this twenty years ago. We have to open it before the ceremony.

ODO: The record says the box was opened in 2030 to check for water damage, then resealed.

VEX, over speaker: The countdown begins in five minutes.

MARA: There is no countdown. The ceremony begins when the students arrive.

NARI: The garden bed where it was buried is being replanted. We moved the box inside last week.

PIP: Is the seal original?

ODO: The wax is new. The key is attached to the archive tag.

SCENE 2. INT. BASEMENT - MOMENTS LATER

Pip fits the key. It turns smoothly. The lid opens without a sound. Inside are drawings, a paper map, and a note in a plastic sleeve.

VEX: It is open. We are too late.

MARA: We are on time. The date on the lid marks when the class expected to reopen it, not an expiration.

PIP: I found the class's old map. It says the box was buried by the pear tree.

NARI: The pear tree was removed after the roots lifted the path. We planted the new tree in another bed.

ODO: The archive entry records the relocation and the 2030 inspection. The seal was replaced the same afternoon.

PIP: So nothing was hidden from us. It was documented.

VEX: That removes the mystery.

MARA: It replaces a false mystery with an actual history.

SCENE 2A. INT. BASEMENT - CONTINUOUS

Odo reads the back of the note. There is a list of names and a date written in pencil.

ODO: The names are the original class. The date is the day the box was resealed after inspection.

PIP: Could one of them have added a second message?

MARA: We can compare the paper to the archive photograph. Don't erase or unfold anything until we know how it is attached.

Odo turns the sleeve over. A strip of old tape holds a second note to the back. The tape is loose at one edge.

ODO: The paper is lifting. We should use the archival spatula, not pull it by hand.

VEX: A secret note beneath the note.

PIP: It says, "Please water the pear tree in summer."

NARI: That tree is gone, but the new one needs water too.

MARA: Add a separate present-day card. Keep the old note in its sleeve exactly as found.

Pip writes the new date on a blank card. Odo photographs both sides of the sleeve before replacing it in the box.

VEX: So the capsule contained a task.

MARA: It contained a request from a former class. We can honor it without pretending someone hid it from us.

SCENE 2B. INT. BASEMENT - CONTINUOUS

Mara compares the note with the archive image. The paper's torn edge matches the photograph.

MARA: Nothing was added after the seal was replaced. The sleeve kept the note flat.

ODO: I'll update the catalog with the two notes and today's opening date.

VEX: Mystery solved before the ceremony.

PIP: We still have to carry the box upstairs without dropping it.

NARI: I'll walk ahead and clear the stair landing.

SCENE 3. INT. SCHOOL HALL - LATER

Students gather. Pip lays the drawings under a clear cover. Odo reads the note aloud.

ODO: "We hope the next class has a shady place to read. Please keep the map with the box."

NARI: The old pear bed has shade now. The map still points to it.

PIP: The new tree isn't on the map. I can add a pencil mark and date it, without changing the old drawing.

MARA: Good. Keep the original and the update together.

VEX: We have found a message from the past.

PIP: We have found a class wish and a map that needs a note.

SCENE 4. EXT. SCHOOL GARDEN - SUNSET

The students carry the box to a dry shelf in the garden shed. Nari points to the relocated tree. Pip updates the map's sleeve, not the drawing.

VEX: Do we bury it again for the next generation?

ODO: No. The record says keep it in the archive cabinet, where it will stay dry.

MARA: The class can add a new letter and schedule a future review. No one needs to dig under a tree.

PIP: I'll write the date on the new envelope.

VEX: The time capsule has survived its own opening.

TAG: A student looks at the empty hole marker by the old bed. Vex quickly places a sign: ARCHIVE SHELF, NOT DIG SITE.
