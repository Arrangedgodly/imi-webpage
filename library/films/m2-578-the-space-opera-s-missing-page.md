# The Space Opera's Missing Page

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space sagas that treat an absent script page as a cosmic rupture*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
CAPTAIN MARA, a macaque
NAVIGATOR VEX, an orangutan
ENGINEER PIP, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon
COMMUNICATIONS NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. STARSHIP BRIDGE - NIGHT

The crew looks at a script binder. Page twelve is missing. Vex watches a star map that is only a projection.

VEX: The galaxy has lost a page.

MARA: The binder is missing page twelve. The flight plan is on the console.

ODO: The page is in the rehearsal folder. It fell out when the binder opened.

PIP: The ship is still on course. The navigation display is normal.

NARI: We can pause the scene and retrieve the page.

VEX: The mission cannot pause.

SCENE 2. INT. STARSHIP BRIDGE - CONTINUOUS

Pip checks the console. A loose paper star drifts from the set ceiling and lands beside the binder.

VEX: Debris in orbit.

MARA: It's a prop. Please don't touch the set rig.

ODO: Page twelve is under the rehearsal chair. I'll get it after the camera stops.

PIP: The engine sound is still running.

VEX: We are losing power.

MARA: That's the soundtrack. The set lights are steady.

SCENE 2A. INT. STARSHIP SET - NIGHT

Mara stops the camera. Odo opens the rehearsal folder on a prop table. A page lies beneath a star-map overlay.

ODO: Page twelve is here, folded under the overlay. It wasn't lost in space; it slipped between the set diagrams.

VEX: The galaxy concealed its final instruction.

PIP: The binder rings are loose. That's why the page came out.

MARA: Odo, can you tighten the rings after we finish the take? Don't bend the paper.

NARI: The floating star prop is still attached to its ceiling line. I can lower it after the camera is off.

VEX: The ship has encountered debris.

MARA: The camera is off. The prop is a paper star. Please don't grab the line.

Odo places page twelve flat and checks its number. It is intact. He compares it with the rehearsal list; the line is the same in both copies.

ODO: We can resume at the previous cue. The page doesn't change the flight plan.

PIP: I'll close the console cover before we walk back to the bridge set.

VEX: What if the missing page contained a surprise ending?

MARA: Then we would still use the approved script. The approved ending is on the last page.

Nari lowers the paper star with the rigging handle and places it on a padded tray. Odo tightens the binder rings.

NARI: Prop is down, line is clear.

MARA: We can reset for the next take.

SCENE 2B. INT. STARSHIP SET - CONTINUOUS

Pip places the recovered page into the spare binder. The original binder stays in the repair bin; the flight plan remains on the console.

MARA: The page never left the set. We do not need a second search.

VEX: The galaxy hid it inside its own archive.

ODO: The binder rings are bent. I'll use the spare binder after this take so the page cannot fall out again.

PIP: The flight plan is on the console. The script page is only dialogue.

NARI: The paper star is back in its padded tray, and the rigging line is secured.

VEX: Could the ship lose power at this point?

MARA: The set lights are steady. We don't need a blackout.

Pip checks the page numbers in the spare binder. Odo points to twelve, then thirteen; both are present.

VEX: The cosmic rupture has been repaired.

PIP: The binder has been repaired. The story continues.

Nari carries the tray to the prop table and sets it beside the star-map overlay, not under it.

SCENE 2C. INT. STARSHIP SET - CONTINUOUS

Vex returns the prop map to its marked hook. Odo leaves the old binder on the repair table, separate from the spare binder.

ODO: Script secured. Prop map returned. Camera can resume at the bridge shot.

SCENE 3. INT. STAGE - LATER

The crew returns to the bridge set. Odo opens the spare binder at page twelve; Pip holds it on the mark. The prop star is secured on its tray and the rigging line is out of frame.

ODO: Page twelve is in place. Page thirteen follows it.

MARA: Flight plan remains on the console. Camera can roll.

VEX: The galaxy continues.

PIP: The voyage proceeds. The ship never changed course.

NARI: Communication is clear. We can take the bridge shot.

SCENE 4. INT. STARSHIP BRIDGE - NIGHT

The crew resumes filming. Vex delivers the line gravely. The star map continues moving.

VEX: The ship continues on its route.

MARA: Cut. Good. The page is back in the binder.

TAG: Vex notices the paper star in its padded tray and whispers, "Meteor." Pip points to the tray label: PROP, NOT DEBRIS.
