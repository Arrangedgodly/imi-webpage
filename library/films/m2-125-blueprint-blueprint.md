# The Blueprint Has Its Own Blueprint

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: heist movies that explain elaborate plans with diagrams, flashbacks, and contingencies more complicated than the job*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: Three monkeys plan to retrieve a key from a cabinet they believe is locked, but the blueprint grows harder to follow than the simple latch.

Cast: Sumi, a lemur planner; Arlo, a gorilla carpenter; Viko, a macaque actor; and Puck, a capuchin lighting technician.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY THEATER, MORNING

The rehearsal room is empty except for a cabinet with a glass door. The cabinet key hangs on a hook inside. Sumi unrolls a floor plan across the stage. It rolls off the other side and covers a chair.

SUMI: Step one: enter through the lobby.

ARLO: The lobby is open.

SUMI: That is why the plan starts there.

Viko puts on a black turtleneck. Puck shines a small flashlight under his chin. Sumi marks the cabinet with a red circle and draws a dotted route through the lobby, hallway, green room, and back to the same stage.

VIKO: Why do we go around the building?

SUMI: The diagram needs a suspenseful curve.

Arlo tests the cabinet latch. It is already open. The key hangs on a hook inside. The door swings wide. Inside is a stack of folding chairs and a sign-up sheet.

ARLO: We need the key for tonight's rehearsal.

Sumi closes the cabinet and asks everyone to reset to their marks. Puck asks whether he should add a camera in the cabinet to film the key. Sumi puts "inside camera" on the plan. Arlo asks who will film the camera. Sumi draws a smaller camera beside it, then a third camera to film that one.

VIKO: Are we retrieving the key or filming the plan?

SUMI: The plan establishes the stakes.

ARLO: The stakes are that the cabinet door squeaks.

Puck opens the cabinet slowly. The squeak lasts longer than the key retrieval.

SCENE 2. INT. THEATER LOBBY, LATER

Sumi draws a second plan on a portable whiteboard. It includes a laser grid, a ventilation shaft, and a diversion involving three stage lights. Puck turns off the lights to test the distraction. The lobby becomes dark. Arlo walks into a coat rack.

ARLO: The plan has blocked our path.

SUMI: That is what the rehearsal is for.

Viko volunteers to act as the guard. He takes a seat at the ticket desk and starts reading the theater schedule. Puck cues a spotlight. It lands on the sign-up sheet, highlighting the words "keys returned after use."

Sumi asks Viko to improvise suspicion. He sees the key ring on Arlo's belt and asks why it is there. Arlo says he lifted it off the hook while the cabinet stood open. Sumi adds a new arrow connecting the belt to the cabinet. The line crosses the laser grid and covers the exit symbol.

VIKO: How do we get out?

SUMI: We go back through the ventilation shaft.

ARLO: It is a prop shaft. It ends above the stage.

Puck reaches into the prop shaft for a spool of cable. His elbow bumps a cup of red paint; it tips onto the plan and bleeds into a shape like a map of the auditorium. Viko looks at the sheet.

VIKO: Could we just return the key to the hook after rehearsal?

SCENE 3. INT. STAGE, AFTERNOON

The group tries the plan in order. Viko enters the lobby, Arlo waits at the coat rack, and Puck turns on the spotlight. The beam hits the empty seat where Viko was supposed to guard. Viko is still at the ticket desk, reading the schedule.

Sumi calls the first contingency. Arlo pulls a curtain to reveal the green room. Puck follows the light cue, turns the work lights on, and shows the whole audience that there is no audience. Viko walks onto the stage and places the key on the hook.

SUMI: That is not the planned finish.

VIKO: It is the place the key belongs.

Arlo checks the sign-up sheet. The cabinet was already unlocked, but the key is kept on the hook so the next stage manager can find it. The real task is to label the key ring and tell the cast where it goes. Sumi rolls up the floor plan. It is stuck to the floor with the spilled red paint. She peels one corner free, revealing a second copy beneath it. The first had been marked "Plan B." The other says "Plan A." Both route the crew through the same hallway.

SUMI: We rehearsed the wrong plan.

ARLO: They have the same hallway.

Viko hands her the key ring. The label is blank. They write "CABINET" in block letters and hang it on the hook.

SCENE 4. INT. THEATER, EVENING

The cast arrives for rehearsal. Viko points out the key hook and the sign-up sheet. Arlo hangs a label on the ring. Puck turns on the work lights without a cue. Sumi asks whether anyone wants to see the blueprint.

The cast looks at the cabinet, then at the one-page instruction card. Everyone signs the sheet. The rehearsal starts on time.

FINAL IMAGE: Sumi tapes the complicated plan inside the cabinet, where it covers the folding chairs, and pins the simple key instruction outside.
