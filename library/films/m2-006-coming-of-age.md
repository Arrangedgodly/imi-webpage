# Last Day at Gull Point

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: coming-of-age summer films, farewell rituals, and symbolic adolescent milestones*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: Three young monkeys plan one perfect final day at their seaside camp before the summer ends, but their elaborate rites of passage keep being interrupted by chores, weather, and the fact that nobody has packed.

Cast: Nori, a thoughtful young langur who keeps lists; Tavi, a capuchin who wants a dramatic goodbye; Jess, a macaque who is already looking forward to school; and Aunt Sol, the camp caretaker who has seen many summers end.

SCENE 1. EXT. GULL POINT CAMP, MORNING

A hand-painted sign reads LAST DAY OF SUMMER. Tavi stands on a rock with a shell held aloft. He has written a speech on his arm. Nori checks the tide chart. Jess stacks life jackets.

TAVI: Today we become different people.

JESS: After we return the paddles.

Tavi asks them to swim to the distant buoy, a traditional camp ritual. Nori points out the water is choppy. They compromise by swimming to the near buoy, which is twelve feet away. Tavi calls it symbolic.

SCENE 2. EXT. DOCK, LATER

They climb into a rowboat to cross the cove. Tavi rows dramatically, facing backward. The boat circles the dock. Jess switches seats. Nori reads the safety card aloud. The boat moves six feet, then stops because the painter rope is still tied to shore.

Aunt Sol unties it. She hands Tavi a bucket to bail water. He asks if this is part of the ritual.

AUNT SOL: It is if you don't want wet shoes.

SCENE 3. EXT. GULL POINT, MIDDAY

The trio reaches the buoy. Tavi wants to dive from it. Nori sees the no-climbing marker. Jess offers to take a photo beside it instead. Tavi poses, then drops his shell into the water. They fish it out using a net and a stick. The photograph captures Jess laughing, Nori holding the net, and Tavi with seaweed on his ear.

Tavi studies the picture and admits it looks better than the dramatic pose.

SCENE 4. INT. CAMP CABIN, AFTERNOON

The friends hold a farewell ceremony. Tavi lights a lantern. Nori asks whether the cabin permits open flame. They use a battery lantern instead. Jess reads a list of things she wants to remember: the gull that stole toast, the leaky canoe, the afternoon they all slept through a thunderstorm.

Tavi's speech begins with a long metaphor about the horizon. Aunt Sol enters carrying a laundry basket. She asks them to fold the towels before the bus arrives. The three fold towels while Tavi finishes his speech. His arm notes smear against one towel.

SCENE 5. EXT. CAMP GATE, EVENING

The bus pulls up. Nori is ready. Jess has packed. Tavi realizes his bag is still open in the cabin. He runs back, returns with two mismatched shoes, and says he is ready to be transformed. Aunt Sol gives him the missing bag and asks him to return the camp key next summer.

The bus starts. Tavi runs beside it for a few steps, then realizes it is moving slowly and climbs aboard at the next stop. The other two saved him a seat.

At the dock, Tavi asks each friend to name the object that represents their childhood. Jess chooses a pencil because it is useful. Nori chooses her tide chart because it changes. Tavi chooses a pebble and says it represents the weight of memory. The pebble slips from his palm and lands in his shoe.

He tries to keep a straight face while walking. Jess asks if the pebble is part of the ritual. Tavi says it is a trial. He lasts six steps, then removes the shoe. The pebble is ordinary, smooth, and damp. Nori writes its measurements in her list. Tavi asks why. She says one day he will claim it was a boulder.

Aunt Sol brings the old camp photo album. Tavi expects a solemn montage. Instead, the photos show him wearing a life jacket backward, eating a lemon, and falling asleep during a sunrise. Jess laughs so hard she snorts. Tavi asks that the last picture remain private. Aunt Sol says it has already been mailed to his mother.

Jess finds the camp's lost-and-found box and returns three hats, a whistle, and one damp map. Tavi asks whether the map represents a journey. Aunt Sol says it represents the trail to the dining hall. Nori adds that to her list under "things we should have known." They each sign the back, then use it to find the bus.

SCENE 6. INT. BUS, CONTINUOUS

TAVI: I forgot my speech.

NORI: It's on your arm.

JESS: It's on the towel.

TAVI: Which towel?

NORI: The camp towel.

JESS: Aunt Sol kept it.

TAVI: My final words are laundry.

NORI: You said the horizon.

TAVI: Before the towels.

JESS: The bus is moving.

TAVI: Should we wave?

NORI: We are inside it.

JESS: Wave to camp.

TAVI: I can do that.

NORI: You dropped your shell.

TAVI: It's still here.

JESS: It's in the seat.

TAVI: A symbol.

NORI: Of losing things.

TAVI: Of carrying them.

JESS: Put it in your bag.

TAVI: The bag is open.

NORI: Zip it.

TAVI: It is zipped.

JESS: The other pocket.

TAVI: Right.

NORI: We have school Monday.

JESS: I know.

TAVI: We are different people.

JESS: We are sleepy people.

NORI: Same bus.

TAVI: New shoes.

JESS: Yours don't match.

TAVI: A new beginning.

The bus hits a bump. Tavi's shell rolls forward. Nori catches it and puts it in a cup holder.

JESS: The bus is here.
TAVI: I am transformed.
NORI: Your bag is open.

FINAL IMAGE: The three look out the bus window at the camp sign. Tavi holds up his shell. It is still tangled with seaweed. Jess says it can come to school too. He puts it in the side pocket and checks that the pocket is zipped.
