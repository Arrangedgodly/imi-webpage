# The Copper Flag of Cutlass Cove

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: swashbuckling pirate adventures, buried treasure maps, mutinous crews, and captains who mistake a tide chart for a prophecy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A crew of primate sailors races rival pirates to a legendary cove, only to discover their treasure map is a tide chart and the real prize is the harbor they nearly destroy.

Cast
- CAPTAIN RELL, a macaque who ties knots with his tail.
- NAVIGATOR ODO, a gibbon who reads currents and dislikes speeches.
- FIRST MATE BEX, a capuchin who keeps the ship's stores balanced.
- RIVAL CAPTAIN VELL, a mandrill in a copper-trimmed coat.
- LOOKOUT PIP, a ring-tailed lemur with sharp eyes.

## Scene 1: The Map

EXT. THE SABLE FIN - MORNING

Captain Rell holds an old parchment up to the light. A red cross marks Cutlass Cove.

RELL: At sunset, we claim the copper flag and the treasure beneath it.

ODO: This is a tide chart.

RELL: It has a cross.

ODO: The cross marks a shallow reef.

BEX: If we sail at high tide, we can pass. If we sail at low tide, we hit rock.

PIP: A second ship is following us.

The rival vessel appears over the horizon. Vell raises a copper flag. Rell orders the crew to hoist sail. Odo points out the wind is against them.

## Scene 2: The Chase

The Sable Fin and Vell's ship race along the coast. Rell orders the crew to row. Bex checks the supplies and finds the water barrel leaking. Pip climbs the mast and spots a line of foam across the sea.

PIP: Reef ahead!

RELL: We can outrun it.

ODO: The tide is going out. The water is getting shallower.

RELL: Then we will outrun the tide.

The ship scrapes bottom. The rival vessel passes, then also runs aground. Both crews are stranded in the narrow channel.

VELL: I have won.

ODO: You have stopped at the same reef.

## Scene 3: The Cove

The crews climb down to a strip of rock and sand. A copper flag stands on the shore, half buried. Rell pulls it free. Beneath it is a metal box sealed with a lock. Vell claims it. Rell draws a cutlass, but Odo notices a carved line on the box.

ODO: It shows the tide height. This is a harbor marker.

VELL: It is a treasure chest.

BEX: It has no hinge. It is bolted into the rocks.

PIP: The water is rising around it.

The crews try to pry the box loose. The tide begins filling the channel. Their ships are trapped, and the rock path to shore is narrowing.

## Scene 4: The Mutiny

Vell's crew refuses to help Rell, claiming the captain has lost the treasure. Rell shouts a speech about loyalty. Bex asks him to stop and distribute the remaining water.

BEX: We have one barrel and two crews. If we keep arguing, the tide will decide who gets it.

RELL: We share the water.

VELL: Pirates do not share.

ODO: Then pirates dehydrate together.

The crews split the water. Pip sees the copper flag pointing toward a safe opening between the rocks. It is not a treasure marker; it indicates a channel used by local boats at high tide.

## Scene 5: The Real Prize

Odo guides both ships through the channel. The tide is high enough to lift them off the reef. The box remains in place, but the rising water pushes a loose stone away from the harbor wall. A gap opens, threatening to flood the cove's sheltered nesting beach.

Rell and Vell order both crews to haul a timber beam from the Sable Fin. They brace the wall together while Bex packs sandbags around the gap. The harbor stays protected. The old box's lock turns when the current reaches it, revealing a small plaque with the names of the first cove keepers.

VELL: This is not gold.

ODO: It is the record of who kept the harbor open.

RELL: We nearly broke it trying to own it.

## Scene 6: The Agreement

The crews return to the port. Rell gives the plaque to the harbor keeper. Vell takes down his copper flag and offers his ship to help repair the wall. The rival crews argue about who will receive credit.

They haul stones from both decks and set them along the gap. The harbor keeper marks the water height on the plaque's frame, adding the latest repair date beneath the old names. Rell and Vell take turns holding the timber while Bex secures the braces.

BEX: We can list both ships on the repair.

VELL: And both captains?

PIP: The harbor keeper's plaque lists the keepers, not the loudest person.

Rell laughs and signs the log beside Vell.

## Final Image

The copper flag flies over Cutlass Cove, now marking the safe channel. Below, the repaired harbor wall holds against the tide while both ships sail out side by side.
