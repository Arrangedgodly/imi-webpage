# The Wedding That Waited for Rain

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: wedding comedies, elaborate outdoor ceremony plans, grand gestures, and a weather interruption that forces everyone to improvise*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
MARA, a lemur wedding planner
JESS, a capuchin bride
TOLL, a gibbon groom
UNCLE VIM, a macaque relative

SCENE 1. EXT. ORCHARD - MORNING

Mara measures an aisle between apple trees. Jess wants a quiet ceremony beneath the oldest tree. Toll's family has brought a brass band, a fountain, and a flock of white doves. Uncle Vim unpacks a fog machine.

JESS: We agreed on simple.

TOLL: The fountain was a gift.

UNCLE VIM: The fog is optional.

MARA: The orchard is not designed for fog, fountains, and doves in one aisle.

SCENE 2. INT. ORCHARD SHED - AFTERNOON

The families argue over seating, music, and whether the vows should mention the rain forecast. Jess and Toll slip away to the shed. They laugh at the decorations and decide to keep the ceremony under the tree, even if the band plays from the path.

MARA: We can move the chairs into the shed if the rain starts.

JESS: Then the shed is the plan, not the backup.

The families begin carrying chairs. Uncle Vim tests the fog machine; it fills the shed. The doves flap out through the door. The brass band follows them, playing louder so the birds can hear.

SCENE 2A. EXT. ORCHARD - LATE AFTERNOON

Mara rehearses the ceremony. The officiant stands beneath the tree, the band waits along the path, and the dove handler asks everyone to keep their hands low. Uncle Vim turns on the fog machine. It pushes the doves out of the arch and sends the brass band's sheet music into the apple trees.

TOLL: We can remove the fog.

UNCLE VIM: Then what will people remember?

JESS: That we got married.

Mara retrieves the music. The band insists on playing from the orchard edge, where the sound reaches the guests without blocking the aisle. The families begin to move the fountain. Its pump leaks, leaving a trail of water across the grass.

MARA: The fountain can stay where it is. We will move the seats instead.

Jess and Toll help. Their relatives join in. The rehearsal turns into a chair-moving relay, and the doves return to the tree.

SCENE 3. EXT. ORCHARD - CEREMONY TIME

The sky darkens. Guests take their seats. The officiant begins. The first raindrops fall on the fountain. The pump shorts with a small pop. The doves return and perch on the wedding arch.

Toll's family opens umbrellas. Jess's family carries the chairs to the shed. The band tries to follow but gets wedged in the doorway. A tuba blocks the entrance.

SCENE 3A. EXT. ORCHARD - CEREMONY TIME

The rain becomes a steady shower. Guests lift umbrellas, but the doves remain under the branches. The officiant begins speaking. A gust carries the first page of the ceremony toward the fountain. Mara catches it with the bouquet.

MARA: We can move into the shed now.

JESS: We can stay under the tree. Everyone can hear us.

TOLL: The band can play softer.

The tuba player lowers the bell. The music becomes a quiet backing for the vows. Uncle Vim switches off the fog machine and holds the shed door so the wind does not slam it. The families move the chairs into a circle, leaving the aisle open.

JESS: I want this to be simple because I want to remember the words.

TOLL: I will remember them, even if the paper flies away.

The officiant holds the pages down with a smooth stone from the orchard. The guests settle under the branches.

SCENE 4. INT. ORCHARD SHED - CONTINUOUS

Mara moves the chairs into a circle. Jess and Toll decide to marry beneath the open shed door, where guests can see the orchard and hear the rain. The fog machine is unplugged. Uncle Vim offers to read a poem, but has brought the wrong paper: a shopping list.

UNCLE VIM: "Apples, flour, one jar of jam…"

JESS: It's perfect. It's the list that brought us here.

TOLL: We met when I dropped the apples.

The guests laugh. Toll and Jess exchange vows. The brass band, still outside, plays a soft tune under the rain.

SCENE 5. EXT. ORCHARD - LATE AFTERNOON

The rain stops. The newly married pair step outside. The fountain is quiet, the chairs are damp, and the doves are sitting on the arch. The band begins a celebratory march. The guests dance between puddles.

Mara presents Jess with the original simple arch, which survived the storm because it was tied to the oldest tree. Uncle Vim gives them the shopping list as a keepsake.

JESS: We had a plan for the rain.

MARA: We had three plans. The one you chose was the best.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty orchard chairs form a circle around the tree. Rainwater drips from the leaves into the silent fountain, while the wedding band's last note rings across the grass.
