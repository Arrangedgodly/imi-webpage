# The Ghost in the Projector Booth

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-cinema horror films, flickering images, ominous whispering, and a skeptic who explains every scare until one runs out of film*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: On the final night of the Old Lantern Cinema, a capuchin projectionist investigates a ghost that keeps changing the movie, only to learn that the theater's automated equipment has developed a dramatic sense of timing.

Cast
- NILO, a capuchin projectionist whose nimble hands can thread film in the dark.
- VELA, a macaque manager who refuses to close before the last show.
- ORI, a gibbon usher with a theatrical whisper.
- PIP, a ring-tailed lemur patron who keeps snacks in her tail pouch.
- THE VOICE, an unseen presence in the projection booth.

## Scene 1: The Last Screening

INT. OLD LANTERN CINEMA - NIGHT

The lobby is dark except for one bulb. A handwritten sign says FINAL SCREENING. Vela places a box of old reels on the counter.

VELA: The movie is called The Sailor and the Moon. It is the last reel in the archive.

NILO: The last reel is missing the first five minutes.

ORI: A mysterious opening.

PIP: I brought a snack. Is the theater closing after this?

VELA: At midnight. The city is turning the building into a bicycle shed.

The projector starts. The screen shows a moon, then the image jumps to a close-up of a sailor's shoe.

NILO: That is not the opening.

ORI: The ghost has entered the frame.

NILO: That is a splice.

## Scene 2: The Whisper

INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - CONTINUOUS

Nilo threads the reel. A whisper comes from the speaker.

THE VOICE: Return... the moon...

ORI: The cinema's former projectionist wants the film restored.

NILO: The speaker wire is loose.

VELA: Don't explain the haunting before the audience arrives.

The film stops. A white circle appears on screen, then grows into a giant moon. The light spills through a cracked shutter and illuminates the booth wall. Vela gasps.

VELA: The moon has left the screen.

NILO: The shutter is open.

PIP: I can see the edge of the moon from my seat.

The edge is the projector beam, shaped by a round hole in the reel.

## Scene 3: The House Tour

Vela leads them through the empty auditorium to inspect each row. Seats creak one at a time as they pass.

ORI: The ghost is following us.

NILO: The seats have springs.

PIP: This one has a spring and a snack crumb.

Pip's tail pouch brushes an armrest. A hidden bell rings beneath the seat. They all freeze.

VELA: That bell was never installed.

NILO: The seats were installed in 1938. It might have been.

A series of seat bells rings, spreading from the back row to the front. Ori shivers. Nilo crouches and sees a small rolling cleaning machine traveling beneath the seats, tapping each spring as it passes.

NILO: The machine is on its night route.

ORI: It is the ghost's carriage.

PIP: It is collecting crumbs.

The machine bumps the booth door and rolls away. The lights flicker.

## Scene 4: The Ghost Appears

INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - LATER

The film's image changes again. A shadow of a long arm crosses the screen. Ori whispers that the ghost is reaching from the booth. Vela grabs the old emergency lantern. Nilo studies the projector.

NILO: The image is reversed. Something is casting the shadow from behind the screen.

They enter the screen room. A hanging curtain shifts in the airflow. Behind it, a maintenance fan rotates a folded poster on a string. The poster's arm-shaped illustration casts the shadow.

VELA: The ghost is an advertisement for the bicycle shed.

PIP: It says "Ride into the future." The arm is holding a handlebar.

ORI: The ghost is selling bicycles.

The whisper returns. The projector's speaker crackles.

THE VOICE: Return... the moon...

Nilo tightens the speaker wire. The voice becomes a clear announcement: "Rewind the reel to the moon scene." The film is running backward through a damaged segment.

## Scene 5: The Real Mystery

Nilo opens the projector. The film loop has caught on a bent guide, pulling frames backward whenever the spool tightens. He straightens the guide and threads the reel by hand. The next image is the sailor's shoe, then the moon, then a scene with the missing opening card.

VELA: We have restored the first five minutes.

NILO: We have found the frames that were stuck behind the splice.

ORI: And the voice?

NILO: The instruction track from the old projector was still connected.

VELA: We can show the movie once before midnight.

They return to the auditorium. The audience arrives, including the city crew that will convert the building. Vela invites them to sit for the final screening.

## Scene 6: One Last Show

The movie plays cleanly. The sailor finds the moon reflected in a tide pool. The audience laughs at the old effects. The cleaning machine rolls quietly beneath the seats, collecting crumbs. At midnight, the credits finish.

CITY WORKER: We can start tomorrow.

VELA: Give us one more minute.

The audience applauds. Ori bows to the screen. Nilo switches off the projector.

## Final Image

The screen goes dark, but the round beam remains on the wall for one last second before the shutter closes. The ghost has vanished; the moon is back in the movie.
