# The Telescope That Saw Yesterday

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-travel adventures, cryptic warnings, and the gadget that makes every clue look more important*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
NARI, a lemur astronomer
VEX, an orangutan inventor
PIP, a capuchin assistant
MARA, a macaque museum guide
ODO, a gibbon visitor

SCENE 1. INT. HILLTOP OBSERVATORY - MORNING

Vex wheels a brass telescope into the public gallery. A paper label reads "VIEW YESTERDAY." Nari adjusts the lens. Pip holds a tray of gears.

ODO: Can it show the future?

VEX: The future is under review.

NARI: It shows whatever light reaches the mirror. Old light, not old events.

VEX: That is less marketable.

The lens catches sunlight and projects a bright circle on the wall. A visitor steps into it and casts a shadow shaped like a giant bird.

PIP: Yesterday's bird.

MARA: That is Odo.

Odo waves. The bird waves back. Vex spins a brass dial, and the shadow grows until it covers the exhibit sign.

VEX: I have located a warning.

NARI: You have located Odo's shadow.

Vex sells Mara on a demonstration for the museum's opening. The telescope will "reveal a forgotten hour" at noon. Mara agrees only if it does not frighten the school group.

SCENE 2. INT. GEAR ROOM - LATER

Nari opens the telescope housing. The lens reflects the sun through a narrow corridor. On the far wall is a row of faded painted birds from an old mural.

VEX: This is an image from the past.

MARA: The mural is still there.

PIP: It is old enough to have yesterday in it.

Vex arranges three velvet ropes around the projection. Odo reads a display card aloud: "The Lost Hour, recovered from distant light."

NARI: That is not the science.

VEX: It is the title.

Mara brings a school group to the door. She tells Vex to show the mural, explain the light delay, and move on. Vex rehearses a dramatic voice. Pip tests the gears. The telescope turns, projecting the mural birds onto the ceiling.

ODO: The flock has escaped.

NARI: The ceiling is just a screen.

VEX: Please don't ruin the reveal before the children arrive.

SCENE 3. INT. PUBLIC GALLERY - NOON

The school group gathers. Vex pulls a curtain from the telescope. A bird shadow lands over the museum clock. The children gasp. Odo gasps louder.

VEX: Behold, a message from yesterday.

NARI: It is a mural.

VEX: Yesterday it was painted.

Pip adjusts the dial. The image slides across the wall to a painted monkey holding a paintbrush. A loose gear clicks. The telescope rotates and catches Mara's reflection.

MARA: Why am I on the wall?

PIP: You are standing in the beam.

The projected Mara raises her arms. The children copy her. Vex calls it a vision of the future. Nari blocks the lens with a folder; the image disappears.

ODO: The future has been canceled.

A child asks why the "yesterday" image includes the new velvet rope. Vex pivots the telescope. The rope's shadow appears beside the mural.

VEX: The museum has entered the past.

NARI: Or the rope is in the room.

Vex asks Pip to turn the telescope away from the rope. Pip turns the whole stand. The shadow now points to the exit sign, then to the museum shop.

SCENE 4. INT. MUSEUM SHOP - MOMENTS LATER

The school group follows the moving shadow. It crosses a rack of star maps and lands on a display of toy telescopes. Mara starts to usher everyone back, but the children form a line and aim the toys at the ceiling.

PIP: They are making their own yesterday.

NARI: They are pointing at the lamps.

Vex sees the shadow of the old mural on the shop's blank wall. He offers a paid "time portal" photo. Mara refuses. Odo stands in the beam again; his long ears become the "portal frame." The children ask him to hold still.

ODO: I have been promoted to architecture.

Vex starts charging admission in star stickers. The children give him stickers from their notebooks, covering the telescope label until only "YESTERDAY" remains. He tries to peel one off. It comes away with the word "VIEW."

SCENE 5. INT. PUBLIC GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Nari sets the telescope to project the actual mural. She invites the children to draw what they think the painter might have seen. Their pictures show Vex's dramatic shadow, Odo's ears, and Mara holding a folder over the lens.

MARA: Those are not historical records.

VEX: They are today's light, which becomes yesterday tomorrow.

NARI: I dislike how well that sells.

The museum hangs the children's drawings beside the old mural, labeled "Yesterday, as remembered today." Vex proudly turns the telescope toward the wall. His own shadow falls over the label and hides his name.

FINAL IMAGE: The telescope points at its inventor's silhouette while the museum shop sells star stickers of his shadow.
