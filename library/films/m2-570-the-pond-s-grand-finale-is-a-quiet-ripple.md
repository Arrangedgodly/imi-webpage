# The Pond's Grand Finale Is a Quiet Ripple

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: family adventure films that build toward a water spectacle and discover the pond is a habitat*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
HOST MARA, a macaque
DIRECTOR VEX, an orangutan
CAMERA OPERATOR PIP, a capuchin
RANGER NARI, a lemur
SOUND RECORDIST ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. POND - SUNSET

A small film crew sets up beside a pond. Vex points to a row of stones; Nari holds a shallow collection tray.

VEX: At the finale, we throw them in together. The ripple will fill the frame.

NARI: The pond has frogs and insects. Please don't throw stones into it.

PIP checks the camera tripod, which stands on the gravel path several steps from the water.

PIP: I can film the surface from the path without disturbing it.

ODO: The sound recorder is mine to operate. I can capture the frogs from here.

MARA: We need a finale that fits the place, not one that disrupts it.

VEX: The audience expects a climax.

SCENE 2. EXT. POND PATH - CONTINUOUS

Vex picks up a smooth pebble from the path. Nari extends the tray. Vex places the pebble directly into Nari's tray; she sets it on the path marker beside her, keeping one hand on the rim.

NARI: Thank you. We are clearing the path, not throwing stones.

VEX: The pebble is the instrument of destiny.

MARA: It was blocking the walkway. Keep the rest in the tray until we return them to the gravel border.

Pip frames a frog sitting on a floating leaf. Odo raises his separate sound recorder and checks its level.

PIP: Camera is rolling. The leaf is drifting toward the far bank.

ODO: Recorder is live. I have the frogs, insects, and park gate. No mechanical noise.

A breeze sends a small ripple across the near edge of the pond. Pip follows it with the camera. Odo records it as a preliminary ambient take.

ODO: Preliminary sound take complete. That's wind on the near edge, not our final leaf shot.

VEX: The natural effect has arrived early.

MARA: It is a small breeze. Save the final take for the leaf and frog together.

SCENE 3. EXT. POND PATH - SUNSET

A cyclist approaches along the path. Nari steps aside, still holding the tray. Pip stops camera recording. Odo stops the recorder; neither equipment operator is asked to restart the other's device.

NARI: Keep the path clear. We'll pause while the cyclist passes.

VEX: The finale has been interrupted.

MARA: We can wait. The pond isn't on a schedule.

The cyclist passes and nods. Nari walks the tray to the gravel border and returns the collected pebbles there. She comes back empty-handed and stands beside the path marker.

NARI: Path clear. Pebbles returned to the gravel edge, away from the water.

Pip checks focus and the tripod feet. Odo checks his sound level separately.

PIP: Camera steady on gravel. The leaf is still in frame.

ODO: Sound ready. I'll start only when you call the shot.

SCENE 3A. EXT. POND PATH - SUNSET

Pip shows Vex a still frame on the camera monitor. The floating leaf is a tiny green spot in the wide image; Vex puts his fingers around it as though framing a movie poster.

VEX: Can the frog be taller than the title?

PIP: On a poster, perhaps. In this shot, it is the size it is.

Odo offers headphones for a brief playback of the preliminary ambient take. Vex hears the park gate creak and points toward the water.

VEX: A final roar from the depths.

ODO: The gate behind us. I'll keep the final microphone pointed away from it.

Nari hangs the already-empty tray on the marker. Mara asks Vex to leave the monitor clear so Pip can watch for the falling leaf.

MARA: You can choose a title after we have the ending.

VEX: Then I shall wait for my inspiration.

He sits on the bench. It squeaks. Odo looks at him over the headphones, and Vex stands again without offering a title for that sound.

SCENE 4. EXT. POND - DUSK

Pip and Odo begin recording together on Mara's quiet hand signal. The crew waits. A fresh leaf drops from an overhanging branch into the water, making a ring of ripples. A frog croaks from its floating leaf.

PIP: Final shot rolling. Leaf falls; ripple crosses frame; frog stays on the leaf.

The running sound recorder captures the frog call and soft water movement at the same time. Odo watches the sound meter.

ODO: Final sound recorded. Levels are clear.

VEX: The pond has delivered the finale.

NARI: The pond continued being a pond. We didn't make it perform.

MARA: Hold the camera until the real leaf ripple fades, then cut.

Pip keeps the frame steady. The ripple dissipates. The frog remains.

PIP: Cut. That is the ending.

SCENE 5. EXT. PARK PATH - NIGHT

Pip powers down the camera and takes it off the tripod. Odo stops and packs his recorder. Nari checks the pebble tray is empty; it rests on its marked hook. Mara checks the path for equipment.

VEX: We have no explosion, no chase, no splash.

MARA: We have one clear shot and an undisturbed pond.

PIP: The film ends on the real ripple.

ODO: Camera and recorder packed separately. No equipment left by the water.

TAG: Vex takes one step away. A fish turns beneath the surface, making a small new ripple. He looks at Mara before claiming credit.
