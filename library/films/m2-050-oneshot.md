# One Take Through the Market

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: one-take action films, hidden cuts, and exhausting choreography*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A single uninterrupted camera shot follows a macaque courier across a crowded market as she tries to return a lost key before closing, while every obstacle insists on becoming part of the choreography.

Cast: Yara, a macaque courier; Fenn, a squirrel camera operator; Bolo, a capuchin street musician; Mrs. Otter, a shopkeeper; and a rolling cabbage that refuses to leave the frame.

SCENE 1. EXT. MARKET ENTRANCE, AFTERNOON

Fenn checks the camera battery. Yara holds a brass key. The shot begins. She enters the market. A vendor asks where she is going. She says the key must reach the locksmith before closing.

The camera follows. It nearly bumps a fruit cart. Yara catches one rolling apple and returns it. The vendor asks whether this is part of the shot. Fenn says it is now.

SCENE 2. INT. SPICE ROW, CONTINUOUS

Yara turns through a narrow aisle. Bolo plays a fast tune. The music speeds her steps. She slips on a spice packet, spins, and lands upright beside a sack of rice. The camera operator holds the shot, though the frame points at the floor for several seconds.

A cabbage rolls into the aisle. Yara steps over it. The cabbage follows.

SCENE 2A. INT. SPICE ROW, CONTINUOUS

A spice cart turns into the aisle. Yara circles it, keeping her pace. Fenn backs up without looking and bumps a tower of empty tins. They wobble but stay upright. Bolo changes keys to match the rhythm of the tins.

YARA: Is this still one take?

FENN: The camera hasn't blinked.

YARA: Cameras don't blink.

FENN: Mine has a battery light.

The vendor offers a shortcut through the flour stall. Yara declines because the white powder would cover the lens. The vendor says it is rice flour. Fenn says the distinction does not help his equipment.

SCENE 3. EXT. COVERED ARCADE, CONTINUOUS

A delivery cart blocks the path. Yara climbs over it, then realizes the camera cannot pass. Fenn ducks beneath the cart and continues filming from the other side. Yara hands him the key through the spokes. He hands it back, saying she is the courier.

The cabbage rolls down a ramp and bumps the camera. Fenn catches it without cutting. The market crowd applauds the cabbage.

SCENE 3A. EXT. COVERED ARCADE, CONTINUOUS

The delivery cart blocks the path. Its driver apologizes and starts to move it. The cart squeaks, the cabbage rolls after it, and the camera follows the wrong direction. Yara calls Fenn back. He pivots in a full circle, catching the cart, the cabbage, and Yara in frame.

A vendor asks whether the camera crew can film a tiny dance. Yara says she is on a delivery. The vendor starts dancing anyway. The crowd joins in, forming a moving lane. Yara walks through the middle at a steady pace while Fenn backs up. He asks the crowd to keep their elbows inside the shot.

SCENE 4. INT. LOCKSMITH SHOP, CONTINUOUS

The locksmith is closing. Yara reaches the counter and presents the key. It belongs to the shop's back door. The locksmith says he was looking for it. He asks why she ran. Yara says the camera was following her.

Fenn asks if they can stop. The locksmith says the key is returned, so the action is over. The shot remains unbroken. Yara turns toward the exit. The cabbage is waiting by the door.

SCENE 4A. INT. LOCKSMITH SHOP, CONTINUOUS

The locksmith examines the key and finds a paper tag tied to it. The tag says "back door," but the ink has run. He squints, then compares it with the lock. Yara asks if the key fits. The locksmith tries it. It turns.

FENN: That is the destination.

LOCKSMITH: That is the back door.

YARA: We could have walked around.

LOCKSMITH: It was locked.

The locksmith opens the door from inside. It leads to the same market aisle they just crossed. Fenn tries to turn around without cutting. He backs through the door, bumps a coat rack, and catches three keys on his sleeve. The locksmith removes them and returns them to the board, one by one. Yara waits while the crew completes the action.

SCENE 5. EXT. MARKET ENTRANCE, SUNSET

Yara walks back through the market at a normal pace. Bolo plays a slower tune. The vendors close their stalls. Fenn's arm shakes from holding the camera. Yara asks if the shot is still going. Fenn nods. The battery warning flashes.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera finally stops recording when the battery dies. Everyone freezes in place. The cabbage rolls past the lens and continues down the street, the only performer who never needed a reset.
