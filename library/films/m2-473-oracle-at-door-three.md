# The Oracle at Door Three

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quest films that encode simple directions as cryptic prophecy and make every doorway a trial*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A fellowship searches a maze-like palace for an oracle, decoding dramatic riddles that turn out to be the building's room signs.

Cast: Quest leader Miri, a macaque; bard Tolo, a gibbon; scout Rena, a capuchin; palace steward Omi, an orangutan; oracle Vasha, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. PALACE ENTRY - DAY

Miri unfolds a scroll. It reads: SEEK THE THIRD DOOR WHERE THE BLUE LAMP WAITS.

MIRI: The oracle has summoned us.

RENA: The reception desk says the oracle is in Room Three.

TOLO: The riddle must be deeper.

Omi approaches with a ring of keys.

OMI: The blue lamp is outside Door Three. Follow the signs.

Miri folds the scroll and leads the group down the corridor. The signs read ROOMS 1-5, arrows pointing left.

SCENE 2 - INT. PALACE CORRIDOR - MOMENTS LATER

They reach a fork. One hall has blue lamps, the other gold lamps. Tolo plays a low chord. Rena points to the sign reading ROOM THREE, LEFT.

TOLO: The blue lamp represents twilight.

MIRI: The scroll says blue, not gold.

RENA: The sign says left.

Miri chooses the gold corridor. They reach a storage room full of folding chairs. Omi unlocks the door and finds them there.

OMI: This is the banquet storage room.

MIRI: It is a hidden chamber.

OMI: It is labeled STORAGE.

SCENE 2A - INT. PALACE CORRIDOR - LATER

The fellowship returns to the fork. Miri holds the scroll beneath a wall sconce, searching for hidden writing. The paper casts a shadow shaped like a key.

MIRI: The key must open the final door.

RENA: It is the scroll folded over itself.

Tolo tries the key ring Omi left on a chair. It opens a broom closet. Inside are a mop, a bucket and a sign that reads PLEASE RETURN KEYS TO DESK.

TOLO: The closet is guarded by cleaning tools.

OMI: The tools are stored here. The key is for the closet.

Miri closes the door and places the key ring back on the chair. Rena notices that the room numbers increase from one to five as they walk left.

RENA: If Door Three is ahead, we need two more steps.

MIRI: Two trials.

They take two steps. Door Three is directly ahead. A blue lamp glows beside it. Tolo waits for the wall to speak. Nothing happens.

TOLO: The oracle is silent.

RENA: The door has a number. We can knock.

Miri knocks. Vasha opens the door from inside and looks at the fellowship, still standing in the hall.

VASHA: Your appointment is in this room.

SCENE 2B - INT. PALACE CORRIDOR - CONTINUOUS

Omi points to the appointment card. Miri refuses to enter until the fellowship completes a ceremonial test. She asks Tolo to announce each step in a solemn voice. Tolo draws a breath and starts a bass note. The corridor's motion sensor switches on a row of lamps one by one, making the party think a procession is following them.

TOLO: The palace acknowledges our arrival.

RENA: It acknowledges movement.

Miri walks backward to test it. The lights go dark behind her, then blink back on as she turns forward. Omi, carrying a box of replacement bulbs, walks past without reacting.

OMI: The sensor is set too low. It catches capes and scrolls.

The lamps flicker every time Tolo's bardic cloak swings. He pins the cloak to his belt. The lights remain steady. Miri looks disappointed that the prophecy has been reduced to fabric management.

MIRI: Could the oracle still be testing us?

OMI: She asked me to bring the appointment cards. She is waiting.

The group enters the lobby. Miri attempts to stamp the scroll at the reception desk, but the date stamp leaves a purple mark across the prophecy. She holds it up as a sign.

RENA: That is the office stamp.

SCENE 3 - INT. PALACE LOBBY - LATER

Vasha, the oracle, sits beside the reception desk, sorting appointment cards. The fellowship searches behind a tapestry. Rena reads the room signs aloud.

RENA: One, two, three. This is Door Three.

MIRI: The blue lamp waits here.

Vasha looks up. A blue lamp is mounted beside the room number.

VASHA: You have found my office.

TOLO: We expected a more mystical entrance.

VASHA: The palace lights are practical. They help people find the rooms.

SCENE 4 - INT. ORACLE'S OFFICE - CONTINUOUS

The fellowship sits before Vasha. She reads the scroll and turns it over. On the back is a reception stamp with the appointment time.

VASHA: You were expected at two. You are an hour late.

MIRI: We crossed a chair-filled chamber and two lamp-lit halls.

OMI: You took the wrong corridor and went to storage.

Vasha offers them tea. Tolo interprets the steam as a sign. Vasha hands him a napkin.

VASHA: The steam means the tea is hot. Wait before drinking.

Miri asks the oracle about the future. Vasha opens a printed calendar.

VASHA: The city council will meet on Friday. That is the future I can confirm.

SCENE 5 - INT. PALACE ENTRY - AFTERNOON

The fellowship leaves by the correct corridor. Omi checks that the doors close behind them. Miri adds a note to the scroll: ROOM THREE, LEFT, BLUE LAMP. Tolo adds a small drawing of a teacup.

TOLO: The quest has given us knowledge.

RENA: It has given us a room number.

MIRI: A room number is useful knowledge.

Vasha returns to sorting appointment cards. The blue lamp remains lit beside the doorway. The fellowship stops at the reception desk to book a new time rather than search again. Omi hands them a printed ticket with the room number.

FINAL IMAGE: The scroll is pinned beneath the palace directory, with its riddle now paired beside an ordinary floor map and an arrow to Door Three.
