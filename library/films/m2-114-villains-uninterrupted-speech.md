# The Villain's Uninterrupted Speech

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action-film villains who deliver long monologues while heroes wait politely instead of solving the problem*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A theatrical villain tries to explain his elaborate plan, but the hero keeps noticing simple ways to stop it before the speech ends.

Cast: Baron Vex, a baboon villain; Kiri, a lemur hero; Toma, a tortoise technician; and Puck, a capuchin director.

SCENE 1. INT. CLOCK TOWER, EVENING

Baron Vex stands beside a large clock mechanism. Kiri is tied to a chair with a loose scarf. Puck calls action.

VEX: At midnight, every clock in the city will strike thirteen.

KIRI: How?

VEX: The mechanism is connected to every bell tower.

Kiri slips one hand free. Vex does not notice. Toma enters carrying a tool bag and asks if the clock is supposed to be running. Vex tells him not to interrupt the monologue.

TOMA: Is the clock supposed to be running?

VEX: It is part of the plan.

KIRI: The plan looks like a belt slipping.

Toma sees the loose guard and asks everyone to stand clear. Vex calls the guard a symbol of his power. Toma says it is a safety cover. Puck tells Kiri to hold her position until the monologue ends. She works one hand free while Vex takes a breath.

SCENE 2. INT. CLOCK TOWER, CONTINUOUS

Vex explains the history of his grievance. Kiri unties the other wrist and moves the chair away from the gear housing. Toma spots an exposed belt.

TOMA: That belt is frayed.

VEX: It is a symbol of my resentment.

TOMA: It is a belt.

Puck tells Kiri to wait for the cue. She asks whether the cue comes before or after midnight. Vex pauses to sip water. Kiri turns the master switch to STOP. The clock halts.

VEX: You cannot stop the plan. It is already in motion.

TOMA: The motor is off.

Vex points to a backup crank. Toma steps between him and the crank, asking the villain to keep clear while he checks the guard. Kiri calls the city dispatcher and tells them the clock is stopped.

KIRI: The mechanism is off, and the bells are quiet.

DISPATCHER (OVER PHONE): Keep clear until inspection.

Vex asks her to wait for the revelation. Kiri says she heard the plan and will not wait. Toma locks the motor cabinet and attaches a repair tag. Vex reaches toward the backup crank. Toma steps in front of it and asks him to move back. Vex returns to his speech. Puck asks whether they should continue the scene.

PUCK: Do we cut to the hero escaping?

VEX: She has not escaped. She has left.

Toma points to the open door. Kiri returns briefly to retrieve her map, which Vex has been standing on. She pulls it free while he tries to continue speaking. The map unfolds across the floor, covering his cue cards. Toma gathers the papers so no one slips, and Vex asks if this is part of the plot. Puck says it is part of the blocking.

SCENE 3. INT. CLOCK TOWER, LATER

Vex continues his speech where it was interrupted. Kiri rolls the chair toward the exit while he describes the thirteen bells. Toma tags the frayed belt and locks the motor cabinet. Vex turns and finds the room empty except for Puck.

VEX: Where is the hero?

PUCK: She left during your backstory.

Vex looks at the script. The villain is supposed to activate the mechanism after the speech, but the switch is locked. Toma tells him the maintenance crew will replace the belt tomorrow. Vex asks if he can monologue to the clock. Toma says the clock cannot hear him. Vex asks if the clock can at least serve as a witness. Toma points to the service log. Kiri has already signed the incident note and left for the dispatch office. Vex tries to start the speech again, but the room is quiet without the mechanism.

SCENE 4. INT. CLOCK TOWER, NIGHT

Kiri returns with a city inspector. They confirm the motor is off and the bells are silent. Vex asks if he may finish his speech for the record. The inspector says he can make a statement after the equipment report.

INSPECTOR: First we document the stopped motor.

VEX: My speech has a dramatic pause.

KIRI: So did the switch.

Puck films the revised ending. Vex sees Kiri by the open door and begins to speak. She leaves. He follows her into the hall, still explaining the first paragraph.

Puck films the revised ending. Vex walks to the clock, sees Kiri by the open door, and begins to speak. She leaves. He follows her.

FINAL IMAGE: The clock shows 11:42. Vex's speech is still on the table, folded beneath the maintenance tag.
