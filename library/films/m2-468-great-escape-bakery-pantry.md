# The Great Escape from the Bakery Pantry

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prison-break thrillers that build elaborate escape plans around an ordinary locked storeroom*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: Three monkeys locked in a bakery pantry map an elaborate escape tunnel, then discover the latch opens from the inside and the baker is waiting outside.

Cast: planner Roto, a macaque; lookout Nemi, a capuchin; mechanic Pali, a lemur; baker Soro, a gibbon; delivery driver Tavi, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. BAKERY PANTRY - NIGHT

A door clicks shut behind Roto, Nemi and Pali. Shelves of flour and baking trays surround them. A small window sits high on the wall.

ROTO: We are locked in.

NEMI: The handle is on this side.

PALI: There is a latch above it. We should check before digging.

Roto unfolds a map drawn on a napkin. It shows a tunnel leading to the loading dock.

ROTO: We escape through the wall, then cross the loading yard.

PALI: The wall is brick. The door is wood.

SCENE 2 - INT. PANTRY - LATER

Nemi uses a spoon to scratch a tunnel route into a flour sack. Pali examines the door hinges. Roto stacks baking trays into a ladder toward the window.

NEMI: The guard is approaching.

Footsteps sound outside. The door handle turns. The three hide behind flour bags. The door does not open. The baker is trying the other side.

SORO (O.S.): Are you in there? The latch stuck again.

ROTO: It is a trick to make us reveal ourselves.

PALI: He said the latch is stuck. I can lift it from inside.

SCENE 2A - INT. PANTRY - LATER

Roto sketches the loading dock route on the wall using a pencil nub. Nemi taps the wall with a tray, listening for a hollow space. Pali reads the labels on the shelves.

PALI: This wall backs onto the mixer room. We would break into the busiest part of the bakery.

ROTO: We need a distraction.

NEMI: The flour sack can make a decoy footprint.

Pali picks up the sack, but flour pours from a small tear. A trail leads directly to the door.

PALI: The footprints are ours. We came in through the door.

Roto erases the map and draws a ladder to the high window. Nemi stacks two trays, then climbs. The top tray slides. Pali catches it before it falls.

PALI: The window is too small for any of us.

ROTO: The narrowest member could fit.

NEMI: I am not volunteering to crawl through a bakery window.

The steps outside stop. Someone knocks three times. The monkeys freeze.

TAVI (O.S.): Delivery driver. I need the flour sack on the cart.

PALI: There is no guard. There is a delivery.

SCENE 3 - INT. PANTRY - CONTINUOUS

Pali reaches for the latch. Roto stops him and points to the window. Nemi climbs the tray ladder. It wobbles, and a baking tray slides down, clanging on the floor.

TAVI (O.S.): Is everything all right?

NEMI: We are testing the tunnel.

PALI: We are in the pantry. The baker is right outside.

Roto unrolls a second map. It shows an elaborate route past the freezer, around the mixer and out through the delivery entrance.

ROTO: We must avoid the flour alarm.

PALI: There is no flour alarm.

SCENE 3A - INT. PANTRY - CONTINUOUS

Nemi looks through the high window. The yard beyond is blocked by stacked crates. A small vent fan turns slowly.

NEMI: The yard is closed. There is no way out that direction.

ROTO: The vent is our last route.

PALI: It is an exhaust fan. We should not climb inside it.

Roto tries to insert the napkin map into the vent. It catches on the grille and falls back onto his head.

ROTO: The map has failed.

PALI: The map is a drawing. We can open the door when the bin moves.

Nemi reaches for the latch again, but the flour bin presses against the door. The handle moves a little and stops.

NEMI: There is a real obstruction.

PALI: We push together while Soro pulls. No tunnel, no window, no fan.

SCENE 4 - INT. PANTRY - MOMENTS LATER

Pali lifts the latch. The door opens a few inches, but a flour bin has been pushed against it from the hallway. Roto and Nemi push from inside while Soro pulls from the other side. The door opens. Flour spills in a soft cloud.

SORO: I put the bin there while loading bread. I thought the pantry was empty.

ROTO: The obstacle has shifted.

TAVI: The delivery is waiting. We need the shelves cleared.

Pali brushes flour from his sleeves. Nemi lowers the tray ladder. Roto folds the maps and puts them in his pocket.

SCENE 5 - INT. BAKERY WORKROOM - MORNING

The monkeys help move the flour bin away from the pantry door. Soro adds a sign: KEEP DOOR CLEAR. The crew checks the latch and opens it from both sides. It works normally.

ROTO: We escaped through a door.

NEMI: A very ordinary door.

PALI: The tunnel can stay on the napkin. It is useful for a story.

Soro offers them a tray of rolls. Roto asks for a map of the bakery instead. Tavi points to the posted floor plan, which marks the pantry, mixer, loading dock and exits.

FINAL IMAGE: The pantry door stands open with the latch visible, while the flour-covered escape map hangs beside the clear door sign.
