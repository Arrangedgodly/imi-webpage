# Cue the Crate Chorus

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: backstage musical films, show-must-go-on crises, and theatrical prop mishaps*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A stage manager monkey tries to keep a touring musical on schedule after the star loses her voice, the scenery gets stuck, and the chorus discovers the crates have better rhythm than the orchestra.

Cast: Rilla, a meticulous macaque stage manager; Bebe, a lemur leading singer with a sore throat; Jojo, a capuchin carpenter; Mr. Plume, a peacock producer; and the chorus of working monkeys.

SCENE 1. INT. OLD THEATER, AFTERNOON

Rilla checks the cue sheet. Bebe warms up with a whisper. The producer says opening night cannot be delayed. Jojo tests the scenery. A painted castle wall refuses to move because one wheel is jammed.

RILLA: We can fix the wheel.

PLUME: We can also call it a design choice.

SCENE 2. INT. DRESSING ROOM, EVENING

Bebe loses her voice completely. The producer panics. The understudy is stuck on a train. Rilla asks if anyone knows the lyrics. The chorus knows every line but refuses to sing without the orchestra.

Jojo taps a rhythm on a wooden crate. The chorus joins in. Bebe can conduct with gestures. Rilla rewrites the entrances on the cue sheet, using colored pencil.

SCENE 2A. INT. ORCHESTRA PIT, EVENING

The conductor arrives late, carrying a sandwich and the wrong score. He starts the overture in a different key. The musicians follow for four bars, then stop. Rilla checks the cue sheet and signals a reset with a flashlight.

The producer asks whether they can call the wrong key "modern." Rilla says the cast has already learned the original. The conductor sets down his sandwich, finds the correct score, and apologizes to the woodwinds. They ask him to keep the sandwich away from the instruments.

SCENE 3. INT. WINGS, NIGHT

The show begins. The orchestra misses a cue. The crate chorus keeps time. The stuck castle wall refuses to move during the big reveal. The actors improvise around it, pretending the castle has grown a new wing.

Plume whispers to Rilla that the audience may notice. Rilla says the audience is laughing. Plume asks whether they are laughing at the show. Rilla says it counts.

SCENE 3A. INT. PROP ROOM, LATER

Jojo searches for a spare wheel. He finds a cardboard moon, a papier-mache horse, and the missing castle window. The window has been used to prop open the door. Jojo installs a wheel on the castle wall, but it rolls backward into a rack of hats.

The chorus helps return every hat to its box. One hat is still on Plume's head. He insists it is part of his producer costume. Rilla says it is a teapot cozy. Plume removes it carefully and asks that nobody mention the incident in the program.

SCENE 4. ONSTAGE, LATER

Bebe conducts the chorus from a balcony. Her gestures become more expressive than the original singing. The lead dancer misreads one signal and exits through the wrong door. He returns carrying a prop roast. The cast folds him into the scene as a visiting duke.

The final number begins. The crate beat accelerates. Jojo tries to slow it down, but the chorus claps faster. The cast dances wildly around the immovable castle wall. Bebe waves both hands. The music stops at once.

SCENE 4A. ONSTAGE, FINAL NUMBER

Bebe conducts the cast from the balcony. The dancers follow her hands. When she raises both arms, they sing. When she lowers them, the crate beat stops. One actor misses the cue and continues tapping. The cast begins dancing again, then notices Bebe has not moved. They freeze in a line.

The audience waits. Bebe coughs once. The cast resumes the number at half speed. The orchestra joins. The finale lands on a quiet chord, followed by a loud crate wobble. The audience applauds the wobble most.

The producer proposes adding three more acts to the encore. Rilla checks the schedule. There is time for one bow and a fire inspection. Plume asks if the inspection can sing. Rilla says only if it has clearance.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY, AFTER THE SHOW

The audience applauds. Plume announces a second performance with the same "innovative staging." Rilla says the wheel will be repaired before then. The chorus asks to keep the crate rhythm. Bebe gives a silent bow and points to a sign asking for warm tea.

SCENE 6. INT. THEATER, AFTERNOON

RILLA: The wheel is fixed.

JOJO: The wall rolls.

PLUME: Can it roll on cue?

RILLA: Yes.

BEBE: I can sing again.

PLUME: Excellent.

BEBE: Quietly.

PLUME: Still excellent.

JOJO: The crate has a chord.

RILLA: Do not hit it.

JOJO: It rings if the wall bumps it.

PLUME: That's the finale.

RILLA: That's an accident.

PLUME: The audience liked it.

BEBE: They liked the show.

CHORUS: They liked the crates.

RILLA: The score says orchestra.

JOJO: The crates are percussion.

PLUME: Add them.

RILLA: After inspection.

PLUME: Can it inspect the rhythm?

RILLA: No.

BEBE: We can rehearse.

JOJO: Which wall?

RILLA: The repaired one.

PLUME: Bring the castle.

RILLA: Not until the crew clears the stage.

CHORUS: We'll clear it.

JOJO: Carefully.

PLUME: Dramatically.

RILLA: Quietly.

BEBE: In tune.

The cast moves the scenery. The wall rolls smoothly. Everyone applauds. The crate makes no sound. Plume looks disappointed and taps it with one finger.

RILLA: Places, everyone.
BEBE: Voice is ready.
JOJO: Wheel is ready.
PLUME: Is the crate ready?
RILLA: The crate is a crate.
CHORUS: We know the cue.

FINAL IMAGE: Jojo fixes the castle wheel. The wall rolls across the stage and bumps the crate stack. The crates sound one perfect chord. Plume tells Rilla to write it into the score. Rilla adds a cue: "Castle hits crates. Do not repeat unless accidental."
