# The Canyon Has a Quiet Warning

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster adventures, rescue-team montages, countdowns, and tour guides who ignore small warnings until they become the whole plot*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
GUIDE LUMA, a lemur
RANGER ODO, a capuchin
FILMMAKER VEX, a macaque
VISITOR PIP, a squirrel monkey
GEOLOGIST NARI, an orangutan

SCENE 1. EXT. REDSTONE CANYON - MORNING

A film crew arrives at a canyon overlook. Vex wants a sweeping shot with a drone. Ranger Odo points to a posted closure around a cracked section of the rim. Luma leads a group to the safe viewing path. Pip notices small stones falling from the ledge.

PIP: Is that normal?

NARI: It is a warning sign.

Vex films the falling stones and calls it a dramatic opening. Odo asks him to lower the drone; wind near the edge is unpredictable.

SCENE 2. EXT. CANYON TRAIL - LATER

Nari studies the crack and says the rain last night loosened the rim. Odo closes the nearby trail and asks Luma to turn visitors back. Vex argues the crew needs one more shot. The drone lifts, catches a gust, and lands in a shrub beyond the barrier. Vex reaches toward it. Odo stops him before he crosses the closed spur.

The group hears a rumble. A narrow strip of rock shifts at the edge and drops into the canyon. Dust rises below. Pip asks if the rest of the overlook will fall. Nari says the crack has not moved toward the path, but no one should cross the barrier.

SCENE 2A. EXT. CANYON OVERLOOK - MIDDAY

Odo brings a portable barrier and closes the narrow spur. A visitor asks if the whole canyon is shutting down. Luma explains that the west overlook and visitor center remain open. Vex turns on his camera and asks them to repeat the line as a dramatic safety briefing. Nari speaks into the lens and describes the crack, the falling stones, and the alternate route. Vex inserts a low boom under her words. The sound is his own boot stepping on a hollow board.

PIP: The ground is talking.

NARI: It is the board.

They remove the loose board and mark the edge of the path. Odo asks Vex to move the drone case away from the barrier. Vex says it is a prop. Odo says it is equipment, and the distinction does not make it safer. The crew carries it to the ranger station.

SCENE 3. INT. RANGER STATION - AFTERNOON

Odo radios the geology team. The signal is weak, so Vex climbs onto a chair to hold the antenna higher. The chair wobbles. Luma steadies it and tells him to use the wall jack instead. He plugs in the radio and reaches the team. Nari reports that the rim is unstable and asks them to mark the closure on the county map.

Vex holds up the drone controller. Its live view shows one shrub at a heroic angle.

VEX: There is our survivor. We need a rescue montage.

ODO: The camera can wait until next week.

PIP: We could rescue its luggage.

Pip lifts the empty drone case. Vex orders him to carry it on both shoulders. Luma opens the station door for their solemn evacuation. The case bumps the doorframe; Vex requests a second take. Odo keeps the door open and points them straight through. The drone remains beyond the barrier. The crew sets up a camera behind the barrier to document the rock movement from a safe distance.

SCENE 4. EXT. SAFE OVERLOOK - SUNSET

The geology team arrives and checks the crack. They place markers on the path and close the section until repairs can be made. Odo asks visitors to use the west overlook. Luma leads them along the path. Pip reads the new sign aloud: the canyon is open, but one ledge is not.

The geology team asks visitors to stay on the marked path while they place the red markers. Vex tries to step across the rope to frame a closer view. Odo holds the rope at waist height and blocks his route. Vex changes to a wide angle, which captures the entire closure and the safe viewing area.

Vex films the group walking away from the edge. His wide shot shows the sign, the barrier, and the sunset. He asks if he can narrate it as a narrow escape. Nari says it was a careful closure. Vex tries the line with less urgency. Odo tells him the drone can be retrieved next week.

SCENE 5. EXT. RANGER STATION - EVENING

Vex edits the footage. The dramatic rumble is only wind hitting the microphone, so he substitutes the thud of the empty case against the doorframe. He writes DRONE LEFT BEHIND across his poster.

ODO: Put the retrieval date on that.

VEX: A sequel announcement? Already?

Odo stamps RETRIEVAL MONDAY beneath the title. Vex adds a countdown of seven days, then asks Pip to pose with the rescued case. Pip opens it toward the lens.

PIP: Your star has not arrived yet.

Vex lowers the lid halfway, trying to make the empty foam compartment look mysterious. Pip signs the visitor log. Luma checks that the barrier is latched. Odo updates the trail map and posts it inside the station. He underlines the safe overlook in green and dates the closure notice.

FINAL IMAGE: The poster promises a rescue in seven days; beneath it, Pip carries the empty case out for its cast photograph.
