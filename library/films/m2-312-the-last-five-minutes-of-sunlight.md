# The Last Five Minutes of Sunlight

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: golden-hour cinematography, sunset schedules, and productions that move the story to chase a flattering beam*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
CINEMATOGRAPHER PIP, a capuchin
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR ODO, a baboon
ACTOR LUMA, a lemur
PRODUCTION ASSISTANT VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. EXT. HILLTOP SET - AFTERNOON

Cinematographer Pip points toward a patch of warm sunlight on the hill. Director Nari checks the schedule. Odo and Luma stand beside a small wooden gate. A lantern hangs from its post.

PIP: We have five minutes of golden light.

NARI: The scene takes seven.

PIP: Then we have two minutes of shade.

The scene is a meeting at the gate. Odo must hand Luma a key; she will decide whether to open it. Vex has put orange cloth over the camera monitor to preview the light.

LUMA: Is the gate open?

ODO: It is latched.

NARI: That is not the dialogue.

VEX: It is the practical condition.

Pip adjusts the camera. The sun passes behind a cloud. He asks everyone to hold. The actors wait in silence while Vex moves the orange cloth.

SCENE 2. EXT. HILLTOP SET - LATER

The cloud clears. Pip calls for the take. Odo starts his line. Luma turns toward the gate. The sunlight lands directly in her eyes.

LUMA: I cannot see the key.

PIP: The light is beautiful.

NARI: The light has blinded the actor.

Vex carries a reflector to bounce light onto Luma's face. The reflector catches the sun and flashes into Odo's eyes. He drops the key into the grass. The crew searches while the golden light moves toward the ridge.

ODO: We should have put a mark where it fell.

VEX: We had no time.

LUMA: We spent the time looking at the sun.

Pip checks the grass with the camera. A small glint appears beside the gate's post. Vex crawls under the reflector and retrieves the key.

SCENE 2A. EXT. HILLTOP SET - LATE AFTERNOON

Vex runs a reflector stand into position. Its leg sinks into the soft ground, tilting the panel toward the actors. Pip asks everyone to wait while he anchors it with sandbags. The sunlight moves to the ridge as they secure the stand.

PIP: We have three minutes now.

NARI: The actors need two.

VEX: The reflector needs a hero.

Odo takes the reflector's handle and steadies it. The wind catches the panel like a sail. Luma holds the opposite edge. A bright stripe crosses the gate, but Odo's shadow cuts it in half. Pip removes the reflector instead of asking them to chase the beam. They turn the camera and use the light that remains on the lantern.

SCENE 3. EXT. HILLTOP SET - SUNSET

Nari shortens the scene. Odo hands over the key; Luma opens the gate. Pip sets the frame. The sunlight turns the actors orange. Luma cannot make out Odo's face.

LUMA: The gate is glowing.

ODO: I am standing beside it.

VEX: Can we shoot the close-up from the other side?

PIP: The sun will be behind the hill.

Nari asks for a test. They rotate the gate, but the lantern now hangs on the wrong side. Vex moves it; its shadow appears on the ground. Odo's shadow points away from Luma, making the two seem to be facing different directions.

Pip asks the actors to switch places. Now Luma blocks the sun from Odo. Vex holds a reflector. It tips and catches on the grass. The camera records its round shadow growing across the set like a second sunset.

SCENE 4. EXT. HILLTOP SET - DUSK

The light fades. Pip lowers the orange cloth. Nari asks if they should wait for another day. Luma suggests using the lantern as the scene's light source. Odo lights it with a safe battery insert. Its small warm glow falls on the key, not the whole hill.

Pip reframes low, with the dark ridge behind them. The key catches the lantern glow. Luma sees Odo clearly. He hands her the key; she chooses to open the gate. The scene takes less than a minute.

PIP: We have lost the sun.

NARI: We found the light.

Vex removes the orange cloth from the monitor. The image looks natural without it. Pip keeps the shot.

SCENE 5. INT. EDIT BAY - NIGHT

They watch the scene. The opening wide shot has sunlight on the hill. The final close shot uses the lantern. Vex asks if the lighting changes are continuous. Pip says the story moves from late afternoon to dusk. Luma says it is the same time as the schedule, only with a better source.

FINAL IMAGE: The tiny lantern glows beside the gate while the orange cloth lies folded beneath the monitor.
