# The Rental Boat's Quiet Hour

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: travel films that insist a short lake trip must become a transformational voyage*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
GUIDE NARI, a lemur
VISITOR VEX, an orangutan
BOAT ATTENDANT MARA, a macaque
ROWER PIP, a capuchin
DOCK KEEPER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. LAKE DOCK - MORNING

Mara ties a small rental boat to the dock. Vex arrives with a large pack and a folded map.

VEX: I seek the far shore and the meaning of water.

MARA: The rental is for one hour. The marked route stays inside the buoys.

NARI: The far shore is closed for nesting birds.

PIP: I can row around the near cove and return to this dock.

ODO: Life vests are on the rack. Choose one that fits and fasten it before boarding.

VEX: My journey begins.

SCENE 2. EXT. LAKE - CONTINUOUS

Vex rows hard on one side. The boat spins in a slow circle.

PIP: Use even strokes on both sides. I can help from the other oar.

VEX: The lake resists my passage.

NARI: The boat is turning because only one oar is moving.

MARA: Keep the boat inside the marked lane. The wind is light from the dock.

Pip rows once on the left; Vex matches on the right. The boat moves forward.

SCENE 2A. EXT. LAKE - MORNING

The boat passes the inner buoy. Nari points to the nesting marker, a small orange float.

NARI: Stop rowing for a moment. We are far enough from shore to watch without closing the distance.

PIP: Oars flat. Boat drifting slowly.

VEX: The far shore is calling.

MARA: The far shore is closed to protect the nests. The rental map shows a circle around this cove.

ODO: Rental time is forty minutes in. We have twenty minutes to return and tie up.

VEX: Time is a current of its own.

PIP: It is the return deadline. Let's turn now while the wind is light.

Vex pulls hard on one oar. The boat begins to spin.

NARI: Equal strokes, both sides. We can turn the bow without crossing the buoy line.

Pip demonstrates a short stroke. Vex follows at the same rhythm. The boat pivots.

MARA: That's right. Keep the oars inside the boat when we pass the marker.

ODO: The route is clear. No other rental boats in the cove.

The boat moves back toward the dock. Vex lowers the map and watches the water instead.

SCENE 2B. EXT. LAKE - CONTINUOUS

A small wave bumps the hull. Vex grabs the gunwale. Mara tells him to keep his hands on the handle inside the boat.

MARA: Don't lean over. Sit centered and let the hull settle.

VEX: The lake has challenged me.

PIP: It is a wave from a passing rowboat. The other crew is staying in their lane.

NARI: We'll hold our line and keep our distance. No need to wave them over.

Vex sits. Pip rows. The rental boat passes the orange buoy with space between them.

VEX: I can see the nesting shore from here.

NARI: That's enough. We should keep moving back to the dock.

SCENE 2C. EXT. LAKE - CONTINUOUS

The dock comes into view. Mara points to the red return buoy.

MARA: We have ten minutes before the rental ends. Keep the boat inside the lane and approach the dock at a shallow angle.

PIP: Oars out of the water. I'll coast the last few feet.

ODO: Dock attendant has the line ready.

VEX: Our voyage nears its conclusion.

NARI: Please keep your hands inside the boat until it stops.

The hull touches the padded edge. Mara catches the line and ties it to the cleat.

MARA: Boat secure. Now step onto the dock, one at a time.

VEX: I have returned changed.

PIP: You have returned on time.

SCENE 3. EXT. COVE - LATER

The boat reaches the inner cove. A family of birds rests along the closed shore. Nari points from the dock.

NARI: Stay beyond the buoy. We can view them from the water without approaching.

VEX: A sign from nature.

PIP: A nesting area. We should keep quiet and turn back.

Vex lowers his voice and dips the oar slowly. The boat turns toward the dock.

SCENE 4. EXT. LAKE DOCK - MORNING

The boat returns. Mara holds the line while Pip ties it to the cleat. Odo checks the vest straps.

ODO: Boat returned at 9:42. One hour rental ends at ten.

VEX: I have crossed the lake.

NARI: You rowed around the near cove and returned.

VEX: I understand the water now.

MARA: You understand the route and the nesting boundary. That's useful.

TAG: Vex unfolds his map. It is the dock rental agreement, not a map of the lake.
