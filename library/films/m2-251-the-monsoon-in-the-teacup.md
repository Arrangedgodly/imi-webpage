# The Monsoon in the Teacup

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster films, where a spill in a teacup becomes a citywide flood emergency*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A tea spill on the archive table triggers a crisis response, and the team must save a miniature map before the flood reaches its paper shore.

Cast: Director Varo, baboon; archivist Mina, macaque; engineer Tavi, gibbon; messenger Piri, capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. ARCHIVE, MORNING

A teacup tips. Tea spills across a table holding a small paper map of the village.

[SFX: CUP TIPS, LIQUID SPREADS.]

VARO: The lower district is flooding.

MINA: It is one table.

VARO: The map is in danger.

TAVI: The cup is empty.

PIRI: The tea has reached the river line.

Varo grabs a towel. Mina stops him.

MINA: If you wipe across the map, the ink will run.

VARO: Then we need a rescue plan.

TAVI: We need a dry corner.

Varo points to the map's tiny bridge.

VARO: The bridge is isolated.

MINA: The bridge is printed on paper.

PIRI: The tea is moving toward it.

SCENE 2. INT. ARCHIVE, CONTINUOUS

Tavi props up one edge of the table with a book. The tea flows away from the map, toward the corner.

[SFX: BOOK SLIDES, TABLE CREAKS.]

VARO: We have redirected the flood.

MINA: You have tilted the table.

PIRI: The village square is now uphill.

Mina lifts the map by its dry corners. One edge begins to curl. Piri holds a ruler underneath like a stretcher.

PIRI: The map has lost its shoreline.

MINA: The shoreline is still there.

VARO: We need to evacuate the map.

TAVI: The map is already off the wet area.

Varo calls for a bucket. Tavi brings a small wastebasket. Varo places it under the table, but the table no longer drips.

VARO: The water has stopped.

MINA: The cup is empty.

PIRI: We have contained the source.

SCENE 3. INT. HALLWAY, MOMENTS LATER

Mina carries the map to a drying rack. Varo marches beside her with a towel like a rescue banner. The corridor is clear, but Varo asks everyone to move back.

VARO: Make a path for the emergency team.

TAVI: The emergency team is carrying a map.

A janitor opens a window. A breeze lifts the map. Piri catches it before it lands.

PIRI: Air current approaching.

MINA: Hold the corners, not the center.

They secure the map to the rack with soft clips. Varo asks if the village is safe.

MINA: The ink is intact.

VARO: And the bridge?

MINA: It is dry.

TAVI: The paper bridge is safe.

Varo asks Tavi to inspect the cup. It is cracked along the handle.

TAVI: The cup caused the spill. We should replace it.

VARO: The disaster began with a structural failure.

MINA: It began with a cup tipping.

SCENE 4. INT. ARCHIVE, LATER

The map is dry. The team returns it to the table. Varo sets a saucer beneath the teacup and marks a safety boundary around the map.

MINA: The map needs room to unfold.

VARO: The boundary will keep cups away.

PIRI: The boundary is too close to the edge.

TAVI: Move the cup to the shelf.

Varo moves it. The saucer stays on the map table.

VARO: We have separated the beverage zone from the village.

Mina finds a tea stain on the blank margin. She suggests using it to mark where a new stream should be added to the map.

VARO: That is not an approved river.

MINA: It is a stain.

PIRI: The map has expanded.

TAVI: We can trim the edge.

Mina carefully cuts the damp margin, then redraws a clean border. The village remains unchanged.

SCENE 5. INT. ARCHIVE, AFTERNOON

Varo sets the cup on the stable shelf, well away from the map. Mina smooths the map flat while Tavi removes the damp wastebasket from beneath the dry table.

VARO: The village is secure.

MINA: It is a map.

PIRI: The cup is on the shelf.

Mina unfolds the map and checks the ink under the lamp. Varo leans over the table, then steps back when a drop falls from his sleeve.

VARO: A new leak.

MINA: Your sleeve is wet from the mop.

TAVI: The archive has survived the storm and the inspector.

Varo dries his sleeve with the towel and asks Mina to turn the map so the bridge faces the audience. She does, and the tea stain now resembles a tiny island.

VARO: An island has appeared.

MINA: It is still a stain.


Varo lays a strip of red tape around the shelf. It circles the cup, the spare cups, and one empty saucer.

TAVI: You have marked the beverage zone.

VARO: A perimeter.

MINA: The emergency is over.

VARO: Then this is the first perimeter in the archive to hold nothing but tea.

Piri pours a small cup. The surface trembles, then settles. Varo watches it for a long moment before taking a sip.

[SFX: SMALL SIP, ARCHIVE ROOM TONE.]
