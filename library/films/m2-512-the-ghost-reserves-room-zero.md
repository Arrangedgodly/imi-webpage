# The Ghost Reserves Room Zero

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-hotel mysteries with a skeptical renovation crew, ominous room service, and a ghost who follows house rules*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: MARA, a gibbon hotel manager; PIP, a capuchin electrician; ODO, a baboon night clerk; LADY HUSH, an orangutan ghost; TAVI, a macaque travel critic; MR. BELL, a mandrill inspector.

INT. HUSH HOUSE HOTEL - NIGHT

A chandelier sways over an empty lobby. The front desk bell rings by itself. Odo jumps, then rings it again to prove the bell is real.

ODO: It rings when touched.

MARA: The guest book says Room Zero is occupied.

PIP: This hotel has rooms one through twelve.

MARA: The guest did not specify a number.

A door at the back of the lobby opens. A cold draft moves through the room and lifts the page of the guest book. Tavi arrives with a small suitcase.

TAVI: I booked Room Eight. The review said the plumbing sings.

MARA: It sings when the pipes knock.

PIP: The pipes knock when the boiler starts.

The front desk bell rings once.

ODO: Someone is checking in.

A pale figure appears at the top of the staircase, holding a silver room key.

LADY HUSH: I reserved Room Zero.

Odo checks the ledger. The last line is dated 1897.

ODO: Your reservation is very old.

LADY HUSH: The hotel is very slow.

ACT ONE

INT. ROOM EIGHT - NIGHT

Tavi follows Mara and Pip into Room Eight. The wardrobe door swings open. Tavi looks inside and finds a stack of folded towels.

TAVI: The ghost has excellent housekeeping.

MARA: Those are clean towels.

PIP checks the radiator. It clanks twice. The mirror fogs, showing the words "NO WAKE-UP CALL."

TAVI: That is a strong review.

MARA: The mirror is fogged. Someone wrote on it.

PIP: The room is cold because the boiler is off.

LADY HUSH appears in the mirror behind them.

LADY HUSH: I asked for a room without a wake-up call.

TAVI: You are the ghost?

LADY HUSH: I am the guest.

She vanishes. The towels fall from the wardrobe shelf. Pip catches one.

INT. HOTEL BOILER ROOM - LATER

Pip opens the boiler panel. One pipe vibrates against the wall. Mara holds a flashlight. Odo comes down with the guest ledger.

PIP: This pipe is the singer.

MARA: Can you stop it?

PIP: I can cushion it. I cannot promise it will be quiet.

Odo finds an old floor plan. Room Zero is printed beneath the staircase, where a small door is hidden behind the linen cart.

ODO: The room exists.

MARA: Why did we seal it?

The hotel inspector arrives, carrying a clipboard.

MR. BELL: The building permit shows a storage closet.

Mara asks Lady Hush whether she wants the room reopened. Lady Hush says she wants a door that opens from both sides. Pip tests the hidden door. It swings inward, then catches on the linen cart. He pushes the cart away from the wall and fixes a small brass stop beneath the hinge. The door now stays open without anyone holding it.

PIP: The cart was acting as a lock.

ODO: We have been using the key rack to prop it there.

MARA: That is why Room Zero never checked in.

The inspector tries the door from the hallway. Lady Hush pulls it open from the room, then closes it from inside. The key remains on the desk. Mr. Bell marks the access plan with a pencil.

The linen cart rolls away from the wall on its own. A narrow door appears.

ACT TWO

INT. ROOM ZERO - NIGHT

The door opens to a small but tidy room with a bed, a writing desk, and a brass lamp. Lady Hush sits at the desk, polishing the silver key.

LADY HUSH: My room has been listed as a closet for a century.

MARA: We can correct the plan, and leave the key on the hook.

MR. BELL: The room has no exit to the hall.

PIP: It has a door. It is behind the cart.

TAVI: Does it have a bathroom?

LADY HUSH: It has a washstand.

TAVI: The review will mention that.

Mara asks Lady Hush to sign the updated guest register. Lady Hush signs, then slides the pen through her transparent fingers. Odo catches it before it falls.

ODO: We will use pencil.

Lady Hush hands Mara the silver key. Mara hangs it on the front desk hook labeled ROOM ZERO. The boiler starts. The pipe knocks, then settles after Pip wedges felt between it and the wall.

LADY HUSH: The hotel is quiet.

PIP: The boiler is on.

LADY HUSH: I said quiet, not silent.

Tavi writes a note in the review book. The inspector changes the floor plan from STORAGE to GUEST ROOM.

FINAL IMAGE: Room Zero's lamp glows through the open doorway while the linen cart waits politely beside it.
