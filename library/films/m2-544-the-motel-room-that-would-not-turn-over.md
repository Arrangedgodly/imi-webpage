# The Motel Room That Would Not Turn Over

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-house horror films that turn housekeeping logistics into supernatural dread*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
NIGHT CLERK MARA, a macaque
GUEST VEX, an orangutan
HOUSEKEEPER PIP, a capuchin
PLUMBER ODO, a gibbon
NEIGHBOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. MOTEL ROOM 12 - NIGHT

Vex enters carrying a suitcase. The room is spotless except for a damp ring on the dresser. A bedside lamp flickers.

VEX: The room is occupied.

MARA: You checked in five minutes ago. The room is yours until checkout.

VEX: Someone has already used the dresser.

PIP: I wiped it after the last guest. The ring came back because the ice bucket leaked.

Vex backs toward the door. A radiator knocks three times.

VEX: It knows I am here.

ODO: The radiator is heating. The pipes knock when the valve opens.

MARA: Odo, can you check the bucket and radiator while I find another room?

VEX: I want to stay. I paid for a haunting.

PIP: We don't sell hauntings. We sell clean rooms.

SCENE 2. INT. HALLWAY - MOMENTS LATER

The room door swings shut by itself. Vex jumps. Mara catches it with a doorstop.

VEX: It has chosen to seal us inside.

MARA: The closer pulls the door shut. The latch is worn and rebounds.

ODO: I can adjust the closer, but I need to keep the door open while I work.

PIP: The room is empty except for Vex's suitcase and the ice bucket.

The bucket gives a slow drip. Vex turns toward it.

VEX: A message from the water spirit.

PIP: The liner is cracked. The water is dripping onto the dresser.

MARA: Put the bucket in the tub. I'll bring a replacement.

Vex lifts the bucket. A folded note floats out. He reads it in a trembling voice.

VEX: Leave at once.

PIP: That's the housekeeping checklist. It says, "Leave the bucket outside for replacement."

SCENE 2A. INT. MOTEL OFFICE - NIGHT

Mara checks the room log. Pip points to the maintenance notes beside the guest register.

PIP: Room twelve's radiator knocks every time the heat cycles. We put that in the welcome card.

VEX: It is a warning from the previous occupant.

MARA: It is a note about plumbing. Odo, should we move Vex while you repair it?

ODO: If the radiator valve is tightened, the room should quiet. I can do it while Vex waits in the lobby.

VEX: I will remain in the room. I need to face the sound.

MARA: You can stay, but stand away from the wrench and let Odo finish.

Odo closes the valve a quarter turn. The radiator knocks once, then stops. Vex leans toward it, listening.

VEX: It has gone silent.

PIP: The heat is still on. The pipe is just no longer loose against the bracket.

MARA: We will check it again after the room warms. The leak is the bucket, not the wall.

Pip sets the cracked liner in a clear repair bin. The water ring on the dresser dries under a cloth.

SCENE 2B. INT. MOTEL ROOM - NIGHT

The replacement bucket is dry. Odo turns the heat down, then waits while the radiator cools.

ODO: No leak at the valve. The bracket is firm.

VEX: If the room is safe, why does the closet smell like a storm?

PIP: The vacuum bag is dusty. I'll move it to the service closet after I finish this room.

MARA: We can note it for housekeeping. No one needs to open the wall.

VEX: I have been denied three possible hauntings.

MARA: You have been given three explanations. The room is ready.

SCENE 3. INT. MOTEL ROOM - LATER

Odo replaces the bucket and tightens the radiator valve. The lamp stays on. The room is quiet.

VEX: I can no longer hear the ghost.

MARA: You can hear the air conditioner. It is still cycling.

ODO: Radiator test complete. Bucket is dry. Door closer is adjusted.

PIP: The dresser is clean. I will put a towel under the replacement bucket in case it sweats.

VEX: What if the ring returns?

MARA: Then you call the desk, and we bring another bucket.

VEX: Could I have the room with the knocking pipes?

MARA: That room is booked.

Vex opens the closet. A vacuum cleaner stands inside. Its handle slowly tilts forward because it was leaning against the door.

VEX: Something moved.

PIP: The vacuum was leaning. I'll park it in the closet bracket.

The handle clicks into place. Vex stares at the still machine.

TAG: The lamp stays steady. The radiator gives one ordinary knock as the heater cools. Vex hides under the blanket, peeking at the thermostat.
