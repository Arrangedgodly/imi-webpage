# The Last Seat on the Ferry

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic road films, where two travelers argue over the final seat and discover they are headed to the same village*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: Two strangers compete for the last ferry seat, then must coordinate a detour when the ferry's cargo shifts.

Cast: Traveler Nara, macaque; traveler Seli, capuchin; captain Odo, baboon; deckhand Pava, gibbon.

SCENE 1. EXT. FERRY DOCK, SUNSET

One seat remains on the small ferry. Nara and Seli arrive at the same time.

NARA: I was here first.

SELI: You were behind the post.

NARA: I was in line.

SELI: The line is painted on the dock.

Odo rings the boarding bell.

ODO: Last passenger, please.

Nara and Seli both step forward, then stop.

NARA: You go.

SELI: No, you go.

ODO: The ferry leaves when someone sits.

PAVA: There is one seat.

SCENE 2. INT. FERRY, MOMENTS LATER

Pava serves them both a cup of water. The cups are strapped to a small tray so they do not slide in the wake.

NARA: This ferry has a careful crew.

SELI: The cargo moved once. They have learned from it.

Odo says the river is shallower near the reed bend. Pava checks the pole against the waterline and signals him to stay in the main channel.

PAVA: The deep route is longer, but stable.

ODO: We have time.

They continue at a steady pace.

They sit on opposite ends of the bench, leaving a gap in the middle. Odo pushes off. A crate shifts on the deck.

[SFX: WOODEN CRATE SLIDES.]

PAVA: Cargo moved.

ODO: The load is leaning.

NARA: We should rebalance it.

SELI: If we both stand, the ferry rocks.

ODO: Stay seated until I secure the crate.

Pava ties down the crate. The knot slips. Nara offers her scarf; Seli offers a length of twine. Together they secure the load.

PAVA: The ferry is level.

ODO: We can continue.

NARA: Where are you headed?

SELI: Willow Landing.

NARA: So am I.

SELI: We could have shared the seat.

NARA: We are sharing the bench.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVER, NIGHT

Fog covers the water. The ferry slows. Odo checks the shoreline. Seli recognizes a bell buoy, but it is muffled by the fog.

[SFX: DISTANT BELL, WATER.]

SELI: We are near the reed bend.

NARA: The landing is after the bend.

ODO: The map says take the left channel.

PAVA: The crate is blocking the map.

Nara and Seli hold the map between them. They read it together, each standing on a different side of the bench. The bench tips.

NARA: Sit down.

SELI: I am holding the map.

NARA: Hold it lower.

They lower the map, and the bench levels. Odo steers into the left channel. The ferry passes the reeds.

As the ferry crosses the fog, a second bell sounds from the wrong direction. Odo slows, unsure whether the channel has shifted.

ODO: We need another bearing.

PAVA: I can listen from the bow.

Nara and Seli stand on opposite sides of the deck to hear the bell. Their weight tilts the ferry.

NARA: One at a time.

SELI: The sound is coming from the shore.

They trade places, then compare the direction. The muffled bell is behind them; the buoy ahead is silent.

NARA: We have passed the bend.

ODO: The landing is close.

Seli folds the map and secures it beneath the bench. The ferry steadies. The bell buoy rings once as they enter the right channel.

Nara and Seli compare travel plans. Nara is delivering a basket of pears; Seli is carrying a repair kit. They learn they are both headed to help prepare the village market.

SELI: I thought you were traveling for a festival.

NARA: The market is part of the festival.

SELI: Then we are on the same errand.

NARA: We could carry your tools and my pears together.

Pava says the ferry has one more crate to unload before it reaches the landing. The crate is the one they tied down. It is filled with empty baskets.

ODO: The cargo is light.

NARA: It still shifted.

SELI: We kept it steady.

SCENE 5. EXT. WILLOW LANDING, NIGHT

The ferry reaches the dock. Nara and Seli lift the baskets together. Odo brings the repair kit.

PAVA: You both got the last seat.

NARA: We got half a seat each.

SELI: And one shared map.

Odo asks who will carry the basket of pears. Nara says she will. Seli carries the tools. They walk toward the village.

NARA: The ferry was slower than I expected.

SELI: The company was better than the schedule.

NARA: We only met tonight.

SELI: The bench had limited seating.

They reach a fork in the path. One sign points toward the market. Another points toward the inn.

NARA: Which way first?

SELI: Market. We can deliver the pears before they bruise.

NARA: And the inn?

SELI: It will still be there.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS TOWARD MARKET, FERRY BELL FADES.]
