# The Last Fence Post

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: western showdown films, where two ranchers prepare for a duel over a fence post that marks the edge of a shared path*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: Two stubborn neighbors square off at high noon over a fence post, while a traveling carpenter tries to build them a gate.

Cast: Rancher Bex, a baboon; rancher Nori, a macaque; carpenter Fia, a gibbon; deputy Pavi, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. DUSTY PATH, HIGH NOON

A fence post stands in the center of the trail. Bex waits on one side. Nori waits on the other. They each hold a length of rope.

BEX: This post marks my side.

NORI: It marks the path.

BEX: The path goes through my land.

NORI: The path was here before the fence.

Pavi rides in on a low cart. The wheel stops at the post.

PAVI: Is this a duel?

BEX: It is a dispute.

NORI: It might become a duel.

PAVI: I am the deputy. I need to know which whistle to use.

Fia approaches with a toolbox.

FIA: I came to repair the post.

BEX: It is not broken.

NORI: It is crooked.

FIA: Those are not the same condition.

She checks the post with a level. It tilts toward Bex's land.

BEX: The ground is sloping.

NORI: The post is leaning.

FIA: Both statements can be true.

SCENE 2. EXT. WATER TROUGH, MOMENTS LATER

Fia asks them to mark where the path should run. Bex places a rock on his side. Nori places a rock on hers. The two marks overlap.

BEX: That is my boundary.

NORI: That is the path.

FIA: You have marked the same spot.

Pavi measures the distance between the post and the trough. The cart cannot pass between them.

PAVI: The post blocks the way.

BEX: It protects the fence.

NORI: It catches my cart.

FIA: We can move the post and install a gate.

BEX: A gate needs two sides.

NORI: So does a fence.

BEX: A gate opens toward my land.

NORI: It should open toward the path.

Fia demonstrates with a board propped on a bucket. It falls over.

[SFX: BOARD CLATTERS.]

PAVI: The gate has surrendered.

FIA: It has no hinges yet.

SCENE 3. EXT. FENCE LINE, AFTERNOON

Bex and Nori each pace out what they consider their side. They meet at the post and turn around.

BEX: The post is exactly where I left it.

NORI: The post did not move.

PAVI: Then why are we walking in circles?

FIA: Because nobody agreed where the gate goes.

Fia uses chalk to draw an opening that follows the path. Bex says the line crosses his grazing patch. Nori says the path crosses both properties. They argue over who will open the gate.

BEX: I will open it.

NORI: You will close it behind me.

BEX: Only if you leave it open.

NORI: That is how gates work.

Pavi notes both statements in a small ledger.

PAVI: I have written "gate has two directions."

BEX: There is no such thing as a two-direction gate.

FIA: There is if it swings both ways.

She tests the hinge placement. The gate swings inward, then outward. It hits the fence post on the second swing.

FIA: The post is in the way.

BEX: It is a very important post.

NORI: It is a very inconvenient post.

SCENE 4. EXT. PATH, SUNSET

Fia removes the post and sets it aside. The path is clear. Bex and Nori inspect the hole.

BEX: The post was holding the wire.

NORI: The wire can attach to the gate.

FIA: I can add a short rail and keep the path open.

Bex holds the wire while Nori steadies the gate. They work together, but each insists the other is pulling too hard.

BEX: Ease it left.

NORI: I am easing it.

BEX: Your left or my left?

PAVI: The gate has two directions. Apparently, left does too.

Fia aligns the hinges. The gate swings clear of the path.

[SFX: GATE OPENS, CLOSES.]

NORI: It works.

BEX: It opens onto my side.

NORI: It opens onto both sides.

BEX: That's not possible.

Fia swings it the opposite direction.

BEX: It does open onto both sides.

SCENE 5. EXT. PATH, NIGHT

The cart passes through the gate. The fence stands, the post is now a marker beside the path, and the opening stays clear.

PAVI: The dispute is resolved.

BEX: Until someone leaves the gate open.

NORI: I will close it.

BEX: Then I will open it when I pass.

FIA: That is the arrangement.

Pavi tucks away his ledger. Bex and Nori exchange their ropes. They both reach for the same end, then let go.

NORI: You take it.

BEX: You take it.

PAVI: I will hold it until you agree.

He ties the rope loosely around the post. The post tips over again.

BEX: The post is broken now.

FIA: It was crooked before. Now it is lying down.

NORI: It can mark the path from there.

Fia rolls it out of the way. The cart passes cleanly.

[SFX: CART WHEEL OVER DIRT, GATE LATCH.]

PAVI: The trail is open.

BEX: At high noon, we will inspect the gate.

NORI: At high noon, we will use it.

FIA: That is the same time.

They walk off on opposite sides of the path. The gate swings softly in the evening wind.

[SFX: GATE CREAKS ONCE.]
