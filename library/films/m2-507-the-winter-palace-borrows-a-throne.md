# The Winter Palace Borrows a Throne

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal biopics that frame every chair, crown, and pause as a symbol of historic power*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A palace film crew loses the ceremonial throne just before a coronation scene and borrows an ordinary chair from the archivist.

Cast: director Leni, a capuchin; actor Varo, a macaque; historian Saba, an orangutan; prop keeper Nilo, a lemur; extra Oren, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - INT. WINTER PALACE SET - MORNING

Varo stands beside an empty platform. Leni checks the script. Nilo reads the prop list: crown, orb, ceremonial throne. The throne is missing. Saba says it was sent to the restoration workshop last week.

LENI: The king must rise from the throne at the moment of doubt.

VARO: I can rise from this platform.

SABA: The scene is about inherited power.

Oren brings a plain wooden chair from the archive. It has a paper tag reading READING ROOM.

SCENE 2 - INT. PALACE SET - LATER

Varo sits in the chair. It is too low for the throne platform. Leni asks him to look imperious. His knees rise above the armrests. Nilo adds a velvet cushion, which raises him enough but slides sideways. Saba objects that the cushion is from a modern sofa.

LENI: The camera will not see the label.

The label is on the cushion's front. Nilo turns it backward. The chair wobbles whenever Varo shifts. The crew wedges a folded program under one leg.

SCENE 2A - INT. PALACE SET - CONTINUOUS

Saba checks the film's reference book. The coronation happened in summer, not winter, and the historical throne is not supposed to have a cushion. Leni removes it. Varo sinks so low that the camera sees only his crown above the armrest. Nilo stacks two cushions. Saba removes both and brings a wooden footstool from the archive. It raises Varo enough to keep his knees below the royal robe.

VARO: Is the footstool historically accurate?

SABA: It is from the archive.

LENI: That is not the same thing.

Oren folds the reading-room tag and tapes it under the chair so it cannot show in the frame. Saba warns him not to leave adhesive on the wood.

SCENE 3 - INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - AFTERNOON

Saba checks the restoration order. The throne is not at the workshop; it is in the neighboring gallery, where a school tour is sitting on it. The pupils are listening to a talk about royal furniture. Leni asks the tour guide to pause while they retrieve the prop. Saba says the chair is not a prop until the gallery closes.

VARO: History has claimed its seat.

Nilo borrows a velvet rope and marks the chair as reserved for filming. The children move to a bench. One student leaves a backpack on the throne.

SCENE 3A - INT. RESTORATION GALLERY - LATER

The crew reaches the original throne. The school tour is still in session. A child asks why the king's seat has a crack in its arm. Saba explains that the throne was repaired after a long reign. Leni asks if the repair can be hidden for filming. Saba says the crack is part of the object's history. Varo sits for one test. The throne fits him, but the camera cannot see the carved crest because a gallery light reflects in the varnish.

Oren tilts the camera, then bumps the velvet rope. The rope drops across the throne steps. The school group helps hold it up while the crew moves the chair back to the set. Leni asks them not to appear in the shot; the children stand just outside the frame and watch.

SCENE 4 - INT. PALACE SET - EVENING

The crew returns with the original throne. It is larger than the set platform. Its feet do not fit between the painted columns. Leni asks the camera operator to frame it from the side. Varo delivers the coronation speech. At the moment of doubt, the throne's foot catches the rug. It tilts. Varo steadies it with one hand and remains seated.

SABA: That was not in the script.

LENI: It looked like the king holding power in place.

SCENE 5 - INT. PALACE SET - NIGHT

The throne is moved back to the gallery. The borrowed chair returns to the archive. Varo asks whether he should keep the velvet cushion for the next scene. Saba removes its reading-room tag and returns it to the sofa. Varo leaves the archive chair beside the script for the next rehearsal. Saba locks the gallery door. The chair remains on the call sheet.

FINAL IMAGE: The king stands beside the restored throne, while the plain chair waits in the archive with its reading-room tag.
