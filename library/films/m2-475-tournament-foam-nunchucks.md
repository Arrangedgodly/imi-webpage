# The Tournament of the Foam Nunchucks

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: martial-arts tournament films that turn a local skills exhibition into an honor-bound rivalry*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: Two monkey martial-arts students prepare for a championship duel, then learn their tournament is a friendly demonstration using foam props and a strict schedule.

Cast: student Kiri, a macaque; rival Jono, a gibbon; instructor Seli, a capuchin; announcer Tavi, a lemur; judge Oru, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY DOJO - MORNING

Kiri practices a slow stance beside a rack of foam nunchucks. Jono enters wearing a red sash. Tavi pins a tournament schedule to the wall.

JONO: I will face you at sunset.

KIRI: The schedule says our match is at two.

JONO: The sun will be high.

SELI: This is a skills exhibition. We do not strike each other.

JONO: Our rivalry will be settled.

ORU: You will demonstrate forms and control. The score is for balance and safe movement.

SCENE 2 - INT. DOJO FLOOR - LATER

Kiri performs a slow form. Jono performs a fast one. He spins the foam nunchucks and accidentally lets go. They fly across the mat and land in a laundry basket.

JONO: The weapon has escaped.

SELI: It is foam. Pick it up and keep the floor clear.

Kiri retrieves it, checks that the handle is intact and hands it back.

TAVI: The audience will arrive soon. Please keep all equipment inside the mat boundary.

Jono tries a dramatic leap. He lands just outside the mat. Oru raises a red flag.

ORU: Boundary violation.

JONO: The move was meant to look dangerous.

ORU: It looked outside the boundary.

SCENE 2B - INT. COMMUNITY DOJO - LATER

Tavi hangs a painted banner reading FINAL CLASH. Seli takes it down and turns it around. The back says FRIENDLY SKILLS EXHIBITION. Jono asks Tavi to hang the dramatic side for the audience.

TAVI: The banner crew made two sides.

JONO: The audience came for a duel.

SELI: The flyer says demonstration.

Jono asks Kiri to agree to a final challenge. Kiri agrees to repeat the slow form without dropping a prop. Jono practices a flourish, but the foam nunchuck handle slips through his fingers and lands inside a water bucket. He reaches for it; Seli stops him and lifts it out by the strap.

SELI: We dry it before anyone swings it.

JONO: The bucket has joined my opponent.

KIRI: The bucket has no scorecard.

Oru marks a point for retrieving the equipment without swinging it wet. Jono protests that a bucket rescue should not count as tournament technique. Oru points to the posted rules: control, balance, care of equipment. Jono reads them twice, then asks for another try at the flourish with the prop dry.

The instructor asks both students to show the opening bow again, this time facing the audience. Jono bows toward the storage rack. Kiri taps the mat with a foot to show him the correct direction. Tavi moves the banner so it does not cover the exit sign.

SCENE 3 - INT. DOJO - AFTERNOON

The demonstration begins. Kiri and Jono bow to each other. The crowd waits for a clash. They perform mirrored forms, keeping their distance. A child in the front row drops a foam baton. Kiri pauses and returns it. Jono waits rather than continuing his sequence.

TAVI: The students have shown control and respect.

JONO: We have not fought.

KIRI: We have performed.

JONO: The rivalry needs a winner.

Oru shows the scorecards. They are tied.

SCENE 4 - INT. DOJO FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Jono asks for a final round. Seli gives him a padded staff. Kiri uses a foam shield. They perform a set sequence: block, step aside, bow. Jono misses the cue and turns too early. Kiri holds position, letting him reset. The crowd applauds the recovery.

ORU: The judge scores the reset, not the mistake.

JONO: We can do the sequence again.

SELI: The schedule says one round. We keep our promise to the other groups.

SCENE 5 - INT. DOJO - EVENING

Tavi announces the results. Kiri and Jono share the balance ribbon. Jono takes off his red sash and folds it. Kiri returns the foam nunchucks to the rack.

JONO: The rivalry will continue in practice.

KIRI: We can trade forms next week.

SELI: Good. Bring your equipment back in the basket, not the laundry.

Oru closes the mat boundary tape and rolls it into a case. The child retrieves the dropped baton and hands it to the instructor.

FINAL IMAGE: Kiri and Jono bow beside the foam weapons rack, while the tournament schedule remains pinned to the wall with no sunset duel added.

The crowd claps when Jono completes the controlled flourish. Tavi flips the banner so the printed event title faces the audience. Jono bows to Kiri instead of demanding a winner-take-all rematch.
