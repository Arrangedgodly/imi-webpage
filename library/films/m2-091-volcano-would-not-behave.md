# The Volcano That Would Not Behave

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: school science-fair underdog films, genius montages, and machines that solve the wrong problem*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A student builds an elaborate volcano for the school science fair, but the judges care more about the neighboring class's fire drill than his spectacular eruption.

Cast: Teko, a macaque student; Iva, a lemur lab partner; Mr. Moss, a tortoise science teacher; and Judge Vela, an orangutan engineer.

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL LAB, MORNING

Teko unveils a papier-mache volcano wired with blinking lights, a miniature crane, and a siren. Iva holds the rubric: state a question, show the evidence, explain the result. Teko dims the lights.

TEKO: The mountain will erupt.

IVA: What are we testing?

TEKO: The audience's courage.

Mr. Moss switches on the room lights. Teko pours vinegar into the crater. Foam surges over the rim, knocks down the cardboard village, and trips the siren. A paper flag sails into the ceiling fan. Moss kills the fan just before the flag wraps around it.

Teko resets the crane. Its hook lifts one cottage, then drops it onto the crater, where it sticks in the foam.

MOSS: That is a useful observation.

TEKO: The village has fallen.

IVA: The village is made of cereal boxes.

Iva writes down the amounts Teko used. He tries to switch the lights to red, but she takes the control box away until they test the mixture safely.

SCENE 2. INT. SCHOOL GYM, AFTERNOON

The science fair opens. Teko wheels the volcano to his table. The crane arm catches the gym door and knocks a cardboard boulder into the aisle. Iva catches the boulder against her hip. Vela arrives as Teko restores the siren.

VELA: What question does your project answer?

TEKO: Can we make a mountain erupt?

IVA: The question is how vinegar and baking soda change the foam height.

Teko points to a slow-motion video. Vela asks for the measurements. Iva opens the notebook. Teko asks her not to show the page with the overfilled first trial. Vela reads it anyway.

VELA: You used twice the planned amount.

TEKO: That was for the trailer.

Vela asks them to run the measured mixture once. Teko reaches for the switch marked GRAND FINALE. Iva moves the measuring cup under the crater. Teko tries to add a drumroll from a small speaker. The cord catches the crane wheel, and the model rotates toward the judge.

Iva unhooks the cord while Moss steadies the table. Teko asks if the turning volcano looked intentional. Vela asks him to begin the test.

Teko pours the right amounts. The foam rises to the marked line and stops. Iva checks the notebook and gives Vela the recorded height. Teko squints at the modest result.

TEKO: It was bigger in the video.

IVA: So was the camera.

Vela asks what changed between the trials. Iva points to the measurements. Teko starts to describe the colored lights, sees the notebook, then explains that doubling the baking soda made the first eruption overflow.

SCENE 3. INT. SCHOOL GYM, CONTINUOUS

A bell rings. The neighboring class begins a fire drill. The gym exit is blocked by the volcano's crane. Mr. Moss asks Teko to move the display. Teko reaches for the controls, hoping to finish with one spectacular eruption before the judges leave.

IVA: The fire-drill path has to stay clear.

TEKO: I can move it after the ending.

MOSS: The ending is in the way.

Teko pulls the model. A wheel lock catches. The volcano tilts, the crane swings, and the cottage slides into the foam tray. Iva grabs the base. Vela and Moss lift the far edge while Teko releases the lock. The siren begins again.

Vela reaches toward the switch. Teko stops her.

TEKO: Wait. It always trips when the foam reaches the rim.

Iva opens the notebook to the overfilled trial. The recorded height is higher than the safe mark. Teko looks at the foam in the tray, then turns off the siren. He guides the model away from the exit with Moss and Iva, keeping the contents level. The drill group passes without anyone ducking around the crane.

Vela asks if the spill ruins the demonstration. Iva hands Teko the wet-floor sign. He places it beside the puddle, then uses a sponge to wipe the floor. As he cleans, he tells Vela that the planned amount makes repeatable foam, while the extra amount causes overflow. Vela asks him to add the observation to the notebook.

SCENE 4. INT. SCHOOL GYM, EVENING

The judges award the project a ribbon for clear evidence. Teko holds it beside the volcano. The crane hook still has a cereal-box cottage stuck to it.

TEKO: Can the ribbon go on the crane?

IVA: The crane is retired.

Mr. Moss hands them a parts tray. Teko removes the batteries from the siren and puts them in the tray. Iva transfers the notebook to a binder sleeve so the foam marks do not smear. Vela asks what Teko will test next. He says crater shape, but only after he writes the question down.

The fair coordinator asks them to clear the table. Teko begins a slow-motion pack-up. Iva walks at regular speed, stacks the cups, and closes the supply box before he has finished winding the crane. He drops the camera pose and helps her carry the model.

FINAL IMAGE: Teko and Iva leave with the ribbon and notebook. The siren sits silent in the labeled parts tray, and the clear gym exit remains open.
