# The Room of Falling Water

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-house horror films, ominous camera glides, reluctant skeptics, and a ghost who wants the leaky roof repaired*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
NIKO, a macaque building surveyor
ADA, a capuchin night caretaker
THE GHOST, a gibbon in a dripping sheet
MR. BURL, a ring-tailed lemur owner

SCENE 1. EXT. OLD MIST HOUSE - NIGHT

A camera glides toward a crooked mansion. Rain falls from a clear patch of sky directly above its roof. Niko, a building surveyor, arrives with a clipboard and a flashlight. Ada opens the door in rubber boots.

ADA: The house makes a noise after midnight.

NIKO: Houses make noises before midnight too.

A long groan comes from the hallway. Niko writes "floorboard" on the clipboard. A shadow floats past the stairs. He writes "curtain." A cold breath fogs his glasses.

NIKO: That one may need a second opinion.

SCENE 2. INT. GRAND HALL - LATER

A portrait's eyes appear to follow Niko. He steps left; the eyes follow. He steps right; the eyes follow again. Ada reaches behind the frame and finds a small pulley connected to the stair rail.

ADA: Mr. Burl uses it to check who is entering.

BURL: It was meant to discourage burglars.

NIKO: It's a moving portrait.

BURL: It has discouraged me several times.

A chain rattles upstairs. Burl grips the flashlight. Ada goes to fetch a ladder. The camera makes a slow, ominous move toward a damp stain on the ceiling.

SCENE 3. INT. UPSTAIRS CORRIDOR - MIDNIGHT

The dripping ghost appears at the far end of the hall. Its sheet clings wetly to a pair of gibbon arms. It glides toward the group, leaving a trail of water.

BURL: The ghost!

GHOST: The gutter!

The group freezes. The ghost points at a roof hatch. Niko climbs the ladder and finds leaves packed into the gutter. Rainwater has backed up and run inside a vent. The ghost's sheet has been soaked by the leak.

NIKO: You were trying to show us the roof?

GHOST: I have been showing you for three years.

ADA: We thought you were haunting us.

GHOST: I am haunting the landlord's maintenance schedule.

SCENE 4. INT. ATTIC - LATER

Niko, Ada and Burl clear the leaves from the gutter. The ghost holds a lantern and points to a cracked roof tile. The water stops dripping. Niko asks why the ghost never knocked.

GHOST: I did. You heard the knock and called it the old house settling.

BURL: It was a very loud knock.

GHOST: It was a sheet over a bucket. I had no door.

A thunderclap shakes dust loose from the rafters. Everyone ducks. The ghost remains still, arms crossed. Niko finds a loose tile wedged under the chimney and lifts it out.

SCENE 5. INT. GRAND HALL - DAWN

The crew repairs the roof with a temporary tarp. The house grows quiet. Burl thanks Niko and Ada. The ghost asks for a dry sheet and a chair by the fire. Burl offers the library; the ghost refuses because the portrait watches the doorway.

ADA: We can turn the portrait around.

BURL: But then how will I know who is entering?

NIKO: Try listening for footsteps.

Burl considers this new technology.

SCENE 6. INT. GRAND HALL - MORNING

A new camera glides down the hallway, still trying to make the house look frightening. It passes the dry ceiling, the repaired gutter diagram, and the ghost reading a book in a chair. The ghost looks into the lens and points upward at one last dripping spot.

SCENE 7. EXT. MIST HOUSE - MORNING

A repair crew arrives with ladders, shingles, and a bucket that has no decorative purpose. The ghost stands on the porch and points at the roof while Niko explains the gutter route. Burl carries the maintenance ledger and reads each item aloud as the crew checks it.

BURL: Gutter clear. Tile replaced. Pipe padded.

GHOST: There is one drip by the chimney.

The crew looks up. A drop lands in the bucket. Niko circles the spot, and Burl orders another ladder. The ghost folds the dripping sheet and hangs it in the sun to dry.

SCENE 8. EXT. MIST HOUSE - LATER THAT MORNING

The crew finishes the last roof tile. The ghost stays to watch. One worker waves; the ghost waves back, startling another worker into dropping a shingle. The ghost catches it and places it on the stack.

GHOST: I can be helpful when the house is not leaking.

NIKO: Put that on the maintenance form.

BURL: There is no box for helpful ghosts.

ADA: Add one beside floor squeak.

FINAL IMAGE: Niko places a bucket beneath the drip; a single drop lands with a sound so loud that Burl, Ada and the ghost all jump at once.
