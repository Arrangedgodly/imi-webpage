# The Briefcase That Would Not Open

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: spy thrillers whose absurdly elaborate gadgets complicate simple tasks and require one final explanation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Agent Pavo brings an over-engineered spy briefcase to a simple exchange, then spends the entire operation trying to open it without triggering its features.

Cast: Agent Pavo, a macaque; handler Seli, a lemur; courier Nima, a gibbon; snack-kiosk vendor Toma, a capuchin; Mox, a baboon supervisor; the briefcase voice, a recorded device prompt.

SCENE 1 - INT. TRAIN STATION WAITING ROOM - NIGHT

Pavo sets a silver briefcase on a bench. It has a fingerprint scanner, three numbered dials, a blinking light and a tiny speaker. Seli whispers into an earpiece.

SELI: Exchange the envelope for the blue ticket. Leave the case closed.

PAVO: Understood. The case is for contingencies.

The case gives a chirp and extends a metal handle.

PAVO: It has detected a contingency.

SELI: That is the luggage handle. Retract it.

Pavo presses the fingerprint scanner. A panel lights up: ENTER CODE.

PAVO: I have to open the case to find the code card.

SELI: The code card is in your jacket.

Pavo pulls out a folded card. The briefcase emits a warning tone because it is no longer being touched.

At the exit, Pavo scans the case. It rejects the fingerprint because his glove is on. He removes the glove; the scanner rejects him because his paw is damp with rain tracked in from the platform. Seli listens over the earpiece.

SELI: We agreed to leave the case closed.

PAVO: I am verifying its biometric lock.

SELI: The exchange does not require verification.

Pavo rubs his paw on his jacket. The scanner beeps and displays LOCKOUT: TRY AGAIN IN 60 SECONDS. He glances at the station clock.

PAVO: The case has delayed the mission.

SELI: The train is on schedule. You are early.

SCENE 2 - INT. STATION PLATFORM - MOMENTS LATER

Nima arrives with a blue ticket. Toma watches from a snack kiosk. Pavo approaches with an envelope.

NIMA: Envelope first. Ticket second.

PAVO: My briefcase can verify the exchange.

NIMA: I can read the ticket. It says Platform Four.

Pavo taps the case. A small camera lens pops out and points at Nima.

NIMA: Your luggage is staring at me.

PAVO: It's a document scanner.

The lens swivels toward Toma's kiosk. BRIEFCASE VOICE: Smile for identification.

TOMA: I am selling tea. I do not need identification.

SELI: Close the lens.

Pavo presses a button marked PRIVACY. The case plays a recorded fanfare and opens its umbrella attachment. The umbrella catches Nima's ticket.

SCENE 3 - INT. PLATFORM - CONTINUOUS

Pavo, Nima and Toma wrestle the umbrella down. The ticket flutters toward the track. Toma catches it with a paper cup.

TOMA: I have your ticket. Buy a tea and I will hand it back.

NIMA: That is fair.

Pavo tries to retract the umbrella. The case's speaker says, "Initiate silent mode." He presses the button. A loud click sounds from every dial.

SELI: The envelope is still in your hand. Give it to Nima.

PAVO: I need a secure exchange.

SELI: The station is public. The envelope is sealed. That is secure enough.

Nima buys a tea. Toma hands her the recovered ticket; she passes it to Pavo, who gives her the envelope. The case now emits a countdown beep.

PAVO: It thinks the exchange is a timed mission.

SELI: It is a transfer. Put the case down.

Pavo sets the briefcase on the bench. The alarm stops. Everyone watches it.

SCENE 4 - INT. STATION OFFICE - LATER

Mox, a supervisor, examines the case. Pavo holds the code card. Seli sits across from him.

MOX: Who approved the umbrella, scanner, speaker and countdown?

PAVO: The gadget team said each feature was useful alone.

SELI: Together they caught a ticket, identified a tea seller and announced our presence.

MOX: The case can be opened with the three dials. What is the code?

Pavo reads the card. He turns the dials. The case opens smoothly. Inside is a paperclip, two mints and a folded instruction booklet.

PAVO: The envelope had the actual documents.

MOX: Why carry all this hardware?

PAVO: To be prepared for anything.

SELI: Except opening the case.

SCENE 5 - INT. TRAIN STATION - DAWN

Pavo returns the briefcase to the equipment counter. He hands in the umbrella and scanner attachments separately; the built-in speaker remains in the case. Nima boards her train. Toma serves Pavo a tea in a plain cup.

TOMA: No hidden lens.

PAVO: Just tea.

Seli hands Pavo a simple canvas satchel.

SELI: For your next assignment.

PAVO: What does it do?

SELI: It carries things.

Pavo places the satchel on his shoulder. The briefcase, left on the counter, begins a cheerful recorded message.

BRIEFCASE VOICE: Thank you for choosing advanced security. Please rate your exchange.

Mox turns it off with one switch.

FINAL IMAGE: Pavo walks toward the train carrying the unremarkable satchel while the briefcase sits silent beneath a small label reading RETURN TO GADGET DEPARTMENT.
