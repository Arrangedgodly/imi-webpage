# The Verdict at Pebble Court

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: courtroom dramas, where a tiny dispute over a pebble receives the gravity of a landmark trial*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: Two neighbors bring a single pebble dispute to court, while the judge struggles to keep the lawyers from turning a garden path into a national case.

Cast: Judge Sava, a gibbon; claimant Odo, a baboon; respondent Piri, a capuchin; advocate Nara, a macaque; clerk Luma, a lemur; witness Tavi, a young macaque.

SCENE 1. INT. PEBBLE COURT, DAY

A pebble sits under a glass cup on the evidence table. Nara stands for Odo. Piri stands alone.

NARA: Your honor, this pebble was removed from my client's garden path.

SAVA: How do you know it is the same pebble?

NARA: It is round.

PIRI: Many pebbles are round.

LUMA: The court will recognize the pebble as Exhibit A.

SAVA: The court recognizes it as a pebble.

NARA: We request a dramatic pause.

SAVA: Denied.

Odo says the pebble was in the middle of the path and could trip someone. Piri says she moved it to the edge so the cart wheel would not catch. Nara calls this a transfer of property.

SAVA: What property?

NARA: The pebble.

SAVA: The path?

NARA: The pebble's original location.

SAVA: That is not property.

SCENE 2. INT. COURTROOM, LATER

Nara presents a chalk diagram. It shows the path, the pebble, and a large arrow labeled "movement."

NARA: At 8:04, the pebble was here.

PIRI: I moved it at 8:05.

NARA: No one denies the movement.

SAVA: Then what is disputed?

NARA: The intention.

Piri says she intended to protect the cart wheel. Odo says the pebble marked a border between two garden beds. Luma asks whether either garden bed has a sign.

ODO: The pebble was the sign.

PIRI: It was one pebble.

ODO: A very meaningful pebble.

Sava asks them to demonstrate the path. Odo rolls a model cart over the chalk diagram. It bumps the pebble and stops. Piri moves the pebble two inches; the cart rolls smoothly.

SAVA: The pebble was obstructing the path.

NARA: We object to the demonstration.

SAVA: On what ground?

NARA: The floor.

Luma notes the objection in the record.

SCENE 3. EXT. GARDEN PATH, AFTERNOON

The court visits the path. Sava, Luma, Odo, Piri and Nara stand around the pebble. A small sign reads "Path" on one side and "Garden" on the other.

SAVA: Who placed this sign?

ODO: I did. After the hearing started.

PIRI: It was not there this morning.

NARA: The sign is an attempt to influence the court.

SAVA: It is a sign. The court can read it.

Odo admits that he used the pebble to mark where the garden bed ended. Piri says she moved it to the path edge but did not know it mattered.

NARA: The pebble carries symbolic weight.

SAVA: It weighs less than a berry.

NARA: Symbolic weight is not measured in berries.

Piri picks up the pebble and places it between the sign and the path. The cart wheel clears it, and the pebble still marks the garden edge.

PIRI: It can do both.

ODO: It can be a border and not a trip.

SAVA: A compromise.

NARA: We reserve the right to appeal the compromise.

SAVA: To whom?

NARA: The garden.

A second witness is called: Tavi, a young macaque who saw the pebble on the path earlier.

LUMA: Did you see who placed the pebble?

WITNESS: I saw Odo place it by the garden. Then Piri moved it later.

NARA: Did it remain in the same place?

WITNESS: No. It moved twice.

NARA: The court has heard that it moved. We ask why.

SAVA: We have heard why. The cart wheel caught.

The witness says the cart hit the pebble, but the pebble stopped the wheel from rolling into the garden bed. Both neighbors are quiet.

ODO: It was doing two jobs.

PIRI: I thought it was only in the way.

SAVA: Then perhaps the dispute is about a sign, not a stone.

Sava delivers the verdict. The pebble will remain at the edge of the path. Odo may place a second pebble beside it to make the border visible, provided it does not obstruct the cart.

LUMA: The verdict is entered.

ODO: I accept.

PIRI: I accept.

NARA: I request one minute to consult with the pebble.

SAVA: You may consult outside.

Nara leans toward the glass cup.

NARA: We have lost the center of the path, but preserved your dignity.

Piri lifts the cup. The pebble is stuck to its underside by a bit of sap.

PIRI: The evidence has moved again.

Sava examines the cup and frees the pebble. She places it carefully at the garden edge.

SAVA: The court orders the pebble to remain here.

ODO: Can a pebble obey a court order?

SAVA: We will know if it moves.

The cart passes smoothly. The pebble stays in place.

NARA: Your honor, the verdict has held.

SAVA: Court is adjourned.

Luma writes "No appeal filed" in the ledger. Nara looks at the garden sign.

NARA: Is the garden accepting cases?

PIRI: Only seeds.

[SFX: GAVEL TAP, CART WHEEL ROLLS CLEAR.]
