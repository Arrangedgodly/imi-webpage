# The Apprentice Cartographers' Race

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quest races, map riddles, secret shortcuts, and rivals who discover the shortest path is the one that is actually open*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
NARI, an orangutan mapmaker
PIP, a capuchin apprentice
VEX, a macaque rival cartographer
LUMA, a lemur trail steward
ODO, a gibbon courier

SCENE 1. INT. MAPMAKERS' HALL - DAWN

The guild announces a race to deliver a map to the hilltop observatory before sunset. Pip and Vex receive identical sealed envelopes. Nari explains that the map must stay dry and arrive with the recipient's signature. Vex rolls out a chart covered in secret marks. Pip folds his map into a pocket.

VEX: The hidden route is the true test.

PIP: The road is on the map.

Luma posts a notice: the west stairs are closed for repair. Vex says he can go around the barrier. Luma says the trail is unsafe.

SCENE 2. EXT. OLD ROAD - MORNING

Pip follows the open path through the orchard. Vex turns into a narrow shortcut and finds a gate locked with a repair chain. He tries to climb it; the map catches on a thorn. Odo arrives with a courier bag and helps free the page. Vex thanks him, then claims he meant to stop there to study the terrain.

Pip reaches a fork and checks the compass. The road marker points toward the observatory. A painted arrow on a nearby rock points the other way; Luma explains it was for last year's race. Pip corrects the marker and continues.

SCENE 2A. EXT. ORCHARD GATE - LATE MORNING

Vex tries another shortcut through an orchard gate. The latch is tied with a bell, and he has no idea which side opens. He tugs at it. The bell rings loudly. Luma appears from the trail office and tells him the gate keeps livestock inside. Vex says the sound was a signal. Odo answers from the next field that he heard it and came to help.

Pip checks the map's old boundary marks. The orchard path is public but the gate must be closed behind them. Vex unlatches it, walks through, and shuts it securely. The bell gives one soft ring as the latch settles. He writes the gate symbol in his margin and marks the public path. Nari, speaking over the guild radio, asks for a route update. Pip reports the gate location and the open trail.

SCENE 3. EXT. BRIDGE CROSSING - MIDDAY

A stream has risen over the lower bridge. Pip stops to check the depth. Vex catches up and proposes they cross together, holding the maps overhead. Luma arrives and directs them to the higher footbridge. It adds a longer bend but remains safe. Vex complains that the map does not show the stream's new level. Nari radios back that maps show routes, not today's water.

The two rivals carry Odo's courier bag across the bridge so he can keep both hands free. The bag has a loud bell attached; it rings at every step. Odo explains it marks the delivery bag so it will not be left behind. Vex removes the bell, but Pip ties it to the strap again after the bag nearly slips.

SCENE 3A. EXT. HIGHER FOOTBRIDGE - MIDDAY

The footbridge has a low rail and a narrow deck. Odo carries the delivery bag while Pip checks each board. Vex follows the water instead of the painted trail arrow and ends up at the bridge's lower landing. The two paths meet there. He has not gained distance, but he has learned which marks point to the safe crossing.

Luma asks them to keep their maps inside waterproof sleeves. Vex wraps his chart in his cape. The cape catches on a branch and tears at the corner. Pip offers a spare sleeve from the courier kit. Vex accepts it and slides the map inside, then writes "waterproof sleeve" into the map legend.

SCENE 4. EXT. OBSERVATORY TRAIL - AFTERNOON

The final hill is steep. Vex climbs too quickly and reaches a fork with no sign. Pip catches up and compares the mountain's outline with his map. The observatory lies behind a cluster of pines. Vex follows his own hidden marks and arrives at an old stone wall. He has mapped the route to a closed quarry instead.

Pip calls him back. Together they reach the observatory before sunset. Nari's map is dry, and Odo signs the delivery receipt. Vex admits the visible road was the useful route. Luma points out that his secret marks would be valuable if he labeled them.

SCENE 5. INT. OBSERVATORY - EVENING

The apprentices compare their maps. Pip adds a note about the flooded stream and the repaired sign. Vex labels his marks: old quarry, steep slope, blocked shortcut. He keeps the secret ink for decorative borders. Nari awards the race to both for completing the delivery safely and updating the route.

VEX: We arrived together.

PIP: The map helped.

ODO: The bell helped me keep the package.

FINAL IMAGE: The two maps lie side by side beneath the observatory's telescope, one showing the road and the other showing the wrong way around it.
