# The Franchise Finale with No Sequel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sprawling franchise finales, cameo overload, world-ending stakes, and post-credit scenes that refuse to let a story end*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: The heroes of a long-running primate adventure franchise face their final threat: a citywide machine that keeps generating new villains, sequel hooks, and reasons to delay the ending.

Cast
- KARA, a capuchin hero who can climb any wall.
- NIM, a macaque strategist with a collection of old maps.
- PIP, a ring-tailed lemur inventor who knows every tool in the team van.
- VEX, an orangutan narrator who has appeared in every film but never left the opening credits.
- THE ARCHIVIST, a gibbon villain who stores unfinished plotlines in glass cases.
- CAMEO, a mandrill from a previous installment who arrives for one scene.

## Scene 1: The Final Threat

EXT. CANOPY CITY - NIGHT

A giant machine rises from the central plaza. Its screens display every unresolved problem from the series: a broken bridge, a missing compass, a mysterious map, and a villain who escaped in the second film.

NIM: The Archivist is combining all our unfinished stories.

KARA: We will stop the machine and close the portal.

PIP: Which portal?

A glowing doorway opens above the machine. The Archivist steps out, carrying a stack of sequel scripts.

ARCHIVIST: You cannot end the story. There is always another threat.

VEX: That is not how stories work.

ARCHIVIST: It is how franchises work.

## Scene 2: The Cameos

The team approaches the plaza. Cameo emerges from a bus, wearing an old costume from a previous film.

CAMEO: I have returned to help.

NIM: Who are you?

CAMEO: I was in the third movie.

PIP: I started in the fourth.

KARA: I was in the first.

VEX: I narrated all of them.

CAMEO asks what his role is. The heroes tell him the machine can only be stopped by disconnecting its story reel. Cameo attempts to give a long explanation, but no one has time for a recap.

## Scene 3: The Montage

The team uses familiar skills. Kara climbs the machine. Nim studies the map, finding a route through the plaza. Pip opens a tool panel and identifies the power switch. Vex speaks over the action, describing earlier adventures. The Archivist changes the machine's output, causing a new boss to appear: a giant clockwork bird.

PIP: The bird is from the holiday special.

NIM: I thought it was a promotional short.

VEX: It was both.

The bird's wings spread over the plaza, casting the heroes in a dramatic shadow. Kara reaches the gear housing, but a second screen shows an old rival waking in another district.

NIM: The machine is generating a sequel while we are still in the finale.

PIP: That is a bad use of power.

VEX: I recognize that rival. We defeated it beside the river.

KARA: We don't have to defeat it again. We have to disconnect the reel.

The group ignores the new threat and keeps moving. The rival stands up, sees the heroes leaving, and sits back down.

Cameo distracts the bird by waving his old costume cape. It catches on a gear, slowing the machine. Kara reaches the control platform.

## Scene 4: The Final Battle

The Archivist challenges Kara to a duel. She refuses to fight until the machine is shut down. Pip pulls the wrong lever, releasing a dozen minor villains from glass cases. They all begin arguing about their own backstories.

NIM: We cannot fight all of them.

KARA: We don't have to. They are stuck in their own introductions.

The heroes use the opening to reach the reel. The machine's lever is labeled FINAL FINAL ENDING. Pip disconnects the power cable instead. The screens go dark. The portal closes. The villains become quiet, no longer receiving cues.

ARCHIVIST: What happens to my story?

KARA: It can end here.

## Scene 5: The Farewell

The team returns to the plaza at sunrise. Cameo gives back the costume. The Archivist takes the unfinished scripts to the city archive. Each hero says goodbye, but the speech grows longer as they mention old events.

NIM: We stopped the machine.

PIP: We can put the tools away.

KARA: We can go home.

VEX: And our story is complete.

A small drone rises from the machine's wreckage and projects the words: "ONE MORE ADVENTURE?"

The team turns. Pip catches the drone with a net and powers it off.

PIP: No.

## Scene 6: The Credits

The city residents rebuild the plaza. The heroes help carry benches and patch paving stones. Cameo departs on the bus without another entrance. Vex narrates the final scene in one sentence.

VEX: The city continued after the heroes left.

## Post-Credits Scene

Black screen. A tiny light blinks. A hand reaches into frame, switches it off, and leaves.

## Final Image

The machine is quiet. The repaired plaza fills with ordinary residents, and the credits finish without a new door opening.
