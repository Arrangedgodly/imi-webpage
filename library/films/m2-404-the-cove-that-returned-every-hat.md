# The Cove That Returned Every Hat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: coastal adventure films, lost treasure clues, and tides that deliver the same object back*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
SERA, a macaque harbor guide
PIP, a capuchin sailor
TAVI, a gibbon naturalist
VEX, a lemur treasure hunter
THE SAILOR, a macaque who mends hats
THE TIDE, a silent performer

SCENE 1. EXT. ROCKY COVE - MORNING

A red hat washes onto the shore. Vex picks it up and finds a note inside the brim: "Follow the tide." Sera studies the shoreline.

VEX: A treasure clue.

TAVI: It is a hat.

PIP: The tide brought it.

Vex says the hat may belong to a missing captain. He sets it on a rock and scans the horizon. A wave knocks the hat into the water. Pip catches it with a boat hook.

SERA: The cove returns everything.

TAVI: It returns the hat.

Vex asks if anyone has found a chest. Sera points to a crack in the cliff. It is filled with hats.

SCENE 2. INT. CLIFF CAVE - LATER

The explorers search the cave. Hats hang from a low ceiling. Each has a different mark: blue thread, green feather, brass pin. Vex says they are clues left by previous treasure hunters.

TAVI: They are all wet.

PIP: They have been here a while.

Sera finds a tide chart scratched into the wall. It shows the water rising to the cave entrance, then falling back. Vex follows the highest mark and finds a small wooden box above the waterline.

VEX: The treasure.

He opens it. Inside are more hat pins, a needle, and thread. Tavi says the box is a repair kit. The hats have been lost by sailors and mended by someone in the cave.

Sera lifts one hat from the ceiling. A shell is tucked inside its crown. She shakes it, and sand pours out.

SERA: This one has been here longer.

VEX: It may contain a second clue.

PIP: It contains sand.

Tavi uses the needle to lift a tiny stitch holding the hat's band. Beneath it is a name, nearly worn away. Sera reads it aloud. It belongs to the sailor who lives beyond the cliff. Vex says they must follow the trail. The cave floor is damp and slopes toward the tide. Pip finds a row of small footprints leading to the passage.

TAVI: Those are not boot prints.

SERA: Someone carried the hats through here.

Vex sets the red hat on his head. It slides over his eyes. He takes it off and follows the footprints by touch, brushing his fingers along the wall.

PIP: The hat is not a lantern.

VEX: It is part of the expedition.

The hat falls backward into Sera's hands. She folds it and puts it in her pack.

SCENE 3. EXT. COVE - AFTERNOON

The water rises. Pip carries the hats to the boat. A wave pushes the boat away from shore. Vex jumps in and reaches for the oars. The boat hook catches the red hat, which floats beside the boat.

PIP: The hat is coming with us.

VEX: It is part of the clue.

Sera tells him to release it. Vex lets go. The hat drifts back toward the cave. Tavi watches the water. A small opening in the rocks becomes visible as the tide rises.

TAVI: The current goes into the cave.

SERA: And comes out at the other side.

Pip rows the boat through the opening. Vex insists on keeping the hat. The boat turns sideways in the narrow passage. The hat blocks the view of the rock wall.

VEX: I cannot see the entrance.

PIP: The hat is not a compass.

The boat bumps the wall. Sera guides it around a bend. Sunlight appears ahead. The passage opens into a shallow lagoon on the other side of the cliff.

SCENE 4. EXT. LAGOON - SUNSET

The crew lands in the lagoon. A small shack stands on shore. An old sailor sits outside, sewing a torn hat. She recognizes the red hat and asks where they found it.

VEX: In the cove. It had a message.

SAILOR: I wrote that.

TAVI: Why?

SAILOR: I lost my sewing kit and wanted someone to bring the hat back.

The box in the cave was her repair kit. She says sailors have left hats there for years, and the tide moves them between the cove and lagoon.

VEX: We crossed the cliff for a hat.

SAILOR: You crossed a passage.

Pip hands the sailor the red hat. She patches a hole in its brim. Vex asks if there is hidden treasure in the cave. The sailor points to the sewing box.

SCENE 5. EXT. COVE - NIGHT

The tide falls. The crew returns through the passage with the sailor. They gather the remaining hats and place them in the shack. Tavi adds a sign directing sailors to the repair kit.

Sera asks Vex whether he will keep the clue. He folds the note and puts it in the hat.

VEX: It was a good map.

TAVI: It said follow the tide.

VEX: The tide was moving.

FINAL IMAGE: The red hat rests on the shack's door. A small wave lifts it, then sets it back in the same place.
