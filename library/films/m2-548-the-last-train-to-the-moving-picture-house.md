# The Last Train to the Moving Picture House

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent-era melodrama parody about missing a screening because the film crew keeps adding title cards*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
DIRECTOR VEX, an orangutan
ACTOR MARA, a macaque
PROJECTIONIST ODO, a gibbon
TICKET KEEPER PIP, a capuchin
PIANIST NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. STATION PLATFORM - DUSK

Mara runs toward the train holding a film can. Vex steps in front of her with a sign: "THE LAST REEL!" He points to the clock. The minute hand is near six.

MARA: The screening starts at six. We have twelve minutes.

VEX: The audience needs to know the stakes.

ODO: The projector is already threaded. We need the can at the theater before the train leaves.

PIP: Tickets are ready. If you miss the train, the next one comes in an hour.

Vex raises another card: "AN HOUR OF DESPAIR."

MARA: We have twelve minutes, not an hour. Move.

SCENE 2. INT. TRAIN CAR - EVENING

The crew boards. Nari sets a small piano keyboard on the seat. Odo secures the film can beneath the bench.

VEX: We need title cards between every scene. The audience might misunderstand.

MARA: The film has no dialogue. One opening title and one ending title are enough.

PIP: The conductor is coming through. Put the keyboard away from the aisle.

Nari folds the stand. Vex holds up: "A MUSICAL INTERRUPTION." The conductor pauses, then points at the luggage rack.

ODO: The film can is secure. The train leaves in two minutes.

VEX: I have one more card: "WILL THEY ARRIVE?"

MARA: They will if we stop delaying the train.

SCENE 2A. INT. TRAIN CAR - CONTINUOUS

The train slows at a signal. Odo checks the film can latch. It remains closed. Vex tries to unfold a title card across the aisle.

PIP: The aisle has to stay open. Fold it and put it in the portfolio.

VEX: This card says the heroine is delayed by fate.

MARA: The signal is red. We are delayed by a train ahead. Put the card away.

NARI: The pianist can prepare a short bridge if the projector stalls, but we should not cover the conductor's aisle.

VEX: What if the audience gets restless?

PIP: They can look out the window. The train is still moving slowly.

Odo lifts the film can and checks the label: one reel, 400 feet, return to theater. The seal is intact.

ODO: The reel is safe. At this speed, we will arrive with three minutes to spare.

MARA: Then we don't need another title card. We need to get off at the right stop.

Vex holds up a small card reading "THE TRAIN ARRIVES." Mara takes it and places it in the portfolio.

SCENE 2B. INT. TRAIN CAR - CONTINUOUS

The conductor calls the theater stop. Pip takes the portfolio, and Mara lifts the film can by its handle.

PIP: Wait for the train to stop before opening the door.

VEX: The hero is poised at the threshold.

MARA: The door remains closed until the chime.

SFX: Brakes hiss. The train settles. Chime rings.

ODO: Stop confirmed. We can step down now.

VEX: The timing is perfect.

NARI: The timing is a train schedule.



PIP: The can is still latched, and the portfolio is closed.

MARA: Keep both in hand as we cross the platform. The theater entrance is across the street.

VEX: No chase?

MARA: Only if you run into traffic.
SCENE 3. INT. MOVING PICTURE HOUSE - NIGHT

The group enters just before six. Odo places the film can beside the projector. The audience sits. Vex takes the stage and lifts a card reading "THE STORY BEGINS."

PIP: The audience came to watch the film, not read your cards.

VEX: This card is essential.

MARA: Start the projector.

Odo threads the reel. Nari plays a simple chord as the first frame appears. The film shows Mara running for a train, but the opening card is blank.

ODO: The title card is missing.

VEX: I removed it to preserve suspense.

MARA: Then we begin without it. The image is clear.

SCENE 4. INT. THEATER - CONTINUOUS

The silent film shows the same platform, the same train, and the same hurried run. This time Mara boards before the doors close. Vex reaches for his sign, but Pip sits on the stack.

NARI: The audience understands. She made the train.

The reel ends. The screen goes white. Vex stands, holding up his final card: "THE END?"

MARA: The film ended. That's all.

ODO: The reel is safely rewound.

PIP: The next screening starts at seven. We can put the cards away before then.

TAG: Vex turns the final card over. On its back is the actual theater schedule, with no question mark.
