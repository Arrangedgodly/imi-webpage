# The Night the Lights Stayed On

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble holiday movies, coincidental reunions, and last-minute public events saved by a single heartfelt speech*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A winter-market power outage sends several neighbors on separate errands, but the event succeeds through labeled lanterns and a tested backup circuit rather than a speech.

Cast: Luma, a macaque market manager; Pip, a capuchin baker; Kora, a lemur electrician; and Boro, a gorilla delivery driver.

SCENE 1. EXT. WINTER MARKET, LATE AFTERNOON

The market lights go out. Luma announces a pause. Pip's oven is on a separate circuit and keeps baking. Boro arrives with a cart of lanterns. Kora checks the power board and finds one overloaded outlet.

PIP: Is this the moment we all learn what matters?

KORA: It is the moment we unplug the kettle.

Luma asks vendors to keep aisles clear. A seller tries plugging decorative lights into the same outlet as an electric kettle. Kora pulls the plug before the breaker trips again.

KORA: One device per marked outlet.

SELLER: The lights are part of the display.

KORA: So is the exit sign.

Boro rolls the lantern cart to its marked station. A child asks if the festival is canceled. Luma says they are checking the backup plan. Boro rolls the lantern cart to the marked station. A child asks if the festival is canceled. Luma says they are checking the backup plan.

SCENE 2. INT. MARKET SERVICE TENT, MOMENTS LATER

Kora tests the backup circuit and finds it powers the exit signs and the first-aid station. The decorative lights are not on the backup.

LUMA: Can we run every vendor from one generator?

KORA: Not safely. The backup is for exits and first aid.

PIP: The bread is warm.

Pip brings a tray to the service tent. Boro gives him a lantern. Pip tries to hang it above the oven. Kora points to the hot surface and Pip sets it by the stall entrance instead. Luma checks the roster and learns two sellers brought no battery lamps. Boro finds two spares on his cart.

Pip brings a tray of warm bread to the service tent. Boro gives him a lantern. Pip tries to hang it above the oven, but Kora asks him to keep it away from heat. He places it at the stall entrance instead. Luma checks the vendor roster and discovers two sellers have not brought battery lanterns.

Boro delivers a spare pair from the cart. One seller recognizes him from a previous winter market. They share a brief greeting, then carry the lanterns to the stalls. The film music swells; Kora interrupts to ask them not to block the service panel.

SCENE 3. EXT. WINTER MARKET, EVENING

The market opens with lanterns along the aisles. Kora isolates the overloaded outlet and replaces the damaged power strip. She tests each vendor circuit one by one. The lights return at half brightness. LUMA: Should we make an announcement?

PIP: The stalls are open, and the exit signs are visible.

KORA: Give me a minute to test the stage outlet.

The choir conductor asks if the finale is canceled. Luma says they can sing without the microphone while Kora tests the circuit. She plugs in one mic and checks the load. The choir sings acoustically as the lights return at half brightness. Boro delivers cider without spilling. A guest asks if the outage was planned. Luma points to the inspection tag and says no.

A choir arrives to perform. Their conductor asks if they should sing the scheduled finale. Luma says the stage outlet is not yet tested. Kora checks it, plugs in one microphone, and measures the load. The choir performs acoustically while the lights remain steady. Boro delivers hot cider to the performers without spilling.

The neighbors do not all know one another. They still help restock the lantern table, direct visitors, and carry empty trays. A guest asks if the power failure was planned as part of the festival. Luma says it was not, and points to Kora's inspection tag.

SCENE 4. EXT. WINTER MARKET, CLOSING

The event ends on schedule. Vendors switch off their equipment in order. Kora records which outlet overloaded and posts a limit notice. Luma thanks the volunteers and adds the lantern count to next year's checklist. Boro returns the cart to storage. Pip boxes the unsold bread for the morning shelter.

No one delivers a speech about the magic of community. Luma reads out the closing tasks, and the vendors complete them. The choir folds its stands and leaves the stage clear. Kora locks the service tent after checking the first-aid station. A seller asks whether the lanterns must be returned. Boro counts them onto the cart. Pip boxes the remaining bread for the shelter. Luma signs the closing sheet, and Kora unplugs the overloaded strip for repair.

FINAL IMAGE: The last lantern is switched off at the marked station. Across the plaza, the exit sign remains lit until the final visitor reaches the gate.
