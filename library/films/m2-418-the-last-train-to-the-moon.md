# The Last Train to the Moon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: science-fiction rescue epics, countdown clocks, and launch crews with one missing checklist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
PIP, a capuchin station engineer
MARA, a macaque pilot
VEX, an orangutan mission director
NARI, a lemur botanist
ODO, a gibbon passenger

SCENE 1. INT. LUNAR STATION - NIGHT

A train car stands on a launch platform beneath a painted moon. A digital clock counts down from twenty minutes. Vex points to a checklist.

VEX: We depart at midnight.

MARA: The track ends at the crater.

VEX: The moon is mostly crater.

PIP: The track ends at a crater wall.

Nari brings a potted fern aboard. Odo carries a suitcase labeled "EARTH."

ODO: Is this train returning to Earth?

VEX: Eventually.

PIP: It has one engine and three wheels.

MARA: The fourth wheel is in the checklist.

Vex turns a page. The wheel is listed as "arrives with passengers." Odo looks at his suitcase.

ODO: I packed socks, not wheels.

The countdown continues. Pip pulls a canvas cover from the front of the train. A bicycle bell is mounted on the nose.

SCENE 2. INT. ENGINE BAY - LATER

Mara checks the controls. The engine is a small electric motor attached to the rear axle. Pip opens a panel and finds a basket of spare gears.

PIP: The train can move.

VEX: How far?

PIP: To the wall.

Vex asks Nari to calculate a safe route. She unfurls a star chart, but the train's headlights project the chart's moon onto the ceiling.

NARI: We are already on the moon.

VEX: Then we have arrived.

MARA: We have not left the platform.

The fern leans toward the headlight. Nari turns the pot so it faces forward. Odo sits in the passenger seat and puts on a helmet. The helmet is too large; its visor covers the checklist.

ODO: I cannot see the schedule.

VEX: The schedule cannot see you either.

Pip sorts the spare gears, but there is no wheel among them. He tightens an axle bolt with a spoon from Odo's suitcase.

Vex reads the checklist aloud while Pip works. "Confirm gravity, confirm engine, confirm passengers." The crew looks at Odo, who is strapped into the seat. He raises one hand. The helmet slips down again.

ODO: Passenger confirmed.

MARA: Gravity?

Pip lets the loose wheel go. It drops onto the floor and rolls straight toward the platform edge. Nari catches it with the fern pot. The fern bends, then springs upright.

NARI: Gravity confirmed.

VEX: Mark the plant as auxiliary crew.

Pip adds the fern's name to the manifest. Vex tries to pronounce it, but the train horn interrupts him. The horn is a rubber squeeze bulb; it gives a soft squeak. Odo salutes the fern.

SCENE 3. EXT. LUNAR PLATFORM - 11:55 P.M.

The team boards. Vex begins a dramatic launch speech. The train bell rings when Mara nudges the throttle. Everyone flinches.

VEX: That was not the countdown.

PIP: That is the bell.

MARA: It is the only working alarm.

The clock shows five minutes. Nari checks the fern. Its pot has slid toward the door. She wedges it between two seats. Odo straps down his suitcase. Vex asks Pip to close the cabin hatch, but Pip sees the spare wheel still outside.

Vex turns to the clock and makes the last five minutes sound like an historic countdown. Each number triggers a different station light. At four, the cabin lamp blinks. At three, the restroom sign lights up. At two, the fern's grow lamp switches on and gives it a halo.

VEX: One minute to destiny.

PIP: One minute to the wheel.

PIP: We need the wheel.

VEX: We have four.

PIP: One is on the platform.

Mara cuts the motor. Odo gets out and rolls the wheel to the front. His helmet visor falls shut. He guides the wheel by sound, following the bell as Pip rings it.

ODO: I can hear the train.

VEX: We need a visual confirmation.

NARI: You have a visual helmet.

SCENE 4. INT. TRAIN CABIN - MIDNIGHT

The wheel is installed. Vex resumes the speech from the sentence where he stopped. Mara releases the brake. The train rolls forward, then stops at the painted crater wall.

VEX: We have reached the moon.

MARA: We started on the moon.

PIP: We have reached the wall.

The team steps outside. A hand-painted sign reads "LUNAR RIM, TWO METERS." Nari sets the fern beside it. The plant's shadow falls across the sign and looks like a second rider.

ODO: We have discovered life.

NARI: It is the fern.

VEX: The fern has completed the mission.

Mara taps the brake. The train rolls backward toward the platform. The crew hurries aboard, each carrying one loose piece: the chart, the checklist, the spoon.

SCENE 5. INT. STATION - LATER

Back at the platform, Vex stamps the mission log "ARRIVED." Pip writes "RETURNED." Mara adds "did not leave." Nari places the fern by the window, where it can see the painted moon.

ODO: When is the next train?

PIP: When we find the fourth wheel.

VEX: We found it.

PIP: We know where it is.

FINAL IMAGE: The train's bell rings as the crew pushes it into the station, and the countdown clock resets to twenty minutes.
