# The Slowest Lap

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: auto-racing underdog films, pit-crew montage clichés, and last-lap showboating*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A slow loris enters a neighborhood soapbox race with a handmade cart, while the rival's high-speed modifications keep breaking and the careful pit crew wins the real contest.

Cast: Momo, a slow loris driver; Bex, a macaque mechanic; Turbo, a capuchin rival; Coach Nibs, a gibbon; and Judge Shell, a tortoise race official.

SCENE 1. EXT. HILLTOP START LINE, MORNING

Turbo's cart gleams with spoilers, lights, and a horn. Momo's is a wooden box with bicycle wheels. Bex checks each bolt. Coach Nibs addresses the team.

NIBS: This is our moment.

MOMO: Is the brake working?

BEX: Yes.

MOMO: Then it's our moment.

Turbo revs his hand-crank motor and the horn plays a victory fanfare. Judge Shell asks him to turn it off. Turbo says the horn is part of his brand. Shell points to the rules: no engines, no horns, no loose accessories.

SCENE 2. EXT. HILL, FIRST HEAT

The starter waves a flag. Turbo launches ahead, then swerves when the horn startles pigeons. A spoiler detaches and lands on the track. Shell stops the race. The crowd groans as volunteers sweep the lane.

COACH NIBS: This delay tests our spirit.

BEX: It tests whether we can clear a spoiler.

Momo helps sweep. Turbo complains that his momentum is gone. Shell says the race is paused, not his momentum. The kitchen timer used as the official clock rings while they are still sweeping. Shell silences it and asks someone to find the actual stopwatch.

SCENE 3. EXT. PIT AREA, AFTERNOON

Nibs asks Momo to practice a victory pose. She tries to raise both arms but loses balance in the stationary cart. Bex tightens the seat belt. Nibs asks her to do it again for the camera. Momo says she would rather practice the turn. Bex draws the corner on the ground with chalk; Nibs adds a starting line, a finish line, and a podium. The diagram now covers the entire pit area.

TURBO: Where is my cart?

BEX: Under the diagram.

The crew lifts the paper. Turbo rolls his cart out, tests the horn once, and removes it himself when Shell looks over.

Turbo's crew replaces the spoiler, then the lights, then the horn. Each addition makes the cart heavier. Bex checks Momo's brake, tire pressure, helmet strap, and water bottle. Coach Nibs asks for a training montage. She demonstrates the check sequence once. It takes twelve seconds.

NIBS: Can we make it more inspirational?

BEX: We can play music.

NIBS: And slow motion?

MOMO: Then we miss the race.

Turbo rolls past, testing the horn. It falls off and plays one note as it hits the ground. He blames the track. Bex picks it up and hands it to him. Judge Shell removes it from the equipment list.

SCENE 4. EXT. FINAL RACE COURSE, SUNSET

Before the final, the announcer introduces Turbo as a fearless speed machine. Momo is introduced as a careful driver. Nibs asks for a more dramatic title. Momo suggests "driver." The announcer uses it. The crowd laughs; Turbo waves as if the joke is his.

At the final, Shell checks every cart. Turbo asks whether the race can start before he loses his nerve. Shell says he can start when the helmet strap is fastened. Turbo fixes it himself. Momo checks the lane markers and points out a hay bale that shifted toward the turn. Volunteers move it back.

The flag drops. Turbo leads. At the last bend he tries to pass on the inside and clips a hay bale. Momo stays in the marked lane, brakes before the barrier, and crosses the line just ahead. She stops cleanly. Turbo rolls to a stop beside her and asks if the finish counts without a dramatic drift.

SHELL: It counts by the clock.

SCENE 5. EXT. FINISH LINE, NIGHT

Momo receives the safe-driving ribbon. Turbo's cart fails inspection because a wheel is loose. Bex tightens it. Turbo asks her to check his brake too. Coach Nibs says this is the most cooperative rivalry in racing history. Bex says it is the first time he has helped inspect his cart.

The race crew receives a ribbon for clearing debris. Momo and Turbo carry the cones back together. Nibs asks for one final victory lap. Momo chooses a slow roll so the younger racers can keep up.

At the awards table, Momo trades the ribbon for a safety checklist so the next racers can use it.

FINAL IMAGE: A pigeon lands on Turbo's detached horn. It sounds one victory note. Turbo bows to the bird; Judge Shell records the pigeon as the day's fastest racer.
