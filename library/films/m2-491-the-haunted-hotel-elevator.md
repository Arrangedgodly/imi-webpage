# The Haunted Hotel Elevator

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: haunted-house films that treat every mechanical noise as a supernatural warning and every locked door as a dare*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A hotel night clerk investigates an elevator that stops at an unlisted floor, where its ghostly visitor turns out to be a room-service cart blocking the sensor.

Cast: clerk Tavi, a macaque; bellhop Omi, an orangutan; guest Pella, a lemur; maintenance worker Kesh, a capuchin; night auditor Rulo, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

The elevator doors open by themselves. No one is inside. Tavi looks up from the desk. A soft bell rings from the shaft. Omi enters from the service corridor carrying folded towels.

TAVI: It stopped between floors again.

OMI: The old plate above the indicator says twelve.

RULO: This hotel has eleven floors.

The doors close. The elevator moves, then opens on the lobby again. A damp footprint appears on the tile. Pella steps from the side corridor wearing wet slippers and carrying a tray.

PELLA: I called for towels.

Omi points at the footprints leading from the service hall, not the elevator.

SCENE 2 - INT. ELEVATOR - MOMENTS LATER

Kesh enters with a flashlight. Tavi follows, holding a hotel map. They ride up and stop at the service landing beside floor eleven. The lights flicker. A bell rings. A shadow crosses the narrow window in the door.

TAVI: Someone is outside.

KESH: We are at the service landing.

The doors open. A room-service cart stands directly in front of the sensor. A silver lid reflects the flashlight like a pale face. Omi appears at the far end of the landing, pushing a second cart from the laundry lift. He sees the first cart, parks his own at the wall and pulls the blocking cart away. The doors shut and the elevator resumes moving.

SCENE 3 - INT. HOTEL BASEMENT - LATER

Kesh opens the control panel. Tavi finds a disconnected wire tagged FLOOR 12, left from the renovation. The elevator display has eleven numbered floors; there is no twelfth stop. The wire is not connected to a control, but it hangs against the door sensor and intermittently interrupts its signal. Rulo says the obsolete label should have been removed years ago.

RULO: No ghost and no hidden floor. Just a loose wire beside the sensor.

KESH: And a cart parked in its path.

A service bell rings upstairs. Omi rushes to answer it. The elevator doors open beside the laundry chute; a stack of towels tumbles out and lands around him. He holds one up like a shroud.

OMI: The ghost has made a bed.

Tavi reads the old maintenance log. Every unexplained stop happened after a cart was parked close to a sensor or the loose wire shifted against it.

Rulo reads from the old renovation log. Floor twelve had been removed from the panel, and the night crew left a wire tag behind. Pella asks whether a guest has been trapped. Kesh says the car cannot travel to a floor it does not serve. Omi rolls his cart away from the sensor; the elevator doors close and open cleanly. Rulo removes the tag and ties it to the maintenance log. Omi offers the towels as a ghostly shroud. Rulo places them back on his cart.

SCENE 4 - INT. HOTEL LOBBY - LATER

Pella returns with the empty tray. She says the elevator kept opening while she was waiting for towels. Tavi removes the obsolete TWELVE plate from above the indicator. Kesh resets the floor list and asks Omi to move the carts to the marked alcove. Omi does so, but the towels make the alcove look like a stack of pale figures.

RULO: The dead are organized by linen size.

The elevator opens. Everyone watches. The car is empty, and the doors close normally. Pella notices a wet footprint on the tile. It belongs to her own slipper; she had stepped in a puddle by the service entrance.

Rulo prints a maintenance report and labels the obsolete plate for storage. He asks Tavi to sign. Tavi writes "haunting resolved" in the space marked cause. Kesh crosses it out and writes "loose wire and cart obstruction." The printer jams and feeds the report back out blank. Omi places a towel beneath it to catch the toner.

SCENE 5 - INT. HOTEL LOBBY - DAWN

The night ends. Tavi leaves a sign by the elevator: FLOOR 12 REMOVED. Rulo checks the final count. Omi returns the towels. Kesh finishes the control panel repair. Pella takes the stairs to avoid getting wet again.

TAVI: We have defeated the haunting.

KESH: We secured a loose wire and moved a cart.

The elevator bell rings once. Everyone freezes. The doors open and reveal a mop leaning against the wall, pressing the call button with its handle. Omi lifts it away and puts it in the closet.

FINAL IMAGE: The repaired elevator travels to eleven with an empty corridor in the mirror, while the mop stands upright in a labeled closet.
