# The Volcano Is Clearly Painted

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: visual-effects blockbusters, green-screen spectacle, and characters who describe effects the audience can plainly see*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: An effects crew films an erupting volcano in a studio, but the actors keep narrating the obvious while the painted backdrop rolls sideways.

Cast: Rava, a macaque effects supervisor; Kett, a gorilla actor; Lio, a lemur camera operator; and Puck, a capuchin sound mixer.

SCENE 1. INT. STUDIO STAGE, MORNING

Kett stands before a green screen. A foam rock sits by his foot. Rava explains that the volcano will be added after filming. Kett looks toward the blank wall and raises his arms.

KETT: The mountain is collapsing!

LIO: It is a green curtain.

Rava asks Kett to look at the taped eyeline. He looks at the camera instead.

RAVA: The volcano is on the left.

KETT: My left or the audience's?

LIO: The monitor's left.

Kett turns toward the sound booth. Puck plays the rumble; it is too loud, and a light fixture shakes. Lio stops the take before the lamp swings into the screen. Rava tapes a second eyeline mark near the lens. Kett asks if he should pretend the sound cue shook the mountain. Rava says the crew knows it was a speaker. Puck adds a rumble. The sound is too loud, and a light fixture shakes.

SCENE 2. INT. CONTROL BOOTH, LATER

Lio shows the first composite. The volcano is enormous, but the light comes from the wrong direction. Kett casts a shadow toward the sun. Rava points it out. Kett says the mountain's power has bent the light.

RAVA: The lamp is over there.

KETT: Then move the sun.

Lio rotates the background. The painted horizon rolls sideways. Puck asks whether they can cover the movement with an explosion. Rava says the audience will see it. Kett suggests a second explosion. Rava asks him to stop explaining the effect and react to it.

SCENE 3. INT. GREEN-SCREEN STAGE, AFTERNOON

The crew marks where falling ash should land. Kett steps onto the wrong mark and catches a foam boulder before it rolls into the camera track. Rava asks him to return to his eyeline. He looks at the tape, then at the blank wall.

KETT: I can see the lava.

LIO: You can see the exit sign.

Rava adds a small monitor beside the camera showing the rough composite. Kett watches the volcano appear on the screen and responds at the right moment. Puck layers in the rumble, then lowers it when Rava points to the shaking fixture.

The backdrop glitches, leaving the volcano's crater floating over a studio chair. Lio freezes the image. Kett says the mountain has become a throne. Rava asks him to hold the pose while the compositor corrects the mask.

KETT: I see the lava.

RAVA: Look at the eyeline.

KETT: I see the chair.

LIO: The chair is standing in for a boulder.

Kett steps around the chair instead of leaping over it. The crew checks the composite. The crater appears behind him, but its shadow falls across his face. Rava moves the studio lamp and asks him to repeat the reaction. This time the shadow matches the ridge.

KETT: I thought the mountain was falling.

RAVA: Better.

Puck asks for one more rumble, but Lio points out that the desk lamp is still shaking. Puck reduces the volume. The effect reaches the room without moving the set. Kett checks the monitor, waits for the crater cue, and reacts without describing what the audience sees. Kett holds it so long that Puck's boom microphone dips into frame. Lio spots it and resets.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The final shot shows the volcano erupting behind Kett. The shadow now matches the light, the horizon stays level, and the sound is strong without rattling the lamps. Kett asks if he should explain that the volcano is an effect. Rava says the audience can see the credits.

At the preview, a child points out the painted rock beside Kett. Lio keeps it in the frame. Rava says it was part of the set, not the volcano. Kett picks it up and carries it to the prop box.

KETT: The rock is safe.

RAVA: The scene is finished.

At the preview, a child points out that the painted rock was visible beside Kett. Lio keeps it in the frame. The child asks if the volcano will erupt again. Kett looks at the monitor and says the next eruption is scheduled for the effects team.

FINAL IMAGE: The green screen rolls away, revealing the studio wall. Puck turns down the rumble, and the volcano continues erupting silently on the monitor.
