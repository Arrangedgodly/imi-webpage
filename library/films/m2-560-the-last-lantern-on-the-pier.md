# The Last Lantern on the Pier

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: coastal adventure films that use a lantern signal to summon a boat that is already docked*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
LIGHTKEEPER NARI, a lemur
SAILOR VEX, an orangutan
MECHANIC MARA, a macaque
RUNNER PIP, a capuchin
HARBOR CLERK ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. PIER - DUSK

A lantern flashes at the end of the pier. Vex runs toward it with a coil of rope.

VEX: The last signal. A vessel is calling for help.

NARI: It is the harbor lantern. The light flickers because the shutter is loose.

MARA: The boat is already tied at the near dock.

ODO: Its name is on the arrival board: Morning Star, arrived at five.

PIP: The crew are unloading supplies.

VEX: They may be prisoners aboard their own ship.

SCENE 2. EXT. LANTERN POST - CONTINUOUS

Mara steadies the ladder while Nari climbs one rung and checks the shutter.

MARA: Power off before touching the housing.

NARI: Switch is off. The hinge screw is loose.

VEX: The signal has stopped.

ODO: The lantern is dark because it is being repaired.

PIP: I'll ask the crew if they need help unloading, from the dock.

NARI: The shutter can be tightened with the small wrench. No one needs to climb above the safety rail.

Mara hands the wrench up. Nari tightens the hinge and checks the housing.

SCENE 2A. EXT. PIER - DUSK

Mara carries the ladder to the lantern post. Pip stays below with the wrench and a tool pouch. Nari checks the power switch.

NARI: Switch is off. I'll confirm the lamp is dark before Mara climbs.

MARA: Confirmed. The ladder feet are on level boards, and Pip is holding the side rail.

VEX: A silent signal from the sea.

ODO: No vessel has called. The arrival board is current through five o'clock.

Pip hands the wrench up only after Mara asks for it. She loosens the housing and tightens the shutter hinge.

MARA: The screw was one turn loose. I'll test the shutter by hand while the power stays off.

NARI: It opens and closes smoothly. No binding.

MARA: Closing the housing now. The gasket is seated.

She descends. Nari steps to the switch and waits for Mara's signal.

NARI: Ready to restore power?

MARA: Housing secure. Restore it.

The lantern comes on steadily. Its beam crosses the pier and water.

VEX: The final lantern shines again.

ODO: The light is functioning. The Morning Star is still tied at the near dock.

PIP: Their crew asked for help with the barrels. We have the cart ready.

MARA: Let's carry those from the dock to the storehouse. Keep the walkway clear and use the cart on the level path.

Vex takes the handle, Pip steadies the load, and they move together. One barrel rocks; Mara stops them and adjusts the cart straps before they continue.

SCENE 2B. EXT. STOREHOUSE PATH - NIGHT

The cart reaches a small ramp. Pip asks Vex to stop before they reach it.

PIP: The right wheel is loose. If we go up the ramp, the barrel could shift.

MARA: Set the brake. I'll check the axle bolt.

VEX: The lantern signal is growing dim.

NARI: The lantern is steady. The water reflection is moving as the waves move.

Mara tightens the axle bolt with the wrench. Pip checks both straps, and Odo holds the storehouse door open.

MARA: Wheel is secure. We can go up the ramp at a slow pace.

VEX: The final cargo ascends.

PIP: Keep the cart level. Stop if the load moves.

They roll the barrels over the threshold and secure them against the wall. Odo closes the storehouse door, then returns the cart to the dock.

ODO: Cargo delivered. Door locked. Path clear.

NARI: The Morning Star crew has the water they asked for. The ship remains moored.

VEX: The signal was answered by a cart.

MARA: It was answered by the harbor crew. The lantern was just being repaired.

SCENE 3. EXT. NEAR DOCK - EVENING

Pip returns with a note. The Morning Star crew need a hand carrying two water barrels to the storehouse.

VEX: A distress message.

PIP: A request for help. They're fine; the barrels are heavy.

MARA: We can carry them with the dock cart. The walkway must stay clear.

VEX: Then the ship is not in danger?

ODO: The crew are unloading safely, and the boat remains tied.

SCENE 4. EXT. LANTERN POST - NIGHT

The lantern is repaired. Nari flips the switch. Its beam sweeps over the water. The Morning Star remains at the dock, ropes taut.

NARI: Light is steady. Shutter is secure.

VEX: The signal has been answered.

MARA: The lantern is working. The boat was already home.

PIP: Barrels are in the storehouse. The dock is clear.

TAG: Vex coils his rope dramatically, then uses it to tie down the empty cart.
