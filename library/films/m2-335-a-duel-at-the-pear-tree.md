# A Duel at the Pear Tree

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: swashbuckling adventure, rival heirs, dramatic sword duels, and treasure maps that are less useful than local knowledge*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
LORD PIP, a squirrel monkey orchard heir
LADY NARI, an orangutan cartographer
CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque adventurer
OLD SULA, a ring-tailed lemur gardener
TOM, a capuchin messenger

SCENE 1. EXT. OLD ORCHARD - MORNING

A windblown map points toward a pear tree. Lord Pip arrives in a velvet coat and draws a polished wooden sword. Captain Vex arrives from the opposite path with a metal blade and a compass. They circle the tree while Lady Nari measures its shadow.

PIP: The map says the treasure lies beneath this tree.

VEX: The map says the tree is north of the creek.

NARI: It is beside the creek.

Both duelists argue that their map is correct. A pear drops between them. They stop to avoid stepping on it.

SCENE 2. INT. ORCHARD SHED - LATER

Nari unfolds the map on a workbench. Its ink is faded; it may be a century old. Pip claims the orchard belongs to his family, while Vex says his ancestors buried a chest there. Sula enters with pruning shears and says the tree has been there longer than either family record.

SULA: You can dig after asking permission.

PIP: We are racing for treasure.

SULA: Then race to ask me.

Vex and Pip sprint to the door. They collide, fall into a pile of baskets, and emerge with matching bruises. Tom arrives with a letter from the town archive. It says the orchard was donated to the community and the chest, if found, belongs to the garden association.

SCENE 3. EXT. ORCHARD - AFTERNOON

Before searching, Vex challenges Pip to a proper duel for the right to dig. Nari insists they duel with garden tools, so each chooses a long-handled rake. They circle the tree as if fencing. The tines catch in the grass, and both rakes pull their owners into a slow spin. Sula calls time and asks them to rake the fallen leaves into a pile. They finish the task without a winner.

PIP: The leaves have been defeated.

VEX: By a team effort.

A gust carries the map away. Tom pins it with a watering can, revealing a faint route scratched into the workbench. Nari compares it with the old map and notices the original marks show the location of the shed, not the tree. The shed is where the gardener would have stored the box.

SCENE 3A. EXT. ORCHARD - CONTINUOUS

The rivals agree to search the ground without harming the tree. They pace off the map's measurements, but the map uses the old length of a gardener's stride. Nari asks Sula to demonstrate. Sula walks the original path, stopping at a flat stone half hidden under grass.

They lift the stone and find a small wooden box. Vex draws his sword for the reveal. Pip draws his. Nari asks them to open it carefully. Inside are seed labels, a pair of pruning gloves, and a note from the original gardener describing how to graft pear branches.

VEX: No gold?

SULA: There is a tree.

PIP: There are seeds.

Vex examines the gloves. The fingers are too small for him, but they fit Tom. The letter says the box was meant for whoever would tend the orchard next. Pip and Vex both claim they are heirs to the gardening instructions.

SCENE 4. EXT. ORCHARD PATH - SUNSET

A storm rolls in. Vex tries to protect the map with his cape, but the cape catches on a branch. Pip cuts it free with the wooden sword. The map begins to tear. Nari places it between two boards and tells them to stop waving it in the wind. Sula brings the box under the shed roof.

Tom reads the remaining directions aloud. They explain how to graft a healthy branch onto a damaged pear tree. The rivals notice a split limb above the path. Together they use a rope to support it while Sula makes the cut. Vex ties the knot; Pip holds the ladder; Nari checks the angle. No one calls it a duel.

SCENE 5. EXT. OLD ORCHARD - MORNING

The branch is secured. The town association agrees to preserve the box and plant the seeds in the community rows. Pip and Vex return their swords to the costume shed. They each take a gardening tool and volunteer for the next workday. Nari updates the map with a new marker for the pear tree and a note that says ASK SULA BEFORE DIGGING.

VEX: The map led us to treasure.

PIP: It led us to instructions.

SULA: The tree led you to work.

FINAL IMAGE: The two rivals prune opposite sides of the same branch as a ripe pear drops neatly into the open basket between them.
