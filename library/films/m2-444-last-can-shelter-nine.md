# The Last Can in Shelter Nine

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: post-apocalyptic survival films that turn limited supplies into moral tests and overlook that the pantry is fully stocked*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: After a storm knocks out the community center lights, a group of monkeys stages an end-of-world survival drama over one can of beans, despite the working pantry next door.

Cast: Reka, a macaque leader; Davi, a gibbon cook; Toma, a capuchin radio operator; Peli, a lemur scout; caretaker Voss, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY CENTER - NIGHT

The lights blink out. A storm rumbles. Reka holds a can of beans above a table. Toma switches on a battery radio.

TOMA: Power is out on this block. The generator should start in ten minutes.

REKA: We have one can. It must feed the shelter.

DAVI: The pantry has cases of food.

REKA: The pantry is beyond the dark corridor.

PELI: I can scout it.

VOSS: The flashlight is on the shelf. The pantry door is beside the boiler room.

Reka turns toward the corridor, then raises the can again.

REKA: We need a ration plan.

SCENE 2 - INT. COMMUNITY CENTER - CONTINUOUS

Davi divides the single can into four bowls with a spoon. The portions are tiny. Peli shines the flashlight. The beam reveals a printed pantry sign directly over the door.

PELI: The supply room is right here.

REKA: The signal says we should conserve.

TOMA: The radio says the generator is delayed, not that the world ended.

REKA: We cannot trust every broadcast.

Voss opens the door. Inside are shelves of beans, soup, rice and crackers, all labeled by date.

SCENE 2A - INT. COMMUNITY CENTER HALL - NIGHT

Reka draws a shelter perimeter on a chalkboard. Peli carries chairs to block the corridor. Toma moves the radio onto a shelf. Davi holds the can of beans.

REKA: We secure the north passage first.

VOSS: That passage is how the volunteers bring supplies in.

REKA: We can use the side route.

PELI: The side route is the same hallway.

A chair scrapes across the floor and bumps the pantry door. It opens a few inches. A stack of crackers slides into view.

DAVI: The pantry is still open.

REKA: The door has been breached.

VOSS: The door was not locked. I left it open for the food delivery.

Reka pushes the chair aside. The chalkboard plan now has a large arrow ending at the pantry.

TOMA: The map has located our supplies.

REKA: The map was drawn by me.

Voss turns on the flashlight and leads them through the doorway.

SCENE 3 - INT. PANTRY - MOMENTS LATER

Reka inspects the shelves as if crossing a wasteland. Davi takes a pot from the rack. Peli checks the inventory sheet.

PELI: There are eighteen cans of beans, six cans of soup and rice for forty servings.

REKA: The supplies have been preserved.

VOSS: It is the center's emergency pantry. We stocked it last month.

TOMA: The radio said the storm might last until morning.

DAVI: I can make a pot of rice and beans. We can share it with anyone who comes in.

Reka holds the single can tightly.

REKA: This can is the last one from the table.

DAVI: It is also open.

SCENE 4 - INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - LATER

The generator starts. Lights return. Davi cooks the beans with rice and vegetables. Peli sets out bowls. Reka writes ration marks on a clipboard, but the marks do not match the number of people arriving.

REKA: We have seven bowls and nine guests.

VOSS: The pot has enough for ten. Use the ladle evenly.

Toma checks the radio. The storm warning has been lifted. The neighborhood is safe.

TOMA: The broadcast never said the world ended.

REKA: It said stay inside.

VOSS: That was for the storm.

SCENE 5 - INT. COMMUNITY CENTER - MORNING

The group serves breakfast. The original can sits empty beside the pot. ReKa crosses out her ration marks and places the clipboard on the pantry shelf.

PELI: The radio says the road is clear.

DAVI: We can send a bowl to the generator crew.

REKA: Take two. They kept the lights on.

Voss closes the pantry door. ReKa stands beside the emergency plan, which lists food, flashlight, blankets and local radio.

REKA: Next time, we will check the pantry before planning the end of civilization.

TOMA: The pantry was on the sign.

REKA: Next time, we will also read the sign.

Davi ladles the last portion into the final bowl and leaves it by the door for the generator crew.

FINAL IMAGE: The single empty can rests in a recycling bin beside a full pantry shelf, while sunlight comes through the windows and warms the remaining bowls.
