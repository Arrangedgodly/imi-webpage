# The Road Trip with No Shortcut

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble road-trip comedies, mismatched travel companions, repeated detours, and roadside revelations that arrive before the destination*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A troop of primates must deliver a restored bell to a hilltop festival before sunset, but every shortcut leads them back to the same roundabout.

Cast
- NARA, a capuchin driver who follows road signs literally.
- BO, a macaque drummer who wants to play the bell at the festival.
- TIL, a ring-tailed lemur map reader with a tail that keeps unfolding the route.
- UNCLE VOS, a gibbon passenger who remembers every road except the current one.
- PEL, a mandrill mechanic who travels with a tool roll.

## Scene 1: Departure

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

A polished bronze bell is strapped into the back of a small van. The festival begins at sunset. Nara checks the map. Bo taps the bell gently; it rings a rich note.

NARA: We take the north road, then the ridge road.

TIL: The bridge is closed. I marked the detour.

VOS: I know a shortcut.

PEL: You said that last time and led us through a car wash.

VOS: The van was clean.

The group departs. The bell rings each time Bo brakes too sharply.

## Scene 2: The First Detour

EXT. ROUNDABOUT - LATE MORNING

Three exits are marked NORTH ROAD. Nara takes the first. After several minutes, they return to the same roundabout.

NARA: We are back where we started.

VOS: We have made excellent time to this point.

TIL: The road sign rotated. The wind pushed it around.

PEL: We need to tie it down.

VOS: I can read the mountain.

BO: Which mountain?

VOS: The one behind us.

They fasten the sign. Nara takes the second exit. This time the van reaches a farm lane blocked by a flock of macaques moving a cart of melons.

NARA: We should wait.

BO: I can play the bell so they know we are coming.

The bell rings. The flock moves toward the sound, blocking the lane more completely. Bo stops.

## Scene 3: Passenger Confessions

INT. VAN - AFTERNOON

The group is delayed beside a meadow. Uncle Vos confesses that he remembers a route from a childhood trip, but the bridge he recalls was replaced decades ago.

VOS: I wanted to take the scenic road. I thought it would still be there.

TIL: The ridge road is open if we go west, not north.

NARA: Why didn't you say that earlier?

TIL: You were following the arrow.

PEL: The arrow was on the sign we tied down.

NARA: It said north.

TIL: It said north on both sides.

The group laughs, but Bo worries the bell's padding is loosening. Pel checks the straps and finds a split buckle.

BO: We need the bell to arrive in one piece.

PEL: It will. We will replace the buckle at the next workshop.

## Scene 4: The Workshop

EXT. ROADSIDE REPAIR SHED - AFTERNOON

A mechanic offers a leather strap, but it is too short. Pel uses two straps and a knot. Bo keeps the bell from swinging by sitting beside it, legs braced.

NARA: Is the knot safe?

PEL: It is safe if no one rings the bell in the van.

BO: I can wait.

The repair complete, Nara sets out west. A road sign says HILLTOP FESTIVAL, 18 MILES. Uncle Vos spots a side road.

VOS: Shortcut.

TIL: The sign says dead end.

VOS: It may be a short dead end.

Nara follows the clear sign instead. Vos starts to object, then notices the view: the road climbs through a grove, revealing the valley and the festival flags in the distance.

VOS: I was wrong. That is the right road.

## Scene 5: The Final Hill

EXT. RIDGE ROAD - SUNSET

The van climbs slowly. A fallen branch blocks one lane. Pel and Nara move it while TIL directs the vehicle past a narrow bend. Bo keeps the bell steady. The bell remains silent.

BO: We are almost there. I wanted to play it on the way.

PEL: Its first note belongs at the festival.

They arrive as the crowd gathers. The organizers are waiting at the hilltop platform. Nara unloads the bell. Vos holds the last strap, then releases it.

ORGANIZER: You made it before sunset.

TIL: We made it by following the road that was open.

VOS: And not taking the shortcut.

## Scene 6: Arrival

The festival keeper hangs the restored bell in the tower. Bo pulls the rope. One clear note sounds over the valley. The crowd cheers. The van is parked safely, its route map still tied open across the dashboard.

NARA: We should have taken the west road from the start.

TIL: We needed the detours to learn which road was west.

PEL: That is a very generous interpretation.

## Final Image

The sun slips behind the ridge. The bell sways once in the tower. In the van below, the map stays open and Uncle Vos folds his old shortcut directions into a square.
