# The Last Train to the Lighthouse

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: isolated-station thrillers that treat a routine timetable mix-up as a night-long mystery with ominous clues*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A lighthouse keeper waits for the last train to bring a replacement lens, but the station's dramatic clues point to a parcel already waiting in the baggage room.

Cast: keeper Runa, a macaque; station clerk Delo, a lemur; porter Mikka, a gibbon; inspector Varo, an orangutan; conductor Suri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. CLIFFSIDE STATION - DUSK

Rain blows across a tiny platform. Runa waits with a lantern. Delo studies the timetable under the station awning. A sign reads LAST TRAIN: 8:10. The clock reads 7:50.

RUNA: The new lens is on that train.

DELO: The parcel arrived at four.

RUNA: Then why is the lighthouse still dark?

Delo points to the baggage room. Its door is locked. A key hangs behind the clerk's counter, on a hook labeled BAGGAGE ROOM.

VARO: Someone has hidden the key in plain sight.

DELO: It is where we keep it.

SCENE 2 - INT. STATION BAGGAGE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Runa unlocks the room. A long wooden crate sits on the floor, marked LENS - KEEP UPRIGHT, SEALED UNTIL INSTALLATION CARD. Mikka wheels in a cart. Varo circles the crate, looking for boot prints. The room is dusty except for a clean rectangle where the crate was moved.

VARO: The package has been shifted.

MIKKA: I brought it inside when the delivery van arrived.

RUNA: The train has not come yet.

DELO: The parcel came by road. The replacement timetable is for the next train.

Varo lifts the crate's manifest and finds a smear of lamp oil. Runa recognizes it from her coat pocket. She had carried the lantern through the rain and brushed the crate while entering.

Before leaving the baggage room, Varo dusts the crate for fingerprints with flour from Mikka's lunch bag. The flour covers the lens label and makes the words unreadable. Mikka wipes the label clean with a dry cloth. Varo examines the flour on his fingers and declares that someone has removed the clue. Runa shows him the open lunch bag.

RUNA: The clue was printed on the crate.

VARO: And now it is clean.

Delo checks the station register. It records a road delivery at four and a scheduled train at eight-forty. Varo circles both entries, drawing a line between them as though charting a plot. The line ends at the baggage room.

SCENE 3 - EXT. STATION PLATFORM - NIGHT

The clock reaches 8:10. The train does not arrive. Suri's voice comes over the station speaker with a schedule reminder: the winter timetable is active and the last train will arrive at 8:40. Varo calls the announcement suspicious. Delo points to the red stripe on the track map; it marks a closed siding, not the main line.

VARO: A red mark, a missing train, and a new timetable.

DELO: The main line is open. The train is not due yet.

Mikka opens an umbrella over the timetable. Rain has blurred the ink, and 8:10 looks like 8:40. Runa spots the printed note: "Winter schedule begins tonight. Last train 8:40." The station clock stopped five minutes ago.

RUNA: We have been waiting for a train that is not due yet.

While they wait on the platform, Varo examines the clock. He presses the glass and the minute hand jumps forward, revealing that the clock was sticking rather than running slow. Delo winds it once; it resumes ticking normally. Varo writes down the change as a possible signal. The winter timetable remains in effect: the train will arrive at 8:40, not 8:10. The inspector asks whether the clock stopped to prevent the lens from arriving. Runa points out that the lens is already inside the station.

VARO: The branch has missed its opportunity.

SCENE 4 - INT. LIGHTHOUSE LANTERN ROOM - LATER

The train arrives at 8:40 and Suri brings a small envelope, not a crate. Runa opens it: an instruction card says the lens delivery came earlier by road and should be fitted before the train's scheduled arrival. They carry the crate up the lighthouse stairs. Varo insists on inspecting every landing for clues; each turn of the stairs brings him back to the same window.

MIKKA: That is the spiral stair.

VARO: It has repeated the evidence.

Runa opens the crate. The lens is wrapped securely, with a paper label showing the correct orientation. Mikka lifts it into the housing while Runa holds the lantern steady. The light rotates across the sea.

SCENE 5 - EXT. LIGHTHOUSE PLATFORM - DAWN

The keeper, clerk, porter and inspector look toward the station. The last train departs in daylight. Suri waves from the caboose. Varo writes "No conspiracy found" in his notebook, then adds "possibly the train." Delo gives him the updated timetable.

RUNA: The mystery was a delivery note and a slow clock.

VARO: Then we have uncovered two suspects.

The new lens sweeps light across the platform sign, making the word LAST shine brightest.

FINAL IMAGE: Varo studies the glowing word LAST while the lighthouse beam circles behind him and the train disappears on schedule.
