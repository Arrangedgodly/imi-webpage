# The Professor's Unsolved Sandwich

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: academic mystery films where a trivial lunch becomes a grand intellectual conspiracy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
PROFESSOR VEX, an orangutan
ARCHIVIST MARA, a macaque
GRADUATE PIP, a capuchin
CATERER NARI, a lemur
CUSTODIAN ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. UNIVERSITY LECTURE ROOM - MORNING

A sandwich sits alone on the lectern. Vex circles it, wearing a scarf and carrying a magnifying glass.

VEX: At precisely nine, the sandwich vanished.

MARA: It is on the lectern.

VEX: Then why is its wrapper open?

PIP: I asked Nari to open it. You said the seminar would run through lunch.

NARI: I unwrapped it and left it there when the lecture began.

VEX: The mystery deepens. Someone has altered the evidence.

ODO: The evidence is lunch. I need the lectern cleared before the next class.

Mara asks everyone to stand back. Vex places numbered markers around the sandwich, blocking the projector beam.

MARA: Your markers cover the screen.

VEX: A true scholar does not fear darkness.

PIP: The slides are about sandwich history. You wrote them.

VEX: I wrote a theory of bread. The sandwich remains unknown.

SCENE 2. INT. UNIVERSITY ARCHIVE - LATER

Mara takes Vex to the archive room. Pip carries the seminar attendance sheet; Nari brings a napkin and plate. The sandwich remains on the lectern, untouched since the lecture stopped.

MARA: We will reconstruct the handoffs, not invent a secret society.

VEX: The napkin bears a crease. That could be a code.

NARI: I folded it under the plate to catch crumbs.

PIP: The sign-in sheet shows I entered the room at eight-fifty. Vex was already lecturing.

VEX: A suspiciously precise alibi.

PIP: It's a clock-in sheet.

Odo enters with a cart. He needs the lectern for a projector test, but Vex blocks the route with a board reading EVIDENCE ZONE.

ODO: My cart cannot pass.

VEX: The suspect must not leave.

MARA: The sandwich is not moving. Let Odo do his work.

Vex shifts the board; its string catches on his scarf. Pip frees the scarf without touching the plate.

PIP: The only thing trapped here is your costume.

SCENE 3. INT. LECTURE ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mara studies the wrapper. A torn corner sits beside the sandwich. Nari recognizes the tear as the notch she made when opening it. Pip notices a faint smear on the attendance sheet.

PIP: That's mustard. I set the sheet down beside the plate when I signed in.

VEX: A mustard trace linking the student to the meal.

NARI: It came from the spoon I used to serve condiments. No one took a bite.

Mara lifts the attendance sheet by its dry edge. Beneath it is a second napkin, folded into a small wedge.

MARA: The sandwich slid against this when I moved the lecture notes. It was never missing; the paper covered it from Vex's angle.

VEX: A false bottom beneath the archive.

ODO: It is one napkin on a lectern.

Vex looks through his magnifying glass. His scarf falls over the sandwich; he recoils as if the meal has disappeared again.

VEX: The culprit has returned.

PIP: That's your scarf. Lift it.

Vex lifts the scarf. The sandwich is still there. The whole group sighs.

SCENE 3A. INT. LECTURE ROOM - AFTERNOON

Vex has returned to the lectern with a fresh evidence board. Mara removes it before it blocks the doorway. The sandwich stays on the side table, on its plate.

VEX: A second examination may reveal what the first concealed.

MARA: The first examination established that the napkin covered it. We are not moving the lunch again.

PIP: The class is waiting for the projector. They do not need to hear a theory of lunch.

NARI: I will wrap the sandwich now, if Vex has finished studying it.

Vex measures the distance from lectern to side table with his magnifying glass. Odo walks straight through the open aisle carrying the projector.

ODO: There is your missing route. I needed the clear path.

VEX: The evidence points toward a practical obstruction.

MARA: The board was the obstruction. Move it to the wall.

Vex lifts the board and reads its heading: THE CASE OF THE OPEN WRAPPER. He erases the heading and writes LUNCH.

SCENE 4. INT. LECTURE ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Nari puts the sandwich on a plate and slides it to the side table. Odo clears the lectern for his projector test. Mara removes the numbered markers. Pip wipes the mustard from the attendance sheet.

VEX: We have solved the case.

MARA: It was a napkin and a poor viewing angle.

VEX: The solution is almost offensively ordinary.

NARI: It is still your lunch. Do you want it?

Vex considers the sandwich as if it might contain a second clue. He takes one careful bite.

VEX: The evidence is delicious.

ODO: Please finish it away from the projector.

TAG: Vex places the wrapper in the recycling bin and labels the bin "archive, open to all." Mara removes the label before the next seminar.
