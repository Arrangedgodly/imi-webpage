# The Seating Chart Is Alive

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: wedding chaos comedies, seating-chart politics, and escalating reception mishaps*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A pair of monkey cousins tries to keep their aunt's wedding reception peaceful, but a seating chart, one runaway cake cart, and an overenthusiastic toast turn family diplomacy into a full-room shuffle.

Cast: Imani, a macaque wedding planner; Puck, a capuchin cousin; Aunt Lela, a mandrill bride; Otis, a gibbon groom; Cousin Fern, a peacock who insists on the head table; and a ring-bearing turtle named Dot.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, MORNING

Imani pins the seating chart to the wall. Fern moves her name to the head table. Puck moves it back. Fern moves Puck's name beside the speakers.

IMANI: The chart is final.

FERN: I have a title.

PUCK: It's "cousin."

FERN: A senior cousin.

Aunt Lela arrives and says everyone should sit where they like. Imani asks if that includes the cake cart. Lela says the cake cart has a reservation near the kitchen.

SCENE 2. INT. HALLWAY, AFTERNOON

Dot the turtle carries the rings in a small basket. Puck walks too quickly and nearly collides with her. He slows down. Dot reaches the altar after the rehearsal is over.

The musicians start the processional. The cake cart begins rolling downhill. Imani chases it with a tablecloth. Fern tries to block it with a chair. The cart turns, misses the cake table, and rolls into the coat rack. The coats fall in a neat pile.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, EVENING

Guests arrive. Fern switches places with the groom's aunt. The aunt switches with a neighbor. Soon the seating chart no longer matches anyone. Imani asks guests to introduce themselves to the people beside them. They do. Fern ends up seated with the band and learns the drummer is her second cousin.

Puck announces the toast. His microphone is connected to the kitchen speaker. His first words come out beside the dish sink. He walks around the room, and the microphone cable pulls a string of paper lanterns down.

SCENE 3A. INT. HALLWAY, CONTINUOUS

Imani tries to stabilize the cake cart. The wheels point in different directions. Puck uses a chair as a brake. Dot crawls underneath to inspect the axle. Fern arrives with a clipboard listing seating corrections.

IMANI: The cake is leaning.

FERN: The chart is wrong.

PUCK: The cart is rolling.

DOT: The wheel is loose.

FERN: My name is missing.

IMANI: It is on the chart.

FERN: Under the band.

PUCK: You moved yourself.

FERN: I was promoted.

IMANI: To the drummer's table?

FERN: He has a view.

Dot tightens the wheel with a small wrench. The cart stops. Puck lets go of the chair. It slides away and bumps the seating chart, which falls face-down. Fern picks it up and discovers her name is printed twice.

SCENE 4. INT. HALL, RECEPTION

Aunt Lela gives a short toast. Otis replies with a longer one. Puck whispers that the cake is leaning. Imani and Dot prop it with a stack of napkins. Fern attempts to start a dance contest. The bride asks her to wait until after the first dance.

The band plays. Guests dance with whoever is nearby. The seating chart falls from the wall and lands face-down. Nobody notices. Fern dances with the groom's aunt, then trades partners with the drummer.

SCENE 4A. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, LATER

The toast begins. Otis thanks the guests, the musicians, and the volunteers who fixed the cake cart. Fern raises a glass to the "senior cousins." The drummer asks whether that means she is family. Fern says it means she can request songs.

The band starts a slow dance. Puck tries to lead Imani, but they are both holding plates. They place the plates on the gift table and dance between the chairs. Guests move the chairs to make space. The whole seating plan changes again, this time because people want to dance together.

Imani sees the cake leaning once more. Dot props it with the seating chart. The paper bends but holds. Fern asks if the chart can be saved. Imani says it has finally become useful.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, LATE NIGHT

The cake is served. Dot carries the rings back to the gift table, still in the basket. Imani finds the seating chart under the table. Fern asks whether her name is still at the head table. Imani says there is no head table anymore.

FINAL IMAGE: Aunt Lela and Otis leave beneath the paper lanterns, now rehung at different heights. The seating chart is reused as a tray liner. Fern sits beside the band, conducting the drummer with her fork.
