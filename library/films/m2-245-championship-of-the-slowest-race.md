# Championship of the Slowest Race

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports triumph films, where a team learns that a deliberately slow race is still decided by the rules*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A ragtag team enters a slow-carry relay and discovers that their coach's inspirational speeches keep making them miss the finish line.

Cast: Coach Boro, baboon; sprinter Fenn, macaque; strategist Lira, lemur; judge Tavi, gibbon; announcer Seli, capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. FIELD, MORNING

A painted line marks the start. Another line, ten paces away, marks the finish. Each runner must carry a ripe fruit without dropping it. Boro gathers his team.

BORO: The slowest race is not about speed.

FENN: It is about being slow?

LIRA: It is about finishing last, but staying upright.

BORO: It is about discipline.

SELI: The competitors are ready. The crowd is quiet.

The crowd is one goat and two picnic blankets. Seli narrates as if addressing a stadium.

SELI: Every step could change the standings.

TAVI: There are no standings yet.

BORO: They will come.

Boro hands Fenn a fruit. It is a little too ripe and leaves juice on his palm.

FENN: This is a slippery contestant.

LIRA: The fruit is the baton.

BORO: Respect the baton.

SCENE 2. EXT. START LINE, CONTINUOUS

The race begins. Fenn takes one careful step. The other team moves faster. Boro shouts encouragement.

BORO: Find your inner champion!

Fenn freezes to look inward.

LIRA: Keep moving.

Fenn inches forward. Seli announces that he has found a rhythm. Tavi says he is still at the start line.

Seli's voice echoes from the hill. The goat bleats, and Fenn mistakes it for the starting signal. He takes another step, then stops when Tavi raises a flag.

TAVI: False start.

FENN: The goat started me.

TAVI: The goat is not an official.

BORO: That's how they want us to think.

Lira tells Fenn to watch the judge, not the crowd. He looks at Tavi's flag. Tavi lowers it.

FENN: Now?

TAVI: The race is already underway.

FENN: I am still holding the fruit.

SCENE 3. EXT. TURNING MARK, MOMENTS LATER

The judge rings a small bell. Fenn and Lira stop.

TAVI: Mid-race check. The fruit must stay above the waist.

FENN: We have not been told that.

TAVI: The rule was on the entry card.

LIRA: The entry card said "carry fruit."

BORO: We were given a rule with no coaching session.

TAVI: The rule is for safety. No one should carry fruit at ankle height on the course.

Lira adjusts her grip. Fenn carefully raises his fruit, which is already above his waist.

SELI: The competition has introduced a new elevation.

BORO: The team has adapted.

FENN: The fruit has not moved.

LIRA: It did. I lifted it.

Boro starts to call another huddle. Tavi points to the finish line. The team keeps walking.

Fenn reaches the turning mark. He must pass the fruit to Lira without dropping it. Boro asks for a team huddle. Lira refuses to stop while holding the fruit.

LIRA: I cannot huddle and carry this.

BORO: The huddle is where champions are made.

LIRA: The finish line is over there.

Boro whispers the play: pass the fruit gently, turn in a wide circle, and keep moving. Lira follows it. The fruit slips, but she catches it against her chest.

[SFX: SOFT THUMP.]

SELI: A miraculous save.

TAVI: A legal catch.

Lira turns too wide and almost walks into the picnic blankets. The goat steps onto the path. Tavi asks the goat's owner to move it. Boro turns this into a tactical pause.

BORO: The course has introduced a challenge.

LIRA: It has introduced a goat.

The goat wanders to the side. Lira moves again. Fenn calls out that he has found his rhythm, but he is still several paces behind.

SCENE 4. EXT. FINISH LINE, LATER

Lira reaches the final stretch carrying the fruit. Boro waves her to stop for a team huddle. Tavi points to the finish and reminds them that the relay ends when the final runner crosses with the fruit.

LIRA: Fenn is the final runner.

BORO: Then we need a ceremonial handoff.

TAVI: You need a legal handoff before the line.

Lira turns back one pace. Fenn reaches her, and she passes him the fruit without either runner stopping. The crowd watches the careful transfer as if it were a championship sprint.

SELI: The baton has changed hands.

TAVI: The fruit has remained above the waist.

Fenn takes the last ten paces at the team's deliberately slow speed. Boro begins a speech about courage, then notices the finish ribbon is drooping. He holds it up just in time. Fenn crosses with the fruit in both hands.

TAVI: Completed relay. Longest time on record.

BORO: We have achieved greatness.

FENN: Can I have another fruit?

LIRA: Not until the next race.

The goat wanders toward the finish and eats the carried fruit after the result is recorded.

BORO: The goat has taken the trophy.

FENN: The trophy was fruit.

[SFX: GOAT CHEWS, CROWD APPLAUDS.]
