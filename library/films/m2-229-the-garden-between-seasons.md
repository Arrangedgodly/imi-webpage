# The Garden Between Seasons

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests, prophecy riddles, enchanted artifacts, and a final battle won by learning when not to pull*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
YARA, a macaque gardener
FEN, a capuchin mapmaker
OLD ROOT, a gibbon keeper
THE BRASS BEE, a small mechanical insect

SCENE 1. EXT. KING'S GARDEN - DAWN

The kingdom's seasons have become stuck at spring. Blossoms open and close on the same branch, and a basket of apples remains green. Yara, a macaque gardener, receives a brass bee from Old Root.

OLD ROOT: Take it to the garden between seasons.

FEN: Where is that?

OLD ROOT: Between seasons.

A prophecy is carved on the bee's wings: "Where two winds meet, do not pull the golden root." Yara and Fen set out with a map showing a path through the hills. The map is printed upside down.

SCENE 2. EXT. BRIAR PATH - DAY

The brass bee buzzes toward a hedge. Fen follows the map and walks into the hedge. Yara turns the map around. A hidden gate opens when the bee lands on a flower. Beyond it, a stone path splits in two.

FEN: The map says take the left path.

YARA: It says "leave path."

Fen squints at the map. The word "left" was smudged by a wet thumb. They choose the path with living flowers instead of the stone one. The brass bee flies ahead, then returns to collect Fen's hat, which is snagged on a thorn.

SCENE 3. EXT. TWO-WIND BRIDGE - AFTERNOON

The travelers reach a bridge where wind blows from both ends. A golden root grows through a crack in the middle. Fen tries to pull it free. The bridge trembles.

YARA: The prophecy says not to pull.

FEN: It also says golden. This root is very golden.

Yara watches the wind. One gust carries pollen from the left, the other carries dry leaves from the right. The root bends but does not break. She places a small pot beneath it to catch the rainwater draining from the bridge.

SCENE 4. EXT. GARDEN BETWEEN SEASONS - EVENING

The bridge leads to a hidden garden where half the trees are flowering and half are bare. Old Root appears, carrying a wooden rake. He explains that the garden's clockwork weather vane is stuck because the golden root has grown around its gears.

OLD ROOT: The root holds the mechanism together.

FEN: Then we should pull it out.

YARA: We should loosen the gears around it.

The brass bee slips through a small opening and buzzes in the mechanism. The vane rotates, but the two winds push it in opposite directions. Yara asks Fen to hold one side and Old Root to hold the other. They move the vane one careful notch at a time.

SCENE 5. INT. WEATHER VANE TOWER - NIGHT

The vane jams again. Fen reaches for the golden root, then stops. He looks at the two winds and notices they arrive in alternating bursts. The group waits for a pause. When both winds quiet, Yara lifts a leaf from the gear, and Fen turns the vane by one notch.

The mechanism clicks. The garden's branches begin to change: blossoms turn to small fruit, bare trees grow buds, and the apples finally redden.

FEN: The riddle was about timing.

YARA: And not pulling.

SCENE 6. EXT. KING'S GARDEN - MORNING

Yara and Fen return with a basket of ripe apples. The kingdom celebrates, but Old Root insists the garden must be maintained. Fen corrects the map. The brass bee lands on the map's corner and pins it down.

SCENE 7. INT. KING'S GARDEN - AFTERNOON

The court gardeners gather around the old weather vane. They have brought saws to remove the golden root. Yara places a pot beneath the gear and explains how to wait for the two winds to settle. The gardeners watch the leaf turn.

GARDENER: We have been pushing the vane.

FEN: It needs to turn.

GARDENER: We thought the prophecy meant pull the root.

YARA: It said not to pull it.

The brass bee buzzes through the mechanism and returns carrying a tiny leaf. Old Root ties a colored ribbon to the crank, showing which way to move it. The gardeners take turns adjusting the vane. The apples ripen in rows, and the court arranges a harvest rather than a victory parade.

KING: Should we build a statue?

OLD ROOT: Build a shed for the rake. Fen pins the corrected map beside the shed door, where a gardener can reach it without entering the thorn hedge. Yara checks it twice.

FINAL IMAGE: The map points the right way; the mechanical bee rests on the word "wait."
