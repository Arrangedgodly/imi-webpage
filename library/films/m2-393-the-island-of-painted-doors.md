# The Island of Painted Doors

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: adventure films built around mysterious islands, hidden entrances, and clues that must be interpreted by the arriving expedition*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MIRA, a macaque expedition leader
TAVI, a lemur botanist
PIP, a capuchin boat pilot
VEX, a gibbon cartographer
THE CARETAKER, an orangutan

SCENE 1. EXT. ISLAND COVE - MORNING

Mira's expedition lands on a rocky shore. Above the beach stands a row of painted doors, each mounted on a separate frame. Vex unfolds a map. It marks one door with a red X.

VEX: The hidden entrance is behind the red door.

TAVI: The red door is in front of us.

PIP: There are seven red doors.

The caretaker approaches carrying a bucket of paint. He says the doors are scenery for a festival that ended yesterday. Mira shows him the map. He turns it upside down.

CARETAKER: This is last year's map. The doors move.

Mira asks which one leads to the treasure. The caretaker says none. The doors were painted to teach visitors that every entrance needs a clear path.

VEX: That sounds like a clue.

CARETAKER: It's a sign.

Tavi brushes sand from the nearest door. It opens inward and hits a rock. The door cannot open fully. Pip tries the next door. It opens outward into the surf.

MIRA: The island has bad entrances.

SCENE 2. EXT. DOOR FIELD - LATER

The expedition walks between the painted frames. Each door has a number, but the numbers are out of order. Vex follows the map and leads them in circles. Tavi points out a trail of blue paint on the ground. It connects the doors.

TAVI: Someone dragged them here.

PIP: Or something did.

They follow the paint to a hill. A door stands at the top, propped open with a wedge. Beyond it is an ordinary path through the brush.

Mira steps through. The wedge slips, and the door closes behind her. She is not trapped, but the others cannot see her.

MIRA: I am on the other side.

VEX: Which side?

MIRA: The side with the path.

Pip tries the handle. It is a stage flat with no latch. He pushes it and the whole frame tips. The caretaker catches the frame before it falls.

CARETAKER: Please don't break the festival.

VEX: We are finding the entrance.

CARETAKER: The entrance is the path.

Tavi notices the wedge is a painted block labeled "DOORSTOP." It is attached to a string that runs down the hill. She pulls the string. Several doors swing open in sequence, revealing a path.

SCENE 3. EXT. HILL PATH - AFTERNOON

The group follows the open doors. The path ends at an old stone platform. Vex finds a carved symbol matching the red X on his map. He kneels to study it.

VEX: A hidden chamber.

Pip brushes away the dirt. The symbol is a map legend: red X means "festival storage."

The caretaker unlocks a small shed. Inside are more doors, paint, and a box of ribbons. There is no treasure.

MIRA: Why mark this on the map?

VEX: The mapmaker wanted the expedition to come here.

CARETAKER: The festival needs visitors.

The group looks toward the beach. Their boat is tied beside the wrong door frame. The tide is rising. Mira asks Pip to move it. The path is now blocked by the painted doors, which have all swung shut.

SCENE 4. EXT. DOOR FIELD - SUNSET

Tavi pulls the string again. Only the first door opens. The next one sticks. Vex checks the hinges. They are painted on, not real. Pip turns the frames sideways to clear the way. Each frame catches on a branch and pivots.

MIRA: The doors are moving again.

CARETAKER: They have to be stored before the tide reaches the lower path.

Mira and Pip carry the first frame. Vex reads the map to guide them. The red X is now upside down. Tavi wipes the marker off and draws a simple arrow toward the boat.

The caretaker asks if the expedition found what it came for.

VEX: We found a path.

PIP: And seven doors that don't open.

TAVI: Six. One is the shed.

SCENE 5. EXT. ISLAND COVE - DUSK

The doors are stacked beside the shed. The boat floats freely in the cove. Mira hands the caretaker a clean map and asks him to add the real path. He draws a line that follows the shoreline and labels it "WALK HERE."

VEX: It's less mysterious.

CARETAKER: It gets people to the beach.

Pip unties the boat. A gust swings the shed door open. It reveals the storage shelves and a jar of festival tickets.

MIRA: A secret room.

CARETAKER: A supply closet.

The expedition boards. Vex folds his map along the shoreline. Tavi takes a ticket as a souvenir and the caretaker waves them off.

FINAL IMAGE: The caretaker props the shed door open with the red X from last year's map.
