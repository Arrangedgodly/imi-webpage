# The Grand Prix of the Garden Path

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: racing films that equate speed with skill and rely on a last-lap miracle from an underdog vehicle*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A team enters a garden-cart race in a borrowed wheelbarrow, then learns that the tight course rewards steady steering over roaring engines.

Cast: Driver Piko, a squirrel monkey; mechanic Tamsin, a macaque; rival driver Odo, a gibbon; announcer Vela, a lemur; marshal Hesh, a baboon.

SCENE 1 - EXT. BOTANICAL GARDEN - MORNING

A course winds between flower beds, beneath an arch and around a compost heap. Piko stands beside a red wheelbarrow with one squeaky wheel. Tamsin checks the handle bolts.

PIKO: This is our racer?

TAMSIN: It is a garden cart. We borrowed it from the greenhouse.

PIKO: The other teams have polished scooters.

TAMSIN: Their scooters cannot fit through the herb gate.

Odo rolls up in a shiny cart with a small electric motor. Vela speaks into a microphone.

VELA: The favorite, Odo, has the fastest motor. Piko's team has the oldest wheelbarrow.

HESH: The event is a slow-course challenge. No powered vehicles.

Odo disconnects the motor but keeps the polished panels.

SCENE 2 - EXT. PRACTICE COURSE - MOMENTS LATER

Piko climbs into the wheelbarrow. Tamsin pushes. At the first turn, the squeaky wheel pulls left and the barrow clips a bale of hay.

PIKO: We need more speed.

TAMSIN: We need the wheel aligned.

Piko asks for a dramatic engine sound. Tamsin taps a watering can with a wrench. The clanging startles a robin, which flutters off the post.

TAMSIN: No more engine sounds near the birds.

Piko studies the course map. The route has arrows and a narrow chicane between flower beds.

PIKO: The straight section is short. We can brake before the gate and roll through the chicane.

TAMSIN: That is slower.

PIKO: It is the only way through.

SCENE 3 - EXT. START LINE - LATER

The teams line up. Odo revs an unplugged motor sound from his phone. The noise fades into a tinny battery warning.

HESH: Phones stay off during the race.

Vela raises a flag. Piko pushes off. Odo's polished cart surges ahead for three meters, then meets the narrow herb gate. The cart cannot turn through it. Odo backs up while the wheelbarrow rolls steadily past.

VELA: The underdog has taken the lead.

PIKO: We have reached the first turn.

TAMSIN: Eyes on the path.

The wheel squeaks around the compost heap. Piko leans left and the barrow tracks straight. A watering can sits at the next corner. Tamsin flags it so the marshal can move it.

SCENE 4 - EXT. FLOWER-BED CHICANE - CONTINUOUS

Piko slows before the chicane. Odo passes on the outside, then clips a low rope and stops. The rope is a temporary boundary for a bed of seedlings. Hesh raises the yellow flag.

HESH: Hold. Rope needs resetting.

Odo climbs out to help. Piko stops too, waiting behind the flag.

VELA: The challenger has stopped with the finish line in sight.

PIKO: The course is paused. We wait.

The announcer lowers the microphone. Vela checks the standings board, where someone has already written Piko as the winner.

VELA: We announced the finish too early.

HESH: The race is not over. The flag was yellow, not checkered.

Piko climbs out to hold the rope while Hesh ties it back to the post. Odo steadies the small boundary stake with his shoe. The rope is now clear of both wheels.

TAMSIN: We have time to align the barrow again.

PIKO: The wheel is fine. We just have to keep it slow through the last bend.

Odo removes the loose panel and tucks it under his arm.

ODO: I can carry this to the finish instead of making the cart carry it.

HESH: Everyone back behind the line. The race resumes when the marshal drops the flag.

HESH: Good call. Both carts may continue.

Odo takes his place again, but his polished panel has come loose and rattles against the wheel. He removes it and hands it to a volunteer.

ODO: The cart is lighter without the decoration.

SCENE 5 - EXT. FINISH ARCH - MINUTES LATER

The teams pass through the chicane. Piko steers between the pots without touching them. Odo follows, slower but clean. Piko reaches the finish first by half a wheelbarrow length.

VELA: Piko wins the Garden Path Grand Prix!

HESH: And both teams leave the plants intact.

TAMSIN tightens the squeaky wheel bolt. Odo examines the barrow and nods.

ODO: I brought the fastest cart. You brought the one that fit.

PIKO: We practiced the turns.

The wheelbarrow is returned to the greenhouse. A gardener places a seed tray inside it. Piko tries to sit in the tray, but Tamsin points to the sign marked EQUIPMENT RETURN.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty wheelbarrow rolls neatly back into its greenhouse bay, its front wheel finally straight and quiet.
