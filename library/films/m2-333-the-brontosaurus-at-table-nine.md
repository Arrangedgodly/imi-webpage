# The Brontosaurus at Table Nine

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: creature-feature blockbusters, military briefings, giant-monster scale shots, and heroes who keep ordering lunch during the emergency*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
DR. LUMA, a lemur paleontologist
MAYOR ODO, a capuchin
CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque rescue pilot
BIX, an orangutan diner owner
THE BRONTOSAUR, a gentle giant

SCENE 1. EXT. RIVERFRONT DINER - DAY

A giant brontosaur rises from the river behind a roadside diner. Its head towers above the sign. A spoon slips from Bix's hand and rings against the counter. Luma studies the animal through binoculars.

LUMA: It is looking for food.

MAYOR ODO: We have a lunch menu.

Vex arrives in a helicopter and hovers over the parking lot. The animal's breath blows a stack of paper placemats across the road. Drivers stop to collect them, blocking the helicopter's landing zone.

SCENE 2. INT. DINER - MOMENTS LATER

The town council holds an emergency briefing beside the pie case. Vex unfolds a map with red arrows showing evacuation routes. The map is upside down. Luma turns it around and explains that the brontosaur is following the smell of the riverbank ferns. It is not attacking the diner.

VEX: We need a perimeter.

BIX: The pie case is already a perimeter.

Mayor Odo orders everyone to clear the parking lot. The diner patrons file toward the old train platform, carrying their plates. Bix keeps the pie case on a handcart and refuses to leave the pies unattended. Luma radios the rescue crew to keep the river road open for the animal, not to surround it.

LUMA: We need room.

Vex redraws the map with a wide loop around the meadow. Mayor Odo marks the diner as a safe rest point and adds an arrow for the footpath. A child points out that the arrow points to the river. Vex rotates the map and finally aligns north with the compass. Bix asks whether the giant will step on the outdoor tables. Luma says it may not notice them. The council moves each table one by one, while the brontosaur carefully lifts its foot over a child's dropped toy truck.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVER ROAD - AFTERNOON

Vex tries to lead the animal toward an open meadow. He plays a loud warning tone through a megaphone. The brontosaur turns toward him, and the gust from its breath knocks the megaphone from his hands. Luma advises using a softer sound. Bix opens a crate of fern fronds from the diner kitchen. The animal follows the leaves away from the road.

The crew marks a broad path with traffic cones. The brontosaur picks up one cone with its lips and sets it neatly on top of another. It walks down the road, stepping between the painted lines. The town residents follow at a safe distance, filming on their phones.

SCENE 4. EXT. MEADOW - SUNSET

The brontosaur reaches the meadow and begins eating ferns. The town celebrates. Vex says the crisis is over, but a giant shadow falls across the group. The animal raises its head, towering above them, then sneezes. A rain of fern bits covers the rescue team and the town banner.

LUMA: It is clearing its nose.

MAYOR ODO: The banner was already wrinkled.

The animal's tail sweeps a picnic table across the grass. Bix catches the table before the pies fall. Vex calls for a second evacuation. Luma points out that the animal is turning away to sleep. The giant settles in a clearing, carefully curling its tail around the table as if guarding it.

SCENE 5. INT. DINER - NIGHT

The residents gather for a late meal. Bix serves pies outside the diner, far from the animal. Mayor Odo announces a new sign for the meadow: PLEASE GIVE THE GUEST ROOM. Vex asks if they can include a military warning symbol. Luma suggests an illustration showing a brontosaur and a picnic table at a safe distance.

The rescue crew returns the traffic cones to the road. One cone remains on the brontosaur's head. Vex approaches with a long pole to retrieve it. Luma stops him and offers a fern frond instead. The giant wakes, lifts its head, and the cone slides off into the grass. It sniffs the frond, eats it, and goes back to sleep.

Mayor Odo asks Bix for the bill. Bix sets the check on the outdoor table. A breeze from the sleeping dinosaur carries it across the meadow and lands it at Vex's feet. Vex pins it under a traffic cone, then realizes the bill is only for the pies. He pays Bix and returns the cone to the road. The town council takes the menu inside so it cannot become another emergency document.

FINAL IMAGE: The brontosaur snores; its breath gently spins a paper placemat like a pinwheel beside the untouched pie.
