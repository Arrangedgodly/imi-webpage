# The Reboot That Kept Its Old Bolts

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: franchise reboots that promise a clean beginning while smuggling every old prop, catchphrase, and obligation into the new story*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR ODO, a baboon
WRITER LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
PRODUCER VEX, a macaque
PROP KEEPER TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. STUDIO STAGE - MORNING

A new adventure set stands bare. Director Odo places a fresh wooden sign reading THE FIRST DAY. Producer Vex rolls in a crate of props from the old franchise: a cracked compass, a brass whistle, a scarf and a bent spoon.

ODO: The new film begins from zero.

VEX: Excellent. These are the zero props.

Luma opens the crate. The compass is engraved with the former hero's initials. The whistle has a label that says RETURN AFTER FILM FIVE. Tavi says the scarf belongs to the wardrobe archive. Vex calls it legacy texture.

PIP: What is the bent spoon for?

VEX: It appears in every installment.

LUMA: No one remembers why.

VEX: That is what makes it a tradition.

Odo asks Tavi to clear the stage. Tavi moves the crate offscreen, where it remains visible in the mirror above the set.

SCENE 2. INT. STUDIO STAGE - LATER

Pip enters as a new explorer. He finds the compass under a rock. Luma asks what it points to. The needle spins and stops at the prop crate. Odo calls cut. Vex says the compass has a mysterious pull.

LUMA: It is magnetic.

TAVI: The crate has a steel latch.

Vex asks for a new compass, but Tavi brings the old one because the new one is still in a shipping box marked FRANCHISE ARCHIVE. Pip tries the brass whistle. It produces the exact signal used by the old hero. A stagehand answers from the wing, carrying a second crate.

ODO: Why is there another crate?

VEX: A surprise callback.

The stagehand says he thought the whistle meant lunch delivery. Vex asks him to repeat the entrance with more awe. He repeats it carrying a sandwich instead of a crate.

SCENE 3. INT. PROP STORAGE - AFTERNOON

Luma interviews Tavi about every object. He pulls out the scarf, the spoon and three identical keys. Each prop has a number from a different sequel. Vex insists the new film needs a clean visual language. He asks for the old objects to be hidden in the background instead of used.

TAVI: If they are hidden, we need to track them.

VEX: Track them invisibly.

Pip tries to leave the props behind. The scarf catches on his pack. He walks across the stage, dragging the entire crate by its lid. The compass points to him. The whistle jingles. The bent spoon taps the floor like a tiny bell.

PIP: The past is following me.

LUMA: The crate is tied to your backpack.

VEX: Keep that line. It is the theme.

Odo cuts the rope. The crate lands open; all three keys slide under the new sign. Tavi retrieves them one by one. Vex asks if the keys can be a post-credit mystery. Odo says the set has no locked door.

SCENE 4. INT. STUDIO STAGE - EVENING

Luma proposes that the new characters give the old props to the archive. Vex worries that the audience will miss the references. Pip suggests using the compass to find the archive. It spins toward the steel latch again. Tavi sets the crate outside the magnetic field, and the needle settles north. The compass finally works, pointing to the studio's north wall.

ODO: There is no door there.

LUMA: There is a painted door.

The crew opens the flat. Behind it is the actual storage room, with a shelf labeled ARCHIVE. Pip carries the props through. Vex follows with the spoon, saying it could still matter. Tavi places each item on the correct shelf and returns the whistle to its labeled hook. The bell on the hook makes a familiar sound. Everyone looks at Pip.

PIP: I am not answering that one.

Odo films a quiet shot of the compass pointing north. No hidden initials, no old hero's scarf, no crate in the reflection. Vex watches and admits the image feels new. Then he notices the first-day sign is upside down.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The preview audience watches Pip discover the compass, follow it to the archive, and choose a path of his own. When the old objects appear, they are put away rather than treated as clues. Vex listens for recognition laughs. A guest laughs when Pip refuses the whistle. Vex whispers that this may be the new catchphrase.

LUMA: It was one sentence.

VEX: So was the old catchphrase.

Odo asks Tavi to bring the props back for the premiere display. Tavi returns with an empty cart. The items are safely shelved. Vex starts to complain, then sees the film's new compass on the screen pointing forward, away from the archive. He leaves the cart in the aisle and follows it to the exit.

FINAL IMAGE: The old bent spoon rests on its numbered shelf while the fresh compass needle points through the theater doors into the dark lobby.
