# The Relay of the Red Ribbon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports underdog dramas, training montages, rival team taunts, and a finish decided by an overly ceremonial relay ribbon*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
PAX, a macaque sprinter
DORI, a capuchin distance runner
WELL, a gibbon high jumper
COACH SENA, a ring-tailed lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. PARK TRACK - MORNING

The Canopy Games are one day away. Coach Sena leads her small team through a training montage: short sprints, long jumps, and a serious group stare at the finish line. A camera circles them in slow motion as they drink water.

PAX: We are the smallest team.

DORI: We have three runners.

WELL: The other teams have four.

COACH SENA: The fourth leg is a ribbon. We will carry it together.

She lifts a long red ribbon used as the relay marker. A gust blows it around the team's ankles, tying them together.

SCENE 2. EXT. TRACK - AFTERNOON

The rival team practices baton handoffs with polished batons. Pax's team uses the ribbon. Each runner must pass it without dropping it. Dori throws it too far; Well catches it with his foot. Pax knots it accidentally around a hurdle.

COACH SENA: The ribbon is ceremonial.

PAX: It is also in the hurdle.

WELL: Then the hurdle is participating.

The team practices again. The ribbon passes cleanly, but the officials say it must be carried, not dragged. Coach Sena trims the ribbon; now it is too short to reach the next runner.

SCENE 3. EXT. PARK TRACK - GAME DAY

The stadium is full. The announcer introduces each rival as a legend. Pax is introduced as "a runner who arrived early." The team takes its lane. The officials display the red ribbon on a pedestal beside the finish line.

ANNOUNCER: The ribbon will be presented to the winning relay team.

PAX: I thought we were carrying it.

COACH SENA: We are. The ceremony is carrying us.

The starting horn sounds. Dori runs the first leg, then passes the ribbon to Well. Well leaps over the hurdle and nearly carries it over the fence. The crowd gasps.

SCENE 4. EXT. TRACK - CONTINUOUS

Pax starts his leg. The ribbon catches on a flag pole and unrolls across the lane. The other teams stop, thinking it marks a new boundary. Pax runs through the confusion. The referee waves him back, but he has not crossed the finish.

PAX: Is this a foul?

OFFICIAL: It is a boundary.

DORI: The ribbon is the finish line.

WELL: It has changed jobs.

Coach Sena runs down the sideline and gathers the ribbon without entering the lane. The officials confer. The ribbon has crossed into two lanes and tangled with the scoreboard cable.

SCENE 5. EXT. TRACK - LATER

The race restarts. Coach Sena ties the ribbon into a short loop and passes it from runner to runner like a baton. This time the handoffs are clean. Pax receives the loop at the final leg and sprints toward the finish. The rival team is ahead by half a step.

Pax sees the ribbon trailing from his hand. Instead of pulling it, he passes the loop to Dori, who has run beside the lane to encourage him. Dori catches it and runs alongside. They cross the finish together, followed by Well and Coach Sena.

SCENE 6. EXT. AWARDS PLATFORM - SUNSET

The judges debate whether a team of three plus a coach counts as a relay. The crowd chants for all four. The officials award the red ribbon to the whole team, then place it around the finish-line post so it cannot tangle anyone.

COACH SENA: We have won the spirit of the event.

PAX: Did we win the race?

The official checks the camera footage. It shows the rivals crossed first, but paused to untangle their batons. The teams tie on time. Everyone receives a small medal.

SCENE 7. INT. PARK PAVILION - EVENING

The team reviews the race footage. The camera shows Pax slowing down when the red ribbon came loose, then Dori running beside him to finish the last leg. The announcer cuts the footage into a heroic montage, adding close-ups of the officials debating the rules.

COACH SENA: They will remember the finish.

PAX: They will remember the ribbon.

WELL: They will remember the judges tying it to a post.

The rival team arrives and offers to train together for next year. The athletes exchange batons, ribbons, and one mislaid shoe. Coach Sena puts the red ribbon in a drawer, where it immediately knots around the handle. The coach closes the drawer and leaves it tied until next season.

FINAL IMAGE: The red ribbon hangs at the finish as the athletes take turns running through it like an extremely slow victory lap.
