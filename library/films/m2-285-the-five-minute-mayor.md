# The Five-Minute Mayor

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: campaign-trail political comedies, stump speeches, staged town-hall moments, and a candidate who confuses applause with agreement*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A gibbon mayoral candidate promises to fix the town's public clock in five minutes, then must decide whether to keep the camera-ready stunt or hear the residents who actually maintain it.

Cast
- CANDIDATE ORI, a gibbon who practices speeches in front of reflective windows.
- CAMPAIGN MANAGER VERA, a macaque who times applause.
- RIVAL KET, a capuchin incumbent who knows the clock's maintenance schedule.
- TOWN CLOCKKEEPER NALA, an orangutan with a pocketful of tiny tools.
- REPORTER SAI, a ring-tailed lemur who asks one question at a time.

## Scene 1: The Promise

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

The town clock is stopped at 4:15. Candidate Ori stands beneath it with a banner: FIVE MINUTES TO A BETTER TIME.

ORI: This clock has been silent for months. Today, we will make it work before the next bell.

VERA: That is a strong opening. Do not mention the wiring.

SAI: Who maintains the clock?

ORI: We have a team.

NALA: I do. The gear needs a new pin.

ORI: We will fix it in five minutes.

Nala explains the clock face must be removed, the housing opened, the pin replaced, and the weights reset. Vera pulls Ori toward the camera.

## Scene 2: The Staged Repair

INT. CLOCK TOWER - LATER

The campaign crew sets up a podium and a ribbon. Ori holds a wrench for a photo. He turns it once. The clock makes a loud clunk, then stops again.

VERA: The clunk sounded decisive.

NALA: It was the pin falling out.

KET: The clock needs a proper repair. We can close the tower for an hour.

ORI: The people want the bell now.

NALA: They want the time to be right.

The crew records a second take. Ori stands in the exact same spot and announces that the clock is almost ready. The town's birds fly away when the bell rings prematurely.

## Scene 3: The Town Hall

INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

The campaign hosts a town hall. Residents ask questions about the stopped clock, the blocked walkway, and the tower's leaking roof. Vera gives Ori a stack of cue cards.

VERA: Start with the clock. Pivot to the future.

ORI: The clock needs a new pin, the walkway needs repair, and the roof leaks over the gear room.

VERA: You were supposed to pivot.

ORI: Those are the problems.

Nala displays the damaged pin. Ket admits the repair was delayed because the town had no spare part. Sai asks why the campaign promised five minutes.

ORI: Because I thought the clock would be simple.

NALA: It is simple. It is not five minutes.

The residents volunteer to help source the pin, clear the walkway, and patch the roof. The meeting becomes a work list rather than a rally.

Vera tries one more staged question, asking a resident to praise Ori's promise. The resident instead asks who will check the clock every month. Nala proposes a rotating committee. Ket offers to lend the town's spare tools, and Ori writes the schedule on the campaign banner's blank side.

ORI: We can start the inspection after the election.

NALA: We can start tomorrow. The clock does not know the election date.

The audience laughs. Vera lowers her camera. The residents begin sorting tools by size, and the reporter records their names for the committee list. Ori stays to carry the heaviest box rather than stand beneath the banner.

## Scene 4: The Repair

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - EVENING

Nala fits a new pin. Ket and Ori carry a ladder. Vera holds the work lamp, though she keeps checking how it looks on camera. Sai films the repair without an announcer. The clock's gears catch, then turn steadily.

NALA: The weight is balanced.

ORI: Is it safe to ring?

NALA: After I inspect the bell rope.

Ori looks at Vera. She gestures toward the banner. He lowers it and asks the crew to move away from the clock face. The inspection takes another twelve minutes.

## Scene 5: The Bell

The clock strikes the correct hour. The bell rings once, clear and even. The square cheers, then returns to the repair crews. The walkway is cleared. A tarp covers the roof until the proper patch arrives.

SAI: Candidate, do you still claim you fixed it in five minutes?

ORI: I did not. The town fixed it after I stopped talking.

VERA: That is not the slogan we rehearsed.

ORI: It is the true one.

KET: I will support your plan for a maintenance fund.

ORI: Even if I lose?

KET: The clock will still need care.

## Scene 6: Election Night

At a small gathering, Ori waits for results without a speech. Sai reads the tally: Ket has won by a narrow margin. Ori congratulates him. Vera folds the campaign banner and uses it to cover the tools from the rain.

KET: I would like you on the clock committee.

ORI: I can be there in five minutes.

NALA: The meeting starts in an hour.

ORI: Then I'll bring a chair.

## Final Image

The town clock advances past 4:15. Ori sits below it beside the ladder, listening as the next bell arrives on time.
