# The Village Where Shadows Left at Sunset

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: supernatural village mysteries, ominous omens, and villagers who treat an ordinary shadow as a missing person*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a lemur village keeper
TAVI, a macaque astronomer
VEX, a gibbon blacksmith
PIP, a capuchin child
THE SHADOW, a silent silhouette

SCENE 1. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - DUSK

The sun drops behind the ridge. The villagers' shadows stretch across the square. One villager ducks behind a cart as a lantern swings; a second, elongated shadow seems to detach and race toward the well.

PIP: Someone has lost their shadow.

Mara rings the alarm bell. Vex closes the forge door. Tavi looks at the sky.

TAVI: The light is low.

MARA: The shadow moved on its own.

The shadow slides behind a cart. Vex grabs a lantern and shines it at the cart. The shadow grows twice as large.

VEX: It is getting stronger.

TAVI: You are making it bigger.

SCENE 2. INT. VILLAGE HALL - NIGHT

Mara gathers witnesses. Each person describes the missing shadow. One says it was long and narrow; another says it was short and round. Tavi asks what direction the light was coming from.

PIP: From the west.

VEX: It went east.

TAVI: The shadow points away from the light.

Mara draws a map. Every witness's description points to the same well. The villagers decide to search it. Vex lowers a rope. The rope's shadow slides down the wall.

PIP: It has gone below.

TAVI: That is the rope.

Vex pulls the rope up. There is no shadow at the bottom of the well. He says the darkness has swallowed it.

Tavi asks each witness to stand where they first saw the shadow. She stretches a cord between their positions. The cord crosses the square at an angle.

MARA: It was moving in a straight line.

TAVI: So was the sun.

Vex shines his lantern from the opposite side of the square. The shadow appears behind Mara's back and stretches toward the hall. Pip follows it, then runs into the wall.

PIP: The missing shadow has entered the building.

MARA: That is your shadow.

Pip moves the lantern. The silhouette slides across the wall. Vex tries to pin it with his boot, but the light shifts again. The villagers move in a line, each stepping over the shadow as if it were a narrow stream. Tavi marks the lantern's position with chalk.

TAVI: We have changed the light source.

VEX: We have changed the suspect.

Mara orders everyone to turn off their lamps. The square goes dark. The shadow disappears.

PIP: It has escaped.

TAVI: It is only visible when something blocks the light.

Vex switches his lantern back on. His own shadow returns at once. He asks whether this makes him the suspect.

MARA: You are the person holding the lamp.

SCENE 3. EXT. VILLAGE WELL - MIDNIGHT

Tavi sets a lantern on the ground. A shadow appears behind the well. Pip runs to catch it. The shadow stretches away from him. He turns. It stretches the other direction.

PIP: It follows me.

TAVI: It moves when you move.

Vex asks Tavi to trap the shadow under a sheet. The sheet lands over the lantern and blocks the light. Every shadow disappears.

MARA: You have hidden the village.

VEX: I have caught the shadow.

Mara pulls away the sheet. The light returns. The shadow lies behind Vex's boots. He steps on it and announces that the creature is captured.

The shadow moves as he lifts his foot.

VEX: It escaped.

TAVI: You lifted your foot.

Vex tries to stand with both boots on the shadow. It stretches past his heels. Pip lays down beside it and covers one end with his arm. Mara moves the lantern, and the shadow slides beneath the villagers' feet.

MARA: It has gone through all of us.

PIP: It is still attached to the light.

The villagers lift their feet one at a time. Each shadow appears and disappears in turn. Vex steps off last. He salutes the empty ground.

VEX: The creature is free.

SCENE 4. EXT. VILLAGE LANE - BEFORE DAWN

The villagers place lanterns around the square. The shadows grow short and gather under their feet. Mara tells everyone to stand still while she counts them.

MARA: One, two, three, four.

The shadow count comes to five. They look around. Vex sees a second shadow from the lantern on the well.

TAVI: We have one lantern too many.

Pip carries it behind the forge. The extra shadow vanishes. Vex asks if the missing shadow has returned. Mara points to his feet. His shadow is there, though faint.

SCENE 5. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - SUNRISE

The sun rises over the ridge. Every shadow stretches west. The villagers cheer. Tavi says the shadows never left; the light changed.

MARA: We should cancel the search party.

VEX: We can search for the lantern.

PIP: It is behind the forge.

Vex walks there. His shadow follows him. The villagers begin to disperse.

FINAL IMAGE: The alarm bell rings once more. Everyone looks down at their shadows before realizing it is only the bell's shadow swinging on the wall.
