# The Orchard Beyond the Fog

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quest films, where travelers seek a legendary orchard hidden behind a fog bank*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A questing party follows a prophecy to an orchard that is hidden by ordinary morning fog and guarded by a gate with a practical latch.

Cast: Guide Nira, macaque; scholar Voss, baboon; scout Pavi, capuchin; keeper Luma, gibbon.

SCENE 1. EXT. FOREST PATH, DAWN

Four travelers stand before a bank of fog. Voss unrolls an old parchment.

VOSS: Beyond the silver veil lies the orchard of endless fruit.

PAVI: The fog is damp.

NIRA: It is morning.

LUMA: The orchard is across the field.

VOSS: The parchment calls it the Veiled Grove.

NIRA: The parchment was written before the path was built.

They enter the fog. Pavi holds a rope so no one loses the group. The rope tangles around a stump.

PAVI: The fog has captured our line.

NIRA: The stump has captured the rope.

VOSS: A guardian.

LUMA: It is a stump.

Voss asks the group to speak the password from the parchment. It says "open the golden gate." Luma says the gate has a latch and can be opened by lifting it.

SCENE 2. EXT. ORCHARD GATE, CONTINUOUS

They find a wooden gate, partly hidden in the fog. Voss bows.

VOSS: Keeper of the Veiled Grove, we seek the fruit of legend.

LUMA: Lift the latch.

Voss tries pushing the gate. It does not move. Pavi pulls. The gate swings inward.

PAVI: The gate has yielded.

NIRA: It opened.

VOSS: It has recognized us.

Luma asks them not to step on the seedlings. Nira leads the group along the path. The fog thins, revealing ordinary fruit trees and a shed.

VOSS: The prophecy is true.

NIRA: The orchard is real.

PAVI: Where is the endless fruit?

LUMA: There is a harvest basket.

SCENE 3. EXT. ORCHARD ROW, MORNING

Voss searches for a tree with golden leaves. Luma points to a plum tree. Pavi tastes one plum and says it is sour.

A small squirrel darts from the shed and steals a plum from the basket. Voss blocks its path with the parchment.

VOSS: The guardian demands a tribute.

NIRA: It is carrying your fruit away.

PAVI: I can catch it.

LUMA: Do not chase it through the seedlings.

Pavi stops. The squirrel drops the plum at the row's edge and vanishes. Luma retrieves the fruit; one bite is missing.

VOSS: It has marked the fruit.

LUMA: It has eaten it.

Nira asks the keeper if the party can take the bruised plum. Luma says it is fine to eat but should not be stored. They share it there rather than pack it with the apples.

VOSS: A feast before departure.

NIRA: A snack before carrying baskets.

PAVI: The fruit is not enchanted.

VOSS: It may be enchanted to test us.

NIRA: It is not ripe.

Luma shows them how to check ripeness. Voss examines each fruit as if reading omens.

VOSS: This one bears a mark like a crescent.

LUMA: A bird pecked it.

VOSS: The bird has left a prophecy.

Nira finds a fallen branch blocking the path. The party moves it together. Beneath it are several ripe apples.

PAVI: The treasure was under the branch.

LUMA: The apples fell yesterday.

VOSS: The prophecy mentioned a burden lifted.

NIRA: The prophecy is the branch.

SCENE 4. INT. ORCHARD SHED, LATER

The keeper gives them baskets. Voss wants to fill them to the brim. Luma warns that the baskets will be hard to carry through the fog.

VOSS: We have crossed the veil.

NIRA: We still need to get home.

They stack apples too high. The top layer tumbles. Pavi catches two; one rolls into the fog.

PAVI: The fruit is escaping.

Nira uses a cloth to cover the basket. Voss insists it resembles a sacred shroud. Luma ties it down with a simple knot.

VOSS: The binding is complete.

LUMA: It is a knot.

They carry the baskets to the gate. The fog has lifted. Voss looks disappointed.

VOSS: The veil is gone.

NIRA: The sun came up.

PAVI: We can see the way back.

SCENE 5. EXT. GATE, DAY

The group reaches the exit. Voss reads the final line of the parchment: "Return only with what you can carry."

VOSS: A test of worthiness.

LUMA: A limit on weight.

They each take one basket. Voss tries to carry two, but the handle stretches. He lowers one.

VOSS: I accept the trial.

NIRA: You accept one basket.

The party passes through the gate. The latch clicks shut behind them.

[SFX: GATE LATCH.]

PAVI: The orchard has closed.

LUMA: It is a gate.

Voss rolls the parchment carefully and tucks it away.

VOSS: The quest is complete.

NIRA: We have not eaten the apples.

VOSS: Then the feast is the next chapter.

Luma hands them each an apple. They eat as they walk.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS, CRUNCHING FRUIT, FOG BIRDS.]
