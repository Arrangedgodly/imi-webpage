# The Submarine With a Window Seat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: deep-sea thrillers that mistake ordinary fish outside a porthole for a discovery*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
CAPTAIN NARI, a lemur
PILOT MARA, a macaque
SCIENTIST PIP, a capuchin
MISSION VOICE VEX, an orangutan
TECHNICIAN ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. RESEARCH SUB - UNDERWATER

The small submarine drifts above a sandy shelf. Every crew member wears a sealed suit with a working voice link. A round porthole shows a school of silver fish.

VEX: Unknown objects are approaching the vessel.

PIP: They're fish. The camera confirms a school moving east.

NARI: We are holding position at the marked depth. No one touches the porthole.

MARA: The pressure gauge is steady. Odo, check the ballast reading.

ODO: Stable. The lift platform is ready for the ascent when the survey ends.

VEX: The shapes are circling us.

PIP: They are feeding on small organisms near the light. I will turn the lamp down so we don't disturb them.

SCENE 2. INT. RESEARCH SUB - CONTINUOUS

Pip dims the lamp. The fish scatter, then gather again farther from the glass.

VEX: The creatures have retreated from our warning.

NARI: They moved away from the light. That's all we observed.

MARA: Turn the camera to the sediment patch on the survey card. We need one image of the marked area before ascending.

ODO: Camera is live. The hatch to the lift platform remains closed until we reach the service bay.

VEX: Could we open it now for a dramatic exit?

MARA: No. We are underwater, and the platform is not aligned.

PIP: The fish have cleared the lens. The sand shows no tracks, just ripples from the current.

SCENE 2A. INT. RESEARCH SUB - CONTINUOUS

The school of fish moves across the porthole. One fish brushes the outer glass, leaving a faint line in the condensation on the inside.

PIP: The mark is on the inner pane. I'll note it, but I won't touch the glass while we're at depth.

ODO: Pressure is unchanged. The outer hull sensor shows no impact.

VEX: It tapped the window.

MARA: It passed the window. We have no reason to call that contact.

NARI: The survey card lists three things to record: depth, visibility, and direction of current.

PIP: Depth steady, visibility moderate, current east. The fish are moving east too.

VEX: Could the current be carrying them toward a hidden trench?

MARA: We can only report what the instruments show. The chart marks a flat shelf for another half-mile.

Pip records the measurements and places the slate in its clip. Odo checks that the lift platform remains locked.

ODO: Dock alignment is confirmed. We can return when the survey pass is complete.

NARI: Finish the image of the marked sediment, then stow the camera before we move.

PIP: The image is clear. No tracks visible beyond the current ripples.

SCENE 2B. INT. RESEARCH SUB - CONTINUOUS

Nari checks the route card against the instrument display. The eastward current remains steady.

NARI: The sample pass is complete. We can dock with the lift platform on the marked bearing.

MARA: Confirm outer door closed and platform pressure equalization before transfer.

ODO: Confirmed. No contact with the seabed required.

VEX: The crew returns without a discovery?

PIP: We discovered that the fish use the light edge and that the shelf is rippled. Both belong in the log.

MARA: That is enough to guide the next survey.

SCENE 3. INT. RESEARCH SUB - LATER

The sub docks to the flooded lift platform. Nari checks the seal indicators. Odo confirms the platform is flooded to equalize pressure.

ODO: Dock locked. Pressure equalized. We can open the inner hatch onto the platform.

MARA: Open it, but keep the outer ocean door shut.

The hatch opens. The crew steps onto the platform, still in sealed suits. Pip carries the camera and the survey slate.

VEX: We have reached the seabed.

PIP: We have reached the service platform. The ocean floor is beyond the outer door.

SCENE 4. INT. LIFT PLATFORM - CONTINUOUS

The platform begins its ascent. The fish appear through the side window, traveling alongside the hull for several meters.

NARI: They're following the light again.

PIP: The lamp is dim. They are moving with the current, which is carrying us upward.

ODO: Outer door remains locked. Inner hatch sealed. Lift is rising at the scheduled speed.

VEX: The school is escorting us home.

MARA: We can record that they swam alongside the window. We shouldn't assign them a mission.

At the surface, the platform locks. The crew removes their gloves only after the hatch opens into the dry dock chamber.

SCENE 5. INT. DRY DOCK - DAY

Pip reviews the footage. The fish cross the frame in one silver wave.

VEX: Our first contact.

PIP: Our observation: a school of fish passed the porthole during survey.

NARI: That's still worth sharing.

TAG: Vex watches the clip again and waves goodbye to the screen. A single fish swims past the edge of frame.
