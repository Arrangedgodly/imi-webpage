# The Room Behind the Curtain

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: stage illusion films, magician rivalries, elaborate misdirection, and a final reveal that the assistant solved the trick first*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
MYSTERIO, a capuchin magician
SADA, a ring-tailed lemur assistant
VEX, a macaque rival performer

SCENE 1. INT. GRAND THEATER - EVENING REHEARSAL

Mysterio announces his greatest illusion to three tired stagehands: making the entire stage disappear. The house is empty. Vex watches from a front-row seat, ready to challenge him. Sada carries a velvet curtain and checks its seams.

MYSTERIO: The audience will see a stage, then nothing.

SADA: The stage will still be there.

MYSTERIO: They won't know that.

SCENE 2. INT. BACKSTAGE - LATER

The crew prepares mirrors, trapdoors, smoke, and a rotating platform. Mysterio rehearses his gesture. Vex sneaks backstage to discover the mechanism. He opens a panel and finds a room behind the curtain.

VEX: I knew there was a trick.

SADA: There's always a trick. The question is whether it works.

Vex steps inside the hidden room. The panel closes. He vanishes from the stage, but no one can find the release latch.

SCENE 3. INT. STAGE - REHEARSAL CONTINUES

Mysterio begins. The smoke machine fails, filling the stage with bubbles instead. The rotating platform turns the wrong way. Sada adjusts the lighting manually, creating a shadow that looks like a second stage.

MYSTERIO: Behold, the impossible!

The stagehands see two stages, both occupied by Mysterio. One is a reflection; one is the real performer. A stagehand applauds with the two ends of a broken broom. Mysterio thinks the illusion is working.

SCENE 4. INT. ROOM BEHIND CURTAIN - CONTINUOUS

Sada hears Vex tapping from inside the hidden room. She follows the sound, finds the panel release, and opens it. Vex emerges carrying a mirror. He has accidentally discovered the missing angle that makes the illusion work.

VEX: I know how you did it.

SADA: So do I. We can either ruin the show or make the final reveal better.

Vex agrees to help. They reposition the mirror, lower the curtain, and create a clean passage for the stage crew.

SCENE 4A. INT. ROOM BEHIND CURTAIN - LATER

Sada checks the mirror against the empty house. The stage did not vanish; the dark fabric hid the platform while the mirror reflected an empty wall.

SADA: The trick works if the audience looks where you point.

VEX: What if they look behind the curtain?

SADA: Then we let them see how it works.

Mysterio enters with a sign reading "NO ASSISTANCE REQUIRED." Sada gives him one end of the mirror. He cannot hold the sign and the mirror together. Vex takes the sign, turns it over and writes "TWO ASSISTANTS REQUIRED." They test the lights. The stage disappears when the curtain closes; when it opens, the crew and mirrors are visible.

MYSTERIO: The audience came to be fooled.

SADA: Keep holding that end. Unless your greatest illusion is going to be a broken mirror.

Vex agrees to step into the hidden room during the performance and emerge at the cue. The crew marks a safe path with tape and removes one loose panel from the trapdoor.

SCENE 4B. INT. BACKSTAGE - REHEARSAL, BEFORE THE SHOW

The theater is still empty. Sada rehearses Vex's escape from the hidden room. Vex tries the marked path and catches his sleeve on the panel. They stop and remove the snagging nail. Mysterio watches, then asks if the audience will notice the change.

SADA: They will notice if Vex gets stuck.

VEX: I would prefer not to be the next illusion.

They test the light cue once more. The mirror reflects the empty stage when the curtain closes. A quiet hand signal tells the crew when to open the panel.

MYSTERIO: No smoke?

SADA: The smoke machine is still full of bubbles.

MYSTERIO: Then we will let the audience see the lights instead.

After the rehearsal, the crew opens the doors. The theater fills for the single public performance. Vex takes his place inside the hidden room, and Sada checks the latch from both sides.

SCENE 5. INT. STAGE - FINALE

For the public show, Mysterio adds a sweeping cape that nobody rehearsed. He hangs its trailing fabric over the hidden room's latch, then raises his hands. The lights go dark. When they return, the stage appears empty. The audience gasps. Mysterio steps out from behind the curtain, then notices Sada and Vex are still invisible.

SADA: The release latch is stuck.

Mysterio pulls the curtain. It falls forward, revealing the entire mechanism: mirrors, platforms, the hidden room, and the crew holding flashlights.

Vex steps onto the stage and shows the mirror angle. Mysterio bows, then introduces Sada as the person who designed the illusion and Vex as the person who improved it. The audience applauds the reveal. Sada and Vex bow together; Mysterio steps aside so the crew can reset the mirrors.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex holds up the reversed sign: "TWO ASSISTANTS REQUIRED." Mysterio reaches to turn it over, sees Sada holding his cape over a wastebasket, and leaves the lettering facing the audience.
