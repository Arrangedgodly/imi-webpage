# The Hotel Guest Who Was Never Booked

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mystery thrillers built around a guest who turns out to be a mirror reflection*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
MANAGER MARA, a macaque
GUEST VEX, an orangutan
CLERK ODO, a gibbon
BELLHOP PIP, a capuchin
HOUSEKEEPER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

Vex approaches the desk carrying one suitcase. Behind him, a dark figure appears in the mirror. Odo checks the booking screen.

VEX: Someone has followed me into the hotel.

MARA: I see one guest in the lobby.

VEX: Look in the mirror.

PIP: That's your reflection. It has the same suitcase.

VEX: My pursuer has excellent luggage.

ODO: We have a reservation for Vex, one room, two nights.

MARA: Welcome. I can show you to the elevator.

SCENE 2. INT. ELEVATOR - MOMENTS LATER

Vex watches the mirrored doors. Pip stands beside the luggage cart. As the elevator moves, the reflected light shifts.

VEX: The figure is still there.

PIP: It will stay there unless the mirror is covered.

MARA: The elevator is moving normally. We're on floor two.

ODO: The room key is in the envelope, with the room number facing inward.

VEX: The figure has read the key.

PIP: The figure is you. The key is in my hand.

SCENE 2A. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

Odo checks the camera monitor. A second dark figure seems to appear near the elevator.

ODO: The lobby camera shows another person behind Vex.

MARA: Is there anyone physically there?

PIP: I see only the wall mirror and Vex's reflection.

NARI: The camera is aimed at the brass elevator doors. They are reflecting the lobby lights too.

VEX: The pursuer has multiplied.

MARA: Let's turn on the lobby lamps and check the room in person. No one follows a reflection into the service corridor.

Vex stays by the desk. Mara and Pip walk to the elevator. The second figure shifts as they move.

PIP: It's the flower stand reflected in the brass. It looks like a person from this angle.

ODO: I can tilt the camera down a little so the elevator doors are not centered.

NARI: Keep the desk and front door in view. That's what we need the camera for.

Odo adjusts the camera mount. The reflection disappears from the monitor, while the front entrance remains visible.

VEX: The pursuer has been banished.

MARA: The camera view has been corrected. Vex, would you still like a room without a mirror?

VEX: Yes, but I no longer require a security detail.

SCENE 2B. INT. HOTEL LOBBY - NIGHT

Mara gives Vex two room choices. One has a matte wall; the other has a framed landscape painting under glass.

VEX: The painting could be a hidden mirror.

ODO: It is behind ordinary glass. We can check it without touching the frame.

PIP: I can stand to the side while Mara looks from the doorway.

MARA: The painting reflects the lamp, not another room. The matte-wall room is open if you prefer it.

VEX: I'll take that one. Please move the suitcase without covering the exit.

PIP: The cart can turn at the corner. The handle is secure.

NARI: I'll bring fresh towels after the guests settle, then clear the hall.

VEX: No one enters without knocking.

MARA: That's the hotel's normal policy.

Odo updates the room number on the key sleeve and hands it to Vex. The group walks to the new room in full light.

SCENE 3. INT. HOTEL ROOM - NIGHT

Mara opens the room door. The mirror opposite the bed reflects Vex's suitcase and the open doorway. Vex steps aside, and a second Vex seems to appear.

VEX: There are two of me.

NARI: I'm housekeeping. The mirror is clean; the room is ready.

MARA: Vex, stand still while I close the door. The reflection will move with you.

Vex freezes. Pip walks behind him, crossing the reflection. Vex spins around.

PIP: Nothing came through the wall.

ODO: Room assignment is correct. No second guest is registered.

VEX: Could I switch rooms?

MARA: If you prefer. The room across the hall has no full-length mirror.

SCENE 4. INT. HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

Pip moves the suitcase across the threshold. Vex follows, looking at the mirror one last time.

VEX: The other guest is staying behind.

NARI: The other guest was your image.

MARA: The replacement room is ready. The key envelope has the room number printed on it.

VEX: Do you have a room with no reflective surfaces?

ODO: The storage closet has a matte wall, but it is not for guests.

TAG: Vex closes the closet door on his own reflection in the brass handle. The handle shines anyway.
