# The Garden Wall's Secret Passage

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: adventure serials that turn an ordinary garden gap into a hidden passage*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
EXPLORER VEX, an orangutan
GARDENER MARA, a macaque
SCOUT PIP, a capuchin
ARCHIVIST ODO, a gibbon
NEIGHBOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - AFTERNOON

Vex presses his ear to a brick wall beside a row of tomatoes. A narrow wooden door stands open at one end of the wall.

VEX: The secret passage is breathing.

MARA: That is the breeze through the open garden door.

PIP: I can see the path on the other side. It leads to the compost bins.

ODO: The garden map marks one entrance and one exit. No tunnel.

NARI: We are expecting the compost cart. Keep the route clear.

Vex sketches a twisting tunnel on the wall map. Mara turns the map around to show its title: COMMUNITY GARDEN.

VEX: The map conceals its secrets.

MARA: It shows the beds, gate, and cart path.

Vex knocks on the bricks. A tomato drops from a vine and lands in his hat. He treats it as a signal.

VEX: The wall has sent an offering.

PIP: The stem was ripe.

SCENE 2. EXT. GARDEN WALL - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the wall's base for loose bricks while Odo holds the map. Pip walks through the open wooden door and returns from the compost side.

PIP: Clear path, no passage behind the wall.

VEX: You may have emerged in a decoy courtyard.

NARI: That is the compost area. The bins are labeled.

Vex tries to push one brick. Mara stops him before it shifts.

MARA: Do not remove masonry. The wall is old, and the bricks support the raised bed.

ODO: There is a maintenance note about a gap near the corner, but it is for air circulation.

VEX: A hidden vent.

MARA: A vent. We can look from the garden side without prying anything loose.

Nari holds a hand mirror near the gap. It reflects daylight from the other side. Vex leans close and sees his own face looking back.

VEX: The passage has a guardian.

PIP: The guardian is your reflection.

SCENE 3. EXT. GARDEN PATH - LATER

Odo finds an old metal ring partly covered by ivy. Vex declares it a secret handle. Mara checks the wall plan; the ring is attached to a short watering pipe.

MARA: It turns the irrigation valve. Don't pull it.

VEX: The passage has a mechanism.

PIP: The water is off for maintenance. The ring is not a door handle.

Nari opens the valve one quarter-turn while Mara watches the marked gauge. Water trickles into the tomato bed. Vex jumps back as though the wall has exhaled.

VEX: It is alive.

MARA: The irrigation is working.

ODO: Gauge is at the right mark. Nari, please close the valve after this row.

Nari turns it back and checks the gauge. The tomatoes stop dripping. Pip brings the compost cart through the open door, avoiding the wall and the beds. Vex attempts to guide it toward the brick gap.

PIP: The cart fits through the door, not the vent.

VEX: The adventure has chosen a wider route.

SCENE 3A. EXT. COMPOST PATH - CONTINUOUS

Nari inspects the cart and sees that the tomato in Vex's hat has rolled against the wheel. She lifts it out before Pip moves the cart. Vex asks whether it is a token from the passage.

NARI: It's ripe produce. I'll rinse it at the sink.

PIP: Cart path is clear. The wall's air gap stays uncovered.

ODO: The irrigation gauge is back to zero, and the valve is closed.

VEX: Could the air gap hide a second garden?

MARA: There is no second garden. The compost bins are on the other side of the door.

Vex peers through the gap again. This time he sees the labeled compost bin, not his reflection. He writes down its label as if it were a clue.

VEX: Organic waste. The secret language of gardeners.

NARI: It means leaves and stems go in that bin.

MARA: Now we can finish the cart run without blocking the path.

SCENE 4. EXT. GARDEN COMPOST AREA - LATE AFTERNOON

The cart is parked by the labeled bins. Odo records the water check; Mara confirms that no bricks were moved. Nari hands Vex the tomato that fell in his hat.

NARI: You can take the ripe one. It came from the vine, not the wall.

VEX: The passage has given up its treasure.

MARA: The plant gave us a tomato. The wall stays intact.

Pip walks through the open door with the empty cart. Vex follows, staring at the brick wall as he passes through the ordinary entrance.

TAG: Vex marks the map "secret passage" in pencil. Odo erases it and writes "air vent; do not use as doorway."
