# The Hero's Cape Is in the Wash

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: superhero origin films, where the hero cannot begin until a dramatic costume is ready*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A new hero prepares for a first rescue, but the cape is still wet and the costume crew keeps changing the emblem.

Cast: Hero Piri, capuchin; tailor Luma, lemur; director Odo, baboon; neighbor Tavi, macaque.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, MORNING

Piri stands in a plain vest. A red cape hangs from a drying line.

PIRI: The city needs a hero.

TAVI: The city needs help carrying these boxes.

PIRI: I need my cape.

LUMA: It is wet.

ODO: We can shoot around the damp patch.

LUMA: The cape drips.

Odo sets up a fan. The cape flaps, knocking over a cardboard skyline.

[SFX: CARDBOARD BUILDINGS FALL.]

PIRI: The wind has attacked the city.

TAVI: The fan knocked it over.

ODO: We have our first disaster.

SCENE 2. INT. COSTUME CORNER, LATER

Luma checks the cape. It is almost dry, except for one heavy corner. Piri asks if the emblem can be changed from a star to a lightning bolt.

LUMA: The star is already sewn on.

PIRI: The lightning bolt looks faster.

TAVI: The hero is standing still.

Odo says the emblem must be visible in the opening shot. Luma turns the cape inside out, showing a plain lining.

ODO: The hero has a secret identity.

PIRI: I am not wearing the cape backward.

LUMA: Then wait for the seam to dry.

Piri tries to practice a dramatic entrance without the cape. He jumps onto a low bench and slips. Tavi catches him.

PIRI: I meant to land there.

TAVI: You nearly landed on a box.

ODO: The cape can wait. We need to move the boxes.

SCENE 3. INT. HALLWAY, AFTERNOON

Tavi carries a stack of boxes toward the storage room. One blocks his view. Piri helps guide him around a corner.

TAVI: I can see the door.

PIRI: You can see the wall.

TAVI: The door is beyond it.

They navigate the hall. The stack wobbles; Piri steadies the bottom box. Odo films the scene.

ODO: This is a rescue.

PIRI: It is moving boxes.

ODO: It is a hero learning his purpose.

Luma brings the cape. The wet corner is now dry. She pins it to Piri's vest. The fabric catches on the bench and pulls him backward.

PIRI: The cape is pulling me away.

LUMA: The clasp is too tight.

TAVI: The hero is trapped by his own costume.

Luma asks Piri to lift his arms and turn so she can test the cape's fit. The fabric catches on a nail in the bench. Odo frees it carefully.

LUMA: The hem is snagged.

PIRI: The city has trapped the hero.

TAVI: The bench has a nail.

Odo hammers the nail flat. Piri takes one step; the cape no longer catches. He offers to carry the boxes again.

ODO: The costume stays clean.

PIRI: I can carry one with the cape folded.

Luma places it over his shoulder. The cloth slips. Tavi pins it to the vest with a harmless wooden clip, and Piri carries the box without dragging the cape.

Luma loosens the clasp and shortens the cape. Piri tries the entrance again. He walks in, turns, and the cape settles neatly behind him.

ODO: Excellent.

TAVI: The boxes are still in the hall.

Piri removes the cape and helps carry the last box. Odo complains that the costume should stay on for continuity.

PIRI: The boxes will not carry themselves.

LUMA: The cape is safe on the bench.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, EVENING

The hall is clear. Piri tests the cape in a small gust from the open door; it lifts, but the shortened clasp holds. Odo asks for one clean heroic turn. Piri turns, and the cape sweeps a stack of empty boxes off the bench.

PIRI: The city is saved.

TAVI: The boxes have fallen.

LUMA: The cape is dry. It is not trained.

Odo asks for another take. Piri removes the cape and folds it on the bench, then carries the boxes to storage. When he returns, Odo has framed the empty bench as a dramatic costume reveal.

PIRI: The hero has left the shot.

ODO: The costume remains.

He sets the camera wide for a final take. Piri walks through the cleared hall, cape folded over one arm, and lifts the last box onto the storage shelf. Odo starts to call for a dramatic rescue pose, then sees the shelf is empty and the hall is ready for tomorrow's meeting.

TAVI: That is useful.

PIRI: It is also finished.

LUMA: No sequel required.


TAVI: It is laundry.

PIRI: It is clean laundry.

[SFX: BOXES SET DOWN, CAPE FLUTTERS.]
