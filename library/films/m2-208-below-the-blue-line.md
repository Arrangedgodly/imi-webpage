# Below the Blue Line

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: deep-sea adventure films, sonar suspense, cramped submersible crews, and a discovery delayed by a stuck snack locker*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
CAPTAIN OMI, a gibbon
PILOT RHEA, a capuchin
SCIENTIST TAVI, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. SUBMERSIBLE - DAY

A tiny research sub descends below a blue marker line painted on its hull. The crew is mapping a coral canyon. The sonar pings twice, then goes silent.

OMI: We have lost the echo.

RHEA: The sonar is on. The canyon bends away from us.

TAVI: There is a ridge ahead. It could be a new reef.

A red light blinks. The snack locker has jammed open, releasing a bag of dried fruit into the cabin. Rhea catches it before it blocks the controls.

SCENE 2. INT. SUBMERSIBLE - CONTINUOUS

The sub turns carefully around the ridge. The windows reveal a field of glowing coral. The crew records the formation. Omi wants to continue deeper; Rhea warns that their battery is at half charge.

OMI: We have time for one more pass.

RHEA: We have enough power for one return trip and no extra pass.

Tavi checks the map. The coral field is shaped like a fan. The sonar returns when they face the canyon wall. It is not a new reef but a reflection bouncing from the ridge.

SCENE 2A. INT. SUBMERSIBLE - CONTINUOUS

Rhea checks the battery gauge. The descent has used more power than expected. Omi wants to keep mapping. Tavi proposes sending a sonar ping and waiting for the echo before moving.

OMI: We came to see the canyon.

RHEA: We came back to surface, too.

The ping returns from two directions. One is the ridge; the other is a school of fish. Tavi records both signatures and asks the crew to shut off the cabin light so they can see the bioluminescence through the viewport.

The cabin goes dark. Pale points appear along the canyon wall. The crew watches in silence. A fish brushes the glass and leaves a streak of light.

TAVI: That is not a reflection. The reef is alive.

RHEA: We have enough data to mark it. We should not chase the fish into the narrow passage.

Omi reluctantly turns the sub toward the return route.

SCENE 3. INT. SUBMERSIBLE - LATER

The jammed snack locker clicks. The door closes, then traps Tavi's notebook. The team tries to open it without disturbing the controls. Omi gives a dramatic speech about the unknown world beyond the window.

TAVI: The unknown world is in my notebook.

RHEA: We can retrieve it after we surface.

The locker opens on its own when the sub tilts. The notebook floats out, along with a small metal spoon. The spoon taps the hull. The sound echoes through the canyon.

OMI: There's another signal.

RHEA: That's the spoon.

The sonar pings back. The canyon's echo is clear again.

SCENE 3A. INT. SUBMERSIBLE - CONTINUOUS

The snack locker slams shut. The crew's last dried fruit packet is trapped inside. Omi tries the latch. It will not move. Rhea warns him to keep the controls clear. Tavi uses a measuring strip to find where the door is catching.

TAVI: The latch is bent, not locked.

The sub rocks gently. The locker opens with a click, and the snack packet floats out. Tavi catches it, then sees the tiny spoon drifting toward the console. Rhea catches the spoon with a gloved hand.

RHEA: Nothing loose near the controls.

OMI: Not even lunch.

They secure the locker with a strap and continue the ascent. The crew records the depth, battery level, and route. The canyon fades into deep blue below them.

SCENE 4. INT. SUBMERSIBLE - ASCENT

The crew records one final pass from a safe distance. Tavi identifies a school of tiny fish sheltering beneath the coral. Rhea plots a return route that avoids the ridge. Omi asks if they should name the formation.

TAVI: Name it after the habitat, not the spoon.

OMI: The Spoon Canyon has a certain drama.

RHEA: The map can call it Fan Reef until another crew confirms it.

SCENE 5. EXT. RESEARCH VESSEL - SUNSET

The sub surfaces beside the research vessel. The crew brings up samples and video. Their data confirms the coral field is a reflection-enhanced ridge, with a living reef below. They plan a return with a second sonar unit and more battery.

Omi gives the dried fruit bag to Rhea. The snack locker is taped shut until repaired. The crew signs the dive log, saves a copy of the sonar trace, and labels the site for a second visit. No one calls it a discovery until the data is checked.

FINAL IMAGE: The sub hangs above the blue marker line. A spoon swings inside the closed cabin, tapping once as the vessel rises toward the deck.
