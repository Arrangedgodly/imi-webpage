# The Last Shot of Wild Frame

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: found-footage creature films, shaky handheld cameras, recorded clues, and characters who keep filming instead of using the lights*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A wildlife crew films a mysterious shape in an aviary after closing, only to discover the frightening creature is their own camera shadow while a real problem develops above the flock.

Cast
- TESS, a macaque filmmaker who records every discovery.
- PIP, a capuchin sound operator who can identify wingbeats.
- LOMA, a ring-tailed lemur aviary keeper with a tail that grips the ladder.
- JORI, a gibbon volunteer who hates being filmed.
- THE SHAPE, a looming figure on the walls.

## Scene 1: The Tape

INT. AVIARY - NIGHT

A handheld camera records a dark corridor. A shadow slides across the wall. Tess gasps. The camera shakes so hard the image blurs.

TESS: Did you see that?

PIP: I heard it.

JORI: That was your elbow hitting the microphone.

Loma switches on a flashlight. The shadow disappears.

TESS: The creature avoids light.

LOMA: The light is on the camera.

Tess reviews the footage. The shadow is a wing-shaped smear. The crew has one hour to identify it before the aviary opens for a dawn tour.

## Scene 2: The Investigation

They follow scratches along the wall. Tess films close to the ground; every sound becomes enormous. A feather falls into the lens.

PIP: That was one feather.

TESS: It sounded like something landed.

JORI: We are in an aviary. Things land here.

Loma finds a loose support strap above the roost. The safety net sags near the keeper platform. Tess keeps filming the shadow instead of the strap.

LOMA: We need to secure this before the birds wake.

TESS: I need one more shot.

JORI: The birds need the net.

Tess lowers the camera reluctantly. They fetch a ladder.

## Scene 3: The Shape Returns

The crew climbs. A massive shadow appears behind them, filling the wall. The birds stir. Jori freezes. Tess turns the camera toward it, but the image shows only dark feathers.

PIP: I hear a wingbeat from the entrance.

LOMA: That is not a bird. It is the ceiling fan.

The fan slows, making the shadow grow. Tess shines her camera light. The silhouette moves exactly with her body. The lens hood has reflected the light and projected a shape on the wall.

TESS: I filmed my own shadow.

JORI: You filmed it three times and gave it a title card.

## Scene 4: The Real Emergency

The support strap slips. A section of net sags, and a pair of small birds fly toward the gap. Loma reaches to catch the strap; her tail wraps around the ladder rung, leaving both hands free. Pip turns off the fan. Jori holds the ladder steady. Tess finally sets the camera down and passes up a replacement strap.

LOMA: On my count, tighten.

TESS: One, two, three.

They secure the net. The birds settle on the inner perch. The crew checks each connection by hand, not through the camera viewfinder.

The largest bird wakes and flaps toward the dim corridor. Its wing catches the loose end of a strap. Loma lowers the ladder so the bird can land on a nearby perch; Jori uses a familiar call to guide it away from the net. Tess holds the replacement strap while Pip knots it. Nobody turns on the bright work light until the flock settles.

PIP: I can hear the small ones moving.

LOMA: They are shifting to the high branch. That is where they sleep.

TESS: I did not record the best part.

JORI: You helped with the best part.

Tess puts the camera down and keeps one hand on the ladder. The birds quiet. The crew waits another minute before packing away the tools.

## Scene 5: The Footage

In the keeper's office, Tess reviews the recording. It contains a shadow, a feather, a loose strap, and several shots of her own sleeve. She cuts the false creature sequence and keeps the repair footage.

TESS: The title can be "Night Maintenance." 

JORI: It should be "The Camera Is Not a Ladder."

PIP: That is a useful safety film.

Loma invites Jori to narrate the final version. Jori refuses to be on camera, so Tess films the repaired net and the empty hallway. The birds appear at dawn, safe behind the net.

## Scene 6: Opening

The aviary tour begins. The visitors see the secure net and hear Jori's voice explain how the crew checked the straps. Tess places the camera on a tripod, out of the birds' flight path. The strange shadow never returns.

## Final Image

At sunrise, the camera's red recording light reflects in the glass. Tess sees it and switches the camera off before the reflection can become another creature.
