# The Very Long Take at Lunch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: virtuoso long takes, hidden cuts, and crews who must keep a scene moving while ordinary needs interrupt the camera's path*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
CAMERA OPERATOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR VEX, a macaque
SOUND RECORDIST ODO, a baboon
CATERER TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - MORNING

Director Nari walks the room, mapping a single continuous shot with her finger. Actor Luma must enter, cross the hall, pick up a letter, and leave without a visible cut. Camera operator Pip follows the route with a handheld rig. Sound recordist Odo trails with a boom pole.

NARI: The camera never stops.

ODO: It can stop for lunch.

NARI: It can stop after the scene.

Tavi enters with a tray. Pip backs into her; the cups rattle. Nari asks them to repeat the route, this time avoiding the tray. Tavi moves the tray to the far end of the hall. Pip turns to follow Luma and bumps into a chair. Odo's boom dips into frame.

LUMA: The letter is under that chair.

PIP: The camera cannot go under the chair.

NARI: Then the chair is in the way of the story.

They remove it.

SCENE 2. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - LATER

Luma begins the take. She enters through the side door, crosses the hall, and sees the letter. Pip glides backward. Odo keeps the boom above her head. Vex waits behind a curtain for his cue.

LUMA: A letter. At this hour?

She picks it up. The paper is blank. Nari calls cut, the first cut of the day.

NARI: The letter needs writing.

VEX: My cue is to react to what it says.

ODO: It says nothing.

VEX: Then I can react to the suspense.

Tavi writes a line on the prop letter: "Lunch is in the kitchen." Vex reads it, enters too soon, and blocks Luma from camera. Pip turns around to save the shot; the camera sees the lighting stand. They reset. Tavi adds the word "after" to make the message less like catering.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - NOON

The crew attempts the long take again. Luma enters. Pip tracks backward. Odo follows the boom. Vex waits. Tavi rolls the lunch cart across a side aisle. Its wheel squeaks on every revolution.

NARI: Keep going.

Luma reaches the letter. The squeak lands on her pause. Vex enters on the wrong beat. He opens the letter and reads aloud.

VEX: Lunch is in the kitchen after we finish the scene.

ODO: It is in the kitchen now.

Tavi's stomach growls louder than the boom microphone. Pip swings the camera toward her. Nari lets it happen. Tavi holds up a covered sandwich like a prop. The camera follows it down the hall, leaving Luma alone with the letter. Odo swings the boom to follow, but the microphone cable is shorter than the camera route. He steps over it, turns sideways, and keeps the microphone just out of frame. Nari signals to continue.

LUMA: This is a very long take of lunch.

Vex pulls a chair into the shot. Pip moves around it without cutting, but Odo's boom catches the chair back. He keeps the microphone steady while the chair rolls behind him. Tavi takes one step to catch the cart; the lid comes off and the sandwiches slide toward the floor. Luma traps the tray with her foot. Vex catches one sandwich. Pip keeps the camera moving.

NARI: That is the scene.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY HALL - AFTERNOON

Nari watches the continuous footage. The accidental chase looks lively, but the letter never receives its dramatic close-up. Pip offers to repeat the whole take, but Tavi asks whether they can just film the letter separately. Nari refuses; the long take's point is that no one can rescue it with a cut.

Odo suggests putting the letter on the cart. The cart moves slowly enough for a close look. They set it in motion. The letter slides off and lands on Tavi's sandwich. Luma reads the line through a smear of mustard. Vex reacts with alarm. The camera glides to the kitchen door, where a table has been set.

SCENE 5. INT. COMMUNITY HALL KITCHEN - LATE AFTERNOON

They perform one last uninterrupted route. Luma enters, sees the letter, and lifts it. Vex reads the lunch instruction, then opens the kitchen door. The entire crew follows the camera inside. Tavi places the saved sandwiches on the table. Pip frames the food. Odo's boom clears the doorframe with inches to spare.

ODO: We made it without a cut.

NARI: You made it to lunch.

FINAL IMAGE: The camera keeps rolling as everyone sits down, and the slate operator quietly eats beyond the edge of frame.
