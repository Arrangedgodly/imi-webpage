# The Bookmobile's Last Stop

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: road-trip dramas, last-chance deliveries, scenic detours, and librarians who carry every book instead of checking the route*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
DRIVER NARI, an orangutan
LIBRARIAN PIP, a capuchin
MECHANIC LUMA, a lemur
MAPMAKER VEX, a macaque
READER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. BOOKMOBILE - DAWN

The town bookmobile is due at its final stop before the library closes for renovations. Librarian Pip stacks books to the ceiling. Driver Nari checks the route map. Mechanic Luma hears a rattle from the rear wheel. Vex says the scenic road is faster because it has fewer turns.

LUMA: It also has a closed bridge.

VEX: Only on the map.

The engine starts, and a stack of picture books slides into the aisle.

SCENE 2. EXT. TOWN ROAD - MORNING

The bookmobile travels along the river road. Odo waits at a stop with a list of books. Pip greets him and discovers the requested atlas is buried beneath a stack of large-print mysteries. He finds the atlas, but the bookmobile's step has shifted. Luma inspects the wheel and tightens the lug nuts.

Vex suggests driving faster to make up time. Nari refuses; the shelves need to stay secure. Pip rearranges the books by stop, not by genre. Odo helps by reading each return label. The bookmobile leaves the stop with the aisle clear.

SCENE 2A. EXT. ODO'S STOP - LATE MORNING

Odo returns three books and asks for a new mystery. Pip searches the shelves and finds the requested title is at the school, the next stop. Nari offers a hold slip so the book can be brought back tomorrow. Odo leaves with a travel guide instead. The bookmobile's shelves are labeled by subject, but the route list is arranged by stop. Pip changes the order of the returns so each book goes to the right place.

LUMA: If you sort by shelf, the books move with us.

PIP: If we sort by stop, they move to readers.

Vex realizes he has been using an old printed itinerary. He replaces it with the current route from the library system. The map shows the bridge closure and the school stop. Nari checks the road report before continuing.

SCENE 3. EXT. CLOSED BRIDGE - AFTERNOON

The scenic route reaches the bridge closure. Vex points to a hand-drawn detour on an old map. Nari checks the road sign and confirms the detour adds three miles. Pip reads a note from the library: deliver the final stack to the school before closing. The group turns around and takes the marked route.

A stack of books leans during the turn. Luma holds the shelves while Pip secures the strap. One book falls open to a picture of a bridge. Vex says the book has predicted the closure. Pip closes it and puts it in the right bin.

SCENE 3A. EXT. TOWN ROAD - AFTERNOON

The bookmobile's rear shelf latch comes loose on a curve. Luma stops the vehicle before any books fall. Pip tightens the latch with a screwdriver from the service kit. Vex holds the stack while Nari checks the road shoulder. They move the heaviest books to the lower shelf and add a second strap.

A passing driver offers directions to the marked detour. Vex looks at the map, then at the sign, and confirms they match. The crew takes the longer route at a steady speed. Pip keeps the atlas on the seat beside him, ready for Odo's stop.

SCENE 4. EXT. SCHOOL YARD - LATE AFTERNOON

The bookmobile reaches the school. Students return their books and help unload the last crate. Odo receives the atlas. Nari parks on level ground. Luma checks the rear wheel again and confirms it is secure. Vex asks whether the road trip has a final scenic moment. Pip opens the bus door to reveal a view of the school garden.

The library doors close for renovation, but the books have all reached their readers. Pip makes a note to return the hold slip when the branch reopens and labels the borrowed atlas with the return date. He checks the shelves before closing the door. Odo offers to keep the atlas until the library reopens. Pip says he can return it through the bookmobile route.

SCENE 5. INT. BOOKMOBILE - SUNSET

The crew drives back along the main road. The shelves are mostly empty. Vex folds the outdated map and labels it OLD DETOUR. Luma signs the repair log. Nari checks the next day's route, which has a newly opened bridge marked in clear ink.

FINAL IMAGE: The bookmobile's empty shelves rattle softly as it crosses the newly repaired bridge at a careful speed.
