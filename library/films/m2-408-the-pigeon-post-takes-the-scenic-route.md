# The Pigeon Post Takes the Scenic Route

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: courier adventures, unreliable animal messengers, and urgent letters that travel at the pigeon's pace*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
LUMA, a lemur postmaster
PIP, a capuchin courier
VEX, a macaque mayor
NARI, a gibbon mapmaker
THE PIGEON, a silent performer

SCENE 1. INT. POST OFFICE - MORNING

A red envelope arrives marked "URGENT, DELIVER BEFORE NOON." Luma assigns it to Pip. The destination is the mayor's office across the square.

PIP: I can walk there.

LUMA: It must go by pigeon post.

NARI: The pigeon knows the route.

Pip ties the envelope to the pigeon's leg. The pigeon flies to the roof and begins preening.

VEX: The mayor is waiting.

LUMA: The bird has a schedule.

SCENE 2. EXT. ROOFTOPS - LATER

Pip follows the pigeon along the rooftops. It hops from chimney to chimney, stopping to peck at crumbs. Pip calls it back. It flies to a weather vane and sits there.

PIP: It has reached the north tower.

NARI: The mayor's office is south.

The pigeon circles the square and lands on the post office roof again. Pip says it has completed the loop. Luma explains that the pigeon is following the old route, which takes it to the bakery before the mayor's office.

VEX: The bakery is closed.

The pigeon flies to the bakery and waits by the door. Pip climbs down and opens it. The pigeon enters, eats a crumb, then flies out again.

Pip checks the route book. It lists the bakery as stop two and the mayor's office as stop three. The pigeon has made both stops in order, although no one placed an address at the bakery.

LUMA: It is following the route.

PIP: The route was written for a person.

NARI: The pigeon knows the landmarks.

The bird lands on a church roof. Pip climbs the stairs to catch up. By the time he reaches the top, the pigeon is on the post office roof again. It preens, then drops a feather beside him.

PIP: Is this a receipt?

LUMA: It is a feather.

Vex calls from the square that the mayor is waiting. The pigeon does not move. Pip offers it a crumb from his pocket. It takes the crumb and flies toward the bakery once more.

SCENE 3. EXT. SQUARE - 11:50 A.M.

The pigeon lands on the mayor's balcony. The envelope is gone. Pip asks Vex if it arrived. Vex says there is no letter.

Nari checks the pigeon's leg. A paper strip remains tied to it. Luma reads the message: "The pigeon has arrived. Please send the mayor outside."

VEX: Who wrote this?

PIP: The pigeon?

Luma recognizes the handwriting. It is hers. She wrote the delivery receipt before attaching the envelope.

NARI: The bird delivered the receipt.

PIP: It delivered something.

Vex climbs onto the balcony and checks the corners. The envelope is stuck behind the weather vane. It has not reached the mayor's desk, but it has reached the mayor's building.

VEX: The address is correct.

NARI: The floor is not.

Pip reaches toward the vane. It turns with the wind and catches his sleeve. Vex holds the ladder while Pip frees the envelope. The pigeon watches from the ledge and pecks at the paper.

PIP: The delivery is complete.

LUMA: The message is still sealed.

Vex says the mayor's office is on the ground floor. Pip carries the envelope downstairs and hands it to him.

SCENE 4. INT. MAYOR'S OFFICE - MOMENTS LATER

Vex opens the envelope. It is still sealed. The message says the noon ceremony has moved to two o'clock.

VEX: We are almost two hours early.

LUMA: The letter was urgent.

VEX: It was also late.

Vex changes the ceremony card from "noon" to "two." The ink runs through the paper and stains the time on the opposite side. Nari holds it to the window. It now appears to say "soon."

VEX: That is not a time.

NARI: It is the pigeon's schedule.

Luma stamps the card RECEIVED. The stamp lands across the two and turns it into a three. Vex wipes the ink with his sleeve, leaving a purple stripe.

The pigeon enters through the open window and lands beside the envelope. Vex offers it a crumb. The pigeon takes the crumb and flies out again.

SCENE 5. EXT. MAYOR'S BALCONY - EVENING

The ceremony begins at two. Vex gives a speech. Luma asks Pip to attach the response letter to the pigeon's leg. Pip ties it carefully. The pigeon looks at the square, then turns toward the bakery.

NARI: It is taking the old route.

PIP: The bakery is open now.

Vex says the reply can wait until morning. Pip unties the letter and walks it to the post office.

FINAL IMAGE: The pigeon flies over the square with an empty leg band, passing the post office twice before choosing a roof.
