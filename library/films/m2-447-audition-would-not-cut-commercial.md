# The Audition That Would Not Cut to Commercial

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: televised talent-show auditions that manufacture suspense, sob stories and cliffhangers around ordinary performances*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A monkey tap dancer auditions for a talent show, but the producers keep interrupting the act to tease the next round before it has even ended.

Cast: Contestant Tiko, a macaque; host Rava, a gibbon; judge Meli, a capuchin; producer Nori, a lemur; floor manager Dasa, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. TELEVISION STAGE - DAY

Tiko waits on a taped X. A camera pushes close to his face. Rava whispers dramatically.

RAVA: Tiko has traveled across town to show us what he can do.

TIKO: I took the bus. It stopped twice.

NORI: Can we get a more emotional answer?

TIKO: I am excited to dance.

Nori signals for a music sting. It plays before Tiko begins. The judge turns toward the sound booth.

MELI: Was that the ending?

RAVA: Not yet. We are building anticipation.

SCENE 1A - INT. TELEVISION STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Rava looks at a monitor. The producer has queued three close-ups, a sponsor card and a slow-motion shot of Tiko's shoes.

RAVA: The sponsor wants the shoes featured before the dance.

TIKO: They are shoes. They make a tapping sound.

NORI: We could ask how long it took to find them.

TIKO: I borrowed them from my aunt. She is sitting in the front row.

Meli recognizes the shoes and waves. Tiko waves back. Nori signals for the slow-motion sequence. The camera records Tiko's small wave as if it were a dramatic farewell.

DASA: That is not his act. The audience came to see the dance.

NORI: The preview needs an emotional button.

MELI: Use the end of the dance. It already has a bow.

Rava removes the sponsor card from the monitor and lets Tiko stand on the X without a close-up. The shoes are now in frame, tied correctly and polished for the camera.

SCENE 2 - INT. BACKSTAGE HOLDING AREA - MOMENTS LATER

Tiko warms up by tapping his shoes. Nori reads a cue card with a headline: HE RISKS EVERYTHING. Tiko ties one shoe lace.

NORI: We need a short personal story.

TIKO: I learned tap from my aunt.

NORI: Did you overcome a major obstacle?

TIKO: My first pair of shoes was too big.

Rava turns to camera and repeats the story as if it were a revelation. Dasa checks the stage marks.

DASA: We have ninety seconds for the act. Please do not step beyond the blue tape.

TIKO: I can finish in one minute.

SCENE 3 - INT. TELEVISION STAGE - CONTINUOUS

The music begins. Tiko performs a quick, precise tap sequence. The camera cuts to Meli's face. Nori signals for a reaction shot. Meli keeps watching the dance, so Nori plays a gasp from the soundboard.

MELI: I did not gasp.

RAVA: Our judge is stunned.

Tiko finishes a clean turn and a short shuffle. The soundboard plays applause early. He stops mid-step.

TIKO: I still have the ending.

DASA: Let him finish.

The applause track cuts off. Tiko performs a final heel-toe and bows. The audience claps for real.

SCENE 4 - INT. JUDGES' TABLE - MOMENTS LATER

Meli offers feedback. Nori hands Rava a card reading WAIT FOR THE RESULT.

MELI: Your timing was excellent. The early music cue interrupted your last step.

TIKO: I can repeat it without the cue.

RAVA: We will find out after the break.

TIKO: Did I pass?

NORI: The card says tease the result.

MELI: He did pass. The answer is yes.

Rava looks at the camera, then at the card. He puts it down.

RAVA: Tiko is moving on to the next round.

SCENE 5 - INT. BACKSTAGE HALL - LATER

Tiko packs his shoes. Nori asks him to film a reaction for the preview. Dasa checks that the camera is off.

NORI: Can you look surprised when you hear the news?

TIKO: I already heard it.

NORI: It is for the teaser.

TIKO: Then show my dance. That is what I came to do.

Meli walks by and gives Tiko a card with the next rehearsal time. Rava follows with a fresh cue card, but Dasa takes it away before he can read another cliffhanger.

RAVA: The next round starts Monday. You do not need to pretend to be shocked.

TIKO: Good. I will bring the shoes that fit.

Nori watches Tiko leave, then crosses out HE RISKS EVERYTHING from the cue cards.

FINAL IMAGE: The stage sits empty except for one taped X, while the applause track waits in the sound booth with its play button covered by a note: AFTER THE ACT.
