# The Courtroom of Small Claims

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: courtroom dramas that stretch a minor dispute into surprise evidence, cross-examinations, and a grand ruling*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A neighbor brings home the wrong picnic blanket after a rainstorm, and the small-claims court turns the mix-up into a dramatic trial before the next case arrives.

Cast: judge Mella, an orangutan; claimant Piri, a macaque; neighbor Vonn, a capuchin; clerk Sato, a lemur; witness Gobi, a gibbon; attorney Yoro, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY COURTROOM - DAY

Piri places a photograph on the exhibit table. It shows her star-stitched blanket on a park bench. Vonn enters carrying one folded blanket inside a clear evidence sleeve. Sato helps him set it down. Mella bangs a wooden block. It slides off the desk into Sato's in-tray.

MELLA: Court is in session.

SATO: The block has entered evidence.

PIRI: My blue picnic blanket disappeared from the park after the rain.

VONN: I brought this one home from the bench. It may be hers.

MELLA: Then we will identify it before anyone unfolds it again.

Piri points to the photo. Its corner shows a small embroidered star. The blanket in Vonn's sleeve has one folded corner turned inward. Mella tells Sato to keep it sealed until testimony.

SCENE 2 - INT. COURTROOM - LATER

Piri calls Gobi to the stand. Sato moves the witness chair to the center aisle for the dramatic reenactment. Gobi tries to sit, finds the seat too high, and uses the witness-box step as a stool.

GOBI: I saw Vonn carrying two blankets out of the park. It was raining hard.

YORO: You are certain there were two?

GOBI: One over each shoulder. He looked like a very small laundry rack.

VONN: I was trying to keep them out of the puddle.

PIRI: The park bench is beside the shared coat hooks. My bag and Vonn's bag were both there.

Mella asks them to show how they fold their blankets. Vonn demonstrates on the empty evidence sleeve, making a neat square. Piri demonstrates with her hands, rolling an imaginary blanket into a lumpy bundle. Gobi identifies the inward fold he saw, but says the rain and Vonn's umbrella blocked the stitching.

MELLA: A confident memory is not the same as a clear view.

SCENE 3 - INT. COURTROOM - CONTINUOUS

Yoro produces a repair receipt. It says Vonn had a moon patch replaced on his own blue blanket last month. Sato reads the receipt into the record, then retrieves the court's lost-and-found ledger. The ledger notes a moon-stitched blanket returned from the park the morning after the storm.

VONN: That must be mine. I had forgotten the patch until you read the receipt.

PIRI: But you still have the blanket shown in my photograph.

VONN: I did not notice the star. I saw blue cloth and assumed it was mine.

Yoro opens his briefcase for a photograph. It is upside down. Sato rotates it. The picture shows Piri and Vonn beside their bags at the park, but the blankets are hidden under the umbrella. The courtroom leans forward as if the image reveals a conspiracy.

MELLA: Does this photograph identify either blanket?

YORO: It establishes proximity, Your Honor.

MELLA: It establishes that two people stood near two bags.

Sato pulls the star blanket from the sleeve only far enough to match it with the photograph Piri brought. The star stitch aligns with the corner in her image. Vonn watches the comparison and recognizes that he carried Piri's blanket home while returning his own moon-patched one to the park.

SCENE 4 - INT. COURTROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Sato brings the moon blanket from the lost-and-found cabinet. Its crescent patch matches Vonn's receipt. Vonn places his hand beside the patch without touching it. Piri's photograph remains on the table as the reason they can identify the other blanket.

PIRI: The star one is mine. The moon one is Vonn's.

VONN: I returned my own to the park because I thought it belonged to whoever left it there. Then I kept yours by mistake.

GOBI: That is what I saw, except I thought the umbrella was a third blanket.

YORO: We withdraw the theory of a deliberate taking.

Mella begins a long ruling. The courtroom timer rings for the next case. She stops, looks at the two blankets, and gives a concise verdict: Vonn returns the star blanket to Piri; the court releases the moon blanket to Vonn. Both owners must label their gear before the next picnic.

SCENE 5 - INT. COURTROOM - CONTINUOUS

Vonn hands the star blanket to Piri once. Sato hands the moon blanket to Vonn once. They write their names on fabric tags at the clerk's desk. Mella sets the wooden block on her desk again. It slides back into the in-tray.

MELLA: The court orders the block to remain on the desk.

SATO: It has chosen its jurisdiction.

The next pair enters carrying one mitten. Yoro closes his briefcase carefully this time. Gobi steps down from the witness-box step and folds his chair into the aisle.

FINAL IMAGE: Piri and Vonn leave with their separately labeled blankets, while the wooden block sits squarely inside the in-tray beneath a tiny evidence sticker.
