# Arrows on the Sand Table

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: war films that present battlefield planning as a grand map-room ritual while ignoring practical supply problems*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A troop's commander moves wooden arrows across a sand table while the field cook keeps pointing out that the bridge will not support the supply cart.

Cast: Kela, a gorilla commander; Moti, a lemur quartermaster; Rusk, a macaque scout; and Puck, a capuchin map artist.

SCENE 1. INT. FIELD TENT, DAWN

Kela stands over a sand table. Puck has painted a blue river and marked three crossings with tiny flags. Moti enters carrying a ledger and an empty crate.

KELA: We move at first light.

MOTI: The bridge is closed.

KELA: The map shows a crossing.

MOTI: The map is from last season.

Rusk returns from scouting. The north bridge has a missing plank; the south ford is shallow but muddy. Kela asks Puck to move an arrow through the river. Puck places it on the blue paint.

RUSK: That arrow is now underwater.

KELA: It is a symbol.

MOTI: The cart still has to cross.

Puck adds a little wooden cart behind the arrow. Its wheels sink into the sand. Kela asks him to clear the route. Puck lifts the cart; it is stuck to the table by the blue paint.

KELA: Your river has trapped the supplies.

PUCK: The camera will be above it.

MOTI: The cart is still stuck.

Puck peels the model free, taking a strip of blue paint with it. The northern crossing is now a dry beige gap on the map.

SCENE 2. EXT. NORTH BRIDGE, MORNING

Kela, Moti, and Rusk inspect the bridge. Puck brings the sand table in a wagon for a matching shot. The wooden bridge model is intact. The real bridge has a loose plank and a rope barrier.

KELA: Can the cart cross?

MOTI: Not until the plank is repaired.

Kela asks Rusk to find another route. Rusk says the south ford is passable for foot soldiers but not the supply cart. Moti explains that the cart carries dry food and blankets. Kela points to the ridge. Puck draws an arrow up the ridge, around the ravine, and back to the bridge.

RUSK: The ridge path is too narrow.

PUCK: It looks heroic from above.

Moti places the crate on the cart. One wheel sinks in the mud. Kela and Rusk push from behind. The cart moves six inches and stops against a rock. Puck marks the rock with a red flag and calls it enemy resistance.

MOTI: It is a rock.

KELA: We will not waste the crew's strength on a symbol.

SCENE 3. INT. FIELD TENT, AFTERNOON

Kela studies the routes. The crew's dramatic music swells whenever she moves a flag. Moti lays out the actual supply ledger. The nearest bridge crew can repair the plank by the following morning. The troop can carry enough water and food for one night at the safe camp.

KELA: Then we wait at the safe camp.

PUCK: The plan has no advance.

RUSK: We advance when the crossing is safe.

Puck erases the arrow. The blue paint smears across the table and covers the supply list. Moti turns the ledger toward Kela and points out that the cart is overloaded. Kela removes the spare tent poles, then checks that everyone still has a blanket. Puck asks her to make a speech about sacrifice. Kela points to the crew carrying the crate.

KELA: They are carrying dinner and water. Let them walk without a speech.

Moti asks Rusk to update the ledger with the bridge crew's arrival time. Rusk records it in pencil. Puck reaches for a dramatic red marker, but Moti moves the ledger out of reach.

MOTI: It is a schedule, not a battle plan.

Kela makes sure the cook has enough dry food for the night. The group follows the footpath.

Rusk identifies a footpath to the camp. It is short and dry. Puck asks if he can paint it as a sweeping flanking maneuver. Moti says the path is already on the map as a footpath. Puck adds a tiny boot.

SCENE 4. EXT. SAFE CAMP, NIGHT

The troop reaches the camp on foot. The supply cart stays behind the rope barrier until the bridge is repaired. Moti distributes food from the carried crate. Kela checks the roster and finds everyone accounted for. Rusk reports that the bridge crew has started work.

Puck sets the sand table beside the campfire. The painted river begins to run as dew reaches the table. The arrows float toward the edge.

KELA: That is the route?

PUCK: The map is adjusting to conditions.

MOTI: Put it under cover.

FINAL IMAGE: At dawn, the repaired bridge carries the cart across at walking pace. Kela walks beside it while Puck redraws the route without the red enemy rock.
