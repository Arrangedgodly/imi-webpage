# A Map of the Unfinished World

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: exploration epics that insist on conquering blank spaces even when residents already know the way*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: An expedition sets out to chart an unmarked valley, where local monkeys have already named every path and are trying to get on with their day.

Cast: Varo, a baboon cartographer; Leni, a macaque guide; Paku, a young gibbon surveyor; and Toma, a capuchin courier.

SCENE 1. EXT. VALLEY ENTRANCE, MORNING

Varo unfolds a huge blank map. Leni points toward a well-worn path.

VARO: We are the first to enter this unknown valley.

LENI: I live here.

Varo marks the path "Discovery Route." Leni says the name is Fern Trail. Paku sets a measuring stick at the entrance while Toma delivers a pouch of figs to a house farther up the path.

SCENE 2. EXT. FORK IN THE TRAIL, LATER

The expedition reaches a fork. Varo asks Leni to stand beside the left path while he surveys it. She points out that the right path leads to the stream and the left to the orchard.

VARO: We must verify the information ourselves.

LENI: You can see the stream.

Varo looks down the slope and marks the stream as "possible water." Toma returns along the right path carrying an empty pouch. He waves to a neighbor, who points him toward the orchard.

SCENE 3. EXT. STREAM BANK, NOON

Paku measures the width of the stream. The current is shallow, and a line of flat stones makes a clear crossing. Varo declares it the "unmapped ford." Leni corrects him: the stones were placed after last season's flood.

Paku writes the local name on the map. Varo asks him to use a bolder hand so the name looks important. Paku writes it exactly as Leni says it. A small monkey crosses the stones carrying a leaf cup of water.

VARO: The route has been proven.

LENI: It has been used all morning.

SCENE 4. EXT. ORCHARD RIDGE, AFTERNOON

The path opens into a grove. Toma has already delivered the pouch and is now returning with a note from the orchard keeper. It says the west side is muddy and the east side is clear. Varo tries to draw a heroic arrow through the mud. Paku stops him and adds a dotted line around it.

VARO: The expedition will advance.

LENI: The expedition will avoid a wet slope.

They follow the clear path. Paku notices the map is no longer blank. He adds the houses, the stream, and the safe return route. Varo studies the completed page and finds room for a decorative compass rose.

SCENE 4A. EXT. RAIN SHELTER, AFTERNOON

A short rain shower begins. Leni leads the group to a shelter built beside the trail. Varo asks if it is an expedition outpost. Leni says it was built by residents after last year's storm. Paku measures the roof and notices one loose woven panel. Toma has a coil of cord in his delivery pouch. The four tie the panel securely, keeping the map dry beneath it.

VARO: We have improved our base camp.

LENI: You have helped with the roof.

SCENE 4B. EXT. NOTICE BOARD, LATE AFTERNOON

The rain stops. Leni shows them a board with path names and seasonal notes. Paku checks each against his map. Two names are spelled differently because one marks the creek route and one the dry ridge. Varo wants to standardize them. Leni explains that neighbors use both names depending on where they enter. Paku records both, with a small arrow showing the connection. Toma adds the delivery times for the orchard and stream, then wipes mud from the board's lower edge.

Before leaving the notice board, Paku checks the map against the sign one last time. He adds a small marker for the shelter and notes that it is open to travelers during rain. Varo holds the paper flat while Leni points out the stream's seasonal turn. Toma folds the delivery note and keeps it with the map, so future visitors can find the orchard without asking the expedition.

SCENE 5. EXT. VALLEY ENTRANCE, SUNSET

The team returns to the entrance. Leni shows Varo where the valley map is kept for visitors. It includes the same trails, stream and orchard, with notes about seasonal mud. Varo asks where to display his map. Toma suggests the notice board beside it.

VARO: We have charted a world.

LENI: You have written down what the people here already know.

Paku pins up the expedition copy, with the local names in large letters. The two maps sit side by side. A breeze lifts one corner and reveals a note on the board: "Please use current trail map." Varo adds his decorative compass rose to the notice board frame.
