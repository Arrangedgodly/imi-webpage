# The Last Perfect Take

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: influencer-fame movies, viral-success montages, and algorithm-chasing reversals*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A macaque baker becomes famous after a cake collapses on camera, then discovers that the platform rewards the staged disaster more than the careful craft that feeds her customers.

Cast: Anya, a macaque baker; Flip, a capuchin editor; Nori, a lemur customer; Mr. Plume, a peacock brand manager; and a lighthouse cake with a suspiciously unstable shelf.

SCENE 1. INT. BAKERY, MORNING

Anya pipes icing onto a lighthouse cake for the harbor festival. Flip films close-ups for the bakery's page. A shelf wobbles. The cake slides, tips, and lands upright in a tray of frosting. Anya freezes, eyes wide. Flip watches the view counter climb.

FLIP: That was perfect.

ANYA: It was an accident.

The clip spreads online. Mr. Plume enters wearing a branded apron and a headset with no cable attached. He offers a sponsorship if Anya can recreate the fall. She points at the cake, still intact except for frosting on the lighthouse beam.

PLUME: We need the same surprise, with a stronger hook.

ANYA: A hook would damage the cake.

SCENE 2. INT. BAKERY, AFTERNOON

Flip props the phone beside the oven. Plume places a ring light around it and asks Anya to push the shelf on cue. She does. The cake falls sideways and breaks. Plume says the reaction lacked authenticity. Anya starts collecting the pieces for cake pops.

Nori arrives to pick up a birthday cake. She does not recognize Anya from the viral clip; she wants the cake to look like her grandmother's house. Plume turns the phone toward Nori and asks her to gasp. Nori looks at him, then asks where the receipt is.

NORI: I came to buy dessert, not audition.

The ring light blinds Flip. He backs into the timer, which rings. Plume calls the jump a second viral moment. Anya pulls a tray of cookies from the oven before they burn. The camera records only Plume's reflection in the ring light, making him look like a glowing peacock egg.

SCENE 3. INT. BAKERY, LATER

Flip edits two versions. The first repeats the cake collapse with a cymbal crash. The second shows Anya decorating a stable lighthouse cake and explaining how she supports its base. Plume predicts the first will reach more viewers. Anya asks which one might help someone make a cake.

FLIP: The first gets clicks. The second gets questions.

A viewer comments asking how to stop a tall cake from tipping. Anya records a short answer using cardboard supports. Plume tries to insert a cliffhanger before the explanation. Flip refuses and lets her finish the demonstration. The clip draws fewer instant views, but three local bakers ask for the support measurements.

Plume proposes a daily schedule: one collapse, one gasp, one sponsor mention. Anya says she has orders to fill. He asks if she can at least tease tomorrow's cake. She lifts the lid on a bowl; it contains flour. Plume calls it a cliffhanger. Nori asks for a cookie.

SCENE 4. EXT. HARBOR FESTIVAL, EVENING

The lighthouse cake sits on a sturdy table. Wind catches the festival banner. Anya and Nori secure the table legs with sandbags before the cake moves. Flip records from a wide angle, showing the customers helping. Plume asks for a slow-motion retake. The wind dies. No one recreates it.

The host announces the cake is ready to serve. Anya cuts it. A child reaches for the frosting lighthouse, and Nori points out the small paper support that should be removed first. Anya hands the child a slice with the support safely set aside. Plume whispers that safety instructions are poor content. The line of customers grows.

SCENE 5. INT. BAKERY, NEXT MORNING

Anya declines the sponsorship contract, which requires her to stage three mishaps a week. Flip proposes posting one practical video every Friday. They agree to film the real work, with customers' permission. Plume takes his ring light and asks if he can keep the apron. Anya says yes, if he stops calling the oven a content partner.

Nori returns to order another cake. Flip films her describing the harbor design, then asks if she wants to review it. She says she'll review it after tasting it. Plume waits for a dramatic verdict. Nori takes a bite, nods, and asks for two more slices to take home.

FINAL IMAGE: The next lighthouse cake stands perfectly straight. The view counter ticks upward one patient click at a time while the bakery door opens and customers enter without looking at the phone.
