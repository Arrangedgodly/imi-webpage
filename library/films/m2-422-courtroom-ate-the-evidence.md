# The Courtroom Ate the Evidence

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: legal thrillers that rely on a last-minute confession, dramatic exhibits and surprise procedural reversals*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A monkey lawyer plans a sweeping courtroom reveal, but the snack cart keeps taking bites out of the evidence before the judge can see it.

Cast: Judge Penda, an elderly macaque; advocate Rumi, a macaque; defendant Bolo, a capuchin; clerk Tessa, a lemur; witness Omi, a gibbon; Bailiff Jax, a baboon.

SCENE 1 - INT. SMALL COURTROOM - MORNING

Rumi opens a briefcase on the counsel table. Inside sits a long paper trail, a sealed jar and a plate of crackers. Bolo is accused of removing the final cracker from the community pantry.

RUMI: Your honor, the evidence will show the cracker was removed at precisely 8:03.

JUDGE PENDA: The clock says 8:17.

RUMI: The clock in the pantry.

CLERK TESSA: Which clock?

RUMI: The one in my opening statement.

Bolo leans toward the plate.

JAX: Defendant, please keep your hands visible.

BOLO: I was reaching for my sleeve.

JAX: Your sleeve has crumbs.

RUMI: Those are not the cracker.

Judge Penda looks over her glasses.

JUDGE PENDA: We have not established that there was a cracker.

Rumi holds up a photograph of an empty plate.

RUMI: Exhibit A. The plate after the alleged removal.

TESSA: The photograph is dated tomorrow.

RUMI: That is the printer setting. The picture is from today.

SCENE 2 - INT. COURTROOM - LATER

Omi takes the witness seat. Rumi places a sealed jar of crumbs on the evidence table.

RUMI: Witness, what did you see?

OMI: I saw Bolo enter the pantry, pick up the jar, and leave.

BOLO: It was my jar. I brought it for the meeting.

RUMI: You admit taking the evidence.

BOLO: I took my jar home last week.

JUDGE PENDA: Is that the jar?

OMI: It looks similar.

RUMI: The crumbs will identify it. Bailiff, open the seal.

JAX: The seal is already open.

RUMI: That proves someone tampered with it.

TESSA: The snack cart attendant signed it open when she served the jury.

RUMI: There is no jury.

TESSA: I meant the gallery.

Judge Penda asks for the pantry ledger. Tessa brings a large binder. The paper trail slides from Rumi's briefcase and covers the floor.

JUDGE PENDA: Clerk, please put the pages in order.

RUMI: Your honor, the disorder itself reflects the defendant's deception.

JUDGE PENDA: The disorder reflects a briefcase with a broken clasp.

Bolo raises his hand.

BOLO: I can tell you about the cracker.

RUMI: The confession! I knew it.

BOLO: I baked a tray for the pantry. The last cracker was placed in a tin for the night shift.

OMI: I thought the tin was empty.

SCENE 3 - INT. COURTROOM - CONTINUOUS

Rumi tries to stop him, then notices the snack cart rolling through the open door. The cart's top tray is missing a cracker. Rumi points to it.

RUMI: The stolen item has returned to the scene!

JAX: That's the court snack cart.

TESSA: The clerk cart is the one with the blue handle. This is orange.

The snack attendant pushes in, steering around the pages.

ATTENDANT: I need the empty plate back. The tray is for the afternoon hearing.

JUDGE PENDA: There was a cracker on that plate?

ATTENDANT: I set one aside for the witness. The rest are still in the tin.

Omi looks at the evidence jar. He recognizes the label.

OMI: That is my flour jar. I brought it for the baking demonstration.

RUMI: Then where is the pantry ledger?

TESSA: In the binder, under yesterday's minutes.

She finds the ledger and reads aloud.

TESSA: "Tray of twelve crackers delivered at eight. Eleven placed in pantry tin. One offered to witness Omi during tasting."

OMI: I ate it. I thought the tasting was finished.

Bolo: I brought the tray. I did not take anything from the pantry.

Rumi steps toward the witness box.

RUMI: You ate the crucial evidence?

JUDGE PENDA: He ate a cracker. It was a snack, not evidence.

SCENE 4 - INT. COURTROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The judge asks the clerk to read the full ledger, not Rumi's selection. It shows the pantry tin was restocked that morning. Bolo had delivered the crackers to the pantry and signed the receipt.

JUDGE PENDA: The charge is dismissed. Mr. Omi, please stop sampling exhibits before they are admitted.

OMI: I thought the courtroom had refreshments.

JAX: Only after the hearing.

Rumi gathers the paper trail. The broken briefcase clasp spills it again.

RUMI: I still have a closing argument prepared.

JUDGE PENDA: Use it to explain why you brought crackers as evidence.

RUMI: They were a visual aid.

JUDGE PENDA: They are now lunch.

SCENE 5 - INT. COURTROOM - AFTERNOON

Tessa returns with a repaired clasp. Bolo distributes a fresh tray to the night shift. Rumi hands Omi a sealed bag marked NOT EVIDENCE.

OMI: I will wait until the break.

JAX: That is the first sound ruling today.

Judge Penda places the pantry ledger on top of the closed briefcase. Rumi starts to rise for one last dramatic speech, but the snack cart squeaks across the threshold.

JUDGE PENDA: Court is adjourned. The cart may proceed.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty exhibit stand holds a single paper plate while the real crackers roll safely toward the pantry.
