# The Last Dumpling in the Competition

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: cooking contests that frame a shared meal as a ruthless elimination*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
JUDGE NARI, a lemur
CHEF PIP, a capuchin
CHEF VEX, an orangutan
HOST MARA, a macaque
TIMER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN - DAY

Two chefs face separate counters. A single dumpling rests under a glass dome between them.

MARA: Welcome to the final round. Each chef will prepare a plate using the same filling.

VEX: And the last dumpling is the prize.

NARI: The last dumpling is for tasting. We have enough uncooked filling for both plates.

PIP: I thought we were making a dozen each.

ODO: The timer card says eight minutes. The filling is already cooked and cooled.

MARA: Start with clean hands and separate boards.

VEX: I have brought my signature steam basket.

PIP: That basket has leaves from the herb bed stuck to its base.

VEX: Those are flavor notes.

NARI: Remove them before the food goes in. The tasting plate must contain only ingredients from the recipe.

SCENE 2. INT. KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Pip folds dumplings at a steady pace. Vex shapes one large dumpling and tries to hide the extra filling inside.

ODO: Six minutes remain.

VEX: The judges need a spectacle.

PIP: They need dumplings that close. Your seam is open.

Vex squeezes the dough. Filling escapes onto the counter.

MARA: Stop. Keep the filling on the board. The counter is clean, but it cannot go back into the bowl.

VEX: The dumpling is bleeding.

NARI: It is leaking. We can still score your other pieces.

Pip wipes the board with a clean cloth and folds a smaller dumpling. Vex attempts a second oversized fold. His dough sticks to the sleeve of his costume.

PIP: Roll your sleeve up before you touch another wrapper.

VEX: I will not alter the chef's silhouette.

MARA: The sleeve is dragging through the flour. Roll it up or step away from the station.

Vex rolls it up. The oversized dumpling drops onto his shoe.

SCENE 2A. INT. KITCHEN - CONTINUOUS

Nari inspects the labels on the bowls. Vex's station has a bowl marked SESAME, but neither chef used sesame.

NARI: I need to ask about the ingredient list. Is the label left over from another class?

MARA: Yes. I will remove it before serving. No one should taste an ingredient they have not agreed to.

PIP: The dumplings contain beans, onion, and salt. Mine have a little herb from the approved bowl.

VEX: Mine contain the same filling, plus an architectural wrapper.

NARI: Your wrapper has split. I can judge the intact dumpling, but not the filling that touched the counter.

VEX: You are eliminating half my plate.

NARI: I am applying the same rule to both plates. Pip's split wrapper also goes to the staff pan.

Pip checks her tray and finds one open seam. She sets it aside without being asked.

PIP: That one won't go to the tasting table.

MARA: Good. We have enough intact pieces for a fair comparison. The leaking filling is separated, and the counter has been cleaned.

ODO: Four minutes remain. Steamer temperature is stable.

VEX: I can make a new dumpling from the leftover dough.

NARI: Only if there is enough time to cook it fully. Odo will give the same timer warning to both stations.

Odo starts the same four-minute timer for each chef. Pip folds a smaller wrapper. Vex folds one carefully, using a measured spoon of filling.

SCENE 3. INT. KITCHEN - MINUTES LATER

Odo opens the steamer. Pip places three dumplings on the tray. Vex has one intact piece and a patched one.

NARI: The prize is not for quantity. We will taste texture, seasoning, and whether the dumpling holds together.

VEX: My patched dumpling is a bold architectural choice.

PIP: It's still open on one side.

MARA: We can cook the intact one. The leaking filling goes into the staff soup, not the judging plate.

Mara slides the spill pan to the kitchen sink.

SCENE 4. INT. TASTING TABLE - LATER

Nari tastes Pip's dumpling, then Vex's. She sets down her fork.

NARI: Pip's wrapper is even and the filling is warm. Vex's is tasty, but the seam opened.

VEX: Who wins the last dumpling?

NARI: Nobody. We cut it in half and share it, because the point of this round was to taste both versions.

Mara divides the final dumpling. Pip gives Vex the larger half.

VEX: I accept this victory.

PIP: It's a shared sample.

TAG: Odo rings the timer. It reads zero. Vex looks at his empty plate, then reaches toward the staff soup. Mara covers the pot.
