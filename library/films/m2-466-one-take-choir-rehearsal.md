# The One-Take Choir Rehearsal

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: one-take musical films that treat uninterrupted performance as more important than clear entrances and lyrics*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A monkey choir records a one-take music video in a school hallway, but each uninterrupted pass includes a new interruption from the building's ordinary schedule.

Cast: Conductor Pavi, a gibbon; soloist Luma, a macaque; camera operator Kesh, a capuchin; stage manager Oru, a lemur; school custodian Bena, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY - MORNING

Kesh frames the choir at one end of the hall. Pavi raises his baton. A poster reads ONE TAKE ONLY. Bena pushes a cart of folded chairs through the frame.

PAVI: We begin at the trophy case, glide past the lockers, and end at the gym doors.

LUMA: The lyric says we end at the library.

PAVI: The hallway is more dramatic.

ORU: The library door is at the far end. The gym is on the left.

Pavi changes the final line on the lyric sheet. The choir takes its places.

SCENE 1A - INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY - MOMENTS LATER

Oru places tape marks on the floor. Kesh rehearses the backward camera walk. Pavi starts the music cue, but the track begins with the final chord. The choir turns to face the trophy case.

PAVI: That was the wrong cue.

KESH: The player is set to shuffle.

Luma takes the device and disables shuffle. The opening verse now plays first. A teacher asks them to leave the center aisle clear.

ORU: The camera can follow the lockers along the wall.

PAVI: We need the whole hallway for scale.

BENA: The chairs have to pass through here before the assembly.

Pavi moves the end mark toward the library door. Kesh measures the route with a tape, then finds the camera cable is too short.

KESH: The one take will end at the power outlet.

LUMA: Or we can use the battery camera.

Kesh swaps in a smaller battery camera. Pavi asks for one more full rehearsal, but the class bell rings. Students enter the corridor, each carrying a book, a lunch bag or a rolled-up art project. The choir shifts against the wall to make room.

SCENE 2 - INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY - FIRST TAKE

The music begins. Kesh walks backward with the camera. The choir sings the first verse. A bell rings for class change. Students flood the hall, crossing between camera and singers.

PAVI: Keep singing. It is still one take.

LUMA: The lyric is about quiet mornings.

PAVI: Sing above the bell.

The choir continues. A student stops to tie a shoe in front of the lens. Kesh steps around her and bumps the trophy case. The camera remains on.

SCENE 3 - INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

The choir reaches the chorus. The custodian's chair cart squeaks in rhythm. Bena stops it, checks a wheel and continues pushing. The camera follows the choir to the lockers. Oru signals the gym doors are occupied by a delivery cart.

ORU: The gym entrance is blocked.

PAVI: We can finish at the library.

LUMA: That was the original lyric.

Kesh pans toward the library. A librarian silently turns a sign to QUIET PLEASE. The choir lowers its volume but keeps singing. The camera operator's shoe squeaks on the polished floor.

SCENE 4 - INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY - LATER

The take reaches its final verse. A student opens a locker, and a stack of music folders falls. Luma catches them while continuing the melody. Pavi points to the end mark by the library door. The choir finishes the line and holds its chord.

KESH: Cut.

PAVI: We have a continuous take.

LUMA: We have a bell, a chair cart, a shoe squeak and a folder rescue.

BENA: The chair cart wheel is fixed now.

SCENE 5 - INT. EDITING ROOM - AFTERNOON

Kesh plays the footage. The image shakes when the choir passes the trophy case. A student ties her shoe, the custodian fixes the cart, and Luma catches the folders. The final chord ends before the camera turns off.

PAVI: We should do it again without interruptions.

LUMA: Then it will not be the one take we filmed.

ORU: We can keep the hallway sounds and credit the school schedule.

Kesh shows the choir the title card. It reads ONE TAKE, NO RETAKES.

PAVI: Change it to ONE TAKE, MANY PEOPLE.

Bena rolls the repaired chair cart past the editing-room door. It no longer squeaks. The choir listens for a moment, then laughs at the quiet.

FINAL IMAGE: The final frame holds on the library sign, the choir's open music folders and the chair cart parked silently beside the wall. The hallway stays clear.
