# The Case of the Missing Bell

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: true-crime documentaries, ominous reenactments, and experts who overstate ordinary evidence*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A documentary crew investigates the disappearance of a town bell, treating a repair job and a noisy squirrel as a sinister conspiracy.

Cast: Della, a macaque documentarian; Finch, a squirrel who runs the repair shop; Officer Lunt, a rhino constable; Pavi, a lemur bell ringer; and Mayor Moss, a tortoise who wants the bell back before the parade.

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN SQUARE, DAY

A bell rope swings above an empty wooden frame. Della stands before it while a camera operator films from a dramatic low angle.

DELLA: At 9:02, the bell vanished. The town would never sound the same.

PAVI: It was taken down for repairs.

Della turns to the lens and pauses. Pavi points to a notice on the frame: BELL AT FINCH'S REPAIR SHOP. The documentary crew adds a black-and-white filter anyway. Mayor Moss arrives carrying a parade schedule and asks whether the bell will be ready by Saturday.

MOSS: We need it for the opening.

DELLA: The case is still unfolding.

MOSS: It's in a shop.

SCENE 2. INT. DOCUMENTARY STUDIO, AFTERNOON

Della interviews Officer Lunt against a dark background. A single lamp lights his horn. She places a photograph of the empty bell frame between them.

DELLA: What did the silence tell you?

LUNT: That the bell wasn't there.

DELLA: Could the silence have been planned?

LUNT: By the repair shop, I suppose.

A producer slides Della a card reading SAY IT WAS A COVER-UP. She ignores it and asks for the repair receipt. Lunt produces it: tighten clapper, replace frayed rope. The receipt includes a drawing of a squirrel. Della zooms in until the drawing fills the screen.

SCENE 3. INT. FINCH'S REPAIR SHOP, EVENING

Finch works on the bell. Della enters with a camera crew. He covers the bell with a cloth, not to hide it, but to keep sawdust out. The camera operator films the cloth lifting in slow motion.

DELLA: Why conceal the object?

FINCH: Because you brought a camera into a workshop.

Pavi confirms the bell was removed with the mayor's permission. Della asks if they coordinated their stories. Pavi says they coordinated a repair. Finch demonstrates the cracked rope. It snaps when he gives it a light tug, sending the clapper rolling into a bucket.

DELLA: A second disappearance.

FINCH: It rolled four feet.

SCENE 4. INT. REPAIR SHOP, NIGHT

Della asks Finch to stand beside the bell for a reconstruction. He refuses to wear the hooded cloak because he is the repairer, not the suspect. Pavi holds up the maintenance log so the camera can read it. The producer asks him to angle it away from the lens.

PAVI: The date is the whole point.

DELLA: A date is not very ominous.

PAVI: Then use a calendar.

The crew stages a reenactment. An actor in a hooded cloak tiptoes toward the bell, then lifts the cloth. Finch stops the scene because the actor is about to trip over a wrench. Della asks for more menace. Finch hands the actor a broom and asks him to sweep the floor.

Meanwhile, a real squirrel rummages in a box of spare bolts. The camera captures its paws, then cuts to Della's grave expression.

DELLA: Someone was here after hours.

FINCH: That squirrel comes every day.

The squirrel escapes with a washer. Finch retrieves it by offering a nut. Della asks if the nut is evidence. Finch says it is a snack. Lunt arrives with the town's maintenance log, which lists the bell repair and a delivery of rope. Della compares the timestamps; they match.

SCENE 5. EXT. TOWN SQUARE, MORNING

Finch returns the repaired bell. Pavi tests the clapper. It rings cleanly. Della films the moment without music. The crew asks her to narrate a final twist. She says the bell was missing because it was being fixed. The mayor asks whether the documentary can finish before the parade.

DELLA: There is no conspiracy.

MOSS: Could you say that with the bell behind you?

Della tries. The bell rings loudly over her sentence. She lets the sound remain in the edit. Finch gives the squirrel its washer back, and the squirrel immediately drops it into the bell frame.

Lunt signs the work order in full view of the camera. Della asks whether he fears being implicated. He says he fears the parade starting late.

FINAL IMAGE: The bell rings once more. Della looks at the camera, ready to declare a new case, but Pavi points to the squirrel. Della turns off the recording before the credits can accuse it.
