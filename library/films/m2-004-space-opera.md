# The Nebula's Lost Lunch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: Space opera sagas, royal bloodlines, prophecy exposition, and oversized space battles*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A young langur pilot discovers she is heir to a cosmic throne, then spends the entire interstellar crisis trying to return a lost lunch tin to its owner.

Cast: Zella, a langur courier who hates ceremonial titles; Admiral Crump, a gorilla with a majestic cape that catches in doors; Prince Orlo, a lemur royal with a map of the stars; Aunt Vela, a capuchin mechanic; and the Imperial Chancellor, a cockatoo who speaks only in declarations.

SCENE 1. EXT. ORBITAL MARKET, DAY

A courier ship docks beside a space market shaped like a giant ring. Zella carries parcels through the busy corridor. She finds a dented lunch tin on her cart. Its label reads "Orlo, Starship Four." The ship has already left.

A trumpet fanfare echoes through the market. Admiral Crump descends from a polished shuttle, cape flowing. The cape catches on a door handle. He keeps walking until the door opens and follows him down the corridor.

CRUMP: The lost heir has been found!

ZELLA: I found a lunch tin.

CRUMP: The prophecy speaks through humble objects.

ZELLA: The label speaks through ink.

Crump takes the tin. It rattles. Inside is a half-eaten sandwich and a star map. He declares the map proves Zella is the rightful heir to the Nebula Throne.

SCENE 2. INT. STARSHIP, IN FLIGHT

Zella refuses the title and asks to be dropped at the next port. Prince Orlo arrives, furious that someone has stolen his lunch. He recognizes the tin and the map. The map is actually a route chart to his home station, covered in mustard.

ORLO: I left it on your cart.

ZELLA: You left it on my cart in orbit?

ORLO: I was hungry.

Admiral Crump insists the mustard stains form a constellation. Aunt Vela checks the navigation system and confirms they need to reach the royal station before the Chancellor's fleet arrives. Orlo says the Chancellor will claim the throne unless the heir is presented with the ancient seal.

Zella asks if she can deliver the lunch first. Orlo admits the sandwich is still inside. Crump says destiny must wait until after the battle.

SCENE 3. INT. STARSHIP BRIDGE, NIGHT

The Chancellor appears on the viewscreen, wearing a crown too large for his head. He announces a blockade in a voice that makes the speakers vibrate.

CHANCELLOR: The throne shall be mine!

ZELLA: It is a seat.

CHANCELLOR: It is a symbol!

ORLO: It's uncomfortable.

The fleet surrounds them. Crump orders a dramatic maneuver. Aunt Vela asks what kind. He says "the legendary one." She asks for the actual controls. He points to a lever marked EMERGENCY TURN. Vela pulls it. The ship rotates slowly, revealing a docking bay on the other side. They slip behind a cargo convoy while Crump's cape gets trapped under a chair.

SCENE 4. EXT. ROYAL STATION, APPROACH

The starship approaches the station. The Chancellor's fleet blocks the main entrance. Zella notices the lunch tin's label includes the station's delivery code. She scans it at a service hatch. The hatch opens.

ORLO: You found the secret entrance.

ZELLA: I read the label.

They dock in a maintenance bay. Crump tries to stride out majestically, but the hatch is too low. He ducks. The Chancellor enters the station hall with guards. Zella carries the lunch tin toward him.

SCENE 5. INT. THRONE HALL, CONTINUOUS

The Chancellor demands that Zella prove her royal blood. She places the lunch tin on the throne. The ancient seal sticks to the mustard on the map. The Chancellor tries to lift it; the tin opens and the sandwich slides onto his robe.

ZELLA: The throne is yours.

CHANCELLOR: It is not about the throne.

ORLO: Then why are you sitting in it?

Crump steps forward to give a speech. His cape catches on the throne. The Chancellor's guards help untangle him. The room's attendants begin laughing. The Chancellor removes the sandwich from his robe and recognizes his own handwriting on the lunch tin. He had borrowed it from Orlo during a committee meeting.

The throne dispute collapses into an argument about who returned whose lunch. Orlo offers to share the sandwich. The Chancellor accepts, then admits the mustard map was his idea. He wanted a dramatic route to the station and thought nobody would notice the condiment.

SCENE 6. INT. ROYAL STATION, LATER

The Chancellor lifts the blockade. Orlo keeps the throne, mostly because nobody else wants to sit in it. Zella declines the title of heir and returns to her courier route. Crump asks if she would accept a ceremonial medal. She accepts only if it can open parcels.

FINAL IMAGE: Zella's ship leaves the station. In the background, the Chancellor, Orlo, and Crump share the recovered sandwich beneath a banner reading PEACE IN OUR TIME. The banner is hung backward. Aunt Vela reaches out from the ship and turns it around as the door closes.
