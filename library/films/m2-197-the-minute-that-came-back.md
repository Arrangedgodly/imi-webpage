# The Minute That Came Back

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-travel adventures, paradox rules, urgent countdowns, and heroes who repeatedly return to fix one small mistake*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
TIK, a capuchin clockmaker
AVA, a ring-tailed lemur apprentice
THE COUNCIL, macaques who regulate town time

SCENE 1. INT. CLOCK TOWER - MIDNIGHT

The town clock strikes thirteen. Every streetlight turns green at once. Tik opens a brass cabinet and reveals a tiny lever labeled "ONE MINUTE BACK."

AVA: You built a time machine?

TIK: I repaired a clock that can repeat its last minute.

AVA: That sounds like a time machine.

TIK: It only rewinds the tower clock. The rest of the town follows its chime.

A bell rings. The clock jumps back one minute. The council members reenter the room, repeating their earlier conversation word for word.

COUNCILOR: We need the clock to strike twelve at midnight.

TIK: I know. I heard you the first time.

SCENE 2. INT. CLOCK TOWER - CONTINUOUS

Ava spots the loose gear that caused the extra strike. Tik reaches for it. The clock chimes again, and the minute resets. The council reenters, repeating its speech.

AVA: If we leave the gear, we repeat the minute.

TIK: If we remove it during the chime, the clock loses the hour hand.

AVA: We can wait for the chime to finish.

TIK: We have one minute.

The clock starts ticking. Tik begins an elaborate sequence involving gloves, tweezers, and a lever. Ava watches the gear rotate. She reaches into the mechanism and blocks it with a wooden wedge.

SCENE 2A. INT. CLOCK TOWER - THE SAME MINUTE AGAIN

Tik flips the rewind lever a second time to test whether the council heard the warning. The bell rings thirteen again. The councilors walk back through the door and repeat their speech, but this time one of them is holding a cup of tea.

AVA: The tea returned too.

TIK: The whole town resets to the previous chime.

The cup sloshes as the councilor repeats his entrance. Ava catches it before it spills. The councilor repeats the same line, pauses, and sneezes at the same word.

AVA: We could use this minute to learn the pattern.

TIK: Or we could stop the clock from repeating it.

Ava watches the gears. The loose gear slips only when the bell strikes thirteen. She marks the position with chalk. Tik uses a wooden wedge to hold the other gears steady. On the next chime, they catch the bad gear before it rolls.

SCENE 3. INT. CLOCK TOWER - LATER

The clock stops. The hour hand remains. Tik pulls the gear free. The tower is quiet. Down in the square, the streetlights return to normal one by one.

AVA: We fixed it.

TIK: We should test the rewind once more.

AVA: Why?

TIK: To make sure it still works.

Ava flips the lever. The clock jumps back a minute. Tik drops the gear. It rolls toward the mechanism.

SCENE 4. INT. CLOCK TOWER - THE REPEATED MINUTE

The council reenters. The repeated speech begins. Ava steps in front of the door and recites the exact words before the councilor can speak.

AVA: We need the clock to strike twelve at midnight.

The councilor pauses. He has never heard anyone anticipate him. Tik catches the rolling gear with a shoe.

TIK: We have the missing gear.

AVA: And the clock has given us one more chance to install it correctly.

They fit the gear back in place. The clock strikes twelve. The streetlights hold steady. The Council applauds, then asks whether they can use the time device to repeat the meeting until everyone agrees.

TIK: It only repeats a minute. You would have to make the same speech forever.

SCENE 4A. INT. CLOCK TOWER - NIGHT

The Council members check their watches. One asks whether they have missed the hour. Ava points to the steady clock. It is one minute later than the repeated chime and exactly on schedule.

COUNCILOR: We heard the same speech twice.

AVA: You can shorten it next time.

The councilors laugh. Tik places the bad gear in a small box and writes "REPAIR, DO NOT REWIND." He locks the box. Ava checks that the glass cover is secure, then gives the key to the tower keeper.

TIK: No more experiments during the town meeting.

AVA: The next experiment can happen in the workshop.

SCENE 5. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - DAWN

The council votes to remove the rewind lever and use the clock as a clock. Tik and Ava carry the lever down the tower. They pass a bakery opening for the day.

AVA: What if we'd left the first mistake?

TIK: We would have heard the same speech again.

AVA: We already did.

TIK: Then we learned not to rewind the repair.

The clock chimes twelve, once, in the distance. The townspeople look up. No lights flash. No one repeats a sentence.

FINAL IMAGE: The lever rests in a locked glass case beside the clock's original gear. A small sign reads "DO NOT USE TO EXTEND MEETINGS." Below it, the bakery opens on time.
