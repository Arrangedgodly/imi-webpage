# The Clock Has Four Hands

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: escape-room thrillers, puzzle-chain escalation, and artificial countdown pressure*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A group of coworkers enters a clockmaker-themed escape room, where the puzzles follow the host's filing system and the only real emergency is their meeting in an hour.

Cast: Nori, a macaque puzzle solver; Dex, a capuchin who pulls every lever; June, a langur who reads instructions; Pat, a gorilla manager; and Felix, the escape-room host.

SCENE 1. INT. ESCAPE ROOM, AFTERNOON

The door closes. A timer starts at sixty minutes. A recorded voice says they are trapped in a clockmaker's shop.

DEX: We are making progress.

JUNE: You pulled a lever. The instructions say don't.

A drawer opens and releases confetti. Nori spots a clock with four hands. Pat asks whether it is a clue. June reads the plaque: DISPLAY CLOCK, DO NOT WIND. Dex reaches for the key. Nori catches his wrist.

SCENE 2. INT. CLOCKMAKER'S SHOP, CONTINUOUS

The host's voice returns with a warning that every wrong answer will cost a minute. Pat asks whether wrong questions count. Felix says no. Pat asks how long the room has been open. Felix pauses. The timer drops a minute.

PAT: That was a question.

JUNE: It was a useful one.

Nori studies the folders. Dex quietly reaches for another drawer, then asks June first. She points to a label that says DRAWERS ARE NOT LEVERS. He opens it normally; a paperclip falls out and lands in the fake key's slot. The lock clicks. Felix congratulates them over the speaker, although they have only opened a drawer.

They arrange filing folders by month. The dates use day-month-year order. Pat rearranges them to match the office calendar. The lock stays shut. June restores the original order. It clicks open, revealing a key and a note: CHECK THE DRAWER.

Dex opens every drawer in the room. One releases a spring that lands inside his sleeve. Another contains a label that says OPEN THE CABINET AFTER READING THIS. Pat asks why a label is hidden in a drawer. June says the game designer probably filed it there.

SCENE 3. INT. WORKBENCH ALCOVE, LATER

The cabinet has four locks. Nori sorts clues by minute, hour, second, and time zone. The last slip is reversed. June uses a mirror to read MONDAY. The combination opens the cabinet. Inside is a fake key and a work order for a loose clock hand.

DEX: The key is fake?

JUNE: The order is real.

Pat checks the timer. It has gained five minutes. Felix speaks through a hidden speaker and congratulates them on finding the bonus time. Nori asks if it was intentional. Felix says it might be. Pat asks whether their meeting can also gain five minutes. Felix does not answer.

SCENE 4. INT. ESCAPE ROOM, FINAL MINUTES

Felix hands them a clipboard through the door. It contains the room's reset checklist. June notices the listed tools match the shapes on the cabinet. Pat asks whether the checklist is another clue or the host's actual inventory. Felix says both, then realizes he has revealed the answer. The team sorts the tools by their labels and fits them into the cabinet.

A clock hand rolls under the door. June kneels to retrieve it. The timer begins counting backward. Pat calls for Felix. The host appears outside and says the clock is part of the game. Nori points to the work order; Felix admits the timer is broken.

NORI: Is this a puzzle?

FELIX: It's maintenance.

DEX: Can maintenance unlock the door?

Felix corrects the timer. It now shows seven minutes remaining. The team sorts the labeled tools into their slots. The final latch opens. Dex starts to pull it before June reads the sign. It says PUSH. He pushes. The door opens.

SCENE 5. INT. ESCAPE ROOM LOBBY, LATER

Felix offers them a coupon for another room. Pat asks for a refund for the backward time. Felix says the extra minutes were a bonus. June points out they lost time when the clock was repaired. Felix checks the terms and gives them a second coupon.

At the office, Pat's meeting begins. Dex pulls the cabinet lever. It opens to reveal the escape-room clock, which Felix accidentally packed with the team's coats. June brings it back the next day with the work order attached.

The next team arrives early and asks if the room is ready. Felix points at the maintenance plaque.

FINAL IMAGE: Felix sets the clock right side up. It still has four hands. He adds a new plaque: DISPLAY CLOCK, DO NOT WIND OR USE TO MEASURE GAME TIME.
