# The Sash That Changed Everything

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fashion makeover dramas, runway reveals, rival designers, and the belief that one sash can solve a whole wardrobe*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
ZOLA, a capuchin tailor
MIMI, a macaque runway model
COUNT PIPER, a gibbon fashion judge
DOT, a ring-tailed lemur seamstress

SCENE 1. INT. HOUSE OF FERN FASHION STUDIO - MORNING

Zola stands before a rack of unfinished coats. The annual Canopy Couture runway show is tonight. Mimi, a macaque model, practices a dramatic turn in shoes two sizes too large. Count Piper enters holding a gold sash.

PIPER: The winning designer will receive the Golden Hem.

ZOLA: What does it do?

PIPER: It is worn across the shoulder.

DOT: That's a sash.

PIPER: With a medal attached.

Mimi spins. One shoe flies into a sleeve and stays there. Zola retrieves it, then notices that the only completed outfit is a plain green coat with a repaired elbow.

SCENE 2. INT. FITTING ROOM - LATER

Zola pins the coat to Mimi. She wants to keep its practical pockets; Piper demands a dramatic silhouette. Dot suggests adding a sash. Piper says the sash belongs to the winner and orders it locked away.

MIMI: Can I walk in the coat?

ZOLA: Can you walk in those shoes?

MIMI: I can fall in them with conviction.

Dot fastens a temporary ribbon around the coat. Mimi steps onto a low platform, turns, and the ribbon catches on a nail. The coat swings behind her like a cape. Piper is delighted.

PIPER: At last, movement.

ZOLA: It is caught on a nail.

PIPER: That is how fashion begins.

SCENE 3. INT. WORKROOM - AFTERNOON

Zola and Dot sew a wide hem so the coat will not snag again. Mimi tests it by walking through the studio carrying fabric, pins, and a cup of tea. She can move freely. Piper returns with a competitor's gold-threaded cape and says the judges expect a transformation.

PIPER: The audience must gasp.

DOT: They will gasp if the model trips.

PIPER: That is not the gasp I mean.

Piper takes the ribbon off the coat and tries the gold sash instead. It is too short to tie, so he clips it to the hem. Mimi turns; the sash lifts the coat and lands across Piper's face. The other seamstresses continue stitching around him.

SCENE 4. INT. DRESSING ROOM - EVENING

The competitor's cape arrives with a split seam. Its designer is missing. Zola offers to mend it, but Piper says repairs look like weakness. Mimi quietly takes the cape and uses the coat's spare thread to close the tear from the inside.

MIMI: It can still move. The seam just needs support.

ZOLA: That's the whole job.

Piper sees the finished repair and assumes it is invisible design. He orders Mimi to wear the cape for the finale. She agrees only if she can wear the green coat underneath.

SCENE 5. INT. RUNWAY - NIGHT

A spotlight hits the runway. Models stride in elaborate coats. One has a chandelier on each shoulder and walks sideways to avoid the doors. Mimi enters in the green coat beneath the repaired cape. The audience applauds the pockets, which hold the program, a spool of thread, and Piper's misplaced judging pencil.

PIPER: We have never seen a pocket used in a finale.

ZOLA: We have never been asked.

Mimi turns. The cape seam holds. The sash slips off Piper's shoulder, travels down the runway and catches on the cape's repaired hem. The garments move together in a graceful turn. The audience gasps, then cheers.

SCENE 6. INT. RUNWAY - LATER

Piper awards the Golden Hem to Zola, but Zola hands it to Dot, who did the stitching. Dot pins it to the studio's curtain so it will stop dragging across the floor. Mimi keeps the green coat and offers the cape back to its designer, who has just arrived with a basket of loose buttons.

SCENE 7. INT. FASHION STUDIO - AFTER MIDNIGHT

Ciel arrives to collect the cape. Mimi shows the repaired seam and the hidden release tab. Ciel tries the move once, but the tab opens the cape directly over Zola's head. Dot marks the release point with a small yellow stitch so the next wearer can find it without guessing.

CIEL: I borrowed this for a quick change, not a runway.

ZOLA: The runway borrowed it back.

Piper brings the Golden Hem from the curtain and tries to award it to the cape. Ciel declines; the cape already has a theater job. Mimi returns it folded, with a note suggesting that the costume crew add the pockets from Zola's coat.

FINAL IMAGE: Mimi walks out wearing the coat's pockets full of sewing tools as the gold sash hangs from the curtain, keeping its hem exactly six inches above the floor.
