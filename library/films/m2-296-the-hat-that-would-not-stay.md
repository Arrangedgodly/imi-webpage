# The Hat That Would Not Stay

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent-era slapstick comedies, intertitle misunderstandings, rapid chases, and physical gags built around one stubborn prop*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A capuchin messenger must deliver a ceremonial hat across a bustling town, but the hat keeps landing on the wrong primate and turning a simple errand into a silent-film chase.

Cast
- LEO, a capuchin messenger with quick hands and a tail that catches falling objects.
- INA, a gibbon tailor whose sleeves swallow her hands.
- VEX, a macaque mayor whose hat is too tall for doorways.
- PIP, a ring-tailed lemur street musician.
- THE HAT, a floppy ceremonial hat with a loose feather.

## Scene 1: The Commission

INT. TAILOR SHOP - MORNING

A title card reads: "THE TOWN'S NEW MAYOR MUST RECEIVE THE CEREMONIAL HAT BEFORE NOON."

Ina places the hat on a cushion. Leo salutes. The hat slides over the cushion and onto his head. He disappears beneath its brim.

INA: [TITLE CARD: "CARRY IT CAREFULLY."]

Leo nods, takes one step, and the hat lifts in a breeze from the door. It sails out of the shop.

## Scene 2: The Street

EXT. MARKET STREET - CONTINUOUS

The hat lands on Pip, who is playing a fiddle. Pip's bow gets caught under the brim. He plays a note that startles a flock of pigeons. Leo chases the hat. Vex steps from the town hall and mistakes Leo's flailing for a parade salute.

A title card reads: "THE MAYOR BELIEVES THE PARADE HAS BEGUN."

Vex joins the chase, marching behind Leo. Pip follows, still playing. The group forms an accidental procession through the market.

## Scene 3: The Bakery

The hat blows into the bakery and lands on a tray of cooling rolls. Leo reaches for it, but the baker's gibbon apprentice places it on a rolling rack. The rack begins moving downhill. Leo grabs the handle and rides behind it, ducking beneath doorways. Vex tries to keep up, but his tall hat knocks a hanging sign loose. The sign falls onto his head, making his hat shorter.

A title card reads: "THE MAYOR HAS LOST HIS HEIGHT."

The rack stops at a stack of flour sacks. The ceremonial hat is covered in flour, except for its feather.

Leo dusts it off with a broom. The feather catches in the bristles. He backs away, pulling the broom, and the hat travels across the room like a kite. A baker grabs the handle, but the hat lifts the broom from her hand and carries it through the open window.

Outside, a tailor's apprentice pins a clean cloth to a line. The hat lands on the cloth, which swings like a hammock. Leo climbs the fence; Vex uses the banner pole to reach across the street. The hat slides down the cloth and lands on the pole's tip. The apprentice raises the line, turning the pole into an accidental flag staff.

A title card reads: "THE TOWN HAS ACQUIRED A NEW FLAG, BUT IT IS STILL THE WRONG ONE."

Vex steadies the pole. Leo climbs it hand over hand, tail wrapped around the lower shaft, and plucks the hat from the top. The pole bends like a fishing rod and lowers him back to the street.

## Scene 4: The Fountain

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MIDDAY

Leo reaches the fountain. The hat floats on the water. He stretches his tail around the basin, but the hat keeps circling away. Pip's fiddle note causes ripples. Leo matches the rhythm, making the hat drift closer. Vex arrives with a fishing net, intending to rescue it. He catches the mayor's own hat instead.

A title card reads: "THE MAYOR HAS RESCUED HIMSELF."

Vex bows. The hat slips off his head and lands on the ceremonial hat, stacking them like a two-tier crown.

## Scene 5: The Chase Ends

Ina arrives with a clothespin and a ribbon. She attaches the ribbon inside the hat and ties it beneath the mayor's chin. Leo wipes off the flour. Pip stops playing. The hat stays in place for the first time.

VEX: [TITLE CARD: "I AM READY TO ACCEPT THE HAT."]

Leo hands it over. Vex steps under the town hall arch. The hat catches on the arch, and the mayor keeps walking, leaving the hat suspended overhead.

A title card reads: "THE TOWN HAS A NEW FLAG."

## Scene 6: The Ceremony

Ina lowers the hat with a pole. Vex removes his tall hat and receives the ceremonial one. He takes the oath beneath the arch. Leo delivers the feather to the tailor, and Pip plays one measured chord. The townsfolk applaud. No one chases anything.

A final title card reads: "THE MAYOR PROMISES TO KEEP HIS HAT ON."

Vex looks up at the arch. The ribbon hangs safely beneath the brim.

## Final Image

The ceremonial hat sits firmly on the mayor's head as he walks through the arch. A loose feather rises in the breeze, but the hat stays put.
