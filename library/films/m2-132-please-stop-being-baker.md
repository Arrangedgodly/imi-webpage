# Please Stop Being the Baker

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: method acting that mistakes prolonged character immersion for preparation and disrupts a production's practical work*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: An actor stays in character as a baker between takes until the actual bakery runs out of bread and he refuses to stop kneading the prop dough.

Cast: Odo, a gorilla actor; Kela, a lemur director; Mina, a macaque baker and consultant; and Puck, a capuchin assistant.

SCENE 1. INT. FILM BAKERY SET, MORNING

Odo wears an apron and flour-dusted sleeves. The prop counter holds round lumps of modeling clay painted tan. Kela explains that Odo's character is a baker who has just opened the shop. Mina, the real baker consulting on set, brings in a tray of fresh rolls.

KELA: Odo, the line is "Good morning. One loaf?"

ODO: I cannot say that before the oven has heated.

MINA: The oven is on.

ODO: My character doesn't know that.

Puck checks the call sheet. They are filming the greeting, not the baking. Odo asks for a starter culture and a flour scale. Mina says there is real dough in the kitchen. Odo disappears through the set door.

SCENE 2. INT. REAL BAKERY KITCHEN, LATER

Odo studies the dough while Mina prepares an order. Kela asks him to return to the set. Odo says he needs to understand how bread behaves. Mina hands him a dough scraper and asks him to portion the rolls.

ODO: The character would know this.

MINA: The character would wash his hands first.

Odo looks at his floury fingers. He washes them, then divides the dough. Puck follows with a camera, filming the work as research. Mina directs him to keep the lens away from the food. Odo puts each piece on a tray, but he makes them all identical. Mina asks him to leave room between them.

ODO: The recipe calls for twelve.

MINA: The oven needs space.

Odo rearranges the rolls. Kela calls from the set that the scene is ready. Odo says the oven has seven minutes left. Mina tells him the real order cannot wait for his character's timing. He finishes the tray and hands it to the baker. Mina checks the shapes and shows him one roll with a seam tucked underneath.

MINA: This one will open on top.

ODO: It looks tidy.

MINA: The oven does not grade tidiness.

Odo remakes it. Puck photographs the corrected roll and asks whether to use the picture in the movie. Mina says the customer wants the bread, not a production still. Odo places the tray by the oven and waits for the timer. The set crew calls him back. He asks Mina to finish the bake, then returns to the prop shop with flour on his shoes.

SCENE 3. INT. FILM BAKERY SET, AFTERNOON

Odo returns wearing a fresh apron. Kela begins the take. Odo delivers the greeting, then starts kneading the clay dough. It cracks under his hands. He asks for water. Puck brings a glass, and Odo pours it over the prop. The tan paint runs down the counter.

KELA: Cut. That is modeling clay.

ODO: Then the scene is lying.

Mina gives Odo a ball of actual dough for a close-up. Kela says the shot only shows hands, but Odo insists on shaping it correctly. He creates a neat loaf, then asks if he can bake it. Mina says the oven is not part of the set and the scene ends at the greeting.

ODO: How does the audience know I am a baker?

MINA: You are standing in a bakery holding bread.

Kela removes the clay and places the real loaf on the counter. Odo says the loaf belongs in the oven. Mina hands it to the crew to bake later.

SCENE 4. INT. FILM BAKERY SET, EVENING

Odo delivers the line and offers the loaf to an unseen customer. Kela calls cut. The take is finished. Odo begins wiping flour from the counter before Mina stops him; the set crew handles cleanup. Kela asks him to remain in character for a promotional photograph. Odo lifts the loaf and looks serious.

ODO: Is the customer still waiting?

KELA: The customer is a camera.

ODO: Then I should not make them wait.

He hands the loaf to Mina. Puck asks for a second take with a mysterious expression. Odo says the loaf has cooled and the shot is over.

Mina tells him the real bakery has closed. Odo asks if he can return in the morning to learn how to shape rolls. Mina says he can, if he is there before opening and wears a clean apron.

FINAL IMAGE: Odo removes the character's apron, hangs it on its hook, and washes his hands before touching the call sheet.
