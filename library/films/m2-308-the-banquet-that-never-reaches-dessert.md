# The Banquet That Never Reaches Dessert

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: lavish costume-drama banquets, table-setting close-ups, and scenes where the meal becomes more important than the plot*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
PRINCE PIP, a capuchin
AMBASSADOR VEX, a macaque
STEWARD LUMA, a lemur
CAMERA OPERATOR ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE DINING HALL - MORNING

A long table is set with candles, silver cups and an enormous covered dish. Prince Pip and Ambassador Vex sit at opposite ends. Director Nari waits for a close-up.

NARI: The dish arrives before the treaty is discussed.

PIP: Why?

NARI: The audience must see abundance.

VEX: I came to sign a treaty.

LUMA: The roast is not ready.

Nari asks the crew to film the silverware. Odo slides a camera along the table. A cup reflects the lights into his lens. He moves it an inch. Luma moves it back to match the place setting. Odo tries from the other end; the same glare fills the shot.

ODO: The cup has interrupted diplomacy.

VEX: The treaty is still here.

Pip opens the covered dish. It is empty. Luma checks the kitchen and says the roast is still cooking.

SCENE 2. INT. PALACE KITCHEN - LATER

Nari asks Luma to bring any food for the first shot. Luma presents a loaf of bread. Nari asks for more visible abundance. Luma puts the loaf on a silver platter. It looks small. Pip adds fruit. Vex adds the treaty scroll. The platter tips; the fruit rolls into the scroll's wax seal.

VEX: The kingdom has signed a pear.

PIP: It is not binding.

Luma wipes the seal. Odo films the recovery as a servant's dramatic gesture. Nari asks him to repeat it. Luma wipes the same spot, but the wax is gone. The second take shows her rubbing an empty scroll.

ODO: The evidence has been cleaned away.

Nari asks for a bowl of soup. It arrives steaming and fogs the camera lens. Odo clears the lens. Steam covers the actors again. Pip tries to speak through the cloud.

PIP: I accept the border.

VEX: I cannot see the border.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE DINING HALL - NOON

The roast arrives. It is too large for the covered dish and leans against the lid. Vex begins his treaty speech. Nari interrupts to get a shot of the knife. Luma sets it down; it is a prop sword, longer than the table. Odo adjusts his camera, and the sword blocks both ambassadors.

LUMA: That is for the ceremonial guard.

NARI: It makes the meal look important.

PIP: It makes the meal impossible.

They switch to small serving knives. Vex tries to cut the roast, but a hard crust sends his piece sliding down the table. It stops at Pip's scroll. Pip signs the paper to keep it from moving.

VEX: You have accepted the treaty?

PIP: I have trapped the roast.

Luma serves portions. The actors finally discuss the river boundary. Nari asks them to pause after each sentence so the camera can capture the gravy. Pip and Vex exchange the same three lines while the dish is moved into better light. On the fourth take, the gravy has cooled and formed a skin.

SCENE 4. INT. PALACE DINING HALL - AFTERNOON

Nari reviews the footage. Every take centers the food; none shows the treaty clearly. Odo proposes filming the scroll at the far end of the table while the meal remains in soft focus. Nari agrees. Luma clears only the center space. Pip and Vex compare maps, draw a boundary, and sign. They speak plainly while Odo holds the frame steady.

PIP: The north bank stays with us.

VEX: The south ferry remains open to both.

They sign. Luma serves the roast after the agreement. It has cooled, but remains edible. Nari keeps the shot. She asks for a dessert close-up. Luma presents a small tart.

ODO: The treaty is signed. The meal can end.

Nari asks if the tart could symbolize peace. Pip takes a bite before she can answer. Vex cuts his half with the ceremonial sword; the tart rolls off the plate and lands beside the treaty. Luma moves the silver cup to catch it. Odo captures the small rescue in a wide shot, with both signatures still visible.

SCENE 5. INT. PALACE DINING HALL - EVENING

The crew packs the silver and folds the map. Nari's final cut shows the signatures, the river boundary and one brief image of the table. Vex tastes the tart. Pip offers him the remaining half. Neither says anything profound.

FINAL IMAGE: The signed treaty rests beneath the silver platter, while one tart crumb sits on the border line.
