# The Forbidden Recipe Was a Salad

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: culinary thrillers, secret family recipes, rival kitchens, and chefs who treat dinner preparation like an international conspiracy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
CHEF ODO, an orangutan
INVESTIGATOR MIRI, a lemur
SOUS-CHEF PIP, a capuchin
CRITIC VEX, a macaque
GRANDMA NARI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. RESTAURANT KITCHEN - NIGHT

A locked tin sits on the counter. Inside, according to family legend, is Grandma Nari's forbidden recipe. Chef Odo wears gloves to open it. Miri watches with a notebook. Pip turns down the stove, which has been left on beneath an empty pot.

ODO: The recipe could change everything.

NARI: It is a salad dressing.

The tin opens with a click. A folded card falls out and slides under the refrigerator.

SCENE 2. INT. DINING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Critic Vex arrives early and asks for the house specialty. Odo serves a sample of roasted roots, but the ingredients are listed on the menu. Vex says the dish needs a mystery. Odo adds a dramatic drizzle from a bottle marked vinegar. Miri asks why the bottle was hidden in the pantry. Odo says the label had faded.

Miri questions Nari about the locked tin. Nari recalls that she wrote the recipe on a card during a storm, then put it away when the restaurant ceiling leaked. The card may have been moved during a repair. Odo opens the pantry, finds a second tin, and assumes it is a decoy. It contains tea bags. Pip opens the drawer beneath it and finds three spoons arranged like an arrow. Vex calls the arrow a clue; Nari says the spoons were drying. Nari says she sealed it after the family misplaced the recipe card, then forgot where she put the key. Pip finds three keys in the drawer; one opens a cupboard, one opens a toolbox, and the third is a bottle opener. They use it to open a jar of olives.

SCENE 3. INT. KITCHEN - LATER

Odo recruits the staff to search for the card. Pip shines a flashlight under the refrigerator. He finds a note, a spoon, and a small toy carrot. The card remains missing. Miri examines the kitchen inventory and sees that the refrigerator was moved last month. A grease mark follows the floor from its old position. Pip shines a light beneath the appliance and finds the card's corner stuck to the underside. Nari uses a dolly to lift the refrigerator just enough for him to retrieve it with a spatula. The recipe says: cucumber, herbs, vinegar, oil, and salt. Its secret instruction is to let the salad sit for ten minutes before serving.

Vex approaches the pass window for a second tasting. Odo serves the salad after the timer rings. Vex recognizes the vinegar from the earlier sample and takes another bite.

SCENE 3A. INT. KITCHEN - EVENING

The restaurant staff search beneath the counters. Miri finds a grease mark along the floor where the refrigerator was moved. She asks Odo why it was moved. He explains that the compressor stopped cooling, and the staff pulled it out to inspect the plug. The recipe card must have slid underneath during that repair. Pip checks the menu and discovers the salad is already being advertised as "Grandma's Forbidden Bowl." Nari crosses out forbidden and writes cucumber salad. Vex says that is less mysterious but more accurate. He changes the menu card before the dinner guests arrive, then sets it beside the actual bowl.

SCENE 4. INT. KITCHEN - MIDNIGHT

Odo prepares the salad while Miri reads the card aloud. Pip measures the dressing. Vex tries to taste it before the ten minutes are up. Nari blocks his spoon with a bread basket. The kitchen timer rings, but it has been set for one minute, not ten. Miri resets it, and the staff wait.

The salad rests. Its flavors blend. Odo serves it to Vex, who takes a bite and pauses. The crew expects a dramatic verdict. He asks for another forkful. He says the recipe is good, but the family's legend made it sound like a weapon.

VEX: This could change dinner.

NARI: It could improve it.

SCENE 5. INT. DINING ROOM - NEXT DAY

The salad appears on the menu with Nari's name and a note that it must rest before serving. Odo keeps the card in a recipe box, not a vault. Miri closes her investigation notebook. Pip labels the bottle of vinegar clearly. Vex writes his review and asks if the restaurant has a second secret.

NARI: The lentils are in the pot.

FINAL IMAGE: A bowl of salad waits beside the kitchen timer as the second hand circles once more before lunch service.
