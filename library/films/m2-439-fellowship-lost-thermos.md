# The Fellowship of the Lost Thermos

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests that require a chosen hero, ominous prophecy and dangerous trials to recover an ordinary object*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A fellowship crosses a park to return a missing thermos, guided by a prophecy that turns out to be a note from the picnic organizer.

Cast: Chosen monkey Luma, a macaque; ranger Ido, a gibbon; bard Perri, a lemur; keeper Osa, an orangutan; picnic host Venn, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. PARK GATE - MORNING

Luma finds a brass thermos by a stone marker. A note tied to it reads: WHEN THE SUN REACHES THE OLD OAK, RETURN THE VESSEL TO ITS KEEPER.

LUMA: The prophecy has found me.

IDO: It is a lost-property note.

PERRI: It uses the word vessel. That is a quest.

Ido studies the park map. The old oak is on the other side of the grounds.

IDO: We can take the paved path.

LUMA: The prophecy says the long road must test us.

PERRI: It says return it. It does not describe a road.

SCENE 2 - EXT. ROSE GARDEN - LATER

The fellowship arrives at a gate marked STAFF ONLY. Perri strums a lute to persuade the roses to part. A gardener opens the gate from the other side.

GARDENER: This path is closed while I prune.

LUMA: We seek passage to the old oak.

GARDENER: Take the paved path around the fountain. It is shorter.

Perri lowers the lute. The thermos rattles against Luma's pack.

IDO: The note did not say to cross the garden.

LUMA: A hero cannot abandon the trial.

The gardener points to the sign, then to the path. They walk around the fountain.

SCENE 2A - EXT. PARK MAP KIOSK - LATER

Luma unfurls the note and looks for a second line. Perri studies the paper against the light. Ido reads the small print on the map kiosk.

IDO: The picnic organizer left a contact number on the back.

LUMA: Perhaps it is the ancient runic script.

PERRI: It says call Venn if found.

Luma tries to use the thermos as a staff. It tips out of her hand and clunks onto the bench.

LUMA: The vessel resists my command.

IDO: It is a thermos. Hold it by the handle.

Perri calls the number. The phone rings once and goes to voicemail.

PERRI: Venn is probably already at the oak. We should follow the marked path.

LUMA: The prophecy points to the oak.

IDO: The arrow on the map does too.

They put the note back around the lid, this time with the writing facing outward. Luma straps the thermos into her pack rather than carrying it like a staff.

SCENE 3 - EXT. FOOTBRIDGE - MIDDAY

A small stream crosses the path. The bridge is closed for repairs. Luma stops before the barrier.

LUMA: The river of doubt.

IDO: It is a stream. The bridge is being repaired.

PERRI: I can play a crossing song.

The repair crew lowers a plank ramp onto the accessible crossing nearby. Luma waits until they signal. The group walks across one at a time. The thermos slips from her bag, rolls to the edge and stops against Ido's boot.

IDO: The vessel has tested its lid.

LUMA: The lid is still closed.

SCENE 4 - EXT. OLD OAK - AFTERNOON

The sun reaches the oak's broad shadow. A picnic group gathers beneath it. Venn checks the labels on two thermoses. One is marked VENN, the other LUMA.

VENN: This one belongs to my sister. The note should have had her name.

LUMA: I am not the chosen one?

VENN: You are Luma? Your sister asked me to leave this at the gate for her. The note got tied to the wrong thermos.

IDO: The prophecy has a clerical error.

PERRI: The journey was still a good song.

LUMA: I carried the wrong vessel across the park.

Venn opens the thermos. It is full of chilled tea. He pours cups for the fellowship.

SCENE 5 - EXT. OLD OAK - MOMENTS LATER

Luma writes a new note with the sister's name and the exact picnic table number. Ido hands her a marker. Perri plays a brief fanfare, then puts the lute away as the group starts lunch.

VENN: The return route is the paved path. The bridge crew has finished, but the sign has not come down.

IDO: We can tell them the barrier is still up.

LUMA: Our next quest is to return the marker to the park office.

PERRI: A shorter quest with a clear ending.

Luma takes a drink of tea, then places the thermos beside Venn. The fellowship has no crown, no prophecy, and no stolen treasure.

FINAL IMAGE: The corrected note rests under the thermos, naming its true keeper in plain block letters as a tiny leaf lands on the lid like a medal.
