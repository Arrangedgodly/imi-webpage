# The Orchid With Two Shadows

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: spy thrillers with undercover agents, coded objects, and a gala exchange mistaken for a flower judging*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: VELA, a macaque courier; ASH, a baboon agent; MOSS, a gibbon florist; DIRECTOR KETT, an orangutan security chief; LUX, a capuchin judge; NOOR, a mandrill rival operative.

INT. GLASSHOUSE FLOWER EXHIBITION - EVENING

Rows of orchids glow under warm lamps. A banner reads INTERNATIONAL NIGHT BLOOM SHOW. Vela wears a volunteer sash and carries a watering can. Ash, disguised as a pruning instructor, approaches.

ASH: The blue orchid is missing.

VELA: The blue orchid is right there.

They look at the display. The flower is purple. A small card beneath it reads BLUE ORCHID, UNDER REVIEW.

ASH: The real one has a white mark shaped like a comma.

VELA: This one has a comma.

ASH: That is paint.

Moss enters with a clipboard.

MOSS: Please do not water the display until judging is complete.

Vela lowers the can. Noor steps from behind a column, holding a second watering can.

NOOR: The flower is the prize. The card is the signal.

DIRECTOR KETT: No coded messages in the exhibit hall.

Noor turns away. Kett stops them with one hand. Noor leaves the can on the floor and disappears through a side door.

ACT ONE

INT. GLASSHOUSE SERVICE CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Vela follows the wet footprints from Noor's can. Ash stays near the display. The corridor forks: one path leads to the loading dock, the other to the judges' room. Vela sees drops going both ways.

VELA: Two routes.

MOSS: One can leak from either side.

Vela tips the can. Its base is cracked. A folded note slides out. It reads, "Switch the label before the bell."

MOSS: That is not a watering instruction.

Kett appears at the corridor entrance. Ash gives the signal: two taps against a metal bucket.

ASH: We need both exits covered.

KETT: The side door is locked. Noor cannot have left.

They search the judges' room. A stack of scorecards sits on the table. One card has a wet corner. Vela notices the paper has an embossed flower, not a security seal.

VELA: The note came from this stack.

Kett asks Lux, the judge, to verify the card. Lux says all scorecards are printed in the room and none use coded marks.

LUX: I judge flowers, not handwriting.

Ash returns to the orchid case. The comma-shaped white mark is now on the opposite petal.

ASH: The display flower has been turned.

MOSS: Someone rotated the pot.

Vela checks the bottom of the pot. A second label is taped underneath, with the real flower's greenhouse number. It matches a shipping crate at the loading dock.

EXT. LOADING DOCK - NIGHT

The team reaches the crate. Noor is already there, holding a small potted orchid. They try to leave through the dock, but the door opens onto a locked gate. Kett closes the inner door.

NOOR: You followed the wrong flower.

VELA: We followed the water.

Noor raises the orchid. The white comma glows under the dock light. It is not paint; it is a pale natural mark. Lux compares it with the registered photograph.

LUX: The mark matches. The leaves do not.

Noor tilts the pot. A strip of blue ribbon is tied beneath the rim. Vela points out that the display card was switched, but the security register still names greenhouse seven.

VELA: You changed the label, not the flower.

NOOR: I changed the prize before the judges could see it.

KETT: The judges were already here.

Noor admits they planned to take the real orchid after the award announcement. They used a fake card to draw attention to the display while the plant was moved through the service corridor. Moss checks the leaf edge and confirms that the stem has not been cut. Lux photographs the pot, the ribbon, and the crate number before anyone moves it.

ACT TWO

LUX: This is the missing orchid. The display flower is a decoy.

Noor says they planned to replace the winner's card before judging, then steal the real plant during the confusion. Vela points to the cracked watering can, which left the trail that exposed the plan.

KETT: The exhibit remains closed until the plant is secure.

Moss carries the orchid back inside. Lux places it in a locked display case and signs the correct scorecard. Ash secures the side door. Noor is escorted away without the plant.

At the judging bell, Kett announces a delay. The audience waits. Lux awards the orchid a blue ribbon, though the flower is violet.

MOSS: The color card was wrong.

LUX: The flower won on its leaves.

Vela hangs the ribbon beside the correct label. The director returns the decoy pot to its marked spot.

FINAL IMAGE: The white comma glints on the orchid while Vela waters the floor, where the only remaining footprints are her own.
