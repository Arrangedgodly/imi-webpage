# The Giant's Garden Has a Tiny Gate

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy adventure where a miniature gate is a normal garden entrance*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
GARDENER MARA, a macaque
GIANT VEX, an orangutan
SCOUT PIP, a capuchin
GATEKEEPER ODO, a gibbon
BOTANIST NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN - MORNING

A small gate leads into a raised herb bed. Vex kneels before it.

VEX: The giant's garden is sealed.

MARA: The gate keeps rabbits out. It opens from this latch.

ODO: The latch is at ground level. The gate is not locked.

PIP: I can reach it. Vex's hand is too large for the opening.

VEX: The entrance rejects the giant.

NARI: It is built for the bed, not for giants.

SCENE 2. EXT. GARDEN GATE - CONTINUOUS

Pip lifts the latch and opens the gate. Vex looks inside at basil and mint.

VEX: The herbs are miniature trees.

MARA: They are full-size herbs in a raised bed.

ODO: The path inside is narrow. We should enter one at a time.

PIP: I'll go first and hold the gate.

Vex tries to squeeze through; his coat catches on the latch.

MARA: Stop. Back up. Don't pull the fabric.

SCENE 2A. EXT. GARDEN GATE - CONTINUOUS

Pip crouches and studies the latch. The gate swings inward, but a small hook holds it open.

PIP: If we use the hook, the gate won't close on Vex's coat.

MARA: I'll set the hook. Vex, wait until it's fixed before you step through.

ODO: The stepping stones begin just beyond the threshold. Stay on them; the soil is soft.

NARI: The basil needs harvesting, but the mint should be left alone until next week.

VEX: The gatekeeper guards the herbs.

ODO: I am the gatekeeper. The latch works, and the opening is clear.

Mara sets the hook. Pip tests the gate once, then waves Vex through. Vex turns sideways and holds his coat close to his body. The coat clears the latch without snagging.

VEX: I have entered the garden.

MARA: You have entered through the gate.

Pip releases the hook after all five have crossed. Mara checks the gate swings shut and the latch catches. Odo records the gate check on the garden sheet.

NARI: Gate closed. Rabbits stay out; people can still enter when they lift the latch.

VEX: A mighty barrier.

MARA: A small gate with a clear purpose.

SCENE 2B. EXT. GARDEN PATH - MOMENTS LATER

Everyone has crossed. The hook has been released and the gate is already latched; Odo confirms it stayed shut. Vex stands inside the bed, his coat free. Mara sets an empty harvest basket on the stepping stones.

VEX: We have breached the miniature fortress.

MARA: We walked through a gate. Now stay on the stones so we do not crush the seedlings.

NARI: Basil is ready. Mint is not. We harvest after the path is clear.

PIP: I'll hold the basket at the path edge. Nobody needs to enter the soil.

Vex raises both arms for a victory pose. His sleeve nudges the basket, which slides toward the bed. Pip catches it before it lands among the plants.

PIP: The basket is not a royal shield.

VEX: The fortress tried to take our provisions.

ODO: The basket is empty. Nothing has been harvested yet.

Mara points out the stepping stones. Vex carefully returns to the path, then studies the tiny gate behind him.

VEX: It has closed against us.

MARA: The latch keeps rabbits out. Pip can open it from this side when we leave.

NARI: We are still inside. We can finish the work before opening it again.

SCENE 3. EXT. GARDEN PATH - LATER

Mara points out the basil stems ready for harvest. Vex holds the basket at the end of the path, away from the soil. Pip takes the small snips from the tool hook and checks that the safety lock is open.

VEX: Shall I gather the whole kingdom?

MARA: One stem from each mature plant. Leave the new shoots.

ODO: The gate remains latched behind us. The stepping stones are clear.

NARI: We need a handful for lunch. Anything extra will wilt before anyone uses it.

VEX: The harvest must be grand enough for the royal table.

PIP: The basket is small because we only need a handful.

Vex spreads his arms to describe an enormous feast and nearly drops the empty basket. Pip catches its handle and passes it back to him.

MARA: Hold it below the cutting point. Pip will place each stem inside.

VEX: I shall be the basket bearer.

NARI: A humble but important role.

Pip waits for Mara's nod before approaching the first plant. The snips remain open in his hand; he has not cut anything yet.

SCENE 4. EXT. GARDEN BED - DAY

Vex holds a basket while Pip harvests basil. Mara checks the stems; Nari labels the bundle.

VEX: We have conquered the garden.

MARA: We harvested enough basil for lunch.

ODO: Gate closes and latch works.

TAG: Pip lifts the latch from inside. Vex steps out sideways; Pip closes the gate once behind them.
