# The Mentor's Very Long Goodbye

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: adventure-film mentor deaths, sudden last lessons, and heroes who ignore useful advice until the final act*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A young explorer prepares for a dangerous expedition while her mentor keeps trying to deliver a final lesson, only to discover the expedition is a supervised trail inspection.

Cast: Nela, a capuchin apprentice; Bram, an orangutan guide; Joss, a macaque camera operator; and Kiri, a lemur park ranger.

SCENE 1. EXT. TRAILHEAD, MORNING

Nela tightens her pack. Bram points to the route map and tells her to check the weather before entering the canyon. She looks toward a distant cloud and says the sky is clear enough. Bram reaches for his raincoat. Nela says she has the map.

BRAM: Maps do not stop rain.

NELA: They show where to go.

Joss films the exchange. Bram asks if they are ready. Nela starts down the trail without the radio. Kiri calls her back and hands her one. The film crew begins a dramatic music cue; Bram asks them to keep the path clear.

SCENE 2. EXT. CANYON OVERLOOK, LATER

Nela leads the group to a fork. She chooses the narrow route because it looks more challenging. Bram checks the map and points out the marked trail. Nela says the map is old. Bram says the cairn ahead is also old and still marks the correct route.

Joss asks Bram to tell Nela one last story before they split up. Bram asks why it must be the last. The director, off camera, says every adventure needs a mentor who gives a final warning and exits. Bram refuses to exit until Nela checks the map. She looks and notices the narrow route is closed for repairs.

Joss tries a tighter shot of Bram silhouetted against the canyon. Bram steps aside so Nela can read the closure sign. The director asks him to look toward the horizon as though he senses danger. Bram says he senses rain. Kiri checks the radio and confirms a weather advisory has just come in. Nela asks why the sign is not on the main trailhead board. Kiri notes its location and calls the ranger station to update the notice.

The director asks if this is where Bram should sacrifice his compass. Bram checks the compass against the map and hands it to Nela so she can learn the bearing. She compares it with the trail markers. The compass works. Joss lowers the camera when the wind blows dust into the lens.

Kiri shows them a new sign installed at the trail fork. Nela reads it and circles back. Joss asks for a solemn close-up of Bram turning away. Bram turns toward the ranger station instead, where he can radio the closure to the crew.

SCENE 3. INT. RANGER STATION, MIDDAY

The trail inspection is postponed until the maintenance crew clears the route. Nela asks Bram to teach her how to read weather changes. He shows her the ridge cloud and explains that the wind is carrying moisture toward the canyon. She checks the forecast on the ranger tablet and sees rain expected in an hour.

The director asks Bram to deliver a final lesson. Bram says he can teach her to check a radio battery. Nela tests hers; it is nearly empty. Kiri gives her a charged spare. Joss records Nela replacing the battery and labeling the used one. The director asks for a sadder expression. Nela says she is glad the radio works.

The film crew wants to stage a goodbye at the station door. Bram asks Nela where she would go if he left. She points to the map, names the trail closure, and says she would stay with the ranger until conditions improve. The director says that is too competent for the scene. Kiri replies that competence is the point of training. Joss records the answer without a close-up tear.

Nela asks Bram how to tell when a guide should step back. He says when she has checked the route, weather, radio, and team. He asks her to repeat the checks. She does, in order, and catches that the spare radio is missing its clip. Kiri finds one in the supply drawer. Bram smiles but does not make a farewell speech.

The maintenance team calls with an update. The trail is safe for a short inspection after the rain passes. Bram hands Nela the checklist and lets her lead the group back out. He follows at a distance, ready to help but not leaving.

SCENE 4. EXT. TRAIL FORK, AFTERNOON

The crew reaches the fork. Nela checks the weather, confirms the trail sign, and chooses the open route. The group inspects the repaired railing. One bolt is loose. Nela radios the ranger station and marks the location with a bright tag. She does not attempt an unapproved repair.

The director asks if Bram can now disappear into the mist. The rain begins. Bram pulls on his raincoat and opens an umbrella. Nela has her own coat and radio. They walk back together. Joss films the wet trail, the clear markings, and the maintenance tag. The dramatic soundtrack swells, then fades when the camera operator slips slightly in the mud and catches the handrail.

Kiri calls the maintenance crew with the bolt location. The crew schedules a repair for the next morning. Nela updates the checklist with the weather and route closure information. Bram signs as the supervising guide.

Joss asks if the crew can reshoot the walk for a more heroic pace. Nela says the current footage shows every trail marker and the loose bolt. The director watches it and agrees that the repair request is clear. He asks Bram to deliver one closing line. Bram says, "We will return after maintenance." The director writes it down and crosses out his planned sentence about destiny.

FINAL IMAGE: The director crosses out "MENTOR FALLS BEHIND" on the call sheet. Bram and Nela share the map beneath the umbrella, both pointing toward the ranger station.
