# The Island of Misplaced Shoes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: stranded-on-an-island survival epics, where one missing shoe prompts a resourceful cast to build a whole civilization*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: After a river crossing separates one traveler from a pair of shoes, the stranded group builds an elaborate recovery plan while the shoe sits on the nearest rock.

Cast: Scout Nima, macaque; builder Odo, baboon; navigator Piri, capuchin; traveler Vela, gibbon.

SCENE 1. EXT. RIVERBANK, DAY

Vela stands barefoot at the riverbank. Her pair of shoes sits on a rock a few feet away.

VELA: The river has separated me from my shoes.

PIRI: The river is behind us.

NIMA: The shoe is on that rock.

ODO: We have stranded footwear.

Nima appoints Odo to watch the river and Piri to monitor the rock. Vela is assigned to ration the trail mix. She opens the bag and finds only crumbs.

VELA: The supplies are depleted.

PIRI: There were three nuts.

NIMA: We must divide them equally.

ODO: There are no nuts.

Nima carefully divides the crumbs into four piles. A breeze blows two piles away.

NIMA: We have lost half our provisions.

PIRI: We had crumbs.

VELA: The riverbank has taken them.

Nima lays out a route using sticks. Odo builds a small raft from reeds.

ODO: The raft can carry the shoe across.

PIRI: We are already on the same side.

NIMA: We need a safe retrieval method.

VELA: I can walk to it.

NIMA: The ground may be unstable.

Piri tests the ground. It is firm.

PIRI: It is a bank.

SCENE 2. EXT. MAKESHIFT CAMP, LATER

The group builds a windbreak and a signal fire, though they are not lost. Vela asks for the shoe. Nima says they are waiting for the tide to change.

VELA: This is not the sea.

ODO: The river is moving.

PIRI: The shoe is not moving.

Odo ties a rope around his waist and walks toward the rock. The rope is too short. Nima adds another length, then knots it. The knot slips.

ODO: The lifeline has failed.

PIRI: You have a rope.

NIMA: We need a reliable knot.

Vela sits on a nearby log and takes off one damp sock.

VELA: Now I have both feet on the ground.

NIMA: That doubles your exposure.

VELA: It doubles the comfort.

Nima draws a map of the riverbank in the sand. Odo adds a perimeter line around the camp, then realizes the shoes are outside it.

ODO: We have left the objective unprotected.

PIRI: The objective is on a rock in plain view.

NIMA: It could be carried by the current.

PIRI: The rock is not in the current.

Vela points to a dry patch beside the trail and suggests they move the camp closer to the shoes. Odo starts lifting the windbreak. Nima says they should wait until the weather clears.

VELA: The weather is clear.

NIMA: Then we should confirm it.

Piri looks up. The sky is blue.

PIRI: Confirmed.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVER EDGE, AFTERNOON

Piri suggests using a long branch to hook the shoe. Odo finds one. It is too short. Nima adds a second branch with vine. The joint bends.

[SFX: BRANCH CREAK.]

NIMA: The tool is unstable.

PIRI: The shoe is still three feet away.

Vela points out a stepping stone that reaches the rock. She crosses carefully and picks up both shoes.

NIMA: Wait for the signal.

VELA: I am on the rock.

ODO: The retrieval has begun.

Vela returns safely. Nima checks the shoe for water. It is dry.

SCENE 4. EXT. CAMP, EVENING

The group celebrates the recovery. Odo has built a small shrine from the branches and vine.

VELA: It is just a shoe.

ODO: It survived the river.

PIRI: It never entered the water.

NIMA: It endured the uncertainty.

Vela puts on both shoes. They fit, though one is still damp.

VELA: Both shoes are back where they belong.

Piri checks the rock. It is empty.

PIRI: The pair has returned.

NIMA: We have completed the operation.

Odo says they can simply cross the three-foot gap again. Nima refuses to rush the mission. Vela ties the two laces together loosely so neither slips off during the walk.

SCENE 5. EXT. RIVERBANK, NIGHT

The group dismantles the camp. The raft, windbreak, signal fire and recovery rope are all unused. Vela walks away wearing both shoes correctly.

ODO: The island is secure.

PIRI: It is a riverbank.

NIMA: The mission is complete.

Vela notices a pebble in her shoe and stops.

VELA: I have another emergency.

NIMA: We must establish a perimeter.

PIRI: Or you can take the shoe off.

Vela removes it and shakes out the pebble.

[SFX: PEBBLE FALLS.]

ODO: The island has sent a warning.

VELA: It's a pebble.

NIMA: We will record it in the log.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS LEAVE RIVERBANK.]
