# The Last Balloon on Earth

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: post-apocalyptic survival epics, where the final balloon becomes a symbol of hope after a town party*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: After a storm scatters the decorations, three survivors protect the last balloon from the wind, only to learn the celebration is still scheduled.

Cast: Scout Kito, macaque; mechanic Nara, gibbon; keeper Odo, baboon; and courier Miri, capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. EMPTY SQUARE, AFTER A STORM

Torn streamers hang from a bench. A single red balloon is tangled in a low branch.

KITO: We have found the last balloon.

Nara asks how the balloon got onto the branch. Odo says it was tied to a chair before the storm. Kito draws a route on the ground, showing its journey from the hall to the roof, down the branch, and back to the square.

NARA: That route has no evidence.

KITO: It has a beginning and an end.

MIRI: The string has leaves caught in it.

Odo removes a leaf. The balloon bobs higher.

ODO: It is lighter now.

KITO: It has shed its burden.

NARA: It shed a leaf.

NARA: The storm took the other decorations.

ODO: The party is tomorrow.

KITO: The party may not survive the night.

Miri arrives carrying a basket of replacement ribbons.

MIRI: The supply shed is open.

KITO: You crossed the exposed square?

MIRI: I walked across it.

KITO: And the wind?

MIRI: There was some.

Nara uses a long pole to lift the balloon from the branch. The string slips free, and the balloon rises toward the roof.

[SFX: STRING SLIPS, BALLOON RUBS ROOF.] 

ODO: It is ascending.

KITO: We must recover it before it leaves the district.

SCENE 2. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, MOMENTS LATER

The crew enters the hall. They find a ladder, a broom, and a box marked "party supplies." Nara sets the ladder near the roof hatch.

NARA: We can retrieve it safely.

KITO: We need a backup plan.

MIRI: We have spare balloons in the box.

KITO: Those are untested.

Odo opens the box. Dozens of balloons spill out.

ODO: The last balloon has companions.

KITO: The prophecy was wrong.

MIRI: There was no prophecy.

Kito studies the red balloon, which is still caught above the roof hatch. He asks Nara to tie a ribbon to the broom. She does. The ribbon catches on a ceiling beam.

NARA: The plan has caught.

KITO: The plan has acquired a foothold.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROOFTOP, LATER

Nara climbs the ladder while Odo steadies it. Kito directs from below, narrating each rung as if a dangerous expedition.

KITO: She has reached the third rung.

NARA: I can hear you.

KITO: The fourth rung is unstable.

NARA: It's the same rung.

Miri holds the broom. The balloon drifts along the roof and settles near a vent. Nara reaches for it, but the string catches on the vent grate.

NARA: I can free it.

KITO: The vent may lead to the old tunnel.

ODO: There is no tunnel. It is an air vent.

KITO: We cannot rule out a tunnel.

Nara frees the string and hands the balloon down. It is intact. The team lowers it carefully. The wind gusts and pulls the balloon toward the open hatch.

[SFX: WIND, STRING TUG.] 

Miri ties it to the broom handle. The balloon stays.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, NIGHT

The team puts the red balloon inside a clear storage box. Kito posts a guard schedule.

KITO: We take turns watching it.

MIRI: It is secured in a box.

ODO: The box is open.

NARA: I'll close it.

The lid does not fit because the balloon is too large. Odo suggests using the box without the lid. Kito refuses.

KITO: A box without a lid is a basket.

MIRI: Baskets are useful.

KITO: This is no time for definitions.

They fold the box flaps around the balloon. The balloon lifts the flaps back open.

NARA: It is pushing through.

ODO: The balloon is not in distress.

KITO: It is resisting confinement.

Miri unties it and fixes the string to a heavy chair. The balloon floats above the seat.

SCENE 5. EXT. COMMUNITY SQUARE, NEXT MORNING

The party setup begins. The storm has passed. The crew brings the balloon to the square, where other decorations are already arriving.

MIRI: The celebration survived.

KITO: The balloon was a symbol.

NARA: It was a balloon.

Odo attaches it to the central post. The post bends slightly. Kito steps forward to save it; Nara braces the base. The post steadies.

KITO: We have secured the symbol.

MIRI: The party is opening.

Children arrive and admire the red balloon. One asks if it is the last one.

ODO: It was last night.

NARA: There are plenty in the box.

Kito looks at the sky, then at the unopened box of balloons.

KITO: Then hope has multiplied.

[SFX: BALLOON STRING TAPS POST, PARTY MUSIC BEGINS.]
