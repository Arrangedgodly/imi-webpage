# The Kingdom with a One-Lane Bridge

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy kingdom politics, royal decrees, bridge toll quests, and councils that turn a simple crossing into a prophecy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
QUEEN NARI, an orangutan
KNIGHT VEX, a macaque
BRIDGE KEEPER LUMA, a lemur
MESSENGER PIP, a capuchin
MAYOR ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. KINGDOM BRIDGE - MORNING

A narrow bridge spans a creek between two villages. Knight Vex arrives with a royal decree ordering free passage. Bridge keeper Luma says the bridge is one lane and currently under repair. Messenger Pip waits with a letter for the queen. A cart carrying turnips blocks the entrance.

VEX: The decree commands us to cross.

LUMA: The bridge allows one cart at a time.

The cart begins to roll backward. Pip holds it by the handle while Vex tries to lift it.

SCENE 2. INT. CASTLE COUNCIL ROOM - LATER

Queen Nari studies the bridge report. The royal council has proposed a new bridge on the old map, but the location is downstream. Mayor Odo says both villages use the existing crossing. Vex requests a guard post. Luma requests a handrail and a signal bell.

NARI: Which one keeps travelers safe?

VEX: A guard could do that.

ODO: The bell can tell each side when the bridge is clear.

The queen asks for an engineer's inspection before issuing a decree. Vex feels his quest has lost its battle. Pip gives him the inspection form and asks him to carry it to the bridge.

SCENE 2A. INT. CASTLE ARCHIVE - NOON

Nari asks the archivist to retrieve the original bridge plan. The old map shows a second lane, but that section was never built because the creek bank was too soft. Vex explains that he had read the map as a royal promise. Odo points to the note in the margin: "Future option after bank survey." The council had never approved the second lane.

PIP: The map described a possibility.

NARI: The decree can fund an inspection, not move the creek.

The queen revises the plan to repair the existing bridge and study whether a wider crossing is safe. Vex carries the revised document to Luma. He asks if it can still be called a quest. Luma says he can call it a repair visit.

SCENE 3. EXT. BRIDGE - AFTERNOON

The engineer finds one support beam is sound, but a handrail is loose. Luma closes the bridge for an hour. Vex guards the entrance. A traveler arrives from the opposite side with a cart. Vex waves them forward; Luma rings the bell to tell them to wait. The cart stops safely.

Pip delivers the queen's form. It authorizes a new handrail and two signal bells. The villagers help carry lumber to the site. Vex holds the plank while the engineer secures it. He asks whether a knight should do construction. Luma says the bridge needs a steady hand.

SCENE 3A. EXT. BRIDGE - AFTERNOON

The engineer checks the creek bank and finds roots holding the soil in place. Widening the bridge would require a separate environmental review. Luma marks a temporary waiting line on each side. The travelers can see one another before crossing. Vex tries to stand in the middle and direct traffic. A goat cart approaches; the goat ignores his sword and follows the bell signal instead.

Odo brings a sign with the crossing rules. Pip installs it at eye level. Nari confirms the bridge is still one lane and asks the engineer to schedule a bank survey for later in the year. The town can use the repaired bridge in the meantime.

SCENE 3B. EXT. BRIDGE - LATE AFTERNOON

Vex tests the bell signal from the far side. One ring travels across the creek, but the second ring is muffled by a cart passing over the bridge. Pip moves the bell to the post where both villages can hear it. Luma rings the test signal. The cart waits, then crosses after two clear rings.

ODO: The bridge has a voice now.

LUMA: It has two bells.

SCENE 4. EXT. BRIDGE - SUNSET

The repair is complete. The villagers test the bell from both ends. One ring means wait; two rings means cross. The turnip cart goes first. Vex follows on foot, then helps Mayor Odo carry a basket across. The bridge holds. The queen arrives and walks over without a procession.

PIP: The decree is fulfilled.

NARI: The bridge is repaired.

VEX: That is a better ending.

SCENE 5. INT. CASTLE COUNCIL ROOM - NIGHT

Nari updates the kingdom map with the repaired bridge and its one-lane signal. Odo writes down the bell instructions for travelers. Vex hangs his sword and asks to be assigned the next inspection. Luma gives him a wrench instead of a shield.

FINAL IMAGE: One bell hangs at each end of the bridge, and the turnip cart waits for the two-ring signal.
