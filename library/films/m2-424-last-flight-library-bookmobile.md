# The Last Flight of the Library Bookmobile

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster blockbusters that stack countdowns, alarms and heroic rescues onto a small solvable logistics problem*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A storm threatens a hilltop library's bookmobile, and a team of monkeys stages an epic evacuation that keeps rescuing the wrong objects.

Cast: Luma, a macaque librarian; Havi, a capuchin driver; Soro, a baboon emergency coordinator; Pelli, a lemur weather reader; Nemi, a gibbon child.

SCENE 1 - INT. HILLTOP LIBRARY - AFTERNOON

Rain hits the windows. A public weather radio crackles. The bookmobile keys hang by the front desk beside a handwritten return slip.

PELLI: The bridge may close at six if the river rises. The bookmobile is parked below the hill.

Soro enters with a red emergency vest and a stopwatch.

SORO: We have ninety minutes to evacuate every book, every chair and every person.

LUMA: We have twelve people and one van. The books can stay on the shelves. We only need to move the bookmobile above the bridge.

SORO: The movie version would move the entire library.

HAVI: The van can move one vehicle at a time. I will start with the bookmobile.

NEMI: My drawing is in there.

LUMA: Your drawing is in the returns bin. We will take it with us.

Soro starts the stopwatch and slaps a map onto a table. It unfolds across the room and knocks over a stack of paper bookmarks.

SCENE 2 - EXT. LIBRARY PARKING LOT - LATE AFTERNOON

Havi checks the bookmobile's route. Soro ties a red ribbon to the front bumper as a supposed storm marker.

HAVI: The front tire is on the curb. I can back it down slowly.

SORO: The rescue team should pull it with the van. That will look decisive.

LUMA: The van is smaller and the hill is slick. Let Havi drive.

Soro fastens the tow strap anyway. It catches on a rubber traffic cone. When the van moves, the cone skitters across the lot and the bookmobile does not budge.

NEMI: The rescue rope is pulling the cone.

SORO: The cone is part of the convoy.

HAVI: No, it is not. Stop the van.

The van brakes. Luma frees the strap. Havi gets into the bookmobile and drives it up the clear access road, reaching the upper lot without incident.

SCENE 3 - INT. LIBRARY - MOMENTS LATER

Pelli checks the radio. The bridge alert has been delayed by an hour. Rain continues, but the river is below the closure mark.

PELLI: The bridge remains open. We have time.

SORO: The countdown is still running.

LUMA: Stop it. The bookmobile is safely parked, and everyone is indoors.

SORO: We can use the remaining time to rescue the lighthouse model.

NEMI: The model is made of cardboard. It will fold if you carry it by the top.

Soro grips the model and its beacon detaches. He catches the beacon, drops the base, and steps on a paper wave.

SORO: The model has sustained damage.

LUMA: We were not evacuating it.

HAVI: I can get the repair kit from the bookmobile.

SCENE 4 - INT. BOOKMOBILE - EARLY EVENING

Havi opens the side hatch. The interior is dry. Nemi finds the drawing in the return bin and shows it to Luma: a row of books beneath a storm cloud, each wearing a tiny rain hat.

NEMI: I made this for the weather shelf.

LUMA: We can hang it by the radio so everyone sees the real alert.

Soro places a blanket over the bookmobile as if shielding it from the storm. The blanket slides down over the mirror, obscuring the driver's view.

HAVI: Please move the blanket. I need the mirrors.

SORO: The vehicle needs protection.

LUMA: It is parked inside, under the library canopy. Fold the blanket and put it away.

Soro folds it into a square, but it is too large for the drawer. Nemi rolls it and fits it into the storage bag.

SCENE 5 - EXT. LIBRARY HILL - NIGHT

The storm passes. The river stays below the bridge marker. Havi backs the bookmobile into its normal space. The emergency team removes the tow strap, stores the cones, and returns the keys to their hook.

SORO: No bridge closure. No stranded readers. No books lost.

LUMA: One damaged cardboard lighthouse.

NEMI: I can repair it with the kit.

She tapes the lighthouse beacon back onto the model. Soro's stopwatch rings. He tries to stop it, but the button is under his red vest.

HAVI: The emergency is over.

Soro removes the vest and turns off the timer. The library clock continues ticking normally.

FINAL IMAGE: Nemi's drawing hangs over the weather radio, and outside the bookmobile rests in its marked parking space beside a neatly folded rescue strap.
