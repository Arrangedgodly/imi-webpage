# The Feather in the Evidence Box

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: courtroom mysteries, dramatic exhibits, and legal arguments over details no witness can see*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
JUDGE PELT, a slow loris
CLERK MIRA, a macaque
PROSECUTOR VEX, a gibbon
DEFENDER NARI, a lemur
WITNESS PIP, a capuchin

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN COURT - MORNING

A single gray feather rests inside a glass evidence box. Judge Pelt enters. Vex stands beside the box, ready to present his case.

VEX: This feather proves the defendant entered the aviary.

NARI: It proves a bird lost a feather.

PIP: I found it on the courthouse steps.

MIRA: The steps are outside the aviary.

Vex says the wind could have carried it. Nari says the wind is not on trial. Judge Pelt asks Mira to read the charge. She says the accused, Pip, is charged with "unauthorized feather proximity."

PIP: I was carrying a sandwich.

VEX: The bird was alarmed.

PIP: It was asleep.

Vex lifts the evidence box. The feather drifts to the other side. He calls it a movement consistent with flight.

NARI: The box moved.

SCENE 2. INT. COURTROOM - LATER

Mira admits the feather into evidence. Vex asks Pip to describe what he was doing at the aviary. Pip says he was walking to the bakery. The route passes the aviary gate. He never went inside.

Vex brings out the sensitive scale. Mira sets the evidence box on it. The needle does not move. Vex asks if the feather has no weight. Mira says the box is still on the scale. She lifts it. The scale tips, and the empty pan rises toward the ceiling.

VEX: The evidence is weightless.

NARI: The box was heavy.

Mira sets one corner of the box down. The needle swings wildly. Pelt asks her to stop. She removes the box and puts it on the table. A crumb from Pip's sandwich has stuck to the bottom.

PIP: That is not a feather.

VEX: It could be a breadcrumb trail.

JUDGE PELT: The court is not a bakery.

VEX: Did you see a bird?

PIP: I saw a sign that said "Keep Out."

VEX: So you saw the aviary.

PIP: I saw the sign.

Nari asks whether the feather is the same color as Pip's coat. It is not. Vex asks whether Pip could have borrowed a gray coat. Pip opens his bag. It contains only a sandwich and a napkin.

JUDGE PELT: The bag is not the defendant.

Vex asks Mira to turn the box so the court can inspect the feather's point. The box rotates on its base. The feather does not move. Vex says the feather is unusually still.

MIRA: It is sealed in a box.

Nari says the evidence should be weighed. The scale reads zero. Mira says the feather is too light for the scale. Vex calls for a more sensitive scale. The clerk retrieves one from the records room.

SCENE 3. INT. COURTROOM - AFTERNOON

Pip remembers a pigeon on the courthouse steps. It had been preening beside the door. Vex objects that the pigeon is not present. Nari asks the bailiff to invite it in.

The bailiff opens the window. A pigeon flies into the courtroom and lands on the evidence box. The lid rattles. Everyone ducks. The pigeon pulls the feather through a narrow gap and carries it to the window ledge.

VEX: The evidence has been removed.

NARI: The owner has retrieved it.

The pigeon drops the feather on the sill and pecks at a crumb. Pip offers his sandwich. The pigeon ignores him and flies out.

JUDGE PELT: Was the feather in the box before the bird entered?

MIRA: Yes.

JUDGE PELT: Is it still there?

MIRA: No.

Vex asks the clerk to replace it. Mira says the bird removed the only exhibit. Vex asks if the trial can proceed with a photograph. The photograph shows a gray blur.

NARI: That could be anyone.

PIP: It could be the feather.

SCENE 4. INT. COURTROOM - EVENING

Judge Pelt dismisses the charge. Vex asks for the feather to remain in evidence if it returns. Mira closes the empty box and writes "owner unknown" on the label.

Pip offers a crumb to the pigeon outside. It flies back, lands on the sill, and leaves a second feather.

VEX: New evidence.

NARI: New bird?

PIP: Same bird.

The judge asks the pigeon to identify itself. It coos and walks away.

SCENE 5. EXT. COURTHOUSE STEPS - SUNSET

The court releases Pip. He returns the sandwich to his bag. Mira locks the empty evidence box in the records room. Vex follows the pigeon, hoping for a statement.

NARI: You cannot subpoena a bird.

VEX: I can ask.

PIP: Ask from outside the aviary.

FINAL IMAGE: A feather drifts onto the courthouse steps. Vex reaches for the evidence box, but the breeze carries it away before he can make another case.
