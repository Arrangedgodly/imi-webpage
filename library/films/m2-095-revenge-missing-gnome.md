# The Revenge of the Missing Gnome

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: revenge thrillers, ominous training montages, and vendettas that grow beyond the original inconvenience*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A gardener launches an elaborate revenge campaign after a neighbor moves her ceramic gnome, then learns the gnome was taken indoors to protect it from hail.

Cast: Kessa, a macaque gardener; Bram, a gorilla neighbor; Tulli, a lemur mail carrier; and Puck, a capuchin who narrates Kessa's plans.

SCENE 1. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, MORNING

Kessa's painted gnome is missing from the path. She finds a bare square of soil and a small wheel mark. Puck tells her to remember every detail. Kessa studies the mark with a magnifying glass.

KESSA: Someone has taken my garden gnome.

PUCK: Then we find who did it.

Kessa draws a red circle around Bram's shed on a map. Bram walks over carrying a watering can and asks if she has seen his gloves. Kessa says nothing. Puck whispers that silence is the first move.

SCENE 2. INT. GARDEN SHED, AFTERNOON

Kessa builds a tracking station from seed trays and a desk lamp. She plans to stake out the shed overnight. Tulli delivers a weather notice: hail is expected. Kessa says the gnome can handle it. Tulli says the ceramic tag on its base warns against freezing and hail.

Puck draws a map connecting Bram's shed to the garden gate. Kessa asks why it also includes the bakery. Puck says every revenge story needs a supply stop. They pack binoculars, a whistle, and three sandwiches. Kessa rereads the tag and notices the maker recommends bringing the gnome indoors during hail. She says Bram may have seen the same warning. Bram arrives to borrow twine. Kessa hides behind a wheelbarrow. Puck whispers, knocks over a stack of pots, and Bram steadies them. He leaves the twine by the shed; it is labeled with Kessa's name.

Puck suggests a decoy gnome. Kessa paints a stone red and places it by the path. Bram sees it, asks if it is part of the garden art, then moves it off the walkway. Kessa mistakes this for a confession and follows him to the shed. She finds the door locked. Puck declares the villain is destroying evidence. Kessa prepares to climb through a high window. Tulli asks whether they have permission to enter. Kessa hesitates as a weather alert sounds: hail is approaching sooner than expected. She and Puck retreat to the garden path to wait for Bram.

SCENE 3. INT. BRAM'S SHED, LATER

Kessa opens the shed door and finds the missing gnome wrapped in a towel on a shelf. Bram says he brought it inside when the hail warning arrived. He planned to return it after the storm. The base tag is visible, with Kessa's name and phone number.

Puck asks why Bram did not leave a note. Bram shows them the note on the garden board. It fell behind the watering schedule. Kessa realizes she never checked the board. She apologizes for treating him like a villain. Bram apologizes for moving the gnome without asking.

Kessa carries the gnome back outside. The weather begins to turn. Tulli says the hail is ten minutes away. Bram suggests placing it in the garden shed until the warning passes. Kessa agrees. They move it together and secure the door. The first hailstones strike while they tighten the latch. Kessa checks the shed roof and sees a loose panel. Bram fetches a strap while Tulli holds the door. Puck moves the decoy stone away from the walkway. They fasten the panel and move the gnome farther from the window. The storm passes quickly. They wait for the warning to expire before reopening the shed. Kessa checks the tag and finds no chips. Bram offers to leave a note next time. Kessa says she will check the board first.

SCENE 4. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, NEXT MORNING

The storm has passed. Kessa checks the garden beds. The gnome is intact in the shed. She places it back beside the path and adds a small weatherproof sign: PLEASE MOVE INSIDE BEFORE HAIL; ASK KESSA. Bram helps clear branches from the walkway.

Puck asks if the revenge plan should be destroyed. Kessa tears the map in half and uses the blank side to write a shared garden checklist. Tulli posts the updated weather notice beside it. Bram places his gloves on the hook instead of leaving them near the gnome.

Kessa paints a second tag with her contact information and ties it securely to the base. She asks Bram to check that the tag does not rub against the ceramic. He adjusts the string. The gnome is back where it belongs, and the path stays clear. Kessa rolls out her stakeout map and stamps a red circle around the forecast symbol for hail. She draws a tiny wanted poster for the storm cloud and pins it beside the gnome. Puck narrates the verdict: the cloud is guilty of moving garden decorations. Tulli arrives with the morning forecast and adds a smaller cloud for the next rain. Bram laughs, then helps Kessa place the gnome beneath its roofed marker, away from the walkway. Her vendetta ends with the gnome visible and the storm report updated.

FINAL IMAGE: The red circle on Kessa's map now marks the shed as a safe place for garden objects during storms. Puck closes his spy notebook and writes "ASK FIRST" on the cover.
