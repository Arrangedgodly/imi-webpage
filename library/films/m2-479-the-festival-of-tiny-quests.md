# The Festival of Tiny Quests

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quests that demand a chosen hero collect relics before a deadline, even when event staff have already posted a checklist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A self-declared hero races through a village festival to gather three relics, while the organizer needs help collecting the same items for the closing ceremony.

Cast: hero Ruma, a macaque; bard Kes, a gibbon; organizer Pala, an orangutan; vendor Nini, a capuchin; child guide Tavi, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - EXT. VILLAGE FESTIVAL GATE - MORNING

Ruma arrives in a cape and helmet. Kes carries a lute. A banner reads FESTIVAL CLOSES AT FOUR. Pala hands Ruma a checklist: ribbon, bell, and painted stone, all needed for the closing ceremony.

RUMA: The prophecy names three relics.

PALA: The list is for the stage crew.

KES: A quest may disguise itself as a list.

Pala points them toward three clearly labeled festival booths. Ruma refuses the map and leads Kes through the wrong gate, into the parking area. A volunteer points them back. Ruma bows as if receiving a challenge.

SCENE 2 - EXT. RIBBON BOOTH - LATER

Nini ties ribbons around baskets. Ruma asks for the ancient red ribbon. Nini shows a roll of red ribbon marked DECORATION SUPPLIES. Ruma asks for a trial. Nini hands her scissors and asks her to cut lengths for the stage.

RUMA: The blade tests my worth.

NINI: It is child-safe craft scissors.

Ruma cuts one length too short and another long enough to wrap around her cape. Kes catches the loose end before she trips. Nini measures three equal pieces and gives them the needed bow. Ruma calls this the first relic.

SCENE 3 - EXT. BELL TOSS BOOTH - AFTERNOON

The bell hangs above a padded target. Ruma asks for the trial of thunder. Tavi, the booth attendant, explains that each participant gets one toss. Ruma throws the beanbag. It misses and lands in a prize bucket.

RUMA: The bell has rejected me.

TAVI: You missed the target.

Kes tosses next and rings the bell. Tavi gives him a small handbell prize. Ruma takes it, then notices the checklist says brass bell for ceremony, not prize bell. The booth's display bell has a loose clapper. Tavi lets them borrow it after Pala signs the prop card. Ruma signs with a flourish, smearing ink across her glove.

Kes finds the festival map on a nearby post and shows Ruma that each booth has a picture matching the checklist. Ruma turns the map upside down, deciding the symbols form a constellation. She walks to the face-painting booth and asks for a star mark. The artist gives her a paper number and asks her to wait. Ruma treats the number as a new relic and pins it to her cape. Pala retrieves her before the ceremony and points out that the number belongs to a child waiting in line.

RUMA: The quest has many tokens.

KES: This one belongs to someone else.

Ruma returns it. The child gives her a sticker shaped like a star, which she accepts only after asking permission.

SCENE 4 - EXT. PAINTED STONE TABLE - LATER

A bowl of smooth painted stones sits on a table. Ruma searches for a stone with a rune. Pala arrives carrying a tray for the closing ceremony.

PALA: Please pick the blue one. It marks the stage center.

RUMA: The prophecy is blue.

KES: It is also marked STAGE CENTER.

Ruma lifts a blue stone. It sticks to the table because someone placed it in wet paint. She pulls harder, and the tablecloth rises with it, carrying several stones. Kes steadies the tray while Pala loosens the stone with a wooden spatula. Ruma receives the stone, now with a blue thumbprint.

Before the ceremony, the event announcer asks Ruma to carry the checklist to the stage. She holds it above her head as if it were a royal standard. A gust turns the paper around; the back is a lunch menu. Kes points her to the right side. Ruma turns it again and marches to the stage with the menu facing forward. Pala takes it and puts it on the refreshment table, then hands her the actual checklist.

SCENE 5 - EXT. FESTIVAL STAGE - FOUR O'CLOCK

The crew places the ribbon, bell, and stone on the stage. Ruma expects a portal to open. Instead, Pala rings the bell, ties the ribbon to the microphone stand, and sets the stone at center stage. The festival volunteers take their bow.

RUMA: The relics have fulfilled their purpose.

PALA: They decorated the stage.

Kes plays a fanfare. The crowd applauds. Ruma starts to announce her heroic deed, but the microphone stand turns slowly, wrapping the ribbon around her cape. Pala unwinds it while the ceremony continues.

FINAL IMAGE: Ruma stands at the edge of the stage, cape neatly tied to the microphone stand, as the festival banner comes down behind her.
