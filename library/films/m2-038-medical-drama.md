# Code Blue, Tea Green

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: medical drama cliffhangers, overworked hospital teams, and miraculous diagnosis reveals*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A patient collapses at a hospital gala, prompting a dramatic diagnostic race that is repeatedly interrupted by the ordinary answers: thirst, a tight costume, and a squeaky tea cart.

Cast: Dr. Vale, a macaque physician who narrates; Nurse Tessa, a langur who reads charts; Dr. Quill, a peacock specialist; Mr. Puck, a capuchin patient; and a tea cart with one loose wheel.

SCENE 1. INT. HOSPITAL GALA, NIGHT

The lights dim for a speech. Mr. Puck slumps in his chair. Guests gasp. Dr. Vale rushes over, stethoscope flying. The tea cart squeaks behind him.

VALE: Clear the floor!

TESSA: He is sitting on the floor.

QUILL: A rare collapse at a rare gala.

TESSA: Check his pulse.

Vale does. Puck opens one eye and asks if the sandwich table is still open. Quill declares that hunger could be a symptom. Tessa brings water. Puck drinks, loosens his collar, and sits upright.

SCENE 2. INT. EXAM ROOM, MOMENTS LATER

Vale orders a dramatic scan. Tessa reads the intake form while Quill adjusts the machine. The form says Puck skipped dinner and wore a costume collar two sizes too small.

QUILL: Could the collar cause the fainting?

TESSA: It can cause the breathing problem.

VALE: We need another test.

TESSA: We need dinner.

Puck asks whether a scan can show the sandwich table. Quill promises an answer after the imaging. The machine starts, flashes once, then prints a picture of Puck's empty stomach. Vale studies it as if it contains a hidden map. Tessa hands Puck a cracker.

SCENE 3. INT. HOSPITAL HALLWAY, LATER

A volunteer turns the hallway into a press area by placing chairs in two rows. Quill stands between them and announces that the next result could change everything. Tessa asks what result. Quill says the tea cart inspection.

VOLUNTEER: Should we be frightened?

TESSA: Only if you block the exit.

The volunteer stacks the chairs against the wall. Vale examines the cart wheel with a penlight. The wheel shines back at him. He calls this a positive reflection; Tessa hands him the wrench.

The gala guests follow the team. Vale narrates their walk to the nurses' station.

VALE: The patient proceeds toward recovery, each step raising new questions.

TESSA: He is walking to get a sandwich.

The tea cart squeaks. Vale spins around and calls it a new symptom. Tessa turns the cart over. A napkin is caught in the wheel.

QUILL: A textile obstruction.

TESSA: A napkin.

The guests crowd around the cart, blocking the hallway. Tessa asks them to move. One guest says she cannot leave until she knows whether the patient is stable. Puck emerges from the room holding a sandwich and says he is stable enough to choose mustard. The guests part to let him through.

SCENE 4. INT. NURSES' STATION, NIGHT

Puck signs the discharge form. Quill asks if he remembers the exact moment he felt faint. Puck says it was when he stood to make a toast after skipping dinner. Vale looks at the monitor. Tessa unplugs it and points to the sandwich tray.

VALE: What if it happens again?

TESSA: He eats before speeches.

QUILL: The treatment plan is remarkably concise.

Quill prepares a case presentation. His board has three headings: MYSTERY, TWIST, and VERY RARE. Tessa erases them and writes: skipped dinner, stood quickly, tight tie. Quill objects that the story needs a reveal. Puck loosens the tie and breathes normally.

PUCK: Is that the reveal?

TESSA: It's the explanation.

VALE: Could we add a second act?

TESSA: You can fill out the incident report.

Vale and Quill reach for the pen at once. Their sleeves catch on the tea cart handle. The cart rolls a few inches. Vale shouts "Code blue!" Tessa catches the cart, applies the brake, and gives them both forms to complete.

SCENE 5. INT. HOSPITAL GALA, NEXT DAY

The hospital manager posts a sign at the buffet: PLEASE EAT BEFORE STANDING FOR A TOAST. Guests read it and take sandwiches. Quill asks whether the sign is evidence-based. Tessa shows him the incident report. He reads it aloud without adding a twist.

The gala resumes. Puck wears a larger jacket and eats a sandwich before the speeches. Quill gives a talk about recognizing basic symptoms. He nearly calls the tie adjustment a breakthrough, but Tessa raises an eyebrow. He corrects himself.

QUILL: He needed water, food, and room to breathe.

The guests applaud. A manager asks Vale to describe the case for the hospital newsletter. Vale starts with "It was a dark and stormy evening" and is interrupted by the tea cart squeaking. He takes a wrench and tightens the wheel.

Puck brings an extra sandwich to the nurses station, where the team is filling out forms. Vale accepts it and finally stops narrating long enough to eat.

FINAL IMAGE: Vale pushes the cart down the hallway. It rolls silently. He looks disappointed until Tessa hands him a cup of tea, which he narrates to nobody.
