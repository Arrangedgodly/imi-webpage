# The Airship That Learned to Land

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sky-pirate adventures, airship battles, dramatic engine failures, and captains who insist every landing is a crash landing*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque
PILOT LUMA, a lemur
ENGINEER ODO, a gibbon
NAVIGATOR NARI, an orangutan
COURIER PIP, a capuchin

SCENE 1. EXT. SKY ABOVE THE VALLEY - MORNING

An airship drifts toward a mountain ridge. Captain Vex stands at the bow, pointing his telescope at a distant cloud. Pilot Luma checks the wind gauge. Engineer Odo says the engine is working but the landing gear is stuck. Courier Pip holds a package marked FRAGILE.

VEX: Prepare for a crash landing.

LUMA: Prepare for a landing.

The airship's dramatic music begins. Nari checks the map and finds a wide field below.

SCENE 2. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - LATER

The crew follows the checklist. Odo tests the gear lever. It moves halfway, then stops. Pip secures the package in a padded crate. Vex tries to help by pulling the lever harder. Luma stops him and asks Odo to inspect the cable.

NARI: The field is two miles east.

VEX: We are drifting west.

LUMA: We can turn with the wind.

The crew releases a small amount of ballast. A sack of theatrical props drops into the valley and lands in a haystack. Vex calls it a supply drop. Pip watches the package and confirms it is still secure.

SCENE 2A. INT. AIRSHIP ENGINE ROOM - NOON

Odo examines the landing gear while Luma holds the ship in a wide circle above the valley. The gear cable is caught on a bracket, not broken. Odo loosens the bracket and checks the pulley. The crew hears the engine sputter. Vex asks whether that means the ship is falling. Odo says it means the fuel valve is uneven. Pip secures the fragile package and closes the cargo net.

NARI: Wind is shifting toward the field.

LUMA: I can keep us in the updraft.

Vex asks to fire a flare. Pip finds the flare kit is packed under the package, and it would need to be unstrapped. The crew decides not to disturb the cargo. Nari signals the ground team by lantern instead.

SCENE 3. EXT. AIRSHIP DECK - AFTERNOON

Odo repairs the landing gear cable while the ship circles above the field. Vex asks if the crew can fire a flare to signal the ground team. Luma checks the signal kit and finds only a lantern. She flashes a message in three short bursts. The ground team responds with a matching light.

Pip asks how they will land without the gear. Odo says the gear is repaired. He tests it; the wheels lower into place. Nari checks the wind and gives Luma the direction. The airship begins its descent.

SCENE 3A. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - AFTERNOON

The package crate slides a few inches as the airship banks. Pip straps it to a rail and checks the seal. Vex offers to hold the box on his lap. Pip says the crate has a cargo net for that purpose. Vex fastens the net. The box stays in place through a gust.

PIP: It is a bell for the school.

VEX: The package has a future.

Luma watches the wind gauge and asks the crew to prepare for a crosswind. Odo lowers the landing gear and checks the indicator. All three lights turn green. The crew reviews the landing checklist aloud: gear, wind, field, cargo. Vex finally sits and holds the telescope case closed.

SCENE 4. EXT. FIELD - SUNSET

The airship approaches the field. Vex shouts for everyone to brace. Luma asks him to sit down and secure the telescope. The ship touches ground gently. The landing gear holds. Odo opens the cargo door. Pip delivers the package to the waiting post clerk; it contains a replacement bell for the valley school.

VEX: No crash?

LUMA: A successful landing.

The ground team unloads the cargo. The airship's propellers stop. Vex tries to describe the approach as daring, but Nari points out that the crew followed the wind chart and the checklist.

SCENE 5. INT. AIRSHIP CABIN - NIGHT

The crew logs the repair. Odo tags the cable for future inspection. Pip signs for the delivery. Vex removes the dramatic music score from the mission report. Luma tells him he can keep the title "Captain" even if the ship lands safely. Pip returns the shipping receipt to its pouch. He asks Luma to sign the delivery record before they leave. He stores the strap with the repaired gear and checks that the cargo is still sealed.

FINAL IMAGE: The airship rests in the field beneath a sunset, its landing gear planted squarely on the ground.
