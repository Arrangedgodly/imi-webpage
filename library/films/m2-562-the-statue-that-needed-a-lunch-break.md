# The Statue That Needed a Lunch Break

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: art-house films where a sculptor's process becomes more important than the sculpture*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
SCULPTOR PIP, a capuchin
CURATOR MARA, a macaque
CRITIC VEX, an orangutan
ASSISTANT ODO, a gibbon
VISITOR NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. GALLERY WORKSHOP - DAY

A clay statue is half-finished. Pip sets down a wooden tool and stretches both hands.

VEX: The artist has abandoned the form.

PIP: I am taking a break. The clay is covered, and the tools are clean.

MARA: The gallery closes in an hour. The statue stays in the workshop overnight.

NARI: The display card says "work in progress." It does not promise a finished face today.

VEX: The unfinished brow conveys the burden of time.

ODO: The brow is unfinished because Pip stopped for lunch.

SCENE 2. INT. WORKSHOP - LATER

Pip returns with a bowl of fruit. Vex circles the statue, taking notes.

VEX: The missing ear is a rejection of symmetry.

PIP: I haven't added the ear yet. The armature needs to dry before I attach it.

MARA: Keep the bowl away from the clay. The table is small.

NARI: A visitor asked if the statue will be in the show tonight.

MARA: Tell them the finished piece is scheduled for next month.

VEX: I can write a wall label about the courage of incompletion.

PIP: Write that the clay needs time to dry. That's true.

SCENE 2A. INT. GALLERY WORKSHOP - AFTERNOON

The clay is covered under a damp cloth. Pip opens the studio schedule and points to the next work session.

PIP: I have twenty minutes today to attach the ear. Then it needs to dry overnight.

VEX: The audience expects a reveal.

MARA: The audience expects to see a work in progress. The card says exactly that.

ODO: The humidifier has been checked and the water tray is full. The clay will not dry too quickly.

NARI: A visitor asked whether the missing ear means the subject cannot hear criticism.

PIP: The ear is missing because I haven't attached it. The rest of the form is finished.

VEX: I can ask the audience to judge the sculpture's emotional honesty.

MARA: Ask them to notice the tool marks and the way the unfinished part changes the silhouette. Don't assign feelings to the subject.

Pip pinches a small piece of clay and smooths it onto the head. Odo holds a small support while it adheres.

PIP: The ear is attached. I won't smooth the seam until the clay firms.

VEX: This is the moment the portrait hears its destiny.

PIP: It's the moment the seam needs a support.

Nari places a clear sign beside the bay: PLEASE DO NOT TOUCH; WORK IN PROGRESS.

SCENE 2B. INT. GALLERY WORKSHOP - AFTERNOON

The critic's camera battery flashes low. Vex asks Odo for a charger, but Odo points to the workshop notice: no cables near the humidifier.

ODO: Charge the camera in the office. The water tray is on this table.

VEX: The documentary is losing power at the crucial moment.

MARA: The documentary can wait while Pip finishes the seam.

NARI: The gallery opens in ten minutes. We can leave the work bay closed and display the process card outside.

PIP: The seam is stable. I'll remove the support tomorrow, after the clay dries.

VEX: What if the ear falls overnight?

PIP: The support is holding it. It won't be moved.

Mara closes the bay cover. Odo checks the sign and the humidifier. The camera cable stays in the office.

MARA: Work session complete. The sculpture remains unfinished by design and by schedule.

PIP: The ear can dry overnight in the covered bay.

MARA: The gallery will open with the process card in place.

SCENE 3. INT. GALLERY - EVENING

Odo wheels the statue into the designated work bay. Mara checks the cover and the humidifier level.

ODO: Humidity is stable. No one touched the armature.

VEX: The audience will never see the struggle.

NARI: They can see the tools, the stages, and the note explaining the process.

PIP: I will attach the ear after the clay is firm. The finished surface should not crack.

MARA: The bay is closed now. We can open it for the next scheduled work session.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY - NEXT DAY

The statue has a new ear, still rough. Pip applies no paint. A small card marks today's step.

VEX: A dramatic transformation.

PIP: A new piece of clay, attached carefully.

NARI: I like that it still shows the tool marks.

MARA: Those marks are part of the surface. Pip will decide when it is ready.

TAG: Vex adds a note to his review: "Artist took a lunch break; critic also recommends lunch."
