# Last Call at the Carrot Stand

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: zombie-apocalypse movies, supply-run montages, and grim survival leadership*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A macaque vendor tries to keep a farmers market open when a trio of drowsy clinic patients are mistaken for the undead, while the mayor's survival plan traps everyone behind their own barricade.

Cast: Nita, a macaque carrot vendor; Grub, a gorilla security guard; Pesto, a capuchin chef; Mayor Plink, a nervous squirrel; Nurse Leda, a slow-talking sloth; and three confused market visitors.

SCENE 1. EXT. FARMERS MARKET, MORNING

Nita stacks carrots as the market bell rings. Three pale visitors shuffle toward a honey stand, groaning softly. Grub raises a broom. Pesto lifts a soup pot like a shield. The first visitor asks for a sample and promptly falls asleep standing up.

GRUB: The dead have come for honey.

NITA: If they pay, they're customers.

Mayor Plink runs in wearing a colander helmet and carrying a hand-drawn map. It shows a thick black line around the market and a small arrow labeled SAFE EXIT. The arrow points into a locked storage shed.

PLINK: We retreat behind the barricade.

NITA: To where?

PLINK: Behind it.

SCENE 2. EXT. MARKET SQUARE, CONTINUOUS

Grub and Pesto stack produce crates across the central aisle. The shoppers flee toward the exit, which is now blocked. Nita moves the crates aside to make a walkway. The drowsy visitors follow it, pausing at every stall to read the signs. Mayor Plink mistakes their slow progress for a tactical encirclement and orders a second barricade.

A crate of garlic rolls across the path. The visitors stop and wait. Pesto declares the garlic has repelled them. Nita picks up the crate and asks them to step around it. They do. Plink orders Pesto to document the garlic defense anyway.

The mayor's banner unfurls: ZOMBIE RESPONSE: DO NOT PANIC. It covers the market map, including the exit. Grub holds the banner up while Nita guides the crowd through. Plink insists this is an evacuation corridor. A shopper points out that it leads to the honey sample table.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY KITCHEN, AFTERNOON

Nurse Leda arrives with a clinic leaflet. She checks the visitors and explains a new medication has made them drowsy and muddled. None has been bitten. They came to ask the clinic about the side effect but followed a market sign instead.

LEDA: They'll be all right after a medical review.

PLINK: Can the review confirm they are not zombies?

LEDA: It can confirm they need their prescriptions checked.

Plink unfolds his banner and asks Leda to pose beneath it. She refuses. Nita offers the visitors water and chairs. Pesto serves soup, then tries to garnish each bowl with garlic as a protective measure. The visitors thank him and ask for less garlic.

Grub puts a sign beside the door: CLINIC THIS WAY. Plink adds a skull-and-crossbones. Nita turns the sign over and writes the route clearly. The mayor watches her erase the skull and says the crowd needs a symbol. Grub draws a simple arrow. It works.

SCENE 4. EXT. MARKET, EVENING

The visitors prepare to return to the clinic. One remembers their bus route; the others disagree about whether it was Route Four or Fourteen. They check their tickets together. Plink hovers nearby, hoping for a statement that will preserve his emergency-management reputation.

PLINK: We contained the outbreak.

LEDA: You contained the customers.

Plink looks at the crates, now stacked around his podium. He asks Grub to help move them. The podium rolls downhill, carrying the mayor toward the honey stall. Nita catches its wheel with a bag of carrots. The visitors patiently hold the door while everyone frees Plink.

Nita gives them a market map with the clinic route marked. The visitors promise to return after their follow-up appointment. Plink asks if this is an endorsement. They say they like the honey.

SCENE 5. EXT. FARMERS MARKET, NEXT MORNING

Before opening, Plink practices an address to the empty stalls. He calls the honey line a perimeter and his cap a command post. Nita hands him a price marker and tells him to label the carrots. He writes CARROT in enormous letters, then asks if this counts as public service. Grub points to the customers already waiting.

The market reopens. The mayor replaces his colander with a plain cap and unveils a new sign: WELCOME, PLEASE QUEUE. Grub keeps the broom for sweeping. Pesto removes the garlic from the soup. The three visitors arrive after their clinic visit and join the honey line. Plink asks whether they feel better. They say yes, then ask where the bus stops.

Plink reads the clinic notice aloud without adding an exclamation point. It is his first accurate announcement.

FINAL IMAGE: Nita weighs carrots for the visitors. Mayor Plink tries a sample, groans theatrically, and asks for honey. Grub reaches for his broom; the mayor points at the price board and pays instead.
