# The Mayor's Parade Has No Route

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: political campaign films, public-relations crises, parade spectacle, and candidates who promise a ribbon-cutting before checking the street map*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
MAYOR PALLA, a gibbon
CAMPAIGN MANAGER VEX, a macaque
MAPMAKER NARI, an orangutan
DRUMMER PIP, a squirrel monkey
INSPECTOR ODO, a capuchin

SCENE 1. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MORNING

Mayor Palla announces a parade to celebrate the renovated bridge. A marching band tunes. Campaign manager Vex unfurls a map. It shows the parade passing over the bridge, through the market, and back to the square. Inspector Odo says the bridge is not open yet.

PALLA: It will be open by the parade.

ODO: The railing still needs inspection.

Vex says the map has already been printed on posters. He holds one up; the river has been labeled "scenic ribbon opportunity." Nari explains that the riverwalk is a public path, not a parade lane for floats. Palla asks whether the band can march it on foot. Pip demonstrates with a drumbeat and a turn. Vex tries to follow, spins the poster around, and ends up facing the office wall. Nari studies the street grid and notices the route crosses a road closed for construction.

SCENE 2. INT. CAMPAIGN OFFICE - LATER

Vex proposes moving the parade through the construction site because the scaffolding looks impressive. Odo says workers are actively repairing the road. Palla asks for an alternative route that still ends at the bridge. Nari draws a path through the park and along the riverwalk. Vex says it is not ceremonial enough.

PIP: The band can make any route ceremonial.

The band tests a drumbeat in the corridor. Palla asks them to move outside. The marchers practice the revised route, but one turn leads them back through the campaign office. Pip uses chalk arrows to mark the proper fork. Vex follows the arrows in the wrong direction, carrying his portrait banner upside down. Nari turns the map around and starts them again. It echoes through the office and rattles the campaign buttons off the table. Vex calls it an energetic soundscape. Nari asks them to rehearse outside so people can hear the inspection announcement.

SCENE 2A. EXT. TOWN SQUARE - MIDDAY

Odo takes the mayor and campaign crew to the bridge for an inspection walk. The crew brings a red ribbon and a pair of ceremonial scissors. The inspector begins checking bolts and railings but has not signed off on the deck surface. Vex asks whether the ceremonial scissors can tighten a bolt. Odo says no and demonstrates with the proper wrench; one loose fastener still needs repair. Palla waits while the inspector finishes each section. A photographer tries to stage her beside the bridge sign, but she steps aside so the repair crew can be seen.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIVERWALK - AFTERNOON

The parade begins along the revised path. Vex leads the march holding a giant poster with Palla's portrait. A breeze catches the poster and turns it into a sail. The campaign volunteers chase it along the riverwalk. Pip grabs one corner while Vex holds the other, and the banner stops turning in the wind. The band follows, playing faster to keep up.

Palla stops at the bridge entrance. Odo is still checking the loose fastener. Vex tries to cut a ribbon across the span, but Nari takes the scissors away. She points to the inspection crew checking a loose bolt. The crowd waits. Vex asks the band to play a victory march. Pip instead plays a quiet rhythm so the inspectors can hear each other.

SCENE 4. EXT. BRIDGE - LATER

Odo finishes the inspection and confirms the bridge is safe for foot traffic. The mayor walks across first, followed by the residents. There is no ribbon cutting. Palla thanks the repair crew and announces that the bridge is open. The band plays from the far bank.

Vex asks Palla to repeat the announcement for the campaign film. She says it once more, then points the camera toward the people crossing. One volunteer notices that the bridge's commemorative plaque is upside down. Nari asks the crew to fix it before the next parade.

SCENE 5. INT. CAMPAIGN OFFICE - EVENING

Vex revises the parade poster. He removes the old route and prints the park and riverwalk. Odo signs the inspection report. Palla asks whether the event counts as a success. Nari says the bridge opened safely, the road stayed clear, and nobody had to carry the portrait back from the river.

PIP: The band got its steps in.

The marching crew folds the banner carefully. Vex checks the map before they leave. It is right side up.

FINAL IMAGE: The parade poster dries on the office wall, showing a bridge full of people and a campaign portrait folded neatly in storage.
