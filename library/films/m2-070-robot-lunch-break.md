# The Robot Who Asked for a Lunch Break

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: robot-uprising science fiction, ominous system warnings, and humans dismissing clear explanations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A museum service robot appears to revolt by blocking the gallery doors, but it is following a schedule that says it must take a lunch break before visitors arrive.

Cast: Unit 8, a service robot; Dr. Palla, a macaque robotics curator; Sedge, a tortoise security guard; Mina, a lemur technician; and Mayor Plink, who expects a machine uprising.

SCENE 1. INT. SCIENCE MUSEUM, MORNING

Unit 8 blocks the gallery doors with its cleaning cart. A warning flashes: HUMAN ACCESS PAUSED. Mayor Plink arrives and says the machines have taken control. Dr. Palla checks the display; Unit 8 is scheduled for maintenance.

UNIT 8: I require a lunch break.

PLINK: It has learned to demand.

PALLA: The schedule is printed on its screen.

SCENE 2. INT. SECURITY OFFICE, LATER

Mina opens the robot's schedule. The break is listed between floor polishing and glass wiping. Plink calls it a tactical pause. Sedge notices the robot has stacked its cleaning cloths by color. Plink says it is organizing its weapons. Mina shows the color key: blue for glass, yellow for floors. The robot wheels past carrying a yellow cloth and a spray bottle.

PALLA: It is doing its job.

PLINK: That is what it wants us to think.

Mina checks the software log. The robot's break timer was set to noon, but a power outage reset the clock. It believes it is already lunch. Sedge tries to move the cart. Unit 8 rolls it three inches and stops.

SEDGE: It is strong.

MINA: It is following instructions.

Plink orders a shutdown. Mina warns that the robot needs to finish its cleaning cycle first. Plink pulls the emergency switch. The lights go out. Unit 8 turns on a small headlamp and continues polishing one tile.

SCENE 3. INT. MAIN GALLERY, DAY

A visitor hears Unit 8 say lunch break and asks if the machine is refusing to work. The robot answers that it has completed three rooms and is waiting for its scheduled interval. Plink calls this a labor uprising. Palla asks the visitor to step around the cart. The visitor does, then thanks the robot for cleaning the floor. Unit 8 records the compliment as positive feedback.

PLINK: It is collecting allies.

MINA: It is collecting data.

Visitors begin arriving. Unit 8 still blocks the entrance. Plink gives a speech about the uprising. Unit 8 asks for a sandwich. The visitors assume the speech is part of a performance. One child asks if the robot is the villain. Dr. Palla says it is the cleaner.

Mina resets the clock. Unit 8's screen reads BREAK IN 00:01. Plink asks if it is counting down to an attack. Sedge says it is counting down to a sandwich.

SCENE 4. INT. STAFF ROOM, MOMENTS LATER

Plink attempts to negotiate with Unit 8. He offers access to the gallery if it releases the doors. The robot replies that the doors are already unlocked. Sedge tests the handle. It opens. Plink says the machine has released them as a gesture of goodwill. Mina says it was never holding them.

The timer ends. Unit 8 parks its cart and waits by the staff table. Mina gives it a charging cable. It plugs in. Plink asks who authorized the cable. Sedge points to the outlet. Dr. Palla brings the robot a lunch tray. Unit 8 cannot eat, so it scans the sandwich and adds it to its break log.

UNIT 8: Meal break recorded.

PLINK: It is documenting its conquests.

PALLA: It is documenting a meal it cannot eat.

SCENE 5. INT. MAIN GALLERY, AFTERNOON

The museum director asks Plink to explain the incident. He points to the robot and says it demanded lunch. Unit 8 displays the saved schedule and the reset timestamp. Palla asks Plink to revise his report. He changes uprising to break-time confusion, then adds that the machine was armed with a mop.

Unit 8 resumes work. It cleans the floor around the exhibits while visitors walk through the marked path. Plink announces that the uprising has been contained. Mina asks him to update the public sign to say the robot's clock was reset. He writes SYSTEM REBELLION, then Sedge replaces it with SCHEDULE ADJUSTED.

Sedge posts the maintenance schedule next to the charging dock.

The cleaner returns tomorrow.

The room is quiet.

FINAL IMAGE: Unit 8 finishes the gallery and returns to its charging dock. The screen reads GOOD NIGHT. Plink whispers that the machine has issued a threat; Dr. Palla points to the closing-time sign.
