# The Pirate Map Made of Receipts

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: pirate treasure quests, weathered maps, mutiny speeches, and crews who mistake old receipts for cryptic clues*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
CAPTAIN PIP, a capuchin
FIRST MATE VEX, a macaque
NAVIGATOR NARI, an orangutan
COOK LUMA, a lemur
DOCKMASTER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. HARBOR - DAWN

Captain Pip unrolls a water-stained map on the ship's deck. The map is covered in faded numbers and a large X. First mate Vex announces that the crew has found directions to buried treasure. Navigator Nari recognizes the paper as a stack of old receipts.

PIP: The X is still there.

LUMA: It marks the fish stall.

The crew sails toward the stall. The tide is low, so the boat stops at the dock. Dockmaster Odo asks them to pay the harbor fee before they leave.

SCENE 2. INT. SHIP CABIN - MORNING

Nari sorts the papers by date. The receipts show purchases from a chandlery, bakery, and boat repair shop. Vex reads the totals as coordinates. Pip decides the treasure is hidden where the crew spent the most money. The receipts point to a rope shop.

Luma says the shop sold them anchor line last year. Odo checks the harbor ledger and confirms the crew's account is overdue by one coin. Vex asks if the fee is a toll on the treasure. Odo says it is a dock fee.

SCENE 2A. INT. SHIP CABIN - LATE MORNING

Vex divides the receipts among the crew. He asks each person to look for a code. Luma finds a bakery receipt with a note about cinnamon buns. Pip finds a chandlery bill with the ship's name. Nari spots the dates and sorts the receipts in order. The purchases tell the story of a repaired mast, a crew meal, and a rope order. There is no treasure route.

VEX: The clues are disguised as purchases.

NARI: The purchases are the clues.

Pip finds the map's X stamped beside the total. It marks the bill as paid. Vex says the stamp could still point to a hidden chest. Odo radios from the dock that the rope shop is open until two, so the crew should collect their coil before closing.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROPE SHOP - NOON

The crew examines the receipt. A grease stain makes the address look like a symbol. Nari holds it up to the sun and reads the shop's name. The rope seller checks the ledger and confirms the crew left both a coil and a wooden pulley after a repair. Neither is treasure, but both belong to the ship. Vex suspects a hidden chest. Pip asks to inspect the inventory; the seller opens the storage chest, which holds rope, pulleys, and repair invoices. The receipt stamp is simply proof that the bill was paid.

SCENE 3A. EXT. ROPE SHOP - EARLY AFTERNOON

The crew retrieves the recorded coil and pulley from the shop. Luma tests the pulley wheel; it turns freely. Nari checks the rope length against the ship's rigging plan, and Pip marks both items on their inventory. Vex searches the chest once more for a secret compartment. It contains only the shop's stock and a bundle of invoices. The seller closes it and points to the door. Odo arrives from the harbor office with the overdue dock-fee bill. He shows them the current harbor chart and points out the labeled shops.

Pip carries the pulley and Luma carries the coil back to the dock. Vex walks beside them with an empty treasure sack, holding its mouth open whenever anyone passes.

SCENE 4. INT. SHIP CABIN - AFTERNOON

The crew returns the rope to the ship. Nari folds the receipts into a file. Pip draws a real map of the harbor, marking the rope shop, bakery, and dock office. Vex asks whether it can still lead to treasure. Luma points at the ship's supplies and says a spare anchor line is valuable enough.

Odo collects the overdue harbor fee. Pip pays with one coin. Odo stamps the last receipt, and the crew adds it to the map pile. Vex stares at the new X.

VEX: Is that a treasure mark?

ODO: It is a paid stamp.

SCENE 5. EXT. HARBOR - SUNSET

The ship departs. The crew uses the updated chart to navigate the channel. Pip keeps the receipts dry in a box. Vex gives a speech about the adventure, but Nari interrupts to point out a buoy. Pip marks it on the chart and steers around it. Vex carries the rope into the cabin and places it in a large chest. He locks the lid and pockets the key.

VEX: The treasure is secure.

LUMA: We need that line for the sail.

Vex reluctantly unlocks the chest. Luma takes the coil onto the deck. Vex looks into the empty chest, then drops the paid receipt inside.

PIP: Will you guard one coin less than nothing?

VEX: Someone has to.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex stands guard over the chest with his sword drawn. On deck, the actual recovered rope lifts the sail above him.
