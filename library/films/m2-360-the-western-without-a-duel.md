# The Western Without a Duel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: frontier westerns, saloon standoffs, wanted posters, and towns that settle every dispute by facing off at high noon*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
SHERIFF NARI, an orangutan
RANGER VEX, a macaque
SALOON OWNER PIP, a capuchin
MAIL CARRIER LUMA, a lemur
TOWN CLERK ODO, a gibbon
CHICKEN, a silent performer

SCENE 1. EXT. DRY CREEK - MORNING

Vex rides into town beneath an enormous hat. A wanted poster flaps against the sheriff's door. He dismounts in slow, deliberate stages. His coat catches on the saddle, ruining the final step. A chicken pecks the trailing hem.

VEX: I came for the outlaw who stole the mail.

NARI: Nobody stole it. Pip found the sack under a saloon bench after the dust storm. It is on Odo's desk.

Vex studies the poster. Its outlaw wears a striped scarf and a wide hat.

VEX: You have their likeness.

ODO: We reused a theater poster. The printer ran out of paper.

Nari turns the poster over. The other side reads A NIGHT WITH THE DUSTY BANDIT, ONE PERFORMANCE ONLY.

VEX: Then I will question the bandit at the performance.

PIP: That was last winter. We can offer you stew.

Vex looks down the empty street. The chicken follows his gaze.

SCENE 2. INT. SALOON - BEFORE NOON

Pip serves lunch. Odo puts the returned sack on a table and checks its contents against the delivery ledger. Luma enters wearing a striped scarf and a wide hat. Vex sets down his spoon.

VEX: Don't move.

LUMA: I have fourteen deliveries. I intend to move quite a lot.

NARI: Her clothes keep dust out. Half the town dresses like that.

Vex holds the poster beside Luma's face. Its villain has a mustache. Luma points to the mustache, then to her bare lip.

VEX: A clever alteration.

LUMA: A convincing lack of one.

Odo reads each address. Every envelope is present. He stamps SACK RETURNED in the ledger. Vex reaches for the stamp before the ink dries.

VEX: We have not reconstructed the crime.

PIP: I reconstructed the bench. The sack was underneath it. I lifted the seat.

VEX: At what hour?

PIP: Nine. The stew was still cold.

Vex sets three salt shakers around the sack, marking the positions of imaginary witnesses. Pip gathers them back onto the tray.

PIP: Those witnesses belong at table two.

SCENE 3. EXT. SALOON BOARDWALK - NOON

Vex faces Luma across the street. Neither carries a weapon. Vex rests one hand above his belt buckle. Luma rests one hand on her mail pouch. Odo steps between them with a route sheet.

ODO: This is the new delivery order. The storm closed the northern track.

VEX: I challenge her account.

NARI: You can challenge her delivery time. Whoever clears their route first rings the clerk's bell.

Luma takes six envelopes. Odo gives Vex six for houses along the same boardwalk. The addresses differ, but each route has six doors within sight of the saloon.

VEX: At high noon?

LUMA: At two minutes past, if you keep talking.

The tower clock finishes striking. Vex swings onto his horse. Luma walks to the nearest door, knocks and passes an envelope to its recipient. Vex spurs his horse toward the same short row, then discovers the hitching rail leaves no room to turn.

He dismounts, leads the horse behind the rail and starts again on foot. The chicken stands in his path. He draws out the wanted poster like an arrest warrant. The chicken pecks its lower corner.

NARI: It prefers the theater side.

SCENE 4. EXT. TOWN STREET - MOMENTS LATER

Vex strides toward the second doorway. He pauses to frame his shadow across the boards. Luma delivers her third letter behind him, then slips past as he turns toward the sun.

VEX: How did you get ahead?

LUMA: I delivered while you arranged the lighting.

Vex lifts an envelope and squints at its address. Wind catches his coat again. He pins its hem beneath a boot, bends for the letter and traps himself halfway down. Pip frees the fabric with a soup spoon.

PIP: Your dramatic coat has detained you.

Vex removes it. Without the coat, he delivers four letters quickly. He reaches the clerk's door with his final envelope just as Luma rings the bell.

ODO: Luma first. Vex second.

VEX: By how much?

ODO: One coat and a chicken.

Nari takes the wanted poster off the sheriff's wall. Odo writes MAIL ROUTE CHANGED on its reverse. Vex points to a remaining space beneath the title.

VEX: We could record the race result.

LUMA: The residents need directions, not your finishing position.

Odo adds both routes to the notice. Vex studies the six doors he has visited, then points out an omitted side entrance. Odo marks it with a small arrow.

SCENE 5. INT. SALOON - EVENING

Pip sets fresh stew before everyone. Vex folds his coat over an empty chair. The chicken enters through the swinging doors. Vex and the chicken stare at each other as though the town has reached its final showdown.

VEX: We meet again.

Pip drops a crumb beside the stove. The chicken walks away without accepting the challenge. Luma finishes her stew. Odo closes the stamped ledger.

NARI: The sack is accounted for. The route is updated. Anything else?

Vex reaches for the discarded theater poster.

VEX: When is the next performance?

PIP: Tomorrow. There is a part for a rider who wants to look important.

VEX: Does he get a duel?

PIP: He delivers an invitation.

Vex lowers the poster. The chicken settles on his folded coat.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex holds the invitation above the chicken, waiting for it to release his costume. Its eyes close before he can begin his speech.
