# The Fall Was Professionally Performed

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: action films that hide stunt doubles, repeat spectacular falls, and give screen heroes credit for dangerous work performed by specialists*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: An action star keeps asking to perform a roof fall himself until his stunt double turns the production's credit dispute into a safety demonstration.

Cast: Varo, a macaque action actor; Tesa, a gorilla stunt performer; Bex, a lemur director; and Puck, a capuchin camera operator.

SCENE 1. EXT. STUDIO BACKLOT, MORNING

Varo stands beside a padded landing mat below a low platform. Bex explains the shot: his character jumps from a roof, rolls, and runs away. Tesa has rehearsed the fall and marked the safe path.

VARO: I can do that.

TESA: You can do the close-up.

Varo asks to try the whole sequence. Tesa shows him where the platform edge ends and the mat begins. Puck turns the camera so the mat is outside the frame. Bex asks for a dramatic take. Varo steps onto the platform, looks down, and steps back.

VARO: Can we raise the platform?

TESA: It is already low.

BEX: We can fake height with the lens.

Tesa asks Puck to keep the mat visible during rehearsal. Puck says it will spoil the illusion. Tesa says the mat is the point of rehearsal. Varo asks if he can use a wire. The wire rig is not attached yet. Puck draws a line in the air with his finger to show the camera's path. Bex asks him to mark the actual lens position instead. He sets a tape marker, and Tesa checks that it does not cross the mat.

TESA: This is where I land.

VARO: It looks close to the wall.

TESA: The wall is padded too.

Varo tests the padding with one finger. It compresses. He asks if the audience can see it. Bex says they do not need to hit it.

SCENE 2. EXT. BACKLOT ROOF SET, LATER

The crew secures the safety line. Tesa clips into the harness and checks each connection with the rigger. Varo watches, then asks if he can do the jump once the camera is rolling. Bex says the stunt is assigned to Tesa.

VARO: I want the audience to know it is me.

TESA: They can know my name too.

Puck films Tesa rehearsing the fall into the mat. She lands, rolls, and stands. Varo asks if he can repeat it for a reaction shot. Tesa says the mat needs inspection after each fall. The rigger checks it. Bex waits while the crew repositions the camera.

VARO: That takes longer than the fall.

TESA: That is why we rehearse.

Bex tries a low angle that makes the platform look tall. The mat disappears, but so does Tesa's landing zone. Tesa asks for the camera to move six inches. Puck moves it. The mat returns to frame, and the platform looks low again. Bex moves a false wall closer to the lens. It makes the platform seem higher, but covers the ladder Tesa uses to climb down. The crew stops to move the wall. Tesa checks the landing mat again, then walks the route from platform to mat at a normal pace.

TESA: Camera, wall, ladder, mat. That is the order.

Puck marks each position with tape. Varo follows the marks on the ground and stays clear of the rig.

SCENE 3. EXT. BACKLOT ALLEY, AFTERNOON

The scene runs. Varo delivers a line near the roof edge. Tesa performs the fall in costume. Puck follows her movement with the camera. She lands safely, rolls, and runs out of frame. Bex calls cut.

Varo asks to film the landing again so his face can be seen. Tesa says the harness needs another inspection. The rigger checks it and discovers a buckle is loose. Everyone stops. Tesa removes the harness and shows the crew the buckle.

BEX: We are done with the fall.

VARO: But we need the shot.

TESA: You have the shot. It is safe.

Puck plays the footage. The costume hides Tesa's face, but the landing is clear. Varo asks if they can cut to his close-up after she lands. Bex adds the shot. The edit makes Varo's face appear after Tesa's completed stunt.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The cast watches the finished scene. The credits list Varo as "Action Hero" and Tesa under "Additional Crew." Tesa asks Bex to correct the credit. Bex changes it to "Stunt Performer: Tesa." Varo agrees the credit should be visible.

The crew screens the take again. The audience sees the fall, the roll, and Varo's close-up. Puck asks whether they should add a sound effect to make the landing louder. Tesa says the mat did its job. Bex leaves the sound clean.

FINAL IMAGE: Tesa's name appears directly after Varo's in the action credit. The padded mat is folded and tagged for inspection. Tesa checks the tag and signs the inspection sheet. Varo signs below her name as the actor who performed the close-up. Bex asks if the credits can show both roles separately. Puck adjusts the card so neither name is hidden by the frame.
