# A Capuchin in Every Mirror

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: psychological thrillers, unreliable reflections, ominous double imagery, and protagonists who interpret ordinary mirrors as conspiracy evidence*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
MILO, a capuchin museum guard
EVA, a macaque conservator
PALLA, a gibbon director
ORRO, an orangutan visitor
THE REFLECTION, Milo's own image

SCENE 1. INT. MIRROR GALLERY - NIGHT

Milo walks through a gallery of antique mirrors. In the last mirror, his reflection appears to turn its head a fraction late. He freezes. A low musical note begins. He raises a flashlight; the image raises one too.

MILO: Who are you?

A cleaning cart squeaks behind him. Milo spins around. No one is there. In the mirror, his reflection still faces forward.

SCENE 2. INT. SECURITY ROOM - LATER

Milo reviews the footage. The camera feed is delayed by three seconds because the gallery runs through an old wireless relay. Eva explains that the mirrors are angled to make the room look longer. Palla, the night director for a documentary crew, asks Milo to repeat the discovery for a better shot.

MILO: It was unsettling the first time.

PALLA: We need the audience to see it.

Milo walks past the mirror again. The reflection lags. Palla adds another musical note. Eva turns off the relay; the image now moves in real time. Palla asks her to turn it back on.

SCENE 3. INT. MIRROR GALLERY - NIGHT

The crew moves to the long corridor mirror. Milo walks in front of it while Eva watches from the side. The reflection appears to follow a different path. Eva puts a strip of tape on the floor, and Milo discovers he has been walking around a hidden velvet rope that bends across the reflected aisle. Palla asks whether the rope can remain out of frame. Eva says it is there to keep visitors away from the glass.

MILO: The reflection is not following me. I am following the rope.

PALLA: It is more frightening if we do not know.

EVA: Visitors need to know where to walk.

She shifts the rope to a clear line. The illusion disappears, but the room is safer. Milo leaves a note for the next guard: "Check the angle before confronting the double." Palla asks the camera operator to film the mirror from the opposite angle. The reflection now shows the crew itself, including Palla looking worried at her own reflection.

 INT. MIRROR GALLERY - NIGHT

A second figure appears in the mirror, standing behind Milo. He turns; the gallery is empty. Eva inspects the corner and finds a small convex mirror mounted in the frame. It reflects a display case from the opposite wall, creating a false hallway.

Milo walks toward the apparent hallway and stops at the mirror. He taps the glass. His reflection seems to tap back. The sound comes from a loose panel behind the frame. Eva opens it and finds a fan used to keep the gallery cool. The motor's vibration makes the mirror tremble.

Palla asks for a reveal. Milo holds a card in front of the mirror; the reflection shows it backward. He tries to read the letters. The camera makes a dramatic zoom on the reversed words: EXIT THIS WAY. The sign is reversed because it faces the mirror.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY - MOMENTS LATER

Orro enters, looking for the exit. He follows the reflected sign and approaches the wall. Milo catches him before he bumps the display case. Eva turns the sign around so it points to the real door. Orro thanks them and says the room always feels like it has another corridor.

Milo checks the monitor once more. It now moves in real time. He waves; his reflection waves with him. Palla keeps staring at the screen as if it has revealed a secret, then slowly waves too.

SCENE 5. INT. SECURITY ROOM - DAWN

The crew powers down the relay and secures the loose mirror panel. Eva records the maintenance note. Palla wants a final line about the boundary between self and image. Milo says the mirror shows where the light is, not where someone is hiding. Palla asks him to say it with more dread. Milo repeats it in a normal voice and leaves for breakfast. Eva writes "light off, gallery closed" on the switch panel. Palla asks if that is the final line. Eva switches off the work light; the monitor goes dark at the same time.

Orro returns to the gallery and sees his own reflection. He waves once, then walks to the actual exit. Palla lets the camera hold on the empty mirror. The cooling fan starts, making the image tremble slightly. Palla begins a spooky music cue; Eva unplugs it.

FINAL IMAGE: Milo's reflection stays still in the dark glass after the lights go out, then disappears when the night guard closes the gallery door.
