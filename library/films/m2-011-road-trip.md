# Two Monkeys, One Map

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: road-trip buddy films, mismatched travel styles, and roadside detours*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: Two monkey cousins drive across the state for their aunt's birthday, but one follows a handwritten map while the other trusts every roadside attraction, turning a simple trip into a parade of wrong exits and local advice.

Cast: Rafi, a capuchin who plans every mile; Milo, a macaque who believes signs are invitations; Aunt Juno, a mandrill waiting at the destination; Bev, a tortoise mechanic; and a cardboard moose named Dale.

SCENE 1. EXT. GAS STATION, MORNING

Rafi unfolds a map across the hood of a small car. Milo buys a souvenir compass from the register. The compass points toward the candy rack.

RAFI: We have one route and three hours.

MILO: We have a compass and unlimited possibilities.

They leave. Milo turns the compass upside down. Rafi corrects him. They take the wrong exit anyway because a sign says MOOSE VIEWING, NEXT RIGHT.

SCENE 2. EXT. ROADSIDE MOOSE PARK, LATER

A cardboard moose stands beside a gift shop. Milo poses beside it. The moose's head tips forward, landing on the car roof. Rafi uses the map to find the nearest mechanic. The shop owner insists the moose is a local landmark and cannot be moved.

They prop its head with a broom and continue. The broom sticks out the window. Other drivers wave. Milo waves back, assuming they admire the moose.

SCENE 2A. EXT. MOOSE PARK, CONTINUOUS

The owner offers to sell them a map of local attractions. Rafi asks for the highway. The owner unfolds a brochure that lists six statues, a scenic gravel pit, and the moose park they are standing in. Milo buys three brochures as souvenirs.

They discover the car's back seat is full of tiny wooden moose. The shop owner explains they are a promotional set. Milo wants to take one. Rafi says there is no room. Milo stacks the moose on top of the cardboard moose head. The whole arrangement blocks the rear window. Rafi makes him put everything in a box.

At the highway entrance, Milo sees a sign for an apple orchard. He asks if they should stop. Rafi says no. Milo points out they have missed the correct exit. Rafi checks the map and admits the orchard road is the shortcut Bev mentioned. They turn around.

SCENE 3. INT. CAR, MIDDAY

Rafi insists they are behind schedule. Milo insists they are near the world's largest spoon. Rafi says the map shows no spoon. Milo points through the window. They are passing a diner with a painted spoon on the roof.

They stop for lunch. The spoon sign rotates in the wind and points toward the highway. Milo calls it a sign. Rafi says it is a sign for soup. A waitress hears them and gives directions. She draws a shortcut on a napkin, but the map is printed on the other side of the napkin, causing both cousins to argue over which route is correct.

SCENE 3A. INT. DINER, LATER

The waitress gives them a road atlas. Rafi studies it while Milo asks locals where they are going. Every person gives different advice. A truck driver says take the river road. A child says follow the blue truck. A dog barks at the window. Milo writes all directions on the map until it is unreadable.

Rafi asks for the atlas back. Milo shows a neat list of landmarks on a napkin. It is surprisingly useful. Rafi admits the list is better than the old map. Milo admits the compass is still pointing at candy.

SCENE 4. EXT. COUNTY ROAD, AFTERNOON

The car sputters. Bev the tortoise mechanic arrives in a roadside service truck. She checks the engine and finds a loose hose. Rafi offers tools. Milo holds the hood. The cardboard moose head rolls out from the roof and lands in Bev's truck.

Bev repairs the hose and asks where they are going. Rafi names the town. Bev says the bridge is closed. The map is five years old. Milo flips it over. The back is a child's drawing of a dragon. He claims it is a route map with a legend.

SCENE 5. INT. CAR, SUNSET

They ask a farmer for directions. The farmer gives them a direct route but warns them about a low railroad bridge. Rafi measures the car. Milo measures the cardboard moose. They remove the moose head, carry it through the bridge by hand, and reattach it on the other side.

They arrive late at Aunt Juno's house. She has already served dinner. She says she expected them to get lost and kept the soup warm. Milo gives her the compass. It still points toward the candy in his pocket.

SCENE 6. INT. DINER, NEXT MORNING

On the return journey, Rafi spreads the new map on the counter. The waitress points out that the bridge is still closed and the orchard road is the only open route. Milo has already ordered pie for the road.

RAFI: We should check the directions.
MILO: I checked. The pie is local.

The waitress draws a clear route on the napkin. Milo asks whether it passes the moose park. She says no. He asks if the moose park can move. She gives him a second napkin with a picture of the moose and a firm arrow toward the exit.

They leave with the map, the pie, and a charged phone. Rafi drives. Milo keeps the compass in the glove box, where it points at the map's metal clip.

FINAL IMAGE: At their next rest stop, the cousins prop Aunt Juno's birthday photograph against the dashboard. The cardboard moose had its own place at her dinner table. Rafi unfolds the updated return map, which Milo has drawn on the back of the diner menu. The path circles the moose park twice.
