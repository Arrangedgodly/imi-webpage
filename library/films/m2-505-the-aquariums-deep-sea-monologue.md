# The Aquarium's Deep Sea Monologue

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: nature documentaries that lend grand narration to a small animal's very ordinary search for food*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A documentary crew narrates a tiny crab's journey across an aquarium tank as an epic survival saga, while the keeper repeatedly points out the feeding dish.

Cast: narrator Koro, a gibbon; keeper Lani, an orangutan; camera operator Sefa, a lemur; educator Piri, a macaque; visitor Deno, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - INT. AQUARIUM TANK ROOM - MORNING

Koro stands before a tank containing one small crab and a shallow dish. Sefa frames the crab through the glass. Lani checks the feeding chart.

KORO (V.O.): Across the abyss, a lone hunter begins its perilous crossing.

LANI: The dish is two inches away.

Sefa zooms in. The crab raises one claw and moves sideways. Koro lowers his voice to a whisper. Piri leads a group of visitors into the room and asks them to remain quiet for the recording.

SCENE 2 - INT. TANK ROOM - CONTINUOUS

The crab pauses beside a rock. Koro describes a dangerous predator lurking beyond the reef. Lani points to a plastic plant and a snail. The crab continues toward the dish. Sefa adjusts focus, but the camera catches the room reflected in the glass. The visitors appear enormous behind the tiny crab.

KORO (V.O.): Giants shadow the explorer.

PIRI: Those are the visitors.

Deno waves at the reflection. The crab turns toward the movement and walks away from the dish. Koro narrates a sudden diversion. Lani asks Deno to hold still. Deno freezes in a dramatic pose.

SCENE 3 - INT. AQUARIUM EDUCATION ROOM - LATER

Koro reviews the footage. The crab's route now seems to cross a mountain range, a forest and a cliff, because the decorations are close together in the small tank. He asks Sefa to add thunder. Sefa plays a soft filter hum. Koro says the creature faces a storm. Lani turns off the filter to clean it. The tank becomes silent.

LANI: It is just cleaning time.

KORO: The storm has passed.

SCENE 2A - INT. TANK ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Sefa tries to film the crab from above. The tank lid reflects the lights, placing a bright moon over the miniature reef. Koro asks for a night scene. Lani switches off one lamp, and the crab retreats beneath the rock. The lens catches only a claw.

KORO (V.O.): The hunter disappears into the darkness.

Lani lifts the rock for a moment to confirm the crab is safe. Koro calls it an emerging cave. Sefa moves the camera to the side and sees the crab's full body under the plastic plant. The crew returns the rock and keeps the lamp at its original brightness.

SCENE 4 - INT. TANK ROOM - AFTERNOON

The crab reaches the dish, climbs onto the rim and slips back into the sand. Koro begins a triumphant speech. The crab circles the dish once more and pauses. Lani moves the dish closer to the crab's path. Koro objects that this would change the documentary. Piri says the animal's food should not be moved for the camera. Lani leaves it where it is.

Sefa frames the dish. The crab climbs in and eats. Koro narrates the victory as though the crab has conquered a continent. Deno asks whether the creature is still hungry. Lani checks the chart and says it has finished its portion.

After the screening, Deno asks whether the crab completed its journey. Lani checks the tank and finds it beside the same dish where it began. Koro writes a new voice-over: the creature rests after a journey across two inches. Sefa shows the audience the original footage without the music. Koro lowers his voice for the final step, and the visitors hear only the tank filter humming. The crab takes one last step toward the dish, and the visitors lean close enough to see it plainly.

Piri posts a small sign beside the screen explaining the tank's scale. A visitor compares the crab on film with the live crab behind the glass. The animal turns sideways. Koro starts to narrate, then lets the filter and the visitor's quiet laugh fill the room.

SCENE 5 - INT. AQUARIUM LOBBY - EVENING

The crew presents the short documentary to visitors. The children cheer when the crab reaches the dish. Koro accepts applause for his narration. Lani hands out aquarium maps, showing the tank's actual dimensions. Sefa labels the footage "Crab: Across Two Inches." Koro changes the title to "The Great Crossing" for the poster.

FINAL IMAGE: The crab rests beside its dish beneath a miniature mountain, while a visitor reads the tank label and leans in for a closer look.
