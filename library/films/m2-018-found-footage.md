# Cabin Camera, Please Stop

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: found-footage horror mysteries, shaky camera clues, and documentary overreaction*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: Three monkey friends investigate strange sounds in a rented cabin using a handheld camera, but the most frightening evidence is the footage of themselves forgetting where they put the camera.

Cast: Tessa, a macaque who films everything; Bo, a capuchin who believes every creak has intent; Remy, a skeptical langur; and the cabin's smart thermostat, which keeps changing modes.

SCENE 1. INT. CABIN, EVENING

Tessa records the group entering. The camera shakes as Bo opens the door. A floorboard creaks. Bo freezes.

BO: The cabin knows we're here.

REMY: The floor is wood.

Tessa films the thermostat. It clicks on. The display says ECO MODE. Bo whispers that the house is conserving energy for something.

SCENE 2. INT. KITCHEN, NIGHT

The refrigerator hums. Tessa turns the camera toward the sound. The image tilts, then shows the ceiling. Remy takes the camera and points it at the fridge. A magnet falls. Everyone gasps.

The magnet reads "SERVICE TECH." Bo asks if the technician is inside the fridge. Remy opens it. There are leftovers and a cold light.

SCENE 3. INT. HALLWAY, LATER

A shadow moves across the wall. Bo runs. Tessa follows, filming the floor. Remy catches up and turns on the lamp. The shadow belongs to Bo's coat hanging from a hook. Bo says he knew it was a coat but wanted the footage.

They discover a second shadow. It moves when the thermostat screen brightens. The screen reflects a hanging plant, which sways near the vent.

SCENE 4. INT. LIVING ROOM, MIDNIGHT

A loud thump wakes them. They film themselves searching the cabin. Tessa asks where the camera is. Remy points to Tessa's hand. She has been filming the entire time.

They review the footage. It shows Bo placing a snack tin on a shelf, then forgetting it and accusing the cabin of moving it. They replay the clip. Bo says the cabin may have planted the memory.

SCENE 5. INT. CABIN, MORNING

The thermostat switches to Away mode. The temperature drops. Remy turns it off. The group realizes the cabin owner has set an automatic schedule. Tessa records an explanation for the final cut. The camera battery dies halfway through. Bo calls it a final warning.

SCENE 5A. INT. CABIN, LATER

The group reviews their clips and tries to narrate the evidence.

TESSA: This is the front door.

BO: It opened by itself.

REMY: You opened it.

TESSA: I was filming.

BO: You filmed the handle.

REMY: That's still filming.

BO: The creak was louder.

TESSA: The mic was close.

REMY: It's a floorboard.

BO: The floorboard knows.

TESSA: I lost the camera.

REMY: It's in your hand.

TESSA: Then who filmed me?

BO: The cabin.

REMY: That was the timer.

TESSA: Why is the timer filming?

REMY: It isn't.

BO: Check the footage.

TESSA: I see the ceiling.

REMY: You pointed upward.

BO: A clue.

TESSA: The clue is my elbow.

BO: It looks suspicious.

REMY: Your elbow looks normal.

BO: That is what it wants.

TESSA: The thermostat changed.

REMY: You pressed eco.

BO: To save us?

TESSA: To save electricity.

REMY: We should leave.

BO: Without the snacks?

TESSA: Pack the snacks.

REMY: Keep the camera off.

BO: What if it follows?

TESSA: Then it can carry bags.

They pack. Bo puts the camera in a tote, then asks who is filming. Remy takes it out and points it at the door. The door remains still. Everyone applauds quietly.

SCENE 5B. EXT. CABIN PORCH, MORNING

They set the camera on a tripod and record a final explanation.

TESSA: We found no ghost.

BO: We found a shadow.

REMY: It was your coat.

BO: A suspicious coat.

TESSA: The thermostat changed.

REMY: You changed it.

BO: Under influence.

REMY: From the manual?

BO: From the cabin.

TESSA: The magnet fell.

REMY: The fridge shut.

BO: The fridge did it.

TESSA: We lost the camera.

REMY: You held it.

BO: That part is unclear.

TESSA: We found the snacks.

BO: That part is clear.

REMY: We should leave.

TESSA: We are packed.

BO: Is the door locked?

REMY: It is open.

BO: Is it safe?

TESSA: It's a door.

REMY: Walk through it.

BO: You first.

TESSA: I'm holding bags.

REMY: So am I.

BO: I have the camera.

TESSA: Put it down.

BO: Then who films us?

REMY: Nobody.

BO: That's unsettling.

TESSA: That's leaving.

They set the camera on the porch rail and carry the bags to the car. The camera records an empty porch for several seconds. A squirrel crosses the frame and steals the lens cap.

FINAL IMAGE: The friends pack and leave. The camera is still on a shelf, recording the empty room. The smart thermostat flashes "Welcome home." The owner enters, sees the camera, and asks the empty cabin who is filming.
