# The Soapbox Grand Prix

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: racing underdog dramas, pit-crew montages, rival drivers, and slow-motion finishes decided by a loose bolt*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A macaque mechanic enters a hand-built soapbox cart in the town's downhill race to save her workshop, but her champion driver keeps chasing speed while she tries to keep the wheels attached.

Cast
- TERA, a macaque mechanic with a tail that steadies a wrench.
- ROL, a capuchin driver who practices victory poses before the start.
- SENA, a ring-tailed lemur rival who knows the course by sound.
- VEX, an orangutan sponsor whose helmet is too large.
- MARSHAL OMI, a gibbon who enforces the downhill race rules.

## Scene 1: The Workshop

INT. TERA'S REPAIR SHED - DAY

A wooden cart sits on blocks. Its wheels are mismatched. A notice says the workshop rent is due after the race.

ROL: The championship cart needs a wing.

TERA: It needs a left wheel that is round.

ROL: A wing could help it go faster.

TERA: A round wheel could help it go at all.

Sena arrives with a polished cart and a sponsor decal. She offers Tera a place on her pit crew if Tera withdraws. Tera refuses. Rol is offended on her behalf, then asks if he should wear gloves for the race.

## Scene 2: The Training Montage

EXT. HILL COURSE - AFTERNOON

Rol pushes the cart uphill, runs behind it, and practices waving to an imaginary crowd. Tera inspects each axle. The montage repeats as he tries different hand positions and she retightens the same bolts.

SENA: You're using too much brake on the curve.

ROL: I need to look calm.

SENA: You need to turn.

Tera listens to the wheels roll. The left rear wheel clicks every rotation. She fits a washer from an old toolbox. It still clicks.

TERA: The hub is cracked.

ROL: Can it last one race?

TERA: It can last until it doesn't.

Vex sponsors the cart with a giant helmet that covers his eyes. He agrees to buy the workshop's remaining parts if they finish the race.

## Scene 3: The Start Line

EXT. HILLTOP - RACE DAY

The carts line up. Marshal Omi checks helmets, brakes, and steering. Rol raises his arms in a victory pose. The cart rolls forward without him.

OMI: The race has not started.

ROL: I was testing gravity.

Sena's cart is cleared. Tera's cart fails the brake test. The brake cable stretches too far. Tera borrows a metal clip from the timing stand and shortens it. Omi tests it twice.

OMI: It stops.

TERA: It also turns.

ROL: It will win.

TERA: It will finish.

## Scene 4: The Race

The bell rings. The carts accelerate downhill. Sena takes the lead, her wheels humming evenly. Rol leans into the first curve, too late. Tera's cart rattles. She shouts from the pit lane, but he cannot hear.

At the second turn, a loose bolt bounces into the road. Sena avoids it. Rol hits it; the cart jolts, and the left wheel wobbles. He reaches down, but the rules forbid touching the cart while racing.

Sena slows beside him.

SENA: Your hub is going.

ROL: I know.

SENA: Stay behind me. Take the wide curve.

They descend together. Tera runs along the side path, carrying a wrench. At the checkpoint, the carts stop for a mandatory brake inspection. Tera reaches the wheel and sees the washer has shifted.

TERA: You have one run left. The hub is not safe.

ROL: If I stop, we lose the workshop.

SENA: If you continue, you might lose more than the workshop.

Rol looks at the finish line below. He steps out of the cart.

## Scene 5: The Repair at the Checkpoint

Tera removes the cracked hub. The checkpoint crew has a spare wheel, but it is smaller. The cart will be slower. Rol approves the replacement. Vex takes off his oversized helmet and holds the cart level while Tera attaches the wheel. Sena waits, though the official allows her to continue.

SENA: The course is better with two carts on it.

OMI: The race clock is still running.

TERA: Let it run.

They finish the repair. Rol resumes from the checkpoint, at the back of the race. He takes the wide curves and brakes when the track is rough. Sena holds her lead but waits at the finish rather than crossing before him.

## Scene 6: The Finish

The carts cross the finish moments apart. Sena wins the race; Rol finishes safely. The crowd applauds both. Vex buys the workshop's remaining parts, but Tera refuses his offer to rename it after him.

VEX: What if I put my name on the helmet?

TERA: You may put it on the spare wheel.

SENA: Next year, I want that pit crew.

TERA: Next year, I want a round wheel.

## Final Image

The old cracked hub sits on a shelf above the workshop door. Outside, the repaired cart rolls slowly down the empty hill, with Tera walking beside it and listening for clicks.
