# The Depth Charge of Tea

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: submarine thrillers, tense sonar readings, and military command jargon*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A monkey crew aboard a research sub mistakes a school of tapping shrimp for an enemy fleet, while the captain's obsession with perfect tea service threatens the mission more than the ocean does.

Cast: Captain Morrow, a gorilla commander; Leni, a macaque sonar operator; Chief Pip, a capuchin mechanic; Dr. Shell, a tortoise marine biologist; and a school of snapping shrimp.

SCENE 1. INT. SUBMARINE CONTROL ROOM, NIGHT

Red lights pulse. A sonar ping echoes. Leni leans toward the screen. Captain Morrow enters carrying a silver tea tray.

LENI: Multiple contacts, bearing east.

MORROW: Are they hostile?

LENI: They sound crunchy.

The captain orders silence. Chief Pip's kettle whistles. Morrow orders the kettle to stand down. Pip says the kettle is not under his command.

SCENE 2. INT. SONAR STATION, CONTINUOUS

The pings accelerate. Leni marks each one with a grease pencil. Dr. Shell identifies the rhythm as snapping shrimp. Morrow says the shrimp may be using code. Shell says they are cracking prey.

Morrow orders a deep dive. Pip checks the ballast. The sub dips, and every teacup slides off the tray. Morrow catches one cup, then the sugar bowl. A spoon rolls toward the hatch. Leni catches it with a sonar headset.

SCENE 3. INT. ENGINE ROOM, LATER

A warning light flashes. Pip opens a panel and finds a tea bag caught in a valve. Morrow insists the bag is evidence. Pip says it is Earl Grey. The captain asks how it got there. Pip looks at the tray.

The sonar pings stop. The crew relaxes. Then the submarine makes a loud click. Morrow believes the shrimp have launched a weapon. Shell explains the sub's cooling pipes are expanding.

SCENE 4. INT. CONTROL ROOM, NIGHT

The crew tracks the shrimp's sound. Leni changes the sonar filter and maps the school. It is moving away from the sub. Morrow orders pursuit so they can confirm the threat. Shell asks whether he wants to chase animals for making noise.

Morrow considers this, then orders a slow observation pass. The crew takes notes through the porthole. The shrimp crack and retreat into a coral shelf. The captain serves tea in plastic cups.

SCENE 5. INT. GALLEY, DAWN

The sub surfaces. Morrow writes the mission report. He describes a "significant acoustic engagement." Leni corrects it to "shrimp feeding." Pip requests a commendation for the kettle. Shell requests a quiet galley.

SCENE 5A. INT. CONTROL ROOM, CONTINUOUS

The crew practices a calm report before surfacing.

MORROW: State the contact.

LENI: Shrimp, not ships.

PIP: Kettle, not reactor.

SHELL: Tea, not evidence.

MORROW: Keep the report formal.

LENI: Acoustic event, small.

PIP: Valve issue, smaller.

SHELL: Species, snapping shrimp.

MORROW: Possible coded message?

SHELL: Feeding noise, Captain.

MORROW: Is it aggressive?

SHELL: Only to clams.

PIP: We have no clams.

LENI: We have crackers.

MORROW: Are crackers classified?

PIP: Only in the galley.

SHELL: I object to this.

MORROW: To the crackers?

SHELL: To your sonar theory.

LENI: The map is clear.

PIP: The valve is clear.

MORROW: The teacups aren't.

PIP: I can retrieve them.

MORROW: No heroics, Chief.

PIP: They're porcelain, sir.

SHELL: Observe the shrimp.

LENI: They are eating.

MORROW: In formation?

SHELL: Around a clam.

PIP: That's a formation.

LENI: They're retreating now.

MORROW: Maintain pursuit.

SHELL: Maintain distance.

PIP: Maintain tea temperature.

MORROW: Fine. Slow pursuit.

LENI: Logging slow pursuit.

SHELL: Please log lunch too.

MORROW: Add one biscuit.

PIP: Two biscuits, Captain.

MORROW: That's classified.

The crew looks at him. He lowers his voice and puts the biscuit on the chart table.

SCENE 5B. INT. GALLEY, DAWN

Morrow writes a new tea protocol. Pip reads each line aloud. Shell checks the window for shrimp.

PIP: No tea near valves.

MORROW: Sensible.

LENI: No sonar near tea.

MORROW: Less sensible.

SHELL: No chasing shrimp.

MORROW: Disputed.

PIP: Keep cups secured.

LENI: Log each contact.

SHELL: Identify before panic.

MORROW: That seems pointed.

SHELL: It is.

The submarine's kettle clicks off. Everyone turns. Pip says it is only the kettle. Morrow asks whether it could be a signal. Shell takes the plug from the wall and hands it to him.

MORROW: Protocol accepted.

LENI: Should we file it?

PIP: In the galley.

SHELL: Beside the crackers.

MORROW: Keep them classified.

PIP: I will label the tin.

MORROW: Label it carefully.

PIP: "Possible biscuit."

MORROW: That's enough.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew raises teacups for a toast. One shrimp snaps outside the porthole. Every monkey ducks. Morrow looks at the report and adds: "The enemy remains active."
