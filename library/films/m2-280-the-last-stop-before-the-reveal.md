# The Last Stop Before the Reveal

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble train mysteries, suspects trapped in a dining car, ominous compartment entrances, and detectives who gather everyone for one final explanation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A ring-tailed lemur investigator has one night train ride to recover a missing silver whistle before the railway museum's opening, while every passenger has a suspicious reason to be near the dining car.

Cast
- INSPECTOR LIRI, a ring-tailed lemur with a tail that curls around evidence bags.
- CONDUCTOR MOSS, a capuchin who knows the timetable by heart.
- PROFESSOR OMI, an orangutan railway historian.
- COOK BEX, a macaque who guards the galley.
- PERFORMER VELA, a gibbon with a traveling puppet case.
- PORTER TUM, a mandrill who polishes the brass fittings.

## Scene 1: The Empty Display

INT. NIGHT TRAIN - DINING CAR - EVENING

The train rolls through the dark. A velvet tray sits empty. The museum's silver whistle, meant to be unveiled at dawn, has vanished.

MOSS: The display was locked when we left the station.

OMI: The whistle was there when we left the station.

BEX: The dining car was crowded. Someone could have reached it.

VELA: My puppet case was under the table.

TUM: I polished every brass surface. The whistle was not on the tray after the first stop.

Inspector Liri places a card on the table: NO ONE LEAVES BEFORE THE FINAL STOP.

LIRI: We have one hour before dawn. I will ask questions, and nobody will perform a confession.

Vela closes her puppet case. It gives a metallic jingle.

## Scene 2: The Suspects

Liri interviews each passenger in the adjoining compartments. Omi admits she wanted to hold the whistle for the museum portrait. Bex had access to the galley and served tea. Vela rehearsed a scene near the display. Moss unlocked the cabinet at the first stop for the museum photographer, then locked it again. Tum handled the brass tray.

LIRI: Who touched the velvet?

TUM: I dusted it.

LIRI: Who touched the cabinet?

MOSS: I locked it.

LIRI: Who heard the whistle?

BEX: I heard it ring after the first stop.

The dining car goes quiet.

OMI: The whistle was stolen after the cabinet was locked.

MOSS: It could not have been inside the locked cabinet.

BEX: It could have been under the table. I heard a ring when the train turned.

## Scene 3: The Dramatic Search

Liri orders everyone to gather in the dining car. The train rattles through a tunnel. The lights flicker. Vela's puppet case tips over, and wooden puppets spill onto the floor. One wears a tiny conductor's cap.

LIRI: The suspect has been revealed.

VELA: The puppet is not the suspect. It has no hands.

LIRI: It has a hinged jaw.

VELA: That is for speaking.

TUM: The whistle's sound came from the brass tray when it struck the table leg. I heard the same ring when I polished it.

LIRI: Then the sound was not the whistle.

TUM: No.

The dining car door slams. A shadow crosses its frosted pane. Everyone turns. It is the conductor passing with a lamp.

MOSS: I have used this corridor for thirty years.

LIRI: And you still need a lamp?

MOSS: The train is old. The bulbs flicker.

## Scene 4: The Timeline

Liri draws the timetable on a fresh napkin from the galley. At the first stop, Moss opened the display for a museum photographer. Omi posed beside it. Bex served tea. Vela's case rested beneath the table. Tum polished the brass tray. The whistle rang once when the tray was moved, but the lock remained intact.

LIRI: At the first stop, Moss opened the cabinet for the photograph.

MOSS: I locked it again as soon as the photographer finished.

LIRI: And at the second stop?

MOSS: The cabinet stayed locked.

LIRI: Then the whistle left the display during that photograph, when the tray was lifted.

OMI: The tray was lifted when I posed.

BEX: It was empty by then.

The group stares at Omi. She turns red.

OMI: I did not steal it. I leaned on the velvet while the photographer adjusted the light. The whistle rolled into my sleeve.

She checks her sleeve and finds nothing.

OMI: I felt it slide down my cuff as I leaned across the display. After the photograph, I pulled it free so it would not scratch the velvet and folded it into the timetable napkin while I helped wrap the display.

LIRI: And left that napkin against the heater.

OMI: I thought it was a loose ornament.

Liri notices a faint metal outline through the napkin. It was pushed against the heater during the second stop.

## Scene 5: The Recovery

Liri unrolls the napkin. The silver whistle lies within. Omi had folded the whistle into the napkin while wrapping the tray. During the second stop, the napkin slid against the heater, warming it.

OMI: Does this count as my museum portrait?

LIRI: You are in every witness statement. I can have them framed.

MOSS: We have reached the museum stop.

The door opens to platform lights. Dawn begins outside. The museum staff prepare the display case. Liri gives the whistle to Omi, who confirms the engraving is intact.

BEX: Does it still work?

OMI: It does.

She blows it once. The whole train answers with a short echo.

## Scene 6: Final Stop

At the museum, the whistle is installed in a secure case. Moss places the timetable beside it. The curator sets a photograph of Vela's conductor puppet beside the whistle, with a plaque reading RECOVERED BY INSPECTOR LIRI. Vela opens the case so the puppet can face its portrait.

LIRI: That is not my portrait.

VELA: You called him the suspect. He wants the credit.

TUM: I only polished the tray.

VELA: And I supplied the dramatic suspect.

LIRI: You supplied the noise.

## Final Image

The display case locks. The silver whistle rests on red velvet, and the train's last echo fades before the museum lights come on.
