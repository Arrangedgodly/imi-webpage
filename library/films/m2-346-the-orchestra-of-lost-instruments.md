# The Orchestra of Lost Instruments

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: backstage musical dramas, the last-minute performance, rival soloists, and a conductor who keeps stopping the music to discuss entrances*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
CONDUCTOR MIRI, a lemur
VIOLINIST ODO, an orangutan
PERCUSSIONIST VEX, a macaque
FLUTIST PALLA, a gibbon
STAGE MANAGER NARI, a capuchin
TALA, a young macaque triangle player in the school group

SCENE 1. INT. CONCERT HALL - AFTERNOON

The orchestra prepares for the town's anniversary concert. The violin section has one empty chair. Odo's instrument is missing. He insists it was in its case five minutes ago. Miri raises her baton and calls for tuning. A triangle rings from backstage.

MIRI: We are missing a violin.

VEX: We have found a triangle.

Nari checks the call sheet; the triangle belongs to the school group performing after the orchestra.

SCENE 2. INT. BACKSTAGE HALL - LATER

Nari asks the musicians to make a list of every missing item. Vex writes triangle, violin, two drumsticks, and one chair.

MIRI: We found the triangle.

VEX: We have not found who should hit it.

MIRI: And the chair?

VEX: I put Odo's spare chair behind the curtain. An empty space looks more dramatic.

MIRI: Bring it back. He needs somewhere to sit.

Palla plays a tuning note. A delayed copy answers from behind the curtain. She follows it to the rehearsal recorder, still replaying her warm-up.

PALLA: I have become my own rival soloist.

Nari stops the playback and levels the wobbling recorder stand. Odo hears a low note from the prop closet and mistakes it for a violin. It is a long brass tube used for a stage trumpet.

ODO: The orchestra has found a new instrument.

MIRI: It has found a storage problem.

They mark the room entrances with colored tape, then discover the tape was left over from the previous opera and the colors indicate costume changes, not exits. Nari removes it before anyone follows the wrong color into the fly system.

SCENE 2A. INT. BACKSTAGE HALL - LATER

The musicians search the storage rooms. Palla finds a flute in a chair cushion. Vex discovers a drumstick inside a flower arrangement. Odo follows the sound of a faint string note to the prop closet. Every door is labeled with a musician's name, but the labels were switched during a set change.

Miri asks the orchestra to rehearse the opening without Odo. The music sounds incomplete. She stops to correct the tempo, then stops again to correct the entrance. Vex taps a rhythm under each pause. The others begin to play along, turning the rehearsal into a strange, choppy march.

SCENE 3. INT. PROP CLOSET - EVENING

Odo's violin case is behind a painted tree. Nari moves the tree and finds a second case. Both look identical. Odo opens one: it contains a rubber chicken. The other holds sheet music and a violin bow. The violin itself is missing. Palla notices a strap hanging from a ceiling beam. The instrument has been lifted into the fly system with the backdrop.

NARI: Someone stored it with the scenery.

ODO: The scenery has no orchestra part.

They lower the backdrop slowly. The violin swings over the stage, held by a loop of rope. Vex plays a drumroll as it descends. Miri tells him to stop. He stuffs the sticks into his vest, but their tips tap against his snare rim twice before he can step away.

SCENE 4. INT. CONCERT HALL - NIGHT

The audience arrives. Odo tunes the violin. Miri gives one clear cue, and the orchestra begins from the top. The opening is smooth until Tala enters early with the school group's triangle. Miri lets the child play the single chime and then keeps conducting. The orchestra adjusts and follows the unexpected note into the next measure.

At the final movement, Vex drops one drumstick. Palla catches it on the flute case and hands it back between phrases. Odo plays the solo with the bow he recovered from the closet. The orchestra finishes together, without another stop.

SCENE 5. INT. CONCERT HALL - AFTER THE PERFORMANCE

The audience applauds. Miri thanks the musicians and the school group. Nari rewrites the storage labels so instruments go in numbered cabinets. She makes a separate shelf for props, where the rubber chicken can no longer be mistaken for a string instrument. Odo places the violin in its case and ties a bright tag to the handle. Vex asks whether the orchestra can add the triangle to the finale next year. MIRI: Only if it arrives at the right time.

VEX: We could hide it behind the curtain and give it an entrance.

[Nari puts its case on the labeled school-group shelf before Vex can reach it.]

Palla takes the spare chair Vex finally returned and moves it into the flute section. The chair produces a soft squeak, which matches the last note of the concert.

FINAL IMAGE: The orchestra leaves one instrument case under its numbered shelf, while the triangle rests on top with its label facing outward.
