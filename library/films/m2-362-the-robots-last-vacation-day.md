# The Robot's Last Vacation Day

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: science-fiction comedy, robot shutdown countdowns, sentimental machine friendships, and one last task that becomes an endless checklist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
ROBOT R-4, a service robot
MARA, a capuchin mechanic
PIP, a macaque gardener
MAYOR NARI, an orangutan
VEX, a gibbon tour guide

SCENE 1. INT. MUNICIPAL GARAGE - MORNING

Robot R-4 is scheduled to take a retirement trip to the seaside. A display on its chest reads VACATION: 08 HOURS. Mara disconnects the work-order printer. The machine prints one final task: deliver a spare wheel to the park.

R-4: I have a free day.

MARA: The task is optional.

The robot looks at the wheel, then at the bus schedule. Pip arrives with a broken cart and says the wheel would help him move garden soil. R-4 agrees to make one delivery before leaving.

SCENE 2. EXT. PARK PATH - LATER

R-4 carries the wheel through the park. Vex leads a tour group and interprets the robot's blinking lights as a farewell sequence. R-4 says the display is a battery meter. Vex asks if it wants visitors to gather around. The group forms a circle, blocking the path. Mara asks them to leave a lane for the cart.

The robot reaches Pip's garden. The wheel is the wrong size. Pip tries it on the cart; it wobbles and falls off. R-4 compares the axle with the wheel and calculates the right diameter. The park shop has a matching wheel, but it is at the far end of the grounds.

SCENE 2A. EXT. GARDEN PATH - MIDDAY

R-4 rolls the incorrect wheel to the park shop. The wheel has a decorative stripe, and Vex tells the tour group it is a rare collector's edition. Pip checks the axle again and shows that the hub is too wide. The group watches R-4 compare measurements on its display. The machine prints a neat diagram. Vex asks it to pose beside the wheel for a farewell photograph. R-4 declines; the camera is covering the shop doorway.

PIP: The wheel could still be useful.

R-4: It fits the compost cart.

Pip tests it on the compost cart. It turns smoothly. The machine leaves the wheel with the gardener and updates the inventory. It prints a second receipt for the compost cart, then powers the printer down. Its task is complete for that item, but its vacation clock keeps counting. R-4 notices it still has time for the bus.

SCENE 3. INT. PARK SHOP - AFTERNOON

The shop is closed for lunch. R-4 checks its task list and finds a note telling staff to return at one. It waits. The display reads VACATION: 05 HOURS. Vex invites the robot to see the fountain. R-4 declines because the wheel delivery is unfinished. Mara suggests leaving the wrong wheel with Pip and taking the bus. The robot says a complete delivery requires the correct part.

R-4 recharges at the solar bench. A child asks if robots dream. It says its low-power mode saves battery. Vex starts a story about the robot dreaming of waves. Mara interrupts to point out that the battery is at 20 percent. The robot plugs into the bench while Pip fetches the matching wheel from the nearby storage shed.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK GARDEN - LATE AFTERNOON

Pip returns with the correct wheel. R-4 bolts it onto the cart and helps move the soil. The cart rolls smoothly. Pip offers the robot a break in the garden. It accepts and sits beneath a tree. Its vacation display reads VACATION: 02 HOURS.

Mayor Nari arrives with a clipboard of municipal errands. She starts to assign R-4 a second job, then sees the retirement tag. Mara explains the machine is off duty. Nari crosses out the task and signs the form. The robot thanks her and points to a bench with a view of the fountain.

SCENE 5. EXT. BUS STOP - SUNSET

R-4 boards the bus to the seaside. Vex waves goodbye, then tells the tour group that the robot has fulfilled its lifelong dream. Mara says the robot planned the trip this morning. Pip gives it a small packet of garden seeds for the beach planter and a note on how deep to plant them. The bus pulls away.

The robot's chest display changes from VACATION: 01 HOUR to VACATION: ENJOY. It stores the wheel receipt in the archive and powers down the work-order printer remotely.

FINAL IMAGE: R-4 watches the ocean through the bus window while the forgotten wheel rolls one inch inside the empty garage and stops against a wall.
