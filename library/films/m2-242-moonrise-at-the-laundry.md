# Moonrise at the Laundry

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: melodramatic romantic dramas, where two neighbors' laundry lines become a grand obstacle to a meeting*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: Two shy neighbors keep missing each other at a shared clothesline because a dramatic breeze, a stubborn sheet, and their friends keep staging the wrong encounter.

Cast: Liri, a macaque baker; Pavo, a gibbon gardener; Tessa, a capuchin friend; and Odo, a baboon caretaker.

SCENE 1. EXT. SHARED COURTYARD, LATE AFTERNOON

A white sheet hangs between two balconies. Liri steps outside with a basket of towels. Pavo appears across the yard carrying a small pot.

LIRI: Good afternoon.

PAVO: I was just leaving.

LIRI: The sheet is still wet.

PAVO: I was just arriving.

They both look at the clothesline. A breeze lifts the sheet between them like a curtain.

[SFX: CLOTH FLUTTER.]

TESSA: You missed him by one laundry cycle.

LIRI: I was hanging towels.

TESSA: You could have hung them dramatically.

PAVO: Odo said the sheet would be dry at five.

ODO: It is four fifty-eight.

PAVO: I could wait two minutes.

Liri looks down at her basket. One towel slips to the ground.

PAVO: You dropped a towel.

LIRI: It is not important.

PAVO: It might get muddy.

He picks it up and hands it to her. Their fingers touch. The sheet falls between them again.

TESSA: This is going very well.

SCENE 2. EXT. COURTYARD, MOMENTS LATER

Odo comes out with a laundry pole. The sheet has sagged in the middle and blocks the walkway.

ODO: The line is too loose.

TESSA: It is creating tension.

ODO: It is creating a trip hazard.

He tightens the line. The sheet snaps taut, knocking a clothespin onto the roof.

LIRI: The pin is gone.

PAVO: I can retrieve it.

LIRI: You do not need to climb.

PAVO: I am already holding a pot.

Pavo places the pot down. Tessa pushes a ladder toward him. Odo stops her.

ODO: That ladder is for the light fixture.

TESSA: It can serve two stories.

ODO: It serves one ladder.

Pavo reaches up with a long garden stake and nudges the clothespin. It drops into Liri's basket.

PAVO: I have retrieved it.

LIRI: You did not climb.

PAVO: I changed the method.

Their eyes meet. The wind lifts the sheet again; it covers Pavo's face.

PAVO: I cannot see the courtyard.

LIRI: I can see you.

TESSA: An improvement.

SCENE 3. INT. ODO'S LAUNDRY ROOM, SUNSET

Odo has moved the line indoors while he replaces a cracked pulley. Liri and Pavo arrive from opposite doors. They stop when they see one another.

LIRI: I thought the line was outside.

PAVO: I thought it was being repaired.

ODO: It is being repaired. You can wait outside.

TESSA: They have both waited outside for months.

ODO: Then they can wait inside for two minutes.

A pulley squeaks. Odo pulls the rope, and a sheet sweeps between Liri and Pavo again.

LIRI: It keeps separating us.

PAVO: We can move the sheet.

They each grab an edge. The sheet folds, then wraps around Odo.

ODO: I am not part of the romance.

TESSA: The sheet disagrees.

Odo frees himself. Pavo and Liri laugh. The awkwardness breaks.

PAVO: Would you like to see the seedlings in my garden?

LIRI: I would. Would you like to try the bread I baked?

PAVO: Yes.

TESSA: That sounded like a meeting.

Odo checks the knot on the line, then steps aside. Pavo and Liri both reach for the same fallen pin.

PAVO: You take it.

LIRI: We can share it.

TESSA: Now you are negotiating laundry.

ODO: That is how I met my neighbor.

TESSA: You met him at the gate.

ODO: The gate needed repair.

LIRI: It was a question.

PAVO: And an answer.

SCENE 4. EXT. COURTYARD, NIGHT

The repair is done. Odo puts the line back outside. Liri and Pavo stand on opposite sides, holding a towel between them.

ODO: The line is stable.

TESSA: Now the two of you can cross.

They walk toward one another, each trying to step around the towel. They meet in the middle and bump shoulders.

PAVO: I should have gone left.

LIRI: I did go left.

PAVO: I also went left.

LIRI: Then we agree on the direction.

Tessa lifts the towel. It is now dry and warm from the evening air.

TESSA: The laundry has concluded.

Odo clips the last clothespin. A gust sends the sheet billowing over all four of them.

[SFX: CLOTH FLUTTER, LAUGHTER.]

ODO: The line is stable. The sheet has other plans.

PAVO: Tomorrow, no sheets.

LIRI: Tomorrow, seedlings and bread.

TESSA: I will bring a basket, in case the story needs another obstacle.

The sheet lifts away. Liri and Pavo walk toward the garden, speaking at the same time and stopping to let the other go first. They laugh and continue together.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS, COURTYARD NIGHT.]
