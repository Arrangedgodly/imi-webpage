# The River That Ran Uphill

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: expedition adventures, impossible natural signs, and maps that insist a journey is heroic*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a gibbon guide
PIP, a capuchin climber
VEX, a macaque surveyor
TAVI, a lemur water keeper
THE RIVER, a silent performer

SCENE 1. EXT. LOWER VALLEY - DAWN

A river flows toward a hill. Mara watches it carry a leaf uphill. Vex opens a map. A red arrow points against the river's direction.

VEX: The river runs uphill.

TAVI: The wind is pushing the surface.

PIP: The water underneath is still going down.

VEX: The map says "follow the river to its source."

Mara asks where they are going. Vex says the source is behind the hill. Tavi says the water comes from the spring above them, so they should walk the other way.

VEX: The expedition follows the arrow.

TAVI: The arrow is upside down.

SCENE 2. EXT. RIVER BANK - MORNING

The team follows the water uphill. Pip tries to step across it on a line of stones. The current pushes his foot sideways. He clings to a branch while the water flows past.

PIP: The river is winning.

MARA: You are standing in it.

Mara pulls Pip back to the bank. Tavi places a leaf on the surface and a pebble on a shallow shelf under the water. The leaf travels toward the hill. The pebble rolls in the opposite direction.

VEX: Two rivers.

TAVI: One stream. Two currents.

Vex ties a bright ribbon to a twig and sets it in the water. The ribbon catches in a whirlpool and circles the same rock. He draws a new arrow on the map. The twirling twig spins the paper around his wrist.

VEX: The map is turning.

MARA: The current is turning.

Pip steps across the stones again. He reaches the far bank by holding the branch, then pulls it free. It bumps the rock and splashes water into Vex's open map case.

Vex holds the map against a tree. The page turns in the wind. The red arrow points downhill now. He turns the map upside down again.

VEX: The direction has changed.

TAVI: The map has.

Mara finds a narrow channel running from a pond toward the river. A strong wind pushes water from the pond over a low ridge. Tavi says it is not the river flowing uphill; it is a surface current.

SCENE 3. EXT. HILL POND - NOON

The expedition reaches the pond. A fallen branch blocks the channel. Water backs up and pours across the ridge in a sheet. Vex says the river is escaping its banks.

TAVI: It is finding a lower path.

PIP: The river is trying to leave the map.

Mara asks them to move the branch. Vex pulls one end. The branch turns and sprays water over his coat. Pip braces it with a pole. Tavi guides the smaller limbs out of the channel.

The water slows. A flat stone beneath the branch redirects it into the channel. Mara sets the stone in place. The pond level drops, and the stream resumes its normal downhill flow.

VEX: The river is going the right way.

TAVI: The river was always going its way.

Vex sets one marker at the pond and another at the channel mouth. He watches the water pass both points in order. Pip picks up the loose branch and carries it away from the bank, clear of the flow.

PIP: Nothing is pushing uphill now.

MARA: Nothing was pushing uphill before.

SCENE 4. EXT. RIDGE TRAIL - AFTERNOON

The team follows the corrected stream toward its source. The map's red arrow points toward a waterfall. Vex says they have reached the end of the expedition.

MARA: The waterfall is not the source.

PIP: It is the next stop.

Vex climbs a rock to get a better view. The waterfall mist wets the map, and the ink arrow begins to run. Mara folds the map into a paper boat and sets it on the stream. It floats downstream, away from the spring.

VEX: The map has chosen a route.

TAVI: It is a boat now.

Pip catches it with a stick. Vex unfolds it. The red arrow is smudged beyond recognition. The team looks upstream and sees a small spring emerging from the hillside.

SCENE 5. EXT. SPRING - SUNSET

Mara cups water from the spring. Tavi fills a container. Vex redraws the map with a blue line following the actual river. Pip removes a leaf caught in the channel.

VEX: The map no longer has an arrow.

MARA: It has a line.

PIP: Lines can go both ways.

Tavi points to the lower valley. The river is flowing normally again. The wind has stopped, and the leaf floats downhill.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex folds the corrected map. It stays folded in his dry case while the leaf beside him floats toward the valley.
