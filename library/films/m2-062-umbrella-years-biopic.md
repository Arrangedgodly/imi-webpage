# The Umbrella Years

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: prestige biopics, compressed timelines, and awards-scene endings*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A dramatic biopic traces a lemur inventor's life through three actors and several eras, though the invention in question is a folding umbrella that keeps pinching its own handle.

Cast: Young Ivo, a lemur tinkerer; Ivo at midlife, played by another lemur; Elder Ivo, played by the same actor in a gray scarf; Nella, a capuchin fabric merchant; and Director Bristle, a mandrill who wants the story to feel important.

SCENE 1. INT. BLACK-AND-WHITE WORKSHOP, 1948

The title card says THE UMBRELLA YEARS. Young Ivo sketches a folding canopy while rain drips into a cup. A biopic narrator begins, but Ivo interrupts.

IVO: It leaks because the window is open.

The narrator pauses. Ivo shuts the window. The workshop darkens. A producer asks him to look at the rain with quiet genius. Ivo looks for a cloth.

SCENE 2. INT. MARKET STALL, 1959

A sudden title reads ELEVEN YEARS LATER. Midlife Ivo demonstrates a prototype for Nella. It folds neatly, then pinches his thumb.

NELLA: Is that the finished design?

IVO: It's the finished prototype.

NELLA: Does it work?

IVO: It folds.

Nella tries it. The umbrella opens indoors and knocks a row of hats off their hooks. The producer asks for a triumphant reaction. Ivo catches each hat before it hits the floor and returns them to the right customers. The scene is cut short for a close-up of his determined jaw.

SCENE 3. INT. BIOPIC EDITING ROOM, NIGHT

Director Bristle watches a montage of Ivo drawing sketches, aging, and staring meaningfully at weather. He complains that the film has no central crisis. The editor points out the broken umbrella's latch. Bristle says the latch is too small for a third act.

EDITOR: It keeps pinching his hand.

BRISTLE: Give it a rival.

A poster is added for a rival inventor who makes rigid parasols. No actor has been cast. Bristle asks the editor to put a shadow on the wall and let Ivo react to it.

SCENE 4. INT. TOWN HALL, 1972

After the committee meeting, Nella asks Ivo to show her the failed prototypes. He opens a crate of bent ribs and handles. One umbrella springs open and catches a chair. Nella frees it with the latch tool. Ivo takes notes while she works. The director wants a slow-motion shot of the invention; Nella asks for a close-up of the screw instead.

Elder Ivo, still wearing the gray scarf, presents the improved umbrella to a committee. He demonstrates how the ribs lock safely. The umbrella closes on its own and rolls down the aisle. The committee scatters. Ivo chases it, catches it at the door, and shows the latch was never fully engaged.

IVO: You have to hear the click.

Nella, now older, tests the latch. She hears the click. The committee applauds. The narrator announces that Ivo changed the world. A clerk asks whether the umbrella is for sale yet. Ivo says it will be, once the handle stops pinching people.

SCENE 5. INT. SMALL WORKSHOP, 1984

Elder Ivo meets Nella, who now runs a repair shop. She has brought two umbrellas with broken locks. A reporter asks Ivo to summarize his life's work in one sentence. He says, "Hear the click." Nella tests both locks and says one still pinches. The reporter asks if this is his final invention. Ivo says it is a repair appointment.

REPORTER: Does that feel like a legacy?

IVO: It feels like a thumb.

Nella replaces the spring. Ivo insists on testing it himself. The umbrella opens safely, but the reporter asks for a dramatic close-up of his eyes. Ivo points the camera toward the latch instead. The shot captures a tiny click and a relieved expression from Nella.

SCENE 6. INT. AWARDS CEREMONY, PRESENT DAY

The film cuts to an awards stage. A presenter announces Ivo's posthumous lifetime achievement award. The actor playing Elder Ivo accepts it and whispers that Ivo is alive in the front row. The camera finds the real Ivo, who is holding a folded umbrella and a repair manual.

REAL IVO: The new latch works.

The audience applauds. Bristle tries to make the moment more emotional by cueing a string swell. The orchestra plays the rain motif. Ivo opens the umbrella indoors. It stays open. A stagehand points out that the roof is leaking. Ivo hands over the umbrella and a bucket.

The presenter asks for Ivo's famous quote. He says "Hear the click" again, then asks whether the award has a safe handle.

A stagehand finds the award is also slightly loose. Ivo asks for the screwdriver.

FINAL IMAGE: The presenter raises the award; Ivo folds it under the umbrella for safekeeping. The trophy's handle pinches his thumb. He asks the committee to hear the click.
