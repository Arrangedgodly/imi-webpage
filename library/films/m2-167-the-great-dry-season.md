# The Great Dry Season

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: epic survival dramas with sweeping landscape shots applied to a troop planning water breaks during a warm afternoon*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A troop's trek to a water source becomes an epic survival story in the voice-over, although the water is nearby and the danger is mostly an empty canteen.

Cast: Guide Nara, a macaque; narrator Veli, a gibbon; camera operator Odo, a baboon; and traveler Piri, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. EXT. DRY CLEARING, NOON

A low sun hangs over a dry clearing. Piri holds an empty gourd.

VELI (V.O.): The group has reached the edge of survival.

NARA: The water bowl is behind that tree.

Odo points his camera toward the distant ridge.

VELI: The route is long.

NARA: It is twenty steps.

SCENE 2. EXT. CLEARING, MOMENTS LATER

The group starts walking. Veli narrates each step as if it were an ordeal.

VELI (V.O.): Their journey tests every reserve.

PIRI: I have a snack.

NARA: Save it for after water.

Piri opens the gourd. A drop falls out.

ODO: A dramatic loss.

VELI: The last drop has disappeared.

NARA: It was condensation.

SCENE 3. EXT. SHADED TREE, AFTERNOON

Nara leads the group into shade. Odo keeps filming from the sun.

NARA: The water is here.

VELI: The group must conserve energy.

PIRI: We are in the shade.

Odo's camera battery warning flashes.

ODO: I may need to conserve power.

Veli asks him to keep filming.

VELI: We need the final shot.

Odo lowers the camera and drinks from the bowl.

SCENE 4. EXT. WATER BOWL, CONTINUOUS

Piri fills the gourd. A small monkey arrives and drinks first.

VELI: A rival has reached the water source.

NARA: It lives here.

The troop waits. The monkey drinks, then leaves.

PIRI: We can refill now.

Veli looks at the camera.

VELI: They have shown patience.

NARA: We have waited one minute.

SCENE 4A. EXT. WATERING SITE, AFTERNOON

Odo's camera gives a warning beep. He sets it on a rock in the shade.

ODO: The battery is overheating.

Veli looks toward the far ridge.

VELI: We need a landscape shot before the light fades.

Nara moves the camera beneath the tree.

NARA: Let it cool here.

Piri checks the gourd. There is enough water for the group and the return.

PIRI: We have supplies.

Veli writes "survival ration" on the slate.

NARA: It is a snack.

VELI: It is a snack that supports morale.

A small monkey approaches the bowl. The group steps back to leave room. The monkey drinks, then climbs the tree.

Odo's camera cools. He turns it on and captures the troop moving through the shade.

ODO: This is a better shot.

VELI: The troop is safe.

NARA: And the battery is working.

Piri seals the gourd and puts it in the pack. The crew prepares to leave.

SCENE 4B. EXT. CLEARING, LATE AFTERNOON

The team leaves the watering site. The sun remains hot, so Nara chooses the shaded edge of the trail.

VELI (V.O.): They begin the final leg with renewed strength.

PIRI: We are going back the way we came.

Odo carries the cooled camera. The battery light is steady.

ODO: I can film the return.

NARA: Only if you keep walking.

Odo films the path ahead. The gourd swings from Piri's pack and bumps a branch.

VELI: The provisions are in danger.

PIRI: The gourd is tied to the pack.

The knot loosens. Nara stops and tightens it. The group waits in the shade.

NARA: We are not in a race.

Veli lowers the narration card. They continue at an even pace.

SCENE 5. EXT. RETURN PATH, SUNSET

The group walks back through the clearing. Piri carries a full gourd.

VELI: The return journey is no less perilous.

NARA: We are going the same way.

Piri offers the gourd to Odo. Odo drinks, then hands it to Veli.

VELI: Water, shared among the travelers.

NARA: There is enough for everyone.

Before the final cut, Nara checks the equipment log. The camera battery overheated because it sat in direct sun beside the bowl. Veli moves it under the table and labels the spare batteries with the time they were charged. Odo suggests calling the pause a dramatic vigil. Nara refuses and leaves the ordinary waiting in the film.

SCENE 6. INT. EDITING ROOM, NIGHT

Veli reviews footage. The wide shots make the clearing look vast. The close shot shows the water bowl behind the tree.

VELI: We can cut the water bowl from the opening.

ODO: Then viewers will think they were in danger.

NARA: They were not.

Veli keeps the opening wide and includes the water bowl in the first shot.

VELI: We can call it a journey to the shade.

Odo saves the edit. The title card reads "Twenty Steps at Noon."

The camera zooms into the title. A monkey enters frame and takes the gourd.
