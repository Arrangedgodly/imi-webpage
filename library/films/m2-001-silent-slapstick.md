# The Quietest Stampede

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent slapstick chase construction, escalating prop gags, and physical timing*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A proud lemur stagehand tries to keep a silent-film set perfectly quiet, but a runaway prop moon drags the whole studio into a chase that ends with the director trapped inside his own title card.

Cast: Mica, a meticulous ring-tailed lemur stagehand; Buster, a young capuchin who cannot stop bowing; Director Slate, a stern baboon who communicates with enormous cue cards; Dot, a practical macaque camera operator; and a goose with a union card.

SCENE 1. INT. SILENT PICTURE STUDIO, MORNING

A painted street stands inside the studio. A cardboard moon hangs over it on a pulley. MICA measures the distance from the moon to a chalk mark with a ruler. BUSTER enters carrying a feather duster. He sees the camera and bows. He bows again, backing into a bucket. The bucket sticks to his tail.

Slate holds up a card: QUIET SET!

Buster tries to remove the bucket without making noise. He tips backward, rolls down the painted curb, and ends at Mica's feet. The bucket lands over his head. Mica removes it. A goose marches in and stamps a union card on the slate.

SLATE holds up: NO GEESE IN THIS SCENE.

The goose points to its card, then to the camera. Dot starts filming.

SCENE 2. EXT. PAINTED STREET, CONTINUOUS

Mica pulls the moon pulley to test it. The knot slips. The moon rolls downhill on hidden wheels. It bumps Buster, who catches it with his chest, then rides it through the fake storefront. Cardboard fruit flies in every direction. A painted window falls forward but lands gently on a cushion marked BREAKABLE.

Mica grabs the moon's rope. The rope wraps around the signpost, the goose, and Slate's ankles. Slate holds up a card: CUT!

The camera keeps rolling. Dot shrugs and cranks faster.

The moon rolls back uphill, dragging Slate on his heels. Buster runs beside it, trying to look dignified. Each time he passes the lens he bows. The moon passes him again. He bows again. The goose pecks a loose wheel. The moon stops inches from the edge of the set.

SCENE 3. INT. PROP ROOM, MOMENTS LATER

Mica inspects the wheel and finds a tiny goose feather wedged in its axle. The goose presents its card. It reads: SAFETY INSPECTOR.

Slate points at the goose, then at a card: YOU'RE FIRED.

The goose tears the card in half and reveals another card beneath: APPEAL APPROVED.

Dot sets the moon back in place. Mica ties three knots and tests each one. Buster holds the rope, then notices his reflection in a tin mirror. He practices a dramatic fall. The rope loosens from his hands.

SCENE 4. EXT. STUDIO BACKLOT, AFTERNOON

A chase sequence begins. Slate holds up: THE VILLAIN ESCAPES!

The goose steals the villain's hat and runs. Buster chases the goose. Mica chases Buster. Slate chases Mica, still holding the card. Dot films from a trolley. They race through a miniature town, around a clothesline, under a table, and across a plank. Every prop that appears has a practical purpose: the clothesline catches the hat, the table holds a bowl of flour, and the plank is supposed to look like a bridge.

Buster slides across the flour, catches the hat, and lands upright in a tiny boat. The boat rolls along a painted river track. Mica catches the stern. Slate grabs her belt. The goose grips Slate's jacket. The whole procession glides past the camera in a neat line.

Dot turns the trolley to follow. Its wheel hits a bump, and the camera points at the sky. The moon prop drops into frame and settles over the boat like a lid.

SCENE 5. INT. EDITING ROOM, NIGHT

The film crew watches the finished reel. Slate gestures at the accidental chase, then points to his cue cards. He wants the shots retaken. Dot places the exposed film beside the retake order. Mica flips the order over. The back is blank. The goose stamps it APPROVED.

Slate tries to object, but the goose takes his stack of cue cards and rearranges them. They now read: THE END. PLEASE TIP YOUR CAMERA OPERATOR.

Buster bows to the room. His hat falls into the projector, briefly casting a huge image of his backside on the screen. Everyone freezes. Slate lifts one last card: ART.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew poses for a publicity still. The goose stands center stage wearing the director's cap. Mica holds a ruler against the frame. Buster bows and knocks the ruler sideways. The photograph comes out crooked, but the goose's union card is perfectly centered.
