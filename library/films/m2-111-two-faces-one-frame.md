# Two Faces in One Frame

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: split-screen comedies, identical-twin confusion, and edits that hide which character is speaking*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: Two lemur sisters play rival shopkeepers in a split-screen comedy, but the editor keeps giving each sister the other's lines and props.

Cast: Della and Deli, lemur twins; Rumi, a macaque director; and Puck, a capuchin editor.

SCENE 1. INT. SMALL STUDIO, MORNING

Della and Deli sit on opposite sides of a painted line. Rumi explains the scene: Della owns a flower shop, Deli owns the bakery next door, and both want the same corner sign. Puck sets up the split-screen camera.

RUMI: Della, you want the sign. Deli, you want the sign.

DELLA: Why does she want it?

DELI: The bakery has a door.

Rumi hands them each a cue card. Both cards say "I was here first." The twins exchange cards. Puck calls action. They deliver each other's lines and point at the wrong shop.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING ROOM, LATER

Puck assembles the first cut. Della appears to steal a loaf from the flower shop. Deli appears to sell roses from the bakery. Rumi asks if the mistake can be used as the joke. Puck says the audience will think the twins are bad at their jobs.

DELLA: I sell flowers.

DELI: I bake bread.

Puck checks the takes. Both sisters wore identical jackets and held identical paper bags. He asks them to switch jackets. They swap jackets, then stand on the wrong marks again. Rumi asks the camera crew to put flower stickers on one side and bread stickers on the other.

PUCK: Della, your mark is the rose. Deli, your mark is the loaf.

DELLA: What if I want bread?

DELI: Then trade at lunch.

They start again. Della lifts a loaf and Deli holds a bouquet. The screen seam cuts the loaf in half, making it look like a floating sandwich. Puck nudges the seam; the roses vanish. Rumi asks if the audience can follow the conversation without seeing both faces. Puck points at the playback monitor, where each sister is smiling at the wrong line.

The next take gets the props right, but Puck's split-screen seam cuts through Della's face. He moves the frame. Now the seam hides Deli's hand as she reaches for the sign. Rumi asks if the seam can follow their movements. Puck says it can, but then the sign appears to move by itself.

SCENE 3. INT. STUDIO SET, AFTERNOON

The sisters improvise a face-to-face scene without a split screen. Della carries a vase; Deli carries a tray of rolls. They argue about the sign, then notice the sign is attached to the shared wall and faces both doors.

DELLA: It points to both shops.

DELI: It has two arrows.

Rumi asks them to recreate the realization for the split-screen version.

RUMI: Look at your half, then look at hers.

DELLA: I see the sign.

DELI: I see the same sign.

Puck moves the seam to the middle. The twins reach for opposite ends and bump knuckles. He laughs, then realizes the sign is now centered perfectly. They try again, holding their own props and speaking to the right camera. Della points left, Deli points right. The two arrows meet at the seam. Puck places the seam between the doors. Della looks left while Deli looks right. The two arrows line up at the center of frame. They each reach for the sign at the same time and bump knuckles.

Rumi calls cut. The twins ask to try again. This time Della holds the vase low, Deli keeps the tray level, and neither reaches across the seam. Puck tracks their eyelines with two small marks on the floor. They deliver the lines to the correct camera and exchange the sign's two ends.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The final cut shows each sister in her own half of the frame. The sign stays centered, pointing to both doors. Puck adds labels with their names at the start. Rumi asks if the labels spoil the mystery.

DELLA: The mystery was the editing.

DELI: And the sign was shared.

At the screening, a child asks which twin baked the rolls. Deli raises her hand. Della offers her the vase to carry while Deli brings the tray. The audience distinguishes them by what they are holding. Puck asks for one last split-screen shot. The twins each look across the seam and nod at the same time. The sisters say the mystery was never who they were.

At the screening, a child asks which twin baked the rolls. Deli raises her hand. Della gives her the vase to carry while Deli brings the tray. The audience can tell them apart by what they are holding.

FINAL IMAGE: The split screen closes into one frame as the twins put the shared sign back between their doors.
