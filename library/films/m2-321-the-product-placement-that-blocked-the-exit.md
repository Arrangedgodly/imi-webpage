# The Product Placement That Blocked the Exit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: product placement deals that force branded objects into scenes regardless of story geography*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR NIMA, a lemur
ACTOR ODO, a baboon
BRAND LIAISON VEX, a macaque
SET DRESSER TAVI, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. MOUNTAIN CABIN SET - MORNING

A rescue scene is arranged inside a cabin. Nima's character has found a radio; Odo's character is outside in a snowstorm. Director Pip blocks the shot toward the door. Brand liaison Vex wheels in a bright red thermos with a silver logo.

VEX: The thermos needs a clean hero angle.

PIP: The hero is calling for help.

VEX: The thermos can help.

Tavi places it on the windowsill. It blocks the only view of the snow. Vex moves it closer to the camera. Now it covers the radio. Nima asks where she should stand. Vex points to a strip of tape directly behind the thermos.

NIMA: I cannot see Odo from there.

VEX: But the audience can see the logo.

PIP: The audience should see the door.

Vex asks the team to keep both in frame. Tavi measures the thermos, the door and the camera. The product is almost as wide as the doorway.

SCENE 2. INT. CABIN SET - LATER

They film the scene. Nima lifts the radio. Odo pounds on the door. Vex signals for a close-up of the thermos. Pip calls cut as the shot fills with steel and branding.

PIP: The storm has vanished.

VEX: The thermos is weatherproof.

ODO: It is inside.

Vex suggests that Nima pour hot water to make steam. Tavi fills it from a kettle, then notices the lid is sealed for the product shot. Nima cannot open it. The scene's radio battery is supposed to be dead, so she cannot call out. Odo pushes the door from outside. The thermos rolls off the sill and stops against the door, preventing it from opening.

ODO: I am trapped by a drink container.

VEX: You are insulated.

Tavi retrieves the thermos and checks for dents. Vex asks whether the scene can include a label shot during the rescue. Pip refuses, but the product deal requires one visible logo. Nima proposes setting it on the table, away from the door and radio.

SCENE 3. INT. CABIN SET - AFTERNOON

The crew resets. Nima reaches for the radio; the thermos is on a table behind her. Odo knocks three times. She answers with three knocks and uses a window latch to signal the rescue crew. Vex asks her to pick up the thermos before signaling. Her hands are occupied with the latch and a flashlight.

VEX: It needs to be used.

PIP: It is a prop, not the solution.

VEX: The contract says featured.

TAVI: Featured means visible. The camera sees it on the table.

Vex checks the contract and admits the logo is in the shot. He asks for Nima to look at it appreciatively. She looks at the thermos, then at Odo pounding the door.

NIMA: You have got to be kidding.

PIP: Keep that.

She signals through the window. A rescue worker outside sees the flashlight. Odo pushes the door; the thermos rolls again, but this time away from the threshold. It bumps into a chair and stays put.

SCENE 4. INT. CABIN SET - EVENING

Pip shoots a wide take. Nima signals, Odo answers, and a rescue worker opens the door from outside. The thermos sits in the deep background, logo visible but not obstructing the action. Vex asks for a product close-up after the rescue. Pip offers a single insert as the crew packs.

VEX: Could she drink from it?

NIMA: She is in a blizzard.

VEX: Warm beverage, emotional relief.

ODO: I am still outside.

Vex agrees to wait until the characters are safe. The rescue worker enters, takes the thermos off the floor and hands it to the set dresser. Tavi signs the prop out. Vex asks whether that counts as product use. Pip says it counts as avoiding a trip hazard. The team runs the scene once more. Nima's signal works, Odo enters, and the door remains clear.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The audience watches the rescue. Some notice the thermos; nobody mistakes it for a radio or a doorstop. Vex waits for applause at the logo shot. A viewer laughs when Odo's character says, "I am still outside." Pip says the line stays because it is the truth.

VEX: The logo was visible.

TAVI: And the door opened.

Vex tries to carry the thermos out. The lobby door swings shut before he reaches it, and the container's wide body holds it open. He glances at the camera, realizes the product is finally helping someone, and stops posing.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex props the lobby door with the thermos, while the cast exits through the clear opening behind him.
