# The Castle's Drawbridge Has a Timer

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: siege fantasies that treat a working drawbridge as an ancient riddle instead of asking the gatekeeper how the timer works*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A visiting knight reaches a castle just as its drawbridge begins to rise, then mistakes the gate schedule for a curse and launches a needless siege.

Cast: knight Mara, a macaque; gatekeeper Volo, a gibbon; scribe Eni, a lemur; guard Rusk, an orangutan; messenger Tavi, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. CASTLE MOAT - DUSK

Mara arrives on a hobby horse as the drawbridge lifts. Volo waves from the gatehouse. A sign reads BRIDGE RISES AT SUNSET FOR INSPECTION.

MARA: The castle has withdrawn its path.

VOLO: We raise it to inspect the chains.

Mara studies the sign but does not dismount. Eni rides up carrying a scroll marked VISITOR HOURS. She says the last crossing is five minutes before sunset. The sun dips behind the wall. Rusk closes the gate shutters.

SCENE 2 - EXT. MOAT - MOMENTS LATER

Mara unrolls a siege map on her hobby horse. The wind catches it and wraps it around the horse's head. Tavi arrives with a small handbell and announces that the bridge will lower after inspection.

MARA: Or the castle is testing our resolve.

TAVI: It is testing the chains. The inspection takes ten minutes.

Mara orders a ladder. Volo points to the visitor entrance around the north wall. She refuses, claiming the quest requires the main gate. Eni reads the scroll aloud: visitors may use either entrance.

SCENE 3 - EXT. NORTH WALL - LATER

The group walks the perimeter. Mara tries to make the detour look like a strategic flank. They reach the north visitor gate, where Rusk checks the guest list. Mara's name is not on it because she booked under her horse's name.

RUSK: Your steed is registered. You are not.

MARA: The horse is my companion.

RUSK: The horse may enter the courtyard. You need to sign the ledger.

Mara signs. The ink freezes in the evening air. Eni warms the pen under her cloak. Tavi rings the bell to mark the inspection's end. From the moat, the drawbridge lowers with a slow creak.

SCENE 3A - EXT. NORTH VISITOR GATE - CONTINUOUS

From the moat, the bridge has lowered for its scheduled inspection. Mara turns toward it, thinking the main gate has opened for her. Volo holds up a palm and asks her to wait behind the marked line. Rusk watches the chains pass through the gatehouse pulley while Tavi reads the inspection checklist aloud. Mara's hobby horse noses over the line. She pulls it back, and its paper mane catches on the visitor sign.

RUSK: The gate is being inspected, not welcoming an army.

MARA: I brought one horse.

TAVI: The visitor sign is for people.

The bridge settles on the moat. Rusk tests each chain, then places a wooden block beneath the counterweight. The bridge rises again after the check. Mara asks how the mechanism knows the inspection is over. Volo points to the bell and the posted schedule. She writes down both details in her quest journal, using the blank page where she planned a siege map.

SCENE 4 - EXT. CASTLE COURTYARD - NIGHT

Mara enters the courtyard through the north visitor gate. Volo shows her the drawbridge chain and counterweight from the safe side of the wall. Mara asks if the bridge can rise while a guest is on it. Volo says the schedule prevents that. Tavi demonstrates the warning bell. It rings before the mechanism moves.

MARA: I was prepared to storm the gate.

VOLO: The gate was waiting for the inspection.

Mara dismounts and parks the hobby horse in a rack. Eni records the bridge mechanism in the visitor log. Rusk stamps the entry with the date and visitor's name. The stamp leaves a horseshoe-shaped mark. Mara asks whether the chain is a relic. Volo hands her the inspection log, which lists the last three checks and a signature beside each. She copies the times into her quest journal. The horseshoe stamp lands beside the entry like a royal seal. Volo pins the same inspection schedule beside it for tomorrow. Mara bows to the notice.

SCENE 5 - INT. CASTLE GREAT HALL - LATER

Mara attends the evening meal. She raises a toast to the gatekeeper and asks when the bridge will rise again. Volo points to the posted schedule. Mara studies it carefully before making a dramatic speech about patience.

TAVI: The bridge will be open tomorrow at dawn.

MARA: Then I shall cross at dawn.

FINAL IMAGE: The drawbridge rests flat over the moat beneath its inspection notice, while Mara's hobby horse waits politely in the courtyard rack.
