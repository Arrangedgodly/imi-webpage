# The Silent Film of the Squeaky Cart

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: silent-film melodrama, where a squeaky cart wheel threatens a grand on-screen chase*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A cart keeper attempts to deliver a silent-film prop before the screening, but the wheel refuses to stay quiet.

Cast: Screenwriter Luma, lemur; projectionist Odo, baboon; actor Kito, macaque; piano player Sava, gibbon.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, AFTERNOON

A projector screen stands at one end. Kito poses beside a cart with a squeaky wheel. Luma holds up title cards.

LUMA: "Our hero must deliver the parcel before sunset."

KITO: I am ready.

Odo pushes the cart. It squeaks loudly.

[SFX: WHEEL SQUEAK.]

SAVA: That was louder than the piano.

ODO: I can oil it.

LUMA: The film is supposed to be silent.

KITO: The audience can hear it.

LUMA: They are not supposed to.

Sava begins playing a dramatic chase melody. Odo rolls the cart; the wheel squeaks in time.

SAVA: The wheel is keeping tempo.

LUMA: The wheel is ruining the scene.

SCENE 2. INT. HALLWAY, CONTINUOUS

They try a second take. Kito pushes the cart without speaking. The parcel falls off. Luma holds up another title card: "The parcel betrays him."

KITO: That is not what happens.

LUMA: It fell.

ODO: The cart has a slanted shelf.

Luma adds a prop string to secure the parcel. Kito pushes again. The string catches on the wheel, spinning the parcel around.

[SFX: WHEEL SQUEAK, PARCEL SPINS.]

SAVA: The piano is now following the parcel.

LUMA: The parcel needs a close-up.

Kito points to the projector. Odo moves the cart toward the camera, but the cart wheel squeaks again. Luma holds up the card "A sound from the past."

KITO: This is not a ghost story.

LUMA: The audience cannot hear dialogue.

KITO: They can hear the wheel.

ODO: We can fix the wheel.

SCENE 3. INT. PROP ROOM, LATER

Odo removes the wheel. It has a loose pin. Sava plays a suspenseful chord while he taps it back in. The sound is rhythmic.

[SFX: METAL TAP, TAP, TAP.]

LUMA: That sounds like a ticking clock.

KITO: The cart is still a cart.

Luma writes a new title card: "The clock races toward sunset."

Odo finishes the repair and spins the wheel. It turns silently.

SAVA: The music has lost its percussion.

ODO: I can add a bell.

LUMA: No new sounds.

Kito pushes the cart down the hallway. It rolls quietly, but now it moves too fast. Kito catches it at the corner.

KITO: It has become a runaway cart.

LUMA: The hero must chase it.

Kito runs after it in exaggerated silent-film style. Sava plays a faster melody. Odo catches the cart before it reaches the stairs.

SAVA: The chase is saved.

ODO: The cart is stopped.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, EVENING

The screening begins.

A second cart rolls onto the screen. It does not squeak, but the silent actor struggles to push it. The audience laughs when he stares at the wheel.

LUMA: We did not write that look.

KITO: I was waiting for the cart to move.

SAVA: The piano can underline the waiting.

She plays three measured notes. The audience laughs again. Odo whispers that the chair squeak has become part of the film. Luma says it was not filmed. They replay the scene; the chair squeak happens in the hall, not on screen.

LUMA: The picture is silent.

ODO: The audience is not.

KITO: The film has two soundtracks.

Sava plays a chord as the audience claps. The real chair squeaks again when Odo stands. The audience watches Kito's performance on screen. Luma reads the title cards at the right moments. The cart rolls silently, the parcel stays in place, and the hero reaches the door before sunset.

LUMA: "He has delivered the parcel."

Kito bows on screen. The real Kito watches from the aisle.

KITO: That is the best take.

Odo relaxes beside the projector. His chair squeaks.

[SFX: CHAIR SQUEAK.]

Luma holds up a title card: "The theater speaks."

Sava stops playing. The audience laughs.

ODO: I can fix the chair too.

LUMA: After the picture.

The film ends. The audience applauds. Sava plays a closing chord. The repaired cart stands in the lobby, silent and ready.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY, NIGHT

Kito takes the parcel from the cart. It is empty.

KITO: The parcel has no contents.

LUMA: It had to be light enough to roll.

ODO: The audience never saw inside.

SAVA: The piano knew.

Luma places the empty parcel on the cart and writes a final title card: "The mystery continues."

KITO: No sequel.

LUMA: Then we should deliver it somewhere.

They roll the cart toward the storage room. It squeaks once.

[SFX: SINGLE WHEEL SQUEAK.]

LUMA: We have our ending.

ODO: I will oil it tomorrow.

SAVA: The wheel gets another performance.

[SFX: PIANO PLAYS ONE SHORT NOTE.]
