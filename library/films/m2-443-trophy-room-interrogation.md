# The Trophy Room Interrogation

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: true-crime documentaries that frame ordinary community mishaps as elaborate conspiracies through ominous reenactments*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A documentary team investigates a missing neighborhood trophy with dramatic reenactments, despite the custodian knowing exactly where it went.

Cast: Reporter Niko, a macaque; editor Tavi, a capuchin; custodian Osa, an orangutan; coach Vela, a gibbon; student Rumi, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY CENTER - MORNING

An empty square sits on a shelf beneath a sign: ANNUAL RELAY TROPHY. Niko narrates while Tavi films a slow push toward the shelf.

NIKO (V.O.): At 6:42 a.m., the trophy vanished from a locked room.

OSA: The room was open. I cleaned it at seven.

NIKO: The disappearance was discovered at six forty-two.

OSA: By whom?

NIKO: The interview request says so.

Vela enters holding a cardboard box.

VELA: I took the trophy to the school for polishing. The team meets there tonight.

Niko turns to camera, unfazed.

NIKO (V.O.): A witness has appeared with a box.

SCENE 2 - INT. INTERVIEW ROOM - LATER

Tavi places a lamp above Rumi, who sits for an interview. The light is too bright, and Rumi shields her eyes.

TAVI: Can we dim it? We want suspense, not a medical exam.

NIKO: Rumi, when did you last see the trophy?

RUMI: Last week. I carried it after the relay.

NIKO: Did anyone ask you to move it?

RUMI: Coach Vela asked me to put it on the shelf.

NIKO: Could that have been a signal?

RUMI: A signal to put the trophy on the shelf?

Tavi lowers the lamp. Niko shows Rumi a reenactment photo of a gloved hand reaching toward an empty stand.

RUMI: That's my hand. I was wiping dust off the shelf.

SCENE 3 - INT. EDITING ROOM - AFTERNOON

Tavi assembles footage. A low musical sting plays over a shot of the empty shelf. Niko speaks in a whisper.

NIKO: We have three possible suspects: the cleaner, the coach and the runner.

TAVI: All three say the trophy was moved for polishing.

NIKO: The story needs a twist.

Tavi pauses the video. The date stamp shows the trophy being placed into a box by Vela. The box label reads SCHOOL POLISHING ROOM.

TAVI: The twist is that there is no crime.

NIKO: The audience came for a mystery.

TAVI: Then let them solve it with the footage.

Niko watches the clip again. Vela lifts the trophy carefully and carries it toward the exit.

SCENE 3A - INT. EDITING ROOM - LATER

Niko hangs photographs of the shelf, the polishing box and the team roster on a board. Red yarn connects every image. The yarn runs from the trophy shelf to the cleaning cart, then to a photograph of the school bus.

NIKO: Every lead connects.

TAVI: The route connects because the bus carried Vela to school.

NIKO: What if the bus was part of the plan?

TAVI: The bus schedule is printed on the wall.

Niko pulls one strand. It tangles around the editing chair and moves the whole board. The photos slide down, leaving only the route schedule pinned in the center.

NIKO: The evidence has rearranged itself.

TAVI: It fell because you tied it to the chair.

Niko takes down the yarn and puts the photographs back into a folder.

NIKO: We will interview Vela at the polishing room, then verify the return.

SCENE 4 - INT. SCHOOL POLISHING ROOM - EVENING

Osa, Niko and Tavi arrive. Vela polishes the trophy. It is intact and gleaming. Rumi holds the relay schedule. The trophy is due back before the awards ceremony.

VELA: I meant to return it this afternoon. The bus was delayed.

OSA: The community center shelf was empty. I told the team it was being polished.

NIKO: The documentary framed this as a disappearance.

RUMI: It was a cleaning errand.

TAVI: We can change the title before publishing.

NIKO: The Missing Trophy has better search ranking.

OSA: The trophy is not missing. It is on the table.

SCENE 5 - INT. COMMUNITY CENTER - NIGHT

The relay team returns. Vela sets the trophy on the shelf. Niko records a correction to camera.

NIKO: The trophy was moved to the school for polishing and has been returned. There was no theft.

TAVI: Use that as the opening, not the ending.

Rumi places a card under the trophy with the return date. Osa checks the room before locking it.

OSA: The shelf is clean. The trophy is here. The door is locked.

NIKO: Are we certain?

OSA: I am standing beside it.

The low suspense sting plays from Tavi's laptop. Everyone looks toward the speakers.

TAVI: That is the old track. It is not evidence.

FINAL IMAGE: The trophy gleams on the shelf under a label reading RETURNED, while the dramatic red circle around the empty square remains on Niko's edit timeline.
