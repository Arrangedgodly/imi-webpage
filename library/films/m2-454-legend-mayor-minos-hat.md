# The Legend of Mayor Mino's Hat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: political biopics that invent sweeping private motives from one ordinary archival object*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A documentary crew tries to make a mayor's old hat the key to a town's history, while the former mayor keeps correcting their dramatic reenactments.

Cast: Former Mayor Mino, a macaque; director Kavi, a gibbon; researcher Tali, a capuchin; reenactor Boro, a lemur; archivist Nessa, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. TOWN ARCHIVE - MORNING

Nessa removes a brown hat from a display box. Kavi frames it in a shaft of light. Tali holds a folder of records.

KAVI: The hat changed the course of local politics.

NESSA: It was worn at the 1982 parade.

TALI: The records say Mayor Mino lost it on the bridge and a child returned it.

KAVI: A symbol of humility.

Mino enters carrying a newspaper clipping.

MINO: I left it on the bus. The child found it at the station.

KAVI: The bridge is more cinematic.

MINO: I was not on the bridge.

SCENE 2 - INT. REENACTMENT SET - LATER

Boro wears the hat and stands beside a cardboard bus. Kavi directs a slow-motion scene. The hat blows off his head, flies past the bus and lands in a paper basket.

KAVI: That is the turning point.

BORO: The fan is too strong.

TALI: The real record says the bus driver gave the hat to the lost-and-found desk.

KAVI: The audience needs a dramatic recovery.

MINO: The desk was in a small room. There was no music.

SCENE 2A - INT. TOWN ARCHIVE - CONTINUOUS

Tali searches the bus log. Kavi moves the brown hat beneath a spotlight and asks Boro to rehearse a solemn expression.

BORO: I am waiting for the director to finish.

KAVI: Think about the burden of office.

BORO: I am thinking about the hat slipping over my eyes.

Nessa brings an index card with the bus route and date. The card says the hat was found on Tuesday, returned on Wednesday.

TALI: The parade happened on Saturday. It was not an election week.

KAVI: Our script says the hat vanished the night before the vote.

MINO: There was no vote that week. The council had already passed the bus schedule.

Kavi removes the election poster from the set. He swaps it for a printed route timetable. Boro takes off the hat and places it safely in the display box.

NESSA: The archive has the ordinary dates. Use those.

TALI: Then we have a short story, not a scandal.

SCENE 3 - INT. TOWN HALL - AFTERNOON

The crew stages a scene in which Mino gives a speech about returning the hat. Boro holds a microphone. Kavi asks him to look at the hat as if it were a rival candidate.

BORO: I am not the mayor.

KAVI: You are playing him.

MINO: I did not make a speech. I filled out a lost-item form.

TALI: I found the form in the archive. It says "brown hat, one size, bus route four."

Kavi reads the form. The handwriting is Mino's.

KAVI: The plot is less dramatic now.

MINO: The bus driver gave it back. I thanked him.

SCENE 4 - INT. ARCHIVE SCREENING ROOM - EVENING

Tali cuts together the real records with the reenactment. The hat flies in slow motion, then the image cuts to the lost-item form. The dramatic score stops. Kavi watches the edit, unhappy.

KAVI: We have a symbol and a form.

NESSA: The form shows how the office worked. It is part of the town's history.

MINO: The bus route changed the next year. The lost-and-found desk moved too.

TALI: That gives us a real consequence. We can show the old route map beside the form.

Kavi removes the bridge scene from the timeline. Boro takes off the hat and returns it to its box.

SCENE 5 - INT. TOWN MUSEUM - NEXT DAY

The crew screens the revised film for visitors. It shows the bus, the lost-and-found form, and Mino returning to the station to thank the driver. The hat sits in the display case.

VISITOR: Was it really important?

MINO: It was important to me because I needed it for the parade. The bus driver kept it safe.

KAVI: We tried to make it a symbol of a turning point.

NESSA: It can be an object and a story without carrying the whole town.

Tali pins the route map beside the display. The map clearly shows the station and lost-and-found room.

Mino puts his current cap on. He checks that the hat in the case is secure and leaves with the visitors.

FINAL IMAGE: The brown hat rests beside the plain bus form, while a caption reads: RETURNED BY ROUTE FOUR, NOT FOUND ON A BRIDGE.
