# The Footsteps Behind Cabin Five

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: summer-camp slasher horror, final-counselor rules, and suspenseful reveal pacing*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: Camp counselors investigate footsteps circling Cabin Five, but a masked figure is only trying to return a lost canoe paddle while the counselors turn every routine noise into a horror set piece.

Cast: Nella, a macaque head counselor; Pip, a capuchin who studies scary films; Sedge, a turtle lifeguard; Bo, a shy bear cub; and the Masked Figure, a raccoon wearing a canoe cover.

SCENE 1. EXT. CAMPFIRE, DUSK

Pip reads from a battered notebook. The campers roast marshmallows as the trees creak.

PIP: Rule one: never split up. Rule two: never investigate a noise. Rule three: never say, "I'll be right back."

NELLA: How do we follow rules one and two at once?

PIP: We stay together and don't go anywhere.

Sedge hears a branch snap. He points to a raccoon near the snack shed. Everyone turns. The raccoon disappears behind a canoe cover. Pip closes the notebook and says the opening has begun.

SCENE 2. INT. CABIN FIVE, NIGHT

Three slow knocks sound on the cabin wall. Pip locks the door. Nella opens it to check the porch. Pip grabs her sleeve.

PIP: That's how they get you.

NELLA: Who?

PIP: We don't know yet. That's why it's suspenseful.

A flashlight beam passes the window. Bo dives under a blanket. Sedge looks through the glass and spots wet prints heading toward the canoe rack. Nella takes a flashlight, Sedge takes the first-aid kit, and Pip insists they all move together. The line stretches from the cabin door to the path, with Pip at the back holding the door so it won't slam.

SCENE 3. EXT. CANOE RACK, CONTINUOUS

A figure in a canoe cover stands beside the rack. Pip raises the notebook like a shield. Nella calls out. The figure lifts a paddle and points toward the ground. Sedge recognizes the cover from the lost-and-found.

SEDGE: That's the raccoon.

PIP: It has a disguise.

NELLA: The disguise is wet.

The raccoon places the paddle down and backs away. Pip says they must not accept the object because it could be cursed. Nella asks how a paddle would become cursed. Pip opens the notebook to check. A gust flips the pages to a sketch of a hockey mask. The raccoon taps the paddle again and points at the equipment ledger tied to the rack.

SCENE 4. EXT. WOODEN BRIDGE, NIGHT

A branch cracks overhead. Pip orders everyone to freeze. A pinecone drops onto his hat. He whispers that the killer has excellent aim. Sedge removes the pinecone and shows it is still attached to a twig. Nella tells Pip to put the notebook away long enough to watch the path. He holds it open anyway, using it as a map.

The group follows a line of muddy prints to the bridge. Bo's raft is tied across the walkway. Pip says the bridge is the midpoint where someone falls. Nella unties the rope. The raft glides away, bumps a rock, and stops without damage.

PIP: The raft has been defeated.

BO: It was tied wrong.

Another pair of prints appears beside theirs. They match Grub the groundskeeper's boots. Grub arrives carrying a toolbox. He explains he moved canoes out of the rain and left the paddle by the rack. The raccoon tried to bring it back. Pip asks why Grub did not leave a note. Grub shows them a note, now soaked and stuck to his boot.

SCENE 5. INT. CAMP OFFICE, LATER

Grub opens the ledger. Pip asks whether the handwriting belongs to a suspect. Grub says it belongs to him. Nella checks the date, and Sedge checks the paddle for the camp number painted on its handle. The number matches. Pip agrees the evidence is overwhelming, then asks if they can still use the flashlight dramatically.

Nella checks the equipment ledger. The paddle was signed out for a practice lesson and returned by no one because Grub moved it. She writes a clear return time and asks Pip to help. Pip crosses out the notebook rule about confronting the monster alone.

PIP: The final counselor is supposed to face it alone.

NELLA: The final counselor is supposed to lock the shed.

The raccoon waits outside. Sedge opens the door and accepts the paddle. The raccoon points at the loose snack-shed latch. Nella marks it for repair. Pip jumps when the latch snaps shut behind him; the raccoon calmly holds it open.

Bo volunteers to make a new sign for the equipment rack. He writes RETURN PADDLES HERE and draws an arrow. Pip examines it for hidden meaning; the arrow points directly at the rack.

FINAL IMAGE: At breakfast, Pip reads the camp's new safety rules aloud: return gear, secure latches, and ask before borrowing a canoe cover. A page turns in the breeze. He flinches; Nella hands him the notebook and tells him to hold it down.
