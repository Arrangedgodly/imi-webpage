# The Museum of Unfinished Endings

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mystery films, dramatic reveals, and the archive that keeps every suspect waiting for a final explanation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a macaque curator
VEX, an orangutan detective
PIP, a capuchin guard
NARI, a lemur archivist
ODO, a gibbon visitor

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM OF STORIES - MORNING

A velvet rope divides the gallery. One exhibit is a locked box labeled "ENDING, DO NOT OPEN UNTIL ACT THREE." Vex circles it, magnifying glass raised.

VEX: Someone has stolen the conclusion.

MARA: The ending is inside the box.

NARI: The box is part of the exhibit.

VEX: Then the thief has not escaped.

Pip stands at the door with a guest book. Odo enters and writes "I did it" before seeing the exhibit.

ODO: I thought this was a mystery play.

VEX: You have confessed before the crime.

ODO: I am trying to be helpful.

Vex interviews him. Odo says he arrived to see the ending. Mara unlocks the box; no ending is visible beneath its felt false floor. A paper tag dangles from the latch: "Please restore after use."

SCENE 2. INT. ARCHIVE ROOM - LATER

Nari searches the records. The museum keeps several endings for the same story: a culprit revealed, a twin revealed, a dream revealed, and a sudden storm. Vex rules out each one.

VEX: The culprit reveal is too obvious.

MARA: You have not met the culprit.

VEX: Exactly.

Pip moves a stack of scripts. A page falls out, ending with "To be continued." Vex treats it as evidence. Odo picks it up and reads the back. It is a shopping list.

ODO: Someone needed milk.

NARI: Or a sequel.

Vex asks the staff to stay in the gallery. He draws a chalk outline around the empty box. The outline is square, and Odo accidentally stands in it. Vex announces that the missing ending has left a human-shaped gap.

MARA: Odo is standing there.

VEX: That is what the ending wants us to think.

SCENE 3. INT. MAIN GALLERY - AFTERNOON

Vex gathers everyone for a reenactment. He gives Mara a key, Pip a flashlight, and Nari a script. Odo gets the role of "mysterious stranger."

ODO: I am already a visitor.

VEX: You are now a visitor with motive.

The reenactment begins. Mara approaches the box. Pip shines the flashlight at the ceiling, where a projector shows a silhouette of a hand reaching for the key. Nari turns a page and discovers the script has no final scene.

NARI: The playwright never wrote one.

VEX: Then we have a writer on the loose.

Mara points to the museum's blank wall. A handwritten note there says "Ask the audience." The crowd reads it. Vex turns to the visitors and demands a culprit. They nominate him.

VEX: I could not have stolen the ending. I am investigating it.

PIP: You are also holding the key.

Vex looks down. The key has been in his hand since the reenactment began. He puts it on the table. A click sounds from the box.

SCENE 4. INT. GALLERY - CONTINUOUS

Mara opens the box. Inside is a stack of blank cards and a pencil. Nari recognizes them from the interactive exhibit. Visitors were meant to write their preferred endings and drop them through a slot.

NARI: The audience supplied the conclusion.

VEX: Then why was the box empty?

PIP: The slot is in the back.

Vex turns the box around. The slot is blocked by his chalk outline. Odo helps move it; the false floor tips and cards spill from the hidden space. Each gives a different ending: "It was the rain," "They were twins," "The detective was a hat," and "Everybody goes home."

VEX: The hat is a suspect.

MARA: The hat is mine.

Odo tries on the hat. Vex arrests it anyway, placing it on the evidence table. The hat's brim tips over the blank guest book, hiding Odo's confession.

Pip takes the flashlight and shines it through the hat. A beam lands on Vex's chalk outline. The visitors gasp again.

VEX: The hat has a secret passage.

NARI: It has a hole.

Vex lifts the hat carefully, expecting a clue. A feather falls out and lands in the square outline. Odo says it belongs to the costume gallery. Vex writes "feather: unresolved" on his pad.

SCENE 5. INT. FRONT DESK - EVENING

Mara asks Vex to write the museum's official ending. He considers the cards, then gives the pencil to Odo.

ODO: What if the mystery is why everyone needs one ending?

VEX: That is not a culprit.

NARI: It is an answer.

Vex accepts it and writes the sentence on the display card. The museum reopens the box with the new ending inside. Odo signs the guest book beneath the card, then crosses out "I did it" and writes "I saw it."

FINAL IMAGE: Vex stands beside the box, waiting for the next visitor to accuse him, while the hat remains under arrest.
