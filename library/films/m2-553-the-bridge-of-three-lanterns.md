# The Bridge of Three Lanterns

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy quest films that turn a safe footbridge into a prophecy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
TRAVELER PIP, a capuchin
GUIDE MARA, a macaque
ORACLE VEX, an orangutan
KEEPER ODO, a gibbon
SCOUT NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. WOODEN BRIDGE - DUSK

Three lanterns hang from the bridge rail. A painted sign reads: CLOSED DURING REPAIR. Pip studies the water beyond it.

VEX: The prophecy says we cross when the third lantern burns blue.

MARA: The third lantern is a work light. The bridge is closed because two boards are loose.

ODO: The repair crew is on the opposite bank. They placed the sign before leaving for tools.

PIP: We can take the marked lower path. It adds ten minutes.

VEX: The prophecy forbids delay.

NARI: The lower path is open and has a handrail.

SCENE 2. EXT. BRIDGE APPROACH - CONTINUOUS

Vex takes a blue scarf from his satchel and wraps it around the work light.

MARA: Take that off. The repair crew needs the light visible.

VEX: It reveals the hidden color.

ODO: It conceals the work lamp, which is supposed to warn people away.

PIP: The sign is clear. Nobody crosses.

Vex turns to the group, cloak billowing. A breeze pushes the scarf across his face.

VEX: The oracle is blinded.

MARA: The scarf is on your own light. Remove it.

Vex removes the scarf. The lamp shines white again. He looks at the wooden boards and spots a loose plank.

VEX: The bridge is indeed in danger.

NARI: The warning worked.

SCENE 2A. EXT. BRIDGE APPROACH - CONTINUOUS

Mara attaches a small barrier rope across the closed bridge path. Odo checks the repair note attached to the lantern post.

ODO: The crew removed two loose boards. They return with replacements tomorrow morning.

VEX: If we cross after dark, no one will see us.

PIP: The boards will still be loose in the dark.

NARI: We can leave a message for the crew that the barrier is in place. That is the safe action.

VEX: But the third lantern points toward the bridge.

MARA: It points toward the bridge because that is where the work is happening. The lower path is marked in yellow.

Vex takes the scarf from his bag and looks at the light. He folds it and puts it away.

VEX: The oracle acknowledges the sign.

ODO: The repair note says the barrier should remain until inspection. I'll add that the lantern is visible from both paths.

PIP: We should move the fallen branch away from the lower trail before it gets dark.

MARA: Only if it can be moved from the path without stepping near the bank. Otherwise we report it.

Nari lifts the branch end with a gloved hand and lays it beyond the trail edge. The walking surface is clear.

SCENE 2B. EXT. LOWER PATH - DUSK

The path forks around a stand of reeds. Vex chooses the wider branch, but a fallen limb blocks it.

VEX: Another trial from the prophecy.

PIP: The limb is too heavy to lift safely. The narrow branch is marked open.

MARA: We'll use the open branch and report the blockage at the trail board.

ODO: The path marker points around the reeds. The slope is gentle on this side.

NARI: I can go ahead and make sure the lantern at the turn is lit.

Vex steps toward the fallen limb. Mara holds out an arm.

MARA: Don't climb over it. The ground beyond the limb is soft.

VEX: The oracle will follow the marker.

He turns onto the open route. The group walks in single file; Pip checks the footing and Odo keeps the barrier note safe in a folder.

SCENE 3. EXT. LOWER PATH - NIGHT

The group follows the lower path. A stream crossing is marked by stepping stones. Mara tests the first stone with her staff.

PIP: The middle stone is wet. I'll go around the wide side.

VEX: The prophecy says three steps.

MARA: Then make three safe steps on the dry path.

ODO: The lower trail joins the bridge on the far bank.

They walk along the path, staying within the lantern markers. Vex keeps the scarf in his bag.

SCENE 4. EXT. FAR BANK - NIGHT

The repair crew tightens the boards. The bridge remains closed. The group reaches the far bank by the lower path.

VEX: We crossed the river without crossing the bridge.

MARA: We followed the open route.

PIP: The three lanterns guided us to the path marker. Their job was not to decode a prophecy.

ODO: Repairs will finish tomorrow. The bridge will reopen after inspection.

NARI: We can return by the path in daylight.

VEX: Could the prophecy say that?

MARA: It already says "closed during repair".

TAG: Vex reads the sign aloud in a dramatic voice. Pip points to the lower path, which has a perfectly ordinary arrow.
