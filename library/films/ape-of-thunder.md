# Ape of Thunder

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: Superhero origin stories and sprawling franchise crossovers*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M1*

Logline: A shy bonobo sound technician gains the power to redirect thunder and must save a city from an attention-hungry villain who wants to turn every storm into a branded spectacle.

Cast: Bongo, a bonobo technician who hates microphones; Thunderape, his caped alter ego; Dr. Glitz, a peacock media villain; Saffron, a squirrel weather student; Captain Lemur, a retired hero with a damaged glider; and the Chorus, a flock of pigeons who run an unauthorized fan club.

SCENE 1. EXT. SKYLINE FESTIVAL, NIGHT

Bongo repairs a speaker beneath the main stage. The city has paid Dr. Glitz to unveil a tower that will "make weather exciting." A storm rolls in. Glitz stands onstage wearing a cape with sponsor patches.

GLITZ: Tonight, we own the sky!

Lightning strikes the tower. Bongo grabs a cable to save a child. The bolt travels through the speaker system and out through his hands. Every lamp in the plaza flashes at once. Bongo stands smoking slightly, his fur lifted like dandelion fluff.

SAFFRON: Did you just catch lightning?

BONGO: I was trying to fix the left speaker.

SCENE 2. INT. BONGO'S APARTMENT, MORNING

Bongo tests his power with a desk fan. He can pull the crackle from a storm cloud and send it through metal, but only when he is calm. When he gets nervous, the lights flicker. The Chorus pigeons gather on his fire escape, cooing his name.

CAPTAIN LEMUR arrives with an old cape and a cracked helmet. He once protected the city but retired after a merchandising dispute.

LEMUR: A hero needs a name.

BONGO: Could we start with a quiet room?

The pigeons have printed shirts. One shirt says THUNDERAPE. Bongo hates it. Saffron says it sounds like a bad cereal. They keep the name.

SCENE 3. EXT. CITY WATERFRONT, NIGHT

Glitz switches on his tower. It attracts every storm cloud over the coast and projects them onto a giant screen. The tower overloads. Lightning leaps between buildings. A ferry loses power, drifting toward the seawall.

Thunderape arrives, wearing the cape backward. Saffron spots a grounded rail line that can conduct the charge away from the ferry. Captain Lemur attempts to launch his glider, which folds in half and lands in a hot dog cart.

Bongo climbs the tower. He can feel the lightning shaking inside it, like a drumroll. He takes a breath and redirects one bolt into the rail. The ferry's engines restart. The passengers cheer.

GLITZ: You are ruining the reveal!

THUNDERAPE: I thought the reveal was the ferry.

SCENE 4. INT. TOWER CONTROL ROOM, CONTINUOUS

Glitz sends the tower's remaining energy toward the stadium, where thousands watch the weather show. Bongo could absorb it, but one surge may fry the equipment and his nervous system. Saffron points out that the pigeons' old roost has copper gutters connected to the tower's ground wire.

Bongo calls the Chorus. The pigeons fly to the roof, where they peck at the cable ties. They are not trained, just very committed to snacks. The line drops. Bongo channels the lightning through the gutters and into the wet earth below.

The tower goes dark. Glitz's screen displays the words PLEASE WAIT, then a large picture of a peacock wearing sunglasses. The crowd laughs. Glitz sees the people watching the ferry crew instead of him. His cape sags.

SCENE 5. EXT. WATERFRONT, LATER

Police take Glitz away. Bongo checks the ferry passengers, then helps the stadium crew mop up rainwater. A child asks for an autograph. He signs a paper napkin with a shaky hand.

CAPTAIN LEMUR: The city needs a symbol.

BONGO: The city needs working ground wires.

LEMUR: Symbols sell shirts.

The Chorus pigeons roll out a fresh batch of shirts. One says CHECK YOUR GROUND WIRE. The other says THUNDERAPE. Bongo buys the first one and wears it over his suit.

Saffron invites him to help teach a storm safety class. He agrees, but asks if he can stand near the back.

SCENE 6. EXT. COMMUNITY ROOFTOP, DAWN

The storm clears. Bongo stands with Saffron and Captain Lemur above the city. The repaired tower now broadcasts weather warnings, not advertisements. A distant rumble shakes the windows.

BONGO: I should go.

Saffron hands him a grounded copper umbrella. He takes it, then realizes the storm is moving inland, away from the city. He lowers the umbrella and listens.

The Chorus lands on the roof, carrying one final shirt. It reads NO SEQUEL UNTIL THE WIRES ARE FIXED. Bongo laughs. The pigeons coo as the sun rises.

He walks down the stairs with them, one flight at a time.
