# The Foreign-Market Crop Cutoff

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: international distribution edits that crop compositions, replace signs, and dub jokes without consulting the story*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR LUMA, a lemur
LOCALIZATION EDITOR PIP, a capuchin
DISTRIBUTOR VEX, a macaque
ACTOR NARI, an orangutan
SUBTITLE EDITOR ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. INT. POSTPRODUCTION SUITE - MORNING

A monitor shows a wide shot of Nari entering a crowded market. Director Luma composed the frame so a red awning points toward a narrow stall, where Nari finds a lost parcel. Distributor Vex places a black matte over both sides of the image.

VEX: The new market needs a square frame.

LUMA: Then the awning and stall disappear.

PIP: I can move the camera in.

ODO: The parcel is on the left edge.

Vex asks to move the parcel toward the center. The crop now cuts off Nari's hand as she reaches for it. Nari watches the frame and asks where her other arm went. Vex says the arm belongs to another territory.

SCENE 2. INT. POSTPRODUCTION SUITE - LATER

Pip reframes the shot for a vertical screen. The awning becomes a red stripe; the stall and parcel remain visible, but the market crowd is gone. Odo adjusts subtitles to fit the narrower picture. Nari's line, "The parcel is under the red awning," now appears over the awning itself.

NARI: The words are covering the clue.

ODO: I can put them above.

VEX: The title is above.

LUMA: Put them below the picture.

The subtitle overlaps the parcel. Odo shortens the line to "There." Nari points toward three stalls. Vex asks if the gesture can be made more universal. She points more broadly; the subtitle now sits over the whole row of stalls. Luma asks for the original wide frame. Vex says the distribution package needs all formats by noon.

SCENE 3. INT. RECORDING BOOTH - AFTERNOON

Vex arrives with the missed noon deadline printed in red.

VEX: The package is late.

LUMA: You exported a film without its parcel. It can be early or complete.

Vex puts the deadline under the booth door to stop it rattling. Pip records a shorter line for the dubbed version. The original dialogue mentions the awning and the parcel's paper seal. The translated line says, "Look at the red roof." In the shot, the roof is brown after a color change requested for the new market palette. Odo edits the subtitle to say, "Find the package." The line appears after Nari has already picked it up.

VEX: The audience will catch up.

LUMA: The audience is watching her hands.

Pip adds the package's paper seal to the dub. The line fits, but the sound continues after the scene ends. Nari is already in the next location while her voice says, "The seal is unbroken." Vex suggests it is a memory. Luma says it is the previous scene.

SCENE 4. INT. POSTPRODUCTION SUITE - EVENING

Luma rebuilds the composition in three safe frames, each preserving Nari's hand, the parcel, and the awning's pointing edge. Pip records a short line: "Red awning. Sealed parcel." Odo places the subtitle in the lower margin and times it to Nari's gesture. Vex asks them to remove the awning color because it varies between formats.

NARI: The awning is how I find the parcel.

VEX: Could it be a sign?

LUMA: The red cloth is the sign.

They test the vertical, square, and wide versions side by side. The square export tries to center Nari's face, pushing her reaching hand out of view. Pip shifts the crop down; now the awning is missing. Luma asks Vex to choose between a centered face and a readable action. He watches the parcel vanish from the first version, then accepts a slightly off-center face. In each format Nari sees the awning, reaches beneath it, and lifts the sealed parcel. The dub ends as her hand closes around the package. Vex tries one more crop, but it cuts off his own reflection in the monitor. He steps back and lets the three frames run.

SCENE 5. INT. SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The international versions play in sequence. Nari's clue survives every crop. The subtitle sits beneath the image, and the dub stops when the scene ends. Vex watches the preview without reaching for the crop tool. A guest asks if the red awning is important. Nari answers that it is the reason she finds the parcel.

VEX: The frame can travel.

LUMA: The clue travels with it.

Odo saves the three versions under clear labels. Vex requests one more export with the subtitle moved higher. Odo shows him the preview: the words would cover Nari's hand again. Vex closes the request window. The projector clicks off, leaving the three frames side by side.

FINAL IMAGE: Three versions of the same frame glow on the monitor, each showing Nari's hand, the red awning, and the sealed parcel in the same order.
