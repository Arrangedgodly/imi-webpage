# The Masked Wrestler's Entrance Takes Ten

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: professional wrestling spectacle that makes the walk to the ring more elaborate than the actual match*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A masked monkey wrestler plans a thunderous entrance, but each special effect blocks the aisle and delays the neighborhood charity match.

Cast: Iron Tail, a macaque wrestler; promoter Javi, a gibbon; rival Toma, a capuchin; stagehand Sela, a lemur; referee Boro, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY GYM - EVENING

A small ring sits beneath fluorescent lights. Iron Tail stands behind a curtain wearing a silver mask. Javi checks a list of entrance cues.

JAVI: Fog, drumroll, spotlight, name announcement, then walk to the ring.

SELA: The fog machine is borrowed from the school play. It has one setting.

IRON TAIL: I need a mysterious entrance.

SELA: It is a gym. We need to keep the fire exit visible.

Toma warms up in the ring, stretching quietly. Boro checks the ropes and the floor mats.

BORO: When the bell rings, stay inside the ropes and listen for the count.

JAVI: Our opening match starts in five minutes.

SCENE 2 - INT. GYM ENTRANCE - MOMENTS LATER

The fog machine starts. It fills the aisle so thickly that Iron Tail cannot see the ring. A spotlight shines into the fog and bounces back into Javi's face.

JAVI: The audience cannot see the wrestler.

IRON TAIL: They can hear my entrance music.

The music starts. Iron Tail takes two steps, trips on the fog machine's power cord and grabs the curtain. The curtain pulls down a banner reading WELCOME, DONORS.

SELA: The exit is covered.

Boro turns off the fog machine and lifts the banner. Javi moves the cord away from the aisle.

SCENE 3 - INT. RING - LATER

Iron Tail enters through the ropes. The crowd cheers. Toma extends a gloved hand for the customary greeting. Iron Tail slaps it away, staying in character.

TOMA: We are doing a friendly exhibition.

IRON TAIL: The crowd expects rivalry.

Boro rings the bell. Iron Tail performs a dramatic pose, but his cape catches on the bottom rope. Toma helps free it. The crowd thinks it is part of the act and applauds.

IRON TAIL: I have been trapped by my own entrance.

TOMA: Keep moving. The cape is loose now.

The match begins. Toma ducks a slow charge. Iron Tail rolls safely across the mat, then realizes the bell has not sounded again. He looks to Boro.

SCENE 4 - INT. RING - CONTINUOUS

Boro raises a hand to pause the action. The crowd quiets. Iron Tail checks that Toma is okay. Toma nods. They restart with a planned hold and a careful escape. Iron Tail drops to one knee for the count; Boro counts aloud. He rises before three, but the cape slips over his mask.

TOMA: I cannot see your eyes.

IRON TAIL: I cannot see anything.

Toma lifts the cape away. Iron Tail ties it around his waist. They finish with a staged shoulder pin. Boro counts to two, and Iron Tail kicks out cleanly. The crowd cheers.

SCENE 4A - INT. GYM - CONTINUOUS

The crowd cheers for the wrestlers after the match. Javi tries to start a second entrance sequence for the award photo. The fog machine sputters once and releases a small cloud toward the ropes.

SELA: I unplugged it.

JAVI: The cord is still in the outlet.

Sela pulls the plug. The smoke clears. Iron Tail takes the silver mask from the mat and checks that the elastic is not caught on the cape.

IRON TAIL: My grand entrance took longer than the match.

TOMA: We had one safe match and three false starts.

Boro reads the charity total from a card. The crowd applauds the amount, then starts to leave.

JAVI: They wanted a second pose.

SELA: They want the exit clear.

The stagehand rolls the equipment toward the storage room. One wheel squeaks. The wrestlers move their chairs out of the aisle and help guide the fog machine around the corner.

BORO: The next event can use a plain walk-on.

JAVI: We can still announce Iron Tail's name.

IRON TAIL: Once is enough.

SCENE 5 - INT. GYM - LATER

The exhibition ends. Javi announces the charity total while Sela folds the banner and stores the fog machine. Iron Tail removes his mask and helps coil the power cord.

JAVI: Iron Tail has raised enough for new floor mats.

IRON TAIL: Next time, we skip the fog.

TOMA: Keep the music. It covered the first tumble.

BORO: No cords across the aisle. No capes near the ropes.

Iron Tail puts the mask on a hook, where it slides down over the exit sign. Sela moves it to a labeled costume shelf.

FINAL IMAGE: The wrestlers hold the new mat certificate between them as the fog machine sits unplugged beside the folded welcome banner.
