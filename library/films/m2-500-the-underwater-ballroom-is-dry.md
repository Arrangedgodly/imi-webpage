# The Underwater Ballroom Is Dry

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: underwater adventure films that depend on dramatic bubbles and slow-motion swims, even when the tank is drained for maintenance*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A crew arrives to shoot an underwater ballroom waltz, but the studio tank has been drained, forcing the director to fake the ocean with fans and a blue sheet.

Cast: director Miko, a macaque; lead dancer Rava, a gibbon; camera operator Seli, a capuchin; tank engineer Pora, an orangutan; choreographer Luma, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. STUDIO TANK - MORNING

The enormous water tank is empty except for a ladder and a floor drain. Miko enters holding a script titled THE BALLROOM BENEATH THE SEA. Rava wears a silver costume with fin-shaped sleeves. Seli checks the camera housing.

MIKO: The water should be up to the balcony.

PORA: The tank is scheduled for cleaning today.

LUMA: We booked the tank for the waltz.

Pora shows them the maintenance calendar. The booking is for the dry rehearsal room, one door over. Miko says the ocean scene cannot be rehearsed on land. Seli points out that the camera housing is still sealed.

SCENE 2 - INT. DRY REHEARSAL ROOM - LATER

Luma teaches Rava the slow underwater waltz. The dancers move gracefully, then stop when their feet hit the floor. Miko asks for more drift. Pora brings a rolling platform. Rava stands on it and glides across the room. The platform squeaks at every turn. Seli films from low angle to hide the wheels.

MIKO: Add bubbles.

PORA: The tank is dry.

Miko turns on two small fans. Rava's fin sleeves flap wildly. Luma pins them to the costume with clips. The dancers attempt another take. The fan blows the blue backdrop forward, wrapping it around Rava like a curtain.

SCENE 3 - INT. STUDIO TANK - AFTERNOON

Pora finishes the tank inspection and allows a shallow fill for a camera test. Miko asks for a full ballroom set. Pora says the tank is not ready for a full fill. Seli lowers the camera housing into the shallow water. It fogs immediately. Miko interprets the condensation as sea mist.

SELI: The lens is fogging.

MIKO: The ocean is revealing itself.

Rava enters the shallow tank for a short waltz. The water reaches her ankles. Miko asks her to swim. She performs a careful toe glide, then steps over the drain cover. A bubble rises from the drain, startling the crew. Pora explains that air is escaping from the cleaning hose.

SCENE 3A - INT. TANK CONTROL BOOTH - MOMENTS LATER

Miko looks at the depth gauge. It reads six inches. He holds the script in front of it so the camera cannot see the number. Seli asks for a wider shot; the frame shows the ladder, drain and empty balcony. Luma rearranges the blue backdrop behind Rava. The reflection makes the shallow water appear deep, but only from one angle. Pora marks that camera position with tape.

MIKO: We have found the ocean's edge.

PORA: You have found the fill line.

Rava rehearses the turn. Her fin sleeves drag in the water and make small waves. Seli captures the ripples in close-up. Miko asks them to move the tape mark half a step. The illusion vanishes, revealing Rava's boots and the floor drain.

SCENE 4 - INT. DRY REHEARSAL ROOM - LATER

The crew screens the test footage. The shallow water reflects the blue backdrop, making the ankle-deep tank look much deeper. Seli adds a slow dissolve between the rolling platform take and the shallow-water shot. The cut hides the wheels and makes Rava appear to float across the ballroom floor. Miko asks for one more take. Pora says the tank must be drained again before the evening cleaning.

LUMA: We have one pass before the floor is dry.

They film the waltz. The fans blow the backdrop into the reflection, creating the illusion of a wave. Rava turns, her fin sleeve catches the blue cloth, and Seli keeps filming as the ocean wraps around the dancer. Pora holds the cloth while Luma frees the sleeve. Miko keeps the shot because the fabric resembles a sea creature.

SCENE 5 - INT. STUDIO SCREENING ROOM - NIGHT

The cast watches the finished sequence. The ballroom looks underwater, though the crew knows it was filmed in shallow water and on wheels. Miko thanks Pora for the deep sea performance. Pora asks them to return the rolling platform to the rehearsal room. Seli labels the footage shallow-water ballroom, and Miko crosses out the word shallow. Rava removes the fin sleeves and helps fold the backdrop.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty tank gleams under blue lights, while a small sign by the drain reads DEEP WATER TOMORROW.
