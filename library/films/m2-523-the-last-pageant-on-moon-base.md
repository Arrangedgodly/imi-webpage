# The Last Pageant on Moon Base

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-station survival adventures mixed with a prestige pageant, a low oxygen alarm, and a show that must go on*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: CAPTAIN ORI, a baboon; SULA, a gibbon pageant director; PAX, an orangutan engineer; TESS, a macaque contestant; VEE, a capuchin announcer; DOCTOR UMBRA, a mandrill medic.

INT. MOON BASE AUDITORIUM - NIGHT

A banner reads LUNAR COLONY PAGEANT. Contestants wait beneath a row of paper stars. A yellow pressure light blinks above the stage. Captain Ori steps to the microphone.

ORI: The base has an oxygen leak in the east corridor. We are moving everyone to the west module.

SULA: The pageant is in the west module.

VEE: The show has moved with the audience.

A contestant's foil cape brushes the door sensor. The pressure seal chirps. Pax hurries over and asks everyone to keep fabric away from the airlock. Sula removes the cape and folds it into a costume case.

PAX: The door is sealed. The sensor is only warning us about the gap.

ORI: We still keep the entrance clear.

Tess checks the seating and sees that three contestants are standing in front of the vent. She moves their chairs into a half circle. The audience shifts with them, leaving a clear path to the emergency hatch.

TESS: If we turn the stage, no one blocks the airflow.

SULA: And the judges can still see the runway.

The director rotates the simple portable platform by hand. One leg catches on a cable. The crew lifts it together and sets it down in the marked square. A loose paper star drifts toward the vent. Pax catches it before it reaches the grille.

Pax checks a wall panel. Doctor Umbra hands out small masks.

PAX: The leak is near the storage bay. The tank is stable, but the corridor is closed.

Tess picks up a paper star from the floor and places it on a contestant's sash.

TESS: Are we still doing the costume round?

ORI: If the corridor stays clear.

Doctor Umbra checks the mask seals and asks the contestants to practice putting them on. One contestant tries to pose with the mask in one hand and nearly drops it. Sula stops the pose and shows how to secure the strap first.

DOCTOR UMBRA: The costume can wait. The seal cannot.

VEE: We will place that reminder before the talent round.

ACT ONE

INT. WEST MODULE - LATER

The contestants and audience gather in the west module. Sula has moved the stage to leave the airlock door unobstructed. Vee reads the program. The first act is a talent round, but the music system is silent.

SULA: We can use hand claps.

PAX: No clapping near the pressure panel.

VEE: The panel has requested quiet.

Tess steps forward. Her talent is a juggling routine with three soft foam moons. One moon floats against the ceiling fan and sticks to the vent grille.

TESS: The moon has escaped.

Doctor Umbra retrieves it with a padded pole. The yellow pressure light turns orange. Captain Ori asks everyone to sit near the far wall.

ORI: We are not rehearsing a crisis. Stay together.

Pax traces the leak to a supply hose near the storage bay. It must be closed from the corridor side. The east corridor is locked, but the maintenance hatch opens into it.

PAX: I need someone to hold the hatch while I reach the valve.

DOCTOR UMBRA: I will go with you. We have masks.

SULA: The audience must remain in the west module.

VEE: I can call the next contestant from here.

Tess takes the program and marks the order with a pencil. She swaps her costume walk for a seated story so that the audience stays away from the hatch.

TESS: My story is about a moon that learned to wait.

The audience listens. Sula uses the quiet to move the chairs clear of the ventilation grills.

ACT TWO

Pax and Doctor Umbra open the maintenance hatch. The hose is leaking at a cracked coupling. Pax closes the shutoff valve. The pressure light changes from orange to yellow, then returns to green.

PAX: The leak has stopped.

ORI: Keep the corridor closed until we replace the hose.

The pageant continues in the west module. Vee announces the final round as "best use of limited space." Each contestant demonstrates one short act beside the stage without blocking the door. Tess wins for her quiet story and the foam moon that never came down.

SULA: The crown goes to Tess.

TESS: I would like to share it with the person who got the foam moon off the vent.

Doctor Umbra bows. The contestants fold the paper stars and put them in a sealed box so they do not enter the air system. Captain Ori records the corridor closure and schedules the repair.

VEE: The pageant is complete. The base remains pressurized.

ORI: That is the result we needed.

FINAL IMAGE: A paper crown rests beside the repaired hose as the moonlight turns the auditorium window silver.
