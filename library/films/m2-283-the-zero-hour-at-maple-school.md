# The Zero Hour at Maple School

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: school competition dramas, genius-student montages, rival teams, and a climactic academic contest where the clock becomes the main antagonist*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A nervous capuchin math captain must lead three very different students through a citywide puzzle contest after the school clock begins losing minutes, turning their rival's perfect timing into a liability.

Cast
- CAPTAIN SENA, a capuchin who solves equations by tapping her fingers.
- ROOK, a macaque speed puzzler with a stopwatch.
- VEE, a ring-tailed lemur who spots patterns in colors.
- TOMA, a gibbon who prefers to test a problem physically.
- COACH PEL, a mandrill who believes every team needs a montage.
- HOST ARI, an orangutan contest announcer.

## Scene 1: The Qualifier

INT. MAPLE SCHOOL CLASSROOM - MORNING

A city puzzle contest will be held after lunch. The school's old clock shows ten minutes to noon, though the sun is high. Coach Pel has arranged chalkboards and a dramatic practice bell.

PEL: You have one hour to prepare.

ROOK: The clock says we have ten minutes.

SENA: The clock is slow.

TOMA: The sun is not a clock.

VEE: The shadows show it is past noon.

Rook checks his stopwatch. It has stopped at 11:58.

ROOK: I am not prepared for time to be unreliable.

## Scene 2: The Montage

The team practices logic grids, shape rotations, and number sequences. Rook solves each first but rushes past the instructions. Vee notices clues hidden in color order. Toma builds a cardboard model to test a bridge puzzle. Sena keeps the group from racing ahead.

ROOK: I have the answer.

SENA: Read the question.

ROOK: It asks which door is open.

VEE: It asks which door is open after the bell.

ROOK: That is a different answer.

PEL: We need a more energetic montage.

TOMA: We can run the bridge puzzle outside.

They carry the model to the playground and test it on the monkey bars. The cardboard bends. Toma braces it with a ruler. The solution works, but the ruler breaks.

## Scene 3: The Contest

INT. CITY HALL - AFTERNOON

The contest hall has four clocks, all showing different times. Host Ari announces that teams have thirty minutes. Rook's rival school, led by a gibbon named Fenn, begins immediately.

ARI: You may use paper, pencils, and the clock on the wall.

ROOK: Which wall?

ARI: The wall with the clock.

The first problem asks teams to sequence colored tiles. Vee spots that the colors match the seating chart, not the number labels. The second puzzle is a bridge diagram. Toma turns the paper sideways and realizes the bridge is shown from underneath. Rook reads the instructions aloud before solving the final pattern.

The wall clock chimes early. Several teams stop, believing time is up. Fenn's team submits its sheet with one blank answer.

## Scene 4: The Clock Crisis

Ari checks the official timer. Its battery has failed. Coach Pel makes a speech about perseverance while the teams sit in uncertain silence.

ARI: We cannot declare a winner without a reliable clock.

ROOK: We can finish the test without one.

SENA: The rules say time is part of the score.

VEE: Then we need the actual elapsed time.

Toma counts the hall's bell intervals from the beginning. Vee compares the sunlight at the high windows to the schedule posted outside. Rook uses his stopwatch, which restarted when the wall clock chimed.

ROOK: My stopwatch started at the first bell. It has been running for twenty-two minutes.

FENN: Mine says twenty-three.

SENA: The contest allowed thirty. We still have time.

Fenn's team asks to continue rather than take the early stop. Ari agrees and uses Rook's stopwatch as the contest timer.

## Scene 5: The Final Problem

The last puzzle shows a maze with one route marked by stars. Rook starts tracing quickly, then stops himself and reads the note: "Begin at the star visible from the doorway." The star in the picture is not the beginning; the correct start is a reflection in the hall's glass door.

Vee finds the reflection. Toma rotates the page to align the hallway. Sena traces the route. Rook checks each turn. The team submits just before the timer rings.

Fenn's team also finishes. The judges compare the answers. Both teams earn full points, but Maple School has one extra point for explaining its process.

FENN: You showed your work.

SENA: We had to agree on the route.

## Scene 6: Awards

The host presents the trophy. Coach Pel lifts it too soon; the contest is still being recorded. He lowers it. Rook asks if they can share the trophy with Fenn's team, since they finished together.

ARI: The rules give it to the highest score.

FENN: We can share the study notes.

The teams exchange their puzzle methods. Rook leaves his stopwatch running so they can time the walk home.

## Final Image

Back at Maple School, the old clock is repaired. It chimes exactly on the hour, but nobody stops studying to check it.
