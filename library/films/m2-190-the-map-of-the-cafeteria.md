# The Map of the Cafeteria

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: teen high school comedies, cafeteria clique maps, makeover montages, and a climactic dance interrupted by a last-minute truth*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
PRU, a capuchin student who loves acoustics
SHELL, a macaque student council president
NIM, a ring-tailed lemur stage technician
COACH VALE, a gibbon teacher
THE TABLES, student groups of macaques, lemurs, and squirrel monkeys

SCENE 1. INT. KETTLE HIGH - CAFETERIA - DAY

A hand-drawn map of the cafeteria hangs on a locker. The jock table is near the window. The drama table is under a light that flickers. The robotics students sit beside an outlet. Pru carries a lunch tray and looks for a seat.

SHELL: You're new, right?

PRU: I've been here three years.

SHELL: You sit behind the speaker. Nobody sees you from the door.

PRU: I like the sound there.

Shell gives her a school map marked with cliques. Pru points to a blank corner labelled "MISC."

SCENE 2. INT. STUDENT COUNCIL ROOM - AFTERNOON

Shell announces the Winter Dance. Each group gets a table, a color, and one minute on stage. Pru is assigned to the "MISC." committee because she has no clique. She will handle the sound system.

NIM: The speakers hum when the gym lights come on.

PRU: The cables run beside the dimmer. I can reroute them.

SHELL: We need you to look more like a dance leader if you're going to announce the songs.

A makeover montage begins. Drama students give Pru a glitter cape. The jocks add a whistle. Robotics fit her with a visor. The cafeteria tables debate which accessory best communicates popularity. Pru emerges wearing all three, unable to turn her head without blowing the whistle.

SCENE 3. INT. GYM - EVENING

The dance setup falls into faction. The drama students want a spotlight. The jocks want the speakers louder. Robotics wants a cable-free floor. Shell wants the school logo centered behind the photo backdrop.

Pru reroutes the audio cables along the rafters and tests the speakers. The hum stops. The lights flicker anyway. Coach Vale discovers the gym's dimmer is overheating because the stage lights are plugged into the same circuit as the snack warmer.

COACH VALE: We'll have to choose: hot snacks or a bright stage.

SHELL: The dance needs both.

PRU: We can move the warmer to the cafeteria outlet. The Robotics table can carry the extension cable along the wall.

The groups refuse to help each other. Shell tries to lead a synchronized dance rehearsal. The whistle in Pru's cape sounds every time she turns. The jocks follow it as a starting signal. The drama students treat it as a cue for a tragic scene. Robotics start timing the pattern.

SCENE 4. INT. GYM - WINTER DANCE

The gym fills with students. The stage lights come on, then dim. The snack warmer trips the circuit. The DJ's music dies. The cafeteria cliques gather in their mapped positions, waiting for someone to take charge.

Pru climbs onto the stage, removes the whistle, and uses it to signal the groups. Robotics move the cable. The jocks carry the warmer to the cafeteria. Drama students hold the photo backdrop while Shell unhooks the school logo. Everyone works together. The lights return.

Pru starts the music. The floor remains divided by groups. She plays a simple beat from the soundboard, then pans the speakers so each side hears it equally. The tables begin to shuffle toward the center.

SHELL: You made the whole room move.

PRU: I just balanced the sound.

SHELL: I thought the dance needed a queen.

PRU: It needed an outlet.

SCENE 5. INT. GYM - LATER

The dance continues. The cafeteria map has been taped to the wall with the clique labels crossed out. Students are seated wherever there is space. Shell asks Pru to announce the final song.

PRU: This last one is for anyone who has ever sat behind a speaker because it sounded better there.

The DJ plays the closing track. Students dance in pairs, circles, and one cautious line along the wall. Coach Vale turns off the lights, but the gym remains bright from glow bracelets and phone screens.

NIM: You are supposed to take the cape off now.

PRU: I like the cape.

The whistle is now tied to the stage cable as a safety marker. The drama students perform an improvised bow. The jocks clap on the right beat. Robotics track the sound levels and announce that the hum is gone.

FINAL IMAGE: The cafeteria map hangs over the gym entrance. Its blank "MISC." corner has been filled with a tiny drawing of a speaker. Below it, students from every table share the last warm pretzel.
