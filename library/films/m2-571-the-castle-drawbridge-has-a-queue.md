# The Castle Drawbridge Has a Queue

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy epics where royal access becomes a ticketing system*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
KEEPER MARA, a macaque
PRINCE VEX, an orangutan
GUARD PIP, a capuchin
CLERK ODO, a gibbon
MESSENGER NARI, a lemur
BAKER LUMA, a lemur
NON-SPEAKING BOOK-RETURN VISITOR

SCENE 1. EXT. CASTLE GATE - MORNING

A line of villagers waits before a raised drawbridge. Vex wears a paper crown and studies a parchment.

VEX: The kingdom is under siege.

MARA: The gate is raised for the scheduled inspection. The bridge operator is checking the chains.

ODO: The queue begins at the painted mark. Admission is paused until the bridge inspection is complete.

PIP: The first person has a delivery for the kitchen. The second is here to return a book.

VEX: These are not the people in my prophecy.

NARI: Your prophecy is a visitor list. The baker is next.

SCENE 2. EXT. GATEHOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Mara tests the drawbridge chain. One link squeaks. Pip keeps the queue behind the marked line.

MARA: Stop the bridge. Don't lower it until I confirm the chain is secure.

VEX: The gate is refusing the king.

ODO: It is paused for maintenance. The operator has the repair wrench.

PIP: The baker can wait under the awning, out of the road.

Vex looks at the queue and moves a velvet rope to make a winding path.

NARI: Please leave the straight route open. The rope blocks the wheelbarrow.

VEX: It adds grandeur.

MARA: It adds a trip hazard. Take it down.

SCENE 2A. INT. GATEHOUSE - MORNING

Odo checks the visitor list against the delivery schedule. Mara holds the chain while Pip examines the hinge.

ODO: The baker is expected at nine. The reader is returning a book. The bridge operator will finish the chain inspection at nine-fifteen.

VEX: We should admit the royal court first.

MARA: The royal court is one person, and he can wait until the bridge is approved.

PIP: The squeak is at the upper hinge. The chain links look intact.

MARA: We'll apply lubricant to the hinge, then lower the bridge for a test with no one on it.

Pip passes the small oil can. Mara applies one drop and wipes the excess.

VEX: The gate has received its potion.

ODO: The hinge has been serviced. We still need a test before foot traffic.

Nari carries the barrier sign to the entrance and places it where the queue can see it.

NARI: Bridge closed for inspection. Please wait behind the mark.

Pip holds the wheelbarrow route clear while Vex winds the rope once and hangs it on the hook. The queue line becomes straight.

SCENE 2B. INT. GATEHOUSE - LATER

Mara lowers the bridge without passengers. Pip stands on the bank and watches both chains. The bridge settles on its support with a solid thump.

PIP: Left chain is moving smoothly. Right chain is still squeaking at the roller.

MARA: Raise it again, slowly. I'll inspect the roller from the platform.

The bridge rises. Mara finds a strip of cloth caught in the wheel. She removes it with a hooked tool and places it in the waste basket.

VEX: A banner from the enemy.

ODO: It is a cleaning rag that blew from the gatehouse window.

NARI: The queue is still behind the mark. The baker's cart has room to turn.

PIP: Lower it once more for the test.

The bridge lowers again. Both chains run without a squeak. Odo marks the inspection sheet and signs his name.

ODO: The retest passed at 9:18. Foot traffic may cross.

VEX: The kingdom is accessible.

MARA: The bridge is open. Keep the line moving and don't crowd the hinge.



SCENE 3. EXT. CASTLE GATE - LATER

The bridge remains lowered after its successful empty retest. Mara uses the key to unlock the pedestrian gate; Pip stands beside the ramp and leaves the wheelbarrow lane clear.

ODO: The bridge is cleared. The foot queue may advance.

VEX: The kingdom is open.

ODO hands out numbered wooden tickets.

ODO: Ticket one, baker. Ticket two, book return.

PIP: The baker is first. The book return is second.

VEX: May I be first?

MARA: You are the host. You can stand to the side and welcome people.

SCENE 4. INT. CASTLE COURTYARD - DAY

Visitors enter. The baker delivers bread; the reader returns a book. Vex offers a royal welcome to each.

VEX: Your mission is complete.

LUMA: I brought rolls.

Vex consults the visitor list as though choosing a knight.

VEX: Will you accept the title Defender of Breakfast?

LUMA: Will it pay for the flour?

Odo hands her the kitchen delivery receipt, which she accepts instead of a title.

VEX: A noble offering.

MARA: The gate is working. Keep the queue moving toward the courtyard.

TAG: Vex takes a numbered ticket and waits behind the next visitor.
