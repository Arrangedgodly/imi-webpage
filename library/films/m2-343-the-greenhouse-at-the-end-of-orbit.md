# The Greenhouse at the End of Orbit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space-exploration epics, ominous computer warnings, impossible plant-growth montages, and commanders who ration everything except speeches*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
COMMANDER PIP, a capuchin
BOTANIST NARI, an orangutan
ENGINEER MILO, a lemur
SHIP COMPUTER, a monotone speaker
SPROUT, a small seedling

SCENE 1. INT. ORBITAL GREENHOUSE - NIGHT

A spacecraft circles a distant moon. Its greenhouse lights flicker. A warning flashes: OXYGEN LOW. Nari checks the plants. The oxygen sensor has been covered by a leaf. Commander Pip enters with a dramatic helmet under one arm.

PIP: How long do we have?

NARI: Until I move this leaf? Ten seconds.

Milo uncovers the sensor. The warning disappears. The computer gives a long, solemn beep, then announces normal air levels.

SCENE 2A. INT. BRIDGE - CONTINUOUS

The ship computer reports that the greenhouse uses more water than expected. Pip orders a ration review. Nari shows him the drip line: water has been flowing into an empty catch tray instead of the soil. Milo turns the valve and redirects the stream. A line of droplets falls precisely into each pot. Pip asks for a dramatic reading of the water gauge. The computer displays a small increase and plays a triumphant fanfare.

NARI: We have reduced waste.

PIP: We have saved the mission.

MILO: We have fixed a hose.

The computer files the event under major planetary achievement because Pip used the word mission. He deletes the label and changes it to irrigation repair.

SCENE 2B. INT. BRIDGE - CONTINUOUS

Pip asks the ship computer for the mission status. It lists power, water, and food stores, then plays a recording of Pip's speech about courage. Milo says the computer is set to remind the crew of morale statements. Pip tries to turn it off, but the command menu asks if he wants to archive his speech.

MILO: Choose no.

PIP: It might be useful later.

The computer plays the speech again. Nari carries in a tray of seedlings and says the greenhouse needs more water. The ship's water is rationed, so Milo adjusts the drip system. One valve sticks; a tiny stream sprays Pip's boots.

SCENE 3. INT. GREENHOUSE - DAY

Nari finds one seedling growing toward the ceiling, away from the lamps. She rotates the tray. The plant bends back toward the light over several hours, shown in a time-lapse montage. The music swells as its leaves open. Pip watches and asks if they have discovered a new life form.

NARI: It is a sprout.

PIP: It is a brave sprout.

Milo checks the greenhouse logs. The lights had been installed upside down, and the sprout was reaching toward a maintenance strip. He reorients the lamp. The crew uses the plant's growth as a marker for adjusting the light cycle.

SCENE 4. INT. AIRLOCK CORRIDOR - EVENING

An alarm sounds. The greenhouse door has sealed itself. The computer says the room is losing pressure. Pip grabs an emergency axe. Nari asks him to check the display first. It reads DOOR SENSOR FAULT, but the computer's voice adds, "Evacuate immediately," from a separate prerecorded safety clip.

Milo resets the door sensor from the exterior panel. Pip tries the keypad from inside, but it is in maintenance mode and counts down from 30. He starts a countdown speech. Nari cuts him off and points to the manual release under the panel cover. Pip opens the cover, reads the printed instructions, and finds the latch already released. He pulls it once; the door slides open. The computer announces that the crew has overcome a critical system failure, then lists the door as safely open. Pip holds the light and waits. The seedling remains upright, but the tray has drifted away from the wall. Nari secures it with two straps and notes that the plants need to be anchored for orbit.

SCENE 5. INT. GREENHOUSE - MORNING

The crew repairs the sensor and the irrigation valve. The computer is reprogrammed to distinguish warnings from drills. Pip deletes his courage speech from the alert queue, then asks to keep a copy for the crew archive. Nari's plants receive the correct light. Milo prepares a new watering schedule. He tapes it beside the valve, at eye level, so no one needs to crawl under the tray to read it.

The sprout produces one fresh leaf. Everyone leans in, expecting a dramatic breakthrough. The leaf is small, green, and ordinary. Pip begins another speech, but the computer cuts him off with a quiet humidity update. The crew laughs and gets back to work.

FINAL IMAGE: The tiny leaf trembles under a steady lamp as the ship crosses the dark, while the warning panel stays calm and green.
