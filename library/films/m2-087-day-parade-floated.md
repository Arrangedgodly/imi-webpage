# The Day the Parade Floated

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster spectacles, civic pageantry, and crowds who assume a loud announcement is a plan*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A town parade loses its giant balloon in a gust, and its organizers keep trying to preserve the photo opportunity while the safety marshal directs a real recovery.

Cast: Leda, a macaque safety marshal; Porr, a gorilla parade chief; Timo, a lemur balloon handler; and Nix, a capuchin announcer.

SCENE 1. EXT. MAIN STREET, AFTERNOON

A giant acorn balloon lifts from its mooring. Timo holds the guide rope. Porr tells the crowd to remain in formation for the cameras. Leda orders the float teams to clear the street and directs spectators behind the barriers.

PORR: The parade is still on schedule.

LEDA: The balloon is above the schedule.

Nix announces that the acorn is "ascending as planned." Timo says it is not planned. The balloon tugs him toward a lamppost. Leda clips the line to a ground anchor and sends a trained crew to lower it.

Porr tells the marching band to keep their formation. The band captain asks whether they should stop playing. Porr says the music reassures the crowd. Leda points out that the crowd cannot hear instructions over the drums. Nix switches off the parade soundtrack and repeats the route closure. The spectators move behind the barriers. A child asks if the balloon is escaping; Timo tells her the crew is bringing it down.

The wind shifts. Leda moves the crew upwind and asks Timo to keep tension on the line. Porr starts a countdown for the cameras. Leda tells him to stop counting because no one is launching anything. Nix gives the actual update: the street is clear, and the recovery crew is working.

SCENE 2. EXT. SIDE STREET, CONTINUOUS

The balloon drifts over the closed side street. The crew follows the evacuation route. Porr tries to keep the parade bands in place, but the first band starts playing the march tune. Leda asks them to stop so the crew can hear instructions. Nix repeats the safety message and accidentally adds a drumroll.

The balloon catches on a low branch. Timo keeps the tether steady while the crew brings a padded pole. Porr asks whether they can pull it down for the camera. Leda says no one should pull until the line is secured. The crew clears the branch and guides the balloon into a schoolyard.

The pole slips against the branch, and the balloon rocks above the fence. Leda tells the crew to lower the pole and reset the padding. Porr suggests sending a float team beneath it. Leda says the team should stay outside the drop zone. Timo keeps the tether wrapped through the ground anchor while a second handler steadies the tail line. They release the snag without tearing the fabric. Nix relays each movement so the spectators know why they are waiting.

SCENE 3. EXT. SCHOOLYARD, LATER

The balloon rests against a fence, intact. The crew closes the valve and lowers it onto mats. Children watch from inside the school building. Porr asks them to wave for the parade photograph. Leda moves the crowd back from the work area. She explains that the balloon needs inspection before it can be moved.

Nix reads the event checklist. The wind warning was posted that morning, but the parade chief never saw it because the notice was placed beneath the sponsor banner. Porr removes the banner and reads the warning. He says he was looking for the schedule. Leda points out that the schedule and wind notice must both be visible.

The crew checks the balloon seams and mooring straps. One strap has torn. Timo tags it for repair and logs the gust. Porr asks if the balloon can still lead the parade. Leda says it cannot fly today. He considers the route, then asks the bands to walk without it.

Porr looks at the route map and sees the hand-carried lanterns can follow the same street. He asks the band leaders whether they can restart at the next block. They agree after Leda confirms the lane is clear. The crew anchors the rolled balloon in the schoolyard storage shed. Timo signs the tag and hands the key to the school custodian rather than leaving it with the prop cart.

SCENE 4. EXT. MAIN STREET, EVENING

The parade resumes with the giant acorn replaced by a hand-carried paper lantern. The bands play at a moderate volume. Porr leads the route on foot, holding the revised weather notice and schedule together. Nix announces the change plainly. Spectators follow the marked path.

The parade reaches the schoolyard. The children hold up drawings of the balloon on the ground, not in the sky. Porr asks for a group photo beside the repaired mooring station. Leda agrees after the crew clears the equipment. Timo ties a new tag to the damaged strap so it cannot be reused by mistake.

FINAL IMAGE: The festival photograph shows the parade walking safely beneath the deflated acorn, now rolled in its storage bag. The weather notice is posted in front of the sponsor banner.
