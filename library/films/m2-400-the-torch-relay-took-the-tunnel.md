# The Torch Relay Took the Tunnel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: sports opening ceremonies, relay choreography, and official flames that must reach a stadium on schedule*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
LUMA, a lemur torchbearer
VEX, a macaque relay marshal
PIP, a capuchin stadium keeper
NARI, a gibbon announcer
TAVI, an orangutan gardener

SCENE 1. EXT. CITY PARK - MORNING

Luma holds a ceremonial torch. The flame is small and steady. Nari carries a wireless microphone linked to the stadium loudspeakers. Vex checks the relay schedule. The torch must reach the stadium by noon, before the scheduled opening speech.

VEX: The route is six miles.

LUMA: The park path is two miles.

VEX: The route includes the official scenic loop.

PIP: It crosses the greenhouse.

Vex explains that the greenhouse is part of the city route. Tavi points to a tunnel beneath the garden wall. It is shorter.

TAVI: The tunnel goes straight to the stadium.

VEX: The torch cannot use an unofficial route.

NARI: The route map shows the tunnel.

VEX: It is drawn as a dashed line.

NARI: That means it is a path.

VEX: It means it is optional.

Luma starts walking. Vex raises a flag to signal the opening relay. A breeze blows it over the torch. Pip lifts the flag away. The flame is still burning.

SCENE 2. EXT. GREENHOUSE WALK - LATER

The relay reaches the greenhouse. Tavi opens the door. Warm air rushes out, making the torch flame stretch sideways.

LUMA: Should I carry it upright?

TAVI: The door is too low.

Luma bends to enter. The flame leans toward a hanging fern. Vex asks her to stop. Pip holds the fern aside. Nari announces that the relay has reached the botanical section.

NARI: We now pass the rare fern collection.

TAVI: Please don't announce the plants.

The greenhouse path winds between benches. Luma makes the turns carefully. Vex calls the pace. The flame wavers whenever he runs beside her.

VEX: You are losing time.

LUMA: You are making wind.

The group stops. Tavi covers the torch with a glass lantern. The flame steadies. Vex says they must carry it without a cover because the ceremony requires an open flame.

PIP: It is still visible through glass.

VEX: The rules say uncovered.

Nari checks the rule sheet. It says "the flame must remain visible." Tavi removes the cover. The flame returns to its small steady shape.

SCENE 3. EXT. GARDEN TUNNEL - 11:52 A.M.

The greenhouse path reaches a stone tunnel. Vex stops the relay at its entrance. The tunnel is not listed as an official checkpoint.

VEX: We need the marshal stamp.

LUMA: The stamp is at the stadium.

VEX: Then we cannot enter the tunnel until it is stamped.

Tavi sees the stadium clock through the tunnel. It is 11:52. The opening speech begins in eight minutes.

PIP: We can run.

NARI: The flame will blow out.

Vex asks Luma to wait while he runs around the wall to the checkpoint. He takes the flag. The wind pushes it across the path and catches it on a shrub. Pip frees it. Vex returns with a stamp, but the paper route card has folded over the map.

VEX: The tunnel is not on the card.

LUMA: It is on the map.

TAVI: It is under the wall.

The clock reaches 11:55. Nari presses her microphone switch and begins an early welcome for the stadium audience while unfolding the paper. The tunnel line is clearly drawn, but the stamp covers its label. She turns the card over. The back says "Approved alternate route."

VEX: I did not read the back.

Luma enters the tunnel. The torch's flame stays upright in the still air. The others follow in single file.

SCENE 4. INT. STADIUM SERVICE CORRIDOR - MOMENTS LATER

The tunnel ends inside the stadium. The relay arrives behind the stage. Nari has been delivering her early welcome over the wireless microphone from the route. She sees the stage entrance and interrupts her introduction to acknowledge Luma.

NARI: Our torchbearer has arrived, just in time for the end of my introduction.

LUMA: I am here.

Vex tries to lead her onto the track. He opens the wrong door and finds the janitor's closet. A broom falls against the wall. Tavi picks it up and uses it to prop the door open.

VEX: That is not the entrance.

NARI: It is an entrance.

The correct door is beside them. It opens onto the track. Luma carries the flame out. The crowd cheers. Luma reaches the track beside the stand at 11:58, two minutes before the scheduled noon start. She waits for Pip to secure the torch while Nari finishes the early welcome.

SCENE 5. EXT. STADIUM TRACK - 11:58 A.M.

Pip places the torch in its ceremonial stand. Vex presents the route card for the official record. Nari reads the finish time. It is two minutes before the schedule.

VEX: The tunnel saved the relay.

LUMA: The route map told us.

TAVI: The plants also gave you shade.

Nari signals the crowd wave, the next segment of the ceremony already underway. It travels around the stadium and stops at the empty section where the tunnel opens.

PIP: The wave has taken the short route.

Vex tries to redirect it. The wave circles once more and reaches the empty section again.

FINAL IMAGE: The flame burns steadily in its stand as the stadium crowd applauds the tunnel entrance.
