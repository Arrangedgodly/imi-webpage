# The Parade Float Must Not Float

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster spectacle films, where a parade float shaped like a boat is launched accidentally before the parade*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A crew builds a boat-shaped float for the town parade, then discovers the pond is closer than the route map suggests.

Cast: Builder Odo, baboon; designer Miri, capuchin; marshal Sava, gibbon; driver Nara, macaque.

SCENE 1. EXT. PARADE YARD, MORNING

A large wooden float shaped like a sailing boat rests on wheels. A banner reads "VOYAGE OF THE HARVEST."

MIRI: The float is ready.

NARA: The wheels are pointed toward the pond.

ODO: They are pointed toward the road.

SAVA: The road slopes down to the pond.

MIRI: The float should look like it is sailing.

NARA: It is not a boat.

Odo checks the wheel blocks. One is a smooth stone, which shifts under the weight.

[SFX: STONE SCRAPE.]

ODO: The block moved.

SAVA: Replace it.

MIRI: It is a scenic stone.

NARA: It is a wheel block.

Miri removes the stone. Nara puts a wedge in its place.

SCENE 2. EXT. PARADE ROUTE, LATER

The float begins moving. Nara drives from the front, while Odo pushes from behind. The route passes near the pond.

SAVA: Keep the wheels straight.

MIRI: The boat is approaching the water.

NARA: It is on the road.

ODO: The road narrows near the bridge.

Before the route continues, Nara backs the float away from the branch. The rear wheels roll toward the slope. Odo braces the cart while Sava sets a second wedge. The wheels stop.

MIRI: We have reversed course.

NARA: We have backed up.

SAVA: The route requires a lower banner and a second wheel block.

ODO: I will keep the block until we are past the pond.

Miri folds the banner. It is less sail-like, but the driver can see again.

The float's mast catches on a low branch. The cart stops. Miri climbs up to free the banner.

MIRI: The sail is snagged.

NARA: It is a banner.

MIRI: The audience expects a sail.

Odo holds the float while Sava lowers the mast. The banner comes free but drapes across the driver's view.

NARA: I cannot see the road.

SAVA: Fold the banner.

MIRI: It will lose its shape.

NARA: It will keep the float out of the pond.

The marshal asks the crew to test the brake on level ground before proceeding. Nara turns the wheel; the cart holds. Odo checks each block and removes the stone Miri set aside.

ODO: The spare block is also a stone.

MIRI: It's decorative.

ODO: It is flat enough to use.

Sava puts the stone behind a wheel and watches it hold. The road appears clear. A gust catches the banner and pulls the float sideways.

NARA: The wind has taken the sail.

MIRI: The wind has no right to steer the float.

SAVA: Lower the banner.

They fold the fabric over the side rail. The float stops drifting.

SCENE 3. EXT. POND TURN, MOMENTS LATER

The road curves along the pond's edge. The float rolls forward when Nara releases the brake. Everyone grabs a rail.

[SFX: WHEELS ROLL.]

MIRI: The voyage has begun.

NARA: The brake has slipped.

Odo runs to the rear and pushes against the float. Sava calls for the safety team. Miri pulls the banner, accidentally turning the front wheel toward the water.

NARA: Do not steer with the sail!

MIRI: I was saving it.

Sava wedges the wheel against the curb. Odo plants his shoulder against the rear. The float stops inches from the bank.

SAVA: Nobody move.

MIRI: The float is still.

NARA: It is not supposed to reach the pond.

Odo replaces the loose brake pin with a metal rod. The wheel locks.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARADE ROUTE, AFTERNOON

The crew takes the float back to the yard. Nara removes the banner so she can see. Miri folds it into a smaller sail shape.

MIRI: It still looks nautical.

SAVA: It looks folded.

ODO: The road is safer without the mast.

MIRI: Then the boat has lost its sail.

NARA: The boat is a cart.

They remove the mast and tie the banner to the side as a flag. The float rolls steadily on the road.

SCENE 5. EXT. TOWN SQUARE, EVENING

The float reaches the parade square. The audience applauds its boat shape. The pond is visible across the street.

MIRI: We have completed the voyage.

NARA: We have crossed the route.

ODO: The brakes held.

SAVA: The float is ready for the parade.

A child asks whether the boat will sail. Miri begins to answer, but Nara steers the float away from the pond.

MIRI: It will stay on land.

The child nods and walks beside it, making rowing motions.

[SFX: PARADE MUSIC, WHEELS ROLL SAFELY.]
