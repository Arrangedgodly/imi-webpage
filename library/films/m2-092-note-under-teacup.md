# The Note Under the Teacup

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: romantic comedies built on mistaken identity, elaborate coincidence, and public grand gestures*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: Two tea-shop regulars keep leaving notes for the wrong table, while their friends prepare a public confession neither person asked for.

Cast: Lala, a lemur map illustrator; Benji, a macaque tea-shop cook; Sumi, an orangutan owner; and Puck, a capuchin friend who directs the supposed romance.

SCENE 1. INT. TEA SHOP, MORNING

Lala leaves a note under the blue teacup: "Your window map needs a north arrow." Benji finds it during cleanup and thinks it is a compliment about his menu. He writes back: "Thank you. I will add one." He leaves the note under the same cup. Sumi moves the cup to wipe the table. The note slides beneath the sugar jar.

Puck sees the exchange and whispers that fate has begun. Lala returns, finds Benji's reply, and thinks the tea shop has a secret cartographer. She draws a map of the room and leaves it by the cup.

SCENE 2. INT. TEA SHOP, AFTERNOON

Benji finds the map and uses it to rearrange the tables so the doorway stays clear. Lala returns and sees her drawing has been altered. She assumes the mysterious stranger is criticizing her work. She adds a note asking for feedback. Benji reads it as a request for directions and draws a route to the public garden.

Puck asks Sumi to arrange a meet-cute. Sumi declines and points to the room schedule. Puck drapes a string of paper hearts across the entrance. It blocks the route for a delivery cart. Benji removes the hearts and uses them as place markers on the tables. Lala follows her map to the garden and waits at the wrong entrance.

The delivery cart arrives with tea tins. Puck tries to redirect it through the front door, where the paper hearts are tangled around the handle. Benji unties them while Lala's map lies under the sugar jar. Sumi finds the first note and asks whether the writer meant to leave it. Benji says he thought the notes were from a customer. Sumi recognizes Lala's sketch style. Benji starts toward the garden but stops to finish the tea order. Puck says a romantic lead should abandon everything for the moment. Benji says he has four waiting customers. Sumi helps serve the cups.

SCENE 3. EXT. PUBLIC GARDEN, LATER

Benji arrives carrying a tray of tea for the garden volunteers. Lala is at the east gate, drawing the fountain. Benji sees the map and assumes she is the volunteer who requested a route sketch. He asks where to place the tea. Lala points to the picnic table. They realize they have been exchanging notes.

Puck arrives with a portable speaker and a bouquet of paper hearts. He begins a song. The gardeners ask him to move because he is blocking the path. Benji asks if he brought the tea for the volunteers. Puck says he brought a public confession. Lala asks whose. Puck points at Benji, then at Lala.

BENJI: I thought you were fixing the menu map.

LALA: I thought you were correcting my map.

They laugh. Benji says the notes helped him arrange the shop. Lala says the new route is clearer. Neither asks for a grand speech. Puck tries to continue the song, but the speaker battery dies. Sumi arrives with a paper cup and tells him to help carry the tray instead.

Benji asks whether Lala wants to see the shop's new map. She agrees and points out that the delivery route still crosses the customer line. They sketch a new path on a napkin, testing it with a tea tray. The cart passes without blocking the door. Puck says the scene has no misunderstanding left. Lala tells him he can wash cups if he needs a subplot. The garden volunteers ask for a copy of her route drawing to mark the accessible entrance. She makes a clean version and gives it to Sumi. Benji asks if she draws maps for a living. She says yes, and asks if he always rearranges furniture from anonymous notes.

SCENE 4. INT. TEA SHOP, EVENING

Lala and Benji sit by the window and sketch a new map together. Benji draws the tea counter; Lala adds the north arrow and the door clearance. Sumi posts it by the entrance. Puck asks if there will be a kiss at the window. Lala says there will be a clear path to the window.

Benji asks whether she would like tea after work on Friday. Lala says yes, if he is not on shift. He checks the schedule rather than pretending to be free. They choose Saturday. Puck writes "MEET CUTE" on the map, and Sumi erases it before it is posted.

On Saturday they meet at the public garden entrance. Benji brings tea in cups with lids. Lala brings the finished route map, folded into a pocket. They walk the path without missing a turn. Puck waits behind a tree with another paper-heart banner, but Sumi takes it from him and folds it into a shopping bag. Lala asks if Benji wants to draw the next shop map together. He says yes, after they finish their tea. They sit with a clear view of the gate and talk about where a bench should go.

FINAL IMAGE: The map hangs straight with a north arrow. Two cups sit beside it. Puck places the paper hearts in the recycling bin, where they make a small, perfectly ordinary pile.
