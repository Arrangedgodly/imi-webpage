# The Room Beyond the Aviary

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: hostage-negotiation thrillers, ticking clocks, and negotiators who mistake every silence for leverage*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A negotiator tries to talk a furious parrot out of a locked bird-supply room, while the supposed hostage crisis is really a jammed latch and a missing snack schedule.

Cast: Sera, a macaque animal-control negotiator; Pock, a parrot who has shut himself inside the supply room; Tully, a tortoise facilities worker; Beep, a lemur radio operator; and Director Moss, who keeps asking for a dramatic countdown.

SCENE 1. INT. AVIARY HALL, MORNING

A door is shut. Pock's voice comes through the vent.

POCK: I want the sunflower seeds returned!

SERA: We can discuss your request.

DIRECTOR MOSS: Tell him we have a team in position.

TULLY: The team is holding a screwdriver.

A digital clock counts down to the aviary's public opening. Moss calls it the deadline. Sera asks Pock what happened. He says someone changed the snack chart without consulting the birds.

SCENE 2. INT. AVIARY HALLWAY, CONTINUOUS

Sera sits by the door. Moss paces behind her, narrating every move into Beep's radio.

SERA: Pock, tell me what you need right now.

POCK: My seeds and an apology.

SERA: We can bring the seeds. Who changed the chart?

TULLY: I did. The old sheet was wet.

Pock goes silent. Moss whispers that silence means escalation. Sera says it may mean Pock is chewing. A seed shell lands beneath the door.

SCENE 3. INT. CONTROL DESK, MOMENTS LATER

Moss draws a floor plan with red marker. He marks the supply room as a danger zone, the vent as an escape route, and the clock as a countdown. Beep points out that the room has only one door and a small window.

BEEP: The latch is probably jammed.

MOSS: Or expertly fortified.

Tully checks the maintenance log. The latch has stuck twice this week. Moss tells him not to ruin the suspense. Tully brings the screwdriver.

SCENE 4. INT. AVIARY HALL, LATER

Moss places a second clock beside the first, claiming two clocks increase pressure. They show different times. Beep asks which one is correct. Tully checks his watch and removes both from the room. Moss says the suspense has been reduced. Sera tells him the opening time remains on the posted schedule. He asks if they can at least dim the lights; the aviary keeper turns them back on for the birds.

Sera returns to the door with a bowl of seeds and a printed snack schedule. She slides the bowl under the door. Pock takes it, then asks whether the chart includes afternoon treats. Tully kneels at the latch and turns the screwdriver slowly. Moss begins counting down from ten. He reaches seven before the latch clicks.

POCK: I was still negotiating.

SERA: You can keep the schedule. We need to post a new one outside.

Pock steps out with the bowl. He points to the old chart, which is indeed soaked. Tully opens the door again and closes it. It sticks a second time. He repairs the latch while Pock supervises.

SCENE 5. INT. AVIARY HALLWAY, MOMENTS LATER

A news crew arrives. Moss tells them the crisis is ongoing until he gets the perfect closing shot. Sera says the latch is fixed and the door is open. The reporter asks if Pock has demands. Pock lists seed types and asks them not to film while he eats.

REPORTER: Are you being held against your will?

POCK: No. I am holding a sunflower seed.

Moss asks Pock to repeat the line more dramatically. Pock drops the seed, and it rolls under the camera tripod. The crew follows it with the lens. Tully retrieves it and gives it back. The reporter asks if this is a hostage exchange. Sera says it is a seed retrieval.

SCENE 6. INT. AVIARY HALL, PUBLIC OPENING

The doors open on time. Moss stands before a camera and says the situation was resolved through patience, communication, and a screwdriver. Beep hands him the updated snack schedule. He tries to make it sound like a hostage release notice.

MOSS: The afternoon treat will be delivered at two.

POCK: Two fifteen.

Sera changes the chart. Pock checks it, then lets a visiting child hand him one seed through the mesh.

The reporter asks for one last question. Pock requests that the snack chart be laminated.

The negotiator leaves the snack chart by the door.

FINAL IMAGE: Moss tapes the snack schedule beside the repaired door. The latch clicks. He looks toward the camera, ready to announce a new crisis; Tully points at the maintenance log and asks him to initial the repair.
