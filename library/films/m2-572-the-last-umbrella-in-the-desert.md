# The Last Umbrella in the Desert

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: survival dramas that turn a shade repair into a quest for a miracle*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
TRAVELER PIP, a capuchin
GUIDE MARA, a macaque
ANNOUNCER VEX, an orangutan
CARTOGRAPHER ODO, a gibbon
RANGER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. DESERT REST STOP - DAY

A sun umbrella leans beside a water station. One rib has slipped through the fabric. Vex points toward the horizon.

VEX: We are stranded beneath the burning sky.

Mara sets her trail pack beside the water station.

MARA: We're at the marked rest stop. The next shade shelter is one mile east.

ODO: The route map shows the path and water points. The weather warning is for heat, not a sandstorm.

PIP: I can hold the umbrella while Mara checks the rib.

NARI: The station has water and a first-aid kit. Stay in the shade while we repair it.

VEX: The last umbrella is failing.

SCENE 2. EXT. SHADE STATION - CONTINUOUS

Mara lowers the umbrella. Pip supports the frame. Odo finds a spare clip in the tool pouch.

MARA: Don't pull on the fabric. The rib needs to slide back into the seam.

PIP: I can hold it steady.

VEX: The desert is closing in.

NARI: The desert is exactly where it was. The shade is limited, so take turns.

ODO: Clip fits. I'll secure it before opening the umbrella again.

Vex opens his canteen. Mara checks that he drinks before they walk.

MARA: We leave when everyone is ready, not when the camera says the journey is dramatic.

SCENE 2A. EXT. REST STOP - DAY

Nari checks the map at the shade station. The group drinks water and waits while Mara repairs the umbrella clip.

NARI: The next shelter is marked by a yellow post. The path is open and mostly flat.

ODO: Heat warning remains active through four. Everyone should carry water and take breaks at the shelters.

VEX: We have to cross the desert before sunset.

MARA: We have to get to the next shelter safely. We can turn back if anyone feels unwell.

PIP: The clip is holding the rib. I'll open the umbrella once to check, then close it for the walk.

Mara holds the handle while Pip opens the canopy. The rib stays seated; fabric pulls taut.

PIP: Repair held. We can strap the closed umbrella to Mara's pack for the walk.

VEX: The final umbrella now guards the oasis.

NARI: It shades the station. We can share it while we refill.

Odo checks each bottle cap and marks the route card. Vex drinks from his own canteen and offers the map to Nari, who points to the next marker.

MARA: We leave together after the rest. No one races ahead to make a better shot.

SCENE 2B. EXT. TRAIL - DAY

Mara closes the umbrella and clips it to the side of her pack. The group starts along the marked trail, keeping the shade station behind them.

VEX: The umbrella will guide us to the next shelter.

NARI: Keep it closed and clip it to Mara's pack. The yellow markers guide us.

PIP: The clip holds. Leave the umbrella closed on the pack while we walk.

MARA: We have enough water for this section. Walk at a pace that lets everyone talk.

Odo reads the distance from the sign: one-half mile. Vex begins a speech, then stops to drink.

VEX: The journey tests our courage and hydration.

PIP: Mostly hydration.

NARI: We have reached the yellow post. The next shelter is visible ahead.

The party enters the shelter together. Mara counts the bottles; Odo updates the route card. No one is missing; the umbrella stays clipped to Mara's pack.

ODO: Rest stop logged. Time 11:20. We can continue after five minutes.

SCENE 2C. EXT. SHELTER - DAY

Mara checks the thermometer at the shelter post. The shade is cool enough for another short break.

MARA: We can rest here, refill, and decide whether the return route is still comfortable.

PIP: The umbrella is still clipped to the pack. We won't need it under this awning.

VEX: The journey has supplied a roof.

NARI: The shelter is built for that purpose. Let's sign the trail log and head back before the heat peaks.

SCENE 3. EXT. TRAIL - LATER

The group follows the east markers. The repaired umbrella stays closed and clipped to Mara's pack while they walk so it won't catch the wind.

PIP: Next post is visible. Water station is another half mile.

VEX: Our quest continues.

MARA: We are walking between marked shelters. Keep to the path.

SCENE 4. EXT. EAST SHELTER - AFTERNOON

They reach a larger awning. Nari checks the shade; Odo updates the route card.

The umbrella stays closed and clipped to Mara's pack. Pip looks at the repair clip through the fabric edge without reopening the canopy.

ODO: Shelter reached. Umbrella repair held.

VEX: We have crossed the wasteland.

PIP: We walked one mile on a marked trail.

TAG: Vex gestures toward the umbrella on Mara's pack. PIP points up at the large awning. Vex nods and leaves it clipped.
