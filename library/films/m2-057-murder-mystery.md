# Murder at Mango Manor

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: ensemble whodunits, theatrical accusations, and clue-board misdirection*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: At a manor dinner, a family portrait disappears and guests accuse one another of theft until the butler's cleaning schedule reveals the ordinary explanation.

Cast: Inspector Lark, an owl detective; Mrs. Bell, a mandrill host; Pippin, a capuchin nephew; Ada, a lemur archivist; and Moss, a tortoise butler.

SCENE 1. INT. MANGO MANOR, EVENING

The guests gather around an empty frame. Mrs. Bell raises a glass.

BELL: The family portrait is missing.

Inspector Lark inspects the wall with a magnifying glass. Pippin holds a paintbrush.

LARK: A suspect.

PIPPIN: I was painting the fence.

MOSS: The fence is outside.

PIPPIN: The paint came inside.

Lark asks everyone to remain in the room. Moss points out that dinner is still on the stove. Lark adds the kitchen to his list of possible alibis.

SCENE 2. INT. DINING ROOM, LATER

The guests examine the frame. One notices a dust mark, another says it resembles a handprint. Lark measures it with a ruler. It matches his own glove. He takes the glove off and finds the dust is on his palm. He calls it a false lead and wipes his hand on the curtain. Moss gives him a cloth.

LARK: The room is full of clues.

ADA: It's full of dinner guests.

Lark seats the guests in a circle and asks where they were. Ada was in the archive; Mrs. Bell was in the kitchen; Moss was polishing silver; Pippin was outside, then inside, then outside again. Lark draws a suspect board. The string he uses to connect names holds up the curtain.

ADA: The portrait is a copy from a school fundraiser.

LARK: A copy of what?

ADA: The family portrait.

LARK: Then which portrait is missing?

Moss says the one from the gallery. Lark circles GALLERY in red. The guests applaud, thinking he has solved something.

SCENE 3. INT. GALLERY, NIGHT

Lark checks the windows and the door. The windows are locked. The door is open. He asks why no one noticed. Moss says everyone was looking at the empty frame. Lark asks whether the open door could be a trick. Ada says it is a door.

Lark finds a paper strip behind the empty frame. It reads CLEANING, TUESDAY. Mrs. Bell says the portrait was supposed to be returned today. Moss says he took it to the drying room because the varnish was still tacky. He shows the cleaning schedule; Mrs. Bell signed it.

BELL: I don't remember signing that.

MOSS: You asked me to clean the frame.

LARK: Why take the portrait off the wall?

MOSS: To clean behind it.

Lark asks whether Moss had permission. Mrs. Bell studies her signature and admits she gave it. The inspector writes "authorized removal" on his board, but leaves the string up because it looks impressive.

SCENE 4. INT. DRYING ROOM, CONTINUOUS

The portrait rests on a stand. The paint is nearly dry. Lark interviews the room as if it might confess. Ada explains the humidity is too high to rehang it. Pippin flaps his wings to help it dry; a loose note sails onto the clue board.

LARK: A message from the culprit.

MOSS: That's the drying instruction.

Pippin reads it: KEEP GALLERY CLEAR WHILE PAINTING. Lark asks who wrote it. Moss says he did. Lark calls the handwriting suspiciously legible. Mrs. Bell asks whether the case is closed. Lark replies that the portrait is still wet. They wait. Pippin's wings stir the air again; Ada stops him before the note can blow away.

SCENE 5. INT. DINING ROOM, LATER

Moss serves pudding while Lark removes the string from the curtain. The curtain falls over his clue board. Pippin helps fold it, revealing that the names were connected in a circle with no conclusion. Lark says the pattern was decorative. Ada asks if it was. He studies it, then asks for more pudding.

Mrs. Bell offers Lark the clean cloth; he checks it for evidence before taking it.

The portrait is dry and rehung. Lark dictates his report: portrait removed for scheduled cleaning, no theft, one paintbrush unaccounted for. Pippin raises his brush. There is a small green smear on the frame. He offers to clean it; Moss hands him a cloth.

BELL: Can we have dessert now?

LARK: After I photograph the scene.

BELL: It is a dining room.

Lark takes a picture. The guests return to the table. Ada asks if the mystery is over. Lark says the mystery ended when they checked the schedule, but the investigation ends when he finishes dessert. Moss brings mango pudding.

FINAL IMAGE: A paper mustache falls from Pippin's pocket and lands on the portrait. Lark reaches for his magnifying glass. Mrs. Bell removes the mustache and puts it on Pippin's face before dessert is served.
