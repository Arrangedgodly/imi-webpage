# The Orchard Repeats Tuesday

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-loop science fiction that mistakes a stopped clock for a cosmic paradox*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
ORCHARDIST MARA, a macaque
INVENTOR VEX, an orangutan
PICKER PIP, a capuchin
TIMEKEEPER ODO, a gibbon
NEIGHBOR NARI, a lemur
DELIVERY DRIVER LUMA, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. ORCHARD - MORNING

Mara hangs a calendar card on the shed: TUESDAY. Pip rolls a cart of apples toward the sorting table. Vex rushes in holding a clock with its hands stopped at 4:12.

VEX: The day has repeated. It is Tuesday again.

MARA: It is Tuesday. Yesterday was Monday.

ODO checks the clock's back. The battery compartment is empty.

ODO: This clock stopped at 4:12 because the battery is missing.

PIP: I picked the apples this morning. The leaves were wet with dew.

VEX: The exact same dew as last Tuesday.

NARI: Last Tuesday it rained. This morning is dry.

VEX: A subtle variation in the timeline.

MARA: We need to pack six crates before the delivery cart arrives at noon. Odo, can you repair the clock now?

ODO: I have a replacement battery in the tool drawer. It will take one minute.

SCENE 2. INT. ORCHARD SHED - MOMENTS LATER

Odo opens the clock compartment. Pip stands by the doorway with the apple cart. Mara holds the delivery sheet.

VEX: Don't insert it. The timeline may reset.

MARA: We need the clock for the packing log. Put in the battery.

Odo places a new battery in the compartment and closes it. The second hand begins ticking; the minute hand still points to twelve.

ODO: Clock working. The displayed time is wrong. My wristwatch says 9:05.

VEX: Tuesday has started moving again.

PIP: Tuesday never stopped. The clock did.

MARA: We'll set the clock from the orchard time signal at the top of the hour. For now, use Odo's watch on the packing sheet.

Vex arranges three apples in a circle on the table.

VEX: These are control specimens from separate loops.

PIP: They're the bruised apples. They go in the compost basket.

MARA: Put them there. The sound fruit goes in the crates.

Vex drops the bruised fruit into the basket. Pip sorts the rest by size. Odo checks the counts against the order.

ODO: Five crates packed. One remains.

SCENE 3. EXT. ORCHARD SHED - LATE MORNING

The town time signal chimes from the radio. Odo adjusts the clock to 11:00. Mara checks the crate labels; one says WEDNESDAY.

MARA: This label is from the wrong stack. The delivery is Tuesday.

VEX: The timeline has advanced.

NARI: The label is wrong. The fruit and the order sheet are right.

PIP: I have a blank label in the shed. I'll write Tuesday, crate six.

Mara checks the number against the packing sheet. Pip replaces the label while Odo initials the correction.

MARA: Tuesday, crate six. The label is legible.

ODO: Clock reads 11:42. My watch agrees. The sundial shadow is also near noon.

VEX: Three clocks confirm the loop.

MARA: Three clocks confirm the time.

SCENE 3A. EXT. ORCHARD SHED - LATE MORNING

Mara checks the bottom of each crate for damp boards. Pip notices the pad beneath crate six is damp and fetches a dry replacement. Odo reads the packing sheet one last time.

ODO: Crates one through six are filled. The bruised apples are in the compost basket, separate from the delivery.

VEX: The basket may be from yesterday.

NARI: It is today's compost basket, and the apples are visibly bruised.

PIP: I can replace the pad under crate six. The old one is wet from the morning dew.

MARA: Do it before the driver arrives. The label has to stay readable on the road.

Pip lifts one edge of the crate, slides in a dry pad, and sets it down. Odo checks that the crate number faces outward.

ODO: Number six visible. Tuesday label clear.

VEX: We have prevented a temporal misdelivery.

MARA: We have packed the order carefully.

SCENE 4. EXT. ORCHARD GATE - NOON

Delivery Driver Luma arrives, pushing her cart. She checks the six crate labels with Mara while Pip loads them. Odo marks each crate off the packing sheet.

LUMA: Six crates, all labeled Tuesday. The receipt is ready.

MARA: Count matches. Sign here, please.

Luma signs the receipt. The cart is loaded and secured. She rolls it out through the gate and down the road. Vex watches until it disappears around the bend.

VEX: The delivery has escaped the loop.

PIP: It left because it was loaded.

ODO: Clock and watch both show noon. The next day is Wednesday.

Vex turns the calendar card over. Its reverse is blank.

MARA: Leave Tuesday displayed until we change it tomorrow morning.

TAG: Vex writes WEDNESDAY on the back of a spare card and pins it beside the calendar, labeled "NEXT DAY - NOT YET."
