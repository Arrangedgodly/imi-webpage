# The Courtroom of Tiny Crimes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: legal dramas that inflate small neighborhood disputes into constitutional trials*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
JUDGE MARA, a macaque
PROSECUTOR VEX, an orangutan
DEFENDANT PIP, a capuchin
CLERK ODO, a gibbon
WITNESS NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY COURT - MORNING

A single acorn rests on a velvet cushion in the evidence box. Vex stands beside it as if it were a crown jewel.

VEX: The people charge Pip with unauthorized relocation of one acorn from the shared path to the community table.

MARA: Is the acorn damaged?

ODO: No. The evidence tag says it was moved at 8:05 and returned at 8:22.

PIP: I moved it because it was under the stroller wheel. I put it on the table so the groundskeeper wouldn't sweep it away.

VEX: A convenient account. We have a witness.

NARI: I saw Pip pick it up. I also saw the acorn roll toward the wheel first.

MARA: Did Pip ask permission?

PIP: There was no one nearby. I left a note on the table.

VEX: The note has disappeared.

ODO: The note is attached to the evidence tag. It reads, "Found by path, moved to table." 

VEX: It could be a confession.

MARA: It is a location note.

SCENE 2. INT. COURTROOM - LATER

Vex lays out a chalk diagram of the path. It has five arrows, three circles, and a large question mark around the acorn's original position.

VEX: The acorn's path must be reconstructed.

MARA: The groundskeeper can describe the sweep route.

NARI: The broom goes from the gate to the bench. The acorn was beside the wheel track, where it could be crushed.

PIP: I used the shared table because it has a tray for found natural objects.

ODO: The tray has a label. I can read it: "Keep clear of the path. Return to ground after inspection."

VEX: This is worse. The defendant knew the rules.

PIP: I followed them. I put it in the tray and came back to return it once the stroller left.

MARA: Then there is no dispute about the action. The question is whether moving it was reasonable.

VEX: I call the acorn.

MARA: The acorn is not a witness.

VEX: I call the table.

ODO: The table has no mouth.

SCENE 3. INT. COURTROOM - CONTINUOUS

Mara steps down and walks the path shown on the diagram. Her shoe reaches the spot marked by the acorn. A child-sized wheel rolls by in the hallway. Everyone turns.

NARI: That is the same wheel. It nearly rolled over the marker just now.

PIP: I moved the acorn to keep it safe, not to claim it.

VEX: But the table was not a courtroom-approved destination.

MARA: There is no law against placing an acorn in the found-object tray. Clerk, check the posted garden rules.

Odo reads the board on the wall: "If an object blocks a path, move it to the nearest marked tray and note its location."

ODO: That's the rule. Pip followed it exactly.

VEX: Then the prosecution rests on a table.

MARA: The charge is dismissed. The acorn is returned to the ground beside the oak, away from the path.

SCENE 3A. INT. COURTROOM - CONTINUOUS

Vex asks to question the groundskeeper. Mara allows one question. Odo places the broom beside the diagram.

VEX: Did you intend to sweep up the acorn?

NARI: I intended to clear the path. I would have left the acorn under the oak if it was not in the wheel track.

VEX: But you did not see Pip move it.

NARI: I saw Pip set it in the tray. I was carrying the broom at the time.

PIP: I returned when the stroller passed. The acorn was still in the tray, so I took it to the tree.

ODO: The log has two entries: found at 8:05, returned at 8:22. The second entry has Pip's initials.

MARA: That establishes both moves. There is no missing period and no missing acorn.

VEX: Could we at least call the tray a temporary custody facility?

MARA: It is a tray. Call it what it is.

SCENE 4. EXT. COURTROOM GARDEN - DAY

Pip sets the acorn beneath the tree. Vex carries the evidence box. Mara points to the clear walkway. Odo pins the location note to the shared table.

NARI: The path is open. The stroller can pass.

VEX: I would like to appeal the dismissal on behalf of the acorn.

MARA: On what grounds?

VEX: It has been moved three times today.

PIP: The last move was back under its own tree.

MARA: Appeal denied. The court will now hear the case of the missing bench cushion.

Odo looks at the bench. Vex is sitting on the cushion, which he carried in as a prop.

TAG: Vex stands; the cushion stays on the bench. The acorn remains untouched.
