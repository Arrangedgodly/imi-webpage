# The Puppet Mayor's First Press Conference

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: political biopics, press-conference montages, campaign puppetry, and advisors who answer every question with a slogan*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
MAYOR PIP, a capuchin
PUPPET VEX, a macaque marionette
ADVISOR NARI, an orangutan
REPORTER LUMA, a lemur
CLERK ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. TOWN HALL - MORNING

Mayor Pip prepares for his first press conference. A puppet from the campaign is set beside the podium. It is designed to resemble him, but its strings are tangled. Advisor Nari offers a speech card. Clerk Odo brings the town budget. Reporter Luma asks who will answer questions.

PIP: I will.

VEX: And I will gesture supportively.

The puppet lifts one hand, then slaps the speech card onto the floor.

SCENE 2. INT. TOWN HALL - LATER

The press conference begins. Luma asks about the broken park fountain. Pip starts to answer, but Vex's mouth moves first. Nari pulls the wrong string. The puppet says, "We are looking into it," while Pip says, "The pump needs a washer." The camera records both.

ODO: The pump needs a washer.

NARI: We have the part number.

Vex's strings tangle around the microphone cable. Pip pauses the conference and untangles them. Nari suggests a campaign slogan. Luma asks for the repair date instead.

SCENE 2A. INT. PRESS ROOM - MIDDAY

Nari arranges the press questions on the table. Vex's puppet has a second string attached to its jaw. When the microphone is turned on, the puppet speaks before Pip can answer. Luma asks whether the town has approved a new playground budget. Pip says the budget is under review. Vex's puppet says, "The park will be transformed." It gestures at a mop bucket by accident.

PIP: We are fixing the fountain first.

ODO: The work order is signed.

A reporter asks whether the mayor controls the puppet. Pip says the puppet is made of wood and string. Vex says it symbolizes the campaign. The puppet's hand drops again and taps the podium. Nari unhooks the strings and places the figure on a side chair so Pip can finish the interview without it moving.

SCENE 3. INT. MAYOR'S OFFICE - AFTERNOON

Pip checks the budget. The town has funds for a washer and a volunteer team to install it. Vex says the press release should describe a bold infrastructure initiative. Odo points to the work order and says the task is one washer. Pip signs the order and assigns the crew for the next morning.

The puppet is moved to the side table. Its strings pull the office door shut. Luma checks the hinge and discovers the door sticks because it is misaligned. Nari calls a maintenance worker. Vex proposes a ceremony for the repair. Pip asks him to hold the door while the worker tightens it.

SCENE 3A. INT. PUBLIC WORKS GARAGE - EVENING

Odo finds the washer in the parts cabinet. The box contains three sizes. Pip compares the pump label with the work order. The correct washer is the smallest one. Vex asks whether a larger washer would make the fountain more powerful. Odo says the pump is designed for one size, and a larger part could damage the seal.

Nari adds the pump instructions to the work order. The team plans to turn off the water, replace the washer, test the pump, and reopen the fountain. Vex tries to add a ribbon cutting. Pip says the repair only needs a sign confirming it is working. Vex writes the sign himself, then checks the spelling with Luma.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK - NEXT MORNING

The fountain pump is repaired. Water flows into the basin. The press conference resumes outdoors. Luma asks Pip what happened. He explains the washer was replaced and the fountain is working. Vex tries to make the puppet deliver the answer. Its strings are now arranged correctly, but the wind spins the marionette around. Pip holds it steady by the base.

LUMA: The repair was clear.

PIP: It was a small part.

ODO: It was on the schedule.

The reporters ask if the mayor will make a speech. Pip says he is going back to work. Nari hands him the next item on the town list: repainting the crosswalk. Vex tries to make the puppet wave goodbye, but it bows instead.

SCENE 5. INT. TOWN HALL - EVENING

The puppet is stored in its case with the strings untangled. Pip posts the repair record on the public board. Luma files her story. Odo updates the budget sheet. Nari writes a short press note with the repair date and part used.

FINAL IMAGE: The puppet rests inside its case, its wooden hand pointing toward the public work schedule.
