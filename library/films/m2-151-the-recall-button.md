# The Recall Button

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster thrillers and product-safety investigations that escalate before anyone checks the label*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: When a community's new acorn sorter begins ejecting every third nut, a crisis team races to shut it down before the harvest demonstration. Their investigation finds the machine is following its instructions exactly.

Cast: Director Varo, a baboon; engineer Limi, a macaque; safety inspector Peta, a gibbon; and operator Salo, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY WORKSHOP, MORNING

A polished acorn sorter sits beneath a banner: "FUTURE OF HARVESTING." Varo turns toward a camera as Salo feeds acorns into the hopper.

VARO: Today we unveil a machine that will save every nut.

The sorter hums. It drops two acorns into the basket and launches a third across the room.

SALO: It has launched a nut.

VARO: It has launched an idea.

LIMI: That is the reject chute.

PETA: Why does it reject every third acorn?

LIMI: The machine is set to reject damaged nuts.

[SALO PLACES A ROUND ACORN INTO THE HOPPER. THE SORTER LAUNCHES IT.]

SALO: This one is not damaged.

VARO: The demonstration is paused until we know if the machine is safe.

SCENE 2. INT. CONTROL BOOTH, MOMENTS LATER

Peta opens a binder of safety procedures. Varo studies the red button on the wall.

VARO: Is this the shutdown?

PETA: It says "Emergency Stop."

VARO: It is red and important. I can press it.

LIMI: Wait. The power switch is at the back.

VARO: In a disaster film, waiting costs lives.

PETA: This is a workshop.

Varo presses the emergency stop. A siren sounds. The sorter locks its hopper and sends the remaining acorns into a clear side bin.

SALO: The machine stopped.

VARO: But the side bin is filling.

PETA: It is a catch bin.

LIMI: The alarm is louder than the machine.

VARO: We need to evacuate.

PETA: The exit is right there.

VARO: Keep the camera on me.

SCENE 3. INT. WORKSHOP, CONTINUOUS

The crew walks three steps to the exit. Outside, Salo turns over the product label.

SALO: "Sorts by size. Every third item goes to the sample bin."

LIMI: The machine is not rejecting anything. The sample setting is on.

VARO: We have a safe machine with a confusing label.

PETA: We have an emergency alarm with a useful stop.

VARO: Can we have a second alarm for confusing labels?

PETA: We can write a clearer label.

SCENE 4. INT. WORKSHOP, LATER

Limi adds a large sign: "SAMPLE BIN: EVERY THIRD ACORN." Peta asks Salo to run twelve nuts while she checks the separate reject chute. Varo props the drooping demonstration banner behind them.

PETA: The round nuts reach the sample tray. The cracked ones reach the reject chute.

VARO: Two destinations. This crisis has divided us.

SALO: It has divided the nuts.

Varo asks for a close-up of the red button. Limi switches off the siren at the control panel. Varo looks around, suddenly deprived of his soundtrack.

VARO: Can we keep the alarm under my closing speech?

PETA: Then nobody will hear your closing speech.

VARO: Just a little alarm.

Salo empties the sample tray. Varo stops him with a raised palm.

VARO: Leave one for the final shot. The survivor.

SALO: Which one survived more?

Varo compares two identical acorns, selects one and places it on the tray. It rolls through a gap and under the machine. Varo drops to the floor after it.

VARO: We have lost our survivor.

LIMI: We have lost our close-up.

Peta holds the banner as Limi sweeps the acorn out with a brush. Varo catches it, then asks them to do that again from the other side so the brush will look like a rescue helicopter.

PETA: The demonstration begins in thirty seconds.

VARO: Then the helicopter must be quick.

SCENE 4A. INT. WORKSHOP, CONTINUOUS

Salo restarts the sorter. Two acorns enter the main basket; a third reaches the sample tray. Varo stands on his camera mark, holding his rescued acorn high.

VARO: Against impossible odds, one nut has returned.

LIMI: Twelve returned. You are holding a thirteenth.

Varo tries to put it back into the hopper without losing his triumphant pose. His elbow catches the banner. It wraps around him, turning "FUTURE OF HARVESTING" into a sash.

The camera tilts to fit the lettering. Varo squeezes a hand through the cloth and presents the acorn again.

VARO: Harvest director. Crisis commander. Nut escort.

SALO: The machine has finished while you were getting dressed.

Peta moves the basket into the demonstration area. Varo follows, still wrapped in the banner, insisting that the camera stay close enough to hide his trapped elbow.

SCENE 5. EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN, AFTERNOON

The team shares the sorted acorns beneath a tree. Varo stands beside the machine for one last shot.

VARO: This technology has survived its first emergency.

LIMI: It has sorted twelve acorns.

PETA: And the stop button worked.

SALO: What does this one do?

He points to a yellow button marked "SAMPLE."

VARO: We will read the label.

Everyone looks at the camera.

PETA: There is no helicopter in that button.

The machine ejects one acorn into Varo's open palm.

VARO: I accept the sample.

He eats it. The sorter gives one gentle beep, then stops.
