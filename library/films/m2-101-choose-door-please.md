# Choose the Door, Please

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: interactive movies, repeated voting prompts, and branching paths that return to the same hallway*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A community cinema tests an interactive mystery in which every audience choice sends the detective back to the same lobby.

Cast: Nori, a lemur projectionist; Tavi, a macaque detective on screen; Vee, a gorilla viewer; and Puck, a capuchin game designer.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, NIGHT

Puck introduces the interactive mystery. Tavi stands on screen beside two doors, LIBRARY and BASEMENT. A silver bell has vanished from the community hall. The audience must choose where Tavi searches first. Nori explains the two buttons on the armrests. Vee presses both.

The film freezes. The projector emits a polite chime.

PUCK: Choose one door.

VEE: I chose the whole building.

Puck resets the program. The audience votes for LIBRARY. Tavi walks through the door and returns to the lobby from the other side. The room is identical, except his hat is tilted.

TAVI: I found a book.

PUCK: Was it about the bell?

TAVI: It was about gardening.

Vee presses BASEMENT. Tavi descends three steps and emerges from the same door. The audience laughs. Nori checks the file list on the booth monitor. The library and basement scenes are both stored under LOBBY.

NORI: The map says two places.

PUCK: The edit says one.

SCENE 2. INT. PROJECTION BOOTH, MOMENTS LATER

Nori pauses the screening. Puck argues that the repeated lobby is suspense. Vee asks why Tavi cannot check the sign-out book for the silver bell. Puck says the audience has to choose the right clue first.

VEE: We chose two clues.

PUCK: You chose both doors.

Nori notices a disconnected button labeled CARETAKER. Puck reconnects it while she holds a flashlight. The wire slips; the caretaker button now triggers a shot of Tavi staring at the lobby clock. Puck pins the connector with a paper clip. The camera view drops to Tavi's shoes. He examines them as if they belong to a suspect. Vee asks if the shoes have a clue. Nori restores the wire and tapes it to the console. Vee presses it. The clock ticks. Tavi checks his watch. Nothing else happens.

TAVI (ON SCREEN): Time is passing.

VEE: So is the screening.

Puck opens the branch chart. The caretaker scene exists, but it is linked only from the garden door, which is not on the screen. Nori adds a visible choice for GARDEN. Puck asks the audience to try it. Tavi enters a greenhouse, finds a watering can, and returns to the lobby. The bell remains missing.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, LATER

Nori draws the path on a whiteboard. Every choice must show a real location and lead to a clue. Puck gives Tavi an on-screen line for each branch. Vee reads the options aloud: library catalog, basement shelf, caretaker interview.

VEE: Library.

Tavi searches the catalog and finds a card for the silver bell. It says "Returned to storage." The screen prompts the audience to choose where to go next. Vee selects BASEMENT SHELF. Tavi checks the shelf; it is empty. He reaches for the lobby door and exits back into the lobby.

PUCK: That is a different shot.

NORI: It is still the lobby.

Puck asks Vee to choose CARETAKER. Tavi interviews the caretaker, who checks the sign-out sheet and says the bell was borrowed for rehearsal. Tavi goes to the rehearsal room. The bell is absent from the case. He asks the stagehand where it is.

STAGEHAND (ON SCREEN): I put it back after the last performance.

Tavi looks at the case again. A small label says "SHELF B." Vee selects the shelf. Tavi finds the bell and signs the return slip. The mystery is resolved. The screen asks whether he should accuse the caretaker or thank them.

VEE: Thank them.

TAVI: Thank you for keeping the record.

Puck starts to add another button. Nori closes the editor.

SCENE 4. INT. COMMUNITY CINEMA, END OF SCREENING

Puck asks if the audience wants a secret ending. Vee says the bell is returned. Nori points to the credits. Puck replaces the new prompt with one plain end card showing the completed sign-out entry.

The credits roll without interruption. A final choice appears: WATCH AGAIN or GO HOME. Vee presses GO HOME. Puck starts to object, but Nori opens the theater doors. The audience files past the booth.

Puck saves the corrected branch chart. Nori hands him the button panel to label. He writes "ONE CHOICE AT A TIME" under the controls. Vee stops at the door and asks whether there is a post-credit scene.

NORI: There is a lobby.

Vee leaves.

FINAL IMAGE: The screen goes dark. On the blank surface, the tiny exit sign from the back wall glows steadily without offering a choice.
