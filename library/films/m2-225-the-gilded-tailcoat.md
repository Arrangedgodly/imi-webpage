# The Gilded Tailcoat

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: masquerade caper films, elaborate costume swaps, slow-motion ballroom escapes, and a priceless garment nobody can wear comfortably*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
VICO, a lemur wardrobe thief
NARA, a capuchin museum guard
LUX, a macaque socialite
PERRIN, a gibbon tailor

SCENE 1. INT. GRAND BALLROOM - NIGHT

A golden tailcoat rests in a glass case at the center of a ballroom. A sign calls it "the most valuable coat in the collection." Guests wear masks. Vico, a lemur thief, enters in a plain jacket with a hidden pocket.

LUX: They say the coat belonged to a prince.

PERRIN: It belonged to a very short prince with a very long tail.

NARA: No one touches the case.

Vico studies the lock. The display stand revolves whenever the band plays a waltz. He expects the turning mechanism to release the glass door. The band begins a waltz, and every masked guest immediately changes partners.

SCENE 2. INT. BALLROOM - CONTINUOUS

Vico slips toward the case. Nara steps in front of him. He pretends to be a tailor and measures her sleeves. Lux mistakes the exchange for a fitting and asks for a new cuff. Perrin arrives with a spool of thread.

PERRIN: Are you here for the coat?

VICO: I am here for the sleeve.

NARA: Which sleeve?

VICO: The one on the coat.

Nara studies him. As the band reaches the third turn, a velvet panel flips out from the revolving stand, concealing the lock. The glass door stays shut. Vico's tail catches in the stand. He spins around it, appearing to perform a deliberate ballroom flourish. He mistakes a rehearsal copy beside the stand for the original coat being moved during the turn, frees his tail and snatches the replica.

SCENE 3. INT. SERVICE CORRIDOR - LATER

Vico escapes with a gold replica folded over one arm. Nara follows him through a service corridor. He enters a costume room filled with identical gold jackets. Lux enters behind them, looking for the restroom, and puts on a coat that is too large.

NARA: Put the coat down.

VICO: Which coat?

NARA: The one that is valuable.

VICO: They all look valuable.

Perrin arrives and explains that the gold coats are rehearsal copies. The real coat is still in the display case. The one Vico carries is a replica with a loose button and a very narrow tail opening.

SCENE 4. INT. COSTUME ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The ballroom doors open. The guests have all changed into the same gold rehearsal coats. Nara cannot tell which is Vico. Lux has put the coat on backward; the sleeves cover her hands. Vico hides his face behind a lace fan.

NARA: The thief is carrying a gold coat.

PERRIN: The original weighs more. It has a gold chain sewn into the hem.

Vico tries to run. The coat catches his tail. He stops, unwilling to damage the garment. Nara helps free him, then asks why he stole a replica. Lux takes off her oversized copy; a label inside reads "REHEARSAL." 

VICO: I thought it was the original.

NARA: You didn't check?

VICO: I was under pressure from the waltz.

SCENE 5. INT. BALLROOM - FINAL TURN

Nara unlocks the case with the key on her chain. Perrin and Vico lift the real coat together onto a padded table. Beneath the velvet display pad, Lux finds a small card describing the original owner's name and the museum's note about the garment's history.

LUX: It is valuable because it has a story, not because it is gold.

PERRIN: It is also gold.

Nara decides the coat should remain in the case, but Perrin demonstrates its unusual tail opening on a mannequin. Vico confesses to attempting the theft. Nara takes his laces as evidence and appoints him to help inventory the rehearsal copies.

SCENE 6. INT. GRAND BALLROOM - AFTER MIDNIGHT

The band ends. Guests return the gold jackets. Lux helps Perrin label them. Nara closes the case and adds a clear sign explaining the tailcoat's design. Vico returns his replica to the costume rack and asks Perrin for a coat with a tail opening.

SCENE 7. INT. MUSEUM STORAGE ROOM - MORNING

Nara and Vico check the inventory. The rehearsal rack holds every copy. Perrin adds labels showing each jacket's weight, button pattern, and tail opening. Nara returns the display key to the guard office's key cabinet. Vico tests every hem for a hidden chain, then signs the inventory as a temporary assistant.

VICO: No original coat in this rack.

NARA: And no replica in your pocket.

Vico opens both hands. They return to the ballroom. Perrin points to the sign on the case: "Please do not touch." Vico steps back three paces.

FINAL IMAGE: The gilded tailcoat hangs under glass while Vico stands beside it wearing an ordinary jacket with a very large, carefully measured tail slit.
