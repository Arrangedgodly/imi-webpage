# The Wedding Cake Has a Seating Chart

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: wedding comedies that escalate minor reception logistics into a public romantic crisis*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Two monkey wedding planners try to save a reception after the seating chart lands on the cake, while the couple quietly asks everyone to stop making speeches.

Cast: Sali, a macaque wedding planner; Ivo, a gibbon groom; Mara, a capuchin bride; Tori, a lemur florist; Niko, a baboon caterer; Aunt Pala, a mandrill relative.

SCENE 1 - INT. BANQUET HALL - AFTERNOON

A three-tier cake waits beside a table of place cards. Sali checks the seating chart. Tori enters carrying a bouquet that is wider than the doorway. She bumps the chart. It sails across the hall and lands flat atop the cake.

SALI: The chart is on the cake.

TORI: The cake is holding it better than the easel.

NIKO: Please lift it straight up. The frosting is soft.

Sali and Tori lift the chart. A corner of paper pulls up a frosting flower. Ivo and Mara enter, already in wedding clothes.

MARA: Is the reception ready?

SALI: It is almost ready.

IVO: We asked for a small dinner, not a public crisis.

SALI: The cake has one missing flower.

MARA: Then it has one missing flower.

SCENE 2 - INT. BANQUET HALL - LATER

Aunt Pala arrives and finds her place card at a table far from the stage. She moves it beside the couple. Niko returns it to its printed seat.

PALA: I am the oldest aunt. I should sit near the front.

MARA: We put you next to the speaker because you like music.

PALA: I like conversation.

Ivo moves the speaker two feet away. Its cable catches a chair. Three place cards slide off the table.

SALI: Please do not change the layout during setup.

PALA: I was improving it.

Tori tries to cover the missing cake flower with a real flower. Niko catches the bouquet before it touches the icing.

NIKO: The stems are not food-safe.

TORI: I can make a sugar flower from the spare frosting.

SCENE 3 - INT. BANQUET HALL - CONTINUOUS

The photographer asks Mara and Ivo to stand beneath an arch. The arch is too low for Ivo's headpiece. He takes it off and sets it on the seating chart table. The chart slides again, this time toward the cake. Mara catches it before it lands.

MARA: Put the chart away. We know where everyone sits.

SALI: The caterers need it to serve dinner.

NIKO: I have the table numbers on my order sheet. I can serve by table.

IVO: Then the chart can stay off the cake.

Sali folds the chart and puts it in a labeled folder. Aunt Pala looks at the place card and then at the speaker.

PALA: I do not want to be next to the speaker.

MARA: You can choose another seat. We are not assigning a family hierarchy.

PALA: I will sit by the window.

Before guests arrive, Sali checks the folded chart against Niko's table list. Two relatives share the same name. She adds the table numbers beside each name.

SALI: The list has two Aunt Pala entries. One is the bride's aunt, one is the groom's.

PALA: I am the aunt with the blue shawl.

MARA: And the other aunt has the green scarf.

NIKO: I will call the table numbers, not the names. That solves it.

Tori lays the bouquet on a side table and removes one loose leaf from the sugar flower.

TORI: No real stems near the cake.

SALI: And no chart on top of the cake.

The team laughs as Sali closes the folder and places it on the service shelf.

SCENE 4 - INT. BANQUET HALL - EVENING

Guests arrive. Niko serves by table number. Tori adds a sugar flower to the cake. It is a little larger than the missing frosting bloom. Mara tastes the icing from a spoon and nods.

MARA: That is good. Leave the cake alone now.

SALI: The final schedule is in the folder, and the speaker cable is clear.

Ivo asks Aunt Pala if the window seat is comfortable. She admits the music sounds better from there. The photographer approaches with another pose card.

PHOTOGRAPHER: The couple should look surprised by the cake.

IVO: We have already been surprised by the cake twice.

MARA: We would rather eat it.

SCENE 5 - INT. BANQUET HALL - LATER

The couple cuts the cake. The frosting flower stays in place. Niko brings plates, Tori collects the bouquet, and Sali sits down for the first time. Aunt Pala joins the dance after the speaker is moved to the far wall.

PALA: I can hear everyone now.

SALI: The schedule is finished.

MARA: The wedding is still happening. No more crisis announcements.

Ivo raises a glass, but the photographer begins to direct him. Mara takes his hand, and they toast without moving for the camera.

FINAL IMAGE: The empty cake stand holds the folded seating chart beneath a single sugar flower, while the couple eats the last slice at a small table by the window.
