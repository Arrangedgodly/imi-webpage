# The Statue Has No Face

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: art-house mysteries that treat an unfinished sculpture as an unsolvable symbol rather than an active commission*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A museum curator summons detectives to investigate a faceless statue, which turns out to be an unfinished commission awaiting its sculptor's reference photo.

Cast: curator Nema, an orangutan; detective Loro, a macaque; guard Seli, a gibbon; sculptor Pava, a capuchin; critic Tavi, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - INT. MODERN ART GALLERY - DAY

A white statue stands on a pedestal, its head smooth and blank. Nema leads Loro and Tavi into the room. Seli stands beside a rope barrier.

NEMA: The face is missing.

LORO: Was it attached yesterday?

NEMA: The artist has not returned.

Tavi walks slowly around the sculpture and writes in a notebook. He calls the blank surface a profound refusal of identity. Loro looks beneath the pedestal and finds a work order clipped to the base: FACE TO BE COMPLETED AFTER SITTER APPROVAL.

SCENE 2 - INT. GALLERY - LATER

Loro interviews Seli. The guard says a visitor has been sitting on a bench opposite the statue each afternoon. Tavi asks whether the visitor could be the missing face. Nema says the commission was based on a portrait of Pava's aunt. Loro checks the gallery sign-in book. Pava signed in, then left to find the reference photograph.

TAVI: The absence has a schedule.

LORO: It has a work order.

SCENE 3 - INT. MUSEUM HALLWAY - MOMENTS LATER

Pava returns carrying a framed photograph. The glass is cracked. She says the photo fell while she was carrying it from the studio. Loro asks if the aunt is available to sit for a new picture. Pava says her aunt is in the garden café downstairs. Nema asks whether the sculpture can remain blank until then. Tavi wants the gallery to stay open for his essay.

SCENE 3A - INT. MUSEUM CAFÉ - MOMENTS LATER

Loro and Seli escort Pava to the café. Her aunt is seated by the window with a tea cup. The framed reference photo shows the aunt facing left, but the glass reflection makes it appear that she faces right. Pava asks her to sit for a fresh sketch. The aunt turns her chair toward the light. Tavi arrives behind them and asks whether the blank statue represents the void between generations.

PAVA: It represents a face I have not finished.

TAVI: That is less mysterious.

Nema says the gallery visitors have been making up stories about the sculpture. Loro asks each one what they saw. One visitor says a ghost, another a judge, and another a sleeping bird. Pava listens, then carries her sketch pad back upstairs.

SCENE 4 - INT. GALLERY - AFTERNOON

The aunt arrives and sits on the bench. Pava places the photograph on an easel and compares it to the unfinished statue. The gallery visitors circle the work and wait for an explanation. Tavi begins a lecture about the void. Pava interrupts and asks her aunt to turn her head toward the window. Loro holds the easel while Seli moves the rope barrier to make room.

Pava sketches the face on a paper pad, then marks the statue with chalk. Tavi objects that the work is changing. Nema says the commission is meant to be completed. Pava smooths the chalk line and begins shaping the clay.

SCENE 4A - INT. GALLERY - LATER

Pava lays the fresh sketch beside the statue. The aunt's features are visible in the drawing. Tavi asks if the image will remain unfinished. Pava says the clay needs to set before she shapes the eyes. Loro returns the cracked photo to its frame and places it beside the work order. The guard moves the rope far enough to make space for the sculptor's stool.

A visitor peers through the barrier and asks whether the blank head is the final version. Nema points to the title card, which now reads WORK IN PROGRESS. Tavi turns his notebook to a clean page and writes a new essay title. The old title, THE FACE THAT WAS NOT THERE, goes into the recycling bin. Nema closes the gallery doors. Pava returns at opening time. Nema leaves the work order beside the title card.

SCENE 5 - INT. GALLERY - EVENING

The face is roughed in but not finished. The aunt leaves to catch her bus. Nema closes the gallery for the evening. Tavi writes a new title card: WORK IN PROGRESS. Loro places the work order beside it. Seli moves the rope farther out so visitors can see the sculptor's tools.

FINAL IMAGE: The statue's unfinished face looks toward the empty bench, while the title card bears Pava's appointment time for the next morning.
