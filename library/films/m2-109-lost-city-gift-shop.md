# The Lost City Has a Gift Shop

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: archaeological adventure films, traps triggered by every object, and museums treated as the final enemy*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: An explorer reaches a sealed chamber beneath the museum, but the treasure is a cataloged exhibit scheduled for return to its community.

Cast: Jori, a lemur archaeologist; Varo, a gorilla adventurer; Seli, a macaque curator; Pip, a capuchin student; and the conservator, courier and van driver.

SCENE 1. INT. MUSEUM BASEMENT, MORNING

Jori studies a floor plan from an old excavation. Varo carries a rope and a wide-brimmed hat. Pip points to a service door labeled STORAGE. Varo says the lost city must be hidden beyond it.

VARO: Every chamber has a trap.

SELI: This chamber has shelves.

They enter the storage room. A rolling ladder moves when Varo touches it. He jumps backward. Seli locks the wheels and shows him the safety label. Jori finds a crate marked "Loan return, North Valley community."

SCENE 2. INT. STORAGE ROOM, LATER

The crate contains a carved stone panel discovered during an old road project. Varo calls it a royal treasure. Jori reads the catalog record: the panel is on loan and due to return after conservation. Seli says the community has requested it for a new cultural center.

VARO: The treasure is leaving?

JORI: It belongs with its community.

PIP: Is there a trap?

SELI: There is a loading ramp.

Jori asks to see the return agreement. Seli shows the signed loan record and the center's receipt. Varo searches for a hidden clause. Pip reads the dates and confirms the van is due at four.

Pip asks if they can open the crate. Seli checks the conservation seal. It is intact. Varo asks whether the seal hides a trap. Jori says it protects the panel from dust. Varo reaches for the rope. Seli stops him and asks him to read the handling instructions.

VARO: It says lift from the base.

SELI: Correct. No rope around the carving.

PIP: And keep the seal facing up.

They release the crate straps, roll it onto a padded cart, and check each wheel. Varo insists on walking point. The cart's wheels squeak at each turn, and he treats every sound as a warning.

VARO: It clicked.

SELI: The cart crossed a floor seam.

PIP: I can oil the squeaky wheel.

Pip uses the approved lubricant. The squeak stops. Varo listens again, suspicious of the silence. Jori directs the cart into the conservation room.

SCENE 3. INT. CONSERVATION ROOM, AFTERNOON

The panel is ready for transport. A conservator checks the support frame and signs the condition report. Varo asks if the ancient chamber is behind the wall. Seli points to the building plan: the room is a loading bay.

Jori examines the panel and notices a small painted mark on the back.

JORI: This mark identifies the makers.

SELI: Is it on the transfer record?

Jori checks the record once and points to the field. It names the stonecutters' cooperative. Seli says the receiving center will preserve the mark and record its condition. Pip asks if they can show the panel's back. Jori says yes, as long as it stays on the padded supports. Varo leans in to study the mark, then catches himself before touching it.

Pip asks whether the community will display it. Seli says the cultural center plans to place it beside oral histories from local families. Varo asks if the adventurers are still needed. Jori hands him the transport strap and asks him to secure the crate while the crew moves it.

SCENE 4. EXT. MUSEUM LOADING BAY, EVENING

The crate is loaded into a padded van. Seli checks the tie-downs. Varo makes one last sweep for traps. He finds a wheel chock and asks whether it is a device. Jori shows him how it keeps the van from rolling. He places it behind the tire.

The courier asks who will sign the transfer form. Jori, Seli, and Pip sign as witnesses. Varo asks whether the mission is complete. Seli says the panel has left the museum and the receiving center will confirm its arrival. Jori asks if the title card should say they discovered the lost city. Seli points to the old road survey: local residents knew the site before the museum excavation. Varo crosses out "discovered." Pip writes the community name in full. The courier signs the transport receipt and closes the van. Varo asks for a dramatic farewell. Jori gives the crew a wave. The driver checks the route card and starts the engine. Pip watches until the van reaches the gate. Seli reminds Varo that the mission was returning the panel, not claiming it. He folds his map and puts it in the gift-shop recycling bin.

FINAL IMAGE: Varo watches the van drive away. Behind him, the gift shop's souvenir map labels the loading bay "EXIT," not "LOST CITY."
