# The Crown Is in the Lost and Found

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal succession epics, prophecy riddles, and ceremonial object reveals*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A prophecy says the next ruler will be chosen by a lost crown, but the palace staff has filed it in the lost-and-found under "small metal hat."

Cast: Princess Nima, a lemur; Prince Rook, a baboon; Archivist Talla, a macaque; Herald Wren, a gibbon; and Guard Sedge, a tortoise.

SCENE 1. INT. THRONE ROOM, DAWN

The throne is empty. The crown has vanished. Herald Wren reads an ancient prophecy from a scroll: "The one who finds the crown shall sit beneath the tallest roof." Rook looks at the vaulted ceiling. Nima asks where the crown was last seen. Wren points at the portrait from last week's ceremony.

NIMA: Did anyone check the cabinet?

ROOK: That would be premature.

Talla enters with the inventory book. The crown was signed out for polishing, then delivered to the palace lost-and-found. Rook objects that a royal object cannot be lost among ordinary objects. Talla asks what makes an object royal if nobody can find it.

SCENE 2. INT. PALACE ARCHIVE, MORNING

The archive shelves are organized by object size, not rank. Rook rejects the system and asks for the royal section. Talla shows him the category called hats. It includes a cooking pot, two helmets, and a crown-shaped pastry mold. Wren insists the prophecy must be interpreted literally. Nima asks whether the tallest roof might be listed in the maintenance book. Sedge finds the greenhouse inspection record and confirms its roofline is higher than the tower.

Talla searches the log. The receipt lists the crown as "small metal hat, one, gold-colored." Rook says the clerk must have insulted the crown. Nima notes that the description is accurate. Wren rereads the prophecy, emphasizing the words tallest roof. Rook orders a search of the tallest tower. Sedge says the greenhouse roof is higher and asks whether they want to check the records first.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE LOST-AND-FOUND, AFTERNOON

The clerk checks the return shelf before they search. Rook asks whether anyone has ever lost a crown. The clerk says three, including the one he is wearing for the portrait sitting. Rook touches his head, then looks embarrassed. The clerk shows him the checkout labels; all royal items are tracked the same way as umbrellas and hats.

A clerk opens a velvet bag. It contains three mismatched shoes. Rook gives the bag a speech about loyalty. Nima asks for the cabinet key. It hangs on a hook beside the clerk. Rook calls this a test of character. The clerk calls it a key hook.

The siblings search the bins. They find a helmet, a cooking pot, and three left gloves. The crown sits in a tray labeled SMALL METAL HAT. Rook lifts it above his head. A bell rings. Sedge asks him to sign it out. The form requires his name and a return date. Rook writes "forever." The clerk asks for an actual date. Nima writes tomorrow and signs beside him.

SCENE 4. EXT. PALACE GREENHOUSE, EVENING

The siblings carry the crown to the greenhouse, the tallest roof in the palace grounds. Rain drips through a loose gutter and lands on the display table. Sedge puts a bucket beneath the leak. Wren reads the full prophecy from the scroll: "The one who finds the crown shall sit beneath the tallest roof, and keep the garden dry." Rook puts the crown on his head. A drip lands in it. He removes it and holds up his helmet to catch the next drop.

NIMA: The prophecy asks for a dry garden.

ROOK: I can hold the bucket.

TALLA: A ruler must organize the repair, not become the bucket.

Nima checks the gutter. Rook steadies the ladder while she tightens the bracket. The leak stops. The council arrives, having followed the crown's checkout log. Wren asks who should sit beneath the roof. Nima points to the greenhouse bench, now dry.

SCENE 5. INT. PALACE GREENHOUSE, NIGHT

The council meets under the repaired greenhouse roof. Nima presents a maintenance schedule and a budget for gutter inspections. Rook presents the crown's return receipt, signed properly this time. The council appoints Nima to chair the garden council and Rook to supervise roof maintenance. Wren asks whether that counts as a coronation. Sedge says it counts as a meeting with assigned roles.

Rook returns the crown to its display case. Talla adds a new catalog label: CROWN, GOLD, CEREMONIAL, SIGN OUT WITH DATE. The lost-and-found clerk brings the velvet bag back, hoping to find its owner. Rook identifies it as his speech bag and checks it out.

The maintenance schedule is pinned beside the crown case, where visitors can read it.

FINAL IMAGE: The crown sits safely in its case. Rook checks the gutter before leaving; Wren starts a coronation announcement, but Nima hands him the maintenance schedule to read instead.
