# The Mountain That Wasn't There

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mountaineering epics, summit fever, heroic rope teams, and the climactic reveal that the route is a nearby hill*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
LEAD CLIMBER TAVI, a capuchin
MAP READER SOK, a ring-tailed lemur
GUIDE MELL, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. BASE CAMP - DAWN

A row of tents sits beneath a dramatic peak. The team gathers around a map. A red line climbs the mountain and ends at a flag.

TAVI: We summit by sundown.

SOK: The map says this is the east ridge.

MELL: The map is upside down.

They turn it. The red line now points to a rounded hill behind camp. The team insists the mountain's shape must be hiding beneath the clouds.

SCENE 2. EXT. LOWER SLOPE - DAY

The climbers rope together and ascend a steep trail. They cross a stream, pass a warning sign, and stop at a trail fork. Tavi chooses the most dramatic route. Sok reads the markers and chooses the safe switchback.

TAVI: The straight path is faster.

MELL: The straight path ends at a cliff.

They take the switchback. A montage shows gloves tightening, snacks rationed, and weather changing. Tavi repeatedly announces the summit is near. Each time, they reach another trail marker.

SCENE 2A. EXT. SWITCHBACK - MIDDAY

The trail narrows. A gust pushes loose snow across the path. Tavi wants to continue straight up the ridge; Mell checks the weather and says the route is exposed.

TAVI: We'll lose the summit window.

MELL: We'll lose the trail if we climb into the cloud.

Sok finds a trail marker beneath a crust of snow. The marker points to a sheltered switchback. They take it, moving slowly and checking on each other at every bend. Tavi keeps looking toward the peak, but the cloud covers it.

SOK: We can climb what we can see.

The group reaches a small ledge and rests. Mell checks the map. The line they followed does not lead to the dramatic peak; it loops back to the viewpoint. The team can return safely before dark or wait for clearer weather.

TAVI: I wanted a summit photo.

MELL: We can take one from the viewpoint. No one needs to pretend it is the top.

SCENE 3. EXT. RIDGE - AFTERNOON

A gust tears the map from Sok's hand. It lands on a rock ledge below. Tavi prepares to climb down. Mell points to a path around the ridge. They follow the safe route and retrieve the map.

SOK: We have been climbing the wrong hill.

TAVI: The view is still good.

MELL: The summit flag is the picnic shelter.

SCENE 3A. EXT. RIDGE - LATE AFTERNOON

The weather worsens. Snow begins to cover the trail markers. Tavi considers climbing one more rise to get a better view of the real mountain. Mell checks the cloud line and says they have enough light to return to the shelter, but not enough to reach the far peak.

TAVI: We came to summit.

SOK: We reached the viewpoint marked on the map.

MELL: A safe turn is part of climbing. We can choose another day for the high route.

Tavi looks at the clouds, then folds the map correctly. The team marks the trail with bright cloth and descends by the switchback. At the bottom, they tell the family at the shelter that the actual mountain is across the valley and needs a longer route.

FAMILY MEMBER: We thought this was the summit.

SOK: It is the summit of this hill.

SCENE 4. EXT. SUMMIT PICNIC SHELTER - SUNSET

The team reaches the summit. It is a low hill with a shelter, a water fountain, and a sign that says "VIEWPOINT." A family is eating lunch. Tavi tries to plant the expedition flag; the ground is too soft, and the flag leans into a trash bin.

A child points to the distant dramatic mountain across the valley. The real peak is far beyond the route they took. The team stares at it in silence.

TAVI: We can try again tomorrow.

SOK: We can first check the map with the guide.

MELL: We'll do that at the trailhead.

SCENE 5. EXT. VIEWPOINT - DUSK

The climbers use the shelter's map board to plan a new route. The real mountain requires a licensed guide, weather window, and two days. They book the route for next month rather than attempt it unprepared.

The team shares snacks with the family. Tavi lowers the flag and plants it beside the viewpoint sign. It stands upright in the soft soil.

FINAL IMAGE: The false summit sign is visible in the foreground, with the true mountain rising far behind it. The team takes a photograph that includes both.
