# The Moon Fell into the Well

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: folktale adventures, mysterious reflections, and rescue plans that confuse an image for a thing*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
NARA, a gibbon village keeper
VEX, a macaque hunter
PIP, a capuchin climber
TAVI, a lemur astronomer
THE MOON, a silent reflection

SCENE 1. EXT. VILLAGE WELL - NIGHT

The village gathers around the well. The moon shines at the bottom of the water. Nara points to it with a lantern.

NARA: The moon has fallen in.

TAVI: The moon is in the sky.

VEX: Then what is in the well?

TAVI: Its reflection.

Vex lowers a rope and bucket. He says he will bring the moon up. Pip offers to climb down, but Nara stops him. The well is deep and the water is cold.

PIP: We could cover the well.

NARA: Then the moon will be trapped under the cover.

Tavi explains that the moon is not in the water, but Vex has already tied the bucket to his belt. The reflection trembles as the bucket touches the surface.

VEX: It is moving.

TAVI: The water is moving.

SCENE 2. INT. VILLAGE SHED - LATER

The group prepares a rescue plan. Pip makes a pulley from a wheel and a long branch. Nara brings a mirror so Tavi can show that the moon is still above them. Vex rejects it.

VEX: We need a moon-sized mirror.

TAVI: We need a clear view of the sky.

Nara holds up the mirror. It reflects a lantern, then the roof. Pip adjusts it. The mirror shows the moon, but only when he tilts it at a precise angle.

PIP: I have the moon.

VEX: Bring it to the well.

Pip carries the mirror outside. The moon disappears from its surface. He turns back toward the shed. It returns.

NARA: The moon follows you.

TAVI: The reflection follows the angle.

Vex says they must lift the reflection from above. He climbs a ladder and leans over the well with a net. The net dips into the water and comes up full of leaves.

VEX: The moon escaped.

NARA: The leaves were there before.

Pip holds a dark cloth over the well. The reflection vanishes. Vex pulls the cloth away, and the moon appears again. He calls this proof that the moon is hiding. Tavi takes the cloth and folds it into a narrow strip. She asks Pip to hold one end while she lowers the other beside the water.

TAVI: The image is below the surface.

VEX: Then the cloth can catch it.

The cloth touches the water. The moon breaks into ripples. Pip jerks it back. A wet corner wraps around his wrist like a silver ribbon.

PIP: It has caught me.

NARA: The well has caught your sleeve.

SCENE 3. EXT. WELL - MIDNIGHT

Tavi draws a circle on the ground to mark the moon's position. As the night passes, the reflection moves across the water. Vex believes it is trying to reach the edge. Pip lowers a lantern into the well to guide it.

The lantern's light scatters across the surface. The moon seems to break into many small moons. Vex grabs the rope. The pulley spins, lowering the bucket too quickly. Nara jams a stick into the wheel.

NARA: Stop pulling.

PIP: I am holding it.

VEX: The moon has multiplied.

TAVI: The lantern made more reflections.

Vex pulls the bucket up. It holds only water and leaves. He looks disappointed. Nara asks if he expected to hold the moon in his hands.

VEX: I wanted to be the one who rescued it.

Tavi says the moon does not need rescue. Pip offers to rescue the bucket instead. He carries it to the trough and pours out the leaves.

The bucket rings against the stone trough. Vex calls it a farewell bell. Nara checks the rope for frays and knots it to a post so no one lowers it again. Tavi draws a line on the ground showing where the moon had appeared. Vex steps on the line while posing for the village record and smudges the mark.

VEX: The moon has left no trace.

TAVI: The moon is still above the horizon.

PIP: The bucket has kept the leaves.

SCENE 4. EXT. WELL - DAWN

The moon sets. Its reflection fades from the water. Vex watches the empty well.

VEX: It has gone.

TAVI: It went west. The sun is rising east.

Nara lowers the mirror into the well. It reflects the bright sky. Pip sees his face. He says the well has taken him.

PIP: There are two of me.

NARA: Only in the mirror.

Pip turns the mirror. His reflection disappears, then returns when he faces it toward himself.

VEX: The well gives back what you show it.

SCENE 5. EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE - MORNING

The village asks what happened to the moon. Vex reports that it returned to the sky on its own. Tavi says it was never in the well. Nara has Pip cover the well with a wooden lid, not to trap the moon, but to keep leaves from falling in.

Pip draws a moon on the lid. Vex adds a tiny rescue rope. Tavi wipes it off and paints an arrow toward the sky.

FINAL IMAGE: The next night, a small puddle beside the well reflects the moon. Vex takes one step toward it, then checks the sky first.
