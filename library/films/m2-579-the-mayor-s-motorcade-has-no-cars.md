# The Mayor's Motorcade Has No Cars

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: political thrillers that promise a convoy and deliver a walking tour*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
MAYOR VEX, an orangutan
AIDE MARA, a macaque
REPORTER PIP, a capuchin
ROUTE CLERK ODO, a gibbon
MARSHAL NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. CITY HALL - MORNING

A banner reads MOTORCADE DEPARTS AT TEN. Behind it, an empty parking lane. Vex adjusts his sash.

VEX: The convoy is invisible.

MARA: The cars were canceled. The mayor is walking to the plaza with staff.

ODO: The route is two blocks and accessible. The sidewalk is clear.

PIP: I came to report on the motorcade.

NARI: You can walk alongside the group, behind the marshal.

VEX: The public expects a procession.

SCENE 2. EXT. CITY HALL STEPS - CONTINUOUS

Mara hands Vex a printed route card. He looks down the street for vehicles.

VEX: I hear no engines.

PIP: There are no cars.

ODO: The route avoids the construction lane and crosses at the signal.

VEX: Can we use bicycles as escorts?

NARI: Not without closing the path. Walk in pairs and keep the sidewalk open.

SCENE 2A. EXT. CITY HALL STEPS - CONTINUOUS

Mara checks the route card against the sidewalk. The first block has a curb ramp; the second has construction fencing.

MARA: We'll cross at the signal, then use the west sidewalk. The east corner is closed.

ODO: The walking group is six people. The marshal stays at the front; the aide remains beside the mayor.

VEX: The motorcade usually has a lead car.

NARI: Today the marshal has a bright vest. That's visible without a car.

PIP: The camera can walk behind the group. I won't step into the crosswalk until the signal changes.

Vex sees a row of parked bicycles and raises his hand as if to summon an escort. None move.

VEX: The fleet is unresponsive.

MARA: They are parked, and they belong to residents. Leave them alone.

SCENE 2B. EXT. CITY HALL STEPS - CONTINUOUS

The route marshal reviews the public walking route. Vex stands beneath the banner and waits while Odo points out the curb ramp.

ODO: Ramp is clear. The construction barrier is on the east corner; our path goes west.

VEX: A convoy would cross this distance in seconds.

MARA: The walking group needs several minutes. We have allowed enough time.

PIP: The camera battery is charged. I'll stay behind the mayor and keep the lens away from pedestrians.

NARI: The marshal will lead, then the mayor and aide, then the reporter. No one blocks the ramp.

Vex tries to call the parked bicycles to attention with a hand signal. A bell rings as a cyclist leaves the rack.

VEX: Our escort has answered.

MARA: That's a cyclist leaving. Do not step into their path.

Nari holds the group until the cyclist passes. Odo folds the route card and puts it in his jacket, freeing both hands.

ODO: Sidewalk clear. We can begin on the walk signal.

VEX: The motorcade is ready.

NARI: The walking group is ready.

They move at ordinary speed toward the crossing.

SCENE 2C. EXT. CROSSWALK - CONTINUOUS

The walk signal changes. Nari starts across; Vex waits for the full group before following.

PIP: Camera rolling. The mayor is walking with the staff, no cars in the frame.

MARA: Keep the crosswalk clear after we reach the plaza.



ODO: We will reach the plaza before the meeting begins.



ODO: The route card is back in my jacket. No one is carrying it into the meeting.

SCENE 2D. EXT. PLAZA APPROACH - CONTINUOUS

The crossing ends at the plaza ramp. Nari checks that all six people have arrived on the same side of the street.

NARI: Group count is complete at the plaza approach. The curb ramp is clear for anyone approaching behind us.

VEX: The convoy has reached its destination.

MARA: We reached the plaza entrance. The meeting is inside.

PIP: I have a clear shot of the walking group and the route sign.

ODO: Route card shows the same west-side path. No detours remain.

SCENE 3. EXT. PLAZA ROUTE - LATER

A bakery delivery cart blocks the west sidewalk. Nari stops the group before the narrow gap.

MARA: We wait until the path is clear.

VEX: The motorcade is delayed.

PIP: The walking group is paused for a cart.

The baker moves the cart into the loading bay. Odo checks the route card; the group can pass along the same sidewalk without crossing the road.

NARI: Path is clear. Stay together and continue to the plaza entrance.

VEX: We proceed on foot.

SCENE 4. EXT. PLAZA - DAY

Vex arrives without fanfare. Pip records the arrival. Mara checks the route card and returns it to Odo.

VEX: The motorcade has arrived.

MARA: The mayor and staff have arrived on foot.

TAG: Vex waves to a parked bicycle as if it were a limousine.
