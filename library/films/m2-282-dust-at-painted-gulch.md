# Dust at Painted Gulch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: western showdowns, lone riders, tumbleweed entrances, and saloon confrontations that postpone the practical solution*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A new sheriff arrives in Painted Gulch to face a notorious outlaw, only to learn the town's missing water pump has been taken apart by the very people waiting for a duel.

Cast
- SHERIFF ADA, a macaque with a steady gaze and a tail tucked under her coat.
- ROLL, a capuchin traveling repairer whose tool belt jingles like spurs.
- MAYOR LUTE, a gibbon who delivers every announcement like a wanted poster.
- BEE, a ring-tailed lemur saloon owner and amateur mapmaker.
- THE RIDER, a shadowy figure on a mule.

## Scene 1: The Arrival

EXT. PAINTED GULCH - DUSK

A tumbleweed crosses the street. Sheriff Ada rides into town. A wanted poster flaps on the saloon wall: THE RIDER, WANTED FOR TAKING THE PUMP HANDLE.

MAYOR LUTE: We have waited for you, Sheriff.

ADA: Where is the pump?

MAYOR LUTE: Taken by the outlaw.

BEE: The handle was removed for repair. The poster is dramatic but inaccurate.

A hoofbeat echoes. The Rider appears at the far end of the street, then turns toward the water tower.

MAYOR LUTE: The showdown is beginning.

ADA: The water tower is leaking.

## Scene 2: The Saloon

INT. SALOON - NIGHT

Bee pours water from a small barrel. Roll, the repairer, enters with a wrench and a rolled pump diagram. The mayor mistakes him for the Rider.

LUTE: Drop the tools.

ROLL: I have come to fix the pump.

ADA: The wanted poster says the Rider took the handle.

ROLL: I borrowed it from the town shed. The thread was stripped.

BEE: He has been repairing it since noon.

MAYOR LUTE: Then why did no one tell me?

BEE: We did. You were practicing your speech.

A gust blows the poster down. Its silhouette lands on the wall behind Roll, making his tool belt look like a bandolier. The room gasps. Ada rolls up the poster.

## Scene 3: The Standoff

EXT. WATER TOWER - NIGHT

The Rider returns, carrying a wooden crate. Ada follows. The Rider dismounts and places the crate beside the tower.

ADA: Are you the one who took the handle?

THE RIDER: I am the one who found it in the dry creek.

The Rider removes a scarf. It is Bee's brother, Tavi, who found the old handle downstream after a flood. He has been trying to return it without being accused of stealing it.

TAVI: I rode in from the ridge. I thought the town would recognize the crate.

ADA: The town recognized your shadow.

TAVI: It is a large shadow.

Roll inspects the handle. The threads are intact; the pump's casing is cracked. Mayor Lute arrives with a ceremonial wrench.

LUTE: Let the duel decide who repairs it.

ADA: We can replace the casing.

LUTE: That is less exciting.

ADA: It is more likely to give you water.

## Scene 4: The Repair

The group dismantles the pump. Roll uses the tool belt's straps to brace the pipe; Bee reads the hand-drawn diagram; Tavi keeps the heavy casing from rolling. Ada turns the wrench while the mayor counts turns like a gunfight.

LUTE: One turn. Two turns. Three turns.

ROLL: Stop. It is seated.

LUTE: The duel is over?

ROLL: The pump is repaired.

They prime the handle. Nothing happens. Tavi crouches and notices the intake is blocked by a tumbleweed. He reaches into the pipe, pulls out the dry bundle, and the water begins to flow.

## Scene 5: The Town's Story

The residents gather. Mayor Lute tears down the wanted poster and replaces it with a notice about the pump repair. The Rider returns the wooden crate to the shed. Ada asks the town to keep a record of borrowed tools.

BEE: We can also write down where the pump parts are stored.

ROLL: I will add that the casing needs replacing before the next flood.

LUTE: Is there a way to make that sound like a showdown?

ADA: Try standing still while someone turns a wrench.

The town laughs. The mayor poses beside the flowing pump, trying to look heroic. The water splashes his boots.

## Scene 6: The New Law

At sunrise, Ada pins up a new sign: REPAIR FIRST, DUEL NEVER. Tavi leaves for the ridge, and Roll stays to rebuild the water tower. The town now has water, and the Rider is no longer wanted.

LUTE: The west road is clear.

TAVI: I know. I came from there.

LUTE: A final western farewell.

TAVI: A regular goodbye.

## Final Image

A tumbleweed rolls past the repaired pump, catches on its base, and remains there as water runs steadily into the trough.
