# The Case of the Unclaimed Shadow

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: gothic mystery cinema, where a moving shadow in a quiet archive is treated as a haunting*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A night archivist investigates a shadow that crosses the records room, only to discover the culprit is a lantern and an overly dramatic curtain.

Cast: Archivist Luma, a lemur; inspector Brik, a macaque; attendant Pava, a gibbon; custodian Seli, a capuchin.

SCENE 1. INT. ARCHIVE, NIGHT

A shadow passes across a tall shelf. Luma drops a ledger.

LUMA: It crossed from the east wall to the atlas shelf.

BRIK: Did you see a person?

LUMA: I saw a shadow.

PAVA: The archive is closed.

SELI: I was in the hallway.

The shadow appears again, moving toward the door.

BRIK: It is approaching.

LUMA: It may be searching for a record.

PAVA: Does it have a request slip?

Brik draws a chalk circle on the floor to mark where the shadow appeared. The shadow crosses the circle again.

BRIK: It has escaped containment.

SELI: I have a lantern.

LUMA: Do not bring it closer.

Seli holds the lantern up. The shadow grows larger.

BRIK: It has expanded.

SELI: The lantern is doing that.

SCENE 2. INT. RECORDS ROOM, CONTINUOUS

Brik places a chair beneath the window and asks everyone to stay clear of the wall. Seli marks the shadow's edge with chalk. When the branch outside sways, the mark moves beyond the line.

BRIK: The suspect has crossed our boundary.

LUMA: The chalk is on the wall. The branch is outside.

PAVA: Should I close the curtain?

BRIK: Not until we see what happens next.

Seli holds still. The shadow stops. She shifts one foot; the lantern swings, and the silhouette changes again.

SELI: I have become part of the case.

BRIK: Then remain exactly where you are.

She steps away, and the shape returns to the branch.



BRIK: The suspect has crossed our boundary.

LUMA: The chalk is on the wall. The branch is outside.

PAVA: Should I close the curtain?

BRIK: Not until we see what happens next.

Seli holds still. The shadow stops. She shifts one foot; the lantern swings, and the silhouette changes again.

SELI: I have become part of the case.

BRIK: Then remain exactly where you are.

She steps away, and the shape returns to the branch.

They close the curtains. The shadow vanishes. Pava opens them again to inspect the window. A branch outside moves in the breeze, and the shadow returns.

LUMA: It comes from outside.

BRIK: Then the archive has a visitor.

PAVA: The branch is not a visitor.

BRIK: It is crossing the window.

Seli ties the curtain back. The branch shadow becomes a long shape across the wall. Luma recognizes the branch as an old lilac tree, but Brik insists it has changed shape.

BRIK: The suspect has grown.

LUMA: Trees do that.

PAVA: We should record the pattern.

Brik notes the time. The shadow moves again when Seli walks past the lantern.

SELI: I think I may be the suspect.

BRIK: You cannot investigate yourself.

LUMA: You can check the lantern angle.

Brik adjusts it. The shadow falls across the ledger shelf, then the map drawer.

SCENE 3. INT. ARCHIVE DESK, LATER

Luma checks the old floor plan. The archive window faces the courtyard. A lamp post outside sits between the window and the tree.

LUMA: The lamp post is making a second shadow.

BRIK: A second suspect.

PAVA: One branch and one post.

SELI: I can move the lantern to see if the shadow changes.

Brik objects that the case must be solved through evidence, not guesswork. Seli moves the lantern anyway. The shadow shifts to the floor.

[SFX: LANTERN SET DOWN.]

BRIK: It followed the lantern.

LUMA: The lantern made it.

BRIK: It followed the maker.

Pava shines a small light on the branch from outside. The shadow moves in the opposite direction.

PAVA: The branch is blowing in the breeze.

BRIK: We have an accomplice.

LUMA: The wind.

SCENE 4. EXT. COURTYARD, MOMENTS LATER

They step outside. The branch brushes the window ledge. Seli secures it with a soft tie. The shadow stops moving.

BRIK: The haunting has been restrained.

LUMA: The tree has been prevented from tapping the glass.

PAVA: And the lamp post?

The lamp post stands still. A moth circles its light, projecting a flickering shadow.

BRIK: A smaller suspect.

SELI: The moth is outside.

BRIK: It can still be questioned.

Luma gently opens the window. The moth flies away. The shadow disappears.

SCENE 5. INT. ARCHIVE, BEFORE DAWN

The team returns to the records room. Brik writes the case report: "Unclaimed shadow, caused by branch, post, lantern, and moth." Luma points out that the shadow is not an object and cannot be claimed.

BRIK: Then it is unclaimed.

PAVA: The archive has many unclaimed things.

SELI: There is a glove under the desk.

BRIK: That is a real object. We can file it.

Luma turns off the lantern. Moonlight crosses the wall. A still shadow lies over the atlas shelf.

BRIK: The suspect has returned.

LUMA: It is the shelf.

BRIK: The shelf has an alibi?

LUMA: It has been here all night.

Brik closes the ledger. The shadow remains.

[SFX: LEDGER CLOSES, QUIET NIGHT.]

BRIK: We will reopen the case at sunrise.

PAVA: The sun will make more shadows.

BRIK: Then we will have a full docket.
