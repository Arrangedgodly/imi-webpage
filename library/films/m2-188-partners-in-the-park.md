# Partners in the Park

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: buddy-cop action films, mismatched partners, explosive car chases, and a case cracked by the partner who was ignored*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
DETECTIVE REX, a macaque veteran
OFFICER LUMA, a capuchin new partner
CHIEF BELL, a gibbon
MR. PEEP, a ring-tailed lemur puppet maker

SCENE 1. EXT. KETTLE CITY PARK - DAY

A puppet of the city mayor is missing from the annual parade. Detective Rex strides into the park with a leather jacket and two holsters. Officer Luma follows on a bicycle with a basket of evidence tags.

REX: We work alone.

LUMA: Then why do we have matching badges?

REX: Department policy.

The puppet's hat lies beneath a bench. Rex lifts it with a gloved hand. Luma notices a trail of felt fibers leading toward the duck pond.

REX: The suspect is headed north.

LUMA: The fibers lead east.

Rex drives away in the department sedan. Luma pedals after him.

SCENE 2. EXT. PARK PATH - CONTINUOUS

Rex's sedan performs a dramatic skid around a picnic table. A juice box sprays the windshield. He wipes it with his sleeve, which leaves a larger red stain.

Luma arrives on the bicycle and points out that the trail ends at a puppet theater. Rex enters first, kicking open the door. It falls inward onto a pile of foam swords.

A parade cart suddenly rolls from the theater. Rex leaps onto the sedan hood to intercept it. Luma turns her bicycle sideways, blocking the path without hitting the puppets. The cart circles a fountain and bumps a stack of folding chairs.

REX: We have a runaway vehicle.

LUMA: It is a handcart. The brake is off.

Rex grabs the cart handle. It swings him around the fountain in a full circle. Luma catches the rear wheel with her boot and stops the cart. Inside are the mayor puppet's legs and a box of felt scraps.

REX: We've recovered the lower half.

LUMA: This is a scheduled repair. The parts are labeled.

Rex studies the tags: "BODY," "HEAD," "HAT," "STRINGS." He flips the "BODY" label over and discovers "RETURN TO WORKSHOP."

REX: The clues are communicating.

LUMA: The labels are doing their job.

SCENE 3. INT. PUPPET WORKSHOP

Mr. Peep is repairing a dragon puppet. Rex interrogates him under a dangling light.

REX: Where is the mayor?

PEEP: In the parade office.

REX: The puppet mayor.

PEEP: On the workbench. I repaired his hat and sent him to the parade office.

Rex turns. The puppet is in pieces on the bench. Luma notices the stitching. The missing mayor was not stolen; the parade crew had dismantled it for repairs, and someone moved its hat to the park for a quick fitting.

REX: The crime scene is a craft station.

LUMA: We could call the parade coordinator.

REX: We don't call. We kick down the next door.

Luma calls. A parade cart rolls up outside with the missing puppet's body. It has been carried there by two volunteers, both wearing matching felt smocks.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK PATH - AFTERNOON

A second alarm: the puppet's head is missing. Rex sees a dark shape sprint across the lawn and chases it. Luma follows the fiber trail. The chase circles the pond, passes the duck enclosure, and ends at a tree.

Rex climbs the tree. A squirrel monkey drops the puppet head into his hands.

SQUIRREL MONKEY: I'm the head's puppeteer. I was practicing from up there so it could wave above the crowd.

REX: You could have told us.

SQUIRREL MONKEY: You ran past me three times.

Luma assembles the puppet's body and head. The chief arrives and asks for the case summary.

LUMA: The mayor puppet was in scheduled repair. The head was being tested. The hat was moved for fitting. No theft occurred.

REX: There was a chase.

CHIEF BELL: Why?

REX: The park has excellent paths.

SCENE 5. EXT. PARADE ROUTE - DAY

The puppet is reassembled. Luma checks each joint and gives the puppeteers a clear route. Rex stands beside her, no longer holding the department sedan's keys.

REX: I thought we were partners.

LUMA: We are. You can drive the parade cart.

Rex mounts the cart. The wheels wobble. He grips the puppet strings and pulls too hard. The mayor puppet bows to every bystander, then turns and bows to a tree.

CHIEF BELL: The city's most respectful mayor.

Rex learns to ease the strings. Luma walks beside him, guiding the cart around the turns. They reach the final platform without a crash.

FINAL IMAGE: The puppet mayor waves over the crowd. Rex and Luma stand shoulder to shoulder beneath it, both covered in foam sword dust. The department sedan remains parked, clean and unused.
