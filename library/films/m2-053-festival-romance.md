# The Last Seat on the Ferris Wheel

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: festival romance films, meet-cutes, and grand gestures interrupted by practical operations*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: Two festival workers meet in the Ferris wheel line, but the announcer keeps narrating their practical conversation as a romance while ride safety interrupts every cinematic moment.

Cast: Pella, a lemur balloon artist; Niko, a capuchin electrician; Tessa, a macaque ride operator; and Mr. Wren, a gibbon announcer who turns announcements into love scenes.

SCENE 1. EXT. FESTIVAL GROUNDS, EVENING

Rain slows the rides. Pella holds a pear-shaped balloon. Niko carries a coil of cable. They reach the Ferris wheel line at the same time.

NIKO: You can go ahead.

PELLA: I was here first.

TESSA: You're both in the wrong line. Staff entrance is behind the booth.

They step aside. The announcer sees them and speaks into the microphone.

WREN: Two strangers meet beneath the glow of the wheel.

TESSA: It's closed for a latch inspection.

Wren lowers the microphone. Niko offers to check the cable. Tessa says only the ride crew touches the equipment. Pella holds the balloon while Tessa tests the latch.

SCENE 2. EXT. FERRIS WHEEL PLATFORM, LATER

Wren asks Pella to stand beneath a string of lights for a promotional photograph. She asks if the lights are connected to Niko's cable. Niko checks; they are connected to a separate generator. Wren requests a dramatic glance. Pella looks at the exit sign. Niko follows her gaze and asks if she needs to leave. She says she is checking where the delivery goes.

Tessa opens the ride. The line has grown. Niko helps direct guests away from a wet cable. Pella ties a loose balloon to a child's wrist. Wren narrates this as a symbolic exchange. The child asks for the balloon back.

NIKO: It was for a customer.

PELLA: You can help me deliver it after the shift.

WREN: A date beneath the lights!

TESSA: A delivery route. Keep the queue moving.

The ride stops after one rotation. The announcer calls it a romantic pause. Tessa radios that she is checking a bolt. Niko asks if she needs a flashlight; she does. He passes it from below without climbing on the machinery.

SCENE 3. EXT. FERRIS WHEEL LINE, CONTINUOUS

A vendor offers them two cups of cocoa. Wren calls it a shared cup. Pella takes one and gives the other to Niko. He offers to pay her back. She says the vendor already charged her. Wren whispers that the exchange has emotional weight. The vendor returns to collect the cups and asks them not to leave litter beside the ride.

Pella and Niko wait while Tessa checks the bolt. The crowd starts clapping. Tessa asks them to stop so she can hear the motor. The clapping dies.

PELLA: Do you work every night?

NIKO: Festival nights. Most of them.

PELLA: I make balloons for events. I like the quiet ones.

NIKO: This one has an announcer.

Wren leans toward the microphone, waiting for a response. No one gives him one. The motor hums evenly. Tessa gives a thumbs-up and restarts the wheel.

SCENE 4. EXT. FERRIS WHEEL PLATFORM, NIGHT

Niko offers Pella the last seat. She asks if he wants to ride too. They board together. At the top, Wren begins a speech about destiny. The ride stops for a final safety check. Tessa calls up that the wheel is fine, but a guest has dropped a mitten. She retrieves it with a pole.

WREN: Their hands almost touch.

PELLA: I'm holding the rail.

NIKO: I'm holding the other rail.

The mitten lands safely on the platform. The ride resumes. They finish the circle and step off. Pella asks Niko to help deliver the balloon. He says yes, after he returns the flashlight.

SCENE 5. EXT. FESTIVAL GATE, NIGHT

Wren asks them to walk through the gate one more time for a closing shot. Pella says the gate is closing. Niko points out the staff exit. The two leave through it, carrying the folded chair cart together. Wren tries to frame the cart as a symbolic burden; Tessa asks him to help push it.

The festival closes. Pella delivers the balloon to the child and returns the string spool. Niko returns the flashlight to Tessa. They stack chairs together. Wren prepares a final announcement, but Tessa takes the microphone and thanks the crew for a safe closing.

NIKO: Coffee after cleanup?

PELLA: Yes. That's a real question.

They stop to check the shift schedule before making plans.

FINAL IMAGE: They leave side by side. The pear balloon slips from the child's hand and lands in an empty Ferris wheel car. Wren starts to announce that fate has boarded; Tessa unplugs the microphone.
