# The Secret Life of the Courtyard Pigeons

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: nature documentaries that assign elaborate motives to ordinary animal behavior through solemn narration and selective editing*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: A documentary crew spends a day filming courtyard pigeons, while the building caretaker keeps interrupting the narrator's grand theories with practical facts.

Cast: Narrator Vela, a gibbon; camerawork by Nori, a macaque; caretaker Duma, a capuchin; Pigeon One, a pigeon; Pigeon Two, a pigeon.

SCENE 1 - EXT. APARTMENT COURTYARD - DAWN

A pigeon pecks at a crumb. Nori slowly pushes the camera closer. Vela whispers into a voiceover microphone.

VELA (V.O.): At first light, the solitary hunter claims its territory.

DUMA: That's the courtyard. The crumb fell from my breakfast.

NORI: Please stay outside the frame.

Duma sweeps a path. The pigeon hops sideways, then returns to the crumb.

VELA (V.O.): A rival approaches. The two prepare for a confrontation.

DUMA: They are both eating the same piece.

SCENE 2 - EXT. COURTYARD BENCH - MORNING

Nori films two pigeons bobbing their heads. Vela records another voiceover, now with more urgency.

VELA (V.O.): The pair performs an intricate courtship dance.

DUMA: They are walking around the bench. One is avoiding a puddle.

Nori pans to a puddle. A pigeon steps around it and then pecks a second crumb.

NORI: The shot is less dramatic now.

VELA (V.O.): The landscape itself has become an obstacle.

DUMA: The puddle will dry by lunch.

Nori captures a close-up of the pigeon's feet. The camera shows a thread caught around one toe. Duma approaches slowly and points to the thread.

DUMA: That one needs help. Can I use your frame?

SCENE 2A - INT. BUILDING LOBBY - LATER

Nori and Vela review the morning shots on a laptop. Vela has written several titles on a board: THE WAR FOR A CRUMB, THE GREAT PUDDLE CROSSING and PIGEON EMPIRE.

NORI: These are all the same three minutes of footage.

VELA: The edit can create a sweeping story.

DUMA: The edit can show the courtyard. People can decide what they see.

Vela cuts between two shots of the same pigeon walking around the bench. The jump makes it appear to vanish and reappear.

VELA: It escaped the frame.

NORI: I moved the camera. The bird kept walking.

Duma steps into the frame carrying a broom. The pigeon ignores him and keeps pecking.

DUMA: The birds are used to us sweeping here. They move when I get near, then return.

Vela removes the dramatic music cue from the edit.

VELA: Let us keep the room tone and the real footstep.

NORI: The broom scrape is louder than the pigeons.

DUMA: I can finish sweeping, then we can film without the broom.

SCENE 3 - EXT. COURTYARD - CONTINUOUS

Duma places a small towel on the bench and waits. The pigeon hops onto it. Duma gently frees the thread, then steps back. The bird flutters to the ground and resumes walking.

VELA (V.O.): The creature survives the trial and returns to its kingdom.

DUMA: It was a loose thread. The pigeon is fine.

NORI: The camera captured the whole thing.

Vela lowers the microphone. She watches a second pigeon drag a twig toward a planter.

VELA (V.O.): A rival steals building material from the nest.

DUMA: The twig blew over from that planter. The bird is carrying it back.

NORI: I can show the twig coming from the planter.

The camera pulls back. The twig trail is visible across the courtyard.

SCENE 4 - EXT. PLANTER - MIDDAY

The crew follows the pigeons to the planter. One bird drops the twig into a nest. The second bird pecks at a leaf and leaves. Vela searches her notes.

VELA: The narration says the pair is building a fortress together.

DUMA: It is a nest. It is made from small things they find.

NORI: The wide shot will show the location and how they move.

Vela crosses out the word FORTRESS. She records a new line: "The birds carry twigs into a sheltered planter." Duma places a sign nearby asking residents not to disturb the nest.

SCENE 5 - EXT. COURTYARD - AFTERNOON

Nori reviews the footage. The first pigeon pecks at a crumb. The second pigeon carries a twig. The courtyard remains quiet. Vela speaks into the microphone without lowering her voice to a whisper.

VELA: Two pigeons share a courtyard. One found a crumb, one carried a twig, and both moved around a puddle.

DUMA: That is what happened.

NORI: It is still a good film.

Vela watches the pigeons fly to the roof. She starts to add a line about their secret migration, then stops.

VELA: They are going to the roof.

DUMA: Yes. The building is in their territory.

NORI: Hold the shot until they leave the frame.

FINAL IMAGE: The pigeons disappear over the roofline while a single crumb remains in the courtyard, just outside the camera's frame.
