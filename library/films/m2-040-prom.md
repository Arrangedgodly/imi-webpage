# Prom Night at the Gymnasium

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: teen prom films, makeover montages, and revenge-plot misunderstandings*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A student council treasurer mistakes a prankster's playlist joke for a revenge plot, setting off a prom-night panic that turns the makeover, fog machine, and photo booth into unplanned evidence.

Cast: May, a macaque student council treasurer; Pip, a capuchin prankster; Ro, a lemur DJ; Dean, a mandrill vice principal; and Lacey, a squirrel who runs the photo booth.

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL GYM, AFTERNOON

May hangs paper stars while Pip sneaks to the sound booth. Ro tests the speakers. A goat bleat blasts through the gym. Dean drops his clipboard.

RO: Is that the first song?

PIP: It's a surprise.

MAY: It sounds like a goat.

PIP: A sophisticated goat.

May sees Pip's phone screen: REVENGE AT PROM. He grabs the phone before she can ask. She tells Lacey. Lacey texts the student council group, accidentally forwarding the message to the whole school. Students begin arriving in sunglasses and dramatic capes.

DEAN: Why are they dressed like suspects?

MAY: They think there's a revenge plan.

PIP: There isn't.

RO: Then why does your playlist say FINAL PAYBACK?

Pip says it is a dance track title. Dean confiscates the phone until the evening.

SCENE 2. INT. COSTUME ROOM, LATER

A makeover montage starts. Lacey drapes May in a cape; Ro sprays glitter; Pip's apology is drowned out by a hair dryer. The cape zipper catches a paper star and traps May's arms at her sides.

MAY: Is this the look?

LACEY: It's the silhouette.

MAY: I can't sit down.

LACEY: It's a standing silhouette.

Pip enters holding a bottle labeled FOG FLUID. May assumes it is part of the plan. Pip explains the bottle is for the rented machine. Dean takes it anyway and orders Ro to test the machine outside. Ro plugs it in; fog fills the parking lot and makes the visiting geese look like a mysterious crowd.

SCENE 3. INT. SCHOOL GYM, NIGHT

The prom begins. Ro plays a normal song. Pip stands by the controls. Dean returns his phone, after removing the playlist with REVENGE in its title. Pip tries to explain the joke to May, but the cape zipper catches again, this time on the photo booth curtain.

The fog machine starts. A dense cloud covers the dance floor. Students scream, then shuffle toward the exit. May reaches the switch but cannot get to it through the cape. Pip runs around the floor and turns it off. Ro switches on the fan. The fog rolls into the foyer.

DEAN: Everyone stay calm!

LACEY: We can't see you.

DEAN: That is a good sign.

SCENE 4. INT. GYM FOYER, CONTINUOUS

Dean directs traffic with his clipboard, but glitter obscures the arrows. Lacey gives him a photo booth sign reading THIS WAY. He points toward the gym. May wriggles free of the cape by pulling the zipper tab through the paper star. She turns Dean around and opens the exit doors to vent the fog.

MAY: Pip, what did REVENGE AT PROM mean?

PIP: The goat sound after my dance-loss song. It was supposed to be a joke. It wasn't funny.

MAY: You could have said that before the smoke.

PIP: I tried. The hair dryer was loud.

May gives him the cape to hold while she clears the power cord. It drapes over him like a villain cloak. Students re-enter and see Pip holding the fog machine, wearing the cape, beside a sign that says THIS WAY. Lacey takes a photo. Everyone laughs, including Pip.

SCENE 5. INT. SCHOOL GYM, LATER

Lacey hands May the photo print. May asks if she can crop out the paper star. Lacey says the star is the best part because it looks like it planned the evening. Pip offers to add it to the yearbook. Dean says the yearbook has a strict no-goat policy.

Ro chooses a song with no animal sounds. May and Pip fold the cape and untangle the paper stars. Dean asks Lacey to delete the photo; she says the camera printed it already. May pins it beside the dance schedule. The caption reads: THE ONLY REVENGE WAS THE CAPE ZIPPER.

Pip helps clean up. He asks May if she will dance. She says after the last chair is stacked. He stacks all the chairs in one leaning tower. It falls into a neat row, thanks to a safety mat. Dean starts to object, then decides the room is clean.

Ro returns the phone and makes Dean promise to keep it off the speaker during cleanup.

FINAL IMAGE: The gym lights come on. A goat bleat plays from Dean's phone, still connected to the speaker. Everyone turns. Dean shuts it off and raises both hands, surrendering to the dance floor.
