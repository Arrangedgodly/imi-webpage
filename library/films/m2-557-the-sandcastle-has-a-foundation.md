# The Sandcastle Has a Foundation

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: beach epics that treat an enormous sculpture like a fortress under siege*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
BUILDER MARA, a macaque
ARCHITECT VEX, an orangutan
WATER RUNNER PIP, a capuchin
JUDGE NARI, a lemur
TIDEKEEPER ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. BEACH - MORNING

A half-built sandcastle stands between two flags. Vex places a shell on its tallest tower.

VEX: The fortress is ready for the tide.

MARA: The tide arrives in three hours. We still need to reinforce the base.

PIP: The sand is too dry. I can bring a bucket from the wet line.

NARI: The contest rules allow natural sand and water. No boards, no glue.

ODO: Tide chart says the water reaches this marker at noon.

VEX: Then we build higher.

MARA: If we build higher without widening the base, the tower falls.

SCENE 2. EXT. BEACH - CONTINUOUS

Pip pours water along the base. Mara packs wet sand with a flat shell. Vex stacks a narrow tower.

MARA: Widen that bottom layer.

VEX: It ruins the silhouette.

PIP: The silhouette won't matter if the tower tips.

A wave rolls up the beach and stops short of the flag. Vex runs to the wall and blocks the water with his arms.

VEX: The sea attacks!

ODO: That was the ordinary tide at the lower marker.

NARI: The castle is outside the marked contest zone. The wave didn't touch it.

SCENE 2A. EXT. BEACH - MORNING

Mara draws a wide circle around the castle with a shell. Nari checks the contest tape along the high-water line.

NARI: Your castle is inside the permitted area. The wall is not blocking the public path.

ODO: Tide will reach the lower marker in three hours. The forecast has no storm warning.

VEX: A fortress needs a moat.

MARA: A moat is fine if it drains. Don't dig into the wet line where water is moving underneath.

PIP: I can bring two buckets, one at a time. Too much water will slump the wall.

Vex points to the left tower. It is taller than the right tower and tilts toward the sea.

MARA: Lower that tower by one layer and widen the base. The sand has dried out.

VEX: It is the tallest part of the design.

PIP: It is also the first part likely to fall. I can press a damp ring around the foundation.

Vex studies the tilt and helps remove the top shell. Mara shapes a buttress at the base with wet sand. The tower settles upright.

ODO: The wall is now stable under a light tap. Don't strike it to test it again.

NARI: The judge will inspect the structure at eleven-thirty. You have time to finish the lower walls.

VEX: I wanted to raise a flag before the inspection.

MARA: Plant it after the tower holds, not before.

Pip fills a small channel. The water runs around the base and drains away without cutting through the walls.

PIP: The moat drains toward the flat sand, away from the path.

NARI: That's within the rules. Leave the channel open so the tide can pass through.

Vex sets the shell crown on the lower tower. It remains in place.

SCENE 2B. EXT. BEACH - LATE MORNING

The judge arrives with a clipboard. Vex begins to explain the fortress, but the judge looks first at the boundary flags.

NARI: Structure is within the marked area. Walkway remains clear. I can inspect the walls now.

MARA: The tide channel is open and drains away from the public path.

ODO: Water is at the lower mark. It has not reached the castle.

VEX: We are ready for the siege.

NARI: This is a sandcastle contest. The score is for design and stability, not battle history.

PIP: The base held when the top leaned, and we reset the crown without pushing the wall.

NARI: That's good construction. Leave the shell on the tower; don't add another one.

SCENE 3. EXT. BEACH - LATER

The tower leans. Mara braces it while Pip adds wet sand at the base. Vex reaches for the shell crown.

MARA: Don't pull the top. Let the base settle.

VEX: The crown is slipping.

PIP: I can move it to a lower ledge.

Vex hands over the shell. The tower stops leaning.

SCENE 4. EXT. BEACH - NOON

The tide reaches the lower flag and pools around the castle's wide base. The towers remain upright. Nari walks around the structure without crossing the marked line.

NARI: The base held. The castle is stable.

VEX: The fortress has survived the siege.

ODO: It has survived a normal tide because you widened the base.

MARA: We can let the water recede before dismantling it. The beach should be clear when we leave.

TAG: A small wave carries the shell crown two inches. Vex salutes it as it settles into a moat.
