# The Parcel in Orbit

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space mission adventure films, countdown sequences, tense airlock rituals, and a birthday parcel treated as a cosmic object*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
CAPTAIN OBI, a macaque shuttle pilot
TESSA, a capuchin orbital courier
BOLT, a gibbon station mechanic
MIM, a ring-tailed lemur botanist

SCENE 1. INT. SHUTTLE CABIN - ORBIT

A countdown flashes across the cabin screen. Captain Obi grips the controls. Tessa holds a small wrapped parcel with both hands.

OBI: Cargo status?

TESSA: Birthday cake, candles, one card, no crumbs.

OBI: We are approaching the station.

The shuttle bumps. The candles float to the ceiling. Tessa catches them. The parcel remains sealed, but the ribbon drifts free and loops around Obi's headset.

SCENE 2. INT. ORBITAL STATION - AIRLOCK

Bolt waits in a pressure suit. He has prepared a serious welcome speech, but the airlock door opens before he finishes the first sentence. The shuttle crew floats in with the cake.

BOLT: Welcome to the orbital station, where every movement is deliberate.

MIM: The cake is drifting toward the vent.

Tessa catches it with her tail. Mim, a lemur botanist, secures the candles in a seed jar. The station alarm sounds. A red light flashes.

BOLT: That is the cargo warning.

OBI: Is the cake cargo?

BOLT: It is the most important cargo.

SCENE 3. INT. GREENHOUSE RING - LATER

The cake has lodged on the greenhouse ring. Mim's plants sway in the airflow. She uses a long vine to pull the parcel toward the center, but a seed pod bursts and scatters seeds across the station.

MIM: The garden is not designed for frosting.

TESSA: Neither is the airlock.

Bolt reaches for the parcel. His magnetic boots lock onto the floor; he cannot bend down. Obi floats past upside down and uses a clipboard to nudge the cake away from the vent. The crew secures the parcel inside a mesh cargo net.

SCENE 4. INT. STATION CONTROL - NIGHT

A station navigation alarm beeps. The net is caught on a loose cable. The cake begins to turn slowly, and the candles spin around it like tiny satellites. Tessa follows the cable to a panel, while Obi stabilizes the shuttle.

OBI: We have six minutes before the delivery window closes.

BOLT: The birthday is at midnight. The window is a metaphor.

MIM: The cake is now orbiting the greenhouse.

Tessa unclips the cable. The parcel drifts free, but the ribbon wraps around the control lever. Bolt holds the lever in place while Obi cuts the ribbon with a safety tool.

SCENE 5. INT. STATION MESS - MIDNIGHT

The crew brings the cake to the mess table. The candles are planted in the seed jar, and Mim has collected the scattered seeds. Bolt starts his welcome speech again, but the cake slides across the table when the station tilts.

BOLT: Before I continue, someone hold the frosting.

Tessa uses the parcel's ribbon as a tie-down. Captain Obi sets the navigation to steady mode. The crew sings a birthday song without gravity, each note floating for a different length.

MIM: Make a wish.

BOLT: I wish the station had a cake strap.

SCENE 6. INT. STATION WINDOW - LATER

The cake is cut and shared. A crumb floats past the window and drifts toward the stars. Tessa catches it in a spoon. Obi records the delivery as successful; Bolt writes "cake strap" on the equipment list.

SCENE 7. INT. STATION GREENHOUSE - NEXT MORNING

Mim checks the marigold while Bolt installs a strap on the mess table. The strap is designed to hold a cake, but it also catches a floating watering can. Tessa tests the parcel net with a box of seeds. It stays in place.

BOLT: The birthday cake has improved station safety.

OBI: The cake has improved our equipment list.

Tessa notices one candle still floating near the window. She catches it with a seed jar. The crew places it in the soil beside the marigold, upright but unlit. Mim labels the jar "spare light."

SCENE 8. INT. SHUTTLE CABIN - LATER

Obi prepares to leave for the surface. The station crew waves through the window. Bolt holds up the new cake strap; Tessa holds up the empty parcel wrapper. The shuttle's countdown begins, and this time every object is secured. The candles stay in the seed jar, the ribbon stays tied, and the cake strap holds through the shuttle's first turn. Bolt waves until he bumps gently into the greenhouse ring. He catches a floating napkin and tucks it under his boot.

FINAL IMAGE: The crew presses the last candle into a pot of soil as the station circles Earth and Mim's first orbiting marigold opens toward the window.
