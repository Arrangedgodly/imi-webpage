# The Court of Last Resort

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: legal dramas with a dramatic trial and expert witnesses applied to a shared footpath dispute*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-006*

Logline: A village court hears whether a private fence may block the path to a shared water bowl. The lawyers prepare grand arguments, but the only witness is a monkey who uses both sides.

Cast: Judge Pavi, a gibbon; claimant Rena, a macaque; respondent Varo, a baboon; clerk Seli, a capuchin; and witness Niko, a young monkey.

SCENE 1. INT. COURTROOM, MORNING

A rope divides the courtroom. Rena stands on one side, Varo on the other.

PAVI: We are here to decide whether the fence belongs across the path.

VARO: The fence marks my garden.

RENA: The path is shared.

Seli calls the witness. Niko walks through the rope and sits beside the judge.

PAVI: The witness has taken the stand.

VARO: It has not been sworn in.

Niko picks up a fig.

SCENE 2. EXT. PATH, DAY

The court visits the disputed route. Varo demonstrates the fence.

VARO: It prevents animals from entering the garden.

Rena opens the gate. Niko walks through, picks a fallen fruit, and exits.

RENA: The gate is already there.

VARO: The gate was locked.

Pavi checks the latch. It is open.

PAVI: Who locked it?

Varo holds up a key.

VARO: I did.

PAVI: Then the gate was not accessible.

SCENE 3. INT. COURTROOM, AFTERNOON

The attorneys present maps. One shows the path ending at the fence. The other shows a line continuing to the water bowl.

Seli places both maps side by side.

SELI: The path crosses the property line.

VARO: The line is the fence.

RENA: The path is older than the fence.

PAVI: We need evidence.

Niko enters carrying a bowl of water. It walks through the center of the courtroom and sets it on the judge's bench.

SCENE 4. INT. COURTROOM, CONTINUOUS

Everyone looks at the bowl.

PAVI: Is that evidence?

Seli checks the bowl.

SELI: It belongs to the shared water station.

VARO: The monkey has moved it.

RENA: The monkey has carried it along the path.

Pavi asks Niko to repeat the route. Niko walks through the rope divider and exits through the courtroom's open door.

PAVI: The witness has demonstrated access.

VARO: The route is not legally marked.

PAVI: It is marked by use.

SCENE 4A. INT. COURTROOM, LATE AFTERNOON

The parties return to the courtroom. Varo presents a diagram of the fence.

VARO: I request a permanent boundary.

RENA: The gate can stay. It only needs to open.

PAVI: How often do residents use the path?

Seli turns to the map. Niko walks from the witness bench to the water bowl, then back.

SELI: The witness has made the route twice.

VARO: The witness does not live here.

Niko climbs onto Varo's chair and grooms his sleeve.

PAVI: It appears to know you.

Varo gently moves Niko to the floor.

VARO: It visits the garden.

RENA: The garden and path meet at the gate.

Pavi asks each party to demonstrate how they use the space. Rena walks to the bowl. Varo walks to his seedlings. Their paths cross at the narrow fence opening.

PAVI: The dispute is the opening, not the ownership.

Varo looks at the diagram. He widens the drawn gate.

VARO: The path can remain. I will fence the seedlings only.

PAVI: That is a workable agreement.

Niko picks up the bowl and carries it toward the exit.

SELI: The witness has resumed the route.

SCENE 4B. EXT. GARDEN, CONTINUOUS

Pavi inspects the seedlings along the fence. Varo shows where the gate will move.

VARO: The new post will protect the young plants.

RENA: And leave room for the bowl.

PAVI: We will mark the path before moving the fence.

Seli stretches a measuring cord from the courtroom door to the water station. Niko walks underneath it, then stops at a low branch.

SELI: The path needs clearance here too.

Varo takes the measuring cord and marks the ground with small stones. Pavi checks the route from the other end.

PAVI: The gate will open toward the garden.

VARO: Then it will not block the path.

Rena picks up the loose rope divider and uses it to mark the seedlings temporarily.

SCENE 5. EXT. PATH, SUNSET

The judge orders the gate moved two steps back and the path cleared. Varo protests that the garden has lost space.

VARO: I will need a new boundary.

RENA: Put a small fence around the seedlings.

PAVI: The path remains open.

Niko carries the bowl through the new opening. Varo moves a post to widen it.

VARO: The witness has returned.

SELI: The court will note the adjustment.

Pavi signs the ruling. The rope divider from the courtroom is brought outside and used to mark the seedling bed.

PAVI: The court has moved its boundary.

Niko passes through the gate again, carrying a fig.

VARO: The witness is still taking fruit.

RENA: It is fallen fruit.

PAVI: The court permits it.

The courtroom crew packs up. The path stays open after they leave.
