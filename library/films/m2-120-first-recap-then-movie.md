# First the Recap, Then the Movie

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: opening recaps that repeat scenes viewers have not seen yet and explain clues before they happen*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A mystery film opens with a recap of its own first act, spoiling every clue before the detective finds it.

Cast: Vira, a lemur editor; Brum, a gorilla detective; Otti, a macaque director; Puck, a capuchin narrator; and a marmoset witness.

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING ROOM, MORNING

Vira plays the opening cut. Puck narrates: "Previously, the detective found a muddy footprint, a cracked cup, and the missing key." Brum watches the clip, then turns to the script.

BRUM: I have not found any of those things yet.

OTTI: The recap creates momentum.

VIRA: It creates spoilers.

Puck says the audience needs a reminder. Otti says this is the first scene. Puck promises to keep the recap short. It continues for four minutes. He summarizes a clue, explains why it matters, then describes Brum looking confused before Brum has entered the room.

BRUM: I am still outside the door.

PUCK: The audience likes to know where you are.

VIRA: Then let him walk in.

Brum opens the door. Puck narrates the opening of the door as a callback to the closed door. Brum holds the door open until the narration catches up, then lets it swing shut on Puck's note cards.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING ROOM, LATER

Vira removes the recap and plays the actual first scene. Brum enters a room, sees a muddy footprint, and asks where it came from. Otti stops the playback.

OTTI: The audience has already seen the footprint.

VIRA: No, they have seen a title card.

Puck inserts a second recap after the footprint, explaining that Brum has discovered evidence. Brum looks at his shoes; the mud matches the print. The clue has become a confession.

BRUM: I have not walked outside.

Otti asks whether they can add a flashback. Vira says the film has no earlier scene to flash back to. Puck says they can use a still image. Brum points out that the image is the same footprint. Puck suggests a slow zoom. Vira asks whether the zoom will reveal anything new. Puck says it will reveal the footprint more slowly. Brum slides the evidence board in front of the monitor. Otti asks for a second angle. Puck rewinds the picture and records a new voice-over: "Previously, the footprint was seen from this side." Vira stops the playback before he reaches the end of the sentence.

VIRA: It is still the same footprint.

PUCK: But now it is familiar from two directions.

BRUM: It belongs to my left boot. My right boot is clean.

The crew checks both boots. The muddy one has a split sole that matches the print. Brum adds it to the evidence board under "Detective's shoes," not "mystery suspect." 

SCENE 3. INT. MYSTERY SET, AFTERNOON

The crew films the cracked cup. Brum examines it, then finds the key beneath the table. Puck starts to narrate the discovery before the camera records it. Vira holds up a hand.

VIRA: Let him find it first.

Puck waits. Brum spots the key, lifts it with a handkerchief, and asks who last used the cup. Otti wants a recap after every clue. Vira suggests a simple scene order: footprint, cup, key, interview. Brum asks the witness about the cup. The witness says it was cracked before the key went missing.

Puck begins an explanation. Vira points to the microphone and lets the witness speak. The answer changes the suspect list. Puck reaches for the microphone to summarize the witness's answer. Vira places the microphone in front of the witness again.

WITNESS: I said the cup was cracked before the key went missing.

BRUM: And who touched the key?

WITNESS: The caretaker. He uses it to open the hall each morning.

Brum writes the caretaker's name on the board. Puck starts to say "Previously." Brum points at the fresh ink. Puck turns the cue card over and uses the blank side to cover his mouth.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The crew watches the cut in order. Puck tries to add a recap before the ending. Vira asks what it would summarize. Puck says the clues. Brum says the audience just saw them.

Otti asks whether viewers will remember the footprint. Vira pauses the film on the evidence board. Each clue remains visible. Brum takes off his muddy left boot and sets it beside the board. Vira replaces the caption UNKNOWN SUSPECT with DETECTIVE'S SHOE. Puck asks whether the new caption needs a recap.

Puck records one short line for the opening: "A key is missing." Vira places it before the first scene, not after it. Otti approves the cut.

FINAL IMAGE: The film begins with the empty key hook. The end card lists the clues in the credits, where Puck finally reads them in order.
