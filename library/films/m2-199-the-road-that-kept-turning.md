# The Road That Kept Turning

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: family road-trip comedies, scenic detours, car trouble, and relatives forced to share the front seat*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-007*

CAST
TESSA, a capuchin mother
JUNO, a gibbon uncle
PIP, a macaque child
MARA, a ring-tailed lemur mechanic

SCENE 1. EXT. KETTLE CITY - MORNING

The family climbs into a small blue car for a trip to the coast. Juno brings three maps, Tessa packs sandwiches, and Pip carries a toy compass that spins whenever the car turns.

TESSA: The route takes four hours.

JUNO: Unless we take the scenic road.

TESSA: That adds two hours.

PIP: I want to see the sea.

JUNO: We will see every hill on the way.

SCENE 2. EXT. COUNTRY ROAD - DAY

The car passes a sign for "Scenic Overlook." Juno insists they stop. The overlook is behind a gift shop with a model mountain blocking the view. Pip climbs the model to see the real mountain. Tessa buys water. Juno buys a postcard of the coast.

They return to the car. The toy compass points toward the gift shop. The engine coughs.

SCENE 3. EXT. ROADSIDE - AFTERNOON

The car pulls over beside a field. Mara, a traveling mechanic, checks the engine. The family argues about whether to keep driving or wait for a tow.

MARA: The radiator is low. The car needs to cool before we add water.

JUNO: We have water.

TESSA: It's for drinking.

PIP: I have a bottle from the gift shop.

Mara lets the engine cool. Pip and Juno walk to a nearby stream to refill the bottle. They return with water and a frog in the bottle. The frog hops onto Juno's map.

SCENE 3A. EXT. ROADSIDE PICNIC AREA - AFTERNOON

The family stops at a picnic table. The sandwiches have warmed in the sun. Tessa wants to follow the original highway. Juno argues that every detour has improved the trip. Pip pulls out the map and finds three pencil marks in the margin.

PIP: These are the places we already stopped.

JUNO: We have made our own route.

TESSA: We have gone in a circle twice.

Juno turns the map upside down and claims the circle is an intentional loop. Tessa points out that the road goes north and the coast is south. They laugh, then plan a single route using the road signs instead of Juno's souvenir map.

Mara checks the radiator before they leave. It is stable. She shows Pip how to read the temperature gauge. Pip asks whether the toy compass can help. Mara places it beside the radio; the needle spins again. They put the toy away and mark the route on the paper map.

SCENE 4. INT. BLUE CAR - LATE AFTERNOON

The family is back on the road. The radio reports a bridge closure. Juno offers another route that doubles back through the overlook. Tessa refuses. Pip checks the toy compass, which now points toward the coast because the magnet in the radio is affecting it.

PIP: The compass is wrong.

MARA: It's a toy. The radio's magnet can pull the needle.

The family follows Mara's paper map instead. They drive through a small town, stop for peaches, and take turns choosing music. The route is longer but avoids the bridge.

SCENE 4A. EXT. BRIDGE DETOUR - LATE AFTERNOON

The bridge is closed for a moving flock of geese. The family parks at a safe distance. Juno wants to honk the car horn to hurry them along. Tessa reminds him that the bridge belongs to the geese until they finish crossing.

PIP: There are more geese than cars.

MARA: They have a shorter route than we do.

The family waits. The geese cross in a slow, uneven line. One stops to drink from a puddle. Juno takes out his postcard and tries to match the geese to the painted scenery. The last goose turns back and walks toward the first, then both turn around again.

TESSA: They are lost.

MARA: They are deciding.

The flock finally reaches the field. The family crosses the bridge at a walking pace. Pip counts the geese, then loses count when one loops back to the water.

SCENE 5. EXT. COAST - SUNSET

The family reaches the sea. The tide is low. Pip runs toward the water, then stops to let a crab cross the path. Juno takes out his postcard and compares it to the horizon. The postcard shows a sun setting over a mountain.

JUNO: I thought the coast would look like this.

TESSA: That's the other side of the postcard.

Mara checks the car. The radiator is stable. The family shares sandwiches on the beach, now slightly squashed.

PIP: We saw hills, a frog, and the sea.

JUNO: And we took the scenic road.

TESSA: We took every road but the original one.

FINAL IMAGE: The toy compass spins on the dashboard as the family parks. Pip picks up a shell and places it beside the needle, which finally settles pointing toward the water.
