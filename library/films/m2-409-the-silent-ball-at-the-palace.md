# The Silent Ball at the Palace

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal masked balls, secret identities, and romance scenes that depend on nobody speaking*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
PRINCESS SERA, a gibbon
CAPTAIN VEX, a macaque guard
PIP, a capuchin musician
NARI, a lemur attendant
THE MASKED GUEST (the night gardener), an orangutan
MASKED BALL GUESTS, a silent ensemble

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE BALLROOM - NIGHT

The palace holds a masked ball. After the first dance, no one may speak until midnight. Princess Sera wears a silver mask. Captain Vex patrols the room. Pip plays a quiet drum.

SERA: We must not talk.

NARI: You just spoke.

SERA: The rule begins after the first dance.

Nari gives her a fan. Sera uses it to signal the musicians. Vex mistakes the movement for a secret code and follows her.

SCENE 2. INT. BALLROOM - LATER

The masked guest approaches Sera and offers a dance. They circle the room without speaking. Vex watches from behind a pillar. The guest points toward the balcony. Sera nods.

Vex writes "SUSPICIOUS" on a card and holds it up. Pip writes back, "YOU ARE THE GUARD." Vex turns the card over and writes "A SECRET PLAN." Pip points at the musicians, who are still playing the dance.

The guest points toward the balcony. Sera nods. Vex signals the guards with two fingers. They answer by forming a line in front of the dessert table. Nari gestures for them to move. They move the table instead, rolling it across the ballroom.

Pip switches to a slow drumbeat. The dancers stop. Vex turns toward the music and bows to the wrong couple. The guest leads Sera toward the balcony. Vex blocks the door and silently mouths "stay."

The guest points to the moon. Vex believes it is a signal. He draws a small moon on his card and shows it to the guards. They carry out a silver serving tray, thinking he wants the dessert.

Sera fans herself. The fan catches a ribbon from the curtain and pulls it toward the balcony. The guest follows it. Vex sees the ribbon move and assumes the intruder has left a trail.

The guards raise the dessert tray as a shield. Pip stops playing. Everyone freezes in a silent tableau until the clock above the dance floor begins to strike.

SCENE 3. EXT. PALACE BALCONY - MIDNIGHT

The clock strikes twelve. The masked guest removes the mask. It is the palace's night gardener, who has been trying to tell Sera that the balcony vines are tangled around the railing.

SERA: You could have said so.

MASKED GUEST: The rule said no speaking.

SERA: You could have pointed.

The gardener points at the vines. Vex arrives with guards. They see the tangled leaves and draw their swords.

VEX: The plants are attacking.

The vines catch Vex's cape and pull him toward the railing. He tries to draw his sword, but the hilt is tangled in the cape. The guards pull the fabric from opposite sides. Sera cuts the loose vine with a small knife. The cape tears, and Vex drops the sword on the balcony.

The gardener explains that the vines grew across the railing after the last rain. The moonlight made their leaves look like reaching hands.

GARDENER: I was signaling for help.

PIP: We thought it was a dance.

SERA: It was a very slow dance.

SCENE 4. INT. BALLROOM - AFTER MIDNIGHT

The ball resumes with speech allowed. Nari asks each guest to remove their mask. Several masks are stuck to their faces with sweat. The attendants use warm cloths to loosen them.

One guest removes the wrong mask and finds another mask underneath. Nari checks the invitation list. Two guests have swapped masks by mistake. They compare shoes and coats, then realize they have been dancing with the wrong partners all night.

NARI: Everyone may introduce themselves now.

VEX: I was guarding the door.

PIP: You were guarding the dessert.

Vex points at his torn cape. Nari gives him a plain cloak from the costume rack. It has no ties, and he can walk freely in it.

VEX: We should have checked the balcony before the dance.

SERA: The silence made every gesture look suspicious.

PIP: The drum was the only one who knew the song.

The gardener returns to the plants. Vex offers to hold the ladder while the gardener ties the vines away from the railing. Vex keeps both hands on the ladder and watches the gardener finish. No one draws a sword.

SCENE 5. EXT. PALACE GARDEN - DAWN

The vines are repaired. Sera and the gardener walk through the garden. Vex follows, holding his cape clear of the railings. Nari removes the "SILENT BALL" sign from the door.

NARI: Should we keep the rule next year?

SERA: We can allow one question before midnight.

VEX: What question?

Sera points at the tangled vines. Vex looks at his cape and steps away from the railing.

FINAL IMAGE: Pip plays one soft drumbeat. The gardener turns, expecting another vine emergency, but it is only the morning bell.
