# The Inflatable Moon Loses Air

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: space epics that present a simple technical failure as a planet-sized catastrophe and hide the stage crew's repairs*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A science museum's giant inflatable moon begins to sag during opening night, while its presenters improvise an interplanetary emergency instead of acknowledging the pump.

Cast: planetarium host Olan, a capuchin; engineer Ves, a lemur; director Kima, a macaque; visitor Neri, a gibbon; floor manager Saba, an orangutan.

SCENE 1 - INT. PLANETARIUM - NIGHT

A huge inflatable moon hangs above a circular stage. Kima checks the audience seats. Olan puts on a silver cape. Ves kneels beside the air pump, which clicks unevenly.

KIMA: The moon should be full before the doors open.

VES: It is connected. The hose has a slow leak at the valve.

OLAN: Can the audience see it?

SABA: They can see a moon getting smaller.

Kima lowers the lights. The moon's lower edge sags just enough to uncover a taped seam. Olan steps beneath it and raises both arms as if holding up the heavens.

SCENE 2 - INT. PLANETARIUM STAGE - CONTINUOUS

Olan begins the presentation. He tells the audience that an unknown force is pulling the moon from its orbit. Ves crawls behind the display and tightens the valve. The pump stops. The moon deflates another inch.

NERI: Is the moon shrinking?

OLAN: It is entering a rare eclipse.

SABA: An eclipse does not make the moon smaller.

Kima cues a star field. The projector throws a black patch over the sagging side. Neri points to the exact seam as it droops below the projection.

NERI: The eclipse has a zipper.

A visitor raises a hand and asks why the moon is drifting toward the audience. Olan answers that the lunar orbit is temporarily unstable. Neri quietly points to the power cord bouncing against the pump. Kima cues an explosion sound to cover the pump's uneven click, but the recording includes a long silence before the boom. The room waits through it. The moon sags again during the pause, and Olan stretches his arms farther overhead.

OLAN: The gravitational field is intensifying.

VES: The pressure is dropping.

Saba cuts the explosion track before the boom. The visitors applaud, thinking the silence is a deliberate dramatic beat. Olan bows; the moon dips over him like a low ceiling. He ducks and continues the tour from a crouch.

SCENE 3 - INT. PLANETARIUM CONTROL BOOTH - LATER

Ves brings a patch kit into the booth. Kima refuses to pause the show because the next segment is a simulated lunar landing. Olan has the audience clap to generate "solar wind." The noise makes the inflatable moon wobble on its hanging straps.

VES: Clapping will not add air.

OLAN: It adds urgency.

Saba announces a brief equipment intermission. Kima covers it with a dramatic countdown. Ves patches the valve, but the pump's power cord is too short to reach the outlet behind the stage. Neri offers a power strip from the visitor desk. Saba checks it and routes it along the wall so nobody crosses the cable.

KIMA: The mission continues.

VES: The repair continues. The mission is a museum show.

Kima tries to persuade Ves to wait until after the landing segment. Ves holds up the patch kit and points to the sagging display. Saba moves the audience back one row, explaining that the next view includes the full moon. Olan asks the visitors to imagine themselves as mission control. One child suggests checking the pump. Kima tells Olan to move to the astronaut dialogue. Olan reads the cue card, then looks over his shoulder at the valve.

OLAN: Houston, we have a maintenance window.

SCENE 4 - INT. PLANETARIUM - MOMENTS LATER

The moon begins to inflate again. Olan tells the audience that Earth has restored its satellite. Kima restarts the landing projection. A tiny astronaut appears on the screen and waves. The inflatable moon is still tilted; one strap has twisted.

Neri asks if the astronauts can fix the moon. Olan says they have deployed a tether. Ves reaches up with a telescoping pole and frees the strap. The moon swings gently into place. It overshoots and rotates, showing the unpainted white side to the room.

OLAN: We are seeing the far side.

SABA: That side is blank fabric.

Kima tries to cue the star field again, but the projection catches the moon's surface and makes a white circle drift across the ceiling. Visitors follow it with their heads. Olan turns his silver cape into a pointing flag and guides their attention back to the display.

SCENE 5 - INT. PLANETARIUM LOBBY - NIGHT

The show ends with the moon fully inflated and the audience filing out. Kima removes the cape from the stage. Ves labels the repaired valve and sets the pump for the next show. Olan signs the guest book under the heading "Lunar Mission Crew."

NERI: Can the moon stay full for tomorrow?

VES: If the pump stays plugged in.

Olan salutes the inflatable moon through the lobby window. Saba unplugs the power strip after the last guest leaves. The moon slowly sags by a fraction, and Kima reaches for the lighting controls before anyone sees.

FINAL IMAGE: The moon hangs round in the dark planetarium while its bright reflection appears in the lobby window, with Ves's valve tag dangling like a tiny flag.
