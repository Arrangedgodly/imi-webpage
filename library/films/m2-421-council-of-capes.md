# The Council of Capes

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: superhero origin stories that treat a minor inconvenience as a destiny-defining catastrophe*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: When a delivery cart rolls down a hill, a monkey inventor tries to launch a heroic identity before anyone can simply catch the parcels.

Cast: Kiko, an inventive macaque; Tavi, a careful capuchin courier; Nori, a gibbon neighbor; Mayor Odo, an orangutan; the Cape Vendor, a lemur; a child at the festival.

SCENE 1 - EXT. MARKET SQUARE - MORNING

A cart of wrapped parcels rolls down the sloped square. Tavi runs behind it, one hand on the handle and the other keeping a paper parcel from sliding.

TAVI: Kiko, the brake pin popped out. Catch the back wheel.

KIKO: This is how the legend begins.

TAVI: This is how the jars break. Catch it.

Kiko leaps toward the wheel, lands on the cart's rear board and rides it. The cart slows against a straw bale. One parcel tips onto his head like a helmet.

NORI: You stopped it.

KIKO: The city has witnessed my awakening.

TAVI: The city witnessed you ride a cart into hay.

Kiko pulls a sketchbook from his satchel. He draws a symbol: a wheel with a lightning bolt through it.

KIKO: I need a name, a costume and a threat worthy of my powers.

TAVI: We need the pin returned to the axle.

SCENE 2 - INT. KIKO'S WORKSHOP - DAY

Kiko pins a blanket to his shoulders. He has arranged three lamps and a fan before a wall chart titled THE HERO'S JOURNEY.

KIKO: First, a public failure. Second, a secret workshop. Third, a costume reveal.

TAVI: You are standing in your workshop with a blanket.

KIKO: The audience needs to know I am ordinary.

TAVI: Your goggles are on your forehead.

Kiko lowers them and steps onto the stool to free the blanket from the fan. The fabric billows around his shoulders while the fan runs.

KIKO: The cape has chosen me.

NORI: The fan has chosen the blanket. Turn it off.

The blanket winds around the fan guard. Tavi unplugs it. Kiko writes down the incident.

KIKO: The costume is unstable. That is good character conflict.

TAVI: It is a snag hazard.

KIKO: A hero must accept risk.

TAVI: A hero can use a clip.

Kiko takes a metal clip and fastens the blanket to his collar. He turns to the wall chart and sees the cart sketch.

KIKO: My origin object. The runaway cart.

TAVI: It was a loose brake pin. We can replace it.

KIKO: I refuse to shrink the story to hardware.

SCENE 3 - EXT. MARKET SQUARE - LATER

Kiko stands on a crate in the blanket cape. Nori holds a phone to record. Tavi kneels by the cart, fitting a new pin.

KIKO: Citizens! When the next cart escapes, I will be ready.

TAVI: There should not be a next cart. The pin is fixed.

KIKO: You cannot fix fate.

The market bell rings. A second cart rolls from a fruit stall. Its handle is caught on a long string of paper lanterns. Kiko leaps down. The clip holds the cape to his collar but the blanket catches on a crate corner. He spins in place as the cart continues rolling.

NORI: Kiko, unclip the cape.

KIKO: The reveal is in progress!

TAVI: The cart is heading toward the fountain.

Kiko pulls the clip. The blanket falls free. He grabs the handle, braces his feet and stops the cart before the fountain. A lantern string drops over his head.

KIKO: The people are safe.

TAVI: The peaches are safe. The people were never in danger.

NORI: The lanterns are on your shoulders.

Kiko tries to stand heroically. The paper lanterns sag like a ridiculous glowing collar.

SCENE 4 - INT. MAYOR'S OFFICE - AFTERNOON

Mayor Odo meets them beside a shelf of civic medals.

ODO: I heard there is a new protector of the square.

KIKO: The Wheel Falcon.

TAVI: The wheel pin has been repaired. The cart is secure.

ODO: Excellent. Can the Wheel Falcon inspect all the carts before the festival?

KIKO: I expected a statue.

ODO: We have a checklist and a toolbox.

KIKO: That sounds less cinematic.

ODO: It also keeps the parcels upright.

Kiko studies the checklist. He notices his lightning-bolt symbol resembles the city maintenance logo.

KIKO: You already have my emblem.

ODO: That is the city's symbol for rolling equipment.

KIKO: I have been destined by municipal signage.

Tavi hands him a wrench. The cape blanket is folded on the chair.

TAVI: You can wear your goggles. Leave the blanket here.

KIKO: A masked technician?

ODO: We call them inspectors.

SCENE 5 - EXT. FESTIVAL SQUARE - SUNSET

Kiko and Tavi check cart pins while Nori records a short announcement. The Cape Vendor arrives, carrying a bright red cape.

VENDOR: I heard the hero needs a proper cape.

KIKO: I have revised my origin. I am a civic inspector.

VENDOR: The cape is washable.

KIKO: Does it come with a reflective stripe?

The vendor sews a reflective strip onto the edge while Kiko holds it. Tavi tests each cart brake. One wheel squeaks, then stops securely.

TAVI: All pins are seated. No runaway carts.

KIKO: Then the city is safe from the threat that never existed.

NORI: The threat was a missing pin.

KIKO: And the remedy was a wrench. It has been a humbling franchise launch.

He places the new cape over his shoulders, reflective stripe facing outward. The market lights catch it. A child approaches with a toy cart.

CHILD: Can you check my wheel?

Kiko kneels and tightens the toy's loose axle nut.

KIKO: Your cart is ready for ordinary adventures.

TAVI: And this time, nobody needs an origin montage.

FINAL IMAGE: Kiko's reflective cape glows beside a neat row of locked carts as the festival crowd rolls safely past.
