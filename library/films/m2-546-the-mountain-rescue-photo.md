# The Mountain Rescue Photo

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: adventure films where the rescue team keeps stopping to frame a heroic shot*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
RANGER MARA, a macaque
CLIMBER VEX, an orangutan
GUIDE PIP, a capuchin
RADIO OPERATOR ODO, a gibbon
CAMERA ASSISTANT NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. MOUNTAIN TRAIL - DAWN

A narrow trail climbs through pine. Vex sits beside a boulder with one boot wedged in a crack. Mara and Pip arrive carrying a first-aid kit and rope.

MARA: Are you injured?

VEX: My boot is trapped. I have been waiting for the rescue team to arrive in profile.

PIP: We are not moving the boot until we know whether your ankle is hurt. Can you wiggle your toes?

VEX: Yes. The boot is the problem.

NARI: I can photograph the rescue from the ridge.

MARA: Keep the camera out of the trail. We need space to work.

ODO, over radio: Weather is stable for twenty minutes. The lower path is clear.

SCENE 2. EXT. TRAIL - MOMENTS LATER

Mara checks the rock gap. Pip braces the boulder with a wedge. Vex points his chin toward the sunrise.

VEX: Could we pause here for the first image?

MARA: No. Hold still while Pip checks the boot.

PIP: The heel is stuck under a root. If you pull straight up, you could twist your ankle.

VEX: I will wait for my close-up.

MARA: Look at me. On three, relax your foot. One, two, three.

Pip loosens the boot; Mara guides Vex's foot free. Vex winces but stands.

PIP: Can you bear weight?

VEX: Yes, carefully.

MARA: Then we walk to the flat turnout and assess the ankle there. We do not pose on the edge.

SCENE 2A. EXT. TRAIL - CONTINUOUS

Mara looks at the boot sole. One lace has loosened and dragged through mud.

MARA: Before we move, we need to secure the lace. Pip, do you have a spare tie?

PIP: I have a short cord in the first-aid kit. I can keep it away from the ankle wrap.

VEX: Could the mud be a sign that the mountain is closing in?

NARI: It's wet ground. The trail is shaded.

PIP: The boot is not torn. We'll tie it loosely so it does not catch on roots.

Mara and Pip work while Vex holds the boulder rail. Nari photographs the repair from the path, then lowers the camera when Mara signals.

MARA: The camera can wait. We have finished the lace.

ODO, over radio: Lower trail is clear. The transport cart can reach the trailhead.

MARA: We'll walk to the clearing first and reassess. No one is carrying Vex unless he cannot bear weight.

VEX: I can bear weight. I can also look determined.

PIP: Look at the path. The next root is high.

Vex steps over the root, slowly. The team follows without leaving the marked trail.

SCENE 2B. EXT. TRAIL - CONTINUOUS

Vex pauses beside the trail marker. Nari asks before taking another image.

NARI: Do you want a photograph of the marker for the incident log?

VEX: Yes. Please include the direction back to the trailhead, not my face.

MARA: That's useful documentation. Pip, check the wrap once more before we descend.

PIP: It's snug, not tight. The toes are warm, and Vex can still flex the ankle.

ODO: The cart is waiting at the trailhead. The direct route is clear.

VEX: No alternate route through the scenic cliff?

MARA: No. The direct route is the scenic route today.

SCENE 3. EXT. FLAT TURNOUT - MINUTES LATER

The team reaches a safe clearing. Mara wraps Vex's ankle. Nari lowers her camera.

NARI: The light is perfect. I could make the wrap look like a flag.

MARA: It is a support wrap. Photograph it only if Vex agrees, and do not obstruct the trail.

VEX: I agree, but please stand on the downhill side.

PIP: The downhill side is a loose slope. No one stands there.

VEX: Then take the photo from here.

NARI: I can frame the mountains behind you without stepping off the path.

She raises the camera. The strap catches on a branch. Pip frees it without moving closer to the edge.

SCENE 4. EXT. TRAILHEAD - LATER

The group reaches the trailhead. Odo meets them with water and a transport cart.

ODO: Rescue complete. No helicopter needed; the trail was open.

VEX: Can we call it a dramatic evacuation?

MARA: We can call it a safe assisted walk. The report should match what happened.

NARI: I have one photo: the team beside the trail marker. Nobody is in danger in the frame.

PIP: Good. It shows where we started and where we finished.

VEX: I wanted a cliffhanger.

MARA: The cliff is still there. We just stayed on the path.

TAG: Vex studies the photo. Behind the group, the rescue cart's handle is angled exactly like his heroic pose.
