# The Director's Cut Needs a Map

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: extended cuts that add deleted scenes without checking whether the new material contradicts the finished story*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: A director adds every deleted scene back into a short adventure until the hero arrives at the same door three times.

Cast: Nera, a macaque editor; Goro, a gorilla director; Tavi, a lemur actor; and Puck, a capuchin archivist.

SCENE 1. INT. EDITING SUITE, MORNING

Nera screens the theatrical cut. Tavi's character crosses a courtyard, finds a brass key, and opens a gate. The film ends with him entering a garden. Goro asks for the extended edition. Puck wheels in a cart of deleted scenes.

GORO: The audience deserves the whole journey.

NERA: The cut is twelve minutes long.

PUCK: The footage adds another hour.

Goro selects a scene of Tavi looking at the courtyard fountain. The shot lasts forty seconds. Puck asks if the fountain has a name. Goro says the audience can sense its importance.

TAVI: It is where my character washes his hands.

NERA: That scene was cut too.

Puck brings up the handwashing scene. Tavi washes before he enters the garden, then again after the gate. Goro asks to restore both. Nera labels the new sequence "hands, key, fountain, hands, gate." Nera asks where it belongs. Goro says before the gate. Puck inserts it. Tavi now sees the fountain after finding the key, then sees it again before the gate.

SCENE 2. INT. EDITING SUITE, LATER

The next deleted scene shows Tavi returning to the courtyard to retrieve a glove. Nera pauses the footage. In the theatrical cut, he is already inside the garden.

NERA: He has crossed the gate.

GORO: This scene shows what he left behind.

NERA: He is walking back in.

Goro says the scene adds emotional depth. Puck moves it earlier, before the key discovery. Now Tavi finds the glove, drops the key, picks it up, and looks at the fountain. The theatrical cut then shows him finding the key for the first time.

Tavi watches the sequence. He asks if his character remembers finding the key. Goro says the new scene explains his hesitation. Nera says the edit gives him two keys. Puck checks the prop log; there was only one key. He searches the extra footage for a second key and finds a close-up of Tavi placing the brass key on a bench. In the next shot, it is in his hand again.

PUCK: The key disappears.

GORO: Maybe the scene is about memory.

NERA: It is about a missing insert.

Puck checks the camera slate. The close-up was filmed after the main take, when Tavi picked up the key to return it to props.

SCENE 3. INT. EDITING SUITE, AFTERNOON

Puck finds a deleted ending. Tavi reaches the garden gate, turns around, and returns to the courtyard. Goro likes the shot and asks to include it before the theatrical ending. Nera points out that the film would end with Tavi entering the garden, then leaving it, then entering again.

GORO: The audience can decide what it means.

TAVI: Can my character decide where he is going?

Goro asks Nera to place the scene after the credits. Puck adds a card reading "Earlier That Day." Nera says the scene has no time marker. Goro suggests a flashback. Tavi says the costume is different. Puck freezes the frame; the glove is on the wrong hand.

NERA: This is a different take.

Puck compares the slate numbers. The footage belongs to an alternate ending where Tavi never found the key. Goro asks to keep it as a bonus scene. Nera puts it in a separate folder. Goro asks if the folder can be included in the extended edition menu. Nera says viewers can already see the alternate take in the bonus section. Puck tries to add another menu option labeled "The Version Before the Alternate Version." Nera removes the option before exporting.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM, EVENING

The crew watches the extended cut. It includes the fountain once, the key once, and the gate once. Nera removes the glove scene and the alternate ending. Goro asks whether the audience will feel they missed something. Tavi says his character now reaches the garden.

Puck plays the ending. Tavi steps through the gate. The credits begin. Goro asks for one more scene after the credits. Nera closes the file. Tavi asks whether the audience gets to leave now. Puck checks the runtime and says the credits are still rolling. They wait through the names and then turn on the room lights.

FINAL IMAGE: The original cut and extended cut appear side by side, each with one key and one gate crossing.
