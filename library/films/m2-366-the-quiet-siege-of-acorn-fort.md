# The Quiet Siege of Acorn Fort

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: war epics, battlefield maps, heroic strategy speeches, and a siege resolved by discovering the gate is open*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-013*

CAST
CAPTAIN NARI, an orangutan
SCOUT PIP, a capuchin
ENGINEER VEX, a macaque
GATEKEEPER LUMA, a lemur
MAYOR ODO, a gibbon

SCENE 1. EXT. ACORN FORT - MORNING

A wooden fort stands in a park. Captain Nari studies a map and points to the western wall. Scout Pip reports that the fort is surrounded by children waiting to play. Engineer Vex rolls a wooden battering ram toward the gate. Gatekeeper Luma says the gate is open.

NARI: We must negotiate.

LUMA: You can walk in.

Vex sets the ram down, and it rolls back into his feet. The crew watches the children line up behind the entrance.

SCENE 2. EXT. PARK HILL - LATER

Nari lays out a strategy map with pebbles representing teams. Odo arrives as mayor and asks whether the fort can be shared. Nari says the plan requires a perimeter. Luma points out that the fort was built for children and has three entrances. Pip crawls through one and emerges at the gate.

PIP: We have breached the wall.

VEX: That is a window.

The children begin building a second tower from cushions. Nari declares it an enemy outpost. Odo asks if he can reserve it for the story hour group. Vex says the outpost may be friendly.

SCENE 2A. EXT. ACORN FORT - NOON

The mayor sets a meeting at the picnic table. Nari insists each side send a representative. Pip volunteers as scout and brings a paper cup as a flag. The children send a squirrel drawing on a napkin. Vex asks whether the napkin is a map. Luma says it is a lunch order. Odo reads it and discovers the children want the fort from one to three, then the story group needs it afterward.

NARI: They have made a schedule.

VEX: The schedule contains no tactical reserve.

LUMA: It has a snack break.

The adults compare their proposed use with the children's times. They can inspect the fort before noon and repair it after the story group. Nari adjusts her map. The strategy now shows two entrances, one window, and a place for the snack table.

SCENE 3. INT. ACORN FORT - AFTERNOON

The adults enter through the open gate to inspect the fort. The children have made a paper flag with a drawing of a squirrel. Nari asks whether it represents a surrender. Pip says it is the fort's mascot. Vex tests the battering ram against the wall; it bounces off the soft wooden panels and knocks over his map table.

The children invite the adults to join the game. Nari proposes a truce and asks each side to share the fort in turns. Luma writes a schedule on the chalkboard. Vex tries to add a battle plan, but the chalk breaks. Odo hands him a colored marker and asks him to draw the next story map instead.

SCENE 3A. EXT. PARK HILL - AFTERNOON

Vex demonstrates how the battering ram works. It is made from a cardboard tube wrapped in felt. The children ask if they can use it as a telescope. Vex looks through it and sees the open gate from the other side. Pip reports that the fort is surrounded by cushions but no one is blocking an exit. Nari calls a halt to the siege scenario.

ODO: We can make it a rescue game instead.

The children place a toy acorn inside the fort and ask the adults to retrieve it without disturbing the cushions. Luma opens the gate, Pip crawls through the window, and Vex stands aside to hold the map. The toy is recovered in less than a minute. The children award the team a paper medal for gentle entry.

SCENE 4. EXT. PARK - LATE AFTERNOON

A light rain begins. The fort's roof leaks through a small hole. Pip places a bucket beneath it. The children use the bucket as a drum and start a marching rhythm. Nari asks them to move the game inside the community room. The fort remains closed until the roof is repaired.

The group carries the cushions and flags away. Vex asks whether the siege is over. Luma says the fort was never under siege. Odo writes a repair request for the roof and posts the schedule for shared use.

SCENE 5. EXT. ACORN FORT - SUNSET

The fort is empty. Nari folds the map and puts it in the park office. Pip removes the battering ram from the path and returns it to the prop shed. The children wave from the community room. Vex asks whether the fort will be open tomorrow. Luma says it will be, if the roof is fixed.

FINAL IMAGE: The fort gate swings in the breeze, open and waiting, while the rain bucket catches the last steady drip.
