# The Historian Who Arrived Early

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: time-travel adventure films, paradox warnings, and historical spectacle tourism*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-002*

Logline: A macaque historian travels back to verify one footnote, then gets mistaken for a royal messenger and changes history by trying not to be noticed.

Cast: Dr. Pella, a macaque historian; Professor Tern, a tortoise inventor; Queen Gilda, a baboon ruler; Jinx, a capuchin assistant; and a court scribe who records everything.

SCENE 1. INT. UNIVERSITY LAB, NIGHT

The time machine hums. Pella checks her return timer and steps inside with a notebook.

TERN: Do not change anything.

PELLA: I'm checking a parade date.

JINX: What if you meet yourself?

PELLA: I won't.

The machine sparks. Pella arrives in the royal court a day early. Her notebook lands at the scribe's feet. He picks it up and asks if it is a decree. Pella says it is not. The scribe writes: decree disputed.

SCENE 2. INT. THRONE ROOM, MORNING

Queen Gilda asks Pella to read her message. Pella opens the notebook to a footnote: PARADE DELAYED BY RAIN? The question mark is faint. The scribe records a firm announcement. Gilda cancels the parade.

PELLA: That was a question.

SCRIBE: The ink does not show questions.

PELLA: It does if you read the punctuation.

The scribe copies the line into the ledger. Gilda orders the courtyard prepared for the new schedule. Pella tries to explain that the parade already happened in her own time. The scribe records: parade happened twice.

SCENE 3. EXT. PALACE COURTYARD, AFTERNOON

The guards ask Jinx to carry the umbrella at the head of the procession. He tries to explain that it belongs to the lab. The scribe follows with his ledger, recording each sentence as protocol.

JINX: It opens when you press this button.

SCRIBE: Royal canopy opens by command.

JINX: It folds the same way.

SCRIBE: Canopy submits to the crown.

Queen Gilda asks whether the umbrella has a name. Jinx says no. The scribe names it Royal Drizzle and writes the title in gold ink.

Jinx arrives through the time machine with an umbrella. The guards mistake it for a royal symbol and form a procession. The umbrella catches a banner and pulls it down. The scribe records that the banner bowed to the queen.

JINX: I brought this for the rain.

GILDA: Is rain expected?

PELLA: Not today.

The court looks up at clear skies. Gilda asks why the umbrella is present. Jinx says it is a prototype. The scribe writes: prototype canopy. Pella snatches the notebook before he can add a ceremony.

SCENE 4. INT. ARCHIVE ROOM, EVENING

Pella and the scribe search for the original parade schedule. The room is locked. Jinx tries a future key card; it does not fit. The guard lets them in when Jinx says it is a royal access device. Inside, the schedule shows the parade was moved once because the street was muddy, then moved back when the sun came out.

SCRIBE: So it wasn't delayed by rain.

PELLA: Correct.

SCRIBE: But you wrote that it was.

PELLA: I wrote that I needed to check.

The scribe finds an earlier ledger note describing a messenger who arrived with an umbrella and prompted the move. The description matches Pella. She has created the historical clue she came to verify. Tern arrives, waving the return timer. It has one minute left.

SCENE 5. INT. THRONE ROOM, NIGHT

The corrected schedule is read aloud. The court objects that the new version lacks a dramatic procession. Gilda asks whether they can still march to the courtyard. Pella says yes, as long as they use the actual route. The guards carry the banner carefully this time. The scribe adds a note that no banners were harmed.

Pella corrects the record. The scribe adds a footnote explaining the confusion, including the fact that the umbrella was ordinary and the parade moved once. Gilda orders the parade restored to its original route. Jinx holds the umbrella over the ledger to protect it from candle wax.

GILDA: Will history remember this accurately?

SCRIBE: It will remember the correction.

Tern opens the time machine. Pella and Jinx step in. The scribe asks if the machine should be included in the record. Pella says it is a prototype. He writes unconfirmed device and adds a question mark.

Pella checks the timer before stepping into the machine. Jinx checks it too, then asks if she has the right day. She shows him the dial.

FINAL IMAGE: Back in the lab, Pella updates the history book. The parade moved once. Tern asks whether that is the whole story. Pella adds a footnote: the umbrella did not cause rain.
