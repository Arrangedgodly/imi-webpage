# The Dragon's Last Audition

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fantasy blockbusters that treat a creature casting call like an invasion*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
DIRECTOR VEX, an orangutan
DRAGON PIP, a capuchin
WARDROBE MARA, a macaque
CASTING ODO, a gibbon
RUNNER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - DAY

A cardboard cave stands behind a taped line. Pip wears a dragon tail that is too long and drags across the floor.

VEX: The dragon has arrived to destroy the village.

PIP: I have arrived for an audition. The tail is catching on the tape.

MARA: Keep the tail behind the line until wardrobe shortens the strap.

ODO: The casting sheet lists one dragon, one page of dialogue, and no fire effects.

VEX: The audience expects flames.

NARI: The fire marshal does not.

SCENE 2. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - CONTINUOUS

Mara unclips the tail. Pip steps onto the mark and reads the line: "I have come to ask for directions."

VEX: A dragon should roar.

PIP: The script says ask for directions.

ODO: That is the line on the page.

VEX: Perhaps the dragon is secretly terrifying.

MARA: The costume is already large enough. Please don't add effects.

Pip turns toward the cardboard village. The tail, now unclipped, lies on the floor. Vex nearly steps on it; Nari moves it to the wardrobe rack.

SCENE 2A. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - DAY

Mara inspects the tail strap. It is fastened backward, making the tail drag. She lays it on the wardrobe table and checks the label.

MARA: This is costume seven. The strap should pass through the loop before the buckle closes.

PIP: I didn't want to tear the cardboard cave, so I stopped when it caught.

VEX: The dragon's power is too great for the set.

ODO: The cave is cardboard. It weighs less than the tail.

NARI: I'll hold the set wall while Mara tests the strap. The tape on the floor marks the safe area.

Mara threads the strap correctly and tests the buckle with two fingers. Pip stands still while she attaches it.

MARA: Tail is clear of the floor now. Walk to the mark without turning sharply.

Pip takes three steps. The tail follows without catching.

PIP: It works. I can stand at the mark and read the page.

VEX: Can the dragon face the village dramatically?

MARA: Slowly. The tail needs room, and the cardboard wall is not braced for a collision.

Pip turns. One cardboard tower leans. Odo puts a support under its base, and Nari checks the wall joint.

NARI: Set is stable again. No one touches that tower during the take.

VEX: The village has survived.

MARA: The set has been stabilized. Let's record the line and finish the audition.

SCENE 2B. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - LATER

The camera rolls. Pip steps to the mark and reads the line in a calm voice. Vex waits for a roar that never comes.

PIP: I have come to ask for directions.

VEX: Cut. The dragon sounds polite.

PIP: The line is polite.

MARA: That's the character. We can record one more take with the tail in frame.

NARI: The frame is clear, the village wall is braced, and the floor tape is visible.

Odo checks the slate, then steps out of the shot.

ODO: Take two. Costume seven. No fire cue.

Pip repeats the line, then waits. Vex gives a small nod. Mara ends the take.

VEX: The dragon has found a peaceful solution.

MARA: The dragon has asked where the road is.

SCENE 2C. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - LATER

Pip removes the dragon tail after the two recorded takes. Mara hangs it on costume hook seven. Odo closes the slate and files the take number.

ODO: Audition recorded, one usable take. Tail returned to wardrobe. Cave wall still braced.

VEX: The dragon may be called back for the invasion.

PIP: The character asked for directions. It did not invade anything.

NARI: The cardboard village is safe. The stage is clear for the next performer.

Mara steps away from the set. The cardboard turret stays braced. Pip signs the costume checkout sheet; the tail remains on hook seven.

SCENE 3. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - LATER

Odo plays back take two on the studio monitor. Pip is already out of costume; the tail remains on hook seven. The recorded line plays once.

PIP, on playback: I have come to ask for directions.

VEX: The dragon has shown restraint.

MARA: The audition is complete. No damage to the set.

SCENE 4. INT. SOUNDSTAGE - AFTERNOON

Odo marks the take as approved for review. Mara checks the floor tape and braces once more before the next performer enters. Pip walks toward the exit without the tail.

VEX: The dragon leaves the kingdom.

PIP: I leave the studio. The costume stays here.

TAG: Vex gives a low roar at the cardboard cave. The cave remains standing; Nari turns the call sheet to the next audition.
