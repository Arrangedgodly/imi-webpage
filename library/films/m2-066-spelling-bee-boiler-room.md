# The Spelling Bee in the Boiler Room

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: spelling-bee competition films, underdog montages, and suspenseful word definitions*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A quiet gibbon competes in a school spelling bee whose organizers keep raising the stakes until the words are being announced beside a loud boiler.

Cast: Sumi, a gibbon student; Coach Pruff, a macaque teacher; Dean Moss, a tortoise principal; Fizz, a capuchin rival; and Etta, a lemur pronouncer.

SCENE 1. INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM, DAY

The spelling bee begins. Fizz spells "orchard" and poses for the camera. Sumi calmly spells "hinge." The audience claps. Dean Moss says the next round will be in the basement because the auditorium has been booked for choir practice.

SUMI: Why the basement?

MOSS: It is quiet.

A boiler thumps behind the stage door.

SCENE 2. INT. BOILER ROOM, LATER

Etta pronounces words into a microphone. The boiler kicks in and drowns her out. Fizz asks for the word again. Etta repeats it, but the boiler repeats the final syllable with a clank. Coach Pruff calls the sound a distraction. Sumi asks if the contestants can move rooms. Moss says the schedule is printed.

SCENE 3. INT. BOILER ROOM, CONTINUOUS

The contest resumes. Etta asks each student to spell a word while the boiler is quiet. Fizz spells rhythm and adds a flourish. Sumi spells gasket. Moss asks why she chose that word. She says it is written on the boiler. The custodian confirms the label is correct.

COACH PRUFF: This is a spelling bee, not a maintenance quiz.

SUMI: The boiler is in the room.

Fizz says she is using outside help. Etta explains that reading the word on the machine is allowed during practice, not during the contest. The custodian covers the label with a towel.

A montage shows contestants studying flash cards under flickering lights. Fizz practices with a spotlight and mirror. Sumi writes the words on a whiteboard. The condensation from a pipe erases the list. She moves the board and continues on the dry wall with chalk.

The boiler stops. Etta announces "maintenance." Nobody knows whether it is a word or an announcement. She defines it as the work of keeping equipment in good condition. Moss looks at the boiler and calls a custodian.

SCENE 4. INT. BOILER ROOM, FINAL ROUND

The spelling bee host announces a lightning round. Etta asks for a word. Fizz buzzes before hearing it, assuming the sound is the bell. Moss awards him no points. Sumi asks the host to wait until the room is quiet. They open a door to the hallway, where a choir rehearses in perfect unison. The choir stops on cue and listens. The boiler thumps once. Etta says the word is boiler. Fizz spells it correctly, and Sumi gives him a nod.

Before the final round, Moss moves the remaining contestants into a smaller room. The room contains the boiler controls. He asks Tully which switch turns the machine off. Tully says only maintenance staff should touch it. Moss declares this an additional challenge. Sumi refuses to guess and asks for the custodian. The custodian switches it off, then hands Moss the work order.

Only Sumi and Fizz remain. Etta gives Fizz "separate." He asks for the origin, part of speech, and whether it has ever been used in a sentence. Etta answers. The boiler clanks loudly. Fizz mishears and spells "celebrate." The bell rings.

SUMI: That was the boiler.

MOSS: It was not entered in the bee.

Sumi gets the word "thermostat." She asks Etta to repeat it after the boiler quiets. Etta waits until the custodian turns the machine off, then pronounces it clearly. Sumi spells it correctly.

SCENE 5. INT. SCHOOL HALLWAY, AFTERNOON

The custodian explains that the boiler was loud because the filter needed cleaning. Moss asks if that is a clue. Tully says it is a maintenance issue. The students help carry the flash cards back upstairs. Fizz carries the dictionary; Sumi carries the corrected certificate. They race each other to the office, then remember there is no prize for arriving first.

The final word is maintenance. Sumi spells it correctly, then explains that the silent letter does not make it less important. Fizz asks for the definition again. Etta says it is the work that keeps things operating. Moss looks at the boiler room and nods.

Sumi wins. Fizz congratulates her and asks if they can move the next bee to the library. Moss says the library has a printer. Coach Pruff presents Sumi with a dictionary. She asks whether she can use it to check the spelling of the certificate. Moss has written "Speling Champion." Etta corrects it in red ink.

FINAL IMAGE: The boiler starts again. Everyone spells its sound together: C-L-A-N-K. The custodian writes it on a maintenance ticket, not the spelling board.
