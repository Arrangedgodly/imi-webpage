# The End Credits Start Too Soon

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: franchise films that tease a sequel after every resolution and roll credits before the current cast has left the set*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A studio's end-credit cue fires after the hero's first exit, forcing the editor and cast to stage a post-credit scene before the film has actually ended.

Cast: hero Rena, a macaque; director Kalo, a capuchin; editor Sumi, a lemur; villain Poro, an orangutan; extra Nilo, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - INT. STUDIO SET - NIGHT

Rena defeats Poro in a painted fortress. Kalo calls for the final shot. Sumi watches the editing monitor. Rena reaches the painted exit. The credits roll over her before she leaves the frame. Poro stands behind her, still holding a rubber sword.

KALO: Cut the credits.

SUMI: The cue is linked to the door.

Nilo opens the real studio door to leave. The credit music starts again, and the names scroll faster. Poro is listed before Rena. Kalo asks whether the villain has won the movie.

SCENE 2 - INT. EDITING ROOM - LATER

Sumi checks the sound timeline. A door chime is attached to the credit cue. Kalo asks her to remove it. She separates the sound, but the credits still appear whenever a door opens in the picture. Rena tests the set door. The music begins. Poro tests it. The music begins again, on top of the first roll.

NILO: I have been credited twice.

SUMI: You opened two doors.

Nilo has opened only one. The second sound came from the studio's automatic door closer. Sumi marks it on the audio track, but Kalo says the audience expects the villain's post-credit reveal.

SCENE 3 - INT. STUDIO SET - CONTINUOUS

Poro returns through the painted exit with a script page. He announces that the villain has a twin. He turns around, revealing a second mustache taped over the first. Rena asks the twin to wait until the names have finished. Sumi rolls the credits. The door chime triggers another roll before Poro can speak.

KALO: We now have credits inside the credits.

Sumi asks Nilo to open the door slowly. He does. The chime plays. She asks him to close it. The chime plays again. She disconnects the door sensor. The music stops. Kalo asks whether they can still use a door in the scene. Sumi says the actor can open it without the sensor sound.

SCENE 4 - INT. EDITING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Sumi builds one credit roll, then places a separate marker for the twin reveal. Kalo requests a second post-credit gag with the broom. Rena says the film has only one reveal. Poro tries on another mustache, but it is the same one turned sideways. Sumi removes the duplicate shot.

Sumi assembles the cut: Rena exits, the names roll once, then Poro appears as the twin. Poro lowers the rubber sword onto the prop mat. Nilo sets down his slate, picks up the broom from its hook and sweeps the set. The end frame holds on the broom. Kalo asks for one more joke after the last name.

SCENE 4A - INT. EDITING ROOM - LATER

At the editor's review, Sumi screens the cut for the crew. The first credit card appears over the fortress, then the names scroll at a steady pace. Kalo notices the art department credit is missing. Nilo says he painted the fortress wall. Sumi adds his name, and the roll becomes longer than the film's final shot. Poro waits for his post-credit reveal. The first card fades, the names finish, and the twin appears. Poro bows. The crew laughs once. Kalo asks for a second bow; Sumi keeps the edit moving.

The last shot holds on the broom. Kalo proposes a sequel title beneath it. Sumi writes SPECIAL THANKS instead. Nilo's broom-carrying shot continues on the review monitor. The room chime sounds when the door closes, but the credits remain still. Sumi marks the cue as disabled on the timeline.

SCENE 5 - INT. SCREENING ROOM - LATE NIGHT

At a later public screening, the audience watches the locked final cut. The credits roll once. Poro appears afterward, reveals the twin and exits through the painted doorway without a chime. Nilo picks up the broom after the credits and sweeps the prop swords into a bin. The audience laughs at the broom in the final frame. Kalo starts to request another tag, but Sumi shuts the editing console.

KALO: Is it finished?

SUMI: The film is finished.

The audience leaves through the real screening-room door. Its chime does not restart the credits. Sumi carries the master reel to storage. Nilo puts the broom away, then checks the door sensor before leaving.

FINAL IMAGE: The final credit card reads SPECIAL THANKS: THE BROOM, while the real exit chime sounds only once in the empty room.
