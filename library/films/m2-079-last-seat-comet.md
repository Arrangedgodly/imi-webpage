# The Last Seat in the Comet

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster movies, ensemble sacrifice speeches, and leadership chosen by whoever grabs the radio*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: A comet tour shuttle loses its cabin lights during an ordinary meteor shower, and a crew of self-appointed heroes must learn which emergency procedure applies.

Cast: Captain Suri, a macaque pilot; Bex, a gorilla tour host; Fen, a lemur mechanic; Lalo, a capuchin passenger; and Director Quill, a pelican from the spaceport.

SCENE 1. INT. COMET SHUTTLE, IN ORBIT

The shuttle cruises above a comet exhibition. The cabin lights flicker, then go out. Bex stands and announces that one of them must take charge. Lalo points to the illuminated EXIT arrows. Captain Suri asks everyone to stay seated while she checks the panel. Fen opens a service hatch and sees a loose connector.

BEX: I am willing to make the ultimate sacrifice.

SURI: Please sacrifice the urge to stand up.

SCENE 2. INT. SHUTTLE CONTROL BAY, CONTINUOUS

Suri reads the fault code. It says CABIN LIGHT CIRCUIT. Bex calls it a systems failure. Fen isolates the affected circuit and finds a connector loosened by vibration. The emergency lights work. Director Quill radios from the spaceport and demands a status update for the public feed.

QUILL: Are the passengers in peril?

SURI: They are in low lighting.

Bex delivers a heroic speech to the passenger camera. Each sentence is interrupted by the automatic safety announcement, which calmly repeats: KEEP SEAT BELTS FASTENED. The passengers listen to the announcement and ignore Bex.

Bex asks the passengers to identify who among them has survival skills. A passenger says she packed snacks. Another says he can read a map. Bex assigns them each a dramatic title. Suri tells him to stop moving through the cabin while the shuttle is in motion. He returns to his seat, then stands again to demonstrate how quickly he can sit. The seat-belt alarm chirps. Everyone looks at him.

Quill requests a camera angle that shows the planet in the background. The operator points out that the cabin window faces the comet and cannot be turned. Quill suggests repositioning the shuttle for the shot. Suri refuses to alter a safe route for a sponsor image. Lalo uses a small mirror to reflect the comet's light onto the cabin ceiling. The passengers can see the display without changing course.

SCENE 3. INT. PASSENGER CABIN, LATER

Lalo asks if they will miss the comet's tail. Suri explains the shuttle will continue once the cabin circuit is inspected. Bex wants to open a manual hatch for a better view. Fen shows him the vacuum warning on the hatch. Bex steps back and calls the hatch "a sealed gateway to destiny."

Quill asks Suri to restart the circuit for the livestream. Fen warns that the connector must be reseated and tested first. Quill says the sponsor wants a dramatic blackout-to-lighting reveal. Suri declines. She asks Fen to finish the repair while she keeps the shuttle on its stable route. Bex offers to hold the flashlight. He points it at his own face for the camera until Lalo turns the beam toward the panel.

Fen seats the connector, tests the emergency light, then restores the cabin circuit. The lights return. The comet fills the viewports. One passenger claps. Bex starts a countdown to the reveal, but the lights have already come on.

The automatic announcement asks passengers to remain seated until the shuttle docks. Bex starts to lead a chant, but the passengers are busy taking photographs. He asks Lalo to hold a flashlight beneath his chin for a final speech. Lalo says the battery is reserved for the inspection. Bex trades the flashlight for a window seat and sits quietly, watching the comet's tail pass.

SCENE 4. INT. SHUTTLE CONTROL BAY, MOMENTS LATER

Quill asks Suri to repeat the restart for the feed. Suri says the system is stable and the event is over. Bex requests a second blackout so the passengers can feel the relief again. Fen locks the hatch and labels the connector. Quill says a safe flight with no crisis will be hard to promote. Lalo suggests promoting the comet.

The crew records a factual update: a cabin light connector vibrated loose; emergency lights remained available; the crew repaired and tested it. Bex tries to add a line about courage. Suri lets him say, "We remained seated and followed instructions." He looks disappointed until Lalo says that was the most useful line.

SCENE 5. EXT. SPACEPORT ARRIVAL BAY, NIGHT

The shuttle lands. Quill meets them with a banner reading HEROES OF THE BLACKOUT. Suri turns it around; the back says ROUTINE MAINTENANCE TEAM. Fen signs the inspection record. Passengers leave and tell the crew they saw the comet clearly. Bex asks whether anyone was frightened. They say only when he stood up.

FINAL IMAGE: The shuttle's cabin lights glow evenly as it rolls into the hangar. Bex sits in the pilot's chair for a publicity photo, still buckled in, while the warning sign on the chair says NOT A SEAT FOR TOUR HOSTS.
