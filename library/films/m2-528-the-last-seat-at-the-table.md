# The Last Seat at the Table

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: family dramas built around a holiday meal, old resentments, a missing chair, and a final toast*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: JUNE, a baboon; PALA, an orangutan; MIKO, a macaque; TESS, a gibbon; UNCLE VENN, a capuchin; NANA RO, a mandrill.

INT. FAMILY DINING ROOM - EVENING

A long table is set for six. Five chairs surround it. June carries a folding chair through the doorway.

JUNE: The table has six places.

PALA: The sixth chair broke last year.

MIKO: It is still at the repair shop.

TESS: The repair shop closed.

Uncle Venn arrives with a covered bowl. He refuses to set it down until someone tells him where the chair is going.

VENN: My casserole needs a seat.

NANA RO: The casserole is not a guest.

Pala takes the old chair's place card from a drawer. It reads "Grandfather." Nobody wants to move it. Nana Ro explains that the chair belonged to him, and each year someone sets it at the table for a few minutes before putting it away.

JUNE: I thought we were saving it for a cousin.

MIKO: I thought the chair was just broken.

TESS: We never agreed where it should go.

Pala brings the old place card to the table. Each family member reads it, then passes it along. Venn tries to put the card under the casserole so it will not blow away. Nana Ro takes it from him and holds it beside the empty setting.

NANA RO: It belongs with the chair, not the food.

Venn lifts the lid. The steam fogs his glasses. He sits at the counter and waits for someone to point him toward the table.

Uncle Venn sets the covered bowl on the counter. He says Grandfather repaired the first table and built the chair from leftover boards. Pala says it was uncomfortable. Nana Ro says he made it too short for himself.

NANA RO: He built it for children who could not reach the table.

The family looks at the folding chair. June asks whether they can keep a place for the story without leaving an empty plate. Nana Ro turns the place card over and writes "For everyone who needs a seat." She places it beside the empty setting. Nana Ro places a plate there and sits at the head.

ACT ONE

INT. DINING ROOM - LATER

June brings the folding chair. It is too low for the table. Tess stacks two cushions on it, but the chair wobbles.

MIKO: It is a folding chair, not a throne.

PALA: It is the only chair that fits.

Uncle Venn places the casserole on the table. The plate at the empty place is still unused. Nana Ro asks who the seat is for. Everyone assumes it is meant for a cousin who has not arrived.

NANA RO: Who invited the cousin?

No one answers. June checks the family message thread. The cousin replied "I will bring a chair." A second message says "I am not coming." The two lines are separated by a photo of a pie.

TESS: The chair is coming without the cousin.

The doorbell rings. A delivery worker leaves a repaired wooden chair outside. The family carries it in. One leg is shorter than the others, so the chair rocks.

PALA: That is why the chair was sent for repair.

MIKO: They fixed the seat but not the leg.

JUNE: We can fold a napkin under it.

NANA RO: No. Someone should repair it properly.

Tess turns the chair over and finds a loose screw. She asks for a screwdriver. Uncle Venn leaves the table and returns with a small toolkit. June holds the chair while Tess tightens the screw.

VENN: I thought you never wanted my tools.

TESS: I did not want them stored in the kitchen drawer.

VENN: They are now in the hall closet.

Miko brings a level from the pantry. They test the chair. It sits flat. The family carries it to the open place.

ACT TWO

Nana Ro asks who will sit in the repaired chair. Everyone says it belongs to someone else. June sets the chair at the end of the table and gives the plate to the person who brought it: Uncle Venn.

VENN: I carried the casserole.

NANA RO: And the chair came in your delivery.

VENN: That does not make it mine.

NANA RO: It needed a person.

Venn sits. The chair holds. The family serves dinner. The casserole lid sticks, then releases with a puff of steam that fogs everyone's glasses. Nana Ro raises a toast. Miko removes the lid before it tips.

PALA: To the chair that arrived after the person did.

JUNE: And to the person who sat down.

They eat. Tess puts the tool kit back in the hall closet, and Uncle Venn watches to make sure it stays there. Nana Ro hands the folding chair to June to store.

FINAL IMAGE: The repaired chair sits square at the table while the folded chair leans in the hallway, waiting for the next guest.
