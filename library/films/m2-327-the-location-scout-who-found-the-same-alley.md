# The Location Scout Who Found the Same Alley

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: location shoots that promise exotic new settings but reuse a convenient alley and rename it between scenes*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
LOCATION SCOUT LUMA, a lemur
DIRECTOR PIP, a capuchin
ACTOR VEX, a macaque
PRODUCTION DESIGNER NARI, an orangutan
LOCAL GUIDE ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. EXT. OLD ALLEY - MORNING

Location scout Luma walks the crew into a narrow alley with a blue door, a drainpipe and a crooked lamp. Director Pip wants three distinct locations: a harbor lane, a mountain village and a city market. Actor Vex looks at the walls.

VEX: This is a harbor?

LUMA: It is near the river.

NARI: The river is two blocks away.

ODO: And the mountain is across town.

Pip studies the schedule. The company has one day to shoot all three scenes. Luma says she can dress the alley differently. Nari brings canvas, crates and a hand-painted sign. Vex asks whether the harbor can smell like salt. Odo opens a jar of seaweed, and everyone steps away from it.

SCENE 2. EXT. ALLEY - LATER

Nari covers the blue door with fish posters and labels the corner HARBOR. Vex walks in as a sailor, looks at the drainpipe and calls it a mast. Pip asks him to keep moving. At the end of the take, Vex turns the corner and returns from the opposite end of the same alley, still carrying the same fish poster.

PIP: We needed the mountain village.

LUMA: Give us ten minutes.

The crew removes the fish posters and adds a painted ridge line to the far wall. Odo carries a crate marked MOUNTAIN. Vex enters again and recognizes the blue door. Nari paints over the door with a gray wash. The paint is wet. Vex touches it, leaving a handprint that resembles a small map.

SCENE 3. EXT. ALLEY - AFTERNOON

They turn the location into the market with striped awnings. Odo stacks fruit crates. Luma positions a camera so the street sign is out of frame. Pip asks Vex to cross the market and find a spice stall. Vex crosses, turns, and arrives at the harbor sign, which has not been removed from behind a crate.

VEX: I have found the sea again.

NARI: You found the sign.

Luma moves the camera to the other side. Now the blue door appears in the background of every market shot. Nari hangs a curtain across it. A gust lifts the curtain, revealing the mountain ridge painted behind it. Odo holds the fabric closed while Vex improvises a line about a merchant hiding his secret entrance.

SCENE 4. EXT. ALLEY - SUNSET

Pip asks the crew to stop disguising the alley. He wants a scene where the characters notice they have been walking in circles. Luma blocks a single continuous shot: Vex leaves the market, passes Odo carrying the harbor crate, climbs three steps toward the painted mountain, and returns to the same blue door.

VEX: We are lost.

ODO: You have passed me three times.

VEX: That is how lost I am.

Nari removes the signs. The alley now looks like an ordinary place, with the blue door, drainpipe and crooked lamp all visible. Vex looks into the frame and says the scene finally feels like a location. Pip keeps the shot. Luma tells the crew they can use the real harbor tomorrow, but the schedule has no tomorrow. The crew laughs, and the scene continues with Vex asking Odo for directions.


Odo marks each setup on the pavement with chalk: HARBOR at the blue door, MOUNTAIN by the drainpipe, MARKET under the lamp. Vex crosses the alley and sees all three labels at once. He asks Nari to cover them. The rain starts, washing the chalk into one broad arrow pointing toward the same blue door. Luma adjusts the shot so the actors notice the arrow and follow it back to their starting point.

SCENE 5. INT. PRODUCTION VAN - NIGHT

The crew reviews the takes. The harbor scene uses fish posters; the mountain village uses the painted ridge; the market has crates and awnings. The final alley scene shows the repeated geography as the joke. Vex asks if the production can claim three countries. Nari says they can claim three looks.

ODO: We can claim one alley.

LUMA: And one day.

Pip asks for a location credit. Luma writes OLD ALLEY, EAST WALL. Vex wants something grander. They leave the credit as written. The van pulls away; in its side mirror, the blue door seems to travel with them.

FINAL IMAGE: Luma pins the alley map to the production board, and it is just a rectangle with a crooked lamp.
