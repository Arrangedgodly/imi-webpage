# The Scenic Route to the Dentist

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: road-trip buddy comedies that detour for scenic montages despite a simple destination and a closing-time deadline*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-015*

Logline: Two monkey friends drive across town to a dentist appointment, but one keeps turning the trip into an epic scenic road movie.

Cast: Daro, a macaque with a dental appointment; Peli, a gibbon driver; map voice, a radio navigation system; receptionist Tima, a capuchin.

SCENE 1 - EXT. APARTMENT CURB - MORNING

Peli opens the car door. Daro holds an appointment card marked 10:30. The dentist is four miles away. Peli unfolds a tourist map across the hood.

DARO: The appointment is at ten thirty.

PELI: The scenic route has a lookout and a covered bridge.

DARO: I need a cleaning, not a travel montage.

Peli starts the car. The radio map says, "Turn right in two hundred feet." Peli turns left.

SCENE 2 - EXT. RIVER LOOKOUT - LATER

They stop at a viewpoint. Peli films Daro looking toward the river. The map voice announces they are now six miles from the clinic.

DARO: We have driven away from the dentist.

PELI: The road has character.

DARO: The appointment has a time.

Peli snaps a photo. The wind blows the appointment card out of Daro's hand. It lands beneath the tire. Daro retrieves it and brushes off the dust.

SCENE 2A - EXT. COVERED BRIDGE - LATER

Peli parks at the bridge turnout. A wooden sign says VIEWPOINT, FIVE MINUTES. Daro stays beside the car, checking the clinic address on the card.

PELI: We have time for one photograph.

DARO: The card says ten thirty. It is ten twelve.

PELI: The bridge is an architectural landmark.

Daro reads the second sign: BRIDGE CLOSED; DO NOT ENTER. Peli turns the camera toward a nearby plaque instead.

DARO: We can photograph the sign and leave.

Peli sets a tripod. The tripod leg catches on a map sleeve and tips. Daro catches the camera before it falls.

PELI: That was an action sequence.

DARO: It was a falling camera. Put it away.

They return to the car. Peli spends another minute folding the map along the route, but the fold hides the clinic's location. Daro opens it to the city map and points to the direct road.

SCENE 3 - INT. CAR - MOVING - LATER

The map voice recalculates. Peli follows a road marked CLOSED FOR BRIDGE REPAIRS. The car stops at a barrier.

MAP VOICE: Continue on the marked detour.

PELI: The scenic route has led us to a scene.

DARO: The bridge is closed. Turn around.

Peli backs up carefully. A farmer waves from a gate and points them toward the main road. Peli thanks him and follows the directions. Daro checks the appointment card again.

SCENE 4 - EXT. DENTAL CLINIC - 10:27

They arrive. Peli pulls into the lot. Daro jumps out, then sees the clinic entrance is across the street. Peli circles the block instead of stopping.

DARO: Stop here. I can walk.

PELI: The map says parking is around back.

DARO: The sign says patient drop-off.

Peli looks at the sign, then parks. Daro enters with three minutes to spare. Peli carries the appointment card and follows.

SCENE 4A - INT. DENTAL CLINIC RECEPTION - CONTINUOUS

Daro signs in. Peli places the appointment card on the counter and notices a coffee stain across the time. The receptionist checks the clinic schedule.

TIMA: We can still see you. You arrived before the appointment.

PELI: Our trip had a bridge closure and a river overlook.

TIMA: The clinic is four miles from your house.

DARO: Next time, the only stop is the parking lot.

Tima clips the card to the check-in sheet. Peli looks at a poster that reads KEEP YOUR REGULAR CLEANING.

PELI: The poster has no scenic route.

DARO: It is a dentist, not a travel agent.

SCENE 5 - INT. DENTAL CLINIC WAITING ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

Tima checks Daro in. Peli sits beside him. A wall screen shows the clinic's dental cleaning animation. Peli studies it as though it were a travel film.

TIMA: You made it on time.

DARO: We saw the river, the closed bridge and the back of the clinic.

PELI: The detour had three acts.

TIMA: The cleaning takes twenty minutes. You can take the straight route home.

Peli folds the tourist map and opens the radio map. It displays the direct road in blue.

DARO: That is the road we should have taken.

PELI: The scenic route was memorable.

DARO: I have a fluoride appointment in six months. You can make a sequel then.

Peli accepts the folded appointment card, this time keeping it inside the glove compartment. Daro heads toward the exam room.

FINAL IMAGE: Peli parks the car in a space marked PATIENT PICKUP, with the scenic map folded neatly beneath the direct-route guide.
