# The Orchard at the Edge of Winter

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: epic fantasy quests, impossible weather, and the prophecy that mistakes gardening for destiny*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
NARI, a lemur orchard keeper
VEX, an orangutan quest leader
PIP, a capuchin mapmaker
MARA, a macaque winter spirit
ODO, a gibbon historian

SCENE 1. EXT. ORCHARD - MORNING

Frost coats the trees. Vex arrives in a fur cape, carrying a prophecy scroll. Nari prunes a branch.

VEX: The last apple must be carried to the edge of winter.

NARI: This is an orchard.

ODO: The prophecy names the red fruit, the silver path, and the keeper who refuses the call.

NARI: I refuse. It is cold.

Pip unrolls a map. The silver path is a row of frost-covered stones leading to the storage shed.

PIP: The edge of winter is twelve steps away.

VEX: The edge is a distant realm.

NARI: The shed is where I keep the blankets.

A gust blows the prophecy into a tree. Vex climbs to retrieve it. The scroll unfurls, wrapping his cape around the branch. Mara, wearing a white coat and carrying a kettle, enters from the shed.

MARA: I heard there was a quest.

VEX: Are you the winter spirit?

MARA: I'm warming cider.

PIP: It has steam.

Vex declares Mara the guide. She hands him the kettle.

SCENE 2. EXT. FROST PATH - LATER

The party sets out with one apple in a basket. Vex announces that each traveler must cross the silver path without touching the snow.

NARI: It is twelve steps from the tree to the shed.

VEX: We must follow the map.

PIP: The map points to the shed.

VEX: In a mysterious way.

Odo studies the stones. He announces each as a sign: one points east, one points west, and one has a boot print. Mara says the boot print is from her.

ODO: It may be an ancient mark.

MARA: I made it ten minutes ago.

Vex steps carefully from stone to stone. The basket swings. Nari catches the apple before it rolls out. Vex calls this the first trial. The apple has a bruise.

NARI: That is why we store fruit carefully.

VEX: The bruise is the mark of the chosen one.

Mara pours cider into a cup. Vex insists that it is a potion. He drinks and warms his hands around the cup.

SCENE 3. INT. STORAGE SHED - NOON

The quest reaches the shed. Nari opens the door. Inside are blankets, baskets, and a shelf of apples. Vex looks for the edge of winter. Pip points to a thermometer nailed beside the door. It reads one degree.

VEX: The realm is colder than I expected.

NARI: It is the shed.

Odo finds a second line on the prophecy, hidden beneath a fold: "Bring the last apple to the keeper who tends the trees."

NARI: That is me.

VEX: You were the reluctant keeper.

NARI: I was pruning.

Mara sets the cider kettle on a stool. It rattles as Vex tries to stage a revelation. He lifts the basket high. The apple rolls off the top and lands among the stored fruit.

PIP: The last apple has joined its family.

Nari identifies it by the small bruise. She puts it in a padded crate, not on a ceremonial pillow.

SCENE 4. EXT. ORCHARD - AFTERNOON

Vex declares the quest complete but refuses to leave until a magical sign appears. Mara takes a pruning hook and cuts a small branch. The remaining apples are now exposed to sunlight.

MARA: Here is your sign. The tree needs air.

VEX: The winter spirit speaks in horticulture.

NARI: She speaks plainly.

Odo searches the scroll for another verse. Pip holds the map upside down and discovers it has a blank reverse side. Vex uses it as a placard: "THE EDGE OF WINTER, ACCESSIBLE BY APPOINTMENT."

A farmer arrives to collect the crate. Nari hands it over. Vex positions the party for a final portrait, but the farmer walks away with the apple before the camera is ready.

VEX: The chosen fruit is leaving.

NARI: It has a delivery.

SCENE 5. EXT. ORCHARD GATE - SUNSET

The farmer returns with an empty crate and a note: the apple was shared by the workers. Vex treats the note as a royal decree. Mara hangs the prophecy on the gate to dry. Wind flips it over, revealing a scribbled shopping list on the back.

ODO: Was the prophecy written on a grocery list?

MARA: I used the paper that was handy.

Vex bows to Nari and announces that the edge of winter has been conquered. Nari hands him a basket of pruning scraps to carry home.

FINAL IMAGE: The map points to the warm shed, and the quest leader follows it without another speech.
