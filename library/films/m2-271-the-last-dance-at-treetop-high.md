# The Last Dance at Treetop High

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: teen dance-competition dramas, rival crews, rehearsal montages, and a final routine that must express the students' entire identity*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-010*

Logline: A graduating capuchin dance captain must unite a crew of rival primate styles before the gym floor is replaced at midnight, even though the competition's required routine lasts exactly three minutes and their school clock disagrees.

Cast
- MIRA, a capuchin senior who choreographs with a clipboard tucked in her tail.
- DEX, a macaque drummer who treats every count as a solo.
- FENN, a gibbon aerial dancer and Mira's former duet partner.
- PALLA, a ring-tailed lemur tapper who can hear a loose floorboard.
- COACH RUMI, a mandrill who insists the dance needs a story.
- JUDGE VEL, an orangutan whose stopwatch runs slow.
- SABLE, a macaque captain of the rival dance crew.
- JANITOR and DEMOLITION FOREMAN, supporting performers.

## Scene 1: The Last Practice

INT. TREETOP HIGH GYM - AFTERNOON

The gym is marked for demolition. A sign reads: FLOOR REMOVED AT MIDNIGHT. The crew rehearses a clean line dance. Dex adds a drum fill, Fenn swings from the rope, and Palla's tail knocks a stack of cones into rhythm.

MIRA: Stop. We have three minutes, and that was three minutes plus Dex's solo.

DEX: The solo is the emotional center.

PALLA: It is also why the second chorus arrives after the final pose.

FENN: We used to fit it.

MIRA: We used to dance together. Now we each fill the whole floor.

COACH RUMI: Every competition film needs a setback before the big night. We have one: the floor is leaving.

MIRA: We have six hours. That is a deadline, not a montage.

Rumi rolls in a portable mirror. The crew's reflection makes it appear twice as large. Mira sketches a new formation that lets Dex drum without crossing Fenn's swing path.

## Scene 2: The Montage That Will Not End

INT. GYM - LATER

A driving beat begins. Mira calls counts. The crew practices in school corridors, on the bleachers, and beside the janitor's mop bucket. Each time the routine nears three minutes, Dex adds another fill.

MIRA: Two, three, four. Hold the diagonal.

DEX: The diagonal is missing a cymbal crash.

PALLA: The cymbal is a cafeteria tray.

DEX: It has range.

Fenn swings past the mirror. Palla uses her ringed tail to tap the beat on the handrail. The railing answers with a loose metallic rattle. Palla stops.

PALLA: That sound belongs in the routine.

MIRA: No. We are not putting a broken railing in the finale.

PALLA: It is a school sound. The routine is about the school.

Fenn tries a grounded step to join Palla's rhythm. Dex follows, then misses his cue because no one is watching him.

DEX: I thought the camera was on me.

MIRA: There is no camera.

DEX: The dance still needs a close-up.

## Scene 3: The Rival Crew

INT. GYM - EVENING

The rival crew enters wearing matching silver jackets. Their captain, SABLE, a macaque, watches one run-through.

SABLE: Your ending is the demolition sign.

MIRA: It is the truth.

SABLE: Judges like a triumphant ending.

DEX: We can end with me on the drum.

SABLE: That would be triumphant for you.

FENN: What does your crew's routine mean?

SABLE: We rise above every obstacle.

PALLA: You are standing on a mat.

SABLE: It is a symbolic mat.

Sable leaves. Mira calls a break. Fenn stays to help Palla inspect the floor. She points out a loose board that springs when a dancer lands. They mark it with tape. Mira watches them coordinate without speaking.

MIRA: We can use that bounce.

FENN: I thought you wanted a clean floor.

MIRA: I wanted us to notice what the floor does.

DEX: Does the bounce have a drum solo?

MIRA: It has one count.

## Scene 4: The Competition

INT. SCHOOL GYM - NIGHT

The judges sit beside the demolition crew's toolbox. A banner reads LAST DANCE. Judge Vel starts her stopwatch before the music. The rival crew finishes a polished routine. The audience applauds, then looks toward Mira's crew.

MIRA: Places. Remember the floor.

The crew begins in a quiet line. Palla taps the loose board. Dex answers with a restrained beat. Fenn swings low and lands on the spring board; the bounce lifts her into Mira's waiting hand. Their formation spreads across the gym, drawing the audience's eyes to the taped floor, the bleachers, and the familiar cones.

Dex's cymbal tray slips. Rather than stop, he catches it with his foot and uses the clang as a cue. Mira moves through the crew, passing the beat from Palla's tail to Fenn's hands to Dex's drum. It is not a solo. It is a chain.

At the final count, the overhead lights flicker. The building's timer has triggered the midnight power test early. The music cuts. The crew keeps dancing in the dim emergency lamps, following Palla's taps and the floor's spring.

## Scene 5: The Last Count

The routine ends in a circle around the demolition sign. Judge Vel checks her stopwatch.

VEL: Your time is three minutes and eighteen seconds.

MIRA: The music stopped at three minutes.

VEL: I started before the first beat.

PALLA: Then your timer has its own choreography.

The judges confer. Sable steps forward.

SABLE: They used the whole room. We only used the stage.

VEL: The competition rules award points for precision and audience connection. Your crew missed a count, but recovered without a restart.

DEX: Do we win?

VEL: You tie for first.

MIRA: A tie is fine.

DEX: Can we share the trophy? It is large enough for my drum.

Sable takes one handle; Mira takes the other. The demolition foreman waits with a wrench.

COACH RUMI: One last picture before the floor goes.

PALLA: We can dance while they remove the sign.

The crew performs its opening four counts again, short and unscored. The janitor joins on the mop bucket. The foreman waits, tapping a wrench to the beat.

## Final Image

The gym floor is rolled up in sections. The trophy rests on the first stack, and the dancers' sneaker marks remain on the last board. Mira taps the loose board once. It answers with the same springy note.
