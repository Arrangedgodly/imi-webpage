# The Four-Minute Ferry

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: thriller films where a tight departure schedule becomes an elaborate chase*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-019*

CAST
CAPTAIN NARI, a lemur
DECKHAND PIP, a capuchin
PASSENGER VEX, an orangutan
DISPATCHER ODO, a gibbon
DOCK WORKER MARA, a macaque

SCENE 1. EXT. FERRY DOCK - MORNING

A digital sign shows departure in four minutes. Vex runs toward the ramp with a suitcase. Nari checks the passenger list.

VEX: The last ferry is leaving. I have the only ticket.

NARI: There are six minutes until departure. The sign counts boarding cutoff, not departure.

PIP: Ramp is down. One passenger is still on the dock.

MARA: The suitcase wheel is caught in the ramp groove.

VEX: We are losing precious seconds.

NARI: Stop pulling. Lift the case slightly so the wheel can clear.

Vex lifts. Pip guides the case onto the deck. The ramp remains level.

SCENE 2. INT. FERRY CABIN - MOMENTS LATER

Odo radios the dock. A coil of rope lies beside the door.

ODO: Passenger list is complete except for Vex's boarding mark.

VEX: Mark me aboard. The ferry must leave.

NARI: The manifest is a safety count. Stand by the clipboard while Pip checks your name.

PIP: Ticket matches. Vex is aboard.

MARA: The mooring line is still tied. I'll release it when Nari gives the order.

VEX: Could we cast off now? The clock says three minutes.

NARI: The clock says boarding cutoff. The departure time is posted for 9:10.

SCENE 2A. EXT. FERRY RAMP - CONTINUOUS

A family approaches with a folded stroller. Pip pauses the boarding line while Mara checks the ramp width.

MARA: Keep the center lane open. The stroller can go up first, with the passenger beside it.

VEX: The departure sign is down to two minutes.

NARI: The boarding cutoff is still ahead. We have time to assist without running.

ODO: I have counted everyone except the dock worker. Mara, are you staying ashore?

MARA: I am boarding after I release the shore line. The manifest counts crew separately.

PIP: Stroller is aboard and folded by the cabin wall. No one is blocking the ramp.

VEX: The countdown is almost over.

NARI: It counts to the cutoff, not departure. We still depart at 9:10.

Mara checks the rope and puts the free end into the cleat. Pip closes the ramp once the last passenger boards.

MARA: Ramp secure. Dock worker is aboard. Confirm crew count.

ODO: Captain, deckhand, dispatcher and four passengers. Count matches.

VEX: The suspense is now a clipboard.

NARI: The clipboard prevents us from leaving someone behind.

SCENE 2B. INT. FERRY CABIN - CONTINUOUS

The family stows the stroller. Vex's suitcase is in the marked luggage area, clear of the exit.

PIP: Is the aisle clear from both doors?

NARI: Yes. The handrail is free, and the ramp is still level.

ODO: Passenger count is four. Crew count is three. We can complete the departure check.

VEX: The sign has reached one minute.

MARA: The ramp is secure, but the shore line remains on the cleat until departure time.

PIP: I can stand by the line. I'll release only on the captain's signal.

NARI: Weather is calm. The channel is clear.

VEX: The final minute is unbearable.

MARA: It is one minute. Check your ticket and sit down.

Vex opens his ticket envelope and confirms the island pier. He sits beside the window as the clock changes to 9:10.

SCENE 2C. INT. FERRY CABIN - CONTINUOUS

The clock changes. Mara checks the channel and nods to Pip. The dock line slips from the cleat into the deck box.

NARI: Departure time. Cast off.

MARA: Shore line secure. Ramp clear.

SCENE 3. EXT. DECK - MINUTES LATER

A gust pushes the loose rope coil toward the rail. Pip pins it with a boot. Vex reaches for it.

PIP: Don't grab the line. It's under my foot.

MARA: I'll coil it into the deck box.

VEX: The ferry is under attack by rope.

NARI: The rope is loose because we are still docked.

Odo reads the schedule: 9:08. Nari waits. At 9:10, she signals Mara.

NARI: Cast off.

Mara releases the line. Pip closes the deck box. The ferry eases away from the dock.

SCENE 4. INT. CABIN - CONTINUOUS

Vex sits by the window. The digital sign on shore still reads "boarding closed."

VEX: We have escaped the countdown.

ODO: We departed on schedule.

NARI: The next ferry is already listed for 9:40. No pursuit required.

VEX: Could I still look back dramatically?

MARA: Look back if you want to see the dock. Don't lean over the rail.

TAG: Vex turns to the window. The ferry's safety notice is reflected over his face: STAY SEATED WHILE UNDERWAY.
