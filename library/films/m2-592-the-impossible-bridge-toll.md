# The Impossible Bridge Toll

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: quest films where a bridge guardian demands an ever-changing toll*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-020*

CAST
TRAVELER MARA, a macaque
TOLLKEEPER VEX, an orangutan
GUIDE PIP, a capuchin
BRIDGE ENGINEER ODO, a gibbon
FERRY KEEPER NARI, a lemur

SCENE 1. EXT. STONE BRIDGE - MORNING

A narrow footbridge crosses a stream. Vex sits beside a hand-painted sign: TOLL: ONE STORY. Mara approaches with an empty satchel.

VEX: To pass, you must pay the toll.

MARA: The sign says one story. I have one.

PIP: The bridge is open to foot traffic. The toll is Vex's festival game.

ODO: Weight limit is two walkers at a time. The center board was repaired yesterday.

VEX: The guardian will judge your tale.

MARA: I carried a basket of pears to the mill, and one rolled down the hill. The baker used it in a pie.

VEX: Too short. Bring a longer story.

MARA: The toll sign says one story.

VEX: The sign has a sequel.

SCENE 2. EXT. BRIDGE ENTRANCE - CONTINUOUS

Vex adds a second sign: TOLL MAY INCLUDE A RHYME. Pip reads it while Mara waits behind the painted line.

PIP: You changed the rules after she told the story.

VEX: The bridge demands variety.

ODO: The bridge demands that people cross one at a time. The toll is not part of its maintenance plan.

Vex asks for a rhyme. Mara gives him two lines about a pear in a pie. He asks for a song. She declines; the bridge is public and other walkers are waiting.

MARA: I have met the posted toll. I am crossing now.

VEX: The guardian blocks your path.

Mara does not push past him. Odo points to the open lane beside the sign, where Vex has left room for walkers. Mara starts across at a careful pace. Vex hurries alongside, trying to add a final condition.

VEX: A riddle! The bridge accepts only riddles.

PIP: It accepted your own feet without asking anything.

Vex looks down at his shoes on the bridge and pauses.

SCENE 3. EXT. BRIDGE MIDPOINT - MOMENTS LATER

Mara reaches the center board. It creaks under Vex's extra weight as he follows beside her. Odo signals him to step back to the bank; the posted limit is one person on the repaired section.

ODO: One at a time across the center. Mara is already there. Wait on the approach.

VEX: The tollkeeper must accompany the traveler.

MARA: You are not collecting a toll. You are making the bridge crowded.

PIP: Stand at the marker. You can hear her story from there.

Vex backs onto the bank, but his robe catches the edge of the sign. Pip frees it. Mara walks to the far side, then turns to listen.

MARA: The pear became a pie. That's the whole story.

VEX: I have not heard the ending.

MARA: The pie was eaten.

Vex considers this. He wanted an epic and got a complete tale.

SCENE 3A. EXT. FAR BANK - CONTINUOUS

Mara waits on the far bank while Odo checks the repaired bridge from the approach. Pip remains beside the toll sign with Vex. Nari unfolds the ferry schedule so travelers have another option.

NARI: The ferry is delayed ten minutes. Anyone who wants to cross can wait here.

VEX: The bridge has been conquered.

MARA: No one conquered it. It was open, and I crossed carefully.

PIP: The center board carried one person at a time, as the notice said.

VEX: The toll's missing riddle leaves a narrative gap.

ODO: There is no requirement for a riddle. The board is secure; the sign is the only thing that needs correcting.

Vex looks at his new sign, then at the ferry passengers. He starts to add a rhyme to the notice. Mara calls across that a clear sign is more useful than a clever one.

VEX: The guardian reluctantly accepts clarity.

MARA: Keep the lane open while I wait here. No one needs to cross again to prove it worked.

Odo places a small marker by the repaired board so the next walker sees where to step. Vex takes the second sign off the post and carries it back to his stool. He does not add another condition.

SCENE 4. EXT. FAR BANK - DAY

Mara steps off the bridge. Nari waits with a ferry schedule for visitors who do not wish to walk. Odo checks the repaired board from the bank without putting weight on it.

NARI: The ferry is free until noon. It departs from the lower landing.

VEX: The bridge has released the traveler.

MARA: I crossed because the bridge is open. I paid your game with a story.

PIP: The posted toll was one story. The tollkeeper cannot keep changing it.

Vex removes the second sign and tucks it beneath the first.

TAG: Vex sits on the bank to collect stories from people waiting for the ferry. Odo adds a new sign: LISTENING IS FREE.
