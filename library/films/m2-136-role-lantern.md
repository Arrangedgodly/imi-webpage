# The Role Is a Lantern

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: casting sessions that ask performers to repeat vague sides while producers search for a pre-decided type*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-005*

Logline: At an open casting call, performers are asked to play "the mysterious helper" without being told what the helper does.

Cast: Yani, a lemur casting director; Odo, a gorilla actor; Seli, a macaque producer; and Puck, a capuchin reader.

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, MORNING

Odo enters a small audition room. A folding table holds three props: a lantern, a scarf, and a wooden spoon. Yani hands him two pages with no character names. Seli watches from the back row.

YANI: We are looking for someone quietly unforgettable.

ODO: What does the character do?

SELI: We will know when we see it.

Puck reads the other part. The page says, "You came back." Odo picks up the lantern.

ODO: Is this a cave scene?

YANI: It can be.

He tries the line as a guide, then a ferryman, then a person waiting for a bus. Seli says the bus version feels too contemporary. Yani asks if he can be more mysterious. Odo turns off the lantern and waits. Puck asks if the audition is over. Yani tells Odo to hold the lantern at chest height. Seli asks him to hold it at waist height, then above his head, then "somewhere between confidence and doubt."

ODO: I can hold a lantern.

YANI: That may be the role.

Seli says the character should have a recognizable silhouette. Odo stands in three different poses. Puck photographs each one. The final pose resembles a person holding a lamp while waiting for the elevator.

SCENE 2. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, LATER

The next performer reads the same sides while holding the scarf. Seli says the role needs a physical presence. Yani asks for a silent take. The performer points at the spoon. Puck asks if the spoon is a clue.

SELI: We do not want to over-explain.

YANI: There are no explanations in the sides.

Seli checks the casting notes. They say "warm but distant, familiar but new." Yani asks whether the character is a friend. Seli says the marketing team will decide the relationship after casting. Odo waits by the door with the lantern.

ODO: May I leave this here?

YANI: That is the strongest choice so far.

Seli asks Odo to repeat the audition with the spoon. Odo lifts it and asks if it belongs to the character. Seli says it represents an object the character cannot name. Odo sets it down. Puck reads, "You came back." Odo looks at the spoon.

ODO: I did.

SCENE 3. INT. COMMUNITY HALL, AFTERNOON

Yani records callbacks on a phone. Seli watches and asks for one more version with "a little more backstory." Odo asks for the character's name. Seli says they have not chosen one. He asks where he has come back from. She says they will see what the scene needs.

Odo walks in, puts the lantern on the table, and sits. Puck reads the line. Odo looks at the empty chair beside him.

ODO: I saved you a seat.

Seli says the role is not a bus guide. Odo asks if the chair is in the scene. Seli checks the notes; it is a prop from another audition. Puck removes it. The space changes. Odo looks at the table instead. Seli asks if the character should be facing a window. Yani points out there is no window in the room. Puck holds up a blue cloth to suggest one. Odo turns toward it and casts a shadow over the written sides.

ODO: Is the traveler outside?

SELI: The traveler is whatever makes the scene work.

YANI: We could let the script tell us.

Seli puts the blue cloth down and asks Odo to try the line once more without an imaginary view.

YANI: That was clear.

SELI: It was not what I imagined.

YANI: What did you imagine?

Seli points to the audition notes. One line reads "someone who has seen everything." Odo asks if that means the character is old. Seli says not necessarily. Yani says the script may help.

SCENE 4. INT. PRODUCTION OFFICE, EVENING

The finished casting sides arrive. The role is a night guard who keeps the lantern lit at a mountain shelter. The character welcomes a returning traveler and offers a seat. Odo's audition matches the scene. Seli asks whether they can call him back for a different take.

YANI: We have the take.

SELI: I wanted to ask him to be mysterious again.

Odo gets the role. Puck labels the lantern "PROP - SHELTER." Yani sends the actual sides to the next performers. Seli starts to retrieve the lantern for the next audition. Odo stops her and points to the prop label. The night guard needs it on the set. Puck signs it out and writes the scene number on the tag.

FINAL IMAGE: The casting sign-up sheet now lists the character's job, location, and name. The wooden spoon sits in the unrelated props box.
