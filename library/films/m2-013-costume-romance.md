# The Hem at Midnight

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: costume romance conventions, mistaken identity, and ballroom etiquette*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: At a royal masquerade, a clever macaque seamstress keeps repairing one noble's torn hem while he mistakes her for three different guests, until she claims the final dance and asks him to hold still.

Cast: Sable, a macaque seamstress; Lord Rowan, a shy gibbon; Lady Pearl, a peacock socialite; King Alden, a walrus with a grand wig; and Pip, a squirrel page who carries sewing kits.

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE WORKROOM, EVENING

Sable stitches a silver hem while Pip delivers masks. The palace is preparing for a masquerade. Lord Rowan enters wearing a coat that is too long. He trips over the hem, catches himself, and pretends this was a bow.

SABLE: The coat is ambitious.

ROWAN: It has a title.

She hems it while he stands on a stool. He asks if she will attend the ball. She says she is working at the ball.

SCENE 2. INT. PALACE BALLROOM, NIGHT

Sable moves between guests with a sewing basket. Rowan enters wearing a fox mask. He mistakes Sable for Lady Pearl, whose mask has feathers. He offers a dance. Sable tells him to look at the shoes. Rowan looks down, sees work boots, and apologizes.

Lady Pearl arrives wearing the same silver trim as Sable's work apron. Rowan mistakes her for the seamstress and asks about his hem. Pearl points to her own gown, which is not torn. Rowan bows and backs into a curtain.

SCENE 2A. INT. PALACE WORKROOM, LATER

Pip delivers a stack of invitations with different names. Sable sorts them by color, then realizes every envelope has the same address. The palace has confused the guest list with the costume inventory. She and Pip carry both piles to the ballroom.

Rowan arrives to collect his coat. He tries it on, but the hem is still long because he has put it on backward. Sable turns him around. He asks how she knows so much about coats. She says she made the coat. He says he knew that. He did not.

SCENE 3. INT. SIDE CORRIDOR, LATER

The king's wig catches on a chandelier hook. Sable climbs a ladder to free it. Rowan holds the ladder. The king insists the wig is part of the crown. It slowly slides down over his face. Sable pins it in place with a large brass needle.

Rowan asks Sable if she likes the ball. She says she likes the music between the announcements. He asks what she would change. She says she would shorten the speeches and make the dance floor wider.

SCENE 3A. INT. BALLROOM, LATER

The guests remove their masks for a guessing game. The host asks each guest to identify someone by their costume. Rowan guesses the king is a gardener because of the green sash. The king says it is the royal sash. Lady Pearl guesses Rowan is a tailor because his coat is pinned. Rowan says the coat is still under construction.

Sable gets asked to guess the host. She identifies him by the headset, not the mask. The host says that is cheating. She points out he is wearing the headset over his mask.

SCENE 4. INT. BALLROOM, MIDNIGHT

A bell rings for the final dance. Rowan searches for Sable but sees Lady Pearl in silver. He asks her to dance. Pearl points him toward the workroom. Sable has changed into a simple dark dress and still carries her sewing kit.

Rowan offers his hand. He asks which guest she is. Sable says she is the person who fixed his coat twice. He recognizes her boots. They dance carefully. He keeps stepping on the hem. She ties it up with a spare ribbon.

SCENE 4A. INT. PALACE BALCONY, MIDNIGHT

Rowan asks whether Sable wants to dance again. She says he has already used up his one dance. He offers to help fold the chairs instead. Sable is surprised. They stack the chairs together, keeping time with the band inside. Rowan is better at carrying chairs than dancing, so she teaches him a small step between stacks.

A servant opens the door and announces the final dance. They leave the chairs in neat rows. One chair is turned backward. Rowan turns to fix it, trips on his repaired hem, and catches himself. Sable laughs. He laughs too.

SCENE 5. EXT. PALACE GARDEN, DAWN

The guests leave. Rowan asks if Sable will come to the next ball as a guest. She says she will come if there is no costume code and no emergency hemming. He promises to tell the palace staff. The king overhears and asks her to shorten his wig.

SCENE 6. INT. PALACE WORKROOM, MORNING

Rowan returns the coat, now clean and correctly oriented. Sable checks the hem. It is straight. He asks whether she will attend the next ball as a guest. She says only if he can keep his feet away from the hem.

PIP: He can hold the chairs.
ROWAN: I can do that.
SABLE: And the wig?
ROWAN: I will not touch the wig.

The king enters wearing the shortened wig backward. Sable turns it around. Rowan offers to hold it while she pins the clasp. The king asks whether the next event has a costume code. Sable says it has a fitting schedule. Rowan asks if that counts as an invitation. She hands him a pin cushion and says it counts as practice.

FINAL IMAGE: Rowan and Sable walk out as the sun rises. The king appears in the doorway, holding his wig. The wig is now shorter than his head. Sable points to the scissors in Pip's hand. Pip says he only trimmed the loose end. The king turns toward the palace as the wig slips sideways again.
