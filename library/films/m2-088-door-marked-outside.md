# The Door Marked Outside

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: escape-room thrillers, arbitrary puzzle clues, and characters ignoring obvious exits for a final riddle*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-003*

Logline: Four primates enter a themed puzzle room for a birthday outing and discover the operator has locked the staff closet instead of the exit.

Cast: Koro, a baboon puzzle enthusiast; Yuki, a lemur birthday guest; Fen, a macaque electrician; and Pib, a capuchin room host.

SCENE 1. INT. ANCIENT TEMPLE PUZZLE ROOM, AFTERNOON

Pib explains the rules. The team has forty-five minutes to find the temple key. Yuki notices a large green button labeled EXIT. Pib says that opens the door in an emergency. Koro says the button is probably a trap.

KORO: The puzzle begins when we trust nothing.

YUKI: The instructions say look for a key.

They search the room. Koro finds four carved symbols and arranges them in a sequence. A drawer opens and reveals a flashlight, not a key. Fen checks the fake stone wall and finds a loose panel. Pib says the panel is decorative.

Koro shines the flashlight at a painted mural and spots a shadow shaped like a crown. He declares it a map to a buried chamber. Yuki turns on the overhead light. The shadow was cast by a coat hook. Pib says the room has three intended puzzles and one decorative mural. Koro asks whether the hook is a clue. Fen checks the mounting screw and tightens it.

The team solves a word lock by reading the clue card as printed. Koro suggests shifting every letter to make the answer more mysterious. Yuki enters the plain word, and the lock opens. Inside is a small brass key. Pib says the first part of the game is complete. Koro asks whether they should celebrate. The timer shows thirty minutes remain.

SCENE 2. INT. PUZZLE ROOM, TEN MINUTES LATER

The group solves a cipher. It reads "TURN THE TORCH." Koro rotates a wall torch. A compartment opens, releasing a paper map. Yuki asks if the map leads to the exit. Koro studies the markings and declares it points to the chamber's secret center. The map has an arrow labeled DOOR.

Fen tests the torch bracket and finds it loose. He asks them to stop pulling on it. Koro says the room is designed to challenge their assumptions. Fen says the bracket is designed to hold a plastic torch. Pib contacts the control desk, but the room speaker plays a prerecorded riddle. The staff radio is inside the closet, which is locked from the inside by a misplaced latch.

SCENE 3. INT. CONTROL HALL, SAME

The room host's supervisor checks the camera feed. She sees the guests standing beside the exit button and tells Pib to guide them to it. Pib cannot hear her because the speaker is still playing the clue track. Fen finds the manual volume switch behind a panel and turns it down. The supervisor's voice comes through.

The supervisor asks Pib to confirm the staff corridor is clear. He checks the camera and sees a delivery cart blocking the hallway. The supervisor moves the cart before directing anyone through. Koro studies the key and finds a label that says STORAGE. He says the symbol may require a dramatic interpretation. Yuki matches the key to the storage chest, opens it, and finds a note that says "Well done." Koro waits for a second note. There is not one.

Pib apologizes and reaches for the exit button. Koro stops him; he has found another clue under the rug. It says "PRESS WHEN READY." Yuki asks if that refers to the button. Koro says it may refer to the final key. Fen points to the emergency exit label and presses it.

SCENE 4. INT. STAFF CORRIDOR, MOMENTS LATER

The door opens. The group steps into the corridor. Pib checks the latch and sees the staff-closet key ring hanging on the inside hook. The operator's storage door is shut from the wrong side. Fen retrieves the ring and opens the closet. The supervisor arrives and confirms no one is hurt.

Koro asks if the escape room is over. Yuki says the birthday outing is over. The supervisor refunds their tickets and offers a free future visit after the door mechanism is repaired. Fen tags the exit button and asks them not to restart the room until the safety check is complete.

The group reviews the clue card. "PRESS WHEN READY" was meant to open a chest. Pib admits the card was placed beside the exit during reset. Koro says the puzzle was unfair. Yuki says the room had an exit. They decide to go for cake instead.

The supervisor records the misplacement in the reset log and removes the room from service until the button and puzzle signage are separated. Pib offers the guests a refund and asks if they want to leave feedback. Yuki says the puzzles were fun, but the emergency exit should never look like a clue. Fen checks that the door latches correctly after it is closed. Koro asks whether he can still claim they escaped the temple. Yuki says they escaped the room.

FINAL IMAGE: A repair worker hangs a sign by the button: "EMERGENCY EXIT - ALWAYS WORKS." Koro studies it, then presses it to make sure.
