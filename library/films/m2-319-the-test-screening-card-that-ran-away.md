# The Test Screening Card That Ran Away

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: test screenings and note cards that turn audience reactions into misleading studio data*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
PRODUCER VEX, a macaque
AUDIENCE WRANGLER PIP, a capuchin
SCREENING GUEST LUMA, a lemur
EDITOR ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. INT. TEST SCREENING ROOM - MORNING

Producer Vex places a stack of response cards on every chair. The film is a comedy about a crab who tries to cross a beach before the tide. Director Nari asks the audience to watch the ending before filling anything out. Pip stands by the aisle with a clipboard.

VEX: Rate the ending from one to five. If you laugh, circle five.

LUMA: What if we laugh at the crab's hat?

VEX: The hat is part of the ending.

NARI: The crab has not put it on yet.

Odo, the editor, queues the film. Vex tells Pip to collect cards immediately after the credits. Pip asks whether blank cards count as positive. Vex says blank cards are potential optimism.

SCENE 2. INT. SCREENING ROOM - LATER

The crab reaches a sand ridge, sees a wave and retreats. One guest laughs when the crab's hat blows off. Vex whispers to Pip to mark the laugh as an ending laugh. Pip records it. The crab then returns the hat to a seabird, who points toward a dry channel. The audience gives a soft "aw." Vex hears a different guest cough and asks Pip to mark that as a suspense reaction.

The film ends with the crab reaching a tide pool. A guest claps. Vex calls it a standing ovation, though no one stands. Pip collects the cards. One says, "I liked the gull." Another says, "I am allergic to the lobby's new paint." Vex sorts both into a pile labeled CRAB ENDING.

PIP: The gull note is about the middle.

VEX: It says liked.

PIP: The paint note says allergic.

VEX: Strong feeling.

SCENE 3. INT. LOBBY - AFTERNOON

Vex asks for a clear score. Odo makes a bar chart. The laugh at the hat gets counted as applause, applause becomes a standing ovation, and the cough becomes suspense. The crab's actual ending receives one star because a guest circled the wrong row while holding a drink.

NARI: They liked the gull and the hat.

VEX: The crab ending scored poorly.

LUMA: The crab gets there. That is the ending.

Vex asks for a rescreen with the ending moved earlier. Odo trims the beach journey so the crab reaches the tide pool before the wave appears. On playback, the crab walks confidently into a dry pool, then reacts to a wave that has not happened. Vex says the audience will admire its foresight.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM - EVENING

Nari restores the original order. She asks the guests to mark which moment they are responding to. Pip gives them a simple card with three labeled frames: hat, gull, tide pool. Vex objects that this is too precise for a screening. Luma points out that it is easier than guessing from a cough.

They run the ending again. The hat blows away. A guest laughs and marks HAT. The crab returns it; a guest marks GULL. The crab reaches the tide pool; the room quiets, then applauds. Pip watches the timing and makes no score until the cards are handed in. Vex tries to move one hat laugh into the final column. Nari covers the chart with the stack of cards.

ODO: The ending got applause.

VEX: How much?

PIP: The people who were still watching clapped.

VEX: Could we have them watch the hat again?

LUMA: It already flew away.

SCENE 5. INT. EDITING ROOM - NIGHT

Odo cuts the film in its original order. Vex examines the corrected notes: guests liked the gull, the hat, and the crab's patient last step. He asks whether the studio can call it a five-star ending. Nari says they can call it a completed film. Vex looks at the stack of blank cards and asks if potential optimism counts toward the average.

Pip hands him one blank card. Vex writes "I enjoyed the screening" and tries to sort it. There is no audience member's name on it. He moves it to a new pile labeled PRODUCER REACTIONS. The pile contains only his card.

The final cut plays. The crab reaches the tide pool, places the hat on a stone and watches the tide rise. This time, the room laughs at the crab's careful plan, then applauds when it works.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex pins the tiny producer-reaction pile to the wall, where it hangs crookedly beside the honest audience chart.
