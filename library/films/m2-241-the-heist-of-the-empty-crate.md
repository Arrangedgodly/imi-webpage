# The Heist of the Empty Crate

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: elaborate caper films, where a crew plans a daring theft of an object nobody wants*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-009*

Logline: A trio plans a precision midnight heist of an empty produce crate, while the night watchman tries to persuade them to take something useful.

Cast: Seli, a lemur planner; Grom, a baboon climber; Nima, a capuchin lookout; and Varo, a macaque night watchman.

SCENE 1. INT. SHED, NIGHT

A chalk diagram covers the wall. It shows the shed, the yard, one crate, and a dramatic arrow that loops over the roof.

SELI: At midnight, we enter through the window, cross the rafters, lower the crate by rope, then vanish through the loading gate.

NIMA: The gate is open.

SELI: It may not be at midnight.

GROM: The crate is empty.

SELI: That is why it is light.

GROM: There are six empty crates.

Seli circles one in red.

SELI: This is the one with the loose corner. It is recognizable.

NIMA: The other five have loose corners.

Varo, outside the window, taps the glass. The crew freezes.

VARO: If you are taking a crate, take the one with the broken slat. It is scheduled for repair.

SELI: We were not planning to take it.

VARO: Then why is there a rope?

GROM: For the roof.

VARO: The roof is not going anywhere.

The plan proceeds anyway. Nima checks the clock, Grom tests a length of rope, and Seli assigns code names: Moon, Fern, and Pebble.

NIMA: Which one am I?

SELI: You are Pebble.

NIMA: I wanted to be Moon.

SELI: Moon stays near the window.

SCENE 2. EXT. SHED ROOF, MOMENTS LATER

Grom climbs a ladder. The ladder is too short, so he places a crate beneath it. The crate creaks.

GROM: The crate has made a noise.

SELI: That is why we're taking it.

VARO: You can take the ladder back to the tool rack after.

Seli signals for silence. A night bird calls. Nima answers with a quiet chirp, then realizes she has used the wrong signal.

NIMA: That was the retreat call.

SELI: We have not entered yet.

GROM: We can retreat from the roof.

He climbs down. The crate shifts, sliding toward the yard. Varo catches it with one hand.

VARO: The heist is now a rescue.

SELI: Keep the crate still. We need it for the operation.

VARO: I need it for the repair. Those are the same crate.

SCENE 3. INT. SHED, CONTINUOUS

They enter through the open loading gate. Seli insists this was an alternate route. Varo follows with the crate and sets it beneath the shelf.

SELI: Place the target exactly on the chalk mark.

GROM: The chalk mark is under the crate.

NIMA: We have reached the object.

SELI: Now we lower it.

VARO: It is already down.

Seli gestures to the rope. Grom attaches it around the crate and pulls. The crate lifts, tilts, and catches on the shelf. A row of folded sacks tumbles into it.

[SFX: SACKS FALLING.]

GROM: The empty crate is full.

VARO: It is full of the sacks I put there.

SELI: The target has changed.

NIMA: Do we still take it?

The crew looks at the sacks. Varo explains that they are clean sacks for tomorrow's harvest. Seli says the heist's objective was always to transport the crate.

VARO: You drew a line from the shed to the gate. That is a transport route.

SELI: We called it an escape route.

NIMA: Same direction.

SCENE 4. EXT. LOADING GATE, PRE-DAWN

The crew carries the crate through the gate together. It is awkward with sacks inside. Varo records the move on his repair clipboard.

VARO: Time of removal: three fifty-two.

SELI: Time of disappearance.

VARO: The crate has not disappeared. I can see it.

GROM: From behind, it is gone.

Nima holds up the red plan. It shows the dramatic roof route, the rope, and the empty crate. Varo turns the page over and draws the broken slat. He picks up two screws from the tool rack.

SELI: You have altered the evidence.

VARO: I have documented the repair.

The crate reaches the tool rack. Varo takes the rope off. He squares the loose corner and drives the screws through the slat. The crate holds its shape when Grom lifts it.

VARO: Done.

SELI: The operation succeeded.

NIMA: We stole no object.

GROM: We lifted one.

SELI: The title of the operation is not the title of the report.

Varo gives them each a sack to carry to the orchard.

VARO: You can take these. They are supposed to leave the shed.

Seli studies her chalk map, then draws three arrows toward the orchard.

SELI: Phase two.

NIMA: We are carrying sacks.

GROM: Under code names.

[SFX: FOOTSTEPS AWAY, CRATE SETTLES.]
