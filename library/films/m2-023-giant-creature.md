# A Very Large Guest

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: giant creature disaster spectacles, evacuation montages, and overconfident city planning*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS*

Logline: A giant gorilla visits a coastal city during its annual bridge festival, and the emergency planners keep deploying dramatic evacuation procedures for a visitor who is trying to return a dropped toy boat.

Cast: Ketta, a macaque emergency planner; Mayor Brindle, a baboon who loves press conferences; Gomo, a gentle giant gorilla; Nix, a capuchin festival mechanic; and a toy sailboat with a yellow flag.

SCENE 1. EXT. COASTAL BRIDGE, MORNING

Festival guests gather on the bridge. A shadow covers the sun. Gomo rises from the bay, holding a toy boat. The mayor orders sirens. Nix notices the yellow flag belongs to a child beside the railing.

KETTA: The gorilla is holding a toy.

MAYOR: A toy could be a device.

NIX: It is a boat.

MAYOR: A maritime device.

Gomo steps toward the bridge. Ketta asks everyone to clear the center lane. The crowd evacuates to the sidewalks, where they all take photos.

SCENE 2. INT. EMERGENCY VAN, CONTINUOUS

Ketta studies the route. The city plan says to close the bridge, open the tunnel, and direct crowds north. The tunnel is full of festival floats. The floats are shaped like boats.

MAYOR: Is that a risk?

KETTA: It is a traffic jam.

MAYOR: Can we announce the risk?

KETTA: We can announce the jam.

The mayor gives a dramatic broadcast about "an unprecedented maritime encounter." Behind him, a volunteer hands out lemonade. The microphones pick up the ice.

SCENE 3. EXT. FESTIVAL GROUNDS, MIDDAY

Gomo walks along the seawall, following the toy boat's yellow flag. The festival band plays a loud march. Gomo stops. Nix asks the band to lower the volume. The band lowers it, but the drummer keeps playing because he cannot hear the cue.

Ketta clears a path. The mayor tries to pose beside Gomo for the news. Gomo gently places the toy boat in the mayor's hat. The hat tips over his eyes. He continues speaking, unaware that his press conference is now a hat.

SCENE 3A. EXT. FESTIVAL GROUNDS, CONTINUOUS

The mayor gathers a response team. It includes two lifeguards, one balloon vendor, a volunteer with a megaphone, and a marching band that has not been told the plan.

MAYOR: We need a controlled retreat.

KETTA: Use the west sidewalk.

NIX: The floats block it.

MAYOR: Then the east sidewalk.

NIX: That's where the crowd is.

MAYOR: Can we move the crowd?

KETTA: We can ask them.

The megaphone volunteer announces an evacuation. The loudspeaker squeals. Everyone evacuates away from the squeal, which happens to be the right direction. The marching band follows, still playing. Gomo hears the music and pauses. Nix waves the band toward the food stalls. The musicians stop playing and Gomo continues toward the toy boat.

SCENE 4. EXT. BRIDGE RAMP, AFTERNOON

The child who lost the boat is reunited with it. Gomo kneels and sets it down. The bridge bends slightly beneath his weight. Engineers rush in with gauges. The mayor orders them to "stabilize the landmark." Ketta says it is a temporary loading limit issue, not a structural collapse.

The engineers ask the crowd to step off the bridge. Everyone steps onto the same sidewalk. Ketta redirects them by color-coded signs. The signs point in circles because a volunteer installed them backward. Nix turns them around.

SCENE 4A. INT. FESTIVAL CONTROL TENT, LATER

Ketta reviews the bridge load chart. It is covered by a photo of the mayor with a giant creature behind him. The mayor says the photo is useful for public confidence. Ketta moves it aside and shows that the bridge is safe if people spread out.

MAYOR: Could we make a dramatic announcement?

KETTA: Say "Please use both sidewalks."

MAYOR: That's not dramatic.

NIX: It will work.

The mayor tries the announcement in a booming voice. The crowd cheers because they think the festival show is starting. He repeats it in a normal voice. People move to both sidewalks. Ketta marks the procedure as successful.

Ketta checks the bridge again after the last guest leaves. The engineers sign off.

SCENE 5. EXT. BAY, SUNSET

Gomo returns to the water. The child waves. The mayor announces the festival was a success, citing "record attendance during the evacuation." Ketta corrects the report: the bridge was closed for twenty minutes while engineers checked it.

FINAL IMAGE: The mayor unveils a permanent sign: "GIANT VISITOR VIEWING AREA." Ketta turns it toward the water and adds a smaller notice: "Please keep the walkway clear." A tourist stands directly in front of the notice to take a photograph.
