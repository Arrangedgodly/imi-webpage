# The Aquarium Has One More Wave

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: disaster blockbusters with a cascading systems failure, emergency announcements, and a small crew coordinating a public evacuation*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-018*

Cast: SANA, a baboon aquarium keeper; TEAL, a gibbon diver; PAX, a macaque engineer; NIM, an orangutan tour guide; DIRECTOR VALE, a capuchin aquarium manager.

INT. CITY AQUARIUM - AFTERNOON

A school group watches a giant tank. A wave of water rolls inside the glass, though the filtration pumps hum steadily.

NIM: The current is part of the exhibit.

SANA: The current is moving the coral display.

PAX checks the control panel. One pump light blinks red.

PAX: The west filter is backing up.

The tank's wave hits the glass. The visitors gasp. A fish swims through the current and hides behind a shipwreck prop.

DIRECTOR VALE: Is the tank safe?

PAX: The glass is safe. The pump is not.

A low alarm sounds. A display screen reads FILTER PRESSURE HIGH. Nim turns toward the visitors.

NIM: Please step away from the glass and follow me to the blue doors.

INT. SERVICE TUNNEL - MOMENTS LATER

Teal enters in a dive suit and carries a rubber wedge. Sana hands over a flashlight.

TEAL: I can reach the intake if the tunnel stays clear.

PAX: No one touches the valve until the pressure drops.

Another alarm sounds. The aquarium lights turn blue. The school group continues moving toward the exit, guided by Nim.

ACT ONE

INT. CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Pax switches the west pump off. The red light changes to yellow, but the pressure gauge keeps climbing.

PAX: The bypass is blocked.

SANA: The backup gate is behind the reef tank.

DIRECTOR VALE: That tank has a public tunnel.

NIM: The public tunnel is empty.

PAX: I can close the side gate and reroute water through the old pipe. Someone has to turn it by hand.

Teal crawls through a narrow service hatch. Sana keeps a hand on the hatch rim. The tank above them groans as water pushes against its frame.

TEAL: I can see the valve.

PAX: Turn it one notch. Wait for the gauge.

Teal turns. The gauge drops, then jumps.

SANA: Stop there.

The backup pump starts automatically. Water begins flowing back through the blocked bypass. A valve wheel spins too fast.

TEAL: The wheel is loose.

Pax runs to the supply rack and grabs a wrench. Nim returns from the exit corridor.

NIM: Everyone is out except the turtle.

DIRECTOR VALE: The turtle stays in its tank.

NIM: The school group left a toy turtle in the tunnel.

The toy rolls along the floor and lodges beside the drain, blocking the water sensor.

ACT TWO

The water level in the service tunnel rises over Teal's boots. Sana closes the public-side gate while Pax watches the pressure gauge. Nim guides the last staff member up the stairs. A second wave rolls through the reef tank and shoves a loose display stand against the tunnel door.

NIM: The visitors are out. The staff corridor is not.

SANA: Teal, leave the valve. We are closing the hatch.

TEAL: One more turn and the bypass will hold.

PAX: The gauge is dropping, but the tunnel is filling. Come back now.

Teal turns the wheel one notch and pulls the rubber wedge from beneath it. The valve holds. Sana opens the hatch and helps Teal climb out as water laps at the threshold. The toy turtle floats out behind them.

SANA: Is that the sensor blocker?

TEAL: It made it through the emergency.

Pax removes the toy turtle with the wrench. The sensor clears. Teal holds the valve steady while Pax tightens the wheel. Sana turns the west pump back on at half speed. The tank's current slows.

PAX: Pressure is falling.

SANA: Keep the bypass open until the reading holds.

They wait. The gauge settles in the green. The lights return to normal. The fish comes out from behind the shipwreck and swims in a small circle.

Nim speaks into the public-address system.

NIM: The aquarium is closed for a filter repair. All visitors have exited safely.

DIRECTOR VALE: How long until we reopen?

PAX: We inspect every seal and replace the filter. It is not a five-minute fix.

Teal climbs out of the service hatch, wet to the elbows. Sana hands her a towel. The manager unlocks the staff exit and asks everyone to leave the public floor clear.

EXT. AQUARIUM - EVENING

The school group waits outside with their teacher. Nim returns the toy turtle to a child. The child thanks the crew. A repair truck arrives with a replacement filter.

FINAL IMAGE: Through the tank glass, the fish glides through still water as the red filter light stays dark.
