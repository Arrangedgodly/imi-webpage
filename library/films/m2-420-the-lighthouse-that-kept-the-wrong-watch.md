# The Lighthouse That Kept the Wrong Watch

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: maritime thrillers, isolated keepers, and the beacon whose schedule is more dramatic than the storm*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
MARA, a macaque lighthouse keeper
PIP, a capuchin mechanic
VEX, an orangutan coast inspector
NARI, a lemur radio operator
ODO, a gibbon sailor

SCENE 1. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE ISLAND - DUSK

A lighthouse stands above a calm sea. Mara checks a clock beside the lamp. Pip carries a toolbox. Vex arrives by small boat with an inspection clipboard.

VEX: The beacon flashes every thirty seconds.

MARA: It flashes when ships need it.

VEX: The schedule says every thirty seconds.

NARI: The sea is calm.

VEX: Calm can change.

Odo steps off the boat, carrying a coil of rope. He says his sailboat is anchored beyond the rocks. Vex asks whether he has seen the light. Odo says he has seen it every night for ten years.

VEX: Then you know the schedule.

ODO: I know where the rocks are.

Mara leads them inside. The lamp turns slowly. A brass label reads "ROTATE BY HAND IF MOTOR STOPS." Pip inspects the motor. It is running.

SCENE 2. INT. LIGHTHOUSE LANTERN ROOM - NIGHT

Vex sets a stopwatch. He insists on observing one full cycle. The beam crosses the water, passes the window, and lands on the island's laundry line. A white sheet glows like a ship.

VEX: Unregistered vessel.

MARA: That is my sheet.

Pip waits for the rotating lamp to carry its beam past the laundry and back to the sea. Nari listens to the radio. Odo reports a small fishing boat beyond the rocks, but it is staying in the safe channel.

VEX: We need a distress signal.

ODO: There is no distress.

VEX: We need a signal that says there is no distress.

Mara tries to radio the coast station. Vex interrupts to announce each part of the beacon cycle. His voice creates feedback in the radio, turning his official "clear waters" message into a long wavering tone.

NARI: The station heard you.

VEX: What did they say?

NARI: They asked if the lighthouse is singing.

SCENE 3. INT. RADIO ROOM - LATER

A cloud covers the moon. The sea remains calm, but the lighthouse window rattles in the wind. Vex calls this a developing storm. Mara checks the barometer; it has not moved.

VEX: The atmosphere is building suspense.

MARA: The window needs a latch.

Pip goes to the tool shelf. The correct latch is missing. Odo offers a spare rope. Pip ties the window handle to a shelf bracket. The next gust takes up the slack and nudges the window open. Pip shortens the loop, pulling it shut again.

ODO: The rope is for a boat.

PIP: The window is acting like one.

Nari receives a radio call from the fishing boat. It is asking whether the shining sheet is part of the lighthouse signal. Mara admits she forgot the laundry outside. Vex tells her not to reveal that during the inspection.

VEX: We must preserve the mystery.

MARA: It is a sheet.

Vex raises the stopwatch. The beam turns. Everyone ducks as it passes the radio room. It illuminates Vex's clipboard, where his own reflection looks like a second inspector.

SCENE 4. INT. LANTERN ROOM - MIDNIGHT

Pip removes the sheet from the line and folds it. On the next revolution, no laundry interrupts the beam. The fishing boat radios that it has passed the rocks safely.

ODO: They followed the light.

VEX: They followed the schedule.

MARA: They followed the channel.

Vex checks his timing sheet. He has written "flash at :00, :30, and whenever the laundry glows." Mara asks him to remove the last note. Vex refuses, saying it is an observed condition.

Pip notices the motor belt has slipped off its pulley. The beam slows. Mara turns the lamp by hand, following the marked arc, while Pip replaces and tensions the belt. Vex starts timing them. Nari gives instructions over the radio. Odo watches the sea and calls each passing boat.

ODO: Clear water. One fishing boat. No sheet.

PIP: The lamp is catching up.

VEX: Keep the pace dramatic.

Mara completes the turn. The motor restarts with a click. The beam crosses the harbor as the fishing boat passes the outer channel buoy. Odo watches from the window; his own boat remains anchored beyond the rocks.

SCENE 5. EXT. LIGHTHOUSE ISLAND - DAWN

Vex signs the inspection sheet. The beacon operated through the night; the laundry has been removed. Mara hangs the sheet inside to dry. Pip repairs the window latch with a piece of the old stopwatch strap.

VEX: The lighthouse passed.

MARA: The boats passed.

NARI: The radio stopped singing.

Odo boards his boat and pushes off. Vex asks whether he will return tonight to confirm the timing. Odo points at the daylight.

ODO: I'll come back when I need the light.

Vex checks the schedule. It says the beacon is off until dusk. He stamps the clipboard "ON TIME."

FINAL IMAGE: In morning sunlight the beacon stands dark, while Vex waits beside it with the stopwatch running.
