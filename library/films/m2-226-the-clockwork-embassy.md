# The Clockwork Embassy

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: political thrillers, tense embassy corridors, coded handshakes, and a diplomatic crisis governed by a broken revolving door*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-008*

CAST
AMBASSADOR SIV, a gibbon
DEPUTY LUMA, a capuchin
MINISTER TARE, a macaque
DOORKEEPER NIM, a ring-tailed lemur

SCENE 1. INT. CLOCKWORK EMBASSY - MORNING

A brass clock fills the lobby. Its gears tick loudly as two delegations arrive to negotiate water rights at a shared orchard. The spring drought has lowered the canal. Ambassador Siv carries a basket of ripe figs. Minister Tare brings green figs from the other side of the boundary.

SIV: We have arrived with a gift.

TARE: We have arrived with the same gift.

LUMA: That is either promising or a coded warning.

The delegations approach the revolving door. It turns once, then stops with both sides trapped in separate sections. Doorkeeper Nim tries the brass lever. The clock chimes, and the door turns backward.

SCENE 2. INT. REVOLVING DOOR - CONTINUOUS

The diplomats circle in the door, unable to enter or leave. Each group tries to step out when the other steps in. Luma places one fig in the center compartment; Tare adds a green fig. The door turns again, mixing the baskets.

SIV: Our figs have crossed the boundary.

TARE: So has your hand.

SIV: I was reaching for the fig.

TARE: It was on my side.

Nim opens the emergency latch. The door releases everyone at once, depositing the ambassadors in the lobby and the figs on a carpet. Two figs roll through a vent beneath the brass clock. Tare checks the basket count. Siv checks the door. The aides check each other's sleeves for hidden notes.

SCENE 3. INT. CONFERENCE ROOM - LATER

A map shows the orchard divided by a narrow fence. Under the fence runs one irrigation canal with two control wheels. Each delegation claims the other has been diverting water. A tree grows over the line; its branches reach both courtyards.

LUMA: The roots draw from one canal.

TARE: Our channel has been dry for a week.

SIV: Ours too.

Tare and Siv point at different tree rings on the map, each claiming the other side had the first turn. The clock chimes every five minutes, interrupting their accusations. Nim silently replaces the conference water with fig juice. The ministers take a sip, then inspect the cups for color.

SCENE 4. INT. EMBASSY GARDEN - AFTERNOON

The diplomats walk outside to inspect the tree and the canal gate. One branch hangs low with fruit. Tare reaches up, but the figs sway out of reach. Siv braces the branch, and Tare picks one. The branch springs back, scattering leaves onto both delegations.

SIV: The tree has no respect for the boundary.

TARE: It is a tree. The canal is the problem.

Luma finds the water gate's brass indicator wedged at the midpoint. Neither side can tell which way it was last turned. The canal bed is dry on both banks. A fresh mark on the gate suggests somebody has tried to force it.

SCENE 5. INT. CONFERENCE ROOM - LATE AFTERNOON

The groups return to the table. They draw two arrows around the shared tree, one for pruning and one for picking. The clock's gears grind to a halt. Nim opens the back panel and finds a fallen fig stuck between two teeth. Beneath it, a small metal chain runs down through the embassy floor toward the canal.

NIM: The clock stopped because it was hungry.

SIV: The clock is not hungry.

A second fig rolls from the gear housing. The clock starts again, and a clunk echoes from below. A brass pointer on the wall's control map jumps toward Tare's side. Tare takes a key from his sleeve. Siv blocks his hand. Luma notices a second keyhole on the clock panel, hidden beneath the brass face.

SCENE 6. INT. CLOCK ROOM - MOMENTS LATER

The diplomats descend into the embassy's clock room. Two keyholes connect the clock to the water gate; one key turns the channel toward either district, but the second must hold the safety brake. Tare has one key. Siv has the other. Neither admits knowing the mechanism.

TARE: If we turn it alone, we can take the full flow.

SIV: And if the other side turns it back, the gate will break.

NIM: That is why the old treaty required both keys.

Tare accuses Nim of hiding the treaty. Nim admits he found the mechanism years ago and wedged it at midpoint last week after seeing the gate lever forced. He kept the water from going to either side until the delegates arrived.

LUMA: You stopped both districts' water while waiting for signatures?

NIM: The emergency form required two copies. They have been disputing which copy was first.

SCENE 7. EXT. ORCHARD CANAL GATE - SUNSET

The ministers carry both keys to the gate. They do not divide the channel in half; they set a timed rotation, with each district taking a turn before the clock chimes. Siv hands his safety key to Luma. She holds the brake while Tare inserts the control key. Siv and Tare turn that handle together, each refusing to be the first to let go. The gate opens one notch. Water reaches the dry canal.

TARE: If the clock stops, the gate holds here.

NIM: If the clock stops, bring it a fig.

They sign the agreement on the gate's stone ledge. The guards watch both sides; neither can close the channel alone. The embassy bell rings once for each delegation.

FINAL IMAGE: Inside the clock room, the twin keys hang beside the ticking brass gears, and a single fig sits well away from the mechanism.
