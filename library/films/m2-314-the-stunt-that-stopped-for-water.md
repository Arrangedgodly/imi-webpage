# The Stunt That Stopped for Water

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: stunt-show bravado, relentless action montages, and productions that treat basic breaks as a dramatic interruption*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
STUNT COORDINATOR LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR VEX, a macaque
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
DIRECTOR ODO, a baboon
MEDIC NARI, an orangutan

SCENE 1. INT. STUDIO GYM - MORNING

A padded mat fills the stage. Actor Vex wears a foam shoulder guard. Director Odo explains the scene: a hero gets up after a tumble, takes a map and escapes.

ODO: It should feel unstoppable.

LUMA: It should stop at the mat.

Nari checks the water station and the floor. Pip reads the stunt sheet.

PIP: One controlled fall, then stand.

VEX: Can I roll twice?

LUMA: Once. Then check in.

Vex looks at the camera. Odo asks him to keep the rehearsal private. Vex says the audience should know how hard he works. He steps onto the mat and does a safe side roll, then stands.

ODO: Again, with more weight.

NARI: No. The sheet says one.

SCENE 2. INT. STUDIO GYM - LATER

Vex asks for a montage. Odo orders another take. Luma marks the roll, and Pip counts the sequence. Vex completes the fall and stands. He waits for the map handoff. No one brings it.

PIP: The prop is at the other end.

VEX: I have escaped without a map.

Luma retrieves the map. Nari offers water. Vex says the scene has not earned a break. Nari tells him to drink before the next take. He takes a sip, turns toward camera, and coughs from laughing.

ODO: We need the stumble.

NARI: That was water.

VEX: It was a brave sip.

Luma says the scene is complete. Odo asks for one more take to show fatigue. Vex repeats the roll, slower. Luma stops him and checks the mat. The mat has shifted a few inches. She resets it before the next attempt.

SCENE 3. INT. EDIT BAY - NOON

Odo watches a montage of Vex's stunt. The fall appears three times. Each repetition is slightly closer. Pip asks why the character needs to fall so often. Odo says the audience expects a training sequence.

PIP: He fell once.

VEX: Three times in the edit.

Nari notes the action is clear without the repeated footage. Odo adds a shot of Vex drinking water. He wants it to look like a recovery moment. Luma says it is a break. Odo adds dramatic percussion. The sip becomes a turning point.

NARI: The break is not part of the stunt.

ODO: It is part of the montage.

VEX: I would like another cup.

Luma hands him the bottle. He drinks normally. Odo attempts to cut the sip to look heroic, but each edit begins before Vex reaches the bottle and ends after he puts it down. The cup appears and disappears between cuts.

SCENE 3A. INT. STUDIO GYM - NOON

Nari checks the stunt sheet after the cut. Vex wants to take a photograph beside the stacked pads, holding his empty bottle upside down. Odo asks if they are ready for another take. Luma says the mat needs to be reset first. Pip notices a tape mark has curled at the corner; he presses it flat while Nari inspects the landing area.

VEX: We are losing the action window.

NARI: The action is a single roll.

ODO: We can shoot after the check.

Vex takes a seat. The camera operator films his reflection in the studio mirror as he waits. He practices looking determined, then notices the water station behind him. He reaches for the bottle, remembers the camera and turns the drink into a heroic toast. Nari lowers the lens.

NARI: You can drink without performing.

Vex takes an ordinary sip and hands the bottle to Pip. Pip refills it. The crew waits until Vex is ready, instead of timing the next fall to the montage music.

SCENE 4. INT. STUDIO GYM - AFTERNOON

They restage the scene with one fall. Pip hands Vex the map before the action. Luma checks the mat and gives the go-ahead. Vex rolls once, stands, and takes the map. He reaches for the water bottle on his own cue after the scene ends.

ODO: Cut before the drink.

VEX: The scene is over.

NARI: The action has one clean ending.

Odo watches the playback. The fall, recovery and escape are visible. The water break happens outside the shot. He asks if they can add the music later. Luma says yes, after everyone is finished.

SCENE 5. INT. STUDIO GYM - EVENING

The final cut shows Vex rise, take the map and leave. Pip follows. No repeated tumble appears. The crew packs the pads while Nari fills the water bottles for tomorrow.

FINAL IMAGE: Vex carries his own bottle offstage, and Odo holds the camera on the empty mat as if expecting another fall.
