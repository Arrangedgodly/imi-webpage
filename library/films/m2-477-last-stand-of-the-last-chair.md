# The Last Stand of the Last Chair

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: siege films that turn a small supply shortage into a grand military campaign with maps, ranks and stirring speeches*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: When a community hall has one chair left before a packed meeting, a group of monkeys plans a siege-like campaign to retrieve it from behind a folding-screen fortress.

Cast: hall manager Noma, an orangutan; strategist Kito, a macaque; scout Fen, a lemur; speaker Daro, a capuchin; custodian Bex, a gibbon.

SCENE 1 - INT. COMMUNITY HALL - EVENING

Rows of residents enter. One chair remains. It is behind a folding screen holding the sound system. Kito pins a hall map to a corkboard and draws a red line to the chair.

KITO: We move at the second bell.

NOMA: The second bell means the meeting starts.

DARO: We need one seat for the guest speaker.

BEX: I can unfold another chair from the storage closet.

Kito marks a defensive perimeter around the screen. Fen crawls under the refreshments table to scout. She returns with a cookie and no useful intelligence.

FEN: The biscuit supply is vulnerable.

SCENE 2 - INT. HALL - MOMENTS LATER

Kito assigns Bex to hold the screen, Fen to watch the aisle and Daro to distract the crowd with a speech. Daro climbs onto a low platform.

DARO: Friends, we gather at a crossroads of comfort.

The crowd turns. Bex quietly opens the storage closet, but Kito mistakes the door creak for a signal and sends Fen forward. She ducks behind the screen and bumps the sound stand. A microphone squeals. Everyone looks over.

NOMA: The speaker is not here yet.

KITO: The signal was premature.

BEX: That was the closet door.

Noma shows them a stack of folding chairs in the closet. The top chair is jammed under a box of meeting folders.

SCENE 3 - INT. STORAGE CLOSET - CONTINUOUS

Kito draws a second map on the back of the first. Bex tells him the box is light. Kito orders a three-point lift. Fen takes the left side; Daro takes the right. The box slides, spilling folders across the floor. Each folder is labeled AGENDA.

DARO: The documents have breached the line.

NOMA: Pick them up before the meeting begins.

They stack the folders. Bex lifts the box with one hand and pulls out the chair. Kito's elaborate rope plan remains tied around the doorknob. He tries to explain it as a decoy. The knot catches his sleeve and pins him briefly to the door.

FEN: The door has captured our strategist.

Bex unties him. Kito stands, brushes his sleeve and announces that the operation has succeeded.

The residents begin asking for seats before the guest arrives. Kito directs them into a formation that leaves the aisle clear but accidentally faces everyone toward the blank corkboard. Daro tries to use the chalkboard as a campaign map and draws arrows between chairs. Noma erases the arrows to write the meeting agenda. Kito watches his strategy disappear under the words APPROVE PARK CLEANUP.

KITO: The objective has changed.

NOMA: The objective is on the agenda.

SCENE 4 - INT. COMMUNITY HALL - LATER

They carry the chair toward the guest speaker's place. A resident has already taken a seat on the floor beside the stage and offers the chair to an older neighbor. Noma nods approval. Daro asks where the speaker will sit.

NOMA: We have a bench behind the lectern.

Kito checks the map. It shows the bench, but he had covered it with his red line. He peels the map from the corkboard. The line comes away with it, leaving a blank board.

KITO: We deployed forces toward the wrong objective.

DARO: The chair went where it was needed.

The guest speaker arrives and sits on the bench. The audience gives the neighbor a hand as she settles into the chair. Kito tries to deliver a victory speech. A microphone stand blocks his path, and he must walk around it in a slow loop.

SCENE 5 - INT. COMMUNITY HALL - END OF MEETING

Bex folds the empty chair and returns it to the closet. Kito offers to help stack the others. Fen carries the cookie plate to the kitchen. Daro thanks the residents for their patience, then sits on the bench beside the guest speaker.

KITO: The hall has enough seats when we stop treating furniture as territory.

NOMA: That is a useful conclusion. Keep it short.

Kito starts another speech. Bex switches off the microphone. Kito finishes silently, gesturing at the map. No one can see it because it is folded under his arm.

FINAL IMAGE: The folded chair leans beside the closet, while the meeting map hangs upside down and the neighbor occupies the seat nearest the stage.
