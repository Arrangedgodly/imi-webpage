# The Dub Does Not Match the Mouth

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: mismatched dubbing, voice replacement, and postproduction crews who change dialogue without checking the actor's mouth*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
DIRECTOR LUMA, a lemur
ACTOR NARI, an orangutan
VOICE ACTOR ODO, a baboon
SOUND EDITOR PIP, a capuchin
TRANSLATOR VEX, a macaque

SCENE 1. INT. SOUND BOOTH - MORNING

Voice actor Odo stands before a microphone. Director Luma watches a silent picture of Nari standing at a mountain gate.

LUMA: Say, "The bridge is closed."

ODO: The bridge is closed.

VEX: In the original script, she says, "The gate is open."

LUMA: The picture shows a bridge.

VEX: The word in the old language means either gate or bridge, depending on the valley.

PIP: The mouth forms one syllable.

Odo studies the screen. Nari opens her mouth, closes it, and turns toward the bridge.

ODO: Bridge is two syllables.

LUMA: Then we need a shorter line.

VEX: "No crossing."

Odo records it. Pip plays the line. The syllables land before Nari's mouth opens.

SCENE 2. INT. EDIT BAY - LATER

Pip drags the audio track across the picture. Odo watches his voice enter as Nari is still looking away.

ODO: I am speaking from behind her.

PIP: You are speaking before she moves her jaw.

LUMA: Can we slow the voice?

PIP: It will sound like Odo underwater.

Vex translates the line as "Crossing is forbidden." Odo tries to fit it into Nari's mouth movements. He gets through "cross" as the mouth closes. Luma asks if they can trim the rest. Pip cuts the waveform. It now says "Crossing is for."

ODO: For whom?

VEX: The sentence is incomplete.

Luma asks for a natural line that fits two mouth openings. Nari arrives from the set and watches the picture.

NARI: I said, "Wait, boat."

Vex checks the translation notes. The phrase is a warning to a small ferry, not a bridge closure. The movie scene is supposed to stop a ferry from leaving before a passenger boards.

SCENE 3. INT. RECORDING BOOTH - NOON

Odo records "Wait, boat." It fits the mouth, but sounds as if he is calling a pet. Vex suggests "Hold the boat." Odo says it in one breath. Pip aligns the first word, but the final consonant continues after Nari's mouth closes.

PIP: The mouth is finished.

ODO: The sentence is not.

Nari asks to see the whole scene. The ferry rope has slipped from the post, and the boat is drifting away. Her line is urgent but not angry. Luma asks Odo to try the words while gripping a rope. Odo leans toward the microphone and says, "Hold! Boat!" The timing fits, but the sound of his costume brushing the stand adds a rustle.

Pip removes the rustle. The voice now arrives cleanly, but the scene appears to be an argument with an invisible person. Nari suggests a two-word call: "Wait, boat!" She turns toward the river and raises one hand at the first word, then points to the rope at the second.

SCENE 4. INT. EDIT BAY - AFTERNOON

They play the new line. The sound fits her mouth, but the subtitle still reads "The bridge is closed." Vex opens the translation file. It has been copied from a different scene about a bridge closure. He changes the subtitle. The film now shows Nari warning the boat, while Odo's voice lands exactly on the hand signal.

LUMA: Good. Now the ferry stops.

PIP: The picture already shows it drifting.

They move the next shot forward. The boat reaches the dock; a passenger steps aboard. Nari pulls the rope tight. Odo records a final breath before the rope catches. The microphone picks up Pip whispering, "Now." He removes it.

ODO: The boat is safe.

VEX: That is not in the line.

NARI: It is in the scene.

Vex asks Nari to mark the two mouth openings on a printout. She draws a line for "Wait" when she raises her palm and a second for "boat" when she points. Odo watches the silent footage and repeats the gesture from his booth, matching the cadence with his own hands. The first word lands too early. Pip slides the audio a few frames. Odo's consonant now arrives as Nari's palm lifts.

PIP: That is the first mark.

VEX: The subtitle still says "The bridge is closed."

NARI: Change it before the audience learns the wrong warning.

Vex replaces the subtitle. He reads it aloud once, then stops himself before adding an explanation.

SCENE 5. INT. SMALL SCREENING ROOM - EVENING

The crew watches the cut. The warning is short. The ferry stops, the passenger boards, and the rope is secured. The translation subtitle matches the spoken line. Luma asks whether any additional explanation is needed. Vex says the words are clear in context.

FINAL IMAGE: Odo signs the final audio track; beneath his signature, Pip writes a note: "Check the mouth before the microphone."
