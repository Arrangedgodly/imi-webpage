# The Captain's Map Leads to a Flower Bed

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: pirate treasure adventures that treat an ordinary map legend as an ancient riddle and mistake gardening supplies for buried gold*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-016*

Logline: A crew of monkey treasure hunters follows a weathered map across a community garden, only to discover the X marks a compost bin scheduled for pickup.

Cast: Captain Jori, a macaque; first mate Fenn, a capuchin; navigator Vala, a gibbon; gardener Omi, an orangutan; volunteer Leka, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN GATE - MORNING

Jori unrolls a parchment map on a wheelbarrow. The paper is old and blotched. A large X sits beside a sketch of three flowers.

JORI: The treasure is buried beneath the three sisters.

VALA: The map key says the flowers mark a compost bed.

FENN: The old ink must have hidden the key.

Omi appears carrying a watering can and a rake.

OMI: That bed is where we keep the compost. Please stay on the path.

JORI: The garden keeper protects the treasure.

OMI: I protect the seedlings.

SCENE 2 - EXT. GARDEN PATH - LATER

The crew takes a compass reading. Fenn spins the compass and points toward the shed. Vala looks at the sun and the map.

VALA: The compass is next to a steel wheelbarrow. It is pointing at the metal, not north.

JORI: A false bearing.

FENN: The shed could be a decoy.

They approach the shed. A sign says TOOLS. Jori opens it. Inside are shovels, gloves and a bag of seed potatoes.

JORI: Our treasure is guarded by farming gear.

OMI: Those are tools. Put them back where they belong.

SCENE 2A - EXT. GARDEN SHED - CONTINUOUS

Jori studies the seed packets in the tool shed. Each has a picture of a plant and a number. Fenn lines them up like a code.

FENN: The numbers must identify the treasure chest.

VALA: They are planting depths in centimeters.

Jori takes a ruler and measures the bag of potatoes. He finds a paper tag, not a jewel. It says PLANT SIX INCHES DEEP.

JORI: The treasure has instructions.

OMI: The potatoes need to be planted this afternoon.

Fenn reaches for a shovel. Omi shows him the path from the shed to the compost bed, then hands him a pair of gloves.

OMI: If you want to help, turn the compost with the fork. Do not dig around the roots.

Jori takes the fork like a ceremonial spear. The compost shifts, revealing a bright orange carrot.

JORI: A golden root!

OMI: A carrot. It was left in the bin by mistake.

Vala checks the map's scale. The X is printed where the bin sits, not beneath a flower bed.

VALA: The map tells us where to collect compost, not where to bury a chest.

Jori wipes soil from the map and rolls it up carefully.

SCENE 3 - EXT. COMPOST BED - AFTERNOON

Leka arrives with the garden committee ledger. It lists the compost delivery, the pickup date and a donation of three flower bulbs. The map's X aligns with the bin's hinge.

LEKA: The map was drawn by the first garden club. The X marks the compost collection point.

JORI: The three flowers are sisters?

LEKA: They are three sister plants: corn, beans and squash. This is a planting guide.

Vala turns the map over. The back reads GARDEN PLAN, YEAR ONE.

FENN: We have traveled across the yard to find a drawing of a vegetable bed.

JORI: The paper is still old.

SCENE 4 - EXT. COMPOST BIN - CONTINUOUS

Jori opens the bin and finds a heap of leaves, peelings and soil. No treasure chest. A small metal tag is attached to the lid, engraved with the garden club name.

JORI: A relic.

OMI: A label. It tells the pickup crew where the bin belongs.

Fenn lifts a shovel as if it were a sword. Omi takes it from him and places it in the shed.

VALA: The legend has a practical ending.

JORI: Every good quest ends with a discovery.

LEKA: The discovery is that the pickup is tomorrow. We need the lid closed.

SCENE 5 - EXT. COMMUNITY GARDEN GATE - SUNSET

The crew folds the map and helps Omi water the three sister plants. Jori places the compass away from the wheelbarrow. Vala copies the garden plan into a new notebook. Fenn closes the shed and checks the tools.

JORI: We shall return when the next map appears.

OMI: The next map is already posted by the gate. It shows where the watering cans go.

FENN: We will study it carefully.

The crew marches toward the gate, then turns back to put the watering can away. The X on the old map is now hidden beneath a fresh green leaf.

FINAL IMAGE: The parchment rests on the garden table beside a labeled watering can, its mysterious X pointing directly to the compost bin's pickup tag.
