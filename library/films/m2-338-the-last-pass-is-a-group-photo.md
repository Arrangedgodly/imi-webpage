# The Last Pass Is a Group Photo

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: championship sports films, underdog montages, dramatic locker-room speeches, and final plays that require everyone to cooperate*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-012*

CAST
COACH NARI, a ring-tailed lemur
TAVI, a young capuchin goalie
MILO, a macaque forward
SULA, an orangutan team captain
REFEREE VEX, a gibbon

SCENE 1. INT. COMMUNITY ICE RINK - NIGHT

The Canopy Comets trail the Quarry Owls by one point. The scoreboard reads 00:42. Coach Nari draws a play on the locker-room board. The diagram looks like a tree with twelve arrows.

NARI: We need one clean pass.

MILO: Which arrow is me?

NARI: The one not wearing a helmet.

Milo is wearing his helmet. He removes it. The diagram now matches the wall hooks.

SCENE 2. INT. LOCKER ROOM - LATER

The team prepares for the final period. Before the final period, Coach Nari screens a training montage. It shows the players practicing passes, missing the puck, and chasing it behind the equipment shed. The final shot is the team mascot trying to put on its leaf head, which rolls downhill and returns to the locker room. Sula watches the montage twice and asks whether the team can practice the pass instead of watching it. Nari shuts off the projector.

SULA: We have trained for this moment. The players nod solemnly. The team mascot's oversized leaf costume is wedged in the doorway. Tavi crawls beneath it and opens the door from the other side.

SULA: We have trained for this moment.

TAVI: We trained on dry ice.

Nari explains the ice is fresh. The players slide across the locker room as they practice their steps. Vex enters with a whistle and tells them to save their energy. The whistle is frozen to his mouth; he pulls it free and accidentally blows a long note.

SCENE 3. INT. RINK - FINAL PERIOD

The mascot crew carries the oversized leaf head from the locker-room doorway and sets it beside the boards, clear of the players. The Comets take the ice. The Owls press hard. An opposing player launches a shot, and Tavi blocks it with his pads. The puck rebounds into the mascot's oversized leaf head, which has been set beside the rink. The mascot spins down the boards. Vex stops play while the equipment crew retrieves it. The crowd chants for a replay; the scoreboard displays an accidental close-up of the empty costume. Tavi blocks one shot and sends the puck to Sula. She passes to Milo, who tries to execute the elaborate tree diagram. He skates in a spiral and returns to the same spot. The crowd applauds the unexpected pattern.

NARI: Use the simple play.

MILO: I am looking for it.

Sula takes the puck and sees Tavi open near the boards. She passes. Tavi stops the puck with his stick, then waits because the play card said to hold for a dramatic pause. The scoreboard continues counting down. The crowd begins a slow chant: pass, pass, pass.

Tavi sends the puck to Milo, who could shoot but notices Sula wide open. He passes across the rink. A defender intercepts, then slips on a patch of ice and slides harmlessly into the goal net. Vex stops the clock to check the net. The scoreboard freezes at 00:09.

SCENE 4. INT. RINK - MOMENTS LATER

The referee confirms no one scored. The teams reset for the final play. Sula looks at her teammates and discards the complicated diagram. She tells them to move into open space and pass to whoever is free. The puck travels from player to player in a quick line. The Owls chase it, turning their backs on the goal.

Milo receives the final pass. He sees Tavi unguarded and sends it back. Tavi scores just before the buzzer. The crowd erupts. The Comets win by one. Vex signals the goal and then asks the camera to check the clock. It reads 00:00 and emits a tiny horn.

SCENE 5. INT. RINK - AFTER THE GAME

The team celebrates with a group photo. The photographer asks everyone to hold a victory pose. The leaf mascot slips on the ice and lands seated in front of the team. The picture captures the whole squad laughing. Tavi is holding the puck. Sula asks if it belongs in the trophy case. Nari says it belongs to the rink until the equipment check is done.

Milo finds the play diagram in the locker room. He flips it over and sees that the back is a lunch menu. The team returns their sticks to the rack. Vex asks Coach Nari whether she will keep the same strategy next season.

NARI: I will keep the part where we pass.

FINAL IMAGE: The scoreboard's frozen arrows melt into clear water while the team photo dries on the wall.
