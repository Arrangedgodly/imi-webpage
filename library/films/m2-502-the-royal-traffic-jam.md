# The Royal Traffic Jam

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: royal procession epics that insist a ruler must arrive by chariot even when the parade route is blocked by everyday traffic*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-017*

Logline: A queen's ceremonial chariot stalls behind a delivery cart, prompting her advisers to stage an epic detour through the market.

Cast: Queen Luma, a macaque; driver Venn, a gibbon; marshal Sori, an orangutan; market seller Pavi, a capuchin; herald Jori, a lemur.

SCENE 1 - EXT. MARKET STREET - MORNING

A royal chariot approaches the square. A delivery cart is stopped in the narrow lane, unloading crates of pottery. The herald blows a trumpet. The seller keeps carrying pots to a stall.

JORI: Make way for the queen.

PAVI: The cart will move when it is empty.

Luma leans out of the chariot and asks how long the delivery will take. Pavi says five minutes. Sori consults the procession schedule and announces that the arrival window is now closed.

QUEEN LUMA: Can we wait five minutes?

SORI: The route plan has no waiting section.

SCENE 2 - EXT. MARKET SQUARE - CONTINUOUS

Venn turns the chariot down a side street. It is blocked by a row of handcarts. The wheels cannot pass between them. Sori calls it an ambush. Pavi walks over and moves one cart, then another. The lane opens, but a fruit stall awning hangs low across the way.

Venn lowers the chariot's canopy. It catches on the awning. Luma ducks. Jori blows the trumpet again, and the awning lifts just enough for the chariot to pass.

SCENE 2A - EXT. MARKET STREET - CONTINUOUS

The chariot crawls behind the delivery cart. Jori blows the fanfare again. The pottery seller begins matching the trumpet rhythm with each crate she carries. The crowd starts clapping along, turning the blocked street into an accidental parade. Sori tells the musicians to stop. They stop, and the crowd continues clapping without them.

Venn tries to turn the chariot in the narrow lane. Its rear wheel catches on a loose paving stone. Pavi sets down a crate and helps lift the wheel free. Luma offers to wait until the entire delivery is unloaded. Sori checks the clock and crosses the scheduled arrival off the route plan.

SORI: The royal arrival window is closed.

LUMA: The market is still open.

The crowd cheers when the last pot reaches the stall. The chariot moves forward at walking speed, with Luma greeting the people beside it.

SCENE 3 - EXT. PALACE GATE - LATER

The detour ends at the palace gate, where a street sweeper is cleaning the entrance. Sori orders the route cleared. The sweeper asks for another minute to finish. Luma gets out and walks alongside the chariot. The royal guards follow, unsure whether to maintain formation.

SORI: The queen cannot arrive on foot.

LUMA: I am already here.

Jori announces the royal arrival. The trumpet plays a short fanfare. The street sweeper finishes and bows. Luma thanks him for clearing the path. Sori checks the schedule and says the queen has arrived twelve minutes late.

SCENE 4 - INT. PALACE COURTYARD - DAY

Sori proposes a ceremonial reset. The guards wheel the chariot back to the gate and ask Luma to repeat the arrival for the photographers. She refuses to leave the square again. Venn suggests a portrait beside the chariot instead. The chariot's wheel is still entangled in a strand of the awning ribbon. Pavi untangles it while the photographers adjust their backdrop.

LUMA: I can greet the market from here.

PAVI: The market is behind the wall.

Luma asks the guards to open the gate. She waves the chariot through slowly, letting the delivery cart follow it. The crowd from the street appears, carrying the last of the pottery. She greets them from the courtyard without redoing the entrance.

The chariot is parked beside the palace gate after the crowd passes through. The queen steps down and buys a small bowl from Pavi, who has brought his stall to the square. Sori asks whether it is an official royal purchase. Luma says it is a thank-you for waiting. The crowd begins clapping the trumpet rhythm again, this time without blocking the lane.

A herald tries to announce the arrival from the steps. His trumpet points toward the pottery stall, so the fanfare sounds from inside the market instead of the palace. He turns around and plays the final note toward the gate.

SCENE 5 - INT. PALACE GATE - AFTERNOON

The procession finishes. Sori amends the route plan with a waiting section and a walking alternative. Venn parks the chariot beside the gate. The street sweeper returns for a final pass, and this time the guards move aside without a trumpet.

FINAL IMAGE: The chariot stands empty beside the open gate, while the queen continues greeting the market on foot.
