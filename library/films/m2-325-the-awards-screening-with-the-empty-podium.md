# The Awards Screening with the Empty Podium

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: awards-season campaigns that stage prestige, speeches, and podiums before anyone has voted*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-011*

CAST
CAMPAIGN MANAGER VEX, a macaque
DIRECTOR NARI, an orangutan
ACTOR PIP, a capuchin
PROJECTIONIST LUMA, a lemur
PRODUCER ODO, a baboon

SCENE 1. INT. SCREENING ROOM - MORNING

Campaign manager Vex rolls a gold-painted podium into the screening room. The film is a small comedy about a ferry crew trying to return a lost mitten. Director Nari asks why the podium is in front of the screen.

VEX: The audience should feel the occasion.

LUMA: They cannot see the movie.

VEX: They can feel it.

Pip tries to walk around the podium and trips over its broad base. Odo steadies him. Vex says the stumble looks humble and asks if it can be used in the introduction. Pip says he was walking to his seat.

NARI: We came to screen the film.

VEX: And we will. After the positioning remarks.

SCENE 2. INT. SCREENING ROOM - LATER

Vex practices a speech into the microphone. He thanks the voters, the crew, the ferry, and the mitten. No voting body has been announced, and there is no award ceremony scheduled. Luma checks the booking sheet: the room is reserved for one preview screening.

LUMA: There is no podium event.

VEX: It is a portable event.

Odo places name cards on the front row. The cards identify guests as DIRECTOR, LEAD, PRODUCER and AWARD. The last chair is empty. Vex asks Pip to sit there for a rehearsal, then rises to thank him. Pip asks whether he is receiving an award or returning the mitten. Vex thanks him for both.

SCENE 3. INT. SCREENING ROOM - AFTERNOON

Nari starts the film. Vex stands in front of the projector beam, delivering an introduction. His shadow stretches across the screen, covering the ferry. Luma moves him aside. He steps back in when he reaches the phrase "a journey of uncommon significance."

NARI: The audience has not seen a frame.

VEX: They are anticipating it.

Pip enters carrying the mitten prop. He places it on the empty award chair so it will not be lost. Vex sees the mitten and asks whether it should be on the podium. Odo says the mitten is the plot's missing object, and the characters should discover it on the ferry. Vex removes it from the chair and sets it on the podium. The camera test shows it looking like a tiny golden trophy.

LUMA: It is not an award.

VEX: It is a symbol.

Nari starts the film again. The opening shows the crew searching the ferry. Vex keeps whispering campaign notes. The audience misses a joke. Luma pauses the projection and asks Vex to sit down. He does, but his podium blocks half the aisle.

SCENE 4. INT. SCREENING ROOM - EVENING

Odo wheels the podium to the back wall. The mitten returns to the screen, where the ferry crew finds it under a bench and argues over whose it is. The joke lands. Vex watches the scene without interrupting. The film ends with the crew hanging the mitten on a lost-and-found peg. The audience applauds.

VEX: That was moving.

NARI: It was a mitten.

VEX: It moved me.

Pip offers the mitten to Vex. He accepts it, then notices the empty award chair. Instead of rehearsing his speech, he hangs the mitten on the peg prop beside the screen. Luma says that is the ending. Vex asks whether a campaign can have an ending. Odo says every screening has one; the projector will turn off.


The projector starts. Vex stands beside the podium to introduce the film, but his shadow covers the first shot of the ferry. Luma switches on the room lights. Vex tries again from the side, where the microphone cable snags the podium base. Odo frees it; the microphone produces a low thump through the speakers. The audience laughs. Vex bows as if the thump were applause, and Nari signals that the film can finally begin.

SCENE 5. INT. LOBBY - NIGHT

The guests leave. Vex stands beside the podium with his speech cards, unable to decide whether to put them away. Pip and Nari carry the prop peg to the storage closet. Vex asks if he should make one final thank-you. Luma tells him the guests have already gone.

VEX: The empty room is a strong audience.

He turns the microphone toward the empty seats. A feedback squeal answers. Vex flinches, then laughs at himself. Odo disconnects the cable. The gold podium's detachable top slides off and lands upside down over Vex's speech cards. Nari lifts it and carries it toward storage.

FINAL IMAGE: The mitten hangs on the peg in the darkened room while Vex's unspoken acceptance speech rides beneath the podium top.
