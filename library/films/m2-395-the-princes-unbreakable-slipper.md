# The Prince's Unbreakable Slipper

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: fairy-tale searches, impossible fitting tests, and royal decrees built around one piece of footwear*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-014*

CAST
PRINCE TAVI, a capuchin
MIRA, a gibbon cobbler
NARI, a macaque herald
PIP, a lemur palace guard
LUMA, an orangutan princess

SCENE 1. INT. PALACE HALL - MORNING

A glass slipper sits on a velvet cushion. Prince Tavi announces that its owner will become the next royal guest of honor. Nari unrolls the decree.

NARI: The slipper will be tried on every resident until it fits.

MIRA: What if it does not fit anyone?

TAVI: Then we will keep trying.

Pip carries the cushion from door to door. At each house, a resident offers a foot. The slipper is made of glass, but it bends slightly whenever someone tries to put it on.

PIP: It is not glass.

MIRA: It is a very hard sugar shell.

TAVI: The story calls it glass.

Luma arrives. She has one bare foot and one shoe made of bark.

LUMA: I lost my slipper at the palace dance.

TAVI: Let us see if this one fits.

The herald calls three more residents forward. The first has a foot too small, the second has a boot over both feet, and the third wears a wooden leg that makes the slipper ring against the floor. Pip asks whether the sound means it fits. Mira says it means the heel is hollow.

NARI: The decree does not mention sound.

MIRA: The decree has never made a shoe.

Tavi insists the search should continue. The residents line up beside the cushion. The slipper bends when each person tries it, then springs back into shape. Luma waits until the line empties.

SCENE 2. EXT. VILLAGE STREET - LATER

Pip sets the cushion on a low wall. Luma tries the slipper. It fits around her toes but not her heel.

NARI: The decree says "fits."

MIRA: It says "fits perfectly."

Tavi asks the herald to read the exact wording. Nari checks the scroll. It says "find the person whose foot fits the slipper." It does not say perfectly.

Luma stands. The slipper stays on. Tavi declares the search complete. Pip notices the slipper has become stuck to her foot.

PIP: It fits a little too well.

Mira warms the sugar with a lantern. The slipper softens. Luma pulls her foot free, leaving the shell on the street.

TAVI: We have lost the slipper again.

LUMA: I have not.

She points to the bark shoe. It has a matching imprint on the sole. Mira recognizes the pattern as her own cobbler mark.

MIRA: I made that shoe.

LUMA: You made both.

Mira says she made the glass slipper as a festival prop. The royal decree was written on its label and copied by Nari before the tag fell off.

SCENE 3. INT. PALACE WORKROOM - AFTERNOON

Mira shows the original tag. It says "fragile, decorative, not for wearing." Tavi asks why the search began.

NARI: The decree was copied without the warning.

PIP: The entire kingdom tried it on.

Tavi asks for the original document. Nari finds the tag under the velvet cushion. It is stuck to the bottom.

Luma tries on the bark shoe. It fits comfortably. She says it was made for walking, unlike the glass shell.

TAVI: The story needs a fitting.

MIRA: The shoe has already been fitted. You are standing beside the cobbler.

Pip offers Luma the slipper as proof. She refuses it. The shell cracks when he lifts it. Mira takes the pieces and sets them on a tray.

SCENE 4. EXT. PALACE STEPS - EVENING

Tavi replaces the decree with a public notice: "The princess's shoe has been found. Please stop trying on the decoration." Nari reads it to the crowd.

A resident arrives holding a glass shoe. It has broken in half. Another resident carries a bent slipper. Pip starts to collect the damaged props.

LUMA: They were never shoes.

TAVI: I owe everyone an apology.

MIRA: You owe them a cobbler.

Tavi pays Mira to repair the bark shoes. The residents line up. The cobbler measures each foot and cuts new soles. The line moves quickly until Nari asks if the repairs qualify as a royal ceremony.

SCENE 5. INT. PALACE HALL - NIGHT

The palace hosts a dance. Luma wears her bark shoes. Tavi walks in ordinary boots. Mira sets the broken glass shell inside a frame with its warning label.

NARI: The slipper has been found.

LUMA: It was never lost.

Pip puts a sign beside the frame: "LOOK, DON'T WEAR."

FINAL IMAGE: The royal guests dance in repaired shoes while the fragile glass shell remains on its cushion, protected from the story.
