# The Dance That Waited for Everyone

*Format: Film parody*
*Parody target: climactic dance finales, ensemble cast reunions, and choreography that pauses the story until every character arrives*
*By: IMI Pictures Monkey Desk*
*Batch: IMI-M2-FILMS-004*

Logline: A dance-film finale keeps resetting so every supporting character can enter, while the janitor tries to finish closing the studio.

Cast: Luma, a macaque dancer; Bex, a gorilla choreographer; Puck, a capuchin assistant; and Rena, a lemur janitor.

SCENE 1. INT. DANCE STUDIO, EVENING

Luma finishes a solo. Bex calls for the ensemble finale. The music starts. The supporting cast enters from three doors, but one dancer is still changing shoes. Bex stops the track.

BEX: We need everyone in the last frame.

RENA: The studio closes at nine.

Puck asks if the janitor can be part of the number. Rena holds up the mop and says it is not a dance prop. Bex adds a fourth entrance for her anyway.

RENA: I am not in the cast.

BEX: The audience needs everyone.

RENA: The audience is not paying for the mop.

Puck asks her to hold the bucket until the take is done. She points to a water spill and the wet-floor sign. Bex calls cut. Rena dries the patch before anyone dances across it.

SCENE 2. INT. DANCE STUDIO, LATER

The ensemble practices the entrance. The first group arrives on the beat. The second group crosses behind them and blocks the mirror. Luma asks them to use the taped marks. Puck moves a speaker to widen the frame. Rena wheels her mop bucket past and catches the speaker cord before anyone trips.

BEX: Can the bucket enter on the downbeat?

RENA: It has wheels. It enters when I push it.

Puck marks an entrance for her. Rena wheels the bucket through, but it blocks the second row. The dancers turn sideways to pass it and accidentally make a clean formation. Luma asks them to remember the position. Bex counts eight beats; they reach their marks on seven, then all stop to watch the clock.

Bex asks Rena to hold the bucket until the take is done. She says the floor is wet from a water bottle spill and points to the sign. Bex stops rehearsal. The dancers wait while Rena dries the floor. Puck tries to include the cleanup in the choreography. Rena asks him to move the camera out of her way.

RENA: The mop is for the floor.

PUCK: It could be a feature.

RENA: It is a mop.

Luma marks three floor positions. The dancers cross behind the mirror again, blocking the view. Bex asks them to turn sideways. They form a narrow line, but the prop basket cannot pass. Luma asks them to use two rows and leave a clear aisle. Bex tests the space by walking the basket through.

SCENE 3. INT. DANCE STUDIO, NIGHT

The final take begins. Luma finishes her solo. Each group enters on cue. A jacket drops from the coat rack into the center of the formation. The dancers stop before stepping on it.

BEX: Who put that there?

DANCER: Puck moved the rack.

PUCK: I needed the frame clear.

Luma asks Bex to leave the number as a solo if a prop interrupts. Bex says the ending needs the whole group. Rena points out the clock; the closing crew cannot wait for every jacket. She turns off the music.

Luma asks Bex to leave the number as a solo if someone is absent. Bex says the ending needs the full cast. Rena says the closing crew cannot wait for every jacket. She turns off the music.

Puck asks if the finale can be shortened. Luma proposes a group line with three steps and a bow. The ensemble tries it. Everyone fits in the frame. The dancer ties the shoe before the take. Rena stands by the door with the mop bucket, ready to close.

BEX: Can we make the bow bigger?

LUMA: We can make it together.

Puck starts the track. The dancers complete the three steps, bow, and hold still until he calls cut. Rena checks the clock and opens the door for the last group to leave.

SCENE 4. INT. DANCE STUDIO, FINAL TAKE

The music begins. Luma performs her solo. The ensemble enters in two clean rows, takes three steps, and bows. The take ends without a reset. Bex asks for one more version with a longer spin. Rena points to the clock. He checks the time and says the short ending is final.

The dancers collect their jackets and water bottles. Rena checks the floor, moves the wet-floor sign, and locks the supply closet. Puck asks if she wants to join the cast photo. Rena poses with the mop, then returns to work. The cast photo fits in one frame, without another entrance. Bex looks at the clock, sees nine, and switches off the stereo himself.

FINAL IMAGE: The whole group fits in the last frame, including Rena at the doorway. The studio lights switch off after the bow.
